# Don't repeat this mistake in raid design

## Post 152475 by Icedcream (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-20T15:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152475#p152475 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My guild has full cleared Kara since I believe the second week of release so about 21-22 times. One major problem that we all could see foreseeing happen and given time has proven to be the case has been these set tokens.

Problem 1: Drop rate
Bizarre uneven drop rates that don't play out in a 1/2 year cycle. We've seen things like 13 of one helm and 18 of another helm. Pendant has a 20% chance of drop per 5 bosses you should see one about each week we have like 15/20-21 weeks. And the item was bad BIS for so many classes.

Why don't you just make a token a 100% drop on a boss? Why a chance that creates uneven distribution that tries to serve the same purpose?

Problem 2: Class tokens
Shared: Warrior, Warlock, Priest, Druid
Shared: Paladin, Mage, Shammy, Rogue, Hunter

Big problem is tokens basically going to offspec very quickly:
I've reviewed many of the top posted logs from this past week and the average guild has 7 dps warriors in their party. There is no DPS warrior set. They also have 1-2 MT who need the set. That means 1/4 of the entire raid is generally warriors because they are generally the best MT and highest DPS.

Suggestions:
 1)It's going to take 30 weeks to gear out 40 people consistently play. This is very long. Increase the drop rate. I know you want people to keep playing... ENGAGEMENT! But they will keep playing, they will gear offsets and alts...

 2)We don't need to be sitting here with 3 armor pieces for 5 months of work still... come ON!
Make a generic token all classes... We had a point were 2/3 of all tokens were warrior tokens and all our warlocks, priests, and druids were geared and the warriors were all passing yet the other token had people with zero still....

 3)Make consistent drop rates. We love chance or lotter on big drops (some days), but why some weeks you see zero class tokens for one side is disappointing for all.

Real problem is that this drop rate is not reproducible.
I have seen that gm posted a video where he kill, loot, respawn some boss and its loot changes. But when it comes to real players, loot is often always the same.

So I think, best thing that could be done is to show random generation seed to players at the start of the dungeon and decide all loot tables on that seed.
It will help to gather information for further testing. People will be able to collect RNG seeds across multiple raids and write down dropped loot. If loot will be same for different seeds, then problem is actually there.

Assuming that seed is often time-based. So if you make raids in same time, seeds might collide. Try to complete the raid in different real-world time of day. It might be hard to assemble it, but worth a try.
