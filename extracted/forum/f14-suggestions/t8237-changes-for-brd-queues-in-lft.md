# Changes for BRD queues in LFT

## Post 52927 by Getplucked (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-11T01:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52927#p52927 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After being 55+ for a while and running BRD a bunch, I'm noticing there's a bit of an issue with queueing for BRD in the Looking for Turtles addon.

When you queue for the "Emperor" one, 99% of the time people want to do a lava run, skipping over everything and going straight to Emp. This is annoying because there's lots of good/prebis loot on the bosses that you normally fight on a normal Emp run, as well as other stuff like the Smelt Dark Iron training.

Even when I get a full group and say that it's a normal run and not using the lava skip, people start dropping group.

There should be two queues:
 -Blackrock Depths (7 Dwarves)
 -Blackrock Depths (Emperor / Lava Run)

That way you can distinguish between doing a normal run and doing a "lava run".

Oh, and for the queue that just says "Blackrock Depths", you could change that to "Blackrock Depths - Quests". In my experiences, those are the three major types of BRD runs:
 -Lava skip runs
 -7 Dwarves/emp runs
 -Quest runs where the focus is going around and completing the plethora of BRD quests that exist.
