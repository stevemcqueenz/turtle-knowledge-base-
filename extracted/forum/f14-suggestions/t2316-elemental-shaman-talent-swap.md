# Elemental Shaman Talent Swap

## Post 14562 by Karrados (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2021-12-14T21:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14562#p14562 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello.   unhappy_turtle

As some of you who have played an Elemental Shaman in the past might have noticed the cast time reduction Talent for their main cast is rather deep inside the Elemental Tree.

Unlike other Caster that get their reduction at level 10 (or 15) they get their with Level 35 which, if you intend to level as a Caster, is simply horrible and not very fun to play with.

My suggestion would be to swap the Call of Thunder box with the Lightning Mastery box which would reduce the level required down to 20 which is still higher than the others but at least it is bearable.
