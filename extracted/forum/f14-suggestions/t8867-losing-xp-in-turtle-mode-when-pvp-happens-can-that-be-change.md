# Losing xp in turtle mode when pvp happens--can that be changed?

## Post 57222 by Sondier — 2023-08-14T07:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57222#p57222 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know you don't normally lose xp from a pvp death but it sucks that if you're being attacked by mobs and someone decides to grief you when your hp is low, you lose the xp if the mob gets the last hit. This just happened to me and it makes me want to completely avoid pvp when questing (although I don't like doing that). Is it possible to disable xp loss whenever you're engaged in pvp, within like a minute of it or something? That way, if I'm getting attacked by a player and die from a mob hit, it doesn't count?

Better yet, can we just get rid of xp loss in turtle mode, entirely? (heh, jking... kind of.)

Okay, stereotypical MMO boomer talk aside, I think this might be a pretty cheesable idea. Like, having a whole minute to die and not lose XP? The possibilities are endless.

Here's how I'd make it less broken, because I can't think of a way to make it function without some level of exploitability.

Have it so when you are attacked by a player of the opposite faction, you're given a buff that prevents you from losing XP when you die. This way, the only way to exploit it is to deliberately have someone following you around, through multiboxing or some other means, and have them attack you once when shit hits the fan. Which is tedious as heck so the only people I could see doing this are the ones that just want to farm the coins and rewards you get from Turtle Mode.
