# Suggestion: Weekly Elite Quests

## Post 6196 by Boros — 2020-06-03T21:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6196#p6196 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi All,

In the interest of building a stronger community to help our leveling turtles, I would like to suggest offering incentives for max-level players to repeat elite quests. On a weekly reset, players would be given the chance to choose one zone and it's elite quests. For example, perhaps the necromancy at work in Duskwood has resurrected those terrible foes that we slayed years ago. Completion could reward transmog charges, gold, or updated Duskwood items (Archeus, anyone) that could serve as low quality level 60 gear.

There could even be a meta-quest, asking heroes to campaign against these elite forces across the Eastern Kingdoms, resulting in a reward after completing 6 or 7 weeks of unique elite quests.

Doing so would boost the population in our leveling zones, and gives us 60's a chance to relive those excellent encounters.
