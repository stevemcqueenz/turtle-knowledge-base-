Telling you my personal perpective.
I usually lose slightly more than 1 gold per death.
When going to a dungeon for farming or for an item, I mostly evaluate the following:
Under a certain amount of time I usually get x gold or I need y time to get to the boss with the item.
If we keep wiping, that's negative gold to the equation and prolongs the time I reach the boss.
At the same time requeue also takes time and there is no guarantee that the new party is better.

I have a rule of thumb:
If we wipe twice because of stupid and avoidable errors (like hunter pet on loose, undergeared tank keeps on pulling 3 groups at once, healer is being afk half the time) I leave party. Not counting in wipes where we were just unlucky. Doing this because if I stay, same errors will likely cause new wipes. Using it as a prevention mechanism.

Also leaving if the party if toxic. Toxicity doen't have to be directed towards me, if it is there, I consider leaving. Playing Wow for fun, @ssholes in party chat are not fun. An example: when I was leveling went to HFQ with 2 lvl60s. An early lvl60 warrior and a high tier epic pala. Dungeon was being cleared very fast but pala kept on sh*ittalking the warrior all the time and the warrior had no guts to stand up for himself. It was painful tonwatch so I quit even if we cleared the dungeon at an unbelievable speed.

For PUG raids I am more tolerant but following a similar principle. If we wipe after wipe or if someone becomes a real @ss, I quit.

Making an exception when helping out guildies or friends. In those cases I always stay.
