I admit you have described some solid observations and issues that AV has, actually one of the best observations about AV I have recently seen on this forum. However, the solutions you propose are totally opposite to original philosophy behind AV. Basically you want to fix the issues you brought by also (maybe unconsciously) destroying the uniqueness of Alterac Valley. If you don't know what I'm talking about (which is OK), please read [this archived post](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112050648/http://us.battle.net/wow/en/forum/topic/2952885503) on Blizzard's forum. Let me start.

> **Critterbug wrote: Mon Dec 16, 2024 3:34 pm**
> 1.   Farming for nodes during what should be a PVP map.

I partially agree with this. I'm against total removal of herb/mining nodes though. It's real issue, I have seen some people join AV only to earn some gold by collecting herbs and watching how the rest of the team gives them passive Honor. Even asking GMs to warn them doesn't work because farming herbs is legal.
However, nodes make this zone amazing when you randomly see a herb that is worth 0.5g.
Also, AV is not purely PvP battleground. So I think the best compromise to this issue is reducing number of nodes on the map so farming isn't profitable there. And making the law that states clearly you can't farm herbs/veins all the time while being in AV without PvP (or any other) interaction.

> **Critterbug wrote: Mon Dec 16, 2024 3:34 pm**
> 2.   Remove excess fluff like other quests that don't serve PVP.

Here is starts the problem. I assume you've read that post I sent you above. If so, then you understand that AV was originally meant to be not the usual battleground but rather war-zone where war never ends - in OG vanilla some AVs lasted a few days. Originally AV was to be a battleground with mixed PvP and PvE content where PvE objectives could enhance your team effort in PvP. Your team struggles to get through the bridge into the Dun Baldar? Summon cavalry/Ice Lord/assaults so they carry your team. Let me present you an amazing video I recorded here on Turtle a few months ago and ask yourself: would it be possible to experience such epic moment with your suggestion implemented?

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/2K_RpQmuqxs?start=1]

Basically every quest in AV serves some (sometimes indirect) PvP purpose. These quests are great if you look at them closer. Example: you are level 52 Hunter in Horde who can't do much. What do you do? You tame the wolves and kill the rams. After some time, thanks to your effort, the cavalry is summoned and will help your team progressing the attack. Isn't that great?

The observation we see here is that currently PvE quests (or PvE objectives) are not that important. What I suggest is to make them relevant again. Visit my another mega-thread about AV.

"It was admittedly designed that way.  Ask the original devs. " Source, please? First time I see this to be honest.

By fence you mean the outer ring of palisade of Frostwolf Village? If so, then I agree 100%. It gives unfair advantage to Alliance because they can skip most of the mobs while Horde must enter through the bridge.

Horde's archers are indeed in worse position than Alliance's ones. Main problem is that archers on Horde's towers are located on height while the area they should control is far below them not in their field of view. In comparison Dun Baldar North and South Bunkers are located far away from the bridge and on middle height - that makes them set in excellent position that allows them to control whole way from the bridge to Aid Station. Archers on Frostwolf towers control only short choke-point in that building in front of first gate - they seem to shine only there but nowhere else.
The solution to this problem is quite easy, just relocate the archers so they stay on the edge of Frostwolf Towers. Yes, it's that simple since the issue is their small field of attack.

I don't think making the other bridge is good idea, it would require massive changes to terrain and the village. And Horde has already had a choke-point I mentioned above - sure, it's not as great as the bridge but still if you made second bridge then Horde would be privileged because of 2 choke-points. I think that if we fix palisade jumps and relocate the archers, the balance would be equal, no second choke-point is needed.

Instead of the timer, another fix should be applied that has the same results as adding timer: honor gained via turtle-mode should be buffed:

My second suggestion could be argued whether or not people should earn bonus honor. But it solves the Mark of Honor issue. Yesterday there was 4 hours AV going on and everyone received only 3 or 1 Mark. At the same time they could get 15 Marks of Arathi/Warsong.
