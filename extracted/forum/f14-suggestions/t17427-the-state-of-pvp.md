Just did a few BG's tonight and few things ive noticed:

The queues are still broken after 2 years, so a nice 18v13 AB was fun.

Arcane mages damage is crazy, basically nuking me down as a 8k hp warrior in a few seconds.

Holy Paladins seem to be healing 50% of their HP by using some kinda melee spell instantly... I mean they were already hard enough to kill before, now this.

Every class seems bursty and its crazy.

I seem to do crazy more damage as a warrior than before 1.17.2 , I can even nuke bears down really fast, but trying to kill paladin is nearly impossible with this instant melee heal they seem to do.

Sunnyglade is still never played, and the Arena is 3v3 still, with no promised return of the 2v2, that people actually liked.

The PvP Server is still mostly dead. Probably due to the reasons stated above.

Its obvious that PvP is an afterthought on this server.

CC1 wasn't perfect, but it looks like perfection compared to this hot garbage, If any new people are here and reading this post, and have an intention to PvP here, Ill do you a favour and say avoid this server like the plague if you enjoy PvP.
