# Fix the flight path to Alah'Thelas

## Post 110796 by Jessterr — 2024-12-01T15:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110796#p110796 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Alliance flight path in Northern part of Eastern Kingdoms connecting to Alah'Thelas really needs work. I have Arathi Highlands, Southshore, and I even walked to Hinterlands and Western Plaguelends (at level 36) to get the flight paths. NONE of them connect to AlahThelas. Apparently we need Eastern Plaguelends to connect AT with literally anything.

I want Alah Thelas to be a good hub for questing in the North in general. The flight from Stormwind or Ironforge is very long and a lot of time gets wasted flying because of it. Its possible to just have anyone flying from Southshore, Arathi, Hinterlands, or WPL fly to AT on the existing path. There are plenty of other zones in the map that have flight paths take you over lands you haven't visited yet. I get a preview of Mulgore, Desolace, and Stonetalon mountains when flying from Ratchet to Astranaar; and I haven't been to any on foot.
