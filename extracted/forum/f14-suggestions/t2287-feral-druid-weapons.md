What do you guys think about making a Claw of the Wildshifter and offhand? So it can be used with other one-handed weapons. Would be cool to give druids ability to get normal wepon procs to make different combinations with it, like eskhandar's right claw in main hand with speed procs instead of crowd pummeler or some weapons with damage procs, like sulfuras for example, ironfoe and other stuff. All this seems kinda strange at first, but the problem is that druids are too limited in terms of weapon choice, even with coming changes there are like +3-4 good options main of them being herald of woe and might of menethil in later stages. Would be awesome to have options to choose from and experiment with. And I don't think it would affect the gameplay in any way or make druids too poverful as they are still unable to use axes and swords.
