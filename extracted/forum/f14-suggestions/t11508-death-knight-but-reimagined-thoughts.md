# Death Knight, but reimagined: thoughts?

## Post 76168 by Mamekumaaa — 2023-11-06T15:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76168#p76168 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of my favorite class fantasies in MMOs are magic + melee, a hybrid that casts long-range spells to get aggro (and maybe a debuff, DOT, etc.) and finishes the job with a big-ass sword. Paladins somewhat offer that, but it's mainly seals and buffs.

Enter: Death Knight!

Death Knights in the main game are considered a Heroic class, overpowered by design and (IMO) pretty damn fun, at least for a while. Hell, even in Retail I enjoy the fantasy it presents, even if I'm largely done with that version of WoW. But what if Turtle WoW were to add them into the game, reimaged not as an OP level 55+ only class but as a lowly "undead" regaining their strength. I don't even care of the iconic abilities didn't make it into the game due to balance issues, I just want to infest my enemies with diseases, cast death bolts, and smack them really hard with my sword.

What do you guys think, and has TWoW ever considered adding a new lore-friendly class?

Death knight has some obstacles:

First of all, balance. It needs to be on par with other clases to not break the game.

How to design the class? Hero units in warcraft 3 had many similarities between them (main spell, passive aura, secondary spell/CC, ultimate) but on wow it requires a complete diferent playstyle. Being too original may be a nightmare to balance, stick to existing mechanics too much would make too similar to other classes.

Elements. Which elements should the dk harness?
The few dk's in vanilla are based on physical abilities, shadow or fire. Shadow and fire are already warlock elements.
Arcane? Nature? It would need some proper explanation and possibly some previous examples

Rescource. Mana? Rage? Energy? Focus? Something new? The blizz version use runes because it grabbed ideas from runemaster, runic power shouldn't be on a DK.

Design of class quests, itemisation, available races, lore of the class... There are waaay too much to make it right and it's a titanic task to do. If you have seen other projects doing something similar you'll notice most of them ended with half assed classes or completely broke the game.

Don't get me wrong, i love death knight as a concept, but given his background i see a very dificult class to implement into the world without breaking the inmersion. Maybe do it horde specific and give another class to the alliance?

"Death Knight" is merely a phrase.  Unlike a Paladin, which has ties to real Earth legend and history (Charlemagne's Paladins, for example) and consequently has to at least broadly follow a certain archtype to appear "normal," a death-knight has no real-world counterpart as a basis.  You can implement a character with that name however you want.  Heck, Blizzard itself had two very unique takes on the idea of a Death-Knight over the years:  First, it was a flimsy long-range caster, then later, a heavy primary melee character.

Identify something missing that existing classes or builds can't do, or at least that not enough of them can do, then design your new class to fill that need.

A nice suggestion made by Mativh (he did a whole post on it) would be to expand on the system of class racials. This would allow classes to function more as archetypes which would then be fleshed out by your race choice.

So if you chose warrior then undead would have racials reminiscent of death knight abilities, high elf could get spell breaker, night elf sentinel and so on.
This allows people more class fantasy options without having to build new classes from the bottom up. A lot of the potentially new classes that are often mentioned are quite niche and would only fit one or two races, so being specialisations within existing classes makes more sense

Turtle has already somewhat gone down this road with the new shaman racials, so hopefully it’s something they’ll expand upon
