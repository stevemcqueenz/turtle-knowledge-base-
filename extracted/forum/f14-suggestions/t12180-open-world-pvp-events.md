# Open World PVP Events

## Post 80998 by Khazrakxiv — 2023-12-13T09:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80998#p80998 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Honestly would love to see some weekly world pvp events made to encourage a battle.

make some special thing that when it is about to spawn it announces it to the entire server 6 hours ahead of time and put the spawn during prime time. Ensure the spawn is random throughout the week to ensure fair distribution to everyone's schedule. Locations of the spawn should be a mid point between horde and alliance graveyard to give both sides a chance to get back into the fight.

If you want world pvp you need positive reinforcement to encourage the action rather then just passively adjusting numbers around which will not really hook or engage people that may have trepidation of it.

A great example of this would be tidal charm, instead of a boring camp that spawns every 2 - 2 1/2 days, create an event inviting all online to fight over the opportunity to obtain it. This would make the god awful wait for the trinket more entertaining as it would announce when it is about to spawn and also make it so you guaranteed have to pvp for one of the best pvp trinkets.

This was implemented in project ascension (Crows Cache), and while that game has its own issues it is a decent template to look into. I am a huge fan of devs that iterate on good ideas.
