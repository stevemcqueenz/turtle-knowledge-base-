I'll comment only summoning stones since it's a frequent topic.

I'm against making them summon party members, baseline. However, I'm fine with giving them that capability for players that have 100% cleared set dungeon (killed all bosses). This applies to both being able to use the summoning stone, and being summoned to set summoning stone

My reason is the following:
 1 - Finding the dungeon is part of the experience and Turtle keeps adding new instances

 2 - Yes, there are a lot of veteran players, but also a lot of new players, who have never physically gone to the original instances.

 3 - It will maintain the encouragement of traveling the world, but also reward those who went through the whole ordeal.

 4- For the above to work, it must be character based and not account based.
