This one is a bit of a long shot as it might require balancing depending on how you go about it. But the druids should be able to get a wolf or wolf-like form that grants them a stacking buff based on other druids also in the form in their party.

This comes from idea of the original druids of the fang using the power with the scythe of elune to take on this form to drive back demons (even if they were ultimately unable to control this power). At the very least, I think this would be a fantastic alternative cosmetic form for Cat Form.

Im going to expand on this a bit using Lore as a reason as a potential factor to help *possibly* bring this into being and even perhaps shed a bit of light on what it could be/do.

The Druids of the Scythe (originally called Druids of the Pack) were druids who used the Pack Form during the War of the Satyr. They shapeshifted into powerful wolf like forms that embodied the fury of the Wild God Goldrinn. This form was eventually prohibited by Malfurion because the druids in this form could not control their rage.

To be clear this is NOT purely how worgen came to be. The worgen came to be later when Ralaar Fangfire tried to help control the rage of this form using the artifact the Scythe of Elune, but this instead strengthened the feral nature of the form and transformed them into the curse-spreading form of Worgen. These were initially Night Elves to start, and in the end they were all banished to sleep in the Emerald Dream. They stayed there until Arugal (the mage in Shadowfang Keep) summoned them back into the world to combat the scourge before realizing he didn't have complete control. Other worgen have been released as a result of this such as the Nightbane Worgen in Duskwood.

After all of this in Cataclysm the Night Elves, specifically druids and priests were in lore able to help the Gilneans that fell to the curse regain most of if not all of their mental control over themselves through the aid of a ritual known as the "Ritual of Balance". Further later in the events of Legion, the Scythe of Elune was found in the Karazhan Archives and it had an ability to return feral worgen found around the Broken Isles back into their completely humanoid state, in full control of themselves.

All of this in consideration I would like to present two possibilities. One, the Pack Form is an altered Bear Form spell that druids can complete a quest line for rewarding a Glyph and this Glyph switches out the Bear/Dire Bear Form's defensive and threat bonuses for more offensive power, with a buff granting additional benefits based on others with this form in their party (not raid) to a max of +5 stacks (of whatever this buff would be), and something akin to anger management where they generate rage while in combat. This would let them still use Rage as a resource and other abilities without needing to alter the resource system or change out abilities.
Two, the alternative (and arguably lamer) option is after a quest line, this is simply a cosmetic glyph for their Bear/Dire Bear Forms.

Keep in mind on my last points above that the Worgen are -different-- from the Pack Form. It is explicitly mentioned at many points that the Worgen are a cursed and twisted version of the original form due to the poor usage of the Scythe of Elune. This should be kept in mind so that way we don't have to worry about it trampling on the Worgen Illusion in the shop, and rather then it being a wolf-like form that Shamans use, I think it would be more like a Worg. These models, while they are subtely different are in fact different in both Lore and Model. Worgs are typically stronger, larger, and have a different jaw from wolves.

If this is done, I would LOVE to help on making the Questline for Druids to complete this task.
