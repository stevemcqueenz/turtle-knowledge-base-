# Wartorn Plate Scrap: a Call to Action

## Post 117266 by Vrograg — 2025-01-19T17:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117266#p117266 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Problem:**
Over the last few months a confluence of phenomena have come together to make the Wartorn Plate Scrap on the Nordanaar server unreasonably scarce and expensive.   crying_turtle

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#a/DasDpY8]

 **Identifying factors:**  neutral_turtle
 1. Warriors and Paladins, the two classes that need this type of scrap are very popular, and have a number of new tier sets that remain desirable. This had helped to increase the demand for plate scrap.
 2. Multiple verified observations point to a seemingly low drop chance from the mobs that can drop scrap. Numbers (reliable ones) for the exact percentages are hard to find since the TWoW DB is on extended hiatus with more updated and accurate numbers.
 3. Often with scrap issues like this, a high price on scrap makes trash farming attractive and lucrative. However the mobs that can be farmed more readily do not have a favorable plate scrap drop chance. So there has been little relief from the gold farmers.
 4. A change by the Team to allow tokens to be disenchanted into scrap has likely helped, but it is not enough to keep up with demand. In truth this likely helped the community face an even more dire situation.

 **History**  wary_turtle
The team has intervened in situations where wartorn scrap drop chances had issues before. Just a year ago this same forum had posts like this: [viewtopic.php?t=13248](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13248) .

And at some point in the fall of last year the team did act following months of chain scrap regularly hitting 40 gold a piece. From the Twow discord a comment from team member Moriena says as much.

"Moriena — 9/23/2024 11:05 AM
We simply equalized the drop chances for all scrap types, since Chain Scraps had only half the drop chance of other types and were disgustingly pricey of the Auction House"

Also as previously mentioned, the team made tokens that drop off of bosses like Desecrated Bracers become disenchantable, and then outputting scrap. This is the other great gesture the team has made to help issues with scrap. It might have taken a while to be fully implemented, but even while it works it still has issues. Tokens that would otherwise be left to rot on a boss doesn't happen frequently enough evidently to stop the runaway plate scrap prices.

Through these two measures the Nordanaar community recognizes that the team is willing to work towards limiting scarcity issues, and I think plate scraps deserves the same treatment in 2025, as chain received in 2024.

 **Solutions**  smiling_turtle
Let's be honest, no one wants to have to keep adjusting scrap drop rates. There might be a more elegant solution, that could even offer some future proofing as well. So I'll list a few common-sense solutions that could be used to help alleviate the plate scrap problem.

 1. **Bump cloth drop rates down a little to make space for plate**
Obviously when changing the drops rates on anything, increasing somewhere means reduction elsewhere. The reason cloth is ideal for a reduction is because of the presumed probability of how disenchanting tokens works. All bosses can drop a tier 3 token item, one for Rogue/Warrior (makes leather/plate), one for Paladin/Shaman/Hunter/Druid (makes plate/chain/leather), and one for Mage/Warlock/Priest (all cloth). Of all the disenchanting of tokens that can be done, cloth is the most likely result, all things being equal. Also Cloth scrap has historically been the most available and affordable of and of the the four.

 2. **Get another profession involved?**
So Enchanters can break down tokens into scrap, but what if all of the much maligned crafting professions had a way to "transmute" one scrap into another? There could be a number of ways of implementing it. Blacksmiths could learn a reputation recipe to take chain scrap and make it into plate, Leatherworkers could do much the same to make chain from leather, and Tailors could make leather from cloth. I'm not a designer just a loud mouth, but I think this could be an elegant way of doing things, and could add some value to the perceived unviability of certain max level professions.

 3. **Repeatable quest guy**
A great example of this would be the NPC in Nordanaar (the city not the server) who can take Bright Dream Shards, and make them into small dream shards, or vice versa. A new or repurposed NPC at Light's Hope Chapel with quests that allow players to transition one type of scrap into another. This would help make all scrap valuable, and even alleviate future scrap issues.

Thank you for your time and consideration   turtle_in_love
