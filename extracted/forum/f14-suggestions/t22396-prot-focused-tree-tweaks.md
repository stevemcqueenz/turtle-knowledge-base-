[https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c ... Vy9sSmsuBd](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator/Vy9sSmsuBd)

Focused mostly on prot, but debloats arms/fury pretty well.

Prot has issues, and it has nonsensical effects in the tree.  These are tweaks to all 3 trees, but with the most focus on prot.  There's a threat and damage gap for being a prot tank that no longer makes sense given the buffs to prot paladins and bears.  This aims to address that while also making Arms and Fury flow better.

It also tries to address how much this server seems to despise polearms and polearm accessories.
