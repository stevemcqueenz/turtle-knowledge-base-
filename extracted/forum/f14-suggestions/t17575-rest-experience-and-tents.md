# Rest experience, and tents

## Post 120007 by Pinkmane — 2025-02-15T06:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120007#p120007 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Had this thought after a good night's sleep—if Turtle WoW is still meant to be an RP server, why does resting under a tent give way more XP than chilling in a tavern? Shouldn’t it be the other way around? Like, lower the XP from tents and boost it in different taverns instead?

Just a fun idea: What if XP gain increased the drunker you got?

I just think it’d be way more fun to see players hanging out in taverns, drinking ale, and chatting, instead of everyone huddling under tents.  smiling_turtle_head

## Post 120056 by Moongirl — 2025-02-15T16:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120056#p120056 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You do have a valid point in an RP perspective. Everyone having fun together as a community should give more rested than a tent being put in the middle of nowhere. However, it should be noted that you still have to craft tents, and you are able to just walk in a tavern. Hence, there would be no need for tents anymore, and everyone can get max rested in an easier way.

While how fast you get rested XP under a tent is a bit much, I don't think its right to make the Tavern faster. Of course, there is the new Guild House feature. While expensive, I believe it gives faster rested XP than tents do. This enhances the community aspect, as well as the Tavern, but is unfriendly to smaller guilds.

I personally don't believe tents should disappear, but lowering the speeds would be alright. The tavern rested XP is somewhat implemented with the Guild House feature. Thank you for reading, and good luck on your journey.   smiling_turtle_head
