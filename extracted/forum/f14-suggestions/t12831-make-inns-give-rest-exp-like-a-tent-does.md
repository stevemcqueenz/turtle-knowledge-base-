## Post 85706 by Trismegistos — 2024-01-23T14:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85706#p85706 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I get not wanting this as a passive feature for every inn, as that would certainly make players interact way less, making the world feel less alive. So, what if by default inns gave rested experience like they do currently, but it could be temporarily amplified to be on par with how tents are currently?

Perhaps the duration could be longer than a tent (and it would not disappear if the player who set it up logs off or zones out), while giving rested experience at the same rate. It would make inns more attractive as meeting places, which they should be, and low level players would no longer be incentivized to gather in makeshift huts at the outskirts of cities.

I think the ability to boost rested experience at an inn should be a survival ability, and share a cooldown with tents (similarly to how different alchemical transmutations have a shared tradeskill cooldown). The materials to do it should not be more expensive than setting up a tent, as players will always prefer to use the optimal method. In addition, I think the effect should be made apparent in some way; a custom music that plays when the effect is ongoing, perhaps celebrating NPCs, or decorations that indicate the effect?

Instead of having players announce where a tent has been set up, they could announce that a party is ongoing at this-and-this inn, welcoming weary travelers to join.

In capital cities the effect could only take place inside of inns, rather than in the entire city. You would still gain a slow rested bonus while in a capital, but by entering an inn that has been boosted in this way you would be gaining rested experience much more rapidly. This would also make the taverns and inns of capital cities more attractive for players to visit, even if only to see if there is a party going on.
