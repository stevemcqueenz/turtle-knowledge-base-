# A little help for the kelp

## Post 136102 by Uppercrust — 2025-06-17T21:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136102#p136102 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I suggest adding a rarespawn version of stranglekelp to the game, similiar to how rich thorium veins and gemstone nodes sometimes spawn in place of small thorium veins. The reason being that stranglekelp spawns all over the world, including several high level zones, but is only used for a handful of mid-level alchemy recipes*. It seems a shame for resources in endgame areas to go ignored, so adding a more useful node (A tangled piece of random gear, or maybe a mutation that contains other herbs?) to the spawns might cause them to see more traffic.

Since it technically qualifies as an endgame herb, I also think it would be nice if there was an endgame use for it. It could be a new recipe (maybe even a non-alchemy recipe, for cooking or survival?), or some kind of hand-in quest, similiar to the purple lotus hand-in in Hyjal. The latter would also be a boon for all the non-alchemist fishermen, who tend to end up with a bunch of stranglekelp in their bags.

 *It does get used for two recipes that are useful at all levels, free action potions and elixirs of water breathing. However, both of those recipes use blackmouth oil, and any fisherman can tell you that the process of gathering blackmouth groupers will always net you a more than matching amount of stranglekelp.
