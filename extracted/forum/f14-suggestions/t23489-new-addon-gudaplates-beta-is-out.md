# New Addon: GudaPlates Beta is Out!

## Post 164347 by Vaitchild — 2026-01-03T17:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164347#p164347 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Tired of messy nameplates in WoW Classic? GudaPlates Beta is here to clean up your screen!

This new addon gives you full control over enemy health bars. Customize their look, track threat (aggro) with clear colors, see your debuff timers, and highlight your target.

![🌟](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f31f.svg) The Most Important Part:
It fixes the annoying "jumping" nameplates! By default, Overlap Mode is ON. This means nameplates can sit on top of each other and stop jumping around when enemies are stacked. Finally, stable nameplates you can actually click!

Quick Install:

Download from: <https://github.com/vatichild/guda-plates>

Extract to Interface\AddOns

In-game, use /gp role tank/dps to set up threat colors.

(For full features like enemy cast bars, also install SuperWoW.)
