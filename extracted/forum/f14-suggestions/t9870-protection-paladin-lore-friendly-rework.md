# Protection Paladin Lore Friendly Rework

## Post 64556 by Partyboyza — 2023-09-15T20:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64556#p64556 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone, I main a protection paladin (Belriose) and have some past experience with playing this build.
In my opinion, Blizzard designed the protection paladin to be quite a versatile tank, a jack of all trade but master of none.
And keeping that in mind I did find some places where the class is certainly lacking and could use some more tools at his disposal and I will offer my suggestions and to try to be as lore friendly as possible.
I know that currently retribution paladin is quite broken in PVP and I will try to avoid suggesting anything that could be cause even more harm to retribution.
Please remember these are just some ideas, that can be expanded.

The main areas I want to focus are:
 1. Lack of mana regen
 2. Lack of lore friendly utility
 3. Lack of libram diversity
 4. Improvements that will expand dungeon farming (besides DME)
 5. Improvements in gear (dedicated lore friendly pre-bis crafted items)
 6. Improvements in spells/talents

 1. Lack of mana regen
Currently the biggest source of mana regen for a prot paladin is by equipping items with +x dmg that proc judgement of wisdom and from shield blocks.
I don't fully agree with this idea since it is way overpowered and the current roster of items that offer +x dmg do not have any tanking stats.
We all know how spiritual attunement works in TBC and I think we can work something around that concept.
I suggest an aura, libram, passive that returns mana to the paladin and to the party equal to 5% for the paladin and 2% for the party from damage **mitigated** by the paladin from hits and spells.
That will increase the need to have protection paladin tanking adds and bosses in raids and dungeon since it will benefit all mana users.
The main focus is to have the paladin taking hits to the face.

 2. Lack of lore friendly utility
Lorewise, the protection paladin is considered a defender of the people, that's why I would suggest an aura, that redirects 5-10% of the dmg taken by the party to be directed to the paladin. This should be a secondary focus of the paladin and party saver, just like the warrior can use shield wall or last stand for himself, the paladin sacrifices himself constantly to save the party. (can be named Aura of Sacrifice)
And yes it synergies with my first point and offer much needed utility (party mana regen) worthy of a paladin.

 3. Lack of libram diversity
There are currently very few librams to chose from, here is my list of what I would implement, for it to not be overpowered only one (maybe two) point should be chosen for the libram:

Libram of consecration:
a.increases consecration dmg
b. slows enemies atack movement cast speed by 20%
c. reduces dmg taken by 5-10% to be on par with the warrior defensive stance, this will also reduce dmg to anyone who stays in the consacration, ( a great party utility)

Libram of sanctuary :
a. doubles the effect of blessing of sanctuary and in addition reduces dmg taken y 3% (an item that will certainly secure a raid spot for a protection paladin)

Libram of Righteousness:
a. while seal of righteousness is active increases threat generated by holy dmg and attacks 2 more targets with half dmg, judging the seal will taunt 3 targets (a decent libram for tanking dungeons and aoe tanking adds)
b. while seal of righteousness is active increase threat generated by holy dmg attacks and also reduces dmg taken by 5-10% (again a good synergy with the mana regen and party dmg redirection aura)

Libram of Holy Shield (2 points can be chosen here):
a. Adds 4 more charges to holy shield
b. Blocking with holy shield will return 5% of mana and 3% to the party 30 yd range.
c. Blocking with holy shield will return 3% of hp and 1% to the party 30 yd range.
d. blocking with holy shield casts lvl 1 holy nova (scale with spellpower)
e. reduces cooldown of holy shield by 2 seconds
f. Increases block value by %10 of stamina.

Libram of Righteous Fury (2 could be picked on the same libram):
a. Reduce damage taken from all sources by 6% (more mitigation for mana regen)
b. Reduces party members threat by 1% and give it to the paladin
c. heals party members by 1% of dmg mitigated (again good synergy to give help the party regen mana and hp)

Libram of Protection:
 1. your divine shield only reduces dmg by 50% but your attacks are also reduced by 50%
 2. can cast divine shield on other players and they also get forbearance

 4. Improvements that will expand dungeon farming (besides DME)
A signature of the protection paladin is solo aoe farming dungeons for gold and since the mobs on turtle wow are a bit overturned compared to vanilla some tools will be needed to increase the level of dungeon farming to SM.
Let's be honest, protection paladin can run solo dungeon because of blessing of sanctuary.
I would suggest a list of crafted shields for levels 20, 40  and 60, that focus on just that.

i.e.:
lvl 60 shield (Silver Hand initiate Shield)
 2100 armor
 40 block
 5 intelligence
 10 stamina
reduces dmg taken from all sources by 20

Or maybe world drop trinkets that reduce dmg taken from all sources by 5 (lvl20), 10 (lvl40), 15 (lvl60)
These items can also be used by druids and warriors to start dungeon farming.

This will be a very flashy item that that can increase threat from holy dmg and from healing party members and reduce damage taken due to the debuff however it will work against the previously mentioned mana regen from dmg mitigation since the attacking mobs will hit slower)

 6. Improvements in spells/talents
I'm sorry but I simply can't agree with a final talent in protection to be a passive while most other builds have active abilities.

A new seal would be ideal:

Seal of the Sanctified: Hit your target and up to 2 more in front of you, adds holy damage on hits equal to 2% of hp divided by 3 (targets), all dmg taken is reduced by 3% for 30 seconds
Judging this seal will apply a debuff, anyone who attacks the target with this debuff, will receive a buff that reduces dmg taken by 3% (adds 3% dmg reduction to warrior/druid MT and the entire raid)
(perfect for dungeons and raids, it offers mitigation and holy dmg 3 target split or single attack)
i.e.: 6000hp *2/100 (for 3 targets) * attack speed coefficient = 40 holy dmg * attack speed coefficient
i.e.: 6000hp *2/100 (for 1 targets) * attack speed coefficient= 120 holy dmg * attack speed coefficient
(weapon attacks speed should also be considered, faster weapons do less dmg, I haven't added any coefficient in the equation)

or weapon ability:

Hammer of the Sanctified: Hit your target and up to 2 more in front of you with holy dmg equal to 4% of hp divided by 3 (targets) and gain a 6% dmg reduction buff for 8 seconds (6 second cooldown)
(perfect for dungeons and raids, it offers mitigation and holy dmg 3 target attack)
i.e.: 6000hp *4/100 /3 (for 3 targets) = 120 holy dmg (needs more balance)
i.e.: 6000hp *4/100 /1 (for 1 target) = 240 holy dmg (needs more balance)

or Holy Shield rework:

Holy Shield Rework: while active reduces dmg taken by 6% and returns 50% that dmg + 50% of block value as holy dmg to the target, 8 second duration or 4 charges (8 second cooldown) should get to 8 charges somehow
i.e.: 300 dmg * 3/100 (dmg reduction) + 50/100 * 140 block value = 73 holy dmg returned (for for each 300dmg from elites)
i.e.: 2000 dmg * 3/100 (dmg reduction) + 50/100 * 140 block value = 135 holy dmg returned (for each 2000 dmg from boss)

and an emergency party save

Shield of the Clemency: Add a 30 sec temporary shield equal to 10% of the paladins hp to all party members (5 min CD)
(perfect utility to save party in dire situations)

and last but not least

 1 button taunt for 3 seconds ( not a spell so it can't be affected by silence)

First of all, thank you for reading this far, these are just some of my ideas, please take into consideration only the effects and not the coefficients as those need to be tested and balanced)
Obviously adding all of these changes would give the protection paladin way more skills compared to other classes, but some of them are urgently required to make the build more fun and lore friendly.

The protection paladin is the reason I still play wow.

Please give me you opinions and build upon my ideas.
