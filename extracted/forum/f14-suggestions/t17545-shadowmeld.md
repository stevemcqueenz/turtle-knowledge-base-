Patch 3.0.2 of World of Warcraft was released on October 14, 2008, and it was a preparatory patch for the Wrath of the Lich King expansion. In this patch, one of the most notable changes was the improvement to the Night Elves' racial ability, Shadowmeld, allowing them to enter stealth (invisibility) even while in combat, which was not possible before.

This change was significant for the Night Elves, as it enabled them to use Shadowmeld as a much more effective tool to escape combat or avoid damage, similar to how rogues use their Vanish ability.

Therefore, since patch 3.0.2 (a long time ago in the Wrath of the Lich King expansion), Night Elves have been able to use Shadowmeld in combat, not just outside of it.
