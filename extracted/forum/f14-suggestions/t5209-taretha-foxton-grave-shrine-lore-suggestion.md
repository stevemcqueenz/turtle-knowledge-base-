# Taretha Foxton Grave/Shrine (Lore Suggestion)

## Post 31656 by Falerian — 2022-12-27T17:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31656#p31656 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi!

This isn't a gameplay suggestion, but I've been re-reading through the Lord of the Clans novel which goes through Thrall's backstory, and it's always bothered me that the character of [Taretha Foxton](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Taretha_Foxton) never gets referenced in WoW beyond a brief cameo in the TBC Caverns of Time dungeon - despite her minor role, I don't think it's an exaggeration to say that the Horde would not exist if she hadn't shown kindness to Thrall.

I always found it strange that Thrall wouldn't have commissioned a grave or shrine to her in Orgrimmar or at least outside somewhere in Hillsbrad Foothills

For those who might not have read this novel, the basic summary is that Taretha is the reason Thrall was able to escape his captivity in Durnholde, becoming something similar to his sister and demonstrating that not all humans are as evil and twisted as his captor Blackmoore was, which would lead to him freeing the orcs from the internment camps, becoming leader of the Horde, and sailing west to Kalimdor...and the rest is history as they say.

I think it would be nice if there was a small in-game shrine or memorial built in her memory somewhere, to give a character that was so important to the formation of the new Horde (and if you want to go down the butterfly effect route, saving the world from Archiemonde) some form of recognition, especially considering Blizzard sadly seemed to have forgotten about her. This could perhaps even be extended to a quest given as a personal favour from Thrall to attend to the shrine, but just a small interactable object itself would make me happy.
