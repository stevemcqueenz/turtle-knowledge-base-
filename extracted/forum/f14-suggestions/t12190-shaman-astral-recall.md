I think it would be cool to allow Shamans to set Astral Recall to a location different than their Hearthstone.
However I would want the limitation applied at you can only change it once every 7 days (or whatever the team thinks is fair). This would be symbolic of the Shaman communing with that area and attuning themselves to it.

Amplifies the flavor of the ability a little bit and gives it a touch more utility.
