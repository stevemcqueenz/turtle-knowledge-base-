# Make warlock spells not use soul shards while in Blood Ring

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27057#p27057 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Title basically. Playing blood ring as warlock is a huge pain because you constantly have to farm for shards between queueing (it takes time and requires us to stay in level appropriate areas to farm more as opposed to just queueing from a capital), as you can easily use 1 to 3-4 shards per match (1 for healthstone, 2 if you're giving one to your partner, 1 to summon demon, 1 for shadowburn). Using drain soul in BR is basically suicide, so recovering shards from kills is not an option. Making spells not cost soul shards in blood ring would make this issue a lot better.
