## Post 15761 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-01-14T00:07:00Z

In the spirit of the upcoming class changes, I wanted to share what I think about the Hunter and how it can be made more versatile and interesting.

 1. 7th and/or 8th level of pet loyalty. I can see this as adding 5% damage bonus to each of those levels making pets a bit stronger. It won't affect balance, because the focus of the server is PvE.

 2. The focus on Survival and Melee hunter with Aspect of the Wolf leads me to believe that more than one new melee active ability should be introduced. I really don't see how a Melee-only Survival Hunter will ever be as viable as a Marksmanship Hunter, so it will at least make it more fun for folks who want to play a pure Melee Hunter and only equip  ranged weapon for the extra stats, without carrying projectiles, due to no intention to ever rely on ranged combat.
