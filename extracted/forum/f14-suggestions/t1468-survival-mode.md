# Survival Mode

## Post 8762 by Himyname — 2020-12-23T03:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8762#p8762 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You got to eat or drink when the meter drops. Around levels 40 and 60 there are equippable items that can reduce the loss of hunger/thirst. Food and drink will replenish them. Certain crafted foods will slow down the effect of hunger/thirst.

Temperature is also something you have to regulate using certain gear. Like in cold regions like Dun Morogh, Winterspring, Silverpine Forest wearing some leather is important to stop or slow down loss of body temperature. In warm areas like Burning Steppes, STV or Searing Gorge you need to keep your body cool and drink a lot of fluids. Certain foods and drinks also help regulate temperature.

You can also get weary and have a meter for that as well. Once that drops you will get tired and suffer from certain conditions. This can be replenished through tents, chairs you can find to sit in, talking to an innkeeper for a price or laying on a bed for a few seconds (like eating).

Neglecting your hunger, thirst, weariness and temperature can result in bad conditions and death eventually. You can pay an innkeeper to replenish your hunger, thirst, energy and temperature for a price.
