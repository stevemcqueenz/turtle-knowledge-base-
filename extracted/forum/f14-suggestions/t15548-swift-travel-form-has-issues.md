# Swift Travel Form has issues

## Post 106250 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-04T01:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106250#p106250 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all, thanks to the team for including this in the game, I've always thought druids on mounts rather than a form for 100% seemed strange, however.... There are a few issues with the swift form I will list now.

 1: A normal mount can go through water without dismounting.
The Swift Travel Form gets removed as soon as you enter water, making it less convenient, as you have to cast it again for 3 seconds.. Which brings me to my next point.

 2: It costs mana to use.
This is another disadvantage to using this over a normal mount, the mana cost isnt much but, it interrupts your 5 second regen rule which makes it kinda annoying to use.

 3: You can get the same skin as the Swift Travel Form as a glyph.... This makes it just seem less Epic and unique that the normal mount can be made to look exact same as this form. Maybe make it different from the glyph version.
Also the model is bugged on the character screen. It's off to one side and you can't see it.

 4: The icon looks the same as normal Travel Form. Not a big deal but I'd prefered a different icon.

 5: When you get hit it removes the form straight away. It seems like the form is easier to 'dismount' you than a normal mount.

All in all I love we got this but, it just seems so much worse than just using a normal mount lol, which is a shame considering how long the quest was and expensive it is.
