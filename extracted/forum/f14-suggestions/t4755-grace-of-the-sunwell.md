# Grace of the Sunwell

## Post 28472 by Apimius (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-04T20:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28472#p28472 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

please lower the mana cost from the high elf priest racial Grace of the Sunwell: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=46042>.

Currently this spell costs 21 % of base mana which is a whopping 288 mana baseline at lvl 60 and 259 mana with 5/5 mental agility. Meanwhile troll racial berserking costs only 7 % of base mana which comes down to 96 mana at lvl 60 and can increase your attacking/casting speed by up to 30 % instead of only 10 %. Grace of the Sunwell can be used on other people, but also has a more than twice as long cooldown as berserking. This should even it out to put both of these abilitys at the same mana cost. Smite priests struggle with mana, this ability should not cost almost as much as a max rank smite (300 mana).
