# Allow Insect Swarm cast in Moonkin form

## Post 12729 by Camanchaca — 2021-09-28T19:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12729#p12729 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Would it be possible to allow Insect Swarm cast in Moonkin form?

In my opinion this would be a possitive change for game mechanics and lore-wise. From the mechanics point of view, it should not break balance (pun intended) since Balance druid is already among the lowest DPS specs. It would also add the benefit of providing a new spell to use alongside the other 3 attacks (moonfire, starfire, wrath). Hence it would improve moonkin possibilities a little bit (and would still not make the class OP or comparable with pure DPS in any way).

From a lore perspective, it would make sense for Moonkins to call upon the small creatures of the forests for aid in its battles. Actually, Moonkin already do call for nature's aid using their wrath spell. This would not break anything lorewise (this is why blizzard moved it to the balance tree for TBC onwards without any needed explanation). It does not make sense to call upon offensive insects as a restoration move. Finally, this change would remove the contradiction of choosing the Moonkin to improve spellcasting while giving up on an offensive spell (Insect Swarm).

I understand that modifying the talents may be complicated from a balancing perspective, but would it be possible to just unlock the spell in moonkin form without changing anything else? I believe casting Insect Swarm while on moonkin form in vanilla is a dream for many fellow moonkins, and I'm sure they would appreciate that. What do you think?

Thank you for reading
