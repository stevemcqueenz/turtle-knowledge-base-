# Crashing during raid boss fights

## Post 170126 by Lane13 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-05T13:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170126#p170126 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

If you crash or disconnect during a raid boss fight and try to log back in, you will be kicked out of the instance as long as combat is still ongoing.

While I understand that this may be to avoid exploits of some kind, it is extremely punishing, especially to those like pugs who are not on voice chat to communicate to others that they have crashed and to be informed of when combat ends so they're still eligible for loot.

Please, let us log back in within the raid even if a boss fight is ongoing!

## Post 172251 by Lane13 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-24T21:14:00Z

Today this happened to me at the start of Hakkar and I was the ML, meaning that I could not see the loot at all and had to ask someone else to distribute it.

I was also online on an alt in case this happened to communicate it to the raid. I noticed that after crashing, I went immediately offline on my main, meaning that there's not even a grace period to come back after the boss is killed to be eligible for loot.

Please, let us remain within the raid after going offline if we were there in the first place, even during combat!
