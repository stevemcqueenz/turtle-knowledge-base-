## Post 163346 by Reinz1 — 2025-12-20T11:58:00Z

Queue times are absolutely terrible here.  There are so many people leveling characters yet it takes 2 hours+ to find a group for an instance.  I understand that if you level tank or healer queue times are reduced

My suggestion is to put a restriction in place so you can only enter dungeons with players that are within 10 levels of you.

Why?
Stops players from multi logging and boosting their alts through dungeons to farm gear and in turns forces players to find groups for dungeons.    I think this may be a big reason why grouping for dungeons is so terrible, as I completely understand not wanting to queue for 2 hours to find a group for a dungeon and that boosting your character ends up being the best way to avoid wasting time, but boosting your alts is no fun and goes against the community aspect of turtlewow and has a negative impact on finding groups.

I appreciate that this may not solve the dungeon grouping problem but I think it would go along way to helping.

No.

First of all, lv 60 players have the right to overpower low dungeons if they want to. That is how WoW was originally and intentionally designed. The level system, with this inclination, allows player to experience and benefit from their characters' progress. This the premise I base the following statement on.

If instances are only available for players within 10 levels, mechanism would feel more inconveniently clumsy. Players at lv 60 would find it too difficult to four team members at close levels for a lv 45-suitable dundeon/instance, such as Zul'Farak for quests, socialising or looting. In other words, People should feel it naturally correct to team with high level players. We, at a wide range of levels, are equal as low level dungeon "users". After all, encouraging (not forcing) players to complete dungeon quests is always healthy to the game's community.

Another thing, your proposed limitation on level gap for instances does not solve any problem when you are trying to find a team as a non-tanking/healing member (and it even makes more problems as mentioned). Solution lies in improvement to the team finder function. For example, boosting dungeon quests' experience yield and experience from instances when players' levels are close and having a challenge feature like Boaring Experience limiting experience yield. And to go further but closer to your suggestion, the game may have a more penalising mechanism for experience and currency collection when low level players are using "boosting service". But, they are still allowed to go together.
