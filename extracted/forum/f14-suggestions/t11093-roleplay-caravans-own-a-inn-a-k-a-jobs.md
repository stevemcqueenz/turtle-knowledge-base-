# (ROLEPLAY)-Caravans, Own a Inn - A.K.A Jobs

## Post 72883 by Federichech — 2023-10-22T22:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=72883#p72883 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Guys i was thinking about how cool would it be to have a custom script or smth, implemented by the devs to be able to have "jobs"  in the main hubs of the game e.g:
"Congratulations! You are now the owner of the Goldshire tavern!" You can:
 - Rename all the consumables (the original name remains but there will be the nickname near it eg. Bread - Federichech's speciality or smth like that) ;
 -Rent rooms for customers or new adventurers for a small fee and they get a well rested buff and a temporary storage and maybe transmog station;
 -Hire waiters to welcome ppl and serve food and  and hire a chef that gets the ingredients by the "hunters guilds" or "gatherers guild" which he can deposit in the food storage downstairs which is a tavern employees exclusive sort of "Bank" and paying them guilds a weekly fee for the services ( in this way guilds or "companies" that take care of others business's needs can get paid for their job and have a steady income and it would help in creating a more lively and believable enviroment that would incetivize people to be part of the rp world and maybe create a new economy dimension).

Another idea is to be able to buy or rent a custom caravan! How could this work?
Well the carovan would be using the default asset look but you can give the it a custom name, and it will function like a portable storage and it's deployable from the inventory, like how you deploy the "Tents"(in this way an ambitious merchant can sell his precious loot to a lucky/unlucky adventurer and perhaps run a business in the most profitable spots in Azeroth and players can click on this caravan and browse its inventory like you would do with the NPCs.

These are just 2 ideas i came up with,im not expert in scripting ecc so i dont know if this requires a lot of effort, but it would be really  cool if they could! Let me know your thoughts about it and if you think it's an interesting idea, peace!
