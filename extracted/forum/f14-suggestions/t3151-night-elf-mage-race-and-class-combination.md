I think you may be misunderstanding the game abstraction for hard and fast adherence to the lore. Star Shard, Moonfire, and Starfire are "arcane" element for game mechanics, but in lore they're not from arcana. They're the will of Elune, some alternate form of divine power that isn't The Light. As for Arcane Shot, no idea what the devs were going with on that. I wouldn't use the existence of these spells alone to suggest that they fit the lore. Lore =/= game mechanics.

Having said that, I'm pretty sure this suggestion has been shot down multiple times before as contradicting the lore of the game. There's even an entire quest chain related to the High Elves on Turtle built around the Night Elves' condemnation of the arcane, so at this point it wouldn't be able to be resolved with lore that exists within the game. I think you should probably assume that the team is not going to pursue this.

What you're talking about, Petrafons, are the quel'dorei – or the "Highborne".
