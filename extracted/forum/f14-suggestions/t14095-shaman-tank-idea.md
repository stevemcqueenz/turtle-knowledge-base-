# Shaman tank idea

## Post 96045 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-05-30T19:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96045#p96045 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I've recently made an article asking you if shaman tanking should be a thing:
[viewtopic.php?f=4&t=14087](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=4&t=14087)

In this article **I'm not proposing anything.** Not saying that it should or shouldn't happen but after a lot of time of playing different shaman builds I want to write about what I think is missing and how I'd change things to make it a viable option.

 **First of all:** in its current, suboptimal form, what are the characteristics of a shaman tank?

 * Pulls via spell
 * Relies on totems
 * Relies on shield (added block chance, added armor for shields)
 * Has a low CD single-target 'taunt'
 * Relies heavily on agility and dodge
 * Loves fast weapons above all else

 **What are their current strengths?**
 * Can buff the party with the totems
 * Can range-pull and can have an okay initial threat on up to 3 mobs
 * Decent dodge

 **What are their current weaknesses?**
 * Oom machine, drinks after every pull
 * Barely has AoE threat, 3+ mobs are very hardly manageable
 * Threat is relatively low, DPS has to hold back not to pull
 * 'Taunt' can both miss and be resisted, shaman tank is in a very hard spot against high nat rez mobs
 * Wears mail, dies fast
 * No defensive CD
 * Needs both hit and spell hit (rockbiter + earth shock)

I wanna take these weaknesses and shake things up a bit. My goal is to make them viable, give them a specific role while not taking out more slots from the enh talent tree than they do now. Again, I'm not in favor of shaman tanks, just writing about how it would be possible in my opinion.

 **What to change?**

 **1. 'Oom machine, drinks after every pull'**
TWOW has introduced water shield. It is perfect for this situation if we make it possible for a shaman tank to keep threat without lightning shield. Solution would be to make it realistical for shaman tanks to hold threat using water shield. In other words, better threat generation but more about it later.

 **2. 'Barely has AoE threat, 3+ mobs are very hardly manageable'**
There are two ways to solve this. I'd include both. First, shaman has aoe fire totems. If there is a talent which links the threat generated by those totems to the shaman (and possibly adds extra threat to them), shamans would have a way to keep aggro.
Elemental tree has a talent called 'improved fire totems', which is mostly useless for them because they don't wanna fight at melee range. But we can even keep the talent in the elemental tree. We just need to change it a bit: the talent currently decreases magma totem threat, it should increase it by the same amount + the talent should link these totems' threat to the shaman.
Other than that we have stoneclaw totem which literally has one job, to taunt. If we increase the threat it gives, it could work as a very good AoE tool. Would link it's threat to the shaman as well and would auto-detroy it after some seconds. It won't taunt away minions to die instantly like now (if it taunts at all). It would help you keep threat.
With these 2 changes, shaman tanks would have a basic way to keep aggro (fire totems) and have an emergency button if mobs start to go rampant (earthclaw totem).

 **3. 'Threat is relatively low, DPS has to hold back not to pull'**
It's easy to fix. 'Elemental weapons' talent buffs the shamans weapon enhancements. We could declare rockbiter to be the 'tanking enhancement' and buff it accordingly. Talent currently gives the rockbiter +20% attack power. Instead of that rockbiter could give added threat which scales with your agility (to make your threat generation better as you gear up).

 **4. ''Taunt' can both miss and be resisted, shaman tank is in a very hard spot against high nat rez mobs' + 'Needs both hit and spell hit (rockbiter + earth shock)'**
This is a tricky one. You could say that palas are in the same spot but in reality they mostly rely on spellpower. A prot pala is basically a shockadin with added armor, they feel the need to get both of these at the same time much less.
First I'd move 'Nature's Guidance' from the resto tree to the enh tree. It has to be moved. One of the most important enh abilities and enh shamans are currently forced to waste 5 talent points on cheaper healing if they want it. Putting it into the enhancement tree gives them +hit without forcing them to waste 5 talent points.
The other thing I'd add here is a new level of earth shock. Same mana cost, half the damage but taunts. Would slightly change 'Stormstrike', too. Instead of debuff it gives you the nat damage buff and makes sure that your next nature spell cannot be resisted. The shock still can miss though as a spell but from now on one high nat rez mob doesn't hardcounter the tank.
In short: making the +3% hit/spell hit more accessible, giving them a lower damage earth shock which taunts and 'Stormstrike' preventing the shock being resisted (but can still miss).

 **5. 'Wears mail, dies fast' + 'No defensive CD'**
Shamans should never wear plate. If we want to make them bulky, it has to be done via the shield. Would buff 'Spirit shield' so that a tank shaman's armor approaches the prot pala (but stays below it; shamans have + dodge anyway).
For defensive CD we have 'Thunderhead' which, in its current form is useless. Even if you put a shield on the current tank to help with threat, the shield's threat goes to you. Rework it and put it down, next to Bloodlust.
New 'Thunderhead' could become a defensive CD, increasing your shield's armor and converting some of the damage you receive into mana.

 **How would this shaman tank look like?**
 * Better mana management because they can finally use and abuse water shield + new 'Thunderhead' helps in
 * Better mitigation (slightly below pala) with better shield talent
 * Can keep aoe threat and has an emergency button for aoe
 * Has a valid taunt. You can spam it every 5 sec if rez is not a problem. Otherwise you can spam it every 12 sec after a stormstrike to make sure it doesn't get resisted. A 2-way combo for a reliable taunt.
 * Lower damage but better threat rockbiter which actually scales with gear.

 **All the changes needed to do this:**
 * Slightly change 'Improved fire totems' talent
 * Slightly change 'Elemental weapons' talent
 * Buff 'Spirit shield' talent
 * Change how Stoneclaw totem works
 * Rework 'Thunderhead'
 * Slight change to 'Stormstrike'
 * Add a new level of 'Earth shock'
 * Move 'Nature's guidance' into the enhancement tree

 **How this shaman would work?**
He puts down agi+stoneskin/strength totem+mana/life totem and range pulls. Mobs approach, fire nova totem is being put down. Rockbiter tab/autohit starts as usual. The water shield which is always recast makes sure that mana regen is acceptable. If a mob gets loose, stormstrike him and earth shock. If many gets loose, stoneclaw totem. At damage spikes using reworked thunderhead.

Would be an agility-based tank with high dodge but lower armor. Prioritizes good shields. Great for team utility with the totems. Probably not strong enough to tank endgame raids but I can easily see an 'almot prot pala with dodge' tank until BWL. Acceptable aoe threat, good single target threat with low CD shocks.
Best races would be tauren and troll (tauren with added HP and an aoe stun, troll with the added threat of berserking). One more change, would allow spirit link to be cast on yourself, so a tauren tank can distribute some of his damage between him and the melee DPS.

 **Added options:**
These two changes are not essential but are very nice to have. I didn't mention them above to keep things simple but noting them here for the full flavor if bigger changes are okay.

 1. Improved Lightning Shield -> Improved Shields
Instead of 15% lightning shield damage, could give 15% elemental shield effectiveness. Boosts up the mana you can get back as a tank.

 2. Ancestral Knowledge -> from +5% mana to +10% mana.
Makes sure the mana pool is big enough. Can also help other builds (famous shaman mana issues).

Here is a suggestion for the rework of thunderhead that takes care of AoE threat, Defensive CD and mana problems all in one, while staying true to the class design:

Empower elemental shield:
 -For the next 15 seconds your elemental shields don't consume charges and can proc twice as often. Also you gain an additional buff, depending on the shield you have active when you cast this spell:

Lightning shield damages all nearby enemies with nature damage that generates high amounts of threat (similar to WC3 lightning shield)

Water shield grants you the ability to steal mana from the target with each melee attack and spell (similar to the passive effect of Black Grasp of the Destroyer, but more powerful)

Earth shield grants you 20% damage mitigation and increased block chance and value for the duration. (Or just vastly increased armor?)

With a 3 min CD it should give shammies an interesting tool for different situations and truly make shields fun to use.
