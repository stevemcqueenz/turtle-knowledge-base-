# "Portable Summon Generator"

## Post 9640 by Lawmonark — 2021-03-04T07:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9640#p9640 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are a couple dungeons that are hard to get to with cross faction. RFC and Stockades come to mind.

It would be cool if we had a "Portable Summon Generator" that used some form of Reagent to summon other players.
We already have the wormhole generator, which is normally  mage ability so why not allow others to also Summon.
I feel an item like this would increase the amount of dungeons being ran. More people would join groups if there was a summon.
