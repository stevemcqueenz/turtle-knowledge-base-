Hello there

The goblin starting zone is very harsh in many ways, while the HE zone is more in line with the original starting zones. We have invisible, hostile nightelves that cluster together in groups of 3, forcing players to be very careful. We have elementals, mechanics and sprouts that are all immune to hunter spell Serpent Sting, clustered together shredders that attack you when you want to just kill that freaking spirit in the crypt, getting gun rewards while not being able to use guns and - this is my gripe - the drop rate of oil-scorched note! It seems to be 30% on Turtle Database <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80114>. I had to kill +30 elementals before it dropped. Seriously, this is a starting zone quest, not some lvl 15 quest. I think the droprate should be increased to at least 50% as the natural progression of the area depends on it (you can continue but there are no quests for you then).

Thank you, I hope you will consider this change to the new, and amazingly beautiful and well-crafted goblin starting zone.
