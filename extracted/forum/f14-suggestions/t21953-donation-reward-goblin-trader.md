# Donation Reward: Goblin Trader

## Post 152112 by Nasdax — 2025-09-17T17:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152112#p152112 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Context:
So I really enjoy crafting and selling that stuff on the auction house. For a profit, obviously. However the account wide limit of 120 active auctions is rather annoying as I am selling a lot of different things and it is not always feasible to sell stacks of 20 (if the item supports that) when people usually only buy a single or few item of that kind. I suppose the limit exists to manage the size of auction related processing queues, which is a valid point and avoids that for example someone sells 50 stacks of Runecloth as single items. However, I would assume that when someone pays money to increase that limit, they know what they are doing and use the increased limit responsibly. For example it is totally fine to post 50 times single Elixir of Giants instead of 5 stacks of 10 as buying volume is high and most people just top up their stack with what was used in the last raid which is like 3 items.

Hence the following idea for a new donation reward:
"Goblin Trader" - Increases account wide active auction limit to 1000.
