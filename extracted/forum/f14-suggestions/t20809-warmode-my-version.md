# Warmode, my version

## Post 144451 by Insabler — 2025-08-11T09:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144451#p144451 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At level 10, you receive a quest from your class trainer called “A Call to Arms”, which sends you to the battlemaster to activate the challenge.

Features:

You are always flagged for PvP and can only disable it once before reaching level 60. Once disabled, you cannot enable it again until you are level 60.

Players who accept the challenge at levels 20, 30, 40, 50, or 60 will receive rewards similar to other challenges (fashion coins, pets, etc.).

If you level from 10 to 60 entirely with Warmode enabled, you earn the title “Warbringer” and a special tabard.

Bonuses (Levels 10–60):

You can earn XP by killing other players who grant honor (3–5% of a level).

You cannot gain XP from players you have killed recently.

Once per week, you can choose a PvP reputation faction to earn extra reputation with by doing world quests.

Double honor from killing players in the open world.

Bonuses and Functionality at Level 60:

You can enable/disable Warmode at any battlemaster at will.

While in the open world, you have a 10% chance to gather extra resources from herbs, mining, or skinning.

Same PvP reputation bonus from quests as in 10–60, plus additional reputation gains from battlegrounds with the faction you’ve chosen.

Double honor from killing players in the open world.

Debuffs:

If you attack first and kill a player who does not grant honor, you receive the “Honorless” debuff. This lasts for a base of 20 minutes, plus 1 additional minute for each level difference between you and the killed player.

While affected by “Honorless”:

You do not receive Warmode benefits until the debuff expires.

You take 10% more damage and deal 10% less damage.

You are visible to everyone, even while stealthed.

If Warmode is not enabled, you cannot toggle PvP off while the debuff is active.

I believe these changes would make Warmode a true world PvP challenge, offering extra XP and other incentives without greatly impacting PvE players. You have to fight for your XP, your extra resource chances, and deal with the risk of being killed — all while having a system to discourage griefers and those who repeatedly farm the same player.

To level from 10 to 60 purely through PvP kills (at 5% XP per honor-granting kill), you would need to kill around 1,000 players in the open world.

Why no bonus reputation in battlegrounds?
Warmode is designed to bring world PvP to life, not battlegrounds. Battleground leveling is difficult and will be full of twinks, so the system should focus on open-world PvP instead.

If you have any suggestions for improving this system, let me know!
