Just some mechanic that makes the game more engaging. I can mention some examples but what I'd avoid is the pet's stat boost that allows the player to play the same with better results.
I.e.
 -Pet aspects: you can activate the spell but it comes with a downside you don't want to keep for too long, like increasing offensive stats but reducing the defenssive ones.
 -Increasing pet stats but debuffing the hunter while it's active. (You should use your own cds while it's off, and the pet's cds while its on).
 -Increasing the damage against marked targets (so not just a plain damage buff).
 -Increasing defenssive stats, focus regen and aggro, but greatly reduce damage (that could allow pet tanking). (This would be active while the pet is doing the tanking part, but you could swap back to normal for the dps increase when necessary).

By using those you will want to use the spell for some time but not for long, rewarding players that are keeping their attention too on whatever the pet is doing, and thus, increasing the skill ceiling for the spec, not just its viability. That's what I meant by engaging mechanics.
