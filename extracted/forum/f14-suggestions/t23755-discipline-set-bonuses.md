# Discipline Set Bonuses

## Post 166603 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-01-27T08:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166603#p166603 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It was confirmed on Discord that Discipline Priest receives new tier sets in the next patch: T1, T2, T3 and T3.5.
That means there are a lot of set bonuses coming to the game so I thought that I could suggest some that might be useful mainly in PvE but also with PvP on mind.

I divided proposed set bonuses into spell categories: each set bonus could enhance one spell.

---

 **SMITE**
- Decrease global cooldown of Smite by 0.5 sec.

 *Currently the GCD is 1.5 seconds. GCD of Smite is mostly important when casting Smite with Searing Light because it is instant. This change would decrease waiting time between instant Smites by 0.5 second each time it procs. It would significantly improve Disc's DPS so I suggest placing this bonus in T3 or T3.5. It also helps breaking 1.5 sec cast limit which is possible for Disc in some haste builds.*

- Increase the range of your Smite and Holy Fire spells by 5 yds.

IMPORTANT NOTE!
This set bonus has already existed in the game. However, it was in the ZG healing set for 3 pieces. I suggest moving this bonus to the other set because this is currently the best bonus that Discipline Priests can have in the entire game. Currently it's painful for Priests to sacrifice 3 DPS items only to have this bonus active. And this bonus is useless for healers anyway so there is no point in keeping it in ZG set.
The bonus has its niche especially in PvP and in some PvE fights where attacking from long range is crucial.

- Searing Light causes your next Smite to cost no mana.

 *Mana saving bonus. Restores original Searing Light from first phase of CC2. From my experience, Discipline Priests have mana problems with weak gear. This might be perfect bonus for T1.*

- Increase the critical strike damage bonus of Smite and Holy Fire by 100%.

 *In other words, crit deals 200% damage, not 150%. This is what other casters like Warlock or Mage already have. It's very strong bonus because Discipline relies on crits. It massively changes damage output so this bonus should be 8/8 T3 or 5/5 T3.5.*

---
 **HOLY FIRE**

- Decrease the casting time of Holy Fire by 0.5 sec.

 *It's worth to note that Discipline in PvP almost never uses talents related with improved Holy Fire because of long casting time of the spell. That should help it. Also useful in PvE - casting faster is always nice.*

- Increase the damage of periodic effect of Holy Fire by 100%.

 *DoT deals twice damage. That would create playstyle that promotes use of Holy Fire over Smite. Very good use against multiple enemies.*

---
 **POWER WORD: SHIELD**

- When your Power Word: Shield is broken, it explodes with Holy Nova (Rank 1).

 *Explodes around the target that is affected by the shield! This bonus would promote shielding raid members that will receive damage. Priest will have incentive to use shield because of extra AoE damage from Holy Nova.*

---
 **CHASTISE**

- Chastise additionally increases run speed of friendly target by 50%.

This one is my favourite. It gives a lot of power to Chastise when used properly. For example, speedrunning guilds could use it for faster clears. Also, when someone is in wrong position (for example has bomb on Baron Geddon in Molten Core), watchful Priest could help them run away.

That would also be very useful in PvP. Currently in PvP there aren't many reasons to Chastise your friends because you remove their HP (and HP is the most important in PvP). That would give Priest a tool to increase the mobility of their friends. I can imagine Chastise would be great to help flag carrier in Warsong Gulch.

 50% run speed is equivalent of Swiftness Potion. Duration of Chastise is 8 to 12 seconds so it won't be overpowered.

---
 **HOLY NOVA**

- Holy Nova also affects raids members for 10% of its healing value.

 *That's not much but would be cool flavour.*

- Increase the radius of Holy Nova by 2 yards.

 *Larger radius = more damage.*

---
 **INNER FOCUS**

- Inner Focus guarantees your next spell to be critical.

 *Currently, Inner Focus is pure RNG. It might crit or not. This bonus makes it more predictable.*
