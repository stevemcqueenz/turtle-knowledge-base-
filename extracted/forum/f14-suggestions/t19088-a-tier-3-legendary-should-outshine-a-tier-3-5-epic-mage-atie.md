# A Tier-3 Legendary Should Outshine a Tier-3.5 Epic (Mage Atiesh)

## Post 129754 by Sparkwens — 2025-05-05T15:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129754#p129754 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Kirel’narak, the Death Sentence (the new Kara40 staff) packing 180 Spell Power and 2% Crit is better than the Mage Atiesh even with Mage Atiesh’s extra 2% Hit. By comparison:

 **Priest Atiesh** hands out 362 Healing Power, outhealing any Tier 3.5 set piece.

 **Warlock Atiesh** brings 183 Spell Power and 2% Crit, surpassing Kirel’narak.

 **Druid Atiesh** grants 600 Attack Power in Feral form, beating every Tier 3.5 feral weapon.

Mage Atiesh’s 150 Spell Power is about 17% behind the Kara40 staff.

Thunderfury, Blessed Blade of the Windseeker was the go-to weapon right up until Tier 3 gear showed up, setting the expectation that legendaries outshine epics. If Turtle WoW wants to stick to that tradition, Mage Atiesh needs to feel just as special.

Plus, it’s Medivh’s staff—found in his own tower—so mages should be wielding the absolute best version. Here’s what I’d suggest:

 **1. Bump Spell Power** up to **200+**, so it clearly tops the Kara40 staff.

 **2. Raise the Crit Aura** to **4%**, keeping it relevant through all of Tier 3.

 **3. Add a small Mana-Regen Aura** (e.g., “Restores 1% mana every 5 seconds”).

With those tweaks, Mage Atiesh would finally feel like the reward we’ve all been grinding for—and no one would ever want to swap it out for an epic drop.
