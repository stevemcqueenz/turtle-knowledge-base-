# The requirements for the new Ravasaur mount are absurd.

## Post 149214 by Stegosaurustrap — 2025-08-29T20:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149214#p149214 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I understand that making mounts take incredible luck or dedication to grinding is par for the course on a private server that is funded by people who will "donate" to get special mounts.

That said, requiring 420 Ravasaur teeth (Cute, why not 42069 amirite hurrr) when you get one tooth per turn in is insane. The fastest way would be to kill the elite Stegodons, which have either 100% drop rate of their quest item or close to it, 3,360 times.

On top of all that, the mount hardly looks any different than the raptor mounts available from vendors. I could see maybe 200 teeth for a mount that really stands out.

So I guess my suggestion (complaint) is just to reduce the amount of teeth required or increase how many you get from turn ins, and maybe make the mount more distinct from what is already easily available. Better yet, make it a different species altogether, like a devilsaur.

Thank you for listening to my nonsense.
