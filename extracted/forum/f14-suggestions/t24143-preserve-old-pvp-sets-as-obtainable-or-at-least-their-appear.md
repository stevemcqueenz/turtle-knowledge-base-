# Preserve Old PvP Sets as Obtainable (or at Least Their Appearances)

## Post 169831 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-02T17:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169831#p169831 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Turtle WoW team and community,
First of all, I really appreciate the work being done on the upcoming PvP rework. New sets and progression updates are always exciting and help keep the game alive.

However, I would like to raise a concern regarding the announcement that the current PvP sets will become unobtainable and effectively obsolete once the new patch is live.

This design philosophy feels closer to modern Retail WoW, where older content and rewards are permanently removed, turning them into “veteran-only relics.” A similar situation happened when Naxxramas transitioned from Vanilla to WotLK and Tier 3 became an exclusive legacy set.

One of the strengths of Turtle WoW, in my opinion, is its respect for long-term progression and horizontal accessibility rather than forced replacement cycles.

My Suggestion
Instead of permanently removing access to the old PvP sets, consider one of the following options:
Keep them obtainable through alternative means
Rare drops from enemy faction leaders
A legacy PvP token system
A challenging but repeatable questline
At minimum, allow players to unlock their appearances
Through achievements
Through a cosmetic vendor
Possibly via the shop (cosmetic only)

This would:
Preserve the history of the game
Respect veteran status without locking content away forever
Allow new players to engage with legacy aesthetics
Avoid creating artificial scarcity
Exclusivity can feel rewarding, but permanent removal often creates frustration for newer players and reinforces FOMO-style mechanics that Turtle WoW has generally avoided.

I believe there is a middle ground that honors veteran players while still keeping the game accessible and fair long-term.

Thank you for considering this suggestion, and I’d love to hear the community’s thoughts.
