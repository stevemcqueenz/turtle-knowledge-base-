# If someday you decide to rework old zones...

## Post 172717 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-03-27T13:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172717#p172717 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Some might say I'm exaggerating or being too much of a perfectionist, but these days, Vanilla WoW is so old that it's easy to notice errors, bugs, or minor details on the map. Honestly, when I see them, I think... maybe they could improve.

This happens to me with the holes and model errors in Stormwind (I sincerely hope they'll one day redo that WMO, which is the most broken and flawed of the three cities), and it happens with the old expansions to zones—those areas that gave rise to something as big as Moonwhisper Coast, Grim Reaches, and Northwind are today—which are more polished and detailed. Let's break it down:

Tirisfal Uplands: It lacks detail, frankly. It's the worst of them all. Despite its lore of Forsaken against the Lordaeron army (or what's left of it), it's impossible not to notice the lack of vegetation, poor textures, and badly designed terrain. It urgently needs a rework.

Westhaven Hollow: As a small, abandoned area in Dustwallow, it's fine, but its textures don't match because that area has the same flaw Blizzard used on the edges of continents. Instead of using the area's texture to decorate the edges, they used the Wetlands texture, so it looks very mismatched and is just another piece of Wetlands in Dustwallow.

Areas in Stonetalon: These are much better, but they still have some flaws, such as a large section that looks unusually undetailed, and Amani'alor, which doesn't share a texture with the rest of the area and again falls into the recycled Wetlands texture.

Hawks Vigil: Same story, but in reverse. It seems odd to see a piece of Elwynn instead of something added to the Wetlands mountains. I also think there's still plenty of room to make the area larger, since Wetlands has that large, unpolished section.
