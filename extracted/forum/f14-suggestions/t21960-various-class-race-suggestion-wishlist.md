Even through Classic Lore, Priestesses of The Moon are a hybrid of Hunter and Priest, leaning on Priest with the sole exception that they generally use bows. Given that Night Elven society has always been matriarchal, the females were generally the leading force for battles and the Sisterhood of Elune was no different.

Night Elf Paladins are the perfect middle ground for those seeking RP flavor and lore accurate class-race fantasy. The argument of 'You can use Hunter' only satisfies a purely RP-focused view on the matter while ignoring something that has actual ground to stand on compared to something such as Tauren Priests.

My argument: If Tauren Priests are able to exist before Night Elf Paladins, where exactly is the line of gameplay and lore stopped?

A couple things to consider:
- 1. I am a genuine fan of Warcraft Lore. [Baring most modern retcons.]
 2. I am 100% for additions that fit the Classic Warcraft Lore.
 3. I genuinely want to understand the line between reasonable and unreasonable in situations like this.
The Tauren Religion centers around the Earth Mother and her children, An'she and Mu'sha/Elune, with their spiritual belief system being almost entirely about nature. It makes sense for Tauren to be Druids and Shamans, but being Priests/Paladins is where you get into a grey area. Let's assume for the sake of simplicity that Tauren get their Light magic from one of the two children of the Earth Mother.

With this assumption - and what Blizzard themself did - we can safely say it's An'she, hence 'Sunwalkers' existing. However, if we MUST look at Classic WoW and earlier Warcraft, Mu'sha/Elune is the more likely candidate because even the Night Elves get their Light magic from Elune.

Along with this, Night Elves already have that base to work off of for using the Light and, because it is entirely in their nature, it is entirely rational to believe that Night Elf Paladins [At least females lore-wise] would exist. The only real gripe is that Night Elf Priestesses almost exclusively use bows, but you can simply point to the entirety of the Sentinels and conclude 'Yea, the Priestesses almost certainly can use melee weapons like any other Sentinel warrior'.

I'm not here to scream and shout, demanding for Night Elf Paladins to exist and stuff like Tauren Priests to vanish, but it's rather odd that Tauren Priests arrive first while Night Elf Paladins seem to be one of the few - if only - lore-favored class-race combos that almost fully exist to be ignored/actively put to the side.

TL;DR - Night Elf Paladins have a lot of solid ground for being added, far more than Tauren Priests, as Night Elf Priestesses are already trained warriors of the Light/Elune.
