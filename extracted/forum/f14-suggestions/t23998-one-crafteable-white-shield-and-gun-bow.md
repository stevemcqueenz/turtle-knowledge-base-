## Post 168658 by Zaruth — 2026-02-17T21:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168658#p168658 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Ahoy mateys!

I recently tried OneForAll again, the challenge of completing all the challenges with rewards at once. I've tried it a couple of times and have improved with each attempt.

The thing is, I'm always trying it with a Warrior (yep, I'm an idiot, and I love it), and I've realized that... there are no craftable white shields or ranged weapons.

I know it's a little ridiculous, but please hear me out.
Could you at least add a simple level 1 white shield and a melee ranged weapon, both craftable?
I know that a lot of items that were white in the base game and were craftable are now green. That's been a big limitation for me in the challenge, but I accept it! It'll be a matter of maximizing survival with what I have.

However... something that really bugs me is that when doing the "Traveling
Craftmaster“ and ”Vagrant's Endeavor" challenges, I literally can't access an entire talent tree (Protection), nor can I attack an enemy from a distance.

That's why I'm asking, please, if it's not too much to ask, to at least add a craftable level 1 shield, along with a ranged weapon, both white. I don't know if it would also be necessary to be able to craft the ammunition to equip it, but it would be something to consider.

Finally, while writing this, I just realized that mages/warlocks/priests suffer the same problem with wands. There are no white wands that can be crafted, so throughout their adventure from level 1 to 60, they won't be able to use them either.

I don't know what you guys think, maybe it's too far-fetched, but I wanted to mention it just in case it was possible.

Greetings to all  smiling_turtle_head  !
