# Level 60 Versions of Low/Mid-Level Dungeons

## Post 164607 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-07T18:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164607#p164607 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Turtle WoW team and community,

I’d like to suggest an idea that could help diversify endgame PvE content while making better use of the existing world and instances.

Vanilla has many iconic low and mid-level dungeons (Deadmines, Shadowfang Keep, Scarlet Monastery, Uldaman, Zul’Farrak, etc.), but once players reach level 60, most of these places become completely irrelevant.

It feels like a lot of great content goes unused at endgame.

![💡](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4a1.svg) Proposed Idea: Level 60 Dungeon Versions

Allow players to re-enter certain low/mid-level dungeons in a level 60 version

Scaled enemies, mechanics, and rewards

Not meant to replace raids, but to add variety to high-level PvE

This could take different forms:

A NPC to toggle a “level 60 mode”

A quest or key to unlock the harder version

Weekly or rotating dungeon availability

![🎯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3af.svg) Why this could be great for Turtle WoW

Adds meaningful PvE content outside of raids

Encourages dungeon play at level 60

Makes Azeroth feel alive and relevant, not disposable

Uses existing assets in a smart Vanilla+ way

Perfect for:

smaller groups

casual guild nights

off-raid days

![🧠](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9e0.svg) Design Philosophy

Difficulty above standard 5-mans, but below raids

Rewards focused on:

pre-raid / alternative gear

utility items

cosmetics, recipes, or fun trinkets

No power creep, just content diversity

![🧭](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9ed.svg) Summary

Revisiting classic dungeons at level 60 would:

Extend the life of existing content

Break the raid-only endgame loop

Offer players more ways to enjoy PvE at max level

I’d love to hear what the community and devs think about reusing and reimagining classic dungeons as part of the level 60 experience.

Thanks for reading!
