## Post 141890 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-28T14:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141890#p141890 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While the idea of gaining a primary stat buff from fully consuming mage food is interesting, it could unintentionally devalue the Cooking profession. Right now, Cooking plays a unique and important role by providing powerful, class-agnostic food buffs that players actively seek out—especially at endgame.

If mage food starts offering similar or even better stat bonuses just from being eaten to its duration, players might stop using crafted food altogether. That would make Cooking feel less meaningful, especially for those who’ve invested time into leveling it up or farming rare recipes.

Mage food is already a huge convenience—free, infinite, and summonable. If it starts replacing the role of crafted food too, we’re removing both the gameplay loop of preparing for content and the economy around it.

A better approach might be to keep mage food focused on regen and utility, while allowing cooking to remain the go-to for performance buffs. That way, each has its own niche, and we preserve the value of professions without overlapping too much.
