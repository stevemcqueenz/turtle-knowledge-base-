# Improve / Add New BG Rep Rewards.

## Post 35795 by Jammyxx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-23T12:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35795#p35795 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I had a thought the other day, I've been playing BGs alot for the past month and I'm still nowhere near exalted with the AB/WSG respective Horde/Alliance factions, however I got Exalted with AV in two weeks (horde) even though we lose 95% of the time.

When you look at the rep rewards for being exalted with WSG and AB, they are very lacklustre for time spent.

Take the WSG leggings for example, I've never ever seen anyone wearing these, due to by the time you've grinded WSG to exalted, chances are you're already R12, or the fact that BWL gear blows them out the water,.
Same can be said for the AB Epic shoulders and cloak.

I feel that these items should be buffed for the time spent obtaining the rep for them, or atleast add some more Epic gear for exalted, Maybe a helm or some Epic weapons,  along the lines of the Mace from AV, but one handers or a 2H Sword for Alliance or 2H axe or Horde, and I still want to see a PvP mace with Feral Attack Power like the ones you get in Naxx/AQ.

The AV reward are great and you can get them alot easier, especially on Alliance side, so why do the WSG/AB rewards kinda suck for time investment.

Not everyone wants to raid, myself included, I like to just PvP and do dungeons so the rep rewards and rank gear are all I've got to rely on.

Thanks for reading :)
