# Idea for the next TWOW Patch/Expansion/Big Content Drop

## Post 78021 by Corfarion — 2023-11-19T20:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78021#p78021 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think the best thing to do next after Gilneas is either the Emerald Dream or the Dragon Isles. Remember those? Both of these zones were in the alpha/beta versions of the 2004 game and they looked pretty fucking cool. The Emerald Dream had all those cool round spirals everywhere and could be a zone meant for players to do after they clear the Emerald Sanctum. The Emerald Sanctum raid could act like an atunement for players to be able to unlock the Emerald Dream. Or just make the Emerald Dream be a zone only druids can teleport to so they can do some cool roleplay quest chains for some cosmetic cool stuff or new spells. The Emerald Dream can also act as a continuation of the storyline of the Mount Hyjal zone.
Also, the Dragon Isles could be a fantastic zone that could be unlocked after you take a quest from Southshore/Steepcliff port. Those two towns at the moment have useless docks and so the Dragon Isles can make these old zones relevant again with ships that can take you to the fabled Dragon Isles that have the giant octopus/kraken as a central setpiece landmark. It can also tie to the storyline in Azhara at the Rethress Sanctum where you help some friendly nagas. The naga can have a huge influence in the Dragon Isles and you as a player can either fight them or help them.
Time to bring in the big guns from some scrapped classes: Death Knights and Demon Hunters. They were planned to be in the original version of the game and so the demon hunters can be added as like some sort of taskforce sent by Illidan to the Dragon Isles in search of some powerful ancient artefact of the old night elf empire. You, the player, and your spec-ops team of nagas are sent to fight some demons or something like that in felwood and that can act as the lvl 1-5 starting zone. Then, at lvl 60, you return to your original starting zone base (that also houses the demon hunter trainers and where you can teleport to at any time) and get a chain specific to your class that sends you to the Dragon Isles and if you complete it its going to unlock some cool endgame spell or something.
For death knights, the will of the mighty Lich King compels you to leave the isolation of northrend and venture forth into the Eastern Plaguelands to meet Kel'Thuzad in Naxxramas, where you will start your level 1-5 leveling journey. Kel'Thuzad will give you a couple of tasks to do in the necropolis and then send you to do some villain stuff around Light's Hope Chapel. And at the end of the leveling journey, mirroring the same Wrath of the Lich King leveling journey, you are sent to assasinate Tirion Fordring. But OH NO, Balnazzar catches you right before you reach Tirion's shed! Balnazzar had some unresolved beef with Arthas in Warcraft 3 so as a personal insult to him, the dreadlord uses his necromantic powers to break the Lich King's hold over your mind. And so, in your weakest state, Tirion somehow beats up Balnazzar and helps you by giving you a letter to give to either Bolvar or Eitrigg so you can join your main faction and start leveling from 5 to 60.

Well, Emerald Dream - in theory - is a copy of prehistoric Azeroth with many layers of its previous (imperfect) variations. It's huge, changeful realm that would require great amount of work for its realization on TWoW. And, of course, it would require a plot - something that would allow us, the players, to enter this realm and do our routine murderhobo things.
Dragon isles are less in scale and, in terms of narrative, are an example of "tabula rasa". But this place is as good as others - Grim Batol, Kul Tiras, Balor, Crestfall, Zul Dare, Zandalar, Hiji, Quel'Thalas, Kezan. And I'm not even mention lesser places in the existing locations that could be filled with content. Possibilities are endless.

Finally, I'm not sure it's wise to add new classes, especially so controversial as dk/dh. I believe that the best way is in adding more depth to existing vanilla classes.
