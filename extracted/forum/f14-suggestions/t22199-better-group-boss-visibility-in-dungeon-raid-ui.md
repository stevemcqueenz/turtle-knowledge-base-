# Better Group & Boss Visibility in Dungeon/Raid UI

## Post 154025 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-02T11:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154025#p154025 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello... Turtle WoW team,

I wanted to suggest an improvement regarding group and raid visibility. Right now, it can be a bit tricky to quickly know who is playing which role. On Retail WoW, we have small icons next to each player’s portrait:

Healer → green cross

Tank → blue shield

DPS → red sword

This allows players to instantly see the composition of the group or raid.

On Turtle, it’s not as clear. A few changes could make a big difference:

Display tanks distinctly, so they are easily recognizable at a glance.

Show boss portraits and health bars continuously somewhere on the screen from the start of the fight, especially useful in multi-boss encounters.

These changes wouldn’t alter gameplay, but they would make dungeon and raid management much smoother and more strategic, particularly for new players or complex fights.

Thanks
