# Feral druid and dps on weapons

## Post 102501 by Topdreamer — 2024-10-04T10:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102501#p102501 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are a lot of items which have had the stat "+X Attack power in Cat, Bear, Dire Bear, and Moonkin forms only" added to them, which is great as it makes feral itemization less limited. However, wouldn't it be better if the "Damage per second"-stat on weapons directly translated to a certain attack power amount for feral druids? That way you wouldn't have to add this stat on every weapon you want to be viable for feral druids, and it would also make the item interface cleaner.
