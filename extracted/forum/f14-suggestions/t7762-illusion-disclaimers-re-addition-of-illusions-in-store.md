# Illusion Disclaimers + Re-addition of Illusions in Store

## Post 49717 by Loopybat — 2023-06-11T23:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49717#p49717 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I thought it might be nice to add disclaimers for certain animations being broken to the page you get after clicking an illusion in the store, where the final purchase button is. There is empty space to the left of the screen that could have a disclaimer. I want to clarify that I am not complaining about the illusions, I am instead thinking of a way that the current illusions plus the removed ones (dryad and naga for example) can be included without people having any good reason to complain after their purchases. Not only would they be given a disclaimer before purchase (which could include extra stuff broken with that particular illusion) but also have the usual refund options to get tokens back.
