# Change the Slam casting animation to the idle attack animation

## Post 30264 by Nyahoho — 2022-12-06T11:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30264#p30264 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, when casting Slam, the warrior simply stands there in the non-combat idle animation with their weapon drawn, but not in any kind of stance.

I suggest changing that casting animation to the "idle attack animation" (like trying to auto attack a target that's too far away or waiting for the swing timer).

This would make playing a slam build feel more immersive, since it wouldn't look like you're simply standing there while the enemy's health bar decreases.

Thanks for reading.
