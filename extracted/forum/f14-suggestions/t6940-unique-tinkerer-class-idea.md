# (Unique...?) Tinkerer Class Idea

## Post 43875 by Mrbadwithnames1 — 2023-04-30T13:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43875#p43875 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Intro
I spent way too much time thinking up a cool tinkerer class last night to not waste even more time typing it up now.
It’s more of a concept – especially as far as the talents are concerned (all five of them) but nobody wants to read the boring talents – especially when they don’t even exist.
So, without further ado, I invite you to waste your time reading this gibberish!

![Image](https://kboosting.com/img/13585/engineering.png)
Tinkerer Class
Armour: Cloth, Leather, and Mail after level 40
Weapons: Dagger, Mace 1 handed, Mace 2 handed, Gun

Basic Gameplay & Abilities
The very base of a tinkerer class is a ranged (gun) DPS.
The special gameplay mechanic for the tinkerer is balancing scrapmetal abilities with the hard scrapmetal limit.
Starting at 5 scrapmetal at level 1 this limit can be increased via talents in your spec of choice up to a cap of 15 – I imagine it as being two or three talents, spaced out in the talent menu rather than a single talent available at either end, mid or early game.
Your scrapmetal can be seen as a buff, stacking up to your cap.
Abilities consuming scrapmetal temporarily decreases your cap until executed. (More on abilities later)
Scrapmetal is used to augment your weapons and create gadgets.

Throughout their levelling journey the tinkerer will learn to…

 **Augment their ranged weapons.**
 **Attach Scope**: Increases attack range.
 **Attach Stabilizer Module**: Increases hit chance.
 **Attach Auto-Reloader**: Increased gun fire rate.
 **Attach Metal Plating**: Increases chance to block/parry melee attacks with guns out.
 **Attach Stand**: Buffing critical strike when in one place for >5 seconds out of combat or >10 seconds in combat.
You may only have 1 gun augmentation active at any given time, they function like enchantments that persist until the weapon is either unequipped or the abilities are cancelled.

 **Augment their melee weapon.**
 **Attach Fuel Dispenser**: Proc chance to ignite the weapon, giving the player the “Blazing Weapon” buff for 5 or so seconds, causing all attacks to deal an additional bit of fire damage, this will also inflict a “Burning Fuel” DOT onto the target, dealing a small bit of fire damage for four seconds (up to 8 with talents)
 **Attach Discharging Rods**: Proc chance to electrify the weapon, giving the player the “Electrified Weapon” buff for 5 or so seconds, causing all attacks to deal an additional bit of nature damage with an additional proc chance stun them for 1 second (as far as 2 with talents).
 **Attach Coolant Jets**: Proc chance to release a spray of freezing coolant that deals little damage but slows the target for 4 seconds (up to 8 with talents)
 **Attach Spring-Loaded Blades**: Proc chance to trigger a bleeding DOT dealing physical damage for 4 seconds (up to 8 with talents)
 **Attach Hand Guard**: Increased block / Parry chance
You may only have 1 melee augmentation active at one time, they function like enchantments that persist until the weapon is either unequipped or the abilities are cancelled.

 **Augment their ammunition.**
 **Flechettes**: Gives every shot you land on your target a proc chance to explode, dealing a bit of damage (an ever slimmer proc to stun for 1 seconds with talents),
 **Tracer Bullets**: Increases hit chance.
 **Hollow Point**: Gives every short you land on your target a proc chance to fracture inside them, inflicting a bleeding DOT for 5 seconds
 **Ricochet Shot**: Gives the bullet a proc to fly off the recipient and onto one additional target, dealing 25% (Up to 50% with talents) of the damage dealt to the first target (With talents the bullet could have a second, even smaller proc to bounce onto yet another target dealing 15% of the damage dealt to the first target).
 **Shell Shots**: Increases damage at the cost of attack range.
You may only have 1 bullet augmentation active at one time, they function like enchantments that persist until the weapon is either unequipped or the abilities are cancelled.

 **Augment their armour.**
 **Attach Heat-Sink**: Increased fire resistance, decreased frost resistance (Increased further with talents).
 **Attach Automatic Heater**: Increased frost resistance, decreased fire resistance (Increased further with talents).
 **Attach Anti-Venom Injector**: Removes all poison effects once every three minutes (Down to 30 seconds with talents)
 **Attach Bulky Exoskeleton**: Stamina and Parry increase at the cost of 10% movement speed (down to 5% with talents)
 **Attach Spring-Loaded Exoskeleton**: Strength and Dodge increase at the cost of 10% movement speed (down to 5% with talents)
 **Attach Mobile Exoskeleton**: Increases player speed by 10% (up to 15% with talents) and increases Dodge while decreasing melee and ranged damage but 25%
You may only have 1 armour augmentation active at one time, they function like enchantments that persist until the weapon is either unequipped or the abilities are cancelled.

 **Create situationally useful gadgets.**
Unlike the previous ability types, a tinkerer may have any number of gadgets provided that they can stomach the scrapmetal cost – they function like other ‘conjured’ items – disappearing 15 minutes after logging out and only being allowed to have one of each in your inventory.

 **Flashbang**: AOE stun grenade – (Identical mechanically to throwable engineering bombs).
Net Launcher: Launches at net at a target, immobilizing them for 5 seconds (up to 10 with talents) – additionally, with proper talents the player can convert the item from single-use to a cooldown.
 **Pocket Oil Spill**: A lingering AOE which doesn’t damage targets but has a proc chance to interrupt anyone standing one it (Can be upgraded with talents to give targets the ‘Oily’ debuff which greatly decreases their fire resistance).
Escapist’s Spring-Boots: Launches your forward a short distance – mostly a travel / escape item (With talents can be converted from single-use to cooldown).
 **Time Bomb**: Places a debuff on the target – dealing big AOE damage after 10 seconds (With talents, this can be changed to be proximity-triggered, exploding when the host is in-range of another enemy) / (With talents, this can be detonated manually).
 **Decoy Bot**: Places an NPC alarm bot facing the same direction as the play – it will walk forward for ten seconds (Up to 20 with talents) aggroing everything in its way (Cannot path-find, only walks in a straight line).

![image](https://wow.zamimg.com/modelviewer/classic/webthumbs/npc/137/14473.png)

 **Shock Absorbent Boots**: Grants your boots the ability to nullify a certain amount of fall damage before breaking (Increased with talents).
 **Disposable Flamethrower**: Cone-shaped AOE blast of fire with a proc chance to ignite the user (decreased and eventually prevented entirely through talents) / (Converted from single-use to cooldown via talents)

Ability Costs
The abilities above cost different amounts of scrapmetal, something like a weapon augmentation would likely not go over 1 or 2 scrapmetal since it would be a shame to convert your tinkerer into a glorified hunter after you pick out a glorified weapon enchant.
The gadgets would be pricier abilities, costing 3 or 4 scrapmetal to use.
I believe there should be talents to decrease the cost of every gadgets by 1 scrapmetal.
In a typical vanilla style, certain abilities could require reagents, gunpower, new electrical bits and bobs from general merchants, etc.

Ideally, the tinkerer shouldn’t be able to change their augmentations / create their gadgets quickly – the process should take thirty seconds and should only be doable outside combat to reward strategizing before combat.

Generic Abilities
Clobber: A basic melee attack dealing a little extra damage.
Calculated Strike: A melee strike with a ‘cast time’ of 2 second which increases the chance of proccing any melee augmentations, this ability would be on a brief, 30 second cooldown.
Evaluated Shot: A ranged shot with a ‘cast time’ of 2.5 second which increases the chance of proccing any ranged augmentations, this ability would be on a brief, 30 second cooldown.
Wallop: Brief stun (leading into a slowness effect with talents) designed to allow the tinkerer to make a brief retreat to resume ranged attacks, this ability would be on a 3 minute cooldown.

 **Generic Talents**
These are talents that could go into either spec, or maybe *should* be in both specs.
 **Dexterity**: several ranks – decreases the cast-time of augmentations, gadget creation, Calculated Strike and Evaluated Shot.
 **Burns and Stains**: A 1% resistance to nature, frost and fire damage.
 **Expect the Unexpected**: several ranks – increases change for the cast bar not to be knocked down when hit casting Calculated Strike or Evaluated Shot

Specializations
 **Inventor (DPS)**
 *Active Abilities:*
 **Pocket Cannon**: A powerful instant-use, single-use gadget dealing damage and knocking a target down to the ground for one second.
 **Blistering Steam Sprayer**: A cone-shaped AOE attacks spraying targets with hot steam – bypassing a certain amount of armour.

 *Passive Talents:*
 **Projectile Sling**: increases the range of your throwable bombs by X distance.
Shrapnel Cannon: Divide the damage from your pocket cannon among up to three targets standing before you.
 **Fuse-Dispenser**: reduces cast-time on pocket cannon.
 **Tactical Bombardment**: Replace your pocket cannon with a ranged AOE ability that can be fired at a specific location. (Requires: Shrapnel Cannon)

 **Machinist (Tank)**
Starting at level 10 the tinkerer can summon a mechanical bodyguard, steadily upgraded via the talents.
This mechanical bodyguard would of course build a lot of threat, the engineer can enable different ‘Modes’ on their bodyguard throughout the fight, each mode having a ‘Special Move” which the tinkerer may activate on a cooldown.
![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/modelviewer/live/webthumbs/npc/161/28577.png)

 **Combat Mode**: Increased attack power.
The special ability for this mode would be:
(Scan Target: Places an ‘Analysed” debuff on the target, increasing your mechanical bodyguard’s hit rating and critical strike chance)

 **Harvest Mode**: Makes all attacks generate a lot of threat and AOE .
The special ability for this mode would be:
(Reap: AOE channelled attack, damaging, slowing, and interrupting mobs in its radius)

 **Defence Mode**: Increases your mechanical bodyguard’s armour and elemental resistances while decreasing his attack speed and power.
The special ability for this mode would be:
(Integrity Check: Increasing healing received by 25%)

 **Overcharge Mode**: increases your mechanical bodyguard’s attack power and attack speed – dealing additional nature (electrical) damage all the while taking damage itself, at 20% health Overcharge Mode cannot be disabled in combat. (Witch specs a safety-breaker will trigger, automatically disabling overcharge-mode upon dipping below 20% health)
The special ability for this mode would be:
(Discharge: AOE electrical explosion dealing nature damage and having a per-target proc to stun or interrupt them – using this ability switches your mechanical bodyguard back to its previous mode.)

In the event that your mechanical bodyguard falls in combat he can be ‘rebooted’, when rebooting your mechanical bodyguard cannot fight or use abilities until his health reaches 100% (80% with talents), he cannot be healed when rebooting.
With the correct talents your mechanical bodyguard will always have an alarm light strapped to it, constantly building threat even when rebooting. (Not when dead, obviously.)

In additional – at the bottom of your talent tree, is the ability to enter your mechanical bodyguard – gaining access to his modes and abilities.
When in the mechanical bodyguard you do not take any damage (excluding DOTs previously put on you) until it is below 50% health (down to 30% with talents) at which point you take 50% (Down to 30% with talents) of the damage he takes.
When in your mechanical bodyguard – if he is either dead or rebooting you will automatically be ejected from inside.
You will also be ejected from inside should you choose to use the self-destruct ability available only to those inside the mechanical bodyguard.
