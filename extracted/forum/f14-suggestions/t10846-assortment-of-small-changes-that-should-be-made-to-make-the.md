# Assortment of small changes that should be made to make the new patch less intimidating to engage in

## Post 70553 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-10-13T09:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70553#p70553 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm going to get straight to the point and split them to categories.

 **I - Consumable costs:**

 **1) Dreamtonic:** Quest reward from turning in one Small Dream Shard. Current price of these shards is 3g 30silver AFTER hyjal's open. The raid from what we've seen so far does not reward much dream shards, and the inflow of these shards is coming from the front-loaded unrepeatable quests people are doing with their alts. Once that's over, it's going to stagnate to people doing the repeatable quests and the little shards guilds get from the raids will go to their gbank for the druid aura, not for individual consumes.

Turtle wow has a history of doing good things to make sure a 20 min consume doesn't cost 3g, the winterspring ekos and firewater changes are a good testament to that. It would be nice to go in that same vein and make the turn in quest reward three dreamtonics instead of one. This is an simple and convenient change that can be implemented in a maintenance patch.

 **2) Tel'abim banana foods:** A portion of the required materials got nicely fixed in 1.17, but that only made the other issue of the recipe more glaring, and that's the bananas themselves. Currently it costs one dream dust and three ripe bananas to make one gargantuan banana.

And I think there's a dark secret behind it
[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#hKKAcz9]

But on a serious note, it's too much of an artificial timesink to make people stay in the tel'abim zone picking up bananas. That content is way past us and 2g for a single banana food lasting 15 mins is too much, considering that the stam and strength foods cost around 70 silver. It's the right time to change the quest to exchange one bundle of dream dust and three ripe bananas with THREE gargantuan bananas. It's only right that after the gnome npc perfects his transmutation process that the cost *decreases* rather than *increases*. Once again a simple and convenient change for the devs, just upping the reward count of the small batch quest from 1 to 3, and of the big batch quest from 5 to 15.

 **3) Elemental Earths:** Elemental earths have always been a hassle to farm, and something where the supply could never catch up to the enormous demand of nature potions and sharpening stones. Back in the blizzard days, they had the exact same issue with a different elemental item, elemental fires. It was reaching upwards of 4g because every guild wanted to run molten core/bwl and they needed a LOT of fire protection potions. Simple solution, just make the mid-tier elemental break down to the low-tier elemental. Fun fact about vanilla is elementals have [three different level tiers.](https://i.imgur.com/xxFe8hN.png).

Because Blizzard's solution worked on elemental fires, reducing their price but STILL keeping the elemental fire farming just as profitable (the same mobs that drop elemental fires also drop hearts of fire, which can be broken down to elemental fires to end up making the same profit as before but supplying the economy with a lot of more elemental fires and finally catching up to the demand.)

Turtle wow should add a [transmute: elemental earth]. Costs 1 core of earth, gives 3 elemental earths, has a 10 min shared transmute cd, and obtainable at high cenarion circle rep (there's no alchemy reward for Cenarion Circle at the moment). This is the perfect analogy to the fire one that is obtained from the Thorium Brotherhood faction.

If this is added in 1.17.1 it would be greatly appreciated by everybody from raiders to farmers, I honestly don't see anybody potentially complaining about this.

II - Hyjal gameplay loop:
 **Too much running, not enough playing:**
The elephant in the room that made a lot of people groan about the new zone, once you're done with the initial questchains and you're left with only the repeatable stuff, it becomes very much like a chores list. We're left with a small number of kill and collect quests that require the player to run a bit deep into the zone then run all the way back to the town to turn them in, reaccept the quest, and do the whole thing over again.

All of the generic "collect hyjalroot" or "collect furbolg things" or "collect stones from demons" quests should be changed into always activated quests. Hyjalroot should be always collectable, and the drops from mobs should always drop no matter if you're on the quest or not. Then you simply engage in the content at your own pace, and go back to the town when you want to turn in as much of the quest items as you have collected on your time out in the wilderness. No need for that run back and forth hassle. Just like winterspring e'kos.

 **Everything happens in hyjal:** All the repeatable quests take you to hyjal areas. Understandably they want people to actually be in the new zone, that's one of the metrics that show it's success, but the quests in that zone would be a perfect opportunity to make players go out and do group content with their friends. There can be daily randomized quests by a mob in hyjal that asks you to go to one of the max level dungeons and get a resource that they need in their hyjal war effort. The people familiar with the lore will know how to work it out, just something like "go to stratholme, gather this thing from a mob that we need to strengthen our connection with the emerald dream" and the reward would be 2 or 3 bright dream shards

## Post 73137 by Ashstache (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-23T18:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73137#p73137 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Thus far the entire Hyjal ecosystem has not been worth engaging with. Nothing there is remarkable or worth grinding for. The repeatable quests are really boring, too, absolutely uninspired stuff. There's little to no lore for the zone, no progressive story, and none of the areas matter. Killing mundane mobs - demons, oozes, furbolgs, wildlife - and picking herbs is the laziest design possible. There's absolutely nothing compelling about this stuff.

I say that because if these quests were interesting and fun to do, at least that would make the grind more acceptable. These crystals are not worth the time investment, and the number of them rewarded per quest is absurd, The troll quest in particular is absolutely hysterical; collect 10 bracelets off of raid-level mobs that require a full party to handle, given their density in that area, and all you get is a single Small Dream Shard. Whoever designed that one should have simply put up a sign that said "do not engage with this content".

There's a couple items, like the enchants and alchemy recipes, that some people will chase. The crafting recipes are outright worthless, and you'd have to be a fool to chase them. The problems with the Hyjal economy are worsened by magnitudes because this stuff literally isn't endgame content. Players can skip this freely and head straight to AQ and Naxx and not miss a thing.

Even if the items available for all this grinding WERE worthwhile...why make it such an eternal grind? The endgame is already a slog of timewasting, farming for consumables, days to weeks of mind-numbing reputation grinds. Why stretch that out even further? It feels intensely hostile, and most of all, just outright not fun. I echo Balake's sentiments, especially as these repeatable quests do NOT promote group play at all.
