## Post 131685 by FFRR — 2025-05-18T00:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131685#p131685 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It could sound so repetitive and was asked a few before from other posts of the community...

But as it sounds and the ideology of the server bringing us the cut content, having the worgen (cata alpha model) as a neutral race with no faction and a mercenary mode for bg, dungeon and raid (Can be in a party with players, welcome in every neutral town but hostile for horde and alliance npc's), with the classes of Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Warlock and Mage. Without the "Two forms" and the racial mount ability at level 40.

The starting zone outsite of greymane wall at level 1 with a few quests to help the starting leveling.

The lore could be: Some of the worgen released from the control of Genn Greymane in the city when some adventurers defeated him in a lethal encounter, now the ferocious worgens are trying to find a way to survive in azeroth.
