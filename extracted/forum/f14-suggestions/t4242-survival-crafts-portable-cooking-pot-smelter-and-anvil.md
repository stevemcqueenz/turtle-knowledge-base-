# Survival crafts: Portable cooking pot, smelter and anvil

## Post 25329 by Zhilo — 2022-09-17T19:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25329#p25329 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Perhaps this has already been suggested before, But i think Survival could be solidified as a tradeskill that places temporary objects in the world for the benefit of players around it. Cooking, smelting, and smithing are all activities one can do to pass time while stationary, so if players were allowed to craft small, portable versions of the facilities needed to perform these activities, they could place them under a tent and make themselves a workshop anywhere in the world during their travels. I think having to use bag space for these portable items is already balancing enough.
