# Rogue Itemization

## Post 30659 by Lanimil — 2022-12-14T15:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30659#p30659 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Being an undead sword rogue, I do not have the human racial perk to help me out in terms of +sword skill on items. The very few items that DO have +sword skill are not good (like ZG swords - MH is fast), not equip-able (Edgemaster's), or inaccessible to the majority of the player base (Hungering Cold from KT).

Daggers (DM & MC), fist weapons (ZG), AND maces (AQ40 trash) have options for items with +weapon skill and yet +swords are pretty much non-existent. I also can't seem to find anything among the custom items displayed on the database.

I have some suggestions that could potentially fix this issue:

 1. Reitemize the off-hand sword of the Dal'Rend set. Remove the 100 armor and +7 defense and replace it with +5-7 sword skill. This would be the easiest and probably most useful fix that avoids having to create custom items.

 2. Introduce a custom item with +sword skill on it. Ideally this would be an off-hand weapon similar to Distracting Dagger from DMW. There's a link at the bottom of a sword that could drop from Prince Tortheldrin for example (the equip effect is +6 swords). An equivalent item to Mugger's Belt could also be an option. Obviously, it would be better if these items weren't copies of other items but I'm mentioning them for example's sake.

 3. Adjust the weapon speed of the MH Hakkari blade to a slower speed (2.6-2.8) so that the set bonus can be achieved without sacrificing on Sinister Strike damage. I'm sure warriors would like this change too!

 4. Convert Edgemaster's to leather.

Would like to hear people's thoughts on this or to hear other suggestions.
