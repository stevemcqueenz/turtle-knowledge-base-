# Ten smaller changes

## Post 91779 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-31T19:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91779#p91779 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I'd advise 10 smaller changes. No overhaul, no theorycrafting this time. The changes I mention here are small and wouldn't change anything fundamental but would be very cool ones, I think.
I put this thread into suggestions because I indeed suggest these changes.

 1. From level 60, make shaman weapon enhancement duration 15 min (just like pala buff uptime changes).
 2. Please add a learnable, new level of earth shock, which taunts.
 3. Please add nerfed version of earth shield and water shield to shamans for leveling.
 4. Please make so that elite mobs give 2 soul shards, to make soul collecting easier as a warlock.
 5. If it doesn't break DPS numbers, please allow rogues to benefit from WF alongside the poison they use.
 6. Nerf tea or put it onto the same CD as mana/health pots. Warlock HS should feel relevant.
 7. Please buff ardent defender talent. TWOW has non-ironical prot palas but even they don't take the tree's ultimate talent. My suggestion is a damage reduction which starts earlier but gets bigger the less HP you have.
 8. Please change holy champion priest talent a bit. Right now it's a weirdly named mark of the wild with 1 useable option. Mark of the wild aspect could be abandoned for more interesting options.
 9. Please allow tree druids to use non-hot healing spells. Each druid form is made for a role in the game. F.ex. THE druid tank is the bear. However, if you are a tree, you are partly punished for healing as a tree.
 10. Please add more mail tanking drops to instances for adventurous hunters and shamans.
