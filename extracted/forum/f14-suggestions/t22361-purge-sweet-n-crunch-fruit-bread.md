# Purge + Sweet 'N Crunch Fruit Bread

## Post 155248 by Shenzy — 2025-10-12T10:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155248#p155248 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Purge being on 8 seconds cooldown and a free usage if the item Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread leads to the following scenario:

A fast alliance flag carrier (Rogue / Druid)
can pop up: Free action potion, Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread, Goblin Rocket boots while Paladins are supporting using Hand of Freedom / heals / Lay on hand.
This leads to an unbalanced caps in WSG, totally feels like Alliance are having the edge there.

My suggestion is remove the Purge cooldown or lower it and remove Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread from the pool.

> **Membrane wrote: Sun Oct 12, 2025 10:12 am**
> Can you explain to a non-PVP'er how  Sweet 'N Crunchy effects purge?

The poison from the bread breaks most CCs.
When someone pops a FAP you could still use CCs like freezing trap, blind, sheep etc to get a breather for your team to catch up and use a few GCDs to purge their FAP.
Now you can cast 1 purge / 8 secs which wont always get their FAP off due to the amount of other buffs. Priest dispel still works ofc but due to the CC immunity granted by the bread the EFC can just zoom out of your range before you manage to dispel.

Hope it helped to understand the issue
