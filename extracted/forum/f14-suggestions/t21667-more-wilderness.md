# More Wilderness

## Post 150116 by Lorddandas — 2025-09-03T22:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150116#p150116 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Azeroth is a world with very rich fauna and flora and is lore-wise far more vast than it´s depiction in WoW. I would really Love to see unused spaces to be filled with forests, lakes, plains and such. Most location extensions contain camps or mines or other buildings but I would really Love to see some pure natural extension, untouched by humanoid races. Make Azeroth feel more wild and untouched by industry.
