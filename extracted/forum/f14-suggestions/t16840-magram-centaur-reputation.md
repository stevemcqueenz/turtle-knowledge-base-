## Post 115128 by Piccolo1833 — 2025-01-01T16:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115128#p115128 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please change something about the Magram Centaur reputation. I have done the Gelkis and Magram reputations before on different toons and I've come to realize Gelkis is much easier to do. The issue is when level 60 characters are grinding for their reputations and completely take over the spot right outside of Maraudon, at least with the Gelkis, you can always go kill air elementals and get the elemental airs to hand in. But with Magram, your only other option is to farm kodo meat, and it's much harder to get 7 kodo meats at 30% than 10 elemental airs at 80%. If there was a drop that we could get from something else, like the thunder lizards that are all over the zone, or if the kodo meat had a higher drop rate, it would make this much easier. Or just make the centaurs outside Maraudon respawn faster. But as it stands, when 60s take over the one good area in the zone, which is inevitable, there is no real alternative, we are forced to just let them farm to their hearts content for hours and hours.
Please, please consider doing something about this, I love the addition that these reputations bring to the game, and it's just such a shame to see this happening constantly. I don't mind doing a long grind but I also do feel like it's pretty unfair the way it is at the moment. Literally no one else can engage in this content when a level 60 is there killing 95% of the mobs on respawn.
