# No one wants heal to hunter pets in raids, and it causes problems for BM playstyles

## Post 145710 by Atlatrozius — 2025-08-15T14:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145710#p145710 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

No one heals pets.. okay maybe a few chads do, but basically no one does. Yes we can mend pet, but its at a bad rate so it ends up being a ton of downtime over fights where you need to do it repeatedly, it feels miserable. The new move to option helps avoid damage, but only the avoidable damage. If healers aren't going to heal pets, pets should be made easier to maintain health on in a way that does not negatively impact PvP balance. There are two changes I want to put forwards.

 1.  Pets take reduced damage boss spells and attacks while they are not the boss' target. They already take reduced damage from some AoE attacks and abilities, but this is not applied consistently.

 2.  Spirit Bond should duplicate healing effects on the hunter and apply them to the pet. This makes it so that healers who don't have pets in their raid UI (99% of them) will still get incidental healing on pets when healing raid damage.

Hunters should still have to look out for their pets, but should not be overly punished for healers never healing pets in raiding content.

I’m not in favor of blanket pet damage reduction on bosses when the pet isn’t the target. Mechanics should have teeth, and pets already bypass plenty of knockback-style effects. Let survival come from play, not passive padding.

A cleaner solution is turning Spirit Bond into a toggleable “Pet Link” in PvE. When you switch it on (on the GCD), a tunable split of the hunter’s effective incoming healing is redirected to the pet—think 70/30 or 60/40 to keep risk real, with no overheal duplication. Add a short lockout so you can’t stutter-toggle, show a clear buff icon, and auto-disable if the pet dies or leaves range. It’s agency with consequences: use it well, or you starve yourself.

For quality of life, let Mend Pet scale a bit with pet max HP and give it some pushback resistance. That reduces downtime without touching PvP or trivializing boss design.

Also… make at least one healer friend. And please don’t send your pet to “compete” with the tanks for aggro without asking—that’s foul, my dude. Be nice to tanks!
