# Quest: Kul Tiran Special Goods

## Post 59593 by Aeternusdoleo — 2023-08-28T18:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59593#p59593 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the newer quests for the Alliance side for the Kul Tiran expeditionaries has you pick up a barrel of gunpowder in Ratchet, and "Smuggled Procurements" in Theramore.

Both these items are looted from an object that then vanishes. The Smuggled Gunpowder in Ratchet reappears after seconds, but the Smuggled Procurements in Theramore are on a five minute respawn cycle. I feel both of these object should be on a short respawn cycle so groups doing these quests together don't need to wait needlessly.
