# A Silly Donation "Mount" Idea

## Post 151741 by Xidus933 — 2025-09-14T22:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151741#p151741 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is honestly just a half-serious suggestion but it's still something I think would be hilarious and wonderful to see. A donation mount that isn't a mount at all, it just makes you run at mount speeds based on riding skill -- like Plainsrunning. It should have particle effects like Plainsrunning to signify that the player is mounted, so PVPers can still visually identify their state and plan accordingly.

Imagine seeing a Gnome sprinting at you at Mach Jesus.
