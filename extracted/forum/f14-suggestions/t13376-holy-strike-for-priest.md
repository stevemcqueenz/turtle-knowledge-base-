# Holy Strike for Priest

## Post 90005 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-12T18:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90005#p90005 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it would be cool to have paladin's Holy Strike in priest's spell book. Priests don't have any melee spell even though in early versions of vanilla Tier 1 set gave priests agility so they could fight better in melee range. Early version of Inner Fire also granted attack power and Turtle WoW restored it here. The idea of battle-bishop playstyle died in next patches of the game forever. However, I think Holy Strike actually makes sense to be used by priest - they could imbue their staff with holy power to inflict holy damage. It fits lore and it wouldn't be overpowered because priest doesn't deal much damage even with the best staves in the game. So why not add some flavor?
