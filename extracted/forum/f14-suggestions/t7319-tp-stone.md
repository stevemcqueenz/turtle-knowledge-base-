## Post 46420 by Vaporizer7 — 2023-05-18T13:54:00Z

Hello,
What you guys are doing with wow is super cool ! especialy the lore respecting extension, reworked talent and your LFT addon !
The only small thing that miss to be perfect is the TP stone working like in BC; why?

Improved Convenience: Teleport Stones allow players to quickly move between different areas of the game, reducing travel times and making it easier to reach remote locations. This can be particularly useful when you dont have unlocked the region's flight. Even with the 60% mount, some trips are really long.

Increased Gameplay: Teleport Stones provide an extra option for players when they need to move around in the game. This can make the gameplay experience more fluid and enjoyable, avoiding long repetitive trips and allowing players to focus more on the aspects of the game that interest them the most. Like actualy finish the dungeon.
In the case of a new party member joining the group during the run, we loose less time waiting for him to join us.

Facilitation of social interactions: TP stones create a meeting point where groups preparing for dungeons can meet and interact.

It is RP friendly because it works like warlock's ritual summoning and it needs 2 people.

If anyone agrees with me please say so :)

With this feature, the server would be really perfect
