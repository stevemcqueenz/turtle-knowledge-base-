# Some low level quest items have a too low droprate

## Post 144006 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-09T15:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144006#p144006 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Okay, I know this is Classic, but some things seem a bit over the top to me.
Example: Harpy Lieutenants
Certain harpy mobs in the Barrens are supposed to drop a ring. You need 6. The problem? That damn ring almost never drops, and the drop rate is ridiculously low. I killed about 10 in a row, and only ONE of them dropped the ring. It's quite tedious. Now imagine if you accidentally pull an extra mob and get killed. Walking back is a pain.

I consider it a poor design decision in that and other similar quests. I remember that in Thalassian Highlands you have to collect eyes from Mana Wyrms, and those things almost never drop either.

I don't see the point of having players running around killing tons of mobs to get an item or a quest that gives you a little bit of experience.

I think increasing the drop rate to 60-70 would be more appropriate, or reducing the amount of items needed to 2 or 3 instead of 5 or 10 and saving drop rates below 50% for high level quests that will be more worthwhile than a low level quest.
