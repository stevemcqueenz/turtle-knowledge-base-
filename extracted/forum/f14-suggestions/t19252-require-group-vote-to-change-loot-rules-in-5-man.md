# Require group vote to change loot rules in 5 man

## Post 130769 by Qokaspar — 2025-05-12T20:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130769#p130769 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I and many others have experienced an increase of the (arbitrary) group leader changing the loot rules to master loot, and ninja'ing a piece of gear then gating out. Understandably, mods don't want to have to deal with this [Loot Distribution Issues](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules#loot-distribution-issues-tab-1) nor should they have to.

Turtle finder is a great resource for the server, but the position of Group Leader shouldn't have the power to change the loot rules at their whim.

My idea is that when a 5 man group leader attempts to change the loot rules, it should kick off a group vote that requires 100% yes to pass.
