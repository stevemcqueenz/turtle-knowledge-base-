<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34517#p34517 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It was pretty dissapointing for me to find out that I didn't have attack animations with a 2h weapon as a gnoll despite being able to wield them and even have a combat idle with proper 2h holding. I went looking for npc gnolls to see if any of them had a 2h attack animation, and it seems that they just don't have any. What they do have, is 1h weapon swings, so, how about making it so gnolls look like they are swingin 2h weapons with one hand? I think it would at least be better than them completelly ignoring the weapon in their hand and punching you instead, which is what they currently do.

I really like to play 2h dps like warrior, paladin and shaman, and it bums me i can't look good doing it as a gnoll.

I understand the disappointment.  Many of the illusions don't have complete animations because the game itself doesn't have certain animations for those models (for example, kobolds have no animation to ready or use a bow or crossbow).  [This post](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18183#p18183) goes over the illusions and what animations are available to them, albeit it is a bit outdated.  Worgen are basically the only ones with almost complete animations, and I believe those animations were ported in from WotLK (for some reason, during WotLK, they decided to add a bunch of animations to the old worgen model, possibly because they were considering playable worgen for that expansion).
