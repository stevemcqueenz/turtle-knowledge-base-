# Immersive Loot & Abilities for Creatures

## Post 154017 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-02T10:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154017#p154017 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtle WoW team,

First of all, thank you for all the work you’ve done to make Turtle WoW such a unique and immersive experience.

I’d like to suggest a feature that could really enhance immersion and bring more life to the world:

Creatures dropping the items they actually wear/carry (instead of generic loot tables).

Abilities tied to what they carry.

This would make encounters feel more logical and immersive, and at the same time make loot more exciting, because what you see is what you can get. It would also encourage players to observe enemies more carefully before engaging.

I’ve seen this system in other projects, and it really adds to the “RPG” feeling of the game. I think it could fit Turtle WoW’s philosophy of immersion and world-building perfectly.

What do you think about this idea? Would it be technically possible on Turtle WoW?

Thanks again for considering player feedback, and for making this server such a great place to play!

## Post 154028 by Aronymous — 2025-10-02T11:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154028#p154028 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I like the idea from an immersion standpoint, to an extent. This would be cool if WoW was a game in which the enemies were varied. But, in many cases, take a Venture Co. camp for example, you've got about 3 to 4 enemy types, and as it stands, each enemy type will have a uniform appearance for the most part. That means, assuming they drop what they have, that they will repeatedly drop the same things over and over again.
If this was implemented, what they could do to offset that would be to have those repeated items be of varying grades or minorly varied types of the same weapon/armor, etc. or fundamentally change the enemies so that it isn't just 3 or 4 types of enemy, but a wide range of more individualistic enemies that wear and drop varied gear.

I like the idea of such immersion, but as I see it, it would be a large overhaul to completely revamp all enemies in the game, assuming it was done to every mob/mob type.

## Post 154032 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-02T11:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154032#p154032 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Lets start with the fact that loot is already immersive in the way it is possible to implement in a game like wow. Spiders drop legs, ichor and stuff. Humanoids drop cloth and money.

If every humanoid NPC would always drop every piece of their equipment as loot, it would be nightmare in bag management. (Like it already is in TES series)
You can add ability to "scrap" grey/white cloth items to get cloth of appropriate level, but why do you need that intermediate step? Humanoids already drop generic cloth pieces.
If you want this for t-mog, then those relevant items should be rare. There are rare cloth pieces from NPCs. They are like salt and pepper - tastes good when amount is low.
