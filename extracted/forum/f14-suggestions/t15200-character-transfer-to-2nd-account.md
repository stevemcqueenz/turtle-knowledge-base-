# Character transfer to 2nd account

## Post 103137 by J0rsche — 2024-10-16T00:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103137#p103137 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello. I main on tel'abim and would like to express my interest in the ability to transfer a character from one account onto another. The rules state,

"Sharing access to your account with others is allowed, as long as it is not used to scam or deceive other members of the community. However, you are responsible for any rule violations that occur while others are using your account. We will not reverse any character changes caused by others while using your account."

The rules also state,

"At our server, we permit the use of a maximum of two accounts at a time, with the following exceptions:,"

I wont post the exceptions, but assuming the conditions are met, I think this would be beneficial for the PvP and possibly PvE server (I only play on pvp can't speak for them). I only post the multiboxing portion to support the "what if" questions you might have about needing two accounts, because even if I wanted to use two accounts for something I technically can within the rules, so having characters split between 2 accounts it not natively a rule breaking activity or out of question request to ask for. For PvP, the population is quite low and this sometimes makes raid composition difficult to manage. Having characters we willingly share with trusted individuals for legitimate reasons would be highly beneficial to the enjoyment of the players on the server. Some of us naturally made our characters on the same account, but with the dwindling population this is becoming a concern and would help a lot.

So, please reply if you agree or disagree and why!
