# New Hardmode: FFA

## Post 4690 by Kazgrim (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2019-12-23T22:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4690#p4690 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Would the community be interested in the possibility of a new hardmode, Free For All (FFA) PvP?

I would suggest that such a mode would pique interest in new players who are looking for a PvP vanilla realm, who would normally overlook our server because it is PvE.

PvP servers have massive appeal, and are generally the preferred playstyle of players who play classic (vanilla) wow. I believe such a feature is a good opportunity to market our server to an entire new audience with something that is unique to the vanilla private server scene: FFA.

The rules of FFA are simple:

 - Free for all pvp. Horde can attack Horde, Alliance can attack Alliance, and so forth.

 - When you enable FFA on your character, you are vulnerable to anyone at all times.

 - You must choose to play FFA before reaching level 10 (To avoid unfair advantage of old versus new players)

 - FFA mode is reversible, but cannot be started again after level 10.

Questions, comments, support?
