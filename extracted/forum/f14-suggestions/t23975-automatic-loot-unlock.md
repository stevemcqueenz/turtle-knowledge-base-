# Automatic Loot Unlock

## Post 168330 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-15T19:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168330#p168330 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I wanted to suggest something small about group loot that I think most of us have run into at some point.

Sometimes someone doesn’t loot a corpse — maybe they go afk for a second, maybe they disconnect, maybe they just miss it. The problem is the body stays locked to that player for way too long, sometimes until it despawns, and the rest of the group just has to sit there waiting (imagine if it was the key to inner scholo for example).

It gets even more annoying if there’s a skinner in the group. The mob can’t be skinned because it’s still tied to the person who didn’t loot. So one inactive player basically blocks everyone else for no real reason.

It slows things down in dungeons and farming runs more than it should. It’s not a huge issue, but it happens often enough to be frustrating.

Maybe after a couple minutes — like 2 or 3 — if the player with loot rights hasn’t looted, it could automatically unlock for the group.

This wouldn’t change drop rates or loot rules or anything like that. It would just prevent runs from stalling because someone stepped away or lagged out.
