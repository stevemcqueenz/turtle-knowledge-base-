# Rework Shaman Weapon Imbue/Totem interaction

## Post 166800 by dioaffo1 — 2026-01-28T22:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166800#p166800 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I just want to start off, as a shaman main since vanilla, who has played every spec, I really enjoy the utility that shamans bring to the group, and I don't want to lose any of the fundamental functionality of the class or its totems. But there might be a way to help the shaman feel more integrated into the buffs they provide as well as allow better usage of their kit.

 **Concept:** Shaman weapon imbues provide a buff to party members in range of the shaman's totem of the same element. Windfury and flametongue: same as current party-imbue totem effects. Rockbiter: Strength of Earth effect (+str). Frostbrand: Mana spring effect (mana regen)

 **Scenario**: Melee DPS shaman is in a melee group, using windfury weapon. The shaman could place any air totem, and the totem would additionally empower party members in range with Windfury Totem. So this party could benefit from Grace of Air as well as Windfury Totem. This essentially implements the effect of old totem twisting, without requiring the Shaman to actively twist. Everybody wins.

 **Second scenario**: A fire shaman might use Flametongue weapon  and their Searing/Magma totem would radiate the Flametongue effect to party members while active. Again, the party buff remains the same, but the shaman gets to use more of their kit. This is fun for the Shaman. Everybody wins.

 **Third Scenario:** If a lightning shaman was in this party, they could forgo a weapon imbue and drop Windfury Totem instead, as usual. This is less effective, and that makes sense for a caster in a melee group. It's sub-optimal. The shaman might swap to windfury weapon just for the added buff, or flametongue, or even rockbiter. ideally though, this shaman would be in a caster group where they can benefit from group buffs there and provide Tranquil Air in turn.

This encourages raids to prioritize putting melee shamans in melee groups, caster shamans in caster groups, etc. Currently, it doesn't matter what spec you play as a shaman--it only matters what group you're in. Integrating your spec/preferred style of play into the buffs you provide is REWARDING.

 **Counterpoints**:  To mitigate concerns about power scaling associated with two air totems active at once--to be frank, these are buffs to other members of the party, primarily, since they might now get a double-totem effect for one element. If this amount of scaling is somehow unbalanced, that is an issue with other class' interactions with these buffs, not with the power level of the Shaman. Again, the biggest scaling concern here, Agi+Windfury, is the exact thing that existed when totems could be twisted. This is not uncharted territory, and TWoW thankfully removed this unfun playstyle as a mercy to Shamans, not because it was too strong.

This still doesn't fix shaman tank totem incompatibility. Shaman tanks still can't use one of their most powerful tanking totems while providing windfury for their group.  This makes tanking feel incomplete, because you're leaving so much crit and avoidance on the table to boost group DPS/rage.

It's no secret that Shamans don't want to be handcuffed to windfury totem forever, at the cost of using their kit to empower themselves and their parties in new, flexible ways that are actually relevant to their individual gameplay style and spec. Please consider this change or something similar, to help remove the windfury albatross from our shamans' necks.
