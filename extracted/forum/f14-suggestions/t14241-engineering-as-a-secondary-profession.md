# Engineering as a secondary profession

## Post 97100 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-16T09:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97100#p97100 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is a controversial one but I'm very curious about your input about this idea.
This is a suggestion but it's radical, wanna see what people think about it.

So
Eng is different from the other primary professions which are there to make money. Eng gives utility in exchange of resources. As a primary profession it kinda limits gameplay imo:
 1. If you wanna PVP, for most classes you only have 1 prof slot because engineering is required.
 2. If you wanna raid with a more min-maxer guild, you also have 1 prof slot only. Sappers and chicken are too good to give up.
 3. If you want to play certain builds without feeling handicapped (like prot pala with range pull), you have 1 prof slot again.
 4. If you wanna have 2 free ports in Azeroth, allowing getting to places fast, again, you only have 1 prof slot.

My point is that eng gives bonuses which limit your gameplay because they're so good you want those. Let's say you dream of a tauren shaman with herbalism and alchemy but you also wanna PVP. You can't do it optimally unless you give up a prof slot.

My suggestion is to make eng a secondary profession, so those 2 ports, the sapper, the grenades, the target dummies and the chicken are available to anyone who spends enough gold to level up the skill.
That way everyone could have 2 professions mostly for money making.

I 100% agree with your general sentiment OP and would like to see something along those lines implemented. So, I and many others end up in a position where we wish we could experience the fun of the new content offered by certain major professions, yet still must decline to train them. Minor profession would be the greatest way to address this without the devs having to rebalance a ton of other factors.
