# World of Woodcraft (new profession)

## Post 161991 by Dangerosul — 2025-12-08T10:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161991#p161991 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Professions in WOW have always made sense for the fantasy world of Azeroth, however i think that Woodcrafting, one of the oldest professions of humanity, wood make a lot of sense too. It surprises me that the original WOW devs didn't think of including this logical profession.

Woodcrafting is a profession with which the player can craft many types of wooden objects and weapons.
 - weapons: staves, wooden maces, wands, wooden shields, bows, crossbows, polearms, fishing poles, etc.
 - trinkets: various small sculptures acting as trinkets
 - relics: a woodcrafter wood also create shaman and druid relics (this wood be the first profession that makes such items)
 - consumables: a woodcrafter could make arrows equivalent to the gun projectiles made by an engineer
 - traps: a few types of traps that can slow or incapacitate a single target for a short duration
 - toys: wooden toys that could follow the player like a choo-choo locomotive or a wooden horse with wheels. Also a woodcrafter could make small wooden chairs that can be deployed in the world like the umbrella/BBQ toy for various RP reasons.
 - materials: we already have 2 types of wood in the game sold by general goods vendors. We could introduce various wood vendors in each large town from each zone in the world that wood sell the wood appropriate for the zone's level range. This way it wood stimulate players to travel to various zones to acquire different types of wood. This wood vendor wood also sell woodcrafting blueprints.
 - tools: a few tools wood be required for wood carving - wooden hammer and set of dowels (for more advanced recipes)
 - Woodcrafter trainer: each capital city wood have a Woodcrafter shop with 2 trainers, a wood vendor selling tools and journeyman level wood types and a work table (new 3D asset) that would be required for certain advanced recipes (like an anvil is needed for BS recipes)
 - crafting: to create a wooden object the woodcrafter wood fashion rods, blocks, planks or other types of shapes from raw wood types. Then he would combine these processed shapes with other materials (gems, cloth, leather, metals, etc) to create an object from a recipe.

The woodcrafting profession can cover a wide range of wooden objects and wood enrich the world of Azeroth a lot. I wood love to see it at some point in TWOW. Wooden't it be nice to have it?   satisfied_turtle_head
