# Add New Libram/Arcanum enchant with Spell Hit

## Post 162660 by Dingacik — 2025-12-13T13:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162660#p162660 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Everyone,
As a fellow player and enjoyer of many different classes and associated specializations that would otherwise not been possible in regular Vanilla, I have encountered a problem that many of them actually have and I actually want to play.
That is **Spell Hit chance** on many of them, in many cases the caster classes have streamlined specializations which people only play because of the given spellhit from talents, until they are geared enough to swap to other speccs (eg. Arcane Mage, Affliction Warlock...). As such I feel the must to swap to them while gearing up, even if I do not like the given playstyle, because otherwise I will have much worse experience in the PvE end game content.

My suggestion:
Add new Libram of Precision and associated Arcanum of Precision, which would enchant the Head/Legs item slot with an enchant that would give 1%  Spell Hit Chance.

 **Libram of Precision**
Quest:
Requirements(TBD): Libram of Precision; Blood of Heroes; Skin of Shadows; Nexus Crystals x2 - Suggestion

 **Arcanum of Precision**
 *Binds when picked up*
Use: Permanently adds 1% Spell Hit Chance to a leg or head slot item. Does not stack with other enchantments for the selected equipment slot.

Reason:
In my eyes, this small suggestion will help many enjoy way more specializations earlier in their journey through End Game PvE content than now. It will help other Specializations to reach Hit Caps even without sacrificing too many pieces of better gear, only because that one ring gives 1% Spell Hit and give new options for early playstyle when their Zul'Gurub Class Enchant is just not for their specialization. And main thing, players will not have to necessarily wait for pieces of gear to swap to their favourite specialization.

This is just something that I would like to see implemented, it is not too much of a big change or something that would break any sort of RolePlay experience or be too different from the vanilla experience. Physical classes have quests for increasing their weapon skill to help them with their hits. Casters need some love too.

Thank you for reading through this and for any kind of feedback.
