# Expansion of Sandmoon Village

## Post 161515 by Mistermadigan — 2025-12-04T09:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161515#p161515 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I have posted about this in the general discussion channel - [viewtopic.php?t=23009](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=23009), but I want to make a more "Dev based" pitch and my reasoning for expanding this particular area.

 1. Sandmoon Village in NE Tanaris is an underdeveloped subzone meant to act as a questing hub for Horde players and primarily serves as a location to get quests for the Zul'farrak dungeon specifically and for Tanaris generally.  As a quest hub, it meets that function.
 - Now, on the website Sandmoon Village is described as "Hidden home of a small sect of Sandfury trolls aware of their tribe's corruption".  And to this end I realize that keeping the village small fits that description.

 2. I see potential for Sandmoon Village in future development, which I think can be backed up by two quests in-game.
 - **Sandfury Redemption** - This questline sees players establish a relationship between the Sandfury and the Darkspear Trolls.  While one could argue that sending the player to fight and kill Chief Uk'roz is "assistance", it is expressed in the quest chain that there is a lot of work to be done even with the death of the Chief.
 - **Desert Voodoo** - Seer Maz'ek at Sandmoon Village tasks players to collect 3 "Ancient Loa Charms" so that he might curse the Dunemaul Ogres.  But I don't think this number is insignificant, especially going forward.

 3. **Building a Loa Shrine**
According to the entry on Loa, the Farraki Tribe have three primary Loa: Shadra, Kimbul, and Mueh'zala.  From what we see in Zul'farrak, it is clear that their worship of Mueh'zala has caused them to revive their ancient warriors - a taboo which might have been the breaking point for the Sandfury Exiles.

If the Sandfury Exiles want to make a change, they're gonna have to have a greater influence upon the Loa spirits than the priests in Zul'farrak.  This is what has led me to suggest that the Sandfury Exiles put forth an effort to build a shrine/temple at Sandmoon Village - which I think would be cool to do via player involvement.

A Shrine to Mueh'zala could be built via donations of Lifeless Stone - taking the gray vendor items as building blocks would likely be appealing to the Loa of Death.

A Shrine to Shadra would be tricky, given that there is already a major one in the Hinterlands.

A Shrine to Kimbul would also be tricky, as there is conflicting lore regarding Shirvallah - the other Tiger Loa.

 4. **Finding a new Loa**
There is Lore to suggest that the ancestral forebearer of the Gnolls was a Hyena Wild God.  Hyenas feature very prominently in the Tanaris zone and are a creature the Farraki Trolls are quite familiar with.  Perhaps Seer Maz'ek embarks on a quest to find a new Loa to act as the guide/guardian/protector of the Sandfury Exiles until such time that they can return to Zul'farrak.
 - A "Shrine" to this Hyena Loa could be constructed out of Bones and have an appearance similar to the great skeletons which can be found in the Tanaris deserts.

 5. **Importance of this Shrine going forward**
The Specifics of how the Shrine is built or to which Loa it is dedicated is not as important as the potential this site would be in coming content.  Two things in particular are of interest to the Loa:
 - Troll Warlocks.  If we get a Shrine to Mueh'zala at Sandmoon Village, it could be a site for a racial quest for Troll Warlocks to visit and explain to the Loa of Death why they think they can get away with keeping Soul Gems to themselves rather than offer them to him.  In contrast the Hyena Loa might not care about the Soul of the victim, just as long as the body was left to carrion feeders.
 - Amani Loa relations.  In the future roadmap we will find ourselves interacting with the Amani Trolls once more, and it's going to be "all hands on deck" to support their struggles against the Scourge and Elves.  Seeking the wisdom of the Loa would be a great benefit to the war effort - especially since the Amani are masters of fighting alongside their Loa.

I want to reiterate that while I think it would be cool to do a player-involved effort (Like the QIraji War Effort) to raise building materials for this proposed Shrine, if nothing else I think that the potential for this location is worth considering for the Dev Team.

This was something I was thinking about, and I guess the best explanation I can think of is that Shirvalla is the Loa of Tigers and Kimbul is more of a Lion Loa than a Tiger.  Though the description of Eraka no Kimbul is that he is a Tiger so that paints a picture of potential rivalries and overlapping fields of interest.

There is... a second option.
The questline involving collecting Shadra's venom has you consulting with Master Gadrin to learn Shadra's "True name" in order to summon her.  You find out that her True Name is Elortha no Shadra.
 - This means that it might be possible that Shirvalla is just another name for Eraka no Kimbul, and retail's lore development dropping Shirvalla to focus on Kimbul might suggest that is the case.
 - Now the summoning of Shadra in that quest does end with players killing her to collect her venom, but it's not "that" easy to permanently kill a Wild God.

So, yes - Loa are dieties with greater domains than merely being biological relations to animal species, but that is also an important aspect to it when you look at the Wild Gods: Aggaman (and the Quillboar), Aviana (and the Harpies), Malorne (and his bloody crazy family tree).
 - Most of the lore we have regarding Loa equates them with the Wild Gods, and it's most likely that the Loa were just that - Wild Gods - before they formed a connection with Trolls.  Thus, all I'm suggesting is that we consider the possibility of discovering a new Wild God and forming a new bond.
