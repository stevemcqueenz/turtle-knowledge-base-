# Broken and Ogres, new race options?

## Post 148538 by Connieg — 2025-08-27T01:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148538#p148538 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For the longest time I have wanted Ogres as a playable race in World of Warcraft retail, as one of the earliest allies of the Horde and having been recruited by Rhexxar during his Warcraft 3 campaign it has always struck me as odd that they never got the chance to be truly playable.

In the interest of fairness if the Horde get their playable race the Alliance need one as well, but out of the currently existing races of Azeroth who would fit the most? While a seemingly unconventional choice I feel like the Broken would work best as that race for the Alliance, as they do have a lore presence during Warcraft 3 and have a few important characters that could be expanded on. As for why they join the Alliance? With their scattered people cut off from the light lost within the Swamp of Sorrow they are found years after they had entered Azeroth from the Dark Portal. With attacks coming from the Horde they beg the Alliance for salvation.

When it comes to mechanics I don'[t know exactly how racial abilities would work for them, I'm sure you could come up with something like Ogres having a bit of extra health due to their size and maybe a bonus to swords for the Broken. What I am sure about is their classes.

Ogres get Warrior, Hunter, Shaman, Mage, Warlock

Broken get Warrior, hunter, warlcok, rogue, Shaman

Each of these have precedent with characters seen in the lore, maybe Priests for Ogres too but I'm not sure. Though of course there is something particularly notable with the Broken having Shamans as an Alliance race, while I understand it was the original design intention for Shamans and Paladins to remain faction specific I think that's a bit limiting and is something worth changing.

When it comes to their starting zones this is something I'm also not sure of but for entirely different reasons, space. While they could just make some islands to put these on I don't think that really works for either of them. That being said here's what I think.

Put the Ogres in a new expanded area of Durotar, honestly not sure what their starting area would be "about" exactly maybe have even more elements of their reestablished allyship with the Horde and what that means for their people's future. Their major faction leader would likely be Rhexxar again due to the Frozen Throne campaign.

Have the Broken in a new part of the Swamp of Sorrows made for much lower level characters, dealing with the effects of losing both their connection the Light that had guided them as a people for so long and their unfamiliar place as members of the Alliance. For the faction leader I'm not sure since a lot of the lore important Broken characters are in Outland.

I hope that this will in some way inspire new race inclusions in the future, whether or not it's these two specific ones I am excited to see what the future holds for Turtle WoW.
