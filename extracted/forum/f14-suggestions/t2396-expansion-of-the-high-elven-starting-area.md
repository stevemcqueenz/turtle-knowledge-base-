# Expansion of the High elven starting area

## Post 14982 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-12-29T11:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14982#p14982 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone!

I would like to suggest an expansion to the High elf starting area bringing it up to approx lvl 10 as other starting zones.
I believe there is enough space near those mountains to expand eastwards and northwards as well. In the south, close to the current dark iron dwarves, a compound cave area could be added with lvl 7-9 (+ lvl 10 boss and rare). In the mountains east of Farstrider lodge a new high elf camp in the process of being constructed (so far just tents and maybe a small command building for the leading officials).

The quests would revolve around these two areas and themes:
 1. further disrupt the dark iron dwarves´plans and kill their expedition leader
 2. Help gather materials for the new HE encampment, quests being in the east and northeast

The dark iron cave would be large enough, say an enlargened Goldtooth mine, to add around 5 quests for it (kill chains, collect items, kill boss, + a random "This begins a quest" scroll.
The gather materials would be gather quests, kill trogg raiders who stole construction materials, kill the trogg raiding leader, kill a party of turncoat Ironforge dwarves hiding out somewhere nearby (they betrayed the HE refugees and thereby the Alliance because they don't feel these long ears should be welcome to settle in *their*/dwarven territory).

I am well aware that this area would be a quite a bit smaller than a full scale Dun Morogh, Elwynn etc. However, as the HE starting area is already different from the classic ones, I don´t think it would be a problem.
The final quest would lead the player to Thelsamar thus ending the area and continuing with the greater Loch Modan.

Final remarks:
With these additions, HEs wouldn´t be forced to go to Goldshire (though it would still be an option). Instead, they could go directly to the main part of Loch Modan and continue questing there. This would, in my opinion, be very immersive, fleshing out the HEs story as refugees in a new land instead of abruptly being teleported to somewhere completely different.

Thanks for reading. I am ready for a discussion about this in the thread :-)
