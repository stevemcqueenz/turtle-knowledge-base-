# Blacksmith weapon attachment that makes weapons slower

## Post 24467 by Flab — 2022-09-06T06:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24467#p24467 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I did Uldaman today and being the only arms warrior in the group I managed to snag some 2handers and a good new chestpiece to boot. I got a Tyrant's Chestpiece, Rockpounder, and Grimlocks Charge.

I'm a Nelf Warrior (bite me) Axe specced, and Grimlocks has amazing stats for the lvl, 10 str, 15 agi and 15 stam, and it would be a very fitting upgrade if it weren't for its abhorrent attack speed of 2.70. It also seems that every polearm I've seen in the game, even up to endgame has this problem with attack speed. Where it may have great stats and good damage, but its attack speed leaves much to be desired and may get easily replaced even if it is needed by someone who may have something worse.

Imagine Vendorstrike being of some actual use after almost 20 years is all I'm saying, maybe you wouldn't be able to pop a crusader on it, but it'd at least do enough damage until you got something that you could put crusader on.

Idk how much slower an attachment like this could make the weapon from a balancing stand point, maybe half a second? But it'd be cool to have this as a little utility thing for players who find a weapon that's too quick for them.
