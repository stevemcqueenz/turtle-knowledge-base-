# Companion / mini-pet renaming

## Post 130435 by Calamitus — 2025-05-10T11:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130435#p130435 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I come to you with an idea that came to be because this week I've lost my cat to cancer and to keep a memento of him I've acquired a companion cat in game to follow me around. I know lots of people do that, I've heard that people have made their dead cats as their Palicos in Monster Hunter for exemple.

I think it would be a nice idea to have the ability, through the donation rewards, to rename our companions to what we want. Obviously there's a roleplay implication as well that would add to the tools we already have with the illusions and the likes.
