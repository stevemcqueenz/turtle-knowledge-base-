> **Norseric wrote: Tue Jul 18, 2023 6:18 am**
> I think we've gotten to the point where the oranges at 3% are just a bit too slow.

What do you mean? They're percentage based. They're always exactly the same speed completely independent from everything else (gear, buffs, etc). That's what makes them so overpowered, they scale infinitely, on top of being virtually free and incredibly easy to distribute.

> **Norseric wrote: Tue Jul 18, 2023 6:18 am**
> So I'd like to propose Grapefruits that do 5% or 6%.

That seems quite ridiculous in my eyes. Getting from completely empty to 100% health and mana in 16s is absurd. It would probably break Bloodring and lots of other PvP situations, because at 6% just 5s of combat dropping would regen 30% of your everything.
And it would further break the comfy Vanilla feeling of dungeons. Drinking times are to chill and chat with the people, plan the next encounter, calm your nerves, gather your thoughts. I think the Classic-style chainpulling is awful and even faster regen would just encourage that. Some people think it's a race to the end instead of realizing that running the dungeon with nice people *is* the goal, the core gameplay of WoW.
> **Jammyxx wrote: Tue Jul 18, 2023 6:40 am**
> This...
>
>  I don't even use them even when I have them, I use the Alterac Manna Biscuits.... Cost alot more but as a hybrid with low mana pool they fill it ALOT faster.

But isn't that an argument against an even more powerful tier of oranges? There are already alternatives. Why must every top-of-the-line consumable come for free from mages? Oranges already cannibalize cooking foods and fishing (fish tend to not offer a buff, just regen health), bandages (from first aid and pvp vendors) and dramatically change the flow of battlegrounds. It's good that they don't overtake *everything* and still leave room for other consumables.
On paper the Alterac or Enriched Manna Biscuits (at 4410 mana over 30s) give you 147 mana per second. Oranges at 3% per second become faster as long as your mana pool is larger than 4900. That already covers pretty much every caster in dungeon blues and leaves mostly just melee hybrids to even consider anything but oranges. And exactly those hybrids have larger health pools and tend to benefit from the simultaneous health regen more, so... I think oranges are plenty strong and don't need a buff or replacement. Maybe bump their duration from 25 to 30s - there are some rare situations where you have to click a second orange to completely top you off - but I don't think the convenience of buffing their regen speed outweighs the negative impact on pvp, economy and dungeon running.

It's Turtle WoW. We have time to drink   satisfied_turtle
