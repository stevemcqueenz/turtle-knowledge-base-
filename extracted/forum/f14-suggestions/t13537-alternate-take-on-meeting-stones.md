# Alternate take on Meeting Stones

## Post 91604 by Evolve (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-03-28T22:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91604#p91604 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know the consensus is that we don't want teleports to dungeons via group finder but my suggestion is to let the meeting stones act as a queue similar to LFT but with the catch that you have to go to the meeting stone to queue.
After the queue has filled a group a 1 time summon opportunity will occur for all members to use to teleport to the meeting stone and enabling the group to start the dungeon without having to wait 20-30 minutes for someone from Silithus to get to Stratholme.

This feature would only be available to those who queued up at the meeting stone and for the longer queue times it also allows you to go farm wherever you choose while waiting. I think this could be a feature used in addition to LFT rather than instead of it.

Cons:
 -Less players queue than LFT
Pros:
 -Can pre-travel to dungeon before group forms rather than wait on group members to travel after group forms
 -Can farm wherever you want while queued
