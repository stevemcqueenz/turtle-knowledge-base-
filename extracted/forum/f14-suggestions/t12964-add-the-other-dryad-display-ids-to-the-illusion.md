Hi!

Currently the dryad illusion only provides one skin/color instead of cycling through all the available colors as some other illusions do. However, beyond the default dryad skin, there are four additional skins currently in the game- frost, autumn, Stonetalon (green and orange), and corrupted. As there are a decent amount of players role-playing dryads, it would be very helpful if we could choose our "look" by having the illusion cycle through the various available skins as some others do.

The display IDs for the other dryad skins are:
Frost - 2721
Stonetalon - 2722
Autumn - 20505
Corrupt - 11714

Blue Keeper (male)- 2725
Stonetalon Keeper (male)- 2432
Corrupted Keeper (male) - 12350

Thank you!
