# Transmog options + tier 1 & 2 swapping

## Post 150092 by Pollsforgoals — 2025-09-03T18:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150092#p150092 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I’d like to make a suggestion about the use of fashion coins and adding in the option of being able to use gold to transmog items as a currency addition to fashion coins.
Fashion coins are a good concept however without hard reserving coins during normal dungeon runs it is quite difficult to transmog frequently (as I do) I’d like if you could also use a small amount of gold (5g) per item slot to transmog that way someone like me is not put into the position where I am solo farming Hate Forge Quarry alone to get fashion coins for my 4 sets of gear, and I could spend my time joining groups doing dungeons which is where I have a lot of fun as an end game player and not need to worry about fashion coin hoarding in these relaxed runs.

Suggestion number 2:
Please make tier 1 and tier 2 gear sets swappable for the different specs in Ratchet - exactly how tier 2.5 and tier 3 are in Ratchet. This would be a great change for people who have full sets of these tier1/2 pieces that are multiple different specs already rotting in the bank. I don’t believe this would negatively impact anyone in fact it would make raiding much more enjoyable so you don’t have tier 3 people soft reserving pieces of tier 1/2 for the third time trying to finish up yet another full set of those beginning tiers to fill out an off spec.

Thank you for your time I really believe these two suggestions would benefit a large number of players and make an already great version of the game that much more seamless.
