# Make PvP gear unequipable in raid instances

## Post 83536 by Tendies (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-01-03T15:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83536#p83536 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

One of the greatest improvements to PvP on Turtle would be to make PvP gear unequipable in PvE raid instances.

Why is that? Because BGs are and have always been filled with AFK'ing/non-objective playing PvE'ers who are only there to grief grind the PvP gear for use in PvE raids, and these people make it miserable for everyone around them, especially those who PvP for fun.

From my understanding this is a feasible solution and easily codeable. It could be implemented this month.

If you want a narrative reason then you can say PvP gear is made to battle members of the Horde and Alliance, none of which you find in raids.

So the huge advantages for doing this are:
- BGs will be a lot more enjoyable for those who PvP for fun.
- Much fewer AFK'ers and grief grinders in BGs resulting in a lot fewer GM tickets and GM work. Fewer conflicts between PvP'ers and GMs who don't understand PvP.
- Fewer intentional turtling AV griefs done for ranking. Rankers who are top honor standing intentionally turtle AV such that their competition cannot gain honor. This is why Turtle once had a 11 hour AV, because Savememage was ranking, he had the most honor on Horde that day so he started turtling, lying to everyone that it was about making Alliance stop queueing AV.
- PvE'ers will no longer feel "forced" (even though it's entirely optional) to do PvP to get gear for PvE. Many new PvE'ers will feel relieved for no longer having to do this.
- It would kill most demand for Chinese RMT ranking services.
Ideally it would be all gear obtained from PvP that would be unequipable in raids. But if you want to start with the low hanging fruit first then it would be to make ranking and blood ring gear unequipable in raids. AV is already a PvE BG which nobody enjoys so it can remain unchanged at first but the rep gear from there can be made unequipable later if need be. No PvE'er really comes to WSG or AB to grind rep gear, so just keep it unchanged in case where a PvP'er decides to start raiding and equips it (you can come up with narrative reasons why this is allowed).

Now I am sure there will be a lot of whining in the comments below from AFK PvE'ers who already grief grinded their rank and blood ring gear. To have that unequipable in raids is entirely deserved by how miserable they have made PvP. At least if they ever decide to enjoy PvP and play it for fun then they still have the gear they once grinded.
