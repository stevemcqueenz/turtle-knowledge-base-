I am currently enjoying my Lv 30 Shaman and started the wind totem quest.
Which sends you to a shaman in thousand needles and... ends there. She just gives it to you. No work, no elemental, nothing. Really disappointing. - Note: I do not know if there is anything else in Twow, but since the air totem quests is still the same, I do not expect any other quests.

Anyway:

I really enjoy class/ race specific quests. The Warlock for example gets to do things almost every 10 levels if not more.

Maybe earning a Doomhammer or something like that?

Earning an elemental companion?
