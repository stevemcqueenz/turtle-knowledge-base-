## Post 32342 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-01-09T15:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32342#p32342 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

+ X arcane/fire/nature etc on weapons does not scale with spellpower. Im not 100% sure on the coding but it essentially makes a part of the damage ignore armor. not sure how it interacts with resitences etc.

The reason why Blade of Eternal Darkness and ONLY Blade of Eternal Darkness does work is because it triggers after spellhit. Extra elemental damage that is dealt as its own damage instance (Such as Incendosaur set storm gauntlets or crimson spellblade) are considered spells by the game.

Therefore they proc the "on spell hit" effect of Blade of Eternal Darkness.

These damage procs themselves do not scale with spellpower. But Frost/Shadowoil, Dragonbreath Chilly and The Shadowbolt from the Dagger do scale.

And you are releasing a ton of shadowbolts since you proc a fuckton of these elemental damage instances. Does work decently well and you won't ever have mana problems but it gets neutered hard by fire immune mobs.
