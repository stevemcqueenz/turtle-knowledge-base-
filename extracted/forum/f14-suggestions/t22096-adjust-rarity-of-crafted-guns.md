# Adjust rarity of crafted guns

## Post 153247 by Frankokor — 2025-09-26T01:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153247#p153247 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, if you're playing a character who has both the **Vagrant's Endeavor** and **Traveling Craftmaster** challenge, you are unable to equip (For warriors) or upgrade (For rogues and hunters) any sort of ranged equipment. There are no craftable bows, crossbows or thrown weapons. Guns are craftable and can be found in the engineering skill, but for some weird reason, *even the most basic lvl 5 weapon is uncommon quality.*

I propose to adjust the rarity of the **three lowest level** craftable guns, changing them from uncommon to common quality. This would apply for the following weapons:

- [Rough Boomstick](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4362) (level 5)
- [Deadly Blunderbuss](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4369) (level 16)
- [Lovingly Crafted Boomstick](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4372) (level 19)
This puts your best possible ranged weapon at around the same level as the best possible melee weapon you could craft and use with blacksmithing (this being the [Bronze Battle Axe](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=7958) at level 22). This change would allow warriors to finally field a ranged weapon and hunters & rogues to craft an upgrade for their starting weapon.
I see no glaring drawbacks from applying this change, and I do not think this goes against the spirit of either challenge. At the very least, adjusting the rarity of the rough boomstick only would be a really nice change for warriors.

Please consider this suggestion!
