Hello Turtles,

Just as druids have glyphs to chance their models, I wondered if it would be realistic to have hunter pets with custom models as well. Nothing that would make it unclear what kind of pet it is (such that it may confuse other players in PvP), but models of pets that are normally not tamable, such as those found [here](https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/gallery.php?id=unavailable).

The same could be applied to warlocks' demons. I know imps have a fair number of models available - not sure about other demons though.

Let me know your thoughts.   smiling_turtle
