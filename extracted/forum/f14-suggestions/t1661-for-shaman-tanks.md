I don't disagree that shaman lacks sustainable AOE, especially as melee, but..

As a tank shaman, I think, you'll never cast chain lightning mid combat or too often pull with it. mark skull, spam bolts rank1 to proc clearcasting until the mob is in melee range and you start stabbing (fast weapon+rockbiter, rockbiter threat is flat). If you really want to excel with AOE threat you use engineering stuff life advanced target dummy, goblin sapper charge, bombs or ramstein lightning bolts trinket.
