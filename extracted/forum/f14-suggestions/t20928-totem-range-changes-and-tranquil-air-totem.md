This is a highly specific thing and I don't think it will be realistically done but I wanna highlight this.
Raising beneficial totem base range to 30 yards is good.
But there is a very specific case it is harmful.
When you are tanking, mostly 5 men dungeons and leave a tranquil air behind, because there is a big pumper caster and this is the best way to use the air totem slot.
A very specific tweak: 'tranquil air totem range doesn't change if the shaman who summoned it has rockbiter on' would solve this but it's so specific I doubt it gets implemented.
