# Hunter and Warlock pets!

## Post 54173 by Pestopasta — 2023-07-20T23:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54173#p54173 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle WoW has already made small changes to help out warlock/hunter pets, from the avoidance passive skill                to adding new items (Breastplate of Beast Mastery, Charm of Dark Domination etc).
This shows that the Turtle WoW team does care about pets, and understands that they need help.
Unfortunately these items have done almost nothing.   scared_turtle_head

So I have a small suggestion to help pets some more:

Why not add the item shown below into the game?
(This is a real item and was added in tbc, but the npc it is named after is already in the game in burning steppes)
![Image](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcS4lK4XEx_48JI4HBRLgXc6_8-i5wjPBRXkpDuIYhVFA42jufHymQ0OrMGJ3I7jQ0GMCzM&usqp=CAU)

As the npc "Kibler" is already in the game, it could be sold by him or given as a reward for completing a quest.
Strength and Spirit are useless stats for the warlock's imp though, so either changing the stats or making a similar item with intellect would be a good idea.

(Personally I think it should grant hit% and spellhit% to your pet, as this is something that is very much needed if class pets are to be of any use in raids.)

Thanks for reading! I might post some more suggestions regarding pets in the future since I have been theorycrafting BM/Demonology in vanilla for a very long time now and have many ideas to share. Let me know your thoughts!
