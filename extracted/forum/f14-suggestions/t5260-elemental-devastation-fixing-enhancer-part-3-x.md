# Elemental Devastation - Fixing Enhancer Part 3/x

## Post 31923 by Thegr8equalizer — 2023-01-01T20:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31923#p31923 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, I would like to suggest a tiny talent change, for a talent that is only used for enhancement spec. **This is the talent "Elemental devastation".**

here are my suggestions:

 1) The talent is too low down Ele tree and requires too many talent points to sacrifice to be getting as enhancement and it is completely useless for any other spec simply because only enhancement shamans actively melee attacks their target more than once ever 30 seconds.

My first suggestion is simple, **move the talent up from 4th row to 3rd OR alternatively 2nd row**. By doing this you open more possibilites for enhancement spec to **either boost their personal performance and dps by going deeper in Ele tree or boost their support performance by going deeper in resto tree.**

 2) **The talent should be working both way, to emphasize the hybrid side of enhancement**. It would also be extremely flavourful to have a talent that completely synergize with enhancement gameplay style. My suggestion is the following:

"Elemental Devastation" - 3/2nd Row - 3 talent points: **Your offensive spell crits will increase your chance to get a critical strike with melee attacks by 3%/6%/9% for 10 sec. Additionally, your critical strikes with melee attacks and abilities will increase your chance to get an offensive spell crit by 3%/6%/9% for 10 sec.**

This is very textbook spellhancer change. Because enhancement shaman is so limited with gear to get decent spell crit and hit chance, **this is effectively a very balanced way to increase enhancement shaman spell crit chance by 9%.** It also scales extremely well with gear since it is completely related to your melee crit and hit chance.

Thanks for reading my suggestion, please give me some feedback.  :)

I like the idea of it being a two way support where criting with melee makes your spells better while criting with spells makes your melee attacks better.

But there is some contention here. Having more reliable spellcrits in PvP might be a sensitive topic. I personally think its not pushing shaman into overpowered territory, but if we get 9% spellhit instead of spellcrit after proccing it with a melee crit we have a very potent talent to drastically improve his shocks. But still tied to enhancement in a way that is not abusable by elemental.

Spellhancers could even forgo the 10% weapon damage and get the Natures guidance alongside eye of the storm. Making it significantly easier to gear for spellhancer.
