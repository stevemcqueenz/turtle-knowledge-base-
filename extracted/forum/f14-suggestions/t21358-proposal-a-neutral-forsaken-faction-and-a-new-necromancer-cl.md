# Proposal: A Neutral Forsaken Faction and a New "Necromancer" Class

## Post 148041 by bitisens064 — 2025-08-24T17:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148041#p148041 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, friends and developers!

I'd like to share an idea that I believe fits perfectly into the Classic+ concept and could make the world of Turtle WoW even more unique and exciting.

As we know from the lore, the Forsaken joined the Horde for pragmatic reasons. Their true loyalty has always been to themselves and their Banshee Queen. On a server that already supports cross-faction gameplay, it would be logical to expand on this storyline and allow the Forsaken to become a completely neutral faction.

This move would not only deepen the lore but also open up a wealth of new opportunities for gameplay and the server's economy.

An Exclusive Class: The Necromancer
For this new faction, I propose introducing an exclusive class: the Necromancer. This would be an excellent way to balance the unique classes of the Alliance (Paladin) and the Horde (Shaman).

Unlike other classes, the Necromancer wouldn't rely on high damage alone. Their strength would come from tactics and commanding their army. Their damage without pets would be at a moderate level, similar to a Paladin's, which would emphasize their reliance on summoned creatures.

Key features of the class:

Two Pets Simultaneously: The Necromancer could summon two creatures at once, allowing the player to create unique combinations for different situations.

Unique Mount: At level 40, the Necromancer would receive a class-exclusive mount: the Bone Steed, raised from the ground.

Pet Variety:

Skeleton Warrior: A classic pet-tank that can absorb damage.

Skeleton Healer: A unique pet capable of healing the Necromancer and other summoned creatures.

Ghoul Scoundrel: An agile pet that can inflict high burst damage with surprise attacks.

Plague Ghoul: A weak but dangerous pet. Upon death, it would release a swarm of worms that deal damage to enemies.

Talent Trees
I propose three talent trees, each with its own specialization:

Plague: A tree focused on damage-over-time (DoT), diseases, and curses to weaken enemies.

Lord of the Dead: This tree would focus on empowering pets and summoning new, more powerful creatures. It would also allow the Necromancer to use dark spells for direct damage.

Rotting Apothecary: This specialization would focus on support. The Necromancer could heal their allies (and themselves) with vile concoctions and curse enemies, making them vulnerable.

I believe this concept fits perfectly with Turtle WoW. It would not only add a new, unique class but also explore a long-simmering story conflict, providing players with a brand new experience.

I hope you'll consider this idea!  turtle_in_love
