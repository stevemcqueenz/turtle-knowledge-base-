# Request: Make it so Swiftness of the Rangers also gives +2% Intelligence, and reduce Quel'Dorei's meditation's cast time

## Post 164262 by Familymaker — 2026-01-02T15:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164262#p164262 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As the title says, The High Elf racials feel the most underwhelming out of all the races, even as they are very thematic.

I understand that Agility is a strong stat, but perhaps Swiftness of the Rangers could also give +2% Intelligence for the High Elf spellcasters? They are supposed to be a magically gifted people after all. Plus, only 2% will mean that they will not be stepping on the foot of the Gnomes, while still feeling like they're a race adept at spellcasting.

Also, the casting time of Quel'Dorei Meditation feels too long for what it provides. You don't have to increase the amount, as I don't want their racials to overshadow those of the others. But giving it a 0.5 sec casting time instead of the current 1.5 seconds (so like the Tauren's War Stomp) would help a lot! Without making it too strong.
