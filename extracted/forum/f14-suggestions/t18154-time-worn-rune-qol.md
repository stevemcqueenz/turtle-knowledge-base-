# Time-worn Rune QoL

## Post 123558 by Tootdust — 2025-03-24T11:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123558#p123558 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

The Time-worn Rune teleport to caverns of time adds a degree of annoyance for no real benefit.
If you're on your last charge you're forced to run down to the seller to buy a new one or use the flight mechanic which can get stuck and you just spin at the entrance for 35 seconds each time it fails, which is regularly.
As one of the few meaningful ways for an alliance to port onto kalimdor side plus it being behind a reputation grind, albeit a small one, i think this annoyance could be removed.

My suggestion would be on of the following:
A) Make one of the 3 npcs at the entrance also sell the time worn rune
B) Allow mounts within the caverns of time if possible to speed up getting to the seller
C) Raise its reputation requirement to honored and make it unlimited charges
D) Leave it as friendly but massively increase the cost and slightly increase the cooldown (20g / 30 minutes)

Not sure what others think but as previously mentioned, at the moment its just an unneccessary annoyance every 5 charges.
