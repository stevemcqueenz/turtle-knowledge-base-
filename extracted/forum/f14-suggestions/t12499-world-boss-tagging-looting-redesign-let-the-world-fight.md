# World Boss tagging+looting Redesign: Let the world fight!

## Post 83205 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-12-31T01:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83205#p83205 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are a LOOOT of suggestions about wboss redesigns, and they mostly suck.
"pump up their spawn rates" See how well that worked with snowball?
"just stuff them into instances" EW CRINGE
"let people spawn them with expensive/timeconsuming reagents" Kinda based, silithus lords are like this

But for true, big boy world bosses, I think we can do better. My proposal:

Lore speaking, if a world entity is threating Azeroth or at least a good chunk of it, the entire realm bands together to defeat it. Lets show this through gameplay.

When a world boss is about to spawn from their once a week or so spawn timer, some italicised lore style message is sent to all lv 60s in broadcast chat, indicating that a threat looms in a certain zone and that a world boss will spawn there in 15 mins. Hundreds of players will of course all dog pile into the zone and band to face the world boss.

Unlike the normal wow mob tagging system, the world boss will be tagged to the entire faction (to both factions on Nord). All will be welcome to fight against the boss and recieve credit. We can have multiple full raids, some 5mans, some solo players and plenty of other shapes and sizes of player groups all coming to fight the boss, who will probably have 5 to 10x the HP pool they do right now as we might regularly see hundreds of players online to hop in.

If the boss dies, the server will have tracked how much damage was done by the various player raids groups and solo players. A simplified example:
 -We have 5 groups that participated.
 -A 30man, a 20man, a 5man, and two 1mans.
 -The 30man did 60% damange. the 20 did 30%. the 5 did 9%. the two 1mans did 0.5%
 -The game rolls the dice. if it lands 1-60 it goes to a roll within the 30man. if it rolls 61-90 it goes to the 20man to then be rolled. etc
 -Once the roll goes into within a party, a message will be sent to the group that won, and the items will follow the party's rules, be they group loot, master loot, whatever.

Concerns with this proposal:
"What if a 5man or 1man wins it by luck? And they have no use for the specific items that dropped?"
I actually think this is a good time to make this loot BOE. Because of how incredibly rare these pieces of loot are per server, I expect them to fetch extreme prices, much more of equivalent craftables and farmables. So even if a large group or a 1man wins the drops and they can't personally use them, well, everyone uses *money.*

"Ostarius and Nerubian loot is too good to be BOE!"
I agree, all of that loot needs nerfing down. But I have suggested that anyway, stuff was way too OP for the effort before, and bis should never be exclusive to wbosses anyway.

"If the chance to receive a drop is based on damage done, doesn't this punish groups for healing and MTing?"
Naturally, this does push groups to only take as few healers as they need, and let the rest go DPS, and the healers that do heal would be wise to only heal their own group. Yes, the group with the MT will be taking a loss, but in a 40man thats just means you are down 0.5man of DPS, it's not much really.

"How will this shake down on TelAbim?"
On Tel'Abim, the fact the rewards are BOE moneymakers will result in some CRAZY pvp, as players will want that sweet reward, and they are now empowered to kill a lot of players who would try and take that from them. If the tagging is done per faction as I propose, It'll stop only once one faction vastly outnumbers the other, which can very well have a snowballing effect if one faction always ends up dominating... that might very well kill the PVP altogether.

In response to that, I am toying around with the idea of the tagging being done xfaction even on PVP realms, perhaps to encourage even a minority faction to still stick their face into the fight and do as much boss damage as they can to put themselves up for a chance at getting the drops. This would be annoying for the times the underdogs snipe a winning 1% chance as a minority faction, but I suppose this annoyance is better than no PVP at all!

"Kazzak has a 3min timer, how do you handle that with 5-10x HP"
This one is interesting, just like right now you need to amass 20 naxx geared or 40 pug players to get a good chance on beating enrage, you would need to amass 5x 40mans to beat 3mins. On Telabim PVP, this might be a problem due to lower pop. IDK, we'd have to have some sophisticated method of scaling the wboss HP based on the server's logged metric of how many unique lv 60s had logged in per week metric (of both factions for Nord, biggest faction for TelAbim)

"Why try to fix what isn't broken?"
The 1.12 system is objectively broken and against the spirit of Vanilla being cooperative and epic. It punishes the small guys and feedback loop rewards the winners. It was really really lame when old TABC was the old mafia, and it's still really lame with the new mafia. This content is 100% dead for a huge percentage of the playerbase.

"Is this too complex for vanilla?"
Vanilla does need to remain simple, but only simple enough to quickly explain to the layman, and I think this works here. "Hey, worldboss is in town. Show up do damage get some faction rep get your cut of the loot drop gold." You don't even need to tell them that they might maybe win some rare items.

So yeah guys, what do you think?

## Post 83365 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-01-01T22:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83365#p83365 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

But let's try and come up with something that has the *potential* of making world bosses available for more people:

 1) Put them in spawn groups.
Every spawn cycle 4-5 bosses should spawn at the same time. This forces camping groups to pick and choose because they can't be everywhere at once. In an ideal world the campers get 2-3, other established guilds get 1-2, and 1 gets PUGed. Spawn groups alone are not enough to make this happen given the size of the camping groups.

 2) Put them on character-based cooldowns.
You defeat a world boss, you get a 7-day debuff "World Boss Slayer". If you engage a world boss while you have the debuff, you get instantly killed because the world boss knows you're dangerous (I don't know, hard to justify lore-wise). Also has the neat side-effect that if you've killed a world boss recently you can't grief other people doing them. This is mechanically similar to getting locked to raid instances for the week - you can pick whatever world boss you want, but only one per week. Even that is probably not enough to level the playing field as people in incredibly farmed guilds tend to have many raid-geared alts.

 3) Increase the spawn rates.
If we get 4-5 bosses spawning 3-4x a week and killing one locks you out of all the others we *might* be at a point where campers just run out of a characters and world bosses remain alive for a few hours so other raids (including PUGs) can organize attempts. The cooldown above makes sure to prevent a Snowball-effect where the same people would farm a fast-spawning boss over and over.

 4) Consider mechanics that enforce orderly "taking turns"
Similar to the cooldown mechanic proposed above - if you've engaged a world boss and died you get a 1h debuff that kills you if you get near the same world boss again (similar-ish to what Azuregos does, but longer lasting). Not all bosses drop the same quality of loot, so it seems likely that camping groups would lock down the juiciest bosses forever. Those are also the bosses with a bit of a learning curve, making it doubly-hard for other raids to get solid attempts. But the farmers might still wipe once in a while? So if they really want that particular boss, they have to wait for their turn and give 2-3 other groups a fair shot at learning and killing the boss. But they can always go kill another boss instead (but only 1 in total because of the 7-day cooldown).
Note: This would have to be part of a larger rework so a single griefer can't put an entire raid on a 1h lock-out and that a raid seeing a loss coming can't drag the boss over the next raid waiting to lock them out as well.
