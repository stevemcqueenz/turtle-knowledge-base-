# Fixing PvP in several easy, tested steps

## Post 67021 by Vaikaris (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-26T18:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67021#p67021 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So we were talking in the newcomer guild in the guild chat about PvP and the GM suggested I post my ideas here.

When I say "ideas" I mean things that various pservers and now retail classic have implemented, tested and are proven to work in many cases. I will classify the ideas from easy/obvious to hard/controversial so you can discuss them piece by piece.

 ***Easiest/most obvious:***

 **1)** Wider middle brackets. Currently on twow the honor grind is **beyond absurd** around ranks 6 to 10. It should be obvious why, but I will explain anyway. Rank 10 gear is best defined as starting gear and RP gear. That said, you're unlikely to ever get much of a use out of it, because by the time you've got r10 you'll likely have exalted with the blood ring, which in most cases is equal or better. Getting rank 10, therefore, earns you next to nothing. It is something, let's say, a casual player or an RPer would desire. However, at this point, r10 requires a hardcore, heavy grind nonstop for months. Not just that, it requires you never take a break, because the decay (more on that later) will just grind YOu down. I just passed rank 5 and playing pvp for about 3-4 hours a day, with 5-6 on AV bonus day gives me about 80% of the rank - at r5 and 6. Not even at 9. This is NOT normal. r10+ is a different story, the gear is elite with some BiS and I feel like its right where its at, even if its harder than vanilla/classic. If you widen the brackets in the middle to double or more, r10 will be achievable with 2-3 hours a day, but consistently, which is the correct time requirement for someone who wants r10 gear. The r14 grind, itself, is not in any way cheapened - as anyone ranking to 14 MUST be in the top 0,2 (and 0,5% for 13) and therefore will blitz to r10 in 3 weeks anyway or he can never get 14.

 ***Easy/obvious:***

 **2)** I don't know how hard this is, but I've seen tons of servers do it, so - mercenary mode or cross-faction pvp for some BGs and/or a random bg queue. Immersion breaking or not, pvp turns off newcomers and casuals a lot, as most matches in WSG and AB are one-sided domination. AV too. If you randomize factions a little (even with just merc mode) and BGs if possible, you virtually remove this issue.

Bonus point - merc mode can only be available to people who have had warmode turned on for a while (turning off warmode can give you a debuff that prevents you from doing it). That way pvp players are incentivized to keep warmode on at 60. Both factions benefit.

 **3)** Remove RANK decay. Honor can decay - but only until your current rank and you cannot derank. This was implemented in classic recently and is an almost universally loved fix. I don't even know what I need to explain here - rank decay is stupid, deranking is stupid. Forcing you to rank in 1 go or not at all is stupid. Forcing you to spend a week making up for every week you took a break is stupid. Having to play 7 days a week nonstop or you LOSE progress is stupid. There is no mechanic that makes you progressively lose t1/t2/t3 if you dont step into mc/bwl/naxx once a week, why is there one for pvp? Even if you widen the brackets its still stupid. On an RP point of view its also stupid, because Commander Isellgold cannot just randomly go back to being a corporal with 20k enemies killed. But I digress. And again, you can separate r10 to r14 if you want, it makes sense that the r10>14 grind is brutal and stopping brings you down, but that said, you also don't need to. Its only technically 3 ranks that need to be brutal and if you stop to take a break and lose 20% of your progress in one, but don't derank, its still horrifying for your grind.

These 3 things will, in my opinion, fix the pvp system that is scuffed from the current state of the server. None of them also impact the ease/difficulty anyway, as they just change the scaling, enabling casuals to do the casual part. Merc mode can remove some immersion and be controversial to some who prefer stomp meta, but I think most see the need for it.

 ***Hard/controversial:***

 4) I assume most people on this forum are the dedicated players (casuals dont check forums, of course) so I might get a lot of hate for even suggesting it, but do keep in mind, this is a wild idea anyway. A bolster/debolster system for lower or higher gear. Higher stamina or more damage for lower gear just doesnt work, since the problem is somewhere around t2.5 you reach one-shot territory and that's not that great, so basically it'd be a flat damage % increase or decrease. I'm not a specialist and it'd need extensive testing, but to just put it out there, maybe someone likes it - t3 gear comes with a flat, lets say, 10% debuff of damage. Or r10/the arena gear come with a flat 10% decrease in enemy damage. OR you place a cap - weapon damage is capped around the t2 weapons (perhaps the r14 weapons are exempt? or they're the cap?) and spell damage is capped around what you'd get with t2 gear on your class or something similar. That means you're still going to enter one-shot territory with the best gear, but you won't get to that point where you're slapping out one-shot shadowbolts/lightning bolts like a chaingun.

Again, that one is a wild idea, not a necessary fix, I just wanted to include it for the sake of discussions. Please don't dismiss the entire post because you have MoM or t3 gear and you don't like it.

That's it. I might have missed some stuff but will edit out if necessary.
