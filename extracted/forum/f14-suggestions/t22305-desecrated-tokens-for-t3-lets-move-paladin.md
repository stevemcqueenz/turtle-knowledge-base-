# Desecrated Tokens for T3, lets move paladin?

## Post 154822 by Aleyar — 2025-10-08T09:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154822#p154822 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So Ive been thinking about the Tier 3 tokens, and the way they’re split feels a bit off with how the game works nowadays.

Right now its:
A: Priest, Mage, Warlock  ( 3 )
B: Paladin, Hunter, Shaman, Druid  ( 4 )
C: Warrior, Rogue ( 2 )

Back in original Vanilla this made sense. Warriors and Rogues were everywhere, so they had their own token. Paladins and Shamans were faction-locked, so each side only had 3 classes competing on the same token. Casters sat neatly in their own group of 3.

But here in Twow Pallies and Shamans are in the same raids. and also highly populare classes due to the great changes done to them ( especially paladins)

How about we move the paladin over to the same Tier tokens as Warrior/Rogue?  Seeing that they also use the Plate scraps alongside of Warriors?

I suppose this has been suggested before but seeing the popularity of Shaman/paladin its prime time to spread it more out evenly. 4 on one type and 2 on the other makes no sense. Splitting the three tokens on three classes each just makes more sense now that we have cross faction raids.

It would make gearing less painful for people stuck on the overcrowded token, and every group would have a fairer shot at it. Honestly I cant see any reason why not doing such a change except for well.. the work in implementing. Changing the token items, changing the quests. But as a quality of life and general improvement to the T3 raid experience I think it would be worth it?
