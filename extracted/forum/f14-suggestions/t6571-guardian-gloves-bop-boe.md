# Guardian Gloves BoP->BoE

## Post 41426 by Jannus — 2023-04-13T17:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41426#p41426 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi guys,
A short preface, the [Guardian Gloves](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=5966) are one of those items that in classic WoW have [no stats](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=5966/guardian-gloves) and are merely crafted by leatherworkers to gain skill and advance into their leatherworking.
On Turtle WoW, these same gloves give you Intellect and 3 mp5, which is great for casters.

At the current time, for some reason, these are the only item from the 'guardian' set that are for some reason set to Bind on Pick, which means the item will be bound to the leatherworker as soon as it is crafted.

My suggestion is to change their bind status from Bind on Pick to Bind on Equip.

Thank you for coming to my TED talk.

Edit: Not to mention they are a reagent for a blacksmithing craft, which means the blacksmith has to be a leatherworker, should they wish to craft a pair of [Truesilver Gauntlets](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=9954)
