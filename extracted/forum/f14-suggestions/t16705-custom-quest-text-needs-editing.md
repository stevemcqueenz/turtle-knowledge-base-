# Custom Quest Text Needs Editing

## Post 114105 by lizpips — 2024-12-24T07:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114105#p114105 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there. I've only just discovered this version of WoW and, as someone who has always felt that TBC onwards was a poor continuation of the Warcraft storyline/lore, I love the ideas presented in this project. There are some great ideas with the new zones and races.

However, the dialogue writing in the custom quests is in quite poor english, often misusing words (such as the confusing use of "upstart" many times in the goblin intro, or the Horde quest that says (of the wyverns): "I remember the days when we first *ensued* the help of these beautiful creatures". This is only the most recent one I've noticed, but the problem is unfortunately *severely* common. Its certainly hard to buy into the world with these constant writing errors and, as time goes on, I find it strongly affecting my enjoyment of the questing experience. I also find a lot of the dialogue to not syntactically hit the mark of quality that I'm used to in WoW.

It would vastly improve enjoyment of the game for players, like myself, who enjoy running around, doing quests, and taking in the world if there was an editing team to fix up some mistakes and deliver some much needed polish in the dialogue. I want so badly to love this version of WoW that I'd easily volunteer time to go over some pages of dialogue and submit thorough suggestions for edits.
