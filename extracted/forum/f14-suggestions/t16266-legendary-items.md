# Legendary Items

## Post 110840 by Neechy (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-01T21:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110840#p110840 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have some thoughts/concerns/suggestions now that turtle is at the stage of introducing new legendaries.

 1. I hope that the devs do not carried away with adding too many legendary items. I really think that legendaries are what can make or break a server and they should be both limited in number and not gamebreaking power level.

 2. Would prefer that they stick to weapons and shields rather than other item slots. Vanilla had only 3 legendaries and they were all weapons - it made them feel more special. This approach continued all through wrath and worked fine. Sure vanilla had a legendary neckpiece but that was removed early on, which was a good move. I would hate to see legendary items added to a bunch of item slots and characters able to just start stacking legendaries.

 3. Scythe of Elune as a trinket? [Please reconsider](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118290#p118290).. I think that many were surprised to see this and were expecting a weapon instead. Personally I would have loved to see a MH dagger for casters that INCLUDES SHAMANS. Why were they left out again? Please reconsider this as well. They already can't get Thunderfury or Atiesh. Anyways, a caster dagger right after Atiesh staff would not be bad at all because it's an alternative equip, it can't be used in combination. As it stands currently casters can have legendary staff + legendary trinket at once, which I foresee as being too OP. The stats on the scythe are already insane...

 4. Don't allow legendaries to lose their relevance. With the current three legendaries: Atiesh is of course bis for respective classes since it comes in Naxx, but the other two Thunderfury and Sulfuras run the risk of becoming obsolete. Thunderfury is still the best tank weapon but with new raids coming TF's proc will not be enough to make up for the increased damage and stats of new higher ilevel items. And Sulfuras is of course a joke fairly quickly and outclassed by basically all Naxx+ weapons.

 5. My suggestion to point #4 would be to introduce quests that ensure legendaries maintain relevance. For example, a new legendary grade resource (e.g. Flame of the Red Dragonflight) drops in the upcoming Grim Batol raid as part of a long quest line that can strengthen Sulfuras to "Sulfuras, Reforged Hand of Ragnaros" increasing its damage and stats to become more competitive with the evolving end game. A different quest and item could boost TF to "Thunderfury, Double Blessed Blade of the Windseeker" too boost its stats but also its name length for even greater memeability. This approach can be used for other legendaries as needed. Assuming that TWoW ends at T5 in Northrend for example, my dream would be that all legendaries are still relevant by then and not massively outclassed by whatever generic purple is introduced at that time.

Interested to hear everyone else's thoughts on this.

Thinking more about power creep and how the Kara40 weapon leaks are showing weapon dps already hitting a whopping 100.0. This dps increase is actually relatively consistent with the pattern between raid tiers (see below). The issue I foresee is Kara40 / T3.5 using the benchmark of Might of Menethil (MoM), which is honestly a pretty overtuned weapon. Vanilla devs probably made it as such because Naxx was the final raid of vanilla wow at a time when very few people ever got to clear it before it became more or less obsolete at TBC release.

So with this in mind, there a couple paths that could be taken...

 **Option 1 (our current path): Continue to introduce new weapons more powerful than MoM benchmark**

Comparison of the DPS of the the top 3 DPS weapons in vanilla / currently available:
 -Might of Menethil (MoM): 95.3 dps (Naxx / T3 raid last boss)
 -Corrupted Ashbringer: 90.0 dps (Naxx / T3 raid)
 -Dark Edge of Insanity: 86.57 dps (AQ40 /T2.5 raid last boss)

And a few other big ones for reference:
 -Ashkandi: 81.81 dps (BWL / T2 raid last boss)
 -Bonereaver's Edge: 75.88 (MC / T1 raid last boss)
 -Severance: 81.81 dps (Naxx / T3 raid)

Some notes:
 -T1 -> T2 sees a 5.93 dps increase in the most powerful weapons for those raid tiers
 -T2 -> T2.5 sees a 4.76 dps increase (half-tier increase)
 -T2.5 -> T3 sees a 8.73 dps increase (half-tier increase)
 -T2 -> T3: 13.49 dps increase
 -T1 -> T3: 19.42 dps increase

Now obviously power increase between raids does not need to be perfectly linear. But nonetheless power creep has become a very common concern on this server. So perhaps it is time to do some stat squashing, rather than necessarily having to buff the world (remember players are supposed to revolve around the world, not the other way around).

And as far as stat squashing to make a more sustainable trajectory for weapon power progression, I think Naxx gear is where it starts to get particularly notable.

So, going back to the top 3 most powerful 2H-ers currently, what if:
 -Dark Edge of Insanity: 86.57 -> 84.57 dps
 -Corrupted Ashbringer: 90.0 -> 88.0 dps in line with the unreleased legendary version
 -MoM: 95.3 -> 90.3 dps

Then maybe Kara/T3.5 top weapon only needs to hit 95.0 dps (reserving the big 100.0 dps milestone for T4 instead).

 **Option 2 (conservative path): Keep MoM benchmark as the power cap for all future weapons**

I.e. 95.3 dps benchmark of current MoM would be the approximate power cap for end-game weapons whether that be T4 or T5.

How could this be achieved? By having a +5.0 dps budget max for weapons between raid tiers, and thereby +2.5 dps budget max for half tiers. So it looks something like:

T1: Bonereaver's Edge: 75.88 (MC last boss)
T2: Ashkandi: ~80.0 dps (BWL last boss)
T2.5: Dark Edge of Insanity: ~82.5 dps (AQ40 last boss)
T3: Might of Menethil (MoM): ~85.0 dps (Naxx last boss)
T3.5: [Most Powerful 2Her]: ~87.5 dps (Kara40 last boss)
T4: [Most Powerful 2Her]: ~90.0 dps (Grim Batol last boss)
T4.5: [Most Powerful 2Her]: ~92.5 dps (Barrow Deeps last boss)
T5: [Most Powerful 2her]: ~95.0 dps (Northrend raid last boss)

What is good about this approach is that we will already know what the max power creep for players would be by final game, since we are using MoM benchmark as the absolute cap. So MoM gets nerfed, and T5 end-game weapon would be around as powerful as what MoM is now. And then weapons never have to break the 100.0 dps threshold.

These minimal increments may seem less appealing to players but you can bet your ass off that players are still going to raid increasingly more challenging content for an extra +2.5 weapon dps.

And this way, with this amount of power squashing it keeps early raids relevant. If T5 weapon is only 10.0 dps more than T3, then there is still nothing wrong raiding Naxx to get MoM since it would still be a very powerful weapon.

And with Option 2, there would be no need to upgrade legendaries. That whole thing can be skipped. Because TF is already strong at 53.9 dps due to the proc, and Sulfuras at 80.41 dps would still be relatively competitive against a T5 95.0 dps weapon.

 **In conclusion:**
 -This post is just some theorizing and very loose approximations of how to reduce some of the power creep between raid tiers.
 -Ultimately there may need to be like a 10-15% stat squash on top of this across a lot of the game's gear.
 -MoM was not designed with Vanilla+ in mind. Had vanilla blizz continued to add new tiers at lvl 60, there is a good chance they would have nerfed MoM benchmark
 -With Option 1 (our current path), new tier weapons go >95.3 dps MoM benchmark. The upgraded legendaries in Option 1 would be a way to keep them relevant and competitive with newer and more powerful weapons.
 -With Option 2 (conservative path), the final end game weapon would be no more powerful than the 95.3 dps MoM benchmark. There would be a much more conservative approach to power increases between raid tiers that is more stabilizing and ensures early content stays relevant. And upgraded legendaries would not be necessary with this option.
