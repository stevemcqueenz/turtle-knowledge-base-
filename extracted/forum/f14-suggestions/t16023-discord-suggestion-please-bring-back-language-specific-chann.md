# Discord suggestion: Please bring back language specific channels

## Post 109158 by Xerilin (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-11-21T08:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109158#p109158 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There isn't really a spot for discord related suggestions, so here I go.

Last night, there was an announcement ([https://discord.com/channels/4666224558 ... 2279837789](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/747135466192633906/1308957402279837789)) and the German chat channels disappeared, along with many others. Only ru, cn and es got to stay and had support channels added as well.

I do agree that the discord has a lot of channels and may be a bit cluttered. I do appreciate that you want to provide support in different languages. Very cool. This, however, is not even what I would like to ask for the German language community. We don't really need it, since players like Jimbo have made it their mission to answer any questions especially new players posted in de-chat.

We had four text channels, two of which (streams and media) nobody ever asked for, used or needed. They should stay in the bin. And we had the de-chat and de-guilds channels. The latter two were very useful and civil. For example, in the guild channel, all guild leads agreed to only post one advert, no spam. There also wasn't a lot of spam in the chat channel, but always somebody lurking to help out new players.

The CM lil_unicorn recently shared a graph showing how 20% of all website visitors for the EU serverd were from Germany (not even including Austria, Switzerland and others). There are four German language raiding guilds and a couple of smaller guilds, including the rp guild I am GM of. I would very much like that community back.

I bet, people from smaller communities feel the same, so I suggest giving them at least some small corner to gather and find each other as well. A channel doesn't need 1000+ messages a day to be useful and needed.
