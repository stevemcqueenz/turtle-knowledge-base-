If you want that reputation you have plenty of options to get it.

 1. Buy zhr mats off the AH 700-800g should be enough to get you all the way to exalted. Make sure to use cores. They are the cheapest option for reppishing
 2. Ask your guild if they wanna chip in with the reputation. These recepies were not meant for lone individuals to get them in the first place but rather a guild effort. If you can leverage the reputation to craft something noone in your guild can craft yet, people might be willing to help
 3. Run/help run a MC pug that shares its spoils with you.

Never ever turn in dark iron ore for rep. Its much better to sell the ore and buy cores for rep instead.
