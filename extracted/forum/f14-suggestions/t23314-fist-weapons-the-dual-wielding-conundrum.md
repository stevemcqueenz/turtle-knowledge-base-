Trasfering them to an OH version has one teeny tiny detail that will ensure this is not properly working:

It allows you to effectively dualwield whichever fistweapon you want, since even a unique one you can just flip and wear in the offhand as a second pair.

For that alone i dislike the idea: chase fistweapons like Remnants of the old God are already rare enough as is.

Especially on caster fistweapons this also has the side effects of giving very powerfull caster offhand items. Somthing survival hunters can abuse for their stinging nettle poison.

Then we have the WF claw (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61541>)
The way this effect is coded, it it generating an extra mainhand swing if procced. Not just an extra swing with this weapon.

It also effectively kills the idea of putting on use effects on claws in the future. So the whole idea imo cant just be slapped into the game as is

Instead of a blanket solution for all fist weapons. Especially for the ones where both halves are already in the game, that could be a concept for a special fistweapon.
