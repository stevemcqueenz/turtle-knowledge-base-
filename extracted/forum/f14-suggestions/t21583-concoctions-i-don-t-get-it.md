# Concoctions: I Don't Get It

## Post 149548 by Healingrain — 2025-08-31T10:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149548#p149548 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Raid consumables need either consolidation or simplification given the hard buff cap of 32 in the client and it's good of the developers to recognise that, but the implementation of concoctions is baffling.

Firstly, it's targeting just hybrids. Why? We have redundant consumables within the non-hybrid roles too. Dreamtonic and Greater Arcane Elixir for example are literally identical. They couldn't be consolidated? Non-hybrid tanks who run a number of both offensive and defensive consumables and have effects from healers on them arguably have worse buff cap issues than say a Ret Paladin or Enhance DPS, although Protadin and Enhance Tank are definitely the worst.

Also it incurs an additional reagent cost of six herbs for the concoctions. So even among hybrids who unfortunately have the worst consumable costs in the game, were taxing them even harder to overcome a rough edge of the client? The concoctions grant no additional power beyond the two potions that go into them. They would be literally pointless in a game without buff-cap. If anything I expected them to be mildly cheaper than the two individual potions to do hybrids a solid on their large consumable bill. What's the justification here?

> **Cheruscan wrote: Mon Sep 08, 2025 8:26 am**
> I dont understand the argument, consumables are just another layer of managment

Druid is my main, so I guess I'll list some of the aspects resto/moonkin might face with this 'another layer of management'. Don't get me wrong, consume stacking isn't the only reason, another one is recent patches have spawned a plethora of items providing beneficial effects in the form of permanent buffs and procs.

So, as a resto druid playing in a fully consumed K40 raid - you won't be able to see your HoT’s on people who are reaching the cap with all their procs and DPS cooldowns (that's true for basically all warriors, rogues, paladins, DPS shamans, now even moonkins and warlocks are starting to get there). There's no way around this issue with addons and client modifications. Thus, as a HoT healer, you can't play efficiently within the role designated for your spec. As a healer, you may also randomly lose tracking of an important beneficial effect on a tank for example.

As a moonkin using all useful consumes and wearing certain items/sets (for example 8/8 T3 has a permanent buff effect and a proc effect; Scythe has a permanent effect and a proc effect; your boon and eclipse procs count as buffs, etc, etc, etc..) - you usually get far beyond the cap, which results in all your tracking addons not receiving data (you are basically in the dark in regards to your core mechanics - by losing the ability to track eclipse/DoT durations, as well as boon availability).

I strongly believe other classes have similar issues in regards to this situation, I just don't happen to have high enough expertise on how drastic the inconveniences are for those.
