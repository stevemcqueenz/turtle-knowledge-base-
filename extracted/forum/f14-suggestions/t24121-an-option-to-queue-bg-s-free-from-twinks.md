# An option to queue BG's free from twinks

## Post 169613 by Ryladenn — 2026-02-28T22:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169613#p169613 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello, as title suggests
An option to queue BG's that are free from people who stop their XP gains can motivate more people to join BG's.

I am suggesting it as option because when suggested as "separate xp stopped people from normal queues" so many people defend this issue with "queue times will be forever". But twinks are dominating the battlegrounds especially when they are premade.

To prove how unbalanced things become, please go and inspect a 59 level twink, as they wear t0.5s and even some epics from raid. They are basically a few spell ranks different from 60s. Since 60's have a different bracket than 50-59 for power difference reason, they are basically avoiding this bracket. Now imagine 5 of them queue ing together, at the same time in 50-59 bracket. Now imagine getting one shot by them at 56 level because you just wanted to pvp. Moreover, cross faction bgs favor them better, because now, not only the opposite faction, but the players from their own faction has to fill the enemy team, as they fill half of the bgs with just one premade group of five.

So an option would be my solution, If people become tired of waiting queue they can que to all player bgs, if they don't want twinks but are ok with que times they can queue "non xp stopped player" bgs. This way everyone would be happy (except probably twinks themselves.)

Premades are on the other hand... another days topic.
