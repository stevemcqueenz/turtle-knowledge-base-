# [Feedback] Protection Paladin Talent Tree

## Post 23144 by Soleman — 2022-08-22T14:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23144#p23144 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all, let me start by thanking the DEVs for all of the hard work they’ve put into this project. The thoughts expressed below should be taken as constructive criticism to mechanics that already work but in my opinion could use a bit of tweaking.

 **Seal of Justice & Judgement**

This has to be the most expensive single target taunt in the game. Let me elaborate – if we take into consideration a base mana pool of 1500 (at level 60) and talented into Benediction 5/5 then we can determine the total cost of SoJ to 166 mana and Judgement to 76 mana. Combining both spells will cost a total of 242 mana which accounts for 16% base mana cost. If we compare this to the other two single target taunts in the game the warrior version and the druid version, which both cost 0% of their base rage pool, then we can safely conclude that the paladin version is the more expensive spell (by quite a bit).

The only advantage the paladin version has over the two other versions is that it has a +5yd range advantage but if you take into consideration the fact that this is a combination between two spells and is negatively affected by silence mechanics while the other two are not then the range advantage becomes a moot point.

Proposed changes:

New Talent: Improved Seal of Justice (Row 4). Reduce the base mana cost of your Seal of Justice by 2/4/6%.

The taunt effect should becomes baseline with the spell itself no longer needing to talent into it, giving leveling paladins a baseline taunt as early as level 22 instead of 27 as it is currently. In addition, Benediction 5/5 will stack with this new talent further reducing the cost of Seal of Justice for all paladins that spec into it.

 **Ardent Defender**

In its current form this is a very valuable talent when it comes to AoE grinding. It is however useless in the context of a boss fight or even multiple target tanking because if you are reduced to 35% HP, a further 25% flat damage reduction on the next boss hit won’t stop you from dying. It is also an inferior carbon copy of the TBC talent as that one provides a 30% flat damage reduction albeit for an additional 4 talent points.

Proposed changes:

New Talent: Ardent Defender (Row 7). When you have less than 45% health, all damage taken is reduced by XX% for XX seconds. In addition, all over healing received during this time will count towards a damage shield that cannot exceed more than 30% of the paladin’s maximum hit points that lasts for an additional XX seconds.

This change keeps the functionality of the original talent for AoE grinding but also adds an emergency component that triggers earlier than the previous version giving the healer an opportunity to react in the next XX seconds. The over healing the healer usually does during this time will benefit both the paladin and the healer on the next boss hit as it will be used to prevent some of that damage making the paladin less squishy and the healer more mana efficient.

 **Improved Righteous Fury**

As it stands this is a very valuable talent as it increases the threat generated by Righteous Fury and is practically a mandatory talent for any paladin that wishes to tank.
I believe however that there are a couple of changes that could be done to further improve this talent by giving the paladin an additional tool to properly do their job. As it stands paladins don’t have a proper pull mechanic without engineering while warriors and druids do in the form of a ranged weapon and Faery Fire respectively.

Proposed changes:

New Talent: Improved Righteous Fury (Row 3). Increase the threat generated by your Righteous Fury spell by 16/33/50%. In addition, while under the effect of Righteous Fury Exorcism can now be used on enemies other than Undead or Demon at a damage penalty of 99/95/90%.

This change would translate in a 50 – 56 Holy damage done costing 345 mana at Exorcism rank 6. This will establish initial threat on the target at range if used at max rank while rank 1 could just be used for the pulling component as it would practically do 1 Holy damage to the target if they are not Undead or Demon. This also gives leveling paladins a pulling mechanic as early as level 20 as opposed to never as it is now. This change has PvP implications as it will allow paladins to keep enemy players in combat under 30 yds if they talented 1 point into Improved Righteous Fury.

Here's one more for Ardent Defender that also fits with the fantasy:

New Talent: Ardent Defender (Row 7). When you have less than XX% health, all damage taken is reduced by XX% for XX seconds. In addition, your next Holy Light or Flash of Light is instant cast. Your Holy Light costs 50% less mana while your Flash of Light is free of cost.

This gives the paladin a choice to make in an emergency situation. Do they use mana for a big heal that could potentially save them from another big hit or save their mana and spot heal giving the healer a chance to react. Note that this mechanic also has a AoE threat building component as healing produces threat and I believe scales differently while in melee.
