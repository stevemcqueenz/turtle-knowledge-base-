# Making every Attribute useable on every Class

## Post 152367 by Troggyboy — 2025-09-19T11:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152367#p152367 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Note that I'm still somewhat of a noob but I've had this idea floating around in my head so I just wanted to share.

Appearantly, back in the day the Classic WoW devs intended for every attribute to be useable on every class but mid-way through development they abandoned the idea because it was too difficult to implement.

But I think it would be cool to revisit that concept, as with Classic and Turtle WoW the attributes usually boil down to one attribute your class is always gunning for (str/int/agi), stamina as a secondary priority and MAYBE some other attribute with certain niche uses. It tends to make builds and itemization feel rather boring and same-y.

So how to actually implement it?

First, I think the exact values and stats each attribute gives should not be tied to classes but be the same across the board.

Secondly, the attack power values an attribute grants should no longer be flat values depending on your class but general values tied to weapon types, so strength for maces, agi for daggers etc.

Here's what this could actually look like:

 **Strength:**
 -Increases attack power with all melee weapons.
 -Increases damage blocked with a shield.

 **Agility:**
 -Increases attack power with Swords, Daggers, Bows, Crossbows and Thrown (maybe polearms and fist weapons too? idk)
 -Increases critical strike chance.
 -Increases dodge chance

 **Intelligence:**
 -Increases attack power with Staves, Wands and Guns
 -Increases mana.
 -Slightly increases maximum rage for Warriors and energy for Rogues.
 -Increases critical strike chance for spells.

 **Stamina:**
 -Increases health.

 **Spirit:**
 -Increases mana and health regeneration rates.
 -Slightly increases energy regeneration rates for Rogues.
 -Slightly decreases rage degeneration rates for Warriors.

As you can see, on the surface it's very simple but it would have the following effects:

 -The exact stat values that each attribute gives stay the same across the board - no longer having to look at the wiki to see how numbers change for each class or even each druid form.

 -While some stats clearly appeal to some classes more than others, like strength being the obvious choice for warriors, it's not nearly as binary as the current system. You could have dagger- or sword-wielding agility warriors in leather for example or gun-wielding hunters that invest heavily into intelligence.

 -No longer do you have to feel tied to one or two attributes and feel bad if diverging gear drops - in this system, there would be a use case for almost everything.

This is not to say that everything would be sunshine and rainbows. Implementing such a change to the core mechanics would heavily stir up game balance and getting the exact values right for each stat would be difficult - there is probably a reason why the WoW devs back in the day abandoned this idea. But I think to enable more fun build options and to bring back the vision they had for Attributes, it would be worth it imo.

What do you guys think?
