The way I imagine this to work is to basically copy the skeleton of one race and give it to other races, I don't know how well that will work considering they are different sizes and have different length of limbs.

I can't imagine the Gnome having the skeleton of the Troll or vice versa. It will break the model completely when the new skeleton is put into action. Also, from a technical standpoint, I don't think one model can have more than one skeleton as the animations are hardcoded into that skeleton and it would be too technically difficult to implement such a feature.

There has to be multiple modified skeletons with the animations of every other race's skeleton, resulting in a confusingly massive amount of skeletons and when you purchase from the store a new animation, your character's skeleton has to be replaced with that new one instead.

Sounds like a lot of useless work that can take probably a full year to implement if no other content is worked on during that time.
