# Add profession channels to the discord! Explorer is too crowded!

## Post 119645 by DivineBanana — 2025-02-11T21:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119645#p119645 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it would be beneficial to have a separate chat for each profession OR at the very least one channel dedicated soley to professions. This way information on things like where to find recipes, materials, trainers and even trading/looking for someone with a specific profession could all be consolidated and categorized to make finding an answer easier and more organized. Currently, all profession-related questions are going into the Explorer chat, which is way too broad of a channel. It is so broad and all-encompassing that its nearly impossible to get an answer because its overcrowded with hundreds of questions on various different topics. Instead of just overwhelming explorer with all that profession-related information PLUS everything else explorer is already used for, I feel like the community would benefit from rerouting those questions to a dedicated Professions channel. Take the professions out of Explorer, and give them their own channel, this way Explorer can stay as the dedicated channel for other questions like quests/dungeons/new zones etc.

Ideally I would love for each profession to have its own dedicated channel, this would be best for organization and also providing an on-topic record so people can easily go back and answer their own questions. But I understand that might be too many channels, so at the very least we should have one extra channel for Professions, (or maybe break it up into two sections by crafting and gathering.)
