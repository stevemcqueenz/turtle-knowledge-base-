# Nighthaven as a Minor Capital

## Post 5796 by Abundance — 2020-04-29T18:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5796#p5796 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It’s no secret that Moonglade never really sees any visitor besides the odd druid that uses it as an alternative hearthstone and that it serves no specific purpose besides housing a handful of quests and a yearly event. (Although the same could be said for any other capital city taking into account our population, but anyhow.)

To encourage player activity, as well as communication between druid players I think it would be interesting if Nighthaven could mimic the Steamwheedle towns by having an inn, banking, mailbox and auction house service as well as general supplies/trade goods and reagents vendor.

This would be completely within the confines of the lore as it used to be the capital of the night elves before the planting of Teldrassil and foundation of Darnassus, as well as playing a significant role as the capital of the Cenarion Circle.

Furthermore it wouldn’t divert any significant attention from any other city in the game (including the Steamwheedle Cartel affiliated towns) due to its remote location and lack of mass transit, which makes it a less desirable destination for the non-druid player.

Thank you in advance for taking the time to read this!  :)
