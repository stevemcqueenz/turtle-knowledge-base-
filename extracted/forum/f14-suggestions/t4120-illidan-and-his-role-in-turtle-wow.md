# Illidan and his role in turtle wow

## Post 24769 by Robinanderson — 2022-09-10T13:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24769#p24769 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know that there has been some controversial discussion regarding Illidan and the role he should play here on Turtle wow once outland is released so I would like to make a suggestion that I have yet to see on any of the forms

Make Illidan's Forces a neutral faction within outland with Illidan as the primary faction leader

The new Faction could be made up of Night elves, Blood elves, Naga, Broken, Demons , sayters and Fel orcs with the Black citadel serving as the faction capital

The Black citadel would act as a fully functional in game capital complete with class trainers, venders, an inn, mailbox and a flight path. The citadel would also house the leadership of Illidan's Forces including Prince keal'thas, lady vashj , akama and Illidan himself

High elf players would have a special Quest chain where they help heal the rift between the High elves and keal's Blood elves

Illidan's Forces would have several Encampments and Bases scattered throughout outland that would serve as Both safe havens and Quest hubs allowing players to build up reputation with the faction to honored

Once players reach honored the Black citadel will become available and players will have access to Quests there to reach exalted

I also came up with a sensible faction to serve as the primary enemy group for outland

As you may remember in warcraft 3  Magtheridon's forces served as the primary bad guys for the Blood elf campaign so why not have them reprise that role here on Turtle wow?

We never really know what exactly happened to Magtheridon as we never actually witnessed Illidan kill him so the turtle wow team can pretty much take the Magtheridon's forces faction either way regarding if Magtheridon is alive and in charge or dead and someone else is leading the Legion forces now

Either way it makes for a Completely different and Unique storyline
