# About SoR proccing and Cs as alternative

## Post 28520 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-11-05T14:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28520#p28520 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

OK so I slept over last nights paladin outcry and wanted to share a honest opinion as a paladin and partially on favour of devs change

SoR acted as an extra meele hit capable of proccing anything a meele hit can.
This is actually rather an odd mechanic. A paladin that doesn't know this, is playing his class wrong. I only learned about it around lvl 35 ish and my mind was blown. I was thinking "eh, that feels like an exploit even if blizzlike"

Atm the hole paladin meta has developed around maximum exploiting this odd mechanic. The class depends on it in endgame just to be visible.

Obvouldy remong it caused an outcry.

Buuut. Honestly it would be for the better of gone, and would allow others seals to have more of a place.

But paladins need it. What's the solution?
Crusader strike!

Allow crusader strike to procc anything.
Currently Cs can procc weapon effects and enchants.
But early this was in the game for a short time and only removed when the feral druid combat point bug was fixed.

Paladins rets and prots are at the bottom of the food chain. They struggle. They can't stomach a nerf without any compensation. It's not like they a warriors and the best tanks and meele dps.

But if offered alternatives. This could work. Would still be controversial. But promise for example a rework of sanctified command to be dmg based Similiar to how, shadows work. And give prot argent defender a rework to work at the first 35% hp instead of last 35% and they won't complain about a SoR nerf.

So this is my honest opinion from a paladin. SoR nerf is the right thing to do but can't be done as a, standalone changing without ruining prot and ret
