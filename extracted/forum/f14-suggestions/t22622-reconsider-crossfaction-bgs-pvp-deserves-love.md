## Post 157413 by Alcilis — 2025-10-27T23:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157413#p157413 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It now has been more than 4 months.
More than 4 long months since this topic came out in the announcements forum : [viewtopic.php?t=19804](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=19804)
More than 4 months that every PvP player is waiting for news about the teased PvP changes in what was supposed to be a quick follow-up patch (1.18.1) after the 1.18.0.

But now they are turning 1.18.1 into a wannabe 1.19.0 with a sh*tload of unwanted new PvE content, and no news about the so-called "upcoming pvp changes"...

Radio silence for months, and now, instead of the changes we've been waiting for so long, what do they confirm ?
Crossfaction BGs... A feature no serious PvP player ever asked...

Why don't you remove the faction system entirely, at that point ? Please allow my paladin to go give a hug to Thrall and complete quests for Sylvanas while riding a kodo. Allow him to have the Warlord title as well if he's gonna have to kill his fellow Alliance mates...
Ridiculous... And it claims to be a RP oriented server ?

I want to bust some skulls around here again so bad, but I'm starting to doubt I'll play again on this server given the direction it takes.
A "Mercenary" glyph/challenge allowing crossfaction tags (hence faster tags for horde players and better winrates for alliance players) but reducing honor gains in counterpart (for instance) could have made a bit of sense, but global crossfaction BG is a big no for me.

And I'm not even talking about the Contribution Points system abuses it will open in the ranking game...
Not the changes we've been waiting for.
Please reconsider.
