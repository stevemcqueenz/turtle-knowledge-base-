# Proposal to create a PvP zone

## Post 152642 by Dimastasc — 2025-09-22T07:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152642#p152642 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’d like to suggest a new PvP zone, something like a “Fortress of the Fading Flame.” The idea is simple: there’s a Town Hall and a Mine, and both factions have to work together to hold these points.
In the Town Hall, players can take turns “on duty” as guards. If a player stands there for 10 minutes and survives, it counts as a guard shift. The longer a faction holds the Town Hall, the more bonuses they unlock. Every 30 minutes a new reward is added: first, NPC guards spawn, then a +10% buff to main stats, then a controllable turret, then resource gains are multiplied, and finally a tent appears where allies can heal over time.
Meanwhile, the Mine works in parallel. It’s located far from the Town Hall, and that’s where players gather resources. To bring them back, you have to use a cart. The catch is: with an empty cart your movement speed is reduced by 50%, and with a full cart it’s reduced by 75%. Delivering it to the Town Hall is tough because you can be ambushed, so you’ll need escorts and defense. And if the Town Hall is under enemy control, you can’t turn in resources at all.
The key point is that if a faction holds the Town Hall for the full 150 minutes, a special NPC vendor will appear inside. This vendor sells unique gear or rare items, but only as long as the Mine is also under control. If the enemy captures the Mine, the vendor disappears right away.
Everything ties into one chain: guards hold the Town Hall, gatherers mine resources, haulers transport the carts, and the rest either escort or sabotage. It’s not enough to just capture a point — you have to hold it, and the longer you do, the more the whole faction benefits. In the end, the zone feels alive, with constant interaction, giving players not just PvP fights but also a real goal to work toward.
Don’t judge too harshly — it’s just an idea from a player. I’d really like to know if it makes sense, if it sounds fun, or maybe how you’d like to see something like this implemented differently.

## Post 152643 by Dimastasc — 2025-09-22T07:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152643#p152643 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We could also add a player imprisonment system to make the zone feel even more alive. It would work like this: if a faction holds the Town Hall for 2 hours straight, they unlock a special ability. After killing an enemy, there’s a 50% chance that instead of just dying and respawning, the player is sent to the Town Hall prison for 30 minutes. They stay there as a prisoner, which immediately creates the feeling of a real war, where you can end up in enemy custody.
To keep it balanced, prisoners can be freed. For example, allies can launch a raid, break the bars, or kill the guards to rescue their teammates. This creates an extra objective and a new PvP event — it’s not just about capturing a point, but also about rescuing your friends.
I haven’t seen this kind of mechanic anywhere else. It adds real tension, because losing a fight isn’t just “respawning at the graveyard,” but risking being taken prisoner. At the same time, it stays balanced: the feature only activates after holding the Town Hall for a long time, there’s a 50% chance of triggering, and there’s always a way to get freed.
I don’t know how realistic it is to implement, but the idea seems fun to me. Don’t judge too harshly — I’d like to know if this makes sense and how you would want to see a mechanic like this implemented.

The idea of using time can be replaced with the amount of resources collected, and guard duty can be made more flexible: instead of a fixed duration, a guard receives a buff that gives, for example, +5% to armor and all spell resistances. This bonus can stack up to 5 times if multiple guards are on duty at the same time. Additionally, during each duty shift, every 30 minutes a guard can earn “Defender Points,” which can later be used at an NPC to buy gear, consumables, and other rewards.
The Mine is now neutral — it doesn’t need to be captured. Haulers take carts with resources and transport them to the Town Hall: with an empty cart, movement speed is reduced by 50%, and with a full cart by 75%. Enemies can attack haulers or destroy the carts, and players earn “Raider Points” for doing so. Additionally, players who collect resources from the Mine also earn “Gatherer Points,” which can be used at an NPC to buy individual gear, consumables, and other rewards.
This way, every player has their own role and earns points for their specific job. For example, collecting a total of 1,000 resources could unlock the additional rewards and abilities that were previously tied to holding the Town Hall for a certain amount of time.
This makes the mechanic more flexible and interactive: guards gain buffs and points for duty, haulers are responsible for delivering resources, gatherers earn points for mining, and raiders disrupt enemy supply lines. All actions are interconnected through points and rewards, giving every player a meaningful contribution to the faction’s success.
