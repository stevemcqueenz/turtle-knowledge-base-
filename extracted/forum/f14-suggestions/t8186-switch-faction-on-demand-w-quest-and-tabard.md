# Switch faction on demand w/ quest and tabard

## Post 52607 by Samsara (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-07-08T09:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52607#p52607 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since factions have a lowered impact in Turtle WoW, I think it would be really cool to have a lore friendly way to join the other faction via questing. Hear me out:

There would be a NPC in Booty Bay who offers you a quest to prove that you would be willing to betray your own faction by collecting a certain amount of honor killing players from your own faction (roughly 5 kills at same level), and once you complete it, that NPC would give you a tabard from the opposite faction.

While you wear this tabard, guards from your faction would attack you on sight, and you would be able to be treated as a member of the other faction when it comes to entering their cities, doing their faction specific quests, and also using their vendors but you would be discriminated against still and the prices would be 25% more expensive for you to buy while you would get 25% less money when selling items.

It would create a situation where anyone can do all faction specific quests if they desire it, while creating unique roleplaying opportunities. And the more you gain reputation with your new faction, the less the price debuffs would impact you but in return they would transfer to your native faction at the same rate they diminish with the new one.

Raising your reputation to exalted with the new faction would unlock a title "the Traitor".
