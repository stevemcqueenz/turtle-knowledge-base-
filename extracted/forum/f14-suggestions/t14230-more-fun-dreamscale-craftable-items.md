# More Fun Dreamscale Craftable Items!

## Post 97032 by Nurincarinun — 2024-06-14T23:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97032#p97032 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have found wisdom and suggest the consideration of following items:

Chameleon-Threaded Cloak
made with dreamthread and dragon scales of all colours including chromatic and dreamscales
+42 resistance all schools
increases stealth level by 3
on-use effect: random effect: reflects the next harmful spell received within 1sec by the user back to its caster (independent of school) OR turns the user invisible (not stealthed) for 1sec
 20min cd
"Timing is all, unless you're unlucky, then you just have to wait for the next chance."

Kaleido-scopes
made with dreamsteel and one of each highest tier gemstone of each colour
Kaleido-scope V1 (for spellpower hunter or to allow mara-dagger proc built as a ranged built)
adds +? dmg of each magic type to your ranged weapon attack (=5 or 6 (low?) magic dmg procs each hit)
Kaleido-scope V2
When shooting (independent of miss or hit, can be used for one shot by non-hunters before going into melee to make the tank's job at the start of an encounter easier) with a ranged weapon carrying this scope, you fall into a peaceful trance (buff lasting ?sec), emenating energies that decrease your threat generation.
"Psychedelic mechanisms let you see the world like you never have before!"

Giant Dream Catcher (trinket)
made of multiple "Fey Dream Catchers" and dream thread
on use effect: placeable AoE shield (all friendly targets staying in the area get a shared shield buff that disappears for all once an amount of dmg has been taken in the area)
"Used like a kite to filter the surroundings of bad energy."
[edit: class restriction to druid and priest (as the most Emerald Dream related classes)
also: reasonably high cd]
