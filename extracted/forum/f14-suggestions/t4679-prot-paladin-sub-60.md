# Prot Paladin Sub 60

## Post 28005 by Wpxpaladin — 2022-10-26T16:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28005#p28005 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have not hit 60 yet and have not the entire story there. However, I do believe there are some tweaks that could be made to make Paladins feel less hit and miss. From what I have experienced so far, the amount of management to sustain longer fights when there are limited melee mobs just isn't there. It makes the tanking experience feel very poor and becomes unenjoyable when you OOM and have no way to regen quickly or pull threat to you so you can use the main regen ability, blocking.

Now I freely admit some of my challenges have been due to me not having the right gear, or just plain thinking some strategies would work when they wouldn't. That said though it's clear a lot of the logic behind the Paladin tank will not scale well because of the way threat is generated to begin with. For example SoR does very low damage, even with the 80% more threat you can generate with it, 80% of a low number is still likely a low number. For example 80% of 65 would net you 117 points of threat (if I read the code correctly) which is still really low at my level, 54. You could argue that the 117+ white damage would offset that more and you are right!

The issue is beyond simple calculations though because characters doing dps are just not doing white damage plus 1 other thing. Before I switched to Prot I rolled Ret. WIth DECENT gear in the mid 40s running Ulda I could see crits with SoC as high as 800 with SotC judged. NO Paladin tank I ran with could stand up to that. Heck even now well geared roguess , warriors and paladins can rip threat even after its built up because the huge bursts they can do -if- I am not able to block!

Holy Shield
A great ability when melee is around and is beating on you. Generates tons of threat and helps us regenerate, hopefully, enough mana to keep on going. The cooldown and mana cost early on can be a serious issue. One way to make this better might be to switch this to an active ability that toggles. You get the 30% base block which will allow better mana and threat generation. You could lower the damage to compensate, however, you would need to increase the amount of threat to be proportionate to ensure its not ruined. You could add a talent, 3/3 or 5/5 that increases the amount of damage and thus threat it causes and/or the amount of damage it will block.

Improved Dev Aura
This just isn't worth taking from what I see. The % is too low and even if it was higher im not sure anyone would take it. Maybe instead of adding more armor, have it added additional HP/Mana regen or something?

Reckoning
This feels completely pointless at 5 points. You are typically gearing to reduce the number of crits you get hit by so why would you want this? Reckbombs are not a thing here, which is good, but this talent feels totally worthless as is. Why not make it more tank oriented instead? Instead of stacking a "extra attack" maybe have a staggering mechanism to the damage? Or use it as a talent to build up a controllable block that would absorb x% more damage? Something like, after blocking 10x Reckoning can be activated to block the next incoming attack generating extra threat and blocking an additional 25% of your shields block value (thats probably low but you get it)

Seal of Justice
Its great there is at least some kind of option here for a taunt, but man does this just not feel good at all. The reliance on the judgement feels horrible and is so situational I found myself not even bothering with it most time. I don't even have to specced anymore and instead do other things to ensure I can get threat back. Such as BoP or salv. Maybe tweak this talent altogether? Make it do a % of the weapons damage as holy and keep the threat judgement component? That might make it feel better. Or better yet just change it completely. Make the talent give the seal a cooldown and limit the amount of time the seal can be active to maybe 5-10 seconds. Each swing acting not as a taunt but as a threat reduction for every other source EXCEPT yourself based on a flat % not of damage. So for example, each strike with justice on reduces the threat generated from all other sources by 5% per swing, max stack 5. So total if you can get in 5 swings you would have reduced everyone, except yourselfs, total threat generated on the target by 25%.

One hand weapon spec
Since we don't get extra threat from white damage maybe make this increases the amount of holy damage you do by 10%? Most of the holy damage done is small number, outside of holy strike and maybe judgements, so that might not move the needle all that much, but it might make it more appealing than the current version.
