# My take on Survival

## Post 100378 by Lomikare1 — 2024-08-29T13:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100378#p100378 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello!
I am a big enthusiast of Gardening and Survival, and I am very glad that you have added this profession to the game. It brings something new and fresh to the game.

At the moment, however, I feel that it is not yet finished and is still in development. And I fully understand that. Creating an original profession that will be available to everyone and won’t interfere too much with other professions is not easy at all! Not to mention that it’s about survival! It’s easy to think that it should be about tents and making fire, but soon you run out of ideas and then the profession is unfinished.

From my point of view, Survival is a very unique secondary profession. It allows the player to create things in the world, which is just really cool in itself. Another big interesting aspect is that it supports other professions (e.g., fishing boat). From my perspective, I would primarily focus on these two things. That is, to really primarily support other secondary professions (cooking, fishing, first aid), make life easier for the player, and last but not least, allow the creation of cozy things that can improve one’s camp.

And so that I’m not just theorizing and talking, I have made a draft of new things that could be created from Survival. These are recipes that can be learned at higher levels of this profession and practically develop it to the full level, i.e., 300.

I wish you a pleasant read and hope that it will serve as an interesting inspiration for the developers of this great game! O:)

If you need any more help, feel free to ask!

 **RECIPES**

 **Wooden chair**
Requires Survival (100), Simple Wood (3), Cooper bolts (5)

Create a comfortable wooden chair which can be used for sitting. Can be used just outdoors and will be spawned for 1 hour. You can have a maximum of 5 chairs in your bag.

 *This is nice for rollplayers or players who want to make their camp more comfortable. Don't need a new model.*

 **Fishing cage trap**
Requires Survival (125), Simple Wood (5), Silk cloth (5), Sturdy rope (1),

Set up a fishing cage trap in the river, lake or sea. You can put Nightcrawlers in the sage trap and after 5 minutes there will be a fish (depending on what can be normally catched) which can be looted. After each fish you must put another Nightcrawler in the cage and the cage will be spawned 30 minutes in total. You can carry a maximum of 5 fishing traps in your bag.

 *Model can be used from Shellfish traps.*

 **Torch holder**
Requires Survival (135), Simple Wood (2), Bronze framework (1), Copper bolts (2)

Set up a torch holder, which can hold a torch. Torch will be giving a cozy buff to nearby allies and has a normál duration as classic normal torch. Torch holder will stay spawned for 30 minutes. You can have only five torch holders in your bag.

 *This will make a torch useful, because it will no longer occupy a hand to wear it. It can be spawned around players and give them a small spirit buff. Model is in the game.*

 **Survival trap**
Requires Survival (150), Simple wood (3), Bronze framework (1), Sturdy rope (1)

Sets up an invisible trap, which can catch the enemy and hold him in place. Catched creature is holded in place for 8 seconds and cannot even turn on the place. Trap will stay placed for 5 minutes and you can carry a maximum of 1 survival trap in your bag.

 *Model can be used as a normal hunter trap (just delete fire or frost effect on hunter trap). Trap will be useful for casters as a form of CC against enemies which want to stay close. The effect of “stopping the turning of enemy” is useful for melee.*

 **Cooking pot**
Requires Survival (175). Simple wood (3), Mithril Casing (1), Sturdy rope (1)

Set up a cooking pot, which is great for cooking. For every attempt to cook something you will have an increased skill for 50 points. Stay spawned for 5 minutes and can be used only outdoors. You can have only one cooking pot in your bags.

 *This item provides a nice support for another side job - cooking. Model is already in the game, and can be used.*

 **Emergency Stretcher**
Requires Survival (200), Mageweave cloth (4), Simple Wood (2), Sturdy rope (2)

Create an emergency stretcher which can be equipped with two hands. You can then pick up the corpse of an ally and transport him with a stretcher to a safer place. Stretcher has a 5 minute duration after it is equipped and you can have a maximum of five stretchers in your bag.

 *There are times, when you want you have a power to move your dead friends. This could do it. But dont know if there it model like this.*

 **Adventurers Chest**
Requires Survival (225), Simple Wood (5), Cooper bolts (5), Lock (1)

Set up a chest which will allow you to open your bank tab. Chest will stay spawned for 5 minutes and you can carry only 1 chest in your bag. Others can use it too.

 *This will motivate players to go for survival. Raiding and gear will always take space in bags. And this chest could help with that. But still it isn't as good as the item from the shop. Don't need a new model, just need a new item and icon of “lock”.*

 **Magic Torch**
Requires Survival (250), Simple Wood (3), Mageweave cloth (3), Vision dust (5)

Create a magic torch which has the power to make invisible things visible. It gives a cozy buff as a normal torch and in the same radius all invisible is visible. Must be holded in hand or can be placed in a torch holder. Has the same duration as a normal torch.

 *Nice think for pvp players and can be nice even for invisible mobs. Model is in the game (green torch), so it can be used.*

 **Survival bag**
Requires Survival (275), Thick Wolfhide (5), Copper bolt (5), Dream dust (5), Greater Eternal Essence (2)

Create a special survival bag which has a 24 sloth of space. But you can only store just the things which are from secondary professions (cooking, fishing, first aid, survival). So you can store bandages, antidotes, survival creations, fishes, lures, rods, food and things like that.

 *Little something for players, which love secondary professions. Many survival creations take a lot of bag space. But this can help with that.*

 **Frostfire torch**
Requires Survival (300), Simple Wood (3), Runecloth (3), Elemental Water (1)

Create a frostfire torch which can protect from heat. It gives a cozy buff as a normal torch and in the same radius gives +30 fire resistance. Must be holded in hand or can be placed in a torch holder. Has the same duration as a normal torch.

 *Little buff for resistance. Could be nice and useful to put this torch in a holder nearby casters or healers in raid. Don't know if there is a model of white or blue torch, but I think there already is one.*

 **Elemental Fire torch**
Requires Survival (300), Simple Wood (3), Runecloth (3), Elemental Fire (1)

Create an elemental fire torch which can protect from cold. It gives a cozy buff as a normal torch and in the same radius gives +30 frost resistance. Must be holded in hand or can be placed in a torch holder. Has the same duration as a normal torch.

 *Kinda the same as the torch above. It's just variation for other raid encounters.*

Thank you Devs for you time and your work! This server is very nice! O:)
