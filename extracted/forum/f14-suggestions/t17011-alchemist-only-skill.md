It would also turn the entire profession market upside down.
Much less value per herb because there would be significantly fewer consumables needed to be crafted. Which in turn leads to more incentive to pick other professions because less demand for herbs. In the end everyone receives less gold for their professions. I don’t think it’s that good of a suggestion. Nice for alchemists, yes. But bad in general.

What might be a better solution to your problem might be extra alchemists only consumes. Like sharpening stones being bs only and bombs being engineer only, a few special alch only consumables could be interesting?
