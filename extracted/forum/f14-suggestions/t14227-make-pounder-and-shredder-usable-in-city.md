Mounts really are a 'show off' type of social thing in cities.  While your idea has some merit, it would potentially intefere in future 'faction leader pvp' as well unless you ONLY give it to the home faction (which would be a huge imbalanced advantage for the defenders in addition to being on home turf).

Occam's Razor suggests the simplest solution is often the best one.  Aka change as little as possible and whatever does change do so in small and meaningful measures.  I'd merely prefer that mailboxes be more prolific in cities (why do we not just add in the mailboxes that were present all over in WotLK?) but some people still think 5 minute paladin buffs pre-60 is ok despite paladin tanks and 5-man dungeons being a thing.  As long as that vocal hostility remains then the community may seem a bit divided on how to proceed.

Another alternative is to keep mounts in cities but have an invisible 'aura' always active around every mailbox, banker, and auctioneer that forces dismount of anyone nearby regardless of faction.  Something like 5 'yards' (basically 'melee range') would be decent.
