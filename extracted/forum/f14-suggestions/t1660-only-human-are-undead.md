There's a lore reason. The plague definitely affects every (or most) living creature. For example, there is plagued wildlife in plaguelands. However the forsaken faction is a very small subcategory of the Undead in WoW. They are Lordaeron humans specifically who broke free from the Lich King's control and are now led by Sylvanas who is actually a high elf. There are some high elf undead that are part of the forsaken but they are a minority. As far as I know, there are no undead of other races that joined the horde.
