*I know... I know.. It has might been brought up before! But for the love of Sylvanas. Give the Undead some love! It is not their fault that Arthas' risen them from their grave!*

Jokes aside...
I love the custom content on TWoW. And I hope to see even more cool features in the future. Which should all add up according to lore and time of place still! So we don't end up with a mess as retail wow.

I play as an undead hunter (main) on Tel'Abim. And I thought of some skin ideas for Undead, that I hope to see in the future. Since I am only able to get a sunburn at this moment ![🙏](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f64f.svg)

 **Eye color**
I'd like to see Undead being able to choose eye colors. As blue, red and green. Some players like to RP Scourge, and for that, blue would just be an extraordinary feature! Myself: I would just love red. You teased us last year with an image of an undead with red eyes, but it hasn't arrived yet (beside the Touch of Darkness and Blood Widow skin) But for a pale skinned Undead, red would just be a great customizeation.

 **Boneless**
Since *the bonesless Undead* has always been a thing for some geographical locations, it could be a nice feature aswell. To be able to play an undead, who hasn't rotten all away. Like a freshly turned undead.

 **Human form**
This might be a little different to implement, than just a color or hide bones. But what if.. What if you could be an ALL FRESH turned Undead? Still in human form? But looking a bit like the human skins: **Death knigh**t (m), **Necromancer I** (f).

This could also be a great feature for the RP community, aswell for others. And even more. A skin for the female undeads as **Dark Rangers**! That would be very fitting.

I know this ideas might not be new. But since Humans have eight different skin options in shop, gnomes two, orcs eight aswell. I would just like to see undeads getting some love too ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)
