# A Change for Warmode that will make Low Lvl BGs actually happen!

## Post 19596 by Moebius24 — 2022-07-02T15:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19596#p19596 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone,

I've been playing on Turtle WoW for two Weeks now and I'm loving it so far. I think the idea of Warmode on a PVE Server is a great idea and I wouldn't have come here without it. Of course it has its problems as many players point out on this forum. The problem with the XP Boost is that it makes people turn on warmode who aren't interested in PvP. This leads to frustration and more negativity etc. (other people have pointed this out already)

I've been thinking about this a bit and came up with this idea:
Instead of a general XP boost the Warmode could give you a large boost for the XP you get in BGs (below 60). Furthermore it could also boost your Honor and Reputation gains in BGs (I always thougt the rep gain was ridiculously low).
Right now there are no BGs happening in 20-29, 30-39, 40-49. And that is a pity! You can only do BGs from 10-19 (at peak times) and then you'll have to level up to 51 so you can at least do AV. I'm currently lvl 27 and I've noticed that I'm now trying to rush the Levelling process to get to do BGs again. So I've stopped being a turtle   crying_turtle   I really wanted to take my time. No rush to get to 60.
Low Level BGs are great because you get to learn your class in PvP while levelling. I once levelled to max Lvl on another Server that had the same issue. No BGs and no PvP at all until max Level. The result was that I was completely overwhelmed by my spells and couldn't really contribute in the BGs. I actually quit the game at that point.

With this change only people who are interested in BGs would choose warmode. So no more people who don't like PvP in this mode. AND it would make BGs in the lower brackets happen.

What do you guys think?
