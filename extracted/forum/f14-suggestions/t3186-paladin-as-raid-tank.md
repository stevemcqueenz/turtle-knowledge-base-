Greetings,

First of all, I would like to thank you for the effort the team has made to bring a whole new life to Vanilla WoW. I appreciate the changes that was made to hybrid classes to make them more viable. However, the greatest limitation does not reside in skills or talents but gears. At the moment, there is no raid tanking set for hybrid classes like shamans, druids, and paladins. I believe with great sets dedicated for tanking, more classes will become viable in raid tanking. Therefore, I humbly suggest changes made to all tier 1 (drop from MC) for shamans, druids, and paladins, to be dedicated for tanking. And stats wise should give them similar HP and mitigation to Warriors (since they have less base health as hybrid, the sets should give them more stamina/mitigation than Warrior set).

Thank you.
