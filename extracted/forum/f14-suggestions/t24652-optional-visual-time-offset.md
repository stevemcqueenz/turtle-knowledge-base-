# OPTIONAL VISUAL TIME OFFSET

## Post 173583 by Neinnlive — 2026-04-01T08:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173583#p173583 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone! ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)

I have a proposal for a **SMALL BUT POWERFUL** immersion feature that I believe many of us would love: An **OPTIONAL TOGGLE** for a **LOCAL VISUAL TIME OFFSET** in the Turtle WoW client.

 **THE IDEA**
A setting (like a checkbox or a slider in the options) that allows players to adjust the **IN-GAME LIGHTING** and **SKYBOX** to match their local time (e.g., CET) instead of the hardcoded Server Time.

 **WHY THIS MATTERS (THE IMMERSION FACTOR)**
We all remember those legendary nights back in the day.

 *Imagine you’re in a grueling Deadmines run that lasts until 7 AM. You finally defeat VanCleef, step out of the dungeon, and as you take your first breath of Westfall air, the sun starts to peak over the horizon — both in-game and outside your real-life window.*

 *Or logging in after work at 6 PM and seeing the beautiful Azeroth sunset right as the sun sets in your backyard.*

Right now, many players are forced to play in perpetual darkness or broad daylight because their real-life schedule doesn't align with the server clock. This disconnect breaks the "world" feeling that makes Turtle WoW so special. Having this as an **OPTIONAL SETTING** would be a massive boost to the atmosphere.

 **ADDRESSING FISHING & TIMED MECHANICS**
I know that certain gameplay mechanics (like specific fishing loot or rare spawns) are tied to server time. This **WOULD NOT BE AN ISSUE**:

Since the **SERVER CLOCK REMAINS VISIBLE** in the UI, players can simply refer to that for gameplay-specific timing. This change would be **PURELY VISUAL** (lighting sets/skybox) and wouldn't impact the actual server-side logic or synchronization with other players.

 **THE REQUEST**
Is it technically feasible to implement a **"VISUAL HOUR" OFFSET** in the native client? If players could choose to shift the day/night cycle by a few hours to match their own surroundings, it would change the game for **MANY OF US** who crave that extra layer of immersion.

Whether it's a simple checkbox or a command, I think it would be a beautiful addition to the unique **"TURTLE EXPERIENCE."**

Looking forward to your thoughts!
