# Enchant rift tear

## Post 174432 by Raizan — 2026-04-08T10:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174432#p174432 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Currently the might of menethil item and its transmog(and I believe a couple of other items like this exist) will remove visible enchant glows and effects. This is ok in my opinion, its always giving that blue glow to it, however enchants that don't give a glow and rather an orb of some kind that circles the weapon like the enchant rift tear does would make sense to stay on the weapon. I just enchanted my might of menethil tmog with rift tear hoping to get that orb rotating on it and am sad to see it doesn't work as the weapon overwrites any enchants put on it. My suggestion is that an enchant should be adding the visible floating orb as it is a part of the unique weapon enchant.
