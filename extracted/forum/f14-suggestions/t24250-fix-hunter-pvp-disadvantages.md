# Fix Hunter PVP disadvantages

## Post 170566 by Duraluminium — 2026-03-11T08:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170566#p170566 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hunter class got 2 main PVP branches: melee and ranged.

 **1. Ranged hunter in PVP lacks ability to break distance**
The main problem is that the supposed way of breaking distance by using Scatter shot + Freezing trap is hard to do, can be easily interrupted by any damage from any source, and has shared diminishing return so it can be used only once per combat. As everyone got access to 2-min CD PVP trinket, this CC combo is simply not enough.
There are several trinkets that can help breaking distance, but they are available only on 60, require time to get (and engineering is a must for some of them), got long cds (15 mins minimum). Also the idea itself that some class is required to get some trinkets and rely on their CDs + addons to swap these trinkets, to be able to play PVP, is wrong in so many ways..
There should be some reliable ability to break distance with medium or short CD. In WotLK they gave hunters the jump, and it was amazing. I'm not sure that it's possible to do so on this client though. I will give my suggestion on how to fix this after the 'melee' section.

 **2. Melee hunter in PVP lack ability to play vs casters**
Melee hunter also lacks ability to close the gap, but I suppose it's fine cause hunter is still a hunter - we can shoot, we can slow the target, we can drain mana, we got pet, etc. We still are able to play on range, just not as efficient as in melee. So not being able to close the gap is a disadvantage, but we got ways to mitigate it.
The REAL disadvantage of melee hunters in PVP is inability to break casts. I do good damage in melee, but it does not matter, cause I cannot heal, and the priest, or shaman, or warlock, or even arcane mage - they all got either ways to mitigate my damage by shields and heals, or to CC me, while doing damage on me. So a well geared caster with the same level of skill will always win vs melee hunter.
On arena I can use Thornpod to stun my target for 2 seconds, and it's a gamechanger. I can lower enemy caster's health to like 50%, and when they try to heal, I stun them and usually it's enough to finish them in the current burst meta.
BUT when the PVP patch comes out with this new PVP gear with a lot of stamina and resilience, I think that they will be able to outsurvive this 2 sec stun most of the time.

 **The suggestion**
So melee hunter needs an ability to kick casts like a rogue (with low cd) or to stun the target like a paladin (with medium cd). And ranged hunter needs some way to break the distance.
I suggest moving Scatter shot from MM talents into an ability available to all hunters from lvl 20.
The effect should be changed from disorient into stun, the length of stun should be 5 sec, 2 minute CD.
Also there should be a talent in MM tree to shorten Scatter shot CD to 1 minute (2 talent points).
