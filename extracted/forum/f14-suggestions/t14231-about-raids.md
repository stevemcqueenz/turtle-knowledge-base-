# About raids

## Post 97053 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-15T11:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97053#p97053 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Listing my change ideas for the current raids.

 **Kara10**
 * Would turn certain items purple (mostly the healer and caster trinket, maybe also the tanking blue one).

 **ZG**
I think it overstays its welcome, way overgeared players are forced to run ZG for rep and idols. While loot-wise it is the most lackluster raid.
 * Would decrease the rep requirement for shoulder enchants to revered from exalted.
 * Would consider making idols boe and sellable at AH. Instead of ZG farming, you could f.ex. farm DM and buy the idol.
 * Would move the idol enchant place to the Isles so you don't need to enter ZG for it.
 * Would consider making the tokens and drinks BoE too. You don't have to enter ZG for that but buying the pots at AH can prevent a 20 min ride when preparing for a raid.
Loot feels very lackluster very soon (compared to Kara or AQ20 where certain items make you come back for a while).
Items I'd consider a buff for:
 * Jeklik's crusher
 * Spider boss loot in general - people tend to fight a boosted Hakkar just to avoid that place altogether
 * Bloodlord's defender (you have to convince a tank at that progression point to consider not using legacy, a custom loot). Maybe added dodge chance or more defense.
 * Madness trinkets. Some of them are good but most of them are extreme farming for a lackluster result.
 * Would consider making the rings with set bonus purple.
 * Will of Arlok is interesting for druids. Would all in on that aspect and remove the added stamina and add that much added spirit to it.
 * Would add an idol to Hakkar. Hakkar not giving idol feels like a flavor fail.
 * Would consider buffing the class set items. By the time I have the rep to use all, my gear is usually way better. I tend to ignore those drops and start with MC usually. Making tier items equipable from friendly could also help.

 **AQ20**
 * Would give better loot to Ayamis, he is too often skipped.
 * Would buff Eye of Moam and make it a purple. It's just a feels good trinket, liking it.
 * Would make Buru's tanking trinket a passive effect to make it a good thorns option.
 * AQ20 has the same class set item problems as ZG, maybe to a lesser degree. Would allow players to get all of items at a lower reputation.

 **MC**
MC is in a very weird place. It overstays its welcome but only for melee. Unless your guild is using GDKP, this is what happens at the moment your guild core is at least T2:
 - Tank/melee: Let's go MC, accuria, bindings, eye, weird neck, yeah.
 - Casters: Nah. Why would I set foot there?
 - Healers without cauterizing band and maybe shoulder: Yeah, MC is cool!
 - Other healers: Why the hell would I go there?
MC is set up that it rewards melee to go there regardless their gear while it barely offers anything for casters after reaching T2 (choker maybe). Guild will eventually farm it, and casters will usually skip it or hate it, unless GDKP happens.
 * Because of this, if we keep the theme of MC (an easy raid which is somehow the main source of in-game legendaries), I'd add items for casters and healers to motivate them farming MC even at lategame. For example using the legendary neck as a reference, giving two other bosses a healer and a caster legendary neck with the same drop rate as the tanking one.

 **Ony**
 * Kara10 hurt this raid a lot. People used to come here for head of Ony, helmet and sword/axe or mana trinket (and bag). Kara10 has a better sword, a better tanking trinket, a better caster ring and a better healer and caster trinket now. Would buff the items in general to make Ony more appealing.
 * Eskhandar's Collar 2% dodge (not 1%) and 1% hit chance (not crit).
 * Sapphiron's drape: adding 1% crit chance instead of the arcane resistence.
 * Ring of binding, while buffed here, is still a worse version of the MC ring. Remove the armor and give it 1% hit chance.
 * Shard of the scale was massacred by the Kara10 trinket. Either give it 20mp5 or add +10/15 spirit to it.
 * Axe and Sword: some minor buff to make it competitive against/alongside Legacy. A minor stat boost or giving the items 1% crit chance.
 * The attunement is extremely long. Would feel justified for AQ40 but not for a raid like Ony which gives worse-than-BWL loot and has 1 boss altogether. Ony attunement is maybe 10x longer than BWL attunement. Would shorten the quest chain for both factions for it.

 **ES**
 * Attunement is okay-ish but asking 100 gold as a part of the attunement feels weird. Would replace that part with 1 additional dungeon run.
 * HM chest feels considerably weaker since the trinket nerf was announced.
 * Trash packs. Make them harder but also make them die faster. More mechanics but faster to advance combination would be good.

 **BWL**
 * I'm mostly fine with BWL. Kinda wishing more raids had some rogue utility like the one that suppression room has.

 **AQ40**
 * I think it's okay. The lack of attunement is weird since BWL or Ony has 1.
 * Would increase the gold received from bosses, though. For more, see the same entry under Naxx.

 **Naxx**
 * Would switch how the tokens are being distributed. Since vanilla didn't have cross-faction, it did a 3-3-2 token distribution for classes. TWOW has put pala and shaman in the same group, meaning that while warriors and rogues fight for 1 set of tokens, pala, hunter, shaman and druid all fight for the same. Would make the distribution 3-3-3.
 * Would buff some of the +frost rez items. I understand why they exist and they give an in-raid niche for some bosses, but they are just feel bad items outside of Naxx. Most of them feel like a worse ZG purple unless you specifically go into a frost-themed fight.
 * Atiesh. From one hand it's good, from the other hand it divides healers. Priest, druid can have it, shaman and pala can't. Feels arbitrary, specially since shamans can use staves.
 * Same as AQ40, would consider increasing the gold dropped by bosses, since these two are consume-heavy raids. Would help a bit for farming, if part of the farming is doing the raid.
