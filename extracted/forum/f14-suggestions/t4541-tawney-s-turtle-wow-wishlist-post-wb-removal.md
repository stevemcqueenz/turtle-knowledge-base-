 **Part 1: Class specific request(for the classes I play).**
 **Part 2: Overall AoE update thoughts and raid trash nerf suggestions.**

 **Paladin:**

 - Holy:

 - Judgement of Light improving talent over Unyielding Faith. Increases healing by judgement of light by 5/10% of total int per talent point.

 - Improved BoWisdom talent (Now shaman totems and blessings stack) give casters + 15/30 spell power.

 - Holy shock crits on allies to reduce the cast time of your next Holy Light by 1 second.

 - Improved Lay on Hands also restores the Paladin equal mana to it’s target unless the paladin self casts it (550 mana to the holy pala if he LoHs a tank)

Reasoning: Holy paladin is great at sniping heals but it’s rather boring. Holy shock is great at sniping HPS from other healers but it’s low range doesn’t allow for uses on spread out fights. Landing great Lay on Hands and Holy shocks on allies should make the Paladin feel happy and encouraged to do so.

 -Ret:

 - Make Repentance work on undead enemies. (every sentient race except beasts and elementals)

 - Sanctified Command talent rework suggestion: Gives your allies 10/20% of the base mana cost → Gives allies 10/20% of base mana cost, divided equally. (What this does is let solo rets get a full 100% mana return on Seal of Command, good for overworld content and pvp, if the coding allows it Rets would get all the mana in a group full of warriors and rogues.)

 - Move Repentance down to where Blessing of Kings is, add “Holy Banner of Conquest” as final ret talent, an aura like ability on a long cooldown that buffs all party members attack speed and gives them holy damage on hit for a time.

 -Prot:

 -Prot is in an okay spot after the changes. The only suggestion I have is make Crusaders Strike deal Holy damage so it’s affected by Righteous Fury and tanks can have an easier time holding threat on a single target being bursted down. For raid tanking, maybe add an increased Block chance to seal of Justice for the first 3 seconds of using the seal.

Non Spec Specific Paladin changes:

Make Blessing of Kings taught at trainers or unlocked after a short questline.
Make Holy Strike cheaper so people aren’t better off using Rank 1 Holy Strike for better mana conserving.
Updates on AoE will mention Consecrate.

 **Mage:**

 - Arcane
(bugfixes please)
 - Arcane Mind: 5 talent points for 2-10% maximum mana → 2 talent points for 5/10% maximum mana.

 -Fire

 - Master of Elements: Your fire and frost crits restore 10/20/30% base mana costs → Your fire and frost spells restore 5/10/15% base mana costs, doubled for critical strikes.

 -Frost

 - Frost mage is in a very good spot for leveling and the first few raids, updates on AoE will mention blizzard.

Reasoning for mage changes: Arcane has to spend a lot of talent points just to be a utility frost mage. This gives them 3 more talent points. Fire is hard to start out because without crits not only do you do less damage but run out of mana fast too, this change helps a bit with the undergeared Fire mages while not affecting geared ones much.

 **Rogue**

 -Assassination

 - Improved Poisons Swapped places with Ruthlessness: Your Finishing moves have a 20-60% chance to add a CP to your target→ 33-66-100% chance to add a combo point.

 - Vigor: 10 max energy → 20 max energy.

 -Combat

 - Endurance is now baseline. Replaced with the spell Tricks of the Trade:
Tricks of the Trade: The threat caused by your next damaging attack and all actions taken for 6 sec afterwards will be transferred to the target. 20 energy cost, usable while stealthed, 1 minute cooldown.

 - Blade Flurry rank 2 will be addressed in the AoE update section.

 -Sub

 - Camouflage → 5 points 3% ms per rank → 3 points 5% ms per rank.

 - Setup swapped with Initiative

Reasoning: Rogue is perfectly fine as it is in vanilla except for combo points. Unless we want to heavily change that these QoL changes should be enough for some variety and fun.

 **AOE update and trash nerf**

AoE:

The idea is to update AoEs to compensate for Wbs being taken away and raids made uncomfortably longer. The goal is to give classes who don’t have the option to contribute sustained AoE damage some tools to compensate, so you aren’t better off inviting 10+ mages for AoE to clear trash then invite Rogues and other AoE less classes.

Overall biggest change suggestion: Update Damage over time AoEs to be able to crit per tick. Examples: Consecrate, Blizzard, Rain of Fire.

Crits not only speed up aoe here but classes like Retribution Paladin could have 100% Holy Avenger uptime on trash or cleave bosses. Making it a great addition to bosses like Gar or Panther lady in ZG.

New spell book drops for AoE spells added to Naxx bosses and trash:

Blade Flurry rank 2: Cooldown goes from 2 minute → 1 minute.

Rogue: Fan of Knives: 30 energy, deal X% weapon damage to up to 6 enemies around the rogue, gives 1 combo point, has a chance to apply poisons.

Divine Storm for paladins: Requires a 2 hander. An instant weapon attack that causes 110% of weapon damage to up to 4 enemies within 8 yards. Divine Storm heals up to 3 party members totaling 25% of the damage caused.

Starfall for druids: After a 3 second cast: Calls down waves of falling stars upon enemies within 25 yards doing Arcane damage. (affected by Nature’s reach)

Hunter: Multi-shot rank 6.
Mage: Flamestrike rank 7.
Warlock: Hellfire rank 3.

 **Trash nerf ideas:**

BWL dragon packs after Vael before suppression room.

Aq40 everything after Twins

Naxx spider wing trash especially poison damage,  Abomination health, military wing all deathknights health reduced by some amount.

Extra suggestions:
Weekly Chosen Raid

With the new Turtle Wow Scarlet Citadel raid coming out, we may get to the point where players are too burned out from doing every raid in a monotone fashion, to change that, I introduce Chosen Raids. Each week a random 40 man raid(except Ony) gets Chosen. During that week, bosses from that raid drop 1 more item. This rotations in a random order with a slight bad luck protection (1 raid cannot be non Chosen for more than 4 weeks.) and the same raid cannot be repeated, so each week players have something new to look forward to without feeling angry that their favorite raid wasn’t Chosen.

 - How do players know what raid is Chosen?
Each Reset players can pick up a Quest that they can turn in automatically to get the news from a gnome/goblin in their respected capital. The quest will give them a buff that tells them what raid will drop +1 items that week.

 - Won’t players feel bad if the BEST possible raid isn’t the Chosen raid for 4 weeks straight?
Lets say we have MC → BWL → MC → AQ40, at this point, the next raid WILL be Naxx until Scarlet Citadel is out. However, with this random element, players can also have MC→ Nax → Nax → AQ40 → BWL → MC. This will create a much more fun month for players who are finished with the first few raid tiers.

 - Wow is time sink. Why would players need MORE incentive to do certain raids?
The way raiding works in Turtle/Vanilla is the following: You don’t want a lot of items in each raid before you get to the next raid, you want 1-3 KEY items from each raid before venturing to AQ40 and Naxx. Molten Core? Band of Accuria, Onslaught Girdle, Mana Igniting Cord, Choker, Bindings. BWL? Trinkets, T2. ZG? Idols. AQ20? Skill books. AQ40? T2.5, BiS weapons and non set pieces. Naxx? Most things.

Classic wow is designed to eventually burn people out of hardcore raiding. A small variety could help retain players without doing any updates to the game itself.

 **Burnout is a very real thing in classic Wow. Ask Macro how many players playing Today have Ashbringers in the best American guild on the server. 0, zero, nada. People come and go that’s natural but there's 0 effort being done to combat burnout because some people enjoy gatekeeping Classic raiding while in their ignorance ruin their own chances of raiding in the future by not realizing their attitude makes them lose more players and eventually will have to reprog old raids with completely new people, who will also go away one day. Preventing this is impossible but combating it and delaying it with neat little features isn’t.**
