The 5 lvl restriction is an attempt to limit help from above in a simple understandable way. It has shortcomings and is not perfect - everybody knows that. But as long as it (at least sort of) works, it is good enough. Its simplicity offsets its shortcomings as it is. If anybody can come up with another SIMPLE system that is also better to replace the current one - by all means, suggest it and promote it. The OP's soulbound idea works for gear, but definitely not for mats and consumables. It makes bank alts (quite necessary for HC60 when it comes) less useful as they would not be able store gear anymore. It may also be somewhat difficult to understand and implement as the concept of bind on trade does not exist at all atm.

To sum up: KISS.
