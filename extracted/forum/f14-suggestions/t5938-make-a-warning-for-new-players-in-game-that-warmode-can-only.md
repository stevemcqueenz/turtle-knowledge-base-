So I made my 4th HC character after 2 I had lost to deaths, and one I had no further interest in playing. I was using the WM boost safely in my starting zone and later turned it off incase I ran into anyone running to the Scarlet Monastery. After racking up a lot of finished quests I decided I was going to turn WM on again to get the XP boost and then I was going to be finished with it indefinitely. Well, I was unaware that WM was unable to be turned off a second time, thus basically rendering my HC character useless. I do not want to risk leveling him up only to be ganked by some 60 who has nothing better to do with their time. It's fine that this is in the game, but a prompt asking if you're sure that you want to be permanently in WM would have been nice.

I love the server and the hard work the team puts into the server; I think it's time to take a break after losing a 3rd HC character in a row. Sorry if there is another post on this subject.

Thank you for considering a change if you did, and may I see you in the future on another HC character!
