Lots of specs share names with another spec.

Resto druids/shams and Holy pallies/priests exist. Hell, Frost DKs were still named Frost DKs despite Frost Mages existing already. Prot pallies and Prot warriors share a spec name too.

This has never been an issue ever at all and I see no reason for this kind of change.

Quick edit: Also, reducing survival hunter to just "trapper" feels a bit silly. Their fantasy is far bigger than just "I place the traps". There's an emphasis on poison. On close combat. On evasive maneuvers and slightly more tankiness than other hunter specs.
