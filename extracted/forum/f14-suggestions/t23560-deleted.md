The second of your suggested features is just an equivalent of Blizzard's PVE&PVE separation that was gradually implemented since TBC. It only widened the gap between PVP and PVE players.

TWoW servers with highest population are built based on a PVE baseplate. I insist that PVP gears should be good PVE gears - PVP quests in leveling zones typically reward better. Making PVP more insulated would only prevent more players from considering updating themselves through PVP. I tend to suggest a method of simplifying inclination. As below they go (though immature):

 1st, adjust the stats of current PVP gears (of two kinds - blues and purples) and boost them to AQ40 or even Naxx level.

 2nd, when the gears are equally matched with their PVE counterparts, have the time and energy consumption of getting them also well matched with corresponding PVE activities'. (The first two are relatively more simple and based on the underdeveloped vanilla system.)

 3rd, encourage outdoor/world PVP activities by rewarding PVP out of BGs. Reactivate quests like "Wanted: Kill xxx" and add one urging players to engage more fight in open world. One character yields non-BG honour only once (or a bit more) per week/day, making it less profitable to abuse the mechanism and motivating players to be more aggressive. Other PVP-dependent activities like leveling with double experience may need to offer more as the risk is higher.
