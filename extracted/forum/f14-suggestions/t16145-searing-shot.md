Hi, at this state of the Game the Searing Shot of Nightelf Priests is a pure meme with absolutly NO practical use. Beside a massive Statwaste wearing a bow it has also no use what so all in any dmg Rotation thats why i would have 3 little sugestions that in my Opinion are all not gamebreaking but would make it an interesting option for Discipline Priests:

 1. Change Searing Shot from Fire Damage to Holy Damage

This change could indeed make Searing Shot more relevant for Night Elf Priests. By converting the damage type to Holy, it would synergize better with other Holy abilities and gear, including T2.5 Smite sets. It also maintains thematic consistency, as Holy and Discipline Priests are primarily driven by their connection to Light. Balancing this change would be crucial to ensure it doesn’t disrupt the gameplay but could help give the spell a niche usefulness that it currently lacks.

 2. Allow Searing Shot Crits to Proc Searing Light

If Searing Shot is treated as a viable offensive option, allowing its crits to trigger Searing Light would encourage players to utilize it more. This change would incentivize players to incorporate it into their rotations and builds, especially for those focusing on crit-heavy gear and playstyles. Given that Searing Shot is categorized under Discipline spells, it seems logical for it to fit into this mechanic.

 3.Make Bows Benefit from Wand Specialization

Given that its otherwise impossible for Priests ot reach physical hit cap, granting Bows the benefit of Wand Specialization would encourage hybrid builds and add a functional synergy for players who wish to engage in ranged combat more effectively. It would provide an incentive to utilize bows in a meaningful way without drastically unbalancing gameplay.

Conclusion

None of these suggestions appear to be game-breaking, and they could serve to enhance the diversity of gameplay for Discipline Priests, especially those focusing on different damage profiles. Adjustments like these could make Searing Shot a more appealing option, encouraging player creativity and exploration of hybrid builds.
