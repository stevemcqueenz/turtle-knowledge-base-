# Hi! paladin protection tree swap 1h spec with imp Justice

## Post 23563 by Amzra — 2022-08-26T17:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23563#p23563 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all
I want to share how impressive is the thought process, the passion and the work you have invested to build this version of vanilla wow. It brings balance to the overall dynamics,  justice to certain underdog specs, respect to the lore and openings for the future.

I was looking at the paladin protection tree. The improved seal of justice is such a good idea. It was an overdue necessary change for paladin tank but also a major difference to give them a taunt compare to the original game.

I would suggest to swap the 1h specialization talent with the new improved seal of justice. First I think the improved seal of justice is a much more powerful talent than the 10% to 1h for the class. Second I think  having a proper taunt should be reserved to the more committed paladin tank, those who invest 30ish point in the protection tree. I think having a taunt is iconic to tanks and as it is not baseline for paladin, only committed prot pal would have access to it. It would not be available for those with other main spec who tank as secondary role. Third, on the other hand, I think putting 1h spec lower in the tree would make it available for sword and board Ret paladin, or sword and whatever beneficial spell power off-hand. That available 10% on 1h would open interesting alternatives to the usual slowest 2hander with +6% dmg talent.

Ret paladin would then loose the benefits of the improved seal of justice talent to tank when needed, but the 10% dmg  on their 1h would help them to build some additional threat as secondary tank role. That would be a softer but still significant benefit they didn’t have access before. Also they would still have all their other tools with holy powers to generate high threat and maintain aggro like they always did.

Edit: Still thinking…  maybe bring 1h spec on the second row, improved armor on fourth row and improved seal of justice on seventh row. that would allow ret paladin to have sanctify aura in holy tree and 1h spec in prot tree if they want a strong 1h build, or invest 21 pts  in prot to be a strong secondary tank, and then only prot paladin would have the imp seal of justice.
