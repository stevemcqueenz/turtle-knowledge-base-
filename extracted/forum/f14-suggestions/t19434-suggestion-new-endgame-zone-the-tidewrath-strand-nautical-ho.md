# [Suggestion] New Endgame Zone: The Tidewrath Strand (Nautical Horror + Titanic Lore)

## Post 132171 by Tenyar — 2025-05-20T16:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132171#p132171 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

“It doesn’t want to rise.
It doesn’t want to be worshipped.
It wants to die.
And it does not know how.”

Hey everyone!
I wanted to share a concept for a new high-level (55–60) zone that’s been kicking around in my head for a while. I feel like it’s consistent with Classic Azeroth, while expanding on some lore, and atmospheric exploration like the other custom zones in Turtle WoW. This zone would be accessible from the Eastern Plaguelands, to the right of Alah’thalas, steeped in ancient mysteries, nautical horror, and forgotten history.

 **Location:** A newly revealed coastal region, hidden for centuries behind the towering cliffs east of the Eastern Plaguelands and shrouded by unnatural tides and arcane mist. Long thought to be unreachable, the way has been revealed by cataclysmic tremors—aftershocks from the Scourge's foul desecration of Lordaeron—which collapsed part of the cliffs and unveiled a forgotten tunnel—revealing the way to a place meant to stay lost.

 **Level Range:** 55–60, with Faction-Neutral Questlines

 **Major Zone Themes:**
- Forgotten Wars – Explore ancient battlefields and sunken relics from the Troll Wars and Arathorian naval campaigns.

Nautical Horror – Ghost ships, drowned undead, Amani ruins, and cliffs howling with storms and secrets.

Ancient Power Misused – A power drawn from something vast and living… a being that never asked to be awakened.

Buried Guilt – The truth is grim: this place was sealed not to contain a monster, but to protect a tormented Titanic sentinel—from us.

 **Main Storyline:**  **The Leviathan Below**

At the heart of the Strand lies **Aegaran, the Deep Sentinel**—a Titanic construct forged by Keeper Ra and Odyn to patrol the darkest oceanic trenches. Designed with command over pressure, sonar, and storm, Aegaran fell to the slow poison of the Curse of Flesh. Unlike others who became mortal, it twisted into a terrible hybrid of metal and meat—immortal, agonized, unraveling.

Through the centuries, mortals found it:

Amani trolls of the coastal tribes worshipped it as Zul’Nathak, the Drowned Star, calling upon its pain-wracked power to summon storms and beasts. Arathorian high mages enslaved it, draining its essence to fuel arcane fleets. Fel-corrupted orcs during the First War sought to harness it—each time it's power stolen, it's mind frayed further..

In time, a coalition of Highborne, Arathorian Sea Guard, and even rogue blue dragons rose to break the cycle. The Leviathan was sealed in slumber, its torment hidden beneath Warding Anchors and obscuring mist. The coastline was erased from maps, and memory.

But now, the Brinebound Cult—heirs to those who once worshipped or exploited the Leviathan—seek to break the seals and awaken their “god” anew.

As haunted ships drift ashore and unnatural storms batter the land, Horde and Alliance adventurers alike are drawn into the Strand, unraveling mysteries, clashing with cultists, and plumbing the depths of ancient wrongs. At the heart of it all lies Aegaran, whose suffering threatens to drown the world.

 **Key Locations:** **Blackstone Anchorage**

A faction-neutral quest hub built into the bones of a sunken Arathorian warship. Potentially even a neutral Auction House location.

 **Tidefallen Watch** (Alliance)

Clifftop bastion overlooking the sea, outfitted with storm-beaten batteries and a network of sea caves accessible via scaffolding.

 **Brinehook Redoubt** (Horde)

A repurposed sea-fort nestled in a tidal grotto, built upon the bones of a failed orcish expedition.

 **Whispering Reefs**

A submerged ruin warped by Naga coral pylons and water-bent runestones. The sea here speaks, if you listen too long…

 **Dungeon:**  Catacombs of the Drowned Kings
A half-submerged crypt housing Arathorian sea-lords and long-buried relics. Within lies a Titan vault—and the override key to the Leviathan’s core.

 **Faction Conflict:**
The zone culminates in a DM Tribute-style choice system:

Free it, allowing it to vanish beneath the waves, never to be used again, hoping its torment fades into myth.
Or destroy it… to end its pain forever, and ensure none may ever exploit its power again.

Thank you for reading! I’d love to hear your thoughts, ideas, and feedback.
