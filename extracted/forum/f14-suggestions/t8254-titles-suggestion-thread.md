# Titles Suggestion Thread

## Post 53132 by Zeldy (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-07-12T05:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53132#p53132 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With Patch 1.17.0 on the horizon, one of the updates I'm most excited for is the addition of new titles. Titles are a nice little personalization for your character that can be used for roleplay or just simple vanity. I would like to propose some potential new titles as well as the means for how to obtain them. The list will be somewhat short and straightforward initially but more will be added later on.

 **Professions**

These titles would be earned through leveling the various professions available in-game. They might help with incentivizing players to level professions they otherwise wouldn't touch, *especially* Fishing.

 **Chef** <Name> - Rewarded for raising Cooking to its max proficiency

 **Field Medic** <Name> - Rewarded for raising First Aid to its max proficiency

 **Salty** <Name> - Rewarded for raising Fishing to its max proficiency

<Name>**, the Bushcrafter** - Rewarded for raising Survival to its max proficiency

<Name>**, the Prodigy** - Rewarded for raising every secondary profession and both primary professions to their max proficiency

 **Reputations**

These titles would be earned through reaching Exalted with various PvE Factions.

<Name>**, Timbermaw Defender** - Rewarded for reaching Exalted with Timbermaw Hold (Suggested by Jammyxx)

<Name> **of the Alliance/of the Horde** - Rewarded for reaching Exalted with all Home Cities of the player's Faction (Suggested by Jammyxx)

 **Bloodsail Admiral** <Name> - Rewarded for reaching Exalted with the Bloodsail Buccaneers (Suggested by Jammyxx)

 **Player vs. Player**

These titles would be earned through participating in PvP.

<Name>**, the Bloodthirsty** - Rewarded for reaching Exalted with Blood Ring (Suggested by Jammyxx)

 **Justicar/Conqueror** <Name> - Rewarded for reaching Exalted with all Battleground Factions (Suggested by Jammyxx)

<Name>**, the Kingslayer/Chieftain Slayer** - Rewarded for killing the enemy Faction's leaders (Suggested by Jammyxx)

 **Player vs. Environment**

These titles would be earned through completing various PvE challenges.

 **Dungeon Master** <Name> - Rewarded for defeating every Dungeon and Raid boss in the game (Suggested by Jammyxx)

<Name>**, Keeper of the Keys** - Rewarded for obtaining every available key in the game.

 **Class Exclusive**

These titles could be achieved by a single class. Still WIP

<Name>**, the Beastmaster** - Rewarded to Hunter players for taming every type of beast in the game (Suggested by Krotux)

<Name>**, the Voidcaller** - Rewarded to Warlock players for enslaving every type of demon in the game (Suggested by Krotux)

<Name>**, the Light of Dawn/the Shadow of Dusk** - Rewarded to Priest players for forging Benediction/Anathema

 **Demonslayer** <Name> - Rewarded to Hunter players for forging Rhok'delar/Lok'delar

If you would like to propose more titles of your own, please do so below. Just make sure to include how the title could be obtained.
