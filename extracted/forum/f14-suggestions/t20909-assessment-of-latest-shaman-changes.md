# Assessment of latest shaman changes

## Post 145336 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-14T08:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145336#p145336 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just as I thought devs had come to their senses and reverted the abomination that is electrify, they drop another bomb on us. I'll go through these one by one.

- **Calming Winds: Increased threat reduction from 5/10/15% to 8/16/25%.**
So, this is indirectly part of the issue. The shaman melee dps and tank spec is devs' golden goose of the month. The CC2 changes to the enha tree, giving elemental access to Stormstrike and very strong tank stats, has been the cause of complains from alliance side for months now. The enha talents are the core of the issue, yet these are not touched. Because devs now seem to have an infatuation with making shaman tank work, which makes them unwilling to make the necessary pvp-related nerfs if that makes shaman tank weaker.

- **Improved Fire Totems: Now also increases Searing Totem's range by 5/10 yards.**
The latest Ele tree has a much stronger emphasis on fire spells for some reason. Devs seem to want to push new specs down people's throat.

- **Purge: Now has a 8 second cooldown.**
Ofcourse, the most significant nerf of them all. This is purely based of retridin whine in pvp-general. This renders purge essentially useless as alliance are already stacked with small buffs, which is why Purge needs to be spammable in order to get to the important buffs. Mage and Priest shields especially. This will be a significant buff for these classes. I don't know how the situation is on Nord but smite priests on Tel'abim are absolutely insane, and this will make them even worse. Ofcourse, this affects melee classes in pvp too, since shamans will be practically unable to purge shields off casters whom they are in melee with.

Between this and the grounding totem nerf, devs are killing the support capacity and identity of elemental shamans. They seem to want to make us just a caster turret. This is not improving class pvp flavor - it's killing it.

- **Eye of the Storm: Now only prevents losing cast time on damaging spells.**
This is a 3 point talent investment that procs after getting critted. Devs have said before they want to decrease the burst of pvp. Here, instead, they nerf basically the only defensive ability of elemental shamans: self-heal.

- **Nature's Swiftness renamed to Ancestral Swiftness and now reads: When activated, your next Nature spell with a casting time less than 10 sec becomes an instant cast spell. Affected damaging spells have 25% reduced effectiveness.**
After Purge nerf this is the most regrettable decision in my personal opinion. I'll say it again: the alliance whine about shamans is not about ele/resto. It's about ele/enha. To get NS, we already sacrifice ca 10 points on mediocre talents in the resto tree - this is the tradeoff we make to get NS, always has been. Now NS gets nerfed, while this spec has not been the problem during CC2. I just can't comprehend it. It seems to be a targeted effort to kill the OG vanilla pvp specs just because. Ele/resto is THE dps/support spec in pvp, and with this and the changes above, you are killing the support role of the spec and making it just another glass turret.

To summarize, as so many times before, players enjoying anything else than devs' flavor of the month spec (in this case shaman tank; see also melee hunter) must suffer for the golden goose to live. The access to Stormstrike, strong armor bonuses, and the T3 set bonuses (which you wisely nerfed) is the chief reason why alliance players have complained about shaman shocks over the last months. Yet, Stormstrike is not only changed but BUFFED since you seem intent on proving your pve pet projects can work. All of the changes listed above are workarounds with significant negative effects for other specs, while avoiding to address the core issue.

Shamans already had three viable roles. Why are you so intent on destroying them to make your fourth idea work?

Advice:
 -Revert the negative changes listed above
 -Move Stormstrike down the talent tree
 -if Enha purges are the problem, give them the cd as part of their armor talents. This won't affect your tank adventures.

## Post 145339 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-14T08:37:00Z

I understand that the Purge cooldown has caused frustration among some shamans, but from the perspective of classes that rely heavily on defensive buffs to function in PvP such as mages, priests and druids, unlimited Purge is oppressive.

Shamans can strip away an entire defensive toolkit within seconds. Inner Fire, Power Word: Shield, Frost Armor, Ice Barrier, druid HoTs and similar effects all dissapears. The shields also have CDs mind you. This is very different from priest dispel mechanics where the mana cost is much higher and sustained spam comes at the expense of actually being able to heal or attack. Enhancement shamans in particular can purge repeatedly while still maintaining full offensive pressure, and in many cases they can global a target even if those defensive buffs are still active.

In battlegrounds it is becoming inconvenient to even buff teammates as Alliance because of the absolute lack of drawbacks when spamming Purge. Shamans can rotate shocks on cooldown and spend the time in between simply removing buffs and HoTs with no real cost.

When key buffs and healing over time effects are removed instantly the fight often becomes unplayable for the defending class. Tremor Totem and Grounding Totem already provide shamans with strong reactive defensive tools and both have cooldowns. It is reasonable for Purge to also have a cooldown given its game changing impact on certain matchups.

This is not about removing shaman identity. It is about preserving counterplay for all classes so matches are determined by skill and timing rather than who can remove a defense bar in under two seconds.
