# Post a schedule somewhere for official ingame events

## Post 69024 by Darkcovenant — 2023-10-05T21:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69024#p69024 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To elaborate on the title, we should have a pinned schedule on the forums+discord which detail the dates of events such as Midsummer and Noblegarden. Perhaps it could also give an indicator as to whether the event contains custom content such as the excellent Twow portrayal of Winter's Veil, I think myself and many others wouldn't bother with some events in their very basic vanilla form, but would be enticed to partake with the lure of original Twow content.
