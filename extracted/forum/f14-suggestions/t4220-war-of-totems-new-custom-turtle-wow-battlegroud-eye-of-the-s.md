## Post 27128 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-10-11T17:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27128#p27128 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I like the idea of adapting Eye of the Storm to Classic. I even like the idea of making it a gorge instead of an island, which I think opens up more location possibilities than leaving it as an island; I think you did a good job showing it. The map edits to add the battleground entrances are also very well done, and the addition of lower level librams and idols for paladins and shamans is interesting.

My main gripe with making it dwarves vs tauren is that it retreads the same narrative as Alterac Valley: A group of dwarves on a sovereign imperialistic initiative by Magni Bronzebeard to secure artifacts to discover the truth about their origins (Bael'dun/Stormpike Guard) invade a foreign region ("Totem Gorge"/Alterac Valley) where they fight the locals that have taken offense to their excavations (tauren/Frostwolf Clan).

Making use of the concept of Bael Modan vs Tauran as an extended questing area for southern Barrens could be interesting, but for a PvP battleground I think it'd be more fruitful story-wise to look to other narratives first.

Right now we have:

 -Warsong Clan (orcs) trying to cut more lumber in Ashenvale for the Horde vs Silverwing Sentinels (night elves) trying to preserve the forest;
 -League of Arathor (humans) trying to retake their home of Arathi vs the Defilers (undead) trying to take over Arathi from the humans;
 -Stormpike Guard (dwarves) trying to secure artifacts for their heritage vs Frostwolf Clan (orcs) trying to protect their home;
 -Blood Ring is an arena ran by the Steamwheedle Cartel (goblins);
 -Sunnyglade Valley is the outlier, being explicitly set in the past, but it pits humans against orcs in one of the first clashes of the First War.

Making "Totem Gorge" dwarves vs tauren makes it so we have two "dwarves trying to secure artifacts for their heritage vs locals trying to protect their home"-themed battlegrounds.

However, the tauren are indeed lacking in battleground presence so I think it's a pretty good idea to have a battleground where they have a vested interest shown.
