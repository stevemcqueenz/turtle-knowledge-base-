# Goblin Priest Idea

## Post 166512 by Kald — 2026-01-26T10:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166512#p166512 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I thought that Goblins could use a Healing class since they currently do not have one.   happy_turtle

Priest Racial Ideas

Priestly Protective Equipment
(Idea behind this is to show Goblin greed to protect themselves over others and funny PPE)
This could also give another purpose to caster shields which currently can only be used by Holy Paladin, Elemental/Restoration Shaman. I figured this would give another purpose to Int and Spirit shields.

Cheap Prayer
Basically Desperate Prayer but could be cast on allies. A cheap and fast racial heal

Confession
Something to reduce damage taken by self or allies if casted on friendly. Something to increase damage taken if cast on enemies.

Healing Spray
Basically just the Alchemist ability from Warcraft 3. An AOE heal.

Acid Bomb
Again the Warcraft 3 Alchemist ability. AOE damage and armor reduc (so a damage increase by like 3% or something)

These are just rough ideas that I thought might could fit for Goblin Priest. Just wanted to get the idea out there and hopefully spur the idea to get Goblins a healing class.   maintenance_turtle
