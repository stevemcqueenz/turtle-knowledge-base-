## Post 85845 by Terramagnus — 2024-01-25T17:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85845#p85845 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

5 minutes versus 30, 60 minutes, or even just a simple toggle akin to an aura;

One player in this thread said "don't get lazy
this kinda of thinking ultimately leads to you laying motionless and everything is done for you.. lol"

But this is ignorant of the entire base argument. *Is pressing the button more often, more fun?*

Blizzard seemed to initially design the weapon imbues as a mirror of the paladin's seals. Seals provide on-hit bonuses and effects and are cast at the start of and during combat due to them only 20 seconds or so.

Shaman weapon buffs, only lasting 5 minutes, seem to have been originally designed to be something you would activate upon starting combat, so the mana cost and duration would factor into balancing the shaman's mana output in combat.

A paladin's seal has a short duration, can be consumed via judgement to cast debuffs upon the target, and swapped between to perform various strategies during fights. Casting seals often is an interesting and involved mechanic, and is core to the feeling of playing a paladin.

No such engaging gameplay exists for shaman imbues- they are simple short-lived buffs that, despite the presence of which being considered baseline to the class, exert a tedious and relevant mana cost without offering additional mechanics, simply feeling like other thing that must be automated with an addon for the sake of "fixing" another annoyance a blizzard employee haphazardly slapped into the game during the mad rush to balance classes before release.

Let's not forget that paladin player buffs were made 5-minutes for this same reason- a misunderstanding of what balances abilities.

In short, if the buff is meant to have a low duration it should be for a specific reason.
Pressing the button more often != fun gameplay, it is tedium. Paladins seals are fun gameplay with short buffs. Shaman imbues are tedium.

 30 minutes seems to be the standard for casted buffs in the game, so make it 30 minutes. And hell, make paladin buffs 30 minutes too. I see no reason why buffs should not share a universal duration.
