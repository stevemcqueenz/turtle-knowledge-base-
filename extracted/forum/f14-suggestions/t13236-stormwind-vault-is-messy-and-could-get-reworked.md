# Stormwind Vault is messy and could get reworked

## Post 88704 by Skumbanana (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-02-25T13:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88704#p88704 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We were a group of fresh 60's. Stormwind Vault was a very messy experience. Suggestions:

Fix aggro range for mobs. It's all over the place. Pulls are inconsistent; you either pull one mob or end up pulling the whole room, without a reason for this.

Fix mob placement. It's awkward and makes for awkward pulls. Placing mobs right behind passageways doesn't make sense.

Remove knockback mechanics or make knockback shorter. This is a bad idea for such cramped spaces. You can't zoom out, because corridors are tight, everything gets in your face and it's a mess.

Side entrance outside Stormwind for horde is a nice idea, but it's just lazy design that this leads you to the same entrance inside Stormwind. Would be consistent if this would lead you to another place inside the dungeon, like you have in Gnomeregan and other dungeons...

Oh, and, please, can we have bosses not spam chat with yells? Don't know about you, but this felt truly annoying.

Otherwise, the dungeon is a nice addition.

Just my 2 cents, anyway

I agree, it needs a total visual and layout redesign. It just feels really cheap that they reused Stockades assets, it literally feels like a 60 version of Stockades (and Stockades is one of the worst designed dungeons in the game). There is nothing in its design to suggest that it's a vault and not another prison.

The hallways and rooms should be more spacious and polished, with big vault doors and other security measures, locked doors (giving rogues with lockpicking, engineers with C4, blacksmiths with skeleton keys and mages with Khadgar's Unlocking more utility in the dungeon), more branching paths (some behind the aforementioned locked doors, accessible only if your group can unlock them). Treasure rooms filled with chests, coins and artifacts (visual only, we don't want to crash the economy kek). Maybe even have some scripted events (other than the stupid buggy rat door). And please fix the chest loot from the final boss so people don't ninja it. Make it roll-able like any other loot (this goes for chests in other dungeon too!)

I like the concept of SWV but not the execution. Let me rant about it like the manchild I am.

 1. Vaults? Proper name would be Crazed Crystal-Mindcontrolled Alliance Alcatraz + there is a chest at the end so it's totally a vault. You know what has a vault? BRD. This one has a chest.
 2. Placement. It is nice that devs put a horde entrance outside of SW but a quest for accessing it would have been better. Right now you motivate a bunch of level 60 hordes to visit the lake next to the beautiful village of Goldshire jut to try out an endgame dungeon and get the whip. I'll try to illustrate the problem with a made-up story. Let's say you are a group of horde friends interested in trying out the custom dungeon. You are Wifebreaker the orc furry warrior. You have a talk with your friends, Siripullalot, the troll hunter and Gankblade, the undead rogue. To get to this famous vault, you take a zep to Grom'gol then head to the North. Upon your journey towards the destination you see like 50 leveling warmode players in Duskwood and another 50 around Goldshire. Your inner zugzug, like a triumphant anthem of honor and destruction starts to go rampant. The next thing you realize is a pile of level 20 elves around you. You didn't plan to gank today, wanted to farm dungeons but to farm one of them you had to travel through the Alliance low-level zones where the grass is green and the level 7 paladins are abundant. After your destructive mayhem, you arrive tio the dungeon which you guys clear. I mean the placement of the horde entrance invites people to some ganking even if they didn't plan to gank in the first place.
 3. Loot. Compare it to Black Morass which gives rep, has a teleport, has many epic behind a rep wall and has many epic boss drops (trinket is even valid until late-raiding for casters). Heck, even the blue drops are early raid-worthy. In SWV you get a whip for the domina fantasy, and some tanking and lock gear which you immediately replace at ZG.
 4. There are dungeons where some classes are more useful than others and it is fine. To a degree. But man. You get a warlock. Warlock controls steroid imp. You clear a wing. Yo go further. Mad crystal meth final boss summons the elementals. Eleemntals hit hard. Would have hit hard I mean. Banish happened. GG. At least make the mobs the final boss summons typeless to avoid CC there.
 5. This might be my shortcoming but what's even the story behind the place? You had a vault in SW which is apparently a prison with one chest. Weird crystal moved in, people went mad. Now the priso... vault is home to flying spiders, steroid imps and zombies. What does this crystal want? How does it exist in the middle of the capital? Why is this a vault??
