Totally a me problem! I've been twinking a level 59 Paladin this year. I've done some MC raiding and have found that, although I do have 2 Greater Blessings, might and wisdom, available to me they are both non max rank so often times I end up just not being assigned a blessing as someone else in raid is level 60 doing might and wisdom.

I've talked with the guru, Laughadin himself. I thought, maybe, making GB of kings available at level 58 would be a good change but because of that blessing being used for AoE threat as prot pally it would not be suitable to drop down to a lower level. I propose that, instead, GB of salvation be dropped down to level 58. Since salvation is a purely pve blessing it would have no balance shakeup to the pvp side of not actually being a level 60 but would give a non-60 paladin a blessing more meaningful to contribute on a raid setting. Thoughts?
