Bug Ticket - Illusion: Tiny Murloc - Missing Skin Colors #14427
<https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=14427>

Describe the Issue:  Illusion: Tiny Murloc - is missing the purple and the white skins.  They were accidently removed when the Illusion system was updated a few months ago around May 13, 2025.

How to reproduce: Keep clicking the "Illusion: Tiny Murloc", you will see the following colors: Blue, Green, Pink, and Red. Missing: Purple and White.

How is it supposed to work: Keep clicking the "Illusion: Tiny Murloc", you should see the following colors: Blue, Green, Pink, Red, Purple, and White.

Character the bug occurred on: All Characters

Side note:  Allowing the "Illusion: Tiny Murloc" to use mounts was amazing.  I would love to see that return.  Helps me RP as a Murloc Paladin :)
