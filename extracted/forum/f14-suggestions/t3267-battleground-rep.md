With how battlegrounds have been going, it's extremely unlikely anyone can make meaningful progress in either WSG or AB rep, only AV.

I think the health of the PvP scene on the server could be greatly helped by a temporary boost to rep gained from both of these battlegrounds in the same vein as other temporary/not-so-temporary assistances like Turtlhu, AH listing fees, lower numbers to make BGs pop, and raid scaling which took into account the pop of the server and the activity therein.
