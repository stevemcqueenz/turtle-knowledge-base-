# Do something about boosting through mob tagging

## Post 110574 by Snoodydood (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-29T19:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110574#p110574 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, there is a new meta to speed level alts for people by dual boxing and having a low level tag a mob then have their lvl 60 mob kill everything and gets load of exp from elite mobs in the open world. I think something should be done about this kind of boosting as it's getting to be a little too much as of late. people have already completed both boaring adventure challenge and the fashion week event simply by boosting their own characters just to get free stuff. Tell me; how is this in the "spirit of exploration and adventure" of turtle wow?  back when people would boost their hardcore character in the same way before they made rules against it, I was one of the people complaining about that meta frequently as well. And just like with that issue, I'm sure people will say "why do you care if it's not effecting you?" or "let people play the way they want" and to that I'll say; there's nothing wrong with playing the way you want, but there have to be rules in place just as we already have for dual boxing or things get out of control. just like I said back then if that's your philosophy, you may as well turn you head to people using exploits or cheats of any kind in the game, after all, if they aren't effecting you why do you care if they are cheating?
If this kind of meta is not defined or restricted by any rules, eventually it will become the new necessary norm to compete on the server and the idea of actually playing the game will be lost. I have no problem with say, running your alt character through a dungeon to get a piece if gear at a certain level or complete quests or partying to complete a particular quest and I think that dual boxing SHOULD be allowed but in my opinion, there should be more clear rules in place for leveling alts this way that require you to be in an actual party with the alt and not simply chasing around you high level character to kill mobs you tag. I think this kind of thing shouldn't be allowed when your alt is taking on challenges of any kind as well.
