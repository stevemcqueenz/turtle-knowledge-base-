For what it is worth, when I did those elite quests in Redridge, people often formed full groups of 5 (except when challenge limitations made it impossible with hc not being able to group with non-hc), and even with that being the case, there usually were more than 5 people camping a spawn, with groups running between the camped spawns trying to snipe a tag or waiting for one of the camping groups to get their kill and leave, so that they can then take their place.

This was 2-3 months ago, with occasional login queues with there being too many players on the server at peak times. I'm not sure if I did do them during a peak time, since my night/day rhythm is often abnormal and I play whenever, so chances are that the issue can be both worse or less of an issue depending on what time of the day/night you try to complete them.

So yes, if your group failed to tag the monster, you had to wait for the 15 minute(?) respawn and try again, and it was always a contest of speed. If you didn't beat your keyboard in autistic frenzy trying to get the tag the chances were high that your group would be looking at another quick-time event for getting the tag in however long it took for the monster to respawn. Getting stuck there for over 30 minutes seems like the norm rather than the exception.

For the 2 characters of mine that did those elite quests, there never was a time when the group the character was in was the only one contesting those spawns. I remember taking a measure of time waiting for Tharil'zun to respawn and that timer did seem to be 15 minutes exactly. Gath'ilzogg seemed to be faster in this regard.

Suggested 'fixes':

The respawn timers could and should be made faster, with a random portion included in the timer, e.g. instead of 900 seconds, it could be 300+random(300). Having randomness involved in the timer makes it less of a 'time the spawn and snipe it' after exactly N seconds after the monster's death', which is more fair for everyone, but I can see how it would aggravate the people who time the respawns exactly.

Another option would be to alter the quest to give you a challenge flag that you plant on the ground at Tharil'zun's area, which would instantly spawn the elite orc with a few minions. This would get rid of the respawn hell altogether for that enemy and is possibly the best approach to this issue.

Or turn that fort into an instanced location like the Stockades is, but this will alter the way pvp behaves in that location.
