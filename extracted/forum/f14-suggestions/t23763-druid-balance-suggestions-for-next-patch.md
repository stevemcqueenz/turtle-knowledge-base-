Hello, i got some suggestions for druid balance i would like to share (not sure if some1 already talked about the topics i will say now in other threads but lets go):

On eclipse talent you guys could add the line "chance to proc is increased by 30% on starfire and 60% on wrath critical dmg" or "chance to proc is increased by 100% on critical dmg"... motive for that is sometimes i cast more than 10 wraths and 6 starfire and eclipse never procs to the point that the other eclipse cd was over (idk maybe this is me and my crappy lucky).

Since you guys are reducing the amount needed to get max talent on "Balance of all Things" (max level from 5 to 3), you could remove the "slow in attack speed" line from "Gale Winds" talent (idk if some1 use this talent for anything but i find kinda useless in PvE) and add a increase to the dmg of hurricane (something like 10% or 20%) AND/OR reduce cd in 10s... motive for that is druid aoe is not like mages, locks and priest that can easily change location of aoe if mobs run around too much (you need to wait and think carefully before cast the hurricane or you can cast in a stupid location and thats it for your aoe dmg in that fight) and shaman aoe have a cd but at least slow enemies movement in the area. And the increase in aoe dmg is a need since even if u get a luck cast on starfire to proc solar eclipse to increase the dmg of nature before cast hurricane (some fights if you try cast a second starfire to get the proc the fight is already over) druids cant compete with the other classes (in this case mages priests and locks) aoe dmg (maybe im being too competitive on this suggestion   angry_turtle ).

Battle Res (Rebirth) mana consumption, you could add in "Nature's Reach" talent a line to reduce the mana cost by 50% (at max level) because in the mid of the fight you are barely hanging with mana, healers are annoying you for innervate and raid leader ask you to Bress and you cant because the mana needed is too much (1057 mana required to cast rebirth) and you still need to go look around for the corpse and stop dps and need to leave moonkin form to use Battle Ress (more 273 mana to return to moonkin form), balance druids already have to carry the burden of dispelling poison and curses when called for since cat druids dont do it unless by the sound of the whip on their backs.

Owlking Frenzy Talent, you guys could increase the proc chance to 50% and add the line to increase balance druid spell threat by 15% when activated, making this talent useful for tanking bosses like twin emperors and increasing the classes that could tank this kind of boss fights, motive for that is sometimes the raid dont have enough locks or locks with gear to tank this kind of fight making it hard and more possible strats and possibilities to do diff types of fights in future raids.

Moonkin form, it should be added something that makes the moonkin form more unique right now we basically got armor (that is useless since half of our gear is cloth and we lack any decent defensive cds and why a dps needs armor in 1st place, unless you sucks at controling your agro maybe), a decrease in mana for balance spells (ok thats helpfull) and the aura that basically is an increase to the crit of other caster classes since druid dont get any extra crit chance from the talents like mages (arcane impact and critical mass), priest (Force of will and divinity), locks (Devastation) and shamans (call of thunder and tidal mastery) and they all can get the full power of moonkin aura (if in the same group), and in comparison to other forms like bear form gets an equal aura, massive increase in armor, increase in stamina and the talents increase further crit, stamina and armor when in bear form, same for cat form that gets extra AP when in cat form and from talents, crit from aura and talents and huge boosts to attack speed and damage and for last the tree form gets a huge boost in Heal Power and same reduction in mana. Now some ideas for Moonkin Form, maybe an increase in Spell Power based on the amount of intellect stat (this way all druid forms gets a boost from one of the stats) or chance to refresh the DoTs when enemy gets hit with a balance spell of the same category (example wrath refresh insect swarm and starfire refresh moonfire) or chance to proc a buff called "blessing of the moon" that increase spell power by 30 and spell penetration 20 or chance to proc a buff called moonkin fury that increase the spell crit by 3% (those two will make the druid comunity go insane saying "another RNG wtf!?"   [mad_turtle_head ). IDK the community can put more ideas in the comments.

Well that's all i got to say for now about balance druid suggestions.

Hey there, I'm Balance Druid main since Vanillla Classic. I started late and didn't know we sucked back then (haha) but I enjoyed being the underdog and celebrated every win.

Coming from this era I was a bit tripped up by changes in later patches. What's always struck me was how "Eclipse" as a spell and game mechanic could stick around for so long.. even make it onto private servers.. I don't usually hate and dwell much on things but this Spell was a major contribution to me quitting retail WoW.

Largely boomkin feels fine to me here - a lot of stuff was fixed. It could be perfect without Eclipse and instead a more Classic  or TBC like talent that is true to the essence of being a druid on Turtle WoW which set out to build on top of Classic, extend and polish it.

Having a single spell dominate my playstyle so much doesn't fit the theme of Turtle WoW nor Druid and is outright wrong in this context. Pre-Eclipse we were more free to choose between what we want to cast and support like an Enhancer Shaman. Dps was secondary - we were utility first.
Treants for instance were a great utility in PvP and also in PvE for situations were a little extra damage was needed. That's a whole different flavor compared to something that's more RNG and prescriptive. What's your flavor as a druid? Does it align with what your playstyle is about or should feel like?
