# Fishing Challenge : Old Ironjaw / Old Crafty

## Post 161818 by Danielcoffey — 2025-12-06T16:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161818#p161818 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I suggest adding in the Rare fishing spawns of Old Ironjaw (Forlorn Cavern, Ironforge) and Old Crusty (Any Water, Orgrimmar) as a Challenge for dedicated Fishing players.

They have a restricted location and a (roughly) 1:2500 spawn rate and are BoP.

Even though these fish could not be tied to an Achievement, it might be possible to create a suitable Title if players score both fish. Soandso the Salty for example. An alternative would be to make them equippable in offhand with a unique skin.

They would make an awesome Roleplay addition to the game.

I am unsure how the Diplomat title would affect trying to obtain these fish. The point originally was that both Guards and Players would have a go at you in the opposing Capital. There may have been a minimum skill requirement to obtain the rare fish?
