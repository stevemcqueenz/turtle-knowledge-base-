# Refining Jewelcrafting

## Post 137149 by Nahath — 2025-06-26T19:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137149#p137149 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In many ways, I like the jewelcrafting profession. Building staves (a gap in the other professions), getting rings/amulets which are often very rare outside of dungeons in early levels, etc.

There were a few frustrations and disappointments, though, that I think need a light shined on them:

 1. Thematic material needs: Based on the amounts of mats used, jewelcrafting feels more like stonecrafting. You can see this in AH prices where stone cost skyrocketed on launch, while most gems are super-cheap. I would like to see the recipes tuned to make jewelcrafting more centered around gems.

 2. A few more leveling blues: I like the idea of building staves, but I never felt tempted to make one. Sticking in a blue staff or two for people in the 20-50 range would be nice

 3. Gems seem poorly tuned. This was covered in thorough detail in this thread, so I'll just refer to it: [viewtopic.php?p=123433](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123433)
