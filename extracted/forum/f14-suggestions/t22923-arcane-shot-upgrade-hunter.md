## Post 160037 by Odlnn — 2025-11-19T08:37:00Z

Hello!

Right now, the Marksmanship Hunter is not the best DPS in PvE, nor is it the strongest PvP class. You rarely see Hunters in the arena. My suggestion will not change it, it will just make the hunter gameplay more convenient.

As you know, Arcane Shot in vanilla is based on old game logic and deals a flat 183 damage with no ap scaling.

My suggestion is to make the spell more like its The Burning Crusade version by adding an Attack Power scaling. In TBC, it had a 15% coefficient. It also dispelled one magic effect, but I'm not sure if that's necessary here.

This change would add a more useful spell to the Hunter's PvP and PvE arsenal, making it more than just a spell to start auto-shotting.
