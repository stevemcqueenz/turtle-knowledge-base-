Hi all,

I've always found the alpha human models in WoW strangely fascinating, especially due to the fact they are still in the game. Players who have played TBC might recognise them from Caverns of Time Southshore, but they pop up frequently in Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor, and Turtle WoW has also placed a few NPCS using their models such as the questgiver 'Aneka Konko' in Ratchet.

I was wondering if it might be possible to add some of the alpha humans as illusions to the store as I think they would be fun to run around as, but more interestingly I feel they did a better job of representing asian and black humans than the playable human models we got, and this would be useful to RP a human outside of the EK, such as the aforementioned Aneka and her home of 'Kamio' (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=70048>)
