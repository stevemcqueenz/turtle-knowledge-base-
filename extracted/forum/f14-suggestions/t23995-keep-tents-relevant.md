# Keep tents relevant!

## Post 168607 by Witchofwest — 2026-02-17T15:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168607#p168607 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Greetings. I am an officer with the Morrowhearth, an rp guild dedicated to offering wanderers of all kinds rest.

Being neutral to both Horde and Alliance, much of our rp and play is done in the open world, away from cities and towns. We set fires and tents and offer conversation to whoever joins. This is an engaging way to interact with the world and foster connection between players. For new/non-roleplayers, this is an excellent introduction to rp. We have concerns that the combined buff to taverns and 25% cap on tent xp will kill these interactions.

The suggestion is this: either remove the cap on tent xp, or provide an additional benefit for using a tent. A bonus to spirit or some such.

Ultimately, tents should exist to make the wilds of Azeroth more lively. It would be a loss for them to become obsolete.

## Post 168630 by Tarael — 2026-02-17T18:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168630#p168630 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Not only does the utility of tents provide an incentive for players to help each other by placing them, which fosters a friendlier environment and brings the community closer, but it also offers a great and immersive way to make a camp away from towns and big cities. Slashing the rested XP cap from tents would make them borderline irrelevant, and, as others have pointed out, create a rift between the new players and the RP community, given that roleplay guilds often host charity events where they place tents for low level player characters.

Overall, lowering the XP cap from tents would be detrimental to the player community, remove some of the incentive for players to interact and help each other, and make tents an almost irrelevant feature.

I hope the devs will reconsider and keep the tents as they are, or make them tier-based like the items of other professions, with higher tier tents providing higher XP bonus.
