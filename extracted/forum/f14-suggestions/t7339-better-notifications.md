The support team did a great job of keeping players up to date on Discord during the last (May 18/19) outage.  However, when the announcement was made the realm was playable, the visual indicator in Discord announcing the server was coming back online was the same color as the other notices, so I ignored the notice...my fault, I know.

I'm far from a Discord guru and don't even have my own channel, so I may be out in left field here.  However, is it possible in Discord to use different colors for types of notifications (red = down, yellow = updates, green = all good/clear) so that users don't have to actually sit at their PCs and can monitor from across the room?
