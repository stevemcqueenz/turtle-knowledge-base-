# Dungeon Queuing: Unusual Builds

## Post 102320 by Eeeeeeeeeee — 2024-10-01T16:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102320#p102320 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd like to see the Dungeon Queuer allow you to queue as any role with any class, but any roles you queue for outside your class's expected are marked "Secondary." Queuing players also now pick if they will accept team mates queuing with Secondary roles.

Players can also self-mark as secondary if they are aware their builds, while in role, are non-standard enough to ruffle feathers.

For instance, player A queues for Mage DPS for a dungeon. They select to allow Secondary tanks as team mates. B-D do similarly, plus a healer.

Player E queues as Warlock tank, a secondary roll. Because players A-D all allowed Secondary roll tanks, the party is matched together. Good to go.

If any of B-D did not allow Secondary roll tanks, they'd wait for a Primary role tank, such as a Warrior, to queue.

This come from my experience Hunter tanking. So far some people have not been interested, but plenty are. It's just difficult to get their attention since Hunter tanks can't queue.

I like the idea but I'm not sure if this is the way.
For tank role this could work for some fringe-classes which, if done properly, can more or less tank.
Not for heals though, warriors having the option even to join as healers is a no.

I'm hesitant because of two things:
 1. It is more or less confirmed that shaman tanks will be totally valid for dungeons after patch. It probably means less waiting time at LFT and less need to do something like this.
 2. While many players play at a high level, many people are really bad. If we give the option to rogues/hunters/warlocks etc to join as tanks and desperate DPS (if waiting time is still bad post-patch) accepts it as 'whatever', chances are that a random Joe will try to tank for them as a rogue having 0 idea what he is doing. Current system works because tank tools are so great that even a random grandma could more or less lead a party through most dungeons. Same doesn't apply to classes with much more limited tanking toolkit.

## Post 102330 by Eeeeeeeeeee — 2024-10-01T20:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102330#p102330 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm very open to ideas on it, but my hope is that since people have to opt in to playing with unusual builds it would mitigate some of the issue there.

The main goals as I see it:
 1. Avoid worsening the experience for the average dungeon queuer
 2. Help alleviate dungeon queue times
 3. Help people wanting to run unusual builds find teammates that are accepting of their style

I can think of some ways to make it more clear, but each one complicates things. Plus at the end of the day if someone doesn't know how to do the thing they're trying to do it's always gonna be rough.

For instance, when the queue pops the ready marker can inform players of their Secondary role teammate's personal blurb. This would include a class/role description such as "Rogue Tank" and a self-reported experience level such as "New to WoW + New to Build" or "Experienced with WoW + New to Build" etc. At which point they could choose to opt out, maybe even avoid that character when further queuing.

I don't think players who opt to play with a random Hunter Tank or such will ever know for sure if they're walking into a very difficult dungeon run, with or without official tooling. Best we can do is help them talk to each other in a structured way.
