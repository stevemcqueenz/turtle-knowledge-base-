# Proposal for increase in profits generated from Donations

## Post 132833 by Constantinecn — 2025-05-24T16:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132833#p132833 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have over a decade of experience in the marketing industry, applying basic psychological principles to marketing for major international companies worldwide. I would like to offer a suggestion that would not only benefit the Turtle WoW community but increase the amount of money the Dev team generates from donations.

I will not be comparing any of the donation rewards to Retail WoW as these are two separate companies essentially!

Things I am NOT aware of: The country of the majority of the player base.
The costs to run, maintain and develop the Turtle-Wow infrastructure.
The current revenue generated from the donations shop.

To begin, the flexibility to donate any amount is a great feature, however it is poor marketing practice, as it does not take advantage of pushing your customers to make additional purchases, this is a simple and effective way to increase donations made.

Furthermore, I believe the items available in the donation shop to be grossly overpriced to the point that it is harming the potential for a significant number of donations, as a result reducing the overall profits that could be gained from the donations themselves.

A classic marketing example that is used to explain this is the two Pottery stores. The first store sells a terracotta pot for $79.99, the second store sells exactly the same pot for $29.99. Now you will always have sales of the $79.99 pot, for several reasons, some people like to pay more for things, this is basic psychology, the belief that paying more means you are getting a better product. However, the second store sells 3 of their $29.99 pots for every 1 the first store sells, giving the second store a higher income and the benefit of their customers thinking they got a great deal.

This example or ones similar too it, are well known in marketing circles.

As it currently stands, to purchase a Bank mule costs 250 Donations coins, this requires spending 25 Euros and equals the following:

AUD: $43
USD: $28
YEN: $4050

As an Australian, if I wanted to purchase a Bank Mule, a Repair Bot, and Auctioneer and a Mailbox, it would cost me 850 donation tokens. That is roughly $73 Euros, including the bonus tokens you receive. That works out to be the following:

AUD: $127
USD: $83
YEN: $11830

What I would like to propose is two suggestions: Firstly, Tiering the donation amounts and locking those amounts to a certain value, so as the minimum donation amount allows the purchase of a single basic item, my suggestion would be 200 donation tokens for a single item, as it is currently. and joint packages of two or three items which allows for the purchase of those items slightly cheaper than if they were purchased individually.

As an example an Auction bot and a Mailbox currently cost 400 donations token for both items, this is:

$38 Euros
$44 USD
$67 Aus
$6185 Yen

Seeing a "Trader Package" that contains both an Auction bot and mailbox for 350 donations tokens would be seen as a great deal and facilitate more purchases.

Secondly: I would like to see the donations based off USD not Euro's. This will bring the overall price down for donations but should have the added benefit of increasing the amount of donations received. 250 tokens for a Bank mule for $25 USD would equal:

Bank Mule
 250 Token USD BASED                            250 Tokens EURO BASED

AUD: $39                                                   AUD: $43
EURO: $22                                                 USD: $28
YEN: $3565                                                YEN: $4050

Bank,Mail,Auction,Repair (850 donation tokens purchase with bonus mechanic)

 850 Tokens USD BASED                        850 Tokens EURO BASED

AUD: $114                                             AUD: $127
EURO: $65                                             USD: $83
YEN: $10413                                          YEN: $11830

As you can see the price difference is not too significant but would definitely entice more players to make Donations. This will open up the donations shop to people who would not normally donate or who had seen the prices and been turned away due to the cost.
It is important to note at this point, these are donations and not purchases, but that will not stop players feeling a little bit cheated if the prices were to drop. this can be smoothed over with a title or gift for those that had made a purchase, different titles for different levels of donations would be my suggestion.

At the end of the day having 1000 people purchase 350 tokens for $350,000 USD is far better than having 250 people purchasing 400 for $100,000 Euros. Not only will players have the benefit of getting some fun items for a cheaper cost, the dev team will also benefit from an increase in donations.

In Summary, this is only a suggestion/proposal that I hope will cause the dev team to re-evaluate and look at their donations system, as i firmly believe they could be generating at minimum, 20% more revenue from donations than they currently do. While everything above is a very loose bunch of examples, the marketing principals are sound and well documented. I would like to see our TWow devs earning just a little bit more for their efforts, but in a way that also benefits the community.

Lowering the donation shop prices by changing the base currency will increase donations.

Additional noteworth comment, I am Australian, so changing the shop to USD is of no real benefit to me.
