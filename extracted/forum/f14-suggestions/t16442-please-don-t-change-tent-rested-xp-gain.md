# Please Don't Change Tent Rested XP Gain

## Post 112150 by lollypoopdeck123 — 2024-12-11T02:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112150#p112150 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please do not change the speed at which rest is gained for the tents, as it currently is during this event. Having a 10-15-minute wait time to get fully rested does not positively impact gameplay and only dissuades new players from wanting to play. Stalled/Unactive gameplay does not equal good gameplay. Either have the tent as it currently is or do not have it at all.

I don't understand why people would like to make inns and tents mechanics the same. It would be easier from development point of view to just give everyone a permanent rest exp because that would be the same.

Example
I start the game, I do some quests, my rested exp is nearly over, I return some quests, I go to inn for 1 min, I have again full rested exp.

What's the point of that? The point of Inn was to make you move your character back to the town so that it is "resting" while you are offline and to reward you for that in some matter if you stayed offline longer.

I like the idea of Xudo, this is how tents should work in my opinion. I would add one more restriction to it. It should be available only to your Party (not raid) members so that we don't see a bunch of people stacking on one tent which is quite ridiculous. Unfortunately, I believe Xudo or my suggestion implementation would be time-consuming and very difficult to put restrictions on. Maybe instead it would be easier to implement a mechanism that would prevent making tents if Inn is XXX yards from you so that you can't abuse it that much? Not sure.

Anyway, the survival profession is great and I believe it's good that people can be rewarded for making tents in a wild. Maybe the rest exp gain should be put again back to default rates instead of being instant.
