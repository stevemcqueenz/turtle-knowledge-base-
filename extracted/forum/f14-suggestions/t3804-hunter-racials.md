# Hunter Racials

## Post 22958 by Notawen (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-20T20:08:00Z

Changes to make them balanced are here: [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3847](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3847)

Ork:

Emotional Bond: [Passive] "If your pet falls in battle you become Enraged. This increases your movement speed by 40%, melee and ranged attack power by 20% and reduces the Cooldown of your Mongoose Bite to zero. This effect lasts 30 seconds."

Tauren:

Spirit Shot: [300 Yard Range] [9 Minute Cooldown] [30% Mana Cost] [3 Second Cast Time] "Shots a spiritual projectile at your target, causing 300% weapon damage. It ignores armor and it can't be blocked, dodged or prevented by any other means. If the target has less spirit than you it will always be a critical hit."

Troll:

Headhunting: [Passive] "Increases your critical strike chance against humanoids by 3%. After Inflicting a critical strike to a humanoid you become Bloodcrazed, increasing your melee and ranged attack speed by 30% for 10 seconds. This effect can stack up to 3 times."

Undead:

Putrid Shot: [10% Mana and 3% Health] [30 Seconds Cooldown] "You dip your next projectile in your own rotting flesh, giving your target Neuro Necrosis for 5 minutes. Only affects humanoids and beasts. Requires a bow or a crossbow. Neuro Necrosis [Disease]: The afflicted target has a 10% chance for their spells to be interrupted on their own while casting. The afflicted target has a 1% chance to be stunned for 5 seconds at each second."

Goblin:

Trap Carrier GO75 [3 Minute Cooldown] [30 Yard Range] "After enabling this masterwork of goblin engineering you only need to choose one of your traps and then shoot it. Fear not! It doesn't even trigger the cooldown of the trap itself. It is guaranteed to work... 75 percent of the time." (it has 25% chance to trigger your choosen trap on you)

Night Elf:

Ambush Hunter: [Passive] "Decreases the cooldown of your Shadowmeld by 5 seconds. While Shadowmeld is active your first attack deals 300% damage."

Dwarf:

Aspect of the Bear: "Increases your armor, stamina and strenght by 30% and your threat generation by 200%. You can wield shields while this effect is active."

Gnome:

Knee Shot: [6 Minute Cooldown] "Blasts one of your opponents kneecaps, reducing their movement speed by 75% if they are humanoid, or 40% if they are a beast, an undead or a demon for 10 seconds. Requires a gun."

High Elf:

Charming Shot: [9 Minute Cooldown] "You imbue your next shot with magic, bewitching your target to fight on your side for 2 minutes. After this effect ends your victim will be very angry with you. Only works on beasts and humanoids." (duration is 30 seconds if it's a player)
