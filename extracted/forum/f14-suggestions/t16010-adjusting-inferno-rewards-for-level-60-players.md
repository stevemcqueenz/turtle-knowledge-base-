# [✅] Adjusting Inferno Rewards for Level 60 Players.

## Post 109060 by Unchallenged (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-20T21:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109060#p109060 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone,

I wanted to discuss potential improvements for Inferno rewards at level 60.

Currently, completing Inferno rewards players with [**Pendant of Mortality**], a trinket offering +44 Stamina and -1 Spirit.

While the stats are solid, taking up a trinket slot can be a significant disadvantage for tanks, healers, and DPS who rely on those slots for critical abilities and gear optimization.

Proposed Change to Inferno Rewards:
Instead of a trinket, provide a shirt as the reward. Shirts don’t occupy critical gear slots and serve as a meaningful, permanent mark of achievement for Inferno players.

Here are some shirt ideas that tie into the Inferno and mortality themes:

Shroud of Mortality – A shadowy shirt symbolizing mastery over life and death.
Infernal Ascension Wrap – A fiery design reflecting the player’s resilience through Inferno.
Undying Vestments – A simple, elegant shirt signifying the player’s unyielding spirit.
Eternal Warden’s Tunic – A durable shirt representing the unwavering resolve of Inferno champions.
Soulbinder’s Garb – A mystical shirt marking the player as one who has bound their soul to the mortal plane.

Additional Context:
Level 60 Inferno players currently don’t gain access to the bonus rewards offered to those who opt out of Hardcore by becoming Immortal. These bonus rewards include:

The title “**Immortal**”
 **A unique tabard**
The mount [**Immortal Champion’s Charger**]

Inferno players miss out on these rewards despite taking the more challenging route of staying true to Hardcore.

Adjusting the reward structure to provide a unique, meaningful reward that doesn’t impact gameplay (like a **shirt**) could help balance this discrepancy while still maintaining the prestige of the Immortal route.
