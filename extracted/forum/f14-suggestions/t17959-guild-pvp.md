# Guild PVP

## Post 122311 by Puxido — 2025-03-12T02:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122311#p122311 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ok, clickbaity title so lets get to the point.

Since there are cross faction guilds and some people would probably enjoy doing pvp with their guildmates, it make be an interesting option to pvp as a guild.  And I'm not recommending some complex new system reinventing the guild system.

When you click to flag yourself as PVP, you have the option of instead of flagging to your faction, you flag to your guild.

If two guild masters are close to each other, they can right click each other as if doing a dual and ask to make the other guild "enemies".  It could also be done through the guild tab but either way the other GM has to accept.

I see this as mostly being something to be helpful with RP situations, though most use dice rolls to be honest.  But it'd be a cool way to set up or organize large scale pvp events between guilds, and it'd be a cool way for cross faction guilds to get together and pvp with their guildmates across faction lines.  If its more than two guilds getting in on this than it'd be a bit difficult to set up, unless you could flag your raid as pvp rather than guild but I don't think that'd work but here is another idea.

What if you could also flag yourself with "The black flag", a pvp option where your flagged against both factions.  Anybody from either faction can engage in pvp with you.  It'd also open up a cool idea for a new leveling challange.  Raise the black flag and declare yourself an enemy of all nations, and try to make it to level 60 with anybody from either faction being able to attack you.  Extra hard if your in Hardcore mode.

I like that Idea more to be honest, and it'd make 3 or more guild battles viable, just have to be careful not to hurt your teammates with area of effect attacks.
