# Pounder Suggestion

## Post 87992 by Creedst — 2024-02-17T09:10:00Z

Pounder mount is currently unmountable in cities. I personally spend most of my time in cities, and I can not stress enough that I have worked many days to get this item to drop and would like to showcase it there, too. I think we should not be all punished because one or two people tried to block NPCs in Stormwind. It's better to punish or prevent the ones who tried from doing it. I personally never do anything like that.

I understand why devs might call this "immersion breaking" when you try to mount a big pounder, but surely there are ways to circumvent this (blocking NPCs or other issues). Anyone can do /tar {NPC name} then F6 to interact with it in case someone blocked it, GMs can issue punishments to people who intentionally do this etc. You can also disable drinking the enlarging elixirs in cities.

Please consider finding an alternate fix for this, because this is a hard worked achievement that I would not like to have gimped like this because of a couple of ill intentioned people. Thanks.
