# More Craftable Pets for other professions

## Post 154473 by Catlike — 2025-10-05T15:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154473#p154473 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

A fun idea I had - if one can use engineering to craft purely cosmetical companions, then why not have other professions to have similiar features? Especially the alchemy or enchanting - it makes thematical sense for the alchemists to be able to craft something like homunculi (could look like little imps, or anything else, really). Or, for more nature inclined alchemists - craft treants.
As for the enchanting - the Warcraft universe is full of examples of magically created creatures - why not add the ability for players to craft little elementals, golems (although this feels something which might require "combining" both engineering or blacksmithing and enchanting - maybe have the first to craft the "body", which is required as reagent for creating pet as enchanter? food for thought, mostly) and so on.

Of course, recipes shouldnt be just sold everywhere - one can make them to be either quest rewards, or rare drops from thematically appropriate mobs - for example, npcs in Scholomance might have a low drop chance of homunculi recipe, while killing mage npcs might yield you an elemental recipe.

Maybe one could even craft their own harvest golem (requiring the recipes from deadmines) - the tailors craft the golem body, and the enchanters enchant it, creating a learnable pet item.
