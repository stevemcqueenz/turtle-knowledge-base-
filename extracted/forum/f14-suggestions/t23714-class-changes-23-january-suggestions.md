# Class Changes 23 January Suggestions

## Post 166140 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-01-23T21:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166140#p166140 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Based on what Jamey shared on 23rd January about class changes in [viewtopic.php?t=15157&start=1435](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=15157&start=1435)

Here are some suggestions to consider when adding some of these changes.
---

> **Jamey wrote: Fri Jan 23, 2026 7:24 pm**
> **PALADIN**
>
>  **General**
> ---
>  Base damage of **Consecration** has been increased by 8% and now deals more damage initially, and less damage with every tick.
>
>  Developer Comment: Consecration has been underperforming in faster paced content due to its slow damage profile. We are putting more power on the initial ticks to help with intant threat of Protection and AoE damage capabilities of Retribution.
>  On the first tick, Consecration deals 156% of the 1.18 base value, followed by 140%, 124%, and so on.
>  Full damage progression per tick is as follows:
>  156% → 140% → 124% → 108% → 92% → 76% → 60% → 44%.

I think you try unneccesarily buffing it in too complicated way.
If the initial threat for Protection and damage in faster paced content is a problem, why don't you simply add additional damage tick when casting Consecration? So when Paladin casts Consecration, they immediately deal damage without waiting. That way you don't need to add this weird 156% → 140% → 124% → 108% → 92% → 76% → 60% → 44% scaling and the goal is achieved without deviating too much from Vanilla design.

---

> **Jamey wrote: Fri Jan 23, 2026 7:24 pm**
> ---
>  **PRIEST**
>
>  New Capstone: **Ascendance**
>  "Call upon your faith for divine aid. When activated, your healing spells cost 33% less mana, are cast 20% quicker and grant their targets the Apotheosis effect, increasing their healing taken by 15% for 10 seconds. Lasts for 30 seconds.
>
>  This spell can be cast while under crowd control effects and clears you of any of those when activated."
>  (280 Mana, 5 Minute Cooldown)
>
>  Developer Comment: Proclaim Champion and its connected spells have struggled to find its place, feeling more like a capstone you give to your target rather than elevating yourself and suffered from some conflicts in its functionality, increasing damage of the target, reducing damage taken and giving the Priest mana when the target takes damage which benefit damage dealers and the tank the most respectively which meant its full power could never be utilized on the same target in group content.
>  For this reason we've pivoting from a direct target buff to a power boosting capstone, meant to feel like a proper reward for mastering your role as a bringer of the Light.

From PvP perspective, I think you guys forget that Undead Priests have too many possibilities to break out from Fear.
That is: WotF racial + Fear Ward + PvP Trinket + and now this CC breaking spell.
Generally, I don't think that adding another CC breaking spell to Priest's toolkit is good idea especially if that is a spell that is insanely OP in its other function and Priest has already had this in their toolkit.

---

> **Jamey wrote: Fri Jan 23, 2026 7:24 pm**
> ---
>  **PRIEST**
>
>  **Shadow**
>
>  **Shadow Weaving:** Effect reworked: “Your Shadow damage spells have a 20/40/60/80/100% chance to grant you Shadow Weaving for 10 sec. Your Shadow damage spells apply a vulnerability increasing Shadow damage taken by 3% per stack of Shadow Weaving for 15 sec. Stacks up to 5 times.”
>
>  Developer Comment: Shadow Weaving struggles heavily when there are multiple targets the Priest has to focus on, especially in the cases those targets die faster than Shadow Weaving can be accumulated on them, preventing them from reaching their full potential both in terms of damage and supporting other Shadow damage dealers.
>  We've shifted Shadow Weaving to be instead an effect that accumulates on the Priest themselves that applies the amount of stacks you accumulate on yourself as the Shadow damage increase debuff on the targets, allowing easier application for multiple targets and carrying over these stacks between creature packs if they're close.

This is certainly too OP in PvP and need to be addressed. That's because the debuff applied stacks up to 5. So if one Shadow Priest gains 5 stacks, his shadow spells immediately apply 6 debuffs to the enemy effectively making it impossible to dispel in PvP. You could effectively apply 6 debuffs (1 dot + 5 stacks of debuff) each global cooldown which shouldn't be possible.

For example, it's impossible to dispel other magic effects on flag carrier in Warsong Gulch if one Shadow Priest reapplies 6 magic debuffs each GCD.
