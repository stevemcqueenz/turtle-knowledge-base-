# Add 'Stats' to Relics

## Post 56674 by Jammyxx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-10T12:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56674#p56674 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One thing I've always thought is that the classes who can't use a ranged weapon are at a stat disadvantage:

Paladin
Shaman
Druid

These classes can't equip a ranged weapon so do not get the stat advantage of those who can use Ranged weps / Wands.

My suggestion is to add a few stats to the Relics.
Just a few examples.

Libram of Fervor
+10 Str

Libram of Divinity
+10 Intellect.

Idol of Brutality
+10 Strength

Idol of Ferocity
+10 Agi

Totem of Rage
+10 Str

Totem of the Storm
+10 Intellect

These are just a few examples, but when you get to Naxx the weapons do offer a great advantage in stats and Paladins/Shamans/Druids, simply do not get these extra stats, and let's be honest most the Relics are lacklustre apart from a select few.
