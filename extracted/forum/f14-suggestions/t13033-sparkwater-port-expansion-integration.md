# Sparkwater Port - Expansion/Integration

## Post 87313 by Tyloris — 2024-02-08T19:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87313#p87313 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The problem: Sparkwater Port works as a minor settlement, but isn't really a "racial capital" in the same way as Thunder Bluff or Undercity are in the base game, or when compared to the new high elf capital. It isn't without its merits, but it doesn't feel all that well integrated with Durotar or Orgrimmar. Some quick examples:
 - There are no engineering trainers in the port
 - There are (were?) no anvils in the port
 - There aren't any clear paths to get from the port to the rest of the zone. You have to go either north or south through some raptors and scorpids, without having a mob-free path to follow
 - After finishing Blackstone Island, there aren't any breadcrumb quests that lead from Sparkwater Port to Razor Hill (the next logical place for a new goblin)

I'm not sure that the port warrants a full new zone and capital city the same way as Alah'thalas in the Thalassian Highlands. After all, the goblins of the Durotar Labor Union are only part of a racial group instead of an entire displaced population of a country. But compared to Booty Bay, it lacks a lot of personality and interesting design. So instead, I'd suggest moving Sparkwater Port north to the cliffs east of Orgrimmar, overlooking the ocean. Right now, it's entirely unused, being just a blank sheer cliff:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/s1GuA1E.png)

I'm imagining it as a sort of fantasy cliffside city, with multiple levels of buildings going down the cliff. The existing zeppelin towers would/could be moved here, turning it into the true "port" of the city, as well as a series of docks at the bottom of the cliffs. A series of cannons at the top of the city would provide defense from on high, and getting to the top or bottom would require either following a winding path up and down the cliffs or using a Thunder Bluff-style freight elevator. Here's a visual example of the what I'm talking about:

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/DFLLibBVYAAKhZI?format=jpg&name=medium)

The port would connect to Orgrimmar through a tunnel at the top, leading into the Valley of Honor. I'm imagining one of the right angle passageways you see between the VoH and the Drag, or between Durotar and the Valley of Strength. The entrance could come out between the Burning Anvil and Arms of Legend. This would directly connect it to the blacksmithing area and the path to the engineering trainers--a logical placement for importing/exporting metals, ores, and explosives, as the Durotar Labor Union specializes in such things.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/nkyt3E1.png)

Some other ideas:
 - A rocket or rocket sled as a method of transport, as a sort of equivalent to the Deeprun Tram. The rocket would launch you in a straight parabolic arc off of a ramp to land on another ramp elsewhere, the rocket sled would be like the one from Indiana Jones 4, where you would be moved at something like 10000% speed to another location, but then be hit with a 1 minute Dizzy debuff that makes you stagger when you walk (like being tipsy).
 - Some additional boat paths: A connection to the small dock next to Sen'jin Village, a series of connections to the other major goblin ports (Ratchet, Booty Bay, Steamwheedle Port)
 - A new goblin NPC with a number of quests: Mr. Torque, a reference to the Borderlands character [Mr. Torgue](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D13ebYdvNf0). The love of explosions, the creation of bigger, badder, guns, the overblown testosterone poisoning...it's a match made in heaven, as far as I'm concerned.
 - Moving the Horde side Goblin and Gnome Engineering trainers to Sparkwater Port. With goblins being a playable race, there should be no need for Horde to go all the way to Tanaris or Booty Bay when an Alliance engineer gnomish engineer can go to any engineering trainer. Perhaps even combine this with Mr. Torque, with a rivalry between the two.
 - EDIT: Apparently Boom Town is an unused name. It would make a nice contrast with Tinker Town in Ironforge.
