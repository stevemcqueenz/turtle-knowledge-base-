# fairness for custom abilities in BGs

## Post 35506 by Kedrek — 2023-02-20T21:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35506#p35506 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently Plainsrunning is disabled in BGs, but Holy Strike and Trueshot are not.

I understand the reasoning behind disabling plainsrunning, but why does this reasoning not apply to the other custom abilities?  Paladins and hunters didn't need buffs in pvp, and the addition of these custom abilities, while nice for pve, creates too much of a power swing in pvp.

What gives?  Makes no sense to disable one and let the others be.  It's all okay or it's all not.  Either enable plainsrunning in BGs or disable the other custom TWoW abilities.
