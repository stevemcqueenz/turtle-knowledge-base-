# Suggestion in making AB and WSG more active.

## Post 25419 by Sofaking — 2022-09-18T17:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25419#p25419 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Daily quest for either AB or WSG that rewards a bit of gold, extra rep with the faction and possibly a extra honor reward for winning once a day.
As it currently stands, AV is the only bg that is consistently played with near instant queue pops while if you want to play some of the other bgs you will sit in q for 30min-2h. The reasons are simple, AV is played the most due to most honor/h and the faction attached to it have the juiciest rep awards.
When WSG or AB pops, you will either be forced to exit due to too few players or have one team have 7 players while the other have 4. you get the idea.
Adding a daily completable quest with the rewards mentioned above would: a. Give a incentive to queue the bg for honor farmers b. Make the rep rewards for both AB and WSG be possible to acquire earlier, making them actually worthwhile for people pre-raid gearing.

Would like to hear some opinions on this.  smiling_turtle_head
