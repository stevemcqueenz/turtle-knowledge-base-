# Boars needed at ~40

## Post 128756 by Omnius — 2025-04-27T21:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128756#p128756 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The main issue is that the boaring challenge is only really viable with hunters due to the gap in boars between level 34 and 48. The max level you get from the Arathi boars is 43, making some classes impossible without getting boosted by a higher level or a hunter friend.

In the DB there are Elder Stonehide Boars at level 38, but it appears as if they have not been added to the game yet? Adding something like this could solve the problem.

In addition, hordes boar spawns are significantly worse in Hilsbrad due to Dun Garok being ontop of the best boars for the late 20s / early 30s level range.

Please fix the challenge. Otherwise, loving the leveling slog.

## Post 131965 by Omnius — 2025-05-19T15:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131965#p131965 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just an update. I have now hit 44, overcoming the hardest level as a paladin with 5 glyphs enabled: HC, white gear, crafted gear, slow and steady, boaring.

I felt it necessary to update on the viability element:
- Even with lvl 4 crafted items, the challenge is viable on locks, druids, paladins and probably shamans solo.

- The boaring challenge is even viable on warriors (not with other glyphs) solo.

- There are plenty other players doing the challenge both hc and non-hc, grouping has not been nearly as bad of a problem as I thought it would be to speed things up.
The challenge is rough on warriors and rogues, but definitely doable if working together (there are a couple different boaring guilds I have seen and plenty people at various stages).

After going through the process, experimenting with builds, profs, etc. I feel like **not only is the great boar gap my original post was complaining about viable, but arguably the most exciting stage in the challenge**.

The theory-crafting, the friendships, the sadness of witnessing a couple deaths due to lack of patience.... It will leave me with many great memories. **I regret making the suggestion**.
