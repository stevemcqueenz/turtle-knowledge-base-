## Post 85762 by Deeno (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-24T10:03:00Z

Im only playing on Nordanaar so i cannot give you a full picture, but here is my opinion:

Nordanaar has thousands of players while Telabim is less than a thousand. So Nordanaar will feel vibrant of life, all sort of players doing all sorts of stuff. This also can be a disadvantage, since from lvl1-30 there are a lot of hc players, and you cannot team with them (think about them as a separate faction) which in some cases can be frustrating when it comes to questing. On the other hand, on Nordanaar you can quest and do dungeons with players from the opposite faction. Also, Nordanaar has some economical issues due to the high number of resource competitive players, so professions and gold may feel a little inaccesible below 60.
On Telabim, there are no challanges but pvp. Smaller population can be more intimate?

When it comes to raiding, Nordanaar is finished, so you see ppl doing raids from zg to naxx, while Telabim is fresh and raids are opened every 3 month, currently BWL  tier raids/dungeons are opened, or will be opened in a few days. Take note that on Nordanaar, in the next few month a custom raid is coming that is higher tier than naxx.

Its your call what you prefer.
