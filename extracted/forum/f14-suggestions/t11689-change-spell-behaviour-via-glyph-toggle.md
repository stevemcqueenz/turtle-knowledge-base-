# Change spell behaviour via glyph toggle

## Post 77207 by Reploidrocsa (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-11-13T17:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77207#p77207 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I saw many post asking "buff class X, buff Y spell".

Today a thought crossed my mind. What about glyphs to make your spells different? Not better, not worse. Different.

I found out that glyphs can be toggleable thanks to blood ring, which turn off any active cosmetic glyphs you got.
We got the glyphs tab, we got the toggle mechanic. Let's add different behaviours. Some ideas:

Pyroblast: now does arcane damage instead of fire damage.

Inferno: spell does increased damage but no longer summons an infernal

Chain lightning: increases damage done to the target, but no longer jumps to other targets

Righteous fury: doesn't increase your threat done with holy spells and instead it reduces it

Feral charge: you can use feral charge in cat form but can't use in bear form anymore.

Sunder armor: now does weapon damage but no longer applies sunder armor debuff

Shadow form: allows you to cast healing spells, but it places an undispeleable damage dot increased by your healing power stacking up to 99

Trueshot: increases the damage of your stady shot as long as you don't have a pet active

thoughts?

## Post 77252 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-14T04:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77252#p77252 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Vanilla have no mechanic to replace one spell with other. Talents allow only to add spells.
It will complicate talent building, but might work.

Regarding example of Sunder Armor - it is a bit overpowered. Warriors already have instant attack with weapon damage - Mortal Strike and it costs 30 rage. Other attack with weapon damage is Slam with 1.5 cast time. Sunder armor untalented costs 15 rage and 12 rage with talent.
I understand that tradeoff is "ability to apply armor debuff for additional damage skill", but some might invent DPS build around glyphed sunder armor alone.
