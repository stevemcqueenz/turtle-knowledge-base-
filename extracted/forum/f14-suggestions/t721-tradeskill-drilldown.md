I don't know if it is possible in this client, much less game in general, buuuuut.....

GW2 has a wonderful feature in crafting called drilldown. I love it. Well Punchbuggy, wtf is it?

Ok, so you are a blacksmith and you want to make a breastplate. You need 4 iron bars and a thing-a-dad. You have one iron bar on hand, in inventory. You click the picture of the iron bar, and providing you have the skill, it takes you to the appropriate skill to make iron bars. Click click click, you have your 4 iron bars. Now you go back to the shield pattern and click on the thing-a-dad and it takes you to the pattern for that, provided you don't have one in your inventory already.

I know this is just a laziness QOL improvement, but I am an avid crafter and having to switch back n forth constantly added to my short attention span means i forget wtf I was doing. Just like what the hell I was talking about in this post.... Ooo sparkly votey thing... must click....
