Hello everyone

I took my time to take a closer look in all the class changes after CC2, and while I do agree with most changes, there are some that looks like it was made in a vacum, not taking into consideration that others specs will have left out points that could be used in theses specs, which are, Arcane for mages, and Disc for priest, the effect of this is that it reduces or even eliminates options for more of a hybrid specs, and if that's is the devs intent then forget about all of this.

I believe that, these two specs are the most aggravating ones because, originally, Arcane and Disc where more of a "support" spec for the other two, and after turtle changes, they got to be the main protagonist, which I think is a good thing and enables diversity, but we can't ignore the fact their purposed was to also "utilify" other specs.

Now with that in mind, I propose some minor changes to these two talent tree, while also make a needed change for Shadow Priest spec, which in my view is the least touched spec on turtle wow.
