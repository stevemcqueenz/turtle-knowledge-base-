# Is it possible, What is the procedure?

## Post 127174 by FracturedBrain — 2025-04-17T18:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127174#p127174 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am wondering the following things:

 1. Is it possible to have some new items added to the game?
 2. What is the procedure for doing so?
 3. Is there anything I can do to reduce the workload of whoever would be responsible for undertaking this task?

I am old, disabled and retired. As such I play a LOT of TWoW. Currently I have not even logged on to my Steam in over a year, TWoW is the only game that I play and I play between 6 and 18 hours per day depending on how my body is holding up.

Because of this I have made multiple 60s and done a fair few of the challenges, I have even succeeded and made it to Immortal yay me. Like a lot of people the more I accomplish the greater challenge I seek to still feel a sense of accomplishment.

I have been doing 6 challenge characters lately (Hardcore, Vagrant's Endeavor, Slow and Steady, Exhaustion, Artisan's Quest and Boaring Adventure). Doing this I have come across a few issues that i believe are not just part of the challenges being difficult. If a developer would like to correct me then please do.

I say the above because for example if you play a hunter you start with a bow, though you cannot upgrade it ever you can still use all of your shots that require a ranged weapon to trigger. But if you play a Paladin you start with a 2handed mace and can never make a shield so can never use Holy Shield or Bulwark of the Righteous which are a large part (in my opinion) of what make the protection tree so attractive to level with as a Hardcore player.

This is a single example but there are quite a few across numerous classes, I will not list them all unless a developer requests it as I feel a single example is enough to illustrate my point that whatever gear Blizzard originally coded starter characters with is insufficient to allow character to fully utilize their class abilities and with the combinations of certain challenges you are unable to obtain the required gear.

Now I am not asking to reinvent the wheel or suddenly negate the difficulty of these challenges as I still want them to mean something, the TWoW team has done a wonderful job adding items already and for example there are already white wands in game, I would just like the weakest one of them craft able. Either added to enchanter or Survival though I think enchanting makes more sense as if we want everything we should have to work for it and switch professions.

I have another request though it is purely my OCD and I completely understand if the answer to this one is no, I would like craft able white items with no or maybe just a plus 1 spirit stat for the neck, finger and trinket slot. I would also like a craft able white tabard and libram/totem/idol so that whatever class you play you are not forced to be constantly faced with an empty slot until 60. Again I am not asking for power, just a non empty slot please and thank you, maybe with some comical flavor text like "well your slot is not empty but it may as well be" or some such.

The list of items I would like to see included is as follows:

white shield
white wand
white bow
white gun
white crossbow
white thrown
white libram
white idol
white totem
white trinket
white ring
white neck
white head
white axe

white 2h sword
white mace
white 2h mace
white fist mh
white fist oh
white dagger
white cloth set
white leather set
white mail set
white plate set
white cloak
white tabard

While I am aware that some of these exist in a variety of professions, not all of them do and I would like the holes filled. If the TWoW team is willing I am happy to do the grunt work of finding what is missing etc, making the xcel documents or whatever I can to help. I have a lot of free time and would be happy to use it to contribute to the betterment of my favorite server (admittedly the only server I play).

Thank you for taking the time to read and consider my proposal and I look forward to hearing from you.
