# Heartstone upgrade

## Post 161573 by Lordstardust — 2025-12-04T18:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161573#p161573 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello everyone, I would like to suggest a small improvement that I think could be useful for those who need it.
I would like the cooldown of the Hearthstone to be reduced.
I thought of a quest chain, or a dungeon with an NPC that has a 20% chance of granting a new stone upgraded "heartstone+" that replaces the previous one: a "heartstone+ (40min cd)". I don't know how difficult this would be in terms of coding, but I think it would be nice to include it. Obviously, if you have the "Hearthstone+" you won't be able to use the basic one. In the case I'm proposing, it would involve creating a new item, but you could also simply upgrade your existing Hearthstone.
What do you think?

I love it, it's everything I've always dreamed of for this game... improving Vanilla without any expansions. I only discovered you a week ago, and I'm thrilled. I will also make periodic donations. I love you!
