# More Warlocks?

## Post 19932 by Notawen (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-07-12T11:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19932#p19932 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think all races should be able to be Warlocks.

From a lore perspective Fel magic is really easy to learn, one only needs to be willing to corrupt themselves.
In most cases it would be straight forward, you just choose Warlock in the character creation menu, not for the Nightelves tho.Their culture is against even the use of Arcane not to mention Fel.... but  the choices of the individual can go against one's culture.

Zenn Foulhoof could offer you a quest called The Path of Darkness. He would instruct you to kill Grells until you find a Glowing Heart. When you bring it to him he will start a ritual and tell you to eat it. As you right click it, there would be a popup that asks "Consume the Heart?" If you choose "No" the quest will fail, end of story. If yes, then another popup: "Accept the Darkness?" If you choose "No" the quest also fails and you get a ten minute curse on you that lowers all your stats by a bit. If you choose "Yes" tho... oh boy.

Your class is now permanently changed to Warlock and later you will be deported to Stormwind. Your reputation with Nightelves will also be reduced to "Neutral". Night elf npcs will be mean to you until you get "Friendly" with the faction, and Nightelf priest trainers (and Tyrande too) will be mean to you regardless.

Your actions have consequences and that's beautiful. <3
