# Rested Experience Should Be Nerfed

## Post 122701 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-16T14:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122701#p122701 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**PROBLEM**

A few days ago I read [one reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/turtlewow/comments/1jbc7ia/do_tents_make_mob_grinding_more_time_efficient/) in r/turtlewow that enlightened me and helped me to realise what is wrong with rested experience on Turtle WoW.

Frankly, the observation of the OP was that they could level up much faster from lv 14 to 18 in Barrens just from killing every mob on sight with accumulated rested experience from tents rather than focusing on quests and exploring the zone.

I've leveled a few characters (only two have hit 60 so far) and I've also observed this disproportion between experience gained by quests and mobs. It's worth adding that the Warmode challenge makes this disproportion even bigger since it gives 20% bonus experience from all sources. My friend has many more 60s than me and also shares similar view: killing random mobs around your level range is the fastest way of leveling. Quests aren't worth it if your focus is to level up as fast as possible.

You might not see the problem and think: "Just don't use tents. Get glyph of exhaustion or start slow & steady challenge". I agree that Slow & Steady challenge make the proportion of experience gain between questing and mobs much in favor of questing and I dare to say this is one of the reasons this challenge is satisfying.

The problem is that in order to level up in the most efficient way (Warmode + tents), players are forced to ignore the quests and kill random mobs within player's level range.

So now, to play in the most efficient way on RP-PVE server with a lot of custom quests and lore behind them, you must skip them and kill mobs within your level range...
I believe this approach is contrary to Turtle WoW foundations where exploration and questing should be the most important activity throughout your journey to level 60.

Although rested experience also existed in OG Vanilla, it wasn't as much impactful as on Turtle WoW because there were no tents nor Warmode and rested experience wasn't that easy to get - you had to spend a few days in the inn. While on this server, you spend 15 minutes under the tent and you become fully rested.

As a side note... rested experience made sense in original WoW. At the beginnings of WoW there weren't as many quests as in the latest version of Vanilla, thus killing monsters was the primary way of levelling, questing was just an addition. This is not the case on Turtle WoW, the server that enhances story of Vanilla by introducing new quests.

 **SOLUTION**

There are a few solutions.

- Completely remove rested experience.
I'm not fan of it. It solves the problem but removes the point of tents and logging off in the inns/cities. It would make more harm than good.

- Remove/nerf rested experience gained from tents.
I don't like this idea either. Tents became iconic part of Turtle WoW and whole process of meeting other players under the tent is nice social experience.

- Decrease the limit of rested experience.
Let's say the limit should be decreased from 150% of whole level experience to 50%. It doesn't remove the problem but nerfs tents slightly. Players would be discouraged from visiting tents every 30 minutes. It's not ideal solution.

- Decrease the bonus experience gained from killing monsters.
Currently you gain 100% extra experience from killing mobs if you are rested. It could be decreased to 50%. In my opinion this change should be merged with the above solution to the maximum limit of rested experience. I find this change good.

But the ideal solution is...

- Change the way rested experience works: instead of bonus experience from killing monsters to bonus experience from completing quests.
In my opinion this is the best solution because it makes questing relevant again. This solution also has one hidden advantage: quest runs in dungeons become much more relevant than ever before. I haven't said that yet but you should know that dungeons aren't worth doing either if the fastest way to lv 60 is your goal. With this change, players with rested experience would really love to see each dungeon at least once to complete their quests and get juicy experience.

EDIT:
Now I think that it's a good compromise to allow rested experience to work with both monsters and quests instead of monsters only. However, that would actually be a buff to rested experience so slight nerf would be needed along, too.

Currently:
 100% bonus for killing monsters while having rested experience.

Suggestion:
 50% bonus for both killing monsters and completing quests while having rested experience.

In theory this wouldn't change overall experience gained - instead it would decrease the gap between these sources of gaining experience.
