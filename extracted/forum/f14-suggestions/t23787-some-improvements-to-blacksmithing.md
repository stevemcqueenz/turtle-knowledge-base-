# Some Improvements to Blacksmithing

## Post 166805 by Darter — 2026-01-28T23:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166805#p166805 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

More Cool Armor/Weapons for Specialized Blacksmiths
I just leveled blacksmithing for the first time. I have always choosen professions that offer more benefits that never end like Enchanting, Alchemy, Herb, & Mining, but for roleplaying and helping equip my friend who is new to WoW, I chose blacksmithing. After leveling blacksmithing to 225 I realized my expectations of the diminishing returns of being a blacksmith at endgame was true when recomparing end game crafted equipment to end game raid equipment T1 and lower. I think that it would be a much more satifying experience being a blacksmith if there where more cool armor/weapons for blacksmiths to craft to make the profession more appealing. The epic loot from lvl 60 dungeons like ZG, and MC can quickly replace a lot of end crafting items. So all the money and time spent aquiring the items to make armor/weapons would quickly feel like a waste. Perhaps making the some existing items better or cost less to craft would help as well such as the Arcanite Reaper/Champion.

I really feel that having at least 3 options for all weapon types, one handed and two handed would really help too as well as having Blacksmith only weapons. And maybe having an upgradable option for lvl 40-60 blue weapons/armor would be cool through a quested or rare drop recipie which included the item as a requirement to make the upgraded version. For example take the Phantom Blade (lvl44 1hs weapon), and have a recipie that drops from ZG called the Exquisite Phantom Blade which requires a Phantom Blade and more materials to make. The Exquisite Phantom Blade would have a better DPS comparable to ZG/MC/BWL 1hs dps weapons, maybe faster, and its effect upgraded. An example would be, "Chance on hit: Decrease the armor of the target by 100 for 20s. While affected, the target cannot stealth or turn invisible.", to "Chance on hit: Decrease the armor of the target by 400 for 25s. While affected, the target cannot stealth or turn invisible."

Dark Iron Destroyer
For an Epic lvl 60 specialized weapon, the effect is so infintismaly small it feels unjustifiable. The 25 armor ignored is far less than 1% more damage. It belongs on a lvl 19 item.  This seems like an oversight when developed as the Annihilator, which is a lvl 58 blue item that can be crafted, can sunder 100 armor up to 3 times. This axe  needs to be much more substancial and make people excited to craft it like the Nightfall. Maybe a flat 10%-25% armor ignored. Something that signifies 'destroying' the target.

I really want to love Blacksmithing. I want people to have a tough decision to make when choosing other professions over it. I want it to feel more special, and to admire their craftsmanship more, to see more equipment worn by high level players, to immediately identify who is a blacksmith by blacksmith only weapons. I am available to help with improvements to blacksmithing. Feel free to reach out to me in game through whispers, or send me a letter in the mailbox and we can exchange emails through there to connect.

Darter

Hello Darter,

TWow have done similar with at least one weapon I can think of, craftable by only Blacksmiths (Swordsmiths to be precise): Untempered Runeblade (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60293>)
which leads to a quest to make the Tempered Runeblade (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81060>).
I also think there's a similar crafting/quest reward for engineers to make a gun!

It's a start for sure. Unfortunately, there are not many items you can craft that will not become obsolete after getting gear from even the lowest of raids. Which is why they likely started implementing augmentation crafting, like the Belt Buckles that only BS can craft and affix to other players.

That said, I would like to see more craftable shields added.

Furthermore, on the subject of augmentation, I have an idea regarding Shield Spikes:
Currently, we can only make shield spikes which deliver Physical damage (eg: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=12645>).

How about this for a suggestion: Elemental Shield Spikes !!

They could be crafted using Enchanted Thorium Bars and another agent similar to the elemental damage, so for example:

Shield Spike of Scorching - Blocking attacks deals 20-30 fire damage to the attacker (Blacksmithing 225 - 4 x Enchanted thorium, 1 x Heart of Fire, 2 x Dense Grinding Stone)

Essence of Undeath for Shadow damage
Living Essence for Nature damage
Azerothian Diamond for Arcane damage
Essence of water/Icecap for Frost damage
Righteous Orb for Holy damage (more expensive as its unresistable)
