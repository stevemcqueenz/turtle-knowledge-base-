This topic has likely come up before alot.

However never hurts to bring it up again.
Hunters get access to thrown weapons and funnily enough Multishot works with thrown weapons.

However the other abilities dont.
So my suggestion is, give the other hunter abilities the thrown property like Multishot
Or, change thrown weapons to count as a ranged weapon. (this i know has been mentioned.)

It would be fun to play a troll axe thrower.
Or a knife throwing specialist.
or an elf spear thrower (throwing spears!)
Or hammer tossing dwarf.

I just see no reason for there not to be the ability to toss a poison coated dagger. Or an arcane dagger, or aiming your throw extremely well.
Even if thrown weapons cannot compared to bow/crossbow/gun.
