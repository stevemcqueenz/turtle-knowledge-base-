The drop rate for the crawler meat is 33% which doesn't seem bad but for a lvl 6 quest you are farming ~30 crawlers to complete it. For comparison, "A Solvent Spirit" in durotar is a very similar quest with 80% rates. I don't think it needs to be 80%, but collecting 8 @ 33% for a starter quest feels too low.

From the player's POV, I have a dozen other quests in my log that I'd like to get to, but I'm stuck grinding crabs. And if you've invested enough time into the quest, you also don't feel like you should abandon it.
