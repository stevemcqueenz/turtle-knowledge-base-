# Give Beast lore like abilities to other classes

## Post 18419 by Xerrash — 2022-04-28T10:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18419#p18419 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Beast lore is a cool ability that let's you see a beasts stats and abilities. It's useful for knowing if you wanna tame a beast or not, but it is also fun and useful to know what stats your enemies have.

So here's some proposed versions of Beast lore for other classes that could work on other races:

Arms Lore: Warrior ability that works like Beast lore but affects Humanoids instead. The logic is that Warriors train a lot with weapons and armor that they can discern the effectiveness of their opponent's armaments. It is also mechanically useful for knowing if it's appropriate to sunder armor or not.

Demon Lore: Warlock ability that affects Demons. Useful for Enslave Demon.

Lore of the Elements: Shaman ability that affects Elementals. Could also reveal their elemental immunities.

Dragon Lore: Mage ability that affects Dragonkin.

True sight: Priest ability that affects Undead.
