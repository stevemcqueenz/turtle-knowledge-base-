# Small things I would pay for (Donation Shop)

## Post 34624 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-12T09:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34624#p34624 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, I've had some ideas of cosmetics that I would be interested to see in the Turtle shop. Some I know I would pay for, and some I think others would. I didn't think any of them warranted their own thread, so I'm presenting them in list form.

1. **"Glyph of Giants" -** You know how some potions can make your character larger? Why not an option to be larger all the time? Sometimes, you just want to be tall.
2. **"Glyph of the Starry Void" -** Shadowform is pretty neat. It adds a unique visual effect to the character. I can't help but think that it would be a little cooler if you were even darker and filled with stars, though. Maybe make the glow more of a pale color as well?
3. **Runes -** (In fairness, this one probably *could* warrant its own topic.) There are a lot of cool magic effects that can appear on enchanted weapons, but why should I have to choose a specific enchant to get the look I want? It would be really nice if you could acquire a visual effect to apply to a weapon separate of enchants. Maybe throw in some visual effects that aren't tied to enchantments, or are from later versions of the game? Let people put dark fire, white light, dripping red energy, or what have you on whatever weapon they want, independent of enchants.
4. **Undead Alternatives -** There are certain skins for other races that I feel like would work well as replacements for the standard undead. In particular, the Necromancer, Stratholme, and Dark Ranger skins. Yes, it would replace the character model, but I feel like these sorts of skins could still be usable for playing a more "preserved" undead.
5. **Reagent Bag -** This is something that exists in retail, but I believe it's actually one of the better ideas they had. An extra large bag that can hold materials used by any crafting profession. Some professions are particularly material-heavy, and it's worse if you do multiple crafting professions, so I can see people gladly paying for the convenience, even at the expense of keeping anything else in that bag slot.
