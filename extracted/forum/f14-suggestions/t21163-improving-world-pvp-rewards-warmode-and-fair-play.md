# Improving World PvP: Rewards, Warmode, and Fair Play

## Post 146990 by dbtdsilva — 2025-08-19T11:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146990#p146990 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

Problems

1. Currently, there seems to be no motivation for players in engaging in PvP.
2. A lot of those that engage have PvP turned off when they engage, which also causes some frustration for those who have it turned on and get a surprise attack from a supposed not harmful target (pvp off).
3. There is no reward for having warmode on at level 60
Suggestions

Prevent non-flagged PvP players to engage in PvP without consequence:
- **Activation Penalty:** When a player has PvP off and wants to engage into PvP, there should be a warning or a disadvantage somehow. Like a debuff when the person changes to PvP, example 75% less damage done for 30 seconds.
- **Longer Cooldown:** Once PvP is enabled, can only be disabled after 15 minutes instead of 5 minutes, there is people exploiting it to kill and disable it immediately while a person runs from graveyard
To motivate people to engage into World PvP, there should be some kind of reward / activity:
- **PvP Hotspots:** Designated zones with objectives or resource nodes where players earn tokens for kills and captures. These areas could also force anyone who enters it to have PvP enabled at all times, without choice.
- **Token Rewards:** Tokens could be exchanged for gear, gold, cosmetics, or mounts—something desirable but balanced.
Motivate Players to Keep Warmode On at Level 60:
- **Bonus honor:** Reward extra honor, like double / triple honor doing World PVP while Warmode is active.
- **Daily/Weekly PvP Quests:**
 - Attack or defend enemy cities.
 - Eliminate X amount of players in active PvP hotspots.
 - Win / Participate in hotspot events for extra rewards.
- **Exclusive Rewards:**Cosmetics, titles or event mounts tied to long-term PvP participation.
Of course, these are just ideas of what can be done in order to increase PvP activity in the server, which I think is very low at the moment, especially due to PvP being enabled on-demand so easily and having no reward for doing so.
These changes would create fairer fights, more meaningful rewards, and ultimately bring back the excitement of world PvP and, most important, those who do not want to PvP are unaffected.

Hi all, to perhaps add some additional thoughts to **Warmode**.

If the intention of Warmode is to support opt-in World PVP perhaps at 60 the bonus goes from 5% XP gain to a series of increased yields which are meaningful.

For example:
- a% of rep gains

- b% yield on loot (additional silver, drops, skins, fish)

- c% yield on harvested nodes (herbs, minerals, schools)

- d% increase chance for BOEs to drop

- e% increase in BOE rarity

- others?
This doesn't have to be a huge bonus like the old 20% XP to make PVE people feel like they are missing out. But a small boost in some of these would work.

Here is the trade-off we would ask ourselves: "Do I have enough fun in world PVP that I am willing to activate warmode to risk X% of my time from deaths for Y% bonus in yield?"

My answer is yes!
