Here we go again

This thread aims to create a more in depth system where trade can be amplified and player be in more need more of each other.

Have you ever dropped the asshole from your butt to get your armorsmithing in 300 just join a guild and proudly announce that you are one just to receive the news that they already have other 5? And, that they already have better recipes than you, and even more, the guild leader is one of them and all recipes that he doesn’t need goes down a ladder?

Yes, that’s sucks, and happens with almost everyone, and I believe that I have the answer, well, at least the base to the answer, where more intelligent and experienced players/devs/gms can improve it.

How it works right now, in some of the crafts, not all, you select a base craft, and in a certain point you specialize in one broader ramification, like blacksmith, that can goes into armorsmith or weaponsmith, that has one extra step into specialization.

My proposal is, you level to 300 in a specific profession, after that you specialize into a broader niche, and each time you select a deeper sub specialization you make better things.

But how do I visualize? Well, for example blacksmith, where the base 300 blacksmith will only give access to craft basic green stuff for level 60 players. Then you must level another 100 skill points to be able to select a new specialization that make you able to craft even better stuff, and for each step, you must level another 100 skill points and be better at a specific something.

And the crafting materials, the key ones, are found in a tier level content, for example, to become a armorsmith the key crafting items are in 5 men, then, for the second higher tier, are in the 20 men and so on, until you reach at nax level stuff where you can craft one specific item of your choosing from a list, where you will almost the only one that can do it.

Is it possible or is it not possible to implement is out of question. We don't have enough technical expertise anyway. So lets stick to design discussion.

I don't want to discuss the fact that engineering looks like must-have profession. There is other threads for this discussion. You can bump them if you like.

Problem described here is that crafters tend to become VIP because they collect all rare raid recipes and reputations. Single armorsmith can supply whole guild with all kinds of armor. So the guild don't need another armorsmith. Also, any other armorsmith will get only those recipes that are missed by main armorsmith.

Wilsonsds proposed quests to narrow specialisation of crafters. So multiple crafters would have to choose which patterns they want to craft and which - they don't.

Crafted tiersets has main flaw - they can be traded. All traded items can be given to players who didn't participated in raid. It is possible to implement a quest-based system where you need some generic craftable BoE t2 generic plate helm and Essence of Onyxia and you get your new BoP t2 helm.
Quests can be used to implement slot-based restrictions. You should choose only one recipe out of multiple recipes per slot. May be in groups of Chest+Pants+Boots or Head+Gloves+Belt.

I don't like this design. It will unnecessary complicate the game.
