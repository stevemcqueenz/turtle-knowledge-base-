Orcs have connections to ogres, trolls, and undead which are all races that can be mage. It's a matter of learning, for the most part, and ogres have actually been in the horde or horde adjacent since WC3 and I think even before that. Orcs learning arcane magic isn't that much of a stretch. As mentioned above magic is similar to a science that simply takes knowledge. Orcs have plenty of reason and ability to pick that knowledge up as we've seen plenty of scholarly or at least intellectual orcs, with alchemists and engineers.

Dwarves are literally directly connected to the titans, which is where the root of arcane magic comes from, and thusly the origin of all magic that a mage uses. On top of this, they have ties to some of the most magically adept societies out there. Humans, high elves, and gnomes are all very known for being flexible or good at magic.

Gnomes being priests would not make sense because they don't have any religion, or a cultural structure that would easily conform to one.
