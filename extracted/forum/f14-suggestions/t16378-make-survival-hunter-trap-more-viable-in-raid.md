# Make survival hunter trap more viable in raid

## Post 111713 by Tokoerpaaisen — 2024-12-07T21:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111713#p111713 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi devs.
Melee hunter is great fun to play. However having raid bosses immune or high resist on fire dmg is not fun when big part of our dps won’t work.
And ppl really should give up the idea, if there is something not working in the game, changes/updates are always hated, because it is not vanilla taste. I mean if it’s pure vanilla, we will all just play classic fresh now.
My suggestion is give melee an epic quest ( long, dungeon involving and expensive) rewarding 3 trap modifiers which changes fire trap dmg to fire, frost or nature.
And even with debuffs like fire = armor melting, frost = slower attack speed, nature = extra spell dmg. But only one modifier can be activated at one time. If fd switching is not allowed, set the cast time for the modifier to 30 sec.
By doing so, melee can also provide good raid buff. And it won’t change the class too much
