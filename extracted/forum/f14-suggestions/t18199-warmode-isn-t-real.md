# Warmode isn't real.

## Post 124023 by Natuaduck (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-03-27T00:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124023#p124023 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Face it; Warmode is used exclusively for the exp boost, and actual pvp doesn't happen.

Having leveled several characters to 60 in warmode, the only times "pvp" is happening is from some high level who's bored and finds the only fun left in WoW is to one shot low levels.

 ***Constantly*** I pass by other people in Warmode around the same level and ***there is no fighting***. Cope all you want, bring up your own precious self-contained examples, but the 1-60 experience takes a long time and even in Stranglethorn Vale I got through it all several times over now with only high levels getting off on the high of griefing (Which the Turtle WoW team seems to not think is griefing anymore).

And if that the "challenge" of Warmode is actually "You are at risk of being one-shot by high levels" than that's some bizarre implication that it's the responsibility of the high level playerbase to be dictating the "challenge". Because again, I've been playing on here a while and from 1-60 there's just so little "real" pvp going on. Simple as.
