# Add level requirement to bandages

## Post 27402 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-10-17T09:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27402#p27402 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently [Expert First Aid - Under Wraps](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=16084)
requires only 150 first aid to learn.
In the same time, [Heavy Runecloth Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=14530) require only 225 level to use.
It means player can use top bandages at lvl 19.
For example PvP consumabes have strict level requirement.
[Highlander\'s Runecloth Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20243) require 45 lvl
[Highlander\'s Mageweave Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20237) require 35 lvl
[Highlander\'s Silk Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20244) require 25 lvl.

My proposal is to add level requirement to bandages. Make it similar to PvP consumables.
[Silk Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6450) should require 20 lvl.
[Heavy Silk Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6451) should require 25 lvl.
[Mageweave Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=8544) should require 30 lvl.
[Heavy Mageweave Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=8545) should require 35 lvl.
[Heavy Runecloth Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=14530) should require 45 lvl.
[Runecloth Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=14529) should require 50 lvl.

My reasons:
 1. This change fits a concept of using consumables and buffs of appropriate level. All potions require specific player level. Low level players can't drink high level potions. All grenades require specific engineering skill. Low level players can't use high level grenades.
 2. This change will reduce the gap between twinks and non-twinks. It will lead to more fair play in low level PvP.
