to justify  the name of this thread, lets have a quick run through the strengths and weaknesses of the assassination rogue in  classic wow:

 **Main Appeal and Strengths**
Assassination rogue main strengths and appeal came from its high burst from Ambush and Backstab crits as well as higher resource and combo point generation from talents like seal fate, relentless strikes etc along you to output more damage than the out specs.  with this power  their can focus targets like low hp players and/or players with defense trinkets on cooldown and  grouping up with players and burst high priority target together or shit gear players and destroy them.

pretty cool spec right! but  you know what most assassination rogues in  classic wow avoid doing? leading a charge into the frontline and  try to all-in  with cheap shot to Kidney Shot combo on full hp nax/pvpset plate gear players. since this isn't a smart use of their damage to justify the risk  of either there damage being  negated through  healing, or the the assassnation rogue getting dogpiled on. which is a large risk since they had to sacrificed a lot of they  Control and escape potential from The combat and subtlety talents they'll have very limited options but make up for it in  raw assassination power!

 **Weaknesses and Counters**

 2 main drawbacks/counters from Original wow.
 1. High armor geared players(FeralDruids, Paladin, Plate warrior).
 2. weaker escape tools (in comparison to other rogues spec)

other rogues specs will struggle with the durability of these classes but focus on controlling  the fight with deadzone-ing ,cc and resets to keep them safe.
Assassination rogues also has the similar struggles the high gear/high armor class match ups and that's even with improved expose armor being the in old assassination talent tree. all there burst and crits damage will be susceptible to huge physical damage reduction from bear form and t3 plate gear, shield gear etc. this means that in a small battle if you was going unload all you damage in a opener you'll get more success on a low armor classes like non feral druid, warlocks, priest,  because even if you didn't kill them they shouldn't have much hp to be a problem.

but with the other High armor classes, where the very same opener will most likely only leave the enemy with  50% or more if you didnt crit and/or if they switch to a shield to take less damage, which put the assassination rogue into a rough spot in comparison.unlike the other rogue specs (like subtle/combat) these high armor gear classes have better opportunity to  out sustain you (e.g paladin feral druids) or out-manfight with gear (warriors and paladins) either in the wpvp or bg opening on high armor is long and costly opening up the chance for interjecting players to counter your bursts.
