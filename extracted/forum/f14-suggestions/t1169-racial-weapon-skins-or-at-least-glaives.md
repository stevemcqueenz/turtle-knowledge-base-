# Racial weapon skins! (Or at least glaives...)

## Post 6845 by Pumpkinbot — 2020-07-13T02:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6845#p6845 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Simple suggestion: each race has their own "racial weapon" skins. They're alright, damage-wise, while levelling, but of course don't hold a candle to high level gear. However, with the transmog system we have in place, it could lead to some nice outfits here.

Let's use the night elves as an example. Their guards tend to use a lot of warglaives (dual-sided curvy blades) and moonglaives (multi-bladed disc...things) but none of these are useable by the player. Not saying we need the Warglaives of Azzinoth to be lootable, but there's a lot of much, uh, -simpler- glaive models. If another Alliance race gains exalted reputation with Darnassus, they, too could wield glaives, while night elves would have the ability to purchase them by default. I'd say these would be best classified as 1H swords.

Similarly, we have tauren and their giant-ass totems. Cairne holds one, of course. They had slightly more prominence in the RPG, but they still do exist in-game. Naturally, these would be 2H maces.

The only issue here is with the other races. What would orcs get? Or the undead? Hell, what about humans? We could keep one to each faction, to be fair, but there's likely a LOT more glaive weapons than totem weapons. Plus, more classes use 1H swords over 2H maces.

I'm open to other ideas, too! I'd love it if each race had their own set of weapons, but it's late and I"m useless for brainstorming right now, lol.
