# Game Development Recommendations

## Post 163875 by Zeu — 2025-12-27T17:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163875#p163875 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

From my actual gameplay experience, the increasingly challenging raids in the current game version have gradually left many players feeling fatigued and bored. For players who prefer solo exploration or only team up with a few close friends, the difficulty barrier of raids is excessively high, making it hard for them to enjoy a satisfying gaming experience.
Therefore, I suggest that the game could develop an epic and cohesive long quest chain with intriguing and immersive storylines, and connect multiple 5-player dungeons to drive the plot and quest progression — this gameplay design can draw inspiration from the acquisition method of the T0.5 set, while featuring significantly richer story content and enhanced gameplay fun. Meanwhile, raising the reputation level of the corresponding factions can be set as a prerequisite for unlocking subsequent quests with a reasonable progressive difficulty curve. The ultimate rewards for these quests can be set as second-tier endgame gear just below the current version's top-tier tier set, such as the current T3 grade individual pieces or 5-piece set gear.
This gameplay design would provide players who do not enjoy raids with clear character progression goals and worthwhile core rewards. It would also further enrich the diversity of the game’s gameplay and cater to the needs of player groups with different playstyles and preferences.  satisfied_turtle_head
