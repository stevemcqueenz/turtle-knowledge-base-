# Increase Droprate of Scholomance Mini-Bosses

## Post 31906 by Dkmoonlilith — 2023-01-01T15:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31906#p31906 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Howdy gamers,

Currently, the droprate of Group 1 droptable items off of Scholomance mini-bosses (Bloodmail, Deathbone, etc.) is set to 30% for each mini-boss per the Turtle Wow Database (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=10502>). I would propose that this rate be increased to somewhere between 50-60% as a QoL improvement, given that some of these items are better than what is present in raids until Naxxramas.

As a paladin, I have run Scholomance almost every day for the past 3 months, totaling around 60+ runs, and still have yet to see one Libram of Divinity drop. I have heard horror stories of individuals taking even more runs before finding one. There is also no alternative to this item for pally healers until Naxxramas progression, creating situations where individuals (myself included) will be equipped with full AQ40 BiS gear without having acquired a pre-raid BiS item such as this. Adding insult to injury, these items cannot be easily farmed, requiring a full 45-minute run to even reach said mini-bosses.

I believe that increasing the droprate of Group 1 as a whole would retain the rarity of finding a specific item (such as libram, totem, deathbone pieces), given how large the pool of Group 1 items is, while still providing a QoL improvement that decreases the likelihood of an individual running Scholomance 100+ times trying to get a specific item from the Group 1 droptable.

You can call it paladin cope if you like, but this minor loot change would be in line with changes made in Turtlewow, in Classic, and in SoM to improve QoL.

Thank you for taking the time to read all of this, and I appreciate any and all feedback on this suggested change.

Best,
Kareni
