# Two quality of life changes

## Post 93187 by Wafflecrusher (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-04-21T04:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93187#p93187 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The first is the questline to get the key for UBRS.

More specifically, the way by which you acquire the gems. I have no issue having to do LBRS a bunch of times to get the gems, but having to do LBRS a bunch of times and then having to roll against party members when the gems do drop is demoralizing. I know many folks strategize with a group, and have a designated key guy, but that's frankly awful to deal with.

A simple solution to the UBRS gem quest, would be to make it to where when the gems drop, every player in the party can loot the gem. You'd still need to do LBRS quite a bit, but it would make the key far more obtainable. I'm sure this has been brought up before, but I think it would be easy to add into the upcoming patch.

The second would be to allow players to shift click and see player information more rapidly.

I know there is like a 60 second or so cooldown on the /who function, and removing said cooldown would be huge for forming groups. My understanding is the cooldown was implemented in order to avoid server overload during the huge peak in population. Now that the population has subsided somewhat, I am optimistic that removing the cooldown wouldn't be too taxing on the server, while providing many benefits.

I know that many use the LFT tool to form groups, however when using the LFT tool you can see the name, but not the classes. This is problematic for a number of reasons, namely group composition for specific dungeons, loot distribution, or even for various role playing elements.

I think these are two reasonable quality of life changes that would not have a dramatic impact on gameplay, and would enhance the play experience on turtle wow.
