# Actual Loincloths

## Post 99233 by Zeran (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-07-12T05:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99233#p99233 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you want to go for a tribal/barbaric look, currently in the game, there are various leg slot items with the name "loincloth". The issue is that they look more like undergarments than loincloths...

Proposal: the bottom part of the guild tabard model fits the loincloth look. And as it turns out, there already exists an item with just that part - "Unused Tabard of Chow" [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=35 ... rd-of-chow](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=3557/unused-tabard-of-chow)
It goes in the tabard slot.

I'm not 100% sure if it's an item present in Vanilla (I couldn't see it in the PfQuest item DB or the TWoW DB) or if it was added later and needs to be ported over, but another vanilla 1.12 private server that I've played on has it as an option to buy in the game store and it works.

Here are some pics without any gear and with random gear:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Isd7csq.png)

The way it works is, the Tabard automatically uses the texture of whatever you have on your upper body. So without wearing anything on top, it just has the skin colour and maybe doesn't look that great, but with various gear it takes on their textures and matches the look (some better than others).

It could also be recoloured by the TWoW team to come in specific set colours rather than using the other items' textures, I'm sure.

This item could either be added ingame directly or be purchaseable from the donation store - it looks cool and I'm sure there will be an interest for it (I'd personally love to see it added).
