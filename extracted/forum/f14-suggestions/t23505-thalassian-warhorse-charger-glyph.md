# Thalassian Warhorse/Charger Glyph.

## Post 164485 by Jcarrill0 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-05T21:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164485#p164485 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I’ve noticed that there’s still a presence of RP players who enjoy playing Sin'Dori, and I wanted to propose a suggestion for two Glyphs (NOT mounts).

The idea would be to introduce a Glyph that allows the High Elven Paladin Warhorse to be changed into a Thalassian Warhorse, and another Glyph for the High Elven Paladin Charger, which would change it into a Thalassian Charger.

This way, players wouldn’t be able to just buy the Thalassian Charger without completing the quest, but they could still purchase the Glyphs to make the mounts match their race and roleplay theme.
