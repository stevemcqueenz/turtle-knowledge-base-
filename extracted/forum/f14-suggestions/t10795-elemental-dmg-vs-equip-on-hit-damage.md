## Post 70395 by Nallkun — 2023-10-12T17:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70395#p70395 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Id like to submit the following for consideration for fix/revamp for standardization.

Currently Paladin Tank Weapon choice is extremely small due to the need to Proc their Seal/Judgement of Wisdom to maintain any hope of mana to finish a single fight OR maintain threat. For anyone that plays Paladin Tank, you know our Threat/mana situation is extremely "nitch". We need certain KEY items.. Certain KEY weapons with small variance in order to maintain Mana/agro. While this is an entire Issue by itself id LOVEEEEE the GMs to address. I'm going after the "easy fix" / "Low hanging fruit"

Examples of Items that "Work":
 - Crimson Spellblade
 - Fiery Plate Gauntlets
 - Fiery Retributer
 - Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros

Examples of Items that DO NOT work:
 - Thunderfury, Blessed blade of the Windseeker
 - Dream's Herald

Weapons like Thunderfury, Dream's Herald etc have an Elemental component, but are not procing Wisdom/Light due to it not being a separate Line item like "Crimson Spellblade"

Note: Equip Damage vs #-# Elemental damage on a weapon.
This change would be a strong help in the right direction of allowing Protection Paladins to maintain Threat/Agro and weapon choice.
As we are currently "Pidgeon hole'd" into 3-5 weapons in total.

Example: Dream's Hearld / Thunderfury etc are not allowing us to gain mana/threat on melee swing from Judgement procs.
As Protection Paladin Threat is VERY reliant on a number of these mechanisms to be viable.
My 2 cents - thank you for consideration
