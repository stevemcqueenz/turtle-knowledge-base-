# Make Dreams Herald raid usable.

## Post 151685 by Lendvell — 2025-09-14T11:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151685#p151685 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We all know that some time ago a patch came out that says that TF procs do not work together with the Dreams Herald proc. I understand that the developers killed the TF + Dreams Herald combination, but this also affected players who use just Dreams Herald. Now if you are the bearer of this(Dreams Herald) sword in raids, you just have a mediocre sword in your hand without effects and stats(coz TF slow procs overwrite it).
Why should the SLOW proc overwrite the POISON proc?
I suggest making both procs hang, but in terms of slowing down, the strongest one works. Or if this is technically impossible, split the proc into two effects - poison and slowing down. Save the poison!
After all, this is not fair to people who farmed Dreams Herald for themselves, in order to end up with a weakly sharpened iron strip instead of a poison sword with SP scaling.
