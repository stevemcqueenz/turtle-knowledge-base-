# Raid Loot Timer

## Post 38958 by Patientzer01 — 2023-03-24T23:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38958#p38958 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Rules Change:**
> For raiders, we have implemented a new system that allows you to rectify any mistakes on your own:
>
>  In raids where Master Looter mode is enabled, for a short period of 10 minutes after the boss has been defeated, all raid members will have a claim on the item and can receive it via trade, provided they were present during the boss kill, have not left the raid map, and share the same raid ID with the current owner of the item. Each item can only be traded once.
>
>  There will be a map-wide announcement to inform the raid leader about this event.
>  Please note that any requests for manual transfers will be refused from now on.

 **Suggestion:**
I wanted to propose that this 10 minute timer could be raised to 1-2 hours.  Being able to just pick up loot in classic and keep pulling/killing saved a lot of time in the long run. It was also nice to just do all loot at once at the end of a raid, or if it was coming close to the timer, near the end of the raid.  This would in my opinion make life easier for a master looter for your raids.  I think this would be a nice quality of life change to build on top of the current 10 minute change.
