# Choice of starting location

## Post 8709 by 17misery — 2020-12-15T15:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8709#p8709 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One thing I feel is missing from TurtleWoW is the ability to choose your starting location regardless of race.
Let's say you want to play with your friend who starts on another continent or you just want to level in different areas, but still play as your favorite race - the system I am proposing would help with that.
Just give each new character an one-time teleport to the choosen starting location of their Faction.
