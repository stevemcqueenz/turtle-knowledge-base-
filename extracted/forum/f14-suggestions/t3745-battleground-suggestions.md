# Battleground suggestions

## Post 22536 by Tenant — 2022-08-16T12:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22536#p22536 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is going to be long list of suggestions for battlegrounds.

I'll preface with some general info to help understand where the ideas are coming from. One specific thing I'd like to note is the target audience. There's a general "PvP players/PvE players" concept, which I think is a little misleading and brews unnecesary animosity. I would say that everyone can enjoy both to some extent. However, BGs are designated PvP areas, and their purpose is to be fun for **people who enjoy PvP**, and are going in for the win. Not levelers, not farmers, not top rankers. Going into a BG and not trying to do everything you can do to win is active griefing of the people who do try.

I'd also like to note that it's speficially about battlegrounds. Not about World PvP nor Warmode - these are separate issues, and should be discussed separately.

The ultimate goal of the changes, as I see it, should be more fun for participants. But, what does fun mean in bg contexts? I think its variety. Which means:
 1. More different BGs popping. Right now we have constant ongoing AV and an odd AB/WSG. Almost no BR and no SGV at all.
 2. A reasonably even win/lose distribution. Mostly AV is dominated by Alliance, and WSG/AB by Horde.

Before making any changes, one thing I'd recommend is collecting and publishing bg statistics for analysis. Count of bgs, levels, duration, bonus honor earned, who won, win reason (actual win or just timeout from losig head count), etc.
Even raw stats would be good, I'm sure someone would take the time to turn them into graphs, although of course seeing some graphs on the website would be even better - and provide valualble info on how changes actually affect the gameplay.

General
 1. Minimum number of players. Right now all BGs have the minimum head count reduced, which is probably a remnant of the lower population time. Now population has skyrocketed, and there's no need for that. Restore the original min head count. Actually, I'd go further and introduce some hysteresis: for example, start AV with a min of 26 players on each side, and start shutting it down if it falls below 20.

 2. Queue dodging. Blood Ring is specifically plagued by this. Introduce a minor honor and rep penalty for dodging. If you're really busy with something important, you can take a minor hit. If you're doing that consistently, it should add up.

 3. Even BG distribution. Ideally, people would participate in all BGs more or less evenly. Right now, it's non-stop AV because of "best honor per hour". Blizzard encouraged multiple BGs with "For Great Honor/Concerted Efforts" quests, providing bonus honor a rep for turning marks from all 3 BGs. We have 2 more BGs now. Multiple suggestions to address this:
 3.1. Reduce AV's "honor per hour". Cut bonus honor and rep from non-essential objectives. But increase bonus honor for killing the enemy general, to encourage people to go for the win.
 3.3. Increase the "For Great Honor" rep/honor.
 3.4. Introduce a new quest, let's say "For Greatest Honor", which would require marks from all 5 BGs.
The specific numbers can be discussed, but they should be calibrated such that
 - Doing all 3 bgs would be more profitable than just running AV, both rep and honor wise.
 - Doing all 5 bgs would be **the** most profitable way to increase rep and honor (bg wise)

 4. Make BG and SGV first-class citizens with their own weekends. Add reputation/rewards vendor for SGV.

 5. Rewards. Many people openly state that the only reason they queue are rep rewards. In particular, AV seems the go-to area for fresh 60s to get cheap epics.
Let me tell you, it's no fun to play with people, who don't even want to be in BG and just view it as a grind chore.
Honestly, I'd give out all those rewards for free instantly to anyone who just wants the items, in return for disabling pvp for them forever. However, that's probably not going to happen.
Let the people who prefer PvE get comparable items in PvE with the same time investment. Go over each epic reward, find similar items that drop from bosses, increase drop rates for those if necessary.

 6. Uneven win/lose rate. If we have statistics, and see that one faction is consitently winning much than another, it could be meaningful to offer **temporary** free faction transfers for active pvpers (say, rank 11 and up) from the winning faction into losing faction.

Alterac

Alterac is the one with most issues.
 - It easily turns into a turtle. (Although, a repeating 15-minute hedgehog day race is not much more fun).
 - Mine farmers and fishermen, who contribute nothing to the battle.
 - After 15 minutes, the "let them win, let's go next" whining starts.

 7. First off, I'm sure that everyone's eager to have its [long list](https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow-bug-tracker/issues/1478) of bugs fixed. That's the priority.

 8. Drop the fish. Fishermen can go elsewhere (increase the fishing chance somewhere in the world, if they complain too much). No fishing. Zilch. Nada.

 9. Remove or at least dramatically reduce drop rates for anything not related to the BG, especially so in the mines. BGs don't need farmers.

 10. Reduce XP for killing mobs (again, especially so in the mines) to 10% of the current value. BGs don't need levelers.
But, it doesn't mean that lower levels are not welcome. Even if they can't go head to head with 60s, let them still contribute to the battle - by granting XP from BG quests: capturing mines, farming materials for cavalry, ground assaults, etc.
To be clear - any of these XP rewards should be just a tiny bit lower that what you can get in questing in the open world with the same time investment. Discourage levelers, encourage PvP players.

 11. Tone down mobs. I'm not sure how's champion archers strength compared to vanilla, but for are 10-man party, 3 of which are fishing, they are definitely too strong.

 12. Horde is chronically understaffed exactly by the margin (3 people). Decrease the margin, make it 1, to equalize the sides.

 13. Someone had a nice idea to swap bases for factions every other bg. While this could be a lot of work, and I don't really hope for it, I think it deserved a mention.

WSG and AB
These are working fine, mechanics are simple and don't really warrant any change.
I heard some people saying that rep grind is too slow compared to AV, but I think it's offset by the fact that you get a head start with these, while AV is available from level 51 only.
Now that teleport bug is fixed, levelers can actually get in bgs while leveling.

Sunnyglade
[SGV is a mess.](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3717)
And not only that, the issues is that no one really knows anything about it.
It's and **RP**-PVE server. Maybe I'm missing something, but where is the lore? Why are we fighting there? Where is the NPC that explains that in game?
Mechanics are unclear too. It would be quite helpful to have an official sticky explaining how exactly it works, and how to actually win it.
And, as already mentioned - reputation, rewards vendor, weekend and inclusion into "The greatest honor" would go a long way for popularizing it.

Rewards, part II
This mostly concerns Blood Ring, but will apply to SGV if a rep vendor for that will be added.
Chances are, people who run many BGs, will rank high. They will have PvP sets, either blue one or epic one. Why are all the epic rewards take all the same slots? If they are better than PvP sets, than they will obsolete those. If not, the other way around.
Exalted rewards from new BGs should be for other slots - rings, weapons, maybe totems/librams, trinkets, etc.
However, I implore you that **no** BG rewards should be made uniquely great. They should be comparable to what you can get in PvE, and just a little bit harder to get. Encourage people who enjoy PvP, let people who prefer PvE get their equipment the way they like.

To developers
 - This is a long list, and I understand that you have to prioritize. Please do just that. It's not necessary to dive into deep BG overhaul right away. Pick low-hanging fruits first. Even some issues addressed would improve player experience considerably.
 - Statistics. That's an important one. At least in machine readable format. Even for yourselves statistics will be useful.

To players
 - I've listed many issues, and offered some ideas. I don't presume that all of ideas are the best way to deal with those issues, and I'm sure that many more good suggestions will come.
 - Report bugs on [github](https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow-bug-tracker/issues). If a bug is not on github, it **does not exist**. But make sure to search existing issues to see if it's already been reported. Don't make dupes.

Also, comment of the bugs. Add details, and let the developers know that these bugs affect you, else how they will prioritize?

Conclusion
I'd like to reiterate the most important points:
 - Changes made should be tailored for the people who enjoy PvP, and go for the win in BGs. This is the priority.
 - For the people who don't really enjoy PvP that much, there should be ways to get comparable loot with comparable time investment, even just a bit easier.
