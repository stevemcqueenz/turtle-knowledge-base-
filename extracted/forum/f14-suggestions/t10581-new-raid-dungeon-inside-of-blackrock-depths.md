Blackrock Mountains do have a lot of dungeons and raids, but more options or new things to do is a good thing. I do agree Winterspring needs...something...to make it more than a farming zone as well.

Is there any dev documents or leaked info on what the original door would have contained? If there isn't, or turtle devs wanted something different than another flavor of MC, we could use the fact that there is a door to our advantage. Doors are generally constructed to keep something out or something in. What if a great treasure was inside the Vaults of Thaurissan? Or it was some form of weapon or magic development facility where new and frightening implements of war were being created and a plan was being put into motion to unleash them on their brethren soon? Or perhaps its something very different, the Dark Iron dwarves delved deep and found a cave network that led to an underground land akin to Deepholme?

The devs have shown they can create, import, or edit models just fine so the new instance wouldn't have to rely on existing models. In the deep dark a player might encounter crystal dragons, sentient fungus that drives men mad, dwarves infected with living thorium, or giant (AQ boss sized) moles that are the third hardest boss of the zone? There could be some fire elementals or roaming Dark Iron clan for familiarity or to lull players into a false sense of safety. What if, deeper in, they find corpses of said dwarves, destroyed golems and war machines, or an abandoned slave pit which upon investigation a massive corpse eating monstrosity explodes out of the rock and attacks the players?
