# Suggestion: Dabyrie's Farmstead Clean-up

## Post 144913 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-12T19:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144913#p144913 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Dabyrie's Farmstead in Arathi Highlands is a farmstead that is being actively used and labored by workers, and actively ran by the Dabyrie family. Yet, its structures inexplicably still look just as rundown and dilapidated as the other farms of the region, that being Northfold Manor and the Go'Shek Farm. There's bones strewn all across the buildings and it looks as though the farmland hasn't been maintained in some time. This is all the more jarring, as [the family is described as being healthy and prospering](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1164) in comparison to the Callow family that was ruined and destroyed in the Third War.

As the farmland is still actively serving Stromgarde and has a plethora of laborers on the land, I would propose using the red-tiled human building models for Kenata Dabyrie's farmhouse, the stables, and the barn (as also being used in Gallant Square in Stromgarde Keep and at the Livingstone Croft). Furthermore, I would suggest replacing the destroyed silo models with the intact silo models. The fences around the field could also use some straightening up, but I'd prioritize cleaning up the structures over fixing the fences.
