# Add Death Knights & Dark Rangers to Horde

## Post 122858 by Morkof — 2025-03-18T06:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122858#p122858 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I advise we allow Death Knight skins for Humans and Dark Ranger (plus Blood Elf) skins for Elves to join the Horde. Perhaps there could also be a way to unlock these without relying solely on the donation shop (a quest or achievement to leave Alliance). Not only would this bolster the Horde’s numbers and equal out the playing field, but this also remains lore-accurate, while ensuring the Horde isn’t overrun with these new options converting from Alliance to Horde. It also preserves the RP aspect for players concerned over those particular skins within Alliance.
