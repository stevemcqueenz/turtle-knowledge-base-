# Suggestion: Increase duration of PvP timer.

## Post 144706 by Gangout — 2025-08-12T04:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144706#p144706 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello fellow pvpers! I have been running warmode since lvl 1 and have not turned it off even after hitting 60, because lemme tell ya, win or lose, pvp is fun. World pvp especially.

On this server where pvp is opt in, there are ofcourse plenty of people out there running pvp off, which is completely fine, some of us just want to PvE. More power to them.

I have however seen plenty of people abuse the 5 minute pvp timer where they sit around with pvp off, and only pick pvp fights they are guaranteed to win, such as killing lowbies, or slowly gathering in a group with pvp off and waiting for the right moment to strike. Or simply to guarantee a first hit on someone already pvp tagged like say, a mage with pvp off charging up a pyroblast that tags him for pvp only once it actually hits. Or a priest having pvp off using Mind control to throw people off cliffs, only to turn it back off again in the 5 minutes it takes for it to turn off.
Should these people lose a fight, they sit around for 5 minutes as a ghost waiting for the timer to run out before ressurecting.

I understand that this server (Nordanaar) isnt primarily a pvp server but rather a RP-PvE one, with the option to opt into PvP should you wish to do so.

Now my question to you all would be this: What would you think about a change that would increase the pvp timer if you do in fact decide to opt into pvp up from 5 minutes to say 30 or even 60 minutes. I believe this would make fewer people abuse the pvp timer to engage in ratty behaviours and perhaps think twice before choosing to opt into PvP. I would love to hear your opinions on the subject. I understand of course there is a pvp server (Banana realm) and naturally I would be playing there should the population be closer to the same on Nordanaar. But from my understanding that peaks at like 2k whereas this one peaks at like 10k.

## Post 144730 by Fugus (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-12T07:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144730#p144730 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am on the PVP server anyways but I can definitely see ways to abuse. I think a proper way to do it would be this.

 1. When you flag for PVP, your flag doesn’t disable for 1 hour afterwards. But if you aren’t in contested territory than it will unflag after 5 minutes of no PVP activity or helping others who are flagged like on the PvP server.

 2. If you flag yourself for PVP, you can’t attack others for 5 minutes until after you have flagged unless they attack you first. So, if they all wait in a group, they just gave themselves a front row seat to watching their friend get hit without being able to hit back until they are hit or the 5 minute timer is up. Again, unless you are in your own territories then it’s instant. You also are marked to show you are waiting to flag, this flag is visible even if you are in stealth so you can’t flag and wait 5 minutes in stealth to avoid being noticed.

 3. Anyone flagged for PVP can’t join a cross faction group. So if you are flagged for war, you can’t group with your enemies.

That would solve much of the abuse. If you flag you are flagged for an hour so long as you are in contested territory, if you try and flag for an opportunity, then they get first dibs on you killing that opportunity as well as the ability to wait as a group and dog pile on them unless you are in your own territory where you got the home field advantage and the ability to defend your zone Instantly.

Can agree with removing Warmode on the PvE. This post wasn't about just Warmode players but those who abuse PvP flagging to grief others safely.

In regards to the 5 minute timer after you flagged. That would be to prevent a group of 5 or more people getting together and all flagging at once just to jump someone. This would mean even if all 5 of them flagged at once, they couldn't all jump in leaving them vulnerable for flagging like that.

They could have easily just ported or flown to a zone they control and waited out 5 minutes of a timer there.
