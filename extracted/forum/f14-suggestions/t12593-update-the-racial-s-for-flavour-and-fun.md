# Update the racial's for flavour and fun!

## Post 83929 by Lawfulevil — 2024-01-07T21:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83929#p83929 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all!

After eyeing up Humans and Goblins with their double weapon skill racial, I believe all races should have a weapon racial.

I suggest the following:

 **Gnomes** - Staves - The iconic spellcasting class and perhaps they use them to assist in knocking over tall things!
 **Night Elves** - Bows - Makes sense. Very WC3 appropriate. Can perhaps also do Polearms or Staves.
 **Tauren** - Mace or Polearm - I'm pretty certain that Tauren in a very early WoW Vanilla build may have had maces before it was given to Humans. Also, every single piece of Tauren art and culture revolves around polearms.
 **Undead** - Daggers. Small, light and easily concealable. Used in horrific rituals or for stabbing people in the back.

Also, give Shamans polearms. That way, every Tauren class can use polearms which just makes sense!
