# New Dungeons could be more Vanilla-like

## Post 99698 by Overtyped — 2024-07-16T23:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99698#p99698 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After doing the new dungeons I feel they were not very vanilla-like. The mobs packs are too manageable without the need for any CC. If you look at dungeons like Scholo/Dire maul North/strath live, the old endgame dungeons, cc is pretty much required, and it was made that way for a reason.

It would be pleasant if there was a new more challenging dungeon in the future, one where cc is important.
The reason many classic players don't like retail is due to the dungeon archtype of join group, go to dungeon, aoe the packs down, rinse and repeat.

It would be sad to continue activision's poor design decisions here.
