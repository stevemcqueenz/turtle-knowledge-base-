# Limit LFT by locations

## Post 50935 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-06-22T06:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50935#p50935 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently players can register to LFT anywhere in the world.
For example Deadmines.
When groups get formed, then some of party members could be in Darkshore, Barrens or Silverpine forest.
Time to travel from those locations to deadmines is huge. It takes a lot of time to travel to dungeon. People near entrance get nervous and bored by waiting.

Proposal is:
version 1  Each dungeon should have a list of locations where you can register to it. If player leave those locations, then he should automatically leave the queue.
To register to RFC players should be in Orgrimmar, Durotar, Barrens, Tirisfal Glades, or Undercity.
To register to WC players should be in Barrens, Mulgore, Thunder Bluff, Durotar, Orgrimmar.
To register to Deadmines players should be in Westfall, Stormwind, Elwyn Forest, Duskwood.
List of locations is subject of discussion and tweaks.

version 2
When you register as tank, you have flag "search for adjacent".
When it is enabled, you invite only people in adjacent zones. You can always remove this flag and search for anyone available.
Adjacent zones to RFC are Orgrimmar, Durotar, Barrens, Tirisfal Glades or Undercity.
Adjacent zones to WC are Barrens, Mulgore, Thunder Bluff, Durotar, Orgrimmar.
Adjacent zones to Deadmines are Westfall, Stormwind, Elwyn Forest, Duskwood.
List of locations is subject of discussion and tweaks.
This option is for tank because LFT is designed to make tank party leader.
On second page of LFT "Browse" - where you can see amount of people of each role. Amount should look like 4/13. First number is amount of adjacent. Second number is amount ot total registered.

If player run LFT from the inside of the instance, then only players in exact locations should be invited.
For example if you are in WC and search for +1, then LFT should search only in Barrens. When you are in Deadmines and search for +1, LFT should search only in Westfall.

It will solve the issue of waiting some people for too long to travel to dungeon.
