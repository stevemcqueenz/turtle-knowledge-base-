# Add Rogue Poisons and Ammo To Field Repair Bot 75B

## Post 87535 by Mannerless01 — 2024-02-11T12:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87535#p87535 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For those of us who play a rogue or a hunter, it's obviously still good to have the ability to instantly sell and repair our junk and gear, but for whatever reason, it holds the reagents for every other class but these two. I get if you might not wanna stock basic ammo, but us Rogues can only get our poisons in major cities.

Obviously we keep a stock in our banks and inventory, but whenver we run low, we're forced to go back to a city and make the track to the Rogue trainer areas to find the shady dealers and make our poisons. Why can't they just be sold in the bot?

Remember, the bot is a purchased item, so it should definately come equipped with the best bang for your buck. I highly suggest placing this fix in your next update or minor patch, as it will go a long way for all of us.
