**About the Removal of the Elwynn Gryphon**

I just wanted to chime in that, while I understand the argument about preserving the *“classic journey”*, I still think removing the Elwynn gryphon without offering any replacement is a mistake.

The **Epoch** server, for example, approached this in a very interesting way: they added **caravans** — scripted wagons with preset routes that work like ground-based boats between cities.
If Turtle were removing the gryphon to add something like that, it would make sense.
But just removing it and adding nothing... feels like a step backward.

Interestingly, this lines up with an old suggestion I made a long time ago:
![👉](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f449.svg) <https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85323&hilit=caravan#p85323>

Also, the gryphon isn’t just for low-level players. Level 60s use it too.
When combined with the second gryphon (the one up the river toward Northshire Abbey), you can actually fly **all the way to Blackrock Mountain**.
That’s incredibly useful, especially for players without access to portals or a full flight path network.

The gryphon became part of the server’s identity.
Removing it now, without a worthy replacement, erases a little bit of the magic that made us fall in love with Turtle in the first place.
