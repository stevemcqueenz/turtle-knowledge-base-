Very nice post. I was working on something similar. I will post my work here soon so we can see a list of available arrows and bullets to further illustrate the problem.

While I like the recipes you suggest, and how more profs are into the arrow crafting, I feel like some of them on engineering are a bit expensive to make. Using feathers makes a lot of sense and gives a better use to the feathers. But the Silver, Gold and Truesilver Headed Arrows feel a bit costly on their mineral requirements, specially if we compare them with their bullets counterpart and realize they don't bring any above average damage for their level. However, it's clear those arrows are just a material that will be used for crafting the green/blue ones.

My personal suggestions would be:
 1-Replace the fore damage on explosive arrows with armor reduction debuff or something more interesting. Those are the signature top arrows crafted by eng.
 2-Make the debuff arrows work stacking their effect. They would apply a smaller debuff but it can be stacked. That way the debuff must be kept and can't be reaplied instantly.
 3-Add more arrows for levels lower than 30.
 4-Reduce a little the metal bars required in the mentioned arrows. Not against adding something else tho.
 5-Add more recipes with less used mats, just like feathers, that are almost useless now. I.e. some scales that barely get any use in leatherworking, some scorpid stings and spider poisons, raptor claws, fangs... You know, all that white items that doesn't sell on the AH yet would fit being used here.

Also, the question about bows and crossbows being craftable in engineering remains. Those and staves are the only weapons that aren't craftable (but that's for another post).

PD: I agree on what you said. The suggestions are just that.
