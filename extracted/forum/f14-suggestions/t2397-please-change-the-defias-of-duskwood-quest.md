# Please change the Defias of Duskwood quest

## Post 14991 by Aykhanchik — 2021-12-29T17:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14991#p14991 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Defias of Duskwood quest currently requires you to go to the Yorgen Farmstead in Duskwood and kill:

 -10 Defias Night Blades
 -15 Defias Night Runners
 -10 Defias Enchanters

Issue is, the area is mostly populated by Night Blades, so killing 10 of them is an easy task, meanwhile there are only 3 Enchanters and around 5-6 Night Runners who are also invisible. You need to wait for enemies to respawn multiple times in order to kill enough of them for the quest objective.

I suggest you change the objective for it to be -15 Night Blades, -10 Night Runners and maybe either add another Enchanter roaming in the area or just reduce the amount we need to kill.

This quest is not broken or anything, just the time you need to spend here waiting for mobs to respawn around 3-4 times is annoying.

Thank you!
