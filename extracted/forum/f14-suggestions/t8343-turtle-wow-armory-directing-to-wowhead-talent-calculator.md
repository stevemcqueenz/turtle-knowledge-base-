Turtle wow has its own talents that are different from classic, and Turtle has its own talent calculator.
Why then does the armory direct to the wowhead talent calculator instead of the Turtle wow calculator?

## Post 53757 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-16T21:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53757#p53757 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Clicking on "TALENTS" (yes, all-caps) on the main site directs to [the on-site talents calculator](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/), so that is a bit odd.  Someone should look into this.
Though, to be fair, the Armory is a bit broken at times, anyway…

For the while, you can do the following:
1. Find the character you're looking for in the Armory.
2. Right-click their Talent and paste the URL somewhere.
3. Copy **everything** that comes after "<https://classic.wowhead.com/talent-calc/>" in the URL.
(It should be something like "class/numbers-numbers-numbers")
4. Paste that code onto the end of the Talent Calculator URL.
(For example: [something like this](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue/020000000000000-3000000000000000000-05000000000000000))
It's irritating, but it works.
