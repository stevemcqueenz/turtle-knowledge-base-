# SAVE TEL ABIM Server

## Post 174241 by ForumGentleman (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-04-06T20:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174241#p174241 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

**Clearly, Tel Abim is not going through a good phase right now.**

Stuck at 1k-1.5k pop... definitely not enough for a PvP server. Server needs at least 2.5k/3k to have constant PvP.

BGs are quite rare these days. World PvP? Barely a thing.

So, what do we do?  **What can the DEVS do?**

A slow trickle of new players is not gonna cut it. The other two servers have too much inertia and their high pop is a big appeal, even to normally PvP-minded players.

My suggestions are:

- Unique features for the server – PvP and world PvP unique content: world PvP quests, capturable objectives with faction-wide buffs, world PvP specific rewards, destroyable minor cities, loot on faction leaders... There are plenty of ideas, some of which don't require too much work.

- Promoting the server – on the server selection screen obviously, possibly also with a dedicated YouTube video (once we get unique features). Maybe giving a limited XP boost for a couple of months?

- Cross-server BGs – I honestly don't like the idea, but maybe it could help the server until it reaches a self-sustaining population.

- Free one-way transfer from other servers to Tel Abim – also controversial, maybe just for a limited time?

What do you guys think? Any other suggestions?
