# A few Surival Hunt Tweaks.

## Post 166284 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-24T13:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166284#p166284 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I saw MM got alot of love this time, which is great, it will probably make SV pretty much unseen in PvP now, and maybe dropped in PvE.

A few talent buffs id like to see:

Trap Mastery
Increases the duration of Freezing and Frost Trap effects by 10/20/30% and the damage of Immolation and Explosive Trap effects by 10/20/30%. in addition reduces the chance that enemies will resist trap effects by 4/7/10%, and reduces trap cooldown by 1/2/3 sec.
(extra 3 sec cd reduce on traps)

Improved Feign Death
Reduces the chance your Feign Death will be resisted by 4/8% and reduces the cooldown by 5/10 sec.

SV hunters have no way to interupt casts, the only way is to FD to drop the enemies cast, This will help by lowering the CD, without giving the class the same tool as everyone else, also barely anyone takes this talent for pvp.

Stinging Nettle:
Your Mongoose Bite and Triggered fire traps now apply your highest rank of Serpent Sting for 50% of the duration, Freezing Trap now removes Serpent Sting and Immolation Trap Effect.

This just makes it so you can CC something if theyve been effected by Serpent Sting.

Viscious Strikes:
Mongoose Bite and Lacerate have a 15/30% Chance to reset the cooldown of your Raptor Strike.

Lacerate:
Attack for 40% of your attack power, damage increased by 20% if the target has ANY bleed effect on it (from any class) and remove the clunky 'From the side' mechanic' and requiring a crit
 12 sec Cooldown.

Carve:
Now strikes with both weapons if dual wielding for 60% melee Damage. (this is a buff for the underused DW SV)

Trap Mastery:
Reduces the Mana cost of all traps by 20%, and they now scale with AP, in addition, 2 traps can exist at the same time for 1 minute, (but still the same cd), so youd have to wait 15 seconds to drop the next trap.
