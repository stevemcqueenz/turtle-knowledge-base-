# Stackable AoE speed buff

## Post 107643 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-12T09:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107643#p107643 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Most desired thing for low level is speed improvement.
Turtle team have added turtle mount which slightly increases movement speed.
One can also level yourself a hunter to 40 to get Aspect of the Pack, which increases movement speed of all adjacent party members for 40%.

Proposal is:
Add some way to get minor version of Aspect of the Pack. Like 10% increase. But it should stack! So if all party members get this buff, then total speed increase will be like 50%.
This buff should fade if party members were hit by attack or spell.
This buff should not disappear if party members gather chest, fish, ore or herbs.
This will encourage people to group up for more content.
This item can be made near cooking fire and be Unique(2). You craft it in the town, all party moves to quest location, do quest, use this item again and quickly travel back to town.
If you spend all 2 items, but have raw materials, flint and tinder, then you can create a camp, everyone will restore their supplies of this item and whole party can swiftly move back.
