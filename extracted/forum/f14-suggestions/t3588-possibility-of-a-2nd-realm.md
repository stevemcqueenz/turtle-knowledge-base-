# Possibility of a 2nd Realm

## Post 21609 by Citizenkane — 2022-08-09T12:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21609#p21609 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Before anyone goes "Cope more, fresh servers only serve as an artificial ego booster"; I never mentioned anything about a fresh server. All I'm proposing is to open a second, identical realm, including all progression etc. The impetus for this is, of course, the rapidly increasing server population.

The recent class update has seen the meteoric rise in popularity of Turtle Wow. Even before that, the hype generated from said updates also gave the population a significant boost. The pop peak has gone from sub-1000 in May to 3000 today. This has not come without problems.

As a reference point, the player cap back when vanilla first launched was 2500. Some might say that this number is irrelevant in today's context, but I disagree. Much of the game was already created on initial launch, with minor tweaks along the way. The queues for quest mobs, escort NPCs we see today are not surprising given that the game was designed for a much smaller population. "But the current peak is not far off from 2500, I don't see the problem", you say. I'm glad you asked, because this leads to my next point.

Make no mistake, this isn't 2004 vanilla Wow where the world was new. Everything is now well-documented, such that even true noobs can simply download addons to know the spawn locations of chests, rares etc. As a result, almost everything worth camping is camped. Turtle Wow has many features which exacerbate the overpopulation issue. An oft-discussed one is hardcore. There are many ongoing discussions about it, so I shan't beat a dead horse. Suffice to say, hardcore effectively doubles, or even triples the population especially at low levels. At peak times, it is almost impossible to quest in leveling zones because mobs are either being farmed by HC or AOE farmed.

There have been occasions in the server's history when the idea of a new realm has been tossed around. Understandably, there was no immediate need for it back then and was relegated to "when the time comes". I believe the time is now.
