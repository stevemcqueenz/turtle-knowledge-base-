Hello to all the turtles!

I was thinking these days, what if this wonderful server also has a launcher?

I noticed that most of the competition has such a launcher. Why would we need it? Well, for the following:
 - downloading the game client. Being facilitated directly from the source and without errors;
 - an options system that contains: exe client on x32bit or x64bit (if both formats are available); customer repair function; the update option (either automatic or not); uninstall client;
 - a connection system on the platform (launcher) that facilitates a direct connection of the personal account to the game account, when the "play" button is executed;
 - functions such as: game, addons, news and forum or store (optional).
