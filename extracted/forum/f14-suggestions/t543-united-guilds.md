# United Guilds

## Post 2815 by Distortedshadow — 2019-05-13T01:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2815#p2815 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1. Allow people to invite opposite faction members to their guilds if they want to. If people are already grouping, raiding and talking to each other, this just seems like a mechanical and superficial hurdle. Sure, you'll have some horde-only or alliance-only themed guilds. Those guilds don't have to invite the other faction if they don't want to.

 2. Combine the two baby turtle guilds together. More people in the new player chat means new players get their questions answered more quickly and have more people to talk to when deciding whether to stay and play on this server. Right now Horde hatchlings has 1 person online. It's not really a roleplay guild anyways, it just exists to bunch together all the new players. ***Note this would only work if everyone was a "diplomat" by default, otherwise the characters wouldn't be able to communicate.***
