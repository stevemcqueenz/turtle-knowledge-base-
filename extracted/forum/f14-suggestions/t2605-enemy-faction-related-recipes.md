I'm new to the server and I do love my journey so far.
One of the things that got me hooked instantly was the custom content. Especially the leveling relevant content.

I always felt like the professions could use some more recipes.

Also there would be another reason to beat them up.   turtle_tongue_head

Nothing to fancy just some things to have fun gathering materials for and use while leveling.
