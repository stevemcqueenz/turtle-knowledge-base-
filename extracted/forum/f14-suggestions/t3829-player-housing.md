We have guild houses already, and they are frigging lovely ways to populate the world!!
I don't think an instance isolated from the world would be interesting or fun. Nor does it add anything to gameplay.
You can check how Dragon's Prophet housing used to work, they had a whole profession dedicated to it, it was a fundamental part of progression,  linked to dragon breeding, making it a used function (visiting others was also encouraged by giving people materials for caring for other's eggs, and increasing the stats of such dragons).

Housing in wow, as it stands now,I feel would give nothing and in fact would be counterproductive to fun in the game.

I feel guild houses are already fascinating enough!
