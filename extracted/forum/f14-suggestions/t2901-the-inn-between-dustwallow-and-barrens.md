# The Inn between Dustwallow and Barrens

## Post 18117 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-04-14T18:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18117#p18117 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is a destroyed in on the road between Dustwallow and the Barrens and I think there were quests from Theramore involving it.

I always liked the idea of some kind of neutral inn where you can see members of both factions drinking and interacting together and was really disappointed that in the game it was destroyed.

My suggestion is to have another inn next to it built, probably on the opposite side with 1-2 additional buildings and maybe a couple of quests that are faction-neutral, given by questgivers from races of both factions, like Orcs, Humans, etc.

I think it would be very suitable for the server where both factions can group together and in general interact together compared to a traditional server.

Some quests can involve:

 - further inquiries on the destroyed inn across the road
 - gathering supplies for expanding the settlement and rebuilding the burned down building across (just plans, never going to happen)
 - helping residents of the area with menial tasks
