Besides, I am relatively more knowledgable on European or western culture and history (above the common level of Chinese English-Chinese translators) and believe I can do better in WoW's localisation.
We could have further discussion about the schedule and other details of the work even if you are not aiming to attract more Chinese players whose English level, in general, is low. After all, it's free.
