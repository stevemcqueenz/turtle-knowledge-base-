## Post 161928 by ForumGentleman (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-12-07T16:49:00Z

Hunters need help. We’ve fallen behind on TWoW.

There have been many posts about the Hunter class. This is an attempt to compile the absolute minimum, essential changes needed in the next major patch to bring all three specs from "struggling" to "viable AND fun.

 **Marksmanship: The outdated spec**

Problems:

- PvE: Average DPS with an outdated Aimed Shot. Severe mana issues.

- PvP: One of the worst specs for Arena/Duel. Lagging far behind, with a broken CC chain and no impactful tools.
Solutions:

- Fix Aimed Shot: Create synergy with Steady Shot (e.g., Steady Shots reduce Aimed Shot cast time, or Aimed empowers the next few Steady Shots).

- Fix PvP CC (CRITICAL): Remove the shared Diminishing Return between Scatter Shot and Freezing Trap.

- Address Mana: Add a low chance for ranged attacks from players under Trueshot Aura to restore 1-2% base mana.

- Give a PvP Identity: Move Trueshot Aura lower in the tree. Add a new 31-point talent with PvP utility, such as:
 **Explosive Shot**: *Fires a shot that detonates after 3 sec, dealing moderate AoE damage and stunning the primary target for 2 sec. (1 min cooldown).*

 **Beast Mastery: broken identity**

Problems:

- The spec is in a weird spot. It lost its iconic Bestial Wrath for a clunky, non-synergistic Baited Shot.
Solutions:

- Move Baited Shot to a lower tier in the BM or MM tree.

- Reintroduce Bestial Wrath as the 31-point talent. Remove Kill Command CC dispel component to balance it.

 **Survival: The Melee Spec with No "Survival"**

Problems:

- Decent rotation but still lower DPS than most classes, with major mana problems

- Subpar in PvP. No gap-closer or reliable control. Effectively a dead spec in Arena.
Solutions:

- Add a late-tier talent: **Trap Launcher.** *Allows you to launch traps at your target from range.*
Why? MM has Scatter Shot, BM has a pet stun. This gives Survival the PvP tool and utility it desperately needs to function, both as a gap-closer and for control. Also gives the spec more utility in PVP

 **FEEDBACK ON HUNTER CHANGES**

First off, I want to say thank you. It’s clear the team has put a lot of work into this overhaul and has  read and considered forum feedback. Big props for that.

 **That being said; the PVP aspect is being CLEARLY overlooked:**

 **The Great (Big Thanks For These):**

- Aspect of the Viper – Huge. Solves the biggest mana pain point.

- BM Changes – Bestial Wrath is back, Scent of Blood is interesting. This spec looks much healthier. Needs testing, but very positive.

- MM Foundation – Baseline Trueshot Aura freeing up the capstone, the Aimed Shot/Steady Shot synergy, and the Lock and Load talent are solid steps forward.
The Crucial Misses / Concerns:

 **1. Marksmanship – Still Lacks a PvP Identity & Has a Broken CC Chain**

- While the DPS changes look good, DPS alone doesn’t make a spec viable in PvP.

- Scatter/Trap DR: This is non-negotiable. Until Scatter Shot’s DR with Freezing Trap is removed, Hunters have no reliable hard CC chain in a meta where everyone has a trinket. This single change is more important than any damage buff.

- Experimental Ammunition: The random, cycling effect seems clunky and impractical for PvP. In a fast-paced fight, you can’t plan around a Fire/Arcane/Nature cycle. This feels like a purely PvE/raid utility talent and doesn’t give MM the “punch” or utility it needs against players.
 **2. Survival – Zero Additions for PvP (Actually Gets Worse)**

- The new 'Alone Against the World' petless option brings nothing to PvP. If anything, taking this talent would make you weaker in PvP by sacrificing a core survival and pressure tool.
