I also think that enchanting could use cooldown on strong enchants.
Compare it with other recipes.
For example with Nightfall. <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23653> Mediocre axe in terms of damage, but you need 10 CDs of Arcanite bar to craft one.
In the same time, all healers on the server can be enchanted with 55 healing in the day when this recipe dropped in MC for single enchanter.
Does it encourage social connections? I don't think so. Just go to capital to any enchanter spamming in /trade and you get it with any tip you feel comfortable + mats cost.

If you compare enchanting to Alchemy, then 55 healing can be compared to Flask recipes.
Enchanting doesn't require you to be in specific place. Just go to bank in orgrimmar and you get BiS upgrade. Flasks require you to be in special place to craft them.

Even if some enchanter provides "exclusive" service only to own guild, any other enchanter will serve this super powerful enchant to rest of the server.

Also, some guy noted another interesting thing. High level enchants don't provide further upgrade to higher gear tier.
You enchant same 55 healing to Jin\'do\'s Hexxer <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19890> and to Hammer of the Twisting Nether <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=23056>
Like you get upgrade only once.
I do understand that special enchants for ilevels 80+ will introduce another step of gear disparity, but who cares, really?
