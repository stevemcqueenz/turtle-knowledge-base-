# Make "Enchant - Fiery Blaze" available to enchanters or blacksmiths

## Post 79744 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-12-03T22:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79744#p79744 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For those unaware, there is a weapon enchantment (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=5421>) that is a quest reward from Badlands (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=706>), that was intended to be a nod to a quest from the Warcraft 3 campaign.
The enchantment is actually pretty good, especially for the level you can get it at. It's essentially a weaker, but AoE version of Fiery Weapon.

The issues I have with this enchant are:
 -It's single-use, so you only get one use per character
 -It's Alliance-only.

Currently the only way to trade the enchant is to apply it to a BoE item. This means that for Hordies, it can never be applied to BoP items, and "buying" it would mean finding someone who hasn't done their quest, trading to them your (possibly very expensive) BoE weapon before you equip it, have them apply it and give it back to you, which involves a lot of trust (and possibly money).
It turns out that it can actually be applied via the trade window if you right-click the item before you open the trade.
Thanks for the insight, @Valadorn!

Wowhead comments also highlight that you can apply it to a low level white weapon to powerlevel your alts with and/or to sell.

So what if, instead of this entire fiasco, we made it available for enchanters/blacksmiths to create?
The material will still be a black dragon heart, which will now be a generic item instead of a quest item, with the same 2% drop rates (maybe buff it to 5% for the mature elite black drakes?). It will require some other mats like dusts and essences, and the recipe will be rewarded by a followup quest. This means that again only Alliance will be able to get it, but they could enchant a hordie's weapon (or the recipe could be BoE)

So why blacksmiths? It's just a nod back to Warcraft 3, the dwarf which enchanted Arthas' weapon was a blacksmith, not an enchanter. Furthermore, I think blacksmiths could use more ways to make money from their profession, with it being one of the toughest to level.
