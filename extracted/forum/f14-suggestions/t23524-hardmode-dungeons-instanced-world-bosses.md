# Hardmode Dungeons & Instanced World Bosses

## Post 164662 by Djaw1 — 2026-01-08T09:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164662#p164662 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey Turtle WoW team and community!
First of all, I really love Turtle WoW and the whole Vanilla+ philosophy. You’ve done an amazing job adding new dungeons, bosses, and reworking old locations - it truly feels like a living version of Vanilla.

However, I’d like to share two suggestions that could greatly improve level 60 gameplay variety and accessibility to new content.

 1. Hardmode Dungeons for Level 60

At level 60, dungeon gameplay feels quite limited. In practice, most players farm Black Morass every week because of the quest rewards, while many other dungeons - including your new and expanded ones - are almost forgotten.

The problem is not dungeon quality, but lack of endgame relevance. So here is my Suggestion -

Introduce Hardmode versions of all dungeons, designed specifically for level 60 characters.

It will give players a reason to revisit all dungeons at level 60 and keeps dungeon content relevant instead of focusing on one “meta” dungeon. This decision fits perfectly into the Vanilla+ philosophy and makes your huge amount of dungeon content actually played, not just leveled through once

 2. Instanced World Bosses (Like Season of Discovery)

You’ve created many interesting new World Bosses, but realistically, most players will never even see them alive
Bosses are killed instantly by organized guilds and casual players and smaller groups never get a chance. So - new content exists but is inaccessible for most of the playerbase

So my suggestion is to create instanced versions of World Bosses, similar to Season of Discovery - with separate instance zones with one or 2 World Bosses. It will allows everyone to experience your new World Bosses content

Thanks for your amazing work - Turtle WoW already feels like the best version of Vanilla, and these changes could make it even better.
