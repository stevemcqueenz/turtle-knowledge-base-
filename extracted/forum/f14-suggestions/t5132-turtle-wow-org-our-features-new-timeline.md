# Turtle-wow.org => Our features => New Timeline

## Post 31141 by Illyane (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-12-20T07:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31141#p31141 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello ! I'm back on Turtle WoW after almost 2 years of absence, and I'm blown away by the progress of this server. Never in years of playing WoW on private and public have I been so hooked by a server. Just: Thank you for your work and your passion.

I am a roleplayer and I want to start a guild, participate and invite friends, play with other players. A small way for me to support this server by making RP even more present. Without wanting to appear more than I am, I have more than a decade of RP on WoW and I noticed that the official Turtle WoW website was missing a really important element: an official timeline.

A timeline is essential in my opinion! It allows to agree with the other players on the understanding of the context as well as the challenges of the moment (who are the baddies ? how are the relations between Horde and Alliance ?), it is a simple and efficient way to avoid interpretation conflicts. It is also a way to make the real difference between TurtleWoW and Retail even clearer. I've noticed that while most players understand that we are no longer in the official version of history, few understand what that implies (for example what about magical lores, demons, Elune etc).

I really think that a page displaying a timeline  would be a great help to the RP community and to show new player how this server worked hard to create something different. A timeline would be a good basis, after which lore fanatics might need help in noting and archiving all the little differences (in the characters of the major characters in the story, in the orientation of the factions, etc), but this is clearly another story.

Thank you for reading :)
