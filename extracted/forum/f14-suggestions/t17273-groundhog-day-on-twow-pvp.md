## Post 117957 by Tc137 — 2025-01-26T09:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117957#p117957 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Every single day in lvl 60 pvp is the same..

You sign up solo, and 90% of the time end up facing a premade group on the other faction, with a mix of ungrouped, low, mid and maybe a few high ranks on your team - it doesn't matter which side you play, your experience will be the same.

Why is it that a raid group of marshal/general+ rankers can be matched against a full group of solo joiners that are mid to low rank with maybe 1 or 2 higher ranks? It's a waste of time for everyone involved, even the winning team.. it's really not "PvP" if all you do is steamroll and capture the objectives without even a slight challenge.

This isn't a new issue, and it really is a huge problem that Twow devs seem to not give a rats ass about. Everyone complains about it in battlegrounds and the resounding answer is always the same .... "Twow devs don't care about pvp"
ok, so why not disable it altogether then? it seems the preferable choice is to leave it in the awful state it's in and has been in for years.

Surely it wouldn't be that difficult to make it so premade groups of 4/5+ can only enter set battlegrounds with other premade groups - if that's so difficult, why not make it so you can't have groups of more than 2 or 3 join BG's altogether.

I get that it's a PVE server - but PVP is still a huge part of the game and neglecting this problem for so long really is inexcusable

Some might argue and say to "grind hard enough to get into the premade groups yourself" - but honestly, I wouldn't want to.. If I wanted PVP without a challenge, I would go to crossroads and gank lvl 15's all day.
Fighting against a premade group with a bunch of randoms isn't a challenge, it is IMPOSSIBLE to beat. Unless you get lucky and every 1 in 10 games you end up with as the spare part in a premade group on your faction, in which my participation is basically pointless.

FIX THIS SH** - It's been absolute aids for long enough and I, as well as many other players, are absolutely SICK of it.
