# Upcoming Ceremonial Magram Dagger changes

## Post 55432 by Hctwowfan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-08-01T06:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55432#p55432 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

According to the 1.17.0 Itemization Changelog post, the

 *Ceremonial Magram Dagger (CMD)* (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60884>)

will be turned into a unique item.

You might want to reconsider this change.

- People spent time to grind reputation to buy 2x CMD.

- People spent 13.5g per CMD so you basically just erase money by turning the second dagger to vendor trash.

- Rogues and Warriors might currently have 2x CMD equipped, so once the patch goes live, they're suddenly stuck with only one weapon.

- CMD has +3 dagger skill, weapon skill items are rare enough, don't delete them.
I got two warriors, a rogue and a melee only hunter with 2x CMD equipped (three of em are goblins, yay). So am I supposed to straight up not play them anymore? But you can get a different weapon. Sure, but maybe I already did quests that had weapon rewards and sold them because they were worse than a CMD. So now I'm supposed to do what? Buy a white vendor item for lack of easy access to anything at all and move on from there, idk... spend some 10h to pick pocket 4x gutrippers? Sounds fun.

Is the CMD too good and should it be changed into a unique? Debatable, likely yes.

Here's my suggestion if you still want to make it a unique item:
Leave the current CMD version as is, everyone who has em, keeps em and they're not unique so you dont screw everyone currently using 2x CMD. Add a new version of CMD, same item but unique. Once the patch goes live, make the vendor only sell the new now unique version of the dagger.

Itemization changes are all good, but this change is a bit over the top. That's not reevaluating the stats of an item - add some stamina, remove some crit, do whatever - you straight up just delete the item, that's just off.
