# A blocking legendary quest every 10 levels

## Post 148902 by Dduricingame — 2025-08-28T11:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148902#p148902 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've always had an idea (since 2007 when TBC was released) to have a limiting factor that allows for leveling past lvl60 and step into the TBC world. I was very frustrated that for around 6-7 months my guild was raiding MC and BWL and we achieved so much, but when TBC came out all of that was thrown away and it felt like we were wasting our time.

It was rumored that TBC might introduce Hero classes where you become a lvl1 Hero and you would be leveling towards a lvl10 Hero like you would in Warcraft III and that's what gave life to this idea of having a legendary quest that would make you a Hero. Now, making a completely new Hero type structure might be complicated and too much work, but there's something even better behind it. Legendary quests for existing content.

Every round number level (like 10, 20 and so on) would have a legendary blocking quest where you are unable to gain experience (above 9.9, 19.9 and so on) until you finish the quest. It would make you travel the world and explore new areas, join forces with other players to achieve the goal of the quest, but ultimately play the game in a vanilla fashion where you need to work with others in order to achieve something worth doing.

Pros:
 - Meet people of the same exact level that you may end up playing with for the entirety of the game
 - Stay at one of the lower levels forever if you enjoy that paritcular PvP experience
 - Have a better understanding of your class at each of the legendary points due to the appropriate requirements of the quests
 - Obtain "end-game" equipment for each of the tiers
 - Gain respect for any players that have managed to overcome the legendary quests and have joined the upper ranks
 - Less skilled players may end up skill locked until they gain the necessary skills to progress further (this could be a con)
 - Please add more pros in the thread (that pop in your head) if you like this idea

Cons:
 - Slightly slower progression
 - I'm sure someone will think of something in the thread

Note:
This is the basic idea, it can be brainstormed further, but I would love to see something like this happen. There was an accidental trial of this in the classic release in 2019, where they were manually locking the levels on round numbers and the feedback was very positive if I remember correctly (correct me if I'm wrong)
