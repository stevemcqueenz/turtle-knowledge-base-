# New Alchemy transmutes

## Post 44302 by Casualnerdity — 2023-05-03T17:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44302#p44302 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Recipe: Transmute Elemental Earth
Requires Cenarion Circle - Honored
Teaches you how to transmute a Core of Earth into three Elemental Earth.

Recipe: Transmute Elemental Air
Requires Wildhammer Clan/Revantusk Tribe - Honored
Teaches you how to transmute a Breath of Wind into three Elemental Air.

Recipe: Transmute Elemental Water
Requires Hydraxian Waterlords - Friendly/Honored
Teaches you how to transmute a Globe of Water into three Elemental Water.

Each has a shared 10 minute cooldown with each other and Transmute Elemental Fire. Triggers the alchemist's transmute cd for the 10 minutes.

Reasons for implementing: reduce the number of raiders in leveling zones taking quest mobs; give alternate means of acquiring elemental components; increase the auction value of Globe/Breath/Core.

Each of these require some form of rep grind, similar to Transmute Elemental Fire's Thorium Brotherhood, but since earth and air are not inside an instance/require a raid reputation, they have a higher requirement.
