I don't think this will ever happen.  Murlocs, as "lovable" as they are, simply don't have any interest in the politics and socio-economic troubles of the modern world.  They would prefer to stick to their ponds, streams, lakes, and seas, slowly growing in numbers and power until they can overtake the land-dwelling races either for themselves or for their queen, Lady Azshara.

Also, for those who need a more "technical" reason as to why not…
| Simply, murlocs aren't currently rigged to use armor. Plus, there are several missing animations from the murloc animation set such as crafting and most emotes. It would take a lot of work to make a murloc model that could use any-and-all armor in the game as well as any possible skills and emotes. |
