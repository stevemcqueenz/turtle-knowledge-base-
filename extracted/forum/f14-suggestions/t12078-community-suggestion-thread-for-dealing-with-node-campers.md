# Community suggestion thread for dealing with node campers

## Post 80046 by Snoodydood (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-12-06T13:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80046#p80046 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Node camping has become a severe problem in twow as of late, with the sheer amount of campers seemly growing all the time. In some places you will see 3-4 people afking on one spot waiting for a rare node like rtv to spawn, which is just plain ridiculous. Some people will afk for literal DAYS on one spot woth the apparent meta being playing on one account, while leaving a toon afk on a spot on their other account and just taking the node every time it respawns, farming something on one account while just gathering the node on the other. My suggestion is simply add an uncommon drop rate of arcane crystals to small thorium nodes and increase their overall spawn rates to stop these ridiculous people. They did this for black lotus having a chance to drop from high level herb, but i think they should make getting AC even easier, as it would stop the campers and while it would de-value AC prices, it would increase the price of tranmutes to keep economy support in a different way.
This is only my suggestion and i made this thread to hear what other people have to say about this issue and hope that devs can get ideas of how to solve it from the community and people who are just tired of these campers.

## Post 80191 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-12-07T16:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80191#p80191 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't know how node spawns work currently, but it can be solved by just randomizing what herb / ore node will spawn on a certain location.

For example, if a zone has 100 mining node spawn locations, give it a distribution of 10 rich thorium, 40 thorium and 20 truesilver. That leaves 30 empty nodes.

When the nodes of a particular type is below its limit, choose a random node location to spawn it. That will make farming just one node way less profitable, because you will get normal thorium most of the time, and if nobody else is mining in the zone, your node might be left empty, encouraging you to move and actively mine the normal way.

The same principle could be applied to herbs. I'd guess the problem is that such a system would be very hard, if not impossible to implement with vanilla's dated and janky codebase. But here's hoping.
