# Suggestion: Druid Talents: Swap Furor and Sylvan Blessing

## Post 164074 by Fugus (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-12-30T22:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164074#p164074 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Feral spec is already spread really thin on talent points and Furor is literally the only talent in the restoration tree feral needs while it has to effectively throw away talent points in the balance tree to get what it needs.

Feral doesn't need quicker wraths, it doesn't really need Nature's Grasp which doesn't even work in most instances and half of all PvP locations while Sylvan Blessing is so-so at best. The only time mana would really be an issue is while you are powershifting which you typically aren't going to be doing on mobs that die fast enough to keep it going anyways.

Also, still awaiting the day that druids can actually use their roots, speed and PvP set bonus in most instances and half of all PvP locations.

Edit: And Balance can easily dip 2 points into resto to get it as they are already going to be putting 5 points into the quicker wraths to get lower anyways. Balance losing nothing, Restoration loses nothing, Feral actually gains out of the improved pathing.

Agreed. Sylvan Blessing is complete and total butt for feral.

If the mobs die so fast for you to benefit from it, you aren't bothering to powershift anyways and if the mobs actually are something to powershift to defeat, they take long enough to the point the buff largely won't active till it's too late to matter.

It's like all it is good for is less down time between pulls for you which doesn't work much when the entire raid is already taking a break. Because leveling, you aren't powershifting world mobs and so when you drop out to heal you have full mana anyways.

It's a solution in search of a problem for feral and is only really decent for Balance and to a lesser extent restoration and Balance already have a mandatory 5 point talent down there with the quicker wrath spells.

Furor is something that is completely feral based and would actually give feral something worth speccing into as they leveled rather than throwing away 5 talent points because around 20% of your total DPS is locked behind 4 talent points above it between the flat 10% increase to everything you do and just how much Omen of Clarity adds to your overall DPS with it's procs. Then the cheaper shifting which is also pretty much pure feral.
