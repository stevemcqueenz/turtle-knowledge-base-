# Suggestions for Quality of Life Changes

## Post 52744 by Bebbop — 2023-07-10T01:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52744#p52744 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings fellow adventurers!

As an new inspiring player on Turtle WoW I would like to suggest a few quality of life changes that could greatly enhance our game-play experience. These improvements aim to streamline certain aspects, saving time and making the journey through Azeroth even more enjoyable. Without further ado, here is a numbered list of key points:

 1. Meeting Stone Summons: One of the most significant changes would be the ability for players to summon their party members directly from meeting stones. This small but significant adjustment would save us valuable time by eliminating the need for tedious travel to the dungeon entrance. It would provide a remarkable convenience, allowing us to jump right into the action and spend more time actually playing the game. Additionally, it is important to note that implementing this change would not render Warlocks obsolete. Warlocks will continue to serve a vital role in raids and maintain their unique summoning capabilities, ensuring their relevance both inside and outside of dungeons.

 2. Druid Resurrection Spell: Druids, renowned for their versatility, could benefit from the addition of a resurrection spell outside of battle. This change would not be game-breaking but rather a valuable asset to players. Imagine the convenience of having a Druid in your group who can revive fallen comrades. This would alleviate the burden on other classes, promote group synergy, and enhance the overall game-play experience.

By implementing these quality of life changes, we can elevate Turtle WoW to new heights. These suggestions are aimed at improving the player experience, promoting convenience, and fostering a more vibrant and engaging community. Let us work together to create a server that is both enjoyable and welcoming to all.

Feel free to discuss these ideas further and provide your own suggestions in the comments below. Together, we can shape the future of Turtle!

Safe travels and epic adventures,
Bebbop

EDIT: To anyone who says that meeting stones encourage a environment or community of laziness as an argument, I say this. By that logic Survival Tents, the new Zeppelins, the new Boats/Ferris, also encourage an environment/community of laziness. However, Most of us can agree that the rested experience gained by tents and these new forms of travel are quite nice, convenient, and most of all, welcomed by the player base en-large.

Firstly, Summoning Stones can enhance convenience and accessibility for players. Not everyone has the luxury of a flexible schedule or ample time to spend in the game. Summoning Stones provide a means for players to quickly join their friends or guildmates in dungeons without having to spend excessive time traveling to the dungeon entrance. This convenience can be particularly beneficial for players with limited playtime or those who have real-life obligations.

Additionally, Summoning Stones can foster a sense of camaraderie and cooperation within the community. They encourage players to work together and coordinate efforts to facilitate dungeon runs. Instead of relying solely on a Warlock to summon players, Summoning Stones distribute the responsibility among the entire group. This shared responsibility can strengthen social bonds and encourage players to actively participate in the process of assembling a group.

While it's true that some players may become complacent and rely solely on Summoning Stones for dungeon access, this behavior is not exclusive to Summoning Stones. It can occur with or without their presence. The issue lies in the mindset of individual players rather than the existence of a specific game mechanic. Encouraging a positive community culture that emphasizes teamwork and active participation can help address such attitudes.

While there are potential downsides associated with Summoning Stones, they can also offer convenience and foster cooperation within the community. With proper moderation and a focus on community-building, Summoning Stones can be a valuable addition to the game without significantly detracting from the overall experience.
