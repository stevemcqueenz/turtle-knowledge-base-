# New SW Harbour

## Post 15136 by Chappinhas3000 — 2022-01-01T20:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15136#p15136 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, I am posting this topic on the Stormwind Harbour addition in 1.16.

In my opinion, it is a great mistake that was committed by the dev. team, for quite some time now they had managed to work within the vanilla framework and thus produced a unique azerothian experience, this is to say, turtle wow is the first time in many a years that I am playing and am being surprised by the small quirky things around the world ( ex: Raven Hill).
By collecting one of the most infamous wotlk assets, the SW Harbour, and adding it as is to the world, I feel they have unexpectedly degraded the capital of the humans for the following reasons:
 - It's size is massive, at least the size of two districts, and I for one, have never ventured further then the pier (towards the lumberyard and the lighthouse)
 -It's location is absurd, the walls face outwards to the back of the cathedral square and it is an obvious addition to the original city.
 -It is not a district, apart from the keep, SW is organized by it's canal in a very evident fashion, the harbour stands out like a sore thumb
 -it is deserted, don't get me wrong, I saw some of the npc's they added, however the pier is far from the bustling centre of movement and transport that would be implied by a capital city harbour, in comparison with spark water it is even shameful.
 - What about the canals, it has no relation to the canals ( where the fishing trainer is btw) and makes both areas loose relatability for it.
 - the map became ugly and half empty
All in all, I think the devs were wrong in grabbing blizzard assets, the harbours dimensions were made to fit the launch of wotlk and nothing more, give a couple good screenshots and sell some more biannual subscriptions.
As the hawks vigil guy says: for too long have we wondered what could've been, what should've been; let us move away from blizzard "improvements" and focus on making an actual immersive world.
The harbour is now in place , but I would suggest it's harm could be attenuated by the implementation of more npc's, stores and movement within it. If possible a size reduction would also be greatly appreciated.
In my opinion the correct way to have innovated the paths would've been to create either a harbour in westfall ( since it has a lighthouse and to have made an in game ( no loading screen) ferry from menethil to southshore. Stormwind harbour is one of the many consequences of a company that no longer cared for it's product.

thank you for reading this rant, it has been bugging me so.
