# Unconventional Tanks

## Post 23435 by Blues — 2022-08-25T18:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23435#p23435 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to see the options of both rogue and shaman tank explored a little more, but they'd need severe balancing in order to be able to tank in PvE without being OP in PvP.

Only small things are needed;

For rogue, they need a reliable taunt and talent points that go into their agitating poison and flourish abilities.  I think they should really double down on the all-or-nothing playstyle of tanking to keep healers on their toes.  Combo points on targets need to be programmed differently too if this is the case, so a rogue can build combo points on multiple targets at once with such talents as the "setup" in subtlety; the one where resisting a spell fully or dodging an attack awards a combo point.

For shaman, they also need a reliable taunt and perhaps a buff similar to that of righteous fury.  Instead of holy damage threat, make nature damage threat.  Increasing stamina by a percentage of intellect could also be a decent tool, however this goes back to the balancing of not making it over-the-top powerful in PvP.

I look forward to see if the developers share the same interests as myself.
