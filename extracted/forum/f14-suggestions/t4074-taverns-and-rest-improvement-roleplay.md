# Taverns and rest improvement. Roleplay

## Post 24538 by Jaswinder — 2022-09-06T20:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24538#p24538 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all.

Since turtle-wow also roleplaying server I think it would be appropriate and atmospheric to make rest value increment when player is in tavern (Like what we have now when we put five tents in a Goldshire). To make the rest in the tavern really fast. Because of tents now taverns are absolutely useless for their intended purpose.

Increased rate of rest will make the taverns of each location more inhabitable, as  players will not need to run to Goldshire.  Not a mountain of people standing on top of each other at a crossroads, which is not very role-playing.
The taverns in each location will be filled with players, which is quite natural and will benefit the role component.
On the other hand, you can reduce the amount of rest that a tent gives, or make it so that the bonus is stacked no more than three times and slightly increase its radius. So the tent will be used for its intended purpose, as a mobile rest point and players will put them in locations between doing quests and the passing player can rest in such a camp. Which will make the tents even more natural and appropriate.

p.s. excuse my English :)

Dear TW team,

I would like to suggest 2 things to make the beds and dining rooms of Azeroth's inns more significant elements as regards to the gameplay but also social aspect of the game.

beds   dead_turtle_head
> Possibility to rent a bed before logging out.
> Rested xp gains would be tremmendously increased (so the people who do not want this can just stay in the inn to get rested xp at a normal rate) if you stay long enough (it could be ex. 3 sleep cycles IRL = 4h**).
> Most of the time, you'd also get a random stat buff for 1 or 2 hours (ex. + stamina or spirit)
> Sometimes, if you get unlucky, you get the "I just had a nightmare..." debuff that fears you at random for a couple seconds and that would stay for like 15' :D
> Importantly, to make sure all inns are visited: if a bed's taken, it's taken, go see another inn! To prevent people just staying in a bed, after the 4 hours proposed above, the bed would free up again and they'd get TPed at the entry of the room when they log back in.
> As regards to the tent-system, I feel tents should not be allowed within cities, and stacking should only be possible in very remote areas and limited to ex. 3 times.

dining rooms   turtle_tongue
> People that sit at tables and buy / eat food that's on it get extra stats for 1 or 2 hours depending on what they chose to eat / drink. It could work by ex. clicking on stuff that's on the table, paying and then staying there for a couple minutes**.
> One can try to stack food and beverages, but at some point it could also trigger an intoxication that would grant like a one hour debuff with ex. less speed, not walking str8, sometimes getting stunned because you start "puking" (cf. TBC mechanics with Arrakoa eggs) or so :D
> Here too, places limited. Wanted to get ready for your raid? Go find another place! The difference though would be that if you log out you automatically lose your chair at the table.

 **If both mechanics would also have auto generated emotes (the orange messages) and animations, it would bring soo much life to these places and turn them into THE places to be before an epic encounter! Pretty sure the already existing visual and audio material would fit in just perfectly!

I'd love to hear your thoughts on this one :D
