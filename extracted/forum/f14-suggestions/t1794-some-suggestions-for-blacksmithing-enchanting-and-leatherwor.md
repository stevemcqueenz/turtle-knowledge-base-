# Some suggestions for Blacksmithing, Enchanting, and leatherworking.

## Post 10737 by Sirgentlemangem — 2021-05-31T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10737#p10737 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was thinking that maybe we could add more crafting recipes to the main three professions. I primarily thought of adding things like more daggers along the leveling path for blacksmithing, having enchanters able to craft staves so the rods that blacksmiths craft have more than one use, and adding bows and crossbows to the things leatherworkers can craft. It's mostly off the top of my head but I think that it could add both variety to the profession choices as well as open up the market a bit.   smiling_turtle_head

Just have the player collect necessary ingredients and turn them in. You can even expand it to include jewel crafting using the same method.

you have to find them yourself on a vanilla database. for example <https://classicdb.ch/>

the spell ID you are looking for you can either google search keywords, search on the database or use an item with the spell ID on it. If you want 2% crit or 2% hit you can use lionheart helm <https://classicdb.ch/?item=12640>
click the green equip link, for example the 2% crit, and the site you get directed to will be <https://classicdb.ch/?spell=7598>, copy the number 7598 from the link and paste it in the item maker.
