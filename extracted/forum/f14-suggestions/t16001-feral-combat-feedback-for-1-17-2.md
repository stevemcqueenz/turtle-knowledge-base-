# Feral Combat - Feedback for 1.17.2

## Post 109012 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-20T17:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109012#p109012 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'll just jump straight in

The first issue we have currently is: BLOAT

 3 New talents were added to Feral Combat in 1.17.2, all 3 of which require in total >>9<< talent points.

Those are:

Open Wounds
Feral Adrenaline
Ancient Brutality.

The problem with this is, the Feral Combat tree, now feels VERY bloated, and we simply just do not have enough free talent points to comfortably take any of these new talents.

But thats just the first issue, the 2nd issue is, the new talents are very lacking, so to speak.

We have open wounds, which is 3 talent points to unlock a different style of play for Feral, as this adds nothing for Poweshifting Shred Druids.

Next is Feral Adrenaline, this one needs a serious look over, as what it provdies for 3 precious talent points is just not enough. 35% dodge ONE TIME, every 30 sec, AFTER being crit - this one needs a serious buff.

Ancient Brutality
 - This one is probably the best out of the new 3 talents, but it could still use a makeover.

Ok, so this brings me on to my 3rd point and what alot of people are worried about -

Feral Combat in Vanilla, has a certain niche, where the talents both improve Cat & Bear form, even in TBC we had the same idea, It wasn't until WOTLK that Blizzard decided to start seperating Cat from Bear, and people hated this.

Basically all 3 new talents are either Cat or Bear in 1.17.2 , in order to address this, I think we need to combine some of the talents in order to reduce the bloat and benefit both feral forms.

Now I will suggest which talents I think should be condensed / combined.

Feral Aggresion: 5 Points

I've always hated this talent, basically you have to spend 5 talent points in order to get 15% Ferocious Bite damage, this just feels bad and a massive talent point dump, the Demo roar part of this underwhelming also, as warriors demo shout always removes this.

I think we should reduce this one to 3 talent points.

Feral Aggresion: 3 Points
Increases the Damage of Ferocious Bite by 5/10/15% and Increases the Effect of Demoralizing Roar by 20/40/60%

Brutal Impact: 2 Points
Now this one is complete garbage, even the best PvP druids on the server dont take this.
 2 Precious talent points for 1 sec stun duration on bash and pounce? - Bash is 4 seconds at max rank , and we have to spend 2 talents points to make it 5 second? Whereas Paladin get a 6 second longer range stun untalented?

Brutal Impact: 1 Point
Increases the duration of Pounce and Bash by 1second, in addition pounce now awards 2 combo points, and increases the bleed damage by 20%

This might actually make this talent be used, and also frees up a point.

Feral Adrenaline & Ancient Brutality combined - 3 Talent points

Whenever you dodge in Cat / Bear form, you gain 15% Vampirism, and gain 5 Rage, or 3 Energy, in addition , whenever you suffer a physical critical strike, you gain 30% reduced damage for the next physical damage done to you. This effect can only occur every 8 seconds.

This gives Cat and Bear form something and combines a useless talent (Feral Adreanline) and frees up some more bloat.

Furor now 3 talent points.

Grants you a 33/66/100% chance when shapeshifting into Cat or Bear form to either gain 40 energy or 10 Rage.
