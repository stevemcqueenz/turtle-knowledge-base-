# Reset/lockout UI

## Post 116984 by tobach — 2025-01-16T14:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116984#p116984 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtle.
My 'simple' suggestion is to implement in-game UI that gives you an overview of timers for raid lockouts, BG of the day, Darkmoon Faire and weekly PvP reset, Daily BG, and maybe even give you some type of overview of each (which raids where you're currently locked on a raid-id, how much honor you have earned in PvP etc.)
I think it could be a massive boost to user-friendliness, instead of having to check third parties for some of this information, and also avoid a lot of headaches with figuring this out.. especially as a newer player.
