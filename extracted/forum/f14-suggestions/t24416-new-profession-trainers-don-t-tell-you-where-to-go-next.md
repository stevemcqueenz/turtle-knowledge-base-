# New profession trainers don't tell you where to go next

## Post 171778 by Snoodydood (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-22T14:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171778#p171778 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I'm really loving the new survival overhaul but one annoying element was just finding where to train after journeyman (150 skill) in the world.
In vanilla, the profession trainers will actually tell you where to go next to further train your professions if you take the time to actually read the text when you talk to them once you've leveled your skill enough. My suggestion is that you implement this to new profession trainers in the game just to match vanilla.
