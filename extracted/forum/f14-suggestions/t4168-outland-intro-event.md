# Outland intro event

## Post 24927 by Robinanderson — 2022-09-12T13:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24927#p24927 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have a idea for a introduction Event for the patch that will add Outland and Quel'thalas that focuses around a Fel orc invasion of Azeroth. The goal of the attacking orcs is to conquer Azeroth and use it as a second front against Illidan

Backstory

Hoping to open a second front against Illidan the Fel orcs have launched an all out invasion of Azeroth with the goal of securing the world for the Legion

The invasion is being overseen by a vile Blade master known as General karel'Gash who directs all military activity from his seat of power at the Dark Portal

The orcs have placed Nethergarde Keep under siege while Fel Warlocks assault the capitals of Azeroth with Internals

In response to this threat the Argent Dawn have established the Argent Front along the border with the Blasted lands and are desperately fighting to drive the invading Fel orcs back through the dark portal

The event is comprised of 3 stages

Stage 1 infernal Storm

The capitals of Azeroth have come under siege from demonic Infernals

Before aid can be sent to the Argent Dawn in the Blasted lands the homelands must be secured

Players during this phase are sent to help fend off the Demonic attacks against their faction capitals as well as slay the warlocks summoning the infernals

Alliance players are sent to defend the following cities

Stormwind
Ironforge
Theramore isle
Darnassus

Horde players are sent to defend the following cities

Orgrimmar
Thunder Bluff
The Undercity

The warlocks responsible for the infernal attacks each have a camp close to each city where they can be found

Players will have to Storm each camp and slay the Warlocks to end the quests

Second phase: Counterstrike

With the homelands secured players are sent to begin aiding the Argent Dawn in their efforts against the orcish forces occupying the Blasted lands

The first few tasks involve setting up supply lines to the Argent Front as well as helping the Argent defenders secure the outskirts of the Encampment

Players are then sent to lift the siege of Nethergarde Keep by Slaying the Orcs and Destroying their catapults

With the siege lifted players of both factions join forces with the Nethergarde defenders who will then launch a Counterstrike against the major Fel orc fortifications in the area

Something cool about this phase is that Horde players will be able to enter Nethergarde Keep and use it's services during the phase. Horde players can speak with the alliance npcs and use the venders as well as flight master but this only lasts until the end of the second phase

The citizens of Nethergarde task players with several tasks aimed at the occupying Demonic Forces that include disrupting supply lines between the dark portal and Fel orc war camps, dealing with the orc's ogre allies and burning their supply crates

Archmage Thas'ranan himself orders a massive Counterstrike where players assault the Fel orc war camps that were established across the Blasted lands. There are a total of 5

Once the war camps are dealt with and players return to Archmage Thas'ranan the second phase ends

Third and final phase: Battle of the dark portal

The Argent Dawn and Nethergarde keep have joined forces and have launched an combined Attack upon the fortified Dark portal

Players are tasked with fighting to the base of the portal itself and eliminating the Fel orc leadership including General karel'Gash

Once the battle ends so does the event after which the portal is held by a combined force of Argent and Nethergarde defenders and can be used to travel to and from outland
