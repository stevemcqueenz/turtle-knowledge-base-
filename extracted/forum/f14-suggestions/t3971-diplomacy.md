## Post 24046 by Feyfolken — 2022-08-31T04:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24046#p24046 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a Horde I would love to visit Alliance cities as a diplomat too!

Imagine, two new reputations with their own rewards: Horde diplomats and Alliance diplomats.
Each quests done for the opposite faction makes you grind your diplomat reputation, but not the opposit race reputations since it would be game breaking.

To begin with, you would have to meet a Horde or Alliance diplomat team in Orgrimmar or in Stormwind to start a quest (available once a month) in order to get a Horde or Alliance disguise/illusion, which would be a consummable item.
This item is here not to break the game with Stormwind full of Orc, Undead etc... And lorewise, would be used by diplomats not to offense the most radical people of the opposit faction.

Once you use the disguise, the item desappear from your bag and you turn into the corresponding race of the opposit faction (same as the Orb of Deception).

 **A few restrictions must apply though:**
 -The disguise must have a limited duration (a few days up to a week).
 -You can no longer do any pvp activities while the disguise is on + an hour after it vanished in order to avoid any pvp issues(especially in capital cities).
 - If you enter your capital cities with the illusion on, it will vanish (in order to avoid seeing Human, dwarves and so on roaming in Orgrimmar).
 -You can not do the opposit faction PvP quests, Dungeons quests and quests involving you to kill member of your own faction.

The rewards related to those reputations would be purely cosmetic such as a tabard, pet, mount, and a title for example.
The requirement for the Loremaster title would be expanded and it would put more weight on it.
And last but not least! More quests>more content>more friends!
