# Small Guild's Last Breath - Removing Scaling

## Post 70031 by Naht — 2023-10-10T19:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70031#p70031 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’ve never posted on this forum..but when it comes to this topic I am very personally invested. As a casual raider from a small guild, the official-unofficial post in the discords #support channel about reducing raid scaling from 20 to 30 (and eventually 40) may be concerning for some larger guilds, while it is devastating for all of the smaller social guilds.

What is the spirit of turtle wow? I’m under the impression the spirit of turtle wow is taking it slow, enjoying the journey, and sharing those experiences with friends.

If that’s the case, how does reducing scaling actually benefit the spirit of twow? Requiring more people to be able to raid *reduces* accessibility. If raids going forward are only accessible to large guilds who can accommodate 30-40 man rosters, it is not benefiting the spirit of the adventure, it’s putting pressure and emphasis on the preparation, recruitment, and leadership of the adventure.

As someone from classic, I’m very familiar with the 40 man raid culture. This culture is not more social than a smaller group, the raid environment is not more social, as typically more people in the raid means less people talking/communicating.

What this change looks like to larger guilds: we will be fine, we will absorb a smaller guild or two, and consolidate. Not much has changed, as we typically fill a 40 man roster anyways.

What this change looks like to smaller guilds: our tight knit community will be shattered. We will either be merged/absorbed into a larger guild for a different raiding experience, or we will not raid. Alternatively the guild disbands and members find new homes individually, completely losing the community they (we) built.

What this looks like to the server: smaller casual guilds can no longer raid, and larger 40 man guilds are less likely to be social/casual. The argument “just recruit” isn’t valid when a large percentage of the population is not on your time zone or doesn’t speak your language. Also not to mention “just recruit” can’t work for *all* the small guilds, it's a numbers game.

Overall, I feel like this change is hurting the people who have passion for your server and your custom changes, and it’s “benefitting” (simply by not hurting) the guilds who don’t need the change.

Having the option to put together a group of (30-)40 has always been there. I strongly feel that making it mandatory is a tone deaf decision that will hurt community and server health.

As someone who found a home on turtle wow, I am devastated by the change and expect to see many smaller communities like ours, crumble because of it.
