# Remove Roleplay

## Post 54528 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-24T23:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54528#p54528 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Roleplay is dead.  PvE and PvP are "where it's at".

The roleplaying sub-forums are no longer active and the latest posts there have nothing to do with roleplaying.  No one on the server seems interested in pick-up-and-play sessions.  Any roleplaying is done with pre-determined groups, or people just use the officially sanctioned AddOn with no intention of roleplaying or to make "troll profiles".  So, if the general public aren't engaging and the die-hards have moved on to self-made communities, there's no point in pretending that the server encourages roleplay, anymore.  The time has passed and the community has lost interest.

Please, **change the realm type to "PvE"** and please, **close the roleplaying sub-forums**.

Thank you.

Roleplay isn’t dead.  There are still roleplayers on the server.  The forums aren’t really used by roleplayers as much as Discord is.  If you are looking for roleplay, it’s advised that you check the Turtle WoW Discord roleplay channels.  You should also download the TurtleRP addon, create a profile, look for people with profiles, and be proactive and outgoing.  Joining a roleplaying guild also helps, and you can find a good list on the roleplay forum or in Discord.
As far as closing the roleplay forum, that would be fine but I don’t see the big harm in leaving it open.
Personally, roleplay is the number one reason I play on this server.  While the roleplay community has not grown as quickly as the rest of the community, it is still very much a part of this server.
