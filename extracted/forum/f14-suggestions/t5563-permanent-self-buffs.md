# Permanent Self Buffs

## Post 34297 by Maxx251 — 2023-02-08T04:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34297#p34297 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One thing that has always bothered me about WoW over all is the unnecessary need to have to rebuff after x amount of time. Functionally, there is no reason why any mage would want their magic armor or arcane intellect to wear off. A paladin shouldn't have to constantly re-apply blessings. A Shaman's weapon enchants should stay as-well. There is no in-game benefit or balance reason as to why a buff that you can cast on yourself from your spell list should ever end. For buffing others,Those should all be 30 minute buffs across the board. A player getting to have thorns for 30 minutes compared to 10 isn't going to destroy azeroth. For some reason a lot of people don't share this opinion (to what I've seen/experienced) and I just don't see why. If you think self-cast buffs should have a timer then that means hunters should have to re-enter their aspects every 30 minutes, and a paladin should have to put their aura on again. It's honestly really just a technical oversight in my opinion. If something like this is at all possible that'd be amazing if it could be implemented.

I rather think that inconvenience is the point. I mean, sure buffs being short duration and/or costing your entire base mana bar is cool "vanilla wow identity" and all.

But at the same time, people should be aware that realistically this just means your average casual player will straight up just not use their buffs. You can have 4 priests in a 40-man raid, and not one of them will rebuff stamina to individual people that died because it costs too much mana for them to care (if they even manage to identify who is missing the buffs) when they want to "play the game" themselves and put Shadow Word: Pain on the boss instead. It just devolves into "someone else will do it, idc".

The average player on the server is very very casual in terms of utilizing their class' strengths. Yeah you may get lucky and have a priest main in the raid that knows how to efficiently play their class (Inner Focus + Prayer of Fortitude to make a buff free instead of costing 4000 mana).
But most of the time you won't have that luck;
Paladin buffs before 60 basically only exist at the start of a dungeon - after that the paladin forgets they exist and "costs too much mana to rebuff 5 people".
Melee Shamans will straight up not notice they had Windfury **TOTEM** on their weapon, instead of their actual Windfury **Weapon** until half-way into the raid ("/p haha oopsie guys I didnt even see I dont have my windfury buff on LOL").

Problem with the thread's suggestion is that this exact issue was addressed and solved incrementally by **future expansions**, which causes a lot of vanilla players to just instantly decline it because "casual retail change, no ty".

My stance on this is, if it's such a design flaw that it promotes players to straight up not use their buffs, then it should be addressed. It doesn't have to go full retail instantly where every buff is free and permanent. Only adjust one aspect at a time, like mana or duration, or I don't know, add a strong dedicated Talent to respective talent trees if people want to desperately hold onto "Buffing should be difficult" ideal - for example give Arcane Mages a 2/2 Talent that reduces the cost of passive utility spells by 70% or something (Arcane Intellect, Mage Armor, Amplify Magic, Conjure Drinks etc.)
