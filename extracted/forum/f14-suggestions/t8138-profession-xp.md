So what? If someone is sitting on enough gold to pay for leveling an alt with AH mats, it would redistribute a huge amount of gold to the community which would combat price inflation and be generally good for the health of the economy. And like I said it would only be a small amount of XP. Not nearly enough to level a full alt. Say if you leveled a profession fully on a level 1 alt you would maybe get enough XP to reach lvl 15~25 maximum, and in practice leveling up professions is throttled by level requirement so it wouldn't even work that way.

Leveling professions as Ibux mentioned is a time intensive task and it should be rewarded by more than professions that are barely in demand at times. Especially when you take the time to level a profession on a hardcore character you aren't even sure to make it to max level, that XP reward is warranted, imo.

Also for the record I have slow and steady + warmode, 14h /played  and I am only lvl 15. I have been leveling tailoring and enchanting on the side and getting nothing for it but a few temporary green items and a decent wand + enchants and bags no one cares about at my level. There is nothing OP about gaining a small amount of XP from professions, if anything it makes the alternative playstyles more rewarding which imo is what Turtle WoW is all about. Fostering a vibrant community that enjoys the game for the game rather than always taking the same path to reach max level. Hell some people don't even bother with professions until max level because they consider it a waste of time and they would be right. It is not optimum for leveling, and it's not a slight XP boost that will change that.

Currently, very few quests require stuff from professions.
For example Warsong supplies requires Deadly Blunderbuss.
This gun is good moneymaking recipe for low levels.

Quests doesn't have to require specific profession because people could trade with each other.
It would be cool to see "WTT Elixir of Wisdom for Hillman's Belt" because some NPC in Hillsbrad asks everyone to bring a belt for him. Alchemists can't make belts, but can make potions. Leatherworkers can't make potions, but can make belts.

Other way is quest requiring items from different crafting professions simultaneously. For example Hillsman's Leather Gloves and Green Iron Bracers.

I think every white gear vendor could feature a quest "bring me X leather/metal/cloth" for him. They need some materials to make items they selling.

Those quests will boost low level trading between players, provide more moneymaking recipes for professions and fill the world with meaning behind those vendors.
