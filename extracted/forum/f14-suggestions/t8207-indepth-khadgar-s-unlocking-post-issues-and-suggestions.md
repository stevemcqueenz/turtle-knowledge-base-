# Indepth Khadgar's Unlocking post, issues and suggestions

## Post 52702 by Nickgreyden — 2023-07-09T14:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52702#p52702 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Before we can talk about issues with this thematic ability and how to solve them, we first have to understand the ability itself, how and when it is gained, and the cost of time/money to gain it.*

Lvl 18
Khadgar’s Unlocking (Rank 1)
Lockpicking skill 15
Cost of Spell 0.50g
Cost of Reagent 6s

Lvl 30
Khadgar’s Unlocking (Rank 2)
Lockpicking skill 75
Cost of Spell 1.50g
Cost of Reagent 8s

Lvl 42
Khadgar’s Unlocking (Rank 3)
Lockpicking skill 135
Cost of Quest Item 3.00g
Cost of Spell 3.00g
Cost of Reagent 10s

Lvl 54
Khadgar’s Unlocking (Rank 4)
Lockpicking skill 195
Cost of Quest Item 5.00g
Cost of Spell 8.00g
Cost of Reagent 12s

This allows mages to unlock several items, the most common of which are:

KU1 allows you to unlock:
Ornate Bronze Lockbox dropped by mobs level 24-27

KU2 allow you to unlock
Heavy Bronze Lockbox dropped by mobs level 27-30
Iron Lockbox dropped by mobs level 30-36
Large Iron Bound Chest found in instances mid 20’s - mid 40’s: Stockade to Uldaman

KU3 allows you unlock
Strong Iron Lockbox dropped by mobs level 35-40

KU4 allows you unlock
Steel Lockbox dropped by mobs level 41-45
Large Mithril Bound Chest in Uldaman Only (untested and possibly inaccurate)

This leaves Reinforced Steel, Mithril Lockboxes, Thorium Lockboxes, Eternium Lockboxes, and all 45+ locked dungeon chests as well as doors, gates,, etc outside the range of a mage’s ability to access via KU.

Now we know what it does, when it does it, and the cost of the ability. Now we can look at the issues of this ability.

- While it does not require practice to level like the rogue, it is rather expensive to learn the spell in the first place, decidedly so for ranks 3 and 4.
- It does not max out at 300. While I have no issue with this being the case, the cutoff period of usefulness is dreadfully early on in leveling.
- It requires relatively expensive reagents for use.
- The greatest value in relation to cost and active levels when it becomes available is rank 2. By the time of learning ranks 3 and 4 the cost is considerable and the situations in which it would become useful have passed. In short, the mage will have out leveled the usefulness of the spell.
So how to combat these issues? Well here are my suggestions.

1. I believe it is important to invest money not time into something rogues will invest time not money into. That said, the books are expensive for their level. I'm ok with that. But the added expense of rank 3 and 4 in the quest aspect are just utterly insane. If turtle wow wishes to make this a quest, make it a true quest. Have something being done or collected or opened. Most other [mage specific quests](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quests/classes/mage#0-5+3+12) are themed very well. This should fall into the same vein. Collect a blown up library lock from Dalrean or wrest a magical key from an insane murloc named Gottem. Hell it could be a three partner called "Ruler of the Key'' with a triumphet ending called "Hey, the Mage is back!"
2. To address the level in which this ability is gained vs its usefulness at the time, I would rework it to be gained first at level 14 and then once every 10 levels after: 24, 34, and 44.
3. To address the cost of the reagents, I suggest letting a blacksmith and/or enchanter make the keys which should be changed to copper, bronze, silver, and gold respectively. Maybe even have the blacksmith forge the simple keys but an enchanter would have to add their mats to make them the usable enchanted keys. While I would prefer no reagents (I am biased after all) I have to point back to my statement of money not time as opposed to time not money. Of course, you always run the risk of not enough dealers for demand so could easily become a double edged sword. Still, i feel it is a good way to get others in on the action.
4. This one is a long one and would be time consuming for the devs given my understanding of how the game works but hang with me for a moment. Raise the LP level of KU3 to 150 and KU4 to 225. While there isn't really an issue with raising rank 3, the issue with increasing rank 4 is there are many locks tailored to 225 lock picking across Azoroth. The good news is the only overworld or dungeon door that mages would have additional access to is the gate from Loch Modan to Searing Gorge. I don't see this as a real problem especially given the current or even proposed level of rank 4. The bigger concern is it appears to allow access to opening several more lockboxes. Reinforced Steel Lockboxes should keep their 225 level. This allows access to late game (ST, ZF, Ulda)  but not end game lockboxes. The three lockboxes above Reinforced Steel should probably have their skill raised above this level (even as much as I want to be able to open my own Mithril Lockboxes). Making this change also allows another way to open locked chests inside dungeons which is just good bonus loot for groups. Given the loot found inside footlockers and the fact KU requires mana, reagents, and 10s to use, I doubt it would create much of an issue on that front for rogues leveling their skill.
The above changes solves all majors issues, as I see it, with a rather cool and unique ability of mages while still allowing rogues to be masters of their art. I welcome any feedback.

I thought a while before deciding to answer this.

I, personally, feel that saying this is an attempt to, what I assume to be, depreciate the most exclusive rogue utility unfounded. The ability already exists in game and even if every change I have asked for is implemented, the rogue ability will still surpass it in every way except for time spent to achieve such a level. So, if anything, it should be a cagey backup plan.

Secondly, KU, like much of the utility you seem so salty about, costs money in reagents. And that utility is expected free of charge in any group you are in. Doing an instance, that's money gone. Portal at end of instance, money gone. Explain to me who exactly, other than the rogue themself, gets upset if a rogue's dagger isn't poisoned or they lack vanishing powder?

Lastly, if you look, I specifically state mages should not be able to open up Mithril Lockboxes or do things like pick the locks in BRD. So all those lovely people that farm lashers or Crocs or raid instances will still be handing over cash to rogues... the only people who have the ability to pick those locks, and they can do it faster and cheaper than a mage could if they even had that ability. And other than opening locked chests found in endgame instances, they cannot help gain access to doors to and within those instances.

Just because someone else can use a dagger or stealth or make use of lock picking does not cheapen the rogue class because they can use it better and do it better. You could argue that the ability shouldn't exist. Hell, I might agree with you! But currently, the ability does exists, just like warlocks casting spells, hunters using Mana, and pallies and druids doing everything. No reason to tear down another class to lift one up.
