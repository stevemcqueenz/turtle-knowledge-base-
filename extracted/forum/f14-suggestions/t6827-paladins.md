Hello. As it stands right now, I think paladins can use a few touch ups to improve the gameplay:

Crusader strike feels absolutely dreadful to use. It feels like a copycat of sunder armor that doesn't seem to fit the paladin role, in my opinion. A single casted spell debuff or one big debilitating strike on a CD that either A: Gives the full holy damage stack bonus on the spot
Or
B: Gives 1 stack initially, but is increased to the same 5 stacks and is refreshed with every attack, whether it is from you or from your allies.

Holy strike bears no distinction to Heroic strike, and I think it could be improved with something in the ball park of what I am about to suggest:

Make Holy strike instant cast, INCREASE the CD ,  REDUCE the damage done by a certain percentage, and in exchange for the nerf in damage, have Holy strike do different things based on what seal you have cast on yourself. For example
Seal of Righteousness HS - Flat out holy damage, like the original spell
Seal of Justice HS - a slow that tapers off overtime, for example 20% slow -> 10 -> 5 -> 0. The ticks could last for 2 seconds or so for a total of 8 seconds.
Seal of command HS - inspires  your allies and increases their haste by between 5-20% for 10 seconds
Seal of Wisdom HS - stops your targets mana regeneration for 5 seconds , on critical strike, grants mana to allies (not yourself) for a % of the damage dealt.

Thoughts? Concerns? Tell me what you think

What the Paladin generally lacks after I've played one is an active ability, something that isn't reliant on Seals or releases and does moderate damage and doesn't have a long cooldown. Probably something on like a 5 second cooldown that works similar to Heroic Strike on Warrior. Simple and straightforward, except Warrior abilities require Rage, but in the Paladin's case, the ability can be Mana-based.

The thing I didn't enjoy in the Paladin is whether it's pure Vanilla or Turtle WoW, it still feels like everything you do is passive. You don't have any real active damaging abilities that aren't somehow reliant on some other factors. Like that ranged ability that only works on Undead or Demons for example, it's nice for roleplay reasons, but in practice it feels pretty stupid because of its limited applicability.
