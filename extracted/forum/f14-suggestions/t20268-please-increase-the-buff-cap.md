# Please increase the buff cap

## Post 138875 by Mobbey — 2025-07-12T21:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138875#p138875 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
Whatever people think of the many class changes you made, one thing I think we can all agree on is that you have added an absurd amount of extra buffs and auras that all add up on top of each other.

This is a huge problem for pretty much all the tank classes in endgame raiding, even when blocking unwanted buffs we all end up buffcapped at some point ; especially when many of those buffs are auras that we cannot click off and we are just stuck with.
And it's especially bad when you design things like priest's proclaim champion, why does this give me 2 buffs? Why not regroup those into a single buff ? Why is the druid's spellhit buff an aura I can't remove ? 70% of the raid doesn't need or care for it but we all have it whether we like it or not. These are just 2 examples but there are plenty more and it is honestly poor design.

Take your beloved prot paladin for example. Do you even realise how many consumes these guys have to use ? They are basically using both tank and caster consumes at the same time, that's like 10 or so buffs, just from consumes alone. Fury prot warrs? They don't have to deal with caster consumes, but look at how many self buffs they have. 1 or 2 crusader proc, battle shout, bloodrage, enrage, blood craze, the useless 5% speed from bloodthirst, last stand, lifegiving gem, shield wall/recklessness. And what ends up happening is you getting extra buffs from healers midfight like inspiration or whatever, and then when you try to hit an important cooldown at a crucial moment nothing happens because you're capped.

So please, increase the buff limit, it has become a real headache at this point.
And if you could also clean up some of those previously mentioned abilities, and the other ones I'm not mentioning, it would be appreciated as well.
Thanks for reading
