# I Played Sunnyglade... My Thoughts / Suggestions.

## Post 53893 by Jammyxx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-18T07:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53893#p53893 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So we played a few SGV BG's on Sunday, was quite refreshing to play something different the BG has potential but has a few flaws.

 1:
No one really knows what they're doing.
The BG seems very convoluted and I think there should be some kinda guide or post on how the devs intended you to win as its not quite as self explanatory as WSG capture the flag or AB's hold nodes.

 2:
The bosses spawn when 100 total sparks have been turned in amongst both teams, the more you turn in the less HP the enemies boss spawns with (1% per spark)  after this you can't turn anymore in, so all the other objectives are pointless to carry on doing. I think if we could continue handing sparks in and doing more damage to the boss this would be better.

 3:
The boss spawns at the enemy graveyard... You can see the problem with this.... If 10 people (less if game isn't full) are trying to kill a mob with 100k>990k hp (based on turned in sparks)  at the enemies GY they will quickly be overwhelmed.... Therefore the only feasible solution is to kite him back to your own graveyard.
A solution to this would be to move where the bosses spawn OR make more graveyards and make them cappable like AB / AV.

 4:
Pointless NPC's scattered throughout the BG with 12k HP.
Atm these are just for fancy and add no purpose to the BG... You can accidently pull them and are just damn right annoying...
Suggestion for this is either make them untargetable like the ones in AB that spawn when you cap a base OR you could talk to them when the bosses spawn and if you defeat the orc/human that they're fighting they join you at the final boss to do more damage / be cannonfodder.

 5:
Towers, are kinda useless, they award passive sparks but at a slower rate... Suggestion is either increase the rate of sparks awarded or make holding a tower give either 10% dmg or 10% hp buff to the entire raid.

 6:
General improvements
The Zone has no grass and looks abit weird... Consider adding more frill density I think it's called and it would look less jarring.

Before the game stats everyone is just stuck ontop of each other, this looks a little odd... I suggest making the start point inside a building with a gate or fence around the horde/alliance camps.

The models for the alliance Footmen look like a beta/alpha model...

The boss randomly transforms into a Night Elf for some reason both Horde and Alliance, this isn't explained and no animation or /yell as to why.

The music is nice but it sounds like it's been ripped outta Warcraft 2 and doesn't really fit.

 7:
Honor awarded is half of the other BG;s
We got 700 honor for a 45 minute game...
This needs atleast doubling or tripling.

Also consider adding a bonus day for the BG and award marks of honor.

Make a seperate faction than the one used for Black Morass dungeon and make PvP rewards for it, EG an Epic belt or amulet, that WSG/AB/AV don't offer.

That's it for now
