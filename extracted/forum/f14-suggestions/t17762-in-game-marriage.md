Other MMORPGs can let you get married to other players, so why not Turtle WoW?
Benefits to getting married would mostly be cosmetic, but also maybe you could have a few free minor abilities? Like a free teleport to your partner once every 2 hours? And possibly having a small boost to EXP gain when near your partner? (free Rested EXP effect)
There could be a small questline that could be done that requires a ring from a Jewelcrafting recipe (or recipeS?) to get started with the quest.
Also, GAY MARRIAGE PLEASE? (even if it requires an extra set of quests?)

The questline could be like:
1. Get a ring from a Jewel Crafter and give it to an NPC in a major city. The NPC will ask you to name a player for proposing to, the other player MUST do the same within 1 minute in order to advance to the next quest, otherwise the quest fails.
2. You must do 2 weeks worth of specific daily quests with your proposed partner. (the quests are all simple enough and based on your level)
3. Once the 2 weeks of daily quests are up you can then shell out gold for the wedding ceremony at a specific NPC to get a wedding hall. Choice of 5 wedding types:
 - Steamwheedle Package (quick and cheap at Ratchet)
 - Cenarian Circle Package (Tauren/Night Elf style minimalist and in an outdoor gathering at Moonglade, not too expensive)
 - Earthen Ring Package (Wildhammer Dwarf/Troll style in the Hinterlands; a mix of the traditional & minimalist outdoors, average price)
 - Argent Dawn Package (Human/Forsaken style in Stormwind/the Ruins of Lordaeron; elegant, traditional, and pricey)
 - The Silvermoon Deluxe Package (High Elf/Blood Elf style in Eversong Wastes; this is THE package to get to flaunt your worth in gold!)
4. A quest will be given by the marriage officiant only while wedding attire is worn, once both players complete the quest and use an item on each other to give one another a buff they will be married.
Once players are both married, they will receive a buff that will last for 1 week that grants a bonus to stats outisde of dungeons, raids, and battlegrounds; while folks that attend the wedding will receive a minor buff that lasts for a couple hours. (akin to the Onyxia/Nefarian head buff)

Sorry if some stuff needs expanding upon or clarification, I'm pulling an all-nighter and am tired as heck.
