# Mage and Rogue available for all?

## Post 28938 by Boj — 2022-11-10T23:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28938#p28938 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was staring at the character creation screen today, pondering how taurens have so little choice in class compared to every other race. I know most (myself included), arent fans of tauren paladins or priests, especially without their own unique spell flavours. I always thought what they shouldve done is made it similar to how classes mirror each-other in SWTOR. Anyways, dragonflight saw the lifting of race restrictions on mage and rogue, meaning every race in retail wow can play rogue and mage. I think this is extremely important in a fantasy RPG for the basic 3 roles of RPG’s to be avaliable (that being the warrior,rogue and mage). After all, is the taurens learning the arcane arts that farfetched considering all of their comrades know them? The same applies to the art of stealth and ill admit the hooves still bother me, but cmon, what other classes are we gonna give to tauren realisticly?

Personally I think all non magic-focused classes should be available to all races. I know hunters use mana but I’d also lump that in with rogue and warrior.

Also keep in mind that expansions represent the passage of time in the world. It would make sense that after a while races would learn from each other and mix classes. This server will stay vanilla though, so it’s up to the devs to decide if such a thing can occur and to what extent.

Good news is we already have some new race/class combinations. So who knows!
