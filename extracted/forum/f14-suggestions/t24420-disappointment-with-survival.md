Hello, IMHO survival need solid rework once again but with LOGIC applied...
First of all I really like some ideas (wood cutting, compass, boat, fishing pole, tents...)
Now what I don't like:
 1. Weapons: Machete requires: Whittle, ANVIL and BLACKSMITH HAMMER, while Hatchet don't require even Whittle, but still you make it of same reagents (copper bars?!): logic = 0.
+ when you use blacksmith hammer and anvil you are NOT SURVIVALIST anymore.
 2. Reagents: you can cut trees to gather wood, but you can't always have mining/smelting as survivalist. Secondary profession as survival should be self-sufficient or use mostly items from mobs (like cloths, meat or so). (BTW I wonder if leaves can be gather through herbalism? Didn't check yet.) Also so many copper bars in this profession destroys economy and make profession more dependent of mining.
 3. New reagents: You can add FAT dropped from beasts same like cloths are dropped of humanoids. Can be used to make balms or preserve some things.
Cutted tree should drop resin to make some sticky things or even this first torch. Now you simple buy and skill (100% pleople skill same way to 15 = no point). Dependent on vendor is not survival.
You can add coal vein for miners (like you added gemstone), and survival could make coal out of wood just need campfire or so, rope should be crafted too.
Vendor could have limited quantity of wood or coal like some of them have herbs.
You can add Pile of mud near water, just use shovel to gather clay and stones like those from mining.
There are milion things to add or change wtih logic, but please, not anvil and hammer for apprentice survivalist...
 4. Trainers: Located just outside of cities = bad lore. They should be located in first location just to start skill and buy axe, then in the middle of the forest appropriate to the level / skill. Just give them tent, campfire maybe some fence/pallisade to be easier to find.
I would be really happy if I could help to plan this proffesion so turtle can be proud of it, now it's bugged, clogged with no sense recipes and not really usefull (unless you have craftsman chellenge but, hey - this is not survival chellenge).
