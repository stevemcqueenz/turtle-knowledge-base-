# Original idea of decay

## Post 153493 by Roylda — 2025-09-27T21:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153493#p153493 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I had a flashback (and i don't know if it is a real memory or not) that Blizzard devs admitted that their original idea for the implementation of Death Knights in TLK was totally different from the "heroic class" "starting level 55 if you have a leveled up character already" etc. I know a lot of vanilla wow fans are really against the idea of new classes and all, but since the philosophy of turtle is to create custom content according to what could have been the original ideas of the devs and artists behind the Warcraft universe, please hear me out !    satisfied_turtle

So, the original idea behind Death Knights : DKs were not meant to be a real separate "class" at first, but an irreversible corruption of your character through a quest line or an instanced scenario. For example, this quest would be available at level 55, you would be able to take it and achieve it but at the end of it you would have had to choose between saving yourself and sacrificing yourself in order to be raised back by the lich king... And with that second option, your character's class would be altered. A little like what was done in Hearthstone's Knights of the Frosen Throne expansion pack.

![Image](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/images/Knights_of_the_Frozen_Throne_death_knights.jpg?bd8de3)

This idea would mean a loooooooooot of work in terms of game design, because it'd require to create at least 2 talent trees for a DKed version of each class OR it would mean to just make this feature available to selected classes only (just like Oathbreakers in BG3). I would also mean a lot of balancing to make the new talent trees interesting and not as broken as DK were when they first came out... But ! Since the community is also very active in Turtle, it could also be a collaborative community work ?

For example : a Hunter being turned into a DK hunter would have a "Beast Maker" talent tree and a "Dark Ranger" talent tree. Paladins would have a talent tree like the Unholy DK in TLK + a second one about Shadow magic. Druids would have something about pests spreading and nerubian themed transformations, etc.

Even tho vanilla wow is not TLK, and the lich king's narrative arc is not present, it could be interesting to reshape the Ebony Blade origin story through the vanilla version of Naxxramas for example.

Anyway ! I know this kind of idea has no chance to see the light of day (here or anywhere), but i was glad to share my "decayed" fantasies with you who might read that. Thanks for your time, fellow adventurer !
