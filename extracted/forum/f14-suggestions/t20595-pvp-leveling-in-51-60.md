# PvP leveling in 51-60

## Post 142418 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-31T22:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142418#p142418 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Suggestion

- Honorable Kills in Alterac Valley should grant experience to players below level 60.
Reasoning

Alterac Valley was unique battleground in the old WoW because it allowed PvP oriented levelers to finish their journey to level 60 by playing this battleground. Source of experience came from killing mobs in the Mines, enemy NPCs or by turning in repeatable quests. This is still a thing on Turtle (repeatable quests no longer award experience though) but the experience gained via this way is miserable at least compared to leveling in open world and dungeons.

I know PvPers who don't raid at all and their entire gear comes from PvP rank progression or battlegrounds reputation. So apparently they level up in zones doing quests and killing mobs which is entirely PvE content. And they end up doing only PvP once they hit level 60 - open world farming, dungeons and raids aren't relevant for them after they hit 60 because they only find fun in fighting against other players.

I know leveling has always been Turtle's principle but it's worth to notice one thing: leveling is the part of PvE journey. I understand forcing people to level up first before they gain access to raids or even dungeons - because leveling same as dungeons or raids is PvE content. However, it doesn't make sense for PvP audience. Those who come to this server to only play PvP in the endgame are literally forced to level up their characters to level 60 doing ONLY PvE content. Thus, I think there is no reason to force this specific playerbase to play the playstyle they not necessarily enjoy. I'm not asking for extending my suggestion to whole 1-60 level range but only to shorten the pain of some people - only for 51-60 level range.

Why Alterac Valley?

First, this battleground was originally designed by Blizzard to be alternative way of reaching level 60 via PvP. And there are some traces to think so. If you look at some boring repeatable quests there like taming low level Wolves/Rams or to deliver supplies from the Mines to the Quartermaster, you can clearly see they weren't designed to be completed by experienced players. If you were level 51 Warrior, you couldn't do much by fighting enemy players. But if you did these quests, in the long-term you could enhance your team with the Cavalry or Infantry that will wipe out enemies. Or you could farm Armor Scraps in the Mines to enhance your faction's NPCs so more experienced PvPers from your team could have easier way defending the base from enemy. And note these quests originally awarded experience.

Second, Alterac Valley was the only PvP content in the game that could give you both experience and good money. Herbs? Veins? No problem, they are in every corner of Alterac Valley. Raw gold? Also not a problem - every enemy player could be looted. I don't know how much gold is it for leveling character but on my level 60 character, each kill is worth like 30 silver in trophy you turn in. Every NPC drops the loot like in open world. Meanwhile, every other PvP activity during leveling has struggled with getting enough gold to - for example - cover learning skills costs.

Finally, if you are worried that it will lower the quality of PvP games for level 60 players - worry not. The fact is nobody considers this battleground a competetive one. Comparing it to Arathi Basin or Warsong Gulch is misunderstanding. It has always been casual by design. It's even more casual here because Turtle WoW restored the old version of this BG. Also, it's the only battleground you can't join with premade. Literally all games aren't well coordinated and I'm sure Alliance knows what I'm talking about. It's impossible to coordinate 40 man PUG with completely random people. This battleground is the definition of casual PvP - and that's the point. There will be no better place for casual PvP other than Alterac Valley. In comparison, in AB or WSG you usually face opponents with at least BWL gear level.
