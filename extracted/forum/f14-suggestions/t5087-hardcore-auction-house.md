It takes away the whole multiplayer aspect (barring single instance runs). The way Turtle has it (with the R2R ruleset) is best. It promotes multiplayer to the max. U seek others actively. I like that aspect a lot. Yes, it is easier. I am not claiming HC is hard or should be very hard for that matter. Having 1k+ immortals proves it is easy. Not the point. The point is that it is fun! (We can add a layer of difficulty by allowing lvl 60 HC mode and attracting ppl to end game HC. But this is a whole other discussion.)

Yes, there will be minmaxers, who place a toon at every 5 lvls to abuse the ruleset. (Just like there are multiboxers, who insist they enjoy playing turtle with characters following each other and then wonder why they get hated on... Again another discussion.) Those are minorities.

What I am trying to say - HC as it is on Turtle - VERY POPULAR. Lots of ppl play it and enjoy it.

The AH thing - not a big thing mind u - but the presence of AH (no matter whether HC or normal) creates a store that sells everything at any time. It promotes single player gameplay. The single player promoting aspects are what I dislike both about AH and the original classic HC rulesets and why I think Turtle and R2R rulesets are the best iteration of HC mode. Imo only thing Turtle HC mode misses is lvl 60 HC support to complete the journey with endgame.
