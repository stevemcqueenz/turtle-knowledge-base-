# Totem radius mark suggestion

## Post 100913 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-08T19:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100913#p100913 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

All I want here is a qol suggestion, benefiting the shaman's party.
Right now we know the radius of the totems (and the changed radius if the talent is taken).
However, you even need PFUI just to be able to display a totem mark at raid icons when the party member is within the totem's range. Without it you just guess based on distance.
This leads to problems, specially if PFUI is not installed or if there's a high mobility fight. Just think about the times when a DPS starts spamming mid-raid that 'WF pls' and shaman just shouts back something like 'WF already down'. We've all been there.
All I'd wanna suggest is a circle around the totem, equaling its radius. Red for fire totems, brown for earth totems, light blue for air totems and deep blue for water totems. Seen by each party member.
To a degree we can say it's a skill issue if the shaman doesn't move with the group. But in practice 90% of the time the shaman puts down the totem kinda close to the melee and goes back to healer range, spamming heals, not realizing that DPS, by stepping to the left 2 times, moved out of the totems' effect.
This change would not buff anyone gameplay-wise but would make totem buffs way less clunky (shaman still needs to move with the group of course but rogue would see a circle which says 'if you step further to the left, there is no WF for you, buddy').
Would make totem buffing way more fun (imagine twisting and healing only to realize that Wifeganker (undear rogue) pressed down the 'W' button for 1 sec and he didn't receive any of the bonus you give. After that Wifeganker whispers to you something about WF+competence+someone's mother). Would also help DPS a lot.
It's just somethign which I think would benefit the game a lot.

tldr: Give the totems' a circle equal to their radius, seen by each party member so everyone can see at the moment where the buffs are applied.
PS: Noone really knows what a yard is so we honestly just pretend to know the totem radius.
