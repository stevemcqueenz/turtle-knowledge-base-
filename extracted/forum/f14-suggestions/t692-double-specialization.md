The main cause of low PvP participation is more likely because of the fact the server is advertised as an RP/PvE server. Because of this, players are less likely to be interested in PvP. Combine that with a small, heavily Alliance-biased (because of RP focus) population and there you go.

The way I would address this would be to try to get more PvP-focused players to join the server, but I simply wouldn't because I fear that would harm the unique community the server fosters.

## Post 3565 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-07-25T22:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3565#p3565 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The idea is tempting (especially as a druid), but I would discourage double specializations or cheap re-specs.

I've found that when players are constantly re-spec'ing they tend not to really learn all the nooks & crannies of their class abilities. I see mages that don't counterspell, priests that don't shackle, druids that don't hybernate / innervate, etc...

One of the benefits of making re-spec'ing difficult or expensive is players really have to commit to a spec. This (usually) results in players getting to know their class builds better, increasing their overall level of play.
