I understand the reluctance to buff both the fellhunter and succubus melee damage too much as it could unbalance the class if the pet is too strong for a destruction or affliction spec'd warlock. However, the felhunter and succubus being this laughably weak makes the demonology tree nearly unplayable. You simply give up too much from destruction or affliction to make up for the complete ineptitude of the two primary demonology pets.

My suggestion to balance these concerns is to keep the succubus and fellhunter base damage the same but add a row 6, or  row 7, 3-point talent that specifically addresses their absurdly weak regular attack damage. And no, Unleashed Potential does not address this issue at all. Having a row 6 or row 7 talent that addresses this would limit access to those spec'd into deep demonology. Allowing their power to be focused where the tree would suggest, their demon.
