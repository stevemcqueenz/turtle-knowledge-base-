Lvl 1 Mana pool: ~60 to 90 : Lvl 1 Drink: Restoring 152 Mana/18s
Lvl 5 Mana pool: ~150 to 200 : Lvl 5 Drink: Restoring 437 Mana/21s
Lvl 15 Mana pool: ~500 to 1k : Lvl 15 Drink: Restoring 835 Mana/24s
Lvl 25 Mana pool: ~1k to 2k : Lvl 25 Drink: Restoring 1345 Mana/27s
Lvl 35 Mana pool: ~2.5k to 4k : Lvl 35 Drink: Restoring 1992 Mana /30s
Lvl 45 Mana pool: ~3.5k to 5k : Lvl 45 Drink: Restoring 2934 Mana/30s

... Now watch that step from Lvl 25 to Lvl 35 and also pay attention to the durations in general...
For example: The increase in mana restored (regardless of (increasing) duration now) from Level 1 to Level 5 is about 289%. From Level 5 to Level 15 (6 levels more difference) is already just 191%.
And then, when mana pools and spell cost really develop and natural regeneration while casting, or even without casting, just isn't enough to fill up quickly the ratio drops incredibly! As for Level 15 to 25 drinks it's only an increase of 161% and next step to Lvl 35 is only 148% increase, which is about the weakest area and we reached the longest duration of 30 seconds.
This is just ineffective and absolutely lowers the game experience. Plus in time it can get really dangerous, when you haven't even finished drinking, but respawn is already blocking you in...

Please, just think about a good tweak here. <3

Drinks being underpowered is also something I've noticed. I think there are multiple factors at play here. People are more aware of good gear, how to min-max stats, there is powerful gear from custom quests and dungeons, and gear is more ubiquitous on a mature server like Nordanaar (where most levelling players are probably alts with a massive bankroll behind them to buy BoEs).

Mage gets passed around as "you make your own water" but half the time you really don't want to. If you're lvl 39 you still make lvl 25 water that barely fills 1/3rd of your mana pool. You definitely want to switch to lvl 35 water as soon as possible, well before you can make it on your own.

But there is also a flipside to better game knowledge and more easily available gear. Most levelling guides are stuck in the stone age, recommending int as a main stat ("of the Eagle", etc). Min-max for spell/healing power instead, even early on from lvl 20+. It's just more output for the same mana cost. You want your spells to hit hard and your mana pool to be small so you can recover it quickly through drinking.

I do think there are ways to work around mana problems - by picking up different gear, changing your roation (wanding the last 5% saves on mana and lets you drop out of the 5-Second-Rule), buying vendor water over conjured water, picking up certain talents for regen/refunds/clearcasts (drinking being annoying is what makes them feel good once you unlock them), etc. But I could also see the benefit of buffing all drinks/foods slightly (!) to account for all the custom gear that buffs players' stats much more on a purely Vanilla server. If you have a well-geared character they really do fall off rather quickly.
