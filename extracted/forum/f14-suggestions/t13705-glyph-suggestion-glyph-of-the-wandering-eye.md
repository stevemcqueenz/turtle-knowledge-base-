# Glyph Suggestion "Glyph Of The Wandering Eye"

## Post 93164 by Malldictus — 2024-04-20T18:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93164#p93164 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there Turtle Team And fellow Turtles.

I was talking about glyphs with my mate while playing, and like that we came up with this idea for a cool and immersive Glyph!

The Glyph Would be called " Glyph Of The Wandering Eye"  The Challenge of this Glyph would be that after activating this in lvl 1 you will have the cammera zoom all the way in so you play the game in first person all the way to 60! where the challenge will end, and you will be able to zoom out again " if you wish"

Now this Challenge would be amazing to bump up the difficulty in a alternative way and also get immersed into the world by looking at it and get a whole new feeling of ever so familiar zones and dungeons! this would also spice HC runs up for those who dare to mix it "I WOULD!". imagine how nice and how different you would play Rogue and utilize your stealth to check corners that you where able to see past with different camera angels before, but now you need to use wit and smarts to overcome what the world has in store for you!

The reward of this strange yet super cool challenge would be a Tabard with a custom eye symbol on it, and a non combat companion that looks like the warlocks eye of killrog, and last but not least the Title "The Observer"

I hope you like the idea and we will be able to SEE this fun Glyph enhance our adventure with this Glyph in the future.

Thank you Team turtle for this amazing world you are reimagining, and thank you Turtles to fill it with glorious adventures to explore it all with!

Please Leave a   turtle_in_love_head   if you would like to have this Glyph as a option to your experience.

 -Mallore-   satisfied_turtle
