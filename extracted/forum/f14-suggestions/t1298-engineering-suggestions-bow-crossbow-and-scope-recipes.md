There is a precedent for this from Blizzard.

[A Fair Trade](https://vanillawowdb.com/?quest=7341) (Horde)
[Arrows Are For Sissies](https://vanillawowdb.com/?quest=7342) (Alliance)

The quest(s) allow you to exchange 200 Thorium Shells for 200 Thorium Headed Arrows.

There is no reason why a similar thing couldn't be done for Guns crafted out of Engineering skill.  You'd just simply need to have a Troll or Night Elf NPC who would exchange crafted Guns for a quest reward Bow or Crossbow with identical stats (so only the item type changes).  You could put the Night Elf in Darnassus and the Troll in Thunder Bluff and give them a catalogue of custom quests that convert Guns crafted by Engineering.
