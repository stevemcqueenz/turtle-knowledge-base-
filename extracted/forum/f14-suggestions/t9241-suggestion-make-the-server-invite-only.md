I suggest a solution to server population problem is to close to new sign ups. When the population naturally drops distribute invitations to the oldest active players that they can use to invite new players in. Manage the number of invitations issued based on the average population.

Let's not make this thread about our personal feelings towards chinese players, your feelings and my differing feelings have been expressed elsewhere already.

Given the population trend prior to the influx of chinese players it seems the server population will continue to be a problem without some greater solution than just moving chinese speaking players, this is a solution.

I'm sure some portion of the player base will migrate to the SEA server without coercion. The incentives will be shorter queue's and the opportunity to get invites for their friends. If we can agree nothing else, we can agree that coercively moving players is controversial and divisive.

Even if nobody moved to SEA, this approach would gradually move the original server back to its earlier language mix as the veteran players, presumably english speakers, would be given the invitations to distribute as the population naturally declines. In this manner, the community itself, led by the most active veteran players, determines who they invite to the server and thus its future make-up.

We've already discussed at length my opinion on how easy circumvention of a hanzi ban is, so there's no need to discuss it further here, I agree that continuing to enforce world chat as english-only makes sense, and that world chat on the SEA server in chinese adds further incentive for migration.
