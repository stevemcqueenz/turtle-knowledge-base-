# Why Dwarves Feel Underpowered

## Post 142904 by Larohir — 2025-08-03T10:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142904#p142904 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey all ,

Looking at Dwarf racials they look a lot weaker compere to what other races can offer (especialy horde) and they are very situational. Let me elaborate:

Stoneform
Used to be amazing against Blind in PvP (now not a poison—major nerf).
Still solid vs Bleeds and some DoTs, but very situational.

![❌](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/274c.svg) Passives
Find Treasure – Fun but completely useless in combat.
Frost Resistance – Rarely relevant anymore.
Gun Specialization – Only affects specific builds (BM /MM), but Dwarf stat layout (lowest base Agi) clashes with Hunter optimization.

Perhaps give Dwarves an additional passive:

![💪](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4aa.svg) Strength of the Mountain
+2% Strength or 1% Strength  and  1% Stamina
OR
Weapon Skill with Axes / Maces by +3

Or consider something more interesting, like  Thrill of Battle / Hot-blooded:
After landing a physical (melee or ranged) critical hit, gains 30% health regeneration in combat for 6 seconds.
If a healing or spell critical hit occurs, restors 1% total mana .
Internal cooldown: 10 seconds.

Why this works:
Fits Dwarf “brawler” fantasy
Rewards crit-heavy builds
Gives healers and casters something useful too
Scales well without being easily abused
