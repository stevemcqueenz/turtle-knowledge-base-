# HC Tel'Abim - remove level announcement every 10 levels

## Post 128999 by Elfje — 2025-04-29T16:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128999#p128999 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

On the Tel'Abim server you can choose to do Hardcore. This is obviously a real challenge, as it is on a pvp server and even a pvp death means the end of it. Therefore if you sign up for this, you know that this is a true challenge with a lot of hide and seek!

 **However, what people do not sign up for, are the announcements every 10 levels that we're still alive!**
 - The message is server wide, both Horde and Alliance.
This obviously puts a **massive** price on your head, as it basically says "Please kill me before I hit level 60."

There are several hardcore hunters on the server. These are not the problem, as again, this is what we signed up for. But when the server tells them our names, and they use their second account to add us to their friends list, and track us wherever we go and follow us to every zone and camp our hearthstone location, then it does become a problem!
Again, I'm **not advocating** for any action against those hunters, as this is what we signed up for. I'm sure it happens on both factions.

I'm **only advocating for removing the announcements that we're still alive every 10 levels**. If we give ourselves away any other way, then it's our own fault. But the server telling us who we are ... that's just brutal. Especially the level 50 announcement is extreme ... the hunt intensifies after that.
Also, I don't advocate for only showing the announcement for your own faction, the spy accounts of the hunters will see that anyway, so just remove the message alltogether.

What are your thoughts?
