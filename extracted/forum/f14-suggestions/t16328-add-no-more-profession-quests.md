# Add no more profession quests

## Post 111375 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-04T16:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111375#p111375 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Making every recipe of each crafting profession available by direct purchase or completing repeatable quests (with blue question marks) is more friendly to players who seek to finish all profession quests and forget profession skills multiple times for shifting to a new profession.
