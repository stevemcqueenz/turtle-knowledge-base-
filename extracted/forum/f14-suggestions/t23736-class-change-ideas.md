# Class change ideas

## Post 166409 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-01-25T10:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166409#p166409 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey!

Wanna list some class change ideas and the reason behind my selection.

 **Priest**
Heal -> I think the healer buffs (specially mana-wise) enabled healers to spam fast heals most of the time, making priests an uncomfortable choice. Forcing the class with the biggest heal variety to be flash heal bots is just degrading.
Imo the solution is not cast speed increase or making flash heal spam competitive.
Instead, add a talent to holy priests, turning their overheal (or part of it) into HoT. That way even the slower heals get a new-found advantage.

Disc -> Imo a holy protection spell would be dope (you use either that or shadow prot), available only for disc priests. Also, some help with mana regen.

Shadow -> A reliable way to spell cleave, even if it can be done on CD only. Alternatively give them shadow nova. Shadow priest holy nova is just brainrot.

 **Warrior**
For God's sake, please fix revenge's threat scaling. Thanks.

 **Druid**
Make boomie rotation less punishing and more flexible.
Make non tree resto viable. We still kinda have 1 resto druid per raid atm.

 **Pala**
Smooth out prot efficiency curve. They can be good endgame tank, A tier even, it is fine. But let it be a curve. Right now pala taking during leveling makes other dungeon tanks a joke.

 **Mage**
Give fire a niche please. Right now it feels like a worse arcane to me. Better aoe? Some kind of utility? Whatever, just let it have a niche.
