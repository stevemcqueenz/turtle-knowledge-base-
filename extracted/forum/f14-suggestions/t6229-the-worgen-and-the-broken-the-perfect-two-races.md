Future content patches are ahead as turtle wow charges full steam ahead in its success. My idea is for the introduction of two future races, the worgen for the alliance, and the broken-draenei for the horde. Thematically its almost PERFECT.

First it only makes sense, the horde got goblins (a cataclysm race) and alliance got high elves (TBC race but alliance) With my suggestion now it would be alliance's turn to receive the cata race (Worgen) and the horde receive their equivalent tbc race (Draenei-but broken).

Since gilneas is being planned as a content patch i cannot think of a more perfect time for the curse afflicted peoples to rejoin the alliance. Hopefully this time however they are done right and not just furry Disney-eyed fuzzy weapon wielders but more like actual grizzled humans that can temporarily transform as perhaps a racial cooldown. Now for broken draenei their lore would have to be taken with a bit more liberty but it can still work. An isolated clan sealed off from the dark portal and the rest of their people in the blasted lands; they desperately cling to survival from their harsh reality by communing with the elements. An envoy from the horde (perhaps rexxar on eastern kingdoms vacation) could empathize with their plight and see their worth inviting them into the horde.

Additionally this works since horde would now receive and extra shaman race much like how the alliance received an extra paladin race through high elves. How the broken look would probably need to be backlogged through tbc-broken models which turtle wow devs have already shown they are capable to do. (i.e bringing back azure drake a model from wotlk, nightmare horse from Karahan). Some of you may say well there's no broken female draenei. Who cares? They don't need to have a gender specific model as they have already been deformed. Just use the same model with maybe different hairstyles.

With turtle wow full steam ahead in its success breaking population records every day its only a matter of time new content patches are announced. These two new races are a though-experiment I've been playing around with a lot, and people in world chat seem to like the idea. Of course let me know your opinions and how you think maybe this could be improved or maybe doesn't work at all. Thanks for giving this a read!
