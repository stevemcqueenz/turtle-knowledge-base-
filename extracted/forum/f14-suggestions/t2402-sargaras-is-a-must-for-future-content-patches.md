# Sargaras is a Must for future content patches

## Post 15009 by Robertscotty — 2021-12-30T03:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15009#p15009 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the Creator and Supreme lord of the Burning legion I feel that facing the Dark Titan Sargaras in Turtle wow should be a guaranteed priority for the team and the issue is finding out how to incorporate him into the game

My thought was to have a new storyline that takes place on the Dreadlord's homeworld of Nathreza where Sargaras has built his capital

Nathreza makes sense lore wise for 2 reasons

 1.we were never told where exactly Sargaras is during the standalone games

 2.  Nathreza actually makes a appearance in game during the Undead campaign so the planet is a perfect choice for the Legion capital world

The Planet would be devided into 5 Diffrent sections and each section would contain both a dungeon and a raid

the Final Dungeon would have Kil'jaeden as it's final boss while the final Raid would have the Dark Titan Himself as the last boss

Storyline

The storyline would start with a massive attempted Legion invasion upon Azeroth that players fight off and result in the abduction of both Warchief Thrall and the young child king Anduin Wrynn

Players are then sent on a series of Quests to discover where thrall and Anduin were taken which leads the player to Nathreza

Once on Nathreza the player learns that a few of the Nathrezim tribes have begun a civil war against the Burning legion with the goal of retaking their homeworld and that the Attack on Azeroth was a ruse in order to capture the racial leaders who Sargaras seeks to Sacrifice in order to obtain the necessary power to overcome the rennagades

The rest of the player's adventures on Nathreza will include earning the trust of the rennagade Nathrezim and uniting them into a single Army, locating and rescuing both thrall and Anduin before Launching a final assault upon the Burning legion leading to a final Battle with Sargaras Himself

I'd love to hear your thoughts on this

I disagree.

The best thing about Vanilla is how grounded everything is. It's a typical run-of-the-mill generic high fantasy MMORPG and that's what's so awesome about it.

The way the Turtle team has been expanding on that idea in a horizontal manner rather than vertical is purely amazing to say the least.

When you start adding spaceships and blue space goats and flying and other bullshit, it's when the charm of that goes away. It's the reason why Retail WoW died in 2007 with the release of TBC - they started catering to the walking, talking, screeching, whining wallets so they could milk this game for all it's worth.

In another parallel reality, if the Blizzard team stood by what they imagined about WoW, the game might have been the same Vanilla in 2022 that it was in 2004, it might have become a niche game, because they would refuse to cater to walking, talking, screeching, whining wallets, but it just might have been what Turtle WoW is now.
