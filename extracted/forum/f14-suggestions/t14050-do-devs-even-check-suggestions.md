# Do devs even check suggestions?

## Post 95708 by Itorch100 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-05-26T14:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95708#p95708 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title says.

I haven't seen any interaction from the team on this sub-forum, which makes posting a suggestion feel like writing a fanfic to soothe your creative itch, rather than something you hope would be considered by the devs.

I've been told that 'the place' to be seen by the devs is the bug tracker, but obviously that's limited to 'bugs', aka something that is objectively flawed/broken in an obvious way and should be fixed in the future. Suggestions concerning objectively flawed areas of the game do not belong on the bug tracker, more so for subjective opinionated ideas. So we're left with this sub-forum. So, coming back to my question.

Is there even a point in posting here? Of course, I understand that not every idea would be considered, but it would be encouraging to know that your suggestion is at the least being considered for the future.

Cheers!   turtle_tongue_head

Yes, but do you understand that for proper development there needs to be dialogue with the suggestee and inquiries into what is being suggested. You can't just take a glance at something and believe you understand what is being suggested, you have to ask. You should ask questions about anything that is unclear and not just dismiss it.
Of course you don't have infinite time and resources to talk to people, but you're already reading stuff, and you can gain a lot more out of the forum by just doing a little bit of real developer interaction. The general playerbase does not like this forum, they find it meaningless and soul-crushing as it is an abyss you just write into, and they don't like the other people here (I believe that is to the structural format here). People have been saying this privately in discord about the forum for years.

If I was a developer and I saw a promising suggestion from a player, then I would ask questions such that I could gain a better understanding of it - pick the player's brain if you will.
If the suggestion was suboptimally formulated or thought out I would leave a rectifying remark that would lead to better future suggestions, not just from the one suggestee, but from the other players reading as well so they know how to not suggest something. That way you won't have an unguided mess of poor posts on the forum.
