# Only Walk Challange Idea

## Post 160369 by Csipesz — 2025-11-23T05:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160369#p160369 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I recently started playing on this server and really enjoy how calm the atmosphere feels. I even have a character who only walks everywhere no rushing at all. Since the server has so many different challenges already, I thought… what if there were an Only Walk Challenge for players like me who enjoy this kind of slow-paced playstyle?

Here’s what the challenge could include:

 - Running is permanently disabled
 - Mounts can also only move at walking speed
 - Flight Paths cannot be used
 - 24h cooldown on Hearthstone
 - Warlocks can’t summon you
 - Party with others who are in the same mode only (similar to hardcore)
 - Travel is limited to boats and zeppelins only.

It's just my silly idea, but what do you think?
