**Suggestion: Add a High Elven Hunter Pet Trainer to Alah’Thalas**

Hello Turtle WoW team,

I wanted to make a small suggestion regarding High Elven Hunters and pet training.

Currently, other major faction cities provide access to hunter pet trainers, but there does not appear to be one available in the High Elven capital of Alah’Thalas. This can leave High Elven Hunters at a mechanical disadvantage compared to other races, despite Alah’Thalas functioning as their primary hub.

From a lore perspective, High Elves have a long-established ranger tradition dating back to *Warcraft II*. The Farstriders of Quel’Thalas are one of the most iconic hunter archetypes in Warcraft, and they are frequently depicted fighting alongside trained animal companions such as hawks, wolves, and other beasts. This tradition predates the Blood Elves and firmly supports the idea that High Elven Hunters would have access to pet training.

Given Turtle WoW’s expanded content and the existence of pet trainers in other major cities, adding a thematically appropriate pet trainer in Alah’Thalas would feel both lore-consistent and mechanically fair.

Thank you for all the work you put into Turtle WoW, and for taking the time to consider this suggestion.
