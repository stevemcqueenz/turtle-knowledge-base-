# New melee Hunter spell and other ideas

## Post 100975 by Sinslayer (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-09T22:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100975#p100975 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, first off I want to say that I hope my post doesn't get buried by the millions of tears ranged hunters are crying on the forum and discord nowadays over their hard cc combo getting DR nerfed in pvp. How terrible. But I digress, here's the summary of my thoughts so you don't have to read the wall of text I wrote:

 ---
MY SUGGESTIONS ARE:

 1) My main idea here is to rework aspect of the wolf so that when used, it gives 10% more attack power to the hunter and the pet while also disabling the ranged usage.

 2) Make aspect of the wolf a stance like ability so that the hunter's ranged spells turn into melee ones. These melee spells be be completely different than the ranged ones but without changing the flavor.

 2) My other idea is to rework all the talent trees so that every talent tree has 51 points and if the available talent points are all put into a certain talent tree, a forth spec for the class would be unlocked.
 ----

This is mainly a hunter class post I want to discuss about so reader beware. Now, what better place to get started than talk about how the new spell that hunters are getting, will affect gameplay in the next patch. The spell reads as follows:'' CARVE - A sweeping strike that attacks up to 5 enemies in a 8 yard cone in front of you for 60% weapon damage. This ability shares a cooldown with Multi - Shot.'' Now, to some, this spell may seem like an effort to make melee hunters viable but what it will most likely do is forever kill melee hunter and make ranged hunter broken.

Make CARVE, the new hunter spell, a melee hunter exclusive spell. How? Rework aspect of the wolf into an aspect that costs 100% of the hunter's mana and make it have a long cast so you can't aspect dance and abuse. That is because when it used, it will transform all the ranged spells into melee ones.

Make the melee aspect give 10% more attk power instead of a flat amount with the hunter pet too. Or just make another aspect that does all this and call it aspect of the lion. These changes should give life to the melee hunter without touching base hunter.  Also, make more of the BM talents melee friendly cos I think that's where the heart of the melee hunter is.

On the side note, I like Twow. I think it is THE private server to play on if you have a nostalgia itch or if you just want to have fun playing on server. Sure, the community can be toxic here and there but what online community isn't toxic from time to time? That being said, it seems to me that Twow wants to keep the blizz-like experience of the game intact as much as possible to provide that nostalgia feeling, while also not being afraid to add novelty with their own creative design choices and updates to keep the game feeling fresh and new.

Back to the hunter and the new spell. How will it delete melee hunter and make ranged hunter broken? Because melee hunters cannot use ranged spells (Aspect of the Wolf disables the use of ranged weapons and abilities) but ranged hunters can use melee spells cos they do not have the opposite restriction. So, whatever spells melee hunters will get in the future, will be better used by the ranged spec instead. That's the disbalance which makes melee hunters not only a meme spec, but a dead spec as a concept unless something drastic changes. I understand that there are many other meme specs that can be brought to life, but for me, melee hunter is just super fun and valuable nostalgia energy cos it makes me feel like I am playing Rexxar from wc3 again. That's why I am advocating drastic changes to fully give life to this meme spec while also remaining faithful to blizz-like hunter.

I do not know if all this is a design choice made by Twow to make melee hunters subpar or to remove the blizz-like feeling of the hunter class but time will tell I suppose. Because it happened before, as with paladin, it is a possibility. If it is, I will distance myself from hunter and just find something else I like. Either way, I still want to argue that in the state that it was leaked, CARVE is a bad spell to give to hunters and I will argue why.

CARVE will no longer make hunter, which is a ranged pet class, weak in melee. In fact, it will make them stronger than any other melee in close combat in terms of dps and survival because of all the tools they have. They will will be able not only to damage the backline in a fight, but also the front line easily thus doubling their power level by doing melee weaving both in pve and pvp.

There is totem twisting and seal twisting but that's just mechanical abuse and probably never intended to be a thing. Melee weaving is more a skill based mechanic but even then, only skilled hunters would dare go melee and ranged at the same time cos it required watching the timer of both melee and ranged attacks. This very rewarding but sweaty min max dps tactic has been replaced with a spell that requires no skill. Also, min maxing raid leaders will demand hunters start melee weaving or there will be dps loss. Maybe this won't happen right away, but eventually, it's a certainty that after hunters start using CARVE for dps gain, they will also have to learn how to use raptor strikes and watch the auto timers not to mess up the timing and so on which is probably not the playstyle someone who chose a ranged class wanted.

So not only will this spell make the hunter overpowered in terms of damage, it will also make the class annoying all in one package. A decrease of the hunter population will be seen because of this change in playstyle and those who will remain, will have no problems being the most overpowered class on the server.

Look at it this way, hunters had to position properly, set up their melee attacks to gain a dps bonus but that's no longer the case. Hunter will act like warriors both in pve and pvp with the added bonus of having ranged attacks and spells. Hunters get an aoe dmg solution to their biggest weakness which is the dead zone and the spell has no drawbacks. That's pretty insane. What's the solution? Have the spell have drawbacks. And I don't mean only sharing a CD with MS.

But enough of ranged hunter, let's talk a bit about melee hunter and try to make CARVE work exclusively for melee cos, well, ranged has MS. One of the ideas I came up with is to rework aspect of the wolf. It's a new spell and as I said before, it makes the hunter unable to use ranged attacks or spells but the hunter gains additional attack power as a trade of. But just having attack power and one or two spell to play with isn't exactly exciting playstyle. And by exciting I don't mean being #1 dps in every instance. That enjoyment is for min maxers who do not care what class they play as long as it's the strongest. Well, nothing wrong with that but it lacks substance. That's why I suggest that aspect of the wolf be reworked to give 10% attack power to both hunter and pet (much like the blizzard version of aot beast) and make turn the hunter's ranged spells into melee ones. So when activated, aspect of the wolf would turn MS into CARVE and AIMED SHOT into a melee HEADSHOT or something like that. That would set up melee hunters to have a bright future ahead without compromising base hunters in any way. I'm sure the dev team would come up with cooler concepts than that.

Speaking of talents as a finish, I would like to suggest to normalize talent trees as in make every talent tree have 51 points in it so that if a player wanted, they could put all their points into one of the talent trees and gain a bonus from it? Doesn't have to include a bonus but the normalization sounds neat. Instead of just trying to grab the best talents from each tree, make the talent trees more flavorful and thematic. I suppose talents are all about that - being cool.

I suggest normalization for the talent trees cos there's an imbalance there as well for example: the fury warrior tree has a whopping 60 talent points in it while protection in paladin tree has only 41. So that's almost 20 points difference. Sounds unfair to me.

Other ideas:
 -make the death knight class the 4th spec for every class. every talent tree would be different depending on the class
 - melee hunter: survival hunter tank, crazy good synergy with the original talent build
 - melee hunter: bm dps, dw is the best option to trigger aspect of the wolf. there's not much synergy with melee dw with the pet in bm talent tree.
 - ranged hunter - consume pet spell for better aspects? the pure archer fantasy

Shaman ele 46, enha 50, resto 52. Lock affli 44, demo 44, destro 53. Druid: balance 48, feral 42, resto 51. Rogue assassin 42, combat 59, sub 46. Pala holy 45, prot 50, ret 46.

While this doesn't showcase which of the talent trees is the weakest, it does show which talent trees have the least and most talent points available. The least have assa rogues and feral druids at 42 and the most have fury warriors with 60 points of talent options.
