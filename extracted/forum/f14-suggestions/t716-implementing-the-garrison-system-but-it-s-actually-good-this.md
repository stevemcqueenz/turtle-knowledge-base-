# Implementing the garrison system but it's actually good this time, andd other features so i guess

## Post 3694 by Cachucamaru — 2019-08-10T03:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3694#p3694 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

PuTtInG tHe WaR bAcK iN wArCrAfT

So i was thinking about the garrisons and millitary centers you see in-game (Westbrook Garrison on Elwynn forest for example) and i thought to myself that DUDE WOULDN'T IT BE COOL if high ranking PvP players that have a lot of reputation with their race capitals could be the sargeants and Whatnot of these garrisons. At first, they could maybe enlist players as their subordinates, then, give quests to other players (Of course, the gold gained from the rewards would be taken out of their pockets, no exploits for me sir). And, for the completionists out there, maybe you could have an outpost progression system, So they could maybe turn their little outpost into garrisons THEN MAYBE EVEN SMALL TOWNS. That would add cool progression systems to areas so players have an influence in the whole zone (well actually just a part of it to be honest but still, that wouldn't just be like a boring draenor garrison and other players could look at your garrison and be like DUDE SO COOL I WANNA BE LIKE THAT WHEN I GROW UP).

Max limit would be an outpost/garrison for player, to not have a single person be the sole warchief of the horde/high-king of the alliance and ruin the experience for other players.

There could even be events on which players from the opposing faction would organize invasions to conquer these outposts for a while and get a bit of custom "Assault-tier" loot (That would make it so they have a reason to sink an hour or two on that). If they manage to actually conquer them, THEY would hold control of it for a timeframe (Would work kind of like a cooldown until the next assault, so they won't have their newly conquered outpost COMPLETELY MAULED by the next time they log-on again). Of course, to make it fair to the still-leveling players, probably wouldn't allow the opposing faction to hold control of garrisons that are in the beginner zones.

Other miscelaneous suggestions i could make would be hunger bars, taking all the healing stuff out of alchemy and adding it to first-aid and more options to survival, to make it so leveling professions is a bit more desirable/rewarding. Could also make it so not EVERY SINGLE TOWN has a tailor, for example, so players have to look for them, ask for directions and even be forced to visit other places semi-regularly (As is done with the survival skill, don't ever change that).
