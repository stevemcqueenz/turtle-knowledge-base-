# Add TBC capstone abilities as tomes you can gain in future raids

## Post 165183 by Familymaker — 2026-01-14T22:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165183#p165183 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So while I absolutely love how you have kept it mostly vanilla, I do miss the capstone abilities from The Burning Crusade, as they were good but still balanced. Would it be possible to make those abilities be available in tomes you could gain from raids as late game abilities?

For example, for Frost mages they could gain a water elemental to aid them. This could provide dps and frost nova of its own (which could benefit from talents and proc your flash freeze). However its duration was only 45 seconds, with a 3 minute cooldown. Similar to how arcane mages had the slow spell but it could not be used on strong enough bosses. And they would gain these abilities as rewards for doing raids, instead of as talents.

This way it would give a motivation for late game players to continue doing raids, along with getting better gear. As it would open more playstyles and posibilities to your late game characters, making the experience more fun.
