# Shaman Shocks

## Post 31679 by Original3 — 2022-12-28T00:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31679#p31679 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there fellow Shaman friends!

A while ago, I started thinking about shocks shamans have to offer and their using. After deeper thoughts I realised, that there is one huge problem with shocks in PVE. They are bad.

Before class changes, shamans had only one raid spec and that was restoration. Luckily enough, Turtle wow developers were able to make Elemental shamans a bit more competetive, and today it's not a surprise seeing an Elemental shaman in high spots of DPS table.
Unfortunately, Enhancement shamans are mostly the same, as they were before class changes. Of course, Bloodlust made Enhancements more of a support role for other DPS people in group and it also increased their own dps. But they still just rely on good Windfury procs, in hope they do good DPS without pulling boss aggro and dying....lol.

Okay, I haven't say anyting new, so let's get to the point of this post - Shocks.
Shamans got 3 shocks in total. Earth shock that does nature damage, interrupts spellcast and causes heavy amount of threat. Flame shock that does base fire damage + fire damage over time. Last one is Frost shock, that inflicts high amount of frost damage and slows target's movement speed by 50% for 8 seconds. All shocks are considered being spells, so they benefit from spell hit and spell crit.
And this us the whole problem with Shocks. Enhancement shamans don't have any Spell hit from gear, so their every fifth ot sixth shock gets resisted, not to mention, that they cost a lot of mana. Also, elemental shamans don't use shocks, cause their rotation is usually just Lightning bolt spam. (At least I haven't seen Elemental shamans using shocks in raids)

Okay, what could solve this problem?

Answer is simple - remake shocks to be attacks, not spells.
This would make all shocks benefit from Melee hit and Melee crit. Enchancements would then have a reason to use them in their rotation, and it would increase their overall dps.

 *A little note: With this change, you should rework talent "Elemental Fury" to only benefit Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning.*

Who else can benefit from this?

Pretty much every version of Enchancement Shamans
on Turtle wow.
DPS of Spellhancement shamans would be increased and their gearing will be less complicated.
Shaman Tanks would have much more stable threat gains, because they could guarantee their Earth Shocks to be landed almost all the time.

How about PVP?

I have no clue, I don't do any PVP as shaman.   insidious_turtle

TL;DR:
Shocks should be attacks, not spells.
