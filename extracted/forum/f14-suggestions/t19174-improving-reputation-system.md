# Improving Reputation System

## Post 130244 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-08T20:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130244#p130244 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey folks, I've been playing a lot lately and have been having thoughts about reputation. I think that the idea of Rep is really fun, but it seems like Blizzard never really figured out how to integrate it into the game well. These are a couple suggestions I have, based on my 20+ years of WoW, on how to make the Reputation system more meaningful in Classic.

 **Faction-Specific World Events**
The biggest and most expensive ask would be implementing some sort of world event for some of the major factions that the devs can activate when it's not Easter or whatever. Example: The Argent Dawn vs. Scourge Incursion – Periodically, zones like WPL/EPL are overrun by Scourge. Players earn extra AD rep by defending towns, cleansing blight, or escorting NPC caravans. Rewards: Unique transmog gear or consumables. this is more "pie in the sky" but it'd be nice to have. More realistically...

 **More Reputation in General**
It makes you feel more connected to the world to be able to gain rep with a faction. It'd be amazing to get Explorer's League rep from doing quests associated with them throughout the world. Or to give another reason to grind lower dungeons, other than loot fishing a specific boss. It also incentivizes full clears, which I think also helps keep dungeon culture healthy. Class-based reputations would be fun, especially if there were 2 for each class and choosing one locks you out of the others, so you have to "reputation spec".

 **Epic Quest Chains**
Add multi-part story quests that unlock at specific reputation tiers (e.g., "The Fall of Lordaeron" for Argent Dawn at Honored). Completing these grants bonus rep and reveals faction lore. Use this to add more in-game depth to areas like the Scarlet Crusade’s involvement with the Argent Dawn in EPL. Quest chains could have parts that are broken up along reputation tiers, so as you level you get folded into more important tasks.

 **Group Challenges**
Faction Raids: 5-man repeatable dungeons (e.g., Timbermaw Hold is going to be a great example of this) with mechanics tied to faction themes. It'd be great to get a Ravenholdt dungeon, just like the centaurs have Mara. Even improving cosmetic rep rewards for the base factions so that you feel cooler for having run a lot of Stockades on your NE because now you have some cool SW cosmetic gear seems like a win. Or things like making an Explorer's League rep and then having Ulda give EL rep.

 **Bounty Boards:**
Post elite mobs or rare spawns in contested zones. If you let people gain rep with Flight Masters, you could have them give quests to explore every subzone of the zone they are in. You could also make these profession item turn ins, etc. Give people another reason to farm Power Crystals and things of that nature.

 **Faction Reputation Rewards**
The new Silvermoon Remnants faction has some pretty great reputation rewards- why is it that so many other major and minor factions are so terrible with the rewards they provide? There should be sets of gear that become gradually unlocked over multiple rep tiers and levels. Each faction should have at least one iconic weapon that you can unlock by grinding with them. Just getting a tabard at Exalted is not enough. For factions that are near dungeons, giving them a faction-specific Cooking recipe that gives a buff that is specifically appropriate to that dungeon.

It would be even better if there were even more thoughtful items (not just crafting recipes)- maybe an item you put in your bag that lets a specific gathering profession have a % chance to loot more of a specific herb or metal  in the faction’s zone. (eg: Night Watch talisman- gives a % chance that when you mine Silver in Duskwood that you’ll get an additional piece).

 **Faction Guard Gear**
I know that this is already available in the gear store for transmog, but it would be really cool if there were actually worthwhile pieces of gear that could be had by rep grinding with specific factions. Getting Orgrimmar or Ironforge up to Exalted should allow you to buy decent gear that lets you look like a guard and actually want the stats. Maybe there’s a PVP requirement too.

 **Paragon Reputation**
Paragon is a post-Exalted tier for reputation with no upper cap to it. See how much reputation beyond Exalted you can get. Maybe there are weekly or monthly leaderboards, with prizes for various ranking bands.

 **Focus Upcoming Content on Existing Factions**
Making factions and reputation more useful is always a win for player satisfaction. Adding more ways to gain that rep or take advantage of it when you have it will always make players feel good about their time investment. Looking to add new dungeon content or expanding on overworld questlines? IMO, Devs should prioritize ways to flesh out and make even low level faction rep (Centaur Clans, Ravenholdt, etc) worthwhile and interesting.

One way to do this would be to put representatives of some of these factions in lower level areas where it makes sense, and allow (repeatable?) turn-ins for small reward amounts. You could have Timbermaw reps and a quest chain with furbolgs in Ashenvale, for example. The Kul Tirans in Stormwind are already a great example of doing this right. Why not introduce players to the Thorium Brotherhood a little earlier if they're playing in Dwarven areas? Why not give meager Zandalar rep to people leveling in STV? It would help tie the world together.
