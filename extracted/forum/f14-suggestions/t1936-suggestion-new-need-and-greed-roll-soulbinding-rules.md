# [Suggestion] New NEED and GREED roll soulbinding rules

[Suggestion] New NEED and GREED roll soulbinding rules

 **TL:DR** Make a **Need roll on a BoE** bind that item to the character, and make a **Greed roll on a BoP** make that item tradable to other present team members for some time.

 1. Basic rolling system is basic

It's mostly the big ticket BoE's, which leads to all sorts of situations. Here's one example: Edgemasters drop and the Warrior of the group demands everyone else pass for him. The group of strangers don't comply and all press need. The warrior wins it anyway and puts the gauntlets on the AH.

I'm not arguing for or against rolling Need on big ticket BoE's. I'm trying to point out the behavior that emerges with the system as is as the foundation. I don't think loot situations should ever be this complicated, and the system should never encourage us to press Need for Gold, when the Greed button is literally a gold coin.

 2. Bind on Need

It is usually understood that Need means you'll equip the item. Let's make it do that automatically. You could add a modified confirmation message, as is already the case with BoP's, to make it unambiguous, such as "Selecting NEED will make this item bind to you. Are you sure?".

 2.1. The upsides

Need for Gold is now a thing of the past. This goes further than valuable BoE's. For example people will also no longer pretend to Need things like 16 slot bags or flask recipes, when they're really just going to sell them on the AH.
Needing an item in order to give it to a friend is also gone.

 2.2. The downsides

Someone might not know the value of a drop, and select Need for the upgrade, when they would have selected Greed had they known the value.
Someone might need on an item that's bad for their class (e.g. Edgemasters on Hunter), and now the group has no chance to rectify it.

 3. Tradable on Greed

There are players that will not need on an item, because they don't know how good it is or because they don't want to tread on anyone's toes. At times, this leads to a BoP landing in a person's inventory, that would be willing to trade, but can't. Personally, I've lost count of the amount of times something akin to the following occured:

"X, why didn't you need on this, it's good for you!" - "I thought Y needed it." - "No, Y already has it."

So essentially I'm advocating for letting BoP's won with a Greed roll be traded to party members that were present for the kill for let's say 30 minutes. It should be longer than just 5 minutes to account for wiping or a person hearthing out, so the trade can still occur. I see no downsides with this.

Thanks for reading, let us know what you think.
