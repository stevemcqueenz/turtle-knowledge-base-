Pummel and Shield Bash both serve the same purpose, but S. Bash is contextually more important due to the bonus two seconds of Lockdown on its Interrupt effect. However, it takes a GCD to use it due to Abilities. Furthermore, it still needs a GCD due to having to swap to a Shield. You can merge two GCDs into one by using an Ability and instantly switching, but then that still means you need to wait the GCD out. This essentially results in SB being undesirable as a superior Interrupt to Pummel, purely for the fact you waste almost as much time to try to get a +2s Interrupt while the Spell you are exactly trying to stop (and its associated Spell School) is more than likely going to finish long by that point. Indirectly, in this context, Shaman did not suffer such behavior to their Grounding Totem. Now it does.

Shaman does not have Stances. Or Stance-bound Abilities. Warrior does. Without doing that in advance, we'd be sitting in any such CC, potentially for the full duration -- if Heartbeat Resist RNG does not favor us.
