# Warrior - taunt in pvp

## Post 157012 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-25T12:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157012#p157012 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Suggestion:
Make Taunt work in PvP by locking the taunted target’s target to the Warrior who used it.

Warriors lack crowd control and reliable ways to influence fights, and currently Taunt, Mocking Blow, and Challenging Shout have no effect on PvP targets. Enabling this would enhance the Warrior’s tank identity and allow them to protect allies more meaningfully, rather than just dealing damage.

It wouldn’t need to force the opponent to attack automatically—cooldowns and duration could be balanced separately for PvP—but it would give Warriors a true protector role and add tactical depth to their gameplay.

Cheers,
Buxbrew / Morkah

I personally like the retail route. I know, genuinely baffling to hear from someone who's typically a very vanilla purist when it comes to these kinds of talks but blizzard is, believe it or not, completely capable of making half decent design choices.

Tank specs universally (I think) have a PvP talent that makes their taunt apply a debuff to the target that makes them take more and more damage for each person currently attacking/targeting them. (Or something to that effect)

The issue with making it work like this, though, is that... Uh... How do you do that? As in, make a spell work different against a player in comparison to how it does against a mob? Is it technically possible in vanilla? Is it even worth the effort? Why don't we just... Make warriors better some other way? etc etc, there's lots of subtle problems.
