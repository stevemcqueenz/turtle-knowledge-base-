# Dungeon Tier 1.5 Sets

## Post 170491 by Lovebucket2 — 2026-03-10T06:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170491#p170491 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**The Suggestion:**
My suggestion is to make the 0.5 Gear sets Upgradable once more. Tier 1.5.  Have an additional quest chain that makes the items upgradable in the same fashion the Tier 0 gear was upgraded. And crank up the difficulty up. I would presume it would still stay true to the "Dungeon Crawling" theme where quests will need to be completed within dungeons (Or possibly 10 or 20 man raids UBRS,K10,ZG, AQ20)

The Upgradable Dungeon sets are one of the interesting Ideas back from Vanilla. It offers a nice path to get relatively decently rounded out gear for Dungeon Crawlers and Raiders alike. The quests are reasonably expensive and requires just the right amount of effort. And when you see someone in full 0.5 you know they've definitely put some time in.

Not everyone can commit to Raiding and its nice to offer multiple avenues of progression(PvP, Dungeon Crawling, Raiding, Questing, Crafting).

Way back when the Tier 0.5 quests dropped and there was huge interest in them. It also had a side effect of Boosting Server Economy and increasing Players Running Dungeons. It's funny looking back and thinking that 45 min Strat dead was a big achievement for some. I remember people spamming LFG chat trying to put together the best of the best to ensure their quest time.

Out of all the Private Servers I've played I've never seen anyone try to replicate what the Tier 0.5 Quests brought to the table. And honestly if anyone could do it right it would be the Turtle WoW team.

Not a fully fleshed out Idea, but you get the essence of it.. Thoughts and feedback is welcome.
