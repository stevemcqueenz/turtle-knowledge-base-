# Add battleground NPCs to the Gurubashi Arena

## Post 12362 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-09-20T14:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12362#p12362 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since everyone is already hanging out in the STV Gurubashi Arena, and given that it is a pvp area already, it would make sense to add battleground npcs there. From an in character perspective, it would also make sense that the different pvp factions would like to recruit strong fighters that they´ve seen fighting in the arena with their own eyes. Alternatively it could be 3 plaincloth "agents" for both factions standing there with the exact same function but with a more low-key visual to it.

 **Advantages of this:**
More pvpers from low to high lvl
pvp more accessible for lower lvled players (especially in the level range 28-45) as they level up in Stranglethorn Vale not having to go to a city for no other reason than to wait forever for a WSG or AB.

 **Possible disadvantages:**
less reason to stay in capital cities
