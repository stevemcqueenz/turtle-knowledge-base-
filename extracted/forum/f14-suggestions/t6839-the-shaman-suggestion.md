# the Shaman suggestion

## Post 43113 by Johnkw — 2023-04-26T03:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43113#p43113 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I studied the Shaman of Turtle WOW for a while, and found that the Shaman DPS design still did not solve two problems:

 1. Lack of mana, either element or enhancement
 2. It is impossible to balance the choice of equipment between AP and SP.

I offer a easy and interesting talent modify that keeps PVE/PVP balanced:
See the Troll's racial talent, Berserking(Increases your attack speed by 10% to 30%. At full health the speed increase is 10% with a greater effect up  to 30% if you are badly hurt when you activate Berserking. last 10 sec.)

how about giving a shaman a similar talent:
Exchange your AP and SP by 0% to 100%.At full mana, convert your AP to SP;  At empty mana, convert your SP to AP;  the change rate could be 3AP :1SP.  The amount of conversion depends on the amount of mana. e.g. If you have 50% mana, convert 50% AP to SP, and convert 50% SP to AP at the same time.
BTW,convert means decrease one and increase another.

The advantage:
 1 Making the Shaman's Lacking mana a useful condition.
 2 You don't need to redesign Shaman's equipment.
 3 Provides a whole new DPS cycle for shamans
 4 Make the Shaman fit the magic warrior setting better.

Finally, this new talent is better to set in the enhanced talent tree.
