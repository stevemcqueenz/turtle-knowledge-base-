This is the root of the problem. Turtle WoW started from a niche server with barely scraping 70-80 players when I first played it in 2018 and everyone was OK with it being niche - the players and most importantly the developers. Then something happened and the server started becoming more famous and populated and something in the developers' philosophy changed and they wanted their server to be as mainstream as possible, I don't know what it was and this is how their decision to make Turtle WoW a "Jack of all trades, Master of none" from it being the complete opposite of that.
