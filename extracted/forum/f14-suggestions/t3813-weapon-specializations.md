# Weapon Specializations

## Post 22988 by Blues — 2022-08-21T05:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22988#p22988 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you seek to balance the end game, every race and every melee class needs a way to increase their weapon skills to +10 to ditch the glancing blow penalties.  If you want survival hunter, retribution paladin, or enhancement shaman to be competitive in dps, you should add talent points to their trees to increase said weapon skills.

That, or add more equipment that gives weapon skill, even a small thing like for example an [Ironbanded Grip] trinket that increases 1h and 2h mace skill by 5.
