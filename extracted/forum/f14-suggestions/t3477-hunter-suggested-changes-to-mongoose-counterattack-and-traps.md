# Hunter - suggested changes to mongoose, counterattack and traps

## Post 20949 by Vladis — 2022-08-03T14:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20949#p20949 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I am new to this server. I have heard that this the best classic+ server out there and decided to give it a try. After I have read the newest patch notes I saw that there are some welcoming changes to classic melee hunter (aspect of the wolf and improved aspect mastery). But it is sad to see that given hunters have 4 melee skills, in pve group content we can effectively use only 2 (raptor strike and wing clip). Since in raids and dungeons mobs do not target us we cannot use MB and CA which is very dissapointing. I think the changes to survival hunter should go a step further.

Therefore I suggest either decouple mongoose bite and counterattack from dodge/parry or in addition to dodge/parry mechanics introduce new or modify existing spell/talent/proc chance - something on the rng/proc bassis with its own internal cooldown.

Example : If you have Aspect of the Wolf active, then
 1. After you land a melee crit or a melee crit with Raptor Strike and Wing Clip, you can use MB without the dodge condition (6 sec cooldown).
 2. After your pet lands a crit, you can use counterattack without the parry contidition (X sec cooldown).

And if traps are not usable in combat, I think it would be very satisfying for hunter players to make them usable. It would provide a very nice AOE for melee hunter in the form of explo trap during pve group content.

Thank you very much for reading this and I am off to my hunter leveling.
