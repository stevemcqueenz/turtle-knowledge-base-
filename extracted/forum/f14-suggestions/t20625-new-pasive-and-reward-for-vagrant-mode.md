New Vagrand Ability & Reward: Ravenous Hunger (Carpanta in spanish)

![Image](https://2.bp.blogspot.com/-MHzBUIz38Lo/TolujlUcmWI/AAAAAAAAJ48/AWptPtoXwyw/s1600/carpanta.jpg)

Hi everyone,

I’d like to suggest a new ability and reward for the Vagrand mode:
Ravenous Hunger (Carpanta)– Once unlocked, this passive ability allows your character to stack two different food buffs at the same time. At 60 you maintain the ability.

To keep it balanced, the duration of both food buffs is reduced by one-third.
For example:

Normally, a food buff lasts 15 minutes.

With Ravenous Hunger, you can have two food buffs active, but both will only last 10 minutes.

This fits perfectly with the Vagrand mode's theme of resourcefulness and adaptability, rewarding creative preparation and smart use of consumables, and  a Vagrant is always hungry ....

Let me know what you think!
