# make taverns and beds great again (for real this time)

## Post 37019 by Teul — 2023-03-06T23:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37019#p37019 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear TW team,

Since you guys and girls seem already to be so inventive and fearless, I would like to suggest 2 things to make inns and taverns more significant places as regards to the gameplay but also social aspect of the game.

Inns: renting beds in the inns for sleep ? It would cost increasingly more expensive with progress, and staying at least 1h20 (1 cycle of sleep) could grant you hour long stat buffs + max rested XP wiiiith a little fun debuff that could sometimes drop "Nightmare" making you feared for 2-3s at random following the next 30' or so ^^'
And importantly, to make sure all inns are visited: if a bed's taken, it's taken, go see another inn! Maybe each inn could have their buff ranges, or chances to give you panic attacks when you wake up ^^
>>> the inns would need to offer something the tents don't. Also, maybe the tents should only be built in remote areas, and the remoter the faster the XP buff?

Second thing : encouraging people to sit at its tables and drink and eat what the tavern would sell! Getting stat but also weirder buffs for an hour or 2 (could be funny ex. + stamina, - agility ... depending on your beverage and food!). The longer you drink, the more buffs and the longer they last (but you're also "drunker"). Yeeeet the more the bigger the chance to "puke it all out" and lose everything you gained, with a "hungover" debuff that'd last hours (less speed, random stun effecs or puking etc haha).

If the sleeping thing and the tavern thing also have auto generated emulations (the red messages) and animations to ppl in the beds or at the tables, that would bring soo much life to it and turn these into THE places to be before an epic encounter! Pretty sure the already existing visual and audio material would fit in just perfectly!

I'd love to hear your thoughts on this one :D
