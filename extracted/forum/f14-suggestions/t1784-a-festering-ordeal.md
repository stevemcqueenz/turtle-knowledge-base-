# A festering ordeal

## Post 10691 by Auxi33 — 2021-05-27T09:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10691#p10691 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is my first time posting a quest idea to the forums so I assume this is the right topic but the idea of this quest is a chain that will follow the player with the aid of some unlikely alllies through the silithid hives in the barrens, shimmering flats, tanaris and in un'guro.

>Title
'A silithid a day...',

Starts at
(A new NPC, like Mankrik's wife, a random dead dwarf body with chat dialog located in the eastern Silithid hive in the barrens)
accepting the quest also gives you a new item (ooze covered ore sample)

Turn in at
Gann Stonespire
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3341>

>Dialog
'You stumble across the body of a recently slain dwarf. It appears the body is covered in bites and lacerations as well as silithid ooze. perhaps someone nearby might who knows about the presence of Dwarves in the barrens could help. You notice after digging around in his personal affects that he has several ore samples also covered in Silithid ooze and you take some with you.',

>Progress
'Find someone who can tell you more about the Dwarves in the barrens.',

>Chat
'A dead dwarf you say? And he was near the Silithid hives, interesting...

>Reward
'Look, I want nothing to do with those bugs <name> but, I think I know where this dwarf might have come from.'
Completion also gives you a new (ooze covered ore sample) that starts the quest chain. (to be written)
