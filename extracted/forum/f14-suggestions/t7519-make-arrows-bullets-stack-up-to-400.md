Adding Quality of Life (by itself) cannot possibly be a negative thing. It's a game, the ultimate purpose is getting enjoyment out of it. The better question is whether such a change *also* introduces detrimental side-effects (see the Summoning Stone debate for an example - shrinking the world and encouraging bad behavior/reliance on others is deemed more detrimental than the added QoL).
It seems to me that changing ammo stacks would affect hunters (who lose a whole bag in class-tax either way) and pretty much nobody else. Maybe a tank would save the occasional single slot (but they could already sell the left-over stack and head into the instance with only 1 full stack - you never need more than 200 pulls). How is that a slippery slope towards breaking the game? Genuine question, maybe I'm just not seeing the obvious big problem.

I sometimes read old Vanilla patchnotes to get a better perspective. At release Scourgestones stacked to 20, even the small Minion ones. It took until patch 1.11 to make leather, enchanting materials and rogue poisons stack to 20. Did those changes "break the game"? They just made it a little more convenient to handle and store things that usually come in large and low-value quantities.

Lots of bad counter-arguments in this thread. Cheap arrows aren't an option for raiding, and one raid can deplete most of a quiver. The easiest way to restock is leaving ammo in a mailbox.

Currently I journey to IF every 1-2 weeks, dump ammo onto a bank alt, and then mail some back after every raid or dungeon. I often use good arrows in dungeons or questing due to lack of bag space.

Doubling ammo capacity mitigates the problem, as it would make it easier to reserve space for cheap arrows, decrease mail slots wasted, and reduce how often I trek to IF. However, it doesn't solve the gameplay problem that **mailboxing ammo sucks**. I propose changing ammo so that people don't have to play mailbox simulator:
- Increase ammo capacity
- Create new 17.5 arrows that don't require journeying to IF
- Make these new arrows soulbound to prevent mailboxing
A new craftable arrow would suffice, equivalent to thorium bullets, as then all you need is an anvil.

For cheap arrows, we have AV frost arrows. You can effectively purchase them from anywhere by queueing for AV. However, it feels gamey to join AV, buy arrows, and leave bg. It's also faction-biased, since Horde hunters get AV queues much more easily. I'd like to see them purchasable in more locations.
