# Damaged Gear Visuals Instead of Disappearing Items

## Post 157973 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-31T18:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157973#p157973 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The idea:
When an item’s durability reaches 0, instead of it disappearing visually, it could appear broken, cracked, or visibly damaged — giving a real sense of wear and tear.

![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) Weapons: could look chipped, cracked, or even snapped in two.

![🛡️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6e1.svg) Armor: scratched metal, torn cloth, or faded leather textures.

![👕](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f455.svg) General gear: maybe darker, dirtier, or dustier versions of the same textures.

That way, when a player’s gear breaks, you don’t just look naked — you look battle-worn, which feels much more immersive.
This could even tie into immersion or RP moments:
A warrior finishing a dungeon with a cracked blade.
A paladin’s armor dulled after long battles.
A rogue’s leather visibly torn after many fights.
It’s obviously a lot of artistic and technical work (extra texture states, client-side rendering, etc.), but it could be optional — a toggleable cosmetic setting for immersion fans.

It would also make repairing your gear feel meaningful again — you’d see the damage and the restoration.
