## Post 142908 by Kwannox — 2025-08-03T11:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142908#p142908 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am currently doing HC with Craftsmanship challenge. As a LW druid, it is pretty rough not having a weapon. And if I try a weapon on, even though i can't equip it, it will take off my starter weapon. 3-5 damage is okay to lose, but it seems echewed that plate and mail classes can gear themselves with weapons where as cloth and leatherworking users can't.

I thought a bit about it and what if you introduced weapons into leatherworking through staffs? Materials could be simple wood and then more expensive types made with different types of leather and hides.

As far as I know it is not possible to make staffs at the moment, which is a big oversight in my mind.

Could make it more balanced when doing these types of challenges as well as put more depth into different professions. Cloth could possibly make off-hand items same for leatherworking. Staffmaster could be an profession specialization.

Well that's my suggestion. Let me know what you think guys and girls! :)
