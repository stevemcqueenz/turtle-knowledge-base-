# Hardcore+ Challenges

## Post 174274 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-04-07T05:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174274#p174274 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I was thinking about how different challenges interact with each-other, and there are three in particular that I think substantially change the feel of Hardcore. Those being Vagrant's Endeavor, Level One Lunatic, and the new Trial of Heroism. I feel like doing any of these in Hardore is a big enough deal to warrant special rewards. Nothing major, just a special title or cosmetic items.

For example, reaching Lv. 60 with both Hardcore and Vagrant enabled could award the title "Ironman" while Hardcore and Heroism awards something like "Living Legend" in recognition of repeatedly beating the odds. Hardcore Lunatics could find that in addition to the existing special dungeon items, an additional cosmetic is awaiting them to commemorate each mini-raid successfully completed (maybe eventually adding up to a full transmog set).
