# Please make the Battery Powered Crowd Pummeler usable for-non engineers

## Post 84892 by Habloid — 2024-01-16T13:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84892#p84892 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear community, dear Devs,

I would like to kindly request you to unshackle the Battery Powered Crowd Pummeler <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60099> from the confines of only being usable by engineers. I‘m a fairly new player on Twow and I main a Bear Druid. Unfortunately I got to know way to late that there is an battery powered version of the most important raid consumable for bear tanks on this server, the Mechanical Crowd Pummeler. By that time I was already set on a different profession path and restarting with a new profession would have not been fun and also not fitting with the rp/fantasy that I have created for my character.
I can’t realistically see a good reason why such an item would be restricted to engineers only and basically force any bear Druid that does not want to farm Gnomer, into the engineering profession.
Please consider that opening up this item would also open up a new pool of players that can spec into a different profession, like alchemy, to help with supply and reduce the problematic situation of growing prices of raid consumables that the server is currently facing.
Lore wise, I also think it is not too far fetched that you don’t need to be an artisan engineer to change a battery on a device.
Especially if we go by the logic, that the same character could perform powerful enchantments or brew potent flasks at the same time.

Please feel free to share your feedback on this.
