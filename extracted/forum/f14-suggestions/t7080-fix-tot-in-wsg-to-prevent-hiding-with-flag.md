(Note: This is a compilation from various pages on *Wowpedia*.)

Various Changes to Warsong Gulch in Retail
> - Patch 2.4.0 (2008-03-25):
>    Added [Focused Assault](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Focused_Assault) and [Brutal Assault](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Brutal_Assault) debuffs to Warsong Gulch
>     - "When both flags are held, the following debuffs are applied to the flag carriers:"
>           - Focused Assault (Patch 2.4.0)
>              [After both flags have been held for 10 minutes,] The strength of the enemy bears down upon the flag carrier, increasing damage taken by 50%, and limiting movement speed.
>           - Brutal Assault (Patch 2.4.0)
>              [After both flags have been held for 15 minutes,] The strength of the enemy bears down upon the flag carrier, increasing damage taken by 100%, and limiting movement speed.
>        These debuffs remain until the flags are returned, meaning if you drop the flag and someone else picks it up they will have the debuff as well."
> - Patch 3.2.0 (2009-08-04):
>    There is a now a **20 minute timer** on this battleground.
>     - After that time, the team with the most flag captures wins. If this would result in a tie, the team that captured the last flag wins. If neither side has captured a flag, then the game ends in a tie awarding a single mark to all participants.
> - Patch 3.3.0 (2009-12-08):
>    The timer has **25 minutes** now.
> - Patch 4.1.0 (2011-04-26):
>     - The Focused Assault and Brutal Assault buffs have changed.
>           - After **3 minutes** of both teams having the flag, both flag carriers will get Focused Assault, which increases damage taken by 10%.
>           - **Every minute afterward**, an additional stack will be applied to increase damage taken by an additional 10%.
>           - **After 7 minutes**, Brutal Assault will be applied in place of Focused Assault. In additional to the damage debuff, this debuff also caps the player's movement speed at 100%. The damage taken debuff works the same and will add 10% to the debuff up to a maximum of 100% damage taken.
>     - The graveyards have been altered.
>           - The graveyards outside both bases have been lowered in elevation and are no longer on the same plane as the main flag room entrances to either respective base. This means it is no longer possible for players to travel directly from their graveyard to their base or flag room.
> - Patch 5.0.5a Hotfix (2012-10-04):
>    The time limit for Warsong Gulch has been reduced from 25 to 20 minutes.
> - Patch 5.4.7 Hotfix (2014-03-04):
>    Focused Assault debuff is now applied to flag carriers once both team’s flags have been picked up starting at the **1-minute mark** (down from 3 minutes).

So… I mean, while there have been various beneficial modifications made to Warsong Gulch over the years, such as a game timer or debuffs for turtling, it doesn't seem like Blizzard really messed with the *terrain* much.
Unless you include this [vaguely-worded hotfix](https://www.reddit.com/r/classicwow/comments/huzz18/july_20th_hotfix_fix_out_of_bounds_warsong_gulch/) from *Classic*, I mean…
