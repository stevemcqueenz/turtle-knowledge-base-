# Guild wide PvP standing

## Post 77260 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-14T05:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77260#p77260 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently Turtle features standard Vanilla standing with additional rule "you don't lose rank because you lose RP".

Main flaw of Vanilla ranking is in solutions for players to get highest rank. There are two solutions:
 1. Negotiate with top rankers and make "queue" to get rank. First pretendent gets 1st standing, next - 2nd standing etc.
 2. Farm honor 24/7 to be top in standing regardless of other effort.

While server has few rankers speaking same language, people tend to choose solution 1 - negotiation and queue for rank.
But when server gets crowded and you don't have any means to negotiate with everyone, people had to choose solution 2 - insane farm.
If server don't have enough players, then people boost standing by playing 10-19 and ask raiders to get 25 kills to be in standing. It is also negotiation argument "if you will break our queue, then we won't keep standing high enough to reach the rank for breaker".

Some players noted that vanilla honor system forces you to compete with whole your faction.

So proposal is:
Calculate standing for each guild individually.
Lower standing amount to 200 (assuming that max guild capacity is 250).

Guilds automatically organize themselves to make ranking queue. They also need to be big. Best solution is to hire levelers and ask them to participate in low level battlegrounds.
It will also hit chinese farming boosters because they will have to make 200 characters and force them all to get into standing.
I believe it will be easier to identify RMT by those rules. If player just joined and get 1st priority for standing in the guild, then it is suspicious.
