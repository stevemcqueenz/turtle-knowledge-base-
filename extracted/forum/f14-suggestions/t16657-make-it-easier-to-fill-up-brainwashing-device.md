## Post 113806 by Invitebott — 2024-12-22T08:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113806#p113806 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Upon having talents reset for the second time recently, it's kind of annoying to have to refill my brainwashing device with 4 separate talent trees. As best I can tell, this either requires waiting for the new brainwashing device cooldown (which imo was unnecessary to add in the first place) or going to a trainer, and then resetting talents 3 times, which costs 5g each time.

Either we should be able to fill up the talent trees for free, or freely use the 'reset my talents' button, or have the talent refund mechanism only clear one of the specs in the brainwashing device, or.... something. It's just quite inconvenient and sort of annoying that the free talent reset ends up costing 15g.
