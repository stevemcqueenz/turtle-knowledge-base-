While I am not sure why thieves are allowed to walk away with a wide smile and full pockets after such an act of griefing, the main fault lies with the guild bank options.
Setting an amount of withdraws for a specific rank only affects that rank. Every other rank that is higher automatically has unlimited access. This complicates things when the guild has multiple ranks and not simply member->officer structure.
Even with separate tabs restricted to ranks gradually, the higher rank can empty out the lower rank's tab within a blink of an eye.
There is nothing we can do to prevent theft, other than locking all the tabs and making officers take in and hand out items upon request, which could be a paid job at that point.

I hope that this kind of griefing will either be punished, or the bank system given more options per tab. Otherwise this will keep happening and will inevitably drive some people away from the server.

It was not "entirely our fault".

It is not the fault of allowing higher ranks to withdraw, but rather that the limit -can't- be set for individual ranks in the same tab.
If you set a tab withdraw limit to 5/week for rank1, all the higher ranks are automatically without a limit in that tab. This is a huge design oversight and it either makes the bank withdraws too limited(only trusted and officer ranks get access), too impractical(manual hand-outs) or liable to theft due to the aforementioned issue.

The solution to this would be to allow withdraw limit for each rank, not just one.
Not sure if the dev team has the capacity to make this relatively small change at this time, but it would resolve the theft issue and people would have no real reason to complain any longer.
