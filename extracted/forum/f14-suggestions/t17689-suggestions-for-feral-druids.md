# Suggestions for Feral druids.

## Post 120616 by Fugus (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-02-22T00:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120616#p120616 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Given you appear to be trying to create a bleed spec but from what I am told it is still a joke. Here is some things that might help first.

 1) Open Wounds talent now also allows your bleeds to work on bleed immune enemies. The whole setup falls apart if they can't bleed which not only guts the bleed damage but also the buffed claw damage. It will forever be a meme spec if it falls apart on every undead or elemental which makes up a huge portion of the game.

 2) Blood Frenzy talent now also allows your rake ability to apply the Tiger's Fury buff when used. As it stands, I hated Savage Roar in WotLK, I strongly dislike how they turned Tiger's Fury into it. And at least this makes it where you aren't stuck babysitting it all the time while allowing you to use that energy for something else. And then you can still pre-pop it when you are trying to intro ravage something.

 3) Ancient Brutality now also allows anything that buffs your melee attack speed to allow buff your bleed tick speed. This would allow your tiger's fury to buff your bleed ticks as well as haste potions and even the Night Elf Racial.

Now for the more general ones.

 4) Remove the Outdoor requirements from Feral Swiftness. Took Blizzard till WotLK to remove this requirement which felt more like a glorified bugfix than an actual buff since it should have never been a limitation to begin with.

 5) Remove the Outdoor requirements for the PvP set bonus. Ditto for this.

 6) Remove the Outdoor requirements for Entangling Roots. Not like most instances or major PvP locations are indoors or anything.... Can't try to balance around something only to take it away in most meaningful areas which 4-6 is doing just that.

 7) Allow druids to use and loot quest items while in forms.

 8) Allow druids the ability to talk to NPCs in forms.

 9) Allow druids to open chests and doors in forms.

 10) Allow druids to use their activated racial abilities in form.

 11) Change Tiger's Fury and Dash to no longer be removed when leaving Cat form but instead have the buff include the condition of being in cat form to function. Sucks losing the buffs just to heal or drop form for anything. Just suspend the effect while outside of it.

I find it quite weird that I can /kiss the frog and turn into one and I can still use all my abilities, use all my potions and quest items, and talk to any NPC just fine, same as the bird or tree, but a Cat and Bear can't do any of that.

Edit:

 12) Swipe changed to a frontal cone.
