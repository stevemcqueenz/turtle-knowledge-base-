# Don’t let the Scarlet Monastery Raid die!

## Post 157122 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-26T08:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157122#p157122 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey Turtle team & fellow adventurers,

Remember that Scarlet Monastery–style raid that was teased a while back? The one with an actual access questline already in game? It looked absolutely epic — a perfect mix of nostalgia and fresh custom content.

But lately… it feels like the project’s been abandoned. That’s such a loss, because this had the potential to become one of Turtle WoW’s most iconic creations — a true bridge between vanilla spirit and Turtle originality.

Many of us are still super hyped about the idea. The Scarlet lore, the atmosphere, the architecture — everything about it screams “classic endgame magic.” Please don’t let it fade away!

Even if it’s on hold, some updates or small teasers would go a long way. The community wants this, and we’d love to see the project brought back to life.

Scarlet Monastery deserves its raid.
Let’s make it happen.

## Post 157189 by Galendor (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-10-26T16:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157189#p157189 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

IIRC the main problem of this raid was in the assets. Alpha Monastery model wasn't enough for 40 people, and simple size scaling didn't solve the question. So, the raid requires fully custom assets.

Perhaps now, with all the 3D work devs already do, it is pretty possible, but they already changed their content schedule, and there are no signs of Scarlet Citadel on a horizon. Besides, I suppose devs don't want parallels to be drawn with SoD content.

Though I'd like to see it in future, honestly.
