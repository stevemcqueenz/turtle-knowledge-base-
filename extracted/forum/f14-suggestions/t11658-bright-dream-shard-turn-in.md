# Bright Dream Shard turn-in

## Post 77022 by Terraform — 2023-11-12T14:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77022#p77022 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there,

Maybe this was suggested before, but I couldn't find a related topic.

Not so long ago the repeatable quests in Hyjal that reward Small Dream Shard were changed to instead give Bright Dream Shard (worth 3x more). This was a welcome change, thanks!

However, this devalued the material turn-in quests that rewarded Bright Dream Shards (e.g. 1 Mooncloth -> 2 Bright Dream Shard). To balance these turn-ins, the number of shards rewarded should also be tripled to be in line with the repeatables.
This is also apparent when one looks at the AH price of said items (Mooncloth around 18-20G, Bright Dream Shard around  3G). Tripling the reward would be in line with the current economy, and would make the turn-in quests be worth it once again.

Thank you for considering it!
Cheers,

No. Counterpoints:
Traded goods turn ins should always be a less effective away at farming these bright dream shards than simply playing in Hyjal. If Mooncloth becomes a good source of these shards, its price is gonna get tied to them and capped down at whatever the price of these shards is.

For example, These shards fluctuate, let's say they're at 4g some day when hyjal hype dies down and people stop going there. If Mooncloth gives 5 of these shards, it will never ever drop below 20 because the two items are 100% linked to each other in price.
Tying the price of a high supply high demand item to a low supply lowish demand item will ruin the market of that second item.

The shard price is already loosely coupled with all the turn-in prices (probably less after buffing the quest rewards). Currently, the turn-ins are not worth it at all, since the shards can be easily farmed.

The shard price is determined by the time it takes to farm them in relation to farming raw gold to buy them in AH. There is an upper cap on how many shards the eco can produce in a day based on the quest mobs/item respawn time in Hyjal. As long as you can farm 5 (or X, doesn't matter) shards in less time than to "farm" a Mooncloth (or its equivalent gold), they will never be linked, doesn't matter the current AH price.

There is already a turn-in quest that uses Mooncloth for example (although it's a BOP token).
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=8782>

One option could be to make the shards BOP, then no issue with market influence (but poses other issues).
