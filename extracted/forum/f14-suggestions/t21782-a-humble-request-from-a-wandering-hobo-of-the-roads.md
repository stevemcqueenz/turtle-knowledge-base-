# A Humble Request from a Wandering Hobo of the Roads

## Post 150854 by Nanotech2222 — 2025-09-08T19:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150854#p150854 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings, noble developers and fellow adventurers!

As one who wanders the realms without hearth or hall, I often find my weary bones drawn to the soft canvas of tents scattered across the wilds. These marvelous shelters gift me the blessing of rested experience, and I count myself fortunate whenever I stumble upon such havens.

Yet, as I sit under this marvelous cloth contraption and sip on my imaginary stew, a thought arises:

Who are these mysterious benefactors of comfort?
Surely such generosity deserves recognition!

Might we have some manner of mark of ownership upon these tents? Perhaps a fluttering banner stitched with the name of the builder, a crest upon the roof, or even a subtle whisper in the ether every so often: “You feel rested under the care of So-and-So’s tent.”

In this way, we weary hobos may raise our tankards in gratitude to our unseen patrons, and their names may echo in the songs of the road.

Such recognition would not only warm the hearts of wandering folk like me, but also encourage more adventurers to raise their tents proudly, knowing their kindness is seen and appreciated. What better way to make the world feel more alive than with travelers exchanging nods of thanks beneath the same canvas?

And, if I may dare whisper a dream into the firelight, perhaps one day a humble donation box could rest beside such tents. Those of us who find solace there might leave a coin or two in gratitude, as a token of thanks to our hosts.

A small addition, yet one that would give warmth not only to our characters, but to the hearts of those who craft such shelters for others.

With muddy boots and humble thanks,
A Grateful Hobo ![🛖](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6d6.svg)![✨](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2728.svg)
