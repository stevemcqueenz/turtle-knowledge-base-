# 8.2 (BFA) AH overhaul

## Post 101570 by Edyglockenspiel — 2024-09-21T04:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101570#p101570 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The default AH UI is objectively, scientifically, mathematically terrible and should NEVER be used without some form of auction addon like Aux, auctionator, auctioneer, TSM ect. First and foremost, one of the most exploited things people do with it, is posting stuff with overly high buyout prices far above what everyone else is, but with a 1copper bid. This is something I have seen on literally every single server I have been on as well as 2019's classic. This is because you can't sort things by buyout, just bid, so the lowest BID is always at the top, meaning that stack of linen cloth for 100g is shown as the lowest price. This is so well known it was memed about on classic youtube channels. Even Blizzard eventually changed the classic AH to have it be buyout instead.

However, this is of course only one example of why the default AH is such a shit show. What I am suggesting is to add BFA's 8.2 AH overhaul to Turtle. It is more than a few minor changes, it's a massive remaking of the AH from scratch basically. Some features it has is:

Being able to buy an exact amount of stuff, at the lowest buy out price. Lets say I need 6 cloth and there is only a stack of one as the cheapest and then loads of stacks of 20s, and a stack of 7 for more than the cheap stacks of 20s.. In the redone AH, you can just buy 6, buying part of someone's full stack. When their auction falls off without having being bought, they will then get 14 back in the mail even though they put up a stack of 20. And they will get the money for the 6 I bought just like they would normally if they had only posted a stack of 6.

'Commodities' like consumables/regents, trade goods ect are no longer able to be bid on and can ONLY have a buy out. This is ofc to prevent the first paragraph from happening. Normal single stack things like gear or such can still be bid on or have no listed buyout and only have a bid.

This new AH incorporates the 'fast posting' of the aforementioned AH addons. So if I have 10 stacks of something and want to post all of them, or just some of them, or hell even just part of a single stack, I can just post that exact amount in one go easily without having to make multiple auctions. My 10 stacks are 'one' auction. When they fall off the AH, they are all put into the mail in ONE SINGLE MAIL instead of 10 separate ones. (up to 12) Lets say I post 100 cloth, someone buys 42 of them, they get one mail with 42, I get the money and then later when the rest fail to sell I get all the remaining cloth in the mail.

You can favorite items/searches in the revamped AH, just like AH addons. There will be a list when you open the default AH with all the things you have 'stared' allowing you to quickly look for them again without typing it out of shift clicking it's name into the search box ect.

There are tons of other features/changes as well as it coming with a straight up actual visual redo to the original. You can view all of these for FREE on Blizzard's own PTR which anyone can DL and play to test it out yourself to see how exactly the new AH works. But these mentioned here are just the big features I personally would use and like to see.

The way I see it, there are only 3 types of people that would hate seeing this change implemented, those who *do* abuse the bid feature to get people using the default AH to buy stuff they think they are getting for cheap, but instead paying 100s of gold for (and abusing so that their posts are at the top when searched with bid price regardless). Those who are #nochanges from later xpacks (why are you on a custom server then that has goblins and belfies as playable races for vanilla?) and esp changes from BFA of all xpacks. And, those from the actual Turtle team that know this would probably take years to make and not be worth the time in comparison to adding new zones and other actual content. I know barely anything about how game design/coding works, but I agree on that. I can't imagine how long Blizzard were working on that 8.2 AH change, but god it's SO good and is the one thing I miss from retail.

BFA was a objectively, scientifically, mathematically terrible xpack, that added furry fox freaks to the game. But it had the AH change, it's one and only redeeming quality in terms of game systems ect.
