# Improving the hardcore data on the website

## Post 44591 by Hcdeaths — 2023-05-05T15:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44591#p44591 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The hardcore game mode is one of the most popular and challenging features of Turtle WoW. It attracts players who want to test their skills and endurance in a high-risk, high-reward environment. However, the current hardcore page does not reflect the true essence of the hardcore experience. It only shows a static list of players who have survived, died, or become immortal in the hardcore mode. This is not enough to satisfy the curiosity and interest of the hardcore community.

The hardcore page could be revamped by adding more interactivity and data visualization. The page could allow you to filter the list of hardcore players by various criteria such as level, class, race, faction and guild. The page could also display more information about the hardcore deaths such as the date and time, the cause and location and show the zone map with death markers. The page could create a timeline of recent deaths and could provide statistics and charts that show the top causes of deaths, the most dangerous zones and mobs, the survival rates and trends of different classes and races, etc.

These features would not only make the hardcore page more engaging and informative, but also more useful and supportive for the hardcore players. They would be able to learn from the mistakes and successes of others, to plan their strategies and routes more carefully, to avoid unnecessary risks and dangers, and to improve their chances of reaching their goal.

To implement these features, reliable and comprehensive data on hardcore deaths is needed. Currently, this additional data can only be collected from chat logs or addons by a third-party source. There is a community based website supplying this data however the features are limited and it may not be available in the future. A more reliable way of obtaining this data directly from the Turtle WoW website is needed. This would also benefit the community addon developers who could use this data to create more useful and innovative tools for the hardcore players.

Revamping the hardcore page would greatly enhance the quality and appeal of the Turtle WoW website. It would show the commitment and appreciation to the hardcore community who are loyal and passionate about the game. It would also attract more players who are interested in trying out the hardcore mode or learning more about it and would create more opportunities for interaction and collaboration amongst players.
