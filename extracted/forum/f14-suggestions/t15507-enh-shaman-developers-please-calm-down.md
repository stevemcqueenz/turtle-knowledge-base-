## Post 106091 by 987004590 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-03T06:31:00Z

I guess you got completely lost because of the unexpected presentation of the enh-shaman. Now you're too busy fixing the problem to know where to go. So please calm down and carefully consider the direction of the enh-shaman before making any changes.

My personal advice: Shamans don't keep us locked into a cycle of skill.  Almost removes mana cost and limits DPS by adding skill CD. And almost removed the skill CD, limiting DPS through mana consumption. Which path should a shaman choose?  I think we should choose the latter , So I ventured to propose a change:

- Lightning Strike as an attack rather than a spell, more WF proc chance, perhaps consuming 50% of total max mana. No CD.

- Storm Strike, as an attack rather than a spell, more WF proc chance, perhaps consuming 50% of total max mana, no CD.
A possible problem with this change is that there will be one shot in PVP.  But enhanced shamans are not paladins, they are really difficult to get close to the enemy and have no ability to escape from the enemy. Nor is it a holy damage without regard to defense. And the huge mana cost makes it unable to consistently kill enemies, so I don't think this change is OP.

The purpose of having LS/SS instead of just one of them is to have one as a physical attack and one as a natural attack against different targets.

Mana regeneration can use the following mechanism by adding a talent: The more mana you currently have, the faster the mana regeneration. Specific values need to be tested.
