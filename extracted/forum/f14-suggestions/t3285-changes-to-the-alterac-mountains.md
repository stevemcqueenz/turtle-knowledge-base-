# Changes to the Alterac Mountains

## Post 20057 by Isabelletexmex — 2022-07-16T19:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20057#p20057 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have always thought that Blizzard could have done more with the vanilla version of the Alterac Mountains as there was plenty of lore from Warcraft 3 that was never touched upon in vanilla wow

therefore i would like to suggest the addition of 2 new Sub zones within the Alterac Mountains known as the Blackrock Valley and outskirts of Dalaran as well as the addition of the Dungeons of Dalaran from Warcraft 3 the frozen throne as a new Dungeon and Quests for these new areas will be available for players to pick up from Tarren Mill and Southshore

The Blackrock valley- the location from the Human and Undead campagins of Warcraft 3 and contains the Ruined Blackrock Villages Destroyed by Arthas and Kel'Thuzad as well as the Demon Gate used to contact lord Archimonde

the entire zone is controlled by the Undead Scourge who are lead by non other then Jubei'Thos who was raised into undeath by The Lich king

all of the Blackrock orcs who fought the Scourge have been Raised as Undead and can be found within their various ruined villages
