# Guild Camps (Different than Guild Houses)

## Post 100602 by Spirittap — 2024-09-04T10:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100602#p100602 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, I love the idea of guild houses and I know that Turtle is no longer making guild houses.

That being said, it would be great if it were possible to create "guild camps" that guilds could set their guild tabard hearth to. There could be 8 or so around the world that could be as simple as a tent and some little decorations that any guild can use for their tabard.

I thought this might be a good idea since it feels somewhat bad looking at your guild tabard that says you can teleport to your guild house but your guild doesn't have one!

Just a fun idea. Thanks for all your hardwork Turtle team!

I see guild houses as WoD style Garrisons with own map accesible only by Guild Hearthstone with low cooldown (like 15 minutes). Guilds could pay with gold to access Bank, Auction House and Blacksmith/Alchemy Table etc. Using the Guild Hearthstone while in this location could teleport you back to the previous location (or nearest town).
