I know a lot of people don't feel Night Elves have been done justice with their racials, myself included. I propose two things which I don't think will massively impact the balance of the game, but will significantly improve the viability of Night Elves. Both these changes were eventually done in retail:

Base racial:
 -Improved Stealth

Priest Racial:
 -No channel on Starshards but acts as an instant cast DoT

Additionally, something which might be good but I'm unsure if viable to produce since this was never implemented:

In addition to the current effects of Elune’s Grace(priest racial), it also reduces the cast range of incoming spells. Now I can see how this might be hard to implement, but it directly correlated with the idea of Elune’s Grace to allow the priest to play as an aggressive support.

## Post 43465 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-04-27T17:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43465#p43465 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Wotlk shadowmeld increased the cooldown 10s -> 120s but allowed it to be used in combat.

In PVE, when melded, its a temproary threat drop like you received blessing of protection or used a LIP, so enemies will go attack somewhere else, but you will still keep your threat when you exit shadowmeld and so they might come running back after you. This is used as a dps to keep yourself alive as a class who cant dip threat, and as a tank as a very last resort "mit" that more likely than not will just get other people killed instead.

In PvP, a well timed meld can break a cast in progress, as well as dodge an incoming projectile like hunter shots, shadowbolts, fireballs etc.

This singlehandedly brought nelfs from awful to very good when it comes to picking classes. Shadowmeld is still like this in dragonflight and it is beloved, though ofc threat is way way less of an issue in wotlk onwards.
