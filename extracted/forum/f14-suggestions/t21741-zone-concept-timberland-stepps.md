Timberland Stepps
---
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/iuqEHNm.jpeg)

There is actually a massive unused mountain south of hyjal I think most people aren't even aware of.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/CPjFBbH.jpeg)

And I think it could be a really cool location for a big furbolg themed zone. Where you climb up the side of the mountain with a winding path of large cliffs and massive waterfalls. It be the sorta aesthetic they did in grizzly hills with lots of rapids and massive pine trees and bear folks. It's a really nice vibe I think is missing from classic so it would feel pretty different from other zones. Also being able to properly climb up into hyjal more directly would be cool compared to doing a large very gradual loop to the top. Would also make sense to have yet another entrance here to timbermaw hold since I think the next thing the dev's are working on is the big unground zone.
