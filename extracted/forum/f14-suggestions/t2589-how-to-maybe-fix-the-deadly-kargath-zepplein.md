And I've wanted to make a suggestion about the recent numerous gencodies of the Kargath zeppelin and how to get rid of them.
From testing the zeppelin (standing in one spot on it) I've come to realize that the zeppelin only kills you when entering Eastern Kingdoms from Kalimdor as opposed to entering Kalimdor back from Kargath.
It seems that the zeppelin only commits a murder on you whenever you load too slowly upon entering Badlands from Orgrimmar. Using a kind of buff on yourself or slowfall specifically slows down the process enough, for me at least, to consistently kill me everytime, while not doing anything at all let's me survive the geneocide zeppelin but deals about 28.3 % of health points to me.
Though I don't have any proof of it, I believe blizzard might have ran into this problem themselfs. It is highly possible that all the zeppelins which go accros continents (a loading screen) are always specifically made to fly in a straight line for a while, after loading in into a new continent, to avoid murdering players by fall damage due to loading problems. Just like when the Kargath zeppelin doesn't kill you after loading back into Kalimdor from Badlands.
So if that was done also to the Kargath zeppelin upon entry to Badlands the issue should be fixed of course. Though it's probably hard to make a new zeppelin flight path which I commend the turtle team for doing, the only way to fix this problem in a simple way it's probably to do it... this way.
