# Improved racials for the high elves

## Post 161783 by Familymaker — 2025-12-06T08:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161783#p161783 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So I'm a big fan of the aesthetic of the high elves, and I love the direction that Turtle Wow has gone into with regards to their lore and overall gameplay. However, I do feel that compared to the other races their racials are a tad weak. As such, I wanted to make some suggestions for changes, to see what people thought of them:

 **Swiftness of the Rangers (could be renamed to "Graceful") - passive**

 *Agility increased by 3% and Intelligence increased by 2%, or Agility increased by 2% and Intelligence increased by 3%.* (Humans and gnomes have +5% on a single stat, so this way they will get a total of +5% as well, but divided)

 **Quel'dorei Meditation - 3 min cooldown**

 *Generates 10% of your maximum mana, 20 rage, or 50 energy over 5 seconds. Mana generated this way is increased by 150% while out of combat. The casting time is 1.5 seconds for classes with mana, or 0.5 seconds for rogues and warriors. Warriors are considered in combat for the duration.* (making it up to par with other racials)
