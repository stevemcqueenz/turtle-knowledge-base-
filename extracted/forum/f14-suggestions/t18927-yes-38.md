*"If you have a suggestion, post it on the forum!"*

Soooooo, welcome to my ted talk, below is a list of changes I’d like to see.
 - These suggestions come in different shapes and sizes, ranging from small tweaks to broader reworks.
 - I highly recommend checking out the **PVP and Book sections** in particular.
 - If any of these ideas seem worthy of their own discussion, feel free to open a new post about them!

PS: The post was written in january, therefore some can be outdated.

---
 ****Miscellaneous****

 1. **Althalas should have a reagent vendor** at the bazaar for convenience and thematic consistency.

 2. An unlockable **transportation route between Ironforge Airfields and Bael Hardul (Stonetalon Mountains)** could be added, gated behind Ironforge reputation or a quest chain. This would reward players who reach exalted with Ironforge or encourage more players to visit this overlooked quest hub.

 3. Please **add a direct flight path between Mudspocket and Brackenwall Village** in Dustwallow Marsh. Flying through the Crossroads doesn't make sense. Alternatively, create a quest with new lore to explain why a direct route isn't possible.

 4. **Finish Ravasaur Trainers**, which would give more purpose to Ungoro and quests for horde.

 5. **Change Mark of the Syndicate to a white item** to avoid accidental sale (It’s for rogues disguise).

 6. Consider **incorporating rogue disguises as part of trash or boss mechanics** into future raid content.

 7. Consider adding **additional graveyard to Azshara**, as the difficult shoreline already makes navigation frustrating, with death imposing an unusually high penalty.

 8. Consider **redrawing the graveyard boundaries in Hyjal**, as dying at the bottom of the zone results in an excessively time consuming run **or make the runestone useable as ghost**.

 9. **Desolace map** could benefit from some visual updates to bring out its potential, such as adding greenery, landmark for the reef section near the horde camp... while staying true to its original theme. Hosting a contest with the many talented individuals in the community could be a fun.

 10. Improve **Black Morass sand drops** by lowering the drop chance but making each drop yield 3-4 sands at once. This keeps the need for rolling intact but reduces the constant interruptions from frequent individual rolls.

 11. **Explore the idea of adding a slow mode to World chat**, limiting players to one message per minute or similar. With high population, chat becomes chaotic, and while some suggest heavy moderation or alternative channels, players would still use all of them, making those solutions ineffective. A slow mode would be a simple way to reduce spam and improve readability without restricting access. Not sure how feasible this is technically. What do you think?

 ****PVP****

 12. **The launcher should recommend the HonorSpy addon** to raise awareness and give players a minimal understanding of the complex vanilla pvp system.

 13. **Azshara Crater could be repurposed into a Temple of Kotmogu style battleground**, as the original pve oriented concept, like similarly AV were designed without the benefit of years of iterations and experience. Lorewise, the battleground would be simply a strategic location for elves and orcs, set within ancient elvish ruins tied to Azshara’s ley lines, perfectly aligning with the orb mechanics.

 14. Holy damage has traditionally been a unique feature to paladins and priests due to its lack of resistance mechanics, however, with CC2, smite priests have become a more viable pvp spec. If they were to become too dominant, one possible **balancing factor to keep in mind could be the introduction of +25 Holy Resistance to Flask of Chromatic Resistance** - a lesser used consumable. While it's a minor mitigation, it could serve as a subtle counterplay tool without significantly altering class balance.

 15. The **cost of the Brainwashing Device could be reduced based on Blood Ring reputation**, following the same logic as the rank 11 mount, which compensates pvpers for their time investment. This change would also provide an additional incentive to do Blood Ring.

 16. The vanilla pvp system is good in general but makes ranking a frustrating experience, particularly due to the necessity of pool boosting. A potential way to mitigate this without drastic changes is **to have level 60 characters count with some kind of weight, such as 1.5 per character in the pool**, reducing the need for artificial inflation. The level 60-only restriction ensures that this mechanic cannot be exploited through pool boosting with lowlevel characters (no scaling). **Another alternative is the use of pvp towers or Temu's token system, where engaging in these world pvp objectives would increases the pool size rather than awarding direct honor.** This would benefit all rankers while avoiding potential exploitation, vulnerabilities, or disruptive paradigm shifts that such features might introduce.

 17. Over time, I’ve seen countless complaints about being pvp flagged, with many blaming warmode as the root cause. However, in my experience, the real issue is a lack of clear feedback on when and why a player becomes flagged. When I first joined the server, I had no idea about glyphs, yet I found myself attacked occasionally just for healing or buffing others. With the general fear of being flagged, players have stopped buffing each other, contributing to a shift in community interaction - something that was also lamented in a forum post. Instead of blaming warmode, I believe the real fix lies in better UX/UI feedback. However, rather than adding a new ui element, my suggestion is a simple chat notification. **When a player is flagged or unflagged, a timestamped message should appear in chat, clearly informing them of the status change.** This would provide better awareness, helping players avoid unexpected pvp encounters and reducing frustration without altering gameplay mechanics.

 18. In vanilla, pvp progression is primarily tied to ranking, but the **Arathi Basin questline is an underdeveloped feature that could serve as the foundation for an extended pvp quest system.** Rather than being an alternative to ranking, this would act as an achievement-like progression, providing structured pvp goals beyond raw honor grinding. Followup quests could be introduced for Arathi Basin, along with new questlines for Warsong Gulch and Sunnyglade Valley (the standard battlegrounds). Objectives would include winning by a large margin, capturing bases, killing 100 players, winning multiple matches, and other battlefield specific milestones - similar to later expansion achievement systems, but structured as a progressive questline. Instead of a single "Win 1000 Matches" achievement, the questline would be broken into smaller steps (e.g., 3 separate "Win 100 Matches" quests), ensuring steady progression since quests, unlike achievements, cannot be completed in parallel. Some quests would reward reputation, recipes (such as a barricade recipe below), +1000 honor tokens (usable when extra honor is needed) and a title as final reward.

 ****19. PVP gearing****

The elephant in the room when it comes to pvp is gear disparity - a hot topic as postnaxx content rolls out. **My solution? Make battlegrounds restricted by item level.**

 **How Would It Work?**
If a player’s item level exceeds the battleground cap, they cannot queue. If they swap to higher itemlevel gear while inside, they are kicked with a deserter debuff.

For example, if the item level cap is 78, there are two primary ways to calculate this restriction, each with pros and cons:

 1. absolute ilvl cap (78 × 15 = 1170 total item level)
✔ simple and clear
✖ doesn’t properly account for weapons, disadvantaging players with dual-wield/mainhand-offhand/shield setups

 2. average ilvl cap (total item level divided by used slots ≤ 78)
✔ accounts for weapon differences fairly
✖ creates an awkward meta where players might intentionally leave certain slots empty to lower their average item level

![➡️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/27a1.svg) the best approach: a hybrid system

To balance both issues, the best solution would be an absolute item level cap, but with weapons contributing as an average rather than a hard limit. This avoids the weapon disadvantage of the first system while preventing the empty-slot meta of the second.

(Additionally, this would require a UI update in the character tab, clearly displaying absolute item level to help players track their eligibility.)

 **Why this is the longterm solution?**

Instead of constantly buffing pvp gear every time a new raid is released - a system that is not sustainable - or doing nothing at all, which would cause pvp gear to gradually become irrelevant as pve content progresses, this approach removes the need for artificial stat inflation.
 - no need for new pvp exclusive stats like resilience
 - pvp gear (including reputation rewards etc) can instead be designed to offer better stats relative to their item level
 - rank 12-14 gear could receive minor stat buffs, but the main balancing factor would be item level itself

 **The ripple effect**

 **This system wouldn’t just balance battlegrounds - it would revitalize other aspects of the game:**
 - players would need to optimize gear within a set limit, making quest rewards, crafted gear, and reputation items more relevant
 - consequently rural areas and trade would become richer, as players seek out diverse gearing options
 - higher item level pieces would still be valuable, but players could balance them out with lower ilvl gear, creating more diverse builds rather than just stacking raid bis

 ****Questing****

 20. **The Elemental Invasion event is often overlooked, but it could be made more relevant**. For example: When the invasion begins, Hydraxian Overlord envoys would appear in the impacted zones, warning of abnormal elemental activity. Players can accept a onetime event quest to defeat an invasion site boss or collect special elemental essences. In return, they would receive a Minor Quintessence (a dousing item with limited duration). This update makes the event more meaningful while providing a practical reward (inspired by a forum post mentioning how some casual players enter Molten Core gambling on having enough douses but not always ensuring they do).

 21. **Consider adding quests where the reward (a book) provides insights into boss tactics for custom raids.**

 22. For future content **make use of the Extra-Dimensional Ghost Revealer** to interact with ghost npcs beyond dungeon set quests. Potential uses include: quests that address the lack of horde content and retell old stories, seasonal events like Winter Veil...

 23. Add breadcrumb **quest for disguise to the new factions** as well (goblin, helf).

 24. **Add a breacrumb quest in Azshara for the alliance and horde quest hubs** (Talrendis Point -> Flaxwhisker Front, Valormok -> Bloodfist Point) to guide players unfamiliar with those custom contents.

 25. **The quest "Identifying the Brood" should have shared loot for the required items.** (A quest in Dustwallow Marsh that explores black dragon lore and Onyxia's influence.) Players must collect 15-15 quest items with a 25% drop rate from elite mobs. The current mechanics make this one of the most frustrating quests in the game. Grouping drastically increases the time required, as the quest items are not shared loot. Completing this in a group can take over 90 minutes of nonstop farming (3 players + killing a mob every 30 sec), making it a punishment to work together. The quest is typically done alongside Army of the Black Dragon, which already tests the players' strength by sending them into a den.

 26. **Add a tooltip OR duration limit to Silithid Goo**, as this item is annoying due to its unclear purpose, leading to confusion and unnecessary inventory clutter. It’s only used to trigger an event (Hive in the Tower quest) at the top of the tower and is not needed afterward.

 27. Currently, in the Barrens, there are only 7 Razormane Geomancers available, while The Disruption Ends quest requires killing 8, leading to tension between players in this lowlevel zone (consider hardcores as well). **Increasing the number of Razormane Geomancers to 10-12 or reducing the required kills to 5-4** would improve the overall player experience.

 28. In the Barrens, Sunscale Feathers drop from raptors and are used for the The Angry Scytheclaws quest, where players need 3 feathers for the nests. However, any leftover feathers remain in the bag after completing the quest, causing unnecessary inventory clutter - a long-standing annoyance noted in comments for over 20 years. **Limiting the number of Sunscale Feathers a player can carry to 3** would resolve this issue and eliminate confusion.

 ****Professions****

 29. **Skinning could provide a reputation boost** by dropping soulbound items that grant modest reputation gains to specific factions, giving it meta relevance without disrupting the economy. For example:
 - high level skinning: Timbermaw Hold, Wintersaber Trainers, *Ravasaur Trainers*: neutral to friendly
 - low and middle level skinning: Revantusk Trolls, Wildhammer Clan, Capitals: friendly to honored.

 30. **Jewelcrafting could make use of Un'goro Crystals** such as:
 - being exchanged for unique recipe unlocks (low economic demand)
 - or crafting a scroll that grants a temporary +7 to all stats, similar to enchanting scrolls (high economic demand)
 - or just simply serving as crafting material (moderate economic demand)

 31. **Recipe: Blood Sausage should be accessible to horde players during leveling.** This fits horde lore as a staple orc and troll food and would give purpose to the frequently dropped Boar Intestines during leveling, which currently go to vendors.

 32. Expand the Survival secondary profession by **allowing players to craft Boat Repair Kits, which could be used to operate boats (pre-placed) in areas like Azshara and the islands of Stranglethorn Vale.**. These boats would function similarly to 30-45 second custom flights, but only on water, making Survival more relevant at higher levels by providing utility beyond just tents. Each use would consume one Boat Repair Kit.

 33. **Make Bright Campfire distinct:** consuming wood but replacing flint and tinder with a Survival Kit, which could also be used for other crafting. This avoids other ideas that could be problematic or exploitable, such as rested xp spam or food related buffs.

 34. A potential **addition to Survival could be a Barricade** as pvp utility. With 3k health, attackable by anyone, it blocks players but allows npcs to pass, offering tactical utility, 10 minute cooldown to prevent overuse, maybe battleground restriction as well.

 35. Explore **the idea of allowing the Survival profession to store quests as items, similar to how some items begin quests.** This would let players convert an active quest into a physical item, freeing up quest log space while keeping progress intact. I'm not sure if this is technically feasible, but since the old client’s quest limit is deeply hardcoded, this could serve as a workaround, while also providing a practical new function for survival.

---
 ****Books****

 36. The game already features various books, ranging from quest items to lore tomes and practical guides (pvp ranks, ungoro crystals, ...). However, these **books could be made more accessible** and useful for players by implementing one of the following ideas:
 - Library access: Players could browse collections for free or purchase copies for personal use at Ironforge and Undercity
 - Books tab: similar to the Mount or Toy tab, when players acquire a book, it gets "added" to their tab, allowing them to reread it anytime without needing inventory space

 37. **New Book: Guide to Azeroth's Leveling Zones**
 - a comprehensive guide breaking the fourth wall slightly
 - list every zone, its recommended level range, and what it offers (herbs, ores, or other notable resources, like fishes or the dream shards)
 - this book would be an invaluable tool for players navigating through the mysteries of Azeroth

 38. The **Survival profession could let players copy lore books (found around the world) for trade or personal use**, adding another practical use for the profession while creating a collector’s market for Azeroth’s written knowledge.

>  If a player’s item level exceeds the battleground cap, they cannot queue. If they swap to higher itemlevel gear while inside, they are kicked with a deserter debuff.
>
>  For example, if the item level cap is 78, there are two primary ways to calculate this restriction, each with pros and cons:
>
>  1. absolute ilvl cap (78 × 15 = 1170 total item level)
>  ✔ simple and clear
>  ✖ doesn’t properly account for weapons, disadvantaging players with dual-wield/mainhand-offhand/shield setups
>
>  2. average ilvl cap (total item level divided by used slots ≤ 78)
>  ✔ accounts for weapon differences fairly
>  ✖ creates an awkward meta where players might intentionally leave certain slots empty to lower their average item level
>
>  ![➡️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/27a1.svg) the best approach: a hybrid system
>
>  To balance both issues, the best solution would be an absolute item level cap, but with weapons contributing as an average rather than a hard limit. This avoids the weapon disadvantage of the first system while preventing the empty-slot meta of the second.
>
>  (Additionally, this would require a UI update in the character tab, clearly displaying absolute item level to help players track their eligibility.)
>
>  **Why this is the longterm solution?**
>
>  Instead of constantly buffing pvp gear every time a new raid is released - a system that is not sustainable - or doing nothing at all, which would cause pvp gear to gradually become irrelevant as pve content progresses, this approach removes the need for artificial stat inflation.
>  - no need for new pvp exclusive stats like resilience
>  - pvp gear (including reputation rewards etc) can instead be designed to offer better stats relative to their item level
>  - rank 12-14 gear could receive minor stat buffs, but the main balancing factor would be item level itself
>
>  **The ripple effect**
>
>  **This system wouldn’t just balance battlegrounds - it would revitalize other aspects of the game:**
>  - players would need to optimize gear within a set limit, making quest rewards, crafted gear, and reputation items more relevant
>  - consequently rural areas and trade would become richer, as players seek out diverse gearing options
>  - higher item level pieces would still be valuable, but players could balance them out with lower ilvl gear, creating more diverse builds rather than just stacking raid bis
