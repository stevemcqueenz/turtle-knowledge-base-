# The 1 Ring not obtainable for Alliance & Not implemented correct

## Post 43682 by Cuthroatelitist — 2023-04-28T21:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43682#p43682 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I recently started a Hardcore character on the Alliance side and decided to level my fishing in Stormwind and hopefully bag myself an amazing low level ring "The 1 Ring"

However upon reviewing Turtle WOW Database, its only obtainable from Undercity, this was also verified by a GM.

When you compare this to WoWHead Classic, where it states the ring is obtainable from  Stormwind City, The Barrens, Thunder Bluff, Darkshore, Orgrimmar, Silverpine Forest and Undercity it leads myself and a GM who i discussed the matter with to think it is an oversight

I also know from playing Vanilla and Classic WOW that this item is specifically designed to be obtainable from several major cities and most level 10-20 zones

In its current state this item is simply unobtainable for an Alliance Player due to being flagged for PVP / Attacked by city guards when entering Undercity which is a real shame as anybody who plays HC knows just how great it feels to progress your character with world drops, quest rewards and such alike. This ring would certainly be one of those items that a low level player would be thrilled to find, i know i would be!

I am asking that the Dev Team look into this issue and potentially correct it or clarify that it is an intentional change please. Im well aware its not a major issue but just one of the small things that would be a great and well received fix!
