# Keeping the sense of constant progression- Clout Levels

## Post 95853 by Ivanturtle — 2024-05-28T02:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95853#p95853 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For me, I always taper off WoW after 60 because killing mobs basically no longer feels good. The sense of constantly moving forward is the main thing I've always loved about WoW, and I'm left questioning life once that's taken away.

Thus I propose the Clout Level system: Once you hit level 60, your xp bar doesn't go away. Now, your xp goes towards clout levels, starting at 1 and going all the way up to like 999 or something. When you get a level, you get a free chunk of ~2000 (number is arbitrary) Reputation points that you can throw at any rep grind you want.

Rep grinds always made me dread playing, and this solves that in large part for me. Now instead of just grinding furbolgs for hours, you can passively acquire a bunch of rep just by playing the game, doing the content you actually want to do.

As Turtle has greatly expanded WoW's reputation system and plans to do even more with it, I feel that a change like this might even be necessary. It's great that reputations are actually getting content behind them, but the more you include, the more daunting it is to open the reputation window and see everything at Neutral. If you have a decent amount of rep steadily rolling in just for playing, it's much less of a problem, and more of a fun, slow crawl towards getting everything leveled, in the true spirit of Turtle.
