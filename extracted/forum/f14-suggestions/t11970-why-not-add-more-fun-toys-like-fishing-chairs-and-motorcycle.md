## Post 92454 by Penfold — 2024-04-09T16:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92454#p92454 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please, for the sanity of 99.99% of the player base, DO NOT ADD MOTORCYCLES into the game.  Having that horrible, horrible sound at every mailbox UTTERLY DESTROYS IMMERSION.  I remember when it happened in Retail — it was TERRIBLE.

The real world is already drowning in the sound of vehicular traffic.  We DO NOT want to import that particular modern nonsense into the game.  This is supposed to be a medieval fantasy setting, after all, not "Weekend at Walmart".

The Auction House - Mailbox - Bank area is already very, very noisy...  If anything, the focus should be on introducing tools that allow players to individually, reliably and persistently remove the most obnoxious sounds and models from the game.

That white cloud rainbow mount thingy that (thankfully) a small number of players have is also immersion breaking and, frankly, ridiculously out-of-place.  WoW isn't "Hello Kitty Island Adventures" and TWoW shouldn't head in that direction.  IMHO.

TL;DR:  Toys are fine, as long as they don't force attention-grabbing and immersion-breaking audio/visual effects upon the population.  As a rule of thumb, if it's screaming "LOOK AT ME, LOOK AT ME!" then it probably shouldn't be allowed.
