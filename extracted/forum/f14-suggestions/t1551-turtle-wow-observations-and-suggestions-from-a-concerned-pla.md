# Turtle WoW Observations and Suggestions from a concerned player.

## Post 9199 by Kreizen — 2021-01-21T16:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9199#p9199 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Howdy pal’s, Lord Kreizen here! I started playing here recently as the server is widely recommended on the internet. I decided to make a goblin mage and after seeing that the goblin racials are completely useless for almost all classes that can make one I noticed something **far worse**.

In my brief period in the starting zone I saw a bunch of players create a character, play for a couple of minutes and log off before even hitting level 5, never to be seen again.
It seems that **many people are interested in trying the server** but something is making them give up on playing almost right away!

Since I'm tough as nails and won’t be stopped by seeing low numbers on the /who list I powered through the starting zone and was delighted to see well-written **custom quests** all around Durotar. **Great job!** It kept getting better and better just to be confronted with a **bog standard talent tree at level 10**, which prompted me to write the first forum post I've ever made. Because I love the idea of Turtle WoW and I want to see it succeed!

I think the number one priority of the staff should be **new player retention.** I put together a list of things that I would love to see in my journey to level 60.

My low level suggestions to make Turtle-WoW **irresistible** are:

 1.**Join /world automatically** when you create a character. Seeing an active chat as a new player is an underrated but important detail!

 2.**Improving Talents!** Making tanks more fun to play would be a great start. I bet everyone reading this has at least one great idea on what to add to their favorite class. I know I'm full of ideas! The goal is to be subtle and not just add overpowered abilities that trivializes content.

 3.**Talent Points from the moment you hit level 2**. Everyone loves Talent Points! So why not add 9 more. Combined with adding new talents that low level players can glance over within the first 15 minutes of logging in would really help keeping new players hooked.

 4.**Reorder the levels you get spells at.** Small things like giving hunters Wing Clip at level 1 (lvl 12 now) so they can actually play there class properly would be fantastic changes!

 5.**Grouping should be rewarded.** Sadly everybody now knows grouping outside of dungeons is not optimal 90% of the time. How sad!  I would suggest adding a 10% BONUS to XP from mobs per group member (up to 150% total) to reverse this mindset.

 6.**Make professions better.** Imagine the Auction House flooded with items because gathering or crafting an item now gives you a small amount of XP! Adding some well itemized green gear to low level crafting professions would really make the game better. This could be expanded to adding amazing epic items at max level for the most dedicated crafters!

 7.**Cross-Faction BG’s.** Separating the player base in 2 factions that can’t properly interact with one another is in my opinion one of Blizzard’s biggest failures. I love that Turtle WoW gives us the ability to group and talk to the opposite faction and I would love to see Turtle WoW go one step further and remove faction imbalance from pvp as well!

 8.**Sitting in combat makes your spirit regenerate mana.** This is just a fun idea but would add some extra spice to the lives of our mana starved friends.

PS: We all know there's only one way to increase the number of tanks.. And it’s to make Thunderfury ridiculously OP and dangle it in front of Warriors and Paladins like a carrot on a stick. I suggest a 25% proc rate and adding crit chance to the proc. Paladin and druid tanking could so easily be fixed with a few Blizz-like talent tweaks!

Thanks for reading =) Do you agree or disagree with any of the suggestions? let me know what you think!
PS:If a dev sees this and would like a talk about any of the suggestions e-mail me!
