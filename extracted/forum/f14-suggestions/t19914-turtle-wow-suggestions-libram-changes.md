# Turtle WoW Suggestions: Libram Changes

## Post 135963 by Wez0 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-16T16:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135963#p135963 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Libram Suggestion** ![📚](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4da.svg):
The current "unique" restriction and high drop rates for Librams (e.g., Libram of Resilience, Voracity, Rapidity) create issues like inventory clutter and chat spam from players trading them. Here’s a fix:
- **Remove "Unique" Restriction** ![🚫](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6ab.svg): Allow players to carry multiple Librams of the same type in their inventory. This reduces the need to spam trade channels to offload extras and makes farming less frustrating. *Why?* The unique limit feels outdated and forces players to sell or destroy Librams, clogging chat.[]([https://www.ownedcore.com/forums/world- ... focus.html](https://www.ownedcore.com/forums/world-of-warcraft/world-of-warcraft-guides/213-farm-libram-of-focus.html))
- **Reduce Drop Rate** ![📉](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4c9.svg): Lower the drop rate for Librams (e.g., from ~1% to ~0.5%) in places like Blackrock Depths, Blackrock Spire, and Dire Maul. *Why?* High drop rates flood the market, driving down prices and encouraging spam. A lower drop rate makes Librams feel rarer and more rewarding, aligning with Turtle WoW’s classic vibe.[]([https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-clas ... ram-drops/](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/news/season-of-discovery-hotfixes-for-july-31-increased-libram-drops/))[](
