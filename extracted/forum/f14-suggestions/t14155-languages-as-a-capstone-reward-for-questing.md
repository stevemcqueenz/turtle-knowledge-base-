# Languages as a capstone reward for questing

## Post 96483 by Mari — 2024-06-07T01:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96483#p96483 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was leveling a gnome and suddenly a realization hit me. I couldn't understand a dwarven NPC because they were speaking dwarven. Normally that thought would end there, but it made me pause because I realized this was ALSO the gnome starting zone. It's a bit silly, isn't it? Gnomes and dwarves live together but none of them understand each other's language?

And that got me thinking. What if you could learn other races' languages by completing all the quests in the zones primarily occupied by that race? Obviously that would faction lock languages since a lot of quests are faction locked, meaning you'd only be able to learn alliance or horde languages.

It just seems like a really neat little reward that would prove your dedication to questing and also give you a small roleplaying reward. And it would be thematically appropriate as a reward for diving headfirst into that culture through it's quests.

I don't know. It just seems like one of those neat little things that add extra features to the server without meaningfully changing the underlying vanilla gameplay experience.
