# Make the new daily quests more rewarding

## Post 23793 by Azeron — 2022-08-28T09:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23793#p23793 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I absolutely love the High Elf and Troll daily areas added to the game, however having the few available daily quests reward just silver feels rather underwhelming.

It would be nice if the quest rewarded something basic to help with leveling, even something as simple as a choice between mana or healing potions, or even food and drink and well as at least a small boost to the 5 copper reward for the lowest level daily!
