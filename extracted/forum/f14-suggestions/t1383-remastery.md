# Remastery

## Post 8436 by Unangwata (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-11-10T15:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8436#p8436 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Once you reach level 60, you would be given choice to go back to lev 1 and start as new class. Benefit of doing so would be passive effect that you receive after 'remastering'. For warrior it would be "former warrior" etc.
Such effect would be different for every class, for example:
 -Former Warrior +10 str
 -Former Rogue +10agi
 -Former Hunter +5agi +5int
etc

These are just examples, you could come up with different ideas for benefit of being former class.
Also there could be permanent buff visual effect icon (in buff zone only) so everybody could see your previous classes.
You could remaster as many times as you want, however you would have to chose carefully your remastery path because you can never return to your former occupation.

A means to keep rerolling your character and keep playing for max efficiency (and show-off).
Side note: all quests would have to be reset but you keep your gear.
