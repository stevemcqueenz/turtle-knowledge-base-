# MOBA Battleground

## Post 47213 by Oommss — 2023-05-24T04:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47213#p47213 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This will be a long and crazy text and the idea is probably never going to get implemented anyway, but it is a fun one and I would like to share it with you, if only to fan fic about it a bit!

I have always been huge fan of MOBA games, I have played especially League of Legends and Heroes of the Storm for very long time. For me this type of game is refreshing because it is easy to pick up, is fast paced and can be played casually. They offer a lot of room for player expression, creativity and teamwork. And I think, there could be some good fundamentals in WOW to implement MOBA Battleground!

The basics are simple: Map with 3 lanes on which waves of minions of two enemy factions constantly go against each other. These factions fight through enemy minions and their structures to reach their core and destroy it. Whoever destroys the core first, wins.
There are several players on each side (could be traditional 5, or even just 4- I hate junglers hehe). They help minions destroy enemy structures and receive resources from the enemy minions (by being nearby or killing them) to grow stronger. Killing enemy player gives more resources. These resources are used to purchase items/spells/talents to increase your strength and thus giving you better chance of winning the game.
There could be jungle area between these lanes which could have some neutral monsters for those bonus resources, buffs and even some nice PvE action!

Now the main question is, how do we implement this into the RPG game? Surely we cannot simply put our characters in there, that would cause inbalance. And ofc, MOBA is typical for being simple, each character core strength is auto attack which some 3 or 4 spells to be cast occasionally, so no possibility to have whole wow class toolkit.

Well, my idea is we play as different characters! I imagine it such as this: you log into the Battleground queue for MOBA (could be one queue for all lvls 1-60), you get invited, you spawn on the map in your base and before BG starts you have to talk to NPC in your base (could be your faction core/boss actually) and this guy will enable you to choose from one of the characters which you are going to TRANSFORM into for whole BG!
We would have amazing selection in WOW, let's say these are some concept characters with their abilities:

Bear - melee fighter/tank; passive - regenerates 2% HP every 5 seconds
- Feral charge - 12 seconds CD; charge to an enemy location

- Bash- 8 seconds CD; stuns an enemy

- Bearwithme - 20 seconds CD; slows Bear and nearby enemies by 50% for 6 seconds

- Challenging Roar - 60 seconds CD; taunts all nearby enemies (also players)
Moonkin - ranged mage; passive - gets 30% CD reduction while near trees
- Wrath -4 seconds CD; casts damaging spell on an enemy

- Typhoon - 12 sec CD; knocks back enemies in front

- Insect Swarm - 16 sec CD; dots enemy target for 6 sec; after it expires or target dies, it is cast on nearby enemy

- Starfall - 80 sec CD; you all know this OP spell   mad_turtle
Dryad - ranged auto attacker; passive - movement speed increased
- Venom Arrows - toggle ability for auto attacks to cost mana, but deal additional magic damage

- Root Trap - 20 sec CD, places a root trap on the ground, rooting first enemy for 3 sec

- Tropical Frenzy - 10 sec CD, shoots target with thing making them feel tipsy (same effect as when your character is drunk, if that is possible hehe)

- Natures Fight Back- 90 CD, for next 8 seconds, your auto attacks deal bonus magic damage and heal for the bonus amount
Ghoul - melee fighter; passive - 10% lifesteal
- Hungry Sprint - 16 sec CD, get's bonus movement speed for 8 seconds, based on missing health

- At all costs - 20 sec CD, hits nearby allied champion to grant bonus attack speed for 10 seconds

- Bloodthirst - 12 sec CD, ghoul damages himself to grant 20% lifesteal on next 6 attacks

- The Dessert - 180 sec CD, 1 sec cast time, ghoul reaches for enemy below 20% HP to execute them, if this ability kills the enemy, the CD is reset
The list goes on, I believe Warcraft is rich universe of unique and interesting characters and races, with abilities we could create based on the lore and balance/action requirements.

Just imagine battleground where you play as a Furbolg, able to control battleground with your totems, your team members are an Imp which casts fireballs, Spider spitting webs and poison, Gnoll with some crazy suicidal aggressive moves and a Human Priest, trying to hold the lot together and trying to figure out how the hell she got there!
Sounds fun to me!
Lore wise - yeah, I have never been into that too much, but I believe something could be put together!

Progression inside the BG could be done by spending the gathered resource on permanent buffs you buy from your core that gives you the standard MOBA stats- HP, attack damage, attack speed, spell power, cooldown reduction, physical and magic damage reductions...

There is tens of problems with this, hundreds even!
Auto attacks would have to be reworked, things like CD reduction or unit collision are pretty unknown in WOW. Aggro systems for all the NPCs would have to be created. Range difference between melee and ranged chars would have to be reduced...

What do you guys think? Is it possible to implement all the crucial MOBA systems into wow? How big of a mess would this create lorewise and would this fit into RPG game? And most of all, would this be fun?

THANKS for reading it all  turtle_in_love   , I had fun constructing this in my head, so I am happy you made it all the way!
