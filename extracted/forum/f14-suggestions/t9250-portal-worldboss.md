Just hear me out.

I'm in a cross faction guild and all of our mages are alliance, which means we almost never have free transport after raids. That got me thinking... why not?

Here's my suggestion:
Step 1: Make portals only usable by the correct faction to prevent the rest of this suggestion being a free pvp raid ambush method.

Step 2: Make every portal trainer a world boss that summons in a bunch of strong crap from portals when it is attacked.

Step 3: Make one of the drops a scroll that mages can use to learn the portal for that city

Step 4: Make the portal trainer rez as a non targetable ghost so players that just want to learn their portal dont have to wait for respawns.

Now alliance mages can pop a portal to org, provided they get a raid to help them infiltrate org and defeat the portal trainer, and of course the same for every other city. I don't know why I'm not a dev you can send me royalties if you want, you're welcome.
