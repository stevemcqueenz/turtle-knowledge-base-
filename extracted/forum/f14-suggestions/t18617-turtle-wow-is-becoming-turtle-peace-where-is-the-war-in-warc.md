# Turtle WoW is becoming Turtle Peace – Where is the WAR in Warcraft?

## Post 126737 by Zerzah — 2025-04-14T14:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126737#p126737 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle WoW Devs and Community,

No offense, but I need to say this clearly:
What happened to the war in Warcraft?

Turtle WoW has amazing content and a dedicated community – but let’s be honest: World PvP is basically non-existent.
There’s no danger in the open world, no rivalry, no real sense of conflict. Just peace and cooperation.

Yes, allowing Horde and Alliance to group up, raid together, and even form guilds does have clear advantages – it makes forming groups easier, improves accessibility, and helps players enjoy the game more freely.
But at the same time, it removes one of the most iconic parts of World of Warcraft: the war.

It doesn’t have to be all or nothing. That’s why I want to suggest a system that brings back some tension without forcing it on anyone:

My suggestion: The Assassin System for World PvP

If a player kills 10 players of the opposite faction without dying, they are marked as an Assassin.

Their location becomes visible on the world map to all enemy players.

If the Assassin is killed, they drop a bounty chest that grants the killer some gold or minor rewards.

This creates an optional layer of conflict for those who want war – while letting PvE-focused players completely ignore it and enjoy the game in peace.

I truly believe this could bring back the spirit of Warcraft for those of us who still crave the thrill of open world conflict – without disturbing the harmony that many players also enjoy.

Thank you for considering it.

Kind regards,
Zerzah
