# High Elf Racials for Warriors and Rogue

## Post 147902 by Antinuke000 — 2025-08-23T20:37:00Z

Hello!

Playing as a both warrior and rogue high elf. I think Our racial [Quel'dorei Meditation] is kinda underwhelming for melee's.

Right now for example the Racial is 1.5sec cast time and 50 energy over 3 sec.
Casting on Melee range that cant move is really kinda bad for a melee perspective.

I have 2 suggestion: *Remember this is only for Warrior and Rogues the 2 Pure melee classes for High elf.*

 1st Suggestion:
Make it No cast time. Nerf the Energy **TOTAL** Gained and make it longer
Example:
 *0 cast time, Restore 25 Energy over 5 seconds*

 2nd Suggestion:
 1 Second Cast time instead of 1.5s and You can move while Casting.

I hope devs can see this as a melee it feels clunky to play then casting racial or sometimes those 2 classes *NEVER* Use the Racial ability at all.

Compare it to orc trolls etc.
