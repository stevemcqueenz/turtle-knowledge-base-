It is very annoying and honestly difficult to find a group willing to do easy mode for ES as there is no real rewards for most people running it at this point on nord. I suggest that after saving Erennius on hard mode, have him give you a quest to turn in for the same loot you would get from killing him on easy mode. This is honestly just for alts of the few classes that can actually find the rewards from the claw of Erennius useful as they are gearing up, but i think it would be a better way to earn the reward for what's supposed to be a higher difficulty run anyway.
