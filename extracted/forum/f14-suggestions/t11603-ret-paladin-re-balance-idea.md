# Ret paladin re-balance idea

## Post 76783 by Achillesheel — 2023-11-10T13:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76783#p76783 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello turtles,
I see a lot of complains about ret paladins in PVP and how they are overpowered.
I want to propose an idea that can reduce the burst in PVP and will keep paladins viable in PVE.
My idea is redesign the holy strike. Instead of holystrike being attached to your next attack, make it as follows:
When you hit your enemy with auto-attack gain a buff for 20 seconds which is stackable.
After you reach 2 stacks you will be able to use your holy strike which should cost no mana, no seal proc and do 100% weapon damage as a holy damage.
Basically the initial burst will be reduced and you will be able to keep track when the paladin is about to burst.
This will be very helpful for ret pallies in PVE and also prot pallies as well.
What do you think about such an idea, or this will work as the holy strike initial opening we have right now :?
