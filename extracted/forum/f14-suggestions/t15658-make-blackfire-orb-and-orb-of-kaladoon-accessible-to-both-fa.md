# Make Blackfire Orb and Orb of Kaladoon accessible to both factions

## Post 106900 by Ayesha (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-07T13:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106900#p106900 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi!

I appreciate that you buffed these 2 niche items that will be useful for a lot of different specs. However, I find it very underwhelming that one of the items is not available to the opposite faction.

My suggestion is to make two items accessible to both factions when finishing the Kara key questline.

I would argue that Blackfire Orb is more powerful than Orb of Kaladoon, since there are less +spellcrit trinkets than there are +spellhit trinkets available in that power range. Epic trinket from BM, trinket from Kara 10, epic trinket from Hateforge all have spellhit. While only Hateforge and lbrs-UBRS trinkets provide crit. Then you also have the Emerald sanctum trinket that also provides spellhit and the BWL trinket if you are lucky, while there are no spellcrit trinkets within that power range. (Do correct me if I'm missing more VIABLE crit trinkets). Therefore it's harder to itemize around crit.

And that's why I think that alliance players should also have access to the Blackfire Orb, and I'm sure horde players would benefit too from being able to get Orb of Kaladoon in case they need it.
