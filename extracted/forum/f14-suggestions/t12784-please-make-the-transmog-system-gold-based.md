Additionally, all the mats required for the "Fashion Demands Sacrifices" quest are level-scaled and BoE, so you can just buy it all on the AH. What they really should change with the transmog system that could solve all our woes, is to make it bound to the equipment slot, rather to the item. This way to can transmog your chestpiece slot at level 10 and have it carry all the way to level 60 (unless you grow weary of it and transmog something else, of course). This would be much more streamlined and user-friendly, and would allow us to save transmog sets that we could swap between without spending 15 tokens anew.

IMO, transmogs only exist because of poor (or rather ill-suited) design choices when it comes to designing the gearing system. In OSRS, there's no need for a transmog system, because wearing combat gear actually penalizes you in non-combat activities by making your stamina recover more slowly. Ignoring that the agility suit is technically 'BIS', you're not only free but actually encouraged to wear an outfit you think looks nice, simply because you can. Meanwhile in WoW, mobs will kill you just while picking herbs.

That being said, I think that transmogs hurt the integrity of the game, because it's something that increases the awareness that you're living inside of a simulation, and I think that that the gear on your character should reflect what's actually being worn. I think the TWoW team have failed in this regard with tents, as well. it's immersion-breaking, you should be sitting in the inn instead

I think there should be instead be some sort of mechanic that can enchant a set of gear you choose for a period of somewhere between like a day and a week, that does something like increase your movement/mount speed by 5%, something small but it still has to incite the metagamer's mind so people are encouraged to "dress up", and only don combat gear when required. It should still function as a gold/resource/time sink, and there should probably be a restriction so that you can't just do that with your BiS gear anyway. Or maybe you should, as a tradeoff between fashion or practicality

I think the system should definitely be free, or at least much more accessible.

I don't feel like a rogue running around in white armor with a bright red cape lol.
If I get a useless item (stat-wise) from a quest, but that item fits my character's role and aesthetic a lot better, what's wrong with being able to just use that as layered armor?

When I first started, I was happy for every bad grey drop just because I thought "hey, I can add this to my wardrobe!  I was so disappointed when I found out you needed those fashion coins. The same goes for weapons. There are some really cool and unique early game weapons that just become redundant so quickly in terms of stats.

That's also why I think the system could still work as a time sink even when it's free, as you can spend a lot of time just farming certain equipment.
Also, the whole thing encourages roleplaying imo.

So yeah, if the devs are reading this: would love to see a slight overhaul of the system <3
