# New Quest: free 100% mount for BG use only

## Post 28906 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-11-10T17:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28906#p28906 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Fresh 60 players who enter BGs are at an extreme disadvantage when up against naxx geared players like me. This is one of many ideas to improve this situation:

Full speed mounts are mandatory for pvp, they are a direct player power increase. This is at big odds to how mounts work elsewhere, where they are simply a conveinece and an investment to improve your gold per hour on farms that require movement.

I suggest a quest be added, one for horde one for alliance, where the officers of the military have you sign up to help on the frontlines, and provision you with a steed or wolf for military use only.

In gameplay, this would be a zone restricted mount like the aq40 bugs are, and these 100% mounts would only work in the 5 BGs that we have.

With this implemented, fresh 60s will still really want to work for their mount if they want it for the open world or if they want a specific mount's look, but this will put them at better odds in PVP instead of being unable to escape anyone with their slow 60% mount and allow them to more easily get right into the action.
