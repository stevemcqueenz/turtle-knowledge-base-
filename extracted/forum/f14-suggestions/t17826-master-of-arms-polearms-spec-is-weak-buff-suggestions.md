# Master of Arms Polearms Spec is Weak (Buff Suggestions)

## Post 121514 by MarsSpitsBars — 2025-03-03T14:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121514#p121514 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tried seeing if that 2y difference actually matters for PvE, and it's not a big enough difference to actually use it over any of the other weapon specs. I thought that the point of Turtle WoW was supposed to make other playstyle viable. I thought Polearms were semi-fine when they were matching with Axe Spec 5% crit.

Now it feels like there's almost no reason to use polearms at all!

My suggestion to make Polearm spec more impactful would be to double the range from 2y to 4y! And if 4y is too much I'd like to see it at 3y instead! It would make it more viable for 2H Hamstring kiting since as of right now there's essentially no discernible difference between the other weapons over using polearms.

Please I would love to see this change in a future patch   turtle_in_love_head
