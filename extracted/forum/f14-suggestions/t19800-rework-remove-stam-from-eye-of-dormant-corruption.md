# [Rework]: Remove -Stam from Eye of Dormant Corruption

## Post 134952 by Jackbiers123 — 2025-06-10T00:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134952#p134952 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all folks and turtles! First of all, i hope you are having a great day.

I bring this post here to bring some attention into an item that got left apart and maybe not that much people care about because we are a few the ones that really play warlock or spriest, that thing is, this kara40 item, Eye of Dormant Corruption.

It gives -16 stamina and gives 3 more sec on corr or sw:p duration, do not mind the database typo.

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#O7vKSij]

Kara40 item balancing started by adding some stamina back in caster items that got it removed or with - effect, like for example the 2% crit gloves, due to the negative reaction of the playerbase on giving less stamina to items that needed it, but for some reason this trinket got left behind, and giving less stamina when Kara40 is already more punishing in health pools than Naxxramas (For example Chess or Rupturan, for picking a few encounters, are indeed very healing demanding).

For giving some numbers, Affliction warlock has less stamina on the gear that for example Holy Paladin that gets some pieces with +33 stamina (chesT) being a healer  (you can at least take care of yourself, your hp is your mana), but warlock health pools are smaller. Also some classes like paladins can opt to hybrid themselves with tank items like 22 stam necks or cloaks, or rings, but locks cannot.

This is why i bring into the devs attention the suggestion for removing this stat discrimination or make it +stam but less spirit, like Seal of Greymane already does from the same kara40 (my guess is some devs give "rp" reasons because the item is supossed to be "corrupted", but also Seal of Greymane is cursed and they turned it in an item that does not gimp your survivality in a dangerous raid, but gives you a penalty over time, less spirit is also less hp regen over time so it makes some sense)

Here we have a good reworked item, with +15 stam and -20 less spirit, i think when it was released it indeed didnt have the +stam

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#VXBcQHG]

For comparation, lets see another melee trinket from Kara40.

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#FC8AGdO]

That is why i want to bring some justice into the forgotten Eye of Corr, to see if our affli and spriest friends get they love they deserve. Also i got some complains from Affliction pro mains like Lilkaren saying that they didnt really find the +3 secs on corruption useful, so maybe that could get also a little change.

Ideas for the bonus effect, from my lock pov (priests will have their own).

 * Maybe 0,5 less GCD on corr for making it less clunky?
 * Give 4% more chances on shadowtrance with corr for having a little proc bonus?
 *give raw damage on application for better affli trash management, let that tick crit?

Please add your own ideas on how this bonus could be used, any help will be apreciated!

Thank you very much for your attention.
