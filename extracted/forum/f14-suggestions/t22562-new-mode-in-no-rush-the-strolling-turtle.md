Simple idea : a leveling mode which forces you to walk at all time (and your mount is also at walking speed).

Take it slow with a new mode in the spirit of turtle - it's time to look at the scenery and smell the roses during your daily stroll.

Reward could be a title and some item for proficient strollers, like a gentleman walking cane in off-hand, or a smoking pipe.
