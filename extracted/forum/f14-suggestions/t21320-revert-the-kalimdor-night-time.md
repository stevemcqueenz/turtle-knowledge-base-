# Revert the Kalimdor Night-Time

## Post 147844 by JWolfsLair — 2025-08-23T12:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147844#p147844 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The day/night cycle of Kalimdor makes me loopy.

The reasoning for the 12h Kalimdor offset was originally, that the server had a world-wide audience. Now that there are various other server hubs (SEA and China), this reasoning is much weaker, not to mention that it simply doesn't make sense lore-wise or ambiance-wise either, as the zeppelin and boat teleports me across continents and my friends are standing where they stood before - it's a meta mechanic and the offset actually makes the world ***less*** seamless.

Please, for the love of all that is holy, revert this change.

Some of you might consider this an overreaction, but this is quite literally preventing me from playing on TWoW, which is perhaps the greatest WoW experience ever, or certainly at this moment - yet I am unable to play.

My brain is incapable of accepting Mulgore and The Barrens covered in darkness. Truly, it is very unhealthy too. The game is meant to be a world to live in, and chill in. It is meant to simulate life. Light outside - light in WoW. I really really want to have a glorious Tauren Druid journey throughout TWoW, but I can't. The discord between day-time IRL and night-time in the game is actually enough to prevent me from playing.

There have been numerous iterations of this request over the past few years here on the forums, and the opening of Ambershire made me so sad about this all over again. I tried playing again, and again failed to get past the bloody nighttime in what is supposed to be a sun-lit savannah.

 **Please!**

What in the seven hells is this? Look and how sad and brooding my boy is!

I've mentioned how it doesn't make sense regardless. One simply has to accept the video game as a video game in regards to some meta mechanics.

The boats and zeppelins literally teleport you across the continents. No time passes. The fact that the world is round doesn't move with that a bit. The transportation is a meta mechanic, not completely 'lore friendly' and so it has to be given some leeway. Because gameplay-wise it is given that leeway. In a better WoW, maybe the continents wouldn't be separate instances, and we would actually literally travel across the ocean. But even then it would take maybe tens of minutes - and the day/night cycle would remain the same.

But if we were to push in some explanation - we might as well claim that the journey takes 12 or 36 or however many hours - and then the day-night time remains the same as when you've left, if that roundness matters so much.

What matters to me, is a beautiful observation one of the previous askers for this change mentioned: We are not adapted to stay up during the night. If you were to travel to another continent, you would still shift your routine to be diurnal there. You simply wouldn't adventure during the day in the Old World, and during the night in Kalimdor.

Thus, I actually claim it makes ***less*** sense fantasy-wise, lore-wise. Including the roundness and all.
