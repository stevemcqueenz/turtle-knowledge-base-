# New Army and Commander Trait system

## Post 87055 by Templar85 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-02-05T15:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87055#p87055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ideas to incentivise World PVP, Capital Sieges and Capital Defense. New Army system with Council of Commanders and Traits

High Honour Rank players can make an Army. This is a raid but with bonus effects and some restrictions.

Traits can level up by spending 200k(variable) amount Honour on it. Players can level up multiple Traits but when they are in an Army they have to choose one which becomes an active Trait.
Honour gain must be cumulative and not depleting by time. Or can be a separate resource from honour but tight to pvp.

War (Raid) leader and 4 of his/her party members becoming Council of Commanders. Their chosen traits will apply to the whole raid. Once War leader chooses his/her 4 council members by placing them in GRP1 with himself/herself the Commander Traits becoming raid wide but the War Leader can not change his/her Council members for 1 hour. Commander Traits works in capital cities (hostile ones too) for party members while in the raid and in an outside zone while there is an opposing Army in the zone.

Commander Traits does not works in Instances. There is a tactical element whom to choose to be a Council member for the Army because maximum 5 Traits can be active. Council members and War Leader gains an icon above their head which enemies can also see from distance. If Council members and War Leader killed by enemy Council members or War Leader(did at least 60% damage to them) then their traits suspended for 12 minutes. In case killed by non council member enemy player then their Traits suspended for 3 minutes.

Successful Leader kill grants gold and high level materials for the raiders.
Defending in a Capital earning more Honour then raiding an enemy one. Successful defence when enemy Army consist 25 raid members at least inside a city and killed at least 100 guards or players (LVL60)  but not achieved Leader kill in 60 minutes. Timer starts when offensive raid killed at leas 10 guards or players in the city or when they reached outskirts of the Leader Room, after that players inside the city will be notified there is an ongoing siege and the timer will be visible. After 60 minutes past without a Leader kill, then defenders gain Rally the Defenders buff (+15% HP, +10% damage, +5% crit, +10% movement, +25% stealth detection) which persist through death while players inside the Capital.

Interactive Traits

Only Council members can initiate:

Assault Team:
LVL 1 summon 3 Elite Soldiers to the battlefield. 2 Council member and 8 players needed to assist the summoning. 25 min CD. Elites have charge ability. They last for 1 minutes or until killed. 3 Elites as a group can be controlled by the Council member.

LVL 5 summon 15 Elite Soldiers to the battlefield. 1 Council member and 4 players needed to assist the summoning. 20 min CD. Elites have charge ability. They last for 5 minutes or until killed. 10 Elites as a group can be controlled by the Council member.

Healing Tent:
LVL 1 Summon a small healing Tent. Players inside the tent replenish HP and Mana. 1 minute to fully replenish. After a player HP replenished can not use the tent again for 3 minutes (player gains debuff recently used tent) 6 healing specced players needed to assist the summoning. 15 min CD. Tent lasts for 2 minute 30 sec

LVL 5 Summon a large healing Tent. Players inside the tent replenish HP and Mana. 30 seconds to fully replenish.  After a player HP replenished can not use the tent again for 3 minutes (player gains debuff recently used tent) 2 healing specced players needed to assist the summoning. 15 min CD.  Tent lasts for 6 minute 30 sec

Berserker's Dance:
Summon a Large Bonfire. Bonfire last for 5 minutes, 20 minute CD. Two other Council member needed to assist the summon. When summoning starts, plays a 5 second war chant which enemies also hear in a 100 yards radius range. After summoned melee can interact with it, forcing to dance/move around it, in a circle for 30 seconds. Character forced to move around once started, can not stop the animation once started. After dance finished, player gain Berserker's Dance buff. Lasts for 10 min. When player got attacked they have 20% chance and when received a critical hit 45% chance to gain Berserking:

LVL 1  Berserking increases melee attack damage by 5%, melee critical damage bonus by 4%, haste by 3% but increases incoming damage by by 4%, can not use shield or block attacks and incoming heals reduced by 3%

LVL 5 Berserking increases melee attack damage by 25%, melee critical damage bonus by 20%, haste by 15% but increases incoming damage by 20%, can not use shield or block attacks and incoming heals reduced by 15%
