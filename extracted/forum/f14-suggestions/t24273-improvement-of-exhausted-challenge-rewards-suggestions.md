# Improvement of 'Exhausted challenge' rewards suggestions

## Post 170721 by Trxfengzidian — 2026-03-13T04:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170721#p170721 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Dear devs

I don’t know if you have statistics on which challenges players prefer, but I'm sure that the 'Exhausted challenge' is one of the least chosen. Thus, I wonder if you can improve or change the rewards of 'Exhausted challenge' in next 1.18.1 patchs.

In my opinion, not being able to gain rested XP essentially doubles the time it takes to level up. I also don't think 'increased weapon skill speed' is a good enough reward anymore, which is why players are avoiding this challenge. People would probably even try the ‘Drunk challenge' over this one. It feels like I'm spending twice as much time leveling up compared to others, but in the end, I get nothing noticeable out of it—because the increased weapon skill speed is barely perceptible.

I agree it's not a difficult challenge like 'Hardcore'. But I think it deserves a title, at least. For example, players could be rewarded with a 'Turtle Speed' title upon reaching level 60. Unlike the current reward, which is invisible, a title is a visible and meaningful way to show off their achievement.

Another possibility is a collectible reward, like a special tent. For instance, a 'Forever Tent' could be identical to the normal one in function (same 2-hour cooldown), but visually distinct with a different color or shape. This type of purely cosmetic reward wouldn't disrupt game balance, but it would create a strong incentive for players to try the challenge, as they'd be working towards a tangible, cosmetic reward.

KR
