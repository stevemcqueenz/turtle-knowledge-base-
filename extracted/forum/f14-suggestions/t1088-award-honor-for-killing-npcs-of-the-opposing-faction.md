# Award Honor for killing NPCs of the opposing faction.

## Post 6278 by Qixel (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-06-09T06:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6278#p6278 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If killing a civilian of the opposing faction is dishonorable in the same manner killing a lowbie of the opposing faction is, then it stands to reason killing a non-civilian of the opposing faction is honorable in the same manner killing another player is. I know that faction leaders like Thrall and Magni award Honor upon being killed, so it's not an unheard of concept, either.

 **What would it accomplish?** There are players who, for various reasons including locale and availability, simply are not able to be on at peak hours. The vanilla pvp system rewards effort instead of skill, but on a server with a population like ours, there are only certain times of day you can even try to rank. Adding honor to the various npcs of the world would allow people to grind ranks at any time of day, regardless of other players' availability.

It's not just a bonus to those who are unable to play at peak hours, either. For all three individuals on the server who want there to be world pvp, this would add it. Attacking npcs of the opposing faction flags you, after all, opening you up to retribution from players of the opposing faction.

 **Why do battlegrounds, then?** The obvious answer would be because you enjoy it, but truthfully no one really does, so honor gains from npcs would obviously be less than you'd get on average from a player. It would still be preferable to do battlegrounds, but for those who either cannot or will not join, this opens an alternative path.
