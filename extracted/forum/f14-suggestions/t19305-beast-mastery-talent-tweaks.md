# Beast Mastery - Talent tweaks

## Post 131143 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-15T00:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131143#p131143 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Let me start off by saying, I don't think Frenzy should be a 5 point talent. Due to how it scales, faster attacking pets get the most benefit from it at lower ranks, while slower ones really depend on those extra points, so they can trigger the effect. Which is why I think it should be changed from 5 points to 4 points. Essentially re-scalling it from 20/40/60/80/100% to 25/50/75/100%. People generally max out this talent while leveling up, but then place fewer points after reaching level cap and re-adjusting their talents.

Ok, but what do we do with that extra talent point? I think it shouldn't be left in the air, and instead provide another option for a BM Hunter. And the following are various alternatives for talents that can get increased by 1 more point or completely new 1 point talents. ____________________________________
 **Loyal Guardian**

> Eyes of the Beast cast time is reduced by 2 seconds. While Eyes of the Beast is active, the Hunter will also channel the highest rank of Mend Pet every 10 seconds.

![Image](https://imagizer.imageshack.com/img923/9043/A67h5B.png)
This talent is unlocked after spending 2 points into Improved Mend Pet. It essentially combined Eyes of the Beast and Mend Pet into a single ability. Now why would you want that? With the talent Improved Eyes of the Beast, your pet hits for a decent amount more, and since you're already channeling while using Mend Pet, your pet will get a decent damage boost during this period.

Again tho, when and why would you want this? This is going to be most useful for Pet tanking scenarios, where you want your pet to keep aggro (Improved EotB helps here), but also want to sustain your pet. This is not going to replace conventional tanks, but in cases where the tank dies in a dungeon, the pet can more effectively hold its own. So, it's generally more of a talent for leveling or soloing content

I'm not a PvP player, and from what I gathered in the forums, Improved Eyes of the Beast doesn't really play much of a role in PvP, so in that regard, I don't think this will cause any balancing issues ____________________________________
 **Feast**

> Your pet heals for 15% of damage done vs enemies that are at 30% HP or lower.

![Image](https://imagizer.imageshack.com/img924/4615/KroQRa.png)
This talent is unlocked after investing 5 points into Unleashed Fury. It's yet another leveling/solo type of talent, that grants your pet more sustain. Some may say that it's a gameplay downside, as it reduces the agency of Mend Pet. However I would disagree with that, as both can work in conjunction. This essentially lowers the downtime between fights for Hunters, and allows pets to tank much tougher opponents.
____________________________________
 **Hunting Thrill**

> After killing an opponent that yields experience or honor, your pet's next Bite or Claw attack strikes with 10% increased Attack Power and has a 40% increased critical chance. Lasts 40 seconds

A copy of the Rogue's Remorseless Attacks, but with some added damage due to being a lower talent, and pets having lower attack in general. It is, you guessed it, yet another leveling/soloing talent. It's an easy way to quickly proc frenzy when the fight starts. Pretty self explanatory.

____________________________________ **Improved Scare Beast**

> Tame Beast channel time is reduced in half and the target is immobilized while under the effect of Scare Beast. The tamed pet's loyalty will also start out at Submissive.

This is a talent with utility exclusive to the open world. It will make taming pets much faster and easier, as part of the loyalty grind will also be shortened.

____________________________________
The talents I proposed are simply examples of what could be done. Both are casual play oriented talents, as reducing Frenzy from 5 talent points to 4 is already a DPS upgrade for BM hunters. I have a separate thread where I discussed revamping Improved Eyes of the Beast to a DPS CD, so if you anybody is interested in that, you can comment there - [viewtopic.php?p=130196#p130196](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130196#p130196)

Feel free to add your own 1 point talent point suggestions. I'll be adding more down the line
