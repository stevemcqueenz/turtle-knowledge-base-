Actually, I don't understand why class designed around pets get pet only at lvl 10. Warlocks get their first pet at lvl 4 normally, but they can get quest at level 1.

Pets usually feature a lot of logistical problems. For example - you need to feed them a lot. Hunters already have 1 bag for ammo, so if they stack food, they would get even less bag space. On early levels it is real trouble. If you consider "alts" then it is not a problem, but how about HCs, how about first char on the server?

Changing pet availability to lvl 1 also means redesigning pet quest for each race. Now it has 3 stages featuring different beasts, but at lvl 1 there are not enough of beast of that level. Maybe 2 at best?

Also it is not clear what would happen with training points. WIll you get more points just because you started earlier?

I don't think it should be changed. It is not really a long-term problem of people, but it requires a lot of effort to change properly.
