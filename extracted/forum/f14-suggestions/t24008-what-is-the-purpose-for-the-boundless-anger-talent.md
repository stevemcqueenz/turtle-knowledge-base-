# What is the Purpose for the [Boundless Anger] talent

## Post 168770 by Cfishet — 2026-02-18T15:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168770#p168770 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi Arms Warriors,

I was looking through the talent calculator and noticed that the new Turtle WoW added the talent [Boundless Anger] to the Arms talent tree.

I can’t find a single reason in PvP or PvE why an Arms Warrior would want to withhold rage when it’s needed and stack it up to 130. It seems very counterproductive. On top of that, in the next class change patch, it will be mandatory to put 3 points into it if you want to use the buffed [Precision Cut], which feels very limiting. its probably taking points away from more fun talents like [Enrage] or [Master at Arms].

Does any warrior have anything positive to say about [Boundless Anger] to justify its inclusion and continued existence?

Personally, I would prefer to see it replaced with something else or removed entirely.

## Post 168806 by Magicknight — 2026-02-18T20:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168806#p168806 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The purpose of Boundless Anger is to allow the Warrior to potentially lose even more Rage when they change stances!

But in all seriousness, (in my opinion) it's a fairly pathetic talent, and should not hold a place on the 6th tier of the Arms tree and grants no other bonuses.

I do have a suggestion: make Boundless Anger actually 'Boundless' !!

The talent could be linked (via a long arrow) from Tactical Mastery down to the 6th tier.

"Boundless Anger - You retain up to [25/50/75] points of Rage when changing your stance"

So, you wouldn't get penalised so much / at all for changing stances (a fundamental in both PvP & PvE)...
But, you would have to invest 8 talent points to maximise the gains, and you would have to be very invested into the Arms tree to get the talent (as it sits on the 6th tier).

...otherwise change the name of Boundless Anger to 'More Rage You Get to Lose' (a more fitting description for the current talent).

Your thoughts, my fellow free honour kills, I mean Warriors! ;-)
