# Provide buffs to Blood Fury.

## Post 50365 by Lichman — 2023-06-17T06:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50365#p50365 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Blood Fury as a racial has always been in a bad spot in Vanilla. Its completely or nigh useless for three of the Orcs available classes, four of them on Turtle with the addition of Orc mages, and the debuff it provides is a massive detriment to tanks who might want to use the damage to increase their threat generation. For PVE in particular, Blood Fury sees minimal or no use and is outshined by universally useful racials like Berserking's haste, Gnome's intellect and WotF's fear break. Especially with the upcoming addition of Troll Warlocks, there's very little reason for ranged DPS to not play a Troll, or Gnome for casters.

I would request that Blood Fury be changed to behave as it did in The Burning Crusade. First, the duration of the debuff should be changed to match the duration of the buff, fifteen seconds. Orcs playing non-casters (Warriors, Hunters, Rogues) would receive a scaling AP bonus, benefitting both ranged and melee physical attacks. Dedicated casters (Mages, Warlocks) will receive a scaling spell power bonus. Shamans would receive a mixed AP/SP bonus. These bonuses would scale with the level of the character with a simple formula. TBC used (lvl*4)+2 for AP, (lvl*2)+3 for SP, and both combined for Shamans, for 243 AP and 123 SP in total.

This would effectively give Orcs a universal damage trinket with a significant downside instead of a mediocre racial, on-par with the aforementioned haste buff of Berserking, Expansive Mind and Undead's five-second duration PVP trinket, doubling as a CC-break for several raid bosses and mobs with AOE fears.

For those who fear this would make Orcs too powerful in PVP settings, the healing debuff is already proven to be a risky and dangerous maneuver when potions, teas, and especially healers can save your life several times over. This is even more true of objective-based battlegrounds, where effectively mortal striking yourself for fifteen seconds can get you killed and lose you an objective. The AP bonus already being provided to melee (which ends at about the same number of 240 on a geared orc warrior/rogue) was already considered acceptable by Blizzard, and still was even when they had the chance to change it for several expansions.

With all that said, I believe that making this change would give the opportunity to those who choose Orc Mage, Warlock, Shaman, or Hunter, either for aesthetic or roleplay reasons, an opportunity to excel in group content even when paired with Troll Warlocks or Gnome Mages. I, and other current or prospective Orc players would be overjoyed at this change, and I'm certain there would be many players who have yet to join the server eager to come try out Orc Enhancement Shaman or Orc Hunter with a change like this. Undoubtedly There are other racials/races that could be improved as well, but I will leave that discussion for other threads.
