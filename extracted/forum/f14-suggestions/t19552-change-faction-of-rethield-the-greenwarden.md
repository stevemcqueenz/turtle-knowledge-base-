# Change faction of Rethield the Greenwarden

## Post 133096 by AverageAlc — 2025-05-26T18:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133096#p133096 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Rethiel the Greenwarden (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1244>) is a vanilla quest-giver in the middle of the Wetlands.
For some reason the original devs set his faction to Stormwind, and as being hostile to the Horde. He's a nature-protecting swamp-monster in the middle of a dwarf leveling zone, so this was probably a mistake.

Since there's a Turtle-original neutral quest-hub of gnolls nearby, I think it would make sense from a gameplay perspective to make him into a neutral quest-giver as well. It would give Horde players a novel leveling option for 20-30.
Thematically, it would also make more sense for an ancient nature-spirit to give Cenarion Circle reputation than it would to give Stormwind reputation.
