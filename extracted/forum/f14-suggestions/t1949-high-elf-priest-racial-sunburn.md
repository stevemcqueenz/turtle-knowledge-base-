# High Elf Priest Racial: Sunburn

## Post 11633 by Stenerjoe2 — 2021-08-12T12:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11633#p11633 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!

My name is Emmizabeth, Im a returning regular player who's always been interested in supporting TW with suggestions and in other ways, and now im back with yet another (very) specific suggestion.

 **Presentation**

I've been playing priest forever in all aspects of wow, and since High Elves were released on turtle I was really interested in what could be done with their racials. I know from talking to the devs/designers that there has been some complications with the two Priest Racial spells they added, but a fix is coming for at least one of the spells in 1.15.2.

While I like one of the spells, Suns Embrace, which reduces mana cost of offessive holy spells for 20 seconds, I must admit that I dont share the same level of affection for the other spell, Divine Sanction, as I feel it is very unrewarding to use (atleast in its current state).

Also, both spells is a buff you apply on yourself or others, and gameplay wise I think it could be more interesting.
Therefore, I've come up with an Idea to replace Divine Sanction (10% Casting Speed for 10 seconds), with a new spell:

 ****Holy Word: Sunburn** Rank 6 (Level 60)**
 350 Mana, 30 yd range, Instant cast, 15/30/60/90 sec cooldown.
 *''Blasts the target with divine energy that causes 524 holy damage over 12/16 sec.  Damage caused by Sunburn heals all party member.''*

Spell Coefficient (How much the spell is affected by spell power stats): 50%

 **BUT WHAT ABOUT DEBUFF SLOTS IN RAIDING CONTENT!?**

You are right, good point. While I aknowledge that yet another Dot that might get pushed off is considered a ''waste'' to some, I still believe that a Dot adds to more diverse gameplay- However, incase this is considered a dealbreaker, I've come up with an alternative:

 **Holy Word: Sunburn** Rank 6 (Level 60)
 335 Mana, 30 yd range, 1.5 sec cast,  15/30/60/90 sec cooldown
 *''Blasts the target with divine energy for 450 to 478 holy damage. Damage caused by Sunburn heals all party member.''*

Spell Coefficient (How much the spell is affected by spell power stats): 45%

Anyway, thats my two cents. I realize this is a very niche specific suggestion request, and I know from keeping up with the turtledashians that the dev team got loooads on their hands (new content, fixing bugs, being serviceminded), but I think this could be a fun addition to holy/disc priests- heck, not just high elves but all races, but thats not what the subject is about :-)

Keep calm and carry on   turtle_tongue_head
