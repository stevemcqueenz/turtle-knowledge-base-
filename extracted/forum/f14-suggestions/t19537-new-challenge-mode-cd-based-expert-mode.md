# New Challenge Mode: CD-Based Expert Mode

## Post 132982 by flyinghome — 2025-05-25T21:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132982#p132982 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle Wow Development Team,

I hope you're doing well!

I’d love to share an idea that could bring a fresh, high-stakes twist to dungeons and raids — a new optional difficulty called “CD-Based Expert Mode.”

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Concept:
In this mode, each character is allowed only one death per lockout (CD). If a player dies, they cannot be resurrected by any means and must wait for the next reset to try again. No battle rez. No soulstone. No second chance.

This adds a powerful sense of tension and consequence — without the harshness of full permadeath.

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Why it matters:
Many players — especially veterans — are craving a more serious challenge in retail WoW, but don’t want to go as far as deleting their character after one death, like in Hardcore Classic.
This mode offers the perfect middle ground:

Strategic, intense gameplay

Meaningful team coordination

High replay value

Totally optional and opt-in

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) How it could work:
Enabled like Mythic+ affixes or raid difficulty toggles

Could include unique achievements, titles, transmogs, or cosmetic-only rewards

Ideal for seasonal events, leaderboards, or limited-time challenges

I believe this mode would not only engage competitive players and content creators, but also breathe new life into existing instances — without disrupting the current experience for others.

Thanks so much for your time and for continuing to evolve turtle wow.

Best regards,

Flyinghome
