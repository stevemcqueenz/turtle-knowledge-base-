# Make the "Khadgar's Unlocking" spell more useful and interesting

## Post 24975 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-13T10:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24975#p24975 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, training this spell is a total waste of time and money. It's always underleveled for the level it requires (can't open lockboxes that mobs of that level drop) and its max level is a joke. Ontop of that it requires reagents to use, that are not cheap, I might add, when you could just stockpile your lockboxes and ask a rogue to open them (often for free).
I understand not being able to open max level locked dungeon doors and whatnot (rogues an engineers trained their crafts after all), but at least I expect to be able to service my own lockboxes at max level, if I want to avoid social contact with shady types and am willing to pay for the reagents.

I have three solutions for the problem:
 1) Just buff the lockpicking skill values of all ranks (and especially the 4th rank) of the spell.
AND/OR
 2) Reduce the level required for training these spells (and their cost, according to the level), so they are more level-appropriate
AND/OR
 3) Add a 5th rank that is either dropped as a skill tome (like Arcane Brilliance) and/or involves a more interesting quest chain to learn. The "go to ravenholdt and pay even more gold" is a lazy way to make a quest, I expected something more exotic.

Also, a small change to the reagents: Swap the places of the "Tiny Bronze Key" and "Tiny Copper Key", because copper is cheaper than Bronze. Also allow blacksmiths to craft them, say 1-3 keys per craft, requiring a bar and grindstone of the appropriate level. This would give us an alternate way to get keys that could potentially be more cost-efficient than filling mr. Truvicus' pocket.
If you decide to add 5th rank, the reagent for it could be "Tiny Truesilver Key" or "Tiny Golden Key" and it would be obtainable only from blacksmithing.
