# Outposts with flight paths close to remote dungeons

## Post 24003 by Zhilo — 2022-08-30T16:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24003#p24003 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was specifically thinking about Maraudon, its pretty out of the way in desolace, so its very annoying to go to despite it having some pretty decent loot. There may be other dungeons that are really time consuming for either or both factions to reach, and since allowing meeting stones to summon party members seems to be a no go in this server, then at least some other form of help in reaching these out-of-the-way dungeons could be made available. It is a shame that some of these dungeons don't see as much visits as they could because they are too much of a time investment just reaching them to run once.

As an addendum, these outposts could contain the basic amenities for dungeon runners to get them all set, such as a mailbox, smelter/forge, npcs that can repair, and the usual vendors of trade goods.

Perhaps the could be cross faction and run by venture co. or some other goblin cartel, seems like a decent scheme to make money off adventurers.
