# Regarding the donation mall "Illusion"

## Post 128803 by M25482 — 2025-04-28T10:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128803#p128803 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have two suggestions for the development team:
First Suggestion:
I thoroughly enjoy the Ogre Transformation Toy rewarded at Level 30 in Turtle Mode. However, its 10-minute duration and 1-hour cooldown feel overly restrictive. I respectfully propose the following adjustments:

Either remove the cooldown entirely and allow unlimited usage,

Or introduce a cooldown-free version in the Donation Shop. I would gladly spend Turtle Coins to purchase a permanent Ogre Illusion without usage limitations.

Second Suggestion:
While the Donation Shop currently offers a Two-Headed Ogre Illusion, its combat animations during attacks appear rigid and lack fluidity. In contrast, the Level 30 Turtle Mode Ogre Transformation features dynamic and responsive combat animations. To enhance player satisfaction and encourage purchases, I strongly recommend updating the Donation Shop Ogre Illusion's attack animations to align with the animation code of the Turtle Mode reward. Given that both models share the same Ogre framework, this adjustment should be technically straightforward.

These improvements would significantly elevate the player experience, and many in the community would enthusiastically support such changes. Thank you for your consideration, and we eagerly await your response!
