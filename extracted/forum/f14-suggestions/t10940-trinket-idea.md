F. ex. the flute which makes party members dance.

## Post 71409 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-10-17T06:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71409#p71409 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Because the trinkets have to be equipped to be used and equipping adds cooldown.
Telling you my personal experience.
I have a fun but useless trinket from Str UD. A flute which makes party membrs dance. But if I want to use it I have to equip it and wait 30 sec cooldown. The problem with this is that I have two trinkets I always use and both have 2 min cooldowns between procs (Zandalarian Hero Charm, Talisman of Ephemeral Power). So if I wanna goof around in a dungeon when people are preparing a pull, I have to equip the flute, wait 30 sec, do it and then reequip one of my useful trinkets and wait the additional 30 sec cooldown before using them. This puts me in a disadvantage because I rely heavily on those two trinkets. Not a big disadvantage but big enough for me to almost never use the fun but useless trinket.
