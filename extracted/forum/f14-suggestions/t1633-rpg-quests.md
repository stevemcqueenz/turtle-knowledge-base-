## Post 9647 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2021-03-04T11:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9647#p9647 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are some ways to make it work with WoW's engine and quest system. Note: It can't be done in just one quest, there needs to be a linear progression of quests to allow such branching storylines.

First way is like Blizzard did with the warlock "Orb Of Orahil" questchain (After doing a quest, the next step is two separate quests [https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=4962/ ... a-felhound](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=4962/shard-of-a-felhound) and [https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=4963/ ... n-infernal](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=4963/shard-of-an-infernal) but the player can only accept one quest at a time, and completing it permanently stops the player from accepting the other one.

Second way is have a quest that rewards an item of the player's choosing from a list (So if the player is supposed to have 2 choices, kill or capture for example, gets to choose between either a lethal poison or a sedative poison). Each one of these items starts a specific quest that then leads to a separate branch of the storyline.
