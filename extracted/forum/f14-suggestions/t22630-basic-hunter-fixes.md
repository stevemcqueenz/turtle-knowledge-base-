# Basic Hunter Fixes

## Post 157486 by Zentraxiusf — 2025-10-28T10:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157486#p157486 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

We can all agree hunter is one of the worst performing classes at 60, your pets dont scale at all unless you're BM spec in which case you are competing with tanks for bottom of the barrel dps, while its clear there's no love lost on hunters and they are intended to have only 3 dps specs, none of which are good, they could at least be functional, and there's a few pretty simple fixes for this IMO

 1) Pet Scaling, give ~20% of armor/stamina/attack power to pets baseline, as it is they peak as a fresh 60 and are functionally useless by the time you're raid geared, even if you go through the hassle of getting them buffed and properly positioned, a pet that just melees for 80 dmg when you are swinging for 900s is more of a burden to manage than it is an iconic class mechanic.

 2) Serpent Sting/Arcane Shot, an iconic ability learned early on that becomes useless before you even hit 60 for a very simple reason, it does not scale off attack power. It adds some basic upkeep to BM/MM rotation and is upkept passively by survival yet it does virtually no damage unless you deck yourself out in meme spell power gear, even something as simple as ~10% of AP is added to arcane shot and 30% to serpent sting would make these at least usable in some situation, arcane shot isnt worth training beyond the first rank and serpent sting isnt even on most hunters bars at 60, when a single glancing auto shot does more dmg than the full duration of the poison

 3) Aimed Shot is just in a bad spot, its given to everyone baseline at 20 but made completely worthless due to the long cast time, exorbitant mana cost, AND low damage compared to steady shot. It should be an integral part of MM, make steady shot baseline, put aimed shot back on the MM tree, and buff it accordingly, either by increasing its dmg so its worth the cost of use (eg 200% weapon damage, guaranteed crit, instant cast after x number of steady shots, increased damage per steady shot used recently, etc)

Currently all 3 hunter specs are bad on almost every fight in every raid(and pvp), even in ideal scenarios where the raid boss isn't immune to any of your damage, and you can freely maintain full uptime, the most complex rotation is BM which does extremely poor damage, I get that nobody wants the pet spec to be viable in raids, but it's worse than just not viable dps, its barely beating tanks. Marks just chains steady shot-auto shot until the boss dies or they run out of mana, which on top of being your only functioning dps rotation, is the miserable 1 button raid rotation I thought TWow was trying to move away from, and its not even good. Meanwhile survival is functionally just drop immolation trap off cooldown and auto attack, ignoring the bosses where you cant even compete with a demo locks pet because theyre immune to fire, all abilities feel just awful to use. Raptor strike is just a regular auto attack with more damage, mongoose bite does less damage than auto attacks, lacerate has janky positioning that cant usually be used on top of many enemies being immune to bleeds in the first place, plus why is survival the only class with dual wield that doesnt get a proper dual wield specialization? Rogues get 5% hit and +50% offhand damage, warriors get +50% offhand damage and 10% offhand hit, survival gets.. +25% offhand damage and +3% hit?

It genuinely feels like there's a negative disposition towards the class from the development team, whether its just "hunter shouldn't be GOOD because x is harder" or just the general WoW community opinion of "only rogues and mages are allowed to do damage" but it's pretty miserable to play a class that performs worse on twow than it did in original vanilla
