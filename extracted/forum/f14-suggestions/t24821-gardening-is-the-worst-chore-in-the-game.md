# Gardening is the worst chore in the game

## Post 174731 by Sylvestro1 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-04-10T20:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174731#p174731 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Yes, it's officially worse than fishing now, wasn't this supposed to be reworked together with Survival? I mean, Gardening was always terrible, but at least you could safely ignore it until the Survival Artisan quest, which forces you to do this now.

Please take me through the thought process of developing Gardening, how is this supposed to be fun? You realize games are supposed to be fun, yes? It's basically 36 minutes of standing around doing nothing and you can't even do it at a capital somewhere so you can do some AH or other crafting stuff in the meantime. It's just more than half an hour just standing around twiddling your thumbs. Absolutely mind-numbing dumb and boring. And you have to do this several time for the quest. And the planters aren't even reuseable! And if the server happens to go down YOU WASTED ALL YOUR TIME AND MATERIALS. This is the worst part really.

Please, PLEASE I beg of you do something about this because it's such a mind-numbing pain in the ass it will drive anyone crazy. At least DRASTICALLY reduce the growth times or even better remove them entirely. The other professions are not realistic, neither does this need to be. Or you think you can forge a sword in 10 seconds? That shit takes hours, days even.

Or you know what, just remove the Nutritious Rations from the Artisan Survial quest and replace them with something else that does not require Gardening, thank you!
