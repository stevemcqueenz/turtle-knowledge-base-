# Artisan's Quest proposed fix

## Post 116274 by Sanniey — 2025-01-11T21:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116274#p116274 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all. I've been wanting to start the Artisan's Quest challenge, but even before leaving the Cactus Apple area you are experiencing troubles. Here's my take on how to fix this so it's a viable challenge for all classes. It's important to note that this challenge should feel fun and rewarding in my opinion. You're giving up all dungeon gear and good leveling items that are part of the memorable 'Vanilla' experience, so you may be rewarded along the way imo.

 **1. Add weapon recipes**
This can be either available to everyone, but I feel some might not like that change for the sake of nostalgia.
So the other option can be one (or more) "Artisan"-trainers/vendors. These trainers hold a few extra recipes for you to help you trough your challenge. These are mainly weapons, as not upgrading a weapon without Blacksmithing is a pain for some, if not all, classes. They will only be available for those who are on this challenge. How hard these weapons are to craft is up to discussion.

 **Example**:
Plate wearers would probably take BS, with both gear and weapons available. Not much to fix.

Leather wearers take LW, but rogues, shamans, and hunters don't want to equip their starting weapon forever. Add some recipes for a level 15, 25, 35, 45, 55 dagger, mace and bow in LW to fix the issue (levels and weapon types are random, other people are probably better at deciding what would be best). Not asking for bis Garden Shovels, but a simple green dagger would make this an actual viable challenge for all rogues. Make the weapons soulbound so they don't mess with the economy at all.

Cloth wearers would pick tailoring and weapons are less of a deal. Still it would feel good to be able to craft a staff every 15 levels or so. Again, not a bis, just a simple green staff with a few int will be something to look forward to! Maybe also a few simple wands?

 **2. Fishinig**
You cannot equip a fishing pole because you did not make it. I'm sure if we're adding "Artisan"-trainers/vendors for weapons, we can include a fishing pole to craft. Easy fix.

 **3. Add an end-game reward for other professions**
Apart from the leveling enjoyers, I feel like this challenge is incomplete without an end-game reward for especially Leatherworkers. Why only give Blacksmithers and Tailors a good reward? I'm not sure what it should be; Salt-shaker perks (like 1 day CD instead of 3) may screw the economy, giving them acces to all LW-specializations may be too OP? Maybe give them a special "Devilsaur helmet" at reaching 300 skill and level 60? A daily bundle of hides that contains a bunch of leathers, with small % for Devilsaur leather? Idk, just pitching some ideas. Doing this challenge as an Alchemist is odd, so skip that. Engineers too I guess. I think it should be focussed on the three gear-crafting professions.

The challenge may feel rewardinig, right? The Slow & Steady challenge has a reward every 10 levels. Why not add a little weapon reward every 10 levels to this challenge? Make it viable for all classes without making it overpowered or easy (you still need to craft your weapons!). Make it fun!

[For role players; you could make the Artisan-trainer/vendors shady guys. "Hey dude, want me to show you how to create a wicked dagger from some hides and a few raptor teeth?..."]

So here are a few ideas on what items to add and how to give them some more value.

 **Example 1: Unique materials for a regular dagger**
At level 27 you can learn how to make a [Scrap Metal Knife](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=5040) (same stats as linked item). You need 2x "Fel Blade Piece", which can drop at 10% drom [Lesser Felguard](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3772) in Ashenvale. These mobs are not part of any quest and this would give them a little niche. You need to combine the 2x "Fel Blade Pieces" with a Strong Flux, a Large Venom Sac and 2x Fine Thread.
The idea here is more of a scavenger; an Artisan can make weapons out of anything he finds. Itemizing like this gives more mobs purpose; the Felguards are equipped with glowing swords, so making a dagger out of the shattered pieces sounds cool to me. It's also in line with the Survival profession; same idea > you work with the scraps you find to survive.

 **Example 2: Easy materials for an easy mace**
At level 20 you can make a [Green Wooden Cudgel](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=2848) (also, same stats same as linked item). It's a very very average mace to have at level 20. So the crafting materials should also be very average. 5x Green Dye + 3x Simple Wood + 1x Strong Flux + 1x Fine Thread. This could be fine because only Vargant and Artisans challengers would be interested in these items. It gives them a little discount because crafting materials are cheaper than the price of the vendor who sells the white weapon.

 **Example 3: This should be HARD!!!**
Oh you want a level 20 [Curved Strongbow](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3027)? Pick up item 1 from Booty Bay, item 2 from a random vendor in Ashenvale, item 3 from killing a mob in Hillsbrad Foothills, and also fetch 5 Small Venom Sacs. You want a challenge? Here is an extra challenge along the way, you can make your leveling easier but you need to work HARD for it. Not my personal preference, but wanted to pitch it anyways.

I would vote for the first option. It gives you a little challenge to overcome, promotes being in the World and gathering some items, gives you an actual decent reward for the amount of effort and also gives a little niche to unused mobs in the world.

Thoughts?
