# Dear Turtle devs, please implement the WotLK-era Mekgineer's Chopper into the game

## Post 170290 by Paladin4life — 2026-03-07T15:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170290#p170290 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**1. What?**

[https://www.wowhead.com/wotlk/spell=608 ... rs-chopper](https://www.wowhead.com/wotlk/spell=60867/mekgineers-chopper) , as well as the Horde version.

 **2. Why?**

Because it is cool as all hell, that's why.

To expand on the answer; classic WoW, and even more so its Turtle version, whila already having a wide plethora of mounts to choose from, lacks in the mechanically-themed mounts. Yes, there are mechanostriders, but they are sadly only available to the shortking Alliance races, the Gnomeregan mech, while cool, requires a lengthy grind process to acquire, and the spider mech is limited to Alliance engineers willing to reach Exalted reputation with Gnomeregan.

Besides, none of the above are a whoopass fantasy chopper.

Adding the chopper would add a much needed variety to the limited mechanically-themed mounts available, especially if it could be obtained without a lengthy grind. More on this below.

 **3. How?**

Lets say that you agree with me that Mekgineer's Chopper has a place in Turtle WoW, and are beginning to wonder how exactly to implement this mount into the game. I have a few suggestions:

a), sell it via the Donation store. It is the simplest option that I am certain would offer a significant increase to your revenue. Put the chopper in the store, slap a price tag of something around a 1000 points and call it a day. This approach would be beneficial to your revenue and also mean that, as long as they are willing to spend real money, all the players could easily access the mount.

b), make it a rare drop. While making the Mekgineer's Chopper a rare drop is my personal least favourite option, it is still an option. I will not speak for others but personally I would be willing to repeat whatever piece of game's content would have a chance for dropping it repeatedly. Maybe the mad scientists in Nefarian's service have obtained a few of these?

c), make it a reward for a difficult questline. We want a cool motorcycle? Fair enough, but make us work for it. It could be a reward for completing a lengthy questline that would require us to explore the world and deep into some of the most dangerous places in it. Perhaps a group of gnomish and goblin engineers has banded together in an effort to create the world's best propelled travelling device, but they require a variety of materials and schematics that happen to be in possession of various dangerous persons on Azeroth? From the irradiated halls of Gnomeregan to the charred corridors of Blackwing Lair, the journey could take us anywhere. **Frankly, I would be willing to write the story for such a questline; I already have a skeleton of the idea on my mind.**

d), allow engineers to craft it, like they can in WotLK. This is my personal favourite idea. In my opinion, engineers are underused in Turtle WoW; teleports are great and bombs are handy, but other than that and a few other items like scopes, we have little to do. While blacksmiths, enchanters and such always have work to do, engineers are rarely required for anything. Being able to make cool mounts for people - mounts that, naturally, would have to require rare and expensive materials to craft - would bring much needed work and revenue to engineers.

Variations on the above ideas are also a possibility. Perhaps engineers could craft choppers but would first have to complete a questline? Or the recipe itself could be a rare dungeon/raid drop (personally I dislike this idea but it is an option)?

 **4. The wagon problem**

One of Mekgineer's Chopper's features was wagon, a second seat that allowed another player to ride along the motorcycle's driver. This feature, while welcome, is not, in my opinion, necessary for the mount to keep its cool factor. While I am not a programmer, I could believe that coding for a secondary passenger seat does not exist in the classic WoW build and that adding it could be difficult for Turtle devs. I could also understand that allowing other people to tag along would go against the philosophy of the game. If these are the case then by all means, do not feel obliged to add the wagon to the mount; even without it, the chopper would be awesomesauce.

I hope at least one of you have bothered to read the entirety of my idea, and hope even stronger that you will consider it. I already love Turtle WoW, as my time spent in the game, my achievements within it and the donations I've given to the team can attest to, but being able to ride my favourite, beloved mount would make the game that much more enjoyable.

Toodles.
