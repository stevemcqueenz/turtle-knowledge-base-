# Make the dungeon sets better

## Post 120326 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-18T16:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120326#p120326 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As you may know, WoW has always had dungeon sets that you get at level 60, with pieces that drop in Scholomance, Stratholme, and Blackrock Spire.

I think those sets are underused and even ignored in most cases.

I was thinking it would be nice to rebalance the dungeon sets for each specialty like they did with the raid sets, making them a bit more varied and an easier and more accessible option to get to start doing more complex raids.
