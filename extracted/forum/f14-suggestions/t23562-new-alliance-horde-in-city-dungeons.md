# New Alliance/Horde in-city Dungeons

## Post 164934 by Zashferret — 2026-01-11T23:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164934#p164934 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Orgrimmar and Stormwind are the only cities to have dungeons directly inside them, with Stormwind having two.

There are also some cities that have little reason to be there outside of maybe questing around them, such as Thunder Bluff and Darnassus. After my Night Elf Warrior got to level 12, I saw no reason to go back to Teldrassil/Darnassus aside from going to the rep quartermaster for the Sentinel's armor "set" that Darnassus rep unlocks at 25.

Here are some thoughts for a few dungeons to parallel the ones that are inside cities already:

 **Deepgnarl Barrow-Den**: A parallel to the Ragefire Chasm in Orgrimmar, this dungeon would be the same level as RFC and found within Darnassus. It would have four bosses, one being a Satyr, one Firbolg, one Banshee and one spider. The basic theme/premise would be based on the idea of a Satyr planning an attack on Darnassus by corrupting the firbolgs and spiders, along with raising the banshee as a servant.

 **Venture Co Incursion**: A horde parallel to The Stockades in Stormwind, this dungeon would be the same level and found in Thunder Bluff. The idea of it would tie into the Venture Co's activities in Mulgore, by having it be a mine/tunnel dug directly under the bluffs, intentionally or not.

 **The Undersewers**: A horde parallel to Stormwind Vault, this would be a dungeon accessed in Undercity (with outside access by alliance available in a little tunnel akin to the waterfall entrance to SWV in Elwynn). Level 60 and cantaining a "Deathguard" variant of the "Elite Guard" pseudo-set, and is a series of canals and sewer tunnels filled with horrible things the Apothecary Society discarded during experimentation and any remaining Scourge forces that found a place to hide and regroup.
