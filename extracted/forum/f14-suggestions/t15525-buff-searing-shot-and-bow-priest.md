# Buff Searing Shot and Bow Priest

## Post 106161 by Stenerjoe2 — 2024-11-03T15:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106161#p106161 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello wonderful devs!

As someone who really enjoys creative and alternative builds, I was immediately intrigued by the addition of night elf bow priests.

I’ve been doing a lot of testing at level 60, and so far using bows over Wands in general feels very lackluster or gimping- not only do bow priests miss out on the new wand talent in the disc tree, but there is also a minimum attack range penalty.
The damage scaling with agility on bows is just counterproductive on a priest, when you can easily get wands that has a lot more dps already build into them.

Now onto the most important part: Searing Shot.

I love the idea of searing shot, but so far it has 1 use, and that is to be cast while moving. Maybe that’s the whole intention behind it, but when you look at the damage it does ( around 170-200 on my 60 prieat) and the fact that it’s on the GCD, it feels veeeery niche to use.

My suggestions:
 1: Make it so the wand talent in disc tree also benefits bows.
 2: make searing shot deal holy damage to scale better, fantasy wise it can be recognized as “holy fire”
 3: buff the overall damage of searing shot by a substantial amount- no one in their right
Mind is gonna smack on agility gear just to make it do a wee bit of damage.
 4: a bit crazy and maybe OP suggestion, but allow priests to auto shoot WHILE casting other spells.

Just my 5 cents. The idea of letting night elf priests use bows is amazing and it would be cool
If it was actually useful and not just RP flavor
