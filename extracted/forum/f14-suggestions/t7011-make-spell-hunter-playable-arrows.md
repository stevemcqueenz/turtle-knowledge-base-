# Make Spell hunter playable + arrows

## Post 44413 by Fomwaag — 2023-05-04T12:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44413#p44413 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To make spell hunter viable you just need to make a few small changes. First we need a talent that converts AP to SP. This is the priority. This way spell hunters wont be interested in getting sp gear from casters. They would still need agility/attack power like regular hunters. The second change we need to be able to keep dps is to traps (explosive and immolation) scale from spell power. OFC you can also adjust talents as well like improved arcane shot/improved serpent sting doesn't worth 5 talent points right now so you could buff those to be viable choices.
There is a third suggestion: Hunters should be able to poison their arrows / light them on fire / freeze them essentially making their shots to do (partial) magical damage. Either do this by survival (secondary profession) or by hunter skills.
