Desolace was so deeply unpopular as a questing zone (according to Blizzard's own playerdata and metrics) that they changed the entire zone to be more like the Barrens (lush greenery, etc) in the Cataclysm world makeover and divided/destroyed other popular areas like Ashenvale and The Barrens itself (divided into North and South) to again encourage going to the newly-reformed Desolace.

Feralas by contrast is mainly there for Dire Maul but it is out of the way for most players who'd rather go to Tanaris instead.  Feralas isn't bad because of how it is designed or how it looks, but it isn't as popular as Tanaris (higher mob density, flat terrain to avoid LoS issues, easy mob grinding, tons of Goblin quests, valuable Goblin reputation with certain mob kills and quest completion).  Tanaris also has the Artisan cooking questline (Clamette Surprise) so nearly everyone HAS to go there at some point just for that.

Portals to Stonard and Theramore exist.  They haven't harmed anything despite already having zepplin/boat/flight paths available.  A mage-exclusive portal to Feathermoon would be fantastic for mages and make them even more desired in groups and otherwise.

From a work/coding perspective, it is a lot less time-consuming to create a new Mage Portal than it is to create an entirely new boat path and reshuffle both paths from other locations.
