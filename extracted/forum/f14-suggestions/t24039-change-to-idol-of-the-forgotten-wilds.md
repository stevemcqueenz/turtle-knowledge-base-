Hi,

It's been a while since i started to use this idole and i noticed that often, the sproutling AI is doing nothing.

In theory the sproutling has a 12 sec life duration and heal for X+% of healing bonus every 2 sec, no idea if it take crit and haste in consideration.
That should be in most of the case 5 or 6 cast, but when there is a tiny bit of movement or for some reason, the sproutling does nothing in this 12 sec or at most 1 cast. It really dimish the impact of this "bis" idole .

Regrowth cost a lot of mana and is not a really "fast" cast, 2 sec, it should be rewarded more.In most of the case the sproutling doesnt do more than 1.5% of added value,if les.

My proposition is to change the AI behavior to a totem AI, not allowing it to move, but removing the component that make the AI bug, so it will only heal in range (like a healing searing totem).
