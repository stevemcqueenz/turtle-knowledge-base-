I'm primarily referring to "Divine Shield" and "Ice Block".
This also applies to mob/NPC baseline immunities (like Black Dragons being immune to Fire attacks) tho.

I just despise these abilities/mechanics (as they work currently).

I understand that its fun to cockblock a Mage via pressing your "Trap Card/Magic Jammer" "Get out of Jail Free Card" right before his Pyroblast connects with your face.

I understand that its cool thematically to have a "Break" in the combat wherein both combatants have a moment to "regain their bearings" and/or "size each other up"
(like that duel in The Phantom Menace wherein Darth Maul & Qui Gon are briefly separated by that Force Field).

But I feel these abilities are overpowered because they not only have the incredible effect of making you invincible (for pretty long periods of time)
...but they also deeply screw over enemies
via utterly wasting their: cooldowns AND mana costs AND their seconds spent casting being a total waste of time.

They made that minor nerf to Divine Shield but its not enough.

MY SUGGESTIONS/POSSIBLE SOLUTION(s):

- You cannot target Immune targets (or make it like Feign Death or Vanish where you automatically untarget them)

- Any cost of a spell or attack made against an "Immune" target should be refunded. Cooldowns not triggered.

- Give some sort of brief visual "Warning" when they are about to cast an Immunity Shield so you can actually have counterplay (ignore them/cancel your spellcast)

- Warrior should be given "Shattering Throw" as Warrior is trash in PVP and Frost Mage + Paladin are both overpowered in PVP.
NOTE: What I am suggesting sort of already exists in the form of Spells such as "Purge" which completely fail to cast against "immune" targets (enemies that do not have any Magic-based buffs).

Blizzard knew it was unfun/unfair to waste time/mana casting spells uselessly against immune targets.
I'm pretty sure retail has it so you can't attack Immune targets. (Cannot Attack: Target Immune)
