Imo wow is at it's best in 5 man dungeons. For example the sound design shines when you can clearly distinguish betweeen individual abilities, barks and events. In a 20-40 raid that just becomes a cacophony of noise.

Similarly in 5 man content you can typically assess the tactical situation by looking at it visually while in a raid you'll need to rely on addons. Healing in a raid for instance feels like watching the raid frames much more than the actual game.

That is to say nothing of the weird gamey mechanical restrictions raids bring like totems only affecting the group of the shaman even when others are standing right next to them.

So yes Turtle devs, bring us more 5-man content for all level ranges. I also wonder how much people favor/oppose level 60+ scaling for extant dungeons like Shadowfang Keep or Blackfathom Depths.

I actually had a concept for something to handle this situation. A trinket, similar to the Scarlet Crusade Commission, but for early dungeons.

It would essentially work by scaling you down to the lowest limit of any pre-60 dungeon you enter. This would be for both your character's stats, and the stats on your gear being equivalent to a green of that level.

Each boss in a dungeon would have a new currency added to their loot table, which is awarded based on the XP you *would*  be earning at the appropriate level, but you would also have a maximized level difference penalty. That means if you have anyone in the party above the highest recommended level, you get nothing.

The trinket itself would be unique, so only one per person, but the effects could stack so running with more people using that trinket would increase the rate of currency gains. The currency would be used at a special transmog or disguise store, where EVERYTHING is BoP. Proof of accomplishment, bragging rights you can't get with gold. Maybe even some special titles or (if possible) character particle effects?

Anyways, it's just something I was thinking about a few days ago. I don't know if anyone would like a chase concept of this nature.
