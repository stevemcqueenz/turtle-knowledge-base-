# Rework dungeon sets to something diferent than raids

## Post 120333 by Caos (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-02-18T16:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120333#p120333 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dungeon sets are pretty weird right now. The druid set for example is for boomkin which nobody plays in dungeons.
They could be split into 3 diferent sets like raid sets but that would just make them a expensive and shittier version of t1 which would be pretty useless.
They could be insted be turned into some jack of trades sets for the hybrid classes.
I mean having more stats overall than raid sets but giving all stats that can be used by some class specialization making them worse than raid sets for performing some specific role.
For non hybrids its pretty hard to design, maybe giving them no hit at all. But making this at least for hybrids would be cool an allow more playstyles.

For example druid pieces would give all stats, spell power and healing power but they would have less spell power than balance t1, less healing than resto t1 and less agi/str than feral t1
