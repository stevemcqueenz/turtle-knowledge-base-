# Change speed of The End of Dreams to 2.6

## Post 54652 by Nyxxis — 2023-07-25T19:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54652#p54652 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I suggest to change speed of The End of Dreams to 2.6.

This change is mostly aimed of protection paladins.
Due to Misplaced Servo Arm, being weapon of choice for some prot pallys, having it's damage reduced in next patch, should have stronger option in form of boss drop, as trash drop items nerf was reasoned.
Changing speed to this value will result in better Holy Strike synergy, as well as a downside of lower amount of Judgement of Wisdom procs.
This change would have no impact on on other classes and specs, nor requires change of item level.
There are no slow end-game weapons with +spell damage on them.
For players that prefer fast types of weapons there are still Lok'amir il Romathis and Wraith Blade.
