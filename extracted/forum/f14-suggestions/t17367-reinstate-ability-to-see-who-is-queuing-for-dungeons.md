# Reinstate ability to see who is queuing for dungeons

## Post 118628 by I3enno — 2025-02-01T13:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118628#p118628 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I have recently started playing Turtle WoW again after a few months break. I am really please to see the player count has shot up, brilliant to see people are enjoying this fantastic server. Now, enough glazing. I am really upset with this change to the Group Finder and have heard a lot of feedback from players in game who feel the same way. Last time I played when queueing up for a dungeon, to save me waiting  a solid 1h+ (the time it takes now) I could go onto the group finder and see who is queueing up for similar level dungeons or dungeons like Scarlet Monastery where say if I want to run graveyard but there's little people running graveyard, I can ask people in Library if they want to join and 9/10 they are happy to join. Now I find myself waiting so damn long just to run one dungeon and it really makes the server feel empty, which is ironic because the player count is at an all time high.

I have heard from new players who think this is a dead server because they cannot seem to find a dungeon when as I say the server has had the highest player count it's ever had. I believe this is also putting people off running dungeons more than they normally would. I know it has certainly had this impact on myself.

I am not one usually  to complain, hell I can't remember the last time I've used a forum. But this change has genuinely upset me to the point where I've came here to express my dismay that I know a lot of other Turtle WoW players feel.

Please please for the love of God, reimplement the ability for people to see who is queuing for what dungeon in the group finder. I personally love running dungeons for loot and levelling and this is really affecting my love for this server.

Thank you to all the mods, admins & devs who make this game great, I have been loving the new dungeon content since my return
