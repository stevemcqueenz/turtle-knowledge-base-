# Warmode changes

## Post 22531 by Tokyoshift — 2022-08-16T11:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22531#p22531 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Warmode as a concept is a great idea. Its purpose is to provide a reward for players who are willing to disadvantage themselves by being semi-permanently flagged for PVP

Warmode in its current iteration has the following issues:
 1: it can only be disabled once, meaning you are perpetually stuck in warmode if you enable it again
 2: it offers no bonus at level 60, meaning there is no reason to not disable it once you hit 60
 3: its XP bonus is too great, meaning players who do not enjoy WPVP are encouraged to take warmode anyway and endure unenjoyable WPVP in order to play the game efficiently. Combined with issue 2, this creates an unfair dynamic where level 60s can stay out of warmode but opportunistically gank warmode lowbies who do not even want to WPVP
 4: its XP bonus applies to content where WPVP is impossible, such as dungeons. This undermines the risk vs reward purpose of warmode; as in dungeons it is only reward. This further encourages players who do not enjoy WPVP to enable warmode anyway

I propose that the following changes are made:
 1: allow warmode to be enabled or disabled an unlimited number of times, with a cooldown on reenabling it. Perhaps 24 hours. This will allow for players to freely enable or disable warmode, with the caveat of being unable to change their mind again for 24 hours.
 2: reduce the XP bonus to 10%, apply it to mob kills only, and only mobs killed outside of an instance. This lower rate will ensure that players who do not enjoy WPVP do not feel they are being penalised by not taking warmode. Applying the bonus to mob kills outside of an instance only also ensures that the bonus is only applicable where WPVP is possible (this ensures the reward is always paired with the risk). And preventing it from being used on quests will ensure dungeon quests (where there is no risk of WPVP) do not benefit from it.
 3: add a 10% bonus to gold looted (just do 1.1x the share of gold received) from mob kills outside of an instance. This provides an incentive for level 60s to enable warmode. By only increasing gold dropped outside of an instance, it ensures the reward is only there when the risk of WPVP is also present
 4: decrease flight costs by 50%. This will provide a small reward to all players; especially those who only raidlog
 5: apply a 10% increase to honour received while outside of a battleground

By implementing these changes, the rewards are proportionate to the disadvantages that come with being PVP flagged, and the rewards are only beneficial when there is the risk of WPVP. I feel that by making these changes, only players who desire WPVP will enable warmode, and players who do not desire WPVP will not feel they are at a disadvantage by not enabling warmode. What’s important here is that enabling PVP will always slow down character progression (levelling, gold gain, etc), so warmode should try to offset that with slightly increased rewards.

You are onto something here, but I'd like to expand on that.
 1. As I said in some other thread, there is no reward that "PvE" players would not want, unless it's useless.
 2. Therefore, whatever you do to "incentivize" WPvP will attract people who do not actually like WPvP, but will complain a lot.
 3. Therefore, you do not want to incentivize it. What you do want to do is to provide an alternative way for "PvP players" to get the same things from PvP that "PvE players" get from PvE. Which, XP and gold (well, for the levelers).

Now, the solution becomes almost obvious:
 - Give tokens on kill (or alterac valley style - spines, heads, ears). Make a quest to turn them in.
 - Scale quest xp reward with level.
 - Killing someone lower level than you awards less tokens, higher levels - more. (Grey levels, obviously none - see, kill 2 birds with 1 stone).

Basically, it's taking WPvP honor awards and adding the corresponding amounts of XP. Maybe even directly reward XP for PvP.
Honor rewards have diminishing returns to prevent wintrading, and that works, so would XP ones.

How does warmode fit into all this? Well, only warmode players would get this XP. Non-warmode players can still pvp and get just honor points.

The beauty of this is that both pvp and non-pvp players do not feel left behind. You'd only get XP for actually spending time pvping. Or you can choose to spend the same time questing, and get same XP in the same time.
