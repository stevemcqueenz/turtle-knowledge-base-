# A little advice about gold farmers

## Post 65257 by Wowy — 2023-09-18T10:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=65257#p65257 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Many Chinese players refuse to leave European servers because they are skeptical of Asian servers.
This is because a large number of gold farmers have flocked to Asian servers, hoping to monopolize production through rapid leveling to achieve their goal of obtaining money.
We have suffered from the pain of these gold farmers, who have destroyed the entire ecology of a server like monopoly organizations.
The important strategy of these gold farmers is to quickly level up and obtain a large amount of gold through 24-hour running scripts, AOE cooperation, and other methods to prepare for future monopoly behavior.
In dealing with these gold farmers, I have the following suggestions for Turtle Server:

 1.If the average experience and gold obtained within a certain time far exceed that of normal players, GM should track and observe their behavior for violations and measure whether it will destroy the game’s economic system.
 2.The online combat or collection time is far longer than that of other players. No one can work 24 hours without rest, but gold farmers can. They usually take turns among several people.
