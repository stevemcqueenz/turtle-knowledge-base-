I think that survival could have a solid identity if these types of items were added.

Gathering skill items.

Mining, skinning, herbalism, etc can all be considered a form of survival. Having the identity of survival revolve are creating items that provide skill ups would be great. You could even create a new type of herb that can only be gathered by having a level 60 epic survival item.
