# Convert hand of edward the odd

## Post 101502 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-09-19T14:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101502#p101502 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hand of Edward the odd is a BoE epic one-handed mace that procs "your next spell cast is instant" (it works even with hearthstone !) with an approximate PPM of 1 per minute (it's a tiny bit more)

( [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=22 ... rd-the-odd](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=2243/hand-of-edward-the-odd) )

Due to its incredible proc, the item is mandatory for some specs, such as tankadin and ensham, similarly to Wolfshead Helm for Feral druids, whose effect was baked into a Hyjal enchant.

I'm suggesting to give Edward the Odd a similar treatment, perhaps as an argent dawn weapon enchant. The item being an epic trash drop makes it prohibitively inaccessible for a piece of equipment that is too critical to the success of a playstyle.
