# Small suggestion regarding the upcoming content patch

## Post 6149 by Azjael — 2020-05-29T03:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6149#p6149 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey Turtles!

Let me start off by saying how excited I am about the upcoming content patch. High Elves, old Outland... I can't wait!  happy_turtle

Anyhow, I read on [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/wowservers/comments/goc731/turtle_wow_new_playable_races_goblins_and_high/) that the upcoming zone for the High Elves will be called "Alah'Thalas". I looked it up and I'm almost certain that it translates into "Kingdom of Light", which I think it's a great name, it has a nice ring to it, and also sounds very regal. However, I'd like to throw in my two cents.

"Thalas" is an ancient word common to all elvish dialects that translates into "Kingdom" (e. g. "Quel'Thalas" meaning "High Kingdom"). In the Warcraft universe, it is used for major cities that were founded long ago (like Eldre'thalas and Nar'thalas that were founded in ancient times by the Highborne, or Quel'Thalas being the youngest one, founded by the exiled Highborne 7000 years before Vanilla).

We get to learn both in the games and in novels that even some of the most important exiled High Elves live in lodges, like the ones we can see in Loch Modan, The Hinterlands and EPL (where the Ranger Lord Hawkspear and the High Priestess Skycaller dwell), while some others live in Human cities, like Dalaran and Stormwind. So to me, it wouldn't make a whole lot of sense to have a city as a central point for High Elves, much less a kingdom. Specially considering that up to this point in Vanilla the Sunwell has not yet been restored and the High Elves are scattered, direly trying to find a way to quench their thirst for Arcane (unlike the Sin'dorei, who are not suffering because they consume Fel). I think it would make way more sense to have a lodge (perhaps a bigger one that the others) as a central hub for them, or at least a small humble village.

Now for the name, I wouldn't go with a "Thalas" name for the same reasons stated above. Rather, I would stick to the lodges fashion. All these havens are called "Danil", which translates into "peak". If you notice, all of these havens in the game are located in high peaks; in *In the Shadow of the Sun* it is suggested that this is for security reasons. As for the first bit, I'd suggest "Ron", which loosely translates into "peace". In the book *Lands of Conflict* (page 20) it is stated that Lordaeron takes its name from three words in different languages: "lorn", which is dwarven for "land"; "daer" which is the human word for "people"; and "ronae", which is "peaceful" in the elven tongue, so Lordaeron means "land of the peaceful people". Since this new zone will be located in the Eastern Plaguelands, which was formerly Lordaeron, and since I think the High Elves are all about looking for a new, more peaceful beginning, I feel like "**Ron'Danil**" would be a proper, fitting name, since the Light is not that big of a deal in Quel'dorei culture anyway (except maybe for priesthood).

Anyways, sorry for the wall of text. This turned out to be longer than I expected   scared_turtle . RP and lore are huge for me, I just love to encounter things in the game that at first glance have no apparent meaning, but if you read the lore, then suddenly they seem to have a deep story behind, like abandoned catapults from past conflicts or hidden buildings like lodges hidden in high peaks   turtle_tongue .

I'm looking forward to reading your views on this matter. Thanks everyone for reading!
