That is not true on twow. Simply go kill a mob, check the experience you gain and invite someone else into the party. The mob gives the same total experience amount (rested excluded) no matter how many players this exp pool is split with. Only if you group with someone of different level (aka, the mob is green/grey for someone in the party), the math starts to change.

If you group with someone of equal killspeed and level you gain the same amount of kill experience per time spent. But many quests can usually be completed by more players at the same time. Such as "kill x rats" or "being me the head of a named mob" that count for all players in the party at the same time.

Two players usually have a higher killspeed than two individual players at the same time due to how they can burst mobs down and need less regeneration breaks in between.

So it is actually usually more efficient to level together if everyone is actually participating. But many people just wanna vibe and chill while leveling, not have to be social and coordinate.
