# Optional raid lockout extension

## Post 97692 by Shambakriger — 2024-06-23T22:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97692#p97692 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Idea is as the title suggests, the option for extending a specific raid lockout into the next week could greatly help more casual guilds progressing the more challenging raids, as well as guilds with limited time/players available for raiding.
This would become even more relevant with the addition of new, harder raid tiers like Kara and Grim Batol

## Post 97729 by Shambakriger — 2024-06-24T10:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97729#p97729 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The whole point would be to have it optional, for example the raid leader could send out something like a ready check and all the players in the group could choose whether they wanted to accept or not. Another option would to have it as a button on your raid lockout sheet, “extend raid lockout” click and your raid lockout would last into the next week.
Progressing naxxramas is tough as a casual guild and is usually not doable in one day, this change would alleviate the pressure of having to find a second day
