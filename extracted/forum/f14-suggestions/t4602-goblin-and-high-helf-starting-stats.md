# Goblin (and high helf) starting stats

## Post 27524 by Toastybagel — 2022-10-19T00:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27524#p27524 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Quick (and hopefully not too crazy) suggestion for Goblins - change starting stats from Orc to more fit Goblin! Orcs are strong, tough, and dumb - and goblins are just about the opposite, cunning, quick, sneaky. Giving them better agility/int starting stats would have 3 benefits:

 - More aligned with the Goblin Race (orc stats really don't make sense for high elf either)
 - A small but very welcome boost to Goblin hunters, mages, and warlocks which only have the gameplay bonus of the baby sprint to work with. Warriors and rogues get a nice benefit from the weapon skills.
 - Gives horde an option for at least one smart race - right now horde only has tough/dumb races as represented by starting stats.

For actual numbers something like 17 str, 23 agi, 19 stam, 22 int, 20 spirit. But even flat 20 like humans would better represent goblins and high elves over orcs I think. Thoughts?
