# Shaman elemental knockback idea

## Post 152983 by Pikestain — 2025-09-24T12:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152983#p152983 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Idea to replace eye of the storm for elemental shaman:

Give earth shield a 4%/8%/12% chance per point per stack to improve knockback resistance (this effect stacks with earth shields knock back resistance).

Also maybe swap this with Reverberation to make it accessible a bit lower level. This would then give 3 stacks of earth shield 36% knockback protection, stacking with the 30% baseline to a good range, and then when stable shields are specced it gets over 100%. Or maybe make it so it can achieve much higher knockback resistance and stable shields don't benefit it?

Also could add a secondary effect to give a % bonus per point per stack for lightening shield - perhaps something to boost nature damage or some nature aoe effect. But obviously not doing anything for watershield as imp water shield is good in resto where it is.

Be good to get other ideas on this!
