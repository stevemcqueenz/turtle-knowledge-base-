# Donation Shop - More Scarlet Crusade Armor.

## Post 127375 by Mrmurdoch — 2025-04-18T18:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127375#p127375 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there,

I want to suggest expanding the selection of Scarlet Crusade-themed appearance items in the donation shop, as the Scarlet Crusade has a large selection of armour pieces that players do not get access to in the game. I believe there is a strong interest in their aesthetics across the roleplaying community on Turtle Wow, and adding more transmog options, like different helms, shoulders, chest pieces, belts, legplates, boots, and gloves, would offer more variety for players who want to align their character visually with that theme.

Whether for roleplay or general flair, I think these additions would be a great way to support character customization and encourage donations.

I thank you for your consideration.
