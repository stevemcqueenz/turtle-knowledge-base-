Greetings to all who are interested! I apologize in advance if there will be inaccuracies in the wording or mistakes; English is not my native language. As you probably know, a ranked PVP set has six parts. Chest, head, shoulders, pants, boots and gloves. The only thing that is missing for the completeness of the visual component are the wristbands and belts. These items are already in the database, they have the appropriate appearance and characteristics. Why not add these items so that players can complete the appearance of the character. Many pvp players collect these sets for transmogrification, so why not finally introduce belts and wristbands to bring the appearance of the set to the final visual appearance? You can leave them off-set with a set of stats, just so they can be used later for transmogrification.
