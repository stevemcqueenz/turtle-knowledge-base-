# A few suggestions.

## Post 58349 by Armet — 2023-08-22T00:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58349#p58349 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First things fist since I am a warrior player I'd like to start there with a few suggestions(mostly for prot).

Rend should scale with AP similar to the way garrote does. Ex. Deals x+AP*0.05*7. So a level 60 arms warrior with imp rend, blue pvp gear and dungeon blues with a rank 6 bs up might deal about 449 damage over the 21 second duration. Which comes out to about 64 damage per tick. And if that seems high scale it down to maybe AP*0.03 making it about 47 per tick and about 329 total damage.

Revenge. Keep the current damage values but add AP scaling ex. Deals x+ap*0.10. So a level 60 warrior in dungeon blues for tanking with rank 6 bs up might deal 152-167 damage on a rank 5 revenge or if that's too much scale it at ap*0.07 for 126-141 damage.

New protection tree talent. Devastator. 20 points. Requires improved sunder armor. Sunder Armor now deals 50% weapon damage plus an addition 15 damage for each stack of sunder armor on the target. Could maybe also reduce the rage cost by an additional 2 points but idk. Suggest moving improved shield wall up a tier to make room.

I also think improved disciplines in the arms tree is not very good and it should be replaced with blood frenzy from tbc.

Also I am seeing an issue with rage generation currently while leveling my warrior especially when I'm just dealing with one mob at a time. To remedy this maybe we can add endless rage as a baseline passive at like level 30 or 40, I think that would solve any rage problems.

I don't see much issue with paladins except that maybe Reckoning should be change from a critical strike causing it to something more like redoubt. And maybe adding some damage reduction to improved righteous fury. Oh and maybe changing Ardent Defender to be a group wide 25% damage reduction on. 10 minute cooldown.

Next Assassination rogues need some love. I suggest moving Vigor down and replacing it with envenom.

Warlocks I think could use some reworks.
I think demonology needs felguard.

New affliction talent. Essence drain. 30 points. 4 ranks. deals 200/275/350/425 shadow damage over 3 seconds and returns 50% of the damage dealt as health to the Warlock. 3 second channel.

This damage can build up over its channel dealing more damage with each tick. Also maybe it should have a 6 to 12 second cooldown.

Maybe we can make Survival an actual melee tree for hunter with a few small changes.

Two new Survival talents.

 10 points, Serpent Strike. Deals nature damage over time comparable to serpent sting. Requires a melee weapon.

 30 points, Wyvern Strike, 4 ranks. Deals damage equal to 35% of attack power and causes your next 5 melee attacks within 10 seconds to deal 10/20/30/40 additional nature damage and restore 5/10/15/20 mana. Costs 100/140/180/220 mana. 12 second cooldown. Requires a melee weapon.

I have some other ideas for other classes but wanted to get these out first.
