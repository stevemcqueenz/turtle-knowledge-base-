Reposting a suggestion I once said to make school fishing more fun:
Because the special school fish like stonescale eels, firefin snappers, and oily blackmouths generally spawn on high mob density shores (The murloc-infested shore of hillsbrad foothills, tanaris beach with its pirates and turtles, feralas beach with its giants and water elementals)

A way to make school fishing more interactive instead of "loot the school, afk to wait for respawn, come again" => Make killing the mobs around these shores reduce the remaining spawn time of fishing schools in the area. The logic behind it is that these mobs are eating the fish themselves or scaring them away, so killing them will reattract fish there which is represented in gameplay by reducing the spawn rate (or by spawning bonus schools that only appear after killing a number of mobs in the subzone)
