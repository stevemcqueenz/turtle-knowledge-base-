# Please add a recipe queuing system to the Enchanting profession window

## Post 98387 by Beenis — 2024-07-02T18:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98387#p98387 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As many may not know, the Enchanting profession does not simply enchant, but also create important crafting goods like Enchanted Thorium and Enchanted leather, as well as highly-coveted consumables like Brilliant Mana Oil and Brilliant Wizard Oil.

This doesn't sound like a problem yet, but there's actually quite a big one; which is that enchanting is missing a crucial part of the crafting experience: the ability to queue recipes! (like every other profession in the game has!)

I would simply suggest to add an ability to queue up craftable items via the enchanting profession, such as 20x - Brilliant Wizard Oil. As of right now, you need to manually click every item you want to craft one by one, a huge QoL issue that no other profession has to deal with, the inconvenience doubled by the fact that these items are often driven down into the sub-1g profit zone.

I inquired with staff about their opinion on using auto-clicking software exclusively to craft bulk enchanting products and circumvent this lack of foresight by blizzard, but was told that's bannable due to "automation". However, I've recently become fully aware of the fact that the official Turtle WoW recommended add-ons pages includes dozens of add-ons that "automate" different aspects of the game. ATSW, or Advanced Trade Skill Window unfortunately does not allow for enchanting queuing, but does allow for you to queue up different recipes one after another, a feature not present in vanilla. For example, a blacksmith could queue up:

 - 10x dream steel belt buckle
 - 10x mithril belt buckle
 - 10x thorium belt buckle
 - 10x arcanite rod
 - 1x lionheart helm

then go AFK, and come back to an inventory full of completed goods. No shade to the makers of ATSW, I love the addon, just pointing out the line of what is considered an acceptable level of automation is extremely blurry right now. If I can get add-ons to make the game buff for me, queue recipes for me, automatically drink consumables, need/greed on certain loot items and automatically accept/reject party invites, it seems like a disparity in judgement that using these add-ons is promoted while automatically clicking in one place to save me from carpal tunnel syndrome due to crafting 50 enchanted leather and 50 enchanted thorium is bannable.

My proposal summed up: Please add a crafting queue to the Enchanting window like every other profession has, or allow/produce some other sort of workaround.

Thanks for reading!
