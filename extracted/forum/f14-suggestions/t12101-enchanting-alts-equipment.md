## Post 80293 by Ishilu (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-12-08T16:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80293#p80293 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I always find it a bit irritating when I want to level enchanting on a character and have to "waste" mats by repeatedly enchanting things like +1 stam on the same boots. There's simply no market for low level enchants, anyway, so allowing low level enchants to be sent to an alt wouldn't be a gamebreaker. It might even slightly alleviate the power gap between maxed out twinks and people who want to hop into a BG for laughs while they're leveling.

However, sending stuff like a crusader enchantment scroll to an alt just feels wrong to me, so I'd say the suggestion is only good for enchantments up to a certain level which would have to be specified.
