Good questions.

IF to Menethil airway is just to connect Ironforge to "its" harbor faster, cheaper so it's less of a hastle and you don't have to run through the deadly Wetlands or jump to your death from Dun Morogh to get the flight path the first time. Think of it like an extension to the Deeprun Tram kind of. I exclude a literal extension of the Tram because that would probably too much of an undertaking for the TWOW devs.

Menethil to Feathermoon: because it's in similar level ranges and there's already a relatively quick path by air from Auberdine/Darnassus to Feathermoon, AND it becomes a reason / opportunity to optimize the Feathermoon to Feralas connector, which should be a much shorter path on an Elven ship.

There's also no quick Alliance access to that part of the World. So where Horde has UC, Revantusk, Kargath, Grom Gol,  in the Eastern Kingdoms, the aim is to mirror the accessibility for Alliance in Kalimdor with Auberdine, Theramore, and Feathermoon.

To have true equality, perhaps your suggestion of the IF airfield to Shimmering flats airway for Alliance would be a great addition as well!
