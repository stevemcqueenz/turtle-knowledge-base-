Shaman tanking is a blast, especially at lower levels (before 50). I urge you to try it out. Here are some tips that could help you:
 -Get 5/5 convection, concussion and elemental focus. Your earth shocks are essentially your taunt and main aggro source. Get one or more "on hit effects" that give you nature or fire damage on each attack so elemental focus procs more often.
 -Use a fast 1h and shield. Get shield specialization 5/5 and other tanky talents from enh at your leisure. I recommend improved lightning shield and thunderhead for some good damage-per-mana. Don't go for Stormstrike, since it's too expensive, and gets eaten up by any nature on-hit effects you have, and sucks for fast 1h weapons. Instead go for the hit talent from resto.
 -Gear-wise, get the things with most armor and stamina. Int and spell power also help. Str, agi and attack power are low priority.
 -I used WF throughout my tanking journey for most bang, but if you need more threat go for rockbiter. Flametongue can also be useful for proccing Clearcasting if you don't have on-hit effects. Don't forget to plop down Stoneskin totem for extra damage mitigation, and kite with Earthbind if things get dicey. Grounding totem is awesome against spellcasters.
 -At early levels get rejuvenation potions, they're dirt cheap on AH and give both hp and mana. At max level tea is your best friend.
 -Above 50 it gets harder to shamatank, so you'll need to collect actual tanking gear with defense and whatnot, which is scarce in the mail variety. At 57 absolutely get Hand of Edward the Odd. Instacast chain lightning, healing wave or astral recall (kek) are awesome. Black grip of the destroyer also helps a little with mana sustain.
 -If you can get some spell crit on your gear, go for Reverberation, Elemental Devastation and Elemental Fury. Once you crit with an earth shock, that mob is glued to you permanently. Get onyxia buff before going in harder dungeons for that spell crit.
