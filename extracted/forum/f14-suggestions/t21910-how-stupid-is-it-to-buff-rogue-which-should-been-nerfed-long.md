There are 3 cores issues with rogues :

 1) they are actually not great in PvE, especially leveling. Nerfing their abilities will render them even weaker. With that being said, they removed everything that made Elesham able to destroy anyone in PvP in exchange for some PvE buffs, so they could definitely tune rogues down.

 2) stealth should have been renamed "permanent invisibility" a long time ago. Unless you have a massive level difference, stealth functions as an invisibility with 0 downsides.

 3) most hard CCs in the game are dispelable. Sheep can be dispeled. Trap can be dispeled. HoJ can be dispeled. But rogues have now 4 hard CCs that cannot be dispeled at all. Sap, cheap shot, kidney, and now even blind. What are you supposed to do? There's now literally 0 counterplay. Before, you had at least the tiny window when you could MAYBE do something against the blind, but now you can just literally drop your keyboard.
