# More Hillsbrad Quests

## Post 151648 by Xidus933 — 2025-09-14T03:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151648#p151648 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A large amount of real estate in Hillsbrad is taken up by questing areas meant exclusively for Horde. Namely, Hillsbrad Fields, Azurelode Mine, and Dun Garok. These areas are all filled with Alliance-friendly NPCs, many of them even named, but -- aside from the Hillsbrad Apprentice Blacksmiths, which function as trash vendors for Alliance -- there's no real reason for Alliance characters to ever really go to any of these subzones.

My proposal is to do something like what was done with the Anchor's Edge area added to Barrens -- still primarily Horde-centric, but a few small quests to encourage Alliance players to venture out there too. Given that Hillsbrad was evidently designed to encourage incidental WPVP by placing opposing towns so close to eachother (and even giving Horde a quest chain to spawn a hostile mob in the Alliance town), it would likewise help to encourage more Alliance players into these other subzones with breadcrumbs and quest rewards.
