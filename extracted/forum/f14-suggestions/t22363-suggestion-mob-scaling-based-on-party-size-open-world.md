# [Suggestion] Mob scaling based on party size (open world)

## Post 155275 by HippoMilo — 2025-10-12T15:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155275#p155275 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I think it would be cool to, have a mode that(?), scales mobs based on team size. I think this would make it more viable to group with friends and level together, versus everyone going off on their own to level and then grouping only for elite quest/dugeons out of necessity.

(I don't know what a good implementation would be)
I was thinking maybe this can work like the drop tables. There is a "scale table" that checks how big the party is and will scale the mob accordingly. And I think it will be cool to have special abilities that the mob will have access to as it scales. Making it so the back line has to be engaged, dodge incoming attacks, etc.

Party of 1 = Normal Stats (nothing has changed. Regular solo play)
Party of 2 = hp + 50%, attack dmg + 10%, defense +10%, etc.
Party of 3 = hp x 2, attack dmg + 15%, defense + 15%, etc. Added ability
Party of 4 = .....
Party of 5 = .....
(Would need to play around with the stat boosts to have a good level of difficulty, and not be overpowered)

I think this will add value to the game, for example, watching streamers go into a dungeon under leveled, or do a 3 man deadmines. They get a higher challenge, and now you have the group dynamics in play as well. When one person makes a mistake the team has to adjust. (That was my favorite part playing wow 20 years ago, a friend and I on our laptops in the same room trying to figure out how to kill something in the game)

A big part of this would be allowing the XP gain to stay the same as if playing solo, and to allow normal looting for each person, as to not disincentivize group play. I think that would mean this type of game play would only be "active" if certain conditions are met. Probably will have to make it a leveling challenge (like craftsman) and the party members will need to be within a certain level range of eachother (no more then a 3 level difference?).

The thing that makes me excited about it is the added abilities once a party is bigger. Horizontally expanding the game play.

For example,
You and a friend start new toons, both go to human area, pick up the quests and party up. When you go to kill a kolbolt, they are strong enough that for 2 people it feels like a regular solo fight. They get full xp, and loot, they get a regular difficulty play feel, and can group play from 1 to 60.

I guess it would be good to make it a leveling challenge, a good name for this could be "Co-Dependent", haha.
