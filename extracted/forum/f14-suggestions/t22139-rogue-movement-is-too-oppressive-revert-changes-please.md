I see that stealth mechanics must be a complex phenomenon to you so let me break it down to you in a way you can understand. Stealth classes get to choose their battles and they effectively close the distance between them and their targets with mostly no counter. If the Rogue does not manage to stunlock their targets to death the rogue can:

 * Sprint
 * Sprint out of roots if talented
 * Vanish out of roots
 * Do all of the above twice with preparation

On the opposite side of this, rogues don’t just move fast themselves, they also strip their targets of speed and mobility. Gouge, Blind, Sap and stuns completely stop you in place, while slows and ranged slows cut your movement down to a crawl. Blind even resets with Preparation, giving them another full stop on top of everything else. So while the rogue always has tools to accelerate or reset their own movement, they simultaneously reduce yours to zero.

And before everyone complains about cooldowns, please consider an Arena setting.
