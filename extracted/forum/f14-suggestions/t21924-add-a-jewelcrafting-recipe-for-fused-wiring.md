# Add a Jewelcrafting Recipe for Fused Wiring

## Post 151878 by Xidus933 — 2025-09-15T23:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151878#p151878 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently the only way to obtain Fused Wiring is from a handful of mechanical enemies (all level 30-60, with the lower end being elites from Gnomeregan) or from letting an Engineering-built Target Dummy die and then looting it. However, the item is used in several low-to-midlevel Engineering recipes (like the Goblin Jumper Cables) as well as two quests. Given the difficulty in acquiring it, it tends to go for exorbitant amounts on the AH (2g40s at minimum as of this writing).

My suggestion, then, is to implement something like the Goblin Rocket Fuel Recipe: an Engineer has to create a recipe using Blank Parchment and Engineer's Ink, and then hand the recipe to another player to learn and use in a different profession to create the reagent the Engineer needs.

In this case, Jewelcrafting would likely be the natural fit. This was even the actual case on retail, where JC was given a Fused Wiring recipe.
