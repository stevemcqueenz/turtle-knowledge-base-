Lacerate was replaced by Wyvern Sting during Vanilla (replaced patch 1.7.0).
Lacerate was rightly(!) regarded as the worst talent in the game, let alone the worst 31 talent in the game at the time.

Lacerate does not have a place among Hunter spells in 1.12.1 patch conditions.  For one thing, a DoT melee spell serves remarkably little purpose when stacked up against Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite and Counterattack melee spells.

At BEST you could perhaps argue for a kind of enabling DoT on Hit bonus to the other melee spells (kind of like Arms Warrior bleeds) ... but even that's a stretch that I find hard to justify (and I play a [Beast o' Melee Hunter](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=26&t=1197) !).

Lacerate was removed for multiple good reasons.
Reviving it lacks good reasons, particularly in its original form.
