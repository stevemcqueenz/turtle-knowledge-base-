# Please balance spellcasting range for arcane mage

## Post 54851 by Xa1amandor — 2023-07-27T10:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54851#p54851 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings, turtles.   smiling_turtle_head

Though it's clear that TWoW has made a great effort to increase the viability of arcane specialization in myriad ways, (and they have) there remains one glaring problem faced by the arcane mage: spellcasting range.

In fact, the arcane mage is the only ranged fighter without an improved-distance talent for their chosen specialization.

Hunters have Hawk Eye, Druids have Nature's Reach, shamans have Storm Reach, fire mages have Flame Throwing, frost mages have Arctic Reach, warlocks have Grim Reach, shadow priests have Shadow Reach, and even holy priests have Holy Reach!

Why should the arcane mage be deprived this benefit?

Fortunately, a fix would be rather simple: use WotLK's version of the Magic Attunement talent.

For contrast, here's the talent in both Classic & WotLK below...

Magic Attunement (Rank 2)
Talent
Requires Mage
Increases the effect of your Amplify Magic
and Dampen Magic spells by 50%.

&

Magic Attunement (Rank 2)
Talent
Requires Mage
Increases the range of your Arcane Spells
by 6 yards and the effect of your Amplify Magic
and Dampen Magic spells by 50%.

Would this not fix the issue in one swift update?

Please let me know your thoughts, criticisms and support in the comments.
