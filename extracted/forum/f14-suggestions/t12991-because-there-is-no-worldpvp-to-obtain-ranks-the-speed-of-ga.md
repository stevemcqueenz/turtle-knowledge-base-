## Post 86996 by Pvpppppppp — 2024-02-05T02:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86996#p86996 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Because the battlefield is not closed on the PVP server, no one is willing to obtain military ranks through World PVP, and it also leads to the game progress of PVE players being too fast, and the game development speed cannot keep up with the game progress of PVE players. Resulting in a large number of players AFK.

I hope to close all battlefields as soon as possible, and only open world PVP to obtain rank levels. World PVP gameplay is very scarce in today's games around the world, and the biggest feature of WOW is world PVP.

PVP players gain ranks by interfering with PVE players, and PVE players will also fight back against PVP players by huddling in order to enter the dungeon smoothly. In this interaction, what seems to be that PVP players and PVE players get gear more slowly. However, compared to the dungeon event, which is like going to work, such a fun interaction brings fun to both players. All kinds of materials in the game are consumed, balancing the economy, and PVE players will not be AFK because they get all the equipment too quickly. Gamers will also be able to spend a lot more time online. The progress of game development will not be rushed.

Therefore, closing the battlefield and promoting world PvP is a model that benefits everyone.
