# Arcane talent tree changes for Mages

## Post 57691 by Jc473 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-17T05:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57691#p57691 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the upcoming ‘1.17 Beyond the Greymane Wall’ patch including another round of class balance changes, I figured it would be good to have a discussion on the Mage's Arcane tree and improve it with the aim of encouraging more pure Arcane Mages!

DISCLAIMER – I know that Vanilla Mages are in a good place, hence why TurtleWoW didn’t make many changes to them. However, I feel like it’s only a good thing if there is more competition across all specs. These changes are not meant to buff the standard Frost/Fire specs you see in all forms of the game.

Here are a few ideas:

 **Magic Absorption** – in Classic, other than when you are grinding low level dungeons for gold, full spell resists are uncommon. Furthermore, gaining only 10 spell resist from the talent is negligible. I think this talent should provide more magic resistance and have it scale with your level. How about 0.2 per talent point which, at 5/5 points, would result in 1 resist/level --> 60 resist at level 60. I think this level of resistance would be high enough to feel like the secondary component (getting mana back) happens 'every now and again' instead of 'almost never'.

 **Arcane Resilience** – I think the theme of this talent is interesting, but the output is very lacklustre for a milestone (11 points) talent. At level 60, and in good gear, this is giving you a couple hundred armour which is barely noticeable. Even if you doubled/tripled it, I don’t think the effects would be that noticeable. But what if the bonus armour was scaled to the Mage’s current mana at a percentage of say, 25%? This would mean that Mages are at their toughest at the start of a fight (or after Evocating) and become ‘weaker’ the more they spend their mana. At 25%, a fairly well geared Mage would probably get 1200-1600 armour at the start of a fight, which would be reasonably powerful, but wear off as the fight progresses when their mana is spent. I think this dynamic is interesting and would make the milestone talent more special.

 **Improved Mana Shield** – Is the ‘20% mana loss reduction’ big enough? At max rank of Mana Shield, you absorb 570 physical damage and lose 1140 (double) mana. Is losing only 1026 (1:1.8) enough to entice people into taking this talent? I feel like if it reduced the ratio 1:2 to 1:1, this would be a more appealing choice.

 **NEW TALENT – Improved Evocation (Evoker?)** – Positioned deep into the tree (second or third last row). Two points reducing Evocation’s cooldown by 2/4 minutes. I always thought that pure Arcane Mages should either have exclusive rights to Evocation or, at least, a better version of it. As mentioned throughout this post, I think the Arcane Mage’s strength should be a deeper mana pool and/or ways to sustain it.

 **Brilliance Aura** – I’ve read through many comments on the Discord server at how this talent feels very lacklustre for a 31 point milestone. I think the talent could either be a straight forward ‘30% mana regen while casting’ for the group or 1% of Mage’s max mana in the form of ‘X mana per 5’ (For a T1/T2 mage, this would give about 50-60 mana per 5).
From a raiding perspective, it seems widely accepted that Frost/Fire will provide better damage output, which is fine, as long as Arcane can bring something else to the raid. If that ‘something’ was a better Brilliance Aura, I think this would be a good balance. It would be really neat if people wanted to bring an Arcane Mage so it could help sustain a healer group, for example.

I welcome feedback on these proposed changes. Please don’t get hung up on the proposed values, I’m more interested on whether people like the concept of each change.
Feel free to also comment on any other Arcane talents not covered in this post.

Thanks for the feedback! As mentioned at the bottom of my post, I was more interested in whether people agreed with concept of the proposals, not necessarily the given numbers.

However, to address your points:
 1) **Magic Absorption** - Yes, 60 to all resistances is quite strong on the face of it but the point is to actually make the secondary effect (mana restoration) proc 'every now and again' instead of 'almost never'. Ask yourself, in PvP, how often would even having 60 resist cause you to fully resist a spell? If you're concerned at the amount of damage reduction 60 resist would give, I agree with you; it would make the mage a pretty tough opponent for casters to face. So, perhaps it should only give 30? But then we're back to the problem of the secondary effect being too rare an occurence.
 **Alternate solution** - Forget the magic resistance / mana gain parts; What if, instead, each talent point gave 4% of magic damage received back as mana? At 5/5, you'd get 20%. So, in a 1v1 caster PvP encounter, you could effectively take 20% of your health and add that to your mana pool (probably around 600 mana). This mechanic seems interesting enough and that number seems balanced? Obviously, it would get more value if you were received heals in a PvP or PvE setting!

 2) **Arcane Resillience** - Remember, that 1200-1600 armour would only be in effect at the start of a fight (assuming you were full mana). It would rapidly diminish once you started depleting your mana. I'd reckon in PvP fight, within about 10-15 seconds you'd blow through half your mana thus halving the bonus armour.

 3) **Improved Evocation** - I wasn't overly precious about this one, to be honest. However, I'll defend it anyway. Yes, Arcane mages are very mana efficient when they using their Missiles in single target situations. But what about when they aoe in a dungeon/pvp/raid situation? They'd obliterate their mana pool rapidly. So, why not let the Arcane mage shine in this situation by being a bit more resilient to going 'out of mana' in these situations? Also, it wouldn't just be for Arcane mages. Think of the standard 31/0/20 spec that is used in MC/BWL? Would you sacrifice points in either Frost Channeling or Arctic Reach for this talent? I think this is an interesting choice for the player to make!

 4) **Brilliance Aura** - I believe the talent snapshots your mana regen hence why you are pointing out the 'innervate' and 'evocation' situations. Even with the upcoming patch, you'll still be able to toggle on/off this ability to leverage these situations. However, this is the awkward thing I'm referring to; is it good that only under these conditions that this aura actually becomes noticeable? I can take it further and say, is it good for the average player (who isn't aware of these sneaky opportunities) to have to jump through this hoop to get a noticeable benefit? This is why I just wanted to make its baseline power better even if you lose the quirky opportunities...

 5) **More abilities**  - By the way, even if they gave us stuff like Arcane Blast, I would **still** have made this post. Why? Because these talents are weak and the proposed changes, altogether, would encourage more build diversity which I think is only a good thing. So, I don't consider this 'change for the sake of change' :)
I echo Xudo's post; I think that Mages should be using the full arsenal of spells regardless of spec. In fact, in PvP, they very much do! In raiding/dungeons, obviously less so, but these environments have aoe situations which definitely call for a wider range of spells to be used.
