# Restore in-game/server time flow to normal

## Post 164175 by Lachtancz — 2026-01-01T14:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164175#p164175 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

While I understand the good intention behind accelerating the server/in-game time flow so all players could experience day and night regardless of their local time, I find this change creates more confusion and complications than benefits:

 1. Addon/timer issues – Many timer and scheduling addons now struggle to account for the faster time, leading to inaccuracies and disruptions for players relying on them.
 2. Player confusion – The accelerated day/night cycle is already confusing for players accustomed to normal time progression and experiencing day or night based on their continent.
 3. In-game realism – The game world already has a natural time offset between the two continents, allowing players to experience daytime at different locations. Simply traveling across continents gives players the opportunity to "play during the day" without modifying server time.

Returning the time flow to normal would simplify addon functionability, reduce confusion and frankly - accelerating time seem a bit over-the-top when the players frequently move across continents anyway, often ending up with the nighttime they were trying to avoid just an hour ago.
