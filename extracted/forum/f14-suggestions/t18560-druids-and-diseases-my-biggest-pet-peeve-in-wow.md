# Druids and Diseases - My Biggest Pet Peeve in WoW

## Post 126516 by tr00dudu — 2025-04-12T10:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126516#p126516 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Every time I'm playing a druid and I get a disease I try to cure it just to realize I can't. It seems so intuitive to me that a class that's supposed to be this incredible master of nature, healing, shapeshifting, would be able to cure something like an infection from a wolf bite.

I also do not think it would affect any kind of end game balance. There's 1 disease ability in PvP and a couple of not super meaningful diseases in Naxx. I think that's it?

I don't think giving druids cure disease ability would break the game, but it would make a LOT of sense. Hell, make it have cast time and be out of combat if you have to for balance's sake...

Even though I started this thread, and not being able to treat disease really does bother me, I have to agree that having 3 normal cleanses would be too generic.

From an RP perspective, I can also see why a druid would prefer removing curses over diseases, as diseases could be viewed as mostly natural (except plague stuff), whereas curses are a magic/demonic perversion of the natural order. Now whether it's more likely for druids in Warcraft universe to have the power or ability to remove curses vs diseases... I still think removing diseases wins.

Perhaps, as I originally mentioned, some kind of RP tier of (cleanse) abilities would be in order, that does not disrupt the current balancing of the game, but makes RP and lore wise sense. Make it any combination or all of: not usable in combat, 3s+ cast time, melee range, cheap reagent use.

Kind of off topic but: I don't see why a mage (master of the arcane..) would not be able to remove a magic affliction after carefully studying it for several seconds. Rogues dabble in poisons too much to not have antidotes on hand at all times (reagent use, self only? But then this is basically already in first aid? Hmm).
