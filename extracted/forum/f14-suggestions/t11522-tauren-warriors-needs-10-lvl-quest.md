# Tauren warriors needs 10 lvl quest

## Post 76250 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-07T05:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76250#p76250 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently when you reach 10 lvl as tauren, you get quest [Veteran Uzzek](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1505) from [Krang Stonehoof <Warrior Trainer>](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3063) in Mulgore.

I know that as tauren you anyway will get to Barrens and do quests there, but almost all other races have their own warrior quest. Tauren hunters also have own pet quest. Only tauren warriors should travel to another starting zone to get defensive stance.
Trolls and orcs have same class quest too, but they start in same zone unlike taurens.

I think that tauren warriors should have their own 10 lvl quest.
