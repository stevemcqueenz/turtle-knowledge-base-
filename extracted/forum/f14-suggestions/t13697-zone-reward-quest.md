Idk man, this might be confusing for most people. Here's two examples that might screw up your counter:

Let's take the 225+ cooking quest. The cooking trainer in your capital city gives you a breadcrumb quest to go to Tanaris to find the 225+ cooking quest (To Gadgetzan you go). If you go to Tanaris without this quest, you can still start the cooking quest there, but the breadcrumb quest that leads you there is no longer available. So now you'd never be able to complete all quests in your capital city and your sw or og quest counter would forever remain at 49/50. There's a plethora of breadcrumb quests that work this way.

Take another quest like The Ashenvale Hunt. Three different NPCs in three different places ask you to go to Ashenvale and speak to a Tauren in splintertree post. You can only ever accept one of them. The other two will disappear once you started the quest, again screwing up your quest counter for these two places.

Sounds like a whole lot of work for little benefit.
