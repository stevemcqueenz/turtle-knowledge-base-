# Stop PVP Honor Decay at the 0% of your current rank

## Post 32207 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-01-06T21:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32207#p32207 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So the PVP ranking system is well known for being completely hostile to people's time and all. People propose radical cahnges, the TBC system, this and that and whatnot... which would all fix the big issues but risk leaving others unhappy with the replacement system.

The biggest problem, as we know, is that if you intend to reach a rank, you need to completely set aside your life for 2-4 months when climbing.

This problem would be immensely mitigated, if honor decay never took you below the 0% of your current rank, like if you were 10% through rank 13, you would only drop to 0% rank 13.

People could choose to rank early on in spurts of 1 week, a few weeks to a month, or still go full degenerate. But the key is that people would be able to pause without losing more than just a little progress. This keeps the system intact almost to a tee, but yeah mitigates its worst issue and allows high rankers to actually have a possibility of taking a break many times down the line.

This also allows people to stay their highest rank for the title which they have rightfully earned, and show it off in their title instead of just their gear.

When this system is implemented, a one time update of everyone's rank to their highest on record should be performed. Someone who got rank 14 should be r14 forever, for the title and for purchasing more of the gear. There's no reason people should feel compelled to buy like 5 copies of their weapons "just in case" they ever need them later, nah, those items should remain permanently unlocked for the character.
