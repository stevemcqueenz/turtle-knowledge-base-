# Early Bag Buffs

## Post 174166 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-04-06T10:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174166#p174166 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

There are a few bags in the early game that, honestly, I just don't ever see the point of getting. Mainly the 4-slot quest bags, but I think that an honourable mention also has to go to the Kodo Hide Bag, which is a Horde-exclusive time sink. All of these are just more trouble than they're worth, and you would have to be doing the kind of speedrunning where you'd skip them anyway to not already have a full set of at least 6-slot bags by the time you can get them, IMO.

For the two quest bags, I would suggest simply turning them into 8-slot ration bags. It's slightly redundant with the Survival skill, but at least makes the quests worth doing for people who aren't levelling their Survival.

The Kodo bag is a bit more of a difficult issue, simply because it's something that only exists on Horde side, but an interesting option could be to change it to a 6-slot "Kodo Hide Quiver" with 11% attack speed, and add a similar quest recipe on Alliance side for a matching Ammo Pouch.
