# [ Hunter ] - Massive talent changes and tweaks

## Post 162812 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-12-14T21:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162812#p162812 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I've delayed posting this for a very long time. I'm still updating things, so some stuff may be overtuned right now and others may be undertunned, but I'd rather post this before another wave of class changes drops. Please focus on the concepts, numbers can always be fine tuned. Throughout the days I'll be adding more in-depth elaborations on the talent changes and possible builds.

 ****Goals****
 1. Give each Hunter spec distinct identity, utility and niche.
 2. Give each spec more build path variety
 3. Improve build diversity

 **Global changes**

1. Aimed Shot - *No longer a universal ability, obtained through the Marksmenship tree like before*
2. Disengage threat reduction increased: *(Old) -140/280/405, [New] -200, 400, 600.*
3. Distracting Shot - *CD increased to 15 seconds. Now interrupts the target's casting.*
4. Aspect of the Wolf [**Removed**]
5. Aspect of the Shark [**New**] - *The hunter takes on the aspects of a shark, increasing swim speed by 30%. If the hunter takes damage, he will be dazed for 4 sec. Only one Aspect can be active at a time.*
6. Aspect of the Monkey **[Rank 2 - New]** - *The hunter takes on the aspects of a monkey, increasing chance to dodge by 8% **and also reducing fall damage**. Only one Aspect can be active at a time. (Fall damage reduction is the same as the Rogue's Safe Fall passive ability)*
7. Eyes of the Beast - While active, pet attacks tag the target.
8. Cower (all ranks) - Now costs 0 training points.
9. Wing Clip - damage component removed.
Comments on the global changes  - Aimed Shot as it is right now is hardly ever used. It's not part of the rotation of any hunter spec and if we buff the ability baseline, then it will end up like Steady Shot, where both BM and Marksmanship spam it. No good. That's why it's going to be moved to be a Marksmanship ability only again, and it is further buffed in that talent tree. Its visuals will also be changed, in order to make the ability stand out more as a powerful high damaging attack.

 - Current Disengage values are not that great. Rank 3 is especially bad, providing less threat reduction than the jump from rank 1 to rank 2, all while the mana cost having the same increment. Disengage is not a bad ability by design, and is useful on Survival hunters. A high spirit leveling build with Rank 2 Disengage grants you very good kpm, all while being HP and Mana efficient. However even with less dmg optimized builds, and attacking from behind, you pull threat from your pet way too easily. Additionally, the threat reduction right now is not big enough to where you reliably lose aggro if you pull aggro from a boss by accident. As such, the values need to be boosted significantly in order to make them useful.

 - Distracting Shot is hardly ever used. There are some very niche situations where you want to pull aggro (your pet is close to dying OR more importantly, you need to pull aggro away from your healer). That's fine, we won't change that niche. We will however give the Hunter some additional PvP and PvE utility in the form of an interrupt. It's not as good as a kick or a flat out stun, since the target can immediately cast again. However it can find its uses in delaying that case. Delaying heals in PvP or the annoying Sleep in WC for example. Rank 1 Distracting Shot is cheap mana wise and doesn't cause much aggro later on, so it can be reliably used for its interrupt purpose.

 - I'm quite opposed to the design of Melee hunters on the server. They are turned into offering everything that a ranged Hunter does, but simply at melee range. I go more in depth in my Survival tree remake, but Aspect of the Wolf is any case is not the way to go.

 - Aspect of the Shark is just a flavorful ability. Nothing more nothing less.

 - Aspect of the Monkey Rank 2 is also a flavorful addition. Can be added, can be ignored. I personally think it will be fun.

 - Eyes of the Beast change is definitely needed. Right now pets don't tag their target. Meaning that if the pet kills the target without the hunter having ever hit it once, he will not gain any XP and won't be able to loot the corpse. This was added as a form of anti-cheat for botters and afk farmers. That's fine, but let's at least remove it while Eyes of the Beast is active. It can still technically be exploited, but it will require botting, and the Turtle team does a great job of pruning such users.

 - Cower can be a very useful pet ability, but as of right now is rarely ever if ever used by hunters. One of the reasons is that it uses up Training Points that are much better spent somewhere else, like survivability or more damage. Growl right now costs 0 training points and Cower is its direct counterpart, so I think it's fair to remove its training points as well.

---
 ****Beast Mastery****

Key changes:
1. Improved Pet damage in a more straightforward way.
2. Improved Pet survivability via late talents.
3. More effective pet tanking is available. I was initially against this, but Tank is one of the hardest roles to find a group for. With the reworked talents the pet will be better at tanking dungeons, but for raids a dedicated tank will still be needed.
4. A pet tank build can still be useful in dungeon/raid situations, where your pet takes aggro for a moment while the healers res up the main tank in case they die.
5. Reworked utility talents to give players more incentive to pick them up.
6. Core build paths: Pat tank and Pet damage
Link to new talent tree - <https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/z9X4FcH40v>
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/Vkcz5VWH/Beast-Mastery-by-Sven-S2-%28Ver-7-0%29.png)

---
 ****Marksmanship****

Key changes:
1. Removed Sting related talents.
2. New early Marksmanship talents provide more dps through white attacks
3. Mana related talents have been improved and placed deeper in the tree.
4. Piercing Shots and Endless Quiver have been removed, as they interacted negatively with Scatter Shot
5. Improved AoE damage.
6. Additional sources of Arcane Damage have been introduced.
7. Playstyle without a pet has been introduced.
8. Only hunter not affected by the Dead Zone
9. Core builds paths: PvE damage or PvP
Link to new talent tree - <https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/ulHurbRfyk>
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/0jYcnnbt/Marksmanship-by-Sven-S2-%28Ver-3-0%29.png)

---
 ****Survival****

Key changes:
1. Improved melee and trap damage during the leveling stage of the game.
2. Added Sting related talents and boosted their strength.
3. Use of traps during combat is accessible at a much earlier level.
4. Increased trap damage scaling. (values are TBD, which is why they're not specified)
5. Can be played either as a melee hunter, ranged hunter or hybrid.
6. Removed Carve and reworked Lacerate a bit
7. Core builds paths: Melee PvE with traps and Ranged PvP with stings
Link to new talent tree - <https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/v0XlGl4ow4>

I'm mostly going to comment on BM stuff as that's what interests me the most.

To begin with, Distracting Shot is situational but not hardly ever used. It is crucial for kiting. When you kite Drakk, for instance, you open with Aimed Shot > Distracting Shot + Auto-Shot and that's enough to keep him, if no one touches him. With your changes, a SV hunter would not be able to use Aimed Shot and would have more trouble with this. The Drakk example only requires this sequence but what about kiting something else for longer that can be in range and pose no ranged attack to threaten you? You can solo anything in the game as long as it's kiteable and DS helps a lot by keeping threat on you and not your pet as you run. Increasing Distracting Shot's CD so much would strip hunter of one of its basic kiting tools. This is partially because we have no mobile shots since Arcane Shot has no AP scaling so if we had more of those, then your suggestion would be better.

Your BM tree is too passive. Essentially, the rotation will be Devour every 5 seconds and Multi-Shot every 10 seconds. That's it. I don't like Steady Shot because I think it's bad game design to have a mechanic hidden from the player (the cast time) that also interacts with another mechanic (Auto-Shot) with no way of knowing this in-game unless someone tells you and you use an addon. However, Steady Shot adds something to go for in the rotation and removing it from ranged BM would make the spec absolutely passive and mind-numbing. Your tree has mostly zero damage interaction between hunter and pet (except for Devour): one critting doesn't give something to the other, using this ability doesn't enable that one or give the pet a stacking buff, etc. I do like the idea behind Elusiveness because I find current Kill Command to be extremely useful due to the fact that healers and dispellers ignore pets all the time so at least we have a way of doing it.

I also don't like Eyes of the Beast for more than its scouting and pulling capabilities. EotB should never be the main or one of the main ways of a hunter to deal damage. We're not druids and we should not be playing as a pet that barely has 4 buttons to press (a bit more with your suggestions). It's already bad enough in the current state because it allows Scorpid Poison snapshotting, which would be better if it was done in other ways but not this one, imho.

Your BM tree also presents another problem that already exists in the current one: talent nodes have too many points. You need 20 talent points in MM for Mortal Shots, which you can't easily take when nodes require so many points to finish. And the choices that suffer from this are the utility ones (you can't even take Intimidation!), as you simply pick whatever gives the most damage and ignore the rest. Therefore, a reduction of points of certain nodes (particularly those further down in the tree) would be required.

I also would not increase pet maximum Focus. Instead I'd have it so that the hunter critting would give the pet 10 focus and the pet critting would give the hunter 1% mana. This would have to go with a pet ability overhaul, especially for Bite, to prevent Bite-only pets and pets in general from overcapping. I'd also boost Bestial Discipline's CD reduction to 30% and let it affect all pet abilities, including Bite, precisely for this purpose. Bite could, in fact, deal additional damage at the cost of more Focus if used when having an X amount of Focus. This way it'd remain as a Focus dump without being a Claw clone with no CD.

On another note, pet survival would still be a problem. Avoidance doesn't protect pets from cleaves and the 80% isn't enough. I'd boost it to 90-95% and have it so that any effect applied by an AoE, even DoTs, are reduced by the 90-95% reduction. This would immensely help the pet although there might be required individual tweaks for specific bosses or mechanics.

I also believe that BM should have an ability to provide happiness during combat. For instance, current Baited Shot should give happiness to the pet when attacking the debuffed target. In your tree, I would give this functionality to Devour.

Overall, I commend you for your efforts (this likely took you a long time to think and make and you're doing it for the whole class) but I believe that current BM is better (there's still tons of bugs and feedback to be addressed, of course). However, I see no reason why some of your suggestions cannot be adopted in a future update.

I understand your desire to create a new viable tanking spec (would be especially useful for dungeons) but imho this should go to Survival, not BM. Pets cannot reposition easily to not give their butt to mobs and there's a whole plethora of issues when it comes to controlling them for tanking specifically. If your suggestion is limited to dungeons, then that's fine, although the amount of talents for tanking would have to be reduced as there's too many for just that purpose. However, if you want a proper tanking spec that can do raids or at least stuff like outdoor world bosses, then it can't be the BM pet.

Now an overview on why I did a lot of the changes, which may appear a bit baffling to some.

I'll start with Survival. Right now, you can't reliably start playing melee hunter at early levels. You also don't utilize your ranged toolkit at all. The Hunter is a ranged class, with some melee capabilities. Removing any form of ranged ability from their rotation goes against the class itself. Hence why I moved stings there. For Survival hunters stings will have more effects, deal more damage and cause significantly less threat, so you can still rely on your pet for tanking. That's also where the change to Disengage came. It gives ample opportunity for the hunter to go all out and still not pull aggro (build dependent of course). The much earlier access to in-combat traps also greatly boosts DPS during leveling, allowing for a smooth increase in power, without the reliance on deep checkpoints.

Marksmanship is my less refined tree. Core goal was to remove awkward talents that can screw up your scatter shot and to give Marksmanship an AoE edge. Survival Hunters not only get a much stronger Explosive Trap during combat, but also Carve. Combined, they provide much greater AoE clearing strength compared to a Marksmanship hunter with multi-shot. I also wanted to emphasize more on Aimed Shot, so it can be used as a nuke vs lower HP targets. Right now, mana/damage wise, you're better off just spamming Steady Shots, as you also have a lower risk of being interrupted. Speaking of interrupts, it's also why I added the pushback reduction, to further encourage the use of Aimed Shot. I gave Survival better ways to deal with threat, so I wanted to do the same for Marksmanship, without having them go into the BM tree during leveling. With a simple 2 point talent, that you can later respec, you get a pretty chill way of farming. Right now, as Marksmanship even during leveling, you quite easily pull aggro from your pet, and you have to just auto-attack and do nothing else. Heck, even using Aspect of the Hawk can cause you to do too much damage and draw aggro. And lastly, I really like the idea of a pure ranger class. Survival for the most part ignores ranged abilities, so why should Marksmanship not ignore the pet? At least in specific situations. As mentioned in one of my replies, I regret having it as the last capstone talent, and instead should've made it obtainable a bit earlier. My idea behind it is to rely on the pet for open world questing/farming, but for dungeons and raids, to just go without it.

BM is one of, if not my fav spec. In one of the comments, it was pointed out that dmg wise you're limited because of the new tank talents, but that's not really true. You are limited only if you go deep in the BM tree, in which case you get the full utility of tanky pet. Having both a tanky pet and deal competitive damage is not particularly fair. The pet tanking build can be taken for leveling. You can unload all of your CDs and still not pull aggro from your pet, even multiple targets. And you'll have the opportunity to find dungeons more often, since you can technically tank. Late game you can keep that spec either for soloing content or respect into BM-Marksmanship or BM-Survival.
