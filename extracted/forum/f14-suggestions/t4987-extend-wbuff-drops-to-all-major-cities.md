# Extend Wbuff drops to all major cities

## Post 30147 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-12-03T05:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30147#p30147 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently SW and Org are the "de-facto main cities" of their faction, mainly because world buffs drop there and nowhere else. (also wormholes)

I do personally wish ironforge was the main city for alliance, like it was back in 2006 and on some older private servers, and personally I'm also a big fan of hanging in darn and thunder bluff. But for this to congeal with how world buffs are huge gameplay incentives for levelers and raiders... yeah we need the buffs to cooperate.

Simply extend Ony, Nef, (and Rend) buffs to all 3 cities on their faction, so that levelers in darnassus and undercity can get their buff passing by without feeling like they have to specifically be in the main 2 cities.

For enjoyers of the other cities, this is a huge boost to their ability to chill in their favorite cities, and form their own different smaller communities in these city hangout spots.

Also, the radius of these buffs should be increased inside SW and Org, they currently only hit trade district and the org bank area, while it is blizzlike for them to reach over the entire city.

This could also be done with ZG buff, perhaps over the 3 other goblin cities, or maybe instead of that you could extend it to most of STV like gromgol, the alliance camp in the north, and the arena as well.
