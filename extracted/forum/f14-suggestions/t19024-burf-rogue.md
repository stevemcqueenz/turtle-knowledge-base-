For the love of God, the rogue class is the meaning of a class that uses cloth and should have the talents of a true assassin BECAUSE THAT'S WHAT IT IS. The class currently has 3 lousy talent bases, one of which is "assassin" and its base is to put poison on the opponent, the other is "combat" and it is only useful for PVE and the other, which should be a talent for PVP, has become SUPPORT, this does not exist. The rogue is definitely not meant to be support, he is meant to be free and be able to have mobility and surprise his enemies using critical hits. It is very necessary that the "Sub" talent base returns to being focused on massive damage so that our dear rogue can be happy, because currently in PVP he takes a long time to finish someone and is easily blown up because he only uses cloth on his body.

SUGGESTIONS (you don't need to include all of them, but any that are added in the next patch would help the rogue to become usable again A LOT):

 1- Increase his mobility by at least decreasing the sprint cooldawn (the assassin class in all games has high mobility to move around due to the use of light clothing, the rogue in turtle only has access to this skill with a VERY HIGH cooldawn;

 2- decrease the rogue's agility=critical ratio, increasing the rogue's chances of dealing critical damage in combat, I suggest 20 agility = 1% critical.

 3- improve and remove some of the support functions from the "sub" talent tree because rogues were definitely not made to be supports but rather ASSASSINS, supports are for other different classes.

 4- increase at least the resistance of the rogues, allowing them to use "mail" on the equipment.

 5- The biggest difficulty for rogues currently is that ALL classes deal damage over time to him, making him very easily visible in open combat and, due to his fragility, he dies very easily. Something interesting to improve on him would be the "vanish" skill, which removes the damage per second debufs that were placed on him.

Repeating this, these are suggestions that if just 1 of them were placed on the rogues it would already help a lot, it is not necessary to place them all as it is unfeasible, but some of these 5 are essential in our beloved rogue.  turtle_tongue_head   turtle_tongue_head
