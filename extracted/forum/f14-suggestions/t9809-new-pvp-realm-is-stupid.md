New EU realm is confirmed to be PvP.

Here is what is gonna happen after the honeymoon of fresh is over:
You will have endless complaints and reports about crossfaction group PvP.

"*but it's against the rules so it won't be a problem*"
It's a common fallacy to believe something won't happen because it's illegal.
And it will. It will happen at least a thousand fold more than on the main realm and there are two outcomes from this:
 - You will not have enough GMs capable of enforcing this rule. This is the most likely outcome, just by how poorly GMs are at understanding and enforcing the current rules in battlegrounds.
 - You will overenforce the no crossfaction group PvP rule. It's unlikely to happen but guess what, others and me will spend our time to bait people into breaking this rule and get them banned for fun, and it will be so easy to bait people into this. And if you don't enforce it guess what, people will complain that you don't enforce your rules and it's a trash PvP server because of crossfaction.

So it's a lose lose from the outcomes. You will get endless complaints about crossfaction pvp rule not being enforced or you will get endless complaints about people getting banned because they accidently broke the rule.

"*but we just remove crossfaction grouping from the PvP realm then*"
Great, now you have to balance dungeons and raids for teams that have both shammies and paladins, and for teams that have only one of those.

"*then we just create a fork and a separate balance for both crossfaction and no crossfaction*"
No you won't.
