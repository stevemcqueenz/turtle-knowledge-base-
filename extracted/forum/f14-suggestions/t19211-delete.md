> **Konstantin19 wrote: Sun May 11, 2025 10:51 am**
> Explain how Shadowmeld is a game changer?

 1) AB (and WSG to a lesser extent): it lets you hide the number and position of your defenders. It’s an amazing strategic tool, and can absolutely be a game changer (not just on an individual level but for the whole team) if used properly.

 2) For several classes, it guarantees the opener (plus the element of surprise):
> Warrior (guaranteed charge — which can be a game changer depending on the matchup)
> Hunter (having the initiative is a game changer)
> Pretty much any ranged class (ex: priest)

This applies in WPvP or when defending in BGs (see point 1).

 3) Gives you a safe window to regen without the risk of getting jumped by a rogue or feral (I also play priest).

 4) For hunters, combined with Feign Death, it opens up a ton of small tricks.

If it were a talent, I’d take it without hesitation. From a hunter’s perspective, I’ve always seen it as the most impactful PvP racial in the game.

It’s obviously weaker since 1.17.2, but the blizzlike version was incredible.
