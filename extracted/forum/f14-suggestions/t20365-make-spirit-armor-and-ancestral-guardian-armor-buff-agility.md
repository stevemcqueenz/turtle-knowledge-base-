Hey!

As the title suggests, maybe with a cap to avoid absurd numbers.
Why?
It would help balancing PVP while granting bulkiness to the builds needing it (enh DPS but mostly tank).

As far as I know, the current PVP shaman build is a very bulky caster with some melee tools.
This change wouldn't turn the build into paper, but ele normally doesn't go for agility and would receive much less armor buff after this change.
A build more heavily relying on melee and usual melee stats would better benefit from the talent but if itemisation is skewed towards that, less remains for things like sp.

The goal is a slight nerf for PVP, not affecting PVE melee (specially tanking) experience.
