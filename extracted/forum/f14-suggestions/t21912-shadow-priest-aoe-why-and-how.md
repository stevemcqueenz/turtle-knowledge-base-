# Shadow Priest AoE: Why and How

## Post 151803 by Camillelapin — 2025-09-15T12:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151803#p151803 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There has been a lot of talk in the community about the shadow priest’s lack of aoe spells. Holy nova is a strange “fix” as it does not fit thematically (holy spell in shadow spec) nor gameplay-wise (melee range on caster, it doesnt benefit from +shadow gear).

Holy nova also makes it so that shadow priests spec into holy as a secondary tree, taking many points that are entirely useless, instead of speccing into disc for mana sustain. Why does this happen? Is holy nova that strong (apart from solo farming)? No. The reason why people do that (and the reason why shadow priest should receive a real aoe) is not to be stronger or more useful to the raid. The reason is that having aoe allows priests to do *something* on large and semi-large packs. It is known that Multi dotting isn’t viable vs too many enemies or enemies with low hp pools. The lack of aoe is, in the end, not a balancing issue but a FUN ISSUE.

I thus propose the following simple & easy rework:
add one 1/1 talent in shadow tree:

Improved Mind Flay
Your mind flay links to up to three additional targets. Each subsequent enemy takes 90/80/70/60% damage. Cannot be used on players.

(this should be a separate spell in the spellbook allowinf to use normal MF to satisfy boss mechanics and not break cc etc)

This spell already exists in the game. It is used by Surgical Assistant in Naxxramas. It should be easy to give it to priests and tweak the numbers a bit.

I will post a more comprehensive idea for a larger-scale shadow rework after more testing.
