There are two significant reasons to argue against introducing troll druids.

First and foremost is the lore. Trolls have a rich history tied to their own culture, gods, and practices, none of which align with the druidic tradition rooted in the natural balance and the teachings of Cenarius. Druidism is deeply connected to the Night Elves and Tauren, both of whom have long-standing ties to the Emerald Dream and the natural world. Trolls, on the other hand, have their spiritual practices, such as voodoo and worship of the Loa, which are distinct and separate from the druidic path. Introducing troll druids would undermine this lore and blur the cultural lines that make each race unique.

Secondly, there's the issue of game mechanics and faction balance. Even if troll druids were somehow justified, it would create an imbalance between Horde and Alliance races eligible for the druid class. The Alliance already has fewer races that can become druids, with only Night Elves historically filling that role. What race on the Alliance side would be introduced as druids to maintain balance? Adding troll druids without a counterpart on the Alliance side could tip the scales unfairly and disrupt the class distribution between the factions.

For these reasons, introducing troll druids would not only clash with established lore but also create practical challenges in terms of game balance.
