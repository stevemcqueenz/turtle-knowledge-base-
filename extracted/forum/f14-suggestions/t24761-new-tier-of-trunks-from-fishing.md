# New tier of trunks from fishing

## Post 174254 by Cellion — 2026-04-07T00:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174254#p174254 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

One of the nice little treats while fishing from pools is getting a Watertight Trunk or equivalent. These cool little boxes pop out a small amount of cloth and leather and some consumables, plus a small chance at a green crafting material or gear piece.

The highest level trunk currently is the Mithril Bound Trunk, which grants gear for levels 35-45, and level 30-40 potions, with mostly mageweave and thick leather (and a small chance for runecloth and rugged leather). Unfortunately once you get into levels 45+ these trunks stop being particularly interesting.

I think it would be awesome if there was one more tier of Trunk for the highest level bracket. These could come from the level 50+ zones and contain level 45-55 gear, level 40+ potions, some of the higher tier gems, and more regular runecloth and rugged leather.
