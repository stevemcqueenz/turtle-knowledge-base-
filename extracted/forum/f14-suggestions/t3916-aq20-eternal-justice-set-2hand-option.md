# AQ20 Eternal Justice set: 2Hand option

## Post 23646 by Kwayver (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-27T01:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23646#p23646 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The AQ20 3set bonus is very nice for prot and holy paladins, however the weapon being 1 handed makes it mostly a non-choice for ret.
I'd like to see the quest [[https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8711/ ... al-justice](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=8711/blade-of-eternal-justice)] offer 2 options for rewards: the existing 1 handed weapon [<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=21395>] and also a 2 handed option.

The stats of the weapon could be similar to ilvl equivalents [such as Draconic Maul <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19358>] or to fit the paladin proc playstyle, and fill a niche i feel to be missing (weapon proc effects which inflict holy damage), instead of raw stats it could have the following proc:

Chance on hit: Strike your foe with holy might, inflicting 175 - 276 holy damage to the target

The proc could have the same functions/operations as the proc from Jeklik's crusher <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19918>
