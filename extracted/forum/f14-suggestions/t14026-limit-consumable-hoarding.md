Why would a reasonable person do that though? Gold is not a fiat currency but basically another form of a trade good which you can create out of thin air and consume/destroy at vendors/repairs/quests etc. so you don't really need to protect yourself against inflation as much as you would in the real world. Deflation as a result of developers' action is just as possible as inflation (see the recent raw gold farm nerfs with possibly more to come).

The recent Chinese exodus which happened due to enabling transfers caused a price spike because of the poorly thought-out restriction that was put in place which resulted in all leaving players taking trade goods with them and leaving their gold here. Also some people might want to diversify a little bit and purchase some high value goods but it shouldn't cause any long term detrimential effects.

If you want to combat people hoarding gold, IMO the solution is adding more gold sinks (expensive mounts and vanity items) rather than implementing artificial restrictions.
