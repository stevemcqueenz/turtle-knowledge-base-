# Missing rogue poison proc animation since patch 1.18

## Post 151091 by Unreal9 — 2025-09-10T07:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151091#p151091 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone,

I've noticed that since patch 1.18, the rogue poison proc animation appears to have been removed from the game. My best guess is that this happened when Noxious Assault was added, since both effects use a similar green glow animation on the hands.

The poison proc animation is one of those vanilla WoW details that really adds to the rogue experience. Without it, playing a rogue feels significantly less aesthetically pleasing and loses that distinctive visual flair we've come to love.

Could the development team please look into restoring the poison proc animation for non-Assassination rogues? Perhaps the Noxious Assault animation could be differentiated enough to avoid conflicts.

Thanks for considering this - it would mean a lot to those of us who appreciate these small but important visual elements that make our characters feel unique!
