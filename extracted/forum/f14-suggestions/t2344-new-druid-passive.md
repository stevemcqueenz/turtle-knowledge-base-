# New druid passive

## Post 14695 by Mikecrow — 2021-12-21T22:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14695#p14695 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey Turtles!

I was talking to some druids earlier about how painful the leveling process is because you dont get your cat form until 20 and until then, short of autoattacking with a staff that still deals nominal damage, you have to level using wrath or autos + heals, and due to the nature of classic, this isnt a very effective strategy when you inevitably quest in an area where you pull 2 or more mobs or are in an enclosed space with frequent respawns. As a result, and I have seen other servers do this, I suggest either a talent in the first row of balance or resto, or a straight up passive at level 1 or 4 or whatever that gives the druid a boost to spirit or mana regen for like 12 seconds whenever you kill a monster or player.

What does this do? Effectively two things:
 1) once your great mana regen at level 5 wears off and mobs start hitting you harder than any staff you can find, allows you to deal the neccessary damage with wrath and not be oom immediately after 4 casts for the next pull or heal yourself enough to survive 2 mobs hitting you to death.

 2) This allows balance to be an effect dungeon and solo leveling talent tree without relying on an ability you get at level 40 because they arent oom every pull or allows for resto healing to be effective even without having a truckload of spirit earlier in the game.

Do you play druid? do you agree or disagree? Why? Post response below! I look forward to reading them!  hiding_smth_turtle_head

Good for them. I'm sure some players might find this easy solution enjoyable and very convenient for their class. Turtle WoW still sets a goal to continue the story of the original Warcraft lore, perhaps even try and extend it a bit, but still based on OG story and class mechanics.

Priests are attuned to both aspects of Order and Divine, which is Light and Darkness. Druids however do not preach such values. For them, life and nature are inseparable and therefore they will never dabble into one-quarter of it for a small benefit. That's why druids, in general, would rather increase their own potential (spirit for mana regen) and come up with a sly way to (mana management) benefit from such prowess.

In other words, I think druids do not need a situational and unreliable talent stolen from another class. Besides, there are two words that describe such a method we all as a team strive to avoid like a plague: homogenization and imbalance.   satisfied_turtle
