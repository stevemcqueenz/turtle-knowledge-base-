# Simple Suggestion to stop pvp burnout

## Post 18123 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-04-14T21:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18123#p18123 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ever felt like, if you don't pvp on, you will lose all your progress.

No pause possible!

Feeling pressured yet?

Here is a simple fix that won't make reaching rank 14 any bit faster.
But It will put people at ease at the same time.
No more pvp burnouts

How?
Make it so you can't lose ranks.
Just that simple.
You still lose ranking points and can fall all the way back to 0 points towards the next rank, but you can't fall lower than your all time highest rank.
if you want to pvp on, you can pick it right up.
IF you want to pvp only to rank 7 and feel pressured that  you should continue atleast until 10 or all your works goes to waste or needs to be repeated later.. With this you dont have to worry about it no more.

Hardcore pvpees never lose ranks anyway until rank 14. This suggestions changes absolutely 0 for them. It won't be any faster. But It will allow them to pause their progress at any time and only lose their current progress towards the next rank, a higher ranks maybe a few weeks, at mid ranks maybe two, but no more than that ans they can always pick up from there again.

To reach rank 14 you still need to be extremely high placed for multiple weeks in order to progress from 13 to 14, since the rp loss is still there unchanged.

I can't believe this would be too hard to implement.
But it would make a HUGE difference towards preventing pvp burnouts without actually dumbing down the system.

Please make it happen, this or something like this to fix a system that encourages, even requires  playing way more than what is healthy for any human being,ans this without a pause allowed.

Even if TWoW isn't a world pvp server, the pvp system and battlegrounds are still a huge thing for players at 60. This would be a significant improvement over vanilla without completely changing it (like tbc)
