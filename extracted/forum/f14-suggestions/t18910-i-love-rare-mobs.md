# I Love Rare Mobs!

## Post 128642 by Darcy33 — 2025-04-26T22:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128642#p128642 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The dungeon extension concept is great and I'm glad it is continuing the next patch, going to stop my exp around 30ish for my first char on this server to experience the turtle dungeons. Something I liked in vanilla was those rares that had a "chance to spawn" in the dungeon, call me greedy but I wish it happened A LOT (ALLOOOOOT) more often. It'd be interesting to add on top of the dungeon extensions. You can spawn the dungeon rares in all kinds of crazy places to add variety/new strategies to dungeons, heck they could even spawn next to a boss for extra challenge/loot and maybe rares can interact with already existing bosses. Some of the rare mobs can be spawned by randomized fun triggers in the dungeons too. You can get crazier with the loot adding new rare mobs into these dungeons as well.

Another thing I liked about rares came from my first time EVER playing a horde, in the barrens there were rare mobs that dropped "items that began quests".... that's also something I wish was more prevalent in the game. Although they added some extra dialogue with quest turn in npcs, most of the quest was just simple turn ins (expect for the rare alliance pack roaming the barrens). Be interesting if it happened more and the rare quest had more depth.
