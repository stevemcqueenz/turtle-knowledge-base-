Hello, my in game main characters name is Gimmeaxe.

I spent 2 weeks participating in an event to acquire the mount, Tel'Abim Gorilla. Having spent a few days of in game time, and receiving my reward, i was told that because my character was on a separate account, the reward could not be transferred to the one character i wanted to play with it. This has caused me a great amount of frustration, as i spent 2 weeks basically wasting my time.
I have enjoyed turtle WoW for years. In recent times, it has become harder to gain assistance for small mistakes, regardless of how impactful they are. Both of my accounts are used from the same IP, and there's no chance that answering a ticket and refusing to transfer the mount take less time than typing remove item and add item commands to 2 characters standing next to each other. This whole situation has made me dislike how little power GM's have to help players.
I completely understand not transferring dungeon/raid loot. Nowadays, you can swap most important quest rewards if you made a mistake at a new npc, and most raid loot is tradable for some time after it is looted. For this event, for the holidays, I thought a little leniency would show and id be able to get some help. Not everybody plays WoW the same way, and Multi-Boxing is allowed to a certain extent. This shows that having more than one account is even encouraged on Turtle WoW.
Having all your accounts on the same IP should allow them to see that you aren't BSing and trying to pull a fast one on the GM's. They can see what IP made the account, what IP made each login. I understand preventing people from taking advantage of the event, but this also hurts real players who make real mistakes and making people feel like they wasted their time and lose faith in the people who run the game we all enjoy.

Cheers, hope one day to be riding a gorilla. if not, GG.

 --Gimmeaxe (Nordanaar)
