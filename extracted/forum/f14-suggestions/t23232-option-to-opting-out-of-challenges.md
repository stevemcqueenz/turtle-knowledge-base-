Dear Devs and Turtle Community.
I really hope you see this and I would love to hear your thoughts on it.

I'll start of by saying that, I'm a little dissapointed that when you make a Level One Lunatic it says and I quote ''The Titles will stay with you, even if you eventually decide to level up.''

Which I expect gives most players the assumption that you can in fact remove the challenge and level to 60. I think in general if a game tells you can do something and you spent hundreds of gold and plenty of hours to farm, because you are under that very obvious assumption, that you can in fact remove the challenge.

Can we atleast remove that part of the text that says if you do eventually decide to level up?
It would be amazing if they brought the mysterious stranger back or even if they added a donation option to remove any challenge on a character. I would gladly pay to have it removed, because I invested a lot of money and time into getting titles on my level 1 lunatic and now it seems like a waste of time and money and I feel a bit frustrated.
PLEASE add the donor option or some way to turn it off, it's like the only thing about turtle that is genuinely frustrating to be honest.
