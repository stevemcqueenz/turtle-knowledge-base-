# Even Slower and Steadier!

## Post 145707 by Lordmarrowgar — 2025-08-15T14:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145707#p145707 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey Turtle WoW community and devs!

First off, I just want to say how much I love what you've done with the game, the custom changes, the new realm coming up, and especially features like Slow and Steady that let us go through the world slowly instead of rushing through it.

I created a character on Nordanaar with Slow and Steady enabled, not for the rewards, but because I genuinely enjoy exploring every nook and cranny while content still feels challenging and meaningful.

Who wants to stomp through Deadmines at level 60 or breeze through Westfall quests when you're already overleveled? Not me! I really like that "pain" and reward of progression, where every mob, quest, and dungeon matters.

After trying Slow and Steady (which halves XP gain for a slower pace), I found it was still too quick for my taste, especially when mixing in dungeons (my favorite way to meet the awesome people of Turtle WoW and form groups).
Without dungeons, I outleveled zones like Westfall or Loch Modan quickly. With dungeons, I skipped even more, missing the full story. It's a real struggle balancing immersion with the social fun of grouping up.

So, here's my suggestion, introduce an even slower version called:

> **Even Slower and Steadier!**

This could be a new challenge players choose at character creation building on Slow and Steady but cranked up for those of us who want to truly explore the entire world and all of the custom content.

 **What Could "Even Slower and Steadier" Look Like?**
- Core Mechanic: Reduce XP gain by 75% (open to ideas, random number). This would make leveling a marathon, giving plenty of time to full-clear zones, do every side quest, and explore without outpacing the content.
- Cool Rewards: Honor the challenge with immersive perks, like a new title, unique transmog skins, +5% quest gold or whatever really as long as it's not too overpowered.
 **Why Add This?**
- Deeper Immersion: For players like me who want to "feel the pain"grinding through Westfall at appropriate level, struggling in Deadmines without overgearing, and having a need to group for elites.
- Social Boost: Slower pacing means more time in low-level zones and in dungeons, where you meet the awesome Turtle Community.
- Fits Turtle's Spirit: You guys have nailed making Vanilla feel fresh and player-driven. This would appeal to the hardcore/RP crowd.
- Community Demand: From forums and Reddit, plenty of folks echo this; Slow and Steady is great, but some want it even slower for true zone mastery.
What do you all think? Would you try Even Slower and Steadier, or what would it take for you to take this challenge?

Thanks for reading, and keep up the amazing work, Turtle team! ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)

 -Lordmarrowgar-

 **Love your “Even Slower & Steadier” — I’ve got a sibling idea focused 100% on exploration**

I’m totally on board with your proposal. I’ve been thinking about a very similar mode aimed at explorers and non-rushers who want to experience the *whole* game at a level-appropriate pace and with an immersive feel.

 **Explorer Mode (quest & map XP only)**
Here’s the core of it, chosen at level 1:

- **No monster XP:** all experience comes from quests and map discovery.

- **Boosted quest & map XP:** about 2–3× (tunable to hit the right pacing).

- **Pacing goal:** reach 60 only after ~95% of quests and full world exploration.

- **Rewards at 60:** a fitting title and cosmetics (no power creep).
 **Why this pairs nicely with your idea**

- **Keeps zones level-appropriate:** you don’t overlevel stories just by grinding mobs.

- **Dungeons stay social, not a rush:** the “adventure” XP is outside, so instances don’t catapult you past zones.

- **Simple & opt-in:** easy to understand and finely tunable by adjusting the quest/map multipliers.
If you’re curious, I wrote it up with more details here — would love your thoughts and help refining it:
[My Explorer Mode post](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142156#p142156)

Thanks for reading, and great pitch! ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)
