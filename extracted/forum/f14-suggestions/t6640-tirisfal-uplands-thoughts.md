# Tirisfal Uplands thoughts

## Post 41829 by Amarifae — 2023-04-17T09:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41829#p41829 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really enjoyed the 2 levels I spent in Tirisfal Uplands tonight! It was great to explore something I had not seen before back in the original game. I must say, it gave some of the same initial feelings that WoW did as well! It's very well done, designed in a way that makes sense, and gives the undead a much needed augment during the late teens without forcing them into the Barrens.

There were three things I noticed in my time there:

 1. Tirisfal Prowlers are bears, yet they drop coin and are unskinnable, this was...confusing at first.

 2. The Human Farmhand and Militia NPCs have no loot table at all, no coin, no cloth, no anything.

 3. Necromancer Reginald is FAR overtuned, even after the quest is long green. This however, may not be a bug, but a feature. Needless to say, there was much scrambling as I tried to run my hardcore butt the opposite direction once the curse hit!

I really hope more stuff like this is/has been added in the future for Turtle WoW. I'm a new player here on a hardcore shaman slowly making my way in the world, but this was a treat and 20-22 flew by with some new content that really fit in well. Keep up the good work, and remember how well this area was laid out! Stuff like this matters and you nailed it!
