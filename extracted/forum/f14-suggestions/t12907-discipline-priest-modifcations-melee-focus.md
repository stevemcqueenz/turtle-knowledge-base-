# Discipline Priest modifcations - Melee focus

## Post 86158 by Cookieboy — 2024-01-28T14:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86158#p86158 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is one thing I like about the Turtle WoW servers - It's the ability to incorporate melee attacks with wand casts and instant casting spells to increase DPS

But I wonder if this can be expanded upon to make a mana efficient class that, while is not as devastating as it's shadow counterpart, is able to deal a considerable amount of damage without relying on Smite hardcasts

By this, I mean modifying existing talents as well as adding new ones to make melee attacks not only more viable, but a possible feature to be built around - Essentially what I am proposing is a monk, but can cast magic

Examples of modifications include:

 **Wand specialization:** (Renamed to "Martial Specialization") - *Also increases melee attack speed by 2% per rank*

 **Inner focus:** *Also increases next melee damage by 20% and critical hit chance by 100%*

 **Force of Will:** *Also increases critical hit chance of melee attacks by 1% per rank*

There may also be new talents:

 **Furied Benediction** (Level 30, 4 talent points) - *Upon melee hit, has a 2.5% chance of causing the next Smite cast to be instant and have no mana cost (8 second cooldown, One-handed melee weapons only)*

 **Inexertion** (Level 30, 4 talent points) - *Each successful attack with a wand increases 2-handed melee speed by 5%, and each successful attack with a staff reduces cooldown with wand attacks by 5%*

There may obviously be some bleed between specializations, but I'd leave that to more creative minds than myself.

Thoughts?
