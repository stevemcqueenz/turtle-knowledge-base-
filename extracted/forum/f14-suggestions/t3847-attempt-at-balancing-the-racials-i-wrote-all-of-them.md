 - Change melee and ranged attack power increase to 10%

Tauren - Spirit Shot: - Take away the part about less spirit causing it to be crit

Undead - Putrid Shot: - Change cooldown to 1 minute
 - Change chance of "Neuro Necrosis" to interrupt spell upon casting to 5%

Goblin - Trap Carrier GO75: - Change to trigger the cooldown of the choosen trap

Night Elf - Ambush Hunter: - Give "Shadowmeld" a 1 minute cooldown

 - Change armor increase to 100%
 - Change threat generation increase to 300% (Yes, I want tank hunters. What about it?)

Gnome - Knee Shot: - Make it have a noticable mana cost

High Elf - Charming Shot: - Make it have a large mana cost

Warlock:

Orc - Dominant Master: - Useing "Sacrifice" only gives you the effect of the non imp minion if there is one. Your imp dissapirs as well.

Undead - Grafting: - Perfection

Goblin - Overwhelming Power: - The explosion of the goblin has a 20 yard radius

Human - Unleash Spirit: - The damage the Unleashed Spirit causes does not heal you.

Gnome - Shrink: - Only affects strenght and stamina
 - Is a magical effect
 - Only one target can be affected by it at any one time

Warrior:

Orc - Lok'tar Ogar!: - Change attack power increas to 20%
 - Makes the warrior unable to use shouts for 30 second

Undead - Will to Kill: - No attack power increase
 - Change duration to 15 seconds
 - Unlimited rage during duration

Tauren - Taurus Charge: - Makes more damage the farther your target is
 - Has a 5 minute cooldown
 - Change knockback to 20 yards

Troll - War Ritual: - Not usable on critters
 - Change humanoid buff to 5%
 - Change giant buff to 15%
 - Beast buff caps at 10%
 - Aberration/undead buff gives 20% bonus shadow damage, and the penalties are 20% each
 - Demon buff gives 25 to your rage pool and rage decays 25% slower

Goblin - Throw Dirt: - Perfection

Human - Adaptive Stance: - Does not give other benefits besides counting as all other stances
 - Lowers your attack speed by 15%
 - Increases chance to be critically hit by 3%
 - Decreases damage done by 3%
(- Makes others call you mean names)
(- Seduces your mother)
(- Does not kiss you good night)
(- Drinks away your college tuition)

Dwarf - Metal Form: - Decreases movement speed by 15%
 - Makes you bigger

Night Elf - Moondance: - Does not give extra dodge chance
 - There is a 75% chance that it performes a weapon attack upon dodge

Gnome - Saphenous Cut: - Perfection

High Elf - Nullify Magic: I think it's ok as long as we discard "Consume Mana"

Druid:

Tauren - Boon of the Earthmother: - I think it's ok
