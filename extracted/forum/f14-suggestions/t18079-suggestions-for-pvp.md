# Suggestions for PVP

## Post 123078 by Qz5733 — 2025-03-20T05:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123078#p123078 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Here are the PVP modifications that I believe need to be made
 1. In terms of the battlefield, the Blood Ring should return to its first appearance, and the losing side should also have reputation. 2v2 is needed for faster matching. I don't think the Lordaeron Arena mentioned in 2023 will be open in 2026, and I don't expect it to be.
Next is the Sunshine Forest Battlefield. Cancelling the boss to end the game and changing it to occupying points to win can solve the problem of no one playing. PVP is about killing, don't use so many useless mechanisms. Why isn't anyone playing? Because they don't understand how to play
 2. In terms of attribute data, we all know that PVP is about killing, because the talent of 1.172 has strengthened many professions, which makes the damage data too large. We need to balance all aspects, so I believe that R10, R12, R13, R14 equipment should receive a 5% reduction in damage per piece to solve the problem. Avoiding the battlefield being occupied by T3 and T3.5 can also alleviate the huge damage data. Many people I have seen only have battlefield equipment and nothing else. They really need these attributes and don't need to create even greater damage because you won't let them exceed T3. Reducing damage is always possible
 3. Due to the talent changes of 172, the PVP of this game has become very different from 2023. Nowadays, there are more DPS fast DPS games, and I think only a reduction of more than 30% in damage can balance this. As for increasing attributes, I think it is minimal. Flame Warlocks, Survival Hunters, Arcane Mages, Balance Druids, Punish Paladins, all can wreak havoc on the battlefield, and only Warriors and Thieves are beaten
 4. Teaming up to enter the battlefield is wrong because there are people from multiple countries on the same team, and we cannot achieve smooth communication. One side is ALL R14 still using Discord, and the other side is a novice player who just reached level 60 and doesn't understand anything. This is one-sided slaughter, which often occurs. This is destroying PVP, and teaming up to enter the battlefield must be prohibited! We went in to play, not to be slaughtered unilaterally
I started playing here in early 2023 and spent most of my time in PVP. I understand that this is an RP PVE server, and I think many people, like me, only stay here because of the environment and harmony. However, PVP is also a part of the game, and now it's very different from before. It's really bad and needs to be changed. If you could finish reading it, I would be very grateful if someone could see it. The translation software I used was able to publish it, and I hope for your understanding
