# New Dungeon: Titanic Park

## Post 140092 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-17T15:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140092#p140092 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**![🦖](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f996.svg) Titanic Park (Dungeon: Un'Goro Crater)**
 *Level Range: 60 | 5 Players | Faction: Explorers’ League*

Deep within Un’Goro Crater, the dwarven entrepreneur **Duwald Goldforge** has built a for-profit zoological research center inside a rediscovered subterranean ecodome: ****Titanic Park****. Originally a Titan facility meant to monitor prehistoric life, it has since been overrun by genetically augmented dinosaurs, malfunctioning animatronics, and unstable elemental experiments gone haywire.

Players will traverse various biomes of the dome, uncovering corporate corruption and scientific catastrophe along the way. Expect quirky quests, savage beasts, and deadly surprises around every turn!

 ---

![📍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4cd.svg) Dungeon Zones

 **1. Visitor’s Center**
The initial zone houses quest givers, broken exhibits, and frantic tourists hiding behind display cases. Trash mobs consist of *Mini-Raptor Swarms* and malfunctioning tour drones. Some dungeon tie-in quest items (like the “Family Discount Bracelet” and “Misplaced Dino Diet Chart”) are found here.

 **2. The Theater**
An animatronic show starring a mechanical version of Duwald Goldforge goes haywire. He accuses the party of disrupting the park experience and summons robotic guards from backstage.

 **Boss: Robot Duwald Goldforge**
An unstable dwarven showbot wielding outdated one-liners and oversized stage props.
 *Mechanics:*
• Summons mechanical adds from trapdoors
• Targets random player with "Spotlight Overheat"
• Drops hazard zones of sparking electricity

 **Loot:**
• ![⚙️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2699.svg) [Goldforge's Retinal Laser] – Wand, shoots a visual beam, moderate fire damage
• ![🪙](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1fa99.svg) [Showstopper's Pauldrons] – Plate shoulders, +Fire Resistance, +Defense
• ![🔩](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f529.svg) [Spark-Jolted Servo Boots] – Mail boots, increases movement speed slightly

 ---

 **3. The Laboratory**
Flickering lights, leaking gene tanks, and overworked leper gnome and goblin scientists make up this decaying research wing. Mutated dino-hybrids prowl the holding pens.

 **Boss: Lead Scientist Wenly Hoo**
An unhinged leper gnome who believes she is perfecting evolution.
 *Mechanics:*
• Casts “Splice Surge” to morph nearby beasts into temporary elites
• Drops volatile puddles of mutagen gas
• Uses “Experiment Recall” to bring dormant adds into the fight

 **Loot:**
• ![🧪](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9ea.svg) [Mutagenic Injector] – Trinket, chance to apply random stat buff or debuff on use
• ![🧠](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9e0.svg) [Wenly’s Overloaded Braincase] – Cloth head, increases spell haste, but reduces mana regen
• ![🔬](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f52c.svg) [Gene-Welded Spaulders] – Leather shoulders, bonus to Nature damage and resistance

 ---

 **4. Feeding Grounds**
Open plains where enormous thunder lizards roam freely. A side quest here involves examining piles of thunder lizard dung to cure a mysterious disease. A rare *Wind Serpent boss* named “Tempestria” may appear.

 ---

 **5. Dining Hall and Kitchens**
Carnage unfolds as packs of rogue raptors stalk shattered dining areas and kitchen corridors.
Players must navigate raptor patrols and unlock the tunnel system using malfunctioning defense panels.

 **Boss: Cleverlurk, the Pack Alpha**
The cunning matriarch of the rogue raptors.
 *Mechanics:*
• Sends out stealthed rogue-like raptors to backstab the rear line
• Screeches to disorient players and summon reinforcements
• Randomly switches targets for surprise pounces

 **Loot:**
• ![🦴](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9b4.svg) [Roguepack Fangblade] – Dagger, fast, chance to apply stacking bleed
• ![🦖](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f996.svg) [Clever Girl’s Talon Band] – Ring, increases stealth and crit
• ![🦎](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f98e.svg) [Raptor Handler’s Jerkin] – Leather chest, +Agility, +Dodge

 ---

 **6. Tunnel Maze & Defense Override**
A raptor-infested maze lies beneath the dining hall. Players must either fight their way through tight corridors or speed-run through to avoid endless waves. Reaching the override console requires puzzle-solving to restore the facility’s defenses.

 ---

 **7. Gift Shop – Final Boss Arena**
Having restored partial order to the park, players are confronted in the merchandise showroom by the final horror.

 **Boss: Project Titanspawn**
A colossal biomodified T-Rex cyborg with exposed reactors and corrupted Titan tech.
 *Mechanics:*
• Spews toxic sludge that leaves persistent void zones
• Charges with laser-guided stampedes
• Must be moved away from collapsing support beams

 **Loot:**
• ![🦾](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9be.svg) [Dino-Bone Reapers] – 2H Axe, strength, causes bleed over time
• ![🦚](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f99a.svg) [Bio-Luminescent Scales] – Cloak, +Nature Resistance, reduces Nature damage taken
• ![🥚](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f95a.svg) [Gene-Spliced Raptor Egg] – Trinket, summons raptor companion for 1 min

 ---

![💰](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4b0.svg) Vending System: Exit Through the Gift Shop
After defeating Project Titanspawn, players can interact with the **Titanic Park Cash Register** to spend **Titan Tokens**, which drop from bosses and occasionally from elite trash. Rewards include:

• Class-specific blue gear sets. This could be a dungeon for fresh-60s, maybe even let some T0 be bought this way.
• Cosmetic souvenirs (e.g. Jurassic-themed tabard, vanity pets)
• Consumables like *Dino Jerky* and *Sludge Sodas*

 ---

![🧭](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9ed.svg) Quests

 **“Don’t Feed the Thunder Lizards”** – Recover medicine from thunder lizard poop to save park scientists.
 **“Defective by Design”** – Shut down malfunctioning security droids and retrieve override schematics.
 **“A Token Gesture”** – Collect enough Titan Tokens to buy a souvenir for a scared tourist outside the dungeon.

 ---

 *Completing Titanic Park rewards reputation with the **Explorers' League** and uncovers correspondence linking Duwald Goldforge to the Venture Co. and Dark Iron mercenaries seeking to exploit genetic experiments for war.*

**Titanic Park - Biocaves**
 *Dungeon Level Range: 60 | Location: Un'Goro Crater | Type: 5-Player*

This is the second dungeon in this series, which operates like SM in that there is a central Visitor Center hub that exists outside of the instances, but you can enter other parts of the park after getting the key card from the first section.

 ---

 **![🌀](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f300.svg) Entrance: The Visitor's Center**
Adventurers enter through a crumbling exhibit hall where malfunctioning exhibits run wild. Goblin huckster Dodgers tasks you with retrieving mutated dinosaur eggs, last seen in the hands of his ex-partner, gnome geneticist Numen Garglesprocket.

 * Trash Mobs: Miniature raptor swarms, broken animatronic greeters, and security drones.

 ---

 **![🦎](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f98e.svg) Raptor Pens – “Venomspit Exhibit”**
The group navigates the collapsed raptor habitat, now overrun by Venomspit Raptors — snake/raptor hybrids with frill-hoods and poisonous spittle. Broken glass and warning signs suggest the creatures escaped containment.

 **Boss: Numen Garglesprocket**
Gnome Engineer
 *Mechanics:*
 * Activates waves of broken animatronics and security turrets.
 * Unleashes Venomspit Raptors from holding pens.
 * Uses personal gadgets like "Turbo Blink Harness" and “Overload Field.”

 *Loot:*
 * **Technomutant’s Tinkerbelt** (Cloth Waist): +Int, haste, chance to reflect spells.
 * **Numen's Shaving Cream Canister** (Trinket): Temporarily reduces threat generation. (5 min cooldown)
 * **Tourist’s Shredded Raincoat** (Cloth Chest): Reduces poison duration

 ---

 **![🪨](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1faa8.svg) Beacon Peak – The Climb**
To restore security protocols, players must scale a steep, craggy mountain surrounded by floating sky domes. Pterrordax of various types swoop from above, with nests and eggs dotting the cliffs.

 * Trash Mobs: baby pterrodax, adult pterrordax, mini-raptors.

 **Boss: Matriarch Talonfiend**
Giant Pterrordax
 *Mechanics:*
 * Wing buffet knockbacks on narrow ledges.
 * Wind vortex pull-ins.
 * When it dies, it falls off the ledge into a cavern below. Players must take a "leap of faith" into a cave that drops them into a deep pool of water.

 *Loot:*
 * **Talonmaw Wingstriders** (Leather Boots): Agi, fall damage reduction.
 * **Updraft Band** (Ring): Use: short featherfall, long CD
 * **Razor Beak Polearm** (2H Polearm): Bleed proc, bonus vs beasts.

 ---

 **![🌌](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f30c.svg) Subterranean Bio-Caves**
Players are inside of the Biocave exhibit, with the animals. Luminescent plants light these caverns, and light from the visitor area shines in, showing raptors eating tourists' remains. Mutated dinosaurs and radioactive runoff blend nature and science in grotesque harmony.

 * Trash Mobs: diemetridons, basilisks, mutated crocolisks.

 **Optional Boss: Radigore the Corrupted**
Mutated lizard
 *Mechanics:*
 * Toxic breath cone.
 * Pulsing AoE radiation fields.
 * Petrify beam after long cast.

 *Loot:*
 * **Sludgecrusted Bracers** (Mail): Nature resist, chance to ooze AoE.
 * **Radigore’s Glare** (Off-hand): +Spell power, chance to blind attackers.

 ---

 **![🌿](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f33f.svg) Bio-Garden Floodplain**
Sludge-tainted water seeps through dense plant life. Bog beasts, lashers, and massive threshers roam. Raptors chase anything that moves. Optional puzzle to unlock security systems.

 **Optional Boss: Karibdis**
Giant Mutated Thresher
 *Mechanics:*
 * Fight happens in underwater cave, waterbreathing needed.
 * Whirlpool effect pulls players to boss.
 * Tidal thrash AoE.

 *Loot:*
 * **Slime-Lined Spaulders** (Plate): Strength, reflect minor nature spells.
 * **Gnashing Jawblades** (Fist Weapons): High crit, bleed chance.

 ---

 **![💧](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4a7.svg) Final Encounter – Skylla-saurus 3000**
At the heart of the park lies Skylla, a monstrous hydra grown in radioactive sludge and augmented with goblin-corrupted Titan tech. Each head represents a different element and attacks with its own rhythm.

 *Mechanics:*
 * Alternates fire, frost, and poison heads.
 * DOT breath zones.
 * Regenerates heads after a while unless they all are dead at the same time.
 * Fears into the water, full of threshers that are yellow when not IC with her, and red when IC.

 *Loot:*
 * **Hydrahide Carapace** (Chest): High armor, all resistances, minor HoT effect.
 * **Venomcore Wand** (Wand): Poison damage on hit.
 * **Skylla’s Heart** (Trinket): On-use: drop an AOE DOT effect where you are that lasts a short amount of time. Randomly choose fire, frost, poison.

 ---

 **![🛍️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6cd.svg) Bonus: Exit Through the Gift Shop**
After defeating Skylla, players emerge into the sealed gift shop. A unique vendor offers cosmetic gear and token-based rewards for collecting park tokens.
