# Alterntive to a HC server

## Post 67761 by Ishilu (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-09-30T13:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67761#p67761 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A lot of HC-vs normals drama comes form competition over quest mobs and the problem normally arises for named mobs (Zalazane, Tazan, Hogger etc.).

My idea:

Let HC players team up with normals under the following conditions:

 1) reasonable level range, +/-5 should be fine, so boosts are less likely to happen.
 2) short duration only. One minute should be fine, since it's enough to kill any named quest mob.
 3) long cooldown, I'm thinking of 30 minutes, to prevent hc's from "cheating" too much. Just give the HC a debuff "can't team up with normals" for that duration and they won't burn their big cooldown for killing some random trash mobs.
