# Scaled Raiding - A Casual Guild GM's Perspective

## Post 70005 by Thejakemf — 2023-10-10T16:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70005#p70005 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello! For transparency's sake, my main is Benhardt and I'm the GM of the <Stormwind Opportunity Lg> a tight-knit and community focused NA guild whose goal is to progress through raiding content together as one group.

We have been raiding ZG/AQ20/Molten Core for a few months now with our roster sitting just over 20 people. About two weeks ago, once we had a pretty solid group of 20-25 people showing up to every raid, we started progressing BWL WITHOUT recruiting PUGs as our goal is to progress *as a guild*. We have been able to run these raids successfully while our roster fluctuates by 5-10 people per raid.

Scaled raiding has been one of the biggest boons to allow our casual and community focused group to raid consistently, despite the difficulties in recruiting members at the MC/BWL tier of raiding when there are half a dozen guilds farming AQ40 and Naxx. This allows us to keep growing and recruiting our core raiders, but also to allow social members who cannot attend every single raid for one reason or another; these are valuable members of our community who we want to keep raiding with, scaled raiding allows us to keep bringing them to raids without being penalized when they're not there. If you have ever had to sit out a raid, you know that being benched because you are not able to attend every raid feels horrible and is one of the worst things about raiding.

A populated server does NOT mean that every raiding guild is going to be full of people, especially on a server of this age; many people don't want to go through all of progression again, but for those who do, scaled raiding allows us to organize and run raids without the burden of finding a full 40 people every single time. I have no doubt in my mind that this will absolutely destroy many smaller guilds like ours, how many others are going to have their planned raids completely halted because their roster is not as large as the new scaling demands?

My final question is, who in your community does this change benefit? When asking why ES wasn't scaling, I was told this:

"We've been dialing back on scaling amounts for most content since population is mostly no longer an issue. We want to encourage the social aspect of it by accepting more raid members.

More importantly though, we want to show players that especially for new custom raid content this is the way it's going to be going forward. This has an extra reason which is that while simple HP and damage numbers are scalable, some mechanics that are intended for x people aren't quite that scalable in that same way. This means that by doing this we can test and tweak our new upcoming raids exactly the way it will be played on live servers too."

How does removing scaled raiding "encourage the social aspect by accepting more raid members?" Raids still fit 40 people before, I wasn't running MC/BWL with 25 people because I made 15 people sit on the bench so the other 25 could have more loot, I did so because that is the size of our roster and we want to raid; I have never stopped wanting to grow our roster or recruit. If anything, it feels like this change will have the opposite effect as smaller more social and communal guilds lose members who don't want to wait for us to start recruiting again as if we had never raided. This is not going to improve the social aspect of the game for anyone outside of a few large raiding guilds with all content on farm.
