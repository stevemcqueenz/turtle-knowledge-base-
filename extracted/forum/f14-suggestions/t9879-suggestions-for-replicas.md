I use /who when I want to form a group.

For example, /who c-druid c-warrior z-barrens 20-22 will give you a list of all warriors and druids ranging from lvl 20 to 22 who are in the barrens. I use that to select candidates for my group (reasonable class composition, little to no loot drama, level not too high or too low) and then /w people.
