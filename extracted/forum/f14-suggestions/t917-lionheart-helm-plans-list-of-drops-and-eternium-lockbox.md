## Post 5098 by Withinamnesia (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-02-22T03:38:00Z

Lionheart Helm Plans have many sources to be obtained from in vanilla. This includes rare chances for world drops and to be contained in Eternium Lockboxes. I have evidence for my case:

Lionheart Helm Plans have a chance to drop off many mobs.
[https://vanilla-twinhead.twinstar.cz/?i ... dropped-by](https://vanilla-twinhead.twinstar.cz/?item=12717#dropped-by)
[http://web.archive.org/web/201111012215 ... ed-in-item](http://web.archive.org/web/20111101221537/http://www.wowhead.com/item=12717#contained-in-item)

Lionheart Helm Plans also have a 0.1-2% chance to be contained within an Eternium Lockbox
[http://web.archive.org/web/201111012355 ... 0+2-15+3+1](http://web.archive.org/web/20111101235508/http://www.wowhead.com/item=5760/eternium-lockbox#contains:0+2-15+3+1)
[http://web.archive.org/web/201111012215 ... ed-in-item](http://web.archive.org/web/20111101221537/http://www.wowhead.com/item=12717#contained-in-item)
[https://vanilla-twinhead.twinstar.cz/?i ... ed-in-item](https://vanilla-twinhead.twinstar.cz/?item=12717#contained-in-item)

The Lionheart Helm Plans are able to drop from select non-raid mobs at low chance and also a very lucky chance in an Eternium Lockbox.

Turtle WoW has many loot bugs such as simple ones like rare mobs not dropping greens when they should for example. We do not have the shackles of a museum server to lock out content although we should strive to be just as good as great vanilla server and then surpass it.

People should not be burning out grinding UBRS just for an end game helm plan when in vanilla the Lionheart Helm Plans in vanilla can drop from select mobs and a very lucky chance with Eternium Lockboxes. Even from a design stand point Turtle WoW should help nullify the raid entry awkwardness to properly gear up in vanilla let alone have needless exclusivity. We have an opportunity to fix a bug here but I am also watching to see how the developers react to this. What is the point of suggestions and bug fixes if nothing is considered?
