# New 20 Man, New Enchants

## Post 160461 by Avereth — 2025-11-23T19:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160461#p160461 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

With the Timbermaw raid in the development pipeline, now is the perfect time to address one of the longest standing imbalances to ever exist in this version of World of Warcraft.

Currently there exist limited options for head and leg enchants, and many of the best ones are class restricted in the form of Zul'Gurub enchants.  These enchants range from best in slot to completely useless depending upon build.  Some builds have zero options in game whatsoever aside from falling back on a massively inferior arcanum enchantment.

From a class balance perspective this is unacceptable because some tank, dps, or healing builds are perpetually locked out of stats other lucky builds happened to be chosen to receive by the original Blizzard developers.  There is no parity.

Please address this finally in this new raid.  Don't limit them per class either, just give us some options and make them on par or a little better than ZG enchants since this is presumably a raid intended to supplement Naxx gear to catch up for Karazhan 40.  Having them be better would also make it feel less penalizing playing builds that don't get to get comparable enchants for raid progression and early gearing until they can access this new later-tier raid.
