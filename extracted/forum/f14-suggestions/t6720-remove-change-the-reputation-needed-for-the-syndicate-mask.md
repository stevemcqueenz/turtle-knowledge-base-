There is an item ingame called the syndicate mask. This colour mask is something I want to obtain, however I cannot. Beside the fact that it's almost impossible to get syndicate reptuation (killing level 50 mobs which give 5 rep ea while you need 40.000 reputation), there is another problem. The item for purchase and for equipment requires friendly reputation with the Syndicate. However, this is not possible. Syndicate reputation doesn't to further than neutral. So either remove the requirement for wearing the item, or change something in the reputation system is my suggestion.
