The problem with engineering is that there's so little you can pass to non-engineers. Yea, you can craft scopes for your friendly neighborhood hunters. And some ez-thro kabooms to add ranged pull capacity to non-ranged characters. But the bulk of the craftables for engineering require engineering skill to be used, so they aren't of use to many players.

I'll admit that many of the devices are crazily powerful. Gnomish Death Ray/Pally bubble combi is a good chunk of health off of any boss fight. The Mithril/Arcanite dragon and the Battlechicken are convenient boss fight support trinkets. The "5 charges of teleport to Stormwind/Ogrimmar" item makes for a convenient RTB if your hearthstone is on cooldown (or allows the HS to transport you to a second location). And explosives, especially iron grenades, are dirt cheap (and even a decent source of gold by just vendoring them after crafting) and make for a nice bit of AOE damage, albeit with a low DPS due to the cooldown. The stun/interrupt bombs do is just icing on the cake.

What I'd like for engineering is more stuff to pass to party members that aren't engineers. EZ-Thro MK3. Maybe a goggle or two, too... And the ability to craft bows/crossbows/arrows maybe? I'm not sure what other profession bow-making would fit with.
