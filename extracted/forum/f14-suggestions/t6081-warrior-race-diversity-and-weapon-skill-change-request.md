# Warrior Race Diversity and Weapon skill change request.

## Post 38207 by Tawneyturtle (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-19T08:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38207#p38207 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Warrior and weapon skills are an age old thing in Classic wow, the amount of Hit you stack is irrelevant when hit alone doesn't reduce the chance of attacks being glanced but weapon skill above 300 (especially 308) help eliminate them on bosses.

As it is, the races Human, Orc and in some rare cases Goblin warriors make up almost 90% of warriors in Naxx, the 10% either already has THC (+6 sword skill) or are using Edgemasters(1000-2000g investment).

If the devs cared enough to change things like Shaman's by giving them racial skills (Hex, Spirit link, Feral Spirits) and even buff things like Night Elf Priest racial, while giving non Dwarf priests access to a worse Fear Ward in Holy Champion, there's no reason why the MOST POPULAR CLASS shouldn't have more alternative race options.

Option 1: Replace the talent Improved Rend with Man of Arms, making it a 5 point talenter that increases your weapon skills by 3/4/5/6/7 points.

I'm not a big fan of this change myself but it'd probably be the easiest solution.

Option 2: Rework Racial bonuses for weapon skills.

Here's my suggestion:

Alliance:
Humans retain Sword and Mace Specialization.
Night Elves gain Sword and Fist Weapon Specialization.
Dwarves gain Axe and Mace Specialization.
Gnomes gain Dagger and Mace Specialization.
High elves gain Sword and Dagger Specialization.

Horde:
Orcs retain their Axe Specialization and gain Mace Specialization.
Tauren gain Mace and Fist Weapon Specialization.
Trolls gain Axe and Fist Weapon Specialization.
Undead gain Sword and Dagger Specialization.
Goblins retain Mace and Dagger Specialization.

Reasoning behind the weapon choices:
Fist weapons: Night elves and Trolls are the most "wild" races in the game with strong connection to nature, it makes sense for them to fight fist and claw against enemies, while Tauren are naturally beefy   turtle_tongue_head   creatures, more than capable of killing a man with a swing of their fists, but balance wise Maces would work better for Tauren with the only downside being it'd be a duplicate of Orc racial.
Mace: Human paladins are iconic from WC3 with their big maces, and Orcs are iconic for Thrall's Doomhammer. Fits both orcs and humans.
Swords: Most popular weapon by far for humans, makes sense for undead humans to not forget their proficiency in swords, High Elves in WC3 that used melee weapons were Footman with swords. The Swords should be the most popular weapon choice for the Alliance to resemble their ranks.
Axes: This has to be the counterpart for Swords, whereas the Humans use dexterity and cunning with blades, the Horde uses brute force and raw strength to cut their foes in two. Fitting for Trolls Tauren and Orcs, while Dwarves who are known for their temper also can emulate a barbarian slicing through hordes of enemies.
Daggers: Daggers are the choice for the small races, Goblins already have this, no reason why Gnomes shouldn't. High elves getting Daggers would fit the stereotype of the non Footmen High Elves lack of physical build having to rely on their agile build and smart thinking to kill foes in close combat, so they'd have a tendency to pick up daggers over any other heavy weapons. Also High Elf Rogues have no benefit from their racial as it is, so it'd be a nice buff. Undead are also rather weak with their bodies being reanimated and falling apart, so their preference to agility based weapons are also apply.

Ranged weapon skills: Ranged weapon skills in classic are absolutely meaningless. You can't dodge a ranged attack nor parry it. There's no reason to buff or change them.

Would this make THC and Edgemaster useless? No because you can still be in a situation where you play a Troll warrior and you simply didn't get any good Axes or Fist weapons.
