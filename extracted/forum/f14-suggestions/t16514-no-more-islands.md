## Post 112807 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-15T21:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112807#p112807 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The shores around the Blasted Lands could be expanded into a Horde-focused land route to the Swamp of Sorrows. Grom'gol Base Camp is supposed to provide a safe chain of supply to Stonard, but the only land route to the Swamp of Sorrows is the road that passes through Duskwood and Deadwind Pass, very much centralizing the path through Alliance territory since a major stretch of the path goes through the Alliance town of Darkshire. Even in Turtle WoW, the path to the Swamp of Sorrows remains heavily Alliance-influenced with the addition of Sorrowguard Keep overlooking the road.

An alternate path that is under Horde control or influence going through the shore surrounding the Blasted Lands could be an interesting addition that would make it more plausible that Grom'gol is able to maintain a supply route with Stonard.

A guy on Reddit came up with ideas for Classic-style retail zones and some other custom zones a couple years ago, one of them was "[The Savage Coast](https://i.redd.it/xslphyinxb431.png)", a custom 45-60 zone that connects Stranglethorn with the Swamp of Sorrows (with a minor connection to the Blasted Lands through a back door to Nethergarde Keep). Something like this could be interesting.

As a follow-up to this, the same Reddit author came up with the idea for the "[Swiftwater Cliffs](https://www.reddit.com/media?url=https%3A%2F%2Fi.redd.it%2F0a0ijeexs4731.png)", which would be located to the north of Azshara and to the east of Winterspring. Though it doesn't appear to be on his version of the map, the ruins of the night elf port village of [Nendis](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Nendis) should be located in the southern-most part of this region, which could work as a hook of sorts for troubles plaguing the region. Looking at [the Warcraft III map displaying Nendis' location](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/d/da/WC3x-N01.jpg), there isn't actually a corresponding location in Azshara's current overworld; in theory, it would be somewhere more to the north in what is currently Winterspring's cliffside. However, the added zone increases the landmass in that area that would give the opportunity to include it.

For those curious, the custom zone to the north of Swiftwater Cliffs is named the "[Veiled Strand](https://www.reddit.com/media?url=https%3A%2F%2Fi.redd.it%2F03dytgz4xl431.png)", which runs along the northern shores of Kalimdor and, along with the Swiftwater Cliffs, connects Azshara to northern Darkshore. It also incorporates an entrance to the Shatterspear troll village, which itself could be made a playable questing hub in the hypothetical scenario of the addition of a zone to the north of Kalimdor such as this.

Both of those zone ideas could be interesting to expand Kalimdor's playable regions in a plausible way, while in the case of Swiftwater Cliffs or it's hypothetical Turtle WoW equivalent incorporating elements of Warcraft III that were not added to the world map in World of Warcraft.
