# Why making eko bop is not a solution

## Post 44991 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-05-08T20:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44991#p44991 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

OK so I hear the rumours that eko is gonna become bop. I would like to make a case why this is not a solution.

First, let's thing about what we achieve with this bop change.
We make it impossible to sell them on the ah, so no more gold farming or trading from these.
We force raider who want to use this to farm it themselves and take away their ability to choose how they want to it either directly farmed or with gold and buy it from the ah with gold

Now I wonder, why is this a good change?
Should we make ungoro Crater crystals bop too since some peolel like to  sell and trade them on ah and some used them in raid.

Some people might be trading ekos for just gold and will have a nasty surprise of bop ekos on their auction house chars or bank chars soon.

Recently people discusses tea with shugar. And one of the only thing people could agree on was that the leaves for the qeust should be tradeable in the ah.
This eko change is the opposite of that. People like to trade and buy things!

The removal of group loot eko achieved already that dual boxers don't have the advantage of getting double loot. Now all that is left is fine tune it.
Incresee the drop chance if it is needed. Or make them drop random 1-3 amount of ekos.

Making the bop solves nothing. It limits the commerce and trading. It's fun buying ekos and reselling for profit.

That's it.
