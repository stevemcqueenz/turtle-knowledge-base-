## Post 75331 by Itankinpvp — 2023-11-01T21:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75331#p75331 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am glad to see this on here.  I just found Ascension, and through the same YouTube creator I found TWOW.  I don't see professions being as useful in Ascension, you might out-level what you are trying to make just by collecting the materials. I think the lumberjack/woodworking could truly shine on TWoW, as it fills some gaps in what professions do for the player.  The temporary weapon buffs is a great idea, thanks Invokersama, while there are some from enchanting, alchemy, and blacksmith there are gaps in the coverage: like types of stats, types of damage, and some could be applied to shields.

I posted this basic thing on the Ascension forum:

The profession should be woodworking with specializations of carving and carpentry: carving is more of the artistic products of woodworking and carpentry is more of the technical products of woodworking.
Carving could have figurines/totems much like jewel crafting, or some engineering items. "Unpowered" wands could also be part of carving, and require an enchanter to power them so they can be used.  They could be "use" items that list the materials required to power and the level of enchanting required, no new interface.  Carving could have hilts/handles/stocks that modify stats other ["Augments"](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Augments) don't.  Carving could add sockets to items (if TBC content is in the future).

More of a new mechanic, lacquer.  This might need a new interface to make it user-friendly.  Each stat possible with a suffix could have a lacquer (shellac dissolved in alcohol) associated with it. The shellac could be a secondary harvest from trees, and a non-gimmick use for alcohol. The buffs could be based on the elixir/potion used to make the lacquer. This would limit the recipes needed, just multiple coats to get the desired suffix. Extra coats should require higher woodworking skills. These would be applied to a hilt/handle/stock and be able to change the ["suffix"](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Item_suffix) of the appropriate item.  The hilts/handles/stocks that can be applied to any weapon could be a carving specialization exclusive as they would be more powerful not limited to suffix items and not removing existing buffs.  Another carving specialization could be fully powered wands.  Staves are another specialization.

Fletching could be a specialization under carpentry. Shields would be another specialty of carpentry. Non-combat items could be another carpentry thing, like a canoe if it can be implemented. Chairs and cots could also be a carpentry product that could grant a buff similar to "well fed" being "well rested" (increase to agility and intelligence?) and could remove disease and poison.  Couple these with the existing tent item for better results.

Marionette puppets could even be similar to engineering mechanical pets, they should require enchanting dust/essences/shards to craft so to animate, and be more debuff/fear based aids than damage.

For player housing, carving would be more decorative and carpentry would be furniture.

The "Rebirth" Seeds should be a drop from Woodcutting, as should Gift of the Wild items.  Rotting wood could be a source of Dust of Decay.  With the manchineel tree being a thing even the poison "essences" could be harvested this way. Symbols, runes, and Ankhs could also be a possible product of carving, and possibly items that do the same kind of spells that require woodcutting/carving to use. Maybe they could be unreliable much like the engineering items that mimic spells, or teleport and portals could be random.  These are more gold sinks, like ammo, that the professions  are meant to help with.
