# Tweak the T3 Enha 6-Set bonus

## Post 106410 by GR3ddy — 2024-11-04T22:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106410#p106410 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is an interesting idea but it has two problems in my opinion.

The way it works right now is if we cast Earth shock we get 5s buff on us that will boost our next Flame shock, if we cast Flame shock we get the same buff for Frost shock and lastly Frost shock buffs Earth shock.

 1. In my opinion the duration of 5s is too short. With talents our shocks cd is 5s so that means we would have to delay SS or LS in our rotation in order to buff up our shock which is a direct dps loss.
An easy fix would be to increase the duration to 7-8 seconds, that way we can never have the buff for two shocks and would allow us the do the rotation properly.

 2. The way it works right now in order to get the full effect we have to cycle through all three different spell schools.
This means that the its usage would be limited for bosses that are immune to one of the schools like BWL bosses or KT/Sapph.
An easy fix would be for shocks to buff the two other schools. For example earth shocks would buff next frost or flame shock.

Thank you for consideration.
