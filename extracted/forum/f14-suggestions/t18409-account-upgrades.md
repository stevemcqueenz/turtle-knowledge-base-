I thought about this a bit and how it could work so mind you it's not a perfect suggestion.

I am sure I'm not the only one that struggles with this but I'd like to donate but well everything is character specific and for people who play multiple characters this kinda well sucks.

So I was looking over the things in the shop and seen the illusions which got me thinking.

Why not add unlockable races that you can purchase for your account such as Draenei for Alliance or I'm not sure honestly for horde I'm not sure.

But what about racials?  Let them be any class on that faction but the racials they get are from a race of that class.

For instance if you made a draenei paladin you could choose human, dwarf, or high elf racials.  If you choose a Draenei warlock you can choose gnome or human racials.

This allows for people to have something that they can access at any time and not be limited to a single character.

I mean there's already tons of out of era mounts, mini pets, and illusions and unlockable races that can only get racials from that factions race and class selection it's not a pay to win feature.
