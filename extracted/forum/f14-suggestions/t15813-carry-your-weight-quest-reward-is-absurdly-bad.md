# Carry your Weight quest reward is absurdly bad

## Post 107791 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-13T05:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107791#p107791 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[Carry your weight](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=791) is "hidden" quest. You get it from tower in Razor Hill. To get to the tower you need to exit town, go round the corner, climb that tower and there will be quest giver.
Quest objective is not very hard - collect 8 "cloth" pieces from Kul Tiras sailors or Kolkar Centaurs.
But reward is just joke. It is 4 slot bag. At the time you get this quest you probably have 4 bags with 6 slots each.
It is very likely that you immediately sell this bag. You can sell it right away or in 1 hour after grinding more mobs.
I believe this bag is relic from old days where 6-slot bags didn't drop from regular monsters.

Proposal is: make [Handmade Leather Bag](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11845) to be 8 slots.
