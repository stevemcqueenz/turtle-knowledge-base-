## Post 138717 by Mekk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-11T10:42:00Z

One thing to remember when it comes to new playable races, especially enemy races, is that it would not be the entire race, but likely a single tribe.  Trolls are a great example of this.  We have Jungle Trolls with the Darkspear Tribe, but no Forest, Ice, Sand, etc.  So they could fairly easily write in a single tribe of Furbolgs, Ogres, Quilboar, etc, and could even have them start with lower reputations with certain races or something similar.

 **However**, the biggest hurdle would be that none of the enemy races are able to display gear.  I *thoroughly* enjoy playing my Harpy and Gnoll Illusions, but outside of weapons (and shields in the case of the Gnoll), no gear is displayed on the character.  While I'm perfectly fine with that, I think a very large percentage of players would find that to be a deal breaker; and I think that's why they best fit as an Illusion rather than a full playable race.

Now, if they wanted to do something like Sand Trolls, Wildhammer Dwarves, Kul'Tiran Humans, or some other variant to a pre-existing playable race to add some additional flavor with new Racials, then yeah, I could see a way for them to do that and make sense.  You could easily have a small group splinter off and do their own thing.  Granted, its still a lot of work, and I'm not sure if they would bring in new players or not.

When it comes to Classes, that's **much more difficult**, because a new class would completely disrupt the balance of the end-game (and PvP).  It would also need to be a lore-friendly class, and would need to be a broadly designed class that is available to both factions.  Tinker would be a good example of this, except that it would likely be limited to Gnome and Goblin, which are the least played races.  Necromancer would be another good example, so long as its not just a re-skinned Warlock, and a more traditional Monk would also work.  Blademaster would be a bad example, since its a very narrow design and its pretty much an Orc class.  Witch Doctor and Shadow Hunter are also distinctly Horde classes, whereas Spell Breaker, Warden, and Demon Hunter are distinctly Alliance classes.

It's been said multiple times, but I will waste some time to explain why the races proposed in the OP can't work.

Centaur, Naga, Dryads have bodies that can't equip normal gear, even if gear is equipped, by default it will have almost no visual representation except chests, shoulders, head, gloves and wrists. Even if the developers waste their time to make gear work for their bodies, it will not be lore-friendly, because those races will require race-specific gear, which as a mechanic is not even in the game, some Asian MMOs have it and it's not making the games more fun or better.

While Broken doesn't have a very specific body, the problem, along with the other three races is that there is no point for all four of them to align with either Alliance or the Horde.

Dryads just serve nature and by coincidence are aligned with Druids and from there with Night Elves and Tauren. They aren't taking sides, because nature isn't taking sides.

Centaurs are the reason why Tauren fled their homeland of Thousand Needles and moved to Mulgore - there is no way for Tauren to accept those who killed them and chased them from their homeland. Also Centaurs are something like Chaotic Neutral and serve some old earth gods.

Nagas also have the same issue and there is no reason for them to join the Horde.

Broken are in a place similar to Dryads, but are more likely to join the Alliance, but neither them or Draenei have place on Turtle WoW. They could exist as some NPCs you can talk to or do quests for, but that's it.

I'm very opposed to races that existed in official expansions be added to Turtle WoW, because it devalues Turtle WoW as its own unique thing and makes it look like a cheap soulless that only copies official things, because they can't come up with something on their own. Also the races proposed would make Turtle WoW feel like a cheap fun server where they add Murlocs, Gnolls, Quillboars, rabbits, squirrels and frogs as playable races, because they have nothing better to do. It's not even a fun server, it's a travesty.

I won't comment on the classes, they may be a good idea, but I didn't bother even reading their names. Adding new classes is something I'm more inclined to align myself with, but races will add nothing interesting. I played both Goblin and High Elf when they released and the excitement wears off in mere hours after creating the character, whereas a class can be interesting from 1 to 60 and even beyond that.

High Elves - cool - your elf has human skin and hair and blue eyes, big deal.

Goblins - outstanding - you get to be a green-skinned gnome that looks like a cross between a Gnome, Troll and an Orc, unbelievable.
