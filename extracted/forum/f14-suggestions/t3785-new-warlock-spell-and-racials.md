# New Warlock spell and Racials

## Post 22830 by Notawen (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-19T14:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22830#p22830 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Felfire Bolt: [Cost: 10% HP] [2 Second Cast Time]
"Unleashes a bolt of felfire causing <10% of casters HP> fire damage.
The target will also burn for <1% of casters HP> per second for one minute,
this effect can be stacked up to five times. Can be casted while moving, however
interruption by any means causes the Warlock to lose concentration and damage
themselves instead." (including the dot)

Racials:

Changes to make them balanced are here: [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3847](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3847)

Orc:

Dominant Master [Passive] "The Warlock's imp can be summoned alongside their other minions."

Undead:

Grafting: [30 second cast time] [Lasts until cancelled]
"Graft an additional arm to your body. This arm can function like one of your already existing ones and
it can even hold a shield. Can only be performed by taking a humanoid or an undead arm."
(gives parry chance too)

Goblin:

Overwhelming Power: "You let malicious energies swell inside you, giveing you 20% additional spellpower for 30
seconds. When this effect ends you die and deal <caster's maximum hp> fire damage in a 60 yard
radius. If you die before the end of this effect this damage is doubled."

Human:

Unleash Spirit: [Instant Cast] [30 Second Cooldown] [1 Soulstone and 20% of mana Cost]
"You break a Soulstone to summon a spirit that fights for you for 2 minutes. The damage it deals
heals you."

Gnome:

Shrink: "Shrinks your opponent, finally making them to be the shorter one. Oh, and also making them weaker."
(reduces all of the target's stats by 0,5 per caster's level)
