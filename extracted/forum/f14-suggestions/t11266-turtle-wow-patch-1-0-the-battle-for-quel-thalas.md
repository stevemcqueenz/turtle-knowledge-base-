# Turtle WoW Patch 1.??.0: The Battle for Quel'thalas

## Post 74582 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-10-29T03:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74582#p74582 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Quel'thalas, once the magical land that housed the great capital of Silvermoon, pride of the noble race of high elves that at the same time contained within it the greatest magical power in all of Azeroth, the Sunwell.
For thousands of years the region enjoyed an eternal spring, inside the magic of the High Elves enveloped the place, protected and fed by the Sunwell.
Until one day the plague, led by the Traitor Prince Arthas Menethil, devastated the place, most of the High Elves were killed in their homeland and the few that remained fled, abandoning their ancient home at the mercy of the Undead and the destruction.
Now, years after its destruction and with the High Elf people partially recovered, they are preparing to fight again for their former home, unaware of the horrors that have been created within the once magical city of Silvermoon.

NEW AREA: Quel'thalas
The ancient home of the High Elf race that they shared hostilely with the Amani Trolls, at its center, the ruins of the City of Silvermoon, converted into the largest bastion of the Scourge in the Eastern Kingdoms.

Sub-zones:

Death Song Forest: Once called Eversong Forest, today it is a corrupt place with the plague of death and rot, the once golden autumn trees of the forest have absorbed the plague and have become twisted and dead trees, among its trees the Abominable creations of the plague roam and lie in wait for any unwary who venture without caution into the cursed forest.

Deatholme: Located at the entrance to the forest, The Bastion that Arthas Menethil established in his attack on Silvermoon City seeking the Power of the Sunwell today serves as surveillance so that no one enters or anything leaves the forest.

New Capital City of the Horde, Zul'Aman:
The capital of the Amani Trolls, located in a remote corner of Quel'thalas and guided by Zul'jin, the Trolls happily watched as the High Elves were slaughtered by Arthas and his abominations of death, eventually the Undead horde began to attack the citadel and the Trolls were forced to abandon it, now, with the help of the New Horde they have managed to partially recover it, while the Goblins have helped them rebuild and fortify it, seeking to once again dominate the region that was taken from them by the High Elves milleniums ago.

Updated Capital City, Alah'thalas:
The new capital of the High Elves, with the help of the Alliance, the High Elves have managed to convert the old outpost into their new capital and home, very close to Quel'thalas, it will now serve as a starting point in their fight to recover their old lands.

New Customizations for High Elves and Trolls:

High Elves:
 ---- Green Eyes: some of the Blood Elves who were scattered around the world after the departure and betrayal of Kael'thas, seeing that their High Elf brothers have managed to prosper and relocate, have decided to abandon the name Sin'dorei given by the Traitor Prince and become Quel'dorei again, although doubtful, the Quel'dorei have decided to accept them and find a way to cure their addiction to magic and purify the fel magic within them while they search for their place again, in the restored elven society.
 ---- Tattoos: Having managed to master their addiction to magic, the High Elves have managed to obtain superior mastery over magic without abusing it, this is reflected in their bodies, on which arcane marks have begun to emerge, whose color varies depending on the type of magic they use or even, the sacred light itself has influenced their bodies.
 ---- Golden Eyes: Other elves, due to the absence of arcane magic, have turned to sacred light, whose influence is reflected in their eyes.

Trolls:
 ---- Body Variant: Amani trolls have trained Trolls in the art of war in a way never before seen, giving the most skilled ones large, muscular bodies like never before, with strength comparable to that of the orcs
 ---- Scars: Due to the rigorous and exhaustive training of the Amani Trolls, it is not unusual for aspirants to acquire distinctive marks on their bodies, demonstrating their dedication and toughness to pain.
 ----- Tattoos: The trolls trained by the Amani are marked with different tattoos to show that they have been trained by them, giving them a distinctive appearance from other trolls.

New Dungeon:

Sunwell Ruins: The location of the High Elves' beloved and shattered magical fountain, destroyed by Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider, is now a lifeless hole spewing corruption and fel magic as the demons roam freely looking for a way to resurrect the source to summon their demonic leaders.

New Raid:

Silvermoon Ruins: The Glorious City of the High Elves, now a gigantic bastion of the plague, death and corruption roam its streets in the form of the Undead, while the souls of the Quel'dorei fallen in the Invasion of the Plague wander wailing in its alleys and abandoned buildings, hordes and hordes of endless Undead emerge from within.
The Horde and the Alliance have resolved that, to root out evil, they must assault the once great city of the High Elves and exterminate whatever is creating these abominations of the Lich King.

This is my first time trying to create lore, so, give me your opinions, this is my personal suggestion lore for a possible future Quel'thalas in Turtle WoW.
