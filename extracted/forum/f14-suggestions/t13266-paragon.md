## Post 88904 by Pristn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-02-28T08:09:00Z

Diablo 3 once introduced an excellent system for players of the maximum level. Which continued to give the feeling of pumping endlessly. Maybe you should think about adding a similar system to  Turtle   turtle_in_love  ? For players who have been playing for many years, this would be a nice addition and an incentive to play.

This could also be extended to all level 60 characters on one account.

For gaining a level, you could give a small reward in the form of 1 stat point or 0.1% crit, a drop of gold and a chance of dropping an additional item from mobs in a solo game. If you come up with 15-20 types of rewards per level (you will receive the same stat once every 15-20 lvls),  then the increase in stats will be scanty, but pleasant for old players. For getting 100 250 500 1000 (for example) paragon level, you could give a reward in the form of a title.

You can use this system in the reputation or honor tab, the system is similar, the only difference is that there are more levels and (and does not decrease as is the case with Honor).

The player will receive exp from any activity, killing mobs, quests, killing bosses, pvp, or even extracting resources.
