Me and one other from my group were waiting for the others near the entrance to the instance. At some point, two of the alliance attacked my partner. He wasn't active and they just beat him up. I couldn't just watch this and started healing him. I ended up saving him, but these scumbags called me a cheater and other dirty words, as well as a message from one of them “reported”. After a while I got a message from the GM “Greetings. Please, remember that Cross faction PvP is forbidden, you cannot actively assist your groupmate of opposite faction who is involved in PvP. Follow the rules.” So I had to watch those bastards beat up my partner and then wait for his resurrection, after which they would attack him again?!
Either ban PvP near instance entry areas, or completely disable PvP in cross-faction groups. So that this kind of ridiculous situation doesn't happen.
