Hellow dear Turtle WoW People   smiling_turtle

after playing tons of different RPGs i can say that none of them gives me the same feeling as fighting bosses in good olde WoW Dungeons - so i came up with an idea for a game mode

the idea is basicly:
 -players start at 60(max level)
 -in groups of 5 they have to beat very hard instances/dungeons
 -the loot is gear

once you made it through a dungeon you can enter the next one.

 -there is no grinding for mats
 -no leveling

just dungeons with friends

im an architect
with master deg ![😄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f604.svg)and ue4 dev - and have a lot of friends that are talented in world building
so we could build the stuff needed in noggit!

but i have no idea of the other tasks that are needed to have a Mod like this running.

im not sure if this game mode can be fit in the turtle WoW universe, but maybe there could be something like
"Turtle Endgame" a new server with this mod on it.

Niralthas a friendly Man on Turtle Disc told me to post this Idea in the forum to hear what you guys think about it.

would love to hear some opinions. :)
