# Idea for Paladin defensive and Offensive Cooldowns

## Post 17699 by Gerdyus — 2022-03-24T08:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17699#p17699 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Bolster :: Seal to Shield spell -- does not consume seal-- Defensive Cooldown at end of prot tree -- >3 minute cooldown
Seal of righteousness -> For the next 20 seconds increases chance to block by x% and increases block value by y%
Seal of Wisdom -> For the next 20 seconds spell manacost is reduced by x% and taking damage generates y% mana
Seal of Light -> For the next 20 seconds flash of light may be instantly cast.
Seal of the Crusader -> For the next 20 seconds judgment has no cooldown.
Seal of Justice -> AOE taunt (??)

Embolden :: Seal to Weapon spell -- does not consume seal -- Offensive Cooldown at end of ret tree -- >3 minute cooldown
Seal of righteousness -> For the next 20 seconds hammer of wrath can be used on a target with any health %.
Seal of Wisdom -> For the next 20 seconds every melee attack gives x mana to your group.
Seal of Light -> For the next 20 seconds recieve healing equal to x of your damage dealt.
Seal of the Crusader -> For the next 20 seconds your attacks become pure holy damage.
Seal of Justice -> Demonstrate the power of the light with a boastful display (RP cooldown flex)
Seal of Command -> Become an avatar of the light, increasing attack and spell power by x for 20 seconds.
