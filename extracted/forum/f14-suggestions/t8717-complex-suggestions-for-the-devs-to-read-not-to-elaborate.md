Monk

Although I saw that currently there are now no plans of adding new classes i just thought of giving out my idea. There are a few reasons why i think Monk would be a good class to add in Turtle Wow.

Major points:

 1.Monk is not as flashy of a class like the Demon Hunter that was added in Legion, the DH feels like an anime protagonist that was added into Wow. It wouldn't ruin the vanilla feel of the server because you won't see Monks dashing around the capital city like a Demon Hunter.
 2. Their raid gear would be simplistic, i don't know about you but i feel out of all the classes in retail wow the Monk seems like they need the least effort to design their raid gear. The whole class is a about tranquility and inner balance or that type of stuff, therefore they don't need to design anything extradionary for the class as they do for the Paladins, Mages, etc..
 3. It could bring Turtle Wow in the spotlight with the newly added Monk class, there are other private servers with their own classes but those same servers have like 10+ new races added and several new classes but all of them poorly designed and buggy.
 4. Will attract players who specifically loved playing monk in retail, although im aware that the Monk is definitely not the most played class it still has its own playerbase. If the class is added in Turtle wow id say the Monk class will top the charts at first but after a few days it will balance itself out, weeding out the ones who just wanted to test the class out.

Lore Reason of adding Monks:

 1. As many may know monks existed in Warcraft: Founding of Durotar, therefore making them cannon in the time period turtle wow is set in. If it was up to me id make the lore reason of adding monks like this: Something bad happened in Pandaria which triggered a pandaren refuge crisis. These few Pandaren who settled in both Alliance and Horde lands brought the art of the Monk with them. The Horde and Alliance demanded, if these Pandaren want a home in their lands they need to share their Monk skills with them.

Available Races for Monk:

Id say all classes except the Blood Elf and Goblin should be able to become monks because these 2 races are refugees also just as the Pandaren Monks.

Roles:

Incentivizing raiders to do dungeons is a double edged sword.
Sure you get more well geared players on average making dungeons easier and quicker to complete.

But in dungeons you need 20% Tanks, while in a raid your group needs 5-10% of the party being tanks. If you convert an equal share of Tanks and DPS from a raid to do dungeons, you only make it harder for the groups that genuinely need loot from dungeons to find a tank.

I would love to run some dungeons on my raidgeared paladin, but i'd want gear appropreate challenges in dungeons and not just aoe cleave everything down since its too easy.
