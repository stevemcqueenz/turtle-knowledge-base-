# Marksman Hunter is pathetic

## Post 154303 by Spitfire33 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-04T07:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154303#p154303 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Marksman hunter is weak and clunky in both PvE and PvP.
This is what i think should be done:

 1. Reduce minimal range on all ranged abilities from 8 to 5.
Deadzone is awful and is not fun. Not being able to use your ranged weapon is already bad, not being able to do anything is just stupid.

 2. Allow traps to be placed in combat.
Survival hunters can do that, why not make it baseline? Untamed Trapper can have something else like maybe allow to have 2 traps at a time, or reduced cooldown on traps.

 3. Add new aspect that gives chance to regenerate mana on hit.
There is literally no ability for mana sustain currently. I think having to switch aspect (makes you do less damage) for some mana is fair.

 4. Make Blessing of Might, Battle Shout give ranged AP.
Recently devs made elixir of demonslaying give ranged AP, i dont see a reason to not give the same effect to other buffs.

 5. Make windfury totem work on ranged weapons (basically same effect as Endless Quiver talent, able to stack).
Melees have fun, why hunter is not allowed to have fun?

 6. Fix Steady Shot cast time.
If I have Swift Aspects proc and Rapid Fire active Steady Shot cast time will be longer than my auto attack which is dumb, why make so many hunter spells, quiver give attack speed if I cant use my main damaging spell with it without clipping my auto?

 7. Make Aimed Shot useful.
Cast time (3 seconds) compared to damage (its laughable) just makes this spell useless. Im better off spamming Steady Shot instead. Make a talent that reduces its cast time or just make it do more damage, or make a talent that have a chance to proc reduced cast time on this ablility, something...

 8. Remove cooldown on Wing Clip
Warriors should have their 6 SECONDS! (omg) cooldown removed too so we can go back to spamming slow on each other once again as intended.

 9. Reduce cooldown significantly and increase range slightly on Scare Beast
This spell is useless in its current state because range is so short and if its resisted (which happens all the time) its gone on a 30 seconds cooldown  dead_turtle_head
