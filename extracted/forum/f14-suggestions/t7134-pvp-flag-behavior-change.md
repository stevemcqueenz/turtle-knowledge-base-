The current problem with our system is it rewards less interaction. A high level can come to a hot spot like barrens or goldshire and flag and kill low levels. This doesn't encourage any PvP if a player is waiting out their flag status and is a result of this Hybrid like server we have. Personally I thinking making the PvP flagging more interactive out weighs a player that isn't familiar with the flagging system getting camped for 5 minutes. If hardcore players can go through the game with the same flag awareness there isn't a reason a normal player can't be as aware.

You know what?  I agree.  The way the original *World of Warcraft* (and by proxy, *Turtle WoW*) implements it, PvP is kind of a seemingly arbitrary thing that doesn't mean a whole lot to anything but dedicated roleplayers or people who are **really** bored.  As I pointed out, some quest mobs are flagged for PvP, but it *really* doesn't make a lot of sense for that to be a thing unless the player is tasked with, say, killing 10 Stormwind City Guards or something like that.

 *Turtle WoW*'s "War Mode" is kind of an odd duck in that it permanently flags the player for PvP while giving them a permanent 30% XP boost.  In theory, you'd think this might encourage more world PvP, but in *practice*, all it does is encourage high-level characters to gank lowbies looking to power through the early levels.  Some appreciate the added tension.  Some don't.  Either way, it doesn't really encourage fair and balanced world PvP.

I wonder if it would be possible to make it so players who are flagged for PvP can only attack other players with a ±5 level difference outside of strict PvP zones?  That might help.
