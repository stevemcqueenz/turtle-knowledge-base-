# Ice Threaded Arrow/Bullet

## Post 6144 by Raymundo — 2020-05-28T02:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6144#p6144 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It would be awesome if Hunter's were able to access the Ice Threaded Arrows/Bullets from something other than PvP. I would get them the normal way but getting an AV started is a tall task in itself, let alone getting Honored in rep.

I think these differ from the other rep rewards because it leaves Hunters with 2 options. Farm/Dungeon/Raid with the level 40 arrows with much less DPS, or constantly craft/buy Thorium arrows, and they go fast.

I have no idea what work it takes to implement these changes so sorry if these seem over the top, but some ideas would be changing the Faction that rewards them to a different, more accessible, but still late game one(I.e Timbermaw or Argent Dawn). Or a possible quest to complete that then allows you to purchase them from a NPC (maybe its in Winterspring killing Frost Giants or something ice themed)
