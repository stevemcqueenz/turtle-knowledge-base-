# Alterac Valley needs some tweaking

## Post 24617 by Floaf — 2022-09-07T21:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24617#p24617 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Alterac Valley is a very poorly designed battleground.

It's actually suboptimal to pvp, and grouping up to pvp the enemy is always, always detrimental to your honor grind. This is because it stalls the game to over 3 hours at times.

Now pvp fans will say "It's a battleground, you can't blame us for wanting to pvp"

And I agree! Pvp should be encouraged! This goes back to fundamental flaws in the bg design.

My suggestion is to add a reinforcement counter similar to newer versions of AV. This way, if one team decides they want to group up for pvp, they can still win the battleground eventually instead of making it an indefinite stalemate.

As it is, the pvp gear for the server is locked behind AV (because WSG and AB can't fill games), and it is bad for player experience to have 3 hour long stalemates every time you try to queue, and AV punishes both teams for pvping because of its basic design flaws.

This would promote a healthier pvp scene because it would make pvp more competitive when more people have access to rank gear, even the lower rank gear. It would also encourage more people to pvp in battlegrounds in general.
