# Dungeon Finder: Protecting Player Time From Instant Leavers

## Post 148047 by Alunaz — 2025-08-24T18:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148047#p148047 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Something that has been bothering me is the Dungeon Finder system. While it often works fine, often enough a group will disband shortly after forming. Tanks can almost always join a new dungeon instantly, so they frequently leave for whatever reason. Meanwhile, DPS players may have waited in the queue for over an hour.

My suggestion is that if a formed group disbands within the first 5-10 minutes, the players who did not leave should be placed directly back at the top of the queue for that specific dungeon. It's only fair to respect everyone's time equally.

Perhaps players who leave quickly should also face a penalty. In Retail, there was a 30-minute deserter debuff for leaving a group, which I think served this purpose very well. However, to me, punishment is not as important as ensuring players don't have to wait for hours just because they were unlucky with an impatient tank.

It only makes sense to me if it's technically possible. Please find a way to alleviate this issue.

Like I said, the debuff is the secondary part of my suggestion. The most critical issue is the queue position.

The core problem is that when a group disbands instantly, players who may have already waited an hour are forced to re-queue from the very back of the line. This can lead to a cycle where they get unlucky with multiple instant leaver groups, adding up their total wait time to several hours, which feels absurd and is a massive disrespect of their time.

I have experienced this several times and I bet many have.
