# [Hunter] lets talk tank

## Post 33533 by Kerenis (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-01-30T07:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33533#p33533 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since a big part of the hunter community wants to melee hunter, I wanted to suggest a couple easy changes that would kill 2 birds with one shot: hunter melee and lack of tanks

Similar to DK avoidance tanking, hunters could become excellent tanks up to 5/10/20 man content. This changes would not affect PVP balance at all

 -Remove Distract Shot range restriction, this is Hunter taunt
 -Rework Improved Aspect of the Monkey to add threat to your melee attacks, along with the dodge bonus. This is the tanking aspect

For 31 pt Survival talent I got a few ideas that would help tanking. Pick one of them

 -Increases your defense and armor by X% of your agility (this is the "druid bear" route)
 -Everytime you dodge/parry you recover 5% mana/hp (this is the DK tanking style route)
 -Allows the use of shields, recover 5%mana/hp every block (same as prot pally tanking)

Feel free to add more ideas below

I played hunter in melee style around lvl 30. Main idea is to slow enemy and then use difference in weapon swing timers to deal 1 strong hit against 1 weak fast hit. Build looked like this <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter/55-0-UUC06>
To be on a safe side, you use aspect of the monkey and get crits with mongoose bite and raptor strikes. Growl on pet was disabled.

Actually, I'd like to play hunter as kiting tank on other servers, but bosses seems to be immune to slow on turtle. Kiting is natural playstyle of hunter. Using shield is not.

Main issue with tanks is that you lack people *willing* to tank. It is not about amount of classes able to tank. Warrior is popular, but not everyone actualy tank.

Keep in mind, that "tanking" build should work in green gear too. If you need t2 to tank 5 mans, this build is not viable.
