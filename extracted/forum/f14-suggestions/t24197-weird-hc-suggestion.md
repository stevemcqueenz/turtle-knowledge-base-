Hear me out here TWoW team.

How about when you die in HC, your character turns into a level 1 lunatic. From there you have the option  to kill a boss in a dungeon, we'll go with Jergosh the Invoker from RFC as an example.

Since Jergosh is a warlock, he can drop a Soulstone that's BIND ON PICKUP(SOULBOUND). If you get this item you can go back and use it on your gravestone to revive your character.

I only suggest this with the addition of Inferno which changes the purpose of HC from getting the Immortal title, to actually playing with consequence permanently.

I feel like this addition will not only help bolster the inferno community, but will also add a nice bit of challenge, making people both try the level 1 lunatic while adding a layer of world lore.

This would not be an easy challenge to complete, either. So people would have to work hard towards reviving their character, and not everyone will be able to complete the challenge to do so.
