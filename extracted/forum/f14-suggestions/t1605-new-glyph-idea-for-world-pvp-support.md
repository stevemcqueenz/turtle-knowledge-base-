# New Glyph idea for World PVP support

## Post 9437 by Starkobjekt (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-02-19T17:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9437#p9437 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So world pvp doesn't happen. Not whilst leveling, nor at 60. This is understandable on a PvE server but also a dealbreaker for some. Personally I haven't enjoyed 1.12 pvping yet and I don't think that I will either but this idea I have feels like a good one, here it goes:

New Glyph obtainable at any level, **Glyph of Head Hunting** (the name fits Horde better.)

When a player has this Glyph he or she can no longer toggle pvp on or off, it will always be **on**.
When this player kills an enemy player he will recieve an item called ''*<race>* Head'' example ''Gnome Head''
this item will also have an ilvl matching the killed player's level. The head can be turned-in at a custom NPC in any major city for a XP reward and loot based on the ilvl of the head. Loot could be gear/weapon/consumables/money, anything.

I think that covers all of it, in theory! Thanks for reading and please comment what you think
