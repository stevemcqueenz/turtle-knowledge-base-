Bigger, highest, stronger. Good but not always. T3.5 already looks like the maximum for classic.
I suggest to focus on horizontal improvement. To add items addotional effects that will changes effects of current spells. Gives us new interesting combinations, rotations, builds around effects.
For example as in DRG (game) modules not just buffs more and more, they changes current main abilities. Similar to glyphs but with benefits u get also penalties. + or - CD, + or - mana, + or - dmg, + or - aoe range etc.  And as a usable trinkets this effects has delay after wearing

Example off-hand.
for wl
Grimore of Soul ripper: stats, spell power as usual for the corresponding ilvl AND additional effect
"drain life drain soul and darkharvest deal /3 dmg but can attack up to 4 targets"

Orb of hellfire: stats..... and additional effect
"after soulfire crits appears imp for 5 sec on target spot, but soulfire generates more threat and has more chance to miss(resist)"

For priest:

lightwell can also regen mana and dispell poison, disease, bleeding. but regen less hp

for pala:

for shama:
