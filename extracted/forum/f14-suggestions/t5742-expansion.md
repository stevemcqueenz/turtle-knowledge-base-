## Post 36019 by Andromath (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-24T22:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36019#p36019 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

From what I understand, there are no plans for a traditional, 'expansion pack.'

Instead there would be patches added over time which expand the world, adding new zones, new quests, new dungeons, new raids, even new battlegrounds. New races may come along as well, but I don't know the Turtle WoW Dev's minds on what would validate a race as a playable option (such as being able to visibly equip gear).

I don't think they have any intent to increase the level gap. The talent trees might get tweaked for the sake of balance, but this is a Vanilla server, so I don't think they want to seriously overhaul them.
