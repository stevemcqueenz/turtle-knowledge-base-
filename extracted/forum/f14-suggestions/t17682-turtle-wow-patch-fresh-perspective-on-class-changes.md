# Turtle WoW Patch: Fresh Perspective on Class Changes

## Post 120600 by Resnica — 2025-02-21T19:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120600#p120600 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone!

I've noticed a lot of discussions and disagreements about the latest updates on Turtle WoW—class changes and raid gear adjustments. I haven't played for a while, but I jumped in to check out the new patch with great enthusiasm!

⚠ Disclaimer: This is purely my subjective opinion. I'm not trying to convince anyone, just sharing my observations.

PvP
If the goal was balance, then... well... we got One-shot. One-kick. One-out. If you don’t have a burst that can delete 50-70% HP in seconds, your class is trash.

![🔥](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f525.svg) Mages and Warlocks – absolute nightmare fuel. Insane crits, absurd damage, and barely any counterplay. Paladins, Warriors, Mages, and Warlocks feel perfectly fine in PvP, easily closing gaps and using situational combos. Not even mentioning Paladins—they're on a whole different level of "balance."

![⚡](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/26a1.svg) Shamans are now auto-nukers. Everything that lands gets instantly purged, and their damage output feels just as oppressive as Warriors’.

![🏹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3f9.svg) Hunters – A good Hunter was always strong, and while they didn’t get as much attention in PvP changes, they’re still a force to be reckoned with. It’s a high-skill class compared to Warriors, Paladins, and Shamans, but fun nonetheless.

🗡 Rogues... oh boy. This is where things get ugly. Easily the most useless class after the update.
They got nothing for PvP. Ambush is still nerfed, and considering how crazy burst damage is across the board, Rogue damage feels laughable, even against clothies. Against Warriors, Paladins, and Shamans? Forget it. Feels like they have a 95% win rate against Rogues.

PvE
This one's simple: everyone got buffed... except Rogues.
They threw them a useless "support build" that is completely irrelevant in modern raid setups.

![💪](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4aa.svg) Warriors, Mages, and Warlocks are absolute DPS monsters in PvE, even with mediocre gear. And Rogues? Even in full BiS, they can’t compete with any of them. Total disaster.

Now, the real question…
Most classes have received abilities from later expansions or at least indirect buffs that have significantly improved their performance.
So maybe it's time to give Rogues something useful too?

⚠ P.S. If you're considering coming back to Turtle WoW, DO NOT ROLL A ROGUE until the next patch. Complete garbage tier.

(At this point, the devs should seriously consider giving Rogues an actual defensive tool, not just throwing them a bone and saying, "If you don’t like it, don’t play it." Why not add Shadowstep or Cloak of Shadows? Or at least something that lets Rogues not feel like complete punching bags. Right now, it’s just a lottery—if you’re not in full BiS and can’t kill in 2 seconds, you get deleted.)

All in all, I had a blast! Huge thanks to the devs—I really enjoyed testing all the changes! My favorite class is Priest, which I didn’t mention much, but trust me, they’re doing just fine. ![😎](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f60e.svg)

Wishing everyone happiness, positivity, and great gaming experiences! See you around!

P.S. Devs, buff Rogues. Seriously. ![😄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f604.svg)
