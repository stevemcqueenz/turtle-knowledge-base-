I feel like you have undersold the value of the Arcane baseline spells and talents in some of these types. Ask any seasoned Raider/PvPer etc and they would probably miss the Arcane spells the most if you took them away.

 1)	**AoE** – Arcane Explosion, across all environments, is pretty much one of the most flexible and widely used spells. Yes, it doesn’t have the control of frost but it’s damage is still significant.
 2)	**Damage (Single Target)** – No debate here, from a raw DPS point of view in any environment, Arcane Missiles falls behind frostbolt/fireball spam. However, I will challenge you on your comment “arcane has one spell that is basically useless”. For raiding, yes, it is outmatched by frostbolt/fireball but it is not “useless”. For levelling/dungeons, it is absolutely fine. For PvP, yes, it is clearly weakest here due to such exposure to lockouts.
 3)	**Defensive Spells** – I just want to point out that Blink is, hands down, the best defensive spell in a Mage’s arsenal. Yes, Ice Block is godly, but how often do you use Blink compared to Ice Block?
 4)	**Supportive Spells (aka Support/Utility)** – There’s obviously no debate here given that Fire has none and Frost has only 1 (Ice Armour) whereas Arcane has an absolutely bucket load of them (and seriously good ones!!). By the way, you forgot Counterspell, Mana Gem, Detect Magic, Slow Fall, Remove Lesser Curse, Mage Armour, Arcane Intellect/Brilliance, Presence of Mind, Teleport & Portal. That’s a total of 16?

So, give Frost/Fire the ‘best AoE’/’best single target’ labels if you wish. I personally think the gap in certain areas like AoE/Defensive spells isn’t as vast as portrayed. But put that aside for a moment. For me, Arcane gets the ‘Support’/’Utility’ label, hands down; absolutely no debate on this one. And you know what? I think that’s great. It completely distinguishes it from the other two trees. Where it lacks in raw DPS or control, it makes up for it, elsewhere. This is in keeping with the classic RPG nature, where things have strengths and weaknesses. Furthermore, these utility spells are incredibly impactful, and I’d argue, they contribute to the existing overall Mage identify more so than the Fire or Frost schools do. As per my other post, if we wish to encourage more ‘pure’ Arcane mages, I strongly suggest it is done via talent tree changes and to concentrate on improving the support/utility that Arcane brings.

So, to address some other points you made:
 1)	**Single target safety** – First of all, I think it’s a good thing that they don’t have similar range enhancing talents. I think this would be bland game design. As per my last paragraph, why should we remove this weakness? Arcane’s strengths are mainly in the Support/Utility aspects.
 2)	**AoE safety** – From my experience in raiding, Arcane Explosion is nicely designed; it’s high risk-high reward. With the leeway mechanics, it is such a reliable way to deliver damage. At the same time, it is up to the Mage to manage their threat/positioning to get away with as much as they can. By the way, I don’t consider this an Arcane problem because it should be used readily whether you are a Frost or Fire mage.
 3)	**PvP viability** – I assume we’re talking about the viability of PvPing with just Arcane damage (Missiles/Explosion). The five second channel just screams ‘lock me out!’ and the Arcane Explosion is very costly & dangerous! So, obviously, this ‘pure’ Arcane mage style struggles in this area of the game. However, I just don’t see why it needs to be a possibility. For any given mage, regardless of spec, they freely dip into the other schools of magic and I think that’s absolutely fine.

When you list the damaging spells across the three schools, Arcane obviously has less going for it:
 **Arcane(2)** – Arcane Missiles & Arcane Explosion
 **Frost(3)** – Frostbolt, Cone of Cold & Blizzard (not counting Frost nova as it’s use is 99.9% for CC)
 **Fire(5)** – Fireball, Fire Blast, Scorch, Blast Wave & Flamestrike

Primarily for PvP, I understand that you feel more like an Arcane mage if you are predominantly using Arcane spells or securing kills with them. Even with the ridiculous amount of support/utility that Arcane brings, I can respect this position. So, how about this for a proposition; what if, instead of adding a whole new spell, we increased the usability of Arcane Missiles? By that I mean, the ease of which it is used, rather than the raw damage output, for example. Nett mentioned a Retail talent, Arcane Bombardment. What if we added a fairly deep talent (2nd/3rd last row?) to the Arcane tree which read something like this;

 **Arcane Bombardment (3 points)** –
 **Roleplay** – *The Mage takes a moment to focus their mind, readying themselves to unleash a devastating bombardment of Arcane energy*.
 **Mechanics** – For 1/2/3 seconds that the Mage is not casting, the cast time of their next Arcane Missiles is reduced by 0.8/1.6/2.5 seconds.

So, for PvP, I think this would be wonderful for ‘pure’ Arcane mages. There are plenty of moments lasting at least a few seconds which would empower your next set of Arcane Missiles and it would provide great burst damage! For raiding, again, I think there are quite a few moments on boss fights (and especially trash) which force you to stop casting whilst repositioning. This change might make Arcane the strong spec in movement based scenarios…
So, what do you think? Is this idea a good enough compromise to avoid having to add an additional Arcane damage spell?

 **NB** –  even with a change like this, I would still like a whole bunch of improvements to the Arcane tree focusing on the support/utility aspects.
