# Urgent Need for Enhanced Account Security

## Post 85393 by Chigurh — 2024-01-20T02:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85393#p85393 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle WoW Team,

As a dedicated player who has invested considerable time and money into TWoW, I'm writing to express my deep concern regarding the current state of account security. It's disconcerting to realize that our accounts, which hold significant value and personal investment, are virtually unprotected.

Currently, the password system is alarmingly basic, with passwords stored in plaintext. This alone is a glaring security risk, as plaintext passwords can be easily compromised. Moreover, the absence of Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is a critical vulnerability. 2FA is a standard security measure in online platforms, significantly reducing the risk of unauthorized access.

Furthermore, the lack of email confirmations for password changes and logins from new IPs is unsettling. These are essential alerts that help users monitor and control access to their accounts.

Given the current state, I strongly suggest the following security enhancements:

- 1. **Implement Hashed Passwords:** Storing passwords in plaintext is outdated and insecure. Hashing passwords is a fundamental security measure.

 2. **Introduce Two-Factor Authentication**: 2FA adds an extra layer of security and is crucial for protecting user accounts.

 3. **Email Alerts for Password Changes**: Sending a confirmation email for password changes helps users stay informed and act quickly in case of unauthorized changes.

 4. **Login Notifications for New IPs**: Alerting users via email when their account is accessed from a new IP address can prevent unauthorized access.
These improvements are not just beneficial for users; they also enhance the reputation of TWoW as a secure and player-focused platform. I hope these suggestions are taken seriously, as the security of our accounts should be a top priority.

Thank you for your attention to this critical matter.

Best regards,
Chigurh

Thank you for reaching out and expressing your concerns regarding the security of Turtle WoW accounts.

Firstly, I want to clarify that we do not store passwords in plaintext. This means that even if our database were compromised, passwords would not be easily decipherable. However, I understand that hashed passwords alone may not provide sufficient security.

I'm pleased to inform you that we are in the process of rolling out Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) as part of our new website backend. This will add an extra layer of security to your accounts, significantly reducing the risk of unauthorized access. The new website backend is already in use for our SEA realms and 2FA is working without any issues. Currently, 30% of the SEA player base has 2FA enabled.

Furthermore, we are also working on implementing optional email confirmations for password changes. However, that won't be the case for IP changes because the majority of our player base has dynamic IPs.

By the way, all of our database backups are encrypted and stored on multiple locations around the world. There won't be any data leak if one of our backup machines is compromised.

Your suggestions align closely with our ongoing efforts to enhance account security and I appreciate your proactive approach in bringing them to our attention.
