# New Classes

## Post 53173 by Mrkrissatan (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-12T12:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53173#p53173 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd like to propose two new classes for turtle wow.

Spellbreaker for the Alliance
Blademaster for the Horde

 **A few ideas for the Spell Breaker:**

Vanguard
 1h + shield tank spec

Siege Breaker
 1h dual wield dps spec

Mana Warden
Focuses on short duration buffs and debuffs

Spells & Abilities

Protector
You designate one ally to protect, reducing all damage they take by 50% as long as they remain within 40 yards of you. Whenever an enemy dares to attack your protected ally you gain X amount of mana cannot occur more than once every Y seconds.

Devastator
A powerful melee attack that reduces your target's armour by x and deals y damage over 15 seconds. Hit the target with both weapons or if a shield is equipped taunt the target to attack you.

Swords Dance
An impressive display of defence which reduces incoming damage by 75% for both you and all group members within 40 yards of you for 10 seconds.

Polished Metal
The magic infusing your armour springs to life, further increasing your armour by X and restoring Y health over 20 seconds.

Obliterate Magic
You Horrify the target and drain 20%/40%/60%/80%/100% of the target's mana, restoring it to yourself. 10 minute cooldown

Warcry
A loud shout that startles your target and all enemies within 25 yards of them, reducing their damage to 50% for 10 seconds and taunting them to attack you.

Fear Nothing
Knockback everyone within 5 yards of you for X damage. Monsters will be knocked down.

Sun Gate
You open a gate to the Sunwell. 2 minute cooldown

Visage
You create a copy of yourself for 10% of your mana, it will cast all the same spells and abilities as you until your mana is depleted then it fades. Drains 1% of maximum mana every second

Second Line
Heal the target of your target for 20%/40%/60%/80%/100% of the damage you deal. Not usable while you have a shield equipped.

Shield Wall
For the next 10 seconds, your chance to block is almost guaranteed.

Assault
You repeatedly attack all enemies in a line in front of you, inflicting X damage every second for 10 seconds, up to 10 yards away, for as long as you maintain channelling this spell.

Mana Barrier (passive)
When your health reaches 15% you create a mana barrier around yourself for 40% of your maximum health.

City Watch
Up to 4 enemies within 15 yards are rooted making them unable to move for 5 seconds

Accelerated Magic (Aura)
You and all party members within 40 yards deal X increased magical damage but receive Y increased magical damage received

Way of the Sword (Aura)
You and all party members within 40 yards deal X increased physical damage but receive Y increased physical damage received

Stalwart (Aura)
 40% of all non critical damage received by party members is transferred to you, this damage is staggered

Challenge
Both you and your target are held tightly in place for 10 seconds, and neither one of you can move. This effect cannot be dispelled or broken.

 **A few ideas for the Blademaster:**

Plan of battle
You gain a buff depending on which stance you are currently using

Battle Abilities have a probability of applying Cutting Winds to the target, dealing Bleed damage over X sec.

Fury:  Every third hit is a guaranteed critical strike

Defensive: Summon 2 copies of you that will attack your opponents. You take 10% of the damage they receive. After 30 sec. the copies will vanish.

Wind Walk
Allows you to vanish from sight, entering stealth for 10 sec and increasing movement speed by 100%

Mak'Gora
You enrage your opponent, interrupting any current abilities/spells and taunting monsters to attack you. For 15 seconds you and the opponent are locked in a duel and nobody else may interfere.

Ignite Weapon
Cover your blade in flames for a short duration, inflicting additional Fire damage on each melee attack. Last X sec.

Keen Edge
Charge through an enemy, stunning them for 2 sec. When the stun fades, the target suffers Physical damage for being slashed.

Decisive Strike
Inflicts Physical damage to all enemies in a cone in front of the caster.

Cutting Edge
Increases your Attack speed by X% and your Movement speed by Y% for Z sec.

Lo'Gosh (buff)
You are unable to wear a chest piece but instead gain 10% stamina, 10% Strength and 10% Critical strike rating
