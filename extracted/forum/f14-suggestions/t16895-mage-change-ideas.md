## Post 115490 by Bahamutxd (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-05T13:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115490#p115490 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Mana Shield is useless even if talented but being able to cast it on others is not the solution. Also - that wouldn't work well with Arcane Mage 2 set bonus from Tier 3, when shield breaks it will deal 20% of the damage absorbed in AoE (useless for mages but good if cast on tanks)

Mana Shield biggest issue is how costly is for the mage to use it. At maximun rank/level - it will draing around 900 mana just to absorb 500 physical damage on a class with barely 1000 armor - pretty much any weak hit will take that shield out.

It's a mix between how little damage the spell can absorb and the amount of mana it will drain from use. It doesn't help that with CC2 the bonus armor talent from Intelect got removed.

Theres other spells I'd worry about too other than Mana Shield

· Blizzard which is an iconic core Mage/game spell is pretty much dead right now. 8 seconds channel, 1300-1400 mana with barely any scaling from gear, making it worse and worse the more advanced the server/you are. The utility nerf made a bad AoE even worse. Even fully talented - mobs will escape the area before the cast is done so it's very hard to justify 6 talent points for such a low damage/utiliy/mana costly spell.

· Flamestrike is almost useless, along with Blizzard, thanks to the last tick bug but also because they lack either damage or utility versus untalented Arcane Explosion that has a much easier time managing mana/aggro and just doing more damage. It's also a very high mana cost spell with very little Area that is almost impossible to aim in most situations  for targets to take the full damage. Even if talented, the damage is superior on Arcane Explosion.

· Blastwave - for a spell with 45 seconds CD that is only avaible from a talent, its damage is too low (similar to talented Arcane Explossion, but this one doesn't have any CD and is able to proc so many Arcane talents/procs potentially doing more damage). It also have the downside/inconvenience of eating all combustion stacks.

· Dampen/Amplify magic are useless with the massive nerf they got on CC2, simply removing the GCD or allowing them to be cast on 63 mobs would do a lot for them.

· Presence of Mind is rarely used with anything other than Arcane Rupture or an instant sheep in PvE. Arcane + Fire/Frost builds are pretty much dead as cap stone talents in Fire/Frost trees are a better option (even combustion which is terribly bad) for a non Arcane Mage than taking Arcane Power. There is not enough reason to spec that far down to Presence of Mind for Fire/Frost mages anymore. Out of Arcane mana talents - mixing trees after CC2 is no more a thing.

· Pyroblast/Hot Streak are not worth 4 talent points even after the massive buff Hot Streak got (it got 2 major buffs after CC2 - this just shows how terribly bad this talent launched), mostly because of Fire mana issues (that forces you to spend 13 points in Arcane +3 in Frost) making you skip those for better options such as Blastwave or Improved Fireblast/Flamethrowing. Pyroblast is a slow casted Fireball with a big DoT - if that DoT is dispelled, removed, target dies before it deals its full damage, the spell is useless.

Additionally some talents could use rework.

· Winters Chill 5 points is way too much. The talent needs additional effects or just a cut to the total talents needed (to 3 points 33/66/100%). A single mage with Flash Freeze will stack Winters Chill to 5 and keep it up without any issue by just spending 3 points in it thanks to Flash Freeze.

· Improved Blizzard talent is simply not worth the 3 points for how weak it's effect is. It either needs an additional or different effect to compensate the talent points it cost + the huge nerf on utility the spell got making it completely useless on the most mana expensive spell in the game.

· Flash Freeze needs to work out of Freeze Inmune monsters (why is Ragnaros not inmune to Freeze anyway?) - Right now it's something that it mostly only works on very few mobs around the world and bosses - the inmunity to Freeze is not consistent among monsters either so you never know what to expect out of this talent.

· Shatter talent is too much of an investment for the gains. 35% chance to crit once every 21 seconds or at 15% chance if you spent 3 points in Frostbite. A total of 7 talent points (+3 from Frostbite) just for that. Contrary to Flash Freeze - this will work on 99% of monsters BUT only if you are solo playing as Freeze effect will break almost instantly if there's someone else hitting your target.

· Wand Specialization mana restored is just not good enough. At max level/rank it averages 90 mana at a 10% chance. Thats around 5-6 mana a second on a 1.5s speed wand. There is never a real reason to wand as a Mage so this extra effect won't do it either.

· Improved Mana Shield (See top of the comment)

· Magic Attunement restriction to <63 monsters kills the use of this spell. With damage amplifiers/reductions of 2-4%, any encounter that doesn't last for long (pretty much all pvp, world monsters, dungeons...) are not worth spending a GCD to cast this spell.

· Hot Streak - (See above) Despite the 2 major buffs it got post CC2, Mages are still skipping it mostly due to Pyroblast not being an upgrade over Fireball out of its DoT and how taxing Fire talent tree is on talent points forcing you to go 13 on Arcane and 3 on Frost.

· Combustion as a 31 talent pointer is just underwhelming. Easily the worst 31 pointer in the game right now. Doesn't add anything to the table for a Fire mage.
