# [Paladin] Exorcism/Improved Seal of Justice talent

## Post 57862 by Saronsen — 2023-08-18T09:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57862#p57862 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello. Please get your booing out of the way, it's another paladin suggestion.

Straight to the point.

I've put a fair bit of gameplay at 60 as prot and ret now, and I can safely say that there is no use for seal of justice. The stun is infrequent and, of course, random. With 3/3 talented, its still less than a 20% chance to proc, and it has diminishing returns for any following stuns. Dungeon bosses generally cannot be stunned, raid bosses not at all, and besides a short duration stun, the seal itself offers minimal to no use in normal gameplay, or even niche gameplay. This is made worse with paladins requiring a 3 talent investment into a seal they will generally not be using to tank as it offers no threat gain at all, and cannot stun bosses, and requires swapping your seal out, judging, and swapping back to your regular seal after getting your taunt out. I'd like to suggest something else.

Exorcism. Allow it to be cast on ANY non-player enemy. Perhaps have it deal additional damage/auto-crit against undead/demons to keep its niche, but that's just a suggestion.
Replace Improved Seal of Justice with a (working named) Improved Exorcism talent.
 1/3 - Reduces the cooldown of exorcism by 1 seconds, and decreases its mana cost by 5%
 2/3 - Reduces the cooldown of exorcism by 3 seconds, and decreases its mana cost by 10%
 3/3 - Reduces the cooldown of exorcism by 5 seconds. and decreases its mana cost by 15%. When Righteous Fury is active, Exorcism taunts your target, forcing them to attack you for 2 seconds.

This offers a few things.
First, Spellpower paladins now have a new tool to use for single target damage in PVE, without bringing another ranged attack to PVP to run alongside holy shock.
Second, it allows Holy Strike to be adjusted for both PVE and PVP without seriously negatively impacting protection paladins ability to generate and hold threat.
Third, it allows protection paladins to pull from range like druids and warriors without having to farm up various world drop alternatives, like candles.
Fourth, 3 talent points will not go into a seal that is, unfortunately, never used, as it has almost no use case. Currently it feels like you're throwing 3 talent points to the void to only receive a taunt in return.

Suggestions are welcome, rants about how you don't like paladins aren't, we already know how you feel.

While the current taunt of Paladin is clunky to say the least, i do not like the suggestion proposed here.

What makes paladin taunt clunky is it sharing cooldown with something you normally use in your threat rotation. By shifting it to exorcism, you are not removing this problem. As if we get the choice to cast exorcism, we do so on cooldown. It also at the same time increases the manacost of the taunt and it increases the cooldown potentially by 2 seconds (since improved judgement will have 8 seconds while improved exorcism has 10)

So it doesnt mechanically really solve the problems the current taunt has.

Tying the taunt to righteous fury also puts an unnecessary extra prerequisite onto the taunt spell where either you can't use exorcism for damage when offtanking on bosses or you have to toggle the massively expensive righteous fury.

But more importantly, its a massive flavor fail. Exorcism while being a situational spell is one of the more flavorful in vanilla. Just turning it into a baseline boring nuke hurts the classes identity. If the upsides gained from this would be worth it, i could live with sacrificing some flavor on exorcism, but as its not really a net benefit except for dps (which paladin does in its current form not really need). And it heavily infringes on holy shocks identity as a broadly useable reasonably hard hitting ranged holy attack.
