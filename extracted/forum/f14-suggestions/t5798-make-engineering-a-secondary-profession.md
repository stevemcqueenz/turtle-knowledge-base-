# Make Engineering A Secondary Profession

## Post 36453 by Forumdweller (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-01T05:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36453#p36453 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hear me out. First of all Engineering will be divided into 3 categories. The baseline is a secondary profession that contains all the standard schematics and recipes such as tubes, guns, bullets, grenades, target dummies etc. HOWEVER, Goblin and Gnomish will become primary professions, but keep it so you can still only have 1 at a time. With Gnomish or Goblin the gadgets become more effective (attain their normal utility) i.e iron grenades stun for 2 seconds instead of just doing damage.

The point of this to make it more appealing to take up a second crafting profession whilst leveling in particular, and still being able to level engineering a bit without getting all the benefits of it. It conflicts with the Hardcore challenge as well, because I want to level engineering but then I can't craft my own gear... It doesn't feel good not being able to explore the update professions because you know you'll just have to abandon the profession at 60 and all the recipes with it.
