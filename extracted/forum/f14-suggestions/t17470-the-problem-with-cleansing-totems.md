# The problem with cleansing totems

## Post 119264 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-07T17:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119264#p119264 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Before attacking the idea because I suggest a shaman nerf: my goal is fair gameplay and balance. I'm totally OK with buffs where needed (f.ex. amount of mana from water shield) but this one gotta be nerfed, I think.

Cleansing totems.
There are two: poison and disease. You summon them and they remove a poison/disease effect from each party member when summoned and every 5 sec for 2 minutes.

[https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=8 ... sing-totem](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=8166/poison-cleansing-totem)

Shaman GCD is 1,5 seconds.

In theory you put them down when needed and they remove 5*1 effect every 5 seconds. Strong but fair. But this is not how they are played.
They are either completely irrelevant or spammed at GCD at places like Kara10 spider.

What they actually do is: You stop casting anything else. Remove 5*1 effect every 1,5 sec. One cleanse every 0,3 sec, not bad.
So the problem is that when these totems are relevant, they trivialize the encounter because of how effective they are. And they are this effective because every time you spam them on GCD, the effect goes off.

So, my proposal:
Keep the totems as they are, except one thing. They won't remove poison/disease when summoned, only in 5 sec and then, in every 5 sec. No more spamming/abusing the totem.

The totems would still be really strong but fair. They do their job and you also have the option to cleanse manually if you want to spend your GCD that way.

tldr: Group.cleansing as a shaman currently requires no braincell. The two totems are way too good at what they do because their effect goes off every time you summon them. GCD spam abuses these totems.

I always thought about this in TWOW like this:
As a shaman, you can spam 4 totems at once (1 from each element) with some limitations (some of the totems have internal CD). In exchange you provide buffs which can be 'purged' by anyone -> Something for something.
The most problematic PVP totem would probably be grounding but it cleverly has an internal CD. In my opinion, if any totem causes a big problem in PVP, we should give it some seconds of internal CD to prevent it being spammed, instead of bringing back those GCDs.
I see Atreidon's point -> buffs worth a GCD should have one. But totems are area-based buffs with a small radius and parties move all the time. You were expected to use 2-3 totems all the time and with flametongue, it's 3-4 now (it's just a plain DPS upgrade for the melee to use it). Imagine a gameplay where you don't really play the game because you start the first 4,5-6 sec of every pull by setting up buffs instead of playing. We could say that these buffs are optional and you have the option to play the game and be more frugal with totems but in reality it leads to angry melee and guild drama. As any other class you put up the buff during downtime and play the game. Old pala buff system was a pain but it still allowed you to put up buffs in downtime then play the game. Shaman GCD version requires you to spend a bunch of time buffing INSTEAD of playing the game.
So yes, if any totem becomes too OP in PVP, I'm ok with an internal CD on it. But bringing back GCD would just make shaman gameplay a pain.
