# Warlock Pet Tanking

## Post 84601 by Powrhouser — 2024-01-13T23:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84601#p84601 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

An idea for a warlock talent that could possibly make demons more viable for DPS/tanking; Soul Pact. Transfer a percentage of your warlock's stats and threat to your demon while active. This could not only make demons more effective and consistent additions to warlock DPS, but give them viability as true tanks, the drawback being that your warlock becomes extremely weak and fragile while your demon is empowered (as to make sure that such a build wouldn't be OP in PvP, or at the least possibly leading to some really fun niche builds). As a somewhat related side note, there is the odd cloth int gear with strength on it for some reason, and if your VW could make use of it, we could see interesting meme builds on par with arcane spell power hunter in implementation.
