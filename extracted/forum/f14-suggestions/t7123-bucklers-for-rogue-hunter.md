# Bucklers for rogue & hunter

## Post 45334 by Mrbadwithnames1 — 2023-05-10T13:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45334#p45334 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I heard that rogues and hunters were meant to be able to use smaller, weaker shields called bucklers - hence why many shields are still called bucklers.

My suggestion is the following:
Make every shield called a buckler into a bucker item type equitable by rogues and hunters as well as the usual shield classes so as not to rob them of existing gear.
Bucklers could be used from the start or learned after level 40.
