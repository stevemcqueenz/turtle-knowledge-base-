**Alterac Valley Changes Part 2**
---

This thread was created by a person who is Alterac Valley enthusiast and who spent dozens or even hundreds of hours playing AV in Turtle WoW without getting bored. I wrote the first part of AV change that concerns Honor system revamp. You can read it here if you haven't yet [viewtopic.php?t=13520](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13520). I'm going to share my deep feedback in this thread presenting all most important issues of this battleground I have noticed during my playing here.

Why do I even share feedback of this battleground in one of the private servers forum? Because as far as I know Turtle WoW is the only WoW server in the WORLD that runs pre-nerfed version of Alterac Valley from old vanilla patch 1.5. Even though this server is RP-PVE focused, it attracts some PvP fans of legendary pre-nerfed AV.
It has some problems though. It's the only server in the world that runs 1.5 version of AV so naturally this version of battleground is not fully tested, it has some flaws that haven't been reported yet. That's why I made this post: to gather all bugs and *unintented mechanics* in one place so Turtle devs hopefuly can read them and deliver the best possible iteration of AV to all WoW fans. Before you read my reports and suggestions you should understand why pre-nerfed AV is so important for us. You should understand how Blizzard changed this battleground in late Vanilla (for worse) and how deeply destroyed it in TBC and WotLK. There was very interesting post about this on Blizzard forum, it's wall of text from one of enthusiasts that also loved old Alterac Valley. It's long text but it's worth reading if you don't know history of AV much. Here it is (it loads slowly though because it's archived): [https://web.archive.org/web/20150112050 ... 2952885503](https://web.archive.org/web/20150112050648/http://us.battle.net/wow/en/forum/topic/2952885503).

I've listed AV problems and bugs from the most important to the least. Leave a comment if you know any other AV problem/bug I haven't mentioned. Enjoy!

 *1. Honor System of Alterac Valley.*

I made separate thread about it [viewtopic.php?t=13520](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13520) because it's the core issue of current AV. When Turtle devs changed 1.12 version of AV to 1.5 they forgot about changing Honor System in this map too. We all know how Honor meta looked like in 1.12 version of AV - you play this BG effectively only if you zerg to enemy base as fast as possible avoiding any PvP action. This is the aberration of what AV meta should be. If you play AV longer than 1 hour, you play turtle-AV, you play inefficiently Honor-wise.  This system is obviously contrary to the design of AV - some objectives require much time for example to summon Elemental Lord by turning in 200 items.

Conclusion is that current Honor System doesn't reward players for playing AV how it was supposed to be played - hours-long or even days-long games should be a NORM because this is what people loved pre-nerfed AV for. Honor System should be adapted to the original design of the Alterac Valley so people are rewarded by approximately the same amount Honor per hour as in other BGs like Arathi Basin. To show you how miserable Honor gained in AV is I will give you example:
 3 hours of playing one turtle-AV rewards players approximately the same amount of Honor as by playing 30 minutes of Arathi Basin. This is because once you burn all the towers and kill all Commanders in AV, there is no more source of Bonus Honor in the entire battleground. Players are doomed to gain Honor only through HKs which of course give miserable amount.

The solution I suggested is to, first of all, remove or limit the impact of "Diminishing returns" mechanic that decreases your Honor gained by killing the same person each time that day - this is the root reason why turtle-AV sucks for people interested in farming PvP rank. Bonus Honor gained for doing one-time objectives like burning the towers and killing Commanders while also giving every player inside AV 1x Mark of Honor and 1000 Honor (we can discuss other values here) every 30 minutes after 1 hour of the game time. Or to reward the team for doing AV objectives for example everyone gets 500 Honor for summoning Elemental Lord. Devs have many ways to fix this problem.

 *2. Armor Scraps are way too easy to farm and have too much impact to the course of battleground compared to effort made.*

I will tell you what is wrong straight: one person can upgrade team's Units to Champion ones in 30 minutes. Yes, I tried it. Champion Units deal about 1000 Physical damage per swing. Do I propose Champion Units nerf? Not at all.
The problem is it's too easy to farm Armor Scraps. After the game starts, you just go to the closest Mine and farm Armor Scraps from Troggs/Kobolds. The weirdest thing is that if you capture the Mine for your own team, you actually make mistake because these NPCs will be killed by spawned guardians nearby so you won't be able to farm extra Armor Scraps for your team.

The best solution I have thought about is to completely remove Armor Scraps drop from Troggs/Kobolds. Armor Scraps should only drop from Stormpike/Frostwolf NPCs because they are generally harder to kill and not so easily accessible as Troggs and Kobolds - the closest ones are in the middle of the map. Upgrading to Champion Units shouldn't be possible in just 30 minutes. This change should delay upgrading a bit.

But delay is not all. If devs are able to script it properly, the units could go down by one level after a certain amount of time. Currently if your team upgrades units to Champion, it's gg - these units will spawn there permanently until the game ends. So for example: your team upgraded units to Champion. 15 minutes pass and the Champion units are reverted to Veteran units - that means your team must keep farming Armor Scraps to have Champion units in base. It would incentivize farming Armor Scraps even after Champion units are in the game.

 *3. [FIXED] Battlegrounds Broken Queue.*

There have been a few reports of the same bug. Disconnect has reported this issue here <https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=6546> and welpenhund here <https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=3239>.

Empirically, because of this bug, Horde must suffer in AV because there are more Alliance players on the server: ratio is 35% to 65% or something like that. How does it impact Alterac Valley? Queue somehow allows a few players join at once.
I will explain it by giving simple example I observed. There was AV game where 20 people from Horde were playing against 23 people for a few minutes. In that time there were 0 players in Horde's queue and let's say 5 players in Alliance's queue. Suddenly, one guy from Horde joins the AV so in theory one guy from Alliance could join too. But the queue is bugged so it allows 3 players from Alliance queue join at once. The result is that we have 21 H vs 26 A now. It's a big disadvantage for the Horde to always have a few heads less.

The queue shouldn't work like this, it should allow to join one player at a time so it's always max 2 players difference.

 *4. Wing Commanders not working as intended.*

Wing Commanders are special NPCs you can find across the Alterac Valley. They are supposed to accept multiple quest items from the players - and after they have enough of these, players can send them to the battle. They are one of the few NPCs in the game that can attack from the air - that makes them unique, especially it adds flavour to the Alterac Valley.

The problem is...

...Wing Commanders were barely ever seen in action on Turtle WoW. As far as my experience is concerned, I spent hundreds of hours on this server's AV and I have seen them only once. That's not because they are bugged (at least I hope so). The problem is number of turn ins needed to send them to the air that is detached from reality. Here are numbers which are likely valid for Turtle WoW's version of AV since there wasn't any mention about changes made to Wing Commanders in Torta's post 2 years ago:

- 120 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Soldier's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17326) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Soldier's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17502)

- 60 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Lieutenant's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17327) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Lieutenant's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17503)

- 30 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Commander's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17328) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Commander's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17504)
In reality, even in long turtle-games these Wing Commanders never serve their purpose. The objective is never fulfilled because the game is too short. Moreover, even if people loot these items, they usually don't turn them in because the reward for doing so is only reputation (likely rankers are Exalted already).

To make things worse, these items drop practically only from other players (technically you can kill one civilian in enemy base but nobody really does it).
The item that players drop depends on their actual PvP rank. I don't know the details but noobs drop one of these 120 and people with high rank drop the one of 30.

Suggestion:

- Decrease these numbers by half:

 - 120 -> 60 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Soldier's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17326) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Soldier's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17502)

 - 60 -> 30 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Lieutenant's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17327) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Lieutenant's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17503)

 - 30 -> 15 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Commander's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17328) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Commander's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17504)

- Add slight gold reward to the repeatable quest to incentivize doing this objective. 5 silver for the most common item, 10 silver for the mid one and 15 silver for the rarest one should be fair.

- Make all of these items tradeable within Alterac Valley. I remember devs changed Armor Scraps, Stormpike Soldier's Blood and Storm Crystal to be tradeable one year ago and the effect was brilliant - PvE objectives became more common to see. But somehow this change didn't affect these quest items. Probably an oversight.

 *5. Respawn time of NPCs is not adjusted to number of players.*

Champion Units hit for 1k per swing. Imagine both teams have Champion troops and there is 20 vs 20 game. Respawn time of these Champion NPCs is the same as for 40 vs 40 game. Empirically, I can say that winning such 20 vs 20 game is nearly impossible when Champion NPCs are in. Not because they are so hard but because all NPCs respawn relatively fast. I didn't count how long does it take to respawn one NPC in AV but as I said from my experience, they respawn too fast in smaller games. To the point when 2 defenders can easily defend against 10 attackers with support of Champion Units.

Note this is the issue only in smaller games, in 40 vs 40 it's fine. Suggestion is to adjust NPCs respawn time to the number of players in AV.

 *6. Items needed for repeatable quests drop to random person nearby.*

Loot system was changed to FFA at some point but shortly after was removed due to Korrak's loot distribution problem. This change is still needed. Currently it's very annoying when a NPC dies and you can't loot the items for repeatable quests. Instead these items drop for random person nearby and we all know not everyone is interested in gathering them. This leads to a problem where these resources are simply wasted instead of being used for summoning Elemental or upgrading Units.

FFA loot in Alterac Valley is needed. At least for quest items.

It doesn't make sense because near every other objective (apart from base) all NPCs despawn after objective is destroyed for example when Tower is burnt all Archers automatically despawn. Recently, I have reported this bug <https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=7282> which is still in process. One time I observed one Hunter was kiting Marshals to Stonehearth Graveyard.  *12. Vanndar and Drek'thar are too easy to kill.*

It's year 2005 no more. People know how to play well and pump big damage. Vanndar and Drek'thar have only 162k HP. I saw 10 man group killing Drek'thar in a minute. When 20 people go in, there is no challenge. Just a race against time.

Boosting their health pool to 200-300k is obvious solution. But I would like to see more *random* mechanics added to them so they target also other players nearby. I don't know what abilities Drek'thar has but I can say Vanndar is just auto attacking main tank. Even with good healers inside, I could main tank him as a Priest. I swear Balinda is harder boss than Vanndar.

 *13. Orgrimmar and Ironforge are favoured factions of Alterac Valley conflict.*

Orgrimmar and Ironforge factions are privileged compared to other playable factions because you can reach Exalted reputation with them only by playing AV. All reputation from repeatable quests (apart from hunting specific Races for trophies) goes only to these two factions. Since all of Horde and Alliance are involved in this conflict, it would make more sense to split reputation gained from these repeatable quests to all factions of Alliance or Horde.

So the quest would give you 2 reputation points with each faction instead of 10 with only Orgrimmar/Ironforge.

I'm fine with them existing, but they should not all be spawned at the start and instead spawn at random times and in more varied locations (to avoid any camping) for the duration of the BG.  *19. Horde's archers in Frostwolf Village stay in worse location than archers of Alliance in Dun Baldar.*

It's game-balance issue - I think everyone who plays AV in both factions can agree. Horde's archers in West and East Frostwolf Tower are in worse position than Alliance's ones. Main problem is that archers on Horde's towers are located on height while the area they should control is far below them not in their field of view. In comparison Dun Baldar North and South Bunkers are located far away from the bridge and on middle height - that makes them set in excellent position that allows them to control whole way from the bridge to Aid Station. Archers on Frostwolf towers control only short choke-point in that building in front of the first gate - they seem to shine only there but nowhere else.

The solution to this problem is quite easy, just relocate the archers so they stay on the edge of Frostwolf Towers. This would allow them to have wider field of attack making them as efficient as Alliance's archers. Very easy to fix.
