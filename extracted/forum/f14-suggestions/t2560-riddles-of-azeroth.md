Design Philosophy: The thing that I loved most in World of Warcraft was Player Interaction, Roleplaying and going on an adventure with my friends to explore the unknown. Patch 1.16 inspired me to come out with some new ideas I had along the way while playing World of Warcraft with you. So this ideas should lead to an increase of Player Interaction, Roleplaying and as Patch 1.16 add more mysteries to Turtle WOW. Focusing also on the experience of the “missing” parts in World of Warcraft classic like: Craftable items for all character slots like: Rings, Amulets and Bows as well as a lack of Hunter pet special abilities. The Reputation System should also be giving an update making more of the minor factions and adding more ways to gain reputation instead of tedious grinds. Last but not least Riddles for each zone should be in the game leading to discussion within the player base and also exploration in the zones.

 **„Feature Overview“:**

 **Riddles**: This new feature allows you to explore hidden secrets in every zone either alone or with your friends to get rewards (Reputation, Crafting Recipes, Gold, Items and Titles). Hunt for treasure or solve mysteries. In WOW classic there are 40 Zones + 6 Cities. Each Area should be given at least one Riddle before this feature is released.

 **Professions:**

Jewelcrafting: New Primary profession. Now Rings, Amulets, Trinkets, Reputation Items and magic stones can be crafted. Goes well with mining.

Woodworking: New Primary profession. Now Bows, Staves, Shields, Polearm, Arrows and “Wood” can be crafted.

Lumberjack: New Primary profession. Allows to collect wood from different nodes all around the world.

Overhaul of the Gardening and Survival Profession – New recipes and mechanics to give them more flavor

 **Class Changes:**

New Hunter pet Abilities: Every hunter pet has now a unique ability to make them more viable.

 **Reputation faction overhaul** Each non-core Horde or Alliance faction, which is not involved in raiding gets a counterpart.

 **TRP Feature – Last Name:** You can add one account wide Last name for Alliance and one for Horde to your account in game. This is can be achieved in game via a Questline starting level 25.

 **Darker Night mod integration or preinstalled in the client** – This Mod can be changes of and on in the Interface. Lowers the bar for new players to play with this feature.

 **Feature Details:**

 **Riddles**
Design-Philosophy:

Riddles are basically that Riddles. These can take a lot of different forms. Treasure finding, cure a curse from a citizen, find lost children etc. and sometimes could also include a small jumping puzzle.

These are “Quests” that won’t appear for a long time in your Questlog. You collect different hints through the zone, via items, talking to NPC’s and combing the pieces together either alone or with your friends.

Every Riddle has a specific theme = story line. For example, if the riddle is in the Eastern Plaguelands it has something to do with undead, scourge etc. and won’t involve something with Nightelfs. This is a help for the players, so they don’t need to search at the other end of the world for more hints.

Riddles can have different outcomes: Fight a summoned elite Miniboss, open a hidden, locked treasure chest with a Key, find lost belongings of NPC’s or just talk to NPC’s

The first riddle you can solve is the one in the capital cities. These are the only ones that appear as a Quest in your Questlog (Starts at level 5.)! Why that? To make new players aware of this feature and nicely integrate it to the already existing framework of World of Warcraft. After solving this riddle, you get a follow up Quest leading you to the Beginning of the Starter Zone Riddle. For example, Stormwind leads you to the Elwynn Forest Riddle. After this there are no more direct hints where the riddle starts for the other zones like Westfall etc. you have to discover it on your own!

For a start: Each Zone of Turtle Wow should have at least one Riddle.

Examples:
Okok, that was a lot of talk but now see how these riddles are implemented in game. Therefore I give you two example of full fleshed out riddles from Stormwind and Elwynn Forest:

 **Stormwind**

The Stormwind Riddle starts in the Orphans building at the Madam there. She gives you a normal Quest, a friend of her is missing a book and he beliefs one of the Children has stolen it. She asks you for help and her friend promised a nice reward. The Quest objective: Find the lost book and bring it to the mage (Yes it’s very vague)

 1. You have to ask the children in SW for more hints.
 2. Few of the children mentioning a boy named “Jimmy - place holder name”, but where is he?. Ask you around for some hints.
 3. After you find him you have to ask him for more details. You have two options you can pay money to Jimmy, and pay him food/sweets. The answers are different for each option you pick. He says you never will find it. It’s in a place were is covered by other books.
 4. Now you have to think: Stormwind Library, Cathedral and Magetower are Places with a lot of Books.
 5. If you have choosen both options all hints should point out to the Stormwind Library.
 6. There you find the book.
 7. You bring it back to the mage. And find out that it was a powerful spell book just stolen by kids. Here the Quest ends.
 8. You can now either choose from 2 Quest. Don’t tell anyone and get the reward from the mage. Items + gold. Or go to the Mages Boss gain Reputation and a smaller amount of money. You can do only one of the Quests because they are looked via a Quest item you gain After ending the first Quest.
 9. After completing you get a follow up Quest: Going bag to the Orphans lady.
 10. She sends you to and friend in Elwynn Forest. Because many children in Goldshire went missing.

 **Elwynn Forest - Theme:**  The Children of Goldshire are possessed by a restless evil Ghost from the Second War
 1.	You arrive at Goldshire at a Lady. She gives you more information. Children are missing. Find out whats going on. (I think most of you know the pentagram children) Here the formal Quests ends, and your task is to gain hints without a Quest in your log.
 2.	You gain hints in Goldshire town on: What is going on and where.
 3.	You go to the Fisher hut in the Pentagram room. And find a pickable item: It’s a Quest item without starting a Quest. The item quotes: “Seems to have a magical effect. You need someone how can deciver the power of this item”
 4.	You find your answer in Azora tower. After talking to a mage, he says the item is processed with an evil curse and you need this rune to cleanse it at the grave of this vengeful spirit
 5.	Now you need two have both items RUNE and POCESSED ITEM at the grave. With both items you can summon the evil spirit of the fallen soldier.
 6.	You kill him and lot his ash. This Ash now starts a Quests preventing you from getting the rewards multiple times.
 7.	You go back to Goldshire and talk to the Marshal and he tells you that you need to bury the solider and cleanse his soul. You go to the graveyard in Goldshire and do so. After this you get your reward.
 8.	The Children are now free and you are awarded with Reputation any (Maybe a title for your good dead?)
 9.	After this there is now hint for the Riddle in Westfall. Maybe ask you fellow players or stumble at it yourself.

More Ideas:

Thunderbluff:
Theme name: Cliff jumpers. Theme: Cliff jumping is extremely popular within the younger generation of Thunder Bluff. Prevent the youth from getting into dangers and stop them from jumping of the cliff. The leader of the gang dreams of jumping at the elevator location between Barrens and 1000 Needels one day. Solution: You have two ways to solve this: Either give the young Horde NPCs lighter feather for a slower fall or stop the Leader of the gang either by beating him or pursuing him. You get: Reputation for stoping the Sport or you get Money, Items and Materials for giving the young NPCs feathers.

Ironforge:

Theme: A dwarf thinks nobody could ever find his big chest of gold and treasure. How foolish.

Hillsbrad Foothills:

Theme: Infected corn is tracked in Hillbrad Foothills. Who is responsible? Is it even a inside job by a traitor? Figure out what’s going on.

 **Professions:**

Jewelcrafting:
!No Jewels like in TBC can be crafted! but Rings, Amulets and Trinkets as well as some material for new recipes in other profession (interconnection). Main resources coming from “Mining”. This profession takes as bases all recipes from TBC (1-300) and should be rather easy to implement.

Furthermore:
Toys, collectable pets can be crafted as well as some magic stones (working like a weaker form of totems: This can be summoned during combat and have effects like a small heal, or taunt. You need materials from the enchanter for this items and essence of life to craft these items.

New ways to gain reputation: Embassies of each faction love gold and diamonds. The Jewelcrafting profession allows you to craft special presents in different quality for the different factions around the world to gain their trust. These items are tradeable and (time gated) like Mooncloth. So, you can’t boost your reputation from 0-21000 in one day.
For example, there is a special “reputation gift” for:
 -Cenarion Circle + Fulborgs
 -Argent Dawn
 -Thorium Brotherhood and more…

These recipes need a lot of different material each but provide a good boost to the Reputations

I think this makes gaining Reputation from different factions more dynamic and player interactive than just killing 10000 undead, Fulbourgs etc. for the millionth time in our life. This should not be an easy way to cheese reputation but just an alternative pathway! Also it allows for an good alternative than to gain some reputation for the minor factions.

 **Lumberjack:**
Like Mining or Herbalism Lumberjacks collects raw Wood from Nodes using a “Axe” that is proceed by Woodworkers. Wood is also now needs for Survival Profession.
There are 9 different tips of minerals in the game and 33 different herbs. I think a lower number 9+ is more suitable for the profession. Wood Noods have a chance (similar minerals and gems) to spawn twigs that are also used for crafting.

Low level: 1-20 (Ranging Skills 1-100 for Lumberjacks)
 1. Simple Wood: Is collected in the starter Area: 1-10
 2. Soft Wood: Is collected in the starter Area: 10-20

Midrange levels: 21-50 (Ranging Skills 101-250 for Lumberjacks)
 3. Hard Wood: Collected between 20-30 in Ashenvale, Hinterlands, Duskwood, Wetlands, etc.
Combined Wood (only crafted): Combines Hard and Soft wood. Makes it durable and more flexible. (Like Bronze is made out of copper and tin.)
 4. Jungle Wood: Can be found in jungle areas starting STV: 30-45
 5. Desert Wood: Can be found in more desert Areas like Badland, Tanaris and Desolocae: 30-45
 6. High Mountain Wood: Collected in Areas like Hinterlands, Alterac Mountains, 35- 45
 7. Swamp Wood: Collected mainly in Smap of Sorrows, Dustwallow March, 35-45
 8. Washed Wood: This wood is washed at the coastline of the Zones 30-45

Endgame wood: 50+
 9. Magic Wood: Collected in more enchanted Areas, like Winterspring and some Areas of Felwood 55+
 10. Infected Wood: Can be cured and transformed to magic wood
 11. Cursed Wood: Collected in cursed areas, Plaguelands and some Areas of Felwood 45+
 12. Black Wood: Burned near the Blackrock mountains and Ungoro Craters Volcano. Hard and durable. 45-60
Enchanted Wood: Enchanted Wood with the help of enchanting materials and Black Wood. 50+

Woodworking:
You need a Woodworking tool or knife like the smith hammer to work in this profession. Also, you need a work bench like a smith.
Some ground Basics: For creating a Bow you need Bowstring, for the starter bows they can be bought for the craftsman, later they must be crafted from Leather you get from a Leatherworker. Arrows need arrow tips that can be bought from a vendor. Also bought from the vendor: wood oil (different qualities), decorations and ornaments (different qualities) for Staffs and Bows.

 *Apprentice (1-75)*
Uses: Simple Wood, (65+ Soft Wood)
 *Journeyman (75-125)*
Uses: Soft Wood, (110+ Hard Wood)
 *Expert (125-225)*
Uses: Hard Wood, Combined Wood, Jungle Wood, Desert Wood, High, Mountain Wood, Swamp Wood, Washed Wood
 *Artisan (225-300)*
Uses: Magic Wood, Infected Wood, Cursed Wood, Black Wood and Enchanted Wood as main resources

 **Overhaul of the Gardening and Survival Profession – New recipes and mechanics to give them more flavor**

Gardening
Gardening is a cool feature, but current has only a small amount of recipes. In my idea it should be intervein more with cooking providing a way to gain also Buff food. via planting different crops: Like Grain, Appels, Tomato’s and so on. Maybe with a lot of different type of food: Fish, vegetables and Meat, Species a cook can craft a “Banket table”: Allowing the whole group to pick up buff food.

So my idea: raise the number of different crops, and intervene it with cooking more.
[Maybe also some cooking recipes could also buff hunter pets when feeding time. Like fish and meat of high rarity leading to a 10 min damage buff. Crops buff than for example Boars]

Survival Profession
Now the survival Profession only can craft only torches, tents and a fishing boat. When you are in the wildes what is important: Good tools to survive.  One idea to boost this secondary profession is to add crafting tools like hammers, pikeaxes, skinning knifes, special gloves with + 1/2/3/4/5/10/15 to the table of craftable items.

This has an effect to all professions and allows in the future areas to add some new rare minerals, herbs and animals for crafting recipes where you need a skill of 315/330 to mine it. Similar things are already done in game with the big dragons, where you need a higher skill than 300 to get the scales. This would also not devalue the existing content and opens the way for some future ideas. (Normal skillcap is of course this 300!)

This items also reduce the failure chances with a higher skill. Which is also nice to not fail on Thorium nodes or some high level herbs.

We all have the same problem in WOW. Survive under water and in Lava. Survival should strengthen our skills to survive in this hostile area. Add a consumable that gives you for a small amount of time more breath and a small swim boost. Also add an item that Reduces for a very short time the Dmg done by Lava. (This should not work on fire spells of mages and other classes!)
