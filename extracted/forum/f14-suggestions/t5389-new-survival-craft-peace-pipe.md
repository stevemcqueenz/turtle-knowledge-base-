# New Survival Craft: Peace Pipe

## Post 32858 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-01-21T18:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32858#p32858 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**The Premise**

Ever seen a cool quest reward, but your character is the wrong faction?

Ever wanted to run that cool dungeon in the enemy capital without fighting with the guards?

Ever need to unload your bags, but the nearest vendor just won't talk to you?

Just want to RP as a chill dude who gets along with people?

It sure would be nice to have a way to make enemy NPCs treat you as friendly!

 **The Peace Pipe**

Peace Pipes would either be a Main Hand item, or a consumable with five charges. While in effect, they would give the player a strong drunkenness effect, *but* make NPCs treat them as Neutral. What does Neutral mean? Think of Goblin cities. Normally, the Bruisers won't attack you, but if you take a hostile action to another player, they become hostile to you. Neutral would be that. You can talk to NPCs and the guards won't immediately aggro, but you still can't start fights with impunity.

As far as crafting recipe and level, I'm a little more loose on. A lot would depend on if it's a consumable or an equip. Plus, I generally am of the opinion that balancing needs to be in the hands of people who make the content. I could suggest, but the final decision isn't up to me, is it? Maybe it should use Star Wood, for reasons of being a special/ceremonial item, but other than that I got nothing.

Personally, I just think that Survival needs more uses, and that a way to more easily do cross-faction content is a cool niche to fill.
