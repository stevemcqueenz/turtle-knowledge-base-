Playing more than 1 toon at a time may make it seem easier but in reality it's not easier. It's safer, sure. But the exp divided between the 2, the amount of time required to pick up extra quest items, the required items and gold to keep them going.. It takes a toll for sure. More than likely if you're in a situation where you might lose 1 toon chances are you are going to lose the other also (more than 2 mobs pulled, etc). It does make it safer. But it also adds a challenge beyond what most people expect. I do no agree with the trading of level 60 to level 1 HC toon through alts - that seems like a major abuse of the system, but anyone can abuse anything in some way if they try. This is just one way and I'm sure there are ways to abuse this system that other's haven't thought of. If someone were to multibox 5 at once doing HC would make it easier and probably be a major abuse of the system. Especially having a couple pocket healers that you wouldn't mind sacrificing to keep that 1 toon alive.
