# [Discussion thread] - Separate Abilities for PvP and PvE

## Post 131968 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-19T15:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131968#p131968 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

PvE and PvP are 2 completely different beasts, and having to take both into account for balancing, means that either will end up with the short end of the stick

An idea I've had for a while is the following:

1. PvE has its own set of abilities for the open world, dungeons and raids
2. World PvP shares the same abilities as the PvE ones
3. Organized PvP (Battlegrounds/Blood Ring) have their own set of abilities
This means that logging into a BG will automatically apply the ability changes. Likewise leaving a BG will revert to the PvE set. In addition to Battlegrounds having their own set of abilities, there is only a single tier for a given ability for a given level range. I.e. 10-19, all Warriors will have the same rank of Heroic Strike. 20-29 will have a higher rank, etc...

Certain PvE abilities will NOT be available in PvP, or will work differently. For example, either the CD will be higher, cast time will be different, or just raw values will be different.

---
Let me clarify something. I'm NOT a PvP player, but this has been on my mind for years, and I wanted to start a topic around it. My perception for what may fix PvP may be wrong, but I'd rather hear it from actual players than just speculate in my mind.

I've intentionally not proposed any specific changes to abilities or exclusions, as again, this ain't my strongest area of the game

The topic here isn't about different dungeon or raid difficulties, nor changes to gear. It's strictly which abilities are present in PvE and which in PvP, and how they function respectively

To give some context as to why I'm even bringing this up. I see a lot of threads about how unbalanced PvP is. If changes are made to remove or nerf fun aspects of talents or abilities that work fine in PvE, just so they're not overbearing in PvP, than that's a negative for PvE enjoyers

Fear Ward being available to all priests being a good example. The majority of comments are about how it affects PvP balance, since it was made baseline for all Priests. So, instead of nerfing the ability to the ground, why not give it some restrictions in PvP (Battlegrounds)? Like longer CD, a cast time, or add a debuff where they can't be targeted by Fear Ward again for X seconds (like how Power Word shield works)
