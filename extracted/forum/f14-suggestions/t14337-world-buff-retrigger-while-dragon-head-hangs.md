# World Buff Retrigger While Dragon Head Hangs!

## Post 97775 by Nuk3 — 2024-06-25T01:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97775#p97775 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I had originally asked, in game, if this was a default mechanic, but someone suggested that I bring this to the forums!

The suggestion!:
In regards to the world buffs from Onyxia and Nefarian...
Maybe, for the duration (or a portion of the duration and a limit of 1 world buff per player per dragon head to prevent unintentional extension of the buff's duration?), of the dragon head's presence players could re-trigger the world buff for themselves or others? This could give more players, who are actively questing and exploring, a chance at receiving a world buff!

For context:
I find it a little bit frustrating and discouraging when I have to sit in Stormwind or Orgrimmar for an unknown length of time *just* for a chance to get a world buff.
I love to quest, but I also like having the buffs occasionally! I have a timer addon for the dragon head duration, but no one announces when they are about to turn in the quest that drops the world buff again. I've missed out on many of these world buffs while questing and exploring all the new-to-me "Mysteries of Azeroth!"

After all, who doesn't like to pick up a buff here and there? :D
