# Idea to address crafting skills falling behind the player's level

## Post 71332 by Terras — 2023-10-16T20:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71332#p71332 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings,

First I would like to say I'm enjoying this madcap server and I appreciate not having to give my money to the bad people to get my classic fix.

Now! On to the 'Idea' I have. I have noticed that a lot of people are having issues with farming crap. I don't have a solution for that. I also noticed that as people naturally level their character, it's a nightmare to keep up with making gear for your own level. I think I have a solution for that. My idea is to match skills together and use that to amplify them. I would add a skill into the professions that effectively doubles/triples certain resources.

Example #1: Mining/Blacksmithing.
A classic combo if there ever was one! let's say we use Blacksmithing to amplify the mining though. The system seems to be able to read skill levels from multiple sources so let's add the ability Scrounger 1 to Blacksmithing. This skill would be added at somewhere around Blacksmithing 50/Mining 65 and give either a percentage chance or just a straight up second bar when smelting copper. We can expand on this with Scrounger 2 at Blacksmithing 100/Mining 125 that adds a third copper and boosts tin and silver. This could continue through a max level/skill quest for the Thorium Scrounger skill but with the number of bars from a smelt event never exceeding 3.

Example #2: Skinning/Leather Working.
Another great combo! If you know skinning in the real world, you know that you get more usable materials you get better at flaying the good lord's creations. So, you guessed it! Add a Scrounger skill. More chances for equivalent leather when you skin a critter. I would love it if you improved the level brackets for leather while you're at it. Same idea for the max level/skill quest for Rugged as for BS/M my dudes.

Example #3: Enchanting/??.
We all know this is pretty standard to be with tailoring, but I've seen a few combos. Easy mode here is give the boy more dis-enchanting materials when dis-enchanting something with their character's made by tag on it. How? SCROUNGER!

Example #4: Engineering.
This fellow is special. He uses mostly mining but dabbles in skinning and sure loves the touch and feel of cloth as well. So, what do we do here? That's right! We don't use the Scrounger skill we give him Material Efficiency! As Engineering goes up make the cost of mats go down based on the gathering skill. Special bonus if this nut also has Tailoring decrease the cloth costs.

Now for the other combos I have some ideas but I'm sure you can see how this pans out. Players can keep up with their own gear easier. Farming only profession builds are less required to make money or gather mats. More available goods and mats on the auction house help control prices. Seems like a good idea to me with a relatively simple application.

Now tell me why I'm wrong and how we can iterate on the idea to give TWoW yet another notch in their belt above the bad retail man.
