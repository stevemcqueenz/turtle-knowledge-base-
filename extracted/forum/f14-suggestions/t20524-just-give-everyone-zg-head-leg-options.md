# Just give everyone ZG Head/Leg options

## Post 141788 by Avereth — 2025-07-27T23:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141788#p141788 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm...honestly shocked that there's still class restricted, very specific head/leg enchants from ZG idols in the game considering how impactful they are.  You either get the best enchant in the game for your class, or it's situational/useless at best.

From a Balance druid perspective I only have the option of an enchanted armor kit or arcanum of focus for 9 or 12 spellpower respectively.  But then you look at, for example, mages, and they get  even more spell damage and 1% hit for the same slots.  That's an incredible power gap.  That's ability to wear different gear sets a whole raid tier sooner.  I am sure other classes and builds feel the same way if their role gets nothing from these impactful enchantments.

Please, PLEASE consider eliminating the class aspect of the enchants so that such a fundamentally unbalanced aspect of early game raiding can be corrected.

If not in ZG, then with additional options trading in Dream Shards at Hyjal, or Ley Essences at Karazhan, or adding new librams to the game to trade, or trading the existing ones to a new NPC at burning steppes or DM library.  Maybe something in AQ 20?

It's important that it be reasonably accessible to early game raiding so that every build of a class gets to enjoy an extra aspect of growing their character, and it makes class balance better as this one aspect is not permanently skewed.

With as many changes to classes and items as Twow has, this one is such an easy win.  Please, let all of us make use of ZG enchants, or some new option.
