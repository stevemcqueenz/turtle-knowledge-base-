# Disable Levitate in AB until game crash bug is fixed

## Post 167152 by Urthius — 2026-02-01T15:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167152#p167152 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Bug #8949 (<https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=8949>) still has not been fixed. Until it has been addressed, Levitate should be disabled in AB.

Today, several guildies reported crashing from AB. While they could not confirm that Levitate was cast, it is likely the cause, since no other source of AB crashes has been identified.

Crashing from an AB is frustrating for everyone involved and gives an undeserved deserter penalty.

Priests can use Noggenfogger as a substitute.

Edit: apparently it happens in other battlegrounds too. So it should be disabled in all affected BGs.
