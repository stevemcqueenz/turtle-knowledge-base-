# Enchanting: Additional slot in the bank to enchant

## Post 137668 by Machakasaurio — 2025-07-01T10:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137668#p137668 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have already read several times that scrolls with enchantments should not be added because it could change the economy, but I have no intention of that.

I propose simply to add an additional slot in the bank to leave an item with a character of your own account and to be able to enchant it with another alter.

I can pass from one character to another potions, armor, fur patches, engineering items, prepared meals, bandages; why can't I enchant a pair of gloves with +5 health to a level 10 alt? obviously respecting the level restrictions for such items and enchantments.
why should I make another account only with the character that has enchantment to give use and utility to my own characters?
I don't see what arguments there could be against this.
What do you think about this addition and what negative things do you see for not incorporating it to the game?
