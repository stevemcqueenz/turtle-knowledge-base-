# Bunch of proposals

## Post 154429 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-05T11:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154429#p154429 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey!

Wanna list some proposals and the justification behind them.

 **1. Allowing warlocks to learn summon earlier, around lvl 14.**
Why?: Would allow Ally players to more consistently try out RFC; all they'd need is a bit of proactivity and finding a lock also waiting for the dung.

 **2. Reworking hunter talents which scale on SP.**
Why?: SP hunter is not a thing. Interesting abilities like volley get wasted.

 **3. Adding lvl 45 water to innkeepers.**
Why?: QoL when leveling when you don't find a mage and don't wanna drag the dungeon with slow water.

 **4. Reworking Hyjal flight route.**
Why?: It is unnecessarily long. Even from Everlook, flying there is a pain.

 **5. Relocating the gnomish eng teleport.**
Why?: Black Morass teleport made it almost useless. Now the goblin one is plain superior.

 **6. Adding a Gnomeregan-like port to SWV.**
Why?: Ally is usually already within SW and horde players gotta walk a lot to get there, causing unnecessary waiting times.

 **7. UBRS loot and endgame class gear buff.**
Why?: With the introduction of custom dungeons and items, UBRS barely feels rewarding loot-wise. It almost feels like a joke compared to Black Morass, a 5-men dungeon, if we count in rep items. Similarly, endgame class items, such as beaststalker's and of valor items feel undesired now, that we have the custom items. I don't say we need an arms race of powercreep but these items should stay relevant. Which leads to:

 **8. T0,5 should feel strong.**
Why?: Considering all the effort you gotta make to get them, they just feel bad in this environment. If nothing else, make them unique, and let them become a strong gear for barely used specs so memers would go for it.

 **9. Barrens would need a rentable mount, just like Goldshire.**
Why?: Symmetry. Also, Barrens is huge.

 **10. Rogue threat poison should be available way sooner.**
Why?: Enabling memers.

 **11. Priests should have no access to battle rez at all.**
Why?: Class identity.

 **12. New dungeons could have a Mara-like scepter.**
Why?: Because of the sheer size of them.

 **13. Survival hunter needs an attack where the player and the pet synergize with each other.**
Why?: Identity. Survival hunter is a unique melee in a sense that it relies on an extra mob to play optimally. That would be the whole point which differentiates them from other melees.

 **14. Both Ratchet and Booty Bay should have a boat going to Tanaris.**
Why?: Feels logical (Gadgetzan is a grand neutral city, too), and makes travelling less problematic.

 **15. Flight master to Deadwind Pass.**
Why?: It was barren in Vanilla but we have a bunch of stuff there now.

 **16. Making Ony more rewarding.**
Why?: Barely any raid compares to the clusterf.ck which is Ony attunement. Yet, it has a very limited amount of loots.

 **17. Making fishing more rewarding.**
Why?: It's just super boring and now that even my grandma would be able to solo lashers, it is not even specially lucrative. It is also barely useful, not a lot of people want the fish boss at ZG. Even if you don't make the act of fishing more fun, add low drop rate treasures, based on the level of the fishing required at the place. Best places could have a minimal chance to give you some epics, a bunch of raid-relevant consumes or even a mount. Lower levels could have greens/blues and some lesser consumes.

 **18. More RP.**
Why?: TWOW is called an RP server and while some RP exists, let's be honest. If your name is not Tel Abim, then you play in a PVE server with some minimal PVP support. More events, mount racing, fashion challenges, God knows, maybe fanfic conventions and rap battles. Making it *feel like* RP.

 **19. Female troll faces, bigger selection.**
Why?: Right now you have the option to select 5 ugly as hell faces and 1 pretty. Leads to most female trolls looking the same.

 **20. Make flight masters fly a bit faster.**
Why?: To respect players' free time while still making the world feel lived and large. Maybe have a flight master reputation and flight speed boost could be based on the rep you have with them.

 **21. Audio-based toys.**
Why?: I want to gather BS like a gnome voice emulator which lets me emote like a gnome while being a tauren. Would totally confuse my surroundings. Just for fun.

 **22. Let players get the world buff in any capital of the faction.**
Why?: More dispersed player base helps with immersion. Having a lagging, overcrowded SW while Darnassus is empty just feels wrong.

 **23. Fire mage revamp.**
Why?: To see some when leveling and accepting that old design assumed a big number of fire mages per raid and was balanced around it. Fire mages should be a valid option both when leveling and when there are 1-2 of them in a 40 men raid.

 **24. Buff prot warrior at the leveling phase.**
Why?: When talking about dungeons, they feel abysmal compared to the alternatives. Threat, specially multitarget threat seems to be a problem.

 **Examples:**
 * At bosses, putting up sunders is expected. You need five. But if DPS easily outthreats your defensive stance sunders, it is not a good design. You are basically expected to go under they threat curve for a debuff for 5 GCD time as a tank.
 * Revenge scales like... Well something which barely scales.
 * Multitarget attack in def stance is TC. It is not only capped at four and does a low amount of threat, it also has a CD. Three limitations at once.

 - Compare this to a bear. They do their 'sunder duty' with 1 GCD, usually during pulling (!) and can start via huge single-target threats right away. Sure, their multitarget is not impressive and only hits 3 mobs but it can be *spammed*, which is a huge difference.

 **25. Give moonkins a spell crit talent.**
Why?: Would turn sad moonkins happy.

 **26. Allow shadow priest to spell cleave with a tolerable CD.**
Why?: Would turn sad shadow priests happy.
