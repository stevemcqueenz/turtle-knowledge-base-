# How do you keep in touch with HC's dead friends?

## Post 55157 by Capricornus — 2023-07-30T10:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55157#p55157 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Anyone who has experienced sudden HC death knows that losing all your friends at once.

This includes friends with similar personalities.

Please set account friends, no matter how many times you live and die, you can always work with your favorite people or quarrel.

I've already lost a Chinese friend and an American friend.

There are many ways to keep in touch, you might say.

For example, you can establish where common characters are placed and keep in touch with each other.

But the reality is, they're all sudden deaths.

You don't know which will come first, the accident or the next minute.

Players always feel that bad luck will not come to them, so they always miss the opportunity to exchange contact information with friends in time.
