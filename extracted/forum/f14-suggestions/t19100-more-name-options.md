# More name options

## Post 129808 by starfall25 — 2025-05-06T04:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129808#p129808 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So i am not exactly sure why it is so, but retail wow and every other wow has strict rules on naming your characters. Cant use spaces, cant use any symbols, only letters.

Now i have ran into an issue where i create a name that was created in my head by mashing together english words in hopes of finding something someone has not already taken. The results have sometimes given me a somewhat satisfactory outcome but later i have learned that such a word in fact does exist, sometimes in another language and not always having a good meaning to it.

I once named one of my toons sharia, by mixing together share and to turn it into a name adding -aria to it. Sharia, came from my mind, by mixing together a good word and common naming "suffix". I had hit about level 24 when someone pointed out that i was the one to talk about women and their rights while being named like that (dont ask what the conversation was about). I was stunned and was wondering what the hell was he talking about. He told me what sharia actually was and after a brief google search, i opened a ticket to have my name changed rather fast.

With billions of people in the world, imagine if everyone was to use only one name without last nor middle names. It would be impossible. Fortunately, we dont have billions of people in this server but its enough to cause trouble when thinking of names.

So please, could we PLEASE get the option to at least use one space when naming our toons? As in having a first and last name? Is it just impossible to implement or what? Because i can see no reason why such a thing has still not been done. Especially taking into consideration that most NPC-s have first and last name. How come heroes of the horde and alliance are strictly single name restricted?
