# Stoneclaw Totem Scaling

## Post 135922 by Spirittap — 2025-06-16T11:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135922#p135922 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Stoneclaw Totem at max rank with the Totem Alignment talent currently does 630 AoE Threat, increased to 936 AoE Threat with Rockbiter increasing it by 30%. This is very good for the 140 mana cost!

The only problem I can see is that this totem slowly becomes irrelevant the further up the raiding tiers you go.

Would it be possible to consider adding scaling to the Stoneclaw Totem either with attack power or spell power?

I also believe this scaling effect should not be added to the base Stoneclaw Totem but rather fit into a talent like Totem Alignment or a spell like Rockbiter. This would prevent DPS shamans from abusing Stoneclaw's aggro function in PvE having high attack power/spell power.

I'm sure there are many ways to improve Stoneclaw Totem, but I think this one keeps its original vanilla spirit while also giving it some added strength for later and doesn't overpower paladin AoE threat.

Thank you for your time!
