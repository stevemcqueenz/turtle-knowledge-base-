# Survival hunter identity

## Post 120674 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-23T00:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120674#p120674 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I can get behind the melee hunter concept but I think it's not well-implemented enough.
Was checking retail survival to compare the flavour & design and found what I felt missing here.
TWOW's survival hunter feels like a melee which also happens to have a pet which helps with some damage.
Retail survival hunter is a melee which relies on the synergies between his melee and the pet damage to perform.

I really miss the pet synergies from this melee hunter design.
Would make survival more unique as a melee by heavily relying on the pet to maximize DPS.

What I mean under synergies? Giving a made up example. The ability is not optimized at all, it only aims to describe the flavour:
A critical hit performed by your pet gives you an 'n' second long buff, increasing your melee damage done against the same target.

Currently I feel this added flavour is missing. Survival hunter is less distinct from other melee DPS and pets are not well integrated in their game plan, at least for instances.
