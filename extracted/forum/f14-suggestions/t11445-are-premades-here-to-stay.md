# Are premades here to stay?

## Post 75798 by Baggsy7 — 2023-11-03T23:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75798#p75798 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Looks like battleground premades are becoming more prevalent on the PVE server.

I think there should be a decision be made into the server rules if this kind of gameplay is acceptable or not.

This would save a lot of unnecessary frustration and questions of anyone interested and participating in the PVP ranking system.

As someone who only participates in battlegrounds as a fun side activity, premade groups on my own or opposite side make that experience very frustrating and unfair,and when repeated only serves to diminish the fun or willingness to join another seemingly random group to do PVP.

Interested to hear opinions of the playerbase about this and also if this kind of behaviour is accepted by the server owners.

Side note: I experienced this in the retail version of classic and it got out of hand fast. Nothing good came out of condoning and taking no action of organized discord groups taking over the battlegrounds.
