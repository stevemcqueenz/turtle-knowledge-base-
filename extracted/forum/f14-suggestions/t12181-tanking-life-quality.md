# Tanking Life Quality

## Post 81007 by Lieberion — 2023-12-13T10:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81007#p81007 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This suggestion has in mind the improvement of life quality for all players in the server, for all levels, classes and specs.

 **Simply put**: improve tanking life quality for druids, paladins, shamans (specially) and maybe even rogues (they already have some tanking abilities anyways).

 **Issue**: Finding tanks for dungeons in WoW has been an issue for as long as WoW exists, as also in the marvelous world of turtle. If you are active in game (and not a tank) you will find yourself struggling to find a tank for dungeons (not talking about raids here), from lvl 14 to lvl 60. No wonder why so many new tanking classes (DK, Monk, Demon Hunter) were developed all these years.

 **Argumentation**: I know tier changes are already coming for tanking classes other than warriors (to make them more tankable), mainly paladins, but what about lower levels? Or fresh 60's trying to gear up? With the dual spec already here, why not make good use of it for better/faster group setting up? Many shamans/druids/paladins that are not tanking in raids but could tank dungeons using dual spec, often don't do it because tanking for these classes are far from optimal.

 **Changes Suggestions**: please increase the quality of life for tanks (gear/talents/abilities). The classes specific forums/discord are always blooming with discussions on how to improve tanking capabilities of these classes. Suggestions are not scarce. And here I'm not talking about having shamans/druids/paladins tanking raids equal to warriors (let warriors have their throne), but at least make them good dungeon tanks. Even a Voidwalker or a hunter pet can tank a dungeon, but no heal/dps wants that in a group as many don't want Shammys/Rogues for the same reason: tanking is possible for these classes but not optimal at all. I do need to repeat myself again: this will increase the life quality of every player in the server aiming for pve. Mainly, please, more love to Shammy tanking talents/skill/gear, since its counterpart (paladin) has far more tanking capabilities than them (if it wasn't by crossfaction this would unbalance factions). This doesn't need to be done as an avalanche of changes at once but minimal changes here and there and see how the community reacts.

And I'm sorry if this topic was already debated, but why not refresh it, since the problems about it persists. **Akalix** posted above: " *We do not have a date for our next set of class changes, however they are something we have our eyes on. They will come--so feel free to post constructive or useful suggestions, but please avoid just posting that we should generally make changes!* " I only ask to prioritize the tanks specs/classes as next class changes.
