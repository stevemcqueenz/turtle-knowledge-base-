## Post 121801 by Stoopiduser — 2025-03-05T21:49:00Z

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/p33rRXy.png)

The Cathedral in Stormwind has some messy double placements when flying in from a gryphon or when viewing from the harbour. The one you see missing its lower half is meant to be viewed from around the city and when flying in, but clearly is a bit bugged out, and you're still able to view the other Cathedral from its true position. The one you see to the left is where the Cathedral actually is within Stormwind.

Maybe edit the Cathedral that's just meant for looks to include its lower half and remove the ability to see the real one unless you're in the right spots in Stormwind, or maybe remove the buggy backdrop Cathedral altogether.
