I don't agree with your reasoning either. Mana reg is more than a pure time sink.

While it's true that the 5 second rule is not sufficiently explained in-game, any caster would likely have figured that out by lvl 10. The block and parry tooltips also don't explain in-game that you need to be facing the mob. Getting dazed by mobs hitting you in the back is not explained either. People have ample time to figure this out.

There's a chance someone played their toon into the 40s and still does not understand the 5 second rule. Let's not dumb it down to cater to this one guy. This is a near 20yo game, there's plenty explanation for near everything from other sources online. If someone can't be bothered to look something up they don't understand, why bother catering to them?
