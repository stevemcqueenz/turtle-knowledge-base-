# Lightning Shield - Fixing Enhancer Part 2/x

## Post 31917 by Thegr8equalizer — 2023-01-01T19:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31917#p31917 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I want to begin this thread by saying that I believe the changes I am about to tell you about are not enough to **make enhancement shaman a decent spec for anything other than leveling and meme pvp montages**.

The ability "*Lightning shield*" is pretty iconic to the shaman class. It features a thundery animation and a floating electric orb around your char. This is one of the first spell the shaman gets, even before lvl 10. It is mainly a passive thorn-like effect that lasts 3 charges.

The spell itself is **very mana efficient** since it has very good base damage and scales 100% with spellpower.
I**t is also, for the specific case of enhancement spec, their designated icon.**

If we look at the 3 trees of shaman, it is also only featured in enhancement tree, reinforcing the idea that the spell is important to the spec.

If we look more in details at the **two talents involving Lightning shield**, here is what we find:

 1) **"Improved Lightning Shield"** 2nd Row - 3 points: Increase the damage done by your lightning orbs by 5%/10%/15%

This talent is pretty much 1-1 vanilla talent. If you look even before that, this talent has not been changed at all since 1.1 patch.

 2)**"Thunderhead"** 3rd Row - requires "Improved Lighting shield" talent - 1 point: Allows your Lightning Shield to be cast on allies and reduces its mana cost by 20%.

This talent is custom made, replacing the 2H weapon skill talent.

I believe **both of these talents are not doing anything to alleviate the shortscomings of Lightning shield spell.**

As a matter of fact, the biggest issues with the spell is:

a)Base mana cost is high which makes it hard to maintain for enha which typically has very low mana sustain
b)Completely outclassed by the 2 other shields
c)Annoying to maintain because of low amount of orbs
d)Only relevant when the target of the buff is getting hit

Considering enha mana pool, and overall extremely low performance when it comes to DPS in a raid/PvE environment, I believe both of these talents needs a complete revamp in order to be efficiently use as a way to improve Lightning shield.

These are my suggestions:

A )"Improved Lightning Shield"2nd Row - 3 talent points: **Increase the damage done by your lightning orbs by 5%/10%/15% and your Lightning Shield gains 2/4/6 additional Charges.**

Allows more charges on the spell, which means better mana efficiency and less hastle to maintain in fights considering shamans already have to totem twist. Also helps for leveling as the mana is even tighter.

B )"Thunderhead" 3rd Row - requires "Improved Lighting shield" talent: **You have a 15% chance to hit your target with a Lightning Shield orb charge when you deal damage with melee attacks and abilities, and your Lightning shield mana cost is reduced by 60%.**

 **Allow Lightning shield to be used offensively by enhancement shaman**, giving a **small dps increase** without increasing too much the burst potential in PvP. The reduced mana cost effectively makes the said shield a better choice than before allowing it to stay on the same level as earth shield and water shield. Also g**ives enhancement shaman rotation a bit more active dps things to maintain**. And finally give a **new boost to shaman leveling which is mostly auto attack based**.

Please give me your feedback on this idea :)
