# Gnome lvl 1-5 area

## Post 153807 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-30T15:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153807#p153807 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is an opportunity to give the gnome players a more unique starting experience so that it isn't just identical to the dwarves; this would be accomplished by giving them their own lvl 1-5 area that they would start in instead of Coldridge valley.  The rest of the 5-10 experience would be in the overall Dun Morogh area same as the dwarf players.
There is an unused patch of land in the mountains on the western side of Dun Morogh, west of the Gnomergon entrance.  The theme of this area would be that Gnomergon refugees fled to a cave in the northen area of the location.  Just south of the cave is the Gnomergon air field that leper gnomes and dark iron dwarves are trying to claim.  The dark iron dwarves want to use it as a landing area to bring troops into Dun Morogh, so that they may eventually attack Ironforge.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/y6bYQL6G/Gnome1-5-3.webp)
The gnomes would spawn in a cave containing Gnomergon refugees as well as supplies and technology they brought with them.  The cave asset used would be the Mazthoril cave found in Winterspring.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/Fs4n44KJ/Gnome1-5-1.png)
Players spawn in the back of the cave with the Gnome refugees.  They have an array of robots that are malfunctioning and need to be destroyed so that their parts may be recycled.
Players will then progress a little farther and meet a gnome that needs them to kill a few troggs and collect an item in the cave.
Closer to the entrance, another gnome will tell them to report to a camp outside.  To get there, players will have to encounter their first aggressive mobs.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/c4Bk1bgZ/Gnome1-5-2.png)
Outside, players will be given quests to reclaim the Gnomergon airfield and collect supplies.  They will have to kill both dark iron dwarves and leper gnomes.
Further south, they will be given a quest to report to Steelgrill's depot.  From there, they will progress in the same manner dwarf players do once they reach Kharanos.

The same can be done for giving trolls their own lvl 1-5 area separate from Valley of Trials, something like another island in the Echo Isles.  I may map that out in the future as an example

Here's my idea for a troll lvl 1-5 area

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/zBhFTFmx/Echo-isles-2.webp)
The trolls starts in a small beach surrounded by a U-shaped cavern.  They get a quest to kill sharks in the water.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/rwzWSNxV/Echo-isles-1.png)
They then are given a quest to swim south to a village.  This village gives them quests to kill Kul'Tirans that have come from Anchor's Edge, kill local vipers, kill local water revenants (inspired by warcraft 3), and collect some fruit.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/sfwvwZVS/Echo-isles-3.webp)
The water revenants would use the model from Wrath of the Lich King.

After this they are given a quest to go west to the main echo island and speak to a troll.  This troll then gives them a followup quest to go to find Sen'Jin village on the main land.
