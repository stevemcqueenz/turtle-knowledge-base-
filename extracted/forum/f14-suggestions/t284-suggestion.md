I think there are multiple factors affecting player numbers growth currently:

 1) With legit classic currently on the horizon, many old private players may actually be holding off to see what Blizz does on legit calssic launch (some may now have the means AND intention to actually go legit once classic legit launches)

 2) This private server seems to be aiming for organic growth and word-of-mouth so having only launched recently I think it just needs holiday season and possibly by mid 2019 it will give a clear picture of the average player number for this private server - it is still very early days at only 2 months since turtlewow launch

 3) Many old private players are likely 'once bitten, twice shy' regarding starting a new private server as the likelihood of a blizz c&d ruining players time effort and progress is not only a concern but also a harsh reality scar for some old nost folks etc

I think the growth will be slow and steady... like a turtle.
