# Any thought on adding a few more racial weapon expertises?

## Post 19256 by Petrafons — 2022-06-20T01:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19256#p19256 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd love to see a few minor racial traits granting some bonus Expertise.  In the spirit of keeping the originals a bit more prominent, leave the original ones at 5 Expertise and put the new ones at 3.

I'd love to see some things like 3 Exp for maces for dwarves and tauren, 3 exp for daggers for goblins and gnomes.  Maybe staves/polearms for night elf and troll or some such.

Would love a step down option like this, while still keeping the originals in their prominence for min maxers.

Just a thought!  Thanks,
