# (Updated 2.0) High Elf racial 'Swiftness of the Rangers' improvement suggestion

## Post 112773 by Papum69 — 2024-12-15T10:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112773#p112773 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently the High Elf racial '**Swiftness of the rangers**' gives an 2% [b*]agility*[/b] increase.

 ***Agility*** currently is translated into ***ranged AP***, ***critical chance***, ***inreased armor*** and ***dodge chance***.

You need for most classes around 20 ***agility*** to be transformed into 1 % ***crit*** and 25 for  1% ***dodge***, now here comes the tricky part for you to reach 1% ***crit*** with this racial you will need an equivalent of 1000 ***agility*** [{1% of 1000 = 10} x2 (wich is the High elf talent %)] wich is roughly the same for the other stats calculations like ***dodge*** %, etc. ... (even if it was raised to 10% like the Tauren has with ***stamina*** you would need 200 ***agility*** to reach 1% ***crit***).
Now as explained above reaching ever such an astronomical number in TWoW you would not even come close in possible years of tier evolution ingame.
Yet if we take into account other races's racial's you see that most already transformed final % values from ***crit*** or ***dodge*** instead of going to base stat % wich still has to be translated into of final value %, like for example the Night elf racial that gives an direct final value % ***dodge*** instead of going for ***agility*** base stat % wich would be nothing almost.
Also have to mention that for the Tauren racial 5% ***stamina*** is a bit different as it translates to an direct health value, 0.5 ***stamina*** = 5 health so 1000 ***stamina*** would give 500 hp wich is easy attainable.

So for the final take on all this i would like to suggest the High Elf racial '**Swiftness of the Rangers**' for it to be re-evalued.
 -As for any suggestion how it possibly could be changed into would be.-

 1- Flat final values: 1% melee/spell ***crit*** (as it goes in the theme of utilizing agilty) + 1% ***dodge*** (as it goes in the theme of utilizing agilty, and it also goes in the theme of being an Elf that are agile like the Night Elf aswell) + 1% melee/spell ***hit rating*** (as it goes in the theme of Swiftness or being quik and precise).
This all comes in inspiration of the recent Night Elf racial change.

 2- Increase the ***agility*** % gain to 10%.

It's not totally true like this. Taurens have 5% stamina via Endurance. Most classes need 20 agi both for dodge and for crit.
We can argue to raise agi to these levels but agi is a way more flexible stat than stamina is.
Stam is a must have for tanks and for PVP, and good to have some otherwise but not really a focus. All it gives is HP.

In the meanwhile, agi gives:
'Rogues and Warriors gain 1 ranged Attack Power per point of Agility.
Hunters gain 2 ranged Attack Power per point of Agility.
Druids in  Cat Form, Hunters and Rogues gain 1 melee Attack Power per point of Agility.
You gain 2 Armor for every point of Agility.
You gain Critical Strike Chance at varying points, depending on your class:
Druids, Paladins, Shaman and Warriors receive 1% Critical Strike Chance for every 20 points of Agility.
Rogues receive 1% Critical Strike Chance for every 29 points of Agility.
Hunters receive 1% Critical Strike Chance for every 53 points of Agility.
All classes but Hunters and Rogues receive 1% Dodge for every 20 points of Agility.
Rogues receive 1% Dodge for every 14.5 points of Agility.
Hunters receive 1% Dodge for every 26 points of Agility.'

[https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/c ... 20Agility.](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/classic-wow-stats-and-attributes-overview#:~:text=points%20of%20Strength.-,Agility,Power%20per%20point%20of%20Agility.)

Not counting TWOW-only things like Lightning Reflexes (+10% agi and AP for each agi you have).

Some examples of what swiftness of rangers with 300 agi gives you.
 300 agi -> 306 agi

You're a helf warrior:
ranged attack power is a joke, skip it
 600 armor -> 612 armor
 15% crit -> 15,3% crit
 15% dodge -> 15,3% dodge

 12 armor, 0,3% crit and dodge are not much but are comparable to the tauren's 5% stam increase which only gives HP.

If you go a helf melee hunter and we assume that you buff up, consume up, go to raids and have 450 agi (taking an extremely good scenario).

 450 -> 459

You get:
 900 armor -> 918 armor
 900 RAP -> 918 RAP
 450 AP-> 459 AP
+ lightning reflexes -> you get it once more > 18 AP gain
 8,49% crit -> 8,66% crit -> 0,17% crit
 17,3% dodge -> 17,65% dodge -> 0,35% dodge
___________________________

tldr: It is true that 2% agi won't give you big numbers but it affects a bunch of things you have, unlike stamina which is just an HP boost.
Plus you focus on the worst-scaling stats (which are indeed lame), but things like RAP, AP, armor are still there, buffing your char.

F.ex. an MM hunter with 450 agi goes up to 459, getting 18 RAP for his shots just because he is a high elf. Bigger buffs could make helf objectively the best MM hunter and would be bad for gameplay.
_____________________

As a comparison I take a tauren with 6000 HP and use the Endurance racial on it. It makes it 6300. It is nice, but much more situational unless you PVP. Usual numbers are lower though, DPS/heal rarely reaches numbers like this. Tauren resto shaman with 3000 HP would go into 3150 HP.
