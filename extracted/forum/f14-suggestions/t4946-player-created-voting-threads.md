Allow players to create forum posts that let players vote (like you did on the WB/shaman-paladin buff stacking topic).

The suggestions subforum is kind of a mess. It allows only for discussion, but not for asking opinion for and against a suggestion. Let us create topics that can be upvoted, so that those that get upvoted a lot can more easily grab your (developers) attention, while you can safely just ignore the downvoted ones (and maybe also the ones that do not get voted on - the ones nobody cares about) and not waste time reading through pages of nonsense.
