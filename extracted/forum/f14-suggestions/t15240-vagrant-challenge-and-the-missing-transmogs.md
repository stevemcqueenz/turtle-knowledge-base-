Hello!
I'm a HC player who enjoys the challenge of Vagrant and some of the other challenge modes, but there's one major issue
The items we get during our quests don't register in the transmog armory, so we either permanently save the item until we reach lvl 60 so we can finally equip them, or we sell them and go on without being able to get those transmogs ever again
I dislike this approach, it feels like I'm sacrificing more than I should for the challenge, as I intend to use my HC+Vagrant characters for every other activity when I reach lvl 60.

I would suggest that we unlock transmogs as soon as they become soulbound to our character, not after equipping (this is WoW's retail approach, if I'm not mistaken)

Thank you!
