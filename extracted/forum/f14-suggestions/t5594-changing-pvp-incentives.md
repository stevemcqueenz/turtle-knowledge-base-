# Changing PVP incentives

## Post 34613 by Artofwill (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-12T06:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34613#p34613 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey guys, been here for a few months and did a bit of everything including a (un)"healthy" amount of pvp ranking.
The closer I get to the elusive R14 the more it dawned on me of the unhealthiness of the current pvp ranking system.

There are a few reasons to why I think the current system needs to be changed, and I think with some minor changes, it'll change the toxic ranking system to one where it encourages more over all pvp participation.

The most obvious issue of the current ranking system is the week to week time investment that's required. Not only that, players who are looking to rank up are discouraged from taking even a single week of break, leading to quick burn out before or as soon as they reach their ranking goals.

A follow up problem that occurs is that, even if a player reaches R14, they immediately loose most if not all incentive to participate as you no longer have any benefit to pvp, since they've obtained all possible rewards from it.

The secondary effect of the system on the non ranking players is that, most do not wish to participate in pvp at all beyond R3, as there are no meaningful rewards to be gained unless they dedicate an unhealthy amount of time pvping for weeks if not months on end. Where as they could just as easily devote a much more flexible and shorter play time per week doing PVE to obtain more powerful items as rewards.

After reading many posts and carefully considering all the options, I think some minor changes would improve the state of the pvp ranking system.
 1. Making Honor a Currency that's required along with Gold to buy pvp gear and pvp consumables, including PVP only Major Healing, FAP, ect.
I imagine 50k + 10g for a blue armor piece and 200k +50g for an epic armor. 750k +200G for an epic 2 hander weapon.
The result of this change would both encourage more casual players to still be able to obtain powerful pvp gear rewards over time, as well as retain some incentive for higher rankers so they can keep their pvp supplies high doing pvp.

 2. Since the above change lowers if not completely removes the rank requirement for purchasing gear, (maybe requiring  Rank10 to purchase the epic tier gears), the Rank system could remain as an earnable title system. As soon as you achieve a rank, you obtain that title permanently and can therefore show it off in front of your name plate.
Keeping the competitive nature of the ranking system for players to show off their achievement that they rightfully earned without impacting the power progression.

I think those two above changes alone, will be able to completely alter the player incentive of the pvp ranking system, allowing it to become a lot more sustainable and encourage more players to participate in it as any little bit of time investment has a direct impact on player progression.

Would love to see more discussion on it as well as any feedbacks.
