**Frequently Posted Suggestions**
This is a collection of the most common suggestions, as well as responses to them.

> Crossrealm PvP

We would love to, however, there are technical limitations which prevent it.

> Progress to TBC!

Turtle WoW plays in the expansion "Mysteries of Azeroth." This expansion aims to take a different path from the Burning Crusade, emphasizing the familiar Azeroth of Vanilla WoW over the cosmic battle with the Burning Legion. We do not plan to ever launch a "Turtle: The Burning Crusade"

> Class Changes 2

Turtle WoW is a server in a state of constant iteration. This means we will continually be updating classes, items, and general balance as we see fit. Do not be surprised if something changes. But please note, **threads saying "just revert cc2"** will be locked, as we do not plan to "revert" our continued development, rather we plan to continue building upon it.

> New Race / New Class

These concepts, while very cool, are not something we are currently planning to add. Feel free to suggest them if you have a cool concept, just note that we aren't likely to adopt them any time soon.

> Add Summoning Stones

As we do not endorse trapping Warlock players in large rocks, the team doesn't plan to add Summoning Stones.

Turtle is about enjoying the journey and exploring the world. As much convenience as this would add, it would discourage players traveling the open world to venture to dungeons.

> Expand Questlog

This is something we would like to do, however, it is hardcoded into the client and would require modding.

 **Closing Statement**
The amount of creativity we see in the community is outstanding. This thread is not to discourage suggestions, rather the opposite--keep them coming! This thread's purpose is to address a few of the very common ones we get, or address some of the ones people ask about.
