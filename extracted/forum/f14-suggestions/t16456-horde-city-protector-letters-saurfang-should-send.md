# Horde city protector letters - Saurfang should send

## Post 112273 by Marwy — 2024-12-11T17:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112273#p112273 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, minor flavour thing here - currently Jaina Proudmoore is the one sending letters to all 'city protectors' - the player with the most honourable kills of that race in the last week. That includes all Horde recipients. This makes no sense lore or flavour-wise. The text of the letter is also very Alliance-feeling. I suggest changing the sender of these letters to Saurfang, and having the text change to something more Horde-like.

From the goblin wondering why Jaina Proudmoore has had anything nice to say to me for three weeks in a row after countless Vann kills, desecration of Ashenvale, and conquering of the Arathi Basin for the Horde.
