Why not?
I believe that was the point of recipes with limited supply.
Though I think this design gets exploited because players hadn't portable auction in the days of vanilla and second account for transferring goods requires additional payment. Those resellers had to travel back and forth to the auction house to actually sell those recipes.
Now you can buy recipes and immediately sell them on AH or store them in mule or send as attachment. Even if you don't have donated things, you can do travelling with your second window.

I don't agree with removing "limited supply" recipes because it is part of the flavor. But there should be counter-play to acquire those recipes. For example by killing NPC which sell it and picking this recipe from loot of its corpse. This NPC should not respawn instantly and cooldown of recipe should start only when it respawns.
You can't camp this NPC endlessly. You can't kill this NPC by any 1 lvl character. You can guard this NPC by your high level character to prevent its killing.
Assuming that Thorium Widget sold by major factions, it can be used as additional reason to visit enemy capital.
