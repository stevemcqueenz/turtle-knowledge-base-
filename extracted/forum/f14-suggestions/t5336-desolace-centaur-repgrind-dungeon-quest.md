# Desolace Centaur Repgrind Dungeon Quest

## Post 32493 by Massie2 — 2023-01-12T12:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32493#p32493 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After going through the entire way of grinding Magram to exalted, if I had to summarize the repgrind, it would be:
 - Easy
 - Quick
 - Boring & Mundane

Compared to other custom reps, such as Silvermoon/Revantusk which either take you a **ton** of time and/or gold to grind, Centaur reps are actually rather fair and quick to max out (assuming you only care about hitting exalted with one of the clans, not both).
However, while it may only take about 2-3 days of non-stop grinding the repeatable quests (you will primarily grind the mobs outside the Maraudon entrance), all the available quests are rather mundane and become boring fast, and on top of that are rather vulnerable to competition (i.e. each of the quest zones only has enough mobs for like 2-3 people at a time before everyone has to start waiting on respawns).

In lieu of that, I thought of a solution that includes several merits beyond the rep grind itself;
Add repeatable quests tied to the Maraudon Dungeon itself.
While the specifics may be up for debate - I included some examples in the spoilers below - including the Dungeon itself not only fits thematically, but also gives a great incentive for people of all levels to band together and clear an otherwise unpopular dungeon with popular loot.
The basic idea should be that grinding solo quests is consistent but slow, while grouping up with players is rewarded with much better rep per time invested, so even the T3 prot Warrior has a solid incentive to band together with some lowies to clear the dungeon, in order to grind for the reputation recipes.
Of course, the implementation has to be made cautiously, as Maraudon is notorious for being solo-farmable including both trash and the last boss.

Examples:  3-wing concept:
As Maraudon has 3 different wings that intersect with each other, a quest per wing could be added. It is important that those are 3 separate quests and not a single "full clear" quest due to the map design, as it's not common nor very enjoyable to entirely clear Purple & Orange and backtrack towards Pristine Waterfalls. This would also kill a lot of group formings if someone is still "missing" a wing for their quest and every other group is looking for the "wrong" wing. 3 individual quests would provide plenty of flexibility, which benefits group forming, so people can run what they **like**.

As for the quests themselves, it could be as simple as requiring to kill every boss of a respective wing.
While certain bosses like Princess are prone to being solo'd, by requiring all bosses, this issue would be mitigated, while also preventing cases where a party suddenly declares they will skip bosses and rush to the end, despite at least one party member wishing for all bosses. Granted, while not every person may be interested in grinding rep/completing the quest, in my opinion, doing "more" content creates a better experience than doing "less" content.

An alternative option to bosses, would be to require trash kills of respective wings. I'm not too fond of this idea, because it can be rather irritating when you are at 13/14 Dryad's Slain and your party decides to move on, or spends a while backtracking to find any mobs you missed. It's just not very practical or enjoyable, as every Boss kill is usually at least a small dopamine rush due to the loot drops, while killing trash is mundane.

On a side-note, I am not 100% sure if it is possible to backtrack to Orange/Purple wing after using the Scepter Portal/jumping down the waterfall. If the portal provides too easy of a path to the Purple/Orange wing bosses, then maybe a mix of Boss & Trash kill for the quest may be required.

Of course one other option would be to copy Argent Dawn's way of repgrinding, by providing those interested in the reputation with an item that adds rep-items to the loottable of dungeon mobs. The issue with this, beyond the potential complications coding-wise, is that instead of co-operating towards the rep with other players, you are now actively competing with them over reputation gains. And in my opinion, you should always be **encouraged** to group up with players, not **penalized**.

Ultimately, I can imagine that the best rep grind would/should be a Pristine Waters/Princess run, as not only is it the most difficult (level-wise) wing, but also contains the best loot (i.e. Blackstone Ring), so it has the most amount of interest of people in mind, while Purple/Orange quests would only realistically be completed if the group is doing a quest-run of those respective wings.

The reputation rewards beyond the recipes quickly lose their value as you outlevel them, however, by involving the dungeon itself, you could in a way interpret the lvl 48 exalted epic weapon as one of those "1% droprate" epic items from last dungeon bosses, considering the time investment.
