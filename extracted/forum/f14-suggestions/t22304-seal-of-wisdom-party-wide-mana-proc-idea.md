# Seal of Wisdom – Party-wide Mana Proc Idea

## Post 154816 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-08T07:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154816#p154816 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,

I was thinking about Seal of Wisdom and how it currently only restores mana to the paladin when they land melee hits.
That’s fine, but since paladins are meant to be support-oriented hybrids, I thought it could be interesting if the seal also had a supportive component for party members.

The idea:

While Seal of Wisdom is active, party members could have a small chance (like the paladin’s own proc rate) to restore a bit of mana when landing auto-attacks — such as:

Auto-shot for hunters
Wand attacks for casters
Or even melee swings for other classes

It wouldn’t make the seal overpowered, since it’s a proc, not a guaranteed return, but it would make having a paladin in the group more meaningful for mana users, especially in longer fights or dungeon runs.

Why it makes sense:

Fits the support / blessing fantasy of the class
Encourages group synergy
Adds a bit more depth to seal choices (Wisdom vs Light depending on party needs)
Wouldn’t break balance if tuned properly (same proc rate and small mana value)

What do you guys think?
Would this make paladin support feel a bit more rewarding without being broken?
