# Pets and Weapon Enchant _ Character selection screen

## Post 155065 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-10T10:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155065#p155065 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,

I noticed that when you play a Hunter or a Warlock, your pet doesn’t appear on the character selection screen.
It would be really nice to see our companion next to our character — it adds a personal touch and reflects the bond we build with them in-game.

I also realized that weapon enchant visuals aren’t visible there either.
Showing those effects (like fiery or frosty glows) could make the selection screen feel more alive and immersive.

It’s a small visual detail, but it would add a lot of charm and personality to the login experience.

What do you all think? Would you enjoy seeing your pet and enchant visuals on that screen too?
