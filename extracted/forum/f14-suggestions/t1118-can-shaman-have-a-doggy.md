# Can Shaman have a doggy?

## Post 6508 by Mac (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2020-06-22T15:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6508#p6508 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dogs are man's best friend.  Wow!  But did you know dogs are also sha***man***'s best friend?

Jokes aside, it seems like Blizzard at one point intended for Shaman to have the ability to temporarily summon a wolf guardian pet.  There are two spells in the game, [Feral Spirit](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=555/feral-spirit) and [Feral Spirit II](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=968/feral-spirit-ii) that allow you to summon a guardian pet for 1 minute on a 10 minute cooldown, plus [two](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=1350/feral-spirit) [spells](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=1351/feral-spirit-ii) that teach those respective spells.  Summoning Spirit Wolves was also something far seers (aka shaman) could do in Warcraft III, and is something they eventually gave Shaman in later WoW expansions.

I think this is a really cool spell that fits the Shaman lore and wouldn't break Shaman as a class.  It'd be cool if it were implemented, maybe along with two quests to obtain the spell.  The first one could be short, just talk to an NPC, read some lore, and then he gives you the spell, since at level 20 Shaman have the Call of Water quest.  But the second quest could be a little meatier since it's a level 40 spell.

Also, I'd like to throw some support to [Lightning Storm](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=1108).  That's another cool Shaman spell I'd love to see added into the game.
