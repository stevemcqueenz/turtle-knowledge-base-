# Protection Paladin Improvement Suggestions

## Post 99490 by Spirittap — 2024-07-15T08:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99490#p99490 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Protection Paladin**
 1. Divine Shield (Ardent Defender Change)
 2. Judgement of Justice (Taunt)
 -----------------------------------------
 1. It is commonly known that Protection Paladins lack a defensive outside of their final protection talent (Ardent Defender). In solo AoE Ardent Defender is quite good, however in the role of a tank for dungeons/raids Ardent Defender not very good. If you are in a situation where you are that low health to benefit from Ardent Defender there is problems already!
Instead, what if we changed Ardent Defender OR developed a brand new protection paladin talent to change how Divine Shield works. This talent could cause Divine Shield (or perhaps replace it with a new spell) to instead of giving invincibility for 12 seconds, provide 30% damage reduction for 10-12 seconds (based on rank) with a 10 minute cooldown.
I believe this not only would avoid being a copy-pasta for a Shield Wall straight from warriors, but would directly empower the Protection Paladin fantasy! A paladin who sacrifices his ability to become invincible only to protect themselves, to rather defend their allies.
The previous idea is enough, however if we want to buff Protection Paladins even further this ability could also grant the Paladin an increase of threat during the duration. P.S. if you think about making it a new talent you could name it Divine Providence or somethin' Paladin-y.
 -----------------------------------------
 2. **Judgement of Justice (Taunt)** as it stands now feels very weak in comparison to warrior and druid taunts. Not only do you need to use multiple abilities to perform this taunt, but it costs mana as well. Keep in mind that warrior and druid taunt cost no resource, are instant cast, and have an equal CD.
Without changing the idea too much, what if we benefited the additional cost with additional power. For instance, the taunt could be a 5 second taunt to give the Paladin time to actually build some threat after casting the spell.
As a protection paladin main, I find myself using the ability only for the mob to run back onto the same target I just taunted off due to not being able to have time to use any abilities for threat. With that being said it's almost always more useful to still use Seal of Righteousness, EXCEPT for when bosses drop aggro with a knockback or stun.
I believe this could be a unique buff for a taunt that is more costly than a warrior/druid taunt.
 ----------------------------------------
Protection Paladin is strong but I believe these fixes could give them a more distinct tanking fantasy than their other tank counterparts. I hope that this inspires new ideas for the Turtle team. I am grateful for all of your hardwork, love, and passion for my Protection Paladin brothers and sisters!

Hmm.
For point 1, I've seen a leaked class change which might or might not happen. Plan is to give a passive 10% damage reduction as long as righteous fury is on. It is not a defensive CD but a quite good defensive passive since it mitigates damage no matter how much health you have.
For point 2, while I see where you are coming from, I disagree. Prot palas are the kings of aoe taunting, can do it in a way bears or warriors can't even dream about. I imagine it as an exchange. In exchange of being this good at aoe, they pay with some struggle at taunting.

To clarify: you can argue that prot palas lack good itemization too which is another disadvantage. But it was confirmed that class-specific tier sets come. I think that alone with this passive damage reduction will push prot palas just as much as they need to be pushed.
