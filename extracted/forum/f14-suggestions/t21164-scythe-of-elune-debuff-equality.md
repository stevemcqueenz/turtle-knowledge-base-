# Scythe of Elune Debuff Equality

## Post 146994 by Apimius (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-19T12:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146994#p146994 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As of now Scythe of Elunes proc gives different debuffs on the target depending on your class and spec. The problem with this is that some debuffs are much more valueable than others and only 1 of these debuffs can be active per mob (new procs overwrite old one). This makes the trinket almost useless for some specs, atleast in high lvl raids where you want to push as hard as possible.

I suggest to streamline the Scythe of Elune proc for every class and spec, and instead of overwriting, the proc should stack up to X (5-10) times (only 1 can be applied per SoE user), with each stack the damage added getting reduced a bit from the previous debuff/stack. Ideally, the damage and threat get split between everyone involved in the debuff stacks.
