# Enlighten needs a nerf

## Post 106138 by Ayesha (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-03T12:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106138#p106138 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi!

I have tried the new disc priest talent "Enlighten" and I think it needs a nerf.

Situation now:
I was in smite spec and I buffed Enlighten on a mage. I was doing smite dps and could keep the proc 95% of the time. It procced immediately after another has ended, so the caster could get a 10% boost to their dmg for the duration of the whole fight pretty much. From the feedback I got from another priest, they could keep it 90% of the time on their mage while HEALING.

Power Infusion was a 20% spellpower increase for 15 sec, let's compare Enlighten to PI.
Let's say it's a 2 min fight. If my estimation is correct, Enlighten provides 3 or 4 times as much of an increase as PI, or possibly even more, considering it works both on bosses and trash, when not every priest used PI on trash, as far as I am aware.

Suggestion:
So my suggestion is to nerf the proc chance but then slightly increase the duration, let's say a 7% proc chance but buff it to a 10 sec duration. Possibly even less of a proc chance.
Alternatively there could be a cd on that.

I love priest, and I enjoy the disc changes that you brought, however, this ability seems like too huge of a buff to casters only.

If someone else tried this ability in raiding environment, please do share your feedback. I'm curious whether it was just "crazy luck" that the person could keep the proc 90% of the time during the whole BWL run, or whether its just how this ability is going to work.
