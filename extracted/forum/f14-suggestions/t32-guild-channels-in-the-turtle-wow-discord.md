# Guild Channels in the Turtle-WoW Discord

## Post 171 by Zazzi — 2018-09-20T23:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171#p171 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is maybe a strange suggestion, and one not directly related to gameplay at that, but I'm wondering if you guys would be willing to create individual channels for different guilds in the main Turtle-WoW Discord? At least while the population stays manageable.

I have played on quite a few small servers before, and I always feel a little bit uneasy about the idea of splitting up the community over several Discord servers from the get-go. Even more so when the devs are actively engaged with the community in their official server. I've held off on making one specifically for my Guild-to-be thus far, and would just like to know if you'd consider it for recruitment and organisation purposes, if nothing else.

Obviously, this is all anecdotal, but on one lost realm long-since shut down, most of the eventual discontent and drama happened in private guild Discords, and if you weren't in the largest guild on the server, there wasn't any indication that many people were planning to quit en masse. The server's main Discord was as slow as molasses, as were their forums.

This got me thinking on the possible benefits of creating spaces in the official Discord for guilds to chat in. This might be especially helpful for roleplaying guilds (hi) who want to offer flexible experience open for strangers to drop-in on, advertise and recruit new players, and promote collaboration between guilds. Granted, I'm not sure how many of them there'll be on this server.

I realise that some guilds might like a space of their own, but there's also ways of locking channels to all those who don't have a certain role, if necessary, and it's not like those who want to can't just make their own private Discord. Plus, if the server gets massive, they can always be pared down.

Sorry for the rambling mess! Hopefully there's a point somewhere in there!   smiling_turtle_head
