I wanna say in advance that this is related only to PVP.

Paladin is overtuned as hell. Holy strike hitting 2k+ crusader strike is super nice filler between autoattacks and a lot of classes cannot do anything about the 10sec divine shield that is enough time to kill. Suggestion would be to reduce the damage and autoattack speed in divine shield by 50%. It should be a defensive spell not offensive. I've seen some shockadins literally flushing targets in HOJ by dealing 4k+ dmg in the stun as well. I do not play the class myself so I don't know what else should be mentioned but this is certainly a problem in pvp. You log into BG and 80% of aliance players are paladins - very fun for horde indeed   smiling_turtle  .

 **Battlegrounds:**
 **Sunnyglade Valley** - should be added to daily rotation for bonus honor so players who never got a chance to even try the BG could finally try it. (ADD marks + ADD quest for the marks)

 **Alterac Valley** - heavily one sided BG for aliance > better towers/bunkers. The aliance base feels more like raiding AQ with all the npcs everywhere and the spawn rate of them. Takes literally 2 hunters with mage to defend graveyard while horde base can be backdoored and is way more accessible to conquer. The winrate of AV for aliance is like 95%. Should be dealt with.

 **Blood Ring** - the concept is very nice, to have arenas in vanilla but unfortunately it's dead and let me tell you why:
so the rewards are some blue gear that u can get from dungeons with having brain off spamming 1 2 3. Why would anyone want to gear up this way? You que, you get full T3 opponents vs you abusing every bullshit possible, spawning 10 mobs to fight for you and so on so on...
Now how to fix it: the reward shouldn't be a gear because nobody gonna gear up this way anyways. I would suggest the reward being some unique cosmetics, gold, (maybe title?) so players would have something useful to fight for. Disabling items that could spawn tons of pets,companions idk what to fight for you so its actually 2v2 and not 20vs2. Limit the consumables available in Blood Ring and limit some engineering items that are busted in 2v2 situations.
Adding ladder could also drag more ppl.

 **Items**
This is very sensitive topic to talk about since a lot of us like items from engineering etc.
However there's one item from Blacksmith that I don't think should exist in the first place and should certainly be dealt with.
"Thick Obsidian Breastplate" - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=22196>
I think this item is omega broken in pvp. It's nice concept for pve but in pvp it's just ridiculous.
The chest procs 3-4x+ easily in 1v1 fight which is 1200-2000 absorb of certain school magic and this is only 1v1 situation. When warrior or paladin has a healer behind him the chest proccing 7x+ in a fight makes the value of the item completely broken. Let's say it procs only the minimum which is 300 damage 4x in a fight its already 120 stamina added of value to an item... this shouldn't even exist having one item that has value of 130-200stamina with 38 str.
The concept and idea is nice but should be dealt with in some way. 30% chance to procs is way too high for 300-500 absorb.
Suggestion: Should have hidden cooldown of 25-30secs.

Thanks for reading and please take these into consideration!

I like one of the rings and Friendly seems somewhat achievable as a casual (I'm half-way there already). Higher tiers would require an insane grind, but that is similar to the WSG or AB factions. You get to the epics after maybe a year of irregular play? PvP rewards were never made for casuals. Dungeons and raids *always* had much more reliable and more easily accessible payouts. It's just the Vanilla way.
But if there were no gameplay-relevant rewards at all (ie just cosmetics) I probably wouldn't give it a second look, I'm not sure this is a good way to encourage more players. It seems right now most people that queue already don't care for the gear (like you mention - a surprising amount enters in full Naxx gear), I don't see how even less impactful rewards would attract more people. If anything it might drive off players since I suspect the highly geared people like a) to crap on considerably weaker opponents and b) to win since it gives 4x the rep. Why would casual players in dungeon gear compete for cosmetics or titles against Naxx geared pvp-sweatlords?
So first things first I would adjust the rewards that losers get to 50 rep (from 25 rep). That makes it much more attractive to new/casual/undergeared people to try it out and maybe gun for some of the items. Honestly consider upping rep gains to 75 for loss and 150 for win.

A ranking/ladder would solve the biggest issue. It seems like overkill, but maybe it's the only way? We need some kind of bracketing if this is supposed to be accessible to casuals and supposed to produce fun and even fights. Of the ~25 Bloodrings I've played, I've had 1 maybe 2 really fun and even matches. The rest was just 1-2min stomps, about half the time I wouldn't survive past the initial stun in my dungeon gear. The gap is too wide to bridge other than by some kind of bracket system. And since gear-scoring seems horrendously difficult and easy to trick, past performance in the form of ladder score is maybe a good idea? Doesn't prevent all the horrible stomps but it would filter out the stompers after a while.
