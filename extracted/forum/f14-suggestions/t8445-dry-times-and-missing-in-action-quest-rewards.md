# Dry Times and Missing in Action quest rewards

## Post 54439 by Peasantunit — 2023-07-24T10:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54439#p54439 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**I believe the rewards of these two quests could use a little glow up.**

 - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

[Dry Times](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=116/dry-times) requires way too much footwork and money spendure to be worth a cloak with +2 spirit (sold for 2.13 silver) and what seems to be a random amount of silver between 30 and 80. And since the quest objective does not envolve killing, there's no EXP gained while doing so either, only upon completion, which is too low (1.05k), further increasing the frustration of walking all over the place collecting booze.

 - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

[Missing in Action](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=219/missing-in-action) rewards one of three items:
 - **Robe of Solomon** *(Cloth | +7 STAM | +3 SPIR)*
 - **Bone-studded Leather** *(Leather | +6 STR | +1 AGI | +5 STAM)*
 - **Deputy Chain Coat** *(Mail | +4 STAM | +7 SPIR)*

AFAIK, it takes much greater values of armor for it to matter and spirit on any class besides Priest is essentially worthless, therefore, I don't see how Warriors and Paladins would pick the Mail instead of the Leather. In fact, a Prot Warr/Pally would most likely be better off choosing the Robe, since it gives +3 STAM compared to the Mail.

The attributes on these items feels like a prank, which can be sour after you just spent minutes escourting Keeshan out of that cave...

 - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

I do not bring any suggestions for what different rewards to change either for.
Thanks for reading   smiling_turtle

Spirit is far from worthless for warriors especially. Spirit regenerates .8 hp per tick for warriors. It may be useless in that regard for (feral) druids (.09) and warlocks (.07). In other words, spirit hp regeneration is 9-11x stronger on a warrior than on those two and is a staple for any leveling warrior.

Stamina on the other hand is way less useful for anyone but warlocks. It's the only stat that neither increases kill speed nor efficiency. If you seriously believe stamina to be superior to spirit for anyone other than a warlock, stack more int please. You also appear to underestimate the value of the damage reduction provided by armor in lower levels. 3 Stamina is 30 hp, that's one more hit you survive at best.

Other than that, the 'old' quest rewards mostly appear lackluster because of the power creep of the custom quest rewards. The right direction wouldnt be buffing the old rewards but rather bringing the custom rewards in line imo. Feel free to disagree on this part.

Are those quest rewards shit? Yes
Do you have to complete every quest? No
