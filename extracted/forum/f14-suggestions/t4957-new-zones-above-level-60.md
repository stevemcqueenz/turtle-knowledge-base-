# New zones above level 60

## Post 29919 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-11-27T18:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29919#p29919 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was thinking what if there were some new zones that were up to levels 65 without increasing the level cap of the players.

Like a few new zones in the spirit of Eastern Plaguelands, Felwood, Silithus, which give the impression that there is something dangerous going on in there and there are a lot of strong enemy forces brewing evil plans. The zones don't have to be corrupted per se, they can be a tropical island, a desert, a lush forest, doesn't matter, but to have some difficult enemies and some kind of overarching story with a few big baddie villain bosses around the end.

How this is going to work in practice is that the zone will have regular mobs (very few elites, just like in other zones) which are levels 61 - 65. Since no player can level up above 60, those enemies will still prove somewhat difficult to fight. But a level 60 character, that is more or less geared should be able to handle them.

I don't know if Vanilla supports entities above level 60, I think I've seen guards that are 65, but I could be mixing things up. If it's possible, I think this can allow for horizontally expanding the overworld adventure content in the game without expanding the game vertically, i.e. with an increased level cap and more powerful gear.

This can work in tandem if there are more alternative zones for levels 1-60. It can provide a completely new leveling experience.

As a sub-idea to this 61-65 zones, I have also been thinking about something like public dungeons, which aren't instanced, are filled with levels 61-65 elite mobs and the respawn rates are faster so that multiple groups of players can run through these public dungeons. It will be mostly for fun so you can see parties of all sizes running through them. Like 40 people mowing through or random strangers trying to solo push through and then meeting someone else and joining up forces together.

I am just under the impression that leveling is the best part of WoW or any MMORPG, at least for me. I rarely engage in PvP or PvE, sometimes I do dungeons out of curiosity or to break the stereotype, but it's not my main goal.
