# Enslave Demon and Greater Demons - Fun improvement and fixing

## Post 137419 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-29T15:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137419#p137419 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As many warlocks have already guessed from personal experience, there is something wrong with the greater demons.
Infernal under control for 10-30 sec.
Doomguard, which without reagents can be summoned with a chance of about 6% by the curse of Doom and even less. About 10-15 sec under control.
After losing control, the demons begin to hit the warlock himself painfully.
It is impossible to enslave him again (nonsense, insanity). Two fun interesting summons are essentially useless ballast in the spell book.

 **There is a suggestion on how to fix this problem and add fun.**

 **First .**The time of 10-20 sec is very little relative to the complexity of the summoning and the consequences. For example, the shaman's wolves (added from WotLK) do not begin to bite him after the effect ends.
 **The minimum time of summoned and enslaved demons should be increased to 25 or even 30 sec.**

 ****Second**.** enslave the demon can be very interestingly improved without compromising the balance
 **Enslave the demon** - Warlock can apply **5** layers of enslavement. After the minimum time has elapsed, the demon has a chance to drop one layer each tick. The chance increases over time. From **5% to 80%** during the next 30 sec for simple (non-demonogist) warlock. Near +5% per 2 sec if it seems low, calculate what the probability will be in **8** seconds and how lucky you have to be for this not to happen.

 **Minimum time for each layer:**
 1st - 25 sec
 2nd - 20 sec
 3rd - 15 sec and minus 5% of the demon's remaining hp
 4th - 10 sec and minus 5% hp
 5th - 5 sec and minus 5% hp
repeated enslavement after - also 5 sec minimum
 **Health funnel** - stops the timer while using
For the deep demonology build - all durations x2(or x3) and also maybe Heath funel has a chance to add 1 layer.

As a result, the warlock will be able to control the demon longer, and this will be active control. But at the same time, he will not be able to effectively deal damage himself. In addition, spending 5+ soulshards at a time is an expensive pleasure. More fun and without negative consequences for the balance (as CC2).

Another annoying and sad thing. WL cant to enslave big badass demons in WS, blasted lands some dungeons.
What if  Enslave demons on big badass elites could stucks by different warlock and requires a certain number of layers Enslave demons. So the 2+ warlocks could enslave and controll it. Its a fun that could be

I absolutely agree with many of the points you brought up man!

Some things i would like to add to these...
First of all, I too am really sad that the enslavement mechanic was removed from the greater demon summons... It was a really nice mechanic and put the Enslave Demon spell into a fundamental spot! It made this spell into a main tool for the Warlock to control their Greater Demons!

The main problem with these Greater Demon summons was that for the amount of effort and risk you put into them, their power and usefulness didnt really make up for it... Honestly, I dont think that the solution to this would be to make the Ritual of Doom and the Inferno (and the Turtle WoW specific Demon Gate spell) into some generic less impressive spells very close to your regular summons but with added duration...

I think it would make sense, and make the Greater Demon Summons mechanically more interesting if they were closely tied to the Enslave Demon spell! Basically, these Greater Demon Summon spells would simply be tools which allow the Warlock to summon a "wild" mob to wherever he is, ready to be Enslaved!

In my oppinion the actual mobs which are summoned by these spells should be buffed rather than the spells and its mechanics changed! Basically:
 1. Ritual of Doom - Should summon a Lvl 61 Elite Doomguard Commander instead of the regular Lvl 60 Doomguard... First of all, the Enslave Demon spell already allows for enslaving up to lvl 62 mobs and also even elites, which makes me question why doesnt it summon an Elite in the first place? It wouldnt be overpowered since the Enslave spell already allows for such enslavements, the ritual just basically lets the Warlock get such a mob to where ever he is...
 2. Inferno - Should summon a Lvl 53-54 Elite Infernal Bodyguard instead of the regular Lvl 50 Infernal... Same reason as above, these spells should be closely tied to the Enslave Demon spell!
 3. Demon Gate - Should summon a Lvl 61 Felguard Elite aswell!

The main problem with these spells as I mentioned was that the basic mobs which get summoned (like the lvl 60 doomguard) lacked any kind of real power compared to regular pets and where the Enslave Demon spell really shines is in enslaving high lvl demons (even elites) but since they are only in specific locations this spell was really situational while i think it really should play a crucial role in the Demonology Warlock gameplay! It would even make sense if the Enslave Demon spell would have a talent in the talent tree which specifically allows the enslavement of elite classified demons, as well as maybe a talent for the inferno and the ritual of doom spells to summon the elite version of each mob! This would really make these spells viable (as well as make the demo tree even more interesting) in my oppinion!
