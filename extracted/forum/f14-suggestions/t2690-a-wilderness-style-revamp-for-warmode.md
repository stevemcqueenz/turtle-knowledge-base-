# A Wilderness Style Revamp for Warmode?

## Post 16913 by Mindboggler — 2022-02-10T01:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16913#p16913 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This has been rambling around my skull for a while and I have no idea how possible it is. But I do think it might make the Warmode Challenge more fun.

Here's the basic concept, more or less lifted from OSRS. You take Warmode and depending on your zones anyone within... 5 levels can attack you. As you move to contested or dangerous areas that gap will increase, giving the possibility you could be ganked. I think this might be more fair, but far more fun.

I don't think anyone enjoys getting smacked by a level 60 when they just got to Hillsbrad. So perhaps a fair playing field would be welcome.

Idea itself is simple as all can be, though as I said before, I don't know how difficult this would be to implement. Looking to hear your thoughts.
