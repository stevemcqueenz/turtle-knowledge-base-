# Current Cross-Faction PvP Rule Isn’t Working

## Post 140762 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-21T18:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140762#p140762 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've noticed that healing members of the opposite faction during PvP—while grouped together—is now against the Terms of Service( "Faction betrayal, which is the act of intentionally healing or otherwise assisting a PvP flagged member of the opposite faction in your party when they are fighting someone from your faction."). That said, I still run into this kind of behavior pretty regularly in-game, and it doesn’t seem like GMs are consistently enforcing the rule.

Personally, I think the rule as it stands creates more confusion and frustration than it solves. A lot of players either don’t realize they’re breaking a rule or they just take advantage of the fact that enforcement is so hit-or-miss.

Instead of relying on inconsistent reports and punishments, I think a better solution would be to make both factions attackable to each other if PvP is enabled and they’re grouped up. That way, players can’t exploit faction grouping to bypass PvP mechanics, and it levels the playing field for everyone involved.

It’s a cleaner, more transparent system that removes ambiguity and lets the gameplay speak for itself. I think a change like this would make PvP interactions feel fairer and reduce frustration for players who just want a consistent experience.
