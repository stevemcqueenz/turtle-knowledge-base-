# Upper Kara scaling.

## Post 137189 by Velka — 2025-06-27T08:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137189#p137189 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Getting 40 people together consistently has been a real challenge. I’ve seen progress kills from guilds running with just 35 players, so it’s clear we’re not the only ones facing this issue. Applying scaling to 35, like it was originally in Naxx, would be helpful for many guilds.

You’ve had your race, you’ve already started rolling out nerfs, why not scaling?

ES is a good example where scaling was applied much too late. It was a lengthy and, at the time, costly attunement, the amount of trash packs excessive, with only a fragment of the loot being interesting. By the time scaling was introduced, a lot of my guildies already lost interest in it.

If I remember correctly, the argument at the time was that scaling wouldn’t be applied because of the server population. Scaling was a solution for the low population of the server once upon a time, and with the population increase it was deemed unnecessary.

It still doesn’t hold up.

The guilds that have large enough raiding rosters to consistently run Kara40 with enough benched players are not the majority, and getting enough for a second raid hasn’t been going very fast for them, either.

Many guilds are structured around scaling. It has always been part of Twow, regardless of population size.

Recruiting is ongoing, but the competition is tough. The larger guilds are also recruiting non-stop and offering full, fast clears. At the same time, the number of guilds has grown with the population, which spreads available players even thinner. So telling people to 'just recruit' isn't that simple. The following entry barriers add additional issues for recruitment:

Kara40 requires a long attunement, 200 AR for Anomalus, which isn’t cheap, and Incantagos demands a specific raid composition because of affinities. All of this adds up to a lot of gatekeeping, especially when the general loot quality isn’t particularly strong.

We’d love to continue in Kara40 but we’re cancelling raids over being a few people short often, or go in with 35 and battling through it with difficulty while praying for affinity RNG luck, etc. This makes the content we’d love to enjoy more stressful than anything, and we’re risking losing people to the full clear guilds over it, or people quitting raiding altogether.

Please, let us enjoy the raid in a way we can play with the people we love to play with, without making the first boss the roster boss.
