# Gemstones from LBRS should be easier to collect

## Post 84585 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-01-13T21:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84585#p84585 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

UBRS attunement is an absolute pain. I have two 60's on this server and on neither one have I had the willpower to grind out this quest.
Let's do some simple math to see on average how many runs it would take you to complete this attunement. Assuming two other people also roll for the gemstones (which in my experience is the average)

Gemstone of Spirestone (25% drop chance) - 0.25*0.33 (a third chance of actually winning it) = 0.0825 (8.25% per run / 12.12 runs on average)
Gemstone of Smolderthorn (20% drop chance) - 0.20*0.33 = 0.066 (6.6% per run / 15.15 runs on average)
Gemstone of Bloodaxe (30% drop chance) - 0.30*0.33 = 0.099 (9.9% per run / 10.1 runs on average)

So, it takes 15 runs on average to collect all three gemstones under these conditions. Now if a run takes roughly an hour (with a good group, I've had much longer and more painful runs myself), you're looking at 15 hours of relentless dungeon grinding. This is absolutely NOT okay for a simple attunement for an entry level raid, and will serve only to discourage players from trying out raiding. It is a barrier to entry, that I suspect Blizzard used back in the day to win time for development or just milk players out of their subscription money.

Now, I know I am not taking into account some things - Having a guild help you attune is much quicker, because nobody rolls for your gemstones, and you can only run as far as the boss you need and re-run, significantly cutting the time required.
Another option is to Reserve it and make the group yourself. Or just be an asshole and ML it without reserving. Or just leave the group to fend for itself after the boss if you don't get the gemstone. Most of these are dick moves though.

However, how much simpler would it be if:
 -The gemstones had 100% drop chance. (I think this option would better preserve the spirit of the quest, still requiring people to roll for them)
OR
 -The gemstones were lootable by everyone, not requiring rolls.

One of these measures would significantly reduce the pain and time investment required in getting this simple attunement. Either one of them would cut down the required runs to 3-4 at most, which is a LOT better than 15.

Thanks for reading. Let me know your thoughts on the matter, or just call me a Casul or write tldr or whatever   happy_turtle

## Post 84609 by Bob022 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-14T00:19:00Z

This is not an "attunement".  It's only a key.  It's entirely optional.  Realistically not even 1 player in 10 needs it.  Someone zones in and opens the door then the rest of the group can go whether they have the key or not.  The person who opens the door doesn't even need to go with the raid otherwise.  It's supposed to be difficult--this is not a quest everyone, or even a majority of players, will be meant to finish.  Every boss that drops the gem pieces can be three-manned at worst.  All three can be cleared to without having to go by the other two so it can be broken up into shorter runs not taking too long per individual trip.  I think this questline is best done by a designated player getting help from a few of his friends.

It could also be argued--fairly, I think--that this questline is an obsolete relic from when the level 60 group/lower-end raid content had more relevance than what it has on a server with AQ and Naxx open.  Hardly anyone cares to bother with as-intended character progression when they can hit 60 and jump straight into being carried in higher-end raids by any of a dozen or more guilds doing such content.  Hence this type of hurdle that may have had a purpose once upon a time, realistically no longer has one.  I say that as someone who has a upper spire key.  It could be removed, nothing would be harmed, and nobody would miss it.
