# Make Shaman Gameplay more fun

## Post 116321 by ForumGentleman (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-01-12T10:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116321#p116321 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi Everyone   turtle_tongue

 ***Disclaimer 1: I am talking mainly about PVP, world PVP and solo pve. NOT RAIDS**
 ***Disclaimer 2: I dont ask to make shaman strongers per say; I ask to make their gameplay more interesting***

I think the last big talent revamp has been overall a pretty big success, making most classes not only more viable but also more interesting and fun

BUT after having tried most of them (except warrior, really), I think shaman revamp has been by far the biggest missed opportunity

 1- Enh Shaman:
Kinda OK, with now a solid Stormstrike ability. But Lightning Strike is imo really unfun. The dmg is low and the empowered charge proc is absolutly negligable
Does anyone get dopamine rush by clicking a 180 mana ability doing barely any dmg and (potentially) gives you back some mana? This is honestly really boring

Suggestion: Remove or redo entirely lightning stike ability with effects that ALSO have some actual use in pvp

 2- Heal
OK, the new spirit link visual is sick. Thats about it. Never using it anyways
The new talents are probably the most boring and redundant of any revamp. Tidal Surge and Undertow are two talent focused on mana generation, which both individually suck and provide ZERO dopamine.
PS: since you nerfed elemental fury talent; the old pvp spec Heal/elemental fury  is gone forcing you to be full heal spec in PVP and die of boredom

Suggestion: remove Undertow and replace it with some PVP defensive talent. Revamp Tidal surge with something cooler than 10% mana refund on a next spell. ANYTHING will do at this point.

 3- Elemental
Now officially the new 2006 boomkin - Went from high Octane explosive (and poor) gameplay to hardcasting lightling noodles...
I am absolutly in favor of revamping elemental mastery, which used to make this spec too "one-trick" only. BUT we need alternative with actual PVP ability(ies) either defensive or offensive
Also, Flame guidance talent is still meh and boring, or at least useless in pvp..Is there anything creative that we could do for fire totems?
Lastly; Lightning Mastery talent (reduces casting time) is still very high in the tree. Why? most other class have this kind of talent lower in the talent tree. Doing that would unlock more hybrid specs.

Disc priest got a stun and instant Smite...ELE to be more than a hardcasting lightning bolt simulator needs more synergy among its abilities...

Suggestion: Sky is the limit honestly. Fking EARTHQUAKE ability, totem that gives you a shield or some short duration offensive boost, some revamped lava burst ability, or even that Thunderstorm ability from Woltk that could be nice in PVP and PVE alike. Most importantly, anything to make the spec more dynamic and FUN

Any opinion on that? Feedback welcome   turtle_tongue_head
