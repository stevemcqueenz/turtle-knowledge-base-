# Delay the patch until all queues are gone

## Post 64943 by Wafflecrusher (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-17T10:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64943#p64943 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I recently saw the announcement that ya'll were planning on releasing a fourth SEA server, which is great news! I'm thankful for all the work that's being done to resolve the queue problem, thank you devs. However in some recent forum posts, there are claims that much of the queue on the SEA servers is being artificially created by bad actors. I have no idea how true this is, but if even partially true, it would mean that the creation of more SEA servers wouldn't actually bring down the queue times. I'd imagine it's a monumental challenge to train up a whole team of GM's and the other various human resources you need to the appropriate quality to combat something like this, especially in another language and across the world, but if this claim is true then it's very problematic. If the claim isn't true, it means turtle has grown so much she no longer fits in her pants, and many folks no longer find her pretty. I've never seen a community go from so friendly and accommodating to so aggressive and hostile so quickly. It's a byproduct queue times 100%, and if the patch is released before they're gone, it's gonna be a madhouse. The patch will attract more people, which means even if the queue times go down 80%, they may just shoot right back up.

I suspect that ya'll are probably itching to release the content that's been in the works for some time, but I think the patch should be delayed until this whole situation is under control.
