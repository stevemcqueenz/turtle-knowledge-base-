Lets be real, "organic world pvp" is not sitting at the fight master or TP and killing someone for no honor the second they zone in. The guards are too easy to drop combat with or safespot. There is a reason why PvP isn't abused in Booty Bay and Ratchet as much, its because the guards have nets. You will get run down and CC'd. Nordanaar guards have no CC and hit like wet noodles. Give them a root, its flavorful and makes sitting in town to "farm" honorless kills less free.

I'm not even mad about the killing, its just.. why leave the guards in such a useless state for so long? Just feels a bit careless in an otherwise well crafted experience.
