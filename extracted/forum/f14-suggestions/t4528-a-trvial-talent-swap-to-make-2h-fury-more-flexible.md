# A trvial talent swap to make 2H fury more flexible

## Post 27116 by Demonluigi420 — 2022-10-11T13:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27116#p27116 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am suggesting to swap the location of Dual Weild Specialization and Improved Slam. At tier 4, DW fury are almost certainly specing into Enrage to later access Flurry. So they already spend 5 points at that tier. Meaning, the swap is irrelevant aside from maybe leveling.

However, at tier 4 there is nothing for 2H too great if you don't plan to take Flurry. But still, you have to do it because you NEED T5 slam. Once there, you must spend another ~5 points on slam itself.

So a change would allow some specs to go further into the arms tree if desired. Im not saying its optimal, but the option would exist.

There isn't much of a point to 2H fury if your not slamming. I just want to make an off-meta spec way less rigid at a trivial cost.
