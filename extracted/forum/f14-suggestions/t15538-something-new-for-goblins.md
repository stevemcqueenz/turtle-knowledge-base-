I thank Turtle WoW for everything they've done for this patch. It's great! However, I happened to notice that Goblins got the short end of the stick with the patch. They have no skin tokens, no new customizations (at least female), and no boars for boaring adventure (even though the Whirling Whizz-bots give xp... glitch perhaps?)

What do you think Goblins should have? Thank you for reading, and good luck in your journey.   smiling_turtle_head
