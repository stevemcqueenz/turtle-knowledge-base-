These two jewelcrafting recipes have their ingredients swapped, probably by mistake.

Heavy Gritting Paper requires Heavy Stone and **Mageweave**, but it should obviously require Silk.
Solid Gritting Paper requires Solid Stone and **Silk** but it should obviously require Mageweave.

Basically, the 3rd tier gritting paper requires 3rd tier stone but 4th tier cloth; whereas 4th tier gritting paper requires 4th tier stone but 3rd tier cloth.

These two recipes should be fixed by swapping their respective cloth ingredients.
