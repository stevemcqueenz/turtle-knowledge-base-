# Corrupted Ashbringer and such

## Post 37751 by Original3 — 2023-03-14T19:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37751#p37751 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there, this post is a suggestion to add spell power scaling to certain items.

We all know it, Corrupted Ashbringer is a badass weapon you want to spend all your DKP on, but when you get it, you realise it's just a crap. Same with with normal Ashbringer, except the fact of obtaining the item yet.

My question is, how can we make this and other items with similar effect better?
Well the best answer would be - add Spell Power scaling into it.

On vanilla servers, such as Nostalrrius, you could have scaled Darkmoon Card: Maelstrom with SP, which made it a really good Retri paladin trinket. And I like that to be honest, 'cause it adds more flavour to the game.

So a simple solution? Add Spell Power scaling to items, that either deal damage on hit, or drain life on hit. What should be the coefficient? 100%.

Now I can answer questions, you would have asked in comments.

Isn't having 700 life drain OP in PVP?
It can be OP, yes. But how do you want to get 500 SP and Corrupted Ashbringer (cause it deals 200 by default) at the same time? It is really demanding and takes a lot of time, and honestly, with Naxxramas gear you don't bother with 500 life drain. On top of that, it still is just a chance, nothing guaranteed, so you play with  dices.

Isn't that OP for paladin using Judgement of the Crusader?
Same answer as before. This isn't an issue in PVP, 'cause it's very tough to get all this gear and SP.
I could understand, that having a Life drain weapon and two trinkets, that deal damage on hit sounds like a lot of damage, but Judgement of the Crusader procs ONLY weapon chance on hit effects.
