# Keep Raid Scaling

## Post 69854 by Sarenrae — 2023-10-10T06:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69854#p69854 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello! As a preliminary statement before I posit my case, I would like to state that I speak from a position which gains no advantage from keeping scaling - in fact, I'd rather benefit, which we'll get into later. I am an officer in Macrochelys, a KT-clearing guild with a consistent forty in all content.

I understand the point made in adjusting scaling, and I can assume the thought process was to encourage more people to come together to clear a single piece of content, rather than having a wider variety of guilds doing so, thus maintaining that 'turtle teamwork' or whatever corporate flair one wishes to put on this decision. This would, of course, benefit larger guilds by helping condense members, ensuring there's more teamwork within that guild...

At the expense of many other guilds.

You see, the Turtle WoW raiding scene is varied. We have a variety of titanic guilds filling to forty on every raid every week, ones off the top of my head being Macrochelys, Thunder Ale Brewing Co, The Blackwing Guard, Doom Turtles, and many more, but for every full guild working together you have a small, tight-knit community of friends, people who only speak a specific language (Spanish, Hungarian, etc), or roleplay guilds dipping their toes into the raiding scene. These are who I think you forgot about.

Let's say we have a guild consisting of about fifty people who would rather keep in that guild for whatever reason I listed above. Of these fifty, let's say thirty of them are raid-ready, and twenty five of those thirty are active raiders. With old scaling, a group of twenty five could reasonably handle Molten Core and Blackwing Lair, as well as begin their trek into Ahn'Qiraj. Now, however, the effects of being below scaling start to show their wear. I'll use the earliest example. Vaelastrasz scaled down is already likely a challenging fight for a progressing guild such as this, what with the DPS requirement being offset by the likely reduced ratio of attending DPS relative to the total number of raiders. Now, let's cleave off about a fifth (or more) of that outgoing damage because Vaelastrasz' health stops agreeing with them below 30. Suddenly, you're shooting them in the foot. For what purpose? They would be forced to take in people they ordinarily would not want to raid with or look at other raiding options to resolve being walled at Vaelastrasz. The same could occur for Firemaw, how the bronze debuff works on Chromaggus, Nefarian, Skeram, Viscidus, Twin Emperors, Ouro, C'thun, and beyond.

Being below scaling is, simply put, not ideal. It hurts progressed teams on a bad night, sure, but it can be the reason a progressing team is unable to clear a fight some night...or ever.

Increasing the scaling from 20 to 30 on MC, BWL, Onyxia, and AQ causes more trouble for the little guilds in the community and forces them to raid outside their comfort zone with the minute benefit of funneling members to the guilds that don't need many more. I don't know what you guys did with Naxxramas, but it should probably still be 35 (and the 20-mans scaling down to 12 is fantastic! It lets even smaller guilds dip into raiding and lets bigger guilds easily complete idol runs!).

I do understand there is a parity issue, what with Emerald Sanctum and Lower Karazhan Halls not scaling, to which I ask: why not? Enabling scaling allows more people to enjoy your custom content, there's no benefit in forcing people to enjoy it with a full set of eight groups.

Please do not take this as a personal attack thread, though I am emotional over this, and consider the value of raiding with scaling to so many people in your community. I've already seen multiple posts about how this will kill people's guilds. The blood's on your hands, now.

PHYSICAL DAMAGE IS NOT 1:1
