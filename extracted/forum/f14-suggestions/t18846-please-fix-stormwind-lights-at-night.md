# Please Fix Stormwind Lights at Night 🌙💡

## Post 128229 by thandlius — 2025-04-23T23:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128229#p128229 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi Turtle WoW team and fellow adventurers!

First of all, thank you for all the love and effort you put into keeping Turtle WoW such a unique and cozy version of Azeroth. It’s amazing to experience WoW in this way!

I wanted to bring up a small visual issue I noticed in Stormwind that breaks a bit of the immersion, especially during nighttime. In the official version of the game, the city’s lamps and house windows light up beautifully when night falls, giving Stormwind a warm and alive atmosphere even in the dark. However, in Turtle WoW, the lights stay off at night, which makes the city look oddly empty and less vibrant.

It would be wonderful if this could be fixed, so Stormwind feels just as magical at night as it does during the day. I believe this small detail could really enhance the roleplay and immersive experience for everyone who loves spending time in the Alliance capital.

Below I’ve included some comparison images between the official game and Turtle WoW to illustrate what I mean.

Thanks for considering this! Keep up the great work and long live Turtle WoW! ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)![💙](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f499.svg)

Best regards,
Paladin Thandlius
