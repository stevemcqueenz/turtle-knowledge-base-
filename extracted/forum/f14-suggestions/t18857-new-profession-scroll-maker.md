# New profession - Scroll maker

## Post 128335 by clearmind — 2025-04-24T16:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128335#p128335 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Before we go, let me clarify:
It has nothing to do with Inscription from WotLK profession and it's weird glyph system. Basically it will be somewhat alternative or a good addiction to alchemy.

You'll have 2 different type of scrolls:

 1. You'll craft scrolls that will increase some stats for a certain time
 2. Teleportation scrolls (these will also have 2 subtypes)
- Scrolls that will teleport you or someone who use it to some location (we already have this kind of scrolls, Westfall teleportation scroll for example). Destination could be faction cities or some places that desperately need a easy access.

- Escape scrolls. Those are interesting one. It will allow user to escape danger and retreat to your HC destination. It will have a instant cast time. This scrolls will be accessible only on high skill level and will be difficult to make.
Pros:
+ Will help people while leveling (alchemy + this boost your leveling experience), in dungeons and raids
+ Easy enough to add for devs (all scroll increasing stats already in game and well organized and divided by levels)
+ Will fill the gap on some places that would benefit from easy access (Gadgetzan, Powder Town, etc etc)
Cons: none

Main materials needed for craft:
- [![Image](https://i.ibb.co/jvdjJGHx/58180-c0a31656782dfecd76621e59bb9784eb.jpg)](https://imgbb.com/) Knowledge of Beast

- [![Image](https://i.ibb.co/twbLXw0t/Inv-scroll-03.png)](https://imgbb.com/) Blank Scroll
Example of crafted scroll:

[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/xtr7b9bD/3.jpg)](https://imgbb.com/)

After increasing a skill, improved types of scrolls will open:

[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/4RSK4mTs/Screenshot-12.jpg)](https://imgbb.com/)

A main idea is that you could craft a scrolls from wide range of animal parts dropped by various sort of animals that considered as junk by turning them in Knowledge of Beast. This element could be obtained from animals of all different level ranges:
- for Scroll of Strength: claws of birds

- for Scroll of Protection: boar tusks

- for Scroll of Intellect: feathers of owls

- for Scroll of Stamina: paws of bears

- for Scroll of Agility: tigers, panthers claws
After obtaining selected parts of the beasts you can freely turn them in Knowledge of Beast and then craft a desired scroll.
[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/cKXh5893/Screenshot-16.jpg)](https://imgbb.com/)
And that's all. Simple as is, nothing complicated but will be useful by everyone.
