# Wildhammer Dwarves on the Alliance

## Post 169067 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-02-20T20:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169067#p169067 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As you may know, the Revantusk trolls are an official part of the Horde, and in the playable races, they are represented through new Troll customizations.

The Wildhammer dwarves, on the other hand, should be part of the Alliance, since they receive significant Alliance support in the Hinterlands and now also in Grim Reaches, and they even have representation on the Dwarves playable race trought customizations, and in the future in Grim Batol. Therefore, I believe that, from a lore perspective, they should already be official members and have a primary reputation instead of a secondary one.
