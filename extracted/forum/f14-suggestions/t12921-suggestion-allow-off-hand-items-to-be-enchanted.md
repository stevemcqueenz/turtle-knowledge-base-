# Suggestion: allow off-hand items to be enchanted

## Post 86299 by Studbucket (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-29T20:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86299#p86299 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Today 1H, 2H, and shields can be enchanted, but I believe off-hand non-weapon items are not allowed to be enchanted. This provides a disadvantage to any characters (specifically druids, mages, priests, warlocks, and possibly hunters + shamans) that would benefit from the stats of a 1H+offhand combo.

My suggestion (and perhaps there are better ones) is to allow any shield enchants to also be applicable to off-hand items. Not blacksmithing items like a shield spike, just things like the +stamina or +spirit enchants.
