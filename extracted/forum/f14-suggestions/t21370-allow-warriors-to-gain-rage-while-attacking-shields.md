A decent priest will pre-shield themselves before entering combat to reduce the uptime on weakened soul, ensuring that they'll get a second shield off the second the first one breaks. By my rough, quick estimate that leaves you with well over 2k hp worth of shielding to chew through without access to your primary damaging abilities. 1 hit? Maybe if you've got the absolute best gear in the game and 100% crit chance; otherwise I strongly doubt it.

It **would** make the frost mage matchup easier. That's a good thing. Frost mages dumpster warriors just by existing in their general vicinity. Letting you get rage from the one hit you'll get to land on them without using consumables before you die isn't exactly gonna turn it in the warrior's favor.

Warriors do have a button against fear, but fear as a mechanic is trivialized and made unreliable by so many other things in the game that it doesn't even come close to serving as an equalizer to an ability that you have no choice but to go through.

We don't have to agree, but I will say that if you consider mechanics that start and stop at making one class beat another with no depth of gameplay added to be good flavor, then we've got very different palates.
