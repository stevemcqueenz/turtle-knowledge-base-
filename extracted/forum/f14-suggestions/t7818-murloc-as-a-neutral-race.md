# [Murloc] as a neutral race?

## Post 50134 by Elkepwn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-06-15T14:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50134#p50134 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/C1tPTJB/Picsart-23-04-03-10-13-36-962.jpg)

Hey m8's
Some will love me for this idea and many will hate it. The thought of masses of murlocs hopping through our capital cities gives me nasty flashbacks from experiences in dustwallow marsh.

Nevertheless, under certain circumstances maybe a very funny idea ![😅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f605.svg)

Lore:
A clan of murlocs from Silverpine Forest near Dalaran has discovered magic and witchcraft and contacted a clan near Stormwind through portals. Now masses of Murloc's are porting into our starting areas. A slaughter is pointless due to the high number of fish creatures.
So why not work together?

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/876xWyY/Picsart-23-04-03-12-14-36-337.jpg)

Conditions:
 - Start at Westfall (Mugrl Sands) with a portal to Silverwood (Gruml Cave)
 - All fractions are neutral
 - Can make all quest from both fractions
 - Wizard Mugrurgl in Gruml Cave can port you randomly to any startpoint
 - Can breathe underwater
 - Can't ride any mounts or use flypoints
 - can't join Battlegrounds or Bloodring
 - racial -10% of all stats for a difficult play experience
 - classes: warrior, hunter, mage, warlock, rogue
 - can't be druid, priest, shaman or paladin
 - racial spells:
homegrgl: port to Gruml Cave 10 sec cast time; no CD
rungrl: +30% speed for 5 sec; 2 min CD
wragrl: +30% attack/cast speed for 10 seconds; 5 min CD
walgrm: move speed +10%
Netgrml: net the enemy for 4 seconds; 60 sec CD

Just some quick ideas   smiling_turtle_head

And now think about it, the feeling of playing a murloc in hardcore with worse values, hated everywhere but tolerated and therefore all quests playable.

What do you think of this idea?   hiding_smth_turtle_head

I like the idea of neutral races… however I think the best way to implement them would be to NOT allow them to join either faction.

In fact, have them not even be able to access areas available to the normal races.

As an example.

Have the murloc starting zone be a custom island. Custom murloc quests and shit, but unable to travel to kalimdor or eastern kingdoms.

It would be impossible to create enough custom zones to have a murloc reach level 60. So start with a starting zone and maybe another area which gets you to lvl 10-15. Maybe down the line more zones can be created, but it’s more for the fun of it.

Also nagas can share the same zone if you wanted custom naga.

It would be fun to play around with, but not ruin the main game
