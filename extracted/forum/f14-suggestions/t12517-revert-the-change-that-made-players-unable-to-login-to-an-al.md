# Revert the change that made players unable to login to an already logged in account

## Post 83340 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-01-01T17:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83340#p83340 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

About a month ago, twow did a change where if you try to log in to an account that's already connected, it'll refuse you telling you that account is already online.

Normally, it shuts down the current session and allow the person logging in now to take control. In the case of being in an instance, if you relog fast enough your character won't be teleported out of the instance.

Unfortunately now after this change, if you crash your character remains in the world for quiet some time, and your account remains "online" to the server so you'll be stuck outside the game trying to reconnect until it's too late, you get ported out of the instance or die.

so yea requesting revert so if we alt f4 or get dc'd we don't have to wait very long to be able to relog.
