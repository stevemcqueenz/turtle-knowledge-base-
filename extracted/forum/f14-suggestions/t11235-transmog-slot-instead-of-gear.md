# Transmog slot instead of gear

## Post 74367 by Silverleafeon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-27T17:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74367#p74367 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the biggest things holding me back from truly enjoying the leveling experience is that I can't have my character look how I want them to look. All the mismatched gear just looks kind of goofy.

I think changing the transmog system to transmog the slot instead of the gear would be a reasonable solution. It would allow us to roleplay the character we want to play while leveling, since we won't need to keep re-transmogging every time we get a new piece of gear.

One argument people might have against it is obtaining new gear is part of the leveling experience! That's true, I love the thrill of finding a better sword / helm / whatever. But I would argue that it's the increase in stats that people care about. The joy of finding a more powerful piece of equipment would still be there, we'd just be able to change it to fit our character's image.
