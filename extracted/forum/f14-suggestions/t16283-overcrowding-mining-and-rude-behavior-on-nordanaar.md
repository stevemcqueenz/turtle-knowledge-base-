# Overcrowding, mining, and rude behavior on Nordanaar

## Post 110990 by Underverse1215 — 2024-12-03T02:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110990#p110990 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Like the title says, the PVE server is beyond crowded. When I came to this server earlier this year, it was amazing. Like I'd found paradise. Over the last 6 to 8 weeks, you can't go anywhere in the game without 20+ other people crowded into the same area doing the same quests. Ore is almost nonexistent in every zone from level 30 down to level 1. I can't speak for the higher level zones, but I imagine it's no different. This is ALL HOURS OF THE DAY, 24/7 now.

On top of the crowding problem, or rather, along with it, is the quality and caliber of the people who have suddenly flooded the server. For the first several months I was here, people were polite. They didn't run up on the named you were waiting on to respawn and then tag it without even asking or trying invite. They also weren't running up on ore nodes that you were already mining and tapping it anyway. World chat has gone from friendly, informative, and helpful to a Blizzard-like state of constant trolling and not-so-witty people who think they're funny. I can go back to retail if I want this kind of behavior. And, sadly, this all happened as I got a new job and was going to start throwing some money you guys' way for mounts and as a thank you for all the hard work. Now I just want to look for a different server.

As for my suggestion(s):

 1. Please restart the failed PVP server as a PVE server and allow free transfers.

 2. Please do a better job of policing chat channels. It's a private server, just chat ban the asshats. I've seen religion and politics in both world and hardcore chat for weeks. The GMs do nothing but spam the same 3 messages all day and occasionally join in the not-so-witty rather than discouraging it. If you continue to be as lax as Blizzard GMs, you will quickly have a Blizzard-sized ass hole problem to deal with.

 3. Up the ore spawn rates. SIGNIFICANTLY. I mean, it really doesn't take a genius to realize that if you introduce a new trade skill without upping the already low spawn rates, you're going to have a problem. As a strictly hardcore player, it would be GREAT to be able actually use blacksmithing or engineering.

Thanks
