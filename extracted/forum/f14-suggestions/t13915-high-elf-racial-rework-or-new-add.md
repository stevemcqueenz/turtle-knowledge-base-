# High Elf racial rework or new add

## Post 94916 by Sark21 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-05-15T14:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94916#p94916 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know this is coming on the cusp of the racial flattening where your race doesn't matter to game play, which in my opinion is a huge mistake. Your race should have a huge benefit to the type of class your choosing to make the world feel more Warcraft.

So talking to a bunch of High Elf players who picked caster class specifically because of the race due to Warcraft 3 lore of sorcerers, priests, blood mage etc... I've come to the conclusion that majority of High Elf players think the racial needs to be reworked or more specifically add a passive spell crit for all classes of magic 2-3% or spell + racial much like orcs in BC at a minimum.
The cast-able mana regen feels lazy just thrown like "oh here is magic something", which is weaker then mage, warlock, priest mana regain abilities. On top of that it feels like it was takin from skyrim's high elf racial, which wasn't even a hold cast just an activation.

Reason
This race is the magic casting race of the world for thousands of years stemming from the time of the Highborne, they refined their ability to control and unleash massive magical destruction to the races pre maelstrom, they taught the humans how to control magic and co-created Dalaran, their cities (pre destruction and post destruction both Highborne and High Elf) is held and controlled magically.

I've faced the argument that High Elfs are "rangers" which is true but only 1/4 of the race were "rangers" but almost all of them were magically imbued and able to cast some type of magic.
As it stands the race doesn't feel distinct and even feels weaker from the other races as a magic casting race. Hell trolls have a casting speed buff and they're only 1/100th Elf from the evolution chain how are they stronger magically then High Elf?

I've gotten hate for this before but if your looking at it from a role playing and even lore aspect the racial is a joke which doesn't fit the race's lore from warcraft.
