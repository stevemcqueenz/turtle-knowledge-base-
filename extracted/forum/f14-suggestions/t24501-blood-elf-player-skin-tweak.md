# Blood Elf Player Skin Tweak

## Post 172433 by Endogladryy — 2026-03-25T19:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172433#p172433 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I want to clarify that this is all being said in good faith and I adore the Turtle WoW team for all that they've done for us over the years. That being said, I would like to respectfully suggest an important change to the Mark of Magic Addiction item in the donation shop.

I believe that the skin tone of this cosmetic skin override does not accurately represent the red undertone that a lot of the skin color options for Blood Elves as seen in TBC and beyond have. Instead, the skin color of your character when the Mark of Magic Addiction is applied becomes this drained and muted color which I don't believe captures the fantasy of allowing one to effectively remake a Blood Elf in Turtle WoW.

I understand that everybody who uses this skin might not want to have that exact skin tone, which is fair, to which I suggest maybe changing the skin to only affect the color of our eyes to be green instead of blue. If the latter is the choice made by Turtle Team, I would please like to suggest the red undertone skins to be added to the character creator as they are lacking there as well.

Again, I love Turtle WoW and really want to be able to make my Blood Elf Paladin look just like a Blood Elf from old Retail.

Long Live Turtle! <3
