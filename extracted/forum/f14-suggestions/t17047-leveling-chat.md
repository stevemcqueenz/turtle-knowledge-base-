# Leveling Chat

## Post 116258 by Canovas — 2025-01-11T17:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116258#p116258 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After playing Hardcore mode for a while I noticed the Hardcore channel is super important while leveling, this channel provides a good trade, looking for group, quests, etc and makes it easier to find people to do something.

In the other hand we have world channel in the so called "normie" leveling (non-HC leveling). I tried leveling without HC mode but it felt impossible to communicate with others just using this channel, its flooded with social discussions and non-game related stuff. Don't get me wrong, the point is not blaming this chat.

It would be a good idea to create a Leveling channel that works the same way as Hardcore does: At level 1 you get this channel instantly and lasts all the way to level 60 (when you finish leveling basically). This one should be separated with Hardcore channel and would help levelers find people for dungeons (Yes I know about the LFG tool), trade, etc while not engaging with the level 60.

What do you guys think about this?
