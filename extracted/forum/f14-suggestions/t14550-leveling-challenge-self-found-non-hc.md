<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99255#p99255 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think this would be a great addition to the leveling challenges. Players would be unable to trade, mail, or use the AH. Also unable to or group with characters greater than 5 higher. This challenge should come to an end at level 60, where all restrictions are lifted.

This would pair well with the slow and steady challenge, maybe offer bonus rewards for having both challenges active at the same time.

A cool reward would be the ability to swap one racial perk with another, it can be like for like also. For example perception swapped with hardiness, since they're both pvp themed. Or The human spirit racial, with the gnomes expansive mind. Certain racials can also be excluded that wouldn''t fit any other race., They can be swapped, but can't be chosen by other races, racials like warstomp or shadowmeld.

I think this actually increases the social aspect of the game because you can't just buy things on the auction house. If you need something and you can't farm it yourself you NEED to group with other players, which you don't need to do if you can just trade for the item. Essentially the only "trading" you can do is when grouped with other players, because then you can essentially "trade" any loot found as a group.

You could argue this splits the player base, but we already have hardcode mode anyway. I think will just bring more people in as a new challenge to level and raid a character in this mode rather than dilute existing players all that much.

This mode doesn't stop you getting raid gear. You can still loot gear in groups and raids. Also, I think there's a rule where any BOP items obtained in a group or raid are tradable within the group or raid within two hours? I'd have that still apply.

You just can't buy/sell gear on the auction house/for gold. It also means you'll have to make your own consumes/enchants (but you could farm the materials as a group).

I think this sort of mode brings players together more not less because you can't just get an item material by buying it from someone, you need to actually be in a group with them and farm it together.

And I don't think the profession idea is OP because like with the hardcore rules, you can only group with people who are self found, so it doesn't affect the rest of the player base. And also you could implement it so you could only have two "active" professions at a time, but just allow free profession changes at any profession trainer. That way you can't go on a raid with mining+skinning+herbalism+engineering, you'll have to pick two for the raid. Also you have to craft your own buffs, so I think on net there's no concern of this mode being OP.
