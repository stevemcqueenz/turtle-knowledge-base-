# Many hunter talents have a very low presence and need to be modified

## Post 88190 by Shyler — 2024-02-20T00:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88190#p88190 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Strengthening the Hunter Mark: Each of the 5 talent points can be exchanged for 3 points of AP, and when fully filled, it increases the AP by a total of 15 points, which translates to a 0.75% increase in DPS. This is incredible! I don't even know how it was designed.
The suggested modification is to increase each point by 20AP, which is in line with Blizzard's design expectation of one talent point for 1%dps. Alternatively, it can increase the hunter's team contribution by increasing the amount of melee AP available.
"Enhanced Arcane Shot" and "Enhanced Viper Sting" also have numerical problems. First of all, these two talents enjoy SP bonuses, with 5 talent points for 1 second CD and almost non-existent damage increase. They are even designed to be in the third and fourth layers, where competition is generally the most severe.
The suggested modifications are to increase the damage of Arcane Shot by 20% or increase the spell bonus by 20%, allowing hunters to have options for dealing with enemies with high armor. The Enhanced Viper Sting should be integrated with the Scorpion Sting and include the enhanced effects of the Viper Sting to simplify the talent.
The survival talents are generally biased towards supporting functions, but with the exception of the meaningless "Excellent Strike", all of them are excellent designs that can be implemented immediately. The design of talents should be like this, rather than the few talents that violate intelligence in the shooting series.
The Beast series is similar to the Survival series, where every point of talent can make changes to gameplay, experience, and environment. However, the numerical design is not as good.
To sum up, only the shooting series has a large number of "temporary worker" talents.
