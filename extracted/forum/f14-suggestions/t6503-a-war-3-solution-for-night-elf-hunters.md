# A War 3 solution for Night Elf Hunters

We all know that Nelf hunters are the butt joke of PVE and PVP isn't that far since shadowmeld isn't that good either.
So i am offering a Warcraft 3 stylized fix for Nelf Hunters sine trolls have bow and thowing specialization

[http://classic.battle.net/war3/nightelf ... cher.shtml](http://classic.battle.net/war3/nightelf/units/archer.shtml)

 **Marksmanship**
Critical and Hit% With Bows and Crossbows Increased by 2%

The idea off the original war 3 upgrade for archers is translated into vanilla, instead of + damage with ranged weapons, it offers a proper Crit and Hit % for them to balance with trolls and dwarves who has their own racials for said class.

Also, i would like to suggest if not the idea of Lone Wolf for them since Nelf Rangers in lore never needed animal pets outside Spectral Owls to Scout.

Nelf Hunters in general brings nothing to the plate on the PVE side compared to horde or dwarves, and now that Humans and Undead are coming next, i think its time to overhaul the class and make it unique for each race.
