I play Druid myself and even my opinion is that frenzied regeneration is the most overpowered spell in the game by far, it essentially is a paladin bubble and lay on hands spell in 1 with a cooldown of only 3 minutes, available to any druid regardless of talent build. It breaks PvP and makes flagcarrying incredibly easy on a class that already in 1.12 affects the WSG meta to an extreme extent. In AB you can spin a flag forever with it, no other class can survive even half as long as druids who just stand still and click frenzied regeneration when they get low hp.
