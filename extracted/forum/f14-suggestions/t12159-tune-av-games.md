The old AV or the way it was played during original vanilla was unique compared to other BGs and I see nothing wrong to restore it in Turtle - it's vanilla server after all. It was never ending war rather than 15 minutes game. This is my personal opinion: these games were much more fun than nowadays, nobody really cared that much to kill Drek/Vanndar. Now optimal AV games don't look like PVP at all. It's all about race to the enemy's base. People prefer avoiding PVP now because they will get more virtual numbers in Honor tab if they just focus on burning all the towers and kill enemy's Battlemaster. The most disappointing (and illogical if we look at it closer) thing is that even if one faction is going to lose, farmers don't even want to recall to defend Drek/Vanndar because faster death of faction leader means that the game will start once again so everyone will get much more Honor. I see nothing wrong that they optimize the gameplay - but the current meta of honor gain in AV is just awful and cause the gameplay not to feel as enjoyable as originally intended. In my opinion this meta should be changed if we want to restore "good, old" AV. My idea is to decrease bonus honor for objectives and to add constant portion of honor to everyone in AV every few minutes to make long matches profitable for farmers.
