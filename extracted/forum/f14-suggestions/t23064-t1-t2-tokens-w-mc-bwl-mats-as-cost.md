# T1 & T2 Tokens w/ MC & BWL Mats as Cost

## Post 160981 by Azinforum — 2025-11-28T11:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160981#p160981 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Please make the tier tokens for MC and BWL split like AQ40 or Naxx. T1 & T2 progression feels really bad compared to tokenized Raid Set Gear progression

You may go 15 raids without ever seeing your T2 shoulders drop, and by that time you will likely have reached AQ40 or Naxx gear.

It removes a whole curve of gear and progression to enjoy, as well as getting to experience those cool and fun 5-set / 8-set bonuses

Additionally, make the cost of them use small amounts of Fiery Core, Lava Core, Core Leather, etc. I love the weekly cache addition, but it has destroyed the economy for MC mats. This would naturally take materials out of a flooded market and restore the value of them.
