# What about Hunters and Locks?

## Post 561 by Sigustron — 2018-10-08T01:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=561#p561 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I played around on this server for a minute earlier today and found that the community was very pleasant and approachable. For a vanilla realm that in and of itself is a feat and something to be proud of. However, as I was playing in the human starter zone something occurred to me: How do human locks get their imp? With the xp rate reduced that means that the little bit of grinding you do to get strong enough to get the book needed for your imp is doubled. This wouldnt be an issue if demons werent a core mechanic of locks and pets for hunters. Given that this server isnt technically blizzlike I say that ALL mobs in a starting area be turned to yellow for this very reason. This way all players can get all of their abilities without having it take much longer in game. If this were for a spell or something and in a later part of the game I wouldn't care but just to get my imp I have to stay in the valley for longer than I want to. At the very least just make it purchasable from the trainer. I also want to make it a point to say that its just the imp I'd like this for not every other pet cause those quests are there to make you see the rest of the world. Just a thought.
