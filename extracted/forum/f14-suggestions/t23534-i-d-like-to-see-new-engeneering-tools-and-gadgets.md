# I'd like to see new engeneering tools and gadgets

## Post 164761 by Sylbrosa — 2026-01-09T14:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164761#p164761 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

There are diferent types of professions:

Item makers like blacksmithing, Leatherworking, Tailoring & Enchanting help give you permanent boosts and build strong items.

Then there is  Alchemy, First Aid & Engineering.

These give you stuff. Tools, consumables and cool stuff that might blow up on your face. And some times blowing up in your face is not even the plan.

I'd like to see more engineering stuff and gadgets. Force-Fields and the sort. And I also think it's a huge shame that so many of the engineer's tools are limited to engineer only. Especially bombs and the sort. If you want to min-max DPS, you need to be an engineer.

It's a shame I can't sell all of the best bombs and gadgets. And it's also sad that it's limited because then you can't give engineer anything super cool like an Engineer equivalent to the Alchemist's Flasks. Because it would force raiders to speck in engineering.

Some of the things I'd like to see for engineering:
 **- Blizzard Generator:** A tool that generates a blizzard around it, think of the Mage's blizzard around a device. Easy to implement, very usefull.
 **- Rain of Fire Generator:** Same thing with the Warlock's Rain of Fire!
 **- Hellfire Generator:** Warlock's Hellfire will cause you damage, and might even blow up for massive damage
 **- Magic Buble:** Generator that makes a magic bubble stoping magic attacks comming from outside
 **- Anti-Rogue-Gellar-Field Generator:** Creates a magical bubble that will reveal rogues inside. Lasts a longe time.
 **- Tount Generator:** Device that changes the target of things in an area to it every 5 seconds.

 **- Decoy Healer Automaton:** A Target Dummy but for PvP. Will follow the player and act like it's casting a heal (does nothing). Players will target the healer and give room for the player to act more freely. Bonus points if you can 'atune' the decoy to a player in you group and change the 'skin' of the target dummy to some actual player.

All of these are easy to implement ideas, and would be very fun to use bouth in pvp and pve!

But I'd actually like to see you guy's opinions and ideas on engineering. Maybe we can have some easy-to-implement ideas that the devs can use!
