# Rework Drinking and Water (Mana)

## Post 173326 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-31T03:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173326#p173326 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hi y’all,

Just started playing a mage, and wow, how totally dehydrated and miserable I would be if I drank as much water as that guy.

While I mind the constant sitting and taking breaks for mana less and less, because I can actually do other stuff while waiting for my mana to replenish, I still question the whole water-drinking situation. It’s a very addictive behavior, and I really think we should do something about it. Drinking water is important, but drinking so often and in such large amounts is not good.

So here’s my concept for keeping the same gameplay without drinking tons of water. The core idea is to make water and conjured water buff items that provide a buff to the character after consuming them for 10 seconds, pretty much like buff food. The animation would be the normal drinking animation, and the buff could be called “Hydrated” or something like that.

Let’s take Conjured Purified Water, for example, which the mage can conjure:

> Hydrated III
>  While out of combat, regenerates 7 mana/sec and an additional 27 mana/sec while remaining seated.
>  Lasts 15 minutes.

So while seated, it’s the same 34 mana/sec as it is now.

This simple change would decrease the ridiculous amount of water we have to carry around. A stack of 10 would be plenty for a dungeon, and the whole thing would feel much less like bad design than it does now.

What do you guys think about that?

## Post 173469 by Bhaum — 2026-03-31T17:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173469#p173469 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

It's tedium, but it's part of vanilla game design. The necessity of drinking forces a rhythm to the game. It creates natural pauses where players chat, look at their surroundings, or plan the next pull. Automating this via a long-term buff turns the game into a non-stop "gogogo" experience, which is more characteristic of Retail WoW than Classic.
