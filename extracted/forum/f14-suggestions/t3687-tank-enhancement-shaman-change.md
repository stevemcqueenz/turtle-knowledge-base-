## Post 22523 by Yirman — 2022-08-16T09:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22523#p22523 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would also like to see that last bit of love towards tanking shaman. I already love how Toughness and Dodge has been combined into 1 talent, and the extra armor from shields. I do not think we need plate, considering the extra armor from shields now.

My suggestion is a more agility based tank, like the druid. We will obviously have a shield, so we will not only be dodging, but agility also grants armor and crit, which adds to both mitigation and threat generation. Strength technically does the same by increasing block amounts and attack power, however that does not tie as well into one of the core enhancement talents, Flurry, which requires you to crit; and increasing block is not a reliable way of reducing incoming damage against bosses, who do single, large blows.

One of the only things I believe shamans needs at this point is the option to talent into a taunt. If you were to go all out on enabling shaman tanks, then a talent that enables taunt on both Earth Shock & Frost Shock, as well as increasing the threat generated by Frost Shock just as Earth Shock has a 2x multiplier. That way you can still work around nature immune or high nature resist mobs.

Lastly few more itemization options would be ideal, but... I don't even think it's necessary. At this point it would only be to make them on par with what is considered "real tanks" :D
