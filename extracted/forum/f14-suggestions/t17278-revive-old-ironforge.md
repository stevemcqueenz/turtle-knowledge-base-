# Revive Old Ironforge

## Post 117988 by Giorgioslatori — 2025-01-26T15:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117988#p117988 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Old Ironforge, an iconic yet unused area beneath Ironforge, holds untapped potential for the game. Reviving it could turn this mysterious location into a lore-rich hub with dynamic uses.

Expand Lore: Dive into Dwarven history, uncover ancient secrets, and explore the origins of Ironforge.
New Content: Add quests, a dungeon, or crafting opportunities tied to the area’s forgotten forges and relics.
Player Utility: Create a functional space for rare vendors, crafting, or as a connection to key underground locations.
Bringing Old Ironforge to life would deliver fresh content and deepen the world’s immersion, transforming a forgotten space into a legendary destination.
