## Post 6060 by Raistlin — 2020-05-16T15:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6060#p6060 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are two quests in the Tauren starting area that are limited to Tauren only and limit the amount of reputation you can get from these areas. One of the quests is the second quest of a quest chain which would yield a large amount of Thunder Bluff rep.

The first quest is called "Attack on Camp Narache" which starts from a Dirt-stained map picked up off the ground in a cave in the bristleback area near the character starting point.

The 2nd quest is called "Winterhoof Cleansing" which you are offered after completing the "Poison Water" quest, but the item used to cleanse the well is limited to Tauren only.

Would there be any way to make these quests available to all the Horde races?
