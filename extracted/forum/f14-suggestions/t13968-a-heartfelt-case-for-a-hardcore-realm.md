# A Heartfelt Case for a Hardcore Realm

## Post 95179 by Khalthul — 2024-05-18T15:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95179#p95179 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Disclaimer**
I understand this is a topic of contention and could be seen as a nuisance in the eyes of those who've had to listen to the same request over and over. Here I offer my story, my insight, and a solution to one of its core problems.  I also hope to entertain you along the way.

I feel the only reason it continues to be a hot topic today is because of how it affects the RP community at large, and thus it is close to our hearts as well as mine, and I believe there is a forward-thinking solution that can benefit all involved if I have your patience and consideration.

 **Exposition**
I've known Turtle WoW for years, back when their population was in the hundreds (never the thousands it regularly is today), and I was always charmed by their focus of RP and keeping things slow and steady. Though I didn't join in that time, I admired it from afar until I eventually moved from Classic WoW's RP-PvP server Grobbulus to this one, which has been my headcanon experience.

I truly love not ditching the world entirely for some 10-level escapade that dwarfs the wonder that can be found across Azeroth as a whole and vibrant world. For Turtle to not move towards expansions, I have been a permanent resident.

But I must admit, I am not the most experienced player. I do not main PvP, nor PvE. I haven't even made it to level 60 yet. What I do main is RP, as there is no place in this digital landscape for me to embody the characters, themes, flavors, and aesthetics of a high and low fantasy MMORPG than this very iteration of World of Warcraft. I proudly hobble around as an old Orc warlock with the likeness of *Avatar: The Last Airbender's* Uncle Iroh.

 **A Certain Disconnect...**
With all that said, I have come to feel a certain disconnect with the Hardcore players on the now-called Nordanaar realm. I feel like many of those players are immersed in an exclusive challenge with themselves or others like them, and I know that has its appeal... but the complications of it being mixed with the rest of the revivable playerbase is a complicated issue.

I think their Hardcore experience would be heightened with a realm of their own, and I think it would be more popular than even the PvP realm that currently exists today.

I say this because RP is meant to be an immersive experience, where you can build connections with players and people of all kinds, and since the implementation of Hardcore, I feel like we are no longer a community that stampedes forward together, but we are instead courteous drivers respecting the rules of a two-lane road.

 **Going Opposite Directions**
Sometimes the itch will come upon me to roleplay with someone, but I am caught by a feeling of disappointment when that player is Hardcore. I can't trade with them or give them a gift, and I can't guarantee that I can continue a story with them if they fall one day. It's disheartening. I still get to talk with them, sure... but it goes back to the two-lane road analogy. I'm going one way, and they are going the other. Our faces meet briefly from out of the window of our characters, as we both go our separate ways.

If we do engage in battle together, I feel a sort of unerring duty to ensure their safety (or at least not creating their demise). It feels like hand-holding, and I don't think they like it either, although they may still be grateful that they didn't end their run there.

And if I am on the search for an adventurer in my level, perhaps another warlock like myself to commune with in covenly ways even... they politely inform me that they are hardcore. Okay, no problem, I'll message another player. Hardcore again. Oof.

 **Roleplay for All!**
How much more exciting would it be to know that the entire world of Azeroth was filled with the threat of permanent death? That each item acquired and preserved in the manner of trade on the auction house was gathered with grit and preservation! How much more meaningful would RP be on this Hardcore realm, where your guild is composed of characters who have slain thousands without being slain themselves - a living testament to their valor, bravery, and steeled mind. How cool to know each one of your brothers and sisters in arms embody this! And not only that, when one does fall, they can be honored for their duty. Death would have meaning, and in this tragedy there would be a side of roleplay not ordinarily seen: funeral rites and heavy words for the fallen.

 **Conquering the Dread of Change**
Now, if you do side with me in the creation of a Hardcore realm, but dread its implications as to how it will effect the current Hardcore community on the realm of Nordanaar, I believe I have some suggestions to pose a solution to the problems therein.

Players who are engaged in Hardcore can stay in Hardcore mode, but the ability to take on that Glyph of Hardcore would be removed. To avoid or mitigate any lashing out from the community, two things need to happen:
 1.) Its removal should be on the same day that the Hardcore server is made public.
 2.) The change needs to have an announcement and a date in order to respect players' expectations.

Those who hold the title as Immortal keep it, along with their mount. Those that die Hardcore on Nordanaar would have laid to rest their last run on that realm. But it would not be the only way to be an Immortal on Nordanaar.

 **The Mysterious Stranger Makes a Return**
In this Hardcore realm, the same Mysterious Stranger who had originally given you a Glyph of Hardcore now becomes the gatekeeper of a dark portal. If you survive to level 60, you can choose to accept a free realm transfer to Nordanaar, where you travel to that realm on the wings of the Azure Blue Dragon, whose magic can claw through the fabric of realities on the journey to a new one, where death is no longer permanent.

 **Conclusion**
This is my case for the creation of a Hardcore Realm. It's in favor of preserving the roleplay of Nordanaar and the creation of a new layer of HC roleplay (HCRP) only experienceable on a Hardcore realm. I would love to pop into a realm like that and know that everyone there is fighting the same struggle together.

 **Remaining Thoughts, PvP?**
In the world, Hardcore duels would be like Mak'goras, but PvP in Warsong Gulch or Alterac Valley could be united with Nordanaar's playerbase too, provided no one permanently dies. To be honest, I don't know how those instances work in Hardcore mode, but I assume... I assume... you don't die? Otherwise, why would anyone dare with PvP like that!
