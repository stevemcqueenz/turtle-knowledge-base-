# Hardcore idea/rework for realistic hardcore

## Post 26239 by F41lz0rs (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-09-26T23:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26239#p26239 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

othe people have already stated hardcore should have some changes and I would like to suggest my take on improving Hardcore mode.

My ideas on hardcore require it's own server.
 1. survival needs to be obtained at lvl 1 with only the dim torch give 5 min cd on making them. leave the tent in STV make teh goblin oil quest a prereq to buying anything from that vendor. add a bright torch at like 50 skill. (cheap alt while not having lantern recipe)

 2. Dark nights in every zone with 30 min timer. duskwood should be impossible to navigate without a torch at night.

 3. Food and water needs to be eaten once every 2 hours of play or get a speed debuff. 4 hours of no food or drink you can only RP walk.

 4. Cold/rain locations need to give chill debuff reducing hit and hp/mana regen.

 5. campfire need to give warmth buff making you immune and removes cold/wet debuff. lasts 45 min
 6. camp fires last 3x longer. and required for alchemy.

 7. torch Weapon enchant for beginner enchanters to function as a dim torch effect,requires user to have survival.

 8. swimming should give wet debuff reducing mellee attack speed. belt crafted by leatherworkers "murloc skin belt"  grants stam and increases melee attack while in the water by a few % just under the debuff of being in the water.
this effect would last for 15 sec after exiting water.

 9. make bleed effects from mobs last longer requiring you to use a bandage to stop bleeding. (troll racial doesnt require you to do this.) this would be hard/impossible to do but i would think it to be really cool.

 10. fire and its debuffs/dots should be removed when entering rain or water. also fire damage not usable underwater or in rain.(this is nuts but man it would make for some cool moments geting dotted up by a mob or player and jumping in water to save your bacon.

 11. Hunter pets die once just like the player. random new demon names on summoning

 12. 2h weapons should have reach, increasing melee range by 50%  polearms by 75%

 13. no weapon restrictions by class/race.    relic users can use ranged weapons as a stat stick. hunter and wand users keep their auto shots all others get shoot to once per few sec to shoot their weapon.  hunter skill dont work with wands so...(balance? to hell with balance...)

 14. Tents....  i feel tents should only be usable by a party member of the dropper. grant them  aoe buff to improve on the before mentioned warmth/wet mechanic. reduce teh CD to 1h30m slightly slow the rested XP gain and increase its duration ( needs to be at least 25 min. (with 30 min day night cycle that will allow you to want to use the tent while in rain and in the feild.)
