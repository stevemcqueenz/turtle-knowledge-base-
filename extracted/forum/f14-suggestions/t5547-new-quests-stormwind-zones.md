# New Quests - Stormwind Zones

## Post 34179 by Andromath (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-06T19:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34179#p34179 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, I heard we could make suggestions for new quests, and I thought I might throw in a suggestion or two.

 **Elywnn Forest**
Location: Tower of Azora
NPC: (New) Archmage Valthoriel (Level 30, High Elf, wearing purple variant of Runecloth Robes)
NPC Gossip Text:

"Welcome, traveler, to the Tower of Azora.

Like yourself, I too am a visitor here. Azora was once my apprentice, many long years ago. Alas that a mentor should outlive their student, but this is an all too common tragedy for my people when we teach other races. Azora was a magnificent Archmage, and they left behind a capable apprentice of their own, Theocritus. After the Second War, I helped several magi establish towers around the lands of Stormwind to aid in the kingdom's reconstruction, until by Anasterian's decree did I return to my homeland.

Now I shall complete that which was left unfinished."

> *Quest 1 - Leylines of Elwynn*
>  Starting Level: 8
>  Rewards: 3 Silver
>  Reputation: Stormwind and Silvermoon Remnants
>  Dialogue:
>
>  "Archmage Theocritus has been kind enough to permit me quarter within his tower, and I intend to repay that kindness twice over. Elwynn Forest may appear idyllic aside from the odd bandit or gnoll infestation, but that may not remain true, what with Duskwood's gloom to the south ever threatening to creep northwards.
>
>  My days of adventuring are beyond me. Even had I the resolve to walk the land with staff in hand, casting the simplest of defensive spells to safeguard my wellbeing would leave me drained, and I cannot risk succumbing to my people's addiction to magic in a moment of danger.
>
>  Thankfully, Divination magic, while draining, need not leave me exposed. Take these scrying crystals and place them around locations of power within Elwynn Forest.
>
>  First, scale the hill beneath which Fargodeep Mine is located and place a crystal. Take another northwest to the top of the Mirror Lake waterfall. Lastly, place a third by the monument on the isle in Stone Cairne Lake. Return to me when you are finished."
>
>  *Progression Dialogue:* "How fare your efforts, <Name>?"
>
>  *Completion Dialogue:* "Excellent. With the scrying crystals in place, I can map the region's leylines and determine the flow of magic, as well as it's nature. This should reveal to me if any further steps are needed to safeguard the forest.
>
>  Quest Completion - Archmage Valthoriel begins casting a spell with the visual effect of a mage casting a portal spell. Once he finishes he falls to a kneeling position with the following emote.
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel's scrying ritual finishes, and he falls to his knees, pale and shaking as the use of magic agitates his addiction.*
>
>  *Dialogue:* "Spare me that pitying gaze, <name>. I may not be the archmage I once was, but I am not yet an invalid."
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel rises to his feet, gathering up his dignity as he sweeps dust from his robes.*

> *Quest 2 - Archmage's Reward*
>  Level: 8
>  Rewards: 5 Silver, [Amulet of Azora] (+1 to Strength, Stamina, Dexterity, Intelligence, and Spirit)
>  Reputation: Stormwind and Silvermoon Remnants
>  Dialogue:
>
>  "My ritual has revealed to me that not only are the leylines of Elwynn Forest in pristine condition, but also several locations of lost treasures. I once served as a mage of the Kirin Tor, and made a living using my scrying magic to discover the locations of forgotten riches, which I would share with enterprising individuals such as yourself, in exchange for a modest fee.
>
>  Your efforts alone on my behalf are more than sufficient. I will entrust to you a treasure which my apprentice created long ago. Travel to the Ridgepoint Tower in the southwest, and move directly south from there to the river bank bordering Duskwood. Once there, let out a hearty /laugh. Azora was ever one who loved to bring a smile to the faces of those around them. I am unsurprised they concealed this creation of theirs with such a whimsical seal. Unsurprised, but also pleased."
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel looks away with a sad smile, fondly remembering his deceased apprentice.*
>
>  **The Player Arrives at the spot and /laughs, causing a chest to appear which, upon looting, opens the following dialogue.**
>
>  Dialogue: "Your laughter is echoed as if others from across time and space are sharing your mirth. The lid of the chest opens, and within, reclining on a crimson pillow, is a golden amulet as your prize. Beneath it, a small handful of silver coins are also present, which you gladly scoop up."

 **Westfall**
Location: Sentinel Hill Inn
NPC: (New) Archmage Valthoriel (Level 30, High Elf, wearing purple variant of Runecloth Robes)
NPC Gossip Text:

"I have witnessed these lands after the devastating occupation of the demonic Horde of the Second War, <name.> Scorched and blackened, corrupted by profane arts, the soil stained crimson with the blood of the innocent and valorous, and black with the blood of those beasts whom invaded our world at the behest of their demonic masters. If you had told me then that I would see them in a worse state, I would not have believed you. This, however, is worse. Outlaws have salted the earth that nothing may grow. Tree's have been felled for lumber. Mechanical monstrosities swarm thick across the land like flies on a corpse.

We have much work to do."

> *Quest 3 - Leylines of Westfall*
>  Level: 18
>  Rewards: 5 Silver
>  Reputation: Stormwind and Silvermoon Remnants
>  Dialogue:
>
>  "Once more I bid you salutations, <name>.
>
>  Although I did not have the distinction of serving with him during the Second War, the name of Gryan Stoutmantle is not unheard of to me. Fortunately, my name from those days reached his ears as well, and so he has welcomed my presence here, despite only being able to render a pittance of assistance to his cause.
>
>  I would not impose upon Lord Stoutmantle save for some unwelcome news. Klaven Mortwake, a mage I worked with in the restoration of Stormwind's lands, has betrayed the Alliance and sided with the Defias. Alas, if I could only make use of the resources of the mage tower I helped him construct...
>
>  It behooves me not to lament on possibilities. If you would aid me once more, I have a supply of scrying crystals I would ask you to spread throughout Westfall. Take one north, and place it atop the hill over which the Jangolode Mine has been excavated. Take a second south to the bottom of Stendel's Pond. Lastly, place the third at the old lighthouse along the southwestern shoreline."
>
>  *Progression Dialogue:* "Please be swift, <Name>. I fear the death of Westfall's flora may have stagnated the leylines, and if that has happened, Duskwood's corruption may seep west."
>
>  *Completion Dialogue:* "Thank you, <name>. Let us pray that Westfall's leylines are as of yet untainted."
>
>  Quest Completion - Archmage Valthoriel begins casting a spell with the visual effect of a mage casting a portal spell. Once he finishes he falls to a kneeling position with the following emote.
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel's scrying ritual finishes, and he falls to his knees, pale and shaking as the use of magic further agitates his addiction. For a fleeting moment a shadow of hunger flits across his face, transforming the otherwise graceful elven features into something that fills you with revulsion.*
>
>  *Dialogue:* "I am fine... although I clearly needed more time to recover since my last ritual. After this I believe I will spend the day in meditation, until I can master myself once more. My apologies for what you've witnessed. It shames me, this weakness, but your steadfast presence is a balm for my soul."
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel rises to his feet, leaning heavily upon his staff as he does so. Still deathly pale, he hides his trembling free hand in the sleeve of his robe.*

> *Quest 4 - Treasure of Moonbrook*
>  Level: 18
>  Rewards: 10 Silver, [Bounty of Moonbrook] (Inventory item used to gain food buffs to restore 1,252 MP and HP, is not consumed on use, 30 second cooldown)
>  Reputation: Stormwind and Silvermoon Remnants
>  Dialogue:
>
>  "The people of Westfall may breath easy for a time at least, as the corruption of Duskwood is not creeping west. That having been said, the leylines of these lands have grown disquietingly stagnant. I shall need to send a report to the tower in Stormwind. Perhaps they will dedicate resources to reclaiming Klaven's Tower, and repurposing it.
>
>  As for you, I have discovered a number of caches which I believe the Defias themselves may have set aside to fund their activities. I will be divulging most of these to Lord Stoutmantle, but I do not believe he will begrudge you one as a reward. Head into the township of Moonbrook, where once a priest blessed the harvests of Westfall. At the fountain in the town square, kneel and pray, and your reward will be revealed to you."
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel closes his eyes as he sways on his feet, fatigue and exhaustion taking their toll.*
>
>  **The Player Arrives at the spot and /prays, causing a Lightwell to appear which, upon looting, opens the following dialogue.**
>
>  Dialogue: "Westfall was ever the breadbasket of Stormwind, and the Clerics of Northshire were pleased to bless it's harvests. Within this basin lies the bounty of Westfall, a cornucopia of fruits and vegetables. Just as Westfall's harvests season after season fed Stormwind's people, so too will this bowl replenish it's stores to provide you sustenance. As you turn to leave, a flash of silver catches your eye from the old basin. Were those coins always there? Without hesitation you pocket them: better for you to have them than the Defias."

 **Redridge**
Location: Lakeshire Townhall
NPC: (New) Archmage Valthoriel (Level 30, High Elf, wearing purple variant of Runecloth Robes)
NPC Gossip Text:

"I remember these lands...

A lifetime ago, we marched from the north. The battle of Blackrock Mountain... so many good men and women gave their lives. Anduin Lothar was chief among them, although my last apprentice...

<Valthoriel shakes his head sadly, his expression hardening with his resolve>.

This is not another day in another age. The Sunwell is gone, and the power to slaughter the enemies of the Alliance is beyond my grasp, much as I would like nothing more. I will be damned if I let these lands and people suffer further."

> *Quest 5 - Leylines of Redridge*
>  Level: 22
>  Rewards: 15 Silver
>  Reputation: Stormwind and Silvermoon Remnants
>  Dialogue:
>
>  "I thought I might see you once more, <name.>
>
>  Magistrate Solomon has graciously permitted me sanctuary in Lakeshire, although with reluctant confession that he had precious little assurance to offer regarding my safety. The magistrate has troubles enough, and although it would burden me to reveal more to him, ignoring the potential until it becomes the consequences would be doing him a greater disservice.
>
>  By now you should be familiar with my crystals. I hope you are a strong swimmer, for I will need one at the bottom of Lake Everstill. The second should be concealed in the ruins of Stonewatch Keep. As for the last... I will require it placed far to the east, at the Tower of Ilgalar. Beware of the traitor, Morganth. It shames me that I ever counted him a friend, seeing what he has become.
>
>  Perhaps Stormwind's leylines have not been further corrupted, but now two of the magi I worked with to rebuild this kingdom have been. It is not my place to deal with them, nor is it within my power."
>
>  *Progression Dialogue:* "Take care while near the Tower of Ilgalar. Morganth has delved into powers unknown to me. I cannot guarantee he will not interfere."
>
>  *Completion Dialogue:* "Thank you, <name>. Let us now learn of Redridge's fate."
>
>  Quest Completion - Archmage Valthoriel begins casting a spell with the visual effect of a mage casting a portal spell. Once he finishes, a blast of shadow magic causes him to get knocked backward.
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel's scrying ritual finishes, and a blast of dark magic strikes him from afar, knocking him down. Grimacing, he regains his feet, pale but in better condition than his last ritual.*
>
>  *Dialogue:* "Damn that man... this was his way of telling me he knows what I am attempting, and that my actions are beneath his care, a sting to wound my pride more than threaten my life. Well, he always was an impatient fool who never appreciated the subtleties of magic, always grasping for the power to destroy."
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel brushes off his robes in an attempt to look supremely unphased, although it seems clear his impotence is eroding his morale.*

> *Quest 6 - Relic of the Second War*
>  Level: 22
>  Rewards: 15 Silver, [Icon of the Silver Hand] (Trinket. Use: Casts the effects of rank 1 blessing of Wisdom and Might on the target, 5 minute cooldown)
>  Reputation: Stormwind and Silvermoon Remnants
>  Dialogue:
>
>  "The leylines of Redridge are not corrupted, although Morganth is tapping into them to spread his influence far and wide indeed. I cannot be certain what he is planning, but I shall report this to the Stormwind Mage's Tower once my report to Magistrate Solomon is complete.
>
>  As for you, I have another treasure for you to seek in reward for your efforts. Anduin Lothar was not the only soul to perish in the Battle of Blockrock Mountain. Among the fallen were a number of Knights of the Silver Hand, born here in Stormwind. An icon used by one of them to bless themselves and their allies lies deep in the waters of Lake Everstill, waiting for a righteous soul to claim it. Why, even someone mired in the darkest arts could benefit from it, if their intentions are just."
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel gives you a curt nod as he conjures a sheaf of parchment, jar of ink, and a quill, and enchants them to begin writing a report for Stormwind's mages.*
>
>  **The Player swims to the bottom of Lake Everstill to find a glittering patch of mud which, upon looting, opens the following dialogue.**
>
>  Dialogue: "As your lungs burn for air in the murky water, your eyes catch a silvery glitter. Sweeping the mud away reveals a silver icon with the symbol of the Silver Hand upon it. A beam of sunlight from above glimmers down on it, as if welcoming it back to the world. As you lift it, a number of silver coins are revealed beneath it. Does this count as grave-robbing? Or just salvaging? If nothing else it seems as if the Light wants you to have this."

 **Duskwood**
Location: Darkshire Inn
NPC: (New) Archmage Valthoriel (Level 30, High Elf, wearing purple variant of Runecloth Robes)
NPC Gossip Text:

"I can feel it, <name>. The corruption of these lands is suffocating.

You may not know this, but this forest was once known as, Brightwood. There was a time this town was called, 'Grand Hamlet.' Alas, the scars of the First War run deep here, and evils both old and new have slithered in to entrench themselves.

This land in particular holds meaning to me, <name>. We had been in the process of planning a fourth tower here, one which would have made it possible to establish a portal network between all the towers of Stormwind, to move troops and resources swiftly about to defend the kingdom against all perils. Instead, Anasterian withdrew Quel'Thalas from the Alliance, and I, against what I had thought of as selfish wishes to remain, heeded his call. I am plagued by thoughts of what could have been had I remained, just as this land is plagued by the ghosts of the past."

> *Quest 7 - Leylines of Duskwood*
>  Level: 26
>  Rewards: 20 Silver
>  Reputation: Stormwind and Silvermoon Remnants
>  Dialogue:
>
>  "I grow uneasy here, <name>.
>
>  This land pulses with dark magic, the leylines carrying it from Deadwind Pass like poison through a vein. I cannot imagine what possibly keeps this darkness from spreading, but perhaps we will learn, and safeguard it.
>
>  Once more I shall have you place three crystals. The first should be placed in a grove to the northeast, beyond the old abandoned watchtower, where you will find a monument to a fallen soldier. The second must be placed at the heart of Duskwood, although I know of no path to the peak of that hill. The last must be placed in the town square of Raven Hill to the east.
>
>  You've fought many dangers to the kingdom on your path to this point, name, but I would not see your journey end here. Be watchful, be patient, and may the sun guide you."
>
>  *Progression Dialogue:* "Duskwood is unnerving at the best of times. While before I beseeched haste, here I will counsel patience. Better to wait a few days for success, than to lose a promising member of the Alliance in an attempt."
>
>  *Completion Dialogue:* "Thank you, <name>. This moment has been long in coming. Perhaps with this ritual, the mystery of Duskwood will be unraveled, and we can mend it."
>
>  Quest Completion - Archmage Valthoriel begins casting a spell with the visual effect of a mage casting a portal spell. Once he finishes, a cloud of shadow magic engulfs him as he /cowers, until a beam of white light descents on him (Lunar Festival Effect).
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel is assaulted by the corrupt magic of Duskwood for a fleeting moment before moonlight descends from above, his expression tranquil as peace settles upon him.*
>
>  *Dialogue:* "Ahhh... this presence. It all makes sense now, <name>. Finally, it all makes sense..."
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel stands there quietly, basking in the glow of moonlight as tears run unashamedly down his cheeks.*

> *Quest 8 - Elune's Gift*
>  Level: 26
>  Rewards: 25 Silver, [Statuette of Elune] (This Item Gives a Quest)
>  Reputation: Stormwind, Darnassus, and Silvermoon Remnants
>  Dialogue:
>
>  "Within the heart of this benighted land lays a grove replete with the power of the Moon Goddess, Elune. I've only heard tales of the ancient beliefs my people left behind in their exile from Kalimdor. I never could have imagined how it would feel to bask in even this, a shallow reflection of her radiance.
>
>  The pall cast over Duskwood is kept in check by her power. That is why Stormwind's leylines have remained pure after all this time. I do not know that any action is needed to safeguard this protection.
>
>  I will send a message to the Mage's Tower with my revelations, to put their minds at ease. Perhaps you would be willing to share what we've learned with the Kaldorei in Stormwind? I would go myself, but I feel myself unworthy of this gift I have been granted. Even the pangs of addiction have not stirred within me. I will need time to muse over today's revelations before I am fit to meet with such esteemed personages.
>
>  Before you go, know that my ritual revealed to me hidden stores of corrupted artifacts that I would not burden you with. I shall consult with Stormwind's magi on an appropriate next step. However, in the moment I felt Elune's grace, I envisioned a gift I believe she would have you claim. Travel to Brightwood Grove, and at the arch, it will be bestowed upon you."
>
>  *Archmage Valthoriel closes his eyes as the moonlight around him fades and he stands trembling like a newborn babe, overwhelmed with gratitude and relief for this momentary reprieve from the pain of magical addiction.*
>
>  **The Player travels to the entrance of Brightwood Grove, where a statuette of Elune will stand in the middle of the road.**
>
>  Dialogue: "As you approach the statuette of the goddess, the selfsame tranquility that so moved the archmage soothes your mind and eases your pain and fatigue. Placing your hands upon the cold stone, you feel a distant sense of approval, and although it may be day (it's hard to know for certain in Duskwood), the moon shines above."

> **Kefke wrote: Mon Feb 06, 2023 7:54 pm**
> This is a really cool and flavourful questline, but I feel like the second stage items are pretty overtuned for the levels you'd be getting them at.

If I'm honest, I'm not really that great at balancing as far as concepts go. I figured ranks 1 for the blessings wouldn't be too great at higher levels. Admittedly a trinket that can remove three types of debuffs every 5 minutes might be good at any level. Honestly, I threw in the rewards as ideas of what they could be, but I'd be happy for people who actually do balancing to decide what they give as far as stats go, or do as far as use effects.

With the amulet, I just wanted something that every class could benefit from, so +1 to all stats felt like it'd balance itself. The repeat-use item for a food and drink buff might be a stage too high for food effects? I imagine it'd still get phased out by mid to late thirties, if not early fourties.

As an added thought, a similar series of quests/rewards for the Horde could probably be done making use of a Goblin running a salvaging or treasure hunting business?
