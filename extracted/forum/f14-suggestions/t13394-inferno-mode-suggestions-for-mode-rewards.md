# inferno mode Suggestions for mode rewards

## Post 90176 by Linkewow — 2024-03-14T17:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90176#p90176 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are no immortal warhorses, There is no title exclusive to Hell Mode, There is only one death pendant, and the reward is too small for players who prefer Hell mode.
Moreover, the Death Pendant occupies a position in the accessory slot, which occupies the Purgatory player and does not have more options to wear the accessory.
My suggestion would be to change the Death Pendant to the reward for the Guild Robes slot, which is a unique reward.
This frees Purgatory players from the trouble of the cosmetic slot.
Death is an inevitable issue for Purgatory Challengers, and some rewards won't affect the normal game balance
Hell players should have a unique title of their own, which will motivate more players to participate in Hell mode.
If this small suggestion can be realized, it will inspire more enthusiasm among current Hell Mode players.
It will also make more players take on Purgatory mode
This is also what makes Turtle WOW unique
Death is the eternity of the Hell Mode player character, and I hope that the Administrator will give more rewards
Maybe this small reward may bring more changes to the game ecology.
I have six characters in Hell mode and three have died, which is my love for Purgatory mode
It's also a love for Turtle WOW
Kudos to the management of Turtle WOW
