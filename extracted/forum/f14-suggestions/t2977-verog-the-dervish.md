Hey everyone! I can see that you're facing some difficulties. I agree that it's not very clear what to do in this quest. It was actually developed back in 2004 for the classic version of the game, when it was still common to read the quests and there were no addons telling you where to go and what to do.

Now, let's get to the main issue. The quest description mentions that you need to kill centaurs around the tent. However, the quest creators forgot to mention a crucial detail. Not all centaurs will work for this quest. Only the Kolkar Bloodcharger can summon Verog. So, you'll need to eliminate around 15 of these centaurs in order for one of them to summon Verog.
