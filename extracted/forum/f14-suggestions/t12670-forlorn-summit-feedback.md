# Forlorn Summit feedback

## Post 84446 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-12T20:57:00Z

Hi,

So I just ran through the new EPL area, Forlorn Summit. Here is my feedback after having done all the quests:

The area is well-made, good job, really! A nice change without the blight that is in the rest of EPL, while still keeping the feeling relevant to the area.

 -The hammer quest needs to have a lower cooldown. Right now, we are waiting 5x5 minutes ( think it is 5 minutes cooldown) in that spider cave just for that quest. It makes no sense. Set the CD to 5 seconds.

 -The elite quest requires you to kill quite strong elites - primarily the one in the mage tower. The rewards are abysmal in comparison. Please consider upping them - maybe even making them blues.

 -The entrance to the area is too annoying to get to from the southern side of EPL. Please do something to add a ramp there. It is especially annoying when you ghost run.

That's all, thank you!
