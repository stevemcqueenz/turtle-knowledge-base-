# World Bosses

## Post 39409 by Original3 — 2023-03-28T09:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39409#p39409 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there!

I play on this server for few years already, and I can say, that this population boom Turtle has expirienced has one big disadvantage - world bosses being hard to catch and kill.

I am personally trying to kill Kazaak for few months already, but whenever I log on my Kazaak spying account I either see his corpse or not at all. He is probably getting killed during NA times, when I am asleep.

Now my question is - If population got higher, shouldn't respawns go higher as well, to not end up as official Classic servers?

My suggestion is to make Lord Kazaak, Dark Reaver of Kharazan, Azuregos and Nerubian Overseer having 3 days respawn timer, instead of 5 - 7 days.

My other idea, to prevent even more camping, is a world boss cooldown for a player. It would simply work like this: I kill Lord Kazaak and I get 7 day long debuff called "Mark of Lord Kazaak" that would make me take 300% more damage from all his abilities, or simply prevent me from attacking him at all.

I hope other people will agree with me and add other suggestions, how to fix the issue with world bosses.

Take good cares! - Altgf

## Post 39414 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-28T10:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39414#p39414 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

World bosses by design encourage toxic gameplay. If you add a 7 day cd with half the spawn timer, it just means a 2nd group will rise up to camp it and you'll have two camping coalitions taking turns.

The fix is phase out world bosses by nerfing their loot. It is awful to make world bosses drop bis loot especially since the classes most affected by this are hybrids (The kazzak neck for rets and pants for spriests).

Dark Reaver is not camped because most of its loot is garbage, but there has to be a solution beside "its loot is good so its camped" and "its loot is bad so its ignored".

People can be encouraged to do world bosses in a way that they don't have to compete for it. Personalized quests where everyone gets to benefit is a good alternative, then once a group kills him once and finishes their quest, they stop camping him and a next group takes their place until the whole server is done with the quest.

## Post 39415 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-28T10:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39415#p39415 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Worldboss tags are currently hard to obtain, especially if you are not in a dedicated community to do them already.

Knowing when the last one dies drastically increases your chances of getting the next one as you know the approx spawntime.

The only exception to this is currently ostarius and his pedestal that is basically only up for grabs after a server reset, since people completing the quest triggers him and he will go on cd from it.

I think it would be awsome to have items that can summon the worldboss in their appropriate location. These items should cost thousands of gold to make and be consumed on use. But it would allow dedicated groups to seek out a worldboss and not have to care about other people who are also after them.

It would still be worth getting the natural spawn of the boss, but it would make it at least possible to do them and get their loot otherwise.

The loot is polarizing, but if it was more consistently obtainable, i dont see a problem with their loot being good.
