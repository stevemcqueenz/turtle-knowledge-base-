My two cents on Transmogrification, I know I'm new to the server and super late to this discussion.

I really like transmog from the role playing perspective for the following reasons:
 1) it allows all members of a guild to wear a similar uniform or outfit, which is immersive and increases cohesion/cool factor.
 2) vanilla clownsuits look silly, it's hard to take a character seriously when it looks super goofy.  "I'm Sir Elrich Caerscion, the last living member of the ancient Order of Lordaeron... gaze upon my emerald scaled capris pants and purple wasp pauldrons and gold gloves with blue trim".  I understand that this is blizzlike but I don't get the impression that the primary purpose of this server is to be an exact vanilla clone.
 3) increased customization of appearance allows characters to not be carbon copies of each other.  NPCs have individual gear sets that distinguish them, PCs should be able to do that as well.  Having every member of a class look the same except hair and face options is less immersive in my opinion.  Maintaining a look for your character would increase in-game recognition, and seeing a specific character go by in the world would have more impact than "lightforge paladin #96848"

From a server/business perspective, transmog brings the following benefits:
 1) Unlocking transmog slots for turtle tokens is a source of server income
 2) increased investment in characters encourages activity and logged in time
 3) acquiring transmog gear can be a significant time investment that gives players something to do and keeps them logged in and playing, which is important in a lower population server that lacks an active endgame raiding scene
 4) max level players hunting for transmog gear would often be moving through low level content and provide opportunities to help low level players

For those who do not wish to see transmog in the server, a simple checkbox option to disable the display of transmogrified items accommodates every reason that people have to not want transmogs.  The given reasons to not want transmogs are valid, but it would very easy to satisfy both sides of the argument.

Sorry for the novel and the necropost
