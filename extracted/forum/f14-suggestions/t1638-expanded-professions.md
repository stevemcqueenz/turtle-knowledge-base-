# Expanded Professions?

## Post 9705 by Mrhamlington — 2021-03-08T13:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9705#p9705 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, I was playing on my alt character, leveling him up slowly but surely and I picked up the two main professions I wanted, those being Blacksmithing and Enchanting. I always wanted to pair the two and it is quite fun leveling them together. Getting a new piece of gear and then being able to enchant it all by yourself is hella fun and adds a different way of playing the game.

However, as I leveled I kinda stopped and thought about something. Ever since the earliest level, I had bought those Scrolls that many of you should be familiar with (Scrolls of Strength, Stamina, Spirit, etc.). They are quite a decent help when it comes to leveling, giving you some extra stats early on to give you a proper edge, but something struck me at that moment.

As much as I loved using those scrolls, they either become redundant fast, have a short duration, are limited in stock or generally underutilized. Then I gave it a thought and I was like, why wouldn't enchanters be able to make them? I mean, think about it. You're technically inscribing a parchment of paper with magical properties to grant you some boon in the long run, effectively acting as a minor yet useful buff and It would make technically a form of sense seeing as to how enchanting doesn't necessarily need to be tied to armors and weapons. But that isn't the only thing.

As I thought about that, I kind of glanced over my inventory and took note of the ring and necklace spot and I wondered why blacksmiths wouldn't be able to craft jewelry? The molding of metal and casting it into different shapes is already something they should be familiar with and compared to the complexities of the usual crafts, I always wondered why they wouldn't be able to perhaps forge jewelry? Some sort of jewelry crafting, where perhaps weak yet efficient rings and necklaces could be made to perhaps spice up a bit of the gameplay and give a different way of approaching professions?

Though there are downsides to consider, with the necessity to make up the items and the stats as well as the factors implementation and such, but nonetheless, I thought it'd be a nice suggestion.

And of course, this isn't just for smithing or enchanting. Other professions could be expanded as well with their own little additions! Though, I can't really think of anything for them as of the moment, but still someone out there might .

Anyway, these are my two coins that I'm tossing out there. Leave your thoughts below and lets see what others think of this.

Best bet for now would be to use the custom quest/item creator and make pseudo inscription quests offered by enchanting trainers.

Players would then have to gather the necessary ingredients and upon completion be able to choose which crafted item they want.

Certain quests could be repeatable for consumable items like scrolls as you mentioned.

This could also be done for jewlcrafting, as many of the recipes from TBC use materials found in vanilla. Simple rings and necklaces using malachite, shadow gems, and tigerseye to give starting players an extra boost all the way to lv 60 mats like black diamonds.

All this takes quite a bit of work however, but if you want to get a head start, check out the custom quest/item guide on the forums.
