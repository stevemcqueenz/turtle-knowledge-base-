# Stoneclaw Totem cannot soak damage with Totemic Alignment

## Post 149098 by Justtobby32 — 2025-08-29T08:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149098#p149098 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Heyo, with the newest patch of 90% threat transfer from totems, one of the core leveling defensive cooldowns such as Stoneclaw Totem are left unusable - mainly its damage soaking function. While it was the most efficient heal per mana multitarget absorbtion cooldown (3x cheaper but 2x longer CD than Power Word: Shield), after transfering the threat, its health remains unused. Since for AoE threat buildup is Magma Totem and Fire Nova Totem suited the best, this core leveling/farming distraction tool (especially for HC characters) feels like bein neglected and could be tweaked a bit to find it's new place in the Turtle WoW.

As far as I know, Shaman Tank lacks both Taunt and Defensive CD. One of thos things could be implemented into Totemic Alignment to compensate loss of this totem's original functionality.

I would be amazed if (with Totemic Alignment) would make Stoneclaw Totem (30s CD) either:
 - Taunted 3 mobs in 10 yards radius (Righteous Defense taunts 3 mobs, has 15s CD)
 - Recived 90% of the damage taken from Shaman. (Power Word: Shield has 15s CD)
