Hello!

So ive been enyoying Demo lock quite a bit here on turtle doing some end game raiding as the spec. I must say the new changes feels alot better than i thought they would BUT the ritual of doom is such a clunky spell to use, especially in a raid setting.

You realy dont want to stop your entire raid to have them all stand around and wait for you to summon your deamon and  summoning while your Raidlead goes over strats instead risks it releasing before the fight even starts, wasting the 30 min cooldown. Just having to catch your groups attention and have them stand there while it happens goes against the flow of whatever youre doing big time and you feel kind of assholy interupting everything the way you do.

It would be nice if it didnt require other players for the summon maybe instead draining your life or something as you use it channeling your lifeforce into the ritual to keep the flavour of the summoning.

These are just my two cents. Thank you for reading :)
