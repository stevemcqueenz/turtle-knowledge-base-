# Plans for a spanish translation or mod?

## Post 81591 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-18T04:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81591#p81591 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Well, I started to think, perhaps such a large server could have a mod to translate the client into Spanish, since, even though I have been playing here for half a year and I understand English, I have
still small difficulties but I can speak and read it, I know that there are many players who, seeing that the client is in English, will simply be discouraged by the language barrier.

It will probably be a "No" since the Spanish-speaking community here is very small compared to the Chinese or English, or current players simply prefer guides or use PFQuest to do the quests.

But if in the future they decide to do it, I offer myself as support, since I have played WoW in Spanish and I know how to do the translations to maintain or avoid certain errors that may occur when translating the strange names given to certain characters.
