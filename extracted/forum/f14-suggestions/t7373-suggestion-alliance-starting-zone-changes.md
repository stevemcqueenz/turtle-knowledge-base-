I think the overpopulation is more a symptom of the larger issue that a lot of turtle changes as well as original classic design centralises alliance players around Elwynn:

- Deadmines: the extensive nature of the vanilla Deadmines quest chains, as well as Turtle's Harvest Golem questline, mean that players are incentivised to always do Westfall on every character, which means that they often do Elwynn on the way since it's simple to do and has breadcrumbs into Westfall.

- World Buffs: the Classic culture of world buffs has made Stormwind the main gathering place for the Alliance, even with WB being removed from raids. Furthermore WB's are still very strong and fun for levelling, especially with the risks of hardcore, so characters will want to train and loiter around Stormwind.

- High Elves: due to High Elves being the most popular and populous alliance race after humans, and then being deposited in Elwynn after their starting zone, massively increases the load on Elwynn.

- Impactful Quests and Utility: Elwynn is home to a number of impactful custom quests including [[Wool Would Work]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=60008) (which rewards a 17 armor cloak at level 6) as well as [[Matter of Trust]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=60070) (giving access to a short duration free move gryphon flight), on top of being the home of the Darkmoon Faire and its essential mount quest. Furthermore, Goldshire, Lakeshire, and Sentinal Hill offer rentable mounts which give a nice boost to travel convenience in these low level zones which isn't then available in Kharanos, Dolanaar, Auberdine, or Thelsamar. Furthermore, the location of the [Fashionista](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=60030) quests and the [High Elf dailies](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80252) in/accessed from Stormwind, incentivises players to remain around Stormwind.

- Ore: although OP mentioned there being a lack of ore in Elwynn due to the overpopulation, this is actually a bigger issue because Teldrassil (for obvious reasons) doesn't have any ore spawns in the first place - this means any Night Elf miners need to go to the Eastern Kingdoms if they're not high enough to start Darkshore, and with them going directly to SW now with the boat changes, it makes more sense to go to Elwynn than to travel all the way to Dun Morogh.

- Tents: all these compound together to mean that tents are then placed *consistently* in Goldshire due to the high population, further increasing said population as even players who wanted to level in other zones have to do a pilgrimage to Goldshire for their rested xp. Additionally, the higher level nature of survival means that even the changes made matter little to the players questing in Elwynn and Westfall since they don't have access to their own tents, and higher level players aren't frequenting those areas.
Now few of these are actually bad things on their own, but they all build onto eachother to make Elwynn really by far the most viable place to level on Alliance.

I don't think these things should be nerfed at all since they're great fun, but I do feel a lot of the convenience needs to be extended to the other starting zones. I would suggest the following changes as a starting point at least:

- Have Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer drop in all capital cities, similar to how Warchief's Blessing drops in both Orgrimmar and The Crossroads.

- Have High Elves dropped in Stormwind itself, rather than Elwynn, with breadcrumb quests to lead them to the different starting zones, similar to the level 15 high elf quest [[A Diplomatic Mission]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40371).

- Have the High Elf zone be easily accessible from other locations/cities, be that by portal, boat, flight point etc. I would say just portals in every capital would work, but that would mean you could just use Alah'thalas as a portal room to fast travel across the world, which I don't like.

- Add mining nodes and a mining trainer to Teldrassil. I know it doesn't make much sense but there are already some rocks in the tree, and you could add the trainer to the dwarven archaelogists next to the temple - I honestly think the benefits outweigh a shaky lore explanation.

- Add more impactful quests to the other starter zones. The new Gnomish Reclamation Facility in Dun Morogh is a great start so long as it has easily accessible good quest rewards. Its less central location does make it less viable as a high footfall location though. I would like to say at least an equivalent to the cloak quest and a pet quest would be nice.

- Add rentable rams and nightsabers to the other early zones - especially Darkshore, despite its questing efficiency, is an *enormous* zone for its level and population.
I'll probably add more stuff to this post as I think of it. But should be good as a start to help alleviate the pressure on Elwynn - with its fame, as well as Deadmines though it'll always be a very popular zone, however if we can increase the viability of other starter zones, it should prevent it from being the de facto only alliance starting zone.

Let me know if you have any further ideas, or Horde ideas as I am less aware of the starter zone issues there - I know it's all centred around the Barrens, but it's a big enough zone at least to support it, especially with being a higher level zone.
