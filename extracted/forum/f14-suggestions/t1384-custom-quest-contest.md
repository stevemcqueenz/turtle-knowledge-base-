# Custom Quest Contest

## Post 8459 by Sandtusk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-11-14T23:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8459#p8459 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the recent art contest coming to a close (great submissions btw) I was wondering if something like that could be used to generate more custom quests? Below I have a rough guideline to direct things:

 1) The devs point out a zone/faction/dungeon that could use a bit more creative touches.
 - Regulations on items created or quest lengths can be controlled here.
 - Restrict the complexity of quests to prevent any overlapping.
 - Restrict the number of entries to a single quest per player.
 - Regulations on class/race/profession specific quests can be controlled here.

 2) Players would create quests/items/npcs for those areas.
 -  How far they take the quests will depend on the devs guidelines.

 3) The players whos quests are ultimately chosen to be implemented would receive a modest prize.
 - There can be a grand prize for the best one.
 - Personally I'd allow a minimum prize to at least encourage players to experiment with the quest creator (within reason of minimum effort).

 **I'm not sure exactly how tough it is to implement a single custom quest into the game**. With the expansion coming up I'd doubt anything like this will be possible soon; just wanted to gauge interest.

Considering how many people have gone through the "road to 60" multiple times, imo the custom content is easily the bread and butter of Turtle WoW advertising(this upcoming expansion speaks for itself). I also selfishly enjoy reading all the custom flare creators put into their quests and would love some more!
