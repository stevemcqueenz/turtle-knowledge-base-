Rather than respond with one word zingers, I'll explain to you why this suggestion wouldn't work for turtle wow.

First, turtle wow is a vanilla server, so the cost of mounts won't be changed. How much is costs now is how much it did on patch 1.12.1

Second, increasing drop rates because the leveling is "slower" goes against the server's ideology . The point of turtle wow is to take it slow to experience more of the pve content. Increased rates for gold and item drops goes against this mentality.

Additionally, the exp rate is now 1x instead of the previous .5x and later .6-.9x, while keeping the improvements like tents and full exp from quests for 25 levels, in addition to the recent addition of rested exp from campfires. It's no longer a slow server, it's faster than the real game.

As Mac stated, there's several quality of life changes for monetary value as well as reduced competition on farming. Of course, this comes with the trade-off that goods sold to other players are not as profitable, but you'll still earn more than enough just vendoring things you loot.

Right now is a great time to earn money as a gatherer with the Ahn'qiraj war effort going on, but even afterward skinning is a great supplement to income. Each skinnable mob adds up to 5s to loot value, assuming you can't find any use for it.
