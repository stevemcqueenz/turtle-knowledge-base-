# Baby murlocs lost their voices

## Post 146290 by Hemovalenciatw — 2025-08-16T21:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146290#p146290 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm not sure if it's just a bug from the new update or an intentional removal, but i and many others adore the singing of gurky and terky. i know not everyone does.

i personally, upon hearing that terky and the murloc illusion were gonna be removed this update, grinded out my slow and steady character from level 20 to level 50 just to unlock them before the update, starting last weekend and finishing on thurs night. i did multiple all nighters to achieve this, and enjoying terky's little singing for the remainder of thurs evening. then came the update and terky was mute.

can we get verification if this was intentionally removed? the change logs and patch notes make no mention of it whatsoever.

if so, i petition for it to be reimplemented and have a suggestion that would appease everyone.

add in an option in audio settings to be able to turn off baby murloc singing.

that way, those of us that love it can still hear it, while those of us that don't, can opt out of hearing it. everyone is happy. there was already an addon to do just that, for those that didn't want it.
