# Protection Paladin buffs/fixes

## Post 53335 by Getplucked (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-13T17:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53335#p53335 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After playing Prot for a while here and talking with various other Paladin players in the discord, I'd like to suggest a few fixes to some glaring issues Prot Paladins face:

 **Lower defenses**
Warrior has a passive -10% damage taken on Defensive Stance, and much higher base health. Feral Druids have massive armor bonuses and a large health bonus, as well as high dodge. Paladin has....nothing similar. This results in Paladin just taking more damage than the other tanks, and I've noticed this myself. Tanking dungeons, often times I just get smacked hard by most trash packs and bosses. But when I heal warrior or druid tanks, they seem to take very little damage in comparison.

I see with the upcoming Libram that gives -5% damage taken when standing in Consecrate, the devs are trying to remedy this, but I'd rather not fix class balance issues with equipment. Since that not only causes certain items to be "forever bis", but also makes it so you only get those class problems fixed when you hit max level and get that item to drop.

Instead, just change Improved Righteous Fury to also reduce damage taken by 3/6/9% while Righteous Fury is active. Easy enough.

 **Taunt being clunky and awkwardly designed**
Warrior has a regular taunt spell and it's off the GCD. It also has Mocking Blow and Challenging Shout.
Druid has a regular taunt spell and it's off the GCD. It also has Challenging Roar.
Paladin's taunt is on the GCD, requires 3 points in a talent, overwrites whatever judgement you had, and also prevents you from using judgement again for the next 8-10 seconds. It does not have any other taunts.

Why can't Paladin just have a regular taunt spell? It's literally [already in the game](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=45806) but can only be accessed by putting 3 points into the talent and using Judgement of Justice.

 **Ardent Defender being pretty useless**
Yeah, -25% damage taken below 35% health isn't really useful in any serious content. If you're below 35% health, chances are you're going to die anyway and -25% damage taken is not going to save you. It's only real use is farming lower level content or things like lasher farming, where you can safely go below 35% health and not die in 1-2 hits. In actual dungeon/raid tanking, it's not very useful at all, especially for something that is a 31-point talent. As it is right now, almost nobody takes it and the most popular Prot spec is 0/21/30.

My suggestion would be to either rework/buff this talent or remove it entirely and replace it with something else. I would turn it into a mini-Shieldwall, something like -30% damage taken for 15 seconds, on a 10 minute CD. Something powerful enough to justify being a 31 point talent. Some kind of cooldown that you can pop when shit hits the fan. Prot Paladin doesn't really have any "cooldowns" like this as it is, since bubble gets mobs off you and LoH is an hour cooldown for a single heal.

On the issue of Pal's damage reduction, I take this opportunity to raise a more serious problem: the design of turtle wow, more and more lost the balance between DPS and survival, whether PVP or PVE. After a period of experience, I found that for each character, DPS build is doing multiplication, while survival build is doing addition, although it seems that turtle designer has improved the survival ability of many character. However, the promotion efficiency of DPS is much higher than that of Tank survival. This will ruin the original fun of WOW 60 vanilla.

This will lead the following results:
 1 Only absolute defense will be accepted and sought after; Things like Holy Shield, instant displacement, stealth, fridge, DPS at super distance or healing.
 2 A lot of talent investment in survival direction, skill selection, will be abandoned because of low efficiency; Like the Ardent Defender.
 3 The primary choice of all character in PVP is how to knock down opponents, not how to protect yourself and your teammates, because there is no point.
 4 Many survival oriented taste will be abandoned.

The above situation will become more and more intense. The Turtle design team finally added the critical reduction in 1.16, but this still does not solve the fundamental problem.
In fact, the absolute balance between DPS and survival is impossible to achieve. But the original WOW 60 Vanilla gave a solution, only to later deviate from the original direction: class suppress: the tank suppress melee DPS, the melee DPS suppress spell DPS, and the spell DPS suppress Tank. Alternatively, plate and Mail suppress leather, leather suppress cloth, cloth suppress plate and mail. In the design of equipment, plate favors survival, mail compromises, leather favors melee DPS, cloth favors AP, and the talent investment amplifies the advantages of armor types to achieve class suppress and dynamic balance between classes.
