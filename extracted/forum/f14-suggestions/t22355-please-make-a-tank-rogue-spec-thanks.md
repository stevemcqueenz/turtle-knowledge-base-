Made a theorycraft for it.

<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/4CKzBpYzwd>

Note that I made no real balancing here, no idea how this would affect PVE/PVP, just wanted to give some base tools for rogue tanking without choosing the way of evasion going brr.

For the idea I assumed Agitating Poison is available early since you need that to tank as a rogue.

 **The ideas behind the changes:**
 **Vitality:** New talent. I'll mess with heals scaling from max HP so I wanted to raise rogue HP. Didn't wanna directly power creep Hunter's Survivalist talent so you get less HP bonus but you also get increased healing received, one of the foundations of this build.

 **Lightning Reflexes:** Added instant HP recharge after dodges. Wanted to give a small base percentual amount to not break PVP. But this + vitality would get you some real healing over time. PVP-er probably wouldn't spend 5TP in Vitality so I feel safe there.

 **Deflection:** Parrying reducing spell damage received for 10 sec, stackable. Rogue has high evasion against melee but wanted to give them something interesting against casters. I don't think it affects PVP, against a caster you don't really parry usually.

 **Riposte:** Added a threat modifier (imagine it as an Earth Shock) and an insta-heal equal to your Agility. It's in flavor of what I try to do: giving a passive healing toolkit to rogue which depends on melee evasion.

 **Interruptive Blows:** Also gave kick a 50/100% to taunt. It has a 10 sec CD anyway, thematically fits, we have the taunt target. I don't think any PVE DPS would take this talent anyway.

 *2 new deeper talents:*
 **Agitating Protection:** Reduced damage if agitating poison is active on any of the weapons.

 **Licence to Stay Alive:** New capstone talent, giving a passive defensive to rogue tank since HP will fluctuate a lot.

 **What we get:**
Kinda squishy tank with some added bulk (10% reduction with threat poison and parrying making spells more bearable) with the strategy to abuse melee mobs for insta heals and spell resistance buildup.
Blade flurry would take care of multitarget threat if needed.
We have a def CD with evasion which also heals you and an emergency 'bubble' every 20 sec.
The build likes HoT since each tick heals more than usual.

I didn't make the build raid-tank capable.
That would have meant adding a bunch of armor to rogue and I think that would have backfried really ugly in PVP.

First, clarify whether the role is intended as a ​​Main Tank (MT)​​ or an ​​Off-Tank​​. The original post seems to lean towards an MT role. However, an MT that relies entirely on Dodge and Parry—a form of "absolute immunity"—would be inherently ​​unreliable​​. This could place immense strain on healers and result in a ​​poor experience for the party​​.

A more robust approach would be to take inspiration from the ​​Brewmaster Monk from Mists of Pandaria​​. Its core mechanic involves ​​actively mitigating damage​​ through "Stagger," converting a portion of incoming burst damage into a Damage Over Time (DoT) effect that can be managed effectively. There's even a precedent in The Burning Crusade, where Rogues had a talent for a "Feint"-like ability to reduce incoming damage.

From a lore and class fantasy perspective, this could be adapted in a few ways:

​​1. An "Assassination" spec with a Poison theme​​: Rework the Assassination talent tree to incorporate a "poison" theme for mitigation. For instance, the Rogue could apply debilitating toxins to weaken an enemy's attacks or coat themselves in a protective venom that "stagger"s a portion of the damage taken, which could then be cleansed or negated by other abilities.

​​2. A "Combat/Outlaw" spec with a Swashbuckler/Pirate theme​​: Much like the Combat spec was reimagined as the Outlaw spec, a tanking version could be shaped around a "Buccaneer" or "Pirate" fantasy. This naturally incorporates an "alcohol" motif, directly paralleling the Brewmaster. Talents could involve drinking special brews to gain temporary shields, increase avoidance, or convert incoming damage into a manageable DoT.

​​The key is to move beyond pure RNG-based avoidance towards a system that allows for predictable and manageable damage intake, making the Rogue Tank a viable and reliable team asset
