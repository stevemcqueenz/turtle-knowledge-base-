# Gnome Starting Zone Concept

## Post 151192 by Teerack (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-10T23:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151192#p151192 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've always felt like there was no reasons the gnomes shouldn't have gotten their own zone at some point. There is crazy amount of land to the left just not used at all in game. I went ahead and just flattened it out and made a zone for them to start in. I also went ahead and made the channel island above the zone so they could also have a new 10-20 level zone. Lore wise this all still makes sense since the gnome lore is they mostly stuck to their own territories. I also like the idea of the gnomes being a bit more of a coast fairing race which I think was a dropped concept from blizzard. In the old games you did encounter gnomish submarines in the channel islands and in retail when they finally added Kul Tira I think they added gnomes and Mechagon close by because they were trying to honor the original lore location of Kul Tira that is very close to Gnomeregan. I think this also helps build up more the rivalry between the goblins and gnomes.

Gnome Region

Invention Coast (1-10)
---

- **Adversity Valley** - You start here from 1-5 dealing with troggs, wild life, and helping refugees from gnomeregan.
- **Voyager Research Site** - Still part of Adversity Valley you would help with some science experiments.
- **The Leper Front** - Would be a bit of a warzone like area with gnomes and leper gnomes fighting.
- **Fallout Site** -  Would be a toxic irradiated place full of radioactive troggs and slimes and would have a backdoor to Gnomeregan.
- **Gnomeregan Project 3** - Would be the first project in the gnomeregan series meant to a great underwater city for research with a tunnel entrance on the coast that takes you down into it. It has been put on hold after the Gnomeregan Prime Disaster. You would go and help plug leaks, deal with naga and maukara and stuff messing up the city.
- **Ruins of Nor'thalas** - An underwater elf ruin full of naga for quests.
- **Discovery Port** - A decent sized gnome town similar in scale to something like Booty Bay or Gadgetzan.
- **Sparkfin Coast** - Shore with some Murlocs for quests.
- **Frostmane Stand** - The Frostmade having made a town on the edge of their homelands for quests.
- **The Coastal Express Tram** - Tram that runs from ghost to ghost easter kingdoms connecting the Invention Coast to the gnomes current capital city.
The Channel Islands (10-20)
---

- **Imagination Submarine Factory** - A big factory where they make all of the gnome's submarines.
- **Dark Thought Passage** - Spooky dark tunnel allowing access into Dun Morogh.
- **Dun Ladur** - A dwarf outpost set up to protect the entrance into Dun Morogh.
- **Innovation Port** - Small port that connects with Corner Stone in Crestfall.
- **CornerStone** - Small Gnome town on the bottom of Crestfall as the gnomes attempt to claim the island.
- **Crestfall** - Was originally a territory of Kul Tira that got obliterated by the horde in the second war. It's now covered in ruins and overrun by pirates that have make the ruins their base.
- **Zul'Dare** - A mountainous group of islands where the horde has set up  a small camp in the north and the alliance on the bottom.
- **Frostmane Retreat** - An group of Frostmane that were tired of the slow genocide at the hands of the dwarfs felt their homeland and settled on the island of Zul'Dare. They have a small town there and have agreed to join the horde. They will mostly be used for alliance quests, but the horde would also have a quest line to recruit them into the horde just like we saw in Sandfury Village.
- **Tol'Barad -** A territory of Arathor that got attacked by the horde in the second war now covered in angry spirits from all the killed humans.
- **Defiant Keep** - Some humans still hold out refusing to give up on their lands.
- **Defiled Rock** - A small Forsaken outpost scouting the islands.
Gnomeregan Project 2 (Capital)
---

- **Gnomeregan Project 2** - The second great city in the gnomeregan series. While Gnomeregan prime was built underground this city was built on the peaks of mountains for study on the wealthier. It has a focus on flying machine development as well while gnommergann project 3 would have had a focus on sea craft development if it had even been finished.
- **Coastal Express Tram** - The tram that connects the capital to the invention Coast.
- **Creativity Shipyard and Airport** - The place the gnomes build all their aircraft.
