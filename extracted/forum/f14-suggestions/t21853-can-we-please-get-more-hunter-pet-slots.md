# Can We Please Get More Hunter Pet Slots?

## Post 151445 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-12T14:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151445#p151445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I want to make a case for more hunter pet slots, because the current system feels way too restrictive given all the new pets Turtle WoW has added, especially the ones tuned for PvP.

Quick story: I’m a priest, and like many priests, I live in mana hell during arenas. The other day I got paired with a Horde hunter (yep, enemy faction!) who had the mana regen pet. And guess what? I was living my best life, mana bar actually staying blue instead of gasping empty. For once, I felt like a functional caster instead of a glorified wand machine.

Afterwards, I told a friend of mine (another hunter) about it, and he was super hyped to try it out. But here’s the problem: he can’t. He doesn’t have the stable space unless he deletes another pet he cares about. That kills the excitement. Why introduce so many cool, specialized pets if players are forced to choose between them or delete their old companions?

A few reasons more stable slots would help:

 **PvP and PvE variety:** Hunters need different pets for different roles. Right now, PvP pets often come at the expense of PvE staples.

 **Class fantasy:** Hunters are supposed to be masters of beasts. Three or four slots doesn’t feel like mastery, it feels like rationing.

 **Balance isn’t affected:** More slots doesn’t let hunters use more pets at once, it just lets them enjoy and experiment with the content the devs already made.

Even just doubling the slots would go a long way. Hunters shouldn’t have to delete beloved pets just to try out the new PvP options. Let us enjoy the variety.

What do you all think, should hunters finally get more pet space?
