# Legion HD models toggle

## Post 106940 by Yonu — 2024-11-07T17:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106940#p106940 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I need to start this suggestion with a disclaimer, I only imagine a change like this to happen after we get our hands on the UETwow client.
Like the title suggests, it would be amazing if we had an option to either pick Vanilla models/textures or Legion models/textures, both being supported by the devs themselves.
Everyone is aware that the TWoW HD project is not official, and whenever we find issues (which is fairly often), we're always directed by the staff to go ask for solutions to the one's managing that project, which doesn't usually get solved.
This all sounds like extra work for the devs but, without knowing the numbers, I strongly believe that a good percentage of the playerbase prefers the new models over the vanilla ones, so having a chance to pick between both options would be yet another selling point for the server.

I hope you consider this, it's currently the only thing that bothers me about TWoW, other than that I'm very happy with the project, and I'm really looking forward for the future!
