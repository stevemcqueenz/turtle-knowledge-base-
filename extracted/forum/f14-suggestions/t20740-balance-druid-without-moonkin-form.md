# Balance druid without moonkin form

## Post 143808 by Cheruscan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-08-08T10:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143808#p143808 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am exited to see the change to Bloom of Aessina in the patchnotes, finally I will be able to play a druid without mandatory shapeshifting.

I would love to see balance get similar treatment, I love the fantasy of a nature "mage" , moonkin looks silly and out of place to me, druids in WC3 could only turn into Bears and birds.

People can ofc play however they like and I dont want to force moonkin form out of the game at all, I just personally dont like it.

Maybe it would it be possible to add a talent to balance that provides a similar lvl of impact as moonkin form? But ofc it would be disabled if a the player actually uses moonkin form and thus be useless to a druid that uses the form.

Druid already gets 2 distinct playstyles contained within the feral tree, the same could be argued for restoration tree after the patch hits, since healing touch cant be used in treeform yet we get talents to buff that spell specifically also in that talent tree. The change to Aessina just makes it actually viable rn, especially if you have a druid in treeform in your raid to partner up with.

A secondary balance playstyle could also work in a similar manner, boosting moonkins dmg further at the expense of your own for instance.

Maybe others have better suggestions how it could work :)
