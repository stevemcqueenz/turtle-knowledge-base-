# Nerf Searing Light

## Post 128716 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-27T14:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128716#p128716 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/bD9WptO.png)

Searing Light should be nerfed. This talent in Priest's Discipline tree makes this class extremely bursty in PvP.
It works perfectly fine in PvE though, let me explain why it is destructive in PvP.

Let's say usual Smite deals 100% damage.
Crit deals 150% damage.

That means with Searing Light talent, your usual Smite hits enemies for 100% damage and if your Smite crits, you will deal 250% damage instantly. Current limit of Searing Light happening only once every 3 seconds doesn't change much.

But not only that.
Your second instant Smite can also crit which enhances our Smite combo with a bit of luck to deal 300% damage instantly.

But how much is 250% and 300% damage? Naxx geared Disc priest with Inner Fire on, deals 1100-1200 Holy damage per Smite.
So 250% corresponds to 2750-3000 Holy damage.
And 300% corresponds to 3300-3600 Holy damage.

But not only that.
Disc priests are privileged over other casters since Holy Resistance doesn't exist. And only 3% spell hit is required against level 60 players to achieve spell hit cap. That means assuming your target doesn't have any extra defense, it has 99% chance of receiving full damage and 1% of resisting.

But not only that.
There are some trinkets like [Zandalarian Hero Charm](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19950) that give massive spell damage boost for a short time. That means your combo will deal even more damage.

But not only that.
Searing Light can proc after Holy Fire, too. Since Holy Fire always deals more damage than Smite, this combo can be even more powerful.

But not only that.
Disc priest has 2 other proccing effects from talents that increase damage done by spells by flat %.
Purifying Flames increase Holy damage done by priest by 12% for next 10 seconds after casting Holy Fire.
Enlighten (casted on yourself) increases spell damage by 15% for 8 seconds.
That means, you combo can be enhanced even more.

Maybe this combo isn't as much visible in PvE when you hit the boss that has massive health instantly for more than 4k. But in PvP, it's very VERY visible. 4k damage is enough to one-shot even well-geared priests/mages. Not to mention fresh 60s.

If you ever decide to nerf Searing Light, buff the Disc priest somehow to properly balance the game. This change is needed for PvP reasons, not PvE ones so keep that in mind.
