I wouldn't call it a niche, per se, however, it would change things up a bit. From what I understand way back when this new patch was under development and the class changes were being talked about, one of the things they said about warlocks was something along the lines of us being shadow bolt spammers, similar to frost mages in MC/BWL. Well here's another spell we could use instead of Shadow Bolt. As for the class changes:

You have a new talent for Soul Fire that makes it a lot more beneficial, not to mention at the same time it refunds the requisite soul shard.

You have a new version of Conflagrate that makes it worth using as well.

You have a new version of Searing Pain which allows it to be used exponentially more than it's ever been used.

You have new versions of all these warlock fire spells, why not a new fire version of Shadow Bolt that will not only complement the aforementioned fire spells, but also benefit from Emberstorm and Improved Scorch brought by any fire mages in your party/raid?

And all that is ignoring the potential benefits for tanking Twin Emps with improved Wrathstones increasing fire spell threat by, what is  it, 60%?
