# Class Changes 3

## Post 161128 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-11-30T20:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161128#p161128 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Maybe it's unpopular opinion but I think that CC3 should be different than CC1 and CC2.
Instead of buffing weak specs to the level of best specs, CC3 should be mainly about harsh nerfs to almost every spec.
I say it mainly from PvP perspective. Here is why.

First, let's examine what is the most noticeable result of all class changes made by Turtle in PvP:

- Damage output of (perhaps) every spec was significantly increased.

Meanwhile, the most important defensive stat in PvP - Stamina - was barely touched at all. Mainly because CC don't affect gear. And health of every player comes from Stamina which can be increased mostly by itemization.

As an example, my spec in Vanilla was a meme in terms of doing damage in PvP. After CC2 the same spec could deal approximately 1.5x more damage. Stamina remained the same.

What is the result of increasing damage while keeping source of Stamina low? Burst.
It was obvious and became noticeable problem after CC2. It forced Turtle to address the issue so we got % damage dealt reduction debuff in battlegrounds.
Even in PvE, all mobs had to receive extra HP because of how CC2 buffed everything.
It wasn't the solution to burst and increased dps problem. The source were class changes.

CC3 might come in the future. They should have different philosophy than CC2. No more buffs to everyone but harsh nerfs are necessary. If you don't adopt this, you will be forced to admit your mistake as happened with CC2 when you introduced band-aid solutions: BG debuff and general buff to all NPCs.

It's unpopular opinion but I think it is necessary. If you decrease damage output of every spec, players probably won't like it but results will be good both in PvE and PvP. Burst will no longer be a problem.
