# How to save PVP on TWOW - revising PVP servers' mechanism, enabling cross-server battlegrounds and character transfer

## Post 107196 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-09T06:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107196#p107196 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As decribed in the title, the PVP servers should be made more attractive if the current PVE-based mechanism in Nordanaar server has limitation. Meanwhile, the BGs should include cross-server players, so chances of joining a BG will be less rare.

Additonally, the honor and gear reward from PVP need a boost/balancing when tier 3.5 leveled gears are approachable to proportionally more players.

PS. I'd like to add that deceased hardcore characters could be given a chance to be transfered to a PvP server (at the moment when that PvP server is of a low population), which is also a compensation to hardcore players who have devoted time but lost their hardcore characters.

Also, cross-server/realm transfering for characters sounds logically worth implementation when relevant servers/realms are mature and in need of balancing. In this case, potential PvP players, who may have completed their achievements and challenges, such as unlocking the Immortal title, might eventually consider moving to a PvP server and play as what they did in Blizzard's original game.
Making new gears t3+ isn't a sustainable move to the game.
