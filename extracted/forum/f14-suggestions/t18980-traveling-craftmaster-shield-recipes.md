<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131277#p131277 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I appreciate the perspectives shared here, and I'd like to offer some thoughts on the Traveling Craftsman challenge.

The challenge, in its current form, seems to lack depth and integration with the world of Azeroth. While the idea of self-reliance is compelling, the execution feels more like an underdeveloped concept that's been maintained for the sake of difficulty rather than thoughtful design.

For instance, the scarcity of craftable shields poses a significant hurdle for players aiming to tank. As highlighted by Durdund, there's only one shield available at level 10 and another at level 60, leaving a vast gap in between. This limitation doesn't just make the challenge harder; it makes it less viable for certain playstyles

These kinds of improvements wouldn’t impact other players in any negative way—unlike challenges that affect creature spawns, like the boar situation mentioned earlier. The lack of shield recipes isn't a clever design choice; it's more accurately a flaw or oversight in the profession system. It simply makes no sense that trained blacksmiths wouldn’t know how to craft basic shields throughout their journey.
