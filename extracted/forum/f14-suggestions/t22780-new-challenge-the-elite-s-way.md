Even if normal mobs still granted XP (even at a reduced rate), what’s to stop players from just farming those and skipping elites entirely? The only way this would really work is if all XP came strictly from elite mobs.

I get the idea, and the title fits, but it feels pretty flawed in execution. If XP only comes from elites, most players would just grind dungeons — and that opens the door for people to bring in a high-level carry to farm XP fast. That kind of defeats the purpose of the challenge and turns it into a shortcut to a title rather than an actual test of skill.

You could, in theory, eliminate XP gains from dungeons or raids, but then you run into another problem: most elites are found in dungeons. So either people lose interest in the challenge entirely, or they’ll find a way to tag farm with a high-level toon — again, defeating the whole purpose of the challenge.
