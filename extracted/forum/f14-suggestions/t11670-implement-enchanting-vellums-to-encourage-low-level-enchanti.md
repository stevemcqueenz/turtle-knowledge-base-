# Implement Enchanting Vellums to Encourage low level enchanting

## Post 77087 by Mechsoap — 2023-11-12T20:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77087#p77087 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Right now, most enchants that are not the best enchants are merely a means to an end in terms of levelling your enchanting skill. as far as i know, i might be wrong on this one, there is also no item level cap for what enchants can go on what.

This creates a situation where low level players do not bother with getting enchantments, because of the inconvenience and opaque costs of it, and those who do get it may be alts who are getting great enchants on low level weapons for the sake of powerlevelling.

I think enchanting scrolls/vellums would make enchanting not merely an end-game obsession that you put on BIS items, but something that is a natural part of the levelling experience, and where new players who pick up enchanting don't come to regreet it when they find out its only useful for enchanting your own gear, or DE items to sell to peopel who are levelling their enchanting.

I know it might cut into the potential profits of some enchanters, as they do not get the tip associated with it, but as it stands enchanting is one of the least useful money makers, as you're only selling to other enchanters things you can DE at lvl 1, and its also something that provides neglible player power.
it would likewise be nice to not have people set up ''storefronts'' in yell or tradechat as the only real means of making money with enchanting.

Belt buckles in world chat are sold:
 10 int 68g
 6 agility 20g (mats cost 12s 25c + 15s 58c * 8 + 55s 20c * 2 + 12s 53c = 2g 59s 82c)
 4 resistances 20g
 25 armor penetration 20g (mats cost 12s 25c + 7s 81c * 8 + 5s 12c * 2 = 84s 97c)
(Arcanite ones are not sold in world chat)
It this guy spam this ad, then he probably have some demand for those prices.
You probably would ask a friend to make buckles for you. But in that case it would not be cheaper than AH materials anyway.

My point is: if you introduce AH competition, then price will go down because more players will participate this competition and they flood the market.

Something should be done to prevent flooding market with this stuff.
Maybe those scrolls should be unique. Maybe their craft should have a cooldown.

Maybe we could have several tiers of vellums, ones that can accept enchants up to lvl 35, ones for over lvl 35 and expensive ones for endgame enchants.

Vellums could be crafted by enchanters themselves using cloths and herbs (or even fish lol) for inks, with the endgame ones requiring felcloth to inflate their price.

The "social" aspect of enchanting is not a very fulfilling one anyway, and I think there will still be people that will buy mats themselves and look for an enchanter instead of buying pre-enchanted vellums, especially if the price of endgame vellums inflates their price on AH.
