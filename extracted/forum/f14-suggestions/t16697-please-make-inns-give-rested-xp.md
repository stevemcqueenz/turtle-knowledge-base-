# Please make inns give rested XP

## Post 114055 by crashloko — 2024-12-23T18:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114055#p114055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It just makes more sense and feels natural. I don't understand why we have to take a 5 minute gryphon trip just to join a deathball outside Stormwind for 10 minutes then go back to the other side of the map and keep leveling (doesn't give you the feel of your character "resting" at all). It would make more sense for us to be able to rest at the nearest inn for 15 minutes perhaps. Inns are a much more immersive place to RP and socialize with other players. Hanging out at inns just feels like you're interacting with the world.
Maybe buff tents somehow as a compensation? Idk. I know a lot of people suggest this, but I feel like this bears repeating over and over. Bundling up inside tents is the only thing that makes me feel like I'm playing a shoddy private server.
