# List of ideas

## Post 100877 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-07T22:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100877#p100877 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

The list will be a bit incoherent. I'll list things that I lack in this game which I otherwise like.

 1. Purple BoP trinket for melee/tanks/healers in late-game dungeons. Casters have Hateforge and Black Morass and honestly, hunting those items down is fun. But the same is not really given to the other roles.
Imagine f.ex. a tanking trinket in SWV for 1% which sais something like '1% hit chance, +5 defense, +2% threat'. I'd totally spam SWV for it.

 2. Making FP speed reliant on your reputation with the FP guy's location. Are you exalted with Org and you wanna start flying from Org? +100% flight speed for you.

 3. Some fashion coins at later raids. Kara10 now has them. I'd love to chase down some from places like BWL, too.

 4. Ayamiss at AQ20. Give him some interesting loot. 90% of the raids just ignore a whole boss.

 5. Same goes to Spider Girl in ZG.

 6. Also, give Hakkar an Idol. It just feels nice to do it.

 7. Allow us some pet builds for raiding which doesn't break PVP. It feels like a missing archetype (puppeteer) at raiding.

 8. Hyjal quest reward items: there are some cool ones but list is small. Would love some added options, for example a shield.

 9. Please rework the Winterspring-Hyjal FP route. You just unnecessarily fly so much in Hyjal.

 10. Allow druid water form to do fishing.

 11. Redistribute how Naxx tokens are distributed among classes (give a class next to warrior+rogue from the bulk of 4).
