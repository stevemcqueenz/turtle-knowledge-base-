# Faction Pride

## Post 148763 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-27T21:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148763#p148763 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I believe that, along with the new zones, dungeons, quests, and more, it's necessary to gradually foster faction pride, the pride of feeling happy about having chosen to belong to the faction you like or the one you chose by default. That's important to have.

What's my point? I feel that, despite the story, lore, and so on, being well-told, I don't feel that rivalry with the Horde since I'm part of the Alliance. This happened because my biggest dislike, the Blood Elves, is simply no longer there.

This race was a bad way to generate faction pride, since, to begin with, it was simply the pretty race Blizzard added to the Horde to give it more players than something that really should have been there. I realized that much of the rivalry I felt toward the faction disappeared, and I no longer find any appeal in it, and Crossfaction kills it even more.

I think this should be worked on, both at the lore, zone, quest, and race levels. I'm not talking about adding a new race, but rather adding something new that feels like it could belong to either the Alliance or the Horde, and in the end, they end up choosing one specifically and hating/rivaling the other.
Likewise, incentivizing war at the lore level, doing quests like: "Go and attack such a place, go and kill such characters, go and steal such a thing, go and claim such a place in the name of the Horde/Alliance." I think Quel'thalas will be the perfect place to start incentivizing this, since both factions are going to collide thanks to a factor that Blizzard has distroyed in TBC, We'll have the classic High Elves vs. Amani Trolls, Quel'thalas vs. Zul'Aman, Horde vs. Alliance.

We need to be proud of belonging to the Horde and the Alliance. Don't be afraid to benefit one faction or another at the lore level. Don't limit yourselves to keeping everything balanced at the lore level.

Don't follow in Blizzard's footsteps.
Make Warcraft, not Love.
