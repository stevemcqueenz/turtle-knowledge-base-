## Post 49397 by Talthos — 2023-06-09T00:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49397#p49397 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To clarify: The Ruins of Andorhal looked very different, prior to Patch 1.4

I managed to record a short video (in 2020) of what it looked like in an earlier patch client version (no mob spawns though):

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/cGcT_DaTRQE]

I also took some screenshots (in 2020) of specific sections (again, no mob spawns):

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#a/NFkhfaF]

Both the video and screenshots, (if I remember correctly; it's been a while since I dabbled in sandbox exploration), were made possible via AIO Sandbox, which can be found here: <https://github.com/barncastle/AIO-Sandbox>

Two mob types were also removed from the Ruins of Andorhal with Patch 1.4: Blood Wraiths, and Cold Wraiths.

Finally, Araj the Summoner was originally summoned via 4 summoning crystals, with 1 crystal in each of the 4 towers within the Ruins of Andorhal (I 'think' you had to destroy them all to summon Araj).

Would it be feasible to implement the pre-1.4 Ruins of Andorhal into Turtle WoW? Very few people even remember that the original iteration even existed anymore, at the very least.
