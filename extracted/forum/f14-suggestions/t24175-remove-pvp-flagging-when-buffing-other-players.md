# Remove PvP Flagging When Buffing Other Players

## Post 170121 by Nuninho32 — 2026-03-05T12:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170121#p170121 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi everyone,

I wanted to suggest a small change that I think would improve the experience for players like me who enjoy helping others while traveling around the world.

I’m the kind of player who likes to buff people I pass by — whether they’re leveling, fighting mobs, or just out in the world. It’s a small thing, but I enjoy helping other players like that. The problem is that sometimes when I buff someone who is PvP flagged, I get PvP flagged as well.

Personally, I’m not interested in PvP. I just want to help people with a quick buff and keep going on my way. Getting flagged for PvP because I tried to help someone discourages me from buffing other players at all, which feels like a shame in a game that has such a strong community spirit.

I’d like to suggest removing the PvP flag trigger when buffing other players, or at least adding some safeguard so that players who simply cast helpful buffs don’t get pulled into PvP unintentionally.

I believe this would encourage more friendly interactions between players without forcing anyone into PvP when they don’t want it.

Thanks for considering!

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170134#p170134 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I feel your plight and I agree that involuntarily getting PvP flagged sucks big time.

But I'm not sure what a sensible solution would be.
I don't think letting you cast helpful spells without flagging you is the right play. You paint an innocent picture of a roadside encounter with a friendly player. But what if your target is in combat with another player? Should you be able to sit and buff and heal them without becoming a valid target for the opponent? That seems wrong.

So if anything I would like to see a change that the spell just won't work on a flagged target. Make flagged players outside parties invalid targets for unflagged players. That way getting PvP flagged is always a conscious decision. You have to type /pvp before you can cast spells on friendly or hostile targets. That would also solve one aspect of "bluewalling" when casters can pre-cast a big spell while unflagged, as only the spell hitting the target would flag them. This is silly as well. They should be forced to flag up before casting.
