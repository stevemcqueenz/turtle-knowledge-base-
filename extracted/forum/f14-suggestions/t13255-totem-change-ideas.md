# Totem change ideas

## Post 88801 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-02-26T11:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88801#p88801 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

In this post I want to suggest some totem changes - my goal is to offer a wider-range of options to a shaman without some 'dead weights' in the spellbook while keeping balance in check (things should become more diverse but shouldn't become OP).
For non-shaman players: a shaman can summon 1 totem of each element (fire, air, earth, water) at a time. If there are several shamans in the party, they can summon totems from each element independently but totem buffs from the same totem do not stack (no double strength totem for example).
I wanna rework some totems which are underused or never used. I will not include sentry totem (imagine far sight linked to a totem) because it's a cool PVP-only utility and was never designed to be more than that.

Tranquil air totem - in no crossfaction servers it is a fine raiding totem for caster groups - it reduces the threat of party members inside an area by 20%. It has two problems though in my opinion:
 1. In dungeons it is very hard for tank to always fight right outside of the totem effect while being in range of healing and LOS. In dungeons this totem is usually skipped since it's regarded as a universal threat reduction for the party, including tanks, eventuially doing nothing (in a WF totem element).
 2. Paladin threat blessing is much better, in raids it is the way to go. Cross-faction kinda killed this totem at raid environment.

How I'd change the totem:
I don't want it to challenge the paladin blessing, it is totally fine that paladins have a finer tool in some utility. I'd change the way how threat reduction works, though. Let's say that at the place where the totem is summoned, it gives the full threat reduction and with each yard of distance, the threat reduction gets weaker by 1.
So if you stand right where the totem is, you get a full threat reduction. Casters some yards away have almost full threat reduction. Melee and tank further away barely receive any. This would mean that the totem doesn't punish you anymore if a tank just steps within it's range while it would also add some extra options to strategize - how to position in a fight.
This could also help shaman tanks. They usually use grace of air as an air totem for added dodge and melee crit. But if survavibility is not a big issue, tranquil air totem can help keeping the threat from casters (another thing shaman tanks have real problems with).
The totem still would be niche and would not be used in raids but it would receive some utility in dungeons, when there is no pala around and threat is a real issue.
Since we're talking about relative threat reduction based on the tank, I'd consider increasing the base threat reduction it gives to 30%. It would still be inferior to the pala blessing because with each yard of distance the buff is weaker and usually tank will receive some threat reduction, too.

Windwall totem - I only used it once when we somehow had a Black Morass party with 4 shamans. Unsure if it did anything, though. Windwall totem could work in a custom dungeon where we inexplicably have to pull 10 Legolas and 5 Robin Hood at each pack. It is very rare that several ranged attacker attacks the party (without spells, using a bow or a gun) and even then this totem is lackluster.

How I'd change the totem:
Would give windwall totem a ranged + spell damage reduction. It sounds a huge upgrade and in a way, it is one. However, as a shaman you always sacrifice all the other totems of a given element to use one. In this case a shaman would give up on giving WF or giving a bunch of agility to be able to reduce damage. Windwall would become a very good, situational utility totem this way.

Flametongue totem - It has two uses now.
 1. Giving a melee buff to players between level 28 and 30 (from there WF overtakes it).
 2. Giving a melee buff if you really need another air totem in a situation and your mana base doesn't allow totem twisting.

How I'd change the totem:
Changing the buff into a non-weapon buff is too OP. Shamans would just suddenly start being serious about fire totems and the damage buff they provide would become too big. I believe that flametongue needs a rework because the niche it fills is filled by a much better totem. If giving shamans a spell damage totem would be within the balance, flametongue totem is a prime candidate to become such a totem. The design problem here is that it wants to challenge WF. If we allow both totems to apply their buff then it's OP. No totem can realistically challenge WF in classic, flametongue should switch job and become something else while keeping things as balanced as possible.

Searing totem - An almost perfect utility totem. Only problem with it that it has a tendency to pewpew in patrols and turn sheeps back into enemies.

How I'd change the totem:
I'd make it so that searing totem only pewpews if a target within its range is being actively damaged by a party member. This way no more pulls or sheep sabotages from this bad boy. If enemies are nearby but noone in the party is damaging an opponent, searing would not cast anything. If the party is damaging several opponents within searing totem's range, the totem would either pewpew a random one of them or would shoot the one with the lowest health.

Magma totem - Would consider adding the threat created by its damage to the shaman.

Stoneclaw totem - The design is very cool but the totem itself is very close to being useless. It usually dies when an enemy mob just looks at it.

How I'd change the totem:
Changing the healthbar is a bad idea. If you optimize it for Scholo than the totem is an absolute trash in late-raids. If you optimize it for Naxx, this totem becomes a god in Scholo. I'd make it invulnerable and make it disappear after 3-5 seconds automatically. Would increase the threat it generates, too. During my shaman tanking adventures mobs tended to just ignore it. I tossed down a normal (not masterwork!) target dummy right after and the same mobs started to care.

Healing stream totem - If the notion of being underwhelming had a poster boy, it would have been this totem. A max level healing stream totem heals the party for 14 every 2 seconds for 1 minute. Sure, it is very cheap but shamans don't always pay with the mana. Shamans pay by not using any other water totems for a whole minute to have a HOT of 420 in this timeframe for each party member. No mana totems, no cleansing totems, no fire rez totem. Sure, resto can have  25% bonus on it turning this totem into a beast which heals 525 life in one minute. Manaspring totem in the meanwhile would have reloaded 300 (or 375 with talents) mana for each party member minus the 100 mana it costs. At this point in the game, your life total is somewhere between 2000 and 7000. Imagine giving up a mana regeneration totem for those 14 health ticks every 2 seconds. The totem fell into the same problem as health regen item effects have fallen. Blizzard probably thought that health worths much more and deeply undertuned these effects. TWOW created vampirism to balance it out but left healing stream totem as it is.

How I'd change the totem:
I'd change its uptime from 1 minute to 30 seconds. People rarely stand at the same place and fight for 1 minute, except for bosses. Would seriously increase the healing it does while keeping it way under the HOTs some classes do. This is a cheap totem with a party wide effect, shamans should not become the kings of HOT. But a totem which recharges 750 life (937,5 with talents) in 30 seconds would be a great utility while wouldn't be an auto-use because you still need to cleanse and recharge mana. I won't create a pluss entry for it but I'd change manaspring totem a bit similarly: make it last 30 seconds but halve its casting cost. Would be more practical.

+1 Would consider a change in totem design that buffs are only active as long as the related totem is down. Now some totems can be abused and double-buffs can be given (for example WF and + agility). I'm fine by keeping this in the game but it should be considered/discussed whether or not this mechanics should be kept as it is.

Really well formulated and thought out ideas! I hope the class changes council reads through it, there ate some really good ideas here. Good job!

To add to some of the totems:
 -I think the flametongue totem should increase spell damage, while still being a weapon buff (not stackable with WF). This way if your group has mostly casters you can decide to use it, and melees would still get at least some fire damage. Also it won't contribute to power creep or change raiding too much, since those same casters could have used (better) weapon oils instead.

 -Searing totem: it should always try to attack the shaman's target. This way the shaman is responsible for any mishaps. If the shaman has no target or their target is not attackable, it should idle.
A side effect of this is that if a resto shaman decides to waste their mana for a searing totem for whatever reason, they have to keep targeting the enemies to keep it shooting. For people using mouseover macros this shouldn't be a problem though.

 -Magma totem: the AoE threat would be a godsend for shamatanks, but otgers would hate it. Maybe add aggro reduction to the fire totem talents in ele? Same goes for fire nova totem.

 -Stoneclaw: maybe instead of having a health bar, it should survive a set amount of hits (increased by its talent). Fast-attacking enemies would be less affected though. Its aggro really needs fixing, it barely works.

The real issue I have with totems though is their miserable 20 yard range. Even pala auras have 30 yards. Both of those are upgradable to 30 and 40 respectively, but totems are static, while paladins are not. I think a baseline range of 30 would be more appropriate.
