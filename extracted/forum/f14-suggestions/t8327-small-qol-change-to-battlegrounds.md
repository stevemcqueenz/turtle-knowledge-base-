# Small QoL change to Battlegrounds

## Post 53671 by Tawneyturtle (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-07-16T11:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53671#p53671 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

BGs are heavily dominated by Damage over time classes and Hunters, while Paladins are already getting adressed, and the Hunters are being adjusted by the addition of the new PVP stat, there are no solutions shown in 1.1.6 for DoT dmg.

For this reason I'd like to request a massive QoL change that is an unspoken requirement to be PVPing: **Orange table inside the starting room for AB AV and WSG.**

Everyone sees people asking in /yell for ORANGE or MAGE TABLE PLS in Stormwind and Org, most of the time it's because of BGs. There is no easy solution to Dots other than running away and sitting down eating.

This doesn't do much other than let players queue up for BGs without having to ask around or wait for an orange table. **Let these oranges only be usable inside pvp instances.**

"But what about the food you can get from the pvp vendor" it's still there, characters with small mana pool benefit more from those than Oranges since they're a flat number vs the 3% orange regen.
