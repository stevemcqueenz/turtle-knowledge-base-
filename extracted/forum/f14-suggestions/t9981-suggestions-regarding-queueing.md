# Suggestions Regarding Queueing

## Post 65207 by Chuchenglai — 2023-09-18T06:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=65207#p65207 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone, I'm a player from China, and I've been playing Turtle WoW for five months now. In the first two months of playing Turtle WoW, I was drawn in by the game's atmosphere. There was no RMT, everyone was polite, and we buffed each other when we met. In the context of the Chinese servers, this feeling hasn't been experienced for over a decade.

I brought a few friends from the Chinese servers here, and we formed our own guild. At its peak, we had up to 500 members online. We love it here, and we've been making efforts to maintain the existing environment. However, with the large influx of Chinese players, we've also felt that the environment has been disrupted. Endless spam in public channels, RMT ads from studios, and constant insults and arguments in public channels have made us quite frustrated. It can be said that the sheer number of Chinese players means that there are more bad actors, and they get exposed more often. I believe that the vast majority of Chinese players still cherish this environment and love this game. I don't want other players to be affected by these players. Regarding this, I have a few suggestions:

 **1.Increase the penalties for spamming.**
If a player shouts more than a certain number of characters within a specified time, issue a warning. If they continue to spam, impose a 7-day suspension.

 **2.Strictly control multi-boxing.**
If a computer runs more than 2 client instances, prohibit logging in with a third one, or when attempting to log in with a third instance, randomly disconnect one of the already online clients.

 **3.Clearing AFK players.**
Based on the common practice on Chinese servers, I suspect that the main reason for long queues isn't necessarily too many legitimate players but rather the excessive presence of gold farmers, which disrupts the gameplay for regular players. *A single gold farming operation might have hundreds of computers, with each computer running over 10 Warcraft clients. This means one gold farming operation could potentially occupy over 1,000 server resources. What if there are multiple such operations? This is likely why there are so many servers with queues of over 5,000 players each.* I suggest that if an account doesn't have any characters above level 20 and these low-level characters haven't gained any experience, honor, skill points, etc., within a certain time frame, they should be disconnected from the server. This could free up a significant amount of server space.

 **4.Fair Allocation of Server Resources.**
Allocate server resources based on the registration time periods to optimize the queue system. Each player would queue within their assigned resource pool based on their registration date. *For example, players who registered before 2023 would be assigned to a pool with a capacity of 3,000, those who registered from January to June 2023 would be assigned to a pool with a capacity of 4,000, and those who registered from July 2023 onwards would be assigned to a pool with a capacity of 3,000.* This way, players would queue within their designated resource pools.

The above are my personal suggestions, and I believe these methods can help address issues with spamming and queuing. I earnestly request that you provide a home for players like us who truly love WoW. Please avoid implementing sweeping measures that punish all players indiscriminately, as this would be unfair to those of us who abide by the game's rules.

Regarding the use of Chinese characters, I kindly request that they be allowed in private chats and guild channels while being restricted in other channels. This would provide long-time WoW fans like us with a final refuge. We love WoW, we love this place, and we are disheartened by the environment on the Chinese servers. Please grant us the choice to stay and play as we prefer, rather than using various methods to make things difficult for Chinese players, or even forcing server transfers.

Lastly, I wish the Turtle team's other servers continued success and improvement!
