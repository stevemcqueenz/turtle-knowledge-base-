# New Playstyle Glyph - Glyph of the Everquester

## Post 98057 by Zeio2k4 — 2024-06-28T15:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98057#p98057 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all,

I was thinking about all of the great glyphs that the Turtle WoW team has added to the game that make playing so dynamic compared to classic Vanilla WoW, and came up with a new one that I think would add an additional layer of enjoyment for classic MMO players and would really separate Turtle WoW from other Vanilla/Classic+ projects.

Glyph of the Everquester

When applying this glyph, the following bonuses and penalties are bestowed upon the player:

 * -50% Quest Experience Gained
 * +100% Mob Experience from mobs your level or higher
 * +100% Mob Experience from mobs your level or higher when in a full group of 5 players who are also using the glyph and who gain experience from the mob (multiplicative with the first 100% bonus, and no high level carries/boosters)
 * Mobs inflict 100% increased damage to the player
 * The player inflicts 25% less damage to all mobs

This is a Vanilla wow classic spin on old school MMOs where the meta was to group up and camp mobs for EXP, because mobs were very strong and typically couldn't be solo defeated beyond a point.  Think Everquest or FFXI.  I think this would be a positive for the game world as a whole, because of the restrictions and bonuses in place.  First, I think this would really encourage players to mix/max their characters all the way to 60, rather than waiting to do so at max.  This would in turn stimulate the lower level raw material economy, causing them to gain in value, since they would be in much higher demand.  It would also give people a different way to level that is more interesting than just running back and forth amongst a massive zone, doing the same quests over and over.  Questing can be fun, but sometimes you need a break and want to try something different after you've done it a bunch of times.  This would also encourage people to be more social and encourage grouping up for reasons outside dungeon farm runs and doing outdoor elite quests, which is a core design feature of MMORPGs which I feel like has been partially abandoned ever since the 'solo era' became a thing.

Thoughts on this one?  The Everquest classic community is pretty strong still to this day, so I'm betting there would be demand for this style of gameplay.
