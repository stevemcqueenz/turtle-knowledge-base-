# To Turtle Team: Communicate With Your Users!

## Post 48230 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-31T11:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48230#p48230 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Has this ever happened to you?  You're sitting there, happily playing your favorite emulated *World of Warcraft* server when suddenly…

 **"Server restarting in X seconds."**

You finish up what you're doing and log out.  Five minutes later, you try and log in, but…

 **"Unable to connect to server."**
Or…
 **"Choose your preferred location / realm style" / "View realm list"**

You wait for a bit longer.  Nothing changes.  You wait more.  Still nothing.  What's going on…?

Confused and hoping for answers, you click the "Community" button on the login page.  It leads you to the forums.  Hope fills your heart.  Surely, if something *big* is going down, the administration has already posted a detailed report.  When you get there, however, all you see is…

"Is the server down?"
"Can't login"
"Disconnected after restart"
And so forth, and so on, ad nauseum.

You click into the topics, hoping for answers.  While it's a relief to see that you're not the only one who was unceremoniously punted offline… there are no official posts to be found.  Worse still, there's this *one person* who keeps shouting: "Hey, join the *Discord* for updates".  Yes, you *could* do that, but you'd rather just have a nice, quick answer from an official source.

Sometimes, silence is the best option for a major, multi-million-dollar company.  But you know what?  Turtle Team isn't a multi-million-dollar company.  Also?  **Even Blizzard-Activision lets people know what's going on if there's a service disruption!!**

I've said this before and I feel like I'll say it again and again: You people at Turtle Team need to communicate more.  Communicate more, communicate better, and communicate **everywhere possible**.  You opened multiple channels of interaction with your community: a forum, a *Twitter*, a *Discord*, and even an e-mail address.
No one likes being uninformed.  Don't favor one over the other.  If something's going on and you inform the masses on *one* channel, then you need to do the **rest** of us the same courtesy.  Hire more people.  Create a piece of software specifically for bulk notifications to multiple locations.  Do **something**.

Thank you, good day.
