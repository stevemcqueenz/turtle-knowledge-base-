# Random Enchanted Gear is totally broken (e.g., Dark Iron Boots)

## Post 147682 by Shokisan1 — 2025-08-22T10:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147682#p147682 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, 1.18 broke the item names for random enchants. For example, you cannot search for "defense" or "arcane" or "nature" etc anymore, since the item only has the generic item name. Even worse, the specific enchant is invisible when shopping. I'm not sure if turtle dev team realized this when making this change, but it makes it completely impossible to go shopping for these types of items now. It's basically a crap shoot what random enchantment you have. This is so incredibly bad for certain things such as Dark Iron Boots and other gear like this.

This is a really big deal when it comes time to gear for resistance sets or gearing up alts. If I want to buy a pair of Dark Iron Boots, I want to make sure those boots have the stats that I want (i.e., not spirit). It's currently impossible to do this. This is game-breaking for auction house shopping and I urge you to look into this. It's actually stupid that we can't distinguish between types of dark iron boots anymore, they all literally look exactly the same on the auction house listing. Very bad.

tldr: The 1.18 patch completely broke named /natural enchanted items.
It's impossible to go shopping in on the AH now for specific stuff, breaking the experience for getting resistance sets and etc., overall making the gameplay experience worse.
