+1

Currently WSG and AB reputations require hundreds of hours spent to be Exalted. And what players get in return? Crap gear that doesn't upgrade the one you've managed to get in meantime in raids while you were farming the reputation.

Mount, toys, tabard or even title would be the best thing to incentivize playing. It's purely visual reward for big effort. Title could be for example if you are Exalted with all WSG, AV and AB factions, maybe Blood Ring too. I'm sure there aren't many people who achieved this so the title would be rare.

I'm against teleport idea though. It's too convenient combined with hearthstone. Locking such convenient teleporting would be unfair to people who don't want to do PvP at all.
