# Please restore the race/faction change service

## Post 76863 by Xa1amandor — 2023-11-11T01:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76863#p76863 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This service was available when I started playing on Turtle just over a year ago, and then somewhere along the road, it was deactivated and I never saw an official post on the matter. That's not to say that the devs didn't post one, but all I've ever found have been rumors from other players in-game. Some said that it was bugged and being fixed, others that due to the population growth the Turtle team could no longer keep up with offering the service in its' present (at the time), manual form and were deactivating until they found a method of automating it. For months now, I've heard essentially nothing in the way of updates or plans to reinstate this beloved service.

I know that there will always be the vanilla-era purists who'd rather not see such a thing available to players but considering that Turtle WoW is a vanilla + server, I think that this was and would be again a fitting amenity that helps both players & the team.

I hope that Turtle indeed plans to return the race/faction change service in some manner as I know it's sorely missed and would be much appreciated by the vast majority of players.

Please post thoughts, criticisms and support in the comments   smiling_turtle_head
