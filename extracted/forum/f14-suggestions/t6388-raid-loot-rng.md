# Raid loot RNG

## Post 40266 by Kaskochron — 2023-04-04T13:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40266#p40266 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, I'd like to share some ideas about improving raid loot RNG in game.

You can enter the raid and get item you want on first try but you also you can kill same boss for almost 2 years and never be rewarded, just as main tank in my guild, who was farming Thunderfury during first classic era, he did it every single week from beginning till the end, never got it. It shouldn't be easy or even close to being easy, but situation were you can have your reward without much effort and time waste or never have it at all while using every possible opportunity,  isn't good game design.
RNG is core of this game, I don't mean to remove it, but slightly alter with more possibilities:

 1. More items similar to "bis" on same raid tier, from professions, reputations, other raids, epic class quests, world bosses ect., more choices reduce urge to have one specific item which never drops and everyone want it.

 2. Boss loot table - almost every raid boss have half of loot table filled with "useless" items.
Option A. Upgrade "useless" items to be worst than best items in raid tier but much better than 5 man dungeon blue items, they should be optional but still decent.
Option B. Reduce raid boss loot table lists. Add more bosses or optional (able to skip) mini bosses (basically bigger, higher HP and damage trash mobs) filled with items no one really want to see to drop from main boss.

 3. Raid currency - raid bosses drop currency, specific  for each raid tier. It has weekly cap so you don't have to farm all raids just for it but it also balances your progression. Let's say cap is 5/week. There is special place with special vendor with every single raid item. Just as example let's say simple epic items costs 20 (4 weeks to get 1 item), set item 15, weapons 40, legendaries 120. Ofc items will be soulbound. Even if unlucky with loot or rolls, you still after every single raid will be closer to you goal.

 4. Another idea: Legendariers shouldn't be a RNG loot drop. Instead make it an object which appear after boss defeat, which everyone can interact to start the quest, but increase the overall effort to acquire it up to insanity. Legendary items should take time and whole guild effort but shouldn't be locked behind RNG to start process.

Please keep in mind, knowledge and feedback that you completely disagree about my opinion on RNG concept, adds nothing to the topic. If you feel so special that you have to let me know that you don't like this idea and you disagree, please keep it for yourself or make another post on how good RNG based loot is for the game so I can compare my point of view.
Feel free to share your ideas on how RNG loot could be improved without changing too much game itself.
