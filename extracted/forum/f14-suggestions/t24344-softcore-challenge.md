# Softcore Challenge

## Post 171261 by Celarc — 2026-03-19T00:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171261#p171261 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hardcore is fun, but can be extremely off putting to people who don't want to put a ton of time and effort into a character, only to get it washed away because of a mistake, a bug/bad disconnect, or worse yet, getting griefed. But some people may still want **some** risk to liven up the stakes, a little something to encourage thoughtful gameplay, and minimizing danger to ones self.

My suggestion for Softcore is fairly simple:
If you die, your character becomes Locked for 7 days. After 7 days have elapsed, they are unlocked, teleported to the nearest graveyard (or perhaps to a temple/church in the nearest capital city), and becomes playable again!

Additional punishments could be tacked on, such as losing all currently gained XP towards the next level on death. But I think it might be better to leave additional risk up to the individual player, by allowing them to select other challenges. Such as Slow & Steady for the XP punishment.

Pros: Players looking to dip their toes into high risk characters get a nice middle ground to try it out.

Cons: Development time.
