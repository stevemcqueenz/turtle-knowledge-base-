Two options here:
 1. Healer gets flagged
 2. Healer don't get flagged.

Option 1 is like it works now. From game perspective heal and buff are the same. You get flagged.
Will all potential drama: you get accidentally flagged while you didn't want it.
Gankers send friendly buffer character and then kill by killer character. Or just wait for careless guy.
Assuming that we want flag toggling in rested xp, flagging by healing should not happen.

Option 2 could lead to three kinds of situations:
a. low level flagged twink supported by healer w/o flag attacks low level flagged character.
b. high level flagged attacks low level flagged character. I doubt that 60 lvl rogue needs any healer in Elwyn.
c. low level flagged random guy supported by low level random healer w/o flag attacks low level flagged.
We also prevent situations:
x. random guy gets suddenly attacked by non-flagged
y. random buff to someone else turn you flagged and potentially killed

Situation a looks the same as currently. Low level victim can't do anything to high-level healer anyway.
Situation b looks the same as currently. No healer required at all.
Situation c looks worse than currently. New in 2 vs 1 fight you have chance to kill both enemies. If healer will be invulnerable to your attacks, then you can't cc him. you have no reason to cc attacker, because you can't kill healer anyway.

So when "healer don't get flagged", only skilled players who can potentially kill 2 attackers will have to run away instead of fighting.

Conclusion. If non-flagged can't attack flagged, healer don't get flagged we make situation X and Y better and situation C worse.
