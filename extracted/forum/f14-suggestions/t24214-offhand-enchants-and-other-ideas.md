# Offhand Enchants and Other Ideas

## Post 170318 by Scr — 2026-03-08T01:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170318#p170318 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Frill Enchants
 --------------------------------------

Offhand frills are items that directly mirror shields, used mainly by casters/healers but occasionally by melee druids as they are the only melee class that can neither dual-wield nor wear shields. As an additional avenue of itemization and utility for Enchanting/Jewelcrafting, I am putting forward these suggestions to get feedback and share ideas on adding content to the game.

Be aware that these lists are not exhaustive and are simply suggestions on what could be added in addition to other enchants.

Some ideas for Offhand enchants:

Enchant Offhand - Meditation: Permanently enchant an Offhand to allow 5% of your mana regeneration to continue while casting.
Enchant Offhand - Luck: Permanently enchant an Offhand to increase your chance to trigger chance based item effects by 5%.
Enchant Offhand - Intellect: Permanently enchant an Offhand to increase your intellect by 7.
Enchant Offhand - Agility: Permanently enchant an Offhand to increase your agility by 7.
Enchant Offhand - Superior Intellect: Permanently enchant an Offhand to increase your Intellect by 15.

 --------------------------------------
Buffed Shield Enchants
 --------------------------------------

Shield enchants are not particularly powerful, either being a small buff to stamina, armor value, block chance, spirit, or, oddly, only frost resistance. As a damage alternative, shield spikes from blacksmithing are also used for farming and as a minor threat generator.

It would be selfish to put forward the idea of Frill enchants without also suggesting a buff to shield enchants like the following:

Enchant Shield - Resilience: Permanently enchant a shield to reduce damage taken from critical hits and damage over time effects by 3%.
Enchant Shield - Avoidance: Permanently enchant a shield to reduce the damage you take from area of effect attacks by 5%.
Enchant Shield - Superior Stamina: Permanently enchant a Shield to increase your Stamina by 15.

Additionally, the current shield spikes could be given a bonus threat modifier to help aoe tanking.

(type) Shield Spike - Attaches a (type) Spike to your shield that deals damage every time you block with it, this damage causes a high amount of threat.

Iron - 8 damage, +150% threat bonus
Mithril - 16 damage, +150% threat bonus
Thorium - 24 damage, +200% threat bonus
Elementium - 40 damage, +300% threat bonus

These numbers are all just thrown at the wall as examples, and could be modified to fit the balance that the designers envision.

 --------------------------------------
Jewelcrafting enchants for frills
 --------------------------------------

Most jewels would be too overpowered to be allowed on an additional item, but new items, trims, could be added to mirror blacksmithing enchants. Trims would be similar to shield spikes or counterweights, except they mimic effects from popular items.

Shadowed Trim - Gives a chance when your harmful spells land to deal 25 Shadow damage and return 25 mana to you.
Thorn-laced Trim - Adds 2 Nature damage to your melee attacks.

More could be added, but I believe that putting unique Jewelcrafting trims under the goldsmith specialization would be an interesting way to incorporate cool effects from niche world items into player builds.

 --------------------------
 2H caster enchants
 --------------------------

From looking at previous posts regarding adding enchants to offhands, the main counterpoint to adding Offhand enchants is that 1H+OH is more powerful than 2H. ([viewtopic.php?p=86302&hilit=offhand+enc ... ant#p86302](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86302&hilit=offhand+enchant+frill+enchant#p86302))

In the raiding scene, this is correct with MC/BWL/AQ40 level gear depending on the caster class but Naxx/K40 level gear has 2H weapons being more powerful for every spec (Brimstone Staff, Soulseeker, Atiesh, "Kirel'narak, The Death Sentence")

However, to balance the addition of offhand enchants, there should be added 2H enchants to equalize the two different itemization styles:
Enchant 2H Weapon - Major Spell Power: Permanently enchant a two-handed weapon to add up to 40 damage to spells.
Enchant 2H Weapon - Vorpality: Permanently enchant a two-handed weapon to add 2% spell critical strike chance.
Enchant 2H Weapon - Lesser Mana Regeneration: Permanently enchant a two-handed weapon to restore 8 mana per 5 seconds.

 ------------------
End Line
 ------------------

Any interesting ideas for enchants would be good to add in the comments, along with ways to keep parity between the Frill, Shield, and 2H archetypes to bring them closer to dual-wielding enchants.
