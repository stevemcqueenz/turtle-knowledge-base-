## Post 117461 by Szarszag (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-21T19:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117461#p117461 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really like how vendors work in Guild Wars 1, by default they do not have any stock. They can only sell what another player sold to them, so its entirely based on supply/demand, and as they start running out of items they increase the prices... consequently rare items can be sold to them for more money.

I like the current system on turtle hc, you get more out of giving away freebies instead of vendoring for chump change. The only annoying thing is the extra bag space taken up by valuables I plan to barter / give away. All in all it it encourages and rewards socialising with other players and I think that is in the spirit of vanilla wow... miles better than looking at a soulless interface that is the auction house + mailbox.
