<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102270#p102270 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

These are very creative but also very strong, would probably lead to some kind of min-maxing and to the problem TWOW tries to avoid rn, that certain class-race combinations are considered correct and others are ridiculed.
F.ex. the gnome's resourcefulness+low profile could probably lead to some disgusting combos for a DPS class and non-gnomes from that class would suddenly feel like bad versions (kinda the PVE dwarven priest situation).
Undead slaying while seems fair would give humans a huge DPS advantage at Naxx which is still one of the final raids.

Also for leveling, goblin and human would kinda feel like easy mode, like using a glyph just because playing that race.

My other problem is that certain races are more pushed here than others. Nelves got some boost but they still feel a bit iffy. Taurens also feel a bit meh in this boosted environment.
Comparing those to humans, gnomes, orcs or goblins feels like buffs from a different game.

Don't get me wrong, loving some of the ideas and appreciating the effort you've put into this but imo this would break race-class comboes worse than weapon skill meta did.
Perso I'd tone down the racials a bit.
