# Druid Suggestion - Insect Swarm to be given at an earlier level.

## Post 124689 by Kohga — 2025-03-31T15:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124689#p124689 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As someone who loves playing Druid and has died many, many, many times on HC while leveling druid, the Lv1-20 on this class is probably the worst of any class. Their competitor for worst, the Paladin received new strike abilities early so that pulled them out of one of the worst leveling classes.

I suggest Rank 1 Insect Swarm be given to druids at level 2, 4, or 6 to help with the 1-20 grind. The skill given this early wont make a HUGE impact, but one that will really make the class leveling feel a bit more enjoyable. I know this wont happen, but thought I'd just throw that out there.

Keep up the great work TWoW team.
