# Suggestion: Horses in Human Lands

## Post 128052 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-04-22T23:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128052#p128052 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Horses are an animal that is ubiquitously associated with humanity. Knights, horsemen and cavaliers are iconic military units used by the human nations, and the humans' racial mount is the horse. Yet outside from those found in horse breeder's stables or as NPC mounts, the animal itself is a rather rare, if even non-existent sight.

In the spirit of giving the iconic animal of humanity a thematic place in the world, I suggest adding packs of horse NPCs and mobs in various fitting regions.

 **Places where horses both domesticated wild and domesticated could possibly be found:**
- Westfall (workhorses left behind by the fleeing farmers, gone wild);

- Arathi Highlands;

- Hillsbrad Foothills;

- Scarlet Enclave (last of the uncorrupted breeds of the northlands);

- Gilneas;

- Tirisfal Glades (skeletal/plagued);

- Western Plaguelands (skeletal/plagued, or uncorrupted Scarlet horses in Hearthglen);

- Northwind (Patch 1.18.0 as-of-yet unreleased zone; hypothetical location)
