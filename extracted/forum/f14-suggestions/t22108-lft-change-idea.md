# LFT change idea

## Post 153369 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-26T17:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153369#p153369 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

The below:

Tickable option:
 * Extended tank rosters

If not selected: Everything goes on as now.
If selected:
 * Allows certain classes to pick tank, too (hunter, warlock, rogue for sure).
or
 * Allows any class to pick tank (I healed a mage at Nord tanking endgame content; sure, partly via overlevel/consumes but still).

Those with extended tank roster options get a party as soon as a team of any combination pops up, whether it is a usual tank or a strange one.
Those without the option only get a party if a team with the current role distribution assembles.

(So the setting doesn't take away your right to have normal runs.
It's LFT speed for the price of potentially awful dungeon experience.
But a goldmine for those, who like challenges and experiments.)

By default would be set as 'no' for everybody, and you manually had to change it so no one would be 'cheated into' a weird dungeon experience.
Other reason behind it: now healing earlier dungeons is fun but at late game becomes super robotic unless the tank is spectacularly bad.
Are you a bored healer? Try to keep 'Freddie' the boar alive while the hunter somehow attempts to get the attention of squishy magic students.
Same with 'McStabbie', the leather-wearing, undead protector.
Do you wanna finally reach the later depths of Kara Crypt, see the cloth helmet with +17 defence drop and see someone being genuinely happy about it?
Do you want to see a ret paladin actually using salvation on characters and devotion aura to prove that even they can be team players?
Do you want to see the enh shaman's face as he learns that if a boar is tanking, opening with SS/LS/Earth shock and WF procs will always magically steal the threat?
Do you want to see absolutely nonsense talent trees circulating at class Discords?
