# Frenzied Regeneration brutally overnerfed

## Post 109945 by Templar85 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-26T02:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109945#p109945 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

CD changed from 3 min to 6 mins.
HP return from full rage bar went to 95% HP to around 30-35%

Frenzy now lost its intended purpose. It is used during enemy burst phase or when a Bear tank dropped to low to regain some HP back to avoid die due to low HP by fatal Crits or Crushes. I know we have abilities to mitigate the damage like Berserk and Barkskin. Berserk is a good significant multipurpose talent but Barkskin have affects on melee attacks only and just for 15 hits. Barkskin is on 10 min CD. Frenzy does not prove enough safety without the other two abilities.

The previously available every 6 min Frenzy + Berserk option now replaced with every 10 min Barkskin + Frenzy + Berserk to have a someway similar but rather worst false safe mechanism. It is worse because if the druids actives too late when HP is at around 20% and keep getting hit by a not physical or by burst attacks then Frenzy just stagnates the HP at 20% rather than pushing out from the danger zone.

Feral Adrenaline suppose to give alternative to the high armor and stamina tanking style to provide a high agi, and dodge focused tanking style. The problem is Feral Adrenaline does not prevent crit + crush or crush + crush scenarios and dodge focused gear with dodge and agi enchants has much lower stamina so Frenzy provide lower HP as well.

We got the new talent Ancient Brutality but that can not compensate. In tanking it is used for rage generation. Only while leveling and in PvP have some good benefit of the Vampiric mechanism but it is very very weak.

To address the laments of the masses who sad frenzy was overpowered. It was not, just meaningfully strong. Have you hit the druid while Frenzy was active? You deserved your fate, giving him more rage aka more heal.  Do you hit Paladins while they have a bubble too? Bear can sustain damage but have weak attack. To defeat them key element is to drain their mana by forcing to switch between forms.

My suggestion it to lower the CD to 4 min and HP regain to 80%
