# Scaling Raid Content

## Post 114767 by Stompi — 2024-12-29T14:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114767#p114767 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello to all Turtles, and the Dev Team,

I have now taken part in a few raids and have noticed that the vanilla feeling is missing a little bit for me. Basically, the raids are the same as before and back in the Day, but they are over far too quickly. It is much more of a brawl than any kind of tactical approach.

This is particularly true for the trash mobs, they fall over sometimes before i can cast one Frostbolt.

The Boss fights don't feel good at all either, as the fights are over far too quickly too. And the Boss encounters doesnt really matter. As I said, it is basically just a brawl. Molten Core can be played with 25-30 people, including Twinks with bad gear, and even so, Molten Core is basically AFK farming.

My suggestion would be, and I would be interested in the community's opinion, what they think about simply increasing the Healthpoints of the Bosses and Trash for the raid content from 10+.

For example, for 10-20 man raids, by a factor of 1.5.
For 40 man raids, by a factor of 2.0.
Armor/magic resistance adjustments if necessary.

So the fights feel a little bit more longer, and the tactical Approach matters a little bit more.

Btw: One should also consider that the recent class changes, and especially the combination of shamans and paladins, lead to more damage output than before. In addition, of course, to the knowledge we have today about the content.
