# Something to think about

## Post 17758 by Bitterbutter — 2022-03-27T17:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17758#p17758 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are still a number of options from warcraft 3 that were either left out of turtle wow or could easily fit in lore wise

Here are a few suggestions

New Horde Race

Stormstout brewmasters

The Stormstout brewmasters are a group of
Pandaren brewmasters founded and lead by
Chen Stormstout following the invasion of Durotar

The Stormstout Brewmasters are officially allied with the Horde and are based out of ogrimmar but operate several Breweries across Central Kalimdor specifically within Durotar, the Barrens,
Stonetalon mountains, dustwallow Marsh and
Desolace

New Pandaren players will start within a new phased area of Durotar until they hit level 5 at which point they will be sent to speak with Chen Stormstout in ogrimmar

The main quests within the starting zone focus on the development of a new brew for the Horde presence within Durotar and then delivering a sample to Chen

New Alliance Race

Outland Refuges

A large group of Broken and lost ones managed to flee Outland sometime after the Third war and established the bases of Harborage and Fallow Sanctuary within The swamp of sorrows

After constant fighting between the two settlements the leader of harborage Magtoor has managed to bring peace and unite the Broken and lost ones into a single unified faction

Now calling themselves the Outland Refuges these outcasts have reached out to the Alliance for assistance and have begun to expand outside of the swamp of sorrows

New Broken players will start out in the misty Valley until level 5.

Forsaken Death knights

The Dark lady has exepted Death knights into the Forsaken's ranks in an attempt to bolster their military against the Scourge and the Alliance

The Forsaken Death knights call themselves the Knights of the undercity and are based within the war quarter

Night elf Demon hunters

Although the night elves distrust anyone who uses the magical or demonic the aftermath of the Third war have forced the night elves to allow the assistance of outsiders

One such group is comprised of Night elf demon hunters who have abandoned Illidan Stormrange and have taken refuge within darnassus

These demon hunters now seek to prove themselves to their people by helping defend the ancient forests from all threats
