# Suggestions: Repair with your profession

## Post 169700 by Strogg123 — 2026-03-01T23:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169700#p169700 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

You want to add relevance to craft professions in an upcoming patch. So why not add them such a logical function as the ability to repair things, which will be much cheaper than standard repair through the vendor/rembot? Such repairs will not cost money, but ask for appropriate materials that will be used either in their pure form (leather, bars, cloth), or craft in the form of special "metal plates," "chain mail rings," "cloth rolls."

For this change (playable), all owners of t3 equipment will thank you.
