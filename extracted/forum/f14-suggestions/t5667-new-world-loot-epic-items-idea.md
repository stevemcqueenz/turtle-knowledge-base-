# New world loot epic items idea

## Post 35380 by Rukopashka — 2023-02-20T08:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35380#p35380 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Stormcaller's Fury
Type: **Two-Handed Mace**
Description: *A massive mace forged from the fiery heart of the Elemental Plane, Stormcaller's Fury radiates with raw elemental power. The head of the mace is made of solid obsidian and crackles with lightning, while the handle is wrapped in sinew from the most powerful beasts of Azeroth. Legends say that this weapon was once wielded by a mighty shaman who could control the very elements themselves.*
Characteristics:
+50 Stamina
+40 Intellect
+30 Spirit
On Hit: Lightning Strike deals an additional 300 Nature damage to your target
Chance on Hit: Calls down a bolt of lightning to strike your target for 400 Nature damage
Fang of the Black Dragonflight
Type: **One-Handed Dagger**
Description: *The Fang of the Black Dragonflight is a wickedly curved dagger crafted from the bone of a black dragon. The blade is razor sharp and glows with a malevolent red light. It is said that the dagger was used by the infamous rogue and assassin, Ravenholdt, to slay the black dragon Nefarian.*
Characteristics:
+35 Agility
+35 Stamina
+20 Critical Strike
Chance on Hit: Deals an additional 200 Shadow damage and increases your critical strike chance by 5% for 10 seconds
Heart of the Phoenix
Type: **Trinket**
Description: *The Heart of the Phoenix is a powerful trinket that glows with an inner fire. It is said to contain a fragment of the essence of the phoenix, a legendary creature that is reborn from its own ashes. When activated, the Heart of the Phoenix grants the wearer incredible regenerative abilities, allowing them to recover from even the most devastating injuries.*
Characteristics:
Use: Restores 25% of your maximum health over 10 seconds
Cooldown: 5 minutes
On Use: Grants the Phoenix's Blessing, increasing your health regeneration by 50% for 10 seconds
Equip: Increases your maximum health by 650
