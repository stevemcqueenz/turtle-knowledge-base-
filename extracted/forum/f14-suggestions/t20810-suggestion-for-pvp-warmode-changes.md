# Suggestion for PVP Warmode changes

## Post 144517 by Xirisxotol — 2025-08-11T12:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144517#p144517 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Considering how most people saw Warmode as a pure exp bonus buff, i feel like changeing it to another different way of getting exp bonus is kind of not really enriching the gameplay here.

What if we went and gave Warmode the same thing but in a more tangible way?
Why not go down the good ol Blizz Route of PVP kills: Ear colletions.

Add quests to collect maybe specific player ears?
Specific races to collect ears from?
Specific gear using ears as materials for tradeskills, granting PVP oriented stats?

Kill a player with ears, be able to steal the ears that were not turned in, and redeem them to your team, honoring the fallen by bringiing their ears to a priest in a main city for X or Y reward?

Hey maybe im thinking too bit here... but it just feels to me we arent really putting the Roleplay into the PVP to make it more immersive.
