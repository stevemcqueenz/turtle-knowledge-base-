# What is the best counter play against Track Humanoids?

## Post 152952 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-24T10:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152952#p152952 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think that Track Humanoids is most OP skill in whole game.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=19883>
> Track Humanoids
>  Instant
>  Shows the location of all nearby humanoids on the minimap. Only one form of tracking can be active at a time.

All players are humanoids by default.
It range covers half of the map and effectively 1 hunter right above the tunnel cover all exits from any base.
With wide variety of slows available to hunters, it makes almost impossible to carry flag out.

On the opposite side, it makes it is too easy to carry flag out if enemy team has no skilled hunter.

There are only two counterplays against Track Humanoids: Bear form of druid and Stealth of rogues. To be honest, I'd like to see more and without class restriction.
Maybe some consumable like "demon blood". When you drink it, you become a demon instead of humanoid for 5 minutes? Or "Forsaken brew" - you become a undead.
