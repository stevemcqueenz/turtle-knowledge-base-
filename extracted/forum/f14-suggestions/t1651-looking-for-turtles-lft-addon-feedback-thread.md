# Looking For Turtles - LFT Addon Feedback Thread

## Post 9771 by Zhohan — 2021-03-15T23:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9771#p9771 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, fellow turtles. By the grace of the Turtle WoW dev team the new LFT group finder tool comes included in the update.

I recommend other players give their feedback on the addon on whether you like it, dislike it, or think it could be improved on.

I think the experience of the LFT could be greatly enhanced with one simple change: A group name and description tab in the Browse section. Who you're grouping with matters. A title would tell players what to expect from that group's experience. Maybe they're running BRD but want to do a lava run and you wanted a full run. Well, you wouldn't know that until after you joined! With a group title, the experience could be enhanced.

Fellow turtles are welcome to post their own thoughts below!   turtle_tongue
