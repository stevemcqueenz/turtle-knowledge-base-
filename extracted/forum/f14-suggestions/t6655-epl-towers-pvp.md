# EPL Towers PvP

## Post 41923 by Valkar — 2023-04-17T19:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41923#p41923 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The idea: to change mechanic of Eastern Plaguelands Towers, making it more similar to Hellfire Peninsula Towers in BC. What it’s needed to be done for this:
 1. Make the quests [Establishing New Outposts](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=9664) and [Bolstering Our Defenses](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=9665) daily. Make them available to be done in party and in raid. Revard – X of special faction badges.
 2. Killing an enemy inside each of 4 towers will give 1 badge.
 3. Badges will be currency to buy some rare gear (nearly T0-T0.5 level) just like [Mark of Honor Hold](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/item=24579/mark-of-honor-hold) in BC. It’s possible to make 1-2 good epic weapons in the really high cost of these badges.
 4. Time for capturing the tower should be reduced twice or thrice.
 5. Fractional bonus for captured towers should baff damage not against just undead in EPL, but every damage dealt by player in location.

What it will give us? It will make world PvP in one of the main high level locations more perky, will motivate 60s to fight more not only on BGs, but in the outdoor.
