# Improvement to Brood of Nozdormu Rep Rings

## Post 83457 by Queenoona (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-02T19:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83457#p83457 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Brood of Nozdormu Rep Rings are a decent reward for gaining Exalted running AQ40, while not all variants are the most powerful they could be excellent options to have for players fleshing out their Off-Spec sets, however the issue is that you can only possess one at a time.

My suggestion:
 1) Create a quest that requires some more costly reagents or simply a gold payment to allow players to own multiple types of these rings at the same time.
or
 2) Make the quest 'The Changing of Paths - Conquerer/Invoker/Protector No More' repeatable.

While some people may argue that this removes the consequence of choice in this RPG, the Server is old enough that making it so people can spend a decent amount of gold for added flexibility (similar to the Brainwashing Device) or through the tedious process of going to CoT and trading in Scarabs every time you wanna swap the ring, would be an overall benefit to players who enjoy changing their spec frequently for varied gameplay or to better help their guild.
