Hi, as a soul link warlock, If i am working as a team in an arena I should
create 3 healthstones
sacrifice and recreate my pet

This means that per arena I will be burning through at least 5 soul shards. This is quite tiresome if queueing from stormwind/org as you can imagine, there are no mobs nearby to grind and Arenas are way too intense to be able to farm soul shards off your opponent, if you try that you are failing your team mates.

I think in wow by TBC they changed this so warlocks did not burn through soul shards in arenas and bg's. It would be super useful if warlocks were not forced to grind for them. I appreciate you receive a soul shard back upon death and this makes sense for bg's because there is time to grind your opponent, but absolutely no chance to do this in arena's. So warlocks, soul link especially have a much harder barrier  to entry into arenas.
