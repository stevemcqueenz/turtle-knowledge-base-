# Scaling world buffs

## Post 121948 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-07T10:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121948#p121948 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Was thinking about this for a while, not sure what your opinion is about it.
World buffs are very cool helps for leveling but I believe that the buff they provide are too big for lower level characters.
Personally I'd create level brackets, f.ex. 0-5, 5-10 etc, each bracket having a differently scaled version of the same world buff.
F.ex. 0-5 receiving 10% of the buff number-wise, 55-60 receiving 100% (random numbers to give an example).

Also, please apply world buffs for each character in each capital city. IF, UC etc could be much more interesting than it is because of that.
