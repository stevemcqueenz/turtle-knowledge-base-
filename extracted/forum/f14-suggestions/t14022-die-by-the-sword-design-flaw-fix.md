# Die by the Sword - design flaw & fix

## Post 95491 by Itorch100 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-05-23T13:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95491#p95491 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There's an unintended tradeoff of offense for defense for warriors when you use Die by the Sword instead of Retaliation.

Since you wouldn't use up all charges of Retal (unless you pull like 5 mobs), let alone DbtS due to extra parry chance, before the buff expires, DbtS nerfs the offensive gain as you use even less charges in the window, making it effectively a nerf to an ability that's supposed to be an all-around upgrade.

I'd suggest adding an effect to Die by the Sword that makes it proc a charge from parry, that way making it an actual upgrade over Retaliation (which I'm guessing was the intention? ^^)

Cheers!
