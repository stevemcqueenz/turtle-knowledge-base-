That'd be cool! To be fair, though, adding unique creature models is a *lot* more work than adding custom areas. Custom areas only need you to do modelling and texturing, which isn't exactly easy, but is a relatively simple artistic endeavor. Custom creatures also require rigging and animation, which are at least as much work again if not more and are much more technical. It's at least double the time/cost if not triple, so it's got to be worth it :P
