# Swiftness of the Rangers should also give +2% Intelligence

## Post 163405 by Familymaker — 2025-12-21T02:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163405#p163405 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So while I know that opinions on Quel'Dorei Meditation are mixed (some say it is fine, others want to have a reduced cast time) I always found it odd that Swiftness of the Rangers only gives +2% to a stat, while the Gnome's expanded mind gives +5%. Of course, one of the Gnome's traits is situational against constructs, so it is in a way fair. That said, I would love it if they could also get +2% Intelligence (so that the Mage FAQ won't put them in B tier XD), for a +4% total. That way their casters will receive a bit more love, without stepping on the Gnome's toes:

 **Swiftness of the Rangers - passive**
 *Agility increased by 2% and Intelligence increased by 2%.*

On a side note, I think that Quel'Dorei Meditation is mostly fine. But the casting time is a tad long for the amount of resources it generates. Perhaps the casting time could be reduced to 0.5 seconds? Similar to the Tauren's racial:

 **Quel'dorei Meditation - 0.5 sec cast - 3 min cooldown**
 *Generates 10% of your maximum mana, 20 rage, or 50 energy over 5 seconds. Warriors are considered in combat for the duration.*

## Post 163745 by Familymaker — 2025-12-25T21:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163745#p163745 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

For comparison, War Stomp has a 0.5 sec cast instead of 1.5 seconds, has a  2.5 minutes cooldown instead of 3 minutes, and instead of only giving a small amount of resources generated it stuns up to 5 enemies within 8 yards for 2 seconds.

Now, I think that the Quel'Dorei Meditation fits the High Elves perfectly, but the high elf racials are universally known to be a tad underwhelming compared to the other racials. So merely reducing the casting time of Quel'Dorei Meditation to 0.5 seconds, while also giving Swiftness of the Rangers a +2% bonus to Intelligence, would fix that and bring them more up to par with the racials of other races.
