First off, moving casinos into major hubs like Stormwind’s Trade District doesn’t solve the underlying issue, it just pushes gambling right into the middle of where people are trying to bank, auction, and trade. Not everyone wants to see dice spam or feel pressured into “just one more roll.” Booty Bay, as inconvenient as it might be, is already the right kind of place for fringe activities. That’s part of the flavor of the game, shady dealings happen in shady towns.

Second, opening up world chat for casino ads would make the game unbearable. Imagine trying to coordinate a dungeon run or sell crafted gear with casino promotions constantly cutting through the conversation. World chat is already crowded enough; flooding it with gambling ads would drive more players away than it would attract.

And on your last point: Accusations of scamming usually don’t come from nowhere. Even if an operator is being fair, the whole setup is based on trust and perception. If someone feels cheated, they should have the right to voice that concern without worrying about being banned themselves. That’s how community moderation works, players call out shady behavior, and GMs step in when needed. Trying to silence accusations, even false ones, would only make people more suspicious of casino operators in general.

At the end of the day, Azeroth isn’t Vegas. It’s about questing, raiding, PvP, and, yeah, grinding. If your casino can’t attract players, maybe that’s because nobody really wants it. Instead of lobbying to turn Stormwind into a slot hall, maybe just pick up a mining pick or fishing pole and farm like the rest of us.
