I don't think arcane mages need another fireball-with-arcane-damage spell. That would create just-another-color-tree. Arcane is mostly supporting tree with greatest sustain, support talents and on-hit effects, that can still utilize both frost tree for crowd control and fire tree for burst damage. Also, using AM properly is more fun and skill-issued due to keyboard mini-game (don't hit the AM key too early or too late).

Stay versatile: use AM for mana-efficent single target tunneling, fireballs for extra pre-casting openers and frostbolts to slow your target. Throw finishing scorches and fire blasts at last. Creating another Arcane Blast or Arcane Barrage (arcane-fireblast) from TBC+ isn't vanilla experience.

What arcane mages could really benefit from is something increasing their damage over long focused fights while using AM. Some deep-tree talents like 5/5 Arcane Trance looks good: "Every time your Arcane Missiles hit the target, your spell's damage increased by 1%. Stacks up to 15 times, lasts 10 sec." This talent will result in +15% to ANY spell damage for a short period of time after using AM 3 times in a row.

Another option is increasing spell haste: "Every time your Arcane Missiles hit the target, casting time of next damaging spell reduced by 3%. Stacks up to 10 times, lasts 10 sec." Works for only 1 non-channeling spell: fireball, frostbolt, scorch, flamestrike and pyroblast.

Talents like these will keep arcane mages versatile alternative to 1-school fire/frost focused builds, utilising all their spells, not just arcane ones. This is still vanilla experience due to no new TBC+ spells and a way to refresh arcane rotation.
