The concept of purified Ashbringer isn't OP. They simply need to start addressing player power creep.. I addressed this in [another thread](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118729#p118729). Until they start squashing item stats and player damage, PvE and PvP will continue to be trivialized and imbalanced. The Kara40 leaks are already a bad sign, where weapons are breaking a 100 dps threshold and stats are making items obsolete from the immediately previous raid. Hell, the Scythe of Elune leak shows it as a trinket that has the power equivalence of like 3 end game items in one slot..

Currently, the highest power weapon available is Might of Menethil (MoM) from last boss in Naxx40, and this was added at the very end of vanilla right before TBC launched. It was hilariously OP at the time but Blizz figured it wouldn't be a big deal since players would soon lvl up to 70 in TBC. However, this is a vanilla+ server with 60 lvl cap, so as it stands MoM is indeed overtuned, and any later weapons will logically be even more overtuned. My suggestion in the other thread is to squash the power level of end game items and keep MoM's power level (~95.0 dps) as the absolute max.

A few things to keep in mind about the Ashbringer in Vanilla.

 1) The Ashbringer is already in Vanilla. It's called the Corrupted Ashbringer. That's the current state of the legendary weapon that once had an entire website dedicated to searching it out during Vanilla. The Ashbringer was used by Renault to betray and murder his father, and that act of patricide completely corrupted the once Holy artifact. That's the story.

 2) It is impossible to cleanse the now corrupted Ashbringer according to Fairbanks. The corruption is far too great, which makes sense given how it was corrupted. Fairbanks could potentially be wrong about this, but given that he's both a powerful Priest of the Holy Light and was Mograine's most trusted advisor, he's the authority on the matter. It would also be against the server's philosophy to retcon Vanilla lore and say he was wrong.

 3) Fairbanks tells you that Mograine's other son (not Renault) is the only one who can forge a new Ashbringer, and that son is in the Outland (which is accessible in Turtle Wow [yet]). To be fair, Fairbanks does say this is a rumor, but again, from a storytelling perspective, it's not worth rugpulling this. One of the massive letdowns of TBC was Blizzard failing to continue the wonderful story it established in the Corrupted Ashbringer event, and it's something Turtle Wow should rectify.

 4) Additionally, Fairbanks says a new Ashbringer could be forged without the need of the old corrupted one, so Corrupted Ashbringer wouldn't and shouldn't be a requirement.

Could Karazhan house some item needed to forge a new Ashbringer? Possibly! Karazhan contains many powerful magical treasures splendid and mysterious. But the quest to create a new Ashbringer would inevitably take you to the Outland which again isn't a place you can visit on this server yet, and I don't believe they play on adding it with Kara40. In fact they've said the Outland will come last.
