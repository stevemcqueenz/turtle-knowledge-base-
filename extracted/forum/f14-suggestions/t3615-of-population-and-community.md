# Of Population and Community

## Post 21834 by Crommush — 2022-08-10T18:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21834#p21834 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there, I will preface my post by saying I'm actually one of the new faces that I'll be talking about down below:

So, what's this all about?

We all know that TWoW is getting a big influx of new players, either by word of mouth, youtubers, or a general desire for a new spin on the Vanilla WoW formula. The server's been growing in numbers day by day, breaking records left and right.  That's a great thing.

But when does this become an issue?
A big part of the original WoW experience to me was the sense of community it fostered with its game design and mechanics, but an often overlooked side is in its technicalities such as the limited amount of players that could fit into a single realm.
The original game's realms were made to house about 2-3k people before issues started to pop up, could it be time to consider an additional realm for the future? What do you guys think?

This is something we have talked about but no decision has been made on this or is close at the moment to be made.

The issue that will happen with opening another server as we have seen in the past with other projects is everyone rerolls on this new fresh server for the brand new experience. This then leaves the original server very low population and the new servers population just as high as it was when there was only one.

So opening a second server usually doesn't level out the population as one would expect it would and also causes issues with some of the existing playerbase.

Keep in mind this is not a no but no decision has been made on something like this as it is a fine line to walk.

## Post 21854 by Adunai (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-10T20:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21854#p21854 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The only reasoning for opening up a second server would be to make it PvP.

Other than that, it would be a highly-risky move with dubious advantages.

 1. We do not know what Turtle's population really is... or will be, when the peak goes down. There are many factors to consider - burn-out, WotLK classic, that new vanilla server, etc. You can never please the FRESH crowd anyway.

 2. For all intents and purposes, the current Turtle realm is reasonably fresh already, *everfresh*.

 3. Netherwing opened a second TBC realm - the first one died. The only one able to open realms while maintaining the original server was Nostalrius - an outlier. (Of course, Molten and Circle, too, the largest in the industry.)

 4. What is it to gain, really? I highly doubt there's a stable population for 2 Turtle realms - in the best case scenario, the original one will return to 300 players, in the worst, it will die, and many will be unhappy.

My advice would be - wait and see. If the population increases exponentially to 5-6k, with no signs of stopping/dropping - by all means. Until then, it would be risky and premature.
