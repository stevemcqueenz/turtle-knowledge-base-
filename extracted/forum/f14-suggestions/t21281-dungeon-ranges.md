Hey!

Admittedly I only did low-level dungeons in Ambershire, up to BFD at the moment.
What I see is that the instances got much more challenging, which is fun.
But making the content harder and not narrowing (or even widening) dungeon level ranges is a bad call imo.

F.ex. when I was doing DM and WC, I've seen many parties with this logic:
 * Party formed
 * Someone was level 16
 * Someone else left instantly

People will see the new dungeon popping up and queue, while now the lower limits are really questionnable.
