# Undo Button

## Post 102834 by 2jswrite — 2024-10-09T21:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102834#p102834 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Put an undo button on the talent tree. Just do it.

Its for those times when you misclick, accidentally click something or immediately know that you made a mistake.

 1 gold is waaay too much for a misclick. 5 gold is an out-of-touch power trip to put players in their place over a misclick.

I, for instance, have one stupid point wasted where it shouldn't be and I will NEVER be able to afford to fix it. A whole level wasted, for nothing. 1/10th of a second to turn hours of work into wasted trash.

And that could be fixed in an instant with a button that lets people go "Oops, *undo*". Just that.
