# Pull the plug on powershifting

## Post 117277 by Eyeburn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-19T18:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117277#p117277 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been a powershift enjoyer.  The playstyle has its charms.  I understand the appeal of "winning a bad hand" because of what is essentially an oversight by the original devs, but "use shred on every gcd" is braindead as a rotation.  Remove the wolfshead effect and go from there.

Powershifting isn't just strong, it is a broken effect.  Wolfshead lasted through not just all of vanilla, but remained a bis head slot item for the entirety of TBC.  The only thing that stopped it in WotLK was Furor being changed to retain energy instead of granting a set amount.

There's zero depth to wolfshead and cat dps.  The idea of suddenly producing 60 energy might seem like an avenue to strategic options, but it's just shred ammo, every time.  Trying to retain this playstyle means that cat dps can't be buffed in other ways without breaking it further.
