# Glyphs/Challenges for level 60s

## Post 107319 by Twili — 2024-11-10T02:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107319#p107319 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We've got tons of leveling challenges with some neat rewards, even if they are just vanity. But as more get added to the game we're forced to level a new character if we want to get those rewards.

Not sure how it could be implemented, but maybe having some way for max level's to get these rewards would be cool too. That or just give max levels their own glyphs/challenges to do all together.
