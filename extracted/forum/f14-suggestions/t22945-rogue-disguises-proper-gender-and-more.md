Can Rogue disguises get some more love?

1. First, make all starter zone Rogue trainers start off the disguise quest. It's very unfun to go out of your way to another race's starting zone, just to pick up a quest
2. Have the disguise represent your gender. If I'm a female character, I want the unit I disguise into to be female too.
3. Add more disguises. Have some hidden in dungeons or raids. Accessible solo, but the danger of reaching the vendor will be quite exciting and fun!
