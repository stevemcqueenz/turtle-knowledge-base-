> **Elenar wrote: Wed Apr 05, 2023 12:49 pm**
> Some rare or hard to acquire items.

I disagree with this part in particular. If the shop ever adds item models for transmogging, the models should be unique to the cash shop.
One of the fun things about endgame in Vanilla (even with transmog in Turtle) is just seeing people with cool-looking, hard to obtain armor and weapons running around the world. You see that player in a dungeon or in Warsong Gulch or out farming Winterspring and you know they've grinded and did dungeons to get that piece of armor, even if they solely use it for transmog since they still have to obtain it to transmog into it.
It would defeat a lot of the vanity aspect of endgame if players simply bought statless transmog items from the cash shop that look like raid gear and tier sets.

The models of transmog armor/weapons in a cash shop should be unique to the cash shop and not share models with items obtainable in dungeons/raids/quest rewards/etc.
Maybe the item models can be backported from later expansions.
