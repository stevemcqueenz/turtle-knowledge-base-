I disagree with moving the destination of the Alliance Portable Meeting Stone.

This item was created in order to encourage roleplay by creating a roleplay hub for each faction hence the description "Use it to meet new friends".  The discussion of location took place here [viewtopic.php?f=17&t=111](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=111) where members “Guffnat”, “Zazzi”, “Valjan”, “Eos”, “Casanova” and “Fast” voted for The Crossroads and members “Thurimar”, “Seabraid”, “Laizek”, “Neinet”, “Recusant” and “Sotnekron” voted for Stormwind Old Town.

The prevailing argument for The Crossroads was:
“While Valley of Honor would be the 'standard' Horde roleplaying hub, I'm more in favour of moving it out into the world as we're not necessarily beholden to what happens in retail. Crossroads is nice thematically as a halfway point between Orc/Troll lands and Tauren lands, with direct paths to Stonetalon Mountains, Ashenvale, Durotar, and Thousand Needles, so there's plenty of reasons for adventurers and travellers to be out and about.” -Zazzi 09 Oct 13:10

Your reasons for changing the location are based in PvE and game mechanics not RP and are therefore not subject specific.  I have responded to each of your points below.

“Both would have their first (human/orc) FP with the fetch quest close by.”
Fetch quests and flight hubs has nothing to do with RP.

“Both would have their level ~20 dungeon close by. “
Proximately to dungeons of any level has nothing to do with RP.

“Both would have it outside of a capital (Mages can already port to Stormwind).”
Both being outside capitals and mages already being able to teleport to major cities has nothing to do with RP.  Having both outside a capital, would be fairer in terms of using the Meeting Stone as a second Hearthstone, although that is not its intended purpose.  This is however a game mechanics argument not an RP one.

Since designating the Pig and Whistle Tavern as a “Roleplay Hub” a few RP guilds have used it as a place of RP events, “The Red Fist” and “Bastion of Lordaeron” for example.  Moving the teleport to the Westfall only stands to reduce RP.  Westfall Inn is in the middle of nowhere compared to The Crossroads.  The Crossroads stand as the name suggests at a crossroads near the geographical centre of Kalimdor.  This means that a lot of travellers will be passing through and therefore the potential for RP is high.  Even if you were to use the stone as a second Hearthstone The Crossroads is in a better spot than Stormwind as you can get fast flights to anywhere within the continent.  Westfall on the other hand is out of the way and not in the geographical centre.  No one goes there unless they are questing or assaulting the Deadmines, which is PvE content.

If anything I think it is outrageous that the Horde gets a central location like The Crossroads and I suggest that the Horde destination be moved to Orgrimmar so they can suffer just as much as the Alliance.
