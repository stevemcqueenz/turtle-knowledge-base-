# Third "Faction"

## Post 54904 by Whalemilk (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-28T00:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54904#p54904 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Okay, it's not really a third faction.

This might be hard to express, but it would be interesting to have a third "faction" which would operate as sort of a lore category. This would allow the devs to create isolated experiences that do not interfere with the regular game. These isolated experiences would prevent interactions, communications, and gameplay with the regular factions.

Example.

Have a "third faction" race Naga.
Racials don't need to be balanced as they will only be played with each other.
Full emote animations and armor visuals do not need to be completed as this is not required.
Only provide enough gameplay as needed, it is not required they create zones up to 60.

Essentially, they could create questlines which allow you to play as a naga and learn more about the lore that would otherwise not be able to be shown.

Just a thought.

## Post 55051 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-07-29T11:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55051#p55051 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Why is everyone obsessed with Nagas? First there was some guy who was really optimistic that Turtle WoW should have a Naga playable race despite all the technical limitations and would go on tangents explaining how it's actually very easy to add into the game and how much sense it makes. It was explained to him many times, by different people that it's neither easy, nor it makes sense, but he kept going until people gave up on trying to explain to him.

Now I see almost the same thing here, but mostly the Naga part... so I keep asking myself what's this obsession with Nagas?

I like the idea for a 3rd faction, but one has to really think hard what races will this faction be comprised of. I can for certain say that races like Ogres, Nagas, Murlocs, Satyrs and whatever other crap there is are not good candidates for a third faction.

The only way I can see a third faction is races from the Horde and Alliance defecting from their faction to establish a 3rd faction that opposes both the Alliance and the Horde and is at war with them. I can imagine this faction having a capital city on some remote island in the middle of the ocean that is still not implemented into the game, the faction having a mixed architecture of all the races involved - Humans, Dwarves, Elves, Gnomes, High Elves, Orcs, Trolls, Tauren, Goblins, Forsaken.

But then comes the most important question:

 *What will be the purpose of this faction?* Will it be some PvE quest device? Will it be about PvP too? Will it add 3-way battlegrounds and arenas or some other kind of 3-way PvP?

And probably the REALLY most important question of all:

 *What will Turtle WoW and its players benefit from this addition to the game?*

I, for one, don't see much benefit from this even though I like the idea. The problem here is games like Dark Age of Camelot or The Elder Scrolls Online were designed from the ground up to be 3-faction, but World of Warcraft was always deigned from the ground up to be 2-faction, so adding a third faction may not only be technically unfeasible, but it most likely won't even change the game dynamic AT ALL.

For me, a third faction won't change absolutely anything and won't make the game better, just like adding new playable races did not change anything and it did not make the game any better. I leveled up a High Elf character to 60 so I've experienced it and it didn't feel any different than playing any other race.

The things that will really change the game and improve it, in my opinion are:

 - a rebirth system that allows you when you reach level 60 to talk to some NPC, receive a soloable quest, complete it and reset back to level 1 with some slightly improved attributes or altered attributes
 - new classes
 - further changes to existing classes, but much bigger changes
 - adding new items into the game, starting from level 1 all the way to 60, such as new green, blue and purple items with interesting stats and effects that can really change the gameplay in fun and unpredictable ways
