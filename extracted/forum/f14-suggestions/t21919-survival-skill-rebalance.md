# Survival Skill Rebalance

## Post 151851 by Upandadam — 2025-09-15T18:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151851#p151851 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The survival skill seriously needs to be reworked. Buying torches to get to lvl 60 is fine, but after that you're completely out of options. Without bright campfire you have to wait 2 hours to craft travelers tents just to gain 1 level. The Iron Lantern that you don't unlock until 90 is already grey by the time you get it and the other options are more expensive than tents so there is nothing to help with leveling progression. By the time you get to farming even after doing the quest its barely worth it. Leveling cooking is much faster and the food is almost always better. There is currently almost no incentive to level past tents. We either need better crop effects or additional high level recipes for this to be anywhere near worth the investment of time and materials.
