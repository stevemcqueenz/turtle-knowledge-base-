# PvP Queue upgrade proposition

## Post 144639 by Jeetkwondo — 2025-08-11T20:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144639#p144639 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
since its sometimes very hard to get a bg to pop up, especially on 40-49 or 50-59 lvl bracket I'd like to suggest few relatively simple ideas to benefit the queuing process.

 1 - Upgrade the queue system so that it is visible how many people are in the queue at the moment. This will possibly attract more people to join the queue since without such knowledge, you're in the dark wether the bg might pop up in 5 mins or 10 hours.

 2 - Add pvp communication channel in chat. One general and potentially one for each lvl bracket. Once you know that there are for example 3 v 5 in queue, you can join the channel and ask others to join.

 3 - A 24hr scheduler for bgs; you pop u button and window opens up - there are potential future bg's every 30 minutes that you can sign up to in form of calendar. 1 pm - AB , 1:30 pm - WSG, 2 pm - AB, 2:30 pm - WSG etc. Make this schedule for 24hrs in advance for people to sign up. Set a minimum required players for the the bg to start so that empty ones don't pop up over and over. A 3v3, 4v4 or 5v5 minimum - up to debate.

I assume main reason why people are hesitant to queue up is because you never know when it's gonna pop up. Can be when you're in the middle of dungeon or right in the middle of when you went to a shop or do some chores. Or not pop at all for few or more hours.  Once the scheduling times are there to see for everyone, I think it would make bg queueing more desirable for many people.

The first 2 seems like a no brainer for me - easy (I assume, im no programmer) to implement. 3rd would require more work but seems possible. Please let me know If those ideas are rational or am I missing something. If this is not gonna be implemented let me know why is it. Thanks. Peace.
