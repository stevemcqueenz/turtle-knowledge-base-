# Quest log Increase to 30 or 40 for HC Characters

## Post 124345 by Blufive — 2025-03-29T15:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124345#p124345 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Why Increasing the Quest Log Limit in Hardcore Mode is a Smart and Balanced Change

The current 20-quest limit in World of Warcraft Hardcore presents an unnecessary restriction that negatively impacts the pacing, efficiency, and overall enjoyment of the game. Increasing the cap to 30 or 40 would enhance the Hardcore experience without making it easier or less challenging. Here’s why:

 1. Encourages More Strategic Questing Without Overburdening Players
Hardcore mode already emphasizes strategic play, but the 20-quest cap often forces players to abandon quests prematurely, even if they are still relevant. Increasing the cap to 30 or 40 would allow players to plan their questing routes more efficiently, leading to a smoother, more immersive experience without fundamentally altering the difficulty of combat or survival.

 2. Reduces Backtracking and Wasted Travel Time
WoW's world is vast, and Turtle WoW expands upon it even further. Many quests are clustered across multiple zones, requiring frequent travel. The 20-quest cap forces players to return to quest hubs unnecessarily, adding tedious and repetitive travel rather than meaningful gameplay. A higher quest log limit would alleviate this issue while preserving the integrity of Hardcore mode’s challenge.

 3. Supports Group Play and Dungeon Questing
In Hardcore, players must be extra cautious in dungeons, often waiting until they overlevel the content before attempting it. However, the 20-quest limit discourages players from picking up dungeon quests in advance. With a 30- or 40-quest limit, players could hold onto these quests until they feel ready, allowing for a more flexible and cooperative Hardcore experience.

 4. Better Accommodates Story-Driven and Class-Specific Quests
Turtle WoW offers additional lore-rich content, and many class-specific or profession quests require holding onto multiple objectives at once. A slightly increased quest limit would make it easier to engage with these immersive elements without constantly having to abandon progress in other areas.

 5. No Impact on Difficulty—Just Convenience and Quality of Life
Increasing the quest log cap does not make Hardcore mode "easier"—it does not grant additional XP, better gear, or reduced enemy difficulty. It simply reduces the artificial restriction on quest management, allowing players to enjoy the content without frustration.

Raising the quest log limit to 30 or 40 would be a non-intrusive, quality-of-life change that aligns with Turtle WoW Hardcore's spirit of immersion and strategic gameplay.
