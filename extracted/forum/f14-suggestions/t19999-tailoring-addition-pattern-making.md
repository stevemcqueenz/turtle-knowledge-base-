# Tailoring addition: Pattern making

## Post 136726 by Ayesha (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-23T12:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136726#p136726 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi Turtlers.

Tailoring has become pretty much useless after the bloodvine nerf. The usefulness now comes down to being a mooncloth cd bot or being able to craft Truefaith Vestments that are going to be replaced in BWL/AQ40 which most guilds on the server are already farming. Most other valuable items are Bind on Equip, so there is little incentive to actually level tailoring yourself instead of asking a guildie/guildie's alt/random to tailor something for you.

So I suggest adding some quirks to Tailoring that are purely cosmetic/transmog related to bring more life into it.

 1) My first preposition is Pattern Making. The basic idea is that you can make a one-time usable item, a pattern, that adds a t-mog appearence to another player's library based on any item that a tailor can equip, including plate, mail and leather. You get access to that ability after completing a questline that asks you to collect a set of magical pattern making tools such as scissors, metal bars to make pins, a set of rulers, a tape, etc. Most of these are dungeon drops from places like LBRS, DM W, summonable t0,5 bosses and UBRS. After you complete the quest, you can create a pattern using Mooncloth, Runecloth bolt and Arthas' Tears (or any other underused herb).

Casters wearing plate might sound immersion-breaking to you, which is understandable. To that I would say, I wholeheartedly support adding the client option to be able to turn off t-mog for all players, as has already been suggested by other players. Besides, male paladins wearing pink dresses and rabbit ears who tank the fiercest bosses in Naxx and Kara sounds more immersion-breaking than a chain wearing caster, at least in my opinion. So I don't think that it would contribute to the existing "immersion breakingness" in any meaningful way.

I understand that there might be some client limitations, so I would be curious to hear people who understand it more weigh in on the issue.

 2) In case client limitations don't allow for it and it's too much dev resources for not enough output in the minds of the devs, I have another proposition. Add more Bind on Pickup as well as Bind on Equip t-mog options to tailoring. Items like White Woolen Dress, White Wedding Dress, and Festival Dress are already there, so it would make a lot of sense to add more options, including chain looking cloth. (Woolen knitwear can look like chain, so...). It can be recolored tier sets, PVP sets, tier sets from TBC or Lich King.

I understand that t-mog purchases are one of revenue sources for the devs, so maybe the options that tailors can craft shouldn't be too extravagant and over-the-top and mostly be the appearances already present in the game but not available for that class.

Maybe my propositions are unrealistic in terms of their implementation and cuckoo-bananas in general, but t-mog and t-mog collecting are one of my fav things in the game. And I'm sure I'm not the only one. So why not bring life and new RP aspects to the profession that now is a lot of the times for 35 lvl mooncloth cd alts?
