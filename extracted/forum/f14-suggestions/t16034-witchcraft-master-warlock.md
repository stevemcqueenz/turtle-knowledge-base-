Witchcraft Master(巫术师)
 ----- Cassia ----
Voodoo
 -----
 1. Voodoo/Voodoo Restoration: Main damage output method, dealing continuous damage to enemies (Synergy Talent: Triggers Shadow Arrow during damage process), healing allies (Synergy Talent: Causes a single instance of damage upon completion).
Talent Skill Poison Cloud: Each instance of damage from Poison Cloud refreshes Voodoo.
 2. Deadly Spores: Creates a spore cloud in a designated area, causing a disorienting effect at the end, similar to C'Thun's Eye of the Tentacle skill.
 3. Voodoo Curse: The type of curse is determined by the idol type, which is tentatively set.
 4. Nightmare: Puts the target to sleep and silences them for 2 seconds, during which they can move. Hate equals the effect of MS Mind Control.
Talent Skill Catastrophic Bone Bell: Links the caster and the enemy, sharing damage.
Talent Skill Cataclysm: Absorbs natural or shadow damage, causing different effects based on the idol type. --- Works in conjunction with the Voodoo Curse skill.
Talent Skill Limited Invincibility (Physical/Magical): After Cataclysm, provides a beneficial effect that increases group magic immunity when absorbing enemy damage and boosts single-target physical immunity when absorbing ally healing.
Talent Skill Amplification: Increases damage based on the percentage of health recovered over 5 seconds.

Mutation
 -----
 1. Active Damage (Can't come up with a name!): Attacks increase physical damage.
 2. Active/Passive Damage Taken Buff: Body Strengthening --- +Strength Agility, gains a beneficial layer upon release.
 3. Active Damage --- Mental Strengthening: Percentage spell damage + spell power % + increases physical attack strength buff. Consuming a layer of Body Strengthening increases Intelligence/Spirit/Stamina.
Rage, duration increases based on the number of consumed strengthening layers. Immune to magical damage and trapping effects, but not immune to stun effects.
Talent Skill Active Damage --- Limb Strengthening: Next attack deals increased physical damage, percentage weapon damage + movement speed/attack speed, consumes one layer of Rage, increasing speed/dodging/parrying. Cannot be cast if no Rage layer exists.
Talent Skill Sacrificial War Song: Cannot be used in combat, a channeled skill that casts on animals, granting a strengthening buff based on the animal type for 30 seconds to 2 minutes.
Talent Skill Frenzy: Attack speed increases by 0.5/1/1.5/2/3, all attributes enhanced by 1%.

Ancestor
 -----
 1. Mirage Dust - Fairy Dust, slow healing
 2. Soul Link - Cast on allies, shares healing, similar to a Paladin's Beacon.
 3. Thorn Growth - Damage, binds without silencing, allowing skill use.
 4. Natural Growth - Healing, a channeled area-of-effect heal.
 5. Witchcraft Potion Making: Profession crafting, creates functional potions to resist voodoo, voodoo curses, and other negative effects.
Talent Skill Enhanced Nightmare - Upon skill completion, teleports to the moved position and deals damage to surrounding targets.
Talent Skill Reversion: All slow recovery effects accelerate. Target can recover health over 5 seconds in combat.
Talent Skill Encouragement: Based on health recovered over 5 seconds, proportionally increases slow healing effects.

Warlock(妖术士)
 ----------
Venom
 ---
 1. Active Physical Attack: Lashing, a ranged physical attack (plant magic physical damage, spell model based on the Green Dragonling - Flower in the Dragonbone Wasteland).
 2. Active Synergistic Attack: Both pet and owner deal a single instance of physical damage simultaneously.
 3. Active Spell Attack: Corruption, shadow magic damage.
 4. Aura Skill: Thorn Spike, a reflection aura. Talent Enhancement: Slows movement/attack speed within radius.
 5. Pet Healing: A channeled heal for the pet.
Enhanced Taming: For example, bears learning Thunder Stomp, wind snakes learning the Viper's venom.
Master's Call: Similar to a Hunter's skill, removes movement restrictions.
Talent Skill Enhanced Bestiality: Increases the endurance of both master and pet.
Talent Skill Enhanced Pet Healing - Increases the amount of healing.
Talent Skill Hunting: Increases the movement speed of both master and pet by 15%.
Talent Skill Summon Swarm: Similar to a cockroach skill, uses materials to summon if there are no corpses available.
Talent Skill Role Activation, Pet Skill: Bestiality, determines the effect based on the pet's skills. The pet also releases its unique skills.
Talent Skill Enhanced Enslavement: Boosts the pet's physical attack strength and endurance equivalent to a percentage of the master's.
Talent Skill Summon Animal Spirit: Summons a temporary animal spirit to assist in battle based on the idol type.

Malevolent Spirit
 ---
 1. Active Spell Attack: Shadow Assault, a backstab attack causing shadow damage.
 2. Active Physical Attack: Wild Swing.
 3. Active Stun: Elbow Strike, similar to Maron's Elbow ~~~ stuns for 5 seconds.
 4. Aura Skill: Similar to Devotion + Defense.
Talent Skill Curse: All attacks by hostile targets cause splash damage, indiscriminate area damage including the target itself.
Talent Skill (Name Unknown) - Links the caster and the target.
Talent Skill Self-Cast Curse - Enemy attacks splash to all, indiscriminate area damage including the target itself.

Spirit of All
 ---
 1. Dreamwalking: Consumes a large/all mana to restore the target's health and mana, lasting 5 seconds. Ends by teleporting to the moved position.
 2. Animal Spirit: If the pet dies, it cannot be revived and becomes a spirit until the end of the battle. It can be revived after the fight.
 3. Vigilant Sentry: Vigilant Sentry + Dreamwalking, long-distance teleportation.
 4. Warlock Potions: Profession crafting, extracts herbs and minerals to create temporary buffs or restorative magical items.
Talent Skill Wilderness Hunter: Critical hits or damage against beasts, humanoids, and creatures increase by 1%.
Talent Skill Necromantic Mastery: Increases armor penetration against undead creatures.
Talent Skill Compensation: Consumes current slow healing effects, turning 5-second healing into 5-second damage.
Talent Skill Consume Damage: Stores healing/damage. Absorbs all buffs and DoTs, storing as healing (up to XXXX) or dealing damage equal to three times the 5-second healing effect.
Talent Skill Bloodlust: After using Compensation, any healing/damage skill increases personal 5-second recovery effects, stacking up to 5 layers.
