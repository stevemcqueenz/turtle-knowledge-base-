# Priest Racials in Shadow Form

## Post 108840 by Scr — 2024-11-20T04:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108840#p108840 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, I would just like to suggest making each Priest racial ability available in Shadow Form.

I would like to ask for the High Elf racial Sun's Embrace, the Human/Dwarf racial Desperate Prayer, and the Night Elf racial Elune's Grace to also be castable in Shadow Form. There isn't much reason for Spriests to be disallowed from casting these spells, especially since Holy Nova was given to Spriests which takes away the reason of "It's a holy spell". I believe that a player with the race for these spells should be able to cast them at any time. There is already a precedent for this with Fear Ward being made a Discipline spell before it was made baseline, I don't see why this can't be done for all holy racial spells.

The only one of these spells that might pose a problem is Desperate Prayer, which might be a bit powerful as an added heal option for Shadow in pvp. However, I personally do not see it as game-breaking and could add an extra bit of choice in Racials for Spriest. I mainly wrote this post because I play a High Elf Spriest, and I wanted to use the racial without having to spend two GCDs dropping and reapplying Shadow Form.

Thank you for reading, and I accept any counter-points. (Even "I don't want Spriests to be more powerful in pvp")
