# New Low Level Relic Ideas (Libram/Totem/Idol)

## Post 98018 by Tzaaav — 2024-06-28T04:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98018#p98018 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's not a secret that the relic item slot has long felt neglected, so I was pleasantly surprised to see that twow added a bunch more relics to the game, and I loved seeing mentions in the new patch notes about more relics coming to lower-level dungeons (ZF, Lib, even WC).
However, I'd like to suggest some more :)

All the other classes have a very wide variety when it comes to their weapons and ranged slots, although to be fair usually one of those slots is mainly used as a stat-stick while relics are often more interesting.
Still, I think there's room to add more relics variety to lower levels as well to enhance the hybrid gameplay styles.
For starters, I wanted to propose some relic ideas that imo should be obtainable from class quests, which will make them more obtainable and impactful on the leveling journey.
It's important to make sure these are effective in lower levels but don't scale to become too powerful at max level, so flat increases or effects that are not combat-centric are good options in my opinion.
I'm going to present those ideas by level brackets and in a broader sense of what they should bring to the table, rather than a specific "item X does Y".
It's also a bit of a long post, but hopefully even if none of those ideas are adapted then at least it'll be some nice food for thought:

Lvl 5~10:
It will be nice to introduce a relic that gives an immediate "oomph" to the three instant cast abilities; Earth Shock, Judgement, and Moonfire, like adding a certain amount of spellpower (e.g. effectively doubling the damage they do) but as a trade-off you receive a debuff that lowers your spellpower too (for maybe a minute?).
The idea is that every other mob pull or so, the player can have a powerfull cast, changing the rotation so that not every pull is essentially the same. Admittedly this might be a harsher penalty on Druids since they play more as a caster in lower levels than a melee hybrid like Shamans and Paladins.

Lvl 15~20:
Leaning more into the tanking/healing capabilities of these classes, giving players an incentive to queue for dungeons as these archetypes over DPS.
It could also be nice to tie this one to WSG, as an introduction to PVP for newer players (similar to the Horde Defender's Axe) and maybe as an incentive for more players to queue for BGs in this bracket, too.
Note: armor is the flat stat that's easiest to scale to a lower level I guess, so some of these that are not based on armor values might be too much.
Healing idea A: Healing Touch / Healing Wave / Holy Light - grants the target +armor for a short duration, basically a mini-inspiration/ancestral healing.
Healing idea B: Healing Touch / Healing Wave / Holy Light - crits grant the caster some mp5, so a less reliable gameplay mechanic at this level, but in a dungeon-healing setting it will definitely see some procs.
Tanking idea - Paladin A: Something to do with Ret aura can be nice since it's obtained at this level bracket and is very useful for AoE tanking (which before Consecration at lvl 20 is not really possible), so maybe increase the threat generated by the Ret aura damage?
Tanking idea - Paladin B: Another idea is to have Seal of Righteous also apply a buff that increases the paladin's armor, so stacking it with Devo and actually not judging it (or reapplying quickly?) can be an interesting playstyle for hard hitting bosses or just harder encounters.
Tanking idea - Druids A: Similar to Ret aura but for Thorns while in Bear form.
Tanking idea - Druids B: A bit more convoluted but maybe something to do with the rage mechanic, so Bear form will grant an armor buff but the more rage you have the lower the armor value gets (maybe achieve that with stacks of the buff?) essentially encouraging players to spend rage effectively.
Tanking idea - Shaman A: Imo they really need a little push towards effective AoE, so again maybe similar to Ret aura but for Stoneskin Totem or Lightning Shield.
Tanking idea - Shaman B: Same line of thought, but maybe it's about giving Rockbiter a minor splash damage (reverse-Thorns haha).

Lvl 20~25:
I was thinking more utility/PvP focused, might be interesting to have a relic that gives a minor speed increase to Druid travel form (although this is level 30, so not sure about it), Shaman Ghost Wolf, and Paladins don't have a spell, but they can talent into speed in the Retribution tree so maybe add to that.
Other class-specific ideas that are less static:
Shaman: Enemies that are affected by Earthbind Totem have a chance to be stunned if hit by Nature damage - it's a rather niche totem, so giving it another purpose or functionality can be situationally interesting.
Paladin: Enemies that hit the Paladin while Blessing of Freedom is active have a chance to be feared, but the cooldown of BoF is increased (1 or 2 minutes?) - again a rather situational ability so making it more interesting can be cool.
Druid: They just get Cat form at this bracket so something to enhance that experience, maybe a chance to resist moving impairing effects while in Cat form? Or maybe have Rip specifically apply that buff, it's just that since neither Cat nor Rip are situational then this can't be something too impactful to not affect high levels.

Lvl 25~35:
Crescent Grove seems to be pretty popular already, but nudging new players into it can be another nice introduction to what twow has to offer.
I thought it could be nice to have a relic that has to do with the classes' resurrect abilities, like reviving players with some % more health and mana. I imagine a quest chain that revolves around the waters of the moonwell in the grove and how it has been tainted by the demons, and cleansing that water sample gives the class trainer an idea to use Enchanted Water as an extra reagent for resurrection. Then, while the relic is equipped it uses this item to apply that relic's buff to the resurrected players.
A bit of a tangent, but just something I had in mind :)

lvl ~40:
That's where we really start seeing the talent trees come to life, so adding three relics per class that help define those specializations even more can be really nice. I don't have ideas for all 9 options, but here are some:
Resto Shaman: casting Chain Heal grants a stacking buff (capped at a certain amount), then when you use any shock spell it refunds mana based on the number of stacks (so similar to combo points mechanics) - can be a static amount so that it's useful enough at lvl 40 but not game changing at 60. Alternatively, lean into the talent-tree-specific abilities, so have Nature's Swiftness or Mana Tide Totem do something extra.
Balance Druid: While in Moonkin form, Omen of Clarity procs also increase the next balance spell's power by a certain amount.
Holy Paladin: Holy Shock also applies a Beacon of Light buff (wink wink) that lasts for some short duration, which heals that target for a small amount every time the Paladin uses any healing spell. I can see it being really useful for group-wide damage situations where you toss a Holy Shock on the tank and then spot heals the rest of the group, but admittedly this goes a bit farther into the "changing the class gameplay" territory so maybe that's too much.

That's all I have, hope you like it!

Not necessarily for low level but here are some totem ideas (I am most familiar with shamans out of these 3):
 * Relic increasing the threat of rockbiter
 * Relic changing the 'effectiveness' of shields
 * Relic allowing healing way proc from lesser healing wave
 * Relic reducing totem's mana cost for the twisters
 * Relic reducing threat of nature spells

For low level I could only see minimal effects. Good effect with big drawback design for low level is either useless or breaks the meta.
F.ex.
 * Relic reducing healing wave casting time by 0,1 sec
 * Relic giving (low number) added damage prevention for stoneskin
 * Relic increasing weapon enh duration by 1 min

There are many interesting ideas here, but it is far too early to implement or consider any of these until after the new items and class sets and other class balance changes drop and we've had time to really dig into them.

 -the Paladin couldn't get any other better relic
 -the Paladin's Blessings of Freedom/Protection/Sacrifice become 'Hands' like in WotLK to not overwrite Blessing buffs; this is a major change and while it would be nice to see I'm uncertain of the class changes coming up if this is already going to be a thing or something else will happen
