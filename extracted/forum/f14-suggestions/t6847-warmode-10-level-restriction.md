# Warmode 10 level restriction

## Post 43226 by Drubarrymooer (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-04-26T14:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43226#p43226 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd appreciate putting a 10 lvl restriction on warmode similar to HC mode.  Warmode as it is makes it difficult to get out of starting zones and lower level towns like XR that are camped by 60s for sometimes hours on end.  I have zero chance at killing a target 45 levels higher than me.  If I file a ticket, the response is have people come and help you.  But you don't always have friends on.  And posting in world chat is very toxic and often just gets more of the opposing faction to come to the town that's already camped and causing it to be camped more, or "git gud", or "turn warmode off".  These folks aren't camping Thorium Point, or other levels in the 50's ranges.  It's always 20-30+ levels lower.  Their soul purpose is to ruin peoples time playing the game.  With the increase in population, getting spawn camped just makes leveling alts no fun.  And turning warmode off, not only gimps the leveling process, but robs the fun of world pvp.  We all know the joy of WPVP at Nesingwary's, for example.  But how many of us have had fun getting nuked at Nesingwary's by a 60?  I could be way off base with this and maybe I'm just missing the insane level of skill in pvp'ing someone 40+ lvls lower than you.

## Post 43229 by Greese (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-26T14:42:00Z

The XP boost from Warmode is nice, but with the buff to tents I think players who don't want to get ganked should just turn it off.  You can still have your PvP at Nesingwary, you just have to manually flag.  Your suggested fix isn't without merit, but it opens up additional cans of worms.  If you can only attack or be attacked by someone within 10 levels, what stops a lvl 49 player from ganking your 39 toon in STV all day now that 60's can't stop him?  It might take you a little longer to die, but the gap is still going to be there.  Now there will be less of a chance of retaliation against the ganker because they won't have to fear max level players.
