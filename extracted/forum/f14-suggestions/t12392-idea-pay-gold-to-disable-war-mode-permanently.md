# Idea; pay gold to disable war mode permanently

## Post 82506 by Snoodydood (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-12-24T03:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82506#p82506 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My idea is basically this; allow the NPC that can hide your pvp rank also give the option to let you pay to disable war mode forever. Why? Well simply because there are people( like me) who didn't read the fine print and ended up in war mode after disabling and then taking the glyph a second time.
Now i realize that this issue is 100% the fault of myself and anyone else who has done the same thing and is on us. I won't try to make any excuse for that, but i would still like to make this suggestion for people to be willing to sacrifice gold for the option if they want to.

I think permanently locking players OUR or IN warmode in part goes against the spirit of the server as casual and "where you left is where you can pick back up immediately". It removes from players the choice to change their playstyle essentially "Oh you don't like to pvp anymore? too bad you're stuck with it in this character, you have to reroll".

I propose that turning warmode on or off imposes a very long cooldown on the option to toggle it again, something around 2-3 months stuck in that mode is enough to keep it an important decision that requires thought, but not something that can do irreversible damage to your character and your enjoyment of the game.
