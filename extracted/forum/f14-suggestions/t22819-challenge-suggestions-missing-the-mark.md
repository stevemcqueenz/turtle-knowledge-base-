# Challenge suggestions missing the mark

## Post 159154 by Phomnk — 2025-11-11T03:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159154#p159154 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I've seen alot of forum posts lately about challenge suggestions that I feel really miss the mark when it comes to Turtle WoW. Most of them seem to be based on good ideas but inadvertantly promote (albeit likely unintentionally) boosting or exploitation.

All of the current TWoW challenges affect only the person doing the challenge, really without interfering with anyone else's gameplay. One could argue warmode affects other players, but those other players have essentially entered into a contract agreeing to interact in pvp. I've also seen complaints about hardcore players who are in zones that they would have otherwise outleveled taking nodes and mob tags from leveling normal mode players, but I can't see that as a huge problem. That will happen even with 2 normal mode players. Level 1 Lunatics may be the outlier here, to be fair, but that can only be an inconvenience for a short period of time for a leveling journey

What most of these challenge suggestions lack is the element of subtlety. More often than not, you probably wouldn't notice at first glance that someone is doing the vagrants challenge for example, unless you inspect them.

These challenges primary goal should be to 'challenge' the individual who's taken on said challenge.

A random thought that popped into my head the other night when I started a character to try out the craftsmans challenge was that the opposite to this challenge would be something like "The Quester" where you would only be able to equip items obtained as quest rewards. Something like this would only affect you, the player, and no body around you. I'm sure there's a hundred other challenge ideas with much more merit that would put this 2am thought to shame, but it got me thinking of all of these outlandish challenge suggestions I've seen lately.

In closing, the challenges that turtle offers (for the most part) appear to be well thought out, with a similar philosophy in mind; challenge the player, with the side effect of slowing down the leveling journey, all the while enjoying all of the amazing extra content the game has to offer.
