# Make it so that Elemental Precision reduces the chance for fire/frost spells to be resisted by 3% instead of 2%

## Post 165348 by Familymaker — 2026-01-16T16:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165348#p165348 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As the title states, right now Arcane mages have a disadvantage compared to frost and fire mages in raids, due to their talent spec allowing up to 10% chance to "hit with spells", whereas that of fire and frost mages only gives up to 6% chance to "hit with spells" with elemental precision.

This can easily be fixed in the following way:

 **Elemental Precision**
 *Reduces the chance that the opponent can resist your Frost and Fire spells by 3% per rank (up to 3 ranks).*

This way Arcane mages will still have more chance to hit, since they have to spend 5 ranks into that, while frost and fire mages will be less reliant on +% to hit items as much. Allowing for more viable gear options.

 **Note**: Yes, this would mean that fire and frost mages will get more per rank spend, but that is only fair seeing as the arcane talent tree is pretty loaded right now.
