(Please don't take this reply too seriously, just my take on things)

Turning one of the shaman's main threat generating spells into a taunt sounds like it would be detrimental to the spec if anything, as there are times as a tank where you'll want to use Earth Shock
to generate threat while not wanting to directly taunt the enemy onto you (off tank role for example)
on top of a whole lot of other issues such as being forced into having Earth Shock off cooldown instead of generating more threat. But I think your frost shock idea could work well.

Personally I think stoneclaw totem should be made into a real aoe taunt with the totem itself having its HP pool scale based on the shaman's, or even add earth elemental into the game for them to play around with. On the topic of shaman tanking, I think the dev team should focus on building on the spec's current strengths and class fantasy rather than taking from other classes. (Not all tanks need to have a direct single target taunt like warriors/bear - give them other tools instead) For example shaman tanks right now are great at mitigating damage from elemental type mobs which just feels right for the class. They take 12% reduced damage from frost, nature and fire damage types, have extremely high single target threat and their low armor does not matter at all when facing such enemies. They should make shaman tanks unique, cool and interesting and give players a reason to want them in their group for specific content, and not just apply band-aid fixes to make the class feel like another.

(Unfortunately there is also the sad reality that enhancement dps is currently suffering as a result of much of the talent tree being occupied by tank effects...)
