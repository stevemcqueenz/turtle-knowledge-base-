# Bring life to World PVP

## Post 7168 by Firevulcan — 2020-08-01T01:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7168#p7168 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since i've seen enough people interested in "Activating" World PVP, while leveling or at level cap, How about a way to please  those people?

My idea is: The Glyph of Battle

The Glyph of Battle can be obtained from Speedy and it will be placed n your Keyring, just like every other glyph.
While the Glyph of Battle is on you, it keeps you Flagged for PVP until you delete it and relog.
If you delete your Glyph of Battle, you can pick up a new one from Speedy.

This way, people who don't want to pvp in the free world are free to not take the item, and those who want to level or play with PVP permanently activated are free to do so!

Cheers,
