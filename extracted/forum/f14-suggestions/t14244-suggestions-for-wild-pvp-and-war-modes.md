# Suggestions for wild PVP and war modes

## Post 97109 by Acew00998 — 2024-06-16T11:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97109#p97109 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Due to the 30% increase in experience gain in the war mode, many players choose to turn on the war mode in order to quickly upgrade, which increases the reason for their innocent killing, and encourages the full level account to kill ordinary players at will.
It is recommended to adjust the war mode, or add a dishonor kill record, every player who kills below their level 3-5, the kill record or PVP record is registered as a "Dishonor kill", I think that killing a player lower than their level is a humiliating and bullying kill behavior, so I recommend adding this record. To guide players to find their own level or similar level of players to duel, this is important, but also a reflection of the right values.
