# Suggestion: Lower the effectiveness of Elixir of Poison Resistance and add a new, stronger version — Elixir of Greater P

## Post 138304 by McC5VgTS — 2025-07-07T02:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138304#p138304 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As you know, Elixir of Poison Resistance can cure up to four poisons up to level 60, which makes it disproportionately powerful given that it's crafted from only Large Venom Sac and Bruiseweed. I believe we should consider reducing its potency, and instead make Huge Venom Sac more valuable by introducing it as a key reagent in a new, stronger elixir — perhaps Elixir of Greater Poison Resistance — as a proper end-game poison solution.
