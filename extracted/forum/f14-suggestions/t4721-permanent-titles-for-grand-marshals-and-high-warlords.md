# Permanent titles for Grand Marshals and High Warlords

## Post 28234 by Pococurante — 2022-11-01T12:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28234#p28234 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi!

As the name of the topic says I would like to propose adding permanent titles for characters that have reached the highest PvP rank. Since titles are awarded for "extremely significant achievements", it would make sense to award them to players completing the gnarliest of grinds in the game. Especially since Naxx is out and many PvPers stop ranking at Field Marshal / Warlord because the weapons don't compete with those you get from raiding. Having an additional prestige reward dangling at the finish line would encourage players to still go for it.

Since the already available titles are placed beneath actual PvP rank + name, you would either need to make the final rank permanent or add custom ones - to avoid ending up with a weird looking double rank. In that case a cool solution might be to use something similar to the race specific city defender titles.

Curious to hear what you think!
