# Deepwood Pipe Improvements/Viability

## Post 110027 by Lutra23 — 2024-11-26T17:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110027#p110027 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Deepwood Pipe isn't powerful enough to justify its cost.
It takes 150 bright dream shards to acquire.
They are currently going at around 4-6g per BDS.
So, this is around 750g (almost the cost of an epic mount).

Problems with the Deepwood Pipe:
- 60 minute cooldown

- Aggro is not broken/reset

- Fears the user around (in druid shapeshift forms)

- Can't cast other abilities during the stealth

- Stealth is often broken during the fear (due to being feared into aoe or other players/mobs)

- Can't control your character or cast

I think this has the potential to be a really awesome item.
I'm proposing these changes to the Deepwood Pipe to justify its cost and make it more viable.

- 20 minute cooldown(in line with other similar trinkets like tidal charm)

- No fear effect

- Allow aggro to break

- Allow casting during the stealth effect but make it break on cast (not after)

- Allow full usage in shapeshift forms

- 6 second stealth effect

This would make it worth the cost and a much more valuable and interesting item.
It wouldn't be too overpowered as it would still have a 20 minute cooldown and would break on cast.
Due to its cost it wouldn't be overused by everyone (without significant effort to acquire the item).

Cheers and I hope you will consider/implement these changes.

 - Lutra (Tel' Abim)
