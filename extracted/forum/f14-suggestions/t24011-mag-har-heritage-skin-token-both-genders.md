# Mag'har Heritage Skin Token - both genders

## Post 168790 by Charredbranch — 2026-02-18T18:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168790#p168790 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Heya!   smiling_turtle_head

As some of you might know, Blizzard recently rolled out the TBC Anniversary servers on their client. Seeking a bit of nostalgia, I decided to give it a go (albeit to be honest I very much prefer TWoW). During my adventures in Outland, I came across some of the Mag'har Orcs. While the men were what I expected, I was pleasantly surprised by the look of the female model (as after the long time I had forgotten how it looked).

And so I started Turtle WoW, figuring I could actually make a character of that sort. Problem is, when I looked into the donation rewards, female orcs were not eligible for the mag'har heritage skin token, which felt very disappointing... Even more so as the model very much exists in the TBC files (same as the male version).   sad_turtle

Thus I decided to come here and try to make the suggestion of having the skin be available for both genders. Feel like it shouldn't be the hardest thing to do as no new assets would need to be created.

Thanks for reading!
