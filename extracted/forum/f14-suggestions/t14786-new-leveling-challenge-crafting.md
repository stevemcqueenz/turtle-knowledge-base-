Jewelcrafting has recipes to craft staves.
But for some classes you have to level 2 characters at once: one is for gathering materials and other is for crafting stuff.
For example to level rogue, you need daggers and leather armor. Same for enh shaman and melee hunter.
As a hunter you can level with engineering only because main power comes from bows/guns.
As a feral druid you totally fine as you don't really need weapon.
Mage, priest, warlock, boomkin druid, elemental shaman can level with tailoring. May be with enchanting to craft wands.
Warriors and paladins are fine. Blacksmithing+Mining covers all their needs.
Other way is to level character naked by tagging (with help of your lvl 60) and don't waste time to level professions at all.

UPD. Fishing might save melee leather wearers, because you can fish for boxes. They contain leather and tailoring bolts. Leather can be used to craft gear. Tailoring bolts (and fish) can be traded for ore/bars to level blacksmithing for weapons.

I hope they can add new craftables soon to help improve this challenge.
- Having no recipe for a fishing pole feels means we are effectively locked out of the fishing profession. Fishing seems like an appropriate profession to have if you are choosing to effectively live as a hermit. Having at least one craftable fishing pole would be great.
- The lack of white items for those also taking on the Vagrant's Endeavor makes it much more punishing to take the two challenges together than they are when taken individually. Adding more white recipes at higher levels would help a lot to alleviate this.
- Being limited to self-crafted bags makes it essentially mandatory to learn tailoring at some point. Leatherworking thankfully also has one 6-slot bag recipe, but having more recipes added to leatherworking and other professions might help keep the options open when choosing what type of 'artisan' you want to become.
I know it's supposed to be a challenge, but it doesn't really seem like the intent was to lock you out of so many systems. It feels like the intent was to force you to have to do more for yourself, but you end up being able to do less because of the lack of recipes.

The idea behind the challenge is really cool though, thanks for sharing it here.
