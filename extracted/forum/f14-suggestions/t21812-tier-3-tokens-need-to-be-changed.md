# TIER 3 TOKENS NEED TO BE CHANGED

## Post 151043 by Afraido23 — 2025-09-09T22:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151043#p151043 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are 3 types of tier 3 tokens currently in the game:

 1. Warrior and Rogue - 3 specs
 2. Priest, Mage, Warlock - 6 specs
 3. Paladin, Hunter, Shaman, Druid - 10 specs

As you can see, there is a disproportion between the number of available specializations. The result of that is Warriors and Rogues have the easiest time getting their items and in the meantime Paladins, Hunters, Shamans and Druids compete for their tierpieces and it's very hard for them to get something for offspec.

The easiest solution to this problem would be moving Paladins to Warrior and Rogue tierpieces. It's just a suggestion. Maybe you will have a better idea or you decide to rework T3 tokens completely. Adding new specs to tier pieces was awesome idea but it created new problem that - I hope - you will take care of.
