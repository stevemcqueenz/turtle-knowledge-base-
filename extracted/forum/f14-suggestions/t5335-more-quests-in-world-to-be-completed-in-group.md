# More quests in world to be completed in group

## Post 32485 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-01-12T09:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32485#p32485 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Right now, there are a lot of quests involve collecting some loot from enemies: meat, rings, bandanas. All kind of stuff.
Usually those quests are supposed to be done alone. Doing them in group don't really help, because party members compete for loot.
I propose to add special mobs, who drop more that 1 item per kill, but those mobs should require a party to kill.
For example Defias Mask Quartermaster who carry 10 [Red Leather Bandana](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=829) for quest [Red Leather Bandanas](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=153)
or may be Duskwood Brakoneer who carry 10 [Lean Wolf Flank](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=1015) for quest [Seasoned Wolf Kabobs](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=90)
Mobs about meat can be mutated or rare meaty ones. They could be enemy scouts who harvest a food for themselves.
Mobs about resources can be peon haulers, storehouse/caravan guards or something.
