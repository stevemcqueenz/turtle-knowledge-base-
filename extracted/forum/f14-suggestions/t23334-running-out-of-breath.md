# Running out of breath …

## Post 163129 by Sinrek (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-12-19T03:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163129#p163129 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**I** would like to suggest a new challenge for those of us who not only likes to play at our own pace, but would like to experience that vastness of Azeroth to its full potential.

 *New Challenge: **The Walker.***

- You walk when out of combat.
- You walk if not on (a rented) mount.
- You walk if not in Stealth (Prowl, Shadowmeld).
- You walk if not using Sprint (Dash, Charge, Intercept, Intervene).
- You walk if not (used) consumed temporarily increasing movement speed item.
- You walk even if you have your Turtle (any other) mount until level 40 (60).
- You walk only in HardCore challenge until level 60.
 **Reward:** you get the title **The Walker** and **[Barefoot Slippers]** (model ID 19391) that increase your movement speed by 30% for 9 seconds every 30 minutes on use.

Here's a small example and a proof it's not just one old crazy elf out there. We actually [exist](https://www.youtube.com/live/9kXN_YwyNa0?si=nGY_nhm0PrCzxrMa).
