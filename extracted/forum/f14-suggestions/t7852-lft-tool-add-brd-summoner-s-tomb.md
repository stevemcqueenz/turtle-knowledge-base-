# LFT Tool: add BRD Summoner's Tomb

## Post 50393 by Manateufel — 2023-06-17T16:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50393#p50393 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**For players in need of 7D loot**
The current problem lies in the fact that most players are focused on running the 'BRD Arena' or 'BRD Emperor' routes because those are the ones listed. However, when players choose the 'BRD Emperor' run, they almost always take the lava route, which doesn't grant access to the Summoner's Tomb. It's important to note that you don't have to jump into the lava to reach the Summoner's Tomb. 'BRD Arena' is mostly chosen by classes that specifically need the epic chest and don't want to go any further. Lastly, 'BRD' usually indicates a full BRD run, which can take up to 2-3 hours.
Players who need loot from the seven dwarves are often forced to do a full run of BRD to reach them. It would be great to have another option in the LFT Tool that allows players to specifically choose a BRD Summoner's Tomb / BRD 7D run.

 **Route for BRD 7D Run**
Enter the dungeon. Open the first door on your left, then turn right and open the next door. On the left side, there will be another door to open. Use the wheel to unlock the bridge and cross over it. The bosses you will encounter in this route are Angerforge (13), Golem Lord Angelmach (14), The Grim Guzzler (15), Ambassador Flamelash (16), and the Summoner's Tomb (18).

 **Estimated play time**
 30-60 minutes
