# Inventory enchancements

## Post 24784 by Rhynera — 2022-09-10T17:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24784#p24784 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Increasing stack size would make things more enjoyable without harming the classic feel. I've discussed this in the official Discord and many agree. One of the few things WoW did right was increasing stack limits for trade skill items to 200, and increased stacks for ammo (until we didn't need ammo), along with soul shards.

One person argued that 20 stack for flasks would be too much. This was not a popular opinion when said but my suggestion is to adopt the higher stack limits for:

Ammo
Soul Shards
Profession items (ore, herbs, leather, raw fish, raw meat, etc)

I think the increased stack sizes across the board like in current would be fine, but if the 5 stack for flasks is still desired, that's fine. Also, due to the amount of new items, the need to keep multiple sets of armor for different specs, etc, this would not devalue the larger bags that are in the shop--only make them more useful by extension as they could hold even more stuff inside.

Also I saw someone else suggest this but I'm piggybacking on it. Add a way to upgrade your default backpack. Either a gold sink or an actual progression path.

Personally, I think that the cash shop should stay as cosmetics and I would love to see the bags in the shop be available in the game in some shape or form. As a gold sink, achievement, or otherwise. But as money is being charged for them, I realize that's a harder sell. The suggestions on increased stack sizes and a way to progress to upgrade the default backpack would vastly improve the game though, I think.

## Post 24839 by Versidue — 2022-09-11T13:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24839#p24839 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think upping the stack size for prof items up to 200 *would* dramatically alter the vanilla feel. I'd support increasing the stack size of finished products like potions up to 20, but *200?* For raw mats?? That would absolutely change the way the game feels!

For better or worse, inventory management is a big part of the game loop, especially in early levels. Like, for example, with my level 15ish miner, I venture out into the wilderness to mine, fighting mobs along the way, until my inventory fills with ore and vendor trash. Then I head back to town, sell the trash, smelt my ore to compress them into bars, and head back out to do it again. And then when my inventory is full of bars, stone, and gems, I know it's time to head back to a main city to unload, level up my profs, train class skills, etc. If the stack size for all those mats was 200, I would barely need to worry about inventory management at all!

It would be cool if you could increase stack size for certain mats as a profession skill, though. Like if you're a high skill miner, you can train or do a quest that doubles the stack sizes for ore, bars, stone, and gems. That would make it feel earned. But I don't know if such a thing is even possible to implement...
