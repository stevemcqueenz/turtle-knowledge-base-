# wl demons. Balance them, instead nerf everything

## Post 171711 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-22T01:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171711#p171711 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Well, these are the most terrible changes from a game design perspective. If Demo were my main spec, I'd have deleted WoW already.

 **Voiced and overheard reasons for the changes:**
"Oh, the Greater Demons are so strong, sooo strong and durable - nerf"
"Oh, we don't like that several Warlocks on the entire server can farm Crocolisks in ZG, not cool, not cool - nerf"
"Oh, we don't like that Warlocks can cast three different cookies and use them with a 2-minute cooldown, a complete disaster, not intended - nerf"
What we have:
 1. The original Soul Link was 30%, we have a crippled 20%
 2. Greater Demons are now glass cannons. Felguard and Infernal have less armor than my pet cat in the MM spec. And the BM spec pet has even more survivability than Greater Demon. Absurd.
 3. Enslaved demons are still just as unbalanced and strong, they're just a little more tedious to heal now.
 4. The spec has become tedious and feels like a babysitter. You're constantly standing there casting Health Funnel, Health Funnel, Health Funnel. It's better to play BM Hunter than suffer like this.
 5. Black Book. The original +100% damage for all normal demons. We have a crippled version with +50% damage to normal and great demons. In other words, normal demons were nerfed for nothing.
 6. Cookies are crap that doesn't scale. The ability to have three different ones compensated for this crap, but now it's just crap. The one and only.

 **I beg you, I implore you, Dear Twow, Fire your current game designer and hire any normal who understands classes and math.**

The only correct actions were three soul shards per soul well and removing the -50% healing reduction debuff. This was incredibly stupid, as it had enough consequences that a demon wouldn't disappear like a shaman's wolves, but could smash a warlock to pieces.

 **This should have been done a long time ago, both instead of nerfs and for quality of life:**

 1. Instead demon speed in 1st row - Improved Void. This is №1 what everyone needs while leveling and original it was on 2nd row. But not 4th
 2. Balance the spells of ALL enslavable demons. Add a CD, cast time, and reduce the damage of those that are too strong. For example, in SWV, the imp Scorcher has a strong AOE without a cooldown. 1k each GCD for everyone, top DPS. In Dire Maul, there are Satyrs who have no mana, no energy, and no rage. If used correctly, they are top DPS, and this shouldn't be the case.
 3. Add the ability to re-enslave before the timer expires. It's annoyingly stupid and inconvenient that you have to press Dismiss or wait until the timer expires to re-enslave.
 4. Make the "cookie" object itself unique and add scaling. For Fresh 60, 1200 HP regen is a lot. For Naxx, Kara40  1200 HP regen is a modest bonus.
 4. Add base HP, armor, and damage scaling to ALL demons before talent bonuses. Imp with 1.7k HP is nonsense. It's just one axe swing. Same situation. For Fresh 60, everything is fine proportionally to the HP and damage of player characters. But in Naxx and Kara, it's almost useless and dies after one AoE. Thats why all not demo specs uses Sacrifice talent.

 **What should be changed/added to make the Demo spec interesting but not imbalanced in some aspects**
 1. Possibility: add an additional layer/stack of enslavement, extending the duration at the cost of more soulstones.
 2. Add unique, interesting spells for demons (party buffs, debuffs)
 3. Possibility to carry enslaved demons through Warlock summons. And through portals to raids and dungeons, if necessary, with a slight stat/damage downgrade to keep things within reasonable limits.
 4. Possibility to re-enslave a Greater Demon at least once.
 5. Expand the list of enslavable demons, with a downscaling of stats and damage while enslaved.
 6. Unleashed potential is a very good and sound idea, but it should be further developed. For example, crit with Fire spells will still inherit 5/10/15% SP and stack three times, along with a bonus to AP, and crit with Shadow spells will receive a bonus to Hit and Crit Chance instead of Demonic Precision.
 7. Power Overwhelming, consuming 40% to 30% HP.
 8. Spell: Summon the last enslaved demon (1 time)
 9. Rework/add talents for being good with greater demons, or being good with normal demons. Not a solid mess
