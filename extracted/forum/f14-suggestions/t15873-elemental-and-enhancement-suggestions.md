# Elemental and Enhancement suggestions

## Post 108122 by Parad1gm — 2024-11-16T11:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108122#p108122 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, few actively raiding Shamans have sat down and discussed some problems and possible suggestions with current state of dps shaman. Thanks to Kordinator, Zucker, Clench, Noshu, Vidda, Abstractz.

 **Elemental**

Damage-wise, Elemental is in the same state as before CC2, and I’ll explain why below. In terms of gameplay, we’re still bound to melee classes, which was one of the least enjoyable aspects of playing Elemental. Unfortunately, this wasn’t improved—in fact, it’s even worse now.

 1.
- ***PvP***
 - Elemental Shaman is a glass cannon in PvP, relying on a “kill or be killed” style. The playstyle has always been based on kiting to set up a one-shot combo. In PvP, you almost NEVER get the chance to stand and hard-cast your spells. The current form of Electrify essentially ruins Elemental in PvP, as it’s nearly impossible to stack in 1v1 situations. This ability needs some form of adjustment.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Allow shocks to grant Electrify stacks.
 - Reduce the maximum Electrify stacks to 3, with each stack granting a 33.3% critical damage increase.

 2.
- ***Electrify***
 - Stacking the new talent to 5 is time-consuming in fast-clear guilds, and the 20-second timer often causes stacks to fade just before the next pull. This mechanic is quite frustrating.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Extend the Electrify buff duration to 30 seconds.
 - Allow Chain Lightning to stack Electrify based on the number of targets, up to a maximum of 3.

 3.
- ***Close Range Gameplay***
 - Elemental Shaman performs significantly worse with a melee group than with a caster group. This is due to the need to provide Windfury, Strength, and Flametongue Totems for melee, which not only reduces effective casting time but also increases risk, often preventing the use of Chain Lightning.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Allow Elemental Shamans to wear more armor, and add shields with solid stats that are obtainable beyond world bosses (this wouldn’t affect PvP, as we already use Gluth and Sapphiron shields there). Update T3 stats and bonuses to make it viable on trash mobs. Currently, cloth and leather off-pieces outperform T3 on trash, but T3 pulls ahead on bosses.

 4.
- ***Fire Totems***
 - We received increased damage for Fire Totems and extended range for Searing Totem, only to end up as slaves to Flametongue Totem. This change negates all the buffs to Elemental by decreasing personal damage output, while also forcing us to drop three totems per pack of mobs in raids. Combined with the Tea nerf, this undoes the mana benefits gained from Elemental Focus.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Revert the stacking of Windfury Totem and Flametongue Totem.
 - Increase the mana returned by Totemic Recall for each recalled totem.

 5.
- ***Boss Damage***
 - Our boss damage still lags behind other classes, especially at lower gear levels. This is particularly problematic since most Elemental Shamans fall behind casters like Mages or Warlocks in gear priority.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Add the Nature school to Curse of Elements. This change wouldn’t impact PvP or trash damage but would solely boost our potential in the area where we’re most vulnerable.

 6.
- ***Gear Progression***
 - The new Elemental T3 bonuses make using 6 pieces of T3 comparable to using the best off-piece caster gear, like Yshgo-lar or Eyestalk Waist Cord, in terms of boss damage.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Adjust the stats and bonuses on Elemental T3 to improve performance on trash mobs, which would also boost Shaman armor.

 **Enhancement**

Although there were some initially promising design ideas, the spec has turned out to be a complete failure in the post-CC2 reality. Our DPS is only marginally higher than before the changes, and we don’t perform better than Restoration or Elemental Shamans outside of the small Bloodlust window. We need serious buffs to be on par with the Paladin class in terms of overall damage and enjoyable gameplay. Both classes provide raid buffs of similar power, and there’s no reason for Shamans to be this weak in comparison.

 1.
- ***Mana Problems***
 - Mana isn’t just an issue for single-target fights or longer boss rotations—it’s a GIGANTIC problem throughout the entire raid, especially on trash. It’s impossible to play without constantly using Mana Potions and Teas on cooldown, even more so than Elemental Shamans. The fact that we are the only class required to rebuff the entire party for every pack of mobs by re-dropping totems—now with an even shorter range without talents in Restoration—leaves us starving for mana. Not to mention the extreme cost of using Fire Nova Totem. Let's count: Fire nova (520mana) + WF totem (100 mana) + Strength of Earth (275 mana). Then switch to Magma (650 mana). That is already almost 1600 mana which is usually 1/3 of enhancement shaman's mana just to drop totems, not accounting for dps rotation, EACH PACK. Keep in mind that fixing enhancement's shamans mana issues might be the easiest way to buff the class while not making it excessively strong in pvp.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Make Totemic Recall refund 100% of mana.
 - Make Totems 30 yards baseline so we don't have to drop them as often.
 - Make Elemental Focus also reduce the cost of Nova, Magma, and Searing Totems.
 - Increase the mana return from Lightning Strike with Water Shield; currently, it’s barely worth using.
 - Reduce the cost of Storm Strike and Lightning Strike.

 2.
- ***Trash Damage***
 - Enhancement Shaman didn’t receive ANY improvements to address its biggest struggle—trash damage. This has always been a major factor holding the spec back in raids compared to other specs, and we had every reason to expect a significant change in this area. Fortunately, it’s not too late, and a few adjustments could improve this situation.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Add Attack Power scaling to Fire Nova, Magma, and Searing Totems.
 - Make Totemic Recall refund 100% of mana.
 - Make Elemental Focus also reduce the cost of Nova, Magma, and Searing Totems.

 3.
- ***Threat***
 - Enhancement Shaman threat is insanely high, making it almost unplayable without a trinket to reduce threat. This has always been a significant issue for Enhancement, and it’s even worse after CC2.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Fix Calming Winds (currently, it increases threat instead of reducing it).
 - Reduce the number of points required for the Flurry talent, making it easier to take Calming Winds without sacrificing other core talents.
 - Lower the base threat of Enhancement abilities.

 4.
- ***Windfury***
 - Despite adjustments to spell-related aspects of Enhancement damage, the majority of Enhancement’s damage still relies on auto-attacks and, by extension, Windfury. Getting massive Windfury procs has always been the most enjoyable part of Enhancement gameplay. Timing Stormstrike right after a weapon hit to proc Windfury and reset the swing timer added a fun layer of skill. Currently, the Windfury proc chance appears to be bugged and significantly lower than the intended 20%.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Allow Stormstrike and Lightning Strike to proc Windfury, making the gameplay smoother.
 - Fix the Windfury proc chance to match the promised 20%.

 5.
- ***Talents Adjustments***
 - Some of the talent changes feel underwhelming, especially given their placement deep in the Enhancement tree. Flurry feels weak for a 5-point investment, particularly with the current low Windfury proc chance. The Windfury component of Elemental Weapons is completely useless, and Bloodlust could benefit from a passive component.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Increase Flurry’s stacking to 20%, providing 4% per point.
 - Reduce Flurry to 3 points providing 5% per point. This will allow shamans to take threat reduction talents easier.
 - Change the Windfury component of Elemental Weapons to activate on miss, dodge, and parry.
 - Add a passive or active percentage mana return to the Bloodlust keystone.

 6.
- ***Itemization***
 - The post-CC2 design makes Enhancement Shamans far more reliant on mana, rendering old BiS melee DPS gear (Leggings of Apocalypse, Belt of Never-ending Agony, etc.) ineffective and locking the spec into using T3. This drastically limits gear progression.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - While this might be difficult to address without further nerfing Enhancement, which already feels weak at this point, we ask for better planning in future raids.

 7.
- ***Visible Buffs Cap***
 - With the amount of consumable Enhancement has to use to be competitive, it is usually impossible to see your own short buffs like shields or procs.
- ***Possible Solutions***
 - Build in some sort of opt-in buff filtering in the client.
