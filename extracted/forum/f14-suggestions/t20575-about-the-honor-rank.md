# About the Honor Rank

## Post 142230 by Cutcutcut — 2025-07-30T19:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142230#p142230 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, in the PVP event I participated in yesterday, I gained honor by defeating another player. When I logged in today, I found that my character had directly leveled up to RANK 2, skipping RANK 1.

This is the second time I've encountered such a situation, as I also skipped the Private rank before. If possible, I would prefer to level up step by step in order. That's why I chose to achieve this by killing a player in the wild instead of entering the battlefield.

I wonder if there is any way to revert back to RANK 1 or clear my PVP data. If not, I would like to suggest that in future updates, could such an option be considered? If we can't downgrade, it seems we can't freely choose the RANK we want to reach. Personally, it even gives me a sense of missing out.

Rather than constantly creating new characters, I'm more passionate about developing a single character to its full potential. That's why I hope we can minimize those irreversible actions that might occur when we are uninformed or acting unintentionally.

I’m sorry if I’ve been a bit long-winded. Many of these suggestions may not be very objective; I’m just trying my best to express my gaming experience. Thank you for reading.
