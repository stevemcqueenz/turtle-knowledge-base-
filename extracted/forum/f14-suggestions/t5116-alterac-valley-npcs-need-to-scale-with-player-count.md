# Alterac Valley NPCs need to scale with player count

## Post 30975 by Sieleyah — 2022-12-19T02:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30975#p30975 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there,

I'm going to say this harsh and blunt - Alterac Valley is a complete cancer on this server and this could so easily be fixed.
Everyone seems to be aware of the faction imbalance and the overtuned paladins, and yet nothing is being done about it, but that's not even the main issue. The unfortunate vanilla mechanics of there being no resource points to limit the length of the matches cannot be helped either, that's an essential part of vanilla.

However even a 6hours long battleground could be enjoyable if it was about PVP and not struggling against overpowered elite NPCs because there are only 15-20 players with no healers and tanks.
You can't increase the amount of players which queue, but you can limit the power of the NPCs to be fair against a smaller sized raid group.
We need a scaling implemented that weakens enemy NPCs proportionally to how many players are missing from the game on each side (similar as the scaling you have in raids already) so a handful of sour players cannot exploit the upgrade npc mechanic to grief the winning team.

Because right now, this is what happens most of the time, particularly at the late hours when fewer players play:

The game starts off as a fair 15v15 or maybe 14v16, there is a trade of wins and losses. Then, either the slightly stronger team wins, while the losing team slowly quits 1 by 1 and you are left with only 7-8 opponents.
Or, which is far more likely, the losing team of sour pussies begin to play defensive, upgrade every troop to max, and then nobody wins. Because a group of 15 including lvl 51s and people with no gear is not able to fight down a pack of 11k health elites who shoot them for 400-500 damage per second.
After hours of struggling, the winning team will give up, and just play defensive too. That's a stalemate.

At that point everyone goes afk or leaves until the game closes, because once there are fewer than 10 on each side, you can't even physically kill the enemy boss.

This is not what a battleground should be like.

Please consider implementing an NPC scaling that reflects the number of players in Alterac Valley. Let us fight against each other instead of the NPCs, and let somebody win the BG even when fewer people queue.
