# Suggested challenge mode: Glyph of senescence

## Post 54942 by Patientzero — 2023-07-28T13:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54942#p54942 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A lot of players seem to care deeply about /played time, and in particular how long it takes to level to 60. Personally, I don't really care - this is *Turtle* WoW after all.   smiling_turtle_head

But since it's a Big Deal™ for so many, let's have some fun with it and make rapid levelling a challenge. Here's the idea:

- You opt in by purchasing a "Glyph of senescence" at level 1.
- Your character immediately begins to "age".
- You have a fixed amount of time to reach level 60. This could be displayed via a (de)buff or something, or just checked via a command.
- (Optional) Each level-up adds a small amount of time to your clock, but your overall time remaining continues to reduce.
- If you reach 60 before you run out of time, you win.
- If you don't reach 60 before the time is up:
 - Hardcore character: "Gnomebait14 dies of old age". F.
 - Normie: You fail the challenge but can continue to play.
I have no idea what would constitute a challenging but realistic overall time to allow. I guess this could be based on overall stats from the server, say fix it at the top 5 or 10%. From a quick Google it seems the record for Vanilla is 36 hours, so another option could be to set it based on say 1.5x that record.

Rewards could be a title such as "the Ageless" or "the Impatient". Plus some swag of course - maybe a new rocket mount?

I note that this challenge could be extra hard while the server is experiencing such high player numbers. But it might have the positive effect of encouraging people to spread out a bit and not camp out popular quest areas.
