# Removing/reducing Transmog for Class / Item Identity

## Post 13278 by Rafale (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-10-28T09:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13278#p13278 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

Skin identity is a huge part of the game.

When you see someone with Might of Menethil, or Sulfuras, you know that player has worked a lot to reach that.When a T2.5 warrior charges on you, you know you will die in seconds if you don't control him. On the other hand, when you see a green geared war of the whale, you know you have some time.

Noggenfogger and others illusions are already identified as tricks, people know there is gear behind, so if they re friendly you inspect, and if they re enemy you stay on your guard.

In every interaction of the game, the skin of the gear has an influence.

That's why transmog is a huge problem : By bringing transmog, you remove the relation between the gear and the skin, and worse, you make people wanting some gear only for the skin.

Tomorrow, you will see Naxx geared warriors charging in robes with a shovel (Might of menethil xmog) critting on you for 3K. Tomorrow, you will see people need your bis for the skin for their transmog set ("Why do you judge me ? I waited years for that item !")

That's why I think transmog need to be removed from the game. Retail stuff is retail stuff. TurtleWow need to bring new content, not recycling retail cancer.

Thx for reading, have fun :)

I've always found this to be something that's greatly overexaggerated. Sure, back in the day tier sets were something of a much more legendary status, but these days a lot of classes' best gear is a bunch of random individual pieces from raids, and with all those individual pieces they're going to look just as weird and as much like hobos as levelers. The best helm for warriors in raids (and pretty good in PvP as well) is a generic yellow helm that's just a reskin of a level 40 helmet. A lot of the caster robes don't look different from leveling robes. The best 2-hander in the game all the way until Kel'Thuzad is the Bonereaver's Edge, which also doubles up as one of the absolute worst-looking weapons in the game (and would you look at that, it's a reskin of the Whirlwind Sword, a leveling weapon). Outside of the very end of Vanilla where Tier 3 finally reigned supreme in terms of stats, the awesome-looking tier sets took second-stage.

And when it comes to PvP, a lot of the pieces of gear that are actually important to keep in note are ones that you can't see at all. The Gnomish Mind Control Cap and Goblin Rocket Helmet? All hideable through the "Hide Helm" option. The huge variety of trinkets which all have unique uses in PvP; AGM, Magic Reflectors, Tidal Charm, Major Recombobulator, Gnomish Death Ray, etc?  All invisible trinkets that you have no way of being able to see whatsoever. An entire class (Druid) has pretty much all of their gear as hidden under their shapeshift forms, and as you just mentioned, literally anyone can hide their gear with costumes and toys anyway. But you don't see anyone who's really PvPing actually raising a fuss in practice.

> Tomorrow, you will see Naxx geared warriors charging in robes with a shovel (Might of menethil xmog)

Paladins currently having to use cloth robes and a bundle of flowers as an off-hand because it's their BiS like  wary_turtle

Let's be real here: the kind of person you're describing is a strawman. For every person who might want to do something dumb like that, there's 10 paladins that desperately want to get out of their shitty-looking priest gear and look like a proper badass paladin and dozens more that want to wear the actually awesome tier sets they've earned to show off rather than wearing completely mismatched gear and having their tier 2.5 raiders looking like fresh level 50s.

> Tomorrow, you will see people need your bis for the skin for their transmog set ("Why do you judge me ? I waited years for that item !")

This isn't any different from people rolling on off-spec gear, and we've already found a solution for that: MS>OS priority. In raids, people will simply put transmog rolls behind or equal in priority to off-spec gear. As for dungeons, people can talk it out and ask if the other folks need that gear before rolling on it, like civil adults; if they don't, they're gonna be the type of people to create drama for a whole bunch of other reasons anyway.

And well, if pre-BiS in dungeons was actually good-looking enough to be used as proper transmog gear, then we wouldn't need transmog to begin with. Transmog exists specifically because BiS gear looks like shit.
