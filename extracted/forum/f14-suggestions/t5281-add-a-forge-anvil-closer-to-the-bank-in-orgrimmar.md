# Add a forge + anvil closer to the bank in Orgrimmar

## Post 32092 by Jessienyan — 2023-01-04T20:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32092#p32092 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The valley of honor is the only place with a forge and anvil in Org (to my knowledge). The distance from the bank is quite long and the run feels tedious, especially for players without mount training.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/q0W8OPe.png)

I propose adding a forge/anvil near the bank. "The Shattered Axe" directly to the left of the bank would be a good location. An anvil could be put inside the building with a large forge outside on the opposite side.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/xWNHUSh.png)
