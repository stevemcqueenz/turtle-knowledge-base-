# Revenge and thunderclap

## Post 171834 by Itschroma — 2026-03-22T21:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171834#p171834 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

In the early tiers, thunderclap and revenge are decent, assuming you invest points into improved thunderclap, improved revenge, and Reprisal. Tier 2.5 5 set and tier 3 2 set give the abilities a boost. To make Thunderclap work, you need 3 talents; to make Revenge Decent, you need 3-5 talent points. Reprisal is contested by one-handed specialization, a alway good talent. These abilities need a small coefficient. The increase in shield slam damage and threat was a step in the wrong direction for increasing warrior threat.
Also, Victory rush would be lit.
