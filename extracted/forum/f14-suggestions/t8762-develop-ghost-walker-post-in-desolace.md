# Develop Ghost Walker Post in Desolace

## Post 56502 by Lahire (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-09T09:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56502#p56502 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Desolace is one of those zones that feel a bit unfinished. The centaurs reputations are just a solid grind ; there are not a lot of quests ; Maraudon is a central and important dungeon but nothing leads to it in the narrative design, it just sits here.

Some parts are well developed, like the north orc fortress, or the naga zone ; the kodo cemetery is a nice and unique place, with a lot of flavour. It's the defining piece of the zone.

Atop this cemetery, there is a little tauren camp named "Ghost Walker Post". It's a very lack luster village : no inn, only 3 quests, no vendor of interest. It has a "superior macecrafter" pnc... but this dude has no green mace to sell which is disappointing.

I feel like the ebb and flow of the zone as a leveling and grinding place would be easily enhanced if this village would be developed. It would make Desolace into a place players avoid less (right now it's often an empty zone because it is unattractive).

It could be done by :
 - giving some rare items to the vendors
 - putting a horde inn (?)
 - putting a quest hub

But more importantly :
 - linking the village to the centaur rep grind and Maraudon, by developing the character of Gurda Wildmane (tauren ambassador to the centaurs). It could be quest chains that give you rep to the centaurs by doing the Maraudon dungeon ; or adding a loot item on the centaurs you could trade to get more rep (like with the furbolg rep).

Another little flavour idea : as the name and graveyard suggest, there could also be a Spirit Walker npc here (a tauren shaman) with quests about the rites of death and afterlife in Tauren culture.

TLDR, Ghost Walker Post has an untap potential with a unique flavour that merits development.
