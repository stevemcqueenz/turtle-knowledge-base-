# Feedback & Suggestions from a Longtime WoW Player – Hardcore Mode, Language Settings & Addon Selection

## Post 139971 by Selstarr — 2025-07-16T19:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139971#p139971 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone,

I’m a very experienced World of Warcraft player, active since 2007, and I recently started taking a closer look at Turtle WoW. I’ve noticed a few things that are quite important to me, and I would like to share them as constructive feedback and suggestions with the community and the team.

 1. Missing Hardcore-Only Realm:

I wanted to play Turtle WoW in Hardcore mode – meaning that death results in permanent character loss. Unfortunately, as far as I can tell, there is no dedicated Hardcore realm. Playing on a mixed realm is very difficult for players like me who prefer cautious, deliberate gameplay. Many players take unnecessary risks or join groups without much forethought, often resulting in preventable deaths. A true Hardcore-only server would be a fantastic addition for those of us who want a more serious and immersive experience.

 2. Language Settings – No Full German Support:

I’ve played WoW exclusively in German since 2007 and have never used the English client – and I have no intention of starting now. I find it confusing that the launcher allows you to select “German” as a language, yet the game remains in English. If there is a simple way to switch to full German localization, I would appreciate any guidance. In general, I believe that if the launcher offers a German option, the game itself should be fully playable in German as well.

 3. Addon Selection in the Launcher – Core Addons Missing:

I really appreciate the idea of having an integrated Addon list in the launcher. However, I was surprised by the selection. Some of the classic, widely-used addons like Questie or popular details are missing. Instead, there are a few addons I’m not familiar with (e.g., PFQuest). While I only looked for the most essential tools, I feel that including a good damage meter and Questie by default would greatly benefit most players – especially newcomers to the server.

Final Thoughts:

Right now, I’m still level 1, waiting until I can play with German localization before I really get started. I’m very excited about the project as a whole – especially the HD textures and the extended engine. But as long as I’m forced to play in English, I’ll unfortunately have to hold off for now.

I hope my points are received as constructive and respectful feedback. Many thanks to the team for all the work you’ve done – I’m looking forward to what’s to come, and would love to hear your thoughts!

Best regards,
Selstar

I’m not interested in trying the English client – otherwise, I would have done so already. I’ve been playing WoW exclusively in German since 2007, and I have no intention of changing that. It’s a personal decision and part of what makes the game enjoyable for me.

Regarding the Hardcore mode: HC characters can interact with Softcore players in the open world – especially when it comes to griefing. I come from a retail Hardcore server where you only play alongside other HC players, and that’s a big part of the immersion and overall experience. A dedicated Hardcore realm should be a basic feature, or at least seriously considered.

It’s fine that people on Turtle WoW use PFQuest – but I don’t. And I’m also not interested in using it, especially not in English. What I don’t appreciate is having my concerns dismissed without acknowledging my personal background and reasoning.

There are a lot of German-speaking players around the world. I’d like to remind everyone of when Blizzard originally announced Classic WoW in English only – the backlash was enormous, It’s a clear sign that localization matters.

It’s honestly disappointing to be labelled as a troll and idiot just for sharing my first impressions. I’m simply trying to give honest feedback. It’s totally fine if people disagree – but it should be possible to discuss different viewpoints in a respectful way.

In the end, this whole situation is also part of a first impression – not just of the server, but of the community and how it treats new players with personal preferences and requirements for their gameplay.
