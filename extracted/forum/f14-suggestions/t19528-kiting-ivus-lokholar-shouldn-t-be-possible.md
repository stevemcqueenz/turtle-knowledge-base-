# Kiting Ivus/Lokholar shouldn't be possible

## Post 132937 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-05-25T14:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132937#p132937 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Devs!

Please, change the way spells of Lokholar the Ice Lord and Ivus the Forest Lord work in Alterac Valley so kiting becomes impossible.

Much effort is required to summon these 2 bosses and it's painful to see that they can be kited so easily by only one person.

It can be abused by Hunters who can kite the boss forever.

Currently anyone in AV can abuse the system and kite these world bosses to the tunnels to make the fight trivial.

VIDEO:
[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/YwWjtfZCKxY]

Suggestion:
- Change the root spell of Ivus and freeze spell of Lokholar so these effect can't be dispelled.
These effects immobilize you but any Priest/Paladin can dispel the effect and kite boss wherever they want.

- Make Ivus and Lokholar move faster. It's important against Hunters who can activate Aspect of Cheetah and kite the boss forever.

- Increase the range of root/freeze spell to 60 yards. This way even Hunters won't be able to kite them.

Lokholar the Ice Lord - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=13256>
