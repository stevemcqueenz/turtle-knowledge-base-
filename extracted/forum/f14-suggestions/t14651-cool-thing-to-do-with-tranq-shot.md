# cool thing to do with tranq shot

## Post 99939 by Wifelie — 2024-07-20T04:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99939#p99939 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Simple idea to make tranq shot not only an enrage dispeller but also make it castable on allies.
It would either provide a buff that reduces threat generated by the target for a time or straight up remove 10% of the threat they currently have.

I think this is really cool cause it allows more breathing room for classes that were never blessed with any threat control (spell ret and enchance) while not being a game breaking ability like misdirect, also it encourages team play and can be used for some cool moves like mitigating the threat of the mage with ignite.

maybe would be too strong or something but it feels like a thing i could see in vanilla
