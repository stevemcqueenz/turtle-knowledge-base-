Ok, I will explain clearer my vision.

Since it's really hard to balance new abilities that would be introduced to the game (not to mention creating them) I came with idea to make a hybrid of paladin and warrior that uses existing abilities. Instead of mana it would use paladin abilities that cost rage. So the only thing in need of balancing would be rage cost of abilities and rage generation. Since the former paladin dropped light and became evil, his choice was to become Black Knight, obviously it doesn't happen to every former paladin. Because he is cut from light he can't heal nor protect himself with divine shield, so he spend some time to learn warrior ways to replace his missing skills with something useful. I thought not of powerful abilities like Mortal Strike that would make it overpowered, instead I came up with Charge that can be used to start fight and generate some rage to begin, as well as Intimidating Shout because it fits the theme. Heroic Strike was logical choice as ability to burn excess rage, because his abilities would retain cooldown.
Lorewise Black Knight appears in Argent Tournament in Wotlk. Black Knight may be not common thing, because some events need to happen to make a paladin drop order, become evil and also decide to become Black Knight. But it is possible.
Also worth mentioning, I thought that Black Knight could use seal/judgement of Light as the only mean to heal himself (the spell would be rename to something like life-draining, vampiric or whatever)
Also Black Knight would retain paladin talent tree so it won't require anything new.

One more thing. I thought what races would Black Knight be available to. For horde only former paladin can be undead. For allies human, dwarf and high elf. Since it would be not fair making 3 races available for allies and only 1 for horde I came up with solution that it will be available only to human and undead. This goes well along with human weakness, they are prone to corruption and other races seem more resilient. Maybe even Black Knight are bit like Scarlet Crusade and don't accept non-humans into their ranks. Something like that.
