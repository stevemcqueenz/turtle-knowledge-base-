Anu belore dela'na!
Is only a couple of days that I've joined the server but it's amazing!
I would like to suggest you some minor (I hope) changes or additions to the High Elf race and to the city of Alah'Thalas.
 - make high elves character understand and maybe speak Thalassian. In Alah'Thalas the npc actually speak Thalassian but as High Elf character you can't understand them.
 - add some interactions with the enviroment such for example the possibility to sit on the benches or chairs in Alah'Thalas.
 - add a portal trainer to Alah'Thalas and the ability for mages to teleport and open portals to the city.
The following I suppose are not exactly "minor" but still something I personally would like to see
 - add a city ruler or a council.
 - add profession trainers to Alah'Thalas.
 - add services such as bank, auction and barber (always found funny that in Silvermoon there where no barbers and then elven ladies concern about that)
 - enstablish a ship route from Alah'Thalas to Menethil harbour.
Do what you can or like to. These are only suggestions that in my opinion would increase the depth of the race and their city.
I understand that, since there is also the new city of Amani'alor, the same addition could and should probably be done to that city as well to keep the balance.
Kind regards
