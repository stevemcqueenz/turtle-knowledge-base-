# Jeff Kaplan Tribute - Green Hills of Stranglethorn quests rework

## Post 170913 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-03-15T21:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170913#p170913 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

A few days ago one of the creators of Vanilla World of Warcraft - Jeff Kaplan - was interviewed by Lex Fridman. The interview is 5 hours long - you can watch it on YouTube (
[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/H9rF1CSSh-w]
it's cool interview for all WoW fans.

At one moment Jeff talks about the worst quests he has ever designed and that were the Green Hills of Stranglethorn quests in Stranglethorn Vale. You take those at Hemet Nesingwary's camp. The quest is infamous for being zone-wide bag filler. Each mob in STV has a chance of dropping a page. You need 15 random-drop unique pages that take 15 slots in your bag in order to complete whole quest chain.

The quest has its faults. I thought it would be cool to improve it as much as it's possible as a tribute to Jeff for his impact he had on Vanilla we know. I propose ready solution below (it's at the bottom of this post in *Details for devs* section).

So I've changed the way pages work and slightly modified the quest. The major difference is that you can now combine pages into missing chapters. So when player gets all pages for one quest, they can combine them and save some bag slots. The pages are no longer needed as an objective to the quests, those missing chapters are only required to complete each quest.

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/gmmn4HwLT5w]

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#a/lT5dSbD]

A few notes what was changed. 4 new quest items were added. 4 new spells that combine pages were added. Pages can no longer be read by players - but you get full story from them when you deliver missing pages to the quest giver so nothing is lost. Properties of pages and quests were changed.

 **Details for devs**  Missing chapters are new quest items, replace entry column (I used 100001-100004) with whatever fits your core and note that entries are referenced elsewhere.
