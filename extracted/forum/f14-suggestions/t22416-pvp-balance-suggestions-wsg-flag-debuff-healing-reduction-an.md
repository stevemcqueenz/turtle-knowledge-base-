# PvP Balance Suggestions: WSG Flag Debuff, Healing Reduction, and Blind Change

## Post 155647 by Vega12 — 2025-10-15T08:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155647#p155647 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,

I wanted to bring up a few PvP-related topics that I believe could really improve the balance and overall fun factor in battlegrounds — ****especially Warsong Gulch****. These are small adjustments that could make a big difference, particularly for the Alliance side.

 **1. Flag Carrier Damage Taken Debuff**

Currently, the flag carrier (FC) gains +10% damage taken per minute while holding the flag.
While I understand this mechanic is meant to prevent long stalemates, in practice it heavily favors the stronger team — which, as data shows, is most often Horde, with Alliance losing over 80% of WSG matches.

If people require proof for that statistic, the easiest way to gouge it would be to look at honorspy and Honor in Bracket 1-2. Most likely horde has 2x more higher Caps then alliance based on the fact that wins give more Honor.

Removing or reducing this debuff would:

- Give properly organized defense teams and FCs more impact.

- Help balance games by slightly favoring the underperforming side (Alliance).

- Encourage more strategic, coordinated gameplay rather than rewarding quick brute-force flag rushes.

- This small change could already serve as a passive incentive for Alliance to queue for WSG more often.

 **2. Reduced Healing in Battlegrounds**

The 20% reduced healing modifier is another issue that negatively affects gameplay.
Healers are already scarce in battlegrounds — and this reduction makes the role even less appealing.

Across all versions of WoW, Blizzard has had to incentivize healers with bonus rewards or faster queues just to keep enough people healing in PvP. Reducing or removing this modifier would:

- Encourage more players to heal in BGs.

- Improve the quality and length of fights.

- Make organized team play more rewarding.

 **3. Blind: Physical → Poison (Revert Change)**

The change of Blind from a Poison to a Physical effect seems to have been made with TBC/Arena in mind.
However, on Turtle WoW, this hurts certain class/race matchups — especially Dwarves, who took Stoneform specifically to counter abilities like Blind.

Reverting Blind back to a Poison effect would:

- Restore an additional layer of counterplay and skill expression. (Druid Abolish Poison, Poison Elixirs/Anti Venom from First Aid, Totem from Shaman etc.)

- Reintroduce some of the classic vanilla PvP flavor and racial utility.

None of these suggestions would drastically change the game — but together, they could go a long way toward improving battleground balance, role diversity, and player satisfaction, especially for Alliance players.

Thanks for taking the time to read, and I’d love to hear others’ thoughts or feedback on these ideas!

Thanks for the thoughtful reply! I completely agree that there should be *some failsafe* against endless stalemates — but I think there are better, more balanced ways to handle it than the current setup.

All modern iterations of WoW already include a timer of around 30–35 minutes, where the leading team automatically wins. Personally, I’ve **never been a fan** of that — some of my favorite PvP memories come from those long, chaotic Warsong Gulch matches where players formed friendships, learned creative tricks, and shared those “only-in-vanilla” moments. Things like jump practice, learning to target-dummy a hunter trap, or timing a skull into a rocket helm, reflect warlock Coils. That kind of organic chaos is part of what made vanilla PvP special.

That said, I understand not everyone enjoys that level of commitment, and most of the PvP pool (around 4k players) are primarily PvE players who just want to play a few fun games each week.

Even before the debuff was added (like in Nordanaar before the change), Horde already had about the same win rate — maybe slightly lower — and games rarely dragged on for hours. The issue isn’t really endless stalemates, since that happened maybe 1 in 15 Games, it’s that the debuff punishes proper defense and benefits the already dominant team.

A fair compromise could be:
→ +10% damage taken every 5 minutes, instead of every 1 minute.
That still discourages indefinite games but gives flag carriers and defenders a semi-chance to shine without instantly tilting the match toward the stronger side.

I’ve also heard rumors that Turtle WoW was exploring incentives for Alliance to queue more WSG, since the current losing rate has really hurt morale in **Ambershire**. Personally, I think this adjustment to the flag debuff could be a great first step in that direction — a small change with a big impact on game balance and player motivation.
