I only have a rarely used lock alt, it's not for me, I think it makes the game better.
Lemme elaborate.
PVE complexity is learning the fights and mastering a coreography, PVP complexity is mostly based on the classes individual power. SOul shard stacking won't make PVE encounters less complex and won't make warlocks stronger.
But! Warlock is one of the least-used classes of the game. Partly because their qol segment is bad.
As a warlock you have less bag space. You need to have as much shards as possible, f.ex. to summon half of your guild because raid, but the more shards you have, the less inventory space you got. It's an unnecessary problem.
Also, since you use up a bunch of souls during pre-raid, you have two options:
 1. Falling behind at DPS meter because you keep drain souling mobs instead of a shadow burn. Feels bad for a DPS.
 2. You spend a bunch of time killing mobs you have no interest in killing to summon the team. Since you can't really stack up enough shards to save them for later, you have to do this almost before every raid. Just adds a 30 min chore.

Soul shars stacking would enable warlocks to have better inventory management and having some hours of farm per month would be enough to not to care about raid summons for that month.
Warlocks have other problems, too but this qol change would make them more represented in the game.
