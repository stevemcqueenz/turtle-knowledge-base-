# Spec-Based Recolors for T3 (and Beyond?)

## Post 169930 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-03T10:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169930#p169930 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey Turtle Team

First of all, I really love the direction you took with tier sets. The fact that each tier from T1 to T3.5 has spec-adapted versions is amazing design-wise. It makes every specialization feel more unique and valued.
I also really appreciated that T1 and T2 even received recolors depending on the spec. It adds personality, identity, and visual progression — which is a huge part of what makes gearing exciting in WoW.
However, I noticed that from T2.5 onward (T2.5, T3 and T3.5), we don’t have spec-based recolors anymore.

And honestly… I would LOVE to see that implemented, especially for T3.

For example:
A Warrior T3 in white could look absolutely insane for a defensive or holy-themed fantasy.
A red version would perfectly fit a more aggressive, berserker-style identity.
That’s just an idea of course, but visually it could be incredible.

Beyond personal preference, it would also create stronger consistency across tiers. Since T1 and T2 already benefit from spec recolors, extending that philosophy to T2.5 and T3 would make the system feel complete and cohesive.

Anyway, just throwing this out there because I genuinely think it would be an awesome addition to the game.

Thanks for all the amazing work you’re doing !
