# Please increase the chance of Flash Freeze proccing against raid monsters

## Post 165998 by Familymaker — 2026-01-22T21:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165998#p165998 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So while I quite like the Frost Mages, their damage is rather low compared to many other classes *cough* rogues *cough*. Heck, their damage is even low when compared to the Arcane Mages, which is why most mages run arcane in Raids now. The only form of real damage that Frost Mages have is in the form of Icicles being cast at a much shorter cast time after you proc a frozen snare effect against an enemy which is immune against that, for which you need both the Flash Freeze and Frostbite talents. And while Frost Mages have more survivability, you often are forced to fight from a distance, so you can't cast Frost Nova or Cone of Cold to proc its frozen effect.

As such, perhaps you could increase the chance of the Flash Freeze talent proccing on frost effects against non-player enemies? Right now your Frostbite talent gives a 15% chance to proc Frozen against both players and monsters, which thanks to Flash Freeze instead reduces your cast time of Icicles to 1 second. So how about Frostbite's proc chance against players remains 15%, while the chance against non-player monsters could instead be increased to 25%?

That way you don't have to stand right next to a monster as a mage in order to proc Frost Nova and Cone of Cold in order to catch up to Arcane Mages with regard to damage, and you can better rely on your Frostbolt to try and proc Icicles more times against a raid monster, thus making you more up to par with other damage classes.
