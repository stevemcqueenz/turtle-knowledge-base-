# Chain Lightning & Swing Timer Change

## Post 127806 by echo noir — 2025-04-21T12:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127806#p127806 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Proposing a single change to shamans, after seeing that the devs added a SP coeffiecent (5%) to Lightning Strike:

 **Allow the swing timer to continue while casting Chain Lightning.**

Promotes build diversity (spellhance), adds to skill expression (timing, positioning, resources), and is thematic to the shaman fantasy (chain lightning=**cool**).

Chain Lightning is self-regulating, in that it has a CD (6 sec) and a high mana cost (lame). Could even make it a talent, though wording would be a disaster.

Not sure if this should be extended to Lightning Bolt as well, you'd have to crunch the numbers.
