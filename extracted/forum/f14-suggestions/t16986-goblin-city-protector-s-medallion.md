# Goblin City Protector's Medallion

## Post 115946 by DonTortuga — 2025-01-08T23:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115946#p115946 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I wasn't expecting anything when I got the "Bruiser of Sparkwater" title (didn't even know there was a title until recently), but when I got the City Protector's Medallion, I was excited because I thought it would act as a second hearth, or possibly send me to Sparkwater Port (since the title has Sparkwater in it), which is close enough to Org that it would basically be a second hearth anyway. Then I tried it and got teleported to Dustwallow Marsh... Erm. Now I'm in a place that's somehow both dusty and swampy at the same time. It's not particularly close to anything, except Onyxia (which I don't need anything from), or alliance boats (which I can't use). I suppose it's a little closer to AQ than usual, but not more than the CoT rune or the Engi trinket gets you.

I'm not trying to look a gift horse in the mouth. I love the idea of the trinket, and it was unexpected which made it even better, but might I ask/suggest please changing the location to somewhere a little nicer, like Gadgetzan, Winterspring, Booty Bay, even Sparkwater Port. Somewhere that's a little more useful to be able to TP to, and is a more populated, well known Goblin town. It would be nice.
