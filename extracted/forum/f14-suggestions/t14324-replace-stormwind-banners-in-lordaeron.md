A minor issue, but I was always bothered by the fact that the ruined towers in Hillsbrad and Tirisfal Glades are using tattered Stormwind banners, not the Lordaeronian ones.

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/881973-at-war-with-the-scarlet-crusade.jpg)

It's definitely not a major issue, but it feels a bit unimmersive. Like, that tower in Tirisfal should be using either old Lordaeronian flag or a Scarlet one. I guess it could be argued that in Hillsbrad it is justified for some towers to use Stormwind banners as the modern Alliance does indeed have a presence in the area; still, the towers are ruined and mostly empty.
