# RP-characters - discord channel

## Post 156887 by Jezy — 2025-10-24T13:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156887#p156887 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Just like guilds have their own thread/post tab for people to post their guilds’ rp stories and to recruit players, it’d be awesome for the same to exist for people to showcase their characters, with a story, and screenshots of their rp, that doesn’t necessarily belong to a guild, as a way to attract more players to do the same and most likely play with, where they’d own the post and show their characters development.

Long story short, a discord channel identical to the guilds’ one, where people can showcase, on their own thread/post tab, usually named after their characters, their own stories, screenshots and character development.
