# Hunter leveling quests! (like every other class)

## Post 172756 by Plagues2 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-27T16:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172756#p172756 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

This is going to be long and rambling..... so I'm sorry ahead of time

It's been well documented that hunters were the last class added to WoW and were a little rushed for release, This lead to the lack of dedicated quests while leveling. Unlike every other class who has a class quest every 10 levels or so, Hunters have a quest to tame their pet at level 10...and then nothing until 60. Warriors have quests for their stances, and whirlwind axe, Warlocks have quests for their demons and class mount, Paladins have quests for abilities and their class mount etc....

I would like to suggest adding in a quest line that would take from level 20-40 to complete, and would give a reward I'm sure every hunter would like. It would go a little something like this...

At level 20 your class trainer would tell you about a legendary hunter (We'll call him Bob for now) who was known all across Azeroth, and sends you to speak with someone (we'll call him Quest NPC) who has been "tracking the hunter" for decades. This would lead you to a Tauren in TB for horde, and a dwarf in IF for alliance.

You go to your faction specific Quest NPC, who tells you more details about Bob the hunter. the Legends says he had a bow crafted by the titans, that could pierce though solid stone, and an amulet that allowed him to control the winds so his arrows always hit their marks. These legends have been around for over 300 years yada yada.

The NPC is too old to continue this search by himself now, But believes he knows the resting place of the legendary Bob the hunter, and his legendary gear and is willing to tell you what he's learned if you can prove yourself and return bobs hunting journal.

This would lead you on a quest at level 20 to bring back trophies from "Elder" Elite beasts out in the world (some of these would need to be added to the world in small packs in specific places). Nothing too crazy, and should be soloable with your pet, and would mainly be a bunch of traveling (classic classic wow quest!)

The level 20 quest "Prove yourself" would require.
 1X Perfect Wyvern tail  from elite Elder Wyverns in stonetalon mountains (lvl 20-23)
 1x Perfect Wolf Pelt from elite Elite wolves in Ashenvale (lvl 23-25)
 1x Perfect Gryphon talon from elite Elder gryphons in Hillsbrad foothills (lvl25-27) - already spawn there in the east.

Once completed, the quest NPC would congratulate you, and tell you he will send word when it's time to continue the hunt. Reward would be some gold and exp.

At level 30 you would get another quest  to hunt down elite  beasts in the world. class trainers would say they received word from "Quest npc", or you can go directly to them.

The level 30 quest would require.
 2x Perfect Raptor claws from Elite Elder raptors in Arathi highlands (lvl 31-34)
 2x Perfect Basilisk teeth from elite Elder basilisks in Desolace (lvl 33-36)
 2x Perfect Crockalisc scale from elite elder saltwater crocs in STV  (lvl 38) - Already spawn north of Grom gol

Once completed the NPC tells you he has one more hunt for you, and will send word when ready.
Once again, some gold and exp as a reward. MAYBE hes impressed enough to give you his old hunting tabard (why? who knows, but it would be cool to have a decent reward:P) this could be a tabard that looks like its made of an animal pelt (maybe brown fur with feathers and teethe strung around it?)

At level 40 you will receive another quest. the NPC will task you with getting
 5x Black dragon scales form the elite dragons in Badlands (level 44-45) - Already spawn to the east.
 5x Perfect shark fins from the coral sharks in Dustwallow Marsh (lvl 46-47) - Already spawn on the coast.
 5x Perfect venom sacks from elite Scorpids in Tanaris (lvl 44-46)

Once completed the quest NPC will tell you its time to find the resting place of the Bob the legendary hunter.

At level 50 you will unlock the final quest.

He will tell you that through his decades of research, he found that the last hunt bob was tasked with was to bring the  heart of a Devilsaur to some royal who thought it would bring them great power or whatever, but this is where his trail ends. No tales of the great Bob can be found past this.  He tasks you will going to Un'goro crater to look for clues of what may have happened to bob.

You will need to go to un'goro crater, where you will be able to talk to someone in Marshals refuge who knows the location of the devilsaur's den, but will only tell you the location in exchange for a devilsaurs hide.

You go out, you kill a devilsaur, you bring back the hide. You get the location.

Your final quest is to investigate the den, and return and information to "Quest NPC"

You go there and interact with a skeleton, where you loot the quest item "Legendary hunters pack" and the quest item "Broken Wooden Bow", and return your findings.

Long story short, there was no legendary bow made by the titans, there was no wind commanding amulet... Bob the legendary hunter was just (for lack of a better term) really fucking good. The journal details all his hunts, how he stalked his prey and waited days for the perfect shot etc. The NPC is amazed, and somewhat let down that Bob did all the things from the legends with nothing more than a wooden bow. No magic items, just pure skill.

As a reward he gives you Bobs hunting pack, saying it should serve you well. This will give you the item "Hunter's pack"... a 28 slot bag!.

NOW HERE ME OUT!  I hear you. . "No great weapon?  No cool new skill?"  No.  Hunters are always forced to carry around an ammo bag, which means they are always short on inventory space. Hell for raids they may even bring extra ammo in their normal bags!!  A single 28 slot bag would make up for this, while still causing them to have less inventory space that other classes could have.

Anyways, this is my long rambling idea.   happy_turtle_head
