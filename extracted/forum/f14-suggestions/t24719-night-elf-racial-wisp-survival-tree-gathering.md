# Night Elf Racial: Wisp Survival Tree Gathering

## Post 174034 by Tyloris — 2026-04-05T02:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174034#p174034 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I haven't really gotten deep into the survival tree yet, but something that feels like it would be thematically appropriate is if, instead of needing/using an axe to chop down trees to gather wood, they could conjure a wisp spirit that gathers it instead. Like how nelfs used wisps in WC3 to passively gather from trees instead of cutting them down.

It would mostly be cosmetic. They interact with the tree as normal, but instead of playing the axe chopping animation, a little wisp spirit swirls up the tree in a spiral and it disappears. You wouldn't even need an axe in your inventory to use it.
