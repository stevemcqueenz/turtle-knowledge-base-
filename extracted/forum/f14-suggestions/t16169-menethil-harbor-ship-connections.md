## Post 110400 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-28T18:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110400#p110400 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I feel like the Southshore dock is probably going to connect to a Kul Tiras-affiliated location, given that the 1.17.1 patch notes' description of the expanding of the dock said "The people of Southshore have expanded their dock, awaiting for shipments from Kul Tiras." So probably either somewhere in mainland Kul Tiras, Crestfall, or Tol Barad.

Kul Tiras as a nation has had an increased presence in Turtle WoW after the addition of the Exploratory Fleet in Caelan's Rest, Lieutenant Alverold's fleet in Anchor's Edge, and the embassy in Stormwind City, so it could possibly be something that ties to Kul Tiras itself, the island of Crestfall or maybe even somewhere in the Channel Islands (though I find the last one unlikely since it's much more minor a location than Kul Tiras and Crestfall); meanwhile, Tol Barad is also name dropped in Gilneas in the Alliance quest to find Isiden Perenolde, where documents found say that he's suspected to have fled Gilneas to Tol Barad with the help of Alteraci loyalists. As it's a plot point that was left hanging in Gilneas, it could be revisited and expanded on if Tol Barad is ever planned to be added.

Ravenshire does have a dock, but it's both pretty small and, if I remember correctly, is in a small cove that would be difficult to navigate with a full-sized transport ship even if the docks were expanded. If a ship connection is added there, it'd probably have to be a smaller ship and thus probably a smaller distance. I haven't checked out the new Icepoint Rock transport yet, but the preview image showed something that looked significantly smaller than the standard transport ships; something that size could be doable for Ravenshire if it really is a smaller boat.
