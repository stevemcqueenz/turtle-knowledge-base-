## Post 449 by Parcival (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2018-10-05T10:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=449#p449 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm up for it! A cohesive main story for us to follow would be great. But we should make sure to keep it with the current events in Azeroth.

We've done classic wow's timeline time and time again, but never in greater depth and detail. Each update brought with it new dangers lurking in Azeroth, so the server timeline should be adjusted to that and show the consequences on the world of Azeroth because of it.

We should look at the updates in classic wow as expansions (they do bring something new to the table as is the case of expansions - albeit not in such a seize) - specifically Cataclysm sort of expansion (where an event brings consequences to the entire world of Azeroth). The material is there already, Onyxia just one to name, with her long quest chain encompassing the whole of Azeroth and her demise, with the event in the main city. But such villians fall flat in sort of the danger they pose (you know they exist, but they're tucked someway away in an area you can't access at your lower level, so your priority is going to be the danger you currently face - even though that is nothing compared to the ones as Onyxia).

My idea is, give depth to the events occuring in Azeroth on a global scale via the existing material of threat of the updates. Not limited to just those threats (we have local dangers posing everywhere as well, it'd be nice to touch and resolve them, for a unified server story progression).

So to summarize, +1 I'm up for it!
