# Chinese players' suggestions for opening new servers.

## Post 64026 by Cc515k2 — 2023-09-14T15:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64026#p64026 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am a Chinese player. I think the solution to the problem is to open more Southeast Asian servers and enable the transfer of roles function. Opening a new European server will not solve the problem and will also cause fragmentation in the player community. A large number of Chinese players have learned that after the Southeast Asian server is opened, there is a role transfer function. So, I upgraded my servers in Europe in advance to facilitate my subsequent transfer to Southeast Asian servers. This is the root cause of severe server queuing in Europe. We should find solutions to the root cause of the problem and add new Asian servers with an overall capacity of at least 50000 people.

I respect the mindset of European server players: this is their home and they want easier and simpler games. Chinese players also have this demand and need their own Asian servers.

Many European players show hostility towards Chinese players due to queuing issues. The differences in cultural and social media information have led to many biases.

Please show mutual understanding.
