Upgradeable TF/Sulfuras questline that is just the right amount of cancer:

step 1: clear MC that reveals a teleport rune in the raid only clickable for 20 mins that gives access to the NPC

step 2: first NPC quest needs you to prove yourself by having Thunderfury Blessed Blade of the Windseeker equipped or in your inventory, and also killing a powerful being. (Mephistroth)

step 3: after clearing an MC again after a week you turn in previous quest and the NPC tells you what he needs to imbue your TF with more powerful elemental essence
requirements:
x5 elementium bar
x50 Essence of Air
x50 Essence of Fire
x1 100% drop chance item from a boss in Grim Batol (preferably first one) if you are on this quest

Step 4: upgraded TF with stats equaling a naxx weapon and the same proc
