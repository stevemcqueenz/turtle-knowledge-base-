# Three EASY Druid Changes for PvP Balance

## Post 126652 by OldM00n — 2025-04-13T21:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126652#p126652 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please…

 1. Allow Entangling Roots to be used indoors. Druids are only class that can’t use their core CC inside. In WSG, my balance druid twink is almost useless indoors.

 2. Allow Travel Form to be used indoors, too. Kiting is part of this class’s main kit, as our damage is low compared to other classes. Also makes us sitting ducks at times.

 3. Give Cat Form “Track Hidden,” to give us a slight edge on rogues who just decimate us with their openers. Even if we get an opener, they often come out on top anyway. As balance, it would be nice to have a chance to not be stun-locked to death, but knock a rogue out of stealth from time to time so they can’t get their guaranteed kill. Make them work for it a bit. (Most good rogues are running in teams of two, as well, making this even worse. One stun locks, the other ambush/backstabs.)

Bonus: Make Moonfire an AoE blast when no target is selected. Just a way to clear mobs like other casters, or give stealth players something to worry about when approaching us.

Thanks for reading/considering. :) You’re wonderful, Turtle Team.
