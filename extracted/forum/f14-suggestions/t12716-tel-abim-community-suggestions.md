 **Tel'Abim Community Suggestions**

 **Those are suggestions collected from IG chat and from the Discord (including some suggestions of my own (Arth IG/Discord) not requirements; their goal is to inspire/give ideas to the Turtle Team.**   turtle_in_love_head

 - Make all level 60 players of the server PvP flagged at all times

 - Create new PvP objectives in the world ;
- Make the tower between Southshore and Tarren Mill a PvP objective

- Make the tower in Dustwallow March a PvP objective

- Create capturable resource nodes in the world that would grant crafting resources to defenders (not afk players)
 - Prevent horde from using Stormwind Vault as a safe space in the heart of Stormwind

 - Make World PvP kills (not BGs) honor gains raid wide (need to be near the killed character and other raid members/ not AFK), at the moment doing world PvP raid grants limited honor.

 - Apply the coward debuff/kick planned for Alterac Valley (AV) to all BGs

 - If there is an imbalance while doing a BG (ex 10 Alliance vs 15 Horde) don't cancel the BG but kick the last players that entered the BG (without Debuff). (In my example last 5 horde that most recently joined would be kicked, more horde would be able to join if other Alliance players join the BG) [If cross faction BGs are implemented I guess this system could still be implemented just by kicking the last entry and balancing both side]

 - Organize a yearly pvp tournament in the arena and give special title or cosmetic reward to the victor of each class that finished first (inspired by Vrograg class war PvP event organized on Nordanaar).

 - Create a server ranking with most PvP kills (finishing blows), most objectives completed, most heals etc.

 - Create an invasion event ; for example waves of NPCs attack Orgrimmar and Stormwind at the same time, the first capital to destroy the waves wins (honor/ buff?) would appeal to both the PvE and PvP enjoyers.

 - Remove ability for Horde players to exit SW Vault inside Stormwind, they should exit from outside, the same way they entered (under the waterfall outside Stormwind gates) [If this is due to a technical limitation make both Horde and Alliance should enter and exit Stormwind Vault from under the waterfall outside Stormwind]

 - Ban/Keep banning trolls and SoD spammers on Discord (and IG), this is not a good look especially for new players and really kills the vibe of the server preventing Tel'Abim to flourish. [If the lack of moderation is due to the team in charge of it stretch too thin, please reach out, some including myself (Arth) would be willing to help if needed/deemed worthy.]

 - Class balance/changes (waiting on the class changes council) ; If not already the case would be great if a PvP expert or several ones would be invited to join the class change council.

 - Lock last honor ranks 11-13, the gear they offer is too powerful for phase 1

 - Create a "Refer-a-Friend" program and offer rewards to both the Referrer and the new player based on the new player progression (lvl 20, lvl 40 milestone etc)

The players are looking forward to faction leaders loot table in the future!

However and from discussion I had on the Discord including a previous popular post I made there, I do have to use this post as an opportunity to please ask the Turtle Team to make a decision about Tel'Abim.
If the Turtle Team is not a 100% committed to Tel'Abim they should end the server.
If the Turtle Team on the other hand is 100% behind Tel'Abim PvP server and is planning to support it for the years to come no matter what, this should be communicated clearly through official Turtle channels.

 **Rome as Nordanaar weren't built in a day.**

Thank you for reading! And Thank you Turtle Team we love you and enjoy your work!   turtle_in_love_head

Having experienced Twow's version of Alterac Valley for the bonus day, here's some toughts :

 1° AV is a more "casual" BG, ruined by a terrible balance :

A lot of player coming in to farm the reputation and their item of choice. Every melee dps wants their don julio, some caster may take the offhands, etc etc

However, in it's current state, it is a very painfull experience, at least on horde side. The base is a joke, the archers dont cover the flags, the inner base graveyard can be pulled by a warlock / hunter pet via a skip around the wooden fence and there is close to no NPC to deter the invader.

Considering that, we're not supposed to expect a complete redesign (even tho the horde base is a joke), we should at least ask for more NPC in the horde base, and maybe (maybe) a wallhack for our archers, that are as of now complety and utterly useless.

 2°AV has a bit of everything for everybody .. but :

It gives rankers somewhat of a "break" when it's AV day because of its chill nature, the mix of pvp / pve is a good introduction to pvp for newer player, and the quests are a fun tidbit to get you going.

However, i can't believe i'm saying this but i think Blizzard was right to get rid of herbs and ores in the Valley. Hell, even the Turtle Team added a debuff for not engaging in combat. I get that having ressources attract farmers inside the battleground, and when they pick up stuff they become easy targets. But it also create a situation when, even with the debuff, you can go around picking stuff and not engage in PVP at all.

I think those two modification, an upgrade to horde base (with more NPCs and a correction of abusive pulls) and a removal of AV ressources (for Tel'Abim, i don't have a clear view of Nordanar situation), could bring a more pleasant experience for players trying to PVP inside this battleground.
