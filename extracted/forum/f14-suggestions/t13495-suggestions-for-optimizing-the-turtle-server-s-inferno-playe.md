# Suggestions for optimizing the Turtle server's Inferno player system

## Post 91209 by Chenyingchao — 2024-03-23T08:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91209#p91209 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1. After the warehouse of the Purgatory Players Guild is opened, only the guild leader can have permission to access materials, and the warehouse usage permission cannot be changed by setting official permissions. Because the exchange of materials among purgatory players is mainly obtained through guilds, focusing so much energy on one person will inevitably result in long game investment time, and there are many guild members, making it difficult for players to stay online at different times. So I hope to enable the permission settings for accessing materials in the guild warehouse.

 2. Purgatory players cannot complete Black Iron's Legacy quests and cannot obtain Black Stone Abyss keys. The 5-person mission requires the participation of thieves, and it is difficult for purgatory players to ensure that thieves participate every time.
