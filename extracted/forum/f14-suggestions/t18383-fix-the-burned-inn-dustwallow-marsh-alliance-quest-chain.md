# Fix the Burned Inn (Dustwallow Marsh) Alliance quest chain

## Post 125503 by Nerat — 2025-04-05T07:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125503#p125503 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Anyone who's done the vanilla version of the Burned Inn quests wanted to see the chain completed instead of ending as abruptly as it did, so I appreciate the fact that Turtle WoW has added a quest chain to bring a satisfactory conclusion to the mystery behind it. However, upon revisiting it on a second Alliance character, it has become apparent that the amount of running back to report your findings and then get sent out to the same place again can get old very quickly.

To further illustrate my point, here are the trips you have to make (most on foot, since there is no flight path nearby the former inn):
- Inn -> Theramore
Theramore -> Inn
Inn -> Ratchet
Ratchet -> STV North
STV North -> Ratchet
Ratchet -> Inn
Inn -> Theramore
Theramore -> Towers
Towers -> Theramore
Theramore -> Brackenwall
Brackenwall -> Theramore
Theramore -> Inn
Inn -> Theramore
Theramore -> Dalaran
Dalaran -> Theramore
Theramore -> Inn
Inn -> Theramore
Theramore -> Quagmire
Quagmire -> Theramore
Most quest chains limit themselves to a third or maybe half of this amount of trekking back and forth, for a good reason. While the reward is good, the amount of time spent on this quest chain makes it not worth doing and I believe it's a shame for players to not engage with the extra content you have added.

In order to fix this, I propose a simple solution: Captain Garran Vimes provides the adventurer with a scrying bowl / radio, which allows the player to interact with in order to talk to the Captain remotely. This would be done similarly to how the quest chain for Uldaman from Talvash del Kissel works, where you contact the gnome while inside the dungeon to advance in the storyline and you can flavour it whichever way you prefer (a magical artefact, since Theramore is no stranger to the arcane or a technological contraption that the gnomes have come up with).
You could also have Falgran be the one to hand you the device once you get to that part in the chain, since it's the 5 or so quests after that which are really annoying trek-wise.
