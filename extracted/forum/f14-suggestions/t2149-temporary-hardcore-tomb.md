# Temporary Hardcore Tomb

## Post 13145 by Lahire (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2021-10-20T06:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13145#p13145 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

Would it be possible for a HC toon to drop a little tomb/grave on death? This tomb would be temporary (1 hour for example) and clickable (open a conversation reading "Here Lies Pancake, a valiant Gnome Warrior lvl 9. May their name be remembered" or something like that).

We talked about it in HC chat the other day and agreed it would be neat, and blizzard is doing the same (they are doing a bad copy of HC mode for SoM and you drop a 1 hour memorial orb when you die, but then can rez... it's not really HC).

What do you think about this cosmetic, fun and non-intrusive change?

## Post 13348 by R4ndomization — 2021-11-01T12:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13348#p13348 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am of the opinion that they should last much longer than both of you have suggested.

I would go even as far as to take Dark Souls as an example, so that players could leave a message intended for others as their tombstone. This would obviously be subject to moderation/banning/removal in case of too many deaths.

I suggest that the tombstones would differ each 10 levels, like suggested by Sinrek, but imo they should last longer than just days. I also think that there should be no tombstones below lvl 10 or 20, as to prevent an overwhelming mass of them at the usual starter death locations.
