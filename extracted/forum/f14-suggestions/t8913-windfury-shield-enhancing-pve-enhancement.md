# Windfury Shield - Enhancing PvE Enhancement

## Post 57587 by Deeds — 2023-08-16T11:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57587#p57587 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Windfury Shield is a way to keep the skill play of "totem twisting", while mitigating the need for twisting totems every 10 seconds for an entire raid. This talent would ideally be placed in late in the enhancement tree, so that the enhancement niche is preserved, while not taking anything away from a Resto shaman who wishes to totem twist at the expense of their mana pool.

Windfury Shield
 25% base mana
Requires Shaman
The caster is surrounded by a ball of wind.
On cast and every 5 seconds after, party members within 30 yards of the Shaman will have the "Windfury Weapon" affect applied. When a spell, melee, or ranged attack strikes the caster, this expends the wind ball.
Lasts 2 minutes.

 **30 second cooldown.**

In practice, this gives us multiple scenarios in a raid encounter:

Scenario 1 - The shaman casts Windfury Shield and engages the boss.  The Shaman is never hit by the enemy.  They no longer have to totem twist, saving considerable mana and complication at the cost of having no other active shield.

Scenario 2 - The shaman pre-casts Windfury Shield and is prepared with it off cooldown for the boss.  The boss spawns an add, which strikes the player.  The player immediately reapplies Windfury Shield after losing threat.

Scenario 3 - The shaman casts Windfury Shield and engages the boss.  They are struck by an enemy.  They must now totem twist multiple times before their Windfury Shield cooldown allows them to reapply it.  If the boss is going to repeatedly damage them, perhaps they'd be better off simply totem twisting for the remainder of the boss.

What about PVP considerations?

The shaman need only be struck once to have this ability be put on a 30 second cooldown, and it has quite the mana cost.  I cannot see it having much impact on PvP, so long as it is placed at least 21 points into the Enhancement tree.

What about talent placement?
I would place it on the 6th row of enhancement.  I think renaming the 5th row talent Improved Weapon Totems to Improved Weapon Enhancement, having that talent also affect Windfury Shield, and making that talent a 2/2 requirement would be the best place for it.

 ------------

In conclusion, I believe this solution will allow minimal impact in PvP, solve enhancement shaman mana problems, simplify the enhancement shaman rotation for those who desire it, and still allow for skilled players to continue to utilize totem twisting to maximize the DPS of their raid in certain situations and boss encounters.

Please let me know what you think, or if you have any issues or ideas to make it a better suggestion!

E: perhaps "shield" is the wrong name for it thematically, but I think it should take the "shield slot".  Windfury Aura?  Windfury Spirit?  I think the lore of it could definitely be improved :)
