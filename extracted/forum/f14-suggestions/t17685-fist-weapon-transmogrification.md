# fist weapon transmogrification

## Post 120605 by Blizik — 2025-02-21T21:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120605#p120605 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

most weapons can be xmogged to either hand while fist weapons are unique to the hand. in other words, an off-hand fist weapon can't be xmogged to the main-hand or vice versa. as far as i know, all other classes of one-handers can be xmogged between each other and to either hand.

i suggest that all fist weapons are able to be xmogged into either hand.

[Silithid Claw] changing from a main-hand to an off-hand shows me this is possible.

## Post 120675 by FrankFankledank (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-02-23T01:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120675#p120675 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The problem with fist weapon models is that in Vanilla, the code doesn't exist that mirrors them when you equip them in your main or off hand. That's why the only fist weapons that aren't hand-restricted are ones that don't need to be flipped, like basic brass knuckles. They had to manually fix the model up for the Silithid Claw change.
