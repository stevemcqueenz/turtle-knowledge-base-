# PALADIN SUGGESTIONS CC2

## Post 114022 by Asdfg — 2024-12-23T14:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114022#p114022 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there im sharing my ideas that i think can help the paladin class with the new CC2.

Seals

 1. Seal of the Crusader

The current version of SotC it's pretty much useless besides the Judgement effect, it's not even used in lvling. I think the solution to bring into relevance SotC is giving it a Holy dmg component based on weapon dmg percentage and giving its Judgement effect a dmg component.

EXAMPLE

Seal of the Crusader
Fills the Paladin with the spirit of a crusader for 30 sec, granting 31 melee attack power. The paladin also attacks 40% faster and deals 20% of normal weapon damage as Holy damage, but deals less damage with each attack. Only one seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time.

Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy for 30 sec, instantly causing 10 Holy damage and increasing Holy damage taken by up to 10 per Judgement. Can be applied up to 3 times. Only one Judgement per Paladin can be active at any one time..

 2. Seal of Righteousness

Right now SoR is the only option for spell damage build, build that fell apart with the cc2 changes. I think a good addition to try to solve these problems, is to give SoR effect a chance to crit based on spell crit chance.

Hammer of Wrath

Hammer of Wrath in its current state is useless, the few situations where it can be used it feels clumsy and frustrating to use, it fails on its sole purpose, which is to be an execute spell, even in niche situations it is better to use other spells such as JoR/JoC/JoJ instead of HoW.
A possible solution for this problem would be :

 - Discard the 20% HP restriction and allow the spell to target any enemy.
 - Make it instant cast.
 - Reduce the base damage.

This solution aim to make HoW a usefull spell for the paladin class, functioning as a range pull for prot paladins and a damage ability for sp/hybrid builds.

Sanctified Orb

This object has an interesting effect “5% chance of critical hit with spells and melee attacks”, but it is not used and fall into irrelevance very soon due to a long cooldown (5 min cd), A possible solution to this problem would be:

 - Reduce its cooldown to 30 sec.
 - Convert the 5% crit to 5% haste.
