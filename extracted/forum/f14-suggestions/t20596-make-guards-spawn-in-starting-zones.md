## Post 142426 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-31T23:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142426#p142426 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Okay, a lot of people are going to tell me, "No, this is part of PvP, disable War Mode, blah blah blah."

I think it's unfair that there are level 1 lunatic ganking people in the starting areas. It's a huge turn-off, or even makes people turn off PvP when they realize they're not even allowed to start their journey because there's already a player killing newly created level 1 players without even armor in the Horde or Alliance starting area. And the worst part is that they're already equipped to deal damage, capable of killing even a level 5. I think the starting zones should be respected.

It would be nice to implement a high-level Horde/Alliance Guard that would spawn as soon as a PvP kill occurs. I don't know, I don't think it's fair that new players have to pay for a silly challenge.

My advice is that if you want to get the PvP achievement, do it in arenas or bg with others who are also doing said challenge and let the others at least advance to Sen'jin or Goldshire, if they are able to kill a level 5 I suppose they will have no problems.
