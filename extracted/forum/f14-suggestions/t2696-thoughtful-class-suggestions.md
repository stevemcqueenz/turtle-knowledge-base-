Arcane Mage: I was reading through some of the changes you people have planned and I saw some great potential that is looking to go untapped. First, let's start with the mage. WoW's combat in general doesn't require movement unless you're playing against another player. That's where moving out of their sight makes sense, but fighting against NPCs, you can just stand and not really move. If you are any melee class and try to run around NPCs, you realize they are programmed to automatically turn to face you.

How Vanilla WoW works is that combat is made equal to whether you're fighting an NPC or a player as much as this gap can be closed.

This change you suggest is unbalanced and it seems like it will only benefit the Mage, everyone else will be at a huge disadvantage.
