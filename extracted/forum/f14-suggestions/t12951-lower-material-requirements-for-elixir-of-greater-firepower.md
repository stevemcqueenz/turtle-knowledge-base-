# Lower material requirements for Elixir of Greater Firepower & Shadow Power

## Post 86696 by Sark21 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-02-01T18:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86696#p86696 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all, I think subject speaks for itself the material requirements to make these elixirs is just way too much for a 30min duration item.

Currently,
Elixir of Greater Firepower requires 3 [Fire oil] (made with 2 [Firefin Snapper]) & 3 [Firebloom] herb.
Elixir of Shadow Power requires 3 [Ghost Mushroom] herb.

I purpose you alter it to,
Elixir of Greater Firepower requires 2 [Fire oil] & 2 [Firebloom] herb.
Elixir of Shadow Power requires 2 [Ghost Mushroom] herb.
