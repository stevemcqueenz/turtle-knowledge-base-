# What is wrong with Alterac Valley

## Post 67463 by Rurin — 2023-09-28T19:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67463#p67463 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This week I returned to playing max level PvP on Turtle WoW - last time I did AV here was nearly 2 years ago and I'm literally SHOCKED that after so much time and changes the are still so many so obvious and glaring issues that make the map completely unbalanced. Why is this still not fixed? Multiple changes were made to the map, it's layout, the very terrain, but many of these changes look... pointless, or even harmful, while the real problems persist.

 1) Alliance "still" gets to skip most of the defense by rushing last GV.

WHY is the wooden wall just short enough to allow them to jump over? Just... increase its height, double it even, why is that a problem?

WHY does like 90% of aggroed horde NPCs de-aggro JUST before the graveyard? At Alliance base EVERY NPC that gets aggro will chase you to the graveyard and attack you there. Horde NPCs conveniently leave you and run back! Increase their aggro range! Why wasn't that done yet?

 2) Why is there so many Alliance NPCs blocking the way between their front towers and graveyards? SH bunker was supposed to be the weakpoint of Alliance defense in the original design! As it is now, horde respawning at the GV have no way of joining those fighting at the SH bunker! It is impossible to take unless alliance completely abandons it! Remove these NPCs! Why are they even there? Horde has no similar concentration of NPCs anywhere near their towers!

There are many more problems, but honestly, they are not worth even talking about as long as the ones above still exist. I'm not alone in my perception of this situation. Every AV game is filled with frustrated horde players, as Alliance gets to basically exploit the map for easy wins that are impossible to copy from the horde side.

Why are the devs not doing anything? It this really such low priority?
