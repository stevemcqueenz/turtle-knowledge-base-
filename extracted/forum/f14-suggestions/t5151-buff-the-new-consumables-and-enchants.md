# Buff the new consumables and enchants

## Post 31292 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-12-21T11:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31292#p31292 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As some of you may know (and some of you may not know), this patch added new foods and new bracer enchants to match the ones that other classes have already. This made many people happy, but as they look into it more they realize these new ones are still much weaker than the ones they should compete with.
I shall go over them one by one:

[45 ranged power food:](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/999867459312033935/1054907537943703612/recipe2.png) Great job, this is a perfectly designed item. Matches the warriors strength food perfectly, its cost is reasonable and it creates a new use for an underutilized item (heart of the wild). Nothing to change about it

[**2% haste food:**](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/999867459312033935/1054907537599774720/recipe.png) This is a cool item, balanced effect that helps ret paladins, enhance shamans, and bear tanks. But its crafting materials are a big shock when you compare it to the other foods here. Essence of Air is the rarest elemental material in the game! It costs 6g on the auction house, heart of the wild costs 4silver. Luckily [Elemental Air](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=7069#reagent-for) exists. It'sa common item, usually sells for 4silver too but because its utterly useless. As you see in the database it's used for just 1 or 2 recipes in very small quantity. Changing the food recipe to use Elemental air is a good balance to hit two birds with one stone, to give this item a reasonable cost and finally make elemental airs a useful item (Helps low levels farm gold too).

[12 spell damage food:](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/999867459312033935/1054907538379903046/recipe3.png) This one sadly suffers from a different problem. It's simply too weak. Generally speaking, 12 spell power is the same stat budget as 7 strength. And since there is already  20 strength food, it must have 28 spell power to match that, not a measly 12. The recipe is also meeh, it's a bit on the expensive side. Silversage is already used for sooo many other recipes. To be honest I would prefer if it used Golden Sansam, this will put it in the same category as using Elemental Air or Heart of the Wild.

[12 spell power bracers enchant](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/691917545049030697/1055072643247779871/image.png) and [7 agility bracers enchant](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/691917309002121216/1055072757035053106/image.png): I will talk about these together since it's the same problem. They are just a tiny bit weaker than they should be for no logical reason. There is a 9 strength bracer enchant. There is no explanation for these being 7 agility and 12 spell power (equivalent of 7 str in budget). Simply buff them up to 9 agi and 14 spell power.

I assure that everyone will be much happier if these changes are implemented and they become a little bit closer to fury warriors in min-max potential.
