# Add CC statistics to battleground

## Post 173714 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-04-02T07:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173714#p173714 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

By CC I mean Crowd Control - abilities that slow, stun, disarm, fear and polymorph players.

People tend to optimize something that is getting measured and ranked.
So instead of competing by damage, let people compete by duration of applied crowd control effects.
People already have DPS meters in raids. So measuring CC in battlegrounds will be pleasant variety.

Just imagine how battlegrounds will change. Everyone will start using other buttons from toolkit.
Instead of fighting in mid for higher numbers, people will keep eachonther slowed and polymorphed.
