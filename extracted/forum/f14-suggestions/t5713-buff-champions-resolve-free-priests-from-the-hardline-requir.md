# Buff Champions Resolve. Free priests from the hardline requirement of dwarf priest PVE meta!

## Post 35758 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-02-23T03:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35758#p35758 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A recent bugfix went out [https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow- ... issues/245](https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow-bug-tracker/issues/245) that fixes fear bosses from changing targets from the tank. This is a huge buff to the viability of druid paladin and memes tanking Nef. Before, a nonwarded fear would almost guarantee a raid wipe. Now, a nonwarded fear means a short mitigation and threat -down for the tank, which is far far less punishing.

Still, due to how a few notable raid encounters operate, having 1 dwarf priest in the raid remains almost mandatory, and due to the boons of redundancy in raid compositions, those who roll non-dwarf priest often are derided for 'trolling' the raid. This sucks.

But yeah, the 3 most important bosses based on these fear mechanics, run on a ~28 second cycle, which means that after a few minutes, 30s cooldown + full duration fear wards will eventually run dry. This happens much sooner for a holy champion due to a 60s cooldown and 10s duration. Because of this, combined with the fact that two champ priests can't champion the same player, champion's fear ward becomes pathetically unusable as a dwarf fear ward substitute, and is relegated to only being used to ward the occasional fear otherwise eaten.

Currently:
Stance dancing warrior tank: Requires only a single dwarfward/zerkdance/ChampRes/BStrinket/tremor/deathwish, but requires a more skilled warrior player and regardless will suffer sudden spikes of both threat and mit loss.
Def stance Warrior: Requires 1 dwarf priest, and a single zerkdance/ChampRes/BStrinket/tremor/deathwish.
Druid tank: Requires 1 dwarf priest, and a single berserk/ChampRes/tremor.
Pally tank: Requires 1 dwarf priest, and a single BubbleInstacancel/ChampRes/BStrinket/tremor.
Meme tank: Requires 1 dwarf priest, and a single ChampRes/BStrinket/tremor.

Ok, so change Champion's Resolve from 1m CD 10s Duration to 30s CD 30s Duration, and we have a more flexible array of viable strats....

With proposed change:
Stance dancing warrior tank: Unchanged
Def stance Warrior: Requires 1 dwarf or champ priest, and a single zerkdance/BStrinket/tremor/deathwish.
Druid tank: Requires 1 dwarf or champ priest, and a single berserk/tremor.
Pally tank: Requires 1 dwarf or champ priest, and a single BubbleInstacancel/BStrinket/tremor.
Meme tank: Requires 1 dwarf or champ priest, and a single BStrinket/tremor.

And when a champ priest is used on the tank instead of a dwarf, the dwarf priest that would previously be only on the tank would now be free to ward the top non-warrior dps, and maybe the top war dps during their single eaten fear, or even spec champ themselves and do the job of 2 champ priests. This single change will retain dwarf priest's swag of providing a stalwart raid with pre-Warding, and will be a luxury to increase dps uptime for those that would be eating fears nonstop. They will still be important with the racial having the strongest impact of any priest racial, but no longer hardline mandatory. Priest players will feel freer to choose other race priests that they enjoy more.

This change also buffs Holy Champion's viability in PVP BGs, while keeping dwarf priest as the alliance analogue of horde's numerous WotF enjoyers.
