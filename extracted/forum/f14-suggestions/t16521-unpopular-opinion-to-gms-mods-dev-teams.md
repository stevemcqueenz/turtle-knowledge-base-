Since i just recently talked to someone about the same topic, here my chain of reasons on why the Summoning Stones are a bad idea:

 1.) People may be easily replaceable, which could lead to (even more) degeneration of the social aspect of WoW
 2.) Summoning Stones (in worst case insta port), are making Dungeons so attractive for people, that they wouldn't bother questing anymore.
 3.) Because of the attractiveness of dungeons, people would propably level up much faster. Which would lead to a retail mentality of "the game starts with endlevel", making new players think that this is the normal way to play the game. People tend to take the path of least resistance no matter what topic.

So Reason 2. and 3. are directly connected to each other.

Vanilla is, at its core, a social and explorational game. Interacting with and helping other players is important. A guild bank does nothing but help a guild keep its valuable resources and lets people share and give to their guild more easily. I've donated plenty of consumes and gold that I normally wouldn't give unless it was, say, mid raid or something like that. Now, while I think dual spec isn't something that really belongs in vanilla, it's not particularly as harmful as something like summoning stones, as it can be used in the context of filling roles for your friends (helping other people) you don't normally try, or maybe swapping to a spec to do different kinds of content in, so like- say, as a ret paladin you want to farm gold or something. Prot is your best bet for that. Spending gold would be a bit counterintuitive to swapping so you can make money, buuuut... You can always invest in dual spec and never have to spend a penny to farm effectively, or spend coin just to fill a spot for your raid. Dual spec used to be designed in a way that it could be abused for efficient raid clears by swapping specs for a specific fight, but that's been changed for the better now and I'm happy its usefulness is toned down while keeping its core function the same.

Summoning stones... Save you a walk to a dungeon, and not much else. There's no meaning or addition to vanilla besides making things easier and giving people less obligation to travel, as you're supposed to do in a game like this. Then comes the issue of people expecting others to do things -for- them rather than doing something themselves. Vanilla is a game where hard work will get you places, and working with and for people will get you even further. Summoning stones, in concept at least, follow that design philosophy, but... This expectation for people to do things for you isn't healthy in any game environment, especially one as sacred and iconic as Vanilla WoW's.
