# Channeling spells are too overpowered.

## Post 130146 by Kuetzy — 2025-05-08T04:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130146#p130146 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Because of the client, channeling spells are too overpowered. Currently, a mage can press 1 button and deal 90% of my health from one channel. This is different than a shaman pressing chain lightning and 1 shotting people. Thats because chain lightning is a cast, so it can be LoS'd while a channel on this client can't. So a solution to this would be changing the spell icicles to a cast instead of a channel. Arcane missiles should go back to its vanilla damage and instead create a new castable arcane ability. Please keep in mind with your class changes how you are affecting PvP in the game. There are other ability class changes that are in dire need of changing because of the imbalances in pvp.
