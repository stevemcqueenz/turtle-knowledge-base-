# Priest suggestions.

## Post 74910 by Luek — 2023-10-31T03:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74910#p74910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Former release-date wow player here... my first real character was a Holy Priest that I leveled and raided with. Quit a few months after TBC came out. I love you guys for making a classic recreation.... anyways.

After all those hours played I always had ideas on how to make Priest more fun to play:

Holy Fire, Smite, and Holy Nova need some kind of utility. Replace the "Searing Light" talent with a talent that gives each of these abilities unique utility....something  like:

"Heavenly Armaments"

Holy Fire: DoT causes afflicted target to do 1%/2%/3% less damage. Change Holy Fire to it's more current form of 2-1.5 sec cast time, 15 sec CD, and 7 second DoT. This would give strategic reason for using the skill.

Smite: Casting Flash Heal/Heal/Greater Heal gives a small chance to make your next Smite instant cast and cost no mana. I think this was even in one version of the game? I could be mistaken?

Holy Nova: Hardest for me to brainstorm but giving it threat reduction with a CD and increase radius sounds good.

Between 5 points in the prerequisite 5 points in "Divine Fury" and another 3 or 5 in a new "Heavenly Armaments" talent, thats quite a points investment to justify the utility.

Spamming Flash Heal 24/7 was always so boring. Flash Heal and Greater heal should probably be on CDs and given buffs. Flash Heal to a 1 sec cast with an 10 sec CD and Greater Heal with shorter cast time and 10 sec CD would add so much more play variety.

Give Disc Tree a talent that adds the Divine Spirit's bonus to Power Word Fort.

Give Shadow Priests a talent high in their skill tree that allows for a unique battle resurrection. Cast the Spell and the shadow priest temporarily "dies", can see the spirit of the dead party member, and brings them back to life with him? Would that be impossible to code?

Cheers!

Looks pretty good overall, just gonna toss some opinions of my own in here.

Holy fire: Good idea on a surface level, but you're replacing one of the big damage boosting talents, so maybe just add the effect onto that one so you're not taking anything away from pure smite priests?

Smite: I think it's pretty flavorful and unique. Might make for a fun rotation that adds damage for regular priests.

Holy Nova: Already has no threat attached to it. Your changes would do nothing but harm the spell, plus it's already got a talent for increased radius. Big no.
