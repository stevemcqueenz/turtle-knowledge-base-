# Cat Druid - Concerns

## Post 134231 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-06-04T08:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134231#p134231 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The classic Cat was the Powershifting Build, where you use shred and reshift into Cat with wolfshead and furor for +60 energy regain.

However in turtle we now have the Open Wounds build, which restores +10 energy for each bleed tick.

This makes powershifting redundant on any monster that can bleed, as the build does around 150 dps more on average than powershifting shred.

The main reason it does more damage is because Tigers fury, can be kept up, however with the powershifting shred build, Tiger's Fury is dropped upon leaving Cat Form, resulting in the DPS Loss.

I think the original goal of the team was to provide an alternate for powershifting but here are the issues:

Bleed Immunes - If you are using the open wounds build, when you run into a bleed immune mob, it disables the use of 2 of your talents that make this build work.

Open Wounds + Ancient Brutality (5talent points) no longer function on these bleed immune mobs, this cuts your dps in half as the custom bleed variant of Cat.

This is not good design, you basically saying:
Here's an alternative to the complex powershifting that you don't like, but it will do zero damage on any mob that is bleed immune, so yeah you're going to have to learn that complex vanilla rotation you don't like anyway, or you'll be a detriment to your raid dps.

Should the damage be less on bleed immune mobs?
Yes, it should - Should it be a 60% or more damage reduction? - No that's way too drastic.

The damage of the Open Wounds cat doesn't come from the bleeds ticking, that's insignificant, it comes from the synergy of the two mentioned talents above.

I think the best course of action is to rework the talents to somehow still benefit on bleed immune mobs, so people can still play this build, and the 10% dps loss would come from only the bleed immune mob not taking damage from the bleeds, not making the 5 talent points useless.
