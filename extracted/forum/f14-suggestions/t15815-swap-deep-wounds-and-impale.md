# Swap Deep Wounds and Impale

## Post 107796 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-13T06:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107796#p107796 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently you need to invest 2 points in Improved Rend, 3 points in Deep Wounds to be able to invest 2 points in Impale.
> Deep Wounds
>  Your critical strikes cause the opponent to bleed, dealing 10/20/30% of your melee weapon's average damage over 6 sec

> Impale
>  Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your abilities by 10/20%

Getting additional 20% to critical damage is very desired for every DPS warrior. Bad thing is that you have to apply bleeding Dot on your target with that talent.

For example Hunters have similar set of talents: Lethal Shots, Mortal Shots, Piercing shots. And bleeding debuff is granted only by last talent, which effectively makes him optional.

In warriors case deep wounds talent feels mandatory.
That bleeding damage is just extra. Warriors don't rely on that debuff to perform their "rotation".
In the same time, other classes need debuffs to perform their rotations.

Proposal is:
Swap Deep Wounds and Impale in talent tree so you have to invest in Impale first and in Deep wounds next.

Deep wounds is a low priority debuff. Any debuff that is actively applied does take priority over it. Therefore you arent actively griefing your raid if you spec it (unless you ofc whirlwind next to sheep targets...)

If we swap the two talents, you are just soft buffing one of the specs that remains dominant in the new patch and remove one o the very few things that punishes them for bad play.
