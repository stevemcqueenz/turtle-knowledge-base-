# Shaman talent change suggestion

## Post 106318 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-04T14:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106318#p106318 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

After some testing I'd propose some small changes to resto and tanking talents.
Will keep out enh dps and ele, my harddrive apparently died on the gamer PC so I can't test those for a while.

Resto

Resto has a very good healing output but has a very one-sided 'rotation'. I see the devs' intention to force some kind of rotation to the class (undertow) but currently it doesn't work. Undertow's reward is pretty niche specially now that resto shaman's mana sustain is much better and expects you to cast a much inferior lesser healing wave or healing wave instead of a chain heal. Can only see it being useful if you are under constant damage (as a healer) or if specific bosses force you to single-target (f.ex. Patchwerk).

What I'd change:
Improved chain heal: Reduces chain heal casttime by 0,1...0,5sec (instead of 0,2...1 sec).
In exchange:
Undertow: Your HW and LHW spells have 50% chance to restore mana shield charges (like now) + they reduce the cast time of your next 2 chain heals by 0,5 sec (not stackable).

This way you get your super fast chain heals but you have to work for them a bit, opening the door for other healing spells into the rotation.

Optional:
Healing Way: Don't proc it from chain heal but make it proc from LHW+HW. This change would give further merit for the other healing spells while it keeps hurting chain heal a bit more.

The end result is a build with a more balanced healing output (slight nerf) and a way more interesting rotation.

Tank/Warden

Here I go by case by case basis at first.

Shield specialization seems nice but has a design flow (the original had, too). Shamans have a low block chance and don't have a way to raise this on-demand (like a shield block). In this form the talent doesn't feel 'shamany' and it doesn't really synergize with what you do. The shaman's relationship with shields is much more emphasized by their relationship with the shield's armor value (spirit armor) and their main way of mitigation is dodging, not blocking. So.

Proposed change: after a successful dodge, shield specialization increases your block chance and the armor of your shield by n% for m sec. Might be stackable, needs testing.
This would be in flavor for shaman tanks and would capitalize on their good agility/dodge while rewarding them with armor which they need since they wear mail. I kept the added block chance aspect but made it rely on dodge. I was afraid of totally removing that aspect because that's percentage-wise less mitigation for a tank.

Enhancing totems talent has a similar problem when it comes to stoneskin totem. Added blocked amount is nice but it's not really what the shaman needs. Instead of the added block amount f.ex. an added crit reduction would be interesting since warden shamans have a really hard time to push out those crits from the table (while wearing mail).

Please consider moving call of flame talent a bit up at the elemental tree. Shamans mostly rely on (badly scaling) fire totems for aoe threat and being able to get that talent without sacrificing too much in the enh tree would make aoe threat keeping much more consistent. Anyway, I doubt that getting relatively early access for added fire totem damage would brake any build.
