Hello. I recently decided to give turtle wow a try, because I wanna experience classic+ and I wasn’t gonna wait another god knows how many years.

Here is some feedback of things I would like to see in a classic + server

Make flying mounts more then just ground mounts:

I’ve seen people request before for allowing faster flypaths. So once someone obtains a ‘flying mount’ they would be able to use that one instead of the gryphon/hippogryph/etc when using a flypath and giving a small speed boost as a bonus. Not sure if it’s possible to code this in. But it would at least allow us to fly with those mounts. Restricted to flightpaths only ofcourse.

Add bonuses to mining, skinning and herbalism in similar fashion as tbc added. Mining = health, Skinning = Crit chance, herbing = HoT (let it scale with healingpower, spellpower or attack power whichever is higher)

The next one has probably been asked already but when I came to this server I kinda expected gnome priests to be a thing. There are medics in gnomeregan. So I don’t see how they can’t be priests. And if troll warlocks a thing, well…

As for their priest racial. Something engineering flavored would be cool. Perhaps a small radius shield generator to put down to protect Allies or something.

A barbershop. Doesn’t need much explanation.

Just my ideas.

## Post 104378 by Moongirl — 2024-10-22T17:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104378#p104378 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I like the effort you have put in your post. Here is my personal opinion on the matter:

 **Flying Mounts:** For one, you control mounts, not flight paths. You are suggesting methods of making your flight paths faster, from my understanding. I am not against this, but I've always felt Turtle WoW is about the journey, not getting to places as fast as possible. On the other hand, if I'm late to an RP event or meetup, this could be quite nice.

 **Profession Stat up:** I believe this was introduced in Pandaria Expansion (or Cataclysm?). These games are about Endgame and therefore Min/Maxing. Turtle WoW is trying to reduce Min/Maxing as much as possible, though honestly it can only be fixed so much due to the nature of World of Warcraft Endgame. Back to the point, this would force one to choose mining to be the strongest paladin, which would be unfair to those paladins that mastered Alchemy for whatever reason. This idea works on a min/maxing server like Retail, but not Turtle WoW.

 **Gnome Priests:** While I would push for this myself, priests are religious. Gnomes, at least to my knowledge, have no religion or religious belief, so this would not fit. Like it was mentioned above, priests are not medics, as most medics are not religious.

 **Barbershop:** There is a barber shop in Turtle, but it only allows you to change your hair color at the current moment. If you want to change your hair, you would need to buy an appearance change token from the Donation Shop. I put a forum suggesting exclusive hair colors from the Barbershop to encourage players to go there, just like there are exclusive hair styles in Retail.

This is simply my opinion and in the end, it is what the amazing Turtle WoW developers do that determines what is included and what is not. Thank you for reading, and good luck on your journey.   smiling_turtle_head
