Increases attack speed gained by Flurry by 5/10% and Increases movement speed by 5/10% while Flurry is up.
while twohand weapon equiped

Improved Onehand Flurry
Increases attack speed gained by Flurry by 10/20% while one-hand weapon eqiuped
(More dmg and threat for shaman tank, performance below 100-200 dps vs other tanks)

Giant StoneSkin Armor
Increases shaman size and Reduces the damage taken from melee attacks and ranged attacks by 75% for 10 sec. 30min cooldown.
(Shieldwall without spell dmg reducing part)

Bloodlust
Fly into a frenzy, increasing your melee and spell casting speed by 15% for 30 sec. While under this effect, your melee critical hits increase melee, ranged, and spell casting speed of all party members within 30 yards by 5% for 5 sec. 2.5min cd
(Halved the Bloodlust Cooldown)

While you have some sound ideas, this is way too overpowered for the moment being.
Let's admit it, this is strictly a shaman tank suggestion article.
I shaman tanked some.

Ancestral Guardian
Increases your armor value from items by 6/12/20% and chance to dodge by additional 2/4/6%
(Armor value up from 5/10/15)
 -> This is already a pretty strong talent as it is. Compare it to pala's toughness or even warrior's toughness. This is clearly the best talent out of the 3, needs no buffs.

Spirit Armor
Increases the armor gained from Shields by 15/30% and Increases defence by 10/20
(added defence)
 -> Anticipation does the same thing for warriors and palas: 20 def for 3tp. Here you just want to attach it as a reward to a 3tp talent which is already good. 900+ armor at lategame.
If we wanna really push this talent, I can see it giving some defense (5, very maybe 10) but never 20. And with the condition that you have a shield currently equipped.
I agree that shaman tanks need some +def but in my opinion itemization and shield spec talent should cover most of it (shield spec's added block amount is not really good for us since there is no on-demand block chance raising ability - a static + def buff next to the added block chance would be much better).

Flurry
Increases your attack speed by 15% for your next 3 swings after dealing a critical strike
(removed amount of talent needed because old nerf from 30% with 5 talent)
 -> I think this would overcomplicate things. I'm okay with having a weaker flurry, after all melee crit already does so much for us (clearcasting, ele deva, bloodlust, 15% flurry). I'd only make it cheaper. 3tp for 15% instead of the old 5tp for 30% seems like an acceptable deal.

Giant StoneSkin Armor
Increases shaman size and Reduces the damage taken from melee attacks and ranged attacks by 75% for 10 sec. 30min cooldown.
(Shieldwall without spell dmg reducing part)
 -> Shaman tanks would indeed need a defensive CD. But this feels out of flavour. An ability has to feel natural for a class, e.g. shield wall matches with a prot warrior perfectly. I can't see why shamans should randomly become giants with this effect. Many possible def CD options exist but I'd accect only suggestions which feel 100% in flavor for a shaman tank.

BL is good at 5 minutes. Since you'll crit regularly, it's basically 20% haste for you and 5% for the party members for 30 sec every 5 min. Not OP good with this cooldown but perfectly viable. + with this cooldown people won't just mindlessly spam it.

Shaman Shield spells activate on dodge/parry/block
(help tanking shaman with mana abit, still have same cd just so it works with dodge/parry/block)
 -> Needs some testing but seems like a perfectly fine idea.

Change how Zul'gurub enchants works.
Keep class Idols but you are able to pick enchant, So mage still need mage idol but can pick druid enchant.
 -> Never. There are other enchants, not only ZG. Sure, sometimes you are class-locked out of the best option but this keeps other places relevant and gives the ZG enchants a unique flavor. People would just forget half of the ZG enchants after such a change.

Shaman weapon enchants
All Weapon enchant totem works with other type of weapon enchant. For exemple Windfury weapon works with Flametongue totem, Flametongue weapon works with Windfury totem, Frostbrand weapon and Earthrock weapon works with both Flametongue and Windfury totem. But you cant use Windfury weapon with Windfury totem.
(Enhancement lack behind in both tanking and DPS vs everyother spec in game)
 -> I'm undecided. Honestly, not liking the idea but I can see it working perfectly fine.
