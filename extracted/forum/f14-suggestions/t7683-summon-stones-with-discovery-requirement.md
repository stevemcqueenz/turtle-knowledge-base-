# Summon stones with discovery requirement

## Post 49225 by Getplucked (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-07T17:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49225#p49225 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Similar to how in Cataclysm, blizzard changed RDF so that you must travel to the entrance of the dungeon before you can queue for it, I'd love if something similar could be done with summon stones here.

All the dungeons I'm doing here end up having to sit and wait 10-15 minutes for the last 1 or 2 people to arrive...not very fun. By requiring you to still travel to the dungeon at least once to be summoned there, I think that's a decent compromise that doesn't ruin the travel aspect of vanilla.

 *"but it ruins warlock class fantasy!"*
I don't think people play warlock to be a summon bot any more than people play mages to be a water vending machine.
I also don't think mages get mad that hearthstones and engi teleporters exist. Having alternatives isn't a bad thing.
Plus, Warlock can summon ANYWHERE, with no level requirements. Summon stones can only summon you to a specific dungeon, and only at specific levels for each dungeon.

It's just obnoxious when you get into a dungeon group only to see that one person is on the other side of the world. Or even when it has happened to me; I queue up and it pops in like 2 minutes and I'm still way far away from the dungeon. Could even make it require 3 people for a summon, I'd be fine with that.
