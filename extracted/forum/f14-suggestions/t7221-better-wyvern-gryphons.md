For the problem of flight durations… check out either *[Dhask's FlightMap](https://github.com/GryllsAddons/FlightMap)* or *[FuBar_ToFu](https://github.com/Road-block/FuBar_ToFu)*.  Both of these require the user to actually make the flight from one location to the other before it can say how long it will take, but once you have that data on any character for your account, it's always available.

As for the "flight won't land" bug, I think that mostly happens when you fly into a heavily populated area.  It could also happen if your connection is acting up or otherwise somewhat slow.  I'm not sure there's much that can be done about it.
