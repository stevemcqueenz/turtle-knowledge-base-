# Narrative Patch Suggestion: Defense of Azeroth

## Post 26244 by Ashkir — 2022-09-27T00:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26244#p26244 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I had an idea for the plot of a future patch for Azeroth and Turtle WoW! In each zone of Azeroth, there are a small number of monsters that serve no purpose. I am aware that they are somewhat uninteresting. What about something titled "For the Defense of Azeroth" or something similar? The purpose of these is to generate quests and missions for characters to complete in order to protect the cities' lands.

 **Goals:**
 - Use monsters that aren't currently used by any quests and that were originally meant to be "filler content".
 - These give these monsters a purpose.
 - These offer 2-3 new quests for zone with an experience boost to make leveling smoother.
 - These offer an update to content and can provide the narrative team a way to look back at old zones and see what hidden monsters may be untouched.
 - You could introduce story lines to random named NPCs who haven't been used yet.

 **Zones affected:**
Nearly every zone could be affected by this.
 - You can focus on monsters to keep roads clear for other players.
 - You can focus on monsters nobody used for a quest in the past.
 - You can give purpose to unused monsters and NPCs.

Far reaches outskirts with barely any Alliance or Horde presence could be supply chain related, or, protecting their settlement.

 **Types of Quests:**
 - I believe these should mostly be the classic Kill X quests.
 - In Elven and Tauren lands you could make it a bit gatherer related, "remove X infections" from trees, etc and use mushroom nodes, etc to mark the items.

 **Possible Title Reward:**
If possible to award a title for these, perhaps "Defender" or something. but, not necessary.

 **Lore Reasoning why:**
Dwarves/Humans - They can call it in Defense of the King's Lands, a play on the previous quest chain.  They are seeking to protect their citizens and lands from invaders and hostile monsters threatening their citizens.

Elves - They are protecting their settlements and the balance of the eco system near them.

Orcs/Trolls - Protecting Orc/Horde settlements. Their lands are mostly unsettled and still wild, would provide a good opportunity perhaps for future expansion ideas lore wise.

Forsaken - Not entirely sure of a good lore reason for them. Could be to protect their once homelands from ruins for memory. Perhaps ridding scourge from their former settlements.

Tauren - Same reason as the dwarves, protecting their settlements, and the balance of their homelands. They can use it to push back against Venture Co again, or push back against the Centaur.
