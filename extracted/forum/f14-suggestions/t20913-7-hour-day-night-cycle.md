# 7 hour day/night cycle

## Post 145385 by Clintonm — 2025-08-14T12:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145385#p145385 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

People play Turtle WoW from all around the world, so for most people the game's day/night cycle doesn't match their own anyway. Also Turtle WoW is a role-playing game, so there's no real need for the time in the real world to match game time.

I suggest a 7 hour day/night cycle as this means it's 3.5 hours of daytime and 3.5 hours of night time, which I don't think is too fast and allows players to enjoy some changes in ambiance over longer sessions. But also it works out that 24.5 hours is 4 days and 3 nights (or visa versa) which means that every (Earth) day, the day night cycle flips, modulo thirty minutes. That means players that play at the same time every (Earth) day will see a mixture of day and night scenery.

You can pick your exact cycle times, for example, 5 hour, 9 hour, 10 hour, 15 hour and 16 hour cycles will all do the job of switching day/night cycles every (Earth) day.

I think this is an easy change that other private servers have done and will be good for players that because of work can only play at certain "real" times of the day and would like to see a diversity of ambiance, and also experience the differing mobs in a few places where the day/night cycle affects the spawns.
