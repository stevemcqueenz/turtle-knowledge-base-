# Guild hub in Tirisfal Glades / The Scarlet Crusade

## Post 5747 by Romanov131 — 2020-04-21T21:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5747#p5747 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Some suggestions involving the Scarlet Crusade guild hub based in Lordaeron, to make the life of new recruits less frustrating and more enjoyable.**

The Terrace of Repose
I think this is where the main Scarlet hub ought to be, not the Venomweb Vale camp. I personally think it feels more natural to have the Monastery as the headquarters and hub, and plus the innkeeper/quartermaster is already there. To accomplish this, this is what I propose:
 -Add a general store to the innkeeper. This one I believe is primordial, as there is a complete lack of food and water unless we walk to Pyrewood or Ambermill.
 -Move the class trainers and current vendors to the Terrace of Repose.
 -Add a basic weapons vendor to the Terrace of Repose. Like the general store, I also believe this is very necessary, as there are limited weapon options as it is. Currently, I am a paladin who starts with a two-handed weapon. If I want to switch to a shield, I have no option but to wait for a one handed mace to drop.
 -Adding a weapons trainer to the Terrace of Repose that teaches the following weapon skills: Swords, Maces, Staves, Daggers, Bows. These seem to be the primary weapons the Scarlets make use of. This one, however, is not primordial.
 -Adding profession trainers; I think Blacksmithing/Mining, Leatherworking/Skinning, Herbalism/Alchemy and Cooking and First Aid are the main ones to consider that would make sense for the Scarlets' situation in Tirisfal. Like the weapons trainer, this one is not necessary, it's just slightly more convenient.
 -Adding mailbox/auctioneer; Just to ease the pain of finding red-colored gear while being mostly isolated in Lordaeron. By adding a mailbox to the Monastery we can encourage roleplay by sending letters and recruiting new members ICly from a distance, without compromising ourselves by going to Alliance cities.
 -Adding a guild bank/storage.

INNKEEPER:
Stock based on: Innkeeper Allison, Trade District
Added items:

Outside the Monastery
 -Slightly expanded minor hubs in Scarlet outposts. Really, just a single basic general/repair vendor per camp/tower would be more than adequate. Means we don't have to go back to Venomweb Vale to empty my backpack every time it gets full.
 -Change the Undead Slayers a bit. Right now they're rampant all over the place and there's no "safe area" if you will. I'd make the tower and the path leading away from it free of undead and leave them to attack the edges of the farm, so it doesn't disrupt Forsaken questing in the area too much. Have a clean split somewhere so that Forsaken characters don't have to fight both Undead Slayers and Tirisfal Farmers all at once, but the Tirisfal Farmers are still obviously being attacked.
 -Move the emissary to just outside the Farmstead, away from the Tirisfal Farmers. This isn't completely necessary, it's just a bit more helpful to not be attacked by the people you're going to be friendly with while trying to get the quest that will make you friendly with them in the first place.

Outside Tirisfal
This is thinking really far down the line, we're probably not even close to being there yet. But I'd like to voice the ideas I've had yesterday while I still have them in mind.
 -Make Hearthglen and Tyr's Hand hubs. Add general goods and innkeepers to them.

About the trainers
 -I don't know how hard it would be to work but it will make sense to put the respective trainers in different wings- (example: warriors trainer in Armory Wing; paladin/priest trainer in Cathedral wing; Rogues trainer in Graveyard torture wing, mages trainer in Library). I don't know how this could work and if it's even possible to happen ingame since the wings are instanced and in a dungeon so it could cause you difficulties in the process.

I hope that some of these suggestions will be taken into consideration and made a reality. Also we're always looking for new ideas by fellow players.

Thanks in advance and best regards to the turtle-wow team and community!
