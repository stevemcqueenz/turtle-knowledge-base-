They are a  neat idea while levelling, but usually the food/water they drop is even too low for the zone they are in.

<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?object=3705> for instance gives you 2-5 milk, the level 5 water equivalent. And it spawns almost exclusively in places you go to at level 15+.

They need to give more and ideally more level apropreate food/water to be worth it. The alternative containing bufffood i like too, at least you get a buff for your teoubles.

Replacing them with regulae chests however is the kind of game homogenisation i dont like. Not only because these foodcrates also spawn inside cities, but also because if you ever were a caster levelling out and about, 3 mobs short of your questcompletion without water in the inventory, it feels great to bump into one of these chests. And classicwow is all about those niche experiences.
