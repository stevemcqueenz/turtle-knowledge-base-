# New letters limit hurts guilds

## Post 49604 by Sunguru — 2023-06-10T21:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49604#p49604 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

2023 — June 7 Update:
> **Torta wrote: Tue Jun 06, 2023 6:45 pm**
> - Added a limit to the number of letters you can send per hour.

I believe this change was made to protect against goldseller's email spam. But regular players and guild banks also suffer. There is lengthy (12 hours?) limit of 25 (?) letters atm. This is too little.

Why using guild bank *characters* and not guild bank *vault*? Vault doesn't allow to distribute items personally to each of 40 ppl static/raid, so we have to use mail system.

There are 2 issues with mail limitations:
 1. It also limits in-account letters, which can't be goldseller's spam. You can no longer send lots of reagents/materials to your crafting characters.
 2. Guild bank characters are having trouble sending items to 40ppl raids. For example, it's no longer possible to distribute consumables, item materials (e.g. Naxx scraps) or enchanting reagents to raiders before/after raid.

Personally, in 9 months of playing TWoW, I have never experienced any mail goldseller's spam. I believe that's a little overkill that hurts regular players and guilds.

What I propose to do:
 1. Remove in-account limit: make unlimited letters count between characters of 1 account.
 2.a) Increase letters limit to 50 OR reduce time period of limit to 1 h.
 2.b) Remove limit for letters with items. At least with items worth >10s.
 3. Remove letter limit for high-level (30+) characters: goldsellers rarery level up higher than 10 lvl.

Please help guild bank characters function without any additional (to relogin) restrictions.
