# Night elf priest racials

## Post 14213 by Ayesha (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-11-29T12:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14213#p14213 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi!

To me alliance priest racials seem imbalanced for high level healing.

Human - increased spirit.
Dwarf - fear ward.
High elf - mana restore.

Night elf - 10% reduce of ranged damage + star shards I would never use in raids or dungeons.

I choose night elves for their aesthetic, but I don’t like that I have to sacrifice any practical benefits. Especially that of increased spirit or fear ward.

I liked playing as a NE priest in WOTLK because I could shadowmeld out of combat. And it was a practical benefit that I actually used.

I would love to see improved shadowmeld or other change that would make night elf priests more powerful and desirable. Now it’s a purely cosmetic choice, as far as I can see it.
Do I want to look like a pretty night elf or do I want to have any practical things I can use? I wish there were no such dilemma for a healer such as myself.

This might sound a bit weird however OG Blizz designed these racials with Lore and history in mind, so Night Elf priests (priestesses) mostly were meant to be battle priests so to speak.

Tyrande is like a hard carbon image of such an example. Priestess. Warrior. Hunter. RPG aspects at least.

So NE priests got some pretty nice racial if you think about the damage mitigation and healing despite all the interruptions right in the heat of a battle. Selfless and fierce they jump into the fray to heal allies and kill their foes. Suits them well to be blessed by Elune and dodge some stray arrows, dodge melee hits and keep their allies intact during a hot battle.
