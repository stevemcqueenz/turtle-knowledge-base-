# World buffs: THE MIDDLE GROUND

## Post 25303 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-17T17:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25303#p25303 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Wall of text incoming
World buffs are very powerful, there is no denying that they make raids faster and easier. There is a lot of arguing back and forth about whether Blizzard designed their raids with them in mind or not and I frankly think that is irrelevant to the topic. This is Turtle WoW and it makes changes the community wants regardless of what Blizzard wanted 20 years ago.

With that out of the way, unto the problem of removing World buffs. Specs are not equal, not only in the "base damage" they do but also in how they scale with stats. Warriors are the outlier that's way above everyone else in this, they gain the most benefit. So removing world buffs will nerf warriors the most, the other classes will of course be nerfed but not as much and the gap will be smaller. This means that now the weaker specs can get a spot in the raid right? Well not really.

They're still going to do less damage than a warrior with buffs or without so a warrior is objectively a better use of the raid spot, but world buffs give raid groups more leeway. What this means is, if the raid is easy already, they can sacrifice a warrior and put a moonkin and they'll still do it very easily and comfortably. World buffs ***give hybrid classes a spot***, they remove the need for sweatiness and warrior stacking and make everyone happy.

But they come with big downsides, the main one is dying **sucks**. An unlucky overaggro, randomly targeted abilities, a disconnect can end up costing you your precious buffs and you'll have to do the rest of the raid feeling very lacking and not contributing to the raid. The second downside is it's one more thing you need to do preparing for a raid. This alone is not an argument for removing it, consumables are pre-raid preparation too and nobody is using that against them.

The solution is obvious, remove world buffs and give that power to the players. Unfortunately doing such a thing all at once is not a wise idea, it can cause balance issues, leave some classes lacking and maybe accidentally leave ones too powerful, warriors somehow always find a way to the top despite blizzard trying to nerf them.

Some people might say, why give that power to the players? Let's just remove the buffs without compensating and let the players do raids a bit harder, after all that is what Blizzard intended! As I said before, what Blizzard intended is completely irrelevant to this server. We have chronoboons, tea with sugar, cross faction raid, custom loot and some class changes already. If we don't give that power back, instead of the player only feeling bad when they lose their world buffs, now they feel bad all the time. That's not fun, Blizzard knew it's not fun. That's why when Blizzard removed world buffs they compensated with new talents, new abilities, and new class-specific buffs and debuffs. WOTLK expanded that even more. With every world buff that gets removed, some buff that gives an equivalent bonus must be added, this is the true way to buff hybrids not nerfing everything overall. [Just look at this and compare it to how few we have in Vanilla and how weak they are](https://tbc.wowhead.com/guides/raid-buffs-debuffs-by-class-wow-burning-crusade-classic#raid-buffs)

The first one that has to go is Songflower, it's a hassle to get and lasts only one hour. The compensation for it can be put as a final point talent of the ret paladin tree along with the very situational Repentance. Unleashed Judgement: 1 point talent, your Judgement of Wisdom/Light/Justice/Crusader additionally increase the critical strike chance of melee, ranged, and spells by 5%.

Next in line, Dire Maul buffs.
- Fengus is easy, it's only 200 attack power. That's going to be compensated by stacking shamans and paladins anyway (Might+the shamans str and agi totems).
- Slipkik is also easy to compensate for. Buff Moonkin aura to a **Raid wide 5% spell crit buff**. I also propose giving the same aura to Ele shamans (without stacking of course). This is because Moonkins and Eles are already a niche within the meme specs and it'll be hard for raid groups to specifically find a moonkin geared and ready to raid. The elemental shaman talent "Wrathful Aura" Should go [here](https://imgur.com/a/GZBJOUm), removing "Eye of the Storm", Move Elemental Fury up and turn it to a 3 point talent 33/66/100%, and put Wrathful Aura in its place.
- Mol'dar's 15% stamina is a nice asset for bear tanks especially, it allows them to be a bit more competitive with warriors and removing it will make them see less use in raids. The 15% stamina can be added simply to Blessing of Sanctuary.
Warchief's blessing. The randomness of getting it and the fact that it's only accessible to horde makes it a very weird buff. But what a lot of people don't know is a lot of Ret paladins ask around for people that want to drop the buff to go to orgrimmar and get it through mind control. It's simply very strong for them and also for Enhance shamans. Warriors and Rogues get a big percentage of their damage from abilities, but for paladins and enhance shamans it's almost entirely autoattacks. Losing 15% haste will be devastating to the underdogs but the warriors will stay on top. Relics are a good solution, a totem that gives 15% haste for a while after Stormstrike, and a libram that gives 15% haste after Holy Strike and the loss is compensated. They can be added to BWL or AQ40 loot tables.

Spirit of Zandalar and Rallying Cry are a special case. They are tied to doing raids. I think it's very important to keep these buffs even if in a nerfed version so that geared players have an incentive to help others with ZG and Ony. If these buffs are removed from raids, and they're only obtainable by raiders in the first place, then the whole server will be seeing less of them dropped for everyone to benefit. Everyone even the non-raiders will be affected. Buff drop times are always social events where everyone especially levelers would gather in the big cities, no more world buffs in raids and all these events will be cancelled.

This is why I think Spirit of Zandalar should be nerfed to 5% stats and 10% movement speed. If we go with the calculation that players already had shaman totem buffs, then blessing of kings stacking with that will compensate for the nerf. Additionally nerf Rallying Cry to 2% melee crit 5% ranged crit, 10% spell crit and 140ap. Also make the feral crit buff raid wide like the moonkin one.

My final suggestion which will be a bit controversial is make world buffs not removed by dying. Losing them to an unlucky death is bad. If the fury warrior loses them then they still do good dps for his spot, if the ret paladin loses them then they're almost dead weight. "Think of the hybrids!" is always used as an emotional rhetorical tactic, and while many of the anti-world buff crowd claims that what they're doing will help hybrids, the facts show that the opposite will happen.
