Can we get some relevant tier set bonuses please warlock's are so trash except the new affli ones that are designed solely for pve. I've always been disappointed by the OG warlock set bonuses.

t2 to t3 are garbage when we compare them to other classes.

T0 is ok with the armor and chance to fear when hit bonuses but after that they're either too situational or outright useless.

T1 is also decent except the 3 set bonus gives 15% healing on drain life, but it's a spell that has literally 0 spell power coefficiency and already hits like a noodle. Not the worst bonus tbh during MC phases but it very quickly becomes irrelevant in all aspects of the game. It adds little more than 20 damage/healing per tick on an ability that is never used anyway. The 5set is actually really good in pvp vs casters and the 8set actually adknowledges the issues with warlock having to kill themselves and waste a billion GCD's on lifetap to not go oom midfight. Somehow these set bonuses are even stronger than ones from future raid and pvp tiers.

T2 are where things start to go downhill Problem here is that it's way too similar to the T1 felheart set except now you get SP on all spells instead of just drain life (the joke is that drain life gets nerfed when u drop the t1 3 set bonus in favour of the t2 3 set bonus because of shit spell power coefficiency). Good for a 3 set bonus for pve I guess. It sometimes worth to just get 3 nemesis pieces for the bonus over named drops like eyestalk belt.

The minus threat on the 8set bonus is a significant downgradefrom felheart with the -threat on abilities. Why not make it interesting and do something like Shadow Bolt has a chance to apply the DoT portion of Immolate? Would also make that ability actually do something in raids even if you aren't directly casting immolate. - Threat bonus should be the 5 set bonus not 8. Mages literally get insta cast procs on their 8 set so -threat is boring.

T3 is also extremely underwhelming for the sheer lack of proc chance on the shadow bolt effect, a 2.5sec cast spell that you NEVER use in pvp unless you're somehow invisible. Would be much better if the set bonus instead just gave a flat chance for your dots to heal a portion of the damage they deal or even all spells have a chance to heal a portion of the dmg on a lower%. Make it actually feel like an epic t3 armor bonus. . Another bonus could be a chance for shadowburn and shadowbolt to proc active corruption effects on the target inflicting 1 tick of corruption, or even ticking it on ALL targets afflicted by corruption (with chance for the dot to crit or proc nightfall). It could even proc other warlocks corruption effects, creating synergy between locks like mages have with rolling ignites. Compare this set to the Frostfire mage set that literally gives them 200sp as a proc and a chance to increase magic resistances when struck...

More -threat bonuses are super lame especially for a 6 set bonus. Not really familiar with how lock threat is in turtle but with salv & spellstone I know there can't be that big threat issues with the new tank specs.

The r13 set bonuses need a major overhaul.. not just with being flat out inferior but the bonuses themselves are garbage.

Stamina shouldn't be a bonus first of all, the bonus +20 stamina should just be reallocated to the stat pool of these items. There's alot of things you could do with these bonus but here are some possibilities.

the new 2 set could reduce the HP cost of life tap by 40% (without decreasing the mana restored ofc). Mana is a huge issues for lock in pvp specifically and it feels shit finishing a fight at 90% hp but being OOM then getting attacked by another enemy and u life tap yourself for 30% of your hp like a fool.

I'd say another bonus for drain life could be very nice but this one is contingent upon future changes to the spell/class as a whole that i'm praying for. As a 3 set bonus drain life could reduce the damage you take while channeling by 20%. I know this would be insane for Soul Link so I kindly ask that you find a work around for this that doesn't keep the other specs utter glass cannon trash that it is currently. It could be as simple as making the effect disabled while soul link is active Alternatively if you have plans to make SL the go-to pvp spec (this looks very likely with the way that class balance currently stands in pvp) then swap the drain life dmg mitigation bonus with a bonus that mirrors the nightfall talent (could also stack) so we can actually play SL without being bored out of our wits because of lack of dmg & range on our spells... SL would be fun if you could have nightfall & shadowburn alas

Since I'm talking about nightfall I think the proc should also reduce the mana cost by atleast half. It feels shit getting a proc and not being able to use it because youre oom and again, having to waste a GCD and kill yourself with life tap. Mages have 0 issues with mana and have clearcasting procs. Even arcane never goes oom now and bdruids innervate themselves to full mana even though theyre now fully fledged dps classes, so why not.

The PvP glove equip bonus needs to be looked at as well, I think it should be swapped with a bonus for fear that actually makes it usable in pvp especially with all the antifear mechanics that now exist. Either a chance to not take spell knockback from dmg while casting or just reduce the cast time by 0.5s. The former would be more ideal for everyone without making it harder to kick successfully.

Can you also make demon armor relevant? Like 60% increased effectiveness for a buff that still costs 950 mana and barely does anything anyway is ridiculous. 3 points increases the armor gained by 320. thats NOTHING when mages version of the spell keeps melees permanently SLOWED for free and the shadow resistance portion does less than a third of priests shadow protection buff. Warlocks have most problems with melees because we have 0 armor and defensives outside of death coil on a 3 min CD so either go all out and makes us a proper anti caster class, giving us a chance to heal for a portion of the dmg taken from spells OR give a 100% baseline buff to to the armor provided by demon skin and a chance to proc an absorb shield that absorbs like 200 damage. I'm not the biggest fan of spells that don't require interaction especially when your entire class already requires so little interaction because we only have 1 CD. So as an alternative to a flat buff to demon armor what if we could 'absorb' the demon armor buff by activating a new ability that empowers the buff temporarily but then removes it entirely after a short duration? I'm reluctant to say make it just provide an absorb since we already have sacrifice (sometimes..) but another major defensive cooldown ability is MANDATORY for this class. I mean shit, remove sacrifice from voidwalker entirely and give us dark pact, which is basically the same as voidwalker sacrifice except you can now sacrifice ANY demon.
