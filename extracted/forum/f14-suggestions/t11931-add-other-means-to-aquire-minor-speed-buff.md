# Add other means to aquire Minor speed buff

## Post 78941 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-27T15:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78941#p78941 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently there is minor speed enchant, which is must-have to enchant for every alt and twink.
It is very desirable because it increases your movement speed by 7% (or 8%). It allow faster travelling.

There is also Spirit of the Wind quest <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=889> which allow you to turn 10 Blood Shards (mined from ore in Barrens) to minor speed buff for 300 seconds.

I think there should be other ways to aquire this highly desirable buff.
For example:
 1. Add cooking recipe, which grant you "Well fed" bonus with movement speed. For example made from fish fished from some low level zones (except capitals). There are too few good fish recipes with "Well fed" bonus. Only mp5 ones.
 2. Add Alchemy recipe of Elixir of Minor Speed. It should use Silverleaf or Peacebloom as ingredient because they are very very underused in the game.
