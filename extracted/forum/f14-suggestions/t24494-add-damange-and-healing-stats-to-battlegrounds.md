# Add damange and healing stats to battlegrounds

## Post 172321 by Duraluminium — 2026-03-25T11:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172321#p172321 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

We all know that many, if not most, people play battlegrounds not to achieve some 'team goal' by carrying flags or capping points, but to actually fight vs players. Sure there are those who need to farm as much honor as possible, so they will either AFK or do anything to win, but they will need something to do after they gear up. We all need additional motivation to play battlegrounds.
And this motivation for me was always battleground statistics.
The current stats show only kills and deaths, so it's not fair. Actually it demotivates many people, because they know that they can never be on the top of the list, cause their class or spec cannot lasthit with instant spells, or they are healers.
Improving the battleground statistics would be a great way to let people compete based on actual damage and healing done instead of simple lasthits.

A scoreboard in battlegrounds shouldn't ever be regarded as a statistic that displays accurately what someone is contributing to the match.
The most important man in your entire arathi might be the one defending blacksmith flag, calling out rotations and never actually getting a kill participation while doing so. Creating a scoreboard that completely accurately tracks everyons contribution is a fools errand.

Damage and Healing are even less useful as a metric as kill participations or last hits. I can stand in the Back of an alterac on my warlock, dotting everyone i get into range. Easily topping the DPS charts. But this does very little to actually move forward the game (Provided there is at least 1-2 competent healers or an orange table on the opposing side)

I know its meant with good intentions, but wow has added scoreboards for dmg and healing in BGs in the past, and it has the exact effect of calling out "low number contributers", who might have very well been more instrumental to a teams success than the top dps.
By adding more useless metrics, we just give players the wrong carrots to chase. The only thing you should care about if it says "Alliance wins" or "Horde wins" at the end.
