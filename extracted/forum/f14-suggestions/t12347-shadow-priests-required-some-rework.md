# Shadow Priests required some rework

## Post 82140 by Erhog (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-21T11:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82140#p82140 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
Currently some changes were made to make shadow spec for a priests viable in raids where they very too weak to take even one. Now they are generating mana for a raid and capable of doing better dps while maintaning their mana better. However this improvements also affected PvP side where shadow priests was already one of the strongest classes with a very limited weak points one of was their need to mana management and inability to heal when needed without losing tons of mana and making themselves weaker due to losing Shadowform. Now they have ability to heal in shadowform, their shadow spells are 15% cheaper and they are also got additional direct damage shadow spell.

So, my suggestion is:
 1) Make different forms for PvP and PvE. Remove <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=45553> and add another form for PvE. For example it could be Paleform which provides 15% discount for shadow spells from Imp. Shadowform <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=45553> but without bonus to damage reduction. Remove 15% mana regeneration in both forms because shadow priests already have another 15% from meditation AND they could buff themselves with Divine Spirit.
 2) Remove Mana Replenishment from Vampiric Touch talent. Add this effect to the new form. This alent already manadatory to take but it's also affects mana efficiency for shadow priests in PvP as addition to already big amount of self healing.
 3) Rework Pain Spike <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=45555> . I'd suggest removing it completely but also could be made this a finisher but with more diminishings if you are failed to use it like - target regenerate double of damage done in 5 seconds. So you should be absolutely sure this is the right time to use it - add some skill involved into gameplay.
