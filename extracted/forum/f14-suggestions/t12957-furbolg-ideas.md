# Furbolg ideas

## Post 86739 by Galendor (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-02-02T09:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86739#p86739 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In game furbolgs are just lootbags and whipping boys for our characters' adventures. Their story of corruption started in WC 3 and Vanilla was replayed in Retail for many times - in WotLK, Legion, BfA and DF there are always corrupted furbolgs that need to be eradicated.

Yet I believe what Twow devs can break this vicious circle of violence and bring some love and respect for these poor creatures. The concept below was planned for a playable furbolgs, but it also can work as a new levelling zone, allowing additional depth to furbolg culture.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/kQLsAS1.png)

Furbolgs' starting zone is Silvermyst Isle, a peaceful land southwest of Teldrassil. It wasn't affected by corruption that tainted Kalimdor during the Third War, and furbolgs here are free from their kin's madness. Their capital is Ursonar - a great lair where, according to the legend, twin gods Ursoc and Ursol slept before the War of the Ancients.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/FanVBIa.png)

A character begins their journey in Slumbery Vale, where youngblood furbolgs are taking care for tribe’s elders until it was attacked by moonkins from Nestlewood Hills. In search of the reasons for their aggression the youngblood should find the Keeper of Sylvermyst who resides in the vale with his kin. He warns the furbolg about strange taint that has overtaken moonkins and requires a help to prevent further corruption. Then the elders of Slumbery Vale ask the youngblood to travel to Stillpine Village and inform the tribe about these events.

After arriving to Stillpine Village and helping with local problems, the totemic guard asks the youngblood to wake druids in Barrow Dens who can help to track the source of the corruption. But turns out that the druids fell victims of corruption, too: they are somnambulary preparing some twisted ritual, referring the youngblood or any other uncorrupted being as a spawn of Nightmare. The only night elf who was untouched by this evil is hiding in the Amberglade but his shelter is in danger of grells. He also tells that the one who is responsible for the druids’ madness and grells’ attacks is the satyr dwells in the depth of the Barrow Dens. The elf asks the youngblood to stop the corruptor.

After the satyr was killed, the youngblood travels to Ursonar and tells with the Elder Sage about the threat of satyrs. The Elder Sage informs that a group of night elves recently arrived on the island. He asks the youngblood to meet the Sentinels in Lunarfall Landing and ask them to help. The leader of the Sentinels mistook the youngblood for the Bristlelimb ambassador. Turns out the elves are here to learn the fate of Silverfall Village and sent runners to Bristlelimb tribe but none of them returns. The youngblood persuates the Sentinels to take no actions until he finds out what's going on in Bristlelimb Village. They managed to find bodies of  the missing elves who were killed by… furbolgs! These furbolgs look sick and are in some kind of lethargy, but once they are disturbed they come to wild frenzy. Yet, aside from such acts of aggression, they are absolutely helpless and can't even eat by themselves. The youngblood managed to find the source of this taint - the foul waters near the village, and returns to Sentinels with amulets of dead elves and a sample of this water.

Night elves tell to the youngblood that the sample has a trace of satyr taint, and that satyrs gather together in the hold on the island nearby. The Sentinels send a runner to Ursonar to bring help to sick furbolgs and ask the youngblood to help them in eradication of Axxarien Hold. Together night elves and furbolgs kills demonic creatures and their leader, bringing peace to unrest souls of Silverfall villagers, and returns to Ursonar.

The Elder Sage proclaims that Bristlelimb furbolgs will be cured in time, and that Ursonar should rejoin with their old allies, the night elves. The youngblood received an invitation from the Sentinels to Darkshore where they could help both night elves and other furbolg tribes.

Thank you, Akos!
It's a sensitive topic due to factions' limitations: that's why I decided to save status quo and do not add shamans to furbolgs.
Fortunately, the background of the zone I created can provide an explanation for such change: isolated tribes settled near their venerated progenitors' lair, living in balance with Keepers of the Grove and dryads (in Slumbery Vale) and having a history of connections with druids of Al'enara barrows - it is enough to make a situation where druidic influence on furbolg society is so strong that it replaces more traditional shamanistic ways. It's an interesting topic to research, considering a long story of night elf-furbolg relations in past, and it could create a cultural schism between playable furbolgs and other tribes. In the end, none people are homogeneous, and different groups of the same species can have different traditions - for example, Pellars of Gilneas.
