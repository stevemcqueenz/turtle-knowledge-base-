# Add Battleground Reward Chests at Level 60

## Post 167555 by Nurpetelian96 — 2026-02-06T02:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167555#p167555 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,

I’ve noticed that while leveling, you receive chests from battlegrounds that contain RNG rewards like materials and sometimes gear. That system feels really rewarding and gives extra incentive to queue for BGs.

At level 60, though, that reward disappears. It becomes difficult to focus on grinding honor while also needing to farm gold on the side. Adding a similar chest reward for level 60 battlegrounds would help a lot, even if it mainly contained gold or basic materials.

This would make nonstop BG grinding more sustainable and rewarding, especially for players who spend most of their time in PvP. I think it would be a great quality-of-life improvement.
