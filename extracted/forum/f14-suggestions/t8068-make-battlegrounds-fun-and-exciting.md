# Make battlegrounds fun and exciting

## Post 51881 by Kobiq (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-06-30T09:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51881#p51881 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As we all know, AV in vanilla is "avoid fight and rush south" or even worse, "AFK till I get exalted and never come back". If people nowadays can esport over and over in freaking summoners rift, why do we need blindly que for AV with randoms, while the map has so much potential for immersive, organized, objective oriented fights?

In addition to that, the profits of playing battlegrounds are debatable. While the blizzard PVP system had some logic in P2, in P6 it makes no point. You get better items from PVE and only play for fun and the "fun" is not so much. So my idea is lets introtuce some new more or less complex reward system to make battlegrounds more "group vs group".

It should be something like:

get good gear from PVE > join epic PVP battles > get gold, consumes, enchants or something else
