Seeing as this will probably be the next big patch I wanted to leave some of my suggestions for it. First of all based on the lore and the location it should be heavily based on the Forsaken(Horde side) and High Elf(Alliance side) lore. This is a perfect time to expand on the undead and high elf/blood elf lore with really fun quest and stories and focus on WC3. I feel like this patch can be the RP dream if done right and should have some RP updates(like new player customization options, new gear, new pets, new mounts...) at the very least. I  aware that Amani trolls will play a big part in this patch also but please don't hand horde just another quest hub with muddy huts, we can be more creative than that. For example we could have a ruined elven city build upon with spikes, crude metal plates, apothecary equipment etc. as a sign of the forsaken takeover or take that same city and wrap it up in vines and hanging bridges to show the Amani influence. Just please no more mud huts

I think the gear, mounts and other rewards should also either be Undead, High elf or Scourge themed. Since we will be walking on scourge infested ground it would be really cool if we could get some cool Scourge gear, masks and hoods, undead horses and such and it would be really fun to make it all actually scary and creepy, basically a horror expansion if you will with a darker tone that reflects the occasional grimness of warcraft. I really can't wait to play this patch and I hope that it's as good if not even better than all the last ones.

 **P. S PLEASE ADD SHADOWS IN THE CUSTOM ZONES**
The world design team has been killing it recently but by god is it all dragged down by all of it not having one single shadow, please implement them any way you can(this is also the perfect time to rework the Tirisfal uplands).

Please leave your suggestion regarding this patch down below, would love to hear em!!
