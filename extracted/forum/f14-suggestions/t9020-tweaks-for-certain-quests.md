Not sure if this goes here but I've noticed a handful of quests that are so awful/time consuming to do on this server that I end up deleting them. I'm trying to do one such quest right now in The Shimmering Flats called "Load Lightening" where you need to get 10 vulture bones. Problem is, there's only like 8 vulture spawns on 5 minute respawn timers, only an 80% drop rate (which I'm starting to question as I killed like 4 of them and not one has dropped), and a congested zone where you can barely ever find them spawned.

Another that just came to mind was one in Thousand Needles where you have to loot 10 of this rare plant on a 3+ minute timer. Again, barely any spawns, usually always camped by someone, etc. I had to sit in the same spot for like 45 minutes right clicking like mad to finish this quest.

Granted, the server population is the main issue with quests like these, but do we know if that will be sorted out anytime soon? Just seems like some tweaks could be made until the pop normalizes.
