## Post 37034 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-07T06:56:00Z

Well this an impact for balancing. Mechanical mobs are immune to sheep, fear, sleep & mindcontrol. So that has some implications for encounters.

For pve thats probably fine. The "worst" that players could do is try tank hakkar with a pet since its mc immune or enjoy the fear immunity on ony, magmadar & nef. Thats niche enough so nobody should get butthurt.

For pvp on the other hand it could become a problem. Priests, Druid & Warlocks often CC a pet when fighting hunters.

I really like the flavor of the change. But i think that would be have to addressed somehow. Maybe give them the Beast tag and put it in quotations ;)
