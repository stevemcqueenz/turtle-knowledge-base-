# Revert the Fix for Aquatic Form aura

## Post 82224 by Jan125 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-21T22:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82224#p82224 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The "Aquatic Form outside of water" bug has existed in TrinityCore for a long time.

A recent patch made it so that the aura is removed when you are on land, instead of on water barrier change.

The bug did no harm to anyone and arguably improved my gameplay by just roleplaying as a fish.

Alternatively, allow players to activate Aquatic Form on land, but give them a -90% speed malus.
