# Revisit Old Zones at lvl 50+

## Post 138641 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-10T13:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138641#p138641 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I believe that many of the iconic vanilla zones are the ones within the lvl 1-40 range, and once you go beyond that the experience starts to feel very different because you don't have much reason to go back to those earlier zones.

I think it would be a fun idea to provide a feature for players level 50+ to have some of the older zone's mobs reset to a higher level with new quests unlocked to do in that zone.  For example, let's say Ashenvale could be transformed to a level 55-60 range if a player activates the feature. This would mean for that particular player, the mobs would appear as level 55+ with the stats that go along with it; for other players, they would appear as their original level and do the associated damage output.

The devs could then create sequel quests that continue the storyline of the zone.  These quests would not be available to players who are still interacting with the base version of the zone.

So Stonetalon mountains could be made level 50-55, and the Barrens could be 55-60, with 15 new quests added to each.

This would not require rebuilding the zones or changing the mobs.  If this were done, it would open up a massive opportunity for endgame content.  As well, a level 25 player could group with a level 55 player to kill the same mobs, and the mob would receive the appropriate amount of damage depending on which player the attack came from, and deals the appropriate amount of damage depending on who received an attack.

The only real issue I see is perhaps it could get complicated writing the logic that accomplishes this, I just don't know how any of this is actually coded in it's current form.

Suggestions:
Stonetalon 50-55
Barrens 55-60
Ashenvale 55-60
Hillsbrad 50-55
Arathi Highlands 55-60
Swamp of Sorrows 50-55
Dustwallow Marsh 50-55

The amount of endgame questing would be drastically expanded with this
