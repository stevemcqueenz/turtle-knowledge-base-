# Mace Specialization for Dwarves

## Post 32581 by Zeldy (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-01-15T06:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32581#p32581 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I feel like Dwarf players would benefit so much more with a +5 Mace expertise racial compared to the +5 Gun expertise they currently have. Only 3 of the 6 Dwarf classes can even equip Guns making the racial completely useless for over any non Dwarf warriors, rogues or hunters, whereas 4 of the 6 Dwarf classes can equip Maces.
Melee weapon specializations are overall much more desirable and useful than ranged weapon specializations, and the nicheness of Gun specialization is further exasperated with most endgame ranged weapons being Bows/Crossbows, whereas there are numerous endgame Maces such as Sulfuras/Might of Menethil etc.
Mace Specialization wouldn't even necessarily have to replace Gun Specialization either, seeing as some other races also have 2 weapon expertise racials.
I'd be interested in hearing other Dwarf players' thoughts about this.

having a melee weapon proficiency as a racial is the most desireable trait for melee dps characters.  The benefit of expanding this makes other races more desireable for them to play as melee dps.  For instance if i want to make a fury warrior, the only options my power gaming mind will allow me to choose are orc, human and goblin for dps and tauren and dwarf for tanking.

It's probably best to keep something like this simple, for example right now there is no division between one and two handed weapons.  Humans have sword spec, it works for both.  It would probably be easiest to implement if left that way.  I think a reasonable expansion would be:

Dwarves get maces
undead get swords
night elves get swords or polearms
Tauren get maces
trolls get axes
high elves get swords
gnomes, are gnomes famous for using any kind of weapon?  daggers to be nice to our tiny friends.

Downside:  If something like this were implemented it would effectively buff an estimated 25-65% of the server's melee characters by +5 weapon skill which would have a significant impact on the difficulty of the content.  rebalancing things can be difficult so you have to really consider what you're asking for here.  It's not as simple as padding health bars a little bit to maintain the challenge.  And I would imagine even ticking up health bars slightly across lvl 60 group content would be very tedious.

As much as i want mace skill on dwarves and sword skill on undead to spice up my gameplay, I expect the devs will want to carefully weigh such things out.  they've probably also thought of this before as there are other custom servers out there that have such changes already implemented.

Also consider how the sum total of the racial skills weigh out.  Humans have sword skill and virtually nothing else significant while if you gave sword skill to undead they also have will of the forsaken which is very strong.  So that's not exactly balanced, of course things aren't balanced now either.
