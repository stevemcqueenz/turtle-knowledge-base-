# Form glyphs for Hunter and Warlock pets?

## Post 33600 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-01-31T01:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33600#p33600 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it's pretty cool that you can donate to change the look of Druid forms. I also think that it's pretty neat that there are classes out there that come with their own companion. What I think would be even better is to combine these two things.

Give altered forms for pets to match a different style. For instance, making a Warlock pet look Elemental or Undead. Undead could also be a cool style for Hunter pets, or Mechanical pets, similar to what was added in Legion.

Imagine an Undead Warlock, or a Human with the Necromancer skin, summoning not an Imp to throw fireballs, but a skeletal mage.

Imagine a Hunter taking Engineering, and being followed around by a robotic wolf.

Perhaps a Night Elf or Troll would want to be accompanied by some kind of mystical spirit.

Pets are an extension of the character, so I think the ability to customize them would be really great.
