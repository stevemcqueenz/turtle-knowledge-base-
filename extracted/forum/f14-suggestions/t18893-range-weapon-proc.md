# Range Weapon Proc

## Post 128580 by Deilemark — 2025-04-26T12:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128580#p128580 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I'm a hunter with Tier 2.5, so I have about 80 spell power on my gear.
I absolutely love weapon procs — they're so fun to play with!

Before I got my Tier 2.5, I used a few alchemy potions to boost my spells and weapon procs.
However, I recently picked up a Dwarven Hand Cannon, and I noticed that its proc does not scale with spell power at all. After spending quite a bit of gold testing all available BoE ranged weapons from the Auction House, it seems like none of the ranged weapon procs benefit from spell power scaling.

This feels strange to me, especially considering that only Hunters and "Bow Priests" could really benefit if ranged weapon procs scaled like melee weapon procs do.

To explore it further, I even made a priest, leveled her to 20 to use a bow, geared her with spell power gear, and picked up the bow from Wailing Caverns. Unfortunately, I found that the proc also didn’t scale.
In the end, I deleted her — there just wasn’t enough cloth gear with agility or ranged attack power past level 35 to really make the playstyle work.

Here’s my suggestion:

Make ranged weapon procs scale with spell power (like melee procs do).

Weapons like Dark Iron Desecrator (+5–10 Fire Damage) and Windbreaker (+7–14 Frost Damage) should also scale with spell power.

Add more gear that has Agility and Spell Power to support hybrid Hunter and Bow Priest builds.

I know this wouldn’t necessarily be "the best DPS," but it would open up a fun and flavorful style of play!
I would definitely remake my Bow Priest if gear and ranged weapon scaling became more viable. :)
I loved the idea of playing a Huntress of Elune — it was so fun getting to level 27 relying only on my bow and staff, without casting spells.
That style of gameplay really captured the fantasy for me, and I would love to see it supported more!

Love what you guys have done — keep it up!  turtle_in_love_head
— Pecolina
