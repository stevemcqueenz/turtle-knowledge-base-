# Rework goblin passive skill

## Post 26390 by Bloodline1x9 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-09-28T23:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26390#p26390 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At first I want to point out that goblins are 1 of 2 races specializing on engineering. And goblins have no any bonuses to engi   wary_turtle_head   Instead of it they have got mining+alch bonus thats looks like far-fetched (pulled by ears). I understand that the admins did not want to duplicate the gnomes passive. I uderstand that creating explosives is chemistry in real life, but in WoW it is created by engineers. And goblins create not only explosives in engineering, but nonetheless they have no bonuses for it.
Though I think that just to give a passive bonus to mining/alchemy or even engineering - it's too common. And boring!
So I propose a rework of the passive goblin skill:

 **[Exploisive specialist]**
 ***All damage caused by bombs, grenades and mines increases by 50%. And also goblins can use them without engineering skill.***

Very important is the second part of the skill, that goblins will be able to use explosives without an engineer skill. They won't be able to create, but they will be able to trade and use what other engineers have created. This will highlight goblins as explosives specialists.

Plus
 **[Industrialist inventor]**
 ***Increase mining, alchemy and **engineering** skills by +5***

So, that is just slight version of current skills bonus includes forgotten engineering.

And one more another option:

 **[Stockbroker]**
Remove commission on auctions up to 0.
 5 minutes duration. 20 hours cooldown
This ability allows you to quickly display the most expensive goods (especially weapons) once a day, which requires significant commission costs

I like Explosive Specialist.  I think that's a much more interesting racial than pluses to a profession that ultimately barely matter.

To feel out some potential flaws in it, allowing them to use bombs without engineering skill is potentially exploitative for low levels.  Say you play a 19 goblin rogue twink.  With the racial as it's written, you could drop a thorium grenade on a group of players and do 501 fire damage and stun them for 3 seconds.  That's a lot of damage for the 11-19 bracket.

The solutions to this would be 1) remove the part where they can use engineered bombs without engineering, 2) add a level requirement to bombs, or 3) what kind of bombs they can use expands as they level, so a level 19 could use anything up to say 95 engineering (at a rate of 5 engineering skill per level), but a level 60 could use bombs as though they were 300 engineering.

At any rate, I like the suggestion.
