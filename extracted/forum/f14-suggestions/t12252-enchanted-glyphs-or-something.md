# Enchanted glyphs (or something)

## Post 81548 by Martin187 — 2023-12-17T21:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81548#p81548 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Of all the professions enchantment is one of the hardest to make profitable.
Most other professions can put their results up for auction making some decent gold.
With enchanting you must rely on spamming the chat.

My suggestion: create a glyph or stone (purchasable  from a vendor) on which you can cast your enchantment, making that an one time usable item available for everyone.

Maybe make the glyph in multiple tiers (lesser, minor etc)

I think this or something like this will make enchanting much more satisfying.

Cheers,

Marty
