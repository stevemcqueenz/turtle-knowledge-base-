I think it's largely correct that feral isn't supported well by the design of the challenge fight. The mechanics very much push you towards being in caster form so you can deal with the debuffs and mobs.

And I adore that fight for it. That's the point of a challenge fight, of a puzzle boss - that you *cannot* just bulldoze it in any spec. Vanilla had several really challenging epic class quests - primarily the well known priest and hunter ones of course. You could not expect to beat those fights in green gear or without researching strategies or trying several times.

The same holds true for the new druid challenge fight on Turtle. You're supposed to prioritize certain stats on your gear. You're supposed to set up a custom talent spec for it. You're supposed to be ready to blow consumables on it. - It's our epic quest after all! Something you'll do only once. The culmination of your growth as a druid. And ultimately druids are not just furry rogues, or feathery mages, or bark-skinned priests. We are all of that together, a true hybrid.

It's a shame that feral doesn't play more into the challenge fight, but it's really awesome how it encourages playing as a balance/resto hybrid. Don't change anything about the fight. Maybe introduce a second fight where you need to use feral skills to beat the mechanics (similar to how hunters have several demons to fight that all cater towards different aspects), so you have to be semi-geared and well-versed in all three specs, like any truly epic druid should be  turtle_in_love_head

This may sound harsh, but if you are unwilling to use any other spec or to pick up any other gear outside feral pieces - you are not playing your class to its fullest extent. The fight is gated behind a long quest line that requires an Emerald Sanctum clear. I think the expectation is that you bring roughly T2 gear. Just roll OS for a couple of weeks and you'll have your caster set that is fit for the fight. First, get familiar with the mechanics, so you actually know what you're doing. And once you have the fight down and you hit a wall in terms of numbers, you go all out. Don't skimp on consumables (Flask of Distilled Wisdom, Elixirs for spell and nature power, Mageblood potion, etc, etc), maybe pick up some worldbuffs (Dragonslayer + ZG alone go very hard and you can even add Tribute buffs or Songflower). And then you just put it all together - your experience as a druid, you having figured out the puzzle mechanics, the gear you've farmed, the consumables, the world buffs - and you one-shot the fight and claim your title as an epic druid  satisfied_turtle_head

Or you just say "it's just a silly gimmick reward, who cares" and call it quits. Which is also kinda fair. But if you think you deserve the reward, there's a lot of things you can do to skew the odds in your favor. It just might take a few weeks of farming and preparing, which I find fair for a truly epic quest. I was very disappointed in the Swift Travel Form quest being quite easy and largely relying on help from non-druids. This finally challenge fight that was introduced much later, is a really awesome prep and skill test in comparison and I love it.
