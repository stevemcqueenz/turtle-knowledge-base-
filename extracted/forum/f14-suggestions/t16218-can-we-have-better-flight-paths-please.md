I would very much appreciate more efficient flight paths.
The worst is likely in Dustwallow Marsh on Horde side.
You cannot simply fly from the southern flightpoint to the northern flight point (I think it's called Brakken wall or something). Instead the flightmaster tells me you will take a nice detour to Taurajo, Crossroads, Ratchet and from there turn souzth back to Dustwallow Marsh. It should just be a straight line.

Starting from the same flight point in southern DWM if yo uwant to fly to freewind Post in Thousand Needles, you will leave the Marsh only slightly north of the Razorfens, only to turn north, fly all the way to Taurajo, then turn around back to the Razorfens and finally fly to Freewind post. :(

Generally a lot of flights go through the Way Cross. Like OG to Ratchet is a huge detour when you can just take the same route as OG to Dustwallow Marsh: Straight down the river.

Or how about Sunrock Retreat in Stonetalon? Zoram Strand is quite literally right above you, but instead we need to go way back to the Way Cross, further East, up to OG and back into Ashenvale.
