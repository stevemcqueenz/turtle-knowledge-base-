# Warcraft III Unit Class Flavor

## Post 22666 by Ctmatsumoto — 2022-08-17T07:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22666#p22666 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Similar to how Priests buy additional abilities depending on their race.

 **Gnome/Goblin: Tinker** (Warrior)
Level 10: Tinker Training I (Passive) - Skill with Guns and two-handed Maces is increased by 5.
Level 15: World Enlarger (Active) - Shrinks the target, reducing attack and spell power by 15% for 10 seconds. 40 yd range. 15 rage. 3 minute cooldown. Requires Tinker Training I.
Level 20: Tinker Training II (Passive) - Engineering skill is increased by 10 and allows you to craft Engineering recipes 1 second faster. Increases resistance to Fire and Nature damage by 2. Requires Tinker Training I.
Level 25: Pyrotechnics (Active) - Throw a stick of dynamite at a targeted area, dealing x amount of Fire damage in a 5 yard radius, and an additional x Fire damage over 5 seconds. 20 rage. 3 minute cooldown. Requires Tinker Training II.
Level 30: Tinker Training III (Passive) - Reduces the cost of Engineering recipes by 50% and increases attack speed with one-handed Maces by 1%. Requires Tinker Training II.
Level 35: Lightning Beam (Active) - Unleashes an arc of electricity at the enemy, dealing x amount of Nature damage and then jumping to additional nearby enemies. Affects 3 total targets. 3 minute cooldown. 20 rage. Requires Tinker Training III.
Level 40: Tinker Training IV (Passive) - When defeated, the warrior detonates their remaining dynamite causing x amount of Fire damage in a 10 foot radius, and an additional x Fire damage over 5 seconds. Requires Tinker Training III.

 **High elf: Spellbreaker** (Warrior)
Level 10: Spellbreaker Training I (Passive) - Skill with one-handed Swords and Defense is increased by 5.
Level 15: Mana Feedback (Active) - The warrior becomes surrounded with anti-magic energy. Each melee attack from the warrior will burn x of the target’s Mana, causing 1 Arcane damage for each point of Mana burned. Lasts 15 sec. Doesn’t affect targets without Mana. 3 minute cooldown. 15 rage. Requires Spellbreaker Training I.
Level 20: Spellbreaker Training II (Passive) - Increases resistance to Arcane damage by 5. Has a 2% chance of increasing melee attack speed by 15% for 10 seconds when hit with a spell that deals arcane damage. Requires Spellbreaker Training I.
Level 25: Control Magic (Active) - Takes control of the nearest enemy summoned unit for 10 seconds, causing them to attack their summoner and allies. 3 minute cooldown. 20 rage. Requires Spellbreaker Training II.
Level 30: Spellbreaker Training III (Passive) - Increases attack speed with one-handed Swords by 1% and decreases your chance to be hit with damaging spells by 1% when equipped with a Shield. Requires Spellbreaker Training II.
Level 35: Spellsteal (Active) - Steals a beneficial magic effect from the target. This effect lasts a maximum of 2 min, but never more than the duration remaining on the target from which it was stolen. 40 yd range. Global cooldown. 20 rage. Requires Spellbreaker Training III.
Level 40: Spellbreaker Training IV (Passive) - Reduces the duration of negative spell effects by 20% when equipped with a Shield. Requires Spellbreaker Training III.

 **Tauren: Spirit Walker** (Shaman)
Level 10: Spirit Walker Training I (Passive) - Skill with one-handed Axes and two-handed Axes is increased by 5.
Level 15: Ethereal Form (Active) - Turns the caster ethereal, granting immunity to physical damage, but unable to attack or interact with objects. Global Cooldown. 2% of Mana. Requires Spirit Walker Training I.
Level 20: Spirit Walker Training II (Passive) - Herbalism skill is increased by 10. Intelligence is increased by 2%. Requires Spirit Walker Training I.
Level 25: Disechnantment (Active) - Removes the most recent buff cast on the target, and deals x amount of Shadow damage increasing relative to how recent the buff was cast on the target. 2 minute cooldown. 2% mana. Requires Spirit Walker Training II.
Level 30: Spirit Walker Training III (Passive) - Increases resistance to Shadow damage by 5. Spirit is increased by 5%. Requires Spirit Walker Training II.
Level 35: Ancestral Spirit (Active) - Raises a fallen ally from the dead with full health. Usable while in combat. 15 minute cooldown. 30% of Mana. Reagent: Ankh. Requires Spirit Walker Training III.
Level 40: Spirit Walker Training IV (Passive) - Increases spell damage and healing by up to 10% of your total Spirit. Reduces the duration of negative spell effects by 20%. Requires Spirit Walker Training III.
