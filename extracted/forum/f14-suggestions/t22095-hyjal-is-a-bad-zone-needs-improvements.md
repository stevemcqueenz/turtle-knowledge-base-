# Hyjal is a bad zone. Needs improvements!

## Post 153244 by Llamalicious5 — 2025-09-26T01:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153244#p153244 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I went hyjal for the first time today. First impressions were great. It looks really good, the music is awesome and everything was good. Until i started questing. The whole zone is one long twisting path. No short cuts as far as I've seen. Going from Nordanaar, to the beginning of the zone, takes so long. You basically travel the whole zones length twice. How is this a new and improved wow+ zone. Traversing it, quite simply, sucks. It's poorly designed. Also, spirit healers are poorly places. I died in the southern part at barkskin village. I spawned in Nordanaar. I have to travel the full length of the zone TWICE to get to my corpse.
Also, the flight path is terrible. Even going from Winterspring to Nordanaar takes so long, even though they're so close. The wind rider follows the path the whole way. Let us fly over the mountains. That flight should take a minute. It feels like 5.

The quests: When i initially entered the zone, i was excited to do all the quests it showed on the map. So many ! signs. Yet as it turns out, most of the quests are merely repeatable quests. Gather x thing, get a crystal. Exciting. There were some normal quests, which lead to chains that led you outside of the zone, gave 1 or 2 crystals for a 5-6 part chain, that then gave a pick of a few very average at best items. Also, the travel. Questing here, takes so much time.

What this zone ends up being: Join a raid. Kill satyrs. Loot satyrs. This is by far the most efficient way to get crystals, yet it takes 4-5 hours to get 150 for an item. Given that all the other ways of getting crystals absolutely suck, outside of AH, this feels like the only decent way to to it. The regular quests requires you to traverse a zone, that is horribly optimized for traveling.

So all in all, this zone is atrocious. One long twisting path with few bonus branches. No shortcuts. Few to no good proper quests. Farming satyrs in a raid group for 5 hours, is all you really should be doing if you want to be efficient. I wanted to like hyjal. But it just sucks. Yet another long grind, I feel forced to, if I want pre bis. Joining a raid grp, looking at my screen every 2 min for 5 hours, while playing other games/watching movies on my other monitor, is bad design.

You need to add more efficient quests. You need to add extra paths connecting the northern part of the zone with the southern, so its actually possible to do short cuts.
