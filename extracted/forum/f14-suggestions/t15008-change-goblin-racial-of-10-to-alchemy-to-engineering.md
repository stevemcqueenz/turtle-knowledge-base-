I kindly suggest you change the racial for 2 reasons.

there is SOME justification for the alchemy racial, but you have to dig pretty deep into lore to find it, and i imagine it was retconned already when WoW vanilla launched.

From the WC2 manual:

"Berserkers are a bloodthirsty sect of Trolls dedicated to the total annihilation of their hated enemies, the Elves. Suffering numerous experiments with strange chemicals and potions by the **Goblin Alchemists**, the Berserkers have had many strange abilities bestowed upon them that make them all but unstoppable in the heat of battle."

so yeah, there is some background to this. but in WC2 goblins also were the techies of the horde, providing them with explosives and zeppelins. And because they were kept as a techie race in WoW it is expected by most for them to have + engineering over alchemy.
