# Eranikus fight for SL currently impossible to complete

## Post 96204 by Neltharion (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-06-02T20:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96204#p96204 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The 1st phase of the Eranikus fight on Tel'abim is currently not doable for a couple of reasons:

 1. Griefing: on this server, somehow, this fight can be easily griefed by positioning low level druids on top of the enemy raid: when those druids die around 5 shades will spawn. This as serious as any other bannable methods of griefing: it has nothing to do with PvP and you can't counter something like that;

 2. The shades themselves are overtuned as hell, it's been confirmed by devs that they AOE too often and spawn too quickly.

 3. Tel'abim is a 200 pop server, it's quite impossible to form 2 whole raids to finish this quest. The Eranikus fight in Moonglade was never intended to be this hard.

A simple solution I suggest is to fix the shades' cd of their AOE, fix their respawn and generally scale this content to 20/25 players as it should be on such a low populated server.

Respect the time of the people who actually love this server.
