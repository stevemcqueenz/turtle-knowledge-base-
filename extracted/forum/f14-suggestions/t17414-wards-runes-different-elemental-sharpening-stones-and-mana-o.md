# Wards, Runes,Different Elemental Sharpening Stones and Mana Oil

## Post 118847 by excin (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-02-03T20:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118847#p118847 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Elemental Sharpening Stones are awesome, I'd love to see different flavors of them with different elements and effects: These can be
 - (BS) A minor Flame Strike Rune using fire, making the mechanic more accessible and less awkward to gear around
 - (Alch) Haste oil using Air
 - (Alch ) A minor Vampirism boost using a bunch of Ichor of undeath and hearts of the wild.
 - (Ench) a [Wizard oil] tier Mana regen oil, something that sits between the lesser and the brilliant tier.
 - (JC ) a Hit/Arpen charm using light : small brilliant shards (so many of them around and little use)

Thorium is a bit under used.  TBC added consumable BS runes and wards that could be applied to chest and shield. I found they were a good use of excess Adamantium for a black smiths and added a nice flavor to the profession.

 - Thorium protective rune:  25% per hit to absorb a small amount of physical damage for 30 minutes
 - Thorium Shield Ward: function like a protection potion but for physical damage and applied to a shield
