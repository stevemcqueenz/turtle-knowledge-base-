So , apparently the main reason for nerfing this was PvE believe it or not, so that Shamans didnt rip aggro straight away after 1 spell.

The PvP burst change was just a 'positive' side effect apparently.

First things first.

NO ONE in this game likes stupid talents on a timer like this, It just makes playing the spec feel like pure dogshit, If you are not in some kind of speed running guild where you are pulling trash packs non stop, you simply cannot keep the buff up and its a massive DPS loss for Shaman - It's not fun gameplay , its stressful and its just a farse.

The DPS burst of Elemental Shamans was one of its niches in PvP, it could be shut down by one Curse.

Did it need toning back a little? - yes perhaps, but this is too much, you have literally strippped half their crit damage from first cast.

In PvP, you cannot keep this buff up to 5 stacks either, PvP just doesnt work like that.

The best possible suggestions is to compromise, So what id say is.

Have the Talent give 50% crit damage, then Electrify can stack 3 times, which will boost the crit damage up to 65/80/100% crit with a 3/6/10% damage increase.

This will return some of Elemental Shamans initial off the fly damage, and not hit like a wet fish for first 2-3 casts.

>  **What does this suggestion address?**

In summary, the stacking mechanism should just be removed entirely.

No other classes have to stack in order to get their 100% crit bonus, but instead have it as static permanent talents (see: Vengeance for Balance Druids and Ruins for Destro Locks).

Additionally, other class specs (e.g. Arcane Mage) have gained newfound burst with CC2 that surpasses the burstiness that shamans had to begin with.

These inconsistencies have no real justification and comes off just as anti-shaman bias.

Electrify is ultimately a lazy solution to a highly exaggerated problem (shaman burst). It is not fun to play and has ruined the identity and gameplay of the ele spec.
