# PvE Bounty Hunter - Mob/Boss of the Day

## Post 10278 by Lawmonark — 2021-04-28T12:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10278#p10278 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really like the bounty hunter system, but it is very limited due to the fact that the player needs to be online and you need to find their location.

What i am suggesting is  a PvE bounty system. Have a "Mob" of the day and a "Boss" of the day, then a third unannouced mob that is also "wanted".  These mobs can be out in the wild or inside of dungeons. Make Rare/Elite and Dungon bosses the targets but the third a "normal" mob.

If it is too much to be a "mob of the day" then there could be "mobs/Bosses" of the week. Would be cooler if it changed daily, but i can see why it would have to be weekly.  Either way, it can be set up to have seven mobs and seven bosses, listed for a certain day... ect,  As for the "thrid" mob, it could just be randoms, not sure how hard that would be, but put a list of mob/bosses in a  random generator and spin.

This would make higher level player go back and run low end dungeons and content. You will need to have a reward that entices them.

As for a reward, it would be a cool way to earn turtle coins in game.

There certainly are a bunch of rare/elites around the many zones with no specific quest oriented with them. Since spawn rates can be quite long for multiple people to hunt any one mob, a simpler method could be to have these elite mobs drop a head/trophy item to be turned into a PvE Bounty Hunter NPC (Much like the 2 named Kodos and Thunderhawk mobs in lower Barrens).

 1) Kill Tak the Leaper
 2) Loot [Tak's Head] and click to begin quest.
 3) Turn in bounty quest for reward.

I figure it be easier to just copy/paste those Taurajo mob loot quests across the many unused elites/rare creatures. Heck have the turn in NPC be in the arena or BB so both factions share 1 Bounty NPC.
