Shaman at the moment is definitely a powerful class, but what many people missunderstand is how he is stilll imited by his talentpoints. And will have one or several exploitable weaknesses as a result.

Elemental:
There are two versions of Elemental Shaman. Regular and Fire. Regular is by far the more useful for pvp. You can see them casting lightning bolts and cls and usually wearing a shield. They can get absolutely fucked by interrupts or even just something wailing on them since their pushback protection is weak unless they got critted. DO NOT EVER LET THEM FREECAST. use Line of sight if you can or get in up close and personal.

Should you see the rare fire elemental shaman, he pumps out more dps than regular ele but he isneutered quite heavily by dispelling flameshock. He also suffers from weak spell pushback protection. And he has no spare points to improve his heals. Very vulnerable to just getting killed himself.

Enhancement
Easily identifiable by their twohanders, enhancers have the ability to kilm absolutely anything they get their hands on quickly. But thats the point. If they cant reach it they cant do much. They dont have enough spellpower for their ranged attacks to do much. Shocks still hurt, but they are limited to 20 yard and if you are roughly equally geared you should be able to outdps them at range on most classes. Hecan have an instant heal or somewhat threatening shocks, but never both at the same time.

Resto:
Curse of Tongues, interrupts or hard CC absolutely ruins him. He is probably playing in a group or alongside other players and you probably should not attack him solo!

Assuming you play battlegrounds, i cant stress this enough: Do never pick fair fights. If you are a warrior or retpala and he is an enhancer, dont go in 1:1, he will kill you. Gang up on him. If you are a mage and he is enhancer, kite him slowly to death. Dont get close just so you can end it quickly.

Against shamans you always need to identify what shaman you have against you and exploit their weakness. If your class cant exploit this weakness you should wait for reinforcements if possible

Additionally, since shaman is the flavor of the month class, there is a ton of very well geared shamans queing at the momet. If they are outgearing you, thread carefully and try to let someone with an equal gearlevel to the shaman tank his burst instead.
