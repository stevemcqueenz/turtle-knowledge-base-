# Combat Rogue Tank, Marksman Hunter with no pet option

## Post 147758 by Quetzocoatl — 2025-08-22T22:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147758#p147758 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hoi! I am relatively new to Turtle WoW, so forgive me if those suggestions already were given. I searched a bit through the forum, but alas, honestly, I spent only about 5-10 minutes searching ^^

As the title suggests, I would like to see if we could add a bit more options for some specs. In my case, the suggestions are not neccessarily my own thinking, but were also inspired by either retail WoW, season of discovery in vanilla wow or by brainstorming through the internet.

COMBAT ROGUE TANK
I like the idea of some talent options for a Combat rogue tank. From the rogues, we Combat rogues already are "decently" tanky, though of course not on par with others. In particular, IF that idea would be something cool, Id suggest 3 talent perks:
 - A talent to increase threat generation (for tank reasons ;D )
 - A talent to reduce damage further. I like the idea of a dodge + parry tank. Like a bandit, that uses dirty tricks and grit instead of stealth to mitigate incoming damage. Ideas could be either giving himself a shield, temporary damage reduction, or reducing incoming damage by reducing all incoming damage via a debuff. This however, could be of course abused in PvP, so balancing could be a problem.
 - A talent for taunting. (For tank reasons ^^). Could be flavourful too or an attack that tunts, like Mocking Blow from warrior. Or something cool like giving the "backstab" ability a taunt, so when the enemy turns around to target an ally, the Combat rogue can punish them with a nasty backstab >:D

OPTIONAL:
In my fantasy, I also like the idea of a Rogue with only one weapon in hand instead of dual wielding. Like either only a dagger or only one big mace like a typical thug ^^ Maybe increased chance to parry + threat increase when single wielding a weapon. Could be cool. The idea with a combat rogue tank of course is to make him viable in a way, that it can be used for dungeons and for fun, just some experimentation! Of course, we dont want them to suddenly break too, and make them unkillable in pvp either.

MARKSMAN HUNTER WITH NO PET.
Much simpler than the combat rogue tank idea: A simple talent for Marksman Hunters to have increased damage with ranged weapons when they use no pet + maybe increased mobility. Like a cool ranger idea, that doesnt rely on pets but on sheer marksmanship and mobility! Could be two talents too, like one talent that says:
"Increase damage dealt (ranged/melee or only ranged) by X% when no pet."
And a second talent with
"When no pet, Increase your movement speed by X%. Stacks/Does not stack with Cheetah", as an example.

Again, just some fun ideas for options ^^
