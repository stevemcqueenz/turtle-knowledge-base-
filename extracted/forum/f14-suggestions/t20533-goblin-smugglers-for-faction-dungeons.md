# Goblin Smugglers for faction dungeons?

## Post 141857 by Teerack (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-28T09:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141857#p141857 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm thinking it be nice to have a way for the horde and alliance to do Stockade and Ragefire Chasm. My idea would be to add a goblin NPC in Menethil Harbor where you can pay him one silver to smuggle you into Ragefire Chasm where you just pay him one silver and are teleported inside of the dungeon, and for the horde it would work the same with a Goblin Smuggler inside of Ratchet that you pay a silver and he smuggles you into Stockade when you pay him one silver. I think it be a nice compromise that doesn't break immersion and still requires you to go to a specific location just not the enemy capital. It be especially nice since there is cross faction play.
