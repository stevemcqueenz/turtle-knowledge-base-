# Make fall and lava damage more punishing

## Post 163874 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-27T16:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163874#p163874 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi,

I’d like to suggest making fall damage and lava damage more punishing.

Right now, both feel very forgiving, especially lava — in some situations you can almost swim in it for a few seconds, which feels quite strange and breaks immersion. Lava should be an immediate and serious threat.

Stronger fall damage would also:

Make positioning and exploration more meaningful

Better punish risky mistakes

Reinforce the old-school, dangerous world feeling

Increasing lava damage (or making it scale up very quickly) would improve immersion and environmental danger overall.

Just an idea — curious to hear your thoughts.
