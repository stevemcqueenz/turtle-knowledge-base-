# 1.18.1 PvP ranks system suggestion

## Post 172327 by Vorosh — 2026-03-25T12:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172327#p172327 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hey there. I would like to pre-face this post with complimenting your amazing patch. It had made me want to start playing on TWoW again and has managed to let me mobilize an entire sizable RP-PvP guild to start playing here.

The honor values leaked from the trailer and suggested from the discord channels sound perfect, you have liberated us from the oddities of the original vanilla pvp rank system with a more linear grind that has the perfect length.

 ----

However, it appears you are now **struggling to find purpose for the original pvp ranks. Perhaps just cause it exists, it doesn't necessarily have to be involved in your new system, which is far better on it's own.**

Currently **it appears you can get rank 14 in about the same time you can get the full t2 new pvp sets**, if we go by the rumored honor numbers, all while there's still t3 to get which requires more than double the grind.

That's great, **but it may end up making everyone a High Warlord or Grand Marshal**, the same inflation that Blizzard's careless approach resulted in - people joked that the rank means nothing. **Why make it so that you get the highest possible rank while not be halfway done even with your grind towards t3 pvp gear?**

Consequently, I'll list some potential **alternative approaches on how to re-use the PvP rank system**, that may give the devs some inspiration on how to approach this matter practically.

 ----

 **1) Increase the total honor needed to earn rank points, but remove decay + unbind the new pvp gear from rank requirements.**

 **Right now, decay has no point, it only serves to reward people who farm honor in specific timeframes, and smartly park themselves at 0% progress of each rank before each wednesday just to avoid the decay's impact**. Therefore you shouldn't take decay into account when thinking "how long should the grind be", as it's easily avoidable - all while other players get multiple ranks every few days.

Overall decay only serves to reward/pressure you into grinding in specific timeframes, which is a terrible system and it's great that  you made it irrelevant with your new currency based system.

The ranks at this point might aswell serve as just a fancy title that shows off your faction war contribution, anything to avoid a pointless inflation of r14 titles all around, or the preservation of the weird timed honor grind mechanics that are objectively unpleasant, cause that's all that decay makes you do.

Consequently, ~300k vanilla honor for rank 8 (t1), ~1.4m vanilla honor for rank 10 (t2) and ~5m vanilla honor for rank 12 (t3) + 2 more ranks earned by those dedicated enough to continue PvPing despite having earned all the possible gear, so that the highest titles are reserved for those dedicated to the faction war truly.

 -----

Even then, ranks this way would only serve as a parallel system that doesn't really add anything and is just a forced way to feign their relevancy, so there's a second suggestion on how this could be approached

 **2) Make new pvp progression completely independent + restore old legacy rank system and items seperately.**

I like this idea even more, because it **adds true variety, two seperate gearing systems that can be intricately combined, and restores the variety of ranks and sandbox system based on each person's continued faction war contribution** without necessarily making pvp gear dependent on it.

 **So basically :**
 - Implement your new pvp gear just as you intended to, requiring honor points and conquest (but no rank title requirements whatsoever)
 - Restore legacy ranking system, with full continued decay past previous ranks, access to old gear for transmog purposes or odd set combinations.

 **So since your new pvp gear progression is far superior, let it exist independently. Attaching rank title requirements to it is sort of pointless since both currency and rank points were made flat/linear grinds**, making the titles completely performative.

 **Make ranks decay normally like original vanilla with weekly calculations, allowing people to experience 2 seperate systems in parallel with 2 seperate rewards**

 **Those who competitively farm more honor gaining access to cool transmogs, and those who continually PvP holding the awesome titles, adding variety to the title-holders in the world --- instead of inevitably ending up with a quarter of the server being Grand Marshal after doing a singular 1m honor grind, deflating the title.**

Potentially add small bonuses or access to odd items for title holders, while keeping the primary normalized gear progression dependent on pure honor/conquest currency, instead of just completely removing the legacy sets.

 -----

If a dev is reading this, of course you don't have to precisely implement this, the new update is already great enough to make us grateful - but due to the nature of how the rank titles work, it's best to figure this out sooner than later before titles are given out too freely, unnecessarily reducing the longevity of the experience.

Thank you in advance for your hard work! Your latest patch has brought at least dozens of players that I know to play on TWoW!

**This appears to be a long wall of text, so ill try to make a tl;dr.**

Currently, the rank pvp system and the honor currency are a straight up copy of each other with no seperate true meaning, both are linear grinds.

This may result in there being too many High Warlords and Grand Marshal, as the equivalent honor points to get r14 is what you need to reach t2 gear.

The rank system only has the caviat of decay, but decay can be ignored if you time your grinds correctly or simply grind too much at once, making it about time-pressured grinds + incentivized to not care if reset is coming soon, a mechanic that serves no good purpose.

You can therefore make the titles carry more weight by :

 - Increasing the rank points required to get r14 by matching the journey to t3 gear + some more for those who continue to pvp after getting the whole set, and removing decay

 - Re-introduce the vanilla pvp ranking mechanics for access to legacy pvp gear/transmogs and maintenance of pvp titles, yet make it completely unrelated to the new custom pvp gear, helping add some variety to player paths in pvp.
