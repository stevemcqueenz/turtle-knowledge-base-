Hello,
I've seen alot of suggestions about disabling healing scaling for a [Diamond Flask](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20130) or reducing it and so on.

My suggestion is a bit simlier and would cancel practice to rolling +healing gear on Warriors while aren't making this significant and famous trinket useless: make it regenerate 2% of total health every tick similiar to effects of [Blood Craze](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=16492) or [Demonic Sacrifice](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=18788). There would be possible to improve it's effect by popping it while wearing +stamina gear but cancer with healing sets on warrior  will gone away.
