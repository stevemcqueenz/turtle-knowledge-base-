# Special Hair Dyes?

## Post 101298 by Moongirl — 2024-09-15T15:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101298#p101298 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone, I hope you are having a great day!   smiling_turtle_head

Have you changed your hair color with the hair dyes? I'll be honest, they are a bit expensive, but crucial if you want a new look. I think there would be more reason to buy them if there was *exclusive hair colors* from the dyes!

I'll give a few examples on what I think should be new hair colors that are not available on the character customization:

 **High Elf Fel Green**: I think it would be a nice addition for Blood Elves. It doesn't have to be bright, but it would compliment the green eyes of the Blood Elf Skin Token. *(I would personally like this a lot!)*

 **Troll Shadow Purple**: Now that there is Troll Warlocks, a purple hair color may be a cool addition. There is a navy blue, but it isn't purple. The purple color can enhance the "shadow" in a Troll Warlock.

 **Orc Light Brown/Brighter Red**: I'm not quite sure which one would be better for orc. On one hand, the light brown would be quite nice for the Mag'har Orc Skin Tokens, while a brighter, fire red may be nice to the Chaos Orc Skin Tokens. Either could work, honestly.

 **Gnome Chemical Purple:** I'm not really sure why, but a bright purple might be nice to Gnome Alchemists. We have all these bright colors for gnomes, why not purple?

These are my suggestions, there could be more, for other races. I think it would be nice for customization and also roleplay as well. Thank you for reading, and good luck in your journey!   smiling_turtle_head
