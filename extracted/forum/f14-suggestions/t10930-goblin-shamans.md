# Goblin shamans

## Post 71219 by Shury — 2023-10-16T13:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71219#p71219 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone! first time here of the forum   smiling_turtle_head

And yes, I'm one of the many people asking for goblins to be able to become shamans.
But instead of just crying or asking for it without reason, I actually tried to provide something that could fit the lore, plus all the sources I used for my argument.
This argument could also possibly work for Goblin Priests, by looking at the remaining aspects that I didn't cover or have utilized. But that's not what I'm here to discuss.
On a side-note, something that I'd also like to mention before going any further, is that goblin shaman npcs already exist in the game, so it's not that big of a stretch for them to be playable.

That said, I really think Troll witch doctors are the key to make goblins able to become Shamans.
Witch doctors are an heavily alchemic-based type of class.
There is no proper class in-game to define them, so they are considered as a mix between Shaman and Priest.
And since Goblins' identity is heavily based on alchemy (and engineering), I think it's a perfect fit.
The witch doctor culture comes from Trolls, more specifically jungle Trolls. Just like our dear Darkspear friends.
But as some already know, it's not something exclusive to them. I'll make sure to mention and provide sources to this later.
To conclude, here's a possible in-lore introduction that I made:

 **The first Goblin Shamans:**

While Goblins weren't directly interested in Shamanism, they were lured by the alchemic knowledge the Darkspear Trolls had thanks to their shamans and witch doctors.
Goblins have an innate ability to create concoctions (often ending up being volatile ones), so they seeked to improve their skills to make an even better profit out of their craft. While also simultaneously and most importantly, getting rid of any possible goblin competition (or any other race knowledgeable enough to compete on the market).

But while seeking to take that knowledge for their own, something occurred within the goblin trainees that were learning from the troll Shamans, like a "side-effect" of sorts, as the goblins would say;
It sprouted an interest in the Trolls' shamanistic culture.
The reason for this newfound interest was simple: To be able to learn the valuable alchemic recipes they so longed for, they were first "forced" to play along, and learn about the role the elements have in Azeroth, and earn a connection with the spirits through various ancient troll rituals.
Therefore, these ignorant "shamans-wanna-be" learned and became aware of the impact of pollution. And the importance of balance between the elements.
And most importantly, thanks to the wisdom of the spirits, it allowed them to gain a new mindset which heavily influenced their beliefs.

With this newly found interest, and by knowing well that this "new self", wouldn't be seen with good eye from other goblins who are usually more profit-oriented;
Most of them decided to stay in Sen'jin Village, or among the Troll, Tauren and Orc shamans of Orgrimmar, in the Valley of Wisdom. So they could learn the shamanistic ways in their entirety.
While others that cared about preserving their social status, are still among the goblin society or even within the apothecaries, in Undercity. Disguised as generic Alchemy trainers, so they wouldn't be considered heretics or ill-minded.
