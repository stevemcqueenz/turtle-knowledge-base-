Greetings, I checked the items you can get from the Wardens of Time and the dungeon you can go to get the reputation up.
The items you can buy from the factions are not realy good considering the difficulty of the dungeon and the time you need to grind that reputation.

For example:
Temporal Bronze Boots (exalted)
 507 Armor
+8 Strength
+9 Agility
+23 Stamina
+1% Block
+19 Block value

Change it to:
 600 Armor
+5 Strength
+25 Stamina
+30 Block value
+3 Defense

Timeskipper's Helm of Alcarity (Honored)
 549 Armor
+12 Strenght
+20 Stamina
+2% Dodge

Change it to:
 580 Armor
+12 Strenght
+22 Stamina
+1% Dodge
+1% Parry
+2 Defense

Time-Worn Rune
Let it show you the remaining charges.

Some items are realy just too weak to justify the reputation grind. If you clear the dungeon, you have better items already. Of course they should not get too strong, but A bit fine tune here and there should be done. I am not sure how much the other items need, because I am not familiar that much with other classes.
