# Some sort of spellblade class.

## Post 157167 by Elias21555 — 2025-10-26T13:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157167#p157167 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Title, basically.

When you look at how magic is distributed across WoW’s class design, you can broadly divide it into three flavors:

- Divine magic – granted by higher powers (Paladin, Priest).

- Elemental / external magic – drawn from the world or other entities (Shaman, Warlock).

- Arcane / internal magic – the manipulation of raw magical energy itself (Mage).
The main thing is though, there's no melee equivalent to the latter category. There's lore that supports the existence of it, but there's nothing that really matches the vibe of some sort of arcane-oriented, melee class. There's also basically no melee classes that are cloth-wearers despite the odd amount of +STR cloth gear you'll find. There's the battlemage archetype with Shaman, the Crusader/Holy Warrior with Paladin yet there's a notable lack of the quick, fast and technical spellblade archetype.

Lore-wise, there's precedence. There's Kirin Tor battle mages(magi?) and spellbreakers.

Thematically, it’s odd that we can play a Shaman channeling lightning through a hammer or a Paladin smiting with holy light, but not a mage who weaves arcane bolts through a blade. The “magic swordsman” archetype is a fantasy staple. Bladesinger is played a ton in DnD despite being pretty meh. Don't get me wrong, Warcraft's lore is borderline schizophrenic sometimes and very unique at that, but on the surface, it does its best to present itself as a generic fantasy world because people enjoy that. A spellblade contributes to that surface image while still having a basis in the lore itself.

I think there's also potential to have this class sort of toy with popular magic themes that aren't really present at the moment. The Caverns of Time are right there, I don't think it's a stretch for basic Chronomancy to also be utilized through something similar to SnD or a buff that turns damage into something taken over time. Likewise, we don't really see a ton of illusion magic despite being common enough to merit being considered its own school of magic in-lore, which could decrease hit or if you wanted to go all out, have a mob aggro an allied mob.

Mechanically, I think this potential class has room to fill a few missing niches:
- There's a real lack of cloth-wearing melee classes. Yes, I get that it'd be squishy, but c'mon, have at least somebody care about the random pieces of +STR or +AGI cloth pieces that you find here and there. The Engi goggles could be great for this class. The DMR robe could actually have a use for this class.

- There's also literally zero melee classes that just want to use 1H + some stat stick offhand.  Legitimately, what's the point of stuff like the Failsafe Activation Key, the Almanac of Savagery and so on when any class that *would* hugely benefit from it is rocking either a 2H or dual wielding. Any class (DPS casters) that does actually end up using it just ends up viewing it as a largely sub-optimal piece of gear they can use to tide themselves over until they can get a better upgrade.

- The potential for a DPS-debuff support hybrid or a DPS-buff hybrid through on-hit spell effects. As of right now it's literally just sub rogue that fills this niche mechanically.
tl;dr: cmon man itd be pretty cool
