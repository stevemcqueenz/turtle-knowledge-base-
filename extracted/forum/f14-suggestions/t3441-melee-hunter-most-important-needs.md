# Melee hunter most important needs

## Post 20795 by Nettleburn — 2022-08-01T14:30:00Z

Hello!

I play melee hunter, from around the patch. i try switching aspects, and other variables. i know stuff. the hunter is "wierd", but i like it. it is considered weak, but in some situation it preveils, so its okey. and yes - its wierd.

 1st change that is very important for the gameplay, and would give some 'flavour' to melee hunters is: thrown weapon. thrown weapons are not considered ranged weapons for other purposes, like shooting a sting or an arcane shot - you cannot do that with thrown weapon, cuz its not considered ranged weapon. it would be great that aspect of the wolf, would not threat thrown as ranged weapon.

the important thing about it, for a hunter with aspect of a wolf, is to be able to start* a fight like a warrior or rogue does - shooting (throwing) 1 hit to proveoke (it actuaaly isnt "autoshot" to shoot more - the hunter got a "throw" ability, not "autothrow"). trust me i miss the ability to start the fight, especialy with setting a trap (as i do not do much more than setting traps, when im in wolf aspect).

THE FLAVOUR THING (i mentioned): is for people that seen some axethrowers or headhunters in warcraft games. or rexxar.

seems not much, but for me its a gamechanger. it would mean i could unwear my ranged weapon worn "just in case", and my 'ammo pouch'. and i could stay in aspect of wolf even in more situations.

 2nd change i suggest, not that important, is for moongose bite. the decryption of moongose and counterstrike differ very much - the counterattack is to be used on the opponent whose attack is parried by you. but moongose is described 'can be used after a dodge'. which should mean: if you are fighting 2 mobs, regardless which would hit you and was dodged, you should be able to use moongose on any of the opponents, not just the one which hit you dodged.

it would make aspect of monkey and improved aspect of monkey more useful. [edit]: and it would be much help at fighting 2 mobs ofcorse

thx vm. have fun, the serveer is an andventure.

 *starting a fight switching aspect from hawk to wolf doesnt do its job (but its still an option, exemple: talent boosts both). i mean my aspect of wolf costs alot of mana (and i see its purpose). so you cannot realy start a fight with hawk and switch to wolf if you doesnt want to depleat all your mana fast.
