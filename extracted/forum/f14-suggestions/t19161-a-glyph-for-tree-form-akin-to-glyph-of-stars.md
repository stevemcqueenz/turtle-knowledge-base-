# A Glyph for Tree Form akin to Glyph of Stars

## Post 130161 by Deilune — 2025-05-08T08:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130161#p130161 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I love playing Resto Druid but I don't like the treant appearance, so I used a trick: clicking "Illusion: Furbolg" after shifting into tree form, which (for whatever reason) reverted my character back to its NE appearance while maintaining tree shapeshift form. Now this doesn't work any more, a side-effect of a recent bug-fix with shapeshifting I guess.

So I would love to see a new glyph for the tree form which changes the treant visual into a modified version of the normal (non-shapeshift) appearance, maybe with leafs falling around you to your feet and/or green sparkles around you or something along those lines.
