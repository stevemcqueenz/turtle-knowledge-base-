# Please ban multiboxing

## Post 120081 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-15T20:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120081#p120081 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I understand that the small player base at the time encouraged many to play two or three characters at a time in order to get past elite mob barriers or do dungeons, nowadays it is no longer necessary, and I feel that this mechanic has even been abused.

I keep seeing many characters of similar level in groups doing some quests, which makes it impossible to do the quests to kill a certain number of enemies because they are all in groups killing the mobs.

This has become common in Durotar and the Barrens, so please do something about multiboxing or ban it outright, it really is no longer necessary to allow such a thing now that the player base is large enough to be able to play with only one character at a time.

Multiboxing also affects socializing and group play, with some covering dungeon or raid slots with multiboxed characters.
