Why not include Golden Sansam to Mongoose reagents though? Instead of other valuable reagents?

Currently to craft Mongoose Elixir you need:
 2x Mountain Silversage (2g57s each)
 2x Plaguebloom (2g84s each)
 1x Crystal Vial (5s each from vendor)
So one elixir costs ~10g87s.

Suggestion to change Mongoose's reagents is smart. My suggestion is following:

 2x -> 1x Mountain Silversage (2g57s each)
 2x -> 1x Plaguebloom (2g84s each)
 2x Golden Sansam
 1x Crystal Vial (5s each from vendor)

This change should lower the price of crafting the Elixir almost by half supposing Sansam's price remain as low as it is now (1s each). Not only that, it won't devaluate Mountain Silversage price much because that herb is also used in large quantity to create Flask of Supreme Power - example. 4 herbs are still needed in order to create the Elixir so this change doesn't cause any herb supply issue. However, this change will probably cause Plaguebloom price to drop a bit - I suppose the Plaguebloom is mainly used for Mongoose Elixir.

It's also worth noting that high price of Plaguebloom affects other elixir prices too. For example 2x Plaguebloom are required to create Mageblood Potion which only gives passive mp5 buff and isn't that good for casters compared to Greater Arcane Elixir (3g78s) which gives the caster 35 spell damage and is cheaper. I find price/power imbalance here.
