# What about survival elements?

## Post 5596 by Cachucamaru — 2020-04-02T17:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5596#p5596 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Very simple; what about introducing survival elements to the game?

OPTIONAL OF COURSE(Maybe toggleable with a day's worth of cooldown?)

Maybe hunger?
 -Gotta eat that food from time to time, or you get a debuff to vigor.
 -Have to eat little in lower levels; in higher levels, either more food or more powerful food.
 -Reason to level cooking; reason to level fishing as well (Easy food).

Maybe thirst?
 -Gotta drink that water from time to time, or you get a debuff to spirit.
 -Have to drink common water in lower levels; in higher levels, more water or higher tier water.
 -Reason to level cooking.

Maybe sleep?
 -Gotta hit that /Sleep once in a while, or get a debuff to
 -A second of sleep for each level; on level 60, have to sleep for a minute.
 -Using a tent or sleeping indoors gets you a buf "Sleep well"(Name not final of course).

ABOUT SURVIVAL SKILL.
 -Maybe add temporary placeable workstations? (Like anvils or whatever, still have to have reagents to make them)
 -Sleeprolls (For RP reasons and bonus for sleeping/having to sleep less)
 -New tent designs? (Only cosmetic difference)
 -Logs to sit on (Like chairs, for RP purposes)

I'm going to end here for survival skill to not enter the infamous "Player housing" territory...

Would be nice.  happy_turtle_head
