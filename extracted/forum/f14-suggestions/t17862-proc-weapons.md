# Proc weapons

## Post 121760 by Cheruscan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-03-05T13:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121760#p121760 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtles,

this Topic is meant to drive some attention to procc weapons like Revenge of Kalimdor, Ashbringer, the new anchor mace from crab boss, the 2h mace from Emerald Sanctum, etc

I really like procc weapons since they give alot of flavor to melees white hits

However many procc weapons have a really bad proc rate and furthermore no scaling on their procs if they are doing dmg.

I understand that balancing these weapons is hard and playing against them when wielded by certain classes can be frustrating due to the RNG involved

so I think it would be a good idea to increase proc rates in general while lowering the potency of their effects and then give them some form of scaling to make them interesting to build upon

Lets take the Corrupted Ashbringer, it has a lifesteal procc that averages ~200dmg as base and (like all lifesteal procs) scales 100% with Spellpower and it has a PPM of 1.8

this means if wielded by a paladin with 150 Spellpower and 1000 AP it would boost their dps by 10,5 on average IF every proc hits

dps procs (generally) are considered spells and thus are affected by spellhit/spellcrit respectively increasing their randomness even more

So what if we increase Ashbringers PPM to 2.5 but lower the base dmg of the proc to ~120 reduce the Spellpower scaling to 50% and add an AP scaling of 15% (to make the weapon more interesting to warriors and AP paladins) on average this would be a dps increase of 9,5

this would actually lower the dps but make the procc much more consistant in performance resulting in a much better "feel" of the weapon

The thing is, proc weapons are meant to be inconsistent. Thats the whole thing about procs.
You cant have strong proc effects that are strong when they proc(or even scale) and be consistently proccing

Firstly, proc weapons conviniently dont care about the hit that triggers them, be it seal of righteousness, hamstring, sunder or mortal strike, the damage comes from the proc, not the ability that triggers it.
Thunderfury  for instance in the hands of a paladin in CC1 is a 110 dps weapon due to sor. This makes it impossible to balance weapons that derrive the majority of their value from procs if the weapon isnt classlocked or ends up being unusably shit for some.

Since of all proccweapons ashbringer was mentioned specifically id also like to point out that even without the proc, this is a decent endgame wespon. And a 200 dmg lifesteal is already a 400hp swing in any fight (you gain 200 health and your oponent looses 200)

It shouldnt just be looked at for its dps potential just like nightfall isnt only balanced forits wielders dmg output

Im all for procc weapons being worthwhile, but there is is definitely too much of a good thing, some might remember then scythe od the harvest an item that despite its abysmal stats has had a spot in endgame pve & pve by being a often proccing well scaling lifesteal.

Between proccing often, scaling well, being on a good weapon base and the procc itself being meaningfully impactful, we cant have more than two out of these four attributes or the item gets laughably problematic.
