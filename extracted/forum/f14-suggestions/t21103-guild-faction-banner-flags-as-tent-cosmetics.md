# Guild/Faction Banner/Flags as Tent Cosmetics

## Post 146678 by Serijas — 2025-08-18T06:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146678#p146678 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Seeing that some place tents in a crowded spot where enemies are gathered around, this could be an interesting kind of "King of the Hill" vibe with others to do while farming rested EXP. Using the survival skill to plant your guild or a reputation rewarded banner on the ground for the time being, you got something entertaining to do while boosting your rested EXP. Since the timers are high anyway and it can be bypassed by having multiple people with the survival skill in your party and banners at hand, too, it doesn't really change much about the gameplay other than the feeling of being out there with the boys claiming a bit of land for yourself.

It is also a sort of advertisement in the open for the Guild, too.

What'cha all think?
