# changing the honor system has gone from necessary to urgent!

## Post 52893 by Darkstromkar — 2023-07-10T21:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52893#p52893 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Right now the pvp is completely broken, bgs full of over-equipped people fighting against beggars, heart attack premades, bears that are literally immortal and impossible to cc... Well... this has been like this for months....

The problem has now grown with the arrival of many people who are ranking and who have not only broken the brackets but have also made accessing a position where you can equip yourself take months.

The classic player profile is made up of people who work, who have families, etc. Obviously, not 100% are like that, but the vast majority are.

What benefit can someone have in doing bg if 1st you are going to die like a dog crushed by people with T3 and 2nd it is impossible to access any pvp piece because honor is very expensive at the moment.

To access the MINIMUM pvp team you need to dedicate several months to it, as I said, having a bad time... bg to bg.....

The time has come to break the system and put something similar to the TBC system so that people feel that they can access the team, even if they have to play as much as they can, even if they are constantly crushed having a hard time, they know that there is light at the end from the tunnel and that at least in a few weeks you can take some GOOD piece....

With the current system, it is practically impossible for almost everyone, which will gradually make people play less and less pvp (in fact it is already showing... how can it be that with 7k players there is only 1 WSG with 15 queue minutes?) That's why people are leaving the pvp BECAUSE IT'S UNSUFFORDABLE AND IT DOESN'T GIVE FRUITS.

If we put a more accessible gear, what we are going to generate is that more and more people do pvp because it is really going to be worth investing time in it, which is great for the format, people who like pvp will always have people to be able to play bgs and the rest of the players feel that it is worth being there to access the equipment they need.

Please, developers... think carefully because the future of pvp is at stake...

It is true that the system of buying items by honor of the tbc is too casual.

Suggestion on how to change the loot system in pvp without changing the Brackets model by making a kind of mix between the classic system and the tbc system. .

 1º Eliminate the ranks
 2º Keep the Brackets system to distribute the RPs depending on the honor of the week.
 3º Eliminate the weekly Rps Decay.
 4º Put the pvp pieces on sale for Rps.
 5º Not to be able to buy R12/13 pieces without having previously bought R7/8/10 pieces
 6º Not being able to buy the R14 weapons without having obtained the R13 equipment
 7º Each piece costs an amount of Rps proportional to the relevance of the item so that it costs several weeks of pvp to get an item depending on the bracket in which you stay each week.
