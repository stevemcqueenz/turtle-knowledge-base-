Adding the Dark Ranger skin to the Undead class would give us a whole new level of customization and diversity. Some of us really dig that Dark Ranger style, you know? It would be so rad to have the option to rock that look and stand apart from the crowd. Plus, it would create a sense of inclusivity, letting players express their individuality and adding more flavor to the whole Undead experience.  happy_turtle

Now, let's talk about the community and player engagement. We all know that WoW is more than just a game. It's a living, breathing world filled with amazing people. And let me tell you, if you guys introduced the Dark Ranger skin for the Undead, it would set the community on fire (in a good way, of course). People would be buzzing with excitement, and we'd see a surge of enthusiasm and dedication from both current and new players. Imagine the role-playing possibilities and the stories we could create with our Dark Ranger characters. It would be a game-changer, no doubt.   turtle_in_love

So there you have it, my fellow adventurers. I really hope you'll take my suggestion to heart and seriously consider adding the Dark Ranger skin to the Undead class in Turtle WoW. It's not just about aesthetics; it's about embracing the lore, offering more player choice, and taking our community engagement to a whole new level. You guys have been doing an incredible job so far, and I believe this addition would make Turtle WoW an even more epic and immersive experience.   smiling_turtle

Thanks for taking the time to read this. Keep up the awesome work, and remember, the Undead are ready to embrace their inner Dark Ranger!   turtle_tongue_head

Cheers,

Skins in WoW are textures that are wrapped around the same model that has the exact same skeleton.

The image you're showing is the Blood Elf model with the Blood Elf skeleton underneath. Currently the Blood Elf model and skeleton are used to represent the High Elf model (and skeleton) in Turtle WoW.

In order to have this for Forsaken, it would somehow require to hack the game database so there are both Forsaken model + skeleton and a Blood Elf model + skeleton.

I don't know if that's possible in Vanilla. There is an example for Retail WoW where Activision made two versions of the Orc - one with a hunched back and one with a straight back.

I have to say that is 99% impossible to do with Vanilla so your dream will never come true. There is a custom patch that adds the Chinese (boneless) version of Forsaken, but for some reason every time the game gets an update, that patch is removed from my game so I stopped using it. Maybe it would be possible with custom patches to achieve what you want, but that would mean that only you will be able to see it and nobody else - they will see a traditional Forsaken - hunched over and with bones protruding.

There are technical limitations to what is feasible and what isn't so people need to learn some things about how games in general work underneath (you don't have to be a game developer to do that, just be curious and inquisitive) and not everything is possible through the power of friendship and positive thinking as it is in animes.

Okay, I understand your point about the technical limitations. Given the constraints of the Vanilla version of the game, it may indeed be difficult to implement a completely new model and skeleton.

However, what about having an illusion instead. Illusions have been used in World of Warcraft to provide temporary visual changes to characters, allowing players to experience different appearances without altering the underlying model or skeleton. This could potentially be a more viable option within the limitations of Vanilla WoW.

By introducing an illusion of the Dark Ranger for Undead characters, players would have the opportunity to temporarily transform their appearance, adopting the aesthetic and style associated with Dark Rangers. This would allow players to enjoy the visual representation of their character as a Dark Ranger, without requiring changes to the core model and skeleton.

While implementing such illusions would still require technical work, it may be a more feasible solution compared to a full skin overhaul. It would provide Undead players with the ability to visually align themselves with the Dark Ranger concept, even if only on a temporary basis.
