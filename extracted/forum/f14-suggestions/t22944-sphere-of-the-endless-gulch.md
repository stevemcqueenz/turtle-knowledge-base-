# Sphere of the Endless Gulch

## Post 160188 by Shillex — 2025-11-20T16:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160188#p160188 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello,

Sphere of the Endless Gulch feels very bad to use. Ive done some digging on issues with it and it seems like there is an ICD on the procs for Wisdom of the Mak'aru of 3 seconds, which really slims the chances of it proc'ing to an unusably low amount. I am a balance druid trying to spam cast wrath (at 1.5s) to fish for procs, and it just does not occur enough. In several 2min fights I never reached the 8 stacks to get the haste buff, which feels horrible.

Perhaps it was designed to only be used with mages? But even for mages having a 20% chance for a stack every 3s is much lower than the intended proc rate and thats if you are only casting Arcane Missles the whole fight.

My suggestions to change it would be as follows:

 1. Increase the proc rate (to 30 or 40%?) and move the 3s ICD to a 2min ICD for the Enlightened State buff, so that it cant occur more often than other trinkets with a 2min or 90s cd.

 2. Change the proc rate to be based on the spell cast speed, similar to SP coefficients (longer spell cast, more chance of proc).

If these seem too powerful, even just increasing proc rate and lowering the %haste you get (from 20% to something like 10%) would feel better, as there is no rewarding gameplay with this currently because you never get to see the benefit of the fun buff!

Yea I understand it's difficulty of being on a 10-20 man raid content, but it is certainly harder to get / more rare to drop than anything blue or purple item that drops in any of the 10m raids.

 1. You need a lot of luck for the summons matts dropping from ZG fish boss (20% i think?), took me 2 months to get 3 clackora summons... Costs gold for mats to summon the boss
 2. Then fishing the other matts isnt difficult at all
 2. Then need luck for the actual trinket to drop from clackora (20% again)

All this to say for a extremely rare trinket that very few people in the game will have (due to this luck, and it not dropping as consistently as trinkets that just have a flat % drop chance in a weekly / bi-weekly raid), there should be more rewarding gameplay than just a 30sp trinket that you can basically match by spamming UBRS.

And yes if you only casted wrath the entire fight, maybe it would proc every 2 minutes, but
a) thats not realistic to encourage single button gameplay (same with a mage, they dont want to just cast arcane missles) and b) even with just wrath spams, im telling you from target dummy testing that it is not proccing every 2 minutes and if it does its at the tail end when a boss would almost be dead anyway. And that assumes no mechanics and no downtime on casting which is not how majority of boss fights are.

Nerf the spell power, nerf the haste, balance the procs, etc. Whatever it is, for an item that is this rare to grab to have such an enticing ability it just feels horrible never seeing it in action, thats all im saying.
