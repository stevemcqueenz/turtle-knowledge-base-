# Making Ony Buff Affect Anyone In Any City

## Post 129994 by Dominance90 — 2025-05-07T09:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129994#p129994 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

This idea comes into mind when trying to figure out how to **address the lack of population in other cities**.

Darnassus is not very well located for Endgame, **but Ironforge is**, and used to be the go to Hub for endgame raiding back in the original vanilla, at least on my server.

Since Ony Heads pop more often than a bag of kernels nowadays, Stormwind became the hub for evryone, including Levelers, making the city feel overly packed.

 **By allowing the Ony buff to affect everyone in any city**, altough albeit a bit difficult to implement, would **probably** incentivize players to make other cities their home, making them feel more alive.
