# Remove PvP flag in dungeons

## Post 38463 by Unwantedpvp — 2023-03-21T13:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38463#p38463 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle WoW is a RP/PvE server with optional PvP capabilities. Although the PvP is nowhere as toxic as it can be on realms such as Warmane, it's still annoying to go into a dungeon with PvPers, and get flagged from AoE-buffs such as Battle Shout.

The issue being that, when I'm done running a dungeon, I sometimes get killed outside by opposing faction members (happens often in Scarlet Monastery, for instance).

As I said, it's nowhere as common as it would be on something like Warmane, but why do I have to get killed because some people chose to play in permanent PvP-mode for the %XP buff? Unfortunately, the buff's incentive is so high, that's is nearly impossible to get into a dungeon party without at least one member that has the flag, effectively spreading it to the whole party like a disease.

So my suggestion is pretty straightforward: make it so that PvP flags are removed when we enter an instance, and restored afterwards.

If PvPers have to lose their XP buff during dungeons because of technical limitations, I reckon that it's only fair. I don't see why PvE/RP people have to suffer the consequences of healing/buffing PvPers in dungeons, it's virtually impossible not to anyway. On PvE/RP realms, you can ask a person to drop their flag before going into a dungeon, but that's not the case with that one-time PvP-mode.

As a tank, I already got killed many times after I was done tanking a dungeon and exiting it. Corpse running is absolutely not fun and pointless, on top of being a huge waste of time, wasting any buffs/consummables you'd still have also. I'm strongly considering kicking any person that has the flag in my party, because not using Battle Shout to stay out of PvP mode is unrealistic.

Let us decide when we want to be in PvP or not please. There's absolutely no reason for us to suffer from the perma-PvP-choice others have made for themselves.
