# Talent proposal: Anger over failures

## Post 90449 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-17T13:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90449#p90449 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just throwing idea about talent or trinket.
Increases your critical hit damage for 25/50/75/100% when your spell/attack missed for 10 seconds. 1 charge, up to 5 charges. Charge is consumed on critical hit. If 1 charge add 25%, then 5 charges too add 25%, just work for 5 crits.

Current meta for everyone is "gather hit cap and enjoy your always-hitting spells and attacks".
I proposed this to add alternative itemisation priorities. This trinket adds different compensation for your failure. So you don't need to stack hit always and can go for crit leaving high amount of misses in your attacks.
