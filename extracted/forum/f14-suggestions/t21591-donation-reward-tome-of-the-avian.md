# Donation Reward:  Tome of the Avian

## Post 149591 by Mekk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-31T16:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149591#p149591 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Allows a player under a Harpy disguise to utilize flight points.

I've noticed that a few flight points allow their usage while your disguise is still active; for example, going between Sparkwater Port and Blackstone Island, or when using the Moonglade FP.  When this occurs, your character "runs" through the air and it looks a bit goofy (OK, it looks *really* goofy   turtle_tongue_head  ).  I'm guessing this is the primary reason why disguises must be disabled when utilizing flight points.

However, since the Harpy's walking/running animation is "flying" by default, it looks very natural to "run" between FPs.  Surprisingly, this also works with the HD patch as the more modern Harpy utilizes its actual flying animation instead of sticking to the running animation (which surprised me, to be honest).

This could possibly also work with the Banshee, Ghost, and Shade, since they technically "float".
