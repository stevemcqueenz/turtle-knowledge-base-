# RE: Survival (It's a bit of a mess)

## Post 172830 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-28T03:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172830#p172830 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

The new version of Survival is out, and while in a lot of ways it's better than the old implementation, it feels like a serious rush job. So this is like...one part suggestion and two parts criticism.

Right off the bat, the skill tiers are just shoved haphazardly into the list without the divider that puts them at the top like every other profession has. It's nice that the profession does more now, but as a secondary type it almost feels like too much. It's a gathering profession and a crafting profession, it's a gap-filler for the craftsman challenge, it's teamwork synergy, it's utility, it's consumables. It's kind of a bit of everything.

Which, hey, I get it. That's kind of what it's meant to be - something to fill in gaps and let players do things. Maybe it's okay for it to do "too much". What's not so okay is how chaotic and inconsistent it is.
- You can't do any gathering until you hit Prof. 5, unlike any other gathering type.
- Gathering gives no profession gain, even at the start.
- Even after the rework, you *still* start out spamming torches.
- You have to grind to get the ability to make basic tools you can just buy. I get these tools are mostly there for craftmasters, but still...
- Also, most of them still require Mining for necessary materials.
- The slingshot, which as far as I can tell is a simple pulling tool, takes to Survival 30 to get?
- The sticks that are one of the first things you can make have no use before that.
- The leaves also just end up cluttering your inventory for a long time.
- You can't make your own dim torches, you just have to buy and light them.
- A lantern takes a torch to make? (And am I wrong or is the lantern dimmer? Either way the light is a janky square.)
- Etc...
I'll admit I haven't been going *hard* on survival, but I have been making an effort, and it seems like it takes intense grinding just to get it to be useful. My personal take it that Woodcutting should *maybe* be its own skill (in which case there would be different qualities of wood for things), but Survival should *definitely* get some tweaks to the materials used in recipes and when they're available so that it doesn't end up spending most of its progression being an inconvenient way to make things you already have. It would be nice to be able to make some of the threads and things that recipes need that currently can only be bought, which could give some more early things to craft, and more reason to invest in the skill.

I'd also like to see rock piles as another gather point besides trees (maybe making stones from Mining less common to make up for having another source) and the Copper in tools that currently exist in Survival replaced with Rough Stone. It's fine for later recipes to use Mining products, but I don't think you should need to lock in a primary profession or hit the auction house to get started. Not for a secondary.
