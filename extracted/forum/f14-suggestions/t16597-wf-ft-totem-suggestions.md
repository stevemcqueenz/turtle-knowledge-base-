# WF / FT Totem Suggestions.

## Post 113274 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-18T21:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113274#p113274 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Windfury and Flametongue totem used to give a 10 second Imbue, so whenever you outranged them, which happens alot, as the totem radius is terrible in Vanilla, you kept the buff for an extra 10 seconds until it refreshed when you got back in range.

Now the totem works as an aura, so asoon as you move away, you instantly lose the effect.
This makes these totems pretty terrible in PvP, especially when you drop them for your warriors, as soon as they charge an enemy they are now out of range.

My suggestion to fix this, would be to sort of reinstate the effect like before, but keep it as a buff.
So when you are in range of the totem, you get a refreshing Buff for 10seconds of Windfury / Flametongue. Kind of like how it worked before but not an Imbue.

I would also suggest that once you drop grace of air totem , it overwrites the Windfury buff, so not to bring back the Totem Twisting mechanic that alot of Shamans hated.

Thanks for reading.

In PvP back in TBC that was very well known tactic to drop your Windfury Totem to buff your warrior before he charges/intercepts so that he can retain the buff for the mentioned 10 sec. I am not sure if it was 10 sec though, I think it was 6 but I can't remember.

For PvE you would switch WF with other totems to keep the buff-up all the time.

Back in TBC totems were working as an aura. So for example they also worked through obstacles. If Warrior and Shaman were separated by an object, Shaman could still save warrior by dropping Grounding Totem if Warrior was in range. I don't know if that works here that way but I did a lot of analysis a few years ago proving it should work like that.
