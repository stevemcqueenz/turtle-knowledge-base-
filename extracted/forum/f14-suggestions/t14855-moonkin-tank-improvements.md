# Moonkin tank improvements

## Post 101221 by Norovirus — 2024-09-13T17:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101221#p101221 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Moonkin tank is a meme but fun for dungeons and small group open world stuff. 2 changes I'd like to see:

 1. increased threat in moonkin form. Make it a toggleable buff like omen of clarity or tied to thorns so that dps moonkin can avoid the effect (ie. 30% increased threat in moonkin form while thorns is active). Could just make it a separate static talent but I'd prefer something toggleable like shamans have rockbiter weapon.

 2. Give moonkin something in the furor talent so it doesn't feel so bad to have to burn a ton of mana to shift into bear form for a taunt or demo roar, then shift back. Maybe a few seconds of no cast time pushback so you can get a cast off. or reduced mana cost of next ability. or you can temporarily cast a healing spell despite being in moonkin form so that you can reapply your own rejuv/regrowth. Or even just gives you some of your mana back that you spent on casting moonkin form
