General things that bother me as a Horde mage in pvp/wpvp and battlegrounds

Mage sheep:
This powerfull spell is the most tilting part of playing mage. Besides the fact that horde players break sheeps all the time, the  shaman "searing totem" will too often break them too.
"Diminishing returns means I can sheep once, wait out the duration, sheep second time, wont last very long, sheep third time, lasts maybe a second or two and BAM, no more sheeping that target. If it was even necessary for a third fear, as in a 1v1 duel, your dead before second fear ends. Sure, Warlocks are the 1v1 champions, and I would'nt want to mess with inequalities too much. Mages usually beat warriors and rogues, but lose to druids, warlocks, priests, and should technically be able to deadzone hunter to death. Shamans are just around impossible to beat even with gear advantage.
I will get back to palladins, as I have a lot to say about them, but for now, to beat a palladin, I would start with a frost bolt rank 1. bait out their freedom of movement, sheep, they trinket out of it, sheep, they bubble out of it, start running... wait for freedom of movement to be off, now do dmg. Unfortunately palladins have a speed buff, so running away isnt very easy, you gotta save blink for their stun spell. So in conclusion, maybe do something about the diminishing returns of sheep.

Palladins in battlegrounds:
Having bubble to spin flags in arathi basin is really an issue that should be adressed. they can litterally spin it forever if they just take turns to bubble. After the patch with the damage reduction in BG's theres litterally no way to burst them down. WSG is extremely annoying to play, usually its 4 out of 10 players being palladins, and freedom of movement plus bubbles makes "schoolbuss" running a flag almost impossible to beat. Please consider changing how bubbles work in battlegrounds, this is busted. Changes suggested are making it impossible to spin flags (hitting someone while they are trying to cap a flag) while being bubbled, or making the bubble either breakable in battlegrounds, or at the very least shortening its duration. This is a problem most horde players are complaining about regardless of their own class.
So you mean to tell me that this class can two shot me while Im wearing couple of t3 pieces, 3 warlord pieces and stacking stamina on all enchants, while this palladin is using t1 + t2 gear, just cause he is retribution? He can bubble, Heal, freedom of movement, break sheeps, be unsheepable, run faster, AND TWO SHOT ME? either be a damage dealer, and have shit heals, or be a tank and do less dmg. Healer paladins spamming flash heals are only dealt with after they run out of mana, and by then, the AB is already lost.
Another solution to palladin bubbles would be to give warriors (who have had many nerfs) a "shattering throw" talent in arms tree, to break palladin bubbles.

Melee Hunters:
If you dont perfectly deadzone them, they do too much dmg. So cant blink away, cant deadzone, cant be too far away and cant sheep due to pushback. This is a bit unbalanced imo.

Druids:
Their tankyness while bear form self healing is overpowered. As a mage, I cant sheep a druid flag carry, I cant slow much, only thing for me to do is spam rank 1 frostbolt and hope someone else comes to aid. I remember starting out with poor gear, a blue geard druid could do moofire + Insect swarm ment I was either dead already, or had to run away and eat, thats 2 global cd's. Now in good gear (not bis) I still have trouble dealing with a druid in full blues duing caster build. druid cat form dmg seems overpowered too. Druids need a nerf in my opinion.

Rogues:
The poison build goes through armor, its not really a pvp build, but the dmg is top on all raids and is completely broken, we are all waiting for the nerf, but wondering how long it will take before devs wake up to this.
Rogue using "freedom of action" potion will absolutely kill a mage before the sheep gets out, and even so, its just a trinket use and your in the graveyard. Nothing that needs changes, just a mention.

Im sure you know how strong shamans are, and so they have been nerfed several times, but I have trouble recalling palladin nerfs, so its often joked about as "the devs all play paladins".

As a mage, you are the squishiest, glass cannon on the battlegrounds, focused by all. Sheeps are needed on healers, but almost impossible to use on palladins on the backline without dying. Jumping in, counterspelling one pally healer, then trying to sheep another means you die, but might help everyone else to push on your team for a push.

Alterac Valley.
So you mean to tell me that alliance can more or less run all the way to Drek'tar without pulling many NPC mobs, while horde can barely take the first gy without being swarmed? Alliance base is usually where the most time is spent, meanwhile 10 ppl can get horde sides last graveyard, while the remaining 30 just hold aid station, with a gazillion NPC's usually upgraded to elite guards withing 20 mins of play... something is off. Still its 50/50 who wins, so maybe not change anything... If I was to suggest a change, it would be to simply have a look at a few AV's and noticing the difference in NPC guards and removing or making some of the quest NPC's not targetable or lesser health. AV is unbalanced to alliance side, no doubt.

Tel'abim horde queue time
I had a alliance alt (named "Avtime") yelling on world chat all the time, asking alliance to "queue please", they have instant queue, while horde waited sometimes for hours, taking turns on battleground pops.

A solution to the queue time of horde vs alliance, and palladins with their bubbles, could be to limit amount of same class players to join battlegrounds. Having 6 rogues on horde side vs 5 palladins on alliance side in most of your BG's is truly exhausting. This would probably kill queue time even more on horde side, but just throwing ideas out there.

I am very much against cross realm pvp, but getting to the point where I would rather see cross faction (horde+alliance) battlegrounds extended from being only in bloodring to also include WSG/AB/SGV.

Another idea is to make it possible to choose a queue that allows cross faction. This needs some sort of equalizer, as some will queue 24/7 and there is other issues like honor gain.

The waiting for queue to pop on horde side was probably the most frustrating part of grinding to 14. And I hope there can be a solution for this down the road.
