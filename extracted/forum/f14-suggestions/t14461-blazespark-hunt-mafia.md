This particular item has very weak stats and is only effective with BoED. However, the use of elemental damage has a broader meaning. Also there are stronger items with moderate stats in the game. Most of all elemental items are represented by two spells:

 1) <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=16615>
 2) <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=7711>
There may be others, but I find it difficult to name them.

Elemental damage proc has synergy with some abilities and talents. The most effective use of elemental damage out of  BoED context i see is paladin.  Each Seal of Righteousness auto attack also produces elemental damage. That is, the number of sources of elemental damage for a paladin is doubled. Each elemental attack on a target with Seal of Wisdom judge has a chance to replenish mana. The same chances as a normal auto attack. This usage of Seal Of Wisdom judge with a combination of Seal of Righteousness and elemental damage sources allows to replenish mana very quickly.  I often swap  weapon to <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81018> + <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=65030> in combat to restore mana fast.

Moreover, the above elemental damage effects are of combined origin. They are a physical school with elemental (electric or fire) damage. The presence of one or two elemental items allows us to have 100% uptime of one of the strongest talents in the game: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=20059> (Vengence)

Also not so strong but not bad talent of shaman (elemental devastation) <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=29180> can activated by elemental damage

I think i don't know all the information so there is probably something interesting still present that has not been revealed
