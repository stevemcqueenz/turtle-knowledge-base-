# New classes, new specs

## Post 127710 by Allarielle — 2025-04-20T17:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127710#p127710 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey hey! So while I've always loved what TWoW is doing, my biggest complaint is that the class scene is too stale. The vanilla wow classes are great, but always felt too limited in scope for me even back in the day. I'd like to see a couple of things to address this!

First of all, the classes that were effectively cut from vanilla - Death Knight, Demon Hunter, and Runemaster. For this, my idea is not to go the retail route, but actually to create an entirely new setup.

Runemaster would be a new class for both factions: Human, High Elf, Orc, Tauren. It would have a focus on very fast paced melee gameplay that balances melee combos with powerful triggered effects. Its specs could be: Cleric (focus on adding some passive healing/support), Monk (focus on going hand-to-hand, grapples, and shrugging off attacks - a control and mobility heavy tank), and Inscription (focus on triggered effects and battlefield control).

Death Knight and Demon Hunter would be new faction-specific classes. DK would be available to Orcs in the tradition of Ner'Zhul, but... reformed... and Forsaken in the tradition of Arthas, but with free will. DH would be available to Night Elves and High Elves both trained in the same ancient traditions that gave us Illidan but maybe with little less crazy. Both would be melee classes with tank specs and some powerful ranged cooldowns, but with their own unique flavours. This would help give each of the factions a bit more of their own identity, and delve a tonne deeper into their unique histories!

The second thing I'd like to see would be new specs added to all of the classes. IE, every class has four or five specs instead of three. These new specs would feature their own trees as well as their own new baseline skills, and would be focused on broadening the concept of the classes rather than simply on adding new specs to the meta. For example, a Druid spec focused on thorns, entangling roots, and summoning treants, bringing home the fantasy of the Keeper of the Grove or of a Quillboar Thornspeaker instead of the lunar focus that Balance has. Another idea could be adding a battlefield medic spec for warriors that uses bandages, splints, and rage-fueled blood injections to create a unique and flavourful healing gameplay.

A way to make these added specs even cooler would be to make it so you have to complete a unique condition or questline to unlock them, adding even more flavour.

Of course I realise that either of these ideas would be an astronomical amount of work even before balancing efforts begin, but I really feel like there is a huge amount of room for improvement in this area. The vanilla lineup was great, but it was also really limited.
