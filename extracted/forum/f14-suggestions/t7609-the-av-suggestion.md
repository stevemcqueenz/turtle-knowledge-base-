# The AV Suggestion.

## Post 48685 by Chronoslicer — 2023-06-03T12:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48685#p48685 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Active towers and bunkers give an aura to all players.  Those that kill close within their radius will get 150-300% more honor per kill, whichever is reasonable. On top of this, Dun baldar/Frostwolf hold Captains/Generals get a "fortified buff", making them nigh impossible to kill without getting the towers/bunkers down. My reasoning is to dissuade back door/CC rushing and encourage players killing the opposing force and destroying their fortifications to achieve victory.
