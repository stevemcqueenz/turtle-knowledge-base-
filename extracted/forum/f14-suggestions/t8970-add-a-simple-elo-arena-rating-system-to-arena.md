## Post 57987 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-08-19T01:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57987#p57987 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yeah I feel ya on the desire to have less pubstomps and more sweat games with all 4 players being sweatbros. It's good fun when i face equals.

It would probably increase population as fresh 60s are better able to. Currently their only saving grace is occasionally queueing alongside a solo queueing sweat who can carry. Duoqueuing is reducing chances of this and removing duo queueing could fix this but cause the opposite effect, where upper players end up annoyed at the RNG of rolling the dice to be paired with a pro bro or stuck with a noob/fresh.

With ratings, there is the obvious downside of increased queue times, as you would have a system to wait X amount of time to pair within close rating, 2x time to pair with med range, and 3x time to pair any. This generally slows down queues and the main beautiful thing with blood ring right now is the super fast turnaround. Could be even faster if they add infinite mana during prep and reduce the time 60s -> 30s.

Whatever the case, there's no "just offer both rated and skirmish" solution. this would either fatally divide the blood ring pop in half, or you end up with 1 queue dead and another fully alive.

And I'm assuming this could be added with 0 rewards for rating, the only reward being that you get better matchups. If you add rewards like rating locked gear, mounts and titles, you get the absolute clown degeneracy we see on tbc and wotlk classic right now with addons that assist with queue dodging to ensure you never queue into counter comps and even only queue into comps you counter.... pure garbage.... So yeah, rated arenas would be fine, rewards are not.
