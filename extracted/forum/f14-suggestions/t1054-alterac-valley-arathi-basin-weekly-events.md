Greetings Turtles, I would like to suggest something which could be one of the first steps in `fixing` pvp on the server.
I would suggest having a weekly AV or AB, depending on the current population and attitude of players every Friday.
Similar to rading which always starts at 18:00 server time, I think we should set a time for this `event` as well.
Now unofficially Tuesday is pvp-day, as I like to call it, since the honor reset is on Wednesdays. We all want some other BGs being active, even after raids we all want to do AB or AV but always end up in WSG. Now wsg might be the `meta` bg for Ranking up, but it does not mean it should be the only bg players should queue for. We just need to come to an agreement ` ok now its time for some AB/AV`. I would suggest Friday 20:00 server time, since after raids it might be a bit too late for me personally. That is why I would like to read your Feedback regarding this topic. What time is suitable for you, which day, and hopefully we can make this happen. Some want reputation, some want, honor and glory, others the salt :D. So what do you say Turtles?
