Personally, daily quests just burn me out because they make me feel like I have to do the same repetitive thing every day.
A quest to do damage to a combat dummy under a certain time limit isn't inherently a bad idea.  Could be a series of quests, where each subsequent quest requires you to deal more damage with less time.  They could reward you with some sort of silly novelty brag item, or like a weapon/piece of armor with no stats on it but it has a cool look so you can transmog your items with it.  I'm kind of curious how the game would track you dealing damage to a dummy, though.
Ultimately, if you want to have quests that challenge players to play as best as possible, why not quests that challenge you to complete various dungeons in a certain amount of time?  That's something that more than just DPS builds can participate in, and there's already a couple quests like that in Vanilla, so it's not that outlandish. Just throwing an idea out there into the mix.
And as for criticism/rejection/rudeness, don't let it get to you.
