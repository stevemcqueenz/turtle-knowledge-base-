# [Request] Add Upright Orcs & Trolls for More Character Diversity!

## Post 156243 by Dyzium — 2025-10-20T01:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156243#p156243 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Why Upright Orcs Fit in WoW Classic**:

Orcs in Warcraft have a rich, tragic history, heavily influenced by the Fel magic of the Burning Legion. Their hunched posture is a direct result of that corruption. However, as the Orcs of WoW Classic continue to rebuild and recover, an option for upright Orcs would reflect their physical and spiritual recovery from the Fel’s grip—without losing the core identity of the race.

 **1. Symbolizing Recovery from the Fel:**
The hunch is a mark of the Orcs’ painful past, but in WoW Classic, Orcs are no longer fully defined by the Fel. Offering upright models would represent their healing, as they reclaim their strength and dignity, aligning with the race’s ongoing recovery. It’s a visual evolution that fits within the Classic timeline.

 **2. More Customization for Players:**
The upright option wouldn’t replace the classic hunched Orcs—it would simply give players a choice. Some might prefer the primal, hunched aesthetic; others might want to play a noble, powerful Orc leader, like Thrall. Both options would coexist, offering more freedom without disrupting the core experience.

 **3. Aligning with Warcraft Lore:**
Icons like Thrall are depicted standing tall, symbolizing leadership and redemption. Upright Orcs would allow players to mirror these heroic figures, showing that Orcs can evolve beyond their corrupted past into proud, heroic figures—perfect for roleplaying a noble warrior or leader.

 **Conclusion:**
Upright Orcs would reflect the Orcs’ recovery and strength, enhancing the WoW Classic experience without changing their core identity. It’s a simple, meaningful addition that respects their lore while giving players more ways to express their character’s journey. I understand that implementing such a change might take some time, and I’m not sure of the complexities involved. However, if it's feasible, I’d love to see this option included in the game! The addition would truly elevate the experience for those of us who want to explore new ways of representing our Orc characters.

 **Cons of Upright Orcs**:

 **Lore Inconsistency:**
Some players may argue that the Orcs’ hunched posture is a vital part of their identity, deeply tied to their corruption by the Fel and their cultural history. An upright model might feel like a deviation from that painful yet defining aspect of Orcs.

 **Cultural Integrity:**
For some, the hunched posture signifies the primal struggle and brutality of the Orcs, reflecting their raw nature before their eventual redemption. Changing this might feel like an alteration of an iconic aspect of the Orcs’ design.

 ---

 **Why Upright Trolls Fit in WoW Classic**:

Trolls in Warcraft have a rich, ancient history. Their hunched posture has long been a symbol of their primal nature, reflecting their connection to the wild and their tribal culture. However, as Trolls evolve in WoW Classic, offering upright models would highlight their strength and leadership without abandoning their core identity. It’s a way to show their growth while respecting their deep lore.

 **Lore & Context:**
Trolls like Vol'jin and Zul'jin are often depicted standing tall in Warcraft lore, symbolizing their power and leadership. While their tribes are rooted in primal traditions, many Troll leaders are portrayed upright, showing that they’re more than just savage warriors. Introducing an upright option would give players the chance to embody these heroic figures while still honoring the Trolls’ savage, tribal roots.

 **Pros of Upright Trolls in WoW Classic**:

 **Alignment with Troll Leadership:**
Legendary figures like Vol’jin and Zul’jin are often depicted standing tall, exuding authority and wisdom. Offering upright Trolls would give players the option to represent these heroic leaders, emphasizing the strength, dignity, and wisdom of the Trolls beyond their wild, primal appearance.

 **More Customization:**
An upright option adds more variety to Troll designs without removing the classic hunched posture. Players could choose to keep the traditional, primal look or opt for a taller, more regal Troll that fits their vision—whether it’s a battle-hardened warrior or a wise tribal leader. This added customization deepens the experience.

 **Reflecting the Evolution of Trolls:**
Trolls have proven they can evolve beyond their savage beginnings. From the Darkspear tribe’s rise to prominence to the struggles of the Amani, the upright Troll would reflect the race’s potential for growth and adaptation. This visual change speaks to the idea of Trolls adapting to modern challenges while staying true to their roots.

 **Improved Roleplay Opportunities:**
For roleplayers, upright Trolls would open up new ways to portray their characters. Whether as fierce tribal leaders or proud warriors, an upright posture conveys strength and respect, allowing for deeper immersion and more dynamic storytelling.

 **Cons of Upright Trolls**:

 **Lore Consistency:**
While many Troll leaders are depicted upright, some players may feel the hunched posture represents the raw, primal nature of Trolls best, especially for tribes like the Amani. Introducing upright Trolls should be an option, not a replacement, to avoid conflicting with these tribal identities.

 **Not Every Troll Should Be Upright:**
Some tribes, like the more savage Amani or Gurubashi, may feel more at home with the hunched look. Keeping both options ensures that the race remains diverse and authentic to its varied cultures.

 ---

 **Conclusion:**
Upright Trolls would add diversity and customization while staying true to the race’s lore. They would give players the ability to reflect the leadership and evolution of the Trolls—allowing them to portray characters that embody the heroism and wisdom of figures like Vol’jin and Zul’jin, without diminishing the savage, primal roots of the race. This is a simple addition that would enrich the WoW Classic experience, offering more choices without disrupting the spirit of the game. I understand that adding new models is no easy task, so if this is something the team can consider in the future, I’d love to see it implemented in a way that respects both the lore and player preferences.
