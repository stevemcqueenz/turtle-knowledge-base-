# BoP trash loot tradeable in raids

## Post 167842 by Lane13 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-09T21:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167842#p167842 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

In raids, players can trade an item to someone else once when they receive it but only from bosses. Trash mob loot is not included but it should, as this causes confusion and is often forgotten due to it being the only exception.

Loot from trash is often desirable and being able to have the option to fix a mistake would be appreciated.
