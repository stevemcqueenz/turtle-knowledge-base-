I would like to request the possibility of a **Faction Change / Transfer in the PVP realms**. I understand the concern in regards to Faction Balance and would like to add a conditional:

 **You must have a lvl 60 char in the opposing faction first.**
If you have a lvl 60 char on the other faction, than you should be able to transfer to that faction given you can't play 2 chars at the same time. Having a lvl 60 in the opposing faction means there won't be an imbalance given the already existence of such character (it's just like adding another toon to a faction).

Could we have this available please?
