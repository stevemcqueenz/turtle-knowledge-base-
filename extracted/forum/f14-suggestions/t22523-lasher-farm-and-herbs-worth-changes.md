Solofarming a specific part of a dungeon meant for 5 man parties should never have been a widespread and one of the most profitable ways to make gold in the game.

It is specific combination of circumstances (e.g. Herbs being more in demand due to more raisers, vastly more players in the world than the 1-2k concurrent peak players that most servers back in actual classic, and adjustments blizz and twow have both made since its original introduction in 2005 to droprates (hello black lotus), recepies, class abilities, convenience items (vendor & post box available anytime and 36 slot bags) as well as gear that can make this farm ever more efficient that have placed lashergrinding among the best spots to farm.

Dont get me wrong, i like having the option to just farm my stuff regardless of other players on the server filling the same farmspot and have my own slice of the world i can farm whenever i want, but a method so completely agnostic of the world in a MMO should never be as ridiculously good as lasherfarm has been.
