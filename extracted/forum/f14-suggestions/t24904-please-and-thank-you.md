Dear TurtleWow,
First off, I have very much enjoyed playing your server. Thank you for all the effort your team has poured into the development of the server. The changes and additions have presented a refreshing experience of a game I practically had memorized and allowed me to re-experience the game with the same wonder and excitement I felt many years ago when I was just a child. I know that I am not alone in this sentiment. Seeing that this server will come to an end is saddening, as its quality and content stood miles above other servers and even above the work of blizzard themselves. The love that the developers had for this game really shows through in the new zones, dungeons and quests. As players, we should all feel grateful to have been able to play and experience this server. Was it perfect? No. But it was fun and unique. Thank you all who developed and worked on this server. Thank you for even staying up as long as you have and thank you for the best version of warcraft that has ever existed.

I know for one, I will not be returning to retail or any version of wow that blizzard produces. I know that nothing blizzard makes will come close in quality to that of TurtleWow. Just look at SOD, it was a half-assed attempt at classic+. I remember being very excited when they announced SOD to the point where I considered purchasing it, but upon watching videos of the lackluster changes I decided against it as it was not what I envisioned a classic+ server to be. It was during this time that I had found out about TurtleWow. I was watching videos of SOD, and a related video had appeared that compared SOD against TurtleWow. I was instantly intrigued and decided to give it a try. And, as it turns out, TurtleWow was and still is everything I had envisioned a classic+ server to be.

I regret not being able to ever reach level 60 and get to experience all of the new dungeons, zones, quests, etc. My personal life has gotten too hectic with school, work, kids, etc. and I get to play turtle wow at-most an hour per week (most of the time even less, I like spending time with my children and wife and playing wow is the last item on my list of desires, but it's still there). My max level character is around level 34, but i have leveled and lost so many HC+Slow Steady characters.

Now, to my request... Although I know it will likely not be fulfilled I hold onto some slim hope that Torta will allow this. I would absolutely love to have a version of TurtleWow that runs on a local server so that me, my friends, and my father can all play together when they come over to my house. My father and I used to play wow together all the time when I was a kid, and recently he has expressed an interest in playing it together again. I would love to be able to play once again with my father, but on TurtleWow so that he can re-experience the game with the same wonder and excitement as I have. Please, if you could, can we make this happen? Thank you Again TurtleWow.
