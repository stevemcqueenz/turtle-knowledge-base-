# Dwarf Shaman / Undead Paladin

## Post 142214 by Longus — 2025-07-30T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142214#p142214 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
I heard Turtle team doesn't want to go in that direction, but I will say my idea anyway.

I was always an admirer and fan of Vanilla Wow. When I first heard of it and the fact there were shaman only in the Horde and paladin only in the Alliance, it felt right and awesome. I have spent several hundreds of hours exploring, leveling and learning the lore. I always like these kind of distinction and ''unbalances' in the good old version of the game.

But Nordanaar is cross-faction. There can already be shamans in mostly alliance groups and paladins in mostly horde groups. In fact, most of the guild run content with all classes. The classes are already a melting pot. The vast majority of leveling dungeons are in cross-faction groups. If you don't keep the factions and classes distinct, why limit the possibilities if it can be lore accurate?

Dwarf shaman is already established in the lore. The Wildhammers are allies of the Alliance. Players can already customize their dwarves as Wildhammers. Turtle is even going to release dwarf shaman inspired items in 1.18, such as the Storm hammer.
Undead paladin, I always tought it wasn't right. It doesn't fit in the lore. But then I took the time to think about it. If there are undead HOLY priests, why wouldn't there be undead paladins? There are already undeads out there who can weild the Light to heal and support. Paladins are priests who took up weapons and heavy armors for combat. Undead priests are reanimated human priests. Why woudln't there be reanimated undead paladins?

I think Nordanaar is a place where these class combos should exist while Tel'Abim should remain with these faction/classes distinction since no cross-faction. I'm sure Turtle would find an awesome way to implement these in the world. Put Wildhammer dwarf shaman trainers in Ironforge and Aerie Peek. Have quest series with the Wildhammers. Have undead paladins trainers in the Undercity with quests in the Scarlet Monastery. These combos are a very popular idea. Turtle is Vanilla +, there are already vanilla basics that are behind and changed with this version of the game. I am convinced that the Turtle universe is the right place where this idea could really flourish.

I just don’t think Undead Paladins make sense, especially when you look at the lore. The Forsaken aren’t just “dead humans”—they’re beings literally cut off from life and the warmth of the Light. Even the few Forsaken who do use the Light, like priests, describe it as absolute torture every time they cast a healing spell. It’s not just uncomfortable—it hurts them.

Now imagine what that would mean for a Paladin, a class built entirely around the Light. Paladins don’t just use the Light—they live by it, they believe in it completely. That kind of faith is what gives the Light its power. And it’s hard to believe that a tortured, bitter soul like most Forsaken could genuinely hold that kind of pure conviction.

Letting Forsaken be Paladins kind of breaks the world’s internal logic. Not every race needs access to every class. Part of what makes the different races unique is how they connect to magic, faith, and power. Forsaken Paladins feel like they’d exist just to check a box, not because the story supports it. And canonically all undead priest are shadow priest. They just get the other specs for gameplay reasons.
