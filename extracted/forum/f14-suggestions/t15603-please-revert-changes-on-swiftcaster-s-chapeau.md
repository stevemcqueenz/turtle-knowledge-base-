# Please revert changes on Swiftcaster's Chapeau

## Post 106567 by Ellifta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-05T17:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106567#p106567 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Swiftcaster's Chapeau was unique amongst other headslot items that shared the same model, it didn't hide player character's hair and allowed for unique looks. The red one from Scarlet Monastry and White one from Nefarion already hides player hair, so if one wishes for a hat that hides their hair they can opt for that. Now unfortunately with 1.7.2 it hides the hair of player character, a change that I'm sure no one asked for.

A lot of people had transmogged their helmets into this because they enjoyed the way their hair looked with the military style cap. People I've talked to who have this hat as their transmog are deeply upset by this change. I'm kindly asking developers to revert the cosmetic change on this hat, as it truly allowed unique styles to flourish. Here's a before 1.7.2 and after 1.7.2 comparison for those who didn't know.

I'm more concerned with this cosmetic change than I am for class changes. Look at how soulless, vague, bland and generic it looks post patch   crying_turtle

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/TvszRTm/chapeau.png)
