# Extend LFT tanking options please

## Post 123836 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-25T22:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123836#p123836 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title says.
Many classes (specially in the lategame) can tank now, it just makes things easier for everyone.
From a selfish POV I really want to level my hunter (maybe even my rogue) as a tank but without LFT it's hard. World chat just LOLs when you wanna grab people around you as hunter tank.

Late game warlocks are def capable of tanking but I'm not sure from which level range, soul link is probably required for mitigation though.
Rogues, if heavily geared for stam might also be able if threat poison level requirement is reduced. It makes no sense capping it to lvl 60.
Hunters are a weird case. They have a decent mitigation and default threat and very good crowd control but they are lacking a threat modifier. Snapping some extra threat for aspect of the monkey is something which wouldn't really hurt the gameplay anyway but would enable something cool.
Maybe even priests can tank now, I don't know about them.

Would make LFT faster and would make it much more fun, even if more runs would end in a disaster, specially during a learning curve.
The request is mostly selfish, I love tanking dungeons, I hate DPSing dungeons and I really wanna level my hunter and rogue in dungeons.
