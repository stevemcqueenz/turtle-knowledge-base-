# Two buffs (elemental) shamans need for pvp

## Post 168026 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-12T08:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168026#p168026 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As 18.1 comes closer and class changes are finalized, I want to raise two past changes to the shaman class that are not balanced for the current BG meta. i.e., they underperform.

 1) Purge needs it's 8 sec cd removed. Adding this cd was a big mistake. Purge is by design the counter to Paladin's many spot buffs and one of the most important support spells in the shaman toolbox. However, currently the inability to 'spam' Purge renders it basically useless. You never 'get' to the important buffs - mage frost shields, priest shields, blessing of freedom, etc. This is a significant buff to the Alliance, and counter to vanilla pvp design.

I've understood that there were many complaints about Purge in the 17.2 bg meta on Nordanaar - however, you cannot make such significant changes based on the performance on a single server where the pvp population is severely skewered in terms of class representation. You need to look at faction pvp performance on all three servers, and assume an even spread of classes in BGs. In addition, we are now in 18.0 and approaching 18.1, and the meta has changed significantly since then, particularly due to Dampening. This means you need to revisit and reevaluate legacy nerfs such as this.

 2) Nature's Swiftness needs to be un-nerfed. Dampening already controls ele/resto burst significantly. The current nerfed version, Ancestral Swiftness, is underperforming: Take Dampening damage reduction of 20%, meaning that spells are cast at 80% of power. Multiply that by 0.75 (Ancestral Swiftness) and the chosen instant spell hits at 60% of base damage. For a 3 minute cd and the players' chosen 51st talent (meaning they sacrifice e.g. Earthquake), this is very underperforming.

I fundamentally do not understand why this nerf was necessary. Ele/resto (30/0/21) was a strong spec in CC1, however since CC2 and onwards there are much stronger and problematic shaman pvp specs that needs addressing.

For that matter, other caster classes that twow has introduced, such as Smite priests and caster druids, regularly outperform caster shamans in pvp. They need urgent addressing.

Thank you.
