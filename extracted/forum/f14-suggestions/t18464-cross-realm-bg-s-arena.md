# CROSS-REALM BG's/Arena

## Post 125980 by Nkarie — 2025-04-08T12:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125980#p125980 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!
While talking to many PvP players on both realms (Nordanaar and Tel'Abim), I’ve noticed that the biggest issue in PvP is the poor battleground queueing and near-zero Arena activity. At level 60 on Nordanaar, we get about 10–20 BG regs per day, while on Tel'Abim, it’s even worse—less than 10. The Blood Ring Arena on Nordanaar (level 60) doesn’t reg at all.

For comparison, I will give an example of the number of  Alterac Valley(AV) on both realms.
Nordanaar 10-15 per day
Tel'Abim 5-10 per MONTH

Because of this, I’d like to ask our beloved Turtle WoW developers to consider implementing cross-realm BG queueing for both realms. Similar systems already exist on other private servers like Warmane, Sirus, WoWCircle, etc.

Personally, I originally planned to play on the PvP realm, but when I compared the populations—7k(10k now) on Nordanaar vs. 700(1500 now) on Tel'Abim—the choice became obvious, and I went with PvE. However, if cross-realm BGs were realisating, I would’ve definitely chosen the PvP server despite the population. I’m sure this change would increase the PvP realm’s population and improve BG quality on the PvE realm.

What do you guys think? Share your opinions!
Best regards.
