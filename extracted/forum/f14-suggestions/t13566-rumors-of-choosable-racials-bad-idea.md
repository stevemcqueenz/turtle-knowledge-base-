Sir Zeliek is only able to cast paladin abilities because he's raised by a necromancer and acting against his will.

The distinction between priest and Paladin in WoW is specifically the fact that Priests wield the light mechanically, but in lore, the source of the power varies from race to race.

Humans, High Elf and Dwarf priests draw their powers from the Holy Light.
Night Elf priests draw their powers from Elune/the Moon
Troll priests draw their powers from the voodoo/Loa
Forsaken priests are former Holy Light priest and draw their powers from a corruption/reinterpretation of the Holy Light faith that revolves around shadows.

Paladins, on the other hand, in WC3 and Vanilla lore, are specifically a branch of the teachings of the Holy Light that specifically WEAPONIZES the Holy Light against the enemies of the Alliance in WC2 by upholding the values and chivalry of the Church of the Holy Light and the Alliance, as well as to oppose the Horde's original Death Knights and Fel magic. Paladins are, for lore purposes, a faction-exclusive class formed specifically to FIGHT the Horde and undeath/Fel. You can't have a "culturally reskinned paladin" because at that point it's no longer a Paladin. Paladins aren't just "priests with hammers and plate armor" because their vows go even deeper than Priests of the Holy Light does. A Paladin will canonically lose their ability to wield the Light if they  WILLINGLY and KNOWINGLY break their oaths and WILLINGLY and KNOWINGLY do evil, and because unlike priests of the Light, paladins weaponize the Light, the Paladin's use of the Holy Light is more lethal than a priest's use of the Holy Light.

Even when the Forsaken broke free of the Scourge, the Curse of Undeath has canonically corrupted and distorted the Forsaken's mind so that almost all of them are sinister, immoral people. They're estranged from the Holy Light and the curse of Undeath's effect on the Forsaken's minds will have made them already go against the tenets of the Church of the Holy Light.

The undead Paladin in Naxxramas has no free will and is forced to obey the necromancer whom raised him.
