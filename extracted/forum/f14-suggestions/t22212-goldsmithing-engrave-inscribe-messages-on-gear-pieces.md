# [Goldsmithing] Engrave/Inscribe messages on gear pieces

## Post 154160 by Lahire (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-10-03T09:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154160#p154160 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello I'm Lahire, sometimes in game as Laitfraise the mascot owl, and I used to suggest stuff like :
 - A plan to make Hyjal into a 60 strict level zone centered around farming (which is close to what was put in the game) : [viewtopic.php?t=2222](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=2222)
 - Tombstones for dead HC characters (which is now in the game) : [viewtopic.php?t=2149](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=2149)

Right now, the specialisation Goldsmithing of the Jewelcrafting profession isn't appealing, because the other one (Gemology) produces buffs for jewel gear.

This is a problem, and I suggest here a fluff solution which does not disturb the equilibrium of the game.

The new idea I suggest :
to give an appeal to Goldsmithing, let them (and only them) produce a soulbound item (a pencil for example) that, when used on a gear piece, authorizes them to write a short Yellow Message (like some gear pieces already have) signed with their name. They can do it on their own pieces, or pieces shown to them in the Trade Menu (like enchanters).

This strict fluff idea, with no player power attached, would give a RP role to Goldsmithers, a distinct allure to their profession, and a fun new function for all players.

You could send a gift to your friend : a nice piece of gear with a special message attached on it.
Perhaps it is a prank : they open the letter and there is some joke on the gear.
Perhaps it is to celebrate an event : my friend gave me the mats for my first T3 gear, I will inscribe on it "This Mantle was made thanks to [Friend]. Eternal gratitude." or this was the weapon i used to smash Kel'Thuzad and i can write on it "With this steel I broke the Lich".

As the name of who does the inscription/engraving appears, you obtain the same kind of fame as when you make the gear and give it to someone.

This pro-social change would up the interest in a lacking profession and give new RP and social interactions to players.

Thankies yall.   satisfied_turtle
