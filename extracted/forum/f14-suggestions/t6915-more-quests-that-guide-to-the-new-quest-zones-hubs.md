What I mean are quests from major cities that guide me to new zones or quests hubs.

In vanilla there are often npc in certain areas(for example: City Callers,Innkeepers or random citizens in Stormwind or in Iron Forge in the Halls of the Explorers, ) that give you quests that lead you to a new zone.
