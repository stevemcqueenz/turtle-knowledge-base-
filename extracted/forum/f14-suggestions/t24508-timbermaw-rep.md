## Post 172477 by Carlculathor — 2026-03-26T01:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172477#p172477 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

So the new raid will have a requirement to be at least neutral with the Timbermaw Faction in order to just enter, which generally isn't much to ask, however:

Some rogues like me farmed "Pet Rock" from Furbolgs in Azshara long, LONG before raid was announced in any shape or form. Now we're extremely hated with the faction and it would take us about as much time to get to neutral as it would take others to get to revered.

I know it's not a widespread issue and mostly affects only rogues that tried getting the pet rocks, so nothing will come of this most likely, but an option to restart to hostile/unfriendly or something just like you can outside Winterspring for Steamwheedle Cartel faction would be a simple fix that makes the grind reasonable for us again, since we would start from step 1 again like everyone else.

But expecting rogues with this issue to waste 15 or more hours just getting to neutral so we can experience new content is insane. I tried fixing the rep by farming but I barely made a dent even after killing around a thousand and turning in 200 beads. It's demotivating beyond words to experience this after being hyped for the new patch.
