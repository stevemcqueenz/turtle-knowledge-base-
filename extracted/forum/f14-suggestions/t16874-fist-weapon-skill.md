# Fist Weapon Skill

## Post 115319 by Northend — 2025-01-03T14:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115319#p115319 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

TWoW Changed the mechanics of weapon skills in 1.17.2 and added a lot of new equipments such as the Gloves of the Primordial Burrower. It can increase Axes, Swords, Maces and Daggers, **but forget Fist Weapon!!** And in the whole game, in addition to Remnants of an Old God, there is only Ashskin Belt with a very low drop rate to increase Fist Weapon, which makes it difficult to meet the requirements of making weapon skills reach 315 for Fist Weapon. **It's a little unfair to the TWoWers who uses Fist Weapon**.  crying_turtle
I think its necessary to add "**Increased Fist Weapons +6**" for the Gloves of the Primordial Burrower. Also, we can add some new equipments to increase Fist Weapon. I believe its also in line with the dev's original intention to modify weapon skills, allowing everyone to choose a variety of weapons without being limited by other equipments.  turtle_in_love_head
