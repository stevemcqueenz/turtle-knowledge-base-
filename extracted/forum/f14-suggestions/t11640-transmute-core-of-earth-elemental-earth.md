# Transmute core of earth -> elemental earth

## Post 76963 by Jessienyan — 2023-11-11T23:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76963#p76963 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Elemental earth is an extremely contested end game mat used by GNPP, resto pots, and now elemental sharpening stones. This leads to lvl 30 mobs being farmed 24/7. It's frustrating for the lvl 60s who need it, and it's frustrating for the lvl 30s who are trying to do quests in Badlands.

Adding a Transmute Elemental Earth recipe for alchemists would be a good step towards solving this problem. It could be a Friendly rep item for Cenarion Circle. A similar transmute already exists for elemental fire: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20761>
