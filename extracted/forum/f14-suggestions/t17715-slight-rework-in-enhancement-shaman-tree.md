# Slight rework in enhancement shaman tree

## Post 120775 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-23T23:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120775#p120775 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Wanted to make a slight rework in enh tree.
Results are here:
<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/local/od0z4UPFw8>

Changes are by design smaller. Wanted to make enh more focused around the elemental shields.
Also wanted to help with mana problems and the lack of defensive for the tank without greatly reworking anything.

The changes:
Shield spec used to give +blocked amount, here it makes the next popping elemental shield more effective. I find this more in flavor, you can switch between added mana, life or damage based on the shield you use.

Calming winds - added shocks to them. Enh has a really high threat compared to its damage output. Shocks are an inherent part of enh DPS rotation and earth shock still has a 1,5x threat modifier. Adding shocks here makes sense.

Flurry - Gave up 5% haste for making your next shield proc 20% more effective after you crit. More in theme for shamans and adds flexibility based on the shield you use. Can help with fast mana recharge if you are in a water shield phase for example or just raise DPS in lightning shield phase.

Enhancing totems - stoneskin used to add +blocked amount. Swapped it to +block chance after a successful dodge. Shamans dodge relatively consistently. It can be used to raise block chance which in turn makes your elemental shield procs bigger with the new shield spec talent. Would give more internal coherence to the build.

Elemental weapons - Meddled with WF weapon imbue effect. Granting you haste for one strike after missing just feels bad. With these changes, WF extra hits with WF imbue recharge mana. Since WF imbue gives 2 free strikes it's a 6% mana recharge after a proc. Numbers are tweakable but I want a built-in mana recharge mechanic for enh because they seriously lack it.

Bloodlust - This one I seriously reworked. Only 10% haste for only 10 seconds. But gives the same haste to every ally in a 10 yard radius. And for you gives damage reduction and recharges 5% of your mana every second. So you can use it for damage spikes as an enh, for as a defensive as a tank or just generally to recharge mana. Wanted to make the main effect weaker but also wanted to give extra effects so the talent could become very flexible to use.
If the 3 min CD is too much, I'm ok with a 5 min CD for this talent.
