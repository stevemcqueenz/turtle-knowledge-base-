Been lvling a destruction warlock was thinking ![🤔](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f914.svg) about some changes to the talent tree.

 1) Increase the Cataclysm reduction by 3% for each point. So for 5 points there would be a 15% reduction for FIRE spells. Could even be more specific on which type of destruction spells.

For instance just for 3 talent points frost mages get a 15% reduction.

 2) Remove: Shadowburn from the talent tree and let it be a learnable skill at Lvl 20 for all warlocks.

 3) In the place of shadowburn move Conflagrate down to its place.
