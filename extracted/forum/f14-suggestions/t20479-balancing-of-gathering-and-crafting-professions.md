# Balancing of gathering and crafting professions.

## Post 141493 by Allarielle — 2025-07-26T03:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141493#p141493 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Mining feeds into Blacksmithing, Engineering, and Jewelcrafting.
Herbalism only feeds into Alchemy.

My thinking is that a new profession should be added which utilizes herbs, such as an Inscription analogue. A cool power an inscriber might be able to utilize would be the ability to inscribe single-use spells onto scrolls. A scroll of Resurrection, Polymorph, or Summoning could be amazingly fun to have for teams that wouldn't otherwise have those tools, as long as the cooldown is long and the cost is high. Scrolls of Arcane Brilliance or Mark of the Wild could also be nice to have.

I'd also like to see more complex Blue/Purple cooking recipes added that use certain herbs as part of the process. My idea is that you would acquire various cooking implements such as pans, skewers, and steamers (or possibly just a 'chef's toolset'), and then would cook recipes in stages with various sub-components such as sauces being used as ingredients for the final meal.
