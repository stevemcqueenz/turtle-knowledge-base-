# Dungeon Story Mode

## Post 54297 by Naginatasquid — 2023-07-22T12:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54297#p54297 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As someone who doesn't have much time to play the game and prefers turtling Solo most of the time, I always feel like I'm missing out on doing the quests related to dungeons. Whether it's social anxiety, a lack of skill and time commitment to become good enough at the game, long queue times for dungeons, or being matched with party members who want to rush through the dungeon, giving one no time to read through quest texts, there are multiple roadblocks to being able to live through the story of dungeons.
My suggestion therefore, would be some sort of "Story mode" for dungeons, in which enemies are much weaker and able to be beaten by a Solo-player. These wouldn't necessarily be completely trivial, but comparable to the challenge level in the overworld, where it is possible for an average player to beat small groups of same-level enemies or a "boss" like hogger on their own. There would be no drops in these dungeons, so players who want to farm rare items would still need to do a regular group run. But quests related to these dungeons would still be fulfilled by doing the solo run, and enemies would drop their quest items.

Anyone else interested in such an idea or am I the only one actually reading quest texts?

I do not want to play a *single player* game, I enjoy player run economies, RPing with others (although as the server has grown in population, the roleplay part has sadly mostly died out), the occasional PvP, or teaming up with a stranger for a short quest.
