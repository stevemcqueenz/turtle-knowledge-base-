# New gems for old professions

## Post 117894 by Seltsamuel — 2025-01-25T11:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117894#p117894 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the addition of new gem types with the jewel cutter profession, tailoring, leather working and blacksmiths could get new patterns as well to support other play styles than the obvious. While levelling you see plenty feral druids, non-holy paladins and enhancement shamans compared to the trickle of their other specs.
There is plenty item support in dungeon rewards for these go-to specs (like multiple melee plate sets added by Gilneas City and Hateforge Quarry), but hardly any leather or mail that actually has caster/healer stats until you hit level 50+.

With that I propose adding new recipes to the professions using mainly the new gems and of course other trade goods to craft some gear pieces between level 20-45 for them to actually use them. Tailoring has plenty to offer for fire, frost and shadow damage, but arcane mages and balance druids could use some. Leather working could use some arcane, nature and healing gear in that level range and blacksmiths could start offering spell power / healing power mail and plate items in addition to the added plethora of tank and bonk-gear it already has.
