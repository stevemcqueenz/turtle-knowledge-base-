# Add Molten Armor spell to mages

## Post 39211 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-26T21:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39211#p39211 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,
I would like to suggest the addition of the TBC spell to mages in a lvl 60 adapted version.
The point is that mage is still, even though arcane is now an option, one of the more limited classes in playstyle and choices.
Molten Armor would make the choice between Mage Armor (more mana long term) and Molten Armor (more crit) in pve a thing, and between Molten Armor and Ice Armor in pvp and specific situations.

(On a related note: I am also a supporter of adding water elemental, slow, invisibility etc. - in a balanced way of course - somewhere down the line)

Molten Armor seems like an obviously easy to implement ability to start with, which would add a little flavour and finally allow mages to have one shield/Armor spell for all three elements.
Win-win.

Thoughts?
