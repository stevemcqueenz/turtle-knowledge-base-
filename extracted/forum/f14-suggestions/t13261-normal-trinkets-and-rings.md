Currently doing the Vagrand challenge and I feel like the lack of Rings and Trinkets sad. I would propose to add small profession or vendor items that give slight bonuses on White quality items. Stuff like :
Teddy Bear : +1 Spirit
Spiked Ring : +1 Strength
Cheat Sheet : +1 Int

And so on. Small items that can non magically buff a stat or a condition.

Agreed!  Generally feel it would be nice to have all the normal items reworked, more recipes for crafting them, maybe even some small bonuses on some of them to non-combat related things, for some fun and variety when playing this glyph.  Grey gloves that give +5 cooking, legs/chests that give +bandage-healing effect to the wearer only, reduce tent making cooldown etc.; Minor stuff like that could fit.  As it is the glyph feels a little bland.

As far as trinkets go I'd rather see even more minor effects than +1 stats, maybe a trinket that casts temporary light source of a specific spot (for those playing with dark nights mod), or on use let you cook for 1 min without a fire etc.  Better to make these vagrant oriented bonuses aligned with the vagrant playstyle
