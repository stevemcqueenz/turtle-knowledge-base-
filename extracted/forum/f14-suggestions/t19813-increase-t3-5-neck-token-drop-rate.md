# Increase T3.5 Neck Token Drop Rate

## Post 135199 by Biteyou (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-10T23:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135199#p135199 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The current token drop rate for T3.5 neck tokens is atrocious. At the best of luck, you have a statistical chance of getting ONE token drop within 3 lockouts. We have seen 2 in clearing Kara since week 2. The problem is almost every single raider wants a T3.5 neck. At the current rate, our entire raid team will be BIS (besides a neck) and everything will be disenchanted by the time we've even gotten a 4th of our raid neck tokens.

I don't see a valid argument in keeping it at the current rate. I'd much rather it be a 100% drop per off mephistroth, rather than roll the dice for the possibility of multiple neck drops from 5 bosses considering every single person in a raid wants a T3.5 neck. Atleast there is a defined timeline we can allocate neck pieces to. I think it would be even better to make this change and keep the drop rate 10% on other bosses as well, but something is better than nothing.
