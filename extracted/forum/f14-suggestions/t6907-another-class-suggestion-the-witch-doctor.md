# Another class suggestion: The Witch Doctor

## Post 43692 by Tenyar — 2023-04-29T00:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43692#p43692 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Shadowtusk Tribe originated in the lush jungles of Stranglethorn Vale. They were once part of the larger Gurubashi Empire, but after a series of conflicts and disagreements, the Shadowtusk Tribe decided to break away and form their own clan. For years, they lived in isolation, practicing their unique blend of Voodoo Magic and Shamanism, and shunning any contact with other races.

However, as time passed, the Shadowtusk Tribe became disillusioned with the constant infighting and lack of unity among their sister tribes. They believed that the Trolls' disunity was their weakness and that they needed to join forces with other races to achieve their goals. They reached out to Thrall, the Warchief of the Horde, and offered their skills and knowledge in exchange for acceptance and value as allies. The acceptance of the Shadowtusk Tribe into the Horde involved a shift in their mindset, as they began to view cooperation and diplomacy as viable options for achieving their goals. They began to send emissaries to other races, learning about their cultures and forming alliances. At the same time, they intensified their focus on Voodoo Magic and Shamanistic traditions, recognizing the power and importance of these ancient practices.

The Witch Doctor is a hybrid class that combines some elements of both the Warlock and the Priest. They are masters of voodoo and dark magic, but they also have the ability to heal their allies. One unique mechanic on the Witch Doctor is it's Voodoo Doll Ability. It is a powerful tool for the Witch Doctor, allowing them to focus their dark magic on a single target and increase their damage output.

Around level 3 for their class quest, the Witch Doctor will learn the spells [Create Voodoo Doll] and [Voodoo Doll].

[Create Voodoo Doll] will require 2 course thread and produce a voodoo doll reagent, needed to cast [Voodoo Doll].

The Witch Doctor can then cast [Voodoo Doll] on a target, consuming one reagent; which will mark the target similar to a Hunter's [Hunter's Mark], then (and only then) allow the Witch Doctor to cast several other spells on the target.

The Witch Doctor would have three talent trees:

Voodoo Mastery - This talent tree focuses on enhancing the Witch Doctor's ability to deal damage through curses, poisons, and voodoo magic.

Final ability:[Curse of the Loa]
Cast Time: 3.5 Second
Cooldown: 10 Minute
Range: N/A
Mana: 10% Base Mana
Description: Curse the target with the power of a Loa, dealing significant damage over time and reducing their healing received. Additionally, the curse causes the target's attacks and abilities to have a chance to backfire, dealing damage to themselves or their allies. The curse lasts for a significant duration and cannot be dispelled.

Dark Healing - This talent tree focuses on improving the Witch Doctor's ability to heal allies through dark magic. It would include abilities such as Dark Regeneration, Blood Ritual, and Life Drain.

Final ability:[Necrotic Burst]
Cast Time: 3.5 Second
Cooldown: 10 Minute
Range: N/A
Mana: 10% Base Mana
Description: This ability unleashes a burst of dark energy, instantly dealing a large amount of shadow damage to all enemies within 10 yards and afflicting them with a powerful debuff that reduces their healing received by X% for X seconds. Additionally, all allies within 10 yards are instantly healed for a significant amount of health.

Summoning - This talent tree focuses on enhancing the Witch Doctor's ability to summon minions and allies to aid them in battle. It would include abilities such as Summon Zombie, Summon Gargoyle, and Summon Blood Elemental.

Final ability:[Spirit Army]
Cast Time: 3.5 Second
Cooldown: 10 Minute
Range: N/A
Mana: 10% Base Mana
Description: Calls upon the spirits of the dead to aid the Witch Doctor in battle. Summons a group of ghostly warriors to fight by the Witch Doctor's side for 30 seconds. These spirits have increased damage and health compared to other summoned entities, and they also have a chance to inflict fear on enemies with their attacks.

This is an excellent set of skills! Nice work!

Thoughts:
 * Several spells have a range of 40 yards which is rare and also potentially abusable in PvP and open-world combat. Having 1 or 2 status effects that are 40-yard range is fine, but I think there are too many. It makes perfect sense for doing the Voodoo Doll spell, just not the others.
 * I do like the regent and theming for the doll spells! I think it's a cool bit of flavor text to have a few stacks of course thread as regents. There could also be some unique (Tailoring|Leatherworking)/Witchdoctor quests that are only available in the mid-levels (30-45).
 * Hex should be short range (~20 yards). Long-ranged "stun-like" effects are not fun to play against.
