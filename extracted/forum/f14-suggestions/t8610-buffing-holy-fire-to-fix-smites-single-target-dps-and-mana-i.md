# Buffing Holy Fire to fix Smites single target dps and mana issues

## Post 55470 by Apimius (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-01T14:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55470#p55470 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey,

holy fire needs a buff. I am playing smite and i only use holy fire if i want to conserve a bit of mana (not much).

To make holy fire be used in an actual rotation i propose these 2 changes to make this spell worth while:

 - increasing the dots spell power scaling from 5.3% to 13% per tick similar to flameshock and moonfire dot

 - giving the dot (10 second duration) also a holy spell power increasing component which does not stack but overwrites itself if casted again by any player (a + damage value scaling with the priests sp). This would give smite priests a reason to be brought to raids to increase paladins damage. Also it counteracts the nerf to both lIbram of fervor and PvP gloves a bit to where its not such a giga nerf to both ret and smite (loosing about 230 holy spellpower).

## Post 55636 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-08-03T07:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55636#p55636 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't think much needs to be done to "prevent" heal priests from applying the debuff. With a 3.5 seconds base or 3seconds talented casttime for a buff that currently lasts 10 seconds is a heavy commitment for a healing priest. I would argue it hinders a healer in his primary function to the point where a smite priest would be the only one who can realistically provide this buff for his raid.

The dot is not horrible baseline, but scales only 5.3% per tick with spellpower. Making it fall off hard as gear progresses. Buffing it to 13% per tick to at least match flame shock & moonfire dots seems like an easy change. This would give holy fire a total 125% spellpower scaling (up from the current 86.5%) making it definitely worth the casttime. It also leads to an interesting mechanic where you have to choose between letting the last tick of the dot hit vs hitting the target while the last holy fire is still active to harness the increased spelldmg. Giving you some gear dependent gameplay choices.

Note: 125% spellpower scaling is gnarly, but it would be on the back of one of the weaker specs and it is a spell that is almost unusable in pvp and the dot remains dispellable.
