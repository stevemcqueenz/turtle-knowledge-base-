# Make Enh Shaman viable via DW

## Post 85678 by Kendog0013 — 2024-01-23T07:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85678#p85678 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I have come with great displeasure to inform you all that enhance shaman just is not viable at level 60, let alone any of the levelling up stages comparatively to Resto or Elemental shaman.

There is one simple fix to this, and it would be to add dual wield to shamans starting at level 20 like other classes -

as it stands, a lot of weapon effects would 100% be busted with dual wield shaman + windfury/extra attack items...

my proposal is to nerf offhand damage when windfury procs by around 50% when dual wielding to bring it in line to what a medium speed 2h would be. open to many other suggestions to combat the insane power creep as well but this was just a quick and dirty thought of what could be done to balance DW enhance... another suggestion would be to make proc chance only 10% per weapon when dual wielding instead of 20% per weapon

This would still allow high damage, slow swinging 2hs to still shine on enhance if you wanted them to, but also give enhance a much-needed weapon pool increase to choose from in the form of two 1 handed axes, daggers or maces and also adding a little bit of a giggle factor. (even with nerfed damage, seeing 5-6 hits on screen is still amazing feeling)

Open to any feedback, but really, this is a much-needed QoL that twow should've done years ago in some way, shape or form i think. (it is fundamentally a class change; but indeed, it is still a needed class change to make enhance viable or for that matter FUN at all in end game)
