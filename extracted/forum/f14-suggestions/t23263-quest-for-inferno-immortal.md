# Quest for Inferno → Immortal

## Post 162641 by Uldrahc — 2025-12-13T05:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162641#p162641 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**The Problem:**
I have an Inferno/Hardcore level 60 character. It took me 190 hours and over a year of real-time investment. Currently, the population of Hardcore players at level 60 is insufficient to form groups for end-game content without having to wait for hours.

For people that play during "non meta hours" like myself, this creates a "soft-lock" scenario:

 1. There are currently only 2 English speaking raid guilds on hardcore. If you cant make their raid times -> you won't raid.

 2. It seems to be very difficult to find any groups for dungeons, especially when playing at the time of me writing this post (NA night time)

 **The result:**  The character is dead by not being played.

 **The Proposal:**
Implement a one-way "Retirement" quest or service that converts an Inferno/Hardcore character to a Normal/Immortal character. I don't care about the title, I just want to be able to log on and play the game with people.

 **The Logic**

 1. Population vs. Skill: I am not quitting because it’s too hard; I’m stuck because there is no one to play with during the times I play.

 2. **Player Retention:** Currently, my only option is to stop playing the character. This change keeps a level 60 character active in the server economy and raiding pool.

 3. **No Exploits:**  Downgrading difficulty (Inferno → Normal) has no abuse potential. I am voluntarily giving up the prestige/challenge to access the game content normally.

I have technical experience and am willing to assist if needed, but this should effectively be a flag switch via a quest that removes the Inferno Aura and acts as a one-way transfer to the PvE rule set.

Thank you for your time :)

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162680#p162680 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Gearing Help / Exploits:** This creates a negative efficiency loop. Leveling and gearing as Inferno/Hardcore is strictly slower and more restrictive than Normal. Using Inferno as a 'shortcut' to gear a Normal character is mathematically the worst way to play.

Unlike Normal mode where you can carry an under-geared fresh 60, Hardcore guilds enforce strict survival thresholds to mitigate risk. You essentially need better gear just to step inside the raid in Hardcore compared to Normal. (where a guild can carry an alt through a raid if they really need that 1 class) If the character dies 20 times, it won't make a difference. There is no arbitrage opportunity there.

 **Guild Investment:** If a player cannot make raid times, the HC Guild loses that player regardless of whether the character is deleted or converted. The conversion doesn't harm the guild any more than the player simply quitting; it just preserves the character for the server's wider population and lets the player continue their journey.

 **Irrevocable Intent:** The intent of the challenge is survival. The character has survived. The current blocker isn't difficulty or skill; it is logistics. I am effectively soft-locked by a timezone/population bottleneck, not a "gameplay mechanic."

Hope that makes sense :)
