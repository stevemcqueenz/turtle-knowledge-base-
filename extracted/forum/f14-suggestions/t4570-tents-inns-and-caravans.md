# Tents, inns and caravans.

## Post 27321 by Velmerk — 2022-10-15T17:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27321#p27321 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I can't find the post where we was disgusting tent changes. So I'm just putting some suggestions out there.

INNS
Make inns have the function of tents. With a cost for a room, or not. Or give the benches a meal to click on and (you sit and enjoy a hearty meal with other players)  the more people sitting around to bench, the faster your rested will increase. Six players to each bench.
Each inn will be more populated with players at the level of the area the inn is in. A prime chance with find fellow adventurers to group up with and quest together.
Seems silly having to run to goldshire, then sit under three tents stacked up on top of each over (very immersion breaking, please make this not possible.), outside the inn. to increase rested.

Maybe a cook has to make a meal through the the profession.
This would just make everyone keep going to one inn though?

TENTS.
If possible make so they can't be put on roads.
I saw someone suggest they give buffs, like +4 strength +4 Stam (leather belt), ect.
Out in the wilderness you find other players, who are taking a quick break from all the killing, regaining their strength for the next battle.

CARAVANS
New idea i have, to go along with the Traveller Glyph. (No HS, no FP challenge) on the road you find caravans, a place to buy and sell.
If you have to Traveller Glyph an npc can buff you with a small movement speed bonus. Even the pally movespeed talent is enough. 6%?
 5 minutes duration
Falls off in combat.
Only useable on roads in the clients can know when on or off a road.
