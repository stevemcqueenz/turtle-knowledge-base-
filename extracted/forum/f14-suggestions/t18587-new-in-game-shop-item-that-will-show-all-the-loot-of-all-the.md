## Post 126601 by Partyboyza — 2025-04-13T10:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126601#p126601 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm a paladin who aoe farms quite a lot and the biggest issue for me is looting overlapping corpses.

I want to propose to the turtle wow team to add an in game shop item (let's call it AutoLoot-Bot), that will contain all the loot of all the lootable corpses in an 10yd radius.

Not sure if it's possible to implement, but I would certainly pay 10 euros for that quality of life.

In my opinion it doesn't seem game breaking.

Anyone who agrees with this please vote this up to gain more exposure.

Thanks.
