# Paladin Taunt Mechanic Broken

## Post 53317 by Lucidius — 2023-07-13T15:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53317#p53317 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Paladin taunt duration is not log enough, and not matching the taunt length for warriors. Taunt should max your threat on a mob and last for a few seconds.

This is an issue because paladin taunt drops before GCD is even up again and you already lose aggro before you can use another ability. This means paladin just spams taunt over and over and has no chance to gain any additional threat.

The only mitigating factor is to taunt just before a melee swing so that you at least get a little threat, but it's still not enough. Need at least 1gcd so we can use another ability to gain threat on top of the taunt before the effect fades.
