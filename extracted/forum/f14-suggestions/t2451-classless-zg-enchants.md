# Classless ZG Enchants

## Post 15264 by Fizzles — 2022-01-04T02:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15264#p15264 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As of now, ZG idol enchants are currently locked to their specific classes, yet it might be interesting to unlock the ZG idol enchants to not have a class restriction. The main intent of this suggestion is to give a new option to those seeking to improve their specific non-meta or non-raiding specs by having a new set of enchants to optimize from. For example, mages can use the shaman ZG idol enchants to give themselves a larger mana pool for AOE farming. Warriors can go look through the ZG enchants for healing classes to improve their flask sets. It is to also incentivize raiding ZG by making it a new reward to go there. The current reputation requirements should stay in place.

I don't think this suggestion will affect the difficulty of raid bosses as they have higher stat values than on classic and use their abilities instantaneously when not on cooldown. It may unintentionally buff any meta classes, yet it seems to be a slight upgrade for them. An example is when the rogue idol enchant replaces the voracity enchant which warriors use for 8 strength(8 strength converts to 16 melee AP). It is only a 12 attack power increase by using the rogue idol enchant. The addition of extra hit might be a wild card in this situation depending on the class.

Please give your thoughts below.
