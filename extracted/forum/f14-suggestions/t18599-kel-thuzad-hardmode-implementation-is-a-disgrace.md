# Kel'Thuzad Hardmode implementation is a disgrace

## Post 126649 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-13T20:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126649#p126649 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have to vent my frustration about the quality of some in theory awesome additions. On todays episode, the newly implemented Kel'Thuzad hardmode for speedrun competitions.

For those that don't know it yet, as of this Week, Kel'Thuzad has an optional togglable hardmode that just makes P1 adds spawn faster. There is no extra reward for doing it, it just speeds up a phase of the fight that isnt particularly interesting. By increasing the mobs spawn speed, it allows guilds who are familiar and proficient with the fight to shave off 2 more minutes off of their Cleartime.

I have to ask tho, how can such nice feature be implemented in such a lackluster way?
Its an interactable doodad right where any group that enters the room will linger about, that any single in the raid can click, no confirmation window or even info what the doodad interaction does. Just a hardmode that persists trough wipes and can NOT be turned off again.

I get why this has been implemented, but why has this not gotten the time in the oven it deserves? Instead now we have a "grief the raid" button where any person in the raid can brick the ID for everyone else if you are not in one of the more disciplined groups to begin with!

Features like these should always(!) be limited to Raidleaders, especially if they can't be reversed! Why is it that twow always implements good things in the carefully engineered dumbest way possible?  unhappy_turtle_head
