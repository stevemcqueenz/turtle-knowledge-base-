# CRUCIAL Hunter Fixes for 1.8.1 – Now or never

## Post 168335 by ForumGentleman (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-15T20:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168335#p168335 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The 1.8.1 preview is out, and Hunters are once again looking at changes that miss the mark.

Here are the three CRUCIAL fixes Hunter needs before patch goes live:

 **TLDR VERSION**

- **Experimental Ammunition:** Replace the RNG cycling with a stronger active ability (1 min CD) that gives MM actual PvP utility.

- **Scatter Shot + Freezing Trap DR:** Remove the shared DR. This is the easiest fix that would instantly improve Hunter PvP.

- **Survival PvP Tool:** Give Survival Trap Launcher or Trapper's Net. Without it, the spec remains absolute trash tier in PvP.

 **BREAKDOWN**

 **1. Experimental Ammunition – RNG Must Go**

Regarding the "Experimental Ammunition" talent concept:

Random cycling effect is both frustating and inefFicient design. Randomness is the last thing MM needs right now; especially in PVP.

Proposed Replacement:

Experimental Ammunition – 1 min CD
The Hunter loads specialized ammunition, enhancing all shots for the next 10 sec with the chosen effect:

- [Fire Ammunition] – Your next Multi-Shot hits explode for 20% ranged weapon damage in a 5-yard radius. (PvE AoE / cleave tool)

- [Arcane Ammunition] – Your next Arcane Shot deals 50% increased damage, silences the target for 2 sec, and weakens their resistance to magic, increasing magic damage taken by 3% for 6 sec. (Crucial PvP utility MM currently lacks)

- [Poison-Soaked Ammunition] – Your next Serpent Sting deals 50% increased damage, reduces the target's armor by 4 per level (240 at 60) for 15 sec, and slows the target by 30% for 3 sec. (Hybrid PvP/PvE utility)

MM needs PvP utility. This gives MM a controlled, on-demand tool instead of frustrating RNG, while keeping the "experimental" theme intact. **Also has some use in PVE.**

 **2. Scatter Shot + Freezing Trap DR – Easiest Fix in the World**

This has been said a hundred times, but here it is again:

Scatter Shot should NOT share Diminishing Returns with Freezing Trap.

In a meta where every class has a PvP trinket to remove the first trap, our two primary CC abilities sharing a DR is ridiculous over-nerf. It leaves Hunters with no reliable long-duration CC in most 1v1 and Arena scenarios.

This is a simple fix that would instantly improve Hunter PvP viability without any new assets or complex balancing.
Please just do it.

 **3. Survival – Still DOG TIER in PvP After 1.8.1**

Changes bring nothing to the spec's PvP kit. Let's be blunt: Survival will remain absolute trash tier in PvP without a gap-closer or reliable control.

Other servers have solved this. Turtle WoW should consider one of these:

- **Option A – Trap Launcher:** Allows you to launch traps at your target from range. Gives Survival the ability to create space or secure a trap without praying the enemy walks over it.

- **Option B – Trapper's Net:** An active ability that pulls the target to you (similar to a Death Knight grip). This gives Survival the reverse gap-closer it desperately needs to stick to targets in Arena and duels.
Either option would give Survival a fighting chance without breaking class fantasy or requiring massive reworks.
