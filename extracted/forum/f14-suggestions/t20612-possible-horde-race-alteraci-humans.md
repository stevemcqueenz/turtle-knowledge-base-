# Possible Horde Race: Alteraci Humans

## Post 142574 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-01T19:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142574#p142574 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are quite a few threads talking about possible new races for either faction.
Furbolgs or Draenei(Broken) for Alliance.
Ogres or Naga for Horde.

I am not saying these suggestions are bad. I actually quite like the Draenei idea, or even the Ogres.
But lets be real. Making any of these a playable race is very difficult. They lack animations, the proper skeleton to put on armor, the female option. All this is not easy to do.

But what if we think about the Alterac Kingdom for a second and how the joined the Old Horde in order to avoid the devestation of their kingdom during Second War? They were occupied by the Alliance and put under martial law. The Kindom deteriorated and the Syndicate arose. The Syndicate are former Alteraci humans which they make obvious by their orange bandanas. After all, the flag of Alterac is orange.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/hviKjcf.png)

But what if not all former Alteraci humans joined the Syndicate? What if some sought refuge with the Horde?
After all during the second war the Horde treated the Alteraci humans quite nicely and promised them protection. If not for the Alliance victory the Alteraci Kingdom could've become a proper member of the Horde, no?

And since animations already exist for humans it would be easy to implement in theory. In practice it would obviously also require a new starting zone, some more Alteraci holdings to get quests at and several alteraci NPCs strewn about in Horde holdings with quests to make their presence within the Horde believable.

However, Turtle devs already did an amazing job with Goblins and Helfs, why not again?
