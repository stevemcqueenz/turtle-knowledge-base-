Turtle devs reworked crafting appox 2 years ago.
Here is most complete list of available gear for lvl 19.
[/viewtopic.php?t=4714](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg//viewtopic.php?t=4714)
It misses items from patch 1.18.1 because I don't have access to database.
Cloth and mail users get good crafted shoulder pieces.
You also can enchant them with naxx shoulder enchant via trade window. You just need to find someone who sells it.

Here is extract about shoulder items
Shoulders
 **Both sides**
Cloth
[Talbar Mantle](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=10657) 30 armor +6 int +3 sta.
Quest chain start: [Glowing Shard](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=10441); drops from [Mutanus the Devourer](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3654)
[Reinforced Woolen Shoulders](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4315) 29 armor +4 sta +4 spi. Vanilla item with TWoW stats.
recipe [Pattern: Reinforced Woolen Shoulders](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4347) world drop
Leather
[Serpent's Shoulders](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=5404) 65 armor +4 agi +6 spi.
Requires lvl 18
Drops from Lady Anaconda in Wailing Caverns
Mail
[Turtle Scale Shoulderguards](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80710) 184 armor. TWoW item
it is around 3% of physical mitigation for my warrior twink with shield.
Drops from [Kresh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3653) in Wailing Caverns
[Rough Bronze Shoulders](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3480) 130 armor +5 sta +2 defense. Vanilla item with TWoW stats.
Recipe taught by trainer

 **Alliance**
Mail
[Shoulderpads of Gazzirik](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60691) 149 armor +8 str +4 sta. TWoW item
Quest chain start: [Christopher Hewen](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=8934) quest [The Harvest Golem Mystery](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40470)
