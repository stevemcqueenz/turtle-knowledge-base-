# Crossfaction PvP need to happen

## Post 87526 by Nitaya (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-02-11T09:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87526#p87526 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello fellow Turtle players!

I know, it has been announced some kind of Mercenary mode, but still no new info about it.
[viewtopic.php?t=10386](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10386)

Disclaimer: All my opinion is based on the PvE realm. I don't care about the PvP one; leave everything as it is there, since they are the über hardcore PvP guys who signed up for this. Also, I'm not a native English speaker.

Nevertheless, I'm going to bring up the topic once again. In the solo queue world on the PvE realm, the situation is trash. In two weeks, I have witnessed two ABs and maybe one WSG win (while participating in at least 50 of them).

The only remediation is AV on non-AV days, simply due to Horde lacking player numbers; Alliance has the advantage by having +3-5 players on the field.

Here, I could end the topic, but I also want to share my reflection on what might be the cause of such imbalance in PvP. Feel free to disagree if you have a logical argument.

 **1. Factions.**
Horde was always with the PvP racials (we can ignore the trolls), until Every Man for Himself was not introduced in the 3.0.2 patch.
Stun resistance, fear immunity, 2-sec long AOE stun (long before Shadowfury, that was a deep Destro talent), higher health. Many of these racials hard counter other classes. As a priest against undead, your 1st fear is destined to be WoTF-ed, while as a Horde priest, you don't have to face such.

Should these racials themselves cause a 90-10 or even worse win/loss rates? Not necessarily. (I admit I pulled those numbers out of my ass, just my gut feeling and my experience)

But when a player creates a character, especially one who wants to do PvP, they do not want to play handicapped. Why would they? They want to play the best racial available for that class. You want to PvP as a druid? What can a night elf offer instead of a Tauren? Shadowmeld? You already have stealth. +1% dodge? You can do faster ghost walks? Nah. Better to pick the one that survives harder: +5% HP and AOE stun, the clear winner in PvP.

So it's not the racial itself, but the PvPer's choice of which race/faction to pick.

 **2. Class balance**
Alliance was always the PvE faction. If you remember, Blessing of Salvation was such a huge buff in raids that it made boss encounters easier for the Alliance alone. Shaman is the PvP.

 **But PvE is cross faction. Alliance has no advantage there. They won't get gear faster, they won't have easy time**

I know here in Turtle DPS paladins are so OP that they can literally 1 shot a complete raid groups.

Nevertheless I have never seen paladin on top of the HK list.
While Shamans on the other hand, quite common.
![Image](https://snipboard.io/LtAkNq.jpg)

I understand, Shaman can dish out huge dmg from 30 yards, while paladin has to be in melee range.

But there is 1 more key ability, that shaman has and paladins doesn't and leads to a huge gap in PvP (again based on my observation)

 **PURGE.**
Remark:
I know priest has dispel, but there are a lot more shamans than priests.
Also purge dispels 2 spells and shamans have more aggressive playstyle and they are more bulky.
 --
In Vanilla there is no dispel resistance, there is no talent for that. Almost every buff is magic.

I don't see any other ability that is as useful as purge.
Blessing of protection / freedom -> Purge
Power word shield / renew -> Purge
Frost mage 31 point talent ice barrier -> Purge
Basically a shaman negates a frost mage half of it's survival tool with 1 purge. While a horde mage rarely faces such scenarios.
Resto druid -> Don't even try. Many times i can't do a basic rejuv -> swift mend combo. Once i apply rejuv, by the time global CD is off rejuv already purged.
Even basic paladins seals that is required to DPS can be purged.
An ability with no cd, no casting time, available to all shamans, that completely negates a dozen of other abilities.

It is sheer pain.

Also if you think buffs are not a big of a deal. Please in your next pvp or pve raid. Don't do buffs, mages should not apply frost Armor or ice barrier in PvP. Paladins in raid should dps without seal. See if things change.

In battlegrounds, the 1st step what shamans do in the 1st clash -> Purge everything.
Now we got x horde players fully buffed, y alliance players without any kind of buff.
 ---

So we got a faction with PvP racials, that's okay.
We got a faction with PvP class, that's okay.
So overall PvPers will pick them as they want to PvP, that's okay.

The problem, they are not facing each other but rather against the other sides that due to all of these destined to be weaker and lose.

And that is the core issue.
That leads to horde stomping allys in BG.
That leads to the fact horde has to collect at least twice the honor for PvP ranking.
That leads to frustration.

Let there be PvP racials, let there be PvP classes, I'm fine.
 **But let them face eachoter.**

Small remark:
Maybe add dispel resistance to resto / holy / disciple talents-

Thanks for readin' it.
Also if your answer is: but muh AV alliance map, don't bother, there is another thread. (and no it isn't) This is not about that.
