# Reduce required level of Sul'thraze the Lasher

## Post 113165 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-18T08:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113165#p113165 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[Sul'thraze the Lasher](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=9372) is the epic sword made by combining two swords from Zul Farrak.
It has 55 ilevel, 52 DPS
Its power very similar to epic axe from Magram rep [Dar\'kar of the Third Khan](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60885).
Currently Sword requires lvl 50, which means it is useless in practice. If you graduate to 50, you don't need that sword because there are wide variety of other swords.

Proposal is: reduce required level of sword to 49.
Reason: to improve variety of cool weapons at 40-49 bracket.
