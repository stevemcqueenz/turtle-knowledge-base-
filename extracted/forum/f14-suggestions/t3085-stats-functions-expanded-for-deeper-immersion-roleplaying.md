# Stats functions expanded for deeper immersion/roleplaying

## Post 18939 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-05-31T11:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18939#p18939 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Some ideas regarding attributes, ment to deepen the roleplaying aspect of the game:

 **Spirit:**

 - the higher the spirit the longer the buffs and shorter the debuffs, charms, fears and stuns
(not by a significant amount though)
 - slightly increase reputation gain

 **Stamina:**

 - higher stamina would mean that one can run longer without getting tired (a short debuff that lowers movement speed)

 **Intellect:**

 - discovered zones on the map can be forgotten after some time, if the intellect stat doesn't reach a certain minimum
 - cartography: it can be overcome though by owning maps that are either sold or crafted by the player (for example in the survival profession, would require the zones being discovered, and bought materials to make the map),
this could be an incentive of a gameplay focusing on the exploration of the world,
 - however, there would be a still a minimum of intellect (lower than for remembering) required to read such maps, as well as scrolls, notes and books
 - higher intellect would also increase how many languages one can understand

 **Strength:**

 - the inventory somewhat simulates space (bigger items occupying more squares would be more realistic),
but it doesn't simulate weight, it's not the same having the inventory full of full stacks of iron bars vs full stacks of peacebloom,
each item could have a weight value, and the higher the strength the more weight can one carry without getting a slower movement speed debuff,
but it should not be a linear increase, instead there should be minimal strength limits required to carry weights that increae the limit by a lot first then less later, so the player takes this concept into consideration but it doesn't alter gameplay too much either,
a crude example:
at base strength of 10, weight capacity is 10 kg,
but at strength 20, 30 kg,
at strength 30, 45 kg,
at strength 40, 55 kg,
at strength 50, 60 kg.

These suggestions aren't based on a stat per level, so just by leveling up the character gains stats and improves in these things, making the leveling experience even more rewarding.

If you also have some ideas regarding some roleplaying mechanism related to attributes (primary or secondary) even if it's a rp flavor without combat alteration, and it's realistic to implement, feel free to write about it here or improve my suggestions.
