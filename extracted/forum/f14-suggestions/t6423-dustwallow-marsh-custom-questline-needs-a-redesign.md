# Dustwallow Marsh custom questline needs a redesign

## Post 40504 by Rurin — 2023-04-06T12:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40504#p40504 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is very long very lore significant questline that is supposed to continue the original unfinished Shady Rest Inn questline.

As of now, because of horrible quest placement and levels most players probably don't even know of it's existence.

From ALLIANCE side, you need to:

 1) Visit Shady Rest Inn, at barrens border, pick up quests.
 2) Visit Theramore, pick followup.
 3) Visit deserters near Barrens border.
 4) Visit Theramore.
 5) Visit Shady Rest Inn.
 6) Visit Ratchet.
 7) Gather quest items from level 34-35 mobs in Stranglethorn.
 8) Visit Shady Rest Inn AGAIN.
 9) Visit Theramore AGAIN.
 10) Go close to Brackenwall Village for followup item.
 11) Visit Theramore AGAIN.
 12) Shady Rest Inn AGAIN (quest <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40557> >> you are here)
 13) Theramore AGAIN
 14) Shady Rest Inn AGAIN
 15) Theramore AGAIN - FINALLY you get <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40565> as a final quest.

The final quest is still marked as level 35 quest.

The entire questline involves <40 mobs, it is intended to be done by characters WITHOUT MOUNTS.

It requires you to cross entire Dustwallow 13 (THIRTEEN) TIMES. PLUS the one time visit to Ratchet and Stranglethorn.
The amount of pointless running is absurd. There are practically no other quests in these areas, you can't do it while doing other stuff.

At the very least, the constant reporting to Captain Garran Vimes should be completely cut.

And quest level should be increased, so players do it while already having mounts.

But even then, I'm not sure if it would be even salvagable. I'm not sure how anyone could even think that designing something like that was a good idea.
