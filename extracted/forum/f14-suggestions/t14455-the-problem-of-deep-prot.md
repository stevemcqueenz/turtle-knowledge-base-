# The problem of deep prot

## Post 98455 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-07-03T14:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98455#p98455 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Everyone knows the baseline problem (fury has more rage and threat).
I basically wanna talk about the same thing, checking prot tree talents and tell why (in my opinion) they are either irrelevant or outmatched by fury talents.

Not counting in some talents from the prot tree, since furyprot is taking them(, too):
 * Anticipation (some survivability + allows you to go deeper)
 * Improved bloodrage + last stand -> Really good stuff.
 * Toughness 3/5 -> Survivability filler to get to Defiance. Nice but there's a reason why no furyprot ever considers even 4/5.
 * Defiance -> The cornerstone of warrior tanking. Easily reachable by fury prot.

Let's see what else the prot tree proposes. **Let's not forget that prot warrior rarely does PVP and most high level bosses can't be stunned. PVP-oriented talents are useless for them with their damage portfolio and stuns become useless lategame.**

Shield specialization -> Wants you to wear a shield. A good tank warrior has a shield swap macro and uses it to mitigate damage at spikes but that's all. OH weapon is much better for threat. You don't wanna spend 5 talent points to have some utility on those seconds when you swap in a shield.

Iron will -> Unreliable for PVE, mostly a PVP talent. Much better fit for arms tree.

Improved shield block -> Would be a cool talent if shields would worth it. They just fall off way too hard in the threat race.

Improved revenge -> A cornerstone tanking ability because it is cheap and has big threat. But! Here you can spend 5 talent points to have a chance to stun the enemy with it. Why? Wanna stun random trash mobs? Wanna yolo a BG as a prot? Wanna try to stun Nef and realize it won't work? Talent would be 10x more useful if it gave added threat to revenge.

Improved sunder armor -> Good on paper but if you can choose to be either somewhat ragestarved (except at raids) and have a somewhat cheaper sunder or just have enough rage for it all the time, those talent points seem like a waste. And if you MT a raid as a prot, you just have so much rage, this won't make a difference.

Improved disarm -> No. Absolutely useless. Maybe if it'd lower the CD significantly and make it cheap so disarm becomes a spammable utility against weapon-wielding trash but like this, no. If anything, this is PVP again.

Improved taunt -> This is actually nice but is mostly cool for leveling (you dont wanna tank as a prot while leveling). At raids, maintaining threat is the play of the game, most important targets can't be taunted.

Improved shield wall -> An extremely situational talent for a 30 min CD ability.

Concussion blow -> Would be cool for leveling if prot warrior could level properly. But just like at revenge. What do you want to stun? Trash mobs, PVP opponents or unstunnable bosses?

Improved shield bash -> I'm getting convinced that Blizzard had access to the world's best cocaine back in the day and wanted to make prot warrior half a PVP spec. Absolutely useless as it is.

One-handed specialization -> It has the sunder armor-problem. You can get some added threat with this or you just go fury and get 234234x more threat.

Shield slam -> Similar problem + usually you dont wear a shield + what about that magic effect removal? Shamans and priests will do it for you.

______________________

To make a shield-wearing warrior an interesting choice, he has to be convinced to mostly use shields (shield bash anytime he feels like it, shield slam on CD etc). Current talents don't do that.
In short, shields have to play a core part in threat management for this build so the warrior doesn't swap to an OH for more threat.

 **Ideas to make it work:**
 * REALLY raising the amount of rage you get from a block via shield specialization.
 * Improved shield block also giving threat on the target if you block his hit via shield block.
 * Improved revenge (with + threat) would be passively buffed. You actually block, it procs much more often, you can use it more often.
 * Improved shield bash giving you a temporary resistance (let's say, 60) from the school of magic you just interrupted.
 * Improved shield wall could reduce CD. Being able to use it every 10 min is indeed a difference for tanks for example.
 * One-handed specialization: instead of 10% damage, give it something like 5% damage and 3% haste. You mostly use 1 weapon to keep threat, not 2. You need to hit things fast.
 * Shield slam, instead of the spell dispell could double the threat of your next melee attack if it hits.

 **Honorable mentions:**
 * Iron will out, improved challenging shout (reduced CD) in.
 * Concussion blow, instead of stun could give a melee haste debuff to the target. Would be usable in boss fights.
 * Keep improved disarm as it is but change the talent's picture to something like this:

![Image](https://media.tenor.com/tHnHLAiMnQIAAAAM/smallbrain.gif)

Even in this case, I think fury-prot would be the better threat generator, but deep prot would become a valid choice:
 * Less rage-starved than before (unrelated at raids).
 * Passive threat via shield block blocks.
 * More threat from revenge and it procs much more frequently than to a fury prot.
 * Shield slam on CD, allowing one very threatening hit every 6 sec.

Other changes are mostly regarding survivability.

Very simple fixes to solve the shield/prot warrior problem:

 -Increase rage gained from shield specialization to 1 rage per point per block. This will solve the rage starvation outside of raids issue.

 -remove rage cost from shield block. Doesn’t make sense that Warrior needs to be partially angry to raise their shield into a defensive position. Also I’m pretty sure blizzard removed the rage cost on this in a later expansion for this very reason.

 -improved revenge should give a massive threat boost to the ability in addition to the stun. If the target is immune, they should still have the massive threat increase applied.

 -add an additional damage and threat modifier of 100% or so to Shield Slam when being used in Defensive Stance. (Or just simply increase the damage and threat of shield slam by 50-100%)

These are all very simple things to do without going deep into the rabbit hole that would make prot warriors absolute threat monsters and make them viable for leveling with after 40 where they gain Shield Slam.
