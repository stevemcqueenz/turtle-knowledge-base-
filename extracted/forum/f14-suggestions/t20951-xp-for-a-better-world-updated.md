# 🌍 XP FOR A BETTER WORLD (Updated)

## Post 145726 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-15T15:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145726#p145726 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’ve been playing for a long time, and from my experiences interacting with other players, I’ve often found myself wondering about how XP is given — and how it could be used to improve the game’s social experience.
Here are my ideas for changes that I believe could make the game better overall.

 **1. Adjusting Group XP Distribution**

After paying closer attention to how XP is distributed in groups, I noticed something important: XP does go up when more people join, but in many situations the **Group Bonus XP (Y)** is too small to fully compensate for the reduction in the mob’s **Base XP (X)** when it’s divided among more members.

In simplified terms, it works something like this:
- **Solo:** XP = **X** (full base XP from the mob)

- **2 players:** XP = (**X / 2**) + 1Y

- **3 players:** XP = (**X / 3**) + 2Y

- **4 players:** XP = (**X / 4**) + 3Y

- **5 players:** XP = (**X / 5**) + 4Y
The issue is that as the group gets larger, the **X / members** portion shrinks faster than the total **Y** increases.
 **Proposal:** Increase the **Group Bonus XP (Y)** scaling so that adding a player is not a net loss in XP-per-member for normal leveling groups. This reinforces the cooperative nature of the MMO without encouraging raid-style stacking.
 *In raids, XP values should remain as they are now — no bonus per extra player — to avoid disrupting raid balance.*

 **2. Level Gap Penalty Adjustment**

Increase the XP penalty when there is a large level difference between group members.
This helps reduce power-leveling and encourages forming groups with players closer to your own level, while still letting friends play together.

 **3. Quest Helper Bonus (Participation-Based)**

Helping friends with quests is a core MMO thing, but it often feels inefficient compared to just doing your own route. I think a small helper bonus could fix that — **as long as it’s tied to participation, not turn-in presence**.

 **Proposal:** When a grouped member turns in a quest, give a **small helper bonus XP** to other group members who were **eligible helpers**.

Eligibility (anti-abuse guardrails):
- Bonus only applies to players who were **grouped during objective progress** (kills/collects), not someone who joins at the quest hub NPC.

- Require **participation/proximity** during objectives (and not AFK), so “do nothing and profit” doesn’t work.

- Make the bonus **small and capped** (per quest / per time window), so it stays a “nice reward” and not something you can farm.
This also avoids the “race to turn in” concern, because the helper bonus is based on participation and can be granted consistently to eligible helpers instead of depending on who turns in first.

 **4. Account-Based Veteran XP Bonus**

Introduce an optional XP bonus based on how many level 60 characters exist on the player’s account.
The more max-level characters you have, the higher the potential bonus you can choose for the new character.

- The bonus is **selected only at character creation** and cannot be toggled later.

- It does **not** apply to Hardcore or Slow & Steady (or is significantly reduced for them).

- This rewards veteran players without impacting those who prefer a slower leveling experience.
 **Additional Note on Multiboxing**

While multiboxing in its current form is limited, it still reduces genuine player interaction.
The XP changes above would naturally make multiboxing far less effective, but additional technical measures like removing **/follow**, limiting **Click to Move**, or using **Extended Fingerprints** could further mitigate it without harming regular players.

A good change could be the key, is to tie any “helper bonus” to actual participation during the quest objectives, not to simply being present at the turn-in:

- **Eligibility:** bonus only for players who were grouped during objective progress (kills/collects), not someone who joins at the quest hub.

That keeps the spirit of the idea (reward helping) while closing the loopholes you described. If you have suggestions for the exact thresholds/caps, I’m open to them — that’s the kind of detail that makes or breaks it.
