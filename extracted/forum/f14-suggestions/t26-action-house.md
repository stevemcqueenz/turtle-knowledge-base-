## Post 137 by Orntz — 2018-09-17T21:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137#p137 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It would probably just lead to more chat spam as you suggest. The Auction House has been a core gameplay mechanic since vanilla and still is in BfA so I doubt that people would be open to lose it. It is still possible to roleplay as a merchant with the Auction House in the game.

Without many concurrent online players it would be difficult to sell goods without the Auction House. Other games provide means to set up your character as an afk merchant bot essentially. It would only work on a massive server and even then the chat would be out of control.
