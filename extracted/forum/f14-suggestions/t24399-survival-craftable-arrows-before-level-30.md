# Survival: Craftable Arrows Before Level 30

## Post 171669 by Jfarre01 — 2026-03-21T18:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171669#p171669 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

It is awesome that the Survival skill now allows for the crafting of arrows, similar to how Engineering allows crafting of bullets.

Unfortunately, the lowest level of arrows available to be crafted, that I've seen, require level 30.

For example: Engineers can make Crafted Light Shot (req level 5), Crafted Heavy Shot (req level 15).

I propose that lower level craftable arrows should be implemented for the Survival skill.

There was a deprecated arrow in the vanilla game files ([https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=30 ... wood-arrow](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=3029/depricated-whipwood-arrow)) which I think could fit the level 15 arrow well.

I even had the idea for a Survival recipe for craftable arrows that drops from the Frostmane Hollow hunter boss (call it Frostmane Arrow).

Thank you for your attention to this matter.
