# Honor Cap maths issue

## Post 173011 by Alcilis — 2026-03-29T12:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173011#p173011 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

After 1 year of waiting, the long-awaited PvP patch is finally out, motivating me to come back to this server.

I agree with most of the changes done so far, great improvements for us PvPers.

But there is a very simple maths issue with the way it is currently implemented...

Indeed, even if you're already R12+, you can't directly buy the last gear pieces as you are forced to buy the previous pieces first.
Hence, to buy a single piece of equipment related to your rank, you have to spend ~80K honor.
And with the current 20K Honor Cap, this results in having to play 1 month to be able to buy a single piece of gear.
 6 months to be able to be fully geared, even if you already are R12-14...
And the starting honor bonus we got linked to our rank is not even (or barely depending on the rank) enough to buy 1 single piece !

I can agree with the grind incentive of having to buy previous pieces to be able to upgrade them towards more powerful pieces, but then please remove this 20K weekly honor cap as it is only nerfing our willingness to grind !
Maybe lower even further honor currency gains to balance things out, but give us high-rankers an incentive to grind more than 3h per week !

Thanks in advance.
