# Hunter - Pokeball for petskill learning

## Post 156355 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-20T20:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156355#p156355 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi there,

Since the process of learning pet skills as a hunter can be a bit unclear—especially for new players—I’d like to suggest an item that allows temporarily stashing your current pet in your inventory. This would let you tame a new pet to learn its skill, then release it and retrieve your original companion.

This feature would make collecting new skills from different beasts much smoother and help players understand which skills their pets can actually learn.

Cheers,
