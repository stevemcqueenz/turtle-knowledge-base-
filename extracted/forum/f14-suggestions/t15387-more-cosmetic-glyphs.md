# More Cosmetic Glyphs?

## Post 105125 by Moongirl — 2024-10-28T17:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105125#p105125 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Greetings! I hope you are having a nice day!**  smiling_turtle_head

If you have looked in the donation shop, (or even in Emerald Sanctum!) you will notice there are Cosmetic Glyphs for Druids! This glyphs do not change the playstyle or grant you power, and are simply to look cool. However, only druids have these glyphs (from my knowledge). *I believe other classes should have these glyphs, and I have put a list of some that may work.*

 **Paladin Glyph Dark Knight**: Lots of people want to be (or are) "death knight" paladins. However, the "light" throws this off. If possible, I would recommend this glyph darkening all the light spells, similar to how Shadowform darkens the priest's model.

 **Priest Glyph Moon Priestess**: Mostly for Night Elves. This would make all the holy spells use an arcane spell look instead, symbolizing the connection to Elune rather than The Light. Perhaps a different glyph where the High Elves holy spells can look fiery, showing connection to the Eternal Sun? (Or is that a blood elf only thing?)

 **Warlock Glyph Fel Minions**: This glyph will change the look of your minion to that of a different model. Perhaps a more green model to make it look more fel?

 **Rogue Glyph Stealth Tricks**: A very minor glyph. When you enter stealth mode, or use distract, a large puff of smoke will appear. Perhaps other things can work too, like a magical "vanish" that uses the blink spell look.

These are just ideas! Be sure to post of your own below! Thank you for reading, and good luck in your journey.   smiling_turtle_head
