# Righteous Fury

## Post 153204 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-25T20:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153204#p153204 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

One thing in advance: it's not a hatepost, I want prot pallies to be both fun and strong.
But there's something I wanna question.
My PoV is mostly dungeons, that's the part of the game I like the most.

At dungeons prot feels extremely strong compared to the other tanks.
Once decently-geared, they basically become a mob-magnet with extreme utility and decent survivability.
I'm not saying prot shouldn't remain strong.
What I question is the threat buff of **righteous fury**.
It is a huge threat increase which was totally justified in Vanilla where prot had fewer ways to inflict holy damage.
But making holy damage more consistent while keeping the threat modifier as it was turned them into a glue, at least it feels like it.

I want prots to be the best aoe tanks, but I don't want consecration to feel like an always on AoE taunt.
I'd imagine prot palas as high utility tanks with excellent aoe threat and serviceable but not too high single-target threat to keep things balanced.
Once again, my PoV stems from dungeons.
