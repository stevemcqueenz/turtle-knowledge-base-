# QOL Wintefall firewater/juju might

## Post 128591 by Franks26 — 2025-04-26T14:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128591#p128591 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So this have been a problem for a while. From a hunter perspective Juju might is a very annoying/expensive consume that is also the only one we can use.

While other clases can just use Winterfall firewater (which is basically the same in terms of power) we have to farm a consume that last half the time and takes at least 3x more time since it comes from elite mobs.

On top of that firewater cant be used on pets...so if you wanna push your damage that means you need  juju might for them too.

To give an example in a one hour MC that means you need 6 for you and 6 for pet compared to the 3 firewater a warrior or rogue 'd use.

So here is a couple of ideas to solve this:
 1) Make firewater give ranged ap too
 2) Increase duration of juju might to 20min (same as firewater)
 3) Make firewater usable on pets

Any of the ones abvove 'd help and is important to notice that im not asking for all of them or for a dps increase. None of the suggestions do that, in fact, if for example firewater becomes usable on pets and give range ap most hunters will go for that (because its so much easier to farm/cheaper to buy) and that means we will loose 10 ap compared to using juju might
