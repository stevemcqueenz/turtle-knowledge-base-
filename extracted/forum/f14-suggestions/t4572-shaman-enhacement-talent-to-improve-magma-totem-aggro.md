# Shaman enhacement talent to improve magma totem aggro

## Post 27331 by Zhilo — 2022-10-15T19:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27331#p27331 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have been looking at magma totem recently and been thinking that it would be nice if shaman tanks could use it as a Consecrate proxy. I do not know if the engine allows for this since the enmity relationships between totems and shamans seems complicated.

In case it is possible, here's my suggestion:

Enhacement tree talent alteration:

Guardian Totems 2/2

Increases the amount of damage reduced by your Stoneskin totem and Windwall totem by 20%, reduces the cooldown of your Grounding totem by 2 sec. (new) Additionally, Magma totem threat increased by 100% and this treat is attributed to the caster instead of the totem.

Magma totem doesn't do all that much damage so I think doubling the threat is reasonable. But if it isnt, it can be a 50% or even 25% increase. The important bit really, is to be able to transform this fire totem into a tanking tool if you choose to take the talent.
