# Mitigate the unfair bad luck strike for Thunderfury

## Post 165013 by Robespoire — 2026-01-12T23:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165013#p165013 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Legendaries are supposed to be rare and hard to get so it both feel rewarding when you get it and to avoid having every single player parading with one.

However no mechanism is made to ensure a player who tirelessly tried to get it will eventually be rewarded. Bad luck can unfairly punish a player out of this loot.

There is a way to only mitigate that bad luck without making the item more accessible.

For Thunderfury bindings one could unlock a quest that would allow them to loot tokens on Garr and Geddon and trade them against a binding.

It takes an average of 22 runs to get both bindings (5% and 6% drop on Turtle). If the quest requires 40 token to get a binding this will mitigate the strike of bad luck to a year and a half in case of no binding drop and a little more than 9 months in case of a binding drop.

Now 40 is a big number and maybe the suffering of the players should not extend that long but at least you get to decide the maximum duration of the player despairs.
