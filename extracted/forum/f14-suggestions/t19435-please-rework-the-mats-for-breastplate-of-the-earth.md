# Please rework the mats for Breastplate of the Earth

## Post 132174 by Noephix (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-20T17:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132174#p132174 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Breastplate of the Earth is a mid-40s lvl Leatherworking rare quality item geared towards bear/cat druids (mostly bears). And it costs around 70 gold to make.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=65022>

Among other materials, it requires 12 Cores of Earth, and those cores jumped up in price recently since it's now possible to break them into Elemental earth, which is required for several endgame consumables, making Breastplate of the Earth WAAAY too expensive to craft for a leveling druid. Please reduce it's material cost to something more reasonable - like 2 cores, maybe.
