# Suggestion for a New Realm

## Post 170582 by Raroro — 2026-03-11T14:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170582#p170582 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Create a new RPPvP realm with 1x rates and no cross-faction.
Add a challenge or an NPC that allows players to activate a Neutrality status. While Neutrality is active, you cannot attack players of the opposing faction in the open world, and they cannot attack you.

This would create a realm where PvE players who dislike cross-faction can enjoy a more lore-friendly and immersive experience. The Neutrality status could also be combined with other challenges, including Hardcore.

At the same time, PvP players would benefit from a healthier realm population, since PvE-oriented players would also be interested in playing there.

This way, all groups of players would have the choice to play with cross-faction gameplay and its conveniences (on Nord and Amb) , or without it, for a more authentic, faction-focused experience.

Finally, after all content phases are released, the realm could be merged with Tel'Abim.
