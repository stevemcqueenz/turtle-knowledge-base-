# A dungeon/raid experience around Un'goro.

## Post 133809 by Mirdath — 2025-05-31T21:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133809#p133809 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I realize this may sound absurd but bear with me. It's a great zone, fairly accessible but still isolated from the adjacent zones. Ancient, full of life, and a test zone for the Titans. Elementals, crystals, dinosaurs, silithid. Un'goro seems to have all the makings of something incredibly cool  while having a sense of absurdity and comedy.

I'm not sure there is a specific reason for it being a crater so here's one idea: A magic meteor hits it. While most of the population remains savage, some of the creatures become intelligent and form their own faction. I'm talking about a dungeon full of dinosaur wizards. A devilsaur in a hat wielding a wand. Goblin expeditions gone awry. Experiments long forgotten by the Titans suddenly granted new life. Rebellious silithid unattached to the Qiraji. Pissed off fire elementals. Flowers. Raptors. Golems.

Hyper-intelligent apes led by A-Me 01.

I'm just spitballing here.
