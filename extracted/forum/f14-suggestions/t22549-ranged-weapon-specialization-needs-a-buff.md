# Ranged Weapon Specialization needs a buff

## Post 156834 by Vellasta — 2025-10-24T08:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156834#p156834 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

For a Row 6 talent, 1% damage increase per point is underwhelming compared to equivalent talents. I believe it's one of the reasons for low MM hunter dps in raids at the moment.

 **Examples from other classes:**
Two-Handed Specialization (Arms, Retribution):
 2% damage + 1 weapon skill per point from a **Row 4 talent**.

Dual-Wield Specialization (Fury):
 5% offhand damage + 2% hit per point from a **Row 2 talent**.

Dual-Wield Specialization (Combat):
 10% offhand damage per point from a **Row 4 talent**.

 **Suggestion**
Please consider increasing the damage per point to 2% at least.
