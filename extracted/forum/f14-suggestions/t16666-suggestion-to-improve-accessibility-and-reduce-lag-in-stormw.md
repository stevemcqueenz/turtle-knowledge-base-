# Suggestion to Improve Accessibility and Reduce Lag in Stormwind

## Post 113870 by Leodimas — 2024-12-22T15:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113870#p113870 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle WoW Development Team,

First, thank you for the amazing work on Turtle WoW. Your attention to detail and dedication to creating a unique and immersive experience are much appreciated by the community!

I’d like to propose a small but impactful addition that could help alleviate some of the crowding issues in Stormwind's main square while enhancing the city’s functionality and immersion.

Suggestion:

Open up the bank in the Dwarven District.
Add a mailbox and possibly an auction house within or near the Dwarven District.
Reasoning:
Stormwind’s main square has become increasingly crowded and laggy, which can detract from the immersive MMO feel. Players are often stacked and clustered around the bank, mailbox, and auction house, which can create performance issues and diminish the sense of exploration in the city.

By making the Dwarven District more functional with these amenities, you could:

Distribute player activity more evenly throughout Stormwind.
Reduce lag and congestion in the main square.
Provide players with a thematic and practical alternative location to conduct their business.
The Dwarven District is a beautifully designed part of the city but often underutilized compared to other zones. Making it a secondary hub for banking, mail, and trading could breathe new life into this area while improving the overall experience for everyone in Stormwind.

Thank you for considering this suggestion, and keep up the fantastic work!
