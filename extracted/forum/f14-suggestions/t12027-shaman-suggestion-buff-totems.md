# Shaman suggestion: buff totems

## Post 79677 by Aavri — 2023-12-02T23:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79677#p79677 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Not windfury totem. That's fine.

But let's compare it to their counterpart, the paladin.

Mana spring totem rank 4 is level 56 and gives 10 mana per 2 for 60 seconds. So it will give a total of 300 mana back.

Blessing of wisdom rank 5 at 54 gives 30 mana per 5 for 300 seconds. In a 60 second timeframe that will give 360 mana back. It also has a level 60 rank version that gives 33 mana per 5.

This also doesn't include the fact the shaman has to refresh the totem every 60 seconds, loosing out on a ton of potenail mana compared to BoW.

I would argue that totems giving so many buffs only benefit really in solo play. In group play, I am not giving casters anything other than salvation or wisdom. Melee will always get might. It doesn't matter if a shaman gives both strength of earth totem and mana spring totem. And in raids it really doesn't matter.

Might gives 185 attack power while strength totem gives 77 strength (154 attack power).  Str does give other values like block so I can understand it being slightly weaker, but not that much weaker. Even if a class has a talent that buffs str by 10%, that's 84 STR / 168 attack power.

Both totems could get like a 10% increase

So the suggestion: buff totem buffs please.

Edit for another thing: blessing can usually be applied pre combat and don't need to be refreshed during except for long fights. Totems for them to be in a good position have to be placed in combat, and usually have to be refreshed, eating into the limited mana pool.
