# Building a Real PvP Server: What Needs to Change

## Post 142810 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-02T20:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142810#p142810 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Building a Real PvP Server: What Needs to Change**

 **A PvP server how it is, is doomed from the start** if there's nothing encouraging players to participate in open-world PvP — aside from griefing farmers who just want to quest. Most PvP in WoW is instanced, so world PvP ends up being seen as more of a hassle than anything fun or rewarding.

 **Here are some core suggestions if the goal is to create a truly engaging PvP-focused environment:**

 **Instanced Battlegrounds (BGs) should have lockouts.**
- No more 24/7 BG queueing that bypasses the world.

- If this is a PvP server, then players should be out there, clashing — not hiding in instances.
 **PvP rewards must be superior to PvE rewards.**
- Raiding should exist, but its gear should be worse than what you get through PvP.

- PvP should be the main progression path.
 **Raids should be easier and drop weaker gear.**
- On a PvP server, the focus should not be on coordinating PvE mechanics,

- but on world dominance, territory control, and open conflict.
 **There must be constant open-world PvP events.**
These could include:
- Treasure hunts in contested zones

- Random base captures

- PvP bounties on enemy faction players

- Escort missions and supply caravans

- Bonus XP or gold/loot multipliers for zones controlled by your faction
These events should have *real consequences*, like increased XP gain, gold, or mob loot for the winning faction.

 **Introduce new PvP modes to spice things up.**
For example:
- Battle Royale–style events, solo or 5-man teams, with exclusive rewards

- Map-wide treasure hunts, with clues and PvP clashes

- Faction Wars over zones, where the winning faction earns bonus XP, gold, and rep while questing/farming in that region
 **Faction balancing should not rely on restricting players from joining one side.**
Instead, the underdog faction should receive dynamic buffs:
- Bonus XP

- Stronger guards/NPCs

- Honor decay only if the player stops logging in

- Faster BG queues

- Better world buffs or loot
 **Class balance should preserve the rock-paper-scissors dynamic.**
- Not every class needs to be equal in every matchup.

- Counters should exist.

- PvP thrives when players adapt, not when one spec dominates everything.
 **Racial abilities must be balanced across the board.**
- No racial should single-handedly dictate class or faction choice.

- All races should have flavorful but fair bonuses.
 **Right now, the server feels like it’s trying to support PvP using the same PvE framework.** That’s never going to work.
 **You can’t build a PvP-first experience without shaking up the core systems.**
A proper PvP server needs conflict, scarcity, imbalance (with tools to fight back), and *reasons* to fight outside of ganking lowbies.

 **Otherwise, it’s just Classic WoW with more frustration and less purpose.**

## Post 142862 by Aelfric — 2025-08-03T03:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142862#p142862 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Once I was in a server that released BGs after month 3, and it had an honor system from day 1. For the first 3 months, I had the time of my life reaching rank 10 pvp. I went all the way to level 60, it felt ultra immersive to adventure pvp, to track down people, to even face revenge. PVP was the perfect RP because there was no layer to hop to. What you did in the world felt really geo-strategic in the RP of horde vs alliance. You could set up a roadblock anywhere with massive emergent gameplay and fun things to coordinate people with. Another aspect is how you could earn a reputation from doing it. However, by month 3 when BGs released - I quit. I don't enjoy BGs, theyre 100x more efficient honor than open world, and I don't even like solo-instancing for pve. Unfortunately, probably almost no one shares my opinion. I think anything against BGs will not happen, but perhaps your other ideas could be considered.
