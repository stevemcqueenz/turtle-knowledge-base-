I know with all the changes to mage and it being a contentious class changes are not like to happen, but one of the least fun and monotonous part of playing a mage is the conjuring consumables.

There already was a change to buffs to reduce the mana cost for them to reduce the monotony of having to drink between buffing, so what I'm proposing is a reduced mana cost for conjuring mana gems, and conjuring water through all ranks, and if possible make crystal water conjure 20 at a time.

I feel like this would be a nice qol change from being the vanilla vendor bot, while not drastically affecting player power.

> **Helinos wrote: Sun Dec 21, 2025 9:01 pm**
> so what I'm proposing is a reduced mana cost for conjuring mana gems, and conjuring water through all ranks, and if possible make crystal water conjure 20 at a time.

I feel that Mana Gems are pretty much at their limit. They cost a little more than they restore, which is a necessity, otherwise people would use Mana Gems to generate mana mid-fight.

The Crystal Water suggestion I don't really understand. Ritual of Refreshment ("Oranges") is already the perfect solution to the vendor-bot struggles mages used to face. How often do you have multiple mana users of lvl 55-59 in your party that you would need to conjure several stacks of Crystal Water? There's probably no downside to increasing the yield from a single cast to 20 (except going against nostalgia), but it seems like a fairly low value change to me.

Lower ranks definitely need some looking into (as discussed [here](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=22559) for instance). Having to conjure water 2 pieces at a time and then it's still 5 levels out-dated and barely fills half of your mana pool is a real QoL problem. Lowering mana cost, shortening cast time, increasing restored mana - some kind of change should be considered there. But at lvl 60 I feel most concerns go away rather quickly due to Oranges.
