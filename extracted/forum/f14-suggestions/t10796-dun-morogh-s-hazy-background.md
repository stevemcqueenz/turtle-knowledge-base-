Hello, follow Turtles. So, I noticed when I leave Ironforge to go to Dun Morogh the atmosphere looks normal, but when I walk down the slope, the atmosphere turns really hazy all of a sudden without gradually turning that way.  I'm not sure if this is a bug, or if it was done deliberately. I think this hazy background gives Dun Morogh a dead and gloomy vibe and it takes away from the lively spirit that the town usually has, imo. Perhaps, others prefer it that way. Of that, I'm not sure. But, I think Dun Morogh would look much more lively without that haze.
