# Aim True, Craft New! Bring Fletching to Turtle WoW!

## Post 125573 by D33FR3SH — 2025-04-05T17:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125573#p125573 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What's the worst feeling when you're playing a hunter? Running out of ammo. Sure, for our engineers out there, you can easily craft yourself some bullets and a gun but what about our bow and crossbow users? **WE AIN'T GOT NOTHIN'**.

Replaying through this classic world on Turtle WoW has made me realize: there are no good bows or crossbows at lower level; and, arrows and bolts? Pfft. Forget about it. The economy isn't getting any better.

Given Turtle WoW’s philosophy of expanding Classic without deviating significantly, integrating Fletching into Leatherworking/Blacksmithing might be more seamless and immediately beneficial. Below you'll find a framework for recipes that could be available through leatherworking and blacksmithing:

 **Leatherworking - Bow and Arrow Crafting Overview**

---

![🏹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3f9.svg) **Low-Level Recipes (Apprentice - Journeyman)**

 **Simple Hunting Bow (Apprentice Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Light Leather (3), Linen Thread (2), Light Wood (vendor-sold)

- Stats: +2 Agility

- Method: Trainer-taught
 **Crude Stone Arrows (Bundle of 200) (Apprentice Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Rough Stone (5), Light Leather (1), Wooden Arrow Shaft (vendor-sold)

- Effect: Adds 1.5 damage per second

- Method: Trainer-taught
 **Hunter's Quiver (8 slot) (Journeyman Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Medium Leather (4), Linen Cloth (4), Coarse Thread (1)

- Effect: Increases ranged attack speed by 8%

- Method: Trainer-taught

---

![🏹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3f9.svg) **Mid-Level Recipes (Expert)**

 **Composite Ashwood Bow (Expert Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Heavy Leather (6), Iron Bar (4), Silk Thread (2), Cured Ashwood (Rare drop from Forest Troll mobs in Hinterlands or Stranglethorn)

- Stats: +5 Agility, +2 Stamina

- Method: Dropped Recipe (rare world drop, Hinterlands/STV mobs)
 **Barbed Iron Arrows (Bundle of 200) (Expert Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Iron Bar (5), Heavy Leather (2), Wooden Arrow Shaft (vendor-sold)

- Effect: Adds 4.0 damage per second

- Method: Leatherworking Supply Vendor (limited availability)
 **Scout’s Ammo Pouch (12 slot) (Expert Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Thick Leather (6), Silk Cloth (4), Heavy Silken Thread (2)

- Effect: Increases ranged attack speed by 11%

- Method: Quest Reward (Hunter-centric questline)

---

![🏹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3f9.svg) **High-Level Recipes (Artisan)**

 **Dragonscale Warbow (Artisan Leatherworking - Dragonscale specialization)**
- Materials: Worn Dragonscale (8), Rugged Leather (6), Essence of Fire (2), Rune Thread (2)

- Stats: +14 Agility, +6 Stamina, Chance on hit: Deals 30-40 additional Fire damage

- Method: Dragonscale Leatherworking Trainer (requires Dragonscale specialization)
 **Thorium Broadhead Arrows (Bundle of 200) (Artisan Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Thorium Bar (6), Dense Leather (3), Elemental Air (1), Wooden Arrow Shaft (vendor-sold)

- Effect: Adds 16.0 damage per second, Chance on hit: +50 armor penetration

- Method: Recipe drop from high-level humanoids (Blackrock Spire, Scholomance, Stratholme)
 **Windrunner's Quiver (16 slot) (Artisan Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Rugged Leather (8), Bolt of Runecloth (4), Essence of Air (2), Enchanted Leather (1)

- Effect: Increases ranged attack speed by 15%

- Method: Dungeon Boss Drop (Dire Maul Tribute Run)

---

![🏹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3f9.svg) **Special or Rare Recipes (Artisan - Epic/Legendary Quality)**

 **Heartseeker Longbow (Epic - Artisan Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Core Leather (4), Lava Core (1), Bloodvine (2), Essence of Earth (2), Elemental Sharpening Stone (1)

- Stats: +18 Agility, +8 Stamina, Chance on hit: Occasionally bleeds the target for 50 damage over 6 sec.

- Method: Recipe drops from bosses in Molten Core
 **Blessed Sunfury Arrows (Bundle of 200) (Epic - Artisan Leatherworking)**
- Materials: Arcanite Bar (2), Sungrass (6), Rugged Leather (4), Large Brilliant Shard (1)

- Effect: Adds 20.0 damage per second, Chance on hit: Deals additional Holy damage to undead and demons

- Method: Argent Dawn Exalted reputation reward (vendor in Light's Hope Chapel)
