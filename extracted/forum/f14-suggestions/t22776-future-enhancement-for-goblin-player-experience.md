# Future enhancement for Goblin player experience

## Post 158803 by Briaros23 — 2025-11-07T18:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158803#p158803 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Can a Jewelcrafting trainer be added to Rustgate Ridge in Blackstone Island?

I created a goblin warlock because I wanted to try out the new Jewelcrafting changes and Goblins get a racial incentivizing this profession.

Without a trainer present, I feel like I am being soft locked out of participating in an incentivized way of playing a goblin.

This is extra noticeable as my backpack is now full of crafting materials I cannot use.

Starting at 16 slots, 3-4 slots are automatically reserved for food,drink, and crafting tools.

Assuming it takes up all 4 slots, I have 12 slots left. Now I have 2 stacks of copper bars, another two stacks of copper ore, and a stack of rough stone reserving slots in my inventory until I can leave the island.

I am now down to 7 slots before accounting for quest items. even if it was only one quest item reserving a slot, all of this combined leaves me with 6 slots for general loot, crafting supplies from vendors, etc.

These numbers are a conservative estimate as I am only level 5 and still have 15 quests in my quest log. So I am going to be on this island for multiple in game hours before I leave.

I understand this is not an issue for an alt character, as bags can be purchased and sent to the alt, but as a first character this is actively deteriorating the player experience.

You only get one first impression in gaming, and if these circumstances were someones first experience, I would not blame them if they stopped playing.

Please consider adding a Jewelcrafting Trainer in Rustgate Ridge so this poor experience can be avoided.

Thank you for reading this and taking my suggestion into consideration.

Respectfully,
Briaros23
