Greetings Staff & Players of Turtle WoW!

First I would like to start off by saying im enjoying my time here after 10+ years of not playing vanilla.

So from the title, my suggestion is cross faction BG's I understand this is a Rp/PvE server. Even though bgs are still happening after about 20 hours in the queue you might get 1 or 2, more if you're lucky.

Now having cross faction PvP would boost the amount of Bgs being popped up, There is very little horde (atleast from what i can see and everyone tells me) at the moment so giving us the ability (even if toggleable or only with the dimplomat) this would allows us PvP players enjoy some more battlegrounds. Even when joining the BG its always almost only 1v1 which is still fun, but doesnt beat the fun we get from 10v10.

I spoke to quiete a few people about this and they all agreed it would be awesome, so here i am with my suggestion :)

I feel like there is no reason why we couldn't have cross faction pvp as we can already group/dungeon/talk/guild with the opposing faction. Could we get a poll about this if enough people "+1"?

Thanks for reading and i hope you take my suggestion into consideration and speak to the community more about it.

 -Noize
