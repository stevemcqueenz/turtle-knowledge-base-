One missing animation is when we activate our guild tabard to teleport, the character just stands there. Can we add a spell casting animation?

Another interesting idea was in retail, when you open up the map, your character opens up a scroll/map in-game. it's a really cool RP effect and also lets everybody around know that you're busy looking at the map and/or other info panels. Could we backport the map-looking animation from retail?

Two more ideas are for when you are fishing, mousing over the bobber makes a gear icon - could we backport the 'fishhook' icon? And the last is the same, but for when you mouse over a portal - it's simply shows a gear icon. I believe retail has a cool little portal icon.

I think these are fun to look at and help slightly with immerson.
