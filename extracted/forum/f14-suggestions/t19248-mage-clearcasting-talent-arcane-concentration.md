# Mage Clearcasting Talent (Arcane Concentration)

## Post 130758 by Avalok — 2025-05-12T19:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130758#p130758 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There has been a **nerf** on the said talent, that is **'Arcane Concentration'**, or the **Mage Clearcasting Buff**. The proc of the talent buff can now only occur once every 8 seconds. I, personally, am not too happy with the nerf. Before the nerf, the buff can proc multiple times within the channeling time of Arcane Missiles, and probably Icicles as well, based on each successful hit of the two spells, and I would think to myself that the multiple procs of that buff feels like a bit of a waste if they weren't used since the buff does not stack.

My idea to that situation would be to allow the Mage Clearcasting Buff to stack on itself twice... Make the Arcane Concentration Talent Buff of Mages stack on one another at a reasonable two stacks, and perhaps with an occurrence time limit as well, to make it useful for single spell casts or channels with multiple hits, and benefit from them the probable multiple procs of the buff, and to not make them feel like a waste.

This is just my idea. If some of you believe that the nerf is the better option, and that my suggestion would cause an imbalance or over-empowerment, then I'm not going to argue. I would just like to add that the probability of the procs of Arcane Concentration don't occur as often as some people may think, even with Arcane Missiles and Icicles spells.
