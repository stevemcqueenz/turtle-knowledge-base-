# Suggestion: Flight Path Connections

## Post 147806 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-23T07:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147806#p147806 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The various new zones are on the whole excellent, but a common point that I found is that they suffer from flight point isolation. They generally have only a single flight point connection, and said lack of flight connections can make reaching them, as well as flying back from them to other points of the continent, feel hampered when you end up in a situation such as you only being able to reach Theramore (Dustwallow Marsh) from Bael Hardul (Stonetalon Mountains) by flying north through Astranaar (Ashenvale) and then again north to Auberdine (Darkshore), and then fly south to Theramore.

These problems can be addressed by connecting these custom, isolated flight paths together a bit more in logical fashion. My proposed connections based off of my own experiences are the following:

 **Alah'Thalas, Thalassian Highlands:**
- Due to how the very zone is isolated with only a land connection to Eastern Plaguelands, I don't believe that any further flight point connections are currently possible for Alah'Thalas. Alah'Thalas was formerly connected to Chillwind Camp, but this was before the revamp of the area into the Thalassian Highlands and thus may not be ideal to be connected anymore.
 **Ironforge Airfields, Dun Morogh:**
- Ironforge Airfields' current flight point connections are suitable.
 **Dun Agrath, Wetlands:**
- Dun Agrath's current flight point connections are suitable.
 **Caelan's Rest, Lapidis Isle:**
- SI:7 Outpost, Balor
 **Maul'ogg Refuge, Gillijim's Isle:**
- Stormbreaker Point, Balor
 **Bael Hardul, Stonetalon Mountains:**
- Ratchet, the Barrens

- Nijel's Point, Desolace
 **Ravenshire, Gilneas:**
- Menethil Harbor, Wetlands
 **Stillward Church, Gilneas:**
- The Sepulcher, Silverpine Forest
 **Nordanaar, Hyjal:**
- As Hyjal is high up on the mountain and the flight point takes the route following the land road from Winterspring, I don't believe that any further flight point connections are possible for Nordanaar aside from *maybe* Moonglade.
 **Tel Co. Basecamp, Tel'Abim:**
- As the zone is already fairly isolated due to being very far south and the next-closest non-landlocked zone being Dustwallow Marsh, I don't believe that any further flight point connections are possible for Tel Co. Basecamp. Theramore/Brackenwall Village could be tentatively considered, but both of these already connect directly with Gadgetzan, which is already the liaison with Tel Co. Basecamp.
 **SI:7 Outpost, Balor:**
- Caelan's Rest, Lapidis Isle

- Ambershire, Northwind
 **Stormbreaker Point, Balor:**
- Maul'ogg Refuge, Gillijim's  Isle
 **Ambershire, Northwind:**
- SI:7 Outpost, Balor

- *Thorium Point, Searing Gorge**

- Morgan's Vigil, Burning Steppes

- Ironforge, Dun Morogh
 **Dun Kithas, Grim Reaches:**
- Menethil Harbor, Wetlands

- Refuge Pointe, Arathi Highlands
 **Shatterblade Post, Grim Reaches:**
- Kargath, Badlands
 *It has been noted to me on Discord during the writing of this post that this flight point is already planned to be added in the future. I am keeping it mentioned here as a full list of potential flight paths and for posterity.
