## Post 72155 by Queenoona (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-19T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=72155#p72155 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't mind giving players additional ways to play but I am against DW for enhancement shamans. Dual-Wielding alone did not 'fix' their DPS in TBC, the myriad talents surrounding it and enabling the playstyle did. The enhancement tree is already flooded with talents for both 2h and Tanking and adding in Dual-Wielding support would further dilute it.

Additionally it seems rather boring from a design perspective to make yet another DPS class that wants 1h weapons.

Warriors want 1h > 2h, Rogues want 1h, even hunters want 1h weapons as stat sticks. 2h weapons are generally undesired aside from PvPers and Ret Paladins and Enhancement Shamans. No need to create additional gear competition for any of the these players

Also, look at the various 1h caster and healer weapons. They would all have to be switched to main-hand or else you would have Resto shamans running around with double healing weapons or Elemental Shamans running around w/ Double SP weapons. Simply putting Dual Wield deep into the enhancement tree WOULD NOT fix this as there are already non-enhancement shamans going deep into the Tree for Bloodlust to support their group.

To add Dual-Wielding to Shaman would require a massive rework of the classes talent trees and abilities as well as changing items in the game so that they couldn't be abused by Dual Wielding Resto/Ele Shamans. I don't foresee that being a viable path by the devs.
