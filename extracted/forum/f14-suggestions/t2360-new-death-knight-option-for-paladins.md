# New Death knight option for paladins

## Post 14842 by Robertscotty — 2021-12-26T20:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14842#p14842 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it would be really cool to allow paladin players the option of becoming a Death knight upon reaching level 60 if they choose too which will open a brand new series of content for the player to complete

The player will be able to begin their journey to darkness upon reaching level 60 and completing the Naxxramas raid

Upon killing Kel'thuzad Father Inigo Montoy revels his true alligence to the Lich king and offers to teach the player the ways of the Scourge saying that defeating Kel'thuzad shows how much strangh and power the player has

If the player exepts Montoy's offer then the treacherous priest will offer a Series of Quests to the player which upon completion will mark the player's journey to darkness and the player will officially become a Death knight

Montoy will then task the newly created Death knight with presenting himself or herself before Kel' thuzad who has returned to Azeroth with Montoy's help

Upon speaking with Kel'thuzad the Arch-Lich officially welcomes the player into the ranks of the Scourge

Upon completion of this Quest the player becomes hated by the Alliance and Horde while friendly with the Scourge

This opens a brand new series of content across both the eastern kingdoms and Kalimdor as the player embarks upon new Missions in the Lich king's service

In the eastern kingdoms Quest hubs for the player will include the following

Agamand Mills
Beren's Peril
Fenris Keep
Andorhal
Sorrow Hill
Scholomance
Darrowshire
Terrordale
Northdale
Stratholme
Deatholme once ghostlands is added

In Kalimdor the Quests for the Death knight will be contained to Razorfen Downs and the area around as this will be the only scourge controlled area within Kalimdor. The players missions here will mostly be helping Amnennar the Coldbringer spread the lich king's will across the continent

All of the cities and small towns listed above will contain flight paths, venders and a innkeeper

Naxxramas will serve as the faction capital with Kel'thuzad serving as the faction leader in the lich king's name

A scourge storyline should also be created for all future regions including outland and Northrend so that Death knight players aren't strictly contained to Azeroth

Upon becoming a Death knight the player's profession will be changed to align with the player's new faction and will give the player new Death knight only patterns to learn and craft

Because all of the Scourge raids and dungeons will become friendly Quest hubs upon becoming a Death knight the turtle wow team should come up with new Dungeons and raids for death knight players to replace the ones lost
