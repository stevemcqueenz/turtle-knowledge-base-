Patch Notes & Changelog: Improved Transparency

Lately, we've noticed that recent patch notes and changelogs have lacked clarity when detailing buffs, nerfs, adjustments, and new features. As players, it’s essential to understand the precise changes that impact gameplay, balance, and overall experience. Without clear communication, speculation and confusion replace informed discussions.

To improve transparency and readability, we believe patch notes should be structured into clear sections, such as:

- **CLASSES & ABILITIES** – Changes to class mechanics, spells, talents, and abilities.

- **ITEMS** – Adjustments to gear, weapons, trinkets, and consumables.

- **ADJUSTMENTS** – Covering minor tweaks that refine mechanics, stats, or interactions.

- **FEATURES** – Detailing fresh content, features, or mechanics introduced in the update.

- **BUG FIXES** – Resolving issues affecting gameplay functionality and stability.

CLASSES & ABILITIES
- **WARRIORS**
 - **[NERF]** **Sweeping Strikes** can no longer critical strike.
 - **Reason:** Previously, Sweeping Strikes allowed secondary target damage to critically strike, causing unintended burst potential in cleave scenarios. This adjustment ensures a more balanced damage output in both PvE and PvP.
- **MAGES**
 - **[FIX]** Mages that faction change from Alliance to Horde will now unlearn **Teleport: Alah'Thalas** and **Portal: Alah'Thalas**.
 - **Reason:** Prevents faction-changed Mages from retaining Alliance-exclusive teleportation spells, ensuring consistency across faction mechanics.

ITEMS
- **ITEM UPDATES**
 - **[CHANGE]** Updated several items to no longer require direct damage/healing landing spells to trigger their effects. These items can now proc from successful harmful or helpful spell casts, respectively:
 - **Possessions of the Lost** (set bonus)
 - **Blade of Eternal Darkness**
 - **Ornate Bloodstone Dagger**
 - **Pure Jewel of Draenor**
 - **Wrath of Cenarius**
 - **Staff of the Qiraji Prophets**
 - **The Scythe of Elune**
 - **Sphere of the Endless Gulch**

 - **Reason:** Previously, these items relied solely on direct damage/healing effects, restricting their usability. This change ensures broader accessibility and improves their functionality for various classes.
 - Tooltips for these items will be corrected and clarified in the next client patch where applicable.

ADJUSTMENTS
- **[CHANGE]** Race changes within the same faction will no longer cause faction-specific spells to be replaced with those of the opposite faction.
 - **Reason:** Ensures faction identity remains intact when race-changing within the same faction, preventing unintended spell swaps.
- **[FIX]** Dual-use spells such as **Dispel Magic**, **Chastise**, **Dampen Magic**, and **Amplify Magic** (when used with the **Magic Attunement** talent) are now correctly considered by **Grounding Totem**.
 - **Reason:** **Grounding Totem** was inconsistently recognizing dual-use spells, leading to gameplay inconsistencies. This fix ensures proper spell interactions.

FEATURES
- **[ADDED]** **Dwarven Battle Loaf** to the bread vendor in **Ironforge**.
 - **Reason:** Provides additional food options for players utilizing **Ironforge** vendors.
- **[ADDED]** **Kul Tiras Tabard** back to the donation store along with new tattoos and faction cloaks.
 - **Reason:** Reintroducing highly requested cosmetic items to the donation store for player customization.
- **[ADDED]** **Victorious Alliance Steed** to the donation store.
 - **Reason:** Restores an iconic mount option for Alliance players.

BUG FIXES
- Raids & Dungeons
 - **[CHANGE]** **Mephistroth** is no longer affected by **Curse of Tongues**, **Mind-Numbing Poison**, or similar cast-slowing effects.
 - **Reason:** The boss was unintentionally affected by cast-slowing mechanics, disrupting raid timing. To compensate, the cast time of **Shackles of the Legion** and **Nathrezim Terror** has been increased to 2.5 seconds, ensuring fair gameplay balance.

Due to ongoing issues with our payment providers during the spring sale, we've extended it until May 6, inclusive.

Disclaimer: This changelog is an example and does not include every individual update or adjustment. Some details may be omitted for clarity.
