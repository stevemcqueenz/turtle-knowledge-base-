## Post 163600 by Newplayersolost — 2025-12-24T09:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163600#p163600 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi there,

First off, I know these suggestions are probably more appealing to the hardcore players, and may be unpopular but... hear me out. As a new player to TurtleWoW, I’ve noticed there’s a real struggle for people leveling, especially those without a gold backed main to gear them up from AH or send gear. Here are a couple of ideas that could help and would be very welcomed by new players like myself.

A. Instant dungeon teleportation
Yes, walking around is part of the classic experience but it’s 2025. People skip dungeons because they’re time consuming, take away from world buffs, and just aren’t worth it for many classes that actually need gear (had many conversations about this on world chat and can confirm, this is indeed how most players feel). I’m level 30 on my first character,a DPS warrior and the gear from dungeons is critical. Being able to teleport instantly would let more players experience dungeons without losing motivation. This would not be the case for RAIDS, just dungeons.

B. Playerbots in dungeons
Setting up bots could give players a chance to actually complete dungeons and get the gear they need. It’s not perfect, but it’s better than sitting in queues for hours as DPS and quitting without a single run. Not everyone wants to grind low level mobs endlessly most of us want to get gear from dungeons we can handle. The bots can easily be configured to have slightly better and worse gear than the dungeon requires to balance out groups as to not be a walk in the park, but at the same time being very manageable to pass the content.

These changes wouldn’t ruin the classic feel, but they’d make leveling and gearing up a lot less frustrating for newer players.

Doing the dungeon is time-consuming in my experience, not getting there or finding the group, especially on turtle with even more added flight paths, zeppelins and boats.

I suggest preparing all the quests before even looking for a group for a specific dungeon, and do your questing/grinding in a nearby zone while searching/forming a group.

I disagree about adding teleportation to dungeons. Current year is usually not a good argument, especially for game design. It doesn't matter what year or time of day it is, it is designed this way for a reason and one reason is immersion. Personally I prefer more diagetic design and I believe even the automatic dungeon finder queue lessens the overall experience. Part because it's non-immersive, it takes me out of the game and reminds me that it's just a game, and part because it makes people less communicative. It results in less coordination, less connections between players being made, where connections makes it easier to form future dungeon groups. It also handicaps players by not forcing them to manually form groups and interact with each other, some might never realize how easy it is to create a group of your own by just whispering players.

I also disagree about adding playerbots. Part of the beauty of the game is that it requires you to cooperate with other real people to achieve a common goal.

My suggestion for you is to try forming your own groups manually. Maybe even try tanking, warriors can tank any low level dungeon without any tank talents. Put on a shield and a fast one-hander and try it. Let the group know that you are new, most people are very welcoming to new players, and if you don't know the layout of the dungeon you can let someone else lead. Being the tank makes it so much easier to get groups going, you just need to find a healer which can be done by using /who and asking priests, druids, shamans or paladins in the appropriate level range. The rest of the dps will fill fast, by whispering, using world chat, or the dungeon finder.

Even as dps, manually forming groups will always be faster than sitting and waiting in a queue.

Finally, I disagree that these changes wouldn't change the classic feel. It would completely ruin the immersion for me. What arbitrary magic is it that puts me together with a group of people and teleports me to the dungeon? The world needs to be consistent and follow its own rules. I want to interact with the world and the players to achieve something. I want TurtleWoW to be less of a theme-park MMO, not more.
