# List of shadow priest issues and proposals

## Post 168911 by Kentorianx — 2026-02-19T16:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168911#p168911 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

In this post I’m listing the main issues we currently have as a class. Most of these are small changes and not hard to implement. They won’t make us OP, but they would at least stop us from being a sitting duck and make the spec more attractive overall.

- **Vampiric Embrace Is a DPS Loss to Apply**
The main reason to bring a Shadow Priest to a guild is Vampiric Embrace and Shadow Weaving. With the last CC change, multi-target Shadow Weaving was introduced, which is nice. But applying Vampiric Embrace is still a DPS loss.

 **Fix:** Let SW:P apply Vampiric Embrace in PvE with its own cooldown (not shared with VE).

- **Mind Blast Cooldown Desync with Mind Flay**
The Improved Mind Blast talent reduces MB cooldown by 2.5 seconds at max rank, leaving it at 5.5 seconds. This doesn’t line up well because Mind Flay ticks every second, so there’s always that awkward 0.5 second gap between a tick and casting Mind Blast. That’s why most of us don’t go 5/5 Improved Mind Blast.

 **Fix:** Reduce Mind Blast cooldown by 0.5 seconds baseline. If you want to give us a real push (which we honestly need), reduce it by 1.5 seconds baseline. That would make crit more viable and better aligned with the 2.5 second reduction from the talent.

- **Essential Talents Locked Too Deep in Holy**
Spiritual Guidance and Holy Nova are basically essential for Shadow, yet they’re too deep in the Holy tree. We end up spending points on talents that don’t benefit Shadow at all.

 **Fix:** Make Holy Nova baseline and move Spiritual Guidance closer to the first tiers of the Holy tree. This would also help Disc priests, since right now they don’t have much flexibility in their builds either.

- **Useless T2 and T3 2-Piece Bonuses**
T2 and T3 2-piece bonuses are useless.

 **Fix:** Just change them. A Mana Burn damage bonus on T2 makes no sense, no one is taking T2 in PvP because of the low stamina, and we’re obviously not using Mana Burn in PvE.
As for T3 2-piece: hit chance on Mind Control. We’re already hit-capped because of our talents. That bonus is effectively nothing.

- **T3 4-Piece Bonus Is Inconsistent and Crit Doesn’t Synergize**
The T3 4-piece bonus doesn’t apply instantly and rarely procs in practice because we don’t stack crit.

 **Fix:** Either make it last longer or have it apply to the Mind Blast that crits and the next one. That would at least make crit more attractive and give us a reason to play around that buff.

- **T3.5 Dot Fails on Many Bosses**
T3.5 dot damage doesn’t work on a lot of bosses.

 **Fix:** Change it to a magical effect instead of a disease so it actually works consistently.
Overall, this probably still isn’t enough. We simply don’t scale like other classes because we lack real multipliers. Maybe letting Mind Flay crit or giving Mind Blast additional crit scaling would help. Right now, stacking crit just doesn’t feel rewarding for Shadow, and that’s a big part of the problem.

And dont get me started with the amount of threat we generate with such low DPS.
