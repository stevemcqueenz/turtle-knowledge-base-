## Post 77776 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-17T05:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77776#p77776 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Turtle WoW wiki absolutely need a lot of help. However, before starting to work on it, right now in the turtle-wiki chat in the discord we're discussing potentially moving the wiki to the wiki.gg domain. Currently, the wiki is hosted on Fandom, which is a fairly horrible host for a number of reasons. Switching to wiki.gg would allow us to switch to a better domain, as well as allowing us to link to Warcraft Wiki as needed (Fandom is currently blacklisting all wiki.gg links, preventing us to link to Warcraft Wiki, which is disruptive to several of our links).
