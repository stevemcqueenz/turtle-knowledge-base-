## Post 154451 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-05T14:05:00Z

I played Epoch and there is one thing from that server that was very well done: PvP leveling progression through WSG.
You could level up in WSG (Turtle does it too) but also there was gear vendor that was selling actual WSG sets for each level. You purchased that gear for Marks. Completing whole set required a lot of games played.
 10-19 set gave mainly PvP oriented stats with trinket that removes CC.

When you entered new bracket, you could upgrade your gear with Marks. So you could upgrade your 10-19 set to 20-29 set with small amount of marks. The gear you earned at level 10 was still worth upgrading at level 60 - there was continuity of these sets. Each upgrade was worth it - you got more stats etc.

What I mean is, Turtle lacks this kind of PvP leveling progression that Epoch did well. It gave incentive for people to join battlegrounds during leveling. Why not to add similar PvP progression to Turtle, too?
