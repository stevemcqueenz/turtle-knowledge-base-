# NPC schedules

## Post 80016 by Tekamthi (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-06T08:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80016#p80016 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm new to both turtle and WoW -- I played retail in beta, bought the game, played out my free month, then never again.  Just wasn't into it back then, but the community here is great, am enjoying revisiting this old title.  Thanks!

The one aspect I've found jarring about nearly every MMO, including wow and turtle, is the static NPC locations, just standing around in town all day and all night, never moving... yet there *are* NPCs that walk around and do things.  Not like the game engine can't handle it.

Given turtle's RP roots, I'd love to see a day/night cycle for NPCs, have 'em go to the pub at dinner time, etc.  Would bring some living quality to the towns and cities.  Probably not enough buildings to have them all in their houses puttering around like in ultima 7 etc., but there are some inaccessible doors etc. they could disappear into til morning.

To avoid breaking quest turn ins, would maybe need some alternate NPC's to staff their locations at night, not sure how to handle this otherwise.  I'd go with night elves and undead for these roles.

Probably a lot of work for thematic-only effect, but I'd enjoy it anyway.  Thanks again for a great server!

True, with Ultima 7 as my model, from what I recall many of the mobs would also have some kind of schedule.  Not all mind you, but there was a strong presence of the day/night cycle throughout the world, other than just visual.  Torches and town lighting generally had to be lit by the NPCs as it got dark, didn't just turn on by themselves.... This amounted to NPC walking up to them, some animation with their arms, and voila they were lit.  Players could do the exact same thing, giving the sense that these player accessible systems were the same mechanisms via which NPCs lived their lives.
None of it was very sophisticated really, but done just right to create an interesting change. Others have done similar things since, I believe the later elder scrolls games, pokemon as was mentioned etc.  It's a nice-to-have feature for an RP setting.

I've always found the mob idle behaviors to be similarly jarring in many mmo's.  That said they need to be static to a great degree to fit their quest purpose, couldn't have large migrations across half the continent etc.  But perhaps if they lay down at night time, in same spot, would be a start.

Schedules for the town/city NPC's in particular strike me as more valuable for thematic effect, but certainly there are a lot of small changes that could improve the general "feeling" of Azeroth's day vs night life
