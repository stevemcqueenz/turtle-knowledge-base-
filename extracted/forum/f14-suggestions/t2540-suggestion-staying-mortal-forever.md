# Suggestion - staying mortal forever

## Post 15777 by Ingameacc12345 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-01-14T12:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15777#p15777 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to add my vote to the suggestion presented in this thread: [viewtopic.php?f=37&t=1673](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=1673)

I'm currently level 59 and I really love this way of playing, so much so that I never want to become immortal. The feeling of constant danger, stress and the need to be responsible and careful make this game mode one of a kind. You might say - "well, just stay 59 forever then!". But that's not the optimal/workable solution because of at least these things that I can think of (I'm sure there are more):

 - missing that last talent point
 - being excluded from some quests and thus missing their rewards
 - no fast mount (this one is major!)
 - general lack of feeling of the character "completeness"
 - last but not least: missing turtle points bonus for hitting 60 in hardcore

To me it would be enough to have a simple popup window upon reaching level 60 in hardcore mode offering a one-time choice of becoming a "normal person" or staying mortal forever.

Please consider this suggestion, twow team! I'm sure I'm not the only one who would wish this feature!
