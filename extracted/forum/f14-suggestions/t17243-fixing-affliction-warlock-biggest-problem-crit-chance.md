# Fixing Affliction Warlock biggest problem: Crit Chance

## Post 117722 by Ridersnitsuga — 2025-01-23T22:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117722#p117722 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, after a lot of Affliction Warlock playing, i have noticed a big problem after reaching Loatheb on Naxxramas...

Afliction Warlocks does absolute nothing with the Crit chance they get.
True, you can do a little bit of talent managing and go for **"Improved Shadow Bolt"** for the chance to increase shadow damage, but even then, you could only get 3 points on it without sacrificing damage for a "Chance" of increasing damage.
And even then, tier sets on the Affliction Warlock side focus on the Casting Speed aspect over the crit chance.

This all leads to all the pasive Crit chance you get from Intelect, and even some items that has a lot of Spell power, but 1 or 2 % crit chance being kinda wasted on an Affliction warlock.

So i propose something:

 **Make the Talent "Rapid Deterioration" convert your Crit Chance into Haste for affliction spells**
or make it a second Capstone for Affliction Warlocks tree.
And when I say convert, I mean "Set Crit Chance to 0% and give that amount as Haste", so it can't get abused by SM/Ruin warlocks

The point of this is to not waste precious stats into nothing, and also overall increasing Afflictions damage on it.
Now, i don't know exactly what would be a good convertion ratio for it, i was thinking 1:1 ratio, but i dont know how much greed will that put on Affliction Warlocks to get every single Crit chance item on earth, and also... kinda killing the "Haste" power.
So maybe a 2:1 ratio of "Crit chance" to "Haste"? Then again, don't know that much about numbers to give a perfect answer.

Another change would be to convert the crit chance into straight up Spell Power, but that would be the most hard to balance in my opinion.

Overall, this is a post mostly to put into perspective and "TRY" to give a solution to one of Afflictions warlocks biggest problems: Crit Chance.
