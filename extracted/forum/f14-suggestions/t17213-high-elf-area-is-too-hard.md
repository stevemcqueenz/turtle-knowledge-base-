Completely agree. It’s doable for any experienced player, but the custom starting zones are way overturned as far as hostile mob mechanics, placement, and density goes, especially compared to the original starter areas. It makes for a really rough new player experience, I think they forget these zones are supposed to serve as tutorials for potentially brand-new players. Starting zones are supposed to be very low-stakes and forgiving, and I don’t think the custom starts reflect that very much.

Personally I believe they would have been better off working on a more polished 1-5 experience, and then introduce new Goblins and High Elves to Durotar and Elwynn Forest (maybe Teldrassil?) respectively. If not that, then the 1-5 areas of the custom starts should be much more separate from the 5-10 areas. As it stands it’s way too easy for a less experienced player to accidentally wander into a bunch of hostile orange/red-level mobs and get torn to pieces for just trying to explore and get a lay of the land.
