# Weapon Skill per Weaponsmith Spec (Blacksmithing)

## Post 15356 by Denniswilson — 2022-01-05T07:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15356#p15356 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My suggestion is to have Blacksmiths gain +5 Weapon Skill to whichever weaponsmithing spec they choose.

Axesmiths would gain +5 Axes and +5 Two-Handed Axes
Swordsmiths would gain +5 Swords and +5 Two-Handed Swords
Hammersmiths would gain +5 Maces +5 Two-Handed Maces

 -Blacksmithing would be a more valuable and meaningful profession.
 -Every melee class could benefit.
 -Diversity.
 -Less wasted weapons in raids and dungeons.
 -Fits with the lore/idea of weapon specialization in Vanilla.
