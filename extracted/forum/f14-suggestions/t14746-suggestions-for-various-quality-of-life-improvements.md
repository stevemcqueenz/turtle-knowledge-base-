# suggestions for various quality of life improvements

## Post 100487 by Fornow (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-01T18:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100487#p100487 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**druids**
- ctrl + click on an item shows you a preview for how it would look on your character. That's useless though when shapeshifted  (bear form, cat form, wisp, etc). Instead the game should automatically present you with a view of your caster form and apply the preview on top of that.

- Sea Lion Form shouldn't be lost if you jump above the water surface briefly. It should only be lost if you actually touch land. It's a minor thing but the camera angle under water often makes it hard to see where you are going and being able to jump above water briefly makes it easier to navigate.
 **dungeon finder**
- One should be able to see all dungeon groups forming in LFT even if they are above one's own level. It means that you can level one character but also keep out an eye for any group that you might want to join with another (higher level) character of yours.

- Let us queue our role individually for individual dungeons. For example a paladin might prefer to come as a healer to one  dungeon to roll on the healer gear there while she wants to visit another just for the tank gear.
 **misc**
- Decrease the size that the player model grows under the bloodlust effect in battleground, specifically in Arathi Basin inside the farm house. As a Tauren you'll be stuck in the door frame and can't walk out if you pick that up.
