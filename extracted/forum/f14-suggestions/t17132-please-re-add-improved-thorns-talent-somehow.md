Quitting druid entirely because Improved Thorns got removed is absurd, but I agree that removing the talent was a bad call and the result of a narrow minded, endgame-focused mindset by the devs.
The way Imp Thorns used to work was that balance and moonglow resto druids would grab it due to it naturally being in their path, but if you were a levelling or recently dinged 60 bear-focused feral druid, Imp Thorns could have a lot of value for both open world content and dungeons. It gave balance and moonglow druids another added benefit, and was especially nice for moonglow druids as most of the talents leading up to moonglow are essentially useless for resto. When tanking 5 person dungeons as a fresh 60, Imp Thorns could make up a very good deal of both your damage and threat.
Now the effect has been added to Feral T2, making it inaccesible for levellers or fresh 60s, but free for anyone whos progressed through BWL. Keeping the tier set equipped is not even needed for keeping the buff up, so instead you just macro it and go back to your normal gear afterwards, same as resto T1. Which means that now if you want the ultimate Thorns buff you should roll on both resto and feral tier sets? It's a bit unclear whether they want the improved version of Thorns to be a self-buff or something relegated to a "support" caster/healer.
My suggestion would be to re-add the talent by tying it into some existing Balance talent since the tree is already so bloated. To replace the T2 set bonus you could still keep the focus on AoE and add something like: 3pc: Your Swipe not hits one extra target.
This would make the set worth it to actually wear while also making it good for Cats (since they like to go Bear and Swipe on trash)
