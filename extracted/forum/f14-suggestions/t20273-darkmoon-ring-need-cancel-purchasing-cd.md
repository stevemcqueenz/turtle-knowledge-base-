I agree. Limited supply items should get the same treatment as limited supply patterns/schematics/plans/formulas received.

Iirc the major problem with limited supply recipes was people hoarding the Thorium Widget schematics in STV and putting them up for auction, which was especially annoying for hc people because you could never get them. But I guess bop recipes like the enchant shield formula in uc or the rune cloth cape pattern in moonglade were even more annoying because you had to stay next to the vendor indefinitely and couldn't even use an alt to buy them.

Limited supply items are a nuisance on a server with the intended 3 to 3.5k players (as it was back in vanilla wow) but are just plain awful on a 9k pop server.

Now what should still remain limited supply is crafting material you can just buy. Just one example is the availability of fel cloth from the limited supply vendor imp in Winterspring. Obviously you either don't want this to be unlimited supply or need to jack up the price to prevent it from becoming the primary source of fel cloth and rendering any fel cloth you can find in the open world pointless.

Now I know that you cannot disenchant purple items you buy from vendors (like purple axe/staff in Desolace) and assume that you cannot disenchant the dmf ring either. I never tried to de a dmf ring but if it's de-able (because limited supply), this would need to be changed ofc.
