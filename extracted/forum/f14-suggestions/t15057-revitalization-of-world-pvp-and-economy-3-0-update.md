Hey there valued reader,

I go by Amarion in our community and I have been a long time member of TWoW since this server had some 30ish daily players.

For past couple of years I knew a lot of people who dropped TWoW after years of playing because they felt the world a bit too stagnant and lacking any major PvP events/shakeups or player driven emergent gameplay. So I tried to think off a way how to keep those players who got their BIS items, are on their way to hit gold cap and have seen inside and out of every single raid, but WITHOUT changing the PvE/slow paced nature of TWoW (or the cross faction camaraderie that can only be found in our community), by forcing everyone into PvP for sake of few bored players with Naxx gear (or suggesting the devs "just to" create/test/implement brand new content that doesn't rely on preexisting game loops or mechanics).

So here is my take one it.

We introduce two exclusive prospecting neutral sub-factions, that can be joined by either Horde or the Alliance members. An entrepreneurial offshoot of free dark iron dwarves of Thorium brotherhood, **Shadowforge Reclamation Association**  and **Undermine Prospecting League**, commerce minded neutral goblins who come from distant Kezan with blessings of great Trade princes' to claim new resources and trade deals for the ever-avaricious Undermine. (the names are just placeholders for concepts, that can/will be changed)

The system begins with a high level player making a choice, between the goblins or the dark iron dwarves that will make one of the factions unfriendly, but not out right hostile to you. The open violence comes as you do more quests with the their opposition.

When joining one of the factions, you will get a trinket, that when equipped will allow you to get a timed stacking multiplier for more end game item yields (that only applies in open world), as you turn in the resources to the respective faction handler in the field (primarily high level zones). BUT as you turn in the resources to your chosen faction to get the buff, you will lose reputation with the opposing faction to the point that players, **OF EITHER FACTION** (be it **Horde** or **Alliance**), serving your rival group, **CAN ATTACK YOU**  and **KILL YOU** to take portion of the stacked multiplier (if they have the trinket equipped and have reached the same reputation requirements).

The reputation rewards can be cosmetically themed to the faction and be on par of the same rep grind items you can see in Caverns of Time.

Example 1:

I hope to see Alliance on Alliance violence even in Strormwind. Imagine you got farmed for resource yield buffs by a member of your own faction for past few hours, who teleported to Stormwind but did not take off the trinket, he goes to a secluded area and is chatting with his guildies before logging off to save the buff. There should be nothing to prevent you from calling couple of your buddies, equipping your opposite side trinket and killing him, but getting debuff "Oathbreaker" for 5 minutes that makes all guards (and potentially players) hostile to you if they can see you.

Small events like this make the world feel more alive, for higher level characters capitals become not just a place to AFK, but a source of opportunities and as much of danger. Player choices become catalysts for vendettas (and roleplay), that will only create more organic content for players just by playing the game.

First of the desired effects of my suggestion to create a high risk/high reward system that rewards those who are willing to spill blood to get what they want, in turn creating a more dynamic and dangerous world, while not punishing PvE players who just want to farm out herbs needed for their raid consumes in DM:E. (which is an absolutely valid way to play the game, you guys will get no hate from a fellow lasher farmer)

The second one is to create open world PvP opportunities that aren't Horde vs Alliance cookie cutter group ups we have seen tens of thousands of times across hundreds of vanilla like servers, the goal is to create naturally emerging situations that creates interesting race/class PvP groupings that were usually reserved for premades in Arena, like Human Paladin, High Elf Mage and Orc Shaman fighting against Undead Rogue, Tauren Warrior and Dwarf Priest over impactful gameplay loops (like economic reasons), without breaking the integrity of the setting or disrespecting the established lore.

Third potential effect of these changes is glut of high end crafting materials that will appear on AH and push the prices down, but that can be offset by increasing the amount of the resources needed for crafting items or the devs can permit the price drop and deflation of economy to happen, that will make raid consumes and pre-raid epic items more affordable. The other potential outcome is status quo, for as the new content comes out, the increase of resources will be offset by arrival of new and returning players, which hopefully will add to even more digital bloodshed.

But the most important goal is to hopefully make Turtle WoW stand out of the crowd of Vanilla+ servers, as a more of a player driven experience that takes the game in an open world/sandbox direction where the players organically create their own emergent gameplay and there is no better way of stimulating that desired outcome than trough promise of improved material conditions.
