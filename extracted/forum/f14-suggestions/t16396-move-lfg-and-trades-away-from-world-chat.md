World chat being all in-in-one was fine when population was really low. Now it can be hard to keep up during peak times. Unfortunately this is a self-reinforcing problem: the livelier the chat the more incentive to spam so that *your*] messages get seen over others.

Imo it would make sense to separate world chat into multipleh  channels:
- **trade**: for anything trade/craft/auction related

- **looking for group**: for dungeons, raids, group quests or leveling partners

- **assistance**: questions about quests, talents, macros, lore, etc

- **guild recruitment**: this is already its own channel but often it's ignored and recruitment ads are posted in /world instead

There was a server called Hellground, which implemented separate channels for separate needs. I remember there was a transition period for the 1st month where GMs had to be active on the chat and reminded to use the proper channels, however the final outcome was as planned and you could talk on the world chat about your skills, how to complete the quest, any other topic while if you were interested to join a dungeon you were joining a dedicated channel. The same with trading.

To summarize, it worked, it just needed a little bit of effort from GM to set some system message reminders about it.

I vote for that idea, especially that there are 8k people on the Nordannar.
