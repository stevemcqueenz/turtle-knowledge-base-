# Yet Another Survival Suggestion

## Post 22711 by Crommush — 2022-08-18T15:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22711#p22711 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Survival needs some tweaking, most people seem to agree that all the value of this profession is frontloaded on tents and little else.
So how about adding bows and throwing weapons (with perhaps crossbows as well?) to the pool of recipes you can craft? Ammo for these weapons would also be a welcome addition, and as most Ubisoft copypasted open world games taught us it's that bows are deadlier than guns.

Engineering covers guns already, and we all know that it's already the best profession in the game with the myriad of tools it offers. I feel it would give survival more worthwhile low level recipes as well, that don't involve spamming the same item over and over.

More suggestions are welcome, though!
