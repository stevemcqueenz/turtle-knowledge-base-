# Fashion Demands (Oddly-Paced) Sacrifices

## Post 16991 by Deekin — 2022-02-12T11:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16991#p16991 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi Everyone!

As part of the Turtle Experience I have been getting the fashion coin in every bracket. I farmed a few at low levels with the assumption that it gets more difficult and more expensive as we get to higher levels. But from detailing the requirements - **they don't.** Some levels require arbitrarily more difficult, arduous and expensive materials than others. I am presenting my data as an argument to level the curve, please, so it gets progressively more difficult rather than with sporadic spikes.

Please see the graph below of costs:

[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/6bsvg9t/Capture.png)](https://imgbb.com/)

To summarise:
 10 - 19: About 60silver AH price, skinners and miners will find it easier.
 20 - 29: About  1g60silver AH price, skinners and miners will find it easier.
 30 - 39: About 3g13silver AH price, skinners will find it easier, a tailor is required.
 40 - 49: About 7gold68silver AH price, skinners and miners will find it easier, a blacksmith is required AND a leatherworker is required. This is the only tier that requires an item (16 thick murloc scales) that are not readily available on the AH. They took me 1 and a half hours to farm just for those. Three seperate components require craftsmen to visit.
 50 - 59: About 6gold48silver AH price, herbalists and miners will find it easier, a blacksmith AND a tailor, potentially an alchemist, is required. However all the materials are readily available and even cheaper than the previous bracket.
 60: About 15gold75silver AH price. Will need a tailor. All the materials are readily available and most of the price is just in the feathers which may fluctuate. Considering these can be farmed like collars / paws / pelts, this is FAR cheaper in terms of time investment than the 40-49 bracket.

Can we streamline this, please, so the 40-49 bracket in particular is less stringent? An obscure item such as furry paws are fine, 5 of em, but 16 murloc scales to contribute to one item is punishing. Perhaps the 60 bracket should have something crafted too. Obviously these are just the  reasonable AH prices, as I see them, rather than picking inflated periods. I tried to choose the cheapest base materials.

I love the system, and having incentives to seek out friendly craftsmen is a great feature! It is very difficult to keep looking RPish while leveling however, but that's the nature of the beast. I do wonder if maybe giving MORE fashion tokens per hand in would be wise (aside from 60), but make the coins only redeemable in that bracket? So people don't feel punished for not stockpiling at lvl10 / 20.
