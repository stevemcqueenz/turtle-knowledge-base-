# Seeking a Fair Solution for Server Crash HC Deaths

## Post 121860 by Buci7 — 2025-03-06T09:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121860#p121860 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone, I wanted to bring up a frustrating experience from last night’s server crash. Me and four others lost our hardcore characters while running RFD—not due to mistakes, but because the game froze, leaving us completely helpless as mobs killed us once it resumed.

I understand that rollbacks aren’t possible, but the response we received—that too few deaths occurred to take action—felt cold and dismissive. When a server-side failure directly causes character losses, there should be a system in place to address it, so players don’t feel like their time and effort can be erased without recourse.

I’m not here to stir drama, but I expected a fairer response than “not enough deaths to care.” I still want to believe in this server and its community, but without a solution, it’s hard to trust that our progress is safe from technical failures.

Mods, admins—is there a way to establish a framework for handling cases like this? A precedent should be set—not for handouts, but for fair treatment when players have zero control over a situation. Ignoring these cases only discourages commitment to the game.

Would love to hear from others who were affected or have thoughts on what a fair solution could look like.

PS: I hope this situation can lead to a long-term positive impact, ensuring future players don’t experience similar frustrations.
