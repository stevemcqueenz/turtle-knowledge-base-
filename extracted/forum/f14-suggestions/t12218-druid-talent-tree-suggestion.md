This is in response to my original post (->[viewtopic.php?t=9101](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=9101)). Few months back I completed the grind. I now have been raiding ZG, AQ20, MC, BWL, and I am starting AQ40 this week. Below are some talent tree suggestions based on my experience.

What informed these decisions:
 1) I wanted to make a tree that had unique selections for both PVP and PVE.
 2) I wanted to bring keep boomkin inline for raiding with other major caster classes. The tree has less + dmg, + crit,  no treat reduction, and more. I don't think we need to hit all the same +dmg thresholds, but I am looking at quality of life and value for the same raid slot.
 3) Lower moons glow chart tier will be an improvement for Quality of life for resto druids.

At this point I have mostly the same gear as our best mages and when I take a flask of supreme power I can still be 8th in raid but multiple significantly down compared to them when they aren't flasking.

AOE utility would be appreciated in tiered raiding where we may have 1-3 mages/warlocks total in a raid on our server. Just getting from 60 s CD to 0 sec cold down so you can cast ever pull or on chain pulls would be nice improvement. I think the scaling from coefficient is the same however, mages starting dmg per second is higher with blizzard and lets not getting into fire strike and AE.

I think Swarm should be able to be cast by multiple druids but the miss chance should stack, but the damage done should still apply as an option if multiples are cast. Also similar to pain you can increase the duration by 6 seconds making it 50% stronger and it will still be questionable at high spell power levels if it is even worth it... UNLESS you add 5% boost to nature dmg as a buff.  This will also be a nice small improvement for shaman dps.

So, I think your frustration comes from the fact that Druids do not have a standard knockback reduction for either Wrath or Starfire which other specs/classes tend to have (e.g. all healing trees, mage fire, warlock ruin etc.). However, I'd like to point out a few things:
 1) Wrath is one of the faster cast times (baseline 2sec, 1.5 with talents) in the game which is why it doesn't have a standard knockback reduction talent. I hope you can recognise that this is quite a typical trade-off.
 2) Starfire (even when talented) should be used primarily as an opener (when undetected). As for raiding, yes, this is a definite weakness of the ability should you attempt to use it in a boss fight with lots of raid wide damage.
 3) Two of the druid's damaging abilities are instant casts (Moonfire/Insect Swarm). Again, hopefully this helps to explain why there isn't a knockback protection talent for the other two.
 4) Thanks to their diverse toolkit, druids are one of the best classes at creating space (should they need it) to more freely cast.

All of these points should hopefully make it clear that there is a reason(s) why druids weren't given a knockback reduction talent to Wrath/Starfire. If people but hell bent on including one, I would settle for it to be part of the Moonkin form. This way, you'd have to at least venture deep into the Balance tree if you wanted this.

The Barkskin talent seems to have been caught in the cross fire when discussing this particular point. Yes, it doesn't offer free knockback resistance but it still has uses and you've given a few examples. It's major strength is to defend against heavy incoming damage. Well, you can leverage that even more if you combine it with Bear form. From a healing perspective, if you're under attack (especially from faster hitting mobs or classes), trust me when I say that the 1s extra cast time is significantly less than the knockback you'd receive had you **not** used the ability.
