Hey,

I just talked with some people about an idea I had and at the moment I don't see a real downside so far.
What about if there are like 4 daily or maybe better weekly quests for Level 60 Characters:

 2 Quests for 5man Dungeons random from BRD upwards
 2 Quests for Open World random Level 50+ Areas

For those quests you could get a small amount of raid consumes (Bind on Pickup, not tradeable).
So raider would have an interest in running dungeons and doing stuff in the open world.
Keeping Dungeongroups and Open World alive and on the other side give people an alternative method to DM East Grinding all day. I think getting consumes for quests should be also as RPG be more fitting than just doing a grindfest every week.

What do you think about this? Did I not see any point, which makes this a bad idea? Would like to discuss about that.

Keep up the great work
So long, peace :)
