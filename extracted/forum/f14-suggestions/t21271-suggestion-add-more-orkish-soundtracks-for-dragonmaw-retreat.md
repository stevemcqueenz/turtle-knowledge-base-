# Suggestion: Add more orkish soundtracks for Dragonmaw Retreat

## Post 147555 by Nilay (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-21T19:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147555#p147555 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Dear TWoW Team,**

I finally got to run the Dragonmaw Retreat dungeon with a friend, and I absolutely loved its depth and sheer scale. Overall I really enjoyed the dungeon. It actually reminded me of the depths of Ironforge (“Old Ironforge”), and I really appreciated the scattered statues left behind by the dwarven remains. Still, just like with the orcs, I would have loved to see even more objects to enrich the atmosphere. A few extra huts, banners, and barricades would have made the Dragonmaw city feel much closer to its big sister Hordemar in Blackrock Spire, more objects, more atmosphere! And again !

Now, here comes my only real gripe:
 **The Music.**
It just doesn’t feel Orc enough.
Like, not at all.
It’s basically just emptysinistervoid.mp3.
Would totally 100% fit for Stormwind Vault or Karazhan Crypt, but for one of the last bastion of our beloved Dragonmaws?

I feel like that was a missed opportunity in the design. Each of the orc-filled platforms could have had something tied to the Second War.
