# New Lore master Quest chain

## Post 17066 by Robbietherobert — 2022-02-17T02:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17066#p17066 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

i had a idea for a new series of Caverns of Time Quests that requires players to go back in time and participate in several key events in Warcraft lore

these Quests will serve to educate players who have never played retail or any other warcraft games on the lore behind the series

the Quests included in this chain include

Siege of Shattrath- players participate in the Old horde's Siege of Shattrath during the genocide of the draenei

Siege of Karabor- players particapate in the Old horde's Siege of Karabor during the genocide of the draenei

opening of the Dark portal- Players must protect gul'dan as he powers the Dark portal

Siege of Goldshire and Stormwind city- players participate in the Old horde's Siege of Goldshire and Stormwind city during the First war

Culling of Stratholme- Players Aid Prince Arthas in the slaughter of the  plagued popluce of Stratholme while defending the prince from Mal'ganis and his Scourge minions

Seige of Silvermoon city- Players take part in the Scourge Attack of Silvermoon city

Seige of Dalaran- Players take part in the Scourge Attack of Dalaran

Freedom of Stormrange- Players participate in Tyrande wishperwind's assault on the burrow dens to free Illidan stormrange

the skull of gul'dan- players defend Illidan stormrange as he fights his way to consume the skull of gul'dan

Twilight of the Gods- Players fight alongside the Horde and Alliance against the undead scourge and the Burning Legion at mount hyjal

completing all these quests will award a new title called lore master of azeroth
