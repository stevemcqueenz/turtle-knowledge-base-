# Adjust Pet HP Regen.

## Post 29535 by Snigery (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-20T05:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29535#p29535 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Some of my thoughts on adjusting how pet resting HP regeneration OOC is handled. It feels odd, unbalanced, and just unnecessary how fast it replenishes.

 - Make the "Mend Pet" spell an instant cast w/ CD. (Other Hunters are already asking for this, it makes sense, strongly incentivizes the "Improved Mend Pet" talent.)
 - Adjust pet resting HP regeneration OOC to be more inline with players rates. (This will provide balance to the previous change, and it just makes sense. Hunters can pull pet OOC, restore 1/3 - 1/5 HP in couple ticks, and send pet back in. Does not make sense and feels like a gimmick.)
 - Adjust the "Feed Pet" spell so that a pet restores HP while feeding. (Further balancing. Basically we are removing the automatically high HP regen OOC and giving it to Feed Pet.)

I think these changes will help said spells and mechanics feel more whole and intentional.
