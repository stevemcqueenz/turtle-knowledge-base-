# Too much rainy weather.

## Post 153486 by Gathaspar — 2025-09-27T20:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153486#p153486 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello. I would like to suggest reducing the frequency of raining. It literally rains ALL THE TIME, everywhere. It makes the world grey and unpleasant. And it starts to be very annoying once you notice it. It's cool to have rain from time to time, but in most games the sunny weather is the default, and for a reason. It makes the world more cosy, more inviting and just more pleasant to be in and explore. I know about weather intensity settings, but even at the lowest value it still rains and is foggy and grey. It has been so annoying for me, that I personally am thinking of quiting just for this reason.

How do you guys feel about it, does it bother anyone else?

## Post 153492 by Gathaspar — 2025-09-27T21:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153492#p153492 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I just made a discovery by the way, that the weather is probably not server-wise but for each client separately. I logged in Elwynn Forest and it was heavily raining as usual. Then I went to Redridge where it was sunny, and came back to Elwynn. The weather somehow "reset" for me and I could finally enjoy a nice bright environment. I asked some people around about their weather and they all said it is heavily raining, so it reset only for my client.

If it is client-related than I guess there should be a technical way to implement a switch-off for this cursed rain or a mod. I don't know much of these things though.

Anyway, if you are looking for a solution then this may be a workaround, try going into different zones to reset your weather.
