# concept for improving Survival.

## Post 27880 by Schwa — 2022-10-25T04:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27880#p27880 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

the problem with survival is that it has level requirements which don't make sense.

the torch you can learn to make can give you +4 or +6 spirit and goes into an offhand slot. this makes sense for characters which are low. creating an offhand when you are sub 10, entirely makes sense. but by the time you are 20 you probably already have a permanent offhand or staff. and by the time you are 32, and going into stv you have much better loot. the prison torch is a permanent version which is better.

during an ancient ceremony atheletes would run across a tract of land called "marathon" while carrying a torch. to put this in game, have quests to run a gauntlet and pick up items on your way through, would create lots of flavor and give some sense to the time gating and the quality of the item. you could have someone near teh bridge to duskwood challenge players to run through duskwood to the flight master and to bring world items scattered allong the zone with them. give the torch a self buff which boosts xp gained from quests, and have it last only 5 minutes. bonus design objective, have people queue for it and run these gauntlets for buffs, consumables, and some xp.

this could eventually become a little kart racer themed game.
you start the race with a torch, and would get to pick up both unique consumables and markers for how far you've gone. consumables could have bonus healing, or temporary aspect of cheetah or similar effects.

by having a minigame, you create both a novel option for play, and you give value to crafting the item.
as it stands, the way to get to level up to tents is by crafting a large number of torches, which have no value. if i recall correctly they are soulbound, and have a time limit, and are vastly worse than anything you would naturally have when you get to the zone.

another way to improve it is similar to turtle mount, have it boost your party's spirit by half of your level. that makes it better by not making people binge to have it. as the items are fairly relevant while you level. this removes the motivation to just buy a bunch of torches and have it craft all and fill your inventory.
you could have different colored torches for different effects. they could buss like scrolls and totems and blessings. or you could have the lowest level torch be called "a burnt stick with an ember" and give 1/4 of your level as spirit. then at level 20 it could be 1/3 of your level as spirit and be called "smoking mosquito repeller", then anotehr version of it at 40 for 1/2 you level as spirit "smoldering torch". at 50 there could be "relentless beacon" for same spirit as creator's level.

another option would be to have the torch consumable if you have another tinder in your inventory. and if you use it, the torch is consumed and a mob currently attacking you disengages/resets if same level or lower.

a third option would be to have it so if you have a survival torch equiped, then you get the city buffs while being anywhere in the world.

abstractly, the design should be relevant for appropriately leveled characters, and it should scale up and down. being able to have better and better torches as you level up, and able to give them to lowbies, helps with community a bit.

some more fill out would be to have the weather effects give actual debuffs in game. like a weaker version of a survival game. tents are really leantos and instead of rested xp, you could have someone who rests get a determination buff which also eliminates weather debuffs.
