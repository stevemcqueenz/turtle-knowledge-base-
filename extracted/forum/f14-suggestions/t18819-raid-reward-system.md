# Raid Reward System

## Post 128055 by Kevinflynn — 2025-04-22T23:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128055#p128055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Hello, Turtle WoW Team & players!**

I'd like to suggest a raid reward system that I believe would be a valuable addition to the game — a reward mechanic inspired by the Badge of Justice system from The Burning Crusade.

Currently, even though all raid members invest their time and resources, only a few players receive loot after defeating raid bosses — the rest leave empty-handed. A system similar to Turtle Badges would ensure that everyone is rewarded regardless of drop luck or rolls. It would help reduce frustration (especially after multiple raids with no loot) and encourage players to keep participating in Raids, even if they already have the items they need.

 **Reward system for all players:**

After defeating each raid boss, players would receive a special currency — let’s call it for example *Turtle Badge*,
which could be exchanged at an NPC vendor for a range of rewards. (The badges & all rewards should be soulbound)

For example:

 - Cosmetic items (unique appearances, tabards, etc.)
 - Unique Titles
 - 18-slot bags
 - Mounts (purely visual, no speed advantage)
 - Companions
 - Discounts on /shop items (which might also encourage players to support the project)

 **Additional Reward System for Raid Leaders:**

To further encourage and recognize the efforts of raid leaders (RLs), I’d like to propose a special reward system that acknowledges their unique contribution to organizing and leading successful raids.

Awarded only to the raid leader (the one who formed the group and initiated the raid), after defeating each raid boss, RLs would receive a special additional currency — let’s call it *Shell Of War Badge* — which could be exchanged at an NPC vendor for a range of rewards. (The badges & all rewards should be soulbound)

For example:

 - Unique Titles only for RLs
 - Raid Banner of the guild, that can be placed
 - Raid Tattoo Marks — a replacement for the current green ground markers, perhaps featuring custom guild-themed visuals (but nothing too flashy to keep the game’s aesthetic intact)
 - Chat Name Color Customization (so people in world chat can recognize you better)
 - Summon Ready Check — an alternative to /readycheck that summons an animated turtle walking through the raid to check readiness in a thematic way
 - Raid Leader Hall of Honor (located in major cities) — an NPC that displays the names of RLs who have earned a significant number of Shell of War Badges, with a plaque showing their name, title, and guild (purely cosmetic/prestigious, no gameplay bonuses)

 **Core Principles for all rewards:**

 - Does not make the game easier
 - Does not affect the economy
 - Does not replace traditional raid loot
 - Rewards consistency and contribution
 - Keeps the Vanilla & Turtle WoW spirit

If implemented with care and lore-friendly flavor, this kind of system could breathe new life into the PvE scene and give players long-term goals beyond just loot drops.

What do you think ? I’d love to hear the thoughts of both the developers and the community  !   smiling_turtle

Hi !
Just wanted to clarify — this idea is focused purely on visual and cosmetic rewards. It doesn’t affect loot systems, balance, or the in-game economy in any way. The goal is simply to offer small, optional rewards that could help maintain motivation, especially in cases where RNG hasn’t been kind.

Speaking from personal experience, I’ve had long stretches of raiding with no loot at all. Even something small could really help players feel that their time and effort weren’t in vain.
It also helps ease cognitive dissonance — that frustrating feeling when you invest a lot but receive nothing in return. A small cosmetic reward can go a long way toward keeping players engaged and positive.

Thanks for your comment ! It's really important that you guys share your opinions – even if you're against the idea.
Every opinion matters here, because if most players don’t like the idea, then it’s better the devs don’t waste time on something people won’t enjoy. So any feedback is super valuable. Thanks <3
