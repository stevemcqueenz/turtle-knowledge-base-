# make +nature spell dmg also add +healing to nature based healing spells

## Post 115541 by Dr Thrax — 2025-01-05T21:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115541#p115541 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

+nature spellpower gear is a bit of an oddball/niche currently.
Only shamans can make a bit of use from it, or the some nature dmg hunter. Druids only have wrath and insect swarm, which doesnt make much sense compared to arcane or general spellpower.

What I don't get, is that said gear does affect offensive abilities, but not healing spells. one would think that if gear helps with a certain school of magic, also healing spells of that same school should benefit?

This can make + nature spellpower gear a bit more useful and not end up being vendored or DE-d as it can be more beneficial for druids and shamans.

Looks like an oversight of the developers to me, the same could actually hold true for +holy spellpower gear.
