Sounds nice. Considering that vanilla dungeons are a bit longer than tbc dungeons, I think that daily might be a bit too much, though. Maybe extend the duration to 2 or 3 days?

We might even consider call to arms quest for lower level dungeons, which can only be picked up in the corresponding level range but only be completed if nobody in the dungeon group exceeds the maximum level for that bracket. For example there could be a quest to kill all bosses in BFD, but only for a levelrange roughly between 20 and 30. The levelcap would prevent people from cheesing these quests with a lvl 60 boost. The reward should be something other than a lvl 60 raiding token, however.
