**Tailoring: Epic Robe Pattern for Priests with Spell Damage**
I don't really play Priest to the point of having one as an alt, but looking at the Atlas Loot, I notice that there is no Epic Robe Pattern with Spell Damage that is class-exclusive for Priests. There is one for healing, however, not the latter mentioned.

These class-exclusive epic robe patterns are equipable on level 57, have an ability unique to their own with the usefulness for each class, and are only exclusive to cloth-only bearing classes being Priest, Mage, and Warlock.

There should be one more Epic Robe Pattern for the Priest Class that, instead of Healing, consists of Spell Damage, and has an Active Ability useful for the Shadow Priest Role.

I might not be that bright when it comes to suggesting stats for the custom Robe, but here's my take.

Vestments of Twisted Faith
 96 Armor
 8 Stamina
 6 Intellect
 40 Spell Damage and Healing
Improves your chance to hit with spells increased by 1%
Use: Reduces your target's resistances to your spells by 30 for 1 minute. (10 minute cooldown)
