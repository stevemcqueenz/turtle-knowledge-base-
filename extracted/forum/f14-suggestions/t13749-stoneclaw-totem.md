Yeah, it could be a nice distraction tactic, but it would be too easy to work around it. For example, an addon could be made that detects when you are being taunted via the combat log and automatically switch your target to your previous one. Still, it would be an interesting mechanic that would surely spice up tanking in PvP.

Anyway, what the totem needs is a talent that redirects its taunt to the shaman. Or at the very least more hp and better chance to taunt, because more often than not mobs completely ignore it.
