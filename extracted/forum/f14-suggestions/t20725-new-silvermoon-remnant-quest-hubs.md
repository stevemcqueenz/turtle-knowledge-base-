**The Silvermoon Remnant – Quest Hub Concepts**
I’ve been thinking about where we could place additional quest hubs to expand the Silvermoon Remnant’s presence in the world. It's not easy getting rep with them, and there are currently several under-utilized areas that could see just a little bit more expansion in terms of quest content to make them fit into the world better.

These spots are chosen to feel lore-friendly, fit naturally into Classic zones, and give us interesting quest and RP opportunities without disrupting existing content- in fact, many of these places already have High Elf presence.

 **Major Quest Hubs**

 **1. Refugee Enclave – Stormwind (Dwarven District)**
Urban refugee community & political lobbying
• *Quest Hooks:*
– Gather food, fabrics, and medical supplies for refugees. (repeatable fish turn-in quest?)
– Investigate theft of Silvermoon refugees' cargo in the canals.
 - Deliver crates of Alah’Thalas crafts to a merchant in the Mage Quarter.
 - Retrieve a shipment of elven wine stuck in customs at the harbor, dealing with a stubborn dockmaster.
 - Track down the Defias who is trying to racketeer the refugees.
– Deliver messages to the Cathedral of Light to establish a connection between priesthoods.
 - A class quest that sends you to specific parts of the city as an envoy, depending on which class you are.

 **2. Quel’Danil Lodge – Hinterlands**
Ranger training & recruitment
• *Quest Hooks:*
– Hunt Witherbark trolls for stolen heirloom bows.
– Deliver trained hawkstriders to other outposts.
– Escort emigrants safely to Southshore.
 - Collect rare gryphon feathers to craft ceremonial arrows for an upcoming festival.

 **3. Dawnwatch Refuge – Border of Alah'thalas / Eastern Plaguelands**
Forward watch over the Scourge
• *Quest Hooks:*
– Scout and map clean ley line points.
 - Recover ashes of fallen Silvermoon soldiers from a ruined building and return them for proper burial.
– Purge undead threats from nearby forest glades.
– Escort masons and magisters to various mage stones to maintain them.

 **4. Azurewing Post – Wetlands**
Resource trade & shipping hub
• *Quest Hooks:*
– Protect caravans from Dragonmaw raiders.
 - Retrieve enchanted giant clam pearls from the sea floor near naga encampments.
– Make diplomatic connections with Menethil Harbor.
– Defeat a naga tidecaller who has been sinking merchant ships with conjured storms.

 **5. Eldara’s Vigil – Azshara**
Arcane archaeology in Highborne ruins
• *Quest Hooks:*
– Beat naga/satyr treasure hunters to sealed vaults.
– Translate ancient runic blueprints.
– Gather relics fragments for elven disenchanters.
 - Channel arcane energy at three unstable ley nodes using a provided focus crystal.

 **6. Feathermoon Envoy Post – Feralas**
Elven diplomacy & allied recruitment
• *Quest Hooks:*
– Aid in clearing naga from coastal ruins.
– Aid in studying and solving magical issues in Dire Maul.
 - Whole DM questline that give more lore attention to both elves' connection to the Highborne.
– Rescue captured scouts from the Isle of Dread.

 **7. Winterspring Lodge – Winterspring**
Rare material gathering & magical warding research
• *Quest Hooks:*
– Get involved in an ever-escalating tension between the Blue Dragonflight and the High elves.
– Gather spectral essences of the Highborne ghosts in the area.
– Test magical frost-wards in the field on yeti.

 *The Silvermoon Remnant may be small in number, but they carry the unbroken heart of Quel’Thalas – and will see its banners rise again.*

**The Long Road Home**
 *A Silvermoon Remnant quest chain spanning from Alah’Thalas to the farthest reaches of Azeroth*

---
The idea behind this quest chain is to provide continuity for High Elf players in visiting the various hubs of High Elf activity throughout Azeroth. You would get the quest to go from one enclave to another after completing one or more of the main quests in that hub. It doesn't give you a way to level up only in High Elf areas, but you don't really want that. What it does do well is make you feel connected to the diaspora of your people and their interests throughout the world.

---
 **Step 1 – Alah’Thalas → Stormwind**
 **Quest:** "South to Sanctuary"
 **Level Range:** 10–12

 **Objective:**
• Travel to Stormwind City and speak with the Silvermoon Ambassador in the Refugee Enclave (Dwarven District).

 **Narrative Hook:**
The Regent believes the Remnant’s survival depends on its ties to the Alliance. You are sent to Stormwind to meet the central emissary and aid the refugee network.

---
 **Step 2 – Stormwind → Loch Modan**
 **Quest:** "Winter’s Welcome"
 **Level Range:** 12–15

 **Objective:**
• Travel to Loch Modan and speak with the Innkeeper to be directed to High Elf settlers in the area.

 **Narrative Hook:**
They explain that a small contingent of Silvermoon refugees has been living in Loch Modan. Supplies are running short, and they’ve requested assistance.

---
 **Step 3 – Loch Modan → Wetlands**
 **Quest:** "Through the Pass"
 **Level Range:** 15–20

 **Objective:**
• Escort a dwarven caravan carrying high elven goods through to Azurewing Post in the Wetlands.

 **Narrative Hook:**
The Frostmantle enclave is trading forged goods for rare arcane components from Azurewing Post. Escort duty introduces you to the Wetlands hub and the naga threats along its coast.

---
 **Step 4 – Wetlands → Hinterlands**
 **Quest:** "Feathers of the North"
 **Level Range:** 30–35

 **Objective:**
• Deliver an urgent message to the Ranger-Captain  at Quel’Danil Lodge in the Hinterlands.

 **Narrative Hook:**
Azurewing’s scouts have sighted troll movements that threaten Quel’Danil’s hunting parties. This connects players to the ranger enclave.

---
 **Step 5 – Hinterlands → Feralas**
 **Quest:** "Southwind Accord"
 **Level Range:** 40–45

 **Objective:**
• Carry a diplomatic letter to the Feathermoon Envoy Post in Feralas.

 **Narrative Hook:**
The Hinterlands rangers have heard about the recent stirrings in Dire Maul and wish to send someone to check on it.

---
 **Step 6 – Feralas → Winterspring**
 **Quest:** "Frostfire Pact"
 **Level Range:** 50–52

 **Objective:**
• Bring a sealed satchel of arcane relics to Loremaster Veyran Snowglade at the Winterspring Lodge.

 **Narrative Hook:**
Feralas has unearthed magical relics in danger of falling into Horde hands; they must be safeguarded by the remote Winterspring lodge.

---
 **Step 7 – Winterspring → Eastern Plaguelands**
 **Quest:** "Final March"
 **Level Range:** 54–56

 **Objective:**
• Travel to Dawnwatch Refuge in the Eastern Plaguelands and deliver intelligence on Scourge movements to Justicar Selyra Dawnwatch.

 **Narrative Hook:**
The Winterspring lodge has intercepted magical omens of a coming Scourge assault. The information must reach the Eastern Plaguelands defenses.

---
 **Step 8 – Finale – Dawnwatch Refuge → Alah’Thalas**
 **Quest:** "The Regent’s Summons"
 **Level Range:** 56–58

 **Objective:**
• Return to High Regent Selathis in Alah’Thalas.
• Participate in a scripted defense event against a Scourge vanguard at the gates.

 **Rewards:**
• Unique tabard or title
• +Reputation with Silvermoon Remnant (if any is needed to get to exalted)
• Choice of rare-quality high elf weapon or armor
