# Reduce the mana cost of  buffs abilities  for classes like Mage Priest and Druid

## Post 94276 by Yzzzzzz — 2024-05-08T01:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94276#p94276 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The abilities like Arcane Intellect and Arcane Brilliance for the Mage class, Power Word: Fortitude for the Priest class, and Mark of the Wild for the Druid class consume a significant amount of mana. In a 40-person raid setting, frequent use of these skills requires a considerable amount of time to replenish mana. This leads to substantial downtime for the raid team. It is suggested to reduce the mana cost of such spells or modify them into group-wide abilities that can provide the desired effect to the entire raid at once.  turtle_in_love_head
