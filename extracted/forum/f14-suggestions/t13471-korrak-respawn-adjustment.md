Turtle WoW runs 1.5 version of AV where Korrak the Bloodrager can be killed unlike in popular patch 1.12.
Currently the problem with Korrak is that he spawns instantly after AV starts and when he is killed, he respawns no more which was not normal back in the day at least looking at some sources.

My suggestion is to adjust his spawn in Turtle WoW so he also spawns randomly within 2 hours. I think this change would be good especially for PVP playerbase that plays AV because we are often annoyed when some people try to kill Korrak for 30 minutes without result (because they suck) instead of helping at front line.
