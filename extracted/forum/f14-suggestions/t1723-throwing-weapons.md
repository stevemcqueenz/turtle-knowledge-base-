## Post 10168 by Nornvari — 2021-04-19T20:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10168#p10168 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Okay so, i want to say this would be a revival project, but honestly? you can't revive what is dead. So lets just go with raw necromancy here(totally not planning that thread sometime or anything).

So, Throwing weapons. What do they do? the occasional rogue proc when rooted or bored....and that's about it? Trolls have one of their racials focused on the use of throwing weapons, but finding throwing weapons with stats, let alone GOOD stats is almost impossible....and lets not forget, they don't have any functionality with the vast majority of ranged abilities.

Maybe its time to change that? Its not like it would have to be considered horde-centric(though the benefits for trolls finally getting good use of that racial is obvious ofc) since there are fighters in the alliance who specialize with such weapons in lore, like Huntresses and Gryphon Riders. If throwing weapons became useable with ranged abilities(I.E hunter shots etc) with maybe a range penalty if untalented(?) it would open up a little more variety.

What do yo guys think? Time to start rolling ninjas?   insidious_turtle  Or just for me to go back into my shell?  maintenance_turtle
