I'd like to suggest either adding proc % chances on the tooltip of items in-game, or allow the database to show the % chances on the proc spell page

Example 1: Destiny (2h Sword) - 200 Strength "Chance on Hit" proc - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=647> (Does not show % chance on tooltip)

Example 2: Destiny's proc buff - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=17152> (Does not show % chance on spell details)

This would help anyone to determine which weapon or item is more worthwhile than another, especially Ret Paladins who use Seal of the Crusader.
