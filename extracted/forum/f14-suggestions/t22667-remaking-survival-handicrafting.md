# Remaking Survival: Handicrafting

## Post 157850 by Voltaxian — 2025-10-30T15:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157850#p157850 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello! Yes, I know there will be a soon update to survival and its workings, but well, I'd like to just leave my suggestion here.

As context, Survival rn is basically making tents or fishing boats, period. That's not incredibly survivalistic, much less because of the fact that nearly all the materials you need to craft them are bought from a vendor, not off the wild like a survivalist would do. So as of now it may as well be called Tenting or something like that. The idea is good, but well, the skill is a bit lacking. I am not sure what they are going to do about this, but here's my take on the remade skill:

Handicrafting: A skill you use to craft convenient little things. It would properly encompass tents, boats, and any and all sort of addons for professions. Build a cooking rack, a small smelter or anvil, a tanning rack, a grinding stone, an enchanting table, a tailoring bench, etc. Each building granting a small bonus to the profession they affect, requiring of course a material from each of those professions to be crafted, which one can make oneself, alongside stone, wood or rope from any trade goods vendor, since it is basically just furniture. Each of these things depend on a rather more normal profession like carpentry or masonry, which can be easily collated into handicrafting, the crafting of useful things.

Need to craft something in the middle of nowhere? What would you choose:
 1) Moving your hands while hunching over like you are devising some evil plan.
 2) Building a small working station and crafting some product while devising some evil plan.
