## Post 110784 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-12-01T13:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110784#p110784 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1st person camera was clearly an afterthought in WoW. It is very janky and not fun to use.

I don't know if it's possible to do as a mod, or an official extension once we get the UE5 client, but an expanded 1st person mode would be a great addition to the game. This should include:

 -Rotating your character AND the camera with the mouse. Your cursor/crosshair would be locked in the middle of the screen and would be unlock-able by holding a modifier key like Alt.

 -1st person player hands models - unique for each race/gender and each druid form. These should match your skin tone and change depending on what chestpiece, shirt, bracers or gloves you're wearing, as well as display what weapon you're currently using. They need to be animated for each weapon attack, cast (with different particles depending on spell school), etc. Not a small amount of work.

 -Better directional audio. Since in 1st person you see much less than 3rd person, you need some way to know if something is happening next to or behind you (like a mob scratching at your backside). Good directional audio would help tremendously and add to the immersion.

 -Maybe add VR support? Combined with all of the above, playing WoW in VR would be a quite new and fresh experience, even for wow veterans. This is easier said than done however, since the abovementioned player hands models need to be rigged to the controllers and we'd need a good controller scheme to play wow with only the limited amount of buttons on the handheld VR controllers.

The fact you're asking for it to be a "challenge mode" shows how inferior of an experience the current 1st person mode is to playing "normally". These suggestions I've outlined would likely never see the light of day, but it is nice to dream.
