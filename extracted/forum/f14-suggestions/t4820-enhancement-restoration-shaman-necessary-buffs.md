# Enhancement/Restoration Shaman Necessary Buffs

## Post 28912 by Neltharion (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-10T19:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28912#p28912 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As someone who mained shamans for years, I felt it was necessary to share my opinions about shamans on Turtle WoW.
The shaman is a Class which has been left in the dark to take dust since the class update, making paladins, their Alliance counterpart, one, if not the best class in the game, for its insane utility and his now incredibly high damage.
And no, racial spells were pretty bad, so they don't really count.

These are the changes I'd recommend for our fellow shamans

ENHANCEMENT:
They lack damage, highly gear dependant. The only time they find themselves useful is when they're in bis gear with Sulfuras.
This is why I suggest a passive ability, called Thundering Strikes [Each time the shaman lands a spell on the enemy, attack speed is increased by 5%, stacking up to 5 times for 15s]. This ability will help the shaman to fish for WF procs on longer fight.
They also lack on 1v1 tools, such as stuns and root removers, and I think Earthquake Totem [Occasionally stuns all nearby enemy targets for 3s, disarming them for 5s. The totem lasts for 1 minute and the proc I believe should be around every 20s] and Freedom of the Wind Totem [Removes impairing (root and slow) effects on all friendly targets around the target every 5s. The Totem lasts for 1 minute] will solve the problem.
Since I want to have mercy on the shaman's mana pool I think they should have a fair mana cost.

TOTEM ADJUSTEMENTS:
Shamans have a lot of useless totems and that's why I think some of them should be united as one or buffed:
 - Mana and health regen totems should be the same totem;
 - Stoneskin and Windwall totems should also the same totem;
 - Flametongue totem and Flametongue weapon should be for caster users, making Flametongue weapon increase SP by 7/15/26 and crit chance by 1/2/3%;
 - Stoneclaw Totem should work as a protector totem aggroing enemies but also giving a 148/243/321 HP shield to the nearby totems.

Before commenting that does changes are too much, remember that totems CAN and SHOULD be taken down asap.
Let me know what you think!   smiling_turtle_head

I agree that shaman is in a poor spot especially when compared to paladin, however i do not agree with the supposed buffs to enhancement.

Enhancer has never been a class that excelled in personal DPS. Its in the name, he ENHANCES those around him. Since he has enough damage for solo content, i don't think buffing his personal damage is the way to go. He has a unique role to fill in raids with totemsuport and nightfall.

In order for shaman to be good, first his totems need to be useful. Thankfully it looks like they will revert the stacking nerf (see: [viewtopic.php?f=2&t=4232&start=40&hilit=worldbuff](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=4232&start=40&hilit=worldbuff)) What they can do if they want to incentivise groups to specifically run enhancers is make the Windfury totem enhancer only. Then you have a very compelling reason to bring them to raids. And ironically flametongue totem would get some use since thats all Ele & Resto could provide.

But thats a shaman nerf, not a buff.

If they actually want to buff enhancer, i think the first stop should be to allow Spirit wolf to break slows & roots. After all, its a shapeshift just like druid. So why does it not remove cc effects. Its not even nearly as abusable since it has casttime. Its way more natural and easy to implement than any new fancy totems aiming to do a similar thing. Also, anti CC totems are just a poor design choice. By their nature anti CC spells should allow you to move freely, while totems are all about staying in one area.

The mana issue is indeed something that is incredibly annoying for enhancement shaman. Enhancer is actually in the unique situation that Freecast would be extremely good. One of the reasons enhancer has so much mana problems despite casting few spells is the fact that his spells have just enough cooldown to always block him from regenerating mana due to the 5 second rule.
But if you have a freecast, you would get a full 6 seconds where your mana regeneration gets to work until the next shock is ready.

Another thing that holds shamans back in PvE is the fact that bosslevel screws enhancer more than most classes. He has to deal with a whopping 17% spell misschance against bosses while being melee, and all his melee hits including WF proccs are subject to glancing blows.
Paladin has similar problems, but at least his SoC doesn't glance.
Some way to bypass his shitty spellhit chance & a way to counteract glancings are vital.
As a possible example, a brothertalent to elemental devastation would be nice, where melee critical hits increase spell hit chance by 9% for 10 seconds

Also due to his role in raids being a provider of buffs and debuffs, the debuff limit on bosses should be completely removed if possible or at least extended to very high numbers. Allowing Nightfall, Annihilator buffs etc that shamans can apply to stay on. And of course, some extra weapons that provide these tactical debuffs certainly won't hurt.
