The retail goblin discount works by giving you a discount as if you were exalted, and its a 5% discount per rep category, maximum 20% at exalted.

In classic however, you get a flat 10% discount for honored, and then another flat 10% discount for being pvp rank 3 or higher, for a total of 20%, but only for merchants and vendors who were a part of your race's faction.

The goblin racial never allowed you to get a HIGHER discount than other players, it just allowed you to access that discount more easily. If there was to be something like that implemented, then it would probably just apply to making you be treated as honored with every faction you meet, for discount purposes only.

But that being said, I'm not sure the whole "goblin greed" lore really exists pre-cataclysm. Goblins were more techies and mercenaries, not so much moneygrubbing, gold obsessed racial stereotypes, so I'm not certain there's lore precedence for goblins getting such a racial ability, considering that turtle wow intentionally ignores all lore post vanilla.
