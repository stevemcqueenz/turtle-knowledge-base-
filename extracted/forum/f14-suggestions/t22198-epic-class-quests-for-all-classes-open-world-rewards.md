# Epic Class Quests FOR ALL CLASSES  & Open World Rewards

## Post 154023 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-02T11:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154023#p154023 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello again and again, etc. Turtle WoW team,

I think it would be amazing to add more open world systems that encourage exploration and long-term goals. For example:

Epic class quests: In Classic, only Hunters and Priests got their own raid quests in Molten Core. In SoD, each class had a legendary-style quest at Blackwing Lair, which gave players a unique and memorable experience. Turtle could expand on this idea, giving every class a true epic journey that fits their lore and playstyle.

World-based progression: Instead of limiting progression to dungeons and raids, players could discover hidden powers, items, or abilities through exploration and long quest chains across Azeroth. These wouldn’t need to be overpowered — just flavorful, fun, and rewarding for those who dive deep into the world.

This kind of system would:

Make the open world feel more dynamic and alive.

Give every class unique goals to strive for, beyond just gear.

Stay true to Turtle’s “immersive Vanilla+” philosophy, without copying retail features directly.

I believe this would really enrich the game and give players more reasons to explore Azeroth outside of raids and battlegrounds.

Thanks for considering this idea too !
