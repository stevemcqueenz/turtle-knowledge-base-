# Suggestion: Ambermill Clean-up

## Post 113445 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-19T23:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113445#p113445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The two-story building next to the stables in Ambermill in inexplicably dilapidated. While one of the farmhouses in the town is a ruined wreck, the wizards don't actually us it because of it's state; meanwhile, the two-story is still in good enough state that the Dalaran wizards still actually use it, yet it's jarring when compared to the other inhabited buildings in Ambermill that it be in such bad shape while being in active use.
