# PvP brackets improved dampening.

## Post 166165 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-23T23:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166165#p166165 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

After reading about the new dampening they are doing per spec or class I thought about making lower levels have a bigger dampening in their brackets so they stand more of a chance.

It would be 1% dampening per lower level they are than the bracket (+ the standard 20% you get right now) so

Example for 20-29 bracket etc I won't list them all but you get the idea.

Lvl 20
+29% dampening

Lvl 21
 28% dampening

Lvl 22
 27% dampening

Lvl 25
+24% dampening

Lvl 29
 20% dampening (current)

So this would give lower levels a better chance to compete if they have bigger dampening than the higher levels.

Also I would reduce the spell hit penalty in PvP. As right now a Lvl 20 has like 25% chance to miss with spells against a Lvl 29.
Which punishes casters way too much in PvP compared too melee.
