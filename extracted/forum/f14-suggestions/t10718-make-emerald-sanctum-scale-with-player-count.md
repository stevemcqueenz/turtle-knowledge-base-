# Make Emerald Sanctum scale with player count

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69843#p69843 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, just attempted Emerald Sanctum with my guild and we were super sad to see that Emerald Sanctum no longer scales with player count. Why would this raid be the only one that does not scale with player count? Our guild is smaller and we couldn't get enough people attuned in the first few days. Now it seems that a stealth update has removed the playercount scaling before we could even attempt it. We cleared all the trash, leaving Errenius and Solnius. We saw that they had 1.12 million and 2.4 million HP respectively. Attempting the fight with our 20 man group proved to be fruitless, once we got to the add phase for Solnius it became apparent that we were not going to finish the raid.

Why would this raid be the only one that doesn't scale down for player count? We actually can't enjoy the new content as a guild because of this, we'll have to find pugs seperately next reset... What's the rationale behind this? Seems like a really questionable decision having the exciting new custom content be the stuff that's gated behind having a full 40 man raiding team. Please bring back the scaling for Emerald Sanctum

## Post 69844 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-10-10T04:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69844#p69844 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

ES scaling was never intended.

I will quote what I said to another player:

> We've been dialing back on scaling amounts for most content since population is mostly no longer an issue. We want to encourage the social aspect of raiding by accepting more raid members.
>
>  More importantly though, we want to show players that especially for new custom raid content this is the way it's going to be going forward. This has an extra reason which is that while simple HP and damage numbers are scalable, some mechanics that are intended for x people aren't quite that scalable in that same way. This means that by doing this we can test and tweak our new upcoming raids exactly the way it will be played on live servers too.

This is really bad news for pretty much anyone that wants to play endgame and isn't already in a guild with all raids on farm. To the dev team it may seem that the server population is big enough to support this now, but the server population is international with players in every time zone. Getting a full 40man raid group to get together multiple nights a week is simply not feasible. Even in 2005 there were only a handful of guilds actually doing endgame raid content on high pop servers (~6000 players), if these changes go through there will only be a few guilds per region actually able to do raids past MC tier. Smaller guilds will not be able to keep up and recruitment is not easy for a progressing guild. I really hope the Turtle team changes their mind on this decision, they may think that this change will increase player interaction or some BS but it's going to do the opposite, you're just going to be gating off end game content to most of your players. You're going to create an environment just like 2005 vanilla, where you have to join a guild that is already clearing things or you just don't get to raid outside of pugs where friendships are rarely made and progression halts at BWL. The guild I'm in will die if this change goes through and I know most other small/medium sized guilds will go as well.

I get that it's easier to test balance for custom content this way, but I'd rather have imbalanced content that I can do with my friends than balanced content that we can't do. I think most players would agree with me.
