I would like to suggest a quest for Alliance to be able to get [Nat Pagle's Extreme Angler FC-5000](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19022), as a mirror to the Horde quest in Hinterlands, [Snapjaws, Mon!](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=7815)

Starts from [Brannock](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=7946) in Feralas.
Quest level 50, minimum 44
[Groddoc Thunderers](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=5262) drop Aged Groddoc Giblets (70%). Bring back 12

Quest text:
Hello friend! May I ask for your help? The naga to the south have been over-fishing the area, and it is becoming difficult to get enough fish for the Stronghold. I may have to start angling for other types of fish, but I don't have the right type of bait to entice them. The innards of the apes to the north of here would make the perfect type of bait for me.

In-progress:
I await your return with bated breath.

Quest completion:
Ah! These will be perfect! Here, take this fishing pole. It washed up along the shore, presumably dropped by another adventurer.

Quest rewards:
[Nat Pagle's Extreme Angler FC-5000](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19022)
 4700 exp.
 100 Reputation with [Darnassus](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?faction=69)
