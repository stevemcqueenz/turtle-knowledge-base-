## Post 155151 by Bluegoblin — 2025-10-11T12:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155151#p155151 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Instead of throwing tusks on something and making it colorful and calling it a day, lets reuse those awesome animal forms them bosses have!

During Cata, Blizzards reason for troll druids was disappointing as all heck, I expected some unique route involving Troll Dance Rituals, Magic Mushrooms, Weird Punchbowls With Questionable Ingredients, and even more Magic Mushrooms. Butt they missed that mark by miles and really screwed over the Dancing Troll Village in the process. Instead of Moonglade, they'd go to Shatterspear village where they uncovered ancient dance rituals and old Dark Troll moon voodoo they stole from the elves.

To turn into a glorious bear we won't use any old hippy elf tricks, we'll go to Amani'alor and talk to Forest Troll Loa to become a groovy Amani black battle bear (because the black bear is the best bear)

Obviously there are the awesome red Doomkin from Hinterlands as the glorious boomkin form, we'd have to throw some tusks onto that one.
