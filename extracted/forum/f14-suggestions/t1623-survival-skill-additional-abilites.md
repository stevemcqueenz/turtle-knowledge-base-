I really like the idea of survial skill, but i feel there could be a lot more done with it.

Right now its just Spam Torches to get tent, which is fine and all, but i think there can be much more.  Here are a few ideas i have for it.

 1-  Scavaging:  Cooldown 10 minute, Tool required: Hatchet "new item"
Upon "cast" you scavage the area for useful items. There can be a list of loot that it can gather. The items and
usefulness could be based on player level/ Survival level/fishing level... ect. Which can change the overall loot table
 -Ores/gems
 -Herbs
 -Rocks
 -wood
 -Linen Cloth
 -Fishing Worms
 -Trash gray gear/possible "decent gear"
 * If you are a hunter there could also be Meat/fruit added for the pet to the loot tables.
 -ect

 2- Forage: Cooldown 1 minute: Similar to the mage conjure,  you forage for "Food/Water" based on player level and survival skill level.
The water can be Spring water and food could be Mushrooms of differant  qualities based on the skill level/player level.
