# Tradable Raid Loot

## Post 154128 by Thepetterofcats — 2025-10-02T23:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154128#p154128 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,
So Im coming from Official realms and in Anniversary realms they made it so that loot dropped is tradable for 2 hours within the same raid group.

As a leader of PUGs I would so love this feature for 2 reasons:

Firstly, just because its easier to do loot like that, gather everything up, roll it out at the end.

Secondly, it discourages puggers leaving the raid after their items did not drop so they stay until the end when all loot will get rolled out.

I may understand how you dont want to implement that so people cant trade loot they and sell it for gold, but if you make it so that its only tradable once (like it is right now, just increase the timer so it lasts long enough until the end of raid), that would counteract that.

I hope you take it into consideration and hopefully even implement it! :)
