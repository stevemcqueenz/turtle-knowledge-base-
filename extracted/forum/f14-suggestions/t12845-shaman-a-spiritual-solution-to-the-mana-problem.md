# Shaman: A Spiritual Solution to the Mana Problem

## Post 85716 by Terramagnus — 2024-01-23T17:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85716#p85716 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

TL;DR: Make Shaman's Clearcasting act like Druid's Clearcasting

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Shaman in both Elemental and Enhancement have little in the way of mana recovery, and it is a major factor that leads to the class feeling sluggish, relying on burst to be effective.

Shamans in either of those specializations have no mana regeneration talents or abilities outside of the mana spring, which provides meager amounts of mana to the individual and is meant to show its usefulness when affecting a group of players. Their spellkit is based around abilities with short cooldowns being used generally as often as possible, which in turn prevents Spirit-based mana regeneration from being utilized. This, I believe, is the source (and solution) of the Mana Problem.

One of the main talents those specs use to conserve mana is "Elemental Focus".

Elemental Focus is a talent which, upon landing a crit with any spell or melee, will provide you a 12 second buff with 2 stacks; This buff will reduce the cost of your next damaging spell (Lightning and Shock spells) by 40%. You can of course do this twice as there are 2 stacks.

What is interesting is that this buff is referred to has "Clearcasting", but operates completely differently from the traditional Clearcasting we see on Mages and Druids, and I think this is what makes it so surprisingly ineffective.

The main benefit of traditional Clearcasting is not the reduced mana cost, but the fact that the mana cost becomes *zero*: this means that the spell *does not interrupt spirit-based mana regeneration*.

I believe that if Elemental Focus was modified to allow Spirit regen like with traditional Clearcasting, it would give Elemental and Enhancement a source of much-needed recovery.

As far as I am aware, this does not necessitate that the spell's cost be reduced to 0, and if it were, Elemental Focus would have to be rewritten to account for this.

I believe that Elemental Focus should more closely resemble the Clearcasting that Druids obtain- A single buff that reduces or removes mana cost of the spell, being activated via a **Chance on Crit**, perhaps 20 or 33%. This allows it to scale with crit, but without reaching a "cap" of effectiveness so easily as we see with the current Elemental Focus, which I found can be kept at nearly 100% uptime well before getting geared.
