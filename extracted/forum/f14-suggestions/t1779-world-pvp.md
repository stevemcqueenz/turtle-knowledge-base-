## Post 10638 by Pahacsinta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-05-22T23:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10638#p10638 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtles!

I just wanted to give my thoughts on world pvp and how it could be possible, I think it would make a great addition to Turtle WoW.
The first thing we need to think about, is that we shouldn't hurt the HC community - that's why my solution would be that only end game zones get flagged for pvp with weekly rotations (Like 1st week Eastern Plaguelands, 2nd week Winterspring, 3rd week Silithus).
In my experience: I was a hunter and I could get to lvl 60 without doing much in Plaguelands at all, so it shouldn't be a problem (or you could wait 1 week for the rotation).
The second requires a bit more work on it but if this suggestion happens we need something to motivate players to go there in addition; like in WotLK Wintergrasp there could be weekly quests and additional currency for custom mounts, pets and of course honor.

Probably its not possible but I would like to hear your opinion!
