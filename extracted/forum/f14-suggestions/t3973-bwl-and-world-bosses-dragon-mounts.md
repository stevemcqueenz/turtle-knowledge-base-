# BWL and World Bosses Dragon Mounts

## Post 24029 by Neltharion (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-30T22:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24029#p24029 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know what some of the devs said about "flying mounts" here in Turtle WoW: they don't really fit and they're forced in vanilla
I think the way to integrate them into the game would be making them obtainable through hard work and a bit of luck. Like us Vanilla enjoyers are used to.
Also, keep in mind that we have still a good amount of flying mounts ingame, such as two hippogryphs in store, gryphon from wildhammer reputation, bronze drake from Black Morass reputation and to be honest, if we want to talk about it, we have the TBC mount from the Sethekk Halls, which really doesn't fit the vanilla theme.
Anyway, my idea would be to bring back three dragon mounts: vanilla is very populated by dragons, the black dragonflight in particular.
ZG and BWL have their own mounts so why BWL shouldn't?
Here's my idea:
 - Reins of the Uncorrupted Black Drake: 1% drop from Razorgore the Untamed;
 - Chromatic Egg: 0.5% drop from Nefarian, which starts a medium/long questline which gives, in the end, the beloved Chromatic Drake mount, which was even in the files! (in the quest the player will learn how to cleanse the Egg freeing the new born creature from the blackrock corruption).

I was also thinking about world bosses:

 -Reins of the Green Dream-Drake: 1% drop from Lethon, Ysondre, Taerar and Emeriss;
 -Reins of the Blue Mana-Drake: 1% drop from Azuregos.

What do you think?
Let me know there, it would be nice to see if you'd like this!
