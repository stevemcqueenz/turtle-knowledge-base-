# Fixing the Auction House

## Post 49296 by Matches (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-06-08T10:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49296#p49296 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1. In Turtle Wow, auction durations have been extended.  I'm not sure by how much, but they are much longer than Vanilla.

 2. The use of Auction House add-on is reasonably common.  This allows players to create large numbers of auctions, where all auctions created in one batch will have the same price.

When a player using an add-on produces a large number of auctions for a slow-selling item, they set a long-lasting price floor.  There might be, say, 20 sniper scopes in the AH, all at say 1.7g.  No one else is going to sell anything, and for days, because the auction durations have been extended.

This is breaking the market for sellers, and in the longer term, this breaks the market for buyers.

The AH originally, I would say, was designed for use without any thought to add-ons.

Players were expected to make auctions manually - so any given player could only make a few auctions, because they take time and effort to produce.

This prevents any player from flooding the AH with under-priced auctions.

We also see the problem where sociopathic players produce many hundreds of auctions of single units of a item, such as silk cloth, as an exorbitant price, trying to force players to buy because clicking through 1000+ auctions to get to the normal auctions is too time consuming.

You only need a few player like that to cause major problems.

When I last looked, two days ago, there were 1351 auctions for silk cloth.

I could not buy silk cloth without using an add-on, to get rid of the problem caused by the use of add-ons - but the use of add-ons still then leaves us with the problem of floods of under-priced items slow-selling items.

The AH fee for placing an auction does not deter this behaviour, as can be seen empirically, and the AH was better when there was no fee; I auction most weapons and armour, because the chance of a sale is always hit-and-miss, and the high AH fee on weapons and armour means it makes more sense to vendor.

I propose;

 1. No player can create more than 5 auctions for the same item, at the same time (regardless of pricing).  This can easily be automatically monitored, and any offending auctions automatically removed.

 2. The AH fee is removed.

 3. Auction durations return to the same duration as Vanilla.

Yes and no.

There is a problem with massive numbers of single unit auctions, and this would be solved by limiting the number of auctions of a given item on a per player basis.

This however is a very obvious, very simple problem.

The real problem I'm looking to solve is that dumps of large numbers of slow-selling items, combined with the very long auction times, are having the effect of very strongly lowering prices, and making it difficult for prices to return to their normal level.

Looking at sniper scopes right now, I've only been selling scopes at a low price, and not very often, for many days; and someone recently dumped a bunch on at about 1g 50s, which is too low to sell at.

Those scopes have been selling, and have after some days cleared, but there's still about seven or eight scopes, all at a still too-low price, put on by one seller, which have yet to time out.

If someone else in the *days* which remains of those underpriced scopes being on the market dumps another bunch of scopes on the market (levelling eng, say), then they will pick a lower price and once again, the market must now wait another three days to reset to normal.

The market is broken by very long auctions of large-scale dumps of slow-selling items, and this problem exists because of add-ons, and the long auction time.

The add-on problem is solve by the 5 item limit, the market reset is solved (at least ameliorated) by moving back to normal auction durations.

I've heard quite often people, without thinking about it at all, by reflex say "use an add-on".

If I use an add-on, I solve the problem of there being 1341 auctions for silk, but I make the problem of bulk auctions of slow-selling items worse (because now everyone has an add-on and can emit bulk auctions), and it does nothing for the problems induced by long auctions.

I must also mention when I've used AH add-ons, they have been very slow, where they have to read so much data from the AH where there are so many auctions.  The problem they are supposed to fix becomes worse when everyone has AH add-ons, making the AH add-ons even slower at the fix they provide.  This is not the way to go.

One point here which I suspect has been overlooked.

I have argued long auctions make it hard for prices to return to normal.

This is a problem for both prices being too low *and* too high.

When prices are too high, and particularly so when an item is fairly rare, when someone comes to market with no idea of the price and asks for say 400g for a level 40 purple, what you then see is that this prices sits in the market, for ages, and in that time another drop will occur, and someone else who doesn't know the normal price, will put in on the AH at just a bit less.

It can take one or two weeks for this to pass.

If auctions ended in one day, it would be far less of a problem, because the crazy high price would have a much better chance of leaving the market before the next item of that type arrives.

Of course, you then have to see if the next seller has a reasonable idea of pricing or not - but at least you have stopped having a crazy price in the AH, which will never sell, as guidance.
