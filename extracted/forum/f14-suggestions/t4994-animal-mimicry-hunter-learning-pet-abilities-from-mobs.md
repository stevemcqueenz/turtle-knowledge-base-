I posted  this idea already once, but I've refined it a little bit, I think it would be a really fun and flavorful addition to the class. Of course I have to say that I have no idea what programmers are able to do in a private server, so the specifics on how it could work are just a suggestion. The idea is that now the Hunter can learn pet abilities themselves, directly from mobs in the world instead of a trainer, to round out their kit and cover some bases that they usually don't. Like a charge for a melee hunter, a growl and defensive CD for a meme hunter tank, some various buffs and attacks they can use even if they have a different pet out. I think the Blue Mage-esque nature of this would be pretty cool personally.

When I suggested this last, it was said that the Hunter shouldn't be able to do things that don't fit their biology, which is why I left things like Claw and Bite out. But I don't have an issue with it personally, because it's easy for me to say they're just intuitively tapping into some druidic nature magic.
