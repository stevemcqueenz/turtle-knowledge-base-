# PVP imbalance is harming the server.

## Post 30431 by Artofwill (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-12-10T23:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30431#p30431 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey guys I'm just a some what casual player here so definitely not a sweaty serious pvper but I do enjoy the pvp aspect of the game along with the raiding.

I've been playing on this server for a few months now and have quite enjoyed my time here.

That out of the way I'd like to discuss the topic at hand which is faction pvp imbalance.

I personally don't have too much issues with classes being imbalanced as it's difficult to balance while maintaining the unique identity of each classes. However that doesn't mean the pvp aspect of the game that is battle grounds should remain just as imbalanced. (Wpvp doesn't exist because RPPVE)

The most obvious example are paladins, they were already extremely powerful in vanilla, their only "weakness" being they lacked reliable burst damage, and with the changes to them on this server, they have become WTLK Death knight level strong.
I think having such a strong class in the game is fine as it allows new players to feel like they can play a powerful classes without the high skill floor. But if you maintain the faction based pvp system in battle grounds it becomes extremely imbalanced and will drive away the already dwindling players that enjoy playing pvp.

There are also many simple options to address this problem. One of the most elegant being cross faction bgs. I'm sure this has been brought up by many and have all been turned down in the past. But besides technical challenges, I fail to see any legitimate reason to not implement this, especially when we already have cross faction play anyways. This change can address the faction imbalance, class imbalance, in one simple sweep, not needing any additional changes.

I'm not one to cry about a few losses in battle grounds, but if anyone's played bgs consistently, they would realize the serious impact paladins have. And I'd like to keep them as strong as they are but still have a chance for a fair match making experience.

To reiterate, the biggest reason I bring it up is because this level of imbalance is a really poor game experience for one faction of the pvpers. Eventually those players get burnt out by it and quit. Hurting the server population overall which I don't see it being good for anyone.

For those if you who may think I'm exaggerating the problem, I highly encourage you to roll a horde toon and experience it for your self. I've noticed more and more horde friends I've met on this server slowly lose interest due to this current situation. The server being RPPVE doesn't mean it can't also have a healthy pvp scene in the battle grounds.

I hope to hear more opinions on said topic and feedbacks are of course welcome.
