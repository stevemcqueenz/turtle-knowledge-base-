 ------- version english ----
Hello, I am an elf priest on this server. I played the old version many years ago.

I don't think the current version is bad, it does quite a bit of damage, especially for a healing priest like me who has few moves.
The spell continues to grow in damage, so I would say that 30 seconds is reasonable. Otherwise, there would be abuse (I personally use it almost all the time). Maybe 25 seconds would be a compromise.

As for bows, I'm using them and it's a nice mechanic. Being an elf, a bow is still cool. In my adventures, I've had bows that were stronger than my wands for several periods. All this combined with that spell (I don't remember the name of the arrow) holy, which does quite a bit of damage if it crits and is useful for giving the enemy the last hit and activating the “spirit tap”. .

So I don't see any need for change at the moment, it's a characteristic of the moon elf. Someone even suggested turning it into a DOT, which is absurd... you would lose the stellar origin of that magic.
