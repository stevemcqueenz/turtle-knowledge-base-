# Make Fashion Tokens sellable on AH

## Post 117186 by Buhfur — 2025-01-18T19:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117186#p117186 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all! I would like to suggest allowing the Fashion Tokens acquired from dungeons sellable on the AH. Since most people in dungeons always need on them. Everyone has an equal chance to acquire many to use for Trasmog. This would also incentivize players who need a little extra cash to sell them for those that are more invested into modifying the appearance of their character. Let me know what you all think! I would love to hear any negative consequences this might have or approval of this addition to the game.

Thanks ! <3
