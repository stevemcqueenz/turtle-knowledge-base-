# Make Southseas Raiders give bloodsail rep until but not beyond neutral

## Post 154191 by Tfwtgt — 2025-10-03T11:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154191#p154191 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Those of us who did the quest that rewards neutral Bloodsail rep and then Booty Bay quests that reduce Bloodsail rep often did not know that there was no way to grind Bloodsail rep back up without ruining our rep with every goblin city. Making Southsea Raiders a viable rep grinding alternative until neutral would solve this issue.
