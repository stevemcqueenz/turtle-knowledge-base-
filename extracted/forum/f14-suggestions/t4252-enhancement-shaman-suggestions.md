# Enhancement Shaman Suggestions

## Post 25364 by Hazhap — 2022-09-18T01:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25364#p25364 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Here are a few ideas I've had about enhancement in vanilla for quite some time, with the addition of the hours I have put in on this server, taking into account what this project has done.

 1. Bloodlust should just be a baseline shaman cooldown, It's very core shaman flavor, and isn't particularly evocative of the enhancement identity. It also does a good job of mirroring the more defensive cooldowns that a paladin has. Also maybe decrease the cooldown from 5 min to 3 min.

 2. Stormstrike, we really need to talk about Stormstrike. Most consider it central to the identity of the offensive Warrior-Mage that enhancement shaman is supposed to be, but in reality, it does not capture the concept well. It accomplishes the desirable instant weapon attack ability that we want, but outside of that It is both boring and doesn't integrate the enhancement weapon buffs at all. So, I suggest a replacement ability-

 -Elemental Strike- a mighty strike that calls upon the shaman's weapon enhancements to much greater effect!
 10 second cooldown and 12% base mana cost at all ranks. Maybe it would deal some additional damage baseline, whether that is a flat amount or a percentage of the weapon damage, idk. The real kicker is the secondary effects that are dependent upon what weapon enhancement buff you have active. This would take the place of bloodlust as the end talent of enhancement.
Rockbiter: Stun the target for 3 seconds, and taunts the target if possible.
Flametongue: Melts the target's armor, reducing it by 50%. lasts 6 seconds.
Frostbrand: Roots the target for 6 seconds.
Windfury: Hits the target, and cleaves the 2 nearest additional targets.

 3. Rock Block- Requires Shield Increases chance to block by 50% for 5 seconds. regain 100% of blocked damage as mana while this is active. 10 second cooldown, no mana cost. This would replace stormstrike in its current position in the talent tree, and would also require 3 points in the Spirit Armor talent.

 4. Shaman should be able to benefit from an applied weapon enhancement AND one that comes from a totem at the same time. Yes, this would mean you would get two effects from elemental strike at the same time. You could tie this into a talent somehow, but I don't think it would be very impressive without the Elemental Strike talent, that and there's only WF and flametongue that come from totems.

I think these changes would bolster an enhancement shaman's value in a large variety of situations, without making them incredibly strong or getting out of their design space. In PvP, you would have some added CC, and actual reasons to use other weapon enhancements that aren't WF (but you would still have WF from the totem with your melee bros!)

In PvE, With the debuff provided by Flametongue Elemental Strike, you could massively buff the damage your party or raid can output, about to same extent as a full stack of sunder armor. You would need at least 2 enhancement shaman to maintain 100% up time on the debuff, but making room for two shouldn't be a problem with this kind of benefit. Also, with the mitigation talents already made available by Turtle WoW, in tandem with these changes, You could more effectively tank dungeons, and maybe off-tank mobs in raids without being much of a liability or compromising your ability to deal damage. All you would need is a shield.

Aside from actual changes to talents and abilities, like with many other "meme" specs, there just needs to be gear available for it. It seems like there is a decent smattering of new stuff to get on an enhancement shaman, but I wouldn't call it enough. We shall see how that pans out with the updates.
