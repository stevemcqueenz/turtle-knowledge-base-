# Dual Deep Wounds for Dual Wield

## Post 102161 by Classicplayer1 — 2024-09-29T11:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102161#p102161 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The the talent says that the critical strikes cause the opponent to bleed, dealing x% of your melee weapon's average damage over 12 sec. But if one is dual wielding the bleed effect can often change based on the weapon that is critting at the time which can be much different with a fast off-hander. I see no reason why both weapon's crit's couldn't apply the bleed seperatly based on their own average damage. This would be a minor change in dps but make Deep Wounds more balanced to fit dual wielding warriors.
