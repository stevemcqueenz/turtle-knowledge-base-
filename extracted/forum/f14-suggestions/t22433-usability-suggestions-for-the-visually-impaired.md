# Usability suggestions for the visually impaired

## Post 155757 by Ashleton — 2025-10-16T04:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155757#p155757 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I have a couple of recommendations to help increase usability for visually-impaired people.

 1) Gathering nodes, especially herb nodes, can be **really** hard to see. Could they have maybe a tiny sparkle or glow to help make them easier to spot?

 2) The fishing bobber barely moves at all when you've caught a fish. I literally missed it because I blinked a few times, and other times I just could not see movement at all. It just does one sharp, teensy little bob, and if you're lucky you get the splashing sound with it. A little more movement and a sound would make a huge difference.

 2a) What about a special fishing bait that automates fishing for a short time? Like for 10 or 15 minutes? It gets boring when you have to stare at the bobber to make sure you don't miss a fish.
