# Pickpocket Tables for new content

## Post 85671 by Piacrt (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-23T05:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85671#p85671 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I notice there are no pickpocket tables for any mobs in many of the new TWoW Dungeons, such as Crescent Grove (Demons and Furbolgs), Gilneas City (Humans), and Hateforge Quarry (Dwarves, Gnomes, Twilight Cult).

This strikes me as odd as NPCs of these races and types almost universally have pickpocket tables, so I am curious as to if this was an oversight or there was a balancing reason for this. I think many of these locations would be great alternatives for junkbox farming compared to what is in Vanilla (Crescent Grove in particular would be a great alliance alternative for Worn Junkboxes, the popular method is scarlet monastery).
