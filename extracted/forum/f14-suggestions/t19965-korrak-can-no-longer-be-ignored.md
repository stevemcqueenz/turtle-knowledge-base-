# Korrak can no longer be IGNORED

## Post 136461 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-06-20T23:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136461#p136461 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are 3 serious problems with Korrak the Bloodrager that can no longer be ignored.
All these 3 problems have recently influenced infamous win rate of Horde in Alterac Valley.

 1. Korrak's bridge bug.

Explained in detail here: <https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=7640>
It's been on bug tracker for 1 year.

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/UjwCpH5npVg]

There is a safe spot that allows you to safely solo kill Korrak.

 2. Korrak's unfair location.

Korrak is usually killed at the start of the game and Horde gets him 90% of the time.
That's because Snowfall Graveyard is on the western part of the map - next to Horde's path.
Meanwhile Alliance's main path after the game starts is going eastern side of Field of Strife.
Going there as Alliance can be hard because you must break the Horde's path - you will likely die - meanwhile Horde has safe passage.

The problem is that the Korrak stays on the Snowfall graveyard instead of fair location that wouldn't benefit only one faction - middle of the map.

Actually Korrak most likely used to be there in OG WoW in patch 1.5. Here is the quoute from one bug report I wrote some time:

> Korrak the Bloodrager in Alterac Valley is located in wrong place.
>  Current state of Korrak in TWoW might be intentional, however, the info I present is based on patch 1.5.0. which is the patch that TWoW's Alterac Valley is also based.
>
>  TWoW Korrak's location isn't based on patch 1.5.0. but on patch 1.8.0. which is Snowfall Graveyard.
> > Due to the avalanche, Korrak the Bloodrager and a small band of surviving Trolls have made the Snowfall Graveyard their new home. Players will find that Snowfall is now under Korrak's control and they must defeat Korrak and his Troll guards if they wish to take Snowfall.
>
>   If the Korrak moved to Snowfall GY in patch 1.8.0. then he must have been somewhere also originally.
>
>  Unfortunately, I haven't played WoW during vanilla and I've found no solid proof where exactly he was located, however, I found 2 videos that show potential original location of Korrak which is the middle of the Field of Strife:
>
>
> [embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/YVCEUBM0twM]
>   I'm not an expert but looking at that guy's UI, no sound recorded, very bad resolution and style of video edition, I can say it's very likely video from original vanilla. It was uploaded in 2015, however, most likely uploaded a few years later after original recording, at least I think so looking at the author's other videos.
>
>
> [embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/yswPGlFufUM]
>  This one has better quality and was likely recorded on Nostalrius when they added old version of AV.
>
>  I read all official patch notes regarding AV and I haven't seen any note about Korrak's spawn at the Field of Strife which could mean it is the original spawn location of Korrak the Bloodrager that was later changed to Snowfall. These 2 videos approve this theory.

 3. Winterax Ritual Dagger.

This is the item that allows you to summon whole team to the position you use the dagger.

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/xqTsfb9fRIU]

It's been possible for 2 years. However, only 3 months ago the quest for item was fixed (it was bugged) and this led to new meta being invented.

It was very niche but also very effective strategy. However, now it becomes more popular than ever before.

Being able to summon team to your location, if used properly, certainly stands against spirit of oldschool AV where the battleground isn't supposed to be a zerg rush like in final patch of vanilla.

The dagger needs to nerfed (or removed).
I see it as a cool addition to AV but it shouldn't be as powerful as it is now.
I suggest making the item usable only near the quest giver so it can be used ONLY for defensive purposes.
