# Please Add Healing & Hybrid PvP Gear Options (Resto Druid, Priest, Paladin, etc.)

## Post 154675 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-10-07T07:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154675#p154675 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Hey everyone,**

I’ve been playing my **Resto Druid alt** lately, and I noticed something that really needs attention, **there are no proper PvP gear options for healing or hybrid specs.**

- The **Honor PvP set for Druids** is entirely **Feral**.

- There’s **no leather AB gear** with +healing.

- The **Arena leather set** has **no +healing or hybrid spell power** options.

- **Priests** only have DPS gear (and the gloves that give Mind Blast pushback resistance which seems to be more shadow oriented than disc).

- **Paladin PvP gear** also lacks strong +healing or support options.
It’s a shame that PvP players who enjoy **healing or hybrid playstyles** can’t grind meaningful gear for their specs. PvE players can bring their raid gear into PvP and perform well, but PvP healers don’t have the same opportunity in reverse. I absolutely love Turtle WoW and everything the devs have done so far, but I really hope the team considers adding:

- A **leather PvP set with +healing** for Druids (Resto-focused).

- **Holy/Disc Paladin options** with healing power and mana regen.

- **Hybrid gear variants** that support both damage and healing (like old-school TBC-style pieces).
I’m sure there are many other specs and playstyles that are also lacking proper PvP itemization but I think it’s fair to say that **healing specs should be the top priority**. They’re **fundamental to PvP balance and group play**, and right now they don’t have any clear progression path through PvP gear.

Right now, my resto druid basically has to wear **cloth PvP gear** just to get any +healing and even that gear is tuned for DPS, not support. **Please, devs, show some love to healers and hybrid specs in the next PvP patch!** ![💚](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f49a.svg)
