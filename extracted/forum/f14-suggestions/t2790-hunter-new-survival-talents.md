Out of line of sight of all that are in combat with you should be the only requirement for setting up a trap, not being out of combat, not having no aggro or not being hit etc.

I don't think it's feasible, but the best would be tinkering the AI so that mobs that have seen you setting up the trap would go around it as if it was an obstacle (perhaps something like the trap having an invisible collision that only the mobs would interract with that have been in LoS when it was set up, I have no idea I have only experience with Warcraft 3 World Editor).
