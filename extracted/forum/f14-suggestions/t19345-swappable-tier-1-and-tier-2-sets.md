# Swappable Tier 1 and Tier 2 sets

## Post 131445 by Pollsforgoals — 2025-05-16T15:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131445#p131445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please make Tier 1 and Tier 2 items switchable at the Ratchet vendor like the Tier 2.5. Tier 3 is also swappable so it doesn't make sense that you cant switch between gear sets of Tier 1 and Tier 2. Arguably it is harder to get a full set of Tier 1 and Tier 2, Which is just ridiculous that if you switch a piece for another specialization you would need to then go back and farm that raid until you get it again...Most likely stealing it from someone who needs it meanwhile you already have it.

I have tried to think of a single reason you shouldn't be able to swap these pieces and it just doesn't make sense. Even if you say that it makes collecting 3 sets of transmog too easy - so does being able to trade items in raid after you equipped it and received the transmog and then give the item to the person who needs it - with this unswappable tier you basically have to ninja it from someone who needs it and go change it to the other item set...
