[Shield Patterns For Blacksmithing.](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=1656)

If you play without challenges, you don't need to craft shields. If you tanking, then there are plenty of them in dungeons. If you DPS'ing then you don't need shield as item.
Situation changes only if you pick Travelling Craftmaster. But I am afraid that "no shields during leveing" is a part of the challenge in devs vision. Otherwise they would add plenty of shields already.
With current set of rules, if you want to tank dungeons as Craftmaster, then pick Feral druid. I think it will totally possible in relevant green gear with some condions. Though it doesn't make sense from perspective of rewards. Leatherworkers don't need portable anvil nor moonwell.

One more thing to consider - if you create a lot of leveling shield recipes, then people will actually tank dungeons as craftmasters. It will produce another social situation - "bad geared" tanks in LFT. Currently, craftmasters don't even try tanking. With that change - they will surely try, but other people might not be very happy of them. It might result in disbanded parties and unnecessary hostility.
