# New Mage pet quest

## Post 28818 by Zeyla — 2022-11-09T12:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28818#p28818 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Before we get into it, I'm not suggesting a powerful combat pet like the water elemental that everyone is familiar with, as that is a bit too strong to exist in vanilla era. However, something more themed around a familiar/helper that involves a level 60 quest chain for mages to unlock would be a welcome addition. Here's my suggestion:

After completing "Lost to the Sands" for Archmage Xylem to teach you the Ritual of Refreshment, his next step to train you as his apprentice is to teach you how to go about making your own contract with a familiar. The components required to summon a powerful mage's familiar are not so simple to come by, however, and will require delving into dangerous places and making connections with other mystical entities.

Step 1. Mage trainer quest to summon you back to Archmage Xylem and continue your "apprenticeship" after the "Lost to the Sands" quest is completed.

Step 2. Archmage Xylem explains the concept of magical familiars to you, and instructs you on how to acquire your own. You will need a number of rare reagents, including:
 - A Kirin Tor Ritual Binder recovered from the depths of Karazhan Crypt. (quest item)
 - A Tamed Lashling granted by the Cenarion Circle once you are revered among them. (New pet/item)
 - A Water Waveling granted by the Hydraxian Waterlords once you are exalted among them.
 - 20 Arcane Powder.

Step 3. With the preparations complete, Archmage Xylem gives you the "Familiar Summoning Focus" which you take to the  Lake Elune'ara in Moonglade, where you summon a powerful elite Swamp Elemental from the combination of the water and life energy in the spell components. Defeating it cleanses the corruption within the lake and grants Elune's Gift (quest item).

Step 4. Return to Archmage Xylem with Elune's Gift and he will convert it into a Tome of Conjure Familiar as your quest reward.

This would be a rather simple pet in terms of combat, merely being a small green water elemental that autocasts waterbolt (no slow effect) and doesn't have a freeze ability, but instead has an aura that increases party members' max mana by 5% and could possibly also have a phase shift ability similar to warlock imps. The true utility of this pet, however, is that it also serves as a vendor when right-clicked by friendly players, wherein it sells each of the types of conjured water and food for all levels, including oranges, crystal water and cinnamon rolls, in stacks of 20 at no cost.

This would allow an infinite source of mage food/water without having to spend time and mana on the conjuring process ever again once the pet is acquired, as you simply have a pet out that will do all of that work for you.
