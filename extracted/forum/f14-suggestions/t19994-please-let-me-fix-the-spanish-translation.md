# Please, let me fix the Spanish translation

## Post 136697 by Alexmmusic — 2025-06-23T07:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136697#p136697 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A few years ago, I personally reached out to the Turtle WoW team to offer to translate their version of the game for free, since I’ve always played WoW in my native language, Spanish. At the time, I was told it wasn’t possible to translate the game—for whatever reason.

Now that I know Turtle WoW has indeed been translated, I decided to give it a try and, honestly, I love it. But the Spanish translation is kind of a mess. There are inconsistent translations, phrases that were already localized in the original game but were poorly “copied,” grammatical and spelling mistakes, some parts that just don’t feel natural, and even words left completely untranslated.

I have experience translating video games as a freelancer, and even so, here I am on this forum BEGGING the Turtle WoW team to let me fix the disastrous Spanish translation—completely for free and purely for the love of it.

Thanks to everyone who read this—hopefully it reaches whoever it needs to.
