# Eyes of the Beast tweak

## Post 104331 by FrankFankledank (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-10-22T12:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104331#p104331 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Eyes of the Beast changes look like a fun little thing to mess around with on paper, but there's one major issue with trying to actually use Eyes of the Beast offensively: your pet doesn't tag mobs, which means you gain no credit, XP or drops for anything you kill in Eyes of the Beast unless you tag it first, awkwardly cast EotB, cat-smack it to death, drop EotB despite it having a massive duration intended for multiple fights especially when talented, tag again, recast again.

Of course, if we made pets always able to tag NPCs then you'd be able to AFK-grind easily inserting them in a congested area with aggressive mode on. And so, my proposal is: make it so pets specifically under the effects of Eyes of the Beast give you tagging credit. With that, you can actually open the door to more playstyles for Hunter and challenges like a 'pet-only' run.
