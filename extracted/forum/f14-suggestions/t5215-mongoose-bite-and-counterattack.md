# Mongoose Bite and Counterattack

## Post 31692 by Timmy — 2022-12-28T11:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31692#p31692 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At the time melee hunter overall is extremely lackluster compared to other melees. Not to mention that two of your 4 melee spells require you to either doge or parry in order to cast, which will never happen in a party or raid environment.
This will not make melee hunter more viable than other melee classes but at least you'll be able to use your 4 melee spells in PvE.

EDIT: Remove the required dodge to cast Mongoose Bite
Implement a talent that support Counterattack in survival tree.
 1/3 "Mongoose Bite has a 33% chance to enable the use of Counterattack"
 2/3 "Mongoose Bite has a 66% chance to enable the use of Counterattack"
 3/3 "Mongoose Bite has a 100% chance to enable the use of Counterattack"
