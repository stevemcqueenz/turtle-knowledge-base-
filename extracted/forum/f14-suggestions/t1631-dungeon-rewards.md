This coincides with the suggestion posted here regarding Heroic Dungeons being brought to Turtle WoW:
[viewtopic.php?f=14&t=1625](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=1625)

Sense I like the idea of having possible rewards for completing Heroic Dungeons (and maybe just regular Dungeons) in the form of an in-game currency, I figured I would make it's own suggestion.

I very much like the idea of perhaps receiving a "Dungeon Token" (or 2 for Heroic Dungeon) completion which could be spent on say, cosmetics, or some special gear. This would definitely make me want to run some more dungeons. This would also cater to the more dungeon-crawler crowd who play WoW for the dungeons/raids and not necessarily questing.

Thoughts?
