# Give Gnomes and Trolls their own starting zone!

## Post 148775 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-27T22:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148775#p148775 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Trolls:
Starting Zone: Darkspear Islands
Level: 1-12

In the lore most of the Darkspear Trolls left the island to join the Horde in Durotar.
Most but not all of them. This could explain why new Troll players start on this island and not together with the Orcs.

The zone has a great variety of enemies: Naga, Murlocs, Gnolls, Kul Tirans, Makrura.
And for wildlife: Crabs, Panthers, Raptors and maybe Crocolisks.

The Darkspear Islands are not small so it could potentially even have a small Troll capital city! I think the Trolls really deserve it since the Gnomes kinda got their little capital city with the Reclamation facility, right?
Getting off the island would probably work with a ship going from the Darkspear Island to Sparkwater port.

Storywise it should probably culminate in killing an elite Naga Sea Witch because in the lore Thrall and the trolls escaped and did not actually kill her! Her name is Zar'jira and she was killed officially during Cataclysm.

To read about the already established lore for the Darkspear Islands click on this link: [Darkspear Islands](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Darkspear_Islands)

Gnomes:
Starting Zone: Gnomeregan Evacuation Facility
Level: 1-6

For Gnomes its a lot harder to think of a proper starting zone. There is no way to make it big enough to be a Dun Morogh alternative but a starting zone more akin to Coldridge Valley should be possible.

Enemy types as per usual when it comes to Gnomeregan themed areas: Troggs, malfunctioning machines, Lepergnomes, slimes.

The setting should be about the Gnomes struggle to evacuate the living and securing resources as best as they can.
Tools, nuts, valves, cogs. Killing the insane Lepers and the Troggs and fixing ventilation systems to reduce radioactive fallout then in the end you end up in the Gnomish Reclamation facility where you are then one way teleported to Kharanos into the hut with the two gnomes to join back up with the Dwarfs!

Warcraft 3 showed Darkspear Island as being sunk by a Naga Sea Witch so it shouldn't be there in its original state anymore. However, she used a volcano in the process so it only takes a little bit of retconning to interpret this 'sinking' as actually being a giant volcanic eruption that explodes the island. There are real life examples of that happening, like at Thera and Krakatoa, and in such events there are often some parts of the island that are far away enough from the eruption to survive the event. I doubt there'll be many survivors, but the Darkspears might very well try to reclaim this circle of small islands surrounding the former volcano, and this could make a decent starter zone.

As for the Gnomes, there is currently a large empty area Northwest of Dun Morogh which I think could be a perfect starter zone for the Gnomes if we say that some of them escaped that way through a backdoor out of Gnomeregan and set up a reclamation base there. The rest of the zone could consist of mountain valleys covered by pine forests opening into big fjords, probably inhabited by trolls and troggs and maybe some isolated Dwarven settlements. I would move the whole Gnomeregan reclamation zone there to have some variation between the wilderness outdoors and the Gnomeregan-themed underground areas.

I wouldn't want to have new capital cities in either zone though, I think it wouldn't fit lore-wise for the Trolls and Gnomes to rebuild a city that fast and I feel like there's already too many cities as is. In fact, I would move all the quest- or profession-related NPCs inside the reclamation zone back to Tinker Town as in my experience their current out-of-the-way location doesn't add anything and instead disrupts the flow of the related quests and professions (and it feels absurd that Ironforge doesn't even have any engineering trainers above journeyman level while Stormwind does!). While reclamation efforts are still ongoing it is probably much more convenient and comfortable for important Gnomes to remain in Ironforge.
