# Racial Tweak Ideas

## Post 88414 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-02-22T11:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88414#p88414 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This was just for fun, but feel free to add any ideas below

New Racials

Orc

Bloodfury: Increases all damage by 7% for 12 sec, but reduces healing effects by 50% for duration. 5 min cooldown

Axe Mastery: Increases critical strike with axes by 1%

Command: Increases your pets damage and hp by 5%.

Hardiness: Chance to resist stun increased by 15%

Troll

Berserking: Increases haste by 25% for 10sec at the cost of 15% HP (will only put you to 1%)

Beastslaying: Damage to beasts and dragonkin increased by 5%

Bow Mastery: Increases Critical strike by 1 % with bows

Regeneration: Restores 2% of your Max HP per 5sec.

Undead

Will of the Forsaken - Removes all fear and charm effects. 3 min CD

Shadow Mastery: Increases your shadow damage by 3%

Shadow Resistance: Increases your chance to resist shadow spells by 2%

Water Breathing: Lasts 500% longer than normal.

Blade Mastery: Chance to Crit with swords and daggers increased by 1%

Cannibilize: Restores 3% HP and Mana per 2sec for 12sec.

Tauren

Warstomp: Stuns 5 nearby enemies 0.5s cast cannot be interrupted. 2min CD

Nature Mastery: Increases your Nature damage by 3%

Nature Reistance: Increases your chance to resist nature spells by 2%

Mace Mastery: Increases critical strike with maces by 1%

Endurance: Increases Max HP by 5%

Cultivation: Your skill in herbalism increases by 2 points instead of 1.

Find Herbs: You can track herbs without herbalism profession. (useful for quests)

Goblin

Explosive Tactics: You can use any grenade in your inventory without consuming it. 5 min cool down.

Goblin Rocket Boots: You gain 70% movement speed for 10secs, but risk exploding and losing 15% hp.

Chemical Superfreak: Your elixirs and potions gain a +15% duration.

Dagger & Mace mastery: Your critical chance with daggers and maces is increased by 1%

Human

Perception: Stealth detection increased dramatically for 15sec.

Keen Eye: Increases chance to Crit by 20% for 6sec. 5min cooldown

The Human Spirit: All Stats increased by 3%

Sword Mastery: Critical hit with swords increased by 1%

Diplomacy: All Rep gains increased by 10%

Mace Mastery: Critical hit with maces increased by 1%

Dwarf

Stoneform: increases Armor by 20%, Immune to poisons diseases and bleeds for 12 seconds. 2 min CD

Find Treasure: Shows treasure chests on the map and can unlock them.

Mining Mastery: Mining speed increased by 50% and skill increases by 2 instead of 1.

Cold Endurance: Chance to resist freezing effects and cold based slows increased by 5%

Axe and Mace Mastery: Chance to Crit with maces and axes increased by 1%

Gun Mastery: Chance to Crit with guns increased by 1%

Night Elf

Shadowmeld:
Slip into the shadows and become undetectable, useable in combat.

Elusiveness: Chance to be hit reduced by 2%

Nature Mastery: Nature damage increased by 3%

Nature Protection: Reduces nature damage taken by 20% lasts 12sec. 5min cool down.

Bow Mastery: Increases chance to critically hit with bows by 1%

Gnome

Escape Artist: Escape any movement impairing effect, in addition increases your agility by 15% for 10sec. 0.5s cast 2min CD.

Expansive Mind: Intellect increased by 5%

Arcane Mastery: Arcane damage increased by 3%

Arcane Resistance: Increased chance to resist arcane spells by 2%

Engineering Mastery: Engineering skill increased by 15 and reduces cooldown on engineering devices by 25%.

High Elf

Quel'dorei Meditation: Increases mana regen by 150% 50% can continue in combat
Increases energy regen by 50%
Increases Rage generation by 30%
Lasts 10sec. 5min cool down.

Enchanting Mastery: Enchanting increased by 15, in addition you gain 2 skills instead of 1.

Arcane Mastery: Increases all spell damage by 2% (Passive)

Bow Mastery: Increases chance to Crit with bows by 1%

Magic Resistance: All spell damage taken reduced by 3% (Passive)

All:

All weapon skills are trainable to 305 after doing a quest at level 60 to any race.
