# Disciple of Naralex in WC doesn't respawn if killed

## Post 85223 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-01-18T18:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85223#p85223 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Disciple of Naralex (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3678>) is an NPC you have to escort so that he can summon the final boss of the dungeon. However, he is rather squishy and if he dies (or you wipe, causing him to die), then he will never respawn and you will not be able to try again. The only option is to reset the dungeon and clear it all over again (an hour or more lost). This is what happened to me and my friends when we tried to 3-man the dungeon - we wiped on Mutanus and waited in vain for the guy to respawn. It was very anticlimatic.

While I realize that this is the same way it works on Classic (maybe even vanilla back in the day I think), it's very punishing for such a low level dungeon and is not consistent with other dungeons, in which if you wipe, you can just run back and try again.

I propose adding a 5 minute respawn timer for the Disciple, so other players won't have the same frustrating ending to a dungeon run that we had.
