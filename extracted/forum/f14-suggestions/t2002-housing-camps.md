# housing camps

## Post 11927 by Robbie0421 — 2021-09-02T18:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11927#p11927 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

i had a new idea for a brand new feature for turtle wow

housing that you build up and maintain just like you did with the bases in warcraft 3

each player can buy a house/camp from any housing vender within the capital cities starting at around level 10

each housing camp site will start out with a gold mine, forests and a town hall for alliance, a great hall for horde or a necropolis for forsaken as well as 5 workers that the player can assign various tasks

from there the player can have the workers gather gold and lumber as well as construct and or maintain buildings in the housing camp

each housing camp can upgrade to the keep and castle for alliance, stronghold and fortress for horde and halls of the dead/Black Citadel
for forsaken. each upgrade allows new buildings to be constructed for military and defensive use

players may also recruit a small army of mercenary warriors to protect the housing camp while their away from the barracks and crypt

a new turtle wow addon that will alert players when their housing camp needs maintenance work or is under attack can also be created and added to the turtle wow forums for download

attacking housing camps belonging to the opposite faction can be added in as a pvp option
