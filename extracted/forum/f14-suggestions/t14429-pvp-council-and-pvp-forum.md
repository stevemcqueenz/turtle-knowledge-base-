# PvP Council and PvP Forum

## Post 98187 by Reklaw — 2024-06-30T10:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98187#p98187 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ayo lads and lasses,

I will now officially put up this Thread to suggest forming a PvP Council just like the Class Council.

The reason being the state of PvP on TWoW in general and just the general noise thats being produced by everyone regarding all sorts of specs, ideas, "YOU HAVE TO DO THIS!!!11"-isms and bunch of other things.

Turtle WoW arguably has a very lively PvE Scene, all raids are getting cleared multiple times a day by various guilds each day, week after week. If TWoW would set their PvP straight, they'd have the potential to become the best Server that is currently around and attract a number of players they didn't attract so far or only for a limited amount of time. There is an entire population on the server feeling left alone and its producing resentment and is detrimental for the server.

 *Now I am not here to propose changes to anything ingame.*

What I am proposing is the formation of a PvP Council similiar to the Class Council.

 **Why a PvP Council?**

Well, a bunch of other servers already had it or still have it. It offers another lane of community interaction with the devs and can act as a catalyst of the hundreds of suggestions happening on Discord/the Forum.

Because as of now all discussion will get reduced to "well u suck", "u must be a pally" or other ad hominems which simply isn't constructive in any way, shape or form.

Now why exactly a council and not just community votes. Well, to start of: because 80% +/- of the playerbase is only interested in their own benefit and will not see the wider interactions of the stuff they're demanding. And actually good suggestions can be hard to be filtered in the ocean of QQ.

As a result, the team shouldn't listen to 95% of the suggestions made on a random basis on any of those places. What should happen is the formation of a Council with an infrastructure that allows civilized and informed discussion about changes and what implications they would have. Just like with the Class Council. It also makes the playerbase feel like "some of them" had a say in things and it's not just changes imposed by the team generating mistrust and separation.

Furthermore the proposed Class Changes or changes to BGs should be passed by a PvP Team to gauge implications of them. Rulesets could be revisited or changed aswell. (treating easy jumps in BGs the same as out of bounds jumps for example)

 **Who whould be part of the PvP Council**

Well, obviously: **Not only PvPers but definitely people with PvP experience.**

Even the sweatiest PvPers will need fresh meat in the grinder to hunt. So the design choices that will be made will have to be made keeping in mind that PvP needs to attract casuals just as much as hardcore grinders.

I get, that a big part of the PvP community is toxic as fuck, but there is the few gems out there that don't succumb to the autism and can still communicate fairly well. It will have to  be people with a reputable history of communications. Even prior offenders might get "pacified" by being offered a spot  to help decide make their little niche of TWoW a better place.

Possibly a referral system, an open vote and people suggest players. Players with most votes get on the council. (abusable, will have to see how to manage it). I'm sure people in this Thread will name other options.

Looking forwards for input. Please keep in mind we're not dealing with specific issues here. I'm just starting this threat to start tackling this beast, as it is very nessecary in my opinion.
