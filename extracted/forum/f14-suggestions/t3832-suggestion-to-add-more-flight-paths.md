# Suggestion to add more flight paths

## Post 23122 by B3tabob — 2022-08-22T12:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23122#p23122 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there, Turtles,

Now before you call me retail Randy please hear me out. I am new to this server and have been playing for just over a few weeks now, but I have noticed how the lack of more flight paths on starter zones makes me despise doing some quests.

I do not know if it's just a problem for zones like Elwynn forest and Dun Morogh, but there have been multiple instances where I am excited to play the game, but then remember I have to cross a whole zone w/o flight paths on foot. While I love that walking makes the world feel much bigger and you only get your first mount at 40, adding some flight paths where it makes sense would be the most appreciated thing.

To add to this, flight paths should feel as they actually belong there. While a remote village should not have a flight path, a central hub like Goldshire and Kharanos makes logistical sense.

To encourage discussion, a question - does it get better as you move through zones up the levels or does it stay as frustrating as it has been to this point? As for my class and level, I am a level 20 rogue, and even with sprint and Endurance talent some travelling for quests makes me close the game faster than I can say vanishing powder.

Cheers
