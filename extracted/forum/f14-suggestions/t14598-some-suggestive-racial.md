# Some suggestive racial

## Post 99623 by Trevor89 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-16T10:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99623#p99623 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Inspired by several source, I'd like to share my idea on potential suggestive alternatives about racials:

Humans - I feel like humans' most common trait is to be specialized in something specific (as also D&d teach =) ) and in wow they could be specialized in the class itself so -> "Class specialization": 1 extra talent point every 20 levels.
Potentially this racial might replace "Perception" (100% elfish trait) and "Weapon skills" (an unhealthy trait for the classes distributions between the two factions);

Night elfs - Quickness is fine from a lore perspective, though the flat 1% dodge is kinda meh with new gears progression compared to the tauren endurance (5% total health increased) - so I would change that 1% dodge with 1 or 2% total agility increasing;

High elfs - Perception;

Goblins deserves Gun specialization;

Side note for melee weapon proficiency: it would be cool to get a +5 weapon skill bonus obtainable and replaceable through some repeatable chain quest or particular task - it would be sweet for warriors - though, to avoid every melee walking around with swords, I think that each race should get a specific list of available weapon skills to improve (this list could be based on what each specific weapon master can teach).

I think there are three ways of approaching racials:
 1. Leaving everything as it is. We know it won't happen since devs will modify at least the weapon talents which kinda have a domino effect in racial power rankings.
 2. Make racials weaker. This is the way retail went. If it barely matters which race you choose you can RP better but it always takes away a certain taste from the game.
 3. Giving equally strong racials to everyone. This has a high chance to cause really big balance issues. I love this path because of the added flavor but for example imagine the unfairness of being an orc in PVP and multiply it by 2 if something goes wrong.

Safe route is tuning the racials down, fun route is to give everyone good ones AND testing them for a LONG time to make sure nothing is too broken.

Personally I'd make the below changes (the safe route, tuning down abilities):
 * All weapon skill racials rather become +1% crit chance with the weapon.
 * Berserking becomes 10-20% haste, not 10-30%.
 * Orc hardiness resist chance reduced.
 * Will of the Forsaken - honestly I'm not sure. Bigger CD?

Not sure how this meta would look like.
