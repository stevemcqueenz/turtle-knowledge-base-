Greetings and Salutations fellow adventurers!

I may be speaking only to a niche community here but, how many of you all would be interested in playing WoW with more survival aspects than the survival skill provides? I am talking hunger and thirst meters or debuff stacks that hinder the player at increasing levels and maybe even causing death should they be ignored for too long. Maybe even having the fatigue bar display as your character goes killing mob after mob, after mob without visiting and inn. Maybe even take a rest and really savor that artisanal cooked meal for longer than the time needed to proc the buff. I am interested to see what thoughts, additions or features you would add, and just overall sentiment on the idea.

Stay safe out there adventurers and I look forward to slaying with you soon.

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78894#p78894 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While it is interesting concept, I think it will be pretty easy challenge. World is filled with beasts, which often drop meat. When I level cooking during leveling, I always craft excessive amount of food.
There are a lot of vendors with unlimited amount of food. There are 36-slot bags and portable bank.
There are Cannibalize for forsaken and conjured food and drinks.

You can make it self-imposed challenge like "once you get well-fed debuff, you should always keep it up".

Hunger and thirst meters look too similar to minecraft ones. You can add them on your own by making addon, which tracks when you eat something and displays correct values.

If you really really want gameplay effects from hunger, then food can give you buff "well fed" during first 15 minutes and when it ends, then you get debuff "starving" which last 30 minutes with effect similar to res sickness. When "starving" ends, you die. When you get another "well fed" buff, then your starving buff gets removed.
