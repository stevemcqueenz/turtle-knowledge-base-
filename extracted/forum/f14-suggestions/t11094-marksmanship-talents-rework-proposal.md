# Marksmanship Talents Rework Proposal

## Post 72886 by Steelx — 2023-10-22T22:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=72886#p72886 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The marksmanship tree is very boring and it looks like developers at the time rushed it and just threw a bunch of filler talent with 5 ranks as a placeholder and it somehow made it into the game. Talents like [Improved Serpent Sting], that at 5 points increases the already low damage and easily dispellable [Serpent Sting] by a whooping 10%. Or the +5% ranged weapon damage from [Ranged Weapon Specialization]... All so very boring and weak.

It seems like there are only 3 or 4 talents that really matter in the MM tree:

Lethal / Mortal Shots
Aimed Shot
Scattershot for PvP or Trueshot Aura for PvE.
Maybe Eagle Eye...

Everything else is pretty much a filler that doesn't interact with any of the Hunter spells significantly, Stings don't scale at all and same goes for Arcane Shot. And the rest is just pure number scaling. So Boring. It honestly feels like the only two talents they gave some thought to were [Improved Concussive Shot] and [Improved Scorpid Sting].

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Kbhvjs2.png)

Not every talent has to be a game changing bonus, but the current tree is just too irrelevant, ineffective and boring. That's why I've come up with a new and with it I seek to make a justice to the only Ranger Class in WoW.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/oXMd3Ik.png)

Here's a list of all the changes:

ADDED 3 COMPLETELY NEW TALENTS

Double Draw [Bow Icon]
Increases your Attack Speed with Bows by 1%/2%/3%/4%/5% and your attacks have a 2%/4%/6%/8%/10% chance to be a Double Draw, shooting an extra arrow towards the enemy. [More Arrows = More Arrows. Better synergy with faster weapons and one of the Bestial Discipline changes I suggested here [viewtopic.php?t=10837](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10837)]

Point-Blank Maneuvers [Gun Icon]
Ignores up to 60/120/180/240/300 of your enemies' Armor when Shooting with a Gun and reduces the minimum distance at which you can shoot by 0.5/1/1.5/2/3 Yards.
[A PvP Favorite. Armor would be scaling with level. 1 armor penetration Per-Rank-Per-Level up to 300 (same as Rogues' Serrated Dirk). With the nice -3 Yards of Deadzone]

Perforating Force [Crossbow Icon]
Increases your Critical Strike Chance with Crossbows by 1%/2%/3%/4%/5% and your Critical Strikes completely perforate the enemy, instantly dealing an extra 4%/8%/12%/16%/20% of your Crossbow's average damage as bleed.
[Damage dealt as bleed to prevent it from critting and to ignore armor. Making the scaling consistent. Considering this would only proc from crits the actual damage increase would be " 4% to 20% * %crit ". if crit was 50% (good luck) the damage increase would be 10%. realistically it will range from 3% to 7%]

Ranged Weapon Specialization [REMOVED]

Improved Serpent Sting 3/3
Increases the damage done by your Serpent Sting by 15%. [Before 5/5 = +10% damage > Now 3/3 +15% damage. It wouldn't bother me if this is 30% instead of 15%.]

Hawk Eye 2/2
Increases the range of your ranged weapons by 3/6 yards [Before 3/3 > Now 2/2]

Mortal Shots 3/3
Increases your ranged weapon critical strike damage bonus by 30% [Before 5/5 > Now 3/3. Damage is unchanged]
Now sits one on the third tier of Marksmanship instead of the fourth.

Improved Hunter's Mark 5/5
Increases the Ranged Attack Power bonuses of your Hunter's Mark spell by 50%. [Before +15% or 110ap to 126.5ap > Now +50% or 110ap to 165ap].

[Edit] At the suggestion of "Equalrites" I un-deleted Improved Arcane Shot from my suggestion.
Improved Arcane Shot now a 2/2 talent
Reduces the cooldown of your Arcane Shot by 0.5/1 seconds.

Let me know what you think of my ideas, Does marksmanship tree deseve some love?   dead_turtle_head
