## Post 15963 by Silverleafeon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-01-17T18:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15963#p15963 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yes please! Lone Wolf for hunters is one of the few things about WoD that I liked. Hunters have always been my favorite class, but having a pet hasn't always been the most enjoyable experience, what with pathing issues, etc.

I vividly remember when I decided to give up on pets as a hunter. It was during a Twin Peaks battleground. I was fighting someone 1 on 1. It was a pretty close match, but I was winning. When I was close to defeating my opponent, my pet, due to some bug, somehow teleported halfway across the battleground. I saw my pet waaaay off in the distance, making its way back to my location, but it was too late. I lost the fight because of my absent pet, as pets are a huge part of a hunter's damage output. This isn't the first frustrating experience I had with my pet, but it was the last.

I decided to give lone wolf a try after that, and I loved it! Being just a plain ol' archer was super fun!

Pets are a very big part of the hunter aesthetic, but I don't think they're totally necessary for all hunters. For me, hunters are those people who wander the wilds and have a close relationship with nature. That relationship can manifest itself in many ways, such as befriending wild animals (perfect for beast mastery), or maybe just finding peace being alone in nature, and being savvy when it comes to surviving in the wilderness.

It would be cool if Lone Wolf was added to the game :)
