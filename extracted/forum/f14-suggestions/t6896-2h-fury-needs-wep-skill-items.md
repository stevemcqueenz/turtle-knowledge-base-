The good news is that +5 skill at lv 10 is just gonna help you fight lv 13s, who are going to crit and crush you anyway, and the same thing will happen at lv 40 vs lv 43s. Leveling skill is almost a useless stat other than the odd time you find yourself fighting an orange level mob in a dungeon you are slightly underleveled for. This is great. We can put skil items all over the low levels that just have +3, +2, +5 skill, etc. Maybe with +2 strength, other tiny stats. These would remain options for lv 60 players, although likely not good options as the skill gear at 60 ideally has a bunch of other stats too. The choice remains natural. The skill stat will have a tiny budget, and all shall be well.
