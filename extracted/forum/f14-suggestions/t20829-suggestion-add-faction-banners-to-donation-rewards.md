# Suggestion: Add faction banners to donation rewards.

## Post 144707 by Gangout — 2025-08-12T05:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144707#p144707 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello fellow turtles!

I would like to add a suggestion to add faction banners to the donation store.

In wotlk there were banners you could gain from doing the daily quests in Icecrown. The Stormwind and Orgrimmar banner. These were cosmetic items a player could place in the world on a 3 minute cooldown, and they would stand in place for 3 minutes. Allowing players to plant a temporary (3 minutes) flag in the world representing their faction.

I suggest we add such fine cosmetic items to the donation store so that turtles all across Azeroth are able to proudly represent their factions! Allowing players to plant temporary flags would grant us yet another roleplay element as well as (hopefully) bring in some additional revenue for this fine server. I know I'd donate to get one. How about you? ;)
