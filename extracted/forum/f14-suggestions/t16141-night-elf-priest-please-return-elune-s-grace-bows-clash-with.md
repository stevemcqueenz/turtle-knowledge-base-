# Night Elf Priest please return Elune's Grace, bows clash with class design

## Post 109949 by Halfgeek9 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-26T06:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109949#p109949 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Lorewise it makes sense for NE Priestess of the Moon to use bows, but the class design of Priests and the game mechanics just clashes badly with trying to make bows work.

When leveling, Priests will use their Wands to finish mobs after some opening spell salvo, the reason has always been to allow mana regen and make the most of Spirit Tap 2x SPI stat for 15s.

On Turtle Wow, Wand spec is further boosted with high hit % and mana on hit bonuses making Wands even more potent and go-to.

NE Priests auto shot suffers from less dmg due to armor, and can't shoot in melee, whereas Wands work any range. Using the bow attack skill ruins mana regen.

While the devs may have thought it seem like a good idea, but due to game mechanics and class design in classic for Priests this is a really useless "feature" for NE Priests.

Having an on demand +25% dodge buff is not OP and it was never an issue with NE Priests.
