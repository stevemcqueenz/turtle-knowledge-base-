# Area Label: Alliance, Horde, Neutral, Contested

## Post 124154 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-27T23:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124154#p124154 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is going to sound crazy or silly maybe, but I've been thinking.
The idea of ​​split PvP/PvE servers is a dumb idea on Blizzard's part, as it completely kills both.
So I remembered the existence of Wintergrasp in Northrend and thought... Why not make some areas PvP-only, completely contested?

Something like BGs but open world. I know PvP is getting a full buff in 1.18.0, though I don't know if they'll label some areas as contested territory, Alliance territory, and Horde territory.

For example:
Labeling areas where the opposing faction isn't present as Alliance or Horde territory, and having it function as usual, with PvP being mandatory for the opposing faction:
Alliance:
 -Elwynn
 -Westfall
 -Redridge
 -Duskwood
 -Darkshore

Neutral Territory: PvP is optional, as both factions are present, but there's no territorial dispute in the lore as such.

War Territory: Mandatory PvP with quests and the presence of warring factions on both sides.

This way, everyone stays happy, and PvP gets the attention it's been getting for a long time.

It sounds more logical, and it mostly prevents ganking from the opposing faction while leveling, as I personally find Blizzard's way of organizing the world in terms of Alliance, Horde, and Contested territory inefficient.

Pretty simple:
Neutral areas are for normal leveling quests, you know, like The Barrens, Stonetalon Mountains, Ashen Valley, Arathi, etc. And they have a presence of both factions without mandatory PvP, but you can activate it if you want.
War zones would be specifically for PvP and maybe some PvE, with the added benefit that both factions activate mandatory PvP.
Faction-specific zones, like those labeled "Alliance Territory" or "Horde Territory," are so that PvP is only activated when someone from the opposing faction enters the zone.
