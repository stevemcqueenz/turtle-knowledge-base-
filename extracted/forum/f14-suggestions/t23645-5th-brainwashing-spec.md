You might be thinking too much into it. I think people largely value the convenience of being able to load a spec quickly instead of having to spend 51 points manually or having to run an addon for it.

Also, if it were truly just a problem of economics, I feel like that can be solved by tuning the price.
And let's not forget that people are not rational. They prefer a one-time cost over having to pay small fees constantly, even if the one-time cost ends up being more over-all. The 4th slot costs 200g, which is equivalent to 40 respecs. 400g seems like an entirely fair price for a 5th slot, ie 80 respecs. I would pay that in a heartbeat, but I don't think I've ever done more than 50 paid respecs in years of playing on Turtle. So - the one-time payment would get *me* to spend more gold than the small fee. Because the small fee and having to do talents annoys me so I just don't do it and accept having a less optimized spec and have less fun with the game. Like I said - not exactly rational. But it's not at all clear to me that a fourth slot would lead to less gold being removed from the economy. It might end up being more. Personally I'd probably pay 600 or even 800g for another slot because of the convenience it would offer, something I would never ever spend on respecs.
