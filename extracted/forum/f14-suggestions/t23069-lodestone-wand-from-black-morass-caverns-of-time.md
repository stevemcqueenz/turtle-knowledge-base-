# Lodestone Wand from Black Morass - Caverns of Time

## Post 161015 by Lazarvs — 2025-11-28T17:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161015#p161015 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello, im here to suggest a modification into this item <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61020> that drops from Black Morass - Caverns of time.

Since it is an item that is mainly worn by warlocks and priests, and from one of the most difficult 5 man dungeons, i think it should have a more considerate use throughout the game, that could get some sinergies with other set tiers to help the warlock dps a bit and to not be disregarded early into raids.

The suggestion i am here to make is to, instead of the proc being triggered by wanding with the item, that it would instead proc when landing offensive spells.

Why am i suggesting this:

At the moment, warlock hellfire and rain of fire is significantly poor in comparison with mage aoe spells, mainly arcane explosion, and asking for a change in spell coeficient scaling with hellfire and rain of fire next to the devs has somewhat gone into deaf ears or the class is being somewhat neglected. The procrate could also be subject to scale with spellpower, even if it was in a low  coeficient around 30%.

In this fashion, at least speaking for the warlock class, our AoE effects could have a more significant way to get leverage in raids, in comparison mainly with AoE spells from mage, and also from other classes.

Hope this suggestion isn't that far from being too much to ask, and would certenately put a glimpse of hope on the warlock class regarding our AoE damage.

Sincerely, Lazzarvs.
