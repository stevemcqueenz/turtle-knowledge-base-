## Post 137697 by Cosmico (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-01T12:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137697#p137697 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/k2mm8F9L/1.png)](https://ibb.co/N2TTjzm0)

For the alliance improvement it might result in a good thing to have a ship go from south shore to alah'thalas second port. That spot is empty and available for a new travel route.

You know it's a thing to cross from the western plague lands, or giving a true round up of 20 minutes to reach scarlet monastery. See, it's not easy to unlock eastern plague lands flying path to unlock thalassian highlands flight point. It feels a little bit rusty that the only choice you have to reach northern tirisfal is to get yourself at risk going through zones that are 20 to 30 lvls above yours, it's also unrealistic that we the alliance have to pass walking in front of under city's doors to reach monastery. And believe me you have to visit monastery at least 30 times short before leaving that content behind. And you're still going back there for quest or helping others.

You may answer like this: just go stormwind then auberdyne then thalassian. However... from the eastern kingdoms perspective you're going to spend a lot of time closer to wetlands and arathi than stormwind.

Guys you've done fixes and updates for the sake of practical focus before, this could be very useful.
