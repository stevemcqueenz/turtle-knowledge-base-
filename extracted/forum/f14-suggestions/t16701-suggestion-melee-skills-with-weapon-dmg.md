# Suggestion: melee skills with %weapon dmg

## Post 114077 by Isvya (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-24T00:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114077#p114077 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently most melee skills that deal x% of weapon damage have their lower ranks deal less %
I can take slam as an example, rank 1 just deals 50% up to 90% at max rank
This type of scaling heavily punishes levellers and very non vanilla-like.

Instead you could find a desired sweet spot for all abilities, which should be lower than max rank's % weapon dmg and add a bonus flat dmg to each rank.

For example Slam all ranks now deal 75% weapon damage
Rank 1 has 15 bonus dmg
Rank 2 has 35 bonus dmg
Rank 3 has 60 bonus dmg
Rank 4 has 125 bonus dmg
