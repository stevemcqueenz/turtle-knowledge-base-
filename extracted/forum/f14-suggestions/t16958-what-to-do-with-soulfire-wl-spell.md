## Post 115718 by Killrogg — 2025-01-07T11:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115718#p115718 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I started out as an affliction warlock, but switched to destro, just to see how it feels.
While I do like the fire focus with quicker damage spikes, it is sad to see the most powerful fire spell "SouFire" being basically useless, even in destuction.

Is there any practical case to make for this spell outside of "opener spell" or "cast it against CCed PvP opponents?

Last time I played retail WoW was during WOTLK and they dealt with the same problem by adding a trigger chance to Immolate, to make the next SoulFire instant and completely free. (I assume it was bound to a skill in the destro tree, but I cannot remember. ) It's similar to the Nightfall procc in the afffliction tree.
I would like to see the same in Turtle WoW.
PvP can still use the regular CC-->SoulFire, while PvE gets a periodic use for SoulFire.
