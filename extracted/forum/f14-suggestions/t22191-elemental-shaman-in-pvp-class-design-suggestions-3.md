# Elemental Shaman in PVP Class design suggestions <3

## Post 153984 by Albear333forum — 2025-10-02T04:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153984#p153984 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

​Hello Turtle WoW Development Team,
​I wanted to start by saying I've been thoroughly enjoying the realm and appreciate the hard work that goes into its unique development. The recent class overhauls, such as those for Boomkin and Survival Hunter, have created some truly cool and engaging specs—it's inspiring to see the creative potential being realized!
​I feel Elemental Shaman has that same potential, especially in PvP, and I'm writing to share some passionate feedback. While the spec feels great and fun in PvE with the addition of Earthquake, its toolkit in player-versus-player combat feels significantly lacking. Currently, when melee threats close the gap, an Elemental Shaman is often left without effective defensive or escape tools.
​To help Elemental truly shine in PvP, here are a few concepts for consideration:
​PvP Utility & Defense Enhancements
​Mobility/Escape: Instant Ghost Wolf.
​Melee Mitigation: A talent that provides base knockback reduction while casting spells.
​Defensive Layer: A talent that grants some kind of absorb shield.
​Crowd Control (CC) Iterations:
​A talent that enhances the Earthbind Totem with a root effect (perhaps tied to the Hex spell line).
​A talent that enhances Frost Shock to apply a root.
​Offensive CC/Burst:
​A % chance to apply a stun effect on a Lightning Bolt cast.
​An ability/talent that enhances the next Shock spell cast (e.g., Earth Shock stuns, Flame Shock damage is applied all at once, or can be applied to multiple targets).
​Cast Time Reduction: A talent that grants a chance to make the next spell cast instant or significantly reduces cast time.
​Thank you for taking the time to read through these suggestions. My passion for the class is what drove me to write this, and I hope some of these ideas can inspire future changes.
​Have a great day!
