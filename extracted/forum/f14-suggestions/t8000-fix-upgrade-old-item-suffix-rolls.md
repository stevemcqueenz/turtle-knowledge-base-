# Fix / upgrade old item suffix rolls

## Post 51444 by Azeron — 2023-06-27T02:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51444#p51444 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently there's an odd system in place for world green drops and the occasional dungeon blue drops where they cannot roll certain suffixes at all, rings and amulets especially cannot roll any of the "of X Wrath" for example.

This suggestion would be to allow this to occur, applying logic to different armor classes and keeping values appropriate so as not to overshadow other loot, but enough to make things a little more interesting. (And also removing old issues like shields with fiery wrath etc)

This would also include allowing suffixes like the rare "of Holy Wrath" , "Marksmanship" , "Regeneration" to roll alongside all the usual suspects instead of being limited to the current small handful of items they can roll on.

Right now it seems a little too much to branch out into more custom suffixes like those added in Burning Crusade, though additional custom suffixes could be very interesting when applied to all eligible random suffix loot, or at least looking into some of the deprecated item suffixes that never made it out of the original vanilla beta (Proficiency suffixes could be a game changer for raiding melee builds)
