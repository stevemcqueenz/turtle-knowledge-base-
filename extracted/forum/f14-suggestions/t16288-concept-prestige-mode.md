Concept: **Prestige Mode** (Inspired by Ascension WoW)

 **How does it work?**
After reaching level 60, players can voluntarily reset their character's level back to 1.

 **Why?**
 - Longevity: Provides endgame players with a reason to keep playing beyond hitting max level.
 - Reputation Grind: Allows players to earn reputation or faction progress for additional rewards.
 - Replayability: Offers a fresh perspective on leveling while maintaining progression incentives.

Allowing multiple Prestiges with cumulative rewards. For example, each Prestige tier unlocks a new title or coins.

Thoughts?   hiding_smth_turtle

Every time I read one of your posts I lose a couple of brain cells.

Anyway, the idea of prestige is good, but there are too many variables and things to consider.

 -What will happen to the player's gold and items? If they were to lose them, isn't it the same as just deleting your character and making a new one with the same class and name?
And if they were to keep them, this creates a bunch of new issues - for example they could use quest rewards that don't have level requirements, or create overpowered twinks that would be otherwise impossible.

 -What will happen to completed quests? Will they reset or stay completed. If they stay conpleted, people will evetually run out of quests to level with (not a huge problem, since dungeon leveling is viable thanks to tents). If they are reset, it could lead to exploits with people acquiring quest rewards multiple times that they are not supposed to, or getting their attunements reset. It would also be very counterproductive if you're aiming for lorekeeper title (or overpowered, if that counter doesn't get reset).

 -What about challenges? Will newly prestiged level 1 characters be able to acquire challenges? What about ones they already completed? Or ones that don't allow trading and delete your inventory and mail, like the HC challenge?

There are a lot of questions that nees answering and a tremendous amount of work and testing to make a system like prestige work properly, and I'm not sure if we'll ever see it on Turtle. It might just be easier to create a new character if you want to level from 1 to 60 again.

Here's how I would do it:

Gold and items will be retained
Quest items retained, once prestiged, gain a level requirement ~5 less than their ilvl. I don't see battlegrounds as an issue since someone can always turn off their experience and twink at any level. However, if it was a problem: create battlegrounds that are separate once prestiged; i.e., prestige bg and non-prestiged bg.

Completed quests refresh. Duplicates of quest items should be fine; if there's a problem, make the item unique.
I don't see it as an issue for lorekeeper since someone could always do lorekeeper at lvl 60.

For prestige, challenges need to be case-by-case. Hardcore would suspend all of a character's inventory, gold, etc. until they reached level 60. At that point, they could return it to themselves and end their hardcore challenge. I don't see an issue with repeating challenges; potentially, further rewards could be available.

I would love to vet and answer more questions on the subject.
