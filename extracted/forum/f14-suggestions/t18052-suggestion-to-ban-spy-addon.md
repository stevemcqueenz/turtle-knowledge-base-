# Suggestion to ban Spy addon

## Post 122904 by Barokai — 2025-03-18T17:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122904#p122904 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Pretty simple post. For those who don't know, Spy is an addon that's simply World of Warcraft Wallhack.

Gankers use it to be instantly alerted of anyone from the opposite faction within a certain distance of them, regardless of anything like Line of Sight being a factor. It does not break stealth, but it will actually alert you of anyone unless they're already in stealth mode before being within about 200 yards of you.

It's quite literally cheating, and was popularized in classic wow servers. Turtle has banned a lot of degenerate stuff that ruined classic, so might as well add this to the list.

I don't mean stuff like HonorSpy or anything else, just Spy.

Greetings, even though i understand your concerns, your gathered informations are false.
Spy uses a very old lua code mechanic which scans for nearby combatlog interactions.
That said, it has not a range of 200yards. The range for combatlog interactions are exactly 50 yards. (and it only shows an enemy player if they used a spell and/or an ability or if you hovered with your mouse over them)

If you have so much concern about that addon you should be even more concerned about the addon named "shaguscan" which uses an advanced scanning filter system of Superwow (which has no range limitations and scans instead by "PLAYER_ENTERING_WORLD". (which also works for pvp scanning)
It is much more accurate and shows up enemy players even if they stand still and cast no spells or abilitys.
