Hello, I have some suggestions for the druid class changes, making them a bit more wotlk-ish, feel free to discuss

Replace Claw with Mangle (or add mangle as a passive for feral  - causes the target to take 30% additional damage from bleed effects)
Predatory Strikes (T4 feral talent)
Increases your melee attack power in Cat, Bear and Dire Bear Forms by 50/100/150% of your level.
Proposed change:
Increases your melee attack power in Cat, Bear and Dire Bear Forms by 50/100/150% of your level and 7/14/20% of any attack power on your equipped weapon.  In addition, your finishing moves have a 7/13/20% chance per combo point to make your next Nature spell with a base casting time less than 10 sec. become an instant cast spell.

Leader of the Pack (T7 feral talent)
While in Cat, Bear or Dire Bear Form, the Leader of the Pack increases ranged and melee critical chance of all party members within 45 yards by 3%.
Proposed change:
While in Cat, Bear or Dire Bear Form, the Leader of the Pack increases ranged and melee critical chance of all party and raid members within 100 yards by 3%. Leader of the Pack ability also causes affected targets to heal themselves for 4% of their total health when they critically hit with a melee or ranged attack.  The healing effect cannot occur more than once every 6 sec.  In addition, you gain 8% of your maximum mana when you benefit from this heal.

Tree of Life Form (capstone resto talent)
Add Glyph of the Forest (similar to Glyph of the stars with green undertone and leaves instead of stars (i.e. similar to regrowrth leaves effect)
