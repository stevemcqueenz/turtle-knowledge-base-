# BG balance

## Post 165024 by Delmar — 2026-01-13T09:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165024#p165024 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Friends, lately there have been many forum threads regarding PvP balance. Most of them are focused on class balance. The problem does indeed exist, but we will most likely see its solution in the next patch. Until that update becomes available, I would like to propose a relatively simple yet effective way to address this imbalance, which could be implemented with a regular weekly update.

Disable battleground queueing as a group (even for groups of two players). In this case, solo players and twinks would be placed into fully random teams, and balance would depend solely on randomness. At the very least, no team would end up with 3–4 overgeared shamans or paladins who currently decide the outcome of the match.
