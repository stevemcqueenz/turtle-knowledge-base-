# Priest racials available to other races

## Post 8370 by Kpbendi72 — 2020-11-05T10:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8370#p8370 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the major fail of vanilla design (was fixed in TBC) is priest racial.
Practically on alliance side you have to be dwarf if you take healing role semi-seriously.
The idea is to let other races get other races' spells but with conditions.
 1. have to be lvl60
 2. have to be exalted with the race
 2. have to finish a long, hard and time-consuming quest chain for each spell

This way we can add some extra content for priests after hitting 60 but would not make choosing the race trivial. If someone wanna play healer and start raiding asap he still can choose dwarf and enjoy op priest racials from the very beginning, but choosing nelf would not make an unavoidable disadvantage to heal in raids, they only need to work a bit more.
