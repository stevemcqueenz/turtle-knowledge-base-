Hello,

I'd like to suggest separating the ogre illusions. As it stands there are 6 variations. Out of these 5 use the same model while the 6th has a separate model. It is my opinion that the 6th and separate model looks far better than the others. On top of that the other model(with 5 variations) barely has any animations. It doenst even have a casting or sitting animation for example. The 6th varation has all these animations because it uses the moonkin skeleton. All ogre NPC's in the world also use the moonkin model, giving them animations.
Unlike the other 5. This one also shows your weapons when sheathed.

I added a link to all the current ogre models: [viewtopic.php?p=18189#p18189](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18189#p18189)

I'd suggest separating the illusions by model. This would also allow for the possibility to add more color schemes to the 6th variation like the purple one from TBC:
