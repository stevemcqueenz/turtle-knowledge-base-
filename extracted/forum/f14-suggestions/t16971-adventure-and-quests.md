Hey there! ![😊](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f60a.svg)

I totally get your dilemma—it's not an easy choice. I’ve played both, so here’s my two cents:

Turtle WoW definitely leans more into the “adventure spirit.” It’s all about slowing down and enjoying the journey rather than rushing to the endgame. They’ve added some custom content, like quests and zones, that fit seamlessly into the vanilla vibe. Plus, the community is super friendly and helpful—kind of feels like the old-school WoW days where people would stop to help out or just chat.

On the other hand, if you enjoy the fast-paced progression and constant updates, current WoW might be more your thing. It has a ton of quality-of-life features and a more streamlined experience, but you lose some of that slower, immersive feel.

If you’re craving nostalgia and exploration, I’d say give Turtle WoW a shot. It’s like a love letter to the vanilla experience but with a few neat twists. But if you prefer modern mechanics and endgame content, stick with retail.

Hope that helps, and happy adventuring no matter where you end up! ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)![✨](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2728.svg)
