# Vagrant's Endeavor is cool... BUT ?

## Post 69796 by Posti — 2023-10-09T16:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69796#p69796 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi y'all

So yeah, this new challenge just came out, and it feels fresh, inovant and **exciting**.

But after a few levels into it, I felt that something was missing at a few points :

_**Professions** : tailorist, blacksmith can gives pretty good white items at the beginning, but then after a few lvls you start getting some epic stuff and nore more white item. you can still sold them, make some money... to buy some generic weapons ? I also don't forget that sharpening stones (for exemple) are usefull after all.

So, I was wondering, could it be possible to make those professions more relevant for this kind of challenge by adding some new recipes that you can by directly to the master profession ?

_**World drop and loot table** : having a cool white piece of item dropping from a mob is cool at the beginning. You begin to stuff yourself with them and after you a while it becomes one of your gold income when you starts to sell them.
But as a character who choose to only wear white and grey items, it can be frustrating to see random npc wearing some cool lonking leather chest or some iron axe. But sadly, you can't loot them.

So, I was wondering... could it be possible to add more white and grey items (not OP items just regular one for your braket lvl) into the loot table, and make it "realistic" in a sense that a Murloc won't gives you a pair of mail boots. and **ALSO** adding more pauldrons and helmet to the loot table "in general" ?

I hope everyone gets my point of view, also english is not my native i'm having a hard time writing this, my head hurts lol.

I also hope a Turlte Wow game developper will put his eyes on this topic.

And again, thanks to them for making such refreshing new challenge content     smiling_turtle_head
