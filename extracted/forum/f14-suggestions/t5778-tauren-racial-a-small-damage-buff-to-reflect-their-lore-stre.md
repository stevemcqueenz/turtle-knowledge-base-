# Tauren Racial: A small damage buff to reflect their lore strength

## Post 36298 by Nightowl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-27T15:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36298#p36298 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Makes no sense that some gnome can punch as hard a literal 10 feet tall Muscle Beast Minotaur. Lorewise a Tauren charging at someone should cause fear, dread and excessive amount of sweat because, duh, 10 feet tall beast charging you with the intent of delivering some top-tier smackdown. You ain't walking out of that one that easily. And they're fast too since Cairne was dancing around Garrosh like he was some toddler punk. Big, tall and fast. Literal machines made for murder, but held back by their shamanistic beliefs. Yet how is it reflected in-game? They're smaller than they should be. They have no damage increasing racial that would imply that this is a nasty beast capable of ripping someone apart with bare hands. Lets fix it. All in the name LORE and Roleplay.

What I was thinking might fit:

 - 1-5% flat Strength or AP increase. 5% is the dream, but realistically that'll be too strong so around 3% maybe?
 - 1-3% flat damage increase - but might be a bit too powerful outside of flat 1%.
 - 3-10% crit damage increase across the board?

I doubt that these buffs will make them over the top, in both PvE and PvP, but will at the same time reflect lorewise strength of these muscle machines. Like come on bro, I bet a tauren can bench a literal (well okay, maybe like 300-500kg but it's still a lot).

I also noticed that a lot of tauren seem to favor Glaives/Polearms as their weapons. Perhaps adding a racial for that would be neat? Just for flavor since there ain't any classes that use Polearms for PvE melee. I guess you can make a case for 2h Slam Spec with windfury totem but it falls off to dual wield unfortunately. Would still be nice tho.
