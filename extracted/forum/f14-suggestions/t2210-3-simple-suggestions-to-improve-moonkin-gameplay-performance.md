# 3 Simple Suggestions to Improve Moonkin Gameplay+Performance

## Post 13686 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2021-11-09T09:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13686#p13686 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The proposed changes to balance are nice bandaids, which improve mana costs, and allows wrath to be useful in certain situation, but still leaves boomie with boring gameplay and the same sustain issues on long boss fights and during leveling.

Level 1 through 10 druid gameplay (where you wrath a target as they walk over then meele them down to death), feels to me like blizz' original idea for balance gameplay, which is a combination of meele and damage spells. This would explain why the tree has 10% meele damage and a clearcast proc only generated from meele swings. It seem that the original design intention was to make balance a hybrid of Ele and Enh shaman, but when it came for us to raid viably, we simply ended up with just a diet-pepsi elemental shaman.

The problem with trying to realize caster/meele hybrid Moonkin in 1.12, is that meele can only be practically used as a very last resort, when completely OOM and 5SRing. Meele weaving DOES exist on 1.12, but one fatal flaw hinders this: unlike shaman and pally, pressing any spell or completing any spell, both will reset the swing timer. This makes waiting 3 or so seconds for a single swing, absolutely not worth it. The only way you can meele weave without penalty in 1.12, is by using a 1.5 speed dagger which can fit in right after a moonfire, but the damage it does is so pitiful that it's not at all worth it.

This is a big shame, because Moonkin-itemized gear like dungeon set 2 and aq40 tier 2.5 all add str, agi, ontop of the spellpower and spell crit this gear offers. The str/agi parts are begging to be used, but not at all worth it with a 1.5s weapon.

As such, to improve how moonkin gameplay engagement feel and performance, and to re-validate the purpose of existing moonkin itemization, I suggest these two simple changes:

 1. Remove swing timer resets completely
 2. Add mana regen proportional to meele damage done

Note that suggestion 2 is similar to TBC, but TBC's solution has a problem, it only rewards proportionally to your AP value. This means that you are still not incentivized to use Faerie Fire, and in practice you simply switch to an AP weapon and switch back once done regenning mana.

My suggestion 2 would instead be based off of damage done, which incentivizes powerful spellpower 2handers that currently only spellpower ret uses, and incentivizes keeping FF up, so more mana can be regenerated.

Combined with the suggestion 1, this would incentivize that aforementioned moonkin tier gear as the loss in pure spell damage would be made up for by both the meele damage added, and extra mana provided (so that dpm inefficient spells like moonfire can be used to push dps).

In single target cases like raid bosses, the rotation with a 3.0 speed 2hander would look like:
 * FF running up to target -> Swing + IS -> Moonfire -> Swing+Starfire 3 times. repeat

What is interesting about this setup is that Natures Grace will throw off the cleanness of this rotation, and allow Wrath's fast casting to fill a niche of buying just 1.5 seconds of dps more before it's time to refresh dots and get 2 swings in. This is an extremely natural way to incorporate Wrath into an ideal rotation filler, compared to WotLK's more hamfisted solution. And also because the gap between swings would max at 9 or so seconds, its no longer optimal to use weapon switch macros like TBC.

In multi target situations, hurricane may be used, but once its done and on cooldown, the ideal method in 1.12 is to tabtarget moonfire. Instead with these suggestions + current turtle changes, this rotation would become:
 * tabtarget swing+FF -> MF -> swing+IS -> tabtarget FF -> Swing+MF -> IS. tabtarget repeat

Far more involved! And the group's meele will be much happier about the druid providing FF uptime on multiple mobs.

However, there's one last problem that I see in this gameplay: Some players still might not FF as it does hurt your DPS in the short term, which isn't as fun for players. Restos would rather focus on healing which is very important for any group, which leaves ferals as the ideal FFer. Which is the case for dungeon bear tanks, but raid bear tanks are usually busy singletargeting 1 mob where targeting another and back is rather undesired, and cats are punsihed for switching targets at all, all this ontop of the 6 second cooldown. This leaves us with Moonkins as the ideal FFer, which blizzard confirmed their intention in TBC with imp FF.

This is why i've thought of a 3rd suggestion, different than TBC's massive buff to moonkin FF, to still incentivize bringing moonkins to raids as ideal FFers:

 3. Place FF off the GCD with its own 1.5s or 3.0s cooldown, when in Moonkin form

I could live without this suggestion implemented, but it would vastly improve people's desire to take moonkins to raids and dungeons like in TBC (instead of how it is in 1.12 where people roll their eyes and sometimes put up with it).

But this solves TBC's problem where like on a 5 pack pull in a dungeon, your meele group members would want you to FF all mobs 5 times, but you'd rather not spend 8 seconds doing that dealing 0 damage, instead you would rather just FF one mob and start DPSing. But with this suggestion, off the GCD means its no sweat off your back to keep FF up on all targets, and and this of course synergizes with the tabtarget nature of dungeon cleaving. With suggestion 3 added, rotations now become completely optimal with a 3.0 speed weapon:

Single target:
 * Swing+FF+IS -> MF -> Swing+3 Starfires. repeat

Multi target;
 * tabtarget Swing+FF+IS -> MF. tabtarget repeat

The single target rotation is streamlined a bit for when every 40 seconds a new FF can be applied. The multiple target rotation now maintains full dps while also providing strong party utility, as you can now tabtarget every 3 seconds instead of 4.5. Combined with hurricane's dps and its effects, Moonkin is now highly sought after for high trash dps and now has a spot in a spellcleave dungeon comp.

Again, suggestion 3 might be too strong, and it'll make warlocks jealous as they have the same problems with their curses. I'm okay with leaving this one out, but it would be a game changer for making moonkin gameplay far more engaging.

 **Tying it all together**

Adding suggestions 1+2 will make the personal fun of a moonkin far better and provide it with unique gearing as a true meele/caster hybrid, which will somewhat increase desire for others to take the spec to dungeons and raids. Adding suggestions 1+2+3 would make it ideal to bring 1 or 2 to a 40man raid, and very well desired for a dungeon.

 **PVP implications:**

In 1.12, 31/0/20 moonkin is vastly subpar compared to 30/0/21 because of how damn good a massive instant heal via nature's swiftness is. Also, since moonkin can only dps at their strongest by hardcasting, they always stay ranged, and bear form's HP buff makes it superior to moonkin for surviving an undesired meele upon them.

However, my suggestions would such greatly improve Moonkin's dps when in meele range, that running away wouldn't be so mandatory, in fact the opposite; this would make moonkin viable as an offensive playstyle spec. This will see roots being used additionally as a method of getting close to an enemy, instead of running away like its used in 1.12. As the new moonkin would be challenging opponents in meele range, they would take more damage and eventually would have to dip out of combat again with roots so they can be healed or self heal. To allow yourself to stay in meele range longer for higher damage, longer, this would incentivize stam gearing, something rather unique to moonkin that most other casters are not incentivized to do.

Suggestion 3 would mean that moonkin is now a counter to be feared by rogues as it can FF while also dealing damage. The entire enemy team could be practically FF'd with moonfire on top, which could be used to apply pressure to enemy healers as they have to work through lots of dispel fodder. Priests' 2x magic dispell will make them fare better here than paladins' 1x.

All these combine to make 31/0/20 moonkin a much more viable PVP spec with its own unique playstyle and would very much rival the 30/0/21 balance/NS spec.

 **Dungeon Tanking Implications:**

Moonkin OFFtanking in 1.12 is very strong and underrated as it stands. Moonfire is very strong ranged snap threat, that can be used to save a squishier class, especially a healer. As ideal dungeon trash moonkin dps has you spreading moonfire on each target, this would normally have a few adds that a prot warrior has low threat on, walking over to you.

But with suggestions 1+2, a moonkin's ideal dps is now in meele range. This effectively increases threat by the ranged threat pull modifier, allowing more adds to be offtanked that would otherwise be running free. This also opens an option for a moonkin to use nonbalance talent points into feral for thick hide, or if they don't want to be offtanking, to instead spec into the new 20% balance threat reduction in the resto tree. A nice shakeup to 1.12's cookie cutter.

This ability to offtank synergizes very very well with turtlewow's new leather spellpower itemization, and again strengthens the value of the existing t0.5 and t2.5's higher armor than normal 1.12 clothkins would use.

My suggestions wouldn't really solve the Moonkin maintanking meme's problem of them being very unsurvivable. No worries here about invalidating prot paladins.

 **Conclusion**:

I had a rather unfun time playing Moonkin on classic wow vanila+tbc. These simple changes, even without the buffs made in the current proposed changes, would be all thats needed for me to really enjoy playing balance in the way i always felt 2004 blizzard intended it to be. I have no desire to play the current proposed Moonkin, as its largely the same unrewarding un-engaging gameplay, but with suggestion 1+2 added, I would absolutely roll a new moonkin to 60 and at the very least raid with it as a fun alt.
