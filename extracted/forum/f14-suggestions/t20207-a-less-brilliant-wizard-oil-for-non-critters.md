# A less Brilliant Wizard oil for non Critters

## Post 138430 by excin (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-08T12:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138430#p138430 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Superior Wizard Oil in TBC was not an upgrade over Brilliant Wizard Oil, but a direct upgrade to plain Wizard Oil, it just served another utility. It was the preferred oil for affliction warlocks and prot paladins.

At 42 spell power it's only 6 sp above brilliant oil without the whole 1% crit.

It'll certainly increase usage of illusion dust, as it will be more worth it to use on ret paladins than brilliant oil.
You can even throw in a Noble Topaz in the recipe to make those things useful.
