# On Tents and Survival

## Post 28066 by Bargmar — 2022-10-28T01:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28066#p28066 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A friend and I had some ideas that I thought would be neat for the Survival skill, specifically with Tents. The idea would involve the following steps:

◘ The effects of multiple Tents no longer stack. Instead through survival you can learn to make other "campsite" items that, when combined within a vicinity of one another, create a "campsite". Each piece added to your campsite increases the speed of the rested XP gained. Imagine it: your friend makes a tent, so you create a campfire, then another friend places a sleeping bag and then a beer keg! By your powers combined you have created a cozy little campsite and each piece increased the rate that rested XP is earned. All of these "campsite" items would be created with Survival.

◘ Make it so that Tents cannot be placed inside a city or anywhere near an Inn. This would prevent tent-huddling in the middle of towns, Inn doors being blocked and also just general clutter from making pretty towns crowded. Tents and the "campsites" I am suggesting here can only be used out in the wild, where a campsite or tent should be!

◘  Add a feature at Inns where you can pay the Innkeeper a small fee (10-15 silver, maybe higher) to gain a buff. While under the effects of this buff, you have a faster rested XP gain (perhaps equivalent to a campsite, possibly higher, Inns should be more comfortable than a tent after all). The buff stays on as long as you pay the fee and remain inside the Inn, but as soon as you leave the Inn it goes away until you pay the fee again. This would be a great option to urge people to want to take up survival to eventually be able to get rested xp status "for free". It would also benefit those who choose not to do Survival at all, whatever their reason, and still give them a source of faster rested XP.

With the above steps you would simultaneously flesh out the Survival skill a bit more, make Inns a hub for socializing/roleplaying again and also make camping a true wilderness/safehouse experience rather than a hobo-huddle only happening at Goldshire and Crossroads with people constantly spamming worldchat for more and more tents.
