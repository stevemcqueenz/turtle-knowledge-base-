# Lightning Storm and Ethereal Form for Shamans

## Post 6423 by Dragunovi (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2020-06-18T20:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6423#p6423 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Alright, not gonna lie, this is a bit different than items and quests and might be a bit controversial but i'll still say it.**

After thinking about the faction balance being severely being tilted towards the Alliance and their hybrid class, **the Paladin** already having **Holy Strike** made me think about improving the **Shamans** of the **Horde**.
After digging around the databases for a long time 2 abilities caught my eye: **Lightning Storm** and **Ethereal Form**. Also they're learnt by using ***Tablet*s**, like how Holy Strike uses ***Librams*** to learn Holy Strike ranks.
I'll start with **Lightning Storm**.

It's an AoE Spell that's casted around your target. It does a small burst of Nature damage and ticks around that area for 15 seconds. It has 3 ranks: For level 20, 30 and 40.
[https://classic.wowhead.com/item=1597/t ... ning-storm](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=1597/tablet-of-lightning-storm) (**Tablet of Lightning Storm**)
teaches <https://classicdb.ch/?spell=2616> (**zzOLDLightning Storm**) (**Rank 1**) (**Level 20**)
[https://classic.wowhead.com/item=1619/t ... g-storm-ii](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=1619/tablet-of-lightning-storm-ii) (**Tablet of Lightning Storm II**)
teaches <https://classicdb.ch/?spell=2617> (**zzOLDLightning Storm**) (**Rank 2**) (**Level 30**)
[https://classic.wowhead.com/item=5715/t ... -storm-iii](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=5715/tablet-of-lightning-storm-iii)  (**Tablet of Lightning Storm III**)
teaches <https://classicdb.ch/?spell=6058> (**zzOLDLightning Storm**) (**Rank 3**) (**Level 40**)

Now to **Ethereal Form**.
It's an ability that makes you (or your party member in a 30 yard range if its ***Rank 2***) immune to physical attacks but stops you from meleeing or using physical abilities for 10 seconds. (Consider adding this ability if you can add a cooldown to this ability, or else it's just broken.) I imagine it having a 10 minute cooldown, as shamans can utilise this ability more with their spells.
[https://classic.wowhead.com/item=5708/t ... ereal-form](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=5708/tablet-of-ethereal-form) (**Tablet of Ethereal Form**)
teaches <https://classicdb.ch/?spell=1945> (**Ethereal Form**) (**Rank 1**) (**Level 24**)
[https://classic.wowhead.com/item=4180/t ... al-form-ii](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=4180/tablet-of-ethereal-form-ii) (Tablet of Ethereal Form II)
teaches <https://classicdb.ch/?spell=5391> (**Ethereal Form**) (**Rank 2, Allows you to target party members**)
(**Level 34**)

Well, i said my piece. I am expecting some negative feedback, but that's understandable, your feedback is important too **<3**.
