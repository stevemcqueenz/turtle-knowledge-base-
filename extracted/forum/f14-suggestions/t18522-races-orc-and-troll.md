Easy there — I didn’t mean to break the balance of the universe with a theorycrafting suggestion. The 20% was clearly just an example, not an official patch note. I even mentioned it could scale with missing HP — it’s not some locked-in, absurd figure.

The idea here is to discuss possible changes. I proposed something that clearly doesn’t appeal to everyone (you included), but starting from a bold suggestion and working toward a reasonable, balanced outcome is a lot more productive — and frankly, more civilized — than jumping straight into personal attacks or mockery.
