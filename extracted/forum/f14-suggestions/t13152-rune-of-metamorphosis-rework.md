Okay so the trinket is abit lacklustre atm, I feel like it needs to give each form a unique effect in addition to reducing the mana cost.

<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19340>

I've had it awhile and never really felt the need to use it, even in PvP I'd rather have my other trinkets on.

Reduces Mana cost of all shapshifting spells by 100% and grants an effect in a certain form, Lasts 20 Sec

Bear Form: Grants 30 Rage -20% Dmg Taken (Bear Barkskin?)
Cat Form: Grants 100 Energy +15% Phsyical Dmg
Travel Form: Grants Immunity to Movement effects (can be dispelled)
Aquatic Form: +100% Swim Speed
Moonkin Form: +15% Spell Power
Tree Form: Reduces mana cost of Hots by 15%

You can only use one of the effects when you pop the trinket, so if you wanted the bear effect you'd have to pop the trinket in bear form etc

I think adding these additional effects to the trinket will help see it more use, as at the min it's just stuck in my bank.

Will give more meaning to the name of the item 'Metamorphosis' Basically powering up your forms for the duration, rather than just a mana cost reducer.
