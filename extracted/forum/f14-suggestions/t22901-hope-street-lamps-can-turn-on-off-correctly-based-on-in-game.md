# Hope street lamps can turn on/off correctly based on in-game time, and add more lamps.

## Post 159873 by Duckwarrior — 2025-11-17T04:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159873#p159873 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I'm using a darker nights mod, which is one of my favorite things about Vanilla WoW. Other versions don't support this. The darker nights make the atmosphere much more immersive. (Mod link: [viewtopic.php?p=158694](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158694)). Of course, it would be great if the devs could officially support a 25% brightness night option in the future (50% and 100% might be problematic for most players).

My suggestion is to fix an existing issue in the base game: the street lamps in Stormwind don't turn on/off correctly according to the in-game time. I haven't noted the exact times, but during the mod's darker nights, I've returned to Stormwind several times to find the lamps out – sometimes when the server time is 21:00 or 04:00. I know server time isn't Eastern Kingdoms time, but even without the mod, the lamps are often off at night.

I also hope the devs can add more street lamps and campfires, not just in new zones but also appropriately placed in old ones. For example, hostile faction camps in Stranglethorn Vale (goblins, trolls, humans) should have some light sources – after all, they aren't beasts that can live in total darkness, right? I envision a future where I can travel from Arathi Highlands through the jungles on a quiet, deep night, following torch-lit mountain paths to visit the dwarf brothers at Aerie Peak.
