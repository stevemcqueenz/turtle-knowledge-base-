Because agi already gives armor and melee/ranged crit. Also dodge. You're really not changing much besides the amount of those stats you get, unless spell crit is included in that 1% crit.

Not every race needs to be good at everything. That's why so many racials are situational or only benefit certain classes/benefit them more. Like resists being better for tanking, or Tauren having a resist racial, health racial, and AOE stun racial. They're like- objectively *the* tanking race, and even got a tanking oriented shaman racial recently.

So like, high elf casters get something even if the passives don't benefit them as much. Not every racial has to cater to every class. Also, every class can benefit from extra armor and dodge just like every class benefits from tauren racials.

Yeah, in retrospect it's a bit of a dumb comparison to use Tauren as the example since their racials are a lot more flexible, but even still, there's some racials out there that only make sense for one class or even none. Like- throwing spec only benefits warrior and rogue for... Maybe the first ten levels? Other than that it's a waste of space. Profession racials only benefit classes that will use those professions. Gun spec is clearly a hunter racial but it also has its niche case use for warrior/rogue, Orcs command only benefits hunter and warlock, etc etc.

You will find a lot of inflexible racials in vanilla. It's not a bad thing, just a quirk of the game and something that contributes to mechanical/lore identity.

My point is not every racial has to cover every single class. If your race has SOMETHING for everyone, then good. If it doesn't? We can change that. But as it stands high elf still has benefits no matter what you decide to play, and trying to give even more benefits can lead to balance issues. Besides, people only play them because they're pretty, not because they're strong.
