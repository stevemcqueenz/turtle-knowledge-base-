They do drop from low-level dungeon bosses, just at a very low chance. I got one from Grovetender Engryss in Crescent Grove recently. Looking at [the database](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=51217) it seems lvl 50+ endbosses get 100%, most lvl 40+ bosses get 5% and the rest 1%, down to even RFC and VanCleef.

But yeah, your best bet at low level are the repeatable quests. The [one for lvl 10-19](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=60030) uses incredibly cheap materials as long as you kill a few low-level cat-enemies (the collars are expensive on the AH, I usually sell them when I level alts who are Night Elfs or Dwarves/Gnomes because those starter zones are drowning in cats).
