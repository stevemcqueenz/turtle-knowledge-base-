I am personally of the opinion that when it comes to world buffs, rather than remove them entirely, they should change the stacking rules. Make it so you can get *some* world buffs, but not *all*. Say, divide them into two tiers based on how big a benefit they give, and you can only have one T1, and one T2 buff. Pick and choose the ones most relevant to you.

It's pretty generally agreed, I think, that full Shaman and Paladin buffs aren't as valuable as full world buffs, so getting to keep some buffs would soften the loss. Plus, it keeps the content related to those buffs relevant. Finally, as I've said in the other thread, it creates the potential to create new world buffs for Turtle without either creating power creep, or making those rewards irrelevant to a subset of the server population, and I'm of the opinion that it's always better design to create new options for expansion than to close them off.
