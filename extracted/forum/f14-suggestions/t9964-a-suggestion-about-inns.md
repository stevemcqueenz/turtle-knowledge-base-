<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=65117#p65117 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

just a thought, maybe others have had the thought as well. In my opinion, Inns should give the rested xp rate of tents to encourage players to congregate in them instead of standing in piles in front of org/sw. Maybe make tents only party wide, so they have a use if you are out questing and don't want to run back to an inn. Just a thought, because the piles of people standing under a tent is kinda jarring

This would completely defeat the purpose of tents and thus defeat most of the purpose for the survival skill. The AFK players are another issue entirely, there needs to be a way to visualize (without a macro, I can provide one.) how much rested XP you have. This would easily prevent them as most assume that sitting under the tent forever will just give them tons of XP in spite of it capping out pretty quickly. I never stand under a tent for more than about 5-8 minutes, and that's if I had 0 rested at all.

Party wide tents is alright, but even then many things would need to be edited such as the length of time the tent stays present for.
