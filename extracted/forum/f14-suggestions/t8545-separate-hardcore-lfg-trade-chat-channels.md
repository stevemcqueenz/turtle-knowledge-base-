# Separate Hardcore LFG / Trade Chat Channels

## Post 54996 by Thedenast — 2023-07-28T20:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54996#p54996 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all, I know that <Still Alive> guild is going to be soon replaced with a separate Hardcore chat channel, but in my humble opinion it will simply turn one overcrowded chat into another overcrowded chat.

I've just logged in at around 8-9UTC and absolute majority of <Still Alive> messages are lfg/trade spam with people sending their copypasted message every 10-15 seconds. Anyone who reaches out for any help drowns in spam messages as the chat window completely refreshes every couple of seconds.

Hence, I would like to propose to create not only a general HC chat with an update, but also HC Trade and HC LFG chats to keep the spam separated from general discussion. I don't know anything about WoW modding, but it seems to me as an easy fix that would help a whole lot.
