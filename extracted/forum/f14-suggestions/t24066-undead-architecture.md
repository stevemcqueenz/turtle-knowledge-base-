# Undead Architecture

## Post 169164 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-02-22T03:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169164#p169164 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Whenever I see this part of Undercity, it gives me vibes of a dark role-playing game from the era of World of Warcraft, something like American McGee's Alice in Wonderland.

I think the Undead deserve more love in their architecture, since they don't have unique buildings; they're just ruined versions of human architecture, which is even used in Darkshire, which takes away some of the charm. I understand they're located in ruined human sites, but I think that after seven years in-game, they deserve to have their own unique buildings in new locations and to have some old ones updated. That part of Undercity fits perfectly with what undead architecture should be.
