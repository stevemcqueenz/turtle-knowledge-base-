## Post 12192 by Karmax — 2021-09-14T07:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12192#p12192 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Disclaimer:

I haven't played a druid on Turtle WoW yet (I'm honestly a bit burned out from playing them right now), but I checked the database and my search didn't return any results in the matter.

Background information:

Druids have questlines for both their aquatic and bear forms, but not for their cat form - this has always bothered me since I first started playing druids on retail back in 2006 (and on several private Vanilla servers ever since).

Upon hitting level 10 druids are sent to Dentrite Starblaze in Moonglade who then asks them to talk to the "Great Bear Spirit" as part of the questline.

There's also a "Great Cat Spirit" (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=11957>) in Moonglade which I'm sure was intended to be part of a level 20 cat form questline which unfortunately was never introduced.

Instead, at level 20 druids simply learn cat form from their class trainers.
Same with dire bear form at level 40.

Suggestion:

Please introduce a level 20 cat form questline, starting in Darnassus and Thunder Bluff at the same druid trainers who also start the bear form questline.
The player should then be sent to Dentrite Starblaze in Moonglade (same as with the bear form questline) who asks you to talk to the Great Cat Spirit.
After that there should be a task similar to the bear form questline where you had to defeat Lunaclaw (Body and Heart).
It would also be nice if there was a particular questline for the dire bear form at level 40.

I'm not a native English speaker and I'm not very good at coming up with specific ideas for the questline - so I leave it to others to elaborate the details of this suggestion.

Good idea, it is evidently a planned content but cut because of lack of time (like a lot of other specific quests, like race quests).

Vanilla production used a simple metric for which quest had to be made first : the more players who can interact with it, the more urgent it was to make (that's an efficient rule, because they knew they had not enough content). So they did first neutral quests and faction quests. Then they had time for some class quests, but not all of them. They planned for race quests but didn't manage to get there.
They were so behind schedule that all neutral quests are not even finished (pirate quest in Azshara is broken for example).
