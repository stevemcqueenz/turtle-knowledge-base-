# Class/race minor powers

## Post 18899 by Tilsair — 2022-05-29T02:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18899#p18899 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there!
I always found Priest passives and the new Shaman passives really cool. Could it be possible to add like a special power for all class/race combos ?

My idea was stuff like giving orc warriors a "Frightening stare" ability, which would fear an NPC, or slow a player. Human warriors could get a "Last breath" ability, which would heal a bit of HP when you go low on health. Undead mages would get a "Dark incantations" ability that could make the next low-mana cost spell be free, while goblin mages could get a "Gnomish brilliance" spell which would speed up one low-mana spell cast by like 0.3 secs.

I understand that these abilities are hard to come up with and then to make them balanced as well is quite a piece of work. Maybe it could be just flavor stuff, with orc warriors getting a salute from horde guards when walking nearby. Could be anything really, just something to add that extra immersion and making your class/race combo just a bit different from your friend who is playing the same class but with a different race.

Thanks for reading!   smiling_turtle_head
