# Alliance Questing in Barrens

## Post 139862 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-16T13:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139862#p139862 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

I think that it would be fun to add additional questing for alliance players in the Barrens; this is a fun zone that Alliance players don't have much playtime in.

This could be accomplished by opening a small walkable path from Bael Hardul into the Barrens, so that Bael Hardul can then be used as a questing hub and flight path.  Quests could then be given to NPCs there such as attacking the Venture Co. in Northern Barrens or fighting Centaurs that have been raiding Bael Hardul.

Further, alliance quest givers could be added to Northwatch and Bael Modan, further extending the questing opportunities.  Bael Modan NPCs could ask you to kill quillboar that have been attacking the digsite, and Northwatch could ask you to hunt nearby raptors.

There are already two alliance outposts in the Barrens with Northwatch and Bael Modan, so from a lore perspective there already is justification for alliance activity in this zone bordered by two Horde capitals.  It would just be an area that is more hostile to alliance forces. If no alliance quests are added, it would not change the fact that the alliance is already present there with armed forces in Northwatch and an entire digging operation in Bael Modan.

Cenarion Hold in Silithus and Chillwind Camp in Western Plaguelands are examples of outposts in hostile territory that would likely be much more dangerous than the two locations I mentioned in the Barrens, and there isn't anything wrong with that. If those two locations can survive without being overwhelmed and destroyed by silithis or scourge forces, then alliance quests in Barrens is justified.

From a lore perspective, there is not difference between an alliance player in the barrens and the alliance NPCs already there.
