# PvP Weekly Quests & Adding an Adventurer’s Guide

## Post 154022 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-02T10:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154022#p154022 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello again again Turtle WoW team,

The new weekly quests are a nice addition to give players objectives. However, I feel like the current system could be improved to better fit the unique spirit of Turtle WoW.

Weekly Quests feel too generic right now (mainly “go collect tokens” in MC, raids, or dungeons). Adding more diversity would make them more engaging. For example:

 -PvE exploration quests (discover specific locations each week).
 -Profession-based quests (crafting or gathering rare materials for a faction).
 -Outdoor challenges (defend/attack a certain area, or defeat a special NPC).
 -Small “adventure” or RP-style quests (find a hidden NPC, solve a riddle, etc.).
 -Add a PvP weekly quest (especially for the PvP realm). This could involve: Getting X honorable kills / Winning a certain number of battlegrounds or arenas / Killing the leader of an enemy faction.

This would make the world feel more alive and give PvP players a weekly goal.

Introduce an Adventurer’s Guide (similar to Retail’s Adventure Journal). Instead of just talking to one NPC, new players could have a simple interface guiding them through available weekly/daily objectives, so they don’t feel lost in the capitals.

These changes would keep the idea of weekly objectives but make them much more immersive, diverse, and engaging for both PvE and PvP players.

Thanks for considering this suggestion, and for all the great work you’re doing on Turtle WoW!

UP — Please consider adding a true PvP Weekly Quest!
I really think Turtle WoW needs a dedicated PvP weekly quest to make the world feel more alive and to give players from every bracket a concrete PvP goal.
It could take many forms, depending on the level range or the setting:
Bring back 50 Goblin Ears, Orc Teeth, or other faction “trophies”.
Conquer or defend a specific zone in the open world.
Kill a faction leader or commander in contested areas.
Complete objectives in battlegrounds for your bracket (10–19, 20–29, etc.).
This kind of system would make PvP more dynamic, rewarding, and tied to the game world — not just random BG farming.
Weekly PvP objectives could even rotate between zones or themes, to keep things fresh and encourage world activity beyond just raids and dungeons.
What do you guys think? Wouldn’t that add a real sense of purpose for PvP players each week?
