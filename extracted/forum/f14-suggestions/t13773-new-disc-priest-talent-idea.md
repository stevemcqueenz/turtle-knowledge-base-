# New disc priest talent idea

## Post 93695 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-28T17:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93695#p93695 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!
From PVE standpoint I feel like holy is an objectively better healer spec except for the very lategame where PI can help clearing raids on farm even faster.
The rotation is very similar and holy has better tools for that rotation. I'd advise a new end talent for disc next to PI which helps them create a different kind of healing build.
My idea is to lean disc more towards shields and give them a bigger edge in prevented damage.
The talent idea is:
Divine shield cast on a target with 30% or less HP costs 30% less and doesn't trigger the spell's cooldown.
It would give disc a new edge: in emergency raid scenarios they could spam shields to protect key targets very quickly.

Shielding low HP targets regardless of Weakened Soul debuff is actually a great idea, but there has to be some limitation or drawback to it, or it would be too powerful.

Here's an idea - create a different spell called "False Promise" (yes, inspiration from Dota Oracle). This spell will shield the target, however if it is dispelled or is broken by damage, it will inflict its shielding as shadow damage on the target. This will be great for emergency situations, because it will shield for more, but overall, normal PW:shield will be better, because they would be cheaper and would reflect damage, and won't damage the target when they expire.
Casting False Promise will also inflict the "Exhausted Soul" debuff, preventing the target from being affected by both PW:Shield and False promise again for 1 minute. False Promise itself would also have 10 seconds cooldown to prevent it from being spammed.

I think it would fit the discipline theme and class fantasy to have such a double-edged spell. My only concern is that it could be used for griefing and other nefarious purposes, even though it shields as much as it damages, so in effect it's just staggered damage.
