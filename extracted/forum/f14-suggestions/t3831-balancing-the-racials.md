## Post 23120 by Paledot — 2022-08-22T11:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23120#p23120 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am sure this has been brought up many times here and there but I would like to share my list of suggestions. Feel free to correct me.
Personally I would change the racials as little as possible, so we can keep the vanilla flavour… But some minor tweaks could help for the balancing and possibly for better race distribution on the server.
Note: Night Elves, Tauren and Undead have absolutely no boost to dmg, so I considered that.

First off major issue with Priest racial:
Starshards – arcane dmg is worthless for the priest… I think it could be still perfectly OK to make it holy dmg when other "Elune" NE priest spells are also holy

Generally:
Elemental resistance racials – could grow slightly with level

Orc:
Blood Fury – also increases spell dmg (Orc casters are just desperate)

Tauren:
Endurance – also increases chance to hit with spells and attacks by 1 %

Troll:
Regeneration – boost it considerably (insert math here)

Undead:
Will of the Forsaken – larger CD
Cannibalize – gives slightly increased stats for 5 mins (well fed… :)

Night Elf:
Shadowmeld – usable in combat, larger CD
Quickness – also increases critical strike chance with spells and attacks by 1 %

Dwarf:
Add Axe Specialization

Human:
Perception – I know there has been calls to replace this, but I think Human racials are still very good even with this. Would at most instead add a minor (thematic) combat effect for the duration – preferably defensive, cause humans already have some better weapon skills (increases healing received by 5 %, reduce the duration to 8 sec?)

Thanks for the great work on the server so far. Much respect   turtle_in_love
