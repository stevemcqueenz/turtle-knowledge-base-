# Craftable leveling gear should have random stats

## Post 77540 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-15T15:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77540#p77540 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently most crafts have fixed stats.
As a result, AH is fulled with tons of same crafted items.
Right now there are 59 auctions of Rough Bronze Leggings because they are best option to level blacksmithing.
Players have to compete with eachother only by price. So prices are going down and sometimes price of crafted item is less than materials required to craft it.

Proposal is:
Stats of crafted items should have random part.

For example [Gemmed Copper Gauntlets](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3474). They can be +3 or +4 or +1+1 or +1+2 or +2+2. As a result, some items are good, some - are not.
[Rough Bronze Bracers](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=2867) is other example. Now they are always +3 stamina. Their static stamina part could be replaced with varied one like Gemmed Copper Gauntlets and keep fixed +2 defense
It will allow crafters to compete not only by price, but also by quality. Crafters could sell good ones for more coin and bad ones for less coin.
Good crafts should be equal or close to "viable" option for PvP for bracket. Bad should be worse than "viable" option.

For some reason recipe of Rough Bronze Leggings is cheaper than recipe of Rough Bronze Boots. Legs don't require grinding stones. It might be good idea to add grinding stones to recipe to equalize them a bit.
