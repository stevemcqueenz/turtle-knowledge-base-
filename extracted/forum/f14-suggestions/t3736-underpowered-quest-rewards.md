# Underpowered Quest Rewards

## Post 22493 by Versidue — 2022-08-15T23:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22493#p22493 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I believe the quest [Reclaiming the Tinkerspark Transponder](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40057) in Azshara has quest rewards that are very underpowered for the level of the quest, and should be buffed.

The quest is the end of a long chain and requires you to kill a 53 elite naga with 18k health, but the items rewarded, while blue, have stats about on par with a mid 30s dungeon. It would be really cool if it rewarded something that a 50s player could actually use XD

Thank you for reading and feel free to post agreements, disagreements, or other quest rewards you feel should be buffed
