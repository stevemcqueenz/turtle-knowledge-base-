 **The Dalaran Wine Bottles, Barrels, Breads, and Cheeses have been added to the store “One More Glass” through the Vendor Christi Stockton since WOTLK. These items are useful for roleplay purposes - with birthdays, parties, and weddings being some main attractions. They allow for more spur of the moment roleplays, and through players being able to place their own decorations which others can loot lessen the need for GM help in placing things/vendors for events.**

These items could be sold in regular faction city shops and inns - as they already exist in most major towns and cities.

<https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cheese_vendors>

This simple addition would help spur RP, and create more world events!

Thank you for your consideration!
