Hi, I'd like to propose a glyph that might very well have been proposed before, in that case I'm sorry. The challenge is inspired by the speedruns of youtuber Tactics:

 - No grouping
 - No AH, mail or trade. Only bought, crafted, looted or quested items
 - Death allowed, allowing death skipping

A variant could be hardcore with no death allowed, making the hardcore challenge significantly more challenging, as the challenge as it stands today is not so difficult with its pocket healers, optimized dungeon groups and traded potions.

I'd actually just like a glyph that's exactly the same as hardcode (<https://turtlecraft.gg/hardcore-mode>) EXCEPT:

 - Remove the one death condition
 - Disallow all trading (not just within 5 levels)
 - Allow one to train all professions, but only allow two active professions at a time.

Basically I want a group playing experience but a solo economic experience. Indeed, as the only way you can interact with other players in an economic sense here is to group with them, group play would be encouraged, particularly with players of your level (as you can only group with players five levels within your level).

I think this would be a really great way to experience WoW. Going out and getting whatever you need with others in real gameplay instead of just impersonally grinding gold and buying stuff on the Auction House.

Working with others to get those recipes to finally make that pot/enchant you want, instead of again, just buying it on the Auction House.

I think this a great mode for a "endless" server like Turtle WoW, because it make the end game huge. There's so much more stuff to do if you have to get it all yourself. And it's Turtle WoW, there's no rush!
