# Make "Dim Torch" reduce mob aggro range for beasts

## Post 24978 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-13T12:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24978#p24978 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

From the quest text in "A night's exploration" it says you can use torches to "safeguard yourself against wild animals". I think this would be a really interesting mechanic to implement. It could work only against beasts and would modify their aggro range as if you were 5 levels higher than you actually are.
Adding some bonus functionality for this item would be nice, since at the time you acquire it, 4 spirit is not worth sacrificing your offhand, even it it's for the entire party.
