# Don't reset players stats of completed quests when extending a festival

## Post 164646 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-08T02:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164646#p164646 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

This is a suggestion. Also, a petition.

Statistics is the core property of players stored in the server. It is the actual achievement system that players dedicate time and energy for. When they find their completion was reset after doing quests to be recorded by the server, they feel disrespected and at least frustrated.

The resets in the past seemed coincidental but don't make that happen again and again.
