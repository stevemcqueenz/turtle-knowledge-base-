# Problems with throwing weapons.

## Post 74605 by Lendvell — 2023-10-29T08:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74605#p74605 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all!
First of all, I would like to thank the  turtle wow team for starting to fulfill my long-time dream of a hunter with throwing weapons.Thanks!
 1) I fully support the idea in the post [viewtopic.php?t=10832](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10832), since throwing weapons lose to other long-range weapons(less damage, no projectile bonus), they should have bonuses. As an example, this can be achieved by using throwing weapons by a hunter with the wolf aspect turned on.
 2) We do not have specialized bags or quivers for throwing weapons. This means that players who want to use thrown weapons either lose their attack speed bonus or lose one bag.
 3) There is no smooth distribution of throwing weapons across levels. For example, the best white throwing weapon that vendor sells requires level 35. I think we need to add white throwing weapons of levels 45 and 55. Mb you might need to think about colored throwing weapons from professions.
 4) The hunter's multi-shot, unfortunately, works on throwing weapons only at rank 1. After that, it only requires
ranged weapons.
All sting-type shots work with throwing weapons, except for the serpent sting.
Was this intended in both, or is this a bug?

Thank you for your attention!
