## Post 126213 by Taralej90 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-04-09T21:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126213#p126213 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After finishing Sunken Temple and defeating Eranikus, players are able to loot a corrupted gem containing the essence of Eranikus. Going to Eranikus's brother, Itharius, with that gem allows you to start a quest chain that would redeem his brother's soul.

...except that this quest chain was never finished by Blizzard. It stops at Umbranse the Spiritspeaker, with him saying you need to get him a special device he lost and... that's it.

I don't know why Turtle-WoW has ignored this quest chain in case Itharius is part of the ES attunement. Is there any intention to finish the quest chain? Maybe the device the Spiritspeaker wanted was stolen by the furbolgs, and then the Spiritspeaker also needs some ectoplasm from the wraiths and banshees at the elven ruins? Maybe then the device will need to be attuned properly by using knowledge from a book found in Scholomance? These are just some ideas.

Turtle-WoW is free to finish writing this chain which Blizzard just completely abandoned and never bothered to finish, even though Itharius remains an active NPC throughout the expansions as an Ambassador to the Green Dragonflight.
