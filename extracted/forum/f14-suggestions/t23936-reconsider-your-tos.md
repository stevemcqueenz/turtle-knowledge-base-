# reconsider your ToS

## Post 167997 by Pocketsmeow — 2026-02-11T22:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167997#p167997 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

"Use of other characters to evade someone’s use of /ignore to end communication."

The turtle staff really need to adjust when they apply punishment for anyone breaking the above ToS, obviously the rule is there to protect people from harassment. However there needs to be a human element / individual review of each case before applying any mutes/bans, currently it is just being blindly enforced and innocent players are getting caught up in it and sadly some players are knowingly taking advantage of such situations.

People can place others on their ignore list for the most pettiest of things, it isn't unreasonable for someone to flick them a message on an alt to figure out if it was a mistake of they accidently wronged them so they can adjust their behaviour going forward. The rule should only be applicable to any case where the perpetrator is either excessively messaging or being very toxic.

Below I have listed some situations where my guildies/myself have been the victim to over moderation from staff enforcing the rule.

 1. I was in a ZG pug and I wanted to clarify with the raid leader who I was healing, I realised I was on ignore so I messaged them from my alt to ask why and I was quickly globally muted by a gm. I still have no idea why/who they are.

 2. Someone randomly sent me a very toxic whisper and instantly ignored me. I messaged them to ask why did I receive such an awful message assuming I might have accidently wronged them. I was instantly 24hr muted across all my accounts.

 3. I was selling a rare item cheaper than someone else through world chat, someone messaged me offering to buy it however when I tried to reply I was on their ignore. Shortly after I messaged them from an alt to let them know I was on their ignore now and to see if they still wanted the item? Well I coped a world mute, I still believe it was the other person trying to sell the same item that wanted me muted so they wouldn't have cheaper competition.

I'm sure there are plenty of other situations that can arise from people unfairly falling victim to how this rule is being applied and as i mentioned it really needs to be reworded or staff need to look at each report on its own merits before blindly moderating the public.
