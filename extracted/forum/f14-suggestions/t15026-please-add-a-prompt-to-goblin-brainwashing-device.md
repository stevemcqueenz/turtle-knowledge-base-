# Please add a prompt to Goblin Brainwashing Device

## Post 102167 by BRadE2002 — 2024-09-29T14:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102167#p102167 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a new player, I was under the impression that once I purchased a goblin brainwashing device, that resetting my talents could be done free of charge, as there is no cost listed on the prompt to do so, when using the device.  I am not talking about switching presets, I am strictly speaking of the "reset my talents" selection.  I did this several times in a row, thinking it was free, at a low level mind you, which is my main character.  I took a hit of about 10g doing so, which had taken me a really long time to acquire.  It never prompted me, or warned me once that it would cost gold, it just immediately snatched the required gold from my player, permanently, and I didn't realize it until after the damage was done.  I'm not arguing against this being a gold sink, It's necessary for sure, but please just add a confirmation, prompt, or at least a note next to it informing the player that it will cost X amount of gold.  This cost me several hours' worth of gameplay in gold that I couldn't afford.  Frustrating.  Thank you!
