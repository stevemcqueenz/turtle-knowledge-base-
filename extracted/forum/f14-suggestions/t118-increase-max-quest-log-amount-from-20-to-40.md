# Increase max. quest log amount from 20 to 40

## Post 675 by Pigglebee (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2018-10-09T21:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=675#p675 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Because of the lower xp rate, we're doing a lot of quests from multiple zones at the same time. This means your log fills up quickly. It's annoying to have to remove some quests from the log and not forget where you picked them up or which quest items belong to it.

Could the quest log be upped to 40 or something so we can pick up all the quests we like? This way we can pick every quest that shows up a ! and not bother with quest log management to much. If we're supposed to quest, this is a QoL thing I would really like.
Example: My quest log is basically full. I have the Darkshore quests from 17-20 which I lagged behind on due to 0.5x, so I went to Westfall and did most quests. Then a couple in redridge, but I cannot continue there because, oops, quest log full amd remaining quests in darkshore/westfall are still 17+. Let alone going to Loch Modan now.
