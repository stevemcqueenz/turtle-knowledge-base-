## Post 162720 by Zmarotrix — 2025-12-14T00:55:00Z

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/AIzEUfY.png)

This should really be updated to something along the lines of:

> You are about to ***donate*** [money] and ***earn*** [tokens] on [server].

Everyone knows what's behind the practically transparent veil, but legally this is very different language. As it stands, it kind of defeats the purpose of "donation points" and just opens up unnecessary liability.

Obligatory "I'm not a lawyer".
