Nah, these 2 to 12-hour games are the essence of old AV. This battleground was never supposed to be 20 minutes rapid game that is 100% PvP oriented - it was intended to be a warzone with PvE and PvP objectives.

Reinforcements literally break the design of this battleground. With rapid games you are not able to complete any of team PvE objectives like summoning lord elemental or cavalry. It makes current content in AV irrelevant.

You want to have reinforcements not because of the better gameplay but because of the poor honor gained in current version of AV in case of turtle-game. That's the issue. Addition of reinforcements maybe fixes the honor gained but at the cost of worse gameplay for everyone. This isn't good solution to the problem.

The best solution, as Zulnam has pointed out, would be to reduce the penalty of "diminishing returns" mechanic that stops you from getting honor when you kill the same person again and again in AV (which is inevitable in longer games). In theory it should fix the problem of getting low amount of honor in stagnation games.

I suggested it some time ago [viewtopic.php?t=13520](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13520).
