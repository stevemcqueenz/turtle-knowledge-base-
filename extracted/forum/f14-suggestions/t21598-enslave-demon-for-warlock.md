Enslave Demon is almost a completely non-functional spell due to an erroneous bug report from years ago. It is not gamebreaking to be able to enslave demons in parts of the world where there are very few people, and where their only use is to kill other mobs which can already be solo killed by other more labourious means, or by regular mechanics outright - see Tainted Scar demons and Winterspring demons, respectively.

Please revert this change! It will have no real effect on the balance of the game beyond making a weak, fun-oriented spell for Warlock actually usable!
