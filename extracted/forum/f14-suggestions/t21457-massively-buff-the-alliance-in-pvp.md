# Massively Buff The Alliance in PVP.

## Post 148741 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-08-27T19:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148741#p148741 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm a dedicated Horde PVP Player (Champ of Blood Ring/Maxxed BG Reputations)
I'm rather bored of utterly dominating / spawn camping Alliance teams (despite my blue gear).

One problem is that as soon as a team BEGINS to lose -- its a snowball effect --
their spirit is swiftly broken resulting in less/zero effort --
so their loss is now inevitable

It's boring (and dishonorable) to kick a cripple especially while they are down.
As a proud Shaman of The Horde I DEMAND a proper challenge.

What I specifically suggest is that there be some sort of "Catch Up" mechanic added to certain PVP battlegrounds.
At least AB and WSG.

I would call it "Underdogs Blessing":
When vastly losing/getting steamrolled/have less players:
Boosts Your Teams Speed/Damage/Stamina by 20%.

Blizz had this sort of concept implemented in WoTLK (Wintergrasp).

Alternatively/additionally:
there could be a 'Team Autobalance Scramble' wherein players are added/swapped depending on game conditions.
