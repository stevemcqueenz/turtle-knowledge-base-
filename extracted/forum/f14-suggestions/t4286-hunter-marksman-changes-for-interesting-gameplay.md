Traps could be used only out of combat and only One trap at time.  wary_turtle_head  . So u can use only one trap each 30sec (CD of Feign Death). Also, because of that, usually notice this pvp(BG) [build](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter/0-V56IV-3CH56IJU))  Using talents only for main abiliies
I think that its just odd to use Feign Death for using trap but not for it main purpose.
 **Suggestion for traps. Several variants.**
 1) Just 30sec CD without out of combat
 2) Or 30 sec CD for each trap starts only out of combat
 3) Or increasing dmg, radius, time x1.5-x2 times
 4) Or buff traps talents x1.5 times
• And also suggestion for dmg traps - depending from RAP. For avereage equipment dmg not changes.
We will have a little bit more relevant traps and trap tallents.

• Wierd talent tree
by the main idea marksman - is a range dps. survival - awesome control. but....
For marksman [Lighting Reflexes](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=24297/lightning-reflexes) and [Killer instincts](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=19373/killer-instinct) are better than [Ranged spec](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=19511/ranged-weapon-specialization) and [Trueshot Aura](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=20906/trueshot-aura) and [Barrage](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=24691/barrage). Even hit chance in survival tree)). A simple mathematic . +15% agility and crit chance gain more than just +dmg. Agility - is a crit chance, dmg, dodge chance. Its awesome talent and bonus scales with gear. Also marksman always need to find weapon with less speed for better results of Aimed Shot. Till wotlk 0/21/30 was better build for dmg
 **Suggestion for talent tree**
• New talent in end of tree **Patience** *"Hunter aimes more carefully. That decrease autoshot speed without changing DPS. For weapons with speed less than 2.00 Value 10/20/30% for weapons with speed between 2.00-3.00 Value 7/14/20% for weapons with speed more than 3.00 Vaue 5/10/15%"*
• [Ranged spec](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=19511/ranged-weapon-specialization)  3/6/9%
• [Trueshot Aura](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=20906/trueshot-aura) for three ranks 5/10/15% to RAP
Instead of RAP, dodge chance and crit chance by agility, instead of crit chance by killer insting - marksman will have just dmg. More dmg(PVE) instead of dmg + survival(PVP)

• PVE. boring rotation and manaproblem
The main stat is agility. As result small amount mana, but no ways to regen it in pve.   wary_turtle_head  . When u fight a boss your rotation is - Aimed shot - serpent string - multi-shot - maybe several new trueshot(here) and waiting cd, then - repeat till u have mana, and then you just observe an autoshot. Or Feign death - and drink some water. Boring.
 **Suggestion for manaproblem in PVE. Few variants**
 1) Adding for Trueshot a chance to restore mana
 2) Or Ading for talent [Ranged spec](https://classic.wowhead.com/spell=19511/ranged-weapon-specialization) for example effect, that all ranged attacks has a chance to restore mana
 3) Or as in tbc - [aspect of the Viper](https://tbc.wowhead.com/spell=34074/aspect-of-the-viper). But not just each 5 boring second, hunter should shoot for mana. Should be in action.

• Arcane Shot (AS) - useless
It depends on spell power . just facepalm. Sure blizzard fixed that mistake after. But in vanilla AS is useless. It has same CD that Aimed shot , spend 190 mana and deal just 183 dmg. Its less in proportion mana/dmg than other spells and its less than autoshot with good equipment.
 **Only one suggestion**
Should depends on RAP.
For average equipment it nothing changes. But for good equipment it will more usefull. To be honest - the other spells. All has a same problem. Just fixed DMG on depending on SP. But hunter is not a spellcaster (( On my opinion - all spells should depends from RAP. For average equipment - Nothing changes, **it relevant only for good equipment**.
