# New Challenge Mode: Set Completionist

## Post 133716 by PowerPaladin — 2025-05-31T00:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133716#p133716 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A new challenge mode where the player must fully complete each set (D1/2, T1, T2, etc.) before they can loot/wear any drops from the next raid in the progression line. Possibly even force the player to complete content wearing the full set before being allowed to move on.

Example: Grabnarr is a Warrior.

Grabnarr wants to do Molten Core, but Grabnarr has a problem...

Grabnarr accepted the Set Completionist [Replace with Clever Name Later] Challenge, and doesn't have his full Valor set. Instead, he went for pre-MC BiS pieces like a silly goose. A silly little warrior goose. Grabnarr enters Molten Core with his best friends and they down Lucifron, and, oh! What serendipity! The Gauntlets of Might just dropped, and they want to give them to Grabnarr!

But Grabnarr can't see them. The loot master can't give them to Grabnarr. Downer, but that's okay, the Flamewaker Legplates also dropped so they'll just...oh?

They can't give him those either?

See, Grabnarr can't get *anything* from MC until he *is actively wearing* his full D1 set during the raid.

He tries BWL because he's attuned to that, too. Uh oh, SpaghettiOs! He *can't get gear there, either,* because he's not wearing his full T1 set.

Meanwhile, Tharnog the Warrior (Tharnog is a smart cookie), who accepted the same challenge and has his full D1, T1, *and even T2* set, has walked into the same MC that Grabnarr has, and can see all of the gear with no problems whatsoever! Rumor has it that Tharnog may even have his own special loot pool with drops only he can see...but that's just speculation.

 -----

As a player who likes completing things in order and doesn't mind the extra wait before moving on to the next gear tier or piece of content, I think this would provide a extra challenge and incentive to folks who like to take it slow or enforce other types of handicaps while tackling content that doesn't include perma-death.

The only type of "prize" or "bonus" I've concocted for players who accept this challenge is access to their own individual loot pools per raid boss (they're still at the whims of the drop rates, and aren't guaranteed to get what they want, but they are guaranteed an item from each boss). I'm not sure that including the same perk for dungeons would work unless those characters also have heavier dungeon lockout restrictions placed on them, but I can also see that drawing in a crowd of folks.

Having that perk would definitely appeal to a number of folks despite the time it would add to gearing for any raid.

There isn't an "end point" where you've "completed" the challenge, so-to-speak. It's more of a persistent state of difficulty, so I don't see a mount or other one-off item being worth it.

Feel free to suggest any other types of bonuses or perks below.
