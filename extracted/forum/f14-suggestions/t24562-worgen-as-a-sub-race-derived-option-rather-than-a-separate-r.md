# Worgen as a sub-race/derived option, rather than a separate race

## Post 172839 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-28T05:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172839#p172839 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Worgen is too Cataclysm-ish for a Vanilla game. In WCII-III, worgen does not exist and in WoW, human-converted worgens are only a minor group of people in Lordaeron, more like Hakkari trolls rather than a new race. To me, their existence just makes the universe of WoW more complicated. Even if Turtle WoW planned to add playable worgen, that had better be made into a quest that grants players (human only, not for elves) the ability to change physical form without influencing their combat stats, which is like the green fire quest of warlock in Mist of Pandaria expansion. Players should also be given the cure to reverse/dissipate the worgen curse.

This would simply implement a race of hot discussion and popularity while having least weakening or minimised damage to the loyalty to early lore.

Allow me to make a correction.

Yeah, Worgen as introduced in Cataclysm are too Cataclysm-ish for Vanilla. Worgen in general? Of course not. They appear in relatively low-level zones and, overall, fit into the vanilla world. Moreover, they are an independent race summoned to Azeroth by the Scythe of Elune and Arugal - almost like orcs coming from another world.

And, of course, humans transformed into worgen are only a small portion of the population, created mainly through Arugal’s efforts, and cannot be considered a proper representation of this race (I recall that the information about transmitting the curse through a bite is a retcon introduced in WotLK).
