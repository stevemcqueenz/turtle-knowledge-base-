"Sworn enemy", huh?  Don't those already exist in-game?  I'm pretty sure every playable race has some faction that they are "sworn to war" against.  Dwarves against Dark Irons, Tauren against (most) Grimtotem, Forsaken against the Lich King, et cetera.  However, I can't think of any playable race that would declare open war on another in the current timeline.

As Elesion, "PvP" in *World of Warcraft* has always been kind of a game-only gimmick.  Sure, you have isolated cases of encroachment…  Dwarves mining Mulgore, Forsaken experimenting in Arathi…  But for the most part, there's no real reason for any one entire playable race to hate any other playable race and stay in a state of open warfare.

Now, here's a more realistic idea: I wonder if it would be possible for *individual players* to go to war with each other?  Both players would have to agree to it through a dialogue box, but if they do, then they are always in a state of Player-vs-All with each other **and only each other**.  This could lead to some interesting scenarios for roleplayers and PvP-hungry people, alike.  And of course, you can only be "at war" with only one player at a time.

Just a though.
