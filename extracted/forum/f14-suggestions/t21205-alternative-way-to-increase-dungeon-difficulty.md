# Alternative way to increase dungeon difficulty

## Post 147225 by Partyboyza — 2025-08-20T10:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147225#p147225 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Instead of increasing the dungeon mobs and bosses stats, I would advise you increase the number of mobs in packs and boss adds.

This would make dungeoning more tactical because it will require more crowd control and coordination from the party.

A more rewarding system where more mobs equals more loot and more reputation gain.

Add a new rule where engaging in combat the last boss of the dungeon will agro the entire dungeon to stop parties from rushing the last boss. Obviously with multiple wing dungeons the last boss will agro only the wing they are located in.

It would feel normal for the last boss to call upon his henchmen to protect him. Rather than to just kill him in front of his henchemen while they watch and do nothing.

## Post 147284 by Allarielle — 2025-08-20T14:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147284#p147284 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think increasing pack size would only encourage people to bring more AoE, rather than more CC. For a more tactical approach it makes more sense to increase mob health and damage, as that allows CC to remain relevant. In my mind, a dungeon party of 5 players should always have 1 CC-capable character and 1 other who has a situational or limited CC like Entangling Roots or Fear. Thus, the ideal pack size should basically always be 3-4 mobs - 1 focus target, 1 cc target, and 1 or 2 more targets to be managed while the priority targets are taken care of. Deviation from that should be designed intentionally with an eye to how group tactics will change.

If a pack has only 2 mobs, then they both have to hit so hard that not CCing one of them isn't an option, and in that case the encounter just becomes a chore to heal. If a pack has 5 mobs, each one needs to be so weak that CC starts to feel optional altogether unless one of them has a wipe ability, and non-Paladin tanks start having trouble holding agro on all of them. At 6 mobs, I think most groups will default to trying to AoE them down, and even if you try to CC, the impact of the CC on the pack's over all damage is marginal.
