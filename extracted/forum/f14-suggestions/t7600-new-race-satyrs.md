 *Satyrs are a vile, insidious and evil race, the opposite of the night elves. They like sneaky attacks, dark magic, corruption, and they get a fun from it all. They can go enrage and become very strong. We know them as goat-mans, but have you ever thought about their society? Would you like to play for them? Only they would wear armor and not go naked.*

A little explanation.
 **[Baseness]** allows you to cast a long spell/shot on a target without the target seeing what you're doing unless the target click on you manually. So your first strike can be a surprise for the enemy.
Also, this ability increases the crit chance of this hit. This all works as long as you are not in combat. That is, for one first blow.

 **[Corruption]** ENEMY curse could become your buff or heal-dot. Depends the effect. It Turns on negative to positive with 1%.

 **[Enrage]** is a strong short buff but requires to be low health and it's it leaves a strong long debuff. I've seen this ability in the Satyrs in Desolace.

 **[Blood Moon]** Cuz Enrage only for melee this is for priests and warlocks. 1% that your heal spell give your target Short Enrage (3 sec) and short Pain debuff for you. Works on every tick of hot. Do not proc if you heal yourself.

 **[Betrayal]** secondary active ability what share cooldown with Enrage.

So Enrage for melee, Blood Moon for casters, what gain hunters? Actually invisibility of Betrayel good for hunters too or finish off an enemy in close combat under Enrage.

How to activate Enrage in PvE? If you get already some dmg then use Demonic Rune or Dark Rune. But only in the last seconds of fight, cuz debuff after 6 second is strong and long what will set all bonuses to zero

Satyrs are a race of bestial demons usually sworn to the Burning Legion. They are created through the spreading of the ancient satyr curse, an affliction which originated with Xavius, a Highborne sorcerer who was destroyed and remade into a demonic form as a punishment for his failures by Sargeras during the War of the Ancients. In modern times, satyrs mostly live in various sects throughout Kalimdor and exist only to harry their hated night elf cousins and obey the will of the Legion, though others have instead sworn fealty to the Old Gods and work to spread the influence of the Emerald Nightmare.

> **Galendor wrote: Sat Jun 03, 2023 12:44 pm**
> how does Baseness work on mobs?

Baseness has two related aspects. The first concerns only pvp.
In pvE, all races already have *Baseness* because the enemy mob starts attacking when you damage or debuff them, not when you just start casting a spell but not finished it yet. Without Baseness in PvP, the enemy player will target you automatically as soon as you start casting something against them. Baseness allows you to use the first attack more meanly, the enemy may not notice it until it deals damage to him.
For example as a hunter you can start casting AIM shot in bushes, but the enemy will not see this until he is shot. That is, he can see you in the bushes and what you are aiming for, target you manually and then he will understand, but you will not automatically show up as his target as soon as you start using something and it has not yet harmed him.
The second aspect is the same for both pvp and pve - the first attack, before entering combat, has +25% to crit.
The one that is not seen.

They have two active skills, but with a shared cooldown for both.
Enrage is useless for casters and they will use an alternative.
