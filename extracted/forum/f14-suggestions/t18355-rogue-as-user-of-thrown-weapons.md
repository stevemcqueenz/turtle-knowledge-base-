# Rogue as user of thrown weapons

## Post 125291 by clearmind — 2025-04-03T17:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125291#p125291 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Backstory:
I really like the design of WC3 forest troll units. So i tried to lvl as one using thrown weapon only, and of course i failed. ![Image](https://i.ibb.co/cS9RrbnZ/199772-4051b3f2ee87f9c0dc532495059fbf27.png)
There are 3 main problems:
 1. Game mechanic does allow you to thrown only at 8-30 yard range and with 2 sec cool down which is huge.
 2. You don't have anything to control enemies. It could be fixed by given some skill to immobilize enemy like this one: ![Image](https://i.ibb.co/4ZWc2yHX/82232-net.jpg)
Besides, Net skill already in game and been used by some mobs.
 3. Damage input. It's so weak, you won't be able to appropriately damage mobs not to mention killing, especially considering the poor thrown arsenal represented in game.

Conclusion:
My preposition is to give variety of tweaks to make rogue throwers a decent range DPS unit. It could be done by changing a combat spec tree, but maybe with some other ways. I want to know opinion on these from players and Turtle stuff if possible.

I agree with Mayson. All rogue trees are focused on a melee gameplay.
Rogues have a small toolkit for range, yes, but it's not designed for an independent gameplay.
Rogues use thrown weapons, deadly throw etc to help their melee gameplay. Let's say the enemy is too far away to kick or you need a last bit of damage to finish off someone.
