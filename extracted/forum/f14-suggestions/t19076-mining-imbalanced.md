# Mining imbalanced

## Post 129678 by starfall25 — 2025-05-05T01:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129678#p129678 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, the problem right now is that you added jewelcrafting to the base vanilla game and made it use mining as its paired profession. This means that even more people are going for mining than before. This makes it much harder to level mining and the base professions that used mining originally because it needs to support another profession.

The mining nodes were not compensated for the extra profession requiring it so now everybody is fighting over mining nodes like crazy. This makes mining more frustrating and makes people more hostile towards each other because "you stole my mining node" stuff. Nobody cares if you are running traveling artisan or if you are hardcore character, they just go for your node even if you reached it first/mined it first because they too are in a bad position with this.

The amount of low level players constantly around isnt helping much either. The mining nodes disappear as fast as they appear and their respawn time is just horrible.
So in order to happily get anywhere with your mining, you have to camp less popular nodes and head for them as soon as they respawn which means that you are not getting anything else done in the meantime.

This really needs a fix in either adding another profession which would support jewelcrafting with its own nodes or the respawn time for mining nodes needs to be greatly reduced along with adding new nodes. Otherwise, you wont really be having very friendly people on your server like vanilla used to have and that just kills a part of what original vanilla had in its allure.
