# PvP should be more profitable than lasher farm

## Post 84693 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-14T16:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84693#p84693 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently a lot of people spend half of their time farming lashers in DME to get gold to spend it on consumables.
While high price of consumables is another topic to discuss, I want to point out that AoE farming become most time consuming ingame activity.
WoW is not abbreviated as World of AoE farms.
From all possible activities, people choose the one which require least amount of interaction with/against other players.

Proposal is:
Increase gold reward for turning marks of honor of any kind
Mark turnin should be more profitable than AoE goldfarms.
PvP should be promoted more in this game.

I didn't count how much gold it is per hour but my estimation is that it's slightly worse than lasher farm. Of course this farm is efficient if nobody from enemy faction disturbs you. There are also other problems like AV match being too short or someone from your team captures the mine you are sitting in. In the mines you collect Armor Scraps that can be turned in to NPC in your base. Every 500 Armor Scraps your team turns in, all units of your faction will get upgrade from Normal->Seasoned->Veteran->Champion units. If you upgrade the troops fast enough, it will take much more time for enemy team to make progress into your base giving your team more chance to win the match. In current AV meta, faster team wins. However, in extreme situation enemy team won't be able to capture anything due to your units being too strong + your team mates who defend the graveyards for example. This can cause "turtle AV" which is good for your farm because AV match will likely last longer but honor gain will be limited. It's worth noting that mobs in the mines hit very hard, you should have good gear to farm them (I do it with my T2 Shadow Priest) and not every class can do it, this farm isn't for melee I think. You should also have many bag slots free, your bag will fill with trash very fast. Using summonable vendor from donation shop is very good idea and saves your time.
