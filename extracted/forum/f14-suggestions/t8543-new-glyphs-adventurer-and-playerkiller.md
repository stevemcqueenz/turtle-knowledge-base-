# New Glyphs: Adventurer and Playerkiller

## Post 54984 by Bloodline1x9 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-28T19:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54984#p54984 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**1. Adventurer**
 *You can only gain experience in dungeons, but it increases by 50%. You can also get experience for completing quests. But you don't get experience from killing creatures in the open world.*

 2. Playerkiller
 *You start getting experience from killing players. But you no longer get it from killing monsters.*

The first one should help those who likes leveling in dungeons full time. The second one is for those who wanna leveling just in the BG. Only one condition is that the BGs must be 5 levels apart, not 10.
