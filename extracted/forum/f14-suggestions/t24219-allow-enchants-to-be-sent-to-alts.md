# Allow enchants to be sent to alts

## Post 170333 by Nuninho32 — 2026-03-08T12:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170333#p170333 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi everyone,
I’d like to suggest a small quality-of-life improvement for players who have multiple characters.
Currently, if your main character is an enchanter but your alt has a Bind on Pickup item that needs enchanting, you can’t enchant it unless that character is also the enchanter. This often forces players to either level enchanting on multiple characters or go without an enchant.
A possible solution would be allowing enchanters to create a tradable enchant scroll (or similar item) that can be mailed to their own alt characters. The alt could then apply that enchant to their Bind on Pickup gear. This would keep the enchant tied to the item but still let players benefit from the profession they already leveled on their main.
This could work similarly to the later scroll system, but limited to self-use or account-wide mailing if needed to prevent abuse.
I think it would be a nice quality-of-life feature for players who enjoy maintaining multiple characters.

What do you all think?
