# 60% mount speed option

## Post 102198 by Beest — 2024-09-30T02:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102198#p102198 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like the **option** to retain the **60% mount speed**, now every mount automatically scales with the riding level. At max riding(150) you cannot go back to 75, but I would like to have that option, which could be handled in these ways:

 - Glyph for Apprentice Riding and Journeyman Riding skill.
 - ingame command to change skill level on the fly (perfered since it's niche).
 - Turtle being capped at 60%? or another obtainable 60% speed mount.
 - Ingame NPC that can change riding skill (relog maybe needed?).

Why?: To jump on specific stuff, which the 100% speed will overshoot or ride allong with a friend who does not yet have the 150 riding skill.
