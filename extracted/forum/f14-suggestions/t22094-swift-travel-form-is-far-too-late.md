# Swift Travel Form is far too late

## Post 153241 by Llamalicious5 — 2025-09-26T01:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153241#p153241 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm new to turtle wow and started on Ambershire. I picked druid because i heard there was a new Swift Travel Form, which sounds really cool! I've been so excited to get it and I imagined itd be similarly gained as warlocks and paladins special mounts. Though sadly that isnt the case. I've seen both palas and wlocks with their cool epic mounts already, yet i have to wait for my new cool form till phase 5?? Them getting theirs in p0, while i have to wait for nearly ½ a year for p5 to get mine, just because you decided to put a p5 raid as requirement, is really quite obnoxious. Who knows if im even playing by then! This could have been done so much better!

Also, the quests for wlock and paladin, can be done naturally, even as a solo player. You end up in dungeons, that people are doing anyway. Usually people dont mind spending another 5-10 min helping someone out doing their quest inside of the dungeon. They're already there!

Yet this swift travel form requires you to go around the world, slaying strong elite dragons which will require 4-5 people. You have to find people who are willing to go quite a bit out of their way, do a whole lot of travel, just for you to kill an elite. That is really bad design, especially when compared to just doing an extra room in Scholo or DM for lock/pala. its truly going to suck getting people to do this with you.

All in all, this new form is nothing compared to what it couldve been. The quests are far more obnoxious to do. Take a lot more travel and is time consuming, making it much harder to find people to help you do them. And more importantly, dont make it require a p5 raid. Let me get it now. Just like palas. Just like wlocks. Why force me to wait? There is NO reason, a raid should be required for this.
