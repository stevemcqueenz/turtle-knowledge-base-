# Shaman Reworked

## Post 76468 by Sjaakie567 — 2023-11-08T12:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76468#p76468 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all,
Lately in my mind I have this idea about the shaman in Turtle WoW and more in line with what a shaman actually is like in warcraft 3. Well more like a focus on the Far seer and Shaman:
Far seer / shaman / witch doctor

First point and major change would be to give the Shamans an auto-shoot. You’re all laughing but hear me out. This is to completely overhaul the class to a caster class, and to create the first (sort of) ranged tank.

Auto-shoot/lightning ball
Shoots a lightning ball to the target based on weapon damage, speed and spell power/nature damage. 20y range. (like the basic attack of Far Seers/Shamans in WC3). Could be set as normal damage, but maybe as nature damage it is better.
I think this would be a great feature and iconic for the class, and will also help elemental shamans by doing damage from range when their mana is low or the mob is almost dead (like the wand for other casters). For enhance it would be incentive to stay a bit more back, outside of the normal melee classes, and the class should keep the normal auto attack (in a same way hunters get auto-shoot and auto-attack)

Weapon buffs
•	Weapon buffs will effect this auto-shoot same as the normal auto attack.
o	Rockbiter gives threath (maybe to everything) and attack/spellpower
o	windfury can cause it to hit 3 times
o	flametongue changes it to a flame ball with additional damage (or give spellpower)
o	frost also the same way (ranged slow proc can be OP which must be looked into for pvp).

Elemental shields
•	I would like to make some general changes as well as procs coming from the auto-shoot and auto-attack
o	Lightning shield: Damage when hit (enhance talent to remove charges but lowers damage like thorns or ret aura, or as an actual aura like immolation potion) , proc: target gets electrified causing X damage around target (4yards). – Damage / threath
o	Earth Rock shield:  give armor instead and/or avoidance, proc: gives health back (based on SP to make it more viable) - mitigation
o	Water shield: 30% spell interruption, proc: gives mana back (to make it useful for resto and ele, and enhance when they really need mana) - sustain

Additional spell:
•	Earthquake: AoE to target somewhere, channeled. And 10% attackspeed reduce to targets (like thunderclap).
o	In enhancement there will be a talent to remove the channeling but reducing damage and additional threat (or maybe only additional threath when Rockbiter is active).
•	A Taunt (20 yards): “Forceful wind” a strong current of wind force the target to focus the caster
o	Tanks need a taunt, and this feels in character
o	Or an enhance talent that changes earth shock to a taunt and reducing damage and not triggering the shock cooldown.

Final note:
A couple of things I have said feel like blizzarification (i.e. making every class basically the same as other classes) as I am doing multiple comparisons to other classes. But I truly hold on to the mid ranged idea (20 yards) for the Shaman with this kind of auto-shoot. And of course when tanking mobs are gonna run to you anyway so you’re not really a ranged tank, but I would like to see the Shaman more as a Static tank rather than chasing after mobs.
This will highly likely cause some problems on different fronts, and I am very interested in other peoples opinions.
