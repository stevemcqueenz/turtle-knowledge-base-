Players are tasked with retrieving a barrel of onions from the hut the Elite ogre guards with the option of grouping up and taking him out or sneaking by him with stealth or invisibility potions (gotta make the combat non-mandatory for ogre lovers), it could play out like a higher level version of the Tundra Macgrann’s Stolen Stash quest from Dun Morogh.

Alternatively for something less straightforward, there could be misdirection in making players expect a standard kill/collect quest only to have the Ogre reveal desperate motives and ransom the onions to you in exchange for handing out “eviction notices” (carved rocks) to the swamp inhabitants who compete with his food foraging, including a seemingly ordinary frog the ogre refers to as his “father-in-law”.
