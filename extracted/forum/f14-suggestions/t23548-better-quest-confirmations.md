For context...

These quests used to reward players with 1 Shellcoin. On Nordanaar price of the Shellcoin back then was above 20g so you could spend maybe 15 gold on the quest chain and receive item worth even more. Because of that, the quest was always worth doing especially for low level characters - the quest was obtainable at level 30.

However, one forum user (I don't remember his name) found out that the Shellcoin awarded through quest line actually corrupted whole Shellcoin system making it pyramid scheme.
Devs had no choice so they changed the final reward of the quest because if they didn't change it, Shellcoin would potentially be worth 0g in the future.
Forum post about this topic is here: [viewtopic.php?t=14805](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=14805)

Now the reward is worth nothing but means of completing the quest haven't changed. Players still need to pay gold for nothing. And no, the quest doesn't inform the player that the gold will vanish from their wallet. There are some quests in Vanilla where special message shows up for example "This will cost you 5g". This quest, however, simply removes gold from your inventory without any notification.

Imagine being level 30 adventurer who plays Turtle WoW for the first time. A few gold coins on level 30 is very much. In my opinion, the quest should be changed either to inform the player that this costs gold or by removing gold requirement - it's not needed anymore since the reward isn't worth much.
