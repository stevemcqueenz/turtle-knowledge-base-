# New Dungeon: Obsidian Sanctum

## Post 136858 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-24T17:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136858#p136858 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Obsidian Sanctum**
 *A 5-player dungeon for level 60s, located in northern Silithus*

 **Dungeon Description**
Beneath the windswept cliffs of northern Silithus, the **Twilight’s Hammer** has uncovered a forbidden temple—the **Obsidian Sanctum**. Once a titan-sealed prison for a lesser **Faceless One**, it now pulses with void corruption. Twisting tunnels of black stone, warped runes, and sacrificial chambers echo with chants to the Old Gods. Players must descend into this ancient sanctum to stop the cult’s final ritual before Silithus is lost to the void.

 **Bosses**

 1. Harbinger Q'zehl – *Twilight Silithid Herald*
 **Location:** Hatchery Caverns
 **Mechanics:**
- Spawns waves of *Void-Touched Silithid*
- Burrows underground, erupting beneath ranged players
- Leaves behind **Void Pools** that silence on contact
 **Loot Highlights:**
- *Broodmind Carapace* – Mail chest with +Shadow Resist and +Hit
- *Silithid Shell Band* – Ring that reduces silence duration
 2. Duskwrought Binder Aela – *Twilight Ritualist*
 **Location:** Chamber of Chains
 **Mechanics:**
- Channels **Shadow Chains** to immobilize one player
- Summons *Twilight Shades* that must be AoE'd down
- Casts **Void Surge**, an interruptible AoE
 **Loot Highlights:**
- *Cowl of Fanatic Whispers* – Cloth helm with Shadow Resist
- *Chains of Self-Denial* – Trinket: On-use, reduce threat and apply a shadow-absorb shield
Mini-Boss: Obsidian Construct – *Unstable Earth-Void Hybrid*
 **Optional Guardian of the Inner Sanctum**
 **Mechanics:**
- Detonates **Fractured Pillars** to cause cave-ins
- Applies stacking *Void Crush* debuff on tank
 **Loot Highlights:**
- *Obsidian Shard-Inscribed Gauntlets* – Plate with Stamina, Shadow Resist, and +Parry
- *Gem of Crumbling Earth* – Usable item: Knockdown nearby enemies (2 min CD)
 3. Inquisitor Xir’thak – *Void-Touched Twilight Judge*
 **Location:** Sacrificial Gallery
 **Mechanics:**
- **Mark of Condemnation**: Curses a random player, causing splash damage
- Teleports behind lowest-health target and casts *Judgment of the Void*
- Summons *Void Graspers* that root players
 **Loot Highlights:**
- *Judgment Dagger of Xir'thak* – Fast dagger, chance on hit to reduce enemy spell power
- *Twilight Tribunal Robes* – Robe with Intellect, Spirit, and +Shadow Spell Power
 **4. High Ascendant Xalzax** – *Final Boss*
 **Location:** The Ritual Core
 **Mechanics:**
- Channels energy into a **Bound Abyssal Harbinger** (adds over time)
- **Void Pillars** collapse randomly, requiring repositioning
- Casts **Twilight Unraveling**: high-damage AoE unless interrupted
- Final phase: Summons **Echo of the Faceless One** – spectral add that copies player spells
 **Loot Highlights:**
- *Twilight Conduit Staff* – 2H Staff with Shadow Resist, Intellect, chance to proc void blast
- *Void-Touched Reliquary* – Trinket: Chance on hit to reduce enemy spell power by 20 for 10 sec
- *High Ascendant’s Mantle* – Epic: Shoulder, +Shadow Power, +10 Shadow Resistance
 **Quest Hooks**

 **1. “Chains Beneath the Sands”** – From a Cenarion Emissary in Cenarion Hold
> Investigate Twilight activity in the cliffs north of Silithus.
 **Reward:** +Cenarion Circle rep, *Sandblessed Band*

 **2. “Tools of the Mad”** – Drops from cultists inside the dungeon
> Collect 5 *Inscribed Obsidian Tablets* from the Sanctum to learn of Xalzax’s plan.
 **Reward:** +Reputation, choice of consumable cache

 **3. “The Sleeper Below”** – Chain quest from a Silithid corpse
> Discover the Faceless One’s prison. Leads to a raid quest involving AQ and N’zoth lore.

 **Location**
Enter from the northern cliffs of Silithus, just east of the Twilight Base Camp. Stone obelisks mark the entrance. Dungeon has three wings:
- Tunnels of Despair (start → Q’zehl)
- Chambers of Ritual (mid-tier)
- The Core Descent (Xalzax finale)

## Post 136860 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-24T17:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136860#p136860 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ever felt like the Twilight’s Hammer stuff in Silithus just... ends? You find robes, read creepy texts, get a few cryptic quests from the Cenarion Circle—and then that’s it. The cult is clearly up to something, but there’s no payoff. **Obsidian Sanctum** is designed to change that.

 **This dungeon gives you a reason to care about all that cultist activity in the zone.** You’ll descend into an ancient obsidian temple newly unearthed by the cult, where they’re trying to resurrect a slumbering Faceless One. Expect to fight through corrupted silithid, fanatical warlocks, and twisted elemental hybrids before facing off against *High Ascendant Xalzax*, a prophet going full Void-mode with collapsing pillars and a giant abyssal monster.

 **Why it would be fun:**
- Classic-style linear layout with a mix of tight tunnels and big set-piece boss rooms
- Cultist lore and mechanics that reward interrupts, positioning, and awareness
- Unique loot like Shadow Resistance gear and a trinket that reduces enemy spellpower
- Additional avenues for Cenarion Circle reputation gains
- Ties directly into the Twilight’s Hammer questlines already in Silithus
It’s the perfect way to close the loop on all that Silithus mystery, give players a high-stakes fight with real lore consequences, and bring some Void flavor to the end of Classic.
