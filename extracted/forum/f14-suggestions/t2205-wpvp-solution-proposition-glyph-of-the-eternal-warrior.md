# [WPVP Solution - Proposition] Glyph of the Eternal Warrior

## Post 13651 by Alrori — 2021-11-08T18:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13651#p13651 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey Turtles, I hope you're doing good,

I'm here to talk about a WPvP Solution I thought of - The Glyph of the Eternal Warrior.

Said Glyph can be picked up in both Orgrimmar and Stormwind, as a quest reward - the same way Hardcore mode works.  After the quest has been given back, you gain the Glyph, which makes you an Eternal Warrior, a **permanently** flagged character, vulnerable at all time by any character of the opposing faction.

The Eternal Warrior may attack any other Eternal Warrior of the opposing faction, but may not attack any regular, non-PvP-flagged player. However, any regular player may attack an Eternal Warrior of the opposing faction anytime he wants.

Hence, an Eternal Warrior must remain careful, and patient. He needs to be prepared to be attacked at all time, even when surrounded by seeminglessly pacific chacaracters. The upcoming of Eternal Warriors could bring the sense of liveliness the WPvP adherents talk about, with the inability to withdraw yourself from the danger other characters represent.

This solution is meant to give the possibility of fluid conflict and alertness to the game without affecting in any bad way the gameplay of PvE players - note that their gameplay can be changed, in the sense that they could attack Eternal Warriors. But for those who never intended to do so, they will not see their gameplay affected.

This would also be a great way to make the dishonorable ranking system more alive - by killing a low-level Eternal Warrior, you would suffer a dishonorable rank.

I would warmly welcome your opinions regarding this potential feature (especially from the WPvP advocates) and / or suggestions.
