# Warrior Class Changes Thread

## Post 35879 by Kingmalakk — 2023-02-24T02:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35879#p35879 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been playing Vanilla WoW a long time, as more of a social player & mats farmer for big raiding guilds. Most of my gameplay is surrounded by leveling and open world PVP, which is the reason why I'm making this thread.  smiling_turtle_head

Warrior is powerful in the late game, they can pump, and they can tank. They're amazing at end game, and the TurtleWoW Devs seem to be aware of this given the small touch ups they made for Warrior. But, I think it's always interesting to see what people think about their leveling speed, and if there's any changes they'd see that fit Warrior.

Me personally, I feel like leveling as a Warrior isn't as much challenging, as much as it is tedious. They lack a lot of the other tools that classes get for leveling, in exchange for the powerful kit for PVP / PVE end-game.
I felt a sensible change for Warrior would be to address some of their problems leveling to make the class more engaging and pro-active.

I thought an interesting addition to their kit would be some kind of Honorable Kill feature that would help them continue pulling mobs to help speed up their leveling moderately. I had two ideas come to mind for this, both as some kind of passive ability or maybe self-buff with a cooldown which creates windows where they're more effective.

 1. +5 / +10 / +15 / +20 Rage on Kill + Minor bonus effect (Strength buff?)

 2. Heal for 5% - 8% of Max Health on Kill + Parry & Block Increase (Both minor buffs, maybe 1% / 2%)

Both these changes I feel would be great to help either their momentum or survivability while leveling. Warrior innately I think deserves to have weaknesses, via a lack of mobility & other useful utility  (Like Rogue vanish & blind or Hunter traps) but these would help improve what they're meant to be good at.
With these changes, since they definitely could / would have an impact in PVP, could definitely make some small tweaks if necessary to do more of a power exchange.

The most obvious change that came to mind would be adding Victory Rush <https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Victory_Rush> to their kit, which I feel would be a bit lazy, but it might be exactly perfect. I'd be happy to discuss the concepts more in the replies. Wonder what people think.

That said, stay safe friends. Drink water, eat, and sleep well.

Bloodcraze is a tough sell even for pvping currently, but for the sake of argument, we say we don't want it to be stronger than it currently is for r14 equipped players because free passive selfhealing in too large amounts can be extremely frustrating to play against.

A lvl 60 Tauren warrior without gear has 2,760 health. In R14 equipment that goes up to about 5,930 give or take depending on the offset pieces. If we make bloodcraze heal 6% of basehealth instead of 3% total health, it will heal said r14 warrior a tiny bit less than it does currently, but it massively boosts leveling warriors.
I think we could even consider giving 7/8% of Basehealth. 8% Basehealth on a level 60 would be 220 Health, compared to the current 178 on a fully rank 14 geared warrior. But notably with basehealth, using Lifegiving gem, Titans Flask or Stamina buff would not further increase the ammount healed.

So i think the abuse potential isn't particularly big.
But it of course would have to get tested, rogues struggle already quite a bit against warriors, but i'm pretty sure bloodcraze isn't the reason why :D
