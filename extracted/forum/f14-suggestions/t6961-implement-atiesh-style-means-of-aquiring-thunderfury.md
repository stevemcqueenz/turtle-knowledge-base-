# Implement Atiesh style means of Aquiring Thunderfury

## Post 44027 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-05-01T19:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44027#p44027 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Thunderfury is an iconic weapon that is BiS for Warrior and Paladin tanks. Getting a BiS never replaced weapon, in the first half of molten core, is something people many people like while many others don't, but that's just how it is right now, and I don't think it would be a popular change if TF were nerfed to a reasonable powerlevel.

So, because TF is so insanely overpowered and at its job, the RNG of what garr and gedon decide to put out each week, singlehandedly decides if your guild will have to worry about threat issues or not. "Ok so whats wrong with that" A higher threat output is by far the best thing that can be done by 1 player that can decide how well a raid does. A high threat tank means that dps rarely have to watch their threat, they can go full ham, warriros use reck, good ignites held longer, and not having the risk of threat pulls means significantly less wipes due to threatpulling a mob that wipes a raid if it faces them. A high threat tank isn't just a nicety, its a night or day difference.

Ok, so maybe now you can understand why TF is HUUGE for a guild once their tank obtains it.

Here's the kicker: TF can be acquired in **1 week**. Or it can take **3 years**. It's 100% up to RNG whether or not your raid team has a TF tank right out the gate.... or never. This is indefensible.

My proposal:

Rework Thunderfury to be aquired like how Atiesh is acquired, the other powerful legendary. This weapon is bis or a bis alternative for most casters and healers, and it brings significantly less pain in its aquisiion due to one simple thing: a guild is basically guaranteed one Atiesh every 3-4 months.

Atiesh is acquired by gathering 40x of 30% chance drops that you get 13 dice rolls on each week. Because of the huge sample size, that means that good luck gets you Atiesh in 3 months, and bad luck keeps you from taking years and instead you'll get it around month 4. I can't put into words how much better this range of RNG is.

Turtle Thunderfury has a 6% chance binding drop from Baron Geddon, and a 5% from Garr. With Garr being the bottleneck, it takes an average of 20 raids to acquire both bindings. So with this math, I propose:

Decimated Shard of the Windseeker
Binds when picked up
Unique (10)
Classes: Warrior, Paladin, Hunter, Rogue
Use: Combines 10 Decimated Shards to form Bindings of the Windseeker.
"A fragment of the Right Half of Thunderaan's Eternal Prison"

This would have 50% drop chance on garr, and of course an identical left hand version for Baron. This means that after 20 runs, the average raid would indeed have 10 left shards and 10 right shards, can form the bindings, and complete the quest.  A bad RNG raid might have this take 30 raids. If RNGesus smiled upon, they could build this in 15 clears. It's pretty obvious that this deal is a MILLION times better than a 1 week or 3 years RNG spread.

Do this, and Thunderfury's nasty asterisk will be removed, and everyone will love the weapon and not want to shoot their head off trying to aquire it.

Oh btw implement an axe version of TF too maybe.
