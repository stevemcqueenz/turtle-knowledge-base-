# Outland factions brainstorm

## Post 13812 by Shatterfury (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-11-11T19:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13812#p13812 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While reading a thread about suggested raids in Outland I got hit by an idea. Bear in mind that this is just personal speculation, I don`t know what the devs have in mind.

What if we had two very interesting factions that fought against themselves and against the Legion as well, both having radical philosophies embodied by their leaders?

On the one hand you have Illidan leading a faction characterized by vengeance against the Legion at any cost, the goal justifying the means. This faction would be the one using anything, throwing even the sink at the Legion.

On the other hand we have Maiev leading a faction characterized by serving justice with a fiery zeal. For Maiev and her followers a wrong deed must be punished! And at the end of the day there is little difference between Illidan and the the Legion.

But until now we have a Night Elf struggle, so those factions would be nice to have an orc element as well so that the Horde is interested in those factions besides the doctrinal conflict.

Thus in Illidan`s faction we will find Teron Gorefiend, a venerable Death Knight of the first generations, a master of the dark arts in the body of a powerful Human Knight. With the passing of the time Teron has become delusioned with the Legion and by the comming of Illidan he was consumed with hatred for it, the entire power given by the Legion was vast, but it destroyed everything, thus Teron wanted to bring vengeance upon the Legion.
Gorefiend leads the Fel Orcs under the command of the Illidan who are ready to use the powers given by the Legion against it.

In Maiev`s faction we will have Kargath Bladefist, the old but fierce former Chieftain of the Shattered Hand Clan. He and a big part of Fel Orcs where found by Maiev`s Watchers while reeling from Fel withdrawal, rather than kill them a priestess convinced Maiev to at least try to cure the Fel corruption. The great majority survived the purge.
Kargath, almost completely being cleansed of the fel corruption vowed to free the rest of the Fel Orcs from the corruption that is still enslaved his people.

This way both factions have an orc element besides the Night Elf ore.

The other two factions would be, or course the remains of the  Alliance Expedition and the Bonechewer Clan who escaped fel corruption by hiding in the mountainous areas or maybe the devs want to go the Mag`har route.

## Post 13835 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2021-11-13T07:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13835#p13835 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would be interested to see how each faction treats the demons of the legion. Obviously they'd all be antagonistic, but Maiev was more focused on Illidan and saw the demons as just roadblocks rather than a primary threat, while Illidan was the opposite and sought to end the demons entirely. How would the orcs in each of their throngs react to this? Would the night elves have conflicts with the fel orcs who want to focus on killing demons over Illidan? Would the orcs in Illidan's group get disillusioned with his tactics that would probably sacrifice them often for meager gains?

And where do the Draenei (real ones, not space goats) fall into this? They sided with Illidan in WC3, but that was because he saved them from extinction at the hands of fel orcs. Wouldn't they react very poorly to those same orcs being welcomed into their ranks? Would they see it as a betrayal to their loyalty? If Akama decides to stay, will it fracture the faction? And how would those dissidents be treated? Would it be a neutral relationship or would it be like catholics and protestants in ye olden days?

And then when you make those decisions, you'd have to imagine how it would sculpt their choice in settlements and structure. War-like protective palisades and hefty fortifications, workshops prioritizing metallurgy and weapons, or possibly more reclusive and naturally protected by terrain. So many ideas could pop up from that line of thought it could really make the continent feel alive.
