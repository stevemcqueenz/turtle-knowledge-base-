# PvE/Leveling: Make the outside world varied, a bit more challenging

## Post 100369 by Nitaya (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-08-29T09:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100369#p100369 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Guys,

A few ideas on how to make the leveling part a bit more unpredictable and the world a bit more varied and going to share them with you (even if no one cares). I guess many of us are on auto-pilot during leveling and besides a wrong pull, there is not much to pay attention to.
Everything that I list here would be valid only for NPCs **outside** of instances.

 **1. NPC racial:**
As I have stumbled upon:
Crushridge Ogre <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=2252>
Mirefin Murloc: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4359>

Mirefin Murloc has more HP / Armor / DMG due to being 1 lvl higher.
I mean one is a large bulky ogre, and the other is a small murloc. Maybe besides the level difference, their race should bring some unique attributes to the NPCs that we are facing and it would be applied to every one of them of the same race.
A few examples, nothing groundbreaking:

Ogres: +10% health, -15% attack speed, and Bash ability:2% chance on hit to stun the target for 1 sec.
Murlocs: -10% health, -15% armor, increased dodge by 3%, increased frost resistance by 20.
Night elf: +5% dodge, 2% health regen every 5 seconds during the night
Undeads: Regenerates 5% health every 5 seconds, increased shadow resistance by 20.
Demons: Attacks ignore 25% armor

At the same time, if a race is buffed slightly, it should be worth 5% more XP.

 **2. Champion buff:**
Let's have some unpredictability in the NPC spawn during questing as they might be stronger than expected.
Every non-elite, non-named NPC, that is higher than lvl 20 when spawns have:
 10% chance to become "Seasoned" receiving 1 random buff
 1% chance to become "Veteran" receiving 3 random buffs
 0,01% chance to become "Champion" receiving all buffs (due to increased size, there would be a visual clue)

The naming would be visible, so the player would be aware he is facing an improved enemy.

The pool of buffs is the following:
+50% HP and increased size
+120 resistance against a random element
+60 resistance against all elements
+35% physical/magic DMG done increase
Dodge and parry chance increased by 10%
Immune to all kinds of movement impairing effect
Every CC effect has -75% duration
Armor is doubled
Casting & Attack speed increased by 30%
On each attack, it has a 5% chance to stun the target for 1 sec
Attacks & spells ignore armor/magic resistance.

The NPC yields 50% more XP for each buff it has. Veterans have an increased chance of dropping BoE green. Champion is for BoE blue.

 **3. More EvE/events/patrols**
Certain factions should be hostile to each other.
If you pull a Gelkis centaur to a Magram village, they won't aggro each other. Why? Arent they at war?
Stromgarde keep: Syndicate and the stromgarde military not aggroing each other when you pull one to another. Again, why isn't there a conflict between them?

I like when in Arathi the undead/human patrol meet and start whacking each other. When Stitches going for Darkshire. When you have to pay attention on Barrens / Loch Modan to not run into the Horde/alliance patrol

Give us some EvE events that happen once in a while. A group of 3-4 centaurs going to raid the other village once every 2 hours or so, going for the clan leader. However, due to running away and agroing other mobs they will most probably fail but let's have some active environment.
From tainted scar sometimes a few demons start pushing Nethergarde Keep.
A group of Defias patrolling in Westfall between the key locations with multiple routes.

That also requires the player to look around and be aware of the surroundings.
(That would also increase the difficulty of the HC challenge, but we all know they are in a zone 10-15 lvl lower than they are. )

EvE in Gurubashi Arena.
When there is no PVP Arena event for the players, I would like to see a team of random NPCs from around the world fighting each other, where players cannot interact just watch and chill.

Thanks for reading.
