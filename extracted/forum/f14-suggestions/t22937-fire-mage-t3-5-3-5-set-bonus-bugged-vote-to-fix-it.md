# Fire Mage T3.5 3/5 Set Bonus Bugged (Vote to Fix it)

## Post 160138 by R3xi3l — 2025-11-20T06:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160138#p160138 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

If you wish to see T3.5 3/5 Set being fixed, vote here to fix it!

Link: <https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=18568>

Currently the Mage T3.5 3/5 Set Bonus does not work properly.

First of it accounts for the "BASE" Damage of the AoE when it "REWINDS" such as if "FLAMESTRIKE" has 419-513 Damage it will 513x0.2= 103 Damage

Second it only effects 1 Mob doesn't apply the damage of the AoE spell to all mobs, but it damages 1st mob and stops. Meaning all the time even if you hit 20 Mobs, and do 13,000 Damage you will get only 100-175 damage depending on the ability rewinded as it doesn't damage all the "MOBS" just the closest one, and stops after it as it has no AoE damage nor it scales with your Spell Power or Crit.

Example. If it only accounts for the "BASE" Spell Damage then it would stil need to account for more hits.

Take 10-20 Dummies (or mobs) and AoE-em + Scrolling Battle Text for better testing.

It should "REWIND" the ability damaging all the "MOBS" not just 1 + scale with Spell Power and Crit.

"The wording “rewind effect, dealing an additional 20% damage” strongly implies that the proc should mirror the AoE spell itself, not just hit one target."

Flamestrike should deal its AoE damage, then proc an extra 20% AoE damage.

Blast Wave should hit all enemies again for 20% of the damage.

Arcane Explosion should splash again for 20%.

The current single-target Mirror Magic proc doesn’t align with the tooltip or the design intent.

"Rewind" is supposed to mean replaying part of your AoE spell’s damage across all targets. Either fix the T3.5 or fix the name of it or change it to "Your AoE spells have a chance to unleash Mirror Magic, striking an additional target for 20% base damage of ability”

Example:

 5 Hits = 515 damage
 10 Hits = 1,030 damage
 15 Hits = 1,545 damage
