These sets are no longer obtainable on Turtle WoW, since the Scourge Invasion event has long since passed:

Regalia of Undead Cleansing (cloth): [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item-se ... -cleansing](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item-set=536/regalia-of-undead-cleansing)

Quartermaster Miranda Breechlock ([https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=115 ... breechlock](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=11536/quartermaster-miranda-breechlock)) should be able to sell one piece of the item set at honored, another at revered, and the final one at exalted, in exchange for Argent Dawn Valor Tokens: [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=12 ... alor-token](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=12844/argent-dawn-valor-token)
