# Make Curse of Elements affect Nature Damage

## Post 28038 by Kuz — 2022-10-27T08:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28038#p28038 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone,

There are 5 types of resistances in Vanilla. Fire, Frost, Arcane and Shadow are all covered by Curse of Elements and Curse of Shadows respectively. Nature is the only school in the game, that does not get a benefit from these curses since Holy Resistance is not a thing in Vanilla. The primary users of Nature Spells are Shamans and Moonkins, which already perform poorly compared to other casterclasses in raids. But Moonkins mostly just spam Starfire, which is an Arcane Spell and thus does indeed benefit form Curse of Shadows. That leaves Elemental Shamans as the only Class suffering form this circumstance and huge partial resists on spells become the norm for them, gimping DPS significantly.

Curse of Elements make thematical sence to Reduce Nature Resistance, since Nature/Earth(Shock) sounds like an Element to me. Please change this to save us form the Meme-Talking-Lords.

Kind Regards
Kuz
