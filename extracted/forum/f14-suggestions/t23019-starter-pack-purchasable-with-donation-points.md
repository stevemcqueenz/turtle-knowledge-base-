# "Starter pack" purchasable with donation points

## Post 160652 by Mortelwombat — 2025-11-25T13:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160652#p160652 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It's been a minute since I started from scratch on a vanilla server.  I forgot how miserly the game is at the start, and what an incredibly pain in the gonads it is having no bag space and not being able to afford training or even using the bird to travel.

You should consider a starter pack donation reward containing just enough to take the agony out of starting from 0 on a new realm.  I'm thinking four 10 slot bags and 5 gold for some reasonable cost like 100 donation points.  A small enough quantity of gold that it would be completely impractical to "buy gold" at any scale with this package, but enough to relieve the severe cash anxiety from the first dozen levels.  You could even make it a one-time purchase per account.
