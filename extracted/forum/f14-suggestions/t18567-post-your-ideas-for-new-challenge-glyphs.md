# Post your ideas for new challenge glyphs

## Post 126533 by Norovirus — 2025-04-12T16:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126533#p126533 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I love all of the different challenge glyphs on turtle wow and have done all but the level 1 lunatic, as well as some combos of various glyphs. Leveling and dungeons are my favorite part of wow, but having leveled to 60 many times it has become quite easy. All of the present glyphs add difficulty and variety in one way or another, making for a refreshing leveling experience. Here are some more glyph features I’d be interested in seeing (whether combined, or spread across various glyphs for greater customization):

 1.	Weakling: increased damage taken and/or reduced damage dealt. Something like 30% of each would emulate the vagrant challenge in terms of difficulty, while still allowing you to progress and customize your character through loot, incentivizing questing and dungeons instead of just mob farming. It also is more balanced across classes than the vagrant challenge (ie. impacts warriors the same amount that it impacts hunters).

 2.	Underleveled- You only get xp from mobs and quests 3 or more levels higher than you. This idea comes from the PVE twinking scene where you try to do dungeons multiple levels above you. It can be quite fun and challenging.

 3.	Level lock mode: a much more difficult to implement version of underleved, in which you get xp locked every 5 or 10 levels until you complete a set of objectives, typically requiring a higher level of gearing and cooperation than normal play and sending you all over the world. Some potential examples: duo kill hogger at level 9, Clear BFD at level 19, SM armory at 29, all 3 Dire Mauls at 59, etc. Ideally you could group with anyone but wouldn’t get credit for the objective if there is someone in the group above the objective's level lock. That way you can join normal groups to grind dungeons for gear, but need to make a group at or below your level to ultimately complete the challenge objetives and unlock the next level range.

 4.	Thanos mode- You get zero or significantly reduced quest xp. The focus is on mob grinding, but here’s the catch: you get less and less xp every time you kill the same mob, forcing you to branch out and kill a variety of mobs equally to keep leveling up.

 5.	Xp loss on death: slow and steady’s 5% xp loss upon death is not enough to make death feel meaningful. Something more like a full level lost on death would increase the threat without discouraging risk taking too much.

 6.	Softcore mode: This mode is for those who like hardcore but feel too attached to their toon. Lose all gold and items on death, trade and grouping restricted only to other softcore players within level range. I’ve played this mode just by imposing the restrictions on myself and it was a lot of fun, but it's a pain trying to do group content without an official glyph, since you can’t group with the hardcore players and the regular community will get you killed very quick. Could incorporate xp loss on death as well.

I’d love to hear others’ ideas for new glyphs.
