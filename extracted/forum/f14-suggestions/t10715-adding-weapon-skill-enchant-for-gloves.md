There are innate racials and cultural/historical/environmental racials, one influences the other over time, and they're all ment to show the difference in relation to other races.
(All racials are positive, although I'd like to see negative racials as well, and more racials being class-bound)
If there was only one race on Azeroth, with the same biology, the same aptitudes, the same cultural development and history, and exposed to the exact same environmental factors, then there would be no need for racials.
As soon as you change only one of these things, and it doesn't even have to be the race, it could be only the location, which then would affect myriad of things, then logically racials come to play to denote the difference, and if they don't then you are not being true to the world that has been built for the sake of game mechanics, also known as standardization. Races of choice in WoW are not just aesthetic configurations that go beyond the ones available for each race, it goes deeper than that.

Night Elves have more acute senses and pay more attention to their environment, they are more savage but also more graceful than humans. Human warriors use specifically swords culturally (and paladins hammers), like orcs use axes, and train with them, even before level 1 your character already knows some abilities pertaining to it's class and wears some class specific items, displaying that it is a person that was alive before you assumed it's role and wasn't just born (except maybe for undeads that were somewhat reborn), and life choices made in the context of its race led it on the path of it's class. (I won't go into detail describing every variable that led to these differences, there is enough lore available for it)
Hence the increased capacity to dodge for Night Elves, for example. If you want to make them more considerable gameplay wise, adding them +bow skill and +hit chance would be much more accurate.
There is no need to homogenize all the racials, and it is alright if one race is better at one thing and another at something else, that the classes of all races have their unique strengths and weaknesses.

Mativh, I’m a very big fan of your post on immersive racials but your take here is not very good.
You can argue for the lore accuracy (or inaccuracy) of any racial. Sure, humans having +5 skill to swords and hammers makes sense, but it’s not hard to argue how it might have made sense for another race as well.
The fact is that vanilla humans were blessed with an insanely overpowered racial, and orcs were blessed with two. We’re not talking about a 1% DPS increase, it’s closer to 10%. Orcs get the added benefit of having the strongest PvP racial on top.

This doesn’t strengthen race flavour, but diminishes it. People feel forced to choose their race based on raid performance rather than fantasy, and when playing one of the “good” melee races, feel forced in their choice of weapons.
If we were to remove weapon skill racials, they could be replaced by new ones with way more flavour

Some people will always cry for more balance, even if differences between racials were closer to 1%. This is called minmaxxing, always making choices based on optimisation rather than fantasy, and it’s a cultural issue not fixable via ingame solutions.
Weapon skill racials are just so overpowered that they require fixing even from a non-minmaxxing point of view.
