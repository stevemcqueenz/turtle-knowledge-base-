# Autosummon last pet after death in bloodring

## Post 74600 by Delalama — 2023-10-29T07:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74600#p74600 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Recommended change:

Auto resummon last pet if you died in Bloodring

Rationale:

If you die in a bloodring match, you have to resummon your pet, using another shard.

Warlocks burn through a lot of shards in bloodring.

This would be a small quality of life improvement for warlock PVP.
