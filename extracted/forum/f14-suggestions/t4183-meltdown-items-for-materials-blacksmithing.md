## Post 25090 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-15T10:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25090#p25090 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This would be very hard to balance, because white gear is most often bought from vendors, and would drive down material prices if people find a profitable (cheap) item to melt.

It is an interesting idea that could be applied to leatherworking and tailoring as well:
Weapons, Plate and Mail armor: Blacksmiths can melt these to get bars
Leather armor: Leatherworkers can dismantle it to get leather (and rarely hides)
Cloth armor: Tailors can unsew (for lock of a better term lol) it to get Cloth and threads

I propose to limit it to green+ gear that can also be disenchanted (so no vendor greens) and having a chance to give grey (junk) items instead of materials, so it wouldn't be too unbalanced.
