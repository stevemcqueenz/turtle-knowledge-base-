Current firefin market looks like this

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/hFNfn20.png)
This is not the kind of fish people deliberately hunt for unless a source of starting money for lowbies, and the price was about 2-4 gold.
Untill the current moment when price has dropped almost to "not worthy to bother with". Yet auction house is full of this fish.

I did not believe some1 is spending his time for that low tier fish obtaining 6 stacks per day while they have much more valuable stuff to farm, so its surely a bot stuff happening no doubts. Due to turtle wow fishing pool nerf most firefin open waters are transparent and i have not found anything suspiciout untill i discovered the Walling Caverns instanced river has remained intact and allows to fish there for firefin while being hidden from random passing players who might report you.

I have reported some people from Walling Caverns i found suspicious (wont write how i found them for obvious reasons) but once GM answered they are not online anymore, surely banned. Botter does not have to raise his bot to 35+ nat paggle, just escort lowbie inside and no1 will bother to monitor you among other 17 levels sitting in the instance list.
While the income is not that big you might say, it still completely ruins start income for honest lowbies.

Suggestion - remove firefin from the instanced fishing pools to prevent hidden botting.
