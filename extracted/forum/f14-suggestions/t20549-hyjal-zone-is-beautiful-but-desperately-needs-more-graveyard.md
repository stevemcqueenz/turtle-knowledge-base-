# Hyjal zone is beautiful but desperately needs more graveyards

## Post 142045 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-29T10:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142045#p142045 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Hyjal zone is stunning and absolutely gigantic, one of the most immersive places I’ve seen so far. But I ran into a big issue when trying some world PvP near the bottom of the map. After I died, I realized the only graveyard was all the way up top, which took ages to climb back down.

On a PvP server I enjoy spontaneous world PvP, but here the time cost is so punishing that I probably will not be spending more time there. I don’t want to spend my entire day just corpse running, and it sadly makes me not want to go there at all. The opportunity cost is just too high, and it kills the fun of open-world fights.

Could you consider adding additional graveyards (even flight path spirits would help) to make it less punishing if you happen to die?
