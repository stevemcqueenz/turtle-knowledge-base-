# Suggestion: Just an experiment they could test.

## Post 167071 by Fugus (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-31T17:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167071#p167071 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

This isn't a long-term thing I am suggesting, just maybe a month or two.

But for that duration, disable all racials, both active and passive, while in Battlegrounds, just to test the overall balance within those BGs. So you will have a baseline if the issue is with racials or not when it comes to wins and losses.

Not 100% sure on here, but during my retail days, the alliance lost a lot until they got really overpowered racials near the time I stopped playing. But a lot of it was mindset as well as most of my friends who wanted to be the bad ass and kick ass rolled the horde while my friends who wanted to RP and be the good guys tended towards the beautiful people on the alliance.

So, just for a while, disable all the racials on both sides while in the battlegrounds and maybe server wide. If the BG wins and losses stay lopsided, swap sides at random for a bit and see if changing the starting points in the BGs impacts it.

That will allow you to set your baseline on what to expect.

> Pretty cool idea, so you saying instead of having debuffs for specs and classes just make everyone the same in pvp. Not very rp but good idea for pvp to just disable racial difference.

I am against the debuff for classes and specs regardless and see them as a universally bad thing, but this wouldn't impact that either way.

Just think of it like this. For 2 months, no one has access to racials, not Will of the Forsaken, not Stonefrom, not Berserk, not even the Gnome intellect passive or elemental resistance racials.

Then you see just how much or little impact that have in battlegrounds or any other PvP arena during that time. And if things stayed lopsided in a few BGs, swap starting points for the sides at random as well.

That would set a nice baseline for things. If you start seeing the wins/losses vary drastically, you know the racials are the issue, if they barely budge, you know it's not. If swapping the starting sides swaps who wins a lot, you know that's an imbalance in that BG, otherwise it's not.

Great way to rule things out and see just where the baseline is.

Edit: And where things go from there can depend on what this experiment shows. But it's at least an informed step.

What I am thinking is more like this. They have a list of the win/loss ratios in PvP overall. Disable the racials and see how it impacts them. The issue with raid geared players throwing it off goes both ways but thats at least predictable to an extent as they can even compare player numbers and average gear scores between the groups during this time as well as the class break downs and all.

So, with the racials disabled, how does it impact the ratios of the wins and losses. If it's a huge swing when you take into account the gear and all, then you know the racials are a huge issue, if the ratios are largely unmoved, then you know the racials aren't a huge thing, its something else.

If the racials aren't enough to test, swap starting sides at the start of the BGs and see how that impacts stuff.

After you take into account the impacts of them, then the only real differences left would be paladins and shamans and the mentality of the players playing and potentially their locations if 1 faction plays with significantly higher latency on average due to different playing locations.

This might not be the perfect experiment, but it is still one they could get really data from on how much of a balance is the racials are in PvP as well as the starting locations in the BGs.
