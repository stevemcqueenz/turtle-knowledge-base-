It is. When you put it into parctical terms, what you're getting from this change is about 300 mana saved per 10 minutes and a GCD (which is neglectable due to plenty of downtime). So essentially it is a 0.5 mana per second (2.5 mp5) buff to shamans who need their weapons buffed - that would be enhancement dps and tank, which have mana issues anyway, so it's a good change. But most of all it's a QoL change.

Keep in mind I don't know the precise cost of weapon enhancements at level 60, so 300 mana is an estimation. The mp5 "buff" will of course be lower at lower levels.
