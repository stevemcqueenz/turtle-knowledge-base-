# Shaman tanks are squishy while leveling

## Post 147545 by Bezawit — 2025-08-21T18:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147545#p147545 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shaman tanks are definitely viable at level 60.

That said, my experience thus far on Ambershire is that they are the squishiest of the four tank classes while leveling. So how do we make tank Shaman less squishy while leveling and not boost them at 60? I think the best way to go about this is to give them access to something similar to a Warrior/Druid’s Demoralizing Shout/Roar.

My suggestion is to make Stoneclaw Totem also apply a Demoralizing Shout debuff, each time it pulses. I think this fits perfectly with what Stoneclaw Totem already does- pulses aggro so mobs attack it instead of the Shaman. An Attack Power debuff on this totem just makes sense.

So how would this help Shaman tanks while leveling? If a Shaman tank puts talent points into Totemic Alignment, [45/90%] of the threat generated by totems is transferred to them. They can drop Stoneclaw Totem with Totemic Alignment and have it not only pulsing threat directly on to the Shaman but also applying an Attack Power debuff so that the mobs deal less damage.

If you make this debuff not stack with Demoralizing Shout/Roar, it is a perfect way to increase a Shaman tank’s mitigation while doing leveling dungeons but not impact raids.

I see what you're aiming for but I don't think this solution would work.
Adding damage reduction to stoneclaw would make stoneskin's niche questionnable.
Warriors and bears have shouts to reduce damage. Palas and shamans (on paper) solve this via blessing (sanctuary) or totem (stoneskin).
It gets a bit complicated since you can't use the damage reduction totem under many situations (tremor might be needed, want bigger aoe nthreat etc), but the option exists.

The shammie mitigation talents kick in later, spec after mail items since percentual increase from armors really work if the items have bigger armor.

An option is the defensive CD but it is a very debated topic, will skip for now.

Imo the best option is to introduce elemental shields at the same time. No reason to wait for water shield and earth shield.
If you give shamans a properly scaled down rank 1 version by the time they enter RFC, earth shield can work as extra mitigation.
