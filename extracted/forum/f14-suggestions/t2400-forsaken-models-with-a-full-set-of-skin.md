Obviously that kind of procedural technology wasn't available around 2004 so Forsaken were left in a semi-decayed state. Now Retail WoW has all these kinds of cosmetical options, but that's only because the developers have no more ideas how to milk the game and are expanding it horizontally with cosmetics of all kinds, not because it's lore-friendly, WoW stopped being lore-friendly when TBC launched.

I'd much rather the Turtle team focus on releasing the Class Update and announce what's coming in the near future in terms of actual playable content rather than some (mostly irrelevant) cosmetic stuff.

I think people need to understand something about Turtle WoW - it's main selling point is that it adds more gameplay elements on top of Vanilla WoW. This is what makes it stand out from other servers and why people are interested in playing here. Nobody is going to get psyched about playing here just because they have a Gnome with a skin where there is a birthmark right between his eyes that makes him look like The Buddha.
