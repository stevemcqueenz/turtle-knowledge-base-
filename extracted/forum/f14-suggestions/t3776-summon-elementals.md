Talented abilities for Mage and Shaman.
Summon Water or Fire Elemental to fight by your side for Mage.
Summon Earth or Wind Elemental to fight by your side for Shaman.

All of below are level 60 talents.
Water Elemental is spell within frost mage talent tree that summons Water Elemental,
it has spells like water bolt and passive skill that let's you regenerate mana.
Fire Elemental has abilities like fireball and it's passive ability is that it damages nearby enemies.
Earth Elemental is tank class summon that has Taunt, Earth Shields that heal it when it's damaged, as well it has ability Tremor that shakes ground around it and slows nearby enemies.
Wind Elemental has Lightning Bolts and ability called Wind Walking aura that increases movement speed of nearby allies.
