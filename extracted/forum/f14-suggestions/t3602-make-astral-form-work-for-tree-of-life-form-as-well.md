## Post 21749 by Greatgrass — 2022-08-10T07:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21749#p21749 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

-Tree of Life is now the only druid form that doesn't have a glyph.
Why not quickly alleviate this by making its first glyph simply work in conjunction with the moonkin Astral Form?
There are very few moonkin on any wow server, so this glyph probably isn't seeing a lot of action as it is. Many moonkin such as myself are probably respeccing to healer regularly, and would be able to enjoy using the astral form on both.
 -Transmogs are a big selling point to the server. Astral Form allows you to still see your transmogs even in moonkin. This is astral form's greatest asset, and why I bought it from the donation shop.
I am probably a minority in saying this, but I think the Tree of Life form is ugly to the degree that I stopped healing in TBC. And now that it's here, I'm no longer going to be healing here either hahaha. If I were able to use the Astral Form while in restoration, I would resume healing personally, as I would be able to see the character and transmogs I worked hard on instead of an ugly treant.
 -This would assumedly be very easy to implement and a reasonable request.
