# Critical Gear Fix Needed for Shaman Tanks (Tier Sets)

## Post 156836 by Crabsi — 2025-10-24T08:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156836#p156836 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**![📜](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4dc.svg) [REQUEST] Critical Gear Fix Needed for Shaman Tanks (Tier Sets)**

Dear Developers,

I would like to address a fundamental issue that severely limits the viability of the Shaman Tank—one of the unique specializations of Turtle WoW.

The core problem is the **extreme scarcity of appropriate gear**.

 **Gear Drought:**
When searching the database for Mail armor and filtering by basic tank stats (*Stamina, Agility, Defense/Parry/Dodge/Block*), you get an **alarmingly small number of hits** outside of the later T2.5 sets.

 **Missing Tank Set:**
A dedicated Mail Tank Set with the necessary stats is missing. Without it, survival as a full-fledged tank and reaching defensive caps becomes nearly impossible.

The **Shaman Needs 4 Tier Sets, Not 3**

I also want to emphasize that the Shaman currently requires four distinct gear sets, not just three, to support all viable specializations effectively.

The core issue is that **Shaman Enhancement DPS requires different gear than Shaman Enhancement Tank**.

- The current Enhancement sets prioritize **Attack Speed, Critical Strike and Hit Rating**—essential for a melee DPS.
- The Shaman Tank, however, requires high **Stamina, Defense Rating (or Block%, Dodge%, Parry%), Agility, and Armor** to survive.
Therefore, we need a dedicated Tank Set in addition to the existing sets for Healing (Restoration), Range DPS (Elemental), and Melee DPS (Enhancement).

 **My Proposal to Solve the Gearing Problem:**

Please consider adding a **fourth, additional set** to the existing Tier Sets (T1, T2, and T3) that is specifically tailored to the needs of the Shaman Tank. This set would provide the necessary gear progression within raids and close the equipment gap caused by the lack of defensive Mail items.

This change would massively enhance the experience for all players dedicated to the Shaman Tank role and boost the appeal of this niche specialization.

 *This is a continuation of the discussion from this thread:* [**Link to the Discussion**](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=48&t=20676)

Thank you for your time and for considering this critical issue!   happy_turtle_head
