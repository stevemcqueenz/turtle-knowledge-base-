# Self Found Mode

## Post 54587 by Kawerte1 — 2023-07-25T11:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54587#p54587 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to suggest a mode in which:
 - You can't trade, can't use the AH and can't send mail with attachments;
 - You will be able to group up only with other characters as long as the difference between your levels isn’t higher than 5.
 - Maybe raise or completely remove the profession amount limit?

There would be some kind of rewards of course and maybe the title "The Collector" or something else.
It should be easy to implement as an additional scroll like the already existing game modes.

Why make such a mode:

 1) I like the idea of HC, but for veteran players, who have gone through the game and know its mechanics and content. Personally I've played the game only as max level on PvP servers many years ago and my girlfriend has never played it. We would give up on the game very fast after a few deaths since losing your character because of your inexperience or PC/internet issues is not fair or fun. Other noobs would have the same issue.

 2) Why not play normal and self impose the rule to not trade and not use the AH?
We could start that way, but eventually, since we have the option to trade and use the AH, we would succumb to the dark side. It would be for something small with the reasoning "this would take hours to find/make by ourselves, we will buy only that item from the AH, it is not a big deal, it's just time saving, we'll be back on the challenge after this". Then we will use the same logic again and again and in the end realise we have been playing trading simulator all along.

 3) I think WoW is actually 3 separate games: PvE, PvP and trading/auction. Some people enjoy the trading game, while others use it as a crutch, but in the end it just affects badly the other 2 games. Crafting or finding a rare item should mean something. Making your gold by playing the game is in my opinion much better than trading/auction.

 4) Why not disable grouping as well and make it SSF?
Removing the grouping would remove a lot of good PvE content and a large chunk of the social aspect of the game,
while removing trading doesn't do that.

If you support what I said, please say so. If you disagree, use constructive critisism.
I hope the devs read this.

I would really love this mode. In particular the lack of profession limit. I like the social aspect of playing together in wow but I think unlimited trading just ruins a lot of the balance, especially trading between characters.

I guess you can enforce no trading on yourself but with the two profession limit there's a lot of things in the game you just can't do.

Would be cool to get your Paladin 60 mount all by yourself. Actually going around and collecting all those mats instead of just buying them. Would be an interesting twist on wow which would really get back into it.

I doubt even with unlimited professions "self found" players would be out performing non-self found players, particularly when they have to gather all their own materials for flasks etc. If players somehow exploit the extra professions to massively increase their DPS in raids then put a DPS penalty on characters with more than two professions I don't care. Or don't allow them to get world buffs or something.

But it would be very cool as an option (I know many people won't be interested, but at least some will) and I don't think it will negatively affect the broader economy much because well, these characters won't be able to trade anyway!
