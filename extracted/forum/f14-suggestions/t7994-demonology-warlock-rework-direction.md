# Demonology warlock rework direction

## Post 51383 by Rakashus — 2023-06-26T17:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51383#p51383 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Personally, I would like to see demonology become warlock tanking spec. Hear me out:

 1. There already is a precedent for warlock tanking (Twin Emperors)
 2. Demonology has a lot of hidden tanking synergies - Demonic Embrace gives you more stamina, Demonic Aegis gives you more armor, DSing voidwalker restores 3% total health every 4 seconds, Master Demonologist with voidwalker out reduces physical damage taken by 10%, Soul Link transfers 30% of damage taken to your controlled demon (probably voidwalker for tanking). Granted, you cant use both DS and SL at the same time
 3. Searing Pain causes a high amount of threat and is ideal for single target tanking
 4. Cloth items that are designed to make you more tanky already exist in the game - like <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?itemset=520>, which gives you a lot of armor and has +armor set bonus
 5. Currently warlocks have 3 dps specs. Balancing a class with three dps specs is clearly very hard if not impossible, while balancing a class with only two dps specs would be much easier. Besides, who wouldnt want more tanks?
 6. This spec can also be combined with the currently underwhelming fire warlock theme since it utilises Searing Pain and possibly Hellfire for aoe threat

Here is a sample talent build with the current talent trees:
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock/ ... H1V-550UCI](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock/0-CUI1LH1V-550UCI)

We grab Intensity to resist pushback while casting Searing Pain, we have 11% crit chance on Searing Pain just from talents, we take all tanking talents from Demonology and Fel Domination/Master Summoner to instantly summon a new voidwalker to eat 30% damage in case first one dies
