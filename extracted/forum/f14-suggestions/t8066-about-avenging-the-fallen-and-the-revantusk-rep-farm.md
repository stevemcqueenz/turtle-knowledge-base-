They could make the repeatable a daily (or make a new daily because the repeatable is a bit easy) but they would also need to add a ton of Revantusk quests.

The High Elves have a ton of quests that give rep as you level, like the orphan quests, the Ironforge quests, the apple quests, the diplomacy quests, and so on.  Revantusk doesn’t have these kind of quests, which often means Horde misses out on capes because it takes a while to Honored as their repeatable is a higher level quest, as are many of their quests which were already in the game.

And honestly the Revantusk faction just isn’t as fleshed out and doesn’t have a solid storyline like the High Elves have.  More quests would help this out.

Alternatively, you could just leave them as is.  The factions don’t have to be the same.  Alliance have Paladins and Horde have Shaman.  It’s not the exact same.

Or you could add a repeatable quest for the elves.

As for adding more Skylords, it’s not necessary.  The best solution is to group up for them.  You can have a hardcore group and a non-hardcore group going since there are two.
