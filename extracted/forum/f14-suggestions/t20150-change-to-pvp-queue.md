# Change to PvP Queue

## Post 138037 by Cosmico (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-04T08:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138037#p138037 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!

The proposal is to make the PvP queue teleport you to the PvP NPC of your race (not faction) city. If you're a dwarf it will take you to ironforge military ward, and so on... the purpose of this is to put PvP and PvE in the same position of enjoyment and it has many benefits:

 1 make faction cities have a reason to go there, increasing population and circulation of players on them.

 2 make military npcs and quarters the purpose they were ment for, because this is an interesting part of the content.

 3 make players actually understand from where the pvp begins and how it must be engaged to assist fights.

 4 make the oportunity to enhance this zones of the cities allowing for further development.

 5 make possible the interaction between players for pvp purposes before battles.

Considerations:

 1 this teleports occurs only when the automatic queue has been asigned and executed.

 2 the players don't need to speak with the npcs (because they might not even know what they're for in the first place or who is the right npc) but it will teleport you anyway to the battlefield 30s to 1 minute later after being teleported, even if you get out from the quarters.

 3 players do can speak with the npcs to enter to the battleground faster as if they had to wait for the timer.

 4 after getting out from the battleground, you won't be teleported to the city but to the other place you were in the world, this to avoid people queuing to get free hearthstones and then leaving the battleground out of malice.

Thoughts:

 1 there has to be a development on this quarters to add some interesting or necessary content to revitalize their purpose.

 2 player who directly speak to the correct npc of their battleground may receive a buff for certain amount of increased defenses that endures just for the first defeat in the battleground, and for blood ring it could be a damage buff or increased resistance to abilities of control.

Rewards for the update:

 1 Winner players of the battleground may be rewarded extra with the option to end up in the military quarters instead of going back to the position they were in the world. Because they won the battleground and they might have enough points already to spend (or not) but it sounds like a good reason to apply for pvp even if the only thing you want is a free heartstone.

Remembering we're talking to racial capitals, not stormwind orgrimmar for all races. That would be too much of a reward, and we're scratching for ways to make racial capitals important.

Final comments:

After delivering multiple feedback to the turtles, I found this as emergency development not fancy and satisfaction.
