# Flightmaster in Tirisfal Uplands

## Post 100211 by Bugtypes — 2024-07-23T20:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100211#p100211 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tirisfal Uplands really needs a flight master either in Glenshire or Steepcliff Port. Currently, the closest flight point is either Undercity or the Sepulcher, and both take quite a while to get back to western Tirisfal.

As a low level zone, you don't have a mount to get there, and having to walk from Undercity or Sepulcher makes it really demotivating to quest there. It's a shame because IMO there should be MORE motivation for people to check out the custom zones and quests, not LESS.

Not to mention the fact that if you walk through Silverpine, you're always running the risk of getting bonked by a Son of Arugal, which is especially demotivating for HC characters.
