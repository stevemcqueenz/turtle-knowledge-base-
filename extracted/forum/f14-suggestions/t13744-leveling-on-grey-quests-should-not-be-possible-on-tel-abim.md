# Leveling on grey quests should not be possible on Tel'Abim

## Post 93500 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-25T16:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93500#p93500 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Current situation.
Now you get full xp from quests, which are 25 levels lower than character level.
It leads to situation that you can reach approx 20 level by doing quests only in starting locations of each faction.
I might not care about it on Nordnaar, but it makes huge difference on Tel'Abim.

You literally overlevel each zone and there is no threat in fighting against mobs there. They are barely threat for you and if any player attacks, you can just ignore the mob and fight player.

You might not follow this strategy and do only yellow quests, but you lose any fight against player 20 levels higher than you. So optimal path is to do every quest in the world.

Proposal is:
For example - treat grey quests like quests on 60 lvl. I mean grant money instead of experience for grey quests.
Or just disable this rule on Tel'Abim.

## Post 93518 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-04-25T21:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93518#p93518 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The thing is, leveling with grey quests is way WAY slower than doing quests for your level, so I don't see who would do that and why.
If you're a completionist, you can just stop your XP and enjoy the content as intended.

I don't see the issue here.

I however DO think quests you complete while your XP is frozen SHOULD give you money instead, as if they were completed at 60. This would open up new ways to farm money by questing, while not being max level. It could help people with their mount and training funds.
