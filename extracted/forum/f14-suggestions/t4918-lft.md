I like that there's an LFT system in concept but I think instead of it queing the players and partying them up automatically, It'd be much more engaging and less automated if it was just a job board, where there is Looking for Memeber, and Looking for Group. Where parties/players leave their details on the boards so other players can check it to request to join. That way players who still prefer "queing up" and waiting can still do that, but for those who are a bit more active also feel time well spent. I would imagine it would also minimize the bugs caused by the current LFT queing system. Just some thoughts.
