> **Garotheon wrote: Sat Jul 29, 2023 1:02 am**
> I would suggest the creation of the gathering professesion sub-division like secundary professions work but also limited at 2 for each time , like mining and herbalist, or skining / mining, those being chosed by the player.

I see several issues with this suggestion.
 1) WoW is a game of choices, and they should have a meaningful impact. It waters down the player experience if you can just re-do all choices at will. Some people like that. They love the brainwashing device, they want ways to exchange powerful quest rewards, they want to double the number of profession slots, they want race changes, etc. I can see the appeal in the short-term, but I think it would hurt the game in the long-term, when everyone can just be optimal and self-sufficient all the time. I enjoy trade-offs, they make your decisions interesting and your character somewhat unique.
With 3 gathering professions and 6 crafting professions giving 4 slots total you can cover way too many professions just on a single character.
 2) The economy runs on supply and demand. Professions are a way to make money, to support your raid/guild, etc. If everyone can mostly do everything themselves that aspect just breaks down.
 3) Gathering professions work in part, because the right people have to be at the right place at the right time. You can go to a super popular spot and still find herbs, because maybe nobody there is an herbalist. If everyone gets 2 free gathering professions, virtually nothing will be left standing. I find that boring and soul-less. It takes away that small happiness kick when you find a good node that matches your profession.
 4) The interface already struggles with 2 gathering professions because you can only have 1 search active to display nodes on the mini-map. Mining/Herbalism isn't super viable for that reason already, so 2 gathering slots would likely mean even more people with skinning, while leather is already a massively devalued and a flooded market.

> **Garotheon wrote: Sat Jul 29, 2023 1:02 am**
> Or make that the profession keep their level (or half their level) and recipes(would be nice to keep all) when its changed for another one. This would still make all gathering professions available and also would make the players need to do some leg day to change it in the most near trainer.

I am a little more open to this suggestion because it seems rather silly that you'd completely forget about your old profession. But I think it's necessary for balance reasons. Otherwise you'd effectively end up with *every* profession maxed out on a single character. Just visit the trainer and you can switch back... what's the point of investing time into your own character and try to sell your services when there's someone in SW who can do literally every recipe of every profession in the game as a one-stop-shop?
