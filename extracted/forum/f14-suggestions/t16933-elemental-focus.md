# Elemental focus

## Post 115579 by amanagor (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-06T10:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115579#p115579 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shaman spell elemental focus has been bugged for months now. Changelog said it had been fixed, yet it is still bugged. Tooltip says next 2 spells after a crit will have mana cost reduced by 60%. Currently only 1 spell is affected as a charge of the buff is consumed on spell cast and another when that spell lands. Due to inability of the devs to fix the bug, despite attempts being made. I suggest to change the talent to something simpler devs would be able to implement easily: on spell crit, 100% mana reduction on next spell cast, and reduction of base mana cost of chain lightning to let us use it in our rotation without ooming.
