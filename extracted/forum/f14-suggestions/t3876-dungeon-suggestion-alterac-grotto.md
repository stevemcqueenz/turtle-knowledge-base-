# Dungeon suggestion - Alterac Grotto

## Post 23309 by Nightowl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-24T11:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23309#p23309 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the more interesting organizations on Azeroth is the infamous Syndicate, which is a union between disgruntled Alterac Nobles and Shadow Council Orcs/Undead(using human illusion) - raised shadow council members who were most likely slain by Doomhammer when he came to power - of Argus Hand. The hook of this suggestion is the still living former King of Alterac  Aiden Perenolde, who ended up being retconned into being dead somewhere after Cataclysm, but since this isn't it, lorewise the king is still supposed to be alive, and most likely being the one pulling the strings behind the Syndicate's human arm. Similarly there's supposed to be an unknown "master" behind the Argus Wake, we never really learn who it is, but judging from the secrecy, I'm guessing that it is either a Nathrezim, or perhaps even an Eredar - one who subscribes to Kil'jaedens policy of secrecy no doubt - which does fit with the choice of "Argus", a planet from which the Eredar species hail from.

Perhaps this can be turned into a dungeon?

Suggestion on bosses:

#1) Syndicate Bandit Lord that was adopted into the "family" of Alterac nobles as a way to increase their manpower - fits the lore as well as it clearly states that Nobles adopted these bandits by making them part of their families since it gave them greater loyalty.
#2) Alterac Lord Mage. Most human kingdoms have a powerful Wizard as their head when it comes to all magical affairs. I refuse to believe Alteracians didn't have their own version.
#3) Orcish Warlock of Argus Wake, with cryptic quotes about Legions return and a cool FELGUARD PET!
#4) King Aiden Perenolde, a human warrior empowered by Fel magick?
#5) Master of the Argus Wake sect. I was thinking either a Dreadlord or an Eredar like the woman from Draenei starting zone in TBC. She was less of Archimonde and more of Kil'jaeden in terms of the engagement philosophy - aka don't engage enemy but fuck them over from within.

Dungeon level suggestion: 42-48, or level 60. Honestly, with introduction of Hateforge and Crescent Grove, I feel that there are more than enough dungeons in most levels, so it wouldn't hurt to make it level 60?

What do you guys think?
