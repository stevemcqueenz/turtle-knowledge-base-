Night Elves, however, are not in any way Shamanistic. In fact, their culture would never devote entire organizations of their people to communing with elemental spirits and worshipping only the elements; because it would go against their druidism, teachings of their goddess elune, and the emerald dream.

With that being said; there are a few races that could potentially become Shamans on the Alliance.

Furbolgs are a popular choice, but by and large are too feral for the Alliance as a whole. They don't participate in organized large scale societies in massive capital cities.

Wildhammer Dwarves are nearly the same issue, and it's debatable whether or not they have Shaman tendencies, or if they are just using lightning powers the same way Warriors do.
