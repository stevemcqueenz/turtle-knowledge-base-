# Priest Fade Mana-Leech & Offensive Holy Improvement

## Post 97604 by Matiel — 2024-06-22T16:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97604#p97604 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**1 Fade as mana regen alternative for spellcasting over wanding:**

 - Improved Fade in Shadow tree could add to Fade that during it, shadow abilities restore as mana x% of the shadow damage dealt to the enemy. So while levelling Shadow doesn't rely so much on Spirit. Shadow is a vampiric power, spirit should be a focus of Holy, and Wanding of discipline, Fade mana-leech from shadow spells for Shadow.

 *Fade is learned at lvl 10, and only reduces threat, for levelling, especially solo, its the perfect candidate to an alternative option for those that want to cast spells intead of spirit-wanding. It fits to the fantasy of shadow also. Fade could by basic restore 5% of shadow damage dealt as mana while fade is on, and improved fade could add another 5/10%.*

 **2 Improved Offensive Holy:**

 - Searing light talent increasing critical damage of offensive holy spells by 25/50%, instead of flat damage increase. 50/100% against undeads and demons. No longer increasing damage of these hy 5/10%.

 *Holy damage has improved crit chance from 2 talents, given the overall crit chance, this change would not be as much a buff to holy offensive l, but rather deepening its identity and playstyle, making it feel more rewarding.*

 - new ability Holy Word: Chastice; apply on enemy target to allow it to be targeted by your healing spells, Renew dealing 100% of its healing as a holy damage over time, the rest of abilities 25%. This % is doubled against undeads and demons.

 - your holy fire cleanses your shadow word: pain from the target. (encouraging to use holy word chastive renew dot for offendive holy)

 *priests have have Shadow word and Power Word, this would make holy healing sbilities especially renew usable on hostile tatgets as holy damage, and the undead/demon increase improves the RP aspect too.*

 - holy fires dot increases holy damage done to the target by x%

 *Giving offensive holy good group utility as well as encouraging the use of holy fire not just smite.*

 - new passive Righteous Determination; pushback of smite or holy fire increases critical chance of smite and holy fire by 1% for 5 seconds, stacks up to 5 times and is spent once smite or holy fire is cast.

 *Quite interesting, pushback for the priest while levelling is very frustrating, now it would be simultaneously rewarding too. Good pushes against Evil pushes back and that's okay, but the Light pushes through regardless via the power of faith and will.*
