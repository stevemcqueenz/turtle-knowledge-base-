# LFT - Raise minimum levels

## Post 31929 by Refuge — 2023-01-02T00:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31929#p31929 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Look, I know 'it's possible, I did it at that level!', 'what if they're the healer, it's fine for them usually', etc., but the lowest band of allowed LFT levels for dungeons in LFT needs to be raised. Often it's complete newbies who assume that if LFT tells them they're ready for a dungeon then they are.

Random Joe Newbie, who has been cruising pretty quick with tents and is also undergeared, can queue up for SM Cathedral at Lv.35. For Uldaman at Lv.40. Nobody wants to see that, or be the grouch who says "Hey, can this guy even scratch the last boss?"

And in the end LFT is just one tool, if someone is really ready below that level you can just invite them outside of the LFT system. People automatically sorted into a group using the tool should follow a rule of the lowest band allowed being 3 levels below the level of the final dungeon boss, to make for a smoother experience without creating bad experiences for newbies and veterans alike when a new player joins at the lowest allowed level with trash gear and no knowledge of the dungeon.

Alternatively, LFT could just be turned into a LFG posting board of player class/level for someone with initiative to make a group out of.

I completely agree with the topic. Almost universally minimum-levels on LFT should be raised by 1 (sometimes 2).

My prime examples: Queuing at 17 against VC (24) in Deadmines. Queuing at 50 against Doomcaller (57) in HFQ. Queuing at 55 against Wyrmthalak (60) in LBRS.

I think LFT should do 2 things:
 1) Create a viable group that has a serious shot against the final boss.
Maybe dungeons weren't intended to be run that way, but the vast majority of LFT users has the expectation that their group will at least attempt the final boss.
What viability does someone at the lowest level bring to the group? Maybe they make the queue pop faster? But if it's the tank or the healer I would argue 95% of runs are doomed anyway. And for dps their utility greatly diminishes due to the level difference just when the dungeon gets hard (in the last third).

 2) Facilitate an enjoyable run.
Who is ever happy when a min-level character joins? Sometimes it's selfish, but more often it's probably just people not knowing better and queuing for whatever LFT makes available to them (which means the addon creates the problem in the first place). So if the best-case means 1 out of 5 people benefit or gain enjoyment while the others suffer for it, and worst-case means nobody benefits, maybe it shouldn't be offered?
Of course you can compensate for low level (to a degree), but the higher aggro-range, lower output, longer regen times usually suck the fun out of it after a while or lead to wipes.

And I also agree that the instances themselves should not be restricted and it should remain possible to find groups for them at lower levels outside of LFT. If the group agrees to take you with it's all well, with LFT you just don't really get a choice (I've seen some tanks kick min-lvl players from LFT groups which always leads to ugly drama).
