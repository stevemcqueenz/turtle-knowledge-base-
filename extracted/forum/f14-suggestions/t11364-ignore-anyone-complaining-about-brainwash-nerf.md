"Specs mean something" - Choosing talents (aka spec) was meant to be carefully considered. This was very much the Vanilla ethos and became nonexistent in later expansions. The game encouraged you to commit to a talent loadout by having increasingly expensive retraining costs. This gold sink/deterrent resulted in people typically sticking to their one talent loadout which gave them an identity within the community. A basic example would be somebody know for being a tank or healer. It also meant that your character has their strengths and weaknesses and couldn't just freely adapt (talent wise) to any situation. As such, you potentially had to link up with other people to tackle particular problems.

TWoW went against this ethos a bit by introducing the Brain Washing Device which, effectively, allows for dual spec but at a cost of 350g (or real money) up front and 5g for subsequent uses. To put it into perspective, if someone was creating a new server and told people that respeccing was going to be 10% of the original Vanilla cost, I think people would've bitten their hand off! Furthermore, I think people are also ignoring the fact that you can use this device **anywhere**! That is ridiculously convenient and easily worth the 5g.

> **Kastok wrote: Wed Nov 01, 2023 2:09 pm**
> Please someone tell me, what does restricting how much someone can play or experiment with their class give them meaning.

As I mentioned in a previous post, I'm torn on this because respeccing costs absolutely dissuaded me from experimenting with talents in Vanilla/Classic/SoM. It's the downside of this system. But to put it into perspective, you know what would've encourage me to experiment? A device which allows me to change to another talent loadout for **only 5g and at any location**!

As far as I'm concerned, this is an amazing tool and the only reason this 'fix' is being received so poorly is because the community had become accustomed to using it in an unintended way which had unrivaled levels of convenience. However, as per others' suggestions, I do think the device should come with additional talent loadout slots, possibly unlockable like bank bag slots.

What matters to the player is the relative power baseline in the moment, in every game there is a huge resistance to exploits being fixed, but it is up to the devs to raise above and preserve coherent world building, character building, appropiate challenge level and immersion. The player is not in in the position to do so as he is hooked on his current power fantasy, and fails to understand that it is a relative thing.
Same way you feel more or less equally powerful against your level of opponents at any given level, it is the illusion of power progression inherent to RPG design.

Once the exploit is fixed, after a little bit of complaining people will accept the new current reality, it'll become the new "the way things are", and once they play with that power level, if someone proposed to make respeccing free, or abilities learned automatically when levelled up or mobs not being able to crit, or flight paths working like teleports, items swappable in combat, infinite inventory etc.. they would be able to see why that is wrong and how it would make the game worse.. especially on a server focusing on immersion and solid world building. Tell them "No."  It's that easy.  It's a video game guild, not your workplace.

Lowering the maximum re-specialize cost to 5 gold was probably a mistake in hindsight.  The game was set up and designed with the idea that talent builds should be a fairly significant commitment.  Dual-spec was effectively a band-aid thrown to tank/healer players for PvP because many of those PvE builds are ineffective in PvP and it was easier to allow for them to re-spec than it was to fix the game properly so those types of roles had better PvP functionality.  Being able to change to any build or role you want on-the-fly is obviously outside the game's intended design and renders the notion of talent trees virtually superfluous:  If players can do that, then the trees ought to be removed entirely because they no longer serve any real purpose.
