I don't think respawn timers need to be touched at all. What would be a better solution to this would be to "unpool" nodes. If you don't know what that means, it means there is a minimum and maximum number of nodes which can be active at a time in a zone, and 100% of the possible node spots for a zone will not ever be occupied. Unpooling would mean that each node would respawn independently regardless of other nodes around the zone, and thus there would be a greater abundance of nodes at all times.

Whether this is a good idea or not has to be considered in terms of peak times versus lull times, and if there will be situations where 1 or 2 players have an obscene amount of resources available to them when much fewer people are playing.
