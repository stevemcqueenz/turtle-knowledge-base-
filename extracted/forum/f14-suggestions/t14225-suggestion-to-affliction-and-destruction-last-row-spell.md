# Suggestion to Affliction and Destruction Last row Spell

## Post 96995 by Ridersnitsuga — 2024-06-14T10:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96995#p96995 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Here I am again to make a sugesstion for a change of spells that no one asked for, but i'll still make it cuz I like discusing these kind of things.

So we all know that "Conflagrate" is kinda underwhelming as a last row talent for Destruction Warlocks, and "Dark Pact" for Affliction is borderline useless, so I propose this:

 **Conflagrate**: Make it consume "Inmolate", but **make their reminder DoT damage into a different DoT**, making them having the ability to put 2 Fire DoTs that interacts with their other Fire Talents on the tree, or you can even make it consume both "Inmolate" and "Shadowburn" into a big DoT with their reminder DoT damage.  (Yes, I stole later expansions "Combustion" idea, but hey, it could be interesting) (I also know this is a Nightmare to code, and maybe balance, so yeah)

 **Dark Pact**: **Make "Agony" no longer a Curse**, seems like a simple change, and sounds easy to implement (Tough I don't know exactly, cuz im not a programmer), but it will bring a lot of damage to their tree by this change only. You could also give them something extra like... (Affliction DoTs can now Critically strike per tick) or... I don't know ( x% more damage to their Afflictions DoT) (Again, sounds like a nightmare to code)

For Demonology, I still have no idea what could they make for them, and I'm not smart enough to figure it out, but maybe make "Soul link" be linked to "Master Demonologist" on the 6th row, instead of "Demonic Sacrifice" on the 5th row, since... ya know, you need your demon alive for Soul Link to actually work. (Not an actual fix for anything, it just been bugging me)

The objective of this is not to kill the "Lol, Shadowbolt spam" meta, but to make a dent to it, giving them the option to go with it, or to make Shadowbolt a legit filler instead of the "Go to" damage spell.

Let me know what you all think about it.

Of course, these suggestions has not taken into account the PvP aspect, mostly cuz i have no interest on it, but also because balancing something purely for the PvP area sounds annoying, but will still read changes regarding it.
