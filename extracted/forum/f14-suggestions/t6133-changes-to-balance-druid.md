# Changes to Balance Druid

## Post 38511 by Vikare — 2023-03-21T23:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38511#p38511 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As one of the vaunted meme specs, balance druid is pretty notorious for lacking mana (OOMkin), lacking damage, and lacking support. While I doubt we're going to see class changes again by the development team for a long time... I do believe that this is the moment to push for quality of life changes that don't necessarily involve sweeping changing to classes themselves. What does that mean? Well, the things that every mainline caster has that balance druid does not (and elemental shaman by extension.) Enchants, consumables, and not having a 1-minute cooldown tied to the only AoE it has access to.

I've played Balance for almost a year now on this server through all of the raids. The previous update helped out the mana efficiency of boomkin and alleviated a sliver of the 0-16% hit issue; however, it is still a very poor specialization at this time and will remain so without help. Here are some extremely simple and low effort things that can assist with that **in the short term:**

- Enchant Gloves - Arcane Power
Permanently enchant gloves to increase arcane damage by up to 20.
Nexus Crystal (3), Large Brilliant Shard (10), Dream Dust (6)

- Elixir of Arcane Power
Increases spell arcane damage by up to 40 for 1 hour.
Purple Lotus (3), Crystal Vial (1)

- Mark of Elune
Classes: Druid
Requires Level 60
Use: Permanently adds 18 to all healing and damage spells and 1% chance to hit with spells to a leg or head slot item. Does not stack with other enchantments for the selected equipment slot.
That might seem like a lot of power to give to druids. 96 spellpower and 2% hit? Wtf? Well, unfortunately, this is just to bring balance up to speed with what most other casters already have in enchant/consumable additions. They have frost, fire, and shadow enchants and elixirs of the same fashion. They don't have to use Librams of Focus from Dire Maul for a whopping 8 spellpower on head/leg items, as they have access to far superior ZG ones. As a first step to help meme specs, this should be it. It's easily replicable for another spec in the dumps like Elemental shaman, too.

Another item that can help balance druids is access to raid-quality relics, ideally ones that aren't total garbage. At the moment there are:

- (3) Restoration Idols
- (3) Feral Idols
- (1) Worthless +2 minutes to Thorns idol
- (1) broken Balance Idol
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/658458602633429017/1087869497223938178/image.png)

That Idol of the Moon for balance increases the damage of moonfire by 17%, a (broken, doesn't scale) relatively small amount for boomkin dps. Yet, that's perhaps ok, as it's a level 58-60 world drop. With no upgrades over it in any of the three tiers of raiding, however, that becomes an issue. Adding a new idol that merely does something like "reduce the cast time of Starfire by 0.1s" also would not help. Most non-healer relics in vanilla are either trash or very powerful (bear druid idol dramatically reduces the rage cost of their 2 primary abilities, and cat druid's powershifting idol gives them nigh-infinite mana). Why not add a couple balance relics? One to the existing raids. One to Emerald Sanctum---the druid raid. In the DB there already exists a totem for enhance shamans that gives a 111 attack power proc effect from casting shock spells, and prot paladins get additional charges to holy shield. Therefore, the door seems pretty open to more interesting relics akin to what we see in TBC and WotLK (where we have 70-some examples to draw from for druid alone. Here are a few moonkin relic examples from future expansions:

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/658458602633429017/1087866508232892507/idols.png)

All of these are good examples of ways to bolster moonkin either by raw power, by supporting their entire party, or by rewarding a balance druid for keeping their DoTs constantly applied. Ideally, whatever comes is an upgrade over the existing random drop blue relic. Numbers are easily changed, and effects can be modified as necessary. The point is there are many readily available examples to draw from and give viable gearing options without changing the class itself yet.

Enchants, consumables, and relics. These three things that do not require changes to the class can be done easily and far ahead of any future class update we're getting in the future. Please help your turtle druids!

 **Now, as for the long-term**, there are a few common sentiments for what the current Turtle WoW boomkin needs:

First and foremost: an improved moonkin aura. 3% crit is far too little to be more valuable for a caster group than simply adding another mage or warlock. TBC increased it to 5—certainly a viable option for us, and a mere number change. Perhaps a more elegant solution would be to introduce a scaling moonkin aura instead. At the low-end for a fresh 60 balance druid they can have the existing 3%. The more critical strike you get can then increases the aura as high as 6% at the level of BiS Naxx gear. One of the reasons world buffs were removed was to pave the way for future class changes and boss design where casters don't have +20% spell crit from world buffs. That's fantastic, but now casters are crit anemic, and the first thing you can do to help that is to make the Boomer Support give *useful* amounts of crit to their party. With that, raids might actually desire 1-2 balance druids for their caster core groups in the same way that shadow priests are now desired as mana batteries for healers and otherwise. This will also create an incentive to direct spell crit gear to boomies, as it will benefit the entire caster party as a whole for the more crit they obtain. One small caveat to this crit prioritization is that at the moment, the more crit a boomkin has means they faster they oom. Each crit procs Nature's Grace, which reduces the next cast by 0.5s. That means whenever this change is implemented, a likewise QoL buff to moonkin mana sustainability will be required.

As for the gameplay of the spec itself? Right now a balance druid spams starfire and moonfire. Pure arcane damage empowered by Curse of Shadows. Wrath and Insect Swarm are only used when you need to be moving or there's arcane reflect or resistance. Simply buffing wrath and insect swarm to be better than starfire and moonfire would just create the opposite problem. What's needed is a reason to weave between both arcane and nature. Earth and Moon. Casting into one rewards the other. Additionally, to fit the critical strike focus, their crit damage should be increased as well. Focusing on the all-in high damage RNG "booms" that boomkin is meant to have. The negative punishment for misplay being going over threat cap and killing yourself.

Naturally, for any nature spells to be used at all there will need to be a change to existing curses or a new ability. I don't think anything new is necessary, though. Druids are infamous for constantly combatting against the Emerald Nightmare within the Dream. So simply adding nature to a warlock's Curse of Shadows fits well enough to the game's lore. The Nightmare is shadow within the nature of the Emerald Dream. Splitting Nature and Arcane between Shadows and Elements curses would harm balance druids far too much. This would also be a tremendous buff to elemental shamans, obviously.

- Curse of Shadow	Rank 2
 200 Mana	         30 yd range

Curses the target for 5 min, reducing Shadow, Nature, and Arcane resistances by 75 and increasing Shadow, Nature, and Arcane damage taken by 10%.  Only one Curse per Warlock can be active on any one target.
Thank you for reading. Feel free to comment and add/edit to my suggestions.  smiling_turtle
