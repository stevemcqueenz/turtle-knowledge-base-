## Post 127339 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-18T13:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127339#p127339 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are buffs and items that allow you to use your spirit based manareg as a paladin. So the stat isnt dead if you choose to utilize it.

It also is a nice stat to have for general gameplay as it allows you to regen more in between pulls etc. Cutting down a bit on the obnoxious drinking. In that sense its a stat thats nice to have and that can be liberally plastered on non druid/priestgear without making the itempieces more disgustingly strong.

Would intellect be better? Yes, but that misses the point. If we design every item to be minmaxed, we might as well go the retailroute where every item is just its itemlevel. I think this is a great way they chose to keep some really good gear balanced while giving paladins the option to experiment with meditation if they so choose

There are plenty of good gearpieces for regular playstile healpala. If the new set encourages some experimentstion with meditation im all for it
