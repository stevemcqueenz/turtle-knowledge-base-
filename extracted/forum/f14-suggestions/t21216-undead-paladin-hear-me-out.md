# Undead Paladin - hear me out

## Post 147318 by berdal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-20T16:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147318#p147318 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know this is a sensitive topic, and I have mixed feelings about it because of lore reasons.

This is my suggestion:
Undead Paladin can be unlocked (only because of cross-faction) by the following "ritual".
 1) Play HC Human Paladin to level 60.
 2) Enter Inferno mode.
 3) Obtain Corrupted Ashbringer
 4) Die to Kel'Thuzad while wearing [Corrupted Ashbringer] as Holy-specced (having the Daybreak talent).
 5) Undead Paladin is unlocked, but can only pick talents from the Retribution tree. (could call the tree "Unholy" instead of "Retribution") Or instead of only allowing Retribution talent, could instead prohibit Holy talents.
 6) Another unique thing could be if all Blessings were swapped for "Maledictions". They could be non-OP single target offensive spells.

Malediction of Weakness - reduce attack power by <half of BoM> for 2 min.
Maledictions of Imprudence - reduce MP5 by <half of BoW> for 2 min.
... etc.

Of course I know this is far out there. This is just the beginning of a fun idea (although perhaps a tad overly complicated), but could be cleaned up for instance by not requiring dying as Holy-specced, and not swapping Blessings for Maledictions.

EDIT: Could [Runeblade of Baron Rivendare] also play a role in the prerequisites?
