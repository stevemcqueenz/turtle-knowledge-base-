# What is the direction of this server?

## Post 21064 by Jongyi (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-04T14:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21064#p21064 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Is Turtle Devs trying to become Tauri Evermoon (PVE) or do they want to become Stormforge  Mistblade(PVP)? They can't be both and it is impossible to please both sides of playerbase.
Population maybe increasing, but sense of community itself is collapsing quickly with influx of gankers, griefers and corpse campers.
Non-warmode ppl are also affected badly as gankers killing quest objective npcs and camping the escort npc. You can sense the increasing the hostility in the air by looking at World Chat and Newcomer Chat. Toxicity is becoming rampart and "Turtle Community is friendly" quote is slowly becoming a joke.
PVPers insulting and belittling RP PVER community in the chat is also becoming rampart. Recent changes catering to PVPers also alienate RP PVE community which most of them are OG players.
Devs should be open about their direction and become complete PVP server like Evermoon if they want to.
I love Turtle and I see many redflags pointing which will hurt the server's community in the long term

## Post 21071 by Proudwell — 2022-08-04T15:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21071#p21071 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think there may be some disagreement with what I say here, but I think that the PvP/PvE split is okay. The real community split is with HC/non-HC, since they literally split the community for a massive chunk of the game.
While I think HC players should have every right to play HC, I have a personal concern with people being (mostly) mechanically split from the other side of the community in a game that relies a lot on cooperation. Thankfully HC can cooperate together, but they're still in a different box from everyone else.
There's also some talk about HC players stopping at 59 to keep the challenge going, so that's a group of people (however large or small) that are permanently outside of the non-HC pool.
