# Make Alterac Valley an Endless Battleground!

## Post 157804 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-30T11:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157804#p157804 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi y'all!

The reason I think it’s important to redesign Alterac Valley is because right now it’s just a mindless grind. And while it happens otherwise, usually everyone rushes to capture a few towers, kill a lieutenant or two, and then zerg the end boss for a big chunk of Honor. The most “efficient” way to play AV is to avoid all PvP, ride straight to the enemy base, and treat the whole thing like a dull PvE instance. That’s not a battleground—it’s a broken loop that kills the spirit of large-scale warfare. This idea is meant to fix that.

Alterac Valley should be a living war, one that never truly ends. Players should be able to join and leave anytime, with the fight constantly shifting and evolving.

 ---

Core Mechanics
Each faction fights to hold and expand territory.
Defending towers keeps the five-minute Honor reward high. Guarding key NPCs maintains your faction’s overall strength. Capturing towers flips them to your side, spawning your faction’s NPCs in defense. Killing enemy NPCs opens space for your own to spawn, allowing your team to take strongholds and push the frontline forward.

 ---

Dynamic Events
Faction leaders should regularly march out with their troops to join the battlefield. Defeating one grants major Honor and could trigger a server-wide yellow announcement to fire up faction pride. Summoned bosses could shift the entire balance of the war, giving meaning to player coordination and timing.

When a faction pushes all the way to the enemy base, the conflict becomes a full-scale siege. Players could climb walls, breach defenses, and fight inside the fortress itself—a true culmination of the ongoing war.

 ---

Persistence and Balance
The war should never fully end. Important NPCs would respawn or teleport back to their bases after defeat, keeping the fight alive. Strategic teleport points could let teams send strike groups to reclaim territory or flank the enemy.

Players could turn in quests and loot to strengthen their faction’s troops and regain lost ground. The deeper one faction pushes into enemy territory, the better the loot they drop—helping balance momentum and prevent one-sided domination.

This design would transform Alterac Valley from a dull, one-run grind into what it was meant to be: a living, breathing front line of war, pride, and strategy.
