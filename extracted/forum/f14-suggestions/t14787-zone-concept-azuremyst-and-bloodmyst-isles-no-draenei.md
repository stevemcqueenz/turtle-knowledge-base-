# Zone Concept: Azuremyst and Bloodmyst Isles (No Draenei)

## Post 100741 by Azuriell — 2024-09-05T18:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100741#p100741 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Introduction:
This proposal aims to revitalize the Azuremyst and Bloodmyst Isles, offering players a fresh and engaging experience set in a familiar location. By introducing new storylines, characters, and challenges, we can create a compelling narrative that complements the existing World of Warcraft lore of Turtle WoW.

Zone Overhaul:
Level Range: 28-36
Visual Aesthetic: Maintain the original aesthetic while removing Draenei elements. The focus will be on the mysterious, resonating crystals that dominate the landscape.
Faction-Specific: Azuremyst Isle will be Horde-only, while Bloodmyst Isle will remain Alliance-only.

Storyline:

Azuremyst Isle
Horde Questline: Chief Engineer Chizbolt Copperplug leads a goblin expedition to Azuremyst Isle, drawn by the promise of riches.
Stillpine Tribe: Players will assist the peaceful Stillpine Furbolgs, to use the crystals as a shamanistic tool agains the dangers of the Naga.
Naga Threat: The Naga have established a presence on the island, guarding ancient crystal deposits. Players will confront them in various encounters, culminating in a battle against a powerful Naga matriarch.
Crystal Mining: The Venture Co. has arrived on the island with nefarious intentions. Players will uncover their plan to exploit the crystals and work to thwart their efforts.
Ammen Vale: In this peaceful valley, players will encounter a friendly Moonkin tribe who are battling Black Dragon whelps. Helping the Moonkin will reward players with powerful artifacts and knowledge about the crystals.
Azure Watch: A Tauren hunter, Wulfgar Freebird, and his Orc companion, Jimboscar Southsnow, have established a hunting camp with a few goblin dynamite anglers. Players will assist them tracking down dangerous creatures of both isles, earning the respect of the hunting community by becoming a "Big game hunter of the isles clouded in the mist".

Bloodmyst Isle
Alliance Questline: High Elf Winten Firesky leads an expedition to Bloodmyst Isle, seeking alternative sources of magic.
Blood Elf and Naga Alliance: The Blood Elves, corrupting crystals with fel energy, have allied themselfs with the Naga to control the island. Players will face off against these formidable foes, liberating captured Alliance soldiers and rescuing survivors.
Cenarion Circle: The Cenarion Circle has established a presence on the island, working to heal the corrupted land. Players will assist them in purifying the environment and restoring balance.
Firesky's Expedition: Players will search for the missing members of Firesky's expedition, uncovering clues about their fate and the secrets of the red crystals.
Explorer's League: A group of adventurous scholars, the Explorer's League, has arrived on the island to investigate the crystals' origins. Players will assist them in their research, uncovering ancient artifacts on both isles and unraveling the mysteries of the past.

Dungeon: Crystalsong Caverns

Dual Entrances: One in Azuremyst's crystal mountains (at the previous place of Exodar), the other in Bloodmyst's "Vector Coil" area.
Shared Goal: Both factions will work together to stop the Venture Co. and Blood Elf/Naga influence within the caverns underneath the isles and try to stop what they put already into motion.
Crystal Exploration: Players will navigate through a labyrinth of crystal-filled chambers, encountering various environmental hazards and powerful crystal guardians.
Final Boss: A crystal giant corrupted by the blood elf fel energy, once a guardian of the Elemental Plane of Earth, is now the last thing that the players need to evercome to get to the origin core of the mysterious crystals of these two islands. Still a small but unstable cracked rift right into Deepholm! Thats why everywhere are resonating crystals full of nature magic spreding like wildfire...
Players will need to defeat the giant and seal the rift to prevent catastrophic consequences.

Conclusion
This concept offers a fresh and engaging experience. By combining familiar elements with new storylines, characters, and challenges, we can create a memorable adventure that expands upon the rich lore of World of Warcraft.
satisfied_turtle
