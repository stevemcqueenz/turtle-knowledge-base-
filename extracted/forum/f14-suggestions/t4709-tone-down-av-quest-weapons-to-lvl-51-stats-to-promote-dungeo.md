# Tone down AV quest weapons to lvl 51 stats to promote dungeon loot

## Post 28158 by Massie2 — 2022-10-30T20:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28158#p28158 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This may be a hot take because Ice Barbed Spear is such a staple for melees in vanilla, but as a Warrior main myself I find that the rewards from the easy lvl 51 Alterac Valley quest kind of spoil all weapon drops from lvl 51 to 60.
Because you can essentially get a level 58 weapon with insane stats at lvl 51, it completely invalidates the dozens of weapons you can find inside dungeons, from elite quests, and crafted weapons for that level range.

Your weapon is arguably the most important item for a melee, which is why weapon drops during leveling always feel really hype and rewarding. But considering lvl 52-60 is the range where people start farming their pre-bis and run endgame dungeons, it's really sad that 90% of the weapons aren't even worth looking at because they're simply too low level and as such have poor weapon damage compared to IBS.

So my debatable suggestion is to lower the itemlevel of the rewards to that of a lvl 51 item, considering that is the minimum level requirement for the quest, thus bringing a lot of dungeon loot back on the table.

It's a little heartbreaking seeing 3 arms warriors in your party with an IBS in their hand, all rolling greed on a weapon they can't even wear yet because they're too low level, simply because IBS is stronger anyway.
