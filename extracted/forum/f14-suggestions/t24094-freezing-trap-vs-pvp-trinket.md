# Freezing trap vs PVP Trinket

## Post 169413 by Tortusenjoyer — 2026-02-26T05:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169413#p169413 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

In vanilla and classic WoW, no pvp trinket removed hunter freezing trap. In twow, this was changed so that all pvp trinkets can remove hunter trap with a 3m cooldown. This means in practice that if a hunter manages to get someone into a freezing trap, there is an 89% chance they're going to self dispel it.

This would be ok if twow had given hunters some tools to help put distance between melee users.  If a hunter gets jumped by a warrior/rogue/shaman, and they put a slow on you, there isn't much natural counterplay left if they can remove your trap.

Suggestion: 1. Remove hunter trap from the list of things PVP trinket will dispel, as it was in vanilla and classic. 2. give ranged hunters more options to create distance once melee are on top of them. Wotlk disengage, for example. Or remove the 3s cooldown on wingclip but keep improved wingclip proc rate at 40%. Or something else
