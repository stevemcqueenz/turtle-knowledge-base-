# Make the Karazhan Crypts trinket reward identical for both factions

## Post 119888 by GR3ddy — 2025-02-13T22:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119888#p119888 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The trinkets that are given out as one of the rewards for finishing the Kara Crypts quest chain have been reworked which is a good thing.

The current issue is that the Horde version of the trinket is not very good itemized compared to the Alliance one.

The Alliance version gives +1 Spell hit +1 Spell haste and 15 Spell power while the Horde one gives +1 Spell crit +1 Spell haste and 15 Spell power.

The alliance version is very good itemized for Affliction locks who desperately need some good items.

My suggestion is to make them identical because the current horde version isn't really good for any dps caster class, the only spec that benefits from haste is Affliction warlock but spell crit is dead stat for them for the most part.

Thank you for the consideration.
