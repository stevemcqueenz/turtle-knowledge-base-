## Post 152161 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-18T02:02:00Z

I think the Caverns of Time area and lore are being wasted quite a bit. I think something could be done related to it since we have Black Morass.

Ideas/Suggestions:

 - The Purge of Stratholme
 - The Escape from Durnholde
 - The Battle for Mount Hyjal
 - The Fall of Quel'thalas
 - The Defense of Hearthglen

These are all important events that can be explored, expanded upon, and explained in more detail through a dungeon. The Caverns of Time are a great tool for shaping the underexplored or underexplained lore in Warcraft III.

I agree, that we could move away a bit from WC3 and step Back To WC1&2.
So therefor i'd Like To exclude Durnholde/Stratholme/Hyjal from CoT, which would propably Copy/Paste from Blizzards CoT Dungeons.

If i Had do create a Personal Wish list regarding CoT Dungeons:

 1.) The Hunt for Gul'dan (WarCraft II)

This Takes place when Gul'dan betrayed the Horde and took the Stormreaver and Twilights Hammer Clan To search for the Tomb of Sargeras.
Disguised AS members of the Black Tooth Grin Clan under Maim and Rend Blackhand we could Fight through multiple groups of Orcs, Ogre Magi, Death Knights and Demons.
Here's a little Lore Stretch though: does anyone know If (until VanilaWoW) it's known, If Cho'gall survived? Or is His survival/reappearance in Cataclysm a retcon? If it's a retcon, He could be a Boss in there To kill. Gul'dan maybe even the Endboss, since the Infinite Dragonflight trys To Safe him?

 2.) Downfall of a Guardian (WarCraft - Orcs & Humans)

After Reading the book "The Last Guardian" again, i'm again kinda disappointed how Karazhan Turned Out To be, since It was described as a very confusing place, which was, when Lothar and Khadgar were hunting for Medih, even more twisted and turned.
For this one the OLD Karazhan Tower from the Alpha could be used. It could well be, that this place of Magic can be twisted, turned and Changed by its master. So that could be an explanation for the different Look of this place.
Again: the Infinite Dragonflight could prevent History from turning Out as it should be.

 3.) The Destruction of the Dark Portal (WarCraft II - Beyond the Dark Portal)

This one could be interesting, since i'm Not talking about the First, but second Destruction of the Portal by Medivh. We'd be on Draenor (Not yet outland.. but it's as good as outland already, since Draenor'e a dying World) and defend Khadgar with the Old Heroes Danath Trollbane, Kurdran Wildhammer, Turalyion and Alleria Windrunner from multiple waves of Orcs, Ogres, Dragons and everything the old Horde Had To offer, before Khadgar Manages To Close the Portal. It might give us the opportunity To See old and Forgotten Orc Heroes, who's fate's unknown after WarCraft II, like Den'targ or Kargath Bladefist.
And of Course they Infinite Dragonflight again WHO also tries To prevent the Portals Destruction.
(Why should they prevent it's Destruction Here? After they failed To prevent the opening, and History continued, they try To prevent IT at some Other Point. Their maingoal is it To disrupt the Timeline at certain Points, To cause alternative outcomes, that would cause Chaos for Azeroth.)

If they would have prevented the opening: the Alliance propably would have never Formed. Thrall would've never been raised by Humans, the pact between Alliance and Horde against the Legion would've never happened.

If they would've prevented the Destruction of the Portal (at that certain Point):
Nerzhul was creating multiple Portals and Draenor was a dying World, about To be torn apart by the Energies of the many Portals. Azeroth too would have suffered from this through the Portal and the remainder of the Old Horde could've propably invaded Azeroth a 3rd and final time, leaving this world crippled for decades.
