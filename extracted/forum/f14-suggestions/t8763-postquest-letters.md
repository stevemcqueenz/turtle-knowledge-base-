Recently, I came across this quest in Auberdine in which a fisher dwarf asks you to fish some Groupers. I thought it was really cool and it put a smile on my face when I saw he sent me a letter thanking me for completing his quest.   crying_turtle

I think it would be cool to have some random (and very few) quest givers to send you a letter after completing their quests! I'm not sure if these could be set on a timer or just upon logging off and in, but I think it'd be a very cool feature.
