# Change Enlighten to proc off of Heals.

## Post 103547 by Dkmoonlilith — 2024-10-18T05:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103547#p103547 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Much of the gripe I've seen on Discord and in forums about Priest changes has been about the removal of Power Infusion (PI) from the priest toolkit and the overspecialization of Disc into damage. This change to the Disc 21 capstone has turned priest healers from a utility + healing class (true support) into a pure healing class. The catch is that no change was made to the comparatively mediocre Holy 31 capstone Proclaim Champion, which becomes even more irrelevant with the changes made to Fear Ward. The result is that there's still no incentive to invest more than 30 points in Holy, *and* the very beneficial 21 point Disc investment is lost.

An easy solution to this would be to allow the new 21 Disc capstone Enlighten to proc off healing *and* damaging Discipline or Holy spells, instead of solely off of damaging spells. This would be in line with sacrificing some of the Holy talents for support that was previously available to Priests prior to CC2 and would negate many of the complaints the developers and admins here at TurtleWoW have been receiving.

Devs have argued and might continue to argue that few changes were made to Holy tree because it was in a good place; however, I would argue that it was in a good place *because of PI*.

Alternatively, if avoiding Disc investment is the goal, then consider revamping the 31 Holy capstone to something more useful or buffing Proclaim Champion to incentivize deep Holy investment. All other healing specs in other classes get something at 31 points that feels truly powerful (Daybreak, Spirit Link, Tree form), I'm unsure why Holy wouldn't.

The alternative is that priest healing just generally feels bad, hence the doomer chat in priest channel on disc.

Not trying to harass devs or make anyone feel bad, I just wanted to formally suggest this change.
