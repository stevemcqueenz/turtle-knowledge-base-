# The Big Problem With Cosmetic Illusions

## Post 99831 by Overtyped — 2024-07-18T22:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99831#p99831 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It would be nice if you could mount up while in the cosmetic illusion form.

And instead of mounting on a horse, you would just stay in the illusion, and move quicker.
This would allow you to stay in illusion form instead of constantly having to switch in and out, and it wouldn't require any extra animations.

This unnecessary drawback is probably one of the reasons most people don't use illusions. Taurens already have plainsrunning, which would look similar, so it's not too strange.
