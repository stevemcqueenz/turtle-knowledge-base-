# Make Stormwind and Orgrimmar no longer No. 1 cities

## Post 169373 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-25T07:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169373#p169373 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Using Stormwind as an example at first, its status as the de facto and only primary city of the Alliance faction started in post-classic expansions. In late-Classic period in around late 2006, the connected auction houses within each faction were online first time. Before and even a long time after this update, Ironforge was the factual primary city of the Alliance because the only Alliance auction house was in Ironforge since version 0.9 (only vague information found). In TBC and WotLK, Shattrath and Dalaran had all facilities players need, so Stormwind and Orgrimmar became less crowded.

My suggestion is simple - encourage players to gather in other major cities like Darnassus and Thunerbluff by adding and balancing facilities and functions in need, such as transmog NPCs, hairdressers and buffs similar to the dragonslayer. Doing so not only alleviates the network pressure in an over-crowded city but also improves the status of other cities. Players' sense of belonging as a fantasy race is also elevated this way.

Supplementary: The sense of belonging of being fantasy races can be boosted by implementing a number of features like race-exclusive weekly quests, for PVE and PVP purposes. In this way, a dwarf player, who takes IF a city more intimate to them, would feel that SW is a more exotic one resided in by human players and NPCs. Human race players could also say words like "Welcome to Stormwind" in a more natural and genuine way because non-human races would be rarer in SW.

Final Fantasy 14 has an interesting system where in cities that are not experiencing high traffic from their side of the auction house, sellers are given a discounted rate for putting up things up for auction through that city's auctioneers. This encouraged players who have things to sell on the AH to move to cities that are experiencing low traffic in order to take advantage of the lower rate.

Might be difficult to implement such things in TWoW but it's worth noting for brainstorming ideas.

Another thing I noticed in that game is that all three major cities are directly connected to each other as opposed to the connectivity between cities of the Alliance and Horde.
In the case of the Alliance, Stormwind connects to both Ironforge and Darnassus but the latter two only to Stormwind.
In the Horde's case, it's Orgrimmar that is the transport hub.
This convenience of travel ultimately contributes to players preferring to make those two cities their go-to.

How about this for an idea?

Every week each city will feature a weeklong event of sorts. Let's use the endgame raid player as an example.

This week, Stormwind have a bounty for killing Karazhan bosses where for every boss you have slain, you get a buff of 1% discount for all goods and services in Stormwind ONLY for the rest of the week before raid reset. This buff is stackable and is capped at the maximum number of bosses of that specific raid.

The next week, Ironforge will have a bounty for Naxxramas and the process repeats. Either one or all cities of respective factions may have a bounty depending on what's best for traffic balancing.

A similar process can be done for levelling players and zones. If say for example Wetlands isn't experiencing much traffic or popularity, there is a month-long event in Ironforge where mobs in that zone and its corresponding dungeons drop certain tokens that can be exchange with an NPC in Ironforge for random items/buffs/consumables - stuff like that.
