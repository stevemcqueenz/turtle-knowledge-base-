## Post 29053 by Rokit (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-12T19:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29053#p29053 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You will likely not find a large amount of support for Achievements here.

They are inherently intangible and abstract. A lot of what people enjoy about vanilla wow (by association TurtleWoW) is the tangible and grounded aspects of rewards and content. So achievements as we saw in 3.0 or later are in direct opposition to what we already have, quests and rewards.

For every achievement, there is instead a quest, reward and title that would fit that aspect more.
