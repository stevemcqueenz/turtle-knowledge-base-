# Idea to replace MCP

## Post 48243 by Jammyxx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-31T13:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48243#p48243 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I was thinking of an approach to fix Feral cats having to use MCP, would be to buff the custom Turtle wow talent "Blood Frenzy"

Currently 2/2 points does 12% attack speed - While good I don't think it's enough.

My suggestion is to buff this to 15/30%.

This will then match Warriors and Shamans Flurry.

Currently Blood Frenzy buff stays up when you shift out of Cat Form, I'd also suggest letting the Tiger's Fury buff stay up while in caster form also, but removed when going into Bear (like Berserk is) so when you're forced into Bear in PvP which is most of the time there will be a drawback to it.

I'd also consider buffing baseline Tiger's Fury as the standard one is nearly useless at 6 sec.

Requires 30 Energy - 6 Sec Duration (baseline)
to
Requires 20 Energy - 10 Sec Duration (baseline)

So in the end you will have a 16sec +40dmg and 30% atk speed buff for 20 energy... Both buffs wil persist through powershifting.

I think these small changes along with some more Feral Attack Power weps coming next patch will really help.

Obviously MCP would have to be nerfed, maybe to 30% or don't let Blood Frenzy stack with it.

If you want to be less reliant on mcp druids need to scale poorly from haste:

Bear maul threat is so good because all its damage comes from your attack power + some bonus and multiplied by some coefficient.
Nerfing to work with your "feral invisible claw weapon" + bonus (increased bonus by ap) kinda like heroic strike makes maul quite bad for threat generation, which can be fixed by giving the weapon part of threat a very good fixated threat value, so either bear tanks produce excellent threat from the beggining (and encourage ppl to go mit) or they stop tanking altogether.
Another option is to nerf maul threat and make savage bite scale insanely good and have insane threat multiplier

For cats it's easier: punish them for having any form of haste.
Give them a talent, every time claw or shred crits the target their next 3 attacks or special abilities in cat form do 30% more damage. You want those 3 attacks to be 3 shreds and by no means you want faster auto attacks consuming those charges.

But hey, it's just an idea
