## Post 67742 by Crunshy1512 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-30T11:34:00Z

Implement tier sets for different specs.

Druid and paladin for example.

Tier 1 2 and 3 having a different set depending on the spec, tank more defense/hp and set  bonus that compliments class imbalance from talents such as paladin mana regen or threat. (Same for druid) And crit and hit for dps and bonuses that compliments imbalance from talents such as mana regen for ret, or bleed scaling for cat. or (energy regen)

Easy fix for many classes, and brings them on par with other dps/tank/healer classes that has a full set that compliments them.
