I’d like to suggest a new optional leveling challenge focused on PvP progression.

![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) Core Idea
While this challenge is active:
![❌](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/274c.svg) No experience from quests
![❌](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/274c.svg) No XP from PvE mob kills

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) XP gained through:
Open world PvP kills (x5 XP multiplier)
Battlegrounds
Dedicated PvP events or objectives

Why This Works
Encourages world PvP naturally while leveling
Revives contested zones
Offers a true alternative progression path
Fully optional & opt-in, with no impact on PvE players
Identity & Rewards

Unique title: “The Warlord”
Cosmetic rewards (tabard, banner, visuals)
