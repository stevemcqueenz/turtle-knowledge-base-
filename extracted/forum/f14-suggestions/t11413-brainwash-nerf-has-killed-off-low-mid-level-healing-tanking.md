> **Foobs wrote: Thu Nov 02, 2023 4:01 pm**
> Why have a heal or prot specc if your not going to use it??

Why?  Because WoW was built more or less as "Everquest with the sucky parts taken out."  The older game was almost entirely PvE.  WoW always had PvP as a core of its philosophy--arguably even moreso than alpha than what it launched with--but its class design is very much derived from PvE games.  The result is you end up with class roles that are desired or necessary (depending on what you're doing) for PvE but which fare only poorly in PvP battles.  What a shocker, they're not very popular on the PvP realm.

There's probably no solution to that issue that remains "classic like" in all ways because this is one area where the original game is outright flawed.  Either you keep the flaw or you alter it someplace, whether via cheap/free re-specs or by re-designing certain class roles to function better in PvP combat.
