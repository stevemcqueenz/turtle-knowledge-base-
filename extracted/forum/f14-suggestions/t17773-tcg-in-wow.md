# TCG in WoW?

## Post 121170 by Panax7 — 2025-02-27T23:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121170#p121170 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, first time posting here and I want to give a suggestion if it's possible. (Maybe it will be with the UE5).
Ever since I played TBC back in the day I started collecting the Darkmoon cards and it became a fun thing to do but they are just mats to create the deck and then to get your reward. But I always imagined how cool would it be to have more cards like Hearthstone which can drop like items from mobs, bosses, etc, and then you can put in a binder and trade with people. Just something fun that you can do while waiting for the raid to start or waiting for a friend to come online.

I don't really care if it's a playable game or just a collecting thing, but it could be fun if you can "challenge" a friend and both of you shuffle your decks and maybe draw 1 card and the card with the bigger number wins. Maybe the first one to get 3 wins is the winner.

I think WoW is missing some things that the players can do while waiting. Could this be made possible, and would other people like doing stuff like this? What do you guys think?
