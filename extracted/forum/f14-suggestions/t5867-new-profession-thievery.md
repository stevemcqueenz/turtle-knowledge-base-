# New Profession: Thievery

## Post 36976 by Ibux (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-06T15:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36976#p36976 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I suggest thievery as a new profession that players could learn  satisfied_turtle_head .

just as with most gathering professions thievery would need to be skilled up to access higher level targets. at low skill level you would have a high chance of failure. but as skill get higher thief's get a better chance to loot targets, and possibly a small chance to get additional items. targets would be crypts and graves. most importantly graves of hardcore characters  insidious_turtle .

to loot graves you would need a [shovel](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=shovel). the normal graves would serve mostly as level up training. these would have a chance of yielding the normal crap npc's nearby the graves usually drop. hardcore player graves would have a small chance to drop loot based on the items the dead character had in inventory. gold, gear, mats etc. hopefully there are many hardcore graves to rob all around. high skill thief's could also have a small chance of some additional loot from dead npc's.

i'm not sure were to find all normal graves, but usually some of them can be found in the same places as you find [grave moss](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?object=1628). in game there are already some grave robbers npc's [link](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=robber#npcs). these all got [thief](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Thief) as occupation. maybe thief's could also find some cool loot at some of the excavation sites.
