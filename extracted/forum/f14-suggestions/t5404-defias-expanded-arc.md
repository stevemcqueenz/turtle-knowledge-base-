# Defias expanded arc

## Post 32977 by Bigspliffa22 — 2023-01-23T02:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32977#p32977 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Is there plans to expand the Defias Brotherhood arc? After Edwin Vancleef is killed by adventurers, their story shouldn't stop there as gangs in real life don't always rely on one central leader. What if there was a power vacuum after his dead and internal fights for power, much like the gangs irl. It'd be cool to see their story arc expand beyond Westfall, what if they have a joint smuggling operation with the Bloodsail Buccaneers and skirmishes between them and the Venture Co.?

Before Vancleef died, wasn't he plotting with the Bloodsail Buccaneers to attack Stormwind?

Even the other criminal orgs, like Ravenholdt and Syndicate. Their arcs should be expanded. Maybe all these gangs end up fighting for smuggling routes in the South Seas when devs eventually include those zones. I don't like the direction retail Legion took them, Twow can do something different. Maybe one of these gangs can be friendly enough to rep grind with.

## Post 32978 by Mac (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-01-23T03:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32978#p32978 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yes, the Bloodsail Buccaneers were working with the Defias to build the Defias juggernaut you find in the Deadmines that would assault Stormwind Harbor according to [orders](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Bloodsail_Orders_%28Classic%29) you find in a quest.

Additionally, the Rogue quest line to learn poisons [reveals](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Klaven_Mortwake%27s_Journal) the Defias were working with The Venture Company to raise an army of undead zombies.  This quest doesn't end with you stopping the plan, just uncovering it and discovering a cure, and you aren't even tasked to kill Klaven Mortwake, the guy behind the plan.

If there was a more definitive conclusion to the Defias storyline, it would be interesting to see a quest where the Defias actually try to attack Stormwind Harbor since that was added to the game.  Maybe you're taken out on a boat from the harbor to meet them out on the sea before they can reach the harbor, and there you fight a zombified VanCleef (complete with a new head).
