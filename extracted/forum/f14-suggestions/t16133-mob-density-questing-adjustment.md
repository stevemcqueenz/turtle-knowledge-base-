# Mob Density & Questing Adjustment

## Post 109874 by Chaseowskii — 2024-11-25T19:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109874#p109874 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Many areas can be **awfully** tedious or annoying to quest in, especially when activity in the lower level zones get massive jumpstarts out of nowhere, such as the new 1-60 progression event for cosmetics, or even just major updates. Namely, these are typically specific example spots such as Gold Dust & Candles in Fargodeep, which could take you up to an hour or two just to do. Murloc Eyes for example already don't drop from some of them, such as Netters - and the Murloc types that can are very inconsistent. This also applies to many other quests, where enemies are *not* laid out incredibly well to account for this sort of player density. This is mostly the quests that require 15-20 kills or 10 drops and so on.   scared_turtle

The idea is simple, spread out more of these mobs more with additional density or minor spots they can be found in. For example, additional Harvest Watchers to the West of Furlbrow's Pumpkin Farm, Westfall. Of which has straw laying all around but absolutely no enemies or NPCs at all. Players aren't always keen on grouping up in account for EXP distribution, and casters make melee questing very annoying.   crying_turtle

There are many checks throughout zones that could use this sort of touch up, but would be well worth it! It doesn't have to be a bunch or too much more, but even a minor amount of these changes make a huge difference.   hiding_smth_turtle_head
