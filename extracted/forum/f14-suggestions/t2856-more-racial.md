## Post 18059 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-04-12T13:34:00Z

I'd love to see the classes being less standardized, differing depending on the race of choice. It is in line with the philosophy of Warcraft 3 and vanilla WoW.
Classes became more and more standardized over the years.
Priest had some racial abilities and the shaman will have too, in accordance to the lore of each race.
I think think this concept should be expanded quite more.
For example the shadow priest means something else in all races, yet it is still too standardized (in later expansions all shadow priests are just voidlord cultists, who were mainly the Ogres before Cata...).
Each race has unique approach, history and view on various classes, humans studied the balance between light and shadow and the philosophy was quite different in comparison to the deeper existentialism of the Cult of the Forgotten Shadow of the Forsaken, who have practiced Shadow Ascendancy, resulting in gaining the ability Shadowform.
The Troll shadow priests differ a lot in their perspective and use of shadow magic, as Loa worshippers and voodoo inspired witch doctors.
The Night Elven Shadow Priests venerate darkness as a benevolent concept of nocturnal activity connected to their moon goddess Elune, or perhaps the lack of her blessing as an eclipse metaphor, or them giving in to darker feelings like sorrow and vengeance because of great sunderring event, in all cases quite different to the Forsaken shadow ascendants.
Dwarves learned about the holy light as well as counterpart forces from humans, and infuse the concept with their worldview and lore, perhaps related to their life digging into the dark depths of the earth, and the potential to be overcome with excess of negative emotions like stubborness and greed.
