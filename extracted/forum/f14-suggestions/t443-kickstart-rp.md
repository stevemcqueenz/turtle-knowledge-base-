 **Peeves' Box** - Should be aquired automatically and should contain all teleportation stones, Speedy, as well as a roll of vanity items like shirts, tabards, robes, weapons and gold.
 **Commercial Hub** - Add easy means of transportation to Gadgetzan.
 **RP Points** - Add a system rewarding group activities, like dungeons, raids and groups.

While this all may seem nice and dandy, the real changes would come only with hard work. An idea that has plagued my mind for months is the idea of making your own RP public. By making a system that would allow for setting RP focus, like fishing, exploration, quests, elite mobs and such, people with similar interest would be gathered more easily together. A notice board in the capitals, with adverts by online players saying "Looking to take down Hogger!" and "Selling herbs, working with mail." would definitely spice up the RP aspect of the game. Notices should include the name and faction of the players, and should disappear completely when logging out for more than 10 minutes. I call this suggestion **Notice Boards**! Heh!

Next suggestion is of an event! Add the mini-game **Follow the Turtle**! A turtle spawns in the capital, in random intervals of time, but daily, and starts walking to a random location of the starter map. There one can find a hidden treasure of little turtles, as well as vanity rewards and gold. This would get more people to the capitals, while keeping the atmosphere fresh.
