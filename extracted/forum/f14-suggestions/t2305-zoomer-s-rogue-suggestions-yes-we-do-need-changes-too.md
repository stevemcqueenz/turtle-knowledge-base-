# Zoomer's Rogue suggestions - YES, we do need changes too!

## Post 14500 by Imagician — 2021-12-11T08:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14500#p14500 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone, you've probably seen me around in Stormwind (Or even Orgrimmar). i'm Zoomer in-game, and also have a level 60 hunter named Zoomies. I have a fair bit of experience with Rogue gameplay, and I feel like a good candidate as to what rogues need. Despite some peoples perception of rogues not needing any changes, i highly disagree. For both PvE and PvP, we are a middle-tier class, being outclassed by Hunters, Priests and Warlocks in PvP (And even Holy/Prot Paladins towards later stages in the game) for overall versatility, CC, Utility and DPS, & Warriors and Mages in raiding for pure DPS. (Mostly due to how those classes scale in later tiers, and the fact Rogues don't have infinite scaling unlike warriors' rage). With the upcoming addition to Surprise Attack, we're only expecting to see a 2-5% DPS increase over our usual raiding rotation. (While 90% of rogues who raid on this server are already mid-tier in terms of Meters).
Yes, we are a very versatile PvP class, although we rely alot, sometimes too much on cooldowns to stay consistent. If you've seen the World of Roguecraft video, you can see what a 10-minute rogue is capable of (Although you're a free target for the next 10 minutes, negating the fact that we aren't OP), but that sadly is far from reality, of how Rogue PvP gameplay looks, and we sadly see ourselves falling behind a lot of DPS classes in later phases of the game.

So, i wanted to make a few suggestions on how to improve rogue gameplay, both for PvE and PvP.
First let's look at individual talents, and discuss why some are and aren't useful.

 ****Assasination****

 *0/3 Improved Eviscerate*: Honestly, this talent is pretty good, although lackluster in PvE, as you rarely eviscerate, opting for Slice and Dice. Personally i don't think this needs a buff.
 *0/2 Remorseless Attacks*: This one is honestly a meme, you only see it being picked for very specific levelling builds, aswell as world-PvP'ers. A possible change could be; "After hitting a Critical Strike with Sinister Strike, Backstab, Ambush, Hemmorhage or Ghostly Strike, has a 20% chance to give you an extra combo point." - Similar to Relentless Strikes, and doesn't severely punish you for taking the talent. Similarly, you could also change it to: "After hitting a Critical Strike with a Finisher, gives you an extra combo point." Same deal, it just helps us keep up on our rotations WITHOUT using Thistle Tea or Renetaki's (Which is super hard to do normally).
 0/5 Malice: Not much to say, this talent is respectful and always picked.

 *0/3 Ruthlessness*: This talent is good, although you don't pick it if you play daggers. Also, a 60% chance to get an extra combo point isn't reliable, and rogues rely on either this or Relentless Strikes to keep their rotation smooth. Buff this to 25/50/75%.
 *0/2 Murder*: Okay, why does this NOT buff your damage to undead and elementals? Becomes useless for most of Naxxramas aswell as AQ40, make it buff your damage to all mob-types, excluding AQ40-ones with no mob-type. (Or just make it a passive 2% damage increase, although it actually buffs yours damage by around 2.5%).
 0/3 Improved Slice and Dice: Same deal as Malice, not alot to say, it's a must-have talent for raiding.

 *0/1 Relentless Strikes*: This talent is also a must-have, as mentioned earlier, no need for a buff, only if Ruthlessness gets buffed, otherwise increase the proc chance to 25% per combo point, so we can guarantee a combo point on our 4-point Eviscerates.
 *0/2 Improved Expose Armor*: Not a lot to say, maybe make it only be 0/1 for the 50% increase? Rogues required to run this spec already lose alot of DPS just by using Expose Armor.
 0/5 Lethality: Great talent, no changes needed.

 *0/5 Vile Poisons*: Meme-spec unless you play Premades in BGs. Could give it potential, by making it a 3-point talent, for a 5/10/15% damage bonus to your poisons, and a 5/10/15% chance to resist dispels. Also possibly merge with Improved Poisons?
 0/5 Improved Poisons: Mentioned above.

 *0/1 Cold Blood*: The staple of a stereotypical Undead rogue in full T2, Perditions Blade and Core Hound. This talent is super strong for pvp, but merely useless in raiding for the most part. No changes needed

 *0/5 Seal Fate*: This should be the biggest reason as to tempt rogues to run Seal Fate. A possible change could be: "Your critical strikes from any damage has a 12/24/36/48/60% chance of adding a combo point to your target". The main flaw of running Seal Fate, is how inconsistent it is, as you could crit outside of your yellow hits (Also rogues crit cap being a really bad thing; hit% + 50 = crit cap). This change could make it a little more consistent to run, and therefore also more competitive with other common rogue specs.

 *0/3 Improved Kidney Shot*: Only used for very sweaty Premades, usually running a PoM-pyro mage alongside. If the decision is made for cheap shot to DR with Kidney, make this talent a passive ability. (Otherwise just let it be, or make it 0/2 for 4.5%/9% improved damage taken.

 *0/1 Vigor*: The worst last-talent for rogues, and only really finds its small niche for PvP Dagger rogues. Make it passively increase your energy regeneration by 10-15% alongside, so you can justify picking it for a Seal Fate build. (Around 22-23 energy per tick. At 25% this would be 25 energy, but can be argued towards being too strong)

 ****Combat****
 *0/3 Improved Gouge*: The staple of a PvP Rogue. At 3/3 you can effectively stealth, and sap the opponent immediately as the timer runs out. Honestly pretty OP for PvP.
 *0/2 Improved Sinister Strike*: Why is this a talent? I honestly believe that Sinister Strike should cost 40 energy, regardless of it being talented or not, letting us spend points elsewhere.
 *0/5 Lightning Reflexes*: Only used for filler points, similar to many other talent builds for other classes. Decent for a meme-spec Rogue tank.

 *0/3 Improved Backstab*: What dagger rogues are famous for, a flat 30% crit chance on backstab, it's fine as it is.
 *0/5 Deflection*: Useless, but required to get Riposte, which i'm gonna get into. Again decent for the meme-spec tank.
 *0/5 Precision*: One of your best talents as a rogue, since you will always scale off of hit% (given crit cap for one.) Fine as it is.

 *0/2 Endurance*: Really strong talent that supports Improved Sprint play for PvP, usually used for filler in a raid build. Maybe reduce the cooldown to 60s instead of 45?
 *0/1 Riposte*: This talent is really useless, even for PvP. Although good for levelling, I'd love to see this talent be more viable. Could change it to: "A strike that becomes active after parrying OR dodging and opponent's attack. This attack deals 150% weapon damage, and disarms the target for 6s". Letting us proc it off a dodge *instead* would make this more viable for both PvP, and for levelling (Not Raiding).
 *0/2 Improved Sprint:* A rogue's BIGGEST weakness in battlegrounds are roots and snares. if you play Cold blood/hemo, you're a dead target to any hunter or mage who knows how to counterattack certain moves. Also burning vanish to break a root seems odd to me, and is one of our most important cooldowns. Make this just a passive thing?

 *0/2 Improved Kick*: Move this up to where Improved Sprint is, giving us the root-breaking ability for free, and letting us spec into Improved Kick easier for PvP. This would reward rogues with 2 things, an option to spec differently, and sprint being a benefitial cooldown in PvP, and sometimes even PvE situations
(Gonna skip all the weapon-skill related talents, although it'd be really cool to see the Weapon Expertise talent giving +5 weapon skill at 2/2 to all weapon types, and not only Sword/Fist/Dagger, naturally remove the +5 weapon skill from Mace Specialization).

 *0/2 Throwing Weapon Specialization*: Really? Nobody is gonna pick this, most useless talent for rogues to pick. (Could possibly replace this with the cleave-finisher idea I'm gonna talk about at the very end).

As for the rest of the talents (Weapon Expertise, Aggression and Adrenaline Rush, these things are fine already. Maybe make Adrenaline Rush increase your energy regen in tick speed, instead of tick amount?)

 ****Subtlety****
 *0/5 Master of Deception*: Usually only 2-3 points put into this for sweaty dagger rogues in BGs, with 2-3 points put into Agression. Fine talent, but maybe just make it scale higher for less points, say 2-3 point talent.
 *0/5 Aggression*: Good talent, a must-have for dagger rogues for both raiding and PvP, passive 20% damage increase to your backstabs at 5/5.

 *0/2 Sleight of Hand*: Nobody ever takes this, needs a severe buff. Merge this with the Heightened Senses ability; "Reduces the chance you are critically hit by melee attacks by 2/4%, Also reduces the chance you are hit by spells and ranged by 2/4%. (Stealth detection is only used for catching Druids in premades, not an issue on this server.)
 *0/2 Elusiveness*: This one doesn't need changes, vanish and blind are our strongest CDs, and should therefore have the longest ones too. Possibly change this to 0/3 for a 20/40/60s cd reduction respectively?
 *0/5 Camouflage*: Why is this a talent again? The passive movement speed bonus is useless, as rogues barely have to stealth if they know what they are doing in PvP. This is another one of those, that I feel should be a passive effect without having to spend the points on it.

 *0/3 Initiative*: Another staple of a PvP rogue. This one deserves no changes, as it's already super strong, even with the 75% proc chance only.
 *0/1 Ghostly Strike*: Super good talent to pick up for Hemo rogues, as a 20s cd to use either with or without evasion, not much to say, super strong in PvP.
 *0/3 Improved Ambush*: Only seen in PvP. Raiding dagger rogues can't afford to go this far down into Sublety, therefore making Backstab a harder hitting ability on average for them. (Yes, opening with backstab is better than ambush for optimal DPS).

 *0/3 Setup*: Never really seen being picked, since other talents are alot stronger, and this talent is very niche. Change the proc-chance to 20/40/60%, and it could see some use for defensive-style PvP Rogues.
 *0/3 Improved Sap*: Not much to say, this one feels like it should be given as a passive ability without the talents. Although the change from 90% to 100% in the next patch was definitely needed.
 *0/3 Serrated Blades*: This talent is bugged (expected fix soon?), decently strong, especially vs casters and requires 3/3 to get Hemmorhage. As I will discuss later, Garrote definitely needs some changes, so make it say; Causes your attacks to ignore 100/200/300 of your target's armor and increases the damage dealt by your rupture and garrote ability by 10/20/30%."

 *0/2 Heightened Sense*s: Mentioned above, merge with Sleight of Hand as this talent is only picked, to pick out druids in Premades, or for the gambler-rogues who feel like the 4% resist chance justifies the 2 points.
 *0/1 Preperation*: Nothing said, good talent.
 *0/2 Dirty Deeds*: Same as preperation, really good and a must-have for PvP rogues.
 *0/1 Hemmorhage*: The big staple of Sublety rogues, and something that should definitely be the key-component as to why people should play Sub in raids. The talent itself isn't bad, but it's too far down in the Sublety tree to justify over Blade Flurry. (Will get into several suggestions further down.)

 *0/5 Deadliness*: Useless talent, as it scales off your base attack power, and not your total. Change it to scale off your total for deep Sub-specs, and justify Premeditation a bit more.

 *0/1 Premeditation*: Only sees it's light in TBC due to shadowstep. It's again a really strong talent, but Cold Blood sadly is a better pick for PvP. Could replace it for where Heightened Senses is now, and make it viable to pick. (No, cold blood/hemo rogues aren't op, overgeared ones vs green-geared people are.) This change could enable Cold Blood/Hemo rogues to spend the last point for 5/5 Camouflage, as well as picking up Ghostly Strike, merge Heightened Senses with either Camouflage, or another similar talent, that isn't a first-row talent.

 ****Additional suggestions****

 - Make Distract work in combat. Can be used for it's 30-energy cost to face a target away from a player, and cancelling certain casts with a facing requirement. 30s CD is still fair this way.

 - Make Garrote a viable opener. Currently there is only ONE beneficial opener for PvP rogues, cheap shot. Even ambush isn't that good, and is only used to tear mages. However, you can still obtain 2 openers with sap, so cheap shot is still superior. A possible change to garrote could be; "Garrote the enemy, causing 552 damage over 18 sec, increased by your total Attack Power. Also silences the target, preventing them from spellcasting for 1s. Awards 1 combo point"

 - A high-cost finisher, that goes through a certain percentage of armor. Could be similar to eviscerate, but deals less damage while going through a certain percentage of armor, for a 50 energy cost. Something like 30-40% of armor being penetrated on the attack only. During later phases of the game, warriors and paladins become impossible to kill as a rogue, due to high armor and defensive capabilities (Most notably the *smoother* leeway and spellbatching mechanics on this server makes Rogue vs. Warrior a more fair matchup, Paladins hard-counters almost any class if specced properly and are geared enough, although still low-ish DPS in PvP as Holy/Prot).

 - Give us a talent similar to surefooted, could be put in the subtlety tree, around the 5th branch, making it impossible to pick that + Improved Sprint; "Gives you a 5/10/15% chance to resist movement impairing effects." Either that or the mentioned suggestion to Improved Sprint. Could also be a nerfed version of 'Burst of Speed'; "Increases your movement speed by 40% for 8 seconds. Also removes any roots or movement impairing abilities. 50 energy cost + 1 minute cooldown."

 - Increase the Crit Cap formula, or remove it completely. My rogue is almost in full T3 and 19% hit, putting my crit cap at 54% (On a level 63 mob. On average the crit cap should be 69 for me with 19% hit). Compared to other DPS classes who have no crit cap, i don't see the justification for a crit cap on rogues. Makes the Loatheb Spore buff useless with World Buffs. Either change the formula to be: +1 crit = 0.75% hit. Effectively making the crit cap on my rogue (as an example), go from 69% to 88%. Also makes itemization easier, as rogues run into so many crit cap issues (especially if Daggers with Kings/Might), that we can't go for certain bis pieces, as we go over crit cap in some circumstances. (Crit Cap, AKA Glancing Blows affects rogues the most, as Warriors can HS queue, and other melee DPS classes either only need 9% hit as to being 2-handed or Ranged, completely negating the so-called crit cap).

 - Give us a way (In PvE ONLY. This could be done, by adding the cleave-finisher to the Blade Flurry talent) to cleave more. Blade Flurry (And only IF talented) is our only way to cleave in open-world, far down in the combat tree and with a 3 minute cooldown. A fix to this, could be giving us a new finisher, that lets us hit a second opponent for X amount of attacks; "A finishing move that lets your next 2/3/4/5/6 attacks hit another opponent. - 10-15s CD (Cooldown starts AFTER the attacks have gone out)" The DPS increase won't be big, as we won't get to eviscerate, but gives us a possibility of cleaving outside of our Blade Flurry cooldown. (Or you know, replace this with the Throwing Knife Specialization).
Could also be achieved by giving combat/assasination rogues (Or a learnt ability for all rogues, doing 150-350 or so aoe damage isn't game-breaking, at all).
Fan of Knives; "Sprays knives at all enemies within 10 yards, dealing 35% of Total AP or 75% Weapon Damage as Physical damage. This attack can not apply poisons from your weapons." 45-50 Energy Cost, and a 8s cooldown.

 - Potent poisons scaling. Our only usable poison for raiding is Instant Poison, and with a possible addition of Anesthetic, things won't change. (an applied Insta-Poison to your off-hand is only about 10 or so DPS during your average boss fight, An elemental sharpening stone is about 10.5 if you aren't crit-capped, and Consecrated if undead, is about a 25 DPS addition)
Making this scale of either base, or total AP would be a huge step in the right direction, maybe even enabling some rogues to opt for Kings/Might over Windfury/Strength of Earth. Maybe we could also see the addition of a "exposing" poison, one to decrease physical resistance, and one to decrease elemental resistance by 20-30, maybe even an armor-exposing poison with x amount of stacks?

 - Combo Points being applied to the player, not the target. As I mentioned in a comment below, having combo points go away by un-targeting your current target kills alot of viability for us, and is honestly the most important thing a lot of rogues will want changed. It will interfere with addons that require targeting, aswell as making you lose **a lot** of damage if you choose to follow certain raid mechanics (Especially since we can't just cleave every add during a single boss fight, given the 3-minute cooldown on Blade Flurry).
Make these self-applied combo points fall off either after you leave combat, or start an internal-timer that gives you 6-8 or so seconds before those combo points get removed.

 - Add Sinister Calling in favor of Premeditation for the Subtlety tree. (Again, merge Heightened Senses with another talent like Setup, Sleight of Hand or Camouflage, move Premeditation to where Heightened Senses is now, and put Sinister Calling as the last talent for the Subtlety tree.
Sinister Calling; "Increases your total Agility by 3/6/9/12/15% and increases the percentage damage bonus of Backstab by an additional 1/2/3/4/5%"

 - Add Mutilate to the 6th row of the Assasination tree and make Seal Fate 1/1 and not 5/5; Your critical strikes from abilities that add combo points have a 100% chance to add an additional combo point. This might be the ONE thing that could enable Assasination-spec raiding and pvping while also not being overtuned.

 - Agression also increasing your Backstab/Ambush damage by 2/4/6% damage, possibly even Garrote & Rupture. This would make Daggers compete a lot more with Swords, which is usually seen as the go-to Spec for meta-rogues, and the talent-build wouldn't look that far off from the usual one;
 1. New Dagger build: [https://classic.wowhead.com/talent-calc ... 0100231-05](https://classic.wowhead.com/talent-calc/rogue/005023103-3203050020550100231-05)
 2. Normal Dagger build: [https://classic.wowhead.com/talent-calc ... 0100221-05](https://classic.wowhead.com/talent-calc/rogue/005023104-3203050020550100221-05)
The only difference as of now, is dagger rogues require taking 2 points into Agression as fillers, to unlock Adrenaline Rush

So to summarize, rogues DO need changes, some minor, and some bigger ones aswell.

If you've read the whole thing, I'd love to hear your thoughts, and possibly even more suggestions. I'm open to hear anyones opinion. Of course i'm not expecting everything, if even anything will be taken into consideration, although I'm hoping the Dev-team can take some of these suggestions into consideration for future updates and class change.

I say apply combo points to yourself because it would be easier to code than letting the rogue apply combo points to multiple targets. None of the finishers that exist in vanilla really have any element to them that indicate a necessity to have been attacking the same target, and one is diametrically opposed to it.

Eviscerate is just a stronger attack than you've been doing. Nothing about it makes me think a rogue isn't just building up adrenaline or getting into a specific mindset to unleash it.

Rupture is just a bleed variation of Eviscerate. I don't see why you need to have slashed a specific guy 5 times as opposed to slashing several guys 5 times to cut them.

Expose Armor is the one example that might justify the "multiple attacks on the same target" angle, in that you've been chipping away at their armor as you attack. But that's still weird, because that's literally what Sunder Armor is, and that's not the rogue's ability.

Slice and Dice straight up works counterintuitively to the idea of needing to hit the same person; it's not as if it goes away once the guy dies, so the rogue is clearly able to maintain the groove if they switch targets.

Kidney Shot is just stabbing someone in a kidney. Arguably, the rogue could do it at any time, but they're more accurate (which is shown mechanically with the increased duration) after building up adrenaline.

As it is, rogues look like chumps in that they're styling on their opponent and then a warrior cuts their head off and the rogue gets grumpy and complains the warrior threw off their groove.
