# Proposed stat change on the Time-Shifting Wheel and Jadestone Protector shields

## Post 112179 by Mimiwarmini (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-11T08:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112179#p112179 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, following update 1.17.2 many items have been changed, it was notably decided to reduce the impact of % chance of avoiding suffering a critical cost by replacing this statistic with a reduction of 3 % of critical cost received, choice which is accepted or not regardless,

On certain shaman tanks the Time-Shifting Wheel shield was very practical because few items exist to mount the defense on the shaman, at higher levels I noticed that the Jadestone Protector shield
could also be used too, plus from a PVE point of view putting defense plus the reduction in critical cost suffered is nonsense for me (for the PVP side it's probably another story)

my proposal is as follows: return these 2 items to their previous stats in order to make it easier to reach the milestone, I do not think that this will significantly impact the game given that they are shields and this will allow the sham tank to be able to do progress without having to go into mode and wait for T2.5
