# Increased Duration for Orb of Deception

## Post 29735 by Zeldy (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-23T21:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29735#p29735 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Orb of Deception is one of my favorite cosmetic items but its usefulness is unfortunately very limited due to its five minute duration on a half hour cooldown.
Perhaps if other players were interested, I would like to propose the trinket's usefulness be improved either by increasing the Orb's duration to also be a half hour or shortening the cooldown to five minutes, allowing players to essentially use the trinket whenever they want so long as they have it equipped.
The trinket doesn't offer any tangible benefits aside from maybe throwing off a player once in PvP but I definitely see its potential for roleplay, especially cross-faction.
If you have any input please share.   smiling_turtle_head
