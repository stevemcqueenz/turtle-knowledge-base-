# Dungeon Set drop rates need adjusting

## Post 19458 by Ashoryc — 2022-06-30T02:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19458#p19458 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently the drop rates on Dungeon set pieces are not even across the board. You're more likely to get druid, warlock, hunter and mage sets but the others all have a varying degree of lower drop rates. Especially now that their is cross faction it doesn't make sense for pally and shamans drop rates to be 3%.

Is it possible to have the drops rates to be made inline with each other and maybe even one step further and cause the set pieces to drop multiple say 2 for General Drakkisath for example plus the normal loot table. I believe it would prevent some of the burnt out from never getting your sets.

## Post 19469 by Mcnair (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-06-30T08:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19469#p19469 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think you checked the wrong drop rates. All dungeon sets have equal weight to drop, there is no discrimination against shamans or paladins. All BoP parts of the sets drop at 11-20% chances, the 3% is only the BoE parts that can drop from trash mobs or even bought from others.
