# Click to move hunter adjustment

## Post 152569 by Zeveer — 2025-09-21T14:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152569#p152569 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, I wanted to suggest a tweak to how "Click to Move" works for Hunters in Classic. Right now, when you right-click a mob, your character runs all the way into melee range and starts attacking up close, even if you're a Hunter. It kind of defeats the purpose of being ranged.

I remember in Cataclysm, it used to stop you at max bow range and start auto-attacking with your ranged weapon, which felt way smoother. Would love to see that behavior brought into Classic, so Hunters stop just close enough to shoot and don’t charge in for melee unless you tell them to.

It’s a small thing, but I think it would be a huge qol adjustment for hunters.
