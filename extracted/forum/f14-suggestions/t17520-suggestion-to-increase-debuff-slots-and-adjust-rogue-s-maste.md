# Suggestion to Increase Debuff Slots and Adjust Rogue's "Master of Deception" Talent

## Post 119585 by Nengneng020 — 2025-02-11T04:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119585#p119585 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear  Turtle WOW Community Team,
I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to address two critical issues that have been affecting the gameplay experience in 40-man raids, particularly in the European servers of WoW Classic. These issues are related to the limited number of debuff slots available on raid bosses and the current state of the Rogue talent "Master of Deception."
 1. Increasing Debuff Slots in 40-Man Raids
In 40-man raids, the current debuff limit on bosses is severely restrictive, especially given the wide array of debuffs that classes can apply. This limitation often forces raid leaders to prioritize certain debuffs over others, which can lead to suboptimal gameplay and frustration among players. Below is a list of some of the key debuffs that are commonly used in 40-man raids:
Warrior: Deep Wounds, Sunder Armor (8T3)
Rogue: Rupture, Deadly Poison, Crippling Poison, Expose Armor, Hemorrhage
Priest: Shadow Word: Pain, Vampiric Embrace, Shadow Vulnerability, Holy Fire
Warlock: Curse of Recklessness, Curse of the Elements, Curse of Shadow, Curse of Agony, Shadow Mastery, Curse of Doom, Immolate
Shaman: Flame Shock, Frost Shock
Druid: Faerie Fire, Swipe, Rip, Moonfire, Insect Swarm
Mage: Ignite, Fire Vulnerability, Arcane Instability, Winter's Chill
Hunter: Serpent Sting, Hunter's Mark, Piercing Shot
Other: Various armor reduction debuffs from weapons
Given the sheer number of debuffs that can be applied, it is clear that the current debuff limit is insufficient. I would like to suggest increasing the number of debuff slots available on raid bosses to accommodate the diverse range of abilities that classes bring to the table. This change would allow for more strategic flexibility and enhance the overall raid experience.

 2. Adjusting the Rogue's "Master of Deception" Talent
The current Rogue talent "Master of Deception" is often taken as a 3-point talent simply to progress further down the talent tree, rather than for its actual utility. The talent's effect is underwhelming, and many players feel that it does not provide enough value for the points invested.
I would like to propose the following changes to the "Master of Deception" talent:
Increase the Duration: Extend the duration of the "Master of Deception" effect from 12 seconds to 15 seconds. This would make the talent more impactful in both PvE and PvP scenarios.
Reduce the Number of Points Required: Adjust the talent so that 3 points invested in "Master of Deception" provide the same effect that currently requires 5 points. This would make the talent more attractive and viable, encouraging Rogues to invest in it for its utility rather than just as a stepping stone to other talents.
These changes would make "Master of Deception" a more compelling choice for Rogues, allowing them to contribute more effectively in raids and other group content.

Conclusion
In summary, I believe that increasing the number of debuff slots in 40-man raids and adjusting the Rogue's "Master of Deception" talent would greatly improve the gameplay experience for many players. These changes would allow for more strategic diversity and make certain talents more viable, ultimately enhancing the overall enjoyment of the game.
Thank you for taking the time to consider these suggestions. I look forward to seeing how the game continues to evolve and improve.
Best regards
