# My Shadow Priest Suggestion

## Post 138335 by Skattberg — 2025-07-07T09:59:00Z

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/sfbdHdFW/Shadow-Ideas-1.png)

After the ridiculous nerfs after the last patch, and in contrast to the insane buffs to arcane mage, Shadow Priest feels... a bit stale, lets say. Here are my suggestions without making anything crazy, imo

 **1st row left to right:**
 1. Spirit Tap synergizes with Mind Blast and Vampiric Embrace, making it useful not ONLY when leveling.

 2. Remove Blackout, useless in PvE, everyone hates it in PvP since it messes with diminishing returns and it is unreliable. And since the nerf its completely useless. Replace it with a flat 10% increase to shadow damage (damage, not spell power). Boring but useful and in line with classic.

 **3rd row:**
Mind Flay is gone as a talent! It is now a standard spell. Replaced by Sanguine Plague - a dot that deals shadow damage and duplicates a low amount of damage caused by the caster and party members, solidifying the role as damage/support.

 **4th row:**
Shadow Reach - now also reduces pushback on Mind Flay by 33/66/100%. It is such a standard spell in the Sp kit and on a 3 second cast i think its justified to give it a 100% chance to ignore pushback and also since its unaffected by haste.. cmon.

 **6th row left to right:**
Void Touch - stuns the target for 4 seconds. 20yd range. Castable when stunned. Replaces the utility of Blackout and makes it more reliable.

Dark Soul - Increases Shadow spell damage by 5/10/15% of total Spirit. In addition, if successfully cast Mind Flay for 3 seconds deal additional damage equal to 50% max rank Mind Blast. Synergizes with Spirit Tap. Gives extra damage under the right circumstances, so its not just press one button rng to blast for 2000hp. Looking at you, arcane mage

Hopefully this will be completely forgotten after 1.18. But until then, check this out!
