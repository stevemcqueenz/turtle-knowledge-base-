# Druid Healing Tier Sets.

## Post 141147 by Zmarotrix — 2025-07-24T22:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141147#p141147 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The original druid tier is was designed to be a general set for all kinds of druids. Because of this, it's not particularly good for any of them. Now with the custom sets for each spec, Resto just got stuck with the original tier set because that's what it was used for before.

It would be nice if Resto druid could actually benefit from the earlier tier sets a bit more. As it is now, most resto druids stay in their Pre-raid BiS until T2, and even then they often don't use T2. It would be nice if they could have a similar gearing experience as other classes/specs and actually be incentivized to get their tier set.

It does not need major rebalancing, just some adjustment. Remove the Spell Damage and move that budget all over to Healing power, or give it a larger chunk of spirit. Something.

## Post 141543 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-26T10:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141543#p141543 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You should maybe take another look at the original sets. Druid T2 was very much a healer-only set. The spell power component is something that Turtle added.

For example, the OG Stormrage Legguards had +48 healing power. On Turtle they have 26 SP, 22 HP. So healing still gets +48, meaning the spell power component is completely free. It does not make the item worse. This is maybe not something to complain about ;-)

T1 has also already been adjusted with free healing power and free spell power (depending on what the Vanilla item had). But I very much agree that T1 is a very weak healer set. The better pre-raid BiS on Turtle does indeed mean you mostly skip T1 as a healer. The fundamental problem are the resistances. They are very costly on the item budget, so the Vanilla tier items often seem understatted. The TWoW alt-tier sets are less reliant on a points system, so the relative power is a bit higher.
