# Adding Cosmetrics/Appearance Changes/Glyphs to endgame?

## Post 136208 by Stryta — 2025-06-18T18:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136208#p136208 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Still a bit new to the server but i had a moment of inspiration thinking about Cosmetics, Glyphs, and custom items in general i thought may be a good addition to the game.Adding a little more + in Vanilla+ if you will.

The glyphs druids have available is pretty cool. I think it would be a good idea to add the idea to the game in the endgame.
Imagine being able to farm for a low drop rate chance at custom Cosmetics/glyphs/titles etc. It could add a lot more to do beyond raiding in the endgame.

I think it would be best to create a new zone specifically for lvl60s.  You could have random events spawn, random world boss spawns and it could also act as a zone for world pvp to those who chose to participate. This zone would be more for farming with the potential to drop Glyphs,Titles, Mounts, Appearances, and whatever else would fit. Overall this zone would be a "hangout" area for lvl 60s. ( the zone would allow for infinite time spent farming for stuff and things that would give players a sense of uniqueness and achievement.) The loot added to this zone would almost entirely be cosmetics but COULD be expanded on into gear but doing so could reduce the need for raids.

 **Specifics**

This zone would come with a Hub with all the vendors and npcs and also had guards (like bootybay) this would allow for the randomness of being raided by players or the ability for pvpers to gank. (could even add rewards/rep +/- for killing guards to add another layer to the zone)
By allowing the hub to be attacked, you could add events that have bosses or mobs swarm the hub at random.

This zone would have areas of different difficulty and each area would be available/ more advantageous to both solos and groups

The zone would have random patrolling world bosses like the outlands felreaver (but an actual boss)(watching people get stepped on would be great   turtle_tongue  )

The loot in the zone would have different drop chance as well as rarity. You could also add another level of rarity. (i think it would be cool to add those drops only 1 in 10,000 players acquire, to bring back that feeling youd get when you got something actually legendary)

This zone could also act as a playground/sandbox by giving players the potential to get items that affect the zone. spawn mobs,events,bosses, cause mobs to rampage,

You could add more items like the tents you get in game. Items that could be spawned to help other players, add localized buffs or debuffs.

You could add towers that once captured change how mobs behave.

Overall this would give players at end game the ability to farm for items that would create a sense of uniqueness and achievement.
