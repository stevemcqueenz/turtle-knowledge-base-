The way the survival professional item and multi-tool function is that you acquire a profession buff for an hour. When you activate the multi-tool and choose a specific profession, that profession gains a 30-minute bonus while the profession item remains active. After the hour, there's an hour-long cooldown period, and both the profession item and multi-tool reset.

Let's use the Herbalist Charm and Survivalist's Multi-Tool as examples, which are my favorites. You start by using the charm for the hour-long buff. After that, you activate and apply the multi-tool to the Herbalist Charm for the 30-minute bonus. With your Herbalism skill at 300 and a stack of Gromsblood, the first two attempts yield no results, but on the third attempt, you succeed and apply the "Blood Fury" effect, granting a 1% critical strike chance for the duration of the bonus! ![😄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f604.svg)![👍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f44d.svg)
