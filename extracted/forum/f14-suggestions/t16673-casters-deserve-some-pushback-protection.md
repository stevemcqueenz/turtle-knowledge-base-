## Post 113926 by Thorm267 — 2024-12-23T00:53:00Z

Hello  smiling_turtle_head  ,

Im a relatively new player on the TurtleWoW surver and to classic in general but im loving almost every minute of it, especially with the new quests and areas that TurtleWoW introduced. The journey to max-level is my favorite part of the game, however one of my biggest gripes about the leveling phase is that it isnt really enjoyable for some specs, particularly on elemental shaman, balance druid, and  priest.

The reason for that is in my opinion the lack of 70% spell-pushback-talents for these classes!

The TurtleWoW-Team eliminated this issue in their 1.17.2 Patch last month for balance druid (trough the new talent "Sylvan Blessing") and on the new discipline priest (where there made "Holy Focus" include holy attack spells)., but elemental shamans and shadow priests got nothing and the problem still existes.

So here i have some suggestions to the talent-trees.

!. Shaman:

The simplest solution here would be to include nature spells (lightning bolt and chain lightning) in the "Healing Focus"-Talent in the restoration tree and maybe put it in the 2nd row to make it earlier accessible. Otherwise we could just create a new 2-point talent in the elemental-Skilltree right next to "Convection".

 2. Shadow Priest:

The simplest way this time is to create a new talent in the first row of the shadow-skilltree, right next to "Spirit Tap". Make it a two-point-talent, naybe name it something like "Shadow Focus" and we would be done.

and on extra:

 3. Warlock:

I thought it would be nice to receive additional 70% spell-pushback-reduction if one decides to take the "Demonic Sacrifce"-Talent. Maybe make it a niche way to level a lock without a pet.

So, these are my suggestions and please excuse any spelling mistakes, english isnt my first language.

Regarding (shadow) priests:
- power word shield gives you 100% pushback protection, while it lasts

- mind flay slows incoming targets

- mind blast has a short cast time of 1.5 sec which means you can time it hetween most enemies' swings which are usually 2+ secconds

- fear (rank 1) is a cheap way to get distance between you and your enemy

- power word pain and wands don't care for pushback
So with the following rotation priests shouldn't have problems against solo mobs while questing:
- pre-shield

- max range mind blast

- apply shadow word: pain

- silence if enemy is caster type

- mind flay until target in melee range

- mind blast

- reapply shield when its used up

- mind flay until shield is used up once more

- rank 1 fear to get distance

- repeat from step 1
You can even take down elites this way if you have the mana.

Regarding shaman: throw a frost shock once in range to let you get in 1-2 extra lightning bolts or preplant earth bind in the enemies approach. As a shaman you're supposed to be a hybrid so get a good melee weapon (including a one-hand & shield set) and finish your enemy off at close range, that's what your shocks and totems are for.
