I don't think that Dual Wielding is gonna make them better. Sure, it'll probably give them a slight boost on dps, but it won't fix their real problems which are mana issues and cluncky itemization. By the latter I mean that you can somewhat reliably hit 9% melee hit with orc racial, 1h axes and miner helm, or 2h maces and Exalted Thunderbluff helm or the "Gloves you ain't gonna see on this server", but you're never ever fixing the issue of not having decent spell hit for your shocks. 18% chance to fizzle sucks man. That's close to 20% of all your shocks going to waste, and those tend to cost an arm and a leg. With talents it's down to 15, but that's still kinda big.

If you can fix the spell hit issue and mana issue then Shamans have potential to be strong, competitive even considering that Windfury is very strong. But fixing that is a headache.

 - Redesign 3% hit talent. It should instead give 3% melee hit and 6% spell hit, at that stage you will need 12% hit, which is easier to get down to a more managable level without sacrificing too much melee power.
 - Reward Shaman for hitting things with his weapons. A talent that passively gives shaman a % to return something like 100-200 mana a hit would be big.
 - Give Shaman a Slam-like ability that is only useable when you're stationary, as in you gotta stand there like a true PVE hero and charge your strike. This is mostly to prevent shamans from having even more burst in PvP. I say that it can be anywhere from 1,5-3 seconds. If it's 3 seconds then it shouldn't stop swing etc.
 - Give Shaman a Dps cooldown that will reduce said Slam ability do down to 1 second, so that you can spam it with 2h, giving you opportunity to proc more of those juicy windfuries. In PvP it's still gonna be worthless because nobody stands there, but in PvE it'll be a godsend.

What's the reward of Shaman having the ability to dual wield?  A lot of people look at Rogues and Warriors and think that the solution to all issues is to simply slap ability to Dual Wield. In most cases what makes Warriors and Rogues such strong Dual Wielders is not their ability to dual wield, but their ability to augment it's potential and their insane scaling.

Rogues have free 5% hit, ability to boost off-hand damage considerably, and have abilities they can freely spam and augment in tandem (such as on demand haste buff) with dual wielding AND on top of that they are not reliant on Mana; Energy is superior to Rage even, at least in early stages when gear isn't as powerful as it gets later at which point Rage becomes better.

Then you have Warriors who scale so well that despite the fact that they lack hit talents, and their off-hand augmentation is weaker they manage to exceed even Rogues. Heroic Strike Queing giving 100% hit to off-hand? A dps cooldown that gives them flat% more damage? AP scaling ability Strike that doesn't take up a debuff slot? Get out of here man, I'm sold - time to roll one of these!

Then you have Shaman. So you give it dual wield, what now? You still can't use Stormstrike because turboautists will cry that their precious debuff slots are stolen. You still need 18% hit to reliably hit with your shocks. You still need to fix your mana issues.

I'm also of the opinion that Blizzard making TBC shaman dual wield was them throwing in the towel and giving up on Shaman's uniqueness. But then again you can generally see that TBC is the beginning of the end in that department.
