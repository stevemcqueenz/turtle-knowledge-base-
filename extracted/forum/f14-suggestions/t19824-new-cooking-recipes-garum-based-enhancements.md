# New Cooking Recipes: Garum-Based Enhancements

## Post 135381 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-11T18:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135381#p135381 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

*These classic-style recipes introduce **Garum**, a fermented fish oil that can be used either as a temporary weapon coating or as a cooking additive that enhances well-fed buffs. Made from extracting oils from fish, Garum was the most popular condiment in the Roman Empire, put on everything from meat to desserts, and it was very similar to the "fish sauce" found in modern southeast asian cuisine. Filtered versions of Garum are quite clear and free of debris, as with any vegetable oil.*

 **Recipe: Garum of the Coast** (Cooking 225)
 **Use 1:** Apply to weapon to add +3 Nature damage to hits for 10 minutes.
 **Use 2:** Combine with cooked food to enhance its Well Fed bonus: +4 Stamina added to any food that grants Stamina.
 **Ingredients:**
- Oily Blackmouth ×6
- Deviate Fish ×2
- Salt ×1

 *"A sharp-smelling oil made from fermented coastal fish. Adds flavor and... bite."*

 **Recipe: Garum of the Deep** (Cooking 275)
 **Use 1:** Apply to weapon to add +5 Frost damage to hits for 10 minutes.
 **Use 2:** Combine with cooked food to enhance its Well Fed bonus: +8 Frost Resistance added to any food that grants resistance.
 **Ingredients:**
- Stonescale Eel ×8
- Elixir of Water Breathing ×1
- Salt ×2

 *"This thick fish oil tingles on skin. Apply at your own risk."*

 **Recipe: Garum of the Trench** (Cooking 300)
 **Use 1:** Apply to weapon to cause attacks to have a chance to reduce target’s armor by 50 for 10 sec.
 **Use 2:** Combine with cooked food to enhance its Well Fed bonus: +1% Melee Haste (non-stacking) for 30 min when eaten with meat-based foods.
 **Ingredients:**
- Nightfin Snapper ×6
- Dark Iron Ale Mug ×1
- Salt ×3

 *"Used by pirates to eat or fight. Sometimes both."*
