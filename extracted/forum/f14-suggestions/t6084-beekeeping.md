# Beekeeping

## Post 38220 by Cervantes (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-19T12:10:00Z

**BEEEEESSSSS** ***The Bees are coming :)***
This morning I was in my real life garden and loving the weather, when I was watering my veg and looking at my beehive then i thought maybe there is a place in wow for this?

In the same taste of **(Gardening)** my suggestion is to add beekeeping into the game.

So I was thinking with beekeeping the aim is to make honey, and it would work like gardening.
Maybe you put down a bee hive made by **[Survival]** with **[Simple Wood]** x10 or brought off a NPC
 **(The Bee Master**) and some bee lure, by **[Alchemy]** or brought off the same NPC.

Apply the lure to the hive first to get some bees moving into their new home.

Then you would get some flowers and interact with the **[Hive]** so you get some pollination.
The flowers come from NPC's **(Herbalism Supplies)** or **(Herbalism)** from the gathering profession.
This would be good for all the herbalism folk out there, and it might boost their income.
Here are some examples

 **[Simple Wildflowers]**
you can buy from:
Felicia Gump **(Herbalism Supplies)** in Stormwind
Brunda **(Herbalism Supplies)** in Orgrimmar
Then interact with a **[Hive]** that has bees in, you will make a simple honey called **[Wildflower Honey]**
and its effects are:

 **1. [Wildflower Honey]**
Level Requirement lvl 1
 835 Mana over 24 seconds + 10 intellect (Stacks up to 20)
but works as a magical buff, so you can still have a food buff from **[Cooking]**

Just like **[Melon Juice]**, but you can use at lvl 1, and it gives you a nice buff, would be a great aid in levelling and dungeons runs.

 **2. [Red Rose Honey]**
Level Requirement lvl 1
 835 Mana over 24 seconds + 5 Fire resistance *(Stacks up to 20)*
(Only ideas at this point, Honey does have great healing element to it and works well applied to burns)

 **3. [Black Rose Honey]**
Level Requirement lvl 1
 835 Mana over 24 seconds + 5 Shadow resistance *(Stacks up to 20)*

Also, maybe with a custom quest or a random pickup from your hives you get a new none combat pet called **(Buzz)**
