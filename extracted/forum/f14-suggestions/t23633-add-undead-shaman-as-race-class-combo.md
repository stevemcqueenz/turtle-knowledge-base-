I want to answer to this thread in a more constructive way rather than being simple-minded (I don't want to point fingers at certain users).

First of all, thank you for sharing your ideas and opinions!

While it is obviously a cool and unorthodox concept, it has a big portion of lore obstacles on its way to being included in the game. The Forsaken are a former nation of Lordaeron, and humans in the Warcraft universe (at least to my knowledge) were never seen as shamans (BUT if we take their ancestors, the Vrykuls (who were shamans), that gives us the window of possibilities for both humans and undead, but I guess the shamanistic knowledge was lost by the time the human kingdoms expanded on the eastern continent). The Forsaken are now seen more as apothecaries and remnants of Lordaeron. A little bit of their new culture they preserved from their past, a little bit from the scourge.

We have a small chance of accepting them as a concept, but it's unlikely they'll appear in the game due to the lore issues I've listed.
