I don't know if this has being suggested already but here I go.

It would be cool to make bounties on rare mobs, yeah those that pack a punch and needs some people to team up and kill, for example Morgaine the Sly, Mother Fang, etc...

The bounty may consist on a quest that can change every week or month, and of course they have to be different in Elwynn, Durotar and all the starting areas for they have a different objective.

I'm suggesting this for starting zones to try and gather people together on low levels where you ussually don't know anybody, also it may have a reward for starters like: Bags, Weapons or Armor.

This would mean making the rare mobs spawn at rate of one per day or each 12 hours for example.
