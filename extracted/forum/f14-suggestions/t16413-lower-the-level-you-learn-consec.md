I know it sounds stubborn but I don't defend this idea because I have any of my tanks near level 20, it is not the case.
There's a minor cause and a major cause.
The minor: Slightly better LFT for DM.
The major: Let's say that you level a char with the concept of tanking. You'd reach a level where your first grouped instance's tanking would be possible for most of the average players (not talking about smooth runs neither optimality, talking about the possibility) but your tanking toolkit lacks something fundamental for 2 more levels. Because of this half of these chars which are in this situation don't go to the dung and people wait more for LFT. But the other half will go ('it's in my range and I'm a tank') and like 90% of those people would open a wipefest. The remaning 10% is skilled enough to more or less manage or has a random lvl 60 in the team. This is my 'major cause'.
