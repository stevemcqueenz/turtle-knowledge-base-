## Post 101814 by brotalnia — 2024-09-24T11:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101814#p101814 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am the creator of this quest and the weapon and I want to thank you from the bottom of my heart for writing this. I needed this validation. I wanted to create a quest chain that felt memorable and to have the reward be something unique. It was my first and last time trying to be creative. I felt bullied afterwards, with other people repeatedly trying to ruin my idea and make it generic and boring. I have never gotten over the trauma of seeing people who I thought liked me aggressively trying to tear down the only part of myself I put inside the game, after working not only on turtle but the open source core it's based on for years. It made me feel worthless and I regretted ever making it. I had made the stats on the weapon intentionally underpowered out of fear, because I didn't want anyone to claim that it was overpowered and use that as a way to advocate for its removal, but sadly my attempt to preemptively address that criticism was unsuccessful. It was still called overpowered and many attempts to remove the effect were made, despite the fact I had given it the stats of a green item 5 levels lower, and that you had to go through this elaborate quest chain to get it. It was only after Torta's intervention that it was restored to my original vision and allowed to stay in unaltered. So while I agree the item is quite weak given its level and effort required to obtain, I do not think it would be possible to buff it without drawing aggro. But to hear that players actually like that it is quirky and different makes me so happy. I feel vindicated. Thank you again for this post. You made my day.
