# An Alternative to worldbuffs?

## Post 25462 by Angulardrift — 2022-09-19T01:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25462#p25462 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to make a suggestion of a new custom profession.

Scribe.
Able to make the various scrolls we would normally receive as rare loot + likely custom scrolls.
Rip cloth to make fibre which can then be used to make papers. ores and dusts used to make magical inks.
eventually craft a scroll.

Worldbuffs could become endgame recipe scrolls (possibly dropped from raid bosses themselves?)

I think this might be a good solution as the main reason people actually hate worldbuffs is the fact you have to do a whole ass other raid just for the buff you want to do another raid, instead it just becomes another consumable.
and we get scroll buffs as a regular and consistent thing instead of "oh maybe once in a blue moon i get a scroll I could use"

I feel this is a good compromise as the tiring part of the Wbuff meta gets thrown out, but wbuffs still remain for those who want them.

Let me know what you think.
