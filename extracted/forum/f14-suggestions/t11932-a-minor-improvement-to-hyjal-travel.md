# A minor improvement to Hyjal Travel

## Post 78942 by Queenoona (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-27T15:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78942#p78942 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently Hyjal only has one Flight Path in the far North of the zone. The main feature of this area is the Emerald Sanctum Raid, there are several barriers to entry to making this raid enjoyable, but one i've not seen talked about as much is the travel to the raid.
Lets look at a couple problems with getting to Hyjal and Emerald Sanctum

 1) Hyjal is a massive zone, getting from Nordanaar to Emerald Sanctum takes a significant amount of time.
Normally I wouldn't be too against travel to raid, after all. You Travel to MC, BWL, AQ, Naxx. However the flight paths to those locations are much closer than those to Emerald Sanctum, which ties into the second problem.

 2) The Hyjal Flight path routing.
I love the scenic view, don't get me wrong, but it takes around 5 minutes to get through just the Hyjal section, that is not even taking into consideration that you're forced to route through Everlook meaning that you end up going on an extremely long FP, only to be met with an annoyingly long walk.

My proposed solution:
Early into Hyjal questing you're set upon the following quest chain (which itself is quite long)
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40984>
You trek around the world for this quest eventually completing it (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40990>) and your main reward is the Runestone network that allows you to teleport from Nordanaar to near the Hyjal entrance and back. Quite helpful but the placement of Emerald Sanctum is just such that you're still having to make the same long run to the raid.

I would like the team to consider adding a follow-up quest.
 **The Sanctum Runestone (Raid Quest)**
"You've done well assisting with the reactivation of the Runestone near the base of the mountain but there is still one more that could serve you benefit if you would be willing to assist me further. If this is of interest to you, please enter the Emerald Sanctum and slay those corrupted by the Influence of Nightmare, do this and I will regard you as capable enough to utilize the Sanctum's Runestone."

This quest would ask you to kill some number of all the trash mobs inside the Emerald Sanctum. Meaning that you have to make the trek at least once. After which you will be able to easily return to the Sanctum in the future. This would serve a double benefit of essentially creating a middle of Hyjal waypoint of sorts, making traversing this behemoth of a zone a little less tedious.
