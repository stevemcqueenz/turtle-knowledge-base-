How should Turtlewow deal with overpopulation with minimal side effect?

I logged out but I want to play. Only being able to view a 50 yard radius of creatures and players just doesn't cut it for me. This is exactly how corporate Blizzard deals with it and why I don't play classic and Wotlk. If you look at the player numbers, the world should be full but it's empty all around.

My suggestion is this: Don't limit the spawn of mobs and other players' view distance when the server population gets over crowded. Can this be done? How can this be done? Is it possible?

I think any alternative would be less damaging. Here's a few ideas off the top of my head:

 1. Since HC players can't use the AH, trade or party with non-HC players, I would suggest an entire server layer for just for them. No more confusion on who we can and can't group invite, buff or trade.
 2. Make a permanent HC server. Which upon reaching level 60, you could either choose to port to the SC server or stay as a permanent HC end game 60.
 3. Make another server but for the same characters. Meaning your characters can access any server.
 4. Server Queue times. Limit the server to its max capacity at which no server side nerf is needed.
 5.Make a super ultra server that doesn't lag because your a bunch of genius neckbeards.
 6.Log out AFKs faster.
 7.Ban multi boxing all together.
 8. All of the above.

From watching player's fight as far as the eye can see while standing on a Westfall hill to being able to see friend from foe into a distant setting sun in the Barrens, the world around you is rich and full of life. There's a rare beauty here in Turtlewow that isn't found anywhere else that I think is well worth preserving and I hate to lose it to a 50 yard bubble. What do you think?
