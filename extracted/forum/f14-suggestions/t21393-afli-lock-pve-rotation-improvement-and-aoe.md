**introduction and reason:** why Affli lock needs QoL improvement.

 1. Curse slave. Trash dies 5-10 sec, 3-4 GCD, during this small time u can only put curses on all targets and trying to do more than 1000 on last mob in pack. Imagine if for each mobs pack, shaman should be put only 1 totem per GCD. This is sad and boring. And shaman not totems slave anymore, QoL was improved

 2. NO aoe at all, all avaliable aoe from Destro tree. Need AOE at least for mass cursing

 3. Spec very overloaded with spells compared to other DPS classes. need to keep all 3 DOTs + curse, 4 different channeled spells . Drain life, Drain mana, Drain soul, and we got one more channeled Dark Harvest. Give life for current 3 Drain spells.

 4. For normal regular dps with Drain soul or DH spell, u need to keep always 4 affliction debuffs on target. Not just zug zug any shadow spell for 5 stacks Shadow Weave. No. watch time and update them all. Why so annoying, just buff improved drains  or scaling if Drain soul so weak. Nobody has such asspain, just 1-2 spell for debuff or buff and dps.

 5. Adding another channeled **(Dark Harvest)** was a dubious idea before. Visually - beautiful, conceptually - interesting. Let me explain what is the main problems of DH:
 - DH deals less dmg than Drain soul per tick. Near -5%
 - DH doesnt proc **Shadow Vulnerability** , when Drain soul can and next tick does +20%shadow dmg
 - If u got resist DH - its annoying and 30sec cd wasted, when Drain soul u just cast again
 - if u got **Nightfall** - its annoying, even more annoying if u got each tick 1 by 1 **Nightfall** proc, because to interrupt dh means waste 30sec cd. When with Drain Soul u can release all shadow bolts by procs.
 - You need to keep all dots 8+ sec (time depends on haste) or u lose dps much more then with Drain Soul.
 - And because dots will end 30% faster - u will need to update them sooner and lose gcd and lose dps.
 - The only with a full **T2.5** where the last DH tick deals 400% damage u have interesting strong spell for dummy bosses, but not for simple mobs (trash), and not bosses when u need to move.

 6. **Nightfall** Either proc per year or each tick. Its so annoying when u cant realise ur rarest proc.

 7. Tons of spare Soul Shards during the raid. Nothing to spend them on

 8. Currently warlock the only caster that doesnt have strong mana return or mana reduction talents. Only poor self killing lifetap with poor talent bonus. Only t3 adding interesting using lifetap. When wl used LifeTap - he lost GCD and as result DPS. And what most important - annoyed healers. Wl mana and dps highly depends on healers mood.

 9. Drain mana and drain life either has bad scaling or bad talent bonuses. Drain mana u will use only on Moam, but instead dps, and worse drain mana as hunters and priests.

 10. Curse of Weakness and talent Improved Curse of Weaknes are very weak and should be improved. Dead useless curse

 11. only 1 build is effective for pve as affli. 35/11/5 . Nobody uses demons, because they weak in high raids and need basic scaling. nobody uses shadowburn, because already overloaded spec. Sad. But this problem idk how to fix easy
