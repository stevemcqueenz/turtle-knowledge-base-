# Add Arrow or System to Reorder Characters on the Character Select Screen

## Post 127749 by Dailyy — 2025-04-21T02:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127749#p127749 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey TurtleWoW devs and community,

I’d like to suggest a small but QoL-enhancing feature: the ability to reorder characters on the character select screen, either through drag-and-drop or simple arrows to move characters up or down.

 **Why this matters:**
Many of us have mains that we play 90% of the time, but due to how we leveled or created alts, they’re buried under a pile of bank toons and mules.

 **Faster access:**
Having your most-played or raid-ready characters at the top saves time, especially during server restarts, crashes, or switching quickly between toons for dungeon summons or professions.

 **Cleaner UI organization:**
Being able to group characters by purpose (PvE, PvP, RP, bank, etc.) makes for a more intuitive and satisfying login experience.

 **How it could work:**
Arrows next to each character slot to move them up or down

Or a settings option to "Set as Main" that pins a character to the top

Even just a one-time sort option would be better than nothing

It’s a small feature, but it would improve the daily experience of dedicated players who juggle multiple characters across various roles.

Thanks for considering, would love to hear if others would find this useful too.
