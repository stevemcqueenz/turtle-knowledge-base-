# Later talents I'd consider for shaman class changes

## Post 91009 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-20T23:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91009#p91009 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

TWOW has announced class changes for this year.
In this post here are my 10 cents regarding the (in my opinion) most interesting abilities from later expansions, watered down/tweaked for a Vanilla+ environment.

Riptide - I like it and dislike it at the same time. Here shamans don't have HoT (if we don't count healing stream totem but it's so bad we can say that shamans lack HoT altogether). But a riptide-like ability giving a buff on the target + adding a healing bonus and being consumed while being targeted with a shaman healing spell seems like a very interesting idea.
Tweaked version of riptide: 6 sec CD, instant - puts a riptide buff on a friendly target for 30 seconds. If a target with riptide gets targeted with any shaman healing spell, the spell heals for 20% more and consumes the buff.
This iteration would be an 'altruistic version', helping each healing shamans in the raid and would trigger for each healing spells, not only chain heal (using WoTLK version as reference). I prefer it this way because chain heal is already overused and buffing chain heal only would be a bad design at this point. Lowered the bonus heal it gives for design.

Lava burst - Very interesting elemental-only ability, very bursty spell. My only concern is PVP since it guarantees a crit with a flame shock on. I'd keep the WoTLK version of the spell while nerfing the crit bonus -> A flame shock having +20% crit chance on a target with flame shock instead of a guaranteed crit. Would keep the 8 sec CD.

Earthliving weapon - A weapon enhancement for resto is definetly something we don't have now. Would decrease the healing bonus, though. A +20 healing power at max level is already decent. Earthliving buff is not needed, it would be a HoT.

Wind Rush totem - Having a utility to raise movement speed in a small area can be a nice tool. Would decrease the added movement speed to 20% though. Would have a CD to avoid spamming it.

Mana Tide totem - Giving it increased mana regeneration instead of the current mana refill would be interesting. Let's say that the totem allows 50% of your mana regeneration to continue while casting (stackable with other talents). Would change CD to 3 min and duration to 20 sec. Could combo very interestingly with certain builds in the party, like arcane mages who refill party mana based on a fraction of their own mana regeneration.

Totemic Projection - Vanilla shamans tend to have 2-4 totems down always and small moves force the shaman to resummon these totems. Totemic projection could be a good alternative of totemic recall. Instead of getting 25% of the mana back, you can just relocate the totems (timers stay, a totem about to expire before relocation would still be about to expire after relocation). Huge qol ability. Would sometimes be better than totemic recall, would sometimes be worse than it.

Stormflurry - Enhancement needs some love and making stormstrike better is a way to do it. For Vanilla this ability would mean a 25% chance of an additional strike in case of each stormstrike, the added strike being 40% damage. Nighfall-wielders would love it.

Electrocute - It's a PVP talent in retail but could be an interesting Vanilla+ ability, adding nature damage to successful purges. Would be situational but a well-spent talent point before places like AQ20.

Seasoned winds: Also, an interesting PVP ability which can be used in Vanilla+. Each earth shock interrupt guaranteeing a 15% damage reduction from the school of the spell you just interrupted is a very cool ability.

Undulation: The perfect talent to motivate shamans using other heals besides chain heal. Making every third healing wave or lesser healing wave spell 50% bigger is I think a very good idea for Vanilla+ meta, motivating shamans to use a bigger variety of healing spells.
