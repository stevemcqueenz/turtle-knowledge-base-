Private vanilla servers values were a lot of guess work and that's part of the reason why a lot of them are overtuned. When I look at the original damage values from blizzclassic and 06 vanilla it does look like classic lines up a lot more with what's accurate, much to my dismay. Example being many end game dungeon bosses mindblast doing 2.2K damage rather than 1.2K on classic. You notice this stuff as a tank.

Private servers just use a 3x melee damage 3x hp modifier of a normal mob for most of the elite mobs. This leads to weird situations where the caster mobs spells do significantly less damage then if they were to melee. The thing is that this tends to be a lot more accurate at higher levels but it does make low level dungeons a lot harder than they otherwise should be. I don't really think this is a bad thing with 2 exceptions. That being RFC where mobs doing 2x melee damage compared to classic unironically makes it the most difficult dungeon until the triple attack troggs in Uldaman.
Then we have DM/VC (just the final boss) where everyone needs to do the cannon strat and people miss out on looting his head & getting his letter after kill him because the adds are still up. (Haven't done it on turtle but this is what we did on every single private server).

 *Do the dungeons get more blizz like in difficulty at higher levels?*
In my experience on other private servers the answer is yes but the end game dungeons also differ a lot more from server to server. They are still more difficult on average though. Places like UBRS range a lot from server to server. Rend Blackhand has either been a joke or one scary motherfucker which needs to be disarmed 24/7 and nuked down ASAP. Not sure what he's like on turtle.
