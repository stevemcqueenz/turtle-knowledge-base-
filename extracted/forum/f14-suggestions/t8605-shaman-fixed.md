# Shaman fixed

## Post 55458 by Regaldandin — 2023-08-01T13:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55458#p55458 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Stormstrike**

- Cooldown changed to 9 seconds

- Mana cost lowered by 25%

- Applies Max Rank Lightning shield to yourself when cast for free

- Now scales with Spell power (0.71)

- Instant attack that does 100% weapon damage converted to Nature damage

- On successful Hit, consumes 1 to 3 charges of lightning shield and deals damage equal to the combined damage of the number of charges consumed

- Gives you a buff that increases nature damage done by 15% for 6 seconds

- No longer debuffs enemy
 **Grace of air totem**

- While active, lowers the threat of all shaman abilities by 30%
 **Elemental focus**

- Spells cast while in the clear casting state cause no threat
