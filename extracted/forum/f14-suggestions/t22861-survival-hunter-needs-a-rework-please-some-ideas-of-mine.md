# Survival Hunter NEEDS A rework please, some ideas of mine

## Post 159484 by Nekoko — 2025-11-13T10:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159484#p159484 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Introduction

The Survival Hunter specialization on Turtle WoW offers a unique playstyle, but several aspects of its kit feel outdated, restrictive, or misaligned with the server’s custom-class philosophy. In its current state, Survival suffers from notable issues regarding mana sustainability, utility gaps, trap effectiveness in end-game content, and rotation complexity.
This document outlines key areas for improvement and potential solutions aimed at both PvE balance and class identity, while avoiding disruptive impacts on PvP.

 **1. Mana Management Issues**
Current Problem

Survival Hunters experience severe mana pressure in all forms of end-game PvE. Optimal DPS effectively requires constant consumption of Tea, Mana Potions, and cooldown-based consumables, creating a resource loop that feels punishing and unsustainable.

Why This Is a Problem

Creates a “pay-to-perform” experience not seen in other physical DPS classes.

Makes the spec dependent on T3 6-piece bonuses or external consumables instead of talent-based design.

Limits uptime in longer boss encounters, especially in Ambershire’s progression environment.

Potential Solutions

Bake the T3 6-Piece Mana Sustain Into Talents

Convert the set bonus into a baseline Survival talent or passive effect.

Could be added to an underused talent node.

Introduce a New Mana-Regeneration Talent

Trigger on critical strikes, trap procs, or melee abilities.

Example: “Rekindle Instinct: Dealing damage restores X mana, reduced in PvP.”

Add Active Resource Tools

A Survival-only equivalent of Rapid Recuperation.

A short cooldown racial-style ability that helps stabilize mana pools.

 **2. Lack of Interrupt Utility**
Current Problem

Survival Hunters lack any form of interrupt or kick, which is increasingly important in modernized 5-man and raid content. Hunters are forced to use Freezing Trap as a pseudo-interrupt, which is unreliable due to cooldowns, immunity, and mob behavior.

Why This Is a Problem

Other DPS specs bring interrupts; Survival brings none.

Trap-based interrupts are inconsistent and often unavailable (trap is on cooldown 100% of the time in 5-mans).

Limits group usefulness and feels out of place compared to Turtle WoW’s philosophy of expanding class toolkits.

Proposed Solution

Introduce a PvE-only interrupt:

Example Ability: “Distracting Slice”

 5–10 sec cooldown

Interrupts spellcasting and locks the school for 2 seconds

Tooltip: “Does not affect players.”

Prevents PvP abuse but fixes PvE weakness.

This aligns with Turtle WoW’s precedent of creating PvE-only functionality (as they’ve done with other custom abilities and items).

 **3. Trap Effectiveness on Fire-Immune Content**
Current Problem

During Ambershire progression, and especially in MC/Onyxia phase content, Fire Traps become ineffective against large portions of encounters due to widespread fire immunity.

Given that Fire Trap synergy is core to Survival’s identity, this makes a major part of the spec irrelevant in Phase 2.

Why This Is a Problem

A spec-defining mechanic has no value on many bosses.

Reduces DPS consistency compared to Marksmanship/BM.

Makes key talents feel wasted in raid environments.

Possible Solutions

Change Trap Damage Type Only When Fire-Immune Targets Are Hit

Similar to Rogue Dissolvent Poison which bypasses specific resistances.

Fire traps could deal a generic “Elemental” damage type to immune mobs.

Introduce a Talent That Converts Trap Damage

Example: “Adaptive Explosives – Your Fire Traps now deal Nature damage to Fire-Immune enemies.”

Scaling Damage Buff for Immune Bosses

Fire Traps could trigger a debuff or secondary effect instead of pure damage.

Any of these keeps Survival’s trap-based identity relevant through all phases.

 **4. Rotation Depth and Gameplay Feel**
Current Problem

Survival’s rotation currently boils down to macro-spamming 1–6 abilities, often bound to a single button or mouse macro. There is little reactive gameplay or decision-making.

Why This Is a Problem

Lacks skill expression.

Encourages automation/macros over actual player engagement.

Feels shallow compared to modernized Turtle custom classes or custom specs.

Potential Solutions

Introduce Procs That Change Priority

Example: “Your Raptor Strike critical hits have a 20% chance to reset the cooldown of X ability.”

Add Short, Optional Buff Windows

Something players plan around instead of spam-rotating.

Improve Talent Interaction

More synergy between traps, melee abilities, and ranged shots.

Something like a combo builder/finisher structure.

Create Conditional Abilities

Abilities that change function based on last trap used, stance, etc.

All options add depth without breaking the vanilla foundation.

 **Conclusion**

Survival Hunter is thematically one of the most exciting specs on Turtle WoW, but currently struggles with:

Severe mana starvation

Lack of PvE utility (interrupt)

Traps being ineffective against major raid content

Shallow rotation lacking meaningful decision-making

Implementing the proposed improvements would keep the spec competitive, engaging, and thematically rich—all while respecting PvP balance and Turtle WoW’s design philosophy.

These changes would not only improve player experience but also strengthen class diversity, group utility, and raid progression options across the board.
