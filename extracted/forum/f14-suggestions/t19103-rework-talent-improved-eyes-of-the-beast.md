# [Rework] Talent - Improved Eyes of the Beast

## Post 129825 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-06T09:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129825#p129825 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Note**: I'm placing this talent suggestion as a placeholder for when(if) an eventual Talent revamp is done again to freshen things up. As of right now, I do not think it's necessary, but something I wanted to share and hear people's opinion on.

 **Improved Eyes of the Beast** renamed to **Beast Unleashed**

> Duration reduced by 45 seconds, cooldown is set to 60 seconds and cast time is removed
>
>  While channeling this ability your tamed pet takes 5/10% less damage, deals 30/70% additional damage and increases its focus generation by 20/50%.

---
With this change, the following will be applied:
 - The duration is now set to 15 seconds
 - CD starts when the ability ends or is cancelled, so it's always on 1 minute
 - Ability is now instant cast
 - Damage taken reduction (all sources) is now added. This compensates for not being able to use Mend Pet, thus improving the pet's survivability.
 - Damage is increased from 15/30% to 30/70% (more than doubled)
 - Focus generation is increased from 15/30% to 20/50%
---
The idea here is to use it more as a DPS cooldown, and part of the Beast Master rotation. As of right now, this talent is rather niche, only mostly useful for tanking pets, so they can keep aggro a bit better thanks to the improved damage. On the down side however, you lose DPS overall, because your hunter is not attacking. Additionally, since no defensive benefits are gained it's not really worth keeping for so long, as you won't be able to use Mend Pet. There are other ways to remedy that, but imo having the ability as a DPS CD may be more fun, as it'll diversify the BM gameplay, and give it some more identity. Plus, it'll be better for the tanking niche I mentioned earlier, as the Pet will generate a lot more threat during that period and be more durable

Please share your feedback, be it positive or negative  satisfied_turtle

Yes, that's my only concern. Lowering the duration below 15 doesn't seem very reasonable, as the effect would be way too brief. I included the part where cast time is removed, so the use of the ability is more seamless and can be macroed together with Bestial Wrath. It will be a big damage nuke, but the hunter will be completely offline during this period. Any disable which interrupts channeling will also stop the effect, and since the duration is much lower, the Hunter can't really hide far away, so they'll be relatively close to the Pet.

Maybe adding the cast time back, so the effect can be interrupted may help this out if it proves annoying in PvP. I'll keep it in the suggestion for now until further feedback.

Thank you for sharing your thoughts!
