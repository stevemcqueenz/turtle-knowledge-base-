# PLEASE STOP THIS FARMING MADNESS

## Post 59520 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-28T12:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59520#p59520 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know i know i know, the new server will come and the server population may get better.

But right now is kinda impossible to farm mats for raids, the high population and gold farmers broke the raider farming.

Or we spend many hours, like myself, to get just a couple stacks of plants or we have to spend the double the amount of time farming gold in dungeons (when i get a group to do so, because gold farming dungeons can’t be made with pugs) to buy the same quantity of plants. I rather compete farming in the world map than farm gold to motivate gold farmers.

The way it is many ppl are just giving up raiding.

WE NEED TO FIX THIS ASAP.

I know that the devs staff is limited, but i believe this is a priority.

PLEASE

 **Make daily quests on 5 men to give raiding mats.**

This is not a normal suggestion anymore, but an urgent matter that has to be dealt with. Because, even if the new server solves the high population, in time the server will be full again and the same thing will happen with raiders.

Please, help the raiding community to farm their mats with another alternative than out of the open world. Because, if the dailies give farming mats, the price in the AH will get lower and in consequence will demotivate the gold farmers.

Im not saying that the dailies has to supply all that the raiders must get, but, at least, to lesser the time wasted trying to get anything in the open world.
