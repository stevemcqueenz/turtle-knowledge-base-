# Change Ancestral Knowledge Talent

## Post 15014 by 5kaeledas5 — 2021-12-30T12:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15014#p15014 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The first row of Enchancement talents feels underwhelming in terms of leveling contribution, trade-off and value, so much that most Enhancement leveling guides suggest to go for Elemental and respec after lv 20 or so.

 **Shield Specialization** has subpar utility for leveling, but fits for the tank build Blizzard intended for Enchancement Shamans and that the TurtleWoW devs surely want to preserve. Therefore it seems reasonable to leave it as it is.

But if that talent is rifghtfully designed for a tank build, the other one in the first row should be designed for damage output and contribute to the DPS build.

The second talent **Ancestral Knowledge** grants 1% of Max Mana per point spent, up to 5% (maximum 5 points).
Now, adding maximum mana sounds like a poor choice for any enhancement build.
Sure, one might argue that a tanking Shaman needs a higher mana pool for building threat with Earth Shock, but why would a tank choose this talent over shield specialization in the first place? Nor spend those 5 points here instead of other better talents going further down the tree?
Besides, for 5 whole points spent in this talent the amount of mana is truly negligible.

My suggestion would be to change this talent by either buffing the mana-increase value or, *better yet*, turning it into something new that fits the Enhancement playstile concept and that could also be felt while leveling.
The main problem lies in the fact that the Shaman during the 10-20 lv bracket learns no damage-oriented enchancement spells.

This new talent could therefore revolve around Lightning Shield, an enchancement spell that is learnt during the first 10 levels, adding a very "Enchancement-like" interaction with melee attacks, such as:
 **LIGHTNING FURY: Your melee critical strikes have a 50/65/75/85/100% chance to refresh Lightning Shield charges if already active**.

I believe this could revamp Enhancement Shaman early leveling by granting a more defined playstyle compared to elemental, as well as adding a fun interaction to a core spell that has already seen some love in the last class changes.

Thanks for your time and I hope to hear some feedback from you! Also, any better name suggestions for the talent are welcome!
