# Rebirth Ritual [Prestige-No Power Creep]

## Post 167864 by Moreweight — 2026-02-10T08:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167864#p167864 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Intro: Turtle WoW has an amazing leveling experience. There are so many zones to explore, dungeons to hunt loot in, and quests to complete on the way to 60. Because there is so much content, people will often miss out on a lot. Once characters reach 60, they try to prepare for raids, then they complete raids, and that’s kind of where it ends. You can always level another character, but I think adding a prestige system would really complement the spirit of this server and improve the health of the game. This idea isn’t new, but I think the specifics are important to keep it also in the spirit of classic.

Ritual Rebirth (Prestige):
A player can opt to reset their character from level 60 to level 1. Essentially, one has the chance to level the same character multiple times to earn titles and a cosmetic rewards (cape).

How it would work:
 -Player reaches level 60.
 -Player goes to NPC in respective starting zone and opts to reset back to level 1 with 0 xp.
 -Player must be completely naked (no gear or bags equipped) and must pay a fee of 500g.
 -Player receives a title.
 -All completed quests, unlocked flight paths, professions, abilities, skills, and reputation reset.
 -Player keeps all items, gold, bank, mailbox, etc.
 -No perks are given aside from title and the respective cosmetic cape become available for purchase from NPC/Vendor to signify prestige level.

Rationale: Turtle WoW thrives on the open world and leveling experience. Hardcore mode and Slow-and-Steady as well as other challenges are also a big part of this server. A prestige system complements this by keeping low-level zones populated for new players, providing a "New Game+" feel for those who love the world but have finished Naxxramas, especially all of the level 60s on Nordanaar, but also the people waiting for phases on Ambershire. This option would add a layer of social status that doesn't introduce power creep (making it “opt in”), as well as providing a gold sink to stabilize the economy and reduce inflation a bit. Personally, I would just like to level my favorite class and race multiple times to thoroughly experience all of the content that TWoW has to offer. This could also be scalable/extendable if the community enjoys it enough.
