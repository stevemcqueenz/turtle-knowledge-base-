# Protection Warrior Talent Tree

## Post 151647 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-14T02:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151647#p151647 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Created a custom version of it.
<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/Heym97BfSF>

The core changes:
 1. Increased Revenge's scaling by default, allowing it to be a decent endgame option.
 2. Removed Iron Will from Prot Tree and replaced it by a custom version of Improved Thunder Clap. This version doesn't reduce the rage cost but allows 1/2/3 additional targets to be hit by it.
 3. Replaced Improved Intervene by Improved Mobility which allows you to Charge in Defensive Stance for 1 talent point.
 4. Changed the Revenge talents. Improved Revenge has no stun chance anymore but received the rage refund from Reprisal. New Reprisal gives Revenge a 50/100% chance to hit an additional target.
 5. Changed Gag Order totally. New version excludes 50/100% of your shield's armor damage reduction when calculating the rage you generate.
 6. Buffed Improved Disarm talent. Now a successful Disarm also applies 1/2 stacks of Sunder Armor.
 7. Buffed One-Handed Weapon Specialization. The new version also gives your auto-attacks a 10/20/30/40/50% chance to apply a stack of Sunder Armor.
 8. Increased the CD reduction of Improved Shield Wall, making it a 15 min CD ability if talent is fully taken. As a price of it, removed the additional duration of Shield Wall.
 9. Reworked Defensive Tactics. Now you can retain 10/20/30% of your rage when switching from Defensive Stance from another one. This helps you access other abilities more easily mid-fight but doesn't reward free stance dance since this bonus only applies if the switch happened from Defensive Stance.
 10. Replaced Concussion Blow's armor penetration to successfully applying 3 stacks of Sunder Armor if it hits.

The logic behind the changes:
 1. Many ways to apply Sunder Armor while keeping the original ability useful. Allows the Prot Warrior to focus on other abilities more.
 2. Not making Tactical Mastery baseline but allowing Charge to be used for pulls in Defensive Stance if a talent was taken gives the warrior more tactical options. Same applies to Defensive Tactics changes.
 3. Improving Rage Generation when tanking. Wanting to be as bulky as possible and not wanting to be bulky because of reduced rage received felt like a bad combination of concepts.
 4. Made Thunderclap more reliable for some AoE threat without making warriors remotely good at it. Imagine Stratholme zombies or for an earlier example, Wailing Cavern plants. Now at least the warrior can affect most of them besides shouts.
 5. Turned Revenge into a good threat generator with a half-cleave. It again, won't make Prot Warrior a good dungeon tank but consistently hitting two targets with high threat and tabbing can make things much more consistent.

PS: Regarding the sunders.
I think that keeping up a 5-stack debuff in 5mans is annoying. Enabling easier stacking would result some stacks being thrown arounf at trash packs and a prot warrior being able to focus more on other rotational elements during boss fights.
At raiding TWOW has 2 big difference compared to Vanilla. First, most raids downscale and second, much more builds are viable. At the end of the day, in average, a raid has much less warriors and then the raid size also dwindles.
As an example, a BWL at TWOW might contain 20 people and because more builds are valid (and hybrids are really strong), we probably have 3-4 warriors out of the 20. Now, these 3-4 warriors would have the same sundering duty, the 15-20 warriors would have had for a 40-men BWL at a Vanilla server. And at many cases there are 2-3 targets at once, dividing the warriors' sundering.

I see where you're coming from but I disagree at the AoE point.
It's true I suggest  increasing the TC limit via the talent but TC by itself (or even with demo shout) is too weak to be a reliable AoE utility.
I wanted to insert a reliable and strong semi-cleave (revenge) because I saw many warriors strunggling in instances with packs but that's the furthest I've gone there, expecting a prot to tab+cleave.

I had 4 main concepts when creating this:
 1. Being better at packs. You correctly pointed it out but I never had the intention to challenge consec. The closest comparison I can make for this Revenge concept is a stronger Swipe which hits 2 targets, not 3.

 2. A semi-stance dance. Besides the added charge to def stance, I wanted to let the warrior keep rage when switching mid-fight from def stance. F.ex. for a mocking blow, execute or preparing for a fear. But would give no benefits when switching from any other stance. The idea was to enable other tools while tanking when needed without actively encouraging switches.

 3. More rage. What I wanted to make with Gag Order... I try to rephrase my intentions.
Let's say you have 7000 armor with a shield and 4500 without it. Armor reduces the physical damage you take which reduces the rage you generate. I wanted the talent to make this: when having 7000 armor with the shield on, damage mitigation is getting calculated for 7000 armor but rage generation is getting calculated as if you had no shield, through 4500 armor.

 4. Easier sunder stacks. I think the distribution of warriors in instances is lower than in Vanilla and wanna make an important but menial duty less time-consuming.
