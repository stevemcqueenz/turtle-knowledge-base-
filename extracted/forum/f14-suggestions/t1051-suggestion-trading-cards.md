# Suggestion: Trading Cards

## Post 6002 by Qixel (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-05-14T06:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6002#p6002 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was thinking about the coins recently (awesome idea, by the way), but as a trading card game player, I was kind of disappointed that you can't trade them to people who want specific coins. And then I thought, why not just add trading cards? :D

So my suggestion is to add a vendor who sells packs of cards on the coast of Feralas, around where Turtlhu is. The vendor is Dick Garfield, <The Wizard On The Coast>. He'll sell players packs of Turtle Cards for 3g 99s, and each pack contains a random lot of three cards, with at least one being uncommon rarity or higher. Additionally, the first pack a player opens will contain an insert card that gives a quest to collect all 20 cards of the set and return to The Wizard On The Coast in exchange for a BoP item showing you collected the entire set, along with something else, perhaps. Maybe a special item for transmog? ;D

Crucially, packs and the cards they contain are not Bind on Pickup, so that players can trade them with each other. Packs may also be rarely found in chests, so that players may randomly find them in their travels and be introduced to Turtle Card collecting. There may also be wizards rival to The Wizard On The Coast that would drop cards when defeated, such as the enigmatic Archmage Bushi, <Wizard of the White Road>.

The nature of being a trading card game allows an easy avenue to add new cards in the future, too! The Wizard On The Coast can release an expansion set of Turtle Cards in the future, allowing unlimited additions while allowing collectors to finish their sets unhindered. <3
