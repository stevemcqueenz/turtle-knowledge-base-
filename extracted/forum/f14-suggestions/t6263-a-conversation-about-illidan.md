# A conversation about Illidan

## Post 39434 by Leroythegreat — 2023-03-28T14:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39434#p39434 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With outland having been all but confirmed by turtle devs I think it's time to have a conversation regarding one of wow's most popular and controversial characters... Illidan Stormrange

In my opinion Blizzard Really Fucked Illidan big time with what they did to his storyline in retail... In tbc they turned him into this revenge seeking scumbag before giving him this redemption ark in Legion

From my experience with Illidan in Warcraft 3 my overall impression of him was a guy who was neither good nor Evil but a dude who has had extremely bad luck over his long life and has had to suffer numerous betrayals and was all but abandoned by everyone who he considered family and Let's face it neither the Blood Elves, Broken or Naga are inherently Evil.... They all are victims of circumstances beyond their control and Demons as well aren't inherently Evil.

Now turtle wow will have to include Illidan in their upcoming Quel'thalas/outland patch because you simply can't have an outland patch without Illidan and the only question is how should he be implemented? Should he be implemented as an enemy? Should he be implemented as an ally?

I would like to suggest a different route... Have Illidan and his Forces serve as a Neutral faction unaffiliated with either the Alliance or horde.... A uneasy yet powerful coalition of Races and outcasts united by their shared hardships and abandonment by the other races of azeroth

The neutral Faction would consist of the following

Illidan's Naga commanded by Lady Vashj

Illidan's Blood Elves commanded by Prince Keal'thas

Illidan's Broken Commanded by Akama

Illidan's Demons commanded by The Butcher who Illidan had revived following his conquest of outland

The faction's capital would be the Black Citadel which unlike it's black Temple counterpart in retail would not be a raid but a neutral capital city along the lines of shattrath city and each of the above mentioned groups would have their own little area within the citadel with Illidan himself residing within Magtheridon's former chamber

Please comment below and share your thoughts/ 2 cents
