## Post 146307 by Teerack (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-16T23:31:00Z

I had fun doing [the other map](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=20952) so I did one for ogres.

Thousand Peaks
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/zYzCuGS.jpeg)

Lore for this one is pretty simple. When the ogres flead Stonemaul some of them went north and founded Brackenwall Village while others fled south into the mountains and founded Rex'sto Mound. You'll just be dealing with the wild life around you while also fighting some quilboar and black dragons. There is a goblin town in the south called Mudrock where you will help them gather some minerals/lost supplies and kill some dangerous things. Once you do all that the goblins will then send you by zeppelin to durotar. Was also imagining part of the lore in this zone is the cashed zeppelin in dustwallow left from Mudrock and was shot down by the black dragons as it passed over them.

The zone looks smaller then it is since its actually pretty close to the side of the skinny part of thousand needles. I only scaled it to 1-5 thinking this could act like an ogre version of valley of trials, before going to durotar, but it might be able to fit content to 10.
