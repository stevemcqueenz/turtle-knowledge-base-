# Allow to cast buffs while mounted

## Post 156237 by Mothius (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-19T23:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156237#p156237 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello there.

I'm playing as Priest right now, and i like to buff other players casting Power Word: Fortitude on them, i think help each other with buffs is part of the Vanilla experience, however, after i got my mount, to cast a buff other player i have to:

 - Dismount;
 - Cast the buff;
 - Summon the mount again;

For every player that i decide to cast a buff i have to do this process.

With that in mind i make this suggestion, to allow the use of buffs while using a mount, without dismount when cast the buff.

Is a small change and suggestion, but i think will be a great QoL improvement, helping to encourage the players to use the buffs on other players, and helping with the Vanilla spirit of cooperation.

Buffs of all classes should be included, here some examples;

 - Power Word: Fortitude;
 - Divine Spirit;
 - Arcane Intellect;
 - Mark of the Wild;
 - Thorns;
 - Blessing of the Might;
 - Blessing of the Wisdom;
 - Blessing of the Kings;
 - There is a few others who fit in this type of spell;

Thanks.
