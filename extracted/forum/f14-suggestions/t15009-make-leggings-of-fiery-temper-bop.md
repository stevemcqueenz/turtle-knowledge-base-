Blazespark is a level ~25 rare mob that was custom made by amazing turtle wow devs and is completely inaccessible to leveling players because a small group of level 60s camp it for every spawn. They log in for 30 seconds every 24 hours to catch the spawn and use pets on aggressive to ensure no one else has a chance of getting the tag. Even spamming a /target macro with an instant cast spell isn't fast enough. The leggings are great for leveling for a variety of classes but the only way to get them is to pay hundreds of gold on the auction house. It's unhealthy for the game, please just make the item BoP like other rare and desirable open world items (eg. tidal charm, arena grandmaster, etc.).

Maybe I'm just dumb, but I can't think of a single other item in the game that is in this situation. Most high value low level items are either BoP or if they are BoE then they're random world/instance drops (such as many of the 19 twink items, edgemasters, etc.) which prevents the market from being controlled by a select few.
