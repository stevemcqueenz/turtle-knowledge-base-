 **THE OTHER IDEA**:

Make the Tea unique stackable item like Zanza potions.
 1 quest = 10 Teas in 1 stack, unique (can't pick up more than 10).
Limit of 10 is arguable. Just the same number stack of Major Pots have.

 - are limited in using it per single raid/BG
 - travel to Winterspring / *Other quest location* more often

 - Makes Tea more *supportive* consumable and less *must-have* one.
 - Softly teaches players to manage mana carefully and get off from Tea-addiction.
