## Post 69690 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-10-09T05:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69690#p69690 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Honestly, I'm on the "books for all racial weapon skills" side. Currently, the meta is that you should be certain races, with certain weapons, to get the most out of them. Anything else is sub-optimal. Letting every race eventually get the highest possible skill with any weapon type means that your racial becomes a neat little extra that helps a little in levelling, instead of a build-defining aspect of endgame that you need to decide on from the moment you make your character. It means that you can play around more, try different things out, and make the character you want to make, without having to worry about what build you'll "need" for endgame.

It would also mean that if someone thought they were going to go for one build, but then found themselves in a position to pivot to a different build later on, they wouldn't have to worry about it not synergizing with their racial.
