# Rework  “Level 1 Lunatic” Challenge

## Post 139977 by Azerroth11 — 2025-07-16T20:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139977#p139977 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’d like to suggest significantly reworking the "Level 1 Lunatic" challenge.
While it’s a fun concept on paper, the reality is that it disrupts gameplay for others—especially in low-level zones.

The challenge encourages high-level characters to funnel gold and enchants into level 1 twinks, turning them into unkillable gankers.

This results in:
 * Constant griefing of new players and leveling characters who have no means to fight back.
 * Frustration for those doing permanent PvP challenges, like myself, where getting killed can mean losing a long-term commitment.
 * Low-level zones becoming warzones, killing the immersion and fun for anyone trying to quest normally.

This feature effectively makes it "1 player versus 10,000" and not in a good way—it benefits a tiny few at the expense of everyone else’s experience.

Please consider disallowing enchants or high-level gear/gold transfers for the challenge.

This would preserve the spirit of the challenge without making it a tool for griefing. Thanks for considering!
