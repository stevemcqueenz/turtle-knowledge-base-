# Night Elf Priests and bows

## Post 55938 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-05T23:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55938#p55938 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone

This is a brainstorm and suggestions (on favor or against it) about the topic . The point is, Nelf priests racial is not great, theres no other way around it. It uses a lot of mana for something that an wand is equivallent. It was so bad that was something that changed a lot over WoW expansions.

For that i would suggest ways to implement the use of bows for Nelf priests. But not aiming to steal the bows with dps stats from hunters. But new itens, a wand equivallent, or slight better (because it will have a dead zone).

Also, these Nelf priests bows would have priests stats, like healing, spirit and shadow damage, maybe with a slight lower white damage DPS for a tradeoff for more stats.

This is very lore accurate and i think is inside the scope of TWOW.

Now for the brainstorm:

 1) Nelf priests could learn the bow skill at level 10, because at this level he would start aiming for group content.

 2) Nelf priests could do their starshards as a dot intead of channeling with a bow eqquiped.

 3) With the bow deadzone, anything at melee range it becomes useless for attaking. (same as hunters)

 4) No pet for priests, so for solo content would not be viable, for that wands would be used.

 5) Hit chance would be the same as using wands.

 6) would need arrows as hunters, and could benefit from a quiver.

 7) the autoattack does not stops, just halts, when casting something, again, as a hunter.

Thats it, look, the objective is just a racial and not a class revamp.

If someone thinks that this idea is OP, i guess the result is just lowering the startshards damage, and the bow white damage for priests.
