I might have an idea for a better solution that cater to both sides here. There should be strict Transmog rules. Battle gear (mail, plate, cloth, leather) should only be able to Transmog as its respective category. I.e. plate -> plate. At the same time non-combat gear should lose the cloth type tag and only be transmogable to other cosmetic (non-tagged) gear.

This way if you want to look like a silly bitch while in combat, you gotta sacrifice the stats of whatever cosmetic gear you choose to equip over the appropriate class battle gear.

At the same time, warriors could choose cloth robes cosmetically, but would not be able to Transmog cloth on plate.

Do we have the perfect solution right here?? Just remove the cloth type tag on cosmetic gear.
