## Post 136424 by Melfrato1 — 2025-06-20T16:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136424#p136424 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings fellow Adventurers.

Its been a great fun all time around so far, I really like it here but there has been smth on my mind, especially after Tanking a lot.

Open World Elites and Instance Mobs just deal way too much damage. With a Level 60 Raid geared character having a lvl 53 Elite Orgre pushing me 20% when soloing.

 2-3 Elites in low level Dungeons leveling a Paladin dropping me so fast that the Heal has to pay attention all the time and oom's himself every 2 pulls.

It feels incredibly bad. You drop like crazy especially when missing gear while leveling and pulling 2 packs can damage you so much that the heal can't even keep up.

Buffing elites in damage makes Tanking as well as healing feel incredibly bad. As a Tank you feel like you are made of literal Paper.
Buffing Elites should be made in an increase in HP and mechanics, and not flat damage.
It just makes running dungeons feel worse.
