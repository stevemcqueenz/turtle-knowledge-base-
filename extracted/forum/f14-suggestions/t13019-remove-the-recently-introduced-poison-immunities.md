# Remove the recently introduced poison immunities

## Post 87217 by Gantulga (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-02-07T18:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87217#p87217 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A somewhat recent update applied poison immunity to all elementals and constructs.

While this is indeed "blizzlike", the server operated without such arbitrary restrictions since its conception, for years, and nobody was hurt as a result.
The change merely hurts DPS-wise a class that's already in need of help while also devaluing the custom Agitating Poison by making it worthless in multiple dungeons.

Since the server's vision is all about class/spec viability, introducing such a change that cripples a whole class that in no shape or form needs nerfs, just seems completely counterproductive and a step back.

Even coming from the lore/flavor perspective, those restrictions make no sense because they're entirely inconsistent. The same creatures are not immune to a myriad other effects from other classes that should only affect living organism with a nervous/circulatory system and such.
