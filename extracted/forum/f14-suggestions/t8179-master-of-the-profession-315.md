There are innate talents of races that give an increase in the possession of a profession, but there is no application for them. For example, gnomes have an increase in the profession of engineering. So why isn't there something special that can only be available to gnomes?
Simply put, I suggest thinking about technologies that require the skill of the profession 315. Moreover, now there is already a goblin - a master of alchemy.
Only it should not be something absolutely useless, otherwise it makes no sense. Innate talents should create a real advantage for the races, not just a text that is useless.
