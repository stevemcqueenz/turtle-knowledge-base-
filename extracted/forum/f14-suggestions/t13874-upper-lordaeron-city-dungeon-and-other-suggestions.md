# Upper Lordaeron City Dungeon (and other suggestions)

## Post 94672 by Mistermadigan — 2024-05-12T22:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94672#p94672 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dungeon Suggestion:
So, I was thinking about city-exclusive dungeons and how both Stormwind and Orgrimmar have 1 that is hard to access as the other faction.  But the Horde still only have 1 city dungeon while SW has 2.  That got me thinking about the ruins of Lordaeron and how they were used in the BFA opening scenario (not very good - but there is a lot of good space up there).

That's when I started thinking about a concept for a dungeon set in Upper Lordaeron.  Most likely it would be a Scarlet Monastery faction type of dungeon where Scarlet Zealots have captured the ruins and are drilling holes preparing to bomb the Undercity, perhaps they have allied with a faction of Blood Elves who are using the Scarlets to experiment with new Runestones to keep away the undead (preventing the Forsaken Abominations from coming up to wipe out the threat).

I see this as 38-44 dungeon, which could be used as a companion dungeon to a potential Scarlet Raid.  Horde would zone in via one of the UC side areas in the courtyard, Alliance would have access to an NPC at the tent at the north bank of Lake Lordamere that would send them to the instance (you know the one by the gap in UC wall that is used to jump into Tirisfal).

Other Suggestions:
 - [Give the Tallstrider/Hawkstrider pet family the Screech pet ability](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13733).  People on many occasions have complained about the pet attack noise that Tallstriders make, they make more noise than pets with the Screech ability.  It kind of makes sense.
 - [More Wands for Enchanters](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13158) to make, including epics.
 - Allow Barkskin to be usable in Bear Form.
