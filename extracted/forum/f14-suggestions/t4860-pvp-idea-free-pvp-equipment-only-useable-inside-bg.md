# PvP idea: Free PvP equipment only useable inside BG

## Post 29273 by Nightowl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-15T19:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29273#p29273 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A rebalancing of honor systems of sorts.

Lorewise the PVP system makes no sense since individuals are forced to bring their own equipment as volunteers. I mean shit Thrall, if you want me to fight the alliance scum at least sponsor some decent equipment for me to wear while we're fighting for that Lumber in WSG, for Stormgardian Resources in Arathi and for Honor of the Frostwolf clan in AV. I think it makes a lot more sense for these Military institutions to sponser equipment for new recruits, hence I propose this solution:

When you enter a new BG, you're greeted by a vendor related to the battleground; for Warsong Gulch it's a Sentinel vendor and Warsong vendor etc. Their job is to equip their new Warhost with equipment which will consist of blue PVP gear from lowest ranks and epic PvP in the middle ranks. The kicker is that while this equipment is free, you will lose it after you leave the battleground (the idea being that you return it back to the owner). As you progress in ranks, you will be able to purchase proper gear and take it with you, as a sort of "thank you for your service to the Horde/Alliance, we grant you this gear as a permanent gift". I also believe that level 60 Epic pvp weapons should get their scaled down level 60 blue pvp weapon versions so that fresh players can start fighting on equal terms from the get go.

As if this wasn't enough, I think it should be better to tie this "Call to Arms" events - with the caveat that Call to Arms is permanent with no downtime (example 1 week in WSG, 1 week in AB, 1 week in AV, then 1 week in new BG etc). This way you will create an insentive for players to play outside of current AV-only meta.

I also suggest to add more rewards to Warsong Gulch and Arathi Basin so that there will be insentive to farm those. I was thinking the best solution is to give each BG an Epic weapon most classes can benefit from (it doesn't need to be as strong as BWL/PvP gear, but lets say something on the level of early Molten Core?).

 -------------------------
I think these ideas are good and will ultimately improve both PvP and community long-term. This server is old, and on top of that it's 1x xp, so many people are discoureged from joining because they not only need to farm to 60, but also farm pvp gear if they desire to enjoy pvp which on an old server is never attractive since nobody want to face AQ or Naxx geared players as a newb. Giving fresh blood easier time to acquire competitive gear will be a nice hook for those who were on the fence on joining this community.

Feel free to debate and add your own ideas, I look forward to what you guys think

But you **are** given military equipment.
When you prove to be worthy of it.
There is quite some scarcity in this pre-industrialization era, especially when factions are in a constant war for territory and resources.
So you slay some enemies, defending the interests of your faction, first using the equipment you've gathered on your own, then you earn honor in the eyes of your faction, and thanks to that proof of honor, you are (if requested) given equipment and mounts at the "pvp vendor".
Honor isn't gold or other items you store in your inventory, it is sort of a reputation currency.
It is a very individualistic approach, which suits especially the social hierarchy of the horde.
Military ratios given once you get a training and employed by governing body, similar to how it was in ancient rome, could perhaps make sense for some of the playable races, but not all.

What would make sense if it was a profession called military service, that requires honor and reaching pvp titles, doing pvp in general and quests against opposite faction npcs, in exchange you'd be signing up for periodic military ratios of various levels that could involve equipment, food and eventually mount and perhaps get paid too.

But given equal equipment in battlegrounds would isolate them from the world, making them even more instanced. Vanilla wow had many features compared to retail wow, that try to blend the player with the NPCs, making you feel like you are part of the world, not a visitor of a themepark built for your amusement.
