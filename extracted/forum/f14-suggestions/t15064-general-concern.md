The problem is, and always was, that MC is literally a free-loot area that renders obsolete all pre-raid content, because it's much faster and effortless to get geared in MC, than it is to run even a 5 dungeon.

Like T0.5, even buffed, is still atrocious considering that you need 1) to get the T0 pieces, and 2) to involve yourself in a lenghty/costly questline, all that for gear pieces that are still inferior to just almost everything that you'd get running MC for 2h. On top of that, dungeons provide no weapon or trinket options.

So yeah, MC is an issue for the balance of end-game activities. They either need to nerf T1 content down to T0.5 levels, or buff the T0-T0.5 content to be approximatively worth MC levels.

Introducing some kind of currency in dungeons that could be traded for MC items could also be an option worth considering, to make the dungeons worth running.
