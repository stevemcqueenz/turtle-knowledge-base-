# Rogue Alt Tier Sets

## Post 128504 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-25T21:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128504#p128504 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With alternative tier sets we got to see a lot of the TWoW team and item devs show much more suitable and ideal versions of tier sets for different classes in a way that made them desirable and cosmetically appreciated to boot. They're by and large a success and I'm glad they're here.

Two classes, however, did not benefit from this change: rogue and hunter. I don't know enough about hunter to make suggestions so I'll have to leave it off, but rogue I play quite regularly and can say it was pretty disappointing not to see new versions of Nightslayer, Bloodfang, or Bonescythe sets (especially with the vocal few calling for "Bluefang" T2). A large amount of work went into making the Assassination, Combat, and Subtlety trees feel distinct and different and, in my opinion, are at least as distinct as Mage, Warrior, or Warlock trees who all have separate sets.

Each of the rogue trees could benefit greatly from targeted set bonuses and stat spreads rather than using the sets they already have. For example, subtlety is notably low on personal damage but was an attempt at more raid utility and, currently, one sub rogue in a raid gives a colossal damage boost on single targets for the rest of the raid and has their niche. Set bonuses that increase the uptime of talents like Tricks of the Trade, Dust of Disappearance, Irritating Agent, etc. would be easy wins and might allow some of these skills (especially DoD) to actually get some use. An assassination set bonus towards reducing backstab energy, possibly by a flat 5 or giving a chance at partial energy refund on crit or something, would be massive and easily fit Assassination. You could reduce the Vigor CD by 2s, give all poisons a chance at double-procs (not double effect, so mindnumbing and crippling don't warp pvp, just two procs at once for more dps), and so on.

Stat-wise, you can't really avoid that all rogues want agility, but combat tier gear could have a higher str/stam component or swap agi for attack power to emphasize raw damage as opposed to crits, possibly utilizing haste as the primary secondary stat. You could push higher agility instead of strength on an assassin set to emphasize crits, obviously increasing crit chance. Subtlety honestly could use more of a mix of stam/agi but possibly reallocated as raw avoidance like parry/dodge instead of agi, or maybe have a greater amount of hit% to maximize ability hits on enemies.

I don't know the raw numbers on stat allocation to offer suggestions, but really this could be a great boon to the class imo. Let me know what you think so it could be better fleshed out.
