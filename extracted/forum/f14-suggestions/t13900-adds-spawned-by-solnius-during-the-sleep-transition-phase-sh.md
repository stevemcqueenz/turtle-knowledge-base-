# Adds spawned by Solnius during the sleep transition phase should not be random

## Post 94829 by Borefficz (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-05-14T08:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94829#p94829 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At 55-60% health Solnius becomes untargettable and summons a few big dragonkin adds surrounded by a bunch of [Sanctum Dreamers](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=60742). The composition of the big adds spawned is completely random. Due to this unpredictable factor the difficulty of this phase will vary significantly between pulls due to the different abilities each monster has, but most importantly due to how much tankier some of these mobs are.

Here are the stats of each of the big adds Solnius can possibly spawn:

[Sanctum Supressor](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61212)
- Health : 112401
- Damage: 3,107 - 4,122
- Armor: 3,320
- Nature Resistance: 50
- Shadow Resistance: 100
[Sanctum Wyrmkin](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=60745)
- Health : 108412
- Damage: 1,687 - 2,235
- Armor: 3,315
[Sanctum Dragonkin](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=60743)
- Health : 131499
- Damage: 1,687 - 2,235
- Armor: 4,452
[Sanctum Scalebane](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=60746)
- Health : 248622
- Damage: 3,107 - 4,122
- Armor: 4,890
- Nature Resistance: 50
- Shadow Resistance: 100
And for comparison, [Solnius](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=60748)
- Health : 2391088
- Damage: 1,999 - 2,650
- Armor: 4,712
- Nature Resistance: 50
- Shadow Resistance: 100

As you can see, Sanctum Scalebanes have about 2-2.5x more effective HP against an average raid group than the other adds. Even if you ignore the mechanical aspect due to which Scalebanes have to be handled in a unique way (cleave) every single Scalebane spawn is pretty much equal to having an extra add. It will make a difference if this causes the big adds to not get killed off fast enough to not get an additional spawn wave of Sanctum Dreamers, as well as still having adds to tank/additional tanks to heal after the boss wakes up.

With good RNG (no Scalebanes) the add phase is trivial as a pug group can easily burn all big adds before the boss wakes up. However, with 2+ Scalebanes it suddenly becomes much more difficult, the only reason for that being bad luck. I don't think this is a good way to design boss fights.

I would instead have Solnius spawn the same set of adds every pull, maybe with some sort of frenzy buff if Erennius is alive so hunters don't fall asleep IRL :)
