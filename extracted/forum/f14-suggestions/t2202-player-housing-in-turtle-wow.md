## Post 13619 by Lahire (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2021-11-08T06:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13619#p13619 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

FF XIV apartment system in WoW vanilla is one of my dreams.
Instanced little house where one can put furniture.

The WoW team 2 never liked this animal crossing mechanic, they thought it was "not fun" because they came from an american crpg background. They wanted to tie it with player power progression and didn't understand the appeal of just a cosmetic system (you put furniture and that's it, the reward is to be proud of looking at your interior decorator skill). So when they came to this idea, they did the garrison: a gamified version of the idea, completely tied to player power and farming, with a forced intern progression and a minigame. Without cosmetic choice, without the real appeal of this system: expressing oneself throught interior decoration.

I never understood why Team 2, old or new alike, didn't understand the appeal of interior decoration though the Sims and Animal Crossing existed on the market for years. That's not their gamer culture, I can't see another explanation.
Now Animal Crossing (switch) has become the best selling game of 2020 and everybody is trying to copy it (Palia for example). FF XIV is bringing a full version of the idea to life (island system). If WoW retail had a marginal capacity of intellect, they would do a simple cosmetic player housing asap to come back into the market. It would have immediate and broad appeal.

Anyway, without anymore rambling ; yes this is an excellent idea. The minimal way to do it is :
 - Instanced little house, mechanically it's a 1 or 2 rooms dungeon. No capacity to change the walls or floors. You just get an apartment or house as is in the game files.
 - Set furnitures as items. When you right click on their icon in your bag, it spawns the furniture in front of you.
 - A way to retrieve into your bag the furniture flagged as your property (so a friend cannot take your table while visiting).
 - As it's mechanically a tiny dungeon, you can enter with a party to invite your friends.

That's all the system requires to work efficiently. The tech difficulty is: the game has to remember/save the house of the group leader. When the party enters the portal, the game should check the ID of the leader, then find the house linked to this ID and render it accordingly.
