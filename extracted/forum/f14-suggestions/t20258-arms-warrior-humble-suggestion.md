we all know and read complains about arms warrior being subpar compared to fury in pve (and even sometimes in pvp) while in orginal wow warrior arms pvp was a melting furnace of pain due to controlled burst and mortal strike, the power creep and turtle classes rework left arms in a stance where he is nice but not needed, in my humble opinion.

I propose the following:
While fury spec is the top damage spec (is called fury for a reason!) I propose to make arms warrior the more "tactical " aspect of the warrior class, sacrificing damage in favour of flexibility.
Here how i suggest to staff:

ARMS TALENTS
improved rend: make the talent also reduce it's rage cost and scale with 10% of warrior strenght
this would make rend reliable and useful in both pve and pvp (normally you just rend to avoid a stelth enemy to get back in there and, honetly, in pve has no use at all except for a min maxing minority
Deflection: bring parry at max talent rank to 10% only if you use a 2 handed sword (otherwise remains 5%)
Precision cut: rework in "increase your mortal strike, heroic strike and execute damage by 20/40/60% per rage point spent; additionally, victims of your mortal strike receive a armor debuff of 20%
classes in turtle became battletanks (most obvious example is paladin/shaman) so this talent make the warrior able to stick on the target and get em head on.
Boundless anger: rework to "reduce disarm durations on you by an additional 2secs and increase the duration of disarm on your enemies by 2 seconds. Additionally, when you get to 25% of your total health, regenerate a 10% of your total health over 5 seconds. this effect cannot trigger more than once per 5 minutes.

## Post 143661 by Magicknight — 2025-08-07T18:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143661#p143661 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think the Devs must've heard you as they have incorporated weapon-switching for Warriors in 1.18 !!

I feel the problem with Warriors in PvP is the limiting damage type that they do: Warriors are unique as they are the only class that do physical damage only, in every one of their specs (Arms, Berserker, Protection). They do not do any other damage type (save any temporary or permanent enchantments) - even the Rogue is not a purely physical damage class with their flagrant use of poisons (i.e. Nature damage).

Doing only physical damage presents a problem when attacking any target, as armor value reduces the amount of damage caused. The greater the value, the less damage is done (this fact is oversimplified as the damage mitigation is a curve of diminishing returns rather than a constant, but its still a fact).
ALL equipable items will come with some armor value essentially reducing the amount of physical damage a Warrior can inflict upon a target. Conversely, most equipable items DO NOT come with a resistance value attached as standard, so a Warrior has next to no damage mitigation against spellcasters (again, an oversimplification as resistance works differently when mitigating damage, but the fact remains its not as readily available as armor).

As a Warrior is purely a physical class I feel this utter rejection of all magics should be rewarded, and to this end, I also have thought of a 'humble suggestion' by way of a Warrior talent. I would have it placed in the Fury tree replacing that awful talent 'Improved Pummel' which no sane Warrior ever chooses to waste points in. Improved Pummel currently sits in the 4th tier of the Fury tree. I suggest my talent sit in the 5th tier making it inaccessible to MS Warriors. The talent I'm suggesting would hopefully give Fury a more 'sole survivor' self-reliant playstyle in both PvE and PvP.

With the new changes to PvP coming in the upcoming patch (1.18) I feel this is even more relevant, and believe it would be a measured response to the minimal survivability Warriors currently have - but we shall see:

Nullified Blood (3/3)
Melee critical strikes with abilities causes the Warrior to gain Nullified Blood, reducing spell damage and spell healing by [10/20/30]% - lasts 10 seconds.
Nullified Blood does not affect the Warriors self healing properties or that of physical healing abilities.

Notes:
 - Nullified Blood to be considered a debuff as it reduces spell healing, and also it cannot be removed by either friend or foe, or the Warrior themselves.

 - The 'does not affect the Warriors self healing properties' refers to Blood Craze and Blood Drinker, and other 'physical' healing like that of potions, bandages, HP5 and Vampirism.

 - Can only be activated on achieving a melee critical strike with an ability (yellow damage hits), and should have no cooldown so it can be refreshed within the 10 second duration.

 - Fury stances' 10% increased damage taken means that spell damage reduction would be 20%, but spell healing would still be reduced by 30%. Defensive stance would garner a whopping 40% spell reduction (due to Defensive stances 10% decrease to all damage taken), further adding to Warrior survivability, but staying in Defensive stance would produce very little damage as a result. Battle stance would get 30% spell reduction.

 - Magical healing from items and enchants would be affected by the 30% spell healing reduction while Nullified Blood was active.

 - Fury tanking would be unaffected and still balanced as taking this talent would only change the playstyle; Fury Prots are not forced to take the talent and all bosses like to attack in melee anyway (as well as casting spells), so its a question of whether the Warrior wants to sacrifice the reduced spell healing done to them over spell damage reduction.

I do love playing a Warrior and a do love playing PvP, but its so deflating when you're just considered some free honor gain from the opposition, and spend most of your time visiting the graveyard!

Please let me know what you think of my humble suggestion?
