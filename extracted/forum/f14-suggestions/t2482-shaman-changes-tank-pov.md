# Shaman Changes (Tank PoV)

## Post 15459 by Haroone — 2022-01-07T01:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15459#p15459 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello. I play Jaanu and have tanked most lvl 60 dungeons or at the very least off-tanked/dpsed most in both WoW Classic and TWoW.
These will be my thoughts on the shaman class from the enhancement dps/tank perspective.

Racials:
Orc: Seems fine enough with Blood Fury and Spirit Wolves. Seems like good dps boosts, but depending on the damage, the cd may be a bit short compared to the other Horde Shaman Racials. Not great for tanking, but the stun resistance and are skill is nice.
Troll: Berserk is really good for a dps/threat boost. Hex seems okay. I can make due with it not being a hard CC, but the cd seems too long for the lower value the debuff gives. I also hope there aren't too many non-boss enemies that are immune to it. The Troll regeneration needs a buff. I believe it should get a calculation change to give per-level regeneration or scale with hp5, if possible, or increase the spirit-healing ratio to give an actual effect. Even if it is slight.
Tauren: The base Tauren radials seem well enough. War Stomp is really useful and the extra stamina is great for tanks. The Spirit Link racial looks very lackluster and the cd seems too long. This isn't going to be useful for tanks since it only mitigates 10% of incoming damage. For every instance of 100 damage taken, only 10 damage will be redirected. This is very underwhelming. It may have some application against a group of mobs, but allies will take more damage due to less armor and still will spread minor mitigation compared to their health pool. To top this off with a 10 minute cd is overkill on a nearly useless ability.

Talent/Spell Changes:
Lightning Shield: Lightning Shield being castable on allies is negligible. If you tank, it isn't getting passed to allies and as dps, it will only have 3 stacks so higher ranks is a waste of Mana to keep maintained on the tank. If anything, starting the fight with it on the tank will give a buffer before dps pulls threat off the tank, but tanks are already getting by without it and Thorns and Ret aura are more useful with unlimited procs per duration instead of 3 stacks.
I think the Lightning Shield talent points should increase the total stacks per point instead of boosting the damage. So up to 6 procs with 3 talent points added. This, with the ability to cast on allies would benefit everyone more tanks, dps, + healers, alike.

Parry and 2H Weapons: The parry and 2h talent removals are a great thing. Parry Baseline and 2h trainable at 20 by default are great additions to allow enhancement shaman to specialize better.

Stormstrike: Stormstrike being lowered on the tree and a new rank reducing the Mana cost is a great addition. I actually avoid talenting Stormstrike because of the high Mana cost and the spell taking a debuff spot in raids. I think having Stormstrike being a personal buff to the shaman would be a better improvement as well since other shaman or balance druids can consume the Stormstrike stack with their spells which would negatively affect a shaman tank trying to raise their threat.

Ancestral Knowledge: This is the Tier 1 talent in the enhancement tree that provides 1% Mana per talent point. For reference, I have 4,000 Mana. Getting this adds 200 Mana to my total which isn't enough for a rank 5 Earth Shock, let alone any max rank Earth Shock or Lightning Shield. I think this should, at least, be increased to 2% Mana per talent point, especially since it's Mana and not Intellect. This change shouldn't become overpowered since shaman are already Mana starved.

Armor Contribution: Due to shaman wearing mail gear, shaman tanks struggle against harder hitting enemies like Alexei Barov in Sholomance or similar armor-checking bosses. I do not believe Shaman should wear plate gear, even if it's an additional talent, due to the lack of Agility. This would affect crits causing Flurry which provides a big impact to the sustained threat generation with Rockbiter Weapon and increasing the Dodge chance of the shaman tank. Currently, the talent provides 10% increased armor contribution, but shaman tanks are already at a deficit and the other tanks have similar talents along with mitigation abilities. My armor is currently around 6,600 unbuffed with the current talent. If this is negated, I'd have about 6,000 untalented. I think this should be doubled to 20% at max stacks at least or maybe 25%. This would raise my armor value to 7,200 (with 20%) or 7,500 (with 25%), a decent bonus when you consider there isn't much +Defense Mail gear. The only issue is that this would also buff dps enhancement shaman. Although, they may be better off skipping this talent to raise their dps from other spec trees. An argument can also be made to add Defense skill points to this talent as well since Mail +Defense gear is hard to come by, but I can't speak on those calculations. I also feel the increased Dodge chance from Agility gear fits a shaman more to differentiate them from warriors and paladins.

Bloodlust: Bloodlust is an interesting talent. It has a nice tie-in to WC3, but it will likely be prioritized on warriors and tanks instead of the shaman getting any benefit. Of course, not all guild will require this and a shaman should be a utility support class, but I believe the shaman should get some benefit to the ability as well, without feeling like they're being selfish or guilty for not choosing the min-max option for Bloodlust. If it isn't possible to give the target and the shaman the buff at 100% value, I think a mechanic where you choose to give a target the buff for 100% and provide a 50% buff to yourself or give just yourself the 100% buff would be a lot more fun and provide incentive to share the buff while still feel like you're doing something for yourself and the group. I understand the role as support, but sometimes it's nice to see yourself do well too, especially with the amount of totem effects you're already giving to the group.

The biggest issues with shaman tanks I've found are Mana costs and durability which are not addressed in the listed changes at all. While Shamanistic Rage from TBC would be really good.
Shamanistic Rage: After getting a melee critical strike, reduce the Mana cost of your next Shock by X%. I think something like this would be really beneficial to shaman tanks who lose a lot of Mana due to their Shock Mana costs, even after the elemental talent reduces the Mana cost by 10%. This also ties in with their play-style as they look for crits to proc Flurry already. As this has been ignored/dismissed by the devs as too strong or not worth adding, I think it will likely not be added, but would be nice and I ask for consideration in adding this as a talent.

If the armor changes are not an option, it may also be an option to add a Shield ability to increase block chance, reduce incoming damage, or increase Dodge chance for a short time on a 1 minute or 2 minute cd. This would benefit the shaman tank and less so, the dps as it would require a Shield. It doesn't have to be very strong, but something would benefit more than nothing but the Tier 1 talent. Of course, a dps shaman can equipt a Shield to use the ability, but they would still have lower armor and defense values to gain a marginal benefit.

These have been my thought on how to improve shaman tanking and even boost dps enhancement to a degree while covering my thoughts on the upcoming changes.
