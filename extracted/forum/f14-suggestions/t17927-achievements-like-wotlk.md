## Post 122215 by Silhouette (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-03-10T23:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122215#p122215 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Achievements lead to more elitism and less freedom.

I don't really want to see people asking for X achievement to invite someone in a raid, and I don't want to see people isolated because they are not given the chance to participate, as well as I don't want to see people mocking others for their lack of completion and all those things related to achievements that raised barriers and toxicity between communities.
As a completionist (currently Seeker of Knowledge+), I can say for sure that, if achievements were implemented, I would change my approach to the game and just like all the expansions starting from WotLK, that would feel like a chore having to organize my day in order to complete all sort of things in an infinite list of dumb objectives. Of course, that is not mandatory and not everyone would react that way if achievements were implemented, but this feature can definitely have an impact over the way we play and feel the game.

If I had to describe what's been a breakpoint for me on Retail WoW, I'd say achievements and dailies definitely played a major role in that, which is why I like Vanilla even more.

Another thing to consider is that there is an upcoming Vanilla+ server this year, and it is already based on the 3.3.5 WotLK client, with some WotLK abilities, achievements and more... so I don't mind Turtle focusing on another route with challenges and titles rather than implementing features too much representative of another era. Not going the achievement route is also a way to remain unique and coexist in a more healthy way than just being too similar to the other one.
