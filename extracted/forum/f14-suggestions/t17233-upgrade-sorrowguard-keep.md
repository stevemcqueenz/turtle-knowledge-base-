# Upgrade SorrowGuard Keep

## Post 117669 by Giorgioslatori — 2025-01-23T13:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117669#p117669 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sorrowguard Keep in the Swamp of Sorrows is a fantastic addition to the region but holds untapped potential. Turtle WoW team, please consider enhancing this location to make it feel more alive and rich in lore. Reflect its history—scarred by past wars, neglected by Stormwind, and standing as a weathered but determined guardian of the pass. Give it a vibe reminiscent of Helm’s Deep.

To improve the keep:
•	Rework the walls: Remove the northern and western walls, extending the eastern wall to the mountains for a more strategic design. Also, some crumbling walls, overgrown ivy, etc.
•	Add a simple tall tower/structure or a path up in the mountains for an old beacon fire to alarm Stormwind.
•	Add central features: Place a worn-out armory or a fortified governance hub in the center of the keep.
•	Expand facilities: Include a blacksmith, barracks/tavern, and first-aid tents for the ongoing conflict with the Horde in the swamp.
•	Add signs of life: Scatter supplies, abandoned carts, campfires, and soldiers preparing for battle to make the keep feel lived-in.
•	Improve accessibility: Redesign the uphill path to the keep for functionality and aesthetics. Consider whether horses should realistically reach the keep—why have stables if the path is so steep?
•	Defensive siege equipment: Old catapults or ballistae, damaged but still imposing.
•	A watchtower upgrade: With sentries constantly observing the swamp for Horde incursions. But the structure is weathered and broken.
•	Hidden areas: A secret escape route carved in the rock leading back into the swamp? (or are they trapped, like the Hornburg in lotr?)
•	The guards are wornout, tired, not full armour, etc.

On X, Turtle posted very nice artwork of the Sorrowguard Keep. Would be nice to give it this more alive feel to it.

What do others think? This could make Sorrowguard Keep an iconic, atmospheric stronghold in Turtle WoW.
