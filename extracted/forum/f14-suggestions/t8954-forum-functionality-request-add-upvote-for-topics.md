## Post 57878 by Borefficz (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-18T09:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57878#p57878 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

No harm in doing that as long as the "upvotes" have no effect on the order/visibility of threads - Reddit threads often fall victim to the echo chamber effect. One day something will be downvoted and hidden, one week later the very same thing will be the top post. It's not only the quantity of upvotes/downvotes that should matter, but their quality AKA the reasoning behind it. Which is why old school forums are the best way to discuss things, people express their opinions and present their arguments - sometimes strong, sometimes weak, sometimes stupid. Everyone can read these posts and decide for themselves if what they just read makes sense and respond accordingly (or not).
Path of Exile developers already learned their lesson about how bad Reddit-style discussion can be and reduced their activity there to the bare minimum and are mostly active on their forums now.

Forum has functionality of voting for some question, but it is disabled for regular users.
Reason is that full feedback is much better than anonymous counters.
Detailed response is valuable because it gives developers insight of *why* do we want some feature. They don't implement features exactly as players proposed, but always listen to complaints and solve players problems if possible.
Some problems don't have fast solution. Like Paladins Holy strike or AV balance or whatever else.

I have never seen +1 trains here.
