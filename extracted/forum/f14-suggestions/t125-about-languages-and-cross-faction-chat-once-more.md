# About languages and cross-faction chat once more.

## Post 698 by Dodge — 2018-10-10T05:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=698#p698 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In regards to all those topics about learning languages and/or cross-faction chat, I've got some question to anyone more familiar with emulating WoW servers.
We all agree learning other in-game languages would be a cool feature, even other languages of your own faction - for example if you start as an inexperienced Night Elf in Shadowglen how the heck you know Common language used by Humans on the other continent?
So here's my question: Is it possible on server side to modify/parse/filter chat messages sent/received by the client ?
My idea is to introduce a mechanism that will *noise* your text messages (and the messages you receive) with some randomness (substitute letters randomly one with another), dependent on the skill level of the certain language, let's say:
Someone says "Hello World!" in Gutterspeak and:
 - you're an experienced Alliance diplomat with 100/100 skill, so you received entire, not corrupted message: "Hello World!"
 - you're a Troll and you've heart a lot of gutterspeak in Orgrimmar, yet you're not quite familar with it (50/100 skill), you hear it a bit corrupted: "Hxllo Wurld!"
 - you're a human living in Southshore and you know only a few words in Gutterspeak (10/100), the message is heavily corrupted: "Hxuuo Wursa!"
 - you're a young gnome and you don't have an idea that the Gutterspeak is (0/100), you received generic horde-speak message (normal WoW mehcanism).

Or perhaps there is already an add-on for RPlayers that will make this on the client side? (this way it would be optional to use)

Other thing is how the language skill could be increased during your adventures: my idea is a long quest chain and/or randomly dropped "dictionary" pages that increase the skill.

I know it looks silly and it may be hard to implement but for me it looks quite enjoyable ;) Let me know what you think about it!
