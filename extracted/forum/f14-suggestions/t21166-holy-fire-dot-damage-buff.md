# Holy Fire Dot Damage Buff

## Post 147020 by Apimius (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-19T14:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147020#p147020 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The new change from 20% mana cost reduction on instant smites to 50% was very well done, mana issues are almost a thing of the past with enough crit.

Disc spec has another problem though, they excell at AoEing mobs efficiently dealing alot of damage while being highly survivable at the same time and shooting instant smites, their weak spot however is dealing decent/good ammount of single target DPS specifically on bosses, which stems mostly from the lacking crit% damage talent most other caster specs have.
But instead of streamlining disc into the same direction the problem could be tackled in a different way: This weak spot could easily be improved by buffing the DoT from holy fire by a GOOD ammount while adjusting the debuff priority so it doesnt get pushed off. This change would barely affect PvP as getting a holy fire off in PvP is quite hard + the debuff is magic so it can be dispelled.

Another thing i would suggest when this is done is, to lower the mana cost slightly and giving holy fire a CD of 8 seconds to balance the damage/mana buffs it has gotten, so that it is mostly a buff to sigle target boss encounters.

Thanks for reading!  sad_turtle
