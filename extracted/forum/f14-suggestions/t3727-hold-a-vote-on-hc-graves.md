# Hold a vote on HC graves

## Post 22440 by Bnana — 2022-08-15T16:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22440#p22440 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As much as I liked the idea when it was first presented, I quickly realised that I do not want these graves all over the world. I'm sure I'm not alone in thinking they look rather ugly and out of place most of the time.

With how dragons were replaced to be more lore friendly and immersive, I have a hard time seeing how it's reasonable that mobs would bury their enemies all over their camps with big gravestones, or how anyone would be buried in the middle of the street just because they died there, and some of them even hover in the air.

It really takes away from the game's aesthethics and I think we should at least hold a vote over keeping them around or not. Or at the very least give people an option to disable them.
