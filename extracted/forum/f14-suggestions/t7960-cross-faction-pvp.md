## Post 51163 by Nandiebs (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-06-24T10:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51163#p51163 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know Crossfaction in PVP would go somewhat against the RP spirit of turtle, but at this point, it is the only possible fix.

It is not only about nerfing paladins, because the whole structure of class/race distribution on the server is HEAVILY influenced by crossfaction PVE, which has strong implications on PVP and makes gameplay quality suffer. There is no semblance of an equal class distribution between factions, as it would be without crossfaction PVE.

(If you don't understand what I mean: people are not forced their choice of races by their guild's faction here. This results in e.g. Alliance having a lot more hunters, nelf and helf being very popular here, and a lot more mages than horde, while horde seem to have more warlocks and slightly more warriors)

This would have the following advantages:

no or less paladin nerfs needed
shorter queue times for alliance players
more balanced teams because players could be assigned evenly to both teams according to their pvp rank (also this would  finally give a real purpose to having the ranking system in the first place)

I reckon cross-faction BGs might be slightly detrimental to average Horde queue times, but I'm very unconvinced it's as cut-and-dry bad for Horde as you make it seem.

Let's consider the current situation. Assume there are 100 Alliance players in queue and the Horde queue is empty. Then you join with your Horde character. 2 outcomes are possible:
A) There is space in a currently running BG. You instantly join.
B) All currently running BGs are full. There would be a full Alliance team to play against, but you have to wait for more Horde players (either new ones or from finished BGs) so you meet the minimum team-size to open a new BG.

Now let's consider cross-faction. Those 100 Alliance players from before have matched against each other, so the queue is completely empty. Then you join with your Horde character. 2 outcomes are possible:
A) There space in a currently running BG. You join as soon as a single other player from any faction gets in queue.
B) All currently running BGs are full. You join as soon as queuing players from any faction combined meet the threshold for creating two teams.

So I think scenario A is undeniably a downside, but come on - it's minuscule. How long will it take for *any other player* to join queue? It might not be instant anymore, but pretty close to it.
As for B it should actually shorten queue times for Horde because you can join a new BG that gets filled up with majority Alliance players. You don't have to wait for players of your faction to finish up other BGs.

I think Horde players are right to be weary about the change. But I suspect the downside will be hardly noticeable. I'm open to discussion when it comes to the traditional BGs (they all heavily tie into faction flavor), but for Blood Ring cross-faction is absolutely a must in my opinon.

Well, as stated in previous posts, there are *coding* reasons as to why cross-faction PvP wouldn't work.

In regard to Battlegrounds, something like Blood Ring would be pretty easy to manage.  Remove teams, set it to PvA, and you're done.  No messy faction stuff to worry about.  However, all other Battlegrounds would need some pretty significant retooling.  As the game does not presently support Alliance characters interacting with Horde NPCs in any meaningful way, or vice-versa, it wouldn't be productive for, say, a Human to gain reputation with The Defilers or an Orc to earn reputation with the Silverwing Sentinels.
At the very least, I'd think Turtle Team would need to implement something like with Booty Bay and the Bloodsail Buccaneers, or the two Centaur tribes in Desolace – where earning reputation with one faction drastically decreases reputation with the other.  This would mostly affect Alterac Valley, where it would theoretically be possible to be an Alliance player who can walk right up to Drek'Thar and /spit in his face, but I think it would just plain make sense for your "normal faction" to treat you like a traitor if you play for the other side.

In regard to World PvP… I honestly don't know why a PvA command hasn't been added.  I'm not talking about a "War Mode" analog or "Wanted Mode", or whatever.  I mean a manual command to enable PvA like in Gurubashi Arena.  It's not like other players would **have** to participate.  The only problem I can see is that how would you award Honor for killing someone of your own Faction when the Honor System is very much focused on "Alliance vs. Horde"?  It makes *sense* in the context of the Blood Ring or Gurubashi Arena, where it's just a big, gladiatorial event, but just out and about in Elwynn or the Badlands?  Maybe not so much.

I think it would be quite a lot of work to make PvA viable in anything outside of certain zones and Battlegrounds.  And that's probably why it hasn't been done.

And yes, I know I've been a little **everywhere** on this thread.  A lot of people have made a lot of good points I hadn't considered.

Re-balance PvP / Battlegrounds Accomplished through a series of class balancing patches, fixing pathing and "exploits" in Battlegrounds, making Alterac Valley a perfect mirror so everyone / no one has a shortcut, et cereta.

 **Speculative Pros:**
- PvP would become more a contest of skill rather than one of min-maxing and Tiers.
(No more Paladins one-shotting same-level cloth-wearers, for example.)
 **Speculative Cons:**
- Puts a little too much "plus" in "Vanilla Plus", which kind of defeats the point of Vanilla.

- *Boy*, would this take a long time to do.

Nerf Paladins / Buff Shamans I said "buff Shamans" as a joke, but people have been **screaming** for Paladins to be nerfed to oblivion for a while.

 **Speculative Pros:**
- Each side would both have an unstoppable force and/or immovable object.
 **Speculative Cons:**
- Each side would both have an unstoppable force and/or immovable object.
(This isn't a mistake.  It's a pro *and* con.  Basically, it just plain wouldn't matter which faction you played in PvP, anymore.)
- This could have negative effects on PvE combat and especially Dungeons.

 
Make the Alliance / Horde Forces factions Neutral to everyone This ties into the very first idea pretty heavily.

 **Speculative Pros:**
- It would allow either side to gain reputation with either side's Forces sub-factions.
- Allows both main factions access to faction-specific gear.

- This would make the "lore" of the various Battleground conflicts confusing and break immersion.
