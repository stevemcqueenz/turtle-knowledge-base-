Hey,

i want to gather all 3 changes i suggested for the smite priest spec here so its easier to see everything in one post and to have a better overall discussion for this spec.

First suggestion is reworking blessed recovery for mana sustainability: [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4443](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4443).

The second is lowering the cooldown from sun's embrace spell: [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4306](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4306).

And the third is lowering the mana cost from grace of the sunwell: [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4755](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4755).

If you have any other ideas for this spec you can mention it here, but i think with these changes smite priest would get its 1 big issue fixed: mana problems.

What do you think?
