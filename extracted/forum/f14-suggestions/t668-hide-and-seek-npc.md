# Hide and Seek NPC

## Post 3413 by Qixel (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-07-11T05:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3413#p3413 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Each day/week/certain period of dev discretion, the Hide and Seek NPC (henceforth referred to as Has) will appear at a random spot in a random zone. When they do, a message will go out across the server, letting players know the game has begun. The first player to locate Has and talk to them will recieve a prize, and a message will be broadcast to all players both letting them know the game is over, and congratulating the seeker on their win.

As time passes and no one finds Has, hints may be given through server messages. For example, if Has has hidden themselves at Thunder Falls in Elwynn, the server message may draw reference to them 'Hiding at a waterfall' or 'Enjoying the forest scenery'. Each of these hints alone would not necessarily mean Thunder Falls, but as time passes with them still undiscovered, a second hint could be given, making the location more obvious.

I don't know what the prize would be, or how feasible such a thing would be, I just think a server-wide game of hide and seek could be really fun aha.

## Post 3506 by Zhevra — 2019-07-23T08:19:00Z

Slight nit-pick from me, but I don't like heavy use of server messages. Look at how Blizzard does events like the Gurubashi Arena chest and Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza--NPCs shout and can be heard from anywhere in the zone the event takes place.

Of course, if the NPC can hide in any zone in the world, alerting only the players that are in the zone at the time would probably mean they would keep that information to themselves so they'd have a higher chance of getting the prize.

A solution to this could be to have the zone the NPC is hiding in be announced in all capital cities by an NPC at a well-known time(-interval?).

To encourage teamwork and participation, the reward could scale based on how many non-afk players are in the zone at the time, and how much time has passed from the time the event started till the NPC was found.

The first player who interacts with/finds the NPC will get the first place reward. For some amount of time after the NPC has been found, other players will get to claim a participation reward.

To prevent scenarios where the first place winner decides to keep the NPC's location a secret, the NPC could teleport to an inn or some other known location so people can claim their participation reward before the time expires.

P.S.: The NPC we have to find is obviously a lost baby turtle.

Edit: I think I lost the plot a bit with the teamwork and participation reward thing. And announcing the zone is probably a bit too much.

I think this is more in line with the original idea: at a scheduled time, NPCs in Orgrimmar and Stormwind shout a hint at the start of the event. First person to find the NPC wins—now the part I’m not sure about is how do you announce to all the participants that the NPC has been found without using server messages? Announcing the exact zone gets around this, but takes away much of the puzzle element.

I also wonder if it would be an issue that horde and alliance start at different locations—it seems unfair that one faction starts closer to the location than the other. Perhaps the event starts in a neutral city instead.

Anyway, I like this idea a lot! I just have a lot of thoughts/concerns about the details.
