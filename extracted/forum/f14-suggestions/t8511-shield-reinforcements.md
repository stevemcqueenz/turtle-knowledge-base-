# Shield reinforcements.

I want to talk about adding shield reinforcements as mid to late game blacksmithing recipes.
Iron shield plating. Cost, 6 iron bars.  Adds 25 shield block value.
Mithril shield plating. Cost, 6 mithril bars. Adds 35 shield block value.
Thorium shield plating. Cost, 6 thorium bars. Adds 50 block value.
Aracanite shield plating. Cost 4 arcanite bars. Adds 75 block value.

Adding the recipes as random world drops seems appropriate since that's how should spikes work.

But the real beneficiary would probably be aoe farming paladins. Paladins have a 30% increased block value talent. Along with a major blockchance increase.  Adding 97 damage block on every single block is easily multiple thousands of points of damage reduction on a single pull.
