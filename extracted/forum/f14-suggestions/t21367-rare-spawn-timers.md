# Rare Spawn Timers

## Post 148093 by FrankFankledank (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-25T02:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148093#p148093 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I did the Broken Tooth farm back in the day, on two servers even. It was an arduous process that took up a good chunk of a day but it was just enough to not get overwhelming. Broken Tooth is listed as an 8 hour respawn in pfquest, but with the unnecessarily obtuse way rare spawn timers works it's nowhere near guaranteed that. Okay, that's fine, don't want dedicated farmers getting the pinpoint timing down. What isn't fine is the base spawn times of some of these rares, both for Hunter taming and just for acquiring certain niche items. I'm sitting here going on my 11th hour waiting on Duskskitterer's spawn as I type this, a rare listed at a 12 hour respawn on the db, an extra 50% over the already borderline Broken Tooth's spawn. I could have went to farm Broken Tooth, arrived just as it was getting killed, decided to wait around the entire respawn timer and still have it in my stable before this spider. Greth'eer, the alternative rare spider if you like speeds that start in 1.00 has a respawn timer of 50 HOURS. This is an untennable model that requires unhealthy amounts of no-breaks dedication or inordinate luck to actually reliably work with, put all rare spawns on the 8 hour timer, I guarantee you our economy will survive the implications.
