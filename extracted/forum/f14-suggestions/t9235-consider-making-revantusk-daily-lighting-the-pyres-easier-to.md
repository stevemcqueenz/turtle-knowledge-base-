# Consider making Revantusk Daily "Lighting the Pyres" easier to complete

## Post 60147 by Specialweek — 2023-08-30T20:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60147#p60147 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

There's been a fix recently where the pyres in "Lighting the Pyres" (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80301>) now have a cast time and cannot be clicked multiple times.

This changes makes a lot of sense, but I think it had the unintended side-effect of making this quest significantly harder to complete when it's busy. Often there's multiple people in the area trying to nab one of the pyres.

I can think of the following suggestions that I think would be a improvement for this quest:
 - Add more pyres deep inside the cave. There's only 2 deep inside, I think adding a few more would be great.
 - Reduce the number of pyres required to complete the quest. If adding new pyres is a challenge, then simply reducing the amount required might have a similar effect.
 - Add more dailies. Definitely the most amount of work, but if there are more dailies, then I think it's likely that people spread out and the pyres are less contested.

I hope this makes sense. I think it's great that there's a daily quest that takes _some_ amount of time, but having to compete with others also looking to complete the quest is just no fun for anyone, and I don't see the point.
