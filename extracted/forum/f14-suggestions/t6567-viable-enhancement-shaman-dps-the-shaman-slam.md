# Viable Enhancement Shaman DPS: THE SHAMAN SLAM!

## Post 41408 by Hazhap — 2023-04-13T15:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41408#p41408 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After long pondering on a way to make enhancement shaman a decent choice as a dps to bring in group pve content without making them overly strong in pvp, I have come to the conclusion that such an ability was right before our eyes this whole time. SLAM! or rather, its shaman cousin.

This is what ive come up with.
Nimbus Blow:
 2 sec. cast, X% mana cost
Deliver a blow, causing weapon damage and additional X nature damage (this would be a separate instance of damage)
Consecutive uses of this ability lower the cast time by .5 seconds, stacking twice for a total cast time reduction of 1 sec.

I haven't hashed out the specific scaling and cost of this spell as of yet, but I think a few generalizations can be made.
 1.) the nature damage flat value should be small, but should have pretty good spell power scaling as to incentivize hybrid gearing.
 2.) the spell cost should probably be pretty low considering the mana pool of most enhancement shaman. It should not be without consideration, however. I think the cost of a theoretical rotation with this ability included should put them somewhere in the same vein as a hunter's rotation mana costs. Enough to have need of the relevant consumables, but not so much that it is untenable.

Let me know what you think.
