Since Dun Agrath, Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility, Ironforge Airfield, Bael Hardul, etc have flight paths, could you please consider giving Horde side the equivalent with Sparkwater Port, Amani'Alor, Glenshire/Tirisfal Uplands, Powder Town, etc? Maybe a direct flight path to Blackstone Island as well rather than the separated flight machine outside of the normal network of windrider flights?
