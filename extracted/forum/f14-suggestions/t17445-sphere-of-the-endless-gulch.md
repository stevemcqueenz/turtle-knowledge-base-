# Sphere of the Endless Gulch

## Post 119104 by Sparkwens — 2025-02-05T22:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119104#p119104 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sphere of the Endless Gulch, the new trinket from the world boss Clack'ora doesn't work like it says.

 *Equip: Your landing damaging spells have a 20% chance to grant you Wisdom of the Mak'aru. When you gain Wisdom of the Mak'aru 8 times, you enter enlightened state, increasing casting speed by 20% for 12 seconds.*

Now the broken part is the part where it says, "*landing damaging spells have a 20% chance to grant you Wisdom of the Mak'aru*". This is blatantly false, because there is hidden cooldown placed on this effect, as confirmed by Turtle WoW developers, after the 20%, "Wisdom of the Mak'aru" effect occurs, there is a 3 second cooldown before it can occur again. So the 20% chance is actually 10% for 3 second cast time spells. And about 6.6% for Arcane Missiles spell for mages, that hit every ~1 second.

By comparison, the vanilla blizzard ring *Wrath of Cenarious* works just like its explained. Every single hit from a spell has a 5% proc chance to trigger the below effect, without any hidden cooldown that is not stated in the items specs placed into it.

 *Equip: Gives a chance when your harmful spells land to increase the damage of your spells and effects by 132 for 10 sec.*

What I request is that either this trinket's description be changed to reflect how it currently works, or that it gets its hidden 3 second icd removed to fit its description. Thank you.
