# Crafting Faction

## Post 175455 by basednoob (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-04-16T09:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175455#p175455 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

If you choose to focus on leveling up crafting and gathering professions while you level up, you can quickly lose quest reputation and such because crafting and gathering take a lot of time. So my idea is that maybe there could be a crafting faction that you get reputation for when you make things and gather materials. When you reach higher reputation, this faction's guild houses could open crafting quests that can give both good xp and rewards.

This could also give new life to many useless recipes that people would never craft or get any use from if you could get something nice out of it with new crafting quests.

I'm all for more class and profession quests. However, my concern is that this may negatively spike some budget crafted gear from the auction house. Kind of like how the Warsong Supplies quest skyrockets the cost of Deadly Blunderbuss, which is a cheap engineering craftable. If this were to be implemented, it should take that into account.

I'm also against it using raw materials like ores and herbs, because their auction house prices are already quite inflated

I thought that when you go to the crafting guild house they could have a board with many different quests on it that people could take. For example quest: after you pick it up from the board it instructs you to talk to a representative from the crafting faction he gives you several pieces of armor that a customer needs specific low level enchants on. Once you have performed these enchantments the equipment is delivered back to the representative who gives you your reward. When the quest is taken from the board it disappears and is replaced with a new random quest. So it's first come first served.

Another cool thing is that if you choose to change professions later on, you don't lose any reputation to the crafting faction, so that can help provide another layer of progression.
