> **Noshu wrote: Sun Oct 22, 2023 3:37 pm**
> Add rating and cosmetic rewards as well as incitement to keep queueing after exalted

I strongly advise against a 'rating' for Blood Ring. If you do this, it gives the players a stick to beat the devs with in terms of class/spec representation at the higher ratings. In other words, there will be never ending cries regarding the balance of classes/specs.

The retail version of the game went down this path. In my opinion, PvP should never be balanced around such small numbers (1v1, 2v2, or even 3v3). Trying to balance this is an impossible task and will give them such a headache. They should only be considering balance for mass-PvP (e.g. Battlegrounds or World PvP).

I think PvP servers thrive when there is an active World PvP scene (and to a lesser extent, Battleground scene). If you add enticements (e.g. a rating system), it detracts from this because people become hyperfixated on improving their arena rating because it, apparently, indicates who the 'best' PvPers are...
