# Simple Melee Hunter thoughts

## Post 53373 by Aegaellian — 2023-07-14T02:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53373#p53373 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Clearly the devs have some idea of Melee hunter being some kind of thing because Improved Predator Aspects and Aspect of the Wolf exist. It's not quite there yet but two small changes could make the spec more interesting to play. Improved Aspect of the Monkey talent could give a chance to give an attack speed buff on dodge while using Aspect of the Monkey like the one gained from attacking while in Improved Predator Aspects, potentially stacking together to incentivize an interesting sort of Aspect weaving playstyle, since Melee hunter is currently pretty boring with only Raptor Strike and Wing Clip/proc abilities to spam. Secondly, giving Hunter some form of small Melee threat/taunt/fixate ability would also be interesting since it would help facilitate Counterattack and Mongoose Strike as well as the aforementioned change to Aspect of the Monkey as well as giving an off-meta tank Hunter spec something to play with, like how Rogues got their threat poison to allow them limited tanking.
