## Post 142466 by Thearturq — 2025-08-01T09:39:00Z

Good day,
There is a major issue brewing within the servers which will lead to their slow deterioration. There are way too many level 60s just standing around in Stormwind, for example, and flexing their gear instead of doing what World of Warcraft is made for: raiding the Horde and vice versa. Additionally, Alliance and Horde players can team up, which COMPLETELY goes against the very foundations of WoW (once again, Alliance battling the Horde). As a result, people are losing their purpose within the game, which will lead to more and more players leaving the server, and you in turn will lose players and therefore, money. The thing Turtle WoW truly lacks is not new, shiny gear from retail, and new mounts, but the constant bloodthirsty battle between the two factions. The best solution to this is to discontinue allowing Alliance and Horde players to team up and interact with each other, and to rekindle the war spirit within the players themselves. Additionally, you could (and probably should) do a full character wipe, so that people must go through the Warcraft journey once again, but this time in the right spirit. If there is no need to grind and fight for Azeroth, then deviations will take place and Turtle WoW will become nothing more than "TikTok" (I think you would agree with me that you are way more than just TikTok). In order for the players not to just lose their spent money, you could simply collect all the paid items and mounts e.t.c. and allow the players to reassign these items upon character creation. Furthermore, you should add more raid quests (such as killing leaders of the Horde, disrupting war preparations e.t.c.), and introduce mounts and gear that can ONLY be gained from these raids. The only case in which the Alliance and Horde should be allowed to team up is to defeat a common enemy (such as the Fel, as seen in the Warcraft movie).
I hope you will take action in order to allow Azeroth to remain Azeroth, and for players to enjoy what is truly the very essence of World of WARcraft: War!

Thank you,
TheArturq

p.s. I am very grateful for you providing an alternative to the soft, stinky and slimy rathole which is Retail WoW.
