# New Arrow Skins (and Possible Effects) for Hunters

## Post 164050 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-30T12:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164050#p164050 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello,
I’d like to suggest an idea to improve hunter flavor and visual identity:
![👉](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f449.svg) adding new types of arrows with unique skins and possibly light effects.
Arrow ideas
![🔥](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f525.svg) Flaming Arrows – fire visuals, small DoT or situational bonus
![☠️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2620.svg) Poisoned Arrows – light poison or debuff (healing reduction, stats, etc.)
![❄️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2744.svg) Frost Arrows – icy visuals, slight slow effect
![🌌](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f30c.svg) Void / Arcane Arrows – dark or purple visuals, interaction with mana or resistances
![⚡](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/26a1.svg) Lightning Arrows – electric visuals, limited proc or chain effect
Possible implementation
Cosmetic-only skins or
Very light and balanced effects (PvE / PvP), unlocked via:
ammo types
crafting (Engineering, Leatherworking, Alchemy)
quests or reputation rewards
Visual effects shown mainly on impact to avoid screen clutter
Why this could be nice
Enhances immersion and hunter fantasy
Adds customization without breaking vanilla gameplay
Fits well with Turtle WoW’s custom but lore-friendly philosophy
I’d love to hear your thoughts:
![👉](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f449.svg) cosmetic only or light effects?
![👉](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f449.svg) any other arrow ideas?
Thanks!
