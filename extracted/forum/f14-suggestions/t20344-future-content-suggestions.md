# Future content suggestions

## Post 140109 by Tytoofthemistt — 2025-07-17T17:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140109#p140109 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi Turtle devs and community,

First off, thank you all for the continued love and care you pour into this beautiful project. The world you've built offers such a refreshing and thoughtful take on Vanilla, and it’s exciting to think about what could come next. I’d love to offer a few suggestions for future content that aim to stay within the spirit of Vanilla while gently expanding it in creative ways:

Player & Guild Housing

One of the most-requested features in WoW's history, player/guild housing could fit naturally into the Turtle WoW philosophy of slow, immersive gameplay. Rather than modern instanced housing, imagine something like:

Small plots or phased houses in areas like Elwynn, Duskwood, Barrens, etc.

Customizable décor via questing or profession crafting.

Guild halls that reflect progress or achievements—trophies, banners, NPCs, etc.

It could be optional, cosmetic, and not power-based, staying true to Vanilla while enhancing community identity and roleplay.

New Class Concept: Monk or Puppeteer (Ninja-Inspired)

These two ideas would add flavor and fantasy without disrupting Vanilla balance:

Monk – A martial class that channels inner energy for bursts of movement, healing focus, or spiritual attacks. Think leather-wearing, fast-hitting melee with mystical utility—rooted in ancient troll or kaldorei traditions.

Puppeteer – A ranged support/control class that uses animated dolls, constructs, or battlefield tools. Could lean into dark troll witch doctors or gnomish tinkerers depending on faction. A mix between a hunter and a mage, with high flavor and positioning strategy.

Both could use existing itemization and lore-friendly visuals while offering something new to master.

Protection Warrior - Shout-Based Utility
Warriors are iconic, but Protection could use a bit more identity in group utility. Expanding on the “shout” fantasy could be both flavorful and practical:

New warcries that either buff allies or debuff enemies—things like:

"Battle Roar" – Increase crit or haste briefly.

"Stonewall Shout" – Nearby allies take reduced damage.

"Dread Howl" – Lowers enemy armor or resistances.

Could be baked into talents or unlocked via class quests—keeping it subtle and lore-friendly.

Show Some Love to Combat Animations One of the underrated joys of Turtle WoW is how alive the world feels. I’d love to see:

Slight touch-ups to auto-attack and ability animations for underrepresented specs (like Feral, Ret, or Prot).

Even just a few smoother or punchier animations can add a lot to the immersion.

Optional toggles or simple updates that enhance the feel of combat without modernizing it too much.

It’s a small thing, but it goes a long way toward making each strike and spell feel good.

These ideas are offered with deep respect for what you’ve built—Vanilla-inspired, immersion-rich, and mindful of lore and scope. I hope they resonate and spark some discussion.

Thank you again for everything you do.
Warm regards,
TytooftheMistt
