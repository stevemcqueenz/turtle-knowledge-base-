## Post 82533 by Doomywoomy — 2023-12-24T07:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82533#p82533 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was told that because of "trolling" and "size abuse" the mechanical pounder mount (unique to turtle wow) is no longer ridable in cities.

This is a very simple, solvable solution, people who are trolling are reported, those repeat offenders are timed out, etc.

Why not only punish the trolls? Not every single person who has EARNED this mount.

This mount takes less for others and plenty more runs for some, but mine specifically, my farm took 70+ resets of 1 boss in gnomer, then retrieving 3 quest items from 3 different dungeons.

This is a special mount meant to be shown off once earned.  I am obviously biased, but this is a really sad issue, and it's more sad how compelled i feel to write this forum post, on a classic wow private server...which i love.

In conclusion, punish trolls, not the entire population, free the pounder.
