# Alter unlocking transmog appearances

## Post 130716 by Kiyaru — 2025-05-12T14:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130716#p130716 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, to unlock an appearance, it requires you to equip the item. I assume this is largely to avoid having to think about what happens if cloth people get leather+ item and the likes, they shouldn't get those appearances.

The flipside to this, however, is that it blocks people from getting some interesting transmorgs that are locked behind profession requirements (or class). A lot of unique appearances lay in items that require tailoring(xxx), engineering(xxx), etc. Once you're maxed out in profs and have rare raid recipes, you're not going to simply drop them to level another prof for fashion. You're not going to lose all your rare raid recipes and unlocks just for that, it's not really an option

My suggested change:
 - Have a NPC that changes these profession locked items for level 1 statless variants without requirements to unlock the tmog.

This has the added benefit that it might give those professions more things to sell, as fashion will never not sell. While retaining the unique advantage of that profession as the statted items are still for them alone. I see it as a win-win for the fashion community and crafting community. More things to sell, more fashion to utilize
