# Builds and ideas

## Post 148507 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-26T23:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148507#p148507 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

The below are not a set of suggestions per say, but wanted to share to which direction I'd push certain builds.
I'm by no means an expert but wanted to share my vision, broken down to classes, based on what I saw and what I think.
 *Will skip some builds and classes because I have no opinion about them.*

 **Rogues**

 *Assassination:*
I like the current route.
Would add 1 or 2 raid utility poisons probably (as a comparison, think about warlock curses).
This would turn assa into a decent DPS with a cool raid utility.

 *Combat:*
Somewhat more damage, unless it messes with PVP.
A build with this low utility should perform consistently really well at DPS meters.

 *Sub:*
Some damage buff (you shouldn't have to humiliate yourself to be able to give buffs; many hybrids dish out buffs and perform well).
A utility kit regarding threat manipulation.
For example: a deep sub talent granting the ability to select a target and to transfer x% of the target's threat onto the rogue.

 **Hunters**

 *BM:*
Saw the strider party buff and I think it is a good direction forward.
Give more pets interesting party buffs and give BM a talent deep down which makes the pet buffs stronger.
BM could become a unique utility DPS which can fit many groups based on the pet in use.
For example: turtles could have an armor increase party buff, let's say 5%.
With the deep BM talent, this could become 10%.
The numbers are random, just to convey the general idea.

 *MM:*
Lone wolf talent, pets are rather an annoyance to them in instances.
Changing abilities scaling by spell power (like volley) to scale by RAP, maybe via talent.
MM's are supposedly sharpshooters who by design perform better if the target is injured.
Would create an arms warrior, feral cat and MM hunter buff-triangle based on bleeds.
For example a cat could have added haste on a bleeding target (a form of bloodlust), arms higher damage, MM higher crit chance. Just ideas, the goal is to create new, flavorful utility connections between builds and bleeding is underutilised for that.

 *Survival:*
More pet+character interaction.
Let's be real, the melee hunters are unique because they perform melee together with a pet.
This should be reflected via some abilities and talents.
For example a talent giving player haste after pet crit and pet haste after player crit.
A kill command lookalike would also be cool for this joint fight fantasy.

 **Druids**

 *Moonkin:*
Those moonkins really want some spell crit. Give them some via talents.

 *Cat:*
This bleed idea I described earlier.

 *Tree:*
Half-joking but would add extra fire damage taken in tree form.
Would keep HoT trees as they are but would push non-HoT healers a bit so resto druids don't feel locked out from a guild roster so easily.

 **Warriors**

Arms as described earlier.

 *Prot:*
I'm not saying that every tank should be good at everything. No.
But recently I'm either tanking or healing and when I'm healing I see the differences between tanks.
Scale thunderclap threat please, warrior tanks in dungeons seem like a joke compared to the other options, and I'm not kidding.
Paladin glues the mobs to itself, dru kinda does something similar. Even shaman does it. Warrior in comparison seems extremely ill-suited for dungeon tanking unless the group knows exactly how to play which is rare in random LFTs.
Also, scale their threat end-game a bit. Not too much but... a bit.

 **Pala**

 *Holy:*
Honestly, just decide which identity you want for them and turn the talent tree into a full and useful toolkit.
Ranged flash light spammer? Okay, but now crits don't fully refund mana, they might need some push.
Melee healer? They need many more fine-tunes to become fully enjoyable and valid.

 *Prot:*
Aoe threat is extremely potent, would consider lowering consec threat or damage a bit.
I'm not hating on them, it just seems too OP now that pala tank is not a one trick pony but a valid build.

 **Priest**

 *Holy:*
I like them but champion talent could be further fine-tuned.
Remove the battle rez from the priest. Give another priest-like option instead.

 *Shadow:*
Shadow nova and better threat/damage ratio, please.

 **Shaman**

 *Resto:*
Chain heal is still too good, 2 sec cast time is a mistake.
Would slightly nerf chain heal while buffing talents making healing wave and lesser healing wave better.
Remove chain heal from healing way. Make the usage of LHW and HW more appealing.

 *Enh:*
Mostly fine but the WF hit threat is not really solved by a generic increase of threat reduction.
In the elemental weapons talent, give WF weapon the ability to decrease added procs' threat.
Also, some mana problems, probably LS scaling from water shield buff.

 *Enh tank:*
Now that mobs hit harder, def CD is a valid concern. Also early game mitigation and nature resistance.
There has to be a way to reliably use the tanking abilities without griefing threat numbers at certain places.
