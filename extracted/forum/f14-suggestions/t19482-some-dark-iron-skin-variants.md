# Some Dark Iron Skin Variants?

## Post 132567 by Wavedash — 2025-05-22T20:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132567#p132567 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just a small request.

We have a few variants of the Wildhammer skins for dwarves, with different kinds of paint/markings. For Dark Irons we only have a single skin without any variants. If possible I'd really love to have Dark Iron options, very similar to the Wildhammer options we already have.

That is, no substantial differences beyond a recoloring of the markings to make them fit the Dark Iron color palette (i.e. black instead of blue).

Selfishly my impetus here is I am a recent retail refugee from the Moonguard RP server and would love to RP a younger version of my main there. That being said, the timeframe covered in Turtle WoW is arguably the most compelling part of the Dark Iron story arc, especially for people that want to RP as political dissidents. This is a minor thing but it'd really let people add a little more distinctiveness to their characters.

This is a lot easier to show rather than tell. Since I have my former main as a reference, I'll attach their look here alongside two from Turtle WoW: the DI skin for reference + an equivalent already in game that could be recolored.
