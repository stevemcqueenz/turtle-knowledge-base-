# Hunter pets need more changes

## Post 152021 by Nalos — 2025-09-17T04:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152021#p152021 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In patch 1.18 Scars of the Past, hunters got a decent upgrade on the pet however it didn't really solve the main issues of bad design that hunter pet had since early WoW development, you might think what kind of issues? simple, entire pet families being a must use or completely useless, but the main complain here will be focused on two sections: the pet **abilities** and the **modifiers**.

The modifiers are the raw stats a hunter pet might have, this is separated between armor, health and damage. There are 3 types of pet specs based on those stats: defense which is focused on tanking, offense which is focused on doing high dps, and general (cunning) which is a medium usually these pets have been good in PvP or for utility.

The abilities are the spells that a pet can use, this can be claw, bite or it's special, the problem is with the special abilities being useless in a lot of pet and causes inconsistencies that i will explain later.

The problem here is that the pets stat are still flawed and unequal in this scenario, you have offense/general pets which tanks better than defense pets, defense pets that deals more dps than ferocity pets, offense pets with tanking spells? This is due to their abilities not matching their spec, for example let's see the most obvious case: The Scorpion, this is a defense pet, with the next modifiers: -6% Damage, +10% armor, 0% health, and yet this pet is the one that deals the most dps, why? because of it's ability which stacks poison until it start to deal a high amount of damage, much higher than pets like cat, wind serpent or cobras. Then let's see the other examples, carrion birds, bats, and owls are better for tanking than defense pets due to their ability of screech which holds aggro in area something that tenacity pets cannot do except Gorilla (which is not very a good case because of it's long CD), then you have useless pets like crabs which have a negative health modifier, and it cannot hold decent aggro like bats for example, then you have the wolf a cool looking pet that's scream high dps in appearance but in reality it's a meme "general" pet with a completely useless ability that cost a lot of focus and increases less dps than bite.. Then you have the fox which is also useless by obvious reasons.. etc etc..

How this can be solved? easy, first we need to have stable pet roles, this can be divided between three, tenacity (tank), ferocity (dps), and cunning (utility), those roles need to have a set of rules to be stable, first ferocity pet should always have higher than average damage modifiers (more than 8% i would say) but low on armor and health, tenacity pets should have low damage, but to compensate they should have high armor and health modifiers, cunning pets would be in the middle for hunters who want a pet for PvP or utility in lvling or pve. the specific pet roles should have a specific ability

What changes should be made? In my opinion some pets need their roles to be switched, right now it's unequal, as tank pets have lower **general modifiers** than dps ones, some don't even make sense like Bear with -9% damage modifier when it should be one of the defense/tenacity pet with the higher damage count.

I would fix this mess with the next modifiers + fixes to their abilities and i'll explain why:

Cat: While it might seem unfair that it has a very high dps modifier compared to the rest of ferocity ones, you need to know that it's special ability: Prowl, it's only good for **short term damage**, and it's only used one time per combat, in order to not totally suck on PvE encounters i given it a bit more of damage to compensate that.

Raptor: Might not bet the best on raw damage, but it ability Savage Rend pays for it, it should be a bit higher than cat at long term

Wolf: Totally deserves to be ferocity, while it's damage might not the best in ferocity, this pet should be able to compensate with an **actually decent buff**, this could be an increase of attack power and critical chance for some time.

Wind Serpent: While it's damage 10% seems to be low compared to the top ones, this pet does non-physical damage (Lightning Breath) which compensates it's low modifier a lot.

Cobra: I think the cobra should be the non-physical counterpart of the Wind Serpent but for long term damage, the problem is that the ability it's given to the scorpion (a defense pet..) instead, give the cobra poison the same effect as scorpid sting, and it should be fixed

Tallstrider: I think being a ferocity pet makes more sense to it's design, after all those are azerothian equivalent of  terror birds, since it now haves [Strider Presence](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=36531) it could be a good pick for hunters who struggle with mana (survival), this would be perfect for leveling survival hunters, i also think it ability could increase the spirit of the group to have more synergy with other classes like priest.

Owl: Since eagle models are already tameable, this family should be renamed to Bird of Prey already, it should be the  cunning pet with highest damage to fit it's theme, it also needs an ability change, [something like this](https://www.wowhead.com/cata/spell=91644/snatch) , good for PvP against meeles and maybe for some pve encounters with humanoids npcs.

Hyenas: To be honest,  [Packleader](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=36532) did not paid justice to this niche pet, i think it should be a moderate damage cunning pet, with a more fitting ability [like this](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=3150), after all, they're scavengers, it would be a good utility pet reducing enemies stats.

Spiders: They're already fine in my opinion, their [new spell](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=36533) fits perfectly, good usage for pvp, the only change would be a bit more of root seconds.

Bat: If Owls are the ones who deals with meeles, then bats would be the equivalent for [dealing with casters](https://www.wowhead.com/cata/spell=50519/sonic-blast), i would rather prefer a silence btw, a stun might be a bit op, but you get the point.

Carrion Birds: Moderate damage, not to big, this pet needs a new ability, i think an AoE slow debuff will be good use for PvP, and for escaping it PvE encounters, part of it's identity should be an annoying pet.

Fox: A lot of people use it only because it's looks, it would be nice to give it something usefull, it's current spell is a total disaster and has no use at all, give something like a speed buff for the group that would be nice.

Gorilla: I cannot decide if this should be tenacity or cunning, since gorillas are strong giving a negative damage modifier would be a joke, but it should not be more tankier than the rest of it's tenacity friends, the role of this pet with it's [current ability](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=51156) would be dealing a good aoe damage and causing a moderate amount of threat, it would be a good pick for leveling and trash clearing, the CC for the ability is still high, 20 seconds would be better.

Boar: Boars have [very good ability](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=27685), but it's current modifier stats are a complete joke, this just need some adjusts and it would be fine and fit it's role as a tank pet that can initiate combats.

Bear: Another tanking pet which is useless for tanking, [it's spell](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=36535) it's only used for a slight increase of dps in raid groups, it also has a very low damage modifier despite being such an animal like a Bear.. This pet could use something better like [this](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=9898) instead, it would make it worth for tanking.

Crocolisk: Since this is a tenacity pet, it doesn't make much sense to have a negative health modifier, so instead  of negative health, i just reduce some of it's damage, this pet already have a [good spell](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=36553) so no complaints here, maybe it should restore a quarter of life after executing an enemy, this would make it last more in PvE encounters.

Scorpid: This pet should be nerfed, it's surreal how a defense pet actually deals more damage than Wind Serpents, Cats or Cobras, this should be a tanking pet focused on utility, it's Scorpion Poison should reduce armor instead of doing exaggerate amounts of damage, it's current spell fits more (statistically) for Cobra like is said before.

Crab: Another tanking pet with negative health modifier, it's [current spell](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=36526) would be nice if it dealt a bit more of damage and absorbed more, while this pet would be not an armored vehicle like the Turtle, in terms of physical damage, it would be great for non-physical damage, being able to absorb caster spells.

Turtle: And finally the turtle, were playing on **Turtle** WoW, and yet the Turtle is the most useless pet, it's ability [Shell Shield](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=26064) is good until you read that it reduces it's attack speed this is very bad because it reduces the aggro generated, remove that negative output + less CD (maybe 30-40s) and it will and should be the best tanking pet.

And with this a lot of problems would be solved, but we would also need something to complement tenacity pets that cannot generate threat by AoE (like Crocolisk or Scorpid), and this would be giving a [new spell](https://www.wowhead.com/wotlk/spell=63900/thunderstomp) in the Hunter Training section for all pets, of course, this would count a considerable amount of training points, and it would be given to pets that fit the criteria for it, a Wind Serpent, Carrion Bird or Owl with that would be absurd and nonsensical.

But there are other serious issues, and that is pet attack speed, right now it is not even divided by pet family, but specific models (Like Zul'Gurub bat or Brokentooth), this forces the hunter to tame a specific pet, for example you want to tame a cool looking sabertooth model? Not the best option, because Brokentooth have 1.0 attack speed which works better with Windfury and procs Baited Shot therefore you lose a considerable amount of dps, i think this could be fixed with attack speed, crit chance and threat generated being something unique to a pet family, For example: A cat would be the fastest pet by attack speed, moderate crit, but poor threat generation. A turtle would have a high threat generation to compensate with it's slow attack speed, A raptor might not be that fast as cat but compensate it with a higher critical strike chance... Or we could select what attack speed a pet might have, so we can have our favorite looks anyway, but i prefer the first solution, it makes the pet families more unique and gives it a solid identity.

With all of this done, i would finally suggest a QoL change that hunters need, beign able to call a secondary pet and being able to have a bigger stable. Right now, in some raids you might want to switch to another pet depending on the situation, but if you don't have the portable stable you're in disadvantage (also even with the portable stable you need to ask the rest of the raid to buff your pet once you switch which can be annoying for some people), being able to switch between 3 pets for example will be a very nice addition, and for the wider stable, despite being bigger than actual classic, it's still very small even if you bought the extra slot with donations, you are still in situations where you need to abandon pets in order to test or get ones, it gets frustrating because you need to level it again and that includes the happiness points.. A part of the hunter fantasy is to be a hunter, have different pets to show, the ideal would be extra slots for each family, so hunters can test all the pets without needing to abandon current ones.

There has to be a balance between pet modifiers and abilities. While the new changes are a step in the right direction, things still got a long way to go.

For instance, it's fine if the scorpid has good damage despite it being a "defensive" pet if its poison takes time to apply because, in theory, it's gonna be good with 5 stacks but worse when things die faster. It shouldn't necessarily have to be the best, but at least competitive with other damage options. The ability thus justifies the pet's stats. Then we have the case of serpents, whose strong family modifier is the main reason why they deal good damage.

Hyenas, on the other hand, got a cool ability but it doesn't compensate them enough to make up for their 0% extra damage modifier. Crabs are supposed to focus on defense and AoE but are they good at it compared to a screecher? Tallstriders' aura effect is negligible (particularly due to it being limited to the party) and it doesn't even provide the pet itself with 1 extra melee damage every X amount of AP when active so it fails on both accounts.

There's also the issue of bite-only pets capping, particularly in the BM tree. Bite could be tweaked so it acts as a pseudo focus dump by, for instance, consuming 50% more focus for 50% more damage if the pet's focus is above 50. Numbers are just examples and could be tweaked up or down as needed. Changing the Bestial Discipline talent 20% CD reduction to 30% and allowing it to affect all pet abilities would also go a long way in helping bite keep its CD to distinguish it from claw while not being strictly inferior to it focus-wise.

Another road that could be taken would be to change the effect of Coordinated Assault so that it's different per family, with weaker ones having stronger effects compared to better families. A deep talent in the BM tree could further enhance this functionality.

If pets are still dying in raids, the solution is not Lone Wolf but to increase their avoidance and make them immune or resistant to any problematic boss mechanics. At the same time, the pet or hunter could have some vampirism that heals the pet when dealing damage. This could be disabled in PvP combat if needed due to balance there. Introducing Lone Wolf to provide a ranger fantasy for MM is one thing but not due to a desire of not managing a pet... in *the* pet class. I'm personally against Lone Wolf but won't be bothered if it's an MM only thing as long as all hunter specs remain competitive with each other, including MM with a pet.

Of course, there's also the clunkiness of the BM rotation after the newest changes that needs to be addressed: Kill Command bugs, Baited Shot window of opportunity being random and the ability not working with Coordinated Assault, etc. Additionally, BM talents (particularly those deep down the tree) need a reduction of points so that the resulting extra points can be kept for utility talents, since as of right now you cannot take *any* once you start the third row, not even Intimidation. Reducing Frenzy to 1 or 3 points and Ferocity to 3 could help alleviate this issue, as builds never put full points in Frenzy but rather 2-4. Players have asked for a return of Bestial Wrath as well, so the BM talent tree still needs more work done to it.

Finally, if we're truly to embrace hunter as the master of beasts, tameable options [should be greatly expanded](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=21800). Beasts such as hippogryphs and chimeras, for instance, could easily fit into the wind serpent family. An increase of stable slots is also required considering the expanding options available for both utility and looks.
