# [Summon Succubus] is too weak

## Post 141074 by Ulukay (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-24T07:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141074#p141074 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It was assumed that [Summon Succubus] is the demon with the highest damage of all, but now she is too weak and hardly differs in damage from [Summon Felhunter] or [Summon Voidwalker], even with all the talents aimed at strengthening [Summon Succubus] and equipment items that increase demon damage.
It would be good to strengthen her by adding more damage, either physical or magical.
