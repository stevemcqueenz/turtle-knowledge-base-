# Demonology Warlocks need some early level love.

## Post 168820 by Phoenixphire (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-18T23:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168820#p168820 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As an avid fan of Hardcore. Leveling, early dungeons, and taking on elite mobs is a lot of what I do.

One of my favorite concepts of a class/spec is Demonology warlock. I love summon classes in games, I love pet classes in games and Warlock is both.

With the class changes 2.0 demo warlock finally got a real identity at endgame which is super cool and I am glad they finally decided to really lean into demons.

Heres my issue with the spec though, before level 35 it is essentially just a more boring and safe version of the other 3 specs, and a more boring BM hunter.

Even after hitting level 35 you get Power Overwhelming, a cool ability with an awesome animation and sound effect from WC3. Nice. But the issue is while leveling (And especially while Hardcore) you basically can only use this spell with the Voidwalker for the most part without risking your pet constantly dying.

And even when you hit level 40, much of the Demo warlock class fantasy is out of your reach due to not having your Greater Demons.

I cannot think of any other spec that feels this incomplete at level 40. Maybe fire mage? But thats more of a lack of crit than missing a huge part of your kit.

I guess you can try to mess with Enslaved Demons, which I have tried, but most just do not feel worth using and messing around with just for them to die, break or just not perform as well as you'd hope.

 **How do we fix it? My suggestions are 1 of the following:**

 1. Make Enslave Demon Permanent until the demon dies or you summon another when using Soul Link (Make it so the demon cannot go into BGs, raids etc, Obviously greater demons still break after a certain amount of time.) This way once you hit 40, a demo lock leveling may not have his greater demons, but if hes in a zone with a cool demon he can go grab it and mess around leveling with it and really embody the class fantasy.

 2. At level 40 give warlocks a new skill that allows them to channel a summon, and after 10 seconds it summons a non elite demon that is their level from a pool of a few like Lesser Felguards, Grell / Imps, Satyrs, or even things like Fel Orcs.

 3. Add a greater demon that you learn earlier. Either Make Demon Portal learnable between 35 and 40, or add new greater demon like a Satyr or Fel Orc to be able to summon and utilize your greater demon talents before hitting 55-60.

You could even make it like a questline where alliance gets a Satyr (Former elf)  and Horde gets a Fel Orc (Former Orc) both do the same thing but a thematic reskin for both factions.
Where you track down this wayward creature that is so corrupted by demonic powers that they are now fully demon. And you trap them within a soulshard and gain the ability to summon them on a cooldown like your other greater demons. Maybe the quest takes you into Ashenvale and Crescent Grove to tie it into a custom instance. You could even give this pet abilities that make it more of a leveling pet if you don't wanna disrupt how endgame Demo Warlock plays.

In any case, Demonology as a spec has a great class fantasy and gameplay style at 55+, but while leveling it feels very much like you are missing so much of what the spec has to offer and I think that needs to change.

Please make Demonology warlock more enjoyable before you get your first Greater Demon in the 50s. Even if you don't like my idea at least get the message that us Demo lock enjoyers would like to see the spec more fleshed out pre60 like our Beast Mastery cousins.

Thanks for reading! Do you have any ideas to flesh out demo lock more before the last 10 levels?
