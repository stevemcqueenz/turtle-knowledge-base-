# Custom trinket idea

## Post 162152 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-12-09T12:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162152#p162152 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey!

I have an idea for a custom trinket. Mostly for memers/challenge-tanks.
It would have an activated effect of taunting a mob with a 10 sec CD if no taunt-effect was cast targeting the mob during the last 10 sec.

Would be useless for PVP.
Would be useless for proper tanks as you can't use this as a backup taunt (you casted a taunt targeting the mob even if it was resisted).
Could be used for rogue etc challenge tanks/memers by giving up one trinket slot for a taunt.

I advise this because it is upside-only. I see no situation where it would hurt anyone.
