The ideas I've come up with involve both zones and dungeons/Raids

Greenwood Pass

The High Elves and Forsaken have begun Attempts to retake Quel'thalas by establishing military outposts within Greenwood pass

Alliance Base- SilverStar outpost
Horde Base- Banshee's Wrath

The following areas within Greenwood are all controlled by the Undead Scourge and the main Quest chain for Greenwood revolves around these areas

Aife- the first high Elf village to fall before the Scourge during the invasion of Quel'thalas

The village is full of zombies, necromancers and skeletons

Aacute;ine- this ruined High Elf village has become the webbed home to a group of crypt fiends

Aodh- Spirits of the Slain elves who once called this village home now haunt the ruins as mindless Scourge

Cormac- Acolytes can be found harvesting Resources from a old gold mine for the Scourge war machine. The harvesting is guarded by a large department of Shades who hide among the ruined Buildings

Deirdre- the final and largest Scourge controlled Village within Greenwood. The Destroyed outer elf gate leading to the ghostlands lies at the vary back of this village

Gnolls- a small group of gnolls who lived within the area before the invasion survived the Third war and still battles the Undead that inhabit the Greenwood

Ghostlands

>  Why should we fight Illidari?

Exactly.

The Illidari are evil, but it's because they are akin to the Forsaken - Opportunistic, rash, and rebellious. They combine this reckless approach with magics that are chaotic and dangerous. This is not a reason to go hamstrung in a conflict when the Vanilla story is setup the way it is, where player factions are weak and face numerous threats that are a much bigger deal.

The Illidari are best written as that "third faction" collecting outcasts and rejects. They have agents that pop up from time to time and do things that aren't directly antagonistic, but cause friction and require us to step in. Lets give the example of stealing a skull for power: No we don't trust them, nor do we want anyone wielding it that way, so we will intervene. But we are not going to personally assault them for no real reason. Especially when, say as Stormwind, we are dealing with borderline civil war with the Defias, and our outposts in Stranglethorn falling apart. The only people who would really want to crusade against Illidan are the Night Elves, who by the way, are ruled by a Priestess that struggled with the concept of enforcing too great a punishment on him.

This was a controversy when TBC was released. "Why are we fighting Illidan?" People would say. And Blizzard quite literally did not want to admit then what they admitted later: A large part of their design team ignored the old story and lore, and were going to write Illidan to be a part of the Legion again. They totally missed, however, that it was known he was two timing them, exiled instead of executed, and the general details of his story that was ruined by turning him into a loot table. After they were confronted on this, they were heavily invested in TBC as an expansion, so they redid a lot of the then Legion assets and quests to look Illidari.

There might be a case for some good old-fashioned Troll killing, but I wouldn't want it to go off the deep end.
