# Dungeon Finder/LFT Improvements

## Post 137003 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-06-25T16:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137003#p137003 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1. Voting System:
It's happened to me and several others that the tank, if there's a DPS of the same class, will kick out the DPS just to get all of their own class's loot for themselves. This is clearly unfair and a total abuse of the tool. I suggest implementing a voting system to avoid these situations. If you use the LFT, you should respect the group and abide by the game's loot system instead of simply kicking and wasting the other player's time on your own whim.

 2. Debuff if you leave the dungeon/group:
In addition to the system above, I suggest adding a debuff if you leave the dungeon unfinished. This is similar to the PvP debuff but requires a little more time. I also find it unfair that some people join the Dungeon Finder, the group builds, they notice another player of their same class who probably wants the loot, they leave, and leave a whole group built just on a whim.

Debuff Suggestion:

 -Coward: You left your party in the middle of a dungeon. You cannot use the Dungeon Finder.

Duration: 45 minutes.

## Post 137012 by RPGnerd (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-25T17:33:00Z

Although it may seem obvious, a disclaimer window could be added, informing players that using the tool may result in filling the group with members of the same class, which can affect loot distribution.

Another suggestion is to lock the loot system for groups formed via the LFT tool. Currently, if a group is created through the tool, the leader can remove a player and invite them back, which allows the loot type to be changed.
