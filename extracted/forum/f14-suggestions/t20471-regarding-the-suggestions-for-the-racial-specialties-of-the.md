# Regarding the suggestions for the racial specialties of the goblins, this is because very few people have chosen them.

## Post 141426 by Qzyieu — 2025-07-25T18:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141426#p141426 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is not because I particularly like goblins,

but rather because I think goblins should be a very interesting race.

should add more elements to make them more interesting.

Regarding the suggestions for the racial specialties of the goblins,

this is because very few people have chosen them.

Perhaps because people think goblins are too ugly, very few people choose to play as goblins.
This results in the goblin race, which is such an interesting one, being unable to enhance the fun for everyone.

Perhaps the following changes can be made to compensate for the ugly appearance of the goblins.

Moreover, these racial abilities will not affect the balance of the game.

 1,Band manager-  Each goblin can hire a musician (and only one). The musician can be a centaur, an elven violinist, or a orc guitarist.The summoned musicians can provide +spirit to their teammates.
Players can choose the music composed by musicians, such as the classic background music of World of Warcraft.

When there are several goblins in the team, the musicians they hire can play together (but if the instruments are not chosen properly, the music will be particularly bad)

 2,first class flight VIP- Goblins are always able to hire the best flying mounts. When they ride the mounts at the flying points,They can reach their destination in less time.

 3,meticulous in planning   -  The buffs of the goblins always last a little longer than those of others. (It can also be designed such that the buffs given by the goblins last a little longer,, 10 minutes?)

 4,The fleeing ability of the goblins can be changed to: movement speed +%100, but it only lasts for 1.5 seconds.

After all, when fleeing, you won't have time to think about where to go.

 5,bargaining with death god  -  If you don't want go to the location of the corpse but instead choose to resurrect directly in the cemetery, your punishment time will only be 8 minutes.

 6，parsimony-  The price of the repair equipment is lower.

 7，The goblin's mount, I think this vehicle can be used as a substitute. It once appeared on Kozan Island.
