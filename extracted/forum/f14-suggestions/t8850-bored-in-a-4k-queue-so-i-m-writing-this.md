At this point what is the point of having the HC people on the same realm with this vast amount of population, it makes ZERO sense.... Back in the day with 5k pop you could argue it was pointless, and i'd agree... but this is getting stupid.. the population is rising by 1k every weekend... and the 1.17 content patch isnt even out..... Come on devs....

Advantages of HC only realm:
 1: Less crowd on the server.
 2: Lower level zones ALOT less crowded
 3: No more fighting for tags and getting toxic as everyone can group.
 4: No more queues!
 5: No more normal griefing low level hardcores.
 6: HC can have their own LFT for dungeons.
 7: Transfer to normal realm at 60 if they want Immortal.
 8: Easier to find people for groups and no fighting over tags.
 9: No more people accidently taking the Mysterious Stranger quest and wondering why they died. (yes people dont read)
 10: No more tedious 'Inferno' mode quest for lvl 60 HC, Keep this in the game and just reward them with the epic trinket.

Disadvantages
 1: More resources and GM's and Mods for a new realm. (But really not being rude but I'm sure the amount of donations you guys are getting with 15k+ population this is a non issue)
 2: You cant play with your normal mode friends... oh wait.. you COULDNT anyway.

At this point it makes so much sense its just a great idea sitting in front of the devs in plainsight imo.
