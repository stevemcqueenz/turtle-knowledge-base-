Paladins don't throw rocks; that's not knightly.  Actually that's why they don't have conventional ranged attacks at all--medieval knights regarded attacking from range as somewhat cowardly and un-chivalrous.  Given sufficient tools to do the job already exist in-game, I'm leery of adding more for that reason.  Classes by right ought to have advantages and drawbacks.  I can live with the Paladin's limited pulling toolset--I know I can because I've done so all these years.  Having a ranged pull isn't going to make the class desired as a tank where it isn't presently desired.  At best it fixes something that's not so much a problem as a mild annoyance.  I don't totally oppose OP's suggestion, but I'm not convinced, either.

I staunchly oppose Avenger's Shield.  That's what retail did, and copying that lacks imagination.  If something's to be done, the original poster's idea was better because it's a unique idea, and better fitting within the class's origin and overall intent.
