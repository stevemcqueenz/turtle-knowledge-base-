# Frustrating Goblin Starting Zone Oversights

## Post 102019 by HerTiSo — 2024-09-27T12:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102019#p102019 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Playing as a new Goblin on Turtle right now, and I must say, you've done a fantastic job with the custom race and its starting zone. However, I've encountered some rather frustrating oversights that make the first hour or two quite cumbersome. These seem like simple fixes, and I'll be moving out of that zone soon regardless. But it would be unfortunate if these 'issues' negatively impacted the experience for other new players.
smiling_turtle

 1. **No merchant offers repair services until you reach Gazzik's Workshop.** There is no NPC available to repair broken gear in Balnack Copperlight or Rustgate Ridge. It may not be a big issue for the first location, but the absence of one at the zone's hub feels strange - particularly with the nearby Black Ash Mine, which can be really frustrating for rogues. I would appreciate having one at Rusticate Ridge.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/jdf9i4d.jpeg)

 2. It's a bit odd that **there isn't an Engineering Teacher at Gazzik's Workshop**, especially with the setting resembling an engineering outpost and having an Engineering Supply Merchant. Surprisingly, I haven't encountered an Engineering teacher at all yet.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/xIgEfCZ.jpeg)

 3. The initial gear progression for Goblins through quests is quite minimal, if not existing at all. By the time you reach Level 8, you may have only received one or two armor pieces as quest rewards. In contrast to other starting zones where you are usually fully equipped after an hour or two, this makes the Goblin starting experience feel even more bumpy.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/7EKZDmj.jpeg)

 4. Even though the 'zZz' animation is shown correctly on the PC's status banner, it appears that Rustgate Ridge is not generating any rested XP at all. I've tested both AFK and logging out there.

 5. There seems to be a minor bug at Gazzik's Workshop where the game glitches out when rotating the camera at a low angle in the workshop's basement. Just wanted to bring this to your attention.
smiling_turtle_head

Apart from these aspects, you've done an excellent job with the custom content in that area, and I truly appreciate your work. Nonetheless, I think that these mistakes could be as annoying for others as they are for me.

(I'll reserve the first comment for any additional information I may want to add later before moving on from this zone.)
