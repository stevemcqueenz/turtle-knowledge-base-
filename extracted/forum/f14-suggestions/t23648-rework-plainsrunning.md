# Rework Plainsrunning

## Post 165485 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2026-01-17T22:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165485#p165485 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I've long had a gripe with how Plainsrunning works - in particular that taking ANY amount of damage removes 1/3rd of its effect. This means that if you get any kind of DoT, you can't "mount up" until it expires. There are a lot of mobs that leave DoTs on you for 10-15 seconds after you kill them, and all of them tick faster than your Plainsrunning gains stacks, so the best you can do is activate it for half a second to get 30% boost, and lose it to a DoT tick, repeat. Not to mention being dismountable by a single DoT.

So here is my suggestion - instead of having 3 separate buffs for the 3 stages of Plainsrunning, have one that stacks up. Each stack will give 1% movement speed and stack up to 60 or 100, depending on your riding skill. They will stack in roughly the same time, but more smoothly and less jank. Also re-casting plainsrunning shouldn't reset it to 1 stack like it currently does.

When taking damage, it should calculate the percentage that damage took from your HP pool and decrease the plainsrunning stacks accordingly. For example, if you're running with 100 stacks, and get hit for 15% of your HP, you should lose 15x3 = 45 stacks. The formula can be tuned and complicated further if needed. That way hopefully the plainsrunning can stack faster than a DoT can slow you down, unless it's a very painful DoT or your HP pool is very small.

It also works nicely thematically - stacking stamina should allow you to shrug off damage easier and keep running. It also complements with the tauren racial for increased health. And as an added bonus, you'll be able to see your movement speed bonus in real time by checking the improvised cow speedometer (the amount of buff stacks).
