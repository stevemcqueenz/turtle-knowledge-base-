# Please rework "chance on hit" mechanic on weapons and enchantments

## Post 45990 by Fomwaag — 2023-05-15T21:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45990#p45990 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Developers, Players,

I really love weapons that work with the "chance on hit" mechanic; they bring an interesting element to the game. However, many of these items go unused. That's why I have an idea: it would be great if we could trigger these effects with spells, ranged attacks, and even in druid form. Similar mechanics already exist in the game, like the Heartseeking Crossbow or the Blade of Eternal Darkness. Of course, this would only apply to weapons where it makes sense (for example, a hunter wouldn't be able to trigger Ravager with ranged attacks). Similarly to weapons, this could be applied to enchantments as well. Currently, a hunter can only choose an agility enchantment for their weapon, while a mage can go for spell power or intellect. What if they could also have lifesteal or fiery weapon enchantments?
