## Post 132420 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-05-21T20:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132420#p132420 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sadly, we're unlikely to remove this for a while. Discord currently has rampant issues with scammers posting links such as "free steam giftcards" which people click on and proceed to lose control of their account. This issue is heavily reduced with this level of verification.

Do keep in mind, this is not verifying your SMS with Turtle WoW -- this is a built in verification with Discord.

If in the future Discord implements better anti-phishing protection, we'd be happy to remove the requirement, but as of now, it's nearly a requirement for a server of our size.
