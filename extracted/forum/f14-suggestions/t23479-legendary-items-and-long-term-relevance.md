# Legendary Items and Long-Term Relevance

## Post 164278 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-02T17:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164278#p164278 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As raids progress, loot from earlier raids gradually becomes obsolete, including legendaries.
I think that, for example, Sulfuras should—like other legendary items—remain something you rarely (if ever) need to replace. Legendary items shouldn’t end up being used only for transmogrification.

With upcoming raids, I believe the stats of legendary items should be re-evaluated and scaled up. This would not only preserve their legendary status, but also give more long-term value and renewed interest to older raids.

What do you think about this? Do you feel legendaries should stay relevant across future raid tiers?

The goal isn’t to nerf endgame gear, but rather to make legendary items viable. In my opinion, each time a new endgame raid is released, legendaries should be upgraded to the level of the previous raid tier. For example, when we reach T4, they would be equivalent to T3, with the possibility to upgrade them to T4 through a rare item or a complex quest tied to the latest raid tier. This would be a good system to keep all raids relevant and ensure that legendary items are truly a meaningful bonus in the game.

## Post 164441 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-01-05T08:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164441#p164441 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Legendaries should be endgame viable for sure. With the exception of the Shard of Binding, the amount of effort and time it takes to complete any of them is leagues above just killing one boss from the most difficault raid and get lucky on a roll.

However, not every legendary is made equal. Shard of Binding, on account of being just a rare item and requiring no input from the player can forever stay a bad resistance piece. Noones hard work is being wasted here.

Sulfuras and Atiesh are probably most prone to powercreep, as the former is just regular ass weapon with a firebolt, and the latter being a literal statstick with a small group buff aura.

Bothof these weapons are just the sum of the stats they provide. So as items stats increase, these can and will become surpassed. I dont think just increasing the stats of the items however is the right choice. Sulfuras still can be obtained by someone only beating MC and it shouldnt be competing with t3.5 items out of the getgo on a character that might othetwise still be wearing blues.

Instead, offering quests to the wielders of said weapons to improve the power of those weapons that require you among other things to beat the current harder content (preferably repeatedly, to prevent legendary tourism in high end raids)
Would be apprechiated.

When it comes to scythe and thunderfury, i think the inverse is true. Those two are so atrociously overstatted that the simple way of how they mechanically work means they wont be obsolete for a long while.
Thunderfury has an extremely unhealthy monopoly on tankthreat. Any tank that wants to be useful in the endgame is either bear, or has to go to MC prison for a couple of month in order to ever be seriously considered as maintank.
Boosting it further is not the winning move here. In fact, making tanks less dependent on TF for threat is. Then maybe the weapon could be buffed to be less subjected to powercreep in the hands of rogues and hunters.
But for tanks, buffing this weapon is making a problem worse.

Lastly, while not currently needing more power, scythe of elune itself is not nearly as prone to powercreep as other items, since its proc scales not only greatly with the primary stat (spellpower) for any of its wielders, it is also a damage amplification for anybody with the same damage type. That alongside the fact that its grossly overstatted for a simple dps item means it certainly wont need more power into it.
A less assanine way of obtaining it however could be in order. I dont think t3.5 geared characters mindlessly grinding the easiest "raidboss" in classic is a very legendary way to start that quest
