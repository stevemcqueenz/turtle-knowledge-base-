## Post 85147 by Adess — 2024-01-18T10:39:00Z

Dear Administrators and TURTLE-WOW players,

Greetings to all! I've been playing on the server for about four months, and I'm having a great time! Huge Thank You for the years of effort put into developing the server!

However, I've come to the conclusion that we need to address the issue of group sign-ups for AV.
The CURRENT solution, which PREVENTS group sign-ups, in my opinion, is terrible and leads to frustration for many players. After all, we're playing an MMO, right? I want to play with my friends, all of them!
I don't mind facing an organized group with better gear - we can hold our ground just fine! If we can't, we will all die side by side! TOGETHER!

As a player with years of experience, I believe there's a better solution that will maintain balance while increasing satisfaction with the game!
My suggestion is to ALLOW players to sign up for AV games in GROUPS but with certain limitations.
I propose that players should have the ability to sign up in a group consisting of AT LEAST five people.

In depths of my heart I believe there is nothing wrong with full raid queing for AV - let people have their fun!
However, I understand that server is not focused on PVP, so I ask for what I believe is realistic.

This approach would allow players to enjoy the game in an organized team, which is crucial in MMOs, while minimizing concerns about potential imbalances in power. I believe this solution would satisfy both supporters of group play and those who prefer solo play.
I realize that this might lead to situations where an organized group of 20+ people ends up in one AV. What's wrong with that? That's how the game always worked. For some, it's a quick win; for others, a quick loss. And then you can sign up again.

Some could argue, that you can join an AV by queing as ten inviduals, however this very often leads to somebody left behind - I do not agree with this, I hate leaving a group member on their own. Queing as 10 solo players, leave way to much chance for someone to be unable to participate in group play.

People complainng about premades slothering them are hilarious! Find gaming companions and fight together! Crush the HORDE or ALLIANCE! It doesn't always have to be about winning. The journey is part of the adventure!

Thank you for considering my suggestion, and I hope we can work together to improve our gaming experience.

Yours, The unkillable steel can, The Saint, 350kgs Sexi Beast - Adess
