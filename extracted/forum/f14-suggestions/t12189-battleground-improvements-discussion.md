# Battleground improvements discussion

## Post 81059 by Jc473 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-12-13T18:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81059#p81059 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’d like to propose some changes to the Warsong Gulch (WSG), Arathi Basin (AB) and Alterac Valley (AV) battlegrounds. I think there has been quite a bit of feedback over the years but I haven’t seen much change other than adjustments to the honour/reputation rewards.

 **Aim** – To encourage people to play any BG for the pure fun of it and not just for the rewards.

 **WSG time limit**  – In later expansions this was done for obvious reasons. Having the BG potentially last for hour or more can be a really painful experience. This is especially the case when the game doesn't feel like it's progressing because both teams have the enemy flag hidden in some corner playing a "catch me if you can" game and too many people are defending. This happens a lot and becomes quite tedious especially when you've killed the enemy enough times and you're getting no further honour from it. Adding a timer for around 20-30 minutes would be very nice.

 **WSG terrain exploits** –  I'm not an expert on this but I've seen enough people complaining about these sorts of antics which dissuades them from playing the BG completely. I have no idea how easy/hard it is to address some of them but would probably go along way to encourage people to participate again.

 **WSG flag capture reward system** – Another off-putting aspect of WSG is the fact that whether the game was hard fought or not, it’s quite common to leave the battle ground with zero (or maybe, one) flag captures which is a lot of time spent for little honour/reputation reward. Even if a timer was used to ensure shorter matches, this is still a deflating feeling. Also, some premades actually don’t finish the match quickly so they can camp the enemy graveyard for maximum honour which is a horribly feeling for the losing team. There’s needs to be a more dependable way of gaining honour/reputation from this BG. Here are a few ideas:
i)	If a time limit is used (e.g. 30 minutes), perhaps at the 20 minute mark, everyone gets a chunk of reputation/honour equal to one flag. Then, again, at the 10 minute mark. So, this encourages teams to ‘give it a good go’ even if a loss seems inevitable (e.g. against a premade).
ii)	Offer more tokens for a loss (2 instead of 1). You can keep the 3 token reward for the winners because they captured 3 flags so their total reward is still very good.

 **AB reward system** – Overall, I like how the game progresses more quickly if the base capture score is 4-1 or 5-0 but perhaps the same solution (as per last WSG suggestion) could be implemented to encourage the losing team to ‘give it a good go’ even if a loss seems inevitable. Create a timer (NB – not a time limit!) and reward people with a chunk of reputation/honour at 10 minutes and then again at 20 minutes.

 **AV terrain exploits** – Even though AV doesn’t need more help with popularity, it feels really bad to be able to bypass defences with these sorts of antics. I believe the Horde are the most inconvenienced in this regard with our current version of AV. Again, I have no idea how easy/hard it is to fix this stuff but it would be very much appreciated.

 **AV reinforcements mechanic** – I think this was a really good change which the TBC expansion introduced. The main reason is because it gives people a ‘light at the end of the tunnel’ feeling if the game becomes too defensively oriented (aka a turtle). This should hopefully dissuade people from ‘afking out of the BG’ and engage in PvP instead. I’m not sure what the reinforcement counter should be set at but it needs to give the feeling that the BG can end in a respectable amount of time.

 **Reputation rewards** –  Add better reputation rewards for WSG/AB which are more competitive with the AV ones. This alone would encourage people to play these BGs because the rewards are tied to them.

NB – I haven’t commented on Sunnyglade Valley because I’ve never tried it.

Please feel free to discuss these ideas or add your own.
