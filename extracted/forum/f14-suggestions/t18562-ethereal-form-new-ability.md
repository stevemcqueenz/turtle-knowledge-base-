# Ethereal Form - new ability

## Post 126521 by Poqoq — 2025-04-12T11:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126521#p126521 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ethereal Form is an ability of spirit walker, shaman-like unit from wc3.
it makes the caster immune to physical dmg  but it takes incrased magical dmg and is unable to attack/cast.
This ability added to twow would be perfectly on theme with wc3 shamans and give shamans the def cd they need.
How i would translate it into twow:
It would be deep talent in enh tree.
It would make the caster look transparent, the same effect as equiping moroes dodge kara 10 trinket.
It would increase the dodge of the caster by 100% to make you immune to dmg but still being able to tank hits.
Something that makes you straight up take no dmg would need searious drawbacks like:
very short duration and long cd, my proposal is 5s duration, 30 min cd.
you take 66% increased dmg from magic sources , 66% is the excact number used in wc3 Ethereal Form, both this and short duration would make timing of this abbility extreamly important.
 3rd drawback would be not being able to cast any offensive spells including instant cast ones and any autoattacks, you could only be able to taunt , place totems or heal, but healing would make this abbility pointless as you cant dodge attacks while you are casting.So becouse of that you would be losing threat while sitting in this abillity.
