# Improve or change Vagrant's challenge rewards!

## Post 68924 by Ramuh — 2023-10-05T15:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=68924#p68924 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think for the Vagrant's challenge, the rewards for completion at level 60 should be changed.

The title is fine as is, but the Mule that is rewarded is the same one from the donation store, and getting it as a reward doesn't feel as rewarding as you can get it through other means.

My suggestion is to change the Mule (bank pet reward) to either have a different model or a unique skin to distinguish it from the store one, or if it is too costly or time-consuming to make or change the assets for it, instead reward Vagrant players with 200 Turtle Tokens, similar to slow and steady, so they can at least get a pick of what reward or companion they want. Given that they are getting a donation item as a challenge reward,

I put this from the perspective of a HC player, as if you are running HC and Vagrant at the same time, you are likely never to use the Wanderer title and instead will use the Immortal/Inferno titles, making the companion/side reward more weighty by perspective. And given that the Mule (bank pet reward) can be gained through other means, it sadly doesn't feel too rewarding.

Thank you for taking the time to read this!
