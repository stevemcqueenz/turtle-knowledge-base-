## Post 125961 by Surtugal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-04-08T08:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125961#p125961 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Always liked that idea.

The thing is, wow PvE is fixed pattern recognition and timing. Without judgement, With all the mods out there it became rather trivial. The mechanics reward good execution but less in vanilla than in later expansions. Here preparation of your character is key more than preparation of you as a player. It’s casual and fun, the progress is linked to time spend.

The idea of adaptive, intelligent encounters is in stark contrast. Dunno if intentional but you highlight a key issue with your example scenario

> If an advanced, player-like AI controls the boss, it might focus on instantly killing healers, completely ignoring the tank's taunts.

The AI still has to play the game! Taunts are taunts, threat is threat. AI cannot simply change targets „on will“ because the rules of the game dictate that’s not allowed. It must be a skill with a cool-down f.e.
It’s not just about balancing numbers wise, it’s a delicate balance between challenge and fun. If that would be easy, there would’ve been much more encounter of that kind.

With complex agents you run also into a compute problem. You‘ll have to run a separate instance for every single raid I suppose. That’s a much heavier toll than a simple finite-state machine.

Than there’s the original question of integrability. Can we add it without breaking the immersion and the validity of the rest of the content? And there’s the question of suitable AIs.
