# Quick profession fixes for a great economy

## Post 94653 by Yggd — 2024-05-12T17:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94653#p94653 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Enchanting
Shatter 1 Nexus Crystal into 1 Large Brilliant Shard. 10 minutes cooldown
Transmute 5 Illusion Dust into 5 Dream Dust. 10 minutes cooldown (shared with above)

 - Alchemy
Elixir of Poison Resistance: Replace Large Venom Sac with Anti-Venom (crafted from First Aid)
Shadow Oil: Change it to 1 Grave Moss and 1 Fadeleaf
Frost Oil: Change it to 1 Wintersbite and 1 Khadgar Whisker
Restorative Potion: Replace Elemental Earth with Ichor of Undeath
Mageblood, Major Troll's Blood, Brute Force, Sages, Mongoose, Grater Arcane Elixirs and Major Mana Potion: Change it to 1 of each herb
Major Rejuv Potion: Replace Imbued Vial with Crystal Vial
Add Transmute: Elemental Earth, Elemental Air, Elemental Water from their upscale versions (Core of Earth, Breath of Wind, Globe of Water)
Flasks: Require 40/15 of each materials, 1 Black Lotus and 2 Imbued Vials. Each craft gives two Flasks instead of one

 - Blacksmithing
Elemental Sharpening Stone: Change it to 1 Elemental Earth
Nightfall: Give the buff a 40% proc rate, only on auto-attacks. It's asinine that a weapon with a 3.6seconds attack speed only gives a 5 seconds buff.
Annihilator: Greatly improve proc rate and consider making it a 2x300 armor debuff instead of 3x200.

 - Tailoring
Transmute Mooncloth: Reduce to 2 days cooldown
Add a transmute to Felcoth: Requires 10 Runecloth Bolt and 1 Larval Acid. Creates 2 Felcloth. Must be at the Fel moonwell in Jaedenar. 5 minutes cooldown.

 - First Aid
Smelling Salts: Replace the 2x Essence of Fire with 1x Greater Holy Protection Potion

 - Herbalism
Add Wild Steelbloom nodes in all maps surrounded by high mountains, like Stranglekelp is found in any map with water.
Add Black Lotus as a chance to be looted from Golden Sansam. Maybe that'll send the 500 bots 24h/24 out of Winterspring and send them elsewhere
Add Grave Moss nodes close to, or around the very vast majority of graveyards around Azeroth. It is *Grave* moss after all

 - Other
Lasher Farm: Why not add Grave Moss as a possibility of herbs that could drop? It's in Eldre'Thalas, an ancient city where a nation got invaded and killed years ago. There's bound to be dead people inside, and thus Grave Moss makes sense to be added somehow in there. Also, add Wild Steelbloom from the mobs there, it makes no sense that it's 1 of the 2 herbs that can't drop.
