Hello, i am making this post in regards to the many sugestions that have been showing has of late that ask for the implementation of later blizzard expansion assets,

I believe the turtle crew has been crafting a rather unique world from the vanilla experience, they have done so by adding fun RP friendly decorations, quests, events and so on;

It would be a sad result to see such care to detail and usage of the untapped potencial that the vanilla models presented become sullied by the careless adition of the newer, and i dare say, uglier models, from later expansions.

I've already been saddened by the hasty SW harbour addition, whose design went against the architecture of the human capital city, it would be sorrowfull to see more areas fall to the coarse implementations that blizzard added along the years.

instead i sugest a temperate aproach, understanding that there are many new players envolved with many good sugestions yet that the masses also have a distinct lack of taste for what truly makes a thing good.

Apart from the harbour, many of the aditions are excelent ( such as sparkwater and hawk's vigil) and reveal a true love for the azerothian world of world of warcraft.

My 2 cents.

For immersion into RP it actually is a bigger deal than one would expect - a living forest actually seeming to be alive rather than static.
