# A very important and simple change: allowing guild tabards to be transmogrified and hidden.

## Post 156258 by Publius — 2025-10-20T07:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156258#p156258 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Players design their outfits by carefully matching each piece of armor, but the guild tabard often ruins these meticulously crafted looks. The problem is, we feel forced to equip the tabard for its utility—namely, the teleport and to save a bag slot. This is why we desperately need the option to hide the guild tabard.
