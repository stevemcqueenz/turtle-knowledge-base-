# Survival Hunter Tweak Idea

## Post 160186 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-11-20T16:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160186#p160186 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey!

Played a bit with survival talent tree.
<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/42ZeaCEHUK>

Wanted to remove weaker talents and abilities I personally disliked and turn the tree into a more pet+hunter combo-focused one.
It is not within the tree but I assumed that Improved Primal Aspects from the BM tree would provide some form of mana regeneration to the hunter using Aspect of the Wolf.

The changes:
 1. Removed Survivalist. A 5tp talent for 10% HP for a non-tank tree feels too situational and weak.
Replaced it with Trained Well.
For 3TP, that talent ensures that your pet hits consistently with the added hit chance and gave the pet a form of vampirism where the damage you deal heals the pet if you stay close to each other. Wanted to add a talent like this to help keep the pet alive.

 2. Removed Carve. This can be a bit controversial but I believe that hunters already have a way to deal AoE damage via traps, and it's enough.
I'd replace Carve with a talent which boosts pet+hunter synergy.
New idea: Bestial Camaraderie. For 3TP, you get a passive crit raise engine where your crits increase the pet's crit chance and vice versa. The idea is to turn the pet into an important enabler for the DPS the hunter does.

 3. Removed Lacerate. Wanted to give a real big hitter instead of it.
New one is Synchronized Strike which is basically a bunch of single target damage as long as your pet is alive.
