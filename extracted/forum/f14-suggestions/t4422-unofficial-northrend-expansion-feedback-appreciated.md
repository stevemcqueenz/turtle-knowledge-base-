Following through on this, actually took the time to write down a few ideas and concepts. As stated, I took my inspiration from the *Lands of Mystery* RPG book rather than the *Wrath of the Lich King* expansion.

 **Mechanic: Cold Weather**
The frozen wastes of Northrend are not hospitable to most races. While travling the continent, some regions may result in exposure to intense cold weather. Tents, campfires and torches crafted from the Survival skill may ward away the worst of the effects, as can specially-crafted cold weather equipment made from the Tailoring and Leatherworking skills.

 **Zones**
Howling Fjord: 15-30
The Dragonblight: 15-30
Grizzly Hills: 25 - 35
Borean Tundra: 35 - 45
Coldarra: 45 - 55
Zul'Drak: 30 - 40
Crystalsong Forest: 40 - 50
Sholazar Basin: 51 - 58
The Storm Peaks: 55 - 60
Icecrown Glacier: *Inaccessible/No-Man's-Land*

 **Potential Friendly Settlements**
 **Valgarde, Howling Fjord**
 **Faction:** Alliance - Lordaeron Expeditionary Force *(In-game reputation faction: Stormwind)*
 **Transport:** Boat *(from either Stormwind Harbor or Menethil Harbor; to be decided)*

Led by the half-elf Magsitrate Randavarr and his town council, the rough seaport of Valgarde was built on the cusp of Daggerfang Bay by the remains of the Lordaeron Expeditionary Forces led by Prince Arthas Menethil on his ill-fated quest to stop the Scourge. Left stranded when their prince burned down their ships to prevent them from returning home, they built fortifications and eked out a tough living in the town under threat of the Drakkari trolls, furbolgs and other monsters until contact was re-established with the Eastern Kingdoms.

 **Icemist Village, Dragonblight**
 **Faction:** Horde - Thunder Bluff
 **Transport:** Zeppelin from Thunder Bluff

Perched on the edge of the Dragonblight above Lake Wintergrasp, Icemist Village is a friendly tauren town overseen by Tundra Coldhoof, a gentle soul who takes care to ensure that weary travelers receive whatever care they need. The Icemist tauren keep a ready supply of food, drink, clothes and basic tools on hand, and sell them to anyone in need.

 **Kaskala, Borean Tundra**
 **Faction:** Neutral - Tuskarr
 **Transport:** Boat from Icepoint Rock

Effectively a collection of grouped-up tribal villages divided into family units, together they form the tuskarr homeland. Though each family runs its own village, Tusklord Hrak'kar has taken charge of scheduling to ensure that no single tuskarr family must bear the brunt of any task or have too few men to bring in food. Friends of the Tuskarr can reach this friendly harbor by traveling from Icepoint Rock.

 **Potential Instances**
 **Ulduar**
Instance Type: Dungeon
Entrance: Northern Storm Peaks
Level: 55 - 58
Attunement: None

Brann Bronzebeard has put his reputation as an explorer on the line by claiming that the storm giants' stronghold of Ulduar was a Titan facility. Hidden in the mountains and rocks of the high peaks of Northrend, a handful of strom giants inhabit the place. Supposedly a dying race, the giants have become reclusive in recent times. If Bronzebeard's claims are correct, Ulduar may very well be a treasure trove of information on the Titans and their activities, but finding it may prove more difficult than expected.

 **Azjol-Nerub**
Instance Type: Raid
Entrance: Northern Dragonblight
Level: 60
Attunement: The Blood Key

The massive underground nerubian kingdom of Azjol-Nerub was once home to one of the oldest civilizations on Azeroth. The majority of the nerubians were wiped out when the Lich King saw them as a threat, and now the Scourge rule over the Upper Kingdom under the Crypt Lord Anub'arak, majordomo of the Lich King. Still-living nerubian rebels continue to live in the tunnels of the Old Kingdom, deep below the surface. The entrance into the Old Kingdom is locked behind a massive, blood-red door. The key is currently held by Baelgun Flamebeard at the camp of Doorward, by the city entrance.

Alliance attunement: Complete a quest chain on behalf of Brann and the remains of the Bronzebeard Expedition, and be at least Honored with the Argent Dawn as proof of your staunch commitment against the Undead Scourge. Baelgun will part with the Blood Key on recommendation of Brann.

Horde attunement: Kill Baelgun Flamebeard. He drops a broken Blood Key, which you must then find a way to repair. Be at least Honored with the Argent Dawn to convince an Argent Dawn craftsman into helping you repair the key.

The Blood Key must be present in your bags in order to zone into Azjol-Nerub.

> **Chnams wrote: Tue Oct 04, 2022 3:00 pm**
> Are all these zones in-lore at the time of Vanilla or are they coming directly from WotLK, by the way?

The zones originated from the *Lands of Mystery* RPG sourcebook, which was published in 2006. *Wrath of the Lich King* was first announced in 2007 and was released in 2008. However, most of the content from *Lands of Mystery* was supposed to be part of the *Lands of Azeroth* RPG book, which was advertised in the *Warcraft: The Roleplaying Game* sourcebook that was originally published in 2003. *Lands of Azeroth* was supposed to be published in April 2004, but it was cancelled and its content was later split in *Lands of Conflict* (2004; focuses on the Eastern Kingdoms) and *Lands of Mystery* (2006; focuses on Kalimdor, the South Seas and Northrend).

It so happens that *Wrath of the Lich King*, and in fact a fair bit of early World of Warcraft lore, kept fairly closely to the RPG book continuity at the time of development with a few number of deviations and additions; nowadays no more, as Blizzard declared the RPG books to be non-canon in their continuity in 2011.
