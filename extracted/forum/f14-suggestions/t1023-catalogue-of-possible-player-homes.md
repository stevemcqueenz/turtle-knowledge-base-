> **Kazgrim wrote: Fri Apr 24, 2020 5:52 am**
> Running around Stormwind I noticed that quite a few buildings were empty of NPCs. This got me thinking; wouldn't it be cool if players could claim these homes as their own?

Not a bad idea.  I remember playing on a roleplay server back in the day and seeing people fight over these buildings.  Haven't seen that here.  But it would be neat if you added a locked door to them, and a single player could buy a deed to the building from a GM to claim it.  Could cost a lot of in-game gold or maybe just be awarded to certain players at the discretion of the administration.  Or it could just be calling dibs in a thread.

Anyway, Stormwind definitely has probably the most empty buildings.  If anywhere has it beat, I'd guess maybe Darnassus, but a lot of those empty buildings are so small.  Ironforge has at least a couple, and I remember using one as my character's home back in vanilla and never getting unwanted guests because everyone was too busy fighting over Stormwind.  Horde-side sort of lacks those empty buildings since I believe their capitals were done last.  Undercity has an unused second floor if memory serves, but not much up there, and of course there's stuff like Old Ironforge.

I think you missed a couple spaces in Stormwind, but I'm just working off memory.  The canal area should have an extra shop or two, I think one might be a tailoring shop.  There's also the old auction house/bank (courthouse?) in the dwarf district, but I wouldn't be shocked if you're also holding that for high elves for the expansion.
