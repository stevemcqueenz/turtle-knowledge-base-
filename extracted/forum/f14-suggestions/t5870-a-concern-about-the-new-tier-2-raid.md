# A concern about the new tier 2 raid

## Post 36985 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-06T16:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36985#p36985 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As most know, Scarlet Citadel has been postponed to an unknown date and the upcoming patch will include an Emerald Dream raid. We are all excited to check it out and kill some dragons though there is some raised doubt about if its fitting to all of the raider populations.

So far info on it has been vague. All we know is that it's a tier 2 raid, and that it drops the following item:

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#a/z7J5wYA]

Unfortunately, the item in the picture is just a worse version of Neltharion's Tear from Nefarian. Making a raid and fitting it into vanilla's existing tiers structure limits it to being inaccessible for the most casual guilds, and almost useless to the most progressed guilds. That's why my proposal is to lean heavily to the hardmode system for many reasons.

 **First, raids with a hard mode can have a less frustrating easy version that can be easily pugged so that everyone can experience the custom raid.** The rewards can be some decent catch-up gear, fill some slots that lack a good drop from zg/aq/ubrs, lots of neat things the adventurers will want to get because they will make them stronger for their open world content (hybrid soloing gear, some totems, idols and librams, the trinket shown above with a cc effect)

 **Second, incremental hard modes to unlock more and more loot tables give the progressing raiders more things to look up than just downing the boss** Examples being bug trio, yoggsaron, hakkar with priests (just the concept, this one sadly doesnt drop extra loot). If this emerald raid was like obsidian sanctum of wrath, the group could choose how many minibosses it wants to kill, have a shot at the boss and they can always kill a few more minibosses if they fail a hardmode. There is always next week! The more minibosses left up, the more items the boss will drop, and of course the minibosses can have their own drops like crafting materials and weaker items so all levels of groups can feel fairly compensated.

 **Third, hard modes push guilds to reach higher levels in a way that is less intimidating and less of a hassle** All progression guilds face an issue. They've got a raid tier to a decent and smooth clear time and they've set their eyes on the upcoming goal, then comes the problem of hesitation. Some members are scared to progress through the next tier, some members don't have the time, some don't have the gold. A very understimated hard step to clearing AQ40 and Naxx is getting the motivation to step into them in the first place. A hard mode on the level of aq40 and naxx bosses inside a bwl tier raid can give players a dose of the higher level experience and motivate them into finally venturing there, as well as rewarding them with equivalent gear.

TL:DR make sure hard modes are a thing in the new custom raid. They can be in the form of Obsidian Sanctum minibosses, activated events like Mimiron, Speed kills like Hodir, the possibilities are endless. There can be an exclusive summoned boss like Algalon that rewards KT level loot especially to give high ILVL goals to some playstyles lacking strong items to pillar their build on (For example, 73 dps axes and maces to stop the sword supremacy)
