# PvP Toggle and why it should be changed

## Post 36082 by Bonelord — 2023-02-25T15:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36082#p36082 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Thanks in advance for reading this!

The suggestion is simple, though I don't know how hard it would be to implement: Only allow toggling of PvP when a player is in a specific location. My initial thought was only in major cities, however I think since rested already provides a function and is found with any Inn(or even tent), this would be a suitable requirement.

The reasoning is tied to warmode, but not entirely dictated by it. All too often, people will run around with their PvP flag off, see someone with it on, then wait for a moment to gank said player at the worst time. Whether this happens questing on a mob, not paying attention, gathering ores/herbs/skins, or simply passing by, it offers no recourse to the gankee! The player who was not flagged will simply turn it off again and wander away, leading to an unsatisfying gameplay element that hurts anyone with warmode enabled. Yes, they want to engage in world PvP, that was the original point of warmode. However, having no initial NOR followup opportunity for these engagements is terrible if it's merely to be abused by those not partaking. This simple change makes gathering more hectic ONLY if you choose to enable it at previously said points, which take far longer than the current toggle(a tent would be 10s cast, at least). This would also signal to the gankee that the pvp flag has a chance to be toggled in such a situation, as they can't simply stealth and toggle it like normal on a rogue/druid/night elf.

Hopefully I have presented a good case and I'd like to hear any ideas on this, as it hurts no one in the PvE realm while making the Warmode far more fun for all engaged in world PvP.

Thank you again, have a nice time on this fun server(which can hopefully become even MORE fun).

EDIT: All this does is make world pvp more engaging for those who CHOOSE to participate already! Please read the actual content before posting as we've already had two posters who have no idea what we are suggesting.
