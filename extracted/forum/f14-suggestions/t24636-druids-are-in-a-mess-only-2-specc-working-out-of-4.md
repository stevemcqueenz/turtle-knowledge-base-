# Druids are in a mess. Only 2 specc working out of 4

## Post 173466 by Templar85 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-31T16:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173466#p173466 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Many Druid specces are in shambles bottom Tier, doing tank damage, hybrid functionality outdated and over taxed but even the ones that are good need major update on their toolkit.

Rework should come with an aim of added many synergies between builds and highly functional specces of which can do at least 80% of the top damage and heal compared to a pure damage or heal class. Druid rework should NOT be in a mindset that's aim for classic wow. Druids sucked ass in classic wow, "hybrid over taxation", way too restricted and got their "true potential" which Blizzard originally imagined in their mind for classic but materialized in TBC. Other classes on Turtle are not in vanilla state anymore.

General Druid problems:

 - 1: Druid Idols providing insignificant boost.

 95% of the Druids idols are so bad (even many of the turtle designed ones) or work in very niche situtation that not worth to try to get them and occupy a bag slot for it. Second part that even if there are 2 good ones and Druid try to switch them in combat it incurs a GCD making it even more worthless trash than they are.

Solutions: Make a baseline spell that greatly improves their damage/heal/haste and reduced or eliminates the GCD. This spell can be on a 2 minute Cooldown

 - 2: There is no normal baseline resurrection spell in our toolkit.

It is very sad and the excuse because druid are already powerful with a battleres. Actually they sucks for 29 minutes to do a role as support healer/resser without a rez and good in every 30th minute to rez ONE member. I heard enough bullshit from players that "Druids just need to teach new players to not die in instances"

Solution: There are million variants how can be implemented with a slight side effect to it but it has to be baseline at some early level like other healers got their res.

 - 3: Hybrid playstyle is highly outdated, not supported with enough/strong talents and synergies.

Only one style that is working in acceptable efficiency is between Bear and Cat. Need to eradicate Hybrid Tax by 90% because Druids can not physically wear 2 sets of gear to be a real actual Hybrids in raids. For PvP our hybrid tax still insanely high because this is designed by vanilla standards against other Vanilla opponents but every class and specc greatly improved on Turtle.
There are 2 options for PvP: 1 Delete 90% of current hybrid tax because druids essentially not working in this environment against non vanilla classes. Option 2: Make insane good spell coefficients and talent synergies that places Druids in a very very strong spot for PvP and only then can try to dampen down the toolkits potential but by small steps.

 - 4: Need proper talent options (proper ways) in early talent rows.

Balance Tree providing the best support for other Trees, but early talents should provide more benefits. If a healer want to reach Moonglow in Arcane tree then has to spend points on useless(non healing support) talents to fill enough for it.

 - 5 Emerald Blessing fades after death.

Druid already taxed with an expensive reagent to cast it. Make it reagent free cast or allow it to persist through death.

Bear PvE problem:

 - 1:  Only 2 buttons to use to make threat.

One is on 6 second CD and other is replacing your next auto attack. Dodge or
Parry a Savage Bite the first 10 seconds is a huge threat loss for the Druid. Can take back the lead in 10 seconds but bad at snap threat. I did not included Swipe because it scaling horribly and only scenario to us on single target boss when rage going to overflow. Warriors and Paladins can use much abilities according to the situation. Druid tank need at least 2 more ways to attack or gain threat.

Solution: Add 1 or 2 more new abilities which Bears can use for tanking and able to use current one like Insect Swarm.

 - 2: No ability to Parry or Block an attack (which is ok) but not enough compensation in other stat and abilities

Because of this, then needs to have a way in talents that increasing our defense skill and reducing overall damage taken. Currently it is fixed by gear and set bonuses. T3 8 piece Tank set bonus should be a talent. Without it makes Druids always need to wear that set and can not interchange items for more dps ones to make more threat. T2 tank gear set do provide +Defense, but the same scenario happens that need to wear all of them to be near Crit immune for bosses.

Solution: Talents providing beneficial effects for tanks beside threat increase.

 - 3: Defensive Cooldowns

Because Druid tank essentially a big HP sponge need their way to mitigate damage beside Parry and Block. Berserk is on 6 Minute CD that means not up for every boss if raid going fast. Frenzied Regeneration nerfed to the ground 2 patches ago because PvP reasons. Healed from 110% of Druid HP, but now only does 40%. To have a meaningful defensive effect Druid need to activate all 3 of their defensive spells (Berserk, Barkskin Frenzied Regeneration) the same time.

Solution: Reduced/improved CD on the abilities and Buff Frenzied Regeneration back to its state and if want to dampen do it in PvP only.

 - 4 There are no meaningful variety of Enchants for Tanking besides +Crit on our "Stat Stick".

Need to have added new enchants to the game with a tag "Equip" so Druids can benefit it vile using their Paw. This is a very old, leftover Vanilla remnant but their is no logical reason behind it why Druids can choose from 2 meaningful enchant on their weapon but other classes can have +10. It is "hybrid tax"

Solution: New Enchants available with the tag "Equip"

 - 5 Druid in Feral form can not use engineering items.

 80% tanks have picked up engineering to be able to make additional aoe or single target damage and threat. Swipe has already very limited scaling and only hit 3 targets. We have Thorns which do abysmal damage and to make it 30 min duration and 44 damage we need to keep in our bag another 6 pieces of gear.

Solution: Either need an ability that do AOE damage or increase Swipe targets by minimum 2 or allow to use Engi items. Engi Bomb or sapper items using in form can has a drawback 1 or 2 seconds delayed activating time. Trinket effects still instant on activation.

 - 6 Dire Bear Form does not scale

It is providing 1200 Health Which is great boost when you are in MC but maybe should scale if our only role is to be HP sponge and eat Crushing Blows night and day.

Solution: Maybe new rank which provide more health, armor, some Fear resistance?

Bear PvP problems:

 - 1 Bear abilities not doing enough damage (need more abilities also)
The trade of for lack of damage is the "high armor and health pool" High armor does nothing against spell casters and even do nothing for melee who can bypass it. Yes, Druid need to change more often and use their full toolkit but leaving our Bear form puts us in extreme vulnerable spot. Bear should not be a 100% health sponge and all Meaningful dps should not come only from Cat or Moonkin forms.

Solution: New abilities that's do damage.

 **General Cat Problems:**

 - 1: Feral Swiftness talent only increases the movement speed in outdoors.

Why is the cat suddenly forgot to move fast inside. It doesn't need to be as much percentage than outside but Should move like 10% faster instead of the 30% outside.

 - 2: Cat HP is very squishy an especially against spells. Feline Grace spell might include like 10%-30% aoe tick damage resistance.

 - 3: There is no +weapon skill for Paw attacks.

Even Hunter Pets can have weapon skill while main druids have zero way to increase it.

 - 4: Hard to track Bleeds on visually debuff capped bosses.

Make the bleed shown in the Druids debuff area where can addons get stabile information from, otherwise need addons and after every patch they broken. It is very unreliable and annoying to have and addon dependency for a very basic thing like track your own bleeds.

Cat PvE problems:

 - 1: There are no meaningful Gear progression after AQ40 set.

Later sets main stat should be agility and Strength and not Intellect.

 - 2: T1-T2-T3 set bonuses are specialised for each role.

That is ok but add one more set bonus additional to the current ones that benefitting the "other spec". For example for Balance set can be like increased healing by 10-5% or something. Same concept that Priest healing gear have small amount of spell damage on them.

Resto PvE problems:

 - 1: STill 1 Resto Druid is the best optimal for 40 man raid.

 2 can be okay-ish if they restrict themselves and divide who do just Regrowth and who do just Rejuvenation. If there are 3 Resto druids in the raid the 3rd one is the worst enemy of the previous too. Needs fundamental redesign by either make a fully functional Healing Touch spec which can compete with Paladins and/or allow 2 of the same type of Hots stack or like 80%-90% healing reduction max.

 - 2: Efflorescence tied to Tree of Life form only.

This means if a Natures Grace/Regrowth druid casting Regrowth then other NG/RG druid casting on the same target then the "overwrites" not trigger Efflo effect. Same for Tree of Life druid, when he overwrites NG/RG druid hot then the original Druid will not trigger Efflo. Make it baseline. There is already and way too long for Resto Druids are their each other worst enemies.

Resto PvP problems:

 - 1: No Hot Dispell protection.

It is insane that a mainly Hot based healing class does not have dispel protection option in talents. Healing Touch has too long cast time to be reliable heal. Regrowth main healing comes from the Hot part. Rejuv does not do much heal if dispelled before 2nd tick.

 - 2: Tree of Life is utter terribly for pvp.

I not saying should lift all limitations on them, but the movement decrease should be erased because it is extreme annoying for both PvE and in PvP. I rather take some increased damage than moving slow.

Moonkin PvE Problems:

 - 1: Extreme lack of DPS and reliance too much on RNG.

Right now they doing tank level damage. There are million ways to fix. Just pls listen to the people on Forum and Discord.

 - 1: Not enough support from talents to reach enough Spell hit cap.

Druid need to prioritize any and all possible items that provide +spell hit and in very end game can reach it with a good combination on a dps gear.

Solution: Balance tree providing 3%, Resto tree can also provide 3% somewhere in row 3

 - 2: Haste stat on endgame gear is very very suboptimal

Only affecting 2 spells Wrath and Starfire but not for Insect Swarm, Moonfire, Hurricane.

Solution: In Balance tree there can a talent that converts haste to a positive effect for instant cast.

 - 3: Roots and Grasp can't be used indoors.

This is silly and there is no any logical reasons to behave like that. Entangling roots already breaking on high damage impact like Starfire and roots is worse than Polymorph because the target still can cast spells and ranged attacks.  Hybrid Tax

Solution: Can cast indoors, but this way the spell have a cooldown for 25 seconds.

 - 4: Can not proc "proc effects" on channeling spells.

Hurricane needs to be channeled for 10 seconds but counts as 1 direct damage application. Mages can proc their trinkets and talents effects on each ticks of Arcane Missiles.

 - 5: Hurricane full mana cost deducted instantly

It is a channeled spell, if got interrupted early still consumed full mana cost.

 - 6: Moonfire and Insect Swarm do meaningless damage.

Only reason to apply them because of synergy(weak) with Balance of All Things.

 - 7: Maybe add one more thematic spell like Starshards into the toolkit?

Aesthetic, Roleplay elements:

Our SWift Travel form looks epic now compared to the very first version of a lvl 20 deer, but it would be nice if it would act as Plainsrunning. Shifting to Swift form would be instant in non combat (disabled in battlegrounds) and the speed ramping up. No movement or riding speed increasing enchant work with Swift travel form which is a shame.

Visual only flying form. Can look like a Raven or Hyppogryph and hover about 5 feet of the ground but act like a ground and can be attacked like riding on any other mount. There are already lots of smiley clouds around that doing the same thing.

Druids needs a toon of fixes. Here is a talent tree suggestion that corrects 90% of issues and provide good synergies across all talent trees. [https://www.talent-builder.dev/calculat ... H6oVQH93NM](https://www.talent-builder.dev/calculator?t0=OCoQ-oWM3n&t1=ghQcBK5F4G&t2=H6oVQH93NM)
