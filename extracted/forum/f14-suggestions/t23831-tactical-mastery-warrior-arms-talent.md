# Tactical Mastery - Warrior arms talent..

## Post 167175 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-01T20:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167175#p167175 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

> Tactical Mastery
>  Talent
>  You retain up to 25 of your rage points when you change stances.

Has ever anyone suggested to remove that and give it as passive to the warrior, or a skill every 10 levels?

The class is crippled if not unplayable without! We all know it... then when everyone has 25 rage when switching, the Talent could be:

> Tactical Mastery
>  Talent
>  You retain up to 2/4/6 additional rage points when you change stances.

## Post 167176 by Magicknight — 2026-02-01T20:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167176#p167176 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I've always hated the fact that you basically lose most (talented) / all (untalented) of your primary resource,  just for switching your footwork! I've noticed it doesn't happen with ballroom dancers or figure skaters, but the Warrior, he's just happy to give it away!

However, I do have a [slight] suggestion to for Arms: make the talent 'Boundless Anger' actually boundless!
So, currently the talent gives 10/20/30 more rage to your rage bar, essentially allowing to lose even more rage if you change stances.
I suggest linking Tactical Mastery with Boundless Anger (using a long arrow that stretches from tier 1 to tier 6) so TM is a prerequisite:

Boundless Anger: 'you retain a further 25/50/75  or your rage when you change stances'

Therefore, the 'anger' built up within you would indeed be 'boundless' as it now is no longer limited by shifting your weight from one foot to the other.

It doesn't gave to be so extreme as to keep all your rage, but for pvp and pve, you wouldn't be punished for using skills that are locked out (in other stances), and being so far down the Arms tree you have to be committed to Arms.

Sounds fair to me (I'm sure others will disagree)

Great, I had the same thought with my version. Retaining 25 base rage gives the freedom to use most abilities in the other stances, and having 31 rage after switching guarantees a Bloodthirst or Mortal Strike, which is already a strong bonus.

Removing the whole mechanic is an interesting idea, but I think the gameplay with Tactical Mastery is very good as it is. It would be even better if it could be improved to 30. Personally, I wouldn’t want to take away the resource management and awareness that comes from dancing at the right moment to avoid wasting too much rage.
