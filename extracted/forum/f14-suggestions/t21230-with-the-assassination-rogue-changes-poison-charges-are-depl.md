Okay, I can confirm that those numbers were correct, so the proc rate does indeed appear to be 60% on a hit with envenom up, and fully poison talented.

Also, to answer your question, posions have a flat proc %, weapon speed does not affect it. This is why traditionally rogues have gone for a slow offhand, to take advantage of high damage sinister strikes, and fast offhands, for more posion procs.

Stormshroud and t0.5 on the other hand, they are procs per minute, meaning they are normalized based on your weapon's speed, so a 1.9s weapon and a 1.3 speed weapon will get, on average, the same number of procs in any given time period.
