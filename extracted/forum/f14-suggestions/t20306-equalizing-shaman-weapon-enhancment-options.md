# Equalizing Shaman Weapon Enhancment Options

## Post 139746 by Manijin — 2025-07-15T23:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139746#p139746 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, at the moment, endgame really only has two decent weapon enhancement options: Rockbiter and Windfury.

Yes, specc'd into it, Flametongue and Frostbrand can be fun options, but they both run into serious obstactles that seriously reduce their usefulness.

 1) Frostbrand, when talented, is AWESOME. It feels so fun, and unlocks a crit heavy playstyle without having to invest in any crit, which is a neat concept. However, once you hit endgame, or are fighting bosses, the talent effectively... does nothing. This just feels bad, but what's worse is that Frost Mages already address this problem! Just do what they do, and the option will feel much better.

 2) Flametongue seems like it should unlock this melee spellpower build, but the scaling on the enchant is too low. My proposal? Either bump the spellpower scaling (not a ton; just to the point where it feels decent) OR allow Flametongue to work with Windfury totem. Frostbrand already does this, and I really don't think allow Flametongue to do it would break the game.

Alternatively, if you didn't want to do either of those, have Flametongue Totem scale with spellpower. Even small amounts of scaling, coupled with the appropriate talents, would help the Fire Shaman talents feel unique and provide a benefit other shaman specs couldn't fully match, and I think that's great. Heck, if you did that AND let Flametone weapon stack with both Flametongue Totem AND Windfury Totem, that'd likely fix the issues entirely.   satisfied_turtle

And, really, if you reduced the attack power on ROckbiter and Windfury and allowed them to work with Windfury totem, that'd be awesome, too.   turtle_in_love
