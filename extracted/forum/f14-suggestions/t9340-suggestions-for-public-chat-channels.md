I had a similar experience on firemaw (tbc). World chat flew by at a speed that was no more readable by humans.

I installed an addon that would highlight messages containing specific expressions back then. For example, I let it highlight "SM" if I wanted to find a group for scarlet monastery. It also had a setting to block messages containing specific expressions. Filtering out stuff like "WTS", "WTB" and "pumper" made world chat much more manageable back there.
