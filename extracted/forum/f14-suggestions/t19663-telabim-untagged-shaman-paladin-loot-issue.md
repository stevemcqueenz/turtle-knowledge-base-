# [TelAbim] Untagged Shaman/Paladin loot issue

## Post 133804 by excin (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-31T19:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133804#p133804 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

These are items with no class restrictions but clearly designed around a class:
A mail item with nature spell damage, A plate helmet with healing etc.

These Items thus aren't subject to faction loot restrictions on Tel'abim

Items that can arguably be used outside of that spec for a, let's say, creative gameplay, are good to have.  But their introduction should not be at the detriment of the experience for the whole raid.

There are ways to make these things still be acquirable for the un-intended faction without having an alliance raid have back to back shaman loot, every week, at times twice per boss.

 -Option A
Put these items on a separate loot list on its own slot, that can either give the item or nothing without influencing the rest of the loot.
If the overall chance of obtaining the item is 6%, make this new list slot have a 6% chance to drop the item, and 94% of dropping nothing at all.

 -Option B
Tokens/Quests, where instead of dropping the item itself, a quest object, a crafting material or a token drops and can be exchanged for different options.

We've been progressing K40 with extra challenge on both horde and alliance side. This extra challenge is causing attrition. And while the short victory moments are worth every hour of farming and wiping.
Discovering unusable items on every boss every week back to back is really damaging.
