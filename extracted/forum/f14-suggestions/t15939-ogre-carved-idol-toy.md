# Ogre carved idol toy

## Post 108529 by Zulkord — 2024-11-18T17:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108529#p108529 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ogre carved idol toy was a trinket before 1.17.2, it worked essentially like an illusion, but limited to 10 minutes duration and 60 minutes cooldown. Now that it is conformed with other illusions in being an easily toggleable toy, it should make sense to remove duration and cooldown restrictions as well and make it just another illusion.
