# Alliance Premades Are Ruining AV Day for the Casual Player

## Post 102700 by sevenvp — 2024-10-07T12:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102700#p102700 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First off, I can already hear the comments coming—yes, you can premade AV. If you think otherwise, you’re either not playing AV on AV day or just kidding yourself and everyone else.

The strategy is straightforward: rush the Horde base and finish the game as quickly as possible (ideally in less than 10-15 minutes). The Horde base is much easier to attack, and with a frontline of many paladins supported by healers, it's nearly impossible for the Horde to defend unless the entire team turtles. Keep in mind, this is a highly geared premade against a non-premade AV.

The strategy is perfectly valid, but it exploits the map's weaknesses. My suggestion: make the Horde base harder to dominate. It has several obvious disadvantages compared to the Alliance base. For example, the Alliance bridge chokepoint is much easier to defend, they can jump our walls to enter, the Alliance graveyard is packed with NPCs and located right outside Vann's building, and you can pull Horde graveyard guards from outside the base walls—just to name a few.

I get that turtle team is not focused on PvP, but AV day feels awful pretty much every time because of this.
