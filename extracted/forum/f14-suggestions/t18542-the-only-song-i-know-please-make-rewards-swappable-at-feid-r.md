<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126439#p126439 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I think it would help guilds in Naxx prog (and tmog enjoyers) to be able to swap quest rewards from the quest The Only Song I Know. The rewards are epic/purple like other swappable items. Being able to swap would enable someone that e.g. mistakenly chose the tank legs to switch for dps legs if their guild asked them to dps for sapphiron instead.
