# Let's make World PvP matter

## Post 106482 by Maaliss — 2024-11-05T08:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106482#p106482 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For many, World PvP, conjures up pictures of shadowy level 60 Rogues ganking lobbies and dancing on their corpses or level 60 Huntards one-shotting from 40+ yards those same lobbies.

But, it doesn't need to be so. World PvP could be both enjoyable, like back in the days or Tarren Mill or WoW-EUs Defias Brotherhood RP-PvP server at its inception where guilds across both factions worked hard to create a "warlike" RP-PvP atmosphere, and World pvp could be meaningful.

How so?

There has been talk of the possible addition of capturable towers such as we have in EPL. But it has been seen that they are virtually ignored once endgame is reached and the zone itself is ignored.

But, what if we had towers in zones where BGs were situated and controlling those towers outside the BGs had effects inside the BG?

For example, there could be 3/5 towers located in Ashenvale which reset to "uncontested" every 3 hours (similar to Gurabashi).

Controlling those towers could give a 1/2/3/3/5% speed and attack speed boost for the controlling faction.

Similar boosts could be made in Arathi with capturable towers there and also in Hillsbrad (bringing back the spectre of Southshore and Tarren Mill). Perhaps an Attack and Armour boost in Arathi and a NPC boost to all GY/Tower/Captains and Generals in AV.

Edit: Or as is mentioned below, the rewards could be Rep and Honour for capturing towers as well as increased tokens, honour and rep inside the BG for the controlling faction whether you win or lose the BG.

Having outside towers effect inside BGs would certainly encourage those areas to be patrolled and controlled bringing back WorldPvP and hopefully boosting BG PvP.

An additional side suggestion - Could we have Rank 10 PvP provide MC/BWL weapons and boost Rank 14 to AQ40/Naxx level please? ![🙏](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f64f.svg)
