## Post 25890 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-23T11:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25890#p25890 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yeah, it would make zero sense to waste materials when it would probably be cheaper to just click one button and have all your gear repaired.

However, I think paying with time would be an interesting concept. Let me explain.
Blacksmiths for example would have a repair skill that would target mail or plate items (also weapons if they are Weaponsmiths? Armorsmiths might cut the cast time in half). It won't cost anything to use (except an anvil maybe), but it would have to be recast on every individual piece you want to repair, and it would have a 30-60 sec cast time. This would make sense to repair at vendors if you're in a hurry, or manually if you want to save a few gold and have nothing better to do. This will also allow crafters to repair other people's gear through the trade window.

Maybe we could add crafting stations for LW and Tailoring, so they won't be able to repair just about anywhere - I propose tanning rack and loom respectively. These would have to be distributed throughout the world next to trainers and vendors though.

I don't think a ton of people would use the feature, but will certainly be nice to have and would be most useful for lategame raiders (whose repair bills are insane) and roleplayers (socialising through "ey, can you repair meh breastplate, lad?" ![😄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f604.svg)) The long cast times would certainly help with the latter.
