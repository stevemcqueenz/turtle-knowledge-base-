# Tents should be exclusive to party members and give additional buffs

## Post 71340 by Frankendank — 2023-10-16T20:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71340#p71340 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tents should be exclusive to party members, I feel like there are too many people gathered in small areas within Tent AOE inside cities/towns/villages.

Not only does it make it easier for players to go around killing others, causing frustration, it also reduces the need to train the survival profession as well as does nothing to help keep items and gold transferring between players.

The items used to make tents (as well as other survival items) are kinda junk items that drop pretty frequently and usually aren't too expensive on the AH because of this. Add on the idea that players may choose to use the Exhaustion Glyph, the survival profession as a whole isn't really worth the time and effort.

I feel like the profession would be more useful if tents provided some kind of additional buff akin to a campfire; another idea is to have the campfires and tents provide buffs if the two are nearby.

This could encourage lower-level players to gain access to tents (and the bright campfires) especially if they could be placed inside dungeons; this would give parties a place to rest, eat, buff, decurse, and whatnot when the dungeon becomes too difficult, ideally leading to less broken parties in the lower levels.

As a side note: the Picnic Basket could be made useful if it was considered to be a cooking fire without giving any buffs; I'm sure this would be an incredible QoL feature that is balanced by the item's 15-minute cooldown.
