 **Add Transmog NPCs to Each Capital City**

[![Image](http://dl7.glitter-graphics.net/pub/727/727097kjhd9gidla.gif)](https://www.glitter-graphics.com)

The Trade District in Stormwind is an extremely congested zone in Turtle WoW. Just running through this part of the city causes lag spikes, and almost makes the location unusable for those playing on lower end computers or suffering poor connection due to IRL location.

I propose spreading out the transmog NPCs to each of the major cities to hopefully cut down on the sheer amount of players in Stormwind. Some good locations can be…

• Hall of Mysteries in Ironforge
•  Craftsman’s Terrace in Darnassus
• Spirit Rise in Thunder Bluff
• and the Apothecarium in Undercity.

With these changes, other cities will likely see more life and it can spread out the currently congested player base leading to better server stability. Plus, it adds to player quality of life.

Thank you for reading!
