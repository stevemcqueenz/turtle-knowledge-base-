# Make birds actually fly in the world!

## Post 159087 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-11-10T17:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159087#p159087 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,

I’ve noticed that in Turtle WoW, most birds are technically “flying” but still move very close to the ground — almost like they’re walking on invisible terrain.

It would be awesome if they could actually fly a few meters above the ground, not too high to be unreachable, but high enough to make the world feel more alive and immersive.

Why this would be great:

![🌍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f30d.svg) Adds realism and immersion — seeing birds flying naturally brings zones to life.

![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) Encourages grouping — melee classes might need ranged help to pull or deal with flying mobs.

![🎯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3af.svg) Adds variety to combat — we’d have to pay more attention to our surroundings, making outdoor gameplay more dynamic.

It doesn’t have to be extreme — just enough height so that they’re clearly flying, but still within aggro or targeting range.
Would love to see this small touch that could make a big difference in world atmosphere!

Thanks for reading, and keep up the amazing work ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)![💚](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f49a.svg)

I think my post was misunderstood regarding the idea that mobs could be positioned up in the air.

The idea is that once they get aggroed — whether by a melee's thrown weapon, a hunter’s bow, or a mage’s frostbolt — these mobs would then come into melee range, just like a bird would swoop down to attack with its beak.

So the entire fight wouldn’t take place at range, only the pull.
This means the loot would indeed end up on the ground.

What would make this interesting is the added world diversity and immersion. We would explore the game world not only by watching out for mobs on the ground (possibly moving in packs), but also for those flying above us that could dive-attack.
