# Use a new toon to give older toon HC rewards?

## Post 8969 by Discostew — 2021-01-08T08:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8969#p8969 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the things I regret doing when first making my toon is not setting up hardcore mode. But now, he's way too far into the game (lvl 60, doing raid content, etc) to where replacing him just to level with HC mode in mind is just not logical. Don't get me started on how long I've spent on the T0.5 questline and RNG turning against me. Yet, my toon is missing out on what HC mode offers.

What about a means to transfer HC rewards and such to the veteran toon by making a new toon under the same account, same class, same race, etc, but runs from lvl 1 to 60 with HC mode enabled? Then when the toon hits lvl 60, that toon can be eligible to transfer their rewards and HC setting to the older toon on the same account, of the same class, race?

## Post 8970 by Lexi (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-01-08T11:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8970#p8970 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Personally  I'd be against it. HC characters are at a disadvantage as far when it comes to getting to 60 and starting endgame content, putting them behind regular characters as far as gear progression. Having a change like this would let you have the best of both worlds even if there's a time sink involved. Despite this I suspect I'm in the minority.

Something to note though is that the points you get at 60 are account wide, not just for that given character.
