# A Hunter's Elegy [capstone overhaul suggestions]

## Post 33380 by Snakeman (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-01-28T19:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33380#p33380 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm a passionate Vanilla hunter player, since the latter half of 2019 - I started playing Classic in September of that year, on Grobbulus Horde-side, though that's neither here nor there. I carried on with the class I'd grown to love coming to Turtle, after brief stints playing other classes I enjoyed in theory but less so in practice, until class changes hit last summer, while I was halfway across the world from my home meeting up with my guildmates in the Rocky Mountains.

I knew from the get-go that Trueshot was never going to be practically implemented as it had been announced - how do you create an ability with a variable cooldown in a game old enough to buy booze, which doesn't support things like random daily dungeon quests? I and a couple of friends rallied against it, but it still shipped, and suddenly one of the few actually involved raid rotations in the game was reduced to yet another one-button masher. I rerolled, again. Twice.

I picked the hunter back up recently, as I was called upon to be a tranq bot in Naxxramas. It didn't feel as bad to play as I expected, but it still feels kinda bad. None of that old vanilla rhythm is there anymore, but I did somewhat enjoy the French weave rotation from TBC classic, and right now with my current weapon speed I can get about half the same high-APM experience that I enjoyed.

But it still leaves me wanting. And Trueshot has done absolutely nothing to combat the fact that all three hunter specs play exactly the fucking same in a PvE environment, which is just terrible game design. Straight up.

My proposal: I think we'd be very well served with some capstone tweaking, similar to how Warriors' talent trees are laid out with a core rotational ability at the top. Yes, the trees are quite well-laid-out as they are now, but there isn't much change in playstyle from picking up each ability.

 **Beast Mastery**'s capstone is incredibly strong, and I cannot deny that. So, in my biased opinion, I feel that Spirit Bond - a relatively useless talent, let's be real - could likely be swapped out for an extra instant melee-range talented attack with a short cooldown, similar to Stormstrike or Bloodthirst maybe? Or if we don't want to lean into the Rexxar-style beastmaster archetype, Kill Command (as it existed in TBC) could be used as a spec-defining ability to fill the rotation.

 **Marksmanship** also is a very well-laid-out tree. In my opinion, a mere reshuffling is in order - move Aimed Shot to the capstone position, Trueshot Aura to where Scatter Shot is currently, and Scatter down to where Aimed is. A huge part of the sameness of hunter currently imo is how easily accessible Aimed Shot is for all specs. My only complaint would be not being able to pick up Aimed Shot as a lowbie until level 40, but it's not like hunter is hurting too much in the levelling department.

 **Survival**, much as I love it, is a bit memey. Nobody ever takes Wyvern Sting over Scatter Shot outside of niche WSG premade scenarios, but it's not like people premade here to the extent they did in Classic. Moving SS down to AS's position on the MM tree already makes it so you can spec both at once, so at that point it'd likely be safe to just make Wyvern Sting baseline and trainable at level 40, and replace it on the Survival tree with something like Explosive Shot or Black Arrow from WotLK. These were, imo, extremely cool, flavourful, spec-defining abilities that made it feel awesome to play Survival. Bonus points if it's like early Wrath Explosive Shot where it did AoE damage to creeps in a certain radius from your target, though maybe nerf the damage a little so it's not too overpowered. lol

As for some baseline suggestions, firstly: make Arcane Shot and fire traps scale with attack power, or at least buff the spell coeffs so spell hunter is a somewhat valid playstyle you can gear towards without coming across like a total asshole. Secondly, and this may be controversial: add a static cooldown to Trueshot. With or without capstone talent changes, it re-adds some of the old dynamic flavouring back to the class' raid rotation, while boosting damage per second for those who don't want to melee weave.

Thirdly: Pet scaling pls. Even just a little. I wanna see my best friend grow with me. (pleading eyes)
