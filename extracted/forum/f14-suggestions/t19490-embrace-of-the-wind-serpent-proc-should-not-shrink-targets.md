# Embrace of the Wind Serpent proc should not shrink targets

## Post 132632 by Suspicious — 2025-05-23T10:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132632#p132632 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, the proc effect of this item applies an armor debuff and life stealing effect.

However, when it does so, it turns the target into a normal sized skeleton. This is a serious issue when many bosses are very large, and can screw up fights like Ragnaros.

Please remove the cosmetic transformation portion of the effect.
