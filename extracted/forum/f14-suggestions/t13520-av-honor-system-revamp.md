# AV Honor System Revamp

## Post 91412 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-26T00:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91412#p91412 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**TL;DR - Honor Rewarding System in AV shouldn't punish players for turtling, instead the Honor gained in turtle-phase should be increased to compensate players' time and to make AV as attractive to farming Honor as other Battlegrounds.**

We can argue which version of AV is better or worse, old 1.5 prenerfed one (currently used in Turtle WoW), 1.12 final Vanilla one or even TBC one with reinforcements system. But no matter what version of AV you enjoy the most, we can all agree that current system of rewarding players with bonus Honor in Alterac Valley is awful and should be changed.

 *Core issue of Honor System in AV*
Let's be honest, people who farm Honor and want to progress their PVP rank choose the battleground which gives them approximately as much bonus Honor as possible. This is why AB is so popular battleground because you are always guaranteed (unless you have really terrible team) to receive at least small chunk of bonus Honor in reasonable time. Not only that, HKs in AB give much more Honor than in AV. In order to gain bonus Honor in AV, you can do four things:

- Kill enemy's Commanders

- Burn down enemy's towers

- Kill Galvangar/Balinda

- Kill last boss: Drek'thar/Vanndar Stormpike
Note all of these objectives can be done only once. Once you kill almost all Commanders, Captain and once you burn most of the towers you won't receive any bonus Honor for the rest of the game until any team fortunately enters the last room and kills AV boss. Moreover, unlike in Arathi Basin the game is not limited by time so you are not guaranteed that if something goes wrong, you will start the game again. The turtle-game is alive for whole time but now without bonus Honor. What's even worse, recently Honor gained in AV from all sources was decreased by 25% meanwhile Honor gained in AB and WSG was increased by 25%. While I don't find anything wrong in reducing Honor gained in objectives of AV (zerging has been efficient strategy for years) I find this change destructive and frustrating for long turtle-AVs because in that games not only you won't receive any bonus Honor but also Honor gained from Honorable Kills is miserable. If turtle-AV occurs, for the next possibly a few hours everyone in AV from Rankers' point of view will play inefficiently. To ilustrate how miserable Honor you gain after entering turtle-phase AV, you approximately won't receive more than 1000 Honor per hour from all these HKs you manage to get. In comparison killing 3 people with War Mode glyph active in open world will approximately grant you that amount of Honor. The Core issue of AV Honor System I'm talking about is that the system doesn't reward the players at all in turtle-phase of AV match. In fact current system actually punishes players for turtling the game if 3 open world HKs grant more Honor than killing dozens of players inside AV. If turtle-match happens, I always notice increased level of frustration and toxicity in the BG chat because people know they are going to waste their time if they have Honor farming on their mind.

The System of Honor rewarding in AV shouldn't be contrary to fundamental design philosophy of old 1.5 AV. Prenerfed Alterac Valley was designed to be a warzone where a battle takes place for long days. In old Vanilla back in 2005 one single match usually lasted for long days until server reset. It was designed to match turtling playstyle from the start. Current Honor System is absolutely contrary to the design if it punishes players for turtling. This should be changed, I tried to make it as simple as possible so developers will have easy way of introducing it to the game.

 *Solution*
What I propose is no fundamental change in AV design but rather adapting Honor System to the design of current 1.5 version of Alterac Valley. The main goal is to make long turtle-games attractive to everyone Honor-wise.

- **Remove "Diminishing returns" mechanic from AV (or set the limit of its impact so everyone is guaranteed to receive at least 20% normal Honor from HK).**

- **After 60 minutes of the match everyone in BG receives 1000 Honor and 1x Alterac Valley Mark of Honor. Then every next 30 minutes everyone gets another 1000 Honor and 1x Alterac Valley Mark of Honor.**

First suggestion is obvious and hits current problem of miserable Honor gained in long games. "Diminishing returns" is the mechanic that lowers the Honor gained from HKs up to 0 every time you kill the same person in one day. In long turtle-games you will end up killing the same guys again and again which results in almost 0 honor-gained-per-hour. I'm not sure if complete removal of this mechanic in AV is good idea though - from my experience I could get about 400 Honor points for killing player of high rank for the first time that day. Thus, the current limit of getting 0% Honor for HK should be raised to 10%, 20% or more - we should experiment and decide what number would be the most fair so Honor gained in turtle-AV remains similar to WSG and AB games. Second suggestion makes turtling more rewarding and also it balances amount of Marks - I find it unfair that you can spent 2 hours in AB and get 12x Marks (supposing you won all 4 games) compared to leaving turtle-AV after 2 hours with no Marks at all. 30 minutes is approximate time of AB and WSG match. Or you played 4 rapid AVs, won all of them in one hour and got 12x Marks. Note that the Mark should be given after 60 minutes of the game so it doesn't affect matches that end rapidly - usual rapid AV game lasts 15-30 minutes.

Personally I enjoy playing turtle-AVs even though they are not rewarding from Honor perspective. Turtle-match feels much more exciting to play and is more tactical. It's much more interesting to engage in large-scale battle in Alterac Valley than zerging to the enemy base and completely avoiding fight with enemies. The changes I suggested seem to be easy to implement and they fix the core issue of AV Honor System. These changes would make AV much more enjoyable battleground for some people. I hope I receive some support for my idea not only from devs but also community.

I like the approach of creating possibilities for turtling in AV in a rewarding manner honor-wise. Since it occurs so much in AV anyways, I think it is legitimate to admit that it is actually part of how the bg is played or plays itself; and since it occurs so much though it is not rewarding in terms of honor as of now, even more so. I think you can make a game only better by realizing how players connect with it, and doing this with AV would communicate to players that they are not randomly bumping back and forth with enemy faction players for hours while feeling that the NPCs stay in their hubs unconcerned staring at the walls.

I believe that it should not just be time-gated though. I would prefer if it would still be connected to the objectives at hand. First thing that comes to my mind is to give honor ticks to towers or graveyards (similar to AB bases, but without bg-winning influences from the ticks themselves), at least under certain conditions. I will try to make a sensible example:

Start of game. Each side has lost no own base, capped no enemy base. No ticks at all.

One side - let's say horde - caps an enemy tower. Ticks start for this one tower for horde. When it is destroyed within this cap, ticks stop and horde gets the usual bunch of bonus honor. The latter gut be bigger depending on how long the game lasts or how often that one tower got recapped.

By having irreversibly lost the tower, alliance would get reduced tick amount of honor if they would cap an enemy tower. This should also put the incentive on actually get to the enemy boss in the end as the structures get capped during a turtle game.

In case of gys, I could imagine the same, which would keep a smaller part of the tick-system active even when all towers are destroyed, should the turtling prevail.

I guess this is not perfect, but among other things mentioned here, I thought I 'd throw in these my 2 pennies should they be proper to become part of a solution.

I imagine this and seeing some bg interface visual representation of this system and its ticks and can not imagine the games becoming as toxic as they do.
