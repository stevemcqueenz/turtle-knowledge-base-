# Noth hates Orcs (just like the rest of the game)

## Post 101795 by Marwy — 2024-09-24T05:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101795#p101795 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Summary: Allowing Noth to drop both the Libram of Light and Totem of Flowing Water in every twow Naxx raid on Nordaanar server exacerbates the player base's bias towards sword and mace-wielding humans by lowering the chance of Hatchet of Sundered Bone dropping (relative to similar items such as Castigator).

Proposal: Move one or both of the Libram of Light and Totem of Flowing Water to another boss's loot table, or move the Hatchet of Sundered Bone to a less crowded loot table where it has a 1/5 chance of dropping.

Issue: unlike in vanilla and classic, cross-faction raiding means that both of the typically faction-locked items on Noth's loot table can drop for every Twow raid regardless of their composition. A libram may drop in a horde-only raid. This would be a mostly benign issue if it did not lessen the chances of items with high ilevel like Hatchet of Sundered Bone dropping relative to similar melee weapons. The Hatchet and the other 6 items that can drop alongside a splinter and a t3 belt token each have a 1 in 7 chance of dropping. Compare this with the 1 in 5 chance that The Castigator has of dropping, the bis 1h mace in the game and which also has 65dps. Or Widow's Remorse, another 65dps weapon. Similarly, Maexxna's fang has a 1/5 chance of dropping, while Iblis has a 1/6 chance of dropping. These are clearly all items intended to be of similar ilevel and intended to meet the needs of different races and classes looking for their 'meta' weapon. In fact, by allowing for double drops of KT loot, a Might of Menethil or a Gressil has more chance of dropping than a Hatchet, because two can drop - and this does indeed happen.

Why should the devs care? Making the bis 1h axe in the game harder to acquire than maces and swords of equivalent ilevel exacerbates the player base's bias towards sword and mace wielding humans, which seems contrary to the proposed weapon skill changes. It also appears to be the result of a small oversight after allowing cross-faction raiding - which can be easily resolved.

Nordanaar vs Tel Abim. With Naxx about to open (?) on Tel Abim, it may be the case that the devs have prevented the faction-specific item from dropping in the 'wrong' raid (i.e. the totem cannot drop in an alliance raid). If so, this means in effect that a player has a better chance of seeing any of the other 5 items on Noth's loot table dropping on Tel Abim than on Nordanaar. This seems like an unintentional, negative difference in experience between players raiding the exact same content. If this loot table issue has in fact not been addressed in Tel Abim yet, it may be that Noth can drop a useless item in an upcoming TA Naxx raid.

Proposed change: to address this issue, the devs can move one or both of the Libram of Light and Totem of Flowing Water to another boss's loot table so that the remaining items on Noth's table have a 1/5 or 1/6 chance of dropping - closer to every other item in Naxx. Or, as is the preference of the author, move the Hatchet of Sundered Bone to a less crowded loot table where it has a 1/5 chance of dropping similar to The Castigator, Widow's Remorse, or caster equivalents such as Wraith Blade or the End of Dreams, and more befitting of its status as the top weapon in its category (1h axes).
