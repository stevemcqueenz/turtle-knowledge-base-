# How to make ships and boat battles

## Post 137058 by Cosmico (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-26T07:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137058#p137058 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!

There is a simple way to make ship battles possible in the game. Also there's a version for single player, party and guild.

Single player mode:

This mode uses the water walking spell applied to the player's ghost when it goes to the shadowlands after being defeated. It creates an image of a simple boat around the player with no further collision issues, with the solely change of not being able to get too close to landmass for it can only be deployed from small and large ports around the world. In the single player boat you can only use the same attack and spells for your character with a range adapted to the boat instead of your player, and the aggro range also widens with it, even affecting the looting range for that purpose.

Party mode:

It follows the same principle of the previous mode with a change, it is no longer a mere fact of graphics but a group mount. We know mounts for several players can be introduced in vanilla without the struggle because the technology has already been done for wow in other expansions, it just has to be introduced. The only significant change, is that the players are all capable attack, use spells and loot. This is no longer a boat but a small ship of a size decent enough. And the spots where the 5 players are sit can all be abandoned to change into the center of the ship to chill and abandon all combat and movement functions, this way all players can switch to all positions of the ship without lacking of options to switch your place. The fun part of this is that one player has to take the back rudder, while two players has to take the paddles in both side to empower the ship movement in order for the player on the rudder to have the charges required to move boat. That leaves only two players capable to perform attack and defensive spells which gives a little bit more of "control" to the chaos of an excessive amount of player attacks.

Guild mode:

This mode now combines the two previous modes: To begin with, it is a mount with the size of a capital ship of any faction however, it can be only ride from the steering wheel. The paddles function are now gone, however there will be three new positions the cannon supplier to charge the cannons, the gunner which is the player who can manage all the canons in the ship, and the trimmer who's the player in charge of changing the sails from the mast for the captain to have the "buffs" instance correct to perform movement actions. The good part of this mode is that it doesn't need to "create physical cannons, mast and sails, it's only graphics. They just need to be interactions of control as if you were riding a battle mount for battle ground for the players to perform their actions for the ship management. Now about the movement, the players are free to move above the ship only, when the captain of the ships turns off the movement and combat functionality. This means, while the ship is moving or during a fight, there is no "permanent" platform and collision structure, this will reduce significantly the charge and weight on the server for that issue. Further on when the captain turns the ship "on" by leaving the helm of the ship, then the ship goes material and is able to sustain players to move freely over all of it's content. Players are able to perform actions like boarding other ships and attacking other ships or players with their own capabilities. The only curious thing about this mode is that once all players are in position and the captain "start" the ship, they all become immune to ordinary attacks but ship attacks, this makes a significant switch in how players receives the damage: "player stance - all damages / ship stance - only ship damage".

The possibilities for content, professions, races, new monsters, dungeons, raids, quest and events, and pirate class is there to be taken!

With the actual content of the game it can be done. It wouldn't change the vanilla content at all. As you're well known, not only the naval battles but pirates are canon has always been canon.

Let's sail to the great seas!
