# Tbc wf and paladin blessings

## Post 23792 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-08-28T09:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23792#p23792 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would liek to suggest changing wf totem into its tbc version.

That's is
 1. Wf totem works for feral druids (feral buff)
 2. Wf totem does not procc on instant attacks (no more hamstring fishing, a buff shouldn't be so powerfull it changes the way to play your class depending you got it or not, slight warrior nerf but will be offset so read on)
 3. Let totems stack with paladin buffs
 4. Blizzard realised this is a necessary change too to balance paladisn and shamans in the same raid with stacking buffs, they also relied hamstring wf design makes balancing a class almost impossible. I not advocating to blindly copy blizzard but in this case they did good!

Now to point 3 and Why it's bad currently
 1. Its very unintuitive, new player keep joining and get surprised by this, because its also nowhere explained in game. Bad design
 2. No other classes biffes have This kind of weird interaction with each other. Bad precendece not fitting vanilla design and contrary to what ppl expect from post tbc
 3. It's only in game for gameplay balance reason of warriors. Therefor if rework wf (slight nerf for warriors) we can allow paladin blessing to stack with wf.
