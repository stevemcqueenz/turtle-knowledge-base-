## Post 133119 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-26T21:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133119#p133119 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Would probably be a b*tch to implement. I don't think there's a way to detect a player standing on a specific tile map, and as such regions will have to be placed (square boundaries) And in order to make them more precise, a lot of em will have to be place.

Some questions then arise, like if it'll stack with other movement speed enhancers. Will it increase only ground movement or also mounted movement? Which paths are going to be deemed as "roads" that will increase MS, as some beaten paths are obviously illustrated on the map as roads, but in actuality don't look the part.

Might also cause players to prioritize walking on roads too much, and thus removing player engagement in certain areas. For example, let's say you're doing a quest and tag a few too many mobs, but luckily for you, a player was running past you and decided to help out. A road may decrease such engagement.

Alternatively, what about entire safe zones, like villages and towns? Will everyone be sprinting while in Stormwind?

I'm not fully against the idea, but the finer edges need to be ironed out before any implementation can even be considered
