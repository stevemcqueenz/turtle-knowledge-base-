# Please re-add Improved thorns

## Post 125538 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-05T11:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125538#p125538 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Okay, so you may have seen this topic before, and while I wholly agree with the sentiment and want to support it the OP wasn't very convincing in regards to why this would be a good change and was only concerned for their own experience, so- I'd like to lay out some positives for bringing this talent back in some way, and then provide suggestions on how we can do that. Everyone wins, even the other 8 classes.

Improved thorns has always been kinda niche unless you're resto or maybe balance playing endgame content. The talent was always easily accessible for them as they had the points to spare. Hell, when I played FERAL I used this talent at endgame. Optimal? No, but, my buddies found it super cool and I remember my main tank always begging for that juicy imp thorns. This is a positive experience.

This isn't solely about that, though, as it had its benefits in levelling content as well. Lemme explain.

Thorns is a semi-niche buff that, in terms of group content, mainly helped tanks hold threat and output a bit of extra damage. This could mean both yourself or another tank that you've grouped with. Improved thorns added to this benefit, making the spell much more useful, but taking up three talent points in the process. Worth it? Debatable, but free will is a concept I'm sure we're all aware of so you can see why I'm in favor of something like this.

Solo play is also another context you can see this talent shine in. Every druid spec will benefit as everyone will get hit sooner or later since most mobs are melee. Thorns adds extra passive damage for mainly ferals, and that's one of the reasons I personally took it as I didn't have to rely on gear as much to get fast kill times.

The reliance on gear for thorns to get better means that, outside of some shiny armor you can put on, it's static and only improves when you get a new rank. I personally believe it would be more attractive of a spell if we gave it some way to be improved outside of gear.

VVV Here's some simple talent suggestions VVV

Just put it back where it was: This one is slightly problematic for mainly one reason. Talent bloat. I think would probably sort of just sit there being mostly unused since there's more talents now.

Bake it into moonfury: This would turn it into a row 3 talent, making it less accessible to non balance specs but literally guaranteed to be taken by balance druids, which gives them a proper niche for support in favor of buffing all druids. Would be cool, but we're trying to do something for everyone, or at least most druids.

Slap it on natural weapons: This one benefits both feral and balance druids, with some very niche usage in resto/balance focused hybrid builds that might want omen of clarity for PvP or something. Not only is it useful, though, it's also... At least a little thematically cohesive? I think thorns counts as a natural weapon, sort of.

Make it the fourth second row talent in resto: This is also an option that could benefit everyone, as ferals need 5 points in furor to get to it, resto druids could access it since they'd take improved MOTW, and balance druids also take imp mark to get to other caster beneficial talents in resto, like subtlety or reflection. The issue is, again, talent bloat.  Resto has a lot of talents already, so for them it would probably not get used, or- at best you're only dipping into it a little. I'm not all that well read on resto druid stuff so someone might have to correct me or explain what talents are and aren't necessary.

Bake it into improved mark and rename it to improved wild blessings (or something cooler idk): This one makes getting this as a feral less worth it due to being tied to a talent they probably can't afford, but still aids restos and balance druids as they always take imp mark anyways.

I think that's about it from me, though. I'm hoping we improve the levelling experience for all classes with changes like these instead of hard focusing on endgame, and offer realistic build variety for those who want to experiment and try something unique. Cheers, turtles.
