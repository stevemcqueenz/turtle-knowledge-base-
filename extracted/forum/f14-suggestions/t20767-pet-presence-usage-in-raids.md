# Pet Presence/Usage in Raids

## Post 144050 by Dragonfury — 2025-08-09T19:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144050#p144050 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the upcoming 1.17.2 changes to Demonology and Beast Mastery, are there any plans to improve how pets react to raid boss mechanics?

Right now, pets often have to be dismissed because they either die to unavoidable mechanics (like C’Thun’s Eye Beam or Vaelastrasz’s Burning Adrenaline) or soak damage that puts extra pressure on healers or disrupts the fight (bounce mechanics, AoE, etc). This makes these specs hard to use consistently in raids — half the time you’re just babysitting your pet instead of focusing on your own rotation and positioning.

Could pets be made mostly unaffected by these kinds of boss mechanics, similar to how they’re ignored during the Thaddius polarity shift? This would let Demonology and Beast Mastery players treat pets more like alternative damage tools (like Survival traps), freeing them up to focus on the main character.

It’s like asking a warrior to manage their melee weapon if it could be killed by AoE or boss mechanics. Modern WoW moved pet specs away from this babysitting model, and I don’t think making pets more resistant to raid mechanics here would break anything. It would simply make these specs more viable and enjoyable to play in raids.

This should be a relatively simple fix on a Mangos-based core - adding immunity or ignoring certain boss abilities for pets can be done by adjusting spell or mechanic handling in the boss scripts. It’s a low-risk change that affects only pets and specific mechanics, so it won’t alter raid difficulty or player balance.

Yeah, the problem isn’t that pets never get spared by mechanics, it’s that there’s no real consistency, so most people just play it safe and call “no pets” for certain fights. Even if C’Thun’s beam doesn’t chain to pets anymore, that’s not exactly common knowledge, and raid leaders aren’t going to gamble a pull just to test it.

The new BM positioning command in 1.17.2 will definitely help in some cases, but it doesn’t solve fights where the mechanic itself is the issue — you can have your pet perfectly placed and it’ll still get one-shot or cause a chain reaction.

The C’Thun P2 “instant death” is a perfect example, it’s not player error, it’s just an unavoidable mechanic wiping out half your damage. The fact a lot of locks swap to SM/Ruin just to avoid these headaches is proof there’s still a friction point, not that the spec’s fine.

As for Brainwashing Device — yeah, it works, but having to buy a gadget to make a spec work on certain fights isn’t really a good long-term solution. Specs shouldn’t need outside gimmicks to function in common raid situations.

Turtle’s definitely been going in a good direction with spec viability, but I think tightening up pet immunity to raid mechanics would take that “most content” to “almost all content,” and make BM/Demo less about pet babysitting and more about actually playing the spec.
