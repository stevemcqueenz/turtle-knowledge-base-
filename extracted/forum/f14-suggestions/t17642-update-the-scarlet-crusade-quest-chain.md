Originally this was going to lead to a Scarlet Crusade Citadel raid that ended up going nowhere, I recently finished it and got an earring that supposedly marks me as part of the Crusade, but it ends up doing nothing, so I got to thinking:
What if they change the objective?
New Avalon is just there, painted, not used much, so I thought maybe, make the player part of the Scarlet Crusade (lengthening the quest chain a bit because it's too simple in my opinion), and have the player put the trinket on and be friendly TO THE ENTIRE CRUSADE, so they can talk to NPCs and have them give the player quests in the Plaguelands, Tirisfal, etc.

After all, there are NPCs who are friendly, we have them in the Light's Hope Chapel itself and in Stormwind, perhaps those from Tyr's Hand and New Avalon are not as crazy as those from the Monastery and they still have good quests to give us that can benefit everyone from the hand of the Argent Dawn.

I say this so that the pendant that the quest gave us is not so useless.
