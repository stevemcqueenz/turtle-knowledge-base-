# Alterac Valley suggestions

## Post 43661 by Rat2156 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-04-28T17:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43661#p43661 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**-Preface and Design Philosphy**

First of all, I think I can speak for many when I say that Alterac Valley is one of the most beloved features of vanilla WoW.

Or at least, what AV was meant to be is. While Turtle's version of AV is far ahead of Blizzard Classic's version already, it's no secret that it's flawed, in large part because of how the players themselves play it. As it stands, 9 times out of 10, it's treated as a purely PvE battleground where teams just avoid each other in the aim of ending it as fast as possible for maximum honor gains. When it does end up lasting longer than that, players often find that the rewards for actually fighting other players tend to be somewhat lackluster, which in turn discourages longer games in the future.

Suggestions given to fix Alterac Valley often stop at simply removing bonus honor from it. I don't believe this would have the intended effect, as while it would encourage PvP, it does take one of the facets of the original PvPvE design away. Furthermore, it would simply turn it into something no different from just fighting in the world without any goal or objective, if honor killing became the only rewarded thing in AV. People could simply go and do that in the other battlegrounds and get greater rewards from it.

My suggestions instead aim at turning objectives from a direct source of honor into something facilitating the PvP aspect of the battleground, and in turn making PvP **indirectly** the most rewarding activity in AV. You'd want to actively kill players in order to generate turn in ressources, and turn ins and point captures would in turn embolden your side to kill more enemy players.

 **-Suggestions**

The core of my suggestions is based on two changes.
 -Dramatically reduce bonus honor from objectives
 -Grant bonus honor to your entire team for turn ins

Turn in rewards should (in my opinion) be tuned as such, in rough ascending order
 *-Minor honor gain*
 -Armor scraps (mostly no PvP involved)
 -Tamed rams and wolves (same as above)
 -Friendly mine supplies (Irondeep for Alliance, Coldtooth for Horde). These should reward slightly more than scraps and tamed beasts, due to the tedium involved)
 -Racial trophies (PvP is involved but those grant no benefit to your team. Simple bonus honor per kill)

 *-Moderate honor gain*
 -Air force : Soldier
 -Ram/Wolf hides (technically PvE, but involves going deep into enemy territory and returning)
 -Unfriendly mine supplies (Coldtooth for Alliance, Irondeep for Horde)
 -Storm Crystals/Stormpike Blood
 -Air force : Lieutenant

 *-Higher honor gain*
 -Air force : Commander

(note that no values are given and those designations are relative. Obviously don't expect to get 5000 honor points for turning in a commander medal)

Furthermore, completing a turn in objective should also be rewarded with bonus honor relative to the difficulty of achieving it. Summoning your lord specifically should be a notable reward due to the nature of that event (and also because it's cool as fuck and you all know it), same for slaying the other faction's lord.

With these changes, rushing to victory should be the less effective way to gain honor, while engaging in combat and turning in the spoils of battle should be the best way. In simpler terms, fight for honor, win for reputation.

The reason for making turn in honor team-wide rather than personal is twofold. First, it means people won't have to fight over every corpse when they're supposed to be a team. Secondly, it means that comparatively weaker players can scavenge corpses to the benefit of the entire team, without forcing the more geared and experienced players to stop fighting. It also means lower level players can benefit the team both in contributing to turn ins and in gaining honor by gathering scraps and supplies, and taming beasts. Everyone can be a cog in the war machine, instead of letting the Naxx geared players fight the battle while the rest tries to not get oneshot.

 **-Snowfall Graveyard**

Another potential change I could see would be regarding Korrak and the Winterax. Tagging and killing Korrak could grant a very significant chunk of honor to the team killing it. I could see concerns behind having a large reward behind a PvE activity, but if the reward is significant enough, both factions will want it. That means fierce fighting for the right to kill Korrak and to deny the other team from getting the kill. That would allow yet another reason for PvP to happen, while making it so Korrak actually matters in AV, as opposed to now where he is literally never fought by anyone.
Now, a concern I've had is that once Korrak is dead, there is simply no longer any reason to interact with the entire Winterax area whatsoever. I believe it could be interesting to give a reason for teams to want to hold the Snowfall graveyard, for example, giving periodic bonus honor to the team currently holding it, in a somewhat similar fashion to AB flags. By doing so, you'd give both teams a reason to fight over the graveyard.

 **-Conclusion**

As stated earlier in this post, my aim is to make Alterac Valley closer to what it was once supposed to me, even if it means changing what it is and what it was. As is the case for Turtle WoW as a whole, the spirit of vanilla matters more than the reality of vanilla, and that was the leading thread for my suggestion. The spirit of AV is having long, drawn out battles for ressources and territory, even if the reality of it is that you can just rush to Drek or Vann, or just let the other team win. As someone who loves AV and remembers it for what it once was very fondly, I would love to see it being made into what it wishes to be.

Of course, I am far from immune to having stupid ideas or bad opinions, so feel free to discuss, point out flaws, make counter suggestions or tell me to jump into Ironforge's lava pits. These suggestions are just that, suggestions, and can likely be tweaked and made better by others.

This must happen in custom vanilla server which Turtle WoW - the server that respects old spirit of the game.

To see how AV looks like now, simply join AV match in the evening or AV day. The average game looks like this: about 75% of the team runs to enemy territory and captures all the towers, kill all commanders along the way in sequence for max honor gain. Generally this largest group is trying to clear all enemies that give bonus honor as fast as possible. A few players (sometimes nobody) go to the closest Mine and farm Armor Scraps. The rest of the team is either defending or trying to kill Korrak. These groups (especially the first) are considered useless or even harmful by most of the honor farmers because they slow down the enemy's attack which makes game longer and can potentially make nightmare of turtle AV come true. Some players don't even want to defend the base because letting enemy team win actually gives more honor per hour to losing rank farmers. The average game lasts 20-30 minutes. Min-maxing has turned this battleground into abomination. All these repeatable PVE quests means nothing nowadays (maybe armor scraps are still valuable). Nobody cares about cavalry, air forces, mine supplies or even summoning elemental bosses no more. Because what's the point of completing these quests if average match ends in 30 minutes. It's sad to see that joy was taken out of this battleground.

This is why Turtle developers MUST introduce changes. Changes to the current ill meta where teams just avoid each other in the aim of ending it as fast as possible for maximum honor gains. Basically everything suggested by OP is a step in the right direction. The idea to restore AV design philosophy is generally to encourage "turtle AV" - make games last longer by encouraging players to do PVE objectives. 2 hours AV games still can happen but let's be honest, they are rare. In other words, bring the fun back to the Alterac Valley!
