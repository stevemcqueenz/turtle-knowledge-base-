# Open a Poll About The Servers Future?

## Post 64340 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-09-15T12:30:00Z

As the title really, I know the Devs are under a tremendous amount of stress getting all these new servers up and running, but if we take a step back for a second, is this really what everyone wants?

I know everyone cannot be pleased, this is impossible, but what we can do is try make an open discussion with the players and how they would like to see the server moving forward.

I think it is the wrong approach for the Devs to just decide amongst themselves to make a PvP Server, it should be open to the players, so I suggest management makes a poll on Google, much like that really good class changes poll that someone made a few months back.

In all my years on Turtle Wow,  I've never seen so much backlash.

This fresh realm is a hot topic on the forums, in discord and in game, it seems alot of people are very unhappy. So I feel we should tread very lightly on how we shape the servers future.

+1. While I do think the devs should have the last say because they see the larger picture and have more insight into game design than players, polling can be a valuable tool in gauging player feedback and interest. Forcing through changes that increase profits, but which hurts the community, isn't necessarily good for the long term health of the game, for example. I'm particularly interested in how OSRS do their development; they only implement changes and new features if 75 % (?) of the players agree. If a change doesn't make it through the poll, it will sometimes get tweaked before going through another poll at a later date where it might pass.

However, in regards to opening a new server, it isn't merely about players' opinions, but also about trying to appeal to people that are not players yet. Most people prefer to play with like-minded people, so in that regards having a separate PvP-realm might reduce friction on the main server while expanding the total amount of TWoW players.
