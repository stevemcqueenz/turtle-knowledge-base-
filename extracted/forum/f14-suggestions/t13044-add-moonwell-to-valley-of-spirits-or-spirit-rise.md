## Post 87509 by Mistermadigan — 2024-02-11T02:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87509#p87509 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, I don't want this to come across as "typical argument from angry Horde Tailor who has to make a trip to make Mooncloth" - because I don't currently have a Horde Tailor.  But I think there are lore and practical gameplay reasons for Horde having a Moonwell.

Granted I get the whole "moonwells = night elves" argument.
But then you get into the topic of Healing Springs/Mana Springs from WC3.  Are those naturally occurring, or were those created by individuals interacting with Nature?  Do those function similar to Moonwells?  You see a number of those around Murloc camps in WC3 - were they created by Murloc Shamanism perhaps?  Or are they also natural well-springs from the same source as the Well of Eternity - the lifeblood of Azeroth.

If we're going with the faction balance argument, the Stormwind Moonwell has always given Alliance a gameplay advantage in terms of travel time.  Now this is not a major issue, but there is nothing to balance the scale.  There's no "Dark Forge" in the Cleft of Shadows that lets Horde Miners smelt Dark Iron Bars for example.

Let's make things even simpler though.  There is no Druid Trainer in Orgrimmar.  Why does Stormwind have/need a Druid trainer when Orgrimmar does not have one?  The Night Elves don't need a presence in SW - for them to have a Moonwell in SW is "way" too trusting of them considering the newness of their alliance with the Humans if they're as special as the lore claims - especially given their mistrust of Arcane magics - which SW is filled with.

Given that Night Elves can make new Moonwells - the question then becomes whether all that water is originally sourced from the original Well of Eternity (which blew up), or if the waters at the base of Nordrassil make up a second Well of Eternity.

This further complicates the Lore because on the one hand you have the natural magic waters of Azeroth and the "blessing of Elune" - which one exactly makes the Moonwells special?
 - Because if it's the Well of Eternity - Tauren would get access to that via the Cenarion Circle.
 - If it's the "blessing of Elune" - Tauren could use their own connection with the moon to replicate that.

So while I get that Moonwells are the Night Elf's "thing" - I don't see a barrier preventing Tauren from being unable to make one of their own.

Not really a big deal, but it always bugged me for a number of reasons.
