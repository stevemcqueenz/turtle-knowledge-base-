# Chronobreaker Needs a Buff

## Post 165237 by Magicknight — 2026-01-15T13:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165237#p165237 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Greetings my fellow Turtles.

As the title suggests, I feel the item **Chronobreaker** ( <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61049> ) needs to be buffed or modified to represent its status as a unique rare weapon, and coincide with other unique weapons for which their extremely low drop rates make them both useful and desirable.

So, what (in my opinion) am I classing as a unique weapon?
Well, any weapon that has a unique ability (active or passive) attached to that item, that no other item has in Turtle WoW. For example:

 **Blackblade of Shahram** ( <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=12592> )
 **Runeblade of Baron Rivendare** ( <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13505> )
 **Felstriker** ( <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=12590> )

All the above weapons are imbued with a unique attribute that no other item has in Turtle WoW. There maybe items that do similar things, but not the same – that is, the spells associated with these weapons are completely unique to their weapon only.
The weapons above are also in the “extremely low drop rate” bracket of about 1%, making them as rare as hens teeth! The unique attributes on all these items are just incredible too, and so unique they really stand out, again making them even more desirable.

Now, getting back to Chronobreaker, the weapon itself is also in the “extremely low drop rate” bracket of about 1% which should give it that useful and desirable status I mentioned before. However, the attribute attached to the weapon is not unique (“Thrash” <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=21919> is found on two other weapons in Turtle WoW) and the swing speed of 2.9 doesn’t make it that user-friendly for any class who can use it.

If we examine its name “Chronobreaker”: Chrono comes from the Greek word chronos to mean ‘time’ and Breaker, well, just crudely means to break something! So, we have a time breaking weapon. A sword that literally means to disturb, disrupt, warp the fabric of time, and by that Turtle WoW mean you have a chance of an extra swing!
In my opinion this does not give it the unique weapon status afforded to the weapons already mentioned, or any of the other ultra rare drop items in the game. Therefore, I propose the weapon itself is modified to reflect this ultra rare status with a few ‘chance on hit’ suggestions:

 1.	**Phase Blade** – Chance on Hit: Summons the Chronobreaker from another point in time to fight for you, before returning to the void. All damage caused by this summoned blade ignores all armour. Lasts 15 seconds.
 *(Essentially, a second Chronobreaker pops up next to you, hitting your target at the same weapon speed and damage of the Chronobreaker, but as it is out of phase with the world, it does so ignoring all armour)*

 2.	**Time Strike** – Chance on Hit: Weapon speed increased by 30% and all melee attacks ignore all armour. Lasts 10 seconds
 *(Increased weapon speed is not unique, but ignoring all armour for the duration is)*

 3.	**Planar Shift** – Chance on Hit: You are partially enveloped by the Void of time, reducing all damage against you by 30% and all healing towards you by 30%. Lasts 10 seconds
(*All damage includes melee damage and all schools of magic including Holy. Planar Shift would be considered a debuff and therefore, could not be removed / purged by friend or foe. The 30% healing reduction would affect external spells from allies only or that of cast spells on oneself, but would not affect self-healing abilities innate to the specific class. For example: Hunters ‘Spirit Bond’ or Warriors ‘Blood Drinker’, etc.)*

 4.	**Void Explosion** – Chance on Hit: the energies of the Void explode from the Chronobreaker causing 200-300 Arcane damage to all targets around the wielder, and reduces the movement speed of all targets by 50% for 3 seconds. During this time all affected targets cannot perform any melee actions or cast spells. The damage caused ignores all resistances.
 *(Good ‘ol all encompassing damage with a bit of CC thrown in!)*

I also feel that as the weapon name alludes to ‘time’ the weapon speed itself should also be altered to either be slower (3.50 – 3.80) or faster (2.10 – 2.40), rather than its current speed of 2.90 which I feel doesn’t suit anyone.

TL:DR – Chronobreaker needs to change to reflect its ultra low-rate drop status.

Let me know in the comments below.

I don't think this Chance on Hit ability would be as broken as you think in PvP, and PvP is not static so the chances of you getting a full 10 seconds of 100% armour reduction on one target would be very low. Furthermore, Druids, Paladins and Warriors have other damage reduction mitigation in the form of dodge, block & parry.

However, with this in mind, I did a simple test of the effect of ignoring 100% of armour on two training dummies. The Expert Training Dummy has 3,000 armour, equivalent to a 30% physical damage reduction. The Apprentice Dummy has 100 armour which can be reduced to zero armour with one application of Sunder Armor.

Using the same weapon: Halberd of the Bronze Flight (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60498>) I attacked both Target Dummies separately, for approximately 10 seconds and recorded the damage done:

Expert Dummy: 6,261 overall damage
Apprentice Dummy: 7,151 overall damage

So, a damage boost for sure but nothing totally game breaking, and furthermore, Chronobreaker's top end damage isn't great anyway for a required 60 item, so even burst damage with the buff will still be in the toilet!

To my mind, ignoring all armour for such a small window of opportunity isn't game-breaking, but can still add flavour to both PvE and PvP encounters alike.
