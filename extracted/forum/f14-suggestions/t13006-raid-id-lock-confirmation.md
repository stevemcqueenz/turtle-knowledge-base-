## Post 87102 by Thejakemf — 2024-02-06T18:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87102#p87102 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Can we please get a “are you sure you want to lock yourself to this raid ID?” confirmation?

A few weeks ago a group in my guild all lost their K10 lockout because someone entered before them with a saved ID; last night I had an alt that was level 53 earlier in the day and attuned about 3 hours before raid get locked to an MC ID from almost a week ago. I don't even really understand how it happened as I was in a raid group with 20 other people, but somehow I was the only one to get zoned into the locked ID of one of our members.

Both of these cases and MANY others could easily be solved by a simple yes no confirmation (like you get for summons, resurrections, BG queues, etc.) asking if you want to enter this raid and become saved to that ID.
