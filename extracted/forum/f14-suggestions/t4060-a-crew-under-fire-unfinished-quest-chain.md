# "A Crew Under Fire" unfinished quest chain

## Post 24473 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-06T08:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24473#p24473 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/dMwU1pE.jpg)
Hey, so I was questing in Azshara and came upon a ship crew barricaded behind the remains of their ship, defending against nagas and decided to help them out. It was tough as nails (especially since I was at the minimum required level for the quest), but with the help of a few healing potions and a few sailor casualties, we were able to defeat the attacking nagas. The captain was thankful and told me to ask her crew for any followup quests. Except there weren't any. And the "A Crew Under Fire" was repeateable. And it didn't have any rewards, not even XP! I felt a bit scammed out of my time and out of potentially interesting content.
Checking on wowhead, it turns out other players have felt the same way and all Blizzard has ever said about it was "Oh, it's an unfinished quest chain, sorry. You can repeat it if you want, lol". Yeah, like we are made out of time.

I think it has the potential to be a really interesting mini-story of quests if developed further. It fits with turtle's philosophy of continuing to develop vanilla further to add some more quests after the initial one, maybe even turn it into an attunement chain for the so-requested ship battle dungeon.

Barring that, at least add some sort of reward to this quest and make it non-repeatable so people won't waste their time for nothing anymore.

## Post 33446 by Mac (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-01-29T14:55:00Z

In Azshara, there's a sunken ship that is supposed to have some unused NPCs in it.  These seem to be connected to A Crew Under Fire.  They aren't in-game on Turtle WoW, but you can actually see them in [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SdJg8Vc0KO0&t=120s) from 2008, and they exist in the [database](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=8394).  There are also two unused quests involving these unused NPCs (a [quest](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=3383/unused) to kill them, and this [quest](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=3384/unused) involving one of the NPC's mana gem, which is also in the [database](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=10478)).

Currently, the sunken ship is now used for a different, unrelated Turtle WoW custom quest I believe, and the NPCs are still nowhere to be seen.  Still, it would be nice to see this quest expanded upon, and as Thol said, anyone who'd like to can feel free to write their own.  It can be a lot of fun writing custom quests.
