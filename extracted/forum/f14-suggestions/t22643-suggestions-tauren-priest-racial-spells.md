# [Suggestions] Tauren Priest racial spells

## Post 157584 by Tartopoires — 2025-10-28T21:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157584#p157584 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I was told to post these ideas on here, for the upcoming Tauren priest combo, so here we go :

 **[Level 10 spell]** **Blessed Blow** (or **Nature Strike**)
Rather short cd, shorter than Nelves' Starshards. Discipline spell as well.
Hits with weapon, scales on weapon flat damage (or dps), attack power, and Spirit. Benefits from spellcrit instead of physical crit, but still linked to physical hit chances.
Holy damage, or physical, or a mix of both. (If Holy synergy too powerful, Nature damage instead? = Nature Strike)
Possibly shorter cd if using 1handed. (Or not, to incentivize using 2handers - See end of suggestion)
Possibly apply on next melee hit, like Heroic Strike and Holy Strike.

[Level 20 spell] **Power word: Grace**
Dodge buff and increases movement speed of X% for X seconds
Applies same debuff than Power word: Shield (maybe shorter?), but way longer cooldown.
Defensive ability / Gap closer

Bonus suggestion : Like Nelves with bows, Tauren priests would be able to use 2handed maces, because they stronk + synergy with Blessed Blow / Nature Strike

Talents changes : Return of Attack power bonus to Inner Fire

Tell me what you guys think of it, or what you would change!   satisfied_turtle_head

> Since tauren are strong by its nature, what about carying shields instead of just a normal offhand?

A priest wielding a shield brings to mind clerics, just like with wielding 2handed maces.
So that'd fit the class fantasy! And look badass!!
We'd be tempted to argue all of these additions would change the priest into a paladin, but paladins are not just touched-by-light 2hander/shield wielders. Spells are differents, and priests can only wear cloth.
That being said, that would be the closest thing to a paladin that the Horde could have.

On another note, considering the huuuge extra armor added to disc/holy priests healing capacities, there could be (probably, in fact) balance issues (for PVP). Maybe, in that case, add it as a perk of Holy tree's 31 points talent, or a parallel second 31 points talent, since Holy seems more PVE-oriented.
And/or adding some sort of debuff to wielders, like being unable to cast or keep Inner Fire.

Now there is a thing I didnt really think about: Shadow priests, and how all these suggestions would interfere with their spec.
We would also be tempted to say that this strays too far from the original design.
In hindsight It is likely that, in fact, racial spells of Tauren priests should have less impact on gameplay.
