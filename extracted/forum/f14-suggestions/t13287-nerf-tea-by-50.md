# Nerf Tea by 50%

## Post 89128 by Krotux (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-03-01T13:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89128#p89128 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tea is objectively OP in classic. Originally, it was a one-time thing. Then it was possible to farm, and now it's freely accessible for around 40s.

It's stronger than healthstones AND also regen mana. Stronger than dark runes / demonic runes AND instead of costing health it also GIVES health.

Tea is objectively OP compared to all the similar non-potion consumables. Heck, even compared to potions it's way better than health and mana potions.

 **Even if you nerf tea by 50%, it would still be really good compared to other options.** Regen 500-600 health AND mana would still be competitive. If people want maximum mana they should use runes, and if people want maximum health regen they should use healthstones.

One counterpoint is that hybrid classes are balanced around tea. That is not true, and even if it was, that should be addressed in the next class change update.

Buffing bosses would not achieve anything for this issue. In fact, if that's the only change they implemented, it would only increase the dependency/value of Nordanaar Tea.

Also, whether any consume is 'mandatory' or not, is besides the point. The fact is, for how easy it is acquire Nordanaar Tea, it simply is too powerful. Compare it to Dark/Demonic Runes (share same cooldown) which are infinitely more difficult to farm, aren't as powerful and have a negative side effect too. Furthermore, the salt into the wound is the fact that the Tea shares a cooldown with two class spells (Mage Mana Gem and Warlock Healthstone) and is more powerful than them. The saving grace is that it doesn't share a cooldown with potions otherwise, trust me, people would forgo using Major Mana Pots too.

The simplest solution is to either reduce the health/mana restored or give it a seperate cooldown which doesn't overlap with runes or healthstone/mana gem. However, if the latter option was done, this would further trivialise mana management in TWoW for raid encounters.
