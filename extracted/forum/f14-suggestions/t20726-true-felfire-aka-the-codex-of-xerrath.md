## Post 143722 by Zaxl — 2025-08-08T00:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143722#p143722 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Suggestion: Implement "The Codex of Xerrath" as a Cosmetic Glyph or Questline Reward for Warlocks**

I'd like to suggest a flavorful addition to the Warlock class that would enhance roleplay and class identity without impacting gameplay balance:

 **The Codex of Xerrath** as a cosmetic transformation for Warlocks' fire spells. Originally introduced in retail WoW, The Codex of Xerrath is a passive ability that changes all of a Warlock’s fire spells to cast green fel fire instead of the traditional red/orange flames. This would fit right in as a cosmetic-only customization, perfect for immersion, roleplay, and veteran Warlocks who want to show their mastery over fel corruption.

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/429239-the-codex-of-xerrath.jpg)

This could be implemented in a variety of ways:

Option 1: Glyph of the Fel Flame
•	A glyph that, when applied, transforms all fire spell visuals into green fel fire. This would work in a way similar to how Druid form glyphs function.
•	This could be offered as a Donation Shop reward or obtained through a custom crafting process.

Option 2: Questline – "Pursuing the Black Harvest"
•	A custom Turtle WoW quest chain available at max level, perhaps involving a demonic ritual or a challenge (akin to the green fire questline in MoP).

...or maybe both!   hiding_smth_turtle_head
