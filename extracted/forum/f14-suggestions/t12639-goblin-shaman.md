So yes, I know it has been suggested and, by some, rejected several times in the past, but I still think goblin shaman would bring the race an influx of new players in addition to those already gained from the new character customization and starting zone.

I don't personally buy the idea that goblins as a race can't align themselves with being shaman - the durotar labour union should be more flexible.
