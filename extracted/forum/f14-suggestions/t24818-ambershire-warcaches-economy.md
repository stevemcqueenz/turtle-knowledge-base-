## Post 174708 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-04-10T15:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174708#p174708 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Economy changes were done because almost every year (probably after content patches) people massively complained about high prices for consumables.

You can find reason why devs implemented caches with consumables in old economy updates:
[PvP & Economy Update](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=12138) - it was the first attempt to intervene economy to reduce prices for consumables.
[Economy Update — Consumables & Gold Farming](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=12931) - elixirs now persist through death
[Economy Update — Nodes, Trash & Consumables](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13448) - they nerfed gold income from lashers.
[Economy Update — GDKP, TDKP, and Turtle WoW](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=12932)

In short - people in raids spend enormous amount of effort to get all consumables possible.
Because of high demand for consumables and low supply of natural herbs in open world, people started to farm lashers.
With lashers they also farmed raw gold, which indirectly increased prices for everything.
Some guilds used to grant items to players for gold. It also encouraged people to farm gold. Best way to farm gold was to farm herbs and sell them.

So all game was reduced to farming lashers, which is not that turtle devs wanted.
Turtle devs added war caches to give people alternative to farming lashers. People can participate in normal dungeon boss killing and get consumables.

I don't exactly know why caches contain final products. Maybe there are additional reasons for that.

Even as someone who was looking forward to the advantage of making more consumes for myself and not having to worry about buying them through herb/alch, I feel like keeping some sort of cache system that lets players at least have the materials to make potions or have other people make potions is alright. This can also lead to more social economic interactions because, obviously, not everyone is going to have alchemy or a character that has it. They'll need someone else to refine the material into a product. Pretty fun multiplayer experience, right?

And, in the case that you're too anti-social to go out of your way to look for an alchemist, this can also gives you an incentive to make more alts. Leveling is a core part of vanilla, so the game giving you reasons in small and simple ways to do it more is pretty neat. Not that you really need incentive, considering the fact that it's, well... You know... Fun.

Quick edit actually:

I'm sort of neutral on already made potions being in the cache. It's less in the spirit of an MMO because, well... You really only need to just do the thing to get the cache and be done with it. That removes the previously mentioned incentive to interact with people. The positive, of course, is not having to dump a bunch of gold into consumes. And we all like saving money, right?
