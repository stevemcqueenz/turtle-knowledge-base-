# Why Thunder Bluff and Darnassus Should Also Have a Jewel Crafting Trainer

## Post 117466 by Valari (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-01-21T20:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117466#p117466 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://64.media.tumblr.com/c5534df89e814134036b31310a43c29b/5e9b4a0c599488d9-63/s500x750/ef576f450d8e01743061e489084ddae4c0870c85.jpg)

The Kaldorei, who were once one society with the Highborne, are known for their shared history in Blacksmithing, Jewelcrafting, Runic Enchanting, and Beadwork having descended from the Trolls. One only needs to look at their official art, the art of the in game items, who they drop from, their history, and the interior design of Darnassus as well as Kaldorei settlements to know that these arts are alive and well in the current Kaldorei society. Having ten thousand years of history in the practice of making fine metalwork, glasswork, beadwork, woven plant matter, feathers, woodcutting, and gemstone enchantments one would imagine the Kaldorei to be amongst the best jewel crafters on Azeroth

You would be wrong in Turtle WoW. ![🥹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f979.svg) But that doesn’t mean we don’t have the chance to make this right! ![😄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f604.svg)

Unfortunately, the Tauren and Kaldorei have had their major cities of Thunder Bluff and Darnassus excluded from having jewel crafting trainers when these are the civilizations that actually decorate every part of their homes with beads and incorporate these motifs into their armor, headdresses, and weapons. This goes against lore, and it would add to the immersion of the game to add jewel crafting trainers to these cities. We do not need a lack of trainers in a certain city for a “vanilla feel” if it comes at the price of immersion. To make matters worse, even in other Alliance cities there is no Kaldorei jewel crafting trainer. The end trainer is a Gnome, the race I would least believe to be interested in frivolities for the Alliance aside from gemstones cut for the creation of Engineered goods. The Kaldorei and Tauren should both see trainers in their cities - and have room for them too. In the Craftsman’s Terrace right next to the First Aid trainer, there is an empty house both on ground and second story level laying open - almost begging for a permanent NPC resident.

The Kaldorei use necklaces to symbolize bonding just as much as wedding rings. Bracers are a sign of honor and friendship and awarded just as much as they are treasured. Headdresses symbolize rank and merit, and they use very specific items which have both cultural and spiritual significance which serves both utilitarian and ornamental purposes.

Below, I have compiled a wealth of lore on the Kaldorei and Quel’dorei. While not all comes Vanilla, the parts that do not fit into Vanilla lore and were continuations of the Kaldorei aesthetic which should be understandable to be included given TBC items that were recolored and added to Alah’thalas in the Quel’dorei Turtle WoW addition.
