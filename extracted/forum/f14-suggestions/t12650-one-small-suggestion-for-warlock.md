# One small suggestion for Warlock

## Post 84299 by Ravador — 2024-01-11T11:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84299#p84299 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the newly appointed class ambassadors, I wanted to chime in with a small change, that in my opinion could by itself create another viable PvE talent spread for Warlocks.

Buff Siphon Life.
How?
TLDR: Make Siphon Life proc Shadow Trance and optionally increase the SP scaling per tick of Rank 4 Siphon Life to 0.075 from 0.05.

The longer, detailed explanation:
#1 Make it able to proc Shadow Trance (from the Nighfall talent)
This effectively doubles the chance of a Shadow Trance proc, while costing a GCD and 365 mana for each Siphon Life application.

#2 (optional) Increase the SP (spell power) scaling.
To justify the increase in SP scaling for Siphon Life, let's look at the numbers. Siphon Life is an instant DoT effect that lasts 30 seconds, ticking every 3 seconds. This effect heals the caster for the amount of damage dealt on every tick. Rank 4 of Siphon Life deals 45 shadow damage every 3 seconds, with SP scaling of 0.05 (5 bonus damage per 100 SP). To adjust for the fact that it heals the caster, let's double its tick value to 90 + 0.1 * SP. This gives us a full value of 900 + 1 * SP.
I know this is not perfect and that the value of healing is dependent on the situation, but this makes the calculations easier.
Let's compare Siphon Life to Corruption, which is the main DoT effect Warlocks use. Corruption is an effectively instant (because all Warlocks max the Improved Corruption talent) DoT effect that lasts 18 seconds, ticking every 3 seconds. Rank 7 of Corruption deals 137 shadow damage every 3 seconds, with SP scaling of 0.167 (16.7 bonus damage per 100 SP). Its tick value is thus 137 + 0.167 * SP, and full value of 822 + 1.002 * SP.
For a proper comparison, we can adjust for Siphon Life having a longer duration by increasing the value of Rank 7 Corruption like this: (822 + 1.002 * SP) * 1.667, which would give us 1370.274 + 1.67 * SP, in comparison to 900 + 1 * SP of Rank 4 Siphon Life.
Unlike Corruption, Siphon Life does not seem to have increased SP scaling with higher ranks. Corruption scales like this:
Rank 1 has 0.08 SP scaling per tick, Rank 2 has 0.155 SP scaling per tick, and Ranks 3 - 7 have the already mentioned 0.167 SP scaling per tick.
The way I propose to buff Siphon Life is to increase its SP scaling. Rank 1 Siphon Life would stay unchanged, Rank 2 Siphon Life would get an increase from 0.05 SP scaling per tick to 0.055 SP scaling per tick, Rank 3 Siphon Life would get an increase from 0.05 SP scaling per tick to 0.065 SP scaling per tick, and Rank 4 Siphon Life would get an increase from 0.05 SP scaling per tick to 0.075 SP scaling per tick.
This change would give Rank 4 Siphon Life a full value of 900 + 1.5 * SP, full damage only value of 450 + 0.75 * SP and a single tick value of 45 + 0.075 * SP.
To put this in context (without taking any talents or effects on the target into account), right now, an 800 SP Warlock deals 85 shadow damage per tick with Rank 4 Siphon Life, for a total of 850 shadow damage over 30 seconds. After this change, the same 800 SP Warlock would deal 100 shadow damage per tick with Rank 4 Siphon Life, for a total of 1000 shadow damage over 30 seconds.
Like I already stated, this buff is optional, if you want to be more careful with the changes.

To close up, after these changes, the currently dominant 9/21/21 talent spread with Demonic Sacrifice could be closely matched by the 30/0/21 talent spread, which adds much needed variety into the Warlock class. I believe that neither of the proposed changes upset the balance of the game, PvE, or PvP. If unsure, I propose to start with change #1 and monitor if the Affliction Warlock catches up to the Demonic Sacrifice build. If not, proceed with change #2 and monitor if it's enough, or if on the other hand, it's too much. The SP scaling numbers should be easy to adjust, thus this change will most likely not require a lot of work to implement, or change later if needed.

Thank you for reading and for considering this change.
