# Viable Arcane mage

## Post 78492 by Dioklecijan93 — 2023-11-23T22:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78492#p78492 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As of now the intellect buff given by mages is capped at 31. However i believe it would be interesting to see mages that go the Arcane tree(if there are any)have the possibility to have an upgraded version of the intellect buff, a good comparison would be how holy paladins can beef up their Blessing of Wisdom by adding 2 points into the talent. If this was done then i believe having one Arcane mage in the raid would be viable. The improved buff could give 38 int with one talent point and 45 with 2 talent points.

EDIT:
As discussed below the post, to make Arcane mages viable the Turtle Wow developers should make Arcane mages the support dps out of the 3 specs available to mages. Instead of buffing up the arcane mages damage abilities so they can be on par with Fire and Frost mages, they should give them some abilities that help the Arcane mage prop up their raid or party.
One of these could be the mentioned upgraded Intellect buff which can be located near the top of the Arcane tree,this ability could also reduce the mana cost of the Intellect buff by 80% for the mage, because then it will be easier for him to buff up the raid if hes the only Arcane mage in the raid with the talent.

Secondly an ability that could be given to Arcane mages that could be similiar to the Shamans Windfury abilitity but for casters, for example if the party has a Arcane mage in it all other dps casters in the party near the mage have a 10%-15% chance to instantly cast a spell they have casted a second ago. Lets say a frost mage is casting a frostbolt...after he casts it and the aura proc drops he will immidiately instant cast the same spell with no mana cost. It could be an aura or something placeable like a totem.
