# THE HUNTER MARK IS NOT WORKING PROPERLY

## Post 93618 by Shyler — 2024-04-27T10:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93618#p93618 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In PVP, after the hunter imprints a thief, the thief's stealth will cause the hunter to lose track of the target. The range of the imprint is only 30 yards, and if it exceeds 30 yards, the stealth will not be visible. There is also no marking on the mini map, which is inconsistent with the original game.
The most fatal thing is that the hunter's mark is also affected by obstructions, walls, and pillars. Stealth professions hiding behind obstacles will prevent the hunter's mark from gaining vision, even if there is only 10 yards between them. This violates the original intention of role-playing and game skill settings.
The Hunter Mark should not have a 30-yard range limit. Secondly, it should still be effective even if it is blocked, which is in line with RPG
