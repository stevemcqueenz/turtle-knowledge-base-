# Druid Talent: Sylvan Blessing - Trigger even when you don't land the killing blow

## Post 121323 by Leeviathan — 2025-03-01T15:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121323#p121323 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It took me a while to figure out why the talent Sylvan Blessing in the Druid Balance tree wasn't triggering someimes, usually in groups. It looks like the talent only triggers its effect when the druid lands the killing blow; my suggestion is to make it so the effect always triggers so long as the druid has credit for the kill + gains experience or honor.

## Post 121325 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-01T16:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121325#p121325 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It works the same as Spirit Tap of Priests. It is just Vanilla.
Looks like it was intentionally designed like that. My guess it is because it is "leveling" talent which you can pick very early and successfully utilize while you play solo.
When you play solo, you have to spend more mana to CC enemies and heal yourself (with pushbacks) while in group you are relatively safe and spend less mana to survive.
Thats why warlocks probably lost it (because they have voids).
Mages hadn't had talent like this because they have conjured drinks.
Elemental shamans are meant to use their melee attack, so they are fine as is.
Since CC2 balance druids got this talent and I think it is great addition.
