## Post 14588 by Sinrek (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2021-12-15T20:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14588#p14588 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Gameplay wise - convenient, cool, unbalanced af and won't be implemented most likely.

Lore - no. Just no.

Druid's forms for a long distance journey apparently took much more strength, psychic powers (mana) and pretty taxing in terms of sustaining on the way (take note that WoW gameplay world is scaled down dramatically and distance between the Barrens and Orgrimmar takes roughly about 2 days hiking).

Also, the longer druid is in his feral form the longer it takes for them to recuperate after shapeshifting. Of course, the level of training and mastery over shapeshifting is an individual trait for each druid out there, however, according to wiki even, not every druid becomes an Archdruid if you do some research on the matter.

Total: I wouldn't advise this change to be implemented due to game balance and several lore reasons.
