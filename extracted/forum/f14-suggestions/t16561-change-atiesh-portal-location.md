# Change Atiesh portal location

## Post 112965 by Sagabal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-16T20:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112965#p112965 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, the Atiesh portal drops you in front of the entrance to Kara 10 instead of Kara 40, and right near multiple hostile mobs, instantly killing you if Dark Reaver happens to be up as well.  I propose moving the portal to the tower outside the Upper Karazhan entrance, as that's both near the next level of progression, away from hostile mobs, and serves as a nice view of the tower and lower pass from a spot most players at that point wouldn't have bothered to go to.  (I personally think that Dark Reaver should be in a better location but that's separate)
