## Post 131859 by Dogeatcats — 2025-05-19T06:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131859#p131859 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey

I would like to suggest you implement an NPC that has the option to make premade teams play vs each other in a Wargame scenario.

This could be more like a skirmish ground, where kills and win doesn't award you any honor/marks.
Currently it's a real hassle to meet up with hordes for premade. You simply just roll the dice with who you facing or who you get in the team.

Many high-end players have lost the urge to queue bg's, because it's really not enjoyable to farm fresh 60's when you are decked out in Naxx/Kara40 gear. Experienced pvp'ers know what I mean.

Gurubashi Arena would be a good location to add this feature, since we already have blood ring Npc located there!

Would love to have this for Tel'Abim, since we are a big community of pvp'ers.
