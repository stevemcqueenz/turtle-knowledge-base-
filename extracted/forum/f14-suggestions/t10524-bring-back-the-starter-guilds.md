So, let's break this down. <Newcomers> and <Still Alive> are disbanded.... Why? This makes no sense. Just stop moderating them, and leave them alone. No player is forced to remain in them, especially with the advent of the HC channel - which is a good call - but it itself is only a solution to anything if <Still Alive> remains... Alive.?. Have any of you seen how many crestfallen posts there have been today, about "what happened to the guild?" or all the influx of absolute mindless garbage in world chat? (It's been A LOT, even for world chat.) Give these people back an outlet where they can cringe, and we don't have to see it if we don't want to, and neither do you! Put the starter guilds back please, and let them be their own animal, that each player can decide whether they want to be a part of it or not.

Just the other day, I made the choice to leave <Still Alive> on one of my HC characters... That had impact, and meaning to it. I no longer had the ability to view HC group chat! I no longer could group as easily. I also no longer had to tolerate all the toxicity. The choice to leave, MEANT something. But now, that choice is gone. The meaning behind that choice, now means absolutely nothing. Give players a Choice. Let the <Newcomers> roll their alts and tell their memes in privacy on a tuesday night, cause maybe it's all they've got in life. Let <Still Alive> remain ALIVE forever, in privacy, as a place for HC's to learn and grow and fight and carry on! They can leave whenever they want, and oh look, that nifty new channel you made means they have literally nothing to lose! And, the cool part for you the moderators is - They're guilds. You can simply no longer consider them public. Leave em if you don't like em, but stay awhile if you want to. What's wrong with any of that? Give us back our guilds please.
