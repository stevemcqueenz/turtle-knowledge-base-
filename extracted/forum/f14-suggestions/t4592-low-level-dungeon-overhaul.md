# Low-level dungeon overhaul

## Post 27443 by Zeyla — 2022-10-17T21:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27443#p27443 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

RFC:
Add an alliance NPC with a rowboat at the docks of Auberdine that offers to row you to Durotar's east coast (the coast east of Orgrimmar, but north of the new goblin town since it's hostile to alliance) where you end up on a secluded shore that horde players wouldn't normally be able to see unless they swim out around north east border of the zone. At that hidden shore, add a small dock where the boat ends up (teleporting dialogue is fine, no need to actually move around the north of the continent) as well as a couple of alliance quest-giver npcs and a cave entrance with an instance portal directly into Ragefire Chasm. Add alliance equivalent quests to the npcs for RFC with the same objectives. This would make it easier for RFC groups to form cross-faction and ensures everyone gets something out of it.

Deadmines/Stockades:
In a similar fashion, add a Horde NPC with rowboat at Grom'gol that takes you to the small abandoned pier on westfall's shore, near the lighthouse, along with quest-giver npcs for both deadmines and stockades, with objectives that match alliance quests. Add a neutral NPC as well that has the appearance of a Stormwind guard with a dialogue option where he offers to "arrest" you and transport you to the Stormwind stockades.

Something similar could be done for a boat from menethil to the coast of Pyrewood village for SFK. Gnomeregan already has a horde solution, obviously, and if the RFC option were implemented then alliance players could simply make the trek from there to Ratchet/Wailing Caverns (or could even have an additional boat option to take them to ratchet from the east Org coast) which would also help more groups form for RFK. If Pyrewood village is utilized then a connector could also be added to the coast north of SM for alliance, though it may not be needed by that level.

I know many will say that fast transport methods tend to break the immersion for players, which I tend to agree with, but I believe that not being able to reach level-appropriate instances without passing through zones that are 10+ levels higher than yourself is even more immersion-breaking. I also kept it mostly to the lowest level dungeons, since in the mid levels it becomes easier to reach such places. I would, however, suggest adding more dungeon quests across the board for the dungeons that lack them at every level.
