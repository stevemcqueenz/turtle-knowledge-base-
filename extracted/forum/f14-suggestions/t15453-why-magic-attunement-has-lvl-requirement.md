# Why Magic Attunement has lvl requirement?

## Post 105855 by Nett — 2024-11-02T01:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105855#p105855 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The level requirement does not make sense, since it would really useful against raid bosses, but in a pve setting, you will be able to use only against adds, which will be attacked by 12+ players at the same time and die after 3 seconds.

This talent is almost useless on raiding, and the spell offensive purpose seems more interesting in the raiding scene.

Stand on the tanks' shoulders at the start of any trash pull so you can quickly slip it on the mob just after they get initial aggro. You probably shouldn't be winding up a damaging spell at this point anyway because you're likely to rip threat.

Does this apply to every single trash pack? Obviously not, especially the AoE packs. But there's plenty of trash packs with chunky mobs which are worth putting this on. Examples; BWL (drake talon mobs after Vael), AQ40 (Big anubisanth things throughout the raid) or Naxx (Gargoyles).

Bottom line is, you can sneak this onto almost any trash mob if it's done immediately after the tank engagement and just before you line up your damaging spells.
