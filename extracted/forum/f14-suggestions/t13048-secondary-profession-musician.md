So I have some thoughts about implementation, just a brainstorm of ideas---

 **As a Secondary Profession-**

You could add it to survival and have the songs give duration buffs, like campfires do. Have the instrument crafting be quite simple, like using simple wood for flutes for example, and have it as an item that can be used like a trinket. This is a very minimalistic addition of the feature.

 **As a Primary Profession-**

I've seen other people already talking about potential Woodcutting and Carpentry Professions, Musicians could have woodcutting and make their instruments out of materials gathered there, as well as some other items such as Copper Wire and Silver Contact for lute strings for example. They could have instruments as crafted items with stats and then to play a song you would need a consumable  "Score" which is made out of paper and ink, paper being craftable from wood pulp and ink being purchased or made by alchemists. Primarily the songs would give durational buffs while being played but could also act like scrolls or potions. You could also just make it part of the Carpentry profession. A little weird to need Carpentry 100 to play Invincible but that's a fairly minor thing.

 **I think balancing the effects is the most difficult aspect. One I cannot help with. So if anyone has experience with this then definitely give your input!!**
Collecting songs would actually be quite easy since it is possible to use a midi of an existing track, WoW has a good database of songs and there can be original compositions too.

If the Woodcutting and Carpentry professions are not planned for implementation then Musicians could pair with Herbalism and either be absorbed into or absorb the Inscription Profession, using ink and paper to create "Scores" as their primary craft.

Another idea is that it could pair with Enchanting and use magic dust to create "Scores" and imbue instruments with magic.

Let me know what you think! I'll write more as I have more ideas.

The idea of consumables is to keep it in line with how the game operates really, if you want to play different songs it makes sense to have some Item that would connect with the song, otherwise you'd need some kind of new menu or have the instrument be linked with a single song. The alternative being that it isn't locked within a profession and then it would be accessible for anyone, either as a secondary profession or as Items that can be found and wielded like any other weapon.
It would work well as a secondary profession in my opinion.
