# Returning to Azeroth and an idea for more player moments

## Post 152108 by Charliejone — 2025-09-17T15:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152108#p152108 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all, I’m Charlie. I’ve been playing on Turtle WoW for a little while now after a long break from the game. What surprised me most is how different the world feels compared to when I last played - there’s a slower, almost more thoughtful pace here, and it made me enjoy just wandering around again instead of rushing.
While leveling, I sometimes wish there were more little reasons for players to cross paths outside of dungeons. Back in the old days, some of my best memories weren’t about bosses or loot, but about random encounters with strangers in the middle of nowhere. Maybe something simple, like spontaneous world events [Pips NYT](https://pipsnyt.com), or even small quests that reward grouping with nearby players, could bring more of those moments back.
Has anyone else felt this, or maybe already has a different idea to keep the world feeling full of life?

## Post 152130 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-09-17T21:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152130#p152130 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There isn't really a way to make players more engaged than they already are. Despite all the new content additions, most players know WoW all too well to need others' help.

You can only expect to see this from more social players who are actively engaging with others. The most I've seen when I played recently was when I'm questing, some random person comes and sends me a group invite without saying a word so we can do the quest together and when they are done they leave the group without saying a word, very rarely saying "thanks" or "bye".

Maybe when the UE5 client releases and the developer team has more creative freedom, they can develop some open world mechanics that incentivize players to interact more with each other. Granted, you can't really force people to chat or talk if they don't want to, but maybe, just maybe if there are world bosses or somehow professions are expanded to be a group effort in a way where you have to do things in a tandem with somebody else it can increase interaction.

Another way that came to me as I was writing this is if quests, if completed in a group have some kind of a better reward compared to doing them alone. Or if the Turtle WoW team can take inspiration from SWG (Star Wars Galaxies) and the Jedi quest before the NGE changes where every player had a personalized and randomized quest to becoming a Jedi so no two people had the same objectives, which made creating a walkthrough guide both pointless and impossible and there are such quests in the game - personalized, randomized or randomly generated, possibly involving solving puzzles and working together can be a way to make players interact actively with one another if they need each other to complete the quest.

For example - you need 2 or more people to complete some puzzle and each player has to perform a different activity and they have to actively communicate and coordinate with one another, like in a raid - one person has to be at a specific location, standing over a "button" on the floor while another person has to activate a switch on the wall while a third player has to be ready to heal if a trap is sprung or something like that.

I doubt that will happen, but I think if the UE5 engine allows for some randomized content (even dungeons) to be added into the game, it will be amazing.
