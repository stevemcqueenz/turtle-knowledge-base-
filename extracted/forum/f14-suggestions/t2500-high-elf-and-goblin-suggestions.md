# High elf and Goblin suggestions

## Post 15577 by Zuesgold — 2022-01-09T17:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15577#p15577 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really like the New High elf and Goblin races that were added into Turtle wow but I feel that more improvements could make them even better then they currently are

A example would be High Elves and elwynn forest. Why make new High elf players go all the way to goldshire at level 5 instead of just having them travel a short distance to Kharanos? This holds true especially for high elf hunters who would have to travel all the way to Ironforge simply to train new abilities due to the lack of Hunter trainers in elwynn forest.

Although stuff like this are a annoyance rather then a bug changes to the current system could really improve both races

High Elves

Silverwing Halls- New High elf area in Ironforge

The Halls will be divided into the following section

Pools of remembrence- Center area of the Silverwing halls where all the Venders are located as well as the innkeeper

Arcane Sanctum- Mage and Priest trainers can be found here

Nature's Lodge- Hunter trainers reside here

War Room- Warrior and paladin trainers can be found here

Command center- located at the vary back of the Silverwing halls where the High elf leadership resides

The The Farstrider Lodge should be given a makeover so that it can serve as the major Quest hub for High elf players until they reach level 10 and can head to
Thelsamar

The team could make it where the Ironforge dwarves and High elven refugees are working together to secure the area with the human soldiers being replaced with mountaineers

New players will face threats from Troggs, Dark iron dwarves, trolls and ogres while questing at the lodge

Upon reaching level 10 the mountaineer captain at the lodge will send players to aid Balir Frosthammer against troggs in coldridge valley

Class Quests- each Class will have access to 3 high elf racial Quests at level 2, 10 and 20

Goblins

After finishing up the Quests in the starting area players are sent to a newly constructed Laboratory where a teleportation device is being constructed for transport to ogrimmar

New Goblin players will quest here from level 5 until level 10 after which they will use the teleportation device to reach ogrimmar and speak with Thrall who will officially bring the Goblins into the horde

After being brought into the horde thrall will give the player a quest to speak with
Kaltunk in the valley of trials

Union station- a new Section of ogrimmar where the Durotar Labor union oversees daily operations

The station is divided into several areas

Central corridor- the large open area within the center of the station where Venders and profession trainers can be found. The union's Innkeeper also resides here

Durotar laboratory- small area tucked away within the southern end of the station where Union members work on constructing weapons of war for the horde. The Hunter trainers can be found here

The projects- all of the poor and downtrodden Goblins have made their homes here. The Warlock, Mage and rogue trainers also reside here

War Academy- home to the Durotar Labor union's Warrior trainers

Class Quests- just like the High Elves each Class will have access to 3 Goblin racial Quests at level 2, 10 and 20

A new Goblin town will be added along the Southfury River in Durotar with new Quests for level 1-10 horde  players to complete
