 70% pushback reduction can be 100% or 0%, with bad luck casters are outputting from 0 to little dps/hps and having to spam channeled abilities eats mana pool very fast.
After this change the rng factor´s affecting performance for casters are
that last 2% or 3% of missing spell hit chance against raid bosses thats left because of spell hit cap at 16%. (if hit rating is same as in original classic) critical strike chance and trinket proc rate.
for melee just critical strike chance and trinket proc rate.
fixing that hit too to make it same as melee would be nice, since prepping for raid for a long time and trying to get a good parse just to get 2 unlucky resist in a row is a real mood killer.
