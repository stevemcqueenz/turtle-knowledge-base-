# Improve Ansirem's Runeweaver for TANK WARLOCK!

## Post 135659 by Suspicious — 2025-06-14T01:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135659#p135659 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm currently a humble warlock trying my best to build for physical tanking. There's a few items here and there that enable it, but a major one I set my eye on was Ansirem's Runeweaver.

I really like the buff on it, but the cooldown is longer than its duration.

I suggest to lower the cooldown from 10(20?) minutes to 5, and increase the duration to 5 minutes. That way, we can benefit from it as long as we have it equipped (unequipping it already removes the buff) and use it to tank physical damage enemies!
