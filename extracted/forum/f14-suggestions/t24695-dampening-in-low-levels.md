# Dampening in low levels.

## Post 173922 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-04-03T16:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173922#p173922 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

There's one problem with this new dampening.

If you're a leveling caster trying to do bgs you're completely screwed by this new dampening:

You already have a massive % miss chance vs others with spells if you're under 3 levels

Now casters have 30% dampening they have to deal with.

These numbers are for Kara tuned level 60 players in PvP. Try doing damage now as a level 35 mage in BG. May aswel not bother doing low level bgs unless you're melee.

Can we please have reduced dampening for lower level players?

If you have lower end gear this simply makes you do no damage as a caster in anything but max level gear especially in Lower levels.

Just set casters and melee to 15% until level 60. As the disparity isn't there until naxx.

Want to briefly touch on the 1-9 Bracket. 10-20% Healing Reduction is fine however I don't think any damage should be dampened, and casters need about 40 Spell Penetration atleast for Level 1. 15 Fire Resist Cape Enchant is 75% Fire Resist thus making Mages, Warlocks, Priests Wanding entirely useless. Same applies to Nature Resist Cape Enchant for Shamans, Druids. So I recommend those 5 classes get Spell Penetration built in to Dampening. As the enchant is appropriate against Melee with Fiery Enchant as physical damage is already much higher than casters by default. I'd have to see how that plays out but in 1-9 we literally can't even kill each other easily at all, and Troll Regeneration is not effected by Dampening so if anything we need 0% Damage Reduction or even a Damage Increase however that can be gained by Gear Progression and other means.
