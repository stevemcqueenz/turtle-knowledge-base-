## Post 36822 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-04T20:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36822#p36822 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey OP, did you not roll night elf? Did you not see that Elune's Grace was giga buffed into 50% dodge? We now have evasion. You cant dodge while casting though, so unlike rogue who can still put out big damage, we're forced into wanding and instant casts to make use of this evasion. It's still really strong especially for pvp now.

But yeah, starshards needs some work, even when a full duration is completed, its poor DPM and awful DPS just do not cut it, even wanding is more practical.

TBC's starshards was a free dot, and so it was just a boring DP reskin, and you would just macro it as an oGCD into all your damage spells. This was really lame. Instead of that, let's take the ideas from both vanilla and tbc, and combine them into something cool and actually not awful:

Starshards, 350 Mana
Rains starshards down at the enemy target's head, causing 950 Arcane damage over 6 sec. If the channel completes, **an additional X damage will be dealt over Y seconds.**

This spell would now serve as a very high DPM spell for healers to use while not busy healing, and a high DPM alternative for mana conserving DPS priests.

A weighty 6 seconds to complete the starshards and apply the dot isn't to be taken lightly. This won't be a massive buff to power levels, but it does open doors where those doors were already open.

The cool thing is that this also buffs starshards for leveling, as the big problem is that unshielded hits to you eat into starshard's length and damage. However, since the new dot activates on a completed cast, it would always apply with a pushback'd channel bar. This is big for making starshards actually usable in PVP and leveling.

In PVP, this would also increase tactical options for priests, as players would need to be mindful that pushback will only serve to get a dot on them faster, and that only a kick or CC will actually prevent the dot, but then that kick or CC isn't available to manage the more important holy and shadow schools. More depth to PVP is created with this.
