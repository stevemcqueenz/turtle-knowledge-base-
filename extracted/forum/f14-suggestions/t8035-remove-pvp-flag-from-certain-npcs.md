# Remove PvP Flag from Certain NPCs

## Post 51690 by Fizzler (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-28T18:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51690#p51690 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I just think it is not an enjoyable gameplay experience to enable non-warmode characters to get flagged for PvE quests which are not intended to make you participate in PvP objectives. It also helps keep HCs from being taken advantage of by certain players.

List of quests with NPCs that flag player for PvP:
Alliance:
 1. Beat Bartleby - Bartleby PvP Flagged, (Stormwind Q ~lv 10), Check for Horde in SW before doing Q.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1640>

 2. The Attack! - Lord Gregor Lescovar and Marzon the Silent Blade PVP Flagged, (Stormwind Q ~lv 31), Check for Horde in SW before doing Q.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=434>

 3. The Missing Diplomat:
Part 7/8 The Missing Diplomat - Dashel PVP Flagged, (Stormwind Q ~lv 31), Check for Horde in SW before doing Q.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1246>
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1447>

Part 11 The Missing Diplomat - Tapoke "Slim" John PVP Flagged, (Menethil Q ~lv 33), Check for Horde.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1249>

Part 16: The Missing Diplomat - Private Hendel PVP Flagged, (Dustwallow Q ~lv 36-40+), Check for Horde.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1324>

 5. Avoiding Detection - Scouts PvP Flagged (You Are Warned), (SoS Q ~lv 37/40+).
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40079>

Horde;
 1. A Rogue's Deal - Calvin PvP Flagged,(Tirisfal Glades Q ~lv 5), Not a big deal
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=590>

 2. The Final Message to the Wildhammer - Dwarves PvP Flagged, (Hinterlands Q ~lv44-50+), Check For Alliance.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=7843>

I do not think I have listed all quests that flag you unintentionally for PvP, but I think this is a good start to remove their PvP flag

Credits to Unchallenged for making this list of unintentional PvP quests:
[viewtopic.php?t=4490](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4490)
