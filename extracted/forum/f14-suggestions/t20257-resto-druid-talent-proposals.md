## Post 138833 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-12T14:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138833#p138833 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For all its faults, Blizzard had a decent vision for healing. Fast heals are for emergencies, so you pay for the utility with much worse mana-efficiency. Regrowth always was a very strong heal, but it was not sustainable. Same with Flash Heal for priests. So you have to use more efficient but slower heals.
This leads to expression of skill. You have predict damage, you have to manage your mana, you have to know when to weave in the expensive quick heals. Healing Touch is the most efficient and best scaling heal in the game - as a trade-off it's slow. Perfect.

TWoW in comparison doesn't seem to have a vision at all. Last big patch reduced the mana cost of Regrowth substantially, which in effect killed HT builds because the balanced advantage of HT was no longer a factor once you could sustain Regrowth forever. They also introduced the T3.5 set bonus for priest making Flash Heal cheaper, taking yet another step into the wrong direction of turning every healing class into a 1-button paladin spamming Flash of Light.
Everyone can chain-mash a 1.5s cast heal paired with some kind of heal-botting addon that auto-select targets for you, so you maximize your personal output at the detriment of all the other healers who get sniped. This is not a good thing, this is not what makes healing fun and skill-based. Healers are supposesd to complement each other, not cannibalize each other.

So buffing Regrowth even further and lowering its cast time even further is the absolute worst proposal to achieve some kind of balance. Because it would kill even more builds and dumb healing down even further. The tuning I'm personally looking forward to is nerfing healers to where 1.5s casts are reserved for emergencies once again. Because right now cheap 1.5s heal spells on every class turns everything into a dumb snipe-fest.
