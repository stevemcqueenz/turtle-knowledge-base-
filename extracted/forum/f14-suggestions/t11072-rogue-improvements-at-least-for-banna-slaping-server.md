Why do you want to prevent rogue tanking, however? This server, like many others, is facing a serious tank shortage. While it won't be solved by simply making everyone able to tank (because not everyone wants to, in any case), having options is always going to improve the situation more than not having them.

As a veteran rogue tank, I do not agree with this idea, but I believe it could be workable with a much shorter cooldown than what you propose, like shield block – or even no cooldown but an energy cost that will ultimately limit how often it can be used: a 20 energy cost would make it possible to use it as a defensive tool for a total of 7 global cooldowns before running dry, if starting from 100 energy and taking into account the energy regenerated during the time, or 6 times if coupled with the Riposte procs that it opens up. Adrenaline Rush could then be used to extend this time, making it something of a defensive panic button for rogue tanks. It'd involve a lot of button mashing, though, which isn't the most accessible way of designing a play style.

In my opinion, rogue tanking was fine before the recent changes to Flourish (even the 40 sec cooldown is excessive, let alone the bugged 400 seconds), with the caveat that hitting avoidance cap seriously does trivialize encounters. I never did, though, and I was still able to tank every dungeon, even solo tanking UBRS with a solo healer, and even many raid bosses where having a taunt is not a necessity – or is made unnecessary by having a high-avoidance tank. I think it proves that avoidance cap is not a necessity by any means, and rogue tanks can be viable even without the means to reach it.

I'm sure there are other ways to prevent reaching avoidance cap, however, like having an overall hard limit of 95% avoidance (only possibly exceeded by using Evasion), or making flourish (in its 0-cooldown form) scale down the avoidance it grants the closer the player is to unhittable – say, by having flourish never take the player over 95% avoidance and instead converting excess parry % to damage reduction (1% parry to 1% damage reduction, including spell damage? On top of a very low armor value and comparatively low health, and lacking defensive stance damage reduction, I don't think it would be entirely unreasonable, but the values should obviously be balanced through playtesting).

This would also soften the blows that do get through, since the spiky damage a rogue takes is something the devs mentioned was an issue. The current fix doesn't make the spikes blunter, it just makes them more regular, making the rogue take damage that is extremely tough to heal (which it often already was, according to the healers I've played with).

Having been played Rogue on Twow for the last months, noticed that this class have not the dedication that other classes have had from the dev team, nor the deserved consideration from other players.

This situation reflects with negative impact expecialy in pve Raid.

Rogues will always be loved/hated because of PvP and that will never change. The complains and rage from other players asking for Rogue nerf have their origin from PvP matters, sure not from PvE and expecialy from Raids.

Rogue class **should be the master of melee single target damage in raid boss fight**.

On Twow, they are not, being overwhelmed by warriors. Warriors have their consolidated role in Raid for situational OT and AOE trash/add damage burst; but, if a Warrior does more damage than a Rogue also in a single target boss fight, well, we have a **global balance issue**.

Anyway, imho, there is a combination of factors causing this situation:

 - a lot of ppl play Rogue in pve, but play it very bad, because their mind is PvP oriented (or they simply are bad players). This leads Rogue not be properly considered in the way they deserve when a Raid comp is making up, because many times you expect a Rogue to be a bad pve player.

 - usually, In boss fights and considering raw damage, many of the best Rogue skills are not usable. Wound Poison, Crippling Poisons, Gouge, Blind and so on are just a niche for few side mechanics in even fewer boss fights. Anyway, the major issue regards raw damage output, not else.

 - no custom itemization seems to be mainly dedicated to Rogue. I think that, fearing to overpower other classes (feral druids? retris?), many nerfs have been done and will keep to be done, impacting more on Rogues than the classes they are meant for (Convergence of Elements nerfs impacted more on Rogues than Bear/Kitty. And if you say that set wasnt good for Rogue you dont properly understand this game mechanics).

 - Rogues have not even a single talent/skill that can be considered of "Raid support".

 - cross-faction and the presence of Shamans (read Windfury) in any Raid is another negative impact factor, being more beneficial for Warriors than Rogues.

 - Assassination tree is a filler, half of the talents in this tree are mainly pvp oriented or weak hybrid. Vigor is a Row3 talent (period). Improved Kidney Shot and Cold Blood are pvp oriented. Seal Fate is very strong, but definitively not worthing what you must drop from Combat for picking it. Seal Fate specs are viable, but weaker. Period.

 - Rogue tank build was a cool idea, but personaly i would never have created skills like Flourish and Agitating Poison. Evasion is enough for the situation where a Rogue finds himself to tank a raid Boss for 15 secs, hoping of not being oneshotted and/or letting to finish the boss encounter (ie, enrage boss fights). In this prospective, Flourish as defensive skill with defensive 5min+ cooldown could be even good, but definitively not as a finisher move.

This long introduction for focusing on which are the issues and what is my personal vision/suggestion.

 1) Raid Support ---> **Tricks of the Trade**. Imho creating this skill should be mandatory for all rogues. ToT is a wonderful skill, providing single target buff and opening a whole world of new and creative strategies in pulls, threat management/building, tank swapping mechanics. Really a shame that a visionary project like Twow has not considered yet to implement one of the best skills ever existed in wow.

 2) Single target melee damage boost ----> add +2%/+5% melee haste to **Weapon Expertise** talent. Imo these numbers are not unbalanced, +1%/+2% would be a weak compensation. Consider that personal melee haste buff was introduced as row1 in 3.0.

 3) Assassination tree improvement ----> move Vigor to the row3 it deserves and create **Master Poisoner** for row7: your poisons will not consume stacks when applied (just a quality of life small bonus) and you deal X% more damage on targets afflicted by any of your poisons (raw damage personal bonus is just my idea, actually any bonus should be good, considering it would be a tier7. Can be crit, lowering nature resistance or any combination of these affects...) **Vile Poisons** is too weak and half of its bonus is pvp oriented: down its ranks from 5 to 3, keep the bonuses it already gives, but add a Raid-wide debuff on the target giving +1%/+2%/+3% increased chance to hit with spells and melee attacks. **Improved Poison** is too weak, considering what you must drop from Combat if you pick it. In general, poisons chance to be applied is too low and imho is the biggest issue in global Rogue weakness/unbalance in Raid dps output (see next points).

 4) Improve chance to apply poisons ----> In my vision, rogue poisons must be stronger than windfury. I am totaly fine with poison from main-hand not stacking with wf, but poison from mh should do more damage than wf if Rogue is specced into poisons (Assassination) and situational same damage output for all Rogues (wf better for burst, poison better for longer fights). Actual base chances to apply poisons (20% istant, 30% deadly) are imo already low, in longer Raid fights this issue is even more impactful. Considering that Istant Poison is mainly used for leveling or very short fights, considering that here on twow there is not the debuff cap on bosses and that in raid fights all Rogues should use Deadly Poison (unless niche situations), my suggestion is to make **Improved Poison** give +20% (or even more) chance to apply a Deadly Poison stack. Considering that here Deadly Poison applies from both weapons and up to 5x2 stacks, with improved chance to apply stacks and improved damage from Vile Poison, all this should lead to the situation where Deadly Poison in long fights give more dps output than windfury (the final goal).

 5) Fan of Knives ----> I am not a fan of this skill, because AoE burst should be other class role, not Rogues'. Anyway, Fan of Knives is what would give AoE to Rogue. In alternative, make Blade Flurry to hit 2 addiction targes and not only 1.

 6) Envenom -----> In my vision, Envenom should be the sobstitution of Eviscerate for Assassination deep tree and for all Rogues when target has too much armor mitigation. Just make a finisher doing **basedamage*CP+APcoefficient*CP**, taking into consideration that Poisons cant crit. No other Envenom mess giving improved chance to apply poison for a short time, just instant Poison damage and the possibility to choose between Eviscerate or Envenom due to target armor/resistance.

Final advice: if any change will ever be applied to Poisons, PLEASE, do not make Poison damage scaling with Spell Power, but only with Attack Power. No more spellpower based itemization for Rogues, like it was in 3.1.
