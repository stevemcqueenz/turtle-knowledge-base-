# Revamp Mage Atiesh to make sense with Turtle WoW's class changes

## Post 165224 by Sparkwens — 2026-01-15T10:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165224#p165224 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Mage Atiesh still has the classic WoW stats, with 2% hit 2% crit aura and 150 spell power. This made sense in classic WoW because 99% of mages were playing frost or fire specs, which required 10% hit chance from gear, however, in Turtle WoW 90% of Mages are specced into Arcane, which only requires 6% hit chance, so the Mage Atiesh becomes a significant downgrade compared to other items, such as Desecration and Kirel'narak, the Death Sentence.
