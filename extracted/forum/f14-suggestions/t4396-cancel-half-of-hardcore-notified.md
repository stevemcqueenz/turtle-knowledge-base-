The server lags a lot now. This is a fact. Many players have a question, how to play pvp in such a situation? This server is well upgraded comparatively with "Light's Hope". It's super cool. But that old one could hold 15.000 online without lags and nonetheless here we already see certain problems. Admins should to focus on server performance first of all right now. This is the main thing.

You could notice that the lags (in addition to the situations themselves) often occur together with **orange system messages** about hardcore players. Someone achieved something, and someone died. In addition, the game modifies the layout by adding a grave as object for everyone without any restarts.
I am not asking you to remove the graves completely, but what should have been something special has ceased to be so.
"**Received level 10**" is this an achievement? Maybe a little, but not so much that the whole server must knows about it. No one pays attention to it anymore! For 10-20 lvl its absolutely correct.

 ***In order to reduce lags, I suggest removing all messages about hardcore players below level 30 (half way) along with their graves.*** Along with this, level 40-50-60 yellow messages will become rarer and more visible to people, so hardcore players will start getting more glory for their achievements

As a consolation prize for this change, I propose giving hardcore players an additional reward at level 40. They have a blocked auction and mount-levels are starting, so the best gift will be some gold.
