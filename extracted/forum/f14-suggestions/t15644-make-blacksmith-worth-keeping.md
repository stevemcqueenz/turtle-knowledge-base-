# Make blacksmith worth keeping

## Post 106798 by Ocklen — 2024-11-07T01:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106798#p106798 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Blacksmith, after nerfs to several items a few patches ago, is extremely lackluster overall. However it is even worse when you look at the different specializations and realize that most are kinda worthless. Other than axe spec for Nightfall there is nothing great about the other specs after you've been raiding for a bit. Armorsmithing is a joke because many items that feel like they should be AS only are blacksmithing (all specs) and they also had the 2 main items that were desirable yoinked away from them due to nerfs. I hate to not have a direct answer for this gripe, but I would really like some good gear for each blacksmithing spec so they feel a bit more fleshed out, and not just the buckle/spike/stone factory I feel like. Also when they did give armorsmiths a neat set in Karazhan they put the recipes on a mob completely out of the way and gave each a 1.44% (from the database) drop chance every 7 days. It just feels pointless.

All crafting professions suffer from the same conundrum. If you let them craft strong pieces of endgame gear, everyone is expected to get those and the itempogression for that slot is done the second you walk onto the auction house.
Case in point, lionheart & titans in classic.

If you do it engineering style of making the strong items bind on pickup, you just shackle people with a profession they didnt want in the first place for just one item.

Leaves only the 3. Option - niche items and item modifications.
Nightfall, Resistence Gear & Beltbuckles.

To make professions work without sabotaging gear progression, maybe we need drops akin to the eye of sulfuras. It allows someone who has bested a encounter to upgrade a auction house tradeable decent crafting item to be upgraded further.

Combining both craftable items and the personal progression in raiding or endgame dungeons/pvp.

Why should blacksmith be worth keeping?
I mean, you can enjoy crafting and leveling this profession, but why do you want to make it must-have?
I actually think that current state is quite good. Professions offer good items for leveling and enchants to different pieces of gear. You can totally ignore it, use AH and lose nothing.
Engineering is the only exception and I don't think that other professions should follow that path.

Professions are good for people who like to collect stuff. IRL it might be postal marks. In game it is recipes or pets. You don't need most of them, but you might like to gather rare ones.

> **Atreidon wrote: Thu Nov 21, 2024 4:05 pm**
> If you do it engineering style of making the strong items bind on pickup, you just shackle people with a profession they didnt want in the first place for just one item.

I add that same reasoning apply for retail style of profession buffs. Like "if you skill mining, then you get more hp". So every tank have to go mining for that buff.

The only thing that is wrong in current state is that all endgame recipes are useless. You can literally skip all crafted stuff and just go raiding.
It is possible to rework quests to get tier tokens. Like you have to bring tier 3 token and crafted chest to get tier 3 chest (now it requires you just to bring CD materials). But do you really want this type of obstacles?

Blacksmithing could use some crafting CD, but it also requires incorporating this CD to all current quests etc. I don't think you need just another profession with CD for the sake of it.
