# WSG Wall Jump from Horde Hut to GY – Please Address

## Post 152891 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-23T23:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152891#p152891 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I want to bring up something I’ve seen a lot recently in Warsong Gulch. There’s a jump from the Horde restoration hut wall to the Horde graveyard that players can consistently pull off. It isn’t lag abuse or a hack, just a precise wall jump with spacebar.

The problem:

 * While it’s technically possible for anyone to learn, it gives an unfair positional advantage (instant shortcut to GY).
 * It feels more like exploiting unintended map geometry than skillful play.
 * It makes the game less fun to play against, especially in close matches.

Questions for the team:

 * Is this jump considered intended design, or is it a geometry oversight?
 * If unintended, could the map geometry be fixed so the jump isn’t possible?

I understand wall jumps exist elsewhere and can add some flavor, but this one directly impacts gameplay balance in WSG. Would love to hear staff’s thoughts on whether it’s allowed, and if not, whether a fix could be implemented.

Thanks!
