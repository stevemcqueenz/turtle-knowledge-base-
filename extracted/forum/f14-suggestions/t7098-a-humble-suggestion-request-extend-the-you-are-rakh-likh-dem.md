# A humble suggestion/request, extend the "You Are Rakh'likh, Demon" chain.

## Post 45152 by Greese (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-05-09T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45152#p45152 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is the quest chain I would like to see extended:

<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=3628>

Currently it ends with some slightly deflating rewards given the amount of work involved in completing it.  The end goal is to better reward the player while also giving them options so that all classes/specs are encouraged to complete the quest chain.

I suggest a new chain opening up revolving around the item "Demon's Blood"

<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=10779>

Return to Loramus Thalipedes in Azshara and he gives you the option to upgrade the trinket in one of four ways.

Take the item to Tirion Fordring in EPL and he will cleanse it when you provide 2x Righteous Orbs, 2x Living Essences, 2x Essence of Water.  The result would be a +25 healing and 6 mp5 trinket.

Take the item to Daio the Decrepit in the Tainted Scar.  He will enhance it with additional 3x blood from the Felguards and 3x blood from Manahounds there to make it a more potent version of its current stat distribution for tanks.  +6 defense, +10 all resistances, +6 hp5.

Take the item to the imp Vi'el in Winterspring.  For 20g and the 5x Horns of a nearby Succubi he will trade for the Demon's Blood and give you a Fel impowered trinket instead.  +30 ATP and 1% attack/casting speed.

Take the item to Mataus the Wrathcaster in Light's Hope Chapel.  For 20g and 10x Insignia of the Crusade he will transmute the item into a spell power trinket.   +15 spell power and 1% attack/casting speed.

The conversion consumes the original item, so you can only upgrade once and cannot swap to another version or revert to the original.

Thoughts?  Just a random idea off the top of my head.  Trying to maintain some semblance of balance between the rewards but also feel like this chain is pretty epic in scope but somewhat limited in rewards which causes many players to skip it.
