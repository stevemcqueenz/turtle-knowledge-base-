Hello

Priest's T3 faith shoulders have this major annoyance about them - the wings. At first glance, they are beautiful. When viewed from the front, the wings look like... actual wings.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Vtw0d6f.png)

This right here? Top tier gaming, absolute peak. These shoulders right here are, in my opinion, straight up the best looking shoulders in the game. Or at least I thought they were, because then I turned my character around...

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/EZTlGII.png)

And this is the side view, which isn't that bad:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/yvGijmu.png)

The sprite only looks terrible from the back. Which is what the players see for 90% of the time :(

Meanwhile, Mages or Warlocks get the access to these sick looking pieces which don't display any sprite issues (because I assume the sprites are basically ball shaped):

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/QdlxFT6.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/0aMyTDZ.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/kBmJ1ya.png)

All the above pieces look awesome. Among priest shoulders, the T3 ones are absolutely beautiful and their look is being cucked by the visual glitch.

I would like to propose considering taking a look into these shoulders - perhaps the sprite can be fixed, perhaps a slight remodel, perhaps delete the sprite and create metal wings in its' place. There are many possibilities.
One of the discord users, @hoeffem (Yoland) posted this concept using AI:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/fT9bieq.png)

There are many priests like me that would love those to look properly. This is, according to many, BiS transmog item. Me included. It would be AWESOME to have the developers take a look into this matter.

Thank you for reading :)
