# Few things I would like to see refined.

## Post 71900 by Twowacc321 — 2023-10-18T15:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71900#p71900 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I would like to give a feedback on a few things I observed that make the custom content feel outside of the vanilla content. This is not to criticise the great work of what TWoW already did, but just some objective observations of what could be improved over time:

 1. Custom quests text is much longer on average than those of the vanilla quests, a wall of text is tiring to follow on every quest and will make many of the players just ignore it after a while. Vanilla has shorter, clearer and more to the point details. I guess it would involve a lot of work to redo all the custom quests but maybe in time this could be slowly done.

 2. The ground noise in the custom content zones is just exaggerated, there are humps and bumps everywhere in the terrain (see for ex. the new Gilneas zone) which is clearly out of line of how the vanilla zones are modelled. Don't know the reason for this, maybe whoever is modelling the terrain is a little scared that the place will look too barren otherwise, but I think other better improvements can be done. Maybe this can also be improved over time and some custom zones can be more levelled out.

 3. Mob leashing problems. The leashing logic is different on many custom mobs, these having much shorter leashes which makes them reset to their initial position after the player starts moving away. The same leashing logic needs to be applied for all mobs (afaik vanilla leash ranges are based on the level difference between mob and player), it's important especially for Hardcore players who need to do things like mob split pulling.

These said, great content overall but some things need to be more refined over time.
