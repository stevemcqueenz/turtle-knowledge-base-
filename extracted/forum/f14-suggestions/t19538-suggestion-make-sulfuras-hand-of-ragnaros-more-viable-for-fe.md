# Suggestion: Make Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros More Viable for Feral Druids

## Post 132984 by flyinghome — 2025-05-25T21:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132984#p132984 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle Wow Development Team,

I hope this message finds you well.

As a longtime player and fan of Turtles, I truly appreciate the effort your team has put into preserving the epic legacy of classic content such as Molten Core. I would like to make a humble suggestion regarding the legendary weapon “Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros“.

Currently, “Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros“ is an iconic two-handed mace that holds immense sentimental and gameplay value. However, its stats and on-hit effect cater primarily to melee classes like Warriors and Paladins, while Feral Druids—especially those focused on DPS—find little practical benefit in using it, despite its legendary status.

To broaden the usability and excitement around “Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros“, I would like to propose adding Druids‘s Attack Power (AP) as an additional stat, or modifying its existing attributes slightly to make it more attractive for Feral Druids. This would make the weapon a more viable and thrilling option for a class that, historically, has had limited access to high-end legendary gear.

Of course, I understand the importance of class balance and the legacy of the original game design, but even a modest change could open up new possibilities and revive interest in this legendary item across a wider player base.

Thank you for your time and consideration. Keep up the amazing work!

Warm regards,
Flying Home
