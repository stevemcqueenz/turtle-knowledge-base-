# Female Dwarf Beards

## Post 140301 by Goldenspatula — 2025-07-18T12:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140301#p140301 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello! I want to address a small customization issue when it comes to the Dwarven ladies: where are their beards?
Beards are one of the **core** elements of Dwarven society and even Tolkien described them as women with beards that made it hard to distinguish them from their male counterpart. Some will say this is a political statement, like it's new instead of in the general fantasy lore since forever. For that reason, there should be customization options to add beards (perhaps less dense, or maybe with more braids to them, who knows!) to our beloved dwarven ladies.

For those who will challenge this saying it's "forced inclusion", here are some bits of lore that will help support my point:

> "Looking at them, the elf could have sworn they were all brothers-although, in fact, at least two hinted at being sisters. Even female dwarves sported strong beards, a sign of beauty among members of the race."

(Day of the Dragon, Chapter 15)

> "Indeed this strangeness they have that no Man nor Elf has ever seen a beardless Dwarf - unless he were shaven in mockery, and would then be more like to die of shame than of many other hurts that to us would seem more deadly. For the Naugrim have beards from the beginning of their lives, male and female alike; nor indeed can their womenkind be discerned by those of other race, be it in feature or in gait or in voice, nor in any wise save this: that they go not to war, and seldom save at direst need issue from their deep bowers and halls."

(c.1951 - Quenta Silmarillion revisions)

TL;DR: Add beards to Dwarven women. This is supported by the lore.

- The Last Guardian

- Day of the Dragon (Knaak)

- Lord of the Clans

- Of Blood and Honor

- The War of the Ancients Trilogy (3 books made by Knaak)

- Cycle of Hatred
If he was some random writer who wrote a small comic for Blizzard once and that was it, I'd understand the point, but I think he's a trustworthy source.

I think them not giving them beards was a commercial choice more than anything. A lot of race models were a lot chunkier/less appealing in the Alpha stages of the game and a lot of female models were feminized for corporation purposes. There are a lot of things Blizzard either didn't do or didn't finish in Vanilla but I think TWoW's whole ethos is doing what Blizzard didn't.
