# [QoL ]Prevent Group Finder from matching players with those on their ignore list

## Post 167333 by Xhuzar — 2026-02-03T17:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167333#p167333 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I would like to suggest a improvement for the Group Finder tool.

Currently, the system can place you in a group with a player you have previously added to your ignore list. This leads to uncomfortable situations where a player has to either leave the group immediately or reform the party without the ignored individual.

The Suggestion: Update the Group Finder tool so that it automatically checks a player's ignore list before forming or suggesting a group. If "Player A" has "Player B" on ignore, the system should never put them in the same party through the Group Finder tool.

Why this matters:
Consistency: As many of us remember, the LFG system in the original WotLK expansion already featured this mechanic to prevent toxicity and friction between players.
Efficiency: It saves time for the rest of the group, as they won't have to deal with people leaving or arguing as soon as the party is formed.
Player Agency: The "ignore" function should be a definitive way to curate one’s social experience, and the Group Finder tool should respect that boundary.
