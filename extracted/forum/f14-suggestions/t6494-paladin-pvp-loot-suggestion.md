Hello guys!

First I'd like to thank the T-WoW team for providing such a great experience and making me fall in love with this project!   turtle_in_love

We have PvP gear that is quite outdated and sadly not fitting at all with the Paladin PvP Meta and in general are not great options here on Turtle WoW.

 1. Currently the **Arathi Basin - League of Arathor** gear is completely forsaken and not worth to go for at almost any point. In fact I don't see these pieces on anyone at all?

A lot of the changes to factions/raid rewards pushed the AB Set away like, the Revered items from **Silvermoon/Revantusk**. You get more damage using them but you can make an argument for the missing intellect and let's be honest, AB Rep is HARD.

 **So far the only item that's somewhat OK are the shoulder pieces from Exalted:**
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20058>

I have a suggestion to change the [1% crit 3/3 Set] to Spell Power instead to compliment the better scaling of the Paladin and also be used as a placeholder until Rank 12/13 Gear.
 - Also perhaps some moderate changes to the Belt/Boots since both are falling short to the simple to earn Revered pieces for us Paladins.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20058>
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20049>
 - The ZG 3/5 Set currently is the absolute highlight for PvP as the 8/8 T2 bonus was moved there instead and is rather easy to obtain (took me 2 ZG runs and 300g).

 2. Rank 12 Boots, Rank 13 Shoulders
These two are above the 0.5 Set in terms of usefulness and are worse than T2 in general due to the 5/5 set.
 - Since they are intended to be used in 5/5 set for the Spell Power you can make an argument there but with all honesty they are underwhelming for Rank 12/13 gear.

 3. The Rank 14 Destroyer/Demolisher needs a +-30 Strength to it.

 **I know that Paladins are complained about in PvP and with all honesty once more, I can say that we can get a small nerf.**

Hope this suggestion goes through! <3
