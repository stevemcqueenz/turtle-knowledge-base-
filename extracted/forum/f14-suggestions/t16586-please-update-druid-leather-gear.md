## Post 113126 by Harkonnan (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-18T04:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113126#p113126 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm referring specifically to the higher level gear (50+) that has stat allowance spent on non-optimum stats. I would like to see your dev team review the vanilla gear with horribly optimized stats and update them like your doing with other gear.

Example:
Demonic Runed Spaulders:
 12 str
 12 sta
 12 Int < --- ??????????

What tanking druid wants a bigger mana pool? answer is, none. no one picks that piece over a piece with just str/dex/sta, or str/sta/secondary stats. and no Druid would pick that over, say, living shoulders that give you all the healing stats you need if they want to heal. If I"m healing, I need not just INT, but SPI, healing spell power, etc. It's just not a good pick for any spec. It may have been back when Druids really had no actual role to fill, but now that you are pushing talent into more focused builds, the gear should represent that.

I don't really care what you replace the stat with as long as it supports the specs it was made for. perhaps a smaller amount of Dex, which would be fine for druid, and less optimal for a rogue, so druids would still prefer that. or AP )with a higher amount of AP for druid forms like I'm seeing more often on gear).

I know you all are working real hard on gear review in addition to many other things, but it does get tiring to have to roll for gear that rightly should go to rogues, but is simply better for 2 of my specs then the gear that should really be for a druid.
