# Rated blood ring

## Post 67643 by Noshu (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-29T18:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67643#p67643 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Atm the current rewards are cool for blood ring, and the 3v3 initiative is fantastic. The only issue I see with blood ring is that there isn't really any initiative to keep playing it once you reach exalted.

I have a suggestion - add rating and matchmaking on top of the already existing system (kind of like solo queue on retail, but ignore the comp requirements), with some cosmetic/mount rewards for rating perhaps (or gear, but cosmetics is enough).

Why?

 1 - With matchmaking and rating system, people will face more equally geared and skilled players. Right now on my full naxx geared elemental shaman I roll over 90% of the people I face, and its not fun for anyone. I want a challenge and something to work towards, and the people I roll over are 3000 hp, ungeared and are probably not having a good time either, they just want to get to exalted for the gear - and they would have more fun doing so if they faced other low gear/rated people.

 2 - With some additional rewards based on rating, on top of the already existing rewards, people will have a higher incentive to queue for the blood ring post exalted.

 3 - The pvp community on the server isn't dead, but it's sleeping. With the naxx gear released people arent as hyped to push honor ranks, since T3 and other off-piece naxx items are better for most classes / specs. Ranking is also a hell of a commitment, which many people don't want to invest in. With a separate system (some kind of ranked arenas, perhaps rated solo queue 3v3) the sleeping pvpers could enjoy some kind of pvp that doesn't require all your spare time with interesting goals to work towards.

 4 - Somewhere to compete. Queuing blood ring as skirmish or bgs are great fun, but there is no show/display of skill towards other players. Being able to attain arena rating for social status on the realm as a revered pvper is something a lot of pvpers would like.

Edit:
Now that T4 and beyond is announced, I think rated arenas should be added as new pvp content, and it should include rewards that are on par with naxx and beyond, which should be purchaseable with arena points and rating.

## Post 100856 by Hardcorehygge — 2024-09-07T10:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100856#p100856 | page 3 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a competitive PvP player and former Arena Tournament tryhard, I would love to see rated arenas (and even battlegrounds) introduced to Turtle WoW! However, I understand that the server’s main focus is roleplay, so here’s my suggestion:

Introduce 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, and 5v5 arenas, and I'll explain how and the reasoning for this.

First, add rated arenas similar to what we know from other expansions. Players would form teams and compete for ratings, with a leaderboard showcasing the top teams. It would be awesome to award titles to the top teams, much like in later expansions.

Additionally, a feature called Wargames (or Mak’gora, for lore purposes) could be implemented. This would allow players to queue up, see the names of other teams in the queue (Maybe just the party leaders name), and challenge them directly. The opposing team would have to accept the match, giving players the freedom to choose their opponents. This is perfect for creating RP events, as teams could roleplay within the arena without the pressure of ratings (since you shouldn't gain rating or rep from these matches). That way you cannot abuse it to gain rating or rep.

Another cool addition to Wargames (or Mak’gora) would be the 5v5 1v1 format, also known as subwars. In this mode, teams send in one player at a time, with the next player entering as soon as the previous one dies. This creates exciting possibilities, like a 1v5 comeback scenario! I used to play this format on a private server years ago, and it was an absolute blast. Here's a newer [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c7ziFG9gDK8) that shows how fun it can be.

The third and arguably best reason to add Wargames (Mak’gora) is for tournaments. Tournaments would offer players a way to create events, with or without gold prizes, fostering a sense of community and giving endgame players a reason to keep playing and competing against one another. To make this work, a spectate option would be essential, allowing players to watch games live without participating. This would make setting up tournaments easier, though duel tournaments in stv works too! (shoutout to @S4rs for the awesome duel [tournament](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tuXcmzQeXJM)!).

In my opinion, ranked arenas should focus on the 2v2 and 3v3 brackets, as 1v1 and 5v5 formats don’t feel as balanced. That said, it wouldn’t bother me if they were included. This system could also work in battlegrounds, both ranked and the Wargames. Imagine a 40v40 AV RP event! If that’s not a roleplayers wet pvp dream, I don’t know what is.
