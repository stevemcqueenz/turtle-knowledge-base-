# equipment balance is more important than class balance

## Post 98557 by 987004590 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-05T05:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98557#p98557 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In fact, I don't always want to use the paladin as the villain, unfortunately, the Paladin as a shaman's counterpart class, only they are qualified for this position.

If shamans are allowed to wear plate armor and hold swords, I believe there are plate armor and sword shamans everywhere, does this indicate that plate armor and sword are too strong? Giving any of these powers to a shaman, I think, can fundamentally change the shaman. But rather than allowing the shaman to wear plate armor and hold a sword, a better way is to strengthen the weapons and equipment that belong to the shaman. Looking at the category of fist weapon, there are only so few of them, and they are all low-level equipment, I think that strengthening the shaman through fist weapon is a feasible way.

The Ornate Bloodstone Dagger and The Blade of Eternal Darkness is a good example, but it shouldn't be a dagger, it should be a fist. this prevents magical classes from abusing it.

Also, should there be a fix for shaman not being able to hold Atiesh. why was this obvious flaw not fixed and inherited the Scythe of Elune?  [mad_turtle_head
