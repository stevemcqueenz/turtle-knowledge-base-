# Cross-faction ganking

## Post 21924 by Jongyi (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-11T07:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21924#p21924 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ganking opposite faction can be a bit boring sometimes, having to travel to Goldshire and Westfall etc. With server not having a large PVP playerbase, how about introducing Cross-faction WPVP and ganking.

Orc ganking tauren at Mulgore would be very fun and  Undead warlock twinking hard in Crossroad.  You can work it out by adding something like Outlaw or Fugitive Mode for those who attack their own faction. It will make WPVP a lot more fun, exciting and unpredictable.

For RPers, it is also extremely lore-friendly. Imagine many RP options,
Orc supremacist who believe they are Master Race and occasionally wiping out troll, tauren, goblins and filthy undead they see. I can see new Orc RP guilds coming out like <Garrosh's Chosen> (or if u want to stick to vanilla) <Blades of Blackhand>  who want to eradicate non-orc members of the Horde.
Or you can roleplay as goblin assassin who is pretending to be horde but secretly hired by Alliance to sabotage horde from  within.
Or you can RP as undead psychopath serial killer who have been prowling and secretly murdering human and fellow undead alike.
This will bring both PVP completely unpredictable and open up many options. There will be no limit to imagination either.
Devs should seriously consider that
