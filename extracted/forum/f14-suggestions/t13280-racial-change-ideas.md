# Racial change ideas

## Post 89033 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-02-29T13:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89033#p89033 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

This time I wanna theorycraft regarding how I'd change the racials. Class racials are not included, except for fear ward.
Currently some classes punish you for not choosing a specific race (non-dwarven priests, non-human or non-orc fury warriors, alliance warlocks if you want to PVP).
My earlier standpoint was that changing racials can change the ranking of some race-class combinations which screws with people who spent many hours min-maxing a character. But at the end of the day I love theorycrafting and I wanted to write an article about racial change ideas which - while still provide race identity - make the race selection more like a preference choice than a meta choice.

 **Let's start with the Alliance!**

 **Hoomans.**

In advance - I change each weapon skill racial the same way. Instead of giving a specific amount of weapon skill, each one of them gives 1% crit chance with the related weapon. This will be true for each race.
So sword and mace specialization means +1% crit chance with these weapons.
Diplomacy is a very strong quality of life advantage for humans. Would consider decreasing it to 5%.
Perception is fine as it is, that added stealth detection is a nice extra utility.
Would keep the human spirit as it is, too. A nice utility for priests and a very niche quality of life racial for anyone else.

 **Dwarves**

Frost resistance is good as it is. In general, I keep all resistance racials as they currently are.
Find treasure is a fun utility. Personally I would buff/change it though. I think a find treasure passive racial which let's you get 5% more money from looted coins is more impactful. Specially since most veterans know where the chests are anyway. Regarding how the changed racial would work: for example, 1 silver found at the corpse of a defias bandit would be 1 silver 5 copper for a dwarf.
Gun specialiazion becomes +1% crit chance when using guns. Also a note: would add some powerful guns both for leveling and to endgame.
Stoneform is a good utility in PVE and is extremely broken for some matchups in PVP. I'd nerf it. The ability would be the same but during stoneform, the dwarf would receive a movement speed and haste penalty. You can get rid off the bleeds and poison but getting away from the enemy is harder and it is also harder to hit/cast when your skin is a stone.

Fear ward. FEAR WARD. FEAR WARD. FEAR WARD. DANGER. FEAR WARD. WHY. FEAR WARD.

No. This is a very good dwarven priest racial. It is so good that all other priest picks feel like a mistake. It is so good that TWOW added a custom talent at the end of the holy tree which has some different abilities. One of those abilities (from the ultimate at the holy tree) is to imitate this racial. Poorly. Holy champion should have a rework, too but it is not a topic of that theorycrafting. Please make fearward a universal priest ability, just like retail did in the past.
In exchange the priests could receive a new racial. We either go the boring way and grant them Chastise from the later expansions or create something new. While I think that chastise is a good utility spell, I'd personally advise a custom racial here.
Dwarves are well-known from their drinking habit. A priest racial called Faithful consumer or something could be created. It is a passive racial decreasing the cooldown of potions for the priest by 10%. This would mean more frequent drinking/mana up during longer fights, or slightly more frequent usage of utility consumables during PVP. I think this would give them a fitting and usable but not overpowered racial.

 **Gnomes**

Arcane resistance is fine, just like all of the resistance racials.
Expansive mind is strong, but I think it is all right. A typical caster raider has around 5000-6000 mana without buffs and consumables. This would mean +250/300 mana at lategame just because you are a gnome. Humans sometimes critting with some weapons just because they are humans, gnomes could cast one more spell before going dry just because they are gnomes. I'm fine with it.
Engineering specialization - I'd change profession racials. These racials help with leveling a profession but are absolutely useless once you've mastered it. My suggestion is to change profession racials in a way that you have a 5% chance when creating an item with your profession that you receive an additional item for free unless the item you made is a unique one. This would mean that in average after every 20 grenades you make there is 1 which just got randomly created in the process, meaning that you have 21 of them, just because you are a gnome. This would not work on profession abilities which have a CD. Same logic applies for each profession changes (no double-transmute for example).
Escape artist. The problem with it is its consistency, mainly at PVP. 1 min CD means that even though you can't spam it, you almost always have it in the ready. I would make this instant cast but would increase the CD to 2 minutes.

 **Night elves**

We seriously need to nerf night elf racials because they are so overpowered that a group of level 30 night elves could easily clear MC just by spamming shadowmeld.
Jokes aside, night elves need some support in my opinion.

Nature resistance is okay, just like all resistances.
Wisp spirit: a purely quality of life talent. I think it is fine as it is.
Dodge - I understand that the night elves are quick but in PVE dodge is only really important if you're a tank. Meaning, that you are a bear because noone in history has ever played a night elf warrior. Would change this talent to become +1-2% haste. I have many reasons for this. Haste helps every build, not just the bears. Haste also represents the quickness of this race. Night elves who might or might not be related to trolls would receive a racial somewhat similar to trolls' berserking, meaning a continuity between them if we consider the night elves a descendant of trolls or another weird connection between them if we consider them unrelated.
Would buff shadowmeld, too. Under buff I mean I want to nerf its CD first to 2 minutes. After that I'd change the ability itself, highly increasing the stealth level of your character for 10 seconds (for an ambush). This would give stealth for non-rogues and druids and MEGA-STEALTH (like the ninjas envy you level of stealth) for prowling feral druids and sneaky rogues.

 **Bloo... High elves**

Arcane resistance, like all resistances, is fine as it is.
The bow weapon skill would add +1% crit chance with bows, as usual.
Enchanting could be more lucrative for them, with the 5% chance of additional product being generated (same way as goblins and engineering). Recipees having a CD would never generate a duplicte.
Quel'dorei Meditation is a very strong ability if it's on CD. However, it doesn't break PVP or PVE, it is somewhere between quality of life and utility. The fact that you have to channel it balances it in my opinion. It is fine as it is.

 **For the Horde!**

 **Orcs**

Orcs are a perfectly bala...
Okay, let's nerf orcs.
Command is fine as it is. Axe specialization should give +1% crit chance for axes, as usual. As a side note, TWOW should have some better axes throughout leveling and raiding. A legendary axe wouldn't hurt the game either. Maelstrom Axe, the Primal Zugzug or something.
After all this, let's get to the problem part!
Hardiness with 25% is way too much. Later expansions reduced the buff to 15% and I think that is the way to go.
Blood fury is at least a risky choice at PVP. However, when you are a fury warrior in a raid and you only expect to get hit by some pathetic cleaves at best, you just turn it on, and go superzugzug with no real punishment. MT and OT are being pummeled anyway, not you. The most diplomatic nerfs are changing the cooldown numbers and the amount of buff. Here, instead of that I want to make a creative but potentially awful proposal. Keep the talent as it is but add one more repercussion for using it. During blood fury each of your hits have a 10% chance to hit one of your party members within a 5 yard radius instead of the target. This change would mean that in a 1 vs 1 scenario the lower self-heal poses the risk if you use the talent, while in a situation where your raid group attacks an enemy, you also have to consider if a damage spike would worth potentially one-shotting your rogue friend. This change would break some friendships after Musclepowa, the level 60 fury warrior kills his buddy, Stabbiboi, the rogue, for the 3rd time within a raid.
If we want to be more conventional, changing the CD to 5 minutes, the damage bonus to 5% and the healing reduction to 10% is also a way to go.

 **Trolls**

My favorite race. I highly doubt that I'll be objective here but let's give it a try!
Beast slaying is fine in my opinion. A situational damage boost which has some very limited use in PVP against hunters. Most raids are not filled with beasts anyway.
Bow and throwing specialization giving +1% crit as usual. And TWOW Team, please rework thrown weapons, they are absolutely useless.
Regeneration, the most fearsome racial. Entire generations of Alliance players were terrified of trolls lurking in the jungle, using their legendary thrown weapons on them for 23 damage and when you hit back in a rush of panic, they just regenerate the damage (at a very moderate speed). I'd change regenerate in a way that on top of your normal health regeneration, this racial regenerates 2% of your life total every 5 seconds. If you are a lategame warrior with 5000 health, it means 100 HP every 5 seconds on top of your normal health regeneration. If it is too much, we can always go 1% instead of 2%.
Berserking is beautiful. Please keep it as it is. Loving it. If we are afraid of its power level in a world of weakened racials, we can make the haste buff interval to be between 10-20% instead of 10-30% based on health. Anyway, berserking is weak because you wanna be kind low at health for the berserking to truely kick in but as a troll you can't be at low health because of your RegenerationTM.

 **Undeads**

Cannibalize - a perfect racial and a very flavorful one.
Shadow resistance is cool just like all of the resistance abilities.
Underwater breathing is very niche. I know it's a quality of life skill but at least make it a real quality of life skill. For example adding a 20% swimming speed boost for undeads alongside with the breathing buff.
And the problem child, the will of the forsaken. We should also note that without this, the undeads are really lackluster. No weapon crit, no added damage, nothing. So will of the forsken has to remain a strong ability, but without oppressing everyone at PVP. People usually advise will of the forsaken to be only able to remove these ffects, not to prevent them, or to raise the CD. I have two idea here besides these ones above. We could either change will of the forsaken to be a passive ability, just like hardiness works, granting a passive x% resist chance for fear, charm and fear effects. The other idea is a haste and movement speed penalty for 2 seconds after using it, similarly to the stoneform change idea. In this case, the debuff comes after the effect takes place because people are numb for some seconds after waking up from a weird dream/illusion.

 **Taurens**

I think this is a balanced one. In case of cultivation I'd add the 5% chance of getting one additional item. Nature resistance and endurance are fine. For war stomp the only change I'd consider is allowing it in shapeshifted forms - for example a bear tank war stomping.

 **Goblins**

Their sneaking animation is a prime racial.
Besides that, weapons could give +1% crit chance as usual. Mining and alchemy could have the 5% chance of additional loot/generated item. This can be a real money-making difference which is fitting for goblins. You also need to ask yourself if you go to the weird mining/alchemy route for maximized free loot or you go for a more ordinary way instead. Goblin speed boost is fine, it could stay as it is.
