# Challenge glyph ideas

## Post 96350 by Rudyraccoon — 2024-06-04T21:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96350#p96350 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Recently I was playing on Project Ascension, and I have noticed that they have a a lot of different challenge modes available that I'm surprised are currently not a thing on Turtle WoW, so I figured it be interesting if more challenge glpths were added, so here are some glyphs I have come up with.

 **Glyph of the Quester**
Use: Enable the Champion of Azorth challenge.
 **"This glyph prevents you from gaining experience from creature kills, and from finding discoveries, but your reputation gain from quests will be doubled."**

 **Glyph of the Lone Wolf**
Classes: Hunter Warlock
Use: Enable the Lone Wolf challenge.
 **"You are restricted from ever using a tamed pet or summoned demon, but you will instead gain new spells and abilities.**

 **Glyph of Survival**
Use: Enable the Survival challenge.
 **"You gain a thirst and hunger bar.**

The last one I'll admit would be crazy to implement, it would make food and drink items more important.
