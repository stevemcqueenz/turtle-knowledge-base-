# Addressing rare loot frustration in WoW Vanilla raids: Memories token system proposal

## Post 109707 by Manolothegreat (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-24T18:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109707#p109707 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, fellow adventurers!

I’d like to share an idea I’ve been brainstorming to address the issue of rare loot drops in raids. I know this suggestion might challenge traditional notions of WoW Vanilla, but I believe it could add a more predictable and fair progression system while keeping the spirit of the game intact.

 **The Concept: Boss Tokens**
The core idea is to introduce a token system tied to raid bosses, particularly for end-game raids. Here's how it would work:
- Whenever a raid group defeats the final boss of a raid, the boss drops a single unique item called a Memory of [Boss Name]. For example, defeating Nefarian would grant you a *Memory of Nefarian*.
- This token is a regular loot. Can be rolled or delivered by the master looter to any player
- These tokens can then be used as currency to purchase specific loot from Chromie, Anachronos (or any other lore-appropriate NPC in Caverns of Time).
- Chromie could offer a list of rare items tied to the respective raid and boss. For instance, for Blackwing Lair, you could buy *Neltharion's Tear*, *Rejuvenating Gem*, *Drake Fang Talisman*, or the new mount for 20 *Memory of Nefarian*.
- Same for all raids
 **Scaling Costs for Items**
Items with higher desirability or rarity (weapons, or trinkets) could cost more tokens, encouraging players to consistently run the raid. Or if you want to keep it simple just add really rare items like trinkets and weapons with low drop rates.

 **Balancing drop rates and player choice**
This system would not replace existing loot drops but would act as a supplement to alleviate frustration from bad RNG. Players could still get their desired loot the traditional way, but tokens provide a safety net for those who are persistently unlucky.

 **The time commitment**
Collecting enough tokens would take a significant amount of time to provide a sense of balance and effort. For example: If *Neltharion's Tear* costs 20 *HMemory of Nefarian*, a guild would need to complete Blackwing Lair 20 times. With a weekly raid lockout, this translates to 20 weeks or around 5 months of consistent raiding. This ensures that items purchased with tokens retain their prestige and effort while providing a reliable alternative to RNG.

 **Legendary Items and Drop-Locked Gear**
The token system could also be extended to legendary items, which currently have notoriously low drop rates or restrictive requirements, making them a significant source of frustration among raiders. In this system, players wouldn’t directly purchase the legendary item itself but rather the key component required to begin its crafting process. Examples include:
- Thunderfury Bindings: The bindings required for Thunderfury are extremely rare drops from Garr and Baron Geddon in Molten Core, often taking months or even years to collect. With the token system, each binding could be purchased for 16 *Memory of Ragnaros*, totaling 32 memories or 8 months of consistent raiding. Players would still need to gather Arcanite Bars, Elementium Ore, and other materials to complete the weapon.
- Eye of Sulfuras: A rare drop from Ragnaros needed to craft Sulfuras, Hand of Ragnaros. This could be available for 30 *Memory of Ragnaros*. Crafting Sulfuras would still require the Sulfuron Hammer and additional materials.
- Scythe of Elune: This item is currently gated behind a drop with less than a 1% chance from a 10-man dungeon that resets every 5 days. 20 *Memory of Moroes* or *Shard of Elune* makes sense for me.
This approach ensures that legendary items retain their prestige and crafting effort while reducing the overwhelming reliance on RNG for their acquisition.

 **Potential Benefits:**
- Encourages continued raiding: Players are motivated to keep raiding even after most gear has dropped, as they can work toward specific items.
- Reduces frustration: Those guilds with bad luck no longer feel stuck in an endless grind.
- Supports guild loyalty: Guilds benefit from members sticking around longer, knowing their efforts will eventually be rewarded.
- Maintains vanilla feel: By focusing only on final bosses and retaining the RNG loot system, the classic experience remains largely intact.
- Eases legendary acquisition: This system gives raiders a fairer shot at legendaries without trivializing the crafting process.
 **Why Chromie?**
Chromie fits well into the WoW universe as a vendor for this system. She’s already tied to time-related mechanics and could be themed as helping players overcome the whims of fate by saving and using their “time” (represented as tokens).

I understand that implementing a system like this might be challenging or may not align with current developer priorities, but I wanted to share it in hopes of sparking a discussion within the community.
Also, I understand that Vanilla purists might have reservations, but I genuinely think this system could enhance the overall experience without detracting from the classic charm.

Also, if the whole thing is too much work then applying this to just legendaries would be ok.

What do you think about this idea? I’d love to hear feedback or suggestions for improvement.
Let’s discuss it!
