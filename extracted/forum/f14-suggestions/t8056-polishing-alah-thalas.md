# Polishing Alah'Thalas

## Post 51777 by Autismoprime — 2023-06-29T11:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51777#p51777 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In my humble opinion, the Alah'Thalas area DESPERATELY needs some polish. High elf is one of the most popular races and being a new custom race, draws in a lot of players. To do the starting zone and then be told to go check out the new cool custom-made high elf hub zone 'Alah'Thalas' only to see a placeholder world map and the land just abruptly ending in the ocean instead of a beach, turned me off of the game completely for a long time, thinking 'if this is the level of the custom content, I'm not interested', and even though I've recently come back to the server and decided to give it a new try thanks to the rest of the albeit amazing new content that's been created, Alah'Thalas still leaves a sour taste in my mouth, and I can't be alone. These things aren't just nitpicking, polish is important and is what leaves a lasting impression on the people playing on the server, and helps people feel immersed in a roleplay-rich, rich world, which in vanilla wow, is crucial.
