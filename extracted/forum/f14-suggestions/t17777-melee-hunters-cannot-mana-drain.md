# Melee hunters cannot mana drain

## Post 121182 by Sirracha — 2025-02-28T02:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121182#p121182 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please allow melee hunters to actually mana drain. We can use viper sting, but due to the stinging nettle talent your mongoose bite will apply serpent sting and wipe away your viper sting. This will be a problem for any raid that requires members to drain mana of their targets (Moam in AQ20). I think the serpent sting applied on mongoose bite is cool and fits thematically but perhaps allow that specific serpent sting to stack with viper sting.

Side note: Please let us have an ability similar to tranquilizing shot that can be used in melee

How about changing stinging nettle to the following ?

"Applies an unresistable Serpent Sting on Mongoose Bite. If the target already has a Sting applied to them, refresh the duration of that sting instead."

That way you can still keep the fact that Mongoose Bite applies Serpent Sting on resistant targets, whilst also being able to use it flexibly with other stings without having to constantly move out of melee or overwriting your sting due to a mongoose bite.
