Turtle WoW is using a unique concept combining RP + PvE + Lowrates together on a single realm. Our target audience is not big and this realm is not meant to have 5000 players online.

 **Yes, it has barely changed between the 11.10 - 11.11, our peak last night at 8PM was 102 online.**

Will we change the current realm to PvP or give players gold on new characters?
 **No, definitely not.**

 **Yes, we are open minded and gladly discuss these. We accepted the [portable meeting stone](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=192) idea and love it.**

Ok, some input from the first (and afaik only) alliance lvl 60 so far:

There are two major issues I see with this server.
 1. There is a rumor that this is essentially an Elysium Project. Dunno if it's true or not, but that's what people on reddit and other channels are spreading.
 2. This server doesn't really know what ruleset it wants to use. There are some non-blizzlike things and it gets changed/there are plans to change it further.

To solve the first problem, issue a statement about who's running the server. Ofc no real names but we want to know a bit about you. If this is indeed an Elysium project, don't lie about it. It makes things worse.

The second issue also isn't too hard to solve, make a pragmatic ruleset and stick with it. 0.5x rates doesn't attract many people, so simply give players the choice to either play at 0.5 or 1x rates, get rid of this progressive stuff. Let people choose at lvl 10 if they want to continue with 0.5x rates till the end or if they - permanently - wanna increase the rates to x1/blizzlike. Rewards for those who level with 0.5: One reward upon choosing the rates at lvl 10 and one reward for reaching lvl 60 at 0.5x.

Another thing: This server is listed as "custom/RP server" on the WoW private server reddit. While the RP-tag makes it somewhat special (you should declare that it's optional though), the custom-tag scares away many people. Having an ingame cashshop... well, if you really need it. But changing too much other stuff besides the rates will quickly ruin the blizzlike experience most people are looking for.

Thanks btw for prodiving this server, appreciation for what you guys are doing sometimes gets lost in all the bickering and moaning.
