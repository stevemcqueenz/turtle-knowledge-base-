# Early Endgame for Casual PvPers.

## Post 40304 by Annoying (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-04T21:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40304#p40304 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Are you a casual player that likes to pvp and play WSG/AB and perhaps do a few dungeons but don’t want to fight Naxx geared players with a fresh/dungeon geared 60?
Perhaps you have no interest in raiding or playing all day without breaks to get the pvp gear?

Consider locking your exp at level 49.

Twinking at level 49 is a lot more washed out than level 19 and 29 where you are basically a raid boss for regular players.
So you can compete and still have fun even if you don’t have the very best items yet.

But I would advise you to only join WSG/AB at level 46+ or else you will have problems with resists.

You get most of your gear from doing regular dungeons around your level like Uldaman, Maraudon, Zul'farrak, Sunken Temple and early Blackrock Depths bosses.
The new dungeon Gilneas City has some decent items as well.

It's very alt friendly because of that, so you can get very varied gameplay.

One important thing is that players of 49 lvl have more means to farm gold compared to lvl 19.
On 19 you inevitably have to make donor character of high level to buy enchants.

Enchants don't give absolute advantage. Your normal gear can provide good stats.

Players of 49 lvl also has mounts. I mean normal mounts with 60% speed, because turtle on low lvl is a joke.
There are also plate for warriors, mail for shamans and hunters. Dire bear form for druids.
Also there is access to final talent in each tree like Mortal Strike, Holy Shock, Shadowform and other stuff.

Ability to stop xp is great option for non-twinks, which allows normal players to prepare for fights by themselves.
