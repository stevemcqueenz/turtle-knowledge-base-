# Enchanting Progression

## Post 166700 by Azinforum — 2026-01-28T02:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166700#p166700 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Please consider improving the enchanting content as well as improving the progression of enchants

Weapon Enchants, Shield, Shoulders, and Cloak Enchants are in a particularly bad spot

BIS Cloak Enchants are relevant resistance, +3 agility, 1% dodge, or Threat Reduction.
The fact that a BIS UKH Cloak enchant is +3 agility feel a bit silly. Please consider increasing the "ranks" of these enchants, or adding some more flexibility in the stat choice. Casters literally have no reason to enchant a cloak

Shield Enchants are Lesser Blocking and Stamina. Spirit is bad for Shamans so it is a dead enchant.
Something like Intellect for healers, +healing, +sp, +threat, etc

Shoulder Enchants being faction/Naxx locked is iconic of Classic, and Nord enchants have helped the problem a bit. However, it devalues the enchanting profession by saying "just go be friends with someone else for the enchant". I think Faction enchants/naxx enchants can still be bis, but adding a small set of level 15/35/50 tier enchants is not a bad idea. Think +3, +5, +7 relevant stat/sp; 5, 10, 15hp

Weapon Enchants also need a review similar to shoulder enchants, but have a different problem.
Weapon enchant progression as a caster is particularly terrible. Your options are 3, 9, 22 int/spirit. And we all know that 22 int/spirit are dead enchants compared to 30sp or 55hp.
Please consider something like 3sp, 9sp, 18sp, 30sp for the progression of enchanting as a spellcaster while also adjusting the progression for the int/spirit enchants to something like 3 > 9 > 15 > 30
Similarly for healing, please consider something like +5, +12, +27, +55

Weapon enchant progression as melee is "okay" cause you can at least get "striking", which to be honest is still bad, but the power gap between melee weapon enchants doesn't really make sense
Firey/Icy/Unholy/etc are all basically dead for end-game, which is fine, but their is a firm gap in leveling and progression enchants
Please consider adding something in the scheme of minor/lesser/stat/major/mighty for relevant melee stats
Going from Striking > Firey > 15 agility/crusader isn't smooth progression or overly logical
