# Make inns great (again)?

## Post 60997 by Azuladrip (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-05T13:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60997#p60997 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm slowly coming to dislike tents. Not because they aren't useful or a cool idea, but because they've become goofy due to the number of players under them at any one time and their omnipresence. You go to a hub and there's a billion people crammed up under a tent and clipping through one another, while the inn, the actual place you should be going to in order to rest, is completely empty. It's just bizarre, especially if you're seriously trying to immerse yourself into roleplaying.

I'm not really sure what could be done about this. It would be nice if there were some item also crafted with survival you could deploy only in an inn which would offer a slightly faster rested EXP increase and potentially some minor benefits, so that while the tent remains, you would still prefer going to the inn since you get more bang for every waited minute. Maybe this could even be a pet or some sort of follower that can only be used at an inn and expires in the same way the tent does.

And since we are on the topic of inns, why not give players the option to buy their rested time directly without any wait?  Just have the innkeeper offer the pricey option to spend the night which would give you a fully rested EXP bar as soon as  you pay the gold for your stay. I don't think it should be cheap enough to be normalized, but it would be a nice goldsink for people who are leveling alts.

Obviously, there are many options that would be much a lot more immersive, like if you actually had to /sleep in an inn bed to get the rested benefits, but that would probably not be popular with the non-RP players and you'd pretty much be replacing people clipping under tents to clipping as they're sleeping in the same bed (which is also a lot more inappropriate...).

I dunno, I kinda just wish tents had a different functionality at this point, but I'm not sure what that could be.
