# When I move my cosmetic pet should always move right next to me facing the same direction

## Post 174122 by Dbl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-04-06T00:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174122#p174122 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

**This is regarding regular cosmetic pets, not the hunter ones**

It has always bugged me during RP events or at times when I wanted to take a screenshot,every time when I move my pet, instead of moving with me and stopping by my side, he just phases through me or goes in a completely random direction. I propose that when you move the pets default reaction should be to always move with you, stand right next to you and stand in that spot for at least 15sec and then if you're idle long enough he can move around.

This will help out in taking screenshots a ton as I don't have to pray that my pet stands where I want it to and it will also improve the feeling of companionship during RP events.
