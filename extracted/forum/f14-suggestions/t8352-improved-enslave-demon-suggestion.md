# Improved Enslave Demon suggestion

## Post 53822 by Pngu — 2023-07-17T14:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53822#p53822 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Before my suggestion I just like to thank the twow devs for all the work and dedication to make vanilla even better and to keep it monitored from bots and gold sellers. I hope I still get to enjoy it for years to come.

The Improved Enslave Demon talent from warlocks currently, even if reduced from 5 to 2 ranks feels like there's no place in the game, the main reason being that after each consecutive time the demon escapes it and we're forced to re-apply the spell, it progressively gets harder to keep it enslaved to us.

So let say I wish to do open world content with the felguard, first I would need to find myself two souls willing to help me summon it first, which frankly is not feasible most of the time, but let's say I find it anyway. The first 5 minutes it will stay enslaved. The second enslave will probably last 3 minutes. The third 1.5 minutes maybe. And considering the fact I need more people to help me summon, you'd expect the felguard to be as tanky as a voidwalker and as strong as a succubus, which isn't the case. The damage is a little bit above of the succubus, but it dies pretty easily.

In a dungeon scenario, if the demon aggro 2 elite mobs it dies way too fast, even when using health funnel. And yes I was using the talents to improve health funnel. Its aoe damage is good though.

In a raid scenario I haven't tested, but considering locks have the ability to summon a doomguard with 5 people I wish I could use it instead of the felguard.

I also haven't tested on pvp mainly because I'm not focusing on pvp for the time being, but I suppose that the felguard should work for pvp?

So my suggestions would be to:
 1. Give the Improved Enslave Demon talent an effect that removes the "If you repeatedly enslave the same demon, it will become more difficult to control with each attempt" effect, and also give a 15 or 20 minute duration so warlocks consider it using rather than just the nuisance of having to keep reapplying ensalve demon every 5 minutes.
 2. Make it so that the warlock can't get killed by the summon doomguard spell, so you know, we can enslave it before a raid boss fight. Yes I tested, and the lock can die from it.
 3. Make the felguard a little bit more tanky so it won't die too fast from elite mobs in a dungeon scenario, just so I can use health funnel on it.

 *Another thing which is not a suggestion, currently if you select the felguard's abilities to be used every time is available, it doesn't work. It will just use its first ability and never the other ones, I have to keep using them manually*.

If all things I suggested are implemented, locks could use the felguard for dungeons and bgs, the doomguard for raids, and the regular demons for open world content. And to make that even more obvious, you could increase the amount of people necessary to summon the felguard from 3 to 5 people, and the doomguard from 5 to 10 people.

Thanks again for all your hard word!
