# Hidden Turtles

## Post 864 by Almondx — 2018-10-18T06:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=864#p864 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd like to add new things to Classic that encourage exploring the world.  Most expansions seem to the delete fun of old content when new things are  added.  Something fun we could add to Turtle WOW is Named Turtles.  They would be turtles you find when exploring.  Like Chests.

When you click on a turtle it gives you a message and a buff or small treasure appropriate to the zone.   After you click on a turtle it moves to another random spawn point and if no-one clicks after two hours it moves to another spawn point.

I would rather see additions to the game that encourage exploration over the quality of life changes.
