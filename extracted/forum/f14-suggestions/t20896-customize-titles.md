# Customize titles

## Post 145196 by Ccsica — 2025-08-13T20:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145196#p145196 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Right now we can only have 1 active title at any time. So if you've managed to gather up multiple titles there's no way to show it off.
A cool feature would be the ability to customize your title based on the current titles you have access to.
So say you have "Crazy Cat Lady" and "Seeker of Knowledge", you would get the ability to make your own title based on the words included in those titles. Maybe add in some extra words like "of, the, and".
You could then call yourself "Crazy Seeker of Cat Knowledge" for example.
Of course it would need to have some limits on total length/number of words/etc.

I have some ideas on how this could be solved on the client with an addon, but all validation etc would of course have to be implemented on the server.
