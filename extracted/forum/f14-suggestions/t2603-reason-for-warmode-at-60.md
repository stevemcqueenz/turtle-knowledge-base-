# Reason for warmode at 60

## Post 16233 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-01-23T19:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16233#p16233 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

currently their is no sane reason to choose to stay on warmode once you hit 60. The meta is use warmode for the bonus exp until 60,then use your one time opt out.

Proper world pvp in Blackrock mountain were it might a tislly be fun and "fairer", doesn't happen, because their is really no reward to exposing oneself to ganks.

i would like to propose a 10% honor and pvp reputation bonus for lvl 60 warmode players. this should icentivice players to stick with it longer, but you pay with the extra ganking risk.

If you go warmode, you are all out at war with the other faction and should be rewarded for your extra war efforts and risks taken in your ranking.

The final problem remains, once you compete your rank 14 grind and got all pvp reps to exalted, their is no reason to stay warmode, but then, players that really reached that faecon pvp might actually really enjoy pvp and just stick with it anyway.

IF you got any other fair idea for a lvl 60 warmode bonus, please share.

Here are some more ideas I had and decided to scrap:

warmode players could get a flat 10% extra reputation Similiar to the human racialand the exp bonus lowered from 30 to 25% in exchange.

But This would give a much more permanent bonus than just faster leveling, since all the quest from 1-60 give a lot of bonus rep.

On the other hand limiting such bonus rep to only lvl 60 warmode players would incetivie postponing quests until 60 for the bonus rep, which is unfun but players would optimizer the fun out anyway and abuse this to hurt their own experience.

So giving warmode players at 60 some kind of bonus for pvp goals makes most sense and fairness.
