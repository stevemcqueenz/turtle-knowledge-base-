# Incentivize leveling crafting professions to reduce profession redundancy

## Post 98218 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-06-30T16:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98218#p98218 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm gonna talk about leatherworking because it's the one I'm familiar with but I figure there's the same issue with Ench, BS, Tailoring, and Alch beyond that of using a long cooldown every few days.

I just reached lvl 250 in Leatherworking which I was excited about: it means I get to spec into one of the three specializations ! But it turns out there are no BOP crafts, nor is there some kind of self-enchantment of any kind. This means that, now that I joined a guild, and that we already have 3 max-leveled Leatherworkers, I'm just a Salt Shaker cooldown bot.

Would be very nice to give professions a niche that can't be filled by a guildie, like WOTLK did. If you're unfamiliar, WOTLK gave, in addition to various crafting BOPs (the latter of which BC had already done):
 - blacksmithing 2 extra gem slots
 - jewelcrafting powerful BOP gems
 - LW, Enchanting, Eng, scribes and Tailoring can-only-be-cast-on-self enchants
 - +crit to skinners
 - +stam to miners
 - a self-cast-only HoT + haste buff to herbalists

I'm not suggesting to add WOTLK perks profession perks to TWOW specifically, but it feels awful to have slaved away to level a profession only to realize at the home stretch that our prof is nothing more than a cooldown
