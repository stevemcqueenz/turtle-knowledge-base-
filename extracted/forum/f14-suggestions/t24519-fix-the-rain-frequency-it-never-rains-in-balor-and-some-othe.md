# Fix the rain frequency (It never rains in Balor and some other zones)

## Post 172540 by Dbl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-26T12:49:00Z

Hi, I'm making this post after a small survey I did. Me and several people have noticed that the rain falls less frequently in some zones than the others which would make sense if it was intentional but it clearly isn't because cloudy zones like Balor and other custom ones never get rain while some desert ones do often. I think the rain frequency should be increased and I think it rains much more often on Ambershire server than on Nordanaar and other people have also confirmed it so. Please fix rain not falling ever in some zones and make it more frequent overall as right now it is inconsistent, it just makes the zone look soo much nicer. The whole Balor experience is ruined as the zone not only doesn't have shadows but also it's cloudy with 0% of rain, why?

Good point. By the way, I have my thought about weathers written below.

The aesthetic design of WoW in most of its zones is oriented to how they look in a sunny scene. Except some bleak and grim themed zones and those densely covered up by foliage, like Duskwood, Dark Shore, Tirisfal Forest and Teldrassil, all other zones have their most beautiful scenes in a sunny weather.

The game is never designed as a world with distinct seasons on basis of mechanism as players' progress are variable. In my opinion, the best-looking of each zone should be shown with priority. For example, Elwynn Forest should be in a bright and favorable weather in a longer time, maybe 3/4, and in rain for about 1/5 and in an even shorter snow duration.

Although rains and snows should be shorter to make zones better-looking and friendly for players to have a better vision, some new techniques can be used for creating water pools after rain and snow build-up to make player realise that this zone has differenct weathers, hence enhancing immersion. Lastly, it still feels disappoiting that TW 2.0 is halted.
