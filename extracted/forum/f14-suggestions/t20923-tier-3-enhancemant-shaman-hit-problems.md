# Tier 3 Enhancemant Shaman Hit Problems

## Post 145473 by Thesquid — 2025-08-14T16:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145473#p145473 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Enhancement Shaman t3 gear has far too much hit on it. We have 6% hit just from this set. This is almost as much as rogues' t3 has, having 7% on a set for a class that is meant to dual wield. But we all know that shamans are not meant to dual wield on turtle. I can't help feeling however that we were intended to dual wield with so much hit on our gear. Otherwise, it's completely wasted stats that means that it goes unused in PvE.

From just t3 and our talents alone, we're already 1% over hit cap, standing at a whopping 9%. This is not including powerful items like Kiss of the Spider, Band of Unnatural Forces, Weapon Skill Books, Weapon Skill Items, and more, that shamans are already want to go after for separate reasons. Shamans can barely aim for the 4 set bonus without going massively over hitcap. And at this point, there's not a reason to do that. Some of our t2 pieces like Boots, Gloves, and Chest are better statted for us because they have haste on them instead of hit. Our t3 gear is being beaten out by offset items as well. Sanctum Bark Wraps from Emerald Sanctum are probably BiS for us in Kara 40 because they have haste on them.

So, to address this, I'd like to ask the dev or team behind itemization to consider changing some of the needless hit on the gear to something more beneficial. Preferably, I'd like to ask for haste. At worst, maybe some crit. I don't understand why our t2 set can have more haste on it than our t3. Only the t3 helmet has haste, and it's great. So instead of using naxx gear while we raid in Kara 40, we're still in our BWL tier gear, simply because its itemized far better for us than our t3.

If it can't be changed to have more haste, then fuck it. Break the emergency glass, press the red button, and make us dual wield.That's a joke don't do that. I would be upset.

Let us do something to make the hit more usable. I think our tier 3 set deserves a look over and some consideration for a rework.
