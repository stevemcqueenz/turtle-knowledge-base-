## Post 38324 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-20T13:12:00Z

While this would increase the amount of content available for a different levels, i think its a terribly idea to do this in classic. Its a core part of feeling stronger in an RPG to be able to easily overwhelm previously difficault or impossible challenges.

It would also lead to questions like what loot would these places drop, do you just Upscale items from low level dungeons? Vanilla items aren't really scaleable in the way modern wow items are. Some items like Ravager might be a bit too good if you could get them with the same effect & Attackspeed but a competetive DPS. (This is the exact reason the weapon from Vaults has such a fast attackspeed)

I'd much rather see effort poured into new dungeons that are custom made and enrich the world in a way that satisfies both mechanical and lore interested players. Rather than regurgitating leveling content.
