While it's a fun thing to do, a rogue tank does not need to be unhittable to be able to tank. Very rare are the raid bosses or mobs that can hit (crit) hard enough to kill a rogue in one go, and I've never had it happen in a dungeon or even Zul'Gurub and Molten Core.

You can also easily use Flourish and Slice and Dice on a 100% uptime if you don't build up all the way to 5 combo points; while the 1 or 2 combo versions grant less duration, they grant more of it per energy spent if you consider the energy used to generate the required combo points. In fact, if you used Flourish only at 5 combo points and used (Improved) Sinister Strike to generate them, you will be spending more energy on it than what you can generate during its duration.
With a Setup spec you can gain more avoidance and plenty of excess combo points for keeping up Expose Armor as well, but in exchange your threat will suffer heavily (and hemorrhage + expose will just make physical DPS outscale your threat even more easily). The only thing I use Setup builds for is duo raid trash farming with a healer.

My recommendation is to slightly adjust the 20/31/0 Combat DPS build to pick up all the tanking talents in Combat, including Riposte. Depending on the amount of utility talents you wish to pick up, the build could be up to 15/36/0.
