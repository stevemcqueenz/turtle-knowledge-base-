Lore-wise, no. You enslave all of the demons against their will. The Voidwalker says, "I don't like this place," and "Send me back!" almost every time it is forced out of the Void; it expresses its utter contempt towards you. They hate you, and if they had a chance, they would attack and kill your character, as can be seen with Infernals and Doomguards. The only reason it doesn't happen with Imps, Voidwalkers, Succs, and Felhunters is that they're not as powerful as Infernals and Doomguards and are easier to enslave.

None of these sentient beings would care for you naming them. The reason Hunters can name their pets once is because they tamed them using love, and there's a relationship that grows, and a pet is more likely to respond to its pet name. There's no relationship between a Warlock and their "pet" other than forced enslavement.
