# We are in desparate need of new biome assets

## Post 153616 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-29T03:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153616#p153616 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The new zones are well structured and fun to play in, but it's starting to get monotonous with the over reliance on old foliage.  In doing so, it restricts the new content into only being derivative of existing settings.  I understand Lapidis Isle and Gilijim Isle looking like STV, but Tel'Abim is on the other side of the world and so should look more distinc; however,it just looks like an extension of STV.
Part of the reason why Thallassian Highlands is aesthetically pleasing is because it stands out relative to the rest of the game since it used TBC assets.

It would be nice if we could take the trees from Azuremyst Isle and use them for a future zone.  Even though vanilla already has conifer trees, having another variety to use for a new zone would be refreshing.
The same goes for caves, because the same cave assets are being repeated too many times now.

I appreciate that we've been getting new building assets, but at this point new natural environments I think needs more attention
