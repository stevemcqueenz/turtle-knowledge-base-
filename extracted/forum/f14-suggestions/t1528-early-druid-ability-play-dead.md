# Early druid ability - Play dead

## Post 9120 by Lexi (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-01-14T20:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9120#p9120 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Play dead was an ability that worked very similarly to the hunter's feign death in early builds of wow. I'd love to see its return. It was useable in kitty form and had a longer cooldown than feign death, 10 minutes if I remember correctly. This could add a new playstyle in raids for druids, allowing them ocassionally fulfill the pulling role. Dash in, feral faerie fire, play dead against mobs that can be awkward to pull. Additionally it could give druids a choice if they wanna pop their brez to recover from a wipe rather than using it in the heat of battle. Overall its a simple ability that gives druids more choices, and gives druids another meaningful  cd, very few druid abilitys have one.
