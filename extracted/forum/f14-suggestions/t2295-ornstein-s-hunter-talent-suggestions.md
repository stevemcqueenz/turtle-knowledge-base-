# Ornstein's Hunter Talent Suggestions

## Post 14415 by Celaris — 2021-12-07T01:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14415#p14415 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone, my hunters name is Ornstein and I've been playing a vanilla hunter for years & have experience at playing my class at its maximum potential. Hunters are the weakest pure DPS spec in vanilla and here are some of my suggestions that I think could improve an overall weak class that was barely given love by blizzard in the early stages of the game because they clearly had no idea what direction to go with the class. I'm not saying these are changes that should be made, but just offering suggestions on how the most useless talents could be improved & add more flavor to the class. I'd also like to see other peoples suggestions to how some of the more useless talents could be improved!   turtle_tongue_head

 **Beast Mastery**
BM is a really weak spec in vanilla raids/dungeons that has a lot of useless talents in the talent tree.

 **Improved Eyes of the Beast**

Never used by anyone, even if you're doing a mend pet build because mend pet requires a channel.
I suggest changing this to a 2/2 talent that increases your pets chance to hit by 2% per level because a hunters pet has no bonus to hit, so they miss a lot.

 **Improved Aspect of the Monkey**

Another talent no one takes as it requires too many points.
Could be changed to a 3/3 that increases dodge by 2% per rank instead, making "hunter tank" more viable.

 **Bestial Swiftness**

Only taken while leveling because it is only usable outdoors.
Making it usable indoors could help a pet survive by allowing it to get out of bad aoes quicker & pets move very slow without it.

 **Bestial Discipline**

The focus regeneration is just too little on this talent to be worth taking. Pets regenerate 25 focus per tick and this talent only adds 5 extra points @ max rank to the regeneration up to 30 per tick. Increasing this up to 30% per level (instead of TBCs 50% per level) would make this worth choosing without making it too overpowered. This makes it 40 regenerated focus per tick and allows pets to use more of their abilities instead of wasting all their focus on one ability.

 **Marksmanship**

The strongest vanilla spec for raiding. Doesn't need much fixing, but some changes could be made to make the spell power build more viable.

 **Improved Hunters mark**

You only ever put a maximum of 2 points in this talent when building a marksman hunter. It requires too many points and only gives a whopping 16 ranged attack power at 5/5. Changing this to a 2/2 talent that increases attack power by 15% per level will make it a more useful talent to buff other hunters' damage if you wanted to be a support hunter and not sacrifice a ton of damage for yourself.

 **Improved Arcane Shot**

This is a talent that spell power hunters usually take because arcane shot is the only single target ability that is usable in raids that scales off of spell power & serpent sting takes a debuff slot. Reducing the cooldown from 1 second at 5/5 to 2 or even 3 seconds will allow a spell power hunter to deal more single target damage.

 **Improved Scorpid Sting**

Quite possibly the most useless talent in the game. Scorpid sting can't even be used against NPCs because they have no stats & in PVP only reduces a targets hp by 200, so its not even worth using over serpent sting. This would be a really good opportunity to replace this useless talent with a 1/1 Lone Wolf type talent that increases your overall damage by 10% (physical and spells) when you dont have a pet out. Pets usually deal about 15% of a hunters damage if they can survive so this would give an alternative to hunters who don't want to deal with keeping a pet alive.

 **Survival**

Pretty much only used in PvP but also a decent personal damage spec in end game Naxx gear. The early tiers of the talent tree are really wonky in general and not worth taking, but are required to get Lightning Reflexes. Could have some changes to help melee weaving in PVE. There are a lot of 5/5 talents that could be useful if they required less points. Probably the spec in most need of love since it seems blizzard had no idea what direction this talent tree should've gone.

 **Slaying talents**

Why are these two seperate talents? Getting both of them requires 6 points for a minor increase in damage. Changing it to a single 5/5 talent that increases overall damage by 5% and crit damage by 5% would be a nice improvement. You could offset this damage increase by making it only increase the damage to the type of enemy you are tracking to give it more of a hunter flavor.

 **Entrapment**

Costs way too many talent points that people usually skip this useful ability to take improved wing clip. If it was reduced to 3 or even 2 points it would be more worthwhile to take.

 **Clever Traps**

This really isnt that good of a talent & usually isn't taken, but it could be decent if you merged it into Trap Mastery so that Trap mastery could be the go to talent for traps. This could open up a talent spot to make survival better at surviving since you'll be in melee more often. A 2/2 talent that reduces overall damage taken by 2% per point could be put in its place. Nothing huge, but it adds flavor to the "Survival" tree. It also makes sense in the positioning of the tree since the slots in the same row add % hp and a defensive CD.

 **Counterattack**

A completely useless ability to take. Does the same thing as improved wing clip, but requires you to parry an attack. A hunter barely has any parry to begin with, so this never even procs & it does minuscule damage. It's not even usable in a raid to deal extra damage if you wanted to play a melee hunter build. Could be replaced with a talent that gives raptor strike a chance to do an extra attack to help melee weaving or general melee hunters. This type of talent is also in the flavor of vanilla since there a lot of talents for other melee users that can proc extra attacks.

 **Wyvern Sting**

Why is this the 31 point talent for survival? Is it supposed to be the trap spec? Is it supposed to be the melee spec? This thing isn't even worth taking for PvP since if you get this, you can't get scatter shot. It has a 2 minute cooldown but you can't even use it in combat. Its dispellable by most healers and you can't even use it in dungeons or raids. This could be the place to make melee weaving really shine and give survival a unique play style and identity. I suggest a talent that improves melee damage when you use a ranged attack and ranged damage when you use a melee attack or just a really strong instant cast melee ability in general that you can use when you go in to weave or want to play a melee hunter.

Thanks for reading this wall of text & I hope some of these suggestions could be helpful to the dev team and maybe even added in future updates of the game!
