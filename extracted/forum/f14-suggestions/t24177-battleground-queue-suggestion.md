## Post 170139 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-05T15:24:00Z

This is an actual issue that is really annoying.
I don't think there is a way to solve the problem.

The smaller the team, the bigger the impact of comp/spec/gear. When I did Blood Ring it was always "great" being matched with a blue-geared healer when I play a healing-oriented hybrid myself. And with the other team having 2 Naxx-geared pure dps, the outcome is a foregone conclusion. Burst still reigns supreme and you can't heal your oponents to death. And as a kicker the next 3 matches are the exact same pairings of course...

So yeah, there's massive issues with completely randomized team comps. But how on earth would you begin to address that? Your spec is not a good indicator as most people run hybrid specs for PvP. Few people run a pure raid-heal spec. The impact of gear might be even bigger than your spec. Your spec gives you a couple of tools, but your gear is the difference between 1400 AP and 1400 healing power.
I wish there was a way to solve this, but I don't think there is. I'm not even talking about players gaming the system and manipulating the algorithm (which they will absolutely do). Even in an ideal world it's super hard to detect what a player will do in a BG.

My wish would be to try and shake up teams as much as possible. Keep a memory of your last 5 BGs and give your past team members lower weightings. That way you might still end up with one-sided comps, but at least you know next time will be different. And you won't be stuck on the mathematically losing team 5x in a row.
