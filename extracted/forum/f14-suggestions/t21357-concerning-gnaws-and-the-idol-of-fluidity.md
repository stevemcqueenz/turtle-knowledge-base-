# Concerning Gnaws and the Idol of Fluidity

## Post 148035 by Drfaustmd — 2025-08-24T15:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148035#p148035 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to start off by saying that I am extremely excited by the inclusion of the new druid idol [idol of fluidity] in the 1.18 patch, however the implementation of this idol has resulted in a terribly unfun and unsatisfying playstyle that is simple camping a rarespawn for hours on end to hope for a 25% for an item that has only small gameplay benefits but is incredibly fun to enjoy.

My recent experience in attempt to farm this idol has lead me to meet several other druids who have also spent hours simple floating in the sea hoping for a glimpse of a shark that may have the fun item they are looking to have, This experience has left us frustrated and disappointed at such a simple and fun item being time locked this way. Pfquest currently lists Gnaws respawn timer at 48 hours and the idols drop chance only 25%, At this rate even if only a handful of druids desire to farm this item they could be looking at weeks to months of floating in the sea for hours on end instead of engaging meaningfully with the rest of the game and community.

My suggestion to resolve this issue would be to reduce the spawn time of Gnaws, I believe even a spawn timer of 8-15 hours could do a lot to relieve some of the bottle neck currently happening on this farm. Alternatively the Idols drop rate could be increased to 100% so that Gnaws need to only be killed once be character for the item freeing up the mob for others to farm.

Thank you for your time and consideration of this issue.
