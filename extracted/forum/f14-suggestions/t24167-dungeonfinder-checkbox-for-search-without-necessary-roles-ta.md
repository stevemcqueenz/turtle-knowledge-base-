# Dungeonfinder checkbox for search without necessary roles/tank

## Post 170041 by Sp3rling — 2026-03-04T12:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170041#p170041 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I pledge for a checkbox in dungeon finder that states if you would be okay for the activity you are searching for if there is someone else available but a tank. Or a checkbox for not care about roles, but 1 person for healing. Most activities and dungeons don't necessarily need a tank if you are organized and strategic. And all damage dealers don't necessarily have to be damage dealers. Healers can also help with damage or play hybrid, so there could be more than 1 healer. A group could also hold more than 1 tank.    maintenance_turtle

In normal Classic I didn't care about roles in dungeons except for at least one person who did healing, it made dungeons way more interesting, varied and exciting, because you have to be more strategic, classes have to use their abilities instead of running through.

Of course I could still build manual groups for this in turtle wow, but with the dungeon finder as it is (and I like how it works, finding people without being teleported to the dungeon) people don't care about manually built groups and it could also be a good way to find people for dungeons when you don't need a tank for example.

With a checkbox stating if you care about the roles (except for 1 healer, because when nobody can heal it would not work) you could decide if you want to wait longer for a save group with 1 tank and 1 healer and 3 DDs or wait shorter for more intensive experience that could take longer or even fail. Some people like me like those exciting groups and challenges instead of getting through as fast as possible. Thats what I play for.

So if it would be possible to program such a checkbox so that it could happen that you are also be thrown into such a random group and not only the groups with fixed roles it would make me extremely happy!    smiling_turtle

In the beginning everyone could be hybrid except for classes that couldn't. In the end a dungeon is just an instanced elite area.
