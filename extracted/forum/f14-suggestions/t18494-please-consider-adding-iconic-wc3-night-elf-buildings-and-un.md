# Please consider adding iconic WC3 Night Elf buildings and units.

## Post 126193 by Ethenil (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-04-09T17:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126193#p126193 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Night Elves are my favorite race, both in WC3 and WoW (including retail, despite everything). However, one of the things that's always bothered me, going back to vanilla, is the lack (in some cases, very small presence) of some of their iconic units and buildings.

In terms of units, I refer to mountain giants, faerie dragons, dryads and keepers of the grove. Of course, some of these are occasionally found working alongside the Cenarion Circle, but there should be way more around night elf settlements! Furthermore, what's the last time you've seen a mountain giant fighting alongside night elves?

In terms of buildings, the Ancient of Wonders has *never* appeared in any version of WoW, while Ancients of Winds, as well as Trees of Life/Ages/Eternity are absent from vanilla and Turtle both. Please consider adding them to the game! Surely places such as Darnassus, Nighthaven and Nordaanar should have a bigass Tree of Eternity in the middle? Nighthaven specifically I feel is lacking in Ancients as well!

Thank you for your time.
