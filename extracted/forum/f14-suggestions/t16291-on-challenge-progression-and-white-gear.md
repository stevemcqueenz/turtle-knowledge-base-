# On Challenge Progression and White Gear

## Post 111091 by Goldenspatula — 2024-12-03T15:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111091#p111091 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello!

As you may know, TWoW is filled with exciting challenges that completely reimagine the leveling experience. At the time of this post, the following challenges exist (aside from HC which deserves its separate category):

 - Warmode
 - Vagrant's Endeavor
 - Slow & Steady
 - Traveling Craftmaster
 - Self-restraint (This supposed challenge needs some revamp or a reason to exist, it isn't "learnt" like the other challenges and is instead kept in your keyring, and there's no real reward to show you actually "completed" it.)
 - Warmode

The issue comes when combining several of these, specifically the topic of this post: **Vagrant's Endeavor** and **Traveling Craftmaster**, a combo that becomes absurd rather than challenging due to the complete lack of white/grey gear you can craft. As en example, Blacksmithing allows to craft white weapons up to level 135 (an two-handed axe) and no armor of this quality can be crafted aside from the one at the base level of the profession. For this reason, ***all professions (maybe even JC) should have white gear alternatives*** throughout the leveling experience. They'd be obviously worse versions of the items and also either cost a lot of materials or be set at a higher profession level so their crafts are not abused to grind the professions.

Leveling as a warrior with level 2 whites and 400 armor all the way from level 10 to 60 is a true headache, and this change wouldn't be too hard to implement.
