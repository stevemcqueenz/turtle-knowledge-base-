# Suggetion: Associating NPCs to their factions

## Post 88745 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-02-25T18:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88745#p88745 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While questing across Hillsbrad Foothills, I realized that a number of NPCs in certain locations aren't tagged with their usual respective factions, despite often being surrounded by NPCs from said faction. For consistency and flavor's sake, I suggest tagging them to those factions.

Current list of NPCs listed under the faction I believe they should be tagged with:

 **Stormwind:**
- Magistrate Burnside

- Clerk Horrace Whitesteed

- Blacksmith Verringtan

- Foreman Bonds

- Miner Hackett

- Citizen Wilkes

- Farmer Kalaba

- Farmer Ray

- Farmer Getz
 **Ironforge:**
- Captain Ironhill

- Tamra Stormpike

- Dun Garok Priest

- Dun Garok Rifleman

- Dun Garok Mountaineer

- Dun Garok Soldier
 **Syndicate:**
- Jailor Eston

- Jailor Marlgen

- Lord Aliden Perenolde

- Otto
 **Ravenholdt:**
- Ol' Biggins
 **Undercity:**
- Forsaken Courier*

- Forsaken Bodyguard*
 *Currently tagged with the "Horde" faction, but the "Undercity" faction would likely be more fitting.
