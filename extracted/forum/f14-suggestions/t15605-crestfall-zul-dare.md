# Crestfall & Zul'dare

## Post 106574 by Galendor (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-05T18:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106574#p106574 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle-WoW brings back many locations from the RTS into the canon, and today I would like to talk about two iconic places of the Second War, the islands of Crestfall and Zul'dare. Nothing had been heard about these places since Wc2, until the island expedition to Crestfall was introduced in BFA - which, in my opinion, unfairly reduced this place to a simple grindfest. On the other hand, Zul'dare remains a blank spot on the WoW map.
I tried to correct this injustice by presenting these islands as locations fitting to Vanilla WoW, with the respect to their history, as well as taking inspiration from some of the RPG stories of Kul Tiras. With that said, set sails!

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/ZMySh7C.jpeg)

 **Crestfall**.

Adventurers who have responded to the call of the Kul Tiras embassy in Stormwind travel to the port town of Drisburg on Crestfall, recently rebuilt by the Admiralty. Despite the fact that many years have passed since the Second War ended, the authority of Kul Tiras over the island is not nearly as strong as the locals would like.

Fort Tandred, which is supposed to serve as the main defence of Drisburg, is forced to deal with the evil spirits of the Lamenting Shore, which leaves the town vulnerable.

The fortifications of the Sentinel's Hill on north are under attack of the naga from the Zethreshi Frontlines. There are rumors that the naga are trying to set free a mad blood elf mage inrpisoned in Tol'Alkaz - but andventurers have yet to find out the truth about this prisoner and his deeds.

Monuments of dwarven work can also be found in the wilds of Crestfall. This is Thunderwings Rest, where Wildhammer warriors who fell in the battle for Crestfall are buried with their gryphons. This place is looked after by a hermit named Hazelbeard, who, it seems, has lived in the thicket too long and is now sure that he hears the voice of the trees.

To the south of his den lie the ruins of the Draggoshar keep, where, according to rumors, the relic of the orcish warlocks is kept. Whether this is true or not, recently an evil force has settled in these ruins, slowly poisoning the surrounding lands. Hazelbeard will need the help of brave souls to deal with the source of this corruption.

Finally, on the shallows in the southernmost part of the island lie the debris of Impeneturtle, an armored goblin war turtle that was shot down during the Second War. Kul Tirans have no use for it, but Venture Co. goblins are extremely interested in studying the technologies of other cartels.

 **Zul'dare**, to some extent, is a reflection of Crestfall - the shadow of the Second War also lies on these lands, and the power of the Admiralty is completely illusory.

New Corwell is inferior to Drisburg in everything - it is not even a town, but a working village for those who want to earn gold from oil, furs and iron. In addition, the local preacher is looking for volunteers to deal with the restless spirits of the Fallen Garrison in the north of the island and return the sacred relic of the Church.

But the true rulers of this island are the forest trolls, who settled here after Horde's defeat in the Second War. With the return of Zul'jin they will be ready to help the Horde gain a foothold in these lands in exchange for help with their new "neighbors".

The greedy hands of the Venture Co. have reached the riches of the island, and goblin platforms have already begun mining black gold. In addition, goblins have a strange interest in the remains of the red wyrm Orystraz, whose rotting flesh and blood has had a strange effect on the fauna of the island.

The Icefin Murlocs also fell victim of this corruption: they spread uncontrollably along the coast and are disturbing the trolls even more than usual. But who hired the Venture Co. to excavate the rest of Orystraz - and why?..

Finally, the ridge in the south of Zul'dare became the refuge of the rebellious Kul Tiran baron Falrevere and his pirate gang. Falrevere's flagship, the Indomitable, is preparing to set sail and, if left unchecked, will capture Drisburg... and then Kul Tiras.

If you read this text wall, feel free to write what do you think of the concept, lore and plot of these places!
