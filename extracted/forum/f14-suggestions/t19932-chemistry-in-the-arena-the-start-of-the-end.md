# Chemistry in the ARENA - the start of the END.

## Post 136144 by Nkarie — 2025-06-18T09:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136144#p136144 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good days Dear devs, and pvp players!

Like a pvp fan, i was excited when heared that arena actually anounced. Like a many pvp players on turtle I also played pvp almost on all private servers in different addons. And I see now that the developers can shoot themselves in the foot by allowing the use of chemistry in arena. There is no information that confirms or refutes this. but I decided to warn the developers about a possible critical error that can kill the pvp arena at the very beginning.

So, next i show reasons why its will be fail.

Reason one. Balance.

The 2x2 Arena statistics have always served the developers as an indicator of balance in pvp, and here we can also include the 1x1 arena, if it will ever be organized (I hope). In case of using potions in arenas, you can forget about adjusting the balance in PvP altogether. Imagine the eyes of an frost mage who faces a warrior with a GFPP prebuff and who uses FAP in combat, no invulnerability potion will not save him. How will you balance classes in pvp based on this?

Reason two. Pay to win and RMT.

Imagine that an arena player will have to drink 2 potions in a fight at a cost of 1.5-2 gold each, plus ZG bufs, food and other. One arena fight will cost 10-15g on middle and high rank. Such prices will either kill the arena (the most likely option) or destroy the server economy, where potion and herbs prices will become unbelievable.
I can already see the RMT specialists rubbing their hands.

You don't want your arena to turn into a pay to win garbage like Sirus.wow?

Reason three. Price and value.

This reason follows from the previous one. Let's say I want to get a weapon for arena that is equivalent to Tier3 Naxx. And let's say to reach a rating of 2200 where I can buy it, I need to play 200 arenas, as an average pvp player. How much will it cost me? 3000-4000 gold? Why do I even need this? I will get what I want in 1 month of playing Naxxramas and spending 300 gold in total on chemistry.

Reason four. Arena sniping and preparing.

here I will simply give an example of how one can dishonestly abuse the system. Imagine that the team is playing: Enhanced Shaman - Feral Druid VS Retri Pala - Surv Hunter. The Horde team saw oponents in any of many ways and  drinked Holy Protect before the fight and during the fight they will drink FAP and attack the Hunter. Paladin can't stop them with control, damage or even prayers :)
How u will balance classes then?

Reason five. Devaluation of high pvp rating in society.

Last on the list but first in importance. On all decent servers, official or private, such as Warmane, a high arena rank gives its owner respect and honor. If the server have p2w system like Sirus, people will just laugh at your rating of 2200-2400 and say that you bought yourself an advantage. The same thing will happen with the turtle if you allow the use of chemistry in the arena, for the reasons listed above in this post.

Conclusion.

I hope the turtle developers are smart enough not to shoot themselves in the foot by allowing the use of chemistry and some other imbalanced items in the arena. Please note that after the arena launches, PvP players from other servers will be come here. It is very important not to screw up...

Love u guys!
Best regards!
