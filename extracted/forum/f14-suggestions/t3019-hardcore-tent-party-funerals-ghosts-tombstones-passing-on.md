# Hardcore "tent-party" Funerals, Ghosts, Tombstones, Passing on

## Post 18635 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-05-15T15:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18635#p18635 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**1. After level 10, allow players to remain in the world after death as ghosts**

It would be nice if we could say goodbye etc as we get quite attached to our HC chars. There could be also dead-only RP abilities that could be used on living players, like "Spook" or "Eerie Presence" that would be displayed as a debuff.

 **2. Pass on from ghost to perma logged out by choice**

As ghosts, an option to speak to the Mysterious Stranger again, who has said when we accepted the quest that "*Should you die, your soul will forever be mine*", or perhaps the Spirit Healer to "*pass on / go beyond*" and only then get logged out for good without being able to remain as a ghost

 **3. Tombstones after death**

When we die in hardcore after level 10, either a tombstone would appear at the place of death for a period of time (hours or days), (with the name, race, class and level displayed if a living player clicks on it or hovers over it), or at the graveyards;
There are many graveyards in Azeroth with a handful of unnamed tombstones in each of them, they could be used as the tombstones of deceased hardcore characters, the nearest free tombstone in a graveyard could get assigned to us at the tome of death.
Or when we talk to the Mysterious Stranger/Spirit Healer, all the unused tombstones become selectable so as ghosts we choose one.

(The graveyard having real players buried in them sound interesting on one hand, on the other it could perhaps take away from the lore for the sake of MMO mechanics, which I'm not a big fan of, so perhaps the tombstones generated at the place of death, or new ones near the graveyards could be a better option)

 **4. Funerals similar to the social gathering under tents**

For the sake of immersion and roleplay, there could be something implemented to be able to have funerals as hardcore players, like a living player could collect the remains that would be at the place of death, and deliver them to an NPC that is a spiritual leader of the race of the deceased (an orc shaman, an undead shadow priest) at a graveyard, then the NPC would say a script with the name of the dead one replaced like in quest texts, perhaps other replaceable info like what caused the death or where, then a tombstone appears in the graveyard like in the previous suggestion, then a little feast would appear nearby. The time spent on the funeral would provide a minor buff or something like a tent xp boost to incetivize socializing. Of course the players can already organise such activities with a little bit of imagination but I think this would be a memorable activity and make the perma death feel more significant in the community.
