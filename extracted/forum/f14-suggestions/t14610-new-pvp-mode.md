# New PVP mode

## Post 99712 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-07-17T01:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99712#p99712 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Guys, how are you?

I thought of a PVP mode that can revitalize Wpvp and the PVP server.

The idea is as follows, to do a treasure hunt between the alliance and the horde in the open world. The search mechanics for these treasures work like the retail archeology skill, where you use an item that points the direction and approximate distance of the clues that lead to the treasure.

My initial idea is three clues before finally reaching the treasure and finishing the event. When starting the event, all players are automatically flagged until the end.

Treasure can be gold, plants, items, and, most importantly, honor.

This event could start every 6 hours on maps within the world, being announced in each capital where it will be, so players can plan in advance.
