# Securing Darnassus

## Post 113864 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-22T15:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113864#p113864 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Custom quest in Teldrassil named [Securing Darnassus](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40205) requires killing 10 Gnarlpine Avengers and 5 Gnarlpine Pathfinders in Gnarlpine Hold.

The problem is there are only 6 spawn points of Avengers in the entire area and maybe 20 of Pathfinder ones. And the area is often populated so people only hunt Avengers because they are rare.

Thus, I suggest swapping these 2 numbers so there are only 5 Avengers and 10 Pathfinders needed to kill. It would be great QoL change for everyone questing in Teldrassil
