## Post 35246 by Gantulga (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-02-19T19:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35246#p35246 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Orc: I like it as it could potentially give some cool counterplay options against classes rogue suffers with, like hunter, mage or druid. I wouldn't say it's op due to the niche application but Orc already has arguably the most overpowered PvP racial (Hardiness). Ideally Hardiness would be toned down to it's TBC version so this ability can get a pass.

Goblin: pretty much a meme considering the low failure chance (and usefulness) of Pickpocket and the fact that this buff has such a low uptime. I'd either increase the duration to something like 30s or rather give them a combat-related ability just like all other races.

Undead: ridiculously overpowered in PvP.

Troll: Nice, I like it and it could be even more interesting if poisons ever get buffed.

Human: I don't really see the point of this at all. Perhaps if it didn't break stealth then it could help with openers but even then it'd have to be 20 extra energy to actually allow some new combos like 5 CP Eviscerate into CS (Hemo build).

Dwarf: The effect should rather cancel pushback protection on the target so there's an actual application.

Gnome: This could be useful but in practice stealth levels are extra wonky and often times don't seem to do anything, especially against many of Turtle's custom mobs. If the bonus is high enough then it could be useful in PvP but the concept overlaps with Night Elf's racial (Shadowmeld).

Night Elf: Interesting effect but the bleeding effects rogue can apply are a total joke when it comes to damage and are only used as stealth-prevention. Both Garrote and Rupture need substantial buffs to be worth using. I also feel like this would fit another race better.
Also, Night Elf rogue already has a largely worthless racial: Shadowmeld. It doesn't really do anything for rogues other than giving a few stealth levels which are largely useless in practice. I think rogues should get a special Shadowmeld which gives you maximized stealth level for a short while and perhaps something like immunity to stealth-revealing effects like Flare of Faerie Fire.

High Elf: I'm not sure what's your idea here, is it a flat +% hit chance or does it let you ignore a percentage of enemy's avoidance? If it's the later then this could be an interesting racial in PvP. Imagine being able to hit through Deterrence or Retaliation. If it's just a +% hit chance bonus then I don't see the point of this at all.
Also, remember that High Elves, just like Night Elves, have a worthless innate racial (for rogues). That should be tackled before creating something new for them.

I don't see any of this happening though since the devs don't care about rogue.
