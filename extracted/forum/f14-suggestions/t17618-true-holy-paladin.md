I think it was a big mistake to make a paladin's holy talent tree like it is now, in v.1.17.2. It's completely unplayable for me. Not only did take away the paladin's opportunity to be a holy damager, but they also broke him as a healer.
I think a paladin doesn't have to be a strong healer, there are priests and druids for that, rather he should stand out for his defensive and preventative abilities (to prevent damage).
