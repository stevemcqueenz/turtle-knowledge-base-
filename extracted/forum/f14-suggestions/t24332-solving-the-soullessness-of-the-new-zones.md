# Solving the "soullessness" of the new zones

## Post 171133 by Sp3rling — 2026-03-17T23:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171133#p171133 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I thought about it for some time now and I can't exactly pinpoint it, but the new turtle wow zones feel kind of wrong to me... a bit liminal. And soulless. Don't get me wrong, I really like them.. basically. I love the quests (even though they really lack directions like original quests give you in the quest texts). Although original areas even if they are also often empty they feel less liminal and "wrong".

After said reflection I came to the thought that I seldom see NPCs walking around in the new zones. I think what could make it more lively would be NPCs walking around, a bit more scripts, a bit more life, maybe even from time to time people talking. Not everyone just standing around in one place. Traveling merchants, guards walking, people doing their daily work or something.
Ah and I think very often objects are too big or small in relation to the character. I know this is normal in WoW, but it's more intense or something with new added things and buildings.

But this is only my view of things. I'd be interested in what others think.

I can tell you exactly what one of the major issues with custom zones is and why they feel off - it's the shadows.

WoW does not have dynamic shadows, so Blizzard baked all the shadows into the terrain texture. Turtle doesn't do that and so all zones look really sterile and "unreal". It's just assets planted into a smooth uni-colored landscape that don't interact with the light in any way.

I'm sorry to those who were not aware - it's something you will never be able to unsee which will ruin all custom zones for you.  crying_turtle
 5 seconds into the trailer for Moonwhisper Coast I noticed the missing shadows. Here we go again...  dead_turtle_head

The real "issue", to use that word, is people expecting literally the world, for free, from a small group which is working real MIRACLES.

However, about the clutter: I agree that the default is not enough, but there is a very easy fix.
Make 2 macros with the text, respectively:
/console frilldensity 30
/console frilldensity 256
The setting at 256 maximizes the "clutter" (usually grass) and you'll have a luxuriant Azeroth. It's a real game changer. If it is too luxuriant for you, you can set it to anything between 30 and 256 instead.
The setting at 30 restores the default, which is sometimes convenient to see some quest items.
Of course, if you want, you can put drag the macros to a toolbar for fast switching.
Remember that sometimes the "custom" setting will be lost: just reapply the macro.
