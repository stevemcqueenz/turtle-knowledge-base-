# Raid ID: Drop Down List

## Post 147779 by Gamarcy — 2025-08-23T03:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147779#p147779 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there all,

I would like to suggest that, under the raid information tab in your raid popup, there should be a menu/boss list that pops up after you scroll over the ID. This list/menu should show what bosses are dead for that specific lockout ID. This change would allow for increased communication for PUGs and allow people to know if they have gotten saved to a bad lockout. Another aspect that this change would affect is SR sheets; not knowing what bosses are dead leads to poorly chosen SRs and frustration.

Thanks for your consideration!
