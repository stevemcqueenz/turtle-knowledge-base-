# Quillboar Illusion in the Donation Shop

## Post 133125 by Soranamane — 2025-05-26T22:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133125#p133125 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/pdyZd55B/Qboar-Barrens.png)

<Snort> Hello everyone !

Since harpies have recently been added to the list of Illusions available in the Donation Shop, I’d like to suggest the Quillboar as the next addition.

Just like the Gnolls — another iconic, tribal race with multiple distinct models — the Quillboar offer a similarly rich and varied visual identity. From the savage Armored Warrior and the mystical Female Caster, to the modest Waterseeker, each model brings something unique to the table.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/RVs2DXXG/quillboarbanner.png)

Each of these models also comes in multiple color variations — including an undead-themed version — which opens up even more possibilities for RP and character variety!

Interestingly enough, Tortusk, although appeared during Cataclysm, is seen mounted on a raptor. This suggests it’s possible to adapt the Quillboar model onto a mount without it looking too clunky.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/3wRp1gnM/Tortusk.png)

The Quillboar have rich lore and a long-standing presence in Azeroth. Their tribal culture, varied appearances, and thematic consistency make them a natural fit for the lands of Azeroth — feeling far more thematically appropriate than some of the oversized additions. Looking at you, big blue dragonkin.

Do not let Agamaggan’s name fade into myth.
Give us the chance to walk in the bristling footsteps of his followers !

Thank you for considering this idea !
