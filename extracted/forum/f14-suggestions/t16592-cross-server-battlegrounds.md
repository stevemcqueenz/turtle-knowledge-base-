# Cross server battlegrounds

## Post 113207 by Zocks — 2024-12-18T13:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113207#p113207 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

several reasons:

Increased Player Pool and Shorter Wait Times: Currently, with a smaller player base on each server, queues for battlegrounds can be long and matchmaking might not always be balanced. By combining players from both Tel'abim and Nord into a larger pool, the system could reduce wait times for battleground matches, making the experience more enjoyable for players who might otherwise be waiting for extended periods.

Improved Matchmaking Quality: With more players available, the system would have a larger pool to draw from when creating teams. This could result in better-matched teams, leading to more competitive and fun battles. It could also help to balance faction populations more evenly if there’s a disparity between Alliance and Horde players on each server.

Enhanced Community Engagement: Cross-server functionality could lead to more interaction between players from different communities. This could foster a sense of rivalry or camaraderie, which is often a key aspect of PvP content. Players from different servers could also form new friendships, potentially joining forces for future battles or other in-game activities.

Promotion of Active Participation: With a larger player base, players may feel more compelled to participate in battlegrounds more frequently, knowing that a bigger pool of opponents and allies will lead to more exciting, dynamic matches. This could boost activity in the battlegrounds, encouraging players to engage more regularly with the game’s PvP content.

Long-Term Server Health: For smaller servers like Tel'abim and Nord, population balance is often a concern. Cross-server battlegrounds could help maintain interest in PvP for players who might otherwise be discouraged by low participation on their individual servers. This could also contribute to the overall health and longevity of the server by ensuring that players have more content to engage with.

In summary, implementing Cross-Server Battlegrounds for Tel'abim and Nord would not only improve the quality of PvP encounters but also create a more vibrant, active, and engaging community, benefiting players across both servers.
