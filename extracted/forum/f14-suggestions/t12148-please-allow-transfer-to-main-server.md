## Post 80699 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-11T15:50:00Z

The problem with transfers is that it could break the isolated economies no matter in which direction we transfer character. If devs allow transfers, there must be some restrictions: transfered character can't own more than X gold in the bag and tradeable items in the bags/bank can't be transfered.

This is necessary because of possible 2 scenarios.

 1. Main realm -> PVP realm transfer
Without restrictions players would be allowed to grab a lot of gold and basically become the king of PVP realm AH due to inflation on main realm. Also, already existing farmed gold of gold sellers could get into the PVP realm and destruct its economy even more.

 2. PVP realm -> Main realm transfer
The other way around. Players could potentially buyout cheaper Black Lotuses, Arcane Crystals or other valuable items from PVP realm AH and later sell them much more expensive.
