# New Update for Trolls and Dwarfs!

## Post 21530 by Javik — 2022-08-08T21:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21530#p21530 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, i have good idea for new Update of Turtle Wow
Greetings friends Do not judge strictly, I do not know English well, and therefore I will use Google translation. I, as a fan of Warcraft, had a question regarding the processing of races / classes. I will touch on this topic regarding two races. As some people know, the lore of Warcraft is vast and multifaceted. I mean, why not add a druid class to dwarves and trolls? I'll explain why. Since I am well versed in the lore of the latter, I will tell you by their example. They are a savage race that believe in their deities, great spirits called Loa. They demand sacrifices, and in exchange endow the trolls with their power. So, why do trolls have to be druids? Consider a later lore, namely the plot of the liberation of the Echo Islands. There we, with the help of Loa Bwonsamdi, free the islands from Zalazane and find the troll Zen'tabra. She received knowledge from the Loa Raptors, packs and reincarnations, how to escape from the dark curse of Zalazane, by turning into animals. This is what she used to survive for many years. This is late lore, justifying how you can become a troll druid. Consider a variant of such a lore that is on the WOW Turtle. Trolls are featured not only as a race of the Horde, but also as antagonists. Jungle trolls are varied and dangerous. They pray to many deities who are both present now and will become known later. I do not think that the presence of the same Akunda or Gonk can be refuted. It is likely that trolls can turn to deities, animal spirits, and those, in turn, bestow their appearance on them so that they follow the path of their children and protect their tribe, Loa and the main qualities that encourage spirits.
A perfect example of those transformations are tigrons and slits. The first relate to trolls who have dedicated themselves to loa cats. They pray to Loa Panther Bethekk or Loa Tiger Shirvallah, the embodiment of justice, an honest test. They look like saberons, or like the same humanoid cat from Karazhan. This theme is then used by Blizzard with the Claws of Shirvallah skin. Sliths are the humanoid form of serpent trolls. They worship Hetiss, the loa of Poisons, snakes and meanness. Such became the chosen ones of Hetiss, Dambala or Sseratus. A perfect example of these creatures is the Gurubashi poison guardian, Venoxis.
Okay, you showed us the skins of the trolls that exist in classic Warcraft with signs of druidism, but how to implement them for the playable race? As suggestions, I've put together a few options for troll druids that developers can implement.
As a cat skin, I have several options: panther, tiger, spider. Let's consider the first two options. We all know that the Dark Spear was once part of the Gurubashi tribe, and, accordingly, can make an offering to the Loa of the Gurubashi pantheon. Panthers are the children of Bethekk, the loa responsible for shadows, stealth killing, and successful hunting. Tiger - Shirvallah, loa of hunting, testing of strength. A spider, why a spider? Everything is simple! This creature is mysterious to trolls and especially revered. Almost all trolls worship him. The Temple City of Shadra, the death dancer, loa of spiders, is located in the Hinterlands. Gurubashi make offerings to her, and Blackspear knows about her, using her power in the fight against the Wildhammer Dwarves. She is worshiped by the assassins (rogue) of the trolls.
The appearance of her fans in the game will perfectly show the opposite of their dwarves, which I will talk about later.
Consider the shape of a bear. what can we do with him? The Darkspear trolls know bears, but they don't pray. They are mostly worshiped by forest trolls such as Brokentusk, Amani and other tribes. Therefore, I want to focus on rather exotic options, but justified by the lore of both the Warcraft Turtle and the retail version world of Warcraft.
As an option for a bear, I will suggest a gorilla. Why gorilla? Everything is simple. This is a strong massive creature that appears in Stranglethorn Valley. The tribe of the bloody scalp prays to him. It is the spirit of the earth, the embodiment of strength, which is respected. You can justify this appearance by the fact that the darkspear trolls remember this spirit of their ancestral home, making offerings to it. Lesser Loa Spirits are more responsive to trolls and are more willing to give them more power. They are loyal and friendly to them and thus spread their cult.
It looks like a very local Loa, worshiped by only one tribe. Are there any other options? Yes, Thunder Lizard. These are distant relatives of the Kodo, powerful and great beasts that accumulate the power of electricity on their plates and shoot it at enemies. They are thick-skinned giants capable of taking on several enemies at once. Their deity is Akunda, loa of storms and new beginnings. They pray to him to atone for sins, choose a new path and be cleansed of crimes. Trolls believe that a storm can wash away all sorrows. Akunda heals, brings rain to the fields, and when the trolls are in trouble, they protect.
My third option will be the appearance of a crocolisk. Why crocolisk? These are dangerous and predatory creatures of Stranglethorn, the main danger of travelers passing by. They wait and then catch the prey and eat it. Their skin is just as thick, able to withstand a bullet. But, I want to say, it would be more logical to use crocolisks in the form of a cat. Their loa - Mahamba in the form of a crocolisk, Loa of water from the Bloodscalp trolls
As a traveling skin, I can offer the Raptor, the most familiar and understandable skin for everyone. Loa, patronizing raptors, packs and druidism in general - Gonk. The aquatic aspect is quite interesting. In the lore there is a Loa fish, namely, a loa shark - the Grail, which the sailor trolls pray for good luck. I have two options - piranha and shark.
As an owl skin, I made a controversial decision. It must be a humanoid creature capable of holding a staff and walking on two legs. For this, I chose Tigron and Slith. Their forms are owned by the priests of Loa, whom I described above, but, unlike the druids, they cannot return to their original form.
The wildhammer dwarves have never officially had druids, except for the Warcraft RPG, which could be canon, given that the Turtles are based on the lore of Warcraft 3 and Classic. And therefore, I suggest that the druid dwarves choose the same bear as the bear, leaving the appearance of the owl bear the same, since owl bears live on their land. And about the appearance of a cat, marching and water appearance is worth thinking about. What could it be? I have a few options. The wolf is a dangerous predatory animal that lives in packs. He is the orderly of the forest, eating carrion, and also hunting weak or sick animals. The night elves have shown that this form is bloodthirsty, dangerous and few have mastered it. Boar. Yes, boar, why not? The patron saint of Boars is Aggamagan. To him, druid dwarves could apply for a shape without knowing his name. There is something similar to the philosophy of the tauren, who glorify the spirits of animals, occasionally naming the names of the Wild Gods. By the same analogy, you can show a wild boar attacking from the shadows. They, in addition to acorns and everything else, can eat meat. Yes, yes, meat. These are omnivores. As a travel form, I can suggest Griffin. Why him? Because it is the totemic beast of the clan, noble and revered in their clan. He is respected, grown and used as his symbol. It is quite logical to use it for such cases. The Aquatic Form can be left the same, or the shark or any other fish can be put back in. I hope I have given you food for thought.
