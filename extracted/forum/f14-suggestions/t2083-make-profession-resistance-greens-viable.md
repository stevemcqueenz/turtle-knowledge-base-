# Make profession resistance greens viable

## Post 12640 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-09-26T23:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12640#p12640 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi again

I would like to suggest a buff to the green resistance items at high skill profession levels that are currently completely crap such as the tailoring examples below:
Wizardweave Leggings
Wizardweave Robe
Wizardweave Turban
Brightcloth Pants
Brightcloth Robe

I think they should have either intelligence, spell dmg or spirit. The point is to make them useful for the resistances while still being mediocre (instead of horrible and useless) in the core functions of either a mage, warlock or priest.

The reasons are two-fold:
 1. Reducing the number of completely useless items that serve no purpose in the game even though they actually require some mats other than pure runecloth (other than skill levelling and vendoring/disenchanting)
 2. Allowing some fun experimentation with high resistance gameplay but mediocre performance in other areas. I always felt it was a shame how little the resistance system is utilised in vanilla except for the very endgame and for tanks. This could change that a bit.
