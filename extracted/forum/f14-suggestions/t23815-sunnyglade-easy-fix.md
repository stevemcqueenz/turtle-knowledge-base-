# Sunnyglade Easy Fix

## Post 167048 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-01-31T13:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167048#p167048 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Sunnyglade Valley is being removed soon so I suggest giving it last kiss so people can enjoy it before it dies.
I suggest easy fix:

- Increase bonus honor awarded when game ends by 2x.

 *Sunnyglade honor sucks compared to other battlegrounds. Most people skip Sunnyglade because even on bonus day the honor is worse than WSG or AB.*

- Increase reputation gained when game ends by 3x.

 *Black Morass and Sunnyglade Valley have same reputation. Black Morass is superior to Sunnyglade when it comes to reputation farming. Increasing rep would give players alternative way to farm it.*

- Decrease number of players needed to start game to 4vs4.

 *Sunnyglade won't even start if it requires too many players to be in queue.*

- Decrease the limit of players from 20vs20 to 10vs10.

 *Revert to original state, changing it to 20vs20 was a mistake. This map is too small for 20vs20.*
