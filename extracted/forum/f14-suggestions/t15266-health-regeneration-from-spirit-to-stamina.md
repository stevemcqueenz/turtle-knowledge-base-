# Health regeneration from spirit to stamina

## Post 103985 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-20T14:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103985#p103985 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I'd consider moving the health regeneration to stamina while keeping mana regenration at spirit.
PVP-wise health regen can be useful for everyone and also in PVE it has an interesting niche, mostly for tanks.
(There is the troll racial and also there are specific items giving effects like '5% of your health regeneration continues normally in combat'.)
Just like meditation but for health. Problem is that no tank in his right mind would gear for spirit and with the spirit amount tanks usually have this effect basically does nothing.
Personally I'd move health regen to stamina which tanks have a lot of and would make it so that if you spec some items to have a bit of hp5 and decent stam then it would feel like always having a smaller HoT on you.
I mostly made this suggestion with the old troll racials in mind, new regeneration would make troll regen super OP if this change ever happens.

Think this could cause MAJOR issues in PvP as that would lead to the point where having more health means you heal that much faster during the breaks in combat.

This would be near impossible to balance, either the regeneration would be so low that it didn't matter or so high you were basically getting a HoT every time you dropped combat for a few seconds and would have to be tuned around a sweet spot for your life to be at.

Brings back memories of when people would try and drop combat for 3 seconds or so just to get a few ticks from food or drink, now throw in an absurdly high health regeneration to go with it for classes stacking stamina.
