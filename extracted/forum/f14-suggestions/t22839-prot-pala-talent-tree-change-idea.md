# Prot Pala Talent Tree Change Idea

## Post 159270 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-11-12T03:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159270#p159270 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey!

Gave my own version to the tree.
My goal was a small revamp ending at a comparable power-level, neither a nerf nor a buff.
There are things I wanted to weaken a bit and others I wanted to make more interesting.

<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/3_IBpNrbUL>
Highlighted the changes I made.

 **The nerfs:**
 *Improved Righteous Fury* talent only buffs the RF threat of single-target attacks and spells.
My goal is to weaken the consec glue effect a bit.

 *Righteous Defense* gives 10% damage mitigation for 5 seconds after a block and can proc once every 5 seconds. Benefiting from blocking feels a very prot pala thing and I wanted to make this effect conditional for a plate-wearing, high block-chance class.
As a comparison: warriors have a similar effect via stance but their damage and threat is hurt in the process.
Shamans have an arguably even better talent for this but they are less bulky (mail) and can't be crit immune until super lategame.

 **The buffs:**
 *Improved Hammer of Justice* receives a secondary effect if the target is already stunned or cannot be stunned (like a raid boss). In those cases it works as a disarm. Wanted to give prot pally an option to a disarm, which is limited in PVP but which can consistently help in PVE.

Blinding Shield is a 30y radius, 30 sec CD range pull option for palas. Didn't want to give them Captain America shield boomerang (tbh it sounds stupid). But a way to blind a far-away opponent with the light of their shield seems something a pala could and would do.
This is mostly for dungeons, would make them less dependent on engineering+body pulls.

 **The questionnable:**
Reckoning is not bad per se, free hits are nice to proc JoW for ex. My idea was to revamp it as a Zeal generator talent instead but I can easily revoke this change. I liked my idea in combination with the Righteos Strikes talent to give extra amount blocked to the pala which feels very in flavor.
Maybe the one proc per five seconds is too strict, would need testing.
