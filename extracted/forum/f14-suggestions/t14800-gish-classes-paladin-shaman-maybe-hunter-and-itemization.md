# Gish classes (Paladin, Shaman, maybe Hunter) and itemization

## Post 100802 by Adamant — 2024-09-06T15:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100802#p100802 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

i really like some of the changes been made to classes in Turtle WoW, specially Paladin that now can be viable, while not excelling, in every spec as of now.

But i'd like to propose a change in classes that could benefit from both AP items and SP items, mainly Paladin and Shaman, but we could also put Hunter to some extent and maybe even a few more (rogue comes to mind? i read that poisons scaled with spell power but i'm not sure about this)

i really like the idea of leaning into spell power to fuel damage on some skills, it is flavourful and opens a total different path for itemization instead of making Paladins and Shaman basically competing for the same items as warriors, hunters and rogues. this made those classes basically warrior and rogues lite in a sense. i don't have to dig hard to find paladins being compared to "Brownies".

So, why not leaning more into the concept without making them work only on a single specific weapon? (looking at you Scythe of the Harvest)

Beside incrementing spell power scaling on some abilities or give some in others i was thinking about items with Chance on Hit effect that would be REALLY cool if the had some type of scaling and improved proc per minute or even a static proc chance.

As of now, weapons of these types are just stat sticks and usually overshadowed by items that give just more raw stats than the cool chance on hit effects. Why don't give them a time to shine for some classes that may want to build at least some spell power?

this would give the incentive to some classes to search and roll for those weapons that aren't usually very useful otherwhise and they may even want to change weapons based on elements or specific effect (like Aspect of Seradane for AoE pulls, Sulfuras against non fire resistant enemies and so on).

i would really like to see ret or enhancement builds shine just a little without niche gimmicks, being a "Nightfall bot" or going the retail way by just "slap some stats and call it a day", i think it would be really flavourful and fix some of the problems both specs have in terms of mechanic and gear.
 ---
TL;DR
wouldn't it be nice for some gish specs to have to rely more on items that give hybrid stats instead of just stealing warrior and rogue stuff?
i would suggest to give Chance on Hit items their time to shine by giving them spell power scaling (not necessarily 100% like drain life have now) and improved proc per minute, or even giving them a static chance to proc.
