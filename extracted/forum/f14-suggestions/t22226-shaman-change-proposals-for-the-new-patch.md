# Shaman change proposals for the new patch

## Post 154271 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-03T20:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154271#p154271 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey!

These are my personal observations and opinions but I wanna share them with you.

 **In advance:**
I do not believe that the shaman class can ever be perfectly balanced, mostly because of WF totem. It generates a huge melee party utility while creating a disadvantage for the shaman (lack of agi totem for melees, lack of caster/healer group for others).
I think this aspect can't be solved: further nerfing WF, closing it up in a talent tree or adding a CD to it would negatively affect basically every melee in the game.
From the other hand, giving shamans an equally potent caster/healer totem which would justify the ele/resto raid placement would probably break the class. A versatility with THAT power level of buff would be probably too much.
So I consider the WF problem as something which can't be solved and I will regard shamans mostly as melee party members.

 **Resto:**
I think resto is in an excellent place.
Would propose two changes though:
 1. Current chain heal is still oppressively strong at raids compared to our other options. If you want resto shamans to use tactics and a variety of healing spells, chain heal needs to be pushed back a bit and other being pushed up a bit.
Tidal Surge and Undertow are not enough to push HW and LHW.
I'd personally advise Improved chain heal only reducing chain heal duration to 2,5 sec and Tidal Surge also giving a 0,5 sec chain heal cast buff on targets with Healing Way.
This would weaken resto since fast chain heal would be conditional.
If needed, HW and LHW push can continue a bit more for balance.

 **But my main take-away is:** super fast chain heal should be conditional (buff-dependent), HW, LHW need push for variety of heals.
 2. Improved Water Shield could be available earlier. Early restos struggle with mana and reaching 8TP in enh and Improved Water Shield earlier would be awesome for leveling.
There's another reason for this but I'll mention it under ele.

 **Ele:**
I think fire ele is a bit too much. It is not a planned alternative playstyle.
A backup spell for high nat rez mobs became super meta because of how many things buff it.
I'd slightly change Elemental Weapons FT ability to only buff instant spells and fire totems to remove the ele shenanigans (no buff for casted spells).

Regarding the **traditional ele**, the two biggest problems I see are raid party placement and mana.
I don't think raid party placement can be solved so regarding mana:
 1. If Improved Water Shield is accessible earlier in resto, and ele can grab the essentials in ele tree, go 8 to enh and get improved water shield (preferably with the spell crit and the threat talents) all at once, that would be huge.
That mana shield orb passive mana regen would be a blessing for them.

Enh:
I think enh DPS is almost perfectly fine. I exclude the WF totem problem again, focusing on two other things instead:
 1. How TWOW handles their threat.
 2. Mana regen.

 1. I mean keeping buffing the numbers in Calming Winds is a workaround and it indirectly helps. But it is way less elegant then addressing the problem itself. Reduce the threat reduction numbers if you want, but add shocks to calming winds and the Elemenatal Weapons WF weapon ability should be a threat reduction for the WF hits.
We are talking about 3 hits at once, after all. Those two changes would directly address the threat problem, instead of raising overall numbers all over the board.
 2. Enh performs smoothly with a pala giving JoW. This is however not a guarantee outside of raid content.
I don't think it is elegant making a class dependent on another to function properly. I'd love to see either a more effective water shield+LS mechanics or a shaman version of JoW which cannot stack with JoW on any target.
That would make the class function properly without needing an outside help to do so.
If this is too much at once, I think we could allow some nerfs to Element's Grace, since compared to other classes' talents, it gives a ridiculous amount of benefits for us for 5TP.

 **Strictly regarding tanks:**
 1. Water shield and earth shield way earlier. Early shammie tank has threat for ages but lacks mana and survivability. Earlier water shield and earth shield would help a lot.
 2. Mana as stated at enh DPS.
 3. A defensive CD. I heard that devs were reluctant to give shammies CD for PVP reasons and I understand the logic there. But there are two ways to circumvent the problem:
a) Giving the def CD via a bottom-tree enh talent. If a shaman goes at least 31 in enh during PVP, things like elemental fury or nature's swiftness wouldn't be reachable anymore. That kinda balances things out.
b) Giving a high CD, def CD totem. At PVP, you can fart at it and it' destroyed + it can't be used for 5+ (or 10+, 15+ whatever) minutes. The weakness I see here is that a defensive CD totem would mean no def CD for shaman tanks if raid boss is doing cleave or any form of aoe damage.

As a cool idea but maybe overdoing it:
Having tranquil air totem not work on you if you have rockbiter on would give shammies a salvation.
Helps if DPS is really eager or overgeared but might be too much.
Also: I'd consider nerfing earlier rockbiters. Shaman tank threat output at early levels is unfairly high.
