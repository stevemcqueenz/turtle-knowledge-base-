# Professions: improvement, changes and balance

## Post 846 by Sinrek (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2018-10-16T18:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=846#p846 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greeting my fellow tortoises!   happy_turtle

I'm sure as you level up and pick trade skills out there had a glimpse of what you can and can not achieve with those, so you picked something most suited for your goals and aimed for the best, however the best is not possible to achieve. Why? Simple, there's a huge gap in the professions which produce something like Blacksmithing, Tailoring and Leatherworking.

 - What kind of a gap,   unhappy_turtle_head   dude?
 - That gap which made people mindlessly grind for certain materials in great quantity to make one simple item which would get replaced in mere 5-7 levels.

However we have a greatest ever possible XP gain rate, so these gaps might increase as well! And to adress this problem I have a suggestion.

Well, I'm sure you do remember one certain mountaineer with a very well known name as Stormpike, don't you?

Thing is he made an ORDER for a certain SHIELD he'd like a certain craftsman to make for him with a very distinctive description and quality. And that quest got me thinking...

Does blacksmiths have such a skill to actually make shields? No. I don't take into consideration an end game right now, since we're on a turtle pace here, m'kay?   satisfied_turtle

So why not make some new recipes or skills from trainers which would make low and mid level crafting more viable and actually sustainable through those levels? There are tones of gaps between levels where you lack some decent gear.
Why not make it craftable? For example you could buy a simple buckler from a vendor add some copper and simple wood to it and perhaps a gem of some sort for a blacksmith could forge a shield for a low level shield users.

There could also be "new" recipes, plans and patterns for other professions which, I'm sure, we'd have a great time inventing together, since I'm not about to force my pov on others, I'd like to see us all either agree or otherwise reject the whole idea together. Everything is debatable.

Example?   wary_turtle_head

OK. Leather users lack a bunch of armor items from Leatherworking while they both use and ply their trade with it. Most of it comes for the gloves and boots. You don't get something decent unless mugh more higher levels and it's also green. How many blue items can you name which comes from Leatherworking at all? Blacksmithing?

I have a feeling Blizz had an idea to make professions so tight each of them might have made a whole set of armor for certain level range which would keep players going and let them at least close the most level gaps through leveling with professions and obtaining quest rewards approriate to their class, but looks like they decided to leave only those most significant so players wouldn't bother much with those sets for lower levels like you know we have Blackened Defias Set, Embrace of the Viper, Chain of the Scarlet Crusade sets… Yeah, they made it drop.

So, what I'm suggesting here is that we make up to these production professions and give them a chance to shine longer while we level up and even after we've leveled them up to 300.

Edit update:

Decided to take a shield as an example;

You can buy a Dull Heater Shield (161 armor, 5 block, 45 durability, requires level 5) from any armorer NPC out there and add to it, let's say 4 Light Leather, Tigerseye and 4 Simple Wood to make Tough Heater Shield (205 armor 8 block, 60 durability, +2 stamina or any other basic stat as a bonus, binds when equipped, requires level 5) as a blacksmith.
