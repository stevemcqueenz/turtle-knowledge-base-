# Holy Strike Normalization

## Post 70983 by Verdict — 2023-10-15T16:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70983#p70983 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello guys,

I'd like to share some theorycrafting with backup on Holy Strike & it's impact in PvP & PvE.

There is a big talk in the PvP side of this server about how strong the Ret burst is, contrary to what people believe, it's not the Holy oriented build that delivers the 3.5k+ Holy Strike crits but the Ret one.

Us Paladins have this thing that should not occur at all but it does called "Double Dip". It's when our Vengeance buff which grants 15% physical & holy damage provides both its portions to one ability. We have two attacks here that benefit from this.
 - Seal of Command
 - Holy Strike

Double dipping occurs from a fault in the formula:
 - Seal of Command should not take the current auto attack number seen in your "c" gear slots but it should use an entirely separate formula that doesn't care about anything but %weapon damage increase effect, ignoring %physical damage increases from the formula. Think of it like this, it takes only what the Weapon's Tooltip is, increased by %weapon dmg effects like two hand & one hand specializations & proceeds to go further. This is actually a bug, confirmed one.
 - Holy Strike is custom ability but in my eyes it should follow the same rules.

Should double dipping go? No. It shouldn't as that's the only reason Rets & Prot Paladins are clinging to hope. However a normalization needs to be discussed as it would resolve many many issues.

My suggestion:
 - I did write here in the past about this, so I will repeat myself. In TBC Crusader Strike is normalized to a 3.3 weapon speed modifier, what this means is that no matter what your weapon speed it, it will take the character sheet DPS value instead on priority & scale it up to 3.3 speed + All %physical increase effects.
 - Providing Holy Strike with such a cap would be really effective in reducing the insane bursts that happen both in PvP & PvE. 3.3 is in my eyes too low for a 10 second ability (considering Crusader Strike had a 6sec) but a 3.4/3.5 seems like an OK move. For one-handers usually you would see a 2.4 on many many abilities but it's in my best interest to request a 2.6 for Holy Strike as of now using a fast 1-hander is actually reducing threat & overall effectiveness due to the % increase effects.
The same thing can be done for Seal of Command but it can be tricky as it's tied directly to speed of the weapon to proc, adding a separate formula for how it scales might conflict.

Now you can say: Just remove Vengeance Double Dip then, fixed. That's true and would be an approach but it should not be done without adjustments to the paladins.

You can adjust the double dip damage loss or normalization nerf with spell power coefficient benefits to push more towards the true Hybrid way of how paladins were gearing, rather than going for nothing but AP + Crit, conflicting with other melee users.

Some time ago I did a calculation for another private platform which yield me that Double Dip Seal of Command is worth almost 400 SP on a pre-raid geared paladin.

Hope you enjoyed the read & take inspiration from this! :)
