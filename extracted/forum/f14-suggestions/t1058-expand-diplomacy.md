# Expand Diplomacy

## Post 6067 by Heroclastus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-05-17T11:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6067#p6067 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey,

Please read my thread 'war parties'  as this suggestion partly builds up on this.

Wouldn't it be a nice option as a diplomat to actually be able to work more with the other side? I know it was said that diplomacy was more like a filler since this server has low population.

Suggestion:

When in group with members of the opposite faction gain rep with corresponding race when doing quests or killing dungeon bosses,e.g. Human kills a dungeon boss with troll and orc in group-->orgrimmar and darkspear rep. Rep could be built up till neutral so after a considerable!!! amount of investment you could actually visit org as human, gain titles etc. This would paint a more realistic view of the world, after all the races allied too in wc3, so gaining trust could be an option within the lore. Furthermore, it encourages cross-faction play.

On the other hand, if killing other players of the opposite faction you lose rep of course.

Further thoughts could be established diplomats vouching for other players (extending the gained neutral status to them) via an item (e. g. Letter of recommendation, expires after some time, e. g. 1 week) for a considerable price, said item possibly restocks very slowly, e. g. once every weeks, so one player can only push one other player at a time.

Please discuss  turtle_in_love_head
