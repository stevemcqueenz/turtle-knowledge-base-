Hello everyone,

I hope you are having a good day and I would like to discus a simple fix to something that has plagued turtle wow for quite some time. The lack of pvp players on turtle wow, now wait before you jump to we dont want to work on pvp just hear me out. My fix has nothing to do with the balancing issues or map imbalances or even horde vs alliance, no my fix has more to do with the reason for wanting to pvp for some players who are currently not interested in PVP, the pve community. Now we could make a simple farm in any of the battle grounds with a simple vendor turn in, but instead of getting gold or silver you instead gain consumables from a grab bag, this would include a random selection of consumables that would be PVE and PVP related, this would also help pvp players who don't want to have to farm gold after grinding for 18 hours a day for rank 14. This is a huge quality-of-life change and will bring in more players to our community thus more people will spend money in the shop which increases the money towards the costs of running this amazing game and the growth of new content, this is a win-win and is so simple i cant fathom why we wouldnt consider adding this to a server that is classic + the + should mean more things that help everyone have fun without breaking the game.  Also you cant sell these consumables on the market you can only BOP.
