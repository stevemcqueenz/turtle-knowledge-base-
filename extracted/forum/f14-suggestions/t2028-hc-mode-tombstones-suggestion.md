# HC mode Tombstones suggestion

## Post 12115 by Suoll — 2021-09-10T00:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12115#p12115 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
I am currently enjoying Hardcore mode on my paladin Nightbreaker, and i have a blast playing it.
One thing came into my mind, and I tough I could share it and leave it as suggestion.
I was thinking that it would be really neat if you could add Tombstones on the places where HC players died, so it goes like this:

 - **HC characters lvl 30+ when they die they could leave Tombstone on that place.**
 *(It could even be higher lvl like 40+, so we can avoid spam of Tombstones around the world)*

 - **Tombstones can have some sort of message on it that dead players leave when they die.**
 *(It can be warning, goodbye message, explanation on how character died, last will etc. It will add a lot to immersion and RP element. Together with character name, lvl, race and class).*

 - **Tombstones doesn't have to last forever.**
 *(They can be time limited and last like 10-20-30 days just like mail in inbox, and after that they could disappear).*

 - **You can add option when other characters visit Tombstone that they can add or place special items to keep Tombstone stay on the world.**
 *(e.g. like adding special flowers on it, it can add to more days of that tombstone so it does not disappear).*

 - **You can even make cosmetic changes to Tombstone.**
 *(Just like different skins for race models that could be bought with tokens).*

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/small/42913.jpg)
 *One small example of Tombstone in the world.*

Hope you will see this message and think about it.
Thank you for your time and service.
