# Enhance shaman 31 pnt talent

## Post 55486 by Regaldandin — 2023-08-01T16:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55486#p55486 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Spirit Totems:**

 *Your totems are unleashed assuming there true elemental forms. They retain the same effects of the totem used to cast them with some exceptions listed below*

Fire totems: Summons a fire spirit to aid you
Health: 1200
Duration: 5minutes
attack speed: 2.0
Attacks deal fire damage equal to 14% of your spell power
If summoned by casting searing totem - attacks become ranged dealing 20% of your spell power
If summoned by casting fire nova totem - attacks have a 7% chance to cast fire nova totem damage on hit
if summoned by casting magma totem - attacks have a 30% chance to trigger magma totems damage in a 10 yard radius around the spirit.

Water totems: Summons a water spirit to aid you
Health: 1500
Duration 5minutes
attack speed: 2.5
Attacks deal frost damage equal to 5% of your spell power

Wind totems: Summons a wind spirit to aid you
Health: 900
Duration 5 minutes
attack speed: 1.0
Attacks deal physical damage equal to 20% of your attack power
If summoned by grounding totem: blocks a negative spell every 30 seconds

Earth totems: Summons an earth spirit to aid you
Health: 3000
Duration 5 minutes
attack speed: 4.0
Attacks deal 45% of your spell power in nature damage
If summoned by earthbind totem: each attack has a 33% chance to slow all enemies movement in 10 yards by 50%
If summoned by stoneclaw totem: attacks cause 4x threat and have a 30% chance to taunt the enemy for 3 seconds
