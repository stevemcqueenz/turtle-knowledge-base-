# Various WoW ideas

## Post 162931 by Sylbrosa — 2025-12-16T18:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162931#p162931 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I've played a lot of WoW over the years, and I've accumulated quite a few ideas.

So here are some of them. Feel free to comment, critique, or add your own ideas below.

 **Survival & World Interaction**

I really liked the Survival profession, and I think adding more world interaction can create interesting problems — and fun solutions.

 **Climate effects as buffs and debuffs**
- Rain → +10% Frost damage
- Heat → +10% Fire damage
- Darkness → -5% chance to hit
Consider the day/night cycle actually affecting visibility. Zones like Duskwood could be permanently dark, while nighttime in other zones reduces vision range.

Players would need to use torches or lanterns. This also opens up design space for utility trinkets — for example, lanterns usable by characters wielding two-handed weapons.

Consider adding sandstorms with strong debuffs on places like tanaris and silithus. Sandstorms in tanaris can cause damage or even kill players that have no cover when the sandstorm hits!

Other places like winterspring can be constantly ticking damage on a player that doesn't have winter protective clothes on, and even give them a movement speed reduction debuff.

Regions like duskwood or even east & west plaguelands could give the players some anoying desiese debuff too.

All of these are things players could avoid if they used resources from the survival profession.

 **Backpacks as equipment**

Backpacks could be equipped on your back instead of a cape:
- Increase bag capacity
- Lose cape stats as a trade-off
- Intended as adventuring gear, not raid gear
You could store tools (pickaxes, lanterns, profession items) in the backpack and swap equipment sets when heading into raids.

Different backpack types could support different professions, making exploration and preparation feel more meaningful.

 **Cities Can Be More Useful**

Currently, cities offer very little gameplay beyond the Auction House and the bank. Most players only interact with Stormwind or Orgrimmar, aside from early leveling.

We could give cities unique incentives without affecting raid balance, mainly through well-rested or utility bonuses:
- Stormwind / Orgrimmar → Faction diplomacy or social bonuses
- Ironforge → Bonuses to Mining and Blacksmithing training
- Undercity → Bonuses to Alchemy training
Add more profession-related vendors to these cities, selling useful items such as Mithril Ore or specific types of leather.

To keep this balanced and immersive:
- Vendors have limited stock
- Stock replenishes over time
- Vendors visually open or close depending on availability
This would encourage players to visit different cities regularly and make the world feel more alive.

City inns could be rentable by guilds:
- Guild pays a weekly fee
- Acts as a base of operations
- Faster well-rested gain while inside
- Guild bank access from the inn
This would add social identity and give cities a stronger sense of community.

 **Mounted Combat (Mostly for PvP)**
I know I'm asking for alot with this one   sad_turtle_head  . But I think it's just so cool.

Vanilla WoW never really explored mounted combat, likely due to time constraints and PvE balance concerns. However, Warcraft III featured mounted heroes like Arthas on Invincible, showing the concept can work in the Warcraft universe.

Mounted combat could be limited mainly to PvP:
- Mount-specific talents
- Hunters can use some ranged abilities while mounted.
- Warriors and Paladins harder to dismount.
- Being dismounted makes remounting difficult (Same out of combat rules)
- Mages are sitting ducks (little or no mounted skills avaiable)
- Rogues and warriors have dismount abilities
- druids don't use mounts
This could add depth to open-world PvP without heavily impacting PvE balance.

Thanks for reading — curious to hear your thoughts and ideas!
