# A new legendary shield: Wrath of the Three Hammers

## Post 111754 by forkyy — 2024-12-08T06:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111754#p111754 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone, I am a dedicated player from Asia, and I have some of my own ideas about a potential legendary shield that I'd like to share with you all.

First, I will temporarily name this shield "**Wrath of the Three Hammers**". (As many of you may have already guessed, this shield is related to the Dwarven wars and the Grim Batol storyline.)

 **Background story**: Since the Grim Batol raid will be designed, and the Dwarven storyline is incredibly interesting—such as the conflicts between the Bronzebeard Clan, Wildhammer Clan, and Dark Iron Clan—this long-standing enmity and warfare provide rich material that is worth adding to the game. It’s not just about creating a legendary shield; it’s about allowing players to dive deeper into the histories and stories of these three great clans.

 **Initial storyline concept**: I'm unsure whether Grim Batol will require a key like the Onyxia's Lair attunement, but if it does, after completing the raid unlocking quest, players who can wield the shield could find npc in a hidden, dark corner where they can accept an epic questline (similar to the Ahn'Qiraj opening event). This questline would be not only very long but also involve interactions with various NPCs from the three great clans, allowing players to further delve into the rich histories and stories of the Bronzebeard, Wildhammer, and Dark Iron clans.

 **Regarding the general shield creation method**: First, I envision this shield as something aimed at very strong raid groups and players who have enough gold, unlike the orange hammer or one-handed sword, which can be acquired through luck. This shield could be tied to three different questlines, each assigned by NPCs from the Bronzebeard Clan, Wildhammer Clan, and Dark Iron Clan—representing their completely different backgrounds. Players would need to complete these three questlines to obtain three shield fragments, symbolizing the Three Hammers War and the division of the three clans. Once the players collect the three fragments, they would then participate in a raid within the Grim Batol dungeon to obtain a fragment, similar to the orange staff fragments, to combine them into the shield.

And this is just part of it. The remaining part would require players to spend a significant amount of gold (I will address the issue of RMT below) to purchase materials from NPCs or directly pay for the fusion process.

Some questions you might ask：

 1
First, regarding the attribute design of the shield: With the upcoming Karazhan Tower version, most shield-bearing classes will already have around 75% armor damage reduction. Therefore, the design concept for this shield focuses on adding additional spell resistance and unique effects, rather than simply stacking more armor or health. For example, the shield could trigger area-of-effect damage (similar to the rare 57-level shield). Alternatively, it could have a low chance to activate one of three different buffs, each representing one of the three clans:

Dark Iron Clan: Triggers fire retaliation damage.
Wildhammer Clan: Increases magic resistance.
Bronzebeard Clan: Increases percentage health or provides physical damage reduction.

 2

Why increase the difficulty of acquisition: This shield is intended for higher-tier teams and individuals. Unlike the traditional orange one-handed sword and hammer, this shield will encompass the entire story of the Three Hammers War and the Grim Batol Dwarven narrative, making it far more epic and grand in scope. In contrast, the orange hammer and one-handed sword have very little lore, and their acquisition difficulty is purely luck-based.

(For example, I’ve seen firsthand in the Asian servers, standing next to the griffon master in Stormwind for just a few minutes, over 20 players with the orange one-handed sword flying past me on screen. Keep in mind, the Asian servers have only been open for one year!)

Therefore, greatly increasing the difficulty of acquisition will emphasize the rarity of this epic weapon and serve as a symbol of a powerful team. For example, players might need to spend 200,000 gold or materials to repair the shattered shield fragments, defeat a time-limited boss, or deal with an extremely low drop rate for fragments, among other challenges. These elements would make the shield a true mark of achievement, rewarding only the most dedicated and skilled players with its power and prestige.

 3

RMT：As I mentioned earlier, acquiring this shield would require a massive amount of gold, potentially even the help of an entire guild. This could lead to the possibility of RMT (Real Money Trading), but the philosophy of private servers, especially on the Asian servers, is to strictly prevent RMT. To obtain this shield, players would need to ensure their in-game actions are legitimate and free from any external transactions.

While RMT events can happen anywhere at any time, it’s important to note that these issues should not be blamed on the expensive cost of this shield itself. Instead, the responsibility lies in maintaining fair and clean gameplay, with the game management team enforcing strict measures to prevent RMT.

 4

Why should such a massive amount of gold or materials be required: This ties into a larger economic theory. In the 60-level version, there are very few ways to consume large amounts of gold. While the addition of a 5,000 gold mount in the private servers helps somewhat, it’s still a drop in the bucket. The economic systems between high-end players and casual players are completely polarized. This is reflected in the auction house, where expensive potions and consumables are practically nothing to high-end players, who buy without a second thought, while casual players struggle to even afford basic potions. It creates a vicious cycle.

The inflation of gold in the game needs to be counteracted by continuous gold sinks, which effectively burn off the excess gold circulating in the economy. This helps stabilize basic prices and creates a healthier economic environment, making the game more appealing to new players. By introducing high-value items like the shield, we can ensure that there’s a consistent gold sink, maintaining the balance between different player groups and encouraging more engagement with the in-game economy.

The Asian servers have been running for a year, and for example, with Hogger, I already have 150,000 gold on this server, and many players have even more than me. I can hardly imagine how much gold players on the European servers might have. The creation of this shield may have little impact on casual players, but it can serve as a gold sink for these in-game financial giants. It allows them to spend a portion of their vast gold reserves to obtain not only in-game glory but also contribute to the improvement of the game’s economy.

By introducing such high-value items, it provides an opportunity for these wealthier players to use their resources in a meaningful way, potentially helping to balance the economy and reduce inflation. This would encourage a more sustainable in-game financial ecosystem while also providing a way for these players to demonstrate their in-game prowess and achievements, which benefits the overall game dynamics.

 ------------------------
These are my thoughts on the T4 legendary shield. Due to time constraints, there are still many ideas I haven't been able to fully elaborate on, but I welcome any questions from the community.
