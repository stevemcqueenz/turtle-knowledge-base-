# Gardening Profession is a waste

## Post 131714 by Celeez — 2025-05-18T07:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131714#p131714 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This scenario just happened to me in game and it pointed out a major flaw in the entire gardening profession.

I was told by a community manager in the discord the efficient way to garden was to make 20 planter boxes and then plant in each of them. You can only hold 5 planter boxes at a time though, So you have to make 5 > Place the 5 boxes > repeat > then plant your plants in all 20.

BUT!!! other players can freely steal your planter boxes and put their plants in them rendering them useless for the actual person who made/placed them.

So someone could spend 50-100g on materials to start gardening and then get everything taken away from them basically.

Made a GM Ticket and the GM responded with "that's not something ill assist with" so they don't seem to care that its broken. Hopefully this is an over sight and needs to be fixed asap or its a complete wasted profession.
