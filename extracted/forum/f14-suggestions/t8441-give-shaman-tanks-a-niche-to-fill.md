# Give Shaman Tanks a niche to fill!

## Post 54396 by Rysramith — 2023-07-23T21:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54396#p54396 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've played a bunch of shaman tank in classic (tanked every dungeon available, MC, most of BWL, AQ20, ZG, parts of AQ40), and continued this trend in this server, and I'm now at level 58. I like the new armor talents that do give shamans a lot more mitigation, but there comes a problem, where your threat is usually single target, and sometimes outdone by other dps, which means spamming earth shocks, which drains your mana like no other. Suggestion: give shamans a talent right under Ancestral Guardian that gives them % more threat on all actions. Not more damage or attack speed, just pure threat. Give shamans enough threat gen where they are quite literally impossible to outthreat without using taunt, so they can fit into a niche in bosses where you want to burn down ASAP without having your dps outthreat the tank. Giving shaman tanks a special place where dps can go crazy would be a nice change and would be interesting too.
