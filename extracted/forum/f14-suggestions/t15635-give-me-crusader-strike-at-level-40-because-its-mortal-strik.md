Let's try to compare the new Crusader Strike with the old one.

Old CS -> lvl 10 - 12 physical damage - 25 mana cost - 1,5s cooldown  - Avg dps = 8
New CS -> lvl 8 - 70%weapon damage + 31 - 20 mana cost - 6s cooldown - Avg dps = 11% wd + 5.1

Weapons don't have a lot of damage in the early game so we could assume the damage is similar to what it was, maybe even a bit lower. The mana cost, however, is way smaller than it used to be. Coupled with the fact that paladin's mana sustain has overall improved with holy strike refunding its mana and Seal of wisdom available at lvl 18 and we end up with an ability that can now be used more frequently than before (when you had to risk to oom yourself incredibly fast).

To me that is the kicker. The ability, by itself, is not much different, damagewise, than it was before. But it is more bursty and costs too little mana for what is does, giving the player the feeling that they can kill things faster (which ends up being true).

If the devs feel this is a problem that has to be addressed, my suggestion would be to up the mana cost or reduce the base damage of the ability at early levels, keeping the ability as is in later ones.

Personally, it feels much better to have tools available and have to choose which one i should use instead of being limited by the game, requiring me to reach a higher level to use said tool, as was the case with seal of wisdom, which, as far as i could see, have had no negative feedback about.
