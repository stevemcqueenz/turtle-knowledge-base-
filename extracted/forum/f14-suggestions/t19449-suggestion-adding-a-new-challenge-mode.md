# Suggestion: Adding a New Challenge Mode

## Post 132266 by Danwith — 2025-05-21T03:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132266#p132266 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For a long time, my friends and I have enjoyed clearing 5-player leveling dungeons with just two people. This allows us to explore class synergies and utilize low-level consumables that are often overlooked. When forming a full party, elite enemies in dungeons become too weak and unchallenging. I truly cherish the experience of cautiously progressing through dungeons with a partner, which creates a thrilling and immersive journey.

I propose adding a "Dungeon Challenge Mode" that can be activated alongside Turtle Mode. In this mode:

 1.Players below level 60 are permanently bound to 1 (or 2) teammates and cannot form a full party.

 2.While leveling, players can only group with their bound teammate(s).

 3.Dungeon quests grant increased experience rewards.

 4.Completing a unique dungeon (killing all bosses) awards exclusive cosmetic appearances or transmog tokens. Note: These rewards become unavailable if the player's level exceeds the dungeon's level by N levels.

This mode would revive the danger and excitement of dungeons while avoiding interference with normal group play (since bound players cannot join others).

Thank you for considering this idea! I believe it would offer a fresh and rewarding way to revisit classic content. Looking forward to your response!

My friends and I have been doing this for a while! We’ve even attempted two-player clears of all dungeons up to Blackrock Depths on Classic, TBC, and WotLK expansions. Most of these challenges were done with just the two of us, and I absolutely love the thrill of strategizing against enemies and squeezing every drop of potential from gear and consumables.

For example, low-level treasures like the "25% Hit Reduction consumable" from the Elwynn Forest quests (e.g., the "Defias Tranquilizer" in Goldshire) became clutch tools to boost survivability in tight situations. I enjoy embracing the tension of navigating these perilous dungeons, and the best part is the lack of time pressure—even if we spend 5 hours clearing a low-level dungeon, the sense of accomplishment far outshines the quick 1-hour runs of max-level raids like AQ40.

Interesting idea, but I would propose two changes/additions.

 1. Don't bind soul to just 1 or 2 other players. Issue with this is that some people don't have a buddy to do this challenge with. In addition, for people like me that don't have much playtime, it can become frustrating for my teammate(s). I would suggest just limit group size to 2 (or 3) with other players within 2 lvls of you (e.g. lvl 20 and 22 can go together, 20 and 23 cannot). This also allows you to make different combo's in case some class combination is not getting the job done  It also keeps the spirit of interacting with the world and the people in it more alive.

 2. Beyond lvl 15 you get reduced xp from open world mob and no longer gain xp beyond lvl 20. The lvls 15 and 20 are chosen because of the lvl difference in RFC and DM such that horde and alliance have somewhat equal chances. In return, boss kills reward triple xp.

I think your suggestions are fantastic! The idea to restrict party levels is particularly clever, especially since the server has already implemented similar restrictions in Hardcore (HC) Mode. This approach feels more immersive than character-binding alone and genuinely encourages player interaction. A challenge mode like this could definitely turn strangers into friends – after all, clearing dungeons shorthanded is no easy feat!

Regarding the EXP adjustment: perhaps instead of removing all non-dungeon quest EXP, we could only disable EXP from quests unrelated to the dungeon’s storyline. This would motivate players to complete relevant pre-quests before attempting the dungeon.

By the way, I recently found a custom cosmetic ring (no stats) in Deadmines. After asking around, I learned it’s a reward for players who clear the dungeon at level 1 in special challenge modes. We could expand this concept! Adding unique rewards like "Bandage Efficiency trinkets" or similar utility items would breathe new life into low-level dungeons and fuel collectors' enthusiasm. Not everyone enjoys endgame raiding, after all.
