# WORLD BOSSES

## Post 168840 by Thebabbler — 2026-02-19T04:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168840#p168840 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Back towards the end of 2024 the devs promised changes to how world bosses worked; primarily a weekly loot lockout as well as making summonable world bosses.

As someone who started playing Turtle WoW on Ambershire I have experienced the latter change with the new world boss "Cla'ckora" and am looking forward to Ostarius in a few months. HOWEVER, the former never got implemented so the rest of the world bosses are still being camped by a tightly knit alliance of guilds, making it impossible for the rest of the community to enjoy part of the endgame experience.

I understand that the devs are already working very hard and have A LOT on their plate with the lawsuit, but is there any info as to why this simply never happened? Is it planned for sometime in the near future?

If these plans ever come to fruition, I think a weekly loot lock out on the non-summonable bosses alongside a massive increase in spawn rate would do WONDERS.
