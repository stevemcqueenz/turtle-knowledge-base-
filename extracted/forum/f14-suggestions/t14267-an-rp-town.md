# An RP Town

## Post 97285 by Babcock — 2024-06-19T02:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97285#p97285 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please consider adding an RP town/village/city, so that role-players can have a place to hang where it is assumed everyone in it is in character and open to walkups.

I figured if guild bases area added, that this could be within the realm of possibility. Besides, the bulk of updates seem to cater to PvE and then some to PvP but never enough focus to roleplaying.
I think this area is needed to promote cross-faction as well as open RP, walkups. Even if the roleplaying ends up somewhere else, it could serve as an area for staging an event. This would also help by limiting low level trolls or out of character toons from the area.

I would suggest an area like Dalaran, but since this wouldn't fit with the lore of the time period, perhaps a location nearby like in Silverpine Forest? Something could be nooked in the mountains there.

Thanks for reading.

I think more RP towns down the road would be a grand idea, especially if one blossoms.
For now, like the PvP playerbase, the roleplaying playerbase in Turtle WoW is a minority.
With roleplaying consisting of player created content, rather than the general PvE theme park design, is that something more centralized, to focus things on to see how it goes, would be a great start to test this idea.

On the bright side, people that participate in player created content can generate an infinite amount of hours of gameplay; however, they need the right environment to foster a culture sustainable for player created content.
Like with the world pvp enjoyers heading off to greener pastures of Tel'abim, I think a lot of them were given more to bite off than they could chew when a focused world pvp zone in the main server may have better focused that itch.

If we divide Azeroth into North Kalimdor, South Kalimdor, North Eastern Kingdoms and South Eastern Kingdoms we could more accurately pick a best location for each zone based on appearance and surrounding lore or possible theme.
North Kalimdor: I agree. Mt. Hyjal makes sense due to lore significance. Even nearby Moonglade.
South Kalimdor: I agree. The Barrens makes sense blending goblins, pirates, horde and alliance influence.
Northern Eastern Kingdoms: ?
Southern Eastern Kingdoms: I'd say Duskwood or near Karazhan for Halloween/Dark aesthetic, but SW Park and Blue Recluse already has enough RP that this area may not need any...

I think the Northern Eastern Kingdoms should be the focus for the first RP town regardless because of its importance in Warcraft 3, it seems there is a large amount of guilds with the focus on the region such so that there is a server Northern Kingdoms trying to host events between the different guilds up there.
I made the suggestion of Silverpine earlier because it seems like Lordamere Lake is a center piece between so many lore significant regions of Warcraft that something near there would work as a good staging point.
