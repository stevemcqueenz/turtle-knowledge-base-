# Master Strike talent ideas

## Post 144893 by Templar85 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-12T17:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144893#p144893 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Master strike talent is interesting but some of the effects are too similar or very niche.

Mace:
 3 seconds disorient.                       // That sounds good but what if the target affected by Rend? //
New idea: Shield Shatter: Shattering the target shield for 4 seconds. All attack will counts as the target not have a shield or shield wall effect.

Sword:
Disarm the target for 3 seconds.
New idea: If the target have a 2 handed weapon on it's next attack you will automatically counterattack 50% of the damage you suffered. Counterattack against lvl 63 bosses will causes 100% of your weapon damage. Does not reset swing timer.

Axe:
Immobilize the target for 4 seconds.    //Sound good but can add a flavor to it.//
New idea: Crushing your opponent feet with you axe. For 4 seconds each second it moves speed reduced more. Starting at -40% to 60% 80% 100% reduced movement speed. IF the target moved whole duration leave a bleed effect on it.

Polearm:
Deals an additional 100% weapon damage if the target is mounted then dismounts them.   //Extreme niche, unrealistic to happen//
New idea: If the target speed is higher then normal walking speed by +40% and in range of 25 yards then throwing your polearm at them. Causing guaranteed crit damage and dismount them and/or reduce their speed to walking speed for 3 seconds.

Fist weapon:
Knock the target down for 2 seconds.     //Not bad but very similar to other effects.//
New idea: Knock the target back for 10yards from their current location and prevent them to charge, blink or any relocating ability for 4 seconds.

Staff:
Increases your parry chance by 25%       //Not too bad but can improve a little bit.//
New idea:  Increases your parry chance by 25% Each successful parry increases your next auto attack and ability damage by X%

Dagger:
Silence the target for 3 seconds.
New idea: Silence the target for 3 seconds. After it for the next  6 seconds trying to cast a spell taking X amount of bleed damage for each spell cast.
