# Give druids some kind of 10sec resurrection (Revive)

## Post 30319 by Saxxon — 2022-12-07T17:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30319#p30319 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I love druids. But when we are looking for people to bring to our group, and there are priest or paladin options, we go with the options that can resurrect. It's nothing personal, but people in pugs get reckless, they die, it's a fact of the game. Anytime there's someone dead with Rebirth on cooldown, instantly any run is immediately slowed down or paused entirely in the case that it was the tank that died.

This is especially nasty if you have someone die at the end of Stratholme where the gate is closed, and rebirth is on cooldown. Well, that party member can't be resurrected, so they can't participate in the Baron fight. The options are: everybody dies and everybody runs back, or try it anyway. With either option, everyone wished the leader would have chosen a priest or paladin instead.

So I suggest we turn that around.

What about lore implications? This isn't serious at all- druids can be healers, and healers in-universe are known to restore life to many things, even other druid NPCs. In fact, in Wrath, druid players even gained access to the Revive spell.

How about class balance? I don't believe that introducing resurrection to the druid class will cause any imbalance at all. Typical resurrection is a 10 second cast that is not usable in combat. It won't tip the scales in battlegrounds, where resurrection is seldom used anyway (and people are coming back to life every 30 seconds regardless.) It won't tip the scales in world PvP because usually someone casting resurrection is very obvious and easy to target. In fact, they will be more valuable for raiding as well, since they can then accelerate recovery from a partial wipe faster.

I don't see much of a reason any healer should be barred from having the standard 10sec resurrection. And indeed, druids are the only class without one. This helps everybody.
