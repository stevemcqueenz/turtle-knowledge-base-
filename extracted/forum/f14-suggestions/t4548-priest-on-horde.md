> Trolls were the first shadow preist. they called them selves Witch Doctor but non the less they were the first shadow spell casters on the planet of azeroth.

Witch-doctor and Shadow Priest Trolls are two different things. The former is casting magic through rituals, mixing stuff like mojo, blood magic (sorta) via Juju (aka life-force) and other to do many things like raise the dead, curse, hex, heal and so on.

A Shadow Priest Troll, like other Troll Priests, just channels their power from their Loa, like other races do with Elune/The Light/Shadow...

A witch-doctor can summon and bind loa but not like a Priest or a shaman, they are more mages, just very different from the classic flashy one.

A Shaman can be both used to RP a witch-doctor (Restoration via Totems) or a Priest that channels power from the elemental Loa of the Darkspear BUT in this case, it's not a shaman like for the Orcs and Taurens.
