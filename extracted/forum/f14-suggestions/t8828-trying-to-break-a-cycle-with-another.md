# Trying to break a cycle with another

## Post 56939 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-12T08:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56939#p56939 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know that the devs are aware of this, and because of this i want to give some suggestions about the subject.

This game, i mean classic, started as a RPG but after a few expansions Blizzard made this game into a casino roulette of loot with an exceeding amount of collectables. This was one of the main reasons form my departure from the retail experience, the store in game and the wow token never affected me since i had no interest in all that.

The loop that I mentioned was exactly about this loot cycle of better gear, always focusing in more stats, more ATP, SP, MP5 etc.

But how to break this cycle? I mean, what else the player could seek after to keep building himself? For that question the obviousanswer that everyone knows is housing, but this is alike a collectable journey, so isn't exactly my proposal to deviate from the retail road.

The characters can work towards improving their skills, but those skills couldn't be things as tedious as fishing (i mean, don't get me wrong, fishing, cooking and fist aid are useful skills but they are boring to grind), maybe adding language skills could be every interesting for RP players, but this wont affect the entire player base. But adding more skills (i mean straight from the D&D players book) could be something that could be an intermediate solution, for that can be very tricky to add in the current game client.

Also, there are some books or scrolls across the game that can improve the character spells, but those also are just another way to build more damage and in the and was alike the loot cycle.

But what else a character could improve you say? I guess i have a very small answer to add. And  in this exactly timing may be controversial.

Fortifications, or more exactly, maintaining a fortification with your guild. But aren't this idea alike housing? In some points it is, but overall housing can be something that one do it alone, and are not intended to be attacked. Otherwise, fortifications can be the target of misfortune. With their own npcs from a already established range of selections.

Now to tackle the next issue that someone may think, but there's is not space in the world to put that many fortifications, and if it would, the game would have to change its name to WoF. So id say, leave those fortifications behind a black portal. In a new map, where those fortifications can be subject of attack from other guilds and random mobs. These ideas can be aligned as (alike) a BIG MOBA game. But where things does not happen so fast. And, most important, player actions, like an attack, have a know window of time to the other guild to defend or act upon, like a day.

Where beside the needs that the fortification may need in its own map, it could do assigned missions for 5 men dungens for critical resourses, and things alike to keep the players roaming the map.

I don't know, I'm just thinking high. But also, gear in this scenario could scale off charts so every match, even if you are max equipped would not matter for this event. And every match the gear resets.

What do you guys think?
