# The talent Spiritual Drain could work better

## Post 138143 by Alexmmusic — 2025-07-05T11:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138143#p138143 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think the activation of this talent might not be working properly, as it only seems to trigger when delivering the final blow to an enemy—making it a rather unversatile talent, and especially useless for healing priests.

Maybe it should activate whenever you're involved in a fight with a mob—even if you're just healing a group?

## Post 138217 by Fornow (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-06T07:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138217#p138217 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yeah unfortunately it's only really useful when solo or duel leveling. In a dungeon group you can try to snipe kills with mindblast and that new instant cast finisher.

However if it were always activated no matter who gets the kill in the group it would be both too strong with its current values and more bland as you don't need to do anything specific anymore.

I think a fair change would be to change the killing blow formula a bit so that you still get a partial effect depending on how much you contributed to the kill (the last 10-15% of life maybe).
