Shaman suffers from no hard control skills a lot, and i remember there is a skill in War3. so here is my suggestion.
![Image](http://classic.battle.net/war3/images/orc/spells/stasistrap.gif)Stasis Trap Ward(Totem)
Summons an invisible and immovable ward(Totem,air) that stuns enemy units around it. The trap activates when an enemy unit approaches. The trap lasts 20 seconds. The stun lasts 5 seconds(any kind of damage will remove this effect).
So any ideas for this?
