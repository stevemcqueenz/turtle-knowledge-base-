# Searing Light is way too strong - Gamebreaking.

## Post 106527 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-05T13:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106527#p106527 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The new talent that disc priests have, (pretty much ripped from wotlk, but alot stronger) is way too strong, it needs dialing back in someway.

Heres the Turtle Version:
[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/WVQffcS/OP.png)](https://imgbb.com/)

Heres the Wotlk Version:
[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/sRnX1tz/notOP.png)](https://imgbb.com/)

So lets compare the two, the Turtle version has NO cooldown, meaning you can just keep chaining crits until your targets dead (Now can you see why this is a problem in PvP) The Wotlk version couldnt crit so it could never be a chain.
On fights like Loatheb, you have pretty much 100% crit, so now you can just spam Instant smites the whole fight outdpsing the whole raid and never ooming, its just absurd.

Lets take a look at this damage meter
[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/cTHwgYz/Busted.png)](https://ibb.co/q1fCyJb)

So Priests are now vastly outdpsing BIS geared warriors like its nothing... but the problems dont just end in PvE

I Also saw a Warrior with 6700 HP in WSG last night get chastised from 30 yards away on his mount, Holy Fired, then Chain Smite crit down in 3-4 seconds, before he could even react.

Now I dont wish to insult the devs, but did anyone actually sit down and think, yeah I think this might be abit too strong?
It seems like it was just pushed in without taking any of these above situations into thought.

How to fix:

Atleast ONE limitation needs to be added, either make it uncapable of a crit like the WoTLK version or add an internal cooldown of 6 seconds.
