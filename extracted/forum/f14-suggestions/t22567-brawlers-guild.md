I do believe a Brawler's Guild in Turtle WoW could work, it just would be a little different.

Brawler's Guild in Mists of Pandaria worked because your character was strong enough to solo elites. The classes had enough abilities to be able to fight all the bosses that required certain mechanics, like the ones that required you to fight at a distance. Some bosses might have to be adjusted based on class, or possibly some bosses exclusive to a class.

The rank should operate the same, where defeating the next boss would give reputation, as well as a small bag of gold. It would be nice if it was some sort of minor gold farm for those who don't raid. The rewards based of rank should only be cosmetic, with unique weapon transmogs, so it does not devalue other items.

I personally would enjoy the early fights possible at a fresh level 60, again for those who don't raid. The higher rank battles would need the max tier gear. Lower level battles could work too, starting at level 20 or 30, and rising in level.

The Brawler's Guild, I imagine, will be in an instance so other players won't get in the way. I enjoy watching other players battles, but teleporting the players who are not fighting out of the arena might not be possible with the Vanilla client. The entire event might have to wait until UE5 to work properly.

I hope the Turtle Team considers some form of Brawler's Guild, even if it is small. I believe a certain amount of people will enjoy random battles here and there. It would also be an opportunity for them to create some interesting mechanics!  smiling_turtle_head
