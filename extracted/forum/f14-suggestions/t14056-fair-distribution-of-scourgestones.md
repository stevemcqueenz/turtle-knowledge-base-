# Fair distribution of scourgestones

## Post 95757 by Jessienyan — 2024-05-27T00:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95757#p95757 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Scourgestones that are lootable when using the Argent Dawn trinket can be looted by anyone in a group. They do not use any loot rules, they are first come first served. This means that melee get the overwhelming majority of scourgestones that drop.

I'm curious to hear what ideas people have that would enable ranged to have a fair chance at collecting scourgestones.
