# Kul Tiran Provisions is one of the worst quests I've ever done

## Post 54749 by Peasantunit — 2023-07-26T13:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54749#p54749 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm going to try my best not to be salty in here, but I'm writing this after finally finishing this quest chain and getting the trinket. I'm probably going to fail.

I'm not even going elaborate about the first painfully time consuming and boring part of collecting 5 Kodo meat, as I just bought them in the AH. God bless whoever advised me to do that. I'm also not going to elaborate on how infuriating it is having to swim out of that island twice with no actual method of transportation. What about waiting for the NPC to respawn? Not elaborating on that either, but I will ask: What happens if a horde player is camping that room besides me, only to attack the NPC as soon as it respawns, because it is ALWAYS dead, once again not letting me get the quest? Do I really have to sit there and witness it as many times as the horde players want? You can't get quests from NPCs that are in combat.

What I will elaborate on, is that on the last step of the quest, where you have to get to a ship in Menethil Harbor and loot a container inside. This container disappears after being looted and takes, apparentely, five whole minutes to respawn. If somebody just got it, and you know they did because players won't stop making HC characters, getting this trinket, dying and repeating the process... then that's ~5 minutes to wait. Five whole minutes of sitting around, waiting, doing nothing, just waiting for it.

I just got out of that ship with my crate, but there were 5 of us in there. I just happen to be the second one lucky enough to have been clicking on that spot faster than the others, or perhaps my connection with the server was faster than theirs. I got there, waited nearly five minutes for it to respawn, lost the clicking game, waited another 5 minutes, won it and left. Ten, 10 minutes wasted. But here's my point: I said there were 5 of us. That means one of these people is waiting 25 minutes. Another is waiting 20.

Waiting for boats, waiting for flight paths, waiting for NPC respawns, waiting for quest item respawns, waiting for my character to auto-run from one place to another, waiting for my character to auto-swin from one place to another, getting killed by something because this quest is so bad my brain has ceased to function and forgot about the high level monsters of Dustswallow Marsh, waiting for my character to auto-corpse-walk to my corpse so it can resume its auto-getting-to-somewhere...

This is one of the worst quests I have ever done in my lifetime, and if I ever make a new character, I will be sure to never step foot in Anchor's Edge again. 10+ Attack power is not worth **nearly an hour of absolutely nothing, 100% footwork, walking around in complete boredom**. This entire questline feels like it's satirizing the gruesome, god-awful quest design from 20 years ago. I was supposed to get this trinket and go to bed happy with one final addition to my progress for the day, but instead I'm going to bed pissed off for having wasted 1 hour doing ridiculous amounts of nothing.
