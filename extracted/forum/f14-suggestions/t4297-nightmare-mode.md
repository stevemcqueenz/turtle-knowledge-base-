# Nightmare mode

## Post 25590 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-09-20T12:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25590#p25590 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all

I started this server just to be a casual, but something hooked me, the hardcore experience, i liked very much of this challange that i am myslef almost completing (in this moment a 54 priest). For this i must praize all effort and creativity of this server.

But i aways wanted something from classic, that i know i could never get from the blizzard servers, a rotation in dificulty like the diablo games. So by now most of you already know what i am about to suggest, a way to start characters in a mode thats harder.

Before anything, this mode cant be imposed to players, they must choose to create a character in the nightmare mode. I was thinking something like after you kill Ragnaros or another endgame boss you recive a quest that leads you to a quartermaster where you can buy an iten. This iten can be send only to alts and usable only on level 1 characters.

This level one characters that use this iten become nightmare mode character, by this they cant trade, mail or party with normal mode characters, only with nightmare ones. Besides that they have their own AH (or no AH at all), but they cant share the same AH with the normal mode players.

And for the nightmare mode, players face everything 30% to 50% harder, and the xp is 30% to 50% lower. And they can only trade and party with another NMC (nightmare mode character) with the 5 level gap. And the NMC are still in this mode even when they reach level 60, its permanent.

And each time you retake the nightmare mode, you can start in a harder difficulty. For example, after you finishing in the nightmare mode (killing Ragnaros again) you can start the hell mode, where everything is 50 %to 100% harder. And you can only party and raid with other hellmode characters.

Sure that in these modes you dont get XP from grey and green mobs.

Sure if the HC challange worked in parallel would be the perfect.

I hope this suggestion be well accepted between all the community, and i hope a great day for all.

thanks for your time.

Schalla   happy_turtle_head
