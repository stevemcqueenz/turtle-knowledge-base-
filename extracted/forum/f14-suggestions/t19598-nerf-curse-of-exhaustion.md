# NERF CURSE OF EXHAUSTION

## Post 133365 by ForumGentleman (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-28T20:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133365#p133365 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Guys, It was fun while it lasted but let's be serious

Curse of exhaustion right now is broken for the following reasons:
 - Available on third row of talent which makes it usable easily by all spec
 - Boosted by a talent on 2nd row that you will take anyway, bringing the slow from 30% to 50% for "free"
 - No Diminishing Return, big range; no CD; low mana cost; usable on multiple targets

Suggestions to balance it are plenty. Harmstring and Wing clip got a short CD but we dont have to go that route

Here are the options:
 - move it further down the talent talent
 - add a 5-7sec cd
 - either remove or move way down the talent tree the talent that increases from 30% to 50% the slow effect (so SL lock cannot get it)
 - add Diminishing return to it
