I have designed and began planning a fun, lore friendly, engaging and rewarding quest hub. I would appreciate community feedback and developer feedback. I have hidden the quest objectives, dialogue and rewards with spoilers to condense and keep the "Mysteries of Azeroth" ethos alive.

ZONE: Gilijim's Isle
ALLIANCE / HORDE 52-54
"Rag Tag Camp"

A small encampment filling the zone where the surviving goblin from the zepplin crash is. This will add a neutral hub and fill the area with great quests.

Visualize in the jungle and lagoons of Gilijim's Isle a small cosy encampment, nestled around a single bright burning campfire is a small selection of tents, crates, benches hosting 4 NPC's. Various doodads fill the site creating a survival camp feel, fish nets, hanging goods, barrels. Vines and palms hang over the site, you are definetely in the wilderness!
