# Revert the recent addition of immunities to poison

## Post 79549 by Gantulga (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-01T17:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79549#p79549 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know it is technically a bug fix but all it does is further hurt a class that's in dire need of attention. Rogue is not exactly in a good spot on this server and changes like that just sprinkle more salt into the wound.

Overnight, all elemental and mechanical mobs became immune to poison. Not only this hurts the rogue's damage output (even if not by much, but every bit counts) it also forces them to spend more gold as now you have to use sharpening stones on your offhand if you're doing MC for example.
It also invalidates the Agitating Poison in many scenarios, making it impossible to hold aggro.

The bug that we had going on for years was beneficial as it helped a neglected class even if a little. It also played right into the server's vision of enabling more build diversity and such.

"Immersion" wise this change isn't consistent because while you can't poison elementals and machines, you still can somehow inflict pain and agony upon them (SWP, CoA), slow their non-existent tongues (CoT) or send a swarm of angry insect to somehow damage the metal/rocks (Insect Swarm). This is just an arbitrary gameplay limitation that affects a specific class more than others for no good reason.

Indeed this change nerfs Rogue's poisons slightly especially in Molten Core if we are talking about raiding. But not really that much in other raid instances: I've checked the database for all elemental and mechanical enemies and here is all I've found:

Elementals are present in:
 -Molten Core (most of the mobs)
 -AQ20 - 2 trash mobs (giant in Ossirian room and elementals spawned by Moam)
 -Naxx - one trash mob
