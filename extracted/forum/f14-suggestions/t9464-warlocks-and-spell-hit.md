# Warlocks and Spell-hit

## Post 62058 by Maladron — 2023-09-09T11:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=62058#p62058 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a Warlock main who raids enjoys Classic WoW+ raiding I always disliked the idea of being under hit-capped throughout the journey, unless I sacrifice so much of my options to get to 16% hit chance. I am aware that Warlocks have their strengths and weaknesses, however I cannot but feel inferior in raids in comparison to other casters, and having to wear Bloodvine and certain items just to be competitive in DPS. I am also aware that certain items of T1 & T2 will have bonus 1% spell hit, but that still does not cut it for us Warlocks, especially with the Bloodvine nerf. To make this more coherent, I'll compare other classes with us.

 **Mages:** 3/3 Elemental Precision (Fire/Frost) receive 6% bonus hit from talent. And Arcane spec receives 10% from talent.
 **Shadow Priests:** 5/5 [Shadow Focus] 10% Shadow Spell hit.
 **Warlocks:** [Suppression 5/5] 10% Affliction Spell-hit-only. No additional hit rating in Destruction or Demonology. I'm positive Warlocks' main DPS rotation revolves around Shadow Bolts, which as mentioned, receives no spell hit from talents.

Zul'Gurub Enchants (Head/Legs):
Mages receive 18 spell damage and 1% spell hit.
Warlocks receive 18 spell damage and 10 stamina.
Personally I do not mind this, as it is the same with Rogues/Hunters and other classes ZG's enchants (being hybrid), although it would be better if we receive hit chance instead of 10 stamina, as if we are supposed to be tanking? Don't want to make this seem like a rant, so I'll stop here.

In conclusion: Give Warlocks destruction/shadow spell hit chance instead of affliction-spell-hit only.
 -Maladia

## Post 62076 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-09-09T12:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=62076#p62076 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The lack of spellhit on talents gives us a lower floor but it allows us to reach a much higher ceiling than other casters.
Chance to hit on gear is just a stat, equivalent to spellpower or crit. It's not a must-reach goal like future expansions and that's fine, you can't reach 100% hit chance anyway even if you wanted (the cap is 99%)

So my personal advice is stop taking hit chance too seriously. At low gear you want to prioritize crit over hit because it's all about Improved Shadow Bolt uptime, and warlocks that stack hit but have no crit will soak all the ISB charges without contributing anything, while warlocks that stack crit and not hit will give a lot of charges without consuming much (missed shadow bolts don't consume a charge)

The Bloodvine nerf might not be as impactful in the end considering 1.17 will grant some much needed improvements to Warlock T1 / T2 - allowing them to have some gearing options with hit. For more details, check this: [viewtopic.php?t=8230](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=8230)

The thing is, Warlock has some excellent talents to scale well as is. Ruin, Improved Shadow bolt, Sacrifice Succubus or Shadow mastery alongside the support from shadowweaving make warlocks the hardest hitting casters... if they hit.

Flavorwise this is intentionally done, since warlocks aren't masters at precision, but rather just barely control incredibly destrictive forces. And missing with shadowbolts is not that big of a deal dps wise. Its more ocd triggering than detremental gameplay, because your incredibly good spellpower & crit scaling will compensate for the lack of hit.

So if warlock is to receive more spellhit "for free", you would have to scale back his other talents to compensate if you wouldn't wanna just powercreep warlocks.

我也发帖提过建议，虽然这边毛用没有，但是大概列出思考与意见的过程
因为过去了解奶妈比较多，所以先提的是奶妈相关的

首先，我希望调整萨满治疗链的跳跃打折与跳跃次数问题，
萨满治疗链希望弹跳4次，每次跳跃为前一次目标的百分之70，
其次，我希望调整牧师治疗祷言的读条时间，
牧师治疗祷言希望在天赋中加入降低读条时间0.5秒

萨满治疗链效果读条2.5秒，治疗一个目标，然后弹跳另一个目标受到上一个目标一半的治疗量，跳跃2次
简单点说如果第一个人受到100点治疗量，第二个就是50点治疗量，第三个就是25点治疗量，合计3个目标总共治疗175点治疗效果
牧师治疗祷言，读条3秒治疗5个目标，合计500治疗量，
从治疗量上可以看出来牧师治疗祷言高于萨满治疗链2倍以上，但是团本中，读条时间决定了很多，而且团本中3秒读条之后几乎很难保证第二个治疗祷言6秒后团队不出问题，所以根据目前打团中的情况，个人认为可以在天赋中添加降低0.5秒治疗祷言的读条，同时将萨满治疗链调整为弹射4次，每次为之前的百分之70效果。
具体蓝耗以及治疗量需要根据实际情况在调整.

在比如关于德鲁伊变熊当坦克相关的建议，
虽然经过和群里朋友探讨知道了一些类似BUG的事情导致了熊比较脆的原因，但是不妨碍我对其设想和建议
大家都知道防御有圆桌理论，坦克和近战必须明白的东西。
对于防战和骑士来说因为能带盾，能凑格挡招架和躲闪，来达到避免碾压，而通过防御等级，可以达到避免暴击。
但是对于熊德来说，天生没有格挡与招架，加上防御等级的装备不好凑，还得凑加护甲的装备，于是小德就很容易吃碾压，也容易吃到暴击。
而我提出的建议是提高德鲁伊变熊之后的护甲减免上限10%，原本护甲减免上限是75%。
当BOSS攻击里100，不计算格挡招架和躲闪，满护甲情况
战士受到25点普通伤害，受到37.5点碾压伤害，受到50点暴击伤害，
而调整之后的熊受到15点普通伤害，受到22.5点碾压伤害，受到30点暴击伤害，
这么对比战士因为格挡招架躲闪而只吃25点伤害来看，熊经常吃到碾压与暴击就显得差不多了。
但是那天群里一个老玩家告诉暴雪当初不是这么设计的，所以计算公式是
普通攻击受到25点，碾压受到75点伤害，暴击受到125点伤害。
当然这是暴雪制作时候为了简单而偷的懒，现在版本啥情况，完全不知道，更别说建议与设想了。
