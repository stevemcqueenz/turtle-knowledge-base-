## Post 64504 by Ishilu (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-09-15T16:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64504#p64504 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Note that the OP at least tried to make his proposal in english.

Unfortunately I have to vote this suggestion down.

Increasing named mob spawn rate sounds nice, depending on where you're standing. I agree that it can be boring to wait for that one specific quest mob to spawn (looking at you, Tazan!).

However, one of my most memorable open world pvp experiences was teaming up with people for the STV hunter quests and competing with an alliance group for several encounters. For close to an hour we would all go "Oh damn, it's THOSE guys again. Let's kill them so we can get Thetis/Bag'thera/Bangalash instead of sitting out a 30 minute respawn". Our battle over Bangalash was epic! We won, of course, even though the allies pulled a rather dirty trick right at the end. For the horde! Stuff like that doesn't happen when spawn times are short.

Increasing the spawn rate of these mobs would add convenience but ultimately suck a little bit of fun out of the game.

Also (as can be seen in numerous other posts from me), I'm very much opposed to QoL "improvements" of the game in general.

Finally, let's face it: the servers are overcrowded. We have too many players per server and communication problems are only the cherry on the turd right now. That's what's causing mob shortages right now and the team is working on it. Let's hope they make the right decisions and this situation will pass. The OP's proposal would only be a temporary fix for playing on an overpopulated server. I'd rather see the team successfully put their energy into a long term solution.

Once the server populations go back to normal (fingers crossed), an increased spawn rate might have further detrimental effects on server economy and open world gameplay.
