# More Lore-Friendly Celebrations

## Post 99826 by Zulnam (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-07-18T20:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99826#p99826 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are a lot of seasonal events in World of Warcraft... that have absolutely nothing to do with the world of warcraft.

Often they seem made this way so that both factions can partake in the celebration without much headache or hassle from the devs.

I propose adding more, smaller, faction and race specific events scaterred throughout the year.

Some examples:

 **Horde specific**:
Hellscream Memorial
Doomhammer Memorial
Kargath Memorial
Liberation Day (celebrating the new Horde's escape from Azeroth and settlement of Kalimdor)
Draenor Day (remembering and honoring the ancestral orcish homeland)
Free Will Night (celebrating the Forsaken's liberation from the clutches of the Litch King)

 **Alliance specific**:
Lothar Memorial
Uther Memorial
Khadgar Memorial
Alleria Memorial
Kurdan Memorial
Victory Day (celebrating the Alliance's Victory during the Second War)

 **Common**:
Hyjal Day (celebrating the races of Azeroth's victory over the Burning Crusade)

They don't have to be as grand as the current events, and they can be much shorter (1-5 days), but it would give the world more flair and be a good excuse for roleplay.

For example during Uther Memorial day, Argent Dawn soldiers can be spawned in the area surrounding Uther's tomb in order to clear it of undead and allow safe passage for pilgrims.
The event could have a single quest attainable from the Stormwind Cathedral called Pilgrimage which places a buff on the player that stops them from mounting or using flight paths and has a single objective: Reach Uther's Tomb.

Other similar flavour-quests could be created for each event.
