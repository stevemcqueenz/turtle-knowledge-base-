# New Minor Horde Subfaction

## Post 159452 by Voltaxian — 2025-11-13T04:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159452#p159452 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello everyone! Have you ever wondered where orcs got the MASSIVE bones they use to build quite literally everything? I mean, yeah there are some big creatures in Azeroth but they are nowhere near the size to get their buildings going, even the stinkiest farm is built with 5 of those Deathwing sized bones.

One answer: Orc Bonemancers, a somewhat ritualistic group of shamans who dabble in a very specific kind of necromancy. They grab bone and, through magic ritual, fuse it into larger bones to be used for architectural projects! They work at the "Boneworks" (which would be some area by the west exit of Orgrimmar) and its members can also be found wherever large creatures can be found: Durotar (Thunder Ridge), The Barrens (Field of Giants), Desolace (Kodo Graveyard), Un'Goro Crater (Terror Run).

A little backstory with the oldest of its members complaining about how small beasts are in azeroth compared to draenor. And of course some good interaction with the Durotar Labor Union, because the logistics wont do themselves, stuff happens, deliveries fail, scavengers munch at the bones, etc. If they even manage to get some quartermaster or vendor, they could sell some bone-looking gear, likely weapons, probably maces, maybe a fishing rod? Some bone puns here and there, and that's all really.
