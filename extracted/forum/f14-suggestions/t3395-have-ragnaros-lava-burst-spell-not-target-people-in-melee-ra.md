# Have Ragnaros Lava Burst Spell not target people in melee range

## Post 20634 by Velite (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-07-29T03:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20634#p20634 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So Ragnaros has a mechanic where he targets a random player with a mana bar and shoots them with a meteor and that knocks people around near them <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=21158>. The strategy to avoid this is to have everyone with a mana bar spread out around the room. This is great for the mana users who deal damage or heal at 30-40 yards, but for the mana users who need to be in melee you can't spread around much, since you still need to be near the boss to do damage, so there's very limited spots for them to stand and they have to avoid standing where all the non mana melee stand. This wouldn't be a problem except that anybody in melee also needs to avoid Wrath of Ragnaros, <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=20566>. So if you are in melee and have a mana bar, you have to deal with more mechanics than melee without mana bars or mana bars that are not in melee. Given how underperforming the people who have mana bars in melee happen to be, Is there some way to maybe have it not trigger when the person is in melee range? That way they only have to deal with the melee user mechanic if they are in melee, and only have to deal with the mana user mechanic when they are not in melee. Thank you.

Before you say "just heal instead" remember that not everyone plays the heal spec if their class can heal. And this server clearly does want to open up the game to more than just healers, but blizzard left mechanics like this which were done with the assumption that mana users were not in melee. This isn't the only mechanic like this, but it's definitely the most punishing.
