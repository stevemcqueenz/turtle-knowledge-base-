## Post 15482 by Mac (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-01-07T14:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15482#p15482 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Would be neat if you could craft bows and arrows from a profession.  Engineering makes the most sense, and Leatherworking also kinda works, although a new Woodworking profession would be cool.

For an arrow, the basic components are a shaft made of wood and an arrowhead typically made from stone.  1 Simple Wood for the shaft and 1 Copper Bar for the arrowhead to make 200 Crafted Light Arrows is fine and comparable to Crafted Light Shot.

The basic components for a bow are going to be the bow stave and bowstring.  Bow staves are made from wood.  For bows and later arrows, you'd probably want to add more wood to the game since Simple Wood is too cheap for even a low level bow, and the only other wood in the game, Star Wood, isn't really appropriate for lower level stuff given its price.  The wood could be bought off a vendor for lower level bow, or gathered using the Survival profession from nodes placed in appropriately leveled zones that use that bundle of wood model for mid to high level bows.  Bowstrings are made from fibers, either vegetable fibers (like linen, hemp, and silk) or animal fibers from hides and sinew.  Additional components would be things like feathers dropped by mobs (although many are considered junk and would need to be updated), leathers for grips, and engineered parts for cams for compound bows.  Let's say to make a level 6 Handcrafted Bow, you'd need 1 Fresh Wood (bought from a vendor for 3s) for the bow stave, 6 Linen Cloth for the bowstring, and 1 Plucked Feather.

To make arrows and bows, you'd need a Wood Carving Knife bought from a vendor as a reusable tool.
