# Paladin talent tree - new issue after recent patch

## Post 130278 by Arys123 — 2025-05-09T08:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130278#p130278 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings,

This is my first post on the forum. I made an account just to share my thoughts on the recent change in the Retribution Paladin talent tree, where Eye for an Eye was swapped with Two-Handed Weapon Specialization.

This change did fix an issue, where it lets Retribution Paladins pick up both Sanctity Aura and Precision at the same time, which is great.

But it also created a new problem. Now, Eye for an Eye is hard to get for Paladins who focus on spell damage in PvP. You either can't reach it, or you have to put 5 extra points into Retribution, which means losing key talents in the Holy tree. Eye for an Eye is really nice in PvP as it puts pressure on casters, so losing easy access to it kind of hurts that build - especially since the build lacks close gap like Repentance.

I’d like to think this was just overlooked, and I’m hoping the devs will take another look at it. It’d be great if future talent changes could consider how they affect all specs, not just the one being "fixed."

I'm a big Paladin fan, and after doing a lot of theorycrafting, I think the root of the problem is that Sanctity Aura was moved to the Holy tree. That change started a chain of issues, and now we’re seeing one problem get fixed only for another to pop up. It’s like a domino effect.

In my opinion, the best solution would be to move Sanctity Aura back where it belongs - in the Retribution (DPS oriented) tree. That would solve the original problem instead of just patching the side effects.

In general, I believe more rework needs to be done for the Paladins.
