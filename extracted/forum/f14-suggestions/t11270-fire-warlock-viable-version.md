# Fire Warlock Viable Version

## Post 74616 by Krokzogg — 2023-10-29T09:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74616#p74616 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Problem 1: Fire Warlock is not viable in dungeons and raids. (A lot of threat from Searing Pain)
Problem 2: Warlock's spell rotation in dungeons and raids is too simple. (Spam Shadow Bolt)

I agree that the high amount of threat from Searing Pain is a warlock trait and a penalty for using demon magic. But the original developers added a large threat without providing adequate ways to reduce it. And as a result, the Fire Warlock is only used in pvp and for tanking a few bosses. Someone can use a Fire Warlock with a high level Gear, Paladin Aura and Master Demonologist + Imp. But at low levels, the Fire Warlock is not viable.

Solution option: Add a spell to reduce the threat of Searing Pain from 200% to 100%.

 ---------------------------------------------------
Soft Flame	Rank 1
Level 18
 0 Mana	30 yd range
Instant
Requires Warlock
Reduce the threat of Searing Pain by half for target that is already afflicted by Immolate,  until 4 Searing Pain are applied.  Effect lasts a maximum of 12 sec. and consuming the Immolate spell.
 ---------------------------------------------------

Rotation: [ Immolate --> Soft Flame --> Searing Pain x4 ]

This is better than spamming Shadow Bolt and doesn't pose much of a threat.
Builds for PVP and for tanking some bosses will remain the same. They simply won't use Soft Flame.

This is an example solution.
How do you like the idea?
