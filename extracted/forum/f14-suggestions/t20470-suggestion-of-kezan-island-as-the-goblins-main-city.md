# suggestion of Kezan Island as the goblins main city

## Post 141419 by Qzyieu — 2025-07-25T18:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141419#p141419 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's not because I particularly like goblins,
but rather, I think goblins should be a very interesting race.

They should add more elements to make it more enjoyable.

On the official server of World of Warcraft, Kezan Island is the birthplace of the goblins.

It is very prosperous (although, the industrial pollution is extremely severe).

However, the buildings and roads within it are all highly consistent with the design of the goblins.

At that time, when I was playing on the official server, I thought the sinking of Kezan Island was a very regrettable design.

It should not disappear; instead, it should serve as the main city of the goblins.

In response to this, I have the following suggestions.

I hope that the administrators of Turtle wow and all the fans who enjoy World of Warcraft can see them.

 1.The Kezan Island is designed as the main city for the goblins.
Due to its well-equipped facilities and ample area, all that is needed is to add trainers, some guards, and the main NPCs, and it can be used.

 2,Helicopters and car-related games can be added to enhance the fun of Kezan Island.

This is in line with the design of the goblins.

 3,Add a neutral area where both the alliance and the horde can enter.

This area will be used to hold a concert, which will be very enjoyable.(This was mentioned in another of my posts. I proposed adding a special talent to the goblins. They could hire a musician they liked, such as a violinist or a pianist. Each goblin could only hire one type of musician. When the goblins got together, they could hold a small concert. Of course, if all of you chose the same musician, it might result in a bad concert.)

 4,The weather on Kezan Island could be made more pleasant instead of being so polluted by industries. For instance, the sky in tropical Hawaii.
The plot setting is that the goblins bought an island.
This fits the character of wealthy people(Goblins)
