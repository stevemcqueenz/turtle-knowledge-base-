# Let warlock demons be targeted with BoP and Abolish

## Post 162808 by Sharmaine (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-14T20:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162808#p162808 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Ability to target demons with buffs was removed for reasons (encourage swapping demons mid fight, reduce amount of time and money spent on temporary demons). However, with it you also removed ability to target them with other spells.

BoP (blessing of protection/hand of protection) is used in raids to protect DPS characters that rip threat. Demons can't be blessed with salvation, nor do they benefit from void stone. Some of them have aoe abilities that do huge amounts of threat. Right now the only way for felguard to use felstorm on big packs of enemies is to use aoe taunt. However, mages can do it via being cast blessing of protection on, being immune to damage long enough for packs to be killed. Felguard should be able to use the ability in strategic way like this too.

Abolish poison/disease is considered a buff, and therefore can't be cast on demons. This is extremely frustrating for healers. Problem is, abolish has same mana cost as regular Cure Poison/Cure Disease, while being strictly better. It results in Cure Poison/Disease not being bound to key or mouseover by priests/druids, there's just no reason to. And so when they see an ill/poisoned demons, they might try click cure on them, see that it doesn't work and stop caring. As a priest player myself, I haven't found a good way to conveniently cure warlock pets - keybinds are limited, and pressing cure with a mouse on action bar is lame, takes several seconds to do this.

TL:DR abolish is not a buff and allowing it to be used is a big qol upgrade for healers that care; BoP is strategic tool that lets people dps, no reason to exclude warlock from being able to aoe on trash
