# Sp. Ret Observations and Caluations. How to Bring back AP Ret.

## Post 133000 by Icedcream (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-26T01:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133000#p133000 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Introduction**

I am writing this article as an opinion about the current state of Retribution paladins in Turtle Wow. I am not here to debate with people, but I just wanted to put out some information into the community on what I am seeing since the class changes. I am an end game raider who completes Kara 40 weekly. I have completed equipment builds including T3 and most melee BIS accessories. I run with full consumes in raids and enjoy theory crafting.

I have endeavored to maximize my outputs in raid with attack power (AP) - ret builds and now moved to a spell power (SP) – ret build. These comments are largely about PVE, and I am not interested that people in blues are mad in pvp when a T3 paladin one shot them. Any information contained here is largely without much consideration to PVP. I want to pass along my observations and hypothesis and discuss what I believe is healthy or unhealthy for turtle wow. Often when we see game design the developers cannot foresee the depths of the experimentation people will do to find the BIS game play. I believe the developers would of course are best intentioned and players just want to be the best. Therefore, people may enjoy the way things are right now with Retribution, but I range from indifferent to unsatisfied.

Now you are probably reading this say, don’t bore us get to the chorus. I have tested the abilities and various gear sets as well as reviewing community leader boards and logs, it is my opinion that SP Ret is the superior spec. What are SP Rets? Generally, Ret paladin’s who skip Seal of Command, and instead use seal of Righteousness. They focus deeply on stacking spell power but also do pick up melee stats (AP/Crit/Hit). They heavily use additional spells consecration, repent, and exorcism. They utilize consumable items such as sapper charges (engineering item) and holy water (a farmed item from strat).  SP Ret doesn’t only beat AP Ret on cleave content (most trash in raid is cleaved), but also on boss fights.  Below are some fundamentals on why this works.

 **Seal of Righteousness – Twow Calculations:**

 *These are my personal calculations / formula’s testing 18 of 26 weapon speeds at four different spell power’s (0, 37, 157, 347), untalented and spot checked with talented on 347 sp set up. Tested on Rank 8. Feel encouraged to peer review.*

 1h weapon ::
Base damage = 21.6+ ((Weapon speed*10)-15)*2.12
Coefficient damage =  ((13.75%*(Weapon speed/1.5)*Spell Power))
Example – <2.4 speed + 400 sp> 40.68 + 88 = 129 holy damage (before bonus damage)

 2h weapon::
Base damage = 21.6+ ((Weapon speed*10)-15)*2.12
Coefficient damage =  ((33.33%*(Weapon speed/1.5)*Spell Power))
Example – <3.6 speed + 400 sp> 66.12 + 185.59 = 251 holy damage (before bonus damage)

BONUS DAMAGE:
 1)	10% from improved seal of righteousness
 2)	10% Sanctity Aura
 3)	15% Vengeance

Multiple final damage by 1.1, 1.21, 1.3915. Bonus damage not factored into the above calculations. ***frustratingly my calculation is always off by 1 damage when multiply is added… close enough.

 *Note: You can observe that the SP coefficient on this ability is much higher then what you can find online stated for classic or event on twow fandom page. This was likely adjusted during a rework for protection paladins.*

 **Consecration:**

Hit unlimited quantity of targets standing in consecration. 1 tick per second x 8.

Twow SP Coefficient – 0.658 (rank 5) / 8 ticks

BONUS DAMAGE:
 1)	10% Sanctity Aura
 2)	15% Vengeance

Multiple final damage by 1.1, 1.2625.

Example- <400 sp> 384 (rank 5) + (400*.658) = 647.2/8 = 81 per tick x 1.2625 (bonus damage sources) = 102 per second per enemy

 **Holy Water:**

Farm Stratholm Holy Water from crates in Strat.  Very short range (basically melee range), damage undead within 5 yards. No threat generation. 1 minute CD.

Twow SP Coefficient – 100%

BONUS DAMAGE:
 1)	10% Sanctity Aura
 2)	15% Vengeance

Multiple final damage by 1.1, 1.2625.

Example- <389 sp> 438-562 base damage + (389*1) = 827 – 951, or 1044 – 1200 holy damage per target with bonus damage.

 **Exorcism:**

 15 second CD – 505-563 damage usable on undead and demons.

Twow SP Coefficient – 42.9% *I think*

BONUS DAMAGE: *I’m reasonable sure it is affected by these*
 1)	10% Sanctity Aura
 2)	15% Vengeance

Example- <389 sp> 505-563 base damage + (389*0.429) = 672 – 730, or 848 – 922 holy damage per target with bonus damage.

 **Holy Strike**

Holy strike is obviously a holy spell. Shares a cool down with crusader strike. Proc’s holy might for 20 seconds from the talent tree.

Twow SP Coefficient – Super hard time confirming a value. Somewhere around 36-39%.

BONUS DAMAGE:
 1)	10% Sanctity Aura
 2)	15% Vengeance

 **Crit and Hit**

Paladin hit and crit work a bit differently then expected because it would be impossible to stack both spell and melee versions of the stat. Below are the stat sources that control the chance.

White Hit – Melee Crit / Hit
Crusader Strike – Melee Crit / Hit
Holy Strike – Melee Crit / Hit
Seal of Command – Melee Crit / 100% Hit Chance (if melee hits)
Judgement of Command – Melee Crit / 100% Hit Chance
Seal of Righteousness – Does Not Crit / 100% Hit Chance (if melee hits)
Judgement of Righteousness – Melee Crit / 100% Hit Chance
Exorcism – Spell Crit / (unsure not enough damage, can be resisted but was low to no %)
Repentance / Repent – Does not Crit / ?100% Hit Chance?
Consecration – Does not Crit / Does not miss

 **Spell Power**

So how much spell power can you get Pre-Kara:
•	T2.5 + accessories and enchants – Easily 450
•	Mark of the Champion for UD/Demon – 85
•	Flash of Supreme Power – 150
•	Arcane Elixir – 30
•	Dreamtonic – 35
•	Brilliant Wizard Oil – 36
•	Judgement of Crusader – 193
•	Food buff – 22, but not counting since you will use Dragonsbreath

Pre-Kara you can achieve 894-979+ (non-UD.D./UD.D.), PLUS options for SP on use trinkets or Wrath of Cenerius for reliable procs of 132+ extra spell power.

Jumping over T3 to Kara gear and T3.5 (5pc) gives a bunch of the best of both worlds maintaining your spell power, but increase Strength, crit, and attack speed.

 **What do I believe the problem is?**

I believe class balancing has been largely done around Naxx performance. There has been community justification (forums, discords, and youtube), that paladins are performing alright because community members are pulling the 1400+ dps boss fight meters in naxx, the same as other major DPS classes. However, I am reasonably confident that these performances are by SP Ret players. They include gimmicks and situational damage (buffed by UD and Demon mobs)

 1)	I believe the current iteration of the talent tree and T3 was designed with the expectations of AP Ret. You will see this with your attacked scaling off your melee damage or JoC having an AP coefficient.  However, in the current state you can expect 25-35% more consistent damage from wearing T2.5 with other spell power items and with an item like Ashbringer and some gadgets. Verses AP Ret with T3 + BIS melee accessories +Ashbringer or MoM.
 2)	I believe Twow’s modified trees and coefficients lead to this being the best option.
 3)	I believe this type of damage however leads to inconsistent performance. There is a period right now where Kara has some demons, and Naxx is full of UD so you have the benefit of exorcism and other factors.
 4)	Problem’s I see in Kara. There are encounters where you must control your cleave as enemies (infernos), consecration is a spell that cannot be controlled and easily picks up agro. As well, many mobs/bosses aren’t demon/UD. In these cases, your performance will fall off more then other classes.
 5)	There are other funny mechanics that are reinforced for good or bad. If an item has a small “on melee hit” function it is used as a source for mana regeneration. This has always existed. This was added in twow to judgement T2 3pc bonus and forgotten as a mechanic on future sets and raid pieces going forward. The SP ret most of all rely on this for sustain. I tried Embergem bracers proc’ing 4 fire damage on hit. When combined with seal of righteousness you can often get 4 wisdom procs in a single swing regenerating 212 mana.

 **Demonstration of SP. Ret damage Sources:**

Below are some average’s taken from raid based on damage done and total hit events. Seal of Command vs Seal of Righteousness. SP Ret in T2.5 + some additional accessories Pre-Kara BIS. AP Ret in T3. Both with approximately the same performance weapons. Both with Flask of Supreme power, spell power potions, ap potions, str potions, mongoose, etc. SP Ret with lower crit chance, but higher spell power. SP Ret with Brilliant wiz oil vs AP ret with stones. I believe turtle wow adjusted the spell coefficients of SoR for Prot paladin’s however it has become the best seal by far for Ret Paladins.

Boss example:
Seal of Command – Average per hit 482.79 (including crits)
Seal of Righteousness – Average per hit 796 (cannot crit)

Judgement of Command – Average per hit 1309
Judgement of Righteousness - Average per hit 1632

You combine this with gimmicks like 5% of damage coming from Spell coefficient and procs of Ashbringer or Consecrations ticking at >145 dps on bosses (vs like 70-80 for T3 paladin), better exorcism, and I holy strikes.  Players ultimately sacrifice 18% of melee damage which used to make up 38-45% of total damage (so 7.5-8% of total damage). For an equivalent boost in holy damage from seals/judgement alone, then milestone gains (a doubling of damage from consecration, exorcism, repent, and weapon procs). Finally, in Naxx you can see over 100k damage coming from holy water usage.

 **Conclusion:**

Generally, this isn’t my preference for the class. People are welcome to their own opinions.
This whole design rubs me the wrong way. The class is using a unique weapon proc, charges, holy water, and a unique ability (exorcism) for 2 mob (ud/demon) types to be in the current position in the meters. The design doesn’t feel Ret Paladin if you’re using SoR and not going boom. It feels bad if T2.5 is dramatically more powerful then T3 (Although T3 is a really good AP set! The problem may be the talent tree / coefficients). This is 100% just an opinion on the state.

 **Recommendations:**

Suggestion 1: Suggested a few moves around with holy, prot, and ret. I did not write out a full holy tree as that is another can of worms.  I didn’t want to get too much into the weeds, but I think the mana efficiency would be find if you are blowing it on consecration. The redesign also functions so the secondary tree is prot with divine strength and precision. It also allows prot to go into the ret tree for low mana cost judgements and non-improved sanctity aura.

To be honest with these revisions, and the loss of consecration, it may be only a minor buff to AP Ret.

•	Remove consecration and make it a skill for Prot Paladins high enough we can’t touch it.
•	Front load Ret Paladin’s damage
•	Give them a form of a melee cleave (yes this is necessary for raid).
•	Fix Seal of Command so it scales better with AP/Crit.
•	Reconsider redoing the cap stone. Can be a similar effect to Repent… Repent’s cooldown is 1 minute and it may or may not overwrite other ret paladin’s repent. I think it does since in some raid I receive low output and it is probably being overwritten by another ret paladin.
•	Sanctity aura belongs low in the Ret tree hits holy damage not holy damage and healing.
•	Add improved sanctity aura so you are a benefit to your team. You’re in the windfury totem group.
•	Buff 2h specialization
