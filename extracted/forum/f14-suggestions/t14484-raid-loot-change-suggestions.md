# Raid loot change suggestions

## Post 98789 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-07-07T11:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98789#p98789 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

In my opinion there are some underwhelming loots which could be made interesting.
Also, there are some raid bosses which people skip because how underwhelming the reward is (f.ex. Ayamiss in AQ20).
My goal is to make loot tables more interesting and give people a reason to go for each raid boss.
Will stop at BWL level. Later raids have some questionnable items, too but in general I think they are fine.

ZG
Jeklik
 * Make primalist band an epic (with the set bonus it is easily arguable) and improve set bonus from 33 healing to 40 healing.

Venoxis
 * Same logic, make zanzil's band a purple and increase set bonus spell damage by 10.

Mar'li
She needs more help, people tend to skip spider boss because of bad loot.
 * Marli's touch getting +1% hit chance
 * Band of Jin -> Would increase set bonus from 30 attack power to 40 and making it purple.

Mandokir
 * Give Mandokir's Sting 1% haste

Gahz'ranka
 * Foror's Eyepatch giving 10 agility instead of 19 stamina. It's mostly for rogues (and cats), it's better for them this way.

Arlokk
 * Will of Arlokk giving 5% meditation (the item is mostly centered around spirit and is currently not good enough).

Hakkar
 * Giving Idol.
Heart of Hakkar trinkets: healer one is very good, the others are so-so.
 * Zandalarian Hero Badge: Giving armor and +threat instead of armor and defense. Armor and added threat gets reduced every time you take a hit.
 * Zandalarian Hero Medallion: Bigger default damage bonus.

 **Tldr**: Set bonus rings are now purple and are slightly buffed. Mar'li is relevant now and Hakkar also drops an idol. Heart of hakkar is good for everyone now.

AQ20

Kurinnaxx
 * Qiraji Sacrificial Dagger getting +agi, for example 7 agi on top of the current things it gives.

Ayamiss
 * Bow of Taut Sinew getting +1% hit chance.
 * Stinger of Ayamiss losing the stamina bonus but giving 2% spell crit chance, not 1.

Kara10

Araxxna
 * Vial of Potent Venoms finding a middle way between being broken (as it was) and being trash (as it is now).
 * Araxxna's Husk becoming a purple and giving 300 armor instead of 140.

Howlfang
 * Besthunter's Blunderbuss is going blue, Sliver of Hope is going purple. Stats unchanged.

Grizikil
 * The Mind's Key has to be purple.

Onyxia
 * Onyxia's Blood Talisman needs buff. Noone choose it, not even the tanks. Would remove its health restoration and would give it 1% hit chance instead.
 * Shard of the Scale gets arguably outclassed by Kara10 trinkets. Would go higher on mp5 to tackle this.

MC -> I think it is okay from this perspective.
