# Change Stormstrike's / Additional Melee Spell for Shaman

## Post 41345 by Kashchei — 2023-04-13T06:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41345#p41345 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Stormstrike's debuff has always been pretty mediocre, the only way as a solo shaman to get the value on the effect is to save your shock CD until after it's applied and squeeze in 2 earthshocks over the effects duration.

This is counterintuitive to the purpose of shocks being that they may share a CD but are varied enough that you change which one you use based on the situation, not dissimilar to how you chose weapon imbues.

This issue with stormstrike isn't seen in later patches because lightning shield procs will also gain the bonus damage and enhance would eventually get the ability to instant cast lightning bolts / chain lightning.

I am NOT recommending that shamans get ANYTHING close to how maelstrom works. I would much rather see Stormstrike get a more universal debuff such as interacting with the next shock the target is hit by.

Shaman could also use an additional melee spell, something that interacts with which weapon imbue that have on their weapon at the time eg;

Rockbiter; knocks down the target
Flametongue; AOE fire cleave
Frostbrand; roots the target in place
Windfury; increases enemy cast time / time between attacks
