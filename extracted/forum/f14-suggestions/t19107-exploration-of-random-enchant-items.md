# Exploration of Random Enchant Items

## Post 129853 by Darcy33 — 2025-05-06T12:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129853#p129853 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, I'm a level 32 holy pally scavenging the auction house for, "of the gorilla" and "of the eagle" mail armor items **as I don't want to wear cloth as a paladin best I can** and with the new "Iron Clad" talent this is an extra benefit. Plus, I'm glad the team wants to encourage paladins to wear mail/plate armor.

Thing is though, some extra enchants that would be beneficial like, "of the owl", "of spirit", "of intellect" weren't quite available until level 35 for mail armor which isn't bad considering I'm 32 but I was curious and checked plate in the auction house, those enchants were darn near nonexistent sadly. Is it intended? Could the variety be increased? Also, there are less useful enchants like 'of Arcane Wrath' dropping on mail items, cluttering the auction house which somewhat benefits hunters but barely.

Another thing, maybe more could be explored with random enchants. Some random enchants could be combined like in TBC. Tbc enchants in classic would be too OP though and overshadow existing gear but there might be room for combining in classic. Would something as simple as adding stamina to resistance gears hurt in classic? Maybe even a little defense too? There's enchants like "of blocking", "of concentration", "of eluding", "of defense" maybe they could be combined to already existing enchants like, "of the bear" "of the tiger", "of the owl". Two examples in item description:

Plate Shoulders of the "Stoic" Bear(lol)
+5 strength
+4 stamina
+%1 block

Plate Helm of the "Grove" Owl
+5 intellect
+4 spirit
+3 healing power

There could be multiple bear or owl variations. Again, overshadowing existing gear is an issue but tinkering with random enchants might have potential and I would love to see it. There could also be restrictions, like that of some enchants only working for specific gear pieces like "of blocking" only working for shields. Could be a lot of possibilities without making it too cluttered.
