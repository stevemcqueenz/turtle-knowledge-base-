# Swarming Pillar as a dungeon for over-58s

## Post 152770 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-23T08:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152770#p152770 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Only an improvision that came to my mind when I saw the Swarming Pillar in Silithus with its spectacular scenery as two raid-level dundeons/instances already exist in Silithus. Currently on 23 September, 2025, no quest is in game involving deep exploration of Swarming Pillar. I believe that a dungeon of five-player scale can reinforce the region's story and questlines.

Quests and the background story of this dungeon could be about the silithid infiltration under the sands and how the Pillar functions as a transit command or logistic centre that projects silithid influence over Kalimdor. The drops/rewards can be of Scholomance and UBRS's level, under Stormwind Vault and Karazhan Crypt's. To encourage players to try beating the dungeon, the route within had better be comparatively shorter than Dragonmaw Retreat's, with fewer minor mobs and more bosses, just like the classic Black Fathom adventure.

Quests should be few but rewarding. Some quests can be related to raids in the future.
