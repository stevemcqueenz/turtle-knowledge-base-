# The positive feedback post

## Post 147550 by Tbcb — 2025-08-21T18:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147550#p147550 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtles,

as always when it comes to feedback, people tend to complain more than they point out the positive things - even if we all appreciate nice words from time to time. As a bulwark against the negativity, i want to start the positive feedback post. The devs can perfectly work with what we like and advance more in this direction.

To understand my feedback, i have to point out, that i only play hc for leveling and raiding on nordanaar.
Nothing else - no normie, no pvp, no ambershire. So let's start.

 1.) I like the new talents.

Playing boomkin is a different experience to other casters, frostbrand is a horde spell-sword-specc and arcane mage is a living glascannon - or a dying one - RIP arcaynewest lvl 52. Some tweaks might not work yet, but you are doing a great job there by giving us multiple ways to play the classes. It tempers me to try new classes and other speccs.

 2.) The new difficulty makes gearing meaningfull

MC felt braindead, 10 man strat even more. Giving every person the reason to gear up for the next step and not just getting dragged through a dungeon in the hope for the next item so you can do the last raid makes sense.

 3.) The new dungeonlootboxes are a refreshing change to openworld farming

Nordanaar has alot of competition in open world farming and the lootboxes with endgame mats, herbs and pots are a refreshing way to fill your consums up. It is also a reason to run dungeons and help guildies. That is an insange good change. Please keep the content of the boxes usefull for raiding mats.

I invite you all to appreciate the work of the devs and the team by writing positive feedback!
Thank you for keeping the server up!!
