# Request for additional skin color options for High Elves

## Post 107427 by Celestilock — 2024-11-10T18:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107427#p107427 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of my favorite additions to retail character customization was the expanded skin color options for the high/blood/void elves and it would mean a lot to have some of the darker skin tones added to TurtleWoW for the high elves.

I've loved the lore around the high/blood elves for so many years now. Their story in WoW and Warcraft 3 resonated with me. When playing them in WoW, though, It always felt weird to me that my character was not allowed to have skin and hair like mine. It feels like a kind of arbitrary thing to draw the line on.

From a lore perspective, all elven bodies are heavily influenced by exposure to magic (arcane, fell, shadow/void). It doesn't feel like a stretch for some of them to have darker skin tones by simply spending some of their long lives in various regions around Azeroth and not just in Quel'Thalas. Turtle WoW is its own story so I think devs are even more at liberty to go ahead and add the options and just not have things be weird around it.

The only argument I can see being leveraged against the addition of more skin options is that it deviates from the art in early WoW and Warcraft 3, but I think the pros outweigh any cons.

If any devs feel up to add the additional customization options in, it'd mean a lot.
