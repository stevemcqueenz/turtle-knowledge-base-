Thinking about it, gnome priests aren't truly outrageous.

- With the fall of Gnomeregan, gnomes are canonically looking for ways to detoxify their home and their people.  There's a [questline](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=2927) in the game where you aid gnomes in their attempts to find a cure.  It does stand to reason that at least some gnomes might turn to the Holy Light in search of a way to cleanse their city.

- As has been said, Gnomes could easily learn the Holy Light from Dwarves or Humans.  Tinker Town is literally a few steps or a train ride to The Mystic Ward or Cathedral Square respectively.  Although humans might reject them, I really doubt dwarves would discriminate against a gnome wishing to learn the Holy Light given that the two races are like brothers.  In fact, the Halls of Mystery is a shared space where dwarven priests and paladins study and train side-by-side with gnome mages.

- There are gnome priests in-game in the form of [holdout medics](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=6392) in Gnomeregan that can cast priest spells who are there using priestly magic to fight against the radiation.  These gnome priests aren't exceptional or special given that they are literally generic NPCs without unique names.
So gnome priests have both the motivation and opportunity to become priests, and there are already gnome priests in the game.  I think the argument can be made in favor of gnome priests.

Additionally, gnomes are a race that generally aren't commonly played.  Adding another class option to their race would give players more of a reason to choose gnomes, especially if they got some good racial spells like Fear Ward.
