# PvP activation in cities/towns

## Post 110691 by Waeven1 — 2024-11-30T17:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110691#p110691 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey I think its quite annoying with pvp activation now that people can just walk up to you and activate pvp and I suggest this change. The way I want it change is so that PVP mode can only be activated inside cities/towns.

An extension of this idea is that a pvp player should only be able to group with another pvp player so if a pvp player want to party up with someone that toggled pvp off one of them has to switch in towns/cities so they have the same mode on.

Another thing that might be worth trying is to make horde and alliance players not understanding eachother if pvp is toggled on so horde gets their language and alliance theirs.

I believe this could improve the world pvp!

## Post 110792 by Galith — 2024-12-01T14:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110792#p110792 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To some extend I'm fine with the way that PvP flagging works, however I'd like to suggest a compromise. IF you flag in the world, you should be permanently flagged until you reach one of your factions major cities. The amount of times where I've seen gankers who just walk up to someone to try and get an easy, and extremely cheesy kill by opening unflagged, just to log off if they manage to escape to remove the PvP flag, is absurd. I feel like there should be more of a "risk" to flagging for PvP, and I feel being permanently flagged until you reach one of your major cities is a decent way to handle it.

Though with this compromise, maybe add some fail safes for HC players, I don't know.
