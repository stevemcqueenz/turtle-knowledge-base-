# Demons in settlements

## Post 17409 by Qwida — 2022-03-05T18:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17409#p17409 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Back in Blizzcon 2010, Chris Metzen answered a question of a player from a RP server: what would happen if guards see warlocks with their demons out going around the city?

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/E7fVTabpyf8?start=1336]

In retail wow RP servers, having your demon out inside settlements is still seen as breaking immersion. A rare thing that nearly all roleplayers agree should not be done. In vanilla wow, you can see a felhunter hidden inside a house in ironforge, while in TBC silvermoon, the succubi were hidden in the basement of a tailor shop.

Given that turtle wow is making changes to add more immersion to the game, I think demons should be restricted access to settlements somehow. Metzen said that this was just one of the hundreds gameplay conventions, that storywise this would not happen.

As this is a RP server, even if ones does not roleplay, I think adding more immersion to how the player interact with the world elements is good. I think the turtle wow team is capable of coming up with solutions that can make the segregation with the narrative and gameplay less severe.

I put some possible ideas:
 - Make the daemons be dismissed automatically when entering into a settlement.
 - Make the guards attack the demon and kill it, while saying some warning to the warlock player.
 - Make the guards say warnings to the warlock player, and the npcs won't open up dialogue until the demon is dismissed.

Thanks for reading. I hope the turtle team takes some of these ideas into consideration.

Are guards supposed to kill demons on sight, yet spare warlocks (knowing that they can summon demons again once inside the settlement)? What about savages arriving with dangerous wild beasts (AKA hunters)? Or other shady-looking visitors (spies? rogues? assassins?), mages capable of setting the entire city on fire, or just [heavily armed individuals](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ckq-4Y6a87s)? RP-wise they shouldn't be allowed either.

Is it a good idea for a paper RPG, but I just can't see it implemented in an MMORPG, at least not without crippling one class or two. Or without additional changes which counter that (such as summoning demons instant and dismissing hunter pets not decreasing their happiness). With such changes, it'd be more fair (and definitely less annoying), just like automatic dismounting is acceptable.

An infiltration mission with such mechanics (not encountered everyday and everywhere) would be interesting, though.
