# A way for Vagrant's Challenge players to collect transmogs

## Post 127993 by Apav (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-04-22T15:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127993#p127993 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Vagrant's Challenge players are unable to collect any transmogs for gear that's not grey or white because they are unable to equip them. You can store your gear in your bank to equip when you're 60, but that's an impossible task for completionists for the hundreds of quest gear you obtain along the way that can't be obtained again, and still a hassle for people who aren't completionists but still like the looks of a few dozen of those. I propose a compromise for Vagrant's Challenge players, transmog collection status is given automatically when an equippable item enters your bag. If anyone has any other ideas, I'd appreciate you sharing them. Thanks!
