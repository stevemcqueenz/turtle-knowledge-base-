# CC2 cat feedback - theorycrafting of Scaling

## Post 104872 by Blackfox — 2024-10-26T11:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104872#p104872 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I realize things are likely being discussed and changed even right now and the Forum post and the Talent calculator isn't 100% final. The last change made me realize that the discussion is still open and so I wanted to provide information and feedback that might be important regarding any changes.

 **Shred**
 100% / (Attack Power>DPS) * (Attack Speed) * (Ability Scaling)
 100/14*1*2.25 =         16.07 % coefficient for Attack Power > Damage
 16.07/60 =                  0.27 % AP coefficient per Energy without talent
 16.07/48 =                  0.33 % AP coefficient per Energy talented

 **new Rake**
Rake hit coefficient + 3* Rake dot coefficient
 12%+3%*3 =                   21 % coefficient for Attack Power > Damage
 21/40 =                    0.525 % AP coefficient per Energy
 21/35 =                       0.6 % AP coefficient per Energy with Ferocity talent

(12%+3%*3)*1.2 =        25.2 % coefficient for Attack Power > Damage with Savage Fury talent
 25.2/40 =                   0.63 % AP coefficient per Energy with Savage Fury
 25.2/35 =                   0.72 % AP coefficient per Energy with Savage Fury and Ferocity
(every feral that is expected to do even the smallest amount of tanking, or does pvp, or does solo content will have these talents)

 **Claw**
 100% / (Attack Power>DPS) * (Attack Speed) * (Ability Scaling)

 100/14*1 =                  7.14 % coefficient for Attack Power > Damage
 7.14/45 =                    0.16 % AP coefficient per Energy
 7.14/40 =                    0.18 % AP coefficient per Energy with Ferocity

 100/14*1*1.2 =              8.57 % coefficient for Attack Power > Damage with Savage Fury
 8.57/45 =                      0.19 % AP coefficient per Energy with Savage Fury
 8.57/40 =                      0.21 % AP coefficient per Energy with Savage Fury and Ferocity

 100/14*1*1.2*1.3*1.25 =   13.93 % coefficient for Attack Power > Damage fully talented and with the new talent Open Wounds
 13.93/40 =                         0.35 % AP coefficient per Energy fully talented and with Open Wounds

(natural weapons is excluded because it scales everything equally)

New Rake is getting way better scaling than any other abilities. This scaling is pushing it high on damage even compared to Shred, and it makes its Damage per Energy scale way better than Shred. At 1550 AP Rake gets better DPE and it keeps going higher and getting more efficient with more AP.

New buffs for Claw also bring it rather close to Shred damage, and due to its lower Energy costs Claw will get better Energy efficiency than Shred.

 **Opinionated summary:**
Lots of druids are playing cat for its playstyle of high octane powershifting and shredding shit from behind for big hits. It might be a playstyle that some dislike, it is however a playstyle that rewards the player's skill. It would be very disappointing if this playstyle was no longer the thing to strive for for us who want to press many buttons and be rewarded for it. People can still play cat without powershifting, or even without using Shred and this playstyle is still viable enough right now for them to enjoy raid content at the lower level. People back in vanilla had no clue how to play and they still cleared content. People today can enjoy clawing away at mobs and do content too (the only thing stopping them are other people)

Rake with its insane energy efficiency scaling will go 2 ways - at low AP it will be bad, but with high amounts of AP it will become stronger than other skills so it will have to be incorporated into the rotation. This means that the rotation might potentially change based on AP values (if we don't consider requiring the dot for other effects). This is not necessarily wrong, but I don't think an ability should have such wild scaling compared to other abilities.
Rake could use a buff for sure. However the current proposed buff is too strong in my opinion. Its scaling should be in line with the other skill so that it doesn't outpace them at high AP values.
Also right now Rake is not completely dead, it's useful in one situation and that is against high armour targets in pvp. There Rake will do more damage against full plate targets than Claw. With current proposed scaling Rake will be n.1 ability to press in pvp and solo content always.

Claw getting better energy efficiency would also mean it could become the go to main ability to use, except for one thing which Shred will keep as an inherent advantage, whether this advantage will be enough is the question.

The only way Shred will still stay as the goto dps ability is if through its higher energy cost the druid will be able to spend more energy faster and then gain considerably more energy through powershifts and as a result be able to spend more overall energy during the fight. As it stands now with the proposed changes, this is the only way for Shred to outpace the Claw spam. Shred playstyle will need to be able to spend considerably more energy to get ahead (people can powershift in between Claw too ofc).

Shred should be keeping its damage and efficiency advantage. It is a higher energy cost ability and it requires to hit from behind. These disadvantages should allow it to keep its damage and efficiency advantage.
Rogues have it the same - Backstab does more damage and is more efficient, it however requires more energy, requires to be behind the target and requires daggers (potentially reducing damage from other abilities)

The buffs to Rake and Claw abilities are welcome, however in my opinion are too strong. Also if cat requires a buff after the brutal homicide of MCP, then I'd want the abilities in my rotation to be buffed, that is Shred, Ferocious Bite, potentially Rip (maybe some mechanic to make it good, bleeds are cool). Getting more haste is boring. Getting more damage from auto-attacks via potential claw-skill as some people suggest is also boring.

______
And as a last note I don't want to say that I don't want any of these changes. I welcome any changes that will make my favourite playstyle more interesting and I also welcome any changes that will help other people enjoy their playstyle. I am just a little concerned that the playstyle I specifically chose this class for will become unviable as the max-dps rotation.
