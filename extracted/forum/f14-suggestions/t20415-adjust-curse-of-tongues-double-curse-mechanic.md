# Adjust Curse of Tongues + Double-Curse Mechanic

## Post 140863 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-22T10:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140863#p140863 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**2 Curses**

It’s great that Warlocks can now apply two curses and it’s probably a much-needed boost for PvE. But in PvP, Warlocks are already incredibly strong. Giving them double-curses in arenas or battlegrounds amplifies their pressure to a point that feels oppressive, especially when tools like Curse of Tongues can shut down casters entirely.

If this design stays, then either more healers should gain access to Decurse, or existing decurse abilities should be able to remove two curses per cast to keep things balanced.

 **Curse of Tongues**

Curse of Tongues is another issue. It basically locks casters out of the game. Its duration should be significantly reduced. When a Mage uses a global to decurse an ally, Warlock DoTs are already ticking, that trade is brutal and unsustainable.

Furthermore, the demonic speech side-effect tied to Curse of Tongues is a fun touch flavor-wise, but it unintentionally hurts gameplay. It makes communication impossible if you're not on Discord, which isn't a fair expectation in random BGs or casual PvP. Clear team coordination should never be gated behind third-party voice chat.
