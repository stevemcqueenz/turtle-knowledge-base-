I talked about this in world chat a few times. I like the idea of a holy ward but I don't think it makes sense on warlocks. For mages, the Fire Ward ability is part of the fire school, and the frost ward abiliy is part of the frost school, so you need to use a school magic to shield yourself from it. Warlocks use shadow magic and have shadow ward, but they don't use holy magic so they shouldn't shield from it as well to be consistent. My personal opinion is it works better as a priest racial for undead and trolls. Or just a spell that all priests get (Alliance players wouldn't get as much use out of it as their horde counterparts though).
