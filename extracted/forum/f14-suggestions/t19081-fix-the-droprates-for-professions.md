# Fix the droprates for professions

## Post 129724 by Elleshar (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-05T10:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129724#p129724 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When the drop rates for profession recipes are bind on pickup as well as having a droprate of only a few percent (if even that, sometimes lower) and you can only do it once per weekly reset then crafters could run the risk of waiting years for some recipes to drop even with perfect attendance. This is even more true on smaller servers where there are less activity, some items may never see the light of day on those servers.

A most obvious example of this would be twow's custom raid emerald sanctum, where there are drops with 1% and respective 4% for the profession items. My guild has run the raid for over a year non-stop without ever seeing the elixir recipe drop (4% droprate) or the formula for the eternal dreamstone shard (1% droprate). By doing a quick maths on both cases to get it to 99% chance on the elixir of greater nature power we would need to run it for 114 weeks non-stop which is over 2 years and for the eternal dreamstone it's 8-9 yrs to get it within 99% drop. That's longer wait time then the existence of this server project.

It's very clear once you do the math that twow devs didn't think this through at all, luckily there is is a simple fix and that is to increase the droprates.
