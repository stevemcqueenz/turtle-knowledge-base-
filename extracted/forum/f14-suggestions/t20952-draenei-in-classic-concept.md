# Draenei in Classic Concept.

## Post 145728 by Teerack (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-15T15:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145728#p145728 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello~ So I was bored today and I was recently doing quests in stranglethorn and since the servers are down it kinda inspired me to try and put my own spin on how they could do Draenei and Broken in turtle Wow.

So first off let me just explain that this concept DOES NOT involve the Dark Portal opening and has nothing to do with the draenei that we meant in outlands/ones who crashed on the planet(at the exact same time for some reason? :P ) in BC. This is an idea for the draenei that are already living on Azeroth. The Lost ones.

Now just some quick lore recap to know before I explain my concept.

The Draenei on Azeroth
The Lost Ones are draenei that managed to escape to azeroth before the destruction of Draenor. They went on to create small settlements in the Swamp of Sorrows. These draenei are at the lowest level of their devolution, a stage below a broken draenei. These draenei believe that Draenethyst Crystals are the key to their salvation, able to help them from losing their minds further, and some even believe it could be a way back to their original home where they might find a way to be cured. The reason Draenethyst exists is the rift walking magic used to transport the lost ones to azeroth somehow infused the crystal into azeroth and is now growing in the blasted lands and in the swamp of sorrows.

The Crystals
These crystals were kind of an original story mcguffin that blizzard pretty much instantly dropped the second we opened the dark portal, but they are kind of the key to my whole concept. A little bit east of the blasted lands there is Stranglethorn Vale and there you can find another set of very visually similar crystals, The Singing Crystals. They are special crystals only found around the troll temple in the north and the caves/mines/mountains on the eastern part of the zone. They pulse with power and even make music like sounds. Their description kind of reminds me of the naru and are pretty similar in look/description to the Draenethyst Crystals. Both also seem to have some kind of magical effect on the mind with how they are used/described in their quests.

The Moons
There are two moons on azeroth and different races call them different things, but for this topic I will just be using the night elven names. The big moon is The White Lady, or Elune is the goddess of the night elves and a deity of life/light. The second moon is called The Blue Child and often shown as a little blue elf girl who likes to wander and travel the cosmos. Blizzard has explained nearly no lore onto the Blue Child since the first lore for them was written.  One of the only things we do know is that when the moons align there is a moment known as the embrace. When this happens there is some kind of magical resonance in the world. It is part of how the dragons were transformed into the aspects and in the old warcraft manual Garona describes seeing the red moon when the dark portal was first opened. Whenever the moon turns red it is meant to represent when the moon(Elune) is angry and her power is flaring up. So it wouldn’t be much of a stretch to suggest the dark portal was created where it was because of some kind of lunar alignment moment. The destruction of dreanor itself as well was ripped apart when the moons of dreanor were aligned as Ner'zhul used their energies to rip open portals into the twisted nether.

Hakkar
The god of Blood and Death the Gurubashi worship is Hakkar. He was revived in Zul’Gurub and whenever he manifested we have also seen the moon turn red as if Elune is furious. There was also a failed attempt at resurrecting him in the sunken temple in the swamp of sorrows. He is a big red snake with wings who’s blood corrupts people.

Sethe
Sethe is a dreanor god of death and shadow. He is also a big snake with wings just like Hakkar was and similar to Hakkar has blood that corrupts. It was shown in outlands/dreanor that arrakoa exposed to his blood would devolve into flightless bird people. This is important because ogres saw this effect and took this blood to make a bio weapon to use on the draenei and this blood was basically what ended up devolving the draenei into the broken.

Lo'Gosh
Is a wolf god of azeroth that also has connections to the moon. It’s origin is not super important so I won’t get into it but a legendary relic known as the sight of Elune that was made using one of Lo’Gosh’s fangs is basically an unstable conduit for Lo’Gosh’s power and was used to curse Dusk Wood. Rehgar Earthfury a notable shaman character also said this about Lo’Gosh. "Long ago the spirit of Lo'gosh revealed itself to the orc clans of Draenor. To this day his ferocity and feral instincts still serve as a guide to the shaman of the Earthen Ring."

_______

Okay now that that is explained I can get into it!

The main sort of concept for this idea is to put the draenei into a spot similar to the orcs where although they are an alien race they came to this world corrupted and were able to find their true salvation here and a true new home on azeroth.

Zone: The Lost Shore (Level 1-10)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/JCNIUgL.jpeg)

Lore:
One day some Draenei started to get headaches claiming to hear whispers and singing coming from the south. These draenei would migrate to the south from the Swamp of Sorrows as if being called by something. They would eventually find the Azure falls, a large magical lake in the middle of a hidden swampy forest full of crystals and bioluminescent plants. In this swamp there was a large Lunar Stone giving off a pleasant vibration that helped clear the minds of the lost ones more than they had felt in a long time. The lost ones would start to settle these lands with their tents starting to build a new city.

Until one day when the moons aligned and The Embrace occurred. A beam of light would strike down on the lunar stone and the entire forest would sing. A magical pulse coming off the stone. Draenei that were hit with this pulse would start to feel themselves change as their bodies would start to be healed finally free of the curse and restored to former glory. Unfortunately for some draenei they were too far away from the lunar stone when this happened and didn’t feel the full effects of this healing pulse. Those draenei would transform from lost ones into broken. Standing there looking around as their brothers and sisters only a few steps away from where they were standing a broken draenei would look to the sky and scream out with such fury and rage brought out by feelings of jealousy and despair. This furry was so great even the elements would react, shattering the very land they broken was standing on. In the aftermath of this moment the primal god Lo’Gosh revealed himself to the broken and the first Broken Shaman were born.

Since then the broken have formed their own shamanistic society in Furymurk close but separate from the cursed draenei that have continued to live around the lunar stone creating a new city known as Lunador. When The Embrace occurred the beam of light that triggered the curing of the draenei was so great it could be seen in Stormwind. This lead the alliance to send a ship to investigate where they meant the draenei. They offered aid and finding kin ship with the night elves do to their strong connection with the moon ended up joining the alliance.

Lore Hooks:
I basically wanted the draenei to be connected to the lore of azeroth. Lo’Gosh having some of his power touching Dusk Wood it would make sense he be able to sense something going on near by which lead him to revealing himself to the broken. The Draenei have always had tied to the moon with their home being in shadow moon on dreanor and just the overall look of their starting zones I wanted something to tap into that aesthetic, so linking them to the moon and making them worship Elune or maybe in this case The Blue Child would be a nice way to help pull them more into setting. It would also allow for their culture to still have a nice connection to astrology without all the scifi space ships. I was thinking the beam of light from the embrace would have never happened before because the crystals were only growing on azeroth since the draenei came to the planet semi recently. The crystals would have formed over an old long forgotten troll temple to the moon. Some ancient trolls did see Elune as a loa, and in Stranglethorn Vale there is an alliance only quest Moon Over the Vale where you get a tablet that reads.

> The First Troll Legend
>  A Moon over the Vale shines
>  Casting its glow upon the jungle
>  Where proud Warriors heed the call
>  To defend our Nation and sacred grounds.
>
>
>  A Moon over the Vale shines
>  Far above the cries of battle
>  Where blood is spilled
>  Of foe and comrade alike.
>
>
>  And when our brethren pass
>  Into realms beyond the known
>  The soul-spirit hardens
>  Deep beneath the Vale.
>
>
>  And when our brethren pass
>  Into the Mountain's Temple
>  We shall protect their eternal spirit
>  Encased within the holy blue crystal.
>
>
>  And when our brethren pass
>  A Moon over the Vale shines.

So the idea would be the gurubashi would have worshiped the moon as one of it’s loa once, but stopped and abandoned it because it somehow weakened their new god Hakkar as a sorta natural enemy. The similarities to Hakkar and Sethe would then mean that Elune’s light would be able to also weaken/break the curse caused by Sethe’s blood on the draenei when Elune’s power were super charged by the embrace combind with all the Draenethyst Crystals that have formed around the shrine since the draenei first came to azeroth. This also allows for the Draenei to join the alliance with the classes Paladin, Priest, Mage, Warrior,  and Hunter. While leaving Broken Draenei with their shamans as a separate group that may or may not ever be playable(basically allowing dev’s to table the idea of alliance shaman for as long as they want).  I would also think that since these draenei were broken and then fixed maybe that impurity and the magics used to fix them could have altered them to have more colors, so blue and purple tones, but maybe also red and orange tones. I’d also like to see some human like beige and brown tones. Considering draenei are designed to fill the tiefling aesthetic from dnd I think it be a nice touch.

Things we could see in the zone. Basic stuff like naga Invaders, murlocs, basilisk, enraged elements, etc. High Elves wanting to study the stone to see if this could be the secret to curing mana addiction and helping the withered. Night Elf druids wanting to help the sufferings of lost ones while also wanting to research if this could be used to turn a satyr back into a night elf. Some kind of quest line where it shows some efforts to bring the broken back into draenei society but them unwilling to assimilate just yet. Human Mages helping the Draenei get used to magic again. Gnomes/Dwarfs tinkering with draenei impressed by their craftiness.

I would also think that in terms of memories it be a bit variable. Some wouldnt remember their time as a lost one, some wouldn't, some would recall their life on dreanor, some wouldn't, etc. Leave it vague that some draenei could have some interesting memories, but for the most part this is a fresh start for them.
