# Let us change class Tier sets ``freely´´

## Post 107263 by Bingabongo — 2024-11-09T16:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107263#p107263 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Its nice being able to use your Tier sets according to your role, but farming 16 pieces for recolour (Transmoging) or 24 pieces so you can have then for use isnt nice. Farming some pieces is already hard due to % drops, and those pieces arent close to being even 3rd BiS pieces, new players have to dispute then aswell with players that might not want then as an upgrade or for use.

My suggetion is a quest so ppl can switch between then, delivery some itens for each piece and the set piece is reverted to the original piece, so you are able to change. This is a way of not beign entirely free, and give players a better expirience on the begginers side and the ones leaning more towards end game.

Though I agree it would be QoL feature for the players, devs made it very smart in my opinion. Without exchanging, some veteran raiders who raid Naxxramas have incentive to visit old raids even if they won't get any upgrades from BWL/MC. Maybe for transmog, maybe for offspec, maybe for meme spec.
Devs made old raids relevant for collectionists. It's good MMO design too since it keeps people playing their game longer. The downside is that tokens will be more desired than ever before.
