# Playable Hardcore Ghost

## Post 109526 by Anns — 2024-11-23T14:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109526#p109526 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all, hello to you who are reading this.

I wanted to suggest an idea when someone dies in hardcore mode and the character's soul is stuck on the character screen.
Some may have died in a battleground or in some deep water so that you cannot see the grave with another character, and your only choice to be reminded about it is the ghost that sits on the character screen. None else can see your grave or name.

But, how about, a choice to be able to log it once or twice more if not every time, and play it in ghost form and to be seen by everyone.

Doing that, you can haunt other players, confuse new players about it so they can ask questions like "How is that possible?" or "Are you a GM? Where is the <GM> on your name?" or just run along a friend who is leveling/doing stuff in Azeroth and keeping him company as some kind of protector.
Along all that, to be able to communicate with them. For example, answer the new players, or tell anyone who wants to know how you were born (how was the character made), how did you decide on the name, how was your journey through Azeroth all the way up to your final moments and how you are a ghost now doing the (Roleplay) stuff you can think of by yourself. Or simply, to give life lessons - tips on how to journey through Azeroth.

To add more to it, add some cosmetic items when a hardcore character dies so he be able to use when he enters Azeroth as a ghost. Something like "Elune Light" to be cast on yourself or some other player, or maybe "Flower Shower" of some sort. It can be something else other than those two suggestion I mentioned.

I suggested this idea via in-game ticket and the answer I got that made me post this on the forums:

Thanks for reading it all. If you liked/disliked the idea or if you have something to add, feel free to reply.

Also thanks to the Turtle Team for doing all the work they do and for implementing stuff like these that are suggested by players. Keep up the good work!

Good day to you all.
