Right now to level early survival you should craft a lot of dim torches. This recipe is good for one time craft. But it would be better to craft something useful.

Proposal: Survivalists could dig nightcrawlers <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6530> from skill of 1 to skill of 50.

To get them, you need to apply some specific item (box of worms) on any killed ghoul type mob in the world (see "dropped by" in nightcrawlers database page).
Nightcrawlers are somewhat useful to craft fish attractor. They can be used themselves to buff fishing skill. They stack in large quantities. They are not give any huge gameplay advantage.
