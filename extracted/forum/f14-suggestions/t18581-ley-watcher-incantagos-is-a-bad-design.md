# Ley-Watcher Incantagos is a bad design

## Post 126572 by Sirracha — 2025-04-13T05:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126572#p126572 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ley-Watcher Incantagos is a poorly designed boss. This boss is forcing a raid meta that requires a absolute ton of caster dps that most guilds do not have. This boss requires a raid to have 5+ caster dps of every element (fire,frost,shadow,nature,arcane). Stop trying to force people to play caster dps. Caster dps suck and they are boring as hell to play. If you wanted people to play shadow priest, boomkin, ele shaman, warlock then you should have made the class deal more damage and require more than 2 buttons to dps.
