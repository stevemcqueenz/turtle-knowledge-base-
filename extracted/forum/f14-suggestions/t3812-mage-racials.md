# Mage Racials

## Post 22984 by Notawen (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-21T03:32:00Z

Changes to make them balanced are here: [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3847](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3847)

Orc:

Throw Manastone: [30 Yard Range] [20% of Max. Mana cost] [4 Second Cast]
"You throw a ball of your crystallized mana at your target, which explodes to deal <20% of caster's Mana + caster's Attack Power> as physical damage. It is castable while moving. You don't suffer pushback when damaged while casting this."

Dwarf:

Heat Metal: [10 Yard Range] [1,5 Second Cast] [??? Mana Cost]
"You heat the armor of your target, dealing fire damage based on their armor value." (more armor = more damage)

Gnome:

Easy Blink: [Passive]
"Your lower than average mass allows you to use Blink more easily. Your Blink has a 50% reduced cooldown, and a 30% increased distance."

Goblin:

Malicious Intellect: [Mana Cost of your highest usable rank of Arcane Intellect]
"Increases your Intellect by <triple the amount of highest usable rank of Arcane Intellect would>,
but reduces your Spirit, Stamina and Agility by the same amount."
(can't be used alongside Arcane Intellect, can't be casted on others)

Undead:

Sap Vitality: [10% of Max. Mana Cost] [40 Yard Range] [3 Second Cast] (Magical Effect)
"Decreases the Stamina of your target by <10% of caster's Stamina> for 1 minute.
When a worthy opponent afflicted by this effect dies, you get Sapped Vitality for 10 minutes."
Sapped Vitality: "Increases your Stamina by 10%. This effect stacks up to 2 times."

High Elf:

Animate Weapon: [20% of Max. Mana Cost] [1 Minute Cooldown]
"Animate your main hand weapon or staff to fight by your side for 40 seconds.
After the duration or upon its defeat it returns to your hand."
(it has 20% of your mana worth of hitpoints and the damage it does is like you auto attacking)

Human:

Arcane Adaptability: [Passive]
"After being struck with a spell you gain 1% additional chance to resist that school of magic for five minutes.
This effect can stack up to 20 times."
(It only works for one school of magic at a time. If you want to change the type you resist, you need to disable your already existing buff.)

Troll:

Conjure Poison Cloud: [??? Mana Cost] [2,5 Second Cast] [5 Minute Cooldown]
"Conjures a Poison Cloud at the targeted area in a 20 yard radius for one minute.
This cloud applies Conjured Poison to enemies, dealing <???> nature damage every second
for 20 seconds. Conjured Poison can stack up to 5 times."

That's nice ! I think that the gnome racial is a bit overpowered though.
Mage (especially gnome) have the advantage against melee classes, and this racial would increase the gap even more.

In my opinion the counters of the mage are shadow priest / affli warlock / resto druid. A good way to balance those match up (without modifying the balance against other classes) could be to add Shadow, Arcane and Nature ward to the mage skills.
