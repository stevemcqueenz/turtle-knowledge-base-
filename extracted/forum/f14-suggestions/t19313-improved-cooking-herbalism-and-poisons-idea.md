# Improved Cooking, Herbalism, and Poisons idea

## Post 131200 by roogan — 2025-05-15T12:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131200#p131200 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!

As I've been exploring various zones, I couldn't help but to notice a few potential opportunities for adding some small in-game mechanics. The first thought that came to mind was related to specific items that remained game objects without any purpose. Let's take Gloom Weed (<https://www.wowhead.com/item=12737/gloom-weed>), for example. Despite Gordo's confusion, the plant itself may serve as an ingredient for a more complex and equally potent poison recipe. Naturally, the same goes for the usage of Doom Weed.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/02Mw0hcf/Wo-WScrn-Shot-051525-134445.jpg)

On that note, venom extraction from the likes of scorpids and other poisonous species could be just as useful to both rogues and hunters (especially Survival) alike.

When it comes to Herbalism, some lore-related herbs like Bathran's Hair, Bear’s Paw, and Ironroot Seeds have played a crucial role in night elven medicine. Their properties can potentially add a new type of flask/potion, a different tea mix for restoring Energy or Focus (if it's going to be implemented as a Hunter resource in the future, that is), or simply as extra Cooking ingredients that exist in traditional medical cuisine.

And lastly, I will get straight into pointing out other neglected plants such as hops and okra. In my humble opinion, players with Cooking should be able to use them for brewing alcoholic beverages that may boost a specific stat at the cost of reducing  another one (after all, one does experience that IRL when a hangover kicks in). Not always a pleasant outcome, but still makes the game a bit more immersive. Dwarves will most probably enjoy this the most, as you can already imagine.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/FRHfd6Y0/Wo-WScrn-Shot-051525-141338.jpg)

Thank you for your time.
