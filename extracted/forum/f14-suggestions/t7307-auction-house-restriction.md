What do you mean by "griefers", exactly?  I frequently split stacks of less than 5 into individual things before I sell them.  That aside, I'm not sure I understand the problem.

When doing a search in *Aux*, you can right-click "Auction Buyout (per stack)" and have all the listings sorted by their individual item buyout price.  For example, if you search for "Raw Brilliant Smallfish", you'll find numerous stacks of 1, 20, and everything in between.  Right-click on "Auction Buyout (per stack)" to change it to "(per item)", then left-click on it to sort the listings.  Now, just like that, the 20 stack **and** the 1 stack are sorted by whatever has the **lowest** buyout price **per-item**!  ![👍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f44d.svg)
