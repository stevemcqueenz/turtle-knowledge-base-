# Roleplay Modes

## Post 9467 by Kazgrim (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2021-02-20T22:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9467#p9467 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The possibility to join ‘evil’ factions such as the scarlet crusade, scourge, and burning legion has been a frequent suggestion and desire of a subset of players, specifically those interested in the roleplay opportunities that arise from such a feature. Being that we are a RP-PvE realm, and have implemented a number of alternative modes such as hardcore and mortal, the concept I suggest is a new ‘roleplay’ mode.

The pitfalls of joining evil factions from a lore and game mechanical pov limit us as developers to make them functional factions that players can gain reputation with, since they simply were designed by blizzard to be the bad guys you fight. Going against this grain is problematic since the game revolves around this concept of you vs. them. Therefore, a solution to this is a new alternative mode, a roleplay mode, where the player can become friendly with a specific faction while costing several tradeoffs.

Note, this mode is purely for roleplay purposes, for characters / alts that do not intend to raid or experience the majority of PvE or PvP content. It’s designed for a specific subset of players who are interested in the roleplaying opportunities that arise from being treated as a friendly protagonist of an otherwise evil and hostile faction.

The way this mode would work is:
 -The player becomes friendly to the specific faction of the mode
 --Ex: Scourge mode  Friendly to Scourge
 -The player cannot group with players outside his mode
 --This prevents the player from running dungeons and helping players outside their mode from cheesing dungeons
 ---Disable them from entering dungeons may also work
 -The player cannot trade or send mail with players outside their mode
 -The player cannot use the auction house
 --This is meant to discourage chest farming,
