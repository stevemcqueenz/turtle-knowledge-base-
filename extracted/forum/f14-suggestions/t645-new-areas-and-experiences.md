# New Areas and Experiences

## Post 3277 by Avarice — 2019-07-02T02:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3277#p3277 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey guys,

So, just spitballing here and throwing some ideas out.  Open to all opinions and viewpoints that you beautiful turtles might have in response.

So, I love the server for the Vanilla experience of course, but Turtle Wow is special because it has something "extra".  That being Survival Skill, turtle mounts, cosmetics, and all that fun stuff.  At first I thought that was all there was to it.

Then, upon hitting level 60 and starting to group up with my other bad-ass server mates, I had my first encounter with Turtlhu.
WOW!  A NEW encounter on a vanilla server.  What a unique way for us to get engaged, practice teamwork, and get some cool loot.  Granted, Turtlhu's mechanics and loot table are kind of a mash-up of world bosses, but still.

Then, I realized that there's other original content being introduced, like the Caverns of TIme (!!!).  With Classic coming out soon, which is sure to cause a slight dip in server population, I think these kinds of initiatives are a bold move on the part of the turtle devs; a strong concept to bolster player interest and maintain a unique experience here.

So I got to thinking about what other sorts of "secret" / "inaccessible" areas and content could have some potential, whether it be in the form of World boss encounters, PvP events, Zones to explore, Roleplay areas to access, or even entire new instanced dungeon or raid content!

Orgrimmar Ring of Valor
Old Ironforge
Ironforge Airport
Hyjal zone
Karazhan Crypts
Stormwind Sewer Instance
Quel'Thalas Island

Obviously there's a LOT that could be done with just 1 or 2 of these areas alone in terms of content, if it interests our shelled overlords.
What do you guys think about providing access and content to some of these zones, and what other areas / ideas do you think have potential for more original content for the server in the future?
