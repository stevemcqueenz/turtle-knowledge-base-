# [Aspect of the Wolf] Melee Hunter

## Post 57607 by Casetofon — 2023-08-16T12:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57607#p57607 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As it is known, at the moment, the only "melee" abilities that the Hunter Class has are Wing Clip for a slow, Raptor Strike for extra damage on a next attack ( like the warrior Heroic Strike) and Mongoose Bite, which is only enabled once the hunter dodges or parries.

I would Like to suggest the following changes to the hunter class :

Given the fact that Aspect of the Wolf disables use of all ranged abilities, this criples the "dream" of melee hunter in a big way.

Here are some changes that I would propose, in order to somewhat increase the viability of the melee hunter but without turning the "melee" hunter in to "just another melee dps but with a pet" type of thing.

The changes are as follow:

While aspect of the Wolf is active some ranged abilities are converted in to melee abilities :

Serpent Sting becomes a toggle like an aura and triggers a mana cost once it deals damage, but also receives the ability to be stacked 3 times

Trueshot is converted to an equivallent of the Legion Survival Hunter Spell "Flanking Strike", but instead of a short 5 second cooldown, I should be a 45 second cooldown with a chance to reduce it's cooldown by attacks while under the Quick Shots effect.

Multishot is converted in to a 20 or 25 second Cleave ability that is only usable while your pet is within 5 yards of you.

Or instead of the multishot conversion to a melee cleave ability with the pet, allow us to use Explosive Trap with 3/3 Talents in Entrapment and 2/2 in Trap mastery, or combine the 2 in to a 5 point talent in the survival spec which at 5 points will allow the use of Explosive Trap for aoe and Immolation Trap for single target.

Please tell me if these changes would be a good ideea or argue why they are not a good ideea.

Thank you!

Edit 1 : While Aspect of the Wolf is active, Rapid Fire grants 20% melee attack speed.
