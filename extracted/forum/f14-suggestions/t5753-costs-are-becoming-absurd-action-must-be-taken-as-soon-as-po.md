# Costs are becoming absurd. Action must be taken as soon as possible or they will continue rising.

## Post 36083 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-02-25T15:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36083#p36083 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The server is having a surge in population again, growing 1000 members in less than 2 months, and most importantly the players of the last surge have finally become level 60 and started raiding. The world of vanilla wow is limited in how many resources the players can obtain from it and it really started reaching the breaking point. Farming became too weak and playing became too costly for the average twow player.

Herbs are expensive, yet herbalists can't make gold. It sounds paradoxical but it's true, a herbalist on a trip will barely find any of the useful herbs because they're all heavily competitive. There are too many herbalists and not enough zones to spread out in. So felwood is full of people looking for gromsblood and finding only golden sansamn, blasted lands is full of people looking for gromsblood and finding only firebloom, plaguelands are full of people looking for plaguebloom and finding only arthas' tears. It's bad design to have that many herbs but only 3 or 4 of them are useful.

Recipes are not varied enough. It's a good step in the right direction to change many of the material costs to something else.
- Replace the one mountain silversage in major healing potion recipe with two golden sansams
- Replace two dreamfoil in major mana potion recipe with two icecaps
- Replace three dreamfoil in greater arcane elixir recipe with three arthas tear
- Replace one gromsblood in Mighty rage potion recipe with one firebloom
 ***"But won't this make the expensive herbs cheaper and me as an herbalist will lose gold?*** Actually not. Yes the expensive herbs will become cheaper, but all the other herbs you were skipping or vendoring will now sell for a respectable price and you will earn much more gold overall.
