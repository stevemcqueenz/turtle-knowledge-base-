Hello,

To improve the viability of warlock tanking without giving them too much that could be exploited for PvP I have a suggestion.

When a warlock has a wrathstone active, make the effect of shadowburn (the 5 second debuff that returns a soulshard if the target dies) have an effect that is equivalent to a taunt. While the increased threat generation from fire spells is great, warlock tanks could really use a taunt.

For context, I just tanked a black morass on my warlock and found the lack of a taunt frustrating.

Thank you!
