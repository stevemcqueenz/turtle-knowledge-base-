# Resilient Shadows Buffs and Changes

## Post 147026 by Arthurice — 2025-08-19T15:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147026#p147026 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Resilient Shadows boils down to a 5/10/15% "Affliction Dispel Protection" Tier 2 Talent that costs up to 3 Talent Points. Not very good due to how low the numbers are and lacking any secondary functions for the point investment. My suggestion would not be to simply buff the numbers more, although that would be kind of nice if it were slightly more comparable to Rogue's Vile Poisons but I understand that Vile Poisons is a deeper Talent in it's respective tree for a different class. Instead, I'd really want it to apply to more things while giving it an interesting secondary effect.

Rename it something along the lines of "Resilient Magic" while making it affect ALL Warlock debuffs AND buffs, so it'd now also provide it's dispel protection to Demon Armor, Shadow Ward, Unending Breath, Detect Invisibility, and Immolate. It'd also provide a secondary effect of 33/66/100% dispel protection for ALL magic effects, including other players buffs, elixirs, flasks, scrolls, etc, against your own Spellstone. This would completely invert the secondary effect of the Spellstone, where you'd lose the ability to cleanse debuffs from yourself in order to be able to use it for the Magic Shield without removing your own valuable buffs, which I feel would be worth the talent point investment.

But what do you guys think? Is this a good idea at all, how would you change Resilient Shadows in your opinion?
