## Post 127674 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-20T14:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127674#p127674 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I get that twow is focused on leveling rp pve etc. But I don't understand why they do so so little to improve pvp content. We have three different BGs that people play again and again. They are time-tested proofs of concept that people don't mind doing the same pvp "instance" over and over.

So rather than this output of 5-6 new lvl <50 dungeons that players MIGHT run through ONCE, why not fix SGV and BR + rewards so they're actually playable and worth the time investment? Why not add Eye of the Storm, which also works really well and could fit within the CoT theme with just a few adjustments?

You bother going through and adjusting basically every 1-60 item EXCEPT the honor gear. Why?

Adding new pvp content means you do not have to go over deadline by 6 months or however long it took to fix the bugged bosses in K40. Players fight themselves. Twow now has a non-negligible community that's been asking for new pvp content since well over a year. Compared to designing whole new dungeons which honestly I don't see the demand for, pvpers would be happy with relatively little in comparison and we clearly do not mind running the same content a thousand times. The return on investment for just a little **well-thought out** extra pvp content would be huge.
