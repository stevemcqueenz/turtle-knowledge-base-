Had a quick conversation in the hardcore lounge and I'm a fan of pets also becoming **'hardcore'** with you. What this means is if you allow your pet to die, you can **no longer summon or resurrect them**; if they die, they're gone for good.

Well, mostly for hunters anyway. Warlocks don't really care for their minions in the WoW universe, so instead if one of them dies they won't be able to summon another for a while (give it a good, solid cooldown).

Done this way, players will have to be much more careful with their pets or risk leaving themselves exposed for some time.

From Akalix in #hc-lounge:
> "It's a suggestion I've heard from time to time, certainly a cool idea."
>  "Would be worth a forum post. Perhaps something like "True Bond" glyph, where pets are permadead as well."

We had the following ideas for Warlocks. I have added the name of the people on Discord who have provided the ideas. Given the fantasy of Warlocks there are a few options we could further explore:

When your demon dies (not sacrificed) it will:
 1. Trigger a long cooldown before you can summon it again (DAKKA);
 2. Burn your soul for 50% max hp (Bonkmeister);
 3. Burn your soul for 100% mana (Silkroad);
 4. Forget all it's skills so you have to learn them again (Ibuks);
 5. Reroll it's name to symbolise enslaving and summoning a "new" demon (DAKKA).

Personally I am a big fan of all of them except the big cooldown. Loosing your pet needs to have a big impact on your Warlock without pausing the game too much. Focussing on a big CD simply means either you log out, or you park your Warlock somewhere and idly wait for the CD to pass. While point 2 till 5 immediately puts in a lot of danger, which you want to avoid at all costs, but if anticipated can be managed with potions and carrying spare grimoires in your bag.
