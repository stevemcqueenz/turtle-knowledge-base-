# Lv1 trade skills for purchase

## Post 9044 by Daddym0e — 2021-01-10T02:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9044#p9044 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Skinning, First Aid, Cooking.  The tools necessary to utilize these trade-skills are already available in at least a few, in not most, of the starting-area zones from vendors. Obtaining the trade skill at Lv1 is a treacherous and deadly (many deaths involved) trip.... just to have to go BACK through the gauntlet of mobs to gain their benefit.

Why not, for a *SMALL* Pay-Pal fee (Small, as in *cheap*. It may not bring you the funds you need, nor deserve, for your time and dedication. But, it WILL appeal to many starting players and those creating new 'toons'.), make them available to players?

You already have the instructions set up to make the donation using the email address specified.

...also... a bit of clarity on those Pay-Pal instructions will help. Screenshots and such. I hesitated trying because I thought it would be a "procedure" requiring many steps. It was not. It really was as simple as 1,2,3.. I do not think that *much* of your player base knows just how easy it is.
