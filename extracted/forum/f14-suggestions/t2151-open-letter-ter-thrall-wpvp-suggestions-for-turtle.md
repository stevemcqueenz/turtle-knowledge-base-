((Hello Turtle devs/community! I’m here speaking for <Blacktooth Grin>, an open world RP-WPVP guild that’s been zugging on various servers since 2006. We’re now looking for a home following a vote to boycott Blizz after the recent lawsuit, and while a few of us have been playing on Turtle, many have been disinterested on account of the lack of open world PVP. Whether or not we end up moving to Turtle, I’d like to share a bit about our history, what’s made RP-WPVP so engaging for us over the years, and some suggestions our members brainstormed for how Turtle might advance the cause of bringing Azeroth to life in a more warlike direction without forcing PVP on those not interested.

A brief overview of our history: we started on Scarlet Crusade, moved to Venture Co when RP-PVP servers first opened, and then played on Grobbulus during Classic. Our clan embodies the Dark Horde, bearing no loyalty to Thrall and continuing to fight the Second War. Our home base has traditionally been Stonard, from where we patrol our borders and launch raiding parties to torch Darkshire and towns beyond, our Reavers taking special delight in the slaughter of civilians and crippling the Alliance’s ability and will to wage war. On VeCo our rivals <Keepers of Stromgarde>, <Midnight Reveries>, and other guilds both attended scheduled battles with us and would respond in force when they saw /worlddefense light up; we maintain a strict set of rules of engagement to try to avoid defeats turning bitter. We ran a series of campaigns, chained storyline battles accompanied by forum RP threads where we set the scene for why these battles took place and our members & foes shared their characters’ experiences during the conflicts. On both VeCo and Grob we created coalition alliances with other Horde RP guilds involving IC and OOC agreements to support one another and further each other’s RP and PVP goals. On both servers, bored Alliance knew that a surefire way to find a fight was to kill a Stonard Grunt and wait for Grin hearthers to boil out of the inn.

Over the years, our members have found a home with us because of the joys and immersion RP-WPVP provides that can’t be found elsewhere. Whether we’re ambushing  Alliance adventurers who trespass in our Swamp before settling down at the campfire or assisting our Dragonmaw allies by laying siege to Menethil Harbor, our activities make the World of Warcraft feel more real for our members and foes alike, the WPVP bringing the world to life the exact way a BG doesn’t. We essentially RP as NPCs and create IC problems for others to solve -- there’s a satisfaction to seeing Alliance questers who were not out for PVP have to band together to defeat our raiding parties, and we’ve been a villainous foil for Good-aligned Horde RPers to set themselves apart from. Combat using the WoW combat mechanics allows for engaging long term rivalries with our IC foes where otherwise Horde and Alliance RP guilds might have little interaction. RP-PVP means living in the world, not just leveling through it and moving on or living in an instance away from the rest of the community. RP-WPVP is a niche for players who love to zug and yet love to cultivate a positive server community, unlike your average WPVP griefer.

OK, on to our proposals for Turtle! We understand that Turtle is not a PVP server and that a majority of the current community do not actively seek out PVP. While our dream would be a PVP server with Turtle’s love for the lore and a living Azeroth, we still have some ideas for how voluntary PVP can be encouraged in ways that allow players to make Azeroth feel more immersive. This is a grab bag sorted by importance/ease, with the most doable and helpful changes near the top and most time-intensive & out there at the bottom.))

- Make sure DHKs aren’t too punishing on the honor grind, but are more a visible badge of dishonor. Let war be a viable option for players who want to RP Azeroth’s many war criminals and evil characters.
Provide illusion rewards for achieving honor ranks or dishonor ranks. Provide entrylevel, subpar PVP gear rewards unlockable through dishonor ranks.

- Make WPVP possible at all levels and encourage players to congregate for WPVP through WPVP “event” days that alter rules in certain zones, including level limits - players over the max level of the zone can’t flag there that day to ensure fair PVP. Provide PVP quests in the event zones to get kills on the other faction. This could involve complex zone objectives (suggested below separately as we came up with many) or be as simple as daily “kill 10 players in <zone>” quests.

- Allow instant rez, skipping rez timer when resurrected by a player (if not already implemented). The rez timer is an important tool for encouraging PVPers to stay alive, but it’s important to be able to revive your teammates if you take the field or a truce is called.

- Push up honor for world kills compared to BG kills but with rapidly decaying returns for repeat kills (resetting daily). It shouldn’t allow for honor grinding anywhere close to BG levels, but if world objectives or other opt-in WPVP mechanics are implemented, they should be valuable to fight over.

- Incentivize flagging even when not actively PVPing (extra resource node drops, bonus xp, etc).

- Allow interested players to RPPVP as Azeroth’s villains through a mercenary system for neutral factions mirroring the diplomat system that allows FFA flagging a la Gurubashi Arena by talking to a faction NPC. Become hostile to all flagged players except others in your group or dedicated to your subfaction; gain rep through PVP kills in certain areas relevant to those factions; be friendly to all that group’s NPCs; unlock PVP illusions for getting kills while representing a subfaction. Subfactions - defias brotherhood, blackrock orcs & dragonmaw, grimtotem, dark iron dwarves, venture co, shadow council / burning legion / burning blade, bloodsail and other extant factions (centaur tribes)

- Allow guilds to pick zones for their home territory, give PVPers bonus honor for seeking out their rivals’ territory and killing that guild in that area & give that guild bonus honor for killing in defense of their zone too.
 **WPVP objectives in the model of EPL towers or BC objectives like Halaa, Hellfire towers, overview**
- Make sure only some are active at a time to concentrate players together. Group all zones into low/med/high (10-24, 25-39, 40+) brackets, and every day one zone of each bracket has their objectives active & advertised by ingame barkers in the zone and other inactive zones of the same tier, and in major faction hubs (org, sw)

- All zones’ objective rewards increase over time for the losing side and decrease over time for the winning side to incentivize balanced participation. Rewards should include zonewide buffs for flagged players, bonus honor, and gold.

- Produce spawnable NPC forces (like raiders or Lok/Ivar AV spawns) as a catchup mechanic to help if the other side has been dominating for too long.

- Rewards for capturing are relevant to the players of level for those zones & cosmetic as well. Cosmetic awards should be tradeable (not soulbound) to encourage alt creation and involvement by endgame players in lower level WPVP.

- Zone objectives include a mix of player kill objectives and NPC-kill/non-kill objectives, though these can only be fulfilled while flagged. This will encourage participation to get things started even if enemy players are not currently contesting an area.
