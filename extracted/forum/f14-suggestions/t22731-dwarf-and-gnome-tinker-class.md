# Dwarf and Gnome: Tinker class

## Post 158411 by Acepox (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-05T05:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158411#p158411 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I've expressed here ([viewtopic.php?p=158382#p158382](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158382#p158382)) how a conversion of paladin into death knight could be implemented to grant this class gameplay to the Horde but with a theme that fits the Horde's flavors.

Similarly, I think a shaman conversion into Tinker could be done to balance the playing field and grant this gameplay to Dwarves and Gnomes using the following formula:

Resto > Medic
Enh > Machinist
Ele > Artificer

Water totem > Hydraulic device
Air totem > Compressor device
Fire totem > Combustion device
Earth totem > Piston device

Reincarnate > Failsafe Protocol
Ancestral spirit > Defibrillate
Ghost wolf > Mechano-tank
Lightning shield > Magnetic field

Rockbiter Weapon > Piston-assist module
Flametongue Weapon > Thermal-edge module
Frostbrand Weapon > Cryo-core module
Windfury Weapon > Gyro-swing module

Lightning Bolt > Arc coil
Chain Lightning > Gigavolt charge
Earth Shock > Electromagnetic cannon
Flame Shock > Flamethrower blast
Frost Shock > Coolant discharge
Stormstrike > Hydraulic Slam

Fire Nova Totem > Landmine
Searing Totem > Flame turret
Healing Stream Totem > Medical Station
Mana Tide Totem > Hydration Station
Poison cleansing totem > Decontamination station
Disease cleansing totem > Sterilization station

Cure Disease > Sterilize
Cure Poison > Decontaminate
Lesser Healing Wave > Cauterize
Healing Wave > Surgery
Chain Heal > Deploy Nanobots

Given this, they would be able to equip guns (like bows have been given to priests) but they would not have autoshot. And of course, this new identity would mean matching armor sets so they don't always look totally out of place wearing shaman gear.

Thoughts?

I find major advantages of this approach, including the Death Knight conversion, is that you balance PvP and give the Horde extra tanks which it often lacks, and the Gnomes their healers, all on a sound foundation of lore, while not having to engage in the excruciating task of designing and balancing entirely new classes.

And, as opposed to simply giving H and A  Paladin and Shaman which typically means Undead Paladin and Dwarf Shaman, with this method, two races on each side get the additional class.
