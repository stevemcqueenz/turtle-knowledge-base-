# New STV Quest Chain

## Post 15204 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-01-03T06:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15204#p15204 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I had an idea for a quest chain in Stranglethorn Vale, I'm just about finished with the zone on my Shaman and it's bittersweet because it's my favorite place to level.

It's called "Sea Creature Mastery"

Part 1: Kill 10 Piranha
Part 2: Kill 10 Sharks
part 3: Kill 10 Threshadon
Part 4: Kill Elite mob, something like  Gahz'rilla from Zul'Farrak

This would be easy to implement because the mobs could be placed in the water and not take up jungle space where there are already a bunch of mobs.  Additionally, it would add some new variety to STV by being water focused, and by focusing on mob types the zone doesn't currently give attention to

I understand the desire to flesh out less developed zones such as Arathi; in fact, I was thinking that it would be good to make Stromgarde a dungeon hub similar to Scarlet Monastery.  There could be a cave entrance in the Ogre portion of the castle that leads to a cavernous Ogre dungeon, and a staircase in the Syndicate portion that leads to an underground portion of the keep similar to Stockade in Stormwind.  The purpose of adding dungeons here is to give people incentive to enter Stromgarde, that way they have a reason to do the already existing Stromgarde quests since they will already be passing through with a group.
