Well, firstly it wouldnt be a skin for the forsaken, it would be a remodelling / revamp, since they use different animations. That would mean for instance that the racial ''Canibalism'' would look weird as the high elf model doesn not have that animation.

Second, lore-wise, the forsaken has a free will to join whoever they want to join. I'd say that it is reasonable that a raised high elf who gets her free will back would like to go with her people, its not given that she follows Sylvanas.
Also, in the Turtle Shop you can buy Dark Iron Dwarf & Scourge Cultist skins for the Alliance races, even though they are highly frowned upon by the races of the alliance. For Horde you can get Blackrock Clan, Sandfurry Trolls etc. Which is ALSO frowned upon by the races of the horde.

If you look at it from a cosmetic point of view, it would just add some nice customization for an existing model/race, just like the rest of the skins from the turtle shop have done. I dont think that you should add it to the forsaken race for multiple reasons stated above, but instead we as a community could provide some ideas to make horde more desireable.
