# Arathi Highlands Improvement

## Post 16027 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-01-18T21:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16027#p16027 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I generally like Arathi Highlands, but it is a bit undeveloped and simplistic in its design; I feel that with improvements it could become a more enjoyable zone, so I propose the following two changes:

 1. Expand the troll presence in the zone by taking the model for the tree forts that the Arakkoa use in TBC and placing them throughout the zone with troll mobs using them.  This will breakup the monotony of the open aspect of the zone by adding in larger objects throughout it, and it will give the opportunity to create new quest lines.  Additionally the buildings from Amani'alor could be used by forest trolls in the zone

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/67344-arakkoa-war-path-veil-reskk.jpg)

 2. Expand the Ogre presence int he zone by using the Ogre buildings from Blades Edge Mountains.  Same reason as for expanding the troll presence

![Image](http://moco.net/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/WoWScrnShot_080716_181553.jpg)

 3. Add sunny skyboxes to the zone so it isn't always dreary and gray; something like the blue sky from mulgore could be used, and alternate between the the current dreary skybox and the sunny one to make the zone dynamic as time passes.  Currently the zone feels like Seattle 24/7.
Since Arathi is far north, it shouldn't always be sunny like Mulgore; perhaps 25% of the time it's sunny and 75% it uses the original skybox

![Image](https://images.wikia.com/wowpedia/images/thumb/9/9c/Arathi_Highlands.jpg/1200px-Arathi_Highlands.jpg)

![Image](http://pm1.narvii.com/6954/bcbf699818101c5c6baedbeff88b95f00abb29b8r1-1920-1080v2_uhq.jpg)
