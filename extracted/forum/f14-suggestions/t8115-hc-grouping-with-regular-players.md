# HC grouping with regular players

## Post 52185 by Houseperenolde — 2023-07-03T12:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52185#p52185 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So there has been some gripes about HC characters clogging spawn points, being off in their own little world, among other things.

I guess the question I have is; Why don't we allow grouping between HC and non-HC characters? Reasonable limits like level range could be maintained so it's not any easier of a challenge.

I think this would clear up a lot of the complaints I hear about two different groups of players sharing the same server.

## Post 52189 by Pepesmite (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-03T13:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52189#p52189 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The answer is very simple. This is done in order to comply with the rules for HC characters.
• You will be able to trade items and gold only with other Hardcore characters as long as the difference between your levels isn’t higher than 5.
• You will be able to group up only with other Hardcore characters as long as the difference between your levels isn’t higher than 5.
• Hardcore characters can not trade, nor buy on Auction House.
• Hardcore characters can use mail, but with no attachments.

## Post 52266 by Ishilu (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-04T07:10:00Z

The original post was about HCs clogging spawn points, not teaming up in dungeons.

Fact is, it is annoying when HCs and normals camp the same quest mobs (sometimes even without any means of communication, due to language barriers), especially named mobs like Zalazane, Tazan, that earth elemental in 1000 needles, elite beasts in STV and many others.

HCs and normals teaming up for an extended period of time or even for dungeons is obviously a bad idea, but giving players camping a single mob with a respawn timer of several minutes a means to cooperate is probably a good thing.

My idea for this would be giving HCs the ability to "break" their team-up rule for maybe a minute or two (long enough to kill that mob) and with a long cooldown so they don't overuse it.
