# Urgent - fix BG que system

## Post 100971 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-09-09T20:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100971#p100971 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear devs,

The last months have made it beyond obvious that the twow que system has serious flaws. As far as I know, at some point there was a decision to enable BGs to start with less than max amount of players. To my understanding this was partly to make it more difficult for premades to que.

Well, the last months on tel’abim have been a disaster in terms of BG player balance. Games go on for minutes or even full length with 3v6s, 5v8s, etc. DESPITE players being in que. Considering the rock-paper-scissors class balances of vanilla wow, even games of 4v4/5v5 are notably imbalanced depending on which classes the factions get.

Pardon my language but I suggest you prioritize to remove your twow soup of a code for the BGs and copy-paste the original code. Starting games with less than 10v10 or 15v15 is a fundamentally bad idea. Further, for Tel’abim to attract players interested in pvp we need to have the possibility of premades.

In addition to this issue, there have been discussions of ghost queuers - players with a character on the opposing faction queuing BG and then dropping out, leaving said faction with an empty slot. I suspect this possibility is partly a consequence of the que system, but I can be wrong.

Regardless, I urge you to revert to the original code for BG ques as soon as possible.

Thanks
