# Engie's Niche Items List

## Post 4090 by Qixel (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-09-26T18:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4090#p4090 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I make niche items that the devs can add as drops or stuff if they want in my free time, and it was recently suggested to make a forum post with my ideas so that they don't get lost and I can just add to it as I come up with them.

SO HERE GOES

Rules:
- Items cannot use spell effects that do not already exist elsewhere, including numerically.
- Items must buff a niche playstyle while not benefiting already good ones.

> **Wand of the Inquisitor**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Ranged    Wand
>  67 - 125 Holy Damage    Speed 1.50
>  (64.00 damage per second)
>  Durability 65 / 65
>  Classes: Priest
>  Requires Level 60
>  Equip: Increases damage done by Holy spells and effects by up to 29. (Nature's Embrace equip effect)
>  Equip: Increases the critical effect chance of your Holy spells by 1%. (Circle of Hope equip effect)

The Wand of the Inquisitor is built with the Smite Priest in mind. In addition to buffing damage done by smite, holy fire, and holy nova, the wand itself deals holy damage for maximum flavor (Wand of Eternal Light deals holy damage, and is in fact the only one I've found that does so).

If I could have, I would have implemented the critical chance in a way that didn't boost healing spells, too, but I feel that 1% crit chance on healing spells is less than what a healing priest would want, anyway.

Changes: Increased the holy damage from 16 to 29.

> **Outnumbered Commander's Reach**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Main Hand    Dagger
>  86-161  Damage    Speed 2.40
>  (51.45 damage per second)
>  -1000 Armor
>  +15 Strength
>  Durability 75 / 75
>  Classes: Warrior
>  Requires Level 60
>  Equip: Increases the block value of your shield by 32. (Dreadnaught Leggings equip effect)
>  Chance on hit: Reduce your threat to the current target making them less likely to attack you. (Black Amnesty CoH effect)
>  Use: Increases the block value of your shield by 235 for 20 sec. (2 Min Cooldown) (Glyph of Deflection use effect)

This one was harder to get to work, and I'm still not sure it's where I want it to be. It was built with a shield slam dps in mind, so I had to make it good for shield slamming, but not good for tanking. Originally it took the form of a shield with low armor and high block value, but I was unable to elegantly come up with a way to reduce threat (punishing tanking, but lessening the inherent threat of shield slam for dpsers), so it took the form of a weapon instead. It's pretty damned wordy for what it does, unfortunately.

Oh, and the reason it's a dagger is twofold; one, no one gets to be the best shield slammer by virtue of racial skill, and two, it would look like a spear, but still use the stabbing animation used by daggers. Who doesn't want to be a badass using a phalanx stance to destroy your enemies? :D

The warrior restriction was added because I was worried backstab rogues would have a field day with an extra slow dagger, and they don't need the help. xD

> **Arcane Archer's Shortbow**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Ranged	Bow
>  44 - 82 Damage	Speed 2.0
>  (31.17 damage per second)
>  Durability 75 / 75
>  Classes: Hunter
>  Requires Level 60
>  Equip: Increases damage and healing done by magical spells and effects by up to 60. (Staff of the Ruins equip effect)
>  Equip: Your normal ranged attacks have a 4% chance of restoring 200 mana. (Beastmaster/Beaststalker set bonus)
>  Use: Infuses you with Arcane energy, causing your next Arcane Shot fired within 10 sec to detonate at the target. The Arcane Detonation will deal 185 to 215 damage to enemies near the target. (2 Min Cooldown) (Arcane Infused Gem)

This is obviously geared towards benefiting Arcane Hunters, a more mobile playstyle than the traditional Aimed Shot users. The bow itself aims to relieve the mana issues spawning from casting the titular spell every 5 seconds (never have a chance for mana regen to start by implementing the set bonus from the dungeon set while increasing the hunter's spell damage in a slot it normally wouldn't, with the Arcane Infused Gem slotted in for an exciting cooldown.

The weapon has a fast attack speed both to synergize more with the chance on hit effect, and also to dissuade the idea of using Aimed Shot with it.

One of the items I'm happier with design-wise.

Changes: Changed attack speed from 1.5 to 2.0 and updated damage accordingly after realizing arrow cost would be absurd as it was.
Changed spell damage from 26 to 60 after realizing that, since only hunters can use it, the overall value of spell damage is less compared to normal (one spell per 5 seconds instead of all the time like most casters).

> **Spellblade of the Quel'dorei**
>  Binds when picked up
>  One-Hand	Sword
>  42 - 80 Damage	Speed 1.50
>  (40.67 damage per second)
>  +10 Intellect
>  Durability 90 / 90
>  Classes: Mage
>  Requires Level 60
>  Equip: Chance on melee attack to increase your damage and healing done by magical spells and effects by up to 95 for 10 sec. (Lightforge/Soulforge set bonus)
>  Equip: On successful melee or ranged attack gain 8 mana and if possible drain 8 mana from the target. (Black Grasp of the Destroyer equip effect)
>  Equip: Increases the damage absorbed by your Mana Shield by 285. (Mage PVP Glove equip effect)
>  Chance on hit: Next spell cast within 4 sec will cast instantly. (Hand of Edward the Odd CoH effect)

Originally, I was going to make the mage niche something benefiting arcane, but I realized that's more limited by lack of attention given to it by Blizzard than anything. Instead, I decided to play around with the idea of a battlemage playstyle, revolving around using instant cast spells while hitting with a melee weapon during the global cooldown period. It was a challenge to design, because I wanted to make it rewarding to be in melee combat, but not so much to just stay at range like normal. HoEtO immediately came to mind as a fun effect mages would love access to. Being in melee is obviously more dangerous, so there's a buff to mana shield included to make the battlemage a little more durable. Finally, the mana drain ability from BGotD is interesting as well as flavorful for a mage branching out from just spells. It will also work on wand hits, unfortunately, but I hope the chance for a PoM or spell damage proc will entice people into melee regardless.

Changes: Removed the 10% mana reduction to instant cast spells after learning it would not work, and replaced it with the soulforge set bonus. I actually like it better this way! :D

> **Dreadmage's Spire of Tenacity**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Two-Hand	Staff
>  137 - 243 Damage	Speed 3.40
>  (55.88 damage per second)
>  480 Armor
>  +75 Stamina
>  Durability 120 / 120
>  Classes: Warlock
>  Requires Level 60
>  Equip: Gives you a 50% chance to avoid interruption caused by damage while casting Searing Pain. (Warlock PVP Gloves equip effect)
>  Equip: When struck in combat inflicts 13 Fire damage to the attacker. (Essence of the Pure Flame equip effect)
>  Equip: When struck in combat has a chance of causing the attacker to flee in terror for 2 seconds. (Dreadmist/Deathmist Set bonus)
>  Equip: When struck has a 3% chance of stealing 120 life from the attacker over 4 sec. (Demon Forged Breastplate equip effect)
>  Equip: When struck in combat has a 1% chance of dealing 75 to 125 Fire damage to all targets around you. (Skullflame Shield equip effect)

Boy, oh, boy is this one wordy, but I forgive it because each of these effects on their own don't do much, and none of them really benefit your standard warlock. As is plain to see, this item only shines when the warlock is under attack, and the niche it's designed for hopes to do just that as it tries to tank. Tanklock's main issue in my mind is survivability, due to being a smushy cloth class, and a caster to boot. Searing Pain allows the tanklock to generate massive threat, and the reduced pushback allows you to lessen the burden your enemies have on your cast bar (PROTIP: Pair it with the pvp gloves or a paladin with improved concentration aura to have searing pain ignore pushback entirely!). The absolutely massive stamina and armor bonuses will be well-served, because the warlock is unable to dodge, block (what's that?), or parry while casting spells (which they will of course be doing often), meaning the tanklock must eat all non-miss attacks coming their way. The plethora of procs on being hit give a lot of interesting outcomes while you hold the attention of your enemies, with the base 13 thorns rounding it out.

> **Duelist's Moxie**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Held In Off-hand
>  Classes: Rogue
>  +20 Agility
>  +10 Stamina
>  Equip: Increases your chance to dodge an attack by 2%. (Boots of Avoidance equip effect)
>  Equip: Improves your chance to get a critical strike by 2%. (Breastplate of Bloodthirst equip effect)
>  Equip: Increases your attack speed by 20% for your next 3 swings after dealing a critical strike. (Flurry rank 3)
>  Equip: 2% chance on melee hit to gain 1 extra attack. (Hand of Justice equip effect)

Like with mages, rogues are fairly straightforward and lack a 'niche build' in the usual sense; anything they do differently is minor at best, and buffs to that would buff the usual builds. Instead, what if there was a rogue who didn't dual wield? The Duelist's Moxie hopes to enable the dreams of those swashbucklers by giving enough interesting things to offset the loss of a second weapon.

Since the duelist only uses one weapon, they should be more competent with it than normal, so there's a higher chance to critically strike when you hit, and the addition of flurry makes it more rewarding when you do. HoJ's effect is always exciting, and fits perfectly into the idea of a duelist outmaneuvering their opponent. Finally, what duelist isn't prepared to make their opponent look clumsy by artistically dodging all of their attacks?

> **Cathedral's Sanctuary**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Off Hand	Shield
>  2539 Armor
>  46 Block
>  +5 Intellect
>  +10 Stamina
>  Durability 120 / 120
>  Classes: Paladin
>  Requires Level 60
>  Equip: Increases damage and healing done by magical spells and effects by up to 18. (Tome of the Lost equip effect)
>  Equip: Increases your chance to block attacks with a shield by 2%. (Drillborer Disk equip effect)
>  Equip: Increased Defense +17. (Deathbone Chestplate equip effect)
>  Use: Taunts the target to attack you, but has no effect if the target is already attacking you. (10 Sec Cooldown)

Originally, the niche I was going to help for paladins was retribution, but I fully believe the addition of Holy and Crusader Strike will let them stand on their own. Instead, I opted to help protection paladins, because shockadin is just intrinsically broken at this point in the game (30 second cooldown on signature spell, why Blizz ;^; ).

Cathedral's Sanctuary (on top of having a *totes* baller name if I do say so myself) aims to patch the holes protection paladins seem to have, namely lack of defense and, of course, a taunt. I don't really have much to add besides notes on designing the shield, since prot paladin is pretty well known, but little bits of trivia don't seem that important in the grand scheme of things. But, for those who care, I'll spoil tag some stuff. :P

Design Musings Before I found out I could theoretically attach a taunt to the shield, it had the Lightbringer set's 8 piece bonus tacked on. Afterwards, though, it seemed excessive and I removed it.

At one point, I considered making it a libram with some +def and the taunt effect, but I decided that, not only is that kind of boring compared to a shield, it may be too good in comparison to the other items in the niche series. So it remained a shield.

> **Chieftan's Ancestral Defender**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Off Hand	Shield
>  2539 Armor
>  46 Block
>  +8 Stamina
>  Durability 120 / 120
>  Classes: Shaman
>  Requires Level 60
>  Equip: The radius of your totems that affect friendly targets is increased to 30 yd. (Restoration talent/Earthfury set bonus)
>  Equip: Increased Defense +17. (Deathbone Chestplate equip effect)
>  Equip: Deals 5 Fire damage to anyone who strikes you with a melee attack. (Cloak of Flames equip effect)
>  Equip: When struck in combat has a chance of returning 350 mana to the wearer. (Surging Mana effect)
>  Equip: When struck by a harmful spell, the caster of that spell has a 5% chance to be silenced for 3 sec. (Jagged Obsidian Shield)

It's clear that shaman tanks were intended to happen at some point, but Blizzard failed to follow through. From what I've heard of those bold enough to play it (including some heroes on this very server!) it has very little issues with threat due to rockbiter weapon and earth shock, but there's a lot of weaknesses given the overall lack of support. So, this shield hopes to improve on an overall basis instead of focusing on anything in particular, while still keeping a shamanic flavor to it.

The added totem radius is intended to help the shaman with mobility, requiring less mana to replace totems as the fight takes them away from where they started, and overall just allowing the shaman more freedom of where to fight (being the restoration 10 point talent, most shamans likely already have this, and I don't feel a single talent point is that big a deal). Each of the remaining effects call to mind a different element aiding the shaman in battle. The spirits of earth grant sturdiness in the form of +defense, the spirits of fire punish those who would harm the shaman, the spirits of water grant the shaman mana, and the spirits of air protect the shaman by silencing their enemies.

> **Elune's Fury**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Two-Hand	Staff
>  112 - 171 Damage	Speed 2.60
>  (54.42 damage per second)
>  +18 Intellect
>  Durability 100 / 100
>  Classes: Druid
>  Requires Level 60
>  Equip: Improves your chance to hit with spells by 2%. (Bloodvine Vest equip effect)
>  Equip: Increases the critical hit chance of Wrath and Starfire by 2%. (Pristine Enchanted South Seas Kelp equip effect)
>  Equip: Increases the damage done by your Starfire, Moonfire and Wrath spells by 10%. (Moonfury rank 5)
>  Equip: Chance on landing a damaging spell to deal 100 Shadow damage and restore 100 mana to you. (Blade of Eternal Darkness effect)

This was, in all honesty, the most difficult of the cycle for me to design, simply because a lot of what I wanted it to do benefitted resto druids at the same time, and they are undoubtedly the primary druid spec. I wanted to make it focus on mana regeneration more than anything, and put less emphasis on 'oom' and more on 'boom', but with the exception of blade of eternal darkness' effect, everything just buffed restoration at about the same rate, and that was against the rules I set for myself.

Instead, I opted to design the moonkin weapon with the future arcane mage playstyle in mind, with a charge-spend style. It's not what I had in mind, but I feel like it may be good enough regardless. <3
