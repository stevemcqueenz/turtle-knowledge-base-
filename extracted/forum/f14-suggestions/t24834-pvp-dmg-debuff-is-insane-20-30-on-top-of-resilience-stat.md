# PvP dmg debuff is insane 20-30%, on top of "resilience" stat

## Post 174812 by Halfgeek9 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-04-11T12:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174812#p174812 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

The new pvp gear has -crit damage and -dot damage taken stat and it's quite high, completely negating crits for many classes with 1.5x modifier, so a crit is same dmg as normal hit.

It massively reduces DoT damage pressure, and gives pvp sets a huge advantage vs pve tier sets.

This is OK, somewhat if the goal is to reduce burst, mission accomplished.

Then the devs throw a 20% and 30% dmg debuff on top, and now basically you destroy balance even worse.

Many classes rely on short burst windows, due to cooldowns, CC limitations, in order to succeed. Because once their burst & CC is gone, they become sitting ducks. While some classes are designed more for endurance, sustained dps, self healing mechanisms are basically buffed big time.

Remove the pvp dmg debuff. The new PvP gear is already a big step in right direction, though it needs some stat tuning or more creative bonuses. Having -crit dmg -dot already achieves goal of less burst damage. Do not double dip with another stat debuff.
