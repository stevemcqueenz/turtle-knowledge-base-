# Glyph of local economies

## Post 82222 by Tekamthi (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-21T21:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82222#p82222 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm relatively new to both wow and turtle server, but with some experience in mmo's and modding other games, and share from this perspective.  I played wow in retail beta, the first month after launch, and then never again til arriving on turtle about 2 months ago. I particularly love the fan-driven content for this old, well loved game, and especially the glyph's for different game modes, all happening in the same space.  Turtle's underlying flavor of encouraging a thematic, lore focused leveling experience, all while tolerating the vast community of min/maxers and more conventional levelers, is particularly important to what makes this server special, I think. I have both unglyphed and glyphed alts, and though I'm not an avid RP'er, particularly enjoy playing with the slow n steady mode enabled, the travel and exploration this requires etc.

A more thematic, localized economic system might improve this experience even further:

proposed effects on local economy Glyph'd player
- all glyph player's banks are separate, local instances.  Player can see all stored items at any bank, but only access items stored at the local bank in which they stand.

- Glyph Player can remotely request that an item from any bank be mailed to another location via localized mail system (more on this below)

- mailboxes are also local instances for items (more on this below).

- Auction house items record which auction house they're deposited into, though this data is ignored for unglyphed player. Glyph Player can see and bid on all AH items, but must specify faction-aligned delivery location at time of bid (or this can just be hearth bound location) and pre-pay postage fee. Glyph Player Purchased item must be picked up via modified mail delivery system (see below).

- Glyph player is charged for gryphon flights on a per-item-carried basis, so that it is cheaper to mail an item rather than fly on Gryphon with it.

- Glyph player cannot send items via mail summon, nor receive items via mail sent from a mail summon.  Cannot access items via bank summon (but can view & request they are mailed to fixed mailbox location).  Can bid on items via AH summon, but not place items for sale.
Modified mail system for glyph player
All item mail sent to or from a Glyph'd player must be moved by player driven system, as follows:
- Every mailbox offers all players that can reach it (not just Glyph'd) a time-limited quest to move all waiting mail at that location to the next nearest mailbox en route to mail's final destination. If time elapses without delivery, mail items stay at the pickup mailbox until a player successfully completes the mail quest for that leg of the trip.

- Mail quest Player is PvP flagged once quest is accepted and until delivered, quest abandoned, or killed.  If killed, corpse is lootable for a short time, and looter given opportunity to continue the mail delivery or reject it back to it's last mailbox.

- For the purposes of item-by-mail interactions with a glyph'd player, the origin mailbox is the mailbox via which sending player is accessing the mail system, and receive mailbox is where receiving player is hearthbound

- glyph player can see text of all mail at any mailbox anywhere in the system, but only retrieve an item from the local mailbox in which the item sits

- Mail quester does not actually carry each individual item, just a "mail sack" which holds them unseen to the quester.

- Mail quester cannot use gryphon or teleports while mail quest is active

- Successful mail delivery quester is paid a portion of postage fees corresponding to all accumulated mail they carry for that leg of the trip, and earns faction reputation local to the mailbox at which the quest originated.

- All inter-faction mail must travel through a neutral location

- Glyph Player can pickup mailed items at whichever mailbox it sits en route to specified destination, if full delivery is taking too long

I conceive of it as more of a thematic reward -- you're right, if each bank were copied as-is and instanced, there'd effectively be more storage space available to player.  But that was not my intent; leaving this "bonus" storage as a reward of sorts might fit well, or it could be mitigated; for example each local bank could be made smaller than the default bank. Personally I'm rather indifferent on this point.

Just like turtle mode encourages moving between quest zones to progress through some of the level ranges, this glyph would encourage more travel to retrieve important items, maybe give player a reason to buy a local white item rather than the green far away in stormwind, etc., for those players that enjoy the journey of WoW over optimized play.  I'd hope it might also create some interesting secondary markets, encourage more player-to-player trade, though these kind of effects would depend largely on uptake.  Provided there *was* sufficient uptake, the moving of mail as outlined might liven up world PvP as well.

Your initial take here strikes me as likely to be a common, majority perspective; this glyph would probably be activated by a minority of players, and serve a niche interest.  Would there be a sizeable enough minority to make it worthwhile? Could be: Some of the other time-tested MMO's that pre-date WoW, still around after decades have thrived on player-driven economic/logistics systems (UO), as have some that have come afterwards as well (Albion).  I'd love to see turtle take a stab at player-driven logistics & economies of some form, and the choose-your-own-leveling-experience via glyphs seems a great way to implement it.
