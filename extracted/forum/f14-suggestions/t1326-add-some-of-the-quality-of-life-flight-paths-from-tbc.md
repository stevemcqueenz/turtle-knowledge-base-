## Post 8136 by Roxanneflowers (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-10-14T02:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8136#p8136 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My personal inclination is to decline adding any more flight points.  The generic rule for vanilla was 1 flight point per faction per zone.  That then gave you all the incentive(s) you needed to get out and actually move around the game world, rather than relying on taxi services for transportation.

The increase in flight points ironically makes the world "smaller" since there's less travel from flight points to destinations.  Taken too far (*cough* Cataclysm *hack* *cough*) you wind up with a balkanized mess in which 85% of travel is done on taxis, you have 6+ flight points per zone (which was just stupid) and the overall game world basically "shrinks" down to a collection of tiny areas around the nearest flight point(s).

Or to put it another way ... it's called World of Walking for a reason, and not just because of Barrens Chat.
