# Add a Teleport Orb in Alah'thalas

## Post 115504 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-05T15:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115504#p115504 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Many people avoid going to Alah'thalas and the Thalassian Highlands due to the remoteness and time it takes to fly or boat there.
You literally have to either wait for two boats (with the time it takes to get there and back), or fly 5-10 minutes from Stormwind (which is the common meeting point in the Alliance), followed by getting there on foot being impossible whether you're high or low level due to having to go through the Plaguewood, which is the place with the highest level mobs in the Eastern Kingdoms.

My solution would be to add a teleportation orb in Regency Spire that leads directly to the Mage Tower in Stormwind, just like it was before 1.17.1 and leave the boat route as just a simple access to Darkshore, which is not bad at all.

Please take this in count.
