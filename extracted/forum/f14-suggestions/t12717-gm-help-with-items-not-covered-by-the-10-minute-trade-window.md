# GM help with items not covered by the 10 minute trade window

## Post 84674 by Liljon — 2024-01-14T13:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84674#p84674 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey all.

I want to begin with by saying that I understand why GMs refuse to help with mishandled loot in groups and raids. After all, there can be a lot of loot drama over nothing, players forget or are unable to show evidence, and the fact that most items can be traded once anyways solves a lot of the chores that GMs normally had to do before. However, I would like to suggest - and I am confident that I can speak in the names of a lot of players - to give GMs the legislation to be able to intervene in cases where item drops are not covered by the 10 minute window and that loot was mishandled. These are primarily Token items or trash drops in raids (think of items like the T2.5 tokens, or basically any and all trash loot in raids, f.e. Cloak of Draconic Might). Of course, proper evidence should be provided (screenshots / videos) for GMs to be able to service players properly, and if there were multiple issues in one raid, put them all in one ticket instead of spreading it all out.

Mistakes happen, sometimes small, sometimes big. This is one of the few cases where players are unable to rectify a mistake without wasting their time. Therefore I proposed the suggestion above. I hope you all can agree with this.

Peace.
