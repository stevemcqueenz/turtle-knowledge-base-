# Bloodsail Buccaneers Flight point

## Post 18897 by Zaccar20 — 2022-05-28T21:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18897#p18897 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtles,

I would like to propose giving the Bloodsail Buccaneers their own flying point in southern Stranglethorn Vale.
I joined the Bloodsail Buccaneers (as some others may have) during the leveling phase and it's a really bad experience to die every time you land due to the overpowered guards of Booty Bay.
Additionally, especially as an Alliance player, it is very frustrating to walk all the way to Duskwood to the next flight point.

As an alternative solution, the guards next to the flight points could simply be removed. This would at least make it possible to fly to the quest drop-off point without being attacked.

What do you think?
