# [Suggestion] New "Master Craftsman" Mark for Self-Found Crafting Challenge

## Post 102986 by HippoMilo — 2024-10-12T20:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102986#p102986 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been enjoying playing Hardcore mode in WoW Classic and have been thinking about a self-found challenge with an additional twist. The concept is that I can only equip items that I’ve crafted myself, creating a "Master Craftsman" type of challenge. After discovering Turtle WoW, I thought I'd propose an idea for a new mark that could cater to this type of gameplay.

The idea: Introduce a new mark that allows players to learn all professions, but with some restrictions to maintain the challenge.

Restriction 1: You can only equip items that you personally craft, meaning no equipping items from loot drops, quests, or other players.
Restriction 2: You cannot use the Auction House or trade for items/materials from other players. However, you can still sell your crafted items to others (maybe only allowing trades where you send crafted items but cannot receive anything except gold in return. So we can still interact with the community, maybe even add a few special items that can only be made by craftsman, so the community seeks them out).

This would create an interesting dynamic where I’d need to farm materials, gather everything I need out in the world, and craft every piece of gear, potion, and enchantment for myself. It adds a solo grind element while emphasizing the mastery of professions.

Endgame: Once you reach the endgame, there could be special requirements where you must have crafted everything in your chosen professions. Completing this could unlock the ability to craft unique, special items that can only be acquired this way, making the journey even more rewarding and providing exclusive endgame content for Master Craftsmen.

Thoughts on the idea and its gameplay potential? Would this be something that fits the spirit of Turtle WoW, or could it create interesting new challenges for those looking for a more self-sufficient, crafting-focused experience?

I would really to just see a self found mode, where you can only group with other self found players, can learn all professions, and can't use mail, trade and the auction house (yes you'll have to make your own pots, enchant your own gear etc).

I guess "Master Craftsman" could be an additional challenge on top of this, but just a non-hardcore self found mode with all professions available would be great.

I understand some people have some balance concerns with people having more than two professions active, but I don't think this is a big issue, because you could:

 1) Restrict people to two "active" professions that can only be switched at a profession trainer (perhaps allow all professions to be used inside cities and rest areas only).
 2) Self found players, with all professions, can't group with non-self found players anyway.
