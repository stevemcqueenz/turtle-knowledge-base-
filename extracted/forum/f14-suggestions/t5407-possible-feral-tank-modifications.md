<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32985#p32985 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Feel free to add to/comment on this list.

 1. Cast Barkskin in bear form.  I can cast OOC in bear form for some reason, but not Barkskin.

 2. Make Brutal Impact talent also reduce the CD of bash by 5/10s with tp's.

 3. Add a fear break or modify beserk's singular fear break.  As is, tanking Nef, Ony, Gluth, etc without FW/tremor tot is extremely difficult.  Nef is an outlier, obviously.  Just an additional fear break mechanic would be nice

 4. Possible modification to idol of brutality or new idol suggestion.  Make it so idol of brutality adds an additional swipe target, or reduces the rage cost of savage bite or something.  Possibly make an epic version drop off trash or a boss in the upcoming raid that does the above mentioned effect.  OR kill two birds with one stone, make it useable in form with a 2-3 min CD and have it do a fear break?!

Let me know your thoughts fellow Bear tanks!  And the devs too.  I'd like to hear from anyone with thoughtful criticisms.
