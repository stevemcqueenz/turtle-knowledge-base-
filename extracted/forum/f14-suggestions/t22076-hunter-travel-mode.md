# Hunter "Travel Mode"

## Post 153118 by berdal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-25T11:07:00Z

My brain was farting around while watching some wow content, and came up with an idea that would be cool, but not affecting gameplay/class balance significantly in any negative way.

I do believe hunter pets grow as they level up to 60? I cooked up the idea while brainstorming how to appreciate and take more advantage of the bond between the hunter and it's pet.

Let us mount up on our pets for a 40% movement boost from level 30. A spell called something like "Ride Pet" or something. Unfortunately some mounts wouldn't be eligible cause it would look weird (those winged serpents for instance) I fear, and perhaps the pet growth has to be boosted or adjusted a little to accommodate the appropriate size.
