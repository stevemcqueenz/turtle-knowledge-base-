## Post 8854 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2021-01-02T17:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8854#p8854 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

> **Valignoth wrote: Sat Jan 02, 2021 4:02 pm**
> From my understanding, Fel magic is created when light and void collide.  I would assume to understand the Fel would require an understanding of both void and light. I hardly think its lore breaking since warlocks have tools at their disposal to deal with opponents wielding shadow magic it would make sense that they have tools to counter the light as well.

This assumption is something I have never heard of before in the lore. Warlocks are born when mages (in the case of humans, gnomes and undead) or shamans (in the case of orcs) take their thirst for power too far and delve into extremely taboo magic (fel/demonic magic) which is different in origin and form from the shadow magic priests use (that one stems from the priest's religion instead of demonic contracts) but ultimately the same school.

What this means is the warlock and shadow priest can both conjure a shadow spell to damage their enemy, the warlock gets this power from the fel while the priest has it spiritually. Both shadow spells will feel the same to the enemy because they are both from the same school just like searing pain and fireball will feel the same to the enemy because they are both fire school spells. Though searing pain might make him a little bit angrier :p

So according to the wiki, the fel was created when light and void collided, but that's a retcon that happened long after Vanilla WoW's release. It might still apply to Vanilla WoW and they just didn't mention it yet at the time, but it still doesn't mean that someone who draws their magic from the fel needs an understanding of both light and void. Since those are not schools of magic to begin with, holy and shadow are. And obviously warlocks don't know anything about holy magic.

Back to holy ward, I think we need to apply the same logic that Blizzard applied to fire ward and frost ward. Fire ward is a fire school spell, and frost ward is a frost school spell. So, to get protection against a certain school of magic, one needs to be proficient in that school of magic (Except paladins getting fire, frost and shadow resistance auras for no reason whatsoever what the frick blizzard?)

TL;DR: Warlocks are not proficient in holy magic, so they can't cast the holy equivalent of the ward spell. That would be the job of a holy priest.

I also think holy resistance on armor should be a line that's never crossed. But a ward spell is an entirely different thing. It's a temporary shield against holy damage, created with holy magic. Holy magic can't be mitigated by something else, so not resistance on armor, but logically it could cancel itself out. Using defensive holy magic to absorb the offensive holy magic. Does not break the 'can't mitigate holy magic' problem.
