# Any race able to learn another race's racial ability (Half race)

## Post 96846 by Sark21 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-06-12T02:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96846#p96846 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This idea was sparked from another project and i figured I'd throw it in suggestion box.

So I was out role playing with some players and I came across a half human / orc who "enraged" which got me thinking.

This idea is very much supported by the lore of Warcraft as there are half orcs / half draenei such as Garonna, half oger / half orc such as Rexxar, Half human / half high elf such as Arator etc... These characters in lore are able to use both of their races heritage abilities, Garonna enrages, Rexxar's half oger skin bounces spells off/ make him eligible to be an Oger warlord, and Arator's natural magical ability from his elf side, which makes me wonder why doesn't turtle implement a chain of quests to be able to harness their other half's racial ability.

Now this wouldn't give ALL the racial perks like a human selected character who (stick with me) is half tauren can not warstomp and stun. However, they are able to have an increased health pool or pick herbs faster. A Orc selected character is half high elf and has the mana regain ability. Now not all races can work like no one can get the undead racials BUT the undead can be half any race.

Don't bring up racial compatibility as far as the game is concerned a tauren and human are humanoids xD. What do you guys think?
