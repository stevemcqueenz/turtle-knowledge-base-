# Death's Stable, Duskwood

## Post 158005 by Acepox (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-01T01:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158005#p158005 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I always found the Twilight grove in Duskwood was quite the useless eyesore, only ever becoming relevant much later in the game and then only briefly.

As we know the dead were raised in Duskwood by the dark energies from Karazhan that made Deadwind pass, I think it would be interesting to build a storyline in the grove with the concept of a dark rider (Lord Evil! It is said (by Sven) that they conspired with Morbent Fel, yet as of now they remain uninvolved) using it to raise skeletal horses, and even trying to exploit the Dream Gate to create an undead dragon.

So the questline would consist of preventing that and end up with the vanquishing of this elite big bad.

Would require adding a manor building, stables, and a few other things. Could draw on the BfA witch coven theme as well!

## Post 158006 by Acepox (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-01T03:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158006#p158006 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The story would begin with the investigation, tying the three undead Big Bads from Duskwood.

 1. Cursed Reins: Stalvan Mistmantle drops Cursed Reins when you have completed the prereqs to his kill quest. Turn in to Jonathan Carevin.

 1b. Runic Letter: Morbent Fel drops Runic Letter, same criteria. Talk to Sven, who confirms he saw the Dark Rider give it to Fel. Same turn-in.

 1c. Half-digested nightmare: Stitches, on death, leaves a half-digested but somehow living and neighing horse. Interacting allows you to extract the half-digested neighing horsehead. Same turn in.

 2. Runic encryption: Carevin gives you a torturing poker to "convince" a defias shadow mage (southwest Duskwood) to decypher the Runic Letter, revealing exchanges between Fel and the Rider regarding protection of his operations in Twilight Grove by Fel's army of the dead.

 3. The Grove of Death: Carevin sends you to report to Commander Ebonlocke, who mentions that may be where missing Night watchmen may have been taken to and sends you to investigate and try to find survivors.

 4. Death's secrets: After fighting your way up the hill and into the grove, you scout out the Dark Tower, the Dark Manor, and the Stables.

 4b. There may be hope: Ebonlocke tells you to look for survivors. You find an injured night watchman in a pile of corpses. He tells you they're trying to raise a dragon and that his buddies have been turned into undead cavaliers. He dies on quest completion.

 5. No time to lose: You report back to the Commander on a 5 minute timer, she pledges to support you in the assault.

 6. The Watch strikes back: You return to an RV point in the Grove and the Commander appears along with watchmen in the bushes.

Outside the manor:

 7. Fel's remnants: You are to wear down the defenses by killing hordes of skeletal champions and avengers (12 each).

 7c. Death's Stables: You are to kill the skeletal horses and their riders, cursed watchmen, and recover the cursed reins. (8x)

In the Dark Manor:

 8. Death's witches: You are to eliminate the witches (forsaken females in black garbs and black witch hats) that assist the Dark Lord. (8x)

 8b. Cauldrons of horror: recover the cauldrons used by the witches.

 8. Knights of darkness: you are to take out the Dark Lord's officers. (6x)

By the portal:

 9. Imminent Doom: Interrupt the reanimation ritual carried out by the Dark Lord and the Witch mothers. You fight the witches and the Dark Lord retreats to his Dark Tower. Commander Ebonlocke assists you along with Watchmen.

 10. The Dark Lord: Fight your way up the tower, kill the Dark Lord. Elite, flanked by guards. Difficulty requires group.

 11. Duskwood pacified: Take Ebonlocke's commendation to Stormwind, win blue trinket (Ebonlocke's Commendation) passive chance to summon a watchman on hit) and a blue weapon: Dark Lord's executioner, Witchmother's cauldron stirrer, Deathrider's scythe, Revenants' dagger, or skullpiercer (crossbow)- all have black/unholy appearance / visual effects, involve lifesteal, extra crit or extra shadow and frost damage.

RP: whenever you accept quests 7, 8, 10, Watchmen run in and start fighting undead mobs (except if they're already spawned). Not enough to trivialize the content, of course. They also throw torches at buildings to set them ablaze.

Throughout the quest chain, mail rewards include the Night Watch chest, legs, gloves and boots. For cloth, a witch hat and black robe, gloves and boots, for leather a black bandit mask and the black defias set models for chest, legs, gloves, but better stats of course.
