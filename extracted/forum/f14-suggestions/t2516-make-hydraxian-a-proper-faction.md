Turtle wow is expanding on vanilla in every positive way.

I would like to further suggest a small expansion to the Hydraxian Waterlords. The faction currently serves the purpose of giving "expanded access" to Molten Core. How about giving some unique reputation items at exalted based around the elemental theme to make their faction relevant long after people are otherwise done with Molten Core?

My suggestions for these items would be:
epic wand recipes (enchanting) , DPS, healer, perhaps also a specific magic type dmg wand
Trinkets/rings/cloaks: 1-2 for each blacksmithing, leatherworking and tailoring
For alchemy, some potion that would be useful throughout the endgame. I'm not sure.
