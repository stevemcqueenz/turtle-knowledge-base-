# Arena Mark of Honor turn-in for experience

## Post 81031 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-13T16:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81031#p81031 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Wow PvP is when you level your character to 60, gather gear, suck a bit and get ranked gear.
The problem is that you practically not interested in playing PvP before lvl 60.
People reasonably afraid of twinks, but they also don't want to spend time on something which don't gain you experience.
Everything that don't give you experience is secondary activity if you are not lvl 60.

Thats why there should be a way to promote low lvl pvp and grant experience to participants.
Bloodring has minimum amount of people to start and has minimum duration.
Assuming that this system should start of low population and low amount of participants, then it should be reward from Bloodring.

I understand that people should not level by playing bloodring *alone*. People should hang out in the world doing quests and enjoying great world made by turtle devs.

Thats why I propose to add bloodring repeatable quest with consumable reward.
This consumable or just buff increases experience gain from killing mobs by 30-50% for 15 minutes. It should not increase quest experience, because it is easy to collect completed quests and turn them only when you get this mark.
It should be possible to get reward even from 1 Arena Mark of Honor.
If you win, you get 45 minutes of bonus xp. If you lose, you get only 15.

This quest should be available from warcallers in main faction cities. Somewhere near the bank, so people won't miss this opportunity.

This way people will combine leveling in the world and PvPing in bloodring.
