Hello! A big fan of your project and would like to know if there are any plans to improve the gameplay features and rework some talents of the classes?
Yes, this can hurt the balance, but I think the game will become more interesting and deeper if the classes get such iconic spells like:
Holy pal - beacon of light (Any heals you cast on party or raid members will also heal the Beacon for 100% of the amount healed. Only one target can be the Beacon of Light at a time. Lasts 1 min.)
Tank pal - avenger's shield (Hurls your shield at an enemy target, dealing Holy damage and then jumping to 2 additional nearby enemies)
Juj pal - avengine wrath or divine storm (Call upon the Light to become an avatar of retribution for powerful buf)
Arms war - blade storm (Become an unstoppable storm of destructive force, striking all targets for Physical damage)
Fury war - heroic leap (Leap through the air and slam down on all enemies within 5 yards of the target area)
Demon lock - metamorphosis (Turn into a demon for improving functionality of many lock abilities)
Destro lock - chaosbolt (Unleashes a devastating blast of chaos, dealing a critical strike for chaos damage)
Balance druid - force of nature (Summons a stand of 3 Treants for 10 sec which immediately taunt and attack enemies)
And other ultimative interesting iconic skills for every class.
I think it will make the game less balanced, but more deep, which is the most important. I also believe that these gameplay features on the classic server will be of interest to many new players.
Readers, what do you think of these sentences? And I hope the developers will take into account these ideas. =)
Thank you for your attention!
