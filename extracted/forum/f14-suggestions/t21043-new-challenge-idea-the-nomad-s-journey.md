# New Challenge Idea: The Nomad's Journey

## Post 146331 by waterfowl — 2025-08-17T02:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146331#p146331 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Experience the world in all its glory: You cannot use flight paths (outside of the areas you can only access via them) You cannot use mage portals or teleportation. You cannot be summoned. Your hearthstone has a 24 hour cooldown. Basically, you gotta go everywhere manually.

Incentive: your mount and training costs 50% less.

Dungeons: to remedy any issue of being stuck in a dungeon, you get an item that teleports you to the entrance with a 1 hour cooldown.

Reward: The Nomad title and unique mount
