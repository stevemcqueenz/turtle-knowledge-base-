# More Freedom to the Transmog System

## Post 92249 by Mcdm — 2024-04-07T03:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92249#p92249 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As we all know, Tmog is the true endgame. It's not about conquering challenges with friends or overcoming adversity through planning and coordination.

IT'S ABOUT LOOKING GOOD WHILE DOING IT!

It's about standing on top of the org bank or SW fountain and catching yourself in people's target-of-target. THAT is the true goal we all strive for. Or at least I do   smiling_turtle

And I have succeeded.
I had a theme in mind, every piece in my mog compliments that theme, the colors match perfectly and all this is done with a unique combination of items from multiple sets of gear. There is just one little problem:
It's only possible in the Dressing Room as two of the pieces are unavailable to my class. Specifically: a piece from a hunter tier set and a 2-Handed Sword.
(Shammy btw. And don't say "Just go play hunter   turtle_tongue  " because my mog has pieces that are only available to a Shaman!)

My request is this: Give us some other means of adding pieces to our Tmog collection other than equiping them. Perhaps a spell not unlike disenchanting that destroys the item but adds the mog for us to use. (Just spit-balling)

I'm not asking for gameplay changes. I shouldn't be able to actually USE another classes tier set, and I don't think you should be able to mog an item into one that uses a different animation set (i.e. 1-handed mace to a polearm), but the current equip-to-get system is limiting in a way that I don't believe benifits anything. It is not arguable that some of the best looking, highest detail gear in the game are the Tier sets. Give us another reason to WANT to go and get them! If we can get it into our inventory we should be able to use the mog.

I say let the Warlocks have plate Tmog to be a Death-Knight!
Let the Paladins have Priest Tier 3 for a Halo and Wings!
Give the Warriors my Shammy T2 shoulders to match their THUNDERFURY, BLESSED BLADE OF THE WINDSEEKER!
And let me be the Tribal Wind-Samurai I was always meant to be.
Cheers!
