# Explicitly ban erp in public channels like /s and /me

## Post 44200 by Xerilin (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-05-03T07:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44200#p44200 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Role players are kind of used to being trolled, but an issue has come up that I feel should be adressed. There have been a few instances of people doing erp (erotic role play) in public channels, even in other guild's guild bases. By public channels I mean the usual rp channels /s and Emotes. It is not quite clear in the rules, whether this is punishable or not, because /s may or may not be considered a public channel. To me, it is. Thus, overly vulgar content in it should be punishable (don't need examples, we've all been under tents...) and so should erp.

Since this is a game played by children and also, plainly, by players just not interested in watching it, I suggest a clarifying rule that bans erp from public channels like /s and /e. Public erp makes people uncomfortable and prevents them from doing other kinds of rp there. This is especially harmful, if it happens in their own rp guild's guild base.

I'm not here to hate on erp, so I would kindly ask any comments to not do that either. Erp is fine. Whatever rocks your boat, as long as only consenting adults are involved and you don't do it publicly. Obviously, most people are already doing it in private channels, but whoever apparently can't be bothered to, should be made to do so.
