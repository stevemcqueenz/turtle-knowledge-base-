# Thunder Clap - simple warrior change

## Post 153183 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-25T17:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153183#p153183 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

I would like to suggest to give the warrior a better aoe tank ability by removing the target limitation (currently 4) of thunderclap, or increasing the affected targets with the talent Improved Thunder Clap (6/8/10).

Example with ability change:
> Blasts up to 4 nearby enemies with thunder, dealing a total of 309 damage split evenly among them, and increasing the time between their attacks by 10% for 30 sec.
>  Cost: 20 Rage

Then with Improved Thunderclap 1:
> Blasts up to 6 nearby enemies with thunder, dealing a total of 371 damage split evenly among them, and increasing the time between their attacks by 10% for 30 sec.
>  Cost: 19 Rage

And Improved Thunderclap 2:
> Blasts up to 8 nearby enemies with thunder, dealing a total of 433 damage split evenly among them, and increasing the time between their attacks by 10% for 30 sec.
>  Cost: 18 Rage

And Improved Thunderclap 3:
> Blasts up to 10 nearby enemies with thunder, dealing a total of 494 damage split evenly among them, and increasing the time between their attacks by 10% for 30 sec.
>  Cost: 16 Rage

That way only improved thunderclap would significantly change the ability but als it would be better against 1,2 and 3 opponents than 4+, because the damage is split evenly to all affected targets.

> If removing the limitation **instead**, abuse could be prevented by adding "if the target is already affected by the Thunder clap debuff, it deals 50 or 75% of the damage, but retains the threat of the full effect"

Cheers!
