# Expanding Survival Rest Items (for RP)

## Post 15413 by Mindboggler — 2022-01-06T09:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15413#p15413 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Had a bit of time to play, and I've had an idea that's been rattling around in my brain. I believe that the tents could really turn out to be an amazing RP tool. Now let's take the idea of tents and remove, well, the tent. What are we left with? It's a small zone in which you gather and accrue rested EXP. Now let's recontextualize it as something new...

What if it were say, a bar counter. How about a picnic basket and mat? It could even be a smattering of pillows and hookahs, a poker table, a vendor's stall, a warlock's summoning circle, ect. These are all wildly different settings that all serve the same purpose, a place for people to gather and interact.

Instead of everyone gathering by the tent for a nap, how about stopping by that bar we keep passing? Hey, that mage just conjured a patio set. Perhaps he has wisdom to share?...
![Image](https://usercontent.one/wp/kirileonard.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/LTL_intro_tavern_robin_web.jpg)

The examples could go on and on, but the point is the same. This could be the most vital RP driver this game has. And I know that sounds like a gross overestimation, but I see infinite potential in this system.

It could even prop up the server. If there were different tent skins you could donate to acquire, I'm sure a lot of people would be very interested in getting their hands on them.

(Anyway, these are just some thoughts I had that I needed to write before I doze off. Ciao for now.)
