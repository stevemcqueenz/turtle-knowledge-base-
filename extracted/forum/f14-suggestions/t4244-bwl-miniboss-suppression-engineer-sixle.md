# BWL Miniboss: Suppression Engineer Sixle

## Post 25341 by Oonadruidqueen — 2022-09-17T20:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25341#p25341 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all, this is my suggestion for a miniboss in BWL that would be a bit more fun and interesting than the trash post Vael, the boss would ideally drop 1 of the items (since its a miniboss) that the trash there normally would drop. Heck it could even replace some number of the lab packs, but I haven't prepared any kind of diagram for that.

The lore for this character is that they are a goblin engineer/alchemist responsible for constructing the suppression room and concocting the gas that produces the suppression effect. I've included a few prospective mechanics and a picture to show how this would work in the room.

 **Suppression Engineer Sixle:**
 *Analyze Target* (20s CD) - Analyze's the current target, reducing their armor by 100% for 15s.
 *Suppression Bomb* (10s CD) - Throws a bomb of suppression gas at a target, dealing damage and causing the area around the target to affected by Suppression Aura for 10s.
 *Activate Spellreaver Construct* (20s CD) - Activates an inactive Spellreaver Construct.

 **Spellreaver Construct**
 *Deploy Volley* (10s CD) - Deploys a Volley of Fire, Frost, or Arcane Bolts which deal damage of the appropriate type to all targets within 30ft.

<https://imgur.com/a/d63gAua>

I've left out the numbers here for HP/Damage values because I don't know what exactly would make sense, but I think that a miniboss would prove more interesting in some places than more trash. May have overdesigned this one, but felt like giving it a go.
