# Please add more interesting librams/relics

## Post 86018 by Westron — 2024-01-27T10:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86018#p86018 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It would be more interesting if there were more librams out in the world.  Some that can add some interesting effects

Since I play a paladin I focus on those but all classes that use those type of items could benefit.  Here are just a few ideas.

 **Libram of Righteous Fury**
Equip: Imblues your weapon with Holy Engergy adding +5 Holy dmg to your melee attacks

 **Libram of Righteousness**
Equip: While Righteous Fury is active, give your Seal of Righteous the ability to crit

 **Libram of Holy Shield**
Equip: Your Holy Shield now also shoots out a ray of holy energy in front of the paladin for xyz dmg
(cone like dmg in front of the paladin)

 **Libram of Ardent Defender**
Equip: Your ardent defender now activates at 50% health and damage taken is reduced by an additional 5%.

 **Libram of Justice:**
Equip: When judging Seal of Justice a surge of Holy Power hits the target for xyz dmg

 **Libram of Exorcism:**
Equip: Gives your excorism the ability to be cast on NPC's

 **Libram of the Holy Avenger**
Equip: Gives the paladin the ability to dual wield
Since you see Paladins of the Scarlet Crusade dual wielding it would be interesting

 **Libram of Judgment**
Equip: All judgements do xyz dmg
(This basically uses Seal of Command judgement dmg)

 **Libram/Relic of Many Things**
Equip: Gives you the ability to switch trinkets during combat.
(30-60 second cd is activated of course.)

From a developer standpoint.  If updating existing spells to add some of these effects is not doable.  The way I see some of these working is it adds a spell to your spellbook that does the additional thing rather than edit existing spells to do the additional things you want.  Not sure if that makes it easier to add these type of effects but it woud be cool and probably increase interest.  They of course would share cd with the original spell.
