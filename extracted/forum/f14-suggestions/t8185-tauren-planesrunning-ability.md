<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52606#p52606 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I just learnt that Taurens were supposed to have a really cool feature to replace mounts called planesrunning, but it was cut out of the final release due to balance concerns.

I think when it comes to roleplay class fantasy however this fits a lot better than a cow mounting another animal.
I hope Turtle WoW would remaster this forgotten ability and rebalance it to fit the world of Turtle WoW and remove the ability for Taurens to use regular mounts. Especially now that Worgens are coming with a similar ability.

Personally as someone who never ever cared to actually level a Tauren (I'm Alliance main and would pick any other Horde race before I give Taurens a full playthrough), this would really make me reconsider giving them a try simply to experience this unique playstyle feature.
