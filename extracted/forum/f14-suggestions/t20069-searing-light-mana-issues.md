# Searing Light Mana Issues

## Post 137325 by Apimius (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-28T11:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137325#p137325 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey,

not counting PvP i think Searing Light still has issues regarding PvE, which i assume the development team was mostly catering towards in CC2. Especially on long boss fights like in Naxx or Kara40 smite priests still tend to go out of mana. The change from class changes 2 was initially a free cast from searing light, that was obviously too overpowered, though the nerf from free cast to only 20% mana reduction was over the top. The thing is, 20 % sounds like a fair ammount, but if you factor in that you are also "casting" faster (normal smite is about 2 seconds cast time, instant smite is with gcd 1.5) you effectively saving about 0 mana. So my suggestion is buff the mana reduction to 40 or 50%, that way the mana saving will actually become noticeable and disc priest will not oom half as much anymore like they do right now. Mana is still the number 1 factor thats holding us back in true endgame, priests dont have evocation, live tap etc.

Thanks for reading and if a dev reads this please think about it!    sad_turtle_head
