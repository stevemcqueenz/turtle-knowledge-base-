# Expanding the Juju's system.

## Post 45332 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-05-10T13:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45332#p45332 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Credit where credit is due, the E'ko changes are a step in the right direction, it's great to see voices were heard and the situation was fixed.

This opens up a big area for the server to explore, to give people more variety in consumables and open more gold making options as activities to do in the world.

What better way to do that than to add more and more e'ko types all over winterspring. Feel free to share your ideas and comment on the balancing of these items.

Highborne E'ko. Drops from Suffering Highborne (7523) and Anguished Highborne (7524).
 - Can be exchanged for Juju Arcane: Increase Arcane resistance by 15 for 600 sec.

Hederine E'ko. Drops from Hederine Initiate (7461) and Hederine Manastalker (7462) and Hederine Slayer (7463).
 - Can be exchanged for Juju Shadow: Increases Shadow resistance by 15 for 600 sec.

Cobalt E'ko. Drops from Spell Eater (10661) and Cobalt Whelp (10659) and Cobalt Broodling (10660) and Cobalt Wyrmkin (7435) and Cobalt Scalebane (7436) and Cobalt Mageweaver (7437).
 -Can be exchanged for Juju Magic: Increases spell damage by up to 45 for 1800 sec. (does not stack with Greater arcane elixir)

Owl E'ko. Drops from Winterspring Screecher (7456) and Winterspring Owl (7455).
 - Can be exchanged for Juju Breeze: Increases the target's Agility by 30 for 1800 sec. (does not stack with any of the agility elixirs, would be weaker than mongoose. Just a cheap little buff people leveling in winterspring can obtain and use)

But we're past the world buff discussion now, they're gone and not coming back.
Way I see it, all consumables use raw materials obtained from the world, processed by a player.
In the case of elixirs, they use herbs (and sometimes other stuff like fishing oils) farmed in the world, processed by an alchemist.

Juju's are the same thing, they use e'kos farmed in the world, processed by a player who did the Cache of Mau'ari quest. You can obtain them at any time, and use them later at any time like any regular consumable, so the raid logging point fortunately doesn't apply.
