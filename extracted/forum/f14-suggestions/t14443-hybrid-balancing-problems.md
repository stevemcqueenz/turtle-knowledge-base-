# hybrid balancing problems

## Post 98328 by Caos (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-01T19:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98328#p98328 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In vanilla hybrids could do various things in a suboptimal level while in retail they just have to spec and can almost only do one thing.
I personally think the vanilla class is way more fun, it doesn't make much sense to play a druid to only be in cat form and don't heal or cast damaging spells.
Hybrid playstyles are perfectly valid for pvp, leveling and open world at max level. Maybe even can be used for dungeons.
The only problem with hybrids in classic is that they don't fit raids since in raids you only have one role. They are mostly heals as there are no pure heal classes. Since raid gear is much better than the rest this is a problem.
(pvp is close to raid but it takes forever to get and a lot of people don't like pvp)
The best solution would probably be adding new ways to get gear that can get close to raid powerlevel from various sources: crafting (with a lot of mats), reps or whatever. This gear could be hybrid so this way it would be bad for raiding.

## Post 98585 by Shambakriger — 2024-07-05T13:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98585#p98585 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I wish they’d try and incentivise this kind of playstyle but instead they seem to be moving away from it by removing all of the OG vanilla hybrid gear like druid t0.5 and t2.5.
I’d love for true hybrids to be explored more since it’s such a historically unique playstyle, but twow has shifted into a focus on endgame raiding content, where balancing hybrids is incredibly hard. Adding dual spec has also made balancing around hybrid classes/gearing a lot harder for PvE.
Let’s take druids as an example: If you look at vanilla holistically they were never underpowered. Druids were some of the best levellers with a lot of flexibility for doing dungeons, and they were great in PvP, even being the best at flag carrying. In a world where few people reached max lvl, let alone got into raids this would be a fine way to balance classes. But on a private server in 2024 most people expect to get to lvl 60 and at least do some raiding, at least the most vocal part of the community.
Making hybrids viable for raids, which are typically balanced around each player having one specific role, is a looooot harder, (and again made harder by dual spec)
So it’s quite understandable that twow ended up taking the route of just buffing the individual specs rather than the hybrid class as a whole.
I love hybrids and will always be vocal about them, I might post some of my suggestions later

If you are not an elemental shaman and you can't do at least a close amount of what pure dps spec can do, there is less incentive to bring you to a raid.

What if you like ret pally above all roles? What if you like smite priest above all things a priest can do? Should you be limited in the countribution you  can bring to the raid?

I believe there are other ways to balance a single spec without making it OP in PVP, possibly by:

 - Not having a lot of stamina on their bis, close to bis pieces;
 - Items giving +spell power only without giving + healing power, so that if you do commit to this playstyle, you cannot also be an extremely powerful healer in PVP;

But im not a PVPer, so maybe I'm completely out of touch. My point was that I usually prefer 1 spec of a class, while I do switch when the raid needs it. And I would like to play a spec that is capable of bringing good dps to the raid.
