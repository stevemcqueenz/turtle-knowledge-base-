You could reduce the duration you get from deserter by leaving a bg proportionally to the size of the premade in the enemy team.
This premading thing doesn't make any sense there is a team with random people, with random gear that don't know each other against another organized one with almost bis and maybe they are even on discord. Even if you have good gear and you want to play your team just stay in ghost or whatever, an I don't blame them it is disgusting.
So a good solution that won't harm anybody would be reduce the Deserter duration, so if you don't wanna play against a premade you just leave and get replaced with someone who wants to plays with them.
Could just be intern to the server, when you leave you get the deserter with less duration to not point at the premaders.
Could work something like this:
if half the players on the enemy team are a premade your deserter will last 7.5 min. If 90% are a premade your deserter will last  1.5min and so on. It is just a simple math formula.

 15 min deserter is not that big deal but you can face multiple premades in a row and you end up not being able to play the game.
