Hey!
Sorry for spamming suggestions but my favourite part of the game is theorycrafting and imagining what I'd change.

This thread is about fishing.
What it is currently: A somewhat rewarding but extremely repetitive side hustle. Game somewhat rewards high fishing with 1 ZG boss which has acceptable but not very good items. Most guilds just skip that boss. Also a money-saving activity for some enchanters and cooks.

I'd think about these changes:
 * Adding some epic/rare items with extremely low droprate so fishing kinda starts feeling like a treasure hunt. Imagine a legendary pirate losing his Krol Blade-like sword in an EPL lake with a 0,0001% droprate. Things like this.
 * Would make the process of fishing more interesting. F.ex. currently you have a 1-2 sec window when the bait is moving. If you press right-click, you catch the target. Would slightly change this into a reflex game. Having the same time window for catch but having a quality loot bonus or penalty based on how fast you reacted (let's say you are slightly more likely to catch the most worthless item if you reacted slow).
 * Would add different lures for the game, each one is making a specific kinf of catch somewhat more likely. Let's say, you wanna catch squids at Azhara and a certain lure adds +5% chance for each catch to a certain type of drops, including squids.
 * Would consider fishing boat a buff (since it is technically a buff) and would show it on the character's buff bar, including the exact time left from the buff.
 * Fishing boat gives fishing bonus because you fish in deeper waters. Someone somewhere mentioned the idea that druid aquatic form should enable fishing with an ability. I absolutely love this and would even add a fishing bonus to the aquatic form since it does so in deep sea. Would also give a similar buff to shamans as long as they are water walking: if you walk to the middle of a lake and fish, you fish above the deep waters. Would consider water breathing for the same but I am less convinced there.
 * Would add ZG-like fishing bosses to custom dungeons to motivate people to have at least one max-ed out fisher in the group. F.ex. an additional boss in ES at the lake is easily feasible and gives a motivation boost for people to fish.
