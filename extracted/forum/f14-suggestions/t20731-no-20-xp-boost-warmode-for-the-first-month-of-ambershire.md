## Post 143745 by Sirturtle — 2025-08-08T05:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143745#p143745 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm thinking like this:

It's a fresh realm, there's nothing to catch up to, let's do it the standard Turtle wow way.

Warmode will be used mostly by people who want to get to 60 before the pack, and will likely not engage in pvp with each other, (even on Nordranaar this is the case more often than not).

The fastest leveling player will no doubt be a warmode player, and they will have no player to even pvp with, so it seems pointless from a pvp/challenge perspective.

Alliance will outnumber the Horde, so if there is pvp, the horde will be bullied and fall behind while Alliance players soar in levels and causes Horde to lack behind, and be perhaps less incentivized to play Horde, creating more faction imbalance.

There's always Tel'Abim and normal pvp toggle for those who like pvp/a challenge.
