# Fix the Guild Bank buy-in to not be hostile to small guilds

## Post 23952 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-08-29T20:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23952#p23952 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, the guild bank buyins are as follows:
 5,000g to open the feature
 1,000g per tab

This is backwards and actively hostile to small guilds.

In game we really have 2 cases of guilds: invested raiding guilds, and casual RP or leveling guilds.

The small casual guilds really usually just want one page, to put all their leveling gear and knickknacks to share with fellow levelers. They have no need for a big bank, and often don't have a lot of gold to buy into a guild bank.

Raiding guilds are often large, have a big raid team base, they ALSO have levelers, and do have big money to throw around. For them with all the various guild raiding mats, rep mats, as well as the leveling gear for levelrs in the guild, there's a big need for tooons of space to put all their stuff. These types of guilds are the only ones who should be putting up a ton of money for their big guild bank.

Please rework the payments to be the opposite, I propose something like this:

 1,000g to open
 2,000g 2nd tab
 3,000g 3rd tab
 4,000g 4th tab
 5,000g last tab

This way, small casual guilds that dont have a lot of money can enjoy this system with just 1 tab, and big raiding guilds will come up with the money to buy it all out.
