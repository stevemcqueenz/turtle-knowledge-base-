# Extraction as crafting fuel - new feature idea

## Post 167685 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-07T16:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167685#p167685 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi,

I would like to suggest a new feature to make crafting more interactive:

 **EXTRACTION**

I guess everyone can already imagine something here, but I would like to introduce my extraction idea for the simple and straightforward world of Azeroth.

Two important things would have to be the case:

 **First:**
Disenchanting would not only create enchanting materials, but also a white item (common quality) of the same type. Instead of destroying the item, the enchanter extracts the enchantment and gets the materials, as well as a common-quality item that can be sold or used.
Example: I disenchant an uncommon sword, I get some dust and a common sword. Here, we could even give enchanting a bit more of a slot-machine effect. For example, if I disenchant a rare item, I extract the enchantment and get an uncommon item of the same type, which I can then disenchant again until I end up with a common item. But that’s another topic.

 **Second:**
Each crafting profession gets a new ability, similar to disenchanting, that allows them to extract raw crafting materials from respective items.
For example, a blacksmith can use “**Extract Metal**” to turn a common metal item into ore, a leatherworker uses “**Extract Leather**” to turn a common leather item into leather, and a tailor uses “**Extract Cloth**” to extract cloth from a cloth piece.
The Item itself will be destoryed in the process!

Just something I would love to see in the game, and I don’t see a reason why not.

## Post 167700 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-02-07T19:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167700#p167700 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As a flavor - might be cool. It might be too difficult to implement to bother.
If we exclude technical question, I not sure it will make game better anyway.
Cloth and leather prices on AH are just above vendor price. This world don't need more cloth and leather.
Same for enchanting materials - it is better to sell items than disenchant them. There are too much enchanting materials on AH and not enough demand for it. Especially for low levels.
I don't follow metal prices these days and they are cost a bit higher than vendor price (except tin).
Ability to extract metals from items will drop metal price and I don't see reason why their prices should be lower.

However this idea could be improved in different way. When you extract stuff from useless item, character can get "spare parts" to repair their current item. Ability to repair items float in forums from time to time. Instead of selling item and getting gold (out of thin air), people will get materials to trade with other players. It will reduce amount of gold in economy.
But it will lead us too far from Vanilla in any case. Which is not very good in general.
