# 18.1 bandaid solutions destroyed pvp even more

## Post 151913 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-16T08:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151913#p151913 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The dmg/heal debuff in pvp is a poor bandaid that did not fix the underlying issues of pvp imbalances. It significantly affects BG gameplay.

 **AB**
Some classes, for example paladins, now take a very long time to kill, it is impossible to burst them down and they have the bubbles etc which means they can spin a flag for a considerable time. This gives alliance a significant edge in AB as it gives them relatively more time to rotate and respawn.

 **WSG**
The flagcarrier debuff needs to go. Pronto. After a certain time as FCs get 10+ stacks, they can barely get out of the flagroom. One geared player in flagroom is enough to uphold or kill a solo fc, and 2+ means teams need 4-5 to get flag out. Then fc usually dies midfield. It also makes for unfair situations in defense: FC is basically reduced to a 2.5k hp player while the opposing team players have the equivalent of 5-6k hp with the dmg debuff. Games now take 40+ mins not because fc is hard to catch but because it is so hard to get flag out of flagroom and across the field. How could you not think of that?

Seriously, revert these changes. The bursty meta was way more playable than this.

If you absolutely need to continue these bandaid buffs/debuff, give a resilience-style buff for pvp instead.

But the main issue with twow pvp is turtle wow’s stubbornness in buffing niche specs in hybrid classes. There are currently basically 4-5 specs that are problematic in pvp and they (ofcourse) are all CC2 inventions.

- Ele/enha shaman: This was slightly nerfed in 1.18 but remains a problem. The core issue is the 21 talents in enha that gives shamans significant armor boost and Stormstrike. But rather than addressing these, 1.18 went for classwide nerfs in increasing the cd of grounding totem and purge. This punishes resto or ele/resto shamans who are not the cause of the problems. These spells are oppressive only in combination with the armor and shock boosts from 21 enha talents. How do I know this? because no one complained about them pre-CC2. In other words, you are ADDRESSING THE SYMPTOMS OF THE ISSUE, not the root cause. The root cause, ofcourse, is the hellbent effort to make shamans be able to tank. Shamans already had three roles, one for each talent tree. We did not need a fourth. If you want to fix shamans in pvp, you need to address these talents.

- Smite priests. These need to be nerfed. One-button spam which does significant damage and the disorienting just procs way too often. They repeatedly go 10/1 kd ratio in BGs.

- Caster druids. I don’t know the spec but the ability to spam moonfire(?) coupled with the class mobility and cc is too oppressive. Again I see individuals with a 10/1 kd ratio. Increase the cast time of the key spells.

- Arcane mage. Remove.

- Melee hunters. Have not had much experience with these myself but discussions with others informs me that their dmg is better than warriors now. Why are you so intent on making classes into something entirely different? Why should hunters be mail-wearing rogues?
Speaking of warriors: They need imp. hamstring back, and their rage gen suffers immensely from the pvp dmg debuff.

Seriously twow devs, if you want to be taken seriously when it comes to fixing pvp you can’t be making bandaid solutions like the dmg debuff. Fix the root causes of the issue, which are the niche hybrid specs you created with CC2 that gives classes new benefits and removes their drawbacks.
