# Paladin Tank Skill Modification Suggestion

## Post 106633 by Anyangming — 2024-11-06T05:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106633#p106633 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

glad  to see the release of patch 1.17.2, which addresses numerous concerns regarding Paladin tanks. However, as a Paladin tank player, I've noticed that we currently lack a ranged pull skill, which puts us at a disadvantage in dungeon rush, particularly when trying to engage mobs from a distance of up to 30 yards.

My suggestion is to allow Exorcism to be used on any type of mob, with the following damage distribution:
.Demons and undead would take 100% damage.
.Other types of mobs would receive 50% or 30% damage.
This change would provide Paladin tanks with a much-needed ranged initiation tool, allowing us to better control the pace of combat and maintain our role as effective tanking class in group content.

I believe this adjustment would not only improve the Paladin tank's gameplay experience but also maintain the overall balance within the game. I appreciate your consideration of this suggestion and look forward to any feedback you may have.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.

Paladin is the best tank to handling pulls that turned bigger thank expected. Additionally, there are plenty of low effort solutions that allow paladins to pull at range already in the game.
There are throwables for Engineers (Grenades) as well as non Engineers (Ez-Thro Dynamites & Explosive Rockets).
These can be picked up for a few silver at the auction house or crafted with ease.

There are on-use items that can work for this job such as linkens bumerang, torch of holy flame, six demon bag, Dark Iron hooked net, and the list goes on.

And most powerful of all, you can coordinate with other classes that excell at ranged combat. Something as simple as "pull triangle" can be established at the beginning of the dungeon and every time you mark triangle someone will pull it.

Not every problem requires to be solved with the base class toolkit.
