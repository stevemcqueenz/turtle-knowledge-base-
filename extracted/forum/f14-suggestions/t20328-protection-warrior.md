# Protection Warrior

## Post 139966 by Lanfear618 — 2025-07-16T19:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139966#p139966 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

0. Improved Shield Wall should be baseline rather than a talent. Having a 30-minute cooldown on any of those three abilities is unreasonable, especially when more powerful spells like Divine Shield and Tranquility have only a 5-minute cooldown. In general, no ability should have a cooldown longer than 5 minutes, with rare exceptions like Lay on Hands.

 1. I feel like there are too many highly situational talents and not enough that offer meaningful impact in PvE. Talents like Iron Will, Improved Intervene, Improved Disarm, Gag Order, Improved Revenge don’t really contribute much in most raid or dungeon settings. I’d love to see more talents that directly support damage mitigation or tanking. Even something simple like a flat 5% dodge increase or improved stamina scaling would be welcome. Right now, there are virtually no talents below the third row that help with survivability, aside from Improved Shield Slam, which isn’t particularly strong in that regard.

 2. Defensive Tactics feels a bit counterintuitive to me. We have a stance that's specifically designed for Protection, yet this talent seems to encourage moving out of it. I don’t think stance dancing should really be part of tanking gameplay. Why would you use Overpower when you have Revenge? Or Whirlwind when Thunder Clap is available? If you need to switch to another stance and rely on abilities from the DPS specs just to generate more threat, then it's a poor game design. Please consider redesign it, and make Thunder Clap and Revenge scale from Attack Power.  It could be better if it just make every ability usable in defensive stance and those abilities generate extra aggro like whirlwind.

 3. Blocking feels really weak overall. For example, from a 130 hit, I only blocked 15 damage. Even with the +5% block chance talent, I’m only blocking about 10% of incoming damage at roughly a 10% chance at low levels. And even with the +15% block value talent, blocking still feels underwhelming. I think Shield Specialization could either increase block chance significantly so that, even if the block value is low, it happens more often and adds up over time. Also, making blocks capable of critical hits would add meaningful value to the mechanic. I’m not sure how much better blocking is at level 60 with best-in-slot gear, but at low levels, blocking definitely feels very weak.
 4. Shield Block feels really weak overall, especially in low-level dungeons where rage management is tight. You often have to choose between holding aggro or spending 10 rage to block just 10% of the next attack, which isn’t very efficient. I think Shield Block could have more charges ér block all attacks till x seconds, and increase block value while it lasts so it would be a considerable option.

 5. Rage generation as prot warrior is really poor especially in low gear. Please consider doing modifications in that regard like:
 -increase rage generation from damage taken while in defensive stance
 -making shield specialization generate rage when dodging and parrying also

I get what you're saying — **Protection Warriors do offer excellent mitigation**, and there's real value in that, especially during tough progression fights.

But here’s the core issue: **mitigation only matters if you’re the one being hit**. And right now, Protection Warriors often struggle to hold aggro consistently. That’s the paradox — you can be the tank with the best survival tools, but if you can't keep threat, those tools never get used.

The main criticism isn't about survivability — it's about **threat generation**, and it’s important to remember that *threat is not the same as damage*. Improving a tank’s ability to hold aggro doesn't require turning them into a DPS machine. There are more nuanced ways to enhance threat output while preserving class identity.

Also, the idea of swapping stances mid-fight to maintain threat feels a bit awkward. It’s like asking a druid tank to shift in and out of bear form during combat — it breaks the flow of the role and adds unnecessary clunkiness.

 **Protection Warriors have strong potential — we just need that core piece of the kit, threat generation, to feel more solid and reliable.**
