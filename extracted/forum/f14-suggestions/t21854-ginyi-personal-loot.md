Just adding the word "Ginyi" so it's easier to search for this thread, since the words "Personal Loot" are too common.

I don't know if this has been suggested before, because it's impossible to search for "Personal Loot" as I tried, but I hope it has, because that means there are other people who want this feature.

 **What is Personal Loot?**
It is a loot system, where everyone gets his own roll for getting an item or not, when a boss is killed. Instead of a boss dropping items, and they get distributed by a Master Looter, or by everyone rolling on them. Drop rates in Personal Loot are different from drop rates in other loot systems.

 **Why should it be implemented?**
It's fast. It can be a good option for PUGs. Distribution of loots takes a considerable amount of time and efforts. For casual players, they may just want to experience running dungeons and raids, and a chance at loots, without the hassle of handling loot distribution.

Some people may think Personal Loot should be a replacement to existing loot systems like Group Loot or Master Loot, because of unfair situations created in those loot systems. I don't think so. I think the flaws of Classic WoW are part of the game. There are no problems to be solved, but I think it is a good option to have. So, I think Personal Loot can be another loot option.

Well, every time a boss is killed, the server automatically does a random draw for each player to determine if they get an item. Of course the item must be useful to the player's class and spec. So a Warrior will never get a Mage gear for example. For example, every time Nefarian is killed, every player of a class that can use 2h weapons will have a 1% chance of getting Ashkandi. Think of it as playing lottery. Every person has his own chance of winning a prize. Whether another person wins a prize has no impact on another person winning a prize.
