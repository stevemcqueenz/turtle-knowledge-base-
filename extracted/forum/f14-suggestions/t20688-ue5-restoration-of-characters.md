# UE5 Restoration of Characters

## Post 143255 by Moongirl — 2025-08-05T14:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143255#p143255 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! I hope you are having a wonderful day!   smiling_turtle_head

I have seen an increasing number of people asking for their characters to be restored. I do not mean Hardcore characters, they cannot be restored. However, even normal characters, the Turtle WoW team have said they will not restore in any circumstance.   scared_turtle_head

I understand there is a lot of characters in the game and it is probably hard work to search for a specific one and 'restore' it to how it was before. I also do not understand how people 'accidently' delete their characters. However, I worry in a situation where the person truly deleted their character by accident or their account got hacked in some way.

This will not be possible in the current client, but in the new UE5 client. I ask for a character restoration button. Characters deleted will stay in some sort of 'box' for 30-ish days and then be erased forever. In this time frame, you can restore them if you would like, but you may require a name change if someone took your name. Perhaps fit around 3-5 characters. At least with this, people *can* get their 60 back, which they have spent hours and weeks on, instead of having that character lost forever.

What do you think? Thank you for reading, and good luck on your journey.   smiling_turtle_head

It does not depend on the client. It's just how databases work. I guess the delete a character actually deletes the character in all databases, leaving no trace it ever existed besides his left space in the character id.

Being able to restore characters for everyone means "Delete Character" would not actually delete anything, but makes them invisible in all databases it exists for the game to read. This would lead to a lot of core coding changes to make it work flawlessly for deleting and restoring on the fly.

Devs can restore chars when being hacked and stuff like that by access their backup database versioned and restore the data to the live database.

So maybe just don't delete characters if you reals don't mean it. There's a reason you need to verify the deletion so you can't to it accidentally
