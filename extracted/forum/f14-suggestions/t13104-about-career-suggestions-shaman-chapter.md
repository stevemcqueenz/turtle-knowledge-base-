# About Career Suggestions: Shaman Chapter

## Post 87841 by Liuyihang0510 — 2024-02-15T12:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87841#p87841 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1.Thunderstorm: Enable your Lightning Shield to be used against allies and reduce magic costs by 20%. Can only be used for one friend. New change: Thunder Shield: increases the chance of shield blocking (the effect is the same as warrior shield blocking).

 2. Ancestor Guardian: Increases the armor value you receive from your equipment by 2%, and increases your evasion and (newly added: blocking) chances by 1%. Ghost armor can be incorporated into the protection of ancestors and increase the mana recovery ability of shaman tanks (and then the Ghost armor talent can be redone as compensation).

 3. Replace the lava totem with the Great Earthquake spell: deal an earthquake to nearby 8 yards, and deal XX damage to enemies and targets every 2 seconds. Causing sustained damage.

 4. Talent Enhancement Weapon Totem: Modified to increase stamina by XX% and restore mana by XX% when blocking. Incorporating enhanced weapon totem effects into elemental weapons.

The above immature suggestions represent me personally, and I hope the team can re-examine the career optimization of shamans. thanks    hiding_smth_turtle_head

## Post 87868 by Ishilu (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-02-15T15:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87868#p87868 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

These look like you want to make enhancers lean more into tanking which I personally like, too.

In detail:

 1) I've also remarked several times that the thunderhead talent right now is simply not good and should be replaced by something that's more fun to use. I like your suggestion here.
 2) Ancestor Guardian: making enhancers a little bit tougher sounds good to me, but somebody needs to do the math, of course.
 3) This looks a lot like "shaman consecration" to me. I have mixed feelings here. My opinion is that if you can AE spam a dungeon, then the mobs need tuning up. I also share the opinion with others that the team should not make the classes too similar. Leaving this out would make shamans the only tanks with a dedicated AE spell, however. I'm also not a huge fan of entirely replacing existing spells with new ones. I like this one less than the others.
 4) tanking totem sounds interesting, but again, people should do the math about this. Note that druids would profit less from this. Maybe change "mana for block" to "mana for block or dodge"? Another question would be which totem slot this would fill. I'd say this looks like an earth or maybe water totem.
