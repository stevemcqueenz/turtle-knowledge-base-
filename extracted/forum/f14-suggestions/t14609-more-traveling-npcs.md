# More traveling NPCs

## Post 99711 by Requeue133 — 2024-07-17T01:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99711#p99711 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really think there should be far more traveling NPCs on every road in the world. It would add an extra level of immersion and life to the gameplay. I could see this being a nice addition to the game. With Turtle 2.0 I could see dynamic random spawn NPCs bringing extra life to the world as well. Some which have useful functions, some that just walk the streets with no real utility. I think it would be cool to stumble upon a traveling Alchemist that could sell you empty vials and random herbs. A wandering Miner eager to sell a precious Gem; Or perhaps they are nobodies who are just out doing their own thing with no interest in interacting with anyone. Thanks for reading!
