1.5 year ago there was a thread [Make vendor white items useful](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=5601). After some time, Vagrant challenge was added.

One day implementation, clearly not enough days designing and analytics.
Refer to this thread to figure out why: [Vagrant’s Endeavor Theorycrafting](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13067) and this [Vagrant's Endeavor is cool... BUT ?](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10709).

Though there is answer from devs:
[viewtopic.php?p=80107#p80107](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80107#p80107)
> it is possible to save the items in the bank if you want them for future transmog options.
>  Thread resolved as: no change planned

Was it enough to make white vendor items useful? In some degree - yes.
Was it enough to make white vendor items relevant to everyone? No and I can't imagine how they could be useful for everyone.
Can we expect any rework or improvement regarding this? I believe no. This issue is too small to bother.
