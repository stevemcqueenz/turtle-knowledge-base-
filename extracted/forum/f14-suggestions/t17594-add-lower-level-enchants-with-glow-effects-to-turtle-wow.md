 **Add Low Level Enchantments With Glows to Turtle WoW**

[![Image](http://dl7.glitter-graphics.net/pub/727/727097kjhd9gidla.gif)](https://www.glitter-graphics.com)

In Vanilla WoW, there are not many enchantments that feature a glow feature until late game. [https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki ... glow#Melee](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_glow#Melee) . For roleplay purposes, it would be nice to see some low level enchantments (think +1 to something) added (perhaps through reputation, new quests, event quests, or dungeon drops) that bear enchantment glows. This would allow players of all levels to have interesting looking weapons, without having to opt for higher level enchantments coming from top tier enchanters.

This would also give lower level enchanters the chance to earn more money earlier in the profession, funding their studies.

It would be a massive boon to the roleplay community, and offer more character customizations. Thank you for reading.
