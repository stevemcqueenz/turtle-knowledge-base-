The worldbuffs still drop quite frequently (usually after people end their raid since they hand in the Head (as it was originally intended)). So if you want to reliably get a worldbuff for twinking, you can just learn the timing when BWL/Ony Raids of major guild happen.

The whole reason why worldbuffs were timed is because people did the effort and scheduled it. You can still schedule it with your guild if you want to do that. And there are enough worldbuffs still dropping. You just have to look at when raidguilds usually end their raid.

If someone wants to buff up with all raidbuffs available to do stupidly easy questcontent quicker, they can do so, but i don't think we need to make it any more convinient.

## Post 38829 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-24T10:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38829#p38829 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Another related suggestion, make chronoboons a permanent item. A 5g cost simply makes it never worth. You can't wait in booty bay to boon hakkar heart, then wait in the capital to boon onyxia head, then wait ages to find a dire maul tribute group. In the past those were all coordinated and you could stack many buffs together before booning all at once, AND it was worth the gold cost because of their benefit to the raid.

Now it's only a dungeons/open world farming thing and 5g per buff for that is prohibitive.

The fix is to make using the Supercharged boon (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83001>) Create an Unused Chronoboon Displacer (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83000>) in your inventory. That way the boon is a one-time purchase, you store some buffs, then when you unleash them you re-receive the boon you bought.
