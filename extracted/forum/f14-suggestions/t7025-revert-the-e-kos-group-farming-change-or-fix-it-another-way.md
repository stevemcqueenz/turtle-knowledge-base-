# Revert the E'kos group farming change OR fix it another way

## Post 44561 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-05-05T11:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44561#p44561 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

2 days ago, the devs changed E'kos to no longer be a shared drop between group members and didn't put it in the patchnotes. This goes against Blizzlike behavior, but this is a custom server after all so can't use that argument for everything.

How it used to work is, blizzard made the mobs who drop E'kos very low in numbers and have a very low drop chance and added this unique mechanic (I can't think of any other item that works this way) to encourage people to have at least one consumable that is better to farm in a group than solo, and I think that's a very nice thing. Making people group up in an MMO is **a good thing**.

Twow devs removed this mechanic without changing anything about the mobs frequency or the drop rate, with this explanation:
> The team decided that people making 40-man raids to kill single enemies in Winterspring for 40 E'kos to play the economy was not something that we felt was conducive to the long-term health of the server economy.

There are many things wrong with this.

First, most of it was not 40-man raids, it was a group of friends, 2 to 5 rarely reaching 10, going past that if it was a guild effort. If your problem was against raids killing it, make it drop in groups and not drop in raid groups at least.

Its obvious an E'ko farm group would never reach a number as big as 40. No guild logistically has those numbers willing to join for what's basically a consumes farming effort. But even if they did reach 40 man, there's nothing inherently bad about a guild grouping up together to do something.

Second, "playing the economy" is a very misleading excuse to change this. The purpose was never to farm these items to sell them, maybe somebody would tag along to put the e'kos he gets on the auction house, but as a gold farm getting into a frostmaul e'ko group to sell the e'kos is WAY below just grinding things on your own, but still those things happened and people who didn't have time to farm e'kos themselves could buy them for acceptable prices on the auction house.
Look at the availability of frostmaul e'kos this week [https://www.superduperauctions.com/turt ... ce_history](https://www.superduperauctions.com/turtle-wow/turtle-wow/alliance/12436#price_history)
[https://media.discordapp.net/attachment ... height=609](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/745013702713016320/1104003545231015966/image.png?width=1299&height=609)

It's going to crash big, the auction house is already almost empty and soon there will be almost none whatsoever. When that happens, whatever e'kos people can luckily get their hands on solo will sell for more than 10g each, and winterfall firewaters will go up to about that much too. Because these two consumes do the same thing, their prices are strongly interlinked.

You can't logically tell me this eventuality is good for long term health of the economy. It's like the people who made this decision 1. Don't raid 2. Don't do activities in the world 3. Don't look at the auction house.

Raiders have condoned 2% haste for 15 mins food that costs an essence of air (6g in mats). They have condoned the refusal to implement dynamic spawn rates on high level mobs and herbs despite the ever increasing population.

Now the devs make this one change that will single handedly add so much time and gold cost to raiding and they're stretching thin the patience of their community.

Revert the change. If your ego doesn't let you admit fault, up the drop rate to 100%.
