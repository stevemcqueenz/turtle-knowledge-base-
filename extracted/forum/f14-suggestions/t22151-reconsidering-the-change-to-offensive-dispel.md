# Reconsidering the Change to Offensive Dispel

## Post 153621 by McC5VgTS — 2025-09-29T04:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153621#p153621 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently the flat 4 seconds cooldown applied to offensive dispels feels more like a blunt adjustment for pvp balance rather than a design choice. Instead I would suggest replacing this cooldown with a short cast time like 2 seconds when used against hostile targets.
This approach would not only preserve the tactical depth of dispelling but also make it feel more immersive, as if the caster is actually discerning the enemy's magical protections before stripping them away. Though Im not certain whether this is technically possible but I believe it would be a better idea overall.
