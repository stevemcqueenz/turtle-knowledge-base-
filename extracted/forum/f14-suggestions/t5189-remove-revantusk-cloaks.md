# Remove Revantusk cloaks

## Post 31537 by Flameroller — 2022-12-25T16:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31537#p31537 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since you so unreasonably removed the Silvermoon faction cloaks AND plate and most of the weapons, it would only be fair to remove at least the cloaks from the Revantusk. While you're at it, remove the troll necklace turn in quest as well, because that's an unfair advantage to getting rep with Revantusk.

Why such a drastic response to whiners? There were many other solutions if it was too easy to get the cloaks for high elves, like making them require revered, or moving them to a different reputation, like stormwind, or even just making it easier for horde to get to honored with revantusk rep, but instead you go scorched earth and switch the unfair advantage? What's with this bias? Hell, even the darkspear rep has really strong items that aren't even accessible to alliance.

You know what would be even better than being punitive and removing things? Giving everyone all of the things. Just sprinkle it all across multiple factions, or better yet, put it on a neutral reputation vendor. My suggestion would be booty bay (or all 4 neutral goblin factions). Just make a repeatable daily of some kind. You could put full 60 blue cloth set on one goblin faction, leather stuff on another, mail on another and then plate on the last one. You have four factions to work from and can assign weapons/offhand items to factions that don't fit well with the armor assigned to them. For example, the caster weapons and offhands could be on the plate reputation, the melee stuff on the cloth reputations and any cool trinkets on the mail/leather reputations. (or maybe just not giving any full sets of 60 gear because it trivializes a lot of dungeon drops. I personally think the rep stuff should be at most level 45, that way even if it's super powerful, it only lasts 15 levels before max level stuff surpasses it)

Taking the stuff away just causes frustration and negativity, like people changing their minds on the donation they were thinking about making recently...
