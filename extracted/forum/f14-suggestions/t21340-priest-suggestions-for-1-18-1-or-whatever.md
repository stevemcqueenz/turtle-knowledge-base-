# Priest suggestions for 1.18.1 or whatever

## Post 147940 by Manowar76 — 2025-08-24T03:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147940#p147940 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The priest changes for 1.18 were pretty nice. We got instant ve back. Champion is now quite nice. Searing light mana return buff is great. I'm excited for the new smite sets. Here's some more possible things I'd like to see in 1.18.1 or beyond. Starting with simpler changes and moving on to bigger changes.   smiling_turtle_head

 1. Make champion buffs (+healing and +damage) last like 30 minutes instead of 10, to go along with the new champion changes. Mostly for convenience/because it costs so much mana.

 2. 24 yd range to Mind Flay (30 yds with range talent) to make it more consistent with other channel spells/quality of life.

 3. Make shadow weaving apply faster (3 stacks, +5% each, for example) to make shadow a bit better on trash mobs.

 4. Blackout 3 second stun like it was in Vanilla.

 5. CC2 added a 15% penalty to all priest healing spell coefficients. Remove that by bringing the baseline healing coefficient up by 15-20%.  And therefore only make Spiritual healing a 15-10% healing bonus (so the total remains 30% healing). This would open up some discipline healing builds (with enlighten, chastise, maybe resurgent shield) to give back that old PI style of play to healers and make off healing in shadow less annoying. The original intention of this change was to make Holy "worth speccing into" but buffs to champion have done that.

 6. Make the DPS priest tier sets generic +damage/healing. Especially for smite. Alternatively, if for whatever reason making it +damage/healing is not seen as a good option, keep the +holy damage but add a small amount of +healing. For example, a piece giving +39 holy damage and +20 healing or something.

 7. Rework/remove champion's bond and make a mini "pain suppression" type cooldown on your champion instead. seems pretty fun/thematic to the ability.

 8. Maybe a relook at the shadow mend rework? It damaging yourself/not working on yourself.

 9. Make psychic scream more reliable against horde/undead especially. It's not easy to fear as alliance priest when there is also fear wards, pvp trinket, and tremor totems at play. I would suggest making will of the forsaken only work when the undead is not already feared (and make them immune for 4-5 seconds or whatever), to make more interesting counterplay on both sides of the matchup. The undead can predict the fear. The priest can juke the will of the forsaken. This also makes sense considering stoneform (dwarf racial) was nerfed versus rogues, which doesn't work on blind anymore. And also make tremor totem less spammable/slightly lower range.

 10. Holy Nova baseline. So shadow doesn't have to spec into holy for it. Optionally make Nova in shadow form do shadow damage (and therefore only make blackout proc from single target spells). Balance accordingly with talents. And put something else in that talent position.

 11. Smite in general could use additional tools, to make up for its lower damage. Either increase its damage/crit scaling to 2x damage instead of 1.5x. or increase its support tools.

> Perhaps a rework to Resurgent Shield (which is underbaked atm)
> Make Purifying Flames give a holy damage buff to the priest's party too.
> Chastise changes/rework or Power Infusion. Perhaps make it similar to the new rogue ability shadow of death (does damage based on the damage people do to a target during a time window). I wonder if something similar for chastise (increases your spell power, does holy damage to a target) based on the damage your hasted target does during the chastise window, would be interesting.
> Some form of party healing.
Hopefully the tier sets can also help in this regard/do some of that stuff   happy_turtle_head

Ty for reading
