# Patch 1.18 has ruined hunters

## Post 147586 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-21T21:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147586#p147586 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I main a Beastmaster hunter. IMO we've not been an overpowered spec. I play on Tel'Abim and have never felt OP compared to other classes in wPvP. I play Hunter and Beastmaster specifically because I like tanking with my pet. Ever since I rolled this toon I have tanked dungeons to lvl 60, and continued to tank lvl 60 content with groups. This playstyle is what has kept me engaged after getting bored with running MC and BWL on my priest. It was a significant improvement over Retail Classic, it was a fun and new playstyle that I was very happy was supported by the Devs.

Between the class changes to BM and the changes to Elites' HP/DMG, I don't know if I want to play on tWoW anymore. I'm not trying to be dramatic, but logging in and trying to do my normal routine is just completely disrupted. While I'm not a fan of Baited Shot and Kill Command (why is Baited the finisher and Kill Command the set-up anyways?) as I think it's finnicky and obnoxious to use, my main problem is in nerfing the early talents of the tree so much. We had a **66% nerf** to the damage and health bonuses our pets get. This is on top of elites now having more health and doing more damage.

I used to be able to take my eagle and with screech solo elite Silithids, or blue dragonkin in Winterspring. Now it's a huge pain and a struggle to down these mobs by myself with my Screech pet. This is not fun. It's not challenging, it's just tedious. I'm not logging in much anymore and this has really killed my motivation to play. I've spent hundreds of dollars on donations to tWoW and been a huge supporter of the Devs and their mission, but this is just a huge slap in the face. I can't even get excited for the fresh server because the build I wanted to play is garbo now. I can tell from the forums that I'm not the only one. If you're going to fuck my class, can you at least un-tune some of these elites or something? Or keep the elites and unfuck my class please? I'd like to keep playing and giving you money.
