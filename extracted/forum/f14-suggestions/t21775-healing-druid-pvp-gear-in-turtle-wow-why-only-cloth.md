# Healing Druid PvP Gear in Turtle WoW – Why Only Cloth?

## Post 150803 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-08T14:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150803#p150803 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’ve been gearing my druid for PvP recently and realized something that feels a bit off. I decided to go the arena route since that seemed like the fastest way, but when I checked the rewards, there’s no leather healing gear or leather spell damage gear available. Everything in leather is physical DPS.

If I remember right, this was also the case for the honor rank gear – again, no healing leather. That basically leaves me with two options: either take the physical leather, which is useless for resto/balance, or go into cloth gear for the resilience. I’ll obviously go with cloth because resilience matters, but it feels wrong that druids don’t have a leather option when mail already has spell damage sets.

Was this an intentional design decision? Or more likely, an oversight? Druids being pushed into cloth makes sense for PVE, but the reduction of armor this way is definitely not good for PvP survivability.

Would love to hear if anyone knows the reasoning or if others are in the same boat of running around in cloth resilience gear as a leather class.
