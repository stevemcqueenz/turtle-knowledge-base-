I see your point. Let me elaborate on mine, how I'd imagine this.
Let's say that you have a new button around the minimap giving you endless mode locations.
For this example let's imagine one option, available from level 60, where 3 characters are queued together. You all get teleported to an instance, having 30 sec before the rush to prepare a bit.
Enemy mobs have a high variety with different resistances to avoid getting used to it and mindlessly kill them for hours. Mobs appear on the screen, approaching you (there are some thematical restrictions for each instance but usually from ooze through gargoyle to scarlet monks whatever can come, they are all elite). After a wave is cleared, a boss appears, which is taken from high dungeons (for example Kirtonos from Scholo comes at the end of the wave).
If defeated, 1-3 loots drop, 30 sec preparation time comes and the wave continues.
Every 5th wave has a super boss (like Ony attunement dragons) giving more precious loot.
As a general rule, each 5-wave part would get slightly harder with more mobs or stronger resistances making sure endless runs are impossible. Loots would be more rewarding the longer you survive.
Could add rep, loot and gold, up to the developers.
Personally I'd love some options calling in 2 close friend and see how much we can stay alive.

PS: Good idea, these could be made more thematical, like making an instance look like a siege of SW or Org where enemy mobs and bosses are more thematically restricted. Would add additional favor.
