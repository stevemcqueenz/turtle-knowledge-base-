# Suggestion: Faol's Rest Plaque

## Post 128781 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-04-28T07:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128781#p128781 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Faol's Rest is implied to be the final resting place of Archbishop Alonsus Faol, Abbot of Northshire Abbey and head of the Church of Light from Warcraft I and into the aftermath of Warcraft II (at the latest he was alive until pre-Warcraft III, as he was one of the jurors for Tirion's trial in Of Blood and Honor, which coincides with Thrall's quest to liberate the orcs from the internment camps), as well as one of the creators of the Knights of the Silver Hand.

Why he was laid to rest in Lordaeron isn't quite clear (however, given that Lordaeron is described as the religious mecca of the Seven Kingdoms, there may be a religious aspect to it), but I think that a little memorial plaque added to the tomb would make a good flavorful addition.

> Here lies Archbishop Alonsus Faol - spiritual advisor, mentor, and friend.
>
>  Though he began his good work in Stormwind, Archbishop Faol was a great friend to the people of Lordaeron. He worked ceaselessly to rebuild the kingdom and bring aid to the remaining nations of the Alliance after the Second War. He was a beloved and generous soul, and may his kindness and wisdom never be forgotten.
>
>  Walk in the Light, brother.
>
>  - Bishop Jarl Benedictus - High Priest of the Church of Light

I meant to write the memorial in a similar fashion to the Valley of Heroes' plaques. "Bishop Jarl" is in reference to Archbishop Benedictus, who is mentioned to have been one of Faol's students, and in the RPG was said to have been a man named Jarl prior to succeeding him. The idea was that the plaque would presumably have been erected shortly before Benedictus was named archbishop given the epitaph is written with him being referred to still as a bishop and high priest.

While the RPG says that Jarl changed his name to Benedictus after ascending to archbishop similarly as to how the pope of the Catholic Church does, presumably this is just an RPG-ism that does not actually apply to the Warcraft universe given that Alonsus Faol is never implied to have changed his name and is referred to as either "Archbishop Faol" or "Archbishop Alonsus Faol" as though it were his full given name. This would make the current archbishop's full name "Jarl Benedictus".
