# Give elemental shaman identity and raid utility spells

## Post 106825 by Rido — 2024-11-07T05:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106825#p106825 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the upcoming kara40 raid and the release of RESTO 3.5 people will prefer even more to bring resto sham over enh/ele shamans, or force Enhancement shaman to wear healing gear due to the powerful 3set bonus and the bloodlust they provide.

But where do this leave elemental shamans?
Nowhere. No one will take elemental shamans to naxx because what do they provide besides medium-dps compared to resto/enh that can heavily buff melee groups, now imagine 1 single reason people will take ele shamans to Kara40.

Elemental shaman is lacking some kind of identity over these 2.
 *if you want to make healers give passive fury/energy regen for melees thats ok.*
 *If you want to make enh give 5% haste to the group and buff melees thats ok.*
But what does elemental shaman bring to the table?
Absolutely nothing compared to these 2 specially now that other casters have this big legendary trinket that we are not even on the usable classes.

Elemental shaman should give raid leaders a reason to bring them over the other 2 specs.
Give elemental shaman caster totems so not everything revolves around melees.
You can give us 3% crit wrath totem to party
You can give us 3% haste totem
You can give us Mana tide totem in our talents
You can give us 5% haste or crit to party proc on lb/cl
These are just a few ideas on what you can give elemental shaman to make it useful and just not a pile of crap, fuck we cant even compete in dps with the other casters in raw dps because we get OOM in 3 CL.
You guys are leaving elemental shamans behind and you dont realize youre forcing players to play something they dont want to play.

You can even buff the 3.5 set to make us useful to the raid groups or smt because compared with resto 3set piece, ele and enh is trash.

There's a few thigs that would be interesting to see for elemental shaman.

Are the changes nice? Yes.
Are they sufficient? No.

There's still no use for fire totem for an elemental. No; walking the extra 30 yards to drop a fire nova totem that hits 2-4s later for a sapper+, with a 12s cooldown, or magma totem or searing totems that hit for peanuts are not good spells. Any shaman trying to pump in a raid will not use any of the fire totems other than in a few select and very specific moments.

Elemental and enhance were the 2 most useless specs in classic (honorable mention to boomkin). TBC fixed it completely and it didn't take much, and still kept the support identity of the shamen anyways Ele Mastery should be a 21 pts talent like Nature swiftness. Not capstone. It's not good enough.

The electrify talent is nice but it's a gimmick that no other class has to go through because, again, it's a gimmick. Give a talent, like everyone else, where crits just are 100% more. That's literally what it used to be.

Having Flame Shock redirect Searing totem as a talent, that's a slap in the face. Why it just wasn't added baseline fix to the game, I'm not sure if it's just to laugh at us or possibly the lack of ideas with talents. And then again, the fire damage crit thing should be with the other crit damage talent.

Let's circle back the totem support. TBC added Wrath of air and Totem of Wrath. Why not have added them here? You can always change them slightly. Why not have a TWoW version baseline of wrath of air totem that gives +2% haste. Not just spell haste, melee haste. Gives the flexibility to run that instead of windfury, or swit h for different packs and circumstances, and also helps casters get a buff from an air totem.

For the constant fire totem, why not have something that procs adds a chance on hit effect to members of the group, kinda like flametonue but also casters. A chance to deal an extra 25 fire damage per cast? A chance to cauterize a heal for 50 more per cast?

As for a 31 points talent, why not make it a fun spell with a cooldown? Why not make a mix of magma and fire totem that generates no threat, with a CD? That could be cool. 5 pulses every 2 seconds for 10 seconds (for 80% current fire nova damage) and generates no threat. 3min cooldown. Now that's a nice aoe spell. Something like that. Something you want to have.

We're close to the finish line with ele shamen. Real close. We're just not there yet. Hopefully people suggest enough ideas to have changes that hit the nail on the head
