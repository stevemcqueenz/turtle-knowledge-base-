# Druid changes feedback + additional suggestions

## Post 104144 by Templar85 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-10-21T17:12:00Z

I think the Balance tree changes are 4.5 out of 5. The Resto tree changes 3.5/4 out of 5. Feral changes for bears 3/3.5 out of 5 for Cats 2/2.5 out of 5.

After the patch note there are still issues with druids which has not been addressed at all.

- Armor cap stayed the same.

- Glancing blows for feral white hits.

- Changing Idols trigger GCD

- Innervate mana regen based on the target's spirit rather than the druid's stats.

- Druids doesn't have enemy's nature resistance lowering spell.

- Roots can't be used indoors.

- Feral Swiftness talent only increases the movement speed in outdoors.

- No defence increase talent in the talent tree.

- No resurrecting spell which make dungeoning better experience for resto druids.

- No purge resistance on druids hots spells.

- Tree of Life still very bad for pvp wise.

- Bleed style damage overall output is the same or lower than powershifting style damage output. If powershifting druid can stay on the target 100% during the fight then his/her damage should be higher than bleeds otherwise bleeds damage can be higher.

First of all I give suggestions about these then later about the talent changes.

Armor Cap should be higher than current and make a solution to eliminate glancing blows for cat white attacks.

Make Innervate more effective for Shaman tank and Paladin tank.
Current Innervate effectiveness is based on targets spirit based mana regen.
It is ideal for Resto druids, Priest and Resto shamans. For anybody else it is not much as effective because their gear and spec are not prioritising spirit.

Reworked Innervate
Regenerate the target 40% of it's Mana or 35% of caster druid max Mana on the target of Innervate. Whichever provides more mana that will be activated. Lasts for 20 seconds

Roots can be used indoors but it is weakened than if used in outdoors. Can cast 3 times on the same target in a 40 seconds timeframe. For the 4th attempt the target becomes immune to Roots additionally when the root effect ends causes the target to be enraged for 3 seconds increasing melee and ranged damage done by 20% and gain 20% movement speed for 3 seconds.
Indoor Roots target can only be humanoids, beasts, demons and undead.

Revive a dead player back to life. Can use in outdoor Battleground and in Dungeons. Required Ironwood Seed reagent Can use out of combat only 10 sec cast time. Can not use in Raid.
This help in dungeons to resurrect dead players without using combat ress, but it does have effect for raids.

Balance tree talent ideas

Improved Innervate

While Innervate active target has 35% chance to resist silence effects. Purging or removing causes high amount of mana burn for the remover.
It is nice for personal use for pvp and good for Shaman and Paladin tanks when they facing silencing mechanics.

Improved Wrath

 5 Ranks
Reduces cast time of your wrath spell by 0.5 seconds additionally druid gaining 5 spell penetration buff which stack up 5 times, lasts 15 second and increases If there is Insects swarm on the target than increase Wrath damage by 2%.

Altogether 25 spell penetration self buff and 10% more Wrath damage if there is Insect Swarm on the target.

Resto tree talent Ideas

Give purge resistance for hots.
The TBC talent Subtlety in Resto tree is the perfect combination of a pve and pvp function in one talent.
[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/WnXVjMr/tbcsubtet.jpg)](https://imgbb.com/)

Natures swiftness can be used for Balance spells but the cd increased to 4 minutes if used this way.

 1 more Swiftmend charge or reduce Swiftmend cooldown if used on somebody when had 40% life or lower.

Empowered Healing Touch. If the Healing Touch healed at least 60% of the target missing health then
the targets gain a buff.
- HT Rank 1 to 5 All attributes increased by 3% and all resistances by 10. Lasts 8 seconds

- HT Rank 6 to 9 All attributes increased by 6% and all resistances by 25. Lasts 8 seconds

- HT Rank 10 to 11 All attributes increased by 10% and all resistances by 40. Lasts 8 seconds
Regrowth and Tree of Life got nice buffs, Healing Touch spec got behind of them. With this it will be as strong as the other two.

Tree of Life Increases Armor by 500% furthermore ranged physical attacks do 20% less but fire spells do 20% more damage. New Tree of Life inclusive defensive spell. Mass roots
 25 yard max casting range. Can root up to 4 targets in a circle. 5 min CD.

Feral tree talent ideas

Rake and Rip needs more spell coefficiency from Attack Power.
Omen of Clarity proc can be used to cast Tigers Fury for free.
Shifting out of feral form does not remove the aura immediately. Fading away after 2 sec.

Additional mechanics to the current ones

 **Brutal impact**

Increases the duration of Bash and Pounce effect 0.5/1 seconds **additionally Savage Bite while above 75% rage disarm target for 2/3 seconds.**  Using Savage Bite against players while above 75% rage trigger a 45 second internal CD on the Disarm part of the spell.

 **Feral Charge**

In Bear form causes you to charge an enemy, immobilizing and interrupting any spell cast for 4 sec and **additionally in Cat form charge to an enemy dazing them for 3 sec and resetting their current spell cast time to the beginning.**
Cat don't have interrupt. Giving them spell reset helping them out in pvp.

 **Thick Hide**

Increases the Armor contribution from your items by 10% **additionally increases defence skill by 5-10-15 and any type of hot effect on the bear by 15%** Easier to reach crit immunity so bear can have more threat generation gear than defence focused gear.

 **Sharpened Claws**

Increases your critical strike chance while in Bear, Dire Bear and Cat form **additionally swipe affect 2 more targets for 75% damage.**

 **Improved Shred**

Reduces the energy cost of your Shred ability by 6/12 **additionally can cast from front but causes 80% damage only.**
There is already a clunky way of front Shreding. Making it more streamline but there is a damage penalty if not doing from the back.

 **Feral Swiftness**

Increases your movement speed by 15/30% while outdoor **additionally by 15% while indoors** and increases your chance to dodge by 2%/4% in Cat and Dire Bear from.
Movement speed increase indoor should been a normal thing. If 15% to high then can be lower.

 **Ravage doesn't need Prowl to be cast from, but does not award combo point used this way. Casted from Prowl awards 2 combo points instead of 1 and better spell coefficiency.**
Ravage is an underused ability, Mostly used in PvP an opener against cloth users.

 **Feral Adrenaline**

Idea of Thraxeus
 **After suffering a crushing blow you gain 25/50/75% dodge until you have dodged an attack" 40/30/20 Second CD**

New feral capstone talents

 **Alpha Cat**

 3% physical critical chance for the group **additionally reduces Claw, Rake, Rip energy cost by 10 and increases maximum Energy to  110.**

 **Ancient Bear**

 3% physical critical chance for the group **additionally In Dire Bear form reduces Any Incoming Damage by 5% and by 20% below 50% HP just for the Bear itself.**
Overall damage reduction good in all situation but especially against spells which armor does not reduce and against critical strikes, crushing blows to reduce the incoming spike damage.

Brand new talents:

 **Adamantium claws**

While the target below 70% HP ignore 10% of their armor. While below 40% HP ignore their 20% of armor. Affected spells are claw, rake, maul, swipe.
Attacking a target from the back by claw, rake, swipe additionally ignore their armor by 5% independently by the target health.

This is a nice personal dps boost talent for raids against high armored Boses. Especially good while the raid members are not geared much and can not reduce the Boss's Armor to low or to minimal.
As the Raid progresses and can place more armor reducing effect on to the Boss making it almost zero then this talent losses its huge dps increase potential.

 **Finish the prey off**

While the target HP 35% below front shreding causes the same damage as back shreding and has 25% chance to cast Tigers Fury automatically without energy cost.

Brand new spells:

 **Anger of the Forest**

In Bear or Dire Bear form can cast an aoe stun spell. Requires 80 rage 10 yard radius. Stun last for 2 seconds. 3 Minute CD
This is a Tauren Warstomp in druid from but requires high amount of rage.

 **Wild Ride**

Increase group speed by 35% for 10 seconds 3 min cooldown Requires Cat form. Placing Dash spell on Cooldown too.
Can use in pvp situations to help team mates who get snared or can use in pve where have react to dodge a mechanic.

Roar of the Forest

Increase group HP by 5% for 10 seconds 3 min cooldown Requires Bear, Dire Bear form. Placing Berserk spell on Cooldown too.
Useful for both pve and pvp situation to mitigate burst damage.
