# Dear devs, Reshift is not the druid fix we were hoping for…

## Post 145830 by Maoeu — 2025-08-15T17:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145830#p145830 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Reshift lets us leave and re-enter the same form, but druids’ main request was different.
We want the ability to leave any form and instantly cast any spell in a single click, like in WoW Classic 1.13 with /cancelform.
Currently, casting a spell from Cat/Bear/Travel Form takes two separate clicks, which feels clunky and unnecessary.

Example: In Cat Form, I want to root an enemy. In WoW Classic 1.13, I press my “Root” macro → Cat Form is canceled instantly → Roots is cast right away.
In Turtle WoW, I press my “Root” macro → nothing happens until I press again, because the first click only removes the form.

This is not about faster shifting into the same form.
This is about making shapeshift cancellation part of any spell cast, without needing a separate ability for each form.
