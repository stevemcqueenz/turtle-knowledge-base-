# We need to talk about Hunter Tier 1 and Tier 2 Sets.

## Post 60035 by Keds (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-30T13:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60035#p60035 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While we all can agree that getting the 8 pieces set bonus is good in both sets, getting each individual upgrade feels kinda lame when some pieces have LOWER agility than the previous tier, you can see that happening to mages aswell, but we don't have spell power to compensate like they do. Upgrading from tier 1 to 2 gives us:
> 19 Agility
>  6 Stamina
>  44 Intelect ??
>  17 Spirit
>  346 Armor
>  +4 Fire Res
>  +4 Shadow Res
>  +10 Nature, Frost, Arcane Res
>  -2% Hit Chance
>  +2% Crit Chance

So, looking at a progression poit of view, basically, if you have Full tier 1 you are almost as good as a full t2 hunter. Looking at logs we can see that our damage stagnate as we start naxx, and I trully believe this is one of things that really don't scale that well.

That being said, I don't want to make hunters OP, but maybe change the 3,5 set bonuses so we don't have to wait for the full 8 pieces to actually see some actually change in our DPS, maybe change Tier 1 to be some sort of BM set, while Tier 2 is more MM focused, since they are kinda interchangeble anyways
