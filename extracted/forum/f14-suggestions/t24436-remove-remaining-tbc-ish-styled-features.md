# Remove remaining TBC-ish/styled features

## Post 171884 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-23T08:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171884#p171884 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

For example, the TBC-styled weapons with pink shining crystal shards are fine, but when they are associated with the Draenei, they just remind us that they are from a TBC feature, such as a quest, an Eredar-Draenei instance or Exodar in TBC. According to my conservative knowledge about Warcraft, the Draenei and Orc are "ancient enemies". No Velen or Exodar should exist in Turtle WoW's universe. Blood elves (and the Forsaken) don't need to balance the playerbase by joining the Horde.

In both this forum and game, I have seen too many people discussing TBC elements and assuming those features to be implemented in TWoW in the future. I never take TBC as a canonical trajectory of story development. I'd rather say that Warcraft III is a better root for basing the plot on and WoW itself is a retcon.

In terms of TBC, it's a result of the expansion-oriented policy as Activision exerted control over Blizzard. Too many features in TBC and later expansions go too far. One of the most annoying facts in game is that most people, not a few, would expect those things. In other words, it is only a tiny proportion of players who truly care about lore preservation. These people with lore-purist inclination are looked down to and mocked.

Lastly, TWoW would become another retail, private server like Ascenxxon and Epxxh or Netease garbage if its developers were obsessed with those retcon changes. Adjustment needs to be carried out by removing those lore-disruptive features.
