I envision the crit damage being implemented like this:

Searing Light Holy Talent reworked
 2 ranks
![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/large/spell_holy_searinglightpriest.jpg)
Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Smite, Holy Fire and Holy Nova by 25/50%, against Undeads and Demons by 50/100%.

Replacing the original "increases smite/HF damage by 5/10%",
with "increases smite/HF/HN critical damage by 25/50%", which is arguably more enjoyable.
Shadow Priest doesn't deal with critical hits, so it'd be nice to have offensive holy taking up that role to a greater extend.

I've made it 25/50%, instead of 50/100% (except against undeads & demons for RP flavor), in order to add also this:

 **Holy Fire also providing Holy damage utility;**
Holy Damage done to the target is increased by 10% during the 10 second DoT from Holy Fire.

New talents:

Divine Providence
Holy Talent
![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/large/spell_holy_divineprovidence.jpg)
Casting Smite grants you 'Divine Providence', which increases your Holy Fire and Holy Nova critical chance by 3% for 12 seconds. Stacks up to 5 times and will be spent once Holy Fire or Holy Nova are used.

Holy Word: Anathema
Holy Talent Ability
![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/large/spell_fire_elementaldevastation.jpg)
Applies 'Anathema' on an enemy for 10 seconds; allowing one Holy healing ability to be cast on the target, but instead of healing, it'll deal half of it's healing value as Holy damage. Against Undeads and Demons, the value is not halved. 45 sec cooldown.

 *Next to Holy Champion, with Spiritual Guidance as a prerequisite (Spiritual Healing position moved sideways out of the way). For all except trolls, the Holy spec in general doesn't fit them.*

Changes to current talents:

Mental Strength
(exchanging position with)
Force of Will

An offensive holy priest build would be more viable and rewarding if these two talents position was swapped in the discipline tree.
This way an offensive holy priest can get both Force of Will from the discipline tree and Spiritual Guidance from the Holy tree. There would still be a choice to be made between Spiritual Guidance and Power Infusion, but it wouldn't feel like a loose-loose choice anymore.

Martyr
Add to Martyr talent in the Discipline tree that apart from being critted triggering Martyr, being hit while affected by the Weakened Soul debuff has a 15/30% chance to trigger Martyr as well.

## Post 75466 by Bob022 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-02T14:18:00Z

I've long been under the impression that Smite is simply the main offense-oriented spell for healer priests.  It's how they defeat stuff if they're out in the world trying to do quests or whatnot.  That's why it has a few talents to improve it in the middle of what's otherwise the priest's main healing tree.  It was never meant to be competitive as a primary damage-dealer--that's what the Shadow tree is for.  Smite seems to do what it's supposed to do, within the framework of that intent.  Now, if Turtle-WoW said someplace that a Smite-based setup was supposed to be a competitive damage dealer in its own right, please correct me.  I've been here awhile, but not since day 1.

To be completely fair, WoW's tank and healer builds tend to be somewhat poorly-designed because 2002-2004 was still early enough in the genre's history that there were fears that allowing either of them to do competitive damage would make them too strong and overshadow the offense-oriented roles.  History, demonstrated by newer games in the genre since, has shown such fears to be largely unfounded.  As such this post should not be perceived as opposition to the original suggestion--more like a question of intent.
