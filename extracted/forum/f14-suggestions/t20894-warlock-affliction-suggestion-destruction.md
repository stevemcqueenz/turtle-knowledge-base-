Hi, it seems each spec is being made to be viable by itself and has new tier sets and items to accomodate them.

Well, 1 idea for Affliction Warlock is to "Remove their critical strike" because the use of Drains and DoT's doesn't benefit from it at all except on Nightfall procs for shadow bolt

idea :
Change Shadow Mastery from:
"Increases the damage dealt or life drained by your Shadow spells by 2/4/6/8/10%."

to include:

"Reduces your Spell Critical Strike by 2/4/6/8/10% and increases your Attack and Casting Speed by 2/4/6/8/10%" per talent point"

something along those lines anyway, it seems like Affliction Warlock can't fully benefit from a lot of end game items, for instance both Legendaries Atiesh and Scythe of Elune both have crit on them. also PVP gear set is based around Destruction.

to make it not ruin Destruction Warlocks, since they spec into Shadow Mastery to play SM/Ruin. well then you can change or add a new talent in Destro for Emberstorm to either include Shadow damage or add shadow as a new Keystone. This would allow destro players more access to other spells in destro and free up affliction to be the "non crit focus" tree.
