This may sound like heresy but I've been thinking about whether Turtlewow should have (probably in its own bg) a fortnite-style battle arena. It wouldn't be divided along faction lines and would have either a free-for-all or team setting. Due to how Fortnite's battle arena mode completely took over the game, and to avoid drawing in that crowd and overwhelming the server, it would be periodic (a week here or there maybe in lulls between seasonal events).

As for what the lore would be behind people abandoning their faction; goblins and greed. The Steamweedle Cartel or similar is in the middle of what would be the fantasy equivilent to a gold rush and its drawn people in to get their share of the spoils.

Until I started writing this out I didn't have a clear idea what the map would look like but what about an actual mine system with the shrinking game border being the caves slow collapse?

Admittedly this would take a lot to design, program, and bug test so it should probably be made its own suggestion at some point.
