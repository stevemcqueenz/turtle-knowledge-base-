# Vendor Pricing for Green Gear issue/suggestion

## Post 108374 by Cosmiccleric — 2024-11-17T21:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108374#p108374 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In Swamp of Sorrows, at The Harborage, there is a vendor, Masat T'andr, who is selling "Outland Exile Hood" and "Harborage War-Mace", both green items. The items are great and would be very nice to own/have, while leveling through the zone. There's just one problem.

The price. They sell for 7g50s each.

As someone who has a level 42 character, and is leveling through the zone, I onlyh have 2g33s to my name. I'm smart with my money, I don't spend money at the AH, and I don't buy all of the spells for my class/spec, and I still struggle to have enough gold to purchase new spells at the next even level threshold.

My suggestion would be to lower the price on these items, if the devs want them to be actually purchased by leveling characters/players. Otherwise, by the time someone can afford them (assuming they do NOT have a level 60 character with lots of gold already to fund the purchase for them) they've outleveled them and ignore them.

Please reconsider the prices (unless these are just meant to be gold sinks for existing players with level 60 mains, then carry on I guess).

Thanks.
