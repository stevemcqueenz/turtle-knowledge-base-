# Buff Dragon's Touch SP

## Post 37409 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-11T17:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37409#p37409 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

I would like to ask for a small change here:

Add extra SP to Dragon's Touch.

I believe it is problematic that the revered Quel'dorei Magister's Spellflinger/Revantusk Mystic's Mojobender
is better than an epic weapon from BWL.

Honestly I have been running endgame raids for almost two years, and yet I still have Quel'dorei because Dragon's Touch is completely useless. Now, you may say that there are other options, but ye, good luck making them drop.

What I am asking for here is improved balance between the items. A very easy to obtain item like the Revered Quel'dorei can be good, but a BWL item with the exact same function (and furthermore being an epic item) should of course be better.

Perhaps you can increase the spellpower of Dragon's Touch to 18 - the same value as the, supposedly lower levelled, Touch of Chaos from Hakkar.

For item level reference and comparison:

Dragon's Touch: 75

Touch of Chaos: 68

Quel'dorei Magister's Spellflinger: 65

Dragon's Touch has some extra stats, but as you can see, the tier and the item level difference should make an SP buff perfectly legitimate (Alternatively you can nerf the int or stam on it a little).

What do you guys think?
