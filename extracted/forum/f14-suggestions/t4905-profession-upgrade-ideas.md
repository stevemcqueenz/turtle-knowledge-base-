# Profession Upgrade Ideas

## Post 29538 by Kaizod — 2022-11-20T06:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29538#p29538 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Inspired by Blutsdrache post ([viewtopic.php?f=14&t=2560&p=15874&hilit=riddles#p15874](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=2560&p=15874&hilit=riddles#p15874)) , I wanted to specifically to target profession ideas.

 **Carpentry + Lumberjack**
First - I think Carpentry is a more eloquent name for the profession, woodworking sounds kind of simple, imo. Then, I also think that Lumberjacking/ gathering wood should actually be all part of the same profession. That way, it doesn't waste a primary profession slot AND one could be a Leatherworker also to make their own bows. To have to gather wood as a primary profession AND only be able supply the lumber for *just* carpenters is kind of lame and inefficient.
Carpenters would make staves, wands, polearms, bows, crossbows(parts from eng?)
Blutsdrache's idea about the different kind of wood to gather is spot on, i think fits really well and is creative.

 **JewelCrafting**
I wholeheartedly agree with the idea of porting Jewelcrafting to Vanilla, while only using the 1-300 recipes like Blutsdrache mentioned: amulets, ring, trinkets, fist weapons, the stones you drop that heal you a bit:
([https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/spell=26873 ... olden-hare](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/spell=26873/figurine-golden-hare))
([https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/spell=32808 ... one-statue](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/spell=32808/solid-stone-statue))
([https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/spell=25612 ... n-knuckles](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/spell=25612/heavy-iron-knuckles))
Getting use out of those gems in vanilla would be awesome. Maybe other baubles and toys like those Diamond Focusing lenses.

 **Inscription**
I also think Inscription would be a welcome addition. While I am against adding glyphs or the glyph system like WOTLK, there are MANY other uses of Inscription:
 - Scrolls (+str, +agil, etc)
 - Town Portal
 - Darkmoon Faire cards
 - Offhand tomes/ books
 - Paladin Librams, other Relics
 *- The runes of warding (+dmg reduction for chest), wards of shielding (+dmg reduction for shields) from TBC Blacksmithing => I think these fit better with Inscription (your inscribing runes/wards onto armor), and also gives more use/recipes out of the profession.
[https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/item=23559/ ... of-warding](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/item=23559/lesser-rune-of-warding)
 *-Enchanting Velums!! These would allow enchanters to sell their recipes on the AH, instead of always having to hunt down a buyer/seller.

This would also get solid use out of Herbalism - providing for 2 professions instead just one.

 **Engineering**
Wondering how everyone would feel about adding the Thermal Anvil recipe to engineering. I know, it's such a cheesy shortcut, but we could make it be very expensive and consumable. So if you *really* didn't feel like running all the way to the anvil or forge in your town, you have one right there in your bags.
(<https://www.wowhead.com/item=87216/thermal-anvil>)
Also, add an engineering toolbox for carrying strictly eng items.

 **Tailoring/ Leatherworking**
Bags for all professions like Mining, LW, JC, etc.

 **All Profs**
Harder recipes can give +2 +3 or +5 skill-ups to make leveling profs less sadistic.
Daily prof quests that give +2 skillups like those added in Cata. (Probably will get told to go play retail, but wanted to gauge interest.) But if not for all, at least for fishing.

I also like this guys ideas for workbenches for each prof:
[viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3518](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3518)

Finally, would be awesome to have larger stack sizes for many kinds of items.

## Post 29603 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-11-21T14:14:00Z

I've responded first to the post that inspired you, and now I've dived into this one:

 **Carpentry + Lumberjack**
I'd like the lumberjack (I prefer the profession to be called logging) to be a primary gathering profession, it's as basic as mining or skinning so it'd make sense.

I agree with you that logging should not provide only for woodworking (I prefer that term, I think carpentry should be a category of woodworking), but also for other crafting professions, where recipes of items that requires wood should have a various logging node types as a required ingredient.

 **JewelCrafting**
I agree with you that if it was ported, which I'm in favor of, it should include item socketing, however not to the extend of it becoming a requirement, I've explained it in detail in the other post.

 **Inscription**
A good addition, it would, just like the other profession suggestions deepen the game and would fit in as it exists in the game world already.

However, in spite of me liking a lot individual ability personalizations allowed by glyphs in WotLK, I am against it, because lore wise it makes absolutely no sense to alter any school of magic and especially not the physical abilities with magical glyphs. I'd like ability personalization, it could be great since yes you learn something from a trainer but you could develop your own way to use it perhaps enhancing one aspect at the expense of another, but only in ways that make sense, not with gamey things like the glyphs, that's already the touch of Activision who don't understand RPG worldbuilding at all.

 **Engineering**
The anvil is somewhat immersion breaking in my opinion.
I like the idea of the engineering toolbox.

 **Tailoring/Leatherworking**
It's a standardization but I think it'd make sense.

 **All Profs**
I agree that there should be recipes that grand more profession skill and I also think that there should be some form of internal diminishing return, so crafting the exact same thing on repeat, because it has the best cheap ingredients to skill reward ratio, would be discouraged, of course only if it's substituted with meaningful alternative skill gains, so leveling a profession efficiently is not an artificial grind but more immersive.
I don't like the idea of profession dailies rewarding skill points though.
