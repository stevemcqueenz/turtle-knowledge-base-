# Playing battleground should give rested xp

## Post 164863 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-01-11T05:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164863#p164863 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Situation now:
there are strong players in every bracket, who have good gear and enchants.
WoW is a game about gear, so gear difference is inevitable.
if normal leveling player want to get gear, he should stop xp at x8-x9 level and should do some dungeons and/or crafting.
battlegrounds grant normal xp now. So it produce false impression that it is possible to level in battlegrounds.
having low level teammate is bad for everyone. For team it is -1 player, for that player it is frustration because of impossible fights.
attracting regular levelers on battleground is good idea to improve queue times
xp is best attractor of regular levelers

The problem with levelers is that best way for them to join battleground is at x8-x9 level. But they need xp at all levels below x9.

So idea is:
Don't grant xp immediately. Grant "rested xp" instead. So people can stop at x9 level, do some preparations in dungeons. Then play in battlegrounds until they get enough "rested xp" to quickly skip x1-x5 levels of next bracket in first hour and then start to prepare to next bracket in dungeons.
It might be in a form of BoP "xp scrolls". Maybe repeatable mark quest should grant xp instead.
Main idea is that you play battleground now and get xp later, on-demand.
