# Remove mana cost from Warlock and Paladin mounts

## Post 38781 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-24T01:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38781#p38781 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

yes, yes I know, it makes RPG sense that these two mounts are spells casted by the player, and so them costing mana does make sense.

BUT

Because these mounts cost mana, they reset the 5SR and this has a noticable impact on your mana while leveling and in pvp, really doing anything. As such, these mounts are strictly subpar to normal mounts which don't cost an effective buttload of mana.

If the mana cost were removed on these mounts, they'd save that mana, and your 5SR mana regen would continue unabated. This will allow good warlocks and paladin players to use their cool RPGey class mounts, instead of homogenizing themselves into using normal mounts.

We lose some RPG flavor in return for better RPG flavor and less annoyance.
