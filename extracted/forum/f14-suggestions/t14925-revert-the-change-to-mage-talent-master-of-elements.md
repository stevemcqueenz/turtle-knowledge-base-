# Revert the change to mage talent Master of Elements

## Post 101573 by Sark21 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-21T06:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101573#p101573 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm gonna start this off with each class is SUPPOSE to be different and have their strengths and weaknesses. There is absolutely nothing wrong with one class being able to do things another class can't that's the point of having different classes. A warlock is meant to provide massive single target damage and curses, a priest is meant to drop a dot nuke and afk, a mage is meant to do massive aoe damage to trash, an elemental shaman is meant to drop windfury totem for melee.

Going into it I'm just really late to this train but a nerf was done to the mages Master of Elements talent which gives 10%>20%>30% mana cost back on a crit from fire or frost spell when hit. My question is why was this messed with at all...

"Your Fire and Frost spells criticals will refund 10%>20%>30% of their base mana cost."
No where does it state in the text of the talent that its meant for a single target critical refund in fact states "criticals" meaning more than one target getting crit.

The lack of mana return is extremely noticeable now when in a raid/group when trying to aoe trash packs down. Pre-nerf you'd have to pop either a mana pot or tea but now you have to use both teas, mana pot and even an evo CD if the mobs arent dead yet for damn near every pull because your going completely oom. A mage single dmg is not great compared to a warlocks shadowbolt crit from sac/ruin, or even shadow priest with 3 dot lol nuke. The other side of that coin a warlocks aoe dmg is not great compared to a mage because its not suppose to be each class has it's reason for being.

Feels like turtle team is making all class groups (melee, casters, tanks, heals) bland and damn near the same but with different mechanics of playing them we're suppose to be different and stronger/weaker in different scenarios.
