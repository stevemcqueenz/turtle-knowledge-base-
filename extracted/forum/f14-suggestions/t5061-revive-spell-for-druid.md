## Post 82171 by Jc473 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-12-21T14:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82171#p82171 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think people are either forgetting or underestimating the fact that the Druid is the only class with an in-combat res. This is insanely powerful and can turn a seemingly lost cause on its head (e.g res a dead tank).

If you give druids a normal res then you’d either have to remove Rebirth or make it a deep Resto talent. Personally, I think it’s really interesting game design to have a healer who doesn’t have a spammable res ability. If anything, I think it would be better to lean into the Rebirth ability by improving it via deep Resto talents.

Ideas:
 1) 2 point talent which reduces the CD by 25%/50%
 2) 1 point talent which gives Rebirth two charges. So, it would be possible to perform a double res if you haven’t used the ability for an hour.
 3) While in Tree of Life form, the Rebirth CD timer is greatly accelerated. Maybe three times as fast? So, effectively, a ten minute CD.
