# PvP Suggestion For complete the 1.18.1 Patch

## Post 172301 by Tylaz — 2026-03-25T07:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172301#p172301 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Add Marks and reputation for the Thorn Gorge battleground. Without that, the battleground is doomed to die out, and I think it’s a shame to invest time in a battleground that will disappear after a while.

Add 2 or 3 weekly quests for PvP. Example:

Win at least once in each Battleground

Thorn Gorge wins: 0/1
Arathi Basin wins: 0/1
Alterac Valley wins: 0/1
Warsong Gulch wins: 0/1

Another example:

Get 1,000 Honorable kills.

And offer rewards similar to the Weekly PvE chests.

 1) This will allow PvP players who wish to do so to devote themselves entirely to PvP while earning some gold and no longer playing at a loss.

 2) This will increase interest in PvP and thus make it even more dynamic or revitalize it.
