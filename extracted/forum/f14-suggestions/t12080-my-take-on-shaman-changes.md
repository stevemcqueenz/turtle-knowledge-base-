# My take on shaman changes

## Post 80074 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-06T16:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80074#p80074 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

My take on the shaman tweaks.

Shamans are defined way too much by WF and chain heal. Nerf their base form but give the good versions to specific builds (good WF for enh, good chain heal for resto). It opens up design space.

Ele -
 * give flametomgue totem spell power
 * increase searing totem's proc rate (decrease the damage if needed)
 * give them a mana regen talent
 * increase shock's radius to match ele's lightning bolt radius
 * give a spell penetration talent to them

ele becomes a caster party utility with better mana management

Enh -
 * give improved WF only to them, it equals current improved WF
 * give some kind of CC for leveling
 * make stormstrike free, on a 1 min CD
 * allow thunderhead to cast any shield on you and on a selected party member at the same time
 * keep BL

enh becomes a melee support DPS build with too bad healing to be forced to the current BL resto hybrid

Resto
 * give chain heal only to them
 * seriously increase mana and life totem effectiveness (life totem should almost feel like an HOT)
 * fuse disease and poison cleansing totems
 * remove manatide totem, give them healing rain as an ultimate (area healing with 1-2 min CD where x% of the amount healed is restored as mana for the shaman)

resto becomes a 'wide healer' specialist with better mana management
