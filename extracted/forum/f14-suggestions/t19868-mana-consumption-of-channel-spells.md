# Mana consumption of channel spells.

## Post 135688 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-14T11:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135688#p135688 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, channel spells, with the exception of a few, consume 100% of all mana at once at the beginning. Regular spells consume mana only at the end of the cast. In PVE and especially in PVP, it happens that a channel spell has to be interrupted prematurely, or it will be interrupted. But the mana is already 100% spent, even if you inflicted 1 tick.

Suggestion. Make all channel spells consume 30-50% of the required mana at the beginning, and the rest for each tick during the channel action.
