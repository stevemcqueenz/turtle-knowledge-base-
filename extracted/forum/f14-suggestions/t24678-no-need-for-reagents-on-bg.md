# No need for reagents on bg

## Post 173781 by Uruguaio420 — 2026-04-02T17:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173781#p173781 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Is there any way to remove the need for reagents inside battlegrounds?

It’s quite frustrating that every time you join a BG, you need to have a large number of reagents (for example, 500 soul shards as a Warlock) just to be able to play. Every time you die, resurrecting your pet consumes a shard instantly.

Other classes are also affected by this, such as Druids, Rogues, Priests, and Paladins.

At least in battlegrounds, reagents could be made unlimited so that it’s fair for everyone. I don’t think this would cause any imbalance. Sometimes players just want to log in and start playing BGs without having to farm or gather reagents first.
