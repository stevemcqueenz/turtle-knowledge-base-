# Add Scaling to Seal of Light, Judgement of Light (only for the person who triggers it)

## Post 17130 by Velite (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-02-19T20:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17130#p17130 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I believe this suggestion will fix the issue that Protection paladins suffer when it comes to itemization. Namely, that all the tier gear is either not useful, or only useful when healing, despite being PLATE gear which one would assume is best for melee combat.

Here is the issue with Seal of Light, compared to Seal of Wisdom which is superior in design:

Over the course of vanilla, while maximum mana increases, your mana consumption is constant. Abilities do not cost more mana as time goes on (barring new spell ranks), which means that the mana regeneration you need to do the job can be constant. Therefore, Seal and Judgement of Wisdom are perfectly implemented and well designed.

Over the course of vanilla, ***while maximum health increases, the amount of damage you take varies on the situation, and not only that, the amount of damage you will take as a tank will increase the higher the content progresses. This means that the rate at which you need healing is not constant, and trends upwards. Seal of Light being a STATIC source of healing in this situation means that its power decreases as time goes on. Therefore, no Protection paladin ever uses seal of light in endgame content. There is always a better seal to use instead.***

Here are my suggestions:

 1) Seal of light should have 15-20% scaling with +healing/+spell damage and healing. The reason I am suggesting this is so that protection paladins have a reason to use seal of light as content becomes more difficult, and as the paladin's health pool increases in a situation where threat is not needed (ie offtanking mobs on trash). The percentage can be debated, but the fact remains that the ability should have some kind of scaling so that it remains a viable choice to use in certain situations. Along with this, it encourages ***paladins to choose spell power/healing plate gear for tanking, instead of warrior based strength/defensive gear, which allows them to have backup gear for healing without needing the dedicated healing gear that regular healers want.***

 2) Judgement of light should have 10% scaling with +healing/+spell damage and healing ***from the player that triggers it, NOT the player who places the judgement.*** We saw in the recent update that having a judgement of light scale off of +healing from the paladin is very powerful and trivializes certain encounters. However, having it scale from the person who triggers it means that spell based melee builds such as paladins/shamans/druids could get a bonus from it, which are generally speaking underperforming in this role currently.

The overall goal of these suggestions is to make the current bad itemization with paladin tier gear useful for more than just healing. The end result of these changes should mean that an inherent advantage of a Protection paladin over other tanks is that they will, in general, need less healing to do their job, as they are able to support themselves in healing as their gear improves.

As I have experience with raid tanking here, I will include a good example where SoL/JoL scaling would be nice for a protection paladin:

Golemagg Dogs. You need to hold the dogs for the entire fight. A paladin will be able to use reckoning in addition to seal and judgement of light to sustain themselves while the other tanks and dps/healers handle golemagg. While you could have a warrior or a druid do this job, they will require more healing than a paladin since the paladin will be healing themselves. And holy shield + ret aura should be able to keep threat on the dogs.

Here is a bonus example:

Another bonus situation is holding adds on Grand window Faerlina in Naxxramas. You do not need any seal to tank them since you don't really need to hold too much threat on them. Hence, healing from SoL would help alot here.

In these scenarios, I would ideally equip Tier 1/Tier 2/Tier 3 gear and tank with that to receive the bonus healing while using Seal of Light.
