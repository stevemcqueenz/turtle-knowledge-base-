I think you have a point, but it's much narrower than what your post suggests. To me it really boils down to how poorly K40 loot tables were designed.

There is not a general lack of raid drops. TWoW has already added drops - like Ouro dropping a second item and C'Thun having a chance to drop a third. And as someone else has already pointed out, by virtue of scaling TWoW already drops massively more items-per-player than Vanilla ever did. AQ20, ZG, Onyxia are regularly run by 12-15 people for instance. MC or BWL splitting loot among ~25 people is a huge boost to individual drop chance.

Secondly - this is Vanilla. Loot is scarce. That's the core of this version of WoW. You run a raid for months with up to 39 other people just to see 1 trinket or 1 weapon drop for you. You may not like that design, but it is what we all signed up for by picking a Vanilla server. And for every 1 person hating that system, there's probably 1 person who is here just because of it. KT's loot table is a trashfire, I completely agree. But changing iconic stuff from the way it was 20 years ago to me needs a *very* good justification. The default should be to keep it nostalgic.

But! There are huge glaring issues with K40 loot tables that greatly exacerbate the problem.
Token drops are styled after AQ40 with 2 types, but unlike in AQ40 you are not guaranteed to receive both kinds of tokens. Indeed you are not even guaranteed to receive a second token. This is needlessly cruel and convoluted and not only is there no historic reason for it to be that way, it actively breaks the tradition of bosses dropping 2 tokens that went from T2 all the way to T3.
Secondly some classes were just utterly forgotten about. Dps casters get their pick of 4 weapons in K40, but healers get none. All healers are still stuck competing for Hammer of the Twisting Nether from KT which puts undue pressure on that loot table. Warriors have no great alternatives for some slots so they suddenly start competing with Rogues and Feral Druids over leather drops from K40 again. This is just garbage design.

If TWoW had designed K40 differently, most issues would go away imo. No need to have every boss drop more items or to veer into personal loot in general. It's really just K40 that's the problem and that's solely on TWoW, not on Vanilla.
