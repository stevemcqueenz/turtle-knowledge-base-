I understand the desire to want to specialize classes and split them up, and even think it'd be neat to play on a server where each race had its own custom classes (heck, it worked for Warhammer Online kind of), but it isn't really in keeping with the class design philosophy of vanilla WoW and thus isn't really Vanilla+, which should be about adding onto Vanilla (such as adding new race/class options) rather than completely reworking Vanilla to the point where it's unrecognizable as Vanilla.
