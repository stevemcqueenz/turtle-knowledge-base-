# Woodcutting Profession

## Post 169141 by Neechy (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-21T18:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169141#p169141 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Suggestion to make Woodcutting its own primary gathering profession. I am kind of disappointed to see it being embedded within the Survival secondary profession as per the upcoming patchnotes.

> **Torta wrote: Fri Oct 03, 2025 10:13 am**
> **SURVIVAL SKILL OVERHAUL**
>  To give Survival additional depth, we are linking it to a new gathering activity: **Woodcutting**. Directly tied to your Survival skill, Woodcutting lets you search forests in the open world for wood and other materials needed for the profession.

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/twitter.min.html#1999243181419118690]

Reasons to make Woodcutting its own separate profession:
 1. Core Warcraft Profession: Mining and Woodcutting are the two core gathering activities since the early RTS games. Why should Mining be its own profession but not Woodcutting?
 2. Prerequisite for Crafting Professions: Notice the pattern here...
Mining (primary gathering) -> BS, Eng, and JC (primary crafting)
Skinning (primary gathering) -> LW (primary crafting)
Herbalism (primary gathering) -> Alch (primary crafting)
Woodcutting (**secondary** gathering?) -> ??
Why the inconsistency? If anything, Skinning and Herbalism are less of standalone professions than Woodcutting/Lumberjack, and would be better suited to embed in Survival...
For consistency, Woodcutting can be a primary gathering skill for numerous crafting professions including a future Carpentry profession that crafts staves, bows, wands, shields, boats, etc.
 3. Economics: A new primary gathering profession can revitalize the economy by diversifying player activities. Players can now earn money through selling lumber, thus reducing some of the insane competition for mining and herb nodes. Obtaining rare lumber nodes is now exclusive to those who choose the Woodcutting profession, rather than available to every single player, as is the case with secondary skills like Survival..
 4. Roleplay: Who doesn't want to RP as a Lumberjack?
 5. Itemization: Opens the door for more thematic flavour itemization. As with +mining skill helmets and pickaxes, or +skinning daggers, there can be +woodcutting skill for appropriate items. E.g.:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/W3QMErG.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/5athLmF.png)

As it stands, the Survival skill is looking to be too bloated and cannibalizing the possibilities of other gathering and crafting profs. I really hope that the plans for embedding Woodcutting within Survival are just temporary, and they can eventually be separated and distinct.
