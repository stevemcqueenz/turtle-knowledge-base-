## Post 113157 by Fornow (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-18T07:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113157#p113157 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The key to performing tanking/healing job in leveling dungeons is to keep a separate set of gear for each. At lower levels the impact of talents is much less pronounced but obviously certain key talents do make a difference.

The real issue I see here is bag space and affordability. Bag space is always at a premium and most players, especially new ones, won't be able to carry around a full alternate set (up to 15 items) let alone two. Storing it in the bank is cumbersome and adds additional maintenance time. It's also expensive to maintain multiply sets of gears: directly if you purchase from the auction house, indirectly if you equip self found items instead of selling them and choose quest rewards for their utility and not their highest sell value.

Experiencing and experimenting with different playstyles is a good thing and adds lots of enjoyment to the game. That's why I advocate for a wardrobe feature to be integrated into the game. It would give you multiple sets of equipment slots, so that they no longer take up bag space. Ideally this would be integrated into the current talent reset device and also store different auction bar layouts (similar to the addons outfitter and action bar profiles). Hopefully that's something that the team is looking at when developing the Unreal Engine client.
