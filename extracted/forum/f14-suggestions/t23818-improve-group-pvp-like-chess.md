# Improve group PvP like chess

## Post 167089 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-01-31T22:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167089#p167089 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

This thread is not about the gear. Lets assume that we want to balance pvp when all participants already have highest gear possible.

 **Context**
In recent patchnotes to 1.18.1 turtle devs announced class/spec specific dampening debuff.
> **Jamey wrote: Fri Jan 23, 2026 7:24 pm**
> **PvP**
>
>  The **PvP Dampening Debuff** introduced in 1.18.0 will now have dynamic reductions of Magical and Physical damage based on your class / spec instead of a flat number for all classes.
>
>  More information regarding numbers will be given at a later date.
>
>  *Developer Comment: Turning these values class-dependent means that we will be less restricted in Design space of making all specs come to life and be viable without adding Player-target exceptions on a lot of spells.*

My proposal is to replace class-specific dampening debuff with something that can be used to outplay opponents.

 **Introduction**
WoW is group-based pvp. Lets assume that each character is some figure on the chessboard. Two teams are like two players against eachother with multiple different figures.

We all know that in chess if one figure attacks another, then victim figure just dies.
In WoW it might look like one player nukes another with some combination of skills.

Why oneshots in chess are fine and in wow oneshots are not fine?
Reason is - figures in chess become vulnerable when they attack another figure.
If you attack, you probably sacrifice attacking figure because retaliation might kill it. (It might not, but it depends on other factors).

 **Idea**
Give every race-class combo baseline passive skill: "when this character dies by other character, his killer gets debuff".
Debuffs should be strong and varied. Duration should be like 1 minute, can't be dispelled. They are aimed to make the killer vulnerable to retaliation of enemy team.
"Reduce melee damage dealt and increase arcane damage taken of killer by 30%"
"Reduce nature damage dealt and increase shadow damage taken of killer by 30%"
"Reduce fire damage dealt and armor by 30%"
"Reduce melee damage dealt and increase shadow damage taken of killer by 30%".
"Reduce frost damage dealt by 30% and stealth level by 10".

 **Additional ideas showed in this thread**
 1. In PvP death, give died player buff, which reduces damage from the class which just killed him. So if you died from rogue, after respawn you get 30% reduction of incoming damage from all rogues until next death or 20 minutes.
 2. In PvP death, give buff to adjacent allies to reduce damage from the class which performed kill.

I think OPs suggestion misses some difference between Chess and WoW PvP.

On a chessboard, you always start with equal teams. This is not always a given, and opening up holes in your teams defense can be devastating. Roll nature weakness and you get a free teleport to the nearest graveyard if a shaman is nearby. Roll nature weakness when all enemies left are warlock and mages, and the debuff might as well not exist.

Wow PvP needs an inherent level of randomness, but thats where crits and evasion comes into play. This brings me to the second and way more important distinction. In Chess you play all the pieces, in wow you play one. Yes you can win games off of making deliberate sacrifice plays in both games, but in wow, it doesn't increase your fun dying, knowing the guy who killed you will be whooped in return. You'd rather have had a fighting chance taking him out yourself.

And i think this is at the crux of all the PvP discussions. A fair 50/50 Battleground doesn't necessarily mean everyone gets to live his powerfantasy. If all people cared about was winning, You'd see way more people actually healing in a battleground.

So any proposal that throws the individual player under the bus for "the greater good" is imo unfeasable for pick up group battlegrounds. If ranked battlegrounds should ever be a thing, that might be a different subject, but as it stands, battlegrounds are inherently having a level of casualness. Many a great PvP game has already nuked its relevancy by making their game more "Fair and Balanced" and while doing so, destroyed its approachability or fun.

For those interested: here a bit about what i think of dampening as a solution  Dampening on the other hand has merrits, If you look at why TBC PvP was so successful is because it increased killtimers by just enough to give players some agency. In a game where you often spend 5 minutes between queing, prebuffing and sizing eachother up, loosing a match to a single crit is incredibly unsatisfactory. Heck winning because of one crit looses its appeal relatively quickly too.
With a bit of Class dampening, single small mistakes don't spell your doom right away, it might put you on the backfoot, but it allows you to recover potentially. We already have had it in place to mostly great success for quite a while, as PvP would be completely unplayable with twow powercreep without dampening.

It however also has its flaws. A shadowpriest in 1v1 is a monster on high dampening % as he excels at outlasting oponents. Meanwhile, on low dampening, he gets just run over in any coordinated group settings.
Spec based dampening inherits the same weakness. But it adds some of their own. The Team can make the most fair, unbiased decision, check winrates of classes for months and fine tune the dampening. It still gives players a number to point to and to cry about when the class they play (and suck with) happens to have low dampening.

Anyone following the PvP scene a little bit will also spot immediately, that this proposed change is a direct result of the Tank shaman talents making shaman way too tanky in pvp for the burst they dish out. And frankly, its a bandaid over an open fracture. Just covering up the mess below instead of treating it.

Fundamentally, most of the PvP problems (not just the turtlewow ones, also stuff we inherited from classic wow) are a result of classes innate abilities and talent trees. And the best way to fix pvp is to stop to pretend PvP and PvE are different games. Talents & Spells like shamans ancestral guardian/spirit armor, mages arcane surge and the absolutely unhinged greater warlock demons are not just too strong in pvp, they are also wildly out of balance in pve and need to be addressed along with other significant overperformers.

Other games like dota get away with having broken stuff due to being able to punish someone before they have access to it. In a WoW battleground, thats not the case.
