# Bloodthistle for High Elfs!

## Post 163905 by Baldessarini — 2025-12-28T10:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163905#p163905 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

HIGH Elfs are supposed to get HIGH on Bloodthistle, this feature was included in their introduction in TBC, where you could collect Bloodthistle with herbalism in Blood Elf starting zone, it gave a +10AP bonus when active, and reduced spirit on withdrawal. It basically was another, quite hidden, Blood elf racial feature.
