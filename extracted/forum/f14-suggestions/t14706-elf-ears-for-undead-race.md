# Elf ears for Undead Race

## Post 100308 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-08-28T07:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100308#p100308 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It will sound bizarre but let me tell you why things are like this.
As far as I know, the Forsaken are made up of humans from Lordaeron and elves from Quel'thalas, so it makes sense that there are Forsaken with elven characteristics.

And the Dark Rangers?
The Dark Rangers were fortunate to keep their bodies, since if I remember correctly, they were turned into Banshees just like Sylvanas.

The Forsaken Elves died and physically decayed like any other Forsaken, and were brought back to life like any other Forsaken, keeping some elven characteristics.

And yes, you will tell me "There are enough elves already", man, it's just a simple customization, don't make a fuss about it.
