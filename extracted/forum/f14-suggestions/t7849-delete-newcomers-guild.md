Noooo, please don't make World even *more* spammy than it already is. We don't need to tack on the legit noob questions and all the garbage from Newcomers on top of what's already there. You've called Newcomers chat "very active" and hard to moderate yourself, why would you want to bring that to World chat?

Like with the planned change to Still Alive - just replace the guild with a Newcomers chat channel that you auto-join. Everyone who wants to help or likes the community aspect can join, everyone who doesn't can leave. In turn put a requirement on speaking in World (similar to the rank requirement for speaking in World Defense) - as long as any character on your account is lvl 40 you can speak in World, otherwise only read. That should help cut down on "tents?", "where is dmf?" and "how much does riding at 40 cost?" that sometimes clog World. Those are all questions that would fit perfectly into a Newcomer chat channel, but I don't think the whole World needs to read about how many tents are in GS for the 50th time in the same hour. If you autojoin Newcomers chat it might also attract the guild recruiting messages away from World, which is also a plus in my books.

So, I feel I should mention this…

The only way World Chat could be deleted is if the "/join World" command were blocked.  This is because custom channels in *World of Warcraft* are world-wide.  Even if the administration got rid of World Chat, someone could easily make another channel that serves the same purpose, such as the "RP" channel.  Difference being, "RP" is not actively promoted through random in-game tips.
(Wonder if there's a way to turn those off, incidentally…)

As for the Newcomers guild?  It lost its point the instant they stopped making people leave at a certain level.  Or, if that never happened, it lost its point **because** it doesn't auto-kick at a certain level.  It became even *less* useful when the Level 5 chat limitation happened.  People who were legitimately new and lost (and shouldn't be playing this version of *WoW*, in that case) no longer had that resource available to them.  Granted, it takes less than an hour to get to Level 5, but still.

Anyway, I **strongly agree** with abolishing the Newcomers guild as an official server-wide thing.  Let some rando establish a Newcomers guild, themselves, and assign their own Moderators.
And on a related note, I'm **strongly in favor** of *removing* any official Turtle Team connections to World Chat.  Honestly, these two things are just major headaches for GMs and players alike, so leaving the players to police themselves seems like a perfectly fine idea.
