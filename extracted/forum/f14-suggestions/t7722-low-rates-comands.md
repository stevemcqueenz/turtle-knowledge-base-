# low rates comands

## Post 49477 by Tortun — 2023-06-09T14:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49477#p49477 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To cover the needs of players in complicated pumping, I propose to introduce additional commands that lower the rating of the experience gained, for example:

/xprate 0.5 - for half xp rates from mobs and quests
/xprate 1 - for normal xp rates

no additional rewards required
