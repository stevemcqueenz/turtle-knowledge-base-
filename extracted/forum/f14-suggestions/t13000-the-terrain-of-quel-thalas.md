# The terrain of Quel'Thalas.

## Post 87073 by Visionmoon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-02-06T05:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87073#p87073 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Quel'Thalas, there should be a mountain road leading to the back door of Stratholme through the mountain forest landscape. At the intersection of this mountain road, on the left is the sea and on the right is a steep mountain peak, leading to the back door of Darrowshire, which is the starting point of the Trail of the Dead. The mountain forest landscape of the back door of Stratholme belongs to Quel'Thalas, which is now a harbor and a large battlefield for the attack on Naxxramas.
