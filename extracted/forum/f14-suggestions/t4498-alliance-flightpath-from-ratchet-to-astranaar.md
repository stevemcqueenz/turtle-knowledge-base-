# Alliance flightpath from Ratchet to Astranaar

## Post 26945 by Darkgestalt — 2022-10-07T14:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26945#p26945 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please allow a direct path between Ratchet and Astranaar, or otherwise Stonetalon Peak.

At the moment, the only connection with Northern Kalimdor and Ratchet is through Azshara. This makes it rather difficult for low level characters who want to quest there or do Wailing Caverns. As well, it makes the flightpath unnecessary expensive, since it collects a tax in Azshara. Finally, it just takes more time flying with such a detour all the time.

I believe it would help alliance players questing in ashenvale, stonetalon and desolace. Without conveniencing them away from the playing world.
