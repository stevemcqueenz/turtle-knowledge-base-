# Rewards for world pvp

## Post 120630 by Darkstromkar — 2025-02-22T06:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120630#p120630 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's clear and I'm happy that Nordanaar is a pve server, that makes it more friendly both for new players and for people who want to have a quiet time without anyone bothering them.

The issue is that sometimes it is too calm, to the point that it can seem bland, a bit of world pvp wouldn't hurt either, something subtle, just as while you level you get 20% extra experience.

I suggest that when you reach level 60, if you have the pvp activated when collecting plants or mines, the collection will be greater, or that there are spots of plants and mines that you can only see with the pvp activated, in addition to giving 20% ​​extra gold for killing npcs, and that every time you benefit from this effect the pvp is renewed so that you can exploit it at no cost.

I think it would be something subtle, that would give the open world a spicy touch so that it feels attractive and challenging, because let's remember that although wow is a pve game, pvp has always given it a special touch.

There will be people who say, if you like pvp, go to a pvp server and if you have read what I explain it has nothing to do with that, I love how Nordanaar is, but I think that with something this subtle, it would still be a better server since you give people the option of playing with pvp or not.
