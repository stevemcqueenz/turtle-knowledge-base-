# Add Doomwarder Lord mobs to Desolace (cut content)

## Post 57774 by Forbearance (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-17T17:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57774#p57774 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Doomwarder Lords are mentioned in the description of the quest [Reagents for Reclaimers Inc.](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1466), yet they cannot be found in the game, as they were cut from the final release of vanilla. Wowhead Classic has a database entry for the [Doomwarder Lord](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=4683/doomwarder-lord) NPC, however this mob was never implemented into the game.

The result is that acquiring ten of the [Doomwarder Blood](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6252) items for the quest takes a very long time on a highly populated server, due to the only accessible mobs that drops the blood being the [Doomwarder Captain](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4680) mobs (there are only a very small amount of spawns available for this mob).

Hence, I suggest adding in these cut Doomwarder Lord mobs into Desolace to make this Reagents for Reclaimers Inc. quest more readily doable, as it would allow for another blood source by getting the Doomwarder Blood to drop from them, as the quest description text implies.
