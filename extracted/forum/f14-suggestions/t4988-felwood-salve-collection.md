# Felwood Salve collection

## Post 30151 by Aubury — 2022-12-03T09:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30151#p30151 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

Since the removal of a gold reward from Maybess Riverbreeze's quests, Felwood isn't looking as attractive for gold farming as it once did. For gatherers with both herbalism and mining, Felwood was another alternative to scouring wild materials in EPL and WPL and Winterspring.
Though EPL and WPL still reign supreme, fel creep, tainted vitriol and corrupted shards provided a nice extra income. This is especially the case during peak hours, when the ''expensive'' herbs cannot be found, so even Golden Sansam and Arthas' Tears could be a gold mine.

The suggestion is to return the gold reward to Cenarion Plant Salve quests. It can be reduced compared to the previous reward of 2G40S.

Pros:
 1. Provides newcomers with some bonus gold during questing and farming - this is vital to help newcomers jumpstart their journey and it fits with the difficulty mode of Turtle WoW.
 2. Makes Felwood a viable alternative for gathering next to WPL, EPL and Winterspring.

Cons:
 1. Boosts inflation longterm

What pros or cons do you see for bringing back gold as a quest reward?
