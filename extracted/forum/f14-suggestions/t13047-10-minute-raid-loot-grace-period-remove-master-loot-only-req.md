# 10 Minute Raid Loot Grace Period - Remove Master Loot Only Requirement.

## Post 87442 by Sagabal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-02-10T06:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87442#p87442 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

>For raiders, we have implemented a new system that allows you to rectify any mistakes on your own:
>
>In raids where Master Looter mode is enabled, for a short period of 10 minutes after the boss has been defeated, all raid members will have a claim on the item and can receive it via trade, provided they were present during the boss kill, have not left the raid map, and share the same raid ID with the current owner of the item. Each item can only be traded once.
>
>There will be a map-wide announcement to inform the raid leader about this event.
>Please note that any requests for manual transfers will be refused from now on.

This is a great QoL change, probably my favorite of such additions of this server.  However, it's unfortunately limited: by only applying to Raids in Master Loot, it's still going to create situations where players have loot assigned incorrectly, whether by bug or by accident, and turn to GMs, to then get rejected as all tickets of this type will be.  This is, unfortunately, going to keep happening.  Removing the requirement that it be restricted only to Master Loot, and instead giving a general purpose 10min timer, where as long as the item hasn't been modified in any way it can still be traded among members of a raid or group, would not only cut down on edge cases like this but also cut down on such tickets.  Thanks for reading.
