# PVP island idea

## Post 27843 by Achillesheel — 2022-10-24T12:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27843#p27843 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello turtles,
Long time ago I was playing on a private server that were offering something very cool, which I like to propose in here.
How about having an island for non-stop PVP. You can go there free (no que to wait) and fight for yourself. You can go as a group (of maximum 3 players). I imagine a small island having battleground buffs to spawn from time to time and every kill there to worth 100 honor (regardless of the enemy fraction). To make it more desirable, we can have a chest spawn in around 1 hour that contains a token for additional 500 honor (or some random consumables).
We can also have a black lotus spawn every couple of hours (it would be best if it is random) and some rich thorium veins. So whenever somebody is bored, he can just go to the pvp island and start fighting or try to steal some sweet rewards.
In addition to this, we can have something more included - every kill will award player rank x 1 amount of reputation with the island.
Once a player reaches exalted he will get the title Conquer of the Island + a class specific trinket, which is something desired (to be strong and used only in PVP island or Battlegrounds, it doesn't need to be required farm for hardcore PVE players).
For example:
warrior - +1% crit/+1% hit, on use - makes you immune to movement imparing effects for the next 4 seconds (something like free action potion) with 5 min cooldown.
rogue - +1% crit/+1% hit, on use - cloak of shadows ( remove all magic/curses/poisons from you and increase all your resistance by 95% for 2 seconds).
mage - 1% spell crit/ 1% spell hit - passive - evocation also restores health (as it is a glyph on Wotlk). 5 min cd
lock - 1% spell crit/ 1% spell hit - casting 1.5 seconds - drops a infernal stunning all enemies in an area for 2 seconds. The infernal is doing fire damage and lasts 20 seconds (with 1500 health) 5min cd
paladin - 1% crit/1%hit, active - for the next 5 seconds your hammer of wrath is active, no mana cost and without casting time.
druid - 1% crit/1%hit, active - increase spell power/attack power by 20% for 8 seconds. 5 min cd
priest - 1% crit/ 1% hit, active - summon shadow fiend fighting for you dealing shadow damage to enemies and restores 1 mana per damage done
hunter - 1% crit, 1% hit - active jump back 5 yards and daze your opponent for 3 seconds. 5 mins cd
shaman - 1%crit, 1% hit - active - thunderstorm, knock back enemies, slowing them 30% for 3 seconds and restores you 10% of your total mana. 5 sec cd
What do you think of a such an idea ?
