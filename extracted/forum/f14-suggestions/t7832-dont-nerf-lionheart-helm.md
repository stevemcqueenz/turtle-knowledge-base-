## Post 50328 by Syrathegreat (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-16T19:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50328#p50328 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The issue is that LHH is BiS for DPS until deep into Naxx which means Turtle CAN'T make any alternatives - gearing progression design should be that you replace gear as you move through harder content, so if you can get your forever BiS pre-60, it means that they can never add interesting helms in the interim raids. As it is, LHH has way to high of a stat distribution for its ilvl anyways - as a crafted item it should only really carry you through the introductory and catchup raids, but better stuff should always come from the content that requires it.

Additionally, it's not a case of just buffing everything else as on a forever Classic server we have to be careful of power creep since there isn't the option to just reset gear with a new expansion.

They've said they're buffing some other items like Helm of Endless Rage, as well as introducing some new possible upgrades, to make a smoother progression.
