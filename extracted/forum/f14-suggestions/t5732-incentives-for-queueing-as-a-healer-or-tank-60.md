I'm not one of those "if you play a warrior, you have to tank!" and "if you play a priest, you have to heal!" type guys, but this is genuinely frustrating.. There's a huge shortage of tanks and healers. 13 dps for 1 tank and 0 healers for a popular dungeon that drops sought after gear? What?

## Post 35940 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-02-24T14:00:00Z

I know this is a hot take, but as a Warrior, Paladin, Druid, Priest, and Shaman player, I feel some slack has to be cut on demanding all of these but Priest to tank or heal. Depending on the class and role, there is more than just one's skill and talents, but also stats on gear to consider. You don't want a Feral Druid or Enhancement Shaman or Ret Paladin with <50% the mana pool they'd need to effectively heal as your healer.

Having said that, hot take time: Priests have Int and spellpower on their gear, regardless if they're Holy or Shadow. If a queue is taking a long time, I cannot see one good reason other than "I don't wanna heal" to not bite the bullet and heal. There's no gear swapping necessary for this class. They will always be geared and have the spells to heal effectively, and it then comes down to motivation. I'm not making the case that they "must heal" especially if they're not comfortable with it, but I feel Priests have less room to complain about long queues when only rolling as DPS for dungeons than any other class.

## Post 35943 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-24T14:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35943#p35943 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Borrowing an idea from Hour of Twilight, give players the option to "Hire a Mercenary" for a dungeon. The mercenary is an NPC built to fill one of the three roles (chosen by the player), and will function like a pet. If the mercenary is a healer, it will do its best to remove status effects and heal players below a certain threshold automatically. Only one to a party, controlled by the leader. Costs a fair bit of coin based on dungeon level, and being an NPC it's pretty brainless, but at least gives an option for groups who can't fill.
