# Grey leveled mobs should flee from player

## Post 46726 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-05-20T06:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46726#p46726 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Now if you are high level and attack low level NPC, then this NPC will (hopelessly) fight you till his death.

Proposal:
When you attack NPC which has much lower level than you, this NPC should run away from you.

It should improve world immersion because every living being wants to be alive. But when they see someone too powerful, they should feel some kind of fear and should avoid combat.
Now mobs are target dummies for killing.

In regular leveling you normally want to attack green or yellow npcs because they yield you experience and probably quest objectives. They should not flee by this rule.

It could affect only beasts and humanoids. I don't think elementals or demons care whether they die or not.
