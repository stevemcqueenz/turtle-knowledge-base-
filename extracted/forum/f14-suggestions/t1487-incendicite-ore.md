Indurium makes the most sense of any. If we do get an Uldum thing in the future, perhaps "Greater Indurium Ore" and a quest for learning how to refine it would be in order, similar to dark iron as stated above.

In a similar vein (heh) you could have REFINED incendicite as a vendor or trade-in reward, possibly from the thorium brotherhood, gadgetzan, or neutral/'friendly to coin' dwarves. Then having it as a reagent for some explosive engineering recipes or blasty weapons. An alternative would be having it drop from some kind of engineering or foundry-focused enemies in a custom dungeon or raid and used to craft rep-locked things, similar to how souldarite, lava cores, and obsidian are obtained.
The reason for that is as Pilot Stonegear states:
" Incendicite is volatile stuff and hard to use without blowing yourself up, but I'm sure if it's smelted the right way it could make cannon shells that pack a mighty punch!

Get me incendicite ore and I'll find someone brave enough to experiment with it."

 ---

The others I don't think have any real promise.

Rethban iron ore is just used for inkmaking in the quest. There's no point to integrating it with professions. Two reasons are:
 - lorewise it's just a soft iron with some form of impurities that makes good ink
 - it's only found in a single isolated alliance zone.

Same with incendicite, it's only found in one cave on the border of wetlands and loch modan, too low level and too deep into one side's territory to be of consequence. The only thing I could think of would be something like a bullet that does +1-2 fire damage in addition to its normal damage, but this seems like a waste and I think my above idea would be cooler.

Lesser Bloodstone Ore has no actual indication of what Krazek wants it for or why it's valuable. Given it's only found in one mine, very easily farmable and horde have excessively easy access to it.. I can't see any reason for giving it a profession purpose. And honestly, it seems set up to be a quest that's intended to spark PvP on servers with that kind of bent, while allowing people who are bad at PvP to just buy it off the AH.
