# Some suggestions to "vagrant endeavor" and "traveling craftmaster" challenges.

## Post 155957 by Uzurback — 2025-10-17T20:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155957#p155957 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So, I'm doing "Vagrant's endeavor" AND "Traveling craftmaster" challenge now (and... other challenges except brewmaster and LOL, including hardcore)

, and I'm trying to survive as long as possible. But, as you know, there is some restrictions, that makes this a very difficult task. Firstly, there is not common/poor items in crafting professions past 110-120 prof level. And this is one of the obstacles which makes this challenge bundle more interesting. And secondly, there is restriction to use enchanting on items. Okay, I stand with this restriction, too. BUT there is one thing to strengthen (at least very slightly) your armor, and this is Armor Kits from Leatherworking profession. And when I tried to strengthen my armor... yeah, it was considered as enchanting and didn't allow me to strengthen my armor. And THIS is what I want you to reconsider. It's not enchanting, after all. It's leather kit, which is sewing up on armor. Yeah, I know, that it replaces enchantment on your armor, but this is always seemed to me strange. Such things as grinders isn't considered as enchantments (but they're working as buffs).

And also, if I found my strength to write this post, I'd wanted to touch other topic, i.e. "Traveling craftmaster" enhancement. I've always thought that this challenge not only must restrict your wearability only to the items you craft yourself but also give you something in return. Namely, make yourself possible to learn more than two (three, ideally four) professions. It's written in the challenge's name, after all: **Craft** - **Master**. The decision is yours, of course, but I hope that you'll take my words into consideration.
