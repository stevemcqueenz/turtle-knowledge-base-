# Can we be more transparent on when new BWL will be released on Ambershire?

## Post 167626 by Fyang — 2026-02-07T03:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167626#p167626 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It has been one month since the original BWL release date on Jan 2nd on Ambershire, can we please have an official announcement on when BWL will be released, even if it is an approximate time?

The recent announcements of new leveling dungeons and zones are cool, so are the new battleground and survival overhaul. But we don't need these on Ambershire to feel fresh, we don't need all of these changes bundled together for a perfect 1.18.1. If it can help speeding up the development process, can we have those as small changes one by one after to give us BWL as soon as possible?

From a Player perspective, even though I voted for BWL to be released on Jan, I understand the vast majority of people voting for ZG is to be able to progress in a new, freshly designed BWL soon after rather than needing more time to gear up their characters. I agree ZG is cool for a month and I think many of us players were expecting BWL to be released one month after since AQ that is only pushed back a month from before. If we delay BWL further, we would be losing resets to get us geared and prepared for AQ.

From a Guild Master perspective, it is a struggle to plan, retain, and recruit new members currently because of people are in an indefinite waiting room for more content without knowing when it will end. Having an approximate schedule will help us greatly, even if the schedule says BWL will be released in some outrageous time like in 2 months. Players will still be more motivated to raid and log on to prepare for BWL, whether in gold, gear grinding or flask making. Players will have more interest in a guild, as BWL is more difficult content that would be harder to pug.

I have been enjoying turtle wow from the great supporting community there is from both the player base and GM staff, and I don't think I am asking too much for a bit more transparency on the new BWL development. Thank you for reading this.
