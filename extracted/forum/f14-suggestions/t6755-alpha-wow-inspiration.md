# Alpha WoW Inspiration.

## Post 42559 by Xfraz — 2023-04-22T10:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42559#p42559 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Looking at early alpha WoW patch that has recently surfaced I cant help but feel like some of the best concepts where scrapped entirely perhaps due to time or laziness.

One thing in particular is the old talent system.
Now in direct comparison I see how the talent system we got was the clear choice however I feel a lot of potential is missing.

Now I do not propose a rework of our current systems but to add some depth and to further reinforce the fact that this game is an RPG.

My vision is likely far outside the realm of what is possible to achieve given the tools available to the Dev team however the team here has proven me wrong before so I will indulge my fantasy.

The Overhaul:
Further improving on weapon skills.
Skill ups for different schools of magic. A mage will have a chance to gain a skill up in Frost Magic when they deal damage to an enemy with a frost spell. Similarly a warlock will have a chance to gain a skill up in "Shadow" when dealing damage with shadow magic.

New/Improved ~"Combat or Weapon Master's"?
A new system to go hand in hand with existing talent system.

Using frost as an example again:
Seek out the master in the world of Azeroth, once found use a limited resource (similar to talent points) along side gold and perhaps even a lengthy quest(s) to receive small bonuses.
Without any levels in Frost due to an inability to cast frost magic one can still take various "talents" from this Master.
A warrior for example could pursue "Frost Resistance" providing +4 frost resistance at the first rank.
Or an expert Mage with a high level in Frost Magic could take "Frost Penetration" or "Frost Precision" increasing penetration and decreasing resist chance respectively.

Reworked existing Weapon Master's,
"Sword Master" location SW:
Now any class without access to using swords be it 1h or 2h can  spend the aforementioned resource to gain access to either 1h or 2h swords. Classes with innate access to a weapon skill will have a head start due to the fact they don't have to spend any resource to initially equip the weapon.

Example Sword Master progression:
Ability to equip 1h sword > 1h sword hit chance > 1h sword crit chance > 1h sword expertise.

Obviously this would be an overhaul to the existing game meaning alot of changes would be necessary to implement this kind of system. Certain talents would have to be removed from class  talents as they would now appear from the relative "Master" for example a rogue or warriors "sword spec".

The idea is to emphasize the RPG aspect of WoW giving players more freedom in there progressive choices. Perhaps even allowing any class such as rogues or hunters to spec into shields. Even take this further by separating shields into two classes, small and large allowing a rogue to spend some of his limited points into small shields. Or a warlock to delve deeper taking Large shield spec.

There is so much room for in the game for this kind of system and the opportunity it provides is endless. As tame or as game changing as desired I believe this system could add something new and intriguing for all.

A warrior that forgoes the learning of mail and plate armour, instead settling for medium and choosing to spread him/herself more offensively?

I think the most intriguing change is actually from Ascension WoW where you can distribute stat points freely rather than have them automatically distributed based on a per-class formula. Diablo 1 and 2 had classes and they allowed you to distribute stat points freely and it didn't ruin anything. You had the choice between a cookie cutter (what's default in WoW) or you could ruin your character by investing in the wrong stats.

But this can make for some more versatile builds by people who know what they're doing, like better Shaman and Paladin healers or some more beefed up but weaker Rogues or glass cannon Warriors. The options are infinite.
