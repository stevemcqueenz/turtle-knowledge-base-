# Shell Shop

## Post 332 by Jondoh — 2018-09-28T17:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=332#p332 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was testing out the client and some addons. I right clicked on speedy the turtle and found the info panel about the Turtle WoW Shell Shop.

I haven't been able to find some of the details that relate to this feature.
 - How much will "Turtle Tokens" cost?
 - How much do you need to make from this shop per month to keep the server running?

I think offering swift mounts in the shop is a minor issue with the shop. It certainly is an "in-game advantage" of sorts to have a swift mount. It will at least save someone a bit of gold. Again, this is a minor issue, but still worth double checking that you really want to do this.

I think offering a 28 slot bag is a major issue and should be removed or at least changed to a 4 slot bag. It is not enough of an issue to keep me from playing, but I think it is a bad idea to offer this in the shop. Working to gradually increase inventory capacity is certainly a genuine part of the Vanilla experience. I think a bag is actually quite a substantial "in-game advantage" especially early on. Offering these for sale at the shop will significantly effect tailors who may want to sell bags to earn gold. It also effects the overall game-play experience as well as the AH economy because normally just getting bags as drops can be quite a helpful find or good for selling on the AH.

I know the reality is that you may need to offer a service like the Shell Shop to make running a server a reasonable endeavor. I would hope that you could make enough by sticking to items that have no game-play value. Bags and mounts do have game-play value, therefore being able to buy them through this shop does in fact offer an in-game advantage. I can see how someone may think they have low game-play impact, but to say they have no effect on game-play is clearly false.

 -JD
