# Lunar Festival - Couple Of Ideas

## Post 169057 by Ganostraza — 2026-02-20T20:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169057#p169057 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Have you noticed how empty Moonglade seems to be, and how “Meh” it feels compared to Winter Veil?

I’ve a few ideas to make it more interesting.

 1. Add more quests to Moonglade, perhaps a couple of dailies for each level bracket where you visit the animal spirits or other NPCs to give/get blessings or something, that way all levels can do them.

 2. Allow elders to give experience for visits. When I visited them yesterday with my lowest level character and got rep with the opposite faction instead of XP, I simply logged off as I’d been looking forward to hopefully gaining a few levels and exploring the world.

 3. World buffs. Could you make Omen kills give random world buffs like the snowman did?

You’d instantly get crowds in Moonglade if it worked this way. Yeah he drops a brief moonlight buff but that’s not enough to get people coming back.

 4. Add “rare” spawns. Firing off all those cluster rockets is sure to attract attention from other creatures as well as our Friend Omen with all those bangs.

That’s all I’ve got for now, anyone else have anything to add?
