# Hardmode Dungeons - PoE Map System

## Post 136146 by Partyboyza — 2025-06-18T10:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136146#p136146 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I want to suggest a similar system to Path of Exile mapping where you can access a more difficult version of a Dungeon.

To access a Hardmode Dungeon you need a new tradable token that has a % chance to drop of bosses from the already existing Dungeon.

This new Hardmode Dungeon should have increased size and number of packs, extra bosses, new unqiue bosses with a % chance to spawn and every pack should have around 2 or 3 levels.

I.E. : You clear Deadmines with your group and a single token drops, the player who wins the roll on that token can either consume it to do the Hardmode version of that dungeon or sell it at the Auction House.
The Hardmode Deadmines should have, new loot and maybe one low level epic with a rare chance to drop, 2-3 extra bosses, 50% more mobs, new quests taken from npcs from inside the dungeon and every enemy should have 2 or 3 levels higher.

This implementation can be gradual, maybe 1-2 dungeons every major patch.

Spamming these Hardmode dungeons would be impossible without heavy investment in the respective token require to open them.

The way it's proposed, I'm highly against this. For what playerbase will this be intended? Leveling players? Introduction of that will only invalidate other dungeon options and make queueing up slower and harder. This will also have a negative effect on Battlegrounds. People already complain about twinks, making battlegrounds not really desirable while leveling. Introducing even more powerful loot will just make the sweats, sweat even harder. Lastly, I'd rather new dungeon content be part of the dungeon baseline, and not locked away behind a separate mode

With all of that being said, I'm not completely against the idea. Having an early game dungeon have a version appropriate for higher levels can be interesting. But the team's current approach of just introducing brand new dungeons is a lot more fun and visually pleasing imo.

Maybe in the future, as part of attunements for other dungeons or raids
