Hi there, just a quick suggestion. I recently saw a bug report on the bugtracker about firestone which got me thinking:

While the team works on fixing that issue, why not give the warlock firestone some spell power scaling? I'm not asking for anything crazy like making melee warlock meta or anything, but I think allowing this playstyle to be something more than what it currently is could be interesting. At least allowing a warlock to use firestone + melee as an alternative to using their wand? just a thought.
