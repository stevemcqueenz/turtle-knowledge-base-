# Down with class discrimination! Equal chances for everyone!

## Post 108751 by Ulukay (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-19T22:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108751#p108751 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For some reason unknown to me, magic has an incredibly low chance of hitting a target whose level is higher than its own. At the same time, this does not affect physical attacks in any way. For example, a bunch of first-level magicians won’t even be able to scratch someone of level sixty. But if the magicians beat him with their hands and feet, they could well kill a level sixty. And in my opinion this is extremely unfair.
I don't know what reason you have for not changing that in so many years. Maybe there are complex mechanics that can't be bypassed. Or no one has asked that question, which is unlikely. I'm gonna bring it up, though, just in case.

I think mechanical variation like this is a bonus like other kinds of variation. Going from Hunter to Mage is a great experience because it's like a whole new game, and learning how frostbolts work differently from arrows is part of it.

The classes get some very unique strengths and weaknesses in part from these variations as well. In a mage's kit they have amazing solo aoe potential, mostly distinct from the hunter, while the hunter can take on powerful single enemies in the open world since they can run and shoot and expect those shots to land.

Wouldn't change it personally, tho I could see the frustration. Especially when trying to keep up with friends, being a low level for the dungeon everyone is doing is rough as a caster, but I love that kind of crunchiness.
