# Allow more Hidden Armor items from Fashionista

## Post 75525 by Starweaver — 2023-11-02T18:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75525#p75525 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings fellow fashionistas, I have a suggestion that I think many of you would appreciate and support: TWoW, please make available a Hidden Armor option for each piece of armor, allowing players the further customize their appearance. This would allow for such looks as barefooted (i.e. rugged tracker/wild ranger/homeless) bare fisted, shirtless (i.e Rexxar or demon hunter look), etc. And of course, for those so inclined.. full birthday suit!

I think this would be a great enhancement to player customization for their creative cosplays or desired style.

There are many NPCs in game without pieces of armor visible too so this change would allow players to draw more inspiration and actualize their own variant on influences and inspiration.

What do you think? Thoughts, criticism, support in the comments please!   smiling_turtle_head
