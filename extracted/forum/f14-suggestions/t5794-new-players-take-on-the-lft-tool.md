# New Players take on the LFT-tool

## Post 36407 by Copycat665 — 2023-02-28T15:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36407#p36407 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there. New player, been here a week or so. So I am sorry if I got something completely wrong here.

The LFT-tool was awesome at lower level. Getting quick groups for Dead Mines and the likes. Yes, the healers and tanks often sucked, but we managed. Now at higher levels (at 40'ish now) the tool isn't working as well as before, now sitting at 3+ hours for a SM run.

Now I can see other posts about players never going tank or healer, this was the case for normal classic as well. But normal classic had a tool that solved this. A place where people could communicate and talk people into giving healing or tanking a chance, even if your mainspec was dps. It was called the city general chat, which worked much like lfg chat.
Now this server does have an LFG-chat that works server wide, but people either do not now about it or don't use it. Is there any chance we could make this chat auto-join for new characters?

The LFT-tool is alright as a backup, but I feel that the lack of people on the LFG chat is the real reason why groups take so long in gathering. This is only a problem on TWoW, not normal classic or other classic servers without this tool (Not to this extend atleast).

I feel this server has a lot of potential, but don't take the communication out of the game. Get people into LFG chat by default, and let those that don't like the channel leave on their own accord and not cause they didn't know it was there.
