Currently, you only get discounts from factions when you reach the reputation level of "honored" -10%. I think it would be better if the discounts were more spread-out, like they are in TBC and later expansions.
Friendly = 5%
Honored = 10% (current)
Revered = 15%
Exalted = 20%
