# Some Jewelcrafting suggestions and recipes

## Post 152602 by Horcos — 2025-09-21T22:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152602#p152602 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First I wanted to ask: "Why are all head pieces and bracelets cloth even when they require metals to craft?"

Now the suggestion. When I was leveling my jewelcrafting I made some Rough Silver Rings (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41319>) and decided to save them on my bank to use later. Many levels after that I realized by checking on the database that they're only used on 1 recipe, just ONE. Because of that I made these recipes to add to the profession so they get some more use.

---
 **Purified Silver Halo**
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/V2r5Rtq.png)
This ring is based on the only ring that uses that material (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41329>), but meant for casters instead of mdps.

This is its recipe:
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/0RyQabS.png)
 1x Rough Silver Ring, 1x Wild Steelbloom, 4x Shimmering Oil, 6x Coarse Gritted Paper, 2x Iridescent Pearls and 1x Greater Astral Essence

---
 **Monastery Signet**
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/swjbrAV.png)
This ring is based on the Monastery Emberbrace item also from JC (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=55332>). I think these 2 pieces could make a set (like some other JC items). Since they give Strength, Intellect and SP they're great for shammies and paladins. If you equip both this ring and the bracers it'd give you +80 or +100 armor to compensate the low armor given by the bracers (since they're cloth).

This is its recipe:
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/XNfDOlQ.png)
 1x Rough Silver Ring, 4x Steel Bars, 4x Lesser Moonstone

Sorry for both items having SP!
