# Thorn damage should not tag

## Post 147645 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-22T05:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147645#p147645 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is "easy" mode for tag-leveling. In short:
 1) buff low level character with thorns (or enable retri aura or fire shield or lightning shield)
 2) put him on follow behind high level character
 3) run around
because of level difference, aggro range of low level is higher. All mobs ignore high level character and attack low level one.
mob that hit low level character got tagged by reflective damage.
Then high level character can kill mobs.

Thing is that this strategy don't require complicated management of two windows. You play just high level character and low level gets all xp.

Proposal is:
damage by reactive shields should not tag mob to the character.
