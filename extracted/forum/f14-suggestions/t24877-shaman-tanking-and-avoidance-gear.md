# Shaman tanking and avoidance gear

## Post 175510 by Johnbradley01 — 2026-04-16T19:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175510#p175510 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hey

Shaman tanking is currently rough after item level 40 where most tanking pieces gets relagated to plate instead of mail.

Endgame, we have 1 set, and its not even a 8-piece, but the t2.5 from AQ40. You really have to be ultra sharp and spin a lot of plate to get 102.3% avoidance, even with kara40 gear. You need to stack everything possible and eat the problem of using lower statted gear with low overall stats. Compared with plate tanking, where the pieces in naxx are so fat with defensives stats you can run 70% tanking and run dps / threat pieces. If you look at the new timermaw hold plate tanking set, shaman could only dream about a set with so many stats and avoidance. We are shoe-horned into stacking str and agility (this is also what is on our t2.5 set) to quickly build up our earthen shield to survive, but we hardly use the str stat to block because there is almost no gear with +block chance available.

For example, for tanking gloves we have 2 options:
Rune Infused Gauntlets from Kara10 and Handwraps of Dead Winds from kara40. Handwraps are only high on the list because of the 28 agi. They are also leather, so a legit tradeoff on 100 armor and 0.6% avoidance loss from the Rune Infused.
Below these, there are 2 blue leather gloves with dodge on them from swv and DM.

Plate ofc has options in all tiers of content, from quests, to dungeons to entry raids all the way through all sets and into ultra lategame.

There are 6 belts with avoidance on them, all of them dodge, half them them leather. You also need to kill a world boss.

Proposed solution:
Instead of making more 1-class one-purpose gear, (plate gear with +healing, etc) give shamans access to plate gear in the enhancement talent tree with a "only 70 % armor contribution from plate pieces or something" It could be a line of requirements all the way from shield-specialization -> Ancestral Guardian -> Spirit Armor -> Plate Specialization if you want to avoid enhancement dps shamans just being auto plate users.

In the ideal world, every set on warrior and paladin with a tanking set, would also have a shaman equivalent that can be exchanged. Until that time, for the next redesign of tier sets, this could be a solution - also without cluttering up loot tables with even more snowflake drops.

Cheers
