# Hyal is unbearable (Read before commenting)

## Post 85888 by Mannerless01 — 2024-01-26T05:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85888#p85888 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Thematically you guys did a good job, but not only is it lacking in quests, it's also a nightmare for gameplay reasons. The fact that it follows an S pattern from bottom to top, one that even the flight path follows, is just ridiculous for anyone who needs to walk both up and/or down for quests or farming.

However, all of that pails in comparison to the frankly retarded spirit healer positioning the map has. For whatever reason the decision to spawn you all the effing way back at Nordanaar any time you dont die right outside the troll base is absurd to say the least. Literally every farming party who wipes on furbolgs calls it there because no one (believe it or not) wants to spend 15 mins running all the way back into deep elite territory.

Was it simply beyond the dev team to place a spirit healer at the base of the mountain? Did they even test what it would be like dying at the bottom and needing to run an eternity back? the fact there is no easy access or path from Nord to the bottom is just against anything called sense, and this is why despite it looking pretty, Hyjal is a complete failure in not just my books, but anyone I've ever asked.

Please consider these changes, thank you.
