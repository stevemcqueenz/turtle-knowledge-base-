# DRUID TALENT: Rage Mastery

## Post 40529 by Beohoof — 2023-04-06T14:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40529#p40529 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

An alternative in theory to Warriors Tactical Mastery in the Arms Tree.

IDEA: Give Feral Druids a 3-5 point talent that allows Druids to delay their complete loss of Rage when shapechanged out of Bear form.

As it is now. a Druid in Bear form can have 100 Rage, but the instance he or she pops out to reapply Thorns etc, they will lose 100% of that Rage.

Mechancis (one way of approaching it) Delays the loss of Rage by 3 seconds per point spent when Shapechanged out of Bear form.

This would give a lot of Quality of Life to Druid tanks/leveling Druids as they can cast a heal/drink a potion etc without completely losing the Rage they built up.
