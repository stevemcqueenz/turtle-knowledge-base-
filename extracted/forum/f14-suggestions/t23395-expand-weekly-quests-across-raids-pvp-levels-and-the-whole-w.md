# Expand Weekly Quests Across Raids, PvP, Levels, and the Whole World

## Post 163777 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-26T13:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163777#p163777 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Turtle WoW team and community,
First of all, thanks for the amazing work on the server. Turtle WoW really shines when it comes to world immersion, long-term progression, and making Azeroth feel alive.
I’d like to suggest an expansion of the weekly quest system, which currently feels a bit limited in scope.
![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) More Weekly Quests, More Variety
At the moment, we only have a few weekly quests focused on:
High-level dungeons
Small raids
Molten Core
It would be great to have many more weekly quests, especially:
One weekly quest for each major raid, from Molten Core all the way to Karazhan
This would encourage players to rotate content instead of always doing the same activities every week
The goal wouldn’t be to add pressure, but rather to diversify endgame activity and keep older content relevant.
![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Weekly Quests for PvP (Instanced & World PvP)
Adding PvP-focused weekly quests would be a huge plus, both for:
Battleground PvP (wins, honor kills, objectives)
World PvP, with objectives in contested zones
This could help:
Revitalize world PvP
Give PvP players meaningful weekly goals
Create more spontaneous player interaction outside of instances
![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) PvE Objectives in Low- and Mid-Level Zones (Even for Level 60s)
One of Turtle WoW’s biggest strengths is its world design.
It would be amazing if some weekly quests:
Sent high-level players back into low- or mid-level zones
Included objectives like killing specific mobs, elites, or zone-based events
This would:
Keep the entire map relevant
Make the world feel alive at all levels
Create interaction between new players and high-level characters
![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Weekly Quests by Level Brackets (Not Only Level 60)
Another important point:
Weekly quests don’t have to be exclusive to level 60.
Having weekly quests by level brackets (for example):
 20–30
 30–40
 40–50
 50–60
would make leveling more engaging and dynamic, and would encourage players to take their time instead of rushing to max level.
![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Optional Ideas (to avoid pressure or power creep)
To keep the system healthy and balanced:
Let players choose 1–2 weekly quests from a larger pool
Focus rewards on:
gold
consumables
reputation
cosmetic or QoL items
Possibly rotate weekly objectives so the same zones/raids aren’t always used
The idea is more freedom and variety, not more mandatory chores.
![🧠](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9e0.svg) Final Thoughts
Expanding weekly quests in this way would:
Support both PvE and PvP players
Encourage exploration of the full world
Keep all level ranges active
Fit perfectly with Turtle WoW’s long-term, immersion-focused philosophy
Thanks for reading, and I’d love to hear what the community and devs think about this idea!
