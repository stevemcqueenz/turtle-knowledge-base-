# Gemstone Enchants - Suggestions and Feedback

## Post 115358 by Terexin (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-04T02:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115358#p115358 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Okay, so I've acquired every possible gemstone enchant that's so far in the game. I waited a little bit to make this post. After doing a lot of gem enchants for hundreds of people, this is my feedback and observations on what could change to make gems more balanced. Some gems are really great, some absolutely terrible. There's a select few that nobody wants because their price far outweigh their usefulness.

I'll put more attention for the ones that I think need to change in a way to make them more balanced, and what I would do to make them more useful.

---

---

---
Stunning Imperial Gemstone (+3 stats) - Fantastic. Great example of proper materials and usefulness. Quite often requested.

Azerothian Ruby Gemstone (6 str) - Excellent gem, often sought after, but there's a +5 str gem that's far cheaper, and this +6 one actually requires a recipe drop from bosses. Materials are fine, but I believe it should be +7 or +8 to make it worth its price and requirement to obtain.

Resilient Arcane Gemstone (40 armor) - Love the concept, but I've never had a player wanting it. High price, sort of underwhelming armor gain. 3 of them would be 120 armor, generally outshined by the +3 stats gem, or the +6 stamina gem. Would help if either the oil was regular, or it was maybe 60/70/80 armor gained. I know Bear Tanks would LOVE that, but there's definitely a need for some kind of boost, since it was never requested. EDIT: Bears don't get boosted armor from the gem.

Resurged Topaz Gemstone (2mp5) - Outright repulsive. Worst gem in the game. 3 arcane essences, 3 enchanted oils for +2mp5 (equivalent of an ancient branch). Far outshined by the +12 healing power gem, which is often requested. Suggestion is to lower requirement to 1 essence, 1 oil, and maybe 4mp5 or 5mp5 (even then I wouldn't see it being used much, high grade healer gear already has plenty mp5)

Arcane Emerald Gemstone (5sp) - Really cool gem, cool concept. Dislike its price. Holy/shadow users have to go for this one since no elemental gems exist for holy/shadow. Perhaps using regular oil would make it more worth the price, or increasing SP to 7-8.

Flawless Black Gemstone - Very cool, but the +3 res gain for all resistances was requested maybe once and never again. Maybe if it was raised to +5 someone would look for it. Underwhelming, especially for its price. Generally elemental +8 resists is what i get requested.

Gorgeous Mountain Gemstone - Not sure what to say of this one. I love the design and it uses a really rare gem. Really expensive, rarely sought after. Maybe some kind of extra effect or reduction of materials?

Elemental damage gems - All should be +9, imo. Some +6 some +9 really seems off. Some of them have weird material requirement too.

---

---

---
Now, about a specific gem... Sigh... Imperial Topaz. Super hyped at patch release, about as worth as its vendor price now. Needs to be part of better or more recipes so its price would make sense. Maybe used in other professions? Hoping at least Kara40 release will bring more use to it.

Huge Emerald, Blue Sapphire - Also needs more usefulness. Also usually vendor price. :(

Gemstone Deposit - Needs to stop dropping crappy mid-tier gems like Citrine, Aquamarine and Emberstone. Nobody wants to see trash when they dig out a rare 310 mining node that's been hyped as a Black Lotus kind of node. The amount of times I've seen gemstone deposits in obvious places is depressing. It sometimes feels like people actually AVOID mining them! Perhaps if we knew the chances of an arcane crystal being there, it would encourage mining.
//
Perhaps the issue is that imperial topaz is on each Gemstone node, but its not used as often, which makes Imperial Topaz seem like a common occurrence. Possibly somehow lower its spawn or drop rate, if it won't be used much.
//
Also, if possible, it would be interesting to have something to enchant trinkets with too, or maybe wands/offhands.
Or maybe if there were gems exclusive to rings, and exclusive to amulets?   smiling_turtle

---

---

---
If I wrote something wrong here, feel free to post feedback! I'm not sure if my observations are correct.
Also, sorry to anyone if they also wrote something similar but I haven't found it.  crying_turtle
