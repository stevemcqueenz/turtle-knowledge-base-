# Warden Illusion for the Donation Shop!

 **![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) A Request to Add the Kaldorei Warden Model to the Donation Shop ⛓**

[![Image](http://dl7.glitter-graphics.net/pub/727/727097kjhd9gidla.gif)](https://www.glitter-graphics.com)

 *The Donation Shop* offers a wide variety of currently unplayable models (due to a lack of emotes, animations, or faction restrictions) that broaden character customization options for the low price of 160 Donation Tokens or $16 USD. Some of the most popular illusions are Dryad (loved by Children of Cenarius), various Scourge options, and Furbolg. It is always a treat to see a Satyr running around in Xavian, or an Ogre following a Goblin around emoting with their ‘Boss.’

Currently, the look of a Warden cannot truly be replicated. Beaststalker’s T1 set comes close, but is quite the grind to get to and only available to certain classes. I imagine that if the Turtle WoW Devs released the Warden model as an illusion (Maiev’s own which is shared with the Warden in Alah’thalas) that many would make this purchase.

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/modelviewer/classic/webthumbs/item-set/1/1/186/186.webp)

Client made a similar item in Legion, where players could from the Wardens receive a toy which acted as an illusion - turning them into a Warden temporarily.

<https://www.wowhead.com/item=147843/siras-extra-cloak>

This means many are already used to this form of illusion necessary for RP, and would be predisposed to purchasing it.

I hope this suggestion is taken for consideration, as the recent additions to the Donation Shop helped to spur on RP quite a bit already.

Thank you for reading!
