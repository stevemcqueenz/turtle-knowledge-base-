This is a solid idea. It opens up options beyond engineering for using grenades, giving paladins and other classes another way to manage pulls. It also brings alchemy into a more active role, rather than just providing passive buffs or long cooldown transmutes. Introducing these types of potions would make the profession more dynamic and engaging, adding versatility to the alchemy toolkit without undermining the core strengths of other professions.

I love this idea because it also draws inspiration from tabletop RPGs, where alchemists have access to a wide array of offensive and defensive tools. For example, the Tanglefoot Bag from Pathfinder RPG is a great representation of how alchemy can be used creatively in combat. Incorporating similar mechanics into World of Warcraft would not only expand the utility of alchemy but also add a strategic layer that players could use in various situations. It really makes the profession more interactive and versatile, rather than being limited to buffs and cooldowns.
