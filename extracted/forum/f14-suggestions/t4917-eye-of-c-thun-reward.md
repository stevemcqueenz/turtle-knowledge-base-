# Eye Of C'Thun reward

## Post 29587 by Zorgdarkness — 2022-11-21T08:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29587#p29587 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is a suggestion to make it possible to repeat the quest from Eye Of C'Thun and choose other rewards that this quest can offer. At this point you can pick only one reward and others will not be able for you because quest is not reputable. I know that this suggestion is not something from original vanilla version of the game, but in original version there is no such things as double specs for example. This is especially important to me, since for both of my specs (holy and shadow priest), the items from this quest are BiS. I hope for the support of like-minded players as well as for a response from the developers.

## Post 29588 by Suggney — 2022-11-21T08:06:00Z

I completely support this. As-is, a priest has to give up the very POSSIBILITY of getting the best Shadow ring in the game just to get the best healing necklace in the game. Realistically, both should be able to come given enough effort. There's no other example in the game of a decision that locks you out of a best-in-slot piece. It'd be a huge quality of life improvement for all raiding priests to be able to turn Eye of C'thun in more than once and reap both the benefits. Of course, they'd need to get the Eye twice!
