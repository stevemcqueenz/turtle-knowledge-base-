i have an idea for a bounty board
doesnt give any xp for quest hand in but it does give coin/rep and (possibly) repeatable?
like go kill x of whatever mob or one specific mob in zone
could also have more wide variety in major cities for further off lands
and can be "go investigate x area" as part of an exploring quest?
for instance have a bounty board in goldshire that asks for 10 defias bandits/rogue wizards and it gives like 10s and 100 rep to stormwind
and go to stormwinds bounty board and it asks you to go in a dungeon to slay x mobs so it also encourages people to do dungeons more because ive seen a lot of problems with people not interested in doing dungeons lately...
or it asks for you to go to an area that isnt always traversed like "we have heard reports of a hidden cave at the southernmost part of kalimdor. go investigate."
having the bounty as a physical note like a letter in your bags might also be helpful in case you have your quest log full and you can have on that page all your bounties that you have jotted down. also having bounties shareable would increase player interaction
