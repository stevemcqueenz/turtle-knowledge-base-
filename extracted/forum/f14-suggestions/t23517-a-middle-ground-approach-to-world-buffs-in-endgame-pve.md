# A Middle-Ground Approach to World Buffs in Endgame PvE

## Post 164605 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-07T18:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164605#p164605 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Turtle WoW team and community,

I’d like to share some thoughts regarding world buffs in high-level PvE.

I personally feel that completely removing world buffs from endgame PvE is too radical.
World buffs massively enhance characters, and more importantly — they’re fun. Playing with them feels powerful, rewarding, and very “Vanilla”.

At the same time, I also feel that their current state represents a missed opportunity.
World buffs still exist, but their actual potential is extremely limited. They are present in the game, yet they barely matter — which feels a bit wasteful for such an iconic Vanilla system.

![⚠️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/26a0.svg) The Current Problem

I fully understand why world buffs are controversial:

dispel griefing

summon / logout meta

pressure to play perfectly or lose everything

Because of this, they tend to be either:

completely removed, or

kept in a very restricted and underwhelming form

In both cases, their design potential is left unused.

![💡](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4a1.svg) Proposed System: Controlled, Weekly Raid World Buffs

Instead of removing them entirely, I’d like to suggest a controlled and non-abusive alternative:

Once a character has legitimately obtained world buffs at least once,
they unlock access to a NPC or mechanism at the entrance of each raid.

This NPC would allow the player (or the raid leader) to activate world buffs once per raid, per week.

Buffs would only function inside the raid instance.

![🎯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3af.svg) Why this could be a good middle ground

Preserves the fun and power fantasy of world buffs

Removes the toxic aspects (dispels, preparation meta, stress)

Prevents abuse through weekly limitations

Turns world buffs into a meaningful gameplay choice, not a chore

Gives value back to a system that currently feels underutilized

Instead of being something players avoid or ignore, world buffs could become a rewarded, intentional mechanic, aligned with Turtle WoW’s Vanilla+ philosophy.

I’d love to hear what the community and devs think about a middle-ground solution like this, or similar approaches that allow world buffs to truly shine without breaking the game.

Thanks for reading!

Thanks for sharing your experience — I actually agree with a lot of what you’re saying.

Everything you describe (raid logging, DMN tribute rushes, spreadsheets, boss item holding, timing pressure) was exactly what made traditional world buffs unhealthy. I don’t think anyone wants to go back to that.

However, my suggestion isn’t about restoring the old world buff meta.

It’s precisely about removing all of those logistical and social problems, while keeping the fun aspect of world buffs as a controlled, intentional mechanic.

In the system I’m proposing:

No DMN tribute runs

No world timers or schedules

No raid logging

No spreadsheets

No holding buffs for hours or days

World buffs would:

be activated intentionally

once per raid, per week

inside the instance only

At that point, they stop being a chore or an obligation, and become a design choice, similar to a cooldown or a raid-wide consumable.

I fully agree that big numbers should primarily come from skill, execution, and gear.
But I also think there’s room for a limited, well-designed system that adds power fantasy without reintroducing the old problems.

That’s why I’m advocating for a middle-ground redesign, not a return to the classic world buff meta.

I understand the reaction, but I think this is mixing up old world buff issues with what’s actually being proposed.

This isn’t about bringing back:

speedrun metas

MC in 15 minutes

raid logging

or forcing anyone to optimize

The idea is not “world buffs everywhere, all the time”.

It’s a strictly limited, opt-in system:

once per raid, per week

activated intentionally

inside the instance only

no preparation, no timers, no world logistics

At that point, world buffs stop being a system that dictates how people play, and simply become an optional raid modifier.

If someone doesn’t want to use them, nothing changes for them.
If a guild wants a bit more power fantasy once a week, they have that option — without impacting anyone else.

Completely removing a system because of how it used to function doesn’t necessarily mean it can’t be redesigned in a healthier way.

That’s all I’m suggesting: a redesign, not a rollback.
