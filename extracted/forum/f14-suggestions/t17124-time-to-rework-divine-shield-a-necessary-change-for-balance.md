# Time to Rework Divine Shield: A Necessary Change for Balance

## Post 116823 by Parad1gm — 2025-01-15T13:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116823#p116823 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Let's talk about the most absurdly broken ability in Vanilla WoW—Divine Shield. Paladins run around in their little bubble of invulnerability, immune to everything, smugly healing to full or dragging fights out for eternity. It’s the ultimate "I don't want to play fair" button. It's time we fix this nonsense.

Here’s the proposal:
Remove Divine Shield entirely. Yes, you read that right. Instead, let’s give Paladins a spell-focused equivalent to Blessing of Protection (BoP)—call it Blessing of Resolve or something equally holy-sounding. This "Blessing of Resolve" would make the target immune to spell damage and effects for 6 seconds.

And while we’re at it, let’s cut the duration of Blessing of Protection down to 6 seconds, too. Six seconds is more than enough for any competent healer to top off two players. Longer durations just drag out fights and promote cheesy tactics where Paladins just cycle BoP and bubble to waste your time.

Why 6 seconds? Simple: It’s enough to:

Get off a full heal.
Let your teammates peel for you.
Avoid feeling completely useless, but not long enough to turn every encounter into a frustrating joke.
Now, I know the Paladin playerbase is probably foaming at the mouth right now. “But Divine Shield is our class identity!” No, your class identity is being a support class that doesn’t carry fights single-handedly. A 12-second immunity to everything is absurd. Even mages with Ice Block don’t have the luxury of healing themselves while untouchable. Why should you?

With this change, Paladins would still have tools to save themselves or allies, but without the unkillable meme bubble ruining PvP. You want to heal to full? Use your 6 seconds wisely, like everyone else who has to think critically about their toolkit.

This change would make Paladins more balanced and less frustrating for other classes. After all, no one enjoys fighting someone who’s literally untouchable. If you disagree, you’re probably a Paladin main who’s scared to play without your crutch.

TL;DR:

Replace Divine Shield with a spell immunity blessing like BoP for spells.
Reduce the duration of both Blessing of Protection and the new blessing to 6 seconds.
No more bubble nonsense ruining PvP balance.
Let’s level the playing field. Thoughts? ![😏](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f60f.svg)
