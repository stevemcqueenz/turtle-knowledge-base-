# Diplomat questline Part II

## Post 8676 by Amyloriley — 2020-12-10T22:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8676#p8676 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Would there be interest in a custom quest in the Diplomats quest line that brings you up to Unfriendly with the opposite faction?

That way, you could enter opposite faction cities without being attacked, but not be able to interact with NPCs. This would be to facilitate roleplay with other diplomats.

The Unfriendly reputation would be the final quest in the quest line, with a few diplomacy-inspired quests leading up to it. Convincing NPCs, working together with other diplomats in group quests, meeting up in neutral territory, that sort of thing.

For instance, I'd imagine a tradeable quest item as a reward from a Night Elf-only quest,a tradeable quest item from an Orc-only quest, a tradeable quest item from a Human-only quest, etc... And a quest with a requirement of all quest items to complete.

> **Sidebar: RuneScape**
>  For the RuneScape players, if this reminds you of the Shield of Arrav quest, you'd be right that I'm going for something like this.

Of course, the quest could be completed with a single account and multiple alts to message the quest items. But the main idea, also for flavour, is that you need to work together with multiple players -- as diplomats -- to gain your eventual Unfriendly reputation.
