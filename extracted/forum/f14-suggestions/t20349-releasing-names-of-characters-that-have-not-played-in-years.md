## Post 140270 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-18T09:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140270#p140270 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is a policy about inactive names and they get automatically released as long as the character is below lvl 40. See [viewtopic.php?t=5599](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=5599)

So if you see a lvl 1 squatting a good name (and there are plenty, I feel your pain) they logged in at least once in the last 6 months.

I'm not sure about the level 40 threshold to retain a name forever, but for lvl 60s I'm leaning pretty strongly towards never losing your name. This server was advertised as "forever Vanilla" and "slow & steady". There will be people dipping in again after extended breaks of up to several years. And lvl 60s are part of the history of the server, even if they're not active anymore.

But in general - yes, namespace crowding is starting to become a huge issue. I've recently created some new alts, and it easily took me 20+ attempts to find decent names. So in a way low-levels squatting names is a blessing, because at least those names can get released eventually (I suspect I snagged at least one of those released names, there's no way nobody came up with it in 6+ years of server operation).
