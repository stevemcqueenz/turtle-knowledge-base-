The item has a 25% droprate chance on mobs that share they spawn with raptors that don't seem to be killed for any quest (Bloodfen Scytheclaw).

I highly suggest boosting the droprate on Acidic Venom, as it's this quests is currently a pain, even though it's part of the very interesting "burned inn" custom quest chain !
