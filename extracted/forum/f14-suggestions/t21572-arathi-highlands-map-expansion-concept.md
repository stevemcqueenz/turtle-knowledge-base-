## Post 149714 by Galendor (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-01T09:55:00Z

A few years ago I also thought about expanding the Arathi Highlands and using the eastern part of this zone, which I called the Arathi Plateau:

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/759727031666737193/915631046194790440/Arath_Plateau.jpg?ex=68b6699d&is=68b5181d&hm=38ca3c660d1ad50a831ae85d362f895d1bc3e0ab91e4871c0cc005b04c45b53a&)

I started from the idea that Highlands are the place where first bonds between humans and elves of Quel'Thalas were created, so somewhere in these lands there should be traces of those events. Thus, the idea of ​​​​Larasgard and the Shrine of the First Union was born, where human and elven architecture are intertwined into a single thing. Of course, over the centuries, this place fell into decay, as did Fort Ignaeus, the ancient keep of the Arathi tribe.

The southern part of the plateau is dedicated to a non-canonical legend from RPG books about a half-elf named Starfeller - the circles of banishing left over from the time of the great ritual of this sorcerer, and the ruins of his magical tower still cast a shadow on the local weald, and the Argus Wake cult invaded these lands in search of some ancient knowledge left here from the time of Starfeller.

I believe that the space in Arathi has very, very big potential for storytelling, and hope to see this place alive in TurtleWoW one day.
