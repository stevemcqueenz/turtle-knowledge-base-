I dont get why there are mobs you need to basically corps run past to get into a zone... You removed the elites when you added a zone entrance in grim batol, so why not hyjal? it just seems not thought out and poorly designed the way it's done now. Also the map art for darkwhisper gorges doesn't even exist since the lay out is nothing like it looks like on the map so you end up having to wonder around dying even more. I'm generally pretty positive and forgiving, but this is the only custom turtle wow content i've seen where I honestly think it's objectively bad as is. Not trying to be disrespectful to the devs I just really think it lowers the overall experience as it is right now and changing it so the elites aren't right in your way would be better.
