The only real news we have about Blood Elf areas being added to the game is that there's plans for Quel'Thalas (Eversong and Ghostlands) to be endgame material and  Silvermoon City will be a raid in the future.

Turtle follows WC3 and Vanilla lore (later expansions are not canon, though some ideas and concepts are canonized if they're cool), all Blood Elves are canonically in Outlands right now. The current High Elves are those who refused to go to Outlands and stayed with Alliance, abandoning Quel'Thalas (which is in ruins).
If anything, when Turtle includes Outlands in the distant future, Blood Elves will be hostile to both Horde and Alliance and unplayable. Playable Blood Elves were only added in TBC because the Horde needed a "pretty race" because of faction imbalance.
