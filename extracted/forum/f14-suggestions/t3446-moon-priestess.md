# Moon priestess

## Post 20804 by Stubbyrole — 2022-08-01T21:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20804#p20804 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The night elf priest racial abilities (Elune's grace and Starshards) are very underpowered. They could be buffed, but I thought instead it would interesting to play on the moon priestess theme by replacing them with some abilities that push the moon priest /night elf / Elune theme a bit more, and perhaps offering a different play style. I don't really have any specific ideas, but I would be interested in hearing people's thoughts on this.

Perhaps a special moonlight form, or a stealth ability, or something that takes the arcane damage theme further.
