I've been RPin with quite a few people with their high elves/blood elves and we started to speculate about Kael'thas' intentions in Outland. Through the new zone's questing we learn that Kael'thas still loves his people and is putting their best interest first in regards to the magical addiction. We talk about the political and hostile divide between high elf and blood elf and how we could bridge back the gap to become one people again. It was an awesome rp event. We came out with possible scenarios with the magical lay lines connections across Azeroth to revitalize the Sunwell.

One of the best scenarios was the connection with Karazan and how it is such a massive fountain of arcane energy with the upcoming patch and our assault through upper Karazan the magical pool can be directed/transferred from the tower back to the Sunwell reactivating it. This could be done with the aide of the blood elves, who now use demonic energy to sustain themselves, with same demonic ritual that was used to resurrect Kel'thuzad in warcraft 3. This act by the Blood elves would bridge the gap between High elves and Blood elves uniting the race once more and work toward rebuilding Quel'Thalas.

I love the Blood elves from warcraft 3 one of the best looking races, with amazing lore in all of warcraft and its a crime how Kael'thas was treated by blizzard from WC3 to wow BC. I hope turtle does his story justice, I have a ray of hope after questing through the new high elf zone and see how they depicted Kael as the leader he was in WC3.

I appreciate the idea, but I feel this 'divide' should be and remain an actual division but with some gameplay changes going forward.  High Elves who side with Vereesa Windrunner (several of them are already in the HE starter zone) would remain as they are with building up and helping their people in their new custom city.  Actually having proper Blacksmithing/Mining trainers, having NPCs that train all class skills properly, etc.  Blood Elves could align with the Horde and be in a new custom zone(s) perhaps east of Swamp of Sorrows or something like that.  That way both sides get elves and it evens out the population a bit.

I don't feel the 'merger' of the High Elves and Blood Elves is wise for RP or lore alike.  They always have had different allegiances and different goals.  The High Elves in WoW Vanilla, TBC, and WotLK in the Hinterlands never tried to 'merge' with their Blood Elf cousins at all.  They attacked on sight.

Trolls have a single tribe (Darkspear) aligned with the Horde; other tribes actively are hostile to EVERYONE else.
Orcs are the same way, other Orcish tribes (Blacktooth, I think) are hostile to EVERYONE else.
Goblins now in Twow version have one group (Durotar Labor Union) with the Horde and the rest are neutral (Ratchet/Gadgetzan) or even hostile to EVERYONE else (Venture Corp).
Tauren have entire tribes that are hostile to EVERYONE else including other Tauren!

So there is a precedent of 'same race but hostile to different groups' among various parts of Azeroth.

Now taking Karazhan energy and syncing it up with the Sunwell is a terrible idea.  First, the Sunwell is a 'fallen' region and everyone has fled the area (Blood Elf or High Elf alike) while it has been overrun with high-level elite undead from the Plaguelands.  All the warding stones destroyed; total loss.  The HE and BE people both fled, with HE establishing a new city Alah'thalas.

If we want to do a full redemption of the HE race it would likely need to come from Dalaran helping create a new 'Sunwell' alternative.  That reinforces the unified bond between Humans and High Elves that has been around for thousands of years since the earliest days of Arathor.

It would require some extensive writing skills to have a lengthy questline for the High Elves (at max level 60) to receive a teleport stone to an instanced version of the ruined Sunwell area to obtain samples of the residual corrupted arcane energy for Dalaran mages to research.  Then there's a few quests to obtain purification materials from various level 60 dungeons and raids.  Once that's done, the Dalaran wizards get together and perform a ritual that takes 14 days real world time to complete.  During this time the player has to do 14 days of dailies to replenish certain arcane supplies from 5-man dungeons or otherwise for the Dalaran wizards working on it.  When this is all done they come together to forge a mighty arcane legendary weapon exclusive to High Elves (*insert cool Thalassian name here*) while other races would get alternatives/equivalents based on race/class.  They also create a new 'Sunwell Core' of sorts that blends Arcane and Holy energies with the help of the Church of Stormwind's priests and healers to infuse it with Holy Light energy.

So basically a roundabout way of doing what happened 'officially' but without the goofy 'Sunwell was a person all along' garbage.
