# Attacking PvP-flagged players should not be possible when you aren't flagged yourself

## Post 83897 by Toaonyk — 2024-01-07T13:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83897#p83897 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As said in the title: If you are not PvP-flagged yourself, you shouldn't be able to attack a player who is.
This serves two purposes:

 1. You don't get the element of surprise against a flagged player (whether it's WM or just him having the flag up for the moment).
 2. You can't *accidentally* attack a flagged player despite never having intended to.
