# Various suggestions in total disorder

## Post 149158 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-29T15:38:00Z

Few suggestions !

 - Add UBRS to the LFG ;

 - Add the ability to see, when inspecting an item, what the original item is and how it is TMog ;

 - Add the ability to more easily transmog weapons regardless of their category (Example: I can transmog a one-handed sword into a dagger) ;

 - Complete the development of the Scarlet raid (We have an access, but we don't really know where it leads us) ;

 - Add intermediate PvP sets (Lvl 19-29-39-49 for example) ;

 - Add the possibility to gain XP with VHs (in War Mode or on the PvP server), to boost wild PvP ;

 -Add the possibility to gain XP with wins in Battlegrounds ;

 - Why can some people have "flying" mounts that don't fly ? Simple question, but I notice it detracts from the immersive aspect of the game ;

 -Introduce past cosmetic items into the shop (like the Tel'Abim gorilla, for example) ;

 -Add a sort of "Adventurer's Guide" a bit like on retail, so that new players can find their way around with the new features !

That's all I have in my mind for the moment ! Feel free to say what you think respectfully :)
