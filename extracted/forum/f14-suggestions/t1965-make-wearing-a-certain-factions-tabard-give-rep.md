## Post 11831 by Lahire (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2021-08-26T05:39:00Z

This tabard mechanic actually promoted a bad gameplay experience in retail. The dungeon braindead farm, completely disconnected from any roleplay reason. Yes it is quicker, but it breaks immersion and becomes a mindless chore.

I think the vanilla design of linking reputation to quests (and specific mobs hated for lore reasons) is a far more intelligent design, as it promotes interacting with the faction you want to courtship. From a gameplay perspective, it is more involved. From a roleplay/immersion perspective, it is more thematic (you are literally aiding the people who respect you).
