# Alterac Valley scrap turn-in rework

## Post 88612 by Jstansberry (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-02-24T02:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88612#p88612 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Alterac Valley - for most players - is a grueling and unpleasant experience. The reasons generally given are that the matches are too easy to stall, the NPCs are too strong, and that the map is biased towards Alliance. I have a proposal for a change that may address two of those points decently well.

Due to the nature of turn in quests being completed over the course of the game and NPCs getting extremely strong - the longer the match goes on, the harder it is to end it. Breaking into bases when both sides have upgraded all the way to champions is not fun or engaging gameplay, to say the least.

My suggestion is that instead of guards, archers, et cetera getting even stronger than their already frustrating baseline, players would get a semi-permanent buff that grows in strength, reducing damage taken from and increasing damage dealt to enemy NPCs. Turn-ins would accelerate the speed of the match rather than slow it down. No more Veterans/Champions, NPCs would get weaker - not stronger - the longer the match goes on.

Thoughts?
