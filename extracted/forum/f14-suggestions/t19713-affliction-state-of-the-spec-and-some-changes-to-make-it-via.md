# [AFFLICTION] State of the spec and some changes to make it viable:

## Post 134145 by Jackbiers123 — 2025-06-03T21:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134145#p134145 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am a warlock main in bis gear for about a year, i mainly play destro in pvp because it is more efficient and pays up better if you need to carry but i'd really like deep affliction to have its own spot to shine. I have more than 120-150 test bgs with dark harvest build so here i will suggest some QoL changes and modifications thought to fix some of the spec more blatant issues, or features that for some reason got left behind in the class changes like drain life.

Problems with full deep affliction in pvp:

 * Dispells use 1,5 gcd, dots take 3 seconds from application to make the first damage tick. A backline healer or a dps with dispell mechanics as ret paladin or spriest can effectively disable all lock damage by spam dispelling, while killing him, even when they moving, for less mana cost. Even dark harvest can get dispelled, arcane missiles not.

Possible fix: an original solution is adding something that locks dots on targets. Resilient shadows could apply an undispellable debuff as second effect from drains that locks dots for about 10 seconds. If you know you are going to get spam dispelled, you open with a drain tick (lose 1 gcd) , you put the debuff, you put dots on target, and manage renewing the debuff with ocassional drain ticks while making your rotation. It would give more skill ceilling and dynamic playstile to the build, while keeping it balanced. Healers can just negate this using range still, and if lock is forced to channel, he is also vulnerable to kicks.

 *Aff warlock gets obliterated by cc, dies to rogue stuns and inside paladin HoJs. Periodic damage takes time to build and you cannot build inside cc, so destro just pushes more pressure, and is overall the smarter choice.

 *Drain life and mana got forgotten in the changes, they do not really scale. In bis gear drain life hits for 160, dark harvest for 500. You earn more life by having some vampyrism on and using DH than from draining life. What i suggest, make all drains form part of Soul Siphon talent, and if in the future drain ticks apply ISB debuff, make drain life form part of it, so all drains get sinergies from other talents, and you can choose to play defensive.

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#a/i6uEzpG]

 *Drain mana scales poorly, i suggest the previous. Right now is a BM skill to trigger people with low mana that you prefer to grief rather than straight up killing with drain soul or DH. Warlock has a large toolset, it would be fun if you could adapt to each situation instead of choosing to straight up DH everything.

 *Dispell resistance got crippled in the new affliction build, when Blizzard servers guaranteed having 100% dispell resistance on periodic damage at least with some gimmick skill that you could use, for balance sake. You can see the lack of equality in some talents. Arcane mage does not need it, arcane missiles are undispellable, with 60 yard range, or destro, or warriors.

See the difference in talent budget option between both imgs.

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#a/crLykXy]

 * I would really add "fear" to the Fel Concentration pushback resist skills, or at least howl of terror considering it always had affliction flavour. Fear casts gets obliterated by pushbacks, and sometimes it is not worth if you lose 4k hp during cast. But this change is for dev consideration.

 *   Remove the -16 from Eye of Dormant Corruption and replace it for - spirit. I have the feeling that the item got cucked by the name election, which seems fairly unfair considering all other kara items with no stam got rightfully reverted. Affliction has already less stamina on gear than healers.

What leads to the question:

Why really play aff lock if you have spriests. Spriests have raw damage, a shield, dispells, shadowform with 15% physical mitigation plus less caster damage, heals on demand, renew, built up vamp.  Aff lock has damage, but thats all. And some sustain. It lacks its own spot. You play it just because kills are fun.

As you can see, i did not ask for more buffs on damage, or things that could break pve raid environment and enlarge the powercreep era we are in. Affliction lock would stay esentially the same, while keeping some very good QoL to take a good use of its arsenal. I think the last buffs to affli were good, but the build still needs a push so we the afflilovers do not pick it just for having something different than destro from time to time.

The reason why affli doesnt have good dispel protection, is because dots in vanilla do **a lot** of damage. Two dots alone are enough to murder someone completely. There is  no resilience to lower the effectiveness of dots. You could mindgame spam rank 1 corruptions in order to bait your opponent into wasting mana on dispels, and then put on the max rank once he realizes  he's  getting baited. Your kill condition against dispellers is usually being able to land a fear so your dots can do their thing, and winning out on the sustain game. Yea, its not easy if a ret is training your ass, but its doable.

Yea, affli is at a disadvantage a lot of the times, especially once someone is able to get ontop of the warlock to murder him, but its main strength is staying as far back as possible and rotting everything. If anyone tries to get close, you exhaust them and keep your distance. If there's a dispeller actively dispelling your dots, that means  he's using GCDs that can be spent to heal up through your frontline's burst damage. If your team isn't able to provide the frontline pressure, you can still rely on hardcast destro spells.  There's nothing stopping you from going the good ol' SM-Ruin spec, apart from maybe dark harvest being a good button to press (which feels redundant to me with the revamped drain soul).

In general, the warlock 1v1 meme in the past has always been "Yea you kill me and I can't do shit other than dot you, but  once the fight is over you are also dead to those dots". Its an overexageration, a good warlock can always pull off more than just full dot someone before they die.

I agree that fel concentration should probably incorporate Howl of Terror for the pushback resist. I also agree soul siphon should incorporate more abilities, but other than that, I don't think affli needs any more help, other than giving it more stamina on its respective sets (Idk who the genius was that thought giving it less health for more mana was a good idea,health IS the mana of warlocks). The overall pvp experience is not too different than in actual classic, and a good affli(SM/Ruin) warlock was a menace, and was always capable of 1v1ing any class, even rogues.

The difference between vile poison talent and resilient shadows, is that rogues need to be in melee range with a **chance** to apply their poisons. Abolish poison  by itself is 1 GCD that can keep clearing poisons for up to 9 seconds, shamans can drop a totem that automatically cleanses, and paladins can spam cleanse to remove them. Without vile poisons rogues have trouble keeping on target against druids, paladins and shamans. Warlocks for contrast can spam their dispellable spells from 36 yards with no cooldown. A rogue without poisons might as well be  good as dead if his target doesn't die in a stunlock.
