# Tents should only work in group/raid group

## Post 152848 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-23T17:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152848#p152848 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there!

Love the game, awesome! Tents too, really cool idea, but ugly because everyone is crowding in one spot.

Idea:

 1: Only one tent effect can be active; it's an aura that affects everyone in the same normal group.
Effect: Many tents will be placed where groups take a break, but only up to 5 people will gather around a tent. Give the tent different styles and this would make a lovely picture. Add that the bonfire from cooking increases the tent effect. Maybe have placeable camping furniture to further increase the effect and sit on.

 2: Give us a big tent for raid groups, one with benches and a bar inside, a dance floor, and festive decoration!

That way the tent culture would improve a lot and it wouldn't just be a spot where everyone goes AFK. Also, it would motivate grouping together and therefore have the effect of increasing social interactions. It might even have the effect that people wouldn't only camp in front of Stormwind, but instead spread out for a more aesthetic picture.

Looking forward to feedback, cheers!
Morkahja

I wasn’t clear.
This isn’t about the choice of a single player. It’s about a feature available to everyone that affects the entire server.
Let’s first look from the perspective of an individual player: it doesn’t have much influence on the game content because I make the choice, and so the impact I create only affects myself.
But if we look from the perspective of the entire server population, things change enormously. The mass use of these tents really does change the gameplay. If 200 people start using tents, something will inevitably get skipped, and that "skip" will have consequences for another 40 players who aren’t using the tents, are falling behind, and will have difficulty finding groups for dungeons or missions.
I’ve experienced this myself, and I don’t use them. I often can’t find people to do missions in a certain area. I frequently encounter people with setups like "exhaustion" or "slow and steady" who stay in the territory for longer periods.
Those who use this bonus have their time spent in the regions literally halved. The general population rises faster.
I don't know if the developers have any tools to monitor this phenomenon, but I'm sure it has a strong impact on leveling.
