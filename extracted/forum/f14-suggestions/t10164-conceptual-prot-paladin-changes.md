# Conceptual prot paladin changes

## Post 66432 by Dogfight — 2023-09-22T21:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66432#p66432 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With upcoming class changes in the future I have a hope that the survivability and threat of prot paladins will see some improvements. This post is meant as inspiration for such changes. While some of these suggestions might be unlikely to happen or have to balanced, it's enjoyable to try to add something to all other suggestions that are being posted.

So here are some conceptual changes to improve prot paladin.

 **General**
 -	Crusader Strike now does holy damage. Can not crit, still 1,5sec CD.
 -	“On hit” damage effects can no longer proc Judgement of Wisdom.
 -	Holy Wrath is now included in Spiritual Focus talent with Holy Light and Flash of Light.

 **Holy Strike reworked (Max rank displayed):**
o	6 second cooldown
o	Deals 80% of weapon damage as holy damage and an additional 100 holy damage every 3 seconds for 15 seconds. This effect stacks up to 3 times.
o	The strike still scales with AP and SP (40%) and the DoT scales with SP 15% pr tick.
o	Shorter CD because 10 seconds is too long for a main damage and threat ability – so much dead time in between. Damage reduced but added a stacking DoT for DPS and TPS sustain.

 **Seal of the Crusader:**
 -	Increases attack speed by 25% and spellpower by 25%. Melee attacks deal 25% less damage, but hits two additional targets and all damage done is considered holy damage.

 **Judgement of the Crusader:**
 -	Judging this seal will deal 40% of weapon damage as holy damage and increases spell damage taken by up to 75 on your primary target and up to two additional targets within 8 yds.

o	I like synergy between paladins and casters. This replaces the proc effect JotC has had up to now, and therefore adding a spell damage taken debuff rather than forcing someone into using Nightfall with todays JotC.

 **Protection**
•	Precision and Guardian’s Favor talents moved to Row 1.
•	Redoubt moved to Row 2.
•	Holy Shield no longer on GCD.
•	Taunt is now baseline and trainable at level 10.

 **Precision:** Increases melee hit chance by 1/2/3% and spell hit chance by 2/4/6%
 -	To help with taunt and Exorcism which are affected by spell hit.

 **Reckoning:** Has a 20/40/60/80/100% chance to reduce the cooldown of your Judgement ability by 4 seconds when you parry an attack.

 **Shield Specialization:** Increases block value by 33/66/100% and all resistances by 25/50/75 while Holy Shield is active. Any damage blocked or resisted will consume a Holy Shield charge. Blocked attacks also has a 33/66/100% chance to restore 2% mana.
o	Instead of adding a flat 10% damage reduction like warriors, this is a more unique way of adding damage reduction. Connecting it to Holy Shield strengthens single target tanking, limits its impact on AoE tanking and farming and ensures it cannot be combined with 31pt talents in other trees.
o	This will push prot paladins towards benefitting more from block, which many appreciates as class fantasy. Being rewarded for stacking block and block value to compliment/make the best use of Holy Shields 30% block chance and 4 charges will make it a unique way of gearing because in general it’s not valued that much atm.

 **One handed weapon specialization:** Increases all damage done by 5/10/15% and total stamina by 5/10/15% when a one-handed weapon is equipped.
o	To compensate for hybrid gear tax and be closer to warriors and druids on health. If paladins are to benefit from SP on gear for threat, then it takes away from other stats, such as stamina, on the item stat budget. Also, it is a specialization, it makes sense for protection to have more health than holy and retribution.

 **Mana Regen talent: New 2 point talent in Row 6:** Critical hits with any holy damaging ability increases your casting and attack speed by 10/20% for 6 seconds and increases the mana the paladin generates from Judgement of Wisdom and Seal of Wisdom by 50/100% for 6 seconds.
o	Using already available abilities to increase mana regen. Also makes prot paladins less dependant on Windfury by adding attack speed increase.
o	To make better use of abilities like Consecration (Mana expensive + new Libram), new Reckoning talent and better mana sustain (I.e mana burn fights). Mana gained is also threat.

 **New 31pt talent: For the Light**
o	20 minutes cooldown
o	Call upon the light to protect you. All holy damage done by Seals, Judgements, Holy Shield and Holy Strike provides you with a shield that absorbs damage equal to the damage done by the paladin for 10 seconds.

 **End note**
Personally I'm a big fan of sword and board tanking. To me that is what a tank should be. However with todays threat meta we're forced into 0/21/30 and 2H tanking at higher gear levels. I believe these changes will improve both threat generation and survivability of a sword and board prot paladin, while still opening up for talent build experimentation into ret and holy depending on situation.
