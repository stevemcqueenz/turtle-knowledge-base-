# Heroics

## Post 76622 by Hazhap — 2023-11-09T12:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76622#p76622 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

PvE in classic is very simple and fun, but after you reach a certain point the only PvE content you really participate in are raids. So I propose adding heroic versions of high level dungeons that are gated by some raid quest, maybe killing nefarian or something. The additional loot tables could be very simple, not even necessarily focused on what the loot tables were prior, as was the case in blizzard heroics. Aside from decent loot to pick up that may supplement what a player might be looking for in raid, I think the heroics should be very worthwhile in terms of gold drops. This way, players could enjoy the best part of the game (doing group content with their friends) while also being a good source of gold. This may also be a way to have more interesting encounters in dungeons, like the arena in Blackrock Depths makes you fight all of the bosses on heroic, which may make access to the Savage Gladiator Chain less of a pain.

Just a thought. Cheers!   smiling_turtle_head

> **Hazhap wrote: Thu Nov 09, 2023 12:18 pm**
> So I propose adding heroic versions of high level dungeons that are gated by some raid quest, maybe killing nefarian or something.

As someone who despises PvE raiding, I vote "No" to locking group content behind raid progression.  Yuck!  Raiding is given too much of the best stuff in this game as it is.  The game could very much use more group-type progression, but keep it separate from and parallel to the raid game.

On a different note, I'm not sure the community and culture of this game would tolerate tightly-tuned group content.  Most groups seem to want to be able to plow through everything with little thought and zero strategy.   The tuning on average on T-wow does feel on the "soft" side; most high-level group content, including the custom instances Karazhan Crypt and Black Morass (2.0) can be trio'd by people wearing blue-grade gear.  I like tighter tuning and the notion that sloppy groups should be met by a big "You Lose," but as the years go by I feel like I'm more and more part of a shrinking minority.
