# Roleplay DM Role

## Post 16562 by Arduron — 2022-02-01T13:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16562#p16562 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Roleplaying is finally making a resurgence on the server! I've seen lots of events starting up all over the place and it's great but the community is still rather small compared to the population overall. It also feels like the RP aspects of the server are no longer as emphasized as they used to be, so I thought perhaps we could bolster that.

I was wondering if it would be possible for a minor volunteer GM role to be created to help facilitate RP events. It could have limited abilities but could spawn some lootless, expless mobs to fill in for enemies or RP events, nothing too fancy. The "DM" could also become a boss or important NPC.

I understand that resources are stretched thin with the explosive growth of the server but it'd be cool if the Roleplayers could try and help out too and get something too. If you had some volunteer "DMs" they could help make it so the varying guilds could get a chance to bring their stories and campaigns to life!

With how much the server's been expanding and with some worries from the community, as well as the RP community growing again, I'd think it would be in the developers' best interest to give RPers a bit more love again.

A dungeon master role sounds interesting, but as someone who both roleplays and plays hardcore, couldn't a dungeon master role be exploited to intentionally grief hardcore players by spawning mobs or becoming bosses? Or accidentally kill a hardcore player who happened to be roleplaying?

## Post 16578 by Arduron — 2022-02-01T16:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16578#p16578 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

That's where community policing goes in, and by boss i just mean they get some fancy gear and RP as the boss. Not becoming an actual boss with spells or anything. And if they do try to abuse, they should get permabanned. It'd be a volunteer role but the person would have to be vetted. This would also be specifically just for events, not just at random. The higher ups should be notified when this is happening so if something happens they can deal with it but don't have to be explicitly babysitting the event. I'd expect if this were to be a thing it'd be a separate account but traced to the same user.
Not just anyone should be able to become one, just not like anyone becomes a GM.

Unless a RP enemy explicitly has like abilities, i'd expect them to just autoattack. I'm sure some kind of training would take place though. There should be limits on how much the DM can do with NPCs and they have to clean up the spawned content immediately after the event. So it'll take a dedicated person to do that.
