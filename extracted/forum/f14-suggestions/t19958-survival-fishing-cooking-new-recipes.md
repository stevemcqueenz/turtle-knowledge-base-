# Survival -> Fishing -> Cooking new recipes

## Post 136433 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-20T17:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136433#p136433 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In each location above level 30, you can catch special fish that is found only there.
Highland cod - in Arathi and hinterlands
Swamp catfish - Dustwallow and swamp of sorows
Plague salmon - Plaguelands
Rotting crucian carp - Felwood
Lava eel - burning steps and sering gorge
etc.
By default, the chance to catch is 1%
In the survival profession, you can create baits for each of these fish, which increases the chance to catch up to 30%. In any place, not in schools.
In cooking - recipes using these fish.
these dishes give additional resistance, damage from different schools of magic, healing, passive restoration of HP and mana, etc.
