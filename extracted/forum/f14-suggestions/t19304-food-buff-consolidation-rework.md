# Food buff consolidation/rework

## Post 131140 by Sagabal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-15T00:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131140#p131140 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As it stands, food buffs are a bit confusing to understand for most players.  The basic advice given to new players is "Food can give a buff called Well Fed that can have different effects, and if you eat a different food it replaces the old buff", which is somewhat accurate but starts falling apart even with vanilla foods.  Most Well Fed buff foods grant Stam and Spirit for 15 minutes, and a few others grant mana regen (Smoked Sagefish and Sagefish Delight) or strength (Smoked Desert Dumplings).  Additionally, the new Turtle WoW foods follow this pattern, with all three banana breads, gilneas hot stew, gurubashi gumbo, and the chocolate dodge fish all using the Well fed buff.

However, Grilled Squid/Sour Mountain Berry (Increased Agility), Hot Smoked Bass (Increased Spirit), Runn Tum Tuber Surprise/Juicy Striped Melon (Increased Intellect), Mightfish Steak/Cooked Glossy Mightfish/Windblossom Berries/Sweet Mountain Berry (Increased Stamina), Dirge's Kickin' Chimaerok Chops/Hardened Mushroom (Increased Stamina), Blessed Sunfruit (Blessed Sunfruit), Nightfin Soup (Mana Regeneration), Poached Sunscale Salmon (Health Regeneration), Dragonbreath Chili (Dragonbreath Chili) all do not follow this pattern, having buffs with different names and different durations [let me know if I missed any here].  These buffs stacked with Well Fed before patch 1.9, but afterwards several were made obsolete as they take up the same food slot.  Additionally, their interaction with newly added Well Fed effects is not always consistent with this.  For example, Grilled Squid fully stacks with Danonzo's Tel'Abim Medley.

I propose either a cleanup of these buffs and others found that behave this way to use a consistent 'Well Fed' nomenclature and stacking behavior (and duration?), or instead otherwise lean into the original pre-1.9 behavior of allowing non-Well Fed food buffs to stack with Well Fed as long as they have different names.  That second one seems more interesting but could also be a right pain to balance and add additional consumable grinds, so there's a lot to take into consideration.

A different idea I've had floating around for another direction to go in is Alchemy-Lab-style Cooking Cauldrons that would allow for mass-crafting Stews/Meals that could allow combining multiple effects into a single buff food, to leave the other items and stacking behaviors in as an odd engine quirk that themselves wouldn't affect balance with the new (balance-affecting) meals.  This is a bit outside the scope here though.
