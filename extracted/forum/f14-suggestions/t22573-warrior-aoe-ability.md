Suggestion:
Give Thunder Clap to Shamans as a Nature-based spell, where it thematically belongs, and introduce a new Warrior ability—Trample.

Trample would function like Consecration but move with the Warrior, pulsing each second to damage all nearby enemies. Its damage would scale with Strength and be divided evenly among all targets.

It’s worth remembering that **Thunderclap was originally conceived as a shaman ability** during early WoW development. Even today, its fantasy and visual identity still feel much closer to elemental shockwaves than to a disciplined, weapon-focused warrior.

Because of that, forcing Thunderclap into the warrior kit as an AoE threat tool has always felt off. It pushes warriors toward a role that **overlaps with paladins** and dilutes what makes warrior tanking distinct.

If warriors need some degree of AoE control, the solution doesn’t have to be another damaging AoE button. A much cleaner approach is **The Art of Insulting**: enhancing Demoralizing Shout to generate meaningful threat.

Since Demoralizing Shout **does not deal damage** and only reduces Attack Power, it naturally generates very little threat and cannot sustain aggro on its own. Turning it into a threat-oriented tool gives warriors controlled pull stabilization without turning them into AoE damage tanks.
