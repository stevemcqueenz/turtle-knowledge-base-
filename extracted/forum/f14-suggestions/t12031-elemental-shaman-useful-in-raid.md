# Elemental shaman useful in raid

## Post 79696 by Momotormat — 2023-12-03T07:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79696#p79696 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are many suggestions for shamans but I didnt find something especially for elementals.
I often encounter that people dont want to bring elemental shaman to the raid because they dont bring anything special. Their damage isnt that high and they dont have any special totem or aura. That means you can enjoy elemental shaman mostly in PVP.
I would say they could have some talents for boosting casters like spell power totem (totem of wrath) or spell crit aura (elemental oath) like in higher expansions. This would make them more wanted in raids.

## Post 79729 by Jstansberry (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-03T18:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79729#p79729 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Elemental shaman dps is actually quite good. Not as high as warriors or fire mages in t3 content but they can compete decently well until then. I consistently see ele shamans higher on meters than warlocks unless the warlock uses Hellfire on a big aoe pack. Ele shamans also bring a WF totem and most guilds/pugs will not have enough shamans to give all the melee groups a WF totem. Other than sub-par aoe abilities, the only weakness ele seems to have is mana - while in a much better spot with the changed clearcasting proc from Elemental Focus, ele shamans will still go oom faster than any other caster and rely heavily on things like paladin BoW and JoW as well as tea and major mana pots.

I think it'd be risky to buff ele for pve when it's definitely not too bad already. You'd risk making a very strong pvp spec into an absolute nightmare to play against.
