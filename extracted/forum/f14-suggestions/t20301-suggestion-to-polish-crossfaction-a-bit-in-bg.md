# Suggestion to polish Crossfaction a bit in BG

## Post 139630 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-15T17:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139630#p139630 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If Crossfaction BG is already inevitable, I started thinking about something that could help make it not feel like a quick, cheap, and poorly implemented solution.

The first thing I thought about was implementing Crossfaction as a choice, not a forced element. This way, everyone can choose how to play and the fun isn't ruined for others because they're crying about not being able to win.

I thought, it would be weird to see a human helping the Forsaken take Arathi Basin, or an Orc helping the Stormpike expel them from AV, or a Night Elo helping the Orcs deforest Warsong Gulch.

Therefore, based on the illusions present in the store, it occurred to me that players who are placed in the opposing faction should be given a temporary disguise as one of the races of the faction they were assigned:
If you are an Alliance player, disguise yourself as a Horde race, and if you are a Horde player, disguise yourself as an Alliance race. This is simply cosmetic so as not to break the immersion and feel like you're playing on a poorly made server where, due to the complaints of some players, a cheap patch was applied to a feature that could have been fixed with time and effort.
