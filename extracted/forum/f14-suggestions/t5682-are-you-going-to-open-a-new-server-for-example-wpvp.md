## Post 35642 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-21T16:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35642#p35642 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, this might be controversial, but I think it would be a good idea to aggressively scale honor rewards from World PVP. Make it much more appealing to go after fair fights, and allow diminishing returns on killing lower level players to go into negative values (so if someone is grey for you, and you kill them several times in a short span of time, it starts counting as dishonorable kills). More importantly, though, make it so that honorable kills in the open world are worth more than that same kill would be in a battleground. Perhaps significantly so.

Beyond that, and off the top of my head, some things that (I have no idea how feasible to implement they are, but) might encourage a healthy PVP environment include;

- Making it so Warmode rewards PVP play specifically, rather than being something *everyone* wants to take, even if they don't want or are strongly opposed to PVP. [*](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=5649)
- Offer more and better rewards for PVP at each rank. Mostly gear and consumables, but another intriguing possibility might be if at a certain rank each class could buy a "manual" that taught them a new spell/ability focused primarily for PVP play. I'm not enough of a PVP guru to think I could come up with a full list of balanced abilities on the fly, but one thought I have is that these abilities need not be equal. For instance, in the current PVP meta, Paladin would probably only get something relatively minor and/or situational, since they don't need buffing in PVP.
- Add an item that can be collected off the bodies of ranked players in world PVP (essentially dog tags or some other mark of rank), drop chance improved based on rank and level, and further adjusted based on level difference. With repeatable turn-ins for gold or useful consumables, and small amounts of faction rep. Maybe have different tiers based on ranks, so there are better bounties on "high value targets".
- More PVP focused quests. Not like the bounty boards, but either things like "Kill X players of this rank.", or quests into contested zones where, when you do the quest, opposite faction players in the area who are PVP flagged get a popup offering them an opposition quest to stop you.
