# A Lore Quest

## Post 22870 by Mikeloss — 2022-08-19T21:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22870#p22870 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there fellow Turtles!

It is me again with another suggestion.

I love the fact that one can learn more about history of Azeroth through all the in-game books scattered across the lands.
But I think this might go unoticed for some. (Although once you find the first one you might be compeled to find others).
Would it not be fun to have a quest dedicated to this?

I imagine that within each Major city where most books can be found (No actually reading books, but decorations) there could be a NPC who tells you about these Lore books and gives you an item with said list. Then the Players could just casually travel around Azeroth while questing and look for said books. After reaching the last Page that one would be marked as read.
After reading the last book one would return to the Questgiver and receive a follow up quest to travel to Ironforges Library and receive a reward, like a title, say, "The storyteller". What do you think?

Problems:
 - Players can just skip the pages and achieve the quest objective
 - Horde player would have to be awared a one time pass in form of a Buff rendering them unflagged for PvP towards the opposing factions and receive Neutral status towards the Alliance. This of course would need an in-game quest timer so not to abuse the stay in Ironforge.
 - There might be other inconvinences I am definitively forgetting so please help me on this.

Big love  turtle_tongue

## Post 22874 by Sinrek (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-08-19T22:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22874#p22874 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good idea! It's almost like an achievement through the quest. However, a mere check for the number of books that had been read IMO is not enough. The best way to let the player feel rewarded is to add a questionnaire to the quest ending NPC so the player would have a chance to complete the quest only if his answers were relevant to books he had read.

As an example; quest ending NPC could ask if player had read the book "Exile of the High Elves" and ask who was the leader of the High Elves with 3 possible answers from the book. Should player answer correctly - next question.

So for each book there could be several sequences of questions and if player answers them all - XP, honour and glory or perhaps a possibility to go and find another book should be presented.
