## Post 158765 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-11-07T12:55:00Z

The lft tool is fairly restrictive when it comes to who can join for certain dungeons, this is more a product of the dungens themselves have a bit of a levelrange in them.
Dungeons like Uldaman and BRD have a levelrange of 10+ levels in between their easiest and hardest encounter.
A level 35 might wanna do his first uldaman quests he just unlocked. But is it fair to the people who que the dungeon expecting to take down thelevel 42 endboss of the place? And yes its not unfeasable to kill the endboss with a lowleveler. But if the lowleveler happens to be the tank or healer its substantially harder on the party.

There is also the case of SM which offers you a quest to kill all the Wing bosses as soon as level 33. Something players usually only manage to complete by their late 30s/early 40s due to cathedral being substantially higher level than library.

Allowing lvl 33 to que cathedral is a social experiment zo say the least

There is always the approach to look for a group in the old fashioned way if the goal is to complete quests that a group at applicable level might wanna do, knowing they might not make it all the way to the Endboss.
