# Gear Matching System (for BGs)

## Post 39013 by Annoying (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-25T11:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39013#p39013 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was thinking of how to make low level WSG/AB more enjoyable for levelers as most people just seem to give up when a twink joins and then not requeue after the game has ended.

So what about a **Gear Matching System** of some sort?
So levelers would fight levelers and twinks would fight twinks.
Everyone gets to have fun that way.

I hope that would make more people try out WSG/AB as we hardly ever fill up a game atm.

I can see how the regular leveler would not want to queue again when the other side gets one of these beasts, it's GG we lost, every time.
Insane hp difference, insane damage difference, unbelievable supply of speed pots, hp pots, granades and other gadgets (like Grand Arena Trinket (absorbs 750 to 1250 damage)).

I agree that twinks should be nerfed. I have twink too and want fair fight instead of bashing unprepared people.

Gear matching won't help against twink with 2k hp. He has Zandalar buff (300 hp). I also don't recall what is shield-looking buff on him.
Twink with 1343 hp has Dragonslayer world buff (attack power and speed).

To improve twinking situation, devs should:
 - disable high-level enchants on low level characters (including Librams)
 - disable world-buffs on battlegrounds (I wonder why they didn't make it with raid wb nerfing)
 - add level requirement for bandages

Those actions will greatly reduce twink power and it will be enough.
 140 from shoulders, 200 from head/leg enchant, 100 from chest enchant, 90 from bracers.
This is 530 hp only from enchants.

Pala with 1617 hp will be left with 1087 hp. Much better, isn't it?
Hunter with 2090 hp will be left with 1260 hp (he is buffed asf anyway)
