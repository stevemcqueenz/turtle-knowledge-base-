# 16. PVP Tower as poolboost

## Post 133507 by Deeno (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-29T13:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133507#p133507 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is a spinoff of suggestion #16 from my list - a concept of a concept, basically.
Let's ignore for now how this would work on a crossfaction realm.

 **Core Concept:**
Twelve PvP towers would be placed across Azeroth - in locations like Hillsbrad, the STV islands, north of Dustwallow Marsh, etc.
 - One tower is active for one hour, every two hours (so always a one-hour gap between active towers).
 - Towers are captured using a flag mechanic, not the old "stand around forever" style (like EPL).
 - Every minute, the active tower ticks. This tick happens regardless of who owns the tower, but: If your faction controls it at the moment of the tick, your side gets +1 to the weekly honor pool.
 - This results in: 168 hours / 2 × 60 ticks/hour = **5040 potential pool points per week**

 **Why Pool Boost instead of direct honor?**
This is meant as a community-focused incentive. The goal is to:
 - Make ranking slightly more tolerable by easing the pool size pressure.
 - Encourage faction-based world PvP with real, long-term benefits
 - Avoid direct honor exploits or imbalance

 **What's Missing:**
Right now, there's no individual reward for taking part. It helps your faction — but doesn’t directly help you.

 **Feedback Wanted:**
 1. Do you think this adds something fun and competitive to the world - or would it feel a pointless/abandoned objective?
 2. Would this kind of faction-based incentive be enough to get people involved?
 3. What kind of individual reward (if any) would make it worth showing up for these towers. (gold? honor? loot at the end of period? some kind of pvp token from player bodies? - that would be used for upgrade items? alternative source of zg/aq/mc recipes? consumables? ability books?)

Anyway, I'm not optimistic. Just because it would detract from queued PvP which will always be the majority preference. But I could brainstorm on it all day. Love me some wPvP.

## Post 140352 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-18T16:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140352#p140352 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is definitely one of the best open pvp objectives i have seen proposed.
But there are two glaring problems for me with it.

 1. It is feast & famine in who gets to benefit. Chances are the majority of the time the faction with more active ranking pvp players will likely claim them (especially if only ever one is active). On nordrannar at least, the majority of the ranker pop is already horde. Having expected higher poolsizes does increase the draw to horde further. Meaning more imbalance with the faction bg ques.
Considering xfaction bgs are teased, that might not sound like a big problem. But the open pvp objective itself needs a somewhat contested status to even be relevant content. If all pvp players play one faction, you wont have it challenged and it turns into a "faster ranking button" some alts go click.

 2. The people who dont play it benefit most. Openpvp is notorious for how bad the honor returns are. So the people who play battlegrounds and push for r14 are the main beneficiaries.
With at best logging an alt between bgs to flip the cap.

I would instead recommend, the people who are actively holding the tower and stay in the area get a portion of their weekly rank decay reduced. That way, you still get to rank up faster if you hold the point, but you also draw the actual ranking characters there.
That unfortunately still keeps the issue that its very easily a famine type situation if one faction is outnumbered to the point where attacking the objective is pointless.

What if it functioned as an open world VIP game mode?

As in, there is a flag at the top of the tower, and when someone picks it up they become the 'VIP'. You would still need to control the flag in the tower when the tick happens, but you can move freely within and without the tower otherwise. The VIP can mount but their movement speed is capped at 130% total and cannot stealth or become completely invulnerable (DI, Ice Block, Divine Shield, Petri).

The defending faction holding the flag gains 1.5x honor from HKs while within the vicinity of the VIP. The attacking faction without the flag gains +100 honor per tick that occured while the flag was in the enemy's possession upon killing the VIP. The killer of the VIP automatically becomes the new VIP.

Additionally, the honor bonus is doubled for defenders while they are within the tower, and doubled for attackers if the VIP was killed outside the tower.
