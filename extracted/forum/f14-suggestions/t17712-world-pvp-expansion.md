# World PvP expansion

## Post 120761 by Artramo — 2025-02-23T20:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120761#p120761 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone !

The announced upcoming addition of new outdoor PvP objectives got me thinking about what could be interesting cross-faction PvP activities.

Basically it would be very similar to the « Silithyst Must Flow » world event in Silithus, you gather special items scattered randomly in the world that dismount you on pickup, but they would flag you for FreeForAll PvP instead (same as the Gurubashi arena).
The items you need to gather could be tied to professions (rare special spawns of illicit ores and herbs) or accessible to all like in the Silithus event (contraband packages maybe?).

You would then have to fight your way to a neutral settlement to turn in said item and earn reputation and tokens that you could use to buy cosmetic rewards, a trinket or a mount maybe with exalted reputation.
It could work well with the Steamwheedle factions as they have neutral bases scattered around the world, but could also be a fun way to give some life to the Bloodsail Buccaneers and Syndicate factions!   insidious_turtle

I think such a system could organically encourage new and interesting world PvP without requiring too extensive changes.

Truth be told, FreeForAll PvP or not, I’d love to see more of that kind of objectives, as it enables purely opt-in world PvP in unusual locations, thus creating more life. There could even be a periodical rotation of active zones to incentivize revisiting zones otherwise irrelevant at endgame.

I would love to have your opinions on this and exchange on the topic of new interesting world PvP objectives, or neutral factions   smiling_turtle_head

Artramo
