# Suggestion: Captain Silas' Letter to Stormwind Rewrite

## Post 146324 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-17T01:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146324#p146324 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

An issue that I've had with the letter sent by Captain Silas to General Marcus Jonathan is how personal and demanding that it is written. Being cut off from the rest of the world for somewhere between four-to-seven years, I found it rather odd that he'd be aware that Marcus Jonathan specifically is a general of the Stormwind Army and that he'd be a proper recipient for a letter requesting aid. He is also very blunt in his demands made to another independent human nation and makes grand, lofty promises that I doubt he'd even be able to deliver as he was even lower in the ranks of the Army of Lordaeron than his former leader, the late Grand Marshal Garithos.

To amend this, as well as giving a bit of love to the Alliance Remnants and tie into the activities taking place during Horde quests, I gave the letter a (significantly more lengthy, thus would span multiple pages) rewrite that takes inspiration from "The State of Lakeshire", a letter from Magistrate Solomon to Stormwind that similarly requests aid, giving Captain Silas' letter a more informative and neutral tone than the original while still making the request for aid from their sister nation. As one example of a questing reference I've added in, one of the Horde quests mentions a recent battle between the Remnants and the Forsaken at the nearby Corinth farmland, which a necromancer of the Cult of the Damned opportunistically uses to raise the dead soldiers and farmhands.

> To the commander of the armies of Stormwind,
>
>  Contained within these pages you will find an account of the state of the Nation of Lordaeron and the Remnants of the Alliance.
>
>  It is with great sadness that I must announce the death of Grand Marshal Othmar Garithos, leader of the Remnants of the Alliance of Lordaeron. His leadership was a beacon of hope in the dark times following the plaguing of Lordaeron, and we are lesser for his loss. His death came at the hands of the traitorous Banshee Queen, Sylvanas Windrunner, who betrayed us in our moment of triumph.
>
>  In his absence, I have taken command of the Remnants and of the surviving forces of the Army of Lordaeron. But the hour grows darker with every passing day. Food is increasingly scarce, and our numbers grow thinner with every day. If there is to be a hope for Lordaeron it is with the brave men and women who stand around me, holding their ground against the traitorous Forsaken and the monstrous Scourge both.
>
>  We find ourselves beset upon all sides by the Enemy; though we still make camp in the ruins of Glenwald Keep, the Corinth Farmstead has recently fallen to the Scourge. Many brave men and women were lost in the battle against the Forsaken there, and the insidious Cult of the Damned has seized the opportunity to defile the fallen.
>
>  To the south and to the west, the Forsaken infest the towns of Glenshire and Steepcliff Port and encroach upon our fortifications and the Garrick farmlands that keep us fed. The road east is no better, as a treacherous group of rogue wizards and their enforcers have seized the Uplands Gate from their camp in the Rogue Heights.
>
>  If the Enemy is to be vanquished in Lordaeron, it is imperative that the Kingdom of Stormwind send us aid. We require food, supplies and soldiers if we are to survive against the undead threat. Lordaeron and Stormwind have long stood together in the aftermath of the Second War. On behalf of Lordaeron and the Alliance, we beseech you - and let our nations stand together against the encroaching Darkness once more.
>
>  Signed,
>
>  Captain Theodore Silas,
>  Army of Lordaeron

I thought it'd give a bit more insight into the Remnants rather than Silas fairly arrogantly and bluntly making demands of Stormwind, which is its own nation over which he would have no sway; he's leader of the Remnants by virtue of being the highest-ranking officer left after Garithos died, so I don't think he's in much of a position to be making demands or promising rewards. Calling back to the bond between Stormwind and Lordaeron followed by the tragedy of being let down because of the issues with Varian being missing and Prestor manipulating events felt more meaningful.

There are three flavor elements that I've added in of my own creation that aren't cemented in any way and subject to any sort of change: Captain Silas' full name, the name of the gate into the Tirisfal Uplands, and the name of the Remnants' encampment. Captain Silas' signature is meant to be inspired by the documents you get from Marshal McBride when you leave Northshire, so I gave him a first name. It doesn't necessarily have to be "Theodore"; the purpose was to make the military signature more formal with his full name and rank. Similarly, the rewrite gives the Remnants' Camp and the gate into the Tirisfal Uplands names, the former due to having a whole barracks and with my assumption that the camp hadn't sprung up overnight; I picked the name "Glenwald Keep" to fit with the nearby town of Glenshire. As with the captain's first name, those are also subject to change if deemed fit.
