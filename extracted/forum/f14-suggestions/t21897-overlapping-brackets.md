# Overlapping brackets

## Post 151708 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-14T17:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151708#p151708 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, brackets are like 1-9, 10-19, 20-29, etc.
Players technically can join to bg as lvl 10 and face lvl 19 character, which has much higher power even if he is not twinking hard. Difference is in natural character stat gain, access to questing and dungeon gear, plus to higher rank of spells, plus wow math of level differences.

Even without twinks, playing as lvl 12 against lvl 19 feels like betrayal of own team. In current setting where you have no option rather than fighting against opponents who massively outlevel you. Also, huge amount of low level players discourage higher level players.

Proposal is: **there should be overlapping brackets like 6-15, 10-19, 16-25, 20-29. If player have alot of wins, then he gets into higher bracket. If player have few wins, then he gets into lower bracket**. If total amount of matches is low, then player gets in lowest bracket possible.
Imagine you are twinking as level 19. If you have high win count on character, you get into 16-25 bracket more often. If you have low win count, then you get into 10-19 one.
If you leveler of lvl 16, you get mostly into battlegrounds 10-19 - works as it is now.
If you leveler of lvl 12, you get mostly into battleground 6-15 - you will face either skilled/twinked characters of lvl 9 either other levelers like you of level 10-14.
If you are just good player of level 9, then you get more often to bracket 6-15.

Winrate should be counted for each battleground separately. High winrate of WSG should not affect getting into higher bracket of AB.

 **Quitting and getting deserter debuff should be counted as "win" in this math.** Otherwise twinks who doesn't need much from battlergound will just stomp players, carry flag to room and do "/afk" to stay at higher power level. Technically, I think it is good to count deserters separately, but in decision math, this number should be added to amount of wins.

My goals of proposing overlapping brackets were:
 1) allow people to "twink" but in the same time they should sometimes meet twinks of other brackets. Like lvl 19 twinks might sometime face lvl 25 twinks. In the same time, lvl 25 twinks might face lvl 29 ones. "There is always bigger fish"
 2) When players win in lower bracket, he get to higher bracket. When he lose in higher bracket, he gets back to lower. So you always have win-lose cycle so you not always bored with consecutive wins and not demotivated by consecutive losses.
 3) give regular players some time to not be overwhelmed instantly by enemies of lvl 19
And in general - to make as few changes as possible.

Wow is a game where your gear matters and I don't want ruin that.
There are tons of games where you have sophisticated skill and gear matchmaking.
There is no need to convert classic+ pvp to full modern game.

Gear matchmaking ruins feel of improvement.
You might be weak initially and lose often, but as you get more gear, you are becoming stronger with each additional item.
