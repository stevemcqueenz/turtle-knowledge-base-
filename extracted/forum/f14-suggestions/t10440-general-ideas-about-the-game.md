# General ideas about the game

## Post 68083 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-10-02T11:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=68083#p68083 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

This is a highly subjective post about my experiences and ideas regarding WOW.
I try to list things I personally think could be improved upon but I disregarded how hard a specific change could be.
Please note that all of my remarks are subjective, I never state anything on the list could make the game objectively better. I only aim to give a list about things which in my experience could make the game (even) more enjoyable.
The topics are not categorized, mentioned them in the order as they popped into my mind.

Travel

Flights. People's attention span got shortened in the recent years and most games use a cutscene and basically teleport you to the destination. In comparison the current WOW flight system seems to be very slow. I don't say we should get to places immediately but a 2x speed boost for flying mounts and a serious spawn time decrease for ships and zeppelins would help a lot. This way the intrinsic feeling of travelling is kept but those waiting times (sometimes up to 20 minutes) while waiting for dungeon members to arrive or waiting for the random guy at Thousand Needles for the Hinterlands elite quests could be significantly shortened, making a better game experience.

Mounts

I personally think riding is too expensive in WOW even with rep bonuses. In my opinion there are two kinds of players among those who reach level 60. Those who are in for the long ride, gathering tier gears, planning on Nax etc and those who reach lvl 60, enjoy it for a week or so then create a new char/try out a new game. The current prices make epic riding only possible for the first group. I don't say it is objectively bad but in my opinion this should be easier to achieve and by doing this a much higher percentage of the playerbase could experience faster travelling.

Auction House

I know there are addons but the game itself could add some additional filters to the AH. Going through 10 pages of linen cloth to find one with buyout and in a bulk can be a pain. Quantity filters and buyout filters would be a welcome addition.

Alliance dungeon

I feel it a bit asymmetrical that horde players can start dungeoning around level 14. It's a minor thing but a similar option at alliance side would be a welcome addition.

Fishing

I find fishing a fun way to spend some empty time but some additional rare loots would make the activity 100x more interesting. For example mounts with 1 in a million catchrate, epic items or chests filled with gold. I don't want to break the balance of the profession. Just making these loots possible and extremely rare (like the 1 ring) would make fishing feel like a treasure hunt.

Enchanters

If I'm in a city a guy always keeps yelling about his enhantments. Same with world chat, it is usually spammed with that. I think there should be a way (like a specific vendor in each town or a specific button in the map) which displays all available enchanters which are online and registered to this interface, alongside with their level, location and the list of enchantments they know. Would be a better interface and would make /yell innecessary.

Companion size

Most obtainable companions are so small they feel irrelevant. Making them slightly bigger and maybe adding some non-utility, non-combat stuff to them (like they dance, sing, you can hug them with animation) would make having a pet 10x more fun in my opinion. I know this requires a bunch of coding though.

AoE effects

It's totally personal but I think our AoEs only damaging enemies makes it too easy and less strategic. Making a blizzard f.ck your tank if positioned badly or a magma totem hurting everyone, not only the enemy mobs would make some classes more challenging and enjoyable in my opinion.

Donation item descriptions

I think clearer description is needed for some donation items. Bought a few and usually had some surprises. An example: bought my repair bot expecting to be able to repair anybody's stuff. Negative surprise, it was only for horde. Positive surprise, I also got a summonable vendor with a bunch of reagents. Paid for a totally different thing but was mostly happy with what I got. Same with goblin mind control device. If I knew that I can save main spec and off spec there and I can switch for free I would have bought it earlier. In my opinion in some cases lacking descriptions prevent people to buy into stuff because they don't know how much the item can do. (The time limit for using the mailbox is stupid in my opinion.)

Predictability of bosses

I know it's a huge topic but if some dungeon bosses had some alternative ways to fight, reacting to our team comp or damage output, that would feel most fights refreshing. For example using different abilities against a caster party and against a melee party. Or if the enemy hexes, this enemy could choose the hexing target based on our performance (Is there a top performing dps? Should the healer be hexed?), instead of random targets.

Transmog

I'd love the option to additionally modify the stuff we wear. Like if we transmog we could have the option to select the color of the stuff we wear. Another option would be to be able to transmog stuff into things we never had if we pay an additional (hefty) sum. This could prevent lvl 60s rushing ZF to get the crazy man wood head gear just for transmog for example.

PvP

This is a highly debated topic, I just give my personal take. I agree that 30% XP boost is a lot and it has to have a drawback but I would limit the ways people can attack eachother. My take is:
 1. You can only attack an enemy player if that player is at least at 70% HP. Would prevent backstabbing whily you're fighting mobs.
 2. You cannot attack a player in a group. 5 vs 1 is never fair.
 3. You can only attack players within your level range (let's say +-3 levels). Would prevent level 60s playing Rambo in Westfall or Durotar.

Looting

I think it would be awesome if there were a way to HR items at the beginning making it possible for only one person to loot said item. Would become handy to prevent ninja loot if people agreed on something in the beginning.
It would also be cool if there were an option to exclude players from rolling if they can't use a specific item. Would prevent a priest ninja looting a mail armor.

Friends' list

Again, my personal take. I like to add people from my guild and everyone I had fun playing with so that later if I LF a specific role at a dung I can just write to people I had a positive experience with in the past. Usually makes party creation much smoother. I might be too social but I always have the problem of having too many friends and the system blocks me from adding a new guy. I'd change the number of people you can add as a friend. Also, categorizing the friends (like close friend, alt, guild member, tanks etc) could be a welcome change.

Mob respawn timer

I think this could help at low-level areas and for big bosses with a huge respawn time. Now we see how much time is needed for a respawn but we don't know how much time is left. Can be annoying if your mob needs 30 minutes to respawn.

Inventory limit of certain items

I don't know if this can be addressed or there is always a way to play around this but limiting players by having a certain max amount from specific items could prevent monopoly in certain fields which can make the game seem unfair (let's say two guys decide the price of some really needed material and they agree to aim at the stars with them).

Player reliability meter

I don't know if this is generally desirable but I'd add some statistics to players like how many times did a player die in average in a dungeon last week or how many times a player left mid-dungeon last week. This way we could prevent bad surprises when we realize that the party's hunter always pulls the full room or that our tank has no idea how to tank. Some filters could be added to LFT this way, too. Let's say I'm willing to wait more time but only recommend guys who never quit mid dung last week.

LFT minor change

There should be an instant message option to every player in LFT. That way if you see that a tank is looking for a dungeon at your level range you can just insta message him if he wants to tank this other dungeon, too. Would save time. (I'd add 10 man Scholo and Strat in LFT, too).

LFT filtered search

I'd love to have the option to specify my search criteria at LFT. For example instead of LFing a tank having the option to specify that I LF a non-paladin, non-shaman tank from level 30. Would help to create specific team comps or to prevent an overconfident guy trying to tank a dungeon way too early.

LFT Elite quests

There could be an LFT form elite quests, making it easier to find people who f. ex. wanna do Ony attunement.

Survival profession change

I find it awersome that we have +1 secondary profession. But in reality you can make tents relatively early then it's mostly nothing. Fishing boat if you are really into fishing. The single most useful item can be aquired around profession lvl 55 and from there there's nothing that really matters. Never in my life have I seen people wearing murloc flippers. I'd add some real utility items to survival, especially at high survival level. F. ex. a combat rez with big CD, some potionlike ability boost, things like that.

Warlock pets

It's very specific but I think the pet abilities could be slightly changed. Currently there is voidee for leveling, imp for dungeons, succubus for dungeons from the moment you can DS and doggo for PVP. I generalized a bit too much but I think it is too one-sided how warlock pets are used at certain aspects of the game. Had an SM/DS and never thought about DSing a doggo. Why would I do that with a life tap ability? I think pets could be balanced a bit more to make a broader usage more viable.

PS: Please buff shamans. Please.

Note, everything I've written is just my opinion. Wanna give out a bunch of ideas. If even one of them is considered useful by the community, I think it can make the game more fun for everyone.
