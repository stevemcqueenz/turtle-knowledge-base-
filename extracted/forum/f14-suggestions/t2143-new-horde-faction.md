Not quite retcon, because as far as I am aware they didn't erase or replace any lore about Dreadmaul Hold in order to "confirm" it to be Rockard. Not exactly meticulously or methodically planned out either, as Blizzard is fond of taking fan speculation or things that they think sounds cool for content they haven't planned out and making it canon. For example, Gilneas being invaded by the worgen and joining the Alliance had been player speculation around the announcement of TBC and the announcement of new playable races before the announcement of the Draenei. Up until Cataclysm, there was basically no information about Gilneas and the developers had seemingly mostly forgotten about it, but in 2009 they evidently took inspiration from that fan speculation to make the Worgen the Alliance race for Cataclysm.

But to get back to my point, [this map](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/2/2f/Warcraft_I_-_Map_of_Azeroth.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20210307072149) of Azeroth (Stormwind) from Warcraft I shows Rockard being just southwest of Stonard. Though obviously, the later maps changed the scale drastically as well as the location of Blackrock Spire, several elements line up quite well and accurately enough to what is currently in WoW. Stormwind City (Stonewind Keep), Northshire Abbey, Goldshire and Darkshire (Grand Hamlet) are all in their approximative areas, Karazhan (Medivh's Tower) is roughly situated where it is, the Redridge Mountains are still to the west of Elwynn, Moonbrook is not too far north of the Deadmines, etc.

The main reason it was speculation that Dreadmaul Hold was Rockard was because Blizzard, in typical Blizzard fashion, never outright said what happened to the town until Cata; so many details from the RTS and RPG were omitted from WoW, and Blizzard remained intentionally incedibly vague whenever they discussed them. But even in original WoW, Dreadmaul Hold [consists of traditionally orcish buildings and decorations](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/f/f2/Dreadmaul_Hold.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20061214163839), simply inhabited by the Dreadmaul ogre clan. And whereas the orcish town of Rockard mysteriously vanished from the maps leaving not even ruins, the clearly orcish-designed Dreadmaul Hold inhabited by ogres apparently appeared out of thin air. Taking into account the flipping of the positioning of the Swamp of Sorrows and the Black Morass (Blasted Lands) from that WCI map to WoW, it was only natural that fans of the RTS assumed that Dreadmaul Hold was Rockard.

[Other locations that were presumed to be Rockard prior to Cataclysm](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Rockard?oldid=2253686#Speculation) were the two Lost One outposts in the Swamp of Sorrows - the Harborage and Fallow Sanctuary - as well as a set of orcish ruins to the south of Nethergarde Keep.
