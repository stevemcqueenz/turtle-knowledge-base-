To implement this change effectively, consider creating a new stacking debuff for each warlock that applies a percentage increase to damage dealt and a vulnerability increase for the target. For example, each warlock could apply a stacking debuff that grants 3% increased damage and 25 vulnerability, stacking up to three times (for a total of 9% damage and 75 vulnerability with three warlocks). This way, you simplify the mechanics by removing the individual curses (CoS, CoE, CoR) and provide a clear synergy that encourages players to work together. Be sure to communicate these changes to your raid group so everyone understands how to maximize their contributions!
