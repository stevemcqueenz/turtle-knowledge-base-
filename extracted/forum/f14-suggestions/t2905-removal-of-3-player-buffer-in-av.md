# removal of +3 player buffer in AV

## Post 18147 by Absolon — 2022-04-15T23:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18147#p18147 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

hi everyone,

from what i know, this +3 player buffer was introduced quite some time ago, when the server was less populated (especially the pvp scene) and to fight endless "ghost AVs" where nobody was able to win or end a game. several months ago, a change/fix was introduced, which now closes AVs when there are less than 9 players on any side.

so id suggest to remove this +3 buffer, since it doesnt seem necessary anymore (match would just timeout then)

thanks for reading

The +3 is actually just how AV works, due to being a 40v40. It rarely matters on the server, ofc, but there's no grand turtle design behind it.

Only issue I'll point out is that it's hard enough to get into AV to grind rep as it is without barring three people from playing. I'd suggest lowering the amount of players required on each side to get a game back to 5v5 as a tradeoff. Less people allowed in, but more chances to get in would be a fine compromise.
