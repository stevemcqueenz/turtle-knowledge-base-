<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120205#p120205 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello.

I was just wondering if there are any future plans for pvp on Nordanaar. Even though Nordanaar is rp/pve server, pvp is still big part (and tbh the most fun part) of the game if you like it or not. Since the Tel’Abim is not getting enough attention, isn’t time to start growing pvp community on Nordanaar? Becouse ppl will naturally choose the high pop server. After the class changes it feels like all of the work goes into pve and pvp is lacking some love and attention.

My ideas are:
 - Eye of the Storm battleground
 - higher rewards on Sunnyglade Valley, so we are not stuck in 2 bgs for eternity
 - adequate pvp sets (or if possible whole tbc honor system)
 - daily pvp quests and rewards

P.S. Big thanks to devs, you make this wonderful game still fun and playable.
