<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25509#p25509 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the Turtle WoW developers trying new mechanics wouldn't a testing realm/server be practical and useful?

The poll for whether or not to remove world buffs is being hotly debated. I really don't think anyone will change their minds about their opinion for or against the change with out actually trying out the change to see how the change will effect the game play under active play circumstances.

So why not have a test server with planned changes to settle the debate? It could prevent a lot of drama and discourse when a change is made (or officially not made) on the main server.
