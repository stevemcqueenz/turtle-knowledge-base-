I decided to write down a different overview again, changed some suggestions and I'll be going through race by race.
I'd like to give suggestions to balance out the PVE/PVP viability of each race so each has something in both categories going for it without changing the overall idea/meta of the existing racials up to much:

Human:   scared_turtle_head

Humans have a lot more going for them PVE-wise than PVP-wise: diplomacy, spirit bonus (spirit doesn't really come into play in PVP), swords and maces bonus.
Perception can be used in PVP more than in PVE and is of limited use - you basically have to know there are stealthers nearby.
In general, the melee classes profit more from being human as they may all draw benefit from the weapon skill bonus (which is rather big in PVE) , casters from other races offer more (gnome mage, gnome warlock, dwarf priest)
 --> Suggestion:
- Change spirit bonus into mana/5sec. based on your level , e.g. 1/5 of your level or lower= 12 mana/sec at level 60, this way its useful in both PVP and PVE and also useful for human warlock (who doesn't profit really from spirit)

- Give perception a passive stealth detection component (e.g. + 1 or 2 effective levels) and also keep the active, this way you have a better chance to see stealthers and react accordingly, optional is to add some sort of caster buff to the perception active skill (+ spell crit damage/spell penetration for the duration)

- Improve Human Priest Feedback (perhaps give it a chance to lock out the attacker out of the used magic school) or just increase the damage

Night Elf   [mad_turtle_head

Shadowmeld has its uses in PVP and PVE (avoiding patrols), dodge bonus is nice but small (and in PVE only really consequential if you tank), Wisp is just a gimmick basically, Nature Resistance bonus is also very small and hardly noticeable.
Night elf Rogue, one of the most iconic race/class combos is sadly the worst class/race combo for night elf as shadowmeld isnt really beneficial for rogue. Night Elves lack in the PVE department.
 --Suggestion:
- Change Shadowmeld for Rogue to either be a usable in combat every 10 minutes OR severely increase stealth level when shadowmelded (or stealthed) for them (in PVP opponent would have to stand on top of you to see you but, the stealth level bonus only applies while standing still, the latter could also apply to druid cat forms prowl)

- Improve the Night Elf Priest racials, especially Elunes Grace is lackluster, Starshards should also be useful for shadow priests (see opening post)

- Give Night Elves one or two weapon specializations for PVE viability (dagger/bow/polearm seem fitting) or give them a low permanent attack speed/cast speed bonus (1-2%)

Dwarf  unhappy_turtle_head

Stoneform has good uses in both PVP and PVE, treasure finding is obsolete with any kind of tracking-addons, resistance again is very small, gun skillbonus is only really beneficial to hunter (and even here not very much, as most good weapons are bow/xbows). Dwarf priest is considered BIS for PVP/PVE on alliance. Overall Dwarves are a bit lacking PVE-wise.
 --> Suggestion:
- Give Dwarves one or two weapon skill bonuses (axe and mace seem fitting), allow rogues to use axes

- Give ALL priests Fear ward and dwarf priest the TBC racial, Fear Ward with higher CD so no buffing up entire groups up with it effortlessly

Gnome  dead_turtle_head

Gnomes are a very good choice for casters on alliance -intelligence bonus and escape artist - are both quite strong for both PVP and PVE. Overall, gnome here is more or less the opposite of human (PVP and caster-centric vs. PVE and melee-centric). Thus they lack a bit on the PVE side.
 --Suggestion:
- Give Gnomes one or more  weapon skill bonuses (dagger, crossbow, thrown seem fitting) and/or give them a higher chance to block with a shield (1 or 2 %, they should have an easier time hiding behind a shield after all)

## Post 5768 by Heroclastus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-04-24T20:09:00Z

Orc   scared_turtle_head

Orcs are quite the PVP kings and have powerful bonuses in general. Hardiness alone is very strong and for many classes alone enough reason to pick orc for PVP, but can be a death sentence in PVE if you're the only one left standing without a stun in a boss fight. Melees profit a lot more here from axe spec and as Blood Fury only affects Melee AP. Commands Pet damage increase is nice but the pet-orientated specs for warlock/hunter arent the best for advanced PVE/PVP. Overall, they are too strong for PVP and a bit lacking in PVE.
 -->Suggestion:

- Lower hardiness stun resist chance to 15% or make it generally reduce the time you are stunned by 25% or more (prefer the latter))

- Make Blood Fury also affect Spell Power for Warlock and Shaman and Ranged Attack Power for Hunter, perhaps also change the healing debuff associated with it to 15 seconds (same as the AP buff) to compensate for hardiness nerf,
maybe let the Buff scale not with BASE BUT CURRENT attack/spell power, in its curent state it loses its usefulness as raid/gear progresses (no other racial does that) and the  - 50 % healing is quite a hefty price to pay for such a low gain

- Allow rogues to wield axes so orc rogue can profit from the axe spec

- Possibly add a weapon spec (polearm, sword, mace seem appopriate)

Troll  smiling_turtle

Trolls have berserking going for them which is good for PVE and PVP but quite risky in PVP. Troll Regeneration is lackluster and hardly noticeable.  Beast Slaying has some uses, but most bosses aren't beasts, in PVP it works against hunter pets and shifted druids. Bow spec is only good for hunter, throwing spec is utter garbage. Overall they are good for PVE but lacking in PVP. Also, female trolls NEVER blink (spooky   hiding_smth_turtle_head  )
 --> Suggestion:

- Buff Troll Regeneration to actually make an impact (also, if possible one could have it work at factor 2x or 3x in BGs to really make a difference in longer fights), alternatively make berserking increase regeneration the more you're hurt

- Reduce the time of slow effects on you (if possible by 15% -25%) or give an increased chance to resist them (15%)

- Add one or several weapon skill specs (axe, polearm, dagger, mace seems appopriate)

- Solution to balance PVE/PVP utility of Berserking/Troll Regeneration: Make Troll Regeneration generally regenerate at 20% speed in combat, Berserking: make Regeneration work at factor 2 or up to 5 for the duration (so 40-100 % combat regeneration while Berserking is active), give stackable attack/cast speed buff instead of the speed buff now in-place(max 5 stacks, 5 % speed per stack, gain a stack when getting attacked (100% chance) or when casting a spell (50% chance). This makes is useful as a combined defensive/offensive CD and buffs it for PVP where you will get attacked often and nerfs it slightly for PVE (25% speed max, you have to build it up). Should probably also up the time it is active as 10 seconds are too short if the buff builds up

- remove throwing specialization, it's bad
Tauren  wary_turtle_head

Tauren have the 'unfortunate' place to be in the middle ground of pvp/pve viability for some classes e. g. orcs are chosen over them for a pvp hunter, troll if going for pve. Plainsrunning has some overlap, as some classes already have a speed increase ability (hunter aotc, shaman ghost wolf, druid travel Form). War stomp has good uses in pve/pvp, stamina bonus is also good for both. Overall, they are quite balanced, perhaps a little lacking pve wise for some classes (especially ranged/caster)
 --> Suggestion:

- give Tauren one or several weapon specs (guns, maces, polearms, crossbows, axes seem fitting), casters could get a minor bonus to healing done/nature damage done (natures warden or some such, 1-2%)

- grant a small bonus to movement speed (5%) for ghost wolf, hunter aotc/(aotp perhaps also) , travel Form once Plainsrunning is obtained, and/or perhaps make plainsrunning work for travelform and ghost wolf out of combat (gradual speed increase, not in BGs)

- minor: make war stomp usable in druid bear form

Undead  angry_turtle

Undeads wotf is very strong for pvp and a deciding factor for many people to not play warlock/priest on alliance pvp, resistance is small and hardly noticeable, underwater breathing has uses in both pvp/pve, cannibalism is good (but ultimately you could just drink /eat/bandage).  Undead warrior is a notable inferior choice since wotf overlaps with the warriors inherent fear removal. Overall, they are very good for pvp and lacking pve-wise.
 --> Suggestion :

- give undead one or several weapon specs (sword, axe, mace seem appropriate) and/or give them a passive 1% bonus to melee and spell crit chance (anatomical knowledge)

- give ALL priests fear ward, evening out a little the predominance of warlocks/priests on horde, since both sides would have more consistent means of fear removal/protection. Wotf would be still useful as you wouldn't be relying on priests

- lower the cannibalism CD (30 seconds or 1 minute) so you can make use of it more frequently, possibly add ooc restriction (not sure if this is already the case). Possibly make it restore mana too for casters.

General:

As you can see I suggested adding weapon specs to all races. Another option would be to remove ALL weapon specs from the races and add general combat related buffs to all of them, e.g.the ones I already suggested for Night Elf 1-2% haste, undead crit, Tauren nature damage/healing bonus etc. Weapon specialization racials tend to limit a players weapon choices, removing them IMHO would be a viable option.
Ideas:
 - 1-2 % attack/cast speed (Night Elf, Swiftness)
 - 1-2 % physical/nature damage/healing bonus (Tauren, Natural Force)
 - 1 % crit bonus, possibly 2-5 % crit damage bonus (Undead, Anatomical Knowledge)
 - 1 % crit bonus, possibly 1 % damage bonus (Orc, Savageness)
 - 1 % hit bonus, possibly 2-5% crit damage bonus (Human, Discipline)
