# Suggestion: Stormwrought Descent Gate Levers

## Post 151132 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-10T13:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151132#p151132 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Similar as to how Stratholme's undead side has a lever on the inside of the Eastwall Gate (side entrance, outside the dungeon), I believe that the side entrance to Stormwrought Ruins that leads into the Descent should have a lever outside the dungeon. As it is on the inside of the gate, this wouldn't invalidate lockpicking or having the Key to Stormwrought Castle, in the same way that the Stratholme side entrance lever doesn't invalidate lockpicking or the Key to the City.
