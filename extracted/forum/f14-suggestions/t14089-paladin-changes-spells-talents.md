Im making this suggestion in order to share my views on the changes that should be done to the paladin class.

 **-===== NEW RESOURCE "HOLY POWER" =====-**

Basically functioning like rage :

Holy Power
Paladin abilities require Holy Power. Holy Power is gained when you cause damage, take damage or heal other players.

By changing some abilities to use Holy Power instead of Mana, it will give more flexibility to the class to use all its toolkit.

 **-===== BASELINE SPELLS CHANGES =====-**

 **-===== SEALS =====-**

Seals now costs 5 Holy Power.

 1. Seal of the Crusader(Rank 1)

Seal of the Crusader
 5 Holy Power
Instant
Fills the Paladin with the spirit of a crusader for 30 sec, granting 31 melee attack power. The paladin also attacks 60% faster, but deals 30% less damage with each attack. Only one seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time.

Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy for 30 sec, increasing the chance to being hit by 1%.

 2. Seal of Fury (New)

Seal of Fury
 5 Holy Power
Instant
Fills the Paladin with divine fury for 30 sec, allowing melee attacks to cause additional threat. Only one seal can be active on the paladin at any one time.

Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy for 30 sec, increasing the time betwen its attacks by 10%.

 3. Seal of Justice

Seal of Justice
 5 Holy Power
Instant
Fills the Paladin with the spirit of justice for 30 sec, giving each melee attack a chanceto stun for 2 sec. Only one seal can be active on the paladin at any one time.

Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy for 10 sec, preventing him from fleeing.

 4. Seal of Light

Seal of Light
 5 Holy Power
Instant
Fills the Paladin with divine light for 30 sec, healing the paladin for 15% of the damage dealt. Only one seal can be active on the paladin at any one time.

Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy for 30 sec, granting melee attacks made against the judged enemy a chance of healing the attacker for 2% of its maximum health.

// Learned at lvl 2 //

 5. Seal of Righteousness

Seal of Righteousness
 5 Holy Power
Instant
Fills the Paladin with holy spirit for 30 sec, granting 30 attack power and increasing Holy damage from spells and effects by up to20 when fighting undead. Only one seal can be active on the paladin at any one time.

Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an undead target for 30 sec, increasing the damage taken by 5%.

// lvl 32 (Rank 2) ---> 115 AP and 115 SP bonus against undead //
// lvl 52 (Rank 3) ---> 180 AP and 180 SP bonus against undead //

 6. Seal of Wisdom

Seal of Wisdom
 5 Holy Power
Instant
Fills the Paladin with divine wisdom for 30 sec, giving each melee attack a chance to restore 3% of paladin's maximum mana. Only one seal can be active on the paladin at any one time.

Well, differences of opinion always exist and although i don't agree with all of your suggestions, some are quite interesting. However, we must always have in mind that turtle is first and foremost a vanilla+ experience. If all theses changes were to be implemented, especially the holy power resource, it would be the same as when Blizzard launched new expansions of the game. Yes, it can be loads of fun to play with new mechanics and all, but it would steer too far from the main goal of playing in a vanilla server.

Even the leaked class changes that have been circulating are quite worrying, for that matter.

That does not mean that your suggestions are useless as they can intrigue and spark discussion among the community, and might lead to some new toys we can play with in the future. Personally, i don't mind the idea of more radical changes in each class being attached to specific sets or items in the end game, preserving the core leveling experience of vanilla, but giving something for those that reach 60 to play with, so that it doesn't become too stale.

Just no

Why change paladin into warrior-lite with a similar rage mechanic that locks the utility and support features of the class behind behind this 'holy power''?
Not getting into the specifics, but balancing the seal system and blessings behind this 'holy power' I think just doesn't work.

Mana as a resource for paladins works just fine, as it's finite and can run out if you're not careful. Wasting your mana on buffs, improper heals or exhausting it too much in DPS should be a welcome challenge and adds value to consumeables such as tea with sugar, major mana potion, mageblood etc (although a case could be argued that endgame holy paladins don't run into these issues due to illumination).

Trying to attach a rage mechanic to paladins just means that seals are just about landing x heal, taking x damage or dealing x damage which just really unbalances the class in both PvE and PvP.

I understand what you're trying to resolve here; the repetitive and boring nature of holy healing and instead adding a system of 'holy power' to reward more proactive gameplay but I think adding an entire new resource to the class shouldn't be considered.

I think that you if you hate the mana management of the class so much, you should go try out warriors or even rogues with their energy.
