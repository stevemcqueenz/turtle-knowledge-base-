# Balancing classes for pvp

## Post 25297 by Culture — 2022-09-17T16:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25297#p25297 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As everyone knows, Vanilla is kind of a rock, paper, scissors when it comes to pvp. Each class has its counters and its easy matchups. I would like to open a discussion about what could be done to balance all this. The goal is to add abilities/talent that helps against our counters, without modifying the other matchups.

As I'm playing mage I will start with this class, and I hope that everyone will add their ideas for their own class.

Counters :
In my opinion the hardest fights as a mage are : affli warlock, shadow priest and balance/resto druid (warlock being the hardest). A simple way to balance all those matchups could be to add Arcane, Shadow and Nature Ward to the mages abilities, that work exactly like the Frost and Fire Ward. The main point is that it doesn't give any advantage against other classes or in PVE.

Already balanced : shaman, hunter and rogue matchups seem pretty fair to me (with ele shaman being very tight, but still acceptable). The nature ward could be less powerful than the others, in order to maintain the balance with shaman and hunter.

Easy matchups : warrior. Maybe the warrior could have a spell reflect on cooldown (like it was added in BC), or something like a "Sword throw" to interrupt the mage at distance.

As I'm playing Alliance I don't know about fighting paladins, so I let you complete me (or contradict me if you don't agree :p ). But don't get me wrong, I know mages are already powerful in general, that's why I only propose subtle changes.

I will add your ideas to this post, in hope that we can build something really balanced :)
