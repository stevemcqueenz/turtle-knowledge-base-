# [Mage] New Talent - Arcane Armaments

## Post 84511 by Danriviera — 2024-01-13T12:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84511#p84511 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been playing WoW Turtle for about a month or so now and fell in love with the design the devs take the game, specially with the new lore around High Elves!

One thing I've ever wanted since the start of WoW was something akin to the Spellbreaker unit from Warcraft 3, I always thought them to be really awesome, and were never explored more than just guards or heritage armor in retail.
Thinking about this while also paying attention to the new spells shamans received from each race, which I also thought was a incredible idea and in-line with the units from W3, came to me this idea:

Change the **Wand Specialization** talent from the second row of the arcane tree to **Arcane Armaments**:

 2 Ranks
First rank - Increase your damage with Wands by 15% (bring 2% from the second rank to the first)
Second rank - Increase your damage with Wands by 25% and allows you to use *_WEAPON CLASS_*

Each race would have a unique weapon class for this talent, bringing a unique flavor for each race of mages:
 **Humans** - 2-Handed Swords
 **Dwarfs** - 2-Handed Maces
 **Gnomes** - 1-Handed Maces
 **High Elves** - Shields (Spellbreakers, kinda, yay!)
 **Orcs** - 2-Handed Axes
 **Trolls** - 1-Handed Axes
 **Forsaken** - Polearms
 **Goblins** - Fist Weapons (Or guns, but giving a ranged weapon with a talent which improve wands is kinda too clunky, even for vanilla/classic)

The only concern I have with this idea is somehow a broken build emerges from the interaction of an specific weapon with the mage spells, which I can't say for certain because I'm not familiar with end-game weapons from classic, neither new weapons the Turtle team developed.

What do you guys think? Would love to hear suggestions to improve the idea!

Much love from Brazil <3
