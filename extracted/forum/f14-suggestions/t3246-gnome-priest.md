To be fair, there are the [Holdout Medics](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Holdout_Medic) in the Clean Zone in Gnomeregan that have existed since the beginning of the game and use priest spells (Heal, Renew and Power Word: Fortitude), so it's not entirely out of left field. Given their abilities and title, it does seem that gnomes look to their healers as doctors, medics and other people in the field of medicine, but it doesn't seem unreasonable that they have faith of some sort in addition to their scientific beliefs.

As a hypothetical idea with that concept, some could even maybe try to mix the two together to create machines that can use the Light as a source of power or that channel it in ways similar to natural magical artifacts, with their faith in their religion and their scientific knowledge making them believe that it could work.
