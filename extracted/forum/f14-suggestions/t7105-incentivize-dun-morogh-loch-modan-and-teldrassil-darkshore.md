## Post 45197 by Bubblebutts — 2023-05-09T19:35:00Z

Elwynn Forest and Westfall are overpopulated. This topic surely already has a few mentions, so I'll keep it brief.

I attribute this phenomenon to a few things:
- Humans are overpowered for melee, so most people playing melee choose it.

- Tents

- There is an 8-slot bag quest at level 6 in Elwynn

- The retired flight master's gryphon makes travel around that area incredibly fast

- Deadmines
There's probably more ways in which the human starter zones are advantageous, but I think this is sufficient for my argument.

 **Suggestion: give starting players more incentives to play in other starting zones.**
