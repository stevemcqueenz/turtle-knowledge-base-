I always thought...why not give all the races the "Common" language, and give the humans a new one and make it the new default language for all Alliance races? Plus, for make all the languages work as intended and if the players desire to talk with others and make the opossite faction don't understand what they say select the language that the other race don't talk.

This could lead to a whole new way to communicate between races and Factions, even when the Cross-faction is enabled.

How?
Easy:

The rest of the race languages: Let you talk only with players of your same race.
