I've been leveling a mage lately and I reckon it should have a Flame Armor. After all it gets an Arcane and a Frost one, so why not fire?

It should be distinct and give different bonuses compared to the other 2. Here's my idea (numbers are omitted for I am not intimately familiar with the maths of balancing):

Flame Armor (R1)
 - Increase Fire Resistance by 5
 - Enemies that hit you take XX Fire damage

I'd imagine both the resistance and the damage to increase as we increase in rank, like the other armors.
