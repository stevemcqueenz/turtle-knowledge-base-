# New Primary Profession and Gathering Progession> Bowyer / Fletching

## Post 104055 by Maaliss — 2024-10-21T07:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104055#p104055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've wondered for a long time why we don't have a specific Bowyer profession and fletching gathering profession.

Would be interesting to see some craftable bows, crossbows, styles of bows from shortbows, composite bows, longbow all with different playstyles such as reducing the minimum range distance, max range, range accuracy and maybe even dual-wield handcrossbows with low damage but high attack speed (much like daggers).

Also, with fetching, have the profession able to gather differing feathers, scales for arrow tips, thorns for tips etc. Perhaps arrows with specific elemental damage enhancers etc.

Would be an interesting addition if possible/workable.
