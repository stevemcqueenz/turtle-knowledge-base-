# Attach the Vengeance buff to Cannibalize

## Post 104089 by Glaive (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-10-21T10:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104089#p104089 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At the time I'm writing this the big change to WotF has been reverted so it is usable under CC but has a longer CD and shorter duration.  I'm fine with however this pans out honestly, but I'm glad to see this changed back because I don't think it would have been very usable the way it was.

The purpose of this post is to address Vengeance.  As of changing WotF back, the damage increase from Vengeance has been changed to not affect players.  This feels a little iffy to me.  My personal feeling is that if a racial ability is too powerful to be included in pvp then it's just too powerful.  I dislike the idea of cherry-picking which things will work in pvp and which won't, because now you've got the human's perception 2% crit.  Are you going to make that not count in pvp?  I do not think this goes in a good direction.

My suggestion would be to attach the Vengeance buff to Cannibalize in a similar way that you did the crit buff to perception.  Cannibalize has always been one of my favorite racials.  What if after cannibalizing, the forsaken gain that 2% bonus to damage for a period of time?  That would allow you to leave the dmg buff in pvp.  I'm just spit-ballin' here, but I think it would be more in flavor, easier to balance than a constant effect and just all-around cool.

Anyway, thanks for reading and keep up the good work.  I'm super excited for this patch to go live.

~Glaive
