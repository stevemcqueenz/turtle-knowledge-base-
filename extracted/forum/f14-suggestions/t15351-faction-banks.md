# Faction Banks

## Post 104839 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-25T20:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104839#p104839 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I suggest adding new faction banks that work on top of current universal bank and that could store some more slots for those who already have multiple 36-slot bags and can't store everything they want. The idea of faction bank is that you get access to bank of each faction that works similar as current bank but they aren't shared - if you visit bank in Stormwind, there will be items stored specifically in Stormwind and nowhere else, you can't access it through Booty Bay bank. Each city shall have its own bank. If you play in Horde, you can visit bank in Undercity, Orgrimmar and Thunder Bluff.
You could unlock more slots in faction bank by raising your reputation level. For example you unlock all slots of Undercity bank if you are Exalted with the Forsaken. Each level of reputation could guarantee a few more slots.

It would revitalize "dead cities", make reputation with some factions relevant and satisfy people who like to hoard a lot of things but they don't have much space now even with 36-slot bags. I believe it would be possible to implement in new 2.0 client.
