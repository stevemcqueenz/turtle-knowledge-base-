Introducing MARRIAGE LICENSES in capital cities near you! Visit your local priest or shaman trainers in game to pay with gold, or in the donation shop to pay with donation tokens to marry the love of your life TODAY![/j]

 1. A [Guild Charter] is an in-game item purchased from a Guildmaster NPC that can be presented to other players for signatures to start a new guild. In this iteration, it shall have the name [Marriage License] and can be purchased from end game priest & shaman trainers in major cities. This is both for ease of implementation, and lore for many cultures in WoW surrounding marriages.

 2. The player who has purchased the [Marriage License] from either the priest trainer in game for in game gold currency or Donation Shop for irl currency will then require the signature of the person they plan to marry. They sign this same as a guild charter. To get them to sign, click on your charter (which needs to be in your backpack) with the person selected and click on 'Request Signature'.
 3. Return this to the Priest or Shaman Trainer in question, preferably with the person you intend to marry you. Upon completion, the player which purchased the Marriage License shall receive two gold rings with no stats, and an optional title. “Spouse of [OtherCharacterNameHere]” for both. By keeping this gender neutral, we make it easier to implement. An alternative to this could be “The Beloved” in case dynamic titles cannot be made.
 4. Marriage Licenses could be added to the Love is In the Air loot table from quest turn in gacha boxes.
 5. To divorce, instead of /gbreak it would be /annulment.

This would be a cute way for roleplayers and couples in the game to celebrate their love stories, as well as a way to aid more in the RP community. Players could of course host their wedding parties, but this would simply be an added bonus in game to assist with oversight and management now that the server has grown so large.

A really sweet bonus (like the guild tabard) if you would want to charge a lot for it in the donation store could be that the rings teleport you to the other player (so long as they are not within a locked or full instance).

Marriages of course can be cross faction, just as guilds and parties can be. Make love, not Warcraft.
