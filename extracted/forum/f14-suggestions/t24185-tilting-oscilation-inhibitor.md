Can someone please explain the design rationale behind this quest to me? Why put a quest in a level 20-30 zone that requires you to gather two Fused Wiring, which run about 4g each, at a time when people are still saving for their riding skill? Who thought that this was an appropriate ingredient to ask for? This is honestly so incredibly ridiculous that it blows my mind. Can we just replace these with Bronze Frameworks or something? It''s not even like there is an especially good reward for this quest either.

Yes, I know that Fused Wiring can drop from Target Dummies, but at a very low drop chance. You would have to craft 20 Target Dummies, and get them all destroyed, for a decent chance at 2 Fused Wiring. Not to mention that not everyone is an engineer. There is a reason that these are so expensive.

How is this a reasonable requirement for a level 24 quest?
