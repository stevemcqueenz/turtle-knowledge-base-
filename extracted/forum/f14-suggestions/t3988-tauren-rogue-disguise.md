I was thinking about rogue disugises and one idea popped into my head, tauren rogues. Since a lot of players like myself would love to play as a tauren rogue, the disuguise would be a perfect tool that allows us to do that. The npc for this disguise could be something like a Suspicious Grimtotem that sells the recipe and he could reside somewhere in the Darkcloud Pinnacle or inside the Grimtotem settlements in Stonetalon or Thousand Needles. Disguises are a great way to play out the fantasises of becoming something interesting and I'm sure that a lot of people would love this. Tell me what you think down in the comments.
