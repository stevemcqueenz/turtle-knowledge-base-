# Neutral factions - Earthen Ring and Remastered Ravenholdt

## Post 15081 by Zarinye — 2021-12-31T18:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15081#p15081 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Both factions are canon in original Classic, and I think there is a lot of potential to use them either as reputation factions or as flavored high-leveled quests.

In Classic, the Earthen Ring are represented by only a few NPCs, such as Je'neu Sancrea in Ashenvale. In the lore however, they are an ancient order which takes care of imbalances in nature and has an important role in the politics of the Horde. For example, they are responsible for letting the Forsaken join the Horde.

Lorewise, Ravenholdt is supposed to be a secretive order of master assassins and thieves. Ingame however, they are represented by their craving of **a metric ton** of unlocked junkboxes, and even then there's no real reason to painstakingly grind these boxes - the only reward is that their guards will have more respectful dialogue. **Yes, that is really it.**

For the Earthen Ring, I imagine that they would want you to restore the balance of the Elements in Azeroth by culling rogue elementals, killing Twilight's Hammer cultists and gathering reagents. Because of the lack of other content in the zone, I could imagine them setting up their base in Azshara. Shaman- and elemental-coded gear and perhaps a wolf mount could be fitting reputation rewards.

For Ravenholdt, they could possibly give you assassination contracts, perhaps with both PvE and PvP elements in remote corners of Azeroth. As they are a secretive order, their objectives shouldn't be known by the player, maybe even having the player do morally questionable acts to complete quests. For rewards, maybe one could get a special horse mount and more subtle yet good-looking gear (AKA "regular" proportions, no big spikes or bright colors).
