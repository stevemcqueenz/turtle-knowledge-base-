# Vellums for Enchanters to sell their wares in the Market.

## Post 110267 by Nandomaubr87 — 2024-11-28T00:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110267#p110267 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

VELINES (WHICH COULD BE MADE BY A LEATHER WORKER) SO ENCHANTERS CAN SELL THEIR CHARMS ON THE MARKET.

Vellum can be crafted by Leather Workes.

The Enchanter can enchant them to level up their profession, and sell them on the market just like other professions.

This will make the profession more friendly, because today, for an enchanter to sell his products, he needs to advertise in "Chat", negotiate a price, which makes profit unfeasible for those who play for a short time, as is my case, as I prefer to spend my time game doing dungeons and pvp, and not staying in the city offering enchants.

In fact, this is the reason why my friends and I hardly take up this profession.

## Post 110284 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-28T04:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110284#p110284 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Enchanting "special" item is too complex in my opinion. Enchanters could directly create scrolls. Like Jewelcrafters/LW/BS can directly create items which can be used to enchant gear.
It will be much easier to implement than WotLK "vellum" system.

Next. Noone interested in low level enchants. Right now you can enchant your lvl 1 gear with endgame stuff. The only type of person interested in enchanting gear are twinks. The only "low" level enchant they would pick is Minor Speed on boots.
If there is no demand, then price for those enchants will go down. So you won't get any profit anyway. One of the fixes for this is to create demand for low level enchants among twinks by adding level requirement to all enchants [Nerf high level enchants on low level gear](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4499)

If you allow to sell enchants on ah, then price of leveling enchants will quickly drop below cost of materials. Like it is now with items you craft to level any other crafting profession.
While it is not very bad outcome overall, I don't think that you expect it.

Pergament or vellums or whatever you wanna call it to be able to trade enchants makes sense if you look at the convinience it offers individual players.

But if you look past the part of your brain that wants the easy and quick solution, you will realize that enchanting is actually one of those professions in the game that works.
Because people want enchants and most of them will just seek out another player to get them.

By having the enchants available in the auction house, not only have you lost a organic bit of player interaction in an MMO, you are absolutely bottoming out the profit margin on auctions. Your well earned 20g tip for a centaur reputation enchant just got redistribited to 20x 1 gold tipps as an enchanter.

You wont be playing the game, you'll run back between AH and Postbox instead (if you are oneof the few deciding to compete on AH prices)
