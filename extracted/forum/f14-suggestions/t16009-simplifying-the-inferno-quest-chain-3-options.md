# [✅] Simplifying the INFERNO Quest Chain - 3 Options

## Post 109056 by Unchallenged (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-20T21:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109056#p109056 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey all,
I suggest making the INFERNO chain/option more approachable for players who have already gone through it a few times.
Or
Remove it all together, and have players stay Inferno 60 until they talk to NPC and opt-out to SC at lv 60.

For those who agree, please give this post a "+" so we can show support for the idea!

Current Challenge:
The current INFERNO chain involves a lot of back-and-forth between dungeons and mailboxes, which can feel repetitive after completing it for the first, second, or third time haha.

Here's the current flow for reference:
Letter -> Deadwind Mailbox (Quest 1)
Tanaris -> Mara -> Deadwind Mailbox (Quest 2)
LBRS -> Deadwind Mailbox (Quest 3)
Scholo -> Deadwind Mailbox (Quest 4)
DMW -> Deadwind Mailbox (Quest 5)
HFQ/BRD -> Deadwind Mailbox (Quest 6)
UBRS -> Deadwind Mailbox (Quest 7)

This chain is immersive and engaging for first-time players, but for those of us on our 3rd or 4th Inferno character, it starts to feel more like a grind due to the reliance on others' schedules, time zones, group composition, etc.

Suggestion:
Streamline INFERNO with optional alternatives:

Option A: At 60 you remain Hardcore. Should you wish to opt out of HC, you can once again talk to Mysterious Stranger to Become Softcore.

 - This would help ensure those that hit "Immortal" mid-dungeon/raid will maintain their awareness until they have visited the mysterious stranger to opt out of Hardcore.

Option B: Complete UBRS once to progress directly to Inferno.

Option C: Reduce it to a condensed version where you collect all required items from all dungeons in a single quest.

This would preserve the narrative while offering convenience for veteran players who’ve already experienced the full chain.

 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What do you all think?

Would love to hear feedback or suggestions to refine this idea further!
