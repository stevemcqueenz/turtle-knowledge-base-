People do need a mechanism improving loot distribution, such as correcting mistakenly assigned items. I once rolled high enough for Shard of the Fallen Star during an AQ40 run, but the loot master in the raid distributed it to another player. The problem is that the gear is dropped by non-boss mobs so it can't be exchanged.

What this thread mainly proposes is a kind of punishing function aiming to correcting wrong distributions and ill behaviours such as pretending to hard reserve/need gears and leaving after a certain boss, making the raid leader take the responsibility with the current loot system. Pretty constructive to improving most players' experience.

GMs' burden only lessens with this function implemented. By the way, a character restoring function on the character list is also necessary because people may suffer from drug's psychedelic effect and make mistakes.
