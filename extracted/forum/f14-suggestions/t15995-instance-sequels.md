# Instance Sequels

## Post 108989 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-20T16:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108989#p108989 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it would be fun if certain dungeon instances from leveling had a 10-man raid counterpart at max level

For example, Zul-Farak could be repurposed as a 10-man where the city has been overrun by Qiraji
Gilnaes City could have a 10-man at max level where the city has been overrun by Worgen.

I also think some raids could have 5-man sequels, so there could be a Dungeon counterpart to Molten Core where you fight the remnants after Ragnaros defeat.  You wouldn't have access to the dungeon variant until you've killed Ragnaros.

There could also be a BWL sequel where you fight dark iron dwarves that have claimed the instance after blackwing's defeat.

These would add some extra content to the game without having to craft entirely new instances from scratch, and would deepen the story that already exists.
