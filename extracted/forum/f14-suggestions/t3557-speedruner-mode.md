I had this little idea of having a Glyph of Speedruning.

It`s pretty simple. You start a new character, get the glyph (xp must be 0) and the play timer resets, from that point onward you run agains time to reach lvl 60. Once you reach lvl 60 you get a reward with increased value the faster you completed the challenge, if you managed to break the current record you get a huge reward.

I think it`s really easy to implement but i`m not a programmer so i dont really know. Let me know what you guys think.
