# I feel he Breath of Solnius greatly affects the balance of the game

## Post 117175 by Secretary — 2025-01-18T17:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117175#p117175 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Breath of Solnius greatly affects the balance of the game. Nowadays, many shamans pursue higher cast speed . When The Breath of Solnius triggers, cast speed increases by 20% and the Chain Heal decreases by about 0.4 seconds. As NAXX farm faster, the heal gap becomes smaller and smaller， 0.4 second gap is like an insurmountable barrier. If my T3 is bis, and another shaman's T2.5 bis, and he has The Breath of Solnius , then his treatment amount directly exceeds my T3. I think it's not right to directly reject all the equipment of NAXX with one piece of equipment. I hope the designer will consider it carefully.
