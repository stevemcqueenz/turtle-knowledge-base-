# Two Solutions for AV Problems

## Post 59631 by Icedcream (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-28T21:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59631#p59631 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Problem 1) Games devolving into long holds
Solution

Mechanic 1: The defensive location of each team next to the most offensive location receives a 5 second spawn penalty per 5 minutes after 15 minutes.

Example : Most offensive point alliance Frostwolf - then RH gains a penalty at 15 mins they get a 35 second spawn, at 20 mins 40 second spawn, and so one. AT the same time, if the horde most offensive is in the north the alliance receive the same penality.

Mechanic 2: after 15 minutes defensive guard respawns are increased by 15 seconds per 5 minutes.

Problem 2) 30+ minute plus alliance queue times

Mechanic: Second queue option players of all factions can open queue. When they get put on a team if they end up on opposite faction they get a disguise applied to them making them look like UD or orc or human etc.

SOLVED.
