# Linking professions in chat?

## Post 24768 by Zhilo — 2022-09-10T12:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24768#p24768 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of my fondest memories of playing retail WoW was back in Cataclysm, when I rolled a goblin warrior and played it until you reach an island in the starting area with a small base that has the basic ammenities on it. I picked up blacksmithing an mining, and, for the fun of it, set up shop in the base. I would announce my services in trade chat and link my blacksmithing so people could see what I could make. I took donations of copper, leatehr and gems as payment, and would craft gear nonstop for the other new players. It even came to the point that players were forming a line in front of my smithy to get their stuff crafted! I felt great about providing a neccesary service that was appreciated, and It all happened organically.

Technically all this can already be done in vanilla, but knowing what a crafter can do and what they need immediatly encourages people to make requests. If a player doesnt know that a blacksmith can make a strong lvl 12  2h axe, then, even if they really could use it, won't even bother looking for a blacksmith's services.

I liked how that system made it easier for people to engage in producing comissions for players directly without having to depend on the auction house. Could we plase have that convenience in Turtle?
