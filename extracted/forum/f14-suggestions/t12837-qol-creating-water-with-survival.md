# QoL: Creating Water with Survival

## Post 85668 by Terramagnus — 2024-01-23T01:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85668#p85668 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

TL;DR: Boil water and get budget orange.

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 **The Problem**

Given how reliant most classes are on water, it is oddly inconvenient to obtain compared to something like food, which even has an entire profession based on it.

Unless you are a Mage you're either shelling out coin by the handful for paltry amounts of water from vendors, or begging the Mages themselves.

Survival is already a source of excellent quality of life improvements and interesting new food items via the planters.

I believe that a solution to water inaccessibility lies within the Survival skill providing a way to create Soulbound, "conjured" water for yourself.

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 **The Solution**

My concept for creating the water is fairly straightforward:

 1. Purchase an item from a vendor- let's call it an "Empty Canteen".

 2. Go to any source of water in the world and use the Canteen to turn it into a "Filled Canteen".

 3. Go to any viable Cooking entity and cook a new recipe- "Waterskin". For the purpose of this example let's say it creates 5.

Reagents: The Filled Canteen (returning the Empty Canteen), Perhaps it could also require something trivial such as Light Leather, one per Waterskin created.

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 **The Specifics**

As for the Waterskin item itself, I would recommend it have the following characteristics:

 -Soulbound, so they are not replacing the team-oriented ability mages posses.
 -Conjured Item, so you cannot stockpile them.
 -Restore mana based on user's level, or lesser % if you don't mind it scaling.

I think this would be a simple, interesting way to provide a much-needed quality of life mechanic to the server that engages the player directly with the world in an immersive way.

They indeed already do have some food gathering in the form of seeds, which you plant in the wooden boxes and water until they grow. Cultivation of foods which also provide some added bonuses like seen from cooking. Collecting meat from beasts or fish and cooking it is a very convenient way of making food for yourself, and many of them even provide the well-fed buff which is really handy for leveling since the added spirit helps you recover between fights.

I'd really like to see a form of water have this level of QoL, I understand that blizzard's intended solution was for players to simply spend stacks of gold on water from innkeepers, but for players who are leveling that will bankrupt them very rapidly. I'd wager that the loot gained from killing mobs would amount to less than the cost of drinking to recover from the fight.

There is also the "off grid" nature of the survival skill, allowing you to gain rested xp outside of cities, log out instantly at a camp you set up instead of having to visit an inn, etc. Being reliant on a vendor seems odd when an entire profession is seemingly built around self-reliance.
