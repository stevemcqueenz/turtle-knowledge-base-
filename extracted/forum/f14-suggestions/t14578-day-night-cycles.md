Hello there.~

There is a little thing that is bugging me and is also causing some confusion amongst the players: The ingame clock and its 12hour shift between Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms. Especially when you try to set up an event and one is currently having the wrong server-clock.

Second: I love playing during night times. And I'd guess other players love the World of Azeroth at night the same as I do.

My suggestion now is to revert the Server clock back to normal and instead changing the cycles in which day and night interchange. That maybe every 6 hours we have a shift between day and night. And while it's night in the Eastern Kingdoms, it's daytime in Kalimdor. I don't know if this is an impossible change or something that might be done with the future 2.0-Client, but i wanted to throw in this suggestion.
