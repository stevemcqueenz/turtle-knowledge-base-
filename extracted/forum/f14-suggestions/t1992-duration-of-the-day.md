# Duration of the day

## Post 11851 by Barrok — 2021-08-27T16:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11851#p11851 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello first post here, a few weeks ago I started playing like a turtle on the server and it is really cool, thank you very much.

Well for the sake of immersion and practicality, I thought you could create a separate Azeroth daytime and duration from the server.

Immersion, by watching the day goes while you are doing quests, or exploring, or maybe enter a dungeon when the sun rises and leave it victorious when the sunset, it will gives so much realism.

Practice, well because of life's issues, in my case I usually connect at the same time generally (10pm Argentina is like 3am on the server), one or two hours and it starts to get tired of seeing Azeroth always at night.

So, what if we separate the server and kingdom hours? Being that the servers remains as it is for organizational reasons. And to the kingdom, gives a schedule with a duration that is not a multiple of 24, so at the same time of connecting, one day you can meet Azeroth during the day, and another days play at night. And with a much shorter duration of the day.

Two options that I thought about and I like are:

I give these two durations, the one of 5 hours perhaps is easier to put together and even program, I don't know. And the other one at 4:20 that I personally like the most, don't know why, It´s feel more organic, more complex.

And later if you like, there is the possibility of implementing meals and rest for our pj, without so many interruptions that it becomes tedious or annoying. More or less a half-hour meal in the kingdom would be little more than 5 min, which seems very cool, even more so if it is shared with someone else.
