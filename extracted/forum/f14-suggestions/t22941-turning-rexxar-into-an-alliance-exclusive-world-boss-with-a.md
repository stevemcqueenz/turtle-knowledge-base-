# Turning Rexxar into an Alliance-Exclusive World Boss (with a Horde Counterpart)

## Post 160169 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-11-20T13:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160169#p160169 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,

I’ve been thinking about a way to enrich both the PvE and world-PvP experience on Turtle WoW, and I wanted to share an idea.
Between Desolace and Feralas, there’s Rexxar — the legendary Horde hunter roaming around with his bear Misha. He’s an iconic, badass character… but in practice he’s barely used. Aside from a quest, he doesn’t add much to the world.
What if Rexxar became an Alliance-exclusive World Boss?

The concept would be simple:
Buff Rexxar with extra HP and unique mechanics _ Turn him into a dangerous roaming boss _ Make him a high-value target for Alliance players !

Of course, for balance, the Horde should get its own equivalent World Boss — an iconic Alliance hero redesigned for the Horde to hunt.

This could open up a ton of possibilities for both PvE and open-world PvP:

✔ Dynamic PvE Content :
Rare boss roaming the world ;
Unique mechanics ;
Powerful loot to make it worthwhile.

✔ Faction vs Faction Gameplay :
Introduce a timed event where:

The boss grants double loot or becomes temporarily weakened ;
One faction gets a quest to protect the boss ;
The other faction must kill it at all costs.

This could create chaotic, organic, large-scale world-PvP moments — the kind of emergent gameplay Turtle WoW is known for.

Honestly, I think this could bring a lot of life to the open world, give players new goals, and turn unused lore characters into meaningful gameplay events.

What do you all think ? Would you enjoy something like this ?

Thanks for the feedback — but I think you’re looking at this too strictly through the lens of vanilla WoW, not Turtle WoW.

On Turtle, the devs have already shown they’re willing to redesign major pieces of content when it makes sense for gameplay. They completely reworked raid encounters, moved Gehennas to the Firelands, added new dungeons, new zones, new class mechanics, new world events… So changing the Ony pre-quest so Rexxar is no longer required wouldn’t be out of character at all for this server. If anything, it would finally free him from a very outdated quest design.

Right now we have an NPC with 600k+ HP roaming the world, doing nothing except giving a quest. That’s an incredible waste of potential for such an iconic character. Turning him into meaningful open-world content would fit perfectly with Turtle’s philosophy:
revitalize unused parts of Azeroth and create organic PvE/PvP moments.

As for the “disrespecting a Horde hero” argument — I don’t really buy it.
If he becomes a world boss, then the Horde can defend him.
Just like the Alliance would defend an Alliance hero turned into a world boss.
This actually reinforces the faction fantasy instead of weakening it.

And honestly, players already talk about wanting rewards for killing faction leaders like Thrall, Vol’jin, etc. Is that disrespectful too? WoW has always been about fighting iconic characters — that’s what raids and world bosses are. You don’t stop killing something just because it’s important to the lore. Otherwise we’d never raid anything.

In the end, Turtle WoW has tons of potential for stronger world-PvP and dynamic open-world content.
Right now, that potential is mostly untouched — and Rexxar is the perfect example.
