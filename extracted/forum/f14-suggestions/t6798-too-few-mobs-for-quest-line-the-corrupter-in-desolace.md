# Too few mobs for quest line The Corrupter in Desolace

## Post 42826 by Qoqdina — 2023-04-24T10:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42826#p42826 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, today I did the quest line called “The Corrupter” in the north west zone of Desolace. And I wanted to post here and suggest something about the 3rd one which demands to the player to loot an “oracle crystal” on a mob called “slitherblade oracle”. [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=1 ... -corrupter](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=1482/the-corrupter)

I realised that there’s only 5 slitherblade oracles on the map (at least according to the addon pfQuest and the time I’ve spent at the location even though wowhead tells there’s many) that can potentially drop this item at a droprate of 15%. In addition to this, mobs are distributed in such a way that there is about 30 seconds to one minute traveling time between them. Plus, you need to fight and travel by swimming. It took me about 45 minutes and luckily there wasn’t another player doing the same quest. Knowing that and the fact that the last quest, which asks you to kill 2 elite bosses, only rewards you by the choice between two green items, which are quite specific for this lvl (intel shield or mail shoulders under lvl 40 <https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1488>) I want to suggest that maybe some slitherblade oracles can be added on the map in this zone to help players spend less time doing this quest or be tempted to abandon it.

I hope I have been concise and clear (sorry my English is not very good). Thanks in advance for your help. : )
