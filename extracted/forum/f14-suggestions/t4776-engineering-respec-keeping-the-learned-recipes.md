# Engineering respec keeping the learned recipes

## Post 28588 by Gantulga (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-11-06T20:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28588#p28588 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It would be a very welcome change not being able to lose recipes, or at least raid recipes when you respec your engineering and use the book in Tanaris.

The profession is in a weird spot where a lot of people don't know that you should go gnomish first and then respec to goblin if you want to do it right, and you may be stuck in a situation where you want to correct this mistake but already learned some of the rare MC recipes.

It's poor game design overall but since it isn't going anywhere, I think a "mistake" should be less punishing.
