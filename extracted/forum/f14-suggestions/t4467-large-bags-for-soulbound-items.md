# Large Bags for soulbound items

## Post 26692 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-10-04T08:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26692#p26692 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So a new proposal to fix the every increasing storage problems.

I am almost fully decked out with 36 sized bags.
Still I can't find space for all those cool soulbound items I have gathered. Every new cool quest reward added is potentially a new problem I don't know how to store.

Let me propose: soulbound bags
A bag you can only put in soulbound items.
This will guarantee it can only be used to store your gear, quest items, but nothing else.

It won't be useful to store up huge amounts of consumables or material or farm in open world.

Main focus is just to give players more room for gear they want to keep. Like rp fashion gear.
Especially hybrid classes could benefit since they are likely carrying around tank, heal, dmg gear.

I saw multiple times now players grieving in world chat because they felt forced to vendor their nature resits gear because of bag issues. Coincidently it was both times paladins, aka hybrids with massive inventory issues.

Pls add a 48 sized soulbound bag that can only store soulbound gear
