# Limit Multiboxing with current server pop

## Post 57445 by Bert47 — 2023-08-15T12:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57445#p57445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, I think it might be prudent to limit multiboxing and only allow one account to be signed in max while we have queues of thousands every single day now.

To help reduce the amount of accounts needed, please allow people to reset their own instances from inside without needing a second account to do so. That would get more people off the server who are just using an extra account to reset their farming runs.

There may be other small changes that could made in that same vein that would alleviate the pressure from people logging multiple accounts on at once.
