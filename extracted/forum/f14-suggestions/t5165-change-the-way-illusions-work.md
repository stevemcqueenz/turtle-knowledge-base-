# Change the Way Illusions work.

## Post 31373 by Veliant — 2022-12-22T10:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31373#p31373 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I was questing in Wetlands and was cursed by a pirate. This changed me into a ghostly pirate for the duration of the curse. I actually liked still being in my bear form as a druid, while shape shifted into a form thats not so bulky and doesn't occasionally randomly make camera go wonky, so when Wintervail came around I was excited about furbolg illusion because I thought my days of tanking as a bear were at last at an end (well at least as a non humanoid one). Sadly this was not the case, Illusion cancels bear form and vise versa. So my question is.. would it be possible to leave illusions the way they are as far as still being perma until canceled duration etc, but make them more in line with the way the curse worked in that they can be used in tandem with shape-shift class forms? I would very much like to continue to tank as a druid as I like the versatility of the class, but other than the furry community, most people probably don't want to stare at the back end of a bear all day.  I am sure I am not the only one, and others like me might be more inclined to buy illusions if they worked but still allowed us to play our class while using them as they work for other classes.

Little hack to use the illusion as an druid in every form:

At first use [Savory Deviate Delight], that's a buff foot that gives u an illusion (pirate or ninja) as a buff

[https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowwi ... 0925152155](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowwiki/images/4/43/Deviateeffects.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20050925152155)

Than use ur Illusion. U will be looks normal in all forms (for example a Tauren in cat Form looks sick :D) Use the illusion again to look like the illusion.

Ur illusion works as long the buff will be on you   satisfied_turtle
