# Drop players into BGs evenly

## Post 28595 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-11-06T23:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28595#p28595 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

WoW has always done this for queue pops, but not queue takes. On old retail and classic, the queueing base was so large that fillins would happen fast and this issue didn't occur. But we're not so blessed.

Currently, if 4 horde and 4 ally queue wsg, all 8 will get the pop. The problem is that, for example, maybe 3 horde were AFK or wanted to go to AV instead, and will ghost their queue pop, while all 4 alliance take the pop. This creates an unfun, unwinnable environment for the 1 horde who did take the wsg queue, where they try to 1v1 or just hide as the timer runs out, or 3 horde do show up later but end up 1 or 2 flags down in WSG or 500 points down in AB.

To fix this, only let a player into the bg when their opposite faction co-queuer also accepts.

In this scenario, 4 horde and 4 ally queue, and 3 horde don't take the pop. Only 1 alliance will make it in, with prioirty given to the first ally to queue. The other 3 ally will see a "waiting for enemy to enter..." message, and if they do end up getting an enemy to take the pop, they then get to go in themselves. The game becomes 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 and so on as it moves on up, and will never see a 1v4 at the start of the game. For any ally whose counterpart closes their queue pop or lets it time out, they will be dropped back into the queue keeping their first in line spot.

For the client side, this can probably be achieved with edits to the blizzard queue pop frames in the mpqs distributed to us. It might take some work server side, but with this in place, you now have probably the best smaller server PVP queue system to ever exist. Huge selling point, and the existing PVP players will be much happier and less reluctant to queue wsg and ab, and even sunnyglade.

Same can be done for blood ring. Right now, if its a 1v2 the game just closes (which happens ALL THE TIME and is a big reason this game mode is less popular). With this system, you'll end up with a few 1v1 matches which is fine, this is way better than just getting sent back to town with our mana drained.
