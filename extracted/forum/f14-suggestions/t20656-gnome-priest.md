Gnome Priests do in fact have a canon basis (Holdout Medics in Gnomeregan) and I fully support their integration alongside Goblin Shamans (and some Horde Priest racial buffs since they get nothing new).

When it comes to Priest racial spells, the general model is you have one that's a bit more generic and either directly shared with another race (Desperate Prayer) or shared in a mostly the same manner (Touch of Weakness and Hex of Weakness), and then your super unique one (Devouring Plague, Feedback, Avatar, etc.). I think since there's enough Desperate Prayer users, Gnomes could get a variation of Starshards as their first Priest racial except it uses Nature damage over Arcane (give it a taser theme) and their unique spell could be a fairly long cooldown offensive dispel (giving them a unique niche but with enough of a gap to not step on the toes of Shaman) with an added utility to make it impactful for its cooldown like empowering you and your allies if it eats a magic effect.
