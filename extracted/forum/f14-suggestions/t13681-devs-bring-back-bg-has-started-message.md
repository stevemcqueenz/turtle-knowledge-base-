# Devs - Bring BACK BG has started message

## Post 93014 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-18T09:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93014#p93014 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

OK so, most of us all remember the server wide message saying e.g "Warsong Gulch 60-60 Started!"

What this did was notify people that a BG had opened, then people would join in hopes to  get in, it was especially useful at low levels and helped the low lvl brackets pop.

Now levelers don't know when BG's pop, so they don't even bother queueing, assuming they never happen, but if on the rare chance a 10-19 pops, it'll be 4v3 then end in 5 mins.

BUT, if we enable the message, "Warsong Gulch 10-19 Started!" then people will see it and join and fill the game, maybe reviving the low lvl BG's again. Yes - 10-19 is filled with twinks but not really the other brackets.

I believe this was disabled originally when server pop was 12000 online, and it was spamming alot, especially with Blood Ring (I think Blood Ring, should stay disabled as they are only 2-3 min BG's).

This would also help Sunnyglade Valley pop, as no one queues this by default. So knowing when it opens would help keep it up

Now the pops 5k max and BG's hardly pop please enabled the messages again...

On another note, I'd also suggest adding on the loading screen tips "You can join BG's from anywhere, just click the Battleground finder icon on your minimap"

Alot of people including myself didn't know you could join from anywhere on this server, as on Vanilla, you had to queue from either the portal or a Battlemaster in a city, this kinda killed low lvl BG's for levelers, as it was inconvenient

I believe there is already a loading screen tip that states: You don't have to wait by a battleground master to stay in the battleground queue, that's the one I'd modify.

Any dev reading this please enable the notifications again, it's the only chance low lvl BG's have to work again.
