# Multibox and AFK Time

## Post 80064 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-06T15:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80064#p80064 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ban multiboxing and reduce AFK time.
First, I think multiboxing should be banned, since it helps a lot to farm rare items and mobs, or to gank lower level users quickly, which makes it difficult or completely impossible for some people to get something from a mob or simply farm minerals and materials for herbalism. I was at Drowned Reef near the Arathi Highlands and I literally saw like 4 max level AFK people, I'm assuming farming Prince Nazjak, I came back HOURS later and they were still there, AFK.
That brings us to the second point, AFK time, reduce it.
It has happened to me sometimes that I have gone to eat or do something that I think will be quick and it really takes me like half an hour and I come back and I'm still connected, AFK. I personally think that the maximum AFK time should be 5 minutes and then you log out, as this can be exploited by bots to farm rare items and mobs.
