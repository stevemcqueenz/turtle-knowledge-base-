I just think anything that promotes "good" PvP in it's optional form without forcing the PvE dudes into it is just a good call in general.

The whole I'm protected from PvP but can initiate with some derpy PvE spell without setup thing is just not good PvP in general. At least for a flag -> fear/sheep/nade etc instead of "I'm following you around waiting to you attack a mob so I can cast my ridiculously long easy to rupt spell on you with no downside.

Not to mention even after flagging there is no real downside even afterwards if they die bcause they can just afk while the timer ticks down and do it again and again.

Seems like the general vibe of the server enforces rules against griefing, while the systems inadvertently promote it to the detriment of people actually just looking for some occasional decent world PvP.

An example of another easily abusable system is cross faction grouping allowing you to heal cross faction without even flagging yourself.
