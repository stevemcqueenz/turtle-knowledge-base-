As stated above, have Turtle Event rewards like Tabard of Discovery be unlockable for the whole account. Once reached with an character it unlocks it for all other past and future characters on the account. (So if you already have unlocked it witch any character, it will be marked as unlocked on your account and will be send to all old and new characters)
