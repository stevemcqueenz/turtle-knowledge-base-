# [Hardcore] Contract of the Foolhardy (New Hardcore Confirmation Quest)

## Post 51096 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-23T16:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51096#p51096 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I had an idea that I think might improve the Hardcore situation – at least on the Game Master side of things.

It's an almost daily routine for people to come into the forums, or onto the *Discord*, and either politely ask… or demand with *hostility*… that their Hardcore character be resurrected.  Whether it be because they went AFK in the wrong place, they were tricked into flagging for PvP, they activated a quest they didn't even know existed, or simply because they did something they could have easily prevented, it's a fact that players clearly do not understand the gravity of the situation.  Maybe they didn't read the [quest text](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80388).  Maybe they didn't read the [game mode rules](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=1636).  Whatever the case, it's pretty clear that people just zip through the quest and be done with it.  And that's why I've come up with the following proposal.

 **Add a Guild Charter-like item that the player has to sign and turn into the Mysterious Stranger.**

As of right now, all a player has to do is talk to the Mysterious Stranger once, accept the quest, talk to him again, and complete the quest.  If there was *another* small hurdle preventing them from just mindlessly completing the quest like any other quest in the game, there's a chance it *might* lessen the amount of "rez plz" requests.  Even if it only curtails this by 1%, I think it would be worth the effort – especially as it probably wouldn't be *that* hard to implement.

Here's how things would go down under this revised version:
- Player talks with Mysterious Stranger at Level 1.
- Mysterious Stranger has the following to say in the Quest Description:
(Italicized text is brand new dialogue.)
> Stay Awhile and Listen...
>
>  Hmm, another one? Very well.
>
>  You seek to prove yourself to your people, no? To rise above all others as the strongest, no? To leave behind a legacy of courage and heroism, rather than to fade from history as yet another wanderer of no consequence, no?
>
>  I have your attention, do I? I have just the offer for you, then. I will grant you the opportunity to become Immortal, should you prevail over death in your journey for glory.
>
>  However... If you die, you shall not return. Your soul shall be forever severed from your body.
>
>  This offer I extend to you only here and now. There will be no second chance should you refuse my deal. All you have to do is read this document and sign it… in your blood!
>
>  <The mysterious stranger cackles before regaining their composure.>
>
>  Dramatics aside, think it over. Read the document carefully. This is a very serious commitment with no "take-backs", as it were.
>
>  Objectives
>  Sign the Contract of the Foolhardy
- Accepting the quest will give the player the following item:
> Contract of the Foolhardy
>  **Item Level 1**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Unique
>  **"It has a menacing aura…"**
>  <Right Click to Read>
- Talking to the Mysterious Stranger again will yield the following:
> Hm? Having second thoughts, are we?
>
>  <The Mysterious Stranger tilts their head.>
>
>  Or, perhaps you're simply delaying the inevitable?
>
>  <The Stranger chuckles, awaiting your decision.>
>
>  Objectives
>  Sign the Contract of the Foolhardy
- Right-clicking the Contract triggers the following quest dialogue:
> Contract of the Foolhardy
>
>  <You unfurl the contract and begin to read what appears to be a… poem?>
>
>  Come, O fool, so brave and bold
>  And with your blood, do sign
>  Your destiny will forever change
>  But life will become unkind!
>
>  Your allies will all abandon you
>  Brokers and couriers, turn away
>  All your possessions will be forfeit
>  And sacrificed on this day!
>
>  Only those of similar mind
>  Will help you on your path
>  And should you die while on your way
>  You'll feel the stranger's wrath!
>
>  No force will save you from yourself
>  No powers will revive you
>  So sign away your life and soul
>  For the glory this will provide you!
>
>  <This is a "contract"? Well, there is a space at the bottom for your signature. Do you want to sign it?>
>
>  [Complete]
- Pressing [Complete] on this quest completes the "Sign the Contract of the Foolhardy" objective for the actual quest, but the player can still cancel "Stay Awhile", itself, to back out, if need be.  It also adds the following item to your inventory:
> Signed Contract of the Foolhardy
>  **Item Level 1**
>  Binds when picked up
>  Unique
>  **"Your signature pulses with a dark, eerie glow…"**

This is a repeatable quest, by the way.  Just in case a player decides to mess around or something. (Hi.)
- Upon turning in the "Stay Awhile" quest, the mysterious stranger says the following:
(Again, italicized text highlights new dialogue.)
> *<The Mysterious Stranger examines the signed document before looking at you with a smirk.>*
>
>  Well then, *it seems you've* decided, <class>? But, perhaps… you don't fully understand the gravity of this situation?  Yes, I want to make sure that you're certain of this before we, to use a colloquialism… "seal the deal".
>
>  <The Stranger lowers the document and takes on a very serious expression.>
>
>  If it wasn't clear from the wording of the contract, once you start walking this path, you will not be able to abandon it. It will forever dominate your destiny. No mail will ever bring you good news, no auction will take your money. *And whatever meager possessions you presently carry will be cast to the void.* Only those who follow the same path will be of *any* aid to you.
>
>  *Most importantly…* should you *fall in battle or otherwise lose your grip on this mortal coil*, your soul *will not return to the Light nor be cast into the Twisting Nether.  It* will forever be mine. Whatever deity you worship will not be able to help you. The end of you will be quite permanent. For that reason, I wouldn't recommend getting into any unnecessary conflicts. You'll have but "one life to live", after all…
>
>  <The Stranger briefly laughs.>
>
>  *Undeterred, are we? Well, as they say… "fools rush in"…* Very well. If you survive the trials ahead, we shall meet again one day. Until then…
>
>  <The Stranger grins ominously.>
>
>  Good "luck"...
>
>  <As the Stranger turns away, you feel an odd sense of dread that you can't quite explain. Everything feels the same… but somehow different? You hope you don't come to regret this decision… especially if what the Stranger said is true.>
>
>  Reward
>  The following spell will be cast on you:
>  Hardcore
- And, that's it.  The player is now stuck in Hardcore Mode until Level 60.

—

So, yeah.  That's my idea.  Would it actually change anything?  Probably not.  But, who knows?  Maybe people just need that momentary, forced pause to really understand things.
