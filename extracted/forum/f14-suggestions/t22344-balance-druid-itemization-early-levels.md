# Balance druid itemization (early levels)

## Post 155162 by Paidian — 2025-10-11T15:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155162#p155162 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey there folks,

Recently, I started leveling a druid as balance to experience it and see how viable it is. While the new talents help a ton, it's still vastly inferior to feral druid. I noticed that one of the main contributors to it is the early level leather items.

I'm leveling the character with leatherworking and majority of the armor I can craft caters to feral specs and the caster items are divided by two for healing and dps aspects. This, with the combination of lack of leather caster rewards in quests and world drop, results in a lack of progression when it comes to itemization. Healers can still do dungeon efficiently, since there's always a need for them, it's more difficult for balance druids to find a spot as quickly.

I'm leveling in HC so maybe the experience (especially dungeoning) is different in SC but that's been my experience. I think if we could have at least more crafting options with caster leather gear, this would alleviate the leading problem by a lot (both for balance druids and elemental shamans).

If I'm missing something, please let me know or point to a source for getting more relevant gear during leveling.
Thanks!
