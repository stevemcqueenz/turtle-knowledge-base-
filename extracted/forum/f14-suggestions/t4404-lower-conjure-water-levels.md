# Lower Conjure Water Levels

## Post 26250 by Ashkir — 2022-09-27T04:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26250#p26250 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know that mage water is effected by level. But, our mana pools greatly outweigh the benefit of waiting. It's simply easier to go buy water. During the leveling experience mage water isn't a pleasant experience in game.

For example I am level 28 and am using level 15 water from my conjure water spell. I don't think it is game breaking or against the spirit of classic to make mage water levels more attainable.

Level 15 mage water should be trainable at 16
Level 25 mage water should be trainable at 26
Level 35 mage water should be trainable at 36

etc

I really feel like having to wait 10 levels to train again kind of defeats the purpose of using the water. It cant be used in groups, etc.

To be fair, I think the food should be readjusted. Conjure water/food creates a social interaction. Being able to make relevent to our level increases this interaction.
