# New mode "Semi-HC"

## Post 98861 by Nerasw (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-08T07:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98861#p98861 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

i want to suggest new "hard"-core challenge:

i found myself pretty engaged into HC and was re-opening WoW. I always wondered why im so in love with hc, especially TWoW implementation? At first i answered to this question just as many other players:

- adrenaline

- troubles that make ppl group

- troubles that make ppl cooperate, trade, chat
and it was pretty like the original WoW, we grouped for quests, elites, trade greens and blues. That was a great time, way before we started to ignore leveling and treat it as "just part before game".

Overthinking this question for months and even years (2+), i'd came to a conclusion that fav part of HC for me is a HARD, strict set of rules: no help from high lvls (trade and groups within your range only), no auction house use, lose character on-death.

 3rd one is simple enough to not discuss.
 2nd is just 1st one on steroids, it's actually same rule (strict trade process) but different implementation.
 1st one is most important - let's see what it really is:

- first of all it makes your character start and grow on it's own. Ofc u can say "hey, i can group with same lowbies and get faster leveling". Yes, but your character SHOULD do work in that group, at first you have to cooperate with others and form that group, which is nice - the more social activities - the better, and more over - others in your group are ALL in equal position, thus they wont do whole work themselves for you, or be carried by you.

- another good thing in not getting gear from high lvl boosters is ... surprise, surprise VALUE. Gear has value, every green that replaces grey piece is an upgrade! Every blue is a shiny beauty,.. even if only good stat on it is armour and stam - it's so damn good to get a random drop that can (and will) help u survive next steps.
All in all it makes everyone start in equal position and value help and luck more than in standart more as it wont inflate no matter how much 60s are on server at anygiven moment. You work for yourself on your own, or for your team, with team, as team. It doesn't matter, you build your own surrounding by your actions. You trade and group with ppl you trust or with everyone no matter what. You get consequences of your choices. And they do MATTER. As you are not the only guy RP'ing or dreaming about set of rules for yourself, but rules exist outside of anyone will - it not only equalize everyone, but also provides strong atmosphere around YOU. You won't risk jumping into 3+ enemies just to get that briathorn for your SwiftPotions. Someone will, And probably die on that low lvl encounter. You can save him, or use his troubles as opportunity and get node by yourself. But what if mobs will kill him too quick and get back for you? Risk. But Swift Potion can save you in future. Risk!

Or just buy it and forget about choices. Softcore life is not about it, it's a different choices. You will get stronger greens in just 5 lvls, you wont do some elites for chances of getting good boe or hard Q for similar purpose. You wont do it not cuz of "risk", but cuz u waste you time.

Ofc for someone it's not true, and they do every quest, they even delete their character after 1st death. They won't ever need hard-coded rules. But they also won't ever experience atmosphere of ppl around you being bounded by same set of rules without chances of denying their choice. Strong bond that tights ppl together even for a short quest, risk of losing everything, excitement of getting rewards that are not devaluated, doubts about their partner, enjoyment of whole process.

Thats just positive sides of HC experience. What about negatives? How much of our brothers and sisters fel down in unfair battle with electricity services? How much of them were scored by local IPS? I lost hafl of my brain cells after both Ethernet and Sim betrayed me. That was horrible. And never could be undone. Ofc it would be stupid to ask for another chance - solution is simple - try another chance on another character.

So i came up with a new IDEA:

Soft-core. But hold your laugh, that's not what u think.
Some may think soft-core is simply normal gameplay, but what im talking about is new mode that let's your character only trade/party within 5lvl range, no auction house, upon death character is in ghost form for a week and maybe lose some HP. Thus we still has engaging gameplay with strict rules that prevent unbalanced help "from above" but let's you get back after disaster somewhat mitigating frustration from situation player had no control over (disconnects, lags, electricity shutdowns, etc)
