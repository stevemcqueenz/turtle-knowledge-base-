# Mechanisms and Addition of Classes and Factions

## Post 92292 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-04-07T18:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92292#p92292 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Suggestions include:

 1. **Necromance replaces/dominates the talent tree of "Affliction" with more undead controling skills as shown in Warcraft III.** Before the outbreak of the scourge, sorcerers/wizards in lore had performed "black magics" manipulating undeath. The literal meaning of "warlock" is just a dark magic caster rather than a "demon summoner".

 2. **A bigger role for throwing weapons.** Javelins and throwing axes can be used as they work in Warcraft III on troll units.

 3. **Making the priest and shaman even more of racial diversity.** For example, night elf priests use Elune's power with differentiated visuals (and may be exclusive for females). Troll shamans may act more like a witchdoctor or a shadow hunter using "wards" rather than "totems". We assume the "voodoo" and "shamanism" are actually similar philosophies. However, homogenisation has a detrimental effect on the game play. Blizzard has always prioritised the balance of mechanism over logics of the story and role-playing and that's why the Vanila WoW smashed a bigger hit while Dragonflight developed a wane.

 4. **Deletion of all weapon specialisations of talents and races.** If an orc warrior has the racial axe specialisation, it would feel awkward to abandon that advantage and turn to use a Hammer for the stunning effect or a sword for more damage by reassigning all talent points merely for a new loot. Other than that, the dual-wielding specialisation in Fury also brings limitation on players' choices of gears. Just make all the weapon specialisations in talents functional when we pick up a corresponding weapon. Reducing the number of talent points of weapon specialisations to only five, alternatively, would also solve the problem that makes players hesitate to use new gears.
(Edited in 29 May, 2025. This one has been considered and to some extent implemented in version 1.17, but there are still elements in game limiting the freedom for warrior players' weapon choices.)

 5. **Demon Hunter and Death Knight classes.** To stick to the original lore of the franchise, demon hunters are night elves. Even in Blizzard's original games, the story of the Vanila version has the possibility to implement the death knight class. In fact, the story of TBC parallels with that of the pre-TBC WoW. To explain the truth, we can assume the demise of Illidan in Outland in TBC is happening simultaneously with the war between Kalimdor coalition and C'thun's silithid forces (not to mention the mysterious red crystal shard in the Darkshore possibly from Exodar and multiple horde-friendly blood elf characters). The playable death knight class in WotLK is from the Ebonblade, which first appear in an episode between Nefarian's defeat and the crusade to Northrend lead by Fordring. Despite being chronologically a bit late, the occurance of death knights as players' roles is logical. They seem more like the jet fighters in the second world war in 1900s. By the way, I personally dislike the story of TBC, where Illidan, who is slain by Arthas in the cinematic of *The Frozen Throne*, got revived to attract new users. The racial relation built between the draenei and eredars is also a bad idea (hope it will never be adopted by Turtle WoW).

 6. **Four factions (at least) for players to choose rather than only two.** Turtle WoW has already broken the barrier of cross-faction partying. In other words, an orc shaman could join a team with a human paladin, retaliating for the harassment from a night elf huntress, which is also visible in Warcraft III.

It's just my personal opinion but:
 1. If anything, we could tweak demonoly tree to allow you a similar playstyle. Affli is rather the shady guy whispering avada kedavra from far away. Not drastical changes, just one flavorful talent for example.
 2. Absolutely. I'm even fine with the tweak that trhowing weapons have infinite charge. That would mean a stat stick like a bow, without having to buy arrows. This is a clear advantage though and has to be balanced to not powercreep bows/crossbows.
 3. You've written it, balance > flavor. What you suggest would mean 'deleting two classes' and adding like 8-9, each only playable by one race. Sure, there would be similarities among them but it's still a huge work. That work could go into projects like new raid tier f.ex. What I'd agree with is a flavor change. Let's say all races keep their current spells but they are animated differentlly and if it doesn't break balance, they sometimes use different schools of magic. Let's say your undead's lesser heal becomes a shadow spell but does the same thing. Possibly a troll's lesser heal becomes a nature spell. It's still a big work though and I'm pretty sure that this would somehow break the balance of the game.
 4. It's a sensitive topic. TWOW has a very limited freedom at changes as a Vanilla+ server. People come here for nostalgia, so while you can make things more fair etc, the core of the game should be roughly the same. Many people would feel that tweaking weapon skill racials is too much for the Vanilla experience. Also, many min-maxer warrior chose the race based on racials and spent 1000+ hours in the game to be as good as possible. Changing the meta would be a middle finger towards the minmaxers. Personally I agree that something has to be done and in an ideal world I'd tweak racials. But in a Vanilla+ server I'd prefer just adding more ways to get weapon skills, like enchants.
 5. Way too much work. Sounds interesting but this alone would be a year-long project, stopping everything else. Interesting but projects like T3,5 are more important for the people. Also, it's Vanilla+. Many people would say that if you want this, you can just choose a private server from a later iteration. Also, introducing DKs without Lich King raid would feel weird.
 6. That's a huge change and not only it isn't Vanilla-like, it isn't Warcraft-like. Stories should have been rewritten, current 6 capitals should have been divided up by 4 factions etc. As you said, TWOW has crossfaction, which solves a lot of problems for PVE. For PVP they (should) make sure that the Ally-only and the Horde-only classes are extremely balanced to keep PVP healthy. A WOW with 4 factions is not a WOW anymore. However, I could imagine a weird masochistic glyph challenge which makes you flagged for PVP by any faction outside of cities. It would make leveling a living hell though.
