# Suggestion: Additional Sources for Ranged Weapons with Elemental Damage

## Post 161811 by Marcuswho — 2025-12-06T15:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161811#p161811 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

To the Turtle WoW Development Team,

I would like to suggest the introduction of additional ranged weapons (bows, crossbows, or guns) that provide bonus elemental damage as an obtainable option within the game.

The primary reason for this suggestion is the current scarcity and accessibility issue. At present, the only reliable source for such weapons appears to be a rare elite drop. This specific elite is consistently dominated by groups utilizing multi-boxing or similar methods, often being killed immediately upon respawn. As a result, the vast majority of players who wish to explore and enjoy elemental-focused builds or playstyles never have a realistic chance to encounter this elite alive, let alone obtain the item.

This situation effectively gates a potentially fun and thematic gameplay avenue behind a resource controlled by a very small subset of the player base. While the previous change to make a certain fiery bow Bind on Pickup (BoP) instead of Bind on Equip (BoE) was likely intended to disrupt farming for gold profits, it may not fully address the core issue. It could potentially shift the problem to methods like pre-arranged group loot distribution followed by real-world trading, which can be equally detrimental to the game's economy and fairness. In some respects, this fix might create complications outweighing its benefits.

Therefore, I respectfully recommend creating alternative avenues to acquire ranged weapons with elemental damage. This could involve:
 1. Adding them to reputations vendors with significant reputation requirements.
 2. Introducing them as rewards for challenging but repeatable quest lines or solo/group tasks.
 3. Including them in the loot tables of other appropriate dungeons or raid content.
 4. Crafting recipes with meaningful material costs.

Providing more accessible (though still earned) paths to such items would:

 1. Greatly enhance the game experience for players interested in elemental builds.
 2. Promote build diversity and experimentation.
 3. Democratize access to a thematic gear choice, aligning with the spirit of a community-focused server.
 4. Reduce the negative impact of monopolized farm spots on the wider player base.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I believe this change would be a positive step towards a more inclusive and varied gameplay environment for all Turtle WoW adventurers.

Sincerely,

A Turtle WoW Player
