I've tried retail, there's nothing to enjoy there.

The problem with retail is all the QoL stuff they've replaced the actual fun with. Some people like vanilla 5-mans because they are nonlinear, sometimes quite long, sometimes hard to reach and (when the group is not overpowered) even require a minimum of skill and coordination. BRD and Maraudon are gems which are unequaled by any 5-mans I've seen in later expansions, except for the polygon count. Also note that leveling 5-mans can be challenging and fun as long as the group setup is not overpowered (i.e. as long as at least some mobs are still red and people don't bring a lvl60 to blow everything way before it can become a threat.).

And let's face it: 5-man gameplay differs from raiding gameplay. While there's a huge and vocal community that often likes to claim that leveling and 5-mans are something to get over with as quickly as possible before finally getting to "the good stuff", there are people who prefer the smaller-scale gameplay that comes before "the endgame" and giving these people some candy instead of yet another 40-man raid that they don't care about sounds good to me.
