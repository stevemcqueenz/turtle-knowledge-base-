# Rested glyph that reduces tent rate but increases inn rate

## Post 11755 by Jessienyan — 2021-08-22T02:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11755#p11755 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I see a lot of discussion in-game about tents, specifically from the hardcore playerbase.

Some people enjoy using them but many are against tents, for a variety of reasons.

The players who are against tents would rather get rested exp through an inn. However, inns have awful rested rates, and comparing them to tents is a joke.

Simply improving inn rest rate doesn't really solve the problem. However, having a glyph that makes you really only be able to use inns for rested exp gives players that option.

That then introduces the issue of players abusing the glyph by equipping it when they logout at an inn, and deleting it when they log back in.

In that case, if you either equip or remove it, you should have your rested exp cleared.

That also means a player can't sit at a tent and rest for a while, equip the glyph and finish resting at the inn.
