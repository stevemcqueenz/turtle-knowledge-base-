Long time ago there was a thread about professions and final recipes.
[viewtopic.php?t=5619](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=5619)
Main idea - there are two profession-locked designs. Both are bad.
 1. Item is BoP and don't require profession to use. It leads to situation when people learn profession only for 1 specific item, craft it and drop profession once they reach the goal.
 2. Item is BoP and require profession to use like Eng goggles. It is even worse, because you have to stay with some profession just because it is BiS in terms of gear.

So professions are now just flavour for leveling and it is good. I think they are also good on first phase, like Ambershire have.
Obsolete profession in t3 servers is a flaw of power creep of t3 gear. It is not a problem of professions themselves.
