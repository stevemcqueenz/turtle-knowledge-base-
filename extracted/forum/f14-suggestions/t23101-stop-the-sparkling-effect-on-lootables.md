## Post 161230 by Rejuve (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-01T15:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161230#p161230 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

First time I comment on something with Turtle, and thus I firstly want to say; Fantastic job Turtle has done with this Ambershire vanilla world !

While doing addons for Turtle I came across SuperAPI -balake-. This makes herbs/chests/Qitems/more sparkle so you can see them immediately, and from afar. This feels like cheating (not to mention that it already is kinda cheating since it takes half a university degree in programming just to understand how to install such addons/mods, or whatever its called).

Through experience with wow in general through the years (Blizz and some other private servers), and herbalism spercifically, I have learnt that such and such herbs grows in certain places (like earthroot near mountains/stone), "tendencies". This knowledge gave/gives me an edge that is more or less erased with this sparkle-nonsense that I like to call it.

It takes skill out of the game !

No cowboys in the old west, wether they were thieves or not, put up an arrow and lit lantern to show where the gold or money they had were at when they slept at night in some camp in the wild.
