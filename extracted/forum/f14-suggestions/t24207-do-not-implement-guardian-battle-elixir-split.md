# Do NOT implement guardian/battle elixir split

## Post 170298 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-03-07T17:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170298#p170298 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I'm well aware there's been zero talk about this yet, and I'm thankful for that, but I've also seen other private servers claiming to be "Vanilla+" implementing things like this just because they can or there's some preconceived ideal that "TBC is already the perfect Vanilla+ mechanically! Let's just import everything from there into the vanilla world!" and while I absolutely love TBC there's a lot of deviations it made that aren't really in the spirit of Vanilla WoW. This is one of them.

The TBC system for elixirs just doesn't feel right in vanilla. Not only does it negatively impact "Muh classic spirit" but it also has the capability to bring issues such as certain elixirs becoming useless or less used compared to others because they're off meta or don't give as much value to certain specs or playstyles compared to others. Wanna stack defensive elixirs as a tank? Nope. Health or armor, take it or leave it. Wanna have ANY strength or attack power elixir? Sorry, mongoose is meta for every melee that doesn't need to take spell/hybrid stats. Wanna have both your spell damage types buffed as a moonkin? Nah mate, it's one spell school or the other, or base spell damage.

You get the idea. There's also less sense of preparation and scale when you stock up on Elixirs. Like, it makes sense that you're going to pull out all the stops when you delve into the molten core to take out Ragnaros the Firelord. It's less epic when you walk in, pop your two optimal drinks, eat your food, get some buffs from your buddies, and that's it.

Idk, this really just feels like one of those magical sacred things you shouldn't tamper with that surprisingly few people talk about. Kinda like how shammy and pally should typically remain faction specific or dungeon/raid teleports shouldn't exist. Or like how adding paid convenience features in a game all about tedium and earned progression is bad...

Wait a minute...

Splitting between Battle and Guardian elixirs is actually Vanilla-, not +. Vanilla was known for having a lot of consumables you could use that stack. The only reason some people didn't like that in OG WoW was because Blizzard didn't increase supply of materials for those elixirs like Turtle does. So on crowded servers consumables were very expensive. I think it's still problem on WoW Classic.

With that being said, it's no longer problem on Turtle WoW because supply of those materials was significantly increased. We have cheap flasks, elixirs (compared to Classic or other crowded vanilla private servers). Everyone can afford raiding with most essential consumables.

Also, I'm not afraid of Turtle consolidating these elixirs into 2 categories. If that was the case, why did they add Dreamshard Elixir, Dreamtonic, Danonzo's food? I'm sure they were intentionally added as additional consumables to increase power of your character on top of vanilla consumables.
