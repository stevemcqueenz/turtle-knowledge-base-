# A little bit more challenging.

## Post 12272 by Exizefra — 2021-09-16T08:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12272#p12272 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, dear Turtles! I know that there is a lot of work for a devs team with new large and fantastic update - but i have a one idea that just not let me go, and now, when there is more content to go (specifically a "party content" - new dungeon and raid) i want to tell you about it and read your thoughts.

I think, i'm not make a large mistake if i say that most of the Twow players are familliar with the vanilla WoW. They know the balance, they know the dungeons, they know the tactics, they know the world elite quest (and ignore most of them - i'll tell later, why) - for them it's all the same, the something that they did before, dozens of times. Same bosses, same gear, same feelings. The new world bosses and the upcoming content makes it some different, gives something truly new, yes (hope it will gives even a old Twow Naxx-geared players a worthy challenge - but of course it's the developers to decide, which part of the game it will be) - but i think that there is something could done with and old content to "refresh" it for a WoW veterans.

Just make it harder.

And no, i'm not say it should be a truly hardcore, like...well, on other custom vanilla server (like +30% of health and damage for every elite and rare mob, new abilities for dungeon and raid bosses, more mobs in packs; when raids lasts ilke a 4-6+ hours and you can't go without all the needed flasks, world buffs, best gear and the perfect knowledge about the tactist for every pack). Just make them slightly harder, like +10% of hp and damage, no reduced chance to hit a high-lvl player or something like that - believe or not, but it will return a feeling that "elite quests and dungeons - it's a something serious, we must prepare and don't forget aboul buffs and consumables, it will help us a lot". It gives for a veteran players a feeling that they can't just running through as usual, they must be more careful. But it will not be the unbreakable wall for a new vanilla WoW (or WoW itself) players: yes, it isn't easy, but they knew that vanilla is hard, everyone told about it, so this is not a surprise; some preparations and a experienced player in party (or tries and errors), and we'll make it.

And for a motivation to do the elite quests (especially the world ones - that most of the time are just the usual quests, but against the elite mobs; gives nothing valuable as a reward or exp, and more annoying that interesting, not worth the efforts (no wonder that this is all gone in TBC)) and beat the dungeons i suggest just to give more experience for them. x3 or x5 for example. It will make them feels like something valuable, something special, a good reward for a toughness, especially for a HC players.

Thank you for your attention!
