# Yearly City-Based WPvP Event

## Post 93938 by Treezus — 2024-05-02T20:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93938#p93938 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There should be a 24-hour event, which I'm calling Purge Day tentatively for lack of a better term, where all the guards in every major city disappear and you become flagged when inside a city. You can say they're all off fighting a major battle and now the players themselves have to defend their cities for just that one day.

This would effectively create a day where worldwide city-based WPvP can happen. You would see rogues and druids in every corner, people barricading themselves into the AH and banks, world leaders getting raided more frequently, raids bashing against raids, etc.

This should be warned well ahead of time so HC players can log out, since the cities are basically the only safe place for them. This also gives time for attack and defence groups to form, including creating interesting RP situations for people based around their faction. For example, an RP group that's based around the Scarlet Crusade would see this as their opportunity to go siege Undercity.

The downside is that you may have to disable DKs during this time too. So many civilian NPCs will end up getting caught in the AOE that it would act as a discouraging factor.
