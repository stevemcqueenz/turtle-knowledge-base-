# Survival overhaul

## Post 25196 by Secularis — 2022-09-16T22:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25196#p25196 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, hear me out, got these ideas while playing hardcore and wondered if the profession could be overhauled without too much work. These are just some ideas and wanted to throw them out there.

Firstly, the tent mechanic, for the moment you have to stay under one for around 11-15 minutes to get the full rested xp bonus. Most people just gather at X-roads or goldshire for this.
What if the tent was summoned by the player when going offline, and functions like an inn when laying in it, providing a rest bonus while offline, but way faster (between 4-8 hours). This accomplishes quite a few things:
 1) It incentives all players to get the profession and use it when logging off
 2) It provides an additional incentive to return regularly to play the game
 3) Considerably more flavor as it satisfies the "camping" aspect of the tent
(this mechanic could be implemented in inns as well with a consumable item that is bought from the innkeeper "room key" where you would get faster rested xp in a room inside the inn while offline)

Secondly, campfires, they could be removed completely from cooking and added here, since they are a far more "survivalist" mechanic. Here they could have additional bonuses, simply adding a greater duration to the well fed bonus when eating next to a campfire, as well as perhaps adding 10-20% bonus to said well fed bonus if you eat for the full duration of the food.

Thirdly, this is perhaps a more controversial mechanic, but, while leveling you sometimes get buffed by random people and sometimes you just wished you could increase that ticking timer. For this, I propose 2 variants, either a magical pemmican made by cooking that has a very long eating period (around 10 minutes) that when eaten next to a campfire, it increases the remaining time of class buffs by 2 minutes for every minute spent eating. Said pemmican can even work with a classic levelling scheme, where the first level pemmican works only for buffs level 15 or lower, next one 25 level or lower etc. Or simply adding a slow refresh when sitting next to the campfire.

Fourthly, profession tools.
A portable anvil, made from materials from mining, progressively better, starting significantly worse than other anvils, increasing all actions time by 100%.
Other such objects can be added, with progressive levelling starting worse than what can be found in world.

At the end, some extra tent functionality, or more exactly, tent levels.
Tent level 1: provides rested xp in 8 hours
Tent level 2: provides rested xp in 7 hours also increases spirit by 4 for 1 hour if fully rested
Tent level 3: provides rested xp in 6 hours also increases spirit by 6 for 1 hour and increases mounted movement speed by 20% for for 5 minutes if fully rested
Tent level 4: provides rested xp in 5 hours also increases spirit by 8 for 1 hour and increases mounted movement speed by 30% for 5 minutes if fully rested

And a small final idea for..
A NEW CHALLENGE : Survival mode
(Drinking now provides proportional spirit when drunk for 10 seconds for all classes that don't use mana)
 -1) When not eating for 5 minutes, the peckish debuff appears, reducing max health by a small amount
 -2) When not eating for 10 minutes, the hungry debuff appears, reducing max health by a large amount
 -3) When not eating for 15 minutes, the starving debuff appears, dealing 5% health as damage every 5 seconds

 -1) When not drinking for 5 minutes, the parched debuff appears, reducing max resources (mana, rage, energy) by a small amount
 -2) When not drinking for 10 minutes, the thirsty debuff appears, reducing max resources by a large amount
 -3) When not drinking for 15 minutes, the dehydrated debuff appears, dealing 5% health as damage every 5 seconds
IMPORTANT: when not eating/drinking can only appear when the "well fed" buff ends (or the "satisfied thirst" bonus that gives spirit)

ADDITIONAL CHALLENGE: (could be added to survival mode) No Fly Zone
 -1) No flight masters are available, you must walk or ride everywhere
 -2) Mounted speed is increased by 30%

There’s a couple of ideas here that I like but, as we all have our own opinion on what is best, there are also one or two that I don’t think are particularly easy to include or the right approach. Portable anvils? -no I don't think so.

I’m not the greatest fan of tents tbh - certainly not the way they currently work. However, being able to camp in the wilds when you log off and this to replicate being at an inn for rested xp is a cool idea. I don’t know what the current rate of rested xp ‘outside’ is set at on the server but it will only be a small percentage of the inn rate.

I’d thought about the idea of a hungry debuff and how this could be implemented. Having a necessity to eat feels right to me, but I can’t figure out how a debuff could just appear from thin air. I’m not sure if it’s possible to have a timer on a character that triggers at the appropriate time.

I believe survival as a skill has huge potential and increases immersion, but if it makes the game harder or means more micromanagement, I don’t think there would be many supporters.
