Problems
Arcane spec is way suboptimal for raiding, not offers enough synergies  for the other trees, requires new main damage spell, need new unique way and playstyle add to it.

Solutions
Whole new talents, synergies and added offensive and defensive toolkits. Arcane tree becomes PVE focused DPS and support tree.

New unique arcane spell:    Arcane Blessing

New unique arcane tree mechanism: **Spell Enchanting**

Arcane Power can enchant: Arcane Absorption, Fire ward, Frost Ward, Arcane Barrier. Enchanting the listed spell by casting them first after Arcane Power. After enchanted any of the listed  spell makes Arcane Power loses its spell power increasing part. See these changes detailed later below.

Arcane Blessing

Reducing the next Arcane Lance cast time by 0.5 sec also increasing its damage but increases its mana cost too.
Buff lasts for 12 seconds. Mage default starting at tier 1 (it is default at tier 1) and with talents can unlock next max tier. Highest Tier is 5. Mage has 12 seconds at any tier to advance or drop. If mage cast another Arcane Lance on a target who has Arcane Entanglement debuff during 12 seconds window then advancing to the next tier (If unlocked the next tier with talent points ) After 12 seconds without casting Arcane Lance or Casting Arcane Lance on an enemy, who does not have Arcane Entanglement causing Arcane Blessing goes back to tier 1 so the cast time of Arcane Lance goes back to 3.5 sec aswell. With each new tier modifies the cast time, mana, and damage of next Arcane Lance.

Arcane Lance

Arcane Lance:  3.5 sec cast time (tier 1 Arcane Blessing)  damage 84-119  mana cost: 46 Range: 32 yards range,

Causes **high amount of threat** like Mind Blast. Tier 1 and 2 Blessed Arcane Lance leaves Arcane Entanglement debuff on target

 **Arcane Entanglement**

Lasts for 40 seconds. Unresistable. While attacking this target with Arcane Lance mage can advance to the next Arcane Blessing tier (if unlocked them) If mage tries to cast Arcane Lance on target who does not have Arcane Entanglement debuff his/her Arcane Blessing tier reduced to tier 1 even if it was higher .
If talented  Arcane Initiate with 5 points then when the lance hit, the target it gains also a  Time rift  debuff

Time rift
Increases the target ranged attack and casting speed by 5% and reduces moving speed by 10% for 9 sec. Stacks up 3 times Does not affect lvl 63 Bosses

Maximum possible lowest cast time is 1.5 granted by haste effects It does not have multiple ranks in the spell book, always the highest level will be the only available. The mage still has to go to the trainer to learn the new highest. At every 10th level can learn new one.
