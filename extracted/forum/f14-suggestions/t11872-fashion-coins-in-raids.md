# Fashion Coins in Raids

## Post 78531 by Zeldy (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-24T08:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78531#p78531 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since Fashion Coins can now be obtained from endgame dungeons, it might be nice to include them in raids as well. Perhaps the final raid boss could have a guaranteed drop of 3-5 Fashion Coins that all members of the raid would be allowed to loot without having to roll against up to 39 other players. This would ensure that everyone in attendance would at least get something, even if the item they wanted didn't drop or they have everything they need from the raid already which would incentivize returning.
