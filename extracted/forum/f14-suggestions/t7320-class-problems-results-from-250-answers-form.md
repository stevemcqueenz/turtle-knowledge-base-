In general, I think a lot of these fixes to problems lack both creativity and originality for Druid in particular. People seem to think allowing HoTs to stack would be a fix for stacking Druids in a raid but I disagree because that leads to some big passive overtime healing. With two geared Druids both on one person with stacked HoTs, their combined healing output would be in the ballpark of 1500-1600+ per three seconds just from HoTs alone. People have to consider that with some of Blizzard's decisions, maybe there's a good reason why they forbade such things.

An alternative fix to having more than one Druid in a raid would be to give them more spells. Perhaps some exclusive to Tree form so in an ideal meta you would have one Druid in humanoid form casting its spells and the other as a Tree doing something different. This route potentially fixes two problems on your form rather than just one and is much less of an opening of a can of worms because the repercussions for enabling HoT stacking in PVP haven't been considered either.

On surface letting HoTs stack is a quick and easy fix, but dig deeper though and you start to see the potential problems.

To lay out a few and I will note beforehand that I am one that believes in staying closer to the server being Vanilla first, extras second and primarily as QoL:
- Enabling Druids to HoT a target then DPS with their GCD's. Would be a funner meta for a healer but I don't think raids are tuned to factor much Healer DPS.
