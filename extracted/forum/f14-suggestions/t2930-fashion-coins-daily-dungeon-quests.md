# Fashion Coins/Daily Dungeon Quests

## Post 18263 by Charliedontsurf — 2022-04-20T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18263#p18263 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it would be a good idea to add a daily and maybe even weekly quests for killing the final boss in the lv 60 dungeon content, and the rewards for doing so would be fashion coins this would add a good way for max level players to earn them without relying on collecting arbitrary materials and would stop the meta of people stacking them up at lv 10 whilst its easy, this would also encourage players who no longer have any reason to run dungeon content to go back and tank/heal dungeons for those who do, maybe even tie the quest to using the LFT tool if that would be possible. it could also be beneficial to reward more coins to those who successfully tank or heal a dungeon to get more of those roles in LFT
