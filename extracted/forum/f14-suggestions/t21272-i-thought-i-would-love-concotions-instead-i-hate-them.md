# I thought I would love concotions, instead I hate them

## Post 147560 by Reiker — 2025-08-21T19:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147560#p147560 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since Ret Paladins have to combine AP + SP consumes, they're very often over the visual buff limit of 32, which is annoying because sometimes you need to make gameplay decisions based on what buffs you have - especially since the class revolves around keeping a bunch of temporary buffs up.

Concotions sounded like a great addition to the game since it would free up 3 of my buff slots. It was probably the thing I was looking forward to the most in the update.

The problem is that they made concoctions cost additional herbs to combine (why?) which has resulted in these potions costing 3x the base potions, and the prices aren't coming down.

Paladin was already the most expensive class to play in raids and now it costs way more to play than before the update.

The logical solution is to just use the old cheaper potions and deal with the extra buffs. But we can't even do that, for some reason Turtle WoW made those potions not stack anymore. We have to use the new concoctions, which cost way more than the old potions.

Please change the concoction recipes, or revert this change to 1.17.
