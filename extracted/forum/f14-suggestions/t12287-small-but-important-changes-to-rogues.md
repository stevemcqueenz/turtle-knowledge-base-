# Small but important changes to Rogues

## Post 81803 by Amazonbl4 — 2023-12-19T23:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81803#p81803 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1) Saving combo points on the target.

I am proposing a very important change for rogues.
At the moment, if you have accumulated combo points on someone and changed the target, then when you return to the previous target you lose all the accumulated points.
I suggest saving points for a target if you have not yet started earning points for another target.
This change will allow rogues to use all their capabilities more effectively.
For example, change the target to blind another target, or throw a net at it, and return to the previous target and continue to score points on it. Nowadays rogues don’t do this so as not to lose their combo points.

 2) Expose Armor

This finisher is much less popular among rogues than the others. I suggest changing it up a bit so that it is used more often and so that the rogues has more options on where to spend his combo points depending on the situation, rather than always using the same finishers because they are always better.

Let Expose Armor always reduce armor by the maximum value, and the number of combo points spent on it will affect the duration of the debuff, similar to Slice and Dice.
For example:
 1 point: 12 seconds
 2 points: 16 seconds
 3 points: 20 seconds
 4 points: 24 seconds
 5 points: 28 seconds
