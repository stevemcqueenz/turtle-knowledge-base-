# Choose your Allegiance (Mercenery Mode)

## Post 86065 by Sylveria (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-27T19:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86065#p86065 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So..

I had a talk with someone ingame about the little bug(?), that Alliance players were able to get reputation with horde via the lunar festival coins and vice versa, and that it'd be cool, if there was some sort of glyph, that would allow players to enter the opposite factions cities unharmed for RP purposes...

But then i got the idea of mercenaries:

 ***What if you could choose, if you're loyal to your faction, or a mercenary?***

 **What advantages/disadvantages would it have to be a mercenary?**

+ A mercenary would be able to work for both factions, so they would be able to access quests from the horde and the alliance, so they have more quests available to level up and make money from.
 - a mercenary wouldn't be very trustworthy, so upon the decision to become a mercenary, they would drop with all their factions down to neutral and would not be able to go higher than Friendly. (or they're doomed to stay on Neutral?), so they wouldn't benefits from any rep-discounts and would not be able to get certain rep-rewards. (Which is actually a huge disadvantage)
+ the before mentioned disadvantage might be countered by... like +5% more money from quests(?). 5% doesn't seem much, but you have to remember the fact, that the number of available quests for mercenaries are propably doubled.

 **What about PvP?**

I'm not quite sure how to solve it. When it comes to BG's they would be able to choose their side from the beginning of the match. For OpenWorld i had the idea, that if they're flagged, that they can be attacked by anyone, including fellow mercenaries. So mercenaries would live a dangerous life when it comes to PvP.
