# Increasing Quest Log Cap as a new Donation Reward

## Post 78456 by Korval1 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-23T18:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78456#p78456 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle has added a lot of quality-of-life improvements. However, the number of quests one can carry in their Quest Log remains as it was during vanilla, capped at 20. There may have been technical or hardware reasons for this limit, but today's modern computers, both server and client side, should be able to handle more than 20 quests.

I propose the creation of a pair of donation rewards that increase the cap by 10 and 20. Donation amounts:

+10 Quest Log cap increase (brings the total to 30) = 180 tokens or € 18 or USD$20
+20 Quest Log cap increase (brings the total to 40) = 315 tokens or € 31 or USD$35

Like the other rewards, these would be for a specific character.

Neither of these would makes things unfair. They only provide conveniences.

I hope you'll consider this. I can tell you I would purchase the +20 cap increase. ![🙂](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f642.svg)
