# Catalyzing Poison [New Rogue +Nature/Poison Utility]

## Post 66121 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-09-21T03:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66121#p66121 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is an unfulfilled niche when it comes to utility for the group, that **Rogues** could fill; increasing nature damage.
There is an aspect of the Rogue class that deserves to be more considerable; poisons.
There is a spec that could become worth diving deep into; assassination.
There is a mushroom with a special property that could be discovered during the upcoming events, growing hidden in the deepest recesses of the acursed Silverpine Forest; the Blight Cap.
An opportunity presents itself to the most astute among Rogues.

Suitable name would be **Catalyzing Poison**.

 **Poison applied on the the melee weapon, crafted with the poison profession:**

~ Catalyzing Poison ~
![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/large/ability_rogue_deadlybrew.jpg)
 3 sec cast
Reagents: Blight Cap (a mushroom collected with herbalism), Empty Vial
Coats a weapon with poison that lasts for 30 minutes.
Each strike has a 30% chance of poisoning the enemy, reducing their Nature resistance by 75, increasing Nature damage taken by 10% and Poison damage taken by 25% for 5 minutes. 70 charges.

We have curse of elements, shadow weaving etc.
But nothing boosts Nature.
There could be a Rogue ability or poison that does that, one that boosts both nature spells and poison damage on the target.
This new poison or ability could be either baseline or a deep assassin talent next to Vigor, since Vigor is quite underwhelming, making poison-focused assassin build possible.
It would also make nature damage builds more viable in a group setting, nature damage boost would exist and rogues could have finally some raid utility.
Apart from all that, I'm also in favor of flavor abilities, I think this concept would contribute to the RP aspect of the Rogue class.

Spell Power does not affect Poison damage, as it should be, but if there was a way that only Nature Damage (and Resist) would, it'd make sense. Right now it doesn't either, so the debuff includes separate poison damage increase. Although it's for the better in this case.

Feel free to suggest improvements/changes.
