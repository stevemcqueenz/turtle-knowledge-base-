# The Option for Goblins to become Priests!

## Post 22480 by Vyntoras — 2022-08-15T20:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22480#p22480 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, fellow Turtles!

Today I'm approaching with the idea of giving Goblins the option to become Priests. They have existed in the WoW Lore for ages and Horde only has two races that can opt into becoming a Priest, while Alliance has 4 total, thus adding more variety to both the new race and the Horde itself. I've seen that the numbers are skewed to the Alliance's favour and seeing how Priest is quite the popular class in Vanilla, it would also help balance out the scales between the two factions!

Also, I do believe there is a very fun RP element to playing out a Goblin healer. I know there's a lot of Naysayers that don't want Goblins to be able to heal, but they do exist and you cannot just 'think them away'. There is also a lot you can explore with Goblins on the topic of the two priest racials that they would then be privy to.

I'm hoping that I could at least interest some people in the idea of this with my little text here!   smiling_turtle_head

A couple of points some made earlier in this thread:

 1 - First is the obvious. The idea that every person of an entire race can and must only adhere to a specific stereotype is rediculous whether it be in a video game or real life.

 2 - Every race/culture should have some type of healing/medical class. Before the alliances were formed, how else would each group of "people" tend to their sick or wounded?

 3 - I think people attach to much additional meaning to the idea of light. If it only worked for "the purest" beings, healing wouldn't work on half of the player base. You couldn't heal a warlock, a blood-thirsty warrior, and a shadow priest who embraces the darkness certainly couldn't heal themselves. It makes more sense to look at the light as something that simply represents goodness (life) in opposition to darkness (death). Healing opposes dying regardless of the "worthiness" of the caster or their recipient.

 4 - Any time multiple cultures mix together, they are bound to learn things from each other. Especially if religion is involved as religions tend to follow the concept of desiring to recruit new members, spread their message, etc.
