# Dungeon/Group Finder QoL

## Post 126676 by Trucido — 2025-04-14T01:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126676#p126676 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I wish either of the following QoL changes were implemented to better the experience for Group Finder / Running Dungeons.  Why?  Because it's a huge waste of time trying to get a group to meet up.  This could be better.  I for one don't want to spend 1 hour running to a dungeon and having the entire group fall apart because its so slow getting there.

 1) Summoning stones for Dungeons actually work
 - Also would be huge QoL if only 1 person needed to be there to start summoning other people
 - I would be fine for having to do some sort of quest(s) in order to use them
 - Maybe also make it cost an appropriate amount of silver to summon people

 2) Faster flying speed
 - Would be nice if it didn't take literally 20 minutes to fly from one end of the continent to the other
 - Wouldn't be opposed to an increased cost option for increased speed
 - Maybe also make some more Alliance/Horde based locations to fly to (example is Alliance has a hard time getting to Scarlet Monestary - 20+ minutes from SW)

 3) Portals to Zones that have Dungeons
 - If a zone has a dungeon, a portal to that zone could be available in any major city
 - Maybe if you've 100% discovered the map and completed every quest in a zone the portal becomes available
 - Maybe it costs silver/gold to use depending on the distance from the major city and destination

If you have any other suggestions feel free to add them.  Please don't comment with "AHHHHHH I WANT TO RUN THERE.  BUCK UP BUCKO!" trolling either.  Lets not kid ourselves.  We're mostly gamer dads and old folk who don't really have 30+ minutes per Dungeon Finder / LFG to physically run to the instance.  Its so absurd and outdated, some QoL is necessary.
