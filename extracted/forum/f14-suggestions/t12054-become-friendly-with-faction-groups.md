# Become friendly with faction groups

## Post 79851 by Korval1 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-05T02:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79851#p79851 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Introduction**

Turtle WOW has resolved many "conflicts," such as unified auction houses, multi-faction guilds, and grouping for quests, dungeons, and raids, and we even communicate in a common language (no more "kek" and "bur").   smiling_turtle_head

Could the next step be a "Friend of" reputation system, such as "Friend of the Alliance" and "Friend of the Horde," where players from both factions earn reputation to build relationships with specific groups within the opposing faction?

 **The Lore**

This suggestion is plausible because it occurred in Warcraft lore and aligns with the Turtle team's expansion of dynamic vanilla content. There are numerous instances where members of the Alliance and the Horde either became friendly or maintained friendly relations.

Turalyon and Orgrim Doomhammer
Jaina Proudmoore and Thrall
Rexxar and Theramore
Broxigar and Krasus

 **Implementation**

One way to make this possible could be through reputation quests for each faction group. The objective would focus less on acquiring coins and loot and more on fostering a friendly relationship with a particular faction group. Here are some ideas for a series of quests that opposite faction players can undertake.

Upon accepting the starting quest, players become Ambassadors to safely travel to the quest location and complete their tasks without faction attacks. The pass grants safe access exclusively to the reputation quests, limiting their interaction with the faction to these specific tasks. If a player abandons such a quest, they would have two minutes to either hearth or escape to safety.

Players must be at an appropriate level to access the zone containing the quests; for example, they must reach level 18 for the Night Elf "Forest Restoration" quest in Ashenvale.

 **Outcome**

Becoming friendly with an opposite faction opens up many opportunities:

- **Access to Opposite Faction Quests:** Ability to undertake quests in zones controlled by the opposite faction.
- **Assist Lower-Level Guildmates:** Help lower-level guildmates from the opposite faction with their quests without the risk of being attacked on sight.
- **Enhanced Cross-Faction Cooperation:** Increased opportunities for cooperative gameplay and joint ventures with members of the opposite faction.
- **Safe Passage in Enemy Territory:** Move through areas controlled by the opposite faction without being automatically targeted by guards or hostile NPCs.
- **Cultural Exchange and Learning:** Learn about the lore, culture, and traditions of the opposite faction through direct interaction and quest content.
- **New Trading Opportunities:** Engage in trade and exchange of goods with members of the opposite faction, possibly including unique items or resources.
- **Participation in Opposite Faction Events:** Access to special events, festivals, and celebrations hosted by the opposite faction.
- **Access to Exclusive Areas:** Entry into areas and cities typically restricted to the opposite faction.
- **Unique Role-Playing:** Embrace the roles and customs of the opposite faction to engage in immersive cross-faction storylines and interactions.
