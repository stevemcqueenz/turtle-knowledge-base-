# Give High Elf animations to Highborne Night Elves

## Post 54882 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-27T17:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54882#p54882 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since there is a remainder of Highborne descendants in the Dire Maul dungeon, it would be cool if the night elf animations they currently have could be swapped out with the high elf/blood elf animations while retaining the night elf model.
This would give them a distinction from the typical night elves we see throughout the world, as well as allude to the fact that the Highborne eventually descended into High Elves

## Post 54910 by Galendor (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-28T07:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54910#p54910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Well, high elves are descendants of highborne followers of Dath'remar. If we consider RPG info about their lifespan, there were approximately twenty generations of elves since then. No wonder high elves are very different from night elves.

On the other hand, Shen'dralar are not Highborne descendants; they are literally those Highborne who lived here before the Sundering. They were immortal due to demonic powers they absorbed. So, if lore team wouldn't do some Retail shenanigans, there's no reason for Shen'dralar elves to be too different from night elves.
