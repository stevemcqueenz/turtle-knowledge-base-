Hi, I'm business analyzer, and noticed that you can wastly improve people willingness to donate by, during donation, allowing them to chose toward which project their money is going.

We all know that each project requires time and money. And by listing your plans "in project" and adding some monetary value to it, (for example, to open the gates of Gilneas, or new server, or new soundtracks) you are actively motivating all players to donate and help develop the server.

The players win, Turtle wins.
