# High level quests for high level players on lower level zones

## Post 138880 by Ogreman999 — 2025-07-12T21:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138880#p138880 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just some suggestions for 'evergreen' content without lazily scaling everything back like they did in retail

For example:
>Duskwood Kharazan related quests
>just some simple kill quests related to lower level zones; some stuff to Desolace, maybe with RFD and the centaur necros, badlands dwarves, hinterland trolls, etc from NPCs in relevant places
>Some version of the heroic board from shattrath but with some goblins wanting 'rare resources' from low lvl dungeons
>...
Now, I could go forever, but WoW is not horizontally progressive, not when you hit 60 and raidlog. I'm suggesting for solo or small partyable, easily digestible side content that you can do for the appropriate rewards of revisiting old zones and interacting with the levellers.
The way I see it there's little incentive for a 60 to go to the troll city in hl, but adding some incentive, like a small trinket that summons a flametongue totem guardian (balance excluded) would be fun.
