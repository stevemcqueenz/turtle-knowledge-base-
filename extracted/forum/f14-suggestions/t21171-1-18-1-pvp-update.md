# 1.18.1 PVP Update

## Post 147038 by Alcilis — 2025-08-19T16:24:00Z

[viewtopic.php?t=19804](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=19804)

The entire pvp community is currently waiting for the pvp update and the announced changes coming for the 1.18.1 patch.

As a former pvp hardcore-player and an all-times Vanilla pvp enjoyer (playing Vanilla wow pvp for 20 years), here's my opinion regarding the following announced changes and how they should be implemented.

About the honor rework :

 - **Change the formula a little, having capped honor-milestones instead of the old Contribution Point system.**
Instead of being dependent of other players investment to be able to progress at higher ranks, have it be a capped (yet still considerable) grind.
For instance, at R10-11 you'd need to do at least 50k honor per week to progress a little bit towards to higher rank, at R11-12 75k, R12-13 125k, R13-14 175k. (To be tweaked, obviously)
If you do at least these numbers you'll keep progressing little by little, and if you do more you'll obviously progress faster.
 - **Remove all decay, even inside the current rank, to make it doable by players who can't always grind every week.**
This very simple modification would change the entire way we approach the grind : we'll no longer have to invest several consecutive weeks full-time in order to achieve the next rank, allowing us to grind the weeks we can without seeing our entire progression ruined if we can't play the week after.
Removing decay to previous ranks was a good first step, but let's push it to the next step removing all decay, we're no longer the 15 years old students with too much time to spend we were 20 years ago.

About rated arenas :

 - Why not, but **be careful in doing so that it doesn't remove the essence of Vanilla pvp to switch towards TBC pvp**...
To prevent this :
 - Make it so **rated arenas only gives exclusive titles and cosmetics** to distinguish the strongests amongst us, **NOT** stronger gear than the pvp ranking gear (unlike TBC).
To keep the core of Vanilla pvp, **the pvp top-ranks gear should be an overall top-tier gear able to fight even the strongest players and monsters.**
After all, it's the exclusive gear reserved for the greatest champions of the Horde/Alliance, and as such it should be the best the faction has to offer !
Which leads us to the next point.

About the pvp itemization update :

 - R7-10 gear should be updated to be equivalent to the current R12-13 gear, as it's already quite a grind to get to R10 in a meta where a pve player can already be full T2.5 in half that play time.
 - **Solid weapons, around 80 item level, should be obtainable at R11 to help pushing towards higher ranks.**
 - R12-13 gear should be updated to end-game level gear.
 **-> Each time a new pve endgame tier-set appears, we should be able to upgrade our pvp gear to a new updated version**, either through gold or through BG marks (or adding a new honor-based currency, which would be ideal but maybe more difficult to put into place).
 - **R14 weapons should at all times be the best weapons in the game in terms of base stats.**
As it was during 95% of Vanilla. They are the most time-consuming items to get in the game, and as such they should be the most rewarding to get.
They should also be updated and upgradeable each time a new pve tier appears.
 - Level 60 pvp reputation rewards should also be reworked in order to provide really solid epic off-sets items instead of shitty 3/4 blue sets as it stands now for BR and AB...
 - **Add solid necklaces, rings, capes, bracers, belts,... All the pieces of gear that aren't part of the pvp rank set, so pvp truly becomes a solid alternative gearing path indeed.**
 - **Please change the bonus on the pvp gloves (R7/12) for paladin**, as improved jugement on crusader seal is useless in pvp...
And overall, **rework all the specific pvp gear-sets bonuses to make them specially appealing in terms of pvp QoL for each classes.**
 - **Add spec-related variants for the pvp gear, as it was done for the pve tier sets.**
This should have been done at the same time the variants were introduced for the classic pve tier sets, and it would do much in favour of balancing gearing paths.

About other pvp changes that should be done :

 - **Balance AV.**
Simply (greatly) reducing the numbers of guards and fixing the archers' LoS in FWK would do much in favour of balancing things a bit.
Horde can jump from the bridge to the first DB tower without aggroing a single npc, while alliance as to aggro 20+ npcs and archers to get to the first FW tower...
 - Increase the amount of carriable BG marks to 100 (because... why not ?)
 - **Either remove Sunny Glade Valley from the daily pvp rotation, or add a specific pvp reputation linked to it, with specific pvp-oriented trinkets as rewards** (inspired by the trinkets found inside the BG, just a thought) to give it some appeal.
 - **Add a "Report AFK" button** when right-clicking a player from your team, that instantly kicks the said player with a regular deserter debuff after 3 separate reports.
Put in place a counter that resets each month, whenever someone gets kicked from reports his counter increases (1 strike). After 3 strikes he gets a 24h deserter debuff, 5 strikes 1 week, 10 strikes 1 month and then the counter goes back to 0.

 - New Battleground.
Great news, and this should be an opportunity to add a new pvp reputation to grind (unlike Sunnyglade in its current state), with solid off-set items filling gaps in the pvp gearing path, and new marks to turn in.

 - New Open-World pvp events.
Great news again. In my opinion, to give life to open-world pvp, **zones with pvp objectives (EPL, Silithus,... OR new specific zones) should be made into open-pvp war zones** where you can't untag pvp (unless you are grouped with members of the opposite faction, in which case you can't partake in the pvp objectives).
Remember there's a war going on.

 **Nothing too difficult to do, only simple adjustments that would give pvp a second birth.**
