Aight, listen, I am sure that the devs dont want to take ideas from SoD, but god, warlock tanking was the most seamless feeling spec update in that entire thing. It felt like it shouldve have been an intended spec for the last 20 years of the games existence. Besides, we have a lot of defensively itemized cloth gear (like that silly cloth helmet with +14 defense), but no real spec to take advantage of any of it. And theres always a tank shortage, so many groups fall apart due to not being able to find one or two, having an extra legitimate tank spec in the game gives everyone more flexibility and options for putting a group together.

While were at it, please give ENH a talent for a DR cooldown, maybe make the taunting totem give a damage reduction to the shaman while it's standing. It's truly the last thing shammies need to have a functional tank spec for raiding.
