# New priest talent changes suggestions for 1.18 and beyond

## Post 144834 by Keds (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-12T14:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144834#p144834 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- This is my suggestion for 1.18 or later, all numbers can be adjusted.
 - Highlighted talents are the ones that i've changed.
 - You can view or calculate all talent trees here: [https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c ... HS8X5BKIj7](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator?t0=nAl0vFZO9P&t1=mSl10EkWkN&t2=HS8X5BKIj7)

 ****Priest:****
 - Holy Nova is now baseline. Learned at level 20.

 ****Discipline:****
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/XwXQJCo.png)

 **- Talent positioning:**
Just with position, It would better benefit other specs as well, as well as removing 2 PvP talents on the same row, while also helping shadow getting it's first row talent to reduce threat since Mental Agility and Piercing Light doesn't benefit Shadow, wand specialization doesn't scale with level, so basically, a level 60 shadow priests doesn't have ANY good talents in the first row of any tree.

 **- (New)Blazing Light:**
 **The light set your Holy Nova ablaze, causing the enemies affect by your Holy Nova to take aditional 4% increased damage from your Holy Nova spell. Stacks up to 5 times.**

 ****Holy:****
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/KEC5hRh.png)

 **- Holy Reach:**
Now increases the range of Divine Nova instead of Holy Nova(Disc Spell).

 **- (New)Divine Nova:**
 **Transforms you Holy Nova into Divine Nova, Causing a explosion of divine light around the caster, healing all party members within 15 yards for 69 to 76, and refreshes the duration of your renew on affected targets.** Increased range, heal and refreshes the duration of the renew on party members, but no longer damages.

 **- Reservoir of Light:**
Now also increases Lightwell's duration by 30/60/90 seconds.

 ****Shadow:****
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/50nvBrE.png)

 **- Improved Mind Blast:**
Now moved to the first row, increases the talent usability without inferring in other aspects, or the weird 2/5 or 4/5 builds.

 **- Blackout:**
Removed, this talent has it's controversies, on coordinated gameplay, it's a liability if this procs and the diminishing returns messes with another person combo. More on this below where we can give SPriests a stun back.

 **- Shadow Focus:**
Now has additional 25% chance to avoid interruption while casting shadow spells because Shadow lacks ANY pushback reduction, would also be nice since the priest max cast range now would be 30 yards, more on this below aswell.

 **- Changed Shadow Reach to Improved Mind Flay:**
Increase the range of Mind Flay by 5/10, now it's 30 yards just like all the other priest spells, to compensate this, it also has a drawback that reduces the slow by 10/20% so it doesn't affect the PvP aspect too much.

 **- Improved Fade:**
Now also removes GCD from it, talking to other priests, even with t3.5, using fade is a DPS loss because of it, and this talent is very underused/underwhelming.

 **- Improved Mana Burn:**
 **Reduces the casting time of your Mana Burn spell by 0.5/1.0 sec and increases the amount of Mana burnt by 5/10%.** This is to compensate the loss of 6 yards on other spells in PvP, and also increase the talent usefulness. Since almost all classes now does huge dps in PvP, taking your time to mana burn someone is worse than just killing your target.

 **- (New)Consuming Shadows:**
 **Consumes your holy power, transforming your Holy Nova into Shadow Nova, that causes an explosion around the caster, causing 86 to 99 Shadow damage to the caster and to all enemy targets within 10 yards, and heals party members based on a % of the damage dealt.** Each Priest spec would have it's own, unique version of Holy Nova, because using Holy Nova in Shadow Form feels wrong and putting points into the holy tree feels terrible, as shadow doesn't benefit from basically anything. This change make it so it benefits from the shadow talent tree as well.

 **- Shadow Weaving:**
 **Your single target Shadow damage spells have a 33/66/100% chance to cause your target to be vulnerable to Shadow damage.  This vulnerability increases the Shadow damage dealt to your target by 5% and lasts 15 sec. Stacks up to 3 times.** Now has 3 ranks, and increases 5% of shadow damage per stack, and stack sized reduced from 5 to 3.

 **- (New)Painspeaker:**
 **Reduces the cooldown of Pain Spike by 4/6/8 seconds, and gives 100% chance that your Pain Spike stuns the target for 1/2/3 seconds, but Pain Spike deals damage dealt to the caster over the next 2/4/6 seconds. (This damage doesn't kill the caster.)** I like the idea that shadow priest has self sustain, and making it so this spell damages the priest makes a nice synergy with it's theme, while also helping the priests with CC's in PvP, and gives the ability for priests to stun back since the removal of blackout.

 **- (New)Surge of Shadows:**
 **While in Shadowform, casting speed increase effects increases the tick speed of your damage over time spells and the tick speed of your Mind Flay, reducing it's duration.** New Idea to make shadow priests benefit from haste like all other casters, this is even more important here since shadow priests doesn't benefit from crit as much as other classes, I don't get why shadow priests is the only caster that doesn't benefit from it.

I would like to add two suggestions to improve discipline priest playstyle (done in a previous post) to consolidate different posts here. In my view discipline priests still need some changes to make the rotation more interesting and deal with mana problems in a way that is dependent on ability usage (i.e. skill).

 **Changes to Enlighten:**

After choosing the talent, the priest builds up “Enlightening Discipline” charges whenever he casts Holy Fire, Smite, Holy Nova, or Power Word: Shield. At 5 charges the priest can use the “Enlighten” ability. Using the ability consumes all 5 charges and restores 2% of the priest’s mana pool.

“Enlighten”: Burns the target for 4% of their health over 2 seconds. Enlightens the target increasing both the priest’s and the targets spell damage and healing by 5% for 14 seconds. If used on the priest spell damage and healing is increased by 7% instead. Instant cast without GCD.

Optional idea: Add different “Enlighten” abilities such as “Enlighten Body”, “Enlighten Spirit”, etc. with other effects. For instance, a movement speed increase for 20% for 4 seconds, mana regen, etc.

 **Explanation:**
- Building charges and adding a usable ability adds things to do and removes another “boring 30 minute buff”.

- Enlighten abilities off global cooldown means that priests can in fact use them during fights.

- 2% mana restore effect when consuming the “Enlightening Discipline” charges helps with mana but only if you actually use the abilities.

- Decreased damage buff from 10% to 5% (15% to 7%) to reduce random burst in PVP and account for higher up time.

- Increased duration from 8 to 14 seconds to force priests to sometimes use the buff on multiple other players as 5 charges can hypothetically be build up in under 14 seconds during e.g. Smite spam.

- Adding other “Enlighten” abilities would add further variety. Movement speed e.g. would help tanks pull, mages kite, or pvp group members move, but at the cost of 5 to 7 % dps.

- Making the 4% burn a DOT that ticks over 2 seconds avoids spell pushback.

- The sound effect of Enlighten is AMAZINGLY SATISFYING. We would get to hear it more often.
 **Changes to Chastise:**

I propose 3 changes…
- Remove GCD: This will make it worth using in fight as you don’t loose (smite) casting time.

- Remove damage when used on enemies completely: Nerf to reduce burst in PvP.

- Change damage on friendly units to a DOT that ticks over 2 seconds: Avoids spell push back when used on caster friendlies.
