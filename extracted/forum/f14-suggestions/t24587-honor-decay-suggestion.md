Actually, studies have shown that they ARE mutually exclusive. The introduction of an extrinsic reward structure has been proven to reduce or even outright eliminate intrinsic enjoyment of an activity.

In other words: when people are motivated by a reward, and then they get the reward, they’re unlikely to continue to do the activity once they get the reward.

In this case, people chasing pvp gear or a title will probably stop pvping once they actually get it. Ask yourself, how many ACTUAL r14 players do you see in bgs, compared to r13 or r12 players? Its pretty low. Once people hit r14 and got that gear? No reason to keep playing, and they stopped.

Same goes for arena awards. I played with multiple people who outright said, as soon as they got the tokens they needed for that recipie or whatever they were after, they were gone.

Transients like that, who arent actually interested in pvp, do nothing but reduce the overall value of the pvp experience.
