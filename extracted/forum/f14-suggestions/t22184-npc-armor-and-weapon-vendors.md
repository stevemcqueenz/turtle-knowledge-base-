I agree that vendor items need to be changed - they are completely useless when you can loot better green or blue items by just questing and not buying from the AH or crafting. Those items need to have some actual advantages over other items, maybe they aren't magical items that give you better stats, but they should have something, like double or triple durability or more damage and armor, or all of that. This gear needs to be made a bit cheaper too, but also have clear benefits over magical gear.
