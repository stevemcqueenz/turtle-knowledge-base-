# "spell coefficient penalty at low levels"

## Post 154364 by Drb — 2025-10-04T20:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154364#p154364 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello everyone,

I’d like to talk about **spell power** and the spell **coefficients** associated with it.
I noticed that the coefficients are not the same as on WoW Classic released in 2019.
There was a rule called "*spell coefficient penalty at low levels*" which was removed in 2019 when the Classic servers opened by Blizzard, because this rule didn’t really have any valid reason to exist.

On WoW Turtle there are plenty of new zones to explore, which is awesome to discover. Along with that, new quests and new items. Some of these items give powerful stats that are very useful both for leveling and for PvP.

But here’s the thing… there is a HUGE difference between casters and melees. On one side, you can get an item that gives 9 agi, 5 strength and 4 stamina, and on the other side we got legs who give 6 intellect and 7 spell power.
Translation: that’s 1.2% crit, 10 attack power (0.71 DPS) and 40 hit points versus 60 mana and +3 damage on a spell.
There are tons of examples like this.

The problem is that spell power at this level just cannot compete with the stats melee classes get.

I pushed the analysis further by comparing two level 19 twinks, one being a warrior and the other a warlock. The warrior is considered one of the weakest classes of this bracket.
For a level 19 warlock with 130 spell power, Shadow Bolt rank 3 (52 to 61 base, 2.8 sec cast) deals around 100–120 damage. That’s a coefficient of 50% instead of 86% like in “Classic 2019.”
The warlock, without buffs or consumables, reaches about 80 DPS free casting without moving.
The warrior, without buffs or consumables, does 120 DPS, while being free to move.

The warlock: 1406 HP, 1417 mana, BiS gear (Lucky Hat, Lucky Boots).

The warrior: 2100 HP, BiS gear too.

Keep in mind that the twink example is just there to highlight the issue, and their DPS doesn’t reflect the DPS of regular levelers—it’s meant to show the clear difference between casters and melee

Big balance problem between gear and nerfed spell coefficients. There are basically **no viable casters** before level 30–40 because it’s just too weak.

I’d like to see the same coefficients as on WoW Classic, at least for leveling, to compensate for the stat gap in quest rewards And about the items obtained from dungeons. Also see more items giving spell power for leveling.

Thank you very much for reading. Maybe this topic has already been discussed, this is my first post on the forum. Please be kind and constructive if you have any comments to share

DRB

My third attempt to answer this topic on the merits.
It is not clear what DRB proposes.
I assume there is two options:
 1. Increase spell power on low level gear
 2. Increase coefficients for low level spells

How point 1 will affect levelers?
No idea, I doubt they ever get enough gear with spell power at all. There are two reliable sources of gear for normal levelers: quest rewards and crafts. Like 90% of gear is int with some other stat like spirit, agility, stamina or whatever.
Additional damage will be less than random variance of damage of each spell. Wand will still be better option, because it also doesn't need mana to damage.

Low level gear can be overhauled to give more SP. Like "if rank 3 frostbolt have 30% SP coefficient, then increase all SP on gear for like 50% to compensate". It will lead to situation when SP gear for lvl 15 will have higher or equal SP than gear for lvl 30, because SP coefficient of rank (whatever) frostbolt available at 30 lvl is higher - gear at lvl 30 should provide less SP to provide same damage.

How point 1 will affect twinks?
Glass cannons will replace raw +damage gear with mix of damage+spell power. They will be more beefy.
TBH, I rarely see caster twinks now on bgs except priests.
> **Springboards wrote: Sun Oct 05, 2025 2:45 am**
> im talking max glass cannon 6 shadow wrath knife/7 offhand, 7 dmg belt, 11 dmg chest , gnoll gloves, 6 dmg bracer 7 dmg cloak , those 7 dmg pants, 9 dmg boots

It is like 6+7+7+11+5+6+7+7+9=65 damage only from gear.
Meanwhile, weapon enchant gives 30SP, glove enchant gives 20SP, ring enchants give like 12 (I'm not sure), naxx enchant provides a bit too. I mean, gear itlsef provide only half of potential power.
Characters that are already beefy will probably stay this way.

How point 2 will affect levelers?
Assuming that sp gear is rare, they will probably get no benefit at all. Wanding is still much better and easier.

How point 2 will affect twinks?
Glass cannons will surely do more damage and become more viable.
Beefy twinks with sp enchants will become more dangerous too.

BUT point 2 will also affect high levels.
Reason why low level spells have lower coefficients is because high levels used to collect lots of +damage gear and downrank their spells. Low level spells also have lower cast time. Like famous Rank 1 Frostbolt.

It is possible to make exception like "low level spells have high coefficient only if you are low level yourself". But it will lead to another gear overhaul, which is time consuming and result is unclear. What exactly will be better if casters get more SP?
Levelers don't need that SP. They infact can level in white gear without much problems. Gear helps to reduce downtime and allow to make bigger pulls.
Will more SP on gear lead to bursty meta like it is on lvl 60 now? Does it actually feel good to play pvp at 60?
