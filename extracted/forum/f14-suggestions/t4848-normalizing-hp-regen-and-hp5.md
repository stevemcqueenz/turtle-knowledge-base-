# Normalizing HP regen and HP5.

## Post 29171 by F41lz0rs (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-14T13:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29171#p29171 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Current system inplace is no natural HP regen happens during combat. Trolls gain 10% incombat.
Each class  gains a diffrent amount of HP and MP regen from spirit
Spirit is basicly non-exisitant from all melee gear after 58.
HP/5 on gear is so low that it doesnt even matter if you have any or not.

Proposed changes.

Class based HP regen rates removed and based 100% by Race.
AND all players regen 10% of spirit regen in combat same as the trolls racial now.

Humans,   1(Spirit)+4       ///                 Orc, 1(spirit)
Dwarf,      1(spirit)+5             ///           Tauren, 1(spirit)+2
Night elf    1(spirit)+7           ///            Undead, .8(spirit)
Gnome,    .8(spirit)            ///              Goblin, .8(spirit)
High Elf,   1(spirit) +6          ///          Troll,   2(spirit)+1   (troll racial regeneration removed, replaced by 30% reduced
bleed times)

The main proponent of this change is to boost HP/5 stats already on gear.  A  human player with 100 spirit would regen out of combat 104hp/2, and 10.4hp/2 in combat.  this gives this player a base hp/5 equivilant of close to 25hp/5. booosting the effect of spirit on leveling gear till 60.

Troll racial be changed as the new system witht the same stats of  100 spirit would have any troll player healing 201 hp out of combat and 20.1hp/2 in combat for a hp5 equivilant of  almost 50hp5.

these changes would also aid in the benifit of adding HP/5 and spirit stacking tank gear for Shamans if they were to add a few sets of mail that focus on self healing as mitigation.  with a focus on Earth shield.
