# Increased reach for Polearms

## Post 14609 by Silverleafeon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-12-17T04:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14609#p14609 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It is my understanding that in the Beta for Vanilla WoW, polearms had an increased reach of +5, but the idea was scrapped because it would have given Tauren a huge advantage, as they already had a +5, which would have given them a +10 total. Very OP.

Do Tauren still have the longer reach advantage in Turtle WoW? If not, I think it would be awesome to implement the original idea of polearms being long reaching. It definitely makes sense from an immersion perspective, since, well, they're really long. Polearms right now are rather sub-par weapons, so this would give people more options in their weapon choice.

What do you all think?

It's unfortunate that I would have to sacrifice DPS in order to achieve this though, since compared to other 2 handed weapons, polearms are kinda terrible.

As an example from <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?items=2.6#0-4+1>

Top 3 DPS 2H Axes: 86.6 (speed 3.5), 81.8 (speed 3.6), 77.4 (speed 3.8)

Top 3 DPS 2H Maces: 95.3 (speed 3.8), 80.4 (speed 3.6), 77.4 (speed 3.8)

Top 3 DPS 2H Swords: 90 (speed 3.6), 81.9 (speed 3.5), 81.9 (speed 3.2)

Top 3 DPS Polearms: 85 (speed 3.7), 77.4 (speed 3.8), 77.4 (speed 3.8)

The highest DPS Polearm isn't awful, but it isn't great either. But then after that, there is a sharp decrease in polearm viability compared to other weapons. They're slower for the most part too (would expect the opposite).

Also, these are just the top 3 of each category (minus legendary weapons). If you look at the link, there are a lot of good to decent Axes, Maces, and Swords as you go down the list... but that's not the case for polearms.

This is why I think polearms need to be buffed a little bit, and I think longer reach than other weapons would be a good way to do it   smiling_turtle_head
