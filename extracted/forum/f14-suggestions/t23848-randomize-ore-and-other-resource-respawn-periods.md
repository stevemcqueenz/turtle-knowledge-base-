# Randomize ore and other resource respawn periods

## Post 167327 by Fluffbucket — 2026-02-03T16:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167327#p167327 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Explanation:**
I was making an iron mining runs on Hillsbrad foothills but for the second day I could not find any decent amounts of it, even in the morning times. Decided to simply stay and camp in a spot where I knew for sure that some iron was spawning to see what's up.

Well and I found out that few seconds before ore spawns, a guy logs in and go precisely to the spot.
He knew exactly where it will spawn and when, as soon as he mined one another node would appear and he would already be there. And this went for hours.

That was last week. He is still going today. Even more, I noticed another of his characters, one logs off, another logs in  at another spot, like a clock, meaning he is expanding his enterprise.

Anyway, this makes extremely hard to make regular mining runs and in my opinion quite unfair against other players since basically he synced ore re-spawns to his clock.

 **Suggestion:**
I suggest making re-spawn periods random. Now they are 30mins exactly, if nodes where to re-spawn at random, between 20-40 min it would make this exploit much less viable and give fair chance for other players.
