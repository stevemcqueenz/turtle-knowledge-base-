Greetings Twow team and fellow Ambershire players,

Twow recently posted the upcoming PvP changes for 1.18.1 and it has sparked heated discussions over twow discord / ingame.

The issue I'd like to address here is that they plan to remove the old PvP sets upon the release of new PvP sets.

This make sense for servers like Telabim & Nordanaar but it does not make sense for Ambershire since a lot of players that are above R11 have been waiting for the current PvP sets to be released since Novemeber, 2025 (for my case).

Please consider to keep the sets available for purchase after the patch release for a period of time so we could claim the rewards that we've been putting effort into.

Thank you !
