# PVP trinket remove all CC

## Post 10461 by Peevpee — 2021-05-07T14:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10461#p10461 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I wanted to suggest to adopt the PVP style trinket that makes it remove all CC instead of making them class specific to certain kinds of CC. This will allow the trinket to be actually useful against all classes instead of only against certain ones as I'm sure there are classes that are tired of being stunlocked or seduce spammed to death where your trinket just doesn't remove that type of CC.
