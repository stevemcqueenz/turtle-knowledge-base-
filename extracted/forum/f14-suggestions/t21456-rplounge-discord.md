## Post 148730 by Daromath — 2025-08-27T18:43:00Z

Hi, forums.

I personally think the TWoW Discord rp-lounge channel is a bit redundant with some of the questions asked there, typically regarding:

 - server health
 - RP vibe / how the community is?
 - is Alliance or Horde more popular?
 - how often is RP actually happening?
 - is RP here mostly guild-oriented?
 - are walk-ups typically okay?
 - where are common RP hubs?

The list here could go on for a little while, but I think I've made my point. Some of this discussion is meaningful, new, and very opinionated such as whether people are heading to Ambershire server for RP or staying on Nord, or maybe where their new character idea would best fit and with which guild, but the ones listed above are pretty objective and have at least somewhat of an agreed upon answer/consensus.

Could we move/add these new FAQs to the rp-information section? I think it'll keep discussion fresh, and possibly be a more open space to talk about in-character happenings and specifics with RP on the server, rather than the same repeated questions day in and day out.
