# QoL change for a few consumables (Stacking/cooldowns/persisting through death)

## Post 47002 by Tawneyturtle (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-05-22T12:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47002#p47002 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Inspired by the qol change to E'kos, here are a few consumables that went under the radar and what I'd request for them as a change:

 1. Zanzas.

Problem: All 3 types are capped at Unique 5. This feels outdated and tedius having to run/swim to the island before every raid to stack up on these.

Suggestion:  Make Zanzas not be capped at 5 for the sake of both convenience of not having to go to STV just for 5 stacks and helping progression guilds that have more than 5 attempts with them on hard bosses weekly.

 2. Blasted Lands consumables.

Problem: Blasted lands as a questing zone is sought after for it's nice mob density and chance of getting free greens from the turn in quest. However the Blasted lands consumables make Blasted Lands be quite an unpleasant experience as lvl 60 hardcore raiders bully low level players out of mob tags for the sake of their consumables. Not only that but the items themselves have a 60 minute duration AND cooldown. The workaround for this is that people log in 1 hour before raid starts, use a R.O.I.D. or any other BL consume, and then log out for an hour. This let's the CD on the item tick down but the buff stays on the players. This design is rather degenerate and reminds me of pre Chronoboon world buff raiding where people just stayed offline to not lose buff times before raids.

Suggestion: Make Blasted Lands buffs simply persist through death similar to flasks and increase their duration to 2 hours. Both to remove the need for players to bother low level players leveling as often and the logging off to get another usage trend.

 3. Weapon Oil

Problem: Shaman windfury/flametongue doesn't stack with weapon oils/poisons, this makes swapping players between groups with and without shamans or tanks swapping between dual wield/wep+shield annoying as you waste a shaman totem/oil/poison.

Suggestion: Change endgame oils/poisons to be a buff you can apply to your character over your weapon that will affect your current equiped weapon for the duration regardless of what weapon you swap to, and it'd get overwritten by Windfury/flametongue and removed.
