# Defense Check for NPC Stuns & Disarms

## Post 166968 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-30T18:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166968#p166968 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Some NPC abilities like stuns and disarms seem to be fully unresistable, or only have a low base chance to be resisted, regardless of player level, gear, or defense. This leads to situations where a much higher-level, well-geared character can be reliably stunned or disarmed by a low-level mob, which breaks the sense of progression and immersion.

Thats why i wanna suggest to introduce a resistance check for these abilities.

Either add a level-based check, similar to other resists, where large level differences reduce the chance for the ability to land.

Or treat these as physical control effects and scale their success chance against the player’s Defense skill (and possibly Parry/Dodge), so investment in gear, level and talents matters.
