## Post 129322 by Gattare — 2025-05-02T00:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129322#p129322 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please excuse my poor English, I wrote this using a translator because something has to be said.
I've been playing Twow since 2021, at the same time, I am also a practitioner in the mobile gaming industry, and I want to point out some things that I am very worried about recently.
I really like their idea of ​​perfecting the vanilla content and returning the overall style to Warcraft 3. Ambition after success is understandable, but it should not breed blindness. In my opinion, CC is the only concern I have about Twow right now.
My overall opinion of CC1 is positive, but starting with CC2, we can see the idea has clearly deviated from "patching up imperfect old talents to make them playable" to "I don't mind modifying any existing mature mechanisms to **redefining** them." As a result, look at the forums, many class section are filled of wailing, and people aren't wailing because the new stuff twow designed is boring, they're wailing because many old mechanics they've been used to and loved for decades no longer exist.I understand that improvement is based on change, but what we old boys want is improvement, not revolution, and what turtle team has done now has exceeded this line.
 **Simply put, we're not against new things, we hail the efforts to save unpopular talents and those improvements to Qol, but please leave the things we've always enjoyed alone - When your primary user base is people over 30, financially independent and under life stress, the last thing you want to do is turn their favorite whiskey into coke.**
This is why I feel a sense of crisis about Twow now, because unlike other game content, class change is extremely controversial in any online game, and can easily cause a game with excellent foundations to fall quickly (we have seen a lot of this, haven't we). And turtle team obviously has not proved that they have a mature class mechanism development team, and this shortcoming is ruining the entire work.
I love Turtle WoW, and I hope they continue to care about the voice of the players. Blizzard has long fallen into blindness and arrogance, and I hope Turtle doesn't fall into that same trap.
