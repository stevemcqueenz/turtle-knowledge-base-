# Suggestion: Improving the Turtle WoW Database

## Post 119828 by Leodimas — 2025-02-13T11:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119828#p119828 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtle WoW team,

First of all, thank you for all the hard work you put into maintaining and improving this amazing server. I’d like to suggest some improvements for the Turtle WoW Database, as it currently feels quite limited in terms of usability and functionality.

Suggested Improvements:

More Search Filters – Right now, searching for specific items, NPCs, or quests can be a bit tedious. Adding filters like item level, rarity, NPC type, and spell effects would make searching much more efficient.

User Comments – One of the best features of databases like Wowhead or the old Thottbot was the ability to leave and read comments. This allows players to share useful tips, hidden quest details, drop rates, or optimal farming locations.

Better UI/UX – A more user-friendly design, with improved navigation and sorting options, would make the database much easier to use.

Drop Rate and Loot Tables – Some items and mobs currently lack detailed drop information. A clearer display of drop rates and loot tables would be extremely helpful for players looking to farm specific items.

I believe these additions would greatly enhance the database and make it a much more valuable tool for the community. Are there any plans for such improvements in the future?

Looking forward to hearing your thoughts!

Meandros
