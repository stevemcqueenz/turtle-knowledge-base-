# Feral Druid Talent to replace MCP

## Post 38753 by Sovietpremier — 2023-03-23T20:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38753#p38753 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Farming MCP or getting the engineer MCP is annoying for Cat druid, and just poor game design. I doubt the original WoW devs intended for level 60 druids to farm a blue Gnomeregan weapon to be raid competitive. Id suggest buffing Berserk to the following:

Berserk:
Removes all Fear effects and increases your energy regeneration rate by 100%, attack speed by 50% while in Cat and Bear form, and increases your total health by 20% in Bear form. After the effect ends, the health is lost. Effect lasts 20 seconds.

Then make MCP not work in druid forms. This isnt overpowered because talent change or no talent change, a feral druid will have a 50% increased attack speed in raids if they are dedicated since they will have an MCP on them. This just removes the annoyance of having to farm for them or the engineer batteries.
