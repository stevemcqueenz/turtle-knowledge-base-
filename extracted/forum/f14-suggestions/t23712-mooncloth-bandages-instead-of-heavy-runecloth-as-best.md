The idea of a bandage providing Mana and/or in addition to health is really good. A cooldown not impossible.

Maybe there could be even multiple different versions of this. Possibly some that are only BoP and have a cooldown. A mana aiding bandage accessible to everyone via Enchanting & Trading is something that is also really needed.

Imho, it is a valuable addition for a couple of reasons:
 - Enchanters get something for making gold from that leftover Dust and the omnipresent Runecloth in their bags
 - More Bagspace and less clean up compared to used mana flasks breaking ;)
 - More people skilling up First Aid for the base material bandage
 - More lower Humanoid areas & dungeons with lots of Runecloth visited
 - Impact on prices: Lower prices for Mana pots, higher prices for dust (usually undervalued) and runecloth
 - Better conditions for more casual raiders vs. another tool for progression
 - Implementation of longer boss encounters due to more healer and caster mana resources
 - more trading activity among players and a good way to upsell any missing enchants ;D
 - ...

Can we have it please TWoW Team?   satisfied_turtle_head
