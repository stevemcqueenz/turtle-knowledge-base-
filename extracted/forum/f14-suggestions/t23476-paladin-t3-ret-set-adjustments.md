# Paladin T3 ret set adjustments

## Post 164228 by Goober4 — 2026-01-02T05:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164228#p164228 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The t3 Ret set is the one of the most unique sets of armor that paladins have. It's a attack power focused set, giving you no ability power from the pieces itself. Instead, it gives tons of crit, attack power, and haste, making it the ideal armor set for seal of command builds.

The first two set bonuses help with that, with a powerful 2pc and 4pc bonus, giving even more crit and haste respectively.
However, the 6pc and 8pc bonuses, which decreases the cooldown of exorcism, a spell power based ability, and the 8pc bonus, which gives 57 spell power, doesn't seem to fit into the armor set at all. Its quite puzzling, as the spell power on a ap paladin build is about half is beneficial as it would be on a spell ret build. Lower cooldown on exorcism is nice, but since the only sources of spell power is the 8pc bonus (and whatever trinkets/back/ring you might be using), the damage doesn't justify the high mana cost.

My suggestion is to change the 6pc and 8pc set bonus into something that'll synergize with the rest armor set. I have a couple of ideas. Maybe, instead of reduced cooldown on exorcism, change to, ''Casting exorcism gives you 5% haste for 10 seconds'', or ''Casting exorcism guarantees a Seal of Command trigger'' (if that's able to be implemented in). For the 8pc bonus, you could give attack power instead, or make seal of command procs do 12% more holy damage.
These ideas might be a little on the strong side, but it's not like Ap rets are going to out damage sp rets anytime soon.  hiding_smth_turtle_head

What are some some ideas you have to change the t3 ret set?
