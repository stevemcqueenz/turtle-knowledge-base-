# Reputation Based Flight Path Additions

## Post 93773 by Valari (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-04-30T04:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93773#p93773 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://images-ext-1.discordapp.net/external/cbEfDf3Rs6KpVu7bHMfOkpTq5IAyuJT6V25SHeAhfaI/%3Fcb%3D20200127103528/https/static.wikia.nocookie.net/hearthstone_gamepedia/images/d/d1/Hippogryph_full.jpg/revision/latest)

Leveling in Turtle Wow is a lot of fun! However, once you finish a zone (or have to traverse it many times) the act of running across the land can take a toll on a player.

In an effort to get more people to level through zones that they would use and grind faction EXP, I suggest that there are flight masters who ignore you until you reach certain reputation amounts with certain factions.

For example, the Druids of Moonglade. Should you reach Exalted with the Cenarion Circle, they should let you use their mounts even though you are not a Druid.

This can be done with many questing zones, and make the “going back” quests less tedious over all while still keeping the Vanilla feel. A good example of a place this would help would be Felwood, as the Southern area is where most of the quest givers are, does not have a Flight Path, Felwood requires you to run from top to bottom about five times, and if you are Alliance the closest flight path to the southern Emerald Circle encampment is Astranaar.

Thank you for your consideration!

## Post 99517 by Valari (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-07-15T14:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99517#p99517 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Bringing this back after discussions with many of my guild members last night.

We feel many of the zones in Kalimdor are a constant back and forth run, which discourages leveling within them while Eastern Kingdom zones are more thoughtfully designed to avoid this constant commute. While the constant commute is great for farming Herbs/Fish/Mining, you feel like you explore less and are just moving around more which leads to boredom.

As Turtle WoW has implemented new forms of transportation (such as the docks in Stormwind), this lead for me to bring up the quest idea again.

Some suggestions are:

 **Darkshore**
- Grove of the Ancients
 **Ashenvale**
- Forest Song
 **Felwood**
- Emerald Circle Camp (neutral)

- This location could also use a vendor, as there is only one vendor in the zone for alliance and it is on the other side of the map.
These are just a few suggestions, and I am sure Horde could also do with more movement possibilities as well! Some flight paths could only unlock once you have completed the quests in one half of the map to move south.

Edit: This is a suggestion which can be worked with the former - as I believe reputation based paths are best. Many zones give reputation already, and your completion can be judged based on that reputation. This also encourages higher level players to go back for old content, as well as to do cloth turn ins stimulating the economy! I just wanted to offer two solutions for more “hard mode” players and softer ones for a variety to see what sticks. ![💜](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f49c.svg)
