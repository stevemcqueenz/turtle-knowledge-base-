1.Increase the duration of Fire Shield to 10 minutes (or more), and make the Imp auto cast it on the player outside of combat as well, if auto-casting is enabled and the buff isn't already present.

 2.Increase the range of the Blood Pact aura?

Make it similar to Soul Link in that it's always active as long as the Imp is out?
