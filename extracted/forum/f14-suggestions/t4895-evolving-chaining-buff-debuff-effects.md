# Evolving/Chaining Buff/DeBuff Effects

## Post 29506 by Snigery (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-19T15:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29506#p29506 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Someone had suggested recently that HoT effects should stack, or apply laterally. This was a nuissance, they said, for druids competing to heal. I thought a bit about this and know some effects stack, others apply laterally, like bleeds, curses, etc. Other effects do not, like slows. I was thinking, sure you could make them stack or apply laterally, but isn't there a buff/debuff limit issue?

Not that this suggestion is mainly to solve this limit issue, but I think it will and more. What if these buffs would evolve into greater effects depending on target conditions? So what if two druids cast HoT's that combined into a greater single  HoT at a slight increase to effectiveness, or what if they just combined, saving debuff slots, but restored mana to both druids yet at the same effect of the two heals. What if a warlock's necrotizing DoT and a warrior's Bleed DoT combined to increase physical damage on target, as if their flesh/constitution was fading.

I think this could make for some interesting tactics and situations rather than players vying to stack their own effects on target.
