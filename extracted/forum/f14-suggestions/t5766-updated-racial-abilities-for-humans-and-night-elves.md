# Updated Racial Abilities for Humans and Night Elves

## Post 36153 by Forumdweller (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-26T11:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36153#p36153 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know this is a sensitive topic, and I don't think my ideas are flawless or have been perfected. I merely want to help incite discussion regarding racial abilities, if only to have some fun.

These changes have not taken into consideration any cascade effects or collateral damage to faction balance, but I believe that those issues can be dealt with separately. The intention with these changes is to make alliances' racials more in balance (not homogenized) with the vastly superior horde racials. With or without these changes Turtle WoW will remain a great server   satisfied_turtle_head

 *Human*: Human easily has one of the least impactful active racial in the game (although that doesn't stop them from being so over-represented if I do say so myself..). I suggest a simple buff when Perception is activated, making it similar to their Orcish counterpart; combat utility. An additional 1% chance to crit when Perception is activated, and perhaps a passive 1% hit chance built into the racial.

 *Night Elf*: Night Elves in particular are dealt a bad hand when it comes to racials. The 1% Dodge (Quickness) is only 'good' for Druid tanks and has no other usage. In PvP it lets you be killed by Warriors faster, I guess. Shadowmeld is a little bit of a meme tbh. I had conversations with people ingame and they describe it as the 'AFK' spell. Because that's kind of extent of it. Don't get me wrong it's great fun and I love shadowmeld, and so I have a suggestion where the identity of the vanilla Shadowmeld is kept and improved to make it stand better against Orc Hardiness for example (25% stun resist). So Shadowmeld could be a two-part spell that makes it function similar to Cataclysm's iteration -- I think it was. If Shadowmeld is used **outside of combat** it retains it's Villain function with a very short 10 second cooldown. But if it **is used in combat** (and now it can!) then it switches to the Cata iteration and it functions similar to vanish, but goes into a 3 minute cooldown. This gives the ability a bit of spice whilst letting Night Elves keep their identity of camouflaging on the road, or stealthing whilst eating.

 *Dwarf*: Stoneform reduces the effect and duration of incoming slows by 50%. After all, they are very familiar with the frigid climates! For example frostbolt (rank 1) will become a 2.5 second slow and only have a 20% slow effect for the duration of Stoneform.  I realize that this makes it similar to Escape Artist, but both Gnome and Dwarf are smaller races so I guess it makes sense to give them some kind of movement utility. I'll admit I haven't thought this one out much but this is my 'in the moment placeholder' for Dwarves.

 *Gnome*: Escape Artist is now an instant cast.

I look forward to discussion on this topic. Please keep in mind I am not trying to upset anyone and would love to know why you think this is all a terrible idea.
