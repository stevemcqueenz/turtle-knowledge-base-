# Option to exclude highlevel chars from dungeon queue

## Post 171803 by Bhaum — 2026-03-22T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171803#p171803 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

When queueing for a dungeon, you often get queued with lvl 60 people (or any other high level chars).
Example are attunement quests, where they end up in your Sunken Temple, Hateforge, BRD runs or Uldaman where a 60 wants to get to their enchantment trainer.

When you're running dungeon to get exp, they decrease the grps exp by a lot.
Also its not fun "being carried".

Make it possible to set a checkbox in the dungeon finder to "exclude highlevel chars".
When players are out of the recommended lvl range, they get excluded

 ---
Comment: Of course I leave the grp when I end up with a highlevel char, but sometimes you get queued 4-5 times in a row with a 60 for sunken temple before you get someone in your level-range (50ish).
