# New bag recipe ideas for LW or Survival

## Post 154812 by Horcos — 2025-10-08T07:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154812#p154812 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Context: Days ago I was leveling up a new character and got myself a couple 6 slot bags from enemies. Thing is, once you get better ones, you can't do much with the 6 slot ones. When I try to give them away to lower lvl players they don't seem to need/want them and you really don't get much more in return if you sell them on the AH rather than to sell them to a vendor NPC.

So I got this idea: What if you could, through a crafting recipe, combine multiple lower capacity bags into better ones? Let's say, as an example, combining two 6 slot bags into one 8 or 10 slot bag? ofc with some other materials needed for the recipe, like some thread or something.

There's one issue with this though, dyed bags. For this to work it would be needed another recipe to "un-dye" the bags. The survival profession already has an item that removes enhancements, so I have this other idea: What if you could learn a spell that, while next to a cauldron, you could remove the dye of these items? In the same way fishing requires you to be 1) next to water and 2) use the fishing spell to start fishing, why not doing something like this? Having the, idk, "bleaching spell?" and needing to be next to one of the multiple cauldrons through out the world to remove the dyes from the bags.

What do you think about this? (I think that was a lot of text lol)

It is actually good idea to add more uses for low level bags.
When I start new character on the server I usually get 4x6-slot bags, then progress to 4x8 slot bags looted from mobs and then buy 4x14 slot bags because why bother with 10 and 12 if 14-slot are affordable. Then I upgrade to 16 slot bags by running dungeons all the time.
On new server 14 slot bags are cheap, on old server it is easy to earn gold.

What exactly can be done with bags? I dunno. But I admit they are abundant.
Bags can be combined by the same color. It will work like trading card game where you have to exchange bags that are not useful to you to other bags.
Combine-upgrade should be restricted by level somehow. It will add some value to tailor-crafted bags. "If you want better bags early, you have to pay for them".
