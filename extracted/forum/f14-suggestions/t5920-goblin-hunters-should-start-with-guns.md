# Goblin hunters should start with guns!

## Post 37210 by Qwxj — 2023-03-09T04:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37210#p37210 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title says! I made a goblin hunter recently, mostly to see the goblin stuff, and quickly found out they start with bows. While there's really not much of a difference between the two, I feel guns fit goblins way more. Two of the starting zone quests even have a gun as a reward option, which you can't actually use.
