# On Improving Skinning

## Post 171947 by Eskayrogue (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-23T13:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171947#p171947 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

****The Myth****
We've all been told as new players to take skinning to get some easy passive gold on our levelling journey, now we all know that this is pretty much a lie, and that most skins sell for a pitance at most on the auction house, and certainly not nearly as much as the same tier of herbs or ores.

 ****Let's compare:****

 *Mining:*
Mining is the primary gathering profession for not one, not two, but three artisan professions, that being Jewelcrafting, Blacksmithing and Engineering, and it also requires quite hefty amounts of resources as well, meaning the supply rarely beats the demand.

 *Herbalism:*
Herbalism is only the primary gathering profession for one artisan profession, but that one is alchemy, which is an ongoing relevant professions, as you will never not need potions and flasks, especially if you are doing high tier endgame content, this means they will always be a fairly high demand for herbs, some herbs more than others, but nevertheless it is relevant on a weekly or even daily basis for most players.

 *Skinning:*
Skinning is only relevant for one single artisan profession, Leatherworking, and really you might go and get some pieces like the devilsaur set once, but that's really it, after that is barely matters. There are the armourkits but you only need those when you get an upgrade to those pieces, and only if your class actually wants armour kits, as there are other competing enchants that often are better, such as the Arcanum of Rapidity giving haste. This means the supply often highly exceeds demand, leading to skins being near worthless, often selling for more to a vendor than on the AH. (it was like this prior to the current survival bubble)

 ****What Can Be Done?****
Now current market boom aside caused by the survival changes(as this is a bubble), what can be done? Well here are some of mine and several others' ideas for how to make skinning more relevant as a profession and more profitable as well, especially while levelling.
The new changes to Survival are nice, and do make skinning more relevant, which is great, but I doubt it'll be enough to get most of the skins from levelling to sell for more on the AH than they do to a vendor.

 *Make Leatherworking More Consistent:*
As previously mentioned, leatherworking is really only relevant on fairly rare occassions, and becomes less and less relevant the deeper into a patch cycle you get. So why not giving leatherworking some kind of consumable that means it will be contintually needed, similarly to alchemy. This could be "armour patches", separate from armour kits, these would be temporary boosts to your armour, akin to weapon oils or sharpening stones. This gives leather working something you will want to bring with you, and use regularly. It could also be something like a raid buff, imagine a fairly expensive recipe that creates a banner that buffs the raid when placed, that would be something guilds would likely spend some funds on, and by those being indemand, so will skins.

Of course weaker variants for lower levels would come with this, that people might want to use on their levelling journey, and by that virtue, skins would suddenly actually be a good way of making some extra cash while levelling.

 *More Than Just Skinning:*
Let's take a little inspiration from history. Now historically speaking when you skinned an animal you did more than just that, you also harvested other items from the animals. This can be made relevant by having skinners also harvest special meats used in cooking, as well as other weird ingredients like a Chimera Bladder or Basilisk Eye, that will then be used in alchemy recipes, this would give skinning itself something to be profitable on other than the skins themselves.

 *Relevance for Engineers:*
Skinning could also be made relevant for Engineers by making it so the Goblin Engineering recipes that require cloth, instead requires skins, this would make it so at least one spec of an artisan profession would utilise skins, making them more saught after and therefore in higher demand.

 ****Conclusion:****
Now take all of this with a huge grain of salt, I think if all of these were implemented in their most extreme forms, it would swing the pendulum the complete opposite way and make skins *too* necessary, all of these are only rough ideas on what could be done. But as it stands now, skinning isn't profitable compared to the other two gathering professions whatsoever. Treat this as an open letter for your ideas as well on how skinning could be improved to make it a better, more interesting and more relevant profession, both in levelling and at endgame.

Also as a last bit, remember we are currently in a bubble when it comes to specifically light leather and copper, because of the survival changes. making those temporarily sky rocket in cost, much higher than they should be and much higher than will stay at after a couple weeks.
