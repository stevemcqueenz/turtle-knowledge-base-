Contested does not mean what you think it does. Redridge very much belongs to the Alliance, but due to it being aimed at level 15+, it is automatically a contested zone.

In WoW, contested simply means that the levels are above 15. There are exceptions to the rule where zones start as low as 10 but end up above 15 while still not being considered Contested.

Examples for Alliance are Loch Modan (10-20), Westfall (10-20), Darkshore (10-20). For Horde, there are Barrens (10-25) and Silverpine Forest (10-20).

These zones are not bound to the contested rule due to their wide level gaps, and making them contested would automatically create issues for every level 10 Horde character entering the Barrens.

Contested Examples:
Stonetalon, which can be started at 15, is heavily Horde-favored but still contested.
Similarly, Redridge is heavily Alliance-favored but still contested because it starts at 15.

So no, it should not be contested because otherwise, the Alliance would have three uncontested leveling zones above level 10, while the Horde would be left with one.

Another significant concern is that it is where the majority of Horde players are leveling, and putting them at risk of being ganked as early as level 10, compared to the Alliance at level 20, is just stupid.
