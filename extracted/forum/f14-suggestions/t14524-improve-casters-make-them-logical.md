I like to play as arcane and feel this spec in need to an equivalent to fireball/frostbolt.

Considering damage spells only, arcane has 2, while:

Fire has fireball, fireblast, scorch (which is a alternative to long casting time fire spells), pyroblast, blast wave and flamestrike.

Frost has frostbolt (which is the most cost effective damage spell for mages), cone of cold, Blizzard(great spell for easy farming).

Arcane has a 5 sec channeling spell and arcane explosion (very effective spell btw).

It feels like arcane lacks some alternative to different situations, imo, arcane blast fills this spot.

Fire and Frost are awesome specs love them, but arcane needs a bit more.

When raiding I did a lot of alt tabbing on my 5 sec arcane missiles channel, it is boring to fight a boss with just 1 spell.

This thread is really mostly about class identity.

An argument could be made that anyone can just insert their favorite expansion name and say that a class felt the most as that class should in that expansion, sure, but one thing is certain that a lot of classes in original vanilla have what feels like an identity crisis other than rogues and warriors.

Specs with a lot of talents that seem to be either irrelevant or contrary to what the identity of the spec by namesake should be (warlock demo pet sacrifice is a big and highlighted node but the demonology class identity should not be requiring a node that ties into the "best and last" one that's practically all about sacrificing a demon and playing without one).

Heck there's even a really effortless way to fix destruction locks; remove the threat increased aspect of searing pain, now warlocks have a fire spell they can use and their talents already make sense for pve, such a small change with huge implications on gameplay viability
