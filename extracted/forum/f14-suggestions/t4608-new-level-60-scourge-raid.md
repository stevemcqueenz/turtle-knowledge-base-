# New Level 60 Scourge Raid

## Post 27576 by Troyleaongillit — 2022-10-19T21:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27576#p27576 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The fact that Kel'thuzad's phylactery Was stolen following his Defeat in nexxramas leaves the door open for a final climatic Battle with the Lich's Forces in Lorderaon in the form of a brand new raid

The raid would be called the Catacombs of Stratholme and would be comprised of a series of tunnels beneath the Scourge controlled city with the entrance lying next to the slaughterhouse

Raid requirements

Players must be level 60, have reached exalted with the argent Dawn and must have completed Naxxramas

Raid background

For years the citizens of Stratholme used the Catacombs that lie beneath the city as both a burial ground and a home for exiled criminals and the homeless

Following the third war the Catacombs became abandoned but now a Large Scourge Army under the leadership of the treacherous Father Inigo Montoy Inhabit the Catacombs and are preparing to resurrect the Arch-Lich Kel'thuzad

The Armies of Azeroth now prepare to Storm the Catacombs in a out all assault to Destroy Kel'thuzad once and for all

Final Battle of raid

When players reach the Cathedral of Darkness they will need to fight their way inside through the various Undead minions

Once players reach the inner sanctum they will find 5 Acolyte Summoners performing a resurrection spell on Kel'thuzad's phylactery

The First phase revolves around the Summoners who players must kill

Once the Summoners are killed Father Inigo Montoy casts an invincible spell around the phylactery rendering it immune to Attack before he joins the battle

As the final boss of the raid Father Inigo Montoy is the hardest and most difficult boss in the entire raid and will use various priest spells such as smite against players

Once Father Inigo Montoy is killed the invincible spell casts upon the phylactery disapats leaving the phylactery open to Attack

Players final task is to Destroy the phylactery and in the process destroying Kel'thuzad once and for all

Once the phylactery is destroyed the raid ends

Lead in Quest chain

Before the Catacombs become available to players they must complete the introductory Quest chain where Father Inigo Montoy is revealed as a Scourge agent and traitor

The Quest chain begins at Light's hope chapel where players are sent by agents of the argent Dawn to Question Father Inigo Montoy regarding the location of Kel'thuzad's phylactery as they have not revieved it

Montoy pretends to have no idea as to what happened and sends players back to the argent Dawn empty handed

Fearing that something foul is in the air the argent Dawn sends the player with a report to either Highlord fordring for alliance players or warchief Thrall for Horde players detailing the dissaperiance of Kel'thuzad's phylactery

Fordring and Thrall both fearful that the lich could return to his corporal form order the player to Locate and Destroy the phylactery

Players are aided in their search by members of  SI:7 and the deathstalkers

The First portion of the Quest chain involves players completing Quests across the entirety of Lordaeron from the plaugelands into Trisfal Glades all the way into hillsbrad and the hinterlands

Eventually players discover the truth and return to Light's hope to inform the argent Dawn of Montoy's Betrayal

The Argent Dawn and players then confront Montoy leading to a showdown with the treacherous Priest. Once players get Montoy down to half health he flees Light's hope chapel with Kel'thuzad's phylactery ending the first phase of the Quest chain

The second and final phase has players working with  SI:7  and the deathstalkers in finding out where Montoy took the phylactery leading to the discovery of the Catacombs

Players then Report the information to Highlord Fordring and warchief Thrall who in turn order an assault on the Catacombs ending the Quest chain
