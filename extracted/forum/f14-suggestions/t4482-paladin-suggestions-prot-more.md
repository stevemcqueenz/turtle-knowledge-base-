# Paladin Suggestions (Prot & more)

## Post 26802 by Zeyla — 2022-10-06T01:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26802#p26802 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After leveling 1-60 as a prot paladin, I must say that I really enjoy the way prot functions on turtle wow. However, I do have just a couple of suggestions that would make the experience far less annoying in a few aspects.

 1. Make Righteous Fury undispellable. It's a simple suggestion, and the reasoning is that tanking a dungeon with mobs that can purge/dispel magic becomes incredibly tedious when you're having to constantly reapply a 30 min buff mid-pull in order to maintain threat. I suggest either removing the magic tag from the buff itself, or in case people prefer it to stay that way as a dispel buffer for pvp purposes, just add a 100% chance to resist dispelling righteous fury to a deep prot talent, like ardent defender.

 2. Change Reckoning to a 2% chance when struck per point rather than 20% chance when crit per point. This should work towards a similar proc rate balance between pve and pvp compared to the current chance when crit and it actually means the talent doesn't become pointless once you reach 440 defense skill and can no longer be crit. Some may argue that it takes away a certain aspect of skill by no longer being able to force procs with a /sit macro but I believe that the benefit of maintaining the talent's viability once defense-capped is more important for the prot tree overall.

 3. Make improved judgement of justice no longer remove the paladin's existing judgement from the target. It's annoying that your taunt removes the judgement that you've been maintaining from the target and also requires one of your taunt cooldowns in order to re-apply. I won't suggest splitting the taunt into a new skill, as I like how improved seal of justice has been implemented. Instead, I would just make it so that judgement of justice no longer removes other judgements from the target, but also only lasts the base 10 seconds and isn't refreshed on the target with melee attacks. I think this would make a decent trade-off so you don't feel like you're losing so many judgement cooldowns just by taunting.

 4. I know this one has probably been suggested many times and will likely never be implemented, but I'll mention it just because I think it's a good idea... Make blessing base duration 10 mins for the single buff. A 20-30 min duration for greater blessing would also be amazing, but I won't ask for too much. Just the 10 min durations alone would greatly improve the leveling experience, since re-buffing 5 people  every 5 minutes while leveling in a dungeon has always been incredibly tedious.

 5. Take a look at auras, blessings, and totems interaction. It's great that we can level alongside the other faction on this server, but it gets a bit awkward when my blessings get removed because a strength totem or mana spring totem got dropped randomly. If there was a way to make the blessings not get removed, but only the more powerful buff is actually in effect, then that would be perfect. Also, some strange things like devotion aura granting armor but getting disabled by stoneskin totem which grants a blessing of sanctuary effect, which it also removes. Some of the interactions just don't make sense.

That's about it for now. Thanks again for making this amazing server and giving us such interesting class changes to play with. Keep up the good work!
