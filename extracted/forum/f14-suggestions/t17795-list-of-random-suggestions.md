# List of random suggestions

## Post 121314 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-01T13:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121314#p121314 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

While playing I gathered a smaller list of change ideas which I wanna share here.
They are not built around any specific topic, the list contains random ideas.

 **1. Replace the Horde's SWV entrance to Booty Bay, next to the Gnomeregan port.**
I agree that it makes more sense for the entrance to be near SW but it has disadvantages.
If your team has no lock, waiting for the horde guy to arrive takes way more time than it should be required.
Also, forcing level 60 players to run through the other faction's leveling zones, including a starting zone to get to a level 60 dungeon is a dangerous design.

 **2. Buff or change hunter's 'survivalist' talent**
Now for 5 talent points you can have a 10% HP boost in the middle of the survival talent tree. It would make some sense if survival were a melee/tank dual tree but it isn't. Plus HP has some merit in raiding and even more in PVP but the melee hunter which is interesting but a bit lacking could use those 5 talent points on a much more interesting talent.
What I'd push is a talent creating synergies between your pet's damage and your melee damage but honestly, whatever works which has melee or trap-making utility.

 **3. Enh shaman's 'calming winds' talent should include earth shock**
What I noticed when I leveled a tank last time is enh shaman's huge threat compared to the damage it does. Part of the problem I think lies in the threat-reduction talent, calming winds. It reduces threat from the rotation's usual pieces, except for shocks and earth shock still has a threat modifier in this server. You could say that sure but shadow priests for example also have mind blast in their rotation and you would be right. But unlike them, enh has no other threat reduction talent (none for shocks) - they have a threat reduction totem but it uses the same element as WF or agi, it basically cannot exist for them.

 **4. Assassination tree capstone talent**
Give assassination a talent which grants the rogue some unique and potent poisons. You wanted to make a poison tree for rogue, okay, but in this case they need some really interesting poisons, available only for them, who fully went down on the assassination tree.
For example 1-2 poisons which work as potent debuffs in raid, 1 good utility, maybe 1-2 interesting damaging options.

 **5. Give warlocks a taunt (at least in demonology tree) and let them LFT as tanks**
There is still a tank problem for leveling and properly-geared warlocks have the option to dungeon tank in this server. At least from mid-game, after having some crucial talents.
Demonic embrace. Demonic Aegis. Wrathstone. Master Demonologist / Voidwalker, Soul Link.
It should be manageable, dungeons, unlike raids don't have nonsensical damage after all.

 **6. Make flight masters faster**
I'm okay with no summoning stones. I can accept the reasoning. But waiting times for instances are huge and we gotta keep in mind two things:
 1. This is not 2004 anymore, people's expectation regarding game mechanics (and in general, attention span) changed a lot.
 2. Target audience has changed. The majority of the player base is a worker in his 30s, having 2 hours of free time every other day and wants to spend it with this game. Let's say a dungeon takes around 1,5 hours, at least the one he is interested in. LFT fills in 20 minutes. People arrive in another 20. We're already over his time limit and we haven't even assumed that someone just randomly quits and the LFT gotta start over again.
I'm just saying that the game mechanic which worked for a young audience with a bunch of free time 20 years ago is problematic for adults with much less free time.
I'm still OK without summoning stones if flight masters start to fly like 2x faster. Makes you reach places reasonably fast.

 **7. Please add an FP to Karazhan.**
As a horde you either take a detour or run through an Alliance leveling zone again just to get there. Since Kara is at the Southern end of the region and it takes time to get there from any locations, I'd think a FP would be really justified.
I understand that Atiesh helps but Kara is also there for early level 60s for Crypts and Kara10.

 **?.**
A way to make it easier for Alliance to get to the Scarlet Monastery without creating a ganking field with a badly placed FP or ship.

Thanks for coming to my TED talk.

There is one argument against making cloth tanks (of any kind).
Before CC2, tanks usually used to wear plate, it means no plate wearers were welcome in the party to reduce competition for loot for tank.
I don't think that new special tanking gear for warlocks will be added. They will share same spell damage gear as usual.
If you add cloth tanks, then cloth DPS will be less welcomed in the party to reduce competition for loot for warlock tanks. In addition, healers would compete for tanking loot too! So you could end up in situation when two key roles in group competes for loot with eachother.
It might be relieved by improving LFT to search leather/mail wearing healers for cloth tanks and cloth/leather healers for plate tanks. Without that, we will see more situations like:
