## Post 25520 by Juicyx — 2022-09-19T17:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25520#p25520 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Why isn't there a permanent hardcore character option?

Some of us think it's really lame the journey essentially ends at lvl 60. At first I thought it made some sense but after leveling and experiencing what the hardcore restrictions actually entail, it became quite a different game from what i'm used to with WoW. You have to socialize and get to know players to a greater degree. You don't go to a dungeon with just anybody, you need to make connections if you want enchants or whatever, professions are much more important, no infinite bank space, etc, etc.
You know what i'm talking about if you legit leveled as hardcore. It introduces a lot of nuances that you simply sidestep in normal WoW, and these nuances could be explored even further if there existed a common ground created by devs.

Let players end the mode if they want and have their Immortal title, i don't care. Just give us the Mortal title, a permanent hardcore mode that is not a scuffed lvl 59 experience.
