# Survival Hunter: About that nerf of Carve

## Post 108920 by Reaver594 — 2024-11-20T10:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108920#p108920 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is a Copy of an answer to a dev on Twow Hunter-Discord, to increase visbility and make it possible for other players to engage directly with what I wrote:

 **Question:**
Was the nerf intended just reduce AoE Damage, or also to reduce single target damage too?

 **Mechanics:**
On a regular Boss-fight, a survival hunter had to use all their abilities (RS, Trap, MB, Carve, WC - roughly in that priority) to not get a small window, where you are basically doing nothing. (PPl who raided MM before, know how un-intuitive it feels for a Hunter to "waste time" when your performance is dependent a lot on making use of every possible GCD without clipping stuff). With the CD increase to 10s, you created that sort of "empty time window" for survival. This feels a bit weird, and I wonder if there wasn't another path of either tuning the damage instead, or a combination of CD and damage.

 **Flavor/Identity:**
After all a CD increase from 6s to 10s (which is a 66% increase), for the only Weapon (aka WPN dependent) Cleave Ability for a spec, whos **Identity is basically being the Cleaving Melee with Long Polearms** feels a tiny bit overdone to me, and also homogenizes the ability a bit too much with Whirlwind, with both having the exact same CD now.

 **Feedback:**
Yes the Spec is performing very good right now, and that may be a surprise to many. But we should not forget that **Hunter is one of the few DPS-Only Classes** and therefor should be among the top performers! (On that ground thanks for the Piercing shot buff, MM deserved some improvements), unlike other Hybrid specs that hog the thrown of top dps since forever.. *cough wayor* cough*

 ***Small addition:**
It seemed Carve was balanced via reducing the % of WPN damage applied, but giving it a shorter CD in Turn, compared to other melee cleave abilities like WW. Now the nuance here is gone, which again puts me to the conclusion that the nerf was a tad bit overdone.
Also please, please, **stop balancing classes mana around having a Palading near to keep JoW up**, and thats not only true for survival Hunters.
