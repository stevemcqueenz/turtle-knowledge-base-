# Vampiric Embrace change idea

## Post 162758 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-12-14T12:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162758#p162758 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey!

I was thinking about changing VE from a party buff into an area buff affecting up to 5 people (so the same number of recipients as if it were a party buff).
Passive heal, while useful in caster groups, it is usually more useful for melee and specially tanks and mind flay has a kinda short radius by default, limiting the shadow priest's distance from the enemy.
Spriest could turn into a melee/tank utility even in raids, turning the shorter MF range into a strategic asset, if melee positions itself cleverly.
