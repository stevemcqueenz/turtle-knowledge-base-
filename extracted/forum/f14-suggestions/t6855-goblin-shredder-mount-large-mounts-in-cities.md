# Goblin Shredder Mount/Large Mounts in Cities

## Post 43324 by Warashocka — 2023-04-26T22:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43324#p43324 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Not too long ago, Turtle WoW disabled certain large mounts in city zones due to players abusing size-increasing buffs/items. It's an understandable change, as it can be quite annoying to see someone the size of the entire area, and while not only immersion-breaking, shouldn't affect someone's gameplay. Such mounts that were included in this disabling were the popular Pounder mount added to Gnomeregan, the custom Gnomish Spider-Tank mounts from Gnome rep, Goblin Shredder mounts from Durotar Labor Union rep, and dragon mounts (Azure/Bronze, respectively) from old Immortals/Black Morass rep.
Each of these mounts require a large amount of grinding and time dedication to obtain, such as:
- the Pounder(s), 0.8% drop chance from Crowd Pummeler boss, with a quest attached to it.

- Spider-Tank, takes ~1500+ stacks of runecloth to exalted rep and another 750 gold just for the mount. (with rank 3 and Honored rep discount)

- Goblin Shredder, requires turn-ins of engineering items to exalted rep and 300 skill in Engineering, then costing 750 gold. (with rank 3 and Honored rep discount.)

- The immortal Azure dragon took, well, becoming immortal before the change to the fiery warhorse.

- Black Morass' bronze drake source of farming has been disabled longer than it was available, but it required a considerable amount of corrupted sand turn-in and **5000g**.
I had recently completed my long grind for Durotar Labor Union exalted rep, and received the Goblin Shredder mount for my goblin warrior main of the past 2 years. You can imagine my disappointment when I was excited to show my mount to friends in Orgrimmar, only to be met with dismounting upon entering the gates.

These mounts are not easily acquirable as shown, and the people who have committed themselves to the long grinds for them are given a slap in the face when wanting to flaunt their accomplishment. For this reason, I would like these mounts **allowed back in city areas**. Disabling the mounts altogether in city zones seems like a huge band-aid "fix".

In my mind, the easiest way to amend this issue would be to limit what size buffs can affect you while on a certain mount/affected by a certain aura/being present in a certain area. This way, size buffs can't be abused in high population areas, and therefore aren't as much of an annoyance. Sadly, it's tough to understand the devs' stance on this large mount debacle, previously adding items like the "Gnomish Enlargement Balm" which buffs gnome size, giving credence to stacking size buffs.

I hope this can gather more traction from other large mount enjoyers. Especially my goblin brothers on Shredders, still not as big as a tauren on their racial mount.
