# High-level Fishing Ideas

## Post 49265 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-07T23:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49265#p49265 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Problem
Fishing actually has decent low-level content, but it fizzles out once you get past the midgame. There are earlygame quests like [this](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1141) and [this](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1580). Different kinds of pools make fishing more interesting. The weekly tournament in STV brings the community together and offers unique loot. But once you reach the coast of STV around level 40 you've pretty much seen it all. Floating Wreckage with Mithril Bound Trunks and Swarms of Eels are the highest tier of pools, there are virtually no more quests, and the most useful fish (Nightfin Snapper, Winter Squid) can be caught from skill 205 onwards.

So why painstakingly assemble fishing gear for up to +52 skill? Why apply +75 or even +100 lures (which might also support engineering) if a basic 300 skill is enough for all the useful stuff? There is one last bracket of endgame zones with a higher requirement (minimum 330, ideal 425), but there are virtually no rewards encouraging you to fish there (something also noted by [others in the community](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=33&t=7322)). There *are* exclusive high-level fish. They are just mostly useless like Whitescale Salmon that creates a [terrible food item](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=18247) or [Plated Armorfish](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13890) that is only used for an Argent Dawn writ. Bay of Storms in Azshara is one such high-level zone and it even has its own unique fishing loot with Darkclaw Lobster and Large Raw Mightfish. But again they produce absolutely [useless](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13933) food [items](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13934).

Positive Examples
Within the endgame fishing zones there is a single counter-example: Zul'Gurub. It has custom pools (Muddy Churning Waters), with custom fish ([Zulian Mudskunk](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19975)) that tie into the zone (summoning the boss Gahz'ranka) and interlink with established fishing lore (Nat Pagle) through [a quest](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=8227).

TBC introduced a really unique and rare end-game fishing reward with [Mr. Pinchy](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/item=27388/mr-pinchy) that would grant you several things including a [unique pet](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/item=27445/magical-crawdad-box).

Even Turtle has given some love to fishing with unique low-level fishing drops like [Undercity Bucket Hat](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=84037) and [Darkwood Fishing Pole](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6366), as well as [Fishing Boats](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=51282) from Survival (there is just little need to deploy them). But again, all that does not make endgame fishing more rewarding or engaging. There is some hope for future content given [the 1.16.6 preview](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=63&t=5391) mentions "Barkskin Fisher (Hyjal) (Fishing Rod) (Level 60)" as a new monster. If that means you can spawn a rare mob by fishing this sounds really cool! It's great if the devs design new zones with fishing in mind, but I would like to spruce up the existing zones as well.

The Challenge
Fishing is definitely not enjoyed by everyone. Avoiding a power creep is a good idea in general, but doubly so when it comes to fishing rewards. Rewards should be unique, thematic and nifty/helpful in some way without breaking the game or becoming too powerful.
As mentioned in the [recent podcast interview](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=7237) the ideal additions to Turtle WoW should tie in with existing lore and fill blanks in underused areas. Since the highest bracket of fishing zones contains Bay of Storms (Azshara), Jademir Lake (Feralas) and Deadwind Pass, spicing up fishing would serve a double purpose here and be a good opportunity to drop some lore as well. My suggestions will therefore mostly revolve around quests, though I'm not opposed to new items that can just be fished up.
Lastly one of the challenges is making the new content visible somehow. The TWoW database does not show you a list of fishing loot for zones. So there might already be rare endgame fishing loot that I'm just not aware of. Without knowing there is something good to catch, people probably won't try to fish in the hardest zones. So the new rewards should (at least in part) be somehow visible in the world to alert people to their presence.

Fixes
There are time-sensitive fishes. [Nightfin Snappers](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13759) can only be caught at night, [Sunscale Salmon](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13760) only at day, with overlap of both in morning and evening. However this is determined by server time, which makes no sense on Turtle. Under the moonlit starry sky of Kalimdor you will catch... Sunscale Salmon of course. Please make the visual offset apply to fishing on Kalimdor. Added benefit is that your timezone does not matter, you can always catch the fish you want by picking a continent instead of saddling players in the wrong timezone with really bad ideal catch times.

The over-all level of a zone usually corresponds to the fishing skill requirement. The big outlier is Tel'Abim, the only level 55+ zone that does not require skill 330 to fish. I think it should be brought in line.

There are plenty of bugged/unfishable pools all through-out the world, notably quite a few for the fishing tournament. This is blizzlike (I recognize some of the broken pools from Vanilla), but might be worth looking into? I know this is a very tall ask, so I'm more asking a question: Where would one report broken pools and what kind of info is needed getting them fixed?

Give high-level fish-based cooking recipes a purpose. [Baked Salmon](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13935) and [Lobster Stew](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13933) do not even give you any kind of buff, they just refill your health. And very little of it too, since it's only lvl 45 food. But they need a fish from a skill 330 zone and cooking skill 275 on top. This was an issue in Vanilla, but is greatly exacerbated by Turtle's Conjured Mana Oranges. These fish seem completely useless to me right now. Either buff their health regen or add some kind of buff (nothing too powerful since they're fairly common fish - maybe +10 for a resistance would be a novel food buff?).

General (mostly unrealistic) Suggestions
The ideal solution to incentivise endgame fishing would be to add a whole new tier of content with new fish (both for Cooking and Alchemy) and new pools (for fish and also Thorium Bound Trunks from new Wreckage pools) on top of making existing end-game fish more useful. Then there would be new fishing stuff worth discovering past level 45. Existing content might have to be rebalanced as well (eg Mithril Bound Trunks feel very valuable compared to how early you can fish them). Fishing doesn't need to become more proftable than it already is, just better paced. This would be a *lot* of work, both creating the items and adding the pools to all zones. It would also have sizable balance implications through the new cooking and alchemy consumables. In short, I don't see it happening, but I wanted to mention it.

Another great addition would be a proper high-level fishing tournament in the style of the Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza. Set perhaps in Azshara's Bay of Storms. Once a week (or even once a month) Storm Eels come to the surface to spawn which creates new pools for a limited time. Catch 40 and be the first to turn them in to be awarded the title "Master Angler" which you can display on your character. Finally a reason to get the gear from the STV tournament since it would give you a competitive edge here. Make it happen later in the day than the STV tournament since a lot of US folk complain about the time. There could also be rare catches like in STV. Admittedly this would be a whole lot of work for a very small community of fishing enthusiasts, so realistically I don't see it happening either.

So on to the smaller and more realistic additions to each zone:

Bay of Storms (Azshara)
Note that this means the center of the bay. The coast of Azshara has a lower skill requirement.

 **Late Return**
Azshara's Bay is a sunken elven city that provides ample opportunity for lore. A very rare catch could be a "Shimmering Volume" that starts a quest.

 *The book in your hands is of Elven origin and seems ancient, yet surprisingly well preserved given you've pulled it out of the Bay of Storm's tempestuous waters. A faint glow hints at some kind of magic that must have protected it from the elements. The hand-written entries on the first page lead you to believe this is a library book. Apparently it belongs to "The Athenaeum in Eldre'Thalas". Maybe you could try and return it?*

I'm not an expert on elven lore, so I'll leave the title of the book and the lore-dump when you turn it in at [Lorekeeper Lydros](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=14368) in Dire Maul up for grabs. The idea is that the library of Eldre'Thalas had loaned the book to someone in Zin-Azshari when it fell during the War of the Ancients.

As a quest reward I propose Shen'dralar rep and "Formula: Enchant Gloves - Advanced Fishing". Like Mining and Herbalism, Fishing has a [+2 glove enchantment](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11152). But both [Mining](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11203) and [Herbalism](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11205) get +5 enchantments while Fishing does not. The spell would fill a gap left in Vanilla and be thematic with the arcane nature of the Shen'dralar Highborne. Don't make the quest reward BoP (similar to most cooking recipe quest rewards or [Shadoweave Mask](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=10463)) so it can be sold on the AH.

 **The Fishing Spirit**
The fishing legend [Nat Pagle](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=12919) is known for [losing equipment in dangerous places](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=8227) and somewhat canonically an alcoholic (he remarks that fishing is "a great excuse to drink ... heavily"). A not-so-rare catch could be "Clam-encrusted Container" that starts a quest.

 *The object in your hands has seen better days. The shape suggests it was once meant to be a container of sorts, but years at the bottom of the sea have left it virtually unrecognizable. Digging through the muck and silt inside you find a small label that reads: "Nat Pagle, Dustwallow Marsh." Maybe you should contact the original owner?*

Turn-in:
Why, is that Martha, my old tackle box? I thought I had lost her forever in the Bay of Storms. She went overboard while I was fighting with the biggest Mightfish you've ever seen. I tried to save her, but ended up losing both her and the fish - one of those eternal regrets that keep you awake at night (if you happen to be sober).
I can't tell you how many days of fishing she has tided me over with her endless supply... just seeing ol' Martha again warms my heart. Tell you what, my own supplies out here in the Marsh are running low. If you fetch me some more, I will teach you how to assemble your own tackle box.

Which leads to a follow-up quest:
My supplies of rare beers and spirits are running dangerously low out here in the Marsh. If you help me restock, I will teach you how to assemble your own tackle box.

In Feralas I've once met this ogre who shared with me a mean grog. Ever since I've been hankering for some more.

A long time ago I ran into a gnome called Plugger. Odd name, but he brewed a great ale! Then he started hanging out with these dark-skinned dwarves and eventually disappeared. Always talked about opening a tavern, I wonder what became of him... Maybe you can track him down and get me some of his ale?

From my travels to the jungles of Stranglethorn I remember this smooth fruity wine from the tavern in Booty Bay. The goblins really know their stuff, bring me some of that.

I used to be able to order some Special Reserve from the tavern over in Theramore. That stuff gets you drunk like nobody's business! Now I can't find it anywhere. There's some kind of exclusive deal with a travelling fair? See if you can track some down for me.

Nat Pagle in Dustwallow Marsh wants you to bring him the following alcohol:
 5 [Gordok Green Grog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18269)
 5 [Dark Iron Ale Mug](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11325)
 5 [Junglevine Wine](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4595)
 5 [Darkmoon Special Reserve](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19221)

Look at all those bottles, you've actually done it! That should almost tide me over until the weekend.
Anyway, a good tackle box needs some basic lures and lots of alcohol to raise the proper fishing spirit. For you I recommend using cheaper stuff than what I pack.

The quest encourages somewhat particular dungeon runs (DM Tribute, BRD with no lava skip) and travel around the world (Booty Bay, DMF). Apart from the Tribute run you could also hit the AH. The reward is "Outline: Tackle Box" for the Survival skill. It takes
 2 Simple Wood
 5 Nightcrawlers
 5 Rhapsody Malt
 5 Southshore Stout
 10 Rumsey Rum Black Label
The craft creates a consumable item that sets up a tackle box in the world (tool box model with no collision). The object lasts 10 minutes and putting it down instills a 30min cooldown. Being around the tackle box raises your fishing skill by 20. Clicking on the tackle box gets you lightly drunk (repeatable). The item has mostly roleplay purpose, allowing you to buff other people's fishing and providing free alcohol for roleplay events. The Malt is Alliance exclusive, the Stout is Horde exclusive (at least to buy, it also drops from mobs - I couldn't find a purely Horde exclusive alcohol), encouraging cross-trading and highlighting how fishing and roleplay events bring everyone together (I'm open to removing the Malt & Stout requirements if it's just a hassle).

Why, this is the work of a skilled Goblin engineer! Clearly based on the Dimension Ripper blueprint, but it has undergone several curious modifications. Frankly I have no idea how to operate it, but I would love to take it apart! Hold on... <he grabs a magnifying lense>
What a pitty. There's an engraving that says "designed by R. Bassbait". Maybe you should return it to learn more about it instead? Wouldn't want to destroy some invaluable prototype of a fellow engineer, although I could see this produce a glorious explosion.

Which leads to a follow-up quest:
 *The small device you've fished up in Winterspring is apparently a modified teleporter, though you have yet to learn how to operate it. According to a fine engraving, it seems to have been manufactured by one "R. Bassbait". Find the original creator to learn more about the device.*

This leads you to [Riggle Bassbait](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=15077) who only shows up for a few hours every week for the fishing tournament:
Stop bothering me, <race>. I have a tournament to run here.

What? Oh my, is that my old Translocator? Where did you find it?

Winterspring, huh? I must have lost it fishing up there while visiting relatives. It helps me get here to Booty Bay to run the Fishing Extravaganza. It's nice of you to return it, but I've already made a replacement. You can keep the old one.

Why are you still here? Will you finally stop interfering with my tournament if I show you how to operate it?

The quest awards the "Bassbait Translocator", a trinket that teleports you to Booty Bay with a 24 hour cooldown. There should already be a spell from the Horde teleporter in Gnomeregan (I think it's [this one](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=4997)) that you can hook into. The longer cooldown and less useful target location should make this balanced against the engineering-exclusive [Dimensional Ripper - Everlook](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18984) and [Ultrasafe Transporter: Gadgetzan](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18986) which both have a 4 hour cooldown. If this is not enough, make Riggle require some expensive engineering component to fix it up in a third quest step.
The intended use is to participate in the fishing tournament without having to travel there (or reset your hearth if you want to compete), or to meet up for a cross-faction exchange in Booty Bay/Ratchet. Though it might also have some use to access ZG from remote locations.

 **Emerald Influence**

After a short struggle at Jademir Lake, the fish seems to have miraculously escaped you, leaving only a tiny iridescent green flake behind on your hook. Upon closer inspection it more closely resembles a tiny dragon scale than the fish scales you are used to.  *A tiny scale half-way between carp and green dragon - how curious! Yes, I can most definitely feel the influence that the Emerald Dream has had upon this creature. You have done well, bringing this to my attention. It definitely warrants further study.*

Which leads to a follow-up:
 *I have to ask you to return to Jademir Lake. Additional Ideas
As mentioned, Zul'Gurub is already fine as-is (and I'm not huge on instanced fishing either). If there's some lore to be told, you could always add some kind of rare artefact to the fishing loot table. Turn it in with the Zandalar tribe for some rep and lore.

Apart from the bigger noticable fishing zones discussed so far, there are also lesser-known tiny pools of the highest fishing difficulty in Silithus, Stratholme, Burning Steppes and Dire Maul. I'm not sure it's worth adding content here, but it's a possibility.

Another cool and thematic reward would be the ability to walk on water. This ability also allows you to fish while standing on the surface, which is fairly useful for an angler. One way to implement this might be by rewarding an Alchemy recipe for the [unused Elixir of Water Walking](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=8827) (though the cost should probably be higher, maybe require a high-level fish?). Another would be through (very rare) boots that allow permanent water walking. I really like these two ideas for rewards, but I did not use them out of balance concerns - in Vanilla water walking is exclusive to Shamans and permanent water walking might even have PvP balance implications (water is used to slow you down in Arathi Basin). If these can be worked around, I would love to see something like this come from high-level fishing.

Another really great reward for anglers would be a way to reduce your aggro radius so mobs don't disturb your fishing and you don't have to murder every murloc on the coast just to get to a pool. This might come as a passive aura from a special fishing pole or might be an active ability (from an item or a consumable) similar to [Sooth Animal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=9901) and [Mind Sooth](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=10953). Again - very cool and very useful, but this obviously has gigantic implications beyond fishing (allowing you to skip mobs in dungeons, etc). Would love to see it but don't know how to balance it.

Let me know what you think and feel free to add your own suggestions, be they fishing quests or just nifty thematic items from fishing in endgame zones.
