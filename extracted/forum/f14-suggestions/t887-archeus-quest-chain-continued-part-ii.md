# Archeus Quest Chain Continued (Part II)?

## Post 4786 by Withinamnesia (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-01-04T08:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4786#p4786 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Duskwoods have a quest that many Alliance players remember well that ends with the player obtaining the two-handed sword [Archeus](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=2000/archeus/url).

Although many players wanted this reward or even quest to be more feeling a bit underwhelmed after battling Mor'Ladim.

My proposal is to extend the quest chain with 6 quests that takes the player into Scarlet Monastery Graveyard summons an extra Bone Golem boss (adds extra regular boss to SM Graveyard) which after defeated leads the player the giant undead serpent in the Desolace which is slain and drops the McGuffin which is use to empower [Archeus](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=2000/archeus/url) into a Rare quality sword with 3 variants for different play types and a fun trinket until for pre-raiding.

[Archeus](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=2000/archeus/url) item now begins a quest.

Quest I: Go to Morgan Ladimore's Grave and summon his ghost and ask him about the unfinished business with Uther, the Scourge and Lordaeron.

 - Ghost of Morgan Ladimore Tells of his brother in arms that found an Ancient Unholy Artifact and the both of them swore to defeat a foreign ancient evil yet had they to answer the call to war battling the scourge in Stratholm with Arthas.

After listening to Morgan Ladimore the first quest is completed (handed in at Morgan) and the second quest opens (at Morgan). Note: [Morgan's grave can be clicked to summon Morgan at any point in the second quest chain to prevent bugs]

Quest II: The Ghost of Morgan Ladimore tells the player of his estranged past unfinished business: Morgan's brother in arms found and had in his possession an Ancient Unholy Artifact yet fell in battle fighting the scourge in Strathholm and was interned with the Scarlet Crusade; the player is directed to search the Graveyard in the Scarlet Monastery for the buried Ancient Unholy Artifact.

(Go to SM Graveyard, click a loose pile of dirt / grave in the back right beside the crypt, Summons a Bone Golem [needs no quest to summon] Morgan's Fallen Brother In Arms [3D Model](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=11622/rattlegore3D%20/url), Kill summon-able boss (drops missing gear type blues for level range), loot quest item Ancient Unholy Artifact).

 - Obtain Ancient Unholy Artifact from Morgan Ladimore's Past.

Return to Ghost of Morgan Ladimore to hand in quest with Ancient Unholy Artifact to complete the quest (opens up quest III from Morgan).

Quest III The Ghost of Morgan Ladimore recollects about trying to find the source of the Ancient Unholy Artifact before the war with the scourge and asks the player to take the Ancient Unholy Artifact to his friend Brother Crowley in Stormwind Cathedral.

 - Seek insight about the source of Ancient Unholy Artifact from Brother Crowley in Stormwind Cathedral.

Hand in Ancient Unholy Artifact to Brother Crowley at Stormwind Cathedral for quest completion (Brother Crowley opens up Quest IV).

Quest IV Brother Crowley divines the source of the foul object and sees a large serpent in the wasteland in far flung Kalimdor. Brother Crowley already had his suspicions about the undead brewing in the Desolace but this furthers his resolve on his war against the Scourge. Brother Crowley urgently asks the player to go to Brother Anton in the Desolace to get to the bottom of this ancient evil and rid to from Azeroth!

 - Seek out Brother Anton in the wasteland of the Desolace in far flung Kalimdor.

Speak to Brother Anton in the Desolace to hand in Quest IV (Brother Anton begins Quest V).

Quest V Brother Anton thinks that Ancient Unholy Artifact is related to the giant skeleton of an ancient serpent and he wants the player to investigate the massive ancient serpent skeleton and the evil emanating from its remains. Brother Anton wants the player to vanquish any evil spirits and black magic they come across; hopefully riding the world of the evil once and for all...

(Go to the base of the massive ancient serpent skeleton use Ancient Unholy Artifact upon a dragon-like skull in to the ground, summons an elite Level 45 draco-lich undead wind serpent (hard group quest like Mor'Ladim) [3D Model](https://classic.wowhead.com/npc=8443/avatar-of-hakkar/url), loot Red Soul Crystal from draco-lich undead wind serpent, return to Brother Anton with Red Soul Crystal and Ancient Unholy Artifact)

 - Seek out the massive ancient serpent skeleton and the evil emanating from its remains in the Desolace and vanquish any evil the player may encounter.

Brother Anton rewards the player's valiant victory against the undead of Azeroth and gives a 'pick one reward' of green level 45 gear: a Caster Staff (same model as the one he is holding), Leather Crit agility stamina boots (full red leather boots), Mail Crit attack power stamina belt (Same model as he is wearing), plate hit crit dps shoulders (same model he is wearing) and an 'also receive reward:' Tabard of the Scarlet Crusade (same model as he is wearing). Quest V ends but the Red Soul Crystal begins a quest for Quest VI.

Quest VI: Return to the Ghost of Morgan Ladimore with the Red Soul Crystal victorious over the ancient evil that he and his Brother in Arms left unresolved.

Morgan Ladimore feels a great deal of fulfillment and wishes to imbue his soul's will into the soul gem and infuse his legacy of retribution and redemption into his past blade Archeus.

 - Return to the Ghost of Morgan Ladimore with the Red Soul Crystal.

The Ghost of Morgan Ladimore raises Archeus for the last time and in a blinding flash (level up animation) Shouts "At long last I have been redeemed! I am the spirit of redemption! I have the power!" and the quest is completed with one of the four rewards chosen.
