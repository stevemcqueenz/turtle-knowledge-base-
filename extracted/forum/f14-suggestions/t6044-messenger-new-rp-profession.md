# Messenger. New RP profession

## Post 38008 by Bloodline1x9 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-16T17:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38008#p38008 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Today, while I was on the ship, fully dressed in a set from Scarlet Monastery, I had the desire to give one of the passengers a letter inviting him somewhere. Well, just for fun. Unfortunately, I couldn't write such a letter to have it in my inventory to pass from hand to hand. And then I had this idea. Make a profession that allows you to write letters that can be transmitted. This will be cool for RP. You can also congratulate someone. Use as a note. Or make a threat. Or an anonymous declaration of love. Troll your friend.
In order for the letter to be anonymous, it must be taken to the NPS and written in the field to whom it should be given. NPS distributes jobs to those who want to be messengers. The reward is a little money and the reputation of the faction to which the NPC belongs. In order to prevent anyone from spamming letters, there will be only one letter per day that can be sent anonymously. In addition, need to be level 30+
Every messenger has a reputation. For those who want to be postmen, they need to do several quests. These are common quests to carry a letter from one NPS to another.
Each letter +1 to reputation. When the reputation is 5+, he can take letters from players. Delivery takes 24 hours. If the letter is not delivered within this time, the messenger receives -3 reputation. If the postman gains 10+ reputation, his flights on the griffin will be free. But not forever. If he stops delivering letters, after a couple of days his reputation will begin to slowly decrease.
If the messenger gains 30 reputation, he will receive an enchantment for boots that gives +10% movement speed when not in combat. If the messenger gains 100 reputation, he will receive a title and and -50% of the price for riding training skill (100% mount) or epic mount (choice 1 of 2 or discount or epic mount) But it will be difficult to achieve, because you can only take one delivery order per day, and uncompleted tasks will do -3 reputation. After 30 reputation, you can take 2 orders per day. If there is a letter from the player in the NPS, and the messenger has 5+ reputation, then the NPS will first of all issue a task from the player.
I do not think that there will be many people who want the profession of a messenger. Rather, it will be a bonus in leveling, which will allow you to save a little on travel and improve your reputation with factions.
Admins will not have to write the text of the letters because they will be sealed. But it allows you to realize some interesting tricks. For example, how Thrall writes a letter to Jaina. The Horde messenger takes the letter from Thrall and carries it to Ratchet, leaving to Gazlowe. And he orders the Alliance players to bring that letter to Theramore Jaina.
