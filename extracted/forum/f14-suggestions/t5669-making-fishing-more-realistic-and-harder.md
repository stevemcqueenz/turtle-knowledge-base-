# Making fishing more "realistic" and harder

## Post 35409 by Remmo87 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-20T10:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35409#p35409 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Make fishing more "realistic" and harder by
dramatically reducing the chance to level up and the chance/amount of fish fished in waters outside of fish schools.

That makes it so you'll have to look for the schools to
be efficient. To me it seems more immersive and challenging, and more rewarding in the end.

At the least, make it a challenge you can accept at level 1.

here are some ways to complicate and make fishing more realistic:
 **Weather conditions:** The fishing system in World of Warcraft could be made more realistic by including weather conditions such as rain or wind that affect fishing success. For example, fishing during a downpour can increase the chances of catching certain types of fish, while fishing during strong winds can reduce the chances of a successful catch.
 **Bait and Wobblers**: To make fishing more realistic, players may need to choose the right bait and wobblers for the type of fish they are trying to catch. Different types of fish may require different types of bait, and using the wrong type of bait can lead to an unsuccessful catch or even damage to fishing gear.
 **Depth and location**: In addition to choosing the right bait and baits, players may also have to consider the depth and location of the fish they are trying to catch. Some fish species can only be found in deeper waters, while others can only be found in certain places, such as near rocks or under docks.
 **Time of Day**: To further complicate fishing, the time of day can also affect the success of fishing. Some fish species may be active only at certain times of the day or night, and trying to catch them at the wrong time may lead to an unsuccessful catch.
Fish Size and Weight: In a more realistic fishing system, players may also need to consider the size and weight of the fish they are catching. Larger fish may need stronger fishing gear, and reeling may take longer, while smaller fish may be easier to catch, but they may not bring as much reward.
