# [SUGGESTION: SHAMAN TALENT CHANGES] To consider: A minimal flavour buffs for Shaman totems and move some talents.

## Post 126071 by Argieman — 2025-04-08T23:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126071#p126071 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!

I come to present an idea regarding a modest and straightforward modification to the shaman’s totems, one that minimally impacts balance while adding a touch of flavor to the totems and simultaneously providing shamans with a subtle benefit, and a better tool for pvp, since improved ghost wolf is unusable, we can make an offensive mobility instead of a personal root break.

The core proposal would be to grant the ELEMENTAL and ENHANCEMENT, talents a slight boost to the area of effect for the FIRE and EARTH totems respectively.

Moreover, i will propose a swap of talent places to make leveling, tanking, and RESTORATION pvp a little easier.

Proposed affected Talents (could be new talents, or other pre-existing talents)

[ELEMENTAL] Call of Flame: Increases range of Fire Nova Totem in 5/10/15 Yards and Searing Totem in 3-5-7 Yards. This might also make this talent more attractive.

[ENHANCEMENT] Enhancing Totems: Besides current buffs, we make it so it Increases the Effects of Earth Totems in 5/10 Yards. This will further shift the role of enhancement's support role in pvp by Affecting Earthbind, for pve balancing pourposes maybe not extend this effect to stoneclaw or make it 2/4 yards.

[RESTORATION] This buff is already present in the form of Totemic Mastery

Talent placement proposal: I propose to swap the placement of these talents. First Ghost Wolf is learnt aat lvl 20, so the talent should be a tier 3 one; second the resto shaman pvp and enha tanking will be greatly benefited by having a shield buff earlier. As many know, Shield specialization is a very bad talent, so we could make possible to reach spirit armor earlier.

There are parts I agree with.
Ghost Wolf talent this early, before you can learn the ability is indeed weird and shaman dungeon tanking's biggest problem is how fragile it is at early game. That swap could totally make sense.
Also, shield spec is not a good talent. It works for warriors and palas who can reach cap and actively increase block% with abilities.
In case of a shaman, the +5% block chance only gies an unreliable mitigation (no shield block, no holy shield), thus +amount blocked is also almost irrelevant, it raises the mitigation amount of blocks you have no control over and happen kind of rarely anyway (shaman primarily dodges, not blocks).

About the totems, I'm less sure. Searing totem is handled in ele tree and it is primarily a totem for ele.
Raising ground totem range seems weird, we have a resto talent raising totem range in general. I don't think enh should have a big totem range, anyway - by concept he is in a party with melees, fighting close to eachother at melee range.
