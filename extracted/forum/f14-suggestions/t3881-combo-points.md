Could we get the TBC version where they stay on the target till you generate a point on another?

The current implementation is unfun and limiting. Losing points by merely dropping a target or targeting another is just awful gameplay design which punishes active, aware play.

Other classes do not lose anything from swapping to interrupt a caster or to break a totem, rogues and druids lose all the points they've been setting up so far, which only incentivizes one to tunnel vision.
Not being able to freely blind/kick is also terrible in PvP.
