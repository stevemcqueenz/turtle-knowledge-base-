Was thinking the other day about how both of these mounts have wings, so It doesn't make sense they wouldn't have any flying capabilities.

My thoughts were and I think this is cool, to add a slow fall effect on these mounts or any mount that has wings, that is obtainable in the game and not the shop (I think it should be exclusive for people that actually farm them out). So not making them able to fly but at least have some sort of skill where they could utilize their wings for the RP element. Idk it's seems cool to me and makes sense and I think it would be fun.
