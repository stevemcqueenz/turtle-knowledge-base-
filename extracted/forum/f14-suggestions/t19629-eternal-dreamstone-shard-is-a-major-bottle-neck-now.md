## Post 133591 by Monkebonk — 2025-05-30T03:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133591#p133591 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Eternal dreamstone shards transmutes are even more on demand nowadays when the droprate of Scythe of Elune is increased.

Lets say 1k players do both raids on lockout. That means 20 Kara10 per day, which gives 0,5 scythes per day. There's 3,6 ES per day, which gives 0,036 formulas per day. You can transmute infinetly but it takes 600 days (!) to "breakeven" with the amount of scythes that drop (0,5/0,036*6 dreamstone shard * 7 transmutation day cd).

It's a major bottle neck for people once they get their scythe. Monthly average prices on AH makes it cost 9,4k for the crafting materials for the scythe and it will get worse.
