So idea is, to add profession that would allow, to craft some types of weapons, usually made from wood and cant be crafted with current professions, that we have now, like: bows, crossbows, staves. Also it can craft some items that other professions can make. List of items, that can be there: bows, crossbows, wands, shields, maces, 2H maces, staves, polearms, relics for druid and shaman, advanced versions of boats (or just change boats from survival to rafts, because boat looks, to advanced for this profession). Lorewise, there can be added other types of weapons like: axes, there was one orc named Broxigar that wielded wooden axe, created by Malfurion and Cenarius. That means, night elves can have racial bonus for this profession. It will provide, wide range of items, that can be used by each class.
Woodcutter would be gathering professinon to back it.

 7.It might be like that, each profession could have some small coverage of item types, that other professions can produce. I was hesitant to comment on this. I didn't think it was a *bad* idea, but there was something that didn't sit right with me. I think other commenters have highlighted what it was. With the professions already in the game, you could cover all of this already. It doesn't *need* to be woodworking specifically. Now, don't get me wrong, I think the idea of more professions is interesting and flavorful, but it does have the issue that there will be a dearth of people able to make the new recipes until people either make new toons or change professions, either of which still requires levelling them up. Fine for something that's doing something new, but less than ideal for something meant to fill in gaps in the progression system.
