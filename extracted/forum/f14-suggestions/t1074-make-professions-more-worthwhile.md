# Make professions more worthwhile

## Post 6177 by Weilan — 2020-05-31T09:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6177#p6177 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My problem with WoW is that professions are somehow tied to your level and the zones you are, they evolve as you travel the world and level up, but there is little incentive to go back to previous zones.

This issue is further expanded by the power creep and vertical progression of the game where you have no reason to go back to old zones once you've done all the quests or leveled your professions past the point where you need to be spending time there.

I don't exactly have any concrete ideas on how this can be achieved, but maybe taking inspiration from Old School RuneScape can be a good idea on how professions can keep players going back to old locations and spending a lot of time there.

Maybe creating new crafting recipes and new items can do that?

The main goal of my idea is to expand the game with something that doesn't make you feel you've reached END GAME TM once you reach 60 and start doing raids to gear up and after that .. at one point your character is truly complete and there is no point in playing that chracter.

I always supported the philosophy of single character MMO experience, where you create just one character and the game provides you with enough content, activities, reasons to keep playing that character forever that you don't need to create additional character and start all over.

I will come back to this thread and expand it once I get more ideas.
