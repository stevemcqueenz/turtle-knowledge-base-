# PvP Suggestion

## Post 128824 by Vorse — 2025-04-28T13:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128824#p128824 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone,

I wanted to bring up a suggestion about PvP behavior concerning players who toggle War Mode. Right now, players can turn PvP on, cheap-shot someone, and immediately flick it off with zero consequences—essentially abusing the system without risk. War Mode is supposed to have risk baked into it, but currently, there’s no real accountability for bad-faith behavior, especially as the PvP server itself is thinning out with people returning to their original servers.

To keep things simple: if someone chooses to initiate PvP combat, they should be locked into PvP mode for 12–24 hours. The idea isn’t to punish competition—getting killed for a herb or outplayed for mobs is part of the game and always will be—but there should be a meaningful commitment when you choose to fight. Right now, players can grief, kill, steal resources, and instantly return to safety before anyone even has a chance to respond or retaliate. That’s not competition; that’s exploiting a loophole.

By requiring players who attack in PvP to remain flagged for a while, it would encourage real skirmishes, ongoing rivalries, and give the world more life and consequence, instead of letting people disappear with no window for a comeback. It would promote more dynamic world PvP, more emotional payoffs, and far less frustration from getting blindsided by people abusing toggles for easy profit.

In the end, World of Warcraft is still supposed to be a world—and world PvP deserves enough respect to ensure players actually stand behind their choices. Just my two cents, but I think it’s a fair way to make fights feel real again, instead of just frustrating, one-sided interactions.

I’d love to hear what others think!

Fair enough. To put things simply: yes, War Mode is a risk — but it shouldn't be something that others can abuse with zero repercussions, especially when most of the PvP server itself is relatively dead, with a lot of folks going back to their original servers.

The only suggestion I’m making is to lock a player into PvP mode (for 12–24 hours) after they initiate an attack, in order to prevent people from abusing the ability to flicker back into safety with no means of retaliation. The primary point is that you shouldn't be able to recluse back into safety after griefing someone. Competition is one thing; abusing a system is another.

I'm all for getting killed over a herb and having it stolen — that's part of the game. All that's being asked is that if a player is ready to commit to a fight for whatever reason, there should be windows of opportunity for others to respond and retaliate, even if it's just for the satisfaction of trying. We are playing World of Warcraft after all, and PvP deserves its own respect as part of the world and the gameplay experience.

Clearly, this is subjective and just my opinion, but it’s a suggestion meant to promote more lasting fights and opportunities for closure, with much less of the "feels bad" situations. I can accept getting beat over and over until my face turns purple — that's fair play. But when you get into a situation where all you can do is just stand there and watch someone grief you again and again, without any chance to respond because they can toggle off PvP, it gets incredibly frustrating. Especially when there's a clear opportunity for people to profit off that loophole.

This change would simply keep players committed to their choice once they engage in PvP. I'm sure I'm not the only one who’s come across this issue before.
