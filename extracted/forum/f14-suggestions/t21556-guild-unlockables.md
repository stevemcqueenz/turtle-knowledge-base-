# Guild unlockables

## Post 149380 by Pawstarat — 2025-08-30T15:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149380#p149380 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, i've overall liked the idea of Leveling a Guild or unlocking things over achievements.
I think especially for Hardcore it might be a nice experience to have some sort of "Guild Goals" you could work on collectively, so even if you die you pushed a "Guild Goal" further.

Thought mainly of little goodies, nothing that would impact Hardcore too much.
Stuff like Transmogs, Pets and such for 50 (or 100) times clearing a Dungeon with a Guild Group.
Like a Little Cookie Jr. for clearing DM 50-100x with your Guild.

Clearing Hardcore Raids could give some more beneficial passives
for example 20 Zul Gurub clears reduce the Heartstone Cooldown of everyone in the guild by 15-20 minutes
