Cheers! 3 ideas:

 1) Let the tool browse ALL dungeons, not only the ones available for the logged in character.
As it is right now, I can check for RFC groups for my alt while I'm logged into my main, but not check for BRD groups for my main while logged into my alt. Why not?

 2) When queueing with a group for a dungeon, let players select multiple roles instead of only one. E.g. a druid might want to queue as dps, heal OR tank.

 3) The automatic grouping doesn't seem to work properly right now (or at least feels that way  turtle_tongue_head  ) .
I'd personally prefer a tbc-like grouping tool for signing up for dungeons and posting small notes like (druid, can tank or heal) and just shows a player who's available for a group, so people are encouraged to form their groups (here it comes...) by communicating instead simply queueing.
