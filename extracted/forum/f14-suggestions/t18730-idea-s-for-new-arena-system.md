# Idea's for NEW ARENA System

## Post 127501 by Nkarie — 2025-04-19T13:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127501#p127501 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!
Dear developers, considering that you are developing a new reward system in PvP and including the arena, I would like to offer you a number of solutions.

So, the system consists of buying a basic item and then upgrading it with TOKEN. Example: Gloves A1 + Token A2 = Gloves A2,  etc. You can buy thing for arena points(AP) or Honor, but a4 only AP. Example: off-hand A1 will cost 60AP or 10k Honor, but off-hand A4 is 700AP only.
For further understanding, I will post a link to a table with the system itself.
maintenance_turtle
[embed: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nf_Lk5h_0TXVgS7Hnh_S2eWE3wnKrzlc-lvFNwSigfk/preview#gid=]

Main points according to the table:
Tier Arena 1(A1):
 - Blue items with low combat stats and low stamina. Lower than current 7 rank pvp, worse than pre-raid
 - Does not require arena rating to purchase
 - There are no rings or trinkets, so as not to break the economy and not to cancel the need for 5-man dungeons
 - Requires resources in honor and arena points to be able to get it in 1 week
 - Needed to play BG or get to the first level of the rating arena(1100AR)

Tier Arena 2(A2):
 - Stronger than current 8 rank pvp items, but weaker than the exalted current blood ring set. Approximately the level of BWL.
 - There are also no rings or trinkets.
 - Requires resources in honor and arena points to be able to get it in 2 weeks
 - Requires arena rating from 1100 to 1400
 - Has a lot of stamina, and not bad damage, but less than the current T2 set
 - Needed for fight confidently on BG's

Tier Arena 3(A3):
 - Stronger than current 10 rank pvp items, and exalted current blood ring set. The difference between the best Blood - Ring gear and rank 13 is too big, that's why I think this is the best place for A3
 - Requires resources in honor and arena points to be able to get it in 3-4 weeks
 - Requires arena rating from 1500 to 1800
 - Strong stamina bonus, strong damage. Damage is stronger than T2 and weaker than T2.5
 - This set adds PvP rings
 - Necessary for BGs dominance and high arena rating rush

Tier Arena 4(A4):
 - Equivalent to rank 13 items or stronger. A Stronger stamina stats and damage equal T3, but lower than new KARA40
 - Requires resources in  arena points to be able to get it in 5-6 weeks
 - Requires arena rating from 1950 to 2200
 - This set adds NEW Trinkets
 - All gear is purchased with Arena Points only. HONOR is not involved in this cycle!!!
 - Necessary for PVP Dominance and Gladiator status  rush

Conclusion
Fully equiped time is 2 months and 2-4 weeks(variable). This time is comparable to getting a full T3 from Naxx when you raid with a good PVE guild on Tel'abim server. I would also like to point out that to get a top A4 set you will need not only resources, but also a serious arena rating, which may not be easy in a highly competitive environment. And the system of upgrading equip to the next level using tokens will avoid situations where people skip buying lower levels of equipment in order to immediately buy higher quality equipment.

Guys, what you think about this system, plz leave your comments.

(1) It should be clear to everyone that the new PvP System must offer exclusive itemization that does not force the PvP player to raid.
(2) This Itemization must somehow be comparable to the PVE Tier System.
(3) It must at least partly rely on getting high arena rankings. Otherwise, we have a system that solely relies on grinding again. Every PvP player would agree that this is not desirable.

Your proposed system checks all that boxes and good gear is achievable in a reasonable time frame.

> No because this system would only favor those who play from the very beginning

I see no way to avoid this. Also, I don't really see the problem there. You will have to put in some effort. The good thing about PvP itemization is that you will improve in the game while getting there instead of just being dragged through early raid content by overgeared people. Of course, the first experiences might be frustrating because you can lose to lower-skilled people with better gear, but again, I see no way to really avoid this.

> Not a big fan of arena ratings either but it's still better than the current system.

What is your alternative approach, then? Mindlessly grinding BGS in a premade group can't be the real deal in PvP, either, can it? I get the vanilla nostalgia, but it was TBC's Arena system that finally made being an exclusive PvP player viable.

Reading all the discussions in the forum about resilience, etc., I can't help but get the feeling that I hear PVE-oriented players complaining that they will lose their chance to hop into PvP content effortlessly. To me, this sounds like whining, that they would not be able to delete people because of the good gear they received from raiding.

Your proposed system sounds really good to me and turtle devs should definitely consider it. I trust in their ability to make unpopular decisions for this. Because their will be an outcry of (mainly PvE) players when the new PvP changes go life. But to make PvP a really good experience for PvP oriented players, itemization like this and arena ratings is the way to go.
