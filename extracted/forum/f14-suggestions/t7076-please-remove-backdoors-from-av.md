# Please remove backdoors from AV

## Post 44915 by Livingsaint — 2023-05-08T13:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44915#p44915 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to address the issue of backdoors in the Alterac Valley battleground. Backdoors are unintended exploits that allow players to bypass defenses and gain an unfair advantage over their opponents. Unfortunately, instead of removing the backdoor on the Alliance side, the devs decided to introduce a backdoor to the Horde side of the map.

Backdoors make the game less fun for everyone involved. They can end battles quickly, leaving players with a sense of dissatisfaction and frustration. They also favor one side over the other, making the game anticompetitive and unbalanced. The Alliance is already favored in Alterac Valley, and backdoors only exacerbate this problem, as the backdoor on the Horde side is objectively easier and better positioned.

Furthermore, backdoors go against the spirit of the original design by Blizzard. Alterac Valley was meant to be a battleground where players would engage in epic battles that could last for hours. The map was designed to have chokepoints and strategic locations that would make battles intense and thrilling. Backdoors were never intended to be part of the game, and their presence undermines the integrity of the battleground, and invalidates much of the original design of the BG.

Removing backdoors would level the playing field and make Alterac Valley a fairer battleground for all players. It would also bring the game back in line with its original design, creating a more enjoyable and competitive experience for everyone.

I urge the game developers to remove backdoors from Alterac Valley. Let's keep the game fun, fair, and true to its original spirit. Thank you for your attention.
