# Trolls - Duel Wield Throwing Weapons

## Post 131241 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-15T15:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131241#p131241 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We got Night Elf priests with bows. Optimal or not, it's a cool addition. So how about Troll Hunters that can duel wield 2 throwing weapons?

 **Let's first start with the immediate cons from a technical standpoint:**
---
 - **[Depending on implementation]** An additional weapon slot for a 2nd throwing weapon must be added. That may not be feasible on the current client version. The reason for this approach would be so durability is lost at a slower rate for throwing weapons. Additionally, since there are fewer throwing weapons with stats (or at least stats suitable for a ranged character)
 - A throwing weapon must be mapped to their left hand, so it looks like they're holding onto it.
 - A completely new animation will need to be added for Trolls throwing with their left hand as well.
 - As to not be entirely useless, Hunter abilities will have to be able to utilize throwing weapons as a resource. That is an entirely different can of worms
 - Possibly others

This is more of a wish list item, since Blizzard never got around to it, and will hardly ever consider it. Historically, Trolls have more often than not duel wielded throwing weapons, starting way back in Warcraft 2. Even in Warcraft 3, Darkspear Troll Berserkers duel wield spears. With TWoW trying to encourage class fantasy, it would be a great addition, even if it was terrible dps wise
