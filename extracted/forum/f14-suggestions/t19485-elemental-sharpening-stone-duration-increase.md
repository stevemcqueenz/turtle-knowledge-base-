# Elemental Sharpening Stone Duration Increase

## Post 132597 by Melvorforum — 2025-05-23T03:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132597#p132597 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Now that the most current raid is not filled with undead, melee dps (fury warrior in particular) now have to use 4 elemental sharpening stones an hour.
At the timing of this post, a consecrated sharpening stone costs 2g 73s and lasts for 1 hour.
An elemental sharpening stone costs 6g 91s and lasts for 30 minutes.
I'm ok with the elemental sharpening stone being at this price, but I think the duration should be increased to 1 hour.
The transmute elemental earth spell did not help very much in reducing the price of these stones, I think we need something else to make them more affordable
