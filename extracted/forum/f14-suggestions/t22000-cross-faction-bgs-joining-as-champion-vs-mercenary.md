# Cross Faction BGs: joining as Champion vs Mercenary

## Post 152518 by Catyngla (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-21T00:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152518#p152518 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's a rough idea that I hope more can help polishing up.

The idea is to allow cross faction BGs and keep in harmony with the lore.

 **The idea:**
We would be allowed to join BGs queues in 2 ways: as a Champion of our faction or as a Mercenary.

If joining as a Champion, we join the BG in our race faction.
Rewards:
 - Reputations
 - Honor
 - Marks
 - Anything else you can think of, that a Champion of the Alliance/Horde could have

If joining as a Mercenary, we join the BG in either side, with the queue assigning between the 2 factions to allow shorter wait and hopefully better balance.
Rewards:
 - Gold?
 - No marks, no honor, no rep
 - Anything else you can think of, that a greedy Mercenary would be interested in

The potential is that someone who is geared up, is more likely joining as a Mercenary, so hopefully it would balance both sides gear wise.

I understand the queues are long on the Horde side. The cross faction should reduce that.

I also understand that Horde wins way more often than Alliance. Again the cross faction should balance that.

Lore wise, if you're a Champion, you proudly represent the faction of your race, hence perks related to it (as listed above). But if you're a Mercenary, you are driven by the greed, and aren't interested in the politics of Azeroth, hence money/gold.

However I don't know how is the economy at max level, or between the servers, so perhaps gold isn't enough motivating as a reward. I know that Nordanaar has more gold circulating than Ambershire, being a fresh server. I just can't think what other rewards to put for a Mercenary. I wouldn't dare saying tokens as I doubt Devs would be keen on that.

Please accept my apologies if there are flaws in my suggestion, I don't have any level 60 characters, so it's mostly based on what I can read on the forum and Discord.
