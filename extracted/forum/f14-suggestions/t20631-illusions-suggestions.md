# Illusions Suggestions

## Post 142765 by Mekk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-02T16:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142765#p142765 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been playing TWoW for a few months now, and I must say that I'm already a huge fan of Illusions.  After playing WoW for 20 years, I find the ability to play as some of the other races a breath of fresh air.  I understand that adding new races can be a real pain, and I know many races would never be "officially" playable due to lore reasons, and I feel that Illusions are a perfect compromise to that.  So I want to offer a heartfelt thanks to the team for that.

Now, I do have some suggestions, as this *is* the suggestions part of the forum.   smiling_turtle

Would it be possible to have the main Illusion teach you each costume as a separate ability?  The round robin approach is perfectly fine as it is, but being able to select the one you want would be a nice QoL feature.  Then, if you add new costume variants (color combinations and the such) in the future, the player would only need to re-cast the main Illusion ability to learn the new costumes.  Speaking of...

Are there any plans to add in new costume variants to already existing Illusions?  I think there are a few additional Harpy combos out there, and I know there are more Naga colors.  This would be another nice QoL update.

Let's talk Flight Paths.  Is there a tech reason why characters cannot use Flight Paths while an Illusion is active?  I ask because the Wyvern/Griffin "Mounts" in the starting zones keep the Illusion active; though I guess those are technically closer to Mounts than Flight Paths.  Again, its not the end of the world, but would be another nice QoL improvement.

Now, on to new Illusions. :)  I would love to see Centaur, Quilboar, and even those Leper Gnomes as Illusions.  Quilboar could be a decent Boaring reward, but I'd also like the ability to start at L1 with the Illusion.

My last suggestion is themed to work alongside Illusions, but would not require the player to use them.  I think it would be interesting to have a Challenge (could be called Diplomat) in which you start as Unfriendly to everyone, and have 50% less Rep gains.  The idea is, if I'm playing as a Gnoll or Harpy or w/e, then having a Rep debuff would be both thematic from an RP sense, as well as an interesting challenge.  Perhaps this could be two separate challenges, a Normal version for just your Faction and a Advanced version for *all* Factions; knowing that the Advanced version is going to slow down your end-game progression (but a Slow and Steady + Advanced Diplomat could be a fun combo).
