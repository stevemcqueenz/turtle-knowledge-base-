# Some low level BG ideas.

## Post 93558 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-26T13:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93558#p93558 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Now that turtle has enabled the BG queue announcer again lower level BG's seem to be opening more often, but there's still work to be done.

I think we could award experience for capping flags or winning the game, so this would give people who want to pvp to level up a way to do so, and make the BG's more active.

Another idea is to remove the level requirements for ranks. You can't go past Rank 3 if you're not level 33 etc. We could then buff the low rank gear like the sergeants cloak or add more of them, I don't know anyone who uses the rank 4 and 5 Bracers and amulet - they need a buff.

Maybe give the Stone Guards / Knights tabard a teleport to the Hall of Champions / Legends in Orgrimmar / Stormwind, on like a 2 hour cool down, this is sort of similar how guild tabard teleport you to your guild house, and would incentivise levelers to get the teleport and cheap potions you get.

Another idea is a daily quest for joining a BG or something that rewards a random blue item from turning the quest in that your class can use.

Sure there's more ideas but this could be a good start.
