# A suggestion to foster the Horde RP community: Better Horde Taverns

## Post 17627 by Bellybutton (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-03-21T16:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17627#p17627 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's not really a secret that the Horde got the short end of the stick when it comes to RP-friendly city and town design. Alliance get so many large taverns across Human and Dwarf settlements across Azeroth and within their capital cities, full of things like chairs, multiple rooms ranging from kitchens, basements, bedrooms all scattered across the world. Horde architecture, especially interiors feel very barebones in comparison.

Love or hate tavern RP, taverns and bars, in retail RP servers, have always served as the go-to hub for casual RPers who don't want to go through the hoops of organizing RP, going to events, or asking around in chat channels. This is also evident on Turtle WoW. Random, walk-up RP tends to be far more common on Alliance because Blizzard designed friendlier RP infrastructure for Alliance cities and Alliance towns compared to the Horde. Because of this, Horde RP tends to be a lot more constricted to organized events between multiple players and guilds, and satisfying tavern RP can only happen in the Cross Roads or in Neutral towns such as Ratchet or Booty Bay.

The Turtle-made changes to the Inn in Cross Roads is a good start, but that's just the Cross Roads; Higher level players don't often go to Cross Roads, so Cross Roads is really out of they way if one wants random walk-up roleplay, and this is evident by the lack of RP in Cross Roads despite the changes. Orgrimmar and Thunder Bluff are devoid of RP friendly hubs, unlike Ironforge and Stormwind who both have many large taverns where a lot of roleplay plotlines start. Orcish and Tauren inns are painfully small by design and often just serve the purpose of setting your hearthstone.

My suggestion, after talking with some roleplayers, is to expand upon the Inns in Orgrimmar and Thunderbluff and transform at least some of them into larger taverns, not unlike what Blizzard already did in Cataclysm. This would mean changing the buildings entirely because the existing buildings are frighteningly tiny,  but I think that's a completely feasible change and in the long run would do wonders to bolster random Horde roleplay. As of right now, on Horde, if you truly want to RP, you either have to join a guild, join an event, ask around OOCly, or randomly stumble across another roleplayer since there's not really any hubs on Horde, unlike Alliance where taverns are a natural beacon for roleplay even outside of major cities.

This also raises the possibility of expanding upon Horde Inns in random towns like Splintertree or Razor Hill; to make up for much better Alliance equivalents such as Menethil Harbour's Inn, Southshore's Inn, Lakeshire's Inn, or Darkshire's inn, or Theramore's inn, you get the picture.
