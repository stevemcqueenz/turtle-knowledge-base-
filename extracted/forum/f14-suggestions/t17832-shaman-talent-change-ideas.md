# Shaman talent change ideas

## Post 121536 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-03T19:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121536#p121536 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

With the power of a custom talent tree creator I wanted to share with you my vision of shaman.
I'll keep abilities as they currently are, will only change talents.
My philosophy was to keep the flavour of each build while adding everything I wanted to see.
Changed talents are always highlighted and I will give a reason for each change in this article.

 **Elemental**
<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/zwdRcAMqAs>

In case of elemental shaman, wanted achieve the below things:
 * Enabling the player-base a fun elemental build even before the late 30s.
 * Pushing elemental a bit to the caster utility direction, helping them find a caster party more often than not.
 * Made Electrify more consistent.
 * Smaller changes I'll describe below.

 **The changes as they occur**
 **Earth's Grasp:** Stoneclaw HP buff is almost totally useless, gave a slight Searing Totem buff there instead. Was considering a Tranquil Air Totem buff but it comes too late and it's a second row ability. Also, giving too much threat reduction can hurt the game.

 **Call of Thunder + Lightning Mastery:** Made a switcheroo. To avoid lower level elemental shamans to be too strong with the reduced cast time, also added a damage reduction to Lightning Mastery, which is later removed by Call of Thunder.

 **Eye of the Storm:** Reduced the focused buff's percentage but made it trigger from the crits you deal.

 **Call of Flame:** Got rid of Flame Guidance and added the critical damage part here.

 **Electrify:** Added shocks as Electrify-enablers and added 2,5% crit chance for each Electrify stack. I considered this pushing the talent too much but I removed the added crit chance from Tidal Mastery (see Restoration tree) and increased Elemental Mastery's cooldown.

 **Elemental Mastery:** Temporarily made it more or less a spell crit-based bloodlust clone. Wanted to keep both the spell crit aspect and the team utility aspect - the current version gives 15% spell crit for 15 seconds and each of your spell crit grants a party-wide 5% spell crit buff which is non-stackable. Raised the talent's cooldown to 5 minutes. I'm willing to change this if it turns out to be game-breaking.

(Nerfed elemental shaman individually but made it much better as a caster group support build. Raised the talent's cooldown from 3 minutes to 5 but granted the effect of Elemental Mastery to each party members. A push towards those caster groups and some unique elemental utility. I'm open to raise the CD up to 7-8 minutes if balance requires it but I think it is fine. <- Dummy ideas goes to trashcan.)

 **Added a new talent, Wrath of Flames Totem.** It is a new totem from the fire element, granting spell damage for casters in your party, equalling to 10% of your intellect. Another push for a caster party utility.

 **How the spec would change?**
Elemental shaman would be playable at early levels, thanks to the faster (but weaker) lightning bolts and chain lightnings. Fire totems, searing in general would receive a slight push but I don't think it'd affect things too much. Made Electrify more consistent so elemental's damage build-up is also more consistent. In exchange, I've cut off from the burstiness with the bigger Elemental Mastery cooldown + the Electrify-Tidal Mastery crit change (also an NS change but it's for Restoration). In exchange gave them a slight passive DPS boost with the spell damage totem - if damaging fire totems can't be used - and a big chunk of caster group utility.
Elemental shamans could provide a lot to caster groups by using Tranquil Air Totem, Wrath of Flames Totem and Elemental Mastery.

 **Enhancement**
<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/NrkwmZ0G6r>

My philosophy here was to push warden shamans into becoming a fully viable raid tank while fine-tuning the current enhancement DPS.

 **The changes as they occur**
 **Shield Specialization:** Took away the extra blocked amount during blocks and gave them instead a one charge refill for their elemental shield in use.
None of these effects are super important since shamans can't have on-demand block chance like warriors and paladins but elemental shield has more internal synergy in this talent tree than block amount does.

 **Calming Winds:** Granted the threat reduction to shocks, too. In my opinion, enhancement does a lot of threat compared to the damage it does, partially because they have an on-CD-pressed shock in their toolkit with a threat modifier.

 **Flurry:** Reduced the haste increase from 15% to 10% after a crit but also added this effect: 'Dealing a critical strike also triggers an empowered version of your active shield, consuming 1 charge.' Pushing elementals into builds which rely more on elemental shields is my intention.

 **Enhancing Totems:** Removed the +blocked amount effect from Stoneskin Totem and added a +block chance effect instead. Blocked amount has no synergies for this build but the added block chance can help pushing out crushing blows and trigger the free elemental shield charges from the Shield Specialization talent.

 **Elemental Weapons:** Pushed flametongue a bit, granting +5% effectiveness of the elemental shield procs. Added defense to rockbiter, equaling to 10% of your agility. With really high-end gear and consumes, it means 45-50 defense. Sounds a lot but mail has real problems to gather defense otherwise, except for T2,5. This, by itself will not push ahead shamans at 'the defense race' but will give them respectable defense for raids and an option to push out those crushing blows (without really compromising at the gear). Finally, gave windfury weapon a mana refill effect when windfury is being procced to give sustainability to enhancement (current effect is bad, removed it).

 **Added a new talent, Astral Phase for tanks:** It works as a moderately good defensive CD paired with a mana recharge option on a big cooldown (15 min). Added shield requirement to avoid it being effectively abused by enhancement DPS in PVP.

 **How the specs would change?**
Enhancement DPS would have somewhat lower threat and better mana sustain. Slight DPS increase, skewing more into the spell damage (more shield charge procs, slightly lower haste).
Warden specially would have an elemental shield synergy with blocks refilling charges and criticals proccing those. It helps a lot with mana management. Survivability is heavily buffed, mostly thanks to the passive defense stat gain and the defensive CD.

 **Restoration**
<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/oOQgusYyoQ>

I think restoration is both a bit too simple and a bit too effective now. Besides having one of the best utility kit a build can provide, they have a super-fast chain heal coupled with an extremely reliable mana regeneration.
All in all I made restoration slightly weaker and forced the spec to make decisions about which healing spell to use and/or to consider spell downgrades as it used to.

 **The changes as they occur**

 **Nature's Grace + Ancestral Healing:** Made a switcheroo to help elementals getting the threat talent easier and faster.

 **Tidal Mastery:** It only gives critical to healing spells now. Elementals can get the 5% via Electrify now.

 **Healing Way:** I started a theme here. Removed Chain Heal from the Healing Way generators but added Lesser Healing Wave.

 **Totemic Reach:** A slight buff, increasing totem radius to 35 yards. I saw often enough how clunky it can be during raiding with 30.

 **Restorative Totems -> Revitalisation Totem:** Added a new totem which becomes free after 5 talent points of investment and combines the health regeneration and the mana regeneration effects of the restorative totems, slightly further increasing the health regeneration, since it was really bad.

 **Improved Water Shield:** Made a nerf here. The full talent used to give 9 mp5 + 2% meditation for each water shield orb (usually near 9). Now it gives 9 mp5 + 2 mp5 for each water shield orb. In short, swapped 18% meditation to 18 mp5. It is still a very good talent but forces shamans to be more careful with spells (and don't forget to downgrade sometimes). One reason I did this is because shamans aren't really encouraged to stack spirit stat otherwise this talent. Gearing can be more consistent.

 **Tidal Surge:** Took away the mana refill-effect and instead of that made Tidal Surge a Healing Wave and Lesser Healing Wave crit enabler to control Chain Heal's cast time reliably (see later).

 **Undertow:** To push Healing Wave and Lesser Healing Wave, made Undertow's success rate 100% but it can only trigger once every 3 seconds to avoid rank 1 spell spam abuses.

 **Improved Chain Heal:** Reduced the base cast time reduction to 0,5 sec from 1 sec but a Healing Wave or Lesser Healing Wave critical makes the next 2 Chain Heals further 0,5 sec shorter. I want to further encourage some kind of rotation with this.

 **Nature's Swiftness:** Only works with healing spells now but also grants a crit. Makes chain heal's cast reduction via HW and LHW more consistent.

 **How the spec would change?**

While still the best wide-healer and a utility powerhouse, resto shaman would have a slower heal output. Correctly placed HW- and LHW-criticals would ensure the reasonably fast flow of chain heals. The rotation changes, a large chunk of it being focused on single targets. Mana is more scarce, downgrading is more frequently needed.
Totems are less annoying now and Revitalisation Totem can become surprisingly useful.

I've rechecked the talents and considered your points.
I'm not sure what EM will be, I'll skip that part of the conversation, specially since we agree there.

As far as I see we have 3 points:
 1. Totemic Grasp (former Earth's Grasp - I have to admit, Manletow made a very fair naming remark there) including Searing Totem but excluding Stoneclaw.
 2. The talent switcheroo.
 3. Eye of the Storm.

For me the easiest to address is 3. I rechecked the wording of the talent and I left the parts 'melee or ranged' there by accident.
After correction the talent is worded like this:
'Gives you a 25/50/75% chance to gain the Focused Casting effect that lasts for 6 sec after dealing a critical strike. The Focused Casting effect prevents you from losing casting time when taking damage.'
I imagine this as a slightly different but weaker Burning Soul Fire Mage talent. For one more talent point each of your melee strike/spell crits give you 75% pushback reduction for 6 seconds. Unlike Burning Soul, it doesn't give threat reduction and is not passive, you need to consistently crit to maintain the pushback reduction.
I was thinking about reducing the TP-requirement for this. Didn't do so because 'freeing up' Tidal Mastery at Restoration gave a bunch of free TPs for elemental.

For the Totemic Grasp part. First I was considering if I want to push the talent deeper but I decided against it. There are certain totems which I'd have been interested in including in the talent, for example Windwall (giving it some kind of actual utility) or Tranquil Air (if the game can survive further threat reduction effects) mostly but both are air totems. I didn't want a player finish this talent potentially by level 16 and wait until level 30 to enjoy half of the benefits.
An option is to keep the talent as it is but I really don't like Stoneclaw there. It only has a potential upside when soloing against NPCs and what it does can usually be done even better via kiting and Earthbind, which is the other totem getting buffed here and it shares the totemic element with Stoneclaw. For me the dilemma was 'either a very rarely used totem which can usually be  substituted to kiting (any maybe losing LOS and using interrupts) or buffing a totem which shamans spam during the early game anyway'.
Was thinking about one more option: making this a half-tank talent and giving Stoneclaw added threat or reduced CD instead but I don't think that tank shamans need that kind of push.

The switcheroo has a simpler backstory. Personally if I level a shaman that's usually dungeon-spamming and resto or tank (here). Noticed that until mid-game I don't see any elemental shaman at all. Enhancements yes, elementals none. Did some asking around. People told me the moment it is worth to consider elemental is when you can finally learn Lightning Mastery.
Here I just wanted to make it happen earlier for more build-diversity during leveling. Gave it a damage reduction to avoid unintentionally high damage and later removed this damage nerf via Call of Thunder.
Here the whole concept was that Discord shamans told me that before finishing LM elemental doesn't worth it so I rushed the talent a bit while trying to keep the damage balanced.

If you want to/enjoy discussing it, we could continue via PM. I haven't really got a lot of feedbacks and I don't wanna force-push this theorycraft to the top of the Recent Topics.
Don't get me wrong, I'm totally okay with continuing the discussion if you decide so.
