# New item for the Glyphmaster: Glyph of the Bard

## Post 33454 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-01-29T15:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33454#p33454 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title says, this would be a new glyph available in the starting zones. Upon use, it would activate a new power. Suggested name and description - **Bardic Influence:** *"You have hired the services of a friendly bard."*

Having this power would increase faction reputation gains by a fair amount (maybe 60 or 75%), but decrease looted money. Basically, the glyph would act like having another player in your party that you share loot with. Coin lost to "pay the bard" would be deducted after party split, so that playing in a party doesn't shaft the other player(s) you're with.

Wanted to go back to this for a second. The most common amounts I've seen for reputation gains are 50, 150, and single digit "kill" reputation (I think I've seen both 3 and 5, and couldn't say what's more common). If those were increased by 15% (and assuming we round up), it would work out to increases of +8, +23, and...well, regardless of amount a single-digit gain would only increase by +1. Meanwhile, an increase of 60% would be +30, +90, and the single digit bonuses actually matter how much they are. Even at a 75% bonus, it only works out to +38, +113, about another +1 or 2 over 60% to single digit increases.

Now, the 75% buff might be a bit big, but keep in mind that it takes thousands to tens of thousands of reputation per level. Even with a big boost, grinding out rep is going to take time, and this is absolutely a boost for people that want to grind out a lot of rep. Also, 10% is equal to the Human bonus to reputation gains, and unless you want to argue that not getting a different racial bonus is a penalty, it doesn't cost them anything. So I'd argue that a bonus that comes with a downside needs to be bigger just based on that.

Now, maybe even 60% is too much, but my thought is that if you're going to stick players with an ongoing penalty throughout their playthrough, then the bonus should be substantial enough to feel worth it.
