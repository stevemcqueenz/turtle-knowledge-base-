# re-think the CD on the Goblin Brainwashing device

## Post 113123 by Harkonnan (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-18T04:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113123#p113123 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's horrible. I understand the logic behind a CD, but it's not just one, there's 2 CD's we have to juggle with the device. one on the device, one on us.

They should just have a CD on your spec change from the device, not on the device itself, or vice versa.  It really limits your ability to keep your off spec up to date w/o being stuck in that spec for 10m. or if you make a mistake, it locks you out of correcting it.

Here's my solution. it should only be one CD, on the character. When you first swap specs, it should put a spell on you for 2m that allows you to do whatever you want, then once the spell wears off, it applies a 10m debuff. That would allow you to update your offspec when necessary, then switch back to the spec you want to play, but still have that 10m CD to prevent spec swapping abuse (which I can only assume was the reason, it doesn't make any sense otherwise).

I would still be happy to remove them all...I paid for a device that did one thing, and now that thing is drastically changed after purchase. Doesn't feel very good at all. But I'd be willing to settle for my alternative above.
