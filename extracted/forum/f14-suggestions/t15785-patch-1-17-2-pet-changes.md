# Patch 1.17.2 — Pet changes

## Post 107607 by Tomavidsquera — 2024-11-11T23:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107607#p107607 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Further to Forum's post:

> PET CHANGES
>
>  These changes are aimed at making other pet families more viable, giving hunters a more diverse pool of pets to use.
>
>  Added two new pet families with new family-specific abilities: Snakes and Foxes.
>
>  Charge (Boar): Rank 4 can now be acquired in the world.
>
>  Lightning Breath: Rank 1 can now be acquired in the world.
>  Arrow: Raptors can now learn Arrow.
>  Furious Roar: Focus cost reduced from 60 to 50 and range increased from 15 to 40 meters.
>  Thunderclap: Now has a 1.5x threat modifier and its cooldown reduced from 1 minute to 40 seconds. Also added a new Rank 4 with higher damage values.
>  Scorpid Poison: Focus cost reduced from 30 to 25, cooldown reduced from 4 seconds to 3 seconds, base damage increased by 20%.
>  Carapace Shield: Attack speed reduction reduced from 43% to 35%.
>  Bite: Cooldown reduced from 10 seconds to 6 seconds.
>  Spell Power adjusted and Attack Power scaling added for most damaging pet abilities:
>  Poison Spit (Serpent): 0.25 for each tick of periodic damage.
>  Lightning Breath (Wind Serpent): 0.5
>  Scorpid Poison (Scorpid): 0.1 for each tick of periodic damage.
>  Thunder (Gorilla): 0.21
>  Bite: 1 bonus damage per 20 attack power.
>  Claw: 1 bonus damage per 42 attack power.
>  Developer Comment: This change primarily affects Beast Mastery Hunters who grant their pets spell power and attack power with the revamped Spirit Bond talent, though the increased attack power should very slightly affect other hunters' pets, increasing their bite and claw damage by 15 and 6-7 respectively when the pet is level 60.

Well, its sad to say, but some pets families are still left on the side ; they need some love.
The spider, the tallstriders and the crabs are really under the other pets families.

Suggestions would be to give some flavor to the other pets families that have been suffering from the recent updates.

 **SPIDERS**
In the real world, spiders are known and feared for being venomous, the following suggestion would be a good idea to live up to that reputation.
 -> **scorpid poison** or **spite poison**? (scorpid poison should be renamed of course but it's acceptable to say that the spider can bite a target repeatedly and thus stack poison on the target) or even the beta spell from vanilla 2004
 -> **venomous web**? *(in the 2004 vanilla beta)*

 **Tallstriders**
In the real world, ostriches are known to use their talons (claws) to fight enemies/predators and they scream like crazy; so why not give it
 -> **claw** or **screech** ?

 **Crab**
Finally, crabs are in real life a good combination of defense and attack; hiding and running between rocks to escape predators and children who want to put them in buckets :D
 -> **turtle shell** / **grace** ?
