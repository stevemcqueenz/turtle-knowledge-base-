# Scarlet Crusade in the Alliance and Defias Brotherhood in the Horde

## Post 86276 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-29T15:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86276#p86276 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I thought a lot about this and came to the conclusion that perhaps it is possible, but at the same time complicated, but it was an idea that occurred to me just because:
Make it possible for the Alliance to join the Scarlet Crusade and the Horde to join the Brotherhood of the Defias.

 ***Why?***
I have always believed that both organizations are wasted in a way, in addition to the fact that their respective dungeons are quite far away and located in areas where it is difficult for the Opposing Faction to access.

The Scarlet Monastery is located in Tirisfal Glades, an area on PvP servers that is Horde Territory, and in Tirisfal Glades there is no flight route for the Alliance, so the journey is long and tedious every time. you decide to do the dungeon.

Same story with Death Mines, located in Westfall, an area that in PvP Servers is Alliance territory and does not have any flight routes for the Horde.

Therefore, both dungeons complement each other with the opposite faction.
The Mines of Death are Alliance-only dungeons and the Scarlet Monastery is a Horde-only dungeon.

 *****But...And the equipment drop?*****
Make it purchasable by farming reputation with the faction.
For example, creating Scarlet Crusade quests in Tirisfal Glades and creating Defias quests in Westfall.

 ***Quests?***
That's the complicated part, I don't know how it could be done, but in my opinion, I see potential in both factions to create something new for the main factions and create a new way of seeing them both and better understanding their interests.

 ***As for the lore:***
Yes, I find it complicated, more so with the Crusade since it is said that they are racist fanatics, but in my opinion, that was a lore that was added after Vanilla, since according to the lore: "The Scarlet Crusade are a faction of The Silver Hand that became corrupted", so I would see something good as a continuation to show that after the death of Balnazzar, the Scarlet Crusade is looking to rebuild itself in some way while still suffering attacks from the Forsaken but are aided by the Alliance in their task of cleansing Lordaeron from the Undead plague.
At one time the High Elves and Dwarves were part of the Scarlet Crusade, so it is not impossible to unite them.

As for the Defias, their history is well known, due to disputes with the Kingdom of Stormwind because the payment to rebuild Stormwind City was not made, the Defias Brotherhood was formed in an attempt to claim what is theirs.
Considering all this, they might as well ally themselves with the Horde for that purpose.
This is in addition to the fact that we have seen Goblins and Ogres attached to them.

This is my suggestion to give more relevance to both factions, whose weight in the lore can be much greater and contribute more.

It doesn't make sense to me at least looking at Defias Brotherhood. This faction was ultimately defeated. Defias hideout in Moonbrook was found and cleared, their leader Edwynn VanCleef as well as his most crucial mercenaries were slain. Nobles who supported them were slain too. There is nobody important still alive in this organisation. Other stonemasons - if they even survived - are common randoms. They have no goal left since Edwynn is dead. Remains od Brotherhood power are common bandits who don't want to risk their lives only to ... assault Stormwind from their ship. Apart from the fact that this faction is dead, it doesn't make sense for them to join Horde. They are humans too, they once rebuilded Stormwind destroyed by Horde. They did it because they wanted Human race to be protected. It's like joining Horde by Blood Elves in TBC.

Looking at the Scarlet Crusade, it makes more sense. At first the Crusade was like Argent Dawn now before it became corrupted by Balnazzar. However, we managed to kill Balnazzar in Stratholme so the Crusade has a chance to recover from fanaticism supposing that their new leader chooses the correct path. Unlike the Defias Brotherhood, the Scarlet Crusade wasn't ultimately defeated, as far as I know there are no quests that clearly say the last bastions of their power - Hearthglen and Tyr's Hand - were conquered by the Scourge, Alliance or Horde. And unlike the Brotherhood, they have still some ideology that drives them. Supposing that new leader will manage to cure Crusade's corruption, their goal will be to repeal both Forsaken and the Scourge from Lordaeron's lands.
