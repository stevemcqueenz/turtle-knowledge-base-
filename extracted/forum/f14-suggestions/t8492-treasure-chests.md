# Treasure Chests

## Post 54714 by Iceweb — 2023-07-26T08:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54714#p54714 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've only been on the server for a few weeks, and although I'm already smitten with turtlewow and am blown away by many thoughtful turtle-exclusive features I have some self-entitled feedback/suggestions:

 **Treasure Chests**- It feels like a dev may have tried to manipulate treasure chest mechanics to make them much rarer but more rewarding with a guaranteed green, but it feels bad to loot late-game chests and get lvl 16 items, or to not see blues which were always a nice rare surprise. If it isn't offensive to suggest, undoing attempted treasure chest improvements would be ideal. Vanilla Dwarves have a powerful two decade old racial which is uniquely crippled in turtlewow.

 **Slow and Steady** - I really appreciate the genius behind this glyph which encourages leveling tanks and healers to queue and grind dungeons to avoid outdoor mob EXP penalties.  LFT queue times for end game dps are utterly brutal with how vast the disparity is compared to other servers. It would be nice for a lvl 60 glyph to encourage tanks/healers to queue in specific specs, maybe with a small chance for unsoloable final bosses to drop shop currency for them or somesuch.

Anyway thank you for reading and for the amazing ride this has been so far.
