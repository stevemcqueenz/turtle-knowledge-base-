# Class/Spec dampening - Terrible Idea.

## Post 166955 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-30T16:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166955#p166955 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

This is not a very good way to balance.

Because some classes and specs act much different at lower gear levels compared to high gear levels.

The class balancing completely changes depending on someone's gear or the phase of the game.

And that's not even bringing the lower levels into it.

This would be very hard to balance just slapping like a 30% dmg reduction on all Arcane Mages for example. As with like 100 spell power arcane mage fresh 60 deals no damage compared to a T3.5 arcane mage with Scythe and T3.5.

Some specs only shine at high gear levels and suck at low levels / low gear.

That's why this is a flawed idea.

I assume every class-spec combination has an ability which deals most of damage.
For example Pom-Pyro mages deal most of their damage with Pyroblast.
If we pretend that lots of people complain about Pom-pyro mage and devs decide to reduce damage of the mages, they could nerf Pyroblast damage or whole mage damage.
But nerfing damage of all mages will lead to nerfing mages that don't spec into Pom-pyro.
Seems like relative power will stay the same.

Also, when developers reduce importance of damage, they make healers more powerful.
Bears with tons of hp and SL Warlocks will be next OP stars.

One more thing. When developers can directly debuff specific class, it has straight social implication "we don't like that class, so we nerf them". I believe it will be very insulting.
