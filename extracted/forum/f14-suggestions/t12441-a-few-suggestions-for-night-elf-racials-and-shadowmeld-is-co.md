# A few suggestions for Night Elf racials (And Shadowmeld is cool)

## Post 82866 by Flab — 2023-12-27T04:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82866#p82866 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I will be going over a few suggestions here on what I think would be some good improvements for the racial abilities that nelfs have and also talking about the racials a bit in general. While pretty bare bones, the current racials they have aren't completely useless. I've been playing Night Elf ever since I picked up wow, and the racial I got the most use out of all of them was Shadowmeld.

The most famous use of Shadowmeld is going afk in the middle of an active leveling zone so you can get food or bio, sat ontop of an enemy you just killed so you can put your wisp spirit racial to good use when you get back.

Shadowmeld has much more uses than a lot of people let on. As a warrior, I use it in raids during every pull and am able to charge a lot more than other warrs. I interrupt other players casts before they're finished, and ambush people in battleground. In my opinion at least, Shadowmeld doesn't really need to be changed, aside from adding more of an incentive for rogues and feral druids to use it. The ability is pretty much useless for those classes.

Something that I think maybe could be done, would be to add the newer version of Shadowmeld, alongside of the older version. The new version, for classes that can already stealth, would still have some use for it. It could work as a secondary vanish for rogues, and give feral another means of escape, with a 2 - 5 minute cooldown. Maybe the newer version of Shadowmeld could be the exception for rogue and druid, and every other class rolling nelf would be stuck with the old, 10 cd Shadowmeld, for the sake of balance.

Another suggestion I have would be a change to quickness that has a small chance to add a small buff when a Night Elf dodges, 1% extra critical hit/ spell crit chance to attacks after a nelf dodges for the next 5 - 10 seconds, or would wear off on the next crit. It would maybe be between 1 - 5 % chance that it would proc. This would be very good for tanking and pvp, giving nelfs a chance at a bit of an edge every time they dodge an attack.

Idk if these would be helpful or balanced, but I do know Night Elf needs just a bit of an edge, I'm not asking for something tremendous like a weapon skill, that's boring to me tbh, every race should have a unique set of racials.
