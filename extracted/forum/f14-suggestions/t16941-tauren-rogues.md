There are Defias members who are different things, but not Rogues in the "class from WoW" sense where they can use Stealth, poisons and Pickpocket.

Which I think explains the variety of different members of this criminal organization. To my understanding, a bandit who's involved with a life of crime - robbing people or killing people or threatening with violence doesn't necessarily has to be stealthy, they can be big, fat, loud, and compensate the lack of stealth by travelling in large groups.

I'm not saying Tauren should be Rogues or trying to promote that idea, but the opposite - that you can be a bandit without being a "Rogue". Also, Tauren Rogues, I think, started as a joke, kind of like Gnome Death Knights, but in Retail, both are reality now, which goes to show how serious Microsoft-Activision are about their lore and so are the players who keep playing it instead of a Vanilla private server, which in my opinion is a more genuine experience than the official versions, regardless which one of them.

Regarding other comments for Tauren Mages or Priests, I really want to back that suggestion up, but I don't see how. I think the Tauren have all they classes they have, unless Turtle WoW invent a new class, something like a Bard, which pretty much every race should have, just like Warrior. But I can't see Tauren becoming Priests or Mages, because one involves worshiping The Light, which they don't and the other is pretty much the same reason why Night Elves can't be Mages - more precisely, because they don't want to, because you eventually fall off even more and become a Warlock or a Necromancer or something even worse.

This is a design choice, which I don't understand why Forsaken can be Priests to begin with and why Warlocks are also playable since they clearly invoke demons to do their bidding. While there is some "lore" about how they are "good Warlocks, who only fight for good and don't give in to their urges like EVIL Warlocks, but it still feels far-fetched to me".

So I don't see why new class combinations are needed, I'd rather see more work being done on new leveling zones, filling up the empty space in the world, new quests and maybe, one day, a new class.

I don’t know why you’re being so hard-nosed about vanilla quest text when that wasn’t a factor in adding new class combinations before. There's no precedent for Orcs having any cultural fascination with Arcane magic yet we have Orc Mages anyway. If the only requirement is "they learned it from another race" then it can be said that Tauren were inspired by the Night Elves' worship of the moon, Night Elves and Tauren have a close history with each other and they already act as thematic parallels between the two factions in-game.

An'she wouldn’t be a “new god”, it’s already in the game and you can see it whenever you look up during the day, it’s just their word for the sun in Taur-ahe. If you don’t like the origin of the name then a different one can be used, like by typing the word ‘Sun’ or ‘Sol’ into the in-game language filter and going with that. Alternatively they don't even need a separate name for it, it can simply be a subset of worship for the Earthmother since she represents the entirety of nature to the Tauren, including the sky. This is backed up by the Warcraft RPG which was considered canon when it was first written, since we're sticking to vanilla-era lore. Besides, Balance Druids already harness the powers of moon-and-sun, this wouldn't be a foreign concept to the Tauren.

The Tauren have always venerated the natural world around them and the sun is a pretty constant and fundamental part of nature, it only makes sense that it would serve as an extension to their worship of the Earthmother as the earth could not host life without the sun. Not to mention that most of the Tauren's native lands have little coverage from the sky, it's not like they live underground or in dense forests and jungles. They're primarily known for living in wide-open plains and mesas, the sun and sky are major fixtures of their natural landscape. I genuinely don’t see how it’s less plausible or fitting than Tauren tribes being Mormon missionary-style converted to Light worship by humans.
