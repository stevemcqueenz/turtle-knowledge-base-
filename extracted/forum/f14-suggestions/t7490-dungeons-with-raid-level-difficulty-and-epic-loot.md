There is one challenging 5-man content, the Atiesh legendary boss. That exposed the problem about making 5 man content hard in vanilla, there is always one ideal team composition for it.

If they balance it so that any 5 man group can complete it as long as they have one tank one healer three dps, then the ideal team comp will oneshot the boss. If they balance it so that only the most cherrypicked team can do it, then only that cherrypicked team will do it and it will be a challenge of getting geared players, not doing the mechanics.

Atiesh needs a decurser, two warrior tanks, immense aoe healing AND single target healing. If you have those, it's just a matter of gearing and consuming for the encounter, get world buffs and you basically oneshot it.

My point is, 5 man hard content in vanilla is not possible. Cataclysm had hard heroics because it's overall a more involved game with more complicated rotations and boss fights. Vanilla is a simple game, it can't work the same.

## Post 48929 by Ishilu (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-05T16:55:00Z

TBC heroics were quite nice, too. Don't know about Cata.

I think one way to make dungeons more challenging would be to simply ramp up mob damage so that AE tank'n spank becomes unviable.

Considering the "perfect team" meta, that's indeed a problem. One way to fix this would be to fill new lvl 60 content with encounters that require a whole set of different class abilities to get solved. E.g. if there's a mage with decurse, there should be demons or elementals that would need banishing, if only a warlock were there, too. Likewise, there could be mobs that can only be trapped by hunters (see my numerous I-want-to-play-my-survival-hunter-posts) or particularly rough encounters that could be completely circumvened by a rogue with maxed out lockpicking. Toss in some varying mob immunities (like those frost and shadow immunities in SFK) and the whole thing gets interesting.

Another idea: make new 5-man content dynamic. One of the few good ideas that came with WotLK were the varying bosses in violet hold. Imagine running a dungeon and not knowing which bosses  are going to spawn before you enter their rooms. The idea is to make the content just a little bit chaotic to prevent a meta beyond "we're going to need tank'n heals" from emerging.

Note that most if not all vanilla dungeons follow the formula "lot of relatively hard trash and relatively easy boss at the end", so there would be room to fill up with varying encounters.
