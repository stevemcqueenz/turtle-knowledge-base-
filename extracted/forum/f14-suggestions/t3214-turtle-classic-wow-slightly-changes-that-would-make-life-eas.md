Hey there!

As a classic veteran from "back then", I have to vote against your well-meant suggestions.

Of course, the suggested changes would make the game slightly more accessible than it is right now. However, there will always be some point or other where a player will personally think that the game could or should be "improved": shorter corpsruns, more farmable resources, lower prices for riding, skills, dual-spec, etc.

At this point, let's just take a look at what years of Qol - changes have done to retail WoW and its community and ask ourselves: Is that the game we want to play? Are those the kind of people we want to play with? Do we want turtle WoW go down that road?

Not saying your suggestions wouldn't make the game easier, and maybe I'm too much of a purist here, but I personally like my games a bit clunky and unforgiving ;-).
