# Enhancement Shaman changes

## Post 54859 by Ravenstone (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-27T11:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54859#p54859 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Couple of changes I think would make for a better Enhancement Shaman experience.

The issues and some solutions.
 **1) Mana**
Enh Shaman's have mana problems, mainly due to having to gear for almost every stat to decent damage and support in raids.
- Rework Ancestral Knowledge - change to be "Allow 5/10/15/20/25% of your mana regeneration to continue after your instant cast spells."
Numbers can change, it is more the idea. Would equate to a fairly substantial amount of mp5 at 60 when buffed. 200 Spirit which is easily achievable with natural stats and buffs would be 35.6 mp5 at 5/5.

- Allow Water Shield (and the other shields also) to be triggered by a % of melee attacks as a replacement to the Thunderhead talent. The pre-req talent could also be changed increase the number of charges of the shields to support this.
Some on demand mana regen would be nice and a fairly natural evolution of the current elemental shields.

 **2) Spell hit**
- Change Concussion to reduce enemies chance to resist your shocks by 2/4/6/8/10%.
Ele Shamans won't like it, but 5% damage can be made up easily with new items. More importantly we need it to reliably hit shocks and not feel like we're throwing our mana down the toilet using them.

 **3) Threat**
- Add 30% threat reduction for the Shaman via Elemental Weapons when any of them are in use except for Rockbiter.
All melee have reduced threat (bar Paladins), this just gets us on par, and helps with the random Earth Shock/WF crit threat spikes.

 **4) Damage**
- Lightning Shield triggered by melee attacks like the Water Shield above.

- Add a new capstone, Shockwave. Similar to the Warrior WotLK talent and inspired by the Tauren Chieftain hero of WC3.

> **Shockwave**
>  Sends a wave of force in front of the shaman, dealing weapon damage and incapacitating all enemy targets within 10 yards in a frontal cone for 4 sec. 20 second cooldown.

Enh need more physical abilities. You could
remove the incapacitate and lower the cooldown for more usage in PvE but then they'd still suck in PvP.
Chose incapacitate over stun as I thought it'd be preferable for PvP to not make it op in bunched situations but also make it decent in 1v1s.

Happy to discuss anything about the above. Obviously they are all just ideas and the numbers can be modified.
Thanks for reading.
