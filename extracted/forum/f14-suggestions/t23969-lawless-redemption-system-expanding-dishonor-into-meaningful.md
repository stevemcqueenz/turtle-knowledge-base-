# Lawless & Redemption System – Expanding Dishonor into Meaningful Progression

## Post 168246 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-15T07:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168246#p168246 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The dishonorable ranks already exist in Turtle WoW.
Dishonored. Exiled. Outlaw. Pariah.
Right now they are titles without teeth.

This suggestion builds on that foundation and turns it into a complete progression system with real consequences, real rewards, and long-term roleplay value.

 **I. General Tabard System Expansion**

Faction Tabards
– Available from Honored reputation.
– Wearing a faction tabard increases reputation gained with that faction.
– Wearing the matching battleground tabard increases honor gain in that battleground.
– Wearing your racial tabard increases honor gain in open world PvP.

Guild Tabard
– A guild can declare support for a faction.
– Wearing the guild tabard increases reputation and honor gains toward that chosen faction.

Tabards become identity, allegiance, and mechanical commitment.

 **II. Declaring Yourself Lawless**

A hidden NPC in a shady location allows a player to declare themselves Lawless.

Effects:
– Permanently PvP flagged for everyone, even the own faction.
– Can attack own faction and opposing faction PvP targets.
– Every kill grants 6 Dishonor Points.
– Every kill causes reputation loss with the victim’s faction.

 -Killing Lawless targets increases honor gained by x2/x1.9/x1.8 etc.

Dishonor Rank is a flat-value progression system.
It is not bracket-based.
It is not decay-based.
It is achievable through commitment, not unhealthy grinding.

Higher Dishonor Rank multiplies reputation loss per kill. Starting with a base of 10 reputation per kill.

 **III. Criminal Ladder (Dishonor Points)**

> Dishonored (7,000)
>  – Vendors stop trading.
>  – Reputation loss x1 per own-faction PvP kill.

> Exiled (14,000)
>  – Mailbox and flight paths disabled.
>  - No access to local defense channel.
>  – Reputation loss x2 on target faction.

> Outlaw (21,000)
>  – Guards hostile within 10 yards.
>  – Reputation loss x3 on target faction.

> Pariah (28,000)
>  – Cannot queue battlegrounds.
>  – Guard hostility increased to 20 yards.
>  - No access to general chat.
>  – Reputation loss x4 on target faction.

> Traitor (54,000)
>  – Kill-on-sight in home faction cities.
>  – No access to any city services and world chat channels.
>  – Reputation loss x5 on target faction.

> Renegade (100,000)
>  – Neutral factions become hostile.
>  – Reputation loss x6 on target faction.

> Enemy of the State (360,000)
>  – Hostile everywhere.
>  – Full exile, only access to outlaw guilds.
>  - Access to outlaw channel.
>  – Reputation loss x7 on target faction.
>  – Unlocks outlaw hideout and redemption path.

 **IV. Outlaw Hideout**

Upon reaching Enemy of the State:

– Access to a secret outlaw hub.
– Unique outlaw questline.
– Cosmetic and niche rewards possible.
– Choice: remain outlaw permanently, or begin redemption.

 *Being a villain should be a playable identity.*

 **V. Redemption Path**

From Enemy of the State, the player may start the Redemption Ladder with the opposite faction.

Mechanics:

– Wearing a desired faction tabard grants +5 reputation per PvP kill, or +3 reputation per NPC kill as well as +60 redemption points per PvP kill, or +5 redemption points per NPC kill.

– Tabards sold in the outlaw hideout:

Low Quality (4g) – +1 reputation gain
Medium (requires Wanderer, 15g) – +2
High (requires Ally, 23g) – +5

VI. Redemption Ladder (Redemption Points)

> Outsider (360,000)
>  – Hostile everywhere.
>  – Reputation gain per PvP kill ÷6.

> Wanderer (100,000)
>  – Neutral factions neutral.
>  – Reputation gain ÷5.

> Mercenary (54,000)
>  – Opposite faction guards neutral in towns.
>  – Limited services (hearth, reagent vendors).
>  – Can queue battlegrounds with opposite faction.
>  – Reputation gain ÷4.

> Defector (28,000)
>  – Full access to one city and its flight paths. (The one with the highest Reputation)
>  – Reputation gain ÷3.

> Ally (21,000)
>  – Full vendor and service access in new faction.
>  – Reputation gain ÷2.

> Banner-Bearer (14,000)
>  – Guild invites allowed.
>  – Mailbox usable.
>  – Reputation gain ÷1.

> Bloodsworn (7,000)
>  – Full integration into new faction.
>  – Treated as native member.
>  – Eligible for that faction’s PvP armor sets and quest rewards.

 **VII. Purpose**

This system:

– Makes dishonor meaningful.
– Creates a grind that is humanly achievable, unlike Rank 14.
– Introduces real risk and consequence.
– Enables faction defection without rerolling.
– Expands roleplay depth dramatically.

A renegade human joining the Horde.
A tauren priest walking into Stormwind to see the Cathedral of Light.
Guilds choosing allegiance through tabards.
Outlaw guilds living outside the law.

Characters stop being loadouts.
They become stories with consequences.

This builds directly on systems already present and gives them weight, identity, and long-term progression.
