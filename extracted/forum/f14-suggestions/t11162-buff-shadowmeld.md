# Buff Shadowmeld

## Post 73616 by Jannus — 2023-10-25T11:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73616#p73616 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the upcoming release of the Tel’Abim PvP realm, I’ve had the idea to give the night elf race a little boost as they’re perhaps the only race that gets no racial ability that can be used in combat.
Humans have perception, dwarves got stone form, gnomes have escape artist, high elves have a mini evocation, orcs have blood fury, trolls have berserking, taurens have war stomp, undead have wotf and goblins have a mini sprint. What do night elves have? They can corpse run faster and have a stationary rank 1 stealth ability on a 30 second cool-down.
My proposed change to Shadowmeld that will not make the ability too overpowered, but will add a great amount of utility for classes that don’t have natural stealth (rogues and Druids) is to give Shadowmeld a 5 second window of lingering shadowmeld effect after you move (unless you right click cancel the buff or /cancelaura shadowmeld) - this will allow night elves to reposition or potentially flee an otherwise unfavourable encounter such as an elite spawn on top of their current location.
Please consider implementing this change or give some other benefit to the night elf race because otherwise their facials are lukewarm, to put it bluntly - 1% dodge and a faster corpserun that does nothing when you’re alive, nor does it let you do any fancy skips when you’re dead.
I do believe a change like the one I proposed above won’t make the night elf race too overpowered but will also give them some out of combat utility that they otherwise lack.

 **Shadowmeld**
Activate to slip into the shadows whenever you stand still for 3 seconds, reducing the chance for enemies to detect your presence.
Movement takes you out of Shadowmeld, standing still again for 3 seconds resumes it.
Lasts until cancelled, or upon entering combat.
The chance to be detected is significantly lower at night while outdoors.
Night Elf Rogues can use stealth abilities while in Shadowmeld.

Instant, on toggle, no cooldown, no duration
Requires level 1 for females, can be learned at level 20 at corresponding class trainer for males, the racial is available to all classes except Night Elf Druids.
