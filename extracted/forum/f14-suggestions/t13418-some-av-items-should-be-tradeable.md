# Some AV items should be tradeable

## Post 90377 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-16T21:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90377#p90377 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**TL;DR Storm Crystal, Stormpike Soldier's Blood and Armor Scraps should be tradeable.**

When I was reporting bug <https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?bugReportId=6582> regarding Storm Crystal, Stormpike Soldier's Blood and Armor Scraps, I came up with the idea of making these items tradeable in Alterac Valley.

I find it great QoL change because sometimes there are situations where your team is close to summon Lord Elemental or there are few Armor Scraps left needed to upgrade the troops. Meanwhile, over half of your team is deep in enemy's territory far away from the base and some people might have a few of these items but they are too lazy to go back and turn them in which is reasonable. If these items were tradeable, one person could gather all of them from people in the attack and go back to the base to fulfill the objective.

Since all these items benefit WHOLE your team in Alterac Valley, I see no reason to keep them BoP. Making them tradeable would give us more teamwork possibilities. These items disappear from your inventory when you leave the battleground so there will be no problem with that. The only problem I can think about is using summonable AH to sell them but I guess this can be blocked by making the items conjured or if it's possible making them unable to auction.

Tell me what you guys think about the idea.

I've investigated how much XP you can get by handing in Armor Scraps and Blood/Storm Crystal. I didn't even know AV is that good place for leveling in 51-60 bracket. According to DB...

Turning in 1x Blood/Storm Crystal gives you 500 XP - these items have 100% drop chance from enemy faction NPCs and players.

 20x Armor Scraps give 1 650 XP. On my geared priest I could farm about ~1000 Armor Scraps in 30 minutes from the Coldtooth Mine in Horde's area. That is about 2000 Armor Scraps per hour which equals to 165 000 XP per hour. You need about 210 000 XP to level up from 59 to 60 btw.

This indeed creates possible boosting environment with these items being tradeable now. Moreover, you can't know if someone is being boosted or not. Imagine 5 people farming these items for one guy, the guy could be boosted from level 51 to 60 in a few hours. I think that reducing XP from these quests will kill two birds with one stone. Firstly, low levels won't be boosted. Secondly, raw gold generated from these quests (on lv 60) will be gone. IIRC you get about 30 silver for turning in 20x Armor Scraps on max level. However, the items should remain tradeable. It's great QoL change.

I also checked how much XP other AV repeatable quests give you. The one with taming Wolfs/Rams awards you 1 650 XP. Turning in one Frostwolf Hide/Alterac Ram Hide gives 3 300 XP! Surprisingly, the quests about transporting supplies from Mines give 0 XP. All quests related to Wing Commanders also don't give any XP.
