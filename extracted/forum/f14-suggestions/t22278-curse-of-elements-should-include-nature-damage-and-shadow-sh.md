# Curse of Elements should include Nature damage and Shadow should include Holy damage

## Post 154618 by dioaffo1 — 2025-10-06T16:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154618#p154618 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Nature and Holy damage are the only schools without any coordinated buff between party members.

Nature damage is fundamentally "elemental", and it is the primary damage type of the **Elemental** Shaman. There is no justification for excluding this damage type from the "elements" umbrella, aside from shaman deprioritization. This would benefit Boomies as well, in an interesting way, as they might alter their rotation depending on the active curse.

Likewise, Holy damage should be included in Curse of Shadow.  It just makes sense thematically, and it balances out any faction-related issues as SpellRet Paladins can benefit from this, just as Shamans would from the above change. Smite Priest is also lacking DPS support, so this would enable that spec to shine a bit more.

So the big winners form this are ele shaman, spellret paladin, balance druid, and discipline priest. These are all support-DPS specs that currently lack any reciprocal support.  The TWoW class changes seem to encourage us to engage with these specs, so some intraparty support like this would be great for them.

Regarding PvP concerns (no Holy resistance gear/don't want to overbuff Chain Lightning in PvP): that is something that can be addressed easily in the upcoming PvP itemization/rework. I don't even see this as an issue, honestly, because warlocks can already Curse of Shadow/Elements to buff themselves/mages/shadow priests/etc. Why not other schools? But because of the faction-locked class impacts, there might be tweaks needed after playtests.
