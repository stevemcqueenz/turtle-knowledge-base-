Frost shock being the new earth shock for tanks is... Smart, I think? But can't you spam frost shock? Might be a bit OP threat wise, but I'm sure some tweaks could be made for it to make sense. I personally would prefer it stay the same but it doesn't seem terrible. That last stormstrike change though... I do not like. Here's why:

Enh sham, and shamans in general, have been known for playing a supportive role for... Literally ever since vanilla. I feel like that's a heavy part of the class fantasy, so making the ability only debuff enemies or buff yourself in a way that's beneficial to you feels a bit... Not shaman. Alternatively, I'd suggest (brought this up before but let's do it again) that instead of a couple instances of nature damage, stormstrike increases nature damage taken for half of the cooldown length. That way, you can support your other nature damage dealers (not broken because there's like 3 specs that rely on nature damage in a significant capacity lmao) and do so semi-consistently, so long as you can stormstrike the target. Now, the amount this increase might be could be something pretty chonky like 10% because let's be honest, most nature casters/dps aren't doing a whole lot of damage anyways, and- like I said, this buff is only up for effectively half of a fight if you're consistently stormstriking.
