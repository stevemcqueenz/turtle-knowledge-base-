# Ancestral Healing Talent

## Post 24481 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-09-06T09:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24481#p24481 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've played many resto shamans. They can be a lot of fun and great healers. So I might eventually make one here as well. Looking at the talent tree, I was struck by a change that struck me as strange: the swapping of the Ancestral Healing and Nature's Guidance talents as regards tiers 2 and 3 in the restoration talent tree (see image, turtle on left and vanilla on right). I know the leveling experience is important to many, and having Ancestral Healing as a second tier rather than third tier ability, as it is in most other vanilla servers, helps out resto shamans quite bit. Having it moved to the third tier makes point allocation less ideal. So I tried to reason for Nature's Guidance and Ancestral Healing were swapped. I reached the conclusion that this was done for the sake of elemental shamans, who now benefit from Nature's Guidance being moved to the second tier and can allocate talent points in a more ideal manner. So elemental shamans benefit while restoration shamans lose out.

My suggestion is to make both Ancestral Healing and Nature's Guidance second tier talents so that neither specialization loses out. Improved Reincarnation can be moved from the second tier to the third tier to make space for this. Moving Improved Reincarnation will not affect many players negatively and will allow for both elemental and restoration shamans to be able to designate talent points more ideally. There is also room to make all the talents mentioned second tier if desired.
