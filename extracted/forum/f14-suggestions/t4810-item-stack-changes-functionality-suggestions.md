# Item Stack Changes/Functionality Suggestions

## Post 28822 by Zeyla — 2022-11-09T13:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28822#p28822 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A few suggestions regarding existing items and their functionality/stacking numbers that I think would lead to a nice increase in quality of life for many players with very little effort involved:

 -Sacred and Holy Candles maximum stack size change from 20 to 200
 -Various seeds for Druid battle rez spells max stack size change from 20 to 40
 -Wild Berries and Wild Thornroot max stack size change from 20 to 200
 -Arcane Powder for Mages max stack size change from 20 to 200
 -Symbol of divinity for paladin's divine intervention max stack size change from 5 to 20
 -Symbol of kings for paladin blessings max stack size change from 200 to 1000 (if possible, or just double the durations)
 -Runes of Teleportation and Portals max stack size change from 10 to 100
 -Ankh max stack size change from 5 to 40
 -All rogue poisons max stack size change from 20 to 100
 -All arrows and bullets max stack size change from 200 to 1000 (if possible)
 -All grenades max stack size change from 10 to 20
 -All bandages max stack size change from 20 to 100
 -Fish Oil, Shiny Fish Scales, & Light Feathers max stack size change from 20 to 100
 -BG marks change from Unique(20) to Unique(200) with max stack size 200
 -Conjured Oranges max stack size change from 20 to 80
 -All ranks of Healthstone given 3 charges
 -All ranks of Mana Gems given 3 charges
 -Soul Shards max stack size change from 1 to 5. Maybe consider generating 5 shards with drain soul.
 -Thieves tools can be placed in key ring
 -Shaman totems can be placed in key ring
