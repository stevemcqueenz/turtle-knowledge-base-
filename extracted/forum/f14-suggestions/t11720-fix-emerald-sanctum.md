# Fix Emerald Sanctum

## Post 77396 by Tawneyturtle (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-14T21:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77396#p77396 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After speaking to officers between 4-6 guilds, it's clear to me the guilds I raid in aren't an outlying guild having trouble filling numbers, but just one of many guilds who have a hard time finding people to raid this with.

Issues:

 - Lack of interesting items for casters/healers outside 1 ring+trinket.
 - Trash takes too long, too much armor/health/resistences on the mobs for the size of the packs.
 - Mechanics are unfun, actively lowering DPS and uptime, forcing you to stop while sleeping or attack very slowly.
 - Loot is unrewarding.

Suggestions:

 - Make 1 bag guaranteed drop, hard mode chest should have a 20% chance to contain a 2nd bag to encourage the raid to do it on hard mode.
 - Make the sleep dispellable again and work with the new Lucidity potion, and not have it go through Immunities like Divine Shield.
 - Make the debuffs give you a different debuff than a longer version of whatever you had. The fight becomes "don't dispell players or you're griefing them."
 - Remove at least 2-3 trash packs, since we have to kill all of them anyway, and they're quite tedious since they not teleport any player out of range for them, sometimes bugged you out.
 - Add gold to the trash mobs, they drop no raw gold (same issue as Kara10)

 -Make it scale to 25 man

The trash mobs are immense damage sponges, if you pool the health points of the mobs in an ES trash pack and compare it to the pooled hp of a mob pack in naxxramas or aq40, you'll find the ES packs have double or triple as much hp.

The trash mobs are repetitive, each mob pack is the same gameplay loop of hoping you don't get slept by the whelps, cleaved by the big ones, or auto'd to death by a loose mob because its tank got slept. Rez up, take the repair bill, repeat as many times as there are trash packs in the raid (and there is a lot. Onyxia is just 4-5 mobs in a hallway)

The boss practically extends its hp bar by debuffing the players, which I suppose is fine if it's balanced around that (huhuran's hp bar is practically extended by making players wear nature resistance). However, the way it debuffs the players is by making their character super clumky to play. Playing with a 80% haste debuff is an absolute bore and should be reconsidered to a different kind of debuff with less effect on how players can access their class' fun.

The adds phase is rng, it can be hard mode if you get many cleave adds or easy mode if you get none.

The mobs have shadow resistance for no logical reason.
