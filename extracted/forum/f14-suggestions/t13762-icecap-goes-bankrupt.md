# Icecap goes bankrupt

## Post 93648 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-27T20:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93648#p93648 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think lasher changes hit Icecap herb too hard. Almost 2 months ago I recorded Icecap's price on 9th March. It cost 1.5g back then. Now it costs 6-7s each. The price has dropped down by 95% within 2 months. Icecap's vendor price is 2s50c by the way. You can track all data about Icecap here: [https://www.wowauctions.net/auctionHous ... ecap-13467](https://www.wowauctions.net/auctionHouse/turtle-wow/nordanaar/mergedAh/icecap-13467).

The cause of this dramatic price drop was adding Icecap to lashers' loot table. 3 days ago there was another change: Black Lotus has increased chance of dropping from Icecap's node from 2% to 4%. This will lead to further devaluation of Icecap. With current price the only reason to gather Icecap is to have 4% chance of getting Black Lotus.

Seeing that there is still big supply (over 2000) of Icecap on the market I can say that this herb goes bankrupt. I like cheap herbs but up to a point. If a herb is close to vendor-selling border that means the supply is too big. **I suggest removing Icecap from lashers' loot table or decreasing Icecap drop chance from this farm.**

Lasher farm adjustment has had impact on all high level herbs, however not every herb became cheaper. Dreamfoil is good example because it's similar to Icecap: is high level herb; has BL drop chance. However, Dreamfoil had already been in lasher loot table before lasher update. I recorded price of Dreamfoil on 9th March - it cost 25s back then. Now Dreamfoil costs 60s.
