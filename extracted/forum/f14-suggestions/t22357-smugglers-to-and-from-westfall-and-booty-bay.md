# Smugglers to and from Westfall and Booty Bay.

## Post 155225 by Soapbar — 2025-10-12T03:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155225#p155225 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

STV is an obstacle for new and low level players trying to travel to and from Booty Bay. Specifically players trying to get to two dungeons: The Deadmines and Wailing Caverns, or at least an Alliance player trying to get to Rachet.

I believe adding a two npcs, that can “smuggle” players between zones will be beneficial with an RPG element to it.

Obviously both npcs cant be in a convenient location for players to get to. A small cave near the shoreline, a small raft to swim to, maybe in stealth next to a couple of boulders.

Thank you for reading.
