# Lower level of Barrens Guards against Ashenvale

## Post 24194 by Ashkir — 2022-09-01T22:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24194#p24194 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In the original classic you were able to run across the Mor'Shan RAmparts from Barrens/Ashenvale on a character in their late teens to early twenties. However all the Barrens Guards are high level and can one shot characters until their late thirties.

This feels very breaking to characters of the alliance trying to enter the neutral areas of the Barrens. In addition the aggro range of these guards are rather large and can aggro people doing goblin quests from quite a distance.

In addition any deaths in the Northern Barrens area spawns you all the way in Ashenvale, a much further run than Ratchet.
