## Post 69770 by Rudezlad — 2023-10-09T13:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69770#p69770 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With regards to #3.

Renegades would be the 'easy' way of doing it.

But scourge itself definitely has enough merits to be considered a full 3rd faction, and/but it would take considerable effort from the devs to reshape the world and its systems into making a full-fledged third faction functional, since a lot of the world has been coded with two opposing factions in mind, with some neutral parties in between, so it might even require a full data overhaul to make things such as battlegrounds even support adding 3rd faction. Instead of only A vs H, you'd have to have A vs S, H vs S on top of that for instanced/scheduled pairings, effectively tripling that scope, and if done with due diligence, it would mean new battleground maps, instead of just dropping a scourge team into what is an alliance/horde base with regards to how the map looks like.

Lore-wise, Scourge would basically be hostile to anything else but them (and maybe some burning legion demons), making it mainly suitable for pvp gameplay, since there wouldn't be even neutral towns like Ratchet/BB/Gadgetzan for them given how their drive is to destroy the world (or whatever lich king commands them to do), unless the player characters of the scourge are undercover cultists of the damned, rp-wise, otherwise game mechanics such as cross-faction support for guilds and groups would feel very odd if you could suddenly have scourge members joining the alliance and horde activities.

I hope that if Northrend ever becomes a thing on Turtle, and that it is completely overhauled to work within vanilla numbers and limitations (topography overhaul to support land access since there are no flying mounts), that it would come with the support to be able to play as a scourge faction member, even if it means not being able to do ~any quests on the two vanilla continents.

But that is indeed a lot of work to do it well enough, so it is unlikely that this is going to be a thing anytime soon. Third faction / playable Scourge has probably been discussed at length at some point already I'd assume, but I didn't actively search to see if that's the case. If so and this brings nothing new to the table, my apologies.
