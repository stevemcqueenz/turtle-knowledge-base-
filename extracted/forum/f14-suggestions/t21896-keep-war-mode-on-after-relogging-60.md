# Keep War Mode on after relogging @60

## Post 151698 by Snut — 2025-09-14T15:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151698#p151698 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Playing on Ambershire, I've noticed that every time you log in on a level 60 character, War Mode is automatically turned off, forcing you to run to a Battlemaster even in cases where you disconnect or have to run off for a little break.

It would be more convenient if it remained on after relogging, and it would be more compelling for those who enjoy War Mode.

For those looking for world PvP and more of a PvP experience, it would greatly benefit the server if War Mode remained on, so we would see more PvP-flagged players around the world.

Please consider this!

Regards,
A PvP enjoyer

## Post 151699 by M3te0r — 2025-09-14T15:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151699#p151699 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I completely agree with this. Having War Mode turn off every time you relog or disconnect feels really unnecessary and just adds an extra chore. If someone has chosen to play with it on, it makes sense that the game should respect that preference until the player decides otherwise.

Keeping it active across sessions would not only make things smoother for those of us who want to stay in War Mode, but it would also help create a more consistent PvP presence out in the world. That’s exactly what makes world PvP fun in the first place, seeing more flagged players around instead of having it cut short by a technicality.

Would definitely love to see this change implemented.
