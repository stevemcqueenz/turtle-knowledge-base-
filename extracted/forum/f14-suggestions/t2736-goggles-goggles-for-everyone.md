Engineering provides multiple things:
 - personal power ahead of the level curve. Many of the "requires engineering (number)" items have no level restriction themselves, so you can theoretically use things which are far beyond your normal loot range, if you have the dedication to skill into it.
 - dynamite and bombs, which are resourceless, ranged, AOE damage. Something many classes lack.
 - certain pieces of powerful, weird gear like the force-reactive disc for warriors and paladins, the rocket helmet, the death ray, and so on
 - In TWoW, it's the only way to obtain certain mounts, and there is the wormhole generator on top.

These are ALL limited because you're essentially sacrificing "marketability" for pure power. Removing the engineering requirement would impose the need to provide heavy level restrictions, defeating the point. Engineering is the "madman" profession for a reason!

But there are more things you can sell than just scopes:
 - Important ammunition, weapons and, yes, scopes for hunters, which become stat sticks for other classes later on too
 - Salt shakers for leatherworkers
 - Pets like the mechanical squirrel

There are other things as well like the Field Repair Bot which, while not "sellable" makes you a valuable contributor to a guild.
