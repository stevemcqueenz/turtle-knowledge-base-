# Arcane Surge Spell Animation

## Post 164883 by Avalok — 2026-01-11T11:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164883#p164883 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

HELLO!

I would like to suggest having a change in animation on the Mage's "Arcane Surge" spell.
Specifically, I am referring to the "Hit" animation of the spell onto a target.
I am not referring to the animation produced by the character as they cast the spell.

The current 'Hit' animation of the 'Arcane Surge' spell is merely just another hit animation of Arcane Missiles.
I would like to suggest changing it to that of the animation of the Shaman's "Stormstrike" ability,
where the hit animation of the latter produces a shocking charge of lightning onto a target.
However, it would be fitting if that lightning shock were to be recolored into something pinkish and purple.

If not, perhaps you could use the "Arcane Blast" hit animation from the TBC expansion,
and have it be used as the hit animation of Arcane Surge.
