I agree that something should be done with twinks.

Your proposal will work only if we get experience from killing other players.
Now there is no xp from pvp. Only very small amounts from winning battleground.

Also, twinks can get Slow and Steady challenge. When you die in pve with this challenge, you lose part of your xp. So it is possible to make twink without stopping xp.

Ability to stop xp overall is beneficial to normal people.
If you can turn off your xp, you can reach top gear for bracket by running normal dungeon groups with lft. You can kill all mobs and don't care for xp. You aren't limited by amount of runs. I made a twink by playing only on lvl 19. I even farmed gold for some of my enchants.

If xp in pve is inevitable, then you have to level yourself boosting character. Dungeon run will look like: you enter dungeon with boosting character, kill everything inside except bosses, enter with your twink character and kill bosses. You get minimal amount of xp, but can't play "normally". Only with boosting.

Instead of level restriction, it is better to reduce power of twinks.
Their power is high-level enchants on low level gear. For example naxx enchant for shoulders applied to low level item.
If you think it is wrong, then vote for my petition [Nerf high level enchants on low level gear](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4499)
Other reason is worldbuffs present in battlegrounds. Petition is [Remove World Buffs from battlegrounds](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=18167)

The thing that it proves, above all else, is that bracketed PvP battlegrounds aren't compatible with PvE progression. It will never not be pointless to play at the low end of a bracket unless the gap with higher levels in the bracket is closed somehow.

So what Battleground PvP actually needs is PvP-gear, at every bracket, which is itemised in such a way that it closes the gap between a lower level character and a twinked character. However, it also needs to be done in a way that prevents PvP gear from competing with PvE gear, because otherwise they'll fuck up progression for both. TLDR, PvP gear needs to be deactivated outside battlegrounds. And then on top of that it needs to be reasonably easy to attain, so while it can be its own PvP progression gearing, it can't be the bracket's top reward either, which should be reserved for stuff like pets, mounts, titles, and other collectibles.

It can be done, but it requires a thorough review of how PvP actually works, so that the solution doesn't end up worse than the problem.
