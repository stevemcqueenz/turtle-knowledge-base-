# Guild houses and tabards

## Post 49031 by Duckie — 2023-06-06T10:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49031#p49031 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In a recent discussion Vrograg did with the GMs it was mentioned that there are no new guild houses being added for the foreseeable future. Primarily due to the difficulty in keeping up with demand and partly due to the complete lack of originality or lore reasoning behind why many guilds want them. Most guilds, especially raiding ones, on this server just want a guild base for the tabard port. Which is understandable, a second hearth is really really nice to have. But this is not the purpose of guild houses. They are meant to be somewhere for a guild to express their identity and as a shared space to engage in rp.
A suggestion then would be to add a default location to the tabard for guilds without a guild house. This would need to be somewhere neutral due to cross-faction play. But just letting everyone have the utility of a second hearth without needing to add the guild house should dramatically cut down on the number of requests for the devs to make one. As well as (hopefully) limit such requests to guilds who actually have RP reasons for wanting one.

Some possible locations for a neutral default tabard location: Ratchet, Booty Bay, Moonglade, Everlook, Gadgetzan, Light's Hope, Thorium Point. But any non-faction specific location would probably work just fine.
