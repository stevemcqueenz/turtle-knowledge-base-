# Whip of Encouragement

## Post 151087 by Gillz — 2025-09-10T07:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151087#p151087 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I feel this trinket sits in a weird spot. 3% haste is the highest you can get on a single item. The majority of players running SW Vaults at this point are extremely geared tier 2.5+ raiders. This isn't the first time a blue preraid trinket has been so ubiquitous (Blackhand's Breadth), but at least that was a guaranteed quest reward. Whip is basically usable by every class, so most times you are rolling 5-ways for it. Worst part is, it sure doesn't feel like it's 25% drop rate is correct.

It's such a miserable item to farm due to SWV's quirks. I like the dungeon, but the aggro radii are obviously whack and being locked out of the last boss until X conditions are met is frustrating for efficiency. The whip essentially has no replacement so if you are trying to be slightly optimal, you need it.

I feel nerfing the haste, so it is inline with other haste trinkets and allows for more variety OR removing the gate before the last boss to allow for "speedruns" would be great improvements.

Curious to hear what others think.
