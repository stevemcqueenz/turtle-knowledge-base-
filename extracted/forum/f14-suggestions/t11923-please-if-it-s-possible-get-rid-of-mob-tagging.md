# Please, if it's possible, get rid of mob tagging

## Post 78870 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-11-26T23:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78870#p78870 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Mob tagging (ie mob ownership after hitting it once) is one of the worst mechanics of classic ; it makes questing in busy areas a huge pain in the ass, especially when trying to find a unique mob like the defias messenger, the forsaken courrier, or baron longshore. It encourages bad behavior like spamming consecrate / aggressive pet / trap on spawn / tab + moonfire when people were there before you, and generally just creates tension between people who just want to complete their quest and move on to another area.

I don't even know the feasability of this, and I understand that this may cause issues in certain cases (though even this is debatable), but I just dont see the benefit of this mechanic

Counter argument:  Getting rid of mob tagging was one of those post-Classic changes which seemed like a good idea at first, but made the game worse in retrospect (at least, from the perspective of enjoying fights while leveling).  It encourages bad behavior like butting into others' fights without asking, to the point where that becomes the norm rather than the exception.

I would support the mods adding an ability or item to slap a tagged creature for zero damage and get credit for a given quest (kind of like the "Murloc Tagger" in TBC, but for any quest).  That way those who want to can speed through busy quest areas without ruining Classic for those who enjoy it this way.  In all seriousness, it seems like kind of a moot point with the more reasonable population numbers lately.
