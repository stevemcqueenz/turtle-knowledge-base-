In all honesty, the sentinel doesn't look too different from a rogue to me and the berserker looks a lot like a fury warrior.

If you take some transmogs and just build your talents more around flavour than around an existing meta (imagine a there's a meta and people just ignore it. Wouldn't that be nice  satisfied_turtle_head  ) you might be able to approximate these ideas, except for the DK.

However, a long term project for the turtle team could be more racials to flesh out and diversify existing classes even more. I once saw an old interview where they mentioned the idea to give night elf warriors access to a fire shield at some point, so the player would go like "hey, I'm a demon hunter now". How cool would that be?

Now let's say, the turtle team picks up this idea and does so via implementation of new racial abilities. Following that road, your sentinel could be a night-elf based rogue or even melee hunter (and yes, I'm still against a melee hunter tree if it's supposed to replace the ranged trap hunter tree. However, why not give only orc hunters a Rexxar treatment while we're at it?). Your berserker or death knight could be troll and undead warrior variants, dwarves might get something inspired by the WC3 mountain king (FOR KHAZ MODAAAAN!), and we could get orc blademasters.
