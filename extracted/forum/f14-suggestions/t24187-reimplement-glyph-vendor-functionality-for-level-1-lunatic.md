# Reimplement Glyph Vendor Functionality [For Level 1 Lunatic]

## Post 170192 by Echtm — 2026-03-06T00:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170192#p170192 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

When the Level One Lunatic challenge was first introduced, I made a character with the intention of getting a few titles and then levelling the character as normal to 60.

Now that I have unlocked all of the titles that I wanted to unlock, I can't disable the Level One Lunatic challenge due to the removal of the Glyph Vendor. This is in spite of the fact that the Level One Lunatic challenge was advertised to be something you can later disable, as can be seen in the spell description of the challenge.
Please either reimplement the Glyph Vendor - allowing for other challenges to be disabled - or allow for Level One Lunatic to be disabled as advertised.

Thank you.
