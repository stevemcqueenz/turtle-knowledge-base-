What are your thoughts/predictions for the Grim Batol raid coming in the next patch?
Where will they go with the lore, and enemy types?
Who will the final boss be?

It seems they TWoW wants to deviate from the Cata approach, so hopefully they don't go with Deathwing as a final boss. He seems too OP (i.e. stronger than LK) and we already have a ridiculous number of dragon bosses.

Would Cho'Gall make sense?
Will there be any Twilight Hammer lore, or could Cho'Gall be implemented regardless? TWoW seems like to draw from RTS characters and we haven't seen any ogre raid bosses thus far. Would be cool to see the team's take on this.
Cho'gall has ties in the lore to Ahn'Qiraj and the old gods which would be an interesting way to explain his power level, but also keep a certain narrative cohesion between raids. For example, the Atiesh staff ties three raids together: AQ, Naxx and Karazhan.

Anyways, I didn't know how I felt about Grim Batol as t4 raid at first, but I've opened up to the idea. Liking the artwork/screenshots we've seen so far, and the teasing with red dragonflight at the entrance. Would be cool if another legendary is added, [maybe a shield](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111662#p111662) this time.

Interested to hear everyone's thoughts/predictions!

## Post 112982 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-17T01:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112982#p112982 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Cho'gall is dead. He's killed in Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness in the mission "The Tomb of Sargeras", which while part of the non-canon orcish campaign is an event that has remained canon in later materials, being referenced to in the Warcraft III game manual in the section "[Gul'dan and the Betrayal](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Gul%27dan_and_the_Betrayal)". The renegade clans of the Twilight's Hammer and the Stormreaver were put to the sword as retribution for their betrayal on Doomhammer's orders, and Cho'gall was actively present as an enemy hero in the mission it takes place and is a required kill in order to complete the mission, establishing his presence at the tomb.

Unlike Gul'dan, who also shows up in the mission despite having been shown to have died entering the tomb in the cutscene immediately preceeding the mission, Cho'gall's presence on the island as one of the Twilight's Hammer members isn't inherently contradictory. Cho'gall's death was retconned in a 2007 novelization of Tides of Darkness that instead had him fake his death, but at the moment to my knowledge, that book remains just outside of the content that applies to Turtle WoW's continuity and thus cannot be taken into consideration here.

Although the Twilight's Hammer clan shows up again in World of Warcraft, it's as a cult of the Old Gods rather than an orcish clan. Scattered Stormreavers also show up in Warcraft III as creeps and as a quest giver in The Frozen Throne, and there will likely be something relating to them in Balor in Turtle WoW, but it remains to be seen to what extent their presence will be there.

With that in mind, it's still possible that Turtle WoW decides to also retcon Cho'gall's death and bring him back. I believe that some of the quest designer developers have spoken of this possibility in the Discord.

The greyish gates of the raid entrance which have an azure glow are guarded by Watame and his army of mind-controlled bugs. He is there to test if your army is strong enough to entertain Hogger and his henchmen.
If you defeat the army of bugs, Watame disappears. At the same time, somewhere in Azeroth, a high-level HC character meets his demise.
You finally step into the fortress. First, the entrance hall seems empty but then your eyes catch a goblin, looking at you, smirking. 'My name is Schmockle' - he is introducing himself before asking if you wish to buy devilsaur coordinates or donate him certain items. Ignoring Schmockle is optional, he will not fight you.
At the other end of the hall, a door opens and to your surprise, a sea coast sooths your eyes at the other side.
There is a ship which is awaiting you. Proceeding further is only possible by embarking. But little do you know that there are priests hiding in the ship, ready to MC raid members and force them to jump into the see! The priests cannot be harmed or CC-d in any way. Only those of your raid who survived the voyage and set foot at the opposite coast can continue the raid. The others can go to sleep.
As you look around at the coastline, you see some weird creatures playing with the toy where you gotta insert shapes into their respective holes. You see them sweating, occasionally swearing and five of them are trying to force a cube together into a circle-shaped hole. Your raid mustn't disturb them though! They are TWOW developers currently working on CC3. Any disturbance or damage is met with class nerfs and further hunter pet weapon skills.
You just gotta walk further, alongside a corridor where the lifelessness of elementium is compensated with red diamonds, which serve as wall-ornaments all along the corridor.
And there, at the end of it, there is a half-open door behind which you hear a maniacal laughter. It's Gamon! And he's ready to whoop your a$$.
If you somehow defeat him, you can finally face the end bosses. Hogger and Mankirk's wife are worthy foes but you defeat them ez.
