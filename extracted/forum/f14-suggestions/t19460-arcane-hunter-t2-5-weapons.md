The other Hunter shots that scale from spell power are Serpent Sting and Volley, with the latter only having a small amount of scaling. If you want to make spell hunter work you'll want to pair it with a Spellstrike on-hit BoED build as melee Survival (detailed here: [viewtopic.php?t=11709](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=11709)). There aren't any meaningful +spell damage ranged weapons, and resists in general are a fair bit weaker in PvP.
