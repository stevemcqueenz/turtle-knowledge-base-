# Expand quest Harpies Threaten

## Post 136798 by Swizz — 2025-06-24T02:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136798#p136798 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know this is far from the only quest in the game with this issue, but it seems to bug me the most.

The quest [Harpies Threaten](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=6282) has you killing 7 of each of four types of harpies.  No big deal.  Except there are two other harpy types that share spawns with the four quest types: [Bloodfury Storm Witch](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4027) and [Bloodfury Windcaller](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4026).  So as folks are harvesting the four types of harpies they need for the quest, leaving the unused types, eventually we're left with nothing but Stormwitches and Windcallers.

Is it a big deal to hunt and peck, or harvest the other two while waiting for respawns?  No.  But if the other two types of harpies were added, there would be more balance in the area.

Or you could add an Alliance quest to kill the other two types.  They're nature casters, I'm sure the hippy druids have something negative to say about that.
