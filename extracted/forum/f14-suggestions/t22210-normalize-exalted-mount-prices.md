# Normalize Exalted Mount Prices

## Post 154137 by Marar0 — 2025-10-03T02:39:00Z

At the Moment the mount prices for each different Faction that sells a mount as part of you reaching Exalted differs Greatly.

Horde:
Orgrimmar:
[Armored Orgrimmar Wolf](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81241) 350g

Thunderbluff:
[Armored Thunder Bluff Kodo](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81198) 100g
[Chieftain's Kodo](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81237) 350g

Undercity:
[Armored (color) Deathcharger](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81245) 350g

Raventusk Trolls:
[Armored Brown Bear](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81226) 300g
[Black/Brown Bear](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80438) 100g

Darkspear Trolls:
[Armored Darkspear Raptor](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81182)100g

Durotar Labor Union:
[Red Shredder X-0524A](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81190) 950g

These are just examples i wanted to find, but trust me when i say that for alliance it also varies from 100g 250g to 950g for the Gnome mount.

My Suggestion is to Normalize these Mount prices, Especially lower the Gnome and Goblin Mount price to match the Rest (and easier to grind) Factions, there's no reason why the factions that has to buy 1000's of Runecloth, needs to also dish out another 1000g just to get the mounts.  dead_turtle_head
