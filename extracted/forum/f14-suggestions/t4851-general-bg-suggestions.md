# General BG suggestions

## Post 29193 by Artofwill (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-14T23:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29193#p29193 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all I'd just like to say that the server is definitely one of if not the best vanilla experiences I've ever had and I've had many.

As a person who typically played on pvp servers, it' wasn't too big of a problem transitioning to the rp-pve sever. However I do find the lack of open world pvp to be an itch I like to scratch on occasions even during leveling, which is where BGs come in.

My goal for this post is merely to present some suggestions, that I think could benefit the overall health of the server while still remain inline with it's direction.

What I've noticed while playing was that, the server definitely encouraged slow and steady pacing of character progression, but I found the lack of low level BGs to discourage me to stay at those level brackets and wanted to reach 60 asap so I can consistently be able to enjoy the pvp aspect of the server. Considering that the world is mostly pve, bgs were my only realistic option.

While low level bracket BGs did pop once a while from 19-39, it stopped after I hit 40. I realized that there was very little incentive for players to engage with the bgs at a lower level other than twinkers. Which both discourages players who wanted to take their time as there are less things to do at lower levels other than leveling and doing professions.

I think the easiest solution to address this problem is to provide more meaningful rewards to both the honor vendor as well as the different pvp reputation vendors for lower levels (ie, good looking transmog items that hold meaningful stat weight for said level bracket). This both invites players to want to jump into bgs early as it's a decent way to progress their character, as well as acquiring cosmetic options for the future, a win win in my opinion.

As a non HC player my self, I would imagine that this could also incentivize more HC players to participate in the lower BG brackets as there are likely few opportunities for them to test their skills out against other players without the risk of perma death outside of bg environments, and I think any chance for gear progression at lower level would be a welcome addition.

Lastly I believe that by providing more reasons for players to engage lower level BGs is extremely beneficial for the overall health of the server, as provides more options for new lower level players to enjoy the game however they choose to play. Thus creating a healthier environment to encourage new comers. As a player who enjoys pvp, I can see many others also looking to participate in more bgs as we level through the brackets, and actively slowing down our leveling process because it's fun to stay at the top of your bracket range and have the chance to be on even playing grounds (somewhat) against other players without the gear race post 60 that is both rewarding but also daunting.

The above are merely thoughts coming from a player who would love to see the server grow and blossom further.
Thank you all for reading my ramblings and I welcome all feedbacks and discussions.
