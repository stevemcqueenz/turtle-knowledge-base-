# Improve MM hunter synergy in melee groups

## Post 156839 by Vellasta — 2025-10-24T09:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156839#p156839 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Background**
Currently, MM hunters are in an awkward position in raids due to them being best placed in a melee group to provide melees with Trueshot Aura, but this results in MM hunters not being able to receive Grace of Air totem most of the time as Windfury Totem is a much bigger benefit for melees in the group. This poor group synergy for MM hunters in raid environments is frustrating and frankly unfun as a MM hunter.

There have been many suggestions to fix this dilemma, such as making Windfury Totem work for ranged attacks, combining Windfury with Grace of Air and so on. Personally, I think the best way to resolve this is to focus on the hunter instead.

 **A New Talent**
Taking a leaf out of the Subtlety Rogue's talent tree, I suggest adding the following talent to the MM tree:

 **Sense Weakness** (Placeholder name)
Physical critical strikes by you or party members within 40 yards cause your next Auto Shot/Steady Shot/Multi-Shot to do X% more damage.

 **Reasoning**
With this talent, MM hunters should benefit more from being placed in a melee group while not being too powerful in levelling and solo content.
