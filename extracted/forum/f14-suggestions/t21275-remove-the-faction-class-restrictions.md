# Remove the Faction-Class restrictions.

## Post 147570 by Nameis2 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-21T20:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147570#p147570 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Trolls should have access to Paladins, and Dwarves should have Shamans.

The Troll empire has had Paladin like figures since its earliest days, and even in vanilla WoW there were troll NPCs that clearly filled that role. On the other side, Dwarves have long been tied to lightning and elemental power in Warcraft lore, from the Mountain Kings to the Gryphon Riders.

The fact that they channel their power or practice their religion differently does not make the classes invalid. By that logic, only Humans and Dwarves should be allowed to be Priests, since they are the ones most directly worshiping the Light.

I wouldn’t mind Undead getting Paladins too, but lore-wise, that’s a hard sell. The real issue is PvP balance; Paladins and Shamans were always forced to walk a thin line so one didn’t overshadow the other. But from a lore perspective, it never really made sense.

Paladins were never meant to be the counterpart to Orcish Shamans. In Warcraft’s history, Shamans lined up more naturally against Mages, and later against Warlocks. With Night Elves joining the Alliance, the more fitting counterpart to Shamans should have been Druids; both classes share travel forms and a nature-based connection.

Paladins, on the other hand, have always been tied to Death Knights as their natural rival, whether against the Orcish Death Knights of old or the Scourge. That means Undead should have received Death Knights to balance Paladins, while Druids would have served as the proper counterpart to Shamans, and Mages to Warlocks.

If making Death Knights is too difficult, event though it could be done by simply recoloring Paladin spells and renaming abilities, then just give the Horde a “Champion” class. Champions could mirror Paladins in function, since Paladins are essentially the elite warriors of the Alliance.

Even Blackrock Orc champions had devotion-style auras back in vanilla WoW, so the precedent is there. The concept also fits naturally with Orcs and Tauren. You can’t exactly add a “Chieftain” class without it feeling ridiculous to have so many chiefs running around, but a Champion class would work perfectly
