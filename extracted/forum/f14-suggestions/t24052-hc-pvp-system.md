# HC pvp system

## Post 169090 by Musing — 2026-02-21T05:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169090#p169090 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello everyone,
I’m playing on the Tel’Abim PvP server in Hardcore mode, and I’d like to raise a concern and start a discussion about how Hardcore currently functions on PvP realms.
In its current state, a Hardcore character—especially at low levels—can be killed in PvP in a single hit, resulting in permanent death. This often happens with a very large level difference, leaving the Hardcore player with no realistic way to avoid or counter the situation.
Because of this, it’s hard to understand what the intended challenge or gameplay value is, particularly for low-level Hardcore characters.
Hardcore is meant to be difficult, but also fair and skill-based. On a PvP server, unrestricted permanent death from PvP often turns Hardcore into a form of unavoidable griefing rather than a meaningful challenge.
On the Everlook server, a more nuanced system existed.
Hardcore characters could die permanently from PvP, but only if the killing player was no more than 5 levels higher than the victim. If the level difference exceeded this threshold, the Hardcore character would not die permanently.
This approach had several advantages:
PvP between comparable levels remained dangerous and meaningful.
Low-level Hardcore characters were protected from one-shot deaths by vastly higher-level players.
Hardcore stayed challenging without becoming unfair or pointless.
Based on this, I’d like to suggest a similar solution for PvP Hardcore:
Keep permanent death from PvP, but only when the level difference is within a limited range (for example, ±5 levels).
This preserves the risk and tension of PvP while preventing unavoidable and uninteractive deaths.
Such a system would:
Maintain Hardcore as a serious challenge.
Encourage fair PvP instead of griefing.
Make Hardcore on PvP servers viable and engaging, especially at lower levels.
I’d be very interested to hear the community’s thoughts on this, particularly from players who enjoy Hardcore but currently avoid it on PvP servers.
Thanks for reading.

First, hardcore mode of TWoW is built on normal mechanism of a PVE server. It is now (in early 2026) a one-life and permanent death gameplay, where players avoid death and risks they cannot handle. In other words, if you are capable of handling the risk in fighting in a battlefield, you can turn on PVP and test yourself. If you truly love both PVP and HC gaming, the current TWoW offers the chance for playing that way. You can even test yourself with hardcore mode and warmode simultaneously on.

What you suggest is a possible mechanism allowing HC players to engage PVP activities without risking their characters with permanent death. However, the example in your advice seems complicated, with analysis on levels and probably regions. Even if there is no technical limit for implementation, the Everlook HC death system may not be good enough for TWoW, as that is only a method of softcore or compromised hardcore value.

In my opinion and a nutshell, HC and PVP gamings are never concomitant for most players who do not want their HC characters to easily die.
