# Rpg Ideas

## Post 13207 by Jesar — 2021-10-22T19:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13207#p13207 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone I come here with few Ideas about adding more rpg options to choose from in this game:

 1. Gaining XP by exploring, crafting, fishing, skinning, mining and herbalism

 2. Player housing

 3. Guild halls

 4. New professions like archeology, jewelcrafting, inscription, lumberjack, carpenter

 5. Farmer

 6. Spellcrafting

 7. New class quests

 8. Dance party studio and musical instruments

 9. Mount, aerial and naval combat

 10. Glyphs that change the color of spells

 11. New quests in starting area for original races

 12. Gaining XP by diplomacy, negotiation, capturing or bribe humanoid mobs

 13. Hiding weapons and armors with transmog

 14. Glasses or goggles as an alternative

 15. New weapons like one handed polearms, chains, javelins, pistols, flails, totems, pikes, glaives, magic orbs, chakrams, scythes and whips

 16. Banners and coats to replace capes

 17. New class abilities

 18. New playable races and class/race combo

 19. New events

 20. New customization for all races

Do you all have another rpg ideas ?

## Post 13208 by Gheor (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2021-10-22T22:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13208#p13208 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello.

We always try to improve the experience for players but we are sometimes limited by the client itself.
I will tell you my personal opinion and pov on all of them, I would like to thank you very much for the dedication and the time you've put in your post.

 1. Sounds fun! Maybe we will explore the modifications we need to do to have this one in.

 2. Tedious and needs a LOT of work, with phasing at what not, probably not impossible.

 3. Already a thing, we are looking to improve the system currently.

 4. Something we've tried to explore! Needs a lot of work and just a little bit of imagination.

 5. Interesting, not something we thought about, may be worth investigating.

 6. Now this one would be cool I think so as well, but it's not in the spirit of Vanilla and it's cause several balance issues.

 7. Already working on it!

 8. Not sure what this was, I hear it a lot but seems flavourful.

 9. Probably to hard to code and implement into Vanilla.

 10. We need an artist for these, modeler, to do it from scratch, we'd love to have that but we didn't seem to have much luck yet.

 11. Already had some! We don't really wish to add that many since now you'd leave Elwynn at about level 13, Durotar as well.

 12. Hmm, sounds cool! But I am not sure that is possible and how it'd work altogether as a system.

 13. Might be a thing in the future.

 14. There's already several goggles in Vanilla, maybe we should try to add some more.

 15. This one is tedious because we can't really make new weapon models at this particular time.

 16. This one as well since it might be really possible with Vanilla.

 17. These count as class changes and needs to be discussed.

 18. We are not looking to work on that as of yet, 1.15.2 will introduce Undead Hunters and Dwarf Mages, pilling up on Orc Mages and Gnomes Hunter, High Elves and Goblins are the only playable races we want in the game right now.

 19. We've already had some, and we might have more in the future.

 20. We've added some of these, either in the customisation options or in the shop.

I would like to note out that I am not trying to diminish your work in any kind of way, we are very thankful for the suggestions and I have responded truthfully for those that I think should be a thing or the ease of implementing them.
