# Taunt in PVP

## Post 97950 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-27T12:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97950#p97950 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!
I was thinking about a way to buff taunt/make it do something at PVP.
The idea seems nice to me but I usually don't PVP, I'm just sharing the idea.
The idea:
Taunting a player switches his target to you and forces him to target you for the duration of the taunt (tab fails etc).
I think it would give some added utility to tanks, redirecting some big damage to them in a brawl.

The problem is taunt does have no opportunity cost it doesnt even use gcd or cost resources
Some classes like rogue get massively punished for a forced target switch, as it erases their combopoints.

In pvp you play your class, not your spec. To that extent making a pve tank mechanic isnt really needed inho as all tank classes can meaningfully controbute with other stuff such as sunder armor, slows or emergency healing.

But as for games that use taunt in pvp i thought warhammer online does it well. It puts a dmg debuff on the target if he attacks anything but the tank that taunted him.

Giving the spell defensive utility without taking away your oponents control of their character

## Post 97957 by Zulnam (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-06-27T14:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97957#p97957 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Warhammer Online was the only game that I am aware of with the trinity role system (damage-healer-tank) that actually implemented tanks well in PvP. Not only are they viable, they are mandatory for any group as tank damage mitigation extends to other party members.

As for how they handled taunt, it simply interrupted any spell being cast; without any other side effects (so no "prevents spells from that school").

This could be an implementation. Would definitely be much easier than locking a player's target (not even sure if that's viable in the vanilla client given that is not a feature anywhere in the game).

Unfortunately it would only be useful against casters. Unlike WO, melee dps do not have "chanelled" abilities in WoW.
