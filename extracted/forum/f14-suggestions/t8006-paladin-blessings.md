I agree with extending the length of at the very least the 15 minute buffs (and possibly the 5 minute ones as well) for a very simple reason: It reduces the hold up for the rest of the players.
It reduces the amount of time you need to stop and rebuff. For the single target buffs you can do it on the fly, but for the group buffs you need a bit of coordination. Reducing that will make things more convenient for everyone involved and realistically wouldn't change the power balance a lot.

Will changing this make playing the paladin class more fun? Yes, less of a hassle to rebuff often.
Will changing this make playing with paladins in your party more fun? Probably, fewer "hold on, need to buff everyone" breaks.

I agree that the paladin class is already pretty powerful in survivability and does not need power buffs, but that isn't a reason to make the class a hassle to be party support with in my humble opinion.

[Edit]
On reconsidering after reading everything here. Paladin blessings are quite powerful as buffs, the shorter duration is a valid drawback for them. It would be convenient to have them longer. But then something would need to balance that. Higher costs or less powerful.
The party having to wait for their buffs periodically is a price too for that powerful buff.
