# Haste rework for druids

## Post 111412 by Templar85 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-04T20:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111412#p111412 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Haste as a concept of a stat is good but need much more refinery to be effective. Right now it is very lacklustre and only effectively work in 1 out of 4 forms.

Haste increases auto attacks.
Bear tank has the most advantage of it. More auto attack means more threat.
Second is Bleed Cat. 35% are auto attacks. Too many glancing blows though. Real effectiveness much lower.
Third is powershifting Cat. Zero advantage because using just abilities.

Resto
There is an insignificant benefit for Healing Touch and Regrowth but zero on instants casts like Rejuve and Tranquiltiy
Only 2 out of 4 abilities affected by haste.

Balance
Only Starfire is affected by it. Wrath is already GCD capped and Moonfire dot and Insect Swarm dot does not affected.
Only 1 ability affected by out of 4 with haste.

My proposal is similar like warriors have with defense stat which provides dodge, parry, block, reduced chance to be hit, reduced chance to be crit.

Reworked Haste on Druids
Increases melee and spell casting by 1% + 1% Ability Damage, 1% Healing Power for instant healing spells, +1% Spell damage for spells that are shorter than 1.5 sec.  +1 spellhit / 3 haste.

With this change melee will not only be affected by autos but by abilities too.
Instant and short cast time resto and Balance spell gain bonus as well.

ANY haste effect that not coming from a gear ( pots, abilities, spells) only increases the attack speed portion but not the other stats.
