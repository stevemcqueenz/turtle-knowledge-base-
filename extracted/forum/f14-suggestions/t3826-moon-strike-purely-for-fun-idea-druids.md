Well I don't think we should try to go too crazy with it since it could cause unexpected interactions with talents and spells. I believe a niche it can play is help with mana regen, something that druids could use a hand with. In my mind, it would be enough if it did low arcane damage on top of the weapon's, lowered the targets resistance to arcane and nature damage by a small percent, and as a plus, if the target is affected by Moonfire, then the druid regenerates 3% (maybe 5? not sure if that would be op) mana. Give it a 5 second cooldown. Something like this seems good enough to provide sustain and a bit of a buff for otehr arcane/nature casters in the party.
