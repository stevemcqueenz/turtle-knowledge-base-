Idea for this came from a GM a few days ago.   smiling_turtle

This thread is meant to gauge what sort of transmog gear players would like to see added to Turtle. The addition of custom armor pieces has been a very welcome inclusion, particularly pieces that were previously exclusive to only NPC models.

If there is a piece of currently unobtainable gear you would like to see added in future patches please either include a screenshot or a Wowhead URL of the armor/NPC. Ideally this will only be gear that was present during Vanilla but certain exceptions could be made for TBC as well such as the Tier 2 recolors.

I'll start off with some of my personal wish list items:

 **Wound Dressing/Anti-Septic Soaked Dressing**

I've seen this one requested before. This is a popular roleplay item since many of our characters tend to get injured on the regular. Has been present in the game since Vanilla. Perhaps they could be crafted via high level First Aid giving players more of an incentive to level that profession.

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/39271-corporal-keeshan.jpg)

 **Gina Lang's Customized Spellbinder Robe**

I LOVE this robe, it's so cute.   turtle_in_love_head  I know it's just a cropped Spellbinder Robe but there's just something about it that made me always want it. I feel like it would be a fairly simple addition. My idea was for it to be either a BoE Green item or a level 20ish quest reward both factions could obtain.

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/2486-gina-lang-gina-lang.jpg)

 **Additional Swashbuckler's Shirts**

Swashbuckler's Shirts are very popular for casual outfits and having a wider choice of colors would be wonderful. I know there exists blue, green and purple versions that are currently exclusive to NPCs. My personal favorite is the one worn by Martie Jainrose in Lakeshire. Like their black, white and red counterparts these would be crafted through tailoring.

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/37450-martie-jainrose.jpg)

 **Darkmaster Gandling's Helm**

I know that Ironweave Cowl uses the white version of this model which is very nice but I really would like to see this version too. Perfect for a Necromancer transmog. My idea was to have the item drop from Gandling himself, or perhaps an Argent Dawn quest reward involving Scholomance.

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/38075-darkmaster-gandling.jpg)

 **Tier 2 Recolors**

These were added in TBC alongside recolors for many Tier 1 and Dungeon sets. The nice thing about these is that they're all class, meaning players can use these sets without restriction while the original Tier 2 remains untouched. They don't even have to be the same colors that were introduced in TBC I would be interested in seeing NPC exclusive colors or even completely custom colors! I could see these being obtained at level 60 either through quest rewards or have them added as drops in the custom endgame dungeons.

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/90176-orik-trueheart.jpg)

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/313920-nemesis-raiment-recolor.jpg)

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/270503-bloodfang-armor-recolor-the-complete-set-on-a-female-troll.jpg)

That's all I've got for now.
I look forward to seeing your suggestions as well!

The position of the weapon on the character model.

When transmogrifying a weapon the position of the main weapon on the character model is taken. Thus, when the main weapon is transmogged to a weapon that should be removed on the back, the position of the main weapon does not change and the new weapon skin is displayed on the belt. The suggestion is that when transmog weapons, in addition to the skin, the position of the weapon on the character model should also be taken into account. Thus when transmogrifying weapons to a model that by default should be on the back, not only the skin should be changed, but also the placement on the character, so that after transmogrification, the main weapon is also placed on the back.

Here are my pipe dreams when it comes to the transmog system and cosmetics in general, and as far as I think it should reasonably go without it all becoming over the top and too highly customizable to the point the classes start to lose their identity.

 1. Reshaping armor. Plenty of the items in game put their own unique meshes on the character, changing the shape of your character to make it look like you're actually wearing something other than a skin tight suit. Imagine if we were able to apply the shape of one piece of armor to another. Like taking the meshes of a skirt and applying it to a leg armor that you've already tmogged. If we could choose between both textures and meshes with our gear then it'd add a lot more depth to customization.

 2. Bracers/shirts over or under chest piece/gloves and boots over/under leggings. Imagine you have a nice long sleeve chest you want to mog into except your bracers are overlapping them, not allowing the shirts shape to show, or maybe you have some gloves that are overlapping your chest or bracers and you would rather their textures overlap the gloves, or maybe you would even like your shirt to show over your chest. This would allow for a lot more color coordination and mix matching between gear IMO. I've tried several combinations of gear that I thought would look really well put together but just didn't fit right because one armor would overlap another. This would fix a lot of things if it were made into the game.

 3. Option for hair through hats/helmets. This would just be so cool. Imagine an orc and his t3 headpiece, he has the mohawk hair and it's poking though, it would add so much. I main a night elf warrior. Night elves have some great long hair options and it would be so sick if my hair was showing out of the back of my lionheart helm.

Idk how easy any of this would be to implement, but I feel like these would add a lot to the system as a whole. For all I know it may be impossible. It would be really cool though and I can always dream.
