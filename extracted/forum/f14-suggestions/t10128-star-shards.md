## Post 67231 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-09-27T19:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67231#p67231 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would add the TBC starshards DoT applied at the end of the starshards channel so we'd get this unique vanilla+tbc starshards combo.

The dps of completing the channel and maintaining the dot, would need to be tuned so that maintaining this dot would be a dps up, compared to spriest flay and smite, but spamming the channel which would clip the dot would be a dps down. This is why I do agree the channel should be granted chance to crit, to better help how it scales. The sp coeff may or may not need buffing now that we'd be getting further sp value out of the dot.

I would also buff channeling for all classes to allow dodging, like we had on this realm via a bug 1 year ago. This would allow synergy with nelf priest's other racial, a +50% dodge spell that completely doesn't work when you are casting/channeling.

With the dot applied on channel end, this also makes suffering knockback a double edge sword. Yes, you lose channel ticks, but it does hasten your dot getting applied. This gives a bit of a reason for enemies to stop your starshards with a kick or other interrupt/CC which might actually not be in their best interest, increasing the skill ceiling of PVP in these matchups.

It wouldn't be necessary to modify the mana cost, spell power coefficience or the damage.

Nor would I make it the only ability of the priest that is immune to pushback.
Simply allowing those starshards to crit would be the perfect and simplest solution, it'd that way be able to benefit from some of the talents.
