# subtlety rogue meme talents (dank)

## Post 152548 by Troggyboy — 2025-09-21T11:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152548#p152548 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You ever just wake up in the middle of the night to take a piss but can't go back to sleep again for like an hour because your brain produces random talent ideas for a spec and class you don't even play? Well anyway here they are, please don't actually add any of these.

 **Dubious Wares:**
 -Increases the vendor sell prices of trash items by 100/200/300%

 **Pump and Dump:**
 -Vendor sell price of Shellcoin is increased to 1 gold apiece.
 -While pickpocketing, there is now a low chance for enemies to carry Shellcoin.
 -Requires 3 points in Dubious Wares.

 **Nimble Hands:**
 -Allows you to pickpocket enemy players. Player pickpocketing item pool is based on the amount of gold they carry, one random trash item from their bags and a low chance for a non-trash item. (Note that you don't actually steal anything from them - this is just their pickpocketing item pool.)
 -Reduces the chance for pickpocketing attempts to be resisted by 20/40/60/80/100%.

 **Right Under Their Noses:**
 -While pickpocketing, allows you to disarm an enemy for 10 seconds by pickpocketing their main hand weapon.
 -Pickpocketing enemy weapons breaks stealth and costs 60 energy.
 -You don't actually get the weapon, you just disarm the target.
 -Requires 5 points in Nimble Hands.

 **Parkour:**
 -Increases jump height and jump distance by 10/20/30/40/50% respectively.

 **Aerial Maneuvers:**
 -Grants 33/66/100% directional movement control while in the air.
 -Requires 5 points in Parkour.

 **Leap:**
 -Jumps at 100% increased movement speed and negates all resulting fall damage.
 -20 Energy cost and 1 minute cooldown.
 -Requires 3 points in Aerial Maneuvers.

 **Bribe:**
 -Attempts to bribe the targeted enemy. If successful, they will no longer attack the player or any party members and instead follow the player and attack whatever enemy the player attacks. Lasts 5 minutes.
 -Bribing cost is based on the targeted enemy's level. The bribe can be looted off the enemy's corpse if it dies or pickpocketed back.
 -Bribing success rate gets increasingly lower the higher the enemy's level is compared to the player and is halved if the enemy is elite. Bribing attempts will always fail against bosses.
 -Only one bribed enemy at a time.
 -30 minute cooldown
