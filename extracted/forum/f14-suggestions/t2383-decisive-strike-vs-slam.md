## Post 37679 by Tarrgus — 2023-03-14T11:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37679#p37679 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Arms warrior is sub par at best in classic end game content (past rag). I think Decisive strike can get Arms where it needs to be in order to get people to play it over fury. I would say have slam stack a debuff that makes Decisive Strike (WW and MS) do more damage. This way you are being rewarded for standing still and casting slam, but once the stacks are at max (say 4 or 5), then you can get the benefits of it with decisive strike.

This will add another layer of rotations to the spec. This will make the rotation Slam to 4/5 stacks, then MS>WW>DS. Allow DS to refresh the debuff.

For aoe, maybe allow WW to spread the de-buff to 4 targets. But tbh, arms doesn't need any aoe help. So you could leave that out. We just need a little nudge in dps on single target.

You will not want Fury to gain access to this, so maybe add the damage buff into a talent at the bottom of the arms tree. Like with MS. Or maybe just have MS add the stacking de-buff. (Not applicable to players, arms pvp is already really good).

This will balance the pve damage needed to get arms single target where it needs, while not making them OP on pvp and not making fury even more OP.

I really really want to run into a raid with a 2 hander arms spec and slap.
