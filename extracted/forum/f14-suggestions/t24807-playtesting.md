# Playtesting!

## Post 174623 by Sylerann — 2026-04-09T21:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174623#p174623 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hey devs, if you happen to be reading this, I'm writing to suggest some sort of playtesting for content.

Of course I assume the devs themselves test things ingame to some extent, but the scope of that is clearly limited, and this could be supplemented with a group of volunteer playtesters. Different skill levels, play styles and class mains.

I believe plenty of people would be more than happy to give feedback on new patches and alert developers to glaring issues or missteps in balance, in order to have a better release. The new content has been incredible but certain changes are simply suffering from a lack of quality control.

For example- It's difficult to believe anyone testing changes has given balance druid or rogue a good test drive. The 'buff' that's actually a nerf to druid, and the incredibly obnoxious poison management would have immediately raised the alarm on a playtester of these classes at an intermediate level. There are other questionable PvE decisions (shaman) but these two illustrate my point pretty well.

I could go on about other easily reported issues- there's still a tunnel after archdruid in Timbermaw Hold that crashes the game if you pass through it, to mention another. It could possibly be haunted. I don't think that's necessary, though.

The point is, many people want to see this project succeed, and they want to help. The aggressiveness and outward frustration of critics is just due to a lack of dialogue between the development staff and community. It would even help to just let people know you acknowledge a mistake.
