# How to mitigate queues in a two single steps

## Post 63934 by Ruko — 2023-09-14T10:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=63934#p63934 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I will explain it in a simple way:
The huge queues that the community suffers is due to the selfishness of a few who have 4+ multibox accounts.
I have some time a day to enjoy the server and the plague of multiboxes makes me have to wait instead of being distributed equally.
I am not interested in the nationality of those multiboxes I am only interested in seeing that 1 player with 4+ multibox accounts is worth more than a legitimate player with a single character in the queue and this is a bad message the community is giving itself.
With multiboxes out of the game the queues will drop drastically.
Secondly every game is a spectacle of characters running against a wall which makes that slot impossible for a legitimate player to occupy unless they are disconnected.
I propose that multiboxes be banned and AFK players can report to improve queues as an emergency measure.
Thank you.
