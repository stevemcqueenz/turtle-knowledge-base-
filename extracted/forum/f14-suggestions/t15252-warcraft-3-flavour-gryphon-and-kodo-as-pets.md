# Warcraft 3 Flavour - Gryphon and Kodo as PETS

## Post 103812 by Delquse — 2024-10-19T10:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103812#p103812 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To bring an even closer connection to Warcraft 3, I was thinking that the Gryphon and Kodo Beast could be implemented as tameable pets. It would be interesting for the Gryphon to be available only for the Alliance, while the Kodo would be exclusive to the Horde (perhaps even restricted to certain races).

In Warcraft 3, the Gryphon deals magic damage, but if we look at the upgrades, its attack increases with the melee upgrades (so maybe it can be a melee or range pet?). It would probably be too overpowered if it had magic damage as an auto-attack (according to wc3 unit), something similar to Wind Serpent, while the Kodo Beast could have a magic-based ability (like chainlightning maybe).

The idea came to me while thinking about a Gryphon Raider build.
