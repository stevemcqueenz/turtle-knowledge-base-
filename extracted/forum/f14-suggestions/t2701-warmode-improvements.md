Topic is War Parties (basically the war mode we have now)

I'll just repost here what I posted a long time ago. Basically what I think and I've also heard it from quite a lot of others there's low incentive to stay in Warmode at lvl 60 as the advantage of gives is gone.

If you read the linked post I offered some suggestions to make PvP mode more long term viable and more interesting (and fairer)

Negatives of current state war mode:

- the reward (30% xp boost)for taking it is pve in nature, so pve players take it for lvling faster, then are unhappy that  they get ganked

- the xp bonus is meaningless at lvl 60

- interaction between warmode and non-warmode players is flawed, e.g. non-flagged players can decide when to engage a warmode player but not vice versa, theoretically also flawed interaction between two mixed-faction groups (this was the case on the no-longer existant Excalibur wow e.g.) where you could attack opposite faction players in another group which led to nonsense (orc rogue attacking ally priest in another group but getting healed by Pala from his own group)

- warmode players can understand opposite faction players (IMO part of the experience is to not understand the other side)
Suggestions on how to improve it:

- remove the xp bonus from warmode and use the BG tabards (the ones sold/given ine.g. crossroads tavern from the warcaller that used to queue you for BG). When worn and you kill a player you get rep for that BG (wear ab tabard and kill a player --> get ab rep). Also perhaps give the opportunity to associate guilds with a faction so e.g. black tooth grin tabard gives org rep etc. Also possible for the new factions like silverwing remnant  or the faction cities

- make warmode the default state at game start where you don't understand the other faction.Reintroduce diplomat NPC with diplomat item enabling cross faction and understanding other side. Make diplomats unable to pvp or make them lose the diplomat item on attacking another player with a debuff (Dishonored diplomatic pledge, can't get the diplomatic item again for several in-game hours)

- make diplomats able to invite cross faction groups granting group members temporary diplomatic status
