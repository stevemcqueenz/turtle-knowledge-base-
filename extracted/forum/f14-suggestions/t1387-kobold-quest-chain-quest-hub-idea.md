# Kobold quest chain / quest hub idea

## Post 8478 by Cogitek — 2020-11-17T15:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8478#p8478 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I Have this idea for a quest chain, that after completing makes you friendly to kobolds.

Start : there is an island on the crossing of rivers that separate Elwynn / Duskwood / Westfall, i found the island today running away from a couple gnolls, on the island there are 2 graves, which i think serve purpose, no npc there.
Part 1: So there would be an added npc of dead adventurer's bodies ( 1 dead body that looks like a warrior / paladin that gives the quest, 2 dead bodies that look like a mage and a priest) the quest makes you look through the bodies, at the mage's body you complete the quest, the rewards can be potions of choice and a Letter, which will start the next quest.

Part 2: The letter. The Letter says something in a language you can't read, the quest tells you to speak with someone who knows a lot of languages, maybe a diplomate? So you speak to the diplomate guy in stormwind, he tells you the letter is actually written in Old Koboldish ! He doesn't know it very well, but the words he could read say something about a reward, a quest, a king, a mine, and the sea coast. You don't really know what does this mean. The next quest is ended at a friendly kobold npc who would be added somewhere on the hill the Jangolode Mine is placed at.

Part 3: Gaining trust. The kobold tells you, his king is hiring adventurers to beat a curse, but before he lets you speak with him, you have to prove trustful, and worthy of the task. The kobold gives you a series of quests that are: 1st bring 20 defias bandanas, as the defias sometimes rob and kill kobolds, 2nd quest makes you bring some fish from murlocs, as the king really likes fish, and the 3rd quest is to kill a noble man, who has a camp, from which he and his soldiers fight with kobolds (camp would be east of the mine). Now, if you kill the noble and finish the quest, you get your stormwind reputation reduced to neutral, and get a 4 hour long debuff that doesnt end on death, which makes you friendly to kobolds, but unfriendly to humans. Now you may enter the Jangolode mine and talk with the kobolds.

Part 4: Kobold hub. In the mine there would be 3 "named" kobolds who would be questgivers.
 1st kobold : Says he lost his candle, everyone now is laughing at him, so you search for the candle and find it under one of the rocks in the mine ( pretty well hidden it should be)
 2nd kobold : king's right hand, gives you 2 quests, one quest to again, bring more (10) fish with 50% droprate from murlocs down the coast, and one quest to bring him: copper chain pants, copper chain vest and copper chain boots ( some low level blacksmith recipes)
And also 1 kobold, who for 20 copper bars gives you a bag with 2 random green items of around your level ( 12 )
And for the best part: The King Toggwaggle now gives you his task, that the 3 adventurers failed on. You get an item "cursed candle" that you need to use when at the small island, it summons a ghost of level 16 (elite, recommended 3 players) 1500 health, casts : shadow word pain on his main target (once), psychic scream after 10 seconds in combat ( once) and spams shadowbolt rank 4. You kill the ghost, go back to the king and get to choose one of 3  items:
 1. Kobold mining pickaxe ( blue) two hand axe, 13 dps, 3.5 speed, equip: + 10 mining)
 2. Kobold Candle ( green): Head, cloth, 1 armor, + 5 spirit, +5 survival skill ( if possible) , +3 intelect
 3. Kobold King's Sash : a 16 slot bag.
Also you get some decent silver and 5 healing potions.

After doing that you still have like 3 hours of debuff probably, so there is one more quest from the kobold king: do /dance with him and he will give you a Rallying Cry of the Ghostslayer buff, which gives you 50 attack power, 20 fire spell damage and 5 % crit chance and spell crit change ( or the normal dragonslayer buff)
So that you can kill some mobs while waiting.

What do you think about this quest chain ?
