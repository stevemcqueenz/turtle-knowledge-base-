# Potion of Empowerment

## Post 49549 by Darkstromkar — 2023-06-10T07:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49549#p49549 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We all know the power that a FAP has, it makes the classes that depend on stun useless, I know that they are not going to prohibit its use in bgs (after 20 years I have already resigned myself)

But since we're on a server custom, it would be nice to come up with a potion that would balance things out.

I propose Potion of Empowerment

This potion does the same thing as PAF only it changes Stun and Movement Impairing effects to Fear and disorient.

Now most of you will come to cry out to the sky saying that I'm crazy, but if you think about it, I don't understand why there are cc's (which the most powerful classes have on top of that) that are very difficult to remove and then there are the cc's of the poor , to which almost everyone is immune for one thing or another.

Also, in pve, with the number of bosses that cast fear and the scarcity of dwarven priests or shamans, they would come in very handy for tanks, especially if they are not able to remove it themselves. Really the FAP is used in many bosses and is very useful

Think about it
