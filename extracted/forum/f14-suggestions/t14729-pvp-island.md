# PvP Island

## Post 100421 by Hardcorehygge — 2024-08-30T15:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100421#p100421 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was browsing through forums and I saw Noshu's [post](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13399) about world pvp, and I thought I would gather my thoughts, and write down a concrete idea of a good pvp island.

PvP Island Ideas:

Constant PvP: Players are always flagged for PvP on this island, making every interaction potentially dangerous.

Free-for-All: Players can attack and kill anyone, including members of their own faction. This encourages a chaotic,            every-player-for-themselves atmosphere.

Party Limit: Limit parties to a maximum of 3 players to prevent large groups from dominating the island. This encourages smaller, strategic skirmishes rather than overwhelming zerg tactics.

Cross-Faction Collaboration: Allow players to temporarily team up with members of the opposite faction. This feature could be unique to the island, making it a place where unusual alliances can form.

Dailies and Quests

PvP Dailies: Introduce daily quests that reward players for defeating other players. These quests could offer both honor and gold rewards, with additional incentives for completing them in specific areas of the island to promote varied encounters.

Progressive Quests: Implement a series of repeatable quests where each level requires more kills (e.g., 10 kills, then 25, etc.), with increasing honor rewards. These quests reset every 24 hours to prevent farming abuse and encourage daily activity. Completing all tiers in a day could grant a bonus reward, such as a unique currency or item.

Custom Rewards: Offer rewards for collecting certain items from slain players (e.g., scalps). These items can be exchanged for transmog sets, mounts, companions, or titles. To prevent farming, place a daily cap on how many items can be looted from the same player or within a short time frame.
Transmog Options: Include gear from future expansions or custom designs, giving players more options for character customization, which is somewhat limited in vanilla WoW.

Resource-Rich Mobs

Valuable Drops: Populate the island with mobs that drop valuable resources. Controlling an area gives players an opportunity to farm these mobs for materials and gold. However, the mobs should not be elite to ensure that PvP remains the focus, rather than PvE farming.

Dynamic Drop Rates: Increase the drop rates for valuable items based on the number of players on the island. This creates an incentive for players to engage in PvP and control areas. To prevent exploitation, implement diminishing returns if the same group holds an area for too long.

Anti-Camping Measures: To avoid spawn camping, design the island with multiple areas of interest and respawn points, ensuring that players can’t be easily trapped in one location. Maybe make people respawn at random places on the island so there isn't one place to corpse camp people.

Additional Ideas

Dynamic Events: Introduce random events on the island, such as treasure chests or rare mobs that spawn with high-value loot. These events could draw players into combat as they compete for the rewards.

Leaderboard: Add a leaderboard specific to the island, tracking top players for kills, objectives completed, or resources gathered. The leaderboard could reset weekly, with top players receiving unique rewards or titles.

Environment: The island should be somewhat small. And maybe add weather change e.g dense fog to limit visibility and create a more intense and unpredictable PvP environment.

This island would likely encourage more players to pursue the 250k kills Bloodthirsty title, and it could be a great feature that doesn't interfere with world. By including resources, players will have more reasons to be on the island beyond just PvP, making it more appealing. I also know that the next patch will introduce some world PvP objectives, thanks to Verdict's [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FrIQy71OYtI&t=2741s). But I still thought that I would share this idea of a pvp island and get it out of my system. Let me know what you think!   satisfied_turtle_head
