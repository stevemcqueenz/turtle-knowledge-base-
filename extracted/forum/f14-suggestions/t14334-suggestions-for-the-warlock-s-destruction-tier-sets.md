## Post 97762 by Tsuisama — 2024-06-24T20:23:00Z

Instead of buff Shadow Bolt or simply increase spell hit/crit,
make Soul Fire and Immolate (many locks don't use Immolate) playable,
those bonuses were refer to the destruction talents in Cataclysm

 3P/4P Bonus: Your Soul Fire damage increases your spell damage by 8% in 30sec (or 45sec since Soul Fire has 1min cd)
 5P/6P Bonus: Damage of Immolate has 4% chance to refresh the cooldown of Soul Fire and reduce the casting time of your next Soul Fire by 100% and make it donot consume Soul Shard
