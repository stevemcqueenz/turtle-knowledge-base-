Let us take a note from the Warhammer Universe for a minute, Warhammer: Age of Reckoning

You see, PVP gear was spread out throughout the ranks split between your characters level and renown rank(pvp rank).
Much the same as Twow/Wow
However, Armor and weapons were available for every level/renown and you would use the previous level/version to upgrade to the next.
IE
Rank 8 pvp blue weapon would upgrade to rank 9(or more) pvp epic weapon. Utilizing pvp currency+ previous weapon to upgrade it
My suggestion would be to take a note at the availability of Fresh Servers and say
Rank 10 = MC equivalent Armor and weapons ( 48dps 1h, 62dps 2h, +60 1h healing, +40 spell power)(I LVL 70 gear to match helms) ( current sets should be reviewed and restatted to be appropriate and should be one specific spec for pvp...not a mixture of 2 specs)
rank 12= First upgrade, this should be available for gear around the BWL/AQ40 range( 55dps 1h, 70 dps 2h, +100 healing 1h, 80 SP) ( ILVL 75 armor)
Rank 14 = Naxx level, items should max what is available inside naxxramas,(IE upgrade all the armor to a final stat array of ILVL 80)s

These items could upgrade through a quest, or through a vendor that requires (previous weapon/armor + 10 marks of wsg/av/ab etc or a mixture)
For those farming honor, that means sacrificing some honor for better gear
for those who have raid gear, that means less sacrificing

Finally for leveling through bgs
lv19 should have cloth/leather/mail greens available for purchase via WSG marks ( 3 marks = 1 iem)
lv 29-49 should have cloth/leather/mail greens available for purchase via WSG marks, and weapons for AB marks ( 3 for 1h, 9 for 2h)
 50-59 will have no upgrades, as at lv60 youll be buying your first set of PVP greens
 60- basic pvp gear, 3 marks of wsg/Ab/AV for first weapons, and 1 of each mark for any green gear armor.
