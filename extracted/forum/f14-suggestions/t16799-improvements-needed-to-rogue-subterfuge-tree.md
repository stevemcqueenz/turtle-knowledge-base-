# Improvements needed to Rogue Subterfuge Tree

## Post 114807 by Maaliss — 2024-12-30T00:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114807#p114807 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title says there needs to be some changes to the Rogue Subterfuge tree.

Now, this isn't a "I hate being a support rogue" thread but is more around the inflexibility of Tiers 1 to 4 if you are a Hemo build which predominantly uses non-dagger weapons.

Tier 1 - Opportunity: not worth putting points in for the small amount (if any times) you will use Garrote.

Tier 2 - Serrated Blades: Not the end of the world but there is no choice but to take this skill if not using daggers.

Tier 3 - Improved Ghostly Strike & Smoke Bomb: Smoke Bomb tbh is next to useless. I would much prefer it to be a skill that perhaps improves evasion or is a skill similar to evasion but works against ranged damage and spells. However for a non-dagger rogue you are forced to take a point in either Smoke Bomb or IGS to progress to Tier 4 there is no flexibility.

Tier 4 - Irritating Agent & Blinding Haze: Both bonuses are somewhat minimal for 3 points each but to be able to progress to Tier 5 for Bloody Mess and Prep you are again forced to take one of them, even if you decide to take 3 points in Improved Ghostly Strike to get your 20points you are required to take 1 point in either Smoke Bomb, Irritating or Blinding.

All this leaves little room for manoeuvre as a Sub Sword Rogue.

Please can the Subterfuge tree be looked at again to provide some flexibility for Sub Rogues in both PvE and PvP and bring them back to what they were, a casters silent nightmare.

Perhaps add Tier 3 skill Improved Vanish 3 point Talent which has a 33/66/100% chance to clear all DoTs and remain in stealth.

Also perhaps add a Tier 4 skill along the lines of Shadowstep as a gap closer.
