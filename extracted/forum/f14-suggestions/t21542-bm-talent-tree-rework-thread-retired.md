Thank you both for your feedback. I'll reply to both at the same time, since the gist of your comments is quite similar.

This suggestion is NOT about making BM a main tank, I mention that several times throughout the thread. The goal is the following:

 1. Improve survivability of your pet when tanking a raid boss. Just enough, so your healers can res up your MT. This is accomplished in the following ways.
a) Imminent Threat increases threat consistently and provides Crit resist.
b) The pet's damage during eyes of the beast is improved (Apex Predator is not dependent on your character, unlike Coordinated Assault. Your pet's increased Focus generation is not dependent on your character unlike Baited Shot.
c) Your pet's threat is improved during Eyes of the Beast via the increased Growl threat.
d) Your pet's health regeneration is vastly improved via Bulk and Bloodlust, so you're not discouraged from using EotB for improving your pet's threat, unlike now.
e) Bestial Wrath returns, granting the pet more consistent damage (not reliant on the target having baited on it), again helping with maintaining threat.

 2. BM's old PvE build from CC2 mostly did not even take BW, so BM's were left as Steady turrets, making them indistinguishable from Marksman. With CC3 BM is not given a replacement for Steady, but instead a huge mana sink via Baited and KC. My way to resolve that is the following:
a) Remove Baited, and change KC to a CC escape only. Baited is not only clunky, but also limited in its use. You want it for initiation, yet it's dependent on your pet landing a critical strike. Changing its values or behavior will not resolve the issues that a lot of players have with it.
b) As a compensation for removing KC, Frenzy now grants 10% extra proc chance for 2 value points.
c) Bestial Wrath is brought back and is easier to access. It's a DPS tool only, so the values can be adjusted for that aspect only. It's also a standout ability visually, which gave BM some identity.

For the other questions:

Q1. The AoE tanking question.
 - I've considered it, but decided against it, as I don't want Pets to be considered or used as main tanks. In a dungeon scenario, your pet doesn't have an issue of maintaining aggro on several mobs. Right now your limiting factor is Intimidate having only 8 sec duration on its threat increase.

Q2. How will the team be notified that the pet is ready to tank.
 - That's a great question and I've considered the following.
a) Bestial Wrath only makes your pet large (no longer tinted red)
b) Using Imminent Threat will tint your pet red.
C) A macro can be created when activating Imminent Threat, to inform allies that you're using it. Though if you're using voice coms, you won't need that.

Q3. Rotating mobs.
 - You can do that with Eyes of the Beast

Q4. Threat indicator on mobs
 - You indeed don't have anything, but again the pet is not intended for such scenarios.

Final thoughts:
I've been playing BM throughout several expansions and I'm aiming for a middle ground where BM's niche is more pronounced and has several build options. The Tank oriented spec is most fun for solo play, which is also part of the game

Also, this is NOT a general Hunter improvement thread, as Hunters have a lot of flaws in the 1.12 version of the client. This thread is purely for the BM talent tree
