# Warrior Racials (by me)

## Post 14789 by Notawen (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-12-24T17:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14789#p14789 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Changes to make them balanced are here: [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3847](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=3847)

Horde:

Orc:

Lok'tar Ogar! [10 min cooldown, 20 rage cost, 30 yard range]
"A war cry that increases the attack power of you and your allies by 30% for 1 minute"

Undead:

Will to Kill [Passive]
"When you die you can still fight for 30 seconds.
In this state you have increased attack power (30-50% ish),
but you can't recieve any kind of healing or hp increase effect."

Tauren:

Taurus Charge [100 rage cost, 40 yard range]
"You charge at an enemy with all of your might,
dealing them (a lot of) damage and knocking them back 30 yards"
(Usable in any stance.)

Troll:

War Ritual [30 min cooldown, melee range]
"You consume part of your slain foe, trapping their spirit inside your body.
Only usable on worthy foes and critters. Not usable on mechanical targets.
Lasts for 2 hours."

You can only have one of these effects at a time.

critter: you gain an effect called "Cruel Snack" that does nothing

humanoid: gives you 10% stamina increase

giant: gives you 20% stamina increase

beast: increases your strenght by 1% every time you kill a worthy opponent, caps at 50%

aberration/undead: your attacks cause +30% plus damage as shadow damage,
but your stamina, spirit and agility is reduced by 10%

demon: gives +50 to your rage pool, and your rage decays 50% slower

dragonkin: gives 2 fire resistence per level you are

elemental: gives 1 resistence to all schools of magic per level you are

Goblin:

Throw Dirt [1 min cooldown, 10 rage cost, 20 yard range]
"You throw dirt in the face of your opponent,
interrupting spell cast and lowering their chance to hit by 10% for 10 sec"

Alliance:

Human:

Adaptive Stance
"An adaptive combat stance.
Increases damage done by 5%, critical hit chance by 1,5% and decreases damage taken by 5%.
Counts as Battle Stance, Defensive Stance and Berserker Stance."

Dwarf:

Metal form [1 hour cooldown, 50 rage cost]
"You assume a metal form, increasing your armor by 1000%.
Grants immunity to Bleed, Poison, Disease, Stun and movement impairing effects.
Lasts 30 sec."

Night Elf:

Moondance [Passive]
"Grants you 2% chance to dodge, after each dodge you will perform a weapon attack."

Gnome:

Saphenous Cut [10 rage cost, melee range, needs a sharp weapon]
"Attack the legs of your opponent, causing them to bleed.
Only useable on humanoids"

High Elf:

Nullify Magic [Passive]
"Your chance to block applies to offensive spells made against you"

Consume Mana [5 min cooldown, 40 yard range]
"Attempts to drain (60% of your hp) from your opponents mana pool.
Heal yourself for every two drained mana points"

Glad you brought that up, priests dont have 100% unique abilities, every class has a variation of 4:

A damage reduction buff or debuff

a damage over time ability

a self heal

or

reflective damage ability

If you wanna do warriors justice or do racials in a similar fashion, you should probably follow a similar scheme rather than give everyone something completely different. Additionally things like knockbacks werent even considered for classic and would be broken in the current environment, its far better to keep it simplistic in design.

An additional point id like to make is that nobody likes having long ass hour long cooldowns, if you make new abilities they should be at least usuable and not a button that doesnt show up except for once per hour. Theres a reason stuff like retaliate was later changed to 10 mins from 30.

My suggestions revised:

night elf: grants 100% dodge chance for 3 seconds 10 min cooldown
High elf: Grants 100% resistance for 1.5 seconds, 5-10 min cooldown.

Dwarf: Decreases physical damage taken X for Y seconds. Short to medium cooldown.
Troll: causes the target to deal X less physical damage for Y. Similar cooldown to above.

Goblin: Reduces the targets chance to hit with physical attacks by X for Y.
Gnome: Reduces chance to be hit by physical attacks by X for Y.

The rest would follow a similar vein. Possible mechanics can be ones that increase healing received, damage taken, crit damage or critical strike rating, chance to be hit or crit, ect.
