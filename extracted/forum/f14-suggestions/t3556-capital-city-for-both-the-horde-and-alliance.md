# Capital City for both the Horde and Alliance

## Post 21376 by Hombrehongo — 2022-08-08T00:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21376#p21376 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Now that the portal to Gurubashi is dead forever meeting with people from the opposite faction can be difficult specially while leveling. Since Thrall, Jaina and Malfurion ended in good terms afther the battle at Mount Hyjal i tought that a capital city for both factions is not out of the question.This will also bring more interactivity between the 2 factions. I have 3 suggestions for locations.

 -Mount Hyjal: This would probably be the hardest to make since it will be a city from scratch but the most lore friendly imo. The city itself would be a monument to all the races of Azeroth to remember the danger of the burning legion.

 -Ratchet: The most boring option, also the easiest one. Expand ratchet, give it a boat to stormwind.

 -Theramore: This would make a lot of sense since Jaina wants the Horde and Alliance to work together so making her city friendly to both factions is common sence. This will require an expansion of the city, maybe have a horde side and alliance side. Put a zep to Orgrimmar and we are set.

This cities can make older cities like Stormwind obsolete and become ghost towns like it happend with Shattrath in TBC. So maybe not adding an AH and bank will be best to keep the old cities relevant.

I cant think of any other place to make a neutral capital city. I know that all option will require quite a bit of work so i`m not expecting for the team to actually take mi idea but it`s fun to imagine how would Azeroth evolve afther the events of WC3.
