# Improving the Hardcore Experience on PvP Realms: Enchanting Profession Bottleneck

## Post 112855 by schitzo — 2024-12-16T07:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112855#p112855 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone,

I've been enjoying the Hardcore experience on a PvP realm, but I’ve noticed a significant issue that I believe impacts the quality of gameplay—particularly for players choosing Enchanting as their profession.

As it stands, Enchanting requires special “rods” crafted by Blacksmiths to progress. However, on Hardcore PvP realms, the lack of trading between Hardcore players creates a frustrating bottleneck for Enchanters. Since there are no viable ways to acquire these rods without relying on other players (who aren’t available in this scenario), the profession becomes effectively hard-capped.

My Suggestion:
To address this, I propose introducing an alternative way to acquire these rods in Hardcore mode. For example:

 **Questline** for Enchanting Rods: Create a quest or series of quests allowing Enchanters to craft or earn the rods themselves. This could involve collecting specific materials or defeating certain mobs.
 **NPC Vendor Exchange**: Add an NPC who accepts raw materials (e.g., ores, gems, or enchanting dust) in exchange for the required rods.
This would ensure that Enchanting remains a viable profession for Hardcore players without compromising the spirit of the mode. The solution would also be in line with the self-sufficient nature of Hardcore gameplay while keeping the experience challenging.

I’d love to hear the community’s thoughts on this! Does anyone else feel restricted by the current system, and do you think this kind of change would improve the Hardcore experience?

Thanks for reading, and happy adventuring
