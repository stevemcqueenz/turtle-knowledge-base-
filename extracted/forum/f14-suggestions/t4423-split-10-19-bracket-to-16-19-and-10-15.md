# Split 10-19 bracket to 16-19 and 10-15

## Post 26378 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-09-28T20:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26378#p26378 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently most popular low level brackets is 10-19.
It leads to situation when 10 lvl can join battleground which is probably filled with 19 twinks or with players stopped at 19 lvl and geared from dungeons.
Players from lowest range of battleground usually can't provide enough competition to anything above average level. But they fill the spot and allow to join player from other team. which is not always be lowest possible level.
Meanwhile high level champions are demoralized by having too many low level characters in team and tend to leave.

Earliest available dungeon starts at level 16 - RFC.
People exit starting zone normally around level 12 with 1-2 green items. Gear matter in wow and we get situation when you technically can join, but you are highly discouraged to do so.

Proposal is to change brackets from 10-19 to 10-15 and 16-19.

10-19 bracket should be narrowed anyway to 10-15 and 16-19. It is not about level, it is about available gear.

Normal 10 level character usually just left starting zone. He usually equipped in white gear from starting quests. He is probably not completed his class quest like bear/def stance/voidwalker/fire totem.

While normal 19 level player usually has blue weapon from DM or WC quest. He probably have green gear from professions or from quests. If he stop xp and do some farming, then he can get good greens and some blues from dungeons. I did so and see enough people doing so.

In addition, lvl 10 characters are usually used to fill a queue for opposing faction to start battleground. It is not neccessary to join. If your team has enough players, you will automatically win when battleground ends by timeout. Increasing requirement to level 16 will increase time to prepare such figureheads twice.

Difference in other brackets is not so radical as in 10-19. Most gear from WC and DM is not replaced in 20-29.

We have not enough players in bg just because people know that there will be twinks with good gear and high-level enchants and high level bandages and rare weapons and world buffs. Almost hopeless to join to fight against them. Remove twink powers and you'll see more bg popping. You wouldn't need to fight each of them like raid boss.

I don't propose to remove twinks altogether. Because it is great subculture which I like. minmaxing possibility should be there. I only propose to reduce the gap between twinks and regular players.
Ability to stop xp helps regular players, not hardcore twinks. With stopped xp any regular leveller can stop at 19, collect good gear and increase the chance to win. Hardcore twinks usually have other account with 60 lvl character who can get any dungeon-related item without any xp.

Though I like the idea of giving xp in battlegrounds. Probably it will attract players who is dedicated to PvP and don't like grinding mobs. But those people should not need lvl 60 mage to grind gold and clear dungeons for low level character.

Reduced gap between regular players and twinks also allow to introduce more PvP activities in the world. For example [Ashenvale caravan supplying](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4594). Merchant coast also has a potential of being PvP zone.
