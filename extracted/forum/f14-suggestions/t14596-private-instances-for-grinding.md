# Private instances for grinding

## Post 99596 by Lemurguy — 2024-07-16T04:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99596#p99596 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Some people -- especially some hardcore players -- like to grind endlessly on mobs to gain levels. However, this often puts them into conflict with all the people coming through the zone to farm the same mobs for quest items. Likewise, it's equally frustrating for the people doing quests when a mob has a low drop rate, but a grinder is trying to monopolize them.

I suggest adding private instances where grinders can enter an open landscape with a limited number of types of mobs, all within a very specific level range. The respawn rate would be identical to ordinary mobs in the field, and they should have the same drop list as they do in field. You could add a NPC to the zone, which would serve as a gateway in and out, and also as a vendor for selling loot.

The mobs would be replicas of common grinding mobs found on both continents. Some instances would offer yellow (non-aggro) mobs, and others would offer red (aggro) mobs. A few instances could include camps or caves/mines, but every level range should offer open terrain (since hardcore players will generally avoid high-risk areas). Popular grinding mobs could include crabs, turtles, stags, wolves, spiders, cats, and a few humanoids scattered and occasionally clumped around the zone.

It's important that the experience match traditional grinding, in terms of exp per hour and loot drops/value. One of the benefits to grinding over completing quests is the higher coin to exp ratio. If that's not preserved, I could see people ignoring the private instances and farming in their traditional anyway.

Since 2.0 will supposedly be easier for the devs/admins to monitor for botting, these instances would be a prime target for monitoring to limit concerns about RMT bot farming.

Please consider this for an upcoming release. It would make life better for everyone.
