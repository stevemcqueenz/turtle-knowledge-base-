# Avatar for Prot Warriors

## Post 27319 by Bloodline1x9 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-10-15T15:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27319#p27319 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since I saw that skill in Warcraft 3 (The Mountain King has it) I always thought that such a skill should be in the WoW.
The new additional ultimate talant for Prot warriors.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/GtJXhRky/Avatar.png)

Let me explain why this ability is needed in the game:
 1. This is lore.
 2. Warriors in pvp can't do anything against magic and control. I write this as a mage. Counter-fear? Ok. I have no fear. And warriors have no spell reflect. I just use nova, sheep, slow, whatever. No diminishing. Kite and kill. Armor? Don't care!
But I'm not saying that all warriors should have Avatar. Only Prot who is extremely dead in pvp against casters.
 3. Prot warriors nothing is gained from dodges, parries, blocks and armor vs casters! Thus, this talent is aimed at buff
exactly Prot warriors specifically for pvp.
 4. It will breathe new life into the classics mechanic without changing anything.

Super analogue for dwarf warriors: The "Avatar" share cooldown with "Stoneform". But for dwarfes Avatar duration 8 sec (instead of 6) and cooldown 3 min (instead of 5)
