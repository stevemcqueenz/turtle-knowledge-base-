# Balance Druid still feels unrewarding and overly RNG after latest changes

## Post 172022 by Auralys — 2026-03-23T21:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172022#p172022 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
I’m posting this as a K40-geared Balance Druid (with Scythe of Elune), and even after the recent changes to Starfire crit, the spec still feels really off to play.

The main issue is that the gameplay just isn’t rewarding when you play it correctly.

Right now, when an Eclipse procs, you’re often forced to stopcast the spell you just started because letting it go through is a DPS loss. Since Eclipse windows are so short, you basically have to cancel your cast and immediately switch to the buffed spell.

At the same time, you’re expected to maintain your DoTs on the target, but you have no control over Eclipse procs, which makes some 12-second windows feel almost wasted.

That means optimal gameplay = constantly interrupting yourself.
It feels clunky, unintuitive, and frustrating.

On top of that, the spec feels way too RNG-dependent. More crit just means more procs, but also more situations where you have to react instantly or lose value. You don’t really gain control over your damage, it just becomes more chaotic. Threat generation also becomes very inconsistent because of this.

At the end of the day, you’re putting in more effort than most casters for equal or lower results. And unlike others, we don’t even have a real burst cooldown to stabilize our damage or create a strong window.

Another big issue is how inconsistent procs are with Balance spells.

Moonfire and Insect Swarm ticks don’t proc trinkets (Scythe, Clackora, Sigil), and Hurricane doesn’t either. Meanwhile, other classes can proc everything very easily with instant or low-commitment abilities (Arcane Explosion or Holy Nova, for example).

This makes DoTs feel underwhelming and AoE even worse, especially considering Hurricane is already harder to use properly (channeled, positioning-dependent, and punishing if mobs move).

I recently got the Clackora trinket, and honestly it feels awful on Balance, even though it should be a strong item. Even trying to stack it with rank 1 Wrath feels extremely inconsistent and unreliable.

I don’t think the spec needs more crit. It needs more consistency.

A few changes that would already improve the feel a lot:

Let Eclipse apply to the current cast instead of forcing stopcasting
(for example: the next Wrath after an Eclipse proc benefits from Arcane Eclipse, and the next Starfire benefits from Nature Eclipse — this would feel much more fluid and rewarding)
Allow DoTs (Moonfire / Insect Swarm) and Hurricane to proc trinkets and effects
Add a meaningful cooldown to give the spec some control over its damage
(for example: a cooldown that extends the current Eclipse by ~12 seconds every 3 minutes)

 **Right now, the spec feels clunky, RNG-heavy, and less rewarding than simpler alternatives.**

Curious to hear if other Balance players feel the same, especially with raid gear and logs to compare.

Thanks for reading.
