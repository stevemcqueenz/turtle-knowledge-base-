# Level Squishing Dungeons

## Post 37054 by Citramate — 2023-03-07T13:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37054#p37054 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I feel like the level range in levelling dungeons can often be way too much.

Example being Uldaman. You have lvl 36 elite mobs and a lvl 47 boss in the same dungeon. If you want to complete the dungeon you need to bring people lvl 42+ and it trivialises the first bit. Once the level difference becomes too high it just becomes an RNG fest where hunters become even more OP at levelling due to a lack of resist/dodge/parry and glancing.

It's definitely vanilla but a level range of 11 is pretty insane. I understand that Blizzard intended to make Uldaman a 'winged' dungeon but obvious this didn't work and basically nobody treats it as a 'winged' dungeon anyway.

I'd prefer a level range of 3 to 5 with most of the earlier proportion of the dungeon being buffed to compensate. I think most people prefer it when the dungeon feels level appropriate from start to finish.

BRD would an exception but I every other dungeon would be more fun without the large level gaps between start and finish.

TLDR: Uldaman would be more fun if the mobs inside it ranged from 40-44 rather than 36-47
