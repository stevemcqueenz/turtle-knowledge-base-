# PvP rank affect experience gain

## Post 87347 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-02-09T09:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87347#p87347 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, you can reach rank 3 at lvl 29, rank 5 at lvl 39, rank 9 at 49 lvl, rank 13 at 59.
Proposal is:
increase xp gain from all sources depending on current rank of a character. Each rank grants 5% to xp gain.
So if you get your max possible rank early, then you get 15% to xp up to lvl 29. 25% up to lvl 39, 45% up to lvl 49, 65% at lvl 59.
This way, people will be interested in spending time to pvp including battlegrounds because in long term it will grant them faster leveling.
I think it will increase amount of people on low level battleground, making it more PvP than "grind for month before your first contact".
