## Post 107606 by Zeldy (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-11T23:53:00Z

Thank you to Akalix for suggesting this.

The Alternate Tier sets introduced with Patch 1.17.2's release have overall been a very welcome accommodation for both hybrid classes and players wishing to experiment with different specs. This thread is for constructive criticism and suggestions regarding the itemizations of the new sets. It doesn't have to be the set entirely but rather individual pieces.
Please be respectful and if possible, use screenshots for clarity. Imgur is a good source for easy uploads.

 **Priest Tier 2 Helmet and Pauldrons for Shadow Spec:**

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/wqUfjaY.png)

The stats for this item are quite good as they are, but the 1% Crit is a bit awkward because it directly competes with this:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/rE8Phg0.png)

As the item currently is, there's no reason to use it over Mish'undare which has extremely comparable stats on top of an extra percent of crit. It's also not worth using over Spellweaver's Turban if the player is aiming for hit cap. Ideally, the 1% Crit would be changed to 1% Hit instead, this way it's no longer competing with Mish'undare and instead becomes a direct PvE upgrade over Spellweaver's rather than a PvP sidegrade still outclassed by Mish'undare. Alternatively, giving the Tier 2 Helmet both 1% Hit *and* 1% Crit would be amazing, but I'm trying to keep expectations realistic. (:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/O136y4O.png)

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Gb3QE2v.png)

The extra 10 Spellpower the Tier 2 pauldrons currently have doesn't really feel justified for the lack of hit, because if the player wanted to prioritize raw SP they would choose Mantle of the Blackwing Cabal from the same raid. The Tier 2 pauldrons are again in an awkward predicament where they lack the 1% Hit advantage that the Tier 1 pauldrons have while also lacking the 34 SP of MotBC.

Feel free to provide suggestions regarding your own class' Tier Sets.

## Post 107760 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-13T00:57:00Z

It's been brought up in Theorycrafting but Druid T3 alt set for boomkins is unusable. All 9 pieces together grant 4% spell hit when they need a minimum of 12% from gear. It makes it extremely difficult to hit your hitcap for FF at the start of fights and also locks you in on spell hit gear for all of the other slots if you want to use it. If it wasn't replacing one of the slots with hit%, the T3 ring would be insane to use and well deserved since it's a KT drop.

Additionally, haste isn't so good on boomkins with how eclipse rotations work. Wrath will refresh at or faster than GCD so any haste is wasted when nature eclipse is up. It helps for Starfire on paper, but then you get so many casting speed reduction procs that in practice it does almost nothing. It's wasted stats on a class that craves %crit even more than it did pre-patch.

I would change every %haste into either %hit or %crit. If you want to allow people some choice on rings, trinkets, neck, and weapon, skew more towards %hit. If you want them to stick to exactly one gear build, make it all %crit.
