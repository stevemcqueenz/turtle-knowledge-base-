# Rogue Vigor Talent Change Idea

## Post 36094 by Tonyboyswag — 2023-02-25T18:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36094#p36094 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Seal Fate has always been a very fun spec to play as a rogue in PvE because it allows you to actually use a finisher that isn't Slice and Dice every other finisher, and while it is viable it always loses out to deep Combat daggers and swords, and even maces.

Vigor is a completely useless talent for rogues for anything other than having 10 extra energy on your opener which does nothing in PvE, and in PvP can't be taken because you can't get it as well as Dirty Deeds (20 reduced energy cost of Cheap Shot), so is again rendered useless. But, I think an interesting and fun change for the talent in PvE would be to have it refund 10 energy on ONLY Backstab criticals. This would make the spec more competitive with Combat swords, AND could be a great way for Improved Expose Armor rogues to not have to feel like trash because they will have enough combo points to keep up Slice and Dice and Expose Armor, and with enough crits could also maybe get an Eviscerate in. Now that World Buffs are removed, however, it is possible that maybe 10 energy is too little considering you'd be missing out on 10% crit, so maybe 15? Would need some testing either way.

Overall I think it would be a super fun spec to play (by Classic WoW standards) because finishers are fun and satisfying to press, and you would be able to press a total of 4 whole buttons (Backstab, Slice and Dice, Expose Armor, Eviscerate) instead of the usual 2!! (Sinister Strike and Slice and Dice). Truly a massive IQ required to play this spec.
