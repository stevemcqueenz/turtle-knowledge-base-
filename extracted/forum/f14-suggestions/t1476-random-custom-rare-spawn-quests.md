# Random Custom Rare Spawn Quests

So we all know what rare spawns are. But what if there are rare quest npcs? And not only are they a rare spawn but they appear randomly. They will appear on a random variable somewhere in the world of Azeroth. Doing some and finishing it could make them disappear or they can remain for a certain length of time. I think it would create more uncertaintly in the game and make it more interesting. Rewards would be random but valuable one of a kind stuff. Vanity items like that with some basic random green loot occasionally. Maybe even some rare crafting recipes as rewards with a handful of silver. People would make rumors about them being here and there to investigate.

The chicken hidden quest was interesting and not many players knew that which was interesting.
