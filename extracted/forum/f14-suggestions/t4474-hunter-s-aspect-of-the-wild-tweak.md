# Hunter's Aspect of the Wild Tweak...

## Post 26756 by Maesus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-10-05T09:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26756#p26756 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Aspect of the Wild is pretty useless, isn't it?

 *The hunter and group members within 30 yards take on the aspect of the wild, increasing Nature resistance by 45.  Only one Aspect can be active at a time.*

Yes, Nature Resistance is important in some instances, but only some. Other Aspects are quite useful through out. Hawk is your base aspect for ranged hunters, Monkey (+Wolf) is for melee hunters, and Cheetah + Pack is for traveling, solo and with group respectively. So what does Aspect of the Wild offer? Not much...

So, my suggestion and to keep with the Wild theme:
- Keep the nature resistance

- 5% mana cheaper Stings (Serpent, Scorpid, Viper, Wyvern)

- 5% mana cheaper Traps
I think this would make it a very good Aspect for Survival hunters, who focus on traps and stings, without being too useful as to make other Aspects irrelevant :)
