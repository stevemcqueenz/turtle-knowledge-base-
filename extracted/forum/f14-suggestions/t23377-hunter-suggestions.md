Hi there!

I love playing the hunter in tcraft and almost can play my playstyle, beside that i lack dps when i go pet-less.
I know hunter is great with pet but i think like the demonologist, the hunter should have a possibility to boost his damage when renouncing the pet, to enjoy playing an archer build.

> Lone Killer
>
>  If no beast is 25yards near the Hunter, increases the damage of all attacks by 25%

That would be awesome for sniper type ranged dps builds and would give the hunter a new role identity variation with just one talent alone. It could be placed isntead of piercing shots and piercing shots could be addition to improved stings (for example: if next autoshot after serpent sting crits, adds bleed effect)

I think the Marksman-talent-tree also could benefit from some color like cool proccs or synergies between the skills (crits on steady shot improve the next aimed shots damage or something like that)

And please give the Hunter a way to replenish a little bit of mana (ranged weapon spec could procc mana or mp5/2) so we arent always out of mana entirely (i am only lvl 53 but even i tried to max mana regeneration i still run out of mana)

just wishes, add yours!
