## Post 121213 by Planetsap — 2025-02-28T14:26:00Z

Hi i'm Tex players from Turtle wow since 3 years now, I'd like to share with you about new ideas that could come up in the next few months about the Turtle wow PVP scene.
I'm not a developer but I have a lot of experience in Wow in general especially in Pvp. When it comes to talking about arena.
I feel legitimate to share with you some ideas that could revive and influence the Pvp scene of the server.
I'm going to send you some ideas about the arena. I hope you'll take the time to read them and see the potential in them.
what you are about to read are only examples and ideas of what could be created.

Let's assume that we want to increase influence and add value to turtle wow's pvp sene,
To achieve this, we'll need to add content
These are the main content ideas to add, which in my opinion would be a huge plus in the evolution of the PVP scene.

 **Entering the Arena :**
First of all, the creation of arene rated teams
Once you have your team of 2, 3,players ready, you can queue for arenas by talking to an Arena Battlemaster NPC in game
let's not forget that we don't want to take away the possibility for players to join the queue in Skirmish (arena no rated)
 *All Arena Teams begin at 0 team rating and must climb their way through the ranks. Communication and coordination is key!*
Don't forget that every time a player wins or loses, he gains or loses rating.
 *But the player will gain honor regardless of winning or losing*
 **Finding a balance, removing the consumables in the arena is the first thing to do.**
(Food/drink/mage food) is the only thing available, the rest is just player skill.

 **Seasonal Rewards :**
 *It's also possible to add lots of exclusive content thanks to this unique new system, which we've never seen on any VANILLA+ server.*
For example, reaching a certain arena rating of “2,400+” gives you the opportunity to buy exclusive transmogrification weapons, armor and more.
Logically, rewards go like this:
Top 0.1% 2.700+ rating (TOP 5/10 players of the server) receive a special mount and a permanent title as listed below “Infernal Turtle Gladiator”.)
Top 0.5% will receive the title of “Turtle Gladiator” 2.400+ rating for the rest of the season (non-permanent)
Duelist 2.100 rating (Top 0.5% - 3%) Duelist Title.
Rival 1.800 rating (Top 3% - 10%) Rival Title.
Challenger 1.600 rating (Top 10% - 35%) Challenger Title.
You can add to this many other thoughtful rewards such as “Tabard for example" at different rating.
At the end of each Arena season, eligible players get rewards based on their rating. Season 1 features a unique title—the “Infernal Turtle Gladiator”— and players who reach the top of the bracket in the server will receive seasonal rewards as follows:

Infernal Turtle Gladiator (Top 0.1%)
Turtle Gladiator (Top 0.5%)
Duelist (Top 0.5% – 3%)
Rival (Top 3% – 10%)
Challenger (Top 10% – 35%)
In addition, recipients of the Infernal turtle Gladiator and turtle Gladiator titles will also receive,
a "Swift Nether Drake" mount for example or something else disgned by the Turtle Wow devs Team.

Players will need to win at least 50 games ahead 2.400+ rating for example to qualify for a seasonal reward.
Once a season has ended and a new one begins.
The Arenas await you. It’s time to earn your place among legends, champion.
