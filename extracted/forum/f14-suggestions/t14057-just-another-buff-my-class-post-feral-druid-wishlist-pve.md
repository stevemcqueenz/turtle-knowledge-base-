# Just Another 'Buff my Class' post. Feral Druid Wishlist - PVE

## Post 95761 by Daddydeath — 2024-05-27T03:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95761#p95761 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We all good, we know what this is? Aight? Great! Let's get to it in no particular order, with nothing needing to be added over something else. I don't want this to boil into a "WOW YOU ADD ALL OF THIS AND FERAL WOULD BE SO OP!!!!!!?!?!?!" Listen, I'm just throwing shit that's mostly been said before I'm sure at the wall as mostly separate suggestions from eachother.

 1. Swipe Buff + Support talent. Inherent buff is giving it x% Attack Power scaling (I've mathed out around 13% being the correct number), and Feral Instinct being slightly changed so it increases the targets Swipe hits by 1 or 2 per point. Bears desperately need AoE.

 2. On the topic of Swipe - Swipe (Cat) being added at 30 Base energy. Unlike Swipe (Bear) it would only have 9% AP Scaling, but benefit from the +2/+4 target cap from Feral Instincts all the same

 3. Crowd Pummeler needs to get the Wolfheart treatment. To be turned into a Druid specific enchant. The engineering option is cute, but it does not solve the inherent issue of "You're not allowed to progress your weapon past level 30". Some people like it, that's fine. I'm sure equally many would argue that you may as well just give Druid +50% base Attack Speed, and you're not exactly wrong. I'd be fine with a weaker option (30%?) as an Enchant. Choice is important.

 4. Tiger's Fury NEEDS to last when shapeshifting out of catform. This ability is essentially 560 AP we're rarely allowed to touch.

 5. [Pipe dream] I'm not asking for Windfury, well okay I kind of am - but if there was a way to separate it, I would be 100% happy to just be able to use things like Shadow/Frost oil so we can at least help more than AAing out of form in the viscidus fight, or similar.

 6. Idol that lets Rip and Rake critically strike. Reason why rip/rake is rarely used outside of either filling energy gaps or "I'm going to have to disengage the boss" is mainly because they scale poorly and Claw/Shred/Fero Bite can all crit instead. Speaking of-

 7. Can we please increase the scaling on rake/rip? Rake having 5% more initial and 3% Per Tick AP scaling would help SO much.

 8. Rip having 1% more AP per tick per combo point would also help a lot :6

 9. Thick Hide kinda needs a reduced chance to get crit baked into it. Bear gets near nothing out of Defense (can't parry/block) and getting 6% critcap requires like exactly a certain set of gear, half which is PVP exalted from bloodring and basically not obtainable. Thus, I suggest making it 1% Less chance to be crit per point.

 10. More idols that change how Leader of the Pack works. Imagine one that also gave everyone affected 3% attack speed?  Or a % chance to trigger nature damage on their target hit? So many options! Capitalize on it!

 11. Improved Shred reducing shred cost by up to 15 with 2 points. Only reason for this is so that the math is more consistent. 45 instead of 48 energy per use doesn't sound like much but squeezing an extra claw/rake/shred out before powershifting is nice.

 12. Most controversial change! Feline Swiftness also increasing the amount of Energy generated per tick by 5 per point. This barely does anything bcs wolfheart powershifting is still better, but it'd be nice.

 13. Better optimized gear for feral :3

That's basically it. Feel free to tear me apart.
