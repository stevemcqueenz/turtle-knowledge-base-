# Item stacks size

## Post 6640 by Diablo — 2020-06-30T23:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6640#p6640 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to make a suggestion, in terms of item stack size, regarding for example, elixirs,flask,potions,trash items in general, and even soul shards.

All of them should have a minimum of stacks of 20.

Reason? Well, it's obvious that one of the greatest problems in vanilla wow is the inventory space, regarding even trash items in general for farming, or while leveling, you either carry heal/mana potions all the time (if you use mana) or you might be just die without hope if you can't heal yourself while in combat, warriors and rogues suffer the most from this, and for hardcore players it would be awesome too.

There's absolute no argument to say no to this, it was just bad game design from vanilla/classic world of warcraft from blizzard, which they lately realized in wotlk for example, with the ammunition sizes, also it would empower the alchemy/engineering market in terms of item crafting and even for cooking materials, some of those materials have stacks of 5 or 10, that sucks and needs to be changed, I'm talking from my own perspective but I also understand many of you guys who enjoy vanilla can see this major problem of inventory space overall, specially with so many questing items we're provided or asked for sometimes.

I sincerely hope you do this, it would only improve the QUALITY of life for everybody in the game but not only that, it would make the server MUCH more appealing for everyone, you know.

I would like to read what you guys might think about this.   smiling_turtle_head

Inventory Management I think is a key aspect of the game that makes it a little more challenging that I would be sad to see go.  I could see balancing/fixing some problems, like Sinrek mentioned with hunters needing their ammo, but its a slippery slope.

An advantage to being an alchemist is that you can carry herbs to a stack of 20, bring a few vials, and by making potions on the fly, can carry many more potions/bag slot than a non-alchemist.  By upping potions to 20/stack you are essentially nerf'ing alchemy.

It is all about deciding for yourself what is and is not worth space, which professions you take for what advantage, etc.
