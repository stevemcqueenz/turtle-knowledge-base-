# Duel Tents

## Post 43416 by Ginro — 2023-04-27T11:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43416#p43416 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I saw two problems that i feel can be resolved with one feature. Currently, tents are just a place for people to afk, you cant wonder too far away so interacting with a ball of players is rather unattractive. The second problem I saw was HC characters can't really PVP due to the risk of dying.

My suggestion is Dueling Tents. Allow HC characters to PVP and give people an activity while waiting for rest.

Potential implementation is A duelling poll. Two people have to activate it and they are forced to duel. After the duel, a small radius around the poll provides tent rested. Players have to keep topping up the effect of the poll by duelling.

This is just one idea for how it could be implemented but more importantly, is the idea of allowing duels while waiting for tent rested.
