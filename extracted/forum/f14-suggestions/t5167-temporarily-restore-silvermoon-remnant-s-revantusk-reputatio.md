# Temporarily restore Silvermoon Remnant's & Revantusk reputation gear.

## Post 31388 by Darkcovenant — 2022-12-22T16:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31388#p31388 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

As some of you may have noticed the latest patch removed the bulk of the gear sold by the reputation vendor's in Alah'thalas & Amani'alor. Quite a few people including myself, had deliberately put effort into getting revered with the Silvermoon Remnant's or the Horde equivalent. This was in anticipation of being able to purchase items when reaching the relevant level and there was no notice given that we had a limited timeframe in which to achieve this. In-fact it wasn't even in the patchnotes, so I enquired with a GM to ask about the reasons for the change. It was explained to me that a new system is being worked upon by the developers & information on this will be announced as soon as possible. Of course in the long term that is a great idea and I'm sure myself and most others appreciate the hard work the developers do to improve our already excellent experience on Turtle WoW.

I would like to suggest however, that as it is unreasonable to expect the developers to rush the implementation of their changes to faction vendor gear. That they should instead temporarily restore the item's removed from the Silvermoon/Revantusk vendor's until the replacement system is ready to be put onto the live servers. After all, no one has any idea whether the new system will be in place next week or in three months; and as said previously it would be unfair and demanding to expect the developers to rush for our sake (especially as the game is free!). Also in my own case I went to bed on the 19th of December with around 500-1000xp left to go until level 60 and I'd have made sure I bought the gear prior to the patch release the next day had I known otherwise.

Best wishes,

Aeldred/Drethorian
