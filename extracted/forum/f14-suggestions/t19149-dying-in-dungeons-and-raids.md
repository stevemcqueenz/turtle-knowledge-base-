# Dying in dungeons and raids

## Post 130118 by gusftavares — 2025-05-07T23:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130118#p130118 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There’s a situation that I believe most WoW players have experienced. It goes like this: you log in after a busy day, start a party to run a dungeon, and everyone heads toward the entrance. It takes a few minutes to get there (which I think is fine gameplaywise), then the group wipes for whatever reason. Now, everyone has to run all the way from the graveyard back to the dungeon, retracing every step up to the point where the wipe happened.

Here’s the problem: after all that preparation, people are already tired. Someone quits, it takes more time to find a replacement, and eventually the group disbands. You end up logging out of the game, frustrated, because you don’t have time to go through the entire process again.

I wrote this short story to suggest a way to make dying in dungeons less time-consuming, with a “simple” change that I believe wouldn't negatively impact gameplay. When a party wipes in a dungeon, players should only be able to release after everyone is out of combat or the entire group is dead. Once released, everyone should respawn either at the entrance of the dungeon or at the last boss that was defeated.

If there’s any way this change could negatively impact gameplay, I’m open to reconsidering. But at this moment, I don’t see a strong reason why this quality-of-life improvement shouldn’t be implemented.
