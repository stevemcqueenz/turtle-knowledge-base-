## Post 55917 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-05T18:02:00Z

This is clearly a human problem.

Firstly, tanking is an unpopular role with a lot of responsibility, so it attracts very different people. Level-headed veterans is one. But power-tripping egomaniacs and incompetent players looking to skip the queue are others. If you get a power-tripping a-hole as a tank, there's not much you can do other than leave.
Secondly, most people playing WoW are min/maxers to some degree. They want to optimize their runs and not "waste" their time (when imo playing the game and having fun should actually be the goal in itself, not just beating the instance in the shortest time possible). So they use heuristics (ie mental shortcuts) and heuristics have some merit but they are always flawed.

People in here describe being inexplicably kicked from groups by tanks. I've never had it happen to me, but I've seen it plenty from the perspective of the healer. So I can offer their reasoning. Sometimes they indiscriminately kicked every min-level player. That's a good heuristic to speed up the run, but obviously can lead to some bad calls (like kicking competent and/or twinked out characters and getting complete slackers in garbage gear that are just higher level but don't offer anything to the group). Sometimes they kick players on different continents - again somewhat sensible to speed up the run. Once I've witnessed a tank kicking players because the tank "needed a mage", thinking they could not beat the instance without ranged dps, aoe and polymorph. That was pretty ridiculous as it affected half a dozen people in queue while the tank ultimately gave up and left the group anyway.

While I don't condone that and while I think running suboptimal comps and making them work is half the fun, there is a point here that hasn't been brought up in this thread yet: LFT does not give a rat's ass about group composition. Sometimes you get a group with almost no buffs, or melee only, or leather-wearing only. And this is where the heuristics kick in again and make some tanks try to "fix" the group because it seems tedious or not viable to them. And truth be told - sometimes really bad comps will fail the instance. For example: LBRS spiders with sub-60 people and zero AoE totally sucks and can be a huge wipe and breaking point for the group half-way through the instance. And if you want to tackle that - someone has to make a call and someone has to leave the group. Given the queue times, this will never be pretty or easy.

I think 90% of time it's tanks being narrow-minded or power-tripping divas. But 10% they're just trying to fix glaring issues in LFT groups to hopefully make the experience more enjoyable for everyone.

PS: Yes, starting to tank myself has also opened my eyes a little to why some tanks are the way they are. If people are actively hurting the group and causing wipes or making the game unenjoyable - there's nobody else to reign them in but you and ultimately the only tool you have to reliably make them stop is kick them and look for a replacement. You just have to make sure to do it to people who deserve it, not pre-emptively do it to everyone trying to build the perfect party for a push-over instance. There's plenty of slack to run sub-optimal parties and still have a blast.
