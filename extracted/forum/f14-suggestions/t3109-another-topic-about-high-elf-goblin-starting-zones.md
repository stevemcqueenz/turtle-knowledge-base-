# Another Topic About High Elf / Goblin Starting Zones

## Post 19038 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-06-07T18:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19038#p19038 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, again.  Time for a *completely new topic* about something *no one* has ever talked about, before!  (;

So, I noticed something rather odd about the new race starting zones.  The High Elves start off in Loch Modan at the pre-existing Farstrider Lodge.  Goblins start off in a **completely inaccessible** valley in the Stonetalon Mountains.  Er… *why*?  It seems odd, to me, that Goblins would have their own little private playground that no one can get into.

Lore-wise, I guess it makes a little sense?  The Venture Company isn't exactly *friendly* with most free-thinking races and they need to keep their "fresh meat" safe during training.  So, what better way than by tunneling into a valley surrounded by completely impassible terrain – from the outside, anyway.
The High Elves, on the other hand, are an *established* Alliance race and have been since the second game, at least.  It would make very little sense to have them isolate themselves from the other races for five levels or so.  And yet… if we follow Blizzard's trends [forum-wide hissing intensifies], that's *exactly* how things should go.

I propose the following:
Since it's possible to edit maps, import them, and even create *brand new ones*, why not make a specific "instance" just for the High Elves and Goblins, a'la the Worgen and Bilgewater Goblins of Retail?  This would give far more space for quests and story-telling.

Alternate proposal:
Open the Goblin starting zone to outside access and allow Level 1 players to make a run for it.  High Elves can *already* scurry across Loch Modan if they want to ignore that quest line, and there *are* safe paths through Stonetalon, so why force Goblins to complete their quest line?  Let them be "renegades"… build their *own* cartel!  With *blackjack*!  And *dancing turtles*!!
(Yes, I know there is a very important lore reason why Goblins *need* to finish their quest line.  I'm just sayin'.)

Cheers.
