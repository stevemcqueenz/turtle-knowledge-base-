# A suggestion for the Turtle team

## Post 33186 by Lisakomo62 — 2023-01-26T14:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33186#p33186 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've tried out many private servers and so far Turtle wow is the best one I've ever seen. It shows so much promise but it still lacks some basic features that other private servers offer

My Suggestion to the Turtle team is the creation of 2 edditional pages that could be accessed from the turtle wow website after logging in

These would be the Account information page and the Character Information page

The Account information page would give players the option to not only view general info about their Account but also change important Account information without having to send an email to the Turtle wow team

The info that players should be allowed to change from this screen is as follows

Email address
Password
Account Username
Fourm Username

I also think that the team should allow players the option to use special Characters when creating or changing usernames and up the username limit to 30 characters per username

The Character information page would show all the characters that a player has and allow them to view general info about each character such as Level, reputation and profession levels and so much more
