# A dual wielding warriors' lament on itemization and talents after the revamped hit cap..

## Post 127874 by Prebolini — 2025-04-21T20:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127874#p127874 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey fellow turtles!
I've played warrior on turtle wow the last two years. I had a nine month hiatus and came back at the end of last year after I heard that all classes got a huge rework and most interestingly, the hit cap got had changed.

During my first year as a fury warrior (2023) a lot of warrior gear got reworked/nerfed. And most notably lionheart helmet and titanic leggings. Disappointed at first I sort of saw that it's kind of boring to have two insanely bis items being craftable and attainable as you hit 60, and skip on all the other cool legs/head loot you can get from raids. There was still a lot of ways to get hit cap without them, albeit it was a lot harder..

Now, to the current meta, with the new hit cap system. I'm basically decently geared from bwl/mc and about to start aq 40 soon, and I'm having some gripes with gearing up my fury warrior. I've gotten +5 sword and dagger specialization, and that means I "only" need +14% hit to reach cap... And guess what, with the itemization and stats on mc/bwl gear makes that really damn hard to achieve!

I have gotten a lot of great gear, onslaught girdle, titanic leggings, drakefang talisman etc. but I feel forced to let a lot of the other great gear remain in my bags simply because it prevents med from getting hit capped. The t1 and t2 are mostly bad fod me with a few exceptions. This also makes gearing up for fury tanking a shitshow unless you are getting carried up to aq40/naxx gear. The balance between +hit for threat/dps and +defense on warrior gear should get a review. Arms warriors and paladins have it easy imo these days.

Please review either the +hit cap on offhand to maybe 12% instead of 15. OR make stats changes to a lot of warrior gear to include more +hit%, revert lionheart helmet would be an easy fix? Maybe even give warriors a talent to get 2-3%+hit from the fury skill tree?

Fury warriors are still fun to play. The dps is still good. But I feel we have too few choices of gear to slap really hard,  and it's very hard to get!  We're being way too punished for using an offhand right now.
