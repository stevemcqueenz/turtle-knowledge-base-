Greetings, after many runs in Black Morass I can say that the dungeon difficulty is good for a end game dungeon, the loot is mostly not worth any effort, the 2h mace and the glowes with resistance where good, the rest is just like a shard mostly.

All bosses exepct the first one should always drop at least 2 blues, so you can at least get shards as a reward for the run.

The whelps mana steal should be tuned down a bit. 100% instant is a bit hard. (Or how fast it is, I have no idea.)

The item to close the portals should not disappear when leaving the dungeon, this is just more and more time for people to run back and forth to get the item again.

Chromie should always spawn in front of the sandwall after killing the dragon boss. So you can proceed with the dungeon.

After killing Evil Chromie, the add spawn should completly stop.

 *edit Chromies dead body should not vanish 3-5 seconds after she died*

The Sand in the dungeon should be tradeable, so people can the sand that is stored on their alts. And those who do not need it for themselves can sell it.

If there are any plans for making it accessable with up to 10 people, it should get a scaling if 5 man is like the current one, more people make bosses and trash health pool higher and a bit more damage from bosses.
