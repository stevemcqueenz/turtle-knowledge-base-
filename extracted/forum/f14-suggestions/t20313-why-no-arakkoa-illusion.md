## Post 139800 by Avereth — 2025-07-16T06:26:00Z

I'm kind of shocked this is not already a shop option. The TBC bird people already had basically every player animation built in if you experimented with emotes while transformed to look like one while doing a Zangarmarsh quest.  It's far better suited as an illusion to buy than so many others that don't even have a jumping animation.

As a personal note, I would really like it for times I want my tauren moonkin to be smaller in size than a molten giant, and changing into a smaller bird to cast my spells would be a fun change.  You guys keep adding TBC models in with stuff like Ysh'Golar, Al'Kazeth, and lots of other weapons and armors, so maybe look into such an easy win putting Arakkoa into the shop?  It could even toggle between different outfits like many other illusions do!
