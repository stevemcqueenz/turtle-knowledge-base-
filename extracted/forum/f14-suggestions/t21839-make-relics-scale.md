# Make Relics scale

## Post 151238 by Cheruscan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-09-11T08:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151238#p151238 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Pls make it so Relics have an appropriate scaling to carry them into endgame.

Turtle Devs have done an amazing job introducing many new relics to our favourite hybrids (paladin/shaman/druid) starting from the early 20s in some cases, but many fall off hard in the endgame.

Now you may think, "ofc a lvl 30 blue will be bad at lvl 60" but relics are not throwaway items that can be easily replaced with an upgrade like weapons and armor! They are supposed to give your gameplay experience a new twist in some minor way!

Shamans in particular are in a very good spot already, they often tie their relics together with spells into macros to reap the benefits of multiple relics.

I'd like to see the same lvl of interaction with relics by druids and paladins (mind you the new paladin Libram from MC is already a step in the right direction here)

Specifically I'd like some minor Healingpower scaling on the Farraki Libram and some minor spellpower scaling on the insect swarm idol from Stormwrought descent, both of these could be great in endgame, but their lack of scaling makes the higher lvl passive relics more appealing
