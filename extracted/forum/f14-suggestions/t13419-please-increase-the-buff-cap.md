The buff cap is as far as I know 30. I use buffalo to see all my buffs.

TWoW added lots of new buffs from new druid aura, to Preist champion buff, to new consumables like dreams hard elixir omand dreamtonic.

Its is very easy to reach 30 buffs and lose important ones, even with world buffs out of the picture.

Being punished for having too many positive buffs is very counter intuitive.

People are using addons to auto remove buffs they seem not worth a slot to solve this problem of limited buff slots. On my paladin I Bann the priest spirit buff because it's not worth it.

TWoW managed to increase the debuff cap from 16 to 64!
Can we please have the buff cap increase too from 30 to 48?

It doesn't even matter if we can't see all our buffs of that's a technical limitation, but losing our buffs during combat because we got a rejuv and regrowth or a power word shield at the same time is very sad.

The buff cap does not make the game better. It woukd be better without it
