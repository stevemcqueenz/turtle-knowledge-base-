# A different take on New Classes: The Legendary Adept

## Post 76860 by Tenyar — 2023-11-11T01:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76860#p76860 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtles!  smiling_turtle

As we know Turtle WoW is known for its commitment to preserving the classic World of Warcraft experience while expanding horizontally and enriching the game's lore and class fantasy. The Legendary Adept Hero Class is a perfect embodiment of this philosophy. Rather than introducing a generic new custom class(Bard, Tinker, Chronomancer, ex..), this class takes players on a thrilling journey that truly makes them feel like they've become legends within their chosen class.

![Image](https://assetsio.reedpopcdn.com/WoW-History-Header-01.jpg?width=690&quality=80&format=jpg&auto=webp)

 **Class Introduction: Legendary Adept**
The Legendary Adept is a truly unique and prestigious class in World of Warcraft, offering players the opportunity to transcend the boundaries of their chosen class and become a legendary figure within the game's lore. Starting off as a standard character of their chosen class, upon reaching the maximum level, they are presented with a unique opportunity to delve deeper into their class's lore and potential.

 **The Quest for Mastery:**
At the level cap, players receive a quest specific to their class that serves as the catalyst for their transformation into a Legendary Adept. Be it a mysterious scroll that shows up in your mailbox, an apprentice from a Master that gives you a personal summons, perhaps your Warlock's newly created soulshard is a little TOO dark, and starts to whisper to you.. However this journey finds you it will start a multi-part, lore-rich quest chain that spans across the entirety of Azeroth, immersing players in the class fantasy and challenging their skills.

 **Evolution of Mastery:**
Upon completing the quest chain, players' characters evolve into Legendary Adepts; While still technically retaining their base class, they are giver a special title to display their newfound position amongst the annals of history. (Druid -> Archdruid, Mage -> Magnus) In addition to their title they will gain access to a set of new spells that align with their class's unique identity and specialization. These new spells are not merely more powerful versions of existing abilities but are carefully crafted to enhance the class's fantasy and utility.

In addition to the new spells, existing class abilities evolve to reflect the player's mastery over their class. These evolved spells may gain additional effects, increased potency, or even unique interactions with the new Legendary Adept abilities, creating a seamless and rewarding transition for players who have dedicated themselves to their class.

 **Class Identity and Lore:**
The process of becoming a Legendary Adept class delves deep into the lore and identity of each class in the World of Warcraft universe. For example, a Legendary Adept Paladin may undertake a quest chain that uncovers lost artifacts of the Silver Hand, while a Legendary Adept Rogue might explore the shadowy history of the Uncrowned.

This emphasis on class identity and lore ensures that players are not just becoming more powerful but are also becoming legends within their class. Each class's quest chain is designed to be unique, with its own storyline, challenges, and revelations that tie into the larger world of Azeroth. I believe this approach would foster a strong sense of class identity. It allows players to grow in terms of character depth and lore connection without simply facing "bigger, badder" threats or pursuing endless power increases.

Let me know what you guys think!
