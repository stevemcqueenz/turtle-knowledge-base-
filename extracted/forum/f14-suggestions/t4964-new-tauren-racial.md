## Post 29982 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-11-29T01:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29982#p29982 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Tauren are a humanoid bovine race that is assuming the native north american archetype. They were never domesticated by another race and bred to give extra milk.
And a cow, like all mammals, gives milk once it gave birth, not by basic because it is a cow.
It makes as much sense for the Tauren specifically, to have a racial that makes milk, as it would for any other female of the playable mammal races... none.

That being said, I think some of the concepts you've suggested are interesting and I like the initiative that starts these brainstorming discussions.
The race of choice for the class should matter almost as much as the spec of choice, the classes being de-standardized is a good thing.
Of course not only flavor matters, but lore-accuracy, balance, and importantly also whether it's grounded (example: orc rogue being able to strangle; is, but undead rogue summoning upon death a group of undead NPCs out of nowhere; isn't).
