## Post 37935 by Liatris — 2023-03-16T05:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37935#p37935 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have mixed feelings about a new server. I see why people want one, but I think many of us are already leveling alongside each other. I've met so many people just from having the same batch of quests as them and grouping up to complete them. I really like how alive that makes leveling feel. Maybe I'm wrong, but I feel like a fresh server could leave the original server empty.

If 75-85% of incoming players end up in this fresh server, that could also alienate older players from the rest of the community. Even if it's just 15-30 months of separate servers, It doesn't seem very fair to older players considering that much of TurtleWoW is about interacting with one another through PvE and RP. I have to agree with Xer on this one, when they say TurtleWoW is about the journey. It's not meant to be a rush to 60, either.

Besides, not everyone will enjoy being forced into warmode, especially if it's simply to rush through leveling. Many also use warmode for the thrill of PvP, which is fine if they choose to do so. In my experience, I joined Turtle to get away from the toxic environment of a PvP-focused server. Putting a majority of players in a PvP enviornment with warmode on might give the wrong impression that TurtleWoW is a PvP server, when it's not.

As a somewhat new player myself, I would rather not go through the struggle of getting ganked by PvPers while leveling. Turning PvP into the focus of TurtleWoW (intentionally or not) would defeat the entire reason why I joined it in the first place (for the RP/PvE, of course).
