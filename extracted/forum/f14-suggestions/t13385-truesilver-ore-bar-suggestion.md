## Post 90071 by Verdict — 2024-03-13T12:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90071#p90071 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey guys,

Seeing the current way the player base treats Thorium Ore and Truesilver Ore, or basically sometimes it's better to simply sell it to a vendor, I'd like to suggest something!

Vendoring a Truesilver Ore piece is 5s, on the Auction house it's around 9-ish Silver.
However, smelting it and vendoring the Truesilver Bar afterwards is 12.5 Silver.
This is a scheme I've been doing for years that nets me every day some gold & is the only thing you can do with Truesilver that has meaning.

Truesilver Ore has its uses, like leveling mining, while the Truesilver Bars are basically without any meaningful purpose.

My suggestion:
 - Arcanite Bar transmutes to cost also an additional Truesilver Bar to create a demand or be replaced entirely from Thorium Bar to Truesilver Bar.
 - Reducing the spawn rates by a little bit as well.

Thoughts?
