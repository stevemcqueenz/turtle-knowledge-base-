## Post 106977 by X2 — 2024-11-07T22:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106977#p106977 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've noticed many players expressing concerns about the high burst damage that has emerged in PvP since the recent patch. To address this, I propose enhancing existing PvP gear by increasing its stamina and potentially boosting its damage output slightly (although this might need further discussion). By strengthening PvP gear in this way, we could mitigate the issue of frequent one-shots, leading to a more balanced and enjoyable experience. I also hear some people talking about adding resilience like future expansions. However I think that wouldn't stay true to the vanilla feel of things. Let me know what you guys think! :)
