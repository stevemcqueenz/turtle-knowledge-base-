# TL:DR Ideas for a Turtle Expansion?

## Post 15328 by Thecubanpete — 2022-01-04T23:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15328#p15328 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I haven't kept up with the updates from the Turtle folk on what they plan to do next besides some quality of life improvement and additions. But what will the next big thing be? I've tried to make some speculation and maybe some ideas. I would've made an organised list but I shouldn't be writing this much, too much pain mentally. But hey, I love old WarCraft and Turtle. Here we go.

Ever since seeing MadSeason's video here, I've always wondered what WoW could've been like (lore-wise) and where we could've been now?
[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/PAtX3OEFoRc]

The most logical thing and safest is most likely going to the Outland, but not the one we know today but what Blizard intended for us to see for Vanilla. Of course, it'd have to be fanfiction to a certain point unless there's an original script.
If this ever happens, I'm excited to see how the Blood Elves have been doing in Outland if what I've seen so far and what different storylines would we get from the Draenei. Illidan and the Burning Crusade.
The Arakkoa joining the Alliance? Finding Rexxar who guides us to more Mok'nathal willing to fight for the Horde?

Perhaps unused ideas, the multiple dark portals WoW originally had, more famously one from in the ocean. Thanks, MadSeason for the 10 myths. That's a nice source for content I reckon.
Perhaps exploring the 'other side' of Azeroth or venturing through the Emerald Dream?

I am loving that High Elves are in Turtle, I've always wanted them to be a playable race and were hoping for a chance they'd be an allied race-okay, Void Elves...like, whatever I guess.

I'm still learning the ropes playing Turtle WoW and in general, I've never been good at WoW anyway. But I decided to play here because I want to experience the 'What If?' scenario or rather the original vision perhaps Blizz had back the days. I love the lore of WarCraft until Draenor, BfA, Shadowlands. Almost got all the novels.

Writing this, probably way too ambitious for an expansion size update. But if so, every little helps   satisfied_turtle_head

I'm going to play some Turtle until I fall asleep, good night everyone.   maintenance_turtle
