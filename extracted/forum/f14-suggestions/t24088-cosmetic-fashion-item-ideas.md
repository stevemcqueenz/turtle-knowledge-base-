# Cosmetic Fashion Item Ideas

## Post 169354 by Echtm — 2026-02-25T00:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169354#p169354 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

There are a few small cosmetics that I'd like to see added, whether it be to the vendor or to the dono store.

Mail Shirts - Both as a chest slot item and as a shirt slot item. Ideally you would have chainmail, as well as perhaps scalemail. Would be very nice for standalone breastplate/cuirass items which reveal a lot of skin, like the Shining Silver Breastplate and other such textures. I would particularly like chainmail - with no plate - for both the chest slot and shirt slot.

Shield Variants - Certain shields with faction crests have different shape variants [see the standard Stormwind Shield vs the Commander's Crest in Shadowfang Keep]. Having these variants for other faction crests would be ideal.

Glasses/Spectacles - The spectacle models found in future versions of retail WoW are nice, subtle pieces which would be nice to see. The existing shade glasses items are nice, and you could very well just retexture those.
