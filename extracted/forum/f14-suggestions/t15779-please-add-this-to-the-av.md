# Please add this to the AV!

## Post 107590 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-11T21:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107590#p107590 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, I want to share with you my idea to add a little new content to Alterac Valley that in my opinion doesn't deviate much from the original idea of old AV but gives some flavour to this battleground.

The idea is to add new group of NPCs: Lumberjacks.

As you may know Alterac Valley has 2 Lumbermill locations that unfortunately are quite empty. Maybe Horde's Lumbermill is guarded by a few soldiers but Alliance's one is literally empty.

Both factions would have their own Lumberjack groups that consist of Peons/Peasants from Warcraft III. Their routine is ... chopping the wood at the Lumbermill. Both groups would have their Lumberjack leader NPC who has repeatable quest to bring him 10x supplies from the Northern or Southern Mine. It's the same item you collect from the Mines and turn in to Ground Assault NPC in the base. After your team brings 50x supplies to the leader, the Lumberjacks could reconstruct destroyed tower/bunker to the team. However, it won't be that easy.

After you turn in 50x supplies and your team has at least one tower destroyed, there will be special dialogue option shown. When you click it, all Lumberjacks move to that tower/bunker. Once they are in the tower, the reconstruction phase starts - everyone gets notification in the game and on the map gray icon of the tower changes to gray-blue/gray-red depending on the faction.

Reconstruction phase lasts 10 minutes. Enemy team can stop the Lumberjacks from rebuilding the tower/bunker by killing Lumberjack leader NPC. This NPC should have strength of usual AV commander - they should have 20k HP and should deal about 1k damage per hit. The leader stays in front of the tower and watches the work of his Lumberjacks. Meanwhile, all Lumberjacks should use the animation of repairing construction like in Warcraft III - peons digging with their pickaxe, peasants swinging their axe.

If enemy team kills the Lumberjack leader, reconstruction stops and all Lumberjacks in the tower/bunker despawn. They will respawn in 5 minutes at the original place at the Lumbermill with repeatable quest still available but from 0. Enemy team also receives amount of Honor that equals to destroying the tower/bunker.

If friendly team supports the reconstruction process for 10 minutes (which means the leader NPC survives for 10 minutes) then the reconstruction of the tower/bunker completes - friendly team rebuilds the destroyed tower/bunker, archers respawn there, the tower/bunker can be captured and destroyed again, the icon on the map changes to full color again and whole team gets amount of Honor that equals to destroying this tower/bunker.

The Lumberjack leaders should be scripted so they can't be kited more than let's say 80 yards from their original spot when rebuilding the tower/bunker.
Killing Lumberjack leaders should grant Honor ONLY when reconstruction phase starts to prevent some weird Honor farm.

I think it add some flavour to the Alterac Valley, add some new source of Honor and make rebuilding towers/bunkers a thing which has never been possible. Also, Lumberjacks would encourage people to delve into the Mines that currently are not much important.
