# Staff should discuss their philosophy on tuning old raids

## Post 147566 by Rorymink — 2025-08-21T20:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147566#p147566 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Staff should really make some kind of post/statement going over their philosophy and plans for future raid tuning, and the end goal they'd like to see by changing the old content so drastically.

I originally assumed MC/Ony/BWL were 100%+ overtuned due to bugs or oversights, and that the actual tuning would be like 10-15% health on bosses and a little more damage.

However, with the the latest hotfix only addressing 3 bosses in MC, while ignoring all other sweeping changes to MC, Ony, and BWL, I'm starting to realize this massive overtuning and difficulty was their intention in the first place.

I'd love to know staff thoughts on the current difficulty of these raids, how they feel about the feedback, and what plans they have in the future for raid tuning and the direction of raid difficulty for older raids.

Examples of questions:

~~~

What level of gear does twow staff expect players to have for clearing MC and BWL?

How long does twow staff think T.5/T1 raids should be taking to clear MC and BWL?

Does staff think T2.5/T3 raids clearing MC and BWL fast is an issue for twow's longevity?

Does staff consider AQ20, Kara10 and ZG to be the only raids fresh 60s should be focusing on while taking time to gear up for MC?

Does staff think MC should be clearable in a full raid of T0.5/ZG/AQ20 geared players?

Does staff think the current level of gears an rewards in MC/BWL/etc appropriately reflect the difficulty?

Should we expect more tuning like this in the future?  IE: revisiting raids like AQ40/Naxx/ZG/AQ20 and making them harder?

Does twow staff consider MC, BWL, and Ony to be in a good place from their perspective?  Do they think the current tuning reflects their vision of early raiding in WoW?

What problem is twow trying to address by making these changes?  What perceived issues are they trying to resolve?

~~~

The purpose of these questions isn't to needle twow staff and harass them by telling them all their changes are awful.  I think it's fair for us to want to understand the vision when it comes to these changes and the future of twow.  Philosophy on early raid design is a HUGE deal to a lot of players, and for many of us, knowing what the plans are will help us figure out if we want to continue investing more time into Turtle WoW.

If pumping up difficulty and numbers for older content is going to be a norm, and you want to cater to a more hardcore crowd, that's your prerogative; I won't tell you how to run your server and maybe there are merits to this I'm not seeing.  However, being verbose and clear with these intentions will better allow the playerbase to make an informed decision about where they want to play WoW.

Thanks for reading!
