# Revitalizing Low-Level Zones: Rare Dungeon Epics & Cross-Faction Sabotage Quests

## Post 168181 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-14T11:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168181#p168181 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello everyone,

I’ve been thinking about ways to make the leveling journey and the twink scene even more vibrant on Turtle WoW. While the current experience is great, I believe we could add some "prestige" incentives to low-level content to keep zones buzzing with players of all levels.

 1. Rare Epic Drops in Low-Level Dungeons
Right now, once you've out-leveled a dungeon, there’s little reason to go back. I suggest adding one unique Epic item per dungeon (from RFC/Deadmines up to SM/RFD) with a very low drop rate (1% to 5%).

The Goal: Create "chase items" that are highly desirable for Twinks and useful for levelers.

The Impact: This would encourage high-level players to help lower levels (or solo farm), keeping the dungeon queues active and the world feeling alive. It adds that "lottery" excitement every time you kill a boss.

 2. Cross-Faction "Sabotage" Missions (World PvP)
To spice up World PvP, we could introduce specific PvP quests that send players deep into "enemy" territory with meaningful objectives.

For the Alliance: Missions in The Barrens (e.g., sabotaging Horde supply lines or scouting Crossroads).

For the Horde: Missions in Westfall (e.g., disrupting Sentinel operations or inciting chaos at Sentinel Hill).

Why? These are iconic zones. Giving the opposite faction a reason to be there (other than just ganking) creates organic skirmishes and makes the world feel like a true war zone.

Why this fits Turtle WoW?
Turtle WoW is all about the journey, not just the endgame. By adding rare rewards to early dungeons and encouraging regional conflicts in iconic zones, we reinforce the "Vanilla+" feel where every zone matters.

I’d love to hear the community’s and the devs' thoughts on this!

Best regards,
Wyrdo

Almost definitionally a twink has a lvl 60 main character behind them. I would estimate that less than 5% of twinks would dungeon queue for rare items and spend 1.5 hours per run, when they could just bulldoze through the instance on their main in 12 minutes and do 5 IDs per hour.

Dungeon queues would not noticeably improve.

I think you have a good intention. I tend to agree that it would be nice if more people ran dungeons while levelling. Most people will continue to powerlevel, get boosted by their main or by a friend, solo farm gear for their twinks, etc. And I just don't see how you can incentivise them to do it differently when this is just the most efficient way (getting boosted by a 60 is quickest, being solo means no loot competition, etc). You would have to introduce a completely new very un-Vanilla system (like successful LFG runs awarding tokens you can then exchange for twink BiS items) to force people to interface with random dungeon runners, and even then they'd probably primarily try to find ways to cheese the system.
