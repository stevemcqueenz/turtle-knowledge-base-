# New PvP ranking system

## Post 39602 by Teky — 2023-03-29T21:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39602#p39602 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtles,

I have never played Vanilla before coming to Turtle WoW.

After reaching lvl 60 and doing some dungeons i set my eyes on the PvP side.

As there is no resilience in this version, so PvP set and items remain efficient in PvE.
As well as the PvE set and items can be strong in PvP.

 **#### HOW CURRENT PVP RANKING WORKS ####**

So I started my journey doing battlegrounds and side research on the PvP ranking system in Vanilla.

The info I gathered are :

Every PvP week, we are ranked in brackets based on our Honor obtained during the week.
Each bracket correspond to a certain amount of Contribution Points (CP) also called Rating Points (RP)

i.e :
bracket 14 (top 0,3%) => 13000 to 12000 CP
bracket 13 => 12000 to 11000 CP
etc.
So the max you can get each week is 13000 CP (by doing a loooot of bg).

PvP ranks are based on your Contribution Points :

Rank 1	At least 15 Honor Kills
Rank 2	2000 CP/RP
Rank 3	5000 CP/RP
Rank 4	10000 CP/RP
Rank 5	15000 CP/RP
Rank 6	20000  CP/RP
Rank 7	25000  CP/RP
Rank 8	30000  CP/RP
Rank 9	35000  CP/RP
Rank 10	40000  CP/RP
Rank 11	45000  CP/RP
Rank 12	50000  CP/RP
Rank 13	55000  CP/RP
Rank 14	60000  CP/RP

At first look we could think that after few weeks of PvP we could reach the top of the ranking ladder.

So this is time to introduce the decay of 20% per week.

Here is an example :
You have 40000 CP and this week you won 9000 CP based on your weekly bracket ranking
you'll have now (40000 x 0,8) + 9000 = 41000 CP
Your Contribution Points increased by 1000

But if your weekly contribution was 7000 CP
You would have (40000 x 0,8) + 7000 = 39000 CP
Your Contribution Points decreased by 1000 even if you did quite a few honor during the week.

Which means that if you don't have enough time in the week to get more honor points, you'll end up stuck in the ladder.
For example, if you get around 8000 CP each week, the best you can reach is rank 10 ( 40000 CP)
Because (40000 x 0,8) + 8000 = 40000 CP.

I did some calculation and for someone you reach this "Stuck point in about 10 to 12 weeks"

If you can't progress anymore in the ladder, what's the point on trying so hard in BG every week ?
The worst of it is if you dare to take a break and can't play for 2 weeks for example :
(40000 x 0,8) + 0 = 32000 CP
(32000 x 0,8) + 0 = 25600 CP
You end up loosing a lot of CP so hardly won.

In the end this system doesn't reward players with good skills (like arena will in next version) but players that can spend a very large amount of time doing PvP/BGs
(which is not something that everyone can).

 **#### MY SUGGESTION ON PVP RANKING ####**

Since the system is not rewarding skills, and that it would be hard to judge about people skills in world PvP or BGs.
My suggestion is to reward hard working, by mainly changing 2 things :

 1) It's no longer possible to loose CP/RP
 2) If you did a minimum of PvP during the week (5000/10000 honor ?) you'll get a minimal amount of additional CP/RP

Let me explain a little further.

My idea is for you to get the max between :
 - the standard vanilla Contribution Points system
 - a percent (for example 10%) of the Contribution Points you get this week (Percent gain)

Let's settle some examples :
Here are few examples :
 1) You have 40000 CP and this week you didn't play for some reason, so you get 0 CP on your weekly bracket ranking
Standard vanilla : (40000 x 0,8) + 0 = 32000 CP <=> -8000 CP
Percent gain : 40000 + (0 x 0,1) = 40000 CP

The best of the 2 is the Percent gain => you stay at 40000 CP
You are now protected and can take a break if you need it :)

 2) You have 40000 CP and this week you did less than usual in pvp, and you get 6000 CP on your weekly bracket ranking
Standard vanilla : (40000 x 0,8) + 6000 = 38000 CP <=> -2000 CP
Percent gain : 40000 + (6000 x 0,1) = 40600 CP <=> + 600CP

The best of the 2 is the Percent gain => You are now at 40600 CP
Even if you didn't get as much honor as your usual you still fought for your faction on the battlefield and deserve a little reward.

 3) You have 40000 CP and this week you did more than usual in pvp, and you get 12000 CP on your weekly bracket ranking
Standard vanilla : (40000 x 0,8) + 12000 = 44000 CP <=> +4000 CP
Percent gain : 40000 + (12000 x 0,1) = 41200 CP <=> +1200 CP

The best of the 2 is the Standard Vanilla => You are now at 44000 CP (gz!)

 **#### IMPACT IN THE RANKING  ####**

As I said it would reward the hard work, cause it would now be possible for everyone to reach the highest rank.
But it doesn't mean that it will be quick and easy.

Let's get a concret example:
Last week I spent most of my playtime doing BGs and was rewarded with about 9000 CP
If we apply my suggestion with a "Percent gain" of 10%
Considering that I keep on mainly doing BGs and get 9000 CP a week it will takes me 32 weeks (7 to 8 months) to reach the rank 14.
For someone who gets 7000 CP a week it will takes 52 weeks (a whole year) to reach rank 14.

In another hand as we take the Vanilla standard calculation when it's better, it won't slow the hardcore gamer that can still reach rank 14 in 3 months with 13000 CP a week.

It just allows softcore player to reach the heights in a very turtle (slow and steady) way.
It also give them a reason / a target in PvP that is actually reachable

 **#### LIMIT CASES / TO GO FURTHER ####**

 1) If it makes it too easy to go up with very low pvp activity we can set the condition of a minimum of weekly honor (let's say 10000 Honor) to benefits from the "Percent gain"

Example :
You have 40000 CP and this week you did less 10000 honor points weekly, and you get 4000 CP on your weekly bracket ranking
Standard vanilla : (40000 x 0,8) + 4000 = 36000 CP <=> -4000 CP
Percent gain applicable : 40000 + (6000 x 0,1) = 40600 CP <=> + 600CP
Percent gain not applicable : 40000 + (6000 x 0) = 40000 CP

As you did less than 10000 honor this week, the "Percent gain" is not applicable so you stay at 40000 CP

 2) Since we can no longer downgrade, why not set a seasonal competition for those who reached rank 14 already.
The season could take place for 1 to 3 months and the players with the higher score would win some exclusive content like mount weapon/armor skin ?
At the end of the season every player above 60000 CP are back to 60000 for the new season to start.

 **#### NOTES ####**

 1) The 10% I gave here is for example (even if I feel that it's not far from a good value) and you could change its value for adjustement purpose.
Same goes for the minimum of weekly honor that i did suggest in a range of 5000 to 10000

 2) If there is a way to updload it I have made an excel file that simulates the Standard Vanilla ranking and my suggestion

Thank you for reading me.
I truly hope that the time I spent writting this will be useful for the server and the players.
