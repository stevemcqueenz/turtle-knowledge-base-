As an effort to provide some structured feedback in response to some issues I faced with account management, I've decided to make a post.

I noticed when creating an account for TurtleWoW, in the email you get it does not include the **Forum username** you chose for your account. In addition to this, it also seems impossible to recover a forum account, since the forgot password function doesn't link anywhere.

So far from what I can understand, if a forum account's info is lost or forgotten, in my case I forgot it due to being inactive for close to a year or so now, it's gone. I've made loose comments in discord about this issue, and made mention of it throughout my time in the community but haven't noticed any changes.

I'm sure it would help a lot of people to make it easier to recover, and track their information.

That being said, cheers folks! I pray server stability comes back soon.   smiling_turtle_head
