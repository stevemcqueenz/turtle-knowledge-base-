# Balancing patch and the future, focus on druids.

## Post 167100 by Icedxylophone — 2026-02-01T00:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167100#p167100 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I've written similar things on the discord, so if you read it before, just pretend you haven't and add to the conversation. This will mostly be about druids, and balance druid specifically.

I've recently played around a lot with the sims for the potential patch to compare it with current, and it made me want to write this post.

I asked to get more talents Points, and 2 extra points from Boat was an example I used, so I'm happy with that. Being able to have both pushback protection and flexibility with our mana talents is a great start, but I don't understand why we would get a dmg nerf when our dmg is already among the lowest, and with this the lowest, and we have minor utility. Every other caster has had a build with functional mana, and pushback protection, so it was just a fix to make us more aligned with the other casters.

How the changes to BoaT will change our mana is something I'd need to test for a raid or two to see how I'd adjust, but 2 points in reflection or even Gale winds for naxx/aq40. I do expect an increased cost to wrath on trash/mobs that die quickly like in dungeons/raids, since we can't use the last kill target as our dot mob, or while leveling it will be worse, and on ST bosses it will be more reliable. With the 5 set, we almost had a free Wrath per proc, so it's going to be interesting to see the reality of the change.

I wanted the difference in power between nature and astral boon to be closer in value and make the nature version more interesting, but I didn't expect this. Making insect swarm buff wrath makes more sense than the previous version and has no issues with it, and is a clear buff against the retarded full-immune mobs/bosses. It did remove a RNG factor, but it didn't change the one we really wanted changed. The BoaT Rng was a minor thing that was brought up because of the eclipse low proc rate on Wrath, and it being annoying with multiple low proc rate functions with high impact, which just does not feel good or perform well, and I think most of us expected an increased proc rate or possible stacks. Either way, the most important change to make the boomkin gameplay feel less rng and sluggish is to change eclipse. I would like to see 35-40% as the Wrath proc rate if we use the same system, because I used to love wrath last patch when I had 39-42% with my full crit gear. I suppose other options could include a base % that then increases after each cast, so Wrath starts at 30 and then increases with 10% per cast or after a set amount of casts it's guaranteed. I also like the idea of giving eclipse more spice with empowering a spell.

I think with your idea for Starfire, it's best to have it at around 2.5 sec cast, currently it's at 2.6 with bis gear, and around 2.8-2.9 for the others without ebb and flow and the t3 haste gear.

Throwing out another thing that could be done with the dots to make them more viable for trash etc would be that rather like CoA you would do the opposite where the dots are front-heavy and reduce their dmg with each tick, or just buff the dots. I played around with the idea that dots would spread to 1 mob after the mob died either with the remaining dot timer or rank 1 or whatever, but might be annoying to do.

The rotation became easier, but also lost a min/max function that will be noticeable. An underrated thing is that the feel of Starfire will be drastically worse, and especially for those that have 5 set t3.5, but also the druids that don't have ebb an flow + t3, that will cast 2.8-2.9 starfires, which just ain't very nice, especially on fights that require movement. Wrath used to feel good, and starfire pretty lackluster, then it was the opposite, and I fear with this change it is possible that for some druids that lack certain items it will feel very bad with both.

Now I think it's important to go back to the start, as I said, I've been playing around with the sims to test the new changes compared to our current patch, and I wanted to test my eclipse band-aid of 5-10% increased proc on wrath. My tests came to these results: the new patch is a -23.6 dps, and that is in the best case scenario of not having multiple mobs to dot with IS or coming into the fight with boon stacks, which will be closer to between -40 and -45 dps. For trash it will be worse as well with carry on boons or trash with a dot target. In any case, this would make us the clear-cut worst dps, with the non-existent smite priest. It would get into the territory of not being worth getting a boomkin, so I question the nerf that was given and would ask for some more adjustments, because the last one wasn't nearly enough and in the right direction, though I appreciate the intent. An easy fix would be to boost eclipse dmg, the % of Moonfury again, but I would consider my band-aid fix as well, it would make wrath feel a bit smoother, and the 5% change would boost the dps 10-13 dps (with bis gear except Scythe, because only newly dinged boomkins have that item). It would be -14.4 dps lower with the new patch than the current (10% would be around -7.9 dps), but at least eclipse would feel a bit better until you reach a conclusion on how you want to do it. I personally think we should get a dps buff, together with the band-aid, but we will see if this is the last change.

I would also hope down the line they make changes to owlkin frenzy, because it's a joke of a talent, gaining mana to be hit and it has 3 points? My rework idea was to have it proc on dmg, you increase dmg taken, and you can reduce it with the amount of talent points taken. When procced you also deal a bit more dmg and either that's it or you add on the pushback protection, which makes a lot more sense with the name. It would be a double-edged sword with the increased dmg taken in pvp and pve, but it could be interesting. The other idea could be the opposite, and you take a bit less dmg with the pushback protection, and you keep it a pvp talent, but people would use it.

Sylvan Blessing needs a buff for the levelers and I think it's especially for the most common situations of not being able to actually have time for the effect outside dungeons. Gale Winds isn't a finished product either, and I kinda expect it will be changed when we stop using 3.5 where it will gain a bit of a dmg buff, but I think there are other options. We desperately need mana everywhere we can get it, but it's just not enough from Gale Winds and nobody cares about the attack speed reduction, it's just more threat and we don't want it.

I still find Imp Moonfire into vengeance a bit lackluster, and if you want to keep it connected to dots you can gain 1% crit per point when IS, is on the target. You can also go with; you gain 1% mana or whatever when you crit on a moonfired target if you want it only connected to Moonfire. Either way, you need to have a general crit talent into vengeance, it just makes sense.

Other changes that would be deeply appreciated is to be able to cast BR in form, we are mana starved, and it cost us so much to cast that shitty spell and then get back in form. We even have the god-awful 2.5 5-set, yet we can't cast it, doesn't make any sense.

Barkskin should not reduce cast time, it makes it barely worth using, and you end up stuck casting instant casts instead.

Resto druids has such a boring gameplay, and could really use a nourish/lifebloom or a wild growth-ish spell make it explode with healing after a few seconds/make it a hot/direct heal, whatever you want. Feral tanks and resto druids desperately need a new spell in their box of fun to use.

Tranquility is too strong, but with a 5-year investment plan after each time, and it's even silly strong for ferals and boomkins during the correct moments. I think some adjustments here could be made, but it is a difficult spell to balance around a full raid of players to heal, could potentially reduce it to the top 20 players who need it most/revamp it into the wild growth version you like if that's easier for you, or just nerf it a bit and change the timer.

I think we can improve the talent ladder for ferals to get to omen, so it doesn't feel useless to get there. My first thought was to either move and/or change nature's grasp from 5 points to just 1 and slot furor in there instead of the resto talent tree. It would require some balancing adjustments, but it would make a lot of sense to fix it eventually. Similarly to resto druids, feral tanks are in desperate need of a new spell to use in their rotation to make it a bit more interesting even if they stay at basically the same level.

 -Hotverdomme
