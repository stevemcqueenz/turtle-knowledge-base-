# Make Mount Purchases Account-wide

## Post 145431 by Xero373 — 2025-08-14T14:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145431#p145431 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it would be really awesome if mount purchases were made available account-wide. At least, for certain mounts that are only available for a limited time and/or are just really expensive.

I have a mage character on Nordanaar, who I purchased the Flames of the Firelands mount while it was available. Not only was it really expensive (in my opinion), but, the cost aside, is no longer available to purchase.

With the new Ambershire server rolling out, I plan on starting fresh, like many others. Again, it's not just a matter of the higher price tag compared to other purchasable mounts and items, in general, but the fact it's no longer available to purchase.

I just think it would be really awesome if mount purchases, at least some (limited release and/or more expensive one's), if not all, were made available account-wide. That way, those who chose to make those investments in their Turtle WoW accounts could enjoy them to the fullest.
