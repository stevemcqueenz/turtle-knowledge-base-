# The real PvP fix is this, and it's not easy

## Post 135600 by Keds (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-13T14:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135600#p135600 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ok so, the more feedback I see about PvP changes announcement, the more I come to the conclusion that you can't please everyone, and both of solutions proposed by the turtle team has it's own problems.

As you all know, the powercreep is real, and with CC2, many classes got even more damage, while some of them being even more bursty than they were previously.

So, with that in mind, this is my suggestion; **Damage stats squish and PvE health pool squish**

Yeah, that's right, reduce **EVERYONE'S** damage stats, from PvP and PvE items, and also reduce overall health from all PvE content.

The discrepancy from PvE healh pool content and players is what cause this problem, and the more end game progression content is added, the worse this problem will become.
