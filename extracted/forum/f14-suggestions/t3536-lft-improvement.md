## Post 21304 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-08-07T09:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21304#p21304 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Staff has no intention of ever introducing dungeon teleports. This detracts from a subtantial part of what made the original game world feel vast and immersive. The teleport of the RDF feature is cited as being responsible for the beginning of the game becoming more "hub-based".

If you wish to get into Ragefire Chasm as an Alliance, your options are to enlist a level 20+ Horde Warlock in Orgrimmar to summon you next to the portal where there are no hostile NPCs, or to corpse walk (significantly more tedious and less recommended).
