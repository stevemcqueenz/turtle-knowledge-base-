# Tier Sets for Offspecs

## Post 24025 by Sovietpremier — 2022-08-30T22:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24025#p24025 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the buff to offspecs I think it would be cool to have multiple versions of the tier sets suited for the different offspecs. Instead of increasing the loot table how about having the ability to turn in the tier set piece to an NPC who will then transform it into something suited for your spec? For example, I turn in the Paladin Tier 1 piece Lawbringer Chestguard to an NPC then they convert it to a version suited for Protection Paladins.

## Post 24039 by Phoenixphire (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-31T00:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24039#p24039 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I could not disagree more strongly with that suggestion but I totally love the overall idea. Not attacking you, I just think that it can be done in a way you and other players would get a lot more enjoyment out of. :D

What I would rather have is new quest lines to get a set of gear for offspecs as an example.

You meet a scarlet npc at Lights Hope chapel and he sends you on a dungeon set .5 style quest chain that gives you a unique armor set for protection paladins. It would send you into the raids, outdoor content, and dungeons so its obtained in similar content to tier sets just not as drops, and it doesn't take away tier set's uniqueness and doesn't start homogenizing all the sets for each class. It would also be an awesome way for offspecs to get a little more love with a unique quest line and get people out into the world and into less popular instances.

Overall I love the idea of tailored sets for offspecs due to tier sets being very specific, I just don't thing simply exchanging them at a vendor is very fun, immersive, or adds much to the game overall.

Thanks so much for brining up the topic though I think its definitely something most players of off specs would LOVE to see in some shape or form. :D

I am all in for alternative tier sets, set bonuses could fix existing problems for offspecs (lets say 5pcs enh set would turn your stormstrike debuff into a buff)
It should not be free yet it would require the original set piece as a token, similar to T 0.5 chain.
I would add epic quality non-soulbound "tokens" that can be traded to tier bosses ( scorching blood of onyxia, molten core of ragnaros, scale of the red drakes, belly of chromaggus...etc) and have them drop ~3-5/raid. These items would be part of the quest chain in addition to class specific stuff.
Lets have a BM hunter set for example in which Rexxar would require you first to unlock the turn-in quest by completing a chain ( take his enchanced crystal vial to UBRS and acquire the a fresh blood sample of the Beast, this would be similar to quel'serrar chain where you have a limited time to complete). This chain can even be extended with the method of getting the vial etc
After having the turn-ins unlocked you would then need the original set piece and 1 of the epic quality tokens (scorching blood of onyxia) for each item.

The devs attempted to somewhat address this by redistributing the spirit on the packaging tier sets to agility, and some other mods (such as the much needed hit rating on the AQ40 chest). While it's helpful for ret it doesn't quite complete the gear (mainly not enough mana5 and some useless set bonuses like the AQ40 2set) and leaves prot very much in the dirt.
While redesigning another tier set could be a solution another would be using talents to award defence rating from a common stat already on the gear, such as stamina or intellect.
