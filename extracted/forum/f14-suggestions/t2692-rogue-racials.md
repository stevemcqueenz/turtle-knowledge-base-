# Rogue racials

## Post 16924 by Krusi — 2022-02-10T11:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16924#p16924 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey guys, I have seen some class racials on the forums and decided to make some for the rogue. This is just ideas and me having fun. Hope you like it and maybe the DEVs can look into class racials more :)

Orc: My father's/mother's axe. Passive: lvl 20 allows the rogue to use axes. (Same rules for swords/maces/fist wep concerning abilities.)

Troll: Your shoot skills are replaced with Spirit shoot: lvl 20. Deals 50-100% extra ranged wep damage as shadow DPS. The extra DPS cannot be resisted.

Undead: Odor of death: passive: lvl 20 Stealth -1 lvl due to rotten smell. Active: Throw down a vial of insane bad smell. Deal nature DPS in a cone 180° front of you. 5 yards. Dps upgrades per lvl. CD 2 min.

Goblin: Jetpack: Active: lvl 20. Shoots you up in the air, dealing fire DPS around you. You land randomly around you 360°, up to 50 yards away. Taking 10% fall DPS. 10 min CD

Human: Defias influence: passive: lvl 20. you gain 10% more money from pickpocketing. After you ambush or cheap shot. You get 1-2% extra DPS on your next 2 abilities. (Might be overpowered in pvp)

Night Elf: Elunes Coating: lvl 20. Applies a Oil with arcane affinity. 25% chance to deal arcane DPS on hit and gives the enemy a dot that lower it's resistance to arcane magic by 20, stacks 3 times. Works the same as applying poison to your wep.

Dwarf: My liver is fine: passive: lvl 20 gives you -1 lvl to stealth due to odor.
You get double effect from beer and dwarven food buffs (also might be overpowered)

Gnome: Adventurous: Passive: lvl 20 deals extra 1% DPS to Giants. Active: Your jolly spirit empowers the party for 1-2 min giving them +2 to all stats. 30 min CD

High Elf: Mana Hungry: Passive: lvl 20 -10 spirit. Requires daggers. Active: Strike the enemy of 25% wep DPS, if enemy has mana bar you steals 5% of their mana pool to renew your HP over the next 10 sec of the mana stolen. 5 min CD. (Overpowered in pvp maybe)
