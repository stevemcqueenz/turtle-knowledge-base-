# Other legendaries for Shamans

## Post 53252 by Yuta — 2023-07-13T05:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53252#p53252 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a Shaman, i'm always inclined to build Enhancement thinking about Sulfuras. The lack of legendaries for Resto/Elemental is clearly a let down and creating it might be good for balancing purposes.

The creation of a legendary item for Elemental can even balance the pvp side of it. "Nerf" his damage but the most powerful version of the spec is with the said legendary weapon.

If the "creation" part is hard, make Atiesh able to be used by them. For Enhancement tanks, make a quest chain to transform Thunderfury into a dagger (the only class that would tank and synergize with the nature resistance aspect of the weapon). It could even solve the aggro problem of the class, having a BIS item like this.

The only spec that have this problem that i can think of is Holy Paladins. They, too, don't have legendaries to pursue and i honestly can't think of way different than *creating* one for them
