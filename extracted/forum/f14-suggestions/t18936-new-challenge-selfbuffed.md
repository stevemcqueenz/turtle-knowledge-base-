# New challenge - selfbuffed

## Post 128828 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-28T13:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128828#p128828 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, worldbuffs are a great boost to stats at early levels.
While it is possible to level to 60 without them, I think that players who actually do this deserve some recognition.
Maybe tabard, maybe title.
Something that shows that this player took some challenge and completed it without additional help.

Proposal is - when player pick that challenge, then any buff lasting longer than minute applied not by the player, should be automatically canceled.
It still allow minor interactions like druid healing or shields of priests.

I wouldn't call it a great boost to stats, but a boost. Just like any other buff, for example paladin's kings. People do tend to say it makes the journey a LOT easier but that is debatable.
There is the time wasted to actually get to the place where the world buff drops (Stormwind/Orgrimmar) or the time wasted for another buff to drop (sometimes you have to wait an hour or two to get the buff) during which you would probably gain more than the buff. At least that is my view of it.

Putting that aside, I like the mentioned idea of a challenge like that.
In addition, if possible, to not be able to receive any healing from other players unless in a party of course. If there is any paladin aura or an imp with buff, that player wouldn't be affected.

Another option is to completely shut off everything so the player cannot even party with someone. That way it would be a single player journey all the way to 60 with only self casted buffs as well as not being able to use any kind of item enchant or Alchemy potions except maybe HP/Mana potions.
This is going to be some kind of a mixture of Vagrant and Artisan or the "child" of those two because you would be able to wear a set of armor made by another player.
