![Image](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/c09e0d_8cd65ead6c344eb7ba10c5de82587946~mv2.jpg)

My suggestion is to add additional bank bag slots for turtle coin. As the server matures and more of the long time players start collecting their 3rd, 4th, 5th tier sets, exotic weaponry, and save me for later transmog items it would be awesome if there was a place to store them all! I think the slots should be decently pricey to discourage buying these instead of buying large bags. Maybe 50%-75% of the price of a 28 slot bag? The fundraising potential on these is amazing because many long time players would not only buy the slot but then the portable black hole to fill it!! I know I would start upgrading one monthly!   satisfied_turtle   turtle_in_love
