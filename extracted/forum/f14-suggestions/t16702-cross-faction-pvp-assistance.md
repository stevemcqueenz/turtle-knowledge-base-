Hey guys, new here, just saw some people complaining in chat about people cross-faction healing during PvP to basically give themselves an invincible healbot.

Healing a player of the opposite faction who is PvP flagged should hit you with a debuff that  flags you to ALL players for several minutes. This way, you solve the issue of cross-faction PvP healing and don't need to make any permanent changes to the system overall.
