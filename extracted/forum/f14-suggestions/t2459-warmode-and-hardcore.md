# Warmode and Hardcore

## Post 15313 by Feroli — 2022-01-04T21:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15313#p15313 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey there, so I’d like to begin this post by stressing one major thing. In the past times this issue had been brought up, many people misconstrued the argument as “HC players are taking the warmode glyph and getting mad when they get ganked.”
That is NOT what this post is about. I am firm on the stance that if you take the glyph, buff a flagged player, do a PvP flagged quest or attack opposite faction guards then that is 100% your own fault.

The issue in question is more in regards to the side of the HC player base who actually do avoid these things, but are still at risk of unwanted flagging. I originally came from the Hardcore Community set up by Kargoz and had witnessed a lot of PvP related griefing on the Blizzard servers, due to players Mind Controlling mobs and sitting on Flight Paths and important NPCS. It’s great that those offences are bannable here, but the argument of “just don’t do X as HC” is starting to get narrower and narrower. I feel like it may go from “just dont go to the arena as a HC” to “don’t quest in any neutral zones as HC”.

I’ve thought over what could be a compromise to this that would keep both sides happy (or maybe civil for lack of a better word). Would it be an idea to give you two options for HC. Either take warmode at level 1, then the HC glyph and be perma flagged, or don’t and then be locked out of PvP until you hit 60. My thought is, you can still be flagged from things like buffing an ally player, attacking guards etc, but all things that are a conscious choice on the players end. This would only mean that you could not attack a flagged player. It prevents accidental flagging and hightens punishment for anyone who flags themselves.

People may not be happy with this, but it’s the closest thing I could really come up with that covers both arguments of “if you flag yourself it’s your own fault” and “HC players can be easily griefed”. This doesn’t account for the recently discovered Mind Control problem, but it would at least be something safer for anyone who doesn’t want to engage in PvP.

I appreciate if you took the time to hear me out. Please keep in mind this has nothing to do with people who willingly take warmode. This is only something to deal with those of us who don’t have any interest in PvP.
