 **PvP**
I love PvP, especially at low levels when it's possible to pass by skill as a low level player (lvl 27 vs 29 for example).
Unfortunately, PvP is dead in this area in Turtle Wow.
But it can be changed and I have summarized the following ideas:

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/N3SBqbP/photo-2022-12-06-18-56-27.jpg)

[viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4220](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4220)

 **Battleground [Thorn Gorge]**
 - 15vs15 Battleground
 - like "Eye of the Strom"
 - Tauren VS Dwarf
 - get reputation by Tauren (Tribe of Balance) or Dwarf (Miners of Bael Modan)
 - lvl 19/39/55/60 Custom PvP Items like Armor, Wappons and Flasks
 - low-level quests for XP and reputation

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/qCPDGJt/photo-2022-12-06-23-00-31.jpg)

 **Battleground [Searoad to Southshore]**
 - 5vs5 Battleground
 - Allianceship vs Undeadship
 - in the hull spawns a flag on both sides, one player catch the flag and takes it to the roof
 - the team who gets 5x the flag wins the match

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/C0NbKSj/photo-2022-12-06-18-56-30.jpg)

[viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4922](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4922)

 **New PvP Reputation System**
 - wins and loses gives more reputation
 - there will be merchants in every staring point in the bg to get buff-food and flasks (no gear) from the specific faction
 - PvP gear from any bg will be classified in 4 classes: low / mid / high / high-end
 - low gear (level 19-29) will be enabled with the rep "friendly"
 - mid gear (level 30-49) will be enabled with the rep "honored"
 - high gear (level 50-59) will be enabled with the rep "revered"
 - high-end (60 epix) will be enabled with he rep "exalted"
 - new "mark of honor" quests
 - low quest: 1 mark = 100 rep
 - mid quest: 3 marks = 100 rep and a random green item
 - high quest: 9 marks = 250 rep and a random blue pvp item
 - revered to exalted will be much much more difficulty (for endgame fun)
 - neutral to revered will be normal (so much faster then before)
 - unic quests for example in warsong: horde "kill 5 night elfs" or alliance "kill 5 orcs" for extra marks, XP and Items

 **PvE/RP**
I have the great honor of creating my own area in southern Tanaris as a beginner project and I have permission to share my ideas with you to create a fantastic new area.

It's about an island chain with a trading post of a relatively unknown goblin population. The inhabitants of "Porto Gatza" live mainly from the black market trade of the pirate fleets and the extraction of crude oil and gunpowder. When a free-lance goblin engineer built a zeppelin and headed towards Gadgetzan, he was shot down by South Sea pirates.

Help him repair his zeppelin and free the islands from pirate tyranny.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/PxnY103/photo-2022-12-06-18-56-33-2.jpg)

 **Dungeon 55-60[Liberation of Porto Gatza]**
 - Entry in the east of Gadgetzan on the Zeppelin Station
 - PvE Quests to allow to go on the Zeppelin for the dungeon entry (lvl 55)
 - dungeon starts on a zeppelin in Proto Gatza
 - kill pirates and liberty the City (lvl 55+)
 - Captn Noe Beard as optional Boss
(lvl 62+)
 - clear the dungeon to get the flightpoint to travel to the southsee archipelago
