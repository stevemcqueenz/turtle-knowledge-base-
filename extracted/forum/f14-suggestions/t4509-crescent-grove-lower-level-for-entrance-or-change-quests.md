# Crescent Grove - Lower Level for Entrance or Change Quests

## Post 27025 by Ashkir — 2022-10-09T05:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27025#p27025 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Crescent Grove requires you to be level 32 to even enter the instance. I couldn't enter it as a level 30. You get the quests to *go* to the dungeon at level 30.

Scarlet Monastery: Cathedral for example lets you enter it at level 21. Feels a bit off balance.

I suggest one of the following;
 1  - Increase the level to get Crescent Grove quests to 32
or
 2  - Lower entrance level to 30, like Scarlet Monastery, but, not LFT queue.
