PvP as a Viable Leveling Path

Hi team, loving the time on turtle so far!
I’d like to propose a couple of ideas to help make PvP a truly viable alternative to PvE leveling, which would open up exciting new ways to play and fit the server’s vision of flexible, custom progression.

 **0. Provide XP Gain for PvP Objectives**
When encountering PvP, either in the Open World or Battlegrounds, use the existing XP formulare for PvE encounters and spread it on the size of the players in range. This would benefit everyone participating equally.
Additionally a BG Win/Lose should yield some XP as a reward. Either direct, or throuhg repetetive Token/Mark turn ins.

 **1. Normalize Base Stats Within Brackets**
When queuing for battlegrounds, players should have their base stats scaled to the max level of their bracket (e.g. level 19 for 10–19, level 29 for 20–29, etc.). In consequence also creating a same chance % to hit x9 players as if they were x9 themselves.

This helps reduce the huge power gap between low- and high-level players in the same bracket.

Max-level players will still retain advantages via talents and gear, but this change allows more meaningful participation for everyone in the bracket.

Additional note for concerned Twinks: As only the Base Character Stats would be upscaled. You would still retain a massive advantage over the "regular bypassing"-pvp lvler. You will have more HP, through gear and enchants. You will have more DMG through gear and enchants. You will have more Avoidance, Mana... I think you get it.

It encourages more people to queue and makes BGs more balanced and fun without fully homogenizing performance.

 **2. PvP Rewards to Support Progression**
Right now, PvP offers no gold, gear, or consumables, making it unsustainable as a leveling path. A small incentive system would go a long way:

Add small end-of-match rewards (random chest, PvP tokens, or consumable bundles).

Possible rewards could include:

- Basic consumables (food, bandages, scrolls)

- Low-level green (chance for blue) gear appropriate for the player’s level

- Small amounts of silver/gold
This mirrors how PvE gives loot and gold from mobs/quests and helps fund spell training and gear upgrades.

I think these changes would help balance PvP progression without breaking immersion or giving unfair advantage—and would make leveling via battlegrounds a genuinely enjoyable path.

Thanks for considering!

I would love for PvP to be a viable leveling path, even if questing would still be faster due to the plethora of custom quests added to the game. The most contentious part -- if the discussion in #pvp-general is anything to go by -- is the normalization of base stats.

I would argue that increasing *base stats* of x0-x8 to that of x9 is more of a benefit than a detriment. It would provide the extra nudge for an x0 to queue, feeling less like dead weight, thereby increasing engagement across all brackets. The x0 would still feel the lack of talent points, lower spell ranks, and lesser gear, but they can at least hit their targets, contribute to the battleground, and be rewarded for their efforts (thus, being a *viable* leveling path).

This normalization of base stats worked wonderfully in Warhammer: Age of Reckoning, where lower tiers were always very active.
