The Eranikus storyline continues and concludes as a part of the Shifting Sands questline (aka the long questline to open AQ).  Spoiler for those who haven't done it and might not want to have it spoiled for them I suppose.
You wind up helping Keeper Remulos purge Eranikus of the corruption of the Old Gods.  This culminates in Eranikus appearing in Moonglade and you and your raid group having to face off against him.  This ends with Eranikus being freed of the corruption and Malfurion gaining a powerful ally in Eranikus.

There are rewards for completing this part of the questline.  Unfortunately, because the questline is tied to the Shifting Sands quests, not many players will get to experience it.
