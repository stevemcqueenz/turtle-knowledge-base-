# Please enable the Scythe of Elune questline for Shaman and Paladin players

## Post 138302 by noth532 — 2025-07-07T01:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138302#p138302 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Karazhan raid is a milestone for Turtle WoW's raid content. It is exceptionally well-designed, and players thoroughly enjoy the dungeon experience. Furthermore, the Scythe of Elune, as Karazhan's legendary artifact, features a perfectly crafted questline.

Elemental Shamans are spellcasters who worship the elements of wind, fire, and lightning, dealing massive elemental damage. Paladins are spellcasters devoted to the Holy Light; whether specced for Retribution or Protection, they rely heavily on dealing significant spell damage. The Scythe of Elune perfectly complements the playstyles of both these classes. Since demon worshippers (Warlocks) and shadow priests are already deemed worthy of Elune's favor through this artifact, why shouldn't Stormcallers (Shamans) and Lightbringers (Paladins) be granted the same opportunity?  wary_turtle

Therefore, I request that Shaman and Paladin players be allowed to embark on the Scythe of Elune quest journey, enabling more players to participate in this legendary quest chain. If concerns exist that this artifact might make these classes overpowered, please consider appropriately reducing its proc chance or damage values. However, please do not deprive us, the passionate Turtle WoW community, of the opportunity to experience this content. Thank you for your consideration.
