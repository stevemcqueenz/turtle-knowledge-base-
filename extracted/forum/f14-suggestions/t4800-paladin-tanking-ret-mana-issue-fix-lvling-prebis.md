# Paladin Tanking / Ret Mana Issue Fix (Lvling -> PreBIS)

## Post 28693 by Unchallenged (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-07T22:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28693#p28693 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, What's Up Devs & Readers,

I'm looking at suggesting a few tweaks that will optimize Protection and Retribution lvling process -> early end-game dungeons mana recovery.

As it stands now after the recent removal of +hitdmg no longer triggering Judgement of Wisdom Prot/Ret paladins are back to going out of mana within 2-3 pulls and slow levelling, dungeon/raid progression,

Please take a min to read and consider these options as potential fixed for mana issues.

======== ---- Protection ---- ========

★ Talent: Improved Righteous Fury 3/3
Increase the threat generated by your Righteous Fury spell by 16/33/50%. In addition, while under the effect of Righteous Fury gives the Paladin mana when healed by other friendly targets' spells.  The amount of mana gained is equal to 3/6/9% of the amount healed.

======== ---- Retribution ---- ========

★ Rank & Threat Update: Seal of Command
Gives the Paladin a chance to deal additional Holy damage equal to X% of normal weapon damage and reduces the amount of threat generated by 30% unless under the effect of Righteous Fury. Only one Seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time. Lasts 30 sec.
Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy, instantly causing X to X Holy damage, X to X if the target is stunned or incapacitated.

R1: 70% of normal weapon damage
R2: 72.5% of normal weapon damage
R3: 75% of normal weapon damage
R4: 77.5% of normal weapon damage
R5: 80% of normal weapon damage

 1. Weapon Damage R1-R5 remains flat at 70%, this change encourages Ret Paladins to Rank Up Seal of Command(SoC).
 2. The threat reduction by 30% would again incentivize Ret Paladins to choose SoC over Seal of Righteous(SoR).
 3. This would tie in well with the suggested *Sanctified Command as R5 SoC would be incentivized."
 4. Judgement damage does not need to be adjusted, as that is already scaled with rank.

★ Talent Update: Sanctified Command 2/2
Sanctified Command: Judgement of Command will restore mana equal to 45/90% of the base mana cost of the Seal of Command to you. Requires Seal of Command as a prerequisite talent and Gives your Seal of Command a 45/90% chance to resist dispel effects

or

★ Talent Update: Sanctified Command 2/2
Sanctified Command: Judgements will restore mana equal to 45/90% of the base mana cost of the Seal to you. Requires Seal of Command as a prerequisite talent and Gives your Seal of Command a 45/90% chance to resist dispel effects

 1. While levelling Ret Paladin, they are rarely able to take advantage of (Blessing of Might) and must use (Blessing of Wisdom) to maintain combat speed.
 2. I levelled Ret to test these changes + received a ton of feedback that Paladins don't use Seal of Command >R1+ outside of PvP (short-term fights) as using the highest available Seal of Command chugs through mana making any rank above 1 of SoC seem pointless to use.
 3. End Game Paladins are NOT placed into Mana group during raids and are often paired with warriors/rogues with Wind Fury Making the mana for the group useless.
 4. While raiding the bread and butter Seal of Command / Sanctified Command are not used as RET, and Judgement is rarely seen in dungeon groups and even raiding - the (RET) seal is essentially dead.
 3. Making this 45%/90% to encourage Ret Paladins to use;
a. Highest Rank Avail of Seal of Command.
b. Bigger use on [Improved Judgement] and To Use Judgement on Cooldown.
c. Have better mana efficiency to use more offensive abilities off cooldown.
d. This ties in changes to the improved SoC Rankings

======== ---- These Changes Would ---- ========

+ Prot paladins now get mana regen when healed (compensate for mana +dmg hit loss).

+ SoC now scales per rank with weapon damage encouraging paladins to use of higher ranks.

+ Highest rank seals are ideal to use, as it would return the greatest amount of mana back per judgement regardless of (SoR, SoC, Etc).

+ Encourage paladins to prioritize judgement > other attacks for consistent mana return.

+ From LV 32 2/2 Sanctified would help to level ret 32+ mana regen and fight uptime -> to early raid without the need to have the blessing of wisdom as the primary blessing all the time.

Per questions in discord;

Paladin healers wouldn't exploit, as you'd only get 90% SEAL mana back of the original cost.

Meaning if holy tried to do this you'd;
 1. Waste time in melee range.
 2. Casting Seal would still lose10% of the mana of the original cost.
 3. Seal = GCD, Judging = GCD

There would be no benefit to trying to judge as your losing out on mana at the very end.
