# Dedicated Custom Quest/NPC/Item Forum Section

## Post 12171 by Leozan — 2021-09-13T12:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12171#p12171 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title states there should be a front and center view of where visitors/players can view/post their custom content.

This feature is definitely TurtleWow’s best competitive edge against other servers imo. Unfortunately all the examples are scattered away in separate forum posts.

Please create a dedicated Custom Content tab in either the “Community” or “Gameplay” subsections. Once created also move the guides, tutorials, and other posts created by players and devs.

It might also help to have one of the devs create separate green posts for Custom Items, Custom Quest, and Custom NPC’s so players will naturally submit their work in one of those 3 posts.

Considering how toxic private server communities can get, nothing would say how great our community is by giving more spotlight to active player participation.

Heck even have competitions sometimes with prizes to fill out Wow’s  empty content sections; get even more people on board. It’s all ammo against other servers since barely any have this feature!  insidious_turtle
