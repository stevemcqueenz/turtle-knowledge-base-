# Add resilience buff instead of current dmg/heal nerfs

## Post 147546 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-21T18:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147546#p147546 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

From the latest patch:

> Beginning with Patch 1.18.0:
> - All incoming Physical Damage from other Players is reduced by 15%.
>
> - All incoming Magical Damage from other Players is reduced by 20%.
>
> - While in PvP combat (in both Open World and Battlegrounds/Arenas) all incoming Player Healing is reduced by 20% and all incoming Player Absorb Shields are reduced by 15%.
>
> - While in an Arena or Battleground all Mana Costs are reduced by 20%.

The fundamental problem with this "solution" is that it essentially gives players two damage profiles - one we know, and one we have to guess (or calculate every encounter if you are so inclined).This is not a good solution.

I suggest instead of these buffs you give all players in BG a resilience buff. For TBC it was:

> Resilience reduces your chance to receive a critical strike from an enemy, and reduces the damage you receive from critical strikes when they do occur.
>
>  At level 70, 39.4 Resilience Rating reduces your chance to be Crit by 1%, and reduces your damage taken from Critical Strikes by 2%

Resilience typically maxed at 350-380, which gave a ca 8-10% reduced crit chance and reduced crit damage by 16-20%. A value of 7-8% reduced crit chance and 15% reduced crit damage could go a long way to mitigate the CC2 burst meta.

I think this is a better, more balanced approach compared to the current one which is not well adjusted for all classes. Some classes will lose and benefit from Resilience too, but the current twow solution is worse.

A BG buff will also solve the issue of pvp itemization - no need to address the debate of putting resilience stats on gear.

Bottom line however, these solutions are bandaids that do not solve the fundamental balance issues of CC2 and for some classes the T3 powercreep.

Replacing plain -damage buff with -crit buff is kinda lame.
Reason - game has a lot of skills, which require you to (get) crit first. For example Enrage, Flurry, Deep Wounds, Sausage shot, Eye of the Storm, Ignite. So if you just reduce everyones chance to crit, then lots of talents will work less often.
In my opinion, those reactive talents are fun to use. There is no need to pessimize crit-dependent builds.

Resilence on gear was supposed to make high geared player to receive less those crit-based combinations. If you add resilience to everyone, you totally miss its point.
