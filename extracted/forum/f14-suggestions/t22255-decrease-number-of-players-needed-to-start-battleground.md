## Post 154462 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-05T14:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154462#p154462 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

PvP playerbase is a bit fragmented right now across 3 servers: Nordanaar, Tel'Abim and Ambershire.
I mainly speak in name of Nordanaar but I suppose the problem is even worse on 2 other servers.
Horde sometimes waits over 2 hours for specific battleground. Alterac Valley is the worst. On non-AV days there are often hours of waiting in between 2 AV games.

I suggest decreasing numbers of players needed to start and maintain single battleground temporarily until PvP playerbase grows (maybe with promised 1.18.1 PvP changes situation will be better).

I don't know the numbers for WSG and AB but currently 15vs15 is needed to maintain Alterac Valley game. 12vs12 would be ok.
Sunnyglade Valley is even more dead with 6vs6 needed to keep the game active. At this point even 4vs4 would be better than current state of this BG.
