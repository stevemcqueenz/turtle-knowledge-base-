# Let people choose which ZG enchant they want.

## Post 75334 by Blackduck (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-01T21:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75334#p75334 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is nothing class specific about the ZG head/leg enchants they only buff things like spell hit, hit, spell crit, spell damage, stamina, attack power, mp5, defense, etc. So why not let people choose which ZG enchant they want instead of restricting it to their class?  Why should a feral druid be restricted to an enchant that just improves healing? They might prefer a warriors or rogue's enchant. The same for a shadow or holy dps priest, they might prefer a mage for pve or even a warlocks enchant if they prefer PVP.

Actually, I think this is good idea because it fits the idea of "tier for every spec".
Each class can get 2-3 options matching different playstyles. It doesn't have to be full freedom of choice.
For example warriors could get choice between current [Presence of Might](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19782) (10 Stamina, 7 Defense, and 15 Shield Block value to a leg or head slot item) and (+14 Strength and 1% crit).
