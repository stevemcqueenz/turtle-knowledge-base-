# Alterac Valley - Shredders Ruined.

## Post 120051 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-02-15T14:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120051#p120051 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A few months ago, Turtle introduced a new weekly change that made items with a duration 'expire' while offline.

Originally the duration only ticked down during your logged on hours, obviously this was done to stop people hoarding the Valentines event buffs

However this had side effects which effected the Shredder item you get in Alterac Valley.
The mats are very expensive, if you only get 3 days of use out of this Shredder now, as it had a 40 min cooldown.

<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17384>

Before I could farm my Shredder, log off for a week , and id still have 3 days to use him in AV.

Now hes gone even if ive logged off.

The solution to this would be to either:

 1: Increase duration of Shredder to 14 days
 2: Increase the cost of the mats, and make the Shredder Permanent.

Please consider this, I used to love using the Shredder in AV, now its too expensive to do with the expiring duration offline , as a PvPer I cant afford this.
