# Compilation fo suggestions: Skills, PVP, Realm, Classes and more!

## Post 43478 by Heimdallr (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-27T18:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43478#p43478 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi All,

I've dropped some suggestions here and there but I thought it would be good to have them in a single post.
I will put my ideas for best changes I got, and then my "wet dream" which would be some out of any boundaries ideas, just because why not? they might inspire something as well.

 **Please note:**  if something sounds weird here, like too OP, I do consider my ideas a "turtle way" ideas, meaning that I keep in mid that even if it would be implemented, Devs would simply reshape it to fit the server, or reshape server to fit changes, or any other direction, simply to make game experience not suffer, but benefit from it in a turtle fiendly way.
 **PVP**
We got many people including me, that want  to have better world pvp, which by far is the best we can have out there from pvp options. It is not fun to get ganked in open world pvp and just be totally owned isn't it? well, how about some changes there? - I want to also point out how many people came before and left due to world pvp being just limited, and that now and later, people will also leave due to that, one idea would be to just make a pvp realm, or:

Be the first server who actually picked that topic and made epic changes to encourage people to do open world pvp.
It is absolutely unfair that people can do twinks with totally OP enchants and gear they got from their main characters, because this also discourages others from tagging to pvp and actively works in reduction of positive attitude towards world pvp, so I would advise to cut world pvp to brackets:

 10-19, 20-29 etc and make these use ONLY custom enchants/active items like pots, food etc, related to their bracket, so for example you could have a custom enchant granting +1 fire dmg on 10-19, or +1 nature, frost, dark, so you can not only pick your element but it also enchances your character concept. Imagine being that warrior that makes a concept under arcane style of warrior - A high elf, with 2 shiny weapons enchanted with Arcane magic glow and some Tmogged items for that, cool right?

This idea also makes bigger balance, not only options to pick from, because people would decide - oh yea, I want to play a 30-39, because I can get these neat talents, that skill, this custom sword/mace/axe from there and there - people start to utilize builds and PVE/PVP/Craft items more  because they think how to customize their characters, utilize items found throughout the world and makes people try to farm items like leather head with 0,5% drop from CG and encourages to create even more items(options) for them in the future

Also, it should  prevent people out of their range to PVP in zones meant for that range, that's right - It's important to NOT allow high levels to bully lower levels.

Then, do some sort of small camps in few zones, that would have PVP items, bank, something like Warhammer had in mmo, called War camps. People would be able to buy new pvp items for their levels and so on, that serves as tactical spot, place to take pvp quests, trigger pvp, sell/buy stuff, RP and more, kinda like small guild houses

From today's exploration - Burning Steppes looks like perfect, full blown conflict zone. It is just marked with wars, orcs, dwarves, dragons and more. It looks brilliant fora full warzone. Recapturing points like Statue of Lothar, patrolling and protecting land and tactical buildings that could be set there - Amazing to have such. For rewards for pvp in this zone we could get some custom looking/working Blackrock Clan items, on ally side some 2nd war resembling items.

Wet Dream:
Some sort of PVP Hubs as mentioned above, but with some lore behind them, like Ashenvale guardians, some sort of shaman covenants(for example azshara could be water thematic place for shammies etc) - simply put, hubs for pvp would belong to some sort of faction taken from lore/created

Wet Dream 2 is to make some sort of world impact done via world pvp, but that is a HIGH amount of work required: <https://wiki.returnofreckoning.com/Open_RvR_Guide>  <-- another game but well made, would have to translate it to WoW
 **Skills**
I do feel that we could have more skills and some sort of separation so we could develop concepts in various ways.
I run an Arcane mage for example and If I pick to play it fully under arcane source, just like druid for example, I got just missiles, so my whole fight is 1-1-1-1-1. If you play a bala druid under arcane, that looks pretty much the same(not sure if moonfire is worth using)

It would be absolutely cool if we could have more skills on characters that would allow us to focus and build our character theme in certain direction. For example Arcane mage. What would it require more, one, two spells? We could have some earth shamans, Fire/Shadow warlocks (just if these two dmg sources can't be effectively separated as in druid example)

All in all, it would require some new skills + gear that would allow people to build under certain sources without being one spell-lowdmg memes. Some people would like to be nature druids, others arcane concept ones. Many builds could be enchanced by it for both PVP and PVE. Some profession based items do boost things, but only few sources like fire dmg and so on...this could also be improved

It could either be using skills from other expansions or some custom ones like arcane spheres, some arcane circle to boost spell dmg, stone wall, whatever would fit the world theme to be honest.

Wet Dream  So new skills would defo be a thing, but how about enough of them or talent reworks, to make them open some rotation ways? I mean, I don't want to end up in rotation style of expansions, where your game was basically a Dance Dance Revolution style of game where you had to push buttons in good order to a point, where you had to make whole UI to show you CLICK ME now - that's killing the purpose of the game, but having it just slightly would be cool.

Wet Dream 2  - Imagine a mage with the floating spheres around, like Kaelthas had, but in Arcane theme, and that wold be a spell like in Dark Souls, they would shoot at close target automatically...or that you could put an spell into them to trigger it off global CD (for example 3 spells)
 **Store**
Some servers (one particularly known) has grand amount of cosmetics to buy - From astral forms, through spheres floating around of I think every source, to set looks and more. I feel we totally lack this here, we got few choices, and for example Astral form, doesn't really look astral, more like a fading out shadow priest.

We should bring more items - spheres, proper looking astral form, set parts, perhaps even spell graphics?
 ****Hardcore****
How about a crypt guild house?^^ fun thematic little stuff.
I've seen some people getting help from normies. I think we should have a mechanic that if a HC tags a mob, every normie has 100% miss chance on that mob.
Also, why casters got no sets from rep?

 60+ Hardcore - I was wondering here, how hard would it be to recreate half of guild or whole guild when a wipe in a raid occurs. Could we get additional features ONLY for 60+HCs that are either crafted or bought, that for example prevents from dying?

We need gold sink on HC and having let's say a phylactery, produced via tons of gold and some crafts from alchemy + enchanting, could simply give us a way to survive, slow down for week/weeks, reproduce that chance if we can and risk less - I get it, it's HC, but keep in mind that Classic  was unforgiving on it's own.
 ****Team & Development****
I made this point becase I see that tons of people got lots of hope in this server, and including me, we want it to flourish. This requires bigger team to process development faster, so I just wanted to point out that this is also a thing to consider if not done yet
 ****Professions****
As I've stated briefly above, there are only certain items to boost someone.
I feel we lack daggers for rogues, we lack generally usable items or the ones that boost sources like Arcane, frost etc. Would be nice to be able to craft items that would boost our concepts bit, right?

I also feel weird with fact that Engi is considered top DPS spec...can we make other specs bit better? I am playing a pally and I totally went Armorsmith as nothing in weaponsmith (at least swords) didn't catch my eye and made it worth picking. Also saw multiple situations where when a bala/shammy asked for best prof for him (except for engi ofc) the proposition was Tailoring for spell damage instead of LW...shouldn't we also add sthing to LW so people would be able to choose from?

And last but not the least...What do you think about adding Inscription ?
Not with glyphs to skills, but generally that could be also made, just different style than WOTLK did it.
I feel that it would be absolutely fantastic to be a class that produces scrolls for others, some head/shoulder enchants, offhands, perhaps other types of enchants on arcane related items? like staves, wands...something not too big but usable and unique.
