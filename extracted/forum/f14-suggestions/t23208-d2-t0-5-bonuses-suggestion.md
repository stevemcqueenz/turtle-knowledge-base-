# D2/t0.5 bonuses suggestion

## Post 162123 by Lane13 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-09T04:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162123#p162123 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Swap the 2-piece and 6-piece bonuses of the t0.5 dungeon set so that the more attractive AP/SP bonus is obtained with only two pieces.

The reason for this is that the entire set is quite lengthy to obtain and most of it will be quickly replaced by easily accessible raid gear. By providing the two better bonuses with 2 and 4-pieces, some pieces of the sets for certain classes will be useful for a bit longer.
