# Mage Missiles or Icicles or both

## Post 130086 by Forumdweller (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-07T18:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130086#p130086 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One or both of these spells needs reworking. They now do more damage than a 4/6sec Soulfire (keep in mind they are also channeled so they tick every 1 sec) and CHANNEL THROUGH THE WALL. Which was fine in classic because they did low-mediocre damage. NOW THEY DO YOUR ENTIRE HP BAR.

 1. Spellcasting time debuffs should affect these spells. Effects such as curse of tongues and mind numbing poison should increase the channelling duration.

 2. They need to immediately stop channeling once the target outranges the spell. As of right now if you're running from a mage channeling these spells and you run out of the casting range (30-36y) they're still channeling it while you're well over 50 yards away.

 3. If a target is LoS one or both of these spells need to stop channeling immediately. At least make the mage have to think by making only one version of the spell shoot through walls or atleast do reduced damage or something.

Or lets say only arcane missiles can shoot through the wall and you kick arcane missiles so they're locked on arcane and can't immediately just switch to icicles (which can no longer shoot through los)

 4. Increase the punishment for being kicked while channeling. Double the time they're locked out of casting in that school or make it lock them to all schools.

Mages should not be able to do 5k damage to you through the wall..!
