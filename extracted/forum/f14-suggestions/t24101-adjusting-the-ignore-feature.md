Couldn't fit the title properly so please excuse that. Anyways-

I've noticed a lot after ignoring a few active people that you still receive notifications when they quote you in a post. Doesn't that kinda go against the whole idea of blocking someone, in a sense? I feel like if I make it known that I don't want to hear from a person that it should more or less completely prevent them from getting my attention, somewhat like how it works in game, with the person sending the message getting a "(Insert person) is ignoring you" pop-up and the person blocking them getting... Nothing. Because they shouldn't be getting anything.

And, as far as I know, there also isn't any way for the person being ignored to know that they're being blocked besides telling them.

This isn't really a pressing issue, but I've had the occasional "Oh boy! A notification! I wonder what it is!" moment only to be met with "person you ignored quoted you in the: FIX PVP!!! NOW!!! PLEASE!! IT'S BEEN 8 YEARS!!!! YOU PROMISED!!! thread." and it's like... Slightly inconvenient.
