## Post 97727 by Matiel — 2024-06-24T10:22:00Z

For one of the Orcs class racials, I thought of tying the Warcraft 3 Firelords Incinerate to the Firestone, making both melee conjurer and fire destruction warlock playstyles more viable, it'd be something like this (still unrefined):

Incinerate (autocast)
While wielding a firestone, each attack made is enchanced with living flames that cling to the target. These flames add a debuff with a small amount of fire damage on the first attack, building up twice as much on the second attack, three times as much on the third attack, etc. If the unit dies while under this effect, the debuff is "unleashed", it is incinerated, causing a portion of the damage to nearby hostile units. If the target is hit with the Warlocks Conflagrate, Incineration is triggered, causing instead it's entire damage to the target over 4 seconds. Costs mana per each strike.
