# Auction house limit  - enchanting materials?

## Post 153626 by Wilczan (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-29T05:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153626#p153626 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello

I understand that max posted items on AH limit prevents from people posting houndreds of single item auctions.

But if you are disenchanter, and posting a lot of mats on AH, current limit is a little painfull.

There are some mats like all the dust, that can be posted in bigger stacks, without any problems.

But if you have a lot of essences, it's hard to sell in stacks of 10 or 20, couse chances that someone gonna buy stack that big, are low. People usually buy only amounts needed for specific enchant. Not to mention that those essences often cost few gold.

So, for example, If I post stacks of 1-3 essences, of many types, I'am already rather close to the max limit. Not to mention that I still need to post all the dust.
I know, I could make another character for just posting, but would prefer doing all operations from my main (and only) character.

My suggestion is:
 - make disenchanting materials on separate limit
 - or apply AH limit only to the stacks of 1, 2 or 3 items, so people won't flood the market with single clothes etc
 - or make donation item, that allows to rise max limit on AH (but still limiting small stacks of items)
 - or make separate limits for materials / armors and weapons / consumables / misc

Additional suggestion: allow lesser essences stack to 20. It does not make sense that greater essences stack to 20 and lesser to 10 only.

## Post 153691 by Edwanji (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-29T15:32:00Z

My opinion is that the whole auction house needs revamped.  For weapons and gear, and anything else that you would only ever conceivably buy one of, it's fine.
For everything else, mats and consumables, it should be something like a commodity trading house.  For the seller, they put up whatever quantity of an item they want, not limited by stack size, for whatever price per item.  For the buyer, they get a list of how many of the item is available at what price in ascending order.  They can buy whatever number they want and pay the total price. A seller will get paid when their entire stack sells, or if it times out, they will get the money along with the leftovers.
