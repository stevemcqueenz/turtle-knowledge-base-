# New Combat Drinks — For More Than Just Rogues!

## Post 155522 by Thesuperwitch — 2025-10-14T02:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155522#p155522 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

For years we rogues have been sipping endless cups of tea mid-battle.
But maybe it’s time others got a taste too! Here are two new brew ideas for our fellow adventurers:

 **1. Arcane Cocoa** - *“A warm surge of sweetness, infused with pure arcane energy.”*
 **Effect**:
Increases spell haste by 0.33% for 10 seconds, stacking up to 3 times.
Upon reaching 3 stacks or when the effect expires, you gain Sweetness Rush,
a debuff that prevents drinking Arcane Cocoa again for 5 minutes.
 **Reagents**:
Premium Chocolate ×1
Arcane Powder ×2
Ice Cold Milk ×1

 **2. Flaming Tea** - *“The fire inside doesn’t burn you — it fuels you.”*
 **Effect**:
Generates 1 Rage instantly and additional Rage each second for 10 seconds.
Each tick increases Rage gained by +1 until peaking at 5,
then gradually cools down — decreasing by 1 per tick as the spice fades.
 5 minutes cooldown.
 **Reagents**:
Firebloom ×1
Sungrass ×1
Refreshing Spring Water ×1

Since we rogues already dominate the combat drink market,
I say we claim these too! ![😄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f604.svg)

Numbers are just off the top of my head, its more about the concept.
Thanks for reading!

Edit:
If you like the ideas/concept I can think of more based on which class need more of what.
All feedback and ideas are appreciated :)
