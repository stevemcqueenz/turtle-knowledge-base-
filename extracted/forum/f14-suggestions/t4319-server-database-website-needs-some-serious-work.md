The <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/> site is woefully out of date, and lacking in a lot of details. Several items that are part of sets are listed without the set bonus such as <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83415> .

Some items arent even listed properly for looking up, unless you intend to tell us that theres only 496 cloth armor pieces in the entire game, some of the ones from the cloth category dont even have a slot assigned to them making it nigh impossible to compare different pieces of the same slot.

All in all, the database needs serious work, its barely functional
