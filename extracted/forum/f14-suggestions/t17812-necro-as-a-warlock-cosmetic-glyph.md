# Necro as a Warlock cosmetic glyph ?

## Post 121408 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-03-02T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121408#p121408 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A very common suggestion on this forum is to add the Necromancer class, but an immediate problem it faces is that they would be very similar to Warlocks in that they're a shadow-based summoner. IDK if Turtle is working on a Necro under behind closed doors, but if not, a good way to be able to play a pseudo-Necro (and RP as one) would be a "Glyph of the Necromancer" that would replace your demons with undead (whilst still classifying them as demons for balance purposes. It's not that much of a stretch: in Warcraft 3, demons were classified as undead for the purpose of Holy Light, Death Coil, and blight regeneration).

This would simply replace the demons' models with undead beings, for example :

Imp -> Skeletal mage

Walker -> Skeleton Warrior with a shield / Bone Golem (sacrifice's visual could even be replaced with that of Bone Shield that a bunch of mobs have)

Succ -> Banshee

Felhunter -> Ghoul (this one isn't a very good fit ...)

Infernal -> Abom (aboms even have disease cloud which mimics the infernal's immolation aura)

Doomguard -> Lich ?? Doomguards have War Stomp, Rain of Fire, Cripple and Dispel Magic, I'm struggling to find a good fit too

Anyways I figured this one would be a good way to satisfy people that suggest Necro as a new class every other week whilst generating a ton of donation token revenue for twow.
