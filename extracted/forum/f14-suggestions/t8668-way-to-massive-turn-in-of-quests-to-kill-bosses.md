# Way to massive turn-in of quests to kill "bosses"

## Post 55856 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-08-05T06:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55856#p55856 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For example Verog the Dervish <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=851>
It requires you to bring head of Verog to prove that we have killed him.
I emphasise the fact that we were asked to kill him. Head is supposed only to prove that fact.

Currently several players have to group up and collect up to 5 (five!) heads of some quest mob.
It leads to conflicts of HC vs non HC, to queues on killing that quest mob. You know it.

Proposal is:
For each named mob there should be event like Onyxia. Where you put head on the pike. In this moment, quest should be considered completed for everyone around that place regardless of being in party or HC status.
Named mob should drop single head and only one player should be able to loot it.
