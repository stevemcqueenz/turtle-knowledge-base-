# Some suggestions on craftsman skills

## Post 152073 by Nabanana — 2025-09-17T12:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152073#p152073 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am a Chinese player from the Asian server. Since my English is not good, I suggest that I use automatic translation here, please understand.

### Suggestions:

 1. Optimize the individual profession skills a bit, and make most production professions able to craft normal white items and white item enchantments.

 1-1. add some white weapon or equipment crafting for Forge, Tailoring, Engineering, Leatherworking, and Jewelry.

 1-2. Gray items, which are often discarded, can be enhanced by the Forging, Tailoring, Engineering, Leatherworking, or Jewelry skills to become white items (e.g., gray leather armor can be enhanced by Leatherworking, using cloth or wood or leather).

 1-3. When each specialty crafts a white weapon or equipment, it can be enchanted with a random attribute if it chooses to add the corresponding enchantment material.

 1-4. Enchantment Decomposition: There is a chance to decompose enchantment mechanisms with special attacks, such as:
Break down [Blade of Slamming], there is a chance to break down special attack attributes: When hit, there is a chance to make your next attack become two consecutive attacks.
After special attack enchantment is obtained, it can add attributes to white items, but there is also a chance to damage the item.

 1-5. Jewelry: you can make common holy grants, idols, and totems (with attributes added by enchantments)

 2. Given that there are fewer long range weapons and wands to craft in the game, would it be possible to add a new specialization: Carpentry

 2-1. Carpentries can make wooden bows, crossbows, arrowheads, axes, swords, staves, wooden long handled weapons, and wooden shields.

 2-2. Carpenters can craft vine armor (equal to leather armor quality, maybe higher armor?). Additional items such as vines and other additions are obtained as materials from additional gathering of herbs).

 2-3. Referring to 1-3, when crafting a carpenter's corresponding white item, if you add an enchantment material, you enchant it with an additional random attribute.

 2-4. A Carpenter can craft wooden boxes, equivalent to packages for Tailoring and Leather Armor.

 2-5. gain the lumbering spell, used when standing within 3 yards of a tree, to cut down and gather wood, which can be used for the carpenter's production of primary materials.

 2. if adding a new specialization is more impactful, maybe take what is in suggestion #2 and put it in the Survival specialization, I think Survival is a great specialization that covers a lot of ground.

 3. add a hiking spell to the survival specialty, similar to the mage's main city teleportation, provided you've explored that main city first, but with a longer casting time, maybe 60 seconds?

 4. Horticulture planting: you can add fruit and mushroom seeds, by cultivating them and then creating a pot to periodically get a set of apples or mushrooms.

 5. Enhance the correlation between specialization and crafting, e.g. Forge with Sword Specialization to craft items that add additional hit/accuracy attributes. Sword specialization also gains a passive spell, Know Thy Enemy: Know all the ways of the sword due to proficiency in the art of swordsmanship, adding an additional 1% dodge or stance to sword-type weapon attacks.

 ---

A lot of the suggestions are related to the artisan specialization, because I feel that with the current Turtle-WOW, it doesn't have to be about playing copies/team copies and getting better gear as the only goal, more pleasant gathering and production is also a way to play the game, and the turtle suit is a different kind of WOW altogether.

### 建议：

 1. 优化一下各个专业技能，并让大部分生产专业可以制作普通白色物品和白色物品附魔。

 1-1. 为锻造，裁缝，工程，制皮，珠宝，增加一些白色武器或装备的制作。

 1-2. 灰色物品很多时候弃之可惜，可以由锻造，裁缝，工程，制皮或珠宝技能，为对应专业的物品进行强化，强化后，变为白色物品（比如灰色皮甲，由制皮使用布匹或木材或皮材，进行强化）。

 1-3. 当各专业制作白色武器或装备时，如果选择添加对应的附魔材料，可为其进行随机属性的附魔。

 1-4. 附魔分解：有几率分解出有特攻的附魔法术机制，比如：
分解[痛击之刃]，有几率分解出特攻属性：命中时有几率使你下次攻击变为两次连续攻击。
特攻附魔获取后，可以为白色物品附加属性，但也有几率损坏该物品。

 1-5. 珠宝：可以制作普通圣契，神像，图腾（由附魔进行属性附加 ）

 2. 鉴于游戏中可制作的远程武器和魔杖较少， 是否可以添加新的专业：木匠

 2-1. 木匠可以制作木弓，木弩，箭头，木斧，木剑，木法杖，木质长柄武器，木盾等。
 2-2. 木匠可以制作藤甲（等同于皮甲品质，也许可以护甲更高？由草药额外采集获得藤蔓等添加物品作为材料）。
 2-3. 可参照1-3，在制作木匠对应白色物品，如果添加附魔材料，则进行额外的随机属性附魔。
 2-4. 木匠可以制作木盒，等同于裁缝和皮甲的包裹。
 2-5. 获得伐木法术，当站在树木3码范围内使用，来砍伐并收集木材，木材可用于木匠的生产主要材料。

 2. 如果添加新专业影响较大，也许可以将建议2中的内容，放在生存专业里，我认为生存是个很好的专业，覆盖面很广。

 3. 生存专业添加徒步远行法术 ，类似法师的主城传送，前提是你需要先探索过该主城，但施法时间更久，或许60秒？。

 4. 园艺种植：可以增加水果和菌类种子，通过培育后创造一个花盆，周期性获得一组苹果或蘑菇。

 5. 增强专精与工匠的关联性，比如有剑专精的锻造，制作的物品可以额外增加命中/精准属性。剑专精同时获得一个被动法术，知己知彼：因精通剑术，了解剑术的所有路数，额外增加对剑类武器攻击的1%闪避或招架。

 ---
建议中很多与工匠专业相关，因为我觉得当前的Turtle-WOW，不一定非要将打副本/团队副本，获取更好的装备作为唯一目标，更惬意的采集和生产，也是游戏的一种玩法，乌龟服完全的是另一种WOW。
