# Completionist Mode

## Post 44240 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-03T12:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44240#p44240 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings strangers. This mode is for the Explorers and non rushers that desire to play the hole game in a immersive experience.

This mode is simple. Upon accepting the mode in a character level one you:

 1) Dont get more XP from monters.
 2) Your XP rewards from exploring the map and finishing quests are multiplied up to 3 times. (tweeking would be necessary to get in the proper multiplier).
 3) Someone would only reach 60 if doing 95% of all quests and exploring areas in the map.
 4) Proper title and rewards at reaching level 60.

The whole point is only gentting on 60 upon exploring something about 95% of all possible quests and exploring all maps.

Thanks for the suggestion! I see the appeal of tying XP to zone/subzone completion in chunks, but I think that direction might create **some unintended issues**.

Having big level jumps can be easily exploited, especially if players coordinate to tag mobs and rush quest objectives. My idea aims for something slower and more immersive — where you reach level 5 only after completing **all quests meant for level 5**, often through long walks and full zone engagement.

Also, locking progression to blocks like “level 10 when you finish this zone” can be frustrating if players miss something or get stuck. It may cause them to feel artificially gated or confused about what they missed.

That said, your post helped me realize something important: maybe instead of 95%, the required completion should be **80% of all leveling quests**. That would give players some flexibility if they miss a few quests or zones.

Really appreciate the input — it’s great to test the limits of the idea like this!
