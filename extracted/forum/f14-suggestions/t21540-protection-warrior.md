# Protection Warrior

## Post 149242 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-29T22:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149242#p149242 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I'll focus mostly on dungeon tanking.
Wanna share my vision about the current prot warrior, things I don't like and things I'd like to see.

 **1. Defensive Tactics**
This idea seems flawed to me.
The warriors get a whole tree to improve abilities linked to the defensive stance (revenge, shield bash) or get new ones (shield slam) and THEN the game encourages you to leave that stance and use abilities you don't have upgrades for in the protection tree.
 *I don't think this is the solution for the warrior threat problems.*

Of course, the stance needs to be swapped sometimes but for specific reasons.
To prevent fear, to help via execute, to mocking blow, whatever is needed.
 *But defensive stance is the stance created for tanking.*

 **2. Ability scaling**
Putting up sunders, doing revenge, adding shield slam should be enough to keep threat, even to let DPS pump if the tank is properly geared. Currently the scaling seems to be way off.

 *I'd make these changes:*
 1. Would further scale shield slam threat and damage (the more OP shield you slam with, the more effective it gets).
 2. Revenge also needs better scaling. The protection warrior fantasy is about a clever tactician who avoids the attack and punishes it. This should stay relevant.
 3. Mostly for early game reasons, would add better threat scaling for thunderclap to help warriors with multitarget threat.

 **3. Exact change ideas**

 *Reprisal (2TP)*
Now: Increases Revenge damage and refunds rage.
Idea: Increases Revenge threat scaling, refunds rage and applies a sunder on the target.
Why: This change would make the rotation more fluid. Instead of forcing the warrior on sunder duty, stacks could be put up by rewarding the warrior from successfully avoiding a melee attack and punishing it.

 *Shield Slam -> Turning it into a cleave.*
Would help a ton with mob management.
Would put thunder clap to its rightful place (defensive talent with haste reduction instead of a cleave for multitarget threat) and wouldn't force prots to leave defensive stance to get an effective way of doing multitarget threat (cleave or whirlwind).
I stand by the philosophy that leaving defensive stance should be done for specific goals but the entirety of the tanking should be effective and possible from a stance designed to tank.

 **4. Maybe category**

 * Stance dance to a degree should be possible for all warriors. Needing 5tp to do just that feels wrong.
 *Should be baseline.*
 * Charging in for a pull should be possible without hurting your rage via forced stance change.
 *This is null and void if tactical mastery becomes baseline.*

 **5. What these would accomplish?**

Protection warrior would have a higher and better scaling threat, an additional way to add sunders and would become much more reliable at multitarget tanking.
