# Suggestion for Racials, Leveling Challenges and other Improvements

## Post 104589 by Nit0genski — 2024-10-23T18:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104589#p104589 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I was thinking for some time how to make the leveling challenges, such as Hardcore, Vagrant... challenges more fun, less clunky and, all in all, more streamlined without damaging the core idea of Turtle WoW and vanilla style of play.

Transmog

Issue: Cannot save transmogs you find while leveling with the "vagrant" challenge, requiring you to fill your bank and your bags with quest rewards and other found items until you hit max level.
Potential Solution: Allow items to automatically be added to the transmog section if they are soulbound without actually equiping them (of course, if you are able to equip them, no adding plate helmet to transmog if character is a priest).

Future challenges, like the boar challenge

Issue: A player who has potentially done the HC, Vagrant, Slow and Steady all on one character cannot receive the future challanges rewards.
Potential Solution 1: Allow the character to gain the reward on main character while leveling an alt. Example: A troll warrior has done all challenges and is the main. Allow the player to create another troll warrior, take on the new challenge and just have the rewards be funneled to the main character.
Potential Solution 2: Allow the character to delevel to 1 again, retaining all items, reputation and rewards. Just /setlevel 1, basically. This would allow the character to take on the new challenge without worrying about losing anything, just a bigger grind to relevel back to max level as said character will not have access to quests he/she has already completed.

Implement more challenges based on the side professions, world events and zones (allowing all characters to partake) regardless of level

Issue: A character has leveled to 60, off he goes raiding. Outside of raiding, they can PvP or grind for gold. Transmog feature being added can massively add to the longevity of the character as players can set their own goals in obtaining the perfect transmog, or even hunting down all available visuals. But here is the problem. It is still not enough.
Potential Solution: Add cooking/fishing/first aid challenges to obtain that are comparable in difficulty as a hardcore challenge or time consuming offering unique titles, shirts, tabards or the like.
For example: Catch X amount of fish and catch every fish available. Cook every meal X times and deliver X amount of side profession materials to some faction (like the war effort for AQ), make it soulbound or find other restrictions as to incentivize personal exploration as opposed to AH buyout.

Other things could be implemented as to allow for further personal challenges. A great example would be from WoW itself. Achievements. They did EXTREMELY well in prolonging the life of a character. Maybe implement those (if possible) or just imitate them in some fashion that you find most fun. Toys, cosmetic items and other non-power based items do well for incentive.

More reasons to visit empty cities and higher level characters to visit leveling zones

Issue: Max level characters have no need to visit other cities, small towns or zones making some places deserted completely while main gathering places become a spamfest.
Potential Solution 1: Offer players a reason to stick around places other than SW and Org making more places feel more alive, smaller gatherings of people allow for easier communication and more engagement in general. RP events, more festivals... Darkmoon Faire is a great example of this. Make a caravan/festival switch between major cities and other towns offering vanity items. Main cities will still be filled by people not interested in those items while Darnasuss, for example, wouldn't be a complete ghost town.
Potential Solution 2: Have fishing, farming, maybe even non-combat pet growing/caring (non power oriented activities) have exclusive items generate in zones and towns periodically like Stranglethorn fishing event or child festival week. More things to do in zones you don't visit often that have nothing to do with raiding, PvP or gold grinding. Lover level people could interact with higher level people organically. New players would see the population right away in a non AFK situation, which is great for the long term health of the game.

Racial balancing and other level 60 min/max power systems

While not really an issue and more of a selfish thought, and more of a personal stance i can certainly see the appeal of racial traits and like them very much. However I dislike the combat power that they bring, especially in a PvP scenario. My favorite racials usually deal with leveling and single-player scenarios while I really hate the PvP and raid scenario aspects that they bring.

Instead of +X to swords, spell to negate fears, passive to boost stun resistance, spell to boost armor... I would like to see some non-combat application and fantasy regarding races.

Humans are adaptable an good diplomats, the racial that boosts reputation gains is incredible! I can say that my character is good with dealing with people as I can see the results and it doesn't affect other people who wish to Exalt themselves with the same faction, it would just take them longer as they are not humans.
Dwarves have find treasure. Cool! It allows them to see treasures in the world. It's not exclusive and not game changing, just a nifty thing that they have in their arsenal.
Night elves with their wisp form? Perfect!

If I were a developer, I would either:
A) Disable ALL racials in an instanced scenario. Cannot use racials in PvP, cannot use racials in PvE. This would make the player pick their race based on flavor and fantasy. They already pick their playstyle with the class, why make them do the same with the race?
B) Change all racials to be something not related to power. % discount on repair armor, discount regardless of reputation, faster profession/weapon/defense leveling, faster flight path mount (gryphon flies 25% faster, for example), a permanent boost to the original backpack that has 20 slots instead of 16... Something of that nature. You have visible benefits while you don't miss out while playing a Nelf warrior instead of a Human one...

I am honestly more partial to the A) option. This would allow characters to have even stronger racials while not caring about the instanced environment. Min/Max players can choose to appear however they would like without affecting their visuals while roleplayers and levelers can have awesome spells and advantages granted to them by their race to use in the world. Shadowmeld that lasts 5 seconds and grants invisibilty totally? Busted beyond belief, but why not when you can only use it to clear a quest in a cave while you are level 32. It's useless in the battle ground or while in Naxx anyhow.

 **I understand that this may contain unpopular opinions and I would like to hear the opinions of other community members as well. I am certainly missing some perspectives and all that I mentioned above can certainly be problematic either to implement or there are some aspects that I did not take into consideration.**

Thank you for comming to my TED talk. /bow

Cool and reasonable ideas, I think most of them are easy to implement too (or at least they will be with new 2.0 client).

One of the problems with Orgrimmar/Stormwind being so dense is that these are the only cities where you can afk for 15 minutes and most likely receive powerful world buffs that will help you along your journey to level 60. WBuffs attract many low level players. And even after that, the buffs work in dungeons too so they are also useful in late game. Min-maxing is unfortunately important factor.

My suggestion is to expand these world buffs to other cities so for example when someone turns in Nefarian's head in Stormwind, everyone in each Alliance capital should get the world buff.
