# Gold Sink in Donation Shop

## Post 84561 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-13T18:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84561#p84561 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think that converting gold in game to donation points (I don't recall the official name of this currency) would be a good idea for turtle economy. It could be a fair gold sink especially for people who have been playing on the server for many years, the people who already have thousands of gold in the bags. I think this is one of the best ways to fight inflation - players who "have beaten" the game could have new thing to spent gold on. Their gold would vanish - instead they will be able to buy mounts, skins and other crap from the shop.

The conversion should be possible only one way to prevent P2W element - players can only convert gold to donation points. All items in donation shop are soulbound so this type of gold sink should work as intended. The only downside is that it could potentialy hurt real income of server owner so they might be reluctant to this change. Let me know what you think about this idea.
