# The direction of Twow

## Post 174124 by Myrcusq — 2026-04-06T00:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174124#p174124 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I have been playing wow on and off since i was 10 years old, that would put me at around 20'sh years of memories associated with this majestic game, with it's highs (Arthas), it's downs (Blizzard's descent into madness - no, not into YoggSaron's chambers) and with it's incredible community that had managed to spark such incredible private projects (among them, Turtle wow).

All these years i have been (devoid of my wish) obliged into witnessing how Wow slowly succumbed to a disease that only the greatest titans in the most heroic myths die off: the disease of becoming too powerfull for your own good. Wow has after years and years become bigger and bigger and Blizzard had to do something about it, it chose the path of vertical progression (the most satisfying path financially) and after years and years we have ended up with the abomination wow has become today: tons of irrelevant content, a tarnished core philosophy, poor social engagement, automated gaming. I do not wish this for Twow.

What got me into Twow was their stated philosophy: horizontal progression, no level cap raising, no descent into obsolete content. It was an awesome thought, but, thing is, this desire is but a pretty ghost with silver linings. A wishfull hope which we already started to depart from, without maybe even beeing aware off.

Now i'll get into it: thing is, twow team has been doing a great job adding content to both lower levels and endgame, but 7 years since it's official release, we stand now at 3 endgame-raids added over naxx, with 2 more planned on the roadmap, better gear than ever, more difficult skillset showcased on encounters and more and more microbuffs (from items and so forth) boosting our toons further. Twow has already started on the way of vertical progression without a raising of the level cap, w/o them even realising maybe. I mean, how manny raids can you add in the endgame before old level 60 raids become totally skiapble and obsolete? How manny can you add before a lvl 60 with endgame gear can 1-2 shot another fresh level 60 (kinda how a 80 1-shots a level 60)?

And i get it, what else is there to do? There is a limited number of things you can add untill you touch the endgame and they had done a good work on those. Touching endgame seems like the obvious choice, no? After everything else is on the better, what else would there be? Well, i have a proposition for you, and i would like you guys to throw your oppinions (give it a thumbs up or down).

Hear me out now: make a special realm (first to test this, see if people are interested) where you disable rested xp, you throw penalty for dying (15% lvl xp) and you activate "slow n steady" by default (basically a mode between hc and normal). Why do that? Simple:

I have never experienced a better journey then the one im having right now leveling my hc slow n steady priest. EVERYTHING is relevant. My brain is always engaged with the game, dungeons feel like they have real life and death stakes (they rlly do tbh), it doesnt pass an hour without me engaging socially because i either need something for a profession i cannot build myself or because i want to sell something usefull for other hc-ers out there, every zone feels like a mini-home (because i'ts been 4 days since i haven't left the barrens area - i mean, today i finally hit ashenvale but they are connected eith go and forth quests between them) and whenever i get an item (good item) it feels like i get a real treasure just because i know i won't be replacing that item for days after days. Enchants and alchemy feel like a must rather than a simple buff, crafting proffesions give acces to such better items from time to time that going off the beaten path just to find a Moss Agate to craft that orange robe feels so satysfying and rewarding.

The only problem? The fact that all this work and hours can go to waste in an instant, so make it bit more forgiving.

Stop making endgame content  for a tiny while (because what i aim for is for people to literally live through their levels, in such a way that a neckbeard nolifer might reach 60 in this mode in maybe a month or 2 -but would have no incentive too, because the fun at 20-30 would be the same as the fun at 60-, all the other normal peeps in half a year, maybe 1 year, who knows- some might never if they keep dying and don't pay attention -and that would not even matter because again, what would trully be different would be the numbers on the screen. You would have to and be able to raid at any point during your leveling and feel like it was relevant ! basically a true system where one raid unlocks the next chapter of your journey from level 20 to level 60, not only in the endgame) and start making middle game raids (10-20 players) that people can run as soon as lvl 20 (SOD showed this is possible).

At some point, with this huge penalty for xp, quests will run out and grinding (dungs/wildlife/and those raids i am talking about) will become the only way to reach the next milestone, next levelling zone if u wish. People would grind those awesome purples you created and put into the newly designed dungeons and would be so happy to have them because it would be months (maybe) before replacing them for real (not just few days...). Make so that we can experience endgame at any level. Make us that we can live the content, not only get through it. Make us so that we literally grow with our accounts, like turtles.

Make us take it slow and steady. Make us turtle wow again.

P.S. and for the love of God, please add PVP at some fing point.
P.P.S. this represents an advice about what design philosophy you could follow for the next 2-3 years. Please, depart from the evergrowing number of endgame raids, they do not help as much (because there are so manny) and yet take so much work. Please depart from the modification of older raids like MC. Please, take into account that you are selling turtles sand and water. Do not sell them streets and highways. Thx.
