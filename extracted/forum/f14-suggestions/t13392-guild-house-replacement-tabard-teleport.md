# Guild House Replacement (Tabard Teleport)

## Post 90170 by Thejakemf — 2024-03-14T16:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90170#p90170 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm the GM of the Stormwind Opportunity League, a semi-casual prog guild with over 100 characters in the guild and about half as many unique players.

We have often talked about how we would like to get a guild house, however my understanding is that they are no longer giving out guild houses as they take up too much space in the open world; I definitely understand why TWoW would want to stop doing that, however the current system leaves some guilds in an obviously advantageous position simply because they're older.

If we're no longer able to make requests for guild houses, could we make requests to have our guild's "House" simply be an in somewhere in the world that our tabard allows us to teleport to? This keeps TWoW from having to make a bunch of guild houses and filling up the open world, while still providing the same functions that older guilds' houses have to new guilds.
