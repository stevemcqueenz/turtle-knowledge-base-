# Arena cooldown rules, this makes no sense

## Post 151190 by Maoeu — 2025-09-10T22:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151190#p151190 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Why are warlocks allowed to use Infernal & Felguard (1h cooldowns) in arena, while every other class has their long CDs banned (Recklessness, Rebirth, Lay on Hands, Ankh, etc.)?
It makes no sense. It kills balance and it’s the reason you only see locks tagging with Gangreguard.
Ban all cooldowns above 10 minutes. Simple, fair, consistent.

## Post 151191 by Maoeu — 2025-09-10T22:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151191#p151191 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Right now in arenas, classes like warriors, druids, paladins, and shamans are not allowed to use their big cooldowns (30 min to 1h). That’s fine, and it makes sense for fairness.

But then why are warlocks still allowed to use Infernal and Felguard (1-hour cooldowns)? These abilities completely swing the outcome of a match and create a massive advantage for a single class, while others are restricted.

On every other WoW server or competitive ruleset, the standard is very clear:
No cooldowns above 10 minutes in arena.

This rule exists for good reasons:

Prevent “one button wins” that rely on luck of cooldowns rather than skill.

Keep the system consistent between all classes.

Make arenas fun and competitive instead of frustrating.

Turtle WoW already struggles with balance and motivation in arenas. Allowing this exception only for warlocks doesn’t help at all. If you want the arena scene to grow, rules need to be fair and consistent for everyone.

Suggestion:

Ban Infernal and Felguard in arena (same as other >10min cooldowns).

Keep cooldowns ≤10 minutes allowed.

Right now, it’s just unfair and makes no sense.
