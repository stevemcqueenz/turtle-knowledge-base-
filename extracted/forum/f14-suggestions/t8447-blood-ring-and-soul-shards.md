# Blood Ring and Soul Shards

## Post 54454 by Weiro7 — 2023-07-24T12:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54454#p54454 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Blood Ring is a quick BG where you get to use most of your cooldowns every time. That also means warlock gets to summon a pet and makes 2 healthstones for each fight. That means he goes through his shards very quickly.

I suggest that there are put for example 3x lvl 50 neutral mobs with 1 hp (modified small bugs from AQ40 Twin emperor room?) in the small preparation room so warlock can get some shards before each fight to keep up with the quick consumption. They must give exp for a shard to be created so set that to 1 as well.
