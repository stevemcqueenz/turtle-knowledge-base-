# Fixing Shaman - Early 1st to 2nd Row Talents 1/x

## Post 31920 by Thegr8equalizer — 2023-01-01T19:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31920#p31920 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Early talents for shamans, outside of Ele tree, are effectively punishing a lot of the spec available for the class, while making the **already weaker specs even harder to work with**. This is my suggestions for **easy fix and changes to the 2 first rows of talents in Enha and Resto trees**:

 1) Resto tree:

Both 1st row talents are extremely specialized and only truly desired for deep resto spec shamans. These 5 points are effectively 5 useless points for shamans that are looking to get the mandatory "Totemic Focus" Talent for mana cost reduction on totems. My suggestion is to **swap "Totemic Focus" on 2nd Row for "Tidal Focus" on 1st Row.**

This would effectively allows both ele and enha deep specs to grab the most important talents of Resto for 3 less talent points, allowing more power to allocate in secondary talents in enha/ele trees. **The requirement for getting Nature's Guidance, Totemic Focus and Totemic Mastery would change from 14 points to 11.**

 2) Enha tree:

a) 1st Row "Ancestral Knowledge" Talent is a very underwhelming talent that give roughly a bit less than 200 more mana at lvl 60 for 5 talent points. For enhancement dps spec shamans, its even worse when you consider the fact they have absolutely no use for the shield talent either.

If you actually compare this talent to what other classes can have on the same row here is what you get:
Paladin: 10% strength for 5 points, 10% int for 5 points.
warlock: 15% stam minus 5% spirit for 5 points.
warriors/rogue: 5% melee crit for 5 points.
Hunter: 15% pet health for 5 points.

For the same max mana talent type, priests and mages get 10% extra mana.

In my opinion, Ancestral Knowledge should be giving intelligence, as all shaman specs struggle with mana sustain, especially enhancement tree which has notoriously bad mana pool because of low int on gear. This is my suggestion for this talent:

 **Ancestral Knowledge - 1st Row - 5 talent points: Increase your intelligence by 20%/40%/60%/80%/100% of your level.**

Effectively this is a 600 mana increase at lvl 60, or roughly 10% more mana for Resto/ele specs and about 20% more mana for enha spec.

b) 2nd Row "Improved Ghost Wolf" Talent doesnt really do anything except for a slightly faster time running around pre 40 as ghost wolf. There is no good reason why this doesnt make GW instant and I believe this is why this talent is effectively useless in both pvp and pve. Considering the form is dispellable, does not remove snare or root effects, the least it could do with 2 talents points is being instant.

This is my suggestion for this talent:

 **"Improved Ghost Wolf" 2nd Row - 2 talent points: Reduce the cast time of your Ghost Wolf spell by 1,5/3 sec.**

Thanks for reading and please give me feedbacks for my suggestions :)
