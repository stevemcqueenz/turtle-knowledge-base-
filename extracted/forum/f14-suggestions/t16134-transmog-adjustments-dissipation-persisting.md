# Transmog Adjustments,  Dissipation & Persisting.

## Post 109878 by Chaseowskii — 2024-11-25T19:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109878#p109878 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It'd be incredibe if ongoing transmogrified slots (for example if you transmogrify your chestplate) that when equipping a chestplate, your transmog does not reset/remove. But instead, persists the ongoing transmog you had on that slot.

All that the dissipation of transmog does when equipping a new item does, is incentivize players to save & stock their tokens until they have gear they will no longer replace or until they have gear that will last them long enough to grind additional tokens.
