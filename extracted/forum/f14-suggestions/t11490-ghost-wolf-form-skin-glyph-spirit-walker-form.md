# Ghost Wolf Form skin/glyph: Spirit Walker Form

## Post 76063 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-11-05T21:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76063#p76063 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It would be cool if we got some glyphs for classes other than druids.

So here is my proposal: A glyph that turns shamans' Ghost Wolf into a spirit form, similar to the one Tauren Spirit Walkers from Warcraft 3 had. It will basically turn the caster's original appearance ghost-like with some cyan-y ghost-like special effects, similar to the Glyph of the Moon.

It would help us stop looking at the same blocky ugly ghost coyote model all the time, while being lore-friendly and cool-looking.
