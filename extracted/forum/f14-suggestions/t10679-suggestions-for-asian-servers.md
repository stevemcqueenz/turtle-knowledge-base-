# Suggestions for Asian servers

## Post 69585 by Liuyihang0510 — 2023-10-08T14:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69585#p69585 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good management, everyone!

建议:

Suggestion:

亚洲服务器的所有账号必须绑定手机号（账号不能换手机）。一个手机号只能绑定数量限制5个或者更少。

All accounts on Asian servers must be bound to a mobile phone number (accounts cannot be changed). A phone number can only be bound to a limit of 5 or fewer.

我知道造成的影响:

I know the impact:

第一，提高工作室开号成本，会降低玩家数量，但是能有效的减少排队，提高正常玩家存活率，能长期有效拉活游戏周期。

Firstly, increasing the cost of studio registration will reduce the number of players, but it can effectively reduce queuing, improve the survival rate of normal players, and prolong the game cycle in the long term.

第二，能有效遏止买卖账号，从源头掐断账号买卖。

Secondly, it can effectively curb the buying and selling of accounts and cut off account buying and selling from the source.

第三，明令禁止XX不分团，遏断工作室代打副本某件装备，如某鱼电锤代打。

Thirdly, it is explicitly prohibited for XX to divide into groups and prevent the studio from producing copies of certain equipment, such as a certain fish electric hammer.
