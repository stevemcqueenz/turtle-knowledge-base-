# A relic idea

## Post 99372 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-07-13T17:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99372#p99372 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think that a low-level shaman relic which has the effect of linking magma totem's threat to you would give more fun to the game while not breaking anything.

I mean:
 * We barely have tanks when leveling.
 * If a shaman joins as a tank, people usually end up crying.
 * While early relics can be gamebreaking, this one doesn't push the shamans in any meta sense.

Why?
For leveling:
One extra valid (?) tank, LFT gets filled faster. Shamans still would be the worst option but a relic helping them avoid healer aggro at least would mean that they can somewhat tank if healer keeps them alive.

For lategame:
If someone likes challenges, this relic could be used later to add an aoe-threat element of tanking without messing with talents or introducing deep changes. Only trick is to macro it to be equiped when summoning magma totem.

Why do I make this suggestion?
 * This is a minimal-effort change (adding 1 custom item which has no effect to PVP or any other power rankings).
 * Makes dungeoning easier.
 * Makes a subculture of players (shaman tank fanatics) happy.

Since magma totem can first be learned at level 26 and we have a very good custom dungeon in almost that range, I'd suggest adding an item like this to Crescent Grove. Shamans wanting to try out tanking could spam the dungeon and later could help LFT-ing people by selecting tank.

PS:
To keep things symmetrical, I'd advise a pala relic from the same level range. Premise is the same, a relic which would make many paladins happy without adding to the class' powerlevel (just like in the case of the shaman one). My suggestion is a paladin relic which adds +3 minutes duration to the non-greater blessings - to keep things totally balanced, from Crescent Grove, again.
