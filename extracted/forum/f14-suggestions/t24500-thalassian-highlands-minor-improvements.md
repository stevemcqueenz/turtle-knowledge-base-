# Thalassian Highlands - Minor Improvements

## Post 172413 by Terrannus — 2026-03-25T16:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172413#p172413 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I'm just playing my first High Elf and really loving the starting zone. It is a bit more difficult and requires more personal exploration but that's honestly been quite fun. Two minor things have really made it more of a struggle than it should be though imo.

 1) Repair vendors. I've been walking around Alah'Thalas for 20 minutes trying to find one and there just doesn't seem to be one. Even the weapon vendors and armour merchants aren't able to repair. Apologies if this is a known bug or something but I couldn't find it anywhere. There's also a bit of a lack of general vendors compared to other cities, and around Thalassian Highlands. It's particularly noticeable that the Silver Covenant Park and (I think) the Thaumarium didn't have any at all.

 2) This is a slightly bigger one and something I've noticed with a lot of Turtle Wow's quests: there's a real lack of cardinal directions. I know a lot of people talk about the fun of getting lost in vanilla wow, and I agree, I wouldn't want the quest to hold my hand. But there was still usually general hints about whether a quest target was east/south/etc of the location. This was particularly noticeable for the quests to the Silver Sun mine, since neither of them explained that this mine was north-west of the starting zone, like most vanilla quests would.

I hope this comes accross as constructive since it's just minor things in an otherwise amazing experience!
