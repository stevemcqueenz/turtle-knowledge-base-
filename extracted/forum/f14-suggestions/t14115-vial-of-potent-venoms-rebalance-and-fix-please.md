# Vial of Potent Venoms - Rebalance and fix, please.

## Post 96191 by Daddydeath — 2024-06-02T16:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96191#p96191 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

> Current In-Game Text: Equip: Your direct melee and ranged attacks have a chance to inject poison into your target that deals 100 Nature damage over 12 seconds. Stacks up to 5 times.

> What it does: Equip: Your direct melee and ranged attacks have a chance to inject poison into your target that deals 120 Nature damage over 12 seconds. Stacks up to 2 times.
>  This effect ticks every 3 seconds and has a 35% Proc Chance.

> Recommended Change: Equip: Your direct melee and ranged attacks have a chance to inject poison into your target that deals 90 Nature damage over 12 seconds. Stacks up to 5 times.
>  This effect ticks every 2 seconds and has a 20% Proc Chance.

In-Game text effect does 8.3 DPS per stack, for 41.6 DPS at 5 stacks. (You always reach 5 stacks with this proc chance)
In-Game Effect (what it does) does 10 DPS per stack, for 20 DPS at 2 stacks. (You always reach 2 stacks with this proc chance)

Recommended Change Effect: 7.5 DPS per Stack, 37.5 DPS at 5 Stacks. However, the odds of consistently hitting 4-5 stacks is low on most specs, and Resistances on enemies means that while this theoretical 37.5 DPS is very high, in actuality it's going to be closer to half of that due to resistances (~18.75) putting it on-par with trinkets like Blackhand's Breadth, a little behind Heart of Dreams. A worth mid-tier Raid trinket
