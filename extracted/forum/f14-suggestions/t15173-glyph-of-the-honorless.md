# Glyph of the honorless

## Post 103021 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-13T18:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103021#p103021 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just a new idea for a glyph, for people pvping who dont want or need honor.

Glyph of Honorless:
Turn off your honorgain and grants you 3 gold per bg win, or 5 silver per honorable kill.

Useful for people who want to keep their rank and not advance but to try gain the Bloodthirsty title, I think some Paladin mentioned he wanted to keep his Commander rank or something.

Would also be nice for pure pvpers who can earn some gold for consumes as if you find farming boring then you cant really make money JUST pvping.

Either that or just make it so you can turn your honor gain off like you can for XP.
