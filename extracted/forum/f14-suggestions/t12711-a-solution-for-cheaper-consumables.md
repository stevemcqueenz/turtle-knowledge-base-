# A solution for cheaper consumables

## Post 84654 by Jc473 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-01-14T11:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84654#p84654 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To help alleviate the issue of rising consumable prices, why don't we replicate the alchemy change Blizzard did in Season of Mastery?

Here's a quick rundown of the change.
On each crafting attempt there is a chance of making additional quantities of the same item:
 20% - x2 items
 15% - x3 items
 10% - x4 items
 5% - x5 items

This worked for all consumables except things like oils (e.g. shadow oil, stonescale oil etc).

Outcomes:
 1) It increases the supply of consumables on the AH which should, in turn, puts a downward pressure on the prices.
 2) If the supply of consumable is greater, this should also create a downward pressure on herb prices.
 3) It is very fun for the alchemist to 'roll the dice' and see how many bonus consumable they get from a crafting session. People worked out that, on average, you'd get around 30-40% increase in output. However, it was always a little nerve-racking that you might get lucky and double your output or be 'unlucky' and only get a 10-20% additional gain.

Blizzard also drastically increased node respawn (and maybe the number of existing nodes?) which helped tackle the issue from both ends. I think TWoW should also take this approach but I think this alchemy suggestion would also help a lot!
