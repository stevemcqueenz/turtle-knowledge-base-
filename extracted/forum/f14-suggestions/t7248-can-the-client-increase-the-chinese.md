## Post 45970 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-05-15T18:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45970#p45970 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Our current development routine does not include translating our content into multiple languages. The only supported language on Turtle WoW is English. We made this decision because we cannot maintain the quality control of our newly written content in several languages.

Doing so would slow down the development process by years. We recommend that users explore solutions such as on-screen AI translation.

There are addons available, at least for the Russian language, and possibly for Chinese as well.
