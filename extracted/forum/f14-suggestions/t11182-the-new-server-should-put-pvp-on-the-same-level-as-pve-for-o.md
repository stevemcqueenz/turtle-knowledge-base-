# The new server should put PvP on the same level as PvE for once.

## Post 73812 by Scelus12 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-26T01:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73812#p73812 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Throughout its existence wow has been a PvE game first and PvP game second. In terms of blizzard development hours spent, balancing prioritization, player participation, quality of gear, etc it's always been PvE > PvP.

Because of this, a PvE player has always been compensated for their time more richly for equivalent effort. And I think most of us agree this was the correct choice. The "game" was progressing through the content and beating the big bad -- beating each other up in the world/BGs/arena was the extracurricular activity. So why not use this fresh opportunity to do something new? PvP = PvE.

I've seen some folks express concern that the fresh server will drain too much life from the existing server. And if it's the same-ish server but just PvP, it might. People love a fresh start. But if the turtle team decides to make a big content shift toward PvP and differentiate it from the existing server, I think you'll not only attract a lot of PvP-first players who are new to turtle wow (like me), but you'll also ensure that the existing server continues to thrive.

The design philosophy is simple: reward PvP and PvE at a similar rate and quality. If a fresh 60 who plays 2-3 hours a day could expect X level of gear through dungeoning and raiding for a month, they should be able to get equivalent gear from PvP.

I'm sure many PvE-only players don't think PvP deserves gear on par with 40 man raids from battlegrounds, and I completely agree... on every server except this one!

What would be important is to throttle the rate at which PvP acquires gear. You'd want to align with the speed of PvE. No one should be able to buy a full set of raid quality PvP gear after a month, even at the highest PvP rank. You would need to accumulate a currency, similar to how BC/WOTLK arena points worked. The very best players had a full set after 3 months or so.

Another throttle you could implement is to tie PvP gear to all facets of PvP. If you want to maximize the speed at which you acquire PvP gear, you need to arena, BG and defend you faction in the world. If you only choose one of those, you'll progress more slowly.

Anyway, while this idea I'm sure is an instant NO from many of you (I completely understand that opinion) this server would attract a huge amount of new players who are PvP first and also minimize the disturbance on the existing server.

I also think you might see a ton of higher quality raid pugs too. Because players like me who in a dream scenario would be 75% PvP, 25% PvE are normally just shut completely out of that facet of the game in vanilla.
