# Item Inferno Challenge

## Post 95307 by Ibux (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-05-20T18:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95307#p95307 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

How about a game mode where you lose all the gear on your character if your character dies. But not your character as in hardcore mode. Perfect for players who don't want to start completely over when they die. But they get to spend more time doing dungeons, battleground and raids. With the joy of a bit of risk but without the huge risk of losing your character. If there is enough interest in such a mode.

I think that in bg's players can only have one life since they lose their equipment. Then they have to play more carefully and skilled. Otherwise it's back to finding new equipment.

I imagine that professions such as blacksmithing, tailoring or leatherwork that can make equipment would probably be more valuable to have.

Equipment you have in the bank does not have to disappear when you die as the bank cannot die. Then it might be a good idea to save some resources and equipment in the bank for the next time you die.

Interesting. Perhaps the key to solving this problem is to better balance the reward with this increased risk. Reward well enough to those who choose to fight the opponent so that they choose to fight over hiding.

It is a big risk. You can lose weeks, maybe months of work. With that in mind, perhaps the hardcore gamers should get a rank up if they take an honorable kill against opponents with a higher rank. Against opponents of about the same rank it could be a fifty-fifty chance. Against players with a lower rank than them, perhaps they should not get rank up. With the assumption that these players are level 60 of course.

If the player were to be of a lower level, the chance of ranking up should be based on more factors. Definitely not as high a chance to rank up at level 19 as at level 49, for example. And in any case no rank up if the opponent is a lower level than the player.
