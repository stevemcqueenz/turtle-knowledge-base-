# GM commands available for enchanted roleplay...

## Post 3676 by Voj — 2019-08-06T19:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3676#p3676 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings people , I have an interesting suggestion that may be very productive for the RP environment.

I was Gm in a very small vanilla server before several years, about 0-5 ppl online daily , and what I did is create an entertainment for those lone warriors, event etc. but in the end I used the most a feature that was an idea for WoW but never used for some reason. The lowest rank of GM have several commands , teleport to xyz , etc. but the most fun are to interact via the environment , such as - speak,emote,move with a NPC/Mob , create visual effects , and more.

A few quick stories to give a better image
 - I see a low level rogue running at low health from a Kobold , suddenly the Kobold says ,, Why are you running ? "'.
 - A warrior is stealing a barrel of milk is some tavern , suddenly the NPC innkeeper starts moving , going directly to the player and start mocking him for being a thief.

Now imagine a player farming his levels, playing a game he played for a several years, and then suddenly the grindy  environment begins to be ,,alive & thinking''.

My proposal is regular players to be able to use several commands from the GM's list and in order create a different and even better WoW experience. This might even bring a completely new gameplay where people are playing as the environment rather as a single character.

There are few thought that I can discuss regarding this idea :
 1) I'm not sure if GM commands can be limited, being able to move,speak etc. from a NPC perspective is just a fun experience, otherwise teleporting and being immortal as .GM command is on will harm the game and leveling experience,but also they can be very useful if used properly - turning invisible is essential for such a idea , teleporting is not mandatory but will improve the pactical side of such gameplay.
 - That leave us that either one should create a new account where he has the GM privileges and will not use for normal gameplay or either he will have just a few , limited set of command in his regular account.
 2) At first I thought that this it should be available to everybody but then I quickly considered otherwise. The best middle ground I found is to be able to purchase a ,,Community GM'' title or something since I actually know that donations are vital for the survival of any server/webpage , but also this way it can be free for people choosing the hardcore mode where at level 60 you are awarded some donation points.
 - In any sense it should be available to people that are serious and responsible since spawning some effects/events might harm the server. Also some people might be confused if you are an actual GM representing the server and thus lead to unwanted results.

I would really like if this server can become what WoW never was going to be... I will make a separate topic soon on things as Ashara Crater Battleground, The Emeral Dream , Hyjail , etc. can bring this server to the top and beyond :)
Well do you like the idea ?

The GM privileges work is a rank system - from 1-5 , thus a rank 1 GM will have very few commands at his disposal compared to rank 2 or 3 for example, but nevertheless some of them are game breaking. Here are basiclly all the commands available for GM rank 1 :

 1) . GM (on/off)/chat/setview/visible(on/off)
 *Note that when the GM command in on you can't die and all mobs are neutral , but you can't attack either.
 *With .visible on/off you can switch between invisible and visible mode thus other non GM players can't see you.

 2) .NPC say/textmode/whisper/yell
 *Allows you to do the following say/emote... via any given NPC or a mob.
 *Note you can't move and mobs/NPC in GM rank 1 , just talk via them.

 3) .GO creature/graveyard/grid/object/taxinode/trigger/ xy /xyz /zonexy / goname / gps / groupgo
 *Teleports you to the given coordinates of terrain/object/etc..

 4) .LEARN ALL_default / all_lang
 *Learn all the spells for your class ( not including talents/quest reward spells such as tame beast etc.) You can learn all spells and ranks even if you are lower level ( let say level 1 ) , but at level 60 it will only save you the training cost.
 *The second command learns all languages - Darnasian/Orkish etc.

 5) .MODIFY speed/drunk/energy/faction/hp/ mana / money / rage
 *This is the most problematic one. You can modify your gold to quadrillion as well as your HP,Rage etc.( Absolutely game breaking)
 *It also works on NPS/mobs.

 6) .MUTE/UNMUTE ( Sadly the .mute command can be used to grief another player if this command is not in the right hands)

 7) .NOTIFY (Notifies the server with a message in the chat , eg. Server is shutting down in 5,4,3,1 OFFBLAST!)

 8) .TELE/Tele group/Recall
 *Teleports a player or/and his party to a given location.
 *With .recall you will teleport him back where he was before being teleported.

 9).SAVEALL
 *Saves all data that has been changed to this moment. ( When creature/player is modified by  command his changes will go back to normal once the server restarts, with .saveall command any changes will saved permanently)

 10).Send Mail ( Save 30 copper ^^ )

 11) .TAXI CHEAT ( Opens all taxi fly-paths )

 12) .Whispers on/off ( Allows or declines the GM to be /whispered )

As you see the .Modify commands are the main problem since they are source of unlimited power, thus not safe in even the most responsible and mature of non staff players.  But I think it is most likely possible to restrict several commands from the above and creating new GM status ( rank 0.5 or something ).
