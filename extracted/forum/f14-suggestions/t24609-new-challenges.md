Ive thought of a few that i think could be added to the Turtle Craft list.

There is already "Slow and Steady" but i thought "Fast and Furious" would be cool. In this challenge you would get 10% movement speed 5% increased damage, but increased damage taken 10%. end rewards "Furious" title. (or whatever deemed possible)

Second one i think would be cool (and can be done if you keep yourself honest). is called "Super Sticky". in this challenge it would be cool that once you equip a piece of gear that you cannot remove that item for 8-10 levels. making it so that people need to plan when to replace an item. Reward at end "stuck like glue" or something else. I also think a reward like special armor kits or enchantment that goes on an item would nice reward every 10 levels. these should be things you cannot get in game but come specifically from this challenge (not applicable to lvl 60 gear). so that way its "Over powered" but cannot be used on lvl 60 gear.

Super Clumsy, in this challenge player randomly has weapon dropped, (Turtle craft can figure this one out). also increased misses. sorta build after the Dyspraxia disorder. in any case could award another title like "unarmed champ" - this one seems more annoying than a challenge probably a bad idea(dunno).

Best Friends Forever (BFF) in this challenge once you group you cannot be ungrouped. in short, finding the friends you will party with, dungeon with etc. is key. with the exception of HC and dying there is no way to leave the party. ""Name's" BFF" title to be rewarded. I think also Trade should be banned outside of party members, this way group composition is, you find it you keep it, and support the group.

Last one sorry, "These boots are made for walking", this challenge is to level without a mount. end reward enchant with 8% movement speed. and Title "walker"

Always thinking of random stuff but id personally like some of these challenges
