# Troll Witch Doctor - and potential for other classes

## Post 27245 by Ravenstone (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-10-13T19:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27245#p27245 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been reading a lot of posts about new classes as they interest me the most and I started to wonder about the possiblity of just mixing current class, spell types and their talent trees, allowing for minimal updates to account for the stuff that just wouldn't work. The example that triggered this idea was to create a Troll Witch Doctor, by taking the Shadow priest spells and talent tree, and merge it with the Shaman, removing one of theirs. At a simplistic level I believe it could open up the role playing aspect of some races a bit more and bring a bit of diversity to the world, whilst being an interpretation of the Troll witch doctors from Warcraft 3 which gets lost in WoW.

Inherently I'd hope it'd be fairly balanced for the leveling and fresh 60 experience, probably just underpowered once raiding due to not having proper tier sets unless they were designed at the same time.

On the other hand, I've not done an extensive dive into the interactions of the two classes and seen whether there are any overpowered aspects to them, or just anything downright incompatible. Whether they'd take a wand or have the totem relic immediately comes to mind, and probably more that I've overlooked.

Another idea that came to mind was a 2h Rogue/Warrior combo, inspired by the Blademaster hero, but that would present even more questions with the obvious energy vs rage resources.

Am keen to hear others thoughts on the idea, whether any cool class types, overpowered combos or just huge incompatibilities come to mind.

## Post 27248 by Rokit (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-10-13T21:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27248#p27248 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think there is a simpler approach to give fresh, lore-friendly, class fantasy. Using Troll Witch Doctor as an example, you can find places that express it already present in the game. Priests have racial spells, they are often limited in their effect and niche in their application. However, it gives a good example of how to differentiate class / race fantasy.

A handful of abilities, differentiated by choice (Talents, Race, Professions) would go a long way to serving this rather than the labor of creating new, and possibly derivative / competitive classes.

"Witch Doctor" could be Troll Shadow / Disc Priests. This could be affected by making Shadowguard and Hex of Weakness more impactful, gain benefits from already existing talents (adding to them benefit from "Darkness" or other relevant talents). Making these more subtle differences available to players would do a ton for serving this kind of individuated fantasy.
