# Syringer

## Post 41106 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-04-11T08:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41106#p41106 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Syringer is a thing which can apply potion effect to your target.
How to use it:
Choose a player and target him.
Use the Syringer, your cursor should become active. Like you use "Disenchant".
You click on a potion in your backpack.
As a result, your target will get effect of your potion.

It should share your trinkets cooldown (like Major Recombobulator).
