# Blizzard didn't finish pets so I did

## Post 76501 by Steelx — 2023-11-08T16:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76501#p76501 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is my suggestion on how to make all pets fun, unique, relevant and viable for different playstyles.

Wolf
Passive. Thick Fur. Takes 10% less Nature, Frost and Fire damage.
Furious Howl. Increases buff range from  15yd to 50yd. cooldown reduced from 10s to 4s.

Bear
Passive. Thick Fur. Takes 10% less Nature, Frost and Fire damage.
Can learn Swipe(pet). Swipe 3 nearby enemies dealing 15/25/35 damage. 40 focus. No Cooldown.

Raptor
Passive. Strider. Base movement speed +30%.

Strider
Passive. Strider. Base Movement speed +30%.

Hyena
Passive. Pack Tactics. Increases nearby allies Attack Speed by 2%.
Pack call. 60 focus. 60s CD. Increases movement speed of party members within 30 yards by 20% for 10s.

Spider
Passive. Devourer. +3% Vampirism.
Can Learn Spider Web. 30 focus. 40s CD. 20 yard range. Roots the target for 3 seconds. Damage may break the effect.

Bat
Passive. Devourer. +3% Vampirism.

Carrion Birds.
Passive. Devourer. +3% Vampirism.

Owl
Passive. Devourer. +3% Vampirism.

Crocolisk
Can learn Fatal Bite. 60 focus. Next Melee drains the target for 25/50/100 and heals the pet for that same  amount.
Passive. Shell Defense. +20% Block Chance.
Rotgrip is now tameable and teaches rank 3 Fatal Bite. The attack would work similar to Heroic Strike, next attack would do extra damage and would heal for only the extra damage dealt, meaning up to 50/100/200.

Turtle.
Passive. Shell Defense. +20% Block Chance.
Shell Shield. 60 focus. 12s CD. Damage taken reduced by 30%, movement speed reduced by 60%. Inflicts 5/15/35 damage to anyone who attacks you. Lasts 12s.

Scorpids
Passive. Shell Defense. +20% Block Chance.
Scorpid Sting. 4s Cooldown Removed. Now only has Global Cooldown.

Gorilla
Thunderstomp reduced cooldown from 60 seconds to 10 seconds. Generates increased threat (threat = damage x2)

Crab
Can learn Crab Grab. Disarms an enemy for 5/6 seconds. 1m Cooldown.
Passive. Parry. +5% Parry.
Crab Grab rank 1 learned from Crabs in Blackfathom Deeps and rank 2 from crabs in Swamp of Sorrows.

Boars, Cat & Wind Serpents
Elusiveness. +5% Dodge

General Changes.
Bite. Now Heals pets for 100% of the damage dealt with Bite. Cooldown reduced from 10s to 6s.
