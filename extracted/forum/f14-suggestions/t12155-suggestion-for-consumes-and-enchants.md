# Suggestion for consumes and enchants

## Post 80765 by Dogfight — 2023-12-11T23:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80765#p80765 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Cost of raiding has increased a lot lately with prices of nearly every consumable going up and it wears on people. Gold is harder to come by. Not everyone likes to, or can, farm in instances, and farming for open world resources is tough. It often takes more time than casual players have to farm and when not coming prepared to raid some may have a feeling of being inadequate.

Here are some suggestions:
 -	Flasks: Change duration from 2hrs to 1hr, and when crafting flasks 2x will be crafted instead of 1. This makes it easier to adapt the use in raids and less time of the flask is wasted. Also, this will encourage people to still play their character in between raids, instead of logging off to save the duration for next raid.
 -	Elementals: Transmutes from Globe of Water(?) to Elemental Water, Breath of Wind to Elemental Air, Core of Earth to Elemental Earth.
 -	Increase number and geographical spread of spawn points of Rich Thorium Veins in zones where these exist and reduce spawn time.
 -	Controversial; consider adding instanced spawn points for mid and high level herbs to reduce the pressure on open world farms.
 -	Reduce spawn time of high level open world herbs

Random other suggestions:

Add more enchants to shields:
o	+8 Fire/Nature/Arcane/Shadow Resistance
o	+12 Stamina
o	+18 Block Value
o	+ X Spell damage and healing

New defensive weapon enchant:
o	1H: Chance on hit to grant +XXX Block Value and restore XXX health
o	2H: Chance on hit to grant +XXX Armor and restore XXX health

New cloak enchant:
o	+X Spell damage and healing
