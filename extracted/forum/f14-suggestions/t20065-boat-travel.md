# Boat Travel

## Post 137290 by Tenyar — 2025-06-28T00:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137290#p137290 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been thinking about how we could really lean into Turtle WoW’s slow-and-steady vibe—and one idea that came to mind is long-form boat travel.

Right now, most sea routes are basically just instant teleports. What if we had a full 5-minute (or longer) boat ride, complete with a visible ship deck that you could walk around on, RP with others, maybe fish off the side for special "ocean only" loot, chat with the crew, and watch the horizon shift around you. Basically just… feel the ocean.

Let me take you back for a sec.

My first memory of World of Warcraft wasn’t some epic raid or fancy mount. It was logging in as a Gnome Warlock in Dun Morogh and getting absolutely *clobbered* by wolves in the starting area. After enough rezzes, I finally made my way to Ironforge and hopped on the Deeprun Tram. (Mostly because I had no idea where else to go.) But when that tram hit the underwater section and I saw Nessy swimming past the glass and the Deeprun Divers? Man, it hit me how big this world actually was. It wasn't just a game.. pick up this quest, kill this many Boars. This was an actual *place*  and I was a part of it. Turtle WoW already captures that slower, more thoughtful pace better than any other server I’ve tried. It is, at it's heart, an RP server. A long boat ride feels like it fits perfectly here. Not everyone will want to sail slow, and that’s totally fair (I've got an idea for that, too). But for the explorers, the storytellers, and the wanderers? I think it could be something special. It's an immersive way for those of us who enjoy soaking in the world as much as the gameplay. Maybe some folks just want to AFK on deck and watch the ocean, others might start a little impromptu deckside RP. Could even have random events, like storms, sea monsters, an NPC emoting from the "crowsnest" things like "One minute until landfall!" or shipmates telling tall tales about things happening in the world around us, like plans of going to Northwind. We could downport ideas like the telescope from "Peeking into the Portal" to look off into the distance, or play a lite version of [Hearthstone](https://i.imgur.com/enl7sSI.png) with a deckhand.

Mechanically, this doesn't have to be hard to do, either. The player can board a boat, and instead of teleporting directly to the other continent, they are teleported to an "in-between" ocean only map for the duration of the journey. After a set amount of time, they are teleported to the other continent, just like normal. Doing it this way, in it's own map, also gives a lot more freedom as to what can be done in terms of scripted events. Maybe sometimes a Forsaken Battleship appears as a "Ghost ship", or a group of whales swim next to the ship.

Now, I get that not everyone wants to sit around for five minutes every time they want to get from Theramore to Menethil. So here’s the compromise:

![Image](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/c/cb/The_Half_Shell.jpg?900d38)

Introduce fast-travel alternatives in the form of Tuskarr-run “Clipper Ships.” These would function exactly like the current boats; instant port-to-port travel. They could be unlocked via a small questline, gold for the convience, like a Flightpath, or rep. Giving players the option to “opt-in” to fast travel.We could also make it really fit Turtle WoW’s theme, the ship model could be a downported version of the giant turtle boat from Wrath of the Lich King. Tell me that wouldn’t feel right at home. This way, players who want the speed can still have it, and those of us who enjoy soaking in the world get something richer and more immersive.

Anyway, just an idea, but one I think could really fit the spirit of Turtle.

Would love to hear what others think! Has anyone else wanted to just… drift across Azeroth for a while?
