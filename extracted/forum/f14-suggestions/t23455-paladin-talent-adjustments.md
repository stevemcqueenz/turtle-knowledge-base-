# Paladin Talent Adjustments

## Post 164103 by Zashferret — 2025-12-31T05:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164103#p164103 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Right now for Paladins, Retribution has a couple of talents that are generally passed up in pretty much every "build" I have seen that could use a bit more to make them appealing.

"Improved Blessings" is one such talent. the (4/8/12/16/20)% boost to the effect of the blessings is nice, but they still have a wet fart of a duration. Perhaps making the talent also increase the duration of each Blessing by 20% per point would make it more appealing? Or at least 10% per point?

The other talent that is underwhelming is "Improved Retibution Aura". A possible addition to make it more enticing could be "deals (25%/50%) of its damage when you Parry"?
