Can we please get a more feature-rich iteration of the Turtle WoW DB? Considering it is molded on the wowhead template, I cannot begin to fathom why we cannot narrow down search criteria in a way that makes sense given this server has unique and custom content.

Case in point: Twinking is more head-scratching as a result of not knowing what items are available to us here on Turtle which are not available on other servers. Yet if you want to find something for a specific armor slot, you're shit out of luck considering there's a 500 returned result limit and the most specific filter you can get on armor is what armor class it is (Cloth, Leather, Mail, Plate, Misc).

Can we please get some more filters added to the DB?
