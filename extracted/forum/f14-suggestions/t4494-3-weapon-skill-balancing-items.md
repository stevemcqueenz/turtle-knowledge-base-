# 3 Weapon Skill Balancing Items

## Post 26913 by Rokit (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-10-07T01:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26913#p26913 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[Concept]
You may have had many similar suggestions, but I hope the clarity and applicability of this one is worth your time.

Weapon skill is often a stat that players struggle to find sources of. It is very sparsely available at lvl 60 and very very rarely before. Many of the pieces that grant it are either tedious or highly contested with raider interest.

In beta, WoW had "Skill" as a random green enchantment. It was removed.

With 308 being the "ideal," in most cases, for player weapon skill use in raid; increments of 1-3 are the easiest to combine towards that cap, especially alongside the 5 skill racials of popular race choices.

Adding trinkets at these levels and rarities would allow players to experiment with and be exposed to Weapon Skill early in their journey and later on when combining early 60 gear sets / fine-tuning their gear options.

[Deployment]

These items would be added to world drops in level 32+ mobs. Each version would be added as level-appropriate, random MELEE weapon selecting, skill trinkets. This is the most basic way to let them percolate into the economy and playerbase.

Secondarily, they are worthy of inclusions in certain troll boss loot.

Chief Ukorz Sandscalp in Zul'Farrak could drop a random melee skill version of the lvl 45 blue trinket

Jammal'an the Prophet in The Sunken Temple of At'al Hakkar could drop a random melee skill version of the lvl 56 blue trinket

Bloodlord Mandokir in Zul'Gurub could drop two random melee skill versions of the lvl 56 blue trinket

[Barriers]

The primary implementation barrier for this would be adding the unavailable values of Increased Weapon Skill spells to the spell.dbc. I assume you all use a utility like Stoneharry's Spell Editor for this kind of thing, so this should be a fairly simple change to effect. For example: There is only +5 Polearm Skill and no other value in the base spell.dbc

This could really smooth out the disparity in weapon value and racial melee power in Turtle Wow. It is a very low labor option and could slide into the current game without much trouble via already established, lore-friendly loot.
