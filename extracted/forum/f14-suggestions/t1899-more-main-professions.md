Well I have to agree with Inve on this one.

To be able to have more professions would open up hundreds (if not thousands) of hours of progression. Yes you could make the argument that player interaction and trading would take a dip. But is that really such a big part of the game nowadays?

I know that I myself rarely trade with other people because it's more of a problem than fun. Try to convince a player (that's probably high level) to go to a low level zone, farm thick leather for 10 minutes so that he then can craft those leather pants that i want. And the price in gold that he would like to do so aint possible to pay by a character that needs them (if it is not an alt). And there are so many people like me, and that I know for a fact. We just rather skip it, saying it's not worth the hassle.

ppl just like to be self-sufficient, and I say let us. Leveling professions is fun in wow and it gives a very minor advantage in pve/pvp. So it wouldet desturb gameplay.

And if you still are concerned about the economy, meaby it could be implemented so that the big crafting cooldowns would invoke cooldown in all professions. For example if you transmute an arcanite bar you get a cooldown on mooncloth as well.

So in short, yes please let us have ALL professions at the same time, that would open up so many hours of quality game time for me and many more.

ps: And this would also ease up another playstyle you could run besides hardcore, “Lone wolf”. Level to 60 without using the bank, auction house or trade with people. OSRS (oldschool runescape) has a similar game mode and it's widely popular.
