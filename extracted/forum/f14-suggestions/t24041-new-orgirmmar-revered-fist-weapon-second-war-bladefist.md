I love that Turtle Wow has attempted to make fist weapons great again. With new additions and changes, changes to rogue talents and even an effect from warrior's Master Strike. I also love rep reward items and fist weapons so I thought I would create one.

I noticed there isn't a Fist Weapon that has the model of one of the most well known and Iconic fist weapons from early wow so I thought I would make one. And I know how much this server loved leaning into the Warcraft 1,2,2 roots so that inspired me to whip this up. This could also be a great RP item for those that like transmog and wanna RP a Shattered Hand Orc. I know that Elves already have some glaive options so it would be a cool addition for the horde.

Heres 4 ideas for unique effects the weapon could have, all in line with the spirit of the weapon:

Chance on Hit: In a whirlwind of steel you attack up to 4 enemies within 8 yards, causing weapon damage to each enemy.

Basically just a free cast of whirlwind on a chance on hit. Cool little proc for a first weapon useful for both warriors and rogues or even a cheeky shaman. Leaning into to the abilities you see enemies with this type of weapons use like sweeping strikes and whirlwind.

Chance on Hit: Your next 2 abilities and main-hand melee attacks strike an additional nearby opponent.

Basically a mini sweeping strikes on a proc. Since this is a mainhand weapon and only a blue for lvl 50, this shouldn't disrupt endgame in any way.

Chance on Hit: Perform a reckless swing that deals 350 damage to the target but also applies a bleed to the wielder for 100 physical damage.

Leaning into the berserk nature of the wielders of these weapons, and the fact that you not being a Shattered Hand orc and have not replaced your hand with it, are not as adept at wielding it as they would be. The proc is the same damage as the Pendulum of Doom proc, but on a 1 hander, but a later level item, and with a downside.

Equip: After killing an enemy that yields experience or honor you feel the rush of victory healing for 300 health.

Kinda leaning into the berserk nature of the weapon and its usual wielders give you a mini heal after getting the last hit on an enemy. Requires the actual last hit like other similar effects. Also a great leveling tool for a rogue or warrior that wants to use fist weapons.
