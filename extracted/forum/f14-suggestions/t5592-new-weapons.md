# New Weapons?

## Post 34604 by Andromath (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-12T02:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34604#p34604 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm curious if this has ever been brought up before, but has anyone ever wanted new weapons in WoW? I think in all the years Blizzard's been running the show, they only ever added Warglaives, which previously just existed as swords.

So are there any you'd want to see added to Turtle WoW?

Personally I can think of a few I would like to see.

Ioun Stones: (Ranged Weapon) Much like Kael'thas' verdant spheres. A stationary set of crystals floating around a character, firing off spell effects such as from wands. I don't think any animation would be necessary as far as the player character, whom could stand perfectly still for it, although maybe just the casting animation looped would work if needed?

Scythes: (One-Handed and Two-Handed) Akama used Scythes (well, sickles, but you get the idea), and it would make for a pretty nifty additional weapon type for Warlocks or Shadowpriests as well! The one-handed variety would probably work best with the sword-attack animation. The two-handed variety probably with greatsword animations? Thematically it feels like a weapon Forsaken would really like. Not just from a Grim Reaper kind of perspective either, but from salvaging bits of their old lives and what not.

Pistols: (Off-Hand Ranged Weapon) WoW has plenty of rifles, but a very limited variety of one-handed type of guns. It'd feel iconic to Nobles and Rogues alike. Not sure if it'd be possible to port the animation for it from retail Rogue skills involving them.

## Post 34607 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-12T04:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34607#p34607 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It can be difficult to imagine where other weapons can go, since the way things are arranged there's a lot of category overlap. Like hammers being classified as "Maces", and the [existing scythe](https://www.wowhead.com/item=20536/soul-harvester) in the game being a staff. I think that Ioun Stones *could* be a good weapon, but I could also see them being more in line with their D&D counterpart as a head slot item. If there were scythes that weren't staves, they would fit under Polearms. Pistols could easily fit into the existing Gun category.

That said...

- **Grenades (Consumable Ranged)** - We have throwing weapons. Why not have hand grenade weapons that work the same way, but are slower, and some have added effects like DoT, AoE, or elemental effects. It could also cover things like thrown potions.
- **Light Sword (Main Hand)** - Rapiers, Estocs, Dueling Sabers...any sword which is made for thrusting rather than slashing. Would make a good added proficiency for Rogues to start with, and be good for players that want to go for the swashbuckler/duelist aesthetic. Would probably have lower damage than other swords, but high speed and some innate armor penetration.
- **Hammer (One-Hand and Two-Hand)** - As mentioned, hammers and maces are folded together, but what if they weren't? What if hammers were a slower, higher damage weapon, and maces were - once again - better at armor penetration?
- **Spear/Glaive/Poleaxe (Two-Hand)** - How about polearms? What if *they* were split into multiple categories? Surely there's room in the game for a difference between polearms made for quick thrusts, ones made to crush armor, and ones made for broad sweeps. What if spears in particular had a one-hand version, so you could pair them with a shield?

It's an interesting idea to think about new weapons being added to WoW, especially for a private server like Turtle WoW. I agree that it seems like there hasn't been a lot of new weapon types added over the years, and it would be cool to see some more variety.

The Ioun Stones idea is particularly intriguing, as it would offer a unique twist on ranged weapons. It could also provide an opportunity to add more variety to spell effects and animations.
