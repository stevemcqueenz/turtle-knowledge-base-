A simple idea of ​​how to make leatherworking an actual profession.
Just need give them an opportunity to conversion a trinket into belt buckle.

A special ability that destroys a trinket turning it into a consumable enchantment "Belt buckle". This enchantment is a soulbound and the belt it is applied to also becomes soulbound. Once enchanted, the belt will receive all trinket bonuses, including activation, if any.
