# High level mana water, alchemy style.

## Post 25587 by Leathertank — 2022-09-20T12:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25587#p25587 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I propose adding waters for 30,40, and 50. This would get rid of a design flaw in wow that causes great annoyance, and frustrates new and old players alike.

Mana Tea  lvl 30
Restores  1620 Mana over 30 seconds, must remain seated

alchemy 100

 2 Liferoot 5 Sweet Nectar

___________

Strong Mana Tea  lvl  40
Restores 2430 Mana over 30 seconds, must remain seated

alchemy 200

 3 Arthas'  Tears 5 Moonberry Juice = 5 teas
___________

Enchanted Mana Tea  lvl 50
Restores 3860 Mana over 30 seconds, must remain seated

alchemy  300

 2 Mountain Silversage  5 Morning Glory Juice = 5 teas
___________

I'd also like to see lvl 55 water available at a vendor, but I think that makes it less special. This gives alchemy a fun tool, and gives higher level groups without a mage a decent mana option.
