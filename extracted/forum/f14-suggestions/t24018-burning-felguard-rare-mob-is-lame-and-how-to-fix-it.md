# Burning Felguard rare mob is lame and how to fix it

## Post 168817 by Phoenixphire (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-18T22:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168817#p168817 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

One of the coolest rare mobs in the game has got to be the Burning Fel Guard from LBRS.

A rare that can only spawn if you let the warlock trash get its portal off and sometimes it will come out with the rest of the demons.

Super cool concept. Heres the thing. Its drops are terrible and basically make it not worth even trying to get him to spawn to begin with. This is blizzards fault originally but Twow can fix it.

Here is what I suggest. Give it an item worth trying to make it spawn for. And something I noticed is a distinct lack of Warlock Items that effect their demons, and with Demonology being changed to be a real demon focused spec. What better boss to drop a Warlock demon themed item then a rare demon that can only spawn after summoned from a warlock portal?

 **Possible items:**

Fresh Felguard Eye:
Trinket
Rarity Epic
Class: Warlock

Possible effects:
 1. Use: Causes your Greater demon's (Infernal, Felguard, Doom Guard) attacks to hit 2 additional enemies for 50% of its damage for 20 seconds.
 2. Equip: Your Felguard's attacks deal fire damage.
 3. Use: Causes your demon to unleash a wave of shadow damage that scales with its current health (Like 25% of current health or something balanced. This would make it something you want to time correctly with Power Overwhelming to make sure you get max damage)

Fel Guard's chains:
Trinket
Rarity Epic
Class: Warlock

Possible Effect:
Equip: Increase the damage of all summoned demons and hunter pets in your group by X% (Something reasonable like 5 or 10. Could make for cool interactions with warlocks and hunters)

Fel Guard's True Name:
Trinket
Rarity Epic
Class: Warlock

Possible Effect:
Use: Summons a Burning Felguard to aid the caster for 30 seconds. (I know there are warlocks out there that would kill to have TWO demons out at once even if one is just a trinket minion. Really cool RP factor and if balanced well could be a decent little trinket summon. Could give it like an immolation aura or an immolate ability for spice. Kind of a meme option but who knows. The fun factor is def there. If you wanna be really spicy you could make it benefit from some Demo warlock talents too.)

This is all just kinda stuff off the top of my head. But seeing as my post about the Tempered Runeblade got some great feedback and ended up (maybe) inspiring the devs give it a make over, I thought I would post about another part of the game I feel is too cool to not be more interesting.

Thanks for reading! And let me know if you have any ideas on how to make this Rare more worth going for.
