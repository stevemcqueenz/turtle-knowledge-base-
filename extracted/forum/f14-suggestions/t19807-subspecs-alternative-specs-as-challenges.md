# Subspecs/Alternative specs as Challenges

## Post 135103 by Atolius — 2025-06-10T14:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135103#p135103 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I just had an idea about subspecs in general, and would like to suggest/begin to devise a few more below. This comes after the Battlemage subspec I posted previously:

[viewtopic.php?t=19773](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=19773)

Instead of modifying existing classes and causing imbalances, subspecs could be:

 - Challenges in themselves.
 - Intentionally designed to be more challenging than standard classes/specs.
 - Designed to be less powerful than the class/spec which performs the least in whatever role that subspec fills.
 - Potentially limited in the gameplay activities they can participate in. For instance, just like how Hardcore players can group with other Hardcore players, perhaps these subspecs could only group up and interact with other subspec characters.
 - While they add additional abilities to base classes that dramatically change the playstyle, they usually also have gimp mechanics thrown in, such as less baseline magical damage in the case of Battlemages, and even less when they wear heavier armors and shields.

The name "subspec" could be changed to something more appealing like "RPG Challenge Spec", "RPG Spec", or "Challenge Spec".

Here are some other subspecs I'm thinking about, though design help is needed because I mostly play Mage:

 1) Mage - Battlemage (Multitree DPS/off-tank)
 2) Warlock - Hexblade (Affliction Melee/off-tank DPS)
 3) Warrior - Gladiator (Protection DPS)
 4) Paladin - Templar (Protection DPS)
 5) Priest - Monk (Discipline Unarmed DPS)
 6) Hunter - Ranger (Marksmanship petless DPS)
 7) Rogue - Venomancer (Multitree spellpower rogue)
 8) Druid - Spider Form (Multitree Nature ranged DPS with acid spit, physical DPS with spiky legs, and control with webs)
 9) Shaman - Earth Warden (Enhancement Tank)

Rewards could be titles like "...the Battlemage" or "... the Earth Warden".

Is anyone else interested in this idea? If so, I could make more fleshed-out suggestions for more subspecs.
