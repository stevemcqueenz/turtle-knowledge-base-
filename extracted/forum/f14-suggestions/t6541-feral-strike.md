Thats right its me again with another idea to make melee hunters great ag... wait they were never great at all Rexxar would be ashamed!

But to the topic, Feral Strike. What the melee hunter lacks (aside from bad scaling and mechanics that requires u to dodge to use one of ur 2 melee ablilites) is the lack of abilities. Here comes Feral Strike an attack that alters with the pet u have in usage. So what do i mean?

Bear / Crocos / Turtles- thematically an armored companion with low dmg - Feral Strike: Reduces Armor of ur opponent by x
(counter weakness)

Cats / Owls - thematically a high dmg companion - Feral Strike: Inflicts bleeding dmg to ur target
(double down ond dmg)

Wolf / Boars - thematically the middleground on pets with melee buff abilities - Feral Strike: Increases dmg dealt of the player and the pet for x amount
(u buff me i buff u)

Serpents / Skorpids - thematically nature and poison dmg - Feral Strike: Inflicts x amount of nature dmg.
(again double down)

Just some ideas combined with good scaling and or high dmg could do the trick. Would make the pet choice more prominent for melee hunters. Could be in the beastmaster section of skills when aquiring it at a trainer but should not be talented to far down in the skilltree to make it accesible for a survival hunter. Maybe no need for a talentat all just an attack more to use.

I really love the idea ofc. i have no clue on the technical side of things. Combined with my thrwoing stings suggestion were coming closser to a half serious class here!
