## Post 28577 by Heimdallr (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-06T16:13:00Z

Hi,

As a Turtle fan that would like to get even more turtlin around on the server, I would like to suggest, perhaps it was suggested multiple times, but it's always for more visibility - Some new skills and more world PVP.

Starting from skills, I think we are fine and I love mu characters, but we do miss few things that could make game more into thematic builds.

Concept 1 - What if I want to be a High Elf Arcane mage? learn enchanting use all that secret arcane powers in PVP and PVE and...how many skills do I have? right. I would have to supplement myself. I am against tons of skills that just are there, or systems that would keep players distracted by doing "skillful" things, rather something to either swap existing spells like - you can have 5 arcane spells but in total there are 9 so choose wisely,  or just add few additional skills.

Arcane Moon kind of druid with Astral Moonking form? Sign me up! But when you limit that to 2 spells...that isn't really fun, you know where I am heading?

World PVP is something that I dearly miss, can we have more into that? I would really like to make tons of chars, twinks on certain levels and have fun with people. Not the type of corpse camping people that aren't any sort of threat to me, but concept of for example lone orc with a spear, protecting one part of a zone etc.I miss that and life taught me, that World PVP is the best PVP out there in games.

Why do we lack here? is it mechanical stuff, or some encouragement from community & Team? how could we make it real at least in some zones?

Pushing world PvP is a questionable decision for any server to make. The Server does already have warmode to promote open PvP. But the only way you can make enforced open PvP anything but a frustrating mess would be to implement sharding to ensure only equal amounts of players of equal levels can find eachother within the zones. Even then, being as alliance priest on the same shard as 5 gank happy undead rogues ain't a good experience.

Now granted some of my best experiences from wow in 2006 are open PvP related. But so are some of the worst ones by a longshot.

Wow classic release made it clear, not even the people who roll on PvP servers want open world PvP thats why the servers are mostly lobsided with 70+% population of one faction. The loosing faction on almost all of these server transfered off. I myself made the mistake and rolled on a PvP server for SoM and i would stay the hell away from any server that enforces open PvP. even when it offers some great new content.

PvP needs to be monitored and players need to have a fair chance, otherwise you just end up alienating a lot of the playerbase.

Doing a bad job at monitoring and cultivating worldpvp is no argument against its potential upsides. Pestland towers and Silithus never managed to drew players into it because its on a part of the map you have to specifically go to to do pvp and you don't get meaningful rewards for doing so. Any decent farmspot is a more well curated hotspot to draw people in and let them fight for dominance over the resources available.

But if you just add a zone with no structure or limitations on players, more often the numerically superior faction will just swarm it given the rewards are good enough. and very little the opposing faction can do makes a significant dent into that.  If you also target players below max level as audience for this environment you need to ensure they ain't being grieved by high level players or otherwise you just create a noobtrap zone where only people who don't know better and trolls go to (see strangleton ;))

Lake Wintergrasp, Ashran etc also showed in the past that fought over zones are most often suffering from a similar problem.
