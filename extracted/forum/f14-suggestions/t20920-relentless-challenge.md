# Relentless Challenge

## Post 145454 by Nikbis — 2025-08-14T15:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145454#p145454 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Maybe it's your breath or your know-it-all look, but something in you annoy your ennemies to the point they will never let go.
You cannot leave a combat by running away.
If you reach the threshold where an NPC would normally give up on you as a target and return to its original position, they will instead gain +10% bonus damage, won't pick another target (cannot be taunted), and gain +5% move speed every 15 secondes.
It's you or them.
