# Blacksmithing Reforged Weapon/Armor "Enchants"

## Post 50497 by Watterboy1 — 2023-06-18T20:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50497#p50497 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey there - I would love devs to consider some utility beyond the obvious blacksmithing endgame patterns like LHH / Titanics / Dark Iron / sharpening stones etc. added to the Weaponsmith / Armorsmith profession trees.

What I'm suggesting are some rare patterns that allow blacksmiths to "upgrade" weapons and armor that are of a certain quality and item level; let's say uncommon and rare quality items only.  The upgrades would take the place of enchants, for the sake of this exercise (so if you enchant the item, you would lose the upgrade in theory).

Ideally, the "enchants" would provide a percentage based boost to an item's weapon dmg / armor value and its stats.  For instance, reforging a pre-raid weapon like Dreadforge Retaliator would raise its quality to somewhere between a MC or BWL weapon - and of course the mats would require expensive high-end reagents like Arcanite Bars, Dark Iron bars, gems, etc.

The main reasons I am suggesting this is:
A. it would be nice to bring relevance back to iconic weapons like Arcanite Reaper, and see players actually incentivized to craft, carry-around and use them for reasons other than transmog.
B. another sort of catch-up mechanic for new level 60 players who don't exactly enjoy spamming the same 3-4 raids over and over, or maybe only have one or two items that they need to upgrade.
C. add even more variety and flavor to the gear people use. There may be instances where it actually makes sense to use a reforged item, rather than the equivalent raid-drop.
D. the same logic could be applied to tailoring and leatherworking professions - adds many more patterns for people to acquire, more profession work = more activity and socialization.

Thanks
