# Give Chromatic Gauntlets, Leggings and Cloak a bonus set (also rework Chromatic Cloak)

## Post 84261 by Neltharion (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-10T21:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84261#p84261 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was thinking to a single 3/3 bonus set which gives: "Chance on spell hit (around 10%) to increase Fire, Frost, Nature or Arcane damage by 15%.
Each school of magic has a specific effect:
Fire: deals fire damage every 3 seconds for 15s, burning the victim mana;
Frost: slows target's casting and speed movement by 10% for 5s;
Arcane: reduces target's spell resistance to all schools of magic by 20 for 30s;
Nature: stuns the target for 3s and when the stun falls off the target is disarmed for 5s.
These effects can proc only once every 1 minute."
Those 3 pieces are very expensive and hard to get their plans and they deserve something special.
The proc from spell would be intended to work with shaman's shocks, hunter arcane shot and paladin's judgment.
