# Add Battlemasters to Alah’Thalas

## Post 165395 by Jcarrill0 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-17T02:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165395#p165395 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Turtle WoW team,

I wanted to suggest adding Battlemaster NPCs to the High Elven capital of Alah’Thalas.

At the moment, Alah’Thalas appears to be missing a few key NPCs that are present in other major faction cities, particularly Battlemasters. Stormwind, Ironforge, and Darnassus all provide easy access to PvP queues, while High Elven players must currently travel elsewhere to participate.

Given that Alah’Thalas functions as the primary hub for High Elves on Turtle WoW, adding Battlemasters there would help bring it in line with the other major Alliance cities. This would improve convenience and consistency without impacting balance, as it simply mirrors access that already exists elsewhere.

From both a gameplay and immersion standpoint, it makes sense that a major High Elven city would maintain military liaisons or officers capable of organizing battleground efforts.

Thank you for your continued work on Turtle WoW, and for considering this suggestion.
