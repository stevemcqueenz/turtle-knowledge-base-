# I made Balance idols since you won't

## Post 164862 by Avereth — 2026-01-11T05:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164862#p164862 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Here you go, feel free to give them better names, tune the effects, and stop making 100 more Feral idols while there's basically nothing to put in a slot other casters can get wand stats in.

Idol of Inevitability - X% increased eclipse chance to Wrath and X% increased eclipse chance to Starfire
Idol of the Night - Starfire stun proc becomes the Nightfall debuff
Idol of Lunar Fire - Starfire stun proc replaced with damage over time effect
Idol of Fate - Increased buff proc chance for dot debuffs
Idol of Opportunity - Dot debuffs have a chance to not be consumed
Idol of Equilibrium - Your highest of nature or arcane spell damage values applies to both schools for damage calculations
Idol of Darkness - Eclipse and its debuff last X seconds longer
Idol of Affliction - Insect Swarm lasts X seconds longer, and Moonfire lasts X seconds longer
Idol of Fireflies - Your critical hits with spells apply Faerie Fire
Idol of Force - Your critical hits with spells apply X penetration to that spell school for X seconds
Idol of Reciprocity - You gain <Haste Effect> For X Seconds/Casts to Balance spells after removing a curse
Idol of Opportunity - Casting dots during eclipse increases their power/duration and reduces their mana cost
Idol of Starlight - Hurricane is replaced with an arcane damage area of effect spell
Idol of Omens - Eclipse now activates as the same element that caused it to proc
Idol of the Moonwell - Regenerate x% of your mana for x seconds when eclipse procs
Idol of Flexibility - You can cast Abolish Poison Rank 1 in Moonkin Form
Idol of Inversion - mobs immune to a school of damage take the opposite school's damage instead
Idol of Wracking - Starfire crits add 3 seconds to Moonfire, Wrath crits add 2 seconds to Insect Swarm(chance %?)

Idols now come with base stats to ensure parity with ranged equipment. They will typically fit with the build the idol is used for. Expect to see one or two base stats on every idol, along with a little feral AP,  armor penetration, spell penetration, healing, mana regeneration in combat, spellpower, or MP5.

Enchantments for Idols are now available in Hyjal for Bright Dream Shards and new materials from Timbermaw Hold!
The following enchantments can now be applied to Idols
 -1% spell crit
 -1% crit
 -1% haste
 -1% spell hit
 -1% hit
 -1% vampirism

Allow me to add some for each spec:

Idol or Stasis: While in Moonkin Form you no longer proc eclipse but all spell damage is increased by X% and all your damage over time effects last Y Seconds longer.

Idol of Feral Versatility: Increases all healing by 100% of your agility and all spell damage by 50% of your strength. Also increases all healing received while in cat form by 20%.

Idol of I Should be Baseline by Now: Rebirth can be cast out of combat with no cooldown or reagent and you can cast it while out of combat even if it is on cooldown.

Edit:

Idol of fairness: All your spells, abilities, talent points and PvP set bonuses now work indoors. You do not need to equip this idol to gain this effect so long as it is in your inventory or bank and stacks with any other idol you have equipped.

Idol of the battle chicken: While in Moonkin form, mana regeneration is halted, melee for mana regeneration is increased, melee hits proc thorns on the target, spells can be cast while moving or auto attacking, and all threat generated is reduced by 20% while within melee range of the NPC.
