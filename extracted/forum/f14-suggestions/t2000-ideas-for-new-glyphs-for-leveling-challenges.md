# Ideas for new glyphs for leveling challenges

## Post 11913 by Hamstercrack93 — 2021-09-01T18:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11913#p11913 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I absolutely love the difficulty that glyphs add to this server. It is something that I think can attract players for a long time, and also ensure player retention (for those that like leveling characters). With that in mind, I think there are a lot of fun opportunities in this regard. If a new glyph was added every few months, it would continuously give a reason to reroll and try a new type of challenge.
I have written down a few ideas, inspired by what players have done already plus some other suggestions.
Comments regarding the glyphs in Italic.

Glyph of the Unskilled
You will not be able to train any new abilities until you reach level 60. When you reach level 60 you gain all skills for free and receive a unique tabard as well as the title “the Patient Laborer”

Glyph of the Peaceful Gatherer
You will gain Experience (XP) from gathering Herbs and Ores as well as Fishing. You cannot gain XP any other way. Reaching level 60 will award the title “the Peaceful Gatherer” for this character.
Alternatively, divide into three: Glyph of the Peaceful Herbalist, Glyph of the Peaceful Miner and Glyph of the Peaceful Angler
Glyph of the Turtle would reduce any XP gain in half.
This would require quite a bit of fine tuning to have the appropriate XP gains. Suggestions below.

Fishing for XP
Fishing gives XP equivalent to Fish Item Level = 8*iLvL/LOG(iLvL)
To reach level 60 you need to kill around 16 300 mobs at the character's level. The above formula requires around 21 200 fishes.
The issue is that there are gaps in iLVL for fish of 10, meaning that the standard formula for calculating XP for a kill does not work (only allows 7 level difference). Therefore a standard formula needs to be used.
An option would be a new reference table where the XP for level difference is changed, so that ZD in the calculation below allows for XP to be gained at 10 steps of level difference
XP = (Base XP) * (1 - (Char Level - Mob Level)/ZD )

Herbalist or Miner for XP
XP is gained through the following formulas
Yellow herb or ore = (Char Level * 5) + 45, this is BASE
Green = BASE * (LOG(level) +1)
Orange = BASE * (1 + (1 / LOG (level +1))
This would give a boost to the first few levels (as you gotta run and get the skill, you gain a bit) but thereafter the time to level ramps up quite significantly. Most of the time ores or herbs will be green. Gathering only greens means over 40 000 herbs or ores, making it a monumental task. That said, yellow is equivalent to mob kills and orange would require about 12 000, but that is very difficult to accomplish.

Glyph of the Pacifist
You are not allowed to kill any living being until level 60. If you kill anything, you have failed the life of peace. Reaching level 60 without any kills will award the title “the Pacifist” for this character.

Glyph of the Naked
You cannot equip any armor until level 60. Reaching level 60 awards the title “the Naked” for this character.
Alternatively, “the Nudist”

Glyph of the Pugilist
You cannot equip any weapons or shields. Reaching level 60 awards the title “the Pugilist” for this character. Only available to Warriors, Rogues, Paladins and Hunters.
 *Possibly, a transmog to hide all upper body armor plus turning weapon & shields into simple fistweapons*

Glyph of the Powerless Caster
You cannot cast any spells until level 60. Reaching level 60 awards the title “the Warcaster” for this character. Only available to Mages, Warlocks, Priests, Druids and Shamans. At 60 you learn a new spell, Armor of the Powerless Caster, which makes your character glow for 60 minutes.
 *The spell is a cosmetic buff*

Glyph of the Necromancer
Until you reach level 60, any humanoid you kill will raise up as an undead that you have to fight. You gain no XP from the kill. At level 60 you get the spell “Raise Dead” that allows you to summon three undead pets to attack your foes. You also gain the title “the Necromancer”.
 *The risen dead from kills has HP based on the character level. Always a yellow mob. The pet you can summon with a spell has 1 HP each and just attacks nearby enemies. It’s not a pet or under player control. It’s a cosmetic effect. It has no cool down.*

Glyph of There Is A Cow Level
You will gain Experience (XP) from killing cows, nothing else. You never gain less than 5 XP per cow. At level 60 you get the title “the Cow Slayer” and a cow mount. Furthermore a new cow will be added to the game with the characters name.
 *A cow in Elwyn Forest is relaxed with a new one that has a unique name. It counts as a regular cow and new cow slayers get XP from it.*

Glyph of Brittle Bones
You and your pet take double damage from all sources until you reach level 60. At level 60 you receive the title “the Unbroken”.

Glyph of the Bloodless
If you take any damage before you reach level 60, you will die instantly. Damage done to pets does not count. You can get this glyph until level 10. At level 60 you get the title “the Unhurt” and a unique tabard.
 *Recolor of the Defilers tabard?*

Another idea is class glyphs with specific challenges for each class that give a unique playstyle.

For example:

Glyph of the Barbarian
Only usable by warriors. You have endless rage but take twice the damage and cannot equip any armor except for a loincloth (provided). At level 60 rage, armor and damage works as normal. If you reach level 60 you get the title "the Barbarian" and a stylish loincloth.

Glyph of the Wand Chooses the Wizard
Only usable by mages. You can only use a wand and cast spells that do not cause any damage. At level 60 spells work as normal. If you reach level 60 you get the title "the Wand Weaver" and a 20% damage bonus when using wands.

Glyph of the Witch
Only usable by warlocks. You cannot summon any demons and only use offensive spells that are Curses. There is a risk that a witch hunter will spawn and attack you when in a major city. At level 60 spells work as normal. If you reach level 60 you get the title "the Witch", a black cat pet and a broom mount.

Glyph of Chosen by the Moon
Only usable by Druids. During daytime you cause half damage, movement speed is halved and you receive no XP. At night, your damage is 20% higher, movement speed 20% higher and XP is normal. If you reach level 60 you get a pet moon that is usable only at night, a unique tabard and the title "the Nightwalker"
