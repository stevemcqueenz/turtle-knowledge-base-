The most recent [patch 1.16.1](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=3334) has made some insanely bold changes - such as bringing back from TBC into vanilla such spells as Hex and Feral Spirit for the Shaman, or the Felguard for the Warlock, or the Tree form for the Druid. The Brilliance Aura is nice and all, but Slow would be a much better counterpart for PvP.

Also, again, I'm not sure as to the balance, but Cyclone is such an iconic spell, all the way to the Druid of the Talon from WC3 - and it was only added in TBC. Thoughts?

To clarify my view, I don't mean the introduction of later expansions' mechanical changes to the classes' gameplay. Thus, no Hot Streak procs from WotLK for Fire, no higher damage from unused mana for Arcane from Cata.

What I would like is the introduction of flavourful, iconic abilities. Aside from the arguably more controversial Slow mentioned above, I would settle for Spellsteal and Invisibility - namely, the harder to use Invisibility from TBC, which made the caster blind, unable to see any players or NPCs (and it would make the Warlock spell Detect Invisibility more relevant).

I would also like it to be tied to quest chains - especially because Mage is genuinely one of the few classes which never had any class quests, aside from the cosmetic Turtle/Piggie Polymorph spells acquired in Azshara.

And if anyone would like to mention Water Elementals - I'm not sure whether it could work. Reworking the Frost talent tree would needlessly buff everything about the mage (baseline Ice Block in TBC, etc.). If anything, here's my brainstorm.

Summon Water Elemental - available to all specs at lvl 60, 20 min cooldown, 5 sec cast time, 60 sec duration, may have access to Frost Nova.

Something like this - not too intrusive, a gimmick with overly long cast time & cooldown, almost impossible to summon in combat, not essential for any DPS, but fun to use when it's out.

I hope I'm not breaking any rules by extending this thread, merely writing new ideas when I think of them.

There are arguably two issues with Destruction Warlocks:
 1) they don't use Fire spells in PvE;
 2) they don't have any green fire spells.

Issue (1) was partially tackled in TBC with the introduction of Incinerate, issue (2) was approached with the addition of Chaos Bolt in WotLK.

 *There is an [anecdote by Xelnath](https://xelnath.com/2016/09/08/post-mortem-i-warlocks-in-wow-alpha-classic/), one of the original Warlock designers:*
> Mike: Well, partially, Metzen really wanted Warlocks to be casting green fire spells, instead of red ones, but we didn’t have the time to get them into the schedule, and I was concerned it would confuse players to the school of magic they needed to defend themselves from.

So, all this could be used as a base of reasoning if Turtle would ever like to add Chaos Bolt as a 31-point talent in the Destruction tree, next to Conflagrate. It could either have a requirement of Emberstorm, or Intensity>Pyroclasm. Or maybe the largely-unused [Improved Searing Pain](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock/0-0-5U0UCWV1) (5 points) to make it more of an investment to get.

Chaos Bolt 3 sec cast 12 sec cooldown [1 Soul Shard because vanilla]
Sends a bolt of chaotic fire at the enemy, dealing {> Shadow Bolt} to {< Soul Fire} Fire damage. Chaos Bolt cannot be resisted, and pierces through all absorption effects.

Again, it's a really flavourful ability that would benefit firelocks and bring green fire into the game (without being confused with Nature spells :P).
