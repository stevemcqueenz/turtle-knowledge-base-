Some reasonable suggestions here, thanks for the folks who responded to my comment above.

I think the idea of pets having specific roles and filling those roles is a great idea. That's changed my perspective a bit into this idea of giving more pets a 1.0 attack speed. If we define roles for pets, then update pets to fit into those roles, it would be great.

 -Caster PvP
 -Physical dmg PvP
 -PvE DPS
 -PvE tank/offtank
 -Support/buffs for PvE (better howl for wolves, for example)
 -PvP & PvE debuffs (like screech)

Maybe there's other roles, but if at least 2 types of pets could be viable in each of those categories, that'd be awesome. It would really make the variety of pets a lot better and make choosing a pet more fun.
