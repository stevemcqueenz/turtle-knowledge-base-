In game, the throne room of Ironforge has "Senator Barin Redstone" imply the existance of a senate, wich is not show ingame, adding a senate to Ironforge gives the opportunity to show the importance of clans inside Ironforge, and the political machinations. Now in game this can be translate it with a senatorial room, this could be located nortwest or southeast of the throne room, two spaces with not much use. now the utility of this place will be for questing, clans giving quest to retrieve family heirlooms from historical battle zones like burning steppes, discover a conspiracy of noble clans with the dark iron dwarves to assasinate the king, giving the anvilwar clan relevancy beyond an statue, a place for the rebrand clan to have more quest related to them, as for senator ryedol to have a place, instead of a corner of the throne room, etc.

conclusion, adding a senate to Ironforge, gives a more lore precise depiction of the city, at the same time that gives the opportunity to add more quests and move npcs to places more appropriate to them.
