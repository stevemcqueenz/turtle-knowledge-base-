# Tankadin: Blessing of Sacrifice

## Post 5528 by Nemain — 2020-03-26T11:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5528#p5528 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Warning: this IS, quite blatantly, a so-called "buff this spec I'm using" type of thread. That said... Let's get right down to it.

Paladins make for great tanks- at least, for 5-man content: taken into raids, they struggle for three reasons everyone can easily list off the top of their head:

 **1.- Lack of a taunt.** Overeager DPS aside, which can be fixed by the DPS themselves understanding what tank they have and how to adapt to their raid configuration, there's some mobs that wipe aggro, and taunt mechanics become more and more important the more one gets into raid content. This is a hard wall for aspiring tankadins.

 **2.- Lack of defensive cooldowns:** True, paladins have bubbles... Which they can't really use in a group as tanks. They also have a VERY nice cooldown in Lay of Hands... But since they can use it on someone else tanking for them, it's added to the list of cooldowns for another tank, not for the tankadin himself. All they have is Holy Shield, and it alone can't quite bridge the gap. That said, I see this as more of a "soft wall": given enough in-built aggro, and gear availability, all a tankadin would need to do is gear up far more defensibly than their warrior counterparts, since they'll be taking more damage.

 **3.- Lack of itemization:** Something druids and Shamans feel as well: Blizzard simply did not make tankadin-friendly gear for raid content.

Of these three, I'd like to address the first concern, with a "small" re-working of an already existing and completely underused spell in the paladin repertoire: the blessing of Sacrifice. In paper, it's cool: makes someone in your team live longer, at the cost of your own hp, and you need to refresh it every so often if you want to keep on maintaining it... The problem is, it doesn't really get used in practice, because there's little benefit to it. As a healer, you want to heal that target, not damage yourself. As a tank, you have access to Sanctuary: a far better version of it.

So, my suggestion is... To pack the later down the line received "Righteous Defense" and "Hand of Reckoning" into this particular spell: it already re-directs a small amount of damage from player A to player B, so how about making it also re-direct all threat, of half of it? Tankadins are already relying on teammates to pull for them- so how about, instead of applying salvation on that overly high damage high threat dps, making him help you generate aggro instead? Yoinking that fury warrior's taunt so that he can taunt for you, who lack the spell, yourself?
