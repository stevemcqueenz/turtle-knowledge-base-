# Suggestion: Add an NPC for Individual Tabard Customization

## Post 161417 by Kanin — 2025-12-03T10:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161417#p161417 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Turtle WoW Team and Community,

first of all, thank you for all the great work you put into the server. I really enjoy the unique features and the attention to detail.

I would like to suggest adding an additional NPC next to the existing Guild Tabard Designer. This new NPC could allow players to create and customize individual (personal) tabards, independent of guild tabards.

This could include:

Custom colors and symbols for personal use

A small gold fee for each design change

No link to guild affiliation, purely cosmetic

This feature would give players more freedom to express their character’s identity, especially for those who are not in a guild, roleplayers, or players who simply enjoy cosmetic customization.

I believe this would fit very well into Turtle WoW’s philosophy of expanded customization and immersion.

Thank you for reading, and I’d love to hear what the community thinks!

Best regards,

Kanin
