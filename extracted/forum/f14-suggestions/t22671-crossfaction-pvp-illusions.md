## Post 157886 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-30T22:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157886#p157886 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Since cross-faction is imminent, I think a simple solution to avoid breaking the immersion would be for players who switch factions to gain an illusion of battleground duration equivalent to their race.

Example:

Human - Orc
Dwarf - Undead
Gnome - Troll
Night Elf - Tauren
High Elf - Goblin

This would maintain some immersion without feeling like the cheap and poorly executed solution it is.

I get what you’re saying about RP taking a hit in PvP with cross-faction — it definitely changes the dynamic when Alliance can face Alliance and Horde can face Horde. That said, I think there are ways to preserve some immersion even with the system in place.

For example, using the Orb of Deception effect (transforming your character to look like the opposing faction for the duration of the BG) could help maintain the visual identity of factions. When you’re on the Alliance side, everyone appears as Alliance; when on Horde, everyone appears as Horde.

It’s not a perfect solution — the underlying mechanics still allow cross-faction fights — but at least it keeps the look consistent, which can help maintain some RP feel even in PvP.

I think it’s worth considering as a compromise between faster BG queues and keeping a bit of that faction flavor alive.
