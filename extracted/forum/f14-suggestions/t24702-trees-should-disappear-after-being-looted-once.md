# Trees should disappear after being looted once.

## Post 173956 by Ellifta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-04-03T22:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173956#p173956 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I'm sure a lot of you have come across trees like this during your survival leveling adventures where you kill several mobs to chop down a tree, only to find out it's actually a piece of feather instead.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/sJq9Hz5x/tree.png)

I suggest that trees should despawn if someone loots anything from them even if they didn't take everything so that we don't have fake trees around.
