I consider not having a standard revive part of the class identity of a Druid.
I don't deny the convenience. I don't deny that forcing fellow dungeoneers to corpsewalk can be frustrating in certain situations. But... that's part of being a Druid in my mind. It just feels right to me. I enjoy these small little differences between the classes, and I don't want them to disappear for the sake of convenience.

> **Gladeshadow wrote: Tue May 27, 2025 9:50 pm**
> The “need” for a normal resurrection spell sound like a… ahem… skill issue.

This is a harsh way of putting it, but... yeah, it kinda is. Not having a standard revive forces druid dungeon healers through a very hard school where mistakes are punished harshly. And I truly believe that this hard school will ultimately make a better healer out of you. You just can't let people die where a priest or a paladin might triage someone because they can always bring them back later.
I have levelled a Slow & Steady druid mainly as a dungeon healer. And I have never felt any discrimination against me based on class. Nobody was annoyed that I could not revive them. The rare cases someone died they just did the corpse walk like champs. But mostly if things went south it was me dying anyway (either as part of a wipe or from heal aggro since we have no way to shed aggro), so having a res would not have done any good there.
