# Tel'abim: Perma-flag lvl 60 players for pvp in cities

## Post 91094 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-03-21T21:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91094#p91094 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

(I have made two separate posts where this is one of other suggestions but due to it's urgency I think it now deserves its own post)

The past months have seen groups from both horde and alliance attacking SW and OG in turns. This has been good fun and appreciated by both sides. It adds alot to the liveliness of our server and is a key aspect of world pvp.

A reoccurring problem however is that players from the defending faction can not be attacked until they attack themselves due to not being pvp-flagged. This adds an unfair advantage since they can approach us untouched, without crowd control spells etc working on them.

Members from several guilds agree on the discord channel that lvl 60 players should be permanently flagged for pvp, even in cities, to remove this unfair advantage and incentivize city raids further. Therefore, I suggest to permanently pvp-flag lvl 60 players in all zones, including cities.

As a compensation for the players who absolutely do not want to be a part of this, one city could be left unchanged. I suggest Undercity and Darnassus.

Another suggestion is to do this over a trial period of say 3-4 weeks. That would give you developers and the players time to evaluate.

Regardless, I hope you can consider deciding on this **urgently** and give a yes or no answer whether you plan to implement it.

Thanks, again, for hosting the server!
