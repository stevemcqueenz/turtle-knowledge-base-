# Please make BoP recipes in mid-level dungeons more available

## Post 152377 by Uppercrust — 2025-09-19T12:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152377#p152377 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, with the new dungeons added to the server came new mid-level crafting recipes, such as [Pattern: Dragonmaw Armor Kit](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=69), and [Plans: Steel Belt Buckle](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=70). This is good, more mid-level crafting options is good. However, a number of them drop exclusively off individual dungeon bosses, at a very low rate, and are bound on pick-up. This is not necessarily a bad thing in high level dungeons, because people tend to run those over and over again, be it for materials, rep grinding, raid attunements, or now weekly quests. However, most people don't run low- or mid-level dungeons more than once or twice per character, which means the vast majority of people will never see these recipes, nevermind getting them on a character that can use them.

I realize the intent is probably to make people more interested in visiting this new content, but making people run a mid-level dungeon 20+ times in order to get a specific recipe cannot possibly be the intended user experience. Please, either increase the drop rate, add them to the loot table of more NPCs, or make them tradeable.
