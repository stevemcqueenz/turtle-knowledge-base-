# Transmogrification

## Post 140457 by Boneslord — 2025-07-19T11:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140457#p140457 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!
I would like to start out this letter of suggestion by first acknowledging the team that has given us the opportunity to play on possibly the greatest version of WOW there has ever been, this is by no means a glaze. Its a thought that pops up in my mind everytime i enter the character selection and i am sincerly greatful for all the effort yall put in. Truly, thank you.

For about 10 or more years now the vision of the character i wanted to build if i ever came back to this game again, was that of a rogue or a warror wielding fist weapons. Great, bad or meta. It didnt matter. It just looks badass and unique!!
I was so delighted to see you had added a fairly substantial amount of new fist weapons and my excitement grew even more.

Now i've reached level 60 on my HC rogue, and my dream is coming into fruition. I recently grinded for an item in Hyjal, by myself, only taking breaks to chat with the Rodfathers turtle Emiga once in awhile, which i will sherish. That i know.

But eventually i reached my goal and obtained the 150 Bright Dream Shards i needed and grasped with my fists that glorious Stagwood Grasp fist weapon. I must have been a child the last time i felt this much joy from a videogame.
My main hand weapon couldnt compare to my now mighty off hand weapon Stagwood Grasp, wielding two small spikes on my right hand, and the head of a dragon on my left. So of course the first thing i do is Hearthstone to Stormwind so i can transmog the Stagwood Grasp unto my other hand like i had imagined it all this time, grinding, almost dying countless times. But to my despair, i find out. Its not possible.

So now i come here pleading, can we please have the opportunity to transmog our weapons based purely on the weapon skill of the item, regardless of it being a one-handed or a off-handed weapon. So many new doors would open!! As someone who has spent their entire time in this game focused on obtaining items and planning how i'm going to look, the blow i felt when i realized this restriction was in place felt so unnecessary.

If you took the time to read all this i'm grateful but my dream of dual wielding those glorious dragon heads would make me the happiest man on the server. And i see no harm in removing this current restriction. I see only new doors being opened, excitement and the server rejoicing together.

Thank you again for your time. Hope you have a wonderful and blessed day, night whichever it might be.
Best Regards Skippin <3
