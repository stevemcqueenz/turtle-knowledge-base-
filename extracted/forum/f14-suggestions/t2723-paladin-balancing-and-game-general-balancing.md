# Paladin Balancing and game general balancing

## Post 17080 by Skandergod — 2022-02-17T16:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17080#p17080 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all!, im skandergod and i do main paladin in WoWTurtle, i have decided to make this post to make a list of different possible changes and ideas you guys might want to hear for the class, most if not all of this ideas come from the discord, there we ramble a lot about paladin balance and ways to make the class feel better in general, from making it a competitive tank to giving retribution mechanics to make feel more of a high skill = high reward class, this post is made with honest and good intentions to make the class feel a lot better for both players and raid members without losing the essence of the class or making it feel like a caster warrior if that makes sense, so please bare with me, so here is the list:

Protection:

Here we have many suggestions, we dont know what if the turtle wow team would like to make the paladin a viable main tank or keep it in the niche of off-tanking/AoE-Tanking, but you guys can pick and analize any suggestion and play around those concepts:

 - Argent Defender: When you have less than 40%, you receive 25% less damage and you get +5% stamina (scaling with gear)
 - Reckoning: gives you a 20/40/60/80/100 chance to gain an extra attack after being a victim of a critical strike OR 2/4/6/8/10% after being struck by the enemy
 - (A)  Judgement from Seal of Justice: Holy Damage the judged target takes from the caster now generates extra threat to the caster and the target cannot flee( removing the stun chance completly or changing )
 - Shield Specialization: Increases the amount of damage absorbed by your shield by 10%/20%/30% and has a 100% chance to restore 1/2/3% mana when a block occurs.
 - One-Handed Weapon Specialization: Increases the Damage you deal by 2/4/6/8/10% if you have a one-handed weapon equipped
 - if (A) is not fine or the wow turtle team doesn't want to change SoJ that dastrically:
 - (New) Seal of Fury: Your auto-attacks will now deal 75% holy damage based of your weapon damage and will generate extra threat, This effect only works if you have a 1h weapon equipped
 - Judgement from Seal of fury: when judged the caster taunts the target and Holy Damage the judged target takes from the caster now generates extra threat to the caster( removing the stun chance completly or changing )
 - Convert Divine Protection into a Defensive Cd inspired in shield wall with a long CD (1 hour at most).
 - Another idea would be make it like Deterrance but instead of parry, it can give 100% chance to block attacks
 - Adding a librarm that increases the number of charges on holy shield
 - Making an improved holy shield talent that gives you an extra attack everytime you block

OK, Now here comes the controversial part, Please remember again, this changes proposed with good intentions and for PvE purposes

Retribution:

Vengence: Gives you a 3/6/9/12/15% bonus to physical and Holy Damage, for 8/10/12/14/15 sec after dealing a critical strike from a weapon swing, spell, or ability

Seal of Command: Gives the paladin a chance to deal holy damage equal to 70% normal weapon damage (as holy damage if the target is not a player). (increasing a bit more the % were this can proc)
Unleashing this Seal's will judge an enemy, instantly causing X to Y Holy damage scaling with weapon damage (removing the stunned bonus so it applies also when the target is not stunned at all)

Two-Handed Weapon Specialization: Increases the damage you deal with two-handed meele weapons by 3/6/10%

(B) Crusader strike: (Move it so it becomes the last talent of the retribution tree instead of Repentence) Make it so the extra holy damage becomes a buff for you and not a debuff on the enemy.

Exorcism: Now can also be used on humanoids And Dragonkin, dealing less damage and leaving a DoT on the target.

Holy Strike: Cd is now 8 secs instead of 10s

(C) Re-implement Seal twisting

if (B) cannot be implemented, we propose to have the debuff limit removed and increasing the bosses hp in raiding enviorments by a x%.
We believe that (C) would be a nice way to make the paladin a high risk high reward class with the seal twisting mechanics, we know really well what this constant twisting will do to mana.

Most of paladins just want the class to feel better and fun to play, not being death weight in raiding or just another dispeller and we believe those changes could be a fun way to make protection and retribution work.
