The war ended with the victory of the Scourge. resurrecting the dead Nerubians, increasing the power of the Scourge (in the game they can be found mostly in the eastern plague lands under the name Crypt?). their best-known member is Anub'arak, who helped Arthas reach Ner'zhul on his second Northrendi journey.
