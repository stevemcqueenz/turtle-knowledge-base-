# New Dungeon: Dawning Wood Catacombs.

## Post 114074 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-23T23:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114074#p114074 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've always loved this place in Duskwood (Ravenhill Cemetery), where you go down a big hole in the middle of the graveyard, it kinda reminds me of in Zelda Ocarina of Time, where you go down the crypt in the graveyard.

Anyway, theres a cool little area under the the Ravenhill Cemetery,  with some Zombies lurking around, then you find a secret passageway that looks like an underground tunnel with loads of tree roots and such, which then leads to the otherside of the graveyard and comes out behind a smashed wall in the Crypt.

Id love if they expanded on this, and made an entrance to a deeper part of the Catacombs, and fill it with Undead and Ghouls, It could be a level 28-33 dungeon for Duskwood.

Anyway Duskwood is my favourite zone and just thought it would be cool to have its own dungeon for lower levels.
