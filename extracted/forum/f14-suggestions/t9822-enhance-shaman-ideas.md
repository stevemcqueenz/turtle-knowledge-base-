# Enhance Shaman Ideas

## Post 64151 by Wormrider — 2023-09-15T01:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64151#p64151 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I have been playing Enhance Shaman for many months and enjoyed all the new changes from last patch. All three races that can be Shaman are great. I am fine with not being a dps powerhouse, which enhance shamans should not be. Enhance shamans are pure support and should excel at that. Here are just a few ideas to help the class be not looked down upon for not healing.

 1) Upgrade the totem enhancement talents. You could do this by reducing by nerfing the base totems and buffing them much higher with the talents. Maybe instead of just giving 15% bonus to 77 (laughable +11 extra) you make it like +30. Also make the WF totem like +3 swings instead of +2 swings 1 level deeper into the talent tree.

 2) I have enjoyed bloodlust, but feel I am not using it to the max capability.  The Bloodlust should be made into a fire totem and provide only like 4%/8%, but have no CD. This would make alot more people happy to have enhance shamans.

 3) Move the Natures Guidance +hit talent out of the heal tree. I hate the fact it is there and does not flow well. It should switch places with Elemental Warding to provide more survivability to healers or with Guardian Totem to reduce damage to group. Either fit better with healer theme.

 4)  I would even be ok with changing the Nature's guidance to be super deep into the enhance tree, but have it add +3 hit and use the larger of the +hit total stats for both Spell and melee. I like the feel of a melee using shocks as part of the dps rotation in raids. You could combine the lackluster Weapon Mastery talent to include +hit combined. It is kinda lame that Shamans are pretty much the main class that get screwed with needing 2 separate +hit stats on gear to be viable.

Thanks for reading,
Wormrider
