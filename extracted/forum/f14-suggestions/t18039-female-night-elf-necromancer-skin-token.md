I do like the vast amount of skin tokens in the Turtle WoW Donation Shop. However, many are gender specific. While many are probably limited to the textures in game, I do want to point this particular one out.

![Image](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1149051412794322964/1351337360461135872/NecromancerNighElf.png?ex=67da027d&is=67d8b0fd&hm=752574e76b266cd4b70fef37104d7e0e93b733b16eda6b4099d667cd307558a1&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)

I believe this texture is used in Scholomance. I found it surprising that a Necromancer Night Elf Token exists, yet Females cannot use the token. This can be added so Females may use the token.

In my view, some of the positives would include:
- Having a new Skin for your character.

- Utilizing the existing Necromancer Skin Token.

- The Turtle Team will get some income for those who buy it.
However, I also believe:
- The Skin Texture is little unpolished.

- It feels more like an Undead Highborne than Undead Night Elf.
What are your thoughts? Do you think we should have a Necromancer Female Night Elf Skin Token? Thank you for reading, and good luck on your journey.   smiling_turtle_head
