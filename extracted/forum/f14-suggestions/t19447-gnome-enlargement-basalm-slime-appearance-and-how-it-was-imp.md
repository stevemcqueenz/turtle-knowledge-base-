Now that I have your attention, I would like to draw attention to the recently deleted item from the game **Gnome Enlargement Basalm**. While I do not know why it was deleted and will not argue with its deletion, I would like to make a case for adding a similar item to the game that continues to allow your character to have the appearance of a slime which should confer no PVP or PVE benefit as it is only used in roleplay.

There were some RP characters (namely Splatricia) who roleplayed as slimes and were very near and dear to the RP community here on Nordanaar. They participated in realm events from Turtle Faire to random public ones - and encouraged people to find out how they were made as an alchemical creation. **Their RP brought a lot of joy to the community and it would be nice to see it continue if possible.**

A way to implement this perhaps is that the same vendor of the original product only has basalm that turns all people into slimes now (effective on every race in game). This would allow for RP to continue to be enjoyed by the community, and for the character of Splatricia to not have to exist as a high elf, *which would certainly put us into a cursed timeline!*

I appreciate the time devs take to answer as well as look over these suggestions, and want to thank Lasciel (peacebrigade) who directed me to post to the forums for this hopeful quick fix! Thank you!
