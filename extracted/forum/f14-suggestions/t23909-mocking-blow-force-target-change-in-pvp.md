Why are we the priority target? You dont kill the healers first?

Warrior doesnt have any meaningful cc, beside the slow. A Taunt that would have to be applied tactically, is exactly what  would give the tank/warrior a strong tool to protect their group, to actually be able to play the warrior with more finesse instead of the ever dull "i hit you dead" tactics we have to immerse in in pvp.

Also, giving taunt the abilities the effect they have in other parts of the game, it would make space for a bigger variety of talentspeccs and give lots of talents more meaning in pvp.

What it would practically mean:
Taunt:
Forces the target to target and keep the warrior in target for 3 seconds

Mockin Blow:
Forces the target to target and keep the warrior in target for 6 seconds

Challenging Shout:
Forces all targets to target and keep the warrior in target for 6 seconds
