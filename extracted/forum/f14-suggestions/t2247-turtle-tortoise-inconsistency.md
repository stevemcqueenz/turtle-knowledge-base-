# Turtle/Tortoise Inconsistency

## Post 13997 by Rorhn — 2021-11-19T03:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13997#p13997 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’d like to suggest making the riding turtle ride in water. Currently the behavior of the riding turtle is consistent with other mounts where upon entering water you become dismounted. However, dismounting in water while riding it gives the impression that it is more comfortable on land than in water, which more aligns with a tortoise than a turtle. While consistency is key we are faced with an impossible decision of consistency across all mounts, or consistency with the nature of an animal, namely the turtle here.
