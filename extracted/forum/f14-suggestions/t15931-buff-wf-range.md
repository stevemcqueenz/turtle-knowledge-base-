# Buff WF range

## Post 108450 by amanagor (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-18T09:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108450#p108450 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Windfury range has been drastically nerfed. Previously the totem gave a 10second buff that would refresh every 5 seconds. Meaning that if the totem was destroyed or outranged, the buff  would persist for 5 seconds at minimum. The thing is, players move at 7 yards per second at normal running speed meaning that someone outranging the totem by running in a straight line, would be able to travel an extra at minimum 35 yards while keeping the buff. (much more than that if the buff was just refreshed, or if the player had faster movement due to mount, charge, intercept, speed boosts etc...) Meaning the effective range of WF totem was far greater than the 30 yard (with talent). Currently as the totem gives an aura, the buff is instantly lost when the totem is outranged, or when it is destroyed.

Suggestion: either increase the range of the totem to make up for this change of mechanics, or make the aura function how the buff used to by giving it a grace period before it fades.

Side note: I find it insane that this massive nerf to WF utility wasn't even mentioned in the changelog.
