I agree that this goes against the spirit of Vanilla, and it's exactly these small quality of life changes that led many of us to dislike Retail.

That said, if such an item were ever added to the store, it should only exist as a consumable with a short duration—around 5 minutes—and be fairly expensive. Still, I sincerely hope it never makes its way into the game.

Some might argue that capital cities, with their vast resources, should offer these conveniences. However, we need to consider two key points. First, having crafting requirements within dungeons or raids is an important game mechanic that adds depth, much like the Dark Iron Forge in BRD, which other professions also benefit from. Second, places like Scholomance and Blackwing Lair had more advanced technology than the Alliance or Horde capitals in that specific knowledge during the Vanilla era, which justifies the lack of such conveniences in the cities.
