# Keep Cross Faction talk in Say(/s) for the EU PvP

## Post 70989 by Dethknot — 2023-10-15T17:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70989#p70989 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A fun part of the original turtlewow server was to be able to talk between factions especially in W-PvP settings for Role-play purposes because you can yell a warcry and ask a high level to not gank you.

In the Warcraft lore, cross factions are able to communicate face to face and players should be able to as well.
