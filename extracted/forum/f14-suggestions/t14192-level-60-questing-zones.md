# level 60 questing zones

## Post 96788 by Gingerale01 — 2024-06-11T02:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96788#p96788 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Questing and exploring the world is my favorite part of vanilla, and it would be cool to have more quests to do at 60. As of right now, there is Hyjal, but that's more of a place to farm consumables/shards. I think it would be fun and something to do besides raiding to have full zones with story lines that culminate in some worthwhile rewards. The rewards don't have to be on par with raiding or anything but maybe a pre-bis equivalent here or there or some useful trinket. Basically some level 60 questing that isn't for consumables or rep or part of a grind, even more quests for existing dungeons/zones would be nice.
