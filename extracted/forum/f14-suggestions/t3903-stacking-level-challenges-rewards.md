# Stacking level challenges rewards

## Post 23519 by Xef42 — 2022-08-26T10:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23519#p23519 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all,
I think there should be a special unique reward and incentive for doing all 3 other challenges with hardcore. Even though it's an easy death sentence for everyone not taking extreme caution it would be cool for more incentive to attempt it. Any player who manages to obtain level 60 while running hardcore, slow and steady, exhaustion and warmode should be granted an additional special title and maybe even mount. It would be really cool if there were tiers to the rewards for stacking challenges. Hardcore being your baseline reward then every challenge stacked on top of it adding an incentive if you hit 60. These are just some thoughts on what could expand on an already great game mode here on Turtle WoW. Let me know what you think and what some cool rewards could be considered!
