# Make Shadowforge Skinner act as a skinning knife

## Post 43941 by Bigbearone — 2023-04-30T21:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43941#p43941 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I completed a quest and received this awesome level 40-ish dagger called "Shadowforge Skinner". It adds +10 to skinning when equipped and the name implies it should be used for skinning.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/FsQJPtk/shadowforge-skinner.jpg)

Imagine my dismay when I realized that having this in my inventory doesn't actually allow me to skin. I have to also have a "skinning knife" in my inventory to skin.

I think this should act as a skinning knife if possible, so that if you have this dagger you can keep it in your inventory (as you would with a skinning knife) and then equip it when you want that skinning boost.
