# Wand Scaling

## Post 93009 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-18T08:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93009#p93009 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's no secret that wands are OP up until around level 30, then they drop off massively, especially at end game.

I think what  we can do to rectify this is maybe let wands scale with Intellect or Spell power?

The idea would be to keep them the same at low levels so they're not too busted, but help them scale when you get to the higher levels.

Priests who basically only level with a wand drop off later as you're just spamming wand most of the time, Shadow form makes up for this somewhat, but improved Shadow form is 60 only, so if you must resort back to wanding if you don't want to OOM.

(On another note, Shadow wands don't seem to be getting added damage from Shadow form, which was a thing in classic)

## Post 93024 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-18T11:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93024#p93024 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Wands have incredibly good basedamage (the highest dps weapon in the game is a wand with 130 dps). The next closest weapon is a twohander with 95.

Their high basestats & lack of scaling is what makes wands kinda good when out of mana. It allows casters to contribute while going oom (something that especially with bad gear does happen a lot). Your scaling as a caster comes from the fact that you have to rely on your wand less and less the more your gear progresses.

There is also not very much gameplay if wands are good. Wands specifically are designed to be a replacement for spellcasting. as they put your spells on CD. Wand weaving is about the peak of gameplay with wands, and that is a clunky arguably buggy interaction.

In their current form i don't see how making wands more powerful improves anyones gameplay

As for shadowwands not scaling with shadowform, i think its best if you open a ticket. But i think that might be intentional, as if they count for shadow dmg bonuses, they keep eating the warlocks imp. shadowbolt effect too.
