# Rogue Mount Quest?

## Post 96406 by Flimkrimbly — 2024-06-05T20:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96406#p96406 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As we all know, both Paladins and Warlocks receive a mount quest at level 40. Why not Rogues as well?

The quest could be centered around sneaking into an enemy compound in order to steal the mount. It could be faction specific as well, e.g. a nightsaber for Horde/a raptor for Alliance.

I just thought it was a fun idea that adds a little more flavor to the Rogue class. Let me know what you think!
