# Suggestions as a New Player

## Post 4850 by Eredun — 2020-01-10T15:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4850#p4850 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi! I just started here yesterday, and I decided to look around on the website here while I wait for my hearthstone, as i'm kinda stuck. I figured I could relay my experience so far and give some suggestions.

I created a character, loaded into the world, and I saw E'llo Turtle'mon. I went and spoke with him, he gave me an apple, and I noticed the turtle in my inventory. I popped down Speedy and realized I could speak with him, and I saw it was the shop. I clicked on the Portable Meeting Stone as I saw "0" next to it and it put it into my bags. I decided to click the stone and suddenly I was whooshed to the Crossroads. There I saw some new NPCs, I talked to each of them, and there was a turtle who wanted my apple! The turtle was now following me, and then I decided to log off for the night because it was a lot later than I thought it was. This morning I hopped on and my turtle bro was gone, I hearthstoned and turned in the quest, E'llo told me I could now ride him, but of course there was no turtle to ride.

I asked around, and someone told me to go back to where I found the turtle, so I used the meeting stone again. No turtle in the crossroads, and of course my hearth was now on cooldown (whoops). I found a food crate with 3 Snapvine Watermelons, which was enough to afford flying to Orgrimmar. I flew to org, and I saw a snowman, and I was like "Oh hey, I remember hearing about this winter veil place". I got sent there, and after accepting the quests (I have no idea how to complete The Icy Menace, the quest text is immensely vague) I realized I had no way back. That brings me to now, my hearthstone just came up as I was typing this, and I just found a new turtle and got my mount, so hooray!

Now for the actual suggestions and feedback.

That Portable Meeting Stone, i'm still not sure what the heck it actually *is*, but it should have an item description and a cast time similar to the Hearthstone. Also, it probably shouldn't send a level 1 to the crossroads. Perhaps it should require level 10 to use?

Winter Veil Vale (which I imagine probably won't last much longer) should have a method of getting back from it similar to Lunar Festival. Trying to think up something original, perhaps you could have some revelers send you to their home city (night elf reveler to darnassus) by knocking you up into the air with a snowball and then teleporting you above wherever the holiday area is in that city with slowfall.

I see you guys have an Item DB editor and a Quest DB editor, i'm not sure if these plans already exist but a Spell DB editor would be awesome, maybe I could even make the snowball idea and post it in that DB editor thread.

Hopefully this format isn't bad for suggestions, thanks for taking the time to read!   satisfied_turtle
