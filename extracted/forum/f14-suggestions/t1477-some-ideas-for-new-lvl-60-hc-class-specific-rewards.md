# Some ideas for new lvl 60 HC class specific rewards

## Post 8805 by Ectolink — 2020-12-28T17:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8805#p8805 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was thinking of some ways to encourage players to
not only play on this server but also play HC.
Some ideas for lvl 60 HC rewards I had for trinkets
that can be equipped at lvl 60 which give some unique
complementary buffs/skills to each class.
I think something like this would create a boost of interest in
playing HC and in general playin on the server.
It would be something exciting and different from all other servers.
These would create some new and interesting rotations, builds
and tactics.

When I write "for a duration" I mean the effect would last
a limited time, I was thinking 10 seconds would be good.

Druid:
Trinket of Nature's Harmony
Ability cooldown: 3 minutes
Increase all stats, critical chance and healing by 3% for a duration

Warrior:
Trinket of War
Ability cooldown: 3 minutes
Imbues the warrior's weapon with a 15% chance
to knock down enemies (single target) for a duration

Rogue:
Trinket of Nine Lives
Ability cooldown: 3 minutes
Imbues the rogue's weapons with a 3% life steal and increases dodge by 3% for a duration

Warlock:
Trinket of Agony
Ability cooldown: 3 minutes
Increase all shadow damage stamina and armor by 3% for a duration

Mage:
Trinket of Frostbite
Ability cooldown: 3 minutes
Reduces the mana cost of blizzard by an amount
and allows blizzard to crit for a duration

Shaman:
Trinket of Four Elements
Ability cooldown: 3 minutes
Increase all elemental damage and healing by 3% for a duration

Priest:
Trinket of The Heavens
Ability cooldown: 3 minutes
Increase healing and mana regeneration by 3% for a duration

Hunter:
Trinket of Efficiency
Ability cooldown: 3 minutes
Increase critical chance by 3% and reduces the mana cost of all shots and stings
by 3% for a duration

Paladin
Trinket of Holy Protection
Ability cooldown: 3 minutes
Increases armor by 3% and grants immunity to stun and fear for a duration

If not then at least it was a fun read :D

and a happy 2021
