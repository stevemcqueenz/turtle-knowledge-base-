# A couple of suggestions regarding pvp

## Post 34859 by Houseperenolde — 2023-02-15T21:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34859#p34859 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Glyph of the Outlaw**
I've heard some people complain that the 30% exp boost in warmode just encourages everyone to take it without encouraging more world pvp. I'd like to suggest the 30% boost be removed from warmode and added to a new glyph. This new **Glyph of the Outlaw** would make you permanently pvp-flagged with players of BOTH factions. Twice the paranoia that you're gonna get jumped with far fewer areas where you are safe. Its also useful for RPing as various neutral factions.

 **Chest I.O.U.s**
We've all been there. You spot a chest, you fight through the mobs to get it, and... you get a couple junk items. My idea to deal with these naughty people is that 30 seconds after a player closes a chest an item is generated (the IOU) tagged to the player. If there is no chest for it to spawn into (because you aren't a sociopath and emptied it) great. But if you left stuff behind it stays there for the next person to find. Once picked up the original looter gets a message stating "PLAYER_NAME knows what you did". The IOU itself has a use effect that puts the two players into a dueling state for 30 seconds but with no death protection. This isn't long but perhaps long enough to exact some sweet revenge and teach an important message about not leaving a chest half empty.
