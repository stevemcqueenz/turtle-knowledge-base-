# Re-Statting Dragonclaw Gauntlets

## Post 131311 by Dewbie — 2025-05-16T04:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131311#p131311 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The following is the take of an end game raiding guild's main tank that has cleared Kara40 consistently every week.

Warrior threat tanking glove progression is in a pretty grim state. Your current bis options are all items from AQ40.
Gloves of the Primordial Burrower:
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=55553>

Gauntlets of Annihilation:
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=21581>

Gauntlets of Steadfast Determination:
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=21674>

All three of these are interchangeable BIS gloves for furyprot/Defensive Tactics warrior tanks. (With Steadfast BARELY making the cut.) All of them are from AQ40, and if you are needing weapon skill the gloves with them are shared between all dual wielding melee classes. So about half your raid's dps since most run with a lot of fury warriors, that's a lot of competition. As of now, to get just 311 sword skill as a non-human you need The Hungering Cold and your skill quest done. As a human this puts you at 314. This means the only current way to have good tank weapons and above 310 weapon skill as a non-human warrior is to have one of the best swords in the game, with Crystal Sword of the Blossom as a close second putting you at 308.

Changing Dragonclaw Gauntlets to AT LEAST have +3 extra sword skill on them would do so much for warrior gearing progression and actually give tanks another item in Kara40 they actually would want that isn't shared with dps. This would also help combat human racial being especially strong for tanks that are cursed to not get The Hungering Cold forever. (By the way there is absolutely no tank loot that drops from Mephi?!?!)

I suggest changing Dragonclaw Gauntlets to have similar stats as the warrior 3.5 set that they match looks wise. It would make a lot of sense because only warrior TANKS actually use the full set, and it doesn't make any sense that a tank still wants gloves designed for dps, from a Raid thats TWO TIERS LOWER than Kara40. Meanwhile Dragonclaw Gauntlets are a drop from the boss in the current hardest raid of the game that's blocking a lot of guilds from reaching Outland. I suggest giving them these stats:

+10 Strength
+25 Stamina
Equip: Increased Swords +3
Equip: Increases your chance to parry by 1%
Equip: Improves your chance to get a critical strike by 1%

While this seems like bloat, comparing this version of Dragonclaw Gauntlets to it's competitors above they are actually relatively close, but this is way more stats in line with a tank role considering the parry that trends in the warrior 3.5 set and the high stamina as well. It also has a crit but a small amount of strength to make GoA or Primordial Burrower's way more appealing for DPS warriors. The +3 sword skill falls right in line with the fact that the best tanking weapons currently (there were no new added ones, no Riftcarver does not count.) are Thunderfury and THC. It would put non humans right at 314 sword skill and humans above 315.

TL:DR why are aq40 gloves better than KARA40 gloves from one of the hardest bosses in the game currently, please change the stats to cater to the tanks using the 3.5 set that these gloves are meant to match and make them actually usable.
