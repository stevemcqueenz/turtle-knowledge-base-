# Finkle's Skinner and Zulian Slicer should work as skinning knife

## Post 81476 by Silverfox — 2023-12-17T01:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81476#p81476 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't use finkle's skinner for combat, but I do need it for skinning high end mobs.  My character can't equip the zulian slicer, but I'm sure there are many skinners who can, but don't use it for anything other than the skinning bonus.  Yet, we must also carry the basic skinning knife in our inventory.

Wouldn't it make sense that if we must equip the skinner or the slicer just to skin those mobs, that those 2 items should act as a skinning knife, making it so all skinners would no longer need to have 2 items in their inventory just for skinning?  If we are equipping one of those items it seems like we are using it as a skinning knife.  So in an upcoming patch, just make those 2 items also act as a skinning knife.

Thank you for your consideration.
