# Quick idea for AH alternative "work board"

## Post 114143 by jeagan2002 — 2024-12-24T17:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114143#p114143 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I had the idea recently for essentially a "reverse auction house" where the buyer posts item requests and the amount they are willing to pay for them, and people can search for requests they can fill based on profession. The person submits the item to the board, and gains the listed reward. Of course, there would be a bit of posting cost based on the reward amount, just like the AH.

Also, if you need any voicework for cutscenes in the future, I've done a couple of small projects before and I'm willing to do it for free as long as it's less than 10 minutes of dialogue.
