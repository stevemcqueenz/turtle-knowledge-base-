# Feedback for improving Stormwind Vault

## Post 25755 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-22T15:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25755#p25755 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Today I did my first three runs in Stormwind Vault, and honestly, a lot could be improved in this dungeon. I'm not saying it's low-effort or the work done by the devs isn't outstanding, it just leaves a bit to be desired. Here are my suggestions for improving the dungeon:

 1. The trash mobs drop nothing, except a couple of silver. Not adding loot table to the trash mobs is a huge oversight in my opinion, especially because the trash is actually a lot harder than in other 60 dungeons. Vendor trash, cloths, other mats, greens and world drop blue/purples are always nice to have and offset any repair costs.

 2. The aesthetics are boring. Honestly, when I entered the dungeon for the first time, I was like "this is Stockades 2.0". Nothing in the doodads and furnishings (and the mobs, honestly) would suggest that this was once a vault, everything just screams "prison", which is funny, because Stormwind already has a prison.
I was expecting vaults full of forgotten riches, magical artifacts, cursed sentries and malfunctioning golems that would guard it. Instead I got a stockades clone with a touch of scholomance.

 3. Furthermore, the layout leaves a lot to be desired. It's just cramped corridors, no big open spaces, no multiple levels, no shortcuts or balconies to jump from. The bosses are not spread out at all, with loads of trash to get through in order to access the first bosses on one hand, and with the last 3-4 bosses having next to no trash between them on the other hand. Also how come both enterances lead to the same place?
I think it would work better if the layout was redone from scratch, using more varied elements and aesthetics, or even mechanics - it could work like Violet Hold in WotLK did, although that would be hard to scrips and prone to bugs.

 4. It's buggy. The fog in front of Aszosh Grimflame prevents right clicks from going through, making looting corpses problematic, unless you get your camera underneath the fog.

The gate that closes and spawns a bunch of rats can easily be bugged if you run in to trigger the gate and quickly run out, not triggering the rats, and locking yourself out of the rest of the dungeon. They should be on the same script. This has happened on 2 of the 3 runs I did. Luckily, we found a workaround, but it requires a mage and a party consisting of both factions. (I was an alliance mage, and a hordie left the group so I could polymorph him, which caused him to wander and go through the door, spawning the rats.)

The last boss can be cheesed easily just by staying in range. When the ads spawn, if there is nothing in their range, they immidiately despawn, which makes the fight considerably easier. As a mage I was able to tank the boss myself from range using Ice Barrier and Frost Ward (and OP heals from my healer) while the rest of the party killed him from range.

 5. The story doesn't make a ton of sense. Why would they stash a malicious and powerful artifact in a prison in the middle of the canal? This is again unsupported by the aesthetics of the dungeon, something more magical and arcane-ish would have worked way better. Also how did imps, an ogre, an orc, a bunch of huge flying spiders, a warlock and whatever else find its way in there? Who would imprison his foes in the same place he keeps his artifacts and riches?

Fixing these issues will significantly improve the general feel and enjoyment of the dungeon and enhance the strong points in it - that is the bosses. They are very well done and have interesting mechanics, but they can't hold the dungeon together on their own.
Let me know if you agree or disagree with any of my points, and present your views or any other suggestions, so we can have a healthy discussion about this interesting, but currently lacking dungeon.
