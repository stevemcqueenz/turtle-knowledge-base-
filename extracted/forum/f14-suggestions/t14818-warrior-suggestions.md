## Post 100996 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-10T07:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100996#p100996 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Its very simple. Would a longer lasting buff be more convonient? Yes

Would the game be better for it? Arguable.

A very short buff such as warcry or paladin seals you just click as part of your normal combat gameplay.

It is basically free and you can always immediately reapply while in combat. And as opposed to buffs like stamina from priest, there is little harm if it ever falls off for a little while.

I got it in my muscle memory years ago to check for it when charging and at combat end.

Longer duration both increases frustrations if you happen to not get hit as a fellow melee by its shitty range because the warrior wont rebuff it for longer. And it cheapens the combat rotation imho.

Buffs arent the most exciting things, but making their duration longer makes them even less engaging to manage rather than more
