## Post 159841 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-16T19:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159841#p159841 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

They made it so you can talent into 36yd dispels as a druid with the last patch. It's convenient, sure. But it also outright breaks certain parts of the game. Nefarian used to be this intricate dance where you had to dip in and out of fear range at just the right timings to decurse the MT. Now you just stand still outside of fear range the entire fight. It used to be an expression of skill and experience and not it's really *really* dumbed down.
Increasing the range further to 40yd and and making it available to other classes probably would need a major rework of boss mechanics. You think it's just increasing the number and there's no drawbacks, but when they did it for druids it was pretty obvious they didn't think it through and it breaks some fights (and not in a good way).

It's an interesting experiment but I'm not sure I like the outcome. I do think making it a talent was the right call. It really is massively powerful and there should be a cost for a huge buff like that. The last thing this server needs is more free powercreep.
