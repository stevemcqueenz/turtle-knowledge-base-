# Talent tweeks

## Post 95572 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-05-24T12:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95572#p95572 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

This is not a balance suggestion thread. I wanna pick up certain talents from classes I played at least until level 50+ and come up with tweaks which I think would make the talent more fun (or a viable choice). Big picture or overall class balance effect is not checked, only goal is to find ways to make certain talents more fun/better.
Will only bring talents from warlock, shaman, priest and warrior because those are the ones I leveled above level 50 in TWOW.

 **Warrior**

 **Arms Warrior**
 1. Improved Thunder Clap
Current: Reduces TC cost by 4 rage points and increases damage done by 60%
Change idea: Would also add 1 more target effected by TC per talent point. Fully talented version would work up to 7 targets.
Why: The optimal lategame tank is still fury prot, they are not interested in this talent. At PVP it might have some extremely limited extra use. But for leveling, it could help warriors keep aoe threat much better in case of big pulls, making the leveling experience as a tank more smooth. Some warriors might even use it at lategame PVE but it would be a rare sight.

 2. Two-Handed Weapon Specialisation:
Current: +5% damage with 5h weapons
Change idea: Also +5 weapon skill
Why: Arms warriors are weapon maniacs highly specialized for a weapon type. Them having no +weapon skill from talent feels wrong. Also, it barely effects PVP because no player is above lvl 60 but can somewhat help them at lategame PVE.

 3. Sweeping Strikes
Current: New ability, your 5 next melee attacks will cleave.
Change: Allowing it both in berserker and battle stance (I can't recall 100% sure but I really think it only worked at battle stance).
Why: Would give the option of +3% crit for those cleaves via berserker stance.

 **Fury Warrior**
 1. Improved Slam
Current: Reducing Slam CD from 1,5 sec to 1 sec.
Change: Reducing it from 1,5 sec to 0,5 sec.
Why: Slam is bad. Would be less bad. Maybe someone would use it.

 **Protection Warrior**
 1. Shield Specialization
Current: +5% block chance, 1 rage per block
Change: 5 rage per block
Why: Rage generation with a prot warrior is slow until raids. If you go prot and dungeon tank, you will most likely be rage starved. Even 5 rage per block might not be enough.

 2. Iron Will
Current: +15% stun and charm resist
Change: Would add fear here.
Why: Priests have it that way + it feels in lore for warriors to have extra fear protection.

 3. Improved Disarm
Current: Disarm lasts 3 sec longer
Change: Also reduce CD by 1 min.
Why: Let's face it, no PVP warrior is prot warrior and bosses usually can't be disarmed. It means disarm is mostly a niche utility against trash which has weapon. Removing its CD would mean that at least a prot warrior could fully use disarm in such situations and make trash packs with weapon much less dangerous. In exchange the +3 sec duration can be skipped.

 4. Improved Shield Bash
Current: Shield Bash also silences for 3 sec.
Change: Gives 20% extra threat to shield bash.
Why: You won't PVP as a prot warrior and interrupt can be done by fury and rogues. Extra threat is much better for them.

 5. Shield Slam
Current: The talent of shield slam. High threat damaging ability modified by the shield's block value, also having 50% chance of dispelling a magic effect.
Change: Delete that magic effect thing and give shield bash this: 'also having 50% chance of doubling the rage generated by your blocks in the next 5 seconds'.
Why: Prot warrior never PVP's and this kind of utility is better done by others. Give them rage and threat instead.

Priest

Discipline Priest

 1. Improved Power Word: Shield:
Current: Increases damage absorbed by it by 15%
Change: Increase the damage absorbed by your PW:S by a fraction of your +healing instead.
Why: Let it scale but don't let it scale too much.

 2. Reflective Shields
Current: 20% of the shielded damage gets reflected without threat.
Change: Scrap it and '20% of the damage absorbed becomes a HoT on the target once the shield breaks'.
Why: Both have their utility in PVP and giving the added healing as a HoT makes it dispellable (more fair). At PVE it makes shielding an interesting option. Shield absorption having some scaling + adding a HoT would lure some priests.

 3. Improved Inner Fire
Current: +30% effectiveness on inner fire.
Change: Also gives spell damage and healing for the instant cast spells (HoT, SWP, calculated in the PW:S scaling).
Why: Priest rarely bonks. This would add them a new tool without making it too strong (this applies only on dispellable things, spells like smite or casted healings are not affected).

 4. Force of Will
Current: +5% spell crit on offsensive spells.
Change: Same on all spells.
Why: Holy would be the overall bigger healer (spiritual guidance + healing) while disc would be the more 'critty' one (both this and holy specialization).

 **Holy Priest**

 1. Holy Nova
Current: Instant aoe which also heals.
Change: Reducing mana cost.
Why: Holy nova is mostly used at farming rn. If it's cheaper, it would have a healing niche (group is stacked, prayer would be too slow).

 2. Holy Champion
Current: Mini mark of the wild with 4 effects but really it only has 1 effect - your heals heal more on the champion.
Change: Make the other effects more interesting.
Why: Currently this is an illuision of choice.

I am skipping shadow, not familiar enough with them.

 **Shaman**

 **Elemental Shaman**

 1. Elemental Focus
Current: Crit gives clearcasting (40% mana bonus for 2 spells).
Change: Clearcasting also giving 20% added threat reduction while it's lasting.
Why: Threat issues. Also, elemental crits a lot (11% from talents only), clearcasting is rather consequent.

 2. Storm Reach
Current: LB and CL +6 yard.
Change: Adding flameshock, making its cast radius equal to new LB+CL cast radius.
Why: The other shocks can be toxic in PVP if they can be cast from this far away, flame shock however is mostly okay (also dispellable). This also gives a rotation option for eles standing max range at raids.

 3. Elemental Mastery
Current: Next offensive spell is free and crits.
Change: 10 sec window where offensive spells have 20% less threat and +20% crit chance.
Why: Currently mostly not taken by ele shamans. A talent reducing their threat window when pumping + adding damage via crits however would be huge for them.

 **Enhancement Shaman**

 1. Ancestral Knowledge
Current: 5% more mana.
Change: Refill 2% of you mana after a melee or offensive spell crit.
Why: Less boring and more useable for shamans. Enh can build both on melee and spell crits and stay in fights much longer. Ele can use it for spells, while resto would need to rely on cheaper totems and mana totem refills for mana.

 2. Thunderhead
Current: Lightning Shield can be cast on allies and is 20% cheaper.
Change: Lightning Shield can be cast on allies and it also gives 2% melee haste while it's on. Mana reduction is removed.
Why: To either make enhancements better with the haste or to make them better at utility by providing mini bloodlust to a melee.

 3. Weapon Mastery
Current: +10% damage via weapons.
Change: +10% damage and +5 weapon skill for 2h maces and 2H axes ONLY.
Why: To make enh in general stronger while also giving a limitation to the buff. Tank shamans would hate it but overall in TWOW, a weapon-wielding shaman should wear a 2h axe or mace.

 4. Flurry
Current: +30% melee haste for 3 hits after a melee crit.
Change: Shaman flurry also giving +5% spell crit while it's on.
Why: To make elemental devastation more consistent without impossible-to-assemble gearing.

 **Restoration Shaman**

 1. Ancestral Healing
Current: Healing crit gives the target +25% armor for 15 sec.
Change: Haling crit giving the target +5% stat for 15 sec (not stackable).
Why: Priests have the same talent. This change would make it less boring and would make both of them more unique.

 2. Healing Way
Current: Subsequent healing waves being 6% more powerful on the same target for 15 sec, stackable 3 times.
Change: Apply the same to lesser healing wave.
Why: More useful in raids, would give alternatives to chain heal-spamming.

 3. Restorative Totems
Current: Mana spring and healing stream being +25% more effective.
Change: Fuse those two totems into one.
Why: To stop healing stream from being a meme.

 4. Mana Tide Totem
Current: 5 min CD mana totem which recharges mana for party in 4 waves under 12 sec.
Change: Make mana tide somewhat affected by your +healing and and make it 3 min CD.
Why: To make it a usable ultimate talent.

Warlock

Affliction Warlock

 1. Improved CoA and CoW
Current: +6% on CoA, +20% on CoW.
Change: Merge these talents.
Why: Both of these curses are situational. Feels much better if you get both for 3 talent points.

 2. Improved Drain Soul
Current: Gives mana regen if you kill a target with drain soul.
Change: Doubles the soul shards you get via drain soul.
Why: To make this more like a quality of life talent.

 3. Nighfall
Current: 4% that your corruption or drain life gives you an instant shadowbolt.
Change: Shadowbolt can proc from each affliction spell, including curses now. This shadowbolt gets instantly casted on the last affliction spell's target and does not consume a GCD. Also, make this 6%.
Why: To enable a playstyle where you put up curses and rely mostly on the instant shadowbolts triggered by nightfall.

 4. Dark Pact
Current: Get mana from your pet (instantly).
Change: Make it a self-buff. As long as the buff is on and the pet has mana, the mana transfer happens (but with lower numbers).
Why: You don't wanna lose all those GCDs fishing for (a small) amount of mana while being the life tap class.

Demonology and Destruction: I am unsure for now.
