# Alcohol brewing

## Post 102993 by Sagabal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-10-13T01:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102993#p102993 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Alcohols are their own entire class of item, even coming with their own buff category from items from fishing and Dire Maul Tribute, but currently cannot actually be directly crafted.  To differentiate this from Alchemy, brewing barrels could be crafted with herb/wood/other ingredients that, when created, start out on a multi-day cooldown.  After the cooldown is up, they could be activated (with vials in the inventory) to be destroyed and create a number of brews in their place.  Advanced crafting could involve extra crafting steps that could extend the wait time for stronger brews.  This could also open up space, not only for new alcohol but for more fun items in the vein of Savory Deviate Delight or Noggenfogger Elixir, thinking in terms of the goofy beers from Deep Rock Galactic.
