To add to the actual topic of this post:

Lichen guard is a shield that is supposed to deal damage. This is 100% a farming item. In classic we got other shields for that job. First and foremost force reactive disk.
But also non engineers have plentiful options with skullflame, drillboar, crest of retribution or even ardent defender to leverage blessing of sanctuary.

So the very niche lichenguard fills does alredy offer quite a few great options. Adding lichenguard just replaces existing options, rather than being an extra option.

Prot paladin is already way too powerful at aoe farming thanks to a consectation which scales massively better with spellpower, betterpurpose built gear and more survivability compared to classic for paladins.

The game is more with less in this case
