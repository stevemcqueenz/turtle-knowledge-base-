Okay, I'm fed up with this. Just leveled my first engineer, got to 260, wanted to buy that recipe and then found out it wasn't there. Checked out the AH, because usually you find schematics like that for a somewhat reasonable price there (the worst I saw so far was triple the price).
But not here:  price between 75 and 100g, for an item that costs 1g20. Tried it again today, almost *immediately* after the maintenance reset (the timer for Nordanaar said 0 days 0 hours 0 minutes), and it still was gone.

This can't go on, and the way I see it, there are three realistic options:

 - make the recipe BoP
 - remove the timer, like you did with the first aid books.
 - make it a trainer-taught schematic, something that was done with patch 1.12 anyway.

And then there's option #4:

 - crack down really hard on scalpers, i.e. you diligently monitor those who buy these schematics (apparently on CD), and if there's *any* indicator the botting mafia is involved, delete all accounts involved. Pretty please.
