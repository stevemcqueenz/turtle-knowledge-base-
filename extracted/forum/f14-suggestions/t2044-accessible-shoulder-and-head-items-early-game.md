E.g:

Rough Leather Shoulderpads (White, vendor)

Ringmail Hood (White, random drop)

I agree with Lahire.

In Vanilla there are certain progression milestones everyone is looking forward to - and among them the ability to wear shoulder armour (around level 15) and headgear (around level 25+).

It also helps to immediately determine someone's (approximate) level.

That's one of the (many) reasons why I didn't like WotLK, because heirlooms completely undermined these rules - suddenly level 1 chars looked like they were level 60.

I'm still new to the server, but I already saw low-level chars (level 7 or so) wearing headgear  (fortunately just a simple hood), so I guess the rules on Turtle WoW have already been breached.
