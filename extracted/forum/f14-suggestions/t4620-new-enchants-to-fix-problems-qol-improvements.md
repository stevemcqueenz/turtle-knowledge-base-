# New Enchants to fix problems & QoL improvements

## Post 27641 by Zeyla — 2022-10-21T05:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27641#p27641 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There were a few issues I saw with some classes/races that could be potentially addressed with some enchants.

 1. Weapon Skill Enchants - A series of enchants added for one/two-handed swords, axes, and maces, as well as daggers, fist weapons and polearms. I don't think staff is necessary since I can't think of a real battlestaff in the game other than the one from ZG. Each enchant would essentially just add +5 weapon skill of the selected type, and must be applied to the appropriate item type. This would mean that you would be sacrificing crusader or agility enchants on those slots, but gaining a method for off-setting common issues like being a non-human/orc/goblin paladin/shaman/warrior. Being forced to acquire edgemasters for 2k+ gold is just not fun gameplay, and other options would be an interesting alternative until higher level items are acquired that include such bonuses to supplement those class/race combinations. I think this would be more appropriate to see on Turtle WoW in order to encourage players to play the race they enjoy without feeling punished later on if they want to participate in raids.

 2. Eternal Darkness Enchant - Essentially enchant a caster weapon with the effect of the blade of eternal darkness from Mara princess. Some builds will benefit greatly from access to that mechanic, and the obvious trade-off is losing the spell power enchant benefit. I might even make it a "Lesser Eternal Darkness" with around 25% of the item's normal effect if the standard version is seen as too powerful.

 3. Health per 5 Enchants - This doesn't really solve a problem, but some low level enchants for various slots that grant health per 5 would be an interesting way to empower low level character to level more effectively/efficiently.

 4. Enchanter exclusive +2 all stats to ring enchant - I know this wasn't a thing in vanilla, but something that only benefits the enchanter themselves would be nice to see, in a similar fashion to BoP crafted gear for other professions. Obviously don't let this be useable in the trade window and make it only useable on bound equipment, so that a BoE can't be enchanted and then traded. Also make the enchant's effects disable if you drop enchanting, to discourage leveling enchanting just to enchant stats on your bis rings and then drop the profession.

 5. Some sort of basic +1 or +2 stats enchant to shoulders at 300 enchanting with an expensive cost. Make it overall less desirable than getting the exalted ZG enchant, but perhaps better for a select few specs who favor the stats. It won't have much impact overall, but it will let people feel like they're "fully enchanted" without having to grind ZG exalted, which is a rewarding feeling for players to have.
