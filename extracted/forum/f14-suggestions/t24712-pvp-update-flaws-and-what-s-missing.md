# PvP Update - Flaws and what's missing

## Post 174000 by Alcilis — 2026-04-04T15:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174000#p174000 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

With the 1.18.1, lots of things were improved, lot's of things added, but also lots of things missing.

 **Thorn Gorge marks and reputation :**
Nice to have a new BG, but why bother if there is no incentive to play it ?
Add a new reputation linked to this BG, with specific PvP gear to be obtained through this reputation, motivating players to tag in order to grind this new reputation.
In the same spirit, add new marks linked to this BG, to be turned just like other marks, and add a new "United Efforts" quest (on top of the existing "Concerted Efforts", not replacing it !) to turn 4*3 marks (TG, WSG, AB & AV), giving a bit more honor bonus.
The same errors were made for SGV, adding a new BG without any incentive to play it, it's sad to see History repeat itself...

 **Rework the existing PvP Reputation rewards :**
With the newly updated PvP itemisation, all the PvP reputations rewards should also be reworked a bit in order to be at the same level (not a full revamp, just a global stats overhaul to make Friendly-Honored pieces matching current PvP T1, Revered - T2 and Exalted - T2/T3).

 **Promote the new events and rewards in-game :**
New events (Redwall Keep and Southshore vs Tarren Mill) are mentioned, but we lack information about when and how they are supposed to take place. Start promoting in-game these kind of events in order to attract a maximum of people.

 **Increase/Remove the Honor Cap :**
As mentioned in a previous post, the 20k honor cap is currently only killing the willingness of high-rankers to grind beyond 20k honor per week, making it impossible to be fully geared before 6-8 months whatever the time spent grinding, for no reason.
Why deliberately frustrate people instead of motivate them to play ?

 **Crossfaction tags :**
Crossfaction have done a great deal in balancing BGs, even if I was at first strongly against it for RP/Lore reasons.
Take it one step further and add crossfaction tagging, allowing people from opposite factions to tag as a group.
Either allow full crossfaction BGs (except for AV due to race-related quests) with crossfaction tags/premades allowed or remove crossfaction entirely, but halves-measures are just frustrating.

 **Fix Warmode :**
I loved the old warmode as it incentivized open-world PvP interactions through a lot of the player base being baited by the XP bonus.
After around a year away from this server, I now realize it's been replaced by a new shitty Warmode hidden behind Battlemasters that most people won't even see before hitting level 60... and that it killed the whole Level 1 Lunatic movement in the process...
But still, as a PvPer, I like to be permanently in PvP mode, so I activated Warmode back and for some unknown reason it keeps resetting to non-PvP (Warmode Off) every now and then...
Please fix it, or even better, bring back the old Warmode or rework it to make it really appealing.

 **Rework the PvP Set Bonuses :**
The old gloves-specific bonuses are now part of the PvP Set bonuses, but these bonuses were largely outdated since 1.4 and should be reworked (for example, Judgement of Crusader bonus for paladins is really circumstancial or PvE-oriented and should really be replaced).

Those are the first things that come in mind, but I'm sure there are more things flawed or missing so every one feel free to add your imputs.

I think these are mostly sensible suggestions.
> **Alcilis wrote: Sat Apr 04, 2026 3:09 pm**
> **Increase/Remove the Honor Cap :**
>  As mentioned in a previous post, the 20k honor cap is currently only killing the willingness of high-rankers to grind beyond 20k honor per week, making it impossible to be fully geared before 6-8 months whatever the time spent grinding, for no reason.
>  Why deliberately frustrate people instead of motivate them to play ?

I think the cap could be adjusted, but by and large I think it is a good idea. It gives the content longevity and it also forces people to pace themselves. Removing the in-faction competition with the ranking brackets was already a huge boon. But I think it's still a decent idea give people a break. 200k honor in the old system is a pretty hefty number and not far away from what people floated as hard-caps to prevent absurd life-destroying grinds (I think 500k was the highest suggested hard cap I heard about in the old system, most hardcore PvP folk suggested that or lower).
Limiting the currency gain is largely also what allowed developers to make the new rewards as absurdly powerful as they are. Getting a T3.5 equivalent piece in 2 weeks is just a ridiculous idea for balance and character progression imo. The PvP system was never meant to offer best-in-slot gear the way it does now. So to me it makes perfect sense to balance the ability for an individual grind to BiS by putting in a minimum length. If the weekly cap goes away, the items should be nerfed severely.
> **Alcilis wrote: Sat Apr 04, 2026 3:09 pm**
> and that it killed the whole Level 1 Lunatic movement in the process...

You might have missed it during your absence, but that is reversing cause and effect. It was level 1 lunatics who killed Warmode by creating unkillable characters and farming brand new players before their opening cutscene was even over. That's one of the major reasons why Warmode was removed as a starting challenge.
