## Post 158721 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-07T00:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158721#p158721 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

A random dungeon queue sounds convenient in theory, but without teleportation it creates a whole new set of headaches. Imagine you roll the dice, get slotted into a group, and then you’re stuck waiting 20 minutes because one guy is in Tanaris and another is in Hinterlands with their hearthstone on cooldown. That’s not really “random fun,” that’s just random downtime.

With the current setup you choose a dungeon knowing you can reasonably get there with your group. If you add a dice button, you’re basically gambling not just on the dungeon but on whether your teammates can even make it to the entrance in a timely way.

So while the idea scratches the itch of variety, the logistics don’t line up with the game’s design. Better to keep dungeon selection intentional, so you’re not stuck waiting on someone’s cooldowns or flight paths.
