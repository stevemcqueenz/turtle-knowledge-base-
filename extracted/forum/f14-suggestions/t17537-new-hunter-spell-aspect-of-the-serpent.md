# New Hunter Spell: Aspect of the Serpent

## Post 119694 by Reathien — 2025-02-12T06:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119694#p119694 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Background: I enjoy hunter, but feel like Beast Mastery could do with a more thematic Aspect to fulfill the ideal of being a hunter dedicated to their pet being as strong as possible. I think a new Aspect with a handful of related talent changes could create a truly fun and engaging new spell that makes hunters FEEL more impactful to the success of their pet without adding that much actual power by removing some damage from the hunter.

Current State and Need: I feel personally that BM lacks direct impact on their pet. In later expansions, pets would interact far more with spells, including specific attacks like Kill Command. I do not feel like that is the right call, but adding an additional support avenue could fill the same niche. By giving an additional avenue to support the pet through direct action, you can better fulfill the fantasy of being a Beast Master who sacrifices their own well being for their pet.

Idea - Spell: The rough idea I have would be a new Aspect I have dubbed Aspect of the Serpent for lack of a better idea.
The basic idea would be a Single Rank spell learned at level 30 that causes all ranged auto shot attacks to restore 3 Focus to your pet. It would add more focus aka more potential damage to your pet, but remove the free attack power provided to you (and in turn, your pet through Spirit Bond) which would balance the overall power more in favor of your pet.

Idea - Talents: In addition to this new aspect, a few talents could be altered.
> Swift Aspects could be changed to give a chance for ranged attacks to improve your pet's attack speed while in Aspect of the Serpent.

> Bestial Discipline could be changed to Bestial Invigoration increasing the Focus granted by your Aspect of the Serpent by 1/2 and causing ranged critical hits to increase focus regeneration by 5 per tick for 6 seconds.

Conclusion: There are a handful of very well known Beast Masters in lore, and I feel like a major aspect of playing hunter is fulfilling that fantasy of being part of a duo. I would argue that this idea IS fulfilled and enhanced by the additions made to baseline BM through passives, but lacks a certain feeling of buffing or empowerment that lends to the enjoyment and fantasy of the support oriented player. After all, if you want to shoot good, you go marksman. If you want to support your pet long term, you go BM. I feel like differentiating the playstyles a little bit more in terms of goals and weapon preferences (faster shooting guns/bows would probably be more interesting for BM with this change) would help make BM feel like a more powerful master of their pet instead of a cucked marksmanship hunter letting their chad pet do the work.

Disclaimer: I also do want to acknowledge that this idea is not perfect but merely something I feel could work. Two major drawbacks would be further enhancing pet attack speeds in pvp with the talent changes which could be problematic (easily avoidable if you make the chance not able to proc vs players, but I don't know how realistic that would be) and the major problem of this not being very beneficial beyond pets that can realistically spend more Focus well. This naturally would bias towards things like Cats and Raptors that can have two focus dumps, but I would argue the system already biases towards these two anyway. Optimal 100% most effective strategies exist regardless of trying to avoid them, so possibly just leaning into the idea that these are already the /best/ pets may just be the best work around philosophically.

Thank you for taking any time to read this that you spend and leave a comment if you think it could hold water or you  share my similar yearning to be a real BEAST MASTER!
