Nothing more infuriating enough to make you quit the game then getting ganked by a non-hc level 60 as a hc level 20 something without having any recourse. These troll level 60s go around killing HC for the lulz without any consequences, ruining people's fun and wasting their time while completely safe as non-HCs. They need consequences because they are malicious assholes who need to learn a lesson about ganking hardcore players.

MY suggestion? Make them Hardcore. No, I don't want them banned, suspended, warned, muted, or anything from the GMs. I want them to be hardcore too so I can work very very hard with all the spite I have for them and get them killed permanently, so they will know what it's like to lose a precious character that you've spent days of play time on. I would make a character, grind them to 60, and raid nax for BiS naxx gear just to do this to them.

I would absolutely LOVE if some naxx geared ganker who loves to gank HC players for his sick schadenfreude to suddenly find himself at risk of losing his level 60 which he spent so much time and effort into building up over the years. Do I care that he spent all that time and will be very upset to lose a character that he worked so hard on? Did he care when he ganked hardcore players just for his own malice and enjoyment?

I would love it if PvP griefers and gankers had to think REALLY FRICKIN HARD before tricking HC players into flagging themselves and then killing them, or randomly killing lowbies on the road for no reason other than the lulz. It wouldn't stop them from PvPing, they would just have to THINK about it first. They'd have to consider empathy for a second, and wonder how they would feel if they were suddenly at risk of the same thing happening to them. If they never want to be at risk for becoming HC then DON'T RANDOMLY KILL PEOPLE IN WORLD PVP, DO BGS.

IF you want to say that ganking and griefing is part of the game, and you should be allowed to do it, I TOTALLY AGREE. I'm not asking for a stop to it, I'm asking to level the playing field. If you grief and murder my HC character then let me have the chance to do the same to you. It's fair, it's good pvp that way. Otherwise, you're not a PvPer you're just a troll coward griefing for the lulz.

Once again I'm not crying about getting griefed and killed as a HC, I understand it. Still, I'm quitting the game because my play got ruined by some asshole who killed my precious RP character and laughed about it and said that people would congratulate him for it. I find that sick and disturbing, but understandable, but also: i deserve revenge. Someone like that, I should be able to rally people to kill them back and make them pay. I should be able to level an alt to 60 and murder them and their character is gone. Of course, my 60 would also then become hardcore, but it would be worth it because it would help me heal from the pain I'm feeling right now.

There's no justice for HC players killed in tWoW. I refuse to RP in a world with no justice. I think it's absolutely stupid to be killed permanently by a player who has no chance of it happening to themself. It makes the hardcore challenge shitty when that can happen. If I had died in a normal way like a random mob i'd accept it, and like I said I would accept pvp death too if only I could kill them back to the same effect. This is an emotional post, but I think I argued my point fairly well. I don't see why this shouldn't be the case. Like I said it doesn't prevent world PvP or ganking but rather just forces people to think more carefully about it, and you will have less people doing it just for the lulz or to ruin people's days (like i would). If I do continue play, it will just to be to hunt HC players out of spite until something changes. My pain is real. I'm not crying or fuming with anger, but it ruined my Christmas. Thanks a lot.
