# Suggestion: Seal of Command should not fail a proc

## Post 20446 by Velite (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-07-26T19:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20446#p20446 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Anyone who has hung around the paladin circles on this server knows that if you want to hit good parses you use Seal of Righteousness because A) The scaling coefficient is higher than classic B) When seal of righteousness procs it always delivers the damage C) It always procs on an attack and triggers other procs so it brings more consistent Mana return and proc uptime.

With the recent update, Seal of Command has been pushed to a 21 talent point position. The spell itself still behaves the same as before: It has a static spell scaling coefficient, it can proc and the resulting proc can fail (dodge parry miss (it can even miss if you are hitcapped!)), and it does not always proc which means it provides less Mana return than seal of righteousness. Now you might say "But Velite, Seal of Command can crit. Doesn't that make it better?". Fair, but I also left out one thing to counter this: It can ***Miss and Partial/Full Resist***! So in the long run, Seal of Command ends up being worse dps than Seal of Righteousness in a PvE scenario provided you have access to windfury, despite costing 21 talent points to learn now.

My suggestion to keep seal of command relevant as a PvE choice provided that it now requires almost half of your talent points to learn: Allow Seal of Command to work like Seal of Righteousness, in that the success rate is dependent on only the auto attack. The logic behind this is the same logic that puts paladin as a low end dps: Our damage is about 40% auto attacks and 40% seal, but since our seal damage is dependent on if our autos connect, that puts it on par with other classes, who have extra abilities like sinister strike, where each ability is only dependent on that ability hitting. With Seal of Command reliant on both the auto AND the spell connecting it has a low dps potential in the long run, and a lower overall benefit than Seal of Righteousness.
