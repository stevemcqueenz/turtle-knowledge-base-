# I love Gillijim Isle, but its kinda boring!

Gilljim Isle is a beautiful zone and is truly a good addition to turtle wow. There is something missing though, monster abilities! Of all the diverse foes you fight in on the isle I only encountered 2 quest mobs with any abilities. I fought basilisks, panthers, crabs, turtles, crocodiles, fen creepers, ghosts, trolls worshipping dark magic, nagas princesses, tidelords and sirens, and various murlocs, but only the fen creeper and the serpent had more to offer than just being a meatbag.

Give the mobs a chance to fight back!
