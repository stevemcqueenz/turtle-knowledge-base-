 **![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) A Listing of Alts on the Armory ![🏹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3f9.svg)**

[![Image](http://dl7.glitter-graphics.net/pub/727/727097kjhd9gidla.gif)](https://www.glitter-graphics.com)

I think that it would be useful for the armory to list alt characters on a person’s database. Although this would not cover alt accounts, it would help to minimize ninja looting, guild bank raiding, and otherwise deceptive or malicious actions making guild moderation easier on guild and raid leaders.

Making an extra account then, while possible, would just be an extra step then making it less likely that someone would abuse others because they could without repercussions.

IDK, I feel like it would be a violation of privacy to do that.  Someone you killed in pvp and ignored armories you and now can spam slurs.  People also create alts and home them in different guilds which is always potential drama.  On top of that, creating a 2nd account isn't hard and I'd venture to say most alts are on a second account.  IDK, in terms of user/data privacy, it just seems like a bad idea.

On ninja looting, are you really going to armory every player in group every dungeon and raid and every new person that joins guild?  That would be insanely paranoid and tedious to do.

I feel like there are more downsides than up here.
