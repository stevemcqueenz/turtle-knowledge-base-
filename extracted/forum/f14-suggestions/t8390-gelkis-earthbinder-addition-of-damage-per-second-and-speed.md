# 'Gelkis Earthbinder' - addition of damage per second and speed

## Post 54071 by Sevensky — 2023-07-19T16:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54071#p54071 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

'Gelkis Earthbinder' (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60902>) is a one-handed mace for lvl 48+, available for purchase to players with revered reputation with Gelkis faction.

Right now, this item lacks any 'damage per second' and 'speed' stat, thus melee damage dealt with it is exactly the same as if the player had no weapon equipped at all. This makes it inferior when compared to other, even lower level rare one-handed maces intended for healers (e.g. 'Hand of Righteousness' - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=7721>), and not an upgrade option worthy of grinding reputation and spending 12.5 gold. On top of that, this is the only Gelkis/Magram weapon which does not have 'damage per second' and 'speed'.

When I've created a ticket asking if the lack of 'damage per second' is intentional for this item, the short reply I've received stated that the item is intended for healers. However, this does not seem to make much sense, as levelling healers have to do some damage from to time in dungeons (or carry only a healing-oriented weapon throughout levelling). Other healing one-handed maces have 'damage per second' and 'speed' stat, too, which does not support the logic of this one lacking the traits because of providing a healing bonus.

Therefore I suggest to add a (standard for level 48 rare one-handed maces) 'damage per second' and 'speed' stat to 'Gelkis Earthbinder' to make this a legit weapon, worth the associated cost and effort.

Thank you.
