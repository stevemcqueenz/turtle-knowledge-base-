An artifact should be at least epic in quality.

The itemization math is legal for a level 60 pre-raid epic and the bonus damage and chance on hit effect (Rank 1 Mana Burn) is well within epic itemization values if not a bit under tuned. If one is feeling generous +8 stamina could be added.

Every melee weapon drop from Molten Core, Onyxia and the World Bosses are far better than this weapon for damage output. Furthermore with Naxxramas trash drops this weapon is no where close to being overpowered.

With the same amount of time to get to level 60 regularly without hardcore activated a regular player can easily get into raiding for a month or two (for example Molten Core, Onyxia, World Bosses) and get better weapons.

This is fun pre-raid item that pays homage to the original <https://wow.gamepedia.com/Sword_of_a_Thousand_Truths>

I would suggest either replace the current version or add a quest that upgrades the current version into this version so non-sword classes can still use the Sword of a Thousand Truths. Let us be straight, this is a sword.
