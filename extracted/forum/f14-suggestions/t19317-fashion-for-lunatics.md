# Fashion for Lunatics

## Post 131221 by Totallynotmel — 2025-05-15T13:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131221#p131221 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At the moment the only way for Level 1 Lunatics to obtain fashion coins is through the store which is a bummer.

I'm suggesting that either the level requirement for the fashion coin quest be toned down from level 10 to 1 to allow Lunatics access or having an alternative means which isn't a ridiculously rare drop from dungeons.

On the topic of fashion, The Summer Collection of Unseen Fashion: Shoulders & Belt both have level 15 level requirements while the other items do not.

I'm also suggesting the level requirements be removed to match the other items so Lunatics can also use them for transmog, considering the fact Lunatics now have alternative level 1 shoulder equips.

*Ahem*

Why do we act like drip is something you earn? Like you need to kill ten boars and survive a dungeon before you’re allowed a decent outfit? No. Fashion isn’t a reward. It’s a right.

You’re weak. You’re broke. You might die to a boar. The least the system can do is let you go out in style.

Looking good isn’t about power. It’s about presence. It’s about saying, “I may not survive this, but I will look unforgettable trying.”

We don’t lock personality behind a level gate. So why are we locking fashion?

Let the Level 1 lunatics wear oversized coats and ridiculous hats. Let them wear heels to the forest. Let them die dramatically. Because drip isn't endgame—it’s day one.
