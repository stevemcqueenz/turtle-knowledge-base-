# Changes to Mining

## Post 75134 by Ayesha (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-01T07:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75134#p75134 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, as a EU time zone player I’ve seen a huge change to mining since the influx of new players when we hit the cap and had queues for the first time.

Since EU morning to late EU nights Winterspring, Ungoro Crater and TelAbim are filled with people who camp the node spawns and are online sometimes for 6-8 hours straight. Before that, you sometimes could find windows and get some nodes, but not anymore.

PVE server with no mandatory PVP doesn’t have built in mechanics to protect itself from this. On a PVP server it wouldn’t have been possible, you couldn’t just stand in one point safely and grab the nodes as soon as they spawn.

I think we might need some revamps to mining. Some suggestions I have is:

 1) add random nodes throughout more zones like in Azshara so that you would have to travel to do mining;

 2) add more Rich Thorium nodes to DM E, so that people who don’t have time to camp nodes could do the runs and get some crystals

I’m curious what other people think about this and if there is any conversation going on between the devs

## Post 75139 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-01T08:24:00Z

Randomness of nodes is limited because you need to stick to terrain and it is hard to implement.
So nodes have potential spawn points and world decides random spawn among those possible points.
The problem is: there are too many campers. They occupy all known potential spawn points. While normal players roam the world and gather less profitable points, farmers just wait on the spots to node to appear.

I actually support to add mining nodes to every dungeon. It encourages group play and allow people to gather some materials.

I heard that RTV in DME is abused by mages blinking through the doors, but I can't confirm that.
Nodes in dungeons should be guarded by enemies.
