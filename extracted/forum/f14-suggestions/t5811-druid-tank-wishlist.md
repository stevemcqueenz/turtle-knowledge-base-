# Druid (tank) wishlist

## Post 36547 by Critter — 2023-03-01T20:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36547#p36547 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone. I'm a lifetime druid (feral) enthusiast and I love how the Turtle WoW team is putting in extra effort to finish all vanilla specs to viability. I've reviewed the changes to feral and I'm liking what I see, but I feel its still missing something to make the class feel complete in PVE. Some might argue that druid tanks perform comparably to warriors after previous changes, but it would be great if we could be strong in a druid way instead of a warrior way.
From the lens of a druid tank, here's my suggestions that might bring the other specs beside resto into lasting raid viability.

Barkskin castable in all forms and does not leave form. A little more damage reduction on demand would be nice utility for moonkins, cats, and bears to survive. Of course this would make the biggest differences for bear tanks who are the only ones not free to shift out while tanking. Other roles can be spared the mana cost to shapeshift back.

Hurricane now an instant cast (like DK's death and decay)
still on a minute cooldown, still has enemy attack speed reduction. Balance is currently bad at AoE and cat has none. Moonkins would be able to drop a hurricane and then start spreading moonfires while cats could spread rakes.
This likely won't make DPS druids be AoE kings like mages, but it will allow them to do more in a fun and unique way. This also gives tank druids something more than swipe which is way less valuable than thunderclap and consecrate to gain AoE threat. I believe druid tanks would still be a step behind warriors and paladins in AoE, but not incomparable.
+ Additional balance talent or moonkin build-in that reduces hurricane's CD to 30 seconds and reduces mana cost by 30%

Maces and staves with shapeshifted attack power like Atiesh and tbc druid weapons.
Anything that contributes more damage than manual crowd pummelers.
Its cool that there is a backdoor methods for ferals to be doing comparable DPS with a level 29 weapon, but it kills the feeling of weapon progression as a melee class.

New talent: Archdruid's Competency (preferably low to mid in feral tree)
Using this ability allows the druid to cast balance and restoration spells while in bear form, dire bear form or cat form. 1 charge. 30 second cooldown. Functions like nature's swiftness
I believe this would be the utility that makes druid tanks competitive with warriors and paladins without making them more like warriors and paladins and is in line with the class fantasy of versatility. Druid tanks would reasonably only use cast time spells like regrowth and rebirth in combination with barkskin (which does not consume the charge) up to avoid pushback and otherwise use the CD on an instant cast spell like rejuvenation, moonfire, hurricane, or innervate. I doubt this would make druid tanks BIS, but rather a particular benefit to bringing a bear more than an inability to find a warrior tank. The ability to bark skin and cast tranquility from bear when the dungeon is in a rough space would be the druid's way to help the party like prot paladins BoP and LoH. The spells and heals don't even need to be made to scale with AP.

Theres plenty of great spells in our spellbook, but ferals actively tanking are the only ones that can't shift freely and use them. This is a way to make feral tanks more than just a bear but rather a druid in a bear-like form. I think players would enjoy this more than giving them more flat damage % reduction or parry.  I personally don't see any of this being broken or OP in PVP, but I'm sure it would not be far away from being balanced.

Thank you for your time and consideration, feel free to improve upon my ideas.

Thank you all for taking the time to read my post. I'd like to clarify some things in response to some of the concerns raised.

 1. As it stands, I don't believe the hard to kill flag running druids constantly healing themselves are feral specced but rather resto. I recommend the ability to cast in bear or cat form be (deep enough) in the feral tree so that resto and balance druids can't use it. I do not believe the ability to self cast healing touch or rejuv once every 30 seconds Is going to make feral druids unkillable because there is no spell power scaling with the strength, agility, and stamina that feral druids are geared for. I would even go as far as to say feral druids that can self heal every 30 seconds will still be inferior flag carriers to resto druids as resto druids are now.

 2. The thing that balances this out is that hurricane is still on a minute cooldown and costs a grip of mana. It wouldn't likely be used every pack pull like consecrate and TC. It will not increase much in value with gear progression because theres no attack power scaling. I'd be content if it had a cast time like flamestrike too. The real benefit for druid tanks is that it provides auto attack damage reduction for a short period. its not even a 25% all damage reduction, just auto-attacks. Bears are second class tanks because of a lack of cooldowns available.
This would be a tad more mitigation the context of a long fight. Swipe is not on par with TC unless it is changed to add a 10% attack speed slow. Warriors are even better tanks on turtle wow than retail classic because they can keep up TC from defensive stance now. That really is not a minor buff.

 3. I would like to emphasize that a druid is a magical class. Feral ought to play like a magic cat or magic bear; not like a mind-controlled cat or bear. I'm not advocating for constant free casting while shapeshifted, but once every 30 seconds.  Without spellpower scaling with feral stats, I really don't see this making a game breaking difference. The real benefit is group utility being able to cast innervates and rebirths while actively tanking a pack or boss like dps and healing druids are free to do. Its not like paladins lose access to their group utility spells like BoP, BoF, and LoH while boss tanking.

 4. Even if Archdruid's competency did turn out OP, it can be easily balanced by increasing the cooldown of Archdruid's competency to 1 minute. Druids tanks as they stand offer nothing more than warriors  with less mitigation and utility. Why not give us more druid things to do while tanking instead of making us more warrior-like to be useful? Warriors got both intervene and defensive-stanced thunder clap. Shadow priest now have a heal every 30 seconds (that **does** scales with spell power) without leaving shadowform as well as group mana regen. Why can't feral tanks do something new to bring value to the group?
