# Mechanical Auctioneer for FREE

## Post 3493 by Bazou — 2019-07-21T13:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3493#p3493 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle Development Team,

turtle_in_love  In order to prosper the economy of the game and let our players interact better with each other, I suggest that every player can have a Mechanical Auctioneer for **Free**.

scared_turtle_head  Imagine two scenarios: After exploring the Elwynn Forest, a mage harvested a large pack of spoils. There are a two-handed sledgehammer and a few pieces of heavy armor. Two options for the mage: either to sell to a blackhearted goblin businessman, or to Stormwind City's auction house. "It's not worth waiting for a few days, and then receiving a rejection letter, and then I have to sort out these heavy loot again..." he muttered, and then turned the two-handed hammer and armor into shiny coins. At the same time, a soldier hesitated for a long time at the auction house. After rummaging through all the pages, the soldier sighed. He didn't find the right equipment, and even said that the auction house didn't have a few pieces of equipment at all. As a brave but lonely warrior, he picked up the shield that had been worn out and continued his journey...

hiding_smth_turtle_head  Is such a scene very familiar? Because the size of the players on our servers currently is still limited, the economic circulation in the auction house is also limited. The less economic interaction leads to less player interaction, which is a vicious circle. Back in the era of classic world of warcraft, auction houses and banks are always the most lively places. People are crowded together, auctioning their own spoils, buying and bidding on others' goods, forming a scene of economic prosperity. Back to Turtle wow, we are stuck in a dilemma. Players are not economically linked to each other, and it is difficult to have emotional link on each other. We need some help, some help with "invisible hands."

happy_turtle  I made a suggestion: Let every turtle player have a Mechanical Auctioneer for free. This little pet can make an expedition and a player can hang the loot very conveniently in the auction house. Well, we also need to consider some negative factors: the value of this pet is 200 Turtle Coins, so it will definitely cause the Turtle development team to suffer some real Euro losses; in addition, the carry-on auction house is equivalent to some increased backpack space. This may affect the potential balance. A few days ago, I noticed that the Turtle development team canceled the auction house's handling fee and extended the auction house's hanging time. I think this should be for the same purpose: to prosper our Turtle wow's economy and bring players  more convenience. In the distant future, I even suggested that the Alliance auction house, the Horde auction house and the Goblin auction house can be unified. Of course, it is another story.

wary_turtle_head  Free Mechanical Auctioneer, what do you think about this suggestion?

turtle_in_love  Hey, thanks for such a quick and warmhearted reply. I know that the Turtle development team has always been the BEST!

turtle_tongue_head  Back to my suggestion, why should I recommend a free, and alt AH (abbreviation of Auction House) rather than "an alt bank"? The answer is simple: I want to help other people, and hope that others can easily get the help. In fact, it is difficult for people to help each other.

sad_turtle_head  Well, let's talk about my own story. I am from a organization in Turtle wow--legendary "MoltenCore Salvage Ops". This guild is awesome! We have a good tradition that when an explorer gets a loot, he/she is often willing to share it with other explorers. The typical format is often like this: "/Guild: Hey [Great Axe of Bear] Who wanna it? Free!/Sale price!" I have helped a lot of people and I have received such help from many people in such way. These are only limited to our guild and I want to help more people. However, you can imagine that shout in the world channel does not seem appropriate. I thought of selling weapons or raw materials at a cheap price in AH. But the problem is that AH is only in the main city, far and troublesome. Time cost and efficiency are too much. When one wants to help others, it is sad to find that it is just too hard to do so.

turtle_in_love_head  So, I recommend a free portable AH that everyone can use! This is called "Unus pro omnibus, omnes pro uno/One for all, All for one". We don't have a lot of players currently, but many people are eager to help others. Why not encourage them?  A newcomer can get more help. Especially after arriving Stormwind/Orgrimar, they can be attracted to a "hot" auction house and therefore may not leave the Turtle wow. A mid-lvl player has more adhesion to the world's economic system, and a veteran player can get more happiness from helping others. Everyone will benefit from it, isn't it? In this way, the number of our players will increase and become a better world!

maintenance_turtle  Long story short, to prosper AH, there are either a lot of players, or each player can be willing to buy or sell. No doubt, that an AH that can be carried around will greatly improve the situation. Sorry for my broken English, I just want to help others and make our Turtle-WoW more and more GREAT!
