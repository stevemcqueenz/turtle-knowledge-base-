# Remove the mana cost of paladin and warlock mounts

## Post 26382 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-28T21:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26382#p26382 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Paladins and warlocks get their own mounts as spells learned from the trainer at level 40 then from epic quests at level 60. They have an easier time getting it than all the other classes. Blizzard in Vanilla gave their mounts a mana cost, maybe as a sort of "drawback" to it since they considered free class mights too good.

This means that once you have access to both the class mount and regular mounts, a regular mount is **always** better from a practicality standpoint. The class mount costs 100 mana AND causes 5 sec rule mana regen, making the player lose even more than just its cost.

Because of this, I can't use my dreadsteed in situations where the riding turtle or any other generic mount is so much stronger especially in things like battlegrounds where mana is very important. Removing the mana cost from these mounts will allow paladins and warlocks to be more comfortable with expressing their class fantasy and not feel like they are intentionally destroying their mana pool when they do it.

TL;DR: remove mana cost from warlock and paladin mounts so they can actually use them in mana-tense situations.
