# Nathrezim Raiment 3p Set bonus is negligible and needs rework

## Post 144274 by Choosingname — 2025-08-10T17:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144274#p144274 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Kara40 Set Bonus for warlock produces negligible difference between wearing the set bonus or not, 0,66% in my testing to be precise, so it might as well be a blank text. I initially posted it as a bug report but was told by staff that the "blank text" is working correctly and as intended.

I have provided a short video of my testing, feel free to watch it or recreate it. The footage focuses on Shadowbolt crits but I tested earlier with Searing Pain and found very similar results.

TLDR:
Average crit with set bonus = 2784.5 (0.66% real increase)
Average crit without set bonus = 2766.3

Suggestions on how to Improve the "blank text" of the 3p set bonus:
 1) layer and calculate the 10% bonus crit as follows (X=normal spell damage)
(X+X*100% [Normal crit 50% + Ruin 50% layered first])*1.1 [Set Bonus 10% layered last] = 10% more overall damage increase on the crit. Shadowbolt in SM/Ruin is around 50% of overall damage done, while crits themselves are around 50% of the entire Shadowbolt damage. This means an implementation of this talent woould result in total warlock damage increase of 2.5%.
 2) Rework entirely into something similar to the Shadow Priest 3p: "Your Lifetap increases yours and your summoned demons Shadow and Fire Damage dealt by X% for 10 to 15 seconds"
 3) Something more flavorful like "Your Lifetap has a 10% chance to send you into a Shadow Trance, allowing your next Shadowbolt to be instant cast, while Searing Pain has a 10% chance to reduce the cooldown of your Conflagrate by X seconds"
 4) Go the tried and tested but boring route of simply adding flat Spell Power bonus like Paladin Tier 2 sets for example. Just about anything is better than the current non-existent bonus damage of 0.66%.
