# Tents & Campfires & Inns

## Post 168979 by berdal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-20T07:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168979#p168979 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

To make campfires more relevant, let the spirit buff linger 30 minutes after you've sat next to it for a minute. Also, only the combination of campfire and (any) tent gives rested XP.

And finally, have a few different tents that gives up to 60 min buff (1 min buff / 5sec). Suggestions (only one tent buff can be active at a time):
 - Jungle Tent: 5% agility, 5% attack speed, 5% movement speed (trainer)
 - Desert Tent: 5% spirit, 5% intellect, 5 mp5 (trainer)
 - Mountain Tent: 5% strength, 5% stamina, 5% armor (trainer)
 - Starter Tent: +2 all stats, +100 health (quest from trainer)
 - Gilneas Tent: +3% lifesteal, 20 str, 30 stam (gilneas City drop)
 - Gnome Tent: 30 intellect, 25 arcane resistance, 5% fortune (Gnome reputation vendor)
 - Tent of the Fang: 105 armor, +4 all stats, +14 nature spell damage, +6 nature damage to attackers on hit (WC drop)
 - Defias Tent (Dm drop)

Like world buffs, these buffs doesn't apply to raids. Tents and campfires despawn only after 15 minutes (regardless of what the creator is doing).

Lastly, getting in-game Rested XP from Inn requires that character is not sober (all Inns must sell alcohol).
