# First-Aid solutions to non-poison debuffs while levelling

## Post 122201 by Sagabal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-03-10T17:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122201#p122201 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently if you get a long-lasting poison from a mob, you can either find another player to cleanse you or spend resources costing an uncommon drop from certain mobs to get rid of it.  On the other hand, if you get infected with some long-lasting disease or curse from a mob, you need to go find another player, and unless you have a restorative cure-all potion, that's it.  A dedicated way to remove low and mid level curses, diseases, or magic effects could provide an alternative for players still leveling, without stepping on the balance of purification and restorative potion's roles as the end game soft-green-echo-eyedrop-antidote cure-alls.
