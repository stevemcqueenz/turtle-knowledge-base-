# Redridge Uncontested

## Post 132096 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-20T11:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132096#p132096 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Time for a topic that will get some people needlessly upset:

How about making Redridge uncontested?

I know it's always been contested and some player love ganking lowbies there as a tradition, but I think this idea does have merit.

 *But humans already have Westfall! This would give them 3 instead of 2 safe zones. Is that fair?*
Short answer: Yes.
Horde have The Barrens. This is essentially 2 zones given its huge size. It even has 2 flightpaths. And, though I wasn't into Cataclysm, the single zone was reasonably split into 2 zones given its enormity. So while Redridge is something like a 2.5 (between secondary and tertiary) zone, Barrens is **both** a 2 and 2.5 zone. It even has RFK to cap it off, which is ~level 30. (Yes, it has RFD, but that's to return to later, same as most  other dungeons in safe zones) And the elite quests of Redridge are also ~level 30, even slightly lower.

Furthermore, Horde have literally no PvE reason to be in Redridge. No quests. Wetlands, which is also Alliance dominated, does actually have some quests Horde can do. The custom neutral ones of course, but Horde warriors always had to go to the Wetlands for  brutal armor. Maybe a few other things. Main things being, Horde do have actual content to complete in Wetlands *and* it's also a proper tertiary zone.

Don't worry about losing the ability to kill lowbies in Redridge; Duskwood, Ashenvale, Wetlands, Stonetalons, and other proper tertiary contested zones exist. I expect ganking will still as normal in all these other zones. Indeed, it will probably grow since more Horde would shift to these zones!

Disclaimer: I play both sides. Horde and Alliance. I also PvP. I just like to do it in more typical PvP zones, like STV.
