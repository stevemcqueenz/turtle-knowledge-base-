# Some thoughts regarding itemisation, pvp burst etc

## Post 129836 by Stirlitz — 2025-05-06T10:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129836#p129836 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I remember seeing a thread where the twow dev team started a discussion on the pvp burstiness and possible solutions to this, and it included suggestions like introducing resilience etc.

I would like to add my thoughts to this discussion.

Burstiness in pvp is seen by many as a problem, something that I personally don't much see as such in itself as there are many pvp games that are bursty, like many shooters where getting shot in the head means you're dead. But another person would like a more strategic approach so I think that the matter itself is subjective.

What I think is a problem though, which also has a great effect on pvp burst in addition to other side effects is itemisation that scales infinitely because at some point items will become so strong that they will naturally introduce pvp burstiness and even if it's fixed in the Kara40 patch, when the next 40lvl raid arrives or the following which will have t5 or whatnot the same problem will arise.

I have a proposal which is a possible solution to some of vanilla's issues but which also implements other problems (like most solutions to such complicated systems do).

So here's my proposal:
Instead of raising ilvl for items of higher tier raids, keep maybe 3 tiers of epic items: S, A and B. B tier items should be at a lvl between T1 and T2 and will be the epic items that drop from most raids. A tier items will be the good stuff that drops from final bosses of the higher tier raids and S tier items will be the items dropped from the newest raid - maybe have an S+ ilvl for items from the current raid's last boss.
When a new raid comes up, the last raid's items get nerfed down to A tier.
Let's say B tier is ilvl 70, A tier is ilvl 76 and S tier is ilvl 82, for example (just random numbers here).
So in our current content let's say: MC/BWL/ZG/AQ20/Kara10 epics are all lvl 70, except for nefarian epics which are ilvl 76, AQ40/Naxx epics are ilvl 76 (and maybe KT drops are ilvl 82) and Kara40 are all ilvl82(with maybe last boss being 85 or so). PvP sets will be A tier and weapons S tier.
When the next big raid comes, KT drops drop to 76, as do kara40 ones, and k40 last boss remains 82 and keep this cycle of introducing new items and nerfing old ones.

This would have some good and some bad side-effects and this is what I'd like to open a discussion on.

Good side-effects:
 - dmg and combat is more easy to tune since scaling will not happen in the future.
 - PvP burst is for this reason reduced
 - It's easier to get past gearchecks - player ability matters more than just gear
 - Incentivises players to partake in difficult content when it's current and relevant, instead of waiting for others to figure it out and then just outgearing and skipping the fight mechanics.
 - Allows for infinite addition of raids in the future without ever encountering overgearing issues that will reintroduce burst or other gear-related imbalances.

Bad side-effects:
 -Limits item creation ability: in a world where we have limited design ability for items, sometimes just a scaled up version of an item is a good upgrade by itself. Ashkandi has the same stats as ZinRokh but more (except for speed) - if they became the same ilvl ZinRokh would be better because it's slower. Nefarian would effectively drop a ZinRokh sidegrade. At some point the devs will have to get really creative with what items they make and these might introduce their own imbalances because of that.
 - Limits player feeling of progression - if not taking part in the top tier raids, everything pretty much becomes a sidegrade.

I don't often have time to take part in forum discussions, so I don't know whether I'll find time to respond to comments, but I'd like to open a discussion with this idea if others also find it interresting/appealing and give devs something to think about.
