Atreidon raises a valid point. by increasing the rewards for world pvp even more, what would stop players from just ganking others once they won a fight? you need to kill a player 10 times to start getting zero honor.

vanilla wow is not built for world pvp. players who lose a fight have the odds completely against them, as not only do they have to corpse run but also start a potential new fight with no buffs and a massive reduction to health and mana. not to mention that dying too many times even gives you a cooldown on accepting a respawn on your corpse.

for healthy wpvp, GYs should function as they do in BGs and spawn players in a safe spot with full health and mana from which they can return to the point of conflict and resume fighting on equal terms.

and keep in mind that this feature already exists in BGs, but the coin return on looting a kill is so bad most players don't even bother with the right click. not even a silver.

lets start by tying goldbounty to pvp rank:
This doesnt incentivize worldpvp with a large part of the playerbase. A lot ofthe population has no or a very low pvp rank. So you limit the impact of the change to only make high rank players be the targetworthy.
Since Ranks arent lost on twow that means anyone who ever seriously played pvp will have a (potentially quite substantial) goldbounty on their head. Encouraging dogpiling them. Not exactly the gentlemens form of PvP.

High rank also is no quantifier for gear, there are plenty od extremely well geared players that do have pvp ranks 0-3. So this is lackluster at best for rewarding based on difficaulty

Then the size of the Goldbounty:
Considering the risk involved in pvp, the fact that it should be an actual motivation to pvp, and the time spent corpsewalking if you do screw it up. For max level players we are looking at a Goldbounty that is at least 1-3 gold.
below that, noones gonna bother.
Higher than that and the above issue becomes very noticeable.

Then there is the issue of some classes being able to delete you without recourse. Arcanemage can kill you from bluewalling without recourse ~75% of the time. Openpvp is already heavily incentivizing getting the first hit in. But on twow there are several classes that if they get a freecast (which they guarantee thanks to bluewalling) you lost already. This problem gets worse thanks to (shop item assisted) savespot jumping.

The reason world pvp is dead is because world pvp is fucking over other players, not having interactions that are fun for both sides of the exchange.
By introducing a bounty that encourages you to play cheaply to gain gold, you dont make people wanna play open pvp. You will have more players turning it off not to get jumped and only maybe engage in worldpvp when they can get some easy cash.

Ehat worldpvp needs is less burst and WILLING participants. Paying players to engage in bad content is the best way to ensure more people will opt out because they are sick of being farmed for cash
