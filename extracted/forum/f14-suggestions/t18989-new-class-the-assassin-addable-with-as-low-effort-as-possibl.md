# New Class : The Assassin (addable with as low effort as possible)

## Post 129222 by Brunogre — 2025-05-01T08:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129222#p129222 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Adding a new class in the game can be a little tricky, but I had an idea for a new class that shouldn't be painful to add in the game.

The Assassin.
It would learn their skills at the Rogue Trainer, so instead of adding a NPC everywhere a trainer is, the rogue trainer can train both.

It would have three specialization.

 1 - The Samurai
It would use either sword and bow or katana and bow (depending on adding the Katana category of weapon could be a thing or not.)
It uses the bow at medium or close range as rapid fire and the 1h sword or katana to make aoe swipes.
It could dash/roll, a bit like the monk.

 2 - The Engineer
It would use a gun with or without scope to kill at long range, and a fist weapon.
It would have a skill that switch to a FPS view to shoot at the target, The rest of the time it would only shoot at very long range. It cannot shoot at medium or long range, the goal for the class is to try to bleed/kill the enemy before it can reach the player.
Then use a fist weapon at close range if necessary.
It has skills of disguise, so picking a humanoid enemy, the assassin can have a temporary disguise with the same appearance, the aggro zone is considerably smaller for that enemy/zone but a bit less efficient if the player choose to run instead of walking.

 3 - The Ninja
It would use throwable weapons and a dagger.
Maybe add some more throwable weapons in the game or just make the skills very powerful with the throwable, for instance a rain of shuriken.
It can go in stealth but cannot move, instead it can teleport in the back of ennemies, so it has to teleport from an enemy to another to get in deeper caverns for instance. Then they can proceed to a silent kill, if they are fast enough they can stay in stealth mode.
It has other movement skills, such as the backward tp, to teleport a few meters behind itself.

In general, The Assassin has a skill called "That's a Bounty!" that will give any player that will earn a share of exp on this mob a share of C/S/Gold. The bounty on its head is proportional to the exp earned, so the skill will not work on monsters that will not give XP.

_

I'm curious to have your inputs to see if what I'm asking is absolutely impossible to add or not, but I feel like even without the new mechanics it should be a fun class to play.
