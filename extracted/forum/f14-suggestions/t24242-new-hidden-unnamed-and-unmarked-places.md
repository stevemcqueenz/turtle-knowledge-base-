# New Hidden, Unnamed and Unmarked places

## Post 170488 by Dbl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-10T04:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170488#p170488 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**What**

The unmarked and unnamed places are usually small bits of immersion and world-building left by the devs that really helps the game feel more alive and atmospheric. I would love to see Twow populate the world with ruins, bridges, fallen trees, remains of battles, camps, small streams, rock formations, shrines, cool flora patches or anything that would make a place just a bit more special and the world more lived in. I would also love for devs to implement anything they feel would immerse yourself in the atmosphere of each zone. While I understand that Twow has more attention to detail and added alot of small cool bits to the old zones I would just love to see more of it, especially after seeing how gorgeous the latest new custom zones are.

 **Why**

Vanilla world altho unique and beautiful in it's own way has it's setbacks. There are countless areas that almost feel liminal because of how barren they are and it just takes you out of the world and ruins immersion. I love just going on foot through zones and just exploring but nothing makes me wanna stop exploring like a badly textured asset-less lump of terrain or the two same copy pasted trees repeating through the whole zone. I want to be able to see cool little things all around the world, not just travel to the main points in the zones. We need more ''density'' in zones which in return would make the world much more beautiful

 **RP, Explorers, Lore enthusiasts**

I think the RP and the aforementioned groups would benefit the most from this seeing as it would provide countless of fun new places to gather and do RP at, explore and immerse yourself in the lore. Please just make this happen, small things like this always go a long way, thank you for all your hard work and support <3
