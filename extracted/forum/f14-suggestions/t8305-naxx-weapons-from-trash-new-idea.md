## Post 53545 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-15T14:58:00Z

I don't like the current dev proposal for a solution either (especially nerfing actual BiS items seems overly harsh), but this is a complicated problem. Your idea would address the issue for the future, but not for the past. It would create yet another situation where people who already have the items will be unaffected by the change and every new raid entering Naxx will suffer the consequences. So I get the dev's desire to nerf the actual items so people without any appreciable raid progression don't keep running around in massively overpowered items. But I also get the problem, that it would hurt actual progression raids.

Simple solutions won't be able to make everyone happy. For a complex problem you need a slightly more complex solution. For instance, they could rework the current items into substantially weaker versions (change the names to "Broken Servo Arm", "Dull Harbinger of Doom", "Tattered Ghoul Skin Tunic", etc), perhaps even weaker than currently proposed to really get them in line with how easily you can attain them while still serving as catch-up items. Then introduce a questgiver to Light's Hope. If you have "Broken Servo Arm" they send you on a quest to kill Grobbulus and as a reward they exchange your item for "Misplaced Servo Arm", a new item that has the same stats as are currently in the game.
Trash farmers can still farm very powerful items, just not hilariously overpowered ones. New raiders still get (weaker) catch-up items early and eventually (slightly later than now) the powerful versions. Established raiders are back to their old power level in a single week.
