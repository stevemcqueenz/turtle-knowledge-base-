# Quest Item Respawn Timers

## Post 52449 by Bladedqueen — 2023-07-06T01:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52449#p52449 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've noticed in several quests that item pickups have considerable respawn timers, like the magical grains in Eperius Needs Grains quest from Arathi Highlands taking around 5 minutes. This is unnecessary, especially given the sheer number of players running through these zones. They could be shortened to 30 seconds or less and it would seriously improve the leveling experience.
