# The Goblin Ogre Hybrid Race

## Post 17641 by Thecakeisalie (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-03-22T01:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17641#p17641 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all!
After reading the other thread related to Ogres as a new race, I just had this weird idea of adding Ogres to the Goblin race. If the ship hasn't already sailed and the Turtle team is willing to rework the goblin class, then hear me out.

Obviously this is a work in progress / not fully thought through / thus open to change.  turtle_tongue

Problem Statement:
- There is currently a faction inbalance in favour of the Alliance.

- The Alliance High Elf race is way more popular than the equivalent Goblin race for the Horde.

Proposed Solution:
- Add an Ogre pet /mercenary system to the Goblin race.

- The Ogre can be either a melee or a caster "pet."

- The Ogre is just "upgraded" at certain level intervalls into a more impressive skin, ie regular ogre into "armored" ogre.

Why Do This?
- Make the Golbin race more attractive for players.

- Offer a new and unique gameplay experience(?)

- Less work than creating a whole new race(?) since creating a whole new race / apply gear / female ogres / voice lines / etc, is a tremendous amount of work.

- "Might" satisfy players who would like to play as an ogre(?)

Suggested Mechanic:
- An Ogre would be "hired", either feed it like a regular hunter pet or pay in gold (unlikely players would like a gold sink).

- The Ogre is either a melee or caster "pet", which levels with you and upgrades with more "armored" skins

- Should Ogre's fighting style be set in stone upon creation(?) Either **melee** or **caster** (?) or switchable like hunter pets? (up for debate)

- The Ogre would be able to be taught a limited amount of skills relevant to its fighting style, similar to hunter pets.

- Would create unique gameplay, ie warrior-melee, warrior-caster, rogue-melee, rogue-caster, mage-melee, mage-caster, etc. (Unless ofc this is too similar to pet classes already, and not so unique after all.... only rogue-xxxx on second thought...)

- You don't gear the Ogre, thus less implementations and gear assets required.

- Ogre can work as a mount, with the goblin riding on its shoulders.

ISSUES with this idea:
- Firstly, is it lore friendly? (I believe so, but I would leave that decision to people more familiar with lore)

- Secondly, is it possible / feasible to implement this kind of "pet" system?

- Thirdly, how can this sort of system be balanced? (Hardest obstacle imo)

- Lastly, do people event want this?   dead_turtle_head

BALANCE (up for discussion / not thought through at this point in time   turtle_tongue  )
- Being two obviously has advantages (looking at pet classes), so what choices do we have?

- Balance it like a hunter class, where the Ogre is the hunter pet?

- Nerf Goblin race and divide its power with the Ogre, ie 50%-50%, 75%-25% etc

- What about pet class (Hunter)? Remove the animal companion and have beast talents buff the Ogre?

- What about pet class (Warlock)? Remove the demon = would just be a mage with this pet system. Is three "characters" overkill and too difficult to play?

- Probably a hella lot of more issues that I can't think of atm.

Well what do you all think, am I hella drunk or does this peak your interest?
Share your thoughts and break my dreams haha!
