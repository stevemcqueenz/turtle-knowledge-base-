# Paladin --> Warlock Character Exchange Program

## Post 136621 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-06-22T12:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136621#p136621 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Problem #1: Too many Paladins.
Problem #2: Too few Warlock (Alliance and Horde).
Problem #3: Shaman overpowered in PVP.
Problem #4: PvE Server(s) Population lopsided towards Alliance.

The past mistake(s) of massively overbuffing Paladin resulted in severe consequences which continue to plague the games health/balance.

They did (eventually) nerf Paladin (and buff Warlock) -- but unfortunately this is simply too little too late.
The damage has been done.

Faction Population Balance, Class Distribution Balance, and PVP Balance have all suffered greatly for years.
And so there needs to be drastic measure(s) to begin Reconstruction efforts.

Proposed Solution:
Free (Level 60) Character Transfers from Paladin into Warlock.
You can also choose to swap over to Horde Warlock if desired.

Theres a variety of ways to implement this program so I wont go into great detail.
They already have/had Race/Faction paid change so I assume this is doable.

Example of a possibility:
Grant a "Warlock Lvl 60 Boost Token" to someone who deletes their Lvl 60 Paladin.
The fresh 60 Warlock would be given some basic starter gear/gold/etc.

Rationale:
Most of the reasoning is self-explanatory.
But to clarify the PVP matter specifically:
Legions of Paladins screw over Horde Warlock (endless dispell/cleanse of DoTs/Fear/etc)
Warlock hardcounters Shaman (Curse of Tongues / Fear / Shadow Dmg etc)
