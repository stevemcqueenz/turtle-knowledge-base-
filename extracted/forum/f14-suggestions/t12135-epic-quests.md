I suggest is an epic quest for each class including an epic reward, like a custom spell/title or an upgrade of a weapon /class item. Or  even an extra skill point. The quest must be doable mostly solo or in group, not fair to require end game rraid bosses, but is mandatory one or 2 real challenges during the quest line.
Exemple a warrior quest witch requires to kill all the classic dungeon bosses, and solo a few mid level dungeons, in a certain orden, even with a timer (on 7 days per exemple) The legendary item can be the The hand of eternal justice, that gives a 4% intead of a 2%  of double attack and +5 all stats. And the skill can be riposte, a pasive skill that deals half of the weapon damage when the warrior parrys.
Example: a druid quest that requires discover and talk with all the relevant druids on the game, with an custom path guided by the quest, even the opposite faction ones, that requires cunning or preparation, with a timer. And ending in a few dungeons group with a new boss event. The leyendary item The eternal staff of the warden, that increases armor by 300 from 250, and gives 150 AP shapeshifted and a 5% to proc insect swarm on melee hits. The spell can be insect swarm cloud, and aoe with a 1 min CD (independent to the hurricane) that deals nature damage on area (3 secs cast/ o secs duration) and gives 4% miss chance on enemies.
They are EQ related quests.
