# Goblin expansion

## Post 25997 by Kindredsoul — 2022-09-24T00:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25997#p25997 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To expand the zone up to level 10 we could have the following:

Run away to Horde as normal OR report potential escapees (hoping for profit) / get caught trying to leave with treasure to remain in zone for more quests and save ourselves from worse punishment. Venture Co could still be unhappy with us letting us get a "persuasive" agreement with the boss putting us into indentured servitude or an underground prison to perform tasks until we can escape.

We could have quests to regain the trust of those we turned in and meet other prisoners to plan and have quests to accomplish an escape. We could join a goblin prison gang for protection, come to an agreement or fight them to protect the foreman's group. We could be kept working with / under guard by machines that we destroy / reprogram to help us.  We could repair animated shredders we destroyed and modify them to dig or mine out of the area then flee to the horde with a gang, the foreman's people or release all the prisoners to join us. Releasing everyone could have a large style combat with Venture Co. Machines could be modified to be on our side or to be defective. Guards could be subject to bribery to look the other way or be turncoats helping us escape, fighting with us. Overall we could escape working for freedom through degrading and humorous tasks like cleaning latrines, via corrupt guards, modifying machines, joining a gang or through trickery poisoning guards food, impersonating them or escape through mayhem like smuggling gunpowder into the prison to escape via KABOOM!

We could have a zone further into the crypt where we find the dead goblins turned into undead with witty and weird jokes. We could have the option to gamble, do bets, solve riddles or to fight them. We could put their remains to rest or demand money from their family to get them for burial.
