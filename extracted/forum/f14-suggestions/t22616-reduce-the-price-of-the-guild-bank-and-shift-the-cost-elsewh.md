I started playing recently and got a lot of people on a forum I'm on to join as well, plus some of my friends. In total, there are around 20 of us who have started playing so far. I formed a guild so we can all chat together, hang out, help each other out, level together, share some leveling items, that sort of thing.

> I am actually interested how exactly people use guild bank. I mean what is the logic behind "store items" for them? Which items do they store? How long? How do they decide when to withdraw items?

Generally, a guild bank has a more open tab for people to toss in and grab out whatever they feel like with emphasis on levelling items and crafting stuff they were already making as they skill up, then the valuable resources and BoEs are in an officer tab for people to see what's up for grabs and ask them for it.

It's worth noting this entire function can be recreated for a fraction of the cost with a bank alt and a public list of its contents on your guild discord or such, but that requires regular updating and for people to go out of their way a bit more to realize what's available to them. So, the guild bank is really just purchasing a minor convenience upgrade, and as such can get away with having a price tag mostly designed to give bigger, progressed guilds something to sink their gold stores into.
