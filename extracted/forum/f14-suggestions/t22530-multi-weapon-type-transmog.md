# Multi-weapon type transmog

## Post 156655 by Andimio — 2025-10-22T23:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156655#p156655 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

For some transmog variations some weapons of a certain type look more fitting than the one you are currently using. I.e a certain mace could look way better for a tmog set than a dagger. This is especially problematic for people who have a spec that favors one type of weapon over another, for instance dagger rogues.

If you could transmog your weapon as another weapon type you can properly raid or pvp while still keeping your character’s looks consistent. For example, a rogue who’s required to use daggers for optimal performance could transmog them to look like swords or maces that better match their transmog set, without losing any gameplay benefits. This would give players more creative freedom and help avoid the awkward mismatch between effective gear and visual style.
