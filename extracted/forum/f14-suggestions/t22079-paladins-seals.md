## Post 153134 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-25T12:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153134#p153134 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While the idea of extending seals to 10 minutes may seem convenient, it runs counter to the core design philosophy of Vanilla. The 30-second duration is not simply an inconvenience, it is an intentional mechanic that shapes how Paladins are played. Seals are meant to be dynamic, requiring refreshes as part of resource and time management. This design emphasizes decision-making in combat and prevents Paladins from becoming overly passive or automated.

Moreover, the mana cost associated with refreshing seals is not as punishing as you're portraying. With proper play and efficient use of Judgement, Paladins are not in danger of running themselves dry. Extending seals to 10 minutes would not only reduce mana use, it would also diminish the importance of managing that resource, which is central to the Vanilla experience.

Adopting Wrath of the Lich King mechanics in this regard would erode the distinct flavor that defines Vanilla. The short seal duration is part of what makes the class feel unique in this era of the game, and lengthening it risks blurring the identity of Paladins between expansions. What may be more comfortable is not necessarily better, particularly when it compromises the original spirit of the game.
