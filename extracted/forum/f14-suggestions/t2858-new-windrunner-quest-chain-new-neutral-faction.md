# New Windrunner Quest chain+New Neutral faction

## Post 17826 by Username800900 — 2022-03-30T11:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17826#p17826 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

New Neutral faction

the Kirin Tor reaserch expedition- a coalition of Kirin Tor mages lead by Vereesa windrunner who have come to Outland in order to study the various magical creatures that call the broken world home

Hay guys I've always had a deep love for the windrunner Sisters so I thought how cool would it be to have a Neutral Quest chain that starts in Outland and would result in a reunion between Lady Sylvanas and her Sisters

Before Vereesa will offer the Quest chain players must prove themselves to her by reaching exalted with the Kirin Tor reaserch expedition

The best way to do this is by completing all the Quests that are offered within the expedition research stations scattered across Outland as well as the many quests that are available within the Kirin Tor expedition outpost

A large majority of the Quests offered by the mages involve helping with their research into th e magical creatures that call Outland home

Once players hit exalted with the Kirin Tor reaserch expedition Vereesa will offer her Quests to the player

The Quest chain would be divided into a series of related quests where players Assist Vereesa windrunner with locating her sister Alleria who disappeared along with the rest of the Alliance expedition following the second war

Later players discover that Alleria was captured by the fel horde and has been a prisoner of both magtharidon and Illidan stormrange ever sense

Upon reporting this to Vereesa players are sent to Raid the illidari prison and free Alleria before escorting her back to the Kirin Tor reaserch expedition Outpost where her sister awaits her

The final part of the Quest depends on which faction your character belongs too

Horde: The windrunner Sisters request that the player speak with Sylvanas within the undercity and let her know that both her Sisters are alright.

Sylvanas in turn leaves Varimatheras in charge of the undercity while she personally travels to Outland for a long awaited reunion with her Sisters

Alliance: the windrunner Sisters request that players deliver a hand written note to the local horde presence on their behalf

Upon receiving the note the horde commander informs the player that he will personally make sure this letter gets to the Banshee Queen and sends them back to the Kirin Tor expedition Outpost to let the windrunner Sisters know this

Players can then view a rare reunion within the
Kirin Tor expedition Outpost between Sylvanas, Vereesa and Alleria that will last 5 minutes before sylvanas returns to the undercity
