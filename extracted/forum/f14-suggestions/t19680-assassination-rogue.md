# Assassination Rogue

## Post 133896 by Bigbosc — 2025-06-01T17:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133896#p133896 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently among the three main end game raiding rogue specs (Envenom Assassination, Combat, and Support Sub), Envenom Assassination is the least useful to a raid barring a few specific encounters with mind numbing poison.

Sub support spec does low DPS but with expose armor, and all of the sub buffs fills a very powerful role to a raid, enhancing DPS by a great deal.  So this spec has a purpose it buffs other physical damage party members, good spec!  It might have its own problems but at least it serves a purpose and is good at that job.

Combat does the most DPS, and has the best cleave, going deep in the tree you can pick up haste while using blade flurry, making it a strong spec.  Easily the best DPS build we currently have.  It has a purpose.

Envenom Assassination however does not really serve a purpose.  You do less damage than combat spec on almost all fights, and you offer no other utility that would give it a role.  The unique playstyle is very fun, and it can do OK damage, but it is doing worse damage than combat, and struggles to even keep up all of its unique buffs that make it fun to play.

Essentially the spec tries to keep up slice and dice, envenom, and rupture (taste for blood) as much as possible.  But you often don't have the combo points needed to keep all the buffs up.  Recently a bug made many rogues do insane DPS by giving out free combo points, which does break the class.  But it made me realize if we simply had a healthy implementation of it, attached to the deep assassination talents, the envenom spec would at least be even more fun, and might become the best single target dps spec.  Which would give it a purpose.

I propose a simple change : Add the following to Vigor a 2 point talent in assassination.  "when you cast a finishing move with at least 5/4 combo points you gain a combo point on the target".

This combined with Ruthlessness means that when you do a 4 point finishing move with 2/2 talents, you will get 2 combo points on the target.  This will make assassination do more damage (which it needs), and still get the fun benefit of getting to push a wider variety of finishing moves.

It COULD make assassination into the best single target spec for rogue, but that would be a healthy and good place for the spec to end up.  As that would mean Assassin is the single target best, Combat is the cleave specialist, and Sub is for support.  All 3 specs would have a purpose and something they shine at.

PLEASE give us this devs, you have done great work and the game is awesome, but currently rogues are just significantly worse than melee hunters, and warriors for melee fighting.  Thank you!
