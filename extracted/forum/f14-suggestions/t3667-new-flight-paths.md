# New Flight paths

## Post 22179 by Jeepdays — 2022-08-13T04:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22179#p22179 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I feel that WoW classic really suffers from the lack of flight paths. How many players have been jaded by having to not only fly a long distance, but also run after flying to a quest or dungeon zone? I think turtle WoW could further distinguish itself from retail and other servers by offering more flight paths. It would make leveling less of a pain and traveling to dungeons easier. An easy example (alliance) is a flight path to northern STV. Please re-evaluate flight paths and ADD MORE! Who's with me!?!?
