> **Ingameacc12345 wrote: Fri Dec 15, 2023 6:18 pm**
> Why... why would you farm Flesh eating worms?

They have a chance to drop around 2-4 Grave Moss each (the Rotted Ones spawn about 2-3 worms on death each time), which is used to craft Shadow Oil, which is used to craft Greater Shadow Protection Potions. Pairing that with the Grave Moss node spawns in the graveyard makes it a prime farming area for raid materials.
