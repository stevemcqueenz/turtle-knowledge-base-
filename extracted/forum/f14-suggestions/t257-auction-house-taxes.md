# Auction House taxes

## Post 1343 by Seductive — 2018-11-18T22:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1343#p1343 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't know if this is viable, but removing/lowering the Auction House tax/deposit for awhile may be beneficial to the economy. I've acquired certain items that are not listed on AH yet, these items are usually vital and popular for crafting, but for me to list them it would cost me ~1g 40s.

Now on a server where the probability of the item selling is highly likely if you price it reasonably due to people having a need to obtain the item, I wouldn't mind paying the 1g 40s. However, because the likelihood that someone who needs the item is willing to pay the 4g (Auctioneer suggests 6-7g) list price is quite low... it deters me from listing the item at all, fearing I will just pay/lose a 1g 40s. This is hindering the enrichment of the Auction House in the early stages of the server.

 **Lowering the tax/deposit would encourage more Auction House listings.**  smiling_turtle_head
