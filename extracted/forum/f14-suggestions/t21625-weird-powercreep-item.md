## Post 149818 by Crake (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-02T03:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149818#p149818 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While not casting, mana regen for priests, mages and warlocks is 1 mana per tick per 4 spirit, so 11 spirit is 2.75 mana per 2 seconds, or 6.875 mp5. However, that is while NOT casting. To match 5mp5 with 11 spirit, you'd need to have 73% mana regen while casting, which is nigh impossible. Even if you up that 11 spirit to 12 by assuming you'll have kings up, that still requires you to have 60% mana regen while casting, which is highly improbable. 5mp5 beats 11 spirit 9/10 unless your fights have significant periods of downtime where you can get mana regen
