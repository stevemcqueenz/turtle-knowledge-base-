# Female High Elf Hunter Bow Animation is BORKED

## Post 92455 by Penfold — 2024-04-09T16:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92455#p92455 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I never played Blood Elves in Retail WoW, so it might be that High Elf models/animation/rigging are closely based on the original Blood Elves.  Even if that is the case, it doesn't change the fact that the animation is completely borked.

When facing an enemy the female High Elf puts her RIGHT foot forward.  This results in the bow, held in the left hand, pointing roughly 45° to the left.  A neck-snapping head animation then occurs as the right hand draws an arrow from the (invisible) quiver on the elf's back.  The body twists 45° to the right so that it's roughly facing the target.  The bow is held more horizontal than vertical in front of the body.  The bowstring is then drawn directly into the abdomen of the elf.  The string is then released and the arrow launched.

That action is **ridiculous**.  It is ergonomically impossible to shoot a bow with both power AND precision in such a manner.  I was planning on levelling a HE Hunter but had to abort on the whole plan within a few levels because the animation was so mind-numbingly stupid.  It looks like it was originally devised/designed by someone who had no clue whatsoever about how bows actually work.

Male High Elves shoot fine.  Both male and female Night Elves shoot fine.  It's just Female High Elves that break immersion with a borked animation.

Blizzard took about a decade before they got around to fixing the animation in Retail.  There's a YouTube video that neatly shows before and after:

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/LuYMrCFi3og]

 **Please fix the Female High Elf Hunter Bow Animation!**

Obvious possible approaches:
- Update it to match the new animation
- Put the elf's LEFT foot forward, instead of the right
- Have the elf be left-handed, and therefore hold the bow in her right hand
Thank you!
