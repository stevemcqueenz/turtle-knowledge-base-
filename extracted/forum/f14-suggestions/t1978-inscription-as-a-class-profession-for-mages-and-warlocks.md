# Inscription as a Class Profession for Mages and Warlocks

## Post 11771 by Khalthul — 2021-08-22T23:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11771#p11771 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It is interesting to find scrolls which can buff oneself or another upon its use, but who makes these? They must come from somewhere... or someone...

Are they plundered from old libraries only to be sold by the page from a vendor?... are they truly the remnants of an ancient art where their spoils can only come from the fortunate few that loot its runic power?...

I think not... they could be created in the same way as the Inscription profession had done, as it were in the Wrath of the Lich King expansion, but I don't think it should be done in *quite* the same way...

Instead, it can be exclusive to the class, similar to a Rogue's manufacture of poisons, except it is the Mage, whom in their careful and precise studies of runic magic, can render themselves capable of crafting these scrolls once supplied with the appropriate parchments and inks.

Furthermore, if we were to go even further into this possibility, to an equal but opposite degree, a Warlock could be well-versed in the type of Inscription that Nartok uses in his [Tainted Parchment], in that it curses those that are exposed to its foul legislation.

I am open to your thoughts, dear reader,
 -Khal'thul
