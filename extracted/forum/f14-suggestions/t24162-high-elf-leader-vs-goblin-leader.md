# High Elf Leader vs Goblin Leader

## Post 169977 by Xeenar2 — 2026-03-03T21:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169977#p169977 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Since Im trying to create PvP events I had a look at the Goblin Leader in Sparkwater Port and made some comparrions.

Grand Taskmaster Nert Blastentom Lvl 60Elite is surrounded by  a bunch of low lvl Npc's and a few scattered guards, non elite lvl 55, the port has 3 entrances, and you can jump the wall at a certain point, making any kind of event with him as the kill-target absolutely impossible to defend. A large force would need to just glimpse at his health pool of 159.85 k. (if you talk to him, he tells you "time is money, friend")

Lets have a look at Vereesa Windrunner Lvl?? of Alah'thalas, leader of the High Elves with her special voice commands, the counterpart of the horde special race Goblins.
Granted, she does not have many abilities, and is easy to kill with a small force, just takes couple minutes. 799.5k healthpool.

In comparison the Gnomeregan Exiles leader has an impenetrable fortress of elite guards and 666k health. Any event with him as the target would be stopped by a melting pot of aoe at the entrance, and if his throne room was reached by a miracle, there is balconies in there aswell for healers and ranged.

If I was to create an event to kill faction leaders, I could set up Thrall (Vol'jin same room), Sylvanas and Cairne.
On alliance side, event could target Bolvar + Benedictus as a bonus boss, Magni and in Darnassus we got Tyrande + Fandral as a bonus objective.

Sidenote: "raventusk has lvl 62 non elite faction leader, not worth mentioning much about this, 55 non elite guards, entrance easily held with a melting pot of aoe".

I guess if I had to suggest anything, it would be to give the horde a little love <3
