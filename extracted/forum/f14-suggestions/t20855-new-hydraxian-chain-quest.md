# New Hydraxian Chain Quest

## Post 144931 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-12T22:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144931#p144931 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Hydraxian Waterlords Questline (Level 55+)**
 *The Hydraxian Waterlords, weakened after centuries of fighting the Firelord’s forces, seek mortal champions to tip the balance before Ragnaros’s influence spreads beyond the Firelands.*

While there are some intro quests to do around the world for this faction before delving deep into the MC quests that will progress you most of the way, I did want to suggest a questline similar to this in intent- to provide a way to dip your toes into Hydraxian rep, when you're at-level for the Azshara area. The quests still send you to BRM because the idea is that the Waterlords hate the Firelord, so you have to go to where the Fire is. Also ramps nicely into the UBRS quest and etc.

---
 **Main Quest Chain**

 **1. Whisper of the Tides**
 **Quest Giver:** Faction-based, in Azshara
 **Objective:** Speak to a Hydraxian emissary in Azshara and collect 5 *Essence of the Waterlord* from elemental creatures in the zone.
 *Gets you going around and interacting with water elementals. Establishes your link to the Hydraxian Waterlords.*

 **2. The Scorched Path**
 **Quest Giver:** Lord Hydraxis
 **Objective:** Travel to Blackrock Mountain and slay 10 *Flamekin Sprites* to weaken the outer defenses of Ragnaros’s servants.
 *Introduces the conflict between fire and water elementals.*

 **3. Prisoners of Steam**
 **Objective:** Find evidence of a coming summoning ritual of massive proportions, the plans are inside Blackrock Depths (detention block area).
 *First major dungeon objective, learning the Firelord’s forces are planning a large-scale summoning.*

 **4. Steam vs. Stone**
 **Objective:** Defeat Bael'Gar the fire giant in Blackrock Depths, and return with his eye.
 *Targeted assault versus the Firelord's emmisaries.*

 **5. To Quench a Flame**
 **Objective:** Defeat Lord Incendius in Blackrock Depths and recover the *Stonebound Vessel*.
 *The vessel can hold a portion of Ragnaros’s power for study.*

 **6. Waters of War**
 **Objective:** Take the Stonebound Vessel, then destroy it at a Twilight Hammer shrine in Silithus.
 *Sets up the final confrontation.*

 **7. The Boiling Cauldron**
 **Objective:** Travel to Grim Guzzler and slay Dark Iron Dwarves who may be carrying Summoning Ritual Fragments. Leave none alive. Return the Fragments to Hydraxis so they can be studied and reverse engineered.
 *Weakens the ritual and prevents further incursions.*

 **8. The Heart of the Firedog**
 **Objective:** In Molten Core, use the Altered Summoning Ritual Fragments to weaken Magmadar, then slay him. Return the *Emberheart Core* to Hydraxis.
 *Gets you into MC.*

**Hydraxian Waterlords: The Azshara Alliance**

The idea with this chain is that you could do all of this at the right level inside of Azshara, so you're getting some rep gains for helping them with their local problem before helping with their world-ending problem.

 **Quest Giver:** Hydraxian Emissary, Bay of Storms, Azshara

 **1. Currents of War**
 *Objective:* Speak with the Sea Giant Chieftain at the Ruins of Eldarath and deliver Hydraxis’s missive.
 *Description:* The Hydraxian Waterlords have long relied on sea giants to guard the deepwater passes. Recently, naga activity threatens this pact. You are to deliver an urgent call for alliance.

 **2. Harpoons of the Tidehunters**
 *Objective:* Retrieve 6 Tidehunter Harpoons from Naga weapons caches along the Azshara coast.
 *Description:* The naga arm themselves to hunt the sea giants. Removing their stockpiles will weaken their threat.

 **3. Drowned in Shadow**
 *Objective:* Slay 8 Male Naga and 5 Female Naga along the eastern shore.
 *Description:* These naga are the vanguard of Azshara’s invasion. Removing them will keep the sea lanes clear.

 **4. Stormcaller’s Debt** (Group Recommended)
 *Objective:* Retrieve the Stormcaller’s Conch from the elite naga Lady XXXXX in the Ruins of Eldarath.
 *Description:* A powerful artifact belonging to the sea giants was stolen and corrupted by naga sorcery. Without it, they cannot summon storms to defend themselves.

 **5. The Pact Renewed**
 *Objective:* Return the Stormcaller’s Conch to the Sea Giant Chieftain.
 *Description:* With the artifact restored, the sea giants will pledge their strength to Hydraxis once more.

 **6. Tides Against the Flame** (Dungeon: Blackrock Depths)
 *Objective:* Use the Stormcaller’s Conch inside Blackrock Depths to disrupt a fire elemental summoning, then defeat the **Molten Tidebreaker** that emerges.
 *Description:* The naga’s actions were part of a larger Firelord plot. Taking the fight inland will send a clear message.

 **7. Waves Unbroken**
 *Objective:* Return to Duke Hydraxis and report your success.
 *Description:* The alliance between the Waterlords and the sea giants is restored, and the naga are driven back—for now.
