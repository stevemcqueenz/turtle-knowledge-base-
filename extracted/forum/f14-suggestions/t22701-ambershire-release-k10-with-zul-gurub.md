Yeah, but zanza rep requirement forces everyone to get revered, not just newcomers.
+ the enchant

It's part of a more broad design problem (I think it is a problem) of raids.
In theory, there are raid levels, and as you progress, you only need a select few, giving you a manageable amount of raiding time per week.
In practice, most raids have something which kinda force players to return, even if severely overgeared.
ZG has zanza and enchants.
MC has some really cool epics and of course, the orange items.
Kara10 has the scythe for casters.
BWL has the trinkets.

These things lure people back to raids they overgeared and increase weekly raiding time overall.
