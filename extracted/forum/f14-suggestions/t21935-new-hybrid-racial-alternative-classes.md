# New Hybrid/Racial/Alternative Classes

## Post 151958 by Gprurik — 2025-09-16T15:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151958#p151958 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

These are inspired by WarCraft lore as well as other RPG games such as Pathfinder. Please leave any comments or questions as well as further suggestions.

Spellsword: Warrior/Mage hybrid; can conjure off-hand trinkets that augment main hand, Leather armor.
Barbarian: Warrior alternative; Leather armor and more glass/risk/reward than a traditional warrior.
Headhunter: Hunter/Rogue; Troll hybrid, throwing specialist hunter without a pet but with stealth and augmented criticals.
Elven Ranger: Hunter alternative, Night Elf hybrid, inspired by WarCraft 2 improved elven archer.
Arcane Archer: Hunter/Mage hybrid; another Hunter without a pet, can conjure augmented magic ammunition, rain of arrows but more glass.
Mechanic: Gnome alternative; begins with Engineering skill that is integrated into class abilities. Can manufacture a pet or even tank in a mech suit.
Merchant: Goblin alternative; very poor in combat, can summon hirelings (NPC companion) and has three professions. Has access to non-combat missions.
Flame Savant: Warlock/Mage hybrid; all the fire from both classes; flame pets and flaming melee.
Theurge: hybrid magic user; arcane and divine casting; jack of all trades when it comes to magic.
Fel Priest: Warlock/Priest; Undead hybrid, more support than a Warlock and less healing than a Priest.
Abomination: Warlock/Druid/Warrior; Undead hybrid, shapeshifting Undead can turn into various abominations.
Witch Doctor: Priest/Shaman; Troll hybrid, mix of both class abilities with more abilities over time and access to debuffs and curses.
Samurai: Alternative Paladin/Warrior; Orc Blademaster or Night Elf Sentinel; doesn't use magic but has access to other buffs. Special katana and wakazashi weapons.
Ninja: Rogue alternative; wears cloth armor and is the ultimate glass Rogue but with alternative abilities such as shuriken, smoke bombs; special wakazashi weapons.
Runemaster: Warrior/Mage hybrid; Neutral starting area, "Hall of the Rune Lords" where player is an apprentice in an institution setting. Has both Alliance and Horde characters in starting area where the Rune Lords govern the span between each faction. Characters begin with Blacksmithing and Enchanting where the runemaster can imbue items with magic runes, can create golems/Mining golems and other special Runemaster items.
