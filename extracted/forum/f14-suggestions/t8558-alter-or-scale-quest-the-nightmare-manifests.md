Knowing the shades are tough we opted to use the Line of Sight strategy in the house, however the waves are spawning groups of 4 shades at a time that were completely overwhelming even for a full raid + extra.

I first thought that too many shades were spawning as they seem to be coming in groups of 4 at a time, but i found a post on the bug tracker seems to say that this is intentional? [https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow- ... ssues/2793](https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow-bug-tracker/issues/2793)

Having a quest require 40 people just so 1 guy can complete it is rough enough, this one currently would need 80+ people which is simply absurd.

Please consider dramatically altering how the shades spawn or some sort of scaling for this event so people can have a chance in hell of ever finishing this quest.
