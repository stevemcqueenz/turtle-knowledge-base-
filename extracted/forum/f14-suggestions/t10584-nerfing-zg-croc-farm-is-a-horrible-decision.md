The reasons it is a bad decision is multi-faceted.

ZG Crocs was an enjoyable and HEALTHY way to farm gold in turtle. People kept bijou prices low and the farm DID NOT flood the market with gold like Lasher farms do, which is now the new alternative every mage will be going too. If Turtle wow devs wanted to keep gold inflation down, then nerfing one of the few farms that does not contribute towards inflation is extremely careless. ZG Crocs is also not like lashers where it's easily learned and quite frankly braindead, there is a lot of learning and trial-error that goes into it for it to become efficient. The method provides more gold but the trade-off is the inconsistency and difficulty. It's something that takes most people days to get clean.

If we are generous and agree with their perspective that it's unfair for a class to be able to perform well in it's niche as an AOE farmer (which I don't, the very logic behind the nerf is steeped in resentment towards mages, and it's effects is nothing but lethargic). Then it's still insane from a balance perspective because they've essentially guaranteed the only people able to farm Bijous from crocs are now paladins and warriors.

Crocs are now a landmine that will insta charge and kill 95% of ZG raiders that are caught unaware, they will then continue to charge point blank in melee and chain stun more people. This change is not interesting, the difficult added to crocs is comically absurd, and the overall raid and server economy is ruined for this.

Bijou farming did no harm to the server and re-distributed wealth. Again, the gold made from it came from selling those bijous to other players, that gold comes from wealthy players, the mages new gold then trickles out to people who make consumables/tems for those mages. There was literally not a single thing wrong with it. I wish I could have good faith in the turtle wow devs for wanting to make an even playing field, but making crocs a charge nuke instead of giving alternatives to other classes seems like they're simply listening to ignorant and bitter people.
