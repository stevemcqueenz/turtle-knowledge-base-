With the abundance of quests in vanilla, and with the quests added by turtle ontop, the space in our quest logs run out in literally no time. It's not a rare occurance that one can't pick up all the quests in a single zone, not to mention that people like to keep dunegeon quests and traveling quests as well as quests for the zone they are in. I would propose a 50%-100% (double) increase in quest log capacity, but if the cap was removed altogether, I wouldn't be too mad about it either.
