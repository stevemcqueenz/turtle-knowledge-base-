# Decurse t warlocks, demagic to mages

## Post 115724 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-07T12:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115724#p115724 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

It's not strictly a suggestion, I'm mostly just curious about your pov.
I think warlocks should be able to decurse and mages to demagic.
Flavorwise my reasoning is that warlocks are the ultimate experts of curses and with this knowledge they also know how to remove them if needed.
Same with mages and magic effects.
Gameplaywise my reasoning is that in a cross-faction server, we have 3 classes to remove poison (dru, shaman, pally) and disease (priest, shaman, pally) from friendly targets, but curse (dru, mage) and magic (priest, pally) can only be done by 2.
Honestly, I'd love to push warlocks with some added utility but at the same time I'm a bit afraid to give mages additional tools.

I highly disagree, just because your class is adept at applying something they should not necessarily be good at removing it.

With that reasoning, warriors and feral Druids should know how to stop bleedings, not just to apply them.

On a gameplay level, Magic effects can also be eaten by warlocks felhounds. Which leaves only curses as an effect only two classes can remove. And both of these classes can be played on both factions.
And while Alliance has one more class who can deal with magic debuffs, shamans have their own way of dealing with some of the more debiletating magic effects in raids.

Its a change that would not solve a real gameplay need while eroding the classes further. Hitting the same wound created by some of the already existing classchanges.

So this hasto get a -1 from me
