# Suggestion to incentivise wpvp - player tokens

## Post 116975 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-01-16T12:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116975#p116975 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is a suggestion for an addition to the rumoured pvp mid-patch with wpvp towers and faction leader loot. To encourage wpvp engagement, I suggest that lvl 60 (or 51+) players drop tokens in world similar to the teeth, flesh etc dropped in AV. These could then be turned in for a number of different rewards, such as:

 1 token = 50 honor
 10 tokens = 10 consumes (BoP, power level slightly above Nordanaar Herbal Tea)
 1000 tokens = unique enchant, for example chest enchant with something like 15stam+100 armor)
 2500 tokens = epic pvp trinket (same as existing pvp trinket but 2 min cd and like 5-10 stam or resilience)
 5000 tokens = cool title, “Defender of the Horde/Alliance” or similar

These are not perfectly tuned but ideas to get the discussion going. For context, a recent horde SW raid on Tel'abim yielded around 85hk for the players. There should be a mix of rewards relatively easy to get (e.g. honor, consumes) and rewards that require a long grind (trinket, title).

This should be an easily programmed and implemented feature that could go a long way to incentivise wpvp, particularly for Tel’abim where there is a big interest among players to engage in this. Some material rewards would go a long way to make players engage in it more.

Looking forward to comments for improvements to these suggestions. And please refrain from the “wpvp is griefing” chatter.

1. wpvp ≠ city raids
This suggestion is meant to incentivize wpvp, but the only example provided is a city raid. Let’s talk about city raids a little:
 - For attackers, the incentive is already clear: loot and/or a big chunk of honor (10k?) for everyone in the zone when the objective dies.
 - The real issue is **why would anyone bother defending a city?** Most of you suggest force flagging, my proposal is quite different: if the objective dies, essential utilities like the ah and bank (but not the fp - that’s important) go offline for 2–3 hours. This motivates defense, and if it fails, the attackers move on to the next city, forcing the defending factions to follow since basic utilities are out of commission. If all cities are taken, it essentially calls for revenge.
 - As for nordanaar: it shouldn’t have faction leader loot or city raids at all. Same logic as world bosses - if you have crossfaction, enjoy its benefits, but accept its limitations.

 2. 51+
I leveled on telabim when the server was empty and everyone was leaving, so I know how brutal it can get. With how the vanilla pvp system works, some rankers will chase every drop of honor. For non 60s or fresh 60s, this creates a terrible environment where rankers farm them for minimal gains, while causing maximum frustration for the victims. It’s cannibalism, plain and simple, and doesn’t create healthy wpvp - especially now that telabim is entering another stagnation phase.

 3. Does this really incentivize wpvp?
 **Yes, it’s a neat addition** - earning tokens alongside honor is cool. **But does it actually incentivize wpvp** in the long run? Probably not. This feels like a cherry on top, not the cake or even a layer of it. It could go the way of bounty hunting dailies: fun at first, but quickly losing appeal when the rewards don’t feel worth the effort. Or it could go the way of the Blood Ring: rewards are too good, grind grind grind, gutting the pvp system from the inside out, only to be nerfed and forgotten.

 4. “wpvp is griefing”
For the record, the most active wpvp players (telabim) on both alliance and horde side are rogues, and their main incentive is griefing. Zone towers would provide a new foundation for wpvp.

As you also said, zone towers and city raids are the lasting incentives for wpvp. I would like the dev team to focus on those, as**im skeptical this suggestion would lead to a positive outcome**.
Beyond that, im working on a list of suggestions that might indirectly connect to this topic by giving level 60s more reasons to visit leveling zones for pvp reason.
