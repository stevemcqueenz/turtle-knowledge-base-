# Mining nodes spawn rates

## Post 121795 by RetroGaming7373 — 2025-03-05T20:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121795#p121795 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been looking around the RPPvE server doing some herbalism and mining, and I've noticed that the spawn rates for copper ore (and so presumably later ores) is pretty low, I can find over a dozen herbs before I see a copper ore, and it's usually already being worked by someone. I'm in no way trying to complain but it does make blacksmithing and engineering harder to level. Skinning is always as abundant as the amount of beasts near you, and herbalism is more rich in some areas rather than others, but nowhere I go can I really find a good supply of copper.

As a suggestion, I'm hoping we could just up the spawn rate a mere 10%, something small that should help out a bit, not looking to make any drastic changes like making herbs harder to find just to balance it, but just a minor increase to the amount of mining nodes. I can walk around during peak server hours or during more quiet times and either way I'm usually not getting any luck.

Hope to get some feedback about this, because maybe it's just my luck, which wouldn't hurt to know.   sad_turtle
