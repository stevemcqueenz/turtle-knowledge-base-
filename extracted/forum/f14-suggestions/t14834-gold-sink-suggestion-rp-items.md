# Gold Sink Suggestion: RP Items

## Post 101031 by Oonafaequeen — 2024-09-10T16:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101031#p101031 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As with any MMO that runs for a long time, gold gets added to the economy and naturally you need some ways to remove it gradually, normally by enticing players with things that provide no actual gameplay benefit but feel worth the money.

Turtle has added some things like this already with the Fashionista Shops as well as the limited time one we saw around the holiday. I hope that we continue to get more fashion options in these shops (particularly things that feel more 'Horde-centric' in design) and perhaps a short questline that will direct players to them so that they know they exist!

But with the preamble out of the way onto my suggestion.
RP Items

There already exist several quests out in the world that award objects that players can use even outside their intended questing zone to enhance the RP experience, Instead of having these players be forced to keep the quests in their log and also faction locking many of them, what if there was a Vendor placed in one of the various Neutral Goblin Cities that allowed players to buy these types of items at a decent price.

Allow people to do the mining or alchemy animation wherever, apply a non-invasive color aura or effect on players (would likely need to be limited to party/raid to prevent griefing). A simple channeling animation with a variety of effects available, fire, shadow, arcane, etc. I think you see where I'm going with this.
With the Toy Tab eventually being added to TurtleWoW we would even have a great place to store these, making it so people wouldn't need to feel dissuaded by clogging their Inventory.

(Some really rough mockups of item + Flavor/effect)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Ekb4Nwl.png)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/3gfe7zn.png)

There are plenty of normal players that like to collect goofy little things just to animate with or mess with while they wait for groups to form or hang with friends. I think that while the RP community would really enjoy having things like this even your average player would be interested in picking some things like this up to goof around.

To wrap up, Gold is continually added to the game and while there isn't a catch all solution, by adding things that don't affect gameplay that players can spend their coin on to have a little fun, it can help to cut down on it here are there.

If you have any other suggestions for effects feel free to leave them here, I'm interested to hear what other people think could be done if a shop like this were to be implemented.
