# Issue with Purchasing a Skin

## Post 39822 by Piccolo1833 — 2023-04-01T03:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39822#p39822 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi I think it's important for the developers to know that I had a bad experience buying something from your in-game shop. I recently purchased the Gnome: Necromancer Skin for my character only to find out that this was a permanent change, and the only way to fix this would be to ask for a refund or purchase a Token: Appearance Change for another 160 tokens. I assumed it worked like toys do in retail wow or how the illusions work here and could be turned on and off. And when I did get my points refunded and my character's appearance changed, it wasn't how she originally looked, it was the gnome skin without the necromancer skin. I'm not asking for a refund because I used my points to change her appearance slightly, I just think there needs to be some kind of warning for people so they don't end up making the same mistake, or make the item work like the illusions do. I think the skin idea is such a good one and I was so disappointed to find out that it worked the way it does.
