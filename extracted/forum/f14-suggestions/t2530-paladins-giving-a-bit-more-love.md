# Paladins; Giving a bit more love.

## Post 15743 by Grayhammer — 2022-01-13T16:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15743#p15743 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Paladin Changes:** V2 These changes are on top of what turtle wow wants to make here - [viewtopic.php?f=2&t=2197](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=2197) Unless stated otherwise these changes do not replace the current effects instead are additive.

Holy:
New Talent: Improved Blessing of Light (2/2) - 1%/2% of healing done to target is applied to all other party members effected by your Blessing of Light. (Row 5)
Holy Power (5/5) - (Row 6 -> Row 3) Replaces Consecration's location in tree.
Ever Lasting Judgments (1/1) - (Gives full benefit) (Row 6)

Protection:
New Talent: Blessed Divine Shield (1/1) (Row 6) - Divine Shield no longer drops threat instead taunting all enemies within 5 yards for the duration of the shield. Each strike against the paladin during this restores 1% total mana.
Improved Righteous Fury (3/3) - Additionally grants 1%/2%/3% damage reduction from all sources.
Guardians Favor (2/2) - Blessing of Freedom, Sacrifice, and Protection now no longer drop other blessings on top of increased duration and CD buffs.

Retribution:
New Talent: Commanding Presence (1/1) (Row 7) - On Hit Chance to deal 70% of normal weapon damage as Holy Damage, Judgment's now deal X Holy damage on top of stated effects.
New Talent:  Zealotry (1/1) (Row 3) - Exorcism and Holy Wrath are now usable on all enemies and now has a 100% crit chance against Undead and Demons.
Seal of Command (1/1) - (Removed)
Repentance (1/1) - (Removed)
Repentance replaced with Commanding Presence
Seal of Command replaced with Zealotry

Notes:
Seal of Light and Retribution Aura's scaling is now 1/4th of what it is currently. ![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/821088501491040337/930515261935726622/Paladin_with_two_guns.jpg)

 **Controversial changes: I would like but think they are a bit much.**
New Aura: Pursuit of Justice - is now an Aura you train Granting all party members your increased movement speed.
New Class Quest Skill: The Pursuit of Justice - Tied to the Paladin Class Quest at level 20 granting the paladin increased movement speed. This does not stack with Pursuit of Justice the Aura or other movement speed effects. (Pursuit of Justice)
Pursuit of Justice (1/1) - (Baseline) / (Removed) / (New Aura)
I would like the additional weapon damage from Command to also be counted as an Auto-attack and proc seals effects and on hits procs.
Blessing of Sanctuary and Holy Shield Should scale with AP/INT
