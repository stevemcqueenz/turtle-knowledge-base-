# Class Quests & Mechanics

## Post 23346 by Sapiverenus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-24T16:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23346#p23346 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Classes should have more unique means of experience gain [leveling] & ability gain/upgrading. Right now it's the low-effort Blizzard version: that means a lot of room to improve.

Have to be careful though. Input welcome.

 **Example:**
> Every odd level 1 - 9 Priests have to go to a holy place, a place of contemplation, partake in some communion, go on a tailored quest; whatever is appropriate for the Race in order to level up. If the level confers a new ability, there is more ritual or difficulty involved.

> Level 1- 9 Every even level requires a certain amount of quests with a certain degree of significance (depending on what quests are available for them) be complete in service of their race and/or faction and/or spirituality.

> For level 10, they have to have served players of their faction through heals, damage mitigation, combat contribution, AUCTION HOUSE ITEMS AT A COST (meaning less than vendor $$$ for the item) and to pray at some specific significant location as well and enter some 'communion' state (long pause in gameplay basically).

> This pattern could repeat all the way up to level 60.

I think classes should recieve less XP for doing things that aren't aligned to their class as well.
So a Warrior should get 1.1x XP for beating mobs up and killing them, but a Priest should get maybe .7x XP, and maybe none for killing creatures that don't "further their cause". Questing would offer something like 1.2x XP depending on the 'good' of the quest, Dungeons 1.1x XP,
and Priests would take less XP from other party members while getting some of it for "free"; so if they're in a duo they would take less from the other player, and recieve a bit more --> something like 55% XP for each player instead of 50% or whatever it is right now.

Overall the leveling pace shouldn't speed up. The more content the developers add, the more reason to slow it down in fact.

Game mechanics should correspond to the class.
