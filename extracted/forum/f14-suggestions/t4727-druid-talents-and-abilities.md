# Druid Talents and Abilities

## Post 28341 by Brokehoof — 2022-11-02T19:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28341#p28341 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've always like the variety provided with being a druid in vanilla/tbc. With a quick shift of form, you can go from being a tanky bear, to a vicious panther, and then a healing tree, and even to an owlbear-thing mage. Pretty cool stuff.

Some problems, though, that weren't fixed until much later makes the druid feel quite awkward when playing this class.

The first and foremost thing are potions and forms, meaning you have to shift out of form to take a mana or health potions. The downside to that is that you lose the safety provided by the form to do so, which often defeats the purpose of taking the consumable anyhow.

There's also the main defensive cooldown for druids, Barkskin, which also can't be triggered in feral forms. And I have no idea why. It works fine in moonkin form, why not the others? Again, to utilize it as a feral, you have to drop your protective form, then active it, then shift back, suffering the long moment of vulnerability during that process.

Then there's viability. The argument is made that as a hybrid class, the gift of being able to do everything is that you don't do any of them particularly well. The most outstanding example of this is probably the pinnacle of the Balance tree, the moonkin form, which is arguably the worst ranged damage spec in all of vanilla wow. Their CC is very limited, with roots only working outdoors, hibernate only working on dragonkin and beasts. Their AOE ability, hurricane, is on a long long minute cooldown.

I'd rather not go into the Restoration tree, as its play style leaves so much to be desired, at least personally.

Here are easy suggestions to fix some of the issues I mentioned:

 1) Let bears/cats/moonkin/trees drink consumables and remain in form!
 2) Barkskin should be castable in any form.
 3) Aside from upping the sustained damage of the moonkin embodied in the balance tree, how about adding some more utility? My favorite thing would be to have moonkin form allowing entangling roots to work indoors. Or, make it so that Hibernate will also affect humanoids, but only in moonkin form, or place these talents somewhere in the balance tree to make them desirable to other playstyles.
 4) Give hurricane two charges, on a minute cooldown. That way you could at least do 20 seconds of sustained ranged aoe damage per pull, which is usually adequate for most situations.

What do you think?
