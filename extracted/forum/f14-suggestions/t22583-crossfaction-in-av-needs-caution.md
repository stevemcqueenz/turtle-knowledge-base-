# Crossfaction in AV needs caution

## Post 157142 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-26T10:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157142#p157142 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I don't like that crossfaction battlegrounds are coming. But it's too late to stop it so at least I can suggest how to implement it correct way in Alterac Valley. This battleground needs special caution when implementing this system because it has many quests that could potentially break the game.

 1. Stormpike/Frostwolf Insignia trinkets.

Each faction has its own quest to get these trinkets. More importantly, these trinkets don't work the same way. Alliance one returns to Alliance's base and Horde one returns to Horde's base. If crossfaction means that you can complete quests from enemy faction, that would mean players could potentially acquire both to teleport between 2 bases.

 2. Reputation.

AV quests and general objectives allow you to gain rep with not only AV faction but also factions of opposite team. Ironforge and Orgrimmar rep are especially easy to get.
And please make Crossfaction voluntary. There are many people who queue AV only to raise their rep to Exalted with Frostwolf Clan/Stormpike Guard. They would be pissed off if they were assigned to the wrong faction.

 3. Quests of the opposite side should be doable.

AV is specific battleground because it has some "challenges" for example summoning world boss or cavalry. If you remove option to complete quests, such mercenary couldn't be as effective as playing normally in their own faction.

 4. Shredders.

Alliance and Horde use two seperate instances of Zinfizzlex's Portable Shredder Unit <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=zinfizzl>
Make sure players can't get both version because that would make this item even more oppressive.

 5. Trophies.

Each race drops its own trophy that can be turned in for gold and rep at base. Make sure crossfaction players also drop CORRECT trophies.
