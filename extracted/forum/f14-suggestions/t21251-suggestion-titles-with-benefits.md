# Suggestion: Titles with benefits

## Post 147463 by Partyboyza — 2025-08-21T10:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147463#p147463 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I good idea would be if the titles would also come with some form of practical minor benefits like:

 1.Reaching exalted with argent dawn would give +2% more damage done to deamons and undead and all damage recieved from deamons and undead is reduced by 2%.

 2. Reaching exalted with Shen'dralar would give 10 intelligence.

 3. Reaching exalted with waterlords would give +15 fire res and +2% more damage to all inhabitants of Molten Core.

 4. Reaching exalted with Thorium brotherhood would give +10 stamina

So on and so forth, this will incentive players not only to master these repuations but also make the content that they have cleared over and over again more easy.

Not to mention any materials that offer reputation would have more market valuation.

Only one title can be active at a time and can only be swapped outside combat.

I can't speak for PVP titles because I don't PVP.

Please consider the idea, thanks.
