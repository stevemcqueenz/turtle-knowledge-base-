# Polearms/Fist Weapons

## Post 85542 by Runescimitar0 — 2024-01-22T01:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85542#p85542 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I apologize if this idea has been suggested before, I am brand new to Turtle WoW (absolutely loving it by the way! Please come over to Tel'Abim, we need more players!)

My suggestion is a simple one. Fist Weapons and Polearms should be made part of the overall loot pool from the very beginning of the game. I see no reason why when we're looting grey or white swords/maces/axes that a spear or brass knuckle couldn't be in those options. I have always thought it was very odd that Blizzard did not include either weapon type into many loot options. I would love to be able to loot a simple spear to level with early on, in the same way that we're looting battle-axes and two-handed swords off of enemies.

I don't feel like this suggestion would drastically change the game or the tone of Vanilla, and would only enhance our role-playing experience and variety in weapon options.

What do you all think? Am I totally overlooking some downside to this?
