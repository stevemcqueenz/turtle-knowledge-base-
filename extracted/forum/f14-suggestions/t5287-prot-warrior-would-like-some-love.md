# Prot Warrior would like some love

## Post 32136 by G10stpd — 2023-01-05T14:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32136#p32136 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been playing warrior since 2005. I mostly like where the class is on Turtle in regards to the Arms and Fury trees. I will admit, I have always loved the sword and board style play but hated the lack of self sustain and low damage.

Buffs to the Protection tree could help the class while not affecting PvE DPS in raids, etc. I believe a few talent tree changes may make it more fun for protection warriors in PvP as well.

 1. Shield Slam ought to be available sooner in the Protection tree with a higher damage coefficient, might need to lower threat coefficient accordingly. It ought to be no higher than the 5th row of the talent tree. I would place it where Concussion Blow is and move that talent to beside Improved Shield Bash.

 2. In place of the last talent, where Shield Slam was, put in Spell Reflect. Requires shields, but any stance. Reflect the first spell cast on you for 5sec. 20 rage cost. 20 second cooldown.

 3. Move Improved Sunder Armor to beside Improved Taunt. In place of where it was, on row 4, add a "Victory Rush" type of talent with the pre-requisite being Last Stand. This ought to keep it available only to those who heavily favor Protection talents in their build since standard PvP build favors Enrage talent.

 4. Remove shared cooldowns of Intercept and Intervene. It's not like they remove roots or anything and you still have to stance dance.

 5. Remove shared cooldowns of Overpower and Revenge. This may be fun in PvP or while soloing with Improved Revenge.

By the way, thanks for this awesome server! It really is a solid vanilla plus type of experience. I am looking forward to what is in store in the future.
