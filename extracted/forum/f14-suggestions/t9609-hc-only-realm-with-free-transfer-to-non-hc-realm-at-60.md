# HC only realm with free transfer to non-HC realm at 60

## Post 62855 by Yidhra — 2023-09-11T14:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=62855#p62855 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The debate around letting Hardcore characters play on the same realm as non-HC characters is always centered around "There's no point if we can't brag about beating hardcore, or showing off our special status"
Which is fine, hardcore is a challenge and should be a nice shiny achievement.

However, it's become bad for regular players to play in the same areas. Can't group, can't share mobs, can't trade. Not to mention the grave stones piling up and just a complete waste of time since most die before level 30.

I propose making a HC only realm, where you can only play hardcore characters and once you reach level 60 on that realm you get a free transfer back to the normal realm if you desire to show off your achievement.

This alleviates both problems, HC can play with HC and normal players play with normals, AND hardcore characters can still show pride in their achievement at 60 once they transfer to the normal realm.
