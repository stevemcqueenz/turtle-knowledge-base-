# "Ninja looting" items in Dungeons and griefing

## Post 149306 by Archyy — 2025-08-30T08:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149306#p149306 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the rules stand now and as they were explained to me by a GM is:

 - IF someone ninja loots a item in dungeon you can go to discord with proof of that and a investigation will take place and if it's proven that he ninja looted actions will be taken (that is ok and how it should be) EXCEPT:
 - If the ninja looter has enchanting and he can disenchant that item is within his right to need on any item even if others in party need them for their spec or class, does not matter, he has a profession that can use it (disenchanting) so he is within his right to need on anything.

Now let's say you are a warrior doing countless arena runs for that Savage Gladiator Chain and a warlock in your party also needs on them with the sole goal of disenchanting them and wins. Doesn't that constitute griefing?

And that can be done with any item, as the rules stand now if you are a enchanter you can need on anything and since you have a profession that can "use" it you are in the clear...

NO this is not pulled out of my ass, this is after a discussion with a GM after a warlock ninja looted healing shoulders then dc'ed.

I made this thread as a suggestion to change the looting rules regarding to ninja looting, enchanting cannot be exempt from it (i am a enchanter btw) but i should not be ALLOWED to need on anything i want and grief my party just because i can disenchant them.

Let's say a member of my party has a green item equipped and a blue upgrade drops from dungeon and i also need on it and take it from him just to disenchant it. Isnt that griefing? Yes, yes it is.
