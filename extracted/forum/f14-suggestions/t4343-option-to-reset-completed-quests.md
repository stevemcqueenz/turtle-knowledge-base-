# Option to reset completed quests

## Post 25866 by Shamma (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-23T01:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25866#p25866 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are a lot of interesting quest chains in WoW. Some I often wish to play through again. The problem arises when these are high lvl. You can only play them again by creating a new character and lvling up to said high lvl.

There are also quests which offer the choice between different items all of which are good and you happen to choose the "wrong" one and regret your decision later. No way to go back.

How about an option to reset quests? If you choose to reset a quest previous rewards are removed (or if you sold them their vendor value in silver is taken from you). And you are free to repeat the quest.
