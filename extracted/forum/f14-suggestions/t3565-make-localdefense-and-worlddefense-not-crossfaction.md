# Make LocalDefense and WorldDefense NOT Crossfaction!

## Post 21445 by Moebius24 — 2022-08-08T09:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21445#p21445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Crossfaction on this server is great. However it goes too far in one aspect:
All chats are Crossfaction as well and that includes LocalDefense and WorldDefense.
This is unnecessary und problematic since those channels are there so you can organize the defense of your factions towns or an attack on the other faction. It's not easy to muster a defence if your enemy knows your every step. Also it's quite awkward.

My suggestion is to make these channels faction specific. Also since you can't chat in WorldDefense I would suggest making this possible. That way we would have a global chat where you can have internal communication with your faction. (for raids/defence etc.)
