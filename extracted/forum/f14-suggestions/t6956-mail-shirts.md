I'd like to suggest a fun addition: Mail looking shirts.

There are many armors in the game, that are revealing, and without a shirt or tabard, its unusable on some characters for Tmogging.
Or, there are many plate, or mail armors without anything defending your arms.

I see NPCs wearing the same armor as me, but he has more armor under that breastplate.
Example:
[![Image](http://www.kepfeltoltes.eu/images/2023/05/01/702armor_shirt.png)](http://www.kepfeltoltes.eu/view.php?filename=702armor_shirt.png)

So what im saying is, it would be nice, if some mail armor looking shirts were added to the game, so us, armor users could be more fashionable.
Could be added to Blacksmithing, even Leatherworking maybe. Or just vendors.
