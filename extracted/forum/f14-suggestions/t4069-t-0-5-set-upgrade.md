Hello fellow turtles

As you know the t 0.5 sets are one of the coolest sets and most epic chain quest in the game. And sadly the most time consuming and expensive to get it (and of course not worth compared with any raid loot)

So most of people never do the questline. They can get any item with best stats from any raid much quicker and cheaper

So my suggestion is make a upgraded version with superior stats that can worth the time and gold invested

My idea is create a quest line that requiere complete the full t 0.5 questline. And requieres have all the items

The quest ask you kill all raid bosses. Each one upgrades a piece of your set (8 pieces set)
Ragnaros. Onixya. Nefarian. Ossirian. C'thun. Kel'thuzad. Kazzak and Azuregos could be the objetives

What do you think? Maybe this give the people motivation to complete this cool questline always forgotten
