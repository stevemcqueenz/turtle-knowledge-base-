# Equalize the PvE and PvP xp event.

## Post 81551 by Thol (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-17T22:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81551#p81551 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

On the 20th with new patch 1.17.1 a new XP event will be introduced:
> Winds of Discovery event will begin upon the launch of our patch 1.17.1: Labor & Legacy which will provide 10% bonus EXP on Nordanaar and 20% bonus EXP on Tel'Abim, with new characters qualifying for a gift upon reaching level 20!

The PvE realm has a War Mode which increase XP by 20%, so the total bonus will be 30% during the event for the PvE realm and 20% for the PvP realm.

I think it would more fair if the PvE realm and the PvP realm had the same bonus. There's a lot more WPvP happening on the PvP realm while leveling than on the PvE realm with War Mode (I leveled on both realms), so it makes sense that the bonus should be at least the same.

I just think players should pick a realm based on their preferred mode and not based on a XP boost difference.

Thank you.
