# Pretty please shorten the Alliance route to Scarlet Monastery

## Post 153308 by Goldenfish — 2025-09-26T12:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153308#p153308 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Both walking from southshore and taking a boat to Alah'Thalas and then swimming can take **15-20mins**, which a bit much to just get to an instance, especially one that doesn't take that long to complete. I see a couple of options that could shorten the commute for alliance to sm and make sense:
- 1) Northeast in the trisfal uplands there is the castle controlled by the remnant of Lorderon, just add a gryphon in the ruins of the castle where horde players don't have anyone to kill for a quest. Either have the fp connected to southshore, Alah'Thalas or both. (Also, there are a couple of alliance quests in Trisfal uplands as well and constantly having to make that super long commute for 2 very short quest chains is kind of pointless so the content is kind of wasted)

- **2) When you swim from Alah'Thalas you run into a high elf ship, there is no one on it and it's in the middle of nowhere. So how about having a ship like that parked a little bit north of the northeastern coast of trisfal (where all the murlocs are and where you would normally get out if you swim to SM) except have it manned with HE spies so it makes sense. And have the player use the teleportation orb in Alah'Thalas to get to it.** *(EDIT: based on reply feedback it seems that this option is much better, because of pvp, alliance players won't be roaming a Horde starting zone with low lvl players which is happening now and would also happen if option 1 were implemented)*
I think both options still require a decent commute, especially option 1.

I am aware that lorewise the existence of SM kind of strategically benefits the alliance so running it as alliance doesn't make that much sense, but you still have quests for it and with the cross faction dungeon queing horde players often have to wait an eternity for alliance group members so I really think that this would be an improvement for everyone.

Also I would like to add that that the release of Stormwrought Descent, the Alliance now does have a good leveling alternative to SM but as previously mentioned most groups for dungeons in turtle are mixed so Horde players pay the price of the long commute as well. Also ally players can't just be expected to ignore SM altogether now since there is still specific loot, quests and the complex has the shortest instances which are often very convenient to run when you don't have time.

Overall I'm fine with a commute but I think this is literally the longest one for a leveling dungeon (from Alliance pov) and it's really a bit too much.
