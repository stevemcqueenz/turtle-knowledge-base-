# Dishonor System

## Post 76185 by Salooka277 — 2023-11-06T17:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76185#p76185 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am enjoying my time on Tel'Abim like most of the community however I am often faced with a situation that happens frequently, a much higher level player comes along and hits me with a single shot and kills me instantly or very quickly.

This is not a good gameplay mechanism and does not keep with the spirit of warcraft and the theme as a whole. I have had so many great PVP experiences and seen some dynamic situations turn out great with players of similar level battling it out and even being forced to group up or run away.

Sadly the dishonor system in the game currently has no real penalization for farming low level players and repeatedly killing them with no fun being had by anyone. I would like to suggest a system that is **disabled**  in cities and towns, only applying to the outside world and gives some reasonable punishment to the high level players who think it appropriate to kill low level players. Some of these would be exampled as:

- Unable to speak with any PVP vendors / buy PVP gear for a duration

- Debuff "dishonorable" which reduces a players attributes by a small amount

- Temporarily unwelcome from neutral towns (Booty Bay, Ratchet...)

- Honor set bonus will stop applying

- Eventually not welcome in any capital city due to dishonor
Edit:
- Become a target by the enemy faction worth more honor depending on dishonorable kills. Being tracked on the map

This is not a perfect list and none of these affects would be long-lasting, just a brainstorm of potential repercussions that would take into affect. The current dishonor system only 'protects' NPCs and they are currently being grinded for the new titles. I know that dishonor can affect ranking but if you're not 60 or interested in a high rank you can kill as many and camps as many areas as you want.

Thank you for reading and I look forward to some further dialog on the issue.

## Post 76195 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-11-06T17:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76195#p76195 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it would be cool if there was a stacking debuff that increases the honor reward if an opposing player kills you. You get 1 stack per dishonorable kill, each increases honor reward by 25%.

At 4+ stacks, you appear on the minimap for opposing players, as if they had "track humanoids" (even when you're in stealth!) - this will warn lowbies to stay away and encourage high level players to deliver justice.

At 8+ stacks, you become hostile with all neutral factions. Further dishonorable kills will reduce your reputation with them.

The debuff would last 2 hours and get refreshed with each new murder. Not to mention the dishonorable kills would remove honor points from you, even being able to go into negative, so being dishonorable will also have long-lasting effects.

However, nerfing stats and set bonuses for being dishonorable makes no sense, and neither does being hostile with your own faction. I think that's too far.
