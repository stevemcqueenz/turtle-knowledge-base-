Event with Moroes was changed several times.
I don't remember what is current state, but in the beginig fight contains 2 phases.
The first phase took place in an arena with spectator benches. During the fight, the boss would blind the tank and take control of someone in the raid. When his health dropped to zero, he teleported to the stage. The second phase of the fight began as the raid made its way to the stage. In this phase, the boss often dropped aggro and started attacking random raid members.
These transition between phases often bugged and boss became frendly for raid and didn't teleported to stage.
So, As I know, for now still only second phase of Moroes's fight

## Post 108118 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-16T11:07:00Z

I agree to a degree.
As others said, Moroes was changed many times and looks like this because of technical limitation. But I agree that he's very underwhelming at the moment. No summoning, no transformation, a regular guy running around with a smoke bomb.

The others have some kind of gimmick which I appreciate.
There is the guy who summons mobs during fight, I'm ok with the concept.
I really like the werewolf boss where tanks keep switching.
Spider has egg mechanics and aoe poison, I'm fine with those. My only problem is that 2 shamans (1 in each group) trivializes the whole thing. Maybe I'd add some magic and/or curse to the mix so a cleansing totem doesn't castrate a whole boss.
Grizikil... Honestly, imp part is the hardest part of the raid. Personally I'd make him like Hakkar: there are 3 rooms you have to clear before the boss so that it won't pull those mobs during boss fight. Instead of that I'd add an item to each room which you can deactivate, each making Grizikil easier. For example one adding daze to the fire aoe, the other making the aoe radius bigger, the third one applying dot on you when hit by fire. You'd have the option to run for the boss directly if you hate that timewaste at the rooms or kill everything as usual to have a 'normal Grizikil'.

tldr:
Moroes needs a change, now he's way too basic.
Spider is good but shamans hardcounter it.
Werewolf summoner is okay but a bit boring.
Tank-switch werewolf is goated, I love it.
Grizikil should have an option to skip the boring trash in exchange of a harder fight.
