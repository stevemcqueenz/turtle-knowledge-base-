# Gnomes and Goblin Hunters should be able to "tame" mechanicals

## Post 139378 by Veinar — 2025-07-15T06:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139378#p139378 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title suggests, Gnomes and Goblins Hunters should have the possibility of "taming" mechanicals. It would be interesting feature to include and instead of "feeding" a mechanical, the players should need to either "oil" them or repair them. Instead of a taming rod, to use a taming remote.

On how this should be introduced... I suggest either at level 10 when the hunter gets familiarized with each type of beast taming; or leave it purely as a new gnomish/goblin engineering skill. It sounds as if it could be a nifty aspect to apply and it gets closer to a hunter-tinkerer vibe that these two races have.

It sounds difficult to implement though, as not every mechanical should be tamed. What do you guys think?   happy_turtle

## Post 139483 by Mekk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-15T11:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139483#p139483 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While I don't disagree with this, as I could see this as a racial class quest for Gnome/Goblin Hunters, I'm trying to think of which Mechanicals would be fitting here.

You've got the Mechanical Chickens at the Goblin start, and they could probably add Mechanical Gorillas and Dragonlings to some areas (if they haven't already, I'm still new here).  Then there's the Mechanostrider, which would also be fitting.  Past that though, you start to get into bipedal territory, with the Arcane Nullifier and Harvest Golems.  Of course, they could always retexture a handful of beasts and make mechanical versions; retail did this with Mechanical Wolves, Spiders, and Scorpids.

As for what all is involved, they would need to create a new Family for each type of mechanical, assuming each type had different abilities, and assign the creatures to that Family.  Then they'd need to create all of the various ranks of abilities.  Most probably already exist, the Mechanical Gorilla could use the same abilities as the regular Gorilla, but they may want to add a few new ones; then assign those abilities to the Family and Hunter.  Finally, create the ability that allows the Hunter to tame them, and the quest(s) to unlock it.  I might be missing one or two things, but its "mostly" easier than it sounds, although it can be a little time consuming.  Oh, they'd also need to set up the diet, which I haven't actually looked into what all is involved in that.
