# crafting over haul idea.

## Post 24253 by F41lz0rs (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-09-02T19:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24253#p24253 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Few changes to give an idea of my overhaul intent.   Started out going to explain everything but too much so I stopped. will require  a fresh server to implement though.

Skinning and leather working changes

REQUIRES LEATHER WORKBENCH!!!!!!

Raw Light hide > Cured Light hide >  LW break down > Light leather x2-6, and light furx2-6

Raw medium Hide > Cured Medium Leather > LW breakdown > Medium Leather x2-5, med Fur x2-5

Raw heavy Hide> Cured Heavy hide > LW breakdown> heavy leather x2-4, heavy fur x2-4

Raw thick hide > Cured Thick hide > LW breakdown > Thick leather x1-3, Thick fur x1-3

Raw Rugged leather> Cured Rugged hide>  LW breakdown > Rugged leather x1-2, Rugged fur x1-2

Spiders/scorpions/turtles skin for carapace not hide.     spiders breakdown for silk, others for scales.
 ***
Fur and silk from skinning spun by tailors to yarn then to bolts. Bolts no longer made by dropped cloth

Mining changes

Raw metal Aggregate > prospected > smelted
new gem dust items for things like “glinting” or gem studded.
Remove silver/gold/truesilver mines and make them chance on drop with prospecting.
Prospecting and smelting to be green until the last few skill lvls of each rank 75-150 etc.

HERBALIST:  stays the same but adds 4 new trade goods called poultices used by them self for first aid use.

FIRST AID:  use of herbalist Poultices to craft a new bandage for each cloth type, this bandage will continue to heal you if you use it for at least of half of its charge duration. And will heal more than regular heavy bandages.

ALCHEMY:  left alone, requires fire to craft.

TAILOR: require workbenches only tweaks to mats and skill upgrades to be explained down below.

RECIPE FIXES:   with new crafting materials, tweak the stats of many items to make them usable in comparison to dungeon blues of the same lvl.   Change many unique greens in professions to blues. Boost stats or base armor/dps to make them desirable.

SKILL MATTERS:  each rank of profession earned, grants +1 to all stats crafted on an item of the below rank recipes.
only for LW, BS, tailor, and enchanting.( raw stat enchants like +1 to stats. or +5 stam on wrists.)
EXAMPLE:    big bronze knife crafted at lvl 100 BS has 10dps 3 stam
If crafted by skill 151-225  the same dagger gets 11dps and 4 stam.
If crafted by skill 226-300 the same dagger gets  12dps and 5stam.

This will give master craftsmen some relevance to crafting older equipment.  Now compare the masterwork knife with 12dps and 5 stam useable at lvl 15 with
the assassins blade with 15.53 dps 3str and 4 agi usable at lvl 19. the gap in power is closed but still an upgrade. Also allows beginner pvprs in this twink bracket to preform even if they cant afford the best.

many more ideas, I don’t wanna type it all out cuz people probably will not like the idea to begin with.
