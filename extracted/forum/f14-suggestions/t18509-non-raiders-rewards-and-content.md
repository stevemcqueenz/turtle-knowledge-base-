The raider part of my brain is instinctively screeching in pain and trying to hiss and claw at this idea.

Thankfully the rest of my brain works so I can judge this with a relatively stable mind.

I can see where the intention lies. Giving players a viable alternative gearing method so they don't have to do something they don't like would definitely be a good change in theory, but unless you tweak it properly, this could cause some problems; one of them being... Effort. 5 mans simply take less people, less general prep (consumes), less gear, etc.

Now, you could always just make the grind super long or RNG extremely bad, but you'd have to tow a fine line. How would emblem distribution work, prices for items, are there rarer emblems you purchase better gear with, could you trade in little emblems for the bigger emblems so you can buy higher tier gear, etc.

This has the potential to be something really fleshed out and surprisingly balanced but you'd have to put more work into presenting the idea than what I'm seeing.
