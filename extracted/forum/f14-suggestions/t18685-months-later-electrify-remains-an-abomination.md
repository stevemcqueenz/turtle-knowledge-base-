# Months later, Electrify remains an abomination

## Post 127183 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-17T19:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127183#p127183 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the release of CC2 last year there were atleast a dozen posts arguing why Electrify was a colossal mistep.
This has never changed. It remains one of the most unwarranted, poorly designed talent changes of CC2.

Please explain to me why elemental shamans get practically a mana tax on a majority of our spells in pvp.
Before 2 stacks we deal way less damage but pay the full amount of mana. Why?

Elemental shamans in vanilla, as it is widely known, are as close to a glass cannon as it gets. Zero hard cc, zero escapes. If we get caught, we're done. Our burst WAS our escape, and the playstyle of the spec. Now, in the CC2 meta, after having played BGs regularly since patch I feel confident in saying we are the least bursty caster class of all. Smite priests are an utter joke, yet you keep buffing them.

Something devs obviously did not consider is world pvp. Our burst here, as described above, is our escape. Elemental Fury was a CORE PART OF THE DESIGN of the class survivability. Now, in a scenario when I get jumped in world, explain how to let me get 2 stacks so I can finally deal my normal damage to my opponent (lets say an endgame geared retri pala), before I'm dead?

Another thing is that its obvious that devs made the decision based on 2-3 endgame-geared oppressive individuals.
But did they consider a relatively fresh 60 looking to try elemenal in pvp in blue/tier1?

I'll say again what me and so many others have said since patch: Remove Electrify, reinstate Elemental Fury.

Compared to iceblock, poly, blink, fears, shields, etc I'd definitely say Elemental has the weakest survivability if targeted. It is possible as you say to heal though it on occasions if uninterrupted, provided, again, that they have endgame gear. Before CC2 we also had the burst as defense, but now and particularly with the 15 sec timer on stacks, we can hardly count on it at all.

Another poor design feature of Electrify is that spells need to land before you generate a stack. Why? It means that a spell that is resisted for any reason does not get you a stack. Max range lightning bolts also can take seconds to land, which means we need to wait before casting the second spell if we want to benefit from the stack. In theory, the second and third spells cast should have 1/2 stacks (thus bringing us to 50/100 percent power), but in practice if we get to hardcast it means we may not have 1 stack until the third spell is cast.

All Electrify does in the current CC2 pvp meta is screw one specific spec over while having a negligible impact on the burstfest twow has created.
