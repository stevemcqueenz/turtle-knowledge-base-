# Theramore long questline travel solution

## Post 138474 by Acepox (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-08T18:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138474#p138474 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Given the security failures at the Barrens - Theramore border with regards to the burning of the Shady Rest Inn, and considering the repopulating of Bael Hardul, I believe it would be appropriate for Theramore to build a simple guard tower with a flightmaster at the top connecting to Theramore, Bael Hardul and Thalanaar.

This would secure routes to both of those latter locations and facilitate the investigations (most notably, the very long quest chain) into what happened to the Inn.
