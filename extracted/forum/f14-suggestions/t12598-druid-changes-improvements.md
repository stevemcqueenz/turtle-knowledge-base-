# Druid changes, improvements

## Post 83965 by Templar85 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-01-08T08:19:00Z

Druid problems                          Yellow text means modified or additional suggestions

- Barkskin only useable in caster form.

- Only 1 healer druid is optimal in a 40 man raid. Can't have multiple druid hots on the same target. The second druid with less healing power has restricted gameplay, half of his/her toolkit not functioning, only his/her direct healing can be used on targets who have hots from a druid with higher healing power

- Druid's dodge chance and rage regeneration by getting hit are contradictionary

- Innervate mana regen based on the target's spirit rather than the druid's stats

- Potions and useable items not useable while shapeshifted.

- Druids doesn't have enemy's nature resistance lowering spell.

- Roots can't be used indoors.

- Improved Thorns talent not good enough, high dodge chance of tank druids make Thorns useless.

- Tranquillity is the worst healing spell a resto druid can have. Very rarely used in any raid. Druid need to stand still for 10 sec and near to the party members, also it has a 5 min cooldown. Inefficient in many ways

- Druid version of Atiesh is the worst of the 4 of them. Aura mana regeneration is abysmal. Need serious rework. Separate into 3 different variations or make it useful for all 3 specs in one

- Omen of Clarity eating up queued Maul

- Druids could have out of combat res. For example 15 min CD out of combat res but when ressing other Druids the cd not applies. Druids keep looking after each other.

Solutions

- Barkskin is useable in feral forms but will have restrictions

- Multiple hots can be on the same target from different druids, but with restrictions. Maybe 30% less efficient the second same type of HOT and 70% less efficient the 3rd same type of Hot on the same target

- Make possible to have 2nd druid healer in a 40 man raids by adding more healing options. Separating Dreamstate healer (single target focused, 70% direct heal , 30% HOT toolkit) and Tree of Life healer gameplay (Aoe focused 70% HOTS , 30% direct heal toolkit) style so they complementing and not restricting each other.

- New spells and talents that allows druids to have good level of dodge chance but still gain rage and do threat on enemies

- Innervate modified to causes the target to regenerate mana by half the druid's and half by target's stats. This way not only priests gain good value out of it but other classes whom don't have much spirit.

- Druids can use useable items while shapeshifted but potions and items that affects health/mana/rage cause 10-30% less result. For example 1000 heal potion will heal only between 900-700

- Faerie fire or Insect Swarm lower nature resistance by 20 on the target.

- While Omen of Clarity is up instant casts got effected by it instead of queued up abilities

- Roots can be used in doors but with restrictions.

New spells and talents + reworked old ones

Balance Tree

Barkskin

 2 minute cooldown Lasts for 15 seconds. It is useable while shapeshifted, but the the melee combat speed penalty is increased from 25% to 35% and overall 15% less dodge chance but + 20% increased healing received from Hots and indirect spells. Druid with a gear which provides 25% dodge chance will have 10% dodge while using Barkskin.

Innervate

Causes the target to regenerate mana equal to 50% of the casting Druid's maximum mana pool and 100% of the targets spirit over 10 sec but the target can not gain more mana than the caster druid's 70% maximum mana.

Combat Resurrection

- Combat Resurrection CD reduced by 1 second to a maximum of 15 mins reduction. Reduction for every 2 Rejuvenation thicks and every 6 Regrowth ticks. Time reduction only applies if the healing ticks are not over healing but effective heals on targets. Only maximum Rank Regrowth and Rejuvenation can lower the battle ress CD

Entangling Roots

Roots can be used in doors and less likely to break early but can only cast 3 times on the same target in a 45 seconds timeframe. For the 4th attempt the target becomes immune to Roots and after the root effect ends causes the target to be enraged for 4 seconds increasing melee, ranged damage and gain 20% movement speed.
Indoor Roots target can only be humanoids beasts, demons and undead.

Rank 5
Reduces cast time of your wrath spell by 0.5 seconds additionally gain 5 spell penetration buff which stack up 5 times, lasts 15 second and increases Insects swarm duration on the target by 2.5 seconds

Hurricane

Improved Hurricane

Cast time increased to 3 seconds, Druid can target himself/herself with it, then Hurricane becomes an aura around the druid. Following where ever goes but druid's movement speed reduced by 20%
