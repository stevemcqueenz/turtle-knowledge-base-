# New class idea - Spellbreaker

## Post 75870 by Heimdallr (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-04T14:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75870#p75870 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

I was thinking what to add to the game so it would be both in accordance with lore, and quite unique to WoW gameplay.

I was thinking about getting this Shadow Hunter that would be a hybrid healer/medium range dps/Support build trees, instead of casual -dps/tank/heal or dps/dps/dps, but I had a small flow and I thought - Damn, it would be grand to see a supporting unit that is even more supporting in PVE/PVP not just nuke, heal or tank.

I've written few examples of abilities and talents and of course numbers aren't anyhow balanced, just wanted to show some sort of scale. Tell me what do you think. I assume that if someone would really like to implement this, Horde should get Shadow Hunter to be not really a counter part, but provide also beneficial support.

Spellbreaker race restriction - High Elf

Basic skills

 **Throw(toggle)** - attack range 15 yards - Medium range attack
 **Melee Attack**  - Melee Attack
 **Block** – Use shield to block attacks
 **Dodge** – even though Spellbreaker is wearing heavy armor, Elven design of the armor and enchants that it wields, allows him to move in a way no human would be able to. Spell Breaker is able to dodge attacks while wearing plate armor. (bonus X to dodge)
 **Elven Magic (Passive)** - Spellbreaker is immune to silence and has higher chance to resist spell interrupt

Spellbreaker sample Spell list

 **Dispel** - Dispels single beneficial spell from enemy
 **Mass Dispel** – Shatters X buffs from targeted area.
 **Spell Steal** - Steals beneficial spell from the target and puts it on Spell Breaker
 **Spell Break** - Interrupts spell with such supreme power, that caster  forgets that spell words, preventing him from casting that spell  for 5 seconds
 **Banish** – Banishes summons including totems that were summoned by target
 **Magebane** – Spellbreaker can choose what school of magic to lock for his target for 8 seconds
 **Globe of invulnerability** – Spellbreaker channels this ability  for 3 seconds spinning his double headed sword and creates a protective ward for himself. Protective ward neglects all Direct spells that target Spellbreaker during this time
 **Feedback (Passive**) – Each hit made by Spellbreaker in melee/range burns part of target’s mana
 **Mana Razor(Toggle)** – Spell breaker coats his weapon in his own mana, extending  and sharpening it. Each ranged attack has +5 yards, penetrates 10% of target armor and deals additional X% damage but costs 25% of total mana per attack.
 **Dampen magic** – Dampens magic used against the targeted party member, decreasing damage taken from spells by up to X and healing spells by up to X
 **Mana Link** – Spell Breaker creates a link between himself and one target to drain mana from him as long as the targets remains in X yards of him. Spell has two seconds to setup the link, and If the target escapes before this is set, Spellbreaker suffers 40% slow for 2 seconds and cannot cast spell. One does not simply open his soul and forcefully connect it to another.
 **Control Magic** - Takes over a summoned unit
 **Spell Warding** – Spellbreaker puts a protective ward on his shield that increases his resistances to all schools of magic (except holy and nature for obvious reasons) by X for 5 seconds
 **Spell Reflection** -  Gives the Spellbreaker the ability to reflect incoming spells back at their caster.
 **Arcane Strike** – Strikes down with Arcane infused sword, dealing X(+int) arcane damage
 **Infused Throw** – Spellbreaker consumes one of buffs stolen from his victims and adds X bonus damage of that type(shadow for shadow fire for fire etc.)
 **Magic Predator** – Spellbreaker is hunting his prey ruthlessly. Whenever enemy casts any spell, he appears on  Spellbreaker’s minimap.(would be cool to see type of magic as well so spellbreaker coul beforehand prepare for that school)
 **Shield Bash** -  Allows the Spellbreaker to stun and disrupt enemies with a powerful shield bash.
 **Tactical Defense** -  Enhances the Spellbreaker's ability to parry and block incoming attacks, reducing damage taken.
 **Magic Infusion** - Empowers the Spellbreaker's melee attacks with arcane energy, adding bonus damage and effects to their strikes.
 **Arcane Precision** - Increases the Spellbreaker's accuracy and critical strike chance with melee attacks.
 **Spellblade Techniques** Allows the Spellbreaker to cast beneficial spells on themselves and allies during cmbat, providing buffs
 **Arcane Nova** - Unleashes a burst of arcane energy, damaging and weakening enemies in an area around the Spellbreaker.
 **Enchantment Mastery** - Enhances the Spellbreaker's ability to enchant their weapons with magical effects, adding elemental damage or status effects to their attacks.

Sample Talent Trees

 **Spellbreaking Mastery** – Spell Breaking Tree
 **Arcane Combat** – Melee tree
 **Arcane Striker** - Ranged support

Sample Talents

 **Magic Resistance** – Increases resistance to all schools (except holy and nature) by X/X/X
 **Channel Magic( Last in Spellbreaking Mastery)** - Moves  any magic effect(can pick if positive or negative) from any unit to any unit.
 **Improved Dampen Magic** – Improves dampen Magic effect by X decreasing magical healing taken by even higher amount.
 **Magic Dispersion(Last in Arcane Combat)** -  Grants the Spellbreaker an aura that provides spell resistance to nearby allies.
 **Improved Mana Razor(Last in Arcane Striker)** - adds  5%/10%/15% of Intelligence to damage, lower cost of mana by 5%)
 **Improved Feedback** – Increases mana burn with every attack by X
 **Improved dispel** – Allows to dispel also negative effects from friendly targets
 **Arcane Supremacy** - Improves damage for every spell stolen and currently on spell breaker
 **Improved  globe of invulnerability** – increases the time by 1/2 second
 **Blade Mastery** -  Enhances the Spellbreaker's melee combat skills, increasing their damage with swords, shields, and other melee weapons.

If you check the link in my signature, I've been doing a destandardization of classes by adding impactful and immersive racials, so each class feels unique depending on the race of choice.

The Spellbreaker of W3 is something I thought about for High Elf Warriors, making them that way through racials, some ideas were quite similar to the ones you've listed here. The classes of WoW are standardized amalgamations of the classes from W3, but through changes to the classes depending on race of choice, the unique W3 classes can re-emerge, instead of adding them as new classes beside the standardized ones.

High Elf Paladin uses mana not rage, so there it could be interesting too, since they are not exactly like the TBC Blood Knights; evoking the Holy Witch Hunter / Inquisitor archetype. But that is not how Spellbreakers are in Warcraft, and their race is very much pro-magic, and Spellbreakers are explicitely described as elven warriors specifying in spell-breaking, so the High Elf Warrior suits the role the most.

Regardless of whether the Spellbreaker would be a new class or the High Elf Warrior, I like what you came up with, a creative and insightful suggestion. I'd definitely like your ideas implemented, one way or another.

They are perhaps the only known warrior class of High Elves (and Blood Elves), and the generic warrior in wow didn't exactly match High Elves (Blood Elves in TBC couldn't even be warriors), but thankfully Turtle WoW allows warrior high elves which in my opinion presents a perfect opportunity to turn them into Spellbreakers.

The WoW players classes had much more depth in Warcraft, and they could be unique again, depending on their race.
I'll add Spellbreaker lore for context:

Spellbreakers (or spell breakers) are a type of blood elven warriors trained to destroy spellcasters by disrupting and contorting magical energies to their advantage.

From RPG

Spellbreaker blades

The deadly, two-bladed swords of the spellbreakers strike fear in the hearts of wizards and sorcerers. The blood elves' elite warriors employ these frighteningly effective weapons to execute outlawed spellcasters, necromancers and other evil practitioners of magic. This blood-elven two-bladed sword is the bane of any evil spellcaster. The blades can cast globe of invulnerability when spun, and can make casters forget spells.

Spellbreaker feat

The powers of the spellbreaker are terrifying to those with knowledge of magic, for their blows shatter the mana from a spellcaster's body. Their abilities focus on destroying spells.

W3:
Spell Breakers are elven warriors who are trained to disrupt and contort magical energies. These Blood Elven warriors, fresh from the destruction of their homeland, have turned a new eye towards... **Alah'thalas.**

Yea, I just tried to redesign stuff but I didn't try to balace it lore/skillwise just becasue it's a concept  and of course as I said, I wanted it to be turtle style, so go nuts, but as long as it fits gameplay and lore.

The concept you said about extending some classes or use them as a "template" instead of designing new class would be a cool idea, D&D had it and projected it through games in that setting. You could be a base class Paladin, and then choose your way further for example to be an oathbreaker.

All the ideas, whether new class, extended, reworked are fine, but I was wondering how to fit that for a bigger expansion, and as far as I know, Turtle team is not into adding new classes, at least not soon.

If you think about it from design perspective, you want to have more and more, while still keeping the game consistent, not like retail does.

It would be  grand to get new classes like that or shadow hunter or new specs, or a way to build your own character to mimic them.

My idea would be to use a system that allows you to have multiple skins, skills and talents and pick the ones you want, but not like Ascension does, so hear me out on this:

As you've mentioned, building a Spellbreaker out of a warrior - cool thing but he does use rage, mortal strikes, techniques that doesn't appeal me visually. Personally I would like to build a spell breaker and get skins that look like him or Blood elf version, so I could do my own concept here, build a story around it you know, that fun RP stuff that everyone wants(otherwise why would people even Tmog stuff?)
Then, have access to a spellbreaker path and pick spells to replace my warrior abilities so you wouldn't see a spellbreaker charging and thunderclapping people.

This could be extended to a top version of the system, where you can put tons of new spells and graphics but they wouldn't differ from each other very much balance wise - You can cast fireball as a mage, but wouldn't it be cool to have arcane bolt of some sort? so you could say - I want to build an arcane mage, not cast fireballs! and use arcane spell connecting to your arcane talents. You could be a shadow hunter and instead of allowing all paths of what that class can do on paper, give player ability to pick whether he would use some sort of death related bolt or voodo type of bolt etc. Same things, different skins and talents.
The only problem here would be how to contain that, in a way that a certain specc would be viable and not absolutely wiping out a full other class.

So to summarize - I would gladly see new classes specs or ways to play, and my spellbreaker here was described as a pvp and slight pve support, rather than full blown dps/pvper giving few new mechanics

and on the other hand, would be grand to be able to pick a concept, buy/farm skins for it and have a list of replacable abilities that would give it a new look and concept, while not giving it power to just make some classes obsolete.
(this works on Ascension but there the classes doesn't have much sense so that's why it would be good to see it under Turtle)
