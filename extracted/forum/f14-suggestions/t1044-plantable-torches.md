# Plantable torches

## Post 5910 by Volitare — 2020-05-10T17:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5910#p5910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When the dark nights fall and it's time for the torches and lanterns to come out, we can't both fight and light the way with the torches.
So it would be a pretty nice feature to be able to plant the torches into the ground right from our inventory. It would provide a small area of visibility where the adventurers could stand their ground.
We could also be able to loot the torch again, when we're ready to move on. After all, it has a 1 hour duration :)
