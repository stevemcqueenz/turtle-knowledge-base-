## Post 48644 by Darkstromkar — 2023-06-03T06:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48644#p48644 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Right now the server is in very good health, people are just getting up to level 60 and starting to equip themselves.

This server has special conditions where a lot of people who have just joined have to face each other in battlefields with people who have been playing for years.

This is not a problem, the one who plays in this version hopes to be able to dedicate enough hours to become a rival of these players, but this can become especially frustrating when you realize 2 things: 1º That the classic system of ranks It is very slow, it can take between 6 months and 1 year to obtain the equipment and the second problem is that once you obtain that equipment, which is theoretically the best in the game for pvp, you are still not up to par with your opponents.

So anyone who's even a little bit smart is going to prefer spending their time raiding because they're going to equip faster and better "for pvp" than for "pvp".

What I suggest:

 1º Buff the R10 and reputation team to match that of BWL, and the R12,13,14 set Match it to AQ40.

 2º Change the honor system to something more "friendly" where instead of investing the current 2000 HOURS!!!.... Yes.... the average is 2000, drastically lower this by equaling the difficulty.
I suggest just lowering the Decay instead of the aggressive 20% leaving it at 10%, you don't change the system, but you don't bore people with an archaic and outdated system.

 3º It would also not be bad if alternative set pieces were added for classes that can play various roles (this suggestion is valid for any set both pvp and pve) it is frustrating to see how some sets are totally useless for certain spects.
