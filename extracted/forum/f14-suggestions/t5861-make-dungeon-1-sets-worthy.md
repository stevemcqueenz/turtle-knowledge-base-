# Make dungeon 1 sets worthy

## Post 36926 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-05T23:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36926#p36926 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Better Dungeon 1 Sets**

Since the early vanilla changes, these sets have been next to useless. That is a shame and I think improving them
would help everyone enjoy dungeon running at early lvl 60 more: hunting for actually useful set items.
Remember how changes have already been made to certain T1 gear - why not do the same and improve the dungeon sets? :-)

My logic would be a general increase of the set items with 30-50% stat increases, rounded down. Full "balance" is not a priority as this is by no means endgame items but just early 60 dungeon sets that should be made useful for fresh 60s.

Instead, the priority is to make sure they aren't too close to the upgraded Dungeon 0,5 sets, as that would make the hard work to obtain the latter futile - a very important thing to avoid!

 **What I suggest is something like this (with chest pieces as reference):**

 **Beaststalker's Tunic:**
+ 2 agility
+ 7% pet armor

 **Breastplate of Valor:**
+ 5 strength
+ 2 agility
+ 1 stamina

 **Devout Robe:**
+ 6 SP
+ 2 mp5

 **Dreadmist Robe:**
+ SP 6
+ 7% pet's armor

 **Lightforge Breastplate:**
+ SP 6

 **Magister's Robes:**
+ 7 SP
+ 9 decrease magical resistances of target

 **Shadowcraft Tunic:**
+ 1 to hit

 **Vest of Elements:**
+ SP 8,
+1% attack and casting speed

 **Wildheart Vest:**
+ 5 sp,
+ 2 mp5

Keep in mind that these changes can potentially also help fresh 60s a bit in their early pvp. Taking dps casters as an example, if you come right from 60 dungeons with +60-100 spell power for example, you are much better off than just having around +25 from a blue weapon for example.
