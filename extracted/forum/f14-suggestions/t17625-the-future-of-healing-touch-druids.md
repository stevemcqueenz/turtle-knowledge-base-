# the future of Healing Touch druids

## Post 120309 by Shambakriger — 2025-02-18T13:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120309#p120309 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi!:)
A little backstory: I first joined the server shortly after CC1 released. I levelled my druid to 60, geared Feral as my MS and slowly started gearing Resto as my OS. Due to the comp of my guild at the time, whenever I was called upon to heal instead of tank/dps, we most often already had 1-2 druid healers in the raid. This meant I spent most of my time playing a HT/Moonglow build, both because it was more mana efficient with my slightly shitty gear, but also because it made sure that I didn't "step on the toes" of any of the other rdruids. Usually the priority for resto druids in raids would look like this, one Tree druid followed by one Regrowth/NG druid lastly followed by one HT/Moonglow druid. I loved the absolute enormous spec variance of CC1 resto druids and how well they could synergise with eachother. CC2 attempted to build on this idea of synergy, but certain changes, whether intended or not, actually ended up working against that.
In CC2 the mana cost of Regrowth was severely reduced, making the once mana-hungry resto druid almost as mana-efficient as HT/Moonglow (which was one of its original selling points). On top of this, the passive Effloroscense was added to Tree druids, allowing them to overwrite eachothers HoTs with much less drawback. These changes made HT/Moonglow, though still very good for tank healing, incredibly underwhelming for raid healing. I have two sets of ideas on how to possibly carve out a new niche for HT druids, I'll start with the less radical one:

(changed talent)
 **Improved Healing Touch:** Reduces the cast time and global cooldown of your Healing Touch spell by 0.5 seconds
This change serves to work in tandem with Aessinas Bloom and the T2.5 for resto druids by allowing HT druids to use r3 HT when raid healing without clipping their gcd whenever Nature's Grace procs. Normally r3 is rarely used due to it having a lower spell coefficient, but because healing from Aessina's Bloom doesn't care about that, it could create an interesting niche for HT druids, especially lower geared ones.

(changed talent)
 **Aessina's Bloom:** Your Healing Touch spells have a 60% chance to instantly heal a friendly target equal to 6 seconds worth of Regrowth or Rejuvenation if the target is affected by either spell.
This change mainly serves to buff HT for raid healing by allowing Aessina's to proc off of more HoTs, meaning a HT druid would be more effective depending on the amount of other druid healers. The procs from Regrowth and Rejuvenation are not meant to stack since HT is already quite good for tank healing.

I believe these two changes would already go a long way in creating more of a niche for HT/Moonglow, as it stands right now the spec is only used as something you "swap" to for intense tank healing fights like Twin Emps or Patchwerk.

My next idea serves to widen this niche by creating a uniqute Moonkin/Resto hybrid build:

(changed talent)
 **Moonkin Form:** Now allows you to cast Nature's Swiftness and Healing Touch while in Moonkin Form
CC2 changed the position of Moonkin Form in the balance tree, making Moonkin/NS builds possible. Because HT/Moonglow already goes so far into the balance tree, you can essentially grab all relevant HT talents while grabbing a lot of good balance damage talents, including Moonkin Form. It looks something like this: [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?po ... DCIQAAAAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=oASYYKFYBIAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAFAoIDIDCIQAAAAA=)
This change would build on my previous ideas of making HT/Moonglow druids a situationally good healer, reliant on having 1-2 other druid healers in the raid. Letting them heal while in Moonkin Form would make them a lot more desireable just for the aura they'd provide, but it'd also make them wanted for their flexibility, being able to quickly change between roles when needed. Even when in caster gear they'd be able to output good healing in case of emergencies, due to Aessina's not relying on their own Healing Power. The trade-off would be that their damage output would be less than an ordinary Moonkin build, and their raid healing worse and reliant on other resto druids.

Let me know what you think!

Uglefar - lvl 60 Druid, Nordanaar
