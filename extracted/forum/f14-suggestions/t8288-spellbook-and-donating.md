The long term effect on the economy would be exactly the same, no matter if you bought gold or the riding skill via cash. Vanilla wow economy has these gold sinks like training and repair costs, respecc cost and especially the cost of riding skills to combat inflation and ensure a stable economy.

You adding gold to the economy by buying it has the same effect as you not spending gold for the riding skill. The outcome in both cases would be you being 81g or 810g richer than you were before. The long term effect on the economy would still remain the same.
