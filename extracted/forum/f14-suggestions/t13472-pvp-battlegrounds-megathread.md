## Post 91037 by Happyshadow — 2024-03-21T09:37:00Z

Hello all,

I know TWoW staff are very busy with all the upcoming content but I feel like for all of us PvP enjoyers I feel like we need some hotfixes urgently seeing the state of BGs currently in Nordanaar PvE (and I guess Tel'Abim too?).

Make BG of the day to only apply for Arathi Basin, Sunnyglade Valley and Warsong. Maybe leave Blood Ring in rotation as well. I'm unsure about BR though, I'd like to hear other's thoughts about this.

 **Alterac Valley**   dead_turtle_head

It seems to be to norm now to start turtle from minute 0. There are probably multiple reasons why this happens and I don't want to put the blame on one side only. I myself have done PvP a lot on both factions.

- 1. Remove all herb & mining nodes from AV. The new economy update should support this change

- 2. Remove honor altogether from AV and make it a rep farm only so people can get their rep items

 **OR** alternatively put a time incentive on objectives that give bonus honor based on how fast they get done. Would apply to how fast towers, bunkers and NPC leaders are captured & killed.

- 3. Put a time limit on AV lobby. 30-40 minutes and game ends. I'd argue 90% of players do not enjoy turtle. If people are there for honor gain, an everlasting turtle won't do any good for anyone. CP/hr is horrible with long games as HKs are worth +1 honor in the end.
Closing remarks on AV: I get it. No one likes losing. Everybody wants to win games. If alliance loses the majority of AB and WSG, many probably resort to primarily queue for AV. However, losing BGs doesn't mean alliance PvPers don't rank up. Quite the opposite. Due to the amount of less dedicated PvPers on alliance there is less competition. Even when horde wins the majority of WSG and AB, their queue times are longer so that bonus honor from capping flags and winning games is compensated by having to wait in queue for longer on average.

 **Sunnyglade Valley:**   dead_turtle_head

I don't have much to say as I've never found a single lobby. Even after honor gain was increased.

 **Make rewards more lucrative.** People are primarily after reputation and rank rewards. TWoW has already added more PvP content by introducing Sunnyglade and Blood Ring. I'd hate to have them remove Sunnyglade just because people are not playing it.

As of now, having Sunnyglade in the mix for BG of the day rotation is wasted as no one queues for it.

 **Arathi Basin & Warsong**   turtle_in_love_head

Fine as it is apart from maybe making rep rewards more lucrative I guess. We already get more reputation. This was a welcome change. These vanilla BGs are simply the best! Matches can be very sweaty at times but I don't mind at all, PvP players are competitive by default.

Please comment below, good luck & have fun   turtle_tongue
