# Spellbreaker Armor and Weapons

## Post 88380 by Hearthshield — 2024-02-22T05:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88380#p88380 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, friends.

I am here to request that warriors (and maybe paladins too) be allowed to obtain a version of the Alah'Thalas Sentinel armor appearance as a plate set (you know, the guards you see roaming about). And rebrand it to make the particular set a Spellbreaker equivalent more fitting the game Warcraft 3. On top of this, I would request that the Quel'dorei Guardian's Battle Glaive from Soalara Dawnstar in the Regency Spire within Alah'Thalas have a one-handed version in some capacity and shield to match. Should that be too much of a request however, at least I'd like to see a nice transmog version that would offer no change to gameplay.

It pains me that Blizzard turned the Spellbreakers appearance from WC3 into a common guard or infantry, and I've always felt like they should be somewhat more special than mere guards given their durable role in WC3 gameplay. But I can easily excuse it as the High Elves seemingly using the remains of whatever they could find to protect their people. Forced to scavenge for scraps and make the best of what they have.

I would hope that there is more cultural expansion of the Thalassian people as a whole in terms of going more into depth with their military and the peoples way of life following the released plans of Eversong Wastes.
