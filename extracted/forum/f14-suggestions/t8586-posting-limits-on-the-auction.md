## Post 55489 by Trismegistos — 2023-08-01T16:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55489#p55489 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think the issue is best fixed where the problem is; at the user end. One can always download an addon that makes the auction house simpler to use; AUX for example allows one to buy every auction they want by simply tapping the buyout key, one click per purchase. Following that, the Mail addon will very quickly open every single mailbox item with just a single click to initiate the process (and at a much faster rate than what CT_MailMod does, for example).

It's also worth considering whether you want to use up your time buying 450 single stack mageweaves for 10 copper less than regular stacks just because the single stacks happen to be the cheapest deal. Your time is worth something, too.

I heavily oppose any "maximum of 10 auctions per hour" or similar arbitrary limitations, as that would directly harm those who use the auction house more than randomly, and in doing so it would also greatly decrease the supply of goods, which tends to increase prices. The result would likely be that everyone would be paying more for what they need, and there'd be much less of a supply to go around. In the end, more people would suffer from this change than what is caused by the current situation of single stack clutter.
