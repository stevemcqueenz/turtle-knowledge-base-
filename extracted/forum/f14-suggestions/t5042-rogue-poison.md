I have been thinking about a Rogue spec that focuses purely on poison damage. My suggestion would be to apply some kind of spell power scaling to all rogue poisons (or just instant/deadly) that scales significantly better than regular attack power. It would be cool to see another spec open up for rogues, even if it's just good for dungeons/soloing. Using spell power instead of attack power would also ensure that it doesn't affect regular rogue specs that with be using agi/str/atk power gear.

Not sure if this has been talked about before but would like to hear people's thoughts.   smiling_turtle

## Post 30421 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-12-10T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30421#p30421 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

TBC had spell power affecting rogue poisons damage, and other unorthodox builds which I've enjoyed experimenting with.
However, I don't think that is a coherent way to make poisons relevant, they are physical toxins applied on a weapon, not magic.

What would make sense perhaps is the following;
 1. Poison build made viable partly through the **intellect** attribute
 - higher intellect would increase your % chance to poison the target
(because intellect would increase your knowledge of the targets anatomy and its equipments weak spots)
 - and higher intellect would increase the poisons charges amount & duration on the weapon (because of having better understanding of how to efficiently apply it on the weapon)
 - and higher intellect could somewhat increase poison damage indirectly - by being a requirement for the rogue to be able to brew and use more potent poisons (tying gear progression with poison damage increase in a way that makes sense)
 2. partly through your **attack power**
 - higher AP would also increase your % chance to poison the target (stronger attacks mean better chance of penetration of the enemy's surface protective layers and bigger & deeper wounds mean more compromised area and more amount of poison circulating in the targets body)
 3. Related to APs reasons; critical hits could also have an increased poisoning chance.

So it'd be mainly an intellect and also attack-power & critical% focused build.
Attributes shouldn't directly increase the poisons damage, as the poisons potency depends on how and which kind of poison (rank) is brewed with the rogues poisons profession.
Main source of increased poison damage output would be through increased poisoning rate with intellect and/or attack power and perhaps critical chance.
