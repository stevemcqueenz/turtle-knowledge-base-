# Professions need some love

## Post 22927 by Pantheon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-20T13:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22927#p22927 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Professions have remained the same honestly since '04 with a few minor tweaks but they still feel the same. Which, in all honesty is fine for retail when it comes to it due to the vertical progression.

However, TWoW is straying from the vertical progression from retail and following a Vanilla+ horizontal progression path. With this in mind I feel like we have an untapped market here to expand upon to help us through our leveling journey, and into end game.

I'm currently unable to make a full post covering all professions at this time but I can add a few examples to spark discussion, and would be able to discuss each profession in greater depth if this post gains any sort of interest/traction.

Where it currently stands with professions, right now we do them along side our current leveling journey. Whether or not I do professions at the start/middle/or end of my leveling journey doesn't really matter in the grand scheme. So how can try to integrate this in to our leveling experience?

Profession Quests. Right now TWoW has no issue with adding custom quest content to the game, that's for sure. Why not add some razzle-dazzle to professions? Similar to class quests, profession quests could become available every 5/10 levels. How would they work, I hear you ask? Well I can give a TL;DR example for how gathering and crafting could work. Let's use Herbalism and Blacksmithing for our examples.

 **Herbalism:**
Profession Quest giver will ask us for a donation of flowers. Pretty obvious right? Unless it were possible within the confines of the Vanilla engine to request "fresh picked" flowers native to the area you're in when taking the quest, that way it sends you off with a time limit to collect fresh flowers to donate, rather than the smelly dry ones from your bag.

Why on earth would we do this though? Well how about some cool Passive abilities, questing XP, and rewards. Since we're using herbalism in the gathering example, I will be using it as a template for the rewards as well.

 **Rewards:**
 1. +n% Speed to gathering herbs(passive - Ranks 1/2/3/...) unlocking ranks at CHARACTER level landmarks, again, similar to class quests.
 2. +X levels to profession(ex. 5/10/15 etc...)
 3. 2 to 4 times regular quest XP for the area
 4. Donation of... repeatable quest for either silver or gold/reputation specific to the area

So with a rough draft of gathering using herbalism as our example out of the way, what about crafting professions? Lets use Blacksmithing as our example.

 **Blacksmithing:**
We're all familiar with the resupply  quests we get at leveling hubs like the crossroads. Profession quest giver will have us bolster their defenses with crafted gear. To combat the issue of AH inflation on this gear, we can have a prerequisite that the gear donated to the quest giver has to have the variable "Crafted by <player doing the quest>", as we're building a reputation for ourselves in the world, we can have the quest givers refuse gear made by other players because they don't trust the quality/irreputable source.

Wait, why are we doing this again?

 **Rewards:**
 1. +n% chance to use less crafting material(passive - Ranks 1/2/3/...) unlocking ranks at CHARACTER level landmarks, again, similar to class quests.
 2. +X levels to profession(ex. 5/10/15 etc...)
 3. 2 to 4 times regular quest XP for the area
 4. Donation of... repeatable quest for either silver or gold/reputation specific to the area

I would like to thank you for reading this rough draft. I can/will elaborate further upon enquiry and when I have more time. Any suggestions, feel free to add them. Any questions about other professions, please ask them. If this has any interest whatsoever, I can expand more on my idea.

Thank you.
