# Roaming NPCs That Fly!

## Post 136679 by Avalok — 2025-06-23T00:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136679#p136679 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

HELLO! This suggestion is about adding Mob and Friendly NPCs that are several yards above ground, not just at the same level as the player on the ground, and have the aesthetics of a flying being!

What I want to talk about here is that since there are models of NPCs that float and fly above ground such as wisps, wind serpents, mana wyrms, winged birds, dragons, and all things of that nature, i would wish for the game developers to add mechanics in game that would have NPCs be programmed to roam around the air, as in several yards above ground!

Since every class has a ranged capability of 30 yards or more, this idea of mine doesn't seem to be too much of an issue in terms to reaching for and aggroing flying Mobs.

This would be such a cool aesthetic to have in-game and would give players an immersive feeling when fighting such mobs!

Also, I would like to add in here that if it would be possible to have the WotLK's Val'kyr models be included in-game as well. They don't have to look exactly like they do in WotLK; they could instead be retextured a bit, and have slight remodelling or so to look more in-theme with the game's Vanilla-WoW aesthetic.

Personally, it would be SO AWESOME to have angels of shadow and light be seen ruling and soaring the skies of Azeroth!
