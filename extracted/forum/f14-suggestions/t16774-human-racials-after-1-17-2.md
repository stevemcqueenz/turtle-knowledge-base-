# Human Racials after 1.17.2

## Post 114670 by Emerisslolz — 2024-12-28T18:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114670#p114670 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Main issue being Perception, which grants +2% crit for 20 seconds on a 3 minute cooldown. This is laughably weak considering it dares to cost a global cooldown, specially if we compare it to orc and troll racials which were stronger than human's even before the patch. Only way to use Perception is to pop it before pull, wasting a few seconds of its uptime in favor of not wasting a gcd while in combat.

A lesser concern is the weapon skill nerf from +5 to +3. I don't see the point in reworking weapon skill, thus diminishing greatly the value of weapon skill racials, AND nerfing weapon skill racials on top of it. Might as well remove them. As an example, something similar happened to Execute by greatly reducing the damage, increasing the rage cost AND adding a cooldown, all at the same time. And we know how that ended, it had to be reverted due to incredibly poor reception.

As things stand, humans basically have no racials whatsoever. This is not acceptable.

I'd suggest either turning Perception into a 1% crit passive, or buffing it to be on par with troll racial since both are a 3 minute cooldown.
