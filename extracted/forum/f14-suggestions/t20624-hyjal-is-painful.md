Hey folks,

Hyjal is just... annoying.

I thought, "Yeah, let’s do the Runestone quest so I don’t have to walk through this whole mess again."
So I started by traveling to Nordrassil—which of course means flying through this wonderful crapland again. I'm using a flying mount, which technically can fly over mountains, but hey, I just love seeing the beautiful skyline of Hyjal every single time I go there. Awesome.

After I landed, I had to slog through this amazing terrain again to get to the quest with the Runestones.

About 30 minutes later...
Just killed my first mob and bam—curse here, curse there, some random Hordie killed me. Whatever.

BUT!
I respawned at Nordrassil. Like—what the hell?!
Now I have to walk through this entire disaster again—as a ghost, without a mount—just to get back to my corpse.

Seriously... this area needs a fix.
Hyjal is just painful.
