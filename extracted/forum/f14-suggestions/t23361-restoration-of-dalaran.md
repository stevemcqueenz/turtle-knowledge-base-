# Restoration of Dalaran?

## Post 163442 by Itsmirageisvoid — 2025-12-21T12:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163442#p163442 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

This was just a thought that popped into my head but the city of Dalaran in Hillsbrad has always been shrouded in a bubble in Vanilla. In Warcraft 3, the city was laid to waste my Archimonde and would no doubt take a lot of time to repair, but we can see that it was already largely restored by the time of Wrath, which in the grand scheme of things is not that long of a time.

I think eventually opening up Dalaran as either an Alliance city or potentially as a neutral hub (though I feel like this would be harder to excuse) that connects serves as a connection for a lot of world-spanning content would be cool.

I guess the main issue would be to model it, though, but you could modify the Wrath's version of the city a little, make it a little more small definition and plop it into the hole probably. I think the main caveat is that it could still be under repair, but "almost done" with scaffolding here and there.
