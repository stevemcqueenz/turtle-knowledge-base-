# PVP South America servers

## Post 119642 by v4nrick — 2025-02-11T20:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119642#p119642 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello!
My simple suggestion is to make the South Seas (SA server) a PVP server with option to use a glyph to disable PVP (glyph of Peace) and/or to make HC automatically disable PVP too. As to not affect the people who want to make challenges and not care about people ruining their run.

Here is why,
 1. South american servers are famous for their PVP servers, at least from back in the day and also recent servers (the small ones that were running even when blizzard launched the official LATAM servers) were mostly PVP. Even the official servers from blizzard i remember to have a very populated PVP servers in LATAM and not as much populated PVE servers.
 2. We know the PVP server in EU is very low pop against PVE EU, i believe this is a matter timing more than preferences, if they were released in a different order it would be very different.
 3. From what i listen to in discord most people in SA are concern about the server not being PVP, i think it would be a missed opportunity to not have the best of both worlds, world pvp by default with an option to disable PVP in case of ganking for some people who doesnt like it. Although from my experience ganking usually solves itself as the community usually come closer and socialize more to fight against rival player trying to sneak into low level zones, low level players warns other low level players, low level players contacting high level player from their faction to fight off a ganker, so its more part of the experience and a reason to be social than a problem in my opinion.
