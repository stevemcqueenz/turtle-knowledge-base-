# Swapping over ES Raid Timer

## Post 140802 by Shadowwolf3010 — 2025-07-22T00:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140802#p140802 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Due to the size and boss count of Emerald Sanctum, I feel that it fits better with Onyxia's Raid Timer of 5 Days over the 7 Day timer it currently shares with signifigantly larger raids like Molten Core, Blackwing Lair and AQ40. The Gear it drops and the Hardmode it contains are good, but having a single room and 2 boss counters, 3 if you are being generous in counting Hardmode as it's own boss with the overlapped mechanics, considered at the same level as larger, longer raids like Naxx and AQ is a bit too much.

TLDR: I suggest swapping Emerald Sanctum's Raid Timer down to 5 Days to align with Onyxia's timer since those two raids are more similar.
