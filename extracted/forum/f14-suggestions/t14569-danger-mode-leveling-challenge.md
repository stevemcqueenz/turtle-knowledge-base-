# Danger Mode? (Leveling Challenge)

## Post 99416 by Dannyp92 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-14T11:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99416#p99416 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A mode which makes the world feel more dangerous:

 *Increased aggro range
 *Increased damage from mobs
 *Mobs get new abilities (that are usable against players having chosen this mode)
 *The player deals slightly less damage against mobs?

Which values to use for these are up to debate, but I was thinking something like a 15 % increase.

But why? The game can potentially feel more:
 *Immersive
 *Social
 *Rewarding (What should be rewarded at 60 from this challenge? Title "the Fearless"?)

I fondly remember when I started playing Vanilla WoW as a kid, when it felt like I was entering a vast and hostile world. A lot of us were clueless and had bad builds and gear. Due to the lack of knowledge, and partially bad hardware and networks, deaths were much more common and the world really felt dangerous. The threat of death made us group up and cooperate more frequently. In addition, even though dying can be annoying, the feeling of danger can make the game more immersive and more rewarding once you do complete the goals you've set. This is something I'm missing today, as the players have gotten vastly better, more knowledgeable and obviously have much better hardware and networks.

Older MMOs (like Everquest) can still invoke this feeling, and I've also experienced it in games like Elden Ring. The latter game would feel nowhere near as rewarding if it was too easy. Practically all modern MMOs have "gamified" open worlds that has no danger and where it's almost impossible to die, which, in my opinion, makes them very boring, un-immersive and unrewarding.
