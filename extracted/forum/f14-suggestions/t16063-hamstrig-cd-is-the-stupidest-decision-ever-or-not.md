# Hamstrig CD is the stupidest decision ever, or not

## Post 109406 by Reyforlife — 2024-11-22T17:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109406#p109406 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been playing wow for 19 years, from official to privates, from vanilla to wod (I've stopped retail since then), and I can say without a doubt, that unless putting a cd on hamstring was the only way to prevent players from "abusing" it for WF procs, it is the single most unbalanced nerf I have ever witnessed in any wow context, both official and custom.

So, in the case that it was not done to prevent abusing, the balance team clearly has no idea how integral hamstring is to the warrior leveling and PVP playstyle. From kiting mobs to providing any essential advantage in a multiple enemy PVP scenario, it is unacceptable that the only way to get a "consistent" cc on warrior is to put points towards piercing howl. And don't get me wrong, I wouldn't be complaining if this was a general turn towards nerfing cc for all classes, but nerfing hamstring while frostbolt, a range 1 second cast slows for just as much with an added chance of rooting you, is readily available, that causes some issues with me.

I have looked into the forums for other posts on this, and I see that the nerf was done for PVP reasons, as in warrior being "too sticky". If this is the case, this is a stupid decision. Gather some data from BGs, put some effort before you make redundant changes, host a duel tournament or something, play warrior for yourselves, you will come to realise the extent at which warrior is in a disadvantage compared to other classes.
