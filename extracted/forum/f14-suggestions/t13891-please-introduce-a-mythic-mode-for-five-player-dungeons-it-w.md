## Post 94928 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-05-15T18:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94928#p94928 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think the idea has a lot of merit, and a lot of people would appreciate having harder 5-man content with more challenging mechanics. However, the rewards would have to be implemented in a careful way, so that we don't make any of the current content obsolete.

If we go the "TBC Heroics" route, we could just make a hard mode of each dungeon that is scaled for level 60 and has more and harder mechanics (maybe even the odd new boss, rare or trash). The loot could be the same as normal mode, just with higher drop rates (e.g. more drops per boss) and higher drop chance for the epics (that have like 1% on normal mode), and maybe have the odd new item mixed in that drops only on Heroic.
This would create a better way to farm rare items from dungeons, but would also require better gear and skills, without making any new gear or making old one obsolete.

If we go the Mythic route from newer expansions, and make new epic gear to drop from the mythic dungeons, we'd have to put an ilvl ceiling on it, or it could make raiding obsolete - or at least early raiding, which might not be a bad thing. This could act as a "catch up" mechanic, so you can gear up from mythics and start from T2 for example, without first raiding T1 (which is a cake walk anyway, so this route might actually be harder, despite requiring less people). So I think the best gear you should be able to get from mythics should be roughly equivalent to ZG/MC, or at most, slightly better.

I think the Heroic route is better, but both are valid, and both could be implemented at once, but I doubt the turtle devs would deviate that far from the vanilla OG experience. Certainly the TBC route is closer to what fits with vanilla.

Edit: I forgot to mention the time trial mechanic of Mythic+. It's needlessly stressful and promotes minmaxing and toxicity, and has no place in vanilla content.
