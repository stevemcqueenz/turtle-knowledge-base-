# Battleground Reputation Boost <3

## Post 7109 by Halea — 2020-07-29T13:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7109#p7109 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings everyone ~

I would like to suggest the reputation rates for battlegrounds: Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin, Alterac Valley(maybe?) get an increase to the reputation earned per battle.

At the current rate, people are reaching Grand Marshal / High Warlord before they get a chance to reach revered/exaulted with even one of these. **I suggest a 10x increase**, this would also allow some to supplement their standard pvp set with pvp faction rewards <3

Also, which is more a concern of mine, doing casual battlegrounds while leveling and get access to the rewards from friendly / honored would be a huge encouragement to have some casual fun in battlegrounds in between questing and leveling.

(And before someone states the obvious - Yes this is a PvE server, but battlegrounds have, and always will be, an active part of PvE servers)

To sum up - I suggest we increase the reputation gained from battlegrounds   turtle_tongue_head
 10x would work fine I think

Best regards and warm wishes
 - Halea

The 10x was only an example based on my personal experience on turtle wow - meaning its likely it will take the same or more time to get 6 matches on turtle, as it took getting 60 matches in vanilla (At least on Argent Dawn, it popped once every 10-20 minutes, often multiple running at the same time). But of course balance should be discussed.

If we would go with a 10x increase, assuming both the rep from flag captures and hand in of tokens would be scaled (3*35+50)*10, it would for WSG take:
 -> Friendly: 2 won matches
 -> Honored: 6 won matches
 -> Revered: 14 won matches
 -> Exaulted: 30 won matches

This of course assumes you WIN all your matches. If you in contrast lost all your matches without capturing a single flag:

 -> Friendly: 180 lost matches
 -> Honored: 540 lost matches
 -> Revered: 1260 matches
 -> Exaulted: 2700 matches

(this is still with 10x)

I think it is also important to remember that the rewards from WSG/AB reputation are only supplements or equivalents to what can be found in dungeons. Even the exaulted rewards are only ilvl 65 (for reference, only 7/104 armor/weapon drops in Molten Core are ilvl 65 or below)

I do wholeheartedly believe these subpar items should not be locked behind an impossible grind, considering how easily they are replaced. Plus, PvP is FUN, and I find it such a shame we arent seeing more lower bracket battlegrounds.

If 10x is too high there are 8 other numbers between 1 and 10 :D

Let me hear what you think <3
