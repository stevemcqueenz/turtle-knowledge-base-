# Make Skinning secondary profession

## Post 82582 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-24T15:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82582#p82582 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, leather is used for leather armor, bags and weapons.
People don't need a lot of leather. Endgame is designed in the way so all your gear is loot from bosses.
Thats why supply of leather much greater than demand. As a result, cost of leather is too low on AH.

I always feel bad that I henocide a lot of beasts and leave their bodies on the ground.
In the same time, we get meat and various hunting trophies from beasts like we are proficient in skinning.

Suggestion is:
all armor crafts should require some kind of leather. Leather ones should require more leather
AND
make skinning secondary profession

It will increase the demand for leather for all levels and force people of all crafts to gather leather. Currently you can't choose skinning because it will take slot of main profession. So making Skinning secondary will relieve this problem.
