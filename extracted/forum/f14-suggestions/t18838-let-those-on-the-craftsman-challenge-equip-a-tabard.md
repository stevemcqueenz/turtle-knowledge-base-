Guild bases cost A LOT to get, both in game with gold and in IRL money through the donation shop. However, due to the armor requirements by some challenges, some players cannot equip or use a guild tabard as well as the guild teleport - making it more difficult for them to engage in activities with their guild. They are essentially being punished for participating in these challenges in unforeseen ways.

If possible, could this be amended so they can equip a tabard only as something they did not craft themselves? Thank you!
