# Lower Cerebreal Cortex Compound Basilisk Brain requirement

## Post 155190 by Witchblade — 2025-10-11T19:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155190#p155190 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Cerebral Cortex Compound is a stackable raid buff used by casters to gain more intellect/crit.

The problem currently with this item is the quest required to obtain it: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=2602>

it requires 10 Basilisk Brains which is too much if anybody is familiar with farming this item in blasted lands, all it takes is 1 person to have 80% of these basilisks on farm which causes a bottleneck and requires a ridiculous amount of time just to farm enough for a week of raiding.

i suggest changing the required amount of brains needed to complete this quest to 5 and the reason for this number is because ROIDs requires 6 reagents altogther and Scorpok Assay requires 7 reagents altogether. changing the brains to 5 will make Cerebral Cortex Compound require 7 reagents altogether which puts it more in line with the buffs available for the other classes
