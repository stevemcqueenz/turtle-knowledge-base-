# Prevent Accidental Raid Lockouts in PUG Groups

## Post 164157 by Relevancewf — 2026-01-01T03:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164157#p164157 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,

I wanted to bring attention to an issue that can be incredibly frustrating in PUG raid groups. Currently, it’s possible for one player who is already saved to a raid ID to accidentally—or even intentionally—lock the entire raid group to a useless instance. This means 9 to 39 other players can end up saved to a raid with dead bosses or no loot, without any warning.

While this mechanic existed in the original game, Turtle Wow has already improved on many aspects of the source material, and I believe this is another area where a quality-of-life safeguard would make a huge difference.

A solution could take many forms—it doesn’t necessarily need to be a pop-up warning (though that would be nice). For example, the server could display a custom message when joining a raid group, clearly stating which raid IDs (if any) are already associated with the group. Turtle Wow already does something similar with the mixed Horde/Alliance group warning about PvP restrictions, so I’m confident a similar system could be implemented here.

Missing a full week of progression due to something completely outside of your control feels terrible, and I hope the team can consider implementing a fix to prevent this from happening.

Thanks for reading and for all the work you do to make Turtle Wow an amazing experience!
