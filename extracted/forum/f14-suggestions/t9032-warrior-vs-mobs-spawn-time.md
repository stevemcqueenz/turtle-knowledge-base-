# Warrior vs mobs spawn time

## Post 58501 by Xkertoip — 2023-08-22T18:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58501#p58501 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, I just wanted to share opinion about increasing spawn time for monsters in zones. It's understandable that more population means frustration for players if they can't get their quest done asap. In the other hand it means that if there're not enough players in that zone, you have to kill those mobs asap and run away before they spawn to restore your health. It's understandable. But i were in situation where mobs just literally surrounded me because they're respawned faster than i actually could kill them. And there was nothing special with those mobs (I was 13 well geared warrior and i went for quest "Securing Darnassus"), i just played as hc warrior. Does it means that if I want to play as warrior(hc mode) i have to stay out of resp and kill 20 times the same mob on the edge of this spawn and that would be mine hc experience all the way down until 60? Ofcourse i died becouse there were no possibilities to run away since my way out was closed by mobs spawned literally after 10s.

I suggest 3 solution for this.
 1. Buff warrior in early lvls to be able kill second mob before first would spawn.
 2. Separate server for hc players (just in case, i now that this is almost impossible);
 3. Set spawn time little bit adapted to all classes to prevent that kind of situation.

Frustration for not enough mobs !== frustration to lose character that you spent lots of hours because some of china-man multiboxing can't finish their quest. I was playing here before that hype on Twow happened and now i feel like there is no room for hc experience here. Hope you will be able to solve that problem somehow, to get everyone happy. Cheers !
