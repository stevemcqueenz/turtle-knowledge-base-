# Abolish Decurse to Druids and Mages

## Post 154419 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-10-05T09:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154419#p154419 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Decurse feels outdated compared to other dispel mechanics.**

From my experience as a priest in battlegrounds, I rarely get decursed and it’s easy to see why. Mages and Druids struggle to keep up, since Affliction warlocks can apply two curses in one GCD, while decursing takes them multiple seconds and often forces them to lose pressure or drop form entirely. As a result, oppressive effects like Curse of Tongues can shut down casters for entire fights with almost no counterplay.

Other dispel abilities have evolved to stay practical in modern gameplay:

 * Priest Dispel Magic removes two magic effects
 * Paladin Cleanse removes one magic, one poison, and one disease
 * Abolish Disease and Abolish Poison provide ongoing protection

In contrast, Decurse only removes one effect and lacks any flexibility or sustained value. With strong effects like Curse of Tongues and Curse of Exhaustion in play, this feels especially punishing.

 **Suggestion:** Consider updating Decurse to remove two curses, allow use in form for Druids, and/or provide a short-duration “Abolish Curse” effect to bring it in line with other dispel mechanics.

EDIT: The affliction talent talent combining curses only works with certain curses, not specifically for COT. Regardless, the issue still stands that Lock damage is mostly dot (not destro) and the fact still remains that ferals/mages lose signficant damage by being on constant decurse duty.
