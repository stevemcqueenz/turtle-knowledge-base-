# New Custom Item (For Non-Human Sword Rogues)

## Post 31595 by Lanimil — 2022-12-26T12:57:00Z

I am back again with this topic. I made a post about a similar item a few days ago, but it seemed a bit unbalanced so I've adjusted it and changed where it could potentially drop.

 **Molded Flayer Grips**
Bind on Pickup
Hands                                    Leather
 122 Armor
+15 Stamina
+10 Nature Resistance
Durability: 35/35
Requires Level 60
Equip: Improves your chance to get a critical strike by 1%.
Equip: Increased Swords +3.

Dropped by: Marrowspike in Karazhan Crypts (the first boss).
Drop chance: 15%

Marrowspike currently drops 6 items, 5 of which have 18% drop rate. The last item has a 10% drop chance which totals 100%. In order to balance this out with the addition of these gloves I would suggest adjusting the items with 18% drop rate to 15% so the total still remains 100%.

Previously I suggested this to be a pair of epic gloves with a low 3% drop rate from Rattlegore in Scholomance, but Marrowspike seems like a better choice (and visually they are they same with Marrowspike even wielding a sword which fits thematically with the gloves).

Anyway, this is just another attempt at convincing the devs to add an accessible +swords item that non-human rogues can obtain without sacrificing some aspect of their toolkit.   smiling_turtle
