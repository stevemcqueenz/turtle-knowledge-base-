# Twin Emperor scaling

## Post 164079 by Twospooky — 2025-12-31T00:19:00Z

The Twin Emperors damage output doesn't scale.
If a raid brings 20 people then there is likely to be less healers.
A single healer cannot keep a warrior tank with 440 defence rating, so when the raid is scaled to 20 people then the twins damage should be reduced.

This exacerbates the already chronic issue of disrespect to tanks on Turtle wow which does not exist in other versions of the game.
