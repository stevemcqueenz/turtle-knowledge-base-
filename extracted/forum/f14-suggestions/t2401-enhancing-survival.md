# Enhancing Survival

## Post 14999 by Tytarjunior — 2021-12-29T20:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14999#p14999 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I LOVE the new patch and LOVE survival. I'd love to see more with it once the dust from this new patch has settled! I've maxed out survival and here are some ideas I think would be great.

 **Experienced Traveler**
{Toggle On/Off}
Grants +5% Foot Run Speed Outside City Boundaries. Stuns when struck. (Aspect of the Cheetah essentially)
Req: 150 Survival

 **Weather Hardened** *(Passive)*
note: in my experience tinkering with Private Servers, you can determine what weather effect is active.

After building a tent in weather conditions (rain, snow, etc.), Gain a 30 minute buff. +2% Total Health +3HP per 5 seconds.
Req: 150 Survival

 **Survivalist's Tonic**
+25 Resistance to all schools of magic for 60 min.
Reagants: 1 Essence of Fire, 1 Essence of Water, 1 Essence of Earth, 1 Essence of Air
Req: 150 Survival

None of these ideas have to be used, but throwing them out there for inspiration. Hoping it's flavorful yet balanced and true to WoW's design.   smiling_turtle
