# Priest Staves

## Post 94805 by Terexin (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-05-14T00:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94805#p94805 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, this has been on my mind for a while now. I rolled a priest, and inner fire's AP boost really intrigued me. I was able to juggle between wand and staff in combat. Surprisingly enough, I managed to score 200ish crits even on lv30. Then I remembered I played a few games where there was an option to train stick fighting.

My idea is for priest to have a couple of melee damage boosting and a melee ability in the discipline tree (cause it fits discipline in martial arts woooo).

Example:

Staff Training (3 points) - Increases your chance to parry(or crit, or hit) by 1%/2%/3% and your staff damage is increased by 5%/10%/15%.

Staff Sweep (Ability 1 point - 10 sec cooldown) - Swing your staff in a circular motion, inflicting 110% weapon damage to the primary target and 60% weapon damage to targets within 5 yds.

As far as animation goes, it could copy heroic strike, crusader strike, and cleave, etc. I'm just not sure what the energy source for these abilities would be... Mana seems kind of silly xD
I guess its a bit of a long shot, but it would be super cool. I don't know, SOME WAY to make a priest employ staff.

I made a suggestion about adding Holy Strike to Priest. It isn't game breaking, rather useless change that won't change DPS much in raiding/PvP but adds flavour - you feel your next attack with staff would hurt your target. Simple enjoyable change. I'm against making Priest melee class though. Paladins are half Priests, half Warriors. We don't have to recreate their job in our class. That's why I think 1 melee spell would be enough for us.
