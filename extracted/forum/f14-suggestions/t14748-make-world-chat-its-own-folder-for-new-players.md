# Make World Chat its own Folder for new players

## Post 100492 by Wafflecrusher (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-02T01:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100492#p100492 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

World chat, lets face it, is degenerate most of the time. When people first log in they should be able to access world chat, but it should be it's own folder, and not what people see at default. I imagine people start on the server, see the non stop spam, don't know how to turn it off or move it, and think "wtf is this, this game isn't for me". This small QOL change would be huge for attracting and retaining new players.

Either this or heavier Moderation.
I cannot count how many Times GM's are reminding people in world-chat to Remember the Chat-rules, but a few minutes later they keep going with the Same BS again.

Unfortunately the World-Chat Is needed When looking for groups or PUG's. So Just leaving it doesnt solve the Problem.

On the other Hand: If people keep on going with the behaviour in world-chat, IT might give New people the Impression that this is toleranted.

Hell, people even Publicly admitted that they didnt Care If they're muted for a couple or days.

Same btw. Goes for verbal harassment via whisper. There were many occasions When i was told by GM to Just Put them in ignore, Which also gives me the Impression that nothing will be done about certain individuals and their behaviour will be tolerated.

## Post 100781 by Fornow (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-06T07:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100781#p100781 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The general world chat should really be separated into multiple channels. For instance:

 1 - looking for groups, quests, instances, raids, etc
 2 - trading and services
 3 - questions about quests, gameplay, macros, etc

That alone would make the chat much more readable. Having everything in a single channel was fine when the server was just a couple of hundred people. But now that it's thousands it can become quite unwieldy, especially during peak hours.

That and stricter moderation. Start giving out short immediate timeouts to offenders and let that serve as the 1st warning/reminder.
