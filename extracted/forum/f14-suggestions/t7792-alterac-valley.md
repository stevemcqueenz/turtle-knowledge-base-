First of all I want to thank the Turtle WoW team for all the hard work they've done. It has been an incredible experience so far.

I really like all the various ways that makes AV feel more like a grand battle rather than 2 trains rushing to the last boss. Feel like its important to acknowledge the accomplishments before giving suggestions.

All my suggestions are things that would improve the overall experience of AV.

The feedback:

 1) Please remove herbs and mining nodes from AV. Every game there at least 5 people that have no interest in playing the game and use it as a place to farm. Most games start with only 12-15 people which brings me to my second point.

 2) Don't let the Q pop unless at least 20 people are in the Q.
The reason is, if there are only 10 of us defending SHGY/IBGY, often times we're having a hard time just against the NPCs

 3) Tower and GY guards should not respawn while contested.

Having working tree bosses that don't stand around useless would be great but I think the 3 points I mentioned have higher importance / probably easier to fix.

Other then that, again, great job on making AV a more grand experience!

I'm going to add my two cents to the above; make Korrak worth killing. I've had the quest to kill him forever and no one ever wants to do it. Especially egregious on the Alliance side since they are so dominant in AV. There are a few ways to do this:

 1. Increase what he drops. Easiest option but if the current incentive isn't enough I don't see this going anywheres either.

 2. Buff for the team that kills him, persists through death. If Korrak becomes an asset to winning maybe people will kill him more.

 3. Hardest option. The team that kills Korrak enlist the help of the ice trolls who now send periodic charges against the enemy base. This would make killing Korrak more vital in the opposite sense to option 2.

 4. Combine 1-3 and make Korrak great again.

EDIT - The devs have implemented #1 and #2 with the latest update.

 ---

On a separate topic, I've done the Ryson quest and checked out the benefits. The extra stealth detection is nice but not vital while the spy on the enemy base is pretty useless as is. Being mindful of not creating too many asks of Twow's programmers here are the modifications I'd suggest:

 1. The increased stealth detection when first triggered applies to everyone on your side but individuals once it runs out and you go to reapply it.

 2. The spy effect at the enemy base shows enemies on the minimap there without having to visit Ryson. A bit harder might be applying it to your own base to see enemies sneaking in.

 3. I've suggested this elsewheres but to restate; this quest should give a small amount of Syndicate rep.
