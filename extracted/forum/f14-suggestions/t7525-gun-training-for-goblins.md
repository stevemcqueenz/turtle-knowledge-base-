# Gun Training For Goblins

## Post 48096 by Kittenlord — 2023-05-30T18:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48096#p48096 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently Goblin Hunters, Warriors and Rogues (those goblin classes which have the capacity to learn guns) do not start with knowing how to use a gun. This feels incredibly weird from a gameplay and lore perspective seeing how goblins are without a doubt the engineer race of the horde.

Add to that that currently the final when leaving the goblin starting zone
(<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80108>) gives you the option of grabbing a gun even though the only gun trainer for horde is in TB, the absolute hardest place to get to from your starting spot in Durotar. Additionally the hunter trainer in the Venture Camp literally speak about his love for guns.
![Image](https://imgur.com/gallery/8qkPLEP)

This feels like an oversight or something since as how it currently stands, it makes no sense for goblins who **In Lore** are engineers and who in quest rewards, descriptions and through npcs, loves guns. I hope a goblin gun trainer or goblin baseline gun skill is added soon or at least that that gun reward is changed to a bow if goblins are to continue using bows for light knows what reason.
