# World pvp, low level bg, warmode and how to fix all of this

## Post 35707 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-02-22T08:46:00Z

I started this writing as reply to Akalix post in recent thread.
> **Akalix wrote: Tue Feb 21, 2023 7:43 am**
> We are open to suggestions on how to expand on our Warmode gamemode, however, we will not be opening another realm in the foreseeable future. It would cause far more damage than it would be worth.

I assume that Warmode glyph was designed to encourage pvp during leveling.
Currently, warmode increases your xp gained and flags you pvp permanently.

My observations:
My lvl 46 gathering alt with warmode ON was intentionally ganked only once. One time I was killed near crossroads while I was afk.
Once I tried to attack player of equal level and failed.
Countless times I met other equal/high level players which didn't attack me and I didn't attack them.
I killed other flagged players of green level 13 times. I had no goal to track and kill them. Just on occasion.
From my observations, amount of non-flagged players is more than flagged.
I did all quests in starting zones and trying to do all quests on my way. Thats why I mostly doing only grey and green quests. Thats why I usually outlevel anyone who I meet in the world.
I have seen complaints about gankers in Elwyn Forest. My other warmode character was ganked in Durotar. I have seen guy roleplaying orc slayer in Wesfall near deadmines.

I do have a twink of 19 and I'm not going to gank people in the world because basic twink goal is to gear up. To achieve this goal, you need battleground reputation. Hanging in the world doesn't help to get reputation.

I think warmode glyph is not needed at all. Whole server could stay PvE, but should be filled with PvP activities.

I think world pvp is in current state because it is not rewarding. People usually busy and do their own quests.
It also not happening because it is not fair. Most pvp in the world is like player of higher power kill player with lower power.

How can you attract leveling characters to pvp?
I think you can reward them with xp. Look at the tent parties. A lot of people hang in there and do nothing to get xp. Any pvp kill could grant experience.
Other thing is completable quests. You have short, very specific goal with clear reward. You go to very specific place and complete it. There should be pvp quests for killing members of other faction.
Other reward is crafting resources. There should be some events which require pvp and reward with crafting resources. For example robberies, caravans, escorts and so on.

How to make world pvp less tedious?
Currently levelers are scattered around location. Not every zone has bottlenecks in paths.
You need speed buff to track other players in the world. You either should be hunter or druid or shaman.
There should be points of interest in the world for people to gather and to expect pvp.
This place should work similar to tent. It should grant xp for staying in zone. Unlike tents, this place should work for horde if no alliance in zone and vise versa.
For example empty pirate ship near Ratchet.

How can you make world pvp more balanced?
First thing is to do something to prevent killing low levels by high levels. For example technically separate lowbies and high levels in different instances of the world zones. Without this change in some way, all other changes will become useless.
Second is to reduce power difference. Main source of difference is high level enchants on low level characters. I already made a thread about it [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4499](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4499) It will enable lvl 1 and lvl 9 battlegrounds. Currently you can just level character, equip him in white gear with enchants and kill unprepared guys. But unprepared guys probably won't come.
Third is fix low level craft, so levelers in all brackets could craft some viable sets of armor and weapons instead of questing weird stuff. Dungeon gear surely should be better. But crafting should be baseline. Especially if people will level by pvp and skip quests.

I've brought this up before. The problem with Warmode is that it works on the assumption that if someone is flagged for PVP, they will actively engage with PVP. Which in practice, isn't the case. People don't see it as "more Exp for PVPing", but as "more Exp in exchange for the risk of being ganked". Having a glyph that makes PVP more appealing isn't a bad idea, but for it to actually get people involved, it has to have a benefit that you need to actually do PVP to get.

I've suggested making PVP gear rewards easier to get as a replacement benefit to Warmode [in my own topic](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=5649), but perhaps the simpler way is to make it so Warmode is how you gain Exp from player kills. It could even have a feature where Exp gain is converted into money if your character is either max level, or has Exp gain turned off.

As for the rest, I'm not quite sure I understand your "point of interest" suggestion. However, quests that push people toward PVP is something I definitely agree with, and I had the same thought about caravans and robberies the other day, pretty much. So I'd say that I'm overall in agreement with you.

One thing missing here that might help increase world PVP? More things like the Arena Chest, where there is some limited time, valuable resource that drops periodically in a specific place that people will have to (and more importantly want to) fight over. The only problem the Arena Chest has is being over quickly, so maybe mix in a bit of design from the Scarab Lord quest and say that while these "contested objectives" are active, multiple players have the opportunity claim them, up to a certain maximum, with yells going out to alert players to how many are remaining. Or borrow from BG capture the flag, with players having to actually carry their prize back to a turn-in NPC in their hands, and others having the ability to stop them. [edit]Maybe there would need to be something to discourage players from not helping their own side because they want to snipe the objective in that case, though.[/edit]

There are quests for killing players of the opposite faction already in SW and Org, but they're not well implemented. Fleshing out the idea more would probably help get the ball rolling for the devs. I'd personally make it something like AV where you need to kill people of certain races and allow flagged NPCs to count. That way you'd see people actually engaging with the world even if other players aren't flagged. It would most likely just result in a lot more dead guards and a few break out fights here and there, but it's better than the current set up imo.

There are already points of interest in the world, but I assume you mean places that automatically flag people like STV and Dire Maul arenas. If that's the case, again, I'd say we need more examples and ideas on that front. Pirate ship is a good start. I'd probably add in an area in the elite troll area in Hinterlands, a fighting pit probably where the oozes are currently near the top. Could also use something similar to the fighting pit near <Blacktooth Grin>'s guild base in SoS. Whatever you get from these places needs to require you to come back a few times, though. A big part of what makes STV arena work as a fighting pit is that the initial reward is fine for someone leveling but the big reward for coming back multiple times is far better AND pvp-oriented. Similar styles of loot would be my preference for any world-pvp locations.

Exp for pvp kills is precisely what warmode should be instead of the current implementation. That would both encourage pvp while not being so enticing to people who don't want to pvp at all, which seems to be by far the largest issue with it as-is.

Instanced overworld is a terrible idea for a lot of reasons. A couple quick ones would be the further segmentation of the world so it feels more empty and the removal of any positive interactions from high level players along with the negative ones. Additionally, this is only ever a problem for people flagged for pvp, so the non-flagged lowbies get the negatives without any positives. If you're suggesting just segregating low-level pvpers, that's just exacerbating the first problem.

+1 on the enchant idea, but I don't think it could/would be implemented since it would just create a lot of grand-fathered gear with the enchants already on it. Hakkari Breastplate is a good example. There's about a half-dozen low levels with it and no one else can ever get it again in those brackets, but they can equip and unequip without issue. Same would happen with the enchants. If that can be prevented, I'm on board, but I don't see that happening due to technical limitations.

Low level crafts are actually pretty decent already. Silver Breastplate from BS at 24 is legitimately incredible and can last any mail user for a good while. Robe of Power in the 30s is fantastic for leveling casters. Engineering hats are infamously good in every bracket and are legitimately worth it for PvE and PvP. Just look at how many hardcore players you see running around with tiger goggles. Imo crafting profs aren't in a bad place as they are for the brackets and the bigger issue is that they have a cost in materials associated with them that is out of reach for new players more often than not. If you're struggling to scrape enough silver for each skill upgrade, you're not going to have the spare coins to buy up the crafting mats you haven't or can't find yourself in the world. Additionally, dungeon gearing is just plain cheaper and better. It takes less time and the items are usually better, although not by that much most of the time. If you think this should change, I'd recommend creating items and submitting them in the item suggestion thread in the forums.
