## Post 64492 by Darrakus — 2023-09-15T16:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64492#p64492 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

IPv4 addresses are not unique anymore. As there are more humans (8 billion) than IPv4 addresses (4.2 billion), ISPs have chosen to route users together on an identical IPv4 address. This technique is known as CGNAT.

Therefore, using the IPv4 address to detect multiboxers will largely affect innocent players as well.
