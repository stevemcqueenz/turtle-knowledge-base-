# City-related wpvp suggestions for Tel'abim

## Post 87343 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-02-09T08:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87343#p87343 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For some time now, horde side on Tel’Abim has actively organized raids to go against Alliance cities (IF, SW, Darnassus) and we’ve had great fun doing so. A few weeks ago, some guilds organized to kill the faction leader in Darnassus which felt like a fun, symbolic achievement for Horde side. However, as time goes and we try to organize to do the same thing again, incentives to repeat this characteristic wpvp activity risk falling as players experience little reward (other than the pure fun of it).

There has been some hints from Twow developers to add wpvp features in the future. This should be done as soon as possible on Tel’abim. I provide some suggestions that should be easily implementable within a short timespan.

To maintain interest in organizing city attacks I suggest:
 -Faction leader loot table. Doesn’t even have to be top-tier loot, but some material reward will make a major difference. Profession recipes, enchant items (like Enchanted Armor Kit), Lucky coins or similar might be enough. Buff guards if you feel you need to. It should require a big raid.
 -Faction leader “head” to provide a quest with city-wide buff reward, similar to Rend, Onyxia, Nefarian etc. Even more fun would be a debuff for the opposing faction!
 -Level 60 players in cities to be permanently pvp-flagged. A major nuisance when attacking cities of other factions is that the opposing players can simply choose if and when to interact (flag pvp). The decreases incentives to wpvp in cities.

I am sure there are other good suggestions related to this and am keen to hear them.

Thanks for hosting a great server
