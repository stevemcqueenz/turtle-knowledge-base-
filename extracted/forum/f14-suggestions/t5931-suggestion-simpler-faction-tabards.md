# Suggestion: Simpler Faction Tabards

## Post 37277 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-10T06:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37277#p37277 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I noticed that most of the tabards related to Alliance and Horde factions seem to come from the Argent Tournament from the Wrath of the Lich King expansion. However, these tabards are very elaborately designed, which causes them to clash somewhat with the original aesthetics. This seems to have been in part realized, as the Stormwind Tabard currently available in-game used to have the Argent Tournament appearance before being changed to its current appearance, which is the classic blue Stormwind tabard seen on guards and other NPCs.

I think that most tabard designs could in theory be reworked into simpler designs that still fit their respective factions while keeping to the original WoW aesthetics. If you look at Ironforge banners that are currently in-game, the idea of the mountain-and-hammer symbology on an orange field has always been there, but the repurposed Argent Tournament tabard adds a bunch of fancy intricacies that do work well for formal events such as a tournament, but less so for a standardized tabard.

Ironically, looking at the original icons for the tabards in Wrath, they seem far simpler there than on the actual final product that your character wears. I think that going for a style more like the one on the icons would allow for the racial faction symbology and colours (mountain-and-hammer for dwarves, the tree for night elves, the gears for gnomes, etc.) to be maintained and carried over in a style that better fits the server's aesthetic.

The one tabard that was taken from the Argent Tournament that I don't think would need much change at all would be the Quel'dorei Tabard (which is a recoloured Silvermoon City Tabard), as it's already pretty simple and the few embellishments on it fit the race's aesthetics.

Also, I have mixed feelings about the Darnassus tabard because I feel like the Silverwing Sentinels' tabard is an excellent tabard to represent the night elves in general; unfortunately, if every faction gets a unique tabard, then Darnassus will need to have something different.

These are, of course, only potential designs based off the standard guild tabard logos. I think that custom tabard logos could be made based off these or other designs, but in a manner that remains faithful to the Classic design style rather than the overly-embellished Argent Tournament tabards.
