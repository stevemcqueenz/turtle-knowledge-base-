# Grouping of HC players and "normals"

## Post 19676 by Ishilu (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-07-04T20:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19676#p19676 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Cheers!

I'm quite new to turtle and so far I really love this server. However, I don't like how I'm not able to make groups with HC mode characters for level-appropriate quests.

Encountering other players in PvE should be an opportunity to team up for a quest or two, and not be an obstacle between oneself and that One Named Mob with a 5-minute-respawn timer.

Understanding the idea of HC mode, my suggestion would be to let HC players still group up with "normals" within a reasonable levelrange, e.g. +-3, or maybe giving them a debuff afterwards preventing them from teaming up too often, so their experience stays "hardcore"enough.

EDIT:
I used to play on PvP servers, and there the options were always teaming up or just killing the competition.
Now I think it's annoying for both HC and non-hc players that Named Mobs can't be shared. Nobody likes to just watch another group kill their named mob without a chance to team up.
I really like seeing lots of players in leveling areas, but I caught myself thinking "darn, it's another of those HC guys" the other day and I feel that this is not the way it should be.
