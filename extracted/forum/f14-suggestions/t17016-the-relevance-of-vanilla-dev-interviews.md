# The Relevance of Vanilla Dev Interviews

## Post 116098 by Turbosaxophonic (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-01-10T09:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116098#p116098 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**I know this isn't a precise suggestion, but I humbly propose that our class devs use original developer interviews and first-hand accounts as a resource to guide the direction of future class changes.**

I don't doubt that people here are pretty tired of the discourse surrounding Class Changes 2 and Vanilla class design, I feel like I've been on crazy pills trying to defend and explain the design ethos of the base game, why it's something that's worth preserving, and how some of our server's overhauls have failed to stay true to certain ideals. I believe that the wealth of interviews from Blizzard's former lead class designer are more relevant than ever, and I truly hope the insights provided by his perspective can help shape the future of our server's class design for the better.

Too often is original vision and what's considered "vanilla" debated about as though the developer's intents are some lost mystery to be reverse-engineered or interpreted from what was already in the game, when that simply isn't the case. Even though these interviews don't cover the classes as exhaustively as a development bible would, they provide a bounty of insight for what the vision was for a lot of the classes, why certain decisions were made, where exactly some of those ideas failed, and what could have been done differently.

But enough rambling, without further ado I'll present what I've found to be the most relevant interviews timestamped for everyone's benefit here:

So I wanted to try to get back to that. Not only that, but we had the history of Warcraft, where some of the most iconic characters were thse aggressive, powerful Warriors. He was not the first in, you had like a ranged class doing the pulling, or a class that could Feign Death once he brought it back, y'know, things like that. The Tank is the guy who's in the front of the group, deciding which rooms to go into, which rooms to pull, you know things like that. I listened a bit into the hunter video because I was curious if there would be any allusion to hunter Carve or melee AoE in general. I did not hear any such thing as part of the dev's vision.

On the other hand, here are some interesting excerpts:

 1:12:15
Interviewer: *"Kevin, do you think that the hunter survival spec fell short of being a proper spec or was it intended from the start to be like mages arcane spec in that it rounded out the class with some useful abilities?"*
Kevin Jordan: *"No, it was definitely supposed to stand on its own. It was supposed to be more melee centric. It was supposed to be trap based, potentially get into poisons more, things like that. But yeah, those mechanics within the hunter weren't strong enough to carry the hunter and so talents built around those things also failed to carry. So we would have had to pretty significantly transform the class with the survival tree and that wasn't really the goal of the specs, to massively transform the class into something else. Which is in departure from the hunter ending today's you know modern WoW in that survival does transform the hunter so pretty interesting but yeah back in the day it was supposed to enhance and help solidify a specific identity but not transform."*
Interviewer: *"Even in vanilla after the hunter revamp like survival itself did feel like it went through a small transformation where it was 100% melee focused or and trap focused early on in the later revamp we saw abilities that increased overall agility critical strike and so forth so was there a lot of design philosophy changed there at some point?"*
KJ: *"Yeah definitely we experimented with more things. And we wanted to try to bring the survival tree up to, you know, up to snuff essentially, compared to the other two trees, and I was struggling to find a place where I could go with it other than the idea that it was trap and melee based. So even though I was struggling with those things I didn't really have a better idea, so that's how it ended up. But somebody recently pitched ideas for, you know we've been talking about on my channel a fair bit, where or how many more tanks or healers I would have added if I could have. And so this idea that the survival hunter had been a healer class or a potentially a tank class would have been interesting."*

Anyways:
 1:15:00
Interviewer: *"Can we put to rest whether Blizzard aka you ever intended to have a melee hunter spec in vanilla? There were many hunters who swore by it much to the annoyance of almost everyone else in the game to the extent that there was a hunter on my server with Thunderfury who admittedly played a half-melee half-range and trap heavy method so what do you have to say about that?"*
KJ: *"Yeah, I think half melee is the best I could expect, at least from a design intent. Like even if I were to stress melee on the survival tree, I don't think I would have ever, and I don't think it would make sense to me nowadays to just transform him into just another melee class."*
Interviewer: *"Was there an intention to have hunters run in attack with raptor strike do a bit of damage and then run back?"*
KJ: *"I would have ultimately seen it as some kind of half-and-half situation where you run in and do a few mechanics then jumping back to range and going back to your ranged DPS for a bit, and lacerate fit that mold in terms of it was a dot and you applied it and then you get back out and then go back in to reapply it once you need it to but yeah at no point did I want to make survival hunters a pure melee class."*
