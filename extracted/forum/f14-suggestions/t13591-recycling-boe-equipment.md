# Recycling BoE Equipment

## Post 92170 by Natuaduck (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-04-05T19:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92170#p92170 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm sure this idea will get hate but purists, however:

What if Bind on Equip items could be resold/traded?

For example, I bought for my level 23 Priest a Thunderwood wand that I purchased off the Auction House for 22 gold. That Priest has reached level 30 and I intend to replace it. As such, that extremely rare drop wand just gets vendored and disappears out of the in-game economy never to be seen again. It'll now just get trashed and it wore its usage out.

Sure, the 22 gold really wasn't that hard to make nor did it take long at all, but it's the item I'm talking about itself. Some other fledgling level 23 Priest, Warlock, or Mage would love to have that.

Coming from Final Fantasy 11 in the past, 'Soulbound' items are almost non-existent save for a few particulars.
