**Role Playing Phases for Dungeons,**
 **Explorer Mode**

Not my idea but I just watch a video by Gavinrad the Bookish, excellent channel about role playing in wow.
He made a fantastic suggestion on his video about RP Phases for Dungeons, I will link it below.

But how possible is it for turtle wow to implement this?
I could imagine my old character Seymour returning to his home city of **Stratholme** and with no bosses and adds.
Then I could take my time and  run an RP event about finding his lost family? What happens to them? Are they somehow alive, or ???

The Guild the **Scarlet Crusade** could run events at the Scarlet Monastery, and they don't need to clear out the dungeon every time.
I understand people might say what about the chests in the dungeon, well maybe have them lock or empty when you run an **Explorer Mode** dungeon for RP.

 *"It allows players to freely explore previously completed dungeons. Using Explorer Mode, players can take their time to enjoy the scenery of their favourite dungeons, with no enemies or obstacles to ruin their fun".*
