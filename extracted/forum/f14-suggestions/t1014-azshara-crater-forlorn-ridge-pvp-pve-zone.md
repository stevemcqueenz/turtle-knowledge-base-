# Azshara Crater - Forlorn Ridge - PvP/PvE Zone

## Post 5708 by Hejtr — 2020-04-16T21:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5708#p5708 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtles! Since this server is bringing back to life alpha features I was wondering about Azshara Crater.

I remember playing WoW private server Prophet's Lair back in 2007 where AC was a remarkable thing - a mix between PvP and PvE - battleground similar to Alterac Valley. The area had custom pve bosses and pvp objectives dropping custom currency that you could exchange for certain items. I would recommend doing something similar. Allow me to picture my idea.

 **Here is my thought process.**

 **Azshara Crater is a zone with 2 opposing bases - West for Horde(Valormok), North for Alliance(Talrendis), middle neutral area and closed off south neutral area (Temple of Eternity). 2 factions fight, whoever wins should get acess to the neutral grounds for benefits. The benefits I will propose here are certain world buffs.**

![Image](https://vignette.wikia.nocookie.net/reznik/images/a/a4/EKnwN3b.jpg)

 **So the PvP aspect:**

- Faction based PvP is always on here, no diplomatic immunity.
- It is a battleground so you can only leave by reloging or by the entrance at you base camp, portals dont work here. If you logout, you lose all your buffs.
- Each base has its own defenses, walls with gates and guards, then a major Boss in the main building - like in AV.
- Each base spawns a few mobs rushing to the enemy base- meeting in the middle (bridge over the river). They can help you push to the enemy base - like 1 lane MOBA.
- Bosses in main bases drop certain amount of AV,WSG,AB marks so everyone can loot them.
- Every player drops blood coins same as in Arena.
- Other buildings have NPCs with repairs, AH, bank, etc.
- If you manage to defeat enemy Boss, your team spawns guards at the entrance to the neutral area where statues of 2 dragons are(Temple of Eternity) and practically denies entrance of another faction for a predefined period of time. (like 2h)
- One spot for NPCs providing World Buffs near the neutral grounds in the middle.
![Image](https://i.redd.it/lomrhtz0o1k11.jpg)

 **PvE Aspect:**

 **Basically a range of mobs and nodes that are worth fighting for on a pvp server are spread across the map.**

- Herbalism + Mining - normal stuff +extremely rare things like Ghost Mushrooms, Grave Moss and Black Lotus and some rare ore nodes are at certain spots where people can fight each other.
- Elementals - of fire, water, wind... with higher lvl - to be farmed for reagents like for fire protections potions etc.
- Cenarion plants - Corrupted Songflower, Windblossom, Whipper Roots, Night Dragons - are spread across the map so you can get buffs from them after applying the salve.
- Blasted Lands Mobs - those which can be farmed for R.O.I.D.S and similar. - blasted boars, scorpoks,snickerfangs, basilisks, vultures
- Winterspring Eko's Mobs - those that drop Ekos if you have Cache of Mau'ari. Giants etc
- New World boss - similar to Turthlu with adequate drop list
- Certain mobs should be high lvl, so they just couldn't be farmed easily and should represent some sort of threat losing the buffs they give.
- A lot of these specialty mobs should be in the area of Temple of Eternity - where one side can get exclusivity by defeating enemy base boss.
![Image](https://vignette.wikia.nocookie.net/reznik/images/1/11/Azshara_crater_1_by_enhreznik-d5vlwx5.jpg)

 **List of NPC needed for World Buffs:**
 **So you get blood coins by killing enemies, you farm mobs for roids and ekos and you need to cash them all in.These NPCs are in unsafe location and you have to travel back to your base to get out of the zone with buffs on you.**

- NPC similar to Arathandris Silversky or Maybess Riverbreeze where you turn in repeatable quests "Salve via something" and get salves for Corrupted plants
- NPCs similar to  Bloodmage Drazial and Bloodmage Lynnore - Blasted Lands Elves where you turn in items for R.O.I.D.S. etc.
- NPC similar to  Witch Doctor Mau'ari that gives you Cache of Mau'ari and where you turn in Ekos for Jujus
- NPC like at the arena that exchanges blood coins for buffs

 **Briefly and clearly:**

1. It's a new and interesting PvP option and a way to farm honor and rep for all BGs
2. Nice way of getting World Buffs and raid preparation in basically one place - but with a risk of losing them!

You can get those buffs and items like black lotus normally in the world without any risk no matter what. This is PvE server with diplomatic immunity - world PvP is non existent. This would be a nice alternative to it! A higher risk - higher reward map, like everywhere on PvP server.

I know it is not exactly an easy task to create something this big. The area itself isn't even fully done, is a bit buggy and must be redone - expecially Temple of Eternity. But I do believe it has potential. So what do you think?
