# Turn Totems into Auras (Like the Tauren Chieftain in Wc III)

## Post 169456 by Nurpetelian96 — 2026-02-26T15:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169456#p169456 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,

I’ve been thinking a lot about Enhancement Shaman gameplay lately, and I wanted to share an idea that could make the spec feel smoother, more engaging, and honestly more fun, while still staying true to Warcraft lore.

The Idea:

What if Enhancement Shamans could transform certain totems into personal auras instead of placing them on the ground?

Think about the Tauren Chieftain from Warcraft III - he carried a powerful presence that empowered allies around him. Enhancement already feels like a frontline, battle-focused spec, so having buffs radiate from the shaman instead of stationary totems would feel incredibly natural.

Why This Would Improve Gameplay

Right now, Enhancement moves a lot. Between mechanics, repositioning, and general melee gameplay, constantly dropping and replacing totems can feel clunky and outdated. Turning core Enhancement totems into auras would:

Keep the gameplay fast and fluid

Reduce constant re-placement of totems during movement

Let players focus more on combat decisions instead of maintenance

Make the spec feel more modern without losing its identity

Instead of running back to a totem or losing value when moving, the shaman becomes the source of the power — which fits Enhancement’s aggressive fantasy perfectly.

Lore Friendly, Not Just a Gameplay Change

This isn’t just a convenience idea. Warcraft lore already supports shamans channeling spiritual power through themselves. The Tauren Chieftain’s aura-style abilities are a great example of a shamanic warrior empowering allies through presence rather than objects planted on the ground.

Enhancement has always been the “battle shaman,” so leaning into aura-style buffs feels like a natural evolution rather than a redesign.

Possible Implementation Ideas

Enhancement-only talent or passive: Totems become short-range auras centered on the shaman

Visual effects similar to swirling elemental energy instead of physical totems

Optional talent choice so players who love traditional totems can keep them

Final Thoughts

Totems are iconic — and I’m not suggesting removing them from the class entirely. But giving Enhancement the option to convert key buffs into a mobile aura could make the spec feel smoother, more immersive, and honestly more fun to play in modern encounters.  turtle_in_love_head
