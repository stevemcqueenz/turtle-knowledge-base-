# Orgrimmar Ring of Valor Changes

## Post 79640 by Kastok — 2023-12-02T13:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79640#p79640 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shellcoin has been relocated out the Ring of Valor in Orgrimmar, a change myself and other roleplayers were excited for. Stormwind enjoys a tremendous amount of spots for roleplay opportunities, while Orgrimmar is quite sparse in comparison. The Ring of Valor oozes Horde thematics, and yet it was used for Shellcoin. With Shellcoin's focus there gone it opens up a tremendous opportunity for server wide events and community get togethers!

But the remnants are still there! A part of this change is requesting to take the vestiges of Shellcoin out of the Ring of Valor entirely. Let the Ring of Valor be a true piece of attraction in Orgrimmar. And for how to do that, on to the actual part of the suggestion:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/BtT17sF.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/vgKDj5J.png)

Sadly due to how the world space was designed, there are invisible walls blocking the fighting pit of the arena. Its totally unusable by the community at the moment which is a tremendous shame. For those that may ask, due to how the world is created, I can safely say that getting those invisible walls removed will never happen as it would require the editing of Orgrimmar itself.

But there is a simple solution. Place just a handful of NPCs at the Ring of Valor that allow teleportation to and from the arena pit with something like the below image! One on the outside of the ring that places players in, and another npc on the inside that will take players out.

> Arena Pitmaster
>
>  "Welcome to the Ring of Valor, [race]. Are you here to test yourself in honorable combat? I can open the gates and allow you access should your conduct be appropriate."
>
>  >*"I am prepared and ready to grace the arena's floor with my blood, Pitmaster."* (Teleport trigger.)

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/9ZBdY3v.png)

And on top of this, I believe that certain subzones in the game can have their attributes changed to allow things. And the Ring of Valor is a uniquely named area within the city. If it would be possible, switching a setting of this subzone to allow duels within it would be extremely cool! The game space is textured, clipped, and playable - ready to be used.

I think with the enormous success of Vrograg's community transmog competition, there is obviously a real craving for community get togethers! This would see use by all types of players on TurtleWoW and from what I understand of the game's limitations would not require any drastic changes or additions other than a handful of NPCs.
