<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78341#p78341 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It is known that Blizzard had plans for additional classes with the base game.

Death Knights were originally seen as a class akin to Jedi in SWG: You have a pre-existing character with its own skills that you turn into a new, more powerful class after serious effort.

Runemasters were originally debated as a class unique to WoW, instead of I believe Druids. They were quite similar to something between a paladin and what most people would consider a "monk" to be (ignoring Pandaria).

And obviously Demon Hunters have been on the table since Alpha as well.

Does Turtle ever consider this a possibility in the future? Or is there a hard stance on classes?
