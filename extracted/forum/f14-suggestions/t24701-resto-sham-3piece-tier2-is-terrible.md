"Increases the number of charges of your shield spells by 2". This combined with the Improved Water Shield talent:

"While your Water Shield is active, you restore 9mp5, and your total mana regeneration is increased by 2% for each active Water Shield globe"

The way this talent works is it increases your mp5 by 2% for every water shield globe you have. This makes Resto Shaman 3piece Tier2 increase your mp5 by 4% total. On Ambershire, my Resto Shaman has 45mp5 while casting.

 **This makes 3piece Tier2 worth a grand total of 1.8 mp5**

Lets compare that to Paladins, who receive +57 Healing for their 3set bonus.
