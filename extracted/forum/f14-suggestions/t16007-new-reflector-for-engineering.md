# New reflector for engineering

## Post 109052 by Flourry — 2024-11-20T21:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109052#p109052 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the update of arcane mage with CC2, arcane rupture and arcane missiles combined have been providing arcane mages with insane burst in PVP.

While with fire and frost we had access to reflectors to give us a viable counter or edge, the same could not be said for arcane.

A new reflector could provide us with some nice counterplay against arcane rupture and arcane missiles which have been doing high bursts of damage. Arcane missiles even worse as it's channeled,  meaning it can continued to be cast even after breaking LoS. It can also provide a nice counterplay to polymorphs without having to use a lucidity or burn a trinket CD.

For PvE, a reflector could also provide some nice arcane resistance for a trinket slot for the upcoming Kara 40 like with Fire Reflector for Ragnaros/Firemaw, Frost Reflector for Sapph (if the frost aura wasn't so heavily nerfed on Vmangos) and Shadow Reflector for Twins tanking in AQ40.

Would be interested to hear other people's thoughts. Maybe a reflector for another school of magic is unfair especially as this is specifically towards mage, and the rupture might se some tuning/tweaking.
