So I'm new to turtle wow and I just hit 60 and JUST hit 275 mining to be able to use rich thorium veins. During my leveling, I saw these RTVs and had to pass them by. Now it is changed and for the WORSE. Literally every small thorium vein I use is **only 1 hit and only 1 piece of ore**. What were you thinking? Surely there must be a better solution than this. If I do a mining run/circuit it is usually once and if I harvest all these STVs then I'm just letting them possibly respawn as RTVs for the other guy. I have played wow since it came out and have always enjoyed mining. I also started a new paladin, 20 currently and he is doing engineering to supply my hunter with bullets. But how much time now am I going to have to invest in the game to get thorium - 1 at a time ?!!? Not happy with this at all and I was loving Turtle wow. Does anyone feel this is wrong? Frustrating?

I'm really tired of the - slap the hand of everyone and no one seems to punish the abusers and leave those who are not abusing the system alone.

I also am angry about the cave camping, RTV camping and rare mob camping. Just the other day I was over in a far off cave with a friend. We worked through all the mobs and got a few nodes. Upon walking out of the cave I noticed a player login inside the entrance, waited and then the player logged out. Obviously stationed there for harvesting those mines. I believe people have multiple 60 players just to abuse these locations and why not? It is so easy. I stumbled upon a mining area where everything respawned 30 minutes later. This game is setup to be abused. Supporting that with the portable mailbox, vendor, etc. The player camping the cave never has to leave. Imagine if I cleared the cave only to have the camping player come take the nodes I was working toward.

I hope things change. As it is now, it feels like I'm being punished for another's actions. Put yourself in my shoes, does it make any sense.
