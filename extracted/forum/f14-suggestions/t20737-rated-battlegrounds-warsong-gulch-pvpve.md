# Rated Battlegrounds: Warsong Gulch (PvPvE)

## Post 143804 by Valysim — 2025-08-08T10:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143804#p143804 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello.

For a while now I am thinking to propose some addition to Warsong Gulch, so that we all could have some new experience and fun in PvP; this should be an option, alongside of the normal battleground.

I am thinking a system (eventually rated battleground) in witch:
 - in the bases are summoned different in game representative heroes of each faction, one per each faction;
 - each hero will poses different abilities (and eventually give a specific buff to the party)
 - this heroes could stay and protect the base, or go and attack the other opposite faction base (by option eventually of group leader)
 - after a hero is slayed, summon a more powerful one; one hero at a time for each faction; up to a third and the last;
 - each level bracket will have a pool of heroes with their abilities; the higher the bracket, more difficult ones;
 - the bigger the pool of heroes (with different abilities, buffs and mechanics), the better.
 - the heroes could be summoned based on the group composition and class balance; also, could take into consideration the gear and the group, compare to the opposite group, and summon a heroes to balance the odds;
 - if the heroes could be chosen by the group leader, or to be summoned by the system automatically , based on the above criteria, should be decided by the developers.
 - and not the last, with this system could be added more items / item sets;

Yes, this will take a while to develop. But if i could help with something, I would.

Hope this is something which  will interest thee player base and will be approved by the Turtle WoW team.

Thank you.
