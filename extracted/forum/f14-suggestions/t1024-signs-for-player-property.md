I think it would be a neat feature to have readable signs (You know how text appears when you hover your mouse over a sign?) that could say "[name]'s Home", "[name] Guild Hall", etc, etc. These signs would be spawned next to a home or building to denote ownership.

I think this could be a nice feature for players to use up a bunch of empty houses and buildings throughout the world. I've started to catalogue empty homes and places that could be used for such a feature here: [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=1023](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=1023)
