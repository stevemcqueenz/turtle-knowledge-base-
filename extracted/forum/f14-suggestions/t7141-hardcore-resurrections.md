<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45479#p45479 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm sure you've seen topics relating to the title too many times already, but this is becoming a serious issue.
Players have been coming on to the forums constantly asking for a hardcore character res because of mainly two reasons. Being ganked, lag, server crashes, or bugs. To put more detail into ganking, and bugs, many people confuse being flagged for pvp as war mode, and think the only way to engage in pvp is via war mode. Another is doing an action that flags for pvp and not realizing it. This includes buffing, and healing characters of the same faction who ARE flagged for pvp. With the bugs issue, I can completely understand this, but they still disregard the rules altogether because they either didn't read them, or think the rules are wrong. Another example is players not understanding basic fundamental mechanics. I've seen someone post a video which displayed death by drowning, and believed that they had "Died for no reason". But I'm not just here to shit on these players, as I'm beginning to think that a language barrier might have some influence in not understanding or reading the rules, as most of these posts are in broken english. Correct me if I'm wrong, but as far as I know, the only language the turtle WoW website supports is english, and all the rules in forums are posted in english, and this can make things hard to comprehend for players who have little experience with the language. My suggestion is simply support (At minimum) the more commonly spoken non english languages in some way.

Its a common misunderstanding from clueless people that a server crash kills HC players. No, when the server crashes, you DC but so does the world and the enemies killing you. The entire game stops, and you won't be killed by that.

As for logging in once the server is back up, the devs have put into place a system that stops NPCs from proximity aggroing for the first 5 mins of the server returning online. This means that HCs deep in a cave or humanoid camp have ample time to run out and reset their progress.

DCs though? Like where you DC from the server yet the server is running? Yes, you will probably die, though you have 60 seconds to log back into your character. having a second client open with your credentials ready, can help you get back in quickly.

But yeah there's no way for there to be an appeals for DCing. It is impossible to automatically tell the difference between someone losing internet legitimately, or them losing internet because they unplugged their router to avoid a certain death.

Anyway, as for the newcomers who have no idea...

Yeah the fact that hardcore is death=delete and is intended for expert players who understand the game inside and out....

This could be better conveyed in simpler english. Or maybe even in translated sections of the website.
