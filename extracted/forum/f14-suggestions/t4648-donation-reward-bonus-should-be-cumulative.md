# Donation Reward Bonus should be Cumulative

## Post 27801 by Ashkir — 2022-10-23T22:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27801#p27801 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I initially believed Donations for Turtle Tokens were cumulative, this was my mistake. However, they are based on per-donation basis. This alters my donation preferences to Turtle WoW. Honestly, there is no use in throwing away 10 to 20 EUR per paycheck, etc., to acquire Turtle Tokens if this is not the case.

There appear to be few rewards for continued membership and dedication to Turtle WoW. I might as well save up and donate a greater quantity all at once, which can result in irregular contributions to Turtle. Your bonuses will always reach their maximum, as they should. However, it should not penalize those who donate smaller amounts as opposed to larger quantities.

I believe that after we reach a certain donation threshold, such as 200 EUR, we should be able to continue receiving the bonus for smaller purchases. Collectively in less than 2 months I have donated 180 EUR to Turtle WoW. I've reached the point where I'm no longer interested in anything else in the store. As a result of donations not being eligible for bonuses, I am less likely to want to donate, so I will only donate during drives in which Turtle is in dire need.

By lacking any form of constant loyalty in this synergistic approach that Turtle WoW need for long-term success, you are cannibalizing your sales and the consumers who have acquired what they want and are more conscious of what they buy. I have an MBA and a doctorate in these tactics, and, can give you a fully sourced argument, if, so desired on why this may be a good move.
