## Post 20652 by Teokama — 2022-07-29T17:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20652#p20652 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Bring your SR folks.... During Classic WoW, I participated in nearly every single World Boss I could be a part of, the PvP was glorious and a few of the world bosses were actually quite fun from a PvE perspective (Lethon and Emeriss are no joke). However, the deadliest of all world bosses was clearly when Kazzak reached his enrage timer and just started unleashing Shadowbolts everywhere.

Seems like the perfect endgame world boss challenge. I'm sure some people can pull it off but it won't be an easy fight, especially with his other mechanics around punishing mana. There's lots of new items coming down the pipeline, an enraged Kazzak could be the iLevel 80+ gear source to help the players on the gear grind to Naxx, while being a right proper fight for those who want a good PvE challenge.

 **For the Legion! For Kil'jaeden!**
