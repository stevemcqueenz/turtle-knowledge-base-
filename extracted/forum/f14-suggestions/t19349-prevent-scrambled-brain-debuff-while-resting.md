# Prevent "Scrambled Brain" Debuff While Resting

## Post 131470 by Zmarotrix — 2025-05-16T18:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131470#p131470 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Having to swap between my specs to train all my abilities then be stuck waiting to correct something or just go back to my main spec sucks. I just don't see any reason to not allow us to swap back and forth between our specs it manage our talents, skills and action bars in a major city or inn. I've considered tents as well and if someone wants to spend a 2 hour cooldown on a tent to be able to swap back and forth between specs faster, that just does not seem like an issue.

## Post 131471 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-05-16T19:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131471#p131471 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's an issue because people will exploit it in raiding and other content. Being able to change spec on the fly means being able to go from, say, a single target build, to aoe, depending on the encounter. It has been done before and that's the exact reason why this feature was implemented.

Quick edit: I re-read the title and, this is also problematic because tents exist. What I said still stands. If turtle could make them only work outdoors or outside of instances/BGs though, this would be a fine change to make, but it's too many things to address just for the purpose of addressing one very minor issue.
