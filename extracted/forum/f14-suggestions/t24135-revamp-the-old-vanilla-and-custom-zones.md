# Revamp the Old Vanilla and Custom Zones

## Post 169734 by Dbl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-02T03:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169734#p169734 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Vanilla Zones**

As we're stuck on EK and Kalimdor I think the both continents would benefit from an asset update and revamp or just a bit of extra personality. Alot of people have leveled already numerous times in the old zones and I believe that adding just a bit of scenic and atmospheric assets and environmental storytelling would go a long way. Something like ruins, remains of a battle, fallen trees, small camps and shrines, maybe even a few quests of a beaten path.I remember how cool it was when they added the Invincibles grave in Tirisfal back in Wotlk, just that small thing really makes the world feel lived in and real. Doesn't have to be much, we want to persevere the nostalgic feeling, just something to break the monotony of an badly textured barren hill or just flat out empty liminal space. The world is too empty and dead at times and it's really hurting the immersion and wonder od it all.

 **Turtle WoW Zones**

While I love the custom zones some are far better created than the others so I think they should revisit some of them. For example Gilneas and Northwind are beautiful but the passage from tirisfal to Tirisfal Uplands and Glenshire looks like somebody got drunk and purposefully made the terrain look like a fever dream, my undead is walking on a road that is allmost 80 degrees tilted and it takes you out of the zone completely, not to mention some empty, asset-less spaces that just look like the zones were unfinished. All of the custom zones sometimes feel liminal and vacant, we need more patroling NPC's, more clutter and unmarked places but above all we need **SHADOWS**. I'm not sure how many of tou noticed but the new zones have no shadows at all and already dated textures and assets look even worse, this is borderline a necessity if we want to make these zones look better. I really appreciate what the team has done so far, much love to you all <333

 **Graphics**

As we are not getting the U5 update the least that can be done is up the graphics a bit. Skyboxes are one of the worst parts of vanilla wow so they need to be redone completely, second one is the aforementioned shadows missing in custom zones, we need more weather effects and shader like integrations. This client is really old and it shows, we either need to make it appealing or switch it all together.

 **Why?**

This is an RP server with lots of RP players who would benefit alot from this change, we just need a bit more scenic views and places and ruins and things that make this world feel lived in and immersive.Lots of people like to explore, have fun, go out into the wild and do random quests, it's sucks doing it in a seemingly dead and barren world. Please think about this <3
