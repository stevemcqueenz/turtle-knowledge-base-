Actually, this issue needs fresh look.
I think people don't like that ranking clownfest in general. Even if there are some individuals ready to spend their time on it.
Thats why optimal PvP strategy for many people is "join top guild, grind your t2-t3 gear and occasionally pop into battlegrounds for reputational gear". Essentially "twinking" but on 60 lvl.

Assuming that high-tier pve gear is not available on PvP server, people don't see reason to grind t1 because it is quite bad and it will be easily replaced in later patches.

PvP was not so popular on Nordnaar server even before Tel'Abim was launched.
Something should be done with PvP system as a whole.

PvP in vanilla as a whole lacks linear obvious progress. You kill mobs all your way up to 60, then you need to gear up in dungeons and only then you can be viable in PvP. I find myself extremely lazy to do that errand maraphone with all that grinding for just another character only to get ... what?
There should be xp for PvP. Players will live in warsong gulch, but they will get PvP ranking progress as their characters levels up. It is important that you should be able to PvP on PvP realm right from the start. Not after 3 months.

I don't even think that class balance issues are priority. It limits potential of players to trio of Paladin, Hunter, Druid, but in the end, people usually play winning side. They just level another character if meta will change.
