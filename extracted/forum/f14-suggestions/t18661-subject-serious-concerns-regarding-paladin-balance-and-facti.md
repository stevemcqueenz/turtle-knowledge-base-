# Subject: Serious Concerns Regarding Paladin Balance and Faction Class Restriction

## Post 127065 by SirBangAlot — 2025-04-16T21:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127065#p127065 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear GM Team,

I’m reaching out with what I hope comes across not just as criticism, but as a genuine concern from a passionate player who wants to see Turtle WoW thrive in fairness and balance.

There seems to be a major disconnect between what is claimed about Paladins on your official site, and how they actually perform in-game. Your site reads:

"Let’s be honest: the only good thing a Paladin can do in vanilla is heal. Sure, with the right build you can tank decently or do decent damage, but it still doesn’t quite fit..."

This implies a well-balanced, modestly performing class. However, the reality in-game is wildly different.

Currently, Paladins are capable of delivering 4 instances of damage in a single button press—a normal hit, Crusader Strike (or equivalent), and double Seal of Command procs, all of which can crit at once. The result? Geared characters—yes, even tanks—being one-shot instantly. This is happening consistently, and it completely undermines any idea of skill or class matchup. There’s simply no time to react.

Additionally, there’s a growing issue in PvE as well. It’s becoming common for Paladins specced as tanks to outdamage the most geared DPS players in the group just by tanking mobs. That’s not just imbalance—it defies the fundamental logic of class roles. If the tank is doing the highest DPS, then why bring DPS players at all? It completely erodes group dynamics and invalidates the effort players put into gearing and optimizing their damage builds.

And here’s where it gets worse: Paladins, in their current state, are exclusive to the Alliance. If you want to play Horde, you’re locked out of what is, by far, the most powerful class in both PvP and PvE. If balance tweaks aren’t on the table, then allowing Paladins for Horde and Shamans for Alliance seems like the bare minimum step toward equity. At least then, both factions would have access to the same overpowered mechanics.

Holy damage, by the way, is completely unavoidable. There’s no resistance, no counterplay. A Paladin can literally two-shot a fully geared Bear Druid with Holy Shock and a few Seal procs. And yet, the narrative continues that “Paladins aren’t overpowered.” Have these interactions actually been tested in practice? Because the current gameplay contradicts that claim entirely.

This isn’t just frustration talking—it’s feedback from players who care. We love this project, but we also want it to be fair. Right now, it’s not. And we hope you’ll take a closer look at what’s really happening in the game, beyond the theory and assumptions.

Sincerely,
[A Concerned Player]  hiding_smth_turtle_head
