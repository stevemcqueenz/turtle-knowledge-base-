# Fix the Ranking system before PVP Server Launches

## Post 71850 by Bert47 — 2023-10-18T12:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71850#p71850 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please adjust the ranking system before the launch of Tel'Abim. The current system is a disaster with the population levels/play styles of many of the newer players that have joined the server.

For instance, Horde has a well-known chinese premade that is circumventing server mechanics to block premades by using an addon. They are doing over 1 million honor per week. It makes games unbearably lopsided as alliance and makes it impossible to get higher ranks as a horde that isn't part of this group. To make things worse, they are selling ranking services to join the premade (~7-10k USD to go from r-14 or so , and you can verify this if you poke around, I won't link it here of course).

There is an easy fix, however. The Classic WOW devs actually did something right for once and implemented a system that allows players to rank without competing against their own faction.

Having something in place like earning 500k honor for the week gets you max RP regardless of what other members of your faction does would be ideal. Scaling down so 450k honor is the second most ranking points etc etc. This is what Era is currently doing and has been a rousing success.

The old system never made sense, is extremely unhealthy and was changed during vanilla (during the tbc prepatch but still) to be more sane.

Please consider making these changes to 1) make ranking in PVP a healthier, safe and enjoyable activity and 2) devalue the pre-made services that are being sold for ludicrous real world monetary value.

Also banning anyone who is exploiting via addon to premade should be a no-brainer at this point as well. It's contributing to harmful RMT and ruining battlegrounds for everyone else.
