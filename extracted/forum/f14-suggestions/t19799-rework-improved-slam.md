# Rework Improved Slam

## Post 134943 by Theempyrean — 2025-06-09T23:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134943#p134943 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At present, the Improved Slam talent reduces both the cast time and the global cooldown (GCD) of Slam by 0.5 seconds. While the reduced cast time is beneficial, the GCD reduction is effectively pointless. This is because the GCD reduction only applies when casting Slam itself—not when using any other ability.

Worse still, the GCD expires before Slam finishes casting. Since Slam still has about 1 second of cast time left after the GCD ends, the GCD reduction does nothing to improve the actual flow of combat or usability of the ability.

Suggested Solution:

Option 1: Have Improved Slam reduce Slam’s GCD universally to 1 second for all abilities, making it feel smoother and more fluid in the Arms rotation.

Option 2: Remove the GCD reduction component entirely and instead increase the cast time reduction to a full 1 second.

This would make the talent more meaningful and impactful in gameplay, rather than a partially wasted talent point.
