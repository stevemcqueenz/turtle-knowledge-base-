# Changing the limit for accepting quests "The Fury Runs Deep" and "Powers Beyond"

## Post 111371 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-04T16:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111371#p111371 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently (in December 2024), unlocking and accepting the quest "The fury runs deep" requires that you turn in the quest "The dark iron war". The problem or illogical part is that we unlock a lower-level quest in a dungeon suitable for players around level 26 after finishing an elite quest involving mobs over level 30.

"Powers beyond" is a quest for players of level 38 or higher requiring defeating the lich Amnennar the Coldbringer, level 41 final boss of Razorfen Downs. And the quest is accepted in Gilneas, a region for leveling from 40 to 48. Taking the quest at around level 43 just makes this quest feel too low for players busy leveling to consider doing it.

My suggestion is simple - enabling the quest The fury runs deep to be taken as an option parallel to The dark iron war. For Powers beyond, lower its level of acceptance to 37, equal to that of another quest "Bring the End", which requires defeating the same boss in the same dungeon. Another option could be moving the quest to a region around level 30 to 40, like Arathi Highland or simply deleting this quest.
