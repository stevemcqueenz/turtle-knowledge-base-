# List of BG and wpvp suggestions to improve twow pvp

## Post 138842 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-12T15:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138842#p138842 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since tel'abim opened the players have been screaming for pvp and wpvp content. But after more than a year since faction leader loot was first announced on the roadmap, not even this simple but time-effective addition has been made to the game.

There is currently talks about other servers who seem do be catering to pvpers much better, and twow risks losing a significant amount of players due to this, particularly following the effects of CC2 on pvp balance. Pvpers across both servers seem disheartened and keen to try something new.

With the upcoming patch indicating a beginning realization that pvp is worth investing developer time in, I thought I'd provide a list of some suggestions I've made over the last 18 months. The majority of these are simple additions (likely, much simpler than adding a new boss to a lvl 25 dungeon) that would provide repeatable content for pvpers. In other words, new pvp content would be a better return on investment than lowlvl dungs:[viewtopic.php?t=18751](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=18751)

There is no doubt that twow devs are talented and have a love for the game. The pvp community (speaking, atleast of the majority of pvpers I've interacted with on telabim) are just asking for some amount of developer time to be spent on improving pvp and particularly wpvp.

Here is the mentioned list, in no particular order:

Wpvp towers suggestion - make it like Gurubashi Arena
[viewtopic.php?t=14618](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=14618)

Challenge for Tel'abim: Perma-pvp flag in cities
[viewtopic.php?t=14618](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=14618)

Suggestion to incentivise wpvp - player tokens
[viewtopic.php?t=17138](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=17138)

Guild banner working in world
[viewtopic.php?t=20089](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=20089)

These suggestions, while written by me, synthesize many of the discussions had between TA players since its launch. I sincerely hope they get some attention.
