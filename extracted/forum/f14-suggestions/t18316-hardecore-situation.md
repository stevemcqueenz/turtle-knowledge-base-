## Post 124999 by Styxlplyx — 2025-04-01T22:30:00Z

So lately I've been hearing things here an there through the grape vine in that, you all do not wish to have anyone or anything impact HC players. If this is the thing going forward, an doing something like giving them a player buff for say 30 minutes, is considered a ban-able offense. Then a few things need to be done.

First. Everyone should be able to tell merely at a glance/targeting of said character = Hardcore challenge. Via in the form of a perma buff, or have it attached to their name or show up in the toolbar.

 2) You all will need to code in the mobs tagged by HC players as non-targetable from non-HC players. By this i mean. If you target them. Abilities will not go off on said mob/player. Doing so will ensure that mob tagging can't be a thing for HC players.

 3) If not already done, it is highly advisable that you make it super blatant that any form of help towards someone in HC, unless the help that is given is from another HC player, is a ban-able offense.

The reason i say this is because on a number of times I've actually helped a HC player kill a mob, simply because they were right next to me, while i myself was killing a mob for a quest. I wasn't even aware doing this was ban-able. Should be noted that at the time of this suggestion, I've only been on T-wow for about a week. If that.

If you are unsure on how to go about this, or are looking for a better example of what I am referring to when i talk about targeting of mobs, I'd advise you to take a look at how project ascension has done their ironman challenges. As it directly correlates to what I'm suggesting being done for Hard Core characters.

The above is probably very complicated to do in terms of coding. [I'm assuming here as I am not a coder myself.] The other suggestion would be to simply make/introduce a new realm, an force those that want to play HC to play on said new server. [This could be a bad thing in that you might see an absurd about of people back lash due to being forced onto another server to play HC] Regardless of what you do, its important that you be transparent with your player base an inform them of the ongoings. Via global post from gm's or even a post made public on the launcher itself. All in all, i hope things get resorted an to those taking the time to read this. Have a wonderful day. <3
