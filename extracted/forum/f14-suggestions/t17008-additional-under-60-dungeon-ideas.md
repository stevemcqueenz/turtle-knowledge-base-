# Additional Under-60 Dungeon Ideas

## Post 116060 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-10T01:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116060#p116060 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

On Tel'Abim world chat the other day we were talking about zones that don't have dungeons that could use them, and even some zones that have an instance that could use another one. These are some of the ideas that I thought were cool and other ones that we had in <Travelers> guild chat. Starting areas were excluded. I hope the dev team feels free to use any/all of these ideas if they're inspiring.

- **Darkshore**- already kind of feeds into BFD, which is similar levels to Deadmines so let's say it goes here.
- **Loch Modan**- already has Gnomeregan
- **Silverpine**- already has SFK
- **Westfall**- already has Deadmines
- **Barrens**- already has like 3 dungeons

- **Redridge**- Stonewatch Keep: Taking the fight against the Blackrock clan of Orcs, enemies of the Horde and the Alliance. Wouldn't this be the first Orc-centric dungeon in the game? Another opportunity to showcase the influence of the Black Dragonflight at lower levels. Could include remnants of the warlock Morganth's influence.
- **Stonetalon**- Bloodfury Valley: The Bloodfury Harpies are getting more aggressive and lashing out at others in the area. Includes sections against the Venture Co and its mechanical tree-cutters, which are devastating the harpies' homes. Night Elf, Tauren, and Cenarion Circle quests here. Maybe include Orcish and Human military forces that are funding Venture.
- **Ashenvale**- already has Crescent Grove.
- **Duskwood**- Manor Mistmantle: Picking up the story threads of the Scythe of Elune from Ashenvale and SFK, we explore more about the [Wolf Cult](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Wolf_Cult) and their vendetta against Malfurion Stormrage. Stop them before they wake up the worgen still trapped inside the Emerald Dream! Includes undead and Dark Riders. Made unique from areas like SFK and Gilneas City by the ties to the Emerald Dream the focus on the Scythe of Elune, and moon lore of Azeroth, as well as Karazhan tie-ins.
- **Wetlands**- will technically be getting Grim Batol, but that's a raid. As this is kind of a low level zone and around a lot of water, it makes sense to me to make this the first Murloc centric dungeon. Bluegill Reef: Blessed by shamans of the Earthen Ring, you are tasked with uncorrupting the local water elementals off the coast. They're being disrupted by Old One worshipping murlocs, who live in a network of sunken ships from The Lost Fleet, part of which is infested with the ghosts of the dead Kul Tiran sailors. Could probably throw some naga in there as well if you wanted, some gigantic aquatic sea life as a boss would be cool.
- **Hillsbrad**- Durnholde Keep: Syndicate forces have stolen necromantic knowledge from the local Forsaken, and are using it to raise the spirits of those interned at the Keep against both the Horde and the Alliance. Fight human bandits, forsaken accomplices, ghostly orcs and trolls, and even the re-animated skeleton of Lord Aedelas Blackmoore. Help give an avenue to make Ravenholdt rep something that could be more relevant.
- **Thousand Needles**- Saltspire Caverns: Help the Earthen Ring shamans preserve the integrity of the elements against the goblins of Venture Co. Delve into the saltwater caverns that still exist deep in the walls of the canyon. Fight rock, water, salt, crude oil, and fire elementals and combat withered husks of plant creatures.

- **Alterac**- Honestly we should probably see a couple Dalaran dungeons, or something done with that city before anything else is done in the area, but there's another obvious choice as well. Ruins of Alterac: Assist either the Stormpike or the Frostwolf in retaking the city from the Crushridge Ogre clan and stealing the Crown of Will from their leader. There's no other ogre-centric dungeon, and this could have great use of Yetis, and reinforce the Frostwolf/Stormpike feud that exists in PvP, as I imagine that each faction would kill the other faction's in-instance quest givers as part of the instance.
- **Arathi Highlands** Stromgarde: This place has always called out to be an instance. A follow-up to Alterac and Durnholde, it's a three-way battle between the forces of The Syndicate who control the inner keep, the Boulderfist Ogres who control the former Mage district with the Witherbark trolls, and the forces of the remnants of Stromgarde who are trying to reclaim their city.
- **Desolace**- already has Maradon
- **Stranglethorn**- while this already has a raid, it doesn't have a dungeon. What it desperately needs is Bloodsail Cove. Deep in tunnels under Booty Bay, the Bloodsail pirates keep their most secret headquarters. Rum-running, banana-smuggling, and maybe a couple of tomes of dark magic? Connections to the Syndicate and Defias? An opportunity to expand the pirate lore.
- **Dustwallow**- this already has Onyxia, and honestly this zone needs more of its own character before it gets another instance. That said, a fun addition would be Alcaz Island: Ruled by the evil gnome Doctor Weavil and his mind-controlled minions as well as their mechanical contraptions and chemical experimentations. Mad scientist vibes all over the place.
- **Badlands**- already has Uldaman
- **Swamp of Sorrows**- already has Sunken Temple

## Post 116061 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-10T01:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116061#p116061 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- **Feralas**- already has Dire Maul
- **Hinterlands**- Jintha'alor: Ruled by the Vilebranch trolls, who worship Hakkar the Soulflayer. There are already a lot of quests to this elite area, and it would make more sense to formalize this sub-zone into an instance that is structured more coherently, and that would inspire people to queue to do this content which is often overlooked.
- **Tanaris**- already has Zul'Farak and Black Morass
- **Searing Gorge**- already has all of the BRM tie-in content and doesn't need any more.
- **Azshara**-The Tower of Eldara: A new wizard tower dungeon! A mostly Naga-themed area who use their makura slaves to do their dirty work. Lower levels are more mundane sea creatures, middle levels have naga witches and highborne ghosts and mana elementals, upper levels have naga and satyrs and demons. Maybe a sea giant boss in there for good measure.
- **Blasted Lands**- Tainted Scar: The obvious choice for this region, and a nice upgrade for Lord Kazzak and his demonic horde. Aid the wizards of Dalaran in not just containing the Burning Legion's final forces, but getting rid of them altogether. Dreadmaul ogres in the front lines, fel orcs, all sorts of demons, and a Doomlord boss.
- **Un'goro Crater**- Titanic Park: After discovering a Hollow Earth style cavern, a dwarven mining billionaire hires a team of goblins and gnomes to make a zoo of all the dinosaur creatures in Un'Goro. Unfortunately their experiments go wrong. Rescue tourists and put down those sins against god and science. Almost exclusively dinosaur mobs.
- **Felwood**- Jaedenar]: Warlocks of the Shadow Council have taken over ancient Night Elf ruins, and are using the area's fel energies to advance their goals. Both demon hunters and druids are working together to stop them. Satyrs, orc and forsaken warlocks, tons of demons (especially succubi and felhunters), the dreadlord Banehollow, and possibly some ties to the Timbermaw who live nearby.

- **Burning Steppes**- see *Seeing Gorge*, above. Also HFQ in case we *really* needed more lvl 50s content in this area lol.
- **Western Plaguelands**- already has Scholomanse Live and Dead
- **Eastern Plaguelands**- already has Stratholme and Naxx
- **Winterspring**- Darkwhisper Gorge: This is already 5-man content, it's just begging to be a full instance for new 60s. You could keep it Burning Legion themed, or do like retail and switch it to Twilight's Hammer, which IMO makes more thematic sense for the area. Twilight's Hammer is fun as it's a clan of orcs that eventually grew to be a multi-racial doomsday cult, and this would be a nice bookend to BFD. There's not a ton of Old God content in classic WoW and this is a great place to add some.
- **Deadwind Pass**- already has Karazhan
- **Moonglade**- putting an Emerald Dream dungeon here is a no-brainer. It writes itself.
- **Silithus**- only exists to support AQ... but it might be fun to have a 5-man level 55-60 dungeon in this zone. There are also Twilight's Hammer cultists here already, so this might be another great spot for that idea. Something that includes bugs and some Old God cult, but isn't all about the AQ ruins. Something more personal and dark, like a Lovecraft story.

Call the dungeon Uldoro, and heck if you really wanted to, you could do wings.  Silithid wing in one section where the waning power of the Titans has led to Silithid bugs establishing a hive under the command of Hive Princess Zin'thara has begun abducting humanoid explorers to be used as hosts for parasitoid wasps while wind elementals patrol the old ruins.
