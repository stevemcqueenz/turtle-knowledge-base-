# A missing dungeon

## Post 95346 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-05-21T11:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95346#p95346 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I mostly level via spamming dungeons and noticed something.
Most level ranges are perfectly covered once you reach RFC/DM, up until lvl 60.
Late 40s-early 50s are different though.
On paper Mara+ZF should handle those with Sunken Temple and Hateforge joining at 50. But in practice it doesn't work.
Mara+ZF might be announced as 44-54 (or something like this) dungeons, but they stop giving noticeable XP from lvl 48-49.
Hateforge and Sunken Temple might be announced from lvl 50 but until lvl 52 you are basically a mob magnet there, making everyone's life harder. Specially since at hateforge everyone wants to skip packs but with a lvl 50 guy it just leads to wipes.
For tanks even more so. If you wanna level via dungeon spamming as a tank, forget to switch to HF or ST before lvl 53 totally.
Tldr: A dungeon is missing which gives good XP from lvl 48-52/53. From then HF and SK can take over.

Ideas about this dungeon:
Azshara is in this level range and it's one of the most beautiful regions of the game. Currently underused specially since satyr farming moved to Hyjal.
An Azsara dungeon (48-52/53) would be awesome. Corrupted furbolgs and satyrs in the beautiful land, like a bigger Crescent Grove. Maybe with some added water region to make it more unique.

Imo the dungeon should not contain any rare purple or preBIS to avoid level 60s flocking it. It would reduce XP gain and would make the lvl 50 tank's life a nightmare when the lvl 60 DS/Ruin purple-geared warlock goes rambo in the hopes of a 1% purple trinket (kinda the Hateforge problem but at an even lower level).

Let me rephrase. Late 40s lack a dungeon with good XP and early 50s have dungeons in which characters can only be carried (maybe unless you're a healer) + they pull everything just by breathing.

For each example:
 * Uldaman barely gives XP for lvl 48+. For dungeons-leveling it is not optimal if the majority of the dungeon is green (or even gray).
 * Same applies to Gilneas but to a lesser extent. It is actually a great XP gain dungeon until the mid 40s but it really slows down at 48+.
 * ZF is noted as 44-54 but honestly, from lvl 47 is starts giving bad XP. Above that bad XP becomes laughable XP.
 * Maraudon has 3 parts. Purple and even orange become kinda obsolete at late 40s for XP gain. Princess is a so-so, but not optimal either. In my last run, if I remember well, got 3% XP for the run (team only went Princess and Croc but + goblin and Landslide would only raise this to 5-6% at best). Had rested XP, didn't have warmode.
 * BRD, even the prison is higher than Hateforge. You can go there (by bypassing LFT which says 52+ but that's the absolutely being carried by friends category).

My point is that an efficient leveling dungeon is lacking from this level range.
