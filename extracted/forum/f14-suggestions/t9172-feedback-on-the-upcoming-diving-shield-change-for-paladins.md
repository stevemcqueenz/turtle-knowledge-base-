# Feedback on the upcoming Diving Shield change for Paladins.

## Post 59566 by Verdict — 2023-08-28T15:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59566#p59566 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello dear turtle developers.   turtle_in_love

Since the post with the change really worried me I wanted to give you a little bit of a heads up on what this change would do & how to address the **Holy Strike** issue. As a passionate WoW Player playing exclusively paladins in classic for over a decade I can share a lot of knowledge with you & also I have helped shape other projects with sometimes direct contact with developers.

[viewtopic.php?t=5391](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=5391) - **the change itself.**
"Divine Shield now reduces the damage you deal by 50% instead of reducing attack speed by 100% during its duration.
Developer Note: One of paladin's strengths in PvP is their armor piercing Holy Strike. With 50% damage reduction while under effect of Divine Shield, it will be much harder from them to oneshot people with this ability, giving other classes more counterplay options. We closely monitor PvP balance in battlegrounds and Blood Ring and expect more balance changes targeted strictly at PvP soon."

 1. So lets really talk about what in reality makes the **Holy Strike** as strong as it does. There are a few factors.
 - The geometric growth of the ability is double dipping & is instead making it exponential. *1
 - The base damage of **Holy Strike** is inflated by the additional damage, seen in the tool tip.
^remember these two, really important for 4.

 2. The change to **Divine Shield** would be absolutely irrelevant outside of removing the "lazy player's" opportunity to deliver the blow.
 - A good paladin will simply cancel the Bubble before they unload the burst to achieve somewhat of the same effect. Trinkets are available, the likelihood of this change ever doing something to stop the burst from happening is insignificant.
 - Actually the change can be seen as a buff in some cases, especially if you by accident auto attack during the Bubble. Once that change lands the paladins will be able to stack their **Crusader Strikes** without worrying about the swing timer. At this moment auto attacking inside your bubble places an irrevertible +100% swing time which does not drop post bubble's removal. Or in other words you guys have not put the trigger on adaptive attack speed, which is not that big, but still. The paladin will be able to have a chance to fish for **Vengeance** twice more & setup an EVEN STRONGER BURST, potentially.

 3. I am not entirely sure you're making this change for **Holy Strike** exclusively however.
 - This will impact more the already extremely strong **Holy Shock** build more than the **Holy Strike** as it does depend on a bigger window to burst with.
 - The **Holy Shock** build is strong only in scenarios against undergeared players as it has a lot of base but scales rather poorly due to it's 20% coefficient. That's somewhat addressed with the upcoming nerf of the Crusader Strike stacking through talents & two items.

 4. HOW TO FIX THIS? Let me try & help & I do hope you guys give this a read, you won't get this from other paladins, they rather stay silent about it. You can do this in many ways, none of which should ever be done together, or we simply revert back to Classic:
 - Fix the exponential growth due to double dipping. Originally in Classic, you'd have **Holy Damage** benefit from only the **Holy Portion** of **Vengeance**. The way this is resolved is in the coding of the spell, it does not use the "current" swing from your character. Rather it uses the base of your weapon & scales it up afterwards. This is done so the geometric growth is unhindered. Now... so you don't nerf the Paladain to Pre-TurtleWoW Days, I'd recommend a slight apology by increasing it's spell power scaling or potential PPM. This is the case for **Seal of Command's Proc, Holy Strike**.
 - **Holy Strike's** Damage can be left with nothing but an attack speed cap, similar to how **Crusader Strike** is in The Burning Crusade, or in other words 3.3 top swing scaling speed, ignoring the fact that you could be using a 3.8 or 4.0, etc. Ideally you can keep the additional damage, but lock it to that 3.3 as it will have a cap on it's burst, artificial caps are never OK, but it's a fair band aid solution. By doing this, the weapon's DPS will be the ruling factor, not the heft of the swing which is so essential for the **Paladin's** design.
 - **Holy Strike's** CD to be cut entirely, instead your attacks will deal additional holy damage (a really reasonable amount) like a fixed Seal of Blood on you that will cost similar mana. This can be outlandish & silly,I do not dare to presume what your idea of the class is & it's place in the World of Turtlecraft the way you see it.

 *1 <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponential_growth> - Exponential Growth difference to geometric, cheeky wiki link to get players to understand the crazy difference.
