# New mage spells v. 1.6

## Post 15043 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-12-30T19:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15043#p15043 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello development team!

I appreciate all the amazing additions and upcoming class changes as well.

However, I feel like the mage has not received enough attention in the upcoming January 7 update.

I think your ideas about arcane missiles are precise and show that you have a good grasp of what needs to be changed for the class - as it is - to be more balanced.

I would like to suggest a further design philosophy to be considered though:
Think of the mage as a dps class with separate, different schools of magic, with hybrid builds being only 1 of several playstyles. Right now only the talents determine the mage playstyle, but I would like to suggest the addition of spells for arcane, frost and fire that would allow a pure 1-school based ability build (more or less).

Spell ideas:
Slow (Arcane)
Invisibility (Arcane)
Ice lance (Frost)
Dragon´s breath (fire)

as you can see, these spells are basically straight from TBC, but I think the balance should be defined by you and changed as you want it. With these spells it would finally be possible to play a proper arcane mage, instead of being forced to use fire blast and frost nova in every fight (they would still be options of course). A fire mage without using frost nova, and a frost mage without using fire blast.

Thanks for reading, hope you´ll take the suggestions into consideration and I am sorry for suggesting these things earlier long before v. 1.6. I simply thought things are different now with the new expansion.

Yeah, my suggestions are by no means meant to increase the power of mages. Instead my wish is to give them more space to play meaningfully in different ways as I, as a long time mage player, think the gameplay is kind of repetitive with few options for changes. Some other classes have the option to fundamentally change their playstyle from tank to healer to dps, but a pure dps class like the mage nevertheless needs to have a bit of the same even though it's all within the DPS frame.
