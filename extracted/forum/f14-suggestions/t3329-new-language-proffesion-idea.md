## Post 20270 by Hir3 — 2022-07-25T17:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20270#p20270 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you wanted to do a hardcore vanilla grind, maybe it could be leveled this way: Every 10k experience you receive (or equivalent at 60) grouped with another player of that race, you receive one skill point in that language. Grouping with a Human or Orc could give 1 for every race in that faction for every 40-50k exp. Diplomacy for Humans could additionally reduce this requirement slightly by 10%.

So if you leveled in a 4 man of 2 Dwarves, Human, and Night Elf, and gained 70k exp, the Night Elf would receive +15 in Dwarvish(14 Dwarf, 1 Human), +1 in Thalassian and Gnomish.

Takes roughly 4.3 million exp to level 1-60, so it'd be a serious effort, but easier at 60, coming naturally in dungeons/raids too.
