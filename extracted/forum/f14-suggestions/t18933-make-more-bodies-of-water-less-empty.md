# Make more bodies of water less empty.

## Post 128806 by DopamineSage — 2025-04-28T11:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128806#p128806 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey there!   smiling_turtle_head

I love it when there are mobs in the water. It adds a sense of life around the area, and if there are enemy mobs in the water, it keeps me engaged. Also, finding Sickly Looking Fish in the Undercity is a cool bit of RP/lore aspect I like.

However, some ponds, rivers, and oceans are a bit empty. Perhaps adding some unclickable fish could add more life to these waters. Why I think this is also good, is that you can fish in these waters, but you don't see any fish swimming about...

Also, sharks and whales, that players need to watch out for. In Amani Alor, near the shore is a fishing dock (pardon, if that's not what it's called), and if you swim to the right from it, and follow the cliffside, you get to Zoram Strand. Nothing there, that can tell a player to be careful. Perhaps if there was a Shark, that could allow players to swim there, but they need to be careful doing so. Even with swimming from The Den to Ratchet, using the Hidden Path, nothing there to tell players to be careful.
