# High Elf voice line "Glory to the Sin'dorei" should be removed or changed

The high elves are referred to as the Quel'dorei. Their original TBC voice line which says "Glory to the Sin'dorei" should be removed or change with AI to say 'Quel'dorei'.

If you notice it, you will see only the Sin'dorei says that, cause in-game you have Sin'dorei and Quel'dorei.
The Quel'dorei only says "What brings you here?", "Hello", "I'm listening", etc. And their voice are for soft and calm.

The Sin'dorei has basically all the quotes of the retail Sin'dorei, and their voices are more loud and agressive.
