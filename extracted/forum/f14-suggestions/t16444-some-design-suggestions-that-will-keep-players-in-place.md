# Some design suggestions that will keep players in place

## Post 112164 by ChelloCsayA — 2024-12-11T05:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112164#p112164 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm a game developer from China, a Steam game developer, and a veteran WOW player. I've noticed that the Turtle WOW is always developing new modes to encourage players to start at level 1. I don't think that's going to keep players.
I hope you will listen to my advice.
You've designed a Shaman set that's very similar to the Shaman in WAR3. And also designed the skill "Heavenly God Descends to Earth".
My idea for this is that this type of design can be expanded.
To use Warrior as an example, because I'm a veteran Warrior player.
We can design a unique skill for warriors of different races and different talents.
For example, the Orc Warrior, the last point of weapon talent, rewards a "Wind Walk" skill: similar to the Orc Juggernaut in WAR3. The final point of the Berserk talent rewards a "clone spell".
As another example, the last point of the Necro Warrior's defensive talent rewards a "respawn". Designs like that.
It's hard to keep the balance of this talent on hand, and I think developers can even design some difficult tasks for talent rewards. Let players have a lot to consider when choosing talents.
This will also encourage players to explore many of the quests in the Turtle Suit, and players will create new characters to play for talent rewards.
I'm sorry that I'm using translation software to communicate, but if there's anything that isn't clear, I can elaborate on it  satisfied_turtle_head

Hi, friends, I just used the warrior as an example. It doesn't just emphasize the warrior.
We can expand our thinking.
For example, take the high elf mage as an example. You can design a design that summons a fire phoenix for the last point of the fire talent, making it look more like the blood mage in WAR3.
Of course, the idea I put forward is not to satisfy a certain fantasy, but to let players re-choose their race through the linkage between race and talent in WOW.
Instead of encouraging players to start upgrading again by constantly developing new modes and rewarding mounts like the current turtle WOW.

Of course, I am very grateful to all friends for communicating with me, and these exchanges and thoughts will also allow me to use them in my own games
