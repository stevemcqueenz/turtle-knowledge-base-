# Suggestions for Class and Quality-of-Life Improvements

## Post 126361 by Jayxu — 2025-04-10T22:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126361#p126361 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’ve seen many improvements in the recent class changes—thank you for all the work that’s gone into them. That said, I still see a few areas where things could be improved further:

 **Enable Class Quest Reward Swaps for All Classes**
As a Paladin, I cannot swap my level 50 class quest reward at the NPC Feid Rota. However, Warriors and Warlocks can. It’s unclear why only certain classes have this option, and I hope the ability to swap rewards can be made consistent across all classes.

 **Make T2.5 and T3 Quests Repeatable for All Classes**
Some classes are unable to repeat T2.5 or T3 quests, which creates imbalance. For example, Hunters and Rogues cannot obtain duplicate items to apply different enchants, while some other classes can. Making these quests repeatable for everyone would ensure equal treatment and more gear flexibility.

 **Provide Limited Item Restoration for Disenchanted Gear**
Players sometimes mistakenly disenchant valuable quest reward items. Offering a limited item restoration service—perhaps once a month, quarter, or year—through a website or GM request would be a helpful safeguard, similar to what Blizzard provides..
