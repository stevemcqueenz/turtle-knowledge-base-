## Post 95478 by Ayesha (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-05-23T08:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95478#p95478 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi again!
Night elf priest racials need rework.

While I think that giving every priest fear ward would simplify the game, look at the condition of night elf priest racials and compare it to other races in PVE:

Humans: insta self heal (PVP + PVE utility), diplomacy, +spirit
Dwarf: fear ward (PVP + PVE utility), stone form (PVP + PVE utility), insta self heal (PVP + PVE utility)
High elf: bloodlust (PVP + PVE utility), self-buff for smite priest, evocation
Undead: will of the forsaken (2 min cd) (PVP + PVE utility), the devouring plague dot
Troll: berserking (PVP + PVE utility), shadowguard
Night elf: starshards (an arcane channeling spell with poor scaling), elune’s grace (which is only useful in pvp or while levelling, as a priest in PVE you never should be hit period), shadowmeld (useful in PVP)

Night elves are in the worst position when it comes to PVE. There is absolutely no PVE utility.

Why not make Elune’s grace buffable on other people again, for example? That would help pala tanks for example and just would be a utility cool down. Or maybe other people have other ideas, please do share.

People talk about racials giving flavour, why can’t this flavour provide utility like every other priest race?
