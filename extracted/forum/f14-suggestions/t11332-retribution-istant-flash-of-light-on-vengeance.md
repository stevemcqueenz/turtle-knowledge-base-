## Post 74988 by Jstansberry (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-31T20:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74988#p74988 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If we want to make Paladins into the "melee healers" that Blizzard employees said they were originally intended to be, then their new offensive capability should be toned down or outright removed. Holy Strike is a very overtuned ability and allows Paladins to have by far the most burst damage out of any melee class, which would be fine in a vacuum but they are also the best single target healers in PVE and best PVP healers over-all while having incredible utility in Cleanse, Blessing of Freedom, HoJ, Repentance, et cetera.

I don't think this would be a good change as hybrid roles would likely never be worthwhile in vanilla-based gameplay. Classes that do one thing at a time very well are always going to be meta. Turtle team should choose between toning down either the utility or burst damage of Paladins.

Even with Holy Strike and Crusader Strike, prot and ret aren't viable in PvE without a Windfury totem in their group. Nerfing Holy Strike to level out their burst capability and buffing Crusader Strike to make them more capable of sustainted damage/threat generation in PvE would be ideal. Give Crusader Strike a short cooldown (4.5 secs maybe) and give it some minor scaling with AP on the initial damage and SP for the bonus holy damage while also increasing the base values of both. As it is right now, DPS and tank paladins are very reliant on WF procs to perform. This means that on the PVP server, paladins will be back to being relegated to one-button healers.
