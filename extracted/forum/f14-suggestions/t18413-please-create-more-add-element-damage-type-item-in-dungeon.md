It's been an ongoing problem for that particular mob since its release over a year ago. The dev team has yet to do anything about it because the posts never get much attention (it's a niche interest), despite it being an incredibly easy fix. I have a feeling they only consider the most talked about posts, which is why I wish there was some sort of rating and voting system to incorporate both popularity and ease of implementation to all suggestions. (If dev team members are considering this issue of rare mobs though we would love a comment to recognize that!)

Rather than add the rare drop items to dungeons, my recommendation is to make high value items BoP and randomize the spawn times of all rare mobs. This would preserve their rarity and the core philosophy and enjoyment behind finding rares in the open world. I don't know if Tofudao really leads a monopoly like you think, but what I do know is that level 60s logging in for a few seconds once every day to monopolize a low level item and fix the price at hundreds of gold on the auction house is not healthy for the game, regardless of who is doing it. If Tofudao is really as they say, just trying to get the item for use on their own HC character, then they would also benefit from the item becoming BoP.

As a sidenote, I'll have to try consecrate, but I'd be surprised if it's the best way to tag mobs on spawn since it requires the tick to line up well with the spawn time. Typically what I've seen and heard from others frustrated about the blazespark spawn is that one or more 60 hunters tend to get the tags using a combo of pets set on aggressive, spamming a target macro, and probably just having good latency.

Lastly, as much as I love the add-element-damage-type items, some caution needs to be maintained about adding more into the game as they are already quite unbalanced at low levels. Since they don't normalize to weapon swing time like most weapon proc chance effects I believe they add quite a bit more dps than any other comparable item level armor piece for fast attacking classes, at least until you get into the level 40s or so, and their interaction with spellcast effects is straight up broken strong. Adding more into the game is a much more labor intensive change then just changing the existing ones to BoP and randomizing rare spawn windows, and also may require some rebalancing around how the proc works.
