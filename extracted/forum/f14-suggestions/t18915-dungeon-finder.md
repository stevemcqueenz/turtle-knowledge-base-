## Post 138704 by Anivers — 2025-07-11T07:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138704#p138704 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yes, please—could we have the Anniversary Realms Group Finder?

 1. It wouldn't group players automatically, but instead show everyone in the queue for the selected dungeons, along with their class, role, level, and notes.
 2. We'd be able to chat with them or send invites.
 3. We could create our own group composition.
 4. For HC players, we could simply add it to the note, for example: SM-Cath-HC, or even have an (HC) tag next to our names.

This would encourage more people to queue for dungeons and group quests. HC players currently can’t use the Group Finder at all.
