# Remove the forced choice from the one-time quests

## Post 45723 by Balake (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-05-13T09:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45723#p45723 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

title is self explanatory, but I'll say WHY this change is needed.
Beginning from around level 50, people feel they are being shoved by the game into sticking to one role and discouraged from being hybrids despite choosing a hybrid class. A big reason for this is the game often forces people to choose one of many items designed for different specs.

The earliest case of that staying relevant to max level is the sunken temple class quests, so these are very important to tackle. For almost all of these, the rewards are very unbalanced. There's one good reward and the others are noob traps, which is very awful because leveling is supposed to be a learning experience yet the game imposes a choice on you that can severely harm your character's power PERMANENTLY.

Instead of trying to balance ALL the rewards which in addition to being a lot of hard work for little benefit, will make the forced choice feel even shittier for the players. Now you're not going directly for the good item, but you're stuck feeling that the thing you pick will make you forever undergeared in another spec.

So instead of all that, just make the quest give you all the rewards at once. In the majority of cases; players won't equip two of the rewards at the same time, because they're pretty tightly designed for a specific build.

Once done with the sunken temple quests, it's important to do the same to the blackrock spire trinkets (blackhand's breadth, mark of tyranny, eye of the beast), Hakkar heart, Onyxia, Nefarian, Ossirian, and C'thun.

I know so many priests who are stuck between two difficult choices, choose the healing necklace and have no good ring for shadow spec, choose the dps ring and use a bad necklace for healing.

Hybrids have a lot of issues on their plate already, this thing is a remnant of crappy design by Blizzard, something very simple to change that it feels like it should have been done already so long ago.

Is that bad? I main a true hybrid class (Druid), but that doesn't mean I'm equally viable in all roles. Skill, experience and your personal playstyle preference mean you probably organically develop a main role, not even looking at gear or spec.
And what does "hybrid" even mean? It usually means swapping roles between encounters (usually even between dungeons). So for any given encounter you are very likely to fill a single role. "True" hybrid gameplay where for example you dps for 20% of the encounter, then main heal for 40% before you switch into the tank role for the rest is almost unheard of and not really supported by fundamental game mechanics (can't swap armor in battle, can't respec, etc). Assembling a gear set and talent spec that allows you to tank and heal means paying a *hefty* hybrid tax and not filling either role particularly well.

So yes, you *are* supposed to pick a lane with your itemization. That's what makes it fun and interesting to plan out your future gear. There are trade-offs. You either put in the work and figure out which gear will benefit you to the most in the long run, or you bumble through picking the most immediate benefit and potentially pay the price for it later. Seems fair to me? That's coming from someone who chose the wrong UBRS trinket back in Vanilla (and GMs refused to swap them).
