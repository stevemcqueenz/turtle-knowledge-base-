# New Set: Cow Kings Leathers

## Post 164099 by Zashferret — 2025-12-31T03:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164099#p164099 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So, currently the "Cow King's Hide" is a level 45 rare leather chestpiece that gives 10 Stamina and 10 resist to all schools. This is not only a reference to Diablo 2, but it was added to Diablo 2 in the Lord of Destruction expansion.

This set consisted of the "Hide" (body armor), "Horns" (a Cap) and "Hooves" (boots)

This blue item could easily be part of a set in Twow, not too powerful but a fun nod to D2X.

"Cow King's Horns" and "Cow King's Hooves" could share the same stat theme as the Hide. 6-7 Stamina and all resists for the hat, and 5-6 Stamina and resists for the boots. A 2-piece bonus of 20 Attack Power and a 3-piece bonus of 3% increased attack and casting speed.

Nothing absurdly powerful for its level, there are better pieces out there at the level 40-50 range, but it's within the spirit of how the set works in D2X.

I also have some (theoretical) conversions for some of the other sets for lower levels from Diablo 2 as well. Civerb's Vestments, Arctic Gear, Iratha's Finery, Infernal Tools, Cathan's Traps and Sigon's Complete Steel.
