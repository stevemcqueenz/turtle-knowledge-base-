# What Druid changes are necessary 2025 March

## Post 122856 by Templar85 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-03-18T06:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122856#p122856 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What are the issues that need fixing:

General

- Should able to interact with loot and use quest items, chests, and doors in feral forms.

- Changing Idols trigger GCD.
 *Remove or lower swap GCD to 0.5 sec.*

- Roots can't be used indoors.
 *Vanilla druid toolkit was flawed so many ways which TBC greatly fixed. In TBC druids have cyclone which is a total enemy lockdown. Allowing to able to cast roots anywhere will not make them imbalanced because they still can do actions while rooted.*

- Remove the cast time from Swift Travel Form
 *Should be instant or maximum 1.5 sec cast time and act like plainsrunning. No riding haste effect affecting it making the Epic riding epically worthless. Also should have much better visual for it.*

Feral

- Should be able to activate racial abilities in feral forms.
 *Also Night Elfs racial Whisp is totally worthless so they have -1 racial compared to other races. Shadowmeld also still not good after the changes*

- Armor cap for Bears should be increased.
 *Just for Bears armor need to be increased. Dodge is much worse than Parry and Block. We should not have the same armor level as Warrior and Paladin when they can mitigate damage much better. It's aimed that we should have higher armor and HP poll for compensation but other classes can reach the same amount of it. Also we can reach it with T2 gear with Priest and Shaman effect. Talents like Thick hide is losing their worth also in early raiding.*

- Cat has no aoe ability but still mediocre at single target dps.

- There is zero talent in the feral tree that support occasional hybrid healing.
 *Heart of the Wild provides some intellect increase but still feral druids healing spells are extreme inefficient and there is no talent that can increase their healing output something like agility to bonus heal conversion.*

- Bear Savage Bite should do more damage and generate more threat.
 *It is doing around 600 damage which is very low compared to Paladin and Warrior attacks. Also it should do more threat to make druid get a snap threat on command on pulls. Can reduce Maul's threat a little and give that amount to Savage Bite. We have very low amount of attacks. Auto attack, Maul and 6 sec Savage Bite. Swipe is not efficient on single target. If other tank classes having much more abilities and executes at low health than we should have more damage on our few ones.*

- There is no talent that giving + Defence.
 *Fixing a talent tree design flaw with 8/9 T3 gear is not a good solution. Also reduces gear variety because can't swap out some pieces for more threat gen ones. If ferals able to cast Insect Swarm that greatly helps with mitigation in a 10-20 man Raid, Dungeon if there is a single druid tank there.*

- There are glancing blows for feral white hits.
 *It is heavily negatively affecting bleed play style. There is no way to reduce it unlike other classes.*

- It is impossible to track bleeds reliably in 40 man raids where multiple ferals in the raid.
Bleeds on enemies should be indicated by the debuff bar on the druid as well. The player and addons can track more easily this way.

- Feral Swiftness and PvP set bonus should work indoors.
 *It is stupid and it is kind of a bug rather than have any good reason behind it why they not working.*

- On hit effects and enchants should work for feral attacks.
 *Most of them have proc per minute so it doesn't matter if cats are hitting very fast.*

- The Idol that supports bleed style is total trash.
 *10% faster bleeds ticking is terrible.*

- Cower, Pounce, Ravage are extreme niche abilities.
 *Need to rework or add additional effects for them.*

- Frenzy regen nerfed to the ground beyond oblivion.
 *The problem was in pvp that people not realised they should not keep hitting the druid. The solution is to make extremely apparent visually that druid is under the effect and can have some movement impairing effect too but need to give back their previous healing amount return.*

Balance

- Only 2 out of 4 Balance spells are affected by haste.
Haste can have additional effect like 2% Healing and 1% Spell Power for instant spells and every 3 haste grants +1 spellhit to be more useful for Moonkins when there is so much Haste on their gear.

- Talents providing minimal crit (3%) for main damage spells Wrath and Starfire.
Balance toolkit to function properly heavily reliant on critting with Wrath and Starfire but their is no direct talent that increases these ones like as do for Moonfire beside Moonkin Form.

- Eclipse is luck based Capstone talent.
 *There is no way to have stable uptime for Eclipse even with 30% crit chance. There are no ANY other spec that have a luck based capstone talent. Capstone should be a controllable effect or have a way to force procc it and place a cooldown for the next force activation.*

Resto

- There is no Regrowth support Idol.

- Rejuvenation tick frequency should be 2s instead of 3 seconds.

- There is no Purge resistance talent option for druid hots spells.

- No resurrection spell for outside world and dungeon only.
 *It can cost reagent and can cause some stat deficiency on players for a short time so every other healers have better rez but at least druid can use it if no other option.*

- Healing Touch greatly lost its usefulness.
 *Currently it's main attribute that it is very mana efficient for the cost of long cast time. For raid it is greatly problematic to use beside with Nature's Swiftness. Regrowth is better option for almost all situation. In raids Chain Heals and Flash of Lights are dominating the heals.*

Talents that are not working well or need rework for them:

- Nature's Grasp
 *Improved Nature's Grasp is lacklustre and weak for 5 points.*

- Owlkin Frenzy
 *Not worth to put more than 1 point in it. If it kept at 3 points than need additional strong effect for it*

- Gale Winds.
 *Badly designed talent. It is near capstone, it need other functions or improve Hurricane damage and lower it's cooldown or casting time*.

- Brutal Impact
 *Very weak, need additional effect or become baseline.*

- Feral Adrenaline
 *It is an abomination. Delete it and have something else useful.*

- Ancient Brutality
 *It is good purpose talent but should have much stronger impact since It is very deep in the tree. It should restore more energy because it is insignificant compared to powershifting energy return. For Bear also kinda low rage return and 9 sec internal cooldown should be lowered to 5.*

- Gift of Nature
 *It have a good purpose but in practicality ONLY useful for Healing Touch build. It is good for Tranquillity too but that is a 30 min CD spell. It is very bad for Regrowth and Reju. Have to rework to give much more percentage of bonus for Regrowth and Reju.*

- Aessina's Bloom
 *It's purpose for help healing tanks but it should be more reliable and have another effect too. Currently it can not compete with Paladin tank healing effectiveness.*

- Preservation
 *It is a good talent but it can have some rework. Allow to increase Reju's tick healing if target have already Regrowth or Increase Regrowth upfront Healing amount as well not only the Hot part.*

- Improved Tranquillity
 *For 2 points deep in the talent tree the effect is not good enough. Beside increasing it's healing effectiveness it should increase the affected area and reduce it's cooldown if only affected by 10 players by 15 mins. So it should be more often used for 10 man raids and 5 man dungeons.*

- Tree of Life
It needs to be much better than current form. It is not increasing armor like other shapeshifts does and the movement penalty is extreme annoying and unnecessary. In BiS gear +60 healing aura is for the party is nice  but at that point they also have BiS and not needing extra +heals as much. At lower gear level providing much less bonus heal to the group.

It is very annoying because you have to keep switch out from it because raid leaving you behind and you have much harder time to avoid boss mechanics and even not having Lifebloom or Healing touch. It should be able to cast Entangling Roots. It is a nightmare to have any PvP in this form. Only exception in AV when your side turtling to defend your base lol. Sap immunity is good but you will still die and no chance to escape.

Conclusion

A lot of changes need. It is very worrisome to hear when devs saying they will do just a few minor ones because they think it's "viable". They are viable in many aspects but because of their hybrid nature they need much more attention and deep understanding how they work. In TBC they been greatly amended many of the design flaws.

I respect Turtle devs for their work but should try focus more on ``Are their toolkit fun and have good logic and practicality behind it?`` instead of plain "viability".
