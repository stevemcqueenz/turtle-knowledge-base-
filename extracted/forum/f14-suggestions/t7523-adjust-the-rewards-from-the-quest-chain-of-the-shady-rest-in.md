# Adjust the rewards from the quest chain of the  Shady Rest Inn

## Post 48082 by Seltsamuel — 2023-05-30T15:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48082#p48082 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello and good morning.

I really like what turtle wow did with the  Shady Rest Inn quest, which was never finished in vanilla. I did the alliance side and it looks like that the horde side is actually different, culminating in needing to kill the same elite. It is just really a shame that the blue coloured quest rewards seem to be underwhelming. Same goes for the required level as it seems.

PFquest and the twow database says this quest chain starts at 35 and ends at the same level. But you need to kill a level 43 elite in a camp of level 41+ monsters. Something feels off there. Same goes for the rewards. For example the caster dagger you get for this (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60820>) is worse than the dagger you can get in the scarlet monastery (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=7714>) by a lot and even a dagger offered in a different quest (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=51767>) outperforms what you needed to work more than hour towards. That quest requires you to kill some 44-45 monsters, instead of a level 43 elite.

My suggestion is to bump up the items slightly so you actually see people in world or guild chat looking for this elite quest.
