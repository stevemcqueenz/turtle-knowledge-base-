# Reduce level requirement for "Stealing a Core" quest

## Post 138170 by AverageAlc — 2025-07-05T16:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138170#p138170 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Change the required level for this quest from 45 to 38.

The Quest: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40129>
The Quest that points to it (should also be lowered): <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41103>

Stealing a Core is a TurtleWoW custom-quest for Uldaman.
In the LFG, Uldaman is listed as level 40-50, and the vanilla Uldaman quests all require either level 35 or 38.
But for some reason, this Uldaman quest requires level 45. A whole 7-10 levels above the other quests for this dungeon.
