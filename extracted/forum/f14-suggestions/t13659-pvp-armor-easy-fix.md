# PvP Armor - Easy Fix

## Post 92795 by Krotux (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-04-16T06:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92795#p92795 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is a lot of talk of how T4 will cause pvp to be even more imbalanced and pvp gear needs to be stronger without being good for pve. Here is a simple and FUN way to fix the problem.

Problem:
PvP gear sucks at PvP compared to PvE gear

Solution:
Simply greatly buff the set effects of PvP armors for PvP only.

For example, take Warlocks:
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17581>

Imagine if these set effects were overhauled to be something like:
(2) Reduce cast time of Immolate & Searing Pain by 1s.
(4) Gives your curses and dots a 50% chance to resist dispel.
(6) Reduce cast time of Fear & Howl of Terror by 1s.

Yes I know it's OP. But, I just proved a PvP set can be stronger than T4 potentially, WITHOUT making it necessary in PvE. IT CAN BE DONE!

This above is just an example. I'm sure we can come up with better ideas, and do it for all classes.

You can have crazy buffs to all sorts of things including entangling roots, vanish, polymorph, pets, etc.

There could even be multiple sets of pvp gear in the future as well, for more variety and ways of playing.
