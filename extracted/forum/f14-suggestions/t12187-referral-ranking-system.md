# Referral ranking system

## Post 81055 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-13T18:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81055#p81055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ranking system now requires insane amount of honor grind OR mafia-like cooperation across whole faction (assuming that everyone follow the rules). In worst case you end up competing against your own faction.
I think that better ranking system should require cooperation between large number of players.
All those players should understand that they are cooperating and accept the rules (now everyone just compete with eachother).
It should also be somewhat healthy. At least compared to raiding. It should not be too easy, but in the same time should not require you to sacrifice your life.
Ideally it should involve participants of all levels.
It should also be simple in its description.
Main and important point, it should be unique compared to other private servers. There are enough system like "just grind your rank", I think it should be more engaging.

There is other my suggestion regarding it: [guild-wide standing](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=11698).
This one is more complicated, but real world shows that it is effective.

In real world, there is concept of referral network.

Rules of proposed system:
Each player can be subordinate of one other player (leader). Both sides should be willing to do so (like joining to party or guild).
Leader can be subordinate of other leader. Leader-subordinate connections create referral network.
Leader gets ranking progress needed for r14 if 300 his subordinates (of all levels) and sub-subordinates in this week farms 50k honor, wins 1 AV, 2 WSG, 1 AV, 1 Sunnyglade.
When current leader gets his r14, people can reassign referrals and promote next leader to r14.

Leaders will not be able just to make premade of 10 people which do all ranking job. Ranking leaders need to help 300 of their subordinates to win AV to get their ranking progress. They even need to get all those 300 people to play sunnyglade at least once.
It does not matter who get more honor. Everyone need equal minimum amount. People can help eachother in fights. People don't need to stop their honor gain.
To get high rank, people need to play multiple character (and make a lot of them) or actually build that referral network.
People can ask to disconnect from their leader or subordinate. They actually disconnect after weekly honor calculation. Next week (after asking) player can join other leader.
Circular referrals should not be possible.
