# reduce late game raids powercreep

## Post 101798 by Caos (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-24T05:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101798#p101798 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The item level of late game raids gear is way too high.
It makes gearing just a matter of getting the higher piece of gear which stats makes some sense for your build while the initial vanilla design looks more like getting the gear with the perfect specific stats you want.

It forces everybody into raiding if you don't wanna get stomped and that causes a lot of balancing issues as every dps must deal the same damage and this is absurd in every other aspect of the game as some classes have a lot more utility than others. It doesn't make any sense that a paladin or a druid deal the same damage as a warrior as they can do a lot more things (without changing spec and gear).

Making the item level more horizontal would give a lot more freedom to play whatever way you want and would make a lot of items with cool effect viable.

It could be done on Tel'abim at least as it doesn't make much sense to force everybody on a pvp server to go raiding or get stomped.

## Post 101880 by Caos (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-25T09:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101880#p101880 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Its way easier to rescale the item level than to add new ways to obtain gear.
Right now the epic item level from level 60 items is in the range 65-92, you just interpolate it to something in the range 65-75.
The ammount of stats depends on the item level so you can just scale them all in bulk.

Having lower powercreep has obvious advantages on pvp but it also has advantages on pve as well.
All level 60 raids and dungeons remain more relevant and challenging. Right now a naxx dps player in Scholomance or Black Morass kills thinks too fast and also messes the aggro a lot if the tank isn't also geared.
Also the open world becomes trivial once you are geared so this change would make it more challenging.

As new tiers are added the whole pve item level could be reescaled again and the pvp item level stay the same. Pvp players should still get some items from new raids if they want to be completely optimal.

Its a low effort in development solution to keep old content relevant, increase the diferent posibilities of gearing a lot and keeping pvp gear relevant forever without needing to design new sets.
