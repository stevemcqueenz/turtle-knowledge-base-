# New +56 dungeon: Pirate Cove

## Post 36709 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-03T12:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36709#p36709 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A suggestion for a new dungeon with entrance at Gillijim or Lapidis.

Mobs will be pirates lvl 56-58. Bosses lvl 59-60. The pirates are the same non-affiliated hostile faction of the mobs on the islands.
Reputation quests and farming for Kul tiras and equivalent Horde faction.

Location: one of the unused mountain sides, facing the ocean, of the two islands. The dungeon doesn't have to be very big, but adding a deep cave will allow for more space than expected.
