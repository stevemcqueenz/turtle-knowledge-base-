# AV problem - deserters closing games.

## Post 90857 by Mcpewpew (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-03-20T04:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90857#p90857 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Several times in the last week we've had games that one team has only defended and had no intention of trying to win.

They do this until they are bored and then leave, the battleground then closes as there are no longer enough players on their team.

This obviously should not happen. Once a BG has had enough players, leaving should not result in closure.

The other choice would be to award the other team with an automatic win with significant bonus honor.
