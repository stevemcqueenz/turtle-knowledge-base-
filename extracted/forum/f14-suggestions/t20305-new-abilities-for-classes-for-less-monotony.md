# New abilities for classes for less monotony

## Post 139705 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-15T21:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139705#p139705 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Classic WoW has, in my opinion, only one downside compared to retail WoW.
It doesn't have enough viable abilities to incorporate into your rotation.
Mindlessly pressing 1 for frostbolt, shadowbolt, etc. on Rag just isn't fun.

This is just a list of spells that can be added for more variety. It does not mean all the spells I list should be added.

Therefore I want to collect peoples thoughts which abilities could be added that could make rotations for the various classes more fun.

If you do not know the spells I listed you can find all of them on wowhead. Most of these abilities are from BC and WotLK.

Arcane Mage: Arcane Blast, Arcane Barrage, Slow
Fire Mage: Dragon's Breath, Living Bomb
Frost Mage: Water elemental, ring of frost

Affliction Warlock: Unstable affliction, already has a nice rota though
Destro Warlock: Incinerate, Chaos Bolt
Demo Warlock: Shadow bolt volley, Felguard

Discipline Priest: Penance
Shadoe Priest: Vampiric Touch, Shadow Word: Death
Holy Priest: No idea

Balance Druid: Sunfire, Starfall, Typhoon, possible Treant pet([viewtopic.php?t=11533](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=11533))
Feral Druid: Mangle
Resto Druid: Wild Growth

BM Hunter: Kill command, tame exotic pets, tame mechanic pets for gnome and goblin hunters
MM Hunter: Chimera shot, Silencing shot, explosive shot, black arrow
Survival hunter: Lacerate, Harpoon, snake trap

Paladin in general: Judgement of Light, Judgement of Wisdom, Judgment of Justice(The yellow, blue and red judgement from wotlk)
Healadin: No idea
Tankadin: Holy shield(the one that bounces between several enemies dealing holy damage)
Retardin: Divine Storm

Elemental Shaman: Lavaburst, Thunderstorm
Enhancement Shaman: Dual Wield, Lava Lash
Resto Shaman: Riptide

Arms Warrior: Blade Storm
Fury Warrior: No idea
Prot Warrior: Devastate, Shockwave

Assassination rogue: Mutilate
Combat rogue: no idea
Subtlety rogue: Shadow step

Most of those spells are from WotLK, I know, but they are great abilities that still fit the Classic vibe. For some specs I had no idea, which is why I made this thread, maybe others can fill in those blanks.

## Post 139739 by Jinxes — 2025-07-15T23:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139739#p139739 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Rotations work on a handful of abilities because the effectiveness of more choices drops off quickly after the first few. There's little to no added benefit to be gained from any additional complexity.

Effectively, adding new abilities wouldn't improve rotations - it would just change them.

What you want then isn't more viable abilities, but more subclasses with different rotations.

This is just a personal idea, was no balancing behind it.

Rogues:
Would improve poisons, at least would remove reagents. They feel awful compared to a shaman weapon imbue.
Would introduce agitating poison earlier for meme tanks.
Would add at least one real raid utility poison which feels like a warlock curse in usefulness.
Would link combo points to the rogue, not to the target.

Warlock:
Would make shadowburn baseline and not costing a soul.
Would make howl of terror instant but raising CD to 1 min.
Would give some spell interruption protection and defenses to deep demonology for meme tanks.

Mage:
No idea.

Priest:
Would give shadow nova for shadow and would nerf pain spike in exchange of instant cast vampiric embrace.

Hunter:
Would give MM lone wolf talent.
Would give aspect of the monkey + threat and would give hunters a taunt for meme tanks.
Would scale beasts better for BM hunter to give them a real beastmaster fantasy. Probably with some downside to balance PVP.

Druid
Would stop mana regen if you are not in a mana-using form. No more getting back to cow and suddenly you have full many to do anything.
Would push cat a bit. I barely ever see one in PVE.
Would unify resto druid toolkit. You are either a tree now or you can use the whole healing set.

Pala
I'd push melee healer holy a bit more. I like the concept, don't like the execution.
Would give a valid niche to spellret.
Might nerf prot threat a bit.

Shaman
Would nerf chain heal a bit.
Would give deep enhancement a defensive CD.
Would slightly improve mana regen for ele and enh.
Would make ele PVE gameplay a bit more interesting with a new, non-OP ability.
Would move SS deeper into enh to prevent the currently OP shaman ele-enh build from existing. At least making it harder to create it.
