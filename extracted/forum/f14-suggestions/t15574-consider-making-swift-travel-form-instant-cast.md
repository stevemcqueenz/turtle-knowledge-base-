# Consider making Swift Travel Form instant cast

## Post 106404 by Midoko22 — 2024-11-04T21:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106404#p106404 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am not alone in being disappointed upon hearing that the Swift Travel Form has a normal mount cast time of three seconds. This seems unnecessary to me for a few reasons:

 1. No other shapeshift form in the game has a cast time, even flight forms in TBC+. This just makes the spell feel clunky, out of place, and bad to use.
 2. There is already a tradeoff involved of not benefiting from +mount speed enchants and items.
 3. In open world PvE content at level 60, I don't see it being "game breaking" or problematic to other classes/players in any way. It could potentially be very helpful for getting out of bad situations, and a tool that other classes don't have, but the same could be said for many classes with many such abilities afforded to them, and could be a fair trade off for the time, effort, and gold involved with getting it.
 4. The time, effort, and gold required are much higher than the other "class mounts," requiring killing a tier 2 raid boss as well as multiple difficult bosses throughout the world. There is also still a hefty gold cost as well.
 5. In PvP content like battlegrounds, it would ideally be disabled in the same way that Plainsrunning is for Tauren.
 6. On the topic of Plainsrunning, I think an agreeable middle ground would be to make the spell function in the same way, with speeds ramping up over three seconds. This particular spell/mount doesn't seem to be negatively impacting much as far as I can tell or have heard, or not heard of.

I was extremely excited when I heard about the spell for the first time, I just never find myself inclined to use it over Plainsrunning for short distances where i dont mind losing the +mount speed effects since Plainsrunning doesn't break the "flow" by having to stop and cast. Its such a great "class fantasy" idea and feel, but only if it was as seamless to use as the other shapeshift forms.

Perhaps there are some specific issues or exploits with an instant cast time that the Turtle Team could predict/want to avoid. Barring this, i would strongly urge an instant cast time to be reconsidered, as I'm sure every druid player would feel much better and eager to use it.
