# Emerald Blessing Idea.

## Post 111971 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-10T00:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111971#p111971 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Okay so in 1.17.1 Druids got the new Spell/Buff Emerald Blessing.

The only issue I have with this is that its basically unused outside raids.

The reason is because the Mat costs around 3.5g for one buff, which makes it very expensive.

My idea is to have two versions.

Emerald Blessing - Party wide buff - Costs 1 Small Dream Shard.

Emerald Gift (Current version) - Buffs your Entire Raid - One Bright Dream Shard (as it currently is)

This will bring the cost down and the buff see use in dungeon groups.
