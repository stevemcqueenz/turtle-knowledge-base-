## Post 147892 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-23T19:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147892#p147892 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This would be very difficult to accomplish, as the way Stormwind is set up in Cataclysm onwards is not the same way it's set up from vanilla to Wrath. Stormwind and Orgrimmar are actually one whole WMO (World Model Object) in vanilla, and are not actually open-aired areas. That is a carefully-crafted illusion. For example, note the giant Cathedral of Light that you can see on your way flying into Stormwind; it's only properly visible flying in and is there to provide the illusion of the sight of the Cathedral in the distance.

In Cataclysm onwards, Stormwind and Orgrimmar were effectively redesigned to be open-air to accommodate old world flying. Terrain is actually modeled, and the districts are each their separate WMO.

There exists a resource named "Open Azeroth Capitals" that has community-made models that turn vanilla Stormwind and Orgrimmar into open-air WMOs, but as these are made for a 3.3.5 client and not the 1.12.1 client that Turtle WoW uses, there are a lot of issues with downporting and incorporating these in without imploding the client.
