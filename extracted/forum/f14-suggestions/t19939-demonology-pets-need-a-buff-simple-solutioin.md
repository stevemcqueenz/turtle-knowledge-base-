## Post 136226 by pillowed — 2025-06-18T22:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136226#p136226 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Warlock pets still need a buff for demonology spec'd warlocks. After previously thinking about various potential fixes that wouldn't cause unfair advantages for Affliction or Destruction spec'd warlocks the solution appears to be in changing the "Power Overwhelming" talent.

As it is, the warlock pets simply do not do enough damage for a demonology warlock. And I understand any reluctance to baseline buff the damage of demon pets. However, the current damage output is simply way too low and the current "Power Overwhelming" talent really only serves to help eliminate Greater Demons before the enslave breaks.

Furthermore, the reward for a 40% to the health of your pet is way too low. 20% of a wet noodle is still a wet noodle.

I suggest changing the talent to increase the damage by a far greater amount like 200%, increase movement speed, and attack speed.
