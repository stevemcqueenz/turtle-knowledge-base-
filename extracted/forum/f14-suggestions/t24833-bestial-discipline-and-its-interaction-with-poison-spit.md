# Bestial Discipline and its interaction with Poison Spit

## Post 174810 by Lane13 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-04-11T12:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174810#p174810 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Bestial Discipline is a BM talent that currently reduces the CD of pet special abilities by 20% and gives them 5 extra Focus per tick.

Ignoring the fact that the talent needs a buff such as it affecting *all* pet abilities and/or providing more Focus because, due to the bloat of talent points in the BM tree and Bestial Discipline's overall lower damage boost, it's the first one to be skipped if going deep BM... the talent has an additional problem.

Reducing the CD of certain abilities is actually detrimental because of the way they work and the main ability in question here is the serpent's Poison Spit. Poison Spit is a DoT that lasts 8 seconds and also has an 8 second CD. Bestial Discipline lowers the CD and so the serpent constantly overwrites the previous DoT with a new one.

The solution: let each application of Poison Spit be an independent debuff on the target so that if a second one is cast, the damage on the first one that is about to fall off is not totally lost.

A similar issue is with Furious Howl. The additional focus is not enough to allow for Bite and Furious Howl to go off naturally while off-CD. In a longer fight, FH will be usually delayed by 1-2 ticks, because Bite ate up the pet's focus. On the surface it may not seem big, but throughout the course of a long fight it adds up. So if you want to have FH available, you have to manually cast your pet's bite and monitor available focus.

I know pet macroing is part of the class, but Bestial Discipline is supposed to remove oddities like that
