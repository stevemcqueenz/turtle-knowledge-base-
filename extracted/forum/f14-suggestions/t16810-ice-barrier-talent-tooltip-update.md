# Ice Barrier - talent tooltip update

## Post 114845 by Ahlen (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-30T13:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114845#p114845 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With so many changes to talents in Twow I believe that being precise in talents' description is quite important for planning your talents.

I have a problem with Ice Barrier although I assume I know how it works.

The tooltip says:
"Instantly shields you absorbing X damage and increasing your frost damage by 15%. Last 1 min. While the shield holds, spells will not be interrupted"

I believe the 15% frost damage applies only when you have an active shield. Otherwise, the bonus is lost (or maybe not?).

I would update the tooltip to the following:

"Instantly shields you absorbing X damage. While the shield holds, spells will not be interrupted and your frost damage will be increased by 15% for the duration. Last 1 min."
