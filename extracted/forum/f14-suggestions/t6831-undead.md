Forsaken players (and NPCs) were categorised as undead for a very short period of time.

That was changed because paladins were able to annihilate entire groups of forsaken players with ease. And that was long before they added a bunch of items increasing attack power or spell power against undead enemies too.

All it'd accomplish would be making forsaken as race completely unplayable. Imagine a paladin with lots of spell power and the anti-undead wizard oil popping their bubble and casting Holy Wrath in the middle of a horde raid and you'll quickly see the issue
