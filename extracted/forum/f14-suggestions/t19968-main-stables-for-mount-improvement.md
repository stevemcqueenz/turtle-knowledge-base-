Greetings!

IMAGINE there was a greater stables outside the main cities of every race, in which players can retrieve their mounts with infinite 500% riding buff with certain amount of charges who gets wasted the more you use your mount, needing to be charged by getting your mounts back to the stables, that we would call it gameplay. Mounts might become unable to be used as long they remain in the stables. Also mounts with the riding buff cannot be withdraw from the world, their remain in the place where you let them and if you get too far from them or die in combat, they return back to the stables. If you do that, I would call you the true vanilla experience. Please don't tell me summoning stones are ridiculous when we are able to summon a horse right from our pockets whenever we want.

Edit: In every minor town of the maps that is civilized there's at least 1 or 2 mounts to rent for 50c, they work if you stay mounted and vanish when they are dismount. However, main capitals of all races do feel short in terms of mobility, and if you ask me there should be mounts for rent in capitals aswell. They don't need to be in the center but as close to the main gates as possible. Is not like you're compromising the experience of high mounts. They are still temporary and will vanish after being dismount.
