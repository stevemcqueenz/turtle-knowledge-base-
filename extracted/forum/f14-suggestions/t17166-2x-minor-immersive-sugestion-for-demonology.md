Hello Dear Forum,
This is my first post and therefore I'd like to say a welcome to everybody & say BIG THANKS FOR MAKING TURTLE WOW SO MUCH FUN!!

I always loved playing classic Warlock, and I'm super happy with all the changes this class have undergo here. Especially, the Demonology tree is now a fun tree, with some very nice utility.

I'd like to suggest two minor tweaks to consider:
- Consider adding an ability to change model (appearance) of the pets. This request is mostly due to very ugly (and obnoxious) model of Succubus. The Succubus model, animation and textures are very outdated and - as a 30 years of male - I must say they are obnoxious & makes using the Succ for daily adventure a little bit ... weird?
 - Proposal could include a model change visible only to the Warlock - e.g., the other players could still recognice what pet you are using easily.
The list of model change options could include few general "demon" models, such as : demon hounds, felguards, wraiths, ghosts etc...

I'm sure with ability to actually be able to change the pet model from time to time it would make them being more used. For me it is personally hard to commit using Succ , due to above reasons.

Simillary, hunters are able to very easily find a pet that fits their's likings & aesthetics - they may choose from very wide portoflio of tameable pets, while Warlocks are forever stuck with ugly models from early classic.   unhappy_turtle

- Consider adding an ability that the summoned pet may benefit from on hit (landed or on-being-attacked) trinket effets. This could go well into last tier Demo tree talent Soul Link, as an additional benefit from actually deciding to use the pet.
Thank you and let me know your thougths , please!
