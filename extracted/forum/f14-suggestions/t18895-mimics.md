# Mimics

## Post 128592 by Midoriko — 2025-04-26T14:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128592#p128592 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This game has plenty of treasure chests in it.

I have seen T-WoW make some crazy additions.

There could be a 1% chance that any chest in the game is a mimic, created by the level (elite?) of the spawned chest node.

The Mimic does not have to trap the player in-place, but it could have a rather extended agro distance.

The Mimic (regular or elite) could drop the original, or different, or no loot table (buff?).

Elite or not, the Mimic could have a substantial health pool, or damage reduction buff lowering all sources to 1 to create the time-to-kill scenario desired.

The opportunity for creating an NPC with wildly unique spells and/or abilities, and the potential for a legendary named mimic is wide open.

Example random items:

Mimic Shield: The Top half of a treasure chest, spending most of its item points on random spell resistance.

Mimic Tooth Bracelet (Trinket): Grant the same ability from the Black Grasp (8 mana burn, 8 mana absorb), 1% vampirism, 1% spell hit.

Mimic Leather Boots: Zero armor, Zero stats, -8 Runspeed (the reverse of enchanting run speed), +20% chance to resist all charm, fear, stun, and movement impairing effects.
