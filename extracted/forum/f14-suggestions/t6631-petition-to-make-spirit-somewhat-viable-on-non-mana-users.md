## Post 41959 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-04-18T03:43:00Z

During leveling we can find spirit on every armor type: on plate, on leather, on mail, on cloth. Like it was meant as general-purpose stat like stamina. I prevously said that "stats should not be useful for everyone", but this particular was designed for everyone, so I thought once more.
Turtle devs decided to remove spirit because it is useless. Eventually it will lead to extinction of spirit and it is sad.

#savespirit

It restores health, but out of combat. It restores mana but when you don't cast spells. It looks like players was not supposed to cast spells so often. They also was not supposed to pull one pack of mobs after another like we do in raids. It looks like there should be more downtime and waiting in the game. Blizz decided to make the game more active and it is good. Players get no time during combat when they wait for regeneration.

Blizz invented food and drinks, which greatly reduce your downtime making spirit less useful outside of combat.
Blizz invented mp5 on the items, which works when you cast spells. Which looks like patch for classes without talent "regeneration during casting". They also added those talents.

Spirit on items with random stats automatically make item less useful. We value "of the Monkey"(sta+agi), but don't value "of the Wolf" (agi+spi). All your way to lvl 60 you meet spirit in multiple quest rewards, but when you reach 60 you play with spirit only if you are priest or resto druid. Sometimes it looks like two different games.

So to save spirit on items and keep it general-purpose stat, we should make it useful during combat.

I propose to keep regeneration properties and add following:
druid - 1 melee ap per point of spirit in Bear and Cat form. 1 spell damage in Owl form
hunter - 1 melee ap and 0.05% of parry per point of spirit
mage - no changes
paladin - 0.05% of parry per point of spirit for 60 lvl (20 for 1%)
priest - no changes
rogue - 1 melee ap and 0.05% of parry per point of spirit
shaman - 1 melee ap, 1 spell damage and 0.05% of chance to proc of enchants per point of spirit
warlock - no changes
warrior - 0.05% of parry per point of spirit for 60 lvl (20 for 1%)

And keep all current items intact. Changing its meaning for low level items does not have to be reflected by changing top tier items.
