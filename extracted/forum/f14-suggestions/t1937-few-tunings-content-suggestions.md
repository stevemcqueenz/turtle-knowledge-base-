# Few tunings/content suggestions !

## Post 11580 by Lindzei — 2021-08-09T14:27:00Z

Hey everybody !   turtle_tongue

I've recently joined the server and I'm having a blast right now playing on Turtle WoW. It's definitly not the same mood as Classic.
Anyway after playing a little bit I noticed a bunch of nice features and I'm really thankful for that.   turtle_in_love

I've started to think about what am I able to do to help the server to grow for the better !
Sadly I'm not an expert in Lore, I'm not good at roleplay, I don't have a lot of imagination so I have chosen to stay bound to what's I'm the best at and it's Pve tuning/class tuning.

I tried to keep in my mind that it had to stay in the Vanilla spirit and here is the result.
These are some raw ideas, some if not most of them are probably BAD and I know it. I wanted to share my thought about them.

- A lot of classes tuning can be done without altering the Vanilla aspect or without turning the server into a "Fun" server. I'll detail some of my thoughts by classes below. (Tuning in meme spec, mana cost of some spec)

- Some changes could be done on raids such as the removal "Debuff limitation" and the "Buff limitation". This point is purely PvE/Raid-wise but for example in PvE as a Rdrood you'll most likely unable to use your Rejuvenation and then you have to spam only Healing Touch which can be boring sometimes. The same problem occur if you want to play Affliction Warlocks and stuff.

- I think we are missing some currencies that could help people to log a little bit more daily. (Without EVER having "the need" to connect)
 - For example we could have a currency :
 - That drop on dungeons bosses
For killing a specific rare mob
For doing maybe daily quests ?
For doing specific ingame event (staff handmade event ?)

 - This currency could be able to buy things such as :
 - Transmogrifications items
Surprise box (with maybe ore, plants or cloth and some money) (Not sure it's a good idea)
Gear ?
Item to launch a World buff (Onyxia, Rend) (Not sure it's a good idea I don't value correctly the importance of these buffs, but since we have a kinda small population it could help for raid.)
Buying an item that launch an epic quest to unlock a big XX(number) slot bag
Buying specific food/water that give more regen

 - This currency should NEVER be able to buy things such as :
 - Important items to craft gear, we need people to connect to each other. I think people need the helps of other in Vanilla it's really important. **One must not be able to do everything on his own.**
Important items to skip the need to communicate with each other.

- Some QoL changes could be done such as (correct me if they are already ingame) :
 - Instant mail between alts
Having a "gold bank" where you can store a part of your gold and all of your alts can withdraw/deposit on it.
Guild banks
Cross-faction pvp

- More ingame event that require communication/coordination (such as the opening of the gate of Ahn'Qiraj, maybe not that big !)

- Daily/Weekly quests for PvP (i.e : Cap a flag in Warsong, Win 3-0 in a Warsong, stuff like this)

 **For class tuning here a quick thought of what can be done to have "more fun" in some classes without having to turn into a "Fun server". It works with the second point of my post on the removal of the Debuff limitation :**  happy_turtle_head

 - **Working on a possible haste stat ?**

 - Paladin :
 - Reduce the cost of mana of some ret/prot spell
 - Judgement, consecration, for a ret paladin, it cost a lot of mana and then potion/demonic rune to have a little bit of fun in Vanilla. Even for leveling it could be more fun actually.

 - Addition of a real taunt ?

 - Mage :
 - Some tuning on the arcane spec to make it viable (It's clearly a meme spec)
 - Buffing arcane missile ?

 - Reducing the mana cost of some of the arcane spells

 - Druid :
 - Reducing the mana cost of some boomie spells.

 - Priest :
 - Working on shadow priest (No clue on how to do it properly, shadow is extremly strong in PvP)
 - Give a spell such as "Vampiric Embrace" from TBC but that give mana back instead.

 - Working on a viable disc spec
 - Reducing the mana cost/cd of Power Word : Shield

 - Working on a viable spec smite

 - Rogue :
 - Working on an assassination spec
I don't have enough knowledge on other classes to talk about them but with a some researches I could do a more detailed list.
Once again I'm definitly aware that a LOT of things in this post might be horribly bad in the game, I'm just giving suggestion from my point of view, but this is my view and most likely not the one of everyone else !
