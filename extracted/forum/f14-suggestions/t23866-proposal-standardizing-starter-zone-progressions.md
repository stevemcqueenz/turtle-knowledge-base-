# [Proposal] Standardizing Starter Zone Progressions

## Post 167483 by Peregris — 2026-02-05T04:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167483#p167483 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,
I’d like to suggest an adjustment to the initial quests across all starting zones.
While playing the Lunatic Challenge and traveling through territories of other races, I noticed the absence of several early quests that could significantly improve the overall experience of the challenge. Many of these quests feel narratively important but are currently gated in ways that break immersion or progression flow.
I believe that revisiting some quest hooks and organizing them into a clearer progression—where quests unlock strictly through narrative completion rather than character level requirements—would create a more coherent experience. Ideally, this would form a chain-based structure leading up to the final quest in each zone (often the delivery to the nearest inn).
________________________________________
Core Proposal: **Chain-Based Unlocks & Removal of Level Requirements**
Remove Level Requirements
All starter quests should be available at Level 1 (or have no level requirement at all), as long as their narrative prerequisites are met. This would allow players of any race—especially those traveling for challenge runs—to experience the full introductory storyline without needing to grind mobs just to unlock quest availability.
Narrative Flow
Basic tasks (such as vermin control or simple gathering) should naturally unlock more advanced threats (leaders, organized enemies, or invasions). This reinforces the feeling of earning the trust of the local NPCs.
________________________________________
After a brief review of the starting quests for each race, I’ve outlined below my suggested quest sequence for Northshire. I’ve separated the quests into tiers, where each tier only becomes available after all quests in the previous tier have been completed.
________________________________________
 **1. Northshire**
Currently, Brotherhood of Thieves unlocks simply by reaching Level 2. This disconnects the Defias threat from the player’s actual involvement in local problems. My proposal integrates both the custom content and a “trust-building” progression with Deputy Willem.
Gate “Brotherhood of Thieves”
Instead of a generic Level 2 requirement, this quest should only become available after completing both [Wolves Across the Border] and [Investigate Echo Ridge].
Deputy Willem should not entrust the investigation of an organized Defias gang to a recruit who hasn’t yet secured the abbey’s perimeter (wolves) or scouted the kobold mine.
Milly’s Integration
Unlock [Milly Osworth] immediately after completing [Wolves Across the Border].
Reason: Milly is standing right next to Eagan Peltskinner. It feels natural to help her after dealing with the wolves in her vineyard, rather than waiting for a level-up.
Quest Grouping
[Down in the Ridge] should be grouped with [Skirmish at Echo Ridge]. Both quests take place deep inside the mine and should be completed in a single run.
Final Gate
[The Report] (sending the player to Goldshire) should strictly require the death of Garrick Padfoot.
________________________________________
Proposed Quest Tiers
Tier 01
•	[A Threat Within]
Tier 02
•	[Kobold Camp Cleanup] → [Investigate Echo Ridge] → [Skirmish at Echo Ridge] → [Down in the Ridge]
•	[Eagan Peltskinner] → [Wolves Across the Border]
Tier 03
•	[Brotherhood of Thieves] → [Bounty on Garrick Padfoot]
•	[Milly Osworth] → [Milly’s Harvest] → [Grape Manifest]
Tier 04
•	[The Report]
•	[Rest and Relaxation]
________________________________________
 **2. Coldridge Valley**
For Dwarves and Gnomes, I propose a progression based on “Logistics leading into Combat.”
The current flow allows players to rush the Trolls too early or to miss the custom content entirely.
Logistics Chain
•	Make [Scalding Mornbrew Delivery] a direct follow-up to [Coldridge Valley Mail Delivery].
Narrative reason: The player starts as a courier—first delivering a letter, then a fragile and valuable cargo (the hot brew).
Geographic Discovery
•	Lock [A Refugee’s Quandary] (Felix) behind [Scalding Mornbrew Delivery].
Reason: The delivery naturally leads the player to the frozen lake, ensuring Felix is discovered organically rather than by random wandering.
Gating the Trolls
•	Make [The Troll Cave] available only after completing [Bring Back the Mug].
Narrative reason: The Mountaineers should only authorize a full assault after the player has completed their logistical duties and safely returned from the lake.
Resulting Loop
Anvil → Letter Delivery → Brew Delivery (Lake) → Discover Felix → Return the Mug → Assault Trolls → Kill Boss → Leave Zone.
Proposed Quest Tiers
Tier 01
•	[Dwarven Outfitters]
•	[A New Threat]
Tier 02
•	[Coldridge Valley Mail Delivery] → [Scalding Mornbrew Delivery]
•	[The Boar Hunter]
Tier 03
•	[Bring Back the Mug]
•	[A Refugee’s Quandary]
•	[The Troll Cave]
Tier 04
•	[The Stolen Journal] → [Senir’s Observations] → [Supplies to Tannok]
________________________________________
 **3. Shadowglen**
Players should prove their capability against corruption in the open forest before being trusted with locating a missing sentinel in a dangerous region.
The Cave “Mega-Phase”
Group [Webwood Venom], [Webwood Egg], the custom boss [Githyiss the Vile], and [Iverron’s Antidote] into a single unlock phase via [A Friend in Need].
This creates a cohesive, dungeon-like experience where the player clears the entire cave—spiders, eggs, venom, and boss—in one focused run to save Iverron.
Final Progression
Saving Iverron should unlock [Crown of the Earth], which leads directly into [Tenaron’s Summons], and finally [Dolanaar Delivery] as the zone’s exit.

 **4. Thalassian Highlands**
Log analysis suggests a clear three-act structure currently held together by level requirements. This proposal enforces a narrative of Survival → Investigation → Retribution.
Survival First
Magic threats should not be addressed while refugees are starving.
Gate all arcane-related quests behind [Safety for Refugees] and [Provisions for Refugees].
The Arcane Threat
•	[A Troubling Presence] acts as the bridge quest.
•	Completing it unlocks [Arcane Golem Revolt] and [Maddening Hunger].
The Highlands Climax
•	[A Score To Settle] should be the final confrontation.
•	It must require completion of [The Highland Menace] and [Grapes in the Hills].

 **5. Valley of Trials**
The current flow allows players to pick up nearly every quest at once, resulting in a chaotic “shopping list” experience.
This proposal establishes a clear threat hierarchy: Beasts → Peons → Demons.
Threat Escalation
•	[Vile Familiars] should unlock only after completing [Cutting Teeth], [Sting of the Scorpid], and [Sarkoth].
•	This represents the transition from grunt to overseer.
Unified Cave Phase
Group [Burning Blade Medallion], [Thazz’ril’s Pick], and the custom [Stalking the Felstalkers] into a single dungeon-lite phase unlocked after [Vile Familiars].

The Tauren starting experience should clearly separate Initiation from Warfare.
The Dividing Line
Require completion of both:
•	[The Hunt Continues] (physical proof)
•	[Rites of the Earthmother] (spiritual proof)
Only then should the war phase unlock.
War Phase

Upper Management Gate

Logistics Dependency

 **8. Deathknell**
Deathknell’s narrative is about establishing order from chaos.
This proposal enforces a Security Perimeter logic: Village → Wilderness → Military Threat.
Internal Cleanup First
Gate exploration and gathering behind:

•	[Scavenging Deathknell]

Wilderness Cleansing

Conclusion
This proposal is not about adding new content, increasing difficulty, or restricting player freedom. Its goal is to replace artificial level-based gates with narrative-driven progression, allowing each starting zone to tell its full story in a natural and immersive way.
By organizing starter quests into clear, chain-based tiers, players earn trust through actions rather than levels, narrative pacing improves, major threats are introduced at the right moment, and custom content is experienced more consistently—especially by players following alternative progression paths or challenge-based playstyles.
Each zone already contains the building blocks for a strong introductory narrative. This proposal simply reorders existing hooks, aligns geography with discovery, and ensures that the final transition quests truly feel earned.
Thank you for taking the time to read and consider this proposal.
