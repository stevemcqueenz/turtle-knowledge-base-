# Exit Strategy - no debuff!

## Post 90562 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-03-18T16:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90562#p90562 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Exactly what the topic says. Goblins currently lack any real reason to be played other than comedic value. Their profession bonuses aren’t helpful in the long term and their weapon specialties aren’t for meta weapons.Exit Strategy could be good, but it’s a single buff that gives one immediate debuff (silence) followed by another debuff (slow). So 1 buff for 2 debuffs. The math there isn’t in goblins’ favor.

Goblins have been crafted as essentially parallel to gnomes. Exit Strategy should theoretically work similarly. That is, gnomes have Escape Artist while goblins have Exit Strategy. Escape Artist is a great ability, generally considered top tier. Exit Strategy could also be good and should strive to be on that level.

Removing Exit Strategy’s debuffs will help give goblins some footing. It’s not a huge speed boost; 30% compared to 70% for sprint shows that, and goblins can still be affected by slows and snares while employing the racial. I’d still consider it inferior to Escape Artist even if it had no debuffs, so please get rid of the debuffs on Exit Strategy. Give goblins some love!
