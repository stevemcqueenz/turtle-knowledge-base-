# Two things that might help Tel'Abim

## Post 85312 by Glaive (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-19T10:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85312#p85312 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings,

I wanted to start off by saying how much fun I've had playing Turtle wow, and how much I appreciate the work that the developers do to make it great.  Overall, well done!

I was very excited to hear about the launch of Tel'Abim.  There two main things about Nordanaar that I didn't like:  One was that the hardcore players were mixed in with non-hardcore players, and the other was that pvp players were mixed in with non-pvp players.  Tel'Abim fixed both of these issues for me.  As a rusult, Tel'Abim is where I want to play.  I have no use for my characters on Nordanaar any longer.  That's a fact.  I'm sure I am not the only person who feels this way, and so It troubles me to see that Tel'Abim isn't doing so well.  (Although, I do get the sense it's doing better in the last week or so.)

Here are two things that I would like to see done, that would make me a happier Tel'Abim player, and possibly entice other people to start playing on Tel'Abim.  If one or both of these ideas have been put forth by others on the forums, forgive the redundancy.

First, I would like to see there be a permanent XP boost of 30% for Tel'Abim.  Nordanaar has Warmode which grants a 30% xp buff for choosing PvP.  When we role on Tel'Abim, we automatically choose to play with pvp enabled; we choose warmode, in effect.  I don't see why we wouldn't also enjoy the 30% xp buff.  I've seen a number of people in world chat (who I assume were new to the server) asking how to activate Warmode, and then complaining about this when they learn the XP buff is not available.  It seems like an easy fix to make the servers equally attractive from a leveling speed perspective.

The second thing is the shop donation rewards.  I spent quite a bit of money on shop rewards that I used on Nordanaar before Tel'Abim was ever even mentioned.  Had I known Tel'Abim was coming I certainly would have waited to use those rewards.   I know that when a hardcore character on Nordanaar dies, all the shop rewards spent on that character are refunded.  I dearly wish something could be implemented that allowed me to recoup some or all of the rewards that I used on Nordanaar and spend them on Tel'Abim.  Perhaps, upon deletion of a character, the rewards spent could be refunded as with the HC characters?  It may be thought that people who spent money on Nordanaar shop items will make additional donations in order to buy rewards on Tel'Abim, but that is not so, at least in my case.  I can't justify spending that money again, therefore, I am just less happy with my Tel'Abim experience than I could be.  If I were able to delete my Nordanaar characters and move my whole Turtle wow investment to Tel'Abim I would be very excited to do so.

Thanks for reading and keep up the great work!
~Glaive
