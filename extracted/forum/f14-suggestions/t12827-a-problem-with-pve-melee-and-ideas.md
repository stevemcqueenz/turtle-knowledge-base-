# A problem with PVE Melee and Ideas

## Post 85554 by Icedcream (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-22T06:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85554#p85554 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I believe when blizzard made vanilla wow oh so long ago they did not anticipate the amount of min/maxing that would happened in years to come. I cannot imagine they added a mechanic of weapon skill and glancing blows while also fulling considering the impact of the racial + weapon skill. For those unaware Lvl 62 and 63+ (boss) have a 20% and 35% damage reduction from glancing blows. this means 8+ weapon skill is a massive staff. Humans and orcs are the only classes with these stats as a passive. It is not healthy for the game for every other player to require a world drop +300g Edgemasters or one of a few weapons that have +skill on them.  This also means people serious about PVE go the path of picking a human or orc. Generally it also makes it hard to balance the game when a racial is 20-35%+ damage. You may see fury warriors in BWL passing 650 dps on a boss fight. Lets not get into stacking -armor...

It is evidence that Wow regretted this mechanic as within 2 expansions it was removed from the game.

Suggestions:
 1) Boring and easy. Give each race 2 weapon passives that cover the equipment limitations of those melee classes

 2) Create an epic quest chain for various weapons mastery trickets or rings

Example RANK 1: 1) Part challenge 3 elite weapon masters scattered around the world that are (sword, dagger, axe, mace, spear, fists --- depends on which master chain you are doing) part 2) collect the flame of rag (quest item 100% drop if on quest), dark iron, other cool stuff 3) Forge rank 1 tricket . Stats +4 "mace skills", 1% hit, 24 AP

Example RANK 2: 1) Train with the best weapon master in the world. Complete 2 quests for him. 2) challenge him to a final duel. 3) Collect something from BWL and return to him to reforge your tricket.
Rank 2 tricket . Stats +8 "mace skills", 2+ hit, 48 AP

There are two models of itemisation in my opinion:
 1. single item/racial with huge amount of +skill (like edgemasters handguards)
 2. small amount of +skill on wide variety of items (like +1 skill on single item, but present more often than now).

Assuming that +skill is not very useful in PvP (because no glancing blows), items with +skill will be considered as PvE gear in most cases.
Current model looks like you need to equip single item, so all other items can be used both in PvP and PvE.

I'd love and dislike the idea of changing racials at the same time.
From one hand, it would refresh the game in a way and would bring much desired balance changes. But many people spent thousands of hours in this gamne with the idea of min-maxing. With a racial balance change we could destroy everything which these people have built during countless hours. Let's say you started an orc to be a top performer fury, spent 1000+ hours with the game and one day found out that despite your min-maxing, gnome (f.ex.) is the best fury DPS from this day.
I'd totally agree to these changes on a fresh server.
I think that some balance changes can work though if they don't change race ranking for classes to 'protect' min-maxers. At least the top of the ranking should stay the same.
F.ex. I could see dwarves with a lesser axe proficiency, taurens with some mace knowledge etc. Main thing is that orc and human should stay the top as fury (to save min-maxers) but the gap could be much smaller.
Additions like the Kara sword are nice and point towards this direction but meddling with racials is also a way to do this (an interesting one).
