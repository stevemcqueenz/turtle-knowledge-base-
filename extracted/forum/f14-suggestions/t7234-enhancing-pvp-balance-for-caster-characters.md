# Enhancing PvP Balance for Caster Characters

## Post 45822 by Desprit — 2023-05-14T11:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45822#p45822 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle Wow Developers,

I am writing to propose a potential solution to an issue that has been affecting the player-versus-player (PvP) experience for caster characters in World of Warcraft. As a dedicated player and fan of the game, I believe that this suggestion could significantly improve the overall enjoyment and fairness of PvP encounters for players of all levels.

The Problem:

Currently, caster characters face a significant disadvantage when engaged in PvP combat with higher level opponents. Due to the substantial resist chance against higher level attacks, low-level casters have virtually no opportunity to defend themselves or retaliate effectively. This imbalance not only discourages players from participating in PvP activities but also detracts from the immersive and competitive nature of the game.

Proposed Solution:

To address this issue, I propose the implementation of a maximum resist chance cap for PvP encounters, specifically for caster characters. This cap could be set at a reasonable percentage, such as 10% or 15%, to ensure that low-level players have a fighting chance against higher level opponents. It is important to note that this change would only apply to PvP situations and would not impact the player-versus-environment (PvE) aspect of the game.

Benefits:

By introducing a maximum resist chance cap for caster characters in PvP, the following benefits can be achieved:

 1. Improved balance and fairness in PvP encounters, allowing players of all levels to enjoy competitive gameplay.
 2. Increased participation in PvP activities, as low-level caster characters will no longer feel discouraged by the current imbalance.
 3. Enhanced overall gaming experience for World of Warcraft players, as they can engage in more diverse and challenging PvP encounters.

In conclusion, I believe that implementing a maximum resist chance cap for caster characters in PvP encounters would significantly improve the overall experience for World of Warcraft players. This change would promote fairness, encourage participation in PvP activities, and enhance the competitive nature of the game. I hope you will consider this proposal and its potential benefits for the World of Warcraft community.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
