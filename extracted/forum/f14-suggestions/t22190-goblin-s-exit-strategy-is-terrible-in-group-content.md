# Goblin's Exit Strategy is terrible in group content!

## Post 153981 by Mypingishuge — 2025-10-02T03:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153981#p153981 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello! I've been playing Warrior (tanking) as Goblin and I've noticed that it's active ability (Exit Strategy) feels awful in any group content, compared to other racial abilities.

Interesting how Undeads have two actives (cleanse and eat corpse), one good for party play and another for solo. Why can't Goblins get something for group content as well?

Maybe Goblins need a new one as well, to complement the solo uses of Exit Strategy, as it's pretty okay when questing on your own.

Especially when tanking dungeons and raids, it's like you have no race at all. No wonder it's the least played race in the game.

When compared, you can see how bad it is:
Taurens, HUGE aoe stun, on top of already good hp bonus.
Trolls and Orcs, HUGE dps boost, helps with threat.
Undead, HUGE cleanse, saves the party from wipes when you're tanking or healing.

So, right now, playing Goblin is just playing a worse character, when doing dungeons and raids. Please, give us something to make it good. :)
