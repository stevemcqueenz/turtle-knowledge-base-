# Making enhancement shaman usable

## Post 67087 by Jstansberry (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-27T07:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67087#p67087 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This post will focus on pve but consideration has been taken for pvp balance. I've also tangled with how to not disrupt elemental too much, as elemental is actually in a pretty decent spot in pve on this server and has always been good in pvp.

For those of you who are unfamiliar - perhaps you've never even seen one in a raid, enhancement is by far the lowest performing melee dps spec. Enhancement is also middling to bad in pvp, being easily kited and offering no more burst damage than comparable burst-oriented melee specs such as rogues, arms warriors, or shockadins.

Currently, enhancement shamans are the worst melee dps by quite a large margin in pve while being middling to bad in pvp. There are many reasons for this - poor itemization, poor scaling, too much threat generation, and especially unfavorable placement of talents in the talent trees. Another reason - exclusive to pvp - would be how easy it is to kite an enhancement shaman, but I feel like that kind of weakness is almost part of the identity of a spec and should be preserved for the sake of allowing it's strengths to shine.

For enhancement to be close to competitive in DPS, they have to be:
- Stormstriking and Earthshocking on cooldown. (this currently can't usually happen as Earth Shock generates extra threat, in conjunction with "lucky" WF crits you will risk a good chance of taking agro from the tank and being laughed at by the entire raid)
- They have to be critting very often to keep flurry up.
- Winning in the casino (WF proccing semi-consistently)
Itemization and scaling are actually interconnected in a way, because just like ret/prot paladins, we want almost every stat. Agility is what enhancement shamans end up stacking the most, as crit is especially beneficial to enhancement due to flurry and due to how efficiently %crit scales with WF. Because WF attacks give a lot of bonus AP, you'll prefer to increase your chance to double the damage on those attacks by getting more crit from agility than take the "safe" AP from strength. This does not mean that enhancement does not want AP, but it does mean they end up having very little AP because they have to choose between agility leather/mail meant for hunters and rogues or the rare "enhancement" pieces sprinkled sparsely throughout endgame content that have (a relatively small amount of) strength and spellpower - most of those pieces being locked behind r13 and exalted rep PvP grinds that are not realistic goals for most players. Giving shamans 1 AP per agility would help a lot in regards to fixing both the itemization and scaling issues that shamans face without having to add a lot of specialized gear to the game.

The other side of the scaling issue is that the biggest benefits an enhancement shaman brings to a raid do not scale with gear, and the enhancement shaman doesn't get to benefit from them, themselves. Totems and Bloodlust do not get any better if the enhancement shaman gets better items. The talents in enhancement that make buffing totems stronger are such marginal increases that the healing from resto or better dps from ele are much more valuable in comparison, so there is no actual reason to bring an enhancement shaman to a raid other than to be a Bloodlust bot. Some shamans spec into enhancement simply for Bloodlust and raid heal as a "restoration" shaman.

The enhancement shaman does not get any stronger when Bloodlust is used on their target, and Bloodlust does not getting any stronger if the enhancement shaman has good enhancement gear. WF totem does not get any stronger with good gear and the shaman does not benefit from it themselves as it does not stack with their own Windfury weapon buff. But what if they did benefit the shaman? What if WF totem buff stacked with the WF weapon buff and an enhancement shaman could roll to proc either or both on each attack, meaning that if both proc on one swing that would be 4 attacks at once? What if casting Bloodlust gave both the shaman and their target the buff? I don't believe adding a small chance for 4x attack instead of 3x attacks would make much of a difference at all in pvp as grounding totem is used almost on cooldown against the squishier casters who have less physical mitigation and this is contingent on WF totem being out. This change would also require changing the WF totem buff from being treated as a weapon buff like poisons, sharpening stones, and shaman weapon buff spells to being an aura or other kind of buff - meaning this would be changing the behavior where WF totem buff sticks around for ~9 seconds after the totem disappears. This means totem twisting WF totem would no longer be possible.

In regards to talents, the %hit rating talent being in restoration is actually incredibly limiting to enhancement's potential dps in pve. Currently, [this](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/shaman/W31-U50CN2JV-U03) is generally what a PvE enhancement spec looks like. Totemic Mastery from restoration is sometimes required and even more has to be sacrificed in those cases, meaning the enhancement shaman [gets much less](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/shaman/U3-U50CN2JV-U0F1) from the elemental tree.

If it could look more like [this](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/shaman/X31U0I6-U50CN1JU), enhancement could do a decent amount more damage in pve. Elemental Devastation and Elemental Fury open up enhancement to be more than just an auto attack bot fishing for WF procs by giving much more value to shocks and allowing the spell crit that was clumsily thrown on "enhancement" pieces such as tier 2.5 and r13 to actually be utilized.

This would require moving up Elemental Fury one row in the elemental tree, or else the shaman would have to sacrifice Bloodlust - which I think was not a good addition in it's current implementation, anyway. Bloodlust and totems do not get any better with gear and are currently the only real boons an enhancement shaman brings to the raid - why give the enhancement shaman gear when the only things they are good for do not scale with gear at all? Either replace Bloodlust with something else or rework it - make it apply to both the shaman and their target, for example. The fact that this was implemented as a single target buff has me suspecting that someone on the dev team just hates enhancement as a spec...

The %hit rating talent is absolutely necessary for enhancement as your stats are spread so thin that having to get 9% sacrifices way too much, even just getting 6% hit rating if you're an orc with an axe feels extremely detrimental. This talent should be moved to enhancement in the second tier, replacing Guardian Totems which could be moved to restoration. This could be seen as a nerf to elemental as they often go deep into restoration for Tidal Mastery and NS and would then be missing out on the 3% hit, even though reaching a "hit cap" isn't as important for casters as it is for melee, this could still end up being a big nerf to elemental in pve and so as an alternative it could be placed somewhat deep into the elemental tree. Merging it with the Elemental Devastation talent that would usually be skipped by elemental shamans but is greatly beneficial to enhancement shamans would have very little impact to elemental while opening up enhancement to much better DPS talents.

Many talents in the enhancement tree itself are very, very bad. Enhancing Totems, Guardian Totems, Improved Lightning Shield, and Thunderhead are simply awful. With the rank 5 Strength of Earth and Grace of Air totems from the AQ20 books, Enhancing Totems gives you 11 more agility or strength. Guardian Totems needs no explanation, the only redeeming quality of it is the reduced grounding totem cooldown which can be useful in pvp. Improved Lightning Shield is useless in pve for obvious reasons and is obsolete in pvp where you will be using Earth Shield for that extra bit of survivability 100% of the time over LIghtning Shield. Thunderhead is a joke of a talent - sure, maybe it could be used to give a tank just a tiny bit more threat but it'd be a miniscule amount and would never be worth 4 talent points to receive such a small and boring benefit. Even Improved Weapon Totems, which seems good at a glance because it makes WF totem better, is actually quite bad. Essentially, when WF totem procs for a party member the extra Windfury attack will have 94.5 more bonus AP than it would if you didn't have the talent. That is going to end up being less than a 1% increase in dps overall for each party member benefiting from WF.

Threat generation is a huge issue for enhancement. If crossfaction raiding weren't a thing on this server, enhancement would not be capable of joining raids due to how important Blessing of Salvation currently is to an enhancement shaman. Extra threat generation effects should be removed from Earth Shock and Rockbiter weapon as a baseline. Rockbiter's extra threat gain should be replaced with armor penetration. Earth Shock's extra threat gain should simply be removed and doesn't necessarily need to be replaced by anything - a high damage ranged interrupt is already a good button to press. Tank shaman will never happen without sacrificing almost all of Enhancement's current tree for the sake of making it viable. Shamans have no taunt, no aoe threat generation, no real damage mitigation, and no itemization for tanking. The entire tree would have to be retooled to allow for shaman tanking so let's just rip off the bandaid and get rid of these half-baked tank mechanics for the sake of making enhancement dps more viable. Stormstrike increases the next 2 instances of nature damage by 20%, 2 also being the same amount of Earth Shocks you could do within the Stormstrike debuff duration is no coincidence. The fact that Earth Shock causes extra threat makes it nearly unusable in raids, unfortunately. An earth shock crit could mean death for you, all it takes is a recent or soon-to-be WF proc or two. Hell, you don't even need to be critting on the Earth Shocks or WF procs sometimes.
A threat reduction talent is absolutely necessary even if the extra threat generation is removed from Earth Shock. 30% threat reduction, the same value that fire/frost mages get, would likely be necessary to allow enhancement shamans to do their basic rotation at ~270 dps without getting themselves killed by threat burst.

Mana would also be an issue without crossfaction raiding, Blessing of Wisdom and Judgement of Wisdom on their own solve this issue, however.

I haven't leveled an enhancement shammy in turtle wow myself, but I have talked to and read comments from those who have. The solo leveling experience sounds rough, at best. Part of this stems from the uneven nature of windfury and the poor itemization of vanilla gear. Enhancement, just like Retribution, were in a state of 'severely not finished' at WoW's launch and they didn't really come together until TBC at the earliest. In fact, Shaman were literally Paladin clones except they used Nature instead of Holy earlier in development. Druids, likewise, were also unfinished/didn't have clear design goals, but luckily the twow devs have worked to address their issues and are more fun to play as a result.

There are many ways that the team could go about trying to even out enhancement's leveling experience and increase the playability and fun factor, but what would be the most impactful? Without increasing the potential multiple windfury crit that one-shots a warrior in bgs or pulls aggro so hard the boss cleaves the squishy members of the party.

The lack of AP from Agility is a fairly easy fix and would help the most at higher levels. Having easier access to overall hit chance would be incredibly useful until they start getting late game gear, moving/adding such a talent is not without precedent as is the case with the protection tree of paladins. I have also seen players asking for another attack skill, such as Lava Lash, or to make Stormstrike an attack instead of a buff effect, partially just to reduce their reliance on auto attacks. If one or both attacks were available, to reduce windfury burst the devs could simply exclude those attacks from benefiting from windfury. Not having some insane Stormstrike triple windfury proc crit of doom might be less exciting to some, but it would be more interesting than just waiting between Shocks or placing totems.

I have also seen some players discuss wanting dualwield as a way of either increasing overall dps or for the cool factor, which is a huge can of worms from a developer stand. The devs would have to go through the entire item database and make sure that spellcaster one-handers are mainhand only to prevent equipping double caster daggers and enabling Shocks or Chain Lightning crits that make Mages envious/furious. I believe Blizzard had to do these itemization changes during TBC which took no small amount of effort.

For Shammy tanking, one Shammy I talked to wanted to see their totems be more involved in the process. Like have the Stoneclaw? totem give them additional aggro or a single shot instant aoe taunt while under the effects of a talent, new buff ability, or Rockbiter. Another topic was on damage mitigation, which sadly I didn't catch due to a client crash and by the time I logged back in after queue the topic had moved onto gnomes, but is a rather important part of tanking.
