# Something to revamp PVP

## Post 164722 by Oldbrok — 2026-01-09T05:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164722#p164722 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello There,

I want to apologize in advance for any grammatical errors as I type how I talk etc etc.

While yes there is some pvp usually in some areas by either a high level ganking a low level, or group on group/solo etc.  There seems to be a major lack in PvP on the PvP Server Tel'Abrim, I do not really see any BG's, no ones queuing or advertising any type of battle grounds and from what I have gathered queuing is like a day long thing to *"maybe"* see one or two partial battlegrounds.

I have a proposition to consider to try and reignite the want/desire for PvP on the PvP server Tel'Abrim as well as any others.  Perhaps instead of it being a random rotation, make a holiday month out of it, for instance

 **SUMMER OF BLOOD**

Make it start in end of May or start of June and last for the Summer, for the duration of that time all rep/honor gains specifically from battlegrounds are increased by double or more.  Possibly revamp/rework some of the gear specifically aquired from this event.  Make them varying tiers with their own special token with weak/low effort versions on par with max level dungeon blues, and the more difficult to aquire rep/rank ones progressing to be on par with Naxx level gear.

I feel that this even would allow the community to join in and have an alternative way to gain gear rather then raid logging, but also allow those who love to pvp just because to be able to queue for battlegrounds again.  So far the raiding community is awesome, but I feel a little love to the PvP side would be welcomed as well! As this usually helps keep the chads in queue saving the poor lowbies in STV from straight up camp type behavior.

 ***Please note that I am open to any conversation or criticism but I do truly think this idea or similar will help the community grow even more!***

Sincerely,
