# Sensible Quality of Life Ideas

## Post 96339 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-04T16:04:00Z

Hey guys. Smerf here. Thought I'd share some GREAT ideas to make the game easier/more accessible. I'm sure you'll probably like them. Let's get started with our little list.

First off- summoning stones. Now, hold your applause, and let me speak first. Before I talk about summoning stones, why don't we bring up where they come from? Meeting stones, of course! Remember those? The fuck you do! They're pointless! I say we capitalize and make it into something greater by letting a party member summon their buddies to a dungeon or raid to speed things along.

Speaking of instances, I'm sure a little boost for going through those would be fun. Not the boost you're thinking though! Those are bad. What if whatever lore characters or forces associated to a dungeon or raid was at the beginning of the instance, and gave out buffs and consumes to everyone? Just imagine all the time and farming once wasted now able to be spent pumping like we're all here to do!

Flight paths are next, and I think while this is one of the more minor changes, it would really help the levellers! Personally, I would put a flight path in every quest hub/town, and give access to some local spots starting out, like ratchet and crossroads for the horde side.

Paladins for undead and shamans for dwarves. Bring us balance! Oh, but nerf paladins first.

Speaking of shamans, enhancement should get dual wield for a nice DPS buff!

Anyways, I think that's it. Let me know what you think down below. :D
