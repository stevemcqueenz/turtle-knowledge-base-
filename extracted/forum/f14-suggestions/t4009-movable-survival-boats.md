# Movable Survival Boats

## Post 24211 by Zulnam (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-02T07:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24211#p24211 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently Survival boats are static items that the player cannot move. This is an oversight in my opinion as the boats have massive roleplay potential. It would be incredible if people could actually move them, and others.

I know the boat has a 50+ for fishing, perhaps to avoid anything being too "overpowered", a new boat could be added for this, at skill-up 100.

 **Rowing Boat**
 *Allows bearer to traverse waters and carry others as well.*

Boat doesn't need any speed increase, and (depending on how difficult it is to do) could have a limit of how many people are on it, blocking it from moving.

Please consider this for the roleplayers.
