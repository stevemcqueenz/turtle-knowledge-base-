# Paladin end talent point changes

## Post 28556 by Rof — 2022-11-06T06:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28556#p28556 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First let me say, I know theres another post with a whole bunch of changes some tame, most wild, what I suggest here isn't as out there as some of the changes they suggest.

First why we need the changes, neither of the prot or ret end talents are taken as of right now, repentance has some use in pvp, and ardent defender isn't useful if you can't actually get hit because you don't have threat.

I suggest this for prot

Ardent Defender is moved to Row 6 (same row as one hand spec)
now five ranks
When you have less than 30/35/40/45/50% health, all damage taken is reduced by 25%

new final talent
Divine Exorcism
The targeting requirements of Exorcism and Holy Wrath are now removed
(requires 5 ranks in Ardent Defender)

these 2 changes should mean that prot paladins are no longer mostly ret and should help with the stat priority to be more clear for prot paladins, instead of trying to get almost every stat under the sun, with this change prot will be more in line with items like Core Forged Helm that stack stam, int, def, and sp

This next suggestion will be a bit more controversial

Repentance is now moved to Row 6 (same as vengenace)

new final talent
Holy Strike as it is right now

removed Holy strike as baseline

these changes look odd at first since its basically a nerf for prot, but its intended to stop prots from taking slow dps one handers from dps in raids and actually using sp one handers, and without a full overhaul of how holy strike works it will always favor slower weapons and who wants an end talent and favor slower weps? thats right ret

now unlike most of the suggestions for paladin these are not strictly buffs, exorcism adds a whole lot of mana issues to the already starved prot paladin, and the change to ret does nothing to them aside of putting one more talent in ret, it mostly nerfs prot

These changes arent meant to be buffs but more changes to align how the class is intended to be played evidenced by the addition of items like core forged helm and crown of divine justice
