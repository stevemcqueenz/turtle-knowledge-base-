# Request: Give Quel'Dorei Meditation more mana generation while out of combat

## Post 161121 by Familymaker — 2025-11-30T19:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161121#p161121 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So while I love Quel'Dorei meditation, I found that while doing quests the racial felt a little bit underwhelming. Heck, according to the official mage FAQ, the high elves (who are supposed to be top tier spellcasters) are B rank for mages.

As such, why not make it so that if you use Quel'Dorei meditation while you are out of combat, that the mana generated will be increased by 100% or even 150%? So instead of 10% of your total mana, make it 25% of your total mana out of combat? For when you are farming mobs in PVE, or doing escorts, and you have little time to drink to regain your mana.
