# Suggestion: Forest Song Quest Chain Should Unlock a Flight Path Instead of a Pet Toy

## Post 142503 by Ashb0rn — 2025-08-01T13:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142503#p142503 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey fellow Turtle WoW players,

I want to highlight a beautifully crafted custom quest chain that starts in Ashenvale at Forest Song, and suggest a change that would make it far more immersive and rewarding — both in terms of world-building and gameplay.

 **Quest Chain Overview**
This quest line is given by the Hippogryph Trainer at Forest Song:

- [Treats for Training](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40798)

- [Words of Wisdom](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40799)

- [The Family Name](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40800)
It tells a heartfelt story of raising and bonding with a hippogryph — a creature closely tied to kaldorei heritage.
 **The current reward?** A hippogryph pet toy.

 **Better Reward:** Unlock a Forest Song Flight Path
Instead of a toy pet, this quest chain should unlock a new flight path at Forest Song.

 **Why This Makes Sense**

- Lore-Consistent
You’ve just helped reintroduce hippogryphs to the region — the perfect opportunity to establish a new aerial connection for the night elves and the Alliance.

- Meaningful World Impact
A flight path isn’t just a cosmetic item — it represents a real, visible change in the world thanks to the player’s actions.

- Huge QoL Upgrade
A flight path in eastern Ashenvale would be a huge quality-of-life improvement, especially for Alliance players traveling between Azshara, Satyrnaar, and the rest of Kalimdor.
Currently, that entire corner of the map lacks fast travel — making Forest Song underused despite its potential.
![💬](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4ac.svg) What Do You Think?
Wouldn’t it be more rewarding and immersive if this quest chain made a lasting difference to the world — something you and other players benefit from long-term?

Let’s discuss! If enough people support it, maybe this change could be considered in a future patch.

Fly safe, champions of Turtle WoW. ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)
