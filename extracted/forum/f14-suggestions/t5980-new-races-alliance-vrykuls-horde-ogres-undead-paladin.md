# New Races: Alliance Vrykuls, Horde Ogres ( + undead paladin)

## Post 37539 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-13T12:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37539#p37539 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ogres; it was discussed and agreed that it is the best option for the Horde. But most weren't sure about what should the Alliance have. I am; Vrykuls, something that I'd love to see and it'd make perfect sense.

I believe Vrykuls should be implemented as an Alliance race, perhaps there could be an island north of Lordaeron as their starting zone, making it as if it was an island near Northrend.
They would be the perfect candidate for the Alliance, distinct enough from the humans, kind of like high and night elves. It could also serve as a foreshadowing of Turtle version of Northrend added to Turtle WoW in the more distant future. Vrykuls would kind of be like a statement that yes Northrend is part of Azeroth there is this mysterious undiscovered land.

They could of course be Shamans. That would mean that the Horde should have a race that can be a paladin, well they already have a race that had paladins and priests.. while alive, but in undeath that race could still be holy priests but for not paladins.. solely because of the idea of faction only paladins/shamans, at the expense of the lore.

So there could be Horde Undead Paladins and Alliance Vrykul Shamans.
And Horde Ogres.
