# Hunter's mana problem

## Post 32544 by Maesus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-01-13T19:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32544#p32544 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm a Hunter newbie, my current one is only at level 45 and I have never had a Hunter anywhere above 20 before, so I don't know how necessary this change would be, but...

Hunters and Priests are, out of all the mana using classes, the only ones without a mana regenerating spell. I don't play a Priest, so I won't focus on them and TWoW addressed Priest's mana issues already, anyway.

In dungeons, mana is not much of an issue as other people can buff your mana regeneration for you and dungeons are also a good place to use consumables. And while loads of Hunter's dps comes from normal auto attacks, Hunters are also the only class who have to regularly pay money to use the vast majority of their abilities, not to mention they get less bag space to carry consumables. So I think it would be only fair to give them a boost.

I have two ideas to improve on Hunter's mana issues:
 1) Rework the practically useless and unused **Aspect of the Beast** into something more akin to **Aspect of the Viper**, sacrifising damage to slowly regenerate mana. It might even be put on a longer cooldown than other aspects.

 2) Rework **Viper Sting** to return a small % of drained mana to the Hunter. (I saw people suggesting the same about Mana Burn for Priests)
____________________________________________
Thoughts from more experienced Hunters?

## Post 32559 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-01-14T09:04:00Z

Don't use serpent sting & arcane shot would be my go to answer

I'm never a fan of giving classes "limitless" mana of sorts. mana should be a resource that is finite and needs to be managed.
Viper sting as manareturn seems not only impractical but also problematic for pvp. You need to get a lot of mana back to pay for the application of viper in the first place, and getting a bit of mana back makes you able to spam viper for much longer in pvp. Which is already plenty annoying.

Hunter doesn't actually have high manacosts for how much damage his spells deal (outside of the two mentioned above) so drinking every couple packs isn't a big deal. And well in raids you shouldn't run out of mana with the normal mana class consumables all to often. Or if you do, a FD + drinking macro works too
