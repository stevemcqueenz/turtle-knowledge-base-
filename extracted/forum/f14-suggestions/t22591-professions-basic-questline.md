# Professions - basic questline

## Post 157194 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-26T16:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157194#p157194 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Give every profession its own themed questline for learning each rank.

Apprentice (1–75) could start with a simple delivery quest.
Journeyman (75–150) could have you gather materials from low-level zones.
Expert (150–225) might send players into dungeons to find rare components.
Artisan (225–300) could involve tracking down masters across the world to learn unique recipes.

This would make progression feel organic and guided instead of confusing. Right now, finding the right trainers and ranks is a mess—we constantly have to ask around or search online just to locate them.

Cheers,
