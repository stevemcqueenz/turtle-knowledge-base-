# Creepy Crawlie Spider Mount

## Post 18631 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-05-14T23:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18631#p18631 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is by no means a "next version" sort of idea.  This is a "hopefully someday" sort of thing.

So, I noticed that the Donation Mounts are rather varied.  As in, a lot of them aren't just reskins of existing rideable creatures.  For example, there's a freaking *Jugem Cloud* as a mount!  That was surprising!  In any case, something I'd like to see implemented into a future version of *Turtle WoW* would be… a big, horrible [black widow](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=60493) spider mount.  Yep.  A spider.  Why a spider?  Well… I won't say specifically why, but suffice to say that while this wasn't my idea, it was interesting enough for me to want to suggest.  Like, imagine rolling into Orgrimmar and seeing, say, a Goblin just sitting there.  On a giant, spindly-legged spider.  Just… staring at you.  *Waugh…*

Cheers, Turtle Team!  Thanks for all your hard work!
