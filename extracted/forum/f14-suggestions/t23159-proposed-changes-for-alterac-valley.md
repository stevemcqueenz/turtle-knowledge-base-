# Proposed Changes for Alterac Valley

## Post 161815 by Randompriest — 2025-12-06T15:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161815#p161815 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

AV is one of the most painful experiences ever. It's not just that one faction loses every game (to the point that the other faction now begs in world chat for them to queue up so they can be farmed as honor pinata's) but that there's almost no way to play the BG at all - the optimal strategy today is just to lose.

Proposed Changes, Pick and Choose, any of these will improve the experience.

 - Crossfaction
 - Remove all XP gain and non-instance item drops.
 - Remove mining veins and herb spawns. (Watching half the raid peel off from objectives to just herb for the next 20 minutes is brutal)
 - Don't allow players under 60 to queue into the instance at all. (Having level 51's off in a corner dying to rams ruins the experience for people who actually want to play)

Thanks for reading.   scared_turtle_head
