# suggestions for Thousand Needles Races

## Post 99161 by Jack40melb — 2024-07-11T08:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99161#p99161 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really enjoy the race and try do it on my low levels to get the googles and mount trinklets. But there are some issues that can be improved to make it more open  and fair access to all players.

The issues are
- The race is limited to only 2 players - results in it be camped by farmers

- 1 player can cancel the race within the first few seconds which results in the race being cancelled for the other player and dismounting them. (Some players do this intentionally when they want to race with their friends/guildies OR race with an alt only. They have macroed the quest cancel/accept so they can do it fast). I have seen them repeatedly do this to stop others from racing just so they can do it with their friend or alt.

- The Goblin/Gnome rewards are attainable without completing the race SO long as one player completes it. This results in farmers (level 5-6)camping one racing spot, denying someone else the opportinity to get the trinklets. Along with the above issues, it leads to the race being controlled (at certain times) by a small group of players.

- The race starts as soon as 2nd player accepts a faction they will race for. which makes the 1st player unaware of when the race will start.

- The timer from Daisy Quest starts as soon as you pick it up. Thus you have to pick this up after the race starts or you loose time waiting for a 2nd player to start the race.

- Daisy Quest rewards are not worth the effort of "being first"

- There are mobs too close to some parts of the track (or even wander onto the track) which makes it dangerous for low level players or even HC players

- As soon as 1 player finishes the race, the other player Automatticly gets dismounted. This can be dangerous for any player under 30 due to the mobs near the race track.
Suggestions to improve it are
- Allow max of UP to 5 people to compete. (Increase the number of Sheep and speed boosts according to the number of players?)

- Include a 1m or 2m race start timer that allowers people to que and are prepared for the start.

- When the race timer reaches 0, all players are ported to the start and a 3s countdown starts

- Daisy's Quest timer starts when race starts - not when player picks up quest. This allows all players to pick up the quest and get the same time to complete it.

- A player can only cancel the race for themselves and results in a 30min abandonment buff that stops them from quing again. It doesn't cancel the race for other participants.

- Rewards will ONLY be given to those who complete the race. This will stop campers/farmers from afk farming the race rewards dening other players a chance at competing

- Race ends when last player reaches finish line. To stop people from abusing this increase the race time to 2:30s. More than enough time for a slower player to finish.

- either remove the mobs near the race track OR make the players in the races unagroable by the mobs ( to protect low level racers.)

- Daisy's reward should reflect coming 1st place  by
 - Including all the rewards of the Gnome/goblin race rewards - So players who win get a 3rd chance at those rewards

 - Making the drop rate higher for the Race googles, mount speed trinklets and gear
Fill up empty race spots with automated racers so if only single player is there, they can compete. Whether its 2 player max or 5 player max.
