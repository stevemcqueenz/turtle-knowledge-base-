All in all, Warmode is something that you should probably just take with a grain of salt since PvP isn't really the focus here, and it will at least allow you to opt into PvP with others who actually WANT to PvP. Otherwise, it really just sounds more like you want to grief, or get some cheap WPvP kills as opposed to actually getting into some engaging PvP, but that's really just how I view World PvP as a whole generally speaking.
