I am hyped about the upcoming mortal mode, but i kinda feel that it might get "abused" and cause a seconday market.

That being said,  i would love to see an "Ironman Glyph" added. This glyph would add the missing restrictions from Mortal Mode.

You must take this glyph at level 1, upon taking it, it clears your inventory, bank, mail... ect.

After equiping the glyph you are unable to interact with AH, Mail, Trades untill you hit 60.

The "Classic Ironman" challenage is a lot harder nomarlly but it might be a bit too much for some: [https://vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com/ ... n_leveling](https://vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Ironman_leveling)

The key restrictions i am looking for is the missing restrictions from Mortal Mode, that are normally in Hardcore mode.
