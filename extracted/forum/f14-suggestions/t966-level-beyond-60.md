# Level beyond 60

## Post 5487 by Poposhka — 2020-03-22T22:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5487#p5487 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, a potentially dumb/controversial topic, but what do you guys think of the ability to level beyond 60?

First thought is for this to be purely cosmetic. Players keep earning xp at 60 and will gain levels following some increasing Xp requirement. I’m not familiar with the source code, so there might be severe implications or ripple effects, but I can spitball that obtaining player level for calculating various interactions (level requirement, to-hit, whatever) would be capped at 60 through some encapsulating function, but for any visualization purposes the true level would be used.

Silly I suppose, but it would be a badge of honor(?) to see someone level 150, or w/e.

If this was seen as fun, and was implemented without pain, one could add extra stuff, like, you could unlock 70+ by obtaining a drop from a certain boss in a dungeon (strat undead maybe?) then 80+ for another. Or maybe he first few unlocks would be soloable?

Another idea would be to reset >60 levels on death, (or maybe down to the closest unlocked cap) having the player grind back up again.

Lastly, and I’d be hesitant and careful about this, is to gain primary/secondary attributes based on >60 levels, but that could get ugly I suppose.

Anyway, just a thought.

## Post 109250 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-21T17:04:00Z

Each level features talent point.
 1 additional talent point will allow to pick additional capstone talent. It was absolutely not planned, so it might break balance in unpredictable way.

Additional levels should not grant talent points.

Vermintide 2 solved it by granting free lootbox with each levelup after 35. It also display levels as 35+13 (where 13 is additional levels after 35).
Deep Rock Galactic solved it by making 2 numbers: character current level and total level.

Those additional levels might be purely cosmetic. For example after reaching 100 additional levels you could get title "ancient". But it don't benefit much to the game.
