# Mobs on Tel'Abim respawn WAY too fast

## Post 85221 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-01-18T18:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85221#p85221 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I suppose it was a good thing at launch when the population was crazy and all the mobs were camped and farmed for quests and such, but that is no longer the case. Now we're hovering around 500 people online and these respawn timers just don't make sense anymore, instead degrading the leveling experience, for example in cave-type areas where you must clear mobs to get to an objective, then you gotta AGAIN clear the mobs you just killed to get out of said cave. Even worse, if you're not fast enough, or take a mana break for example, the mobs will respawn in your face while you're pulling the next one, which is ridiculous and very annoying.

Please bring the mob respawn rates back down to normal on Tel'Abim.
