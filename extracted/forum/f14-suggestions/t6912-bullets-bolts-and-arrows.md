**Bullets, Bolts and Arrows**

 **Bows, Crossbows and Staves**

Another idea from me.

I do like the idea of **Engineering** how we can make bullets for guns in game but think we could go further.
I was thinking about  **Blacksmithing** how you could make **Bolts** and **Bolts** would be used for the ammunition for **Crossbow**, maybe we need to add more **Crossbows** in game to make this viable this will replace **Arrows** as their projectiles. **Arrows** would be used for only **Bows**, which would be more inline with how these weapons work.

Furthermore, I know a lot of people have suggested this following idea making, **Arrows**, so this would be another profession or a part of **Survival** profession, or a skill within the Hunter Class kinda like how Rogues have poisons to craft.

Buying wood or harvesting some in the world with metals /ore /bars and crafting them would be a great feature.

Or a more simple idea is more quests like [Arrows Are For Sissies][Alliance] or [A Fair Trade][Horde]
But for every tier of ammunition for e.g.
 **[Crafted Heavy Shot]** [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=3 ... heavy-shot](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=3930/crafted-heavy-shot)
can be exchange for
 **[Depricated Whipwood Arrow]** [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=30 ... wood-arrow](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=3029/depricated-whipwood-arrow)
from the same **NPC** or this **NPC** would be in small towns throughout the world, so you don't need to travel back to the city every time you need to reload your pouch or quiver
The quests this **NPC** offers for every craftable bullet would have an exchange for **Arrows** at the same level/Dps.

These are my thoughts about  **Ammo** in game, I really like the idea of many of us making our own death projectiles

I would like to see more crossbows in game, :) Thank you for ya time.
