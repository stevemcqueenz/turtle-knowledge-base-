# Goldshire on RP server

## Post 102625 by dkam0 — 2024-10-06T12:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102625#p102625 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone,

As many players I love to RP and I'm filled with love for the Alliance and hatred for the Horde. We are at war and everytime I see orcs or forsaken roaming the lands I'm either scared to the bones running for my life or sheating my sword to jump into the brawl to show hordies where they belong. I love it.

But there is one thing that's so immersion breaking and literally butchering the server's vibe. It's constant horde/alliance hangouts in the Goldshire. High level players just sit there around whole day talking and griefing low levels that are trying to enjoy their time and do some RP. High level alliance aren't interested in fighting hordies, breaking RP, writing out of character staff, playing on few different accounts/clients and flaming on others.

As far as I'm updated on rules that should be forbidden.

I don't mind the horde trying to spend the time in Goldshire to be honest but when they do they turn off PVP and simply terrorize newcomers. The server is really great, new content is awesome and feels fresh in comparison to the battle.net and Season of Discovery but the small and loud part of the community mentioned before is destroying the server's opinion. Could you do something with it? Maybe expand the guards aggro to flag hordes, or create new patrol that's above level 60.

I don't think people like it that way that few guys with too much time are just destroying the purpose of role playing and butchering warcraft's lore.

I know I'm not the only one feeling that was and in my opinion the decision to give up on language barrier was a immersion breaking mistake especially in the world where we have diplomacy/language skills to learn - this could be obtain over the time not on the level 1.

Please do something with trolls and griefers cause it's literally destroying the community. Readers feel free to leave your opinion, I'm eager to listen to the both sides.

Thanks
Loving player
