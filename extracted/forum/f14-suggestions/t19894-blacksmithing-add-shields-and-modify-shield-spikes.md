In history, shields have been generally manufactured with organic materials, such as wickers or wood, and in some cases only a little metal as surface plating. I don't like this specific part at all. Shields already come with armor that reduces incoming physical damage and a block value that reduces the damage further if a block occurs. Those block values are generally rather small numbers and only occur on an actual block. Adding shield plating that provides this high amount of flat damage reduction is asking too much. When a mid level shield comes with (idk spit balling) 20 black value on a 10-15% block rate, adding a thorium plating to always "block" an additional 20 DMG is just bonkers. If you want to block more incoming damage, there's +2% block enchant for shields.

Edit: when some low ass shield with a plating absorbs more damage unconditionally than a high level shield without plating absorbs only on an actual block, block value becomes pointless and shields only serve the purpose of holding a plating
