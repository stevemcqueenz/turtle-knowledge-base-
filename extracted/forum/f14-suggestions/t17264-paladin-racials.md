Paladin will have racial seals on release.
Dwarves will get seal of the mountain that increase their armor with each attack and aoe on judgement based on the number of charges.
The rest of the racial seals will be based on tbc.

Hope turtle starts scaling back the bloated and frankly weird strike abilities, does not fit the class at all.
Its just a mindless button click every 6 second.
The strikes mana cost should increase to be more in tune with the reality and believability.
Imagine if hunters arcane shot suddenly gave back as much mana as the spell cost+ healing.
You would just spam it every time that the cooldown is over, this is how classes play in retail and other modern games and its sad to see turtle is going in the same direction.
The solution might be to increase holy strikes mana cost by 3x, or simply remove it and move the strenghth buff over over to crusader strike and add some holy damage on crusader strike in protection.

In the holy tree crusader strike could give back some mana or reduce the cooldown of holy shock by 1 second per strike and the cooldown of holy shock is increased to 25-30 seconds
That way Holy Shock can be Restored.

One of the strikes should go in my opinion and the ability that remains should be balanced so it does not become a staple ability that you just use when the cooldown is over,but something you use strategically.
