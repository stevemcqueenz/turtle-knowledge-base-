**Portable Meeting Stone Suggestion**

I'm fairly new to this server. I personally believe the *Portable Meeting Stone* is a very neat idea.

That being said, and I apologize if this has been brought up before; I think it would be cool to have the item port you (*everyone*) to a different capital city at different times of the year.

Primarily because I believe this could potentially (*but maybe not?*) bring new life to cities in the game that aren't Orgrimmar or Stormwind.

The teleportation coordinates could be set for specific, role-play friendly area as they are for Stormwind (I've not personally used the Horde *Portable Meeting Stone* as of this post).

Just an idea. Just to sort of mix it up a little.
