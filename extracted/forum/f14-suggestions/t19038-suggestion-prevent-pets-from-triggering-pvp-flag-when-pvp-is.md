# [Suggestion] Prevent pets from triggering PvP flag when PvP is off (exploited by rogues)

## Post 129458 by Fin0001 — 2025-05-03T05:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129458#p129458 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I'm reposting this in the correct section, as advised.

I would like to raise a gameplay concern related to pet classes (Warlock/Hunter) and PvP flagging behavior.

### Situation:

While PvP mode is turned **off**, enemy players (e.g., Rogues) can still:
 1. Hit a pet-class player once;
 2. Vanish or flee;
 3. The player's pet auto-attacks in response;
 4. This auto-attack causes the **player** to become PvP-flagged;
 5. The enemy then returns and legally kills the now-flagged player.

At no point did the player actively engage or enable PvP — the flag was triggered **purely by pet AI**, not the player’s will.

### Why this is a problem:

 - It bypasses the player's choice to remain out of PvP.
 - It specifically punishes pet classes for having standard pet behavior.
 - It can be abused intentionally by stealth classes (Rogues).
 - It creates frustration and loss of progress for solo/PvE-focused players.

This is **not a bug**, but a mechanic that could be improved. The game already promises that PvE players will not be flagged unless they initiate attacks. But in this case, **the player did not initiate anything — their pet did**.

### Suggestions:

 - Pet auto-attacks should not trigger PvP flag unless the player manually commands the attack.
 - Alternatively, when PvP is off, pet aggression should be suspended against players.
 - PvP flag should be tied to player intent, not pet AI.

I understand this may have existed in the original mechanics, but Turtle WoW has already improved many systems for modern QoL and fairness. This is another chance to protect player intent, especially for solo and casual players.

Thank you for reading and considering this.
