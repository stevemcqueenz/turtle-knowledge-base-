# Earthshaker Slam doing damage

## Post 124627 by Taralej90 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-03-31T11:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124627#p124627 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For a spell that says "Slams your target with your shield", it's strange that Earthshaker Slam does no damage. Meanwhile, both warrior and paladin have shield attacks that cause damage.

My suggestion is quite simple and it will neither cause PvP disbalance nor somehow make shaman OP:

"Slams your target with your shield, taunting it. Also, causes damage modified by your block value and attack power and triggers your currently active elemental shield, consuming 1 charge."

 - the damage scaling from block value makes sense since the attack already requires a shield to use
 - the damage scaling from attack power is only fair, seeing as warriors received that treatment for Shield Slam
 - the attack triggering the currently active elemental shield will make the spell overall more synergistic, much in the same way as Lightning Strike but without the "empowered" part since Earthshaker Slam doesn't cost mana (like other taunts)

The main issue with my proposed change is that this would make Earthshaker Slam double as both a taunt and a threat-generator, which is never a good idea. The most obvious solution to this, therefore, would be to make a *separate* shield attack that does what I described. Nevertheless, a damage portion **should** be added after Earthshaker Slam, if only to make it slightly better at sticking threat to the shaman after the taunt wears off.
