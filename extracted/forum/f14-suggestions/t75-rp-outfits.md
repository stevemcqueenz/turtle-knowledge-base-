## Post 447 by Parcival (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2018-10-05T10:33:00Z

I'd have to say no to this. We're not a strictly RP server, so we shouldn't diregard the PVE aspect.

Obtaining said gear from quests, dungeons and especially crafters, helps the PVE aspect and brings with it its own enjoyment. Now I understand that this entails your character will look shabby until you've acquired it, but look at it like this: you can buy from actual vendors clothing sets which range from normal cloth to plate which can be mixed easily and give off a rewarding, aesthetically pleasant look of your character. Obtaining the said items from quests, dungeons and crafters is then a character progression.

Only way I can see this being good (turtle shop cosmetic items) is if the developers would insert models/textures not obtainable through normal gameplay (certain textures of clothing from certain mobs and NPC's cannot be found in regular armor in the game). Good example is that rugged jacket the Defias bandits use.
