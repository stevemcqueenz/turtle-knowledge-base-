# QoL for Theramore: Second bridge!

## Post 164810 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-10T07:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164810#p164810 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Duskwallow Marsh has seen some changes. Especially more quests for Alliance and Mudsprocket for Horde.
With the Shady Rest Inn questline extended there is also now quite a bit of running going on.
The Horde has in both Mudsprocket and Brackenwall a flightpoint. So it doesn't matter if for questing or to quickly access Ony's lair they have a convenient destination.

Level 60s travelling to Theramore usually just jump into the water and swim to get to Onyxia's Lair quicker.
Same for people leveling who try to get to Tabetha. Aswell as saving some time to get back and forth to the questgiver near the Shady Rest Inn.

I was thinking a second bridge for Theramore that connects to the already established path to an unused camp would be a great addition to the zone. The Horde has way shorter distances to travel in that zone compared to Alliance so this would balance this zone out a bit.

I will attach two images. One that shows Theramore as it looks at the beginning of Cataclysm.
And one that shows how it would connect on a map.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/cEYGIU5.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/7NPNjE4.jpeg)

 *Optional: Add another tower belonging to Theramore at the marked area(blue X).*
During the quest "[The Shield's Owner](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40554)" you must collect three reports, yet you collect two reports at the Sentry Point Tower and one at the North Point Tower. Now why would you need two reports from the same tower? You could move one of the Privates you collect the reports from to this new tower. The tower could be name something like "Bridge Point Tower". This tower would explain why the Alliance is aware of the crashed Zeppelin AND how they eventually spot the deserters hideout!
