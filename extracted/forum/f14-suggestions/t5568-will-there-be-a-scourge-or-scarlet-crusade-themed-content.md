I suppose the biggest issue in adding a third faction would be leveling. It's not as if a member of the Scourge could level through Alliance or Horde content really. They'd need their own 1-60 questing experience, and if it's made too fast by having fewer available quests and zones, players might flock to it in order to level up faster.

Maybe something at max level like a traitor system might work, but even then we'd be splitting the playerbase up more than it already is.
