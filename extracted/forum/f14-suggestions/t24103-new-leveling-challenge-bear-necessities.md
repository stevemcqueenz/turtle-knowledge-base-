# New leveling challenge: Bear Necessities

## Post 169493 by Casualnerdity — 2026-02-27T00:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169493#p169493 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Alternate name: Un-bear-lievable!
Mutually exclusive with Boaring Adventure

Players under this challenge do not receive XP from killing mobs or from most quests. Instead, they must kill bears in order to gain challenge-exclusive BoP drops from bears across Azeroth. These special drops are BoP to prevent players from stockpiling them and selling on the AH. They must then take these special drops to a Bear Expert (name needs workshopped) in a capital city (not ALL capitals) to complete various repeatable quests for XP. (This could potentially cause problems for people trying to skin carcasses.)

These items can be based on already existing drops from bears, just with a qualifier, ex: Diseased Bear Gall Bladder, Fatty Bear Meat, Pristine Bear Carcass, Discolored Bear Claw, etc.

The items could either be non-stackable, low drop chance turn-ins for a large chunk of xp, or stackable, moderate drop chance for smaller, rapid turn-ins. If the devs REALLY wanted to, they could make the drops Unique (X) so that players are compelled to head back to town to cash in every so often, and give others on the challenge a chance to farm drops.

I'm thinking of having the quests level gated, similar to the series of Fashion Demands Sacrifice quests, but with some overlap (1-3 levels) so that players don't have to completely dump their older quest items if they incorrectly calculate their level up.

There would need to be a new bear added in the mid-30s, since there is a definite gap from 32 (Goldpelt Grizzly in Northwind) to 41 (Ironfur Bears in Feralas).

Reward at level 60: one of the Armored Bear mounts, or the Big Turtle WoW Bear, plus a title (TBD)
