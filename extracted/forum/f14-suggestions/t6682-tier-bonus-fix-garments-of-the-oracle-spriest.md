> the bonuses do not synergize well

No, they don't.
However, that is the genuine "OG" stats & set-piece bonuses. It's not a misrepresentation or anything.

Like all the hybrid class sets from Vanilla, they're only geared towards 1 role (Healing).
Multi-role tier sets were not introduced until TBC. The Oracle / AQ40 set was just Blizzard's 1st attempt at giving the 0.01% of the player base that were allowed to raid non-Disc/Holy some sort of "set".

Regarding the set itself, SP is really all you care about as Shadow.
Crit / hit (spell pen) aren't needed, so you just stack SP. The head / chest are quote good, actually BiS during P5 running on into Naxx. You can get better Shoulders (BWL), pants (WB), boots (WB).
