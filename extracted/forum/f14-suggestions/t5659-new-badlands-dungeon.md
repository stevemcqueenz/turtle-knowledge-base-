# New Badlands dungeon

## Post 35294 by Rukopashka — 2023-02-19T22:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35294#p35294 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Dungeon Name**: Tomb of the Forgotten Kings

 **Location**: The dungeon entrance is located in the Badlands, *here's a chain of quests* to summon a portal to the Tomb of the Forgotten Kings:

Quest 1: Uncovering the Ruins
Players receive this quest by talking to an NPC located in the Badlands. The NPC explains that they've discovered ancient ruins that appear to be of Titan origin, but they need the players' help to clear out the area and uncover more of the ruins.

Objective: Clear out the enemies in the ruins and report back to the NPC.

Quest 2: Investigating the Ruins
The NPC informs the players that they've uncovered a portal deep within the ruins, but it appears to be inactive. The players are tasked with investigating the surrounding area for clues on how to activate the portal.

Objective: Collect artifacts from nearby creatures and bring them back to the NPC for analysis.

Quest 3: The Missing Component
The NPC informs the players that they need one more component to activate the portal, an ancient crystal of Titan origin. The crystal is said to be located in a nearby temple, guarded by powerful guardians.

Objective: Enter the temple and defeat the guardians to retrieve the crystal.

Quest 4: Summoning the Portal(or not a portal otherwise it doesnt matter)
With all the necessary components collected, the NPC instructs the players on how to activate the portal to the Tomb of the Forgotten Kings.

Objective: Follow the instructions and activate the portal.

Upon activation, the players are transported to the entrance of the Tomb of the Forgotten Kings.

 **Boss 1**: King Tutenra, the Mummy King
Description: King Tutenra is a mummified ruler of the ancient dynasty, brought back to life by dark magic. He commands an army of undead soldiers and can summon swarms of scarabs to overwhelm his enemies.

 **Boss 2**: Queen Neferia, the Serpent Queen
Description: Queen Neferia is a cunning and deadly snake-woman who once ruled alongside King Tutenra. She uses poison and shadow magic to debilitate her foes, and can summon giant snakes to aid her in battle.

 **Boss 3**: Prince Horus, the Falcon Prince
Description: Prince Horus is a fierce warrior who was transformed into a half-falcon creature by a curse. He wields a deadly spear and can call upon the power of the winds to create whirlwinds that suck in his enemies.

 **Boss 4**: High Priestess Setesh, the Shadow Priestess
Description: High Priestess Setesh is a powerful shadow priestess who serves the forgotten dynasty. She can summon shadow demons to do her bidding and can drain the life force of her enemies to heal herself.

 **Boss 5**: King Ramses, the Deathless Pharaoh
Description: King Ramses is the final boss of the dungeon, an immortal pharaoh who has survived for millennia thanks to dark magic. He wields a powerful staff and can summon sandstorms and undead minions to aid him in battle.

Overall, the Tomb of the Forgotten Kings is a challenging dungeon filled with traps, puzzles, and ancient relics. Players will need to work together to defeat the powerful bosses and uncover the secrets of the long-lost dynasty.э
