# Cohesive Racial Druid Forms

## Post 120445 by Leeviathan — 2025-02-20T01:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120445#p120445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone! This is my White Whale in WoW, the thing I've wanted for 20 years at this point: I want a *complete set* of racially cohesive druid forms for Tauren and Night Elf. Specifically I'm talking about race-tailored aquatic and travel forms for both Tauren and Night Elf.

Here's a compilation image of models I made/modified to show off my idea of cohesive druid forms, the highlighted bottom section are the forms that are normally shared between both races (travel, aquatic, and tree of life forms)
![Image](https://www.mediafire.com/convkey/d926/zxtmnwm06hzap299g.jpg)

Unfortunately a cosmetic mod can't achieve this in-game because the shared forms for Night Elf and Tauren refer to the same database entry; changing the Night Elf travel form, for example, changes it for the Tauren travel form as well.

In my perfect world I would want every form to be coherent in regards to color scheme and racial features i.e. long-ears/glowing-eyes on Night Elf forms, horns/non-glowing-eyes on Tauren forms. Additionally I'd keep the family of the original animal forms the same and differentiate the species between Night Elf and Tauren based on the types of species found in their respective environments: i.e. Night Elf panther vs Tauren lion, Night Elf black bear vs Tauren grizzly, Night Elf Stag vs. Tauren Antelope, Night Elf sea lion vs. Tauren water-buffalo-like seal, etc.
