# Radio and music loudness

## Post 68529 by Yurylexg — 2023-10-03T19:03:00Z

Hello!
The new Radio's loudness knob seems to be the same as in-game settings music fader.
But the thing is that loudness of in-radio music is much more loud by itself than in-game music.
Thus every time we turn the radio off, we have to go in game settings and raise the standard music volume up back.
That all seem to be not that proper:
Two different music sources, two different initial loudness levels and the same linked tuning pot.
I would suggest then to calibrate the whole Radio's loudness along with in-game music or just make these settings true separated.
Cheers )

P.S.: Btw the radio was truly a great idea. A flow of GTA to Wow really brings the game ambiance to a higher level ;-)
