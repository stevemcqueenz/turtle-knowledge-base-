# Convert Jintha'Alor into Full Dungeon

## Post 18278 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-04-21T07:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18278#p18278 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Jintha'Alor should be upgraded to a dungeon, allowing groups to be formed for it with the turtle finder.  Essentially what would need to be done is put an instance portal at the first staircase, add in extra bosses, place important gear drops throughout the dungeon, and make more quests for it.  Most of the work has already been done since the mobs are elite, the work would predominantly be tweaking content.
It could be expanded with caves that enter the mountain in order to make room for more mobs and bosses, and perhaps extend the structure itself by adding in an extra level or two at the top (right now its 5 levels high)

This would give players more incentive to use the area, as right now people typically do it once just so they can get the hammer for ZF, and don't go back because finding a group is frustrating
