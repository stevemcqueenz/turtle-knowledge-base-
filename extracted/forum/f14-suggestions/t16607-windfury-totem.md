# Windfury Totem

## Post 113379 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-19T13:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113379#p113379 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I propose no buff or nerf per se, all I'm saying is that WF totem is in a very weird spot from shaman PoV.
To understand the problem: shaman buffs work like party auras, which effect every party member in a given area, and they have four elements. They can use one totem from each element at a time. Also, weapon imbues don't interact with totems affecting weapons, except for frostbrand weapon apparently.
There are some exceptions -> very gimmicky buffs like water walking and water breathing.
tldr: shaman buffing is linked to area-based party auras.

The problem is that in raids no shaman likes to do WF totem. It is a huge party buff, so it is done but my point is that each build has to work against his own interest/best performance.
As a weird example: imagine pala's receiving a new aura which gives the effect that party members' damaging, non-instant spells have a 20% chance to duplicate. Something like the new arcane talent.
From one hand it is a huge group utility and palas would be distributed to caster groups and would be f.ex. forced to give up the holy damage aura (and the beneficial group comp) for the caster advantage.
It's just a feels bad scenario from an individual pov, even if it helps the group.
Same applies to shaman, since they can't single-target buff the melee with wf, it's a party aura.

Why the builds don't like using WF totem?:

Enh DPS: They have WF imbue which is better and the two don't interact. They prefer agi totem as air totem for the 4,5% crit chance (better than leader of the pack after Kara10 relic upgrade) since it benefits them and enh DPS relies on crits very heavily for many talent procs.

Enh tank: They use rockbiter, can't benefir from WF. Moreover, they need agi totem for crit (same reason as enh DPS), for the 4,5% dodge and for the 176 armor, too.

Ele: Even though CC2 made ele threat more manageable, they really prefer using tranquil air which works with salvation (in a way) + they prefer caster groups, with a good old moonkin. (Moonkins are different, they have a party aura which also benefits them the most, too + they have 1 of it).

Resto: They are the closest to be happy using WF since they don't really need other air totems, but needing to use WF means no mana regen party comp and no druid tree buff in the party.

Again, I'm not saying WF should be buffed or nerfed, giving no specific proposals. I just say that because of the way how shaman totem works, using it means to play subotimally from an individual pov for each build, specially for melee.
Same applies to a degree to the new flametongue totem, too.
