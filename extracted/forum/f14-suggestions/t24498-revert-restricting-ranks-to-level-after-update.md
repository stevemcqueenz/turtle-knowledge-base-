# Revert Restricting Ranks to Level after Update.

## Post 172401 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-25T15:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172401#p172401 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

When the 1.18.1 patch was first released, players were finally able to progress through PvP ranks regardless of their level, albeit at a much slower pace. This change felt refreshing and removed the somewhat arbitrary restriction of being capped at certain ranks (e.g., Rank 3 until level 33).

During that time, some players were able to reach higher ranks at lower levels (for example, Rank 4 at level 19 or Rank 6 at level 39). While these ranks didn’t provide immediate gameplay advantages since the gear remains unusable at those levels, it added a strong sense of progression and personal challenge.

After the recent maintenance, this system was reverted. As a result, players lost ranks they had legitimately earned and were pushed back to the previous level based caps. Imo this feels like a step backward.

Even if rank gains were slow at low lvls, it gave people something to work toward. For many, it wasn’t about power it was about progression and the fun of pushing to the next rank.

I’d like to suggest reverting this change and restoring the pre-maintenance behavior. It added fun to lower level players who want to stay level locked.
