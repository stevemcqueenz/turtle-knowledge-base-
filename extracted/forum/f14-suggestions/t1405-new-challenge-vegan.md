Vegan Mode
 1. Cannot kill beasts. You can harm them or kite them to keep them from attacking you however. Killing them will make you die. If you kill too many your character will be permanently deleted. So you shouldn't take beast killing quests at all.
 2. You cannot take skinning, leatherworking professions.
 3. You cannot wear leather
 4. You cannot buy, sell, cook or eat meat. That includes cooking recipes.
