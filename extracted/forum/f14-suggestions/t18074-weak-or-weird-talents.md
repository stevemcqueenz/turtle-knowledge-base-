# Weak or weird talents

## Post 123053 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-19T21:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123053#p123053 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

In this article I wanna collect some talents which I think are too weak or they are in a wrong talent tree.
Will only sample some, I'm happy for new additions.
I know that a build's strength depends on many factors, like gear, abilities, the sum of the used talents, but still, I feel like some talents just really fell off and could give some builds a push just by reworking them a bit.

 1. Survivalist (Hunter, Survival tree, 3rd row)
This 5TP talent gives you 10% HP buff if fully learned. It has some uses in PVP and would make some sense if hunter tanks existed but as it stands it seems a kinda deep talent with a questionable effect which raises a fringe stat for a DPS for a bunch of TP.

 2. Divine Strength (Paladin, Holy tree, 1st row)
It just feels misplaced since the melee healer is still not really fleshed out. Would make more sense being somewhere in the ret tree. At least it's a 1st row talent.

 3. Improved Rend (Warrior, Arms tree, 1st row)
Besides maybe some fringe use in PVP its main use is to give access to Deep Wounds and Impale later. But I think being forced to spend 2TP on (mostly) trash is not a good design.

 4. Earth's Grasp (Shaman, Elemental tree, 2nd row)
It feels like it buffs the wrong totems. Earthbind is nice and has an effect in solo-leveling and PVP but stoneclaw is extremely niche for everyone who is not a tank and tanks don't really prio the HP of that totem (CD or threat amount are bigger factors). The talent feels like a cool idea to buff elemental-related totems. Maybe moved further down so later totems, f.ex. air totems could be included.
