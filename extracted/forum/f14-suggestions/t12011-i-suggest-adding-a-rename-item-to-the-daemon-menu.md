# I suggest adding a "Rename" item to the daemon menu.

## Post 79508 by Krokzogg — 2023-12-01T13:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79508#p79508 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1) After clicking the “Rename” item, the demon shouts: “Mortal, how dare you give me a name? Do you think I’m a dog? You will die!!!”
 2) The player dies instantly.
 3) It is impossible to summon this demon for a week.
 4) During the week, if you summon other demons, they say a random phrase: “Bad Master!”, “What a weirdo, Bad Master!”, “What, you came up with a name for me too, Bad Master?”, “Are you counting demons?” with your dogs, Bad Master?"

Let the Demon Trainer say a random phrase after parting: “Don’t give them names.”, “They don’t like names.”, “Don’t even think about giving them names!”.

For all Demon Trainers, add a dialogue item "Can I rename the demon?"
