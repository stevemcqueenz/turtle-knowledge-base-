At times, transmogs have holes in them and rather than to have skin show, players wish to cover these holes up. As any chest piece can be added as a shirt, we had a few requests for models we would like to see possible as shirts in game.

 1. Silver (Champion's Armor), Gold (Golden Scale Cuyrass), and Black (Blackforge Breastplate)

![Image](https://static.icy-veins.com/images/thumbnails/bd2046ce8cfe7544eb02e010b02f451fa84925cfca25fd8b3c06f2c0c670a05b-small.jpg)

![Image](https://static.icy-veins.com/images/thumbnails/cd9039671eb8428912013ad5e41aaa126d5ca0c8c8a06bd23c69b7fc1fa974da-small.jpg)

![Image](https://static.icy-veins.com/images/thumbnails/4a7e328e0406d71431f0c78276e278cde12d07517f9d562aecc9e91317c003c8-small.jpg)

 2. Often, quest givers are given the top half of the Truefaith Robe, Runecloth Robe, Frostweave Robe, Wizardweave Robe, Robes of the Lich, Shadoweave Robe, and the Arcane Robe to go beneath their outfits. We know this exists already, so it would be nice to have as an option for player characters too.

You can see an example of this in this Quest NPC from Quel'thalas on the Isle of Eternal Autumn.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/wzqDFMd/Wo-WScrn-Shot-020824-015859.png)

 3. Arm drapes from the Venomshroud, Arachnidan Robes, and Chan's Imperial Robes. We know these also exist from Priestess Amalthea in Darnassus. She wears an entirely custom outfit, Lucky her!

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/76341-priestess-alathea.jpg)

 4. Lastly, a shirt version of the Cindercloth Vest, Elegant Tunic, Frostweave Tunic, and Black Mageweave Vest. These items work extremely well as armor to go beneath other appearances you may want to show (chest, tabard) for customization, and would likely do well on the Donation Store too given that the mageweave tabards are seen all around. They sub in well for a metal mail option for cloth wearers.

![Image](https://static.icy-veins.com/images/thumbnails/226dc170a5569596077bb408abd60605d5c23c5121709c1fd362ff8ffdf4dd3a-small.jpg)

![Image](https://static.icy-veins.com/images/thumbnails/096dc9390d5bfe8e3bff1ea4e1ec093b3add4fe05c31423248ae0fddf4ec4678-small.jpg)

![Image](https://static.icy-veins.com/images/thumbnails/3fe46b51ffdeefa702ca880d653b34f2c525c04a246d39218838f6fa410e186b-small.jpg)

![Image](https://static.icy-veins.com/images/thumbnails/7f264e71fedbeccc371a03555b92a1419ca73f56b6cd7c18a9001f2d4c6db750-small.jpg)

This is so desperately needed.

So many otherwise excellent chest pieces are avoided due to weird gaps or coverage.

I would also like to suggest simple chainmail shirts. A lot of chest pieces start the arms with chain, and then suddenly stop midway up the arm. Why?!

I would be beyond overjoyed to have a simple chainmail shirt to wear underneath my armor, which is how plate would have been worn anyway!

Let the people cover up their arms with armor!
