# Let people exchange racial bonuses.

## Post 9799 by Qixel (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-03-18T18:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9799#p9799 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We've all been there; you love the look of night elves or dwarves, forsaken or trolls, but humans and orcs have weapon skill, and it's so busted you suck it up and roll one of those instead. Maybe you're a caster; you love the aesthetic of human mages, but gnomes get that +5% intellect, so what can you do?

I get the idea of racials. Different races with different strengths, so you get minor bonuses. But Blizzard wasn't very good at balance in Vanilla, and what should be minor oftentimes wasn't. Every melee player knows how strong +5 weapon skill ended up being. Every caster knows what 5% more Intellect would do for them. But what can you do?

Turtle is a roleplay server. Turtle is a PvE server. People should not be punished for wanting to take part in both. Let people pick what racial bonuses they want. You could call them 'Inherent Bonuses' to avoid having a nonindicative name. Lock them to races that can be the same class as the character who seeks to use inherent bonuses, and replace them wholesale. Want to be a undead rogue with human racials? Fine. You get the human bonuses and lose the undead ones. Want to be a dwarf paladin with orc racials? No can do; orcs cannot be paladins.

But what about PvP; it would change the meta, so what do we do about that? Simple; just lock players who choose to use inherent bonuses out of pvp. The PvPers can keep their meta, and the people who care about pve and rp can have fun as a night elf who is skilled at using swords.

When you get down to it, if you make the bonuses be replaced wholesale from another race, it becomes entirely cosmetic. Mechanically, you're effectively playing a human who looks and sounds like a gnome. You could make it a donation reward, in the same lieu as the race change tokens; instead of changing your race, you simply change the bonuses your character has.
