Congratulations to the Turtle Team on the ever increasing population and success of Turtle WoW. But with an ever increasing population comes long queues, and that brings me to my next point:

## Post 64468 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-09-15T15:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64468#p64468 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Multiboxing is really neat and I'm glad it's permitted. It's something that was never really feasible for me on retail servers due to the subscription cost. Here there are numerous constraints on it that limits its usefulness. Chiefly it's virtually impossible to multibox on the same machine during peak hours because the second client gets lowest queue priority. It's not even worth trying to log in on a second client, you'll never get in. And that's honestly completely fair. Beyond that I don't really understand what the problem is people have with mutliboxers.

 *) They shouldn't take up 2 spots while queues are in effect. This is already pretty much enforced for multiboxing on the same machine. I'm not averse to expanding it to multiboxing on different machines, but it gets very hard to differentiate between 2 people living in the same house and someone multiboxing.
 *) They cannot PvP and this should absolutely stay like that.
 *) They cannot use more than 2 characters and I'm completely fine with that. It's already hard enough to control 2 at the same time without any technical assistance.
So all they get is a *potential* edge in PvE. And I find that highly theoretical. With divided attention you'll never pull the weight of 2 competent players through multiboxing.

It's really more a fun thing to do. I like gearing my alts in dungeon loot because LFT queues for dps classes are awful. Something like this does not take away from anyone else, so I don't really see what the problem is. If anything it alleviates the tank/healer shortage on LFT. And in a few levels all that gear will be outdated and meaningless anyway.
Since you can't use warmode with multiboxing and you get a hefty XP penalty for having a lvl 60 in group I'm not really convinced multiboxing allows you to level faster. Tag farming is indeed a bit iffy, but I've seen guilds organize that to boost players they need to fill raid gaps. Not sure this is worth any multiboxing outrage in particular beyond the general issue.
There's also the issue of class disparities. There are certain classes that can already solo dungeons way more easily than other classes. A warrior with a pocket healer will still not be as fast as a druid who can just stealth to the target and heal themselves. A mage can just solo farm ZG. Personally I multibox for the fun and the challenge, not to gain an advantage. There are many better ways of doing that.

Also, combat multiboxing should be differentiated from administrative multiboxing. With the latter I see even less reason to get up in arms about it. If I can just hop on my second account and mail myself needed stuff during the raid all I do is not make up to 39 people wait while I relog (or worse: hearth and fly back). Being able to enchant your gear - you would have to level up your alt to a useful enchanting level and get them all the useful enchanting recipes. That will be an effort that costs more than tipping the occasional enchanter. It's a convenience, not really and advantage, and it probably pours more money into the economy than it takes away.
Someone mentioned bag space - please, it's Turtle WoW. The real cheating are the donation rewards. People have 36 slot bags or bank mules or portable mail boxes, they really don't need to level an alt to follow them around.

I think it's fair to be concerned. I suspect there will be single people who find a way to abuse the system. But the vast majority of people multiboxing (and that's probably still a fairly small community) do it in a legit way that doesn't really provide unfair advantages. It's just a different way of enjoying the game. I don't think declaring them scapegoats for the current miserable situation is fair or helpful.
