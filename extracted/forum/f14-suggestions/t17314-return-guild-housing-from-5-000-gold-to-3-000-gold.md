 **Although all things are subject to change until released…** *This feels intentionally back handed considering the price announcement of 3,000 gold being made public by the Twitter just a few days ago. This will cause further inequality between guilds, and animosity towards those who got to keep their tabards which this entire change was attempting to alleviate in response to. It will also cause community stagnation, stopping people from making new guilds and instead swarming to older ones which may be full due to their secondary teleports. The guild house system was never supposed to be for the teleport - it was supposed to be for the community which is grieving now.*

 *Turtle Wow has for years had a bustling RP community. I cannot count how many roleplayers I know who have spent a lot of time making art for the game, engaging in content, creating music, purchasing items for RP from the donation shop, and spending a full 40 hours each week or more making events for the realm which draws in players both new and old as basically free employees. This actively will begin driving them off, and forcing many of them to refund the gold fundraised by their members due to the impossibility of a 5,000 gold price tag - taking away the dream of a guild house away once more. The price of the guild house should be lowered to reflect the promised value; the mission statement was clear: affordable housing for everyone.*
