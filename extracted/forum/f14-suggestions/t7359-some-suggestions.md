> **Wez0 wrote: Sat May 20, 2023 3:06 pm**
> Disable cloudflare loading on IPs that already have an account

The *Turtle WoW* web servers use *CloudFlare* in order to, I'm guessing, maintain stability and availability at all times and to prevent unwanted automation.  It's not really something that can be "turned off" for specific users.

In my experience, it rarely gets in the way and rarely requires actual human interaction. (I get a simple "click here" CAPTCHA, sometimes.)

> Direct new players to patch/mod/hacking their .exe so they stay longer

The client is already modified in several ways and there is already a link to the forum and *Discord* server for further inquiries.

> explain things like the tent in-game

[The tooltip says it all.](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=51283)  Otherwise, it's very easy to get context from just observing it in action.

> make everyone able to talk at level 1

A "minimum level" barricade was put in place to prevent people from making a character, logging in, spamming gold-selling websites in the channels, then fleeing.

> change you cannot talk yet so it displays at what lvl you can and why it is forbidden for me.

Honestly, this isn't a bad idea.  Even something in the Message of the Day or Newcomers guild would be helpful for new players expecting to talk at Level 1.

> give me settings to enable/disable bg & hc yellow txts

This is another thing that should really be in the MotD.

Right now, there doesn't seem to be a way to disable Battlegrounds messages.  However, typing ".hcmessages ##" will disallow Hardcore messages from any level below whatever level you specify.

> add .help or disable. in retail it justs let you say it in-game

This is a *MaNGOS*-exclusive thing.  Usually, it does exactly what you'd think, but on this server, it's been replaced by the ".commands" command.

> at the donation items, display the item too

Honestly, this would be nice for Skins, Tabards, and Mounts.

> remove unlit poor torch from vendor as it has no usage

The Unlit Torch item is related to the reintroduced [Survival](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Survival) secondary skill.  Much like in the *World of Warcraft* betas, a Poor Unlit Torch can be turned into a [Dim Torch](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6182).
