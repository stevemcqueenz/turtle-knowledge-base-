# outland prebatch event for turtle wow

## Post 15790 by Zuesgold — 2022-01-14T15:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15790#p15790 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

with outland having been confirmed as on the way i have come up with a outland prepatch event for turtle wow that i feel could be a huge hit

the event follows a massive invasion of azeroth from outland by the illidari which results in Nethergarde keep being placed under siege by the enemy forces

the goal of the invasion is to conquer the blasted lands and use the area as a staging ground for the conquest of all of azeroth which illidan seeks to use as a buffer in his struggle against the burning legion

the entire event takes place within the Blasted lands where players work with the Argent dawn in driving the illidari back and securing the dark portal

the Argent dawn have established their primary outpost within the swamp of sorrows just off the road from the entrence to the blasted lands and a forward outpost within the blasted lands itself. all the quests during the event including lifting the seige at nethergard and the final Battle for the dark portal quest will be offered at these 2 outposts

after completing the Battle for the dark portal quest and completing the event the portal will be secured by agents of the argent dawn while players begin their adventures in outland
