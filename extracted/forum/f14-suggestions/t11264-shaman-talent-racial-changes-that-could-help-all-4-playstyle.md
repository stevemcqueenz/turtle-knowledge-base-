# Shaman Talent/Racial Changes That Could Help All 4 Playstyles

## Post 74574 by Ghola (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-10-29T01:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74574#p74574 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I had an idea to help all 4 shaman playstyles directly and indirectly by removing one talent, adding a talent, and adding a racial.

Talent Change 1: Make Mana Tide Totem baseline and only affect the shaman. This will help alleviate elemental and enhancements downtime, without affecting balance too much especially since it could be destroyed in pvp like any other totem. It could be reworked to give mana based off spirit like innervate, or just be based off total mana or mp5. An additional idea I had was a talent in either deep elemental or deep resto which allows the shaman to share a percentage of the mana regen with nearby players (as opposed to your party since many shamans will be in a melee group), bringing some caster support to shamans.

Talent Change 2: Replace the capstone resto tree talent with tauren's racial, Spirit Link. By making this spell into a talent, it can be worked into a resto shamans abilities without worrying about buffing one race above the others. This will also encourage shamans to spec deep resto, which is an indirect boon for enhance shamans as they would be brought more for their bloodlust. You could give Spirit Link synergy with Chain Heal, for example having Chain Heal crits on Spirit Linked targets return mana or something similar. A more complex idea I had was making Spirit Link start at a 5% damage sharing, that stacks (up to 3 times, for a maximum of 15%) everytime a linked target is hit with Chain Heal.

Racial Change: With Spirit Link moved to resto tree, taurens would need a new racial. I propose Ethereal Form, which could function as a defensive cooldown for tank shamans, such as increased dodge chance or flat damage reduction. This would still be useful for other shaman specs because of the relatively high chance they have to pull threat. I haven't put as much thought into this one as I have little interest in shaman tanks, however many of them seem to be taurens for the added stamina and warstomp. My worry is making one race the defacto shaman tank, however this kind of design is already prevalent in vanilla wow.

With these changes, all shamans would have increased mana sustain, enhancement would have more of a niche that gets them into raid groups, restoration would have a reason to spec into deep resto and another spell that plays to their strengths, and tank shamans would gain a defensive cooldown
