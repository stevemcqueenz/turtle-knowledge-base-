# PVP/BG balance suffering immensely from CC2

## Post 107154 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-08T20:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107154#p107154 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Making another post on this topic to raise attention to this issue.

CC2 has redrawn the game completely in terms of pvp. And not in a good way. With the powercreep of items and poorly considered spell changes you have had impacts on pvp that you should have foreseen but clearly didn't.

Players now die in BGs so fast you can barely finish lesser heal casts to save them. Half the classes burst you down in seconds. It more resembles a call of duty mmo than the (admittedly not perfect) vanilla pvp we were used to.

I thought CC2 would rebalance things in a moderate way but it went the complete opposite direction. You have created a monster.

I am genuinely disappointed in the lack of care for pvp balance and it has very much affected my respect for the twow devs.

I urge you to prioritize pvp balance when making the short-term updates that are promised.
