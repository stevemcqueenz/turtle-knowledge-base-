# Tauren Shrinking

## Post 28007 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-10-26T17:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28007#p28007 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tauren are the biggest race in WoW - so big that they have a larger hitbox and are infamous for taking up the player's screen. Some of us would like to play somewhat smaller tauren, such as we see in the form of the tauren glyph seller in the image.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/vmnC1rzS/Screen-Shot-2022-10-26-at-6-57-06-PM.png)

There is gnome enlargement balm to make gnomes bigger. Might we get something to make taurens smaller, perhaps even permanently or something like a druid glyph that we could carry on our keychain and use as we desire? It would be a nice quality of life improvement and would make me not dread playing a a tauren again. All the better if it make the tauren hitbox the same as that of every other race in the game.

This isn't bashing anyone who likes large tauren. We should keep large tauren of course. The option to be smaller would simply be lovely.
