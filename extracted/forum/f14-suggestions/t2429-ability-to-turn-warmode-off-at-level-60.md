# Ability to Turn Warmode Off at Level 60

## Post 15153 by Pistol (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-01-02T02:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15153#p15153 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It would be a great thing for folks that want to level up with the pvp glyph on to be able to turn it off once they're level 60. There will likely be RPers and Raiders that would like to avoid being ganked, having buffs dispelled or other annoyances once they reach 60. They will be 60 for far longer than they've been leveling.

To avoid people flipping it on and off constantly it at 60 could be made to have a long cooldown, perhaps even a month or longer. Another idea is that level 60 players could request by ticket to have it turned off, this would avoid it being flippantly used and being turned off after ganking someone or whatever.
