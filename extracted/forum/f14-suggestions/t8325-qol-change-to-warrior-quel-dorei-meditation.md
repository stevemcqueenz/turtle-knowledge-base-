# QoL change to Warrior Quel'dorei Meditation

## Post 53665 by Tawneyturtle (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-07-16T11:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53665#p53665 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At the moment the high elf racial generates Rage for warriors and energy for rogues, while the rest of the classes get mana. The former 2 are both very situational as you never want to stand still generating a resource as those classes since you want to auto attack. Seeing as how High elf is a very unpopular choice for any physical dps class in endgame, here is my suggestion

A Small suggestion would be: Have warriors be considered in combat for the duration of the channel, so they do not lose the rage they gained while channeling.

The only way you can use this racial is: 1. Use Bloodrage. 2. Use racial. Otherwise you lose a chunk of the rage you generated. I feel like this is an oversight, as you shouldn't need to wait for a class spell to make your racial ability be usable.

So again, the suggestion: For the channel duration, have the warrior be considered to be in combat for 5 seconds once you press the button. The spell lasts 5 seconds and generates 6 rage every second, with rage decay, you end up with 27 instead of 30 out of combat. With the change I propose this means that instead of 27 rage you'd end up with 30, if you have anger management then 32. Extremely small QoL change so you don't have to use Bloodrage before you use this every single time.
