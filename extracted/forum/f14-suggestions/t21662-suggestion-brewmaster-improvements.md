# [Suggestion] Brewmaster Improvements

## Post 150080 by HippoMilo — 2025-09-03T17:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150080#p150080 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey all, had a go at Brewmaster, some ideas I think could be good.

 **Startzone / XP gains refinement**
Eary levels, could be hard to get money to become completely smashed, could be nice to have booze for sale at the starter area and just make it weak and typical because of the money contraints. That being said, could be nice to do an exeprience gain break down based on level of drunk. Something like:
Tipsy = 25% experience gain
Drunk = 50% experience gian
Smashed = 100% experience

 **Plugin**
I made a plugin to show a status bar, how drunk you are. Could be nice if there was a way to see the level of drunk (API?), vs just having to read the combat chat to see what level you are at.

 **Gameplay**
This I think is the big one and I hope some of these are doable and there is an interest in doing them.
Effects on gameplay (could be tied to the level of drunk and make the effect bigger the drunker you are)
 - When attacking, It would be cool if your level of drunk messes with your swing timer, making it a little sporatic going faster/slower at random time.
 - When you melee attack you "stumble" and move in a random direction. (This would be a huge boost to gameplay I think).
 - When using a skill/spell, the character "misclicks" and activates a different one. (Maybe this is just locked to the skills on the bar). Or, at a random time, they activate a skill, or when click a skill, it doesn't activate. (Would be wild haha). Also, messing with the cast time here as well.
 - When opening a bag, it open all of them or the wrong one. When opening a map, it opens to the wrong location, When opening a profession tab, it opens the wrong profession's tab.
 - Blurr. I didn't get blurr for some reason, was it removed? Having blurr I think adds to the gameplay. and motion sickness. haha
 - In the middle of a swing/cast, character stops or stumbles. Character randomly sits down or lays down. Character randomly starts dancing or emoting. Saying "Thank you" to someone repeatedly.
A lot of these can be sporatic, happening at random time. With more frequency the drunker you are. When completely smashed, maybe the percentage is 50% at most? I'd imagine aroun 40% change a weird thing like that happens would be high enough to give good gameplay?

 **Crafting**
As someone else mentioned, could be cool to add booze to alchemy

The darkmoon fair brew that is extremly potent make the challenge a bit to easy, maybe remove those for Brewmaster, or, limit how many they can buy. Not sure what a good way to balance that would be.

What are your thoughts on these? Pros/Cons?

 **Update**
More interactions that can happen based on drunk level:
 - Lose/Change target focus ( in the middle of attacking something you lose focus on it ). It can even switch to a friendly target. >.<
 - Randomly Drink/Eat, even randomly take a potion, mistaking it for alcohol to drink.
 - Randomly send a party invite to near by friendly players ( trying to be friends with everyone. haha ).
