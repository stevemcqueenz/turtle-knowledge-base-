# [Hardcore] Randomized Death Message

## Post 50375 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-17T11:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50375#p50375 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Inspired by [this "*toe-tally cringe* fo-rem toe-pic, *dewd*"](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=7837).

I've always found the Hardcore death message to be a bit dramatic.  What's so "tragic" about some person dying during a Hardcore run, exactly?  Honestly, I find it absolutely **hilarious** whenever I see someone croak to something several levels below their weight class, or some Level 10 fella get hosed by an Orgrimmar Guard or something.

Something I'd like to see is a "death message" randomizer.  Like, the commentary after the death message should be randomized, depending on the level.  For example:
> *[player]* has perished at Level *[level]* *[to a [target (Level)] / cause]*.
>
>  Level 10-19
> - Well, easy come easy go…
> - At least they weren't Level 59, huh?
> - It's back to the drawing board for them!
> - It's a shame, but these things happen.
> - A harsh reminder that there are no shortcuts in life.
>  Level 50-58
> - They will be remembered as a hero by those closest to them.
> - Truly, they were not only brave, but skilled!
> - Wouldn't it be funny if they just GRINDED their way to that level?
> - Let this death be a lesson for all.
>  Level 59
> - A lost of this magnitude surely sends a message…
> - What a shame! They were only one day from retirement!
> - And so, a long journey comes to a disappointing and dramatic end…
> - Let this be a reminder that even the best laid plans can sometimes go awry.
> - The gods weep for the loss of their champion…

I'm sure I could think of more, but these are only random examples to get the point across, so… yeah.  Have at it.
