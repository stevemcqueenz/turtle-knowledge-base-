# Challenge for Hostile/Cross Faction Rep

## Post 147767 by Serijas — 2025-08-23T01:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147767#p147767 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We have Blood Elf / Dark Ranger / Sanlayn looks, Dark Mage / Twilight looks etc. that we can already buy from the Shop but generally speaking there is no good way to even establish a neutral stance to Factions you could see your character associated with. Would it be possible to at least be able to grind your reputation to Neutral towards the opposing or an hostile faction so you can Roleplay around them or even receive some items from a Quartermaster? I feel like that could expand the overall experience and RP a lot. (Especially not being aggro'd because you **earned** your right/standing to be there in that faction.)

Possible things that could raise reputation between the Alliance faction for Horde could be killing Defias or Syndicate. For Alliance towards Horde it could be Centaurs, Demons etc.

On another note roleplaying a Defias for example, you could flag yourself as Hostile to Alliance / Horde and killing PvP-enabled Players or killing Guards/NPCs that could additionally be spawned in the future. The Defias could have an hideout that serves like a Capital that only you and other players who have gotten to Neutral / Friendly with them can enter and there you could access a Hearthstone, Blackmarket Auction House etc. Essentially, Neutral Hubs across Azeroth would be your "capital" to meet other players in general like Ratchet, Bootybay, Everlook, or even more custom Neutral Hubs like an tribal, Ogre Outpost or Centaurs etc.

TL;DR what I suggest:

 **As a Challenge:** This all could be framed inside an additional Challenge over the Challenge menu, turning all Alliance/Horde factions to Neutral towards you, or disliked (not At War/Aggressive), so that you have to do some Quest / Grind to interact with NPCs in the City.
 **Neutral Start Zone:** There could be a Pirate places of sorts, something nudged towards Neutrality in a bigger sort of Update/Patch that removes the player from the traditional start of their usual Race-oriented Zone and it sets them into a different atmosphere. They could technically leave for Ratchet at any time and continue leveling at about 10 - 15 ranges there with some new Level 10 - 15 quests added just for them.
 **Optionality:** This is purely an "You can do this and receive cosmetical rewards" kinda thing, first and foremost you essentially shoot yourself in the foot for going Neutral/Abandoning your Faction.
 **Rep Grind Quests:** You could have repeatable Quests to do that you get from Blackboards or Wanted Posters seeking out Ears etc. as proof of the Kill to turn in to a local guard who in return gives you some coin and most importantly a bit of reputation. This could enhance the Bounty Hunter feel to it, too.
 **Expanding the Roleplay, not the Mechanic:** As a new feature it could greatly expand the experience and richness of the World while staying on the horizontal progression of what a Classic experience.
 **More Faction-oriented Outposts/Cities:** Your faction relations could gain you access to a Hostile Faction's Hideout, in particular to RP in or have an unique Hearthstone to.
 **Opt-Out:** If you like to drop out from the Experience for whatever reason, you can spend 100 Gold to "buy yourself" back into a Friendly standing to all your inherent factions you started out with for the Alliance/Horde.
 **Traditional PvP:** You only make yourself a target to Alliance AND the Horde, if you activate PvP on. With the use of the Challenge thing, you could have an invisible Buff at all time that makes you friendly with every other Neutral player.
 **Unique Cosmetics:** There could be unique cosmetics to buy from Quartermasters of the particular faction. For anyone else who would for example grind up Reputation for the Darkspears they'd have the ability to buy items from the Quartermaster in Sen'jin etc.
 **Wanted:** You could have a Debuff stack up to 3/5/10 if you kill players or NPCs of the Alliance or Horde Faction and if stacked fully, you gain a "Bounty" on your head that makes your body lootable for a Token if a Player kills you, which they can turn in as small reputation boost (we're talking like 25 Reputation) to whichever Quartermaster they turn the Token in.

Either way, this is just some fun stuff I thought others might find fun, too!
