# *UNDO* what has been done to Solliden Farmstead!

## Post 9301 by Daddym0e — 2021-02-06T02:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9301#p9301 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When I first joined this amazing server, the Solliden Farmstead, in Tristfall Glades, had an "Eternal Battle" going on between the Undead and the Humans. That was great because the NPC's kept the human spawns in "check" as it were. A patch or so ago. The Undead NPC spawns were removed, leaving only the **VERY** fast spawning humans. No quest of the appropriate level can be accomplished now without many deaths OR a party. Please! Either slow down the human spawn rate to allow the "level-appropriate" quests to be done without getting 'dog-piled' by respawning humans on the farm OR bring back the little war that was going on there that distracted some of the humans and allowed the completion of quests in a ....timely fashion....and far fewer deaths.

Thank you for your time...
