# Move a class from the T3 Sham/Druid/Pally/Hunter token to another token.

## Post 154293 by Rorymink — 2025-10-04T01:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154293#p154293 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

That's it.  It makes more sense in vanilla where we didn't have paladins and shamans raiding together, but it's just unbalanced on Turtle WoW.

Regardless of class popularity, we inevitably end up with A LOT of players on this token in each Naxx raid, while the wariror/rogue tokens often get no rolls at all.

The three hybrids being versatile and bringing a lot of utility means a lot of them end up in Naxx.  Resto druids, balance druids, and feral druids of both types are wanted for their utility, so we inevitably end up with 4-5 minimum.  Shamans also essentially fill four specs, and we want one in as many groups as possible for totems...  Not to mention, we love having 2-3 enahnace specifically for bloodlust in melee groups.  Same deal with paladins.  All specs are welcome, we want multiple seals/judgments going, multiple auras, and multiple blessings.  Not to mention, they're the first or second most played class on twow I believe, and there's always so many.

We usually end up with less hunters than these other classes, but that's still another 3-4 players every week rolling on these tokens.

Suggestions:

 1) Move hunter to the warrior/rogue token.  Thematically, this makes sense since it puts the non hybrid physical DPS without a healing spec with the other two non hybrids without a healing spec.  Having all three hybrid classes rolling on tokens will still be a pain in the ass, but this will alleviate some of the pressure and at least make the godawful warrior/rogue token more sought after.

 2) Move paladin to the warrior/rogue token.  Thematically, it makes sense having both plate classes on the same token just like all three cloth dps are on their own.  Also, 'sabatons' and whatnot make one think of plate.  Balance wise, it lets us move one of the most played calsses in the game to a token with very little competition.  The remaining hunter/shaman/druid token will still have plenty of competition, and the three tokens would now match much more closely in regards to raid memeber distribution.
