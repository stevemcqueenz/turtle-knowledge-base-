# No repair challenge

## Post 99417 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-07-14T12:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99417#p99417 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is not necessarily a suggestion, just an idea for a leveling challenge.

Challenge: Repairs can't be used, if an item reaches 0% it gets destroyed.
Reason: Turning leveling from an experience where you only look after upgrades and ignore other items into a challenge where you want to stack up back-up items. Itemization experience is not about constant upgrades, it's about always having something to use until reaching level 60.
Potential reward: Take the sprite of the repair bot, make it 5x bigger and change it into a mount. Characters accomplishing this challenge get rewarded by a huge repair bot mount and a free repair bot.
