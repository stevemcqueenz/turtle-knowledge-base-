# Blade of Eternal Justice (AQ20 weapon) got nerfed instead buffed

## Post 74782 by Elisleris (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-10-30T12:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74782#p74782 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

From [Midpatch Itemization Changes Changelog](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10971)
AQ20 Set Reworks:
All set weapons' item level has been increased from 70 to 74 and turned into Uniques, increasing their stats and DPS/Healing Power/Spellpower...

Actually Blade of Eternal Justice got nerfed in comparison of other classes weapon.
Weapon DPS increased but Spell power reduced.
Stats before update:
![Image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/99164611/277928352-6fe5889d-6f1a-43b7-b879-f71073570ad9.png)
Stats after update:
![Image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/99164611/277928485-a0a2d853-7157-4449-b92a-dd4d48548d86.png)

That weapon definitely for protection paladin, because it is one-handed weapon, has 1% dodge. Retribution paly used two-handed weapons. Holy paly will look for weapon with a more large number of healing power.

Prot paladins for tanking used in own rotations abilities like Consecration, Seal of Righteousness, Holy strike, Holy shield (threat from holy damage increased by Righteous Fury). All these abilities scaled with Spell power, only HS scaled with both weapon damage and SP. If check any guide for protection paladin for WOW Classic, it propose to you use weapon with spell power for raid content (Thunderfury is exception).

Testing shown us in result reduction Spell Power despite the increasion DPS, TPS and damage got lowered. Caster weapon with high number of Spell Power show better result nevertheless the fact that they have less DPS.

Please refactore that changes.
Keep DPS as was before patch but increase spell power according to other AQ20 caster weapon.

This really does seem like a strange change, every other AQ20 weapon got strictly buffed in terms of stat distribution. Physical damage is very unimportant for prot paladins, which is the spec this weapon is meant to cater to. Taking away stat budget from spell power to weapon damage is a really bad change and makes this weapon worse than MC tier alternatives in terms of threat generation. Prot paladins care about weapon speed (slower is better as Seal of Righteousness scaling increases the slower the weapon is) and spell power. Almost all their threat gen is from holy damage, so on auto attacks they want spellpower for Seal damage waaay more than physical damage.
