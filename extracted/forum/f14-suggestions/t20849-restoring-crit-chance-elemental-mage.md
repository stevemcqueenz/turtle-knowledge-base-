# Restoring crit chance elemental mage.

## Post 144882 by Profgiggles — 2025-08-12T17:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144882#p144882 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings turtle players.

When the major round of class changes were rolled out, the mage's Shatter talent got a reduction to its crit chance. It used to be 50% chance to crit against frozen targets, but now its capped at 35%. I believe this change was necessary because of arcane mage being completely reworked, however elemental mage (fire and frost hybrid) has taken a considerable hit from it. In order to restore this, I propose adding this missing bit of crit chance to a different talent which is deep in the fire tree. The Fire power talent.

Proposed Firepower Change:

Current: +2% Fire spell damage per point

Proposition:
+2% Fire spell damage per point
+3% Fire spell crit chance against frozen targets per point

Rationale: "Elemental Mage" while a niche build has had its place among some individuals. While the "Shatter" balance (50% crit vs frozen targets -> 35%) needed changing due to the frost tree changes, the shatter change also invariably affected the Elemental Mage build. While it can still be done, the difference does quite notably hurt this playstyle.

The proposed solution (+3% firespell crit chance vs frozen targets per rank) added to the "Firepower" deep fire talent option prevents exploits while restoring the mechanic central  to this build.
