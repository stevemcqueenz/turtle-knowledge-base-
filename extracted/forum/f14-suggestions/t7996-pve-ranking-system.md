## Post 51394 by Drubarrymooer (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-06-26T18:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51394#p51394 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Someone mentioned a PVE ranking system to me in game and I thought it was a good idea, especially since the quality of player has drastically gone down hill.  It feels like its becoming more and more Classic-like every day, which is something I know a lot of us left Classic for Turtle.

Basically, the way it would work is kind of like Uber.  After dungeon completion, the LFT or some other addon would put a poll (1-5 stars) how would you rate your tank/heals/dps.  I'm not sure who would host the data, probably something 3rd party, legacy players, etc.

I feel like this could curtail a lot of the players skirting around GDKPs by taking all the drops in a dungeon.  I'm not against tanks or heals asking for gold for their services, however more than a few fresh 60s have commented to me on how hard it is to find a tank they don't have to pay for.

If people have bad days but are normally good players, stats indicate that they may accrue some number of negative reports due to outliers but overall their score will be positive. If a player has so many bad days that it negativity impacts their overall score in this theoretical. Thats a trend. However in practice the sample size needed to reflect that would need to be quite substantial and I don't know that many people do enough PUG PvE content to gain a representative sampling. But it seems like rating based on identifying those types of trends is whats desired.

Personally I'm not really into the ratings but that's because I don't do content enough in pugs for it to really matter to me as I would never grow my sample to be properly representative. But i can see how it would be useful. Similar to how raiders in Turtle can go to LegacyPlayers to check a players performance across multiple raids.
