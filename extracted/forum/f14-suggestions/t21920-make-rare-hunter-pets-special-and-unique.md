# Make rare Hunter pets special and unique

## Post 151857 by Dark0 — 2025-09-15T19:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151857#p151857 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hunter being my favorite class, I always loved the idea of some pets being harder to get and giving more advantage to the player, and there were quite a few pets with really cool and unique abilities at the start of Vanilla:

Lupos: Auto attacks dealt Shadow damage instead of physical.
Searing Roc: 240 base Fire resistance.
The Snarler: 100 base Elemental resistance for all schools.
Takk the Leaper: 100% extra movement speed / 1.1 attack speed.
AV wolves: 100% extra movement speed.

Sadly Blizzard after 1.12 patch normalised all pets, except for very few exceptions like Broken Tooth having 1.0 attack speed. This being a Vanilla+ experience, I think it would be really cool if Turtle WoW could return some of these pet abilities or add new ones for rare pets. More variety in attack speeds, movement speed, damage, some unique skills or so? Would make playing Hunter more exciting and catching rare pets worth the challenge.
