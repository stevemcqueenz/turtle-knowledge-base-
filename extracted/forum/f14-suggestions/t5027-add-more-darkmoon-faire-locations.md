# Add more Darkmoon Faire Locations

## Post 30326 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-12-07T19:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30326#p30326 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So turtle has made the darkmoon faire up every week, as they move back and forth between elwynn and mulgore. I didn't hit me until later, how cool this change was. In blizzard's old wow, the DMF seemed to dissolve into thin air for 3 weeks, this sucks. With this turtle change, DMF feels like these characters are real people that permanently exist in the world of azeroth. This is great!

However, it's a bit silly that the faire would only travel to just 2 places in the entire world, back to back. To elaborate upon this immersion granted by the change turtle made, I suggest further DMF locations be added.

Here's my quick ideas, you guys probably have better ones:
Week 1: Elwynn
Week 2: Dun Morough, maybe in the brewfest spot, maybe near kharanos, or maybe out in the good side of loch modan
Week 3: Undercity. Could even use custom props to give it a spookier theme to fit in with the zone's vibes, and in lore with the forsaken culture
Week 4: Ratchet, as they take a boat from southshore
Week 5: Thunder Bluff
Week 6: Darkshore (maybe bring out the spooky props again) or Rut'theran

And the cycle repeats with elwynn.

You can see that theres a logic to how they'd be traveling around the world. Delicious RPG flavor.

Sure raiders will be more annoyed when they need to get their world buffs. But this can be combined with my suggestion to add dragonslayer drops to all major cities, [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4987](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=4987) and suddenly this problem is fixed.
