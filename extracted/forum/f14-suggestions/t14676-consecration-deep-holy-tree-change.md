Consecration - Deep Holy Tree Change

Just a thought, could there be a talent, in the deep holy pala tree, that would change consecration to an aoe healing spell?

It could be like the righteous fury spell - switch on, switch off. flipping your choice between aoe healing or the regular consecration spell rather than a permanent change.

The mechanism of a certain sized aoe patch on the ground, and the length of time it's active, would remain the same, except for maybe a colour change to indicate the healing zone more clearly.

Any thoughts?

Disclaimer: This idea is thoroughly undeveloped

It would certainly give healpaladin something else to do than flashing, but i see a potential risk with this.

Ground based effects such as consecration can heal an infinite amount of players, as long as they are standing in it. On fights like Thaddius, Golemagg or any other encounter with decent raiddamage and no positional requirements you can trivialize the encounterby the raw amount of healing such a consecration could produce.
You would have to limit it for those cases if you want it to be useful while still being worth casting in  man content

Anything that makes healpala more interesting to play than he currently is is great, but it also plugging one of hpalas very few weaknesses im not so sure is smart

What if it gets weaker for each additional target you heal? Could have an interesting utility in dungeons and even at raids but if 5+ people stack for heal, it would barely do anything.

Under the 'consec giving less heal for each added targets' I'd mean a fixed healing amount which gets evenly distributed among the members affected by 'consec healing'. Would maybe exclude the holy pala from the effect so that the teammates get a bigger chunk of the heal.

Since it's a consec, it also should HoT in the area just as it DoTs now. But being able to use it on the same targets 1x/minute would be interesting.

## Post 100225 by Daedalus007 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-07-24T03:50:00Z

I'd support a libram being added to make this a thing, rather than a talent in Holy.  Both Prot and Ret (being in melee range) would be better equipped to do group healing and it could distribute a fixed amount of healing among the ~3 lowest health targets in the AoE as an example instead of everyone who stands in it.  Any more than that and it begins to impede on the specializations of other better healers.

Librams being so rare and difficult to obtain that they go for hundreds or potentially 1k+ gold means that a support libram like this as a 'filler' wouldn't be a bad idea even for situational use.

Also anyone who's actually played Paladin knows that 'spamming' Consecration (even at Rank 1) is the single easiest way to be oom within minutes if not sooner.  Not a good idea so no need for extra debuffs or other silliness.
