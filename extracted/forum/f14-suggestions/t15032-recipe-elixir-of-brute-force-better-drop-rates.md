Request to Adjust Drop Rate of Recipe: Elixir of Brute Force to 5%

After extensive testing and numerous attempts, it's clear that the current drop rate for Recipe: Elixir of Brute Force from Small Brown-wrapped Packages is far too low, currently sitting at around 0.7%. This makes obtaining the recipe incredibly difficult and frustrating for players, especially those aiming to collect all recipes. Based on the data I’ve gathered and many others' experiences, this drop rate should be adjusted to the originally suggested 5%.

The Problem with the Current Drop Rate (0.7%)
Let’s break down why this is an issue. If the drop rate is indeed 0.7%, the probability of not getting the recipe in a single Small Brown-wrapped Package is 99.3%. To calculate how many packages need to be opened to have a 99% chance of getting the recipe, the following equation applies:

𝑃(not getting the recipe) = (0.993)𝑛

Solving this, a player would need to open approximately 439 Small Brown-wrapped Packages to have a 99% chance of obtaining the recipe. This is an incredibly high number for a single item, considering what it takes to even obtain one package.

Bloodpetal Sprout Requirement
To acquire each Small Brown-wrapped Package, players need to gather 15 Bloodpetal Sprout. Assuming it takes around 30 seconds to collect one Bloodpetal Sprout, that’s 7.5 minutes per package:

 15 Bloodpetal Sprout × 30 seconds = 450 seconds (7.5 minutes)
For 439 packages, a player would need:

 439 × 15 = 6,585 Bloodpetal Sprouts
 6,585 × 30 seconds = 197,550 seconds, or approximately 54.88 hours of gathering time.
This is nearly 55 hours of pure gathering time, just for a single recipe drop that still isn't guaranteed due to the low drop rate!

Auction House Supply and Pricing
Adding to the issue, there are very few Recipe: Elixir of Brute Force available on the auction house. This scarcity has driven the price to absurd levels, often over 800 gold for a recipe that isn't even particularly powerful. This inflated price not only limits access for players seeking to complete their recipe collections but also distorts the in-game economy by placing an unnecessary premium on a relatively basic item.

Conclusion
The current drop rate of 0.7% makes this recipe unnecessarily rare and costly. To maintain fairness and balance, and to alleviate frustration, I strongly urge the developers to adjust the drop rate for Recipe: Elixir of Brute Force to 5%, as originally intended. This would still require effort but would make the recipe more reasonably obtainable, both through Small Brown-wrapped Packages and on the auction house, ultimately improving the player experience.
