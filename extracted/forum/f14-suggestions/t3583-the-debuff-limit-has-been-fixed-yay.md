# The debuff limit has been fixed yay

## Post 21554 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-08-09T02:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21554#p21554 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Edit: [viewtopic.php?f=63&t=5658](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=63&t=5658)

The raise of the debuff limit from 16 to 24 has done one thing for 40man raiding: allowed a single shadow priest to join raids. The rest have been taken up by the last remaining raidwide DPS-up debuffs, like Gift of Arthas, arcanite dragonling...

What this raise has not allowed:
Crusader Strike, Consecration from Rets and prots
Holy fire from smite priests
Multiple other spriests
Shocks and stormstrike from shamans
Mortal strike, rend from arms
Dot based affliction and fire destro warlocks
Bleeds from feral cats
Bleeds and dot poisons from rogues
Dots from moonkin
Dots from hunters and their pets
Starshards and devo plague racials

Basically, all of the 1.12 meta builds are doing just fine and maybe even better, but only 1 of 40 people gets to play what used to be a meme. The rest are all heavily restricted from doing their full potential dps.

Remove the debuff limit, set it to 100 or 200 or something that will never be reached.

If the dev team is worried about power creep from this, do not worry, the meta builds won't gain anything else from extra debuff slots, with 24 we are currently putting up everything left for meta boosting buffs. Increasing the limit further... now there becomes room for selfish debuffs that dot classes/specs need for better DPS and/or DPM. Meme specs and alternate playstyles are buffed and instantly become vastly more interesting to play. The memes were not brought to raid before, but hopefully in the future they will be, and will end up the same dps as what would have been in their slot before. No power creep here.

If the dev team is worried about the UI not displaying every debuff, consider that both blizz' Season of mastery and the Vanilla+ server have made this exact change, and the UI only shows 16 debuffs. Literally nobody on those servers, cares about that problem, it's a complete nonissue.

Remove the limit, dont let the memes be dreams.
