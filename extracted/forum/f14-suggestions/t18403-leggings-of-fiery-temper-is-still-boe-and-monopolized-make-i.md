# Leggings of Fiery temper is still BoE and monopolized. Make it BoP please!

## Post 125629 by Norovirus — 2025-04-06T02:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125629#p125629 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The leggings of fiery temper are a level 21 green item that hasn't dropped below 300g in the past month! (don't believe me just check wowauctions.net). What level 21 can afford that?

I would be more amenable to it if it was actually supply and demand governing the price, like the rare world or zone drop boe's (think staff of jordan, Pendulum of doom, edgemasters, etc.), but it's not a rare drop, it's a 50% drop chance off of a single mob with a 24 hour respawn that has been camped since its release (over a year ago with 1.17.0) by a few level 60s who log in every day on the dot for the respawn and have some method of always getting the first tag (aggressive pet, target macros, good latency??? Idk honestly, nothing I've tried has worked). The price is controlled by a monopoly on the item, not a supply demand fluctuation. As far as I know, no other high value item in the game works like that. Imagine if tidal charm or arena grandmaster were BoE and controlled by a level 60 that logs in once a day.

Lowest effort intervention to give leggings of fiery temper back to players who actually want to use it would be to make it BoP. Other options would be to randomize the spawn or add in a low drop chance from other mobs in the area. If for some reason you actually prefer to maintain this status quo, the least you could do is make the spawn time something other than exactly 24 hours, so that there is daily variation in the hour that it spawns at, so everyone has a chance to try to get it. Right now if the spawn is while you're at work you're screwed because the only way the spawn time changes is if the server goes down during a spawn, causing the kill to be delayed.
