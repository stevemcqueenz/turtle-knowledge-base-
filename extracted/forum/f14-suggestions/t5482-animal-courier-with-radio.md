# Animal courier with radio

## Post 33614 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-01-31T07:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33614#p33614 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are a lot of quest chains where you need to travel back and forth between quest hub and some place.
For example harpies in barrens. Quest in 3 stages, all of them located in same place.

Thats why I propose special item: Animal courier.
When you use it, you focus on controlling mecnanical courier. It can travel twice as fast as player by foot and can carry 1 stack of items.
It can talk with NPCs, turn in your quests, take new quests and carry rewards to you.
It can't fly or use zeppelins/ships.

In general, it could reduce boring running in the world.
