# Dual wielding on enhancement shaman

## Post 75379 by Haiofor — 2023-11-02T03:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75379#p75379 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Give shamans the ability to dual wield 1h axes, maces, and daggers and make stormstrike hit with both weapons, you could make it another talent adjacent to the 2h weapon talent that enhancement shamans get. Also make both weapons be able to be imbues one at a time as it was in tbc onward, but don't remove the 2h ability for the enhancement shaman either as I know some players enjoy the 2h shaman aspect of the game as well.

## Post 75391 by Jstansberry (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-02T05:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75391#p75391 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is another limitation to dual wield for shamans in a vanilla-based server - weapon enhancements like Windfury are only able to be applied to your main hand weapon.

Dual wield would in theory be a good way to allow enhancement to have decent PvE dps without diproportionately affecting PvP, but the talent tree would also have to altered heavily to accomodate this change. Hit rating is also already hard to get as an enhancement shaman, you don't want to make it even more of a pain.

Other than the issues and baggage that come that come with implementing dual wield for shamans, 2h enhancement also only existed in vanilla and should probably be preserved because of this.

I do appreciate all of the feedback. I do think that hit would be the biggest concern unless you're an orc dual wielding axes. In terms of gear competition, I do see the concern but since turtle wow isnt going anywhere from vanilla the gear concern is really a time thing and not how much drops thing. With the talent and dual wielding spellpower weapons for the ele and resto trees, the simple way to circumvent that is to put dual wielding at the bottom of the tree if it would help with the balance of dual wielding, and you could tie melee hit chance into the talent that deep and i could forsee the problem not being as big as if it was put into the 20-24 talent range, make it like 2nd to last row or even last row. Concerning the 2H enhancement argument and in terms of them being meh compared to the other melee DPS classes, there could be another 2nd to bottom row talent that adds incentive to the 2h weapon playstyle of enhance, like 20% -40% additional melee attack speed while wielding a 2H as a passive could bring in a great deal of a DPS boost and not make it dual wield or bust. I'm sure with a descent amount of testing there could be a good balance of 2H and dual wield in terms of power, the trade off for dual wield could be a more hit based class and 2H more luck/attack speed based.
