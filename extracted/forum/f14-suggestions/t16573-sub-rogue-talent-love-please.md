# Sub Rogue Talent Love, please.

## Post 113013 by Maaliss — 2024-12-17T10:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113013#p113013 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sub Rogues are suffering, especially in PvP as well as DPS.

As a single target DPSer our damage should be much higher than it is but sadly it is lacking. Please can we get an adjustment to "Irritating Agent" or that at significantly increases our personal damage for that short period due to our opponents defenses obviously being less effective.

Also, and possibly more importantly in my opinion, could Smoke Bomb please be changed?

Multiple classes have multiple ways to take us out of and keep us out of stealth through either shouts or AoE effects and then powerful DoTs.

While I don't dispute that they should be able to take us prematurely out of stealth the DoTs that they then subsequently put on us to keep us out of stealth should equally have a counter by us rogues.

Would it be possible to have a Tier 3 or 4 2 point talent that has a 50/100% chance to heal DoTs when we use Vanish.

I certainly don't think that this change would be game breaking but would certainly help rogues more in PvP against caster classes who have escapes, AoE, DoTs and CC which can all be used to counter us rogues.

Sub rogue for pvp has a very standard rotation when 1v1 against almost every class.

Cheapshot -> hemo -> hemo -> 5pt kidney shot -> hemo -> blind -> restealth -> sap -> wait for Cheap shot DR to drop -> re-open with a cheap shot -> 5 point coldblood evisc if the enemy is squishy enough to finish

There's some counterplay that needs to be done around when users trinket, and mages with blink, also some cases where you can't sap, like druids in form, but that is the general opener for pvp.
