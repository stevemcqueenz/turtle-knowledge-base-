# New Leveling Challenges

## Post 14818 by Vittis720 — 2021-12-26T02:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14818#p14818 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is a pretty simple challenge in terms of explanation - Only Questing on the continent that you start your character on. So for example:

The Eastern Kingdoms Challenge (Leveling 1-60 only in Eastern Kingdoms, only avalible to Human, Dwarf, Gnome, High Elf, and Forsaken)
Reward: Custom Title- Champion of Azeroth

The Kalimdor Challenge (Leveling 1-60 in Kalimdor, only avalible to Orcs, Trolls, Tauren, Goblin, and Night Elf)
Reward: Custom Title- Champion of Kalimdor

I don't know how the system would work though. Would it be possible to stop players from gaining EXP when on a specific continent, or after a specific loading screen? Since loading into each continent has its own unique loading screen. In terms of actual execution, I have no idea how to make it work, but in theory and idea I think its a fun challenge and its something that I've done myself many times. (Especially on Alliance which has pretty solid quest chaining and can take you from Northshire to Plaugelands with no problems).

And even though I suggest it be a racially specific challenge,its something I don't totally agree with it. The only reason I suggest it is because that seems to be the easiest and most digestible concept. I think it would be a cool concept for an Orc or Troll to take on a Eastern Kingdoms Challenge, and ring a bell of familiarity from early Warcraft lore, from before the voyage to Kalimdor- something I have also done myself, leveling an Orc through the Forsaken quest chains and throughout Eastern Kingdoms. There is however a foreseeable problem though in the form of Class and Riding Quest, which require you to return to your home-land, and which you would receive no EXP for if doing a challenge on the opposing continent (I.E. Orc Warriors doing their Stance Quest, Dwarf Hunters doing their Taming Quest, Any class doing their Riding quest). So maybe make that a separte challenge? the Foreign Continent Challenge perhaps?
