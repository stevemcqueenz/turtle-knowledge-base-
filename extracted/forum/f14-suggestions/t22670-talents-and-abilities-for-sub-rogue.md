# Talents and abilities for Sub Rogue

## Post 157875 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-10-30T20:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157875#p157875 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I really enjoyed how BC and Wotlk heavily leaned towards sub being the stealth specialist tree. The one who's openers are most deadly and can quickly stealth again. So even though I find the new subtlety tree a bit interesting, I'm mostly quite disappointed with the direction. I am not a fan of how the sub rogue is being pushed as a support class so heavily. A few talents is fine, but his capstone talents are mostly focused around buffing everyone else. In this thread I'm going to propose some new talents. Some original, some from other expansions. Please judge the concepts and not the values (energy costs and CDs can always be adjusted). I'm also not proposing a specific talent tree (not right now at least)

 ****Into the Shadows****
 - Finishing move that causes the rogue to go into stealth mode. Energy cost is lower per combo point. 60 sec cooldown.
- 1 point: 100 energy
- 2 points: 80 energy
- 3 points: 60 energy
- 4 points: 40 energy
- 5 points: 20 energy
 **Notes:**
 - This finisher can be useful for either escaping a fight or using an opener again. It's not as strong as Vanish, because you don't go into improved stealth. Meaning, that unless you quickly create distance, the enemy will still have a chance to intercept you. I've intentionally added a CD, because I'm afraid of infinite loops. The CD may need to be longer, lower or not necessary at all based on player feedback and testing.
 - With this, you can technically go into stealth in 3 quick successions and apply Ambush 3 times. You'll need some thistle tea and there will be downtime, but it's something that only the Sub rogue will be able to do.

---
 ****Shadowstep****
 - Step through the shadows and reappear behind your enemy with improved stealth mode for 3 seconds. Only usable during stealth and does not break stealth. 10 sec cooldown. 25yd casting range. 10 Energy cost.

 **Notes**
 - A much weaker version of Wotlk's Shadowstep pretty much, as it requires the Rogue to be in stealth to use. Due to that limitation, the cd has been significantly reduced.

---
 ****Meditated Assault****
 - Increases energy recovery during stealth by 10/20.

---
 ****Silent Execution****
 - Killing an opponent that yields experience or honor with Ambush will instantly refund 15/40 energy and cloak you in shadows, entering stealth mode.

---
 ****Killer Debut****
 - Your critical strike damage is increased by 5/10/15/20% when the target's health is at or over 80%.

---
Some of these may be too good,too weak or too situational. In any case, some things or similar that I'd like to see for the Subtlety Rogue instead of the current support-ish talents
