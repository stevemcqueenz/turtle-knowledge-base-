## Post 42335 by Mcnair (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-20T13:05:00Z

I do not think that would be a great addition for Turtle, for multiple reasons.
 -From the lore perspective: We are in vanilla timeline, the horde and the alliance are _not_ at war. Assassinating the leader of your neighbouring kingdom is considered a dick move in bird culture.
 -From the player motivation perspective: As I understand, you can't find a big enough population that would do a PvP raid on an enemy capital (and defend it on the other side) for sake of itself, so - like in the case of war mode - you suggest a non-PvP rewards to enlist PvE-focused players. I think war mode has already shown that is very flawed approach.
If I am wrong in this latter, then why not just go ahead and do it as it is already? (Just as with war mode - anyone who wants to do PvP at any times could just /pvp any time. War mode only changes this in the aspect to enlist PvE-focused into the grinder)
 -From the current balance perspective: In Turtle the alliance is multiple times the size of the horde (as it was the case in almost all PvE/RP servers even in vanilla), and we now have the incredibly buffed paladins too. I'm afraid this might lead to many more strifes even within the PvP community. Just do a quick search of the number of recent topics on PvP balance.

It is not the Alliance and the Horde fighting depicted in the intro, but some random orc, night elf, tauren, dwarf, etc. Probably younger generations are not used to the idea to fights happening (border skirmishes, proxy wars, shot down aircraft, etc) without being at war, but it was the norm for a rather long time in the last century. There were a lot of fighting here and there, but there was no war between the powers. Now imagine what would have happened if an adventuring party of one would have assassinated the leader of the other side.
I am very happy that this really important lore point, that at this given time there is a truce between the Horde and Alliance is not overlooked by the devs on Turtle, and even actively expanded. Please check the quite nice new quest line in Durotar, where you have to get explicit okay from Stormwind to attack the remnants of Kul Tiras on the southern isles. The leadership in Orgrimmar is concerned that Kul Tiras might be part of the Alliance and attacking them would lead to a war between the powers.
