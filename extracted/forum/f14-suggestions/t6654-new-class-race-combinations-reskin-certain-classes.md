# New class/race combinations (reskin certain classes).

## Post 41917 by Anonymousfish — 2023-04-17T18:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41917#p41917 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tauren - Totem Warrior (Pala reskin)
The idea is to introduce paladins to the horde, but not as paladins as that might be a bit problematic lorewise. However you could "create" a new "class". That effectively mirrors paladins. Replace holy damage with nature damage, change some effect colors and replace "icons" and spell names to better fit the theme of a magic enchanted nature warrior.

Undead - Death Knights (Pala reskin)
Same concept as above, except holy damages becomes shadow instead. Consecration could for example become Death and decay.

 ------------------------
Class reskins might offer some problems, in that class-specific gear are tailored to the specifications of that class, as such it might not be viable or they might need to add an NPC that transforms pala-gear into custom gear that mirrors that of paladins but suit the reskinned classes.

Undead paladins might work, as they can be priests and they were also once human.
 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Troll - Druid
For some reason it makes sense to me that Trolls fit the druid bill better than Taurens. After all druids were originally a night elf only class back in WC3. Taurens were bloodcrazed warriors, not so peaceful when I think about the Tauren units in that game. Trolls are, if im not mistaken, are also connected to Night Elfs.

Dwarf - Shaman
If the horde gets paladins, the alliance should get Shamans. And Dwarf fits the bill the best, actually the only race that fits it, when it comes to Shamans given the Wildhammer clan.

Night Elf - Mage
Some renegade NELF might want to decide to learn the arcane arts, if they so decided. They might be percieved the same way as warlocks for other classes.
