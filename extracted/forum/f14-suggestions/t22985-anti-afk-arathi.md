As we all know, afking in ab is a big issue, a large portion of the players in ab tend to just sit next to a base while looking at something else on another monitor, this inevitably leads to frustration from the team due to "ninjacaps" as these players don't react to the base being capped as base being capped is only a small visual animation that can easily be missed if wathcing something on another monitor. Due to Gms unwiligness to punish these players, i propse to instead make it easier for these players to react to a base being captured, by having a loud sound be played when standing next to an objective being captured. That way even if playing arathi while watching a movie on a second monitor, one can react to the flag being capped =)
