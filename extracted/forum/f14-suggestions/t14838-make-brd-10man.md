Blackrock Depths is the only Vanilla dungeon that cannot be done as a 10man group - despite being one of the longest/biggest dungeons in the game.

It would be great to expand the player cap for BRD to 10 to match Blackrock Spire, Scholomance, Stratholme, Dire Maul and other end game dungeons to more encourage groups and farming of the dungeon!
