Misc:
Token - Race change (within own fraction)
Token - Fraction change (only available from alliance->horde for better fraction balance)
Token - pet name change (hunter and warlock)

Backpacker - give the rested effect (as from tents) up to level 60
Study hour - one hour in game time when each profecion skill level up reward 2 points instead of 1
Gold seller filter - block any messages with "gold" in it. I would gladly pay real money to block adverticements from gold sellers
Flight path atlas - open up all of your usable flight paths

Skins:
Demon pimping - Small graphical changes for the warlock demons
Pet pimping - small graphical adds to the look of a hunter pet

Mount:
Gnome only - tauren mount

Tabard:
Turtle WoW tabard - design a new, really good looking tabard. Wearing increases reputation gains slightly
Tabard of shame - ugly ass tabard to shame the lazy. If equippped, cannot be removed until the character reach level 60. Increases the leveling speed when worn, but prevent equipping better then uncommon gear.

Pet/demon pimping sounds great. Probably easier for warlock due to the small number of basic models.

However, I am strictly opposed to non-cosmetic donation advantages. I know, portable banks and mailboxes are already a thing but I hope the turtle team will leave it at that. Therefore, backpacker, flight atlas and study hour get a categorical 'no' from me.

Race change is a bit of a mixed bag. It's normally vote this down. The upcoming new race-class combinations and new class racials (e.g. for shamans, and whatever the team might come up with in the future) give people a reason to reroll. With turtle being more about the journey than the destination, I'd say that's a good thing.

However, low level areas are crowded enough at the moment. Maybe giving people a chance to bypass these under some circumstances might be a good idea. I wouldn't use this service, but a race change of an existing character is different from a maxlevel-for-money feature.
