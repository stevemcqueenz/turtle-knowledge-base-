# Don't punish clueless for finishing quests

## Post 52336 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-07-05T05:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52336#p52336 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are a handful of quests in the game you should NEVER finish. The rewards are forgettable meanwhile you get some quest items you would permanently lose access to by finish. Some of these are crucial. If you don't know you these since you are new, you, can permantely get a disadvantage. This is bad design, we can fix this.

Lets see which quests items I mean:
 1. Crystal zin malor
Needed by, druid and warr for example to tank aq20 endboss easily, I breaks the stun that drops threat
Can also be used to die anywhere at will
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13347>

 2. Darols furborb rod
Turns you into a furbolg. While there is a Illusion for that too from winter veil. The quest Version allows you to fake cast as priest and paladin, which makes it usefull tool in pvp you will forever lose by completing the quest
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=5462>

 3. Ashenvsle Master glaive questline water
You get a item that can summon a bowl of water anywhere in the world. An intersting rp item you might want keep access to.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=5251>

 4. Moonstalker closk
Allows to transform into a ghost cat. Only usable in darkshore. While yiu aren't in darkshore often, it's still kinda cooler that just some green reward you will replace soon anyway
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=5387>

Are there more? Probably.

What's, the solution?
Add Dialog options from the quest giver npcs after completing the quest to request these items back. Noone gonna have to be punished for finish something
