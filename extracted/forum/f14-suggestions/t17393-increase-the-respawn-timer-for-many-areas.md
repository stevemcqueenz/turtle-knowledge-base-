# Increase the respawn timer for many areas.

## Post 118751 by Fugus (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-02-02T19:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118751#p118751 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I can understand a really short respawn timer when a new DLC cames out in that area and people are waiting in line just to kill stuff for quests. But the timer is honestly too short in many areas.

I was in a cave for a quest in northern darkshore and the mobs respawned in maybe 3 minutes or so. And with how tightly grouped they were and the fact I wasn't over leveled for the place or anything and killing them took a while, they were respawning about as fast as I could go forward.

Sucks killing your way into a cave only to get killed by a pathing mob you killed 3 minutes prior while you are fighting a pack in front of you. Or having one of them flee to agro a pack of 3 behind you that you killed recently and they are already back.

Not an issue if you can kill them quick enough, but if you are doing a quest where the mobs are 2 levels above you and you have to be strategic and not just charging in, those short respawn timers are brutal.
