# Resurgent Shield Overhaul

## Post 137347 by Apimius (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-28T14:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137347#p137347 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey,

Resurgent Shield looks fun on first glance but effectively its not worth the 4 talent points, neither for a offensive disc build nor for a defensive shield based build.

Here are my suggestions:

Improved Power Word Shield: 3/3  Increases the damage absorbed by your Power Word: Shield by 15% and reduces its base mana cost 15%. (Even if the shield does not break you would not waste as much mana which is good)

Resurgent Shield: 1/1 Reduces the cooldown of your Power Word Shield by 1 second. When your Power Word: Shield is broken, 25% of its mana cost is refunded to you, and the player of the broken shield gains one of the following effects depending on their class/shapeshift: Warriors and Bear Druids gain 20 rage, Rogues and Cat Druids gain 20 energy, pure mana classes and druids in humanoid form gain 100 mana.

Please let me know what you think and if i should adjust some numbers!
