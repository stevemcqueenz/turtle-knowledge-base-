# Accessibility: port the giant fishing bobbler from retail.

## Post 82665 by Nonnoanselmo — 2023-12-25T11:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82665#p82665 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Oversized Bobbler is an item introduced to retail many explansions ago. It can be bought from any fishing supplies vendor.

It costs 2g20s (not adjusted for inflation) and applies a buff that fundamentally makes your bobbler 2-3 times larger, thus easier to click on and to see in the water.

While its use may not be immediate, it was a great accessibility addition to the game as it made fishing possible for people with eye conditions and made fishing in cluttered and grassy areas easier too.

I would vouch for the item to cost no more than 20 to 30 copper and have no sell cost.
