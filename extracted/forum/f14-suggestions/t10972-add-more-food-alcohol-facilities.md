# Add more food/alcohol facilities

## Post 71713 by Reploidrocsa (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-10-17T23:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71713#p71713 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Mulgore spice bread, Darnassian bleu, Dalaran Sharp, Alterac swiss, Southshore stout, Alterac mana biscuit, Hyjal nectar, Dalaran Noir, Darnassus kimchi pie, Badlands Bourbon, Stormwind Brie (and probably many more)...

For those foods and drinks to be available around all azeroth they must be produced en masse!

I want to see those facilities in the world! Most of them don't even need big places (tel'abim went the extra mile for the bananas). What i have in mind is some kind of a medium sized thematic household with a Sign, some decorative elements and a specialized food vendor.

For mulgore spiced bread a medium sized tent with some windmills, water and maybe some crops nearby would do. On cheeseries, having cows nearby in a house or a barn is enough. Hell, even Stormwind Brie could be manufactured in the SW cheesery from Elwyn's cows milk.
Dalaran food and booze can be just mages conjuring them in a small lab like building, Teldrassil would require another cheesery (i wonder where NE get the milk from) for the bleu and some cabbage fields/bakery to make their kimchi pies, alterac defenitelly requires a bakery and another cheesery...

Wow so many locations involving cheese btw.

Anyways, that would be a great POI addition
