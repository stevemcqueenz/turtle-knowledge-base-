## Post 89883 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-11T10:46:00Z

hello, i come with a new suggestions, this time to help with consumables prices.

currently there are 4 different seeds for survival gardening and give food buff consumables
mushrooms (seeds cost 2g in daranssus)
pumpkins
watermelons
berrys

how about extending this, here are some ideas, not thought out (devs will change it anyway), but to get the main idea out

cold plants seed mix (Icecap, Mountain Silversage)
menancing seed mix (arthas tear, plagubloom, groomsblood)
root plant seed mix (liferoot, goldthorn, golden sansam)
each could have a 1% change for lotus too

bascially you buy a seed pack from the gardening npc for a fixed gold amount like you do for magic mushrooms, then you spent time gardening and then you harvest a random amount of random herbs, depending on the seed mix you choose.

this could vastly lower the consumables prices and make gardening a hole lot more relevant and rewarding.
