# Undead priests using holy spells

## Post 140135 by Socniver — 2025-07-17T20:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140135#p140135 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This post is brought upon because of the near release of a certain private server which introduces undead paladins. This doesn't make any sense lorewise. Human paladins worship The Holy Light, and it's their unbroken belief and conviction in this power and the power of good that grants them power. That's why even though the Scarlet Crusade is objectively evil, they can still channel the Holy Light, because they believe they are right and doing good (while being corrupted from within by a dreadlord).

Undead however can't use any holy derived power without experiencing extreme pain. All undead priests lore-wise are shadow, because channeling the Holy Light is sending pain through their whole body. They can actually feel their rotten corpse returning to life. They feel maggots whrithing in their skin, their bone exposed to air. That's why undead paladins are the most ridiculous thing you've ever heard.

But all of this reminded me of the biggest gripe with undead priests. That their holy spells all look ... holy. Blizz could have made the light gray or black or something to represent their new affinities. I realize the consequences of changing the animation to shadow. This wouldn't fly in pvp. But if the devs of Turtle Wow ever want to take a look at Holy Priests, then my recommendation is to make the holy animations gray.
