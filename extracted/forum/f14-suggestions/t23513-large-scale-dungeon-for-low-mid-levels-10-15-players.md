# Large-Scale Dungeon for Low/Mid Levels (10–15 Players)

## Post 164569 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-07T11:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164569#p164569 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Turtle WoW team and community,

I’d like to suggest a piece of content that would fit very well into the Vanilla+ philosophy:
a large dungeon, comparable to UBRS in size and scope, but designed for low to mid-level characters, playable as a 10–15 player group.

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Core Idea

A long, multi-boss instance, closer to a mini-raid than a standard 5-man dungeon

Intended for grouped progression, coordination, and social play

Accessible somewhere around level 30–45 (flexible)

Not necessarily UBRS-themed — only similar in scale and ambition

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Why this would be great

Gives players a “raid-like” experience during leveling

Encourages guild play, rerolls, and grouping beyond 5-man content

Fills the gap between classic dungeons and endgame raids

Makes the leveling journey feel more epic and memorable

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Gameplay Philosophy

Trash and pulls that reward crowd control and planning

Bosses with simple but engaging mechanics

No speed-running focus: meant to feel like an adventure

Flexible raid size (10–15) to stay accessible

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Rewards (Vanilla-friendly)

Loot on par with late SM / Uldaman / Zul’Farrak

Blue items with:

utility effects

resistances

interesting procs or group bonuses

Recipes, cosmetics, or situational trinkets

No power creep — just interesting, flavorful rewards

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Possible Settings (examples)

Large underground fortress

Forgotten titan or troll ruins

Rogue Scarlet stronghold

Ogre, trogg, or naga-controlled zone
(Theme is flexible — the scale is the key point)

![🧭](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9ed.svg) Summary

A large, UBRS-sized dungeon for lower levels would:

Strengthen social gameplay

Add a unique progression milestone during leveling

Stay true to Vanilla while expanding it thoughtfully

I’d love to hear what other players think, and whether the devs see potential in this kind of content.

Thanks for reading ![🙂](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f642.svg)
