# 💡 Suggestion: New Additions to the Survival Profession

## Post 153562 by Dimastasc — 2025-09-28T17:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153562#p153562 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello!
I’d like to suggest an expansion to the Survival profession that would make it feel more authentic and useful — truly about “surviving in the wild” when resources are scarce.
![🪳](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1fab3.svg) 1. Minimal Food from Insects
Idea: Add the ability to craft basic “snacks” made from insects or small critters.
Why: Sometimes a player is stranded in the wilderness without food or access to a vendor — forced to survive on whatever is available.
Effect:
restores a very small amount of health;
applies a minor negative effect (e.g., -2 Spirit for 10 min or -1% movement speed).
Balance: Such snacks would be far weaker than any Cooking food but provide a last-resort option to keep going.
Atmosphere: This truly captures the survival theme — eating whatever you can find, even with drawbacks.
![💧](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4a7.svg) 2. Water Filtration System with Flask
Idea: A survivalist can craft a simple water filtration system (e.g., a flask with a primitive filter made from charcoal/herbs/cloth).
Why: To make raw water from rivers or lakes drinkable.
Effect:
restores a small amount of mana;
applies a minor negative effect (e.g., -2 Intellect for 10 min or -1 Spirit).
Balance: Much weaker than conjured Mage water or Cooking drinks, but it provides an emergency fallback.
Atmosphere: Fits Turtle WoW perfectly — harsh survival with a cost for every sip.
![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Benefits for Gameplay
Expands the Survival profession and makes it more unique.
Provides players with genuine “survival tools” — food and water, but with trade-offs.
Adds immersive RPG flavor without breaking game balance (effects weaker than other professions or Mage conjures).
![✨](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2728.svg) These are small details, but they enrich the gameplay and reflect the spirit of Turtle WoW — a slower, thoughtful, more roleplay-focused version of WoW.

Idea: Instead of simply crafting insect food, introduce a unique tool for Survivalists — a foraging shovel.
How it works:
The shovel is craftable through Survival.
When used anywhere in the world, it triggers a 10-second channeling cast.
After casting, one of three results occurs:
nothing is found (fail);
 65% chance to find 1–4 insects (random amount).
The shovel has a cooldown of 3–4 minutes (or as decided by the devs for balance).
Eating the Insects
This is where the fun begins. When a player consumes an insect:
Possible outcomes (random):
Nothing happens, just a tiny bit of HP restored.
Negative effects:
mild poison (small ticking damage);
dizziness or other debuff.
Positive effects:
Hallucinations — a visual green screen effect + a 15-minute buff granting 100% poison resistance;
or other quirky/random buffs.
(Exact effects and balance can be decided by the administration, but the core idea is that insect eating should feel unpredictable and immersive.)
