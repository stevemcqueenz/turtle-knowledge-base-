# Arcane Mage Ultimate Destruction Spell Capstone: SuperNova

## Post 98142 by Daedalus007 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-06-29T16:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98142#p98142 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Intro
I'm proposing a new Mage Arcane-tree capstone talent.  The ultimate talent that will make every other class jealous and tremble before the absolute RAW UNTOLD POWER of a Mage.

We can use the name: "SuperNova" as a nice copyright-free version.  Brings inklings of Sephiroth's ultimate ability from FF7, the Black Mage ability from 8-bit Theatre, the Kamehameha/Spirit Bomb from DBZ/DBS.  You know the one.

I was inspired by the 'Khadgar' cinematic on the official WoW channel.  A fantastic mega-blast of raw absolute arcane power that has such potency and strength as to one-shot a dreadlord.  I understand that the fire mage 'Pryoblast' is already a fantastic 'one shot' ability, but Arcane arguably deserves such an ability even more powerful than that.  With great power...you know the rest.

Proposal
So my proposal for the new capstone in Arcane tree (Arcane Brilliance and the other linked talents could be shifted up to make room).  The debuffs are a lot because of how powerful the spell is, but this of course can be altered/changed as necessary.

Details
SuperNova
- Cast time of 15 seconds (cannot be instant cast with Presence of Mind aka PoM).

- 2 hour cooldown

- Mana Cost equal to 75% of your total mana pool.

- Damage done is equal to 750% of total mana consumed (simple 10x ratio); ignores any spell resistances or immunity that would normally negate or reduce such damage (including Paladin/Warlock/Priest Bubbles and Raid Bosses).

- Will always crit 100% of the time.
Debuff Caveats
Using this ability applies two undispellable/unremovable debuffs:
- A 75% reduction in all mana regeneration from abilities and spirit for 15 seconds; any spells, items, or abilities that regenerate mana faster will be ineffective (so no mana potions, druid innervate, evocation, etc).

- Hard-capped maximum movement speed where Mage is forced to 'walk' and cannot run or move faster for 15 seconds; cannot use any items or abilities that alter movement speed such as consumables or engineering rocket boots, etc to mitigate this.
Balancing and Thoughts on PvP
I absolutely realize how stupidly overpowered this is.  This is the kind of insanity that Vanilla could very well use to shake up the meta and make players of all classes actually fear mages instead of seeing them as only food/water/portal dispensers.

- For BGs, it is up to the devs if they want this enabled because it would be basically a 'one shot' mechanic; could be limited to once per BG or disabled altogether.

- For Arenas, this could be considered too powerful and could be thus disabled in Arenas (along with any other class abilities with a cooldown over 30 mins such as Paladin Lay on Hands).
Hard-Counter Classes
I would greatly love to see Mages being more of a 'hard counter' to Paladins with this ability and Paladins adapting to the situation with every tool they have available.

Playing Against Mages
As far as countering this ability I feel the lengthy cast time would be enough of a hindrance.  However, if a Mage wanted to hard-stack haste as much as possible to get the cast time down to 9.9 seconds or less, they could insta-cast it with PoM.
To negate this potential workaround, the devs could put a hard-limit to prevent such an interaction between PoM and Supernova entirely but I'd rather they allowed this as the hard-stacking of haste would require some sacrifices in other ways (int/spell damage/etc) and haste as a stat isn't as prevalent enough in Vanilla WoW itemization on mage-equippable items to make it much of an issue IMHO.
