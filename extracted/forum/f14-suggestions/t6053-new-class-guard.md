# New Class - GUARD

## Post 38096 by Bloodline1x9 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-17T15:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38096#p38096 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/Gm01TY0S/Guard.png)

New class? Heh, why not. Dreams come true on Turtle. Actually, this class was always in the game, but it was unplayable.
So let me tell you about this concept. The uniqueness of this class is that he uses a shield, so he is a tank, but also throw as main weapon. He knows how to craft powerful throw weapons for himself, and also receives them for class quests.
Many of his skills are tied to the throw.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/8z6wsKMx/warcraft-icon-19.png) Thanks to this skill, the attack jumps between enemies, which will help keep AOE aggro.

 **[Second Spec]** is a shooter. He does not have a pet, because he is not a hunter, but a soldier.
Instead of all that animal tracking stuff, he just shoots and controls. For solo leveling, he first shoots, kites a bit, and then switches to melee. If he gives up the shield, he has the option of taking **a polearm**. With this he can use knock back ability. This will repel the enemy and stop him, then range-guard can shoot again.

 **[Third Spec]** A fighter with a polearm. You know this is a frequent weapon of the guards. It's not a very popular weapon in WoW, so it will occupy that niche. The main feature of a fighter with a polearm is that his attacks will also penetrate a target that is behind the target of the attack. This makes for a very interesting mechanic. The pole-guard needs to enter from that side of the stand in order to penetrate two targets at once.

I described my idea briefly. But if you like it I will continue.

## Post 38142 by Bloodline1x9 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-18T01:11:00Z

What races can be guards? I will answer - any. Because defenders and warriors are needed everywhere. You can see guards in every faction city.

The first class quest for guards is like any other - a welcome newbies.
The class trainer says:
 *I congratulate you, the future defender of our land. While others are looking for adventures, stealing, committing crimes, fighting for money or learning magical practices, we are doing the most important thing - protecting our home. What could be better than protecting your family from enemies?*

When a Guard up level 10, he gets his first normal class quest. "Bodyguard". This is a follow quest.
Need to accompany the NPS from point A to point B

From Dolonaar (Teldrassil) to Darnassus
From Goldshire to Redridge Mountains
From Khanaros (Dun Morogh) to Gol'Bolar Quarry

From Brill to Steepcliff Port
From Razor Hill to Crossroads
From Bloodhoof Village to Crossroads or to the area behind Thunder Bluff

After completing the class quest, you can choose 1 of 3 items as a reward: shield, ranged weapon or polearm.

 **The next class quest will be at level 30**. Half way.
In it, you will have to defend a village or a Sentry Point from enemies that spawn and attack.
You need to hold on for 5 minutes.
In addition to the reward, you will also receive a guard trinket (first time) and a Guard Badge.
The trinket allows you to teleport to this location.
Here you can go through the quest again once every 4 days and get reputation, some money and a new badge.
But each time the quest becomes more difficult. Therefore, it is better to gather in a party with other guards or ask friends for help in the last levels of the quest.

Badges, of course, need to be collected. You can use them to buy class equipment from a vendor. Or exchange for money. 1 badge = 10 gold.
