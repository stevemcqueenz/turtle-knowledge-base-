# Transmog and quest rewards

## Post 159543 by Lane13 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-11-13T21:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159543#p159543 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Completing a quest should automatically learn all transmogs available as rewards. We shouldn't have to choose between a cool transmog and an upgrade.

Additionally, allow us to transmog any ranged weapon into another. The reason behind this is that most endgame weapons are crossbows and so we barely get to use bow or gun animations.

Ammo can be kept as is or simply make guns only transmoggable to themselves. Adding ammo transmog could also solve this problem, as there's some of them in the game like the AV ice arrows.
