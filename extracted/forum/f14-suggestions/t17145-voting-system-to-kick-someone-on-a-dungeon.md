## Post 117049 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-17T06:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117049#p117049 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A couple months back the team experimented with taking away the ability to kick players in its entirety.
Which had so many bad side effects that they reverted it.

The thing is, if we absolutely wrap every piece of the game oin protective foam so noone can abuse it, the game isnt very practical for it.

Even if you add a votekick system, you then still end with the same problem. But now its not a single bad actor who can abuse it, but rather a group. While better, the exact same behavior can still occur.

These downsides come with a large serversize and a large volume of dps perpetually looking for dungeons. The more replaceable someone is, the more they will get replaced.

So frankly, finetuning lft is futile, as you will always have bad actors undortunately.

I recommend either forming your own groups, or looking for em the old fashoned ways. Even if you fill the last 1-2 spots via lft. You are much less likely to have hostility break out.

I'm generally against it.  Usually the people who are getting kicked are detrimental to the group.  There's no need to vote for it. I admit that's completely anecdotal, but that how my pug experiences have been.  It's also a risk to kick players because it means you have to either 1) finish the dungeon with less people or 2) find someone whose willing to do a partially cleared dungeon.

Considering that people who are team players get to stick around and that there are risks to kicking players, I get skeptical whether someone was truly innocent about being kicked. Voting system complicates things because there are always player who think doing the inconsiderate thing is the considerate thing ("I don't want to be mean kicking this bad player who is wasting 4 other players time") or because there are players who have no idea what's going on (there are many players from different regions of the world on turtle-wow, and while most of us speak English, I've experienced enough language-barriers).
