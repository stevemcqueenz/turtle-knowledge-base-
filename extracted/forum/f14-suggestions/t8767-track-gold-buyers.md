## Post 56582 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-08-09T19:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56582#p56582 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We track all players who are punished internally.

When a player is caught participating in RMT, they are banned for two weeks, their gold is all removed (including what they had earned legitimately), and they are flagged. Their account will be monitored closely going forward to ensure they do not participate in RMT again.

Very few players reoffend, thankfully.
