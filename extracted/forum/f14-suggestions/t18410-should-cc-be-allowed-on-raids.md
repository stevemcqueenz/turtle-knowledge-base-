# Should CC be allowed on raids?

## Post 125660 by Caos (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-04-06T09:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125660#p125660 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Most of the balance issues in late game PVE are related to the fact that most CC is useless in raids and then classes that have more CC in their toolkit become worse than the ones that are just pure damage.
Would allowing CC with some PVP like diminishing returns solve this and let warrior hit harder while other classes being stil useful?
Maybe some mix of stun duration reduction + DR.

## Post 125674 by Wolf of Rage (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-06T09:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125674#p125674 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Let's not make Rogues worse in PvE, alright?

I can see this can be misunderstood due to my poor wording so let me preface: Rogues are... not in a good DpS spot. They are mostly support bots. Which is great. It's unique (look at Sub). But they just suck for DpS in _most_ cases. This would be a bandaid instead and that is not a solution. Not to mention CC in PvE on Immune enemies is likely to cause... a lot of memes in the Raid scene. It's already a meme with speedruns past Vanilla capabilities.

My given example is that Rogues would just continue to be support bots with DpS that is sub-par until give or take BiS with high skill level or at least a bit before BiS with not sweatlord-level tryhardiness. They would just get used for their CC on top of what they have. That is good... but not a good way to look at their PvE-side problems. Plus Pure Classes pumping DpS would also have some minimal CC in their toolkit added which would just... inflate them more, indirectly. Because then they too would be able to do something whilst sacrificing minimal DpS for an occasional GCD or two for that.

That's how I'm looking at it. You can disagree with that, understandably. I just don't have faith in it.
