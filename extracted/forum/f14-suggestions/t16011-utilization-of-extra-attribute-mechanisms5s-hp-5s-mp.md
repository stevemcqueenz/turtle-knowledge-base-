5s/HP 5s/MP
 5-second Health Regeneration (5s HP Regen)
This can be increased to become a self-recovery mechanism in BOSS battles, exemplified by the Nature Wardens in Ulduar. Additionally, for example, Priest healing, Druid slow recovery, Warrior self-healing, etc., and skill property modifications can limit the BOSS's 5s HP regen, rather than using lethal strikes to reduce healing amounts.
 5-second Mana Regeneration (5s MP Regen)
For Shamans, Overload allows active melee and stacking skill releases, where overload reduces 5s MP regen to a negative state/consumption. For spellcasters, for instance, consuming mental attributes for temporary damage enhancement.
This can also be used to create new classes such as Witch Doctors or Sorcerers, or to enhance the skills of support classes.
