# Commanders weapon

## Post 23673 by Kwayver (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-27T06:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23673#p23673 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Remove seal of command and replace it with the following spell:

Weapon Command
 200 mana
Instant cast

Imbues the paladins weapon, causing it to have a chance to strike for 70% weapon damage as holy damage. Lasts 5 minutes.

In order for ret paladins to have reliable sustain in raid (and sometimes dungeon) settings they need windfury weapon for extra swings to proc judgement of wisdom. The purpose of this change to seal of command is to provide the Paladin with a means of performing this without the dependency of a shamen and specific group configuration. It's operational mechanics work the same way seal of command does however it moves the effect to a temporary weapon enchant.
I want to make it very clear that this is **not intended to increase Paladin dps in PvE or PvP** , it is a quality of life change which encourages the optimal play style for ret in the game's current form. If the damage is considered too high then lower it to 60%.

Sanctified command could work essentially the same way, providing dispel resistance to the paladins **active seal**, and refunding mana based on the cost of commander's weapon each time it procs.

Yes there are other options however my proposal fits the existing lore/fantasy/vanilla image of paladin perfectly.

In vanilla wow the only way to get close to acceptable damage was by seal twisting; essentially timing a seal change so you had both effects on at once. this is because Seal of Command was able to proc Seal of Righteousness. Twow doesnt have seal twisting, however if the SoC effect was moved to a weapon enchant it allows to be a reality again without the need for perfectly-timed seal changes accounting for latency.

The main reason for this proposal is to remove the paladin dependancy on WF (so your statement "assuming WF" becomes a thing of the past) and also to remove a redundant seal. There are 6 seals in the game including command, and almost half of them see little or no use. I agree that seals should be more circumstancial, hence why i made the suggested adjustment to SotC. By adjusting SotC it also allows prot paladins to cleave without having to spec too deep into ret for it.

Good MMORPGs design the leveling experience to teach the player how to perform their character's role as best as possible for the best result. Presently the only way to avoid going compeltely oom as a ret paladin while performing the Crusader Strike spam of proc fishing is to have Windfury. Being dependent on another class mechanic will not teach a new player or even seasoned wow player how to achieve best performance in raid or even dungeon settings. The playstyle should be part of the class toolkit.

Finally, I started this thread to get feedback from mods/community about my suggestion. If you wish to suggest your own overhaul of SoC, or another paladin mechanic, you should start a new thread with your suggestion/idea   smiling_turtle_head
