 1. Bag space is an actual game mechanic in vanilla so you're just going to have to accept that fact
 2. World of TeleportCraft? No thank you... Besides there are multiple ways to get around the map at level 60. Some guilds have guild houses and their tabards allow you to teleport to said spots on the map.

 4. The DEVs have already stated that they are unwilling to add summoning stones because it devalues the feel of a big world and although I would like to have the convenience, I tend to agree with their sentiment

As for x2 XP rates, leveling is an actual feature in Vanilla WoW, if you don't like it then maybe stop playing it?
