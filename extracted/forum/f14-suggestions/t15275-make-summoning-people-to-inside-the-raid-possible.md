# Make summoning people to inside the raid possible

## Post 104044 by Monkebonk — 2024-10-21T04:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104044#p104044 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Summoning people to raids as lock is horrible. 90% go inside raids and only a few help out to click outside. PUGs also tend to replace people dropping off and when you do so you have an option to either wait inside to summon them or go outside and summon them. It would be a huge quality of life improvement to do as later expansions did (I think it was TBC already) and make summoning people from outside the raid to inside the raid possible.
