Guardian Totems:
Rename it “Enhance Earth and Flame Totems.”
Increase Strength of Earth, Stoneskin and Flametongue Weapon totems.

Improve Weapon Totems:
Rename it “Enhance Air Totems.”
Increase Grace of Air, Windfury and Grounding totems.

Replace Enhancing Totems with a talent that can help with tanking. Maybe increase threat generated by Rockbiter and all Nature Spells.

Guardian Totems and Improve Weapon Totems aren’t worth putting point in since Windwall totem doesn’t get used and Flametongue Totem gets replaced as soon as shamans get the Windfury Totem.
