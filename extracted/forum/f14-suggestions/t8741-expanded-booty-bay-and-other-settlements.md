# Expanded Booty Bay and other settlements

## Post 56183 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-08-07T20:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56183#p56183 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I always thought that Booty Bay should have been the neutral capital city in Vanilla, it's the most iconic city in the game, TBC has Shattrath, WoTLK has Dalaran, after that I stopped playing retail and don't care anymore, but I think it would be nice if Booty Bay was expanded with more buildings, more NPCs, more quests, more storylines about Pirates and stuff, quests that lead you to Steamwheedle Port, which should also be expanded with more buildings and more pirate-related quests. Ratchet should also get a few more buildings, NPCs and more pirate-related quests.

Booty Bay, Ratchet, Steamwheedle Port, Southshore and all other ports should have a few decorative docked ships or fishing boats that serve no purpose, but make everything feel more busier and alive. Because even the smallest ports in real life have more stuff going on that even the busiest ones in WoW.

Just like how Goldshire got expanded with a few more buildings, NPCs and quests, other settlements should also get some more love. I don't play Horde so I don't know how's the situation with Razor Hill, The Crossroads and other settlements, but they should get this too.

I think this is the perfect way to expand the game in a small and easy ways sideways, but give it more life. It's a lot more work to develop new zones from scratch, but I think it will be easier to just do more with existing ones, in a do a revamp to old zones in the spirit and philosophy of Cataclysm, of course not as drastic and as horrible as Cataclysm, but enough for people to notice the that while the zones are the same, they also feel different in a good way.

Like Jimmy Valmer from South Park says "I mean, c-c-c-cum ... come on!"
