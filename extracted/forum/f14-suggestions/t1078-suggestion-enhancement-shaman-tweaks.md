# Suggestion: Enhancement Shaman Tweaks

## Post 6193 by Heroclastus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-06-03T19:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6193#p6193 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtles,

I'd like to suggest making a few tweaks to the Shaman abilities/talents to make the Enhancement specialization more worthwhile. In it's current state it unfortunately is inferior to both elemental or resto in both PVP and PVE. Also I'd like to revive/incentivize the shaman tank more with these changes.

General Problems with Enhancement:

- Enhancement specialization is the go-to leveling spec for most, but unfortunately not really viable in PVP (too much RNG, elemental is much better and consistent for healing/damage) or PVE raids (debuff slots limit use of stormstrike, sub-par DPS)

- Some Enhancement talents (Anticipation, Shield Specialization and Toughness) are obviously meant for tanking duty but this is badly implemented (no real gear, not enough focus on this aspect)

- Shamans have the least active talents of all classes, the passive milestone talents are in some cases extremely underwhelming and outright boring (yay *Parry)

- Itemization for Shaman non-healing pieces is bad in PVE

- Enhancement doesn't offer any new utility that a Elemental/Resto can't, therefore often relegated to Nightfall Duty (a spec/viability shouldn't revolve around an item IMO)

- the elemental weapon self-buffs aren't balanced, reasons to change away from windfury with a slow or flametongue with a fast weapon aren't very strong

- as a primarily melee-centered spec enhancement is too easily kited

- Stormstrike is not worth a debuff slot in a raid

Proposed Changes:

New Suggestion for the Milestone Talent Line in the Enhancement tree:

- Make 2h Mace and Axe trainable at weapon master for all specs

- make Storm Strike trainable for all specs at level 20 (or even 10 as holy strike) making it  deal bonus damage depending on the shaman weapon buff, windfury->nature damage, flametongue->fire damage, frostbrand->frost damage, rockbiter->extra physical damage. Renaming it to Elemental Strike would make sense as the shaman would use all the elements

- 11 Point Talent --> Improved Stormstrike (replaces 2h Weapons): Now buff the player after using Storm Strike for 10 seconds depending on the element of the weapon enchant. Windfury->10% nature damage buff, Flametongue->10% fire damage buff, Frostbrand->10% frost damage buff, rockbiter->armor penetration buff. The next shock spell purges the listed effect and grants the shaman bonus mana regeneration for a short time. This would make this also somewhat attractive for elemental/hybrid specs for the damage, buff and mana reg.

- 21 Point Talent-->Spiritual Weapons (replaces Parry). Requires 1 point in Stormstrike. Allows you to parry and reduces the threat you generate by 20% unless under the effect of lightning shield. Furthermore your buff effects from Storm Strike also now affect all party members. This would pull double duty as a support and tank talent. Placing it at 21 points makes it impossible to get without sacrificing the other 31 talents (NS/EM) thus giving enhance a unique buffing role

- 31 Point Talent--> Shock Mastery (1 or 2 Min. CD): when activated makes your next shock spell unable to be resisted and grants them an individual effect. Earth Shock-> 5 sec. Silence and Taunt, Frost Shock->3 second root, Flame Shock-> triples the DOT damage. This would work as a mini-oh-shit button which shamans lack and give them some needed on-demand control/damage/taunt.

 **Improved Lightning Shield:**
this talent is rarely taken, it doesn't offer enough. I suggest making it  require 5 points in shield mastery.
Increases the amount of Lightning shield orbs by 1/2/3 and increases your chance to block by 1% for each remaining orb (so up to +6% block with all 6 orbs active). This could support shaman tanking.

 **Toughness:**
this talent only adds 2-10% armor value - not a lot for a mail wearing class. Reduce to 3 talent points as it offers too little return for invested points ATM. Also a possibility could be to decrease duration of slows/roots on you by 17/34/50% or 10/20/30% to help with susceptibility to kiting in PVP. Perhaps someone else has a better idea here :)

 **Anticipation**:
Increases your chance to dodge by 1/2/3/4/5 %. Every time you dodge increases the amount blocked by your shield by 5%. Lasts 30 seconds and stacks up to 5 times.

 **Improved Ghost Wolf:**
Even with 2 points invested this reduces Ghost Wolf to a 1-second cast. I suggest increasing it to 1,5/3 seconds reduced cast time so Ghost Wolf actually becomes an instant cast to help with mobility. Its still inferior to Druid Shapeshifitng as Ghost Wolf doesnt reduce any movement impairing effect.

 **Weapon Mastery**
I'd suggest adding 1-5%  hit in addition to the damage increase. Shamans already have to deal with both spell and normal hit and suffer because of this itemization. This would  make gearing a little easier as some hit could be taken out of the equation and help increase dps by focusing on other item stats.

I personally would immensely love a change to Enhancement. This spec itself has so many things wrong with it and so much potential.

Though much of the suggestions sound great, especially those changes suggested to the weapon enhancements, I would propose some thought put towards having the possibility of *emphasizing* Parry rather than Block.

The reason for this is because I would love to see a Shaman tanking with a two-hander, getting Parry-Hasted enhanced-weapon strikes.  With every parry, your weapon speed has a flat 0.5 sec decrease in its current cycle.  So in this case stacking parry would assist their threat generation as well as their survivability.

Not only that, both warrior and paladin fight over block gear and shields enough.  Adding another class into that mix would make potential loot rolls messier.  I'd be interested to see if this sparks some interest in the minds of the devs, because Shaman should be more interesting than just a Chain-healing Windfury bot.
