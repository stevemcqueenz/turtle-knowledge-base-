# Molten Core mount via reputation only

## Post 169098 by Duids — 2026-02-21T08:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169098#p169098 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Greetings,

The title summarizes my suggestion. The Hydraxian Waterlords reputation is basically useless once you're done with the eternal douse quest. People end up getting exalted after some months raiding Molten Core, but without the typical excitement you get for maxing a reputation bar out since this time there is no reward in sight.

What if the chance to get the Molten Corehound was the reward? I'm not exactly suggesting to make it guaranteed or even easier to acquire the mount. I think it could retain its low drop chance, but somehow restricted to characters who are exalted with the Hydraxian Waterlords (through an exalted-only quest, maybe?). You could even make getting exalted harder, changing it so only Ragnaros and Majordomo kills granted reputation once you're revered or something, since guilds (on my server at least) are skipping these two final bosses and doing only "bindings" runs or "weekly quest" runs.

This change would serve both to give this faction its missing ultimate prize and to help Molten Core retain veteran raiders (good job on the weekly quests btw).
