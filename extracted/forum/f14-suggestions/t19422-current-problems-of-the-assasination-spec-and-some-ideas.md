# Current problems of the Assasination spec, and some ideas

## Post 132021 by Kallegan — 2025-05-19T23:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132021#p132021 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The changes made to Assa's specializations, in particular the scaling of poisons and the buff to envenom have been very appropriate and have made the spec viable, yet it still seems to be below combat in most situations. Among the problems I find:

- Assa lacks the burst of adrenaline rush, and lacks a form of cleave, whithout getting anything in exchange.

- Starting SnD + Envenom requires time and combo points, making it difficult to use in trash.

- Seal of fate is a talent that does not have much utility, as SnD and envenom increase only the duration but not its damage, becoming meaningless to the use of multiple combo points, since the added tiem doesnt give enough time to use Eviscerate.

- Vigor feels like a very minor talent, having 2 points in it is useless, and the cd of the energy proc causes it to have little impact.

- Throwing weapon specialization is limited to throwing weapons, which are rare.

- The buff to utility poisons (crippling and mind-numbing) is welcome, but requires losing 1 poison slot.

- Relying so much on poisons implies that when they are unusable (most of MC and several Kara40 bosses) the specialization becomes unviable (Corrosive is useless in short fights)

- Poisons are not affected by crit, making the stat less useful.
Regarding ideas about what can be done about it, most of these ideas revolves around talent changes:
- Increase the damage of the spec on single target, making it the "single target specialist" of the three of them. This can be done adding some kind of bonus for attacking the same target, this could be tied to the amount of combo points on the target to give some play to Seal of Fate, or simply adding a x% damage modifier after attacking the same target for X amount of seconds

- Allowing vigor to keep combo points on the player for x seconds after the current target dies, this would allow the renewal of SnD and Envenom when changing targets.

- Vigor should be buffed on his current iteration, or changed in some way to make it impactful (see above)

- Adding some high lvl throwing weapons between MC and Naxx

- Allowing the use of a third poison on the throwing weapon, tied to the efficient poisons talent. This would allow some sort of utility, or even ranged damage on limited situations, all while maintaining the focus of the spec as the "master of poisons". This third poison could be triggered with eviscerate, or deadly throw, or ghostly strike, to make it the "situational" poison of the kit.

- Adding more poisons, the most important one being an "instant corrosive poison". Currently Assa deals the most damage with instant poisons on both hands, and instant is the top choice for really short fights (like trash mobs), but this doesnt work in certain scenarios (MC, Spellbinders, Twins, Anomalus, Chess), making the spec way weaker.

- On top of that, there is some room to add different kind of poisons, or more effects, unique to assa. I.E: Corrosive reduces armor by X per stack, Deadly increases nature damage by X%. Currently periodic poisons are currently less useful than instant for assa except for extremely niche cases.
