# Clarity issue for the quest “Repowering Chaser”

## Post 159955 by Elvendheim — 2025-11-18T03:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159955#p159955 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I had a problem (and I'm clearly not the only one, after doing some research) with the “Repowering Chaser” quest. We are asked to find a “Gold Power Core” at “Livingstone Croft” without any further details, except that... It's impossible to find one in the boar field, or in or around the two buildings located there, and... there's no information on the Turtle forums, and the internal Turtle wiki is equally lacking in information on this subject...

So, I spent a really long time looking for the solution to Livingstone Croft, searching houses, the surrounding area, killing lots of wild boars, looking for information on the internet, on Turtle forums, asking other players who were just as lost as I was, etc,and...

And finally, just like that, I tried something and, after hours...

I ended up finding the solution (which was really not obvious given the text summarizing the objective): You have to retrieve the famous Gold Power Core from an engineer (or at the auction house).

So, rather than summarizing the objective as:
“Collect a Gold Power Core for Chaser at the Livingstone Croft in Arathi Highlands”
You should write:
“Collect a Gold Power Core ***from an engineer*** for Chaser at the Livingstone Croft in Arathi Highlands”.
