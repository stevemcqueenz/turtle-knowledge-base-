# Suggestions on Hunter Class Changes

## Post 13906 by Celaris — 2021-11-16T00:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13906#p13906 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone, I really like the new hunter changes, but theres still 3 big hunter problems i'd like to suggest fixes for as a long time hunter player. On the server my hunter's name is Ornstein.

 1. Hunters have really bad mana problems. During long fights its pretty common for me to run out of mana even in end game gear using major manas and even tea with sugar. There could be a mana reduction across the board on abilities or a clearcasting type talent that could help slightly. Maybe a talent that restores mana to you when your pet attacks or when you deal critical strikes? TBC solved this problem with returning mana on critical strikes talent & aspect of the viper.

 2. A hunters aoe sucks for a main DPS class. Traps & volley are virtually worthless to use. They don't scale with anything (except volley scales with spellpower but who builds a spellpower hunter lol) and cost a hefty chunk of mana. Some attack power scaling and maybe a mana reduction them would be nice. Maybe even a level 60 version of explosion trap & immolation trap that does more damage would be a good change since those cap off at level 54 and 56 respectively. TBC solved this by making traps scale with attack power & adding more ranks so hunters could have a decent aoe and even allowed traps to be used in combat, but added a 30 second cd to them instead of 15 to replace feign death trapping, which imo is pretty clunky and doesn't work 100% of the time. I don't think scaling these would make hunters overpowered either because they still would not have as good as mage aoe since the traps & volley have pretty long cooldowns. Hunters are a pure dps class and I feel like they should bring both good single target and decent aoe damage as all they can do is bring damage to a party.

 3. The pet avoidance talent is a very nice addition, but it doesn't solve one of the major problems hunter pets have with survival - people just don't like to heal hunter pets, heck some people don't even have hunter pets on their raid frames. It's not even a good idea for a hunter to use mend pet mid combat because not only does it waste precious DPS time since its a channel, but it also costs a huge chunk of mana to use. TBC solved this problem by making mend pet an instant cast that added a heal over time to your pet over 15 seconds instead of 5 and halved the mana cost.

Thanks for reading this wall of text and I hope you guys agree with some of my suggestions!  smiling_turtle_head

On the Manacost topic i can not agree.

Trueshot costs 5% base mana. Hunters have about 2400 base mana. That is 120 mana for a spell that deals weapon damage + 125. That is extremely cheap and quite easily sustainable in a raid environment if you have blessing of wisdom and judgement of wisdom.
The only hunters with mana problems are the ones using serpent sting. Serpent sting costs double the mana of trueshot for less damage.

And even if you go oom as a hunter, it is not that big of a deal compared to most classes. you still have quite a bit of auto attack damage at your disposal. If you want even with melee weaving.
If you are minmaxing, you won't go oom, and if you don't minmax, you shouldn't care about your dps dropping off a bit when you waste mana.

The only cases where i do see manacost as a potential issue is with Tranquilizing shot, which can be quite a strain on hunters mana. But then again, this is used in fights where tranqshot and the hunters mana is a core aspect of the fight difficaulty.

It also has to be said, that similarly to enhancement shaman and ret paladin, if you completely forgo mana in your gearing choices, having to manage a constrained manapool is part of the gameplay.

On the Salvo topic however i have to agree, Salvo is rarely if ever worth casting and you are in the overwhelming majority of cases better off just autoattacking and tab targeting. Which is a shame, the spell is quite flavorful. It should either have higher base damage or a attackpower or weapondamage scaling. And i think making it worth casting in the first place is highest priority, the cooldown is a secondary concern.
