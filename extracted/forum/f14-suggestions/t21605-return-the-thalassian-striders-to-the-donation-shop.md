Well, it's simple, the title says it all.

Can they come back?

Personally, it was fun, and I love those chocobos.

They could also come back in all the colors and armor they had in TBC, since Hawkstriders are essentially part of Thalassian culture, in addition to being, as their lore dictates, an essential part of Quel'thalas' fauna. Aside from unicorns, Hawkstriders are a beautiful mount.

It would even be awesome to have some way to purchase them in-game, perhaps through a quest line like the Winterspring Tiger or the Un'goro Raptor.

I bought the Victorious Thalassian Hawkstrider with my main a few months ago and I love it, I was planning on buying the scarlet one for my Scarlet Crusade Elf Paladin and I saw it disappeared.
