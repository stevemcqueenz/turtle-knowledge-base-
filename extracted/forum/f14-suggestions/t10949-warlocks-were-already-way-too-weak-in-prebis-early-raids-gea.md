# Warlocks were already way too weak in prebis/early raids gear and 1.17 item changes made it worse

## Post 71357 by Kaiben — 2023-10-16T22:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71357#p71357 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

TL:DR
Devs had a nice idea to nerf the prebis and up the t1/t2, but **with 1.17 warlocks now start 2 steps back and will only go 1 step forward with the first raids**. Which put them in a worse position than retail were they were already the worst non-meme dps class.
Adding hit to t1 is imo the most simple and effective option.

Warlocks have always been weak before getting BWL/AQ gear, and it's still the case on turtle. **Lack of hit rating is the main reason for that.** I'm going to focus on all the gear you can get before : dungeons, crafts, MC, ZG, Ony and Silithus summons.
It's not an arbitrary line I'm drawing to fit my points, the devs themselves decided those changes to make those raids actually worth doing and give a proper sense of progression.

When the 1.17 itemization changes were announced I was really surprised to see all the good hit options getting a nerf (turban and bloodvine) and the buffed t1 pieces not becoming better than the previous option, except belt and bracers that you don't even need to raid to obtain.

I asked people in charge of those changes and I realised they "think" they actually buffed early warlocks, so I decided to go into details to make sure it's not me whining because I feel my beloved class deserves more.

I'll put the gear detail below, **but when equipping the current bis, the changes compared to previous bis are : -7% hit, +2% crit, +51sp.**
If we go by the debatable 1 hit = 15sp, 1 crit = 12sp, **the total is the equivalent of -40sp, the dps more rng and spikey than my cryptos and the banishes 7% more resisted.**

The perfect exemple symbolizing the changes, if you don't care about details, are the legs. Previously you'd wear Bloodvine (+1hit, +36sp) but now the t2 from Ragnaros, Nemesis Leggings (+40sp). Losing hit for a bit of sp.

When it come to the suggestions, I know changing talents to provide hit would have an effect on all tiers, might be a nightmare to balance and could create other inequalities. Since locks do fine in end game, **the simple way would simply to buff t1 even more, especially by adding hit and changing those laughable set bonuses.**
Focusing on hit is also a way to not create pvp imbalances since the cap is 4%.

By buffing a bit more the t1 gloves, they'd finally become better than the crafted felcloth gloves, and the bis gear would include 5 pieces of t1.
Which means the 3 and 5 set bonuses would be active, and instead of getting a bonus to drain life (lmao) and pet survivability (lmao), we could get hit/hit or sp/hit.
The rest of the set could also give less stats and sp, and give some hit instead.

To be honest, you could add +1% hit to every t1 pieces' current value + actual set bonuses and the locks would still be below paladins, warriors, rogues and hunters. But for some reason it sounds absurd even to me to ask that locks could come close to compete, that's how **cucked** we are.
