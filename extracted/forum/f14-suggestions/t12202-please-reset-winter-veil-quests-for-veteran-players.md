# Please Reset Winter Veil Quests for Veteran Players

## Post 81138 by Oolaatris — 2023-12-14T05:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81138#p81138 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I remember last year during Winter Veil there was a reset of quests so that people could do them again who had done them before.  This year I was disappointed to find that I could not re-do the quests, I assumed it was a bug and submitted a bug report but was told that it was not planned to reset the quests for players to be able to do them again.  I feel really disheartened by this choice and would ask the team to reconsider.  It feels like I can't be part of the festivities this year, even quests like the quest to hunt down players cannot be picked up even though the players have changed this year.  I was really looking forward to this seasonal event because the custom Turtle WoW version is so much fun.  Additionally, the festive winter outfit is awarded as random pieces from gift boxes from the quests, because I was unlucky last year in what I randomly got (a bunch of hats) I can now never complete the outfit since I can't do the quests again.  Seasonal events in WoW have always been repeatable every year, to be excluded because I did the quests a year ago feels really bad.  I have many friends in game who joined since last Winter Veil who will be doing the quests and not being able to participate feels like being punished for having played on on this server for almost 2 years.
