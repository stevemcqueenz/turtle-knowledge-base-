# Outlaw Mode

## Post 28670 by Valadan — 2022-11-07T19:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28670#p28670 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think the debate about PvP on this server is varying, I'm not here to open it up, I just wanted to share an Idea that I had that seemed like an interesting thing, and based off of some really cool experiences I've had in the past.

Has an Outlaw Free-for-All Mode been discussed? Like whenever you're not in a Rested Area, you'd get the Free-for-all PvP mark, and you could duke it out with whoever also had this mark? This is would be a way of incentivizing PvP for those who want it, and also make it so that Tents would have more use out in the wild for those who would be Outlaws. World PvP wouldn't be limited to just ally v horde. The idea is that it works just like the Free-for-all so you can only attack those who have it as well, it's a much more extreme choice than Warmode, and since only people who partake in being "Outlaws" or "Neutrals" could take it, it's not like they could grief people who don't have it activated.

Warmode for me is really cool, and I would actually activate it even if it didn't give any resources. I don't get mad when people gank me and I tend to just talk things out civily with people who are being grieffy (to varying results). Most of my friend group is the same, and we constantly go out and try to find World PvP, and just goof around. We have a code of never camping or griefing, but it's very Rare to find people willing to partake even though we whisper them and tell them to get a group going and do some skirmishes. I'm pretty sure there's a small percentage of us that would like to do the same, problem is, we're separated into Ally and Horde, this is why our own solution is to go to DM or Gurubashi and duke it out in between us, which is really really fun, but it is ultimately just us.

Now, I don't know if this would be a permanent thing, maybe it should be a 1 Ingame Hour effect (Just like the Trick-or-treat) one that you could activate in a City or at a certain NPC, or a Glyph.
