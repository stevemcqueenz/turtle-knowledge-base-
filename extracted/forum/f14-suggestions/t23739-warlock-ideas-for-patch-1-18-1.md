This is just a list of ideas for the Warlock that might give others better ideas than mine. Or that could work in helping specs in the warlock tree.

Ideas for Destruction. ------------ ( Keep the Damage of Time Theme of Warlocks with Destruction )

 -Since they want to keep Searing Pain a short cast time why not add a Damage Over Time effect that stacks 3 or 5 times?

 - Talent Improved Searing Pain
 -  adds a Damage Over Time to Searing Pain that stacks up to 3 or 5 times.
 - ( They could also add some sort of interaction with Soul Fire with a later investment into Improved Soul Fire. )

 - Talent Cataclysm
 - Also increases periodic damage of Fire Spells. By  a %

 - Again with Dots... Why not add some to Rain of Fire?  Or Fire debuff on the ground. Or..

 - Talent Emberstorm
 - Increase damage by your fire spells stays the same
 - Adds a Damage Over Time to Rain of Fire that stacks up to 3 times.
 - Or creates a Burning circle on the surface of the ground that increases Fire Damage taken by mobs inside it that remain in the circle.
 - Or Rain of Fire damage increases the longer it is channeled.

 - Soul Fire could have Interaction's with new DoT of Searing Pain

 - Improved Soul Fire
 - Add the ability to consume Searing Pains Damage Over Time Effects and release them instantly upon Successful landing of Soul Fire. - ( This would add to the bursty theme of Destro.
 - Or Add the ability to increase the Critical Strike chance of Soul Fire by a % increasing with the stacks of Improved Searing Pains DoT's.

Demonology Tree -------------------- Ideas

Add a few more effects to the Stone Abilities.   ( These would half to be adjusted/ balanced for Talent Improved Stones )

 - Felstone
 - Add % increase to Haste.
 - Or add mana cost reduction for Shadow Spells.
 - Or (chance on hit effect) add small amount of Shadow damage to basic demons ( Very small amount, ( less dmg than Firestone wand effect )

 - Firestone
 - Add (chance on hit effect ) add a small amount of Fire Damage to basic demons ( Very small amount, ( less dmg than Firestone wand effect )
 - OR - Fire Damage over time effects get % more Damage increase.

 - Voidstone
 - Add Basic Demons get 5% reduced damage.
 - or Basic Demons get + Damage increase by % or Scaling attack power of some sort.
 - Add effect Greater Demons and Enslaved Demons don't break as easy. Stack's with Soul Link for More Greater Demon uptime.

 -Talent Improved Stones
 - What about moving this talent to the 3rd row?
( Could make this talent enticing for other Specs like affliction or Destro ) ( Could be a little to powerful, some abilities might need to be lessened. ) Thought it would be to helpful to Destruction in particular with increase Critical Strike Chance.

 - Talent Soul Link
Since its Damage Reduction is going from 25% to 20% why not Increase Damage from 5% to either 8-10% ?

Affliction ---------------------

 -Dark Harvest
 - Also reduces mana cost of damage over time spells by % ?

These are just some ideas I thought I would throw out there. Obviously some seem very overpowered. Of course they could decrease or increase values to their liking. Just wanted to put some ideas out there especially for Destruction and Demonology. Would love for those specs to be more enjoyable and effective. Of course pvp is going to half to be thought of with some of these ideas. What are your ideas for Warlock?
