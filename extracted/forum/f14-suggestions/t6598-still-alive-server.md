With the increase in online, I often began to hear from Hardcore players, and I myself have come across more than once, about the problem of serious competition in quest spots.

STILL ALIVE server.
On this server, ALL players will participate in the hardcore leveling challenge. All the rules of hardcore pumping will work here as well.

At level 60, the auction remains unavailable. Which will complicate the preparation for the raids and complicate their passage. But it will increase the interaction between players, which for Hardcore players already works differently than for Regular players.
Mail will become available at level 60.
