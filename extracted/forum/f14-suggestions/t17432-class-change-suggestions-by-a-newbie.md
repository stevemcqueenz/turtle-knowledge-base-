# Class Change Suggestions by a Newbie

## Post 118982 by Oinkman — 2025-02-05T02:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118982#p118982 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there!

A little heads up: i'm kind of a newby, so it might be that my suggestions sound straight up insane, but since i can't tell due to missing experience, here they are:

 1.  "Titangrip" for Warrior Tanks, as their elite. what i mean with this is a talent that allows to equip a shield alongside a 2h weapon, which might work with another 10-20% weapon dmg reduction. the reason why i think this would be nice is, besides 2h weapons giving more stats, it would give warrior tanks something unique and personally, i always wanted to play a 2h weapon+shield (besides it would enable roleplaying as a spartan) and if you move the arms talent for specific 2h weapon buffs (like range for polearms), i could imagine it being quite funny if that would apply to abilities as well (maybe even taunt) making tanking more relaxed.

 2.  Turn BM Hunters or better Demonology Warlocks into tanks. alongside enhancement shamans i always felt like "making the pet play tank" should be somewhat possible. Demo-locks even have a talent that increases dmg dealt and THREAT GENERATED while succubus is up (which confuses the hell out of me, like why does this exist? as a dps, i dont want to cause more threat, do i?) to make this work, i'd recommend a reverse version of the shaman talent, that makes them take 60% of the totems threat, so that the pets get the aggro the player causes, with warlocks you could make like an automated health funnel,that (for example) makes the locks and pets HP even out every second, so the healer still heals the lock and it would also kind of fit and not be much of a hustle for healers to suddenly look after a pet (might as well just focus it around the current existing health funnel, but not sure how much adjustment would be needed and lets be honest, it would be more comfortable and other options are still possible, like having simply the lock take the pets damage after a certain percentage, but let the damage be reduced by the pets armor AND by the locks armor as well, that would make them tanky, their pets could tank and the WL would be the target to heal, give it a talent to transfer like 60+% of threat generated onto the pet and maybe give it another one to increase threat generated (or maybe another version of demon armor that works similar to warriors defensive stance?) and they should mostly be good to go)

 3.  would it be possible to make a ranged build for rogues? like based around throwing weapons maybe? not much of a rogue player myself, so very unsure about this one and i don't want to mess up anyones favorite class/specc, but i feel like some classes/spec actively using throwing weapons is kind of missing and rogue would be the most fitting i guess.
 3.5.: also a racial skill for trolls with the same idea would do, honestly, give them auto-throw by default and let them maybe learn 1 ranged attack skill as racial, i think that would be logical and not shake up meta too much.

 4. Buff Early level Enhancement shamans please! their tanking in itself is ok and managing threat isn't much of a problem either, the big issue is Mana, usually one runs out shortly after pulling a group and need mana breaks every pull, idk but i saw others with similar problems at those levels, so maybe move water shield to a lower level or at least the magma totem? having only fire nova totem as early aoe is kind of a handicap considering how much mana it costs and how impractical it is with its delay, the other spells aren't very cheap either and idk, maybe full caster shamans can handle these mana costs, but without caster equipment you most just use normal attacks and an earthshock here and there, hope it gets better with the other spells, but playing till 26 under these conditions was rough.

 5.Arcane Mage Heal -  idk, but i feel like arcane mage ain't that great currently, (frost might also need adjustments for PVE, if intended to be played there, but arcane looks like... does this work without stressing the hell out?) and since mage already has 3 DPS trees, why not make it a Support/healer? yes i know, mages healing might sound weird, but tbh, arcane magic consists mostly of gimmicks/usefull stuff like food and water, portals and sheeping, so why not? to have it somewhat make sense, i'd say a little excurse to chronomancy might make sense, give arcane mages a spell like "rewind wound" that has most of its healing scale with damage taken (within the last few seconds) and a max bonus as well as a base amount, so leveling it will be worth it, add to that a hot (which might, simply for fun, be a spell like "summon applied bandages") and let them buff their mana barrier on allies instead of themselves and we got a new heal/prot. obviously this is only a rough idea, but since healers are always rare, i feel like another heal spec is needed and arcane mages kinda makes the most sense to me. to make it more unique, one could add buffs that for example allow a player to ignore AOEs for a few seconds (like in a sense of space manipulation) and have their mana restoration be based around their current damage spells (like arcane missiles and -explosion restoring mana for each time they hit, but dealing less dmg, so they would have something to compensate for the loss of mana from buffing the tank with mana barrier, which, as long as it is enough, could even speed up runs and with proper balancing it could fit in just fine and would also benefit mages that play DPS specs as they would have more options to restore mana in combat)

(6. not sure if that fits here, but i'll add it just as an idea) i saw that one of the FAQ is about new classes, races and a possible next addon and i thought, what about, if that would be possible, adding another talent tree to every class? i know it might be hard to come up with new "identitys" for all classes, but i think it could be very interesting and would open many possibilities, as well as maybe balancing out the roles a bit more. and if you think this would be a good idea (in case that would ever happen of cause) what do you think would be specs you'd like to see for what class and what role would they fill? honestly curious what people think about it.

Anyways, since i'm new i don't really expect much positive feedback, but i'd still like to hear what you think about it and if only one (preferably the first as this is a more personal request by my side) of my ideas finds its way into the game, i'd love to see that :D
