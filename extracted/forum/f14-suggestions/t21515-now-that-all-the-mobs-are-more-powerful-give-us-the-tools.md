# Now that all the mobs are more powerful, give us the tools

## Post 149075 by Partyboyza — 2025-08-29T06:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149075#p149075 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since all the mobs have become more powerful at the very least give us some tools to invest our equipment slots.

And yes I'm mainly referring on the mob damage aspect, we need tools to handle multiple mobs like "reduced damage from all sources" in the following form:

 1. Amor patches
 2. Shield enchants
 3. Rings
 4. Trinkets
 5. Potions / elixirs
 6. Food buffs
 7. City buffs

As it stands right now, the only source for this effect is from the blessing of sanctuary which sadly is only available to protection paladins.

The "vampirism" stat has proven to be quite the successful addition to the game which would open the door to a more open minded direction in terms of future development.

Allow the community to chose how they want to play, through offense to kill single mob fast or through defense to slowly kill multiple mobs at once.
I for one enjoy the power fantasy of being a tank, an almost unkillable mountain of meat, I might not do enough damage or threat, but when it comes to surviving the hardest hits I'm there for my team.

And for anyone complaining I want to point out that dungeon aoe farming at low levels was part of the vanilla experience.

Thanks for taking the time to read this.

I may be wrong, but it seems to me TW has for a long time been buffing players and objects, and as a result the game had become increasingly easy, and unbalanced, and PvP had become problematic (players doing too much damage too quickly).

The changes to mobs are an attempt to bring the difficulty level of the game back up.

It seems to me then to ask now for *more* power, to compensate, it really to ask for an easy version of vanilla.

To the extent that is the case, it's a reasonable ask, but for example, speaking for myself, I think TW should be getting harder over time, not easer - players and objects should generally be being nerfed, not buffed.
