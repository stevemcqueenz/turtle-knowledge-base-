# Nerf Lasher Farm

## Post 89793 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-09T20:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89793#p89793 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it's been suggested many times but I think this change is necessary because in the long term it's destructive for economy. Lasher farm should be nerfed. I know this change would be controversial because people claim lasher farm brings many herbs in to the market which greatly influence herb prices at AH so they aren't that expensive. In my opinion it's the most positive thing about lasher farm.

However, there is only one single problem with Lashers: they drop 3 valuable gray items which generate gold out of the air, thus this farm cause inflation for the server in the long term. You might say that if lasher farm is nerfed, people will find new farm which is more efficient - that's true but as far as I know lasher farm generates the most raw gold in the entire game. Every other farm is not as destructive as lasher one in this case.
Gray items I talk about are:

- Lasher Root - worth 17s28c - 10% drop chance - drops 1-3

- Thorny Vine - worth 30s70c - 7% drop chance - drops 1-2

- Serrated Petal - worth 61s42c - 3% drop chance - drops only 1
Lasher farm is especially destructive for economy in Turtle WoW because unlike in other vanilla private servers, you can donate the owners and get summonable vendor which don't stop your farming inside DM. In other servers if your bags are full, you must move to vendor outside DM, I think goblin at the coast is the closest one. But here you summon vendor and sell all gray items - gz you generate free gold. What's funny is that it's actually P2W element because if you don't donate to the owners, you won't get access to the vendor unless someone else summons it for you outside. So currently in order to farm lashers at the most efficient pace, you must pay real money.

My suggestion is to remove these 3 items from Whip Lasher loot table. I suppose this would hurt this farm a bit because 1/3 of all gold in this farm is raw gold got by selling vendor items, even more on Tel'Abim. As compensation some in-demand herbs drop chance should be increased a bit. Plaguebloom could be added to the loot table.
