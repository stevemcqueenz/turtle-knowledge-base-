# New Gnome Racial

## Post 116044 by Evilbitty — 2025-01-09T22:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116044#p116044 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The current Gnome Racial-Disassembler: 5% increased damage versus Mechanicals-Feels like a rushed idea and lacks creativity...and is completely useless to boot. Here is my suggestion for a new Gnome racial that is fitting for their in-game lore and is quite good-but not game breaking:

"Polymath: Allows the Gnome to train up to 3 primary professions (instead of just 2.)"

Why it works: Gnomes are known for having big brains and this is a great expression of that. This racial adds some good flavor and makes them an attractive choice for players without being OP.
