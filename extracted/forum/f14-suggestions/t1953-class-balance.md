While I get what you are comning from, being a meme/hybrid player myself, boosting a ''jack of all trades, master of none'' can be risky, unless something else is nerfed/tuned., sometimes.

Currently on Turtle most specs are viable, but some of them require more dedication and work than others.
There are four categories in which a spec falls off in my oppinion:

 1: Too mana hungry.
 2: Overall low damage output.
 3: Too mana hungry & Overall low damage output.
 4: Poor threat management (No taunt or high threat generating spells, like shadow priest).

So far, the only spec I have not seen work on turtle, is arcane mage.

 **That being said**, having more than 16 debuff slots would certainly boost some of the other specs, without a doubt.
Heck, even adjusting a spell like [Curse of elements] would give Ele Sham and Bala Druids a little help.
