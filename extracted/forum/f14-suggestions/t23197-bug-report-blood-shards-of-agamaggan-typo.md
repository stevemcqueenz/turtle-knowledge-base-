Location : Camp Taurajo
NPC : Mangletooth

ISSUE : Typo in the Quest text when talking to Mangletooth in The Barrens after completing his first objective. The dialog begins "Ah, you do understand." and ends with "Now, I ***lose*** you upon them. <snort>"

## Post 162072 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-12-08T17:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162072#p162072 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Lose = to be deprived of, to misplace, or to set free.
Loose = not tight or untied. It’s an adjective, not the verb you need here.

In this sentence, it means to release or let go of someone onto others. As a verb, “to loose” does exist.
