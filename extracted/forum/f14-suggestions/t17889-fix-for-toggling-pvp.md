# Fix for toggling PvP

## Post 121923 by Dragons604 — 2025-03-07T01:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121923#p121923 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

PvP is fun and I agree with the current stance of "having PvP enabled is consent to all forms of PvP including the annoying ones" that's totally fair. The only thing that actually annoys me is people who walk around with PvP off, see someone they have a clear advantage against who isn't already running because they don't see the PvP coming, and then toggle on to take a super one sided fight only to toggle off 5 min later. This IMO is the main killer of world PvP on the server.

Give the PvP enabled player the advantage, or at the least some hope in one or both of these ways:

 1 - Enabling PvP gives a visible buff/debuff that all other players can see. This lasts for 20s-120s and the toggler only actually becomes PvP enabled after the effect wears off. This would give the likely lower level player time to run / be aware of the impending threat.

 2 - Only PvP enabled players can see other PvP enabled players.(meaning a non-PvP player sees everyone as blue and can't see the PvP tag) This would be a good iron fist approach completely disallowing togglers by not letting them tell who a potential target is. This could work in conjunction with the buff to give the toggler a grace period so they can still see who has PvP on around them before they become enabled. I'd argue the buff in this case could be as long as 2 min to allow certain escape if they toggle in a truly terrible spot.

The point of both of these is to promote world PvP while mitigating unsportsmanlike conduct. Toggle abuse is inherently unfair and strongly discourages world PvP. The alternative server approach, while good in theory, splits the player base causing population issues largely to PvP players' deficit. The changes above would allow players with world PvP constantly enabled to play more like they were on a true PvP server without the risk of being toggled while not separating them from the non-PvP world and vice versa.
