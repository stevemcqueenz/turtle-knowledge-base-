 1.0 General Stuff

Before the class changes, I made a different forum post (check [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=2305&p=14500&sid=d ... d71#p14500](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=2305&p=14500#p14500)), regarding what I'd want to see being added for rogues in the future, all of which were all rejected sadly. With some more thought put into, what Devs find acceptable, I want to propose some new ideas.
I'm gonna start this post off with talent adjustments, and then move down into suggestions and new abilities, and then move into general ideas and thoughts.

I will have to note a couple of things:
Changing Subtlety is gonna be super hard, due to the fact it was designed around with PvP in mind. Making it viable for PvE would change the tree too much, so Hemorrhage is always gonna be lackluster for that reason.
And again, these are all suggestions and ideas. Feel free to strike back with your own thoughts, objections and suggestions in this thread!

 **1.1 Assassination**

First up, lets talk about ruthlessness. More of a QoL change, but something I say is important nonetheless. The rng behind it makes it feel really shit, considering you NEED this, or relentless strikes to proc, to do your rotation perfectly.

 **(OLD) Ruthlessness**: Has a 20/40/60% chance to add an extra combo point when using a finisher.
 **(NEW) Ruthlessness**: Has a 33/66/100% chance to add an extra combo point when using a finisher.
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097321764297650226/image.png)

My idea here is, that ruthlessness is in itself already a pretty niche pick, but dagger rogues pick this up for AQ/Naxx since Murder doesn't add undead damage. Picking this is very rng-dependant, and does hinder our rotation quite a bit:
Example: Sword rogues' most popular rotation is to do 2x SS > SnD > 4-5 SS > SnD. The reason you do 4-5 SS's after the first SnD refresh, is because we HAVE to rely on either a ruthlessness proc, or relentless strikes proc to hit the 5-point mark without losing any uptime on SnD, this would be very beneficial and QoL, also to PvP.

 **(OLD) Improved Poisons: Increases your chance to proc poisons by 1/2/3/4/5%**
 **(NEW Idea 1) Weak Points: Getting 5 stacks of Deadly/Wound Poison opens up the targets weak points, making all your attacks hit the target for the next 1/2/3/4/5 seconds.**
 **(NEW Idea 2) Improved Poisons: Increases the damage of your poisons by 4/8/12/16/20%, increases the chance to proc them by 1/2/3/4/5% and gives an additional chance to resist dispels by 8/16/20/24/32/40%.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097321991494696970/image.png)

The second idea, is to merge Vile Poisons with Improved Poisons. This boost wouldn't be as huge as it sounds, since rogues will generally favor Windfury in their main-hand slot.
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097322262098624612/image.png)

 **(NEW Idea 3): Envenom: Finishing move that consumes your Deadly Poison doses on the target and deals instant poison damage:
 1 dose:  153 damage
 2 doses: 306 damage
 3 doses: 459 damage
 4 doses: 612 damage
 5 doses: 765 damage**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097324670153412688/image.png)

This talent idea would play into the idea of Assassination as well, and giving them more buttons to press.

 **(NEW Idea) Find Weakness: Your finishing moves increases the Damage Done to the Target by an additional 2/4/6/8/10% for 10s.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097322499911454782/image.png)

This would be enabling the idea behind playing Cold Blood: Pushing as many finishers as you can while maximizing DPS.

As you'll be reading, my idea for assassination is to try to make it more viable for raids, as currently only Combat is *the* viable spec for raiding.

 **(OLD) Vigor: Increases your maximum energy to 110.**
 **(NEW) Mutilate: 60 energy. Instantly attacks with both weapons for an additional 66 with each weapon.  Damage is increased by 50% against Poisoned targets.  Must be behind the target.  Awards 2 combo points. 6s CD**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097322597294809128/image.png)

Vigor is such an useless talent in any type of content as is. Accessing mutilate as your 31st assassination point, would enable a new way of playing this class. You won't have access to Blade Flurry, the weapon skill or adrenaline rush, but with these new talents in mind, assassination could become super competitive with some specs.

 **(NEW) Shiv: 20 Energy. Performs an instant off-hand weapon attack that automatically applies the poison from your off-hand weapon to the target.  Slower weapons require more Energy.  Awards 1 combo point. CD**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097322731063738438/image.png)

Adding this alongside Seal Fate in the assassination tree, would finish off what I think would be fitting for the tree, giving you 2 more buttons to press, and synergizing with whole idea of Mutilate.

Overall, adjusting every talent tree to be considered "viable" is gonna be hard, due to the sheer amount of good things combat has going for it, like Weapon Expertise, Sword Spec, Adrenaline Rush and Blade Flurry. I'm hoping, this would make Assassination considered viable.

 **1.2 Combat**

Before we move on to the Combat tree, I want to propose a new finisher.

 **(NEW)Blade Flurry: 50 energy. Your next 2/3/4/5/6 attacks will strike an additional nearby opponent. 20s cooldown**

The reason I'm throwing this in now, is to better suit my idea for Blade Flurry.

 **(OLD) Blade Flurry: 25 energy. Increases your attack speed by 20%.  In addition, attacks strike an additional nearby opponent.  Lasts 15s.**
 **(NEW) Improved Blade Flurry: Increases the amount of strikes Blade Flurry does by 1, and additionally increases your Attack Speed by 20% for 15s.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097322935414431796/image.png)

Working around Blade Flurry is gonna be a bit tough, seeing as how good of a cooldown it is, but with the addition of making Blade Flurry accessible without going deep into combat, and just improving it by going into Combat, would further improve balance between all specs.

(NEW Idea 2) Quick Attacks: Your Blade Flurry now holds 2 charges, taking 2 minutes to refresh 1 charge. Additionally Increases your maximum energy by 20 for the duration.

This, should just work with Blade Flurry being a baseline level 60 ability.

 **(OLD) Improved Kick: Your Kick has a 50/100% chance to Silence the target for 2s.**
 **(NEW Idea) Improved Kick: Your kick has a 100% chance to silence the target for 2s.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097322935674482758/image.png)

This talent is hardly ever picked, due to how far down the combat tree it is for PvP, and because it's useless for PvE content. This would just be a QoL thing. In a Similar fashion, i believe Improved Sprint should follow the same route.

 **(OLD) Improved Sprint: Has a 50/100% chance to break any movement impairing effects when you activate your sprint ability**
 **(NEW) Improved Sprint: Has a 50/100% chance to break any movement impairing effects when you activate your sprint ability. Additionally makes you immune to any movement impairing effects for 1s.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097323128268525588/image.png)

Improved Sprint is very abuseable if a warrior, or a class with a similar slow is facing you. Popping Improved Sprint a lot of times, ends up having you get slowed immediately after, removing the effect and nullifying going 15 points into Combat.

 **1.3 Subtlety**

 **(OLD) Sleight of Hand:
Reduces the chance you are critically hit by melee and ranged attacks by 1/2% and increases the threat reduction of your Feint ability by 10/20%.**
 **(NEW) Reduces the chance you are critically hit by melee and ranged attacks by 1/2%. Additionally reduces the chance your Pick Pocket will get resisted by 2/4%.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097323532335198298/image.png)

This would be a HUGE QoL Change for a really poor talent, and would be picked for gold farming.

(OLD) Initiative: Gives you a 25/5075% chance to add an additional combo point to your target when using your Ambush, Garrote, or Cheap Shot ability.
(NEW) Initiative: Gives you a 50/100% chance to add an additional combo point to your target when using your Ambush, Garrote, or Cheap Shot ability.
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097323614942023820/image.png)

Again, another QoL chance, that wouldn't affect gameplay much, just make it play out smoother. (Except making Ambush worth using when vanishing, as is right now, Backstab is on average more dps as a dagger rogue)

 **(OLD) Setup: Gives you a 15/30/45% chance to add a combo point to your target after dodging their attack or fully resisting one of their spells.**
 **(NEW) Setup: Gives you a 25/50/75% chance to add a combo point to your target after dodging their attack or fully resisting one of their spells. Additionally reduces the cooldown of Ghostly Strike by 0.3/0.6/1s every time you dodge or resist an attack.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097323787810263273/image.png)

This would be playing into the Rogue Tank idea, and also making this Talent possibly worth picking up for wanting Rogue tanks.

 **(OLD) Heightened Senses: Increases your stealth detection by x/x and reduces the chance you are hit by spells and ranged attacks by 2/4%**
 **(NEW) Heightened Senses: Increases your stealth detection by x/x. When hit by a lethal spell or ranged attack, has a 10/20% chance to reduce the chance you are hit by any attacks by 15% for 5 seconds**

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097323862569521243/image.png)

Strengthening the weak talents into more survivability, is something I'd say is key for subtlety.

 **(OLD) Deadliness: Increases your Attack Power by 2/4/6/8/10%.**
 **(NEW Idea 1) Deadliness: Increases your armor penetration by 1/2/3/4/5% Of your targets total armor value.
(NEW Idea 2) Deadliness: Increases your *Agility* by 2/4/6/8/10%.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097323950209519626/image.png)

These 2 ideas are both optional. Increasing Agility increases your crit, armor, dodge and attack power, armor pen just increasing your damage. The first also benefitting Rogue Tanks.

 **(OLD) Premeditation: When used, adds 2 combo points to your target.  You must add to or use those combo points within 10 sec or the combo points are lost. 2m CD**
 **(NEW Idea 2) Smoke Bomb: Throws a smoke bomb on your location for 10s, making you invisible for up to 5 seconds or until broken. You cannot use crowd control abilities while in this state. Your first attack made will give you an additional 2 combo points. Entering the smoke bomb again increases your elusiveness, increasing movement speed by 30% for 4 seconds. 5m CD**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097324048490438726/image.png)

This definitely sounds super niche and out of this world, but I'm really trying to go for the PvP Approach that blizzard clearly were for the Subtlety tree as well. One thing to note, is that this is not an Improved stealth like vanish, but an invisibility, making you unable to use things like Cheap Shot or Sap.

 **(NEW) Master of Subtlety: Attacks made while and for 6 seconds after breaking stealth causes an additional 4/7/10% Damage.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097324162311270551/image.png)

This would make Vanish more of a DPS Cooldown alongside other cooldowns, included with Trinket swapping, you could have a lot of fun with this.

 **(NEW) Tricks of Trade: 15 energy.The current party or raid member becomes the target of your Tricks of the Trade.  The threat caused by your next damaging attack and all actions taken for 6 sec afterwards will be transferred to the target.  In addition, all damage caused by the target is increased by 15% during this time. 30s cd**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097324247824736257/image.png)

This would work similar to how Shamans can use Bloodlust, and Grace of Sunwell can work for tank assisting.

 **(NEW) Insult: 20 energy. Verbally insults the target, increasing threat by a large amount. 8s CD

This would give rogue tanks a way of doing more threat, as they are severely threat capped, due to doing less damage and not being able to do a set rotation. This is a filler ability. This and Trick of Trades should be baseline, and learnt at level 60.

 1.4 QoL
 **- Change Relentless Strikes.**
 **(OLD) Your finishing moves have a 20% chance per combo point to restore 25 energy.**
 **(NEW) Your finishing moves have a *25%* chance per combo point to restore 25 energy.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097325113780736020/image.png)

This just makes it much more rewarding to spend 3 points on this talent, and overall makes the gameplay smoother.

 **- Make Eviscerate scale with your Total AP% instead of nothing.** This way it actually has some form of scaling, or make this talented into Improved Eviscerate. - This includes Rupture and Garrote, potentially even making poisons scale with a lower coefficient of your AP%.
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097325290012819466/image.png)

 **- Change the way Combo Points work.** Currently, targeting anything will remove your Combo Points, even if it's a missclick. Either add a time limit before said points run out, and have said points on your first target still go away, if you attack another opponent; OR make combo points apply to yourself. (Kick was fixed in later expansions to not drop combo points aswell). An additional note, is that some addons that require targetting do not work for Rogues, as you will constantly lose combo points!

 **- Fix Weapon Procs/Poisons/Effects/Enchants with abilities.** I've made 4 bug reports on it so far, the 1st giving us the chance to apply poisons on Sap/Gouge, with a lot of setbacks- Damaging effects would break the CC's if procced, something that isn't blizz-like either. Ideally it should proc, but not break said CC like on Classic.
Additionally, Feint does still not have the ability to proc ANY weapon effects, which is also a bug.
Thirdly, Stealth now also has a chance to break when proccing Crippling Poison off a Sap with Improved Sap 2/2. This is a weird bug, that sometimes puts you in combat when you sap a target, and if you proc a poison at the same time, you have a chance of having your stealth broken.

 - **Adjust Deadly Throw to work with Ranged weapons, while reducing damage slightly.**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097325471290630144/image.png)

 - **Make the T3 8/9 bonus also work for Expose Armor and Rupture, Potentially Mutilate**
Another QoL Stuff, that now could reward you for using any finisher.
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097325584536830104/image.png)

 - **Make Improved Slice And Dice Baseline and not talented, & Increase Flourish Duration to match Slice and Dice** Ok so, Flourish as is, is equal to Slice and Dice Duration -1 combo point. This makes it so, rogue tanks have no difinitive rotation, as you can't do 5 point Flourishes > 5 point Slice and Dices without extreme luck and the right talents. Fixing the duration, would make it so Rogues now have a set rotation for tanking.
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097325705651572847/image.png)

 - **Merge Oppurtunity (% damage from behind) with Improved Backstab, OR make Oppurtunity increase damage done by any special attacks (not just Backstab, Ambush & Garrote)**
This would open up more options for Dagger rogues to spec into, and would also let Sword rogues spec into it for a DPS gain. Even making a hybrid spec viable! (Imp backstab with sword talents!)
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097325900044980284/image.png)
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097325943896416346/image.png)![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097321097101320352/image.png)

 - **Make Endurance baseline (3.5m CD on Sprint/Evasion)**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/992788120355491951/1097326049257345054/image.png)

 - What hinders a rogue the most, is lack of survivability in raids compared to Warriors, due to low armor values, as well as lack of on-demand AOE and multi-target damage, that a lot of other classes have. The only noticeable thing a rogue will bring to a raid that doesn't demand disarming traps or unlocking doors, is Expose Armor, talented at that too.
Going forward, I feel like those 2 points should be the main focus of improving Rogue gameplay for a future class changes patch.

 - Also, what happened to Surprise Attacks? We were promised this skill, which would've been really good, but instead we got nothing..?

 1.5 Finishing Thoughts

I wanna finish this off by saying, all of these are just suggestions, although there are definitely some things I'm really hoping to see in the future. Other things are more seen as a quirky thought. Subtlety being the best example, as the tree is just so down bad. What I'm hoping to see, is making Assassination work like a raid-dps Talent tree, increasing various bonuses for the raid, while still holding their own ground. The idea of using as many finishers as possible, and exposing the target of its weaknesses.
As for Subtlety, sneakiness, elusiveness and survivability is key. Adding said tank abilities to this tree would make sense, but having some abilities baseline learned would be more optimal.
The point of this post is not to see everything being added, but just throwing general ideas out to take into consideration. Thanks again!

Yeah I agree, that blizz tried and failed to make sub a viable pve spec until what like cata? Ideally we see every spec viable in pvp and pve, which is a tall order but it's definitely better than purposefully relegating one tree to just pvp, one to just pve... etc... way less build diversity if you do this.

Envenom is an easy add. I can definitely also see spellpower scaling with Ass rogue be a thing with t1 t2 t3 gear support, similar to the t2.5 of hunters which has agi and sp. Though this would be an alt build of Assa, with phys Assa also supported by the spec.

Seeing vigor replaced with something not laughable would be great. Or even a buff to vigor to 25 or even 40 instead of +10. But yeah mutilate is also a logical backport.

Leaning into daggers is a good idea for non combat spec, as attaining dagger weapon skill from gear is trivial. Offrace nondaggers players would continue staying combat. Or perhaps should dagger skill be added to deep Assa or maybe even Sub? A Skill talent is a really good way to buff a build only pve and not in pvp at all.

Blade flurry is the only cleave that rogues get, in a game full of cleaving, and forcing people into deep combat to get this indeed is a guarantee that only combat will be any good. Your rework to make blade flury a baseline finisher is a great idea though I expect it to be controversial.

The sprint change would be good. You'd still be vulnerable to a warrior's intercept and the warrior matchup is still mostly stacked up against you, but this allows for more skill expression on both sides.

The change to sleight of hand does fit the description. Though, this change and others does raise the question of:
Is removing all RNG resist and fails a good thing? WotLK did this and while yes the game does strictly speaking play better, it did remove a component of the game inherited from pen and paper RPGs, where sometimes you just roll bad dice, and its important to have these failures so that you are rewarded if you have backup plans prepared for these failures. This is one of the things that makes old wow really great on a subtle level and keeps it feeling more like an RPG than an action game like in later wow.

As I understand, ambush doesn't scale with gear at all, and only the weapon? This seems to be the crux of many issues found at lv 60 across a lot of classes. While nonscaling abilities are relatively more powerful for the average leveler, they end up being lost in the high end play of lv 60s and indeed this sucks. A full rework on what scales and how, is really needed, instead of these piecemeal buffs to nonscaling abilities.

Revamping premed is probably the right call. Its a super powerful ability for pvp and pretty much useless in pve. This kinda stuff throws off the balancing really harshly and stands in the way of further balancing work. It would be a shame if premed were lost though, but instead reworked into something with equal pvp and pve power.

For sure making vanish useful in pve other than when you pull threat, is nice. You either play it safe and save it for a threat pull, or you spend this cooldown for a marginal dps increase. These are interesting choices that make you think ahead, a big part of the rouge class design.

So tricks, misdirections, all of this would probably trivialize tanking and allow warirors to tank up more and stop being greedy furyprot machines... but then again tricks and misdirection frontloading tank threat will allow dps classes with huge frontloaded burst like rets do their thing more. This kinda does take away the one boon that prot pally has: huge burst threat on pull. They can do 5k threat in 2 seconds where a warrior and bear do 3k.

The combo points change from tbc absolutely needs to be backported. I'm sure the original idea was for rogue to tunnelvision, but this just makes them a very limited less skillful class to play in pvp, and means they can't really use their support and CC toolkit in raids and dungeons. This is the #1 thing that should be changed for rogue I think.

Good list, a lot of ehh whatever kinda changes in here, but mostly good suggestions to get ourselves a few steps closer to other specs being viable and make rogue actually not the same exact thing as on Classic.
