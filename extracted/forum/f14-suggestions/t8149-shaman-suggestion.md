Hi there, kinda related to my last suggestion about giving warlock firestone a spell power scaling, but:

How about giving shaman's flametongue totem effect when granted to allies a similar change? Like if a shaman places flametongue totem which imbues a paladin or mage in their group, their attacks will scale with spell power just like how flametongue weapon works for the shaman already. I think this makes too much sense and is how it should've worked from the start, since shaman flametongue weapon already scales with spell power. Flametongue totem is probably the least used totem in the game due to being outperformed by windfury so hard, so I think giving it some extra love wouldn't hurt.
