# Fix beast master talent unefficiencies and improvement ideas.

So lets start:
 **1) “Coordinated Assault” is inefficient in BM because procs only of Arcane shot**

 **Problem.** By description the effect can trigger only once every 3 sec, but in our BM setup we don’t have *Steady Shot*. So it only procs from *Arcane Shot* (t once per 6 sec).In a build without Steady, the theoretical max uptime caps near ~50%, and in practice it’s lower.  Even on paper it’s just “+30% of pet AP on the pet’s next auto-attack” with a hard 3s ICD — very low value. So now we have at 74 ilvl cat pet ( like 40-50 dmg once in 6 seconds which is around 8 dps from a talent, looks quite low.)

 **Proposed fix.**
• Allow the talent to also proc from **Baited Shot** and **Kill Command**.
• *Power tweak*: make the bonus apply to the pet’s next **ability** (not only auto-attack) and add **+30% of pet AP** as bonus Physical damage.

This keeps the 3s ICD intact but lets BM actually interact with the talent and raises the ceiling from “token damage ping” to “noticeable, skill-linked value”.

 **2) “Frenzy” overcaps too early**

 **Problem.** With **3/5** points, Frenzy is effectively ~100% uptime in almost all raid scenarios. The last ranks bring little real DPS return, so the talent doesn’t scale with investment.

 **Proposed fix (simple and safe).** Scale attack speed per rank, e.g. **30–35–40–45–50%** across ranks 1–5 (or similar numbers at your discretion). This rewards point investment without changing the feel (it stays a sustained buff, not a burst button).

 **3) Pet survivability makes BM non-playable in many raids**

 **Problem.** In MC/BWL (and plenty of other places) the pet gets hammered by raid AoE/cleave and constant dispelable effects. Healers already juggle ~40 players; adding a “41st target” for dispels/heals is unrealistic. Self-casting *Mend Pet* is often not feasible (control, movement, GCDs, and heavy mana drain) — I rolled Hunter, not a healer. Yes, smart control and positioning are part of BM, but the current frequency of “park/call off/re-engage” is so high that you basically abandon DPS.

 **Proposed fixes.**
• Turn **Kill Command** into a reactive pet-defensive: when pressed, the pet gains **50% damage reduction for 5 sec** (PvE-flag if needed).
• Rework **Spirit Bond** regen: current **1/2%** every 5 sec doesn’t matter in raids. Instead, grant **1/2 of a Mend Pet tick** on **your or your pet’s critical strike**.
– This synergizes with the T1 set bonus (which is questionable as a raid set right now) and gives new life to *Improved Mend Pet* without turning it into free HPS spam.

 **Note on talent budget (why some power back is needed).**
If you adopt these survivability/QoL changes, BM will naturally spend more points inside the BM tree. That means we lose DPS from other trees. Giving back power through **Coordinated Assault** (more relevant triggers + stronger hit) and **Frenzy** (ranked AS%) would keep BM competitive without creating PvP one-shots.

—

 **Why this is safe for PvP**
• Numbers are modest and mostly shift reliability/QoL rather than burst.
• Defensive is tied to *Kill Command* with a short window; easy to tune or PvE-flag.
• Frenzy change rewards point investment but doesn’t alter gameplay loops.

 **Expected outcome**
• BM becomes playable in MC/BWL with less pet micromanagement tax.
• Talent ranks feel meaningful; fewer “dead” points.
• The fantasy of commanding a beast survives raid mechanics without asking healers to baby-sit the pet.
