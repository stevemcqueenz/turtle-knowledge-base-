# Combat Drinking

## Post 31694 by Dakka97 — 2022-12-28T11:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31694#p31694 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think most casters understand this.

 **Scenario:** You're drinking because you're low on mana and the tank/dps pulls the next pack. You either have to sit stubbornly and keep drinking or stop drinking halfway and start casting again. Now that the pack is dead and you're low again, you go back to drinking. The cycle repeats.

I suggest **"Combat Drinking"** where if you're already in the process of drinking and you're placed back into combat, you simply quaff the rest of it to speed up the mana recovery. In order to not lead to abuse, there could be some "loss" in mana recovery (like you drink so fast you cough some back up or something) or a debuff letting players know you quaffed too much already and need to relax.

Instead of being stubborn or berating my party, I'd like to take a different path and get some help from the system.

Thoughts and/or opinions?
