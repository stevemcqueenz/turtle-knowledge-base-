# Day/Night Cycle Makes Me Sad

## Post 126708 by JWolfsLair — 2025-04-14T09:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126708#p126708 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there!

I wonder, is anyone else bothered by the shift of day/night cycle of TWoW between Kalimdor and Eastern Kingdoms?

It bothers me to no end, to be forced to experience my beloved Kalimdor journey in darkness, as I am so used to the glorious Barrens sun. I would so very much like to play a Tauren Druid, but I just can't force myself to trudge through in the dark.

I realise this is quite selfish, as there are many people playing on Nordanaar from all over the place, experiencing different day/night cycles. It simply never occurred to me, until TWoW, that every server I ever played on was synchronised with the central European time. This allowed me to experience Azeroth mostly during daytime and I find that mandatory to enjoy this beautiful game.

Not to mention, it seems dizzying and immersion breaking too (I know - it's a planet, but there are some meta immersion defining rules, which supersede 'realism').

I know I may be out of line, but it is worth a shot, as I truly wish to experience the best server ever in the best way possible - is there a chance that this day/night shift will ever be reverted, or perhaps a cosmetic day/night change item introduced to the shop?

Hopefully, this shall one day come to pass. Until then, thank you for all the beauty of TurtleWoW.

It has just occurred to me, that a potentially funny, yet perfectly viable solution would be, to do what many European countries are doing IRL, summer-winter time shift every half a year.

Except in our case, it would shift the day/night cycle between Kalimdor and Eastern Kingdoms. During the winter - the current setup, during the summer - Kalimdor day, Eastern Kingdoms night (by EU time). Who knows, perhaps Azeroth as a planet turns and twists in ways much fancier than Earth.

The shift also wouldn't need to coincide with the IRL one, it could for example be marked by the beginning of the Midsummer Fire Festival, then revert with the Winter Veil.

Either way, selfishly, this would allow me to experience the central Kalimdor summer together with my summer, and it really would make me leap for joy.
