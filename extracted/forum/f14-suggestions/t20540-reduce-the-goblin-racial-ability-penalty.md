# Reduce the Goblin racial ability penalty

## Post 141955 by Kelun — 2025-07-28T22:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141955#p141955 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there.

I would like to leave a suggestion about the Goblin racials

 1. The main suggestion is about the "Exit Strategy", i think the counter effect(debuff) making the player unable to attack and cast spells it's very punishing, it a too big trade off, the result is a racial that not that encouraging to used, unless you are fleeing of combat.
Instead of making your character completely unable to attack and use spells, i suggest to give the racial a more fair trade off, as follows:

While under the effect:
 - Time between attacks is increased by "X"%
 - Spell casting time is increased by "X"%
 - Global cooldown is increased by "X"%

For X i think about **20% - 25%** would be a fair enough value to this debuff.

With this change, we can still attack and cast while using his racial ability, but it got a trade off in the form of slower attacks/spells, without making it so punitive, in a similar way of Orc's Blood Fury, allowing a better use to the Racial.

 2. This is a secondary suggestion, but i think Goblins should get one more racial, this ability could be a passive buff which references the high intelligence and agility/cunning of Goblins, their lore supports this, describing them as very intelligent and cunning, furthermore, this would benefit from classes that use magic and agility in game.

New Racial: **Swift Brain**
 **Intellect and Agility increased by 2%**

This would give a small stat buff useful for both magic classes like Mage and Warlock, but also for agility classes like Rogues and Hunters, which would be a small incentive to create a Goblin with those classes, and would be accurate to the Goblin lore.

Obs: A bit off-topic but, one small observation, i think the High Elf Racial, **Swiftness of the Ranger**, could also receive a +**2% Intellect bonus alongside the agility**, since High Elves also are a very intelligent race.

That's the suggestions, thanks!
