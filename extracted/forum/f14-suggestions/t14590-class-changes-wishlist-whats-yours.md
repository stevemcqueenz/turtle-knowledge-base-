# Class changes wishlist - whats yours?

## Post 99530 by Phoenixphire (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-15T16:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99530#p99530 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the new patch draws closer I find myself hoping and wishing for a few new changes to some of the classes I frequently play. I have played turtle wow for years and also classic for years and while I think the first round a class changes was an amazing start, I think a few more things could be nice.

I have included specific examples as I hear the Twow devs appreciate specific examples over vague suggestions.

Here is my “Class changes 2.0 wishlist.” I would love to hear yours as well especially for classes I don’t play all that much!

Hunter:

I haven’t gotten the pleasure to play hunter in raids yet but without commenting on is Trueshot fixed hunter scaling at all these are what I would wanna see:

 1)
A little more support for melee hunter in some form. Survival was originally supposed to be a melee spec so it isn’t breaking vanilla design to add a tad more melee support. Nothing crazy just maybe a way to use mongoose bite without having to be attacked or one new button to press that only works with aspect of the wolf.

Specific examples:

A talent that makes mongoose strike have a chance per weapon swing to become usable without a dodge or parry.

A bleed that scaled with weapon dmg and causes you and your pet to place individual bleeds on the target (to prevent something like Luffa instantly invalidating it) instead of something like wyvern sting since its a meme talent anyway.

 2)
A family pet skill for all the families without a unique skill. This is a big one for me as I hate how a once exciting part of hunter is now extremely formulaic and boring. When you hit level 10 and wanna select a pet it really shouldn’t wipe out like half of the options right off the bat unless you just care about looks. Even if its not the best, give me SOMETHING to justify a spider or a bear or something. Pretty please. I’ll take anything.

Specific Examples:

Tallstriders-
Soothing Lick, remove one hostile debuff from the pet or hunter on a between .30-1.30 min cooldown. (This will make them the defensive support pet they were always meant to be)

Bear-
Infected bite, does a moderate amount of dmg and increases physical dmg taken by the target for the next maybe 10-30 hits by X amount of dmg. On maybe a 30-45 sec cooldown. (Kinda like hemorrhage, its a tank pet so tanking and assisting a bit in dmg as support fits very well.)

Warlock:

 1)
Please just give demonology the Arcane Mage treatment. Leave their support talents for other trees like sac but give them a real reason to go full demo in PVE besides just dipping in for sac or only going deep for pvp.
Like buffs to enslaved demons like the felguard or something that makes them actually a viable way to play like making enslave easier to keep up in raids.

Specific Examples:

Make improved enslave demon stun the demon for 1.5/3 seconds after enslave breaks, giving a skilled lock time to reenslave and causing it to be much less of a headache to use.

Let your felguard, doomguars, and infernal take advantage of the hp,mana, and dmg talents (even at a reduced percentage if needed)

Add an “enslaved demon” benefit to Master demonologist such as “Enslaved demons have a 30% increased enslave demon duration”

Paladin:

Ret Paladin seems to be over-tuned in pvp, and still kinda just in a weird spot in pve. I believe that the ret paladin changes were the one real “Miss” from the twow team on class changes. I believe they went to far into increasing their burst/direct dmg when they were ALWAYS a defensive support class. If you try to buff ret’s dmg in most ways in vanilla you will always reach 2 conclusions. A we are just a bad dmg dealer still. Or B we are way too bursty. Thats why you have to go the route of support.

As a long time ret paladin player in vanilla for over a decade on different servers trying EVERYTHING you can think of to make ret work, I believe they should be pushed more in a support melee direction than a burst melee. I think talents like sanctified command were perfect and ret should lean more in that direction and have its burst toned down. I PERSONALLY think the philosophy of small tweaks will always leave Paladin in an unbalanced state good or bad and something on the level of proclaim champion is needed as well as toning down burst a bit. These suggestions come from years of raiding and pvp experience as well as experience on turtle wow.

Ret needs an ability to hang their hat on. Just tuning numbers up or down will always leave them in a bad spot. Enhancement has bloodlust, shadow has vamp embrace for mana and heal regen, ret needs something other than more or less dmg.

Specific Suggestions:
I would try to make ret as its class fantasy intended, not a bursty plate class, but a plate wearing class charging in with his team attacking and providing buffs.

 1)
Make holystrike only deal something like 50% holy dmg instead of full holy dmg as a start

(this should be tested and tweaked but I think 50% is a great start)

 2)
Completely rework Crusader strike to now be the end talent of retribution and now work like this:

Crusader strike deals physical dmg equal to maybe 75% weapon dmg (this may need tweaking) and causing you to rally your party members (not including yourself) based on the seal you are using on a 15-30 (would need to be tested) second cooldown. Seal based effects below.

Seal of Righteousness-

Causes your party members to become infused with the light, causing their next 3 melee attacks to deal extra holy dmg scaling with the swing speed of their weapon.

(This would be a flavorful use of a seal on bosses or targets with high armor when in a melee group. Dmg should be something under max rank instant poison)

Seal of the crusader-

Causes party members to gain 3% increased attack and cast speed for 5 seconds.

(This is in an intentional attempt to make it not better than bloodlust when split between 4 players)

Seal of Light-

Applies a heal over time effect based on dmg dealt.

(A good number here would be something less than a strong HoT from a dedicated healer, but not something so insignificant that its not worth using. The paladin is giving up both the dmg of his seal and the buff others to use this, so it should not be a crap nothing burger heal. But less than a strong HoT or chainheal this could be good heal support on a high dmg fight, or a good emergency heal when a wipe might be starting or even a good opener for a pvp engagement to throw a small HoT on his or her group)

Seal of Wisdom-

Gives the paladin’s party increased spell power based on a percentage of their own for 10 seconds.

(This once again is sacrificing the paladin’s own dmg and other buffs to buff their party. ALSO with it being a percentage of their OWN spellpower, it can lead to paladins adding more spellpower to their set up to roll with the healers or casters providing a possible new way to itemize and use the paladin in a raid comp. The ultimate nightfall paladin. Ideal level of spell power would be something a little more you could understand getting from a totem as the paladin is sacrificing dmg for it)

Seal of Command-

Increases the paladin’s party’s chance to resist fear, sleep, charm, and stun effects by 8% for 10 seconds

( this is specifically supposed to be the most situational and weak compared to the others. Specifically leaving out polymorph, slow, snare, and root, disorient, etc. While the fear and stun resist might appear very strong, it is less than you would get just from holy talents and over twice as weak as orc racial. This is done because it is the highest dmg seal so if the paladin wants to go for personal dmg, he is going to lose support utility. Also sanctified command already exists)

Seal of Justice-

Increases the paladin’s party’s damage against targets under 25% health by 5% for 10 seconds.

(This is one I think needs work. It can be very strong but only in coordinated very well in pvp as that 20% increased dmg if all 4 are attacking a low health target, but also the benefit is lost if the target is healed. But its flavorful as its the “stun” seal that most people don’t use and also has good use in PVE but only when targets are low health and again, sacrificing the paladins own dps.)

Those are my thoughts on possible upcoming class changes. I put a lot of thought into these so I would love some honest feedback. Numbers of course would need testing and tuning but I hope the overall ideas or design principles make sense.

Would love to hear your own suggestions for your favorite classes as well. If you made it this far, thanks for reading!
