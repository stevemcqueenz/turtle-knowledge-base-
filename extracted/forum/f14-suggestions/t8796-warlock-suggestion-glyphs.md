# Warlock Suggestion: Glyphs

## Post 56724 by Guthrhin — 2023-08-10T17:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56724#p56724 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For Warlock Flavor, Warlock-specific Glyphs could appear as unique Soul Shards. These could be kept in the Warlock's Soul Bag to enable their cosmetic effect.

 **Shard of Xerrath**
 *"While in your Soul Bag, Corrupts your Warlock Fire spells, infusing them with Fel magic."*

 **Shard of Setesh**
 *"While in your Soul Bag, Imbue your Warlock Fire spells, infusing them with Shadowflame magic."*

 **Shard of Xoroth**
 *"While in your Soul Bag, Engulf your Warlock Shadow spells, infusing them with Xorothian flames."*

Possible Fire spells
[Immolate, Conflagrate, Searing Pain, Soul Fire, Hellfire, Rain of Fire, Felsteed, Dreadsteed, and Imp's Firebolt]

Possible Shadow spells
[Shadow Bolt, Corruption, Curse of Agony, Fear, Life Tap, Ritual of Summoning, Drain Soul, and Voidwalker]
