# Profesions Quest and QoL

## Post 162110 by Themota11 — 2025-12-08T22:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162110#p162110 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Turtle Team,

I would like to propose a Quality of Life / Profession progression feature that would add depth to crafting without affecting game balance.

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Summary of the idea

Each profession would receive a 10–15 quest chain, involving:

Crafting specific items

Collecting materials in dungeons

Gathering rare resources

Completing small profession-themed challenges

Finishing the entire chain would reward the player with a unique, equipable profession item.

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Example rewards

Alchemy: a trinket that increases potion crafting speed by 20–50%

Engineering: gloves or goggles that speed up crafting

Tailoring / Blacksmithing / Leatherworking, etc.: profession-specific gear that provides similar benefits

Instead of (or in addition to) crafting speed, these items could also provide a small chance at double crafting (for example 5–15%).
This keeps the reward useful but not overpowered.

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg) Why this would be good for Turtle WoW

Encourages players to explore the world and run dungeons

Adds long-term progression to professions

Keeps rewards useful but optional (not mandatory for meta)

Fits perfectly with the “RPG progression” style Turtle WoW is known for

Gives each profession more identity and more reasons to engage with it

Optional improvements

Rewards could have multiple tiers (Friendly → Exalted) with small upgrades

Each profession’s chain could be themed differently for variety and immersion

Thank you for reading — I think this system would add a lot of flavor and replayability to professions in Turtle WoW!   hiding_smth_turtle_head
