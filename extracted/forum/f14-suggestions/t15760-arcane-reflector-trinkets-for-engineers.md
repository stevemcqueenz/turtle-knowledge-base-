# Arcane Reflector Trinkets for Engineers

## Post 107453 by Piacrt (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-10T22:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107453#p107453 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Seems like a wasted opportunity to add these in, considering we got new Arcane Resistance sets!

Suggested recipe:
> Giga-Dense Arcane Reflector
>  Trinket
>  Requires level 55
>  Requires Engineering (Level 300)
>  +20 Arcane Resistance
>  Use: Reflects Arcane spells back at their caster for 5 sec. (5 Min Cooldown)

Code: Select all

```
8 x Dark Iron Bar
4 x Arcane Essence
3 x Truesilver Transformer
2 x Huge Emerald
1 x Arcane Crystal
```

As for how this recipe is obtained, maybe a neutral quest in Gadgetzan that sends you into Black Morass to obtain a quest item off of trash mobs (like the Time-Lords) and some materials like Arcane Essences, and Arcane Crystals.
Or to keep in spirit with the arcane resistance gear, an Overcharged Ley Energy could be part of the required materials to learn the recipe.

Other similar recipes are obtained from a vendor, Solakar in UBRS, or Crimson Inquisitors in Stratholme. So this being obtained in a completely different way isn't strange.

> Arcane Deflector
>  Requires level 15
>  Use: Absorbs 600 Arcane damage.  Lasts 1 min. (15 Min Cooldown)
>  5 Charges

Code: Select all

```
1 x Small Glowing Shard
1 x Whirring Bronze Ghizmo
```

Could be added to Gnomeregan trash loot tables.
