# Time Travel for Low Level Dungeons

## Post 162857 by Moomoobro — 2025-12-15T13:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162857#p162857 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Suggestion: Time Travel for Low Level Dungeons**

I am really looking forward to the release of Windhorn Canyon in patch 1.18.1. However, as someone who plays a single character, I don’t get to experience new low level content in a meaningful way once I outlevel it.

At the same time, it is quite common to see groups searching for healers for low level dungeons for hours without success. As a healer myself, I would genuinely love to jump into these groups and help out, but without trivialising the dungeon or reducing the challenge and experience for players who are running it at the intended level.

 **Proposed Idea: Time Travel Scaling**

The core idea would be a form of time travel that allows higher level characters to temporarily scale down in order to participate in low level dungeons.

This could include:
- Temporarily scaling character level and gear to the dungeon range
- Restricting spells and talents to those appropriate for that level
- Ensuring dungeon difficulty and experience gains remain authentic for low level players
The goal would be to preserve the original challenge and progression while making it easier to form groups.

 **Optional Difficulty Modes**

It could also be interesting to allow players to choose a difficulty within the dungeon’s intended level range.

For example, using Windhorn Canyon which is designed for levels 26 to 30:
- Easy mode could scale players to level 30 gear and abilities
- Hard mode could scale players to level 26 gear and abilities
This would add replayability while still respecting the dungeon’s original design.

 **Closing Thoughts**

I fully appreciate that this would be a complex system to implement and likely a significant development effort. That said, I cannot imagine I am the only player who genuinely enjoys low level content and would love more ways to engage with it without rolling multiple characters.

Thanks for taking the time to read, and I would be very interested to hear what others think
