## Post 121430 by Mezzomixx — 2025-03-02T19:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121430#p121430 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone. I've been playing wow for almost 20 years. And I've always been a big fan of Stranglethornvalley. I like this tropical, dangerous jungle theme with all the lore about the trolls and the pirates. And Booty Bay is my absolute favorite place in all of wow! I noticed the small room (on the left) at the entrance to the Booty Bay transition, I don't know exactly what that is supposed to be... (Perhaps a pirate treasure trove?) In any case, I still find it interesting and it gives me a few ideas..

I would like to suggest a dungeon in the STV (south). A dungeon with the lore of the South Sea pirates, enemies of the goblins, maybe even some evil trolls. This would be a dungeon in the 40-50 lv range. Maybe the dungeon would be from a secret location under Booty Bay, or it would consist of some ships floating on the islands.

Tell me your opinion about it and maybe the admins would implement it one way or another, I would think it would be great, thanks for reading :)
