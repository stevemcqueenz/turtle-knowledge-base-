# The fixes Paladin now need

## Post 122016 by Icedcream (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-03-08T02:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122016#p122016 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is regarding mostly retribution, but also some observations about holy paladins.

Retribution:

 1) Sanctity Aura is in the holy tree, but literally does nothingggggg for holy paladins. It adds the absolute most minor buff to ret paladins maybe... 2% overall increase to the paladins total dmg.

Move spell to Ret tree, change spell to 3% "Damage" increase. At least could benefit the parties damage.

+ add libram (since there is no good re paladin libram) - 1% additional damage increase with sanctity aura

 2)Hammer of wrath suffers from cast time, and the fact that lets be honest the GCD is not worth the 500 dmg. Any other ability or white dmg would be more worth that 1 second.

Feedback: add improved Hammer of wrath - row 6 - 1 point -
 - cast time becomes instant
 - increase damage by 50% of attack power
 - max range reduced to 5 yards.

Example: buffed raid AP of a AQ40 ret paladin would be 1400 so damage 504+700 = 1200 which just makes it worth the gcd.

Tie crit chance to melee crit rate.

 3) Ret paladins spec is plagued by ramp up damage abilities (CS, Vengeance, Holy strike) It would take 3-10 seconds to get vengeance stacks, 24 seconds to get both CS and holy strike buffs up. This is a problem is a 1-3 minute fight as it may make up as much as 20% of your damage duration. Warriors can often go in with both rage built up (as it takes long to decay), and rage potions. Rogues have energy when they start the fight although they do need to ramp up like a paladin to a degree. I am generally kind of comfortable with this as it doesn't seem too bad. They could use more help:

a) increase vengful strike --- holy strike buff to 30 seconds. You'd be able to feather in more of your more powerful CS per buff rotation. Right now it is CS, HS, CS, CS, HS, CS, CS, etc or HS, CS, CS, HS, CS, CS, etc. Rotation could be HS, CS, CS, CS, CS, repeat.

b) Add ability - ret tree capstone - avenging wrath - increase all damage by 25% for 20 sec, causes forbearance.

 4) Update 2h weapon talent to include - "while wielding a 2h weapon, mana burn against the paladin is reduced by 33/66/100%, only effective in dungeons and raids.

Note: Paladins suffer from a few mana burn instances in raid and naxx and cannot engage properly. Enhance shaman suffer a similar problem and would benefit from a solution.

Holy Paladins:

I understand the idea behind the holy class change. Either lean really had into it or take a 180 back home.

Option 1: Melee healer

A) Shield bash move - Ally's a 200 shield to allies within 15 yards
B) Judgement of life - big holy tree buff
C) Holy Strike - does better healing
D) Inspire - Paladins damage from all sources does 15% healing to those around them. This triggers off shield bash, judgements, holy strikes, cs, white dmg, etc.
E) Consecration - heals allies in it....

Get crazy! lean into it. make all these fun things as good as a shaman chain heal. Make it so he never casts a flash heal again.

Option 2: Spam healer

Maybe within the same tree even you have the focus fire healer paladin.

A) healing light
B) holy shock
C) holy spell crit
D) Daybreak
E) Illumination
