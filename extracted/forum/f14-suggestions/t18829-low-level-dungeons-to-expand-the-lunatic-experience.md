 **ALLIANCE DUNGEON**
 *Hub City, Stormwind.*

The Defias Storehouse
Location: Elwynn Forest, behind Thunder Falls

Design:
Just a cave with boxes!

Bosses:
 1. **Shipping Master Greasefuse**
Lore: Greasefuse is a goblin who serves as a senior quartermaster on three of the ships which travel from Stormwind Harbor to Booty Bay and back, baby! In support of the Defias cause, he sends supply crates to incognito members of the Defias on Thunder Falls to distribute by river items to the forces of Westfall.
Mechanics: Just avoid his AOE bombs which he throws once every ten seconds.
Loot: Quest item, + 1-3 stacks of early professions items such as Linen Cloth, Copper, Malachite, Tigerseye, Peacebloom, Silverleaf, Vials, Strange Dust, and Light Leather.

Quest:
<https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Nayura>

Nayura looks at you, then back towards a trio of dock hands carrying heavy boxes up the ramp towards Stormwind City proper. She seems ever attentive to the comings and goings of passengers and parcels… And something is not right.

“<Name>, if you have some spare time on your hands and a penchant for the shadows… Then I have a  task for you. There is a Goblin named Greasefuse who often comes and goes from these ports with shipments for a noble residing in Thunder Falls, but the packages he leaves with each time have not been included in the ship’s inventory list. At first I thought this to be a lesser race’s folly in attempted leadership, but now suspect embezzling and theft from the likes of him. However, the Stormwind officials will not act on a representative of the Steamwheedle Cartel without proof. Please follow him to Thunder Falls, and see if there really are nobles up there who have a love of fine silk and Tel’Abrim Bananas.”

Objectives:
Kill Shipping Master Greasefuse in the Defias Storehouse, and obtain his inventory list.

Return:
Nayura reads through the list, her brow furrowed as the shipments of metal, engineering supplies, linen cloth, blasting powder, and ignition fuel goes on and on.

“Take this to the Gnomes of the Dwarven District… Lilliam Sparkspindle would be the best for this, I would wager. He’s know what they were building in this cave or… Trying to build. You have my thanks, <Name>.

Rewards: Silver + Second Quest.

[https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Lillia ... 0Stormwind](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Lilliam_Sparkspindle#:~:text=Lilliam%20Sparkspindle%20is%20a%20gnome,in%20the%20Vision%20of%20Stormwind)

Upon reaching Lilliam Sparkspindle, you are greeted by an explosion as a puff of smoke fills the air. A Gnome coughs, before a fan is flicked on clearing the area.

“Greetings! You just witnessed my brand new Rocket Fuel! One day, the Alliance will have flying fortresses I tell you! One day! It will revolutionize travel and… Who are you?”

You show the note to Lilliam Sparkspindle, explaining the situation. Despite the soot on his face, you can tell that he has gone pale.

“These are rather… Worrying items and indeed entirely of a design I recognize. One of my schematics was stolen a few years back for a flying machine - and this is it! Oh, I need to get this to Shaw. You didn’t kill Greasefuse, right? Ah, I see. We’ll see what we can do with this but it would have been easier with a living suspect! Here, take this for your troubles.”

Trinket, Dockmaster’s Stopwatch. *On Use:* Increase movement speed by 10% for 10 seconds. Cooldown of five minutes.

 2. ** Duncon Ratsbon**
Lore: Duncan Ratsbon is a name you’ve seen on the wanted posters around Elwynn Forest. A cruel man, who steals from the rich… and steals from the poor. He’s left many gutted in a dark alley and the kingdom would be better off without him in it!
Quest: Wanted Poster in Goldshire, outside the Inn. Return to Marshal Dughan for silver reward.
Mechanics: Garrote — Inflicts 3 Physical damage to the target every 3 sec for 12 sec. Kidneyshot Kidney Shot — Stuns the target for 2 sec.
Loot: Random Ring which grants +3 to any one stat or ability. Intellect, Spirit, Stamina, Agility, Strength, Attack Power, Healing Spells, Fire Spell Damage, Arcane Spell Damage, Frost Spell Damage, and Shadow Spell Damage.
Flavor Text: This was obviously pick pocketed from a wizard at the the Tower of Azora.

 3. **Lord Slackjaw**
Lore: Lord Slackjaw is a Gnoll who has eaten the actual Lord of Thunder Falls, Lord Thorn of Thunder Falls. The Defias Brotherhood pays homage to the Gnoll in order to use the estate.
Mechanics: Loot piñata, until 1 minute when he enrages and does 50% more damage.
Loot: Random Necklace, similar stats to the above.
Flavor Text: Also likely stolen from the Temple of Azora…

 **HORDE DUNGEON**
 *Hub City, Undercity*

# The Dead Crypts
Location: A new crypt by the Agamand Mills. Implied to be older, and as the family aged they expanded the family crypts. This one is dilapidated, and for the most part is only skeletons and spiders.

Bosses:
 1. **Agnes Agamand**
Lore: Agnes was the great, great, great grandmother of Gregor Agamand and is naught but a skeleton now. However, she seems to be just as strong a mage in death as she was in life. Find her in the family crypts, and bring her down!
Quest: Wanted Poster in Brill, outside the Inn. Return to Executor Zygand for silver reward. Implied to be powerful enough a mage that she may have served the Council of Tirisfal generations ago. You’re lucky she’s mindless now.
Mechanics: Chill of the Grave — Inflicts 3 Physical damage to the target every 3 sec for 12 sec. Ice Block — Traps Agnes in ice for two seconds where she recovers HP.
Loot: Random Necklace which grants +3 to any one stat or ability. Intellect, Spirit, Stamina, Agility, Strength, Attack Power, Healing Spells, Fire Spell Damage, Arcane Spell Damage, Frost Spell Damage, and Shadow Spell Damage.
Flavor Text: An heirloom of the Agamand family. Inscribed with the date of Agnes’ wedding, ‘From your Beloved Cecil.’

 2. **Bishop Lawrence Agamand**
Lore: Many, many years ago when Lordaeron was young, Bishop Lawrence brought many to the Light and away from the old ways of nature and harvest witches. In Undeath, his connection to the Light has not faltered. End his suffering!
Appearance: Burning skeleton, at times /yells “RELEASE ME FROM MY TORMENT!”
Mechanics: Constantly casting fire explosion, doing damage to any in melee range. Encourages bringing ranged members along or leveling engineering.
Loot: Random Ring, similar stats to the above.
Flavor Text: Carries the seal of the Bishop of Lordaeron.
Death Speak: “Thank… You…”

 3. **Lieutenant Mahieu**
Lore: A skeletal necromancer who serves under Captain Dargol in another wing of the family crypts. Lieutenant Mahieu calls upon the dead to serve the Lich King, now and forever!
Quest:
Step 1: Upon killing Lieutenant Mahieu, you loot a Scourgestone which causes your mind to ache. You feel watched while carrying it, this is a dark object, and you would be happy to be rid of it soon! It would be best to get this back to Brill and someone who knows what to do about it…

Step 2: Turn in to Executor Zygrand, get silver and a follow up quest to go to Undercity and visit Aelthalyste who shall through the shadows hide its location while still tracking the movements of the Scourge Forces through Tirisfal Glades. He congratulates you for wiping out the Captain and Lieutenant who served as the Scourge leaders in the northern region, but worries for what is to come. Aelthayste begins work on reworking the runes through shadow magic to perhaps use the Lich King’s powers against him. Reward is same trinket with a speed boost, but modified to be undead themed. “The Ghoul’s Gambit,” perhaps.
[https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=460 ... ity%20(5).](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=4606/aelthalyste#:~:text=Aelthalyste,found%20in%20Undercity%20%285%29.)
<https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Scourgestone>
Mechanics: Every 30 seconds, Lieutenant  Mahieu begins to cast “Raise Dead.” If not interrupted in 3 seconds, he will raise 1 add. The party risks being swarmed by them if they focus the boss alone.
Loot: Quest objective item, and a **Cloth Shoulder Piece** called “Mourning Shall” or “Death Shroud.” Grants a +1 in Stamina, Spirit, and Intellect. Level 5 requirement. This stops low levels from being dependent on the Fire fest shoulders for their lowbee twinks.
