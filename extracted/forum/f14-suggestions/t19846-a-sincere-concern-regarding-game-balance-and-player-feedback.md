# A Sincere Concern Regarding Game Balance and Player Feedback

## Post 135524 by flyinghome — 2025-06-12T23:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135524#p135524 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Development Team,

As a passionate player of Turtle WoW, I’m writing to respectfully express my concern about a recent trend that seems to place too much weight on player suggestions without sufficient evaluation of their broader impact, representativeness, and majority's view.

One such case is the community feedback regarding Elune’s Scythe, a legendary-quality item. Some players have requested that the drop rate be increased and the spell power effect be nerfed. I believe this is a fundamentally flawed suggestion for several reasons.

First and foremost,  legendary item cannot be kept being nerfed again. For example, Elune’s Scythe is a legendary item, and with that title comes an expectation: it MUST BE both powerful and rare. Reducing its strength while increasing its accessibility contradicts the very essence of what makes legendary items aspirational. If it becomes too common or too weak, players may feel LESS motivated to pursue it, diminishing long-term engagement and emotional investment in the game.

There is a misconception that players will quit the game if they can't obtain a powerful item. But consider this:

 ***casinos never worry about losing customers because people don’t win often.***

In fact, the pursuit is what keeps players engaged. The same principle applies to WoW. The thrill of chasing something rare and meaningful is a key part of what keeps many of us coming back.

Players who truly love Turtle WoW will continue to support it, not because everything is easy to obtain, but because it stays true to its principles and offers depth, challenge, and reward that feels earned—not handed out.

Of course, community feedback is important—but it should be weighed carefully against long-term design vision, item hierarchy, and overall game health. I hope this perspective helps reinforce the importance of preserving the integrity and allure of legendary items like Elune’s Scythe.

Legendary items, especially those of orange quality，Scythe, represent the countless hours, effort, and dedication players invest in the game. I hope the development team considers suggestions based on objective balance and broad community feedback, rather than isolated opinions rooted in envy.

Thank you for your time and for all the hard work you do in keeping this world alive.

 ***It would be unfair to keep weakening such items simply due to feedback driven by jealousy or frustration from players who haven't obtained them.***

Warm regards,
A normal devoted Player of Turtle WoW

I understand the frustration, but let’s be fair at some point:

 1.Scythe's strength doesn’t justify a nerf alone. Plenty of legendaries have had their moments of power—Atiesh included in its time. Just because something is strong doesn’t mean it’s broken or unfair. Balance should be approached holistically, not just based on standout numbers.

 2.Rarity is a feature, not a flaw. The low drop rate is what makes Scythe feel legendary. Many iconic items in WoW’s history were rare and powerful—that’s part of what gives the game its sense of reward and long-term goals. Not every player is supposed to get everything easily.

 3.Alting abuse is a player behavior issue, not an item design issue. If people are creating and deleting alts just to chase a drop, that’s their choice—not a justification to nerf an item that others got fairly through long-term commitment.

In short: Don’t punish those who got the item the right way just because others tried to game the system. Nerfing something out of envy or perceived imbalance—especially something so rare—only cheapens the journey and undermines what makes game’s loot system special.

Also before making any decision to nerf powerful orange legendary items, I suggest allowing players to vote and follow the opinion of the players from both sides. Personally, perhaps balancing decisions should not only be based on feedback from a small number of players—especially even when such feedback clearly comes from those who do not own the item and are driven by emotional or even jealous motives.

I agree that developers have an unfortunate tendency to listen to very loud and very shortsighted people. The squeaky wheel getting the grease is understandable, but not ideal in a community this large and diverse. Unfortunately the multiple times where they eventually gave in to ceaseless nagging bordering on harassment set a very bad precedent, and now they're reaping the rewards.

I agree that the increase in drop chance was not warranted and that people should not feel entitled to legendaries. Rarity is both what makes them special and also a means of balancing them.

But if you think the Scythe shouldn't be severely nerfed (and I mean severely nerfed beyond last week's tiny nerf), then you must have an entirely different idea of game and item balance than I have.
 2% hit, 2% crit, 40 SP are already 3 effects that make the Scythe a very desired trinket by itself.
Then on top it gets
 1) An add summon which buffs the entire raid.
 2) A personal damage proc that scales.
 3) One of several debuffs on your target that increases spell school damage raid-wide.
 4) One of several special effects on top, most of which again buff the entire raid.
Scythe in its current form is an absolutely broken item that is utterly outside the Vanilla design paradigm. This is not about "strength". Any single of the 4 additional effects on top of the baseline stats would make it a very desired legendary and very clearly best-in-slot. But cramming all 4 in there is insanity. They overloaded a single item with literally 7 different effects, of which 2 come with multiple different sub effects based on spell school. There is nothing like it in the Vanilla design space - in terms of complexity, in terms of individual power, in terms of buffing an entire raid. And there shouldn't be imo.
And all that on an item where the grind happens in a UBRS+ / pre-MC 10-person dungeon. The rest of the chain is guaranteed, in theory you could finish the Scythe in 1 ID of K40 if a guild clearing it lets you tag along. So on top of all the fundamental design problems it's also just an entirely inappropriate powerlevel for how easy it is to complete once you're past the RNG step at the very beginning that you can farm solo with random people from world chat.

tl;dr: It should be very rare. It should be very powerful. But the Scythe can easily lose 2 effects and still fulfill that criterion.
