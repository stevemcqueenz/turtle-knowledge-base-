# Don't Remove Shape shift upon death.

## Post 102211 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-30T09:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102211#p102211 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was doing an escort quest of a druid in bear form, and he ended up getting killed, and died in bear form, which got me thinking: Do druids actually unshift forms when they die or do they 'revert' back to humanoid form lorewise?

It would be cool to actually die as a bear / cat and have that animation or death sound, never made sense to me that the NPC druids always die in their forms but players magically revert back to tauren/ne form.

Gameplay wise it's due to the shifts being a buff, so the 'buff' goes away when you die.

Would be an easy fix to just make the cat/bear forms persist through death like they did with the elixirs.

I always thought shifting back on death was the canon occurrence and dying druids not shifting was an engine quirk in vanilla, since later expacs have druid NPCs revert back to their humanoid forms as far as I remember. I think Cata even explored that with Worgen being able to shift into humans but dying as worgen since the curse changed their “true” form.
