# STACKABLE Curse of Weakness!

## Post 30336 by Khalthul — 2022-12-17T02:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30336#p30336 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the subject title suggests, no longer would your enemy be afflicted by only the strongest Curse of Weakness in the team, but by each Curse of Weakness placed upon the foe.

This affords Warlocks a defensive position as a class known for its debuffs, and bolsters the utility of a curse that is known to be not be very effective.
