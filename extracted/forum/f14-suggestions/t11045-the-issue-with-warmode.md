# The issue with Warmode

## Post 72421 by Silverleafeon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-20T17:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=72421#p72421 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As far as I've observed, it seems like a lot of people use Warmode just for the extra exp, rather than actually wanting to go out and engage in PvP. More than once I've been in a situation where I attacked a fellow Warmode player of the same level, and they don't even fight back. One person even did /sigh at me, like I was a nuisance, lol. I like PvP, and it just stands to reason that people using Warmode would also like it.

I think that's the issue. Wanting to level quickly and wanting to do PvP are two very separate playstyles. Currently warmode attracts the wrong demographic.

I think it would be a great idea to change the Rewards one gets for participating in Warmode. Instead of boosted exp, the rewards should be PvP related, such as honor, PvP gear, or a cool title.

Just my thoughts on the matter.

Thanks
