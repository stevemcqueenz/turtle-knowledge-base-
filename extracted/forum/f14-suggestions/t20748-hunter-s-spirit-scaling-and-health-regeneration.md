# Hunter's Spirit Scaling and Health Regeneration

## Post 143875 by Larohir — 2025-08-08T18:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143875#p143875 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At the moment, Hunters do not scale well with Spirit. The current health regeneration rate for Hunters is 0.25 per point of Spirit, which puts them on par with Paladins — a healer class. This seems inconsistent, considering Hunters are a non-healing, non-caster class.

For comparison:
Warriors regenerate health at 0.8 per Spirit
Rogues regenerate health at 0.5 per Spirit

Given these numbers, it would make more sense for Hunters to be placed in the same bracket as Rogues in terms of Spirit scaling. Both classes are non-healing, agility-based, and rely on sustained combat without access to healing spells.

Additionally, with the introduction of the new pet passive "Strider Presence" (which provides +25% health regeneration in combat), improving Hunter health regen would synergize nicely — especially with melee build.

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143947#p143947 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This change opens up interesting possibilities for alternate builds , particularly for Survival Hunters. That said, for Spirit to have any real value, players would need to actively stack it, which naturally comes at the cost of lower Agility—and therefore reduced DPS. This creates a meaningful tradeoff.

I agree that additional Spirit scaling isn’t really necessary for Marksmanship (MM) or Beast Mastery (BM), but for Survival, it’s a different story. The spec is already built around a more rugged, sustained-combat playstyle. There’s a strong case for tying improved Spirit scaling directly into a talent, such as “Survivalist”.

For example:
Survivalist
Increases Stamina by 2/4/6/8/10% and improves health regeneration from Spirit by 20/40/60/80/100%.
At full investment, this would bring Spirit scaling in line with Rogues (0.5 per Spirit), reinforcing the idea that Survival Hunters are more durable than their MM and BM counterparts.

Adding it this early at talent tree its becoming viable for leveling , and for sure MM and BM would not sucriface 15 talent points to get it.
