## Post 116846 by Zorgdarkness — 2025-01-15T16:14:00Z

I've have jewelcrafting since the start of the new patch and have mastered both Goldsmith and Gemology specializations on both my characters, got pretty much every recipe and have completed every quest that this proff can offer. ***-special thanks for quest making team, coz they was really fun to do-***
But bringing this profession to the end point where i got all the formulas, I want to say that it still does not feel like something from vanilla  wary_turtle
Here are some of my suggestions:
 -  maintenance_turtle  Add recipes for Jewelcrafting from reputation vendors, mostly because many crafting professions in classic WoW have a large number of them, not only drop from the open world and dungeons but also as rewards for exalted fractions reputation. This not only adds additional recipes to the profession but also rewards players who have achieved an exalted reputation with the factions. As an example, it would be nice to see, a recipe for a 1% speed gem for exalted Wardens of Time ***-Get it? Speed gem for Wardens of Time? Fits perfectly!-***
 -  maintenance_turtle  Also, many professions has a CD recipe, special recipes that have a recovery time, for jewelers it would not be bad to have something like that, as an example - the recipe to make an Arcane crystal from Arcane Essence + Dense Stone? With respectful CD timer ofc. ***If it would bring down the economy of Arcane crystal then you can replace this with other gems as well***
 -  maintenance_turtle  Gemstone Clusters - does not work I don't know why, but i asked peoples who also have jewelcrafting, and no one uses Gemstone Clusters, maybe for low drop chance, or maybe coz we don't really know what are the gems drop rate, but people does not using it. Perhaps remove the sandpaper from the recipe and increase the amount of ore needed for the cluster? I think then people will use this recipe more often
