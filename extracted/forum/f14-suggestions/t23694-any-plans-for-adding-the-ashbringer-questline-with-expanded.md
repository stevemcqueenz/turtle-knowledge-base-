# Any plans for adding the ASHBRINGER questline with expanded lore elements?

## Post 165887 by Sylbrosa — 2026-01-21T18:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165887#p165887 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi Turtle team and community,

One of the things I really love about Turtle WoW is how it expands on Classic by letting us explore stories and places that were never fully implemented in original Vanilla. It keeps the spirit of Classic while giving us new mysteries and adventures to discover.

Because of that, I wanted to ask about the Ashbringer.

More than just adding the weapon itself, it would be amazing if the questline could also explore the myths and legends surrounding it — secret steps, unusual triggers, maybe even things people once speculated about back in the day (like odd interactions, hidden NPCs, or even fishing-related discoveries tied into the legend). Part of what made Ashbringer so iconic was the mystery and all the rumors around it.

So my question is less if Ashbringer will ever be added, and more when and in what form. It feels like a perfect opportunity for Turtle’s design philosophy: expanding on Classic’s unfinished stories in creative but lore-friendly ways.

Would love to hear if this is something that’s on the long-term roadmap, or if there are already ideas being discussed ![👀](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f440.svg)

Thanks for all the amazing work you do!
