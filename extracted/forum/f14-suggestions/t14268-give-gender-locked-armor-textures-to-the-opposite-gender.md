# Give gender locked armor textures to the opposite gender

## Post 97291 by Chromate — 2024-06-19T05:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97291#p97291 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

More transmogs!

Cool pants right?

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/nCA9jiS.png)

Maybe you want them for your transmog set but they look like this on your character:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/nCkLfuw.png)

That's always a bummer, so i'm suggesting giving some of the gender locked texture via new items to give female characters currently male only pants and boots (or vice versa).

Why only pants and boots?

The current textures already work on any character regardless of gender as I can show with some renaming and .mpq modding.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/h7B3kqw.png)
Male Night Elf with as of now female only pant textures.

As I said, it will be nice if some of these gender locked textures were given to the opposite gender via new items, these items could give more flavour to some low level quest or be a random BoE drop, that way all players get more transmog options.

Why I don't suggest the same with chest armor?

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/z2sS38o.png)
(Sorry about the potato quality screenshot)

Unfortunately due to painted on boob shadow most female only textures look really wonky on male player models and it would require a lot of effort to modify all of them.
