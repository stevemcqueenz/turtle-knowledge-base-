# "Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread" needs further balancing

## Post 165782 by Betcoverr — 2026-01-21T08:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165782#p165782 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I'm making a suggestion to further balance the item "Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread" which was recently changed to have a 5-minute cooldown, simply because it is still far too powerful--and at no cost in PVP, and I believe that it'll have a very negative impact in PVP if it were to become widely used.

 *Context*: Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41935>) is an item that does periodic damage to you for 30 seconds when consumed. While I don't know the actual intended use of the item, it is used in PVP by some people to deny CC (e.g. sap, polymorph, trap, etc.), similar to Skull of Impending Doom (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4984>). This effect is extremely strong in PVP, and it is not more commonly used only because of obscurity.

While the two items are similar in function, the fruit bread:
- doesn't take up an equipment slot

- does negligible (10 per tick) self-damage compared to Skull's 100-500 health AND mana per tick

- lasts 3 times longer than Skull
While Skull also has a speed boost, it needs appropriate timing (with both gear swapping and usage) and is quite punishing to use due to hefty self-damage, while fruit bread is spammable due to being a consumable and is not at all punishing to use. Therefore, I believe the item needs further balancing in PVP.

Some of my personal suggestions for balancing would be:
- increasing the self-damage to a few hundred per tick (similar to Skull)
or
- making the debuff also slow you
or
- Simply disabling it in BG's/arena if it was originally intended for another use
Thanks :)
