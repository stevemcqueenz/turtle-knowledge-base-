# Healing touch in Moonkin form

## Post 166630 by Ayesha (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-27T15:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166630#p166630 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi!

My suggestion is to make healing touch and abolish poison castable in moonkin form. Wouldn't it be great if people could provide the sought after utility while enjoying the game, making it a win-win for both the druid player and their raid?

The druid will be able to off-dps and provide healing via healing touch. They won't be able to use druid's bread and butter: Rejuv and Regrowth, but they will be able to decurse, remove poison, and off-heal with only one spell in their rotation - healing touch. That would be the true support build for druid.

Shamans get to keep their essential utility, i.e. windfury, no matter the spec, so do paladins to a large extent (except for the kings buff). I don't see any reason why druid support shouldn't be a thing. You won't be as effective a healer, you won't get into a dedicated healer group, so you will lose your +healing buffs, you cant cast rejuv or swiftmend, so you cant snipe heal except for nature's swiftness every 3 min.

If a healer with too much armor is a problem, paladins exist. Healing shamans would have equal or higher amount of armor. With full leather gear, it's difficult to reach their numbers, especially paladin, and you are not as effective in moonkin form as a pala with all of the spells in their disposal.

I understand that it might make dps balance druids less desirable because of their low dmg output compared to other hybrids, to which my response is: hey, let's buff balance druid dps then to make them desirable beyond their aura and battleresses. Which in most cases is not even an issue now, because from what I've seen, guilds are just trying to hit the min numbers for raids and they are not always afforded the luxury of pick and choosing who to decline, unless they have too many of a certain class.

I have no idea what implications it might have for pvp because I'm a little PVE baby, so please enlighten me on that.

Does it break the identity of balance druid? Probably. But if I'm being honest, I'm so sick of balance druid rotation, the damage is too low, overall its not in any way enjoyable. My thought was, well, if I could heal with this aura, it would be a win-win for me and the group/raid/guild I'm in.
