# Rules required for transmog greed/need, transmogging for only lesser looks and more permanent transmogs

## Post 110447 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-29T04:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110447#p110447 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've posted the general points below in a reply, but I hope devs can find these more easily.

 1. Priority of transmogging gears is lower than that of equiping gears in need/greed system, which is a non-official rule among players. I do hope dev team can take this into account and improve the current rules and mechanism on official level. Questions like whether transmogging should be considered a higher option than selling loot for coins. (Maybe the roll&win-loot system is outdated.)

 2. There, as I recommend, could be an additional limit on transmogging that **the transmogged item that a player actually use must be over the item-level of the gear providing the look**, in case some people may transmog grey gears into epic looks or MC's gears into Naxx's. I don't think it's over the top to allow players with high-end gears to use a low-profile look, so transmogs only strengthen the feeling of fantasy, not killing it.

 3. More radical one. The transmogging bank can be combined with collections, enabling players to keep their looks without changing gears to be transmogged. For example, you can pick up a shoulder armor's look as your permanent shoulder transmog, and when you're wearing a shoulder armor, that transmog is activated. Of course, the item-level should be considered as I mentioned above.

The first point is unnecessary - just ask your group if you can need something for transmog, and if everyone is okay with it and nobody needs the item for upgrade, go for it.
Editing the entire loot roll system is complicated.

The second point is extremely stupid and will kill the entire transmog system. Hard no.

The third point is actually not a bad idea, depending on how it's implemented. Being able to save and load transmog presets, without spending all the tokens again will be a godsent for transmog fans, so we're not stuck with one transmog set all the time.
Having transmogs bound to the slot rather than the item will be great for levelers and early endgame, when you're swapping gear frequently and most people don't bother with transmogging them due to lack of tokens.
