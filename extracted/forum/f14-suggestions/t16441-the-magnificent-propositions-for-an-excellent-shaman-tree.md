# The magnificent propositions for an excellent shaman tree

## Post 112143 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-11T00:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112143#p112143 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I've done many things like this before.
But I enjoy making them and while I'm playing or reading about others' feedback, I try to create new ways to address the current issues with the shaman builds without overpowering them.

Below I'll go through the points where I'd change the shaman talents, also giving my reason why the specific change is necessary in my opinion.
Even though I like new and shiny spells, I'll keep them at a minimum. Shaman has many things, most of their problems can be solved from the inside.
Will touch upon some ability changes, too.

So.

 **Ele.**
Ele currently performs well in raids (more or less) but it is less desired elsewhere. Kinda lost its burstiness. Can 'provide the usual shaman utility'. True. But every other spec can do the same.
Has slows and can be bulky - but no real CC and totems are destroyed by Uther's Thursday night fart.
Leveling until midgame is a chore, lightning bolt is too slow. After that it just becomes boring.
The dev's idea to ally up flame shock and searing totem to unleash some kind of hidden potential in ele didn't work out. Creating a talent where you gotta spend talent points to solve a bug (searing totem pew-pew turned sheep back to enemy wizard) is also a spectacular move.
Electrify won't be remembered as the most popular move TWOW has ever made.
But I give credit where credit is due: it indeed helped in PVE since ele's initial threat spike is more in check.
But me (let's be real, I'm not qualified for this) will give an alternative which in my opinion works but it was impossible for me to test.

 **Problems in short:**
 * Kinda boring rotation.
 * No unique utility.
 * Bad burst.
 * No CC.
 * Totem squishiness in PVP.
 * Bad leveling.
 * Nature rez griefs them.

 **What I'd change:**

Earth's Grasp talent first. Gives radius to earthbind totem which is okay. Ele's way of 'CC' is keeping others far away via slows. It also gives HP to stoneclaw totem. The threat totem. The taunt totem. No.
Mighty proposal: Instead of the +HP on it, give stoneclaw totem the ability that it absorbs 50%/100% damage dealt to your other totems. Max level stoneclaw totem has 480 HP. The enemy wants to fart away your grounding totem? No more! He has to deal 480 damage if he wants to gain access to your other totems. Stoneclaw also has 30 sec CD so spamming it wouldn't be possible. Since it's an earth totem, it has some limitations like it can't protect earthbind totem. Absorption would work with cleave and AoE too, stoneclaw would receive the sum of the damage. Overdamaging it via 1 ability wouldn't transfer any damage to the protected totems.

So instead of a totally useless buff (+HP to stoneclaw) we solved a shaman PVP problem without making them OP. Once every 30 seconds, they can summon a 480 HP wall which protects their air, water and fire totems. You can still go face, destroying totems is only an option.

Elemental Warding with a question mark. Shaman tanks are so-so having a niche of spell-tanks. Elemental Warding is good but it's very situational. You face a shadow wizard and all your warding is null. At the same time I understand that fire, frost and nature magic are closer to shamans than shadow, holy or arcane.
But hear me out!
Mighty suggestion: Elemental Warding gives you 10% damage reduction against fire, frost and nature (just like now) and 5% against holy, shadow and arcane.
We kept the shaman flavour while adding consistency to the spell-tank niche.

Next problem is Call of Thunder - Lightning Mastery. The leveling ele's problem is that lightning bolt takes ages to cast, not the crit chance. Swap those two talents and elementals can from lvl 24 enjoy the full cast speed reduction of their beloved spells.
But: This could very easily lead to ele being too OP at low levels, since those bolts were not planned to be spammable this quickly.
So add a damage reduction to the cast time reduction talent which you take away with the spell crit talent. Early eles can cast lightning bolt quickly but bolts are somewhat weaker (still, DPS increase in a given time period) and they get the full power of their bolts back later, after fully learning the spell crit talent.
This helps immensely for leveling ele's by just swapping two talents (more or less).

Next problem is Eye of the Storm. Just reverse it -> When YOU deal a spell crit, you become focused, instead of waiting to receive a crit yourself. For PVE it is currently not reliable enough to qualify as a spell pushback while at PVP you get the crit and 99% sure that you are dead anyway, let's be honest.
Eles crit a lot with their spells, this logic of focused serves them much better.

Next problem is a mixture of Storm Reach and Flame Guidance. Make Storm Reach include searing totem and flame shock while the searing totem's erratic behavior should be solved via a weekly bug report, not via a talent. Flame Guidance is unnecessary.

Next problem is Electrify. Make it stack 3 times, and let shocks add to the count. Let it go 33-66-100% for crit bonus and 3-7-10% added damage. This also helps PVE raiders threat-wise without gutting burstiness.

 **What remained from the problems?:**
 * Kinda boring rotation.
 * No unique utility.
 * No CC.
 * Nature rez griefs them.

The solution for nature rez is boring but easy. Instead of fancy talents like Flame Guidance, add ele a talent which lowers the target's chance to resist nature spells. Just like most casters have it.

For unique utility, I'm thinking about a new capstone talent, next to Elemental Mastery. We are talking about a totem class, so it's a totem. Probably a spell damage totem, maybe a spell haste one. Definitely a caster buff and spell crit buffing is privatized by the boomkins.

For the boring rotation I won't propose anything since inventing a bunch of new abilities defeats the purpose of this article (making a lot but small and realistic changes to solve problems).

For CC I have an idea though.
Honestly, not exactly a CC. But ele slows his enemy down, a good movement speed buff can ensure that distance between him and the target is given.
Ghost wolf. Give it 1 min CD, make it last 5 sec only but make it instant, usable indoors, too and during combat. Shamans could get a good 'get out of jail card' without a proper CC (I don't believe that shamans should ever have access to fear or stun). You are in trouble, you press the button. You are now a wolf, movement impairment effect disappeared, you don't have any abilities, but you can run 40% faster. So you frost shock the enemy, go wolf and run. Would help the shamans escaping a sticky situation or rushing into the battle if needed. Or just to reposition quickly. 1x in a minute.

I agree that the custom totem and the wolf change are rather big ones but I think these changes answer most of ele's problems while only creating one new problem:
Travelling during leveling is kinda griefed.
For that, stay tuned, will answer it later.

_________

 **Enhancement**

Enhancement tries to be the new feral tree. But unlike druids, shamans don't have forms which magically give armor, stamina, threat and big brown paws.
The task to do this properly is not impossible but hard, since we try to fit in two roles in one talent tree.
Currently most leveling DPS shamans play as enhancement and unlike their elemental brothers, they have some unique utility, too. Relevant totems are getting somewhat stronger and bloodlust sometimes buffs allies, too.

 **As I see the biggest problems with the enhancement tree are:**
 * Has two builds, both are 80% ready.
 * No CC (solved earlier).
 * Mana can be bad, specially during leveling.
 * The 'they want agi but they gotta use windfury totem' problem.
 * Tanks have no def CD and blocked amount buff are kinda randomly given in talents.
 * Flurry is a joke.
 * Lack of defense stat for tanks.
 * DPS numbers for enh (shouldn't be a top performer but nah).

This out of the way, sharing my ideas.

Ancestral Knowledge: better than it used to be but base stats are meh stats. Let's give a simplistic alternative: you get 5% agility and 5% intellect if you spend 5TP here. Not base ones. Right now it somehow gives 10% post-item upgrades etc, making the talent way too strong. My compromise is making it a decent talent, nerfing the current state and giving enh power elsewhere.

Shield Specialization. Shaman tanks suffer from the fact that they can't just raise block chance at will. This makes blocked amount buff very situational. Remove it from the talent and replace it with something cool! Defense? Not armor, several talents give armor buffs. Max HP? Something along the way. Something reliable.

Improved Ghost Wolf doesn't make sense in this article since ghost wolf has become something else.

Lightning Strike. I like it but it's a bit overcomplicated. I intend to overcomplicate it further to solve some of enh's existing problems.
Currently when you LS, you do nature damage, physical damage and proc one of your orbs which has an effect based on the elemental shield you have equipped. It can add damage, recharge mana or restore health.
My mighty proposal is to add a 10 sec buff to this. Lightning Shield would have an immediate effect and a lasting effect. LS with lightning shield? Increased spell crit chance for 10 seconds. Water shield? 20% meditation effect for 10 seconds. Earth shield? *Add a defensive here* For example reduced damage taken from crits for 10 seconds.
Why? Would buff enhancement in a unique way without adding a new ability. Would also add some decision-making options for enh. You go for the most DPS? Keep up the LS with lightning shield. Your mana can be a problem? Switch to water shield when your shield gets expired. In the meanwhile tanks would receive a unique 'defensive-lite' to mitigate more damage.
So it helps enh DPS, grants the option to recharge mana and can be used by tanks if there is a problem.

Flurry. First, make it scale properly -> 3 6 9 12 15. Not 7 9 11 13 15. Second, justify its price. Keep it 5tp but change it a bit.
Warrior flurry: 30% haste for 3 strikes after a critical strike.
Shaman flurry: 15% haste for 3 strikes after a critical strike and while Flurry is active, your elemental shield procs are 15% more effective. Bigger procs, even from LS.

I plan these two changes to help enhancement DPS. Better LS with the passive effect, and consistent critting (which an enh should do) enabling some further passive bonuses.

Elemental Weapons: Just add some +def to the rockbiter. Let's say 15.

Enhancing totems: Just forget the +amount blocked buff from stoneskin. We can't block reliably. Instead of that just give more damage reduction or a defense buff for example to it.

I'd also add a new capstone talent to enhancement. Name it however you like, it shall be a proper defensive CD.

So from my earlier criticism let's see what points remained:

 * The 'they want agi but they gotta use windfury totem' problem.

Honestly, my solution would be removing WF weapon enhancement but allowing existing weapon enhancements to work with totems. This would however cause really big unforeseen consequences.
So I acknowledge the 'WF problem' and I won't propose anything to solve it.

___________

 **Resto.**
The besto. At least from the shaman builds.
The only thing I'd change is some push to HW and LHW and some nerf to chain heal to keep things more interesting.

 **My honest criticism with resto at the moment:**
 * Boring.
 * Healing stream totem is a joke.

My mighty suggestions:
Chain heal shouldn't be able to proc healing way. But lesser healing wave totally should.
Give undertow a 100% chance to make your next two chain heals 0,5 sec faster if you used a max rank HW or LHW BUT nerf Improved Chain Heal so that it only gives 0,5 sec cast time reduction.
So you'd have the good old chain heal by default and the fast one if you actually cared to cast other healing spells every now and then.

Restorative Totems. My only real problem. Instead of increasing the healing stream totem's effectiveness by 25% just let it have a scaling if you learn this talent. Not a really good scaling, it shouldn't challenge proper HoTs, But with some good +healing equipped, healing stream totem should have a VISIBLE EFFECT on the character's life bar. Thank you.

With these mighty proposals resto would become slightly less boring since sometimes you use other heals, too.
And healing stream totem could be salvaged.
I wish tanks would have access to a good healing stream totem but that would require more drastical changes so nah.
