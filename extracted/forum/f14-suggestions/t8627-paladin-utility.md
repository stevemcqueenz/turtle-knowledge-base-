# Paladin utility

## Post 55588 by Smerf — 2023-08-02T17:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55588#p55588 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think some simple changes to the spec tree could make some huge improvements for paladins without unbalancing the class for pvp.

(holy path) Converts consecration damage to healing.
(Grace) Exchanges divine shield's damage immunity for 40% damage reduction and recues the cooldown of divine shield by
 50%.

A deep spec talent in prot.
This gives no advantage in pvp and makes a prot pvp pally weaker in fact but allows an oh shit button for tanking . Also an actual taunt would be nice instead of a twostep castable seal.

A deep spec talent in holy.
Converts consecration to a healing spell so paladins can have aoe group heal.  These are trades of one talent for another without adding new things or giving anything extra which keeps things balanced allowing for better utility for their roll and no gain against other players or damage.

I don't have good gear on my paladin yet, but I feel retribution needs mana regen to make it useable. I don't know about its damage yet because i know as crt goes up on pally gear it makes a big difference but if you can only fight for 12 seconds before running out of manna it's not very wgood.  I do more damage as a healer in pvp using seal and holy shock... So, ret suggestions mana cost reduction for abilities perhaps.
