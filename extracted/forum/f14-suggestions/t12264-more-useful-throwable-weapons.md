# More useful throwable weapons.

## Post 81644 by Ulukay (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-12-18T13:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81644#p81644 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Why are "throwable" weapons so useless? Even the worst bow will be better than throwing weapons. It would be good to make it more in demand, for example: give the ability to throw it on the move, removing the casting time. This would have a lot of uses, including for the survivalist hunter.
And also, it would be good if the hunter was given the opportunity to use at least some of his distance skills with throwing weapons.
