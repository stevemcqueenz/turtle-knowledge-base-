# Remove race restriction for goblin shredder mount!

## Post 84365 by Snoodydood (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-01-12T02:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84365#p84365 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The goblin shredder mount available for sale at exalted with the durotar labor union is the only mount restricted to a single race, and while i understand the logic, i think it's a bit unfair considering two things;
 1. The pounder mount is available to any race and is essentially the same so why can't orcs and trolls fit in a shredder?
 2. the alliance equivalent, the gnomish mecha spider mount is available to **ALL** alliance races.
Why the bias? Why can't horde work to get a unique engineer mount as well?
I think with these two things in mind, it would not be unreasonable to make the shredders available to all horde races.
