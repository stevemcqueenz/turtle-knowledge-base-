# Warsong should not be endless

## Post 77779 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-17T06:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77779#p77779 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Original suggestion:Currently WSG ends when one team bring 3 flags or
when one team leaves battle and battle gets closed by timeout.

If teams are willing to fight, then in current state WSG is prone to last for hours.
It might be fun, but is tiresome as hell. From honor farming perspective it is also inefficient.
It leads to situation when players prefer AV for honor farm (because it is more efficient)
or they dodge enemy premade on Arathi Basin for fast victories

I see multiple solutions here:
 1. Limit time to 20-30 minutes. Team with higher amount of flags will be considered winner. Team with 3 flags should be considered winner and game finishes early.
 2. Limit amount of respawns per side. Spirit healer runs out of mana and after long fight in middle, some faction will eventually lose because no reinforcements spawn.
 3. Add kill count to battleground progress. If you need 300 progress, then bringing flag should give 100 progress, flag return and attempt should give 25 progress and kill should give 1 progress.
 4. Add FC debuff like retail. Though I don't really like that option

In patch 1.18, debuff to FC was added. I am not sure of timing. Probably each 5-10 minutes, FC gets stacking debuff +10% incoming damage.
