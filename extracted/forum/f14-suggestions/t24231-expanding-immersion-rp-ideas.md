# Expanding Immersion: RP Ideas

## Post 170406 by Smokeybbq — 2026-03-09T15:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170406#p170406 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

One of the things Turtle WoW already does really well is making the world feel more immersive and meaningful than standard Vanilla in many small ways. I would love to see that direction expanded even further with more worldbuilding, social features, and roleplay-friendly systems.

I tried to pair these ideas with small gameplay effects

 1) Cooking
 - Feast (placeable)
 - Sharing Meal
 - Tavern Dishes (Inns serve small regional dishes)
 - Flavour Text for regional dishes

 2) Inns
 - Fed and Well Rested Buff (from Inn)
 - Accumulate Rested XP faster when sitting/laying/food buffed
 - Notice Board with storytelling (hints for the region/questing)
 - Guest Book written by players (could have player experience (funny stories, hints) [resets]
 - Rent a room (once per day: recieve hospitality bonus -> reduces hearthstone cooldown by 20 minutes, access to bank (might be too strong), travel ration)

 3) Survival
 - magical tents with larger interiors that can function as cozy shelters

 4) Class-Themed World Building
First of all, I love places like Moonglade or Ravenholdt Manor, and I am excited for the upcoming Earthen Ring changes.
Travel utility, class progression, oder repeatable class-specific interactions

Heres an idea for Warlock:
 - Add Ritual Shrines for Warlock -> some shrine ideas-> (a) sacrife health to recieve a soul shard, b) sacrife health to recieve ritual chalk (1x use (reduces cast time of next summon) c) change succubus <-> incubus shrine

 5) weather effects
 - more harsh weather
 - more weather effects (fog/wind/ash/heat haze)
 - Small fishing bonus during rain
 - Weather should add immersion and small situational gameplay, not annoying penalties. For example, heat does not necessarily need a buff or debuff. Even stronger visual effects, lighting, haze, and atmosphere in hot zones would already make the world feel more alive.
