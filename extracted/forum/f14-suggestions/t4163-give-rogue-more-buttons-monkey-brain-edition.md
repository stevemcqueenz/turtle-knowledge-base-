# Give Rogue more buttons. [Monkey Brain Edition]

## Post 24910 by Mothernature — 2022-09-12T06:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24910#p24910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Im a former rogue main turned into a once again rogue main cause of the single idea of Surprise Attack and Deadly Throw giving the class a new shine.
Rogue is aesthetically my favorite class, and thematically too. However my biggest gripe has always been the lack of buttons to press. (Smooth Brain, absolutely)

The primary reason why I am leveling a 3rd rogue on the server. .. Yes 3 rogues on Turtle WoW is all because I love the leveling skills: Riposte, Ghostly Strike etc.
Both of which fill no purpose in raids and is bummed down to having 3 buttons to press in raids and then some cooldowns.

Sinister Strike, Evicerate and Slice and Dice.

Having an ability like Surprise Attack which was removed for being too powerful as I heard would spice up the gameplay a lot.

Why remove surprise attack completely? Instead of removing the ability, rework it. As it was written previously:

 **Surprise Attack** *30 Energy Cost, 15 seconds cooldown*

A strike that deals 90% weapon damage. This attack cannot be blocked, dodged or parried. Awards 1 combo point.

I understand why this was removed. It would once every 15 seconds remove the need to cast sinister strike and change 1 ss for Surprise attack. And then back into sinister strike.
 1. It wouldn't feel very good to play
 2. It would just be better than sinister strike

Removing it is smart for balancing but it's not fun!

In my forum lurker opinion, I think rogues should be given surprise attack back. Simply because its fun!
However, change it to 1. Not gain a combo point  or give it a secondary requirement like Riposte or backstab?

Second ability I want to talk about the coolest and most useless ability: Deadly Throw.
First off, where do you get this spell? There seems to be inconsistencies in if this spell even exists to begin with. Some say you get it at level 60, some say it doesn't exist at all. I can't find it in the class changes but I can see that the talent for Throwing Weapon Specialization mentions Deadly throw. So which is it?

With my qualms about the ability: It's useless.

It does low damage, it is a combo point spender, also does it really really require equipped throwing weapons as some people say?
Since I don't actually fully understand it I can't talk about it at length. But:
 1. I haven't seen it yet, Im level 50 and still haven't had access to the ability yet. I think that's weird. Its not like its really powerful. The least we can have is being able to get it earlier.. right?
 2. Give it 30 seconds cooldown. Make it have a higher poison chance and if it has a requirement to have equipped throwing weapons, remove it. Or make throwing weapons not be ammunitiion and give them stats. Cause, whatever throwing weapons are right now its dumb and only useable before you get another ranged weapon you can use as a statstick.

As you probably realised in this post, I didn't give it as much love as I should. I really mostly wanted to blow off some steam about my disappointment in the rogue changes, but also in myself for not being able to read properly and being so hyped up by the new abilities that I was blinded by greed and lust for power. Also I suck at writing so please take my words with a bit of salt. Incredibly dyslectic but non the less filled with love for the server.

Whatever happens to my suggestion, trash them, thrash em, stick em in a stew. But give rogues more buttons to press.
Cause we rogues are simple people, we just like to press buttons. Just like the monkey girl gamers, boys and smooth brains we are.

Thanks for coming to my Turtle Talk.

Yeah, Deadly Throw is a total dud.

I does the damage of a single hunter's auto-shot for 5 CPs, doesn't scale with any talent, requires a throwing weapon equipped (they might as well not exist) and has a slow travel time. I did the Ravenholdt 5 junkbox turn-ins for the 15 AP throwing knife just to test this out and the skill is completely worthless.
It shouldn't require a throwing weapon until those are reworked to be viable and to actually exist. It should do more base damage and scale with all talents Eviscerate scales with. The animation is also just that of a normal weapon throw and not the actual DT from expansions, which is faster and actually looks distinct.

You wouldn't use the Surprise Attack over SS for DPS, the whole point of the ability was being able to securely get 1 CP for SND/Flourish/KS. I'd like to have it back.
