# Please consider reverting weaponskill

## Post 106960 by Snoodydood (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-07T20:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106960#p106960 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The new patch has brought a lot of fun (even if controversial) changes to twow and so far i am having fun experimenting with different specs with my characters, but the one thing I think is being purely detrimental is how weapon skill has changed.
Previously, 310 weapon skill was considered the soft cap for reducing glancing blows to as much as we could for maximum DPS but now that has been raised to 315, and lower weapon skill below that has taken a big hit from the glancing blow numbers. It's true that there are more items that offer weapon skill but the problem with that is now those are basically requirements for every single melee class in the game. This means we all have to compete for these items in raids instead of playing with the gear we want or already had even break up tier sets because we need gloves with +7 weapon skill to get back the dps we had before. Wasn't the point of the change to weaponskill and racials to make the whole meta less punishing to players? So we didn't have to rely on certain races to maximize dps and just play what we want? It seems now hat it's more punishing than ever and even just having +3 weaponskill is desirable from those races. Plus, now we have to spend a lot of gold just to get the extra +5 from the book quests JUST to match what we had before without it. isn't this just purely punishing players for no reason? it seems like simply reverting the system to how it was before while also introducing the extra skill quests would have been enough to even it all out for everyone regardless of class and race.
