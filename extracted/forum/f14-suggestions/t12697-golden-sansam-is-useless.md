# Golden Sansam is useless

## Post 84580 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-01-13T21:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84580#p84580 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For a herb that spawns in endgame zones, seeing Golden Sansam nodes is very disappointing. It seems it doesn't have any good uses, vendors for 1 silver and usually sells for just as much on the AH, because nobody wants it. Unlike other endgame herbs, it doesn't have a chance to drop Black Lotus.
Despite this, it can and will spawn in the place of more valuable herbs and usually nobody will bother to pick it up because it is so worthless.

I propose to give it some kind of use - make some new potion recipes that require it, and give its nodes a chance to drop Black Lotus or something else that is not trash.

Interestingly, in the database it is listed as a reagent for creating Scrolls of Protection (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=46689>). I think this is a great idea and should be implemented, if not as part of a new profession (Inscryption), then as part of Enchanting. Using it as ink for endgame scrolls is the way to go!
