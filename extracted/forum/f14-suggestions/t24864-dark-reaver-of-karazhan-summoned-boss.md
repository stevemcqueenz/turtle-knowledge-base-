# Dark Reaver of Karazhan summoned boss

## Post 175341 by Lane13 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-04-15T14:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175341#p175341 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

The summoned version of the Dark Reaver of Karazhan spawns in an area often traversed by players. Due to the easily accessible Karazhan 10 raid, people will come across the item to summon the world boss, complete the quest and leave it there due to ignorance or griefing.

So when an actual raid is formed to kill the boss, there's usually a tagged version standing on the spot and being aggroed all the time. Summoned world bosses despawn after 30 minutes of no action, which is essentially almost impossible for this one due to the spot it's in, because players accidentally bump into him and aggro.

The previous two kills of this boss that I've done went very well because we submitted tickets to the GMs kindly asking to remove the tagged mob, and they did. However, GM Septentrion refused to remove the boss today, citing the 30-minute rule that can realistically never be met in this case.

Another player wrote a ticket asking for help and a GM decided to move the boss away but not kill it. Therefore, we tried summoning a new one and couldn't because only *one* can be active at a time. This meant that we lost the summoning item and thus, the chance to kill the boss. You cannot even kill the tagged mob, as doing so will give you a lockout for the rest of the week, not letting you loot any items from new kills.

I thought the quality of TWoW and its GMs was better than this. Considering how poorly placed the boss is, why can't they just remove the tagged mob that is obviously not going to be killed by anyone instead of negatively affecting players like this? Every single time I tried to make a raid for the Dark Reaver, there was also a griefer constantly pulling the boss to reset the timer and disrupt the forming raid, which is obviously done on purpose. They could even summon a new one when they see there's a group forming and block the attempt as long as they have a summoning item.

I hope both the GMs and TWoW reconsider how they tackled this situation today because it was nothing short of Blizzard-like "support" on an otherwise excellent server.
