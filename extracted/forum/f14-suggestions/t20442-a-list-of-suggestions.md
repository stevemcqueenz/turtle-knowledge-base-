# A list of suggestions

## Post 141089 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-24T11:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141089#p141089 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!
Sharing a list of ideas below.

 1. Increasing flight master speed. Not allowing porting to instances is probably the right call but still, making flight between locations easier would be a big QoL improvement.
If we wanna be fancy, maybe create a flight master faction and speed bonus increases with rep.

 2. Receiving world buffs in each faction capital. World is overpopulated (think about SW lag). It would be cool to distribute players more evenly. Would also make the world more alive.
If we wanna be fancy, add fashonistas to each capital, too.

 3. Either a further tea nerf or a healthstone buff. I'd consider a secondary effect after consuming a HS. For example, increased healing received by n% for m seconds. Would make HS more valuable.

 4. It's a bit generic, but show some love to assassination rogue, subtlety rogue, kitty druid and shadow priest. Most priest would happily exchange pain spike for an instant casr vampiric embrace.

 5. Add some interesting reward to SWV (way less visited than BM) and possibly an easier access for horde, but one, which couldn't be abused for ganking.

 6. A FP to Deadwind Pass, near Kara. Magic stick helps porting there but it's realistically for regular Kara40 raiders. Kara Crypts and Kara10 are visited by lesser-geared people who don't have the instant port. Vanilla had no FP there because the whole place was empty (kinda).

 7. Give something cool to Jandice. People ignore her wing way too often. Same for Baelzar (?, whatever, the BRD golem, near the entrance).

 8. Making LFT less miserably bad. Also, add BRD - Marshal as an option. Maybe also UBRS.

 9. This is a bigger one but I'd love to have caravans hopping from place to place, let's say with 40% movement speed buff. People could use them as a bus at low level. Was proposed by someone else but liking it.
