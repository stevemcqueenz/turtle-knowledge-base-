# Weapon Skill

## Post 42648 by Bubblebutts — 2023-04-22T22:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42648#p42648 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I am new to Turtle WoW. I've been playing hardcore and I've been having a lot of fun.

I want to keep playing, but Vanilla WoW has a fatal flaw, and I hope that, this being a private server, that we can implement a minor change that would have a resounding positive effect on the health of this server.

Anyone who is a veteren to Vanilla WoW knows just how impactful weapon skill is. The problem is that impact is so massive it highly discourages rolling anything other than Human/Orc for any melee class.

Don't get me wrong, I personally like the idea of weapon skill. I like the intricacies of it, and I like how my knowledge of the mechanics can set me apart--even while leveling. The issue is that it forces players who like to optimize into a single path.

I propose that there be an implementation of Weapon Skill Mastery: I suggest that we explore the possibility that weapon skills can be mastered after the skill reaches 300.

Perhaps weapon masters provide some way of giving a player the opportunity to permanently increase weapon skill by 5. This could be done through a long quest chain, or perhaps a single purchase of the skill--with a high cost, of course.

I love this game, but honestly, I'm tired of playing human. I'm tired of Elwynn forest being so packed that you cannot move without bumping into another warrior. I want to see more racial diversity, and I want to play a dwarf.

Increasing the opportunities for other races to obtain weapon skill would improve the game far more than it would hurt it. I can't even think of any downsides, other than maybe the Edgemaster's Handguards suddenly dropping in price. That's a compromise I'm willing to take.

What do you all think?

Bumping for posterity.

I have been looking through old forum posts about the topic of weapon skill. Over the past two years, there have been more than ten threads made about this topic alone. There is also discussion of weapon skill in other threads not specifically targeting this topic.

Multiple people have suggested my idea of 'weapon mastery' as well, in addition to other possible *fixes*:
- Ugoboom (who has been mentioned earlier) had an interesting implementation of expertise in the form of weapon skill.

- I've seen someone suggest talents that give weapon skill.

- Many people have asked for the implementation of more weapon skill racials.

- Some people have even asked for weapon skill to be removed entirely.
This doesn't even cover all of the suggestions I've seen so far.

Out of all of the threads I've seen, one thread has had a moderator respond:

> We do not currently plan to remove weapon skills, as they are a staple feature of the old-RPG feel in vanilla WoW. While they do impact endgame, they are a very immersive feature.
>
>  Racial changes are more likely to be implemented if you would like to suggest replacements.

Posted by Akalix in this thread: [viewtopic.php?t=3897&hilit=weapon+skill](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=3897&hilit=weapon+skill)

As this is the only 'official' response I have seen about this topic, I would like to respond to it specifically:

This post by Akalix implies that weapon skill must come from sources that are proven to be immersive or support an RPG-style atmosphere. I agree--design decisions that are supported by the atmosphere we inhabit are great choices.

Weapon skill is an immersive feature, and I appreciate its intricacies. However, being forced to play Human, Orc, or Goblin is anti-immersive. I would be more immersed if my Dwarf had Mace or Axe skills (if Racial skills are the hill we are to die on).

In addition, if we are staying true to Vanilla, we can presume that buying weapon skills to begin with is also immersive. Indeed, we already have the purchasing of other skills, like professions and horse riding (which you become increasingly proficient by forking over some gold).

Why not, then, allow us to buy weapon mastery?

Similarly, racial proficiencies in weapon skills are not inherently true to the RPG-style approach we are looking for. For example, we already level our weapon skills from 1 to 300. It only makes sense that we could improve our skills further after hitting level 60.

I want to emphasize here that I'm not necessarily claiming that my approach is better than any of the other plethora suggestions about improving weapon skill. And I'm not here to make design decisions for the Turtle WoW team.

The way it is now, weapon skill is a well-understood mechanic of the game; if you are interested in performing at a high level in end game content, you are forced to choose a narrow path of progression that does not improve the immersion or quality of player's experiences.

Please, at the very least, give each race at least one weapon skill proficiency to improve the lives of all melee classes.
