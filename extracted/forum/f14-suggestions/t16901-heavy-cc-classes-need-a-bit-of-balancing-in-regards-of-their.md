# Heavy CC classes need a bit of balancing in regards of their ability to kill most classes while in an CC. 2.0

## Post 115439 by Papum69 — 2025-01-04T22:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115439#p115439 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Been doing some PvP and have been noticing that in the state things are some classes mainly locks can just kill you with various cc's they have at their disposal stuns/fear/slow with little to no cd without any diminishing returns plus the time spend in the cc's against players are completly unbalanced, and charm/silence depending wich pet is out.
Basiclly they kill you without you being able to do much in return if not having any way of interrupting spells.

Also mentioning they have an very high damage output in an short amount of time wich coupled with the above mentioned is simply to much. Way to fix it make cc's have an seperate reduced duration time against players (10 sec. max.) as what happend in retail in the early expansions because of the new abilities the respective classes gained and by so to say if giving such abilities in TWoW you should also have in consideration the unchanged state of CC's. Implement diminishing returns by so making continuous further usses of such cc's last shorter times and the probability to break on damage. And as last an heavy cc class that has instant casts and can self heal should at least have to buildup for an reasonable time to reach an higher damage output instead of in a space of a couple seconds being able to dish out enough damage to kill an player during the first 1 to 2 CC's.
