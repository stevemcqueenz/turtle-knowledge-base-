# Arcane Power - change to the punishment

## Post 148625 by CloudDistrict — 2025-08-27T10:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148625#p148625 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone!

Could you please consider changing the punishment of failing the mana-management mechanic for the Arcane Mage capstone - Arcane Power? At the moment, Arcane Power increases the caster's casting speed and passively drains mana. If the caster drops below 10% of their maximum mana while Arcane Power is still active, the caster dies.

I really like the idea of having a surge of power during which the caster has to manage their mana but I dislike the current punishment. This is the only ability in this game that has such a dire consequence. As you can probably understand, this punishment makes Arcane Power a no-go talent for the HC community, rendering the entire Arcane tree a very unpopular choice (unviable even) despite the amazing work the TWoW team put into making it really fun.

I agree that there should be a severe punishment for failing the mana management, but not death. My suggestion would be something along these lines: if the caster falls below 10% mana during Arcane Power, they enter Arcane Exhaustion, which increases casting speed by 60% (or even 100%) for 20 seconds.

This kind of punishment is still VERY dire but it would be much more viable for the HC people.

Thank you!
