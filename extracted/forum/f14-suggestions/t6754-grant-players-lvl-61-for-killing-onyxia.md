# Grant players lvl 61 for killing Onyxia

## Post 42549 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-04-22T09:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42549#p42549 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Current situation.
When player reaches lvl 60, he starts progressing in gear instead of progressing in levels.
Thats why two different lvl 60 players could have completely different powers.
It lead to situation while on battleground 60 players in green-blue gear meet players in t2-t3 gear.
Battlegrounds are not balanced by gear, but they are bracketed by level.

Idea is to reflect gear progression with level of the player and separate t2-t3 guys from blue-green guys.

Proposal.
When player is in raid which kills Onyxia or Razorgore (first t2 bosses) and this player is level 60, then he should get additional level.
There should be additional battleground bracket 61-61. All players who have t2 will be separated from rest of playerbase who is in gearing phase.
Achieving r13 should grant 61 lvl too.
Levels of mobs in t2,t2.5,t3 dungeons should be increased by 1.
Naxx and AQ40 should have hard requirement of lvl 61 to enter.
 --I don't know if additional talent point will be good or bad, need opinions of others--
