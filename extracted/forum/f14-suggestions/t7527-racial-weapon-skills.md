# Racial Weapon Skills

## Post 48114 by Saerynn — 2023-05-30T20:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48114#p48114 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey Devs,

I am sure its been suggested before, but I would like to again bring up the issue of Weapon Skill for "optimal" melee performance. I personally think its a cool part of vanilla wow but I do not like that only Orcs and Humans have this skill built in, there for causing other races to be sub par for melee of primarily the Warrior class.

In game the only good way to resolve this is to get Edgemasters, which personally is an item I dislike strongly. Locking up your gloves slot forever unless you play Orc with axes or Humans with swords/maces is a feels bad.

I think the best fix would be to distribute some weapon skill racials to more races in  a lore friendly way, so that more races can be "optimal" with different weapons without absolutely requiring 1 item to be worn forever. It dosent have to be split this way but I have included an example list below.

Orc - Axe, Fist
Tauren - Mace, Polearm
Undead - Sword, Dagger
Troll - Bow, Sword.
Goblin - Gun, Dagger

Human - Sword, Mace
Dwarf - Gun, Mace
Night Elf - Bow, Polearm.
Gnome - Gun, Dagger
High Elf - Bow, Sword
