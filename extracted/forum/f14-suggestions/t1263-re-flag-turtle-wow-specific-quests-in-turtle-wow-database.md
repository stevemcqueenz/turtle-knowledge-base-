# Re-flag Turtle-WoW specific quests in turtle wow database

## Post 7479 by Nachtritter — 2020-09-05T17:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7479#p7479 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Digging through the growing pile of turtle wow specific quests looking for something for a specific zone or dungeon is a little daunting, especially to newcomers. Could we get these flagged for their zones/class/race/dungeons/whatever *in addition to* their being marked as turtle-wow specific?

For example [Arugal's Folly](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=60108) only shows up in the database under turtle-wow quests(also, it's marked wrong - it's alliance only, not both...) I would suggest that it showed up under [Shadowfang Keep](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quests=2.209) *as well* - perhaps a flag can be added to the category table so turtle wow quests have a category mark like, using the above example:
Code: Select all

```
Category:
Shadowfang Keep
Turtle-WoW Custom
```

This will make it much easier to find turtle wow specific quests as well as quite possibly making it easier for people who encounter bugs on them to find out what went wrong and get bug reports submitted.
