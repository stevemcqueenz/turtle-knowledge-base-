# Protection Paladin New Talent Tree - Constructive Criticism

## Post 103728 by Verdict — 2024-10-18T22:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103728#p103728 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Heyo,

First of all, a gigantic applause for the Turtle Devs and all involved for this huge endeavor which is the Tower of Karazhan Patch.
From reworking the Talents, Kara 40 and all the hard work being done for Class Fantasy, Old Content breathing and making this experience called Turtle WoW so amazing!

With the Talent Calculators and Dev Note updates in regards to Paladin & specifically the Protection tree and the playstyle it promotes I really must address some major concerns whilst knowing that things may change and other factors may take place such as:

 - Tier set itemization changes and bonuses that might affect the playstyle like alter cooldowns and priorities.
 - Itemization outside of sets which might prove important as well to determine the course of how Protection will play.
 - Changes from the Dev Team which might already be considered based on feedback.

 1. **The talent tree of Protection bears a contradicting resemblance in a similar way to how the original Protection Paladin tree was made to function - as a crit retaliating specialization best put and described as a "Martyr" but the game's design went in a different direction making the spec literally useless in practice.**
 - This comes in **primarily** from *Righteous Strikes* new talent which shifts and provides an option for the new stacking *Zeal* passive from the new *Crusader Strike* to provide additional benefits such as Block % and Attack Speed %.

 2. This issue resides in the shared CD of *Crusader & Holy Strikes*.
 - The idea that this provides "choice" and is "reactive" is far from the reality of one taking priority over the other due to *Righteous Strikes*.
 - In general, having a talent and a spell that promotes prolonged fights where the *Zeal Stacks* can be gather to 3, but in reality we calculated that in full raid environment you will still be using *Holy Strike* in priority and spending time on *Zeal* stacking via *Crusader Strike* is at face value a waste of time.
 - The general conflict here is the question "Do I need threat or not?", that's not a question that your kit should answer, it should be your itemization instead. Having a scenario where you have to alternate between **Holy Strike** (low damage, higher threat) and Crusader Strike (higher damage, lower threat) is **not fun when they share cooldown**.

 3. **The capstone slap in the face.**
 - Instead of making *Holy Shield* a *Shield Bash* ability for similar damage that activates the buff & giving the Paladin a *Shield Wall* as capstone, a direction and argument was presented that the capstone should be a *Shield Wall* ability that's on a 3 minute cooldown and is a *Shield Bash* instead. Yeah.

The rest of the Talent Tree with it's options are cool and there is synergy.

Such as *Crusader Strike* giving Block to help with the avoidance of the class in AoE scenarios, helping with Reckoning, and in general synergize with the fantasy of "blockadin" which is superb.

 4. My suggestion.
 - Make *Righteous Strikes* a smarter talent that plays into the fantasy and doesn't overpower *Holy Strike* or *Crusader Strike*, but truly promotes rotational use which rewards both abilities.
Example: *Crusader Strike* increases the damage and threat of next *Holy Strike* by 50%. *Holy Strike* makes your next *Crusader Strike* grant 3 stacks of **Zeal**.

 - Totally delete the "Bulwark of the Righteous" and rework it ground up, SURELY you see how ridiculous this statement of yours in the Dev Note reads:
"This ability can serve both as an emergency threat generation and a powerful defensive cooldown."
Whoops, my Holy Strike missed, LET ME BLOW MY SHIELD WALL UP. Guys, this is just... *heavy breathing* wow. Plot twist, in order for me to Shield Wall - I need to hit something.

Outside of **Righteous Strikes** and **Bulwark of the Righteous** being total errors in game design that reminisce on someone who had to cook a Salad and a Steak but did them both in the pan at the same time, the rest of the Trees, and Talents in them seem really nice, well thought of and overall exciting.

Sorry for the last part of this being a little too much, I'm just dumbfound.

Love all the work and passion you guys put towards Turtle <3

Regards,
