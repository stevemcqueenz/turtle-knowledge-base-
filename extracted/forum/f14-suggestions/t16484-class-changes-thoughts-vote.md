This poll is problematic for several reasons:

 *It was posted weeks after the patch release, which means that some players who disliked the patch may have already quit the game and won't see the poll. This skews the results toward active players who are more likely to be accepting of the changes.

 *The poll doesn't include an option for those who are neutral or haven't formed an opinion yet. This exclusion forces such respondents to pick another option, potentially leading to "social desirability bias," where people lean toward more positive answers because there isn't a suitable alternative.

 *Even many critics of the patch are likely to select "I like CC2, but want tweaks" because it feels closest to their perspective. These players might appreciate the idea of class changes but dislike the implementation, leading them to choose an option that doesn't fully represent their stance. Some might hope "tweaks" means something more significant, further distorting the results.

 *The poll lacks an option for those who support class changes but prefer a different style or philosophy, such as "I want comprehensive or moderate class changes, but in the style of CC1." This omission forces people to choose a less accurate option, like "tweaks," because they hope it implies a rollback or redesign.

I think something like this would be better:

 1.I am very satisfied with CC2.

 2.I am satisfied with CC2, but I think it needs minor tweaks.

 3.I am neutral or have not yet formed an opinion about CC2.

 4.I am dissatisfied with CC2 and believe significant changes are needed.

 5.I am very dissatisfied with CC2 and would prefer to return to CC1 or its philosophy.

 6.I want comprehensive class changes over time, but in the style or philosophy of CC1.

 7.I would prefer the original 1.12 vanilla talents with only minor or conservative class changes.

 8.I would prefer the original 1.12 vanilla talents without any class changes.

Edit: Turbosaxophonic made a couple of additional points that are good:

 *Being able to see the results of the poll before voting can influence your vote.

 *Such substantial changes to the game should require a significant majority in order to go through, in order to reflect a broader and more stable consensus within the population (again, look at OSRS).
