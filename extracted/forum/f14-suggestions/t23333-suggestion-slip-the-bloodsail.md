# Suggestion: 'Slip' the Bloodsail

## Post 163127 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-19T02:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163127#p163127 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

'Slip', the shady quest giver in Booty Bay, gives out quests that reduce your reputation with the various Steamwheedle Cartel factions and increase it with the Bloodsail Buccaneers. Although his quests don't outright spell it out, the implication is that he is one of several Bloodsail-affiliated characters that have planted themselves in the port. However, he is still affiliated with Booty Bay by reputation; in the unlikely event that you're visiting Booty Bay while hated by them, he will be just as hostile towards you than the rest of the town despite obviously working against their interests.

My suggestion would be to give him the same treatment as Garfield Sparkblast's appearance in Booty Bay: don't make him affiliated with the Bloodsail Buccaneers outright, as this would prevent the acquisition of his quests, but instead make him friendly to all characters regardless of Booty Bay reputation.
