# Increase the magnitude of Enchant Gloves - Major Strength to +15 strength

## Post 90326 by Borefficz (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-03-16T13:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90326#p90326 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Enchant Gloves - Major Strength is an enchant that grants +9 strength. It is unlocked by turning in 25 Tokens of Blood (375 Arena Marks of Honor), which is a fairly significant time investment especially if you consider the fact that on non-Blood Ring days it's hard to get into a lot of matches on a given day.

Since the other available enchant for that slot gives +15 agility (which is also a great stat for warriors and druids) the 9 strength enchant is nearly useless. In 6 months of being an enchanter and thousands of enchants applied I was asked for that +9 strength on gloves twice. Bringing it in line with the agility enchant would make it a great alternative for characters that greatly benefit from strength. An alternative solution is nerfing the agility enchant to +9.
