# A few small changes to the end content

## Post 117615 by Galois — 2025-01-22T22:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117615#p117615 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear all,

I would like to introduce you to my idea for the turtle wow end content.

Motivation
Vanilla/Classic raid dungeons have never been known for being challenging. But after a few weeks of leveling up, even NAXX turns from a relatively relaxing dungeon into a bad joke. The increased damage can cause many bosses to die after just 2 minutes. In many boss fights you don't see a second phase because the boss fights end too quickly. And the excessive use of consumables such as protection pots negates the other boss mechanics. Especially for the end content of a game, running from boss to boss without any challenge is simply boring.

Solution
I would like to introduce a second mode for NAXX for geared raids. From now on, I will call the previous one the first mode. This will reflect NAXX at its current level of difficulty. It is for guilds who are entering NAXX for the first time and want to get to grips with the mechanics. The second mode is a slight adjustment that will do nothing more than force the raid to play the bosses as they were originally designed.
The easiest way to achieve this is to make two changes:
 - Changing the boss HP. Example Heigan: Simply double or triple the HP so you have to dance.
 - Change the duration of protection pots from 1h to 10s and the cd to 5 min

why would the players do something like that? Apart from the fact that the dungeon could be a bit more fun again, I have a few suggestions here. The loot could be reduced somewhat in the first mode. Special weapons and trinkets (from the current NAXX loot table) only drop in second mode. Important: The loot advantage from the second mode must not be so significant that the second mode leads back to the first mode at some point.

The second mode will therefore not become a hard mode, although I would welcome it if we could introduce one in the future. But that's another topic.

What are your opinions on this?
