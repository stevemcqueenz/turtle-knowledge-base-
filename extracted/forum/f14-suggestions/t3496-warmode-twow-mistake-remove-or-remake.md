# Warmode. Twow Mistake. Remove or Remake.

## Post 21080 by Aszura (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-04T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21080#p21080 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After 1 year i came to the conclusion that the Warmode was one of the big mistakes on Turtle Wow, and everyone can see that with the increase of toxicity and ganking.

This is a RP/PVE server. "Slow and Steady" remember?

If people want PVP, do /pvp, it's very simple, or even better, go to a PVP server.

Let's also remember that the only reason for warmode, was the group of PVP players complaining about the /pvp disabling itself after some minutes, which is the normal for a PVE server...

If warmode was just the /pvp for ever, that would be cool and wouldn't have any problem at all, but the great problem was the XP Boost.

Why?

Let's take a step back to the "Slow and Steady"

Turtle Wow was all about the journey, the leveling, exploring the world, making friends, creating memories... so why have you devs added a XP Boost to attract people for PVP, which in the end, has increased the amount of toxic players AND ALSO completly broke the entire Turtle Wow slogan?

That's why i came with the suggestion to either completly remove it or remake.

I know some people like warmode, and of course writting /pvp is too hard... so just remove the XP Boost if you guys want to keep the glyph.

Alternatively.
If you guys want the XP boost, make a new challenge.
One that increase all damage you recieve while increasing the XP Boost, that would make people group together to play the PVE of the RP/PVE server we have here, instead of camping low level zones...
