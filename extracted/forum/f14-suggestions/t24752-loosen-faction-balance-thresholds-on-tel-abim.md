## Post 174336 by Urthius — 2026-04-07T13:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174336#p174336 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

My main argument is that we should be looking at PvP activity to determine faction balance, not raw character counts. Expecting Ally and Horde to PvP at similar rates is a reasonable default assumption, but empirically isn't true.

For example: every 15 minutes, count how many Ally/Horde are either in BGs or queued for BGs. Take weekly averages and adjust faction balancing based on that. On Tel'Abim, I suspect that the skew is 60/40 in favor of Horde, at least.

If this is the case, it doesn't make sense to lock Ally character creation, especially when the server needs more population.

Other metrics should also be considered, of course, including raw character counts. But something like the example above should definitely be a factor.

There is a lag between when players join and when they are PvP active, so any changes should be conservative.

The slippery slope can be avoided by making decisions based on such data.
