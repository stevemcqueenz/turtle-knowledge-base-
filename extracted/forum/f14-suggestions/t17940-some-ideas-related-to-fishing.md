# Some ideas related to fishing =)

## Post 122200 by Ckirion — 2025-03-10T16:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122200#p122200 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey i just wanted to post some ideas related to fishing, making it more fun and a little bit more valuable as a farming method. =)

 1. Improve loot in Reinforced Locked Chest and Mithril Bound Trunk. Here i would be nice with perhaps a little bit more gold in the loot, bit higher chance of getting something green, adding some rares that can drop in these, and even a very low chance of some Epics dropping in these would make it super fun to open. =D

 2. Recipe For Large Raw Mightfish. I always love fishing up this fish because its semi-rare and looks exotic. however its value isnt that good, so my feelings are for naught.. I would love for there to be a Max level cooking recipe for Large Raw Mightfish. What it should be, i am not sure, as long as it doesnt take away from any of the other fishes usability. Perhaps +400 Armor would be cool for this fish, would be usable for casters. Perhaps something related to PvP? I dont know, but i really feel like this fish is missing something that should be there.

 3. Something fishing related that improves fishing skill that can only be fished up randomly. Like perhaps a line that increases fishing skill by +3/+5? Perhaps a Lure that does something cool? i dont know, but i feel like there should be something fishing skill related that can only drop from random fishing.. =)

Thank you!
