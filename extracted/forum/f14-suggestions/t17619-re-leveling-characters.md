# Re-leveling Characters

## Post 120270 by Arraris — 2025-02-18T06:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120270#p120270 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Considering all the new leveling challenges being constantly added to TWoW, I would appreciate if the team would consider an option to "reset" a level 60 character and return it back to level 1 so that players can try out the new leveling challenges on their primary character. It would also allow older players to experience a lot of the newly added characters on their mains, without needing to make Alts they have no intention of playing further at 60.

I know that allowing a level 1 to keep a bunch of level 60 gear, items and benefits could be overpowered for twinking - and would be already if all of the bags/items/gear/bank of the level 60 were to be "locked" - only able to be used again by said character once they hit 60 again.

Such a service could even be a 150-200 token item sold on the Shop, so that it could serve as an added revenue source for the server for players who like to attempt the challenges, want to experience the new content at-level, or who just love leveling over everything else.
