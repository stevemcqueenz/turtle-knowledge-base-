The Scarlet Crusade was joinable on this server at one point.  As I remember, if your character was human, you could do a quest and get a ring that would make you friendly to Scarlet Crusade NPCs when worn.  There was also a Scarlet Crusade guild, and I think they still have a guild house near the Scarlet Monastery.  That's why mobs like the quartermaster exist.

These features were removed because I believe they were being exploited by other players who weren't interested in roleplaying as a Scarlet Crusader.  I don't think there were too many Scarlet Crusade roleplayers around at the time either to justify it being left in the game.

It would be interesting to reintroduce it in some fashion, but considering the server has plans for a Scarlet Crusade raid in the future, I don't think it would happen.
