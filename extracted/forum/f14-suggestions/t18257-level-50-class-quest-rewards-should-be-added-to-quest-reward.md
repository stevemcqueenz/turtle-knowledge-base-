# Level 50 Class Quest Rewards Should Be Added to Quest Reward Swapper

## Post 124503 by Gunni — 2025-03-30T22:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124503#p124503 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After making a couple tickets asking to get a quest reward from the Level 50 Hunter quest "[The Green Drake](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=8232/the-green-drake)" swapped for another, I was first directed to Ratchet, where the NPC Feid Rota is supposed to swap your quest rewards.

Unfortunately, Feid Rota does NOT offer services to exchange ANY of the rewards from the quest "The Green Drake" for another. As a result, I am unable to exchange my [Hunting Spear](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=20083/hunting-spear) for the [Devilsaur Tooth](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=19992/devilsaur-tooth).

I feel that this should not be the case, and that the Level 50 Class Quest items SHOULD be allowed to be swapped. It'd help other classes, as well as folks like me who had no idea the Devilsaur Tooth was a BiS item for Beast Master Hunters, able to correct mistakes like I made.

When I first started playing on this server on my shaman, I was planning on doing Enh and/or Resto having read the preview of changes the server had made for the class, so when I had to choose between Blackhand's Breadth and Eye of the Beast, I chose Blackhand's as it was going to be the most useful. But then got to AQ and saw how they changed itemization so enh was more involved in spell casting, and also made ele more viable. I put in a ticket for a swap and they just let me have both no questions asked.

The reason I lay this out is because I think having a swapper is necessary on the server by the nature of the changes it provides from vanilla and even from itself as time passes. If not a swapper, then allow people to redo endgame quests for the alternative rewards. The customer support seems to imply that they have no issue with remedying misinformed choices, so I think the obvious way to alleviate the necessity for hands-on support is to add it as a function in the game.
