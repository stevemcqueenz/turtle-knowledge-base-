# The Problems with Adding new Races.

## Post 42661 by Valadan — 2023-04-23T02:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42661#p42661 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I've been on a Brainstorming Binge these last few days thinking of what Races could be added for each faction. More than a real effort or suggestion, it is just an analysis. The most feasible Races I came up for each faction all have problems, mainly around the Shaman/Paladin faction exclusivity issue.

 **Furbolg for the Alliance:**This is the most natural fit for the Alliance, the Lore can support this, the Blackmaw tribe could join the Alliance, a tribe of Ashenvale could join, etc. The problem with Furbolgs, is that undoubtedly you'd have to add Shaman class to an Alliance race, and depending on your stance, that is a divisive issue.

 **Ogres for the Horde:** This just makes sense, no problems here, only problem is that if they're adding Furbolg/Shaman to alliance, ogres definitely wouldn't be Paladins.

 **Broken for the Alliance:** This could make sense if Ogres are joining the Horde, since a Hatred for the Horde could drive them to ally with the Alliance, yet we have the same problem as Furbolgs. You'd have to add Shaman Class, so you'd have to allow Paladins on Horde, again a divisive issue. Unless you wanted to take this line of lore into play *****"For some draenei, the shamanistic teachings of the Broken were even considered as heretical."*****

 **Centaur and Dryad:** These would use similar technology/rigs, would be difficult to implement though. Dryad for Alliance and Centaur for Horde.

What do you guys think?
