# My Turtle WoW Feature Wishlist

## Post 135451 by Papachinoforum — 2025-06-12T10:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135451#p135451 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Zones with re-used music. Any zone that has Elwynn Forest, Westall, or another re-used music should be given newly composed soundtracks. Zones like Hillsbrad Foothills, Ashenvale, Durotar, all of which use Elwynn, Teldrassil, and Westfall music respectively

 - A FF14 golden saucer inspired zone or cross faction mini-city/zone. Apart from the goblin gamblers you can find in goblin inns and taverns, I think it would be pretty cool to have a goblin-run casino with slot machines or npc controlled mount race betting. You could earn tickets that can only be used at the casino to purchase toys, mounts, and unique transmogs for example

 - If it hasn't already been discussed, Kul Tiras I believe would be an awesome continent/landmass to have players visit. It would be a level 30-40 zone. Maybe not to the scale it was in Battle for Azeroth, but perhaps the combined sizes of Lapidis, Gillijim's, and Tel'Abim

 - If not already being worked on, hovering over certain zones on the world map, their lit up version is cut off. To my knowledge this is only noticeable when hovering over Silithus on the Kalimdor world map. Scarlet enclave should also be properly discoverable

 - Improved existing and new fishing content. I don't know what that would be but maybe fishing improvements and additions from future patches

 - Straight shot flight paths. It doesn't make a lick of sense to go from Orgrimmar to Ratchet, going to Crossroads first and then Ratchet. This may just be a hardcoded thing that can't be fixed in the current client but I hope it can be fixed/added in the upcoming UE5 client

 - More plant placement in bodies of water. It feels really weird seeing nothing but sand when swimming off the coast of Ratchet. In a body of water like that I expect to see forests of kelp, rocks, and other decorative props. Make oceans, lakes, and ponds feel like actual oceans, lakes, and ponds

 - Settlement sieges? Say I'm in a town like Lakeshire, and all of a sudden it's invaded by a group of Gnolls. I think it would be a fun, optional activity for players to participate in defending against local enemies attacking these questing hubs. I don't they should be able to go past where you actually enter these areas as to prevent people who are afk'd from getting killed. Make this world feel alive

 - WoW Classic features. This includes foliage rendering and QoL changes

 - Addons such as BetterCharacterStats,  ModernMapMarkers, and ShaguTweaks should be vanilla features
