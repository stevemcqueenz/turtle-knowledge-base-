# Paladin Repentance does pitiful damage.

## Post 113718 by Ellifta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-21T15:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113718#p113718 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After taking almost 2 months to fix the talent, now according to many logs of paladins it does... 2.5% of their overall damage in a raid. Being a capstone talent at the end of retribution tree, I think it's incredibly lackluster compared to any other capstone talent for other classes.

Bloodthirst, Mortal Strike, Leader of the Pack, Adrenaline Rush or Trueshot Aura are all significantly better options that either provide a very useful damage dealing tool, or good group buffs. To give you an idea how weak repentance is, it is weaker than Rend on an equally geared warrior that spec'd into improved rend and keeps it up.

It should be completely reworked to a very strong utility buff like druid or hunter buff, or something that noticeably increases paladin DPS.
