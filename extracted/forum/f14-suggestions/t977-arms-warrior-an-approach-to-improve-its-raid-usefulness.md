# Arms warrior: an approach to improve its raid usefulness

## Post 5539 by Connelly — 2020-03-27T00:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5539#p5539 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Oh boy, a “I like this spec, let us buff it plz” thread. I know, I know. And yes, I know, other specs like moonkin or arcane mage could use buffs a lot more. But Arms and 2Hander Fury warrior are what I understand best, so that’s what I'm sticking to. Please, bear with me.

Some months ago I had an ingame conversation with Qixel about how paladins were getting Holy Strike, suggestions for new items made in the forum for future custom content, and how some lacking specs could be brought up to the level of others. This was time ago before I went on a break from playing WoW, but recently I got in another similar, although simpler, conversation that brought the online quest & item creator to my attention, and made me start thinking on how the Arms warrior spec could be given a facelift to address its own problems, with the aim of making it more desirable to have in raid without making it overpowered.

I might tinker with the online creator later following these ideas to see what could be actually possible, when/if I have the time, but I thought best to lay it all here in case someone might have anything to add. And I know, I know, most of all this might not be practical enough to be implemented, it's just some preliminary theorycrafting.

Usual issues:

- The Mortal Strike debuff is perceived by many as a waste against PVE encounters in relation to the debuff limit. This is the main obstacle the spec faces to be brought to a raid. Changing it to a more beneficial or utility-geared debuff/buff/aura, removing it, and/or increasing the skill damage potential to compensate are possible considerations.
- The main strength of Fury (both dw and 2h) over Arms is attack speed in single target. The Flurry talent is just that strong, and at some point a Fury player with enough critical strike chance will activate it so often that the end result may just be another baseline aspect of the spec. A similar increase in speed might be provided to Arms, in a different enough way that it doesn't steal Fury's shtick.
- On a lesser degree, Arms PVE only has damage, and emergency tanking capacity (I once tanked Sapphiron as Arms when our tank died, so yes, that’s totally a thing we can do in a pinch. Justdon’tmakemedoitagainpleaseohmypoorheart). While more party/raid utility could be welcomed and even interesting, like the Trauma talent introduced in TBC that made 1 Arms player desirable in every raid composition, it might be overstepping some bounds, so such a thing has to be properly weighed to avoid problems.

Objectives:

1. Improve Arms PVE viability.
2. Arms PVP capacity should not be disrupted.
3. Arms PVE should not be made so strong that it leaves Fury ashamed from inadequacy.
4. Dual wield Fury should not be given more power than it already has.
5. Any changes incidentally beneficial to 2H Fury can be allowed, but should be kept minimal.
6. The changes must be provided by a piece of gear to facilitate ease of use, and discourage its use in pvp content.*
 *(Creating tier sets for dps warrior dps could also help, but they should be done with both Arms and Fury in mind, and if there are to be one set for raid like the rest of classes and specs, it would make for an impractical solution.)

While providing Arms with new effects can be interesting, it runs the risk of not addressing the core issues and providing potential and undesired exploits to the Fury and Protection specs. Working around the talents might be a more organic and elegant way to address them. Dual wield Fury only has enough talent points to reach to the 4th tier of Arms talents, and only for Impale. Hence I propose working with the following talents, and nothing else:

 *Two-Handed Weapon Specialization
 *Sweeping Strikes
 *Pole/Axe Specialization
 *Mace Specialization
 *Sword Specialization
 *Improved Hamstring
 *Mortal Strike

While improving Improved Rend and Improved Overpower in some ways could also be ways to reach our objective in simpler ways, they can be grabbed by a Fury player, which runs counter to our intention of making Arms more desirable without providing more power to Fury.

There are several possible effects I’ve figured out that could be helpful: one to Mortal Strike, required for the entire project, and three additional but exclusive changes to other talents to support the Mortal Strike change.

- *Change the Mortal Strike debuff to reduce its negative impact in pve and undesirability by raid organizers, and/or improve the damage provided by the skill.*
The MS debuff has always been a point of contention in the matter of bringing Arms warriors to a raid, as it isn't useful in most encounters. Turning it into a a) buff/debuff helpful to the raid, b) buff helpful to the player, or c) taking it away altogether, any of these options as needed, would be a first step in this task.

Depending on the change, the damage of the skill could use an improvement in exchange for any "nerfs".

An appropriate way to make this change should be through an early gear piece. A class specific neck, ring, or cloak would be most appropriate, as Arms can afford not getting hit chance from those pieces. The stats in the proposed gear piece (or the beneficial effect applied to Mortal Strike, if that's the case) would need to be reasonably strong to justify using this gear piece in all levels of pve content, and not too weak to discourage the player from substituting it with another piece with stronger stats. And to facilitate its early use, it would be appropriate to be a reward from a custom class quest that can be finished without stepping in a raid; making it a drop from a 5 man content is another option, thought less interesting content wise.

It should be important to try to design the benefit so that it isn't so strong that a player would want to use it it in pvp, as the Mortal Strike healing debuff is the main point of being Arms in that situation, both tactically and strategically, and the change might potentially break pvp balance in an undesired way.

- *Modify the weapon type specialization talents.*
These talents aren't *bad*, but have a few failings in comparison to each other. The 5% critical strike chance from pole/axe is stupidly strong, and a constant benefit. The 5% change of extra hit from sword is nice and can have great moments in conjunction to other things like Hand of Justice and a shaman's Windfury totem in party, but unreliable most of the time. While there are some strong maces, the 6% chance of a stun from the spec is useless in bosses and unreliable in trash.

There can be two approaches to this. Either increase the % for sword, change the mace effect, and leave axe alone, or add secondary effects to each one, that complement the main effect. This would be my preferred option of the tree to complement the MS change, as it is potentially the more interesting, though also the most complex.

As the first option is just as simple as changing a number for pole/axe and sword, or an effect for mace, I dedicated my time to think of a few ideas for the second option:

 *Mace spec*: add a strong constant effect or proc, and/or change the stun for a different main effect.
Example: Each stun, be it successful or not, provides the player with the same proc effect as the one from Bonereaver Edge, which reduces the armor of the target. This helps to make those mace weapons (at least those not called Sulfuras or Might of Menethil) more desirable to an Arms warrior as more than just as roleplaying items or transition weapons. Though it would require a buff to be more reliable (make it's duration long enough that it won't drop before the next stun), this is a *very* strong proc and it fully justifies the fame of the BRE as an endgame weapon on par with the Naxxramas final weapons. It might be necessary to tune down the final increase to a more modest number for the long run, without lowering the initial increase too much or even increasing it so that an incomplete stack is not too weak at the start.

I don't consider the side effect making Sulfuras and MoM even stronger a problem, as both should be incredibly rare and strong weapons to begin with, while other more common maces tend to be either geared for pallys and shamans, or average to moderately-strong.

 *Sword spec*: add a moderate to strong constant effect or proc.
Example: A Flurry-lite proc to each Sword spec hit. Each spec hit gives an increase in the Flurry stack, up to x instances and y total speed attack. Correctly tuned, it keeps the random and speedy nature of the talent while making it more reliable and strong.

 *Pole/Axe spec*: add a weak proc along the 5% crit chance.
Example: increase the 2h damage for each critical hit by 1%, up to 5%, for 5 seconds (not renewable after the 5th application). Basically a second instance of 2H Weapon Specialization, with lower uptime that can be lost in certain circumstances. Technically axe shouldn't need anything, but something like this should do to keep it on par with the others, and the 5% isn't that much (though it can be tweaked somehow).

The polearm particular talent could have a different effect to the axe one, to account for the different weapon, while retaining the 5% crit chance. That way it provides those strong polearms like Barb of the Sand Reaver and Eye of Nerub, if no hunter in raid want them, with an special use, that more stronger axes and sword won’t serve. A survival effect could be an idea, but that would be too much of a hassle to make use of it.

An appropriate way to apply these effects should be through a trinket, like the BWL class trinkets that provide only effects, to force a choice in pairing pairing it with a stat trinket (ie Diamond Flask, Mark of the Champion) or an effect trinket (ie Hand of Justice, Kiss of the Spider), and avoid the player getting the best of all worlds. This also helps to discourage the player from using it in pvp combat, as the badge is almost always necessary, and both survival and engineering trinkets tend to be more useful as a second trinket.

Making this trinket an early dungeon or raid reward, either by boss drop or a custom class specific reward, would be fine enough, I think. My first thoughts would be to b reward it either in Stratholme, early Molten Core, or some quest chain involving cities and battlefields in the world that’s either long and soloable with decent dungeon gear, or require a party of 5 players, tops. Though if it ends being too strong, it could be moved to a higher level content

- Increase the damage provided by 2H Weapon Specialization beyond 5%.
An increase in damage across the board is straightforward enough, relying only in fine tuning the correct % number. It can also allow the improvement of the 2H Fury spec if desired, in a very minimal way without touching the Fury talents. Like with the first option, it would be provided by a trinket, or maybe some other piece of gear.

The main advantage of this change is that it capitalizes on the Arms spec role of bringing superior pain each hit, instead of overwhelming attack speed, and it relies on simply changing one number. But it would be easy to overshoot the mark, and create problems with the rage generation and and make Arms overpowered compared to Fury, and make it too weak for the change to matter.

- *Make Sweeping Strikes increase attack speed while active (in addition to the swipe attack).*
Another straightforward change. As long as it’s active, it provides a similar effect to Slice and Dice. SS has a cooldown of 30 secs, a cost of 30 rage, and a duration of 10 secs, so it could provide greater speed than Flurry while having a lower uptime. Like with the first option, it would be provided by a trinket, or maybe some other piece of gear.

Pros: a) it demands active effort from the player, instead of being the passive constant advantage that Flurry is, b) that it applies to all weapon specs equally with a single source, and c) it provides Sweeping Strikes with a use in single target that while requiring tactical consideration in encounters with adds.

Cons: a) the attack speed should be fine tuned to match Flurry at all levels, which is one headache, b) requires accounting for all weapon specs, which would be a second headache (and may not solve the weakness of mace spec), and c) it could be (even more) overpowered in multi target fights; not sure how would it affect pvp battles.

I consider this a potentially interesting change, but too hard and problematic to implement to be practical.

So, that’s all I have for now. Any thoughts?
