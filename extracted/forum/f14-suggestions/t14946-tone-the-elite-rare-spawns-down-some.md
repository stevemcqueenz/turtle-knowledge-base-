**Standard rare:** Silver portrait, no dragon wing. Generally not intended to be that much tougher from standard mobs around its level, thus ought to be soloable.
- Examples: Watch Commander Zalaphil (Durotar), Timber (Dun Morogh), Fedfennel (Elwynn Forest), Dalaran Spellscribe (Silverpine Forest), Garneg Charskull (Wetlands), Boss Galgosh (Loch Modan)
![Image](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/archive/5/51/20070729212630%21Rare.jpg)

 **Rare elite:** Silver portrait, but has the elite mob dragon wing. Very much intended to be significantly tougher than other mobs around it, especially if located in a subzone filled with standard mobs.
- Examples: Captain Flat Tusk (Durotar), Anathemus (Badlands), Scarlet High Clerist* (Western Plaguelands), Narillasanz (Alterac Mountains), Vile Sting (Thousand Needles), Captain Gerogg Hammertoe** (Barrens)
![Image](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/archive/a/ac/20070729212812%21Rare_elite.jpg)

The standard rare mobs (silver portrait only, no wing) in Turtle WoW are indeed currently slightly overtuned, very specifically in terms of damage output. Ideally, they they should be either kept at their current power level and classified as rare elites, potentially with a health increase from their current "standard mob"-level health to match, or their damage should be nerfed slightly to be more in tune with standard mobs, potentially to being a touch higher than standard mobs to make them tougher without overlapping with the distinction of a rare elite. The rare tag is currently a somewhat misleading game mechanic, as there already is an intended visual distinction for rare mobs that are meant to be group content and thus significantly tougher and harder to kill than their surroundings.

 *The Scarlet High Clerist is located close to Hearthglen, an elite area, but his actual spawn location is in the tower on the road leading up to Hearthglen, which is still entirely non-elite up to that point outside of an instance during the "In Dreams" quest when Grand Inquisitor Isillien and his elite guards spawn. His rare elite tag is the visual cue that he isn't an average mob meant to be taken on by a solo player.

 **Captain Hammertoe is located in Bael'dun Keep, a non-elite area. He's still tagged as a rare elite, which notably is in contrast with his other colleagues from the Bael'dun: Malgin Barleybrew, Heggin Stonewhisker and Digger Flameforge, all of whom are in the same area but are tagged as standard rares. As with the Scarlet High Clerist above, this is meant to distinguish his difficulty scale in regards to both the other rare mobs in the area as well as the standard non-elite spawns.
