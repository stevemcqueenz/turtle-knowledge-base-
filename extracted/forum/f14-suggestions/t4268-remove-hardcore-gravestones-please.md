# Remove Hardcore Gravestones Please

## Post 25440 by Nanibold — 2022-09-18T20:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25440#p25440 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The gravestones that spawn to mark the death of a hardcore character are a cool idea, however, now that the server has such a huge population, these gravestones are littering the game world to an excessive degree.
To make matters worse some of them even have hit-boxes, which becomes a problem when they're just all over the place.

Please remove them or at least give non-hardcore characters the option to opt-out of seeing them, along with the spammy system messages that inform us whenever a hardcore character levels up or dies.

It's becoming to be a little bit too much now that the server regularly has 2500+ players.
