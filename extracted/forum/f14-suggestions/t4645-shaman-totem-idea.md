# Shaman totem idea

## Post 27763 by F41lz0rs (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-10-23T13:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27763#p27763 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Instead of totems having a duration, have them reduce mana pool while they are active.

grace of air totem rank 2 costs 250 mana with a duration.

the idea is to remove the duration or make it VERY long,  remove 250 mana from the shamans mana pool via a Debuff while the totem is down.  usign the totomic recall you regain all the lost mana instantly.

this will greatly increase the totems use as they cost so much they are neglected on most fights to reduce downtime.
the reduced mana debuff still allows the retention of spell crit from int while balancing via reduced mana pool.
