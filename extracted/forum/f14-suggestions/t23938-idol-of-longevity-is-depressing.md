# Idol of longevity is depressing

## Post 168027 by Tbcb — 2026-02-12T09:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168027#p168027 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello Team,

first of all - twow is awesome. It is the best classic pve experience that you can get and imo the way classic plus should be.

I just have a little thing: Druid Resto Idols for HC because there is no idol progression.

Here my perspective:
Dungeons: You get a Reju-idol
t1: You get a regrowth idol
t2,5: You get a HT-Idol
T3: You get Idol of longevity and depression

Here is the problem: While the plantlings from t1 are a cool thing and i like the idol, we banned it in our HC guild because the summons have a wierd pull range and drop through the floor. It is just a big no-no on hc. Since our class-identity is hots and you can add something to the raidroaster, going HT feels wierd for me. So you are stuck with the Reju-Idol from lbrs. It is just sad gearing experience to know there is no progression. And there is a bigger reason for that.

I totally get that you can get BIS in MC or maybe even single pieces in crafts or 5mans, but there are idols in the game that feel like mockery: Idol of longevity. You make it to naxx, you kill a hard boss for HC and you get a piece of crap. Removing the item from the game would improve the experience atm.

Can you please rework idol of longevity to give resto druids a gear progression in idols? It is one idol that needs change to improve the endgamefeeling.

Here some ideas:

 - While affected by Reju/Regrowth, damage taken is reduced by n%
 - Reduces the CD of tranquility by X
 - Reju/Regrowth has a chance to restore x Rage, y Energy, z mana (basically 2/8 t3 bonus)
 - Reduces the global cd of reju by 0,Xs.
 - Reju stacks 2 times on one target.
 - Barskin applies 50% innvervate on you.
 - barkskins increases your healing power by X% of your spirit (?)
 - Reduces the CD of natures swiftness by x.
 - Ending regrowth heals the target for y.
 - Innervate heals the target for the same mounts as mana gained.
 - You can cast HT while in treeform.

I would really prefere an idol, that does not go a specific reju/regrowth/HT build, because it would be an idol useable no matter what specc you use. So relevant skills could be: Barskin, Tranq, Mark of the wild, Emerald blessing, innvervate.

Community, do your thing. I only play hc. What idol do you want to see to give resto druids gearing progression?

Thank you for listening to my TED Talk
