# Changes to corrupted zones

## Post 24915 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-09-12T08:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24915#p24915 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Probably the only thing I liked about Cataclysm was *SOME* of the changes to corrupted zones in the game, such as:

 - Duskwood
 - Felwood
 - Eastern Plaguelands
 - Western Plaguelands
 - Blasted Lands
 - Desolace

and some non-corrupted ones, like

 - Dustwallow Marsh
 - Stonetalon Mountains

What is most notable for the corrupted zones is that there were attempts to purify them and it's evident in the change of the terrain to how it used to be. I think it might be a good idea to have a small part of those zones have something like this and have quests where you help the NPCs looking to purify those zones, could be the Argent Dawn working with the Cenarion Circle or something, you do a whole bunch of quests, and in future patches you see a bit more of the zones purified. And some additional quests added as a result.

It would be most interesting for places like Stratholme City or Kharazan and maybe having small portions of them be like neutral hubs with quests, vendors, other services, etc.

Tirsfal Glades can also have something like this in places where the Scarlet Crusade are.
Searing Gorge and Burning Steppes can also have something like that too.
Silithus can also get some love.

For me it has always been bumming me out that there were some corrupted zones and there is no chance to see how they used to be.

As for the non-corrupted zones, it was nice when they got more opened up and space on the map that was never used was finally used. Blasted Lands getting a shoreline, Stonetalon Mountains too.

I don't really like the idea. Whole point of being 'uncorrupted' stems from them being corrupted now - in the vanilla timeline. For people like some us, playing the game for 15+ years, it indeed can be a move forward - we know how these places were/are like, and can extrapolate from that into a cleansed or in-cleansing state. But we are not there in the story, we are still in vanilla times. I felt a loss when I saw the changes in Cataclysm, but I might be alone with it. Yet, for people just learning this world, they would not 'know' the story of these places. And there are already a lot of places that were 'healed' after a corruption or demolished state. The Stormwind you see now was rebuilt after the first war. Ashenwale is nice and green again after the Legion destoyed most of it so long ago. Azshara is teeming with life after the cataclysm so long ago that destroyed everything within the zone. In Winterspring the snow covered most of the tracks of the demonic incursion. The oases in the barrens show the change the druids started. etc etc But these are places where we only see the aftermath of the return of life and civilisation, in the areas you mention this is still in the future. I would keep it that way.
