# Sun's Embrace

## Post 25638 by Apimius (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-09-20T22:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25638#p25638 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

please lower the cooldown from the Sun's Embrace spell: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=46046>.

Smite priests have 1 problem: mana sustainability - they go oom very quickly.

The high elf racial Sun's Embrace is a great way of reducing the mana cost of your offensive holy spells, but a 5 minute cooldown is too long to have a big enough mana improvement in endgame. I would love to see sun's embrace at a 2-3 min cd. This would allow the usage of power infusion and most spell power trinkets combined with sun's embrace on a consistent basis.

Thanks for reading and considering!
