## Post 172146 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-03-24T13:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172146#p172146 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I feel like part of vanilla WoW's magic stems from all achievements being player made and organic and sought after not because the game told you but because the design led you there in a way that's natural and intended. Stuff like completing your first raid, getting attuned for onyxia, acquiring full pre-bis for raids, getting thunderfury, your epic mount/riding, or, hell, even just reaching max level for some people. There's a reason people tend to go "grats!" or "gz!" when you ding in a dungeon, because that's just not always a thing that happens in a run and you really feel like you've earned the level, as opposed to how retail works where you probably get a level once per dungeon and then get an extra one at the end of the dungeon because you get a ton just for completing it.

Anyways, the point is, this game is filled with tedium and natural things to strive for. When you throw achievements as a system into the mix, it kinda feels hamfisted and as if you're being -told- "hey this thing is impressive, rewarding and cool!" as opposed to you going out of your way to find a thing you think is impressive or cool or rewarding.
