Been kicking around this idea in my head for a few days.

 - Cannot receive or complete quests.  Most NPCs won't even talk to you.
 - Cannot gain any reputation.  Nobody likes you.
 - Vendors will begrudgingly give you 1 copper for every item.
 - Vendors will only let you buy white items, and charge you 10x as much for them.
 - When you use your Hearthstone, it just says "You have no home."
 - Your class trainers will charge extra for teaching someone like you.
 - Profession trainers tell you to figure it out yourself. (MAYBE they could charge extra like class trainers)
 - Flight masters tell you you can walk.
 - You can only group, trade, and exchange mail with other outcasts.
