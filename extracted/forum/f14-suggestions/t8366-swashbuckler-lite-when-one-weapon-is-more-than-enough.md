# Swashbuckler lite - when one weapon is more than enough

## Post 53900 by Nixodrius — 2023-07-18T08:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53900#p53900 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings and salutations  smiling_turtle  ,

Its been almost a year since I've joined this community - and I am really glad that I did - I was able to witness so many changes in a such short period of time. I really like the fact that Lore/RP aspect is intrinsically connected to the gameplay, and If I may, I would like to give my input on how in my opinion it could broaden the possibilities of broadening further.

I was always a huge fan of one weapon-fighting, without the shield - it just looks great, feels awesome, and gives that pirate or "melee and spell style" vibe (Just look at the papa Thrall below - Doomhammer in one hand and nothing in the other)

![Image](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_folder_media/7BTU614O0LXL1420680808364.jpg)

Creating mechanics for this type of gameplay is however completely out of the question as it would need a lot of the resources in order to develop it from the scratch. However what if we could use already existing mechanics to bring something really similar into the game - actually we already have in some degree. Enter the non spell-related off-hand frills.

What I am suggesting is adding more off-hands with physical stats, in similar fashion to [Almanac of Savagery](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60431)

They do not to be a very competitive, as I understand the dangers of adding such items (especially in hands of druids and non-melee hunters) - however they should at least make players using them in combat not like they are punishing themselves too much with that free off-hand slot (surprise, surprise - RP and looks are also kind of important in combat - if they weren't we would be throwing calculators at ourselves to see which has a larger number).

And also... they should not have model at all for maximum swashbuckler feel   satisfied_turtle

Thank you for reading through all of this, this is just small suggestion from my side that would make my gameplay more immersive, however i can see why it may be way too much work.

Hope everyone have a wonderful day!

Nixodrius
