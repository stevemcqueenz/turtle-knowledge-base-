Mrrrglglgl! Listen! LISTEN!
We all know it. Murlocs best race. YESSS. And now… it 2025. Time to make murlocs… PLAYABLE!

I hear you already: “But how, little fishy? What faction? What if I no want to play Murloc?”
To you I say: MRRRGLGLGLGLGLGLGLGL!! (evil laugh)

Simple answer. Make… it… a CHALLENGE.
The greatest, slimiest, floppiest RP challenge ever!

Horde or Alliance? NEITHER! Everyone hate you! (Okay okay… maybe neutral rep with Booty Bay. But only if shiny coin.)

Gear? Give fishy sword. Maybe shield. No need fancy pants—murlocs don’t wear pants!

Quests? Hah! Only a few—like spooky ghost in Westfall. Mostly you GRIND. Many mobs. Many bubbles.

Lore? You want lore? Pfft. It’s a murloc!
…Fine, fine. You are humble Elwynn murloc… glubbing around… then BOOM! crazy tower wizard or shiny relic zap you. Now you have… brain wrinkle. Go adventure! Go flop to level 60!

At level 60, maybe magic trinket lets you wear real gear… but still show off glorious scaly body. And of course—must be HARDCORE. What is challenge if not risk of permadeath glub-glub?
