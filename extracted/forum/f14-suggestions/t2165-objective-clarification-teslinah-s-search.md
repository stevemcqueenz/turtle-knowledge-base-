# Objective Clarification: Teslinah's Search

## Post 13248 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-10-25T17:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13248#p13248 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In the quest "Teslinah's Search I", I believe that the "Mage District Tavern" and "Mage District Tavern Basement" objectives should be clarified further, as unlike the other districts of Stormwind there are two taverns in the Mage District ("The Blue Recluse" and "The Slaughtered Lamb"; Old Town has the "The Pig and Whistle", and the Trade District has "The Gilded Rose"). The tavern that qualifies for the objective is "The Slaughtered Lamb", but due to "The Blue Recluse" tavern being situated immediately outside of the Wizard's Sanctum and the objective syntax referring to "tavern" in the singular, implying the existence of only the one tavern in the district, players could be tricked into investigating it instead of the Lamb. Warlock players and experienced players would know of "The Slaughtered Lamb", but non-warlock, new, or inexperienced players could be left wandering "The Blue Recluse" aimlessly, wondering why the quest objective is not working when they are clearly in the Mage District Tavern.

To make matters more complicated, this quest chain has very finicky triggering to complete objectives and I've ran through the trigger area without it working a few times, so someone who does not know to go to "The Slaughtered Lamb" could end up thinking that they just haven't walked in the correct area in "The Blue Recluse" and could wander around the tavern for a long time before thinking of going to "The Slaughtered Lamb".

If the vagueness is intentional (as Teslinah, being a high elf refugee child, may not know of the specific names of some areas of Stormwind), perhaps add hints that there is another tavern in the Mage Quarter, possibly a trigger upon entering "The Blue Recluse" from one of the NPCs inside.

A similar issue occurs in "Teslinah's Search V", as the objective tells you to search the Cathedral of Light's basement and catacombs. The only part that will trigger the quest's objectives is the basement and catacomb on the side that contains the Scarlet Emissary, but there is a separate basement and catacomb on the opposite side of the Cathedral as well. It is slightly offset by the fact that you can clearly see both basement entrances, but due to the finicky triggering you could be running around the wrong side of the Cathedral for a while.
