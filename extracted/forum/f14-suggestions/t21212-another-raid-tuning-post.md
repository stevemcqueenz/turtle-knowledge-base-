# Another Raid Tuning Post

## Post 147278 by Manijin — 2025-08-20T14:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147278#p147278 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm sure these have been done to death by now, but as someone who is somewhat new to the WoW endgame experience, I'm at a crossroads.

I had run a few MCs before, and about half of BWL progression, and had some enjoyable experiences. Sometimes it was rough, but we always felt like victory was just around the corner, and getting bosses on farm was pretty cool.

I ran MC for the first time post patch, post "fix", last night, and I have to say that the issues with it are not exaggerated. We had a full 20 with 5 healers, "overgeared" tanks, and we were still struggling like mad. Even when we learned the mechanics on some bosses, particularly the newer bosses, damage and health were so high it didn't feel like a victory, it felt like a sigh of relief that the thing was finally OVER. We didn't even progress all the way through the raid, as 2+ hours in we had only beaten 4 bosses.

I can't imagine what it would be like with people trying to run it in fresh gear, which is what the dungeon was originally designed for.

But that doesn't even get to the crux of my issue. See, I'm not geared enough for something like AQ40 yet, and with the changes to BWL I'm not even sure I'm geared enough for that. As it stands, I'll have to either throw myself at MC, which just doesn't feel fun at the moment, or stop playing. I don't want to do that, though! I really love TWoW, and the new class changes, especially for shaman, breathed new life into the game for me! But if this iteration of MC is the only thing going forward, then there just isn't anything left for me to play.

However, let me not just complain. This is a forum for suggestions after all. My thoughts? Just reduce those damage and health numbers. I know you wanted to make the fights more difficult, but especially for entry-level raiding, the goal shouldn't be "numbers high=hard". The boss MECHANICS are what make the fight interesting! Incindus is a VERY cool fight, and the lava golem twins are awesome too! They just take SO long, and the damage numbers are SO high, especially across the raid, that you can find yourselves wiping even when you know the mechanics, and that just feels bad. You were right to look at tuning down numbers. I suggest more. Heck, do 50% less health across the board, and I bet it'll still be fun. (Okay, 33% reduction is probably more feasible, but I'd rather overdo it and then do small adjustments). For damage, 25%, particularly on raidwide boss mechanics, though I'd want to see more. This is supposed to be the first BIG raids for people, and rather than feeling challenging, they feel PUNISHING, but in all the worst feeling ways. Thanks.

Except the thing is they don't allow world buffs in raids anymore, so while yes, classes overall are generally more powerful than their unbuffed vanilla counterparts (though I would argue that the overall dps ceiling isn't raised that much, more that there's a wider variety of classes available to meet that ceiling), overall, a world buffed vanilla raid group is still going to outmatch a turtle wow raid group, so this argument doesn't really fly.

That said, I remember classic wow raiding pretty well, and the turtle wow iterations prior to this were definitely undertuned in may ways. I think the best bet would actually be to get some people into classic era, hop into the raids, and check the numerical values for things like mob HP, damage, armor, resistances, and put some work into actually reflecting original blizz-like values, I know private servers were notoriously wrong about a lot of those things, and people playing classic wow had to spend a bit of time adjusting to the higher values than they were used to. I think after that's done, THEN have a look at adjusting numbers and mechanics.

For reference, BWL prior to the update felt wayy undertuned, but now I genuinely got a sense of nostalgia from running it with my raid group this week, as it actually felt much more like raiding BWL in vanilla.

MC on the other hand... way too difficult.
