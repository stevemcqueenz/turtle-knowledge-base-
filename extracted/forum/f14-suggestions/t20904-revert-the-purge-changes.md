I don't PvP so I'm not going to talk about purge from that perspective. What I'd like people to consider instead is the following scenario:
 ------
Imagine you're a new player to the server that's just rolled a shaman. You're leveling up and things are going well. You unlock a new skill called purge at the trainer, and read the description. Removes a magical effect? That sounds useful. You've seen those before: quillboars with thorns and harpies and centaur with lightning shields. These mobs hurt to attack, and you'll now have a way to make fighting them easier. Nice. You buy the skill and place it on your bar.

A short while later you pick up a quest to kill some murlocs. And hey, you recognize that magical effect that some of them have! That's lightning shield, just like yours. Except now you have purge, sweet. You can get rid of it so it doesn't hurt you while you're grinding them for their fins or whatever. You run up to the first murloc, cast purge, and the murloc *instantaneously* recasts lightning shield the millisecond your purge lands (as almost all mobs do when you purge them on this server).

What just happened?

That was weird. You're pretty sure the spell went off: it made a sound and there was a little effect. But the murloc's still hurting you when you attack it. It still has the little lightning ball. Maybe the spell was resisted or something? You try to cast it again and whoops, it's on an 8-second cooldown. By the time purge is available again, the murloc's probably almost dead. So you keep trying to use this new spell to do the thing the tooltip says it's for, not realizing (at first) that by casting purge on the mobs with purgeable buffs you are making them *harder to kill* because you're spending extra mana and a GCD on each one for absolutely zero benefit.

...what conclusion do you think the new player in this scenario will draw about the skill, the class, and the server?
 ------
It just kinda seems like a bad new player experience, right?
Maybe changing how mobs cast self-buffs should be included in this patch alongside the purge change.
