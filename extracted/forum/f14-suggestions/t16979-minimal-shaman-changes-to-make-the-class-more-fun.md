# Minimal shaman changes to make the class more fun

## Post 115885 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-08T14:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115885#p115885 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

 **What I want:**
Call out problems/annoyances while playing different shaman builds and offer minimalistic solutions for them.

 **What I don't want:**
No intention to push the builds too much. My goal is a more fun gameplay, not a selfish power fantasy.

To understand how to make shamans more fun, we gotta understand what the different builds want and what they love/hate when playing their respective builds.
Many times the solution is not about mindless buffing. We can in theory create S+ tier monstrosities and keep the baseline problems of the class intact.

 **Ele:**
 1. What most elemental players want is a unique utility only an ele can bring which would usually grant them a place in a caster group.
You can make eles 2x as strong as now, most eles would complain as long as they are in melee groups. Shamans suffer from having a mindlessly OP melee party aura at their disposal which alone determines their group composition and totem selection. I don't wanna touch WF here and now but I'd definitely touch FT totem. CC2 tried to find a place for it outside the old paladin groups and made it the little brother of WF. Gameplaywise it's boring. If you are in a melee group and your name is not enhancement, you just mindlessly put down those 2 all the time with a strength totem, resummoning them every time someone from the party is taking a step to any direction.
My proposal is to remove FTT and hide it to a late elemental talent (row 5+) and make it a spellpower totem.
If the ele's DPS output gets too big, we can always tailor things like electrify charges or spells' base damage.
But believe me: an elemental shaman having a unique utility is 10x happier than an elemental shaman from the same power level without one.

 2. Earth's grasp talent is the ele's totem talent. Earthbind is understandable, stoneclaw is useless there. Swap stoneclaw to tranquil air totem and give it some further effect, like additional threat reduction in case of crits or just a flat upgrade of threat reduction.
That's something elementals actually need and this, alongside FTT gives them a unique spot in the caster groups ->
You put ele to caster group -> Caster groups basically will never aggro + group has added sp. Everyone wins.

_______________

There are some other things but they are much less important. The above two would give ele reason to be a member of a caster group and they'd find their own uniqueness as a build.

Below ones are more optional:
Reverse the eye of the storm talent. Maybe reduce the pushback reduction it gives if needed, but let it activate after you deal a crit, not the other way around.

Make flame guidance séxy. Make it a cool talent to use if ele is not in a caster group and FTT just doesn't worth it. Or even better, remove it and introduce a talent which lowers the chance of your opponent to resist nature spells.

____________

 **Enh** is very tricky. Its main gameplay relies on 20% chance free insta-hits which are extremely difficult to balance, specially since WF weapon gives 2 of those.
My honest or in-depth solution would be reworking how WF is working, but I wouldn't consider that a small change so not here and now.
Enhancement has to be balanced around the huge variance it innately has.
Right now enh is in an interesting spot, performing well when farming content, relying on short burst windows, but faring much worse during progression.
I don't like the idea that a build works optimally for already cleared content but changing it would be a big change, again.
So I work around the high-variance + burst premises.
Enhs usually complain about two things nowadays: mana management and threat.
Some feel like always chasing the MT's TPS whether they want it or not.
Others complain that while enh rotation is SS LS ES SS with LS via lightning shield, this rotation kills their mana pool way too fast so they need to switch to water shield or go auto-attack.

To make enh happy with minimal changes, we need to accomplsh two things:
 * Allow them to do their rotation longer without being oom.
 * Help a bit with threat.

 1. Let calming wind include shocks. ES has a 1,5x threat modifier after CC2 and it is used regularly by enh DPS. Shocks being included in calming wind talent would mostly solve the threat problem.
 2. For mana problems, there's flurry. Let it be 5tp. Let it give 15% haste only, in contrast of the 30% of warriors. Yes, 15, not the current 20. But this in exchange of being a shaman version of flurry.
Let flurry give 15% haste for your next 3 swings after a melee crit and let those hits in flurry recharge n% of your mana. Not too much, don't make LSing with a water shield useless but make it good enough that enh rotation can be decently maintained while shaman tanks would have a new advantage of mana abundance (almost).

There is a lesser talked-about problem also. Shocks scale from AP, too, which is cool but since rockbiter gives you a bunch of AP, enh DPS is in a weird spot that they are cut away from the really big shocks since they are locked out of the big threat weapon imbue. Might reconsider the scaling, f.ex. done by agi. End numbers could be very similar, my point is not to cut off enh DPS from the big shocks.

_____

 **Tank shamans** are only shortly mentioned. This article is all about small changes. Not inventing a def CD, etc, only touching some talents here and there.
None of these are super-essential, anyway, just listing them as optional ones, just like in case of the ele tree.

Let call of flame talent give a price reduction for fire spells and totems, too. Magma and fire nova have a big price tag.
Make spirit armor a 3tp talent, allowing to get 45% armor bonus. Would mostly resolve squishiness problems.

_______________

 **In case of restoration**, the main problem is boredom.
Before CC2 a full T2,5 allowed you to cast chain heals really fast for the price of awful stats. Or you went for decent stats and slower chain heal.
CC2 brought us the super fast chain heal which doesn't require you to use bad items.
At the same time, mana problems were mostly solved, too (improved water shield + stable shields).
Now you can spam chain heal without really paying attention to proper downgrades all day long for extreme numbers.
My idea here is some push to healing wave and lesser healing wave and some nerf to chain heal.

Would nerf improved chain heal to give 0,5 second only (so it's like the old chain heal).
At the same time would rework healing way and tidal surge. Chain heal has no reason to grant healing way, it is already pushed. But lesser healing wave should. That's all for healing way.
Tidal surge however, should reduce the casting time of your chain heal by 0,5 sec if the heal's target is affected by healing way.
Basically we're back to 2 second cast time chain heals but now, you have to work for that cast time and have some kind of rotation to get it.

__________________

Small, optional changes for resto: let restorative totems talent buff healing stream totem way more. It is still a joke. Maybe some scaling from +healing would do.

______________________

 **Other suggestion.**
If possible, a party-wide visual representation of the totem radius would help a ton. Melee usually runs around and makes the surprised Pikachu face if they run outside the WF area for 0,0001 second.

That's all.
