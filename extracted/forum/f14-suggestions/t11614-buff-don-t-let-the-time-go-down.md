# Buff don't let the time go down

## Post 76842 by Kilered — 2023-11-10T20:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76842#p76842 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The idea I propose is that while the characters are in a resting area, such as inside a tavern, in a city, etc. the buff time does not decay. This would give a lot of sense to the players to be able to wait, for example, for the World Buff and be calm to do things inside the cities like organize events, groups for dungeons, etc without the rush of knowing that the time of a buff can be lost.
I hope you like the idea because it works very well in the acclaimed EverQuest 1 that I started playing back in 2000 until 2007.
