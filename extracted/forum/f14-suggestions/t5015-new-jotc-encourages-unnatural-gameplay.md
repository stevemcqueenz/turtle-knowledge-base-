# New jotc encourages unnatural gameplay

## Post 30278 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-12-06T18:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30278#p30278 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What if I told you new jotc basically wants you to weapon twist.

Yes this is very Similiar to seal twisting, a mechanic not present on twow.

So how is it done.

 1. Get 2 good procc weapons. My favourite so far shadowstrike+ nightfall
 2. Enchant both of them with cusader
 3. Apply shadow oil on both
 5. Equip nigjtfall
 6. Judge jotc, your weapons procc will often be activated and also the crusader enchant  and shadow oil.
 7. After judge actisve SoR

Watch your swing timer, changing weapons resets it, changing weapons also triggers gcd unless you do it during gcd.

 8. Just before your judgment comes of cd, click seal of crusader, switch to vendorstrike during gcd and don't reset your swing timer
 9. Judgemntet of the crusader to trigger shadowpil, the lifesteal dmg procc and your enchant. Now you have double crusader activated
 10. Immidietly, switch back to nightfall to maximise uptime and active SoR

 11. Keep switching and judging for double crusader enchant uptime.

Its mana intensive to weave seals like this and quite clunky, one could say unnatural gameplay.
But this seems to be the best use of the new jotc so far.
