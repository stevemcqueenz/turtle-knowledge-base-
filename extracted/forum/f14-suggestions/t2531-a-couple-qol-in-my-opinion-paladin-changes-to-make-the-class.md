# A couple QoL (in my opinion) paladin changes to make the class feel better to play

## Post 15745 by Chnams (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-01-13T17:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15745#p15745 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, so I had a couple of ideas in mind to make paladin feel better to play. Nothing insane, just a couple of QoL changes that I feel could be considered for more enjoyment of the class.

 1) Make blessings (might, salvation, wisdom) last longer. 5 minutes baseline is very short and you constantly have to rebuff everyone. It's very tedious. Suggestion : Push it to 15 or even 30 minutes (like other main buffs like arcane brillance, fortitude or wild mark). I don't really see a reason not to do that, I don't think it would make paladin stronger, it would just make it less tedious. But I'm welcoming any arguments as to why it's a good thing that it's so short compared to the other buffs :)

 2) Make seals last longer. 30 seconds is very short and once again, I don't really see a reason for them to be so short apart from adding a mana management constraint (which imo depends more on how much you're spamming judgement and holy strike). This change would probably be a little more controversial as, like I said, having to re-cast seals every 30 seconds means more mana management, which is a big part of vanilla gameplay.
Thus, I propose an idea: Add a glyph (let's call it something like "Empowered Seals") that increases the duration of seals by a certain amount (30 minutes like shaman weapon enchants for example) but that increases their mana cost by 50% or even 100%. That way the mana management component isn't eliminated (as you will need to re-cast it because of Judgement and spamming it will add up quickly) but the seal itself becomes a lot less tedious to use for solo/levelling (since you don't necessarily want to spam judgement right now to not have to refill your mana every 3 mobs). And you let the player choose whether he wants it the old way or the new one.

Discussion about these ideas is of course welcome.

I dont agree with making blessings last longer since we already have Greater Blessings and the blessings themselves are rather strong. (Annoying they dont stack with Shaman Totems but thats just me)

Seals are spent for judgments and doing that would just make the one thing you can do to boost your damage less viable as giving paladins that option will just mean they apply SoR and forget rather than doing anything else. If they cost more to cast, you won't see judgment spamming. I could see this being viable for Rets that just want to apply a seal and then slam Holy Strike however you are taking away a core part of what makes a paladin a paladin.

I'd rather see judgments buffed than changed.
