# Undead Cataclysm Architecture

## Post 115010 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-31T16:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115010#p115010 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the things WoW Cataclysm did right was giving the Undead their own architecture that blends perfectly with the look of the Undercity.

I consider that in-game it's been quite a few years, 7 to be precise, since Lordaeron fell, so I think that in that time the Forsaken could have rebuilt and fortified the abandoned villages they inhabited, leaving behind the human architecture and developing their own to give them more personality.
