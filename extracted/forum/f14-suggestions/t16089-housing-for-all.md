## Post 109609 by Brikoth — 2024-11-24T06:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109609#p109609 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you put all the core features of a city in a player's pocket, it's no question you'll barely ever see anyone outside of them. The core to any good housing system is that it's got no actual benefit to the player other than to feel like you have a special little place in the world that you can mark and call your own. It's a cute idea that everyone so far is correct in that it's a low priority in what should actually be added to the game, but it should be readily apparent people asking for housing don't mean *garrisons*.

Garrisons weren't housing, they were a bad base builder sim that isolated you from the rest of the world with no meaningful customization or coziness you would expect from the term "housing". Also while they were at it, they went and tanked the rest of the expansion tied to it because "hey we said this would happen but YOU guys wanted housing so nyeh".
