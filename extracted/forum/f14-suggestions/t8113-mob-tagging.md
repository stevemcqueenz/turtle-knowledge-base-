# Mob Tagging

## Post 52177 by Bladedqueen — 2023-07-03T12:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52177#p52177 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've run into numerous situations where hardcore and non-hardcore players are questing in an area where the killable mobs are bottlenecked, and the competition to gain kill or item pickup credit is fierce. An obvious solution would be allowing shared mob tagging even among players who aren't grouped. I don't know if this is technically feasible.

This HC/non-HC divide is a source of tension that can't currently be resolved, and it's the one major issue that I and some of my friends have on Turtle WoW, which has otherwise been a great experience. The game's questing and mob spawns were not designed for it, and it's led to some quests simply being dropped, or taking many times as long as they should to complete.
