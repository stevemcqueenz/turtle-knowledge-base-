# Rare spawn timers

## Post 136220 by Rafale (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-18T19:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136220#p136220 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey

 **Problem :**

It seems a lot of rare mobs have a fixed timer for respawn.

At the specific time of his spawn, multiple players including hunters are using traps to get the kill and the loots.

Rare are supposed to be...rare. If you know exactly when the rare is spawning, it's not a "rare" anymore, it's a mob which will give you rare loot at a specific time.

 **Suggestion :**

Instead of a fixed timer, put a random timer on every rare. You need to hardcode a reasonable minimum,but after that include some randomness.

No player would be able to just connect 2 minutes before the spawn, knowing it will happen.
