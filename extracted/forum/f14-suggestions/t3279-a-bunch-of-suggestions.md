# A bunch of suggestions

## Post 20018 by Hithere — 2022-07-15T20:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20018#p20018 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I might add more as I think of them or see them.  Most of my suggestions involve bringing classes further up rather than bringing down stuff that seems strong.  I also know that not all of my suggestions are feasible, realistic, or even necessarily wanted by the developers, but I still think it'd improve the game.

 **Cosmetic/Doesn't Affect Much:**
- Place Paladin/Warlock class mounts into the mount tab and remove their mana cost.
- Make quests grant all cosmetic styles immediately, rather than waiting for the player to equip an item.  Or at least of the stuff the quester can wear.  Sometimes quests have more than one item of the same type and it's kinda a bummer to have to pick and choose.
- Move Hedwig into the pet tab from the general tab of the spellbook.
- Make end raid bosses (like specifically Ragnaros, not just any Molten Core boss) drop non-combat pets of themselves or their offspring rarely (like 1-3% of the time).
- Turn all guardian rewards from quests (like Cleansed Timberling Heart from Planting the Heart, a follow-up to the quest dropped off Blackmoss the Fetid in Teldrassil) into non-combat pet rewards with the same model (just scaled down in most cases).  Probably also make some kind of script that goes through and sends a mail of these items to any player who has already completed the quest, in case they used or threw away their guardian items.
- Let the Shark non-combat pet levitate, and maybe throw a water bubble around his head so he can breathe?
- Add a UI element to allow quests to be accepted as soon as you open a quest, rather than waiting for you to hit accept.
- Add the ability to track things like mailboxes/flightmasters/innkeepers/banks/whatever like they did in future expansions.
- Make the talent pane show all the talents at once, rather than one section at a time.
- Add castbars to the target frame and the nameplates.
- Add debuffs to the target frame and nameplates.
- Change the Stonetalon map so it has art for the Revantusk/goblin areas.
- A button/checkbox/whatever in the spellbook for casters to only show the highest rank of any spell.
- Update the Character Creator to something more like BFA, so each class/race icon is always seen on the screen, for the intents of not limiting a race to only having six class options.
- Update the server/client lua so #showtooltip and other modernized macro stuff works, as well as potentially more updated and functional addons.
- An officially supported HD patch.  Nobody will know how to set up the MPQ files better than you all will.
 **Balance:**
 *Racials:*
- Consider removing weapon skill racials.  They make way too much of a difference in the lategame.  Or just convert them to like +1% crit with those weapons or something like that.  But as it stands, deciding to be any physical class that isn't a human or orc (or in rare cases goblin) means you'll have to buy Blademasters for a huge chunk of change.  It doesn't feel super fair.
- Orc: Make Blood Fury give spellpower to caster classes, and half and half to shaman.  Maybe make healing reduction debuff the same duration as the buff?
- Tauren: Make War Stomp instant.  Reduce male tauren auto attack range to normal.
- Troll: Seems fine, maybe make berserking affect channeled spells but it seems fine without it.
- Undead: Seems fine.
- Goblin: Reduce cooldown to 2 minutes on Exit Strategy.  Remove slow from end.  Probably even remove the pacify but that might be a bit much.  You could throw more flavor at it and make them spend money to do it, as long as it wasn't too crazy I'm sure people wouldn't mind.  Like, 0.5*silver*level or something.
- Human: Perception Replace Perception.  Don't know what with.  Maybe spell pushback immunity, maybe some kind of damage absorption or reflect or some kind of rallying cry, I don't know.  Perception is rough bad; you need to know that something with stealth is there which means it's either an NPC (so who cares?) or that player really just screwed up in PvP.  Could even probably just make Perception a passive like they did in WotLK and remove the spirit buff, but I wouldn't want to see Every Man For Himself from WotLK so don't look too hard at that for inspiration.  And if flavor is all you care about, humans having Perception makes no sense considering how much nonsense is going on right under their noses; they are literally unaware.
- Dwarf: Seems fine.
- Gnome: Make Escape Artist behave like Will of the Forsaken: instant, 2 minute cooldown, and temporary immunity to (in this case) slows and snares.
- Night Elf: Make Shadowmeld behave like Vanish and have a 2 minute cooldown that only starts once you leave Shadowmeld.
- High Elf: Arcane Meditation Reduce Arcane Meditation to 2 minutes and reduce the effect to match.  Maybe make it instant and reduce it further to match.  Honestly, it's just evocation so maybe just replace it with something cooler and more unique to the race, like "push this button for higher magic resistances equal to your level" or something.  Especially with all the mana regeneration coming from retribution paladins and shadow priests and whatnot, I think this racial is gonna be a lot less useful soon anyways.
 *Race/Class Combos:*
These choices are made with just an "okay" understanding of the lore.  Also, sometimes, they're made with the idea that the character creator would be updated to support more than six classes per race.
- Tauren Warlock
- Tauren Paladin
- Night Elf Warlock
- Troll Druid
- Dwarf Shaman
- Human Druid
- Human Hunter
 *Mechanics:*
Just gonna give a quick aside to mention that I don't know for sure if any of these are already in the game or not.
- Remove debuff limit on bosses.
- For MOST debuffs (not stuff like Curse of Elements or Sunder Armor, stuff like Rend or Corruption) allow the players to have their own copy of the spell on the enemy.
 *Quests:*
Please make an effort to change these first two right away.  Even if it's just economically, it's pretty glaring faction misbalance.
- Increase the monetary reward for the Revantusk level 20 daily quest to match the High Elf level 20 daily quest.  Reduce the amount of materials required to something similar to their requirements.  Currently, you spend like three times as long on the Revantusk quest (mostly due to travelling to and from the frogs part) for barely more than a fifth of the reward.
- Reduce requirements dramatically on level 40 Revantusk daily quest.  I mean, seriously, why do Horde have to kill 43ish **ELITES** when Alliance just have 43ish **NORMALS** to kill OR avoid?
- Reduce the amount of demons you kill in Ashenvale for that Horde quest chain where you wind up killing something like 80 or 90 demons across 3 quests.  I know it's a long chain but three quests having you killing the literal same demons to that degree got old.
- Reduce kill counts required for (especially the custom) quests to being no more than 2/3s of the available spawns.  On certain quests, like Securing Darnassus in Teldrassil, if you have so much as one other person there, you might have to wait for respawns two or three times over just to get the quest done.  Or maybe spread spawns in areas like this further out into neighboring areas.
- Make rares that drop quests spawn more quickly.  Like 30 minutes tops.
- Increase drop chance for cow scroll.  Like at least to 3%.  It's one thing for stuff like the Westfall Deed to be such a low drop chance because at least you get loot, if not also experience, out of killing all the Defias, AND they're pretty well grouped up so you don't have to go far, but running all over Elwynn (or whatever) going on a cow slaughter spree for potentially hours is tedious.
- Remove the PvP flag from mobs that you are forced to fight for quests, such as the human farmers in northwest Tirisfal (the custom area) or the two quests in Stormwind (one where you fight in an alley, one where you fight near the library.)
 *Map:*
- Add flightpaths to various Horde camps, like Mudsprocket, Revantusk area in Stonetalon, Wildtusk Village, etc.
- Remove elites and dragons surrounding Mudsprocket, or at least make the nearby guards aggressive towards them, so people who are the level for the quests given there (35-40) aren't trying to avoid 42 elites and 44 dragons.  Hell, there's a level 30 quest that sends you there, so it should probably be reigned in quite a bit if that's the idea.
- Make path up to Wildtusk VIllage more clear.  Darker soil or more tribal doodads or something, way too easy to walk past.
- Make Thunderbluff stop dismounting you all the time.
- Let male tauren into Undercity without dismounting.
- Put anvils closer to the banks in Orgrimmar and Stormwind.
- Give better Horde access to Alah'thelas.  It's pretty easy for Alliance to reach Revantusk, only feels fair.
 *Professions:*
- Give tailoring some kinda way to make money again.  They're getting the short end of the stick with such huge bags in the server store.  Not to say the server store should change or whatever, I get the need for it, but tailoring doesn't have a very good lategame way of making money now.  Not to say there's nothing but it's almost as low of a payout lategame as Engineering now, if not lower.
- Add the enchanting vellum from WotLK so enchanters can just sell their stuff on the auction house.
- Implement Fish Tracking from Burning Crusade.  Could be something you find randomly while fishing up pools during the Fishing Extravaganza or just some quest from Natt Pagle or whatever.
- Allow multiple types of tracking at once, optimally with different colored icons for whatever they are (brown for ore, green for herbs, blue for fish if added, etc.)
- Improve gardening.  I think it'd be more fun if literally you had a UI reminiscent of the hunter stables UI and it just handled itself, took longer (like a day or three or whatever), but would work through logging out and would give more rewards, rather than standing around doing nothing for like 45 minutes.
 *Items:*
- Make items that randomly give spellpower (like of Shadow Wrath or Frozen Wrath or whatever) also give either stamina or intelligence, and maybe also mana regen.  Also make it so items can't randomly have bonus spellpower for an armor slot that doesn't make sense; looking at you, +arcane power plate gear.
- Make items that randomly give resistance also give stamina or defense, and maybe also health regen.
- Make items that randomly give defense also give stamina or armor, and maybe also health regen.
- Make Ring of Thorns actually have a thorns effect instead of just spirit.  Maybe replace the spirit with armor?
- Make Manual Crowd Pummeler have unlimited uses but a longer cooldown (like a minute or two) so we can move past balancing cat druids around them.
- Stop Unlit Torches from being able to be used through right click, since that doesn't give Survival skill anyways.
 *Challenges:*
Hardcore:
While I'm generally loathe to making games easier, the trade level restriction is just forcing hardcore characters to seek "middlemen," or people who act as trade intermediaries.  Having to seek out middlemen is annoying at best and opening yourself to getting stuff stolen from you at worst.  Maybe any hardcore character should be allowed to trade with any hardcore character.  Or at least widen the gap.
As an aside, while I'm not sure what, I feel like something should happen regarding hardcore players having their 60 alt/main follow them around killing stuff for them after they tag it.  Feels like it's going against the point of what's happening here.  I don't see it a lot but it comes off like somebody won a footrace by riding a motorcycle.

New - Glyph of the Diplomat (or Glyph of the Cartel):
Under no circumstance can you be flagged for PvP and you cannot queue for Battlegrounds.  Opposing faction guards/citizens/players see you as an ally, but your reputation remains where it was - hated.  You can do quests for the opposing faction, but all quests (or all Horde/Alliance quests) give 50% less reputation.  Must be taken at level 1, and cannot be turned off until you are neutral with the opposing factions.

 *Classes*
I'm gonna keep this section relatively light in consideration that a lot of class changes are already down the line, but there are still a few I'd like to suggest here.

Druid:
- Let Entangling Roots work indoors.
- Let shifted druids use potions/devices.
- Maybe make on-hit weapon effects work for shifted druids?  Don't know if necessary or how easy it would be.
- Make weapon damage give feral attack power (like several Naxx weapons already do) so druids can escape the Hell of farming Crowd Pummelers.
- Make Travel Form rideable by party members.
Hunter:
- Wyvern Sting Reduce Wyvern Sting to a 30s (or zero) cooldown, reduce the mana cost, and maybe make it castable in combat.  It's explicitly worse than Scatter Shot in basically every sense, it should at least compete considering how far down the talent tree it is.  Or maybe replace it with a better hunter effect like Black Arrow/Explosive Shot (from WotLK/BC respectively) or something if you don't want it to compete with Scatter Shot.
- Also give hunters some way to disable tracking allied creatures/humanoids.  Optimally at will but I can't be the only person that wouldn't mind just not seeing allied humanoids at all.
- Does the deadzone really need to exist?
- Make right-click start ranged auto attack at a range.
- Make feeding a pet buff food give it that buff. (the price of Dragonbreath Chili just skyrocketed)
- Make all pet families have a unique ability, like boars, turtles, birds/bats, wolves, and gorillas have.  Then rebalance the pet abilities so gorillas/turtles don't have garbage cooldowns compared to the rest.
- Change the hit chance formula for ranged attacks to work more like melee attacks, so hunters have reasonable mitigation against glancing blow mechanics.
- Make Mend Pet instant so it's not totally on a healer to keep your pet alive.
Mage:
Reduce Pyroblast cooldown to like a minute.  It feels poopy enough having to pick it up just so you can get Blast Wave for PvP, you don't have to twist the knife.  Or you could have direct fire spell criticals reduce the cooldown on Pyroblast and make it rewarding to cast, but that'd take a lot more talent reorganizing to make sense, I think.

Shaman:
- Give enhancement some form of better spell hit (through melee hit I would assume) the same way paladins have it with judgement, at least with shocks and totems.
- Maybe give elemental Thunderstorm from WotLK over Elemental Mastery so they don't just feel like bad destro warlock in mail?  I dunno.
- Consider changing or removing windfury totem.  It's pretty garbage for balancing when like 20-30% of the warrior's damage (I don't know the exact amount, but in addition to a bunch of extra white damage it gives the warrior all that extra rage) comes from somebody else's move.  It also promotes a really boring style of play called Totem Twisting, wherein the shaman is expected to sit there using Windfury Totem into Grace of Air Totem and then back into Windfury Totem over and over and over the entire boss fight.  Maybe with the addition of haste this coming patch, Windfury Totem could just give a minor haste buff instead.
Paladin:
- Either minor dispel resistance for everything (like 30%) in the form of a protection talent, or dispel punishment on Eye for an Eye - just like, hit them for the mana cost of whatever my spell was or something, I don't know.
- Make Blessing of Sacrifice/Protection/Freedom into their own category of buffs (hands or whatever) so they stop removing other blessings.
- Make Lay on Hands a reasonably accessible cooldown (like you're planning for infernal for warlock) and stop making it deplete the rest of the paladin's mana.  Maybe make it cause Forbearance at this point to keep it balanced.
Priest:
- Make priests unable to Mind Control civilians or any mobs already targetted by an enemy player, and make the mind controlled mob visibly different (like shadowformed or some such), to help prevent griefing (especially hardcore) players.
- Give Fear Ward to everyone, give Dwarf a new, less OP racial.  Maybe do the same thing to Devouring Plague and Undead.  I don't know, less bad there but still kinda bad, especially considering people are more likely to want Shadow Priests when the talents change.
Rogue:
- Replace Vigor.  I don't think I've seen anyone ever use this in years.  It's just kinda mediocre compared to having Preparation for a PvP build (30/0/21) or just being Combat for PvE.  Maybe make it Deadly Throw from Burning Crusade?
- Stop dropping combo points when the rogue loses their target or if the target dies.
Warlock:
- Curse of Exhaustion Curse of Exhaustion is probably the worst talent in the game.  You have to spend 6 talent points to get a 30% slow which is outperformed by literally every other slow in the game unless you amplify it to 50% once every three minutes.  On top of that, it's VERY removable, being able to be removed by mages (through cleansing or blinking), druids (through shifting or cleansing), paladins (through blessing of freedom), rogues (through improved sprint), gnomes, some items (like spider belt), or certain potions - and it won't stop warriors or shaman from reaching you so I don't really know who it's for.  I'm not saying every talent should be AMAZING but it should be better than this.

I have a few ideas, any combination of which I think would be fine:
 1. Just roll Improved Curse of Exhaustion into the base spell, thereby reducing it to 2 talent points total (Curse of Exhaustion and Amplify Curse).
 2. Give ranks to Curse of Exhaustion, so it gets better as you level.
 3. Just buff Curse of Exhausiton by 10%, so it's 20% base, 40% with talents.
 4. Remove Curse of Exhaustion, roll the effects of the talents into Curse of Weakness, and maybe pull the Curse of Weakness talent up to here and combine it.

Or you could just replace Curse of Exhaustion because to be honest I don't really want Warlock to feel like it's just imitation mage.
- Maybe do something similar to warlocks and curses as paladins and blessings, making Curse of Agony/Doom into "Banes" that don't remove the other curses your allies might want you to be using.
- Make any unenslaved demons summoned by the warlock (such as Doomguard or Infernal) only attack and harm the warlock, and automatically be tagged by them.
- Make Health Funnel instant and apply a HoT to the demon, so healing the demons is still mostly on the healer but not totally.
- Give specifically late-stage Demonology some kind of way to restore their demon's mana so the demon can keep fighting.  I'd imagine it would be through either Life Tap or Health Funnel as a talent.
- Also, I don't see any reason Healthstone should share a cooldown with engineering stuff, like Target Dummy or Recombobulator.  To the best of my knowledge, no other class has something like this; though I might be mistaken about the mage mana gems, at which point I'd argue to change that too.  EDIT: Apparently, Healthstones, Mana Gems, and Thistle Tea all share a cooldown with engineering stuff.  While I could be swayed about Thistle Tea, I still think this is odd, and that something like an engineering item (with the generally high cost of crafting) shouldn't share a cooldown with basic class equipment.
 *Warrior:*
Give all the warriors a standardized set of equipment and make them fight to the death to reduce the amount of warriors on the server.  Only the mighty can survive.

 *"I'm gonna keep it light," they say, as they type out the biggest section here.*
