# Priests Prayer of Healing usable on Raid

## Post 52898 by Noisestorm — 2023-07-10T22:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52898#p52898 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So from playing a while as a Priest now I feel somewhat frustated about the groupheal in raids. Most of the time melee parties are set up with 4 warris/rogues and a shaman, which means no place for a Priest. But sadly melees tend to get a lot of aoe damage. From what I've seen so far Shamans can use a chain heal to heal pretty much everyone, while I as a priest have to resort to using single target heals 95% of the time. This sometimes feels like a huge waste instead of being able to support with a heavily needed groupheal.

Basically my question or suggestion:
Why not giveus the possibility to use Prayer of Healing on other raid parties like you can do with Prayer buffs?
Would this make Priest too strong?
