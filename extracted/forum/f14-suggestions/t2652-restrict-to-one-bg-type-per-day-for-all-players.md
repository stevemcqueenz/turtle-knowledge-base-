# Restrict to one bg type per day for all players

## Post 16584 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-02-01T20:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16584#p16584 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
The discord user minno suggested the following today on the pvp General discord and mutkipel people thought it was a great idea:

Minno:
"With this low pop, they should unlock one bg only and rotate every week. So if it's AV week, that's all you can join, If it's AB, then that's the one."

Yx answered:
"interesting concept, would allow them to implement rep bonuses and such, would stop the issue of queue hopping too"

Zaas added:"people would actually play the new BG if they did this"

Minno:
"if there is an AV pop and then either AB or WSG.  The first problem is we don't have enough players to field 4-5 BGs at once.  So what you end up seeing is a stacked AV and then two bgs where one side dominates over the other.  Forcing the pop into 1 type will ultimately fix the issue, until the server grows to 4-5k (which might not happen). "

Nikoali:
" That's a great idea, could even be one per day "

To which zaas agreed:" one per day is prob best"
