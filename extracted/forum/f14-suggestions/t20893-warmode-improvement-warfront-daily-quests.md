# Warmode Improvement - Warfront Daily Quests

## Post 145174 by Orcandpals — 2025-08-13T18:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145174#p145174 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dropped this as a response to the Warmode changes announcement that recently went out but felt it could benefit from standing alone as suggestion so reposting here as it's own thread.

Thank you devs for the update to War Mode to provide a small amount of XP for kills in the overworld. It's not a huge thing but even a small reward for engaging in open world pvp (which is what I believe the premiere value of war mode should be) is huge. I understand why ya'll have gone so light on the rewards initially in fear of avoiding incentivization of negative play patterns with farming players being more valuable a method of xp farming then interacting with the world and the possible abuse cases of kill farming.

However I do think there is one very simple solution that leans into the direction you have taken the open world aspects of Warmode to the next level while avoiding all of the abuse cases. Daily Warfront Quests!

Please add a series of daily quests to the game that say something to the equivalent of "Kill X enemy players in Y zone while you are flagged for PvP through the War Mode challenge"

Ideally these quests should have a 10 level band starting at level 15 for 15-25, 25-35, 35-45, 45-55, and 55-60, turning gray and giving zero experience to any players above that max level. These quests should direct players to a specific zone each day/week (depending on the rotation cadence you would prefer) with one daily quest available per level band. These quests should ONLY be for contested zones that have quests/expected presence for both factions NOT any zone that is "contested" in name only.

EX: For the 15-25 Bracket the daily would alternate between Stonetalon Mountains and Ashenvale
For the 25-35 Bracket the daily would alternate between Hillsbrad Foothills, Stranglethorn Vale, Desolace, Arathi Highlands, and Alterac Mountains
For the 35-45 Bracket the daily would alternate between Dustwallow Marsh, Badlands, Swamp of Sorrows etc.
For the 45-55 Bracket the daily would alternate between Ungoro Crater, Felwood, Azshara, Blasted Lands, etc.
For the 55-60 Bracket the daily would alternate between Eastern Plaguelands, Winterspring, Silithus, Burning Steppes, etc.

Completion of these quests should give a good chunk of XP and honor and basically act as a respectable once-daily reward for engaging in world PvP. This accomplishes many things. First off it naturally routes world PvP minded players to quest in specific zones where there will be other PvP minded players within an appropriate level range for them to engage with in the area. Having the zones rotate on a daily or twice daily basis also ensures that the same zone isn't completely overrun with a scourge of PvP gankers for entire weeks on end.

Focusing the XP and honor in these daily quests also prevents abuse of the system. Because the system is hardcapped at one quest per level range per day and can only be completed while you have the war mode challenge active, you can't just get your friends to jump on a bunch of alternate faction toons and powergrind to 60 by killing each other over and over. This allows you to give MEANINGFUL rewards to players seeking world pvp and engaging with the war mode system without enabling abuse cases or motivating large scale griefing.

There are definetely tweaks that could be made to this system that would require more or less effort (for example you could "tighten" level bands, tie them instead to recommended min/max level for zones and have one quest per unique zone level range and just accept that in some areas you will have overlap where level 20 players will have access to both 15-25 Warfront quests and 20-30 Warfront quests) but I think as presented it is an EASY win that has almost no effort required to massively improve the experience for players looking to engage with War Mode.
