# Proposal for Adjusting PvP Balance in Battlegrounds: ratio-based system to deal with twinks

## Post 160286 by Micreedt — 2025-11-22T00:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160286#p160286 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi everyone,

I’d like to propose an idea regarding the current state of twinks in battlegrounds. I understand that twinking is a part of the game’s mechanics and should be respected as such. However, there are many players who are not twinks and simply want to enjoy leveling through PvP rather than questing.

From my observations, many players tend to quit or AFK during battlegrounds when they face a team with too many twinks. This results in a less enjoyable experience for non-twinks, as the outcome often feels predetermined.

I’d like to suggest implementing a ratio-based system to better balance teams. Here’s how it could work:

If a player is a twink , they could be assigned a higher point value based on their stats and winning potential. For example:

Twink (A) with a higher win rate gets 2 points.

Non-twink players get 1 point each.

A balanced team would then be formed by matching the total points of both sides, ensuring that teams with twinks are not overwhelmingly strong.

For example:

Team 1 could consist of a twink (A with 2 points) + one non-twinks (each with 1 point), giving them a total of 3 points.

Team 2 would be  consist of 3 non-twinks.

I believe this system would create a more enjoyable experience for all players, ensuring that both twinks and non-twinks can have fun in battlegrounds without feeling either overpowered or underpowered.

Thank you for considering this proposal. I’m open to discussing it further if needed!
