# Relocate/Retcon Amani'Alor (to Hinterlands as an option)

## Post 155840 by Catlike — 2025-10-16T21:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155840#p155840 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Lore-wise it doesn't really make sense for the forest trolls to be present in Kalimdor - vanilla wow shows that the only Horde-aligned forest trolls, Revantusk tribe, is not a very large tribe, meaning it wouldn't make sense for them to split their resources, endangering their survival in Hinterlands by sending a lot of people not only to the other side of Azeroth, but to an isolated location, behind a part of Stonetalon that is controlled by the Horde. And while there are a lot of outposts of different factions in vanilla WoW that might not make sense from the logistics' standpoint, they're at least not connected by *portals* of all things.

Overall, I think it would be much better if the entire place got relocated to the Eastern Kingdoms - particularly, into that spot between Hinterlands and Scarlet Enclave - it's a perfect place for Amani settlement controlled by Revantusk to exist. As for getting there - a small forsaken port can be added (maybe use one in western tirisfal uplands) from which a boat will go to Amani'Alor. Alternatively, just slightly change the goblin port boat route in Durotar - although I believe it makes more sense for travel route to start in Tirisfal, since it is closer geographically and it would not centralize travel routes too much.

(as for the relocated zone plot - I imagine something like Revantusk finding the old ruins, reclaiming them, while also fighting off the Scarlet Crusade and occasional Scourge necromancers)

Second option: in case the relocation is for some reason not an option, then I propose the next best thing - retcon local trolls into Dark Trolls and change scenery to be more Ashenvale-like. While the Dark Trolls weren't shown to be Horde-aligned in Warcraft 3, I believe it is much more believable for a Dark Troll tribe to join the Horde, than for the Revantusk to suddenly double in size and make an outpost on the other side of the world.
