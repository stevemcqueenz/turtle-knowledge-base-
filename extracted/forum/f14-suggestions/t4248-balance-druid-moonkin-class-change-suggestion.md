**Moonkin changes by Draxer**
Guide:
> ( ) = some explaination
>  [ ] = Example
>  { } = Note

 **A b i l i t i e s :**

Wrath
Increase the dmg the less hp the enemy has, so it would do more dmg than Starfire against enemy with 30% hp or less. (Makes Moonkin to balance his Arcane and Nature to make his dmg higher and increase the skill ceilling)

Starfire
Always stuns in 10yds. (Stolen from Vanilla+).

Moonfire
Applies personal buff that increases the dmg taken of the enemy by Arcane spells.

Insect Swarm
Applies personal buff that increases the dmg taken of the enemy by Nature spells.

Moonkin Faerie Fire
Doesn't activates GCD but has 3s CD. (Allowes Moonkin to combine spell with FF, making them best to keep FF up while they can keep on Casting without issue).

Barkskin
% of incoming dmg gives mana while this buff is active. (could be like 25% of dmg taken)

Hurricane
Remove CD.
Chance to spread Moonfire/Insect Swarm of already afflicted enemies to enemies nearby to them with current duration of the dot. [Example: When it procs on dot that has 5s duration left, the enemies that gets it from spread will have 5s of it]
^(Moonkin sucks at aoe, thought this might increase the aoe of Moonkin without breaking the game)^
{Note: This will eat Moonkin's mana fast, so it is Risk/Reward}

Melee changes
Allow melee weaving.
Melee hits restore mana depending on the melee dmg [Example: Attack that does 300 dmg restores 300 mana] (in Moonkin form).
Melee has chance to make next spell cast instant (in Moonkin form).

Mount changes
Allow Moonkin to mount up, the form is essentially the same as Shadow Form that Shadow Priests have, and yet one can mount, the other cannot. Immunity while mounted to polymorph doesn't really change anything and most Moonkins wear Caster gear anyway.

 **T a l e n t s:**

Moonkin Form talent
Increase Spell Crit chance by 5% (Make it stronger than Atiesh).
Increase Spell Crit dmg by 90% (Self only). {NOTE: Value is just randomly written without thought, in real it might be closer to like 20-30%.}
Increases Spell Crit dmg of the party by 10% {NOTE: Combines with personal increase, to make Boomkin actual BOOMkin when they crit.}
Chance on crit to give people in the party Nature's Grace (30% chance).
Allow Cure Poison(Dispells 1 poison, not the one that gives buff that keeps dispelling) and Mark of the Wild/Gift of the Wild in Moonkin form with the Remove Curse and Innervate.

Nature's Grace talent
Mandatory for Moonkin Form talent so the Nature's Grace buff to party would make sense.

Moonfury talent
Increases the damage done by 10% and make it affect all dmg, not just base dmg when 5/5

Improved Moonfire talent
Rename it onto Elune's Blessing
Increases the damage and crit strike chance of Moonfire AND Starfire by 10% when 5/5.

Improved Wrath talent
Reduces cast time by 0.5s and makes Wrath have a chance, let's say 10% to re-cast Insect Swarm on the enemy when 5/5.

Synergy idea: Make Warlock's Curse of Elements be actually Curse of Elements, so it increases dmg taken of Nature Abilities aswell. Buffs both Moonkin and Elemental, the only two struggling Casters.
Synergy idea 2: Make ZG enchant's Classless, make all classes be able to pick which ZG enchant they want.

 **PS: To all who think it would make Moonkin broken never actually seen or played Moonkin to know how in a BAD shape they are compared to the REST of the Casters.**

But honestly I've been saying we need buffs like these for close to a year now. Starfire/Wrath just doesn't do the damage it needs to for competition, but often neglected is the dps increase on the party with moonkin aura. I think the numbers come out to ~150dps increase total in a 4-mage party just for having a boomkin there, so I'd imagine the extra 2% crit chance would be linear and increase it by another ~100dps, meaning a boomkin doesn't have to be top dps to contribute to the top damage. Just take their dps, add in the crit buff estimated dps, whatever that actually is exactly, and it should give some good estimates on just how much left there is to improve them to make them viable.

Some of these ideas are nice for flavor or soloing, but I don't see hurricane being used in more than low-level farming and very niche support for tanks. Barkskin, weaving, and both wrath changes I'd love to see, and the rest are definitely worthy of at least some consideration.

Combine this with a few more itemization additions or changes to existing items and they might measure up to 80% of a warlock one day.
