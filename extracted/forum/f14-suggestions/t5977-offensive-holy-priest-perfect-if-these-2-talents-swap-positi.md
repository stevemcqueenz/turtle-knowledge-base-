# Offensive Holy Priest: perfect if these 2 talents swap position

## Post 37535 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-13T12:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37535#p37535 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

An offensive holy priest build would be perfect and very enjoyable if the following two talents position was swapped in the discipline tree:

Mental Strength
(exchanging position with)
Force of Will

This way an offensive holy priest can get both Force of Will from the discipline tree and Spiritual Guidance from the Holy tree.

## Post 37782 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-14T23:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37782#p37782 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Additionally, it'd be a good thing if this happened because thematically it'd make more sense that Force of Will is what is the requirement for the last talent, Power Infusion.

Also, it would increase overall talent tree coherence, Mental Strength giving passive 2/.../10% increase of something, fits more to be in the place of Force of Will, as in that row in many specs there is a talent giving that sort of bonus (for example shadow priest 10% shadow damage, subtlety 10% atp, prot war 10% one hand dmg etc.)

## Post 60376 by Quidoba (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-31T21:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60376#p60376 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

That would be awesome, but I have major reservations about such an impactful change.

The priest talents are actually pretty amazing as they stand right now. I'm coming to realize just how wide of an array of viable play styles and meaningful choices they offer.

I would have to run some numbers to be certain but I'm pretty sure making just the change you suggest would risk making spiritual guidance + Force of Will mandatory for a holy damage priest and make PI irrelevant for that play style.  This would remove some choice you have currently between (Force of Will+PI) vs. (Spiritual Guidance + optionally spirit tap if you are mostly doing solo content).  As it stands now, it is a pretty well balanced trade-off, although admittedly slightly tilted towards the FOW+PI side for dungeons/raids.

I do however like the idea of making holy priests more spirit focussed as it carves out a niche for an under-utilized stat on the damage dealing side.  However, I would like to see a change that doesn't just create a mandatory min-maxing spec, and leaves some room for variation in play style.

Tbh, all talents improving the offensive spellcaster Holy Priest are in the Holy tree, except for the Force of Will which is deep Discipline tree talent. Another way to go, for the sake of coherence, would be to include a new talent next to the Spiritual Healing talent which is deep in the Holy tree, (there is only 1 talent in that row):

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/large/spell_holy_divineprovidence.jpg)
Divine Providence
Talent
Requires Priest (Holy)
Casting Smite grants you 'Divine Providence', which increases your Holy Fire and Holy Nova critical chance by 3% for 12 seconds. Stacks up to 5 times and will be spent once Holy Fire or Holy Nova are used.

This would allow the offensive spellcaster Holy Priest to be more clearly defined (as well as be a more viable build).
