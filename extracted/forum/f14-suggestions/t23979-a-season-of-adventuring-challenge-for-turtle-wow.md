# A ''Season of Adventuring'' challenge for Turtle WoW

## Post 168364 by Sineva1 — 2026-02-15T23:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168364#p168364 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I think it would really fun to have a ''Season of Adventuring'' in turtle wow.

But i don't like gatekeeping challenges that don't let you form groups with others players.

So, this ''Season of Adventuring'' should be open and at the same time closed enough so you have to work to adventure and get your gear and do the new dungeons in a new way.

''Season of Adventuring'' is a challenge that can be stacked with other challenges, you can play ''Season of Adventuring'' challenge and slow and steady and boaring adventure at the same time. you could even play ''Season of Adventuring'' and hardcore.

Adventurers can form a group or trade with other players (except hardcore players) if they are the same or lower level than the adventurer.

i think it would be fun if it is also divided in phases.

Each phase should last a certain amount of time (2-3 months) until the next phase is released.

My suggestion is:

Phase 1: maximum level 29. Recommended final content: Dragonmaw Retreat.

Phase 2: maximum level 39. Recommended final content: Stormweought Ruins, Sacarlet monastery, etc.

Phase 3: maximum level 49. Recommended final content: Maraudon, Gilneas city, Sunken temple, etc.

Phase 4: Maximum level 55. Recommended final content: Hateful quarry, Dire maul east, LBRS, Black rock depths, etc.

Once you reach level 30, 40, 50 and 60 (when it's available) you get multiple rewards. Mounts, titles, etc.

Why this would be fun?

People would form groups to  get their bises, they would level their professions to get their enchants, consumables, etc. people would be very interested about doing ''low level'' content in a serious manner.

i think you should be able to speak to an NPC somewhere in the capital cities that let you abandon this challenge if you want.

What is the difference between this and twinking? Twinks are carried by their mains, they carry them in dungeons so they get the best items, they enchant their items with high level enchant so they are overpowered. in this case you can't trade or form a group with higher level characters so you can´t carry your adventurer. You need to form a group with 4 other adventurers (or players with same or lower level) and do the dungeons to get your BIS gear! and that is fun, isn't it?

Yes, of course, this challenge is heavily inspired by WoW Season of Discovery. But... one small difference, this would be in Turtle WoW! With harder, more interesting, and much better dungeon content!

Who doesn't want to run a Stormweought Ruins for their BIS epic staff?

Thank you.

I like the intention of encouraging low level content.

But people already have stopxp feature. If they don't use it, then they are not really interested.
I assume you propose mounts, title and other rewards as a reason for people to stop.
If some guy wants to skip leveling content intentionally, I doubt that any leveling reward will encourage him to stop. I don't think it is good idea to reward "lvl 30" with item that is useful at lvl 60.

I think it is just much easier to ban boosting in dungeons and ban high level enchants.
In this case, wannabe twinks will have to do dungeons with everyone else.
Though boost ban is not very good idea too. People just don't want to socialize intentionally. So don't force them to do.

There is another potential problem - lack of tanks/heals and long queues for DPS.
If you force people to stay at specific level, then they all get stuck in super long queue for dungeons.
So you need to encourage people to play as tanks and heals in that challenge.

If you propose that as server-wide feature, then it doesn't need additional rewards. Sounds like another "fresh" server with progression. It might work, though. There is Ambershire is in progress.
I'm not sure that limiting whole server at level 30 will be fun for long time because there is not enough content for long time. PvP is endless, but I assume you mean dungeons too.
Every epic staff from Stormwrought Ruins will be replaced in next phase. I'm not sure it worth grinding.
