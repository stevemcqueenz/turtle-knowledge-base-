# Consider Giving The PVP Trinket Multiple Charges

## Post 159812 by Solveit — 2025-11-16T15:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159812#p159812 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

"Custom" pvp might be more bearable for some if the functionality of the pvp trinket allowed it to build up more than one charge with the same or similar cooldown per charge.

I.E. your trinket could have up to 2 charges and each time you use a charge it would add a 2 minute cooldown for that charge. You could frontload two trinket uses in a bad matchup or situation, but it would be 2 minutes to get the first charge back and then another 2 minutes to get the next charge back.

You could make it so equipping the trinket resets the charges to mitigate gear swapping abuse.

The net result is no increase in total trinket usage as the cooldown per use is retained, but it gives players more control and more options for dealing with custom content in pvp.
