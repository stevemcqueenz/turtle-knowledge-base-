Good Day,

I trust this suggestion makes some sense, remove or restrict world buffs from being applicable in Battlegrounds especially  at lower levels as from level 1-49 the effects of World Buffs can grossly affect how the battlegrounds feel. Characters who are already at a disadvantage due to gear which is fine are at an even greater disadvantage with movement speed world buffs, +15% stat increases and Attack power & Critical strike chance increases due to these buffs.
