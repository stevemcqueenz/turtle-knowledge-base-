# Add weapon enchant glows to transmog

## Post 107441 by Ai96 — 2024-11-10T20:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107441#p107441 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Add a dummy item that can be enchanted with weapon enchants. If you get one of these enchanted with say fiery weapon and turn it in to the transmog NPC, the dummy item is consumed and fiery weapon glow is added to your set of weapon enchant appearances. Now you can make your weapon glow any way you'd like that fits with your transmog set.
