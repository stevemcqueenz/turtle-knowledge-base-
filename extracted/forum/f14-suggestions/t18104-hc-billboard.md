# HC billboard

## Post 123252 by Torek22 — 2025-03-21T19:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123252#p123252 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I ve lately seen the hc chat people spamming looking for group, or selling/buying something. Which is ok but kind of disrupts any conversation that could occur on that chat.
It would be better to have it separately, it could be a billboard on the main cities, where you can add a request that has 1d duration. Since all this kind of interactions really depend on the lvl 1 day is more than enough for the need of it to be updated or discarded.
It could be a simple tab with Trade and other for Group. You just add what you sell, and people that wants to buy could search for it directly (for simplicity just the item, the level and the name so you can wisp and arrange, just as you would on the chat). Same goes for the Groups.
This way you would prevent spamming on the chat, and even have the tool to look for something (lets say im looking for X item, i can search for it directly).

The idea of the billboard would be so it keeps the hc kind of rp as it mostly is.
