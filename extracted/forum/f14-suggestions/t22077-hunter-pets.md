Two ideas:
 - I like how retail takes MM hunters in the direction of eagles
 - Introduce a new pet class family or something being spirit beasts that have some heal ability and focuses on support. It shouldn't be OP, but a flavor and a new style. Could be combined like having "pet family" depending on which beast. For instance the anyway existing Ghost Saber would be one. Claw or Bite abilities would have to be swapped for the spirit heal ability.

Ghost Spirit Wolf could be another, where one of the wolf abilities would be swapped for the healing one. Etc.

I believe I've already seen other transparent/spirit/ghostly hunter pets around that would fit into this new niche.
