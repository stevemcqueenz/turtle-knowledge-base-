# New practical lower level weapon Enchantments

## Post 107449 by Killyourtv — 2024-11-10T21:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107449#p107449 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I take everything into consideration when writing this post. Hear me out.

By this I mean to suggest affordable, practical but appropriate weapon enchants for leveling 10-40.
I see a lack of 'practical' weapon enchants during leveling. We have 'Minor Beastslayer' and 'Minor Impact' aswell as enchants with damage to elementals, demons etc. Apart from the first two, these are very specific and impractical 90% of the time, when you are not encountering these mobs. Plus, these only apply to melee damage, so has no use for, forexample Hunters.

What if we got more low Enchants, where the bonus wasen't that great, but it was affordable and it made your weapon look a lot nicer?

One idea:
'Minor Penetration' +4 to Attack Power (Only for 2h melee weapons) - As a hunter, this would give me a bonus to my gun and give my polearm on my back a cool glow.

As far as I know other than the expensive and high level enchant 'Enchant 2H Weapon - Agility' there are no other weapon enchant that give ranged damage bonus.

Maybe instead of my idea, there could be a ' 'Enchant 2H Weapon - Minor Agility'?

Forgive me if this is not a good suggestion, I just think Enchanting is a great porfession and I feel like it isn't a big enough part of early game of WoW and I have always wanted it to be!

I am not critizizing the server in anyway. This is something I have always felt was missing in WoW since Vanilla.

Let me know what you think.

From a true WoW classic fan,
SIgning out.
