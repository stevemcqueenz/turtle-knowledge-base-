# Rogues and their poisons

## Post 8075 by Discostew — 2020-10-09T08:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8075#p8075 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Rogues can apply poisons to their weapons, but what about cleaning the poisons off? I know in later expansions rogues were able to clear the poisons off their weapons (via right-click on the buff I think). After running a dungeon with a shaman, I found it annoying that I couldn't benefit from totems like Windfury until the poison cleared by itself, whether by charges or time. Would be nice to clear them on demand, whether by the method used in retail, or via item/skill, like a cleaning cloth.

Other rogue stuff, but not as important. Some were dealt with in later expansions.

 - Removal of charges  (or increase charge count)
 - Increased buff time (currently 1/2 hour)
 - Increased stack size of poisons and reagents in bags

Personally, I like the last of the 3, if only one could be chosen.

Yeah they're all filling the temporary weapon enchant slot. You can't simultaneously have a weapon oil and windfury, poisons and windfury, poisons and a weapon oil, etc.
This is why you see in raids melee will often ask if they are getting in a shaman group not to beg but to know if they need to apply poisons/sharpening stones.

Edit: Adding more charges or extending the duration of charges makes this problem worse bc it makes it harder to get rid of poisons so you can start benefiting from windfury.

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8144#p8144 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If weapon poisons are turned into a character buff (instead of a weapon buff), then rogues can stack Instant Poison x2 with Windfury on main hand & ESS on off hand in raids. This would be a sizable DPS increase to one of the already most powerful classes. This would also be a PvP nightmare as it would result in Rogues getting a +4% crit bonus in PvP because they would be able to apply double ESS as well as the usual PvP poison line-up. People already complain that Rogue is broken in PvP and this would only create more drama.

Edit: Now that spells can be created, a universal solution would be best IMO. It could mechanically be an item that applies a buff to your weapon for 1 second, and it would be available to all classes. This would also allow other classes to "clean" sharpening stones, oils, etc off their weapons as well as poisons off of Rogue weapons.
