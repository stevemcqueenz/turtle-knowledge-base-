# Make engenireeng not mandatory for feral druids

## Post 93726 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-29T12:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93726#p93726 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This post is about the bind on pickup battery powered crowd pummeler. The best feral weapon in the game besides maybe some adjusted naxx weapons with added feral attack power.

There is the standard mcp from gnomer, but it has limited charges and needs regarding, whereas the battery version can be enchanted and reused with batteries.

The problem is, why is the battery mcp bind on pickup. Basically means druid need to lvl engie, craft it and then they could drop engineering again if they wanted. This is very limiting.

Why is this needed. Blacksmith items aren't bop either. Would it make sense to have lionheart helm blacksmith self exclusive by making it bop. Or blood vine sets?

Please change the battery powered crowd pummeler to bind on equip so feral druid can just get one crafted, and can choose alchemie to fulfill their role play without being punished

P. S. I don't play druid on twow but It makes me sad when I tell guildies about this that picked the wrong proffesion
