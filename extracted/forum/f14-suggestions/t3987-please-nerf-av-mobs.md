# Please Nerf AV Mobs

## Post 24101 by Davemustang — 2022-09-01T06:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24101#p24101 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I will be the first to say, the stuff Turtle WoW has implemented into AV is definitely interesting (especially the mines), and would probably be amazing in 40v40 games.

However, the average game I've played in usually has 15-20 at best and the way AV works right now makes it hard to enjoy with so few people.

The biggest issue is with the PvE Mobs. They respawn ***WAY*** too fast, sometimes virtually instantly and right on top of themselves. This makes it impossible to consistently hold a flag or even move around the map sometimes cause you have to fight 5+ groups of mobs just to recall back to your base.

I can already see the "Skill Issue" response, I just feel that more people would queue AV or at least stay in the games they do queue if the mob respawn timer was increased (even if just slightly)
