# Class Quests!

## Post 23285 by Twili — 2022-08-24T03:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23285#p23285 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all,

I think adding some class quests would be really cool and add a lot of flavor to each class. Now I know there are several class quests that already exist in the game, but I mean something really above and beyond a simple quest of 'kill x and return to me' that's only available for certain classes.

A good reference to use would be the warlock green fire questline in MoP. A completely solo experience that's basically a challenge-mode skill check of sorts that fully respects the lore of the class. You start with talking to your demons to learn more about a mysterious grimoire and it ends with a final fight where you have to fully utilize all the tools that warlocks have to offer - enslave the pit lord and use his abilities to help you throughout the fight, manage your fears and cds to fight the incoming waves of demons, etc.

And for all your efforts you get a cool cosmetic that showcases your mastery of the class. Someone had mentioned some priest wings picked up from the DB, those could easily be a reward for a quest line like this. This concept could be easily applied to every class, and would offer the quest writers a real opportunity to get really creative on the lore and devs to design some really cool encounters.

I'd like to stress that no material reward be given for a quest line like this (IE no items with stats). Just some cosmetic that respects the lore of the class, a class specific transmog set, something along those lines.
