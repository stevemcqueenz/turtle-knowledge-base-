# Duelist Glyph/Mode

## Post 69173 by Kawerte1 — 2023-10-06T13:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69173#p69173 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What do you guys think about a new glyph/mode called "Duelist":

 1) Every 10 levels you are forced to win 2 out of 3 Mak'gora against the same enemy in order to continue leveling and get some reward.
 2) No buffs (except self-buffs from spells) and no item usage
 3) Your must use the dueling gear which is given to you for free (so everyone has same gear)
 4) The duel is done in the STV arena (where you will get ported to from a NPC)
 5) You choose which classes you are willing to fight before you queue from a NPC.
 6) You get to choose your talents for the duels from a NPC before you queue. Those talents will only be applied for the duel.
 7) A player leaving during or after a duel will count as a red flag. The player that didn't leave will get small compensation reward, but no win.
 8) If you have a red flag or lose/leave the duel area 2 times, for 3 in-game days you will have the title "The Defeated" and a debuff which forbids you from dueling.
