"Why do people do this thing I don't like to do, I don't have to do, and that I don't do?"

Because people are there for scourgestones and gear, but don't need the quests. If they wanted to do the quests, they'd do a 5man. Also keep in mind, when you have 10 people, your chance of getting scourgestones yourself compared to one of the other 9 people is reduced, so it's a cost-benefit weighing that people do.  They decide that they'd rather have a faster and more efficient run at the cost of less potential rep or gear gains. It balances itself out, and if people want to make that choice, they're allowed to. These dungeons were, in fact, 10 man allowed in vanilla.

No one is making you do 10mans. If you were genuinely curious, I've answered your question. If you're criticizing it... Shut up and don't worry about things that don't affect you?
