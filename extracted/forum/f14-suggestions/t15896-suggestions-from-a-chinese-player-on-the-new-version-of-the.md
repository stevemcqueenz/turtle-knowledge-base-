# Suggestions from a Chinese player on the new version of the Warlock

## Post 108261 by muzhiling1 — 2024-11-17T08:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108261#p108261 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone, I am a player from the Turtle WoW Asian server.
huge thanks to the Turtle WoW team for the Warlock class changes. This article focuses solely on the performance of the new Warlock in the current Naxxramas (NAXX) endgame and PVE. All theoretical analyses and suggestions are solely my own opinions. The talents and skill effects mentioned in this article are as of November 12, 2024.
This article was roughly translated using GPT. Some abbreviations and short forms may not be accurately translated, and there may be grammatical issues. Please excuse any errors.

=========================================================

Destruction（shadow）

In the previous version, the Warlock's raid talents were very uniform, mostly consisting of 21/9/21 Sacrifice-shadow Destruction. The optimal rotation was to maintain Corruption while summoning Infernal and spamming Shadow Bolt. In terms of operation, it was still stronger than Frost Mage.

The new version has increased the tier of the Demon Sacrifice talent, but its effect has been severely nerfed. The current Sacrifice effect is essentially 3% without a demon + 6% from Sacrificing a Succubus = 9%. This makes Sacrifice Destruction not an excellent choice in the current version, because you can invest more talent points in the Affliction tree to gain a 10% bonus to shadow damage, along with additional tricks like Nightfall and dual curses.

Therefore, while ensuring a 100% critical strike bonus in Destruction, the optimal talent build for shadow Destruction in the current version is 30/0/21.
图像

GRIM REACH and DESTRUCTIVE REACH may peace of mind , In mobile battles (such as Grobbulus), you can cast Corruption and Curse of Agony as fillers. The DOTs can slightly benefit from haste, and occasional Nightfall procs with double crits can be quite satisfying.

Therefore, in theory, this talent build is quite comfortable in raids. However, in practice, it doesn't hold up for the entirety of Naxxramas (NAXX) or Ahn'Qiraj (TAQ).

This is because the main damage-boosting mechanism of shadow Destruction, Improved Shadow Bolt, has been heavily nerfed. A 5% damage reduction isn't the issue; the core triggering method now directly limits the current version's output capability of Dark Destruction.

For example, in my group, with more than three Warlocks in the previous version, we could almost maintain Shadow Bolt vulnerability coverage, and for some special bosses like Loatheb, we could achieve full coverage. But now, the number of Warlocks and gear (mainly critical strike) doesn't seem to significantly increase Shadow Bolt vulnerability coverage. Based on my recent runs and a sample of 15,000 Shadow Bolt casts, the damage of Shadow Bolt is approximately 12% lower than in the previous version .

Additionally, without investing points in demonology talents, summoning Infernal quickly gets out of control and serves no purpose other than causing chaos in the team. Therefore, this talent build can utilize Imp/Succubus for auxiliary damage. However, in actual raid experiences, pets die too easily! Apart from Patchwerk, where pets can fully perform their roles, pets die too easily on other bosses.

Even with the support of Nightfall and dual DOT tricks, the damage output of Destruction Warlock has decreased compared to the previous version.

Moreover, with the significant reduction in the number of Shadow Priests, who are good partners for Warlocks, the damage output of Destruction Warlocks is further limited.

In summary, the current version's Destruction Warlock, in terms of rotation and raid experience, is quite similar to the previous version's Sacrifice Destruction, but the damage output experience and damage-dealing capability have slightly decreased.

Destruction（FIRE）

A rising star in the new version, previously unknown in the older version, has seen a surge in popularity due to the wave of novelty and the underwhelming performance of Dark Destruction.

Additionally, the primary damage-dealing skills (Searing Pain, Immolate, Conflagrate) have quick cast times, are less affected by hit rating, and have an interesting rotation. Some experts even assert that this will be the only viable option for Warlocks in the future.

For example, using a Flask of Supreme Power, your average damage for Shadow Bolt will increase by around 128 points, but Searing Pain will only increase by around 63 points.

The spell power scaling is not as significant as that of Shadow Destruction, which makes Fire Destruction more reliant on critical hits than Fire Mages. Additionally, in the current version, there is a significant drawback with Soul Fire—if it misses, the 8% fire damage bonus is not applied, making the next 30 seconds very difficult.

Furthermore, Fire Destruction does not have Improved Life Tap, and Life Tap benefits from shadow damage bonuses but not fire damage, leading to more Life Taps needed during boss fights, which interrupts the damage rotation. Consequently, Fire Destruction's performance in boss fights is not as good as Dark Destruction's.

Fire Destruction performs fairly well on trash mobs; from my Naxxramas (NAXX) and Ahn'Qiraj (TAQ) runs, it is indeed stronger than Dark Destruction. Firstly, Goblin Bombs benefit from fire damage bonuses, and secondly, Immolate and Searing Pain do not have travel time, avoiding the awkward situation where a Shadow Bolt is still in the air when the target dies.

Although Fire Destruction's AOE performance is still not as strong as that of Mages and Discipline Priests, it has slightly improved compared to the previous version.

Since the number of trash mobs in a raid is always greater than the number of bosses, this leads to an interesting result: Fire Destruction's overall damage is currently the highest among all Warlock talent builds.

Affliction&Demonology

I don't even know why I tested this talent; the actual experience was quite painful for me, so I discussed both together.

To directly state the conclusion: what Affliction can do, Shadow Destruction can also do (specifically, multi-target), and Shadow Destruction even performs better. Although it's not as complex as Affliction Warlock in the official servers, achieving decent damage output requires strong gear and addon support.

The advantage is that it doesn't rely on critical strike and hit, but this also leads to a very low ceiling. Additionally, the gear required for Affliction and Destruction are extremes; the former needs high haste and damage, while the latter requires high critical strike under the condition of ensuring hit. In other words, wearing Affliction gear to play Destruction is suicidal, and vice versa.

This leads to another interesting result: although Affliction spells have talents to reduce resistance, Shadow Bolt does not benefit from this talent effect. It's foreseeable that fully equipped Affliction Warlocks will find their Shadow Bolts mostly resisted.

The drawback is that since DOTs in Turtle WoW do not crit and the benefits from haste are far less direct than those from crit, the performance is extremely poor given the current scarcity of haste gear. Moreover, as a filler skill, Life Tap consumes a lot of mana. Despite the high shadow damage support, Affliction suffers from more frequent mana issues and interruptions in its rotation compared to Fire Destruction in boss fights.

Additionally, I have to criticize the inclusion of so many channeled spells for Affliction. The new skill, Dark Harvest, lasts 8 seconds, and Drain Soul lasts 6 seconds, with very low initial damage.

Now, consider the following scenarios:
Q: If Nightfall procs while channeling Drain Soul, should you interrupt Drain Soul to cast Shadow Bolt, or patiently wait for the Drain Soul channel to complete?

Think that scenario is too simple? Let's up the difficulty.
Q: If Nightfall procs while channeling Dark Harvest, should you interrupt Dark Harvest to cast Shadow Bolt, or just watch helplessly as the Nightfall effect expires?

Now for some blood pressure-raising scenarios:
Anubrekhan: While channeling Dark Harvest, the boss knocks you into the air.
Grand Widow Faerlina: While channeling Dark Harvest, a Rain of Fire appears above your head.
Maexxna: While channeling Dark Harvest, you're knocked against the wall.
ENJOY!

So I gritted my teeth and finished the entire NAXX, feeling like I was constantly refreshing DOTs, monitoring, moving, and leaking mana quickly. It's not just one DOT, but three that I had to maintain. Siphon Life seems useless? Not at all, I need to keep this DOT to maintain the damage boost from the new fifth-tier talent!

Even though I haven't acquired enough haste gear, I can already foresee the outcome of having higher haste in the future: DOTs will tick faster, and you'll find it nearly impossible to find a suitable moment to channel an 8-second spell.

As for Demonology, it's just three words: they don't survive.

There is a widely circulated image of a deep-demonology Imp using BWL trinket, with each Firebolt hitting for 1000+ damage, like a machine gun. My suggestion is to just look at it and not take it seriously. You can try to see how long an Imp in attack mode can survive in the current various AOE environments. And high-level demons cause more trouble for the team than the benefits they bring.

====================================================

In summary, I appreciate the changes Turtle WoW has made for Warlocks. They have slightly addressed the threat issue, resolved the Soul Shard problem, and developed new playstyles for Affliction and Fire Destruction.

However, the shortcomings of Warlocks in raids have become more pronounced (to be precise, it's not that Warlocks have more shortcomings, but that other classes have become more excellent). If this continues, we are not far from becoming mere utility providers with Summon Stones. Based on my complete runs of Naxxramas (NAXX) and Ahn'Qiraj (TAQ) with all three specs, and combining the feedback from a few Warlock friends, I propose the following modifications:

First, regarding Shadow Destruction: if there are no plans to further modify the triggering method of Improved Shadow Bolt, please consider increasing the shadow damage of the sixth-tier Affliction talent. The current version's Shadow Destruction Warlock output is lower than the previous version, and I can't find a reason for such a nerf.

Second, for Fire Destruction: the current filler skills for Fire Destruction are quite weak. Please consider adding new ranks for Searing Pain. The MISS penalty for Soul Fire is too harsh; please consider changing its triggering method.

The Hellfire damage reduction effect of the Stone of Wrath successfully gives Warlocks a caster taunt, but please consider transferring this effect to the Firestone or Felstone.

Finally, what I personally believe to be the biggest issue is Affliction & Demonology. Demons severely lack survivability in the current environment; please consider allowing the Imp's Phase Shift to be usable in combat.

Perhaps the designers wanted to convey 'Don't attempt to control forces beyond your grasp (referring to demons),' which made the high-level demon skills so unstable. Then why should I try to use uncontrollable powers in high-risk raids? Or maybe I was wrong from the start, and this talent was never designed for PVE.

The mana consumption of Drain Soul is too high, making it the lowest mana-to-damage ratio skill among all caster classes (i.e., how much damage can be dealt per point of mana). Additionally, the gradual damage increase logic of this skill seems somewhat meaningless since this is a channeled filler skill lasting 6 seconds, during which the caster can do nothing else. The last few seconds especially require concentration, and you have to pray that the boss's skills do not affect you.

If you want to use this skill to replenish Soul Shards, it becomes unsuitable due to its high mana cost, thus you can only use lower ranks of Drain Soul. It is recommended to add: 'If the target dies during the Drain process, X mana is refunded / When used on a target with less than X health, mana cost is reduced by X.'

Similarly, the incremental damage of Curse of Agony does make it more fitting to its skill description, but it also makes this curse perform poorly on trash mobs.

The new skill, Dark Harvest, has too long a channeling time and cooldown, making it rarely suitable for use in the current raids. It is recommended to completely rework this skill.

Regarding Nightfall, please increase the duration of the Nightfall effect and give Nightfall a 2-charge capacity. Too many Nightfall procs have been lost for various reasons, resulting in significant output loss. If Nightfall is talented, please allow the triggered Shadow Bolt to benefit from the first-tier Affliction talent effect.

Currently, Affliction lacks burst capabilities, and the benefit curve from haste is not excellent. Instead, the reduced DOT duration due to haste leads to a continuous cycle of refreshing DOTs, resulting in a random output rhythm. These changes have not made Affliction players excellent sustained damage dealers, but rather make them feel like immobile and awkward standing targets.

Lastly, thank you to all the experts who have patiently read this far. May the Fel energies guide your path.
