# Give Each Hunter Spec Its Own Identity!

## Post 157457 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-28T08:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157457#p157457 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey fellow hunters and Turtle devs,
I’ve been playing hunter for a while now and I think it’s time to talk about something that could really make the class more fun, flavorful, and balanced — giving each hunter specialization a stronger identity.

![🎯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3af.svg) Marksmanship — The Lone Wolf Archer
Sometimes, your pet feels more like a burden than a companion. For a spec that focuses on precision, strategy, and ranged mastery, it would make perfect sense to have an option to play without a pet — maybe a talent that dismisses the pet in exchange for extra ranged damage or utility.
This would open up a more tactical playstyle and fit perfectly with the fantasy of a marksman who fights alone with skill and focus.

![🐺](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f43a.svg) Beast Mastery — The True Beast Lord
On the other hand, Beast Mastery feels great while leveling but kind of loses its place in endgame content. It would be amazing to see this spec shine through a pet-focused rework, with more synergy between hunter and pet and maybe even the ability to tame exotic beasts (spirit beasts, special skins, or unique abilities).
Make BM the spec that truly embodies the bond between hunter and beast — a real master of the wilds, not just a buffed-up leveling build.

Why This Matters
These changes wouldn’t break the Vanilla feel — they’d enhance it. Turtle WoW has done a fantastic job of adding depth to existing classes, and hunters could really benefit from this kind of design attention.
Each spec would then have a clear fantasy and role:
MM: master archer, independent and precise.
BM: loyal beastmaster, commanding powerful creatures.
Survival: trap expert and melee ranger.
It’s a small change in philosophy, but it could make the hunter class feel fresh, flavorful, and more rewarding to play across all stages of the game.
What do you all think? Would you play a petless Marksman or a Beast Master with exotic companions?

I agree with MM.
WoW covers fantasy archetypes kinda well (with the exclusion of martial artists which get later covered by monks).
But still, one of such archetypes is the ranger, the Legolas kind. You wanna deal damage from a distance without using magic, a totally common trope.

Lone wolf would help there.

BM is a bit dangerous.
The more viable you make the pets, the more oppressive BM hunter gets in PVP, unless you introduce some kind of damage sharing between the pet and the hunter which would fundamentally change the class.
If we just assume PVP will stay balanced, I could imagine a BM class with a strong beast and an easy raid rotation. There is nothing wrong with easy classes, many players had been playing freeze mage before CC2, too.
An alternative I see is a support-DPS route. Imagine a party buff being added to each pet (like striders at the moment) and a BM talent turning those buffs from niche into valid raid buffs.
BM hunter could be the ultimate swiss knife of raid comp, bringing a different pet/party buff based on which team he is assigned to.

Where I'd want to see more pet+hunter combo attacks is survival.
MM fights and has a pet. BM let's the pet fight for him. Survival is the melee guy fighting alongside the pet. Would fit them the most and the pet+character combo attacks would make them feel unique compared to other melee.
