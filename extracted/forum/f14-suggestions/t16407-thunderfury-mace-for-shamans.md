# Thunderfury Mace for Shamans

## Post 111846 by Neechy (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-09T02:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111846#p111846 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

No, not the actual Thunderfury.

I accept the L that shamans don't get access to it (..which is why we should get Scythe of Elune, but that's [another topic](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16266)).

So what am I proposing here?

An EPIC MACE version of Thunderfury, obtainable via lvl 60 shaman class quest. Think how paladins gets Verigan's Fist quest early on, or how Priests and Hunters get lvl 60 class weapons via Molten Core.

Let's face it, the whole design of Thunderfury always screamed shaman weapon - but of course it is a sword, so shamans could not use it. Thus, I give you:

Example:
Sham'Rok, Hammer of the Storm Lord
Item Level 75
Binds when picked up
Unique
Main Hand	 ......................................... Mace
 42 - 110 Damage ..................... Speed 1.90
+ 16 - 30 Nature Damage
(52.1 damage per second)
Durability 100 / 100
Classes: Shaman
Requires Level 60
Chance on hit: Blasts your enemy with lightning, dealing 150 Nature damage and then jumping to additional nearby enemies. Each jump reduces that victim's Nature resistance by 15. Affects 3 targets. Your primary target is also consumed by a cyclone, slowing its attack speed by 10% for 12 sec.

What this accomplishes:
 -Thunderfury-themed weapon that satisfies shaman class fantasy. Of course this is not a legendary so I have reduced its dps slightly, proc considerably, and removed stat bonuses entirely
 -Allows for comparable tanking mechanics as with TF, but just considerably weaker. Not intended to stay viable as long as TF (however, would be cool to see the proc scale with shaman spellpower to extend its use with certain builds)
 -Opportunity for a long and challenging lore-friendly shaman questline
 -Paladin and Warrior tanks don't have to cry, because they can still get their legendary Thunderfury which is considerably more powerful and should [remain viable indefinitely](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16266).

Vanilla blizz team probably would have added epic lvl 60 weapons for each class as they did for Priests and Hunters in MC, but they ran out of time to implement. I would love it if TWoW could add comparable MC-level weapons for all classes, and so this would be one possibility for shamans.
