# Remove Consumes from BG

## Post 51712 by Shoezvengeance — 2023-06-28T22:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51712#p51712 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Negates the entire point of most classes having a weakness.
Better option
 - Have pvp vendors sell alternate versions of commonly used consumes as shorter duration variants.
 - Limit stacks to 5 as per HP and mana pots
 - Put all on shared CD timer.
 - Trinkets and craftables should remain as is.

Encourages players to have to make a decision and expands accessibility to all pvp players and would make bloodring not such a meme fest.

I prefer arena-esque bg mindset, you have what you have and you have to play your class effectively, not buy your way out of a classes weaknesses, just my thoughts. Interested in others opinions.

I think buying yourself out of a weakness is kind of the Vanilla way? Flasks, special consumables (blasted lands, zanza, etc) in raids are pretty much the same thing. Formerly also world buffs, Felwood plants, etc. You can farm MC for 4 months until everyone is geared in T1 and Thorium Brotherhood craftable fire resistance gear before you naturally take down Ragnaros. Or you can flask up, blow Greater Fire Resistance pots and take him down in the first week (if you can get douses).
Some people have their focus on raiding, others on PvP.

Personally, I play PvP very casually and I wouldn't dream of expending consumables on a game mode with an average respawn timer of 15s. If I see my BG opponent use a health pot I laugh at them and declare myself the victor of the encounter, regardless of whether I end up dying. If you want to turn PvP into a job you have to go farm money or consumables for - knock yourself out. I think it's pathetic, but I can totally see how it comes naturally for someone with their main focus on PvP. They probably just play it as "seriously" as hardcore raiders take raid prep and consumables.

tl;dr: Whatever. I don't use 'em, but I can see why someone else would. They fit with the Vanilla mindset.
