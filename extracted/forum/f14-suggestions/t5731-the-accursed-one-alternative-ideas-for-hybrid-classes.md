# The Accursed One. Alternative Ideas for Hybrid Classes.

## Post 35935 by Ibux (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-24T13:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35935#p35935 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I got some ideas that i wanted to share. Maybe someone here can help develop them into something cool.
The ideas are that you lose the hybrid nature of your class. where in one of your quests you will be cursed.

As the feral Druid or Enhancement shaman you  are cursed to be one animal forever. Feral cat or Ghost wolf.

You become more wild. Which ends up making you stronger and faster. But you can never heal again. Never shapeshift again. Never use crafting again. You lose your hybridity, but gaining back the hybrid tax.

The paladin may have been cursed with a blood curse. which means that when he attacks with skills he loses life. Such as with [Seal of Blood](https://www.wowhead.com/tbc/spell=31892/seal-of-blood) in The Burning Crusade expansion.

This could be a new way to balance hybrid classes. or just something unique and fun.
