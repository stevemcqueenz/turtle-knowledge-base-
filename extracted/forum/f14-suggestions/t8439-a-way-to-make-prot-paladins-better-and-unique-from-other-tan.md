# A way to make prot paladins better AND unique from other tanks

## Post 54388 by Bonelord — 2023-07-23T20:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54388#p54388 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The goal here is to make prot pally a more unique tank, different from warrior and druid as they already have their own strengths/weakness. Paladins are not great at mitigation, not great at having a health pool, not great at having armor, and not great at generating threat. But, I think we could make them quite a bit better at threat generation via 1h+shield with a few relatively simple tweaks.

 1. Restrict Crusader Strike from proccing windfury with one or two handed weapons unless talent in two handed specialization(the talent). This skill is what pushes us into a "casino" playstyle even outside of Seal of Command, wherein we generate 20%-60% more threat/dmg because of Crusader Strike spam. Honestly, leave this for Retribution by letting two handers regain the function with the three points they put in 2H spec and leave them to their fun. I don't play ret nor know enough beyond the fact that one of the best paladin tanks foregoes the prot tree and a 1h+shield simply because threat generation in the casino 2h style is much, much better. This would have the effect of most likely making faster, high spellpower weapons optimal as things like MSA are currently desired for high top end holy strike hits that also benefit greatly from spamming Crusader Strike for extra windfury procs.
 2. Change redoubt so that it has the old effect in addition to the current one. It should read as so: Successful melee and ranged attacks against you have a 10% chance to increase your chance to block by 30%. This effect has a a 100% chance when you are the victim of a critical strike. Lasts 10 sec or 5 charges.

 3. Improve Holy Shield by increasing the threat generation and threat coefficient. The math for this ability makes it rather poor when it could and should be a major part of the prot paladin's capability. A 5% spell coefficient being increased won't affect PvP as in the following example: 500 spellpower with 20% coefficient sees a roughly 100 dmg increase rather than under 30 on max rank holy shield, translating to 250-260 from 190. A reasonable increase to the 20% additional threat would also go a massive way to assist in this endeavor. There is also a precedent for better scaling on the paladin spells as their base numbers were quite low, seen in JoR and consecrate changes from turtle devs prior.

 4. Make Righteous Fury a more effective "tank" buff by combining it with the old Seal of Fury. Make it so YOUR judged enemy receives additional threat while using a one hander and add the "phantom swing" component that functionally is similar to what many paladins do with Fiery Plate Gauntlets/Storm Gauntlets/Incendosaur/etc.
 5. The most radical idea and one that probably needs the most tweaking/adjusting: Make reckoning a tanking talent for one handers. Switch it's location with one handed specialization in the talent tree and change it's functionality. "When blocking an attack from your judged target, counterattack with a one handed weapon." This could function the same as Pauldron of Deflection, with the list of caveats seen below(taken from someone else, hence wording):

it can proc from any direction (you dont have to face the target).
its damage does scale off your wep
it can proc weapon procs like dreams herald and lifestealing
it CANNOT proc on hit damage ie fiery plate gauntlets
it can proc while casting
i cannot confirm nor deny if it can proc while bubbled
it cannot proc seals
i had one miss so presumably it can be blocked/dodged/parried aswell

These restrictions could come with an CD and could be altered to reflect tanking better(IE: must face target, doesn't proc while casting/bubbled, can proc seals).

Hope you enjoyed my long post.
