# Weapon procs should have spell power scaling.

## Post 152414 by Greziz77 — 2025-09-19T20:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152414#p152414 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I originally posted about this in bugs but was told it should be in suggestions. I think it is a bug that these things are not getting spellpower applied to them like they were in classic.

Describe the Issue: Weapon procs like fiery and things like alcors sunrazor produce fire dmg procs and should be gaining a spellpower coeffecient like in classic. You will notice the majority of spellpower items say increase damage done by spells and effects.

How is it supposed to work: I do believe back in classic most of the procs had a .25-.50 coeffecient some of the big ones like wraith scythe life steal actually had 100% spellpower coefficient.

Character the bug occurred on: To be fair I only tested this with meteor shard as I intend to make a spellpower rogue but I needed to test to see if procs were gaining any coeffecients so this could just be that the meteor shard has no coeffecient.
