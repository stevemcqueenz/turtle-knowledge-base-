# True Band of Sulfuras

## Post 124884 by Cheruscan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-04-01T10:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124884#p124884 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greeting Turtles and Turtledevs,

I play paladin and have a Holy set for healing and havent been lucky at all with healing rings so I settled for the Band of Sulfuras as part of this set. It has quite nice stats especially for paladin with its whopping **23 Intelligence** boosting my spell crit which is a very powerful stat for Holy Pala.

When I red the patchnotes I got excited

> Band of Sulfuras: Can now be molded into its true form by using the ring with a Sulfuron Ingot next to the Black Forge. Doing so will turn it into the True Band of Sulfuras with improved stats and a new effect.

Improved Stats? Even more Intelligence!
Now I did anticipate that the "new effect" will probably not be smth that helps me healing.

I just did "Upgrade" my ring only to find that the intelligence stat is now nerfed by 10 points, the added haste isnt a useless stat and some extra spellpower is also ok, but overall it doesnt make up for that loss in intelligence.

The upgraded version is certainly quite an improvement for casters. Not for me though and I feel quite a few healers will have the same negative experience. There are barely any healing rings in the game as it is and so players tend to substitute them with caster rings, since there are plenty of those especially from raid quests.

Can I possibly revert my ring back? I already wrote a ticket and the GM told me to take my issue to the forum.

On another note, I would suggest just adding more intelligence to the upgraded ring and in turn remove/reduce the stamina on it, maybe you could even make it a healer/caster item by including healing spells to trigger the new effect.
