I think it depends on a lot of factors, for the most part world rares ARE soloable and intended to be by blizzard. Some classes can do it easily, others require a bit of preparation, long cds and consumables, and some can cheese it with pathing or kiting but it's always possible even for warriors, they have 30 minute cooldowns for it. Elite quests are a bit different, they were never intended to be soloable. Nerfing the elite mobs imo will make the classes that are already good at it much better, while barely helping the ones that struggle.
