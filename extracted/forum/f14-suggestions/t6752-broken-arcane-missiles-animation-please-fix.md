# Broken Arcane Missiles Animation | Please Fix

## Post 42545 by Schryonz — 2023-04-22T08:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42545#p42545 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello! I'm a Mage main and I've been playing Arcane Mages in Classic WoW private servers for a long time now.

I would like to ask the Turtle WoW Devs to alter something from the Spell, Arcane Missiles. I notice that the spell's animation, that each of the missiles launched, can be very speedy and won't look very satisfying. With each hit of the spell, the damage may come first before the missiles reach the target, and with that, the missiles will fly off too fast and won't be too pleasing to see.

However, there is something I have noticed while playing my mage on Classic Vanilla, like Turtle-WoW. When my Mage is floating on water, in stationary swimming position, the animation of the Arcane Missiles spell looks very good; It looks satisfying to see, and that is what I want the spell to have something altered. I would like for the Arcane Missiles animation to look as if when channeled in deep water.

The broken animation will always occur if the spell was cast on a moving target. If the target is stationary, like a training dummy, the broken animation would occur less, though the speed of the missiles of each spell cast would differ from normal, to faster, to very fast. The spell animation would look normal if I cast a different spell first, like Frostbolt, then follow it quickly with the Arcane Missiles.

Please, consider this suggestion, and do something about the Animation of the Spell. I would be very happy, and I would play on this server more often.
