# Petition thread to revert AV weapon quest

## Post 114802 by Snoodydood (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-29T23:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114802#p114802 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The quests Hero of the frostwolf/hero of the stormpike were changed a while ago from their regular vanilla state to their very early vanilla sate to go from simply winning one AV to having to kill a boss in the middle of the map and I still don't understand the point of this change.
The quest and it's rewards were always kind of a staple of vanilla for leveling into your early 50's and provide a decent weapon for various classes before getting nice pre-raid weapon later at 60. So why was this changed here in the first place? It's honestly difficult just to find people willing to complete it as most people actually able to finish it would be 60 anyway, so whats the point?
it also doesn't stand to help PvP in any way since it's a PvE quest anyway so I just don't see the point in taking away the ease of access. The quest was meant to give people a decent piece of gear while leveling while introducing them to AV but since the boss required to finish the quest needs higher level players there's no point anymore.
I think most people disagreed with this change so I would like people to respond to this thread to try and support the idea to change it back to it's vanilla state if twow devs are willing to listen.
