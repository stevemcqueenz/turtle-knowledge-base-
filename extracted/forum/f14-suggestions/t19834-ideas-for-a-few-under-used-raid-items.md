# Ideas for a few under-used raid items

## Post 135437 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-12T08:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135437#p135437 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In several raids, there are items that are just considered bad. Even in a progressing guild, no one ever wants them except maybe for tmog. I know it's the item dev's policy that there should be some "bad" items, but some of these could stand to be a little less awful, at least good enough or unique enough to give some pause on whether or not someone would want to try it out. This is not meant to be a comprehensive list of terrible items that no one wants, only some that stood out to me for various reasons.

The first of these are four mp5 items: Pendant of the Fallen Dragon, Digested Hand of Power, Manastorm Leggings, and Shimmering Geta

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/EFTBRoG.png)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/fWAu7xq.png)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/OGT3aVz.png)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/c6phl6W.png)

They all have the same issue: their stats are only stam, int, and mp5. Each of these stats on their own isn't inherently bad, but the spread means only a healer would consider them, but the regeneration and int is simply way too low to compete with just having better heals, especially since all of these slots have easier to acquire and/or better items in the same raids or earlier in progression. As an example, Padre's Trousers, a DM:W pre-raid option, trade 14 stam and 8mp5 for 6 MORE int and 42 healing than Manastorm Leggings, an MC boss drop. It takes almost a minute for the mp5 from Manastorm Leggings to catch up to the added int and your heals are significantly weaker the entire time and after.

I'd propose, then, to swap out stam for spirit 1-for-1 and mp5 for meditation. It won't make these items GREAT, but if they're designed for mana generation then they should actually generate some decent mana.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/6KDIGJy.png)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/oWp4x48.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/0pCuAKg.png)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Whbnb2m.png)

The next item I wanted to look at is Magma Tempered Boots. They're a plate healing item with worse healing than several pre-raid options, less int, less stam, spirit that's useless for paladin healers, and 8 fire res which does next to nothing. They're just awful.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/VaTfxsb.png)

After getting a bit of early feedback, there's two ways I decided to go about changing this item, and they're both a little more extreme than above. One is to turn them into a set of paladin fire resist tanking boots, an item space that isn't really in the game. There's Dark Iron, sure, but that's just random stats and fire res, no SP, so this change might appeal to the pinks more.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/SYRuml0.png)

The other approach is to make them a "more extreme" healing piece. Removing a lot of stats in favor of raw healing and mana, enough int to give half a spell crit as well, might just make these a viable choice for new paladin healers.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/YT860My.png)

So those are my ideas for now. Let me know what you think and I'll look into a few more later that could use a change.
