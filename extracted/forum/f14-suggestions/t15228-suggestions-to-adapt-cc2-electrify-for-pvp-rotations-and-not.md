# Suggestions to adapt CC2 Electrify for pvp rotations (and not just pve)

## Post 103445 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-10-17T17:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103445#p103445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As with many other of the upcoming class changes, Electrify seems to have been tested and designed with primarily pve rotations in mind, foregoing common pvp situations, with the spells adding Electrify stacks only being lightning bolt and chain lightning. In a pvp situation for elemental shamans however, there are often many spells flying inbetween the fifth and sixth LB/CL.

I suggest you expand the stacking spells to include nature spells, not just LB/CL. This way Earth Shock would add stacks aswell, and it would have minimal impact in pve rotations since to my knowledge ES is not used overly much in pve (giving high threat).

Alternatively, I suggest you buff the stacks to give 25% or 33% more damage instead of the current 20%. The risk is that you will have Ele shamans slacking on their group utility spells (shocks, purge, heal etc) in favor of trying to build Electrify stacks.
