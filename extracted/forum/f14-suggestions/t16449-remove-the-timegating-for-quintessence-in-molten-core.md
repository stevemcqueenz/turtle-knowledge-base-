# Remove the timegating for Quintessence in Molten Core

## Post 112229 by Shellius — 2024-12-11T12:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112229#p112229 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The reason the douse rep grind was added in original classic was to give the developers more time to finish BWL. The only purpose douse serves now is a point of contention when alt characters or new players realize they are locked to a raid missing the final boss. Here is a chain of events I have experienced personally:

Pug group fills, raid begins
Lucifron is cleared
Someone asks "who has douse btw"?
Turns out everyone is either an alt or a new player
cartoonishly_dotted_outline_of_missing_douse.gif
Drama ensues, people leave, raid disbands

My suggestion is, remove the rep requirement for the quest chain so that it may be continued without interruption, compact the quest "The Molten Core" and "Hands of the Enemy" into one so that it may be completed seamlessly during the progression of a single raid, and finally, hydraxis gives you this item upon taking the quest:
"Hydraxis's Watery Beacon"
Flavor text: "A shimmering, waterlogged bauble that pulses with elemental energy. Use it to call upon Duke Hydraxis in the heart of the Firelord's domain."

This summons Duke Hydraxis in a protective bubble and allows you to turn in the quest, upon which point he will give you the Aqual Quintessence to summon Ragnaros, without having to go all the way back to Azshara for the first Quintessence. You could still keep it so that successive Quintessences would have to be reaquired in Azshara, and upon reaching exalted, you get the Eternal Quintessence like normal. But the point is to not risk the gameplay interruption of not being able to summon Ragnaros as long as someone did the bare minimum of the getting the quest and completing the objectives within the raid.

Some might say that this will rob players who want to experience the original progression of Molten Core. My counter to this is that turtle wow is not an authentic vanilla experience to begin with. If they really wanted to raid Molten Core for a few weeks before they could access Ragnaros there would be other servers they could choose that would be better suited for that. Additonally, the server is so progressed that everyone simply takes it for granted that someone has douse within the raid, and it's usually the case. It is an outdated, net negative mechanic and I've always thought of turtle wow as the server that resolves many of the issues the original game had while keeping the experience intact. No other raid in classic has this form of timegating for the final boss, and it is strange that it should exist for the introductory raid.
