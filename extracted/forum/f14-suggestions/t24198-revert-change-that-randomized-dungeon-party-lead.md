## Post 170250 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-03-06T21:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170250#p170250 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It was made because some tanks exploited dungeon finder. Tanks knew their role had often instant queue on every dungeon. So they optimized this tool to instantly kick out melee dps who would compete against that person for melee and tank oriented loot. Low levels were also kicked out sometimes.

So if you were melee dps, you could wait for 2 hours for one specific dungeon only to be instantly kicked out from the group after it was formed. Then, you would have to wait other 2 hours... you see why it was frustrating for some people. BTW it's better to form a group via world chat, it's way faster and people play better.

Now, leader is randomly selected. The only downside is that tank can no longer mark targets.
