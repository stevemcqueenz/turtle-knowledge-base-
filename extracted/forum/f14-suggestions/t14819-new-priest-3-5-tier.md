## Post 100970 by Calevarn — 2024-09-09T20:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100970#p100970 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I believe the main criticism of this set should really be how off-theme it is. It's too concerned with being a plague doctor rather than looking like an actual priest.

Ostensibly, that's the class it's meant to represent.

There's nothing remotely connecting a plague doctor to the priest fantasy, if not by the broadest simplification that "they heal".

Now, I implore you to ponder that idea for a minute. If "healing" is all it takes to relate a class to being a doctor, I am not sure what separates any other healing class from being portrayed as surgeons, nurses and veterinarians.

I find it curious how some seem to acknowledge how poorly suited to priests this set is, only to continue by saying "it would be good for shadow priests".

And again, how? Because the colour scheme is dark? What do plague doctors have to do with shadow-wielding cultists adept at inflicting mental anguish on their foes?

Barring one spell that has the word plague in it (which by the same reasoning could have priests be vampires, for the matter) there's just no dots to connect.

An evil brain surgeon would be closer, perhaps, and even then still be miles away from being a priest.

My suggestion would be to open this set up to any cloth-wearing class and maybe develop something more priestly to dedicate to the priests, because being a plague doctor is a nice concept - just not a good basis for a priest-exclusive set.
