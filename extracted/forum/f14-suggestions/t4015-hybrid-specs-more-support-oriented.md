Hard to judge. I like the theory, but I'm not sure that adding more buffs/debuffs would really create more satisfying gameplay (especially when dealing with the debuff limit). Based on some of the talents and gear, I actually suspect that some of the original design intent by Blizzard was that hybrid classes were meant to genuinely play as hybrids--i.e. sometimes the holy paladin needs to tank or the enhancement shaman heal--but given the design of encounters, the aggro system, and the gear, it was more effective for everybody to just go full specialist.

The hybrid characters would then be considered support: not just from buffs and debuffs (though they do have more of those than most classes) but because they could fill in whatever the team needed in the moment. i.e. extra healing during a damage spike or extra tanking when there are more adds.

I would have loved to see a design where hybrid characters actually played as hybrids, but I think they'd have to make too many other changes to make it feasible.
