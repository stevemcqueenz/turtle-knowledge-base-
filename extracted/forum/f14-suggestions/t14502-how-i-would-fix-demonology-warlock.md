# How I would fix Demonology Warlock

## Post 98948 by Phoenixphire (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-08T23:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98948#p98948 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello fellow enjoyers of Turtle WoW.

In light of the prospective upcoming class changes we know are coming eventually. I want to tackle the most underloved spec of from what I am often told is a very underplayed class.

Demonology.

Lets start with the class fantasy of Demonology. Obviously it involves summoning demons, and that's where I think the spec runs into immediate issues. The warlocks current pets mostly provide utility, slightly less so when leveling but some of the fastest leveling builds use their pets almost purely for utility. But I think just buffing warlocks pets would make them basically glorified hunters with casting and I think the 4 current demons fit nicely in their own little Niches.

The other class fantasy is sacrificing demons which I think the spec already does well and should be kept that way. I think the ability to dip into demonology to get Sac if desired is good and an interesting part of warlock's talent trees. But just like how Arcane mage was given at least SOMETHING to hang their hat on besides just being a support tree, I think Demonology needs that.

What is the solution then? I think there could be many.. and I know many warlock players could have their own opinions from small changes to big sweeping changes.

I want to stay more in line with what Twow has seem to be leaning them in the direction of and make it a bit more viable to use. Of course this is mostly from a PVE prospective as soul link is already good in PVP but I am taking PVP into account in these proposals.

The philosophy of these changes is assuming Twow's design ideas for going DEEP demo is to utilize the enslaved demons IE Inferal, Felguard and Doomguard.

If I were to suggest more "minorish changes" I would say the following:

I would add on to Master Demonologist that for "Enslaved Demons" "While soul link is active and the warlock is above 50% health, Enslave demon cannot end." This means that in PVE the enslaved demon is much more viable and a much smaller headache to a raid as well as providing counterplay in pvp, getting the warlock under half health will turn his demon into a ticking time bomb that can turn on him at any moment. Feeding into the fantasy of "Who is really in control here?" This may require tuning of the demons to prevent PVP balance issues but most locks going soul link I believe would choose the safety of a voidwalker and shield.

I would also add a class quest chain available around lvl 40 that provides another summonable enslaved demon to add access to this gameplay if desired for levelers and HC players. Maybe allowing them so solo summon it at the cost of like 50% of their health so it doesn't take other players but does have a downside. Options could be something like a Satyr or Grelkin (larger melee imp sporting a machete) these could be more glass cannon style demons that function like the Succubus but with more damage and no utility.

Possible abilities include:

Fiendish strike (low cooldown low mana melee ability)

Shadow lash (higher mana higher cooldown melee that consumes corruption of yours from the target and does a burst of shadow dmg based on the rank of corruption used making it a part physical part shadow dmg attack) Allowing the lock to try to time this at the end of corruption for maximum efficiency adding a bit of nuance to the gameplay.

Flaming Smite (higher mana higher cooldown melee that consumes immolate of yours from the target and adds a DoT effect based on the rank of immolate removed) again adding a bit to think about when using the ability.

These "Think a bit" abilities are an effort to not have them just become glorified BM hunters.

More crazier Ideas:

Adding an Eyes of the Beast style ability on a channel that can be interrupted that lets you control your enslaved demon yourself and increase its dmg by a percent. Maybe like 15-20%. Maybe even causing this channel if fully finished to reset the duration of enslave.

Increase the Dmg of enslaved demons further like making them benefit from the INT, HEALTH and DMG talents but make them give you down sides like infernal decreasing your resistances, felguard making you take more dmg, and doomguard increasing cast time.

Let me know what you folks think. I am a big advocate for less meta playstyles and specs and have played the game since it came out as well as playing Twow for a long time so if there are people who enjoy posts like this I could do them for other specs as well.   smiling_turtle_head
