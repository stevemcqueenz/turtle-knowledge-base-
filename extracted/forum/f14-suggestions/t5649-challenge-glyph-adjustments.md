# Challenge Glyph Adjustments

## Post 35199 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-19T02:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35199#p35199 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The addition of the challenge glyphs to Turtle is, in my opinion, a great addition. Slow And Steady gives more time to check out all the levelling content, makes gear upgrades feel more meaningful (in my opinion) since you'll be keeping it for longer, and even if you just dip it for ten or twenty levels is a great way to get your transmog going. Hardcore provides a challenge that completely changes how I approach the game. I did it with a class and content I wasn't familiar with, just as a way to teach myself to be a better player (and somehow I never get as frustrated starting over as I do running back to my corpse to try the thing that just killed me over immediately). The other two glyphs, though?

They have problems.

 **1)** Warmode giving +30% exp is a really good reward. Too good. It leads to a lot of people who aren't interested in (or straight-up *don't want*) PVP flagging themselves, just to level up faster. Even players doing Hardcore are tempted, despite it basically being a guaranteed lost run when they get found by a higher level player. It's not fun for the people getting killed when they didn't actually want to PVP, and it's not fun for the people who *do* want to PVP, and instead get people not fighting back and then complaining about it.

I know that Warmode giving bonus exp is based on the Blizzard version of it, but there's no reason Turtle has to do it the same way. In my opinion, Warmode should offer rewards that appeal to players who want to PVP in the first place, like increased Honor gains, or a discount on the cost of PVP reward items. It's not like it isn't still a disadvantage for PVP players to not be able to toggle it off. Also, not being tied to exp means a reason to stay in Warmode at Lv. 60.

 **2)** Glyph of Exhaustion, on the other hand, is just bad. It makes your weapon skills increase faster, in exchange for, essentially, 50% exp. Yay? Weapon skill is pretty easy to train normally, and even easier on Turtle with the ability to go smack a training dummy for skill. Not to mention the number classes that have no need to worry about being able to train weapon skill quickly in the first place. Hypothetically, I could see this *maybe* appealing to super impatient Warrior and Hunter players, except that it wouldn't, because of making levelling slower. The only reason I can think of to actually take this one is as a deliberate handicap...which can also be achieved by just not visiting tent parties.

Getting faction rep is a slow grind, and I've wanted a way to speed it up for some time. Well, hard workers get recognition, right? And who's exhausted all the time? People putting in some serious overtime. Flimsy logic, but I think that a noticeable boost to reputation gains would be a good exchange to get people to take Exhaustion. In essence, it's making one grind take longer, in exchange for making another shorter.

 **3)** I know what you're thinking. There's no more glyphs left, are there? Well, yes and no. I think I've covered the problems with the existing glyphs pretty well, but making those changes would introduce a new one. People are going to be upset if you just take away their exp bonus. So there still needs to be a +exp glyph that we can swap existing Warmode players over to. Maybe not necessarily even still 30% any more, so that it's not an automatic grab, but I'd personally be fine either way when it comes to that. As for the trade-off, I think there are a few options;

- Losing the bonus to reputation gains with your own race. *"Glyph of the Hermit"*
- Increased training costs. *"Glyph of Scholarship"*
- Increased durability loss. *"Glyph of Hard Knocks"*
...or perhaps, all three could be added, with each only giving a 10% bonus to exp, so that people have to think a bit more about how many penalties they're willing to stack up for faster levelling.
