# Viable Gear for Hybrid Classes

## Post 29897 by Neloth — 2022-11-27T01:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29897#p29897 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Some of the hybrid classes Tier Sets (up to Tier 3 at least) have some weird itemization, leading to them not being as good as many other, better itemized epics (or even blues). Some other pieces, on the other hand, are well enough itemized, but only for a specific role, like healing or tanking.

This is certainly immersion breaking, everyone wants to use that cool looking T1 or T2 set, yet most classes/roles won't get to. An idea to fix this problem, without making excessive and/or dramatic changes, would be the addition of quests (like the tier 3 quests) or vendors, that allow to swap the "Normal Tier Set" with another one, tailored to a specific role. For example, paladins could swap the "Normal T2 Redemption Set", which is great for Retribution, for a Tanking or Healing itemized version of it, the same could be done for the Druids, finally giving those classes the occasion to acquire decent Tier Gear, instead of hunting for other decently itemized pieces all over the place.

In order not to make it non-abusable, the quest could be made non-repeatable, or the vendor could be allowed to trade the Tier Pieces with an added cost of either Gold or Tokens.

There's no point in changing the talents and giving the classes new abilities if there's no gear available to support those changes.

Better yet would be a system like the one used in TBC and forward, having the bosses drop only tokens, that can then be redeemed for Tier Gear.
