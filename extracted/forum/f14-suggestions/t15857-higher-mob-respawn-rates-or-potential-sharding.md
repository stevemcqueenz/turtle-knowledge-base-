# Higher Mob Respawn Rates or Potential Sharding

## Post 108037 by Andant — 2024-11-15T15:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108037#p108037 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Title says it all, I've leveled on a lot of vanilla servers and I do LOVE this server but most of my time spent while leveling is waiting on mobs re-spawning rather than actually questing. Even in the 40s I feel like there are 10 people per quest fighting over the same exact mob.

I'll stick it out but it is hard to convince my friends to continue playing when they have same exact experience. They want to love the server but when you're sitting around waiting on stuff to respawn fighting with HCs or other people who wont group for tags it sucks all the fun out of it. If my friends don't manage to stick it out because they feel like their time is being wasted I don't see how much longer I'd be able to play even though I desperately want to.

Success is a great thing in the vanilla server community but the leveling experience seems to suffer for it without it being addressed and changed. I can't imagine how bad open-world gold farming feels at 60 as I'm not even there yet. I don't mind the occasional 3-4 quests per zone waiting on mob respawns but it feels like every single kill quest is an absolute slog.

Tents somewhat fix this with boosted XP rates 24/7 but it isn't enough to counterbalance the time wasted and doesn't make sitting around waiting on respawns feel any better. I just want to be able to play the game more with what limited time I do have to play.

Devs you do a fantastic job with just about everything here and I applaud you for doing what most see as a thankless job, but please consider finding a solution to fix this problem that works for your vision for Turtle-WoW and the community alike. The open world solo questing experience shouldn't feel so aggravating.

Thanks!
Maxenne/Andant
