# multiple-use chronoboon

## Post 95786 by Artificialowl — 2024-05-27T09:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95786#p95786 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Multiple facts makes the Chronoboon not that fun/useful and expensive:

 - no wb in raid,
 - purchaseable in WP,
 - single-use,
 - unique.

If WB were a thing in raid, I could understand the pain to use this item to stack and store your World Buff. However, the wb can only be used in outside and dungeon; why not having a more friendly Chronoboon ? And less expensive !

If you start using one Chronoboon each time you want to add a WB to your storage you need to get to Chromie, spend 5g, get back to your capital and wait for the buff. Once buffed, restore your previously stored buff, and use your Chronoboon, ... and get back to Chromie for the next one and spend 5g again.

Some solutions would be:

 - multiple use Chronoboon,
 - (or) not unique Chronoboon (the empty one), and slightly less expensive,
 - (or) infinite use Chronoboon, for a larger price (someone said /shop ?)
 - available in capitale (at a smaller price)
