# Removing Raid Scaling is a bad idea that will kill casual* raiding

## Post 69914 by Liljon — 2023-10-10T11:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69914#p69914 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Recently, in TWoW's Discord Support channel, it has been revealed that an undocumented change of pulling back raid scaling to 30 people and Emerald Sanctum having been released without scaling was deployed, and that they are eventually looking to completely get rid of it. Their reasoning behind this is that they'd like to promote the more social aspect of raiding now that their server is much larger, forcing people to bring 40 people into each raid.

For those unfamiliar, and in short, if you bring less people to a raid, the health and some other oddities of the monsters inside the raid would lower to a certain cap. In the case of 20man raids, this goes down to 12 people minimum, and in the case of 40man, this goes down to 20 minimum.

In my guild Atlantis (where I am the primary raid lead), depending on the days and irl circumstances, we raid with a fluctuating roster of anywhere between 25 to 40 - this change will kill any hope for us to bring in new people or be able to raid at all consistently and progress as we are rather casual about our raiding approach. And God help you if you are firing up a new fresh guild wanting to progress from MC -> Naxx.

This change is extremely outrageous, and even if you were to make this change regardless of what we say, the TWoW team should have absolutely given us at the very least a month's worth of notice because we would have to fundamentally restructure how we - and others - run our raids. I'm not sure if any MMO dev ever realizes this but the majority of MMO players are casuals because they can't be bothered to do stupid ass shit korean grinds just to be able to participate in raiding - you are sacrificing a large portion of your own audience with this change.

The "social" aspect of this change literally does not function as an argument as raids will go from a fun social time that can occur even if some people can't make it to a very rigid-structured raiding experience that has the possibility to not even be able to start raiding due to a lack of people. Just because our absolute unit of a bear tank can't make a few raids here n there shouldn't mean that we are now collectively punished for it, we should be able to adapt and enjoy the game just as much while also enjoying each other's company.

Scaled raids is one of TWoW's flagship features that makes raiding approachable and convenient. Please, don't take this away. There is no reason to shoot everybody in the foot.
