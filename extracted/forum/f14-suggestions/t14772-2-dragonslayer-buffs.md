# 2 Dragonslayer Buffs

## Post 100594 by Mistermadigan — 2024-09-04T09:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100594#p100594 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It always bothered me that Onyxia and Nefarian's heads give the same buff.  With World Buffs no longer being allowed in raids, I think there is space to have distinct buffs for each of the heads - just to help out the folks hanging about by the gates.

Onyxia's Head can still give "Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer"
"Increases critical chance of spells by 10%, melee and ranges by 5% and grants 140 attack power. 120 minute duration"

but Nefarian is a tricky and dastardly sort of dude, much different than his sister.  Defeating him should grant a unique bonus.

I propose that turning in Nefarian's head should grant - "Bolstering Shout of the Dragonslayer"
"Increases all stats by 20, +20 to all resistances, and +5% dodge chance.  120 minute duration"

I know it's basically just Mark of the Wild swapping armor for dodge, but I always felt it kinda dumb standing around in a city and one head drops after the other and it just resets the timer on the same buff.  But that's my take on it.
