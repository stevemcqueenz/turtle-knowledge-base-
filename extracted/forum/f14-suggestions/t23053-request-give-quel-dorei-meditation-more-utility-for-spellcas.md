# Request: Give Quel'Dorei meditation more utility for spellcasters

## Post 160837 by Familymaker — 2025-11-26T19:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160837#p160837 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So first of all, I absolutely love Quel'Dorei meditation for high elf Warriors and Rogues. It feels fair that you'd have to give up 1.5 seconds in a cast (so basically an auto attack) in order to gain a burst of rage or energy generation.

That said, while the 10% mana generation for high elf spellcasters seems fair compared to the racials of the other races, it does feel a little lackluster. As such, I wanted to suggest two ideas that might make it more interesting:

 1) Make it so that during the 5 second duration of Quel'Dorei meditation, non-instant spells that you cast will not lose casting time when you take damage and will also generate less threat. For when you are focussed down.

 2) Make it so that if you use Quel'Dorei meditation while out of combat, that the mana generated will be increased by 100% or even 150%. For when you are farming for mobs in PVE.

Either of these two suggestions, or perhaps even both, would give the racial a little more utility for spellcasting high elves. Right now, Turtle Wow mage FAQ even lists the high elves (who should be known for their magical talent) as a B tier race for mages due to their racial being a little underwhelming for spellcasters. And I feel that both these changes would make the racial more useful for raids and dungeons, and for PVE, without overtuning it.
