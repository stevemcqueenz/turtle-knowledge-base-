# New Class concept - Spellblade

## Post 150667 by Tyrelas — 2025-09-07T14:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150667#p150667 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

*Hello, I had this idea for a new class. It's just a concept and not fully fleshed out with abilities. I will might think this through if this suggestions gets any traction :D :P Anyways, I hope thise idea will intrigue you.*

 **Spellblade**

 **Flavour**: Somewhat ancient form of magic, that is considered inferior/primitive by "modern" scholars and mages, even though they inherited some spells passed down from generations of spellblade cultures.

 **Primary stats**: Intellect, Spirit

 **Armor type**: cloth
 **Weapons**: fist weapons, daggers, 1h swords, staves, polearms, thrown weapons, held-in-off-hands (NO SHIELD)

 **Races**:
[*] Horde:
[*] [*] **Trolls** - very ancient people (hexers and witch doctors)
[*] [*] **Taurens** - ancient to azeroth too, with deep tradition based on spirituality and harmony (spirit walkers)

[*] Alliance:
[*] [*] **Night elves** - very ancient people born from magic. Although arcane magic is shunned in their society after the War of the Ancients, spellblades are not seen as such, considering they were shunned by the same mages before. **High elves are NOT included** as they dropped spellblades in favour of paladins after their exodus.
[*] [*] **Dwarves** - masters of runecraft and imbuement. They utilized their runes and concepts from archeological discoveries.

 **Specs:**
 **Concentration** (Tank):
instead of using stamina as HP **he will use MANA** through Mana barrier (*Concentration* - as concentrating on holding the shield barrier up). *Intellect* will standardly increase his mana cap, and **Spirit** will increase this barrier potency (and/or armor) + his mana regen.

 **Infusion** (melee DPS):
Infusing their weapons with magic. They will gain melee AP from Spirit as it requires strong will to hold the infusion stable.

 **Imbuement** (ranged DPS):
Imbuing (temporary infusion that dissipate quickly) **thrown weapons** with extra punch and the **ability for them to return back** to the user for continual throwing. **Spirit** will increase their ranged AP slightly + they will need **agility** for that too. Thus they need Spirit + Agility for best potencial.

 *Authors notes: I hope you like the idea. It always bogged me how the thrown weapons were always useless, primarily used as stat boost more than anything. Let me know of what you think in the comments :) .*
