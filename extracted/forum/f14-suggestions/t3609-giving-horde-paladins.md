# Giving Horde Paladins

## Post 21803 by Phoenixphire (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-10T12:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21803#p21803 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yes, I believe that horde should get undead paladins (and ally shamans but this isn't that post). First let me start by saying thank you for reading this post, I hope it provokes a civil discussion and maybe change some minds. I have also been told the Twow devs have commented on this before and said that it isn't gonna happen, if so, I hope maybe I can make some good points on why it would be a good add for the game to maybe change their minds. Before we start NO 6 classes per race is not a limiting factor, modders have shown it can be worked around, not an argument against.

Point 1 Fun:
Number 1 point is it would be fun for the players. A new, custom made, undead paladin experience in the best vanilla plus server out there would draw a lot of interested players. Paladins are a well liked class by a lot of people both softcore and hardcore alike. With a lot of the new updates to the class it has become a lot more fun to play and being able to take those new skills and kit through the Horde questing experience would be an awesome experience. On top of all that for those that like RP (not a big RPer sorry) it opens many new doors for character expression (Fallen knight of the silver hand, ex scarlet crusader etc.) Lastly the awesome story to be told through class quests would be a great experience, the possibilities are so exciting. Coping with being an undead paladin in constant pain from your holy abilities and knowing that most other paladins see you as an abomination would make for riveting quest lines.

Point 2 the vanilla feel:
Originally blizzard wanted some classes to be a lot more restricted (best example is druids only being able to be male night elves). But they came to the conclusion that letting lore effect gameplay in a negative way is bad for the game. And I would also argue that on a server in which you can group with the other faction already you have mostly lost the "Alliance have paladins and horde have shamans feel." This would be a ground breaking step in the vanilla plus journey, and I think The Turtle WoW devs are the ones to do it and do it right. I completely understand if you disagree, but Twow is not vanilla wow and hasn't been for awhile, it is something better. new, fresh, interesting, and fun. I am a massive vanilla wow fan, but even as a big fan I can admit that trying TOO hard to stay vanilla isn't always a good thing. I don't want this to be retail, I don't think anyone does, but I think if Twow decided to do it they could do Undead Paladins with skill, grace, and care. And show some of the skeptics it wouldn't "ruin the vanilla feel."

Point 3 Lore:
This is where a lot of people go when they argue against undead paladins or horde paladins in general and I think its fairly bad faith. "Undead can't use the light." Well they do in game (Sir Zeliek is an easy example of risen paladin that uses the light). "The light hurts undead to use." Warlocks life tap themselves for mana all the time. "Paladins cannot die to the plague." But they can be risen by necromancers. My point is most points against undead paladins have counters, but if you already are against the idea you will never agree. Point is, there are points that can be made against most of the newly added race/class combos added to turtle wow, the real question is, is there enough points there to make lore that will make the race/class combo fit and will it be fun for the players. If yes? Then it is a good add.

Point 4 What makes Turtle/vanilla wow great:
In my opinion, a huge part of what made vanilla great and now makes Turtle WoW great is new experiences. Being able to experience wonder and mystery in vanilla again is a massive draw from Twow. This change falls right into that as a new experience for the veteran or fresh vanilla player to play through the game and experience new things. As can be seen on the population tool, some of the new class combos are very popular if not the most popular options for some races, showing that people love new experiences (Undead Hunters as an example). I bet if you asked people what brought them to Turtle WoW many would say the new custom content.

Conclusion:
While a controversial topic, I believe it would be a massive positive for the server. But if you don't like the idea I don't think I will ever be able to convince you, but I hope you think I made some good points. Once again this is my opinion, you are free to have your own, and if you disagree with me I'd love a civil comment to hear why. Lore has never been a limiting factor for Twow or blizzard, the vanilla feel is already different on Twow with cross faction play, and why not add something to the game that would be fun for so many? Thank you so much for reading if you got this far, at the end of the day I am just an avid Turtle WoW player that loves the server, and this was all just my personal opinion.
