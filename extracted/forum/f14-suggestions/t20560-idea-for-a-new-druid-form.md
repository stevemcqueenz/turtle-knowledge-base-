# Idea for a new Druid form

## Post 142127 by Allarielle — 2025-07-29T21:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142127#p142127 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Spider Form**
The idea behind this form is threefold. First, it is a nice way for Restoration Druids to be able to level up efficiently or quest in the wild without feeling like they need to respec. Second, it's a nice extra tool in the Feral toolkit that doesn't notably increase their power, but does reinforce the fantasy of the jack of all trades who has a form for everything. Third, it introduces a fun new gameplay and gearing path without significantly affecting the existing ones.

The main idea for the gameplay of this form is that you prepare the battlefield ahead of the fight and use your threads to control everything, while sitting back and quietly watching until you're ready to strike. Your auto attack damage is basically ignorable, so almost all of your damage comes from Energy based abilities, and with a very high Energy pool, you aren't really affected by just sitting there. To reinforce this, the Reflection talent actually makes it so your Energy regen in Spider Form increases if you haven't attacked or taken damage in a little while, and the Hide ability is basically impossible to use unless you're already at the very periphery of the fight. When you do decide to strike, though, you leave a poison DoT behind that easily makes up for all the waiting.

Some of the numbers might need to be adjusted. Some of them might need to be adjusted a whole lot. It's the sort of thing where you sort of need to get in there and actually play it to know what works and what doesn't haha.

---
 **Spider Form**
Learned from a quest at level 30.
 35% of base mana
Instant
Shapeshift into Spider Form, gaining increased Spell Power equal to your Agility and reducing all threat generated by 20%. Also protects the caster from Polymorph effects and allows the use of various spider abilities including Berserk, Nature's Grasp, and Nature's Swiftness. The act of shapeshifting frees the caster of Polymorph and Movement Impairing effects.
- Spider Form uses Energy and has a max energy of 300.
- Works with Furor, gaining the same amount of Energy as Cat Form.
- Does NOT gain the move speed bonus from Feral Swiftness, but does gain the dodge chance bonus.
- Gains full benefit from Predatory Strikes.
- Gains full benefit from Predatory Strikes.
- Gains full benefit from Feral Adrenaline.
- Gains full benefit from Leader of the Pack.
- Gains the energy regen bonus from Berserk.
- Gains increased Agility from Heart of the Wild.
- Gains an Energy regen benefit from Ancient Brutality based on Venom effects - 1/2 Energy per stack of Envenomed Bite plus 1/2 Energy from Slurp.
- Gains 30% increased Energy regeneration from Reflection if you have not attacked or taken damage in the last 5 sec.

---
 **Webbing**: Reduces the target's chance to hit by 5%, and reduces their movement speed by 5-20% based on the difference between your Attack Power and theirs. Movement, melee attacks, and damage taken all have a chance to remove this effect based on the difference between your Attack Power and theirs, but no more than 1 stack can be removed every 3 sec. Leaving Spider Form disconnects the webbing from your body. Stacks up to 5 times. Each stack lasts 10 sec. Targets affected by your Webbing remain in combat with you, even if they cannot see you.
- Appears in your spell book, but cannot be cast directly.
- Feral Instinct reduces the chance for enemies to detect the threads.
- Leaving form does not remove the Webbing effect, but it does 'disconnect' the Webbing effect. Disconnected webbing cannot be used together with other skills that interact with Webbing. Adding a new stack of Webbing to a disconnected stack, however, reconnects the full stack (provided they were both yours in the first place).
- Applying a new stack refreshes the duration of all stacks. However, if one stack is removed (such as from taking damage) or if its duration expires, the other stacks remain.
- Targets affected by Webbing cannot drop combat with you. This means that if you Hide while they are Swathed or Wrenched, they won't start to evade. Thus, your Hide>Envenomed Bite combo can still be used when soloing.
- Threads are detectable *and attackable*, so in PvP there's strong counterplay to prevent you from simply webbing up the entire field in stealth.

---
 **Hide**
Learned together with Spider Form
 30 Energy
 2 sec channel
Requires Spider Form
Attempts to hide the caster from view over 2 sec. Drains 30 Energy each second, and if there is not enough Energy or if any enemies within 20 yards are facing the caster, the effect is broken. After 2 sec the caster begins prowling, but with 40% reduced movement speed.
- Omen of Clarity only applies to the initial cost.
- Gains full benefit from Feral Instinct's prowl bonus.
- Gains up to a 50% reduction in the in-channel Energy drain from Tranquil Spirit (15 Energy drained every sec instead of 30).

---
 **Anchor Thread**
Learned together with Spider Form
 40 Energy
Instant
Requires Spider Form
Attaches a sticky thread at your current location. The next time you use Anchor Thread within 20 yards, the thread is extended to connect both locations. Enemies that pass through the thread gain 1 stack of Webbing.
- Gains up to a 100% chance to apply an additional stack of Webbing from Primal Fury.

---
 **Envenomed Bite**
Learned together with Spider Form
 100 Energy
Instant
Requires Spider Form
Bites the target with envenomed fangs, dealing [low] physical damage plus [moderate-high] Nature damage over 21 sec and increasing the damage of your Nature damage over time effects by 30%. Each tick deals increased damage proportional to the target's movement speed reduction. Stacks up to 3 times. Applying a new stack does not refresh the duration of existing ones unless used while prowling. Replacing an existing stack instantly deals Physical damage for up to 50% of the remaining duration, decreasing based on the amount of remaining time.
- Counts as a Poison effect.
- The physical damage portion scales with Attack Power and physical crit stats.
- The (much higher) Nature damage portion scales with Spell Power and magical crit stats.
- Gains up to 10 reduced Energy cost from Ferocity.
- Gains up to 20 reduced Energy cost from Tranquil Spirit.
- Gains up to 20% increased damage from Feral Aggression.
- Gains up to 20% increased damage from Gift of Nature.
- Gains up to 30% increased critical effect chance from Improved Regrowth (applies to both initial attack and ticks).
- Gains up to 9 sec increased duration from Preservation.
- Gains full benefit from Subtlety.
- Gains full benefit from Genesis.
- The nature damage increase also applies to itself, so one stack deals x * 1.3 damage, while 3 stacks deal 3x * 1.9 damage. It also applies to Insect Swarm and Entangling Roots.
- If used from stealth and refreshing the duration of multiple stacks, the instant damage effect applies to all stacks refreshed. This has the potential to be Big Damage.
- When the replacement damage effect happens, it deals physical damage scaling with your physical stats, but the physical damage it deals is based on the magical damage of the initial effect, which scaled with your magic stats. This results in double dipping, especially if you have a balance of both physical and magical stats.

---
 **Swathe**
Learned at level 36
 40 Energy
 8 sec channel
Attempts to swathe the target in Webbing, draining 24 Energy every 2 seconds for up to 8 seconds, reduced by 1 second for each stack of Webbing it has. While the target is being swathed and for 2 seconds after it breaks free, its movement speed is reduced by 50% and its attack speed and casting speed are both reduced by 30%. Once the swathing is complete, the effect becomes permanent and weak targets become unable to move, attack, or cast spells at all. The target has a 60% chance to break free every 3 seconds, increased by 3% with each chance, but reduced by up to 20% for each stack of Webbing it has. Effects which reduce casting time also reduce the channel duration.
- Omen of Clarity only applies to the initial cost.
- You can still cast it even if there are no stacks of Webbing, but it'll take 8 sec, and the target has a 60% chance to break free every 3 sec both during and after the channel. Luckily you still get 2 seconds of benefit even if the target breaks free immediately while you're still channelling.
- Can be used together with Nature's Swiftness to take effect immediately. This is still subject to the normal rules for breaking free, though, so if you do this before Wrapping you'll want to start Wrapping soon after.
- If you cast it on a target with 5 stacks of Webbing, the channel only takes 3 seconds, and the target has no chance of breaking free.
- Webbing lasts only 10 seconds per stack at maximum, however, so after 20 seconds of not applying Webbing, the target starts to have a chance to break free again.
- Webbing also has a fairly significant chance to break on damage taken, so if you and your party are attacking the Swathed target, it'll probably break free fairly quickly unless you're continually applying more Webbing.
- The chance to break free increases every 3 sec, so after 40 seconds the target has a chance, however slight, to break free even with 5 stacks of Webbing. The 60% + 3% every 3 sec is for the final rank of the spell. The first rank is 60% + 9% every 3 sec, so it gains a chance to break free after only 15 seconds.
- IMPORTANT NOTE: Envenomed Bite and Slurp both deal increased damage based on movement speed reduction. A fully Swathed target is unable to move, and therefore has a 100% reduction. However! The 'unable to move' effect doesn't necessarily replace any/all other movement speed reduction effects, including that of Swathe's own 50% that it has before, after, and DURING the full cc. If the target has 5 stacks of Webbing, that is at least another 25% up to potentially another 100%, and yes, this does also stack on top of the other effects, giving your Envenomed Bite a potentially 250% damage increase (this stacks additively with the up to 90% that Envenomed Bite gives itself). However, the 'unable to move' effect doesn't apply to bosses or any other target immune to cc.

---
 **Wrap**
Learned at level 36
 30 Energy
Instant
Wraps the target in sticky threads, granting 1 stack of Webbing.
- Gains up to a 100% chance to apply an additional stack of Webbing from Primal Fury.

---
 **Wrench**
Learned at level 42
 40 Energy
Instant		60 sec cooldown
Requires Spider Form
Wrenches on your threads, interrupting and all targets connected to your Webbing and stunning them for 0.5 sec per stack.
- Brutal Impact increases the stun by 0.25/0.5 sec per stack (max becomes 5 sec per target)

---
 **Slurp**
Learned at level 48
 20 Energy
 6 sec channel
Injects the target with digestive enzymes, draining 20 Energy every 2 sec to deal [low-very low] Nature damage to the target and restore health to you and any targets affected by your Rejuvenation or Regrowth equal to 25% of the damage dealt. Each tick deals increased damage proportional to the amount the target's movement speed is reduced. Effects which reduce casting time also reduce the channel duration and cause the damage to be dealt more rapidly.
- Counts as a Poison effect.
- Scales with both physical stats and spell stats.
- Omen of Clarity only applies to the initial cost.
- Can be used together with Nature's Swiftness for Big Damage.
- Gains up to 10 reduced Energy cost from Ferocity, applying only to the initial cost.
- Gains up to a 50% reduction in the in-channel Energy drain from Tranquil Spirit (10 Energy drained every 2 sec instead of 20).
- Gains up to 2 sec shorter duration from Improved Healing Touch.
- Gains up to 2 sec shorter duration from Blood Frenzy.
- Gains full benefit from Nature's Focus.
- Gains up to 20% increased damage from Gift of Nature (does not gain increased healing from Gift of Nature over and above what it gains naturally from dealing increased damage).
- Gains up to 30% increased critical effect chance from Improved Regrowth (its ticks can crit).
- Gains full benefit from Subtlety.
- Gains full benefit from Genesis.
- The healing portion double dips, scaling with both spell power on the tick damage and healing power on the healing itself. The scaling coefficient on the healing portion is very generous, allowing the skill to easily do more healing than it does damage even with only a moderate amount of spell/healing power.
- This is your only really spam able damage skill, as its Energy cost is low and you can use it effectively even if you can't get out of site to Hide. With that said, it is NOT the main source of your damage, even IF you can't get out of site to Hide. In fact, once you have 3 stacks of Envenomed Bite rolling, if you can reasonably keep 5 stacks of Webbing up you'll get better damage for your Energy by using Wrap and Swathe than you would spamming Slurp. Unfortunately, Webbing gets knocked out pretty fast when the target is being hit by multiple players at once, especially in raids, so you'll need to have a sense of whether trying to keep a target Wrapped is worth it, and if it isn't, Slurp is your fallback.
- The main use case in group play is in that rare situation where your Webbing is going strong, your Swathe is already applied, your Envenomed Bites are ticking away, and you have no need to spread Webbing or Envenomed Bite to anything other targets, and you just need something to spend Energy on in between doing your Hide>Envenomed Bite combo or reapplying Webbing and Swathe. This is late fight stuff, but it's still nice to have.
- The actual main use case of this skill, however, is in solo play. If you're Feral you want to be in Cat Form, but if you're Restoration you can use this against targets that are facing you in melee. Envenomed Bite>Wrap>Swathe is STILL your better damage option, but Slurp heals you and can be used more than once every 21-30 sec.

---
At max level, you have 3 paths available to you.
 **Path 1**: Feral Combat. Build Strength and Agility gear. This maximizes your Attack Power, which increases the effect of your Webbing and decreases its chance to be broken. It also increases the damage of your auto attacks, however slight that may be. Your CC ability will be extremely strong, but your damage will be mostly useless. Envenomed Bite and Slurp do get some nice bonuses from your targets being CC'd, and their decreased ability to break free frees up some Energy to cast Slurp more, but your main role will be as support. Most likely you'll start out the fight with some pre-spun webs, pop off a few Wraps and a Swathe, maybe an Envenomed Bite, then shift over to Cat. Once you've left Spider Form, though, there's not much point in shifting back in, as you'll be disconnected from your Webbing and would need to spend your limited Energy reconnecting. You can also just stay in Spider Form and be a minimally effective DPS while CCing everything in sight, though, and if you do you'll be the literal God of CC.

 **Path 2**: Restoration. Build Spell Power just as you would any other healer build. Without much Attack Power, your Webbing won't be very strong. It'd won't be nothing, though, and your Swathe can still cc individual targets for a respectable duration. With some pre-fight set up you can effectively cc a single target before the fight begins in earnest, then shift out to do your healing. Since all of your damage scales from Spell Power, however, you'll actually be a pretty strong damage dealer, and it won't be the biggest of deals if you shift out to pop out some heals now and then, since the bulk of your damage will keep ticking away anyway, and you can fully refresh the duration of Envenomed Bite by hitting it from stealth so that even if you take ~20 seconds between attacks, your damage loss will be minimal. It costs 130 Energy to do a Hide>Envenomed Bite combo, which is 6 seconds of letting your Energy regen with 5/5 Furor (+60 Energy on shapeshift), 5/5 Reflection (+6 Energy per tick if you've not been attacking or taking damage), 5/5 Tranquil Spirit (-30 Energy to Hide, -20 Energy to Envenomed Bite), and 5/5 Ferocity (-10 Energy to Envenomed Bite). Since you only need 6-7 uninterrupted seconds to keep your main damage rolling, that leaves you with a respectable amount of time to heal. Since the 90% increased Nature damage over time from Envenomed Bite also applies to Insect Swarm, that's also nice little damage boost you can give yourself.

 **Path 3**: Hybrid. Build both Spell Power and Attack Power. You'll be limited to basically only being in Spider Form most of the time, as all of your other forms will be weaker for the lack of stat stacking, but you'll be a beast. Your CC won't be as strong as a full AP build, but it'll be a whole lot stronger than a full SP build, which will in turn increase the damage of your venoms. Slurp scales just fine with both physical and spell stats, so it'll be doing mostly the same damage as for either of the other two builds (though higher than the SP build due to better Webbing!), but Envenomed Bite double dips for potentially crazy physical crits once it's built up. You can get up close and personal with Slurps whenver your CC powers aren't needed, and get a nice but not critical damage boost that way, or you can stay at the edges of the fight and watch, only dipping in to do your Hide>Envenomed Bite combo, spread Anchor Thread or Wrapping around, or refresh Swathe when one of your victims is about to break free. To be clear, even if you're in full Slurp mode, your damage won't be at the level of a Feral Cat or any pure DPSer. What you will bring, however, is a massive reduction in the main target's movement speed plus a 25% reduction in their casting and attack speed, the ability to cc as many targets as you choose with some preparation, and the ability to stun/interrupt basically the entire battlefield on command. With this build, you are both incredibly strong at CC (granted, your CC is worse than if you went full AP), and you have competitive DPS (remember, a full AP Spider Form hits like a wet noodle).
