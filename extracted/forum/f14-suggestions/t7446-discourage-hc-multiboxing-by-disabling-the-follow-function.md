# Discourage HC multiboxing by disabling the follow function

## Post 47408 by Drubarrymooer (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-05-25T18:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47408#p47408 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I feel like I've been seeing a lot of HC's dying and dinging in pairs and it seems like they're multiboxing.  I have reported a handful as well, because its pretty obvious when a person is on one character and running around with another that's on follow and only moves a half second after the other during quests that require mob looting.  I feel like an obvious way to counter this is to disable the follow functionality.  After all...."you stand alone" in the HC challenge.

It would also prevent players from being followed around unwanted as well.  This is probably a much more rare event, but I did have a level 10 HC follow me around once.  No idea why.  I tried losing them and failed, and they eventually aggro'd and died.
