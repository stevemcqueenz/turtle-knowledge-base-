# Limit Quest XP range for HC players

## Post 56863 by Winningone — 2023-08-11T18:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56863#p56863 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Seems like HC characters should have to use the original quest xp level ranges when turning in quests. I was just thinking it doesn't feel very hard core for players to get all the way to 60 completing quests which are 10 or more levels below where they're at.

The way it currently sits players can bypass all of the level 50+ content by doing level 40 quests. Then, once they hit 60, they start to gear up in dungeons because they no longer risk the death penalty. That seems pretty lame core to me.

There's also the issue of mobs being no threat at all. Was it really hard core if you hunted level 22 raptors in the Wetlands but you're level 30+? What challenge did you really face other than the challenge of searching for a guide on how to complete every low level quest in the game?

Maybe I'm misunderstanding the definition of "challenge" and "hard" but right now the Hard Core challenge doesn't seem to be in sync with what the accomplishment implies.
