# Revive champion seems really pointless/boring

## Post 21820 by Mimmy — 2022-08-10T15:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21820#p21820 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What is the point? I already have resurrection. Yeah, revive champion revives the person with more hp/mana, but so what? The rest of your group is going to need to be healed anyway. Also it has a 5 minute cooldown and can't even be cast in combat, it's really nothing special.

I got a suggestion for you here, instead of being a slightly more powerful resurrection with a different name, do some thing cool, like, the ability to instantly battle res your dead champion into an angelic warrior in the middle of battle through the power of the light, he could be able to fight on for 15-20 seconds before dropping back dead. Also if this was the ability function then of course you would need a reagent for it as well.

I have plenty of clutch epic moments when a druid battle ressed me, how big of a deal could it be to give priest something similar? Just give the priest version some yellow glow and angel wings, and there you have it.

Also some lore flair type stuff, this light form resurrection could possible do increased damage to undead/demons or make them cower in fear, but thats just a fantasy
