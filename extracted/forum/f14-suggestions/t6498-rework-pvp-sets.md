# Rework PVP Sets

## Post 40916 by Heroclastus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-09T16:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40916#p40916 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

what has been my gripe fo a long, long time is the usefulness or uselessness of PVP sets that differs greatly from class to class. In some cases it's good but often there's a lot of PVE gear that's plain better than PVP gear for well, PVP. As i play mainly Rogue, some common examples:

- Basically the entire Rogue T1 Set has bonusses that fit much more with PVP than the actual PVP set. Vanish CD reduction, bonus energy , heal on vanish are all very PVP centric and don't rly do anything for PVE

- T2 gloves with immune to disarm (which pve mobs at that point in progress disarm !?)

A good course of action IMO would be not necessarily to improve the PVP not raw stats-wise but pvp-utility wise, e.g.:

- Give all melee classes pvp gloves immune to disarm trait so it frees up a weapon for another enchant

- Give all PVP boots minor speed trait as that is kinda mandatory anyways and frees up an enchant also

- More armor and all resistances on melee PVP set pieces

- Alternative: PVP enchants that can be bought from PVP vendor and only applied to the blue and epic pvp set items. Like stamina AND move speed for boots, agi or str AND disarm immunity for gloves with several different options to choose from

## Post 40942 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-04-09T18:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40942#p40942 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think flat out making melees immune against one of the best cc effects against them is a extremely unfun and boring way to deal with a gameplay problem.

It always felt like disarm could be more prominet in both pve and pvp in a similar way to how interrupts are a frequent threat to casters. (Ofc that includes ways fo counterplay)

So passive blanket immunity to disarm on even more gear is imho the wrong approach.

Resistences on a pvp set i also regard as problematic. A warrior can always rely that intercept will bring him closer to his target, even when the target resists the stun. Why should mages have to deal with a resistence stat based chance their frostnova gets ignored completely?
It would be absurd if your hamstring had a 50% misschance due to your target having armor. Why should spellcasters have to deal with such a thing constantly?

Addmitably id like for spellpen to have a more prominent place in the game, but id say screwing over everyone without spellpen by giving players "free" resistences is the wrong way to make spellpen useful
