# New Gear that helps with class spec roles that are neglected

## Post 8530 by Himyname — 2020-11-25T01:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8530#p8530 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So what this means is new gear and weapons introduced as drops or something you can earn in any new content out there or in existing ones that can help class specs that are neglected like protection tank paladin, enhancement tank shaman. I don't like the idea of changing talents and abilities too much and would rather they left as vanilla as possible. But new items could sort of create a loophole for this.

<Protection Paladin>

Shield of the Templar (Paladin only): Chance on hit. When you block gives you a X% chance to regain a flat amount of mana. Helps with mana management for tank paladins and since they're getting hit very often this would help them. The shield also grants a very high amount of spirit

Libram of the Protector (Paladin Only): Blessing of Protection will drop all threat on the ally down to 0. Blessing of Protection however no longer protects the ally from physical damage. Also reduces cooldown of Blessing of Protection down by 1 min. This would make BoP the closest thing to a paladin as a taunt.

Greater Sword of Zeal (Paladin Only): Increases holy spell power by a lot and has a chance on hit to deal additional holy damage. This would help generate more threat through holy damage through Righteous Fury.

Standard of Justice (Paladin Only): A trinket that allows your Judgment of Justice instead of giving you a chance to stun the target, give a chance to force the target to attack you (this chance to proc is lower than chance to stun). So it's a taunt by chance.

<Enhancement Tank Shaman>

Superior Invulnerable Mail: Gives a chance on taking a hit to give you invulnerability to physical attacks for a few seconds and you won't lose any threat or aggro during this protection. Increased armor and defense.

Warding Totem: When equipped, your Stoneskin Totem will direct 15% of threat your allies generate directed to you as long as the allies and enemies are within the vicinity of the Stoneclaw Totem.

Bindings of Windslasher: Bracers when equipped Grace of Air Totem will also increase your dodge chance up by 10%.
