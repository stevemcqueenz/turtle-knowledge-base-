# Reword + Simplify Hardcore Announcements

## Post 50399 by Fantist — 2023-06-17T17:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50399#p50399 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I enjoy the hardcore level and death announcements but they need to be reworded and simpler when they are seen a thousand times a day.

The current death message is:

A tragedy has occurred. Hardcore character <Player> has fallen to <Enemy> (level <X>). They laughed in the face of death, but have learnt that death always has the last laugh. at level <X>. May this sacrifice not be forgotten.

It's unnecessary to say that the player is hardcore.
The level of the enemy is sort of interesting but the parenthesis feel weird.
The entire line about laughing at death gets annoying the tenth time seeing it let alone the thousandth.
Saying the player level again is unnecessary and feels weird, not to mention the first word of the sentence isn't even capitalized.
The last line "May this sacrifice not be forgotten" is off. The death was a failure and an accident not a sacrifice.

Keep it simple:

A tragedy has occurred. <Player> has fallen to <Enemy>. May their deeds of valor be remembered.

The last line is even a reference to Diablo hardcore deaths.

The leveling message is good. I would just take out the word "will" because it feels awkward:

<Player has reached level <X> in hardcore mode! Their ascendance towards immortality continues, however, so do the dangers they face.

I hope this doesn't come across as overly critical. Again, I enjoy the messages. I would just like the see them polished up a bit when they are seen so often.
