While I think that's exacerbated to a degree by the low priority on PVP content development, this is a chronic problem with MMOs as a genre. At some point, the 'expansion' (version) is exhausted for content, and the player has to get creative (an example given above by Sinrek).

This has never been fully addressed, and I am unsure it really can be. It was the business model for Blizzard and other MMO developers to make expansions. That's just how it works.

There is a life to it, in general, and it's finite. Which is why so many think FRESH is the savior to any given server's state of being played out. It really just restarts the cycle, and some people are happy to play that same cycle over and over.

TWoW is enhancing the game, but no one will change the fact that a server can get played out.
