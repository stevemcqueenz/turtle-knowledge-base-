# Increase min amount of starting players for Tel'abim BGs

## Post 103132 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-10-15T23:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103132#p103132 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

From pvp-news on discord:

> Jamey — 2021-10-13 19:37
>  Dear players,
>
>  As an ongoing effort to keep both PvE and PvP exciting here on Turtle WoW we have decided to semi-revert some of the minimum player Battleground barriers.
>
>  • Warsong Gulch will now require a minimum of 4 (was 5) players per team to start.
>  • Arathi Basin will now require a minimum of 8 (was 15) players per team to start.
>  • Alterac Valley will now require a minimum of 12 (was 20) players per team to start.

This change from 2021 is currently ruining BGs on Tel’abim. Starting games with 5 ppl leads in initial imbalance, considering the rock-paper-scissors class design of wow, leads to the rest of the game being skewered in favor of one faction. It’s never, ever fun to join halfway through a game.

Some might argue, it’s better with a 5v5 than no game at all, but this is a poor way of thinking as the consequence is that all the other games suffer. In addition, it very often leads to imbalances as the unreliability of qued players actually joining leads to games becoming 6v7, 6v8, and so on.

Another issue is that it destroys group queuing. Allowing players in one by one by one means it is practically impossible to get in as a group of (for example 3) at the same time. If you want Tel’abim to be a genuine pvp server you need to allow for groups/premades (up to atleast 5) to happen.

The current system also contributes to making queuing very unpredictable as games open differently for everyone. The uncertainty of the que being 5 or 45 minutes, and if you will get into the same BG as your guildies/mates, takes alot of the fun out of BGs and disincentivizes queuing.

WSGs need to start with atleast 8 players on each side, and ABs 10-12.
