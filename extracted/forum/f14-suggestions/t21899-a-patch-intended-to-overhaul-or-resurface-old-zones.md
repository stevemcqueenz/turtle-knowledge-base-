# A patch intended to overhaul or resurface old zones

## Post 151718 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-14T18:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151718#p151718 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For years, I've seen Turtle greatly improve the quality of its content. The current zones are definitely much better planned and developed than the older zones. It's exciting to see the quality achieved in Grim Reaches, Balor, and especially Northwind. These zones are simply beautiful, full of detail and color.

It's a shame I can't say the same about older zones. I understand they were implemented in the early stages of the server, just getting used to working on such an old client with tools that I assume were limited. My current proposal is to do a small, quick patch to rework older zones like Tirisfal Uplands, Gilneas, and others—small, forgotten areas tucked away in the corners of existing zones that I think could be improved with some of the love being given to the zones currently in development.

I don't have any issues with Gilneas, especially with the quests, but with the terrain. It's by far the most uncomfortable area to explore due to the absurd amount of hills and the unevenness of the terrain. I don't deny that Gilneas would be beautiful with hills, but it needs to be thought through, the roads and alternate paths should be more detailed, and for the love of God, the terrain should be flattened a bit. Gilneas is a peninsula of a kingdom. I don't think people would have come to live in such an uneven place. Gilneas is not Stranglethorn.

Moving on to older areas like Tirisfal Uplands, they are very lacking in detail and things to do, when you first enter you notice that they tried to add detail to make it look like a forest with hills, but as you go deeper it seems that they lost the spirit of continuing to add detail and everything becomes empty, a couple of ruins, the occasional tree and clumps of grass here and there, it looks like an area made by a single person who got bored and just added random things, there are several areas like this everywhere that need a revamp to be implemented properly into the current game.

A patch type 1.18.1 or 1.18.2
