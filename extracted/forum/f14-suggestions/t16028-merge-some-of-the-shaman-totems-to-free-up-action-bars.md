# merge some of the shaman totems to free up action bars

## Post 109206 by Fornow (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-21T12:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109206#p109206 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I love playing shaman but I dislike how my action bars are overflowing. For convenience I keybind even the more niche abilities like far sight, spy totems or frost resistance totem (they all have their uses in PvP). Plus for shaman it's a good idea to have keybinds for various rank 1 abilities (shocks, lighting bolt, etc). And then there's consumables like bandages, healing potions, etc. It's all a bit much for the 4 hotbars in the default interface.

For this reason I really wish some of shamans abilities would combine. I think totems offer great grounds for this without affecting core gameplay or balance too much. Thus here are some suggestions:
- mana spring totem and healing stream totem merge to become healing spring totem

- poison cleansing totem and disease cleansing totem combine to become malady cleansing totem

- sentry totem and tremor totem combine to become guard totem; unlike the default behavior of sentry totem vision only swaps to the totem once you click on the buff icon

- the resistances of fire resistance totem get folded into flame tongue totem

- the resistances of nature resist totem get folded into grace of air totem

- the resistances of frost resistance totem get folded into windwall totem
All in all this would reduce the total number of totems by 6. While this power creeps shaman a bit in a limited amount of scenarios the quality of life should be worth it. I don't know every single raid fight. Maybe there's some where having an extra totem to plop down breaks something, but I can't imagine it. In PvP it's also only a minor buff.
