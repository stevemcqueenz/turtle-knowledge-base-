# Dwarf blacksmithing racial

## Post 21087 by Nightowl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-04T18:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21087#p21087 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Have always been bothered by this. How come dwarves, the masters of metalurgy and everything related ot that, do not get a racial that reflects these capabilities? There are tons of cool lore related weapons and armors being made by dwarves, most notably the Ashbringer that was forged by Magni himself, which seemed to be capable to rival and even destroy Frostmourne. That's pretty impressive to say the least.

Would it be too much to ask for a +15 blacksmithing racial? Or to go even beyond that and ask for a cool ability to learn an extra type of blacksmithing? As in allowing dwarves to be the only race that is capable of learning two types of blacksmithing instead of one? Or give them the ability to smith a few armor/weapon pieces that can only be made by dedicated dwarven blacksmiths?

On that note, is it possible to implement suggestion #2, or would it break the spagetti code? Two types of blacksmithing that is.
