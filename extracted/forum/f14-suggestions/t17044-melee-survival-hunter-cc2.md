# Melee/Survival Hunter CC2

## Post 116231 by Snigery (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-11T15:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116231#p116231 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, I had a melee hunter prior to the CC2 changes. He is a long-standing personal project that I enjoy, about lvl 40's. What I enjoyed about the melee hunter meta, was the goal was to make it viable, but there was no clear-cut way to do it and a lot about the class at it was that almost made it possible but also got in the way.

My approach was a more broadly focused hunter that provided utitlity. I mainly specced BM, but dipped into marksman for the Concussive stun, and Survival obviously for the crits.

While I quite like many of the changes brought with CC2, and as a hunter to the BM tree all the more appealing, Survival as nice as the changes read it does not make me want to take it; And I don't quite think retrofitting the meta to a pure melee role really makes sense for the class.

I don't want to rant but I thought I'd present two ideas I had that I think would be more interesting.

 **Untamed Trapper -> "Possum Play**
It makes sense, it is what everyone wanted, but for a final talent it's just boring. Hunters could already deploy traps in combat with a macro and some skill using it. The drawback was you are adding the cost of FD and being restricted by the recharge which was twice that of your traps. I think that play should be kept but made easier by reducing the cool-down and mana-cost of FD considerably. For me the act was more a slight of hand as a melee-hunter to slip down a trap, than actually committing to the act of playing dead. This change won't be much different than the CC2 improvements of Mana Spring for shamans. I see that they got even more mana cost reduction, which makes that ability more useful as it is, so that shamans get some return on the effect compared to the cost. Same idea here. You could even integrated the in-combat trap placement into FD usage by making it less glitchy without requiring a macro?

 **Carve -> "Leap Away"**
I think any melee hunter would be happy for another melee ability to trigger, and it is already great that MS's trigger condition was removed. I have heard others displeased with Carve however, and I can understand that. But what I think would be more interesting to replace Carve, and rather than just another melee ability, some disengage like ability that removes the hunter from their target, opposite to most engaging moves like Charge or Pounce. This grants the hunter an opportunity to use what he already should be, strings/shots/etc.

Prior to CC2, hunters still had to use them since they were part of the tool kit, we would just weave out of combat to pop it off. Strings are an affordable DoT for the cost, so rather than just boiling down the class to a pure melee role, I think a melee hunter should be more about weaving in and out of melee range, a bit of an acrobatist; Swapping aggro with pet, taking on a stray mob from healer, slipping down traps deceitfully with FD, popping of Strings and Shots where enemies think they're just beyond their reach, and utilizing all of the other tools in the game to stay up to par with other classes, but providing a lot of support and utility to any situation.

I guess I ranted a little anyway, but those were just some ideas. I think the changes were done good, but they just do not draw me any more toward Survival then having a beefed up pet at my side while still engaging in melee the way I already was. Actually moving the trap benefits further into the Survival tree, reducing the earlier crit benefits because a deep Survival spec would benefit equally from later talents, etc, etc, these only hurt the hybrid meta, at least for my playstyle. Plus I just don't think the pure melee route is very interesting for hunter, who has so many tools for any situation.

I agree with a lot of this thread, especially the melee-weaving direction for the spec. The intended vision for Survival Hunter was always a melee/ranged hybrid spending about half their time in melee and half at ranged, though that’s not to say I think a melee-only build shouldn’t be an option, quite the opposite in fact. I've thrown around a pet idea before for a talent to encourage that 50/50 playstyle by reducing and eventually eliminating the deadzone for Survival. I know you can't just trade out a single-rank talent for a multiple-rank one, but I feel like this could be a fitting replacement for Carve:

---
“**Hip Shooting**” (3 ranks)
Reduces the minimum range of your ranged weapons by 1/2/3 yards.
---
Alternatively it could be a two-rank talent to make spending only one point not feel like a waste or just something to push through. There’s a pretty big jump in having a one-yard deadzone compared to the regular 3 yards, and there's an even bigger difference between one yard and no deadzone at all. However, I'd understand if an effect like that would deserve to be locked behind three points. I also don't know what row it would belong on, but my gut says that such a dramatic effect should be deep enough in the tree that low-level players and other specs wouldn't have access to it.
