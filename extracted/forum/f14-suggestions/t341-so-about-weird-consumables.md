I have been enjoying the server exploring and questing and I noticed something there is a slew of consumables that have status effects attached to them like the Slumber sand or Bag of Marbles. My question is this would it be possible to have a vendor for each faction selling these items. I personally love the idea of a warrior carrying extra equipment to supplement his fighting like armor wax or wizards carrying slumber sand to knock someone out in a tight spot. Stuff like that, I think it would be a cool unique edition to the game, a small one but cool none the less.
