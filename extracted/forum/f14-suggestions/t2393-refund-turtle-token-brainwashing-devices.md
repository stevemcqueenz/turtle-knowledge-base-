Important Preface : Things change. That’s simply the fact of life. Besides, donations should come from a heart of charity, and it is not meant to be transactional. The rewards are simply bonus stuff. So this is not a complaint/angry demand, but just a cheeky player trying his luck to maximize the value of his donations.

With the introduction of the Mysteries of Azeroth expansion, Brainwashing Device can now be purchased using 350 in-game gold. This is a suggestion for the server team to consider refunding people that had bought the device using Turtle Tokens previously.

If I had known that this item (or any Turtle Shop item for that matter) would eventually make its way to be available in-game, I wouldn’t have purchased it using my tokens. With that said, I do feel it is a good addition for players to be able to acquire the device without having to spend real life money. Just that I wish I had gotten another wormhole bag instead :D
