## Post 120323 by Koci53z — 2025-02-18T15:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120323#p120323 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's year 2018. Little John hears about Turtle WoW and decides to try it out. Little John creates a Tauren Hunter and names it "John". After 4 months he hits level 60 and decides it's time to leave the server because the only part he liked about Classic WoW was the leveling journey.

Little John never logs back to Turtle WoW again.

Nickname "John" is now stuck in the void and will never be able to use again because of the server policy [viewtopic.php?t=5599](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=5599):

> ....we will free the names of the characters that meet one of the following criteria:
>  Below level 40 and haven't logged in for 24 months.

Which means every nickname that is above level 40 is permamently assigned to an account.

Can you please reconsider your policy about inactive character names and do it similar to Blizzard which is:
 *"free the names of a characters that didn't login in two years"*?

There are 8k+ players at peak hours, a lot of them are leveling and also a newcommers that will never be able to play with their nickname because someone tagged it 6 years ago and is no longer playing on the server.
