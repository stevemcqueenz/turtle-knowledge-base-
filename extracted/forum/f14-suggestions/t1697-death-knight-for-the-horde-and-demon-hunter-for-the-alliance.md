## Post 10039 by Afaslizo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-04-09T10:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10039#p10039 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

That would be extremely difficult because they use spell effects not to be found in vanilla so you would have to put the data in from somewhere else. We are talking about establishing an empty class slot and filling it with 20-35 spells to feel like a proper class. Each of these abbilities would take a long time to test and implement and then to balance against both content and other classes. Every class added means a lot more balancing which is exactly why the death knight was unbalanced all of Wrath and redone completely in cata with specific roles to simplify the balance issues. Since the server team has a drop of the ocean of Blizz ressources adding even a single class would take one year at least without working on other content.
While at least your suggestion is able to be implemented without breaking the six class maximum for vanilla races frame (night elves and forsaken have five class options) that should also be respected as a limitation. Trolls, Orcs, Humans and Dwarves have their six slots filled. Any class added would exclude those races naturally.

Also the lore would only support demon hunters in a limited capacity since death knights are more of Scourge domain. The implementation of death knights in Wrath was a narrative mess purely engaged for gameplay as the intro storyline was half assed to reintegrate unfitting characters with their factions without respecting the narrative framework.
