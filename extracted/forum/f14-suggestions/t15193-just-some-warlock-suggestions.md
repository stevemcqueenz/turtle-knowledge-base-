I used to play during WOTLK so I still remember a few very welcome changes and I would welcome them here as well.

 1) Make Soulshards stack or do what they did later on to overhaul/replace the system entirely.
 2) It really sucks for dots to not start their first dmg tick on finished cast.
 3) Maybe I havent found the right settings yet but my Imp will only open fire on an opponent If I get hit in the face. Preferably it should start shooting a target once I shoot at it/enter combat. The same goes for the other demons too of course.

Just some ideas.
