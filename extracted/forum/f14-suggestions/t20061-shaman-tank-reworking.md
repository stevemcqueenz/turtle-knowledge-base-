## Post 137250 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-27T15:14:00Z

**•        Intro:** Since childhood, I remember adventurers who tried to tank as a shaman. We managed to do it only in BRD. And it was the most unusual and fun gameplay at that time. And all because you had to not just stand like a punching bag, get hit on the head and only watch out for aggro, but actively use spells and move.
Later, with the TBC and WoTLK updates, Blizzard added several spells, talents, glyphs (if anyone still remembers), which increased survivability in battle. And we were like, "This is what we were missing, why didn't you add it then, at least for the shaman tank with a shield"

In the CC2 update (trash update) Instead of a competent approach to increasing survivability, here in twow simply added a passive increase in forehead thickness to the level of plate armor. Another thick-headed tank, another dummy for beating. I categorically disagree.
I suggest to take a close look at what interesting mechanics Blizzard added to the shaman for survival, interpritate  here and limit the use only with a shield.

For example, here are my ideas

AOE temporary aggro - Stonclaw Totem, Fire Nova and Magma Totem.
