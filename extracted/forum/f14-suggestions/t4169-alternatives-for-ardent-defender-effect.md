# Alternatives for Ardent Defender Effect

## Post 24930 by Gerdyus — 2022-09-12T14:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24930#p24930 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With holy shield moving up the prot pala tree ardent defender has taken its place. Receiving 25% less damage when you are below 35% is the original idea of the spell as later implemented in retail but I think its not all that useful in most tanking environments. Note this post is written with raid tanking in mind, its a decent ability for dungeon tanking but prot pala is already amazing at that.

 - It does not seem to trigger on the damaging hit that brings you below 35% health.
 - If you are that low health then you are probably a few seconds away from dying regardless of 25% more damage reduction.
 - Its just not very exciting.

Possible alternatives - numbers are placeholder.
 -  Increases maximum health by 8.75%. (The ideal mitigation provided by ardent defender as of now just pre-emptively)
 -  When targeted by a single enemy triple your block value. (Fun way to incorporate block value being useful in raids but not imbalance it against trash) This is probably a pain to implement though.
 - Divine protection (the spell you forget about after getting divine shield) no longer provides immunity but reduces damage taken by X % and duration is increase by Y seconds. (A shield wall for prot pala essentially)

And since ardent defender requires holy shield to be talented how about some interaction with it
 - When blocking with holy shield active heal for (X + block value)* (1+spellpower coefficient).

Or go for a bit of scaling fix to help with single target threat:
 - Converts weapon damage into holy damage (now you don't need fishbringer, every weapon is fishbringer and strength has more value!) make it scale with crusader strike and you even make that useful.
