<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174407#p174407 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Basically allow horde and alliance 1 new race each. But…

 1.  This race HAS to be able be a Druid as one of his class options and….

 2. All passive and activated racial abilities actually work while in feral forms and…

 3. There is a jewelcrafting trainer in its starting town and capital city.

As it stands, Druids only have 1 option per faction, can’t even use its own racials in feral forms, and jewelcrafting isn’t an option for them unless they go way out of their ways to get it while the profession was some really nice BOP items in it for Druids.

And once again, allow roots and speed indoors and give a proper resurrection spell to Druids. Not even playing at the moment, just watching and lurking the forums waiting to see changes I am waiting for to make it worth my time again and not just an exercise in needless frustration.

> **Fizuzfil wrote: Wed Apr 08, 2026 7:44 am**
> Imagine paladins will want their favourite class on both sides. And what about shamams.

I mean, hypothetically you could give each side a class that plays similarly but is thematically different. Like putting Death Knights on Horde (not the Wrath version, but an actual Paladin-equivalent based on its RTS appearances) and then giving Alliance some sort of class that works around...I don't know...smaller runestones, rather than totems. I take your meaning, though. For an idea to work, it has to be balanced and have the right flavour.

That said...instead of just going, "But what about X?" let's try to theorycraft here.

You want Naga, right? I think that Naga would be a race that could actually be a Druid on Horde side, given their common ancestry with elves (though for that matter, Trolls could also be justified as Druid, just learning their forms from different Loa, by much the same logic). Sure, Naga don't have legs, but Trolls don't have shoes show up on their models either.

For Alliance, maybe a tribe of Furbolgs that has fled the corruption taking their race. Or Dryads, who were a part of the Night Elf faction in WCIII. Either one would be a race with ties to the Alliance, via the Night Elves, and who has strong ties to nature. Furbolgs have the advantage of already being known to have several character classes that exist in game, including Druids, but would be a little difficult to model armour for. Dryads would be easier, but have fewer obvious class options, and like Naga there's the issue of either needing special lower body armour or having it not show on the model.
