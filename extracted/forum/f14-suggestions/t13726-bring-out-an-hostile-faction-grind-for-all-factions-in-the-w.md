# Bring out an hostile faction grind for all factions in the world

## Post 93356 by Sark21 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-04-23T20:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93356#p93356 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been roleplaying out in the open world and have to occasionally kill an NPC. This does ruins to the mood of that moment and I have wondered why turtle doesn't expand on its roleplaying capabilities and allow you to grind reputation with an enemy faction you want to be friendly or at least neutral with.

I understand it can lead to abuse but hear me out, there is a reputation vendor with the faction your trying to get friendly with and once you reach exalted or friendly you cab buy an item that gives you a buff. This buff makes you friendly with that faction but if you try to pick up a node or chest you lose the buff and you turn hostile. Really love to roleplaying as a Scarlet Crusader and have specific quest items to keep me friendly in WPL and EPL but its a chore to keep going back and getting that item over and over again. I'd rather have a more immersive experience by grinding a reputation with them.

This item would not work in a dungeon or raid so no exploiting can be done on that end.

There are many factions in the open world that are hostile but should be allowed to ally with them if you do the work much like the pirates in STV.

## Post 93448 by Mac (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-04-25T01:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93448#p93448 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It was possible years back to become friendly with the Scarlet Crusade. If you were a human (and only if you were human), there was a quest you could do in Tirisfal to get a ring that would make you friendly with them as long as you wore it.

This ring wound up being abused by non-roleplayers (who now massively outnumber the roleplayers on this server, but back then much less so). They would put on the ring and farm chests in places like Scarlet Monastery. They tried nerfing the chests' contents for a while which was also disappointing and unfair for players playing legit. And with plans for a Scarlet raid on the horizon, ultimately the decision to remove the ring from the game was made. You can actually still see the remnants of the idea. There's a Scarlet quartermaster in the outside courtyard of Scarlet Monastery who can sell to no one, and here's an ancient forum post asking how the ring worked back when it was still in-game: [viewtopic.php?p=7229](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7229)

Not saying this idea is inherently bad or shouldn't be done but rather that you have to consider there are going to be players who abuse it, especially if you're seriously proposing it on this server where it's been tried. Concerns like this should be considered and addressed. Personally, I'd like to see it happen.
