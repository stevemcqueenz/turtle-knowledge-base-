# Add some benefit for Warmode at level 60

## Post 49565 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-06-10T13:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49565#p49565 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently once you hit 60, there is no reason to keep Warmode on, besides the obvious one - to be tagged for pvp all the time. Hence, very few 60's ever have PvP enabled, so max level world pvp is pretty dead.

My suggestion is to add a small bonus that could incentivise people to keep Warmode on at max level. Here are some suggestions:
+10% Honor Gain
OR
+10% Reputation Gain
OR
+10% More gold from quest rewards
