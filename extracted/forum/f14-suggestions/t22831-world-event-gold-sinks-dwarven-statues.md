![💰](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4b0.svg) Concept: The Great Reforging of Ironforge

Lore Hook:
Word spreads throughout Dun Morogh that King Magni’s legacy must be immortalized in stone and gold. Dwarven craftsmen call upon the kin of Khaz Modan — and their allies — to donate materials and wealth to build a grand statue of Magni in the heart of Ironforge.

![🏗️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3d7.svg) Mechanics

 1. Global Donation Event

Each race or faction can contribute materials and gold to the construction effort.

Example donation items:

Gold: pure currency sink.

Bars: Thorium, Mithril, Truesilver, Arcanite.

Gems: Star Ruby, Azerothian Diamond.

Special crafts: Dense Grinding Stones, Gold Power Cores, etc.

Progress is server-wide and tracked via an NPC or UI (like Ahn’Qiraj war effort).

 2. Phased Construction

The statue begins as a scaffolding in front of the Great Forge.

As milestones are met, more of the statue appears.

When fully completed, a permanent statue appears, visible to everyone on that realm.

![🧭](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9ed.svg) Rewards & Incentives

Contributors receive cosmetic titles or vanity rewards (no power creep):

“Benefactor of Ironforge”

“Patron of the Stonefather”

Minor perks for participation:

Temporary reputation boosts with Ironforge.

A small chance for flavor items (e.g. “Commemorative Coin of Magni”).

Top contributors could get a small plaque in-game near the statue listing their names.

![⚖️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2696.svg) Economic Impact

A massive gold sink: encourages wealthy players and guilds to dump surplus gold.

Drives up demand for mid-tier materials (Thorium, Arcanite), stimulating trade.

Gives gathering professions purpose beyond consumables and crafting.

![🌍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f30d.svg) Expandable System

This concept could expand across races:

Orgrimmar: Horde rebuilds a monument to Grom Hellscream.

Stormwind: Humans donate to restore the Old Town district.

Night Elves: Rebuild a shrine in Ashenvale.

Each cycle could refresh every few months, pulling gold and mats out of circulation continuously — a living economy event that reinforces racial identity and server cooperation.
