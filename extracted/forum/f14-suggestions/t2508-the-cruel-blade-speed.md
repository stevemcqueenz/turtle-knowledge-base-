## Post 15617 by Raukodor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-01-10T15:41:00Z

Hello fellow turtles

I have a suggestion about this item <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60413>

The new sweet item named "the cruel blade" (the upgraded version of the ravager) i think have the wrong speed

His weapon speed is 2.6.

The problem is when it procs the whirlwind does damage every 3 seconds

So in 1vs1 or some situations like sweeping strikes active and autoattacking or spamming cleave you will do less dps against 1-2 mobs if the bladestorm procs

So my suggestion is change the bladestorm proc to do damage every 2'6 seconds (instead 3) or make the cruel blade slower. So the dps during the bladestorm become a buff and not a debuff

Opinions?
