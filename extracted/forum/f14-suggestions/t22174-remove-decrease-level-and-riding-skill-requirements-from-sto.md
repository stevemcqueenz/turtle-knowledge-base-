# Remove/decrease level and riding skill requirements from Stormwrought Deathsteed

## Post 153818 by Resonanc3 — 2025-09-30T17:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153818#p153818 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There're already custom mounts dropping from low-level dungeons, and none of them have these weird requirements like Stormwrought Deathsteed. For example, the only requirement for Violet Feral Raptor (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=23800>) is to be level 20, while Ivory Boar has no level requirements at all.
![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/bug_tracker_images/1000x700/3mg54WOHEUMiUHvpXd9dD0pPXaej65XT3MEVJk7U.png)
There're very harsh requirements on the Deathsteed that basically make it useless to any player who gets it during leveling or on their first character on the server. Because not only they need to hit level 60, but also pay 800-900g for epic riding too.

Suggestion: reduce level and riding skill requirements, or remove them entirely, to make the mount more accessible to everyone, and so that lucky players (like me) can actually use it and not let it rot in their banks forever.
