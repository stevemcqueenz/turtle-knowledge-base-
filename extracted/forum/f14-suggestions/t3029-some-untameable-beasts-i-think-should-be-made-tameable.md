# Some untameable beasts I think should be made tameable

## Post 18673 by Snakeman (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-05-18T15:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18673#p18673 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a somewhat longtime hunter player through Classic and now on Turtle WoW, there are some beasts in this world I've looked at and thought, "that would be an amazing pet to have." But then when you cast Beast Lore on them, or you look them up on Petopia, there's an indicator saying that the beast cannot be tamed. Most of these I don't really understand, like the Sky Shadows in Deadwind Pass, who were made tameable in TBC; others I think should get separate tameable looks due to them being high-level dungeon bosses or something else that doesn't make sense to tame.

By definition, this is gonna get long. Please bear with me!

> **Bats wrote:**
> The only untameable Bat listed on Petopia currently is Duskwing from the Eastern Plaguelands - I think this makes sense, and should definitely be left as-is.

> **Bears wrote:**
> There are some skins that exist but are unused in-game - I think it'd be awesome if a diseased white bear were added to Turtle!
>
>  The Rabid Thistle Bears in Darkshore are listed as being untameable also. I think this makes sense due to the "Rabid" name, but that would either mean Rabid Shardtooth from Winterspring should be made untameable as well, or the Rabid Thistle Bear should be made tameable.

> **Boars wrote:**
> There are a few untameable boars in the game, but I think these mostly make sense due to them either being already tamed by Quilboar NPCs, they're ghosts, or they're a boss. Agathelos the Raging could be made tameable to fit in with the Blind Hunter from the same instance also being tameable, but honestly I think it can go either way, since the bat is a rare spawn and the boar is not.

> **Carrion Birds wrote:**
> My beloved Sky Shadow. I definitely think this should be tweaked, especially since this was done during progression to The Burning Crusade in the original game. There is also a very cool unused skin listed on petopia that I think would look amazing on a tameable world rare or maybe inside a custom instance, but the drops might need to be comparatively weaker than other rares to disincentivise hard camping by geared players.

> **Cats wrote:**
> We've got a few here. My favourite cat skin in the game, the green tiger with orange stripes, is only shown on Horde-friendly quest mobs in Vanilla. There were different, tameable skins added in later versions of WoW, so I don't think it's completely out of the question to have a tameable fel cat somewhere in Vanilla!
>  The white sabre cat skin with black stripes is also listed as not being tameable, and I think it'd be a good addition to the game somewhere as well.

> **Crabs wrote:**
> The Crawler mobs from western Westfall are said to be tameable but throw an "Already Controlled" error if you try - I have not tried this, if anyone else has and can confirm it gives the error so it can be flagged for fixing that'd be cool. The savannah cubs from the Barrens are said to do this also but there are so many different mobs using that skin that I don't think it matters as much.

> **Crocolisks wrote:**
> I think Rotgrip being flagged tameable would make sense. Might allow for some dungeon trolling, but also allows hunters who may be soloing the instance anyway (as I did for my Blackstone Ring) some extra fun times. Also add more tameable basilisks to this family plz & ty.

> **Gorillas wrote:**
> Mokk the Savage and King Mukla being made tameable could make sense, I think. Otherwise, Gorilas seem pretty ok. Y'know, outside of them not being very good pets to actually use in Vanilla. :(

> **Hyenas wrote:**
> Gordok Mastiffs and Hyenas being untameable seems odd considering they have some patrols in DMN that aren't attached to ogres.

> **Owls wrote:**
> Was always a little bummed we only got two different owl skins in the game. I think that adding some of the unused skins - the black, brown and opalescent textures - as mobs to existing areas akin to the foxes added to various zones would be a great idea. Also means there are more owls out there to be tamed at a wider level range, instead of just the 10 and under owls on Teldrassil and the 45+ owls in Felwood and Winterspring.

> **Raptors wrote:**
> Not a cosmetic thing, but, first of all,these guys *deserve* Dash. Give Dash To Raptors 2k22
>
>  There is one standout untameable raptor look - the Venomhide Ravasaur in Un'goro Crater. I think it wouldn't hurt to make these guys (along with the other pink and blue Ravasaur mobs [except Lar'korwi's mates] in Un'goro Crater) tameable.

> **Scorpids wrote:**
> These guys aren't actually missing any appearances in game. I will take this opportunity to suggest that we have tameable snakes (like the ones from WC and ZG) added to TWoW in a similar fashion to foxes, but make them part of the Scorpid family, so they can use poisons. :)

> **Spiders wrote:**
> All of the untameable spiders in Vanilla are found in the Arachnid Quarter of the dread citadel, Naxxramas, so I definitely don't think those should be made tameable or brought under their current minimum level (61). I do, however, think that the purple spider could be added elsewhere! It's a really cool skin, I love purple.
>
>  Razzashi Broodwidows, however, ARE marked tameable - it wouldn't be unwelcome if they were tweaked to spawn at 60-61 so at least a level 60 spider could be tamed, but I'm not sure how levels on raid trash operate (or are supposed to operate).

> **Tallstriders wrote:**
> The only unused (technically) skin these guys have is the purple one, currently used by the Turtle Mode level 60 mount reward. Having a purple tallstrider pet to go along with Mazzranache would be fun, but I'm not sure where it could be added. Maybe as a rare spawn around wherever DMF is on a given week?

> **Turtles wrote:**
> These guys, similar to Scorpids, seem mostly fine. Gammerita not being tameable makes sense, since she's a quest mob, and the rare spawn from Azshara could go either way.

> **Wind Serpents wrote:**
> The best family, don't @ me.
>
>  They have one unused skin, but it's almost identical to the one found in the wilds of the Barrens, so I can't bellyache too hard about this one. However, the Soulflayers from ZG are flagged as tameable, same as the Razzashi Broodwidows, but their default level of 61 means they are, in effect, untameable, and the only way to get a blue serpent to 60 is to catch one in Wailing Caverns and raise it to 60 yourself. As someone with a level 60 blue serpent, I'd love also if Soulflayers could be a 60-61 mob, but, again, I have no idea how any of this works. Besides this, they're pretty ok in terms of skin availability, especially with the new green serpents added to Gilijim's Isle.

> **Wolves wrote:**
> These guys are fine. We don't need MORE incentive for people to shill them as the best raiding pets when it's been proven time and time again that they aren't. /s
>
>  On a serious note, they are missing a few tameable skins in game that I'd love to have access to! The spectral wolf and the blue/brown worg are two really cool skins I'd love to tame, but the only mob of the latter variety we have in game at the moment (as far as I'm aware) is Halycon in LBRS, who would make sense to not be tameable. Blizzard added a rare featuring this skin to the game with the Cataclysm world revamp, so I think it would fit well as an open world mob too.
>
>  There are also a couple of unused worg skins that could be dropped somewhere if an orc hunter wanted a worg pet to match their mount!
