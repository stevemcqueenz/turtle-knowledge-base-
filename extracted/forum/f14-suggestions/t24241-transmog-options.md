# Transmog Options.

## Post 170481 by Shirazen — 2026-03-10T01:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170481#p170481 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello.

I'd like to see more customizability when it comes to weapon transmogs: specifically whether or not a piece is backsheathing/hipsheathing - except this time: not as a bug, but as an actual feature. Perhaps even a separate gold sink to change the sheathing animation, different from Fashion Coins.

After accidentally damaging an outfit I'd spent a lot of time cultivating after changing it for RP reasons, only to find out that there was a patch I missed so it couldn't return to how I had it for years prior, I'm extremely upset, borderline sad.   crying_turtle

Transmog and RP are huge parts of Turtle and I'm not sure why benign 'bugs' are squashed that effect so few people besides those who are intentionally transmogging lower-quality backsteathing weapons up for the sake of having a cool RP set.

Please take this into consideration. It seems as if every time I experience by an RP, cosmetic, or transmog-related change it is a negative one, from illusions being strangely altered or outright removed, or legacy transmog outfits being permanently at risk over minor mistakes that can be missed by not reading one line in a patch among many.

Thank you.
