# Revise "Hero of the Stormpike/Frostwolf" Quest / AV PVP Quest

## Post 45403 by Daragan — 2023-05-10T21:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45403#p45403 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I've no doubt this suggestion is going to be perceived in one way or another - *git gud, laziness, not trying hard enough*, and so on. So I suppose the only thing I can do is outright express my suggestion and my basis for it, and encourage you to consider the below.

I was looking forward to completing this quest as it rewards the Ice Barbed Spear. The quest is usually to win a game of AV. However, the version of the quest used on Turtle WoW seems to be either an older or different version of the quest - unfortunately I'm not really sure of the specifics, and I'm simply going by a comparison to the details on WoWhead-Classic.

This means that rather than the more reasonable task of winning / losing a game, the quest is instead asking you to defeat Korrak the Bloodrager, a powerful NPC at the Snowfall Graveyard - that one area in the middle of the map that **no-one** ever goes to.

I'll be the first to admit that my 10 or 20 games of AV played so far are a comparatively small drop in the ocean compared to what some players - including myself, have put in - and I know that I'll never complete the quest if I don't at least try, and I intend to keep on trying. I haven't really been keeping account, but I've levelled up from 51 to 52 killing mobs in the 5 minutes between games, so it's been a good number of attempts. It's not as though I dislike PvP either - I raked up hundreds of thousands of HKs in my time playing the game years ago and I look forward to participating in it further.

However, it's become clear that AV even here in Turtle WoW has become very much a choreographed routine of capping the right graveyards and towers and working as a semi-cohesive group, and unfortunately that group's interests fall outside killing the NPC needed for this quest 99.99% of the time. Short of forming a dedicated pre-made group (that would have to be rather sizeable and well-geared, as Korrak is a rather tough cookie), I doubt I'll to see this quest unless I get lucky with a group of random strangers in PvP. Unfortunately, that simply isn't very likely to happen. Most well-geared individual are beyond needing the quest complete, or have already sufficiently ranked up their reputation and no longer require it even for that.

I'm fortunate in that I'm playing Alliance, which has about a 95% win-rate in the battleground if my experience so far is anything to go by. Even then, with such favourable odds, I haven't been able to coerce enough people to help me. The Horde, I imagine, have an even less fun time with this quest as they're very swiftly forced into a spot where they have to avoid the middle of the map.

I don't doubt some of you out there have done this quest, and seeing the occasional person with them has compelled me to keep trying and convinced me that it's possible. But that's also me just be being horribly optimistic; I've not been playing long and I don't have the clout, resources or connections to do much else besides group up with others looking to do the quest (also in AV) and gauge interest on completing the quest. But it's a long, long shot.

So, there lies my suggestion; if the quest could be revised to mirror its quest on Classic WoW. to a more reasonable goal of winning a match. If it isn't, so be it - and if anyone else is in the same boat in game, give Reneya a whisper sometime and we can group up to attempt it.
