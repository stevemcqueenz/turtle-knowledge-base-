# Shaman Ghost wolf tanking

## Post 54070 by Regaldandin — 2023-07-19T15:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54070#p54070 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Here we go lads,Vanilla Themed Shaman tanks

 31 point talent for enhance - Dire Ghost wolf

- Casting Dire Ghost wolf uses your air and water totems, using any air or water totem ability will bring you out of Dire ghost wolf form

- Dire ghost wolf **can** be cast indoors

- Armor contribution from items **increased by 180%**

- While in Dire Ghost wolf form, each point of spirit grants 0.25 defense, 0.15 spell hit, and 1 stamina

- While in Dire ghost wolf form, you cannot block, instead your block chance is converted to parry chance

- each point of shield block value on your shield is converted to 35 armor

- All instant cast spells can be used in dire ghost wolf form

- Your auto attacks still function the same as in humanoid form

- Your shield spells mana costs are reduced by 75%

- While in Dire ghost wolf form, the following abilities become new abilities that are usable in Dire ghost wolf form
Stormstrike - Stormbite Code: Select all

```
Instant yellow attack, your next 2 nature spells taunt all targets hit for 0.5 seconds
```

Chain lightning - Howl of Lightning Code: Select all

```
No cast time, deals 50% damage, all damage done causes 350% threat
```
