# Suggestions for Holy Paladin (& Alchemy) from a Holy Paladin (& Alchemist)

## Post 173610 by Fionthatch — 2026-04-01T12:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173610#p173610 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

While the new patch had a lot of new content and good changes, I cant shake the impression of feeling a bit left out as a Healer, especially Holy Paladin (& Alchemist). Im on Ambershire and maybe other alchemists and different healing classes, like Shamans, can relate.

Here is what I mean, including some suggestions:

Some new **cooking** recipes have been added (for "variety"), especially with regard to fishing. However, there is still nothing viable for Healers (apart from Herbal Salad which is not available on Ambershire any time soon).
 - Add some *food with healing power, spell crit or high Int*.
 - Since there is food for ranged attack power, there could also be food adding spell power of a certain school (fire, frost etc.).

Some new **seeds** and **gardening produce** have been added and its good new stuff, also for leveling. However, some buffs are just the same as the buffs from other foods and there is also nothing viable for Healers here again, apart from the old +10 Int Watermelon.
 **- Add some *Seeds/gardening produce (olives?)* with healing power or spell crit, or just some more Int, like 15/20.**

Im really happy that TWoW added a **PvP Plate Healer Set for Holy Paladins**. However, for PvE/raiding its slighty worse than the PvE gear, which is, of course, understandable, but its still not good enough for PvP, because the Set bonuses are just copies of the Retribution Paladin PvP Set (PvP Holy Paladin wont use Judgment of the Crusadar a lot, etc.). The solution would be simple:
 - Change the Healer Set bonuses to something useful. I know Stamina/HoJ/recuded crit & dot is, generally speaking, good for PvP, but Crusader seems off and, in general, it lacks variety. You could add Int, exclusive Healing Power, Spell Crit or some On Equip effect like: "*Your healing spells have a chance to increase your spell crit by X for Y seconds.*"
 - Add a *PvP Healer Shield*.

AFAIK, there has been **no new recipes for Alchemy**. I would have betted a lot on there being one of these new Upgrade Recipes (like with the blacksmith weapons, engineering goggle or leatherworking upgrades) for the Alchemists Stone. **The Alchemists Stone** (which barely dropps anyway) has no real use for raiding apart for some niche situations for maybe Tanks or Warlocks.
 - Add an upgrade recipe for the *Alchemists Stone* with either several upgrades (like in TBC) or the obvious one with added *Healing power or spell crit or Int*. Also, add the Recipe: Alchemists Stone to the random boss loot table of ZG.
 - Add some *Elixir/Potion with healing power* (you could then remove healing power from Dreamshard Elixir, and add a *Concoction* for these two). *Groms Blood, Plaguebloom, Essence of Life* could work as mats as they are underused at this point.

While the new **Survival** is way better than before, Im asking myself what I actually use of it now that I have skilled it to 300, apart from the gathering trinkets for Black Lotus and Arcane Crystal. It is a nice touch that you can use healing salves in combat and with no shared cooldowns from other items (though I doubt that people will use it), so why not:
 - Add a *Mana Salve* which is also useable in combat and does not share the cooldown of other items (like mana potion, tea, rune).
 - Add an Item with high CD which *Reduces CD of Transmutes*
 - There is still a lot of room for useful items.

 **Generally**, there are zero new items for Holy Paladins, neither from the new reputation faction(s) nor from the loot of the new bosses in Onyxias Lair/BWL. I dont think thats a catastrophe, but its a bit boring, especially since TWoW tried to make plate healer gear viable. We only have few selected items (which, thus, everybody tries to get), but maybe there could be some more variety, like:
 - Add a new *healer shield* with a lot of armor to capitalize on Ironclad, and distinguish it from Tank shields by adding exclusive healing power, spell crit and/or Int or an On Equip effect comparable to Daybreak or T1-8 or T2-5/8 bonuses.
 - Add a new *Libram* for Holy Paladins. We only got Divinity (flash of light + healing power).
 - Maybe add a funny 2 hand weapon with exclusive use for healers (see above for ideas for stats/on equip).
 - Im not a BS, but *Dreamsteel* seems underused and could work for Plate Healer items.
 - Add a new enchant like *Shield: Spell Crit* or just *Shield: Intelligence*
 - and so on

I apologize if some of these suggestions have already been posted (like the PvP one). This is my personal impression after playing WoW since release and TWoW for a year now and I know that I am biased to Holy Paladin and Alchemy. Either way, maybe some people can relate to some of my suggestions and extrapolate on different ideas. Thanks for reading & greetings from Ambershire!
