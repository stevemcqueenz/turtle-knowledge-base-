# Moving Forward Together: Expectations, Trust, and Roadmap

## Post 163344 by Wyrdo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-20T11:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163344#p163344 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

First of all, thank you to the Turtle team for the time, effort, and passion invested in the project over the years. This message is written with respect and with the sincere intention of contributing constructively to the future of the server.

What is being expressed here—perhaps quietly, but very widely—is what many people are thinking: if TurtleWoW becomes TurtleCraft, and if the UE5 project is now abandoned, it is difficult not to draw a connection with Blizzard’s lawsuit. Turtle is clearly trying to survive by keeping a lower profile, which is an understandable approach. Nevertheless, the disappointment is real and profound for a significant part of the community.

Many players chose this server primarily because of UE5. It was a major differentiating factor compared to other private servers, each with its own strengths. Many came here precisely because Turtle was the only one proposing such an ambition. Some invested a great deal of hope in it, and some also supported the project financially with that goal in mind.

From this disappointment arises an obvious risk: a gradual loss of players in favor of servers perceived as more stable, more transparent, and more reliable in the long term.

If Turtle wishes both to continue existing and to retain its player base, restoring trust in the short term becomes essential. Several elements could contribute to this:

First, a gesture of recognition toward players who experience this announcement as a significant setback could be considered—for example, through a reflection on the in-game shop or other forms of symbolic compensation.

Second, the community now expects a new roadmap that is clear, realistic, and concrete, published in a timely manner, so that players can meaningfully envision the future of the server.

Third, without any intention of placing excessive pressure on the team, the next patch will be particularly important. The additions will need to be solid and engaging, and the PvP aspect—especially on Tel’Abim—would benefit from no longer being relegated to the background, at the risk of seeing player interest gradually erode.

It is also worth noting that this is not the first time the community has seen promising projects put on hold or quietly abandoned. The previously teased Scarlet raid is a notable example that remains vivid in many players’ minds. It would be greatly appreciated to see such projects revisited, clarified, or updated, even if only to confirm their current status or long-term feasibility.

Moreover, this announcement also highlights the remarkable work of the many community modders, who have greatly contributed to improving the game’s visuals over the years. Their efforts deserve recognition and take on even greater meaning in the current context.

Turtle still has a major asset: an invested, creative, and loyal community that only asks to be reassured in order to continue engaging with the project over the long term.

Once again, thank you to the Turtle team for your continued work and dedication. We sincerely hope that the expectations and concerns expressed by the community will be heard and thoughtfully taken into consideration.
