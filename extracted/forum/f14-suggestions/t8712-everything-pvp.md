In original vanilla, when there were constant large-scale skirmishes between Tarren Mill and Southshore or at Crossroads on a PVE server, players were deciding to join the battle for the love of PVP, or when either side gathered an army to siege a faction's capital to kill its leader, those were spectacular moments I will never forget. Most of these experiences happened before BGs. They and the Honor Point system forever ruined genuine open-world epic-level PVP.

I have YET to see this kind of gameplay on any private and official Classic server. The problem is it's vanilla with today's mindset. Players today aren't the players from vanilla in 2004/05, and so, as a result, the cadence of a PVP server is significantly different today than it was back then. Today, it's about HK and gearing. But back then, it was about the sheer joy of it.
