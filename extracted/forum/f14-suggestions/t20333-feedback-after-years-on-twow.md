# Feedback after years on twow

## Post 139988 by Tbcb — 2025-07-16T21:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139988#p139988 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear twow-team,

i know this is probably just the next "my feedback"-posts, but i am bored and maybe i catch a spark with multiple others. To understand my feedback better, first something about me:
I startet playing on twow 4 (?) years ago and pretty much started playing instant hc and fell in love. Long story short: I did some pvp in between, but that is not my thing and i am100% into hardcore now. All my feedback is based on hc leveling and raiding experience. With that said, lets start:

 1.) New talents are so nice
I leveled warrior 3 years ago, now boomie druid, frostbrandweapon shaman and now on my way to 60 on arcane mage. The new talents are pretty cool and i really like them in the hc-pve-enviroment. Well done. For me you really improved classic trees and kept the identity. I hope you work on rogue, that class is pure garbo atm. On boomi i can just request to change one of the damage talents to increase crit chance. So much is based on crit-triggers and it is sad for level experience. Keep it up :)

 2.) JC is op
I like a higher powerlevel. Even if i play HC, i enjoy a casual and chill evening after an exhausting day. The additional items are overall okay, but some pieces are just too much. I cant log in and check the names, but there is a ~lvl 30 staff with too high dps (easy to craft, dps of a blue dungeon weapon) and the lvl 51 stam/str/agi ring is also just too much. Compare it to painweaver band from ubrs. Mabye i dont get something here, idk.

 3.) Custom areas are fun
I like that the quest are not pinpointing to the location. The original quests are more like "to west, there you are, gratz". It is harder with the new quests and they have more lore. I really like it. New areas are a solid 10/10 and way less buggy than 3 year ago =D Also thanks for enlargening the dungeons.

 4.) The levelexperience is dead - sorry
I like all the new content, the changes, the quests but the levle experience is dead. On my first hc char everything, every cave, every item surrounded by 4 mobs was a problem and an adventure. Now you have to fight over mobs to get shit done. Kunzenquest with the item at the fireplace? Easy, everything is dead anyways. Getting into a cave? Biggest problem is that respawn might kick in simultaneously. You get worldbuffs ALL THE TIME. I could solve the last thing on my own, but not the rest. Please give us the adventure of hc leveling back with a hc-only-server. It does not add in any way that we mix normie and hc.

So far: Awesome work overall, i gonna donate with each new char created :) Give me a reason to level rogue next!!!
