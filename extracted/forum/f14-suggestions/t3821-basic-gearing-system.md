# Basic Gearing System

## Post 23042 by Bricius — 2022-08-21T17:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23042#p23042 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For starters I do in fact realize this is likely not going to be a liked idea by some, particularly those in the more hardcore raiding scenes. Second, this is not entirely in line with what Classic WoW had done, but its not out of line either based on what we see from raids like AQ 20/40 and ZG. Lastly, I know this doesn't hit everyone, more or less just those interested in PvE and it does miss a portion of the players who are just here for the Roleplay. Those points out of the way, on with the show--

As a suggestion for gearing I'd like there to be some form of currency system not too unlike what was done in late Classic/BC onwards. I feel like here with how the devs are so involved with the game and its community though it can be better managed. For each boss you beat in a 'tier' of content you should be given a token to spend at a vendor or have a repeatable turn in quest (so you have to gather other reagents to make it into gear) so that players can work towards getting something. There is nothing more painful or annoying then being denied entry into a raid because you don't have a specific 2-set or specific item that vastly helps you do your role to keep up with other classes.
Fire Mages are abandoned in Molten Core because everything is immune to fire and most things in Blackwing Lair are resistant or immune as well or have some form of reduced damage from only for it to turn around and be a good spec when they get to AQ or ZG or Naxx. Druids have some growing pains, especially in the Balance Spec (which by the way TWoW has done a great job improving, its still not the best of solutions yet with some of its quirks), and only become moderately good when they have specific set bonuses from obscure or late game sets that they'd have trouble getting to anyways because the early game sets can't be reached when they get denied from entry as a Moonkin.

To this point I am not saying that a class overhaul is needed or a way the numbers system works or how any spec fills a niche. Bears should still be amazing single target tanks, Assassination Rogues can still can still DoT the hell out of a target to keep up with the Combat Rogues without worry about the debuff cap (now that it  has been expanded (though I think this should be removed as the original reason why it was a thing is original wow's engine couldn't handle the unlimited debuffs, which TWoW clearly could handle I feel)), fire mages can still be ignored in Molten Core. But a token system for these classes so they can gear up an alternate spec quickly for either the next level of raid in their progression would be amazing so players didn't get forced into playing spec's they didn't like, and they didn't have to start from square one and work an uphill battle from basic dungeons back through the raids or get special permission to have entire sets of gear reserved for just them for the next three weeks while everyone else who shows up gets nothing at all.
A minor addition to this point by the way -- If this does become a thing (or even if it doesn't) can we see more spec sets available? Back to the point of druids, their only set bonuses are for healing except for a single one in (I think) ZG for boomkin, which doesn't help a great deal. If I remember right Paladins and a few other classes also suffer from this.

The final portion of this, the gear does not need to be over powered, BiS, or as strong if not better then the tier its being spent on. Even half as good as the drops or slightly weaker is fine so that you can participate and be excited about upgrades, but not so much to the point where you have to rely entirely on this gear and ignore it later. Something to bridge the gap so you can keep up with team mates.
