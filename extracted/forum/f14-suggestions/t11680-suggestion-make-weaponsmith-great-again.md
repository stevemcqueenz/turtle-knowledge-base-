## Post 77157 by Noob — 2023-11-13T10:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77157#p77157 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, would the following suggestion be welcome ?

Blacksmithing have some specialisation, on a commercial point of view, issues. Meaning :
Armorsmith have a lots of specialisation pattern to sell and are usefull (either dps or tank)
Weaponsmith ... (axesmith at least) have 2 usefull pattern for raids (annihilator and nightfall),  while swordsmith have a niche one (dream herald and lets say dark iron reaver for RF fury or the 2% bonus speed...) and macesmith ... well the new kara pattern...

What i see here is a bit of unfairness.

Would it be nice to add a new content about it.
One of choice would be bring "similar" stuff to each spec, which will kill the aspect of the specialisations i believe.
So what could be done?
What about something a bit RP like, a weaponsmith earning a passiv of weaponskill for this specialisation like +3 for axe for an axesmith (...) or an equivalent BOP bonus of this.
To balance things with armorsmith there could be an armor efficiency bonus.

This suggestion might help most of close combats tank and dps while still beeing RP without breaking that much the balance.
