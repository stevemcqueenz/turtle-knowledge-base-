Beastmaster:

can use pet to do tank roles. (turtle, bear, etc)

improved eyes of the beast:

can be channeled indefinitely. can use pet's ability while channeling.

intimitaiton:
threat genearation increasing works on raid boss.

Spikey shell(new):
can be used as pet's ability.
passive: your pet's basic threat regeneration rate increases x %. your pet do aoe damge to nearby enemy every x seconds.
Massive: Your pet deal x-y damage to nearby enemy Immediately.

wild potential(new):
your pet's ability effect increase x %.

master's call (same level as baited shot in talent tree) :
can be used while channeling eyes of the beast. Your pet's health regeneration rate increases x % and damage taken is reduced x % for x seconds. Focus regeneration rate increases for x seconds. x min cooldown.

improved feign death(in survival talent tree):
reduce the chance...
allow you to keep channeling eyes of the beast while doing and after doing feign death.
If it's not be resisted, you cannot be selected during feigned death (only marked as a corpse). Area damage taken while channeling feign death is reduced by X %. （inspired by Rexxar's talent, Heroes of the storm）

Marksmanship & Survival:
develop more gameplay mechanics than the difference between range/melee.

hunter can learn new skill arrow-making/bullet-making at lv40.
for example:
poisoned arrow (lesser) x200 : Jagged Arrow x 200 + Large Venom Sac
poisoned arrow (superior) x200: Thorium Headed Arrow x 200 + Huge Venom Sac
deal extra nature damage. cause posion effect on target. last for  x seconds. can stack up to x times.

fire arrow (lesser) x200: Jagged Arrow x 200 + Oil of Immolation
fire arrow (superior) x200: Thorium Headed Arrow x 200 + element fire
deal extra fire damage. Ignite the target, dealing X- Y fire damage over X seconds.

ice arrow (lesser) x200: Jagged Arrow x 200 + Frost Oil
ice arrow (superior) x200: Thorium Headed Arrow x 200 + element water
deal extra frost damage. reduce x % of target's attack and move speed for x seconds.

dark arrow (lesser) x200: Jagged Arrow x 200 + Shadow Oil
dark arrow (superior) x200: Thorium Headed Arrow x 200 + essence of death
deal extra shadow damage. if target died in x seconds after being dealt damage, summon its spirit to fight for you. las x seconds. only one spirit can be summoned at any time.

silver arrow x200: Jagged Arrow x 200 + silver bar
true silver arrow x200: Thorium Headed Arrow x 200 + truesilver bar
deal extra divine damage. When dealing damage to a target, you and your party member have a chance of restoring health.

arcane arrow x200: Jagged Arrow x 200 + XXX
arcane arrow (superior) x200: Thorium Headed Arrow x 200 + XXX
deal extra arcane damage. When dealing damage to a target, you and your party member have a chance of restoring mana.

ability:

envenomed shot: （need range weapon, posion arrow）
Inflicts X-Y nature damage on the target immediately. Inflicts additional damage based on the number of poison arrow effect stacks applied. Inflicts additional damage based on the number of natural damage effects present on the target. (benefit from serpent sting (hunter), poison(rogue), insect swarm(druid), etc)

bursting shot: (need range weapon, fire arrow)
fire at target, Inflicts X-Y fire damage upon the target and X units surrounding it. If targets is already affected by a fire damage effect, targets are disoriented for x seconds. For each different type of fire damage effect inflicted upon the target, the duration is extended by x seconds. (benefit from fireball (mage), immolate (warlock), immolation trap/explosive trap (hunter), etc)

blessed shot: (need range weapon, silver arrow/truesilver arrow)
Can only be cast on friendly targets. Instantly restores X-Y health to the target. The healing effectiveness target received is increased by X% for X seconds. the effectiveness increases extra x % for each different types of healing effects or spells on target. x min cool down. （entertaining...）

elemental shot: (need range weapon) / elemental strike: (need melee weapon)
Inflicts X-Y physical damage immediately upon the target. plus additional damage based on the number of different types of damage effects present on the target. For each distinct type of damage effect present(fire, frost, nature, divine, shadow, physical, 7 maximum), an additional X-Y points of damage are dealt. If the damage effects were inflicted by you, the extra damage increases X % Y times for Y type of damage effect you applied.

iron sting:
Reduces the target's armour by X points and deals X points of physical damage over X seconds. Each time you land a critical strike, this duration is extended by X seconds.

hornet sting:
Reduces the target's fire resistance by X points. Inflicts X-Y points of fire damage over X seconds. Each time you land a spell critical strike, this duration is extended by X seconds.

Combustion Strike (need melee weapon):
Inflicts X-Y points of fire damage on the target immediately. This damage is enhanced by your spell power. Increases all fire damage taken by the target from all sources by x%. last x seconds. x seconds cool down.

talent:

marksmanship:

wild guard:
When your pet is within 5 yards of you, your ranged attack power increases by X, and your accuracy when using ranged weapons increases by X%, critical strke chance increase by x%. All damage you dealt is increased by x%.

envenomed weapon:
The maximum stack count of the damage effect inflicted by poisoned arrows is increased to X stacks. The natural damage you deal is increased by X. Your spell power is increased by X.

survival:

trap specialization:
You and your pet deal x% increased damage to targets affected by trap effects. If the trap effect was applied by you, this damage is further increased by x%. You and your pet deal X% increased damage to immobile targets.

Cooperative hunting：
When your pet is within 5 yards of you, both you and your pet gain X% increased melee attack power. Your hit rating increases by x%, and your critical strike chance increases by x%.

Short-range sting:
stings can be used in melee range.

two-hand weapon specialization：(polearm, two-hand axe, etc)
Your melee attack power increases by X. When wielding two-handed weapons, upon landing a critical hit, there is a chance to inflict ongoing damage to the target equal to X% of your weapon damage.

Dual-Wielding Specialisation：
Your attack speed increases by X%. Upon landing a critical hit, your attack speed further increases by X% for X seconds, and you gain a chance to restore X health.

Will to survive:
When you feign death, you immediately regain X-Y health and recover an amount equivalent to X% of your maximum health over X seconds.

primitive technique：
The fire damage you deal is increased by X%. Your spell power is increased by X. Your spell critical strike chance is increased by X%.
