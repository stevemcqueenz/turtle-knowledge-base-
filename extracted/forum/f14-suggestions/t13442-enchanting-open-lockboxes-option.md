The core game mechanics aren't balanced around hardcore. Mages already can do so many things other classes can't, there was no need to give them this ability. They have a very developed toolkit, you could argue their spellbook looks like it belongs to a TBC character.

Back to my point, lockpicking is a trademark thief ability of rogues that makes them special and allows them to make a little extra money or simply give them an opportunity to sometimes be useful to their friends/guildmates. Besides enchanting already is one of if not *the* strongest profession in the game and it doesn't need such additions.

If you want to go that route and give access to lockpicking or any other abilities via a profession, weaker professions should be prioritized (maybe make crafting keys with blacksmithing more efficient). Though again in my opinion lockpicking is in a good state just the way it is now and I wouldn't change anything about it.
