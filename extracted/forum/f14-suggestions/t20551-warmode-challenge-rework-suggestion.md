# Warmode Challenge rework suggestion

## Post 142078 by Annihilaki (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-29T12:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142078#p142078 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings fellow turtles!

Before I proceed with my suggestions, I wanna first ask the devs: What is truly your vision/goal behind the Warmode challenge?

From a new player's perspective (*mine and two of my mates'*), the idea of Warmode was pretty straightforward: Get to fight with other people leveling while leveling, while also occasionally being ganged by 60s (it wouldn't be WoW without it!).
The xp boost reward baffled us at first, but we thought it was there just to entice more people to enable this challenge, thus promoting a lot more of world pvp engagement.

But as we played more and more, we begun to doubt the true reason behind this challenge - it really started to feel it was not actually there for promoting organic world pvp, but more to appeal to some kind of cat and mouse game, that of *****the bully and the bullied*****.
Gangers would always reply with "You can always turn Warmode off" or "Warmode tax", but then again they would say anything to excuse their unsolicited bullying.
The real tooltip of this challenge is:
 ***In reward for 20% more exp, you will have to do more corpse runs.***

Add on top the Level 1 Lunatic Challenge, with ***level 1 goblins running havoc*** in Elwynn, and the design choice behind this challenge becomes even more questionable.

 *****Our suggestions:*****

 *****No auto PvP-flag while in your faction's capitals, while in Warmode*****
---
There is no reason to be flagged while in a city, other than to get ganged by lvl 60 griefers. Capitals are supposed to be a place you can turn your mind off and go about your chores while partially afk. In official PvP servers, you would always be ***pvp flag disabled*** by default in cities. This was also true in later iterations of "Warmode" in retail.

 *****Reduce extra exp in Warmode / Replace extra xp in Warmode with other rewards*****
---
Currently, a lot of the players enable it only for the extra xp, without even wanting to engage in world pvp. Best solution would be to replace the exp bonus with rewards similar to those of ***Slow and Steady*** (maybe more pvp themed rewards? Insignia-like rewards?). As an extra bonus, this will destroy the griefers' "Warmode Tax" claim.

 *****Reduce Level 1 Lunatic Challenge Honorable Kills requirement*****
---
Make the required kills achievement much smaller, but make it count only for *****unique*****  player kills. Right now, as much as you want to hate the gangers, they themselves must keep ganging the same people if they ever want to even have a chance of attaining that comically high amount (i think it's 10k HKs?). Which makes the new players' experience feel awful in return.
---

...or at the very least, rename the challenge to something more akin to ***"Lamb to the Slaughter"***.
Because this challenge really makes you feel more of a civilian in wartime rather than an actual participant/combatant.

Thank you for your read, see you in game!
