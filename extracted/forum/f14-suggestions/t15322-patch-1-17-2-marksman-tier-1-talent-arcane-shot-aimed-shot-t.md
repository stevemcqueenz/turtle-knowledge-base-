# [patch 1.17.2] MarksMan - tier 1 talent + arcane shot / aimed shot talent

## Post 104621 by Tomavidsquera — 2024-10-24T01:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104621#p104621 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

It really feels like a missed opportunity to put the trueshot as a talent point.
In my point of view it should be like it was and all the inpiration from BC ; a normal spell that a hunter can learn along its journey.

The sad part of the MarksMan hunter and all the stings is that they dont really have any scaling (cant crit on tick for the serpent sting for example or be linked to the attack power).

 **Suggestion for the tier 1 key stone.**
Why cant we just swap the tier 1 talent from MarksMan to allow all hunters to learn the spell like it was and just add something that could be called : " sting mastery ".
This new talent would allow :
 - Serpent sting to crit on it based on your critical chance and/or allow it to scale on the attack power of the hunter.
 - Viper sting to give the mana drain from the target to the hunter (having some kind of regen).
 - Scorpid sting augment the chance to crit on the target afflicted by it for the duration.

This way it would make all the stings usefull in all the scenario possible Pve / Pvp and transform some " deads " spell into really good options ; also making each choice a game changer.

img : <https://ibb.co/TrdCBKy>

 **Suggestion for the arcane shot / aimed shot talents**
The talent has been a meme and sadly still is.
Why dont we make something very unique here to give a real identity to the MarksMan.
Multiple possibilities :

Option 1 -> Reduce the global CD generated by the arcane shot by 33% (3 points needed to totally removing it from the GCD) ;
Also reduce the casting speed and mana cost of the aimed shot when you crit on ranged attack by 33%. (can stack up 3 times, making it instant cast and free when you've 3 stacks and consuming them.

Option 2 -> Arcane shot has 33% chance on hit to remove a buff from the target if the arcane shot crit it steals it from the target ;
Aimed shot 33% to disarm the target, on crit increase the mana regen for a duration. (for both of the option 2 effect, 3 points needed to make it 100%)

Option 3 -> Arcane shot has 33% chance to give a stack on stack that reduce the casting speed of aimed shot ; Aimed shot has 33% on crit to free cast an Arcane shot out of GCD. (like a double shot, the arcane shot cant crit).

img : <https://ibb.co/L1ywBSm>

All these suggestions are meant to make the hunter rotation in raid having more flavours than just auto > trueshot > auto > multi ... based on procs etc ... you'll have to time stuff.
