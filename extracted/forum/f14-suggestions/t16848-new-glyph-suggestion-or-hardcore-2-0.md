# New glyph suggestion (or Hardcore 2.0)

## Post 115168 by Alexanriard — 2025-01-01T23:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115168#p115168 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As we know, the whole story with hardcore started with a simple user modification. The creators could not somehow reconfigure the gameplay to make hardcore even more difficult and interesting, and therefore we have what we have - a simple rule "don't die" and some restrictions, such as a ban on using the AH.

Today, a player who decides to start playing hardcore gets a very truncated version of the game. He cannot fully trade, he cannot go to PVP, he will not explore the world and do difficult quests, because, as we know, "don't go to caves" and all that.

My idea for hardcore 2.0 is a plague.

 1. We abandon the concept of "1 life". It's not fun.
 2. When choosing a new glyph, a debuff is applied to the player, similar to the debuff after spirit healer resurrection  i.e. - 60% (for example) to all stats, health, damage, etc.
 3. No need for a separate server - regular players, joining a group with plague characters, receive a similar temporary debuff, as long as the players are in the same group (in the case of battlegrounds, the restriction also applies to opponents).
 4. It can be lore-integrated, with final "healing" quest at 60.

What is the advantage of this mode over the current "hardcore"? Firstly, all aspects of the game are open to the player. He can freely trade with anyone he wants, use the AH, explore the world, do dangerous quests, play PVP (except wPvP, here we're keeping current state). Secondly, players are not divided into hardcore and non-hardcore. A common community that has no restrictions on interaction. Thirdly, the game will become truly difficult, players will need to cooperate as much as possible for difficult quests, and prepare for elite quests and dungeons as if they were raids. It will be vital to strengthen yourself as much as possible at each level, and low-level enchantments and consumables will be relevant as never before. But at the same time, players will not be afraid to take risks, because failure will not mean losing everything.

These changes will provide the very challenge in leveling that players need, without the artificial restrictions imposed by the current "hardcore". And if desired, this can be extended to the endgame.

What do you think?
