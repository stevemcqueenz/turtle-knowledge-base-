## Post 24268 by Kwayver (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-09-03T01:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24268#p24268 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it's important to understand what the Librams were originally designed to do in wow, and what their limitations are given how they work.
Librams (and all relics) modify a spell (ideally someone's favourite spell) to do something else out something extra to compliment a build in some way. They also need to be balanced against other classes and relics. This is much easier to do at level 55+ when the class toolkit has pretty much been established. These relics must also not be too overpowered that they outperform max level Librams as coming back to get them at a higher level defeats the challenge associated with acquiring them considering their value to the player.
I would say level 40 is a good time to introduce relics to the player as that's the same level final row talents are available. There's a handful of rank 1 abilities above that level but nothing too iconic or build dependent.
