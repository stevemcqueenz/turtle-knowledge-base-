# Enchanters Craft More Wands

## Post 53249 by Learngoat — 2023-07-13T02:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53249#p53249 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tailors lose out on money because every item made sells for less than the reagents, but Enchanting can turn them into wands and oils, which sells for more than the reagents. However, Enchanting does not have any wand to make after level 30, which means this option to help make a profit is only for the early game.

It would help Tailor/Enchanters earn their mounts for the later game if they had a new progression of wands to make and sell after level 30.
