## Post 133703 by Nost2 — 2025-05-30T18:54:00Z

I was recently muted for saying something political in world chat. I understand I clearly broke the rules listed in TOS and am not looking to debate the rules. But it seems exceptionally punishing to have a 24 hour mute preventing me from any whispers, party chat, emotes, etc.

It makes it impossible to organise running dungeons and finding parties, especially as tank, I can't direct dps to not do things that will wipe us or myself ask for guidance in a dungeon. I can't ask to be invited to someone looking for roles for their dungeon run. I can't thank people for random acts of kindness and so it leads to them thinking im an A-hole. I can't reply to whispers from friends.

Imo breaking the rules of world chat should mean a ban from world chat, not from all other forms of communication as it's essentially a soft ban on engaging in the game. The punishment doesn't really fit the crime, I wasn't harassing any individual or being aggressive towards anyone.
