Just as the title says. Transmogging is painful to say the least. I get the idea of "Sacrifice for fashion" and I understand them being hard to obtain while leveling.

But I don't understand why at max level transmogging is STILL a pain in the ass and even HARDER than earlier levels.
I feel that Turtle, as an **RP Server**(even thats debatable now...), should have a transmogging system that isnt an utter pain.

I would like to Re-Iterate, I understand it being expensive and hard to obtain **WHILE LEVELING**
I know you can farm Coins at 60 doing lvl 60 dungeons, but perhaps it could drop more often from dungeons? Maybe a guranteed drop per boss instead of like 1 per dungeon?

Thank you,
Sincerely, a Transmog addict.
