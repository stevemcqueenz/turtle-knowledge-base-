# Kul Tiras Faction Additions and Changes

## Post 20780 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-01T05:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20780#p20780 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, with the most recent patch, the "Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet" faction originally based out of Caelan's Rest was renamed to simply "Kul Tiras". An interesting change, which I'm hopeful will mean a greater inclusion of Kul Tiras in Turtle WoW.

With that change however, there's a few things I would like to suggest:

- Remove the <Kul Tiras> title from the relevant NPCs in Caelan's Rest, Anchor's Edge and Davenport. It's a bit redundant for them to have a reputation faction tag saying "Kul Tiras" along with the title saying <Kul Tiras>.
To further my point, this is what the tooltip currently looks like:
> Marine John Doe
>  <Kul Tiras>
>  Level 15 Humanoid (Elite)
>  Kul Tiras

This is what I believe it should look like:
> Marine John Doe
>  Level 15 Humanoid (Elite)
>  Kul Tiras

- Change the Tiragarde Keep NPCs in Durotar (Lieutenant Benedict, Watch Commander Zalaphil, Kul Tiras Marine, Kul Tiras Sailor) from being tagged with the Stormwind faction to being tagged with the Kul Tiras faction. When I checked I saw that they were still tagged with Stormwind. If Kul Tiras is to be its own separate faction, may as well put them all under the same tag.

- Shorten the faction description slightly. It is currently extremely wordy and actually doesn't quite fit the textbox. My suggestion would be:

> Masters of the seas and retainers of Azeroth's finest navy, the merchant nation-island of Kul Tiras is now one of the last standing human kingdoms.
