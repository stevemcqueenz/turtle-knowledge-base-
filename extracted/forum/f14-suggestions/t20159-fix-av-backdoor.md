# Fix AV Backdoor

## Post 138113 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-04T22:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138113#p138113 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please fix backdoor to Alliance base in Alterac Valley.

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/f08-SkOJee0]

Any Warrior/Druid in Horde and any engineer with Goblin Rocket Helmet can charge to one NPC on the cliff. This allows them to get to the Alliance base without crossing the bridge. Dun Baldar was designed to be accessible only by crossing the bridge. This backdoor trivializes the conquest of base and gives Horde advantage. Today I saw a guy who used this backdoor and later summoned whole team to Aid Station.

Please remove NPCs that stand on the cliff or place invisible wall that prevents people from charging to these NPCs.
