# Fixing Fire: Making It Work in Kara40 and Beyond

## Post 158852 by Bolthardrok — 2025-11-08T05:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158852#p158852 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Fire has always been kind of clunky and inconsistent, but back in earlier content like Naxx and AQ40, it worked mainly because everyone played Fire. With pretty much every mage running the same spec, Ignite rolling was easy to maintain, and older raids didn’t really have much in the way of complex mechanics, target switching, or movement. That made the spec’s problems a lot less noticeable at the time.

Now with Kara40 and probably future raids being a lot more mechanic-heavy, add/target switching, and decurse heavy, those issues are really starting to show. Fire struggles to keep Ignite up, loses a ton of DPS when target switching, decursing, immune phases, etc. and just feels frustrating to play in the current raid environment in Kara 40.

Here are a few ideas that could help smooth things out and make Fire more consistent without overbuffing it:

 1. Make Ignites personal.

 2. Revert Ignite to the old 6-second duration so solo Ignite rolling doesn’t feel so punishing.

 3. Allow Scorch to stack up to 3 times so it’s easier to get things rolling again during fights with lots of target swapping.

 4. Let Ignite stack up to 3 times instead of 5 at around 65–70% per crit (instead of 40%). This keeps total damage close to what it is now but helps it ramp back up faster after dropping off in mechanic-heavy fights.

 5. Make Combustion a passive effect that resets to zero instead of ending after three crits, so it feels smoother and more reliable for solo Ignite rolling in fights with a lot going on.

Once the spec actually feels good to play under these raid conditions, the numbers can always be adjusted afterward for balance.

Curious what other Fire mages think about these ideas... especially those running Kara40. Do these changes line up with what you’ve been feeling too, or do you think there’s another direction that could help Fire keep up in the current raid design?

 -Ahrkon-
