# Enchanting wands recipes suggestions

## Post 28872 by Azkuel — 2022-11-09T23:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28872#p28872 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Lesser magic wand and greater magic wand are great recipes.

[https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=1 ... magic-wand](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=14293/lesser-magic-wand)
[https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=1 ... magic-wand](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=14807/greater-magic-wand)

The wands are great for priest and mage enchanters, but beyond that, they give a floor price for magic essences. Since you can vendor these wands for 5 and 15 silver respectively, the floor price for one lesser magic essence will be 5 silver.
This works because the price of simple wood is small and baked into the selling price of greater magic wands. (Simple wood costs 38 copper, greater magic wand sells for 15s35c). This feels like a good way to ground the price of the essence and reward disenchanting.

However this is not the case for mystic wands
[https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=1 ... ystic-wand](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=14809/lesser-mystic-wand)
[https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=1 ... ystic-wand](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=14810/greater-mystic-wand)

These wands sell for 35s81c and 52s63c respectively, however the price of star wood is 45s, subtracting that gives these wands a vendor price of -9s19c and 7s63c. These numbers don't really make sense given that you need higher level mats to craft these wands. I feel like the price of star wood should be 27s40c, in which case the vendor price of the wands would become 8s41c and 25s23c. This in tun would give lesser mystic essences a ground price floor of 8s41c. Which is better than 0 as it is today. This is merely a suggestion given if one wants to keep the original wand prices, however devs can use this mechanism to give any price they desire by also changing the price of the wands.

But that is it. There are no other wand recipes out there. It would be nice to perhaps have one wand recipe requiring each type of essence, similar to how dust can be used to craft oils. Thanks for reading my TED talk
