# Enhancement Shaman deep rework

## Post 106616 by steamachine1336 — 2024-11-06T00:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106616#p106616 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After the latest patch, many will agree that the enh shaman not only has not become better, but has somehow become even worse. Some blame it on nerfs to some of the enh shaman's key mechanics, others blame it on talents.Well I see the problem in the lack of a foundation, a certain core around which the entire spec would be built. It seems to me that before trying to balance it, like changing abilities, flurry, etc, the devs should highlight a certain core around which all the enh shaman mechanics would be built.
As far as I can tell, the original idea was to move away from no brain damage with WF towards using abilities, mechanics and procs. This was implemented perfectly in patches starting with WOTLK. But as we know, twow wants move on their own way. Lightning Strike was a great idea, as was giving ench shaman the lead in shield handling. But at the same time, subsequent attempts at balancing destroyed any semblance of rotation, tracking of procs, etc. We were made into a monkey, pressing 3 buttons on cooldown until we run out of mana. I think that before you start changing abilities for the sake of balance, you need to build an enh shaman around some core of abilities and mechanics, cooldowns and procs. And only then try to balance it. And the main thing in this is not to forget that the game does not start in Molten Core. The game starts somewhere in the crossroad, in wailing caverns, in dark halls with worgens, in fights with the scarlet crusade, as well as in countless locations with their quests. Since all these years the shaman was something that just hit you on the head with a stick and sometimes it procs a huge damage WF, then he didn't have any problems at any level. Or rather... you just hit with a stick. That's it. It's not hard to balance, right? If you want to add abilities, add mechanics, then you need to consider how it will work at all levels. And it's doesnt seems done yet.

And even if the DPS enh shaman looks like something that can be assembled into a single whole and made to work, then the shaman tank doesn't. Since Blizzard never fully implemented this spec, and what was there looked more like a sketch, we don’t have any understanding of what a shaman tank is. What is he? The warrior is a classic shield tank, based entirely on physical damage. We have the paladin, which is somewhat similar to the warrior, but is a hybrid semi-magic tank. He has his strengths, he has his weaknesses. He has a niche that he can fill. Same goes for the druid. They all have some kind of core around which their are built, their strengths and weaknesses. Shaman basically has none of that. The new patch did not bring any clarity to this. What is this, one more semi-magic tank with a shield? What is his purpose in raids? In dungeons? Where is his main set of abilities? All we've been told is that it's more suited for dungeon tanking. But what does that mean? We also don't have any special abilities in dungeons that somehow justify our existence. So basically, he's not finished. He's just incomplete, so he's not suitable for raids.
Yes, we now have a taunt and threat from our totems. And also new weapon ench, which in theory should help us. But we still don't have an understanding of what a tank shaman is. We still don't have any abilities that increase our avoidance. No save CD. Nothing.

All this is not an attempt to somehow devalue devs work, but simply an attempt to somehow direct this. Because the latest balance changes right before the patch rather showed that you have reached a dead end. I think we need something more substantial than just a taunt and minor changes to a couple of talents.

Well, since this is a suggestions channel, not whining, I will try to share some ideas that may help. And I advise everyone who is not indifferent to also share their ideas, rather than just whine and blame that your class is dead now

////
Long story of being short on mana

The mana problem is the main issue for both dps and tank. And right now we have exactly zero tools to get mana.
The water shield should have helped us somehow. But its usefulness is extremely questionable. The only one who can benefit from it is the tank. But there is one simple problem with him. As avoidance increases, the effectiveness of the water shield decreases. The more often we avoid damage, the more often we do not receive mana. Thus, we come to a fairly simple mechanic, which is currently implemented for all tanks. Namely, the encouragement of avoidance. At the moment, any tank, be it a paladin or a warrior, is rewarded for blocking/dodging/parry. Paladins gain mana from blocks, boosted by high block chance with talents and abilities. Warrs and druids gain rage after their dodge/parry/blocks. So the shaman needs his set of abilities that allow him to gain mana. Especially considering that we are still a support class. We have one of the highest mana costs. Totems are insanely expensive. Even with the WF totem changes and the mechanics associated with it, we still spend an awful lot of mana. My proposal is quite simple. Since the shaman does not have any abilities that significantly increase the chance of blocking, the method implemented for the paladin does not suit us. At the same time, the enh shaman is a class with high avoidance, especially dodge.
I suggest adding an ability to the shaman's base set that allows him:
- ***Gain x% of total mana per dodge or/and parry with some CD, about 1-2sec***
This is a great way to get mana at all levels. When tanking dungeons, when solo questing and even pvp

We can also try to get mana from healing, like it was done for paladins in tbc/wotlk. That's also cool idea that also may be considered, although it is difficult to implement in such a way that it gives enough advantage in all aspects of the game, compared to the previous method

But the worst situation with mana is with our dps enh spec. So we have to have some kind of active ability to get mana. Shamanistic rage could be the ability that allows us to solve this. But first of all, it's just copying abilities from later patches, which, as I understand it, is more of a compromise than a good idea for Twow. Also it's not working good with 2h shaman. Especially considering the nerf of flurry. It's also may add that save CD we all want. But any ability like that should have big CD so it cant be used as a good way of getting mana. Many thought that the interaction of LS with the water shield could solve this problem, but it rather leads to confusion.
But I think my next idea can solve this problem, also make our rotation more fun
- Adding a new talent to the branch, following from the LS talent.
New mechanics related to our LS-shields interaction:
 - **When used with a lightning shield, the shaman's attacks have an x% chance to deal additional nature damage with each attack. This effect lasts 10 sec**

 - **When used with a water shield, the shaman's attacks have an x% chance to restore mana equal to the charge of the water shield. When triggered, a charge is consumed. This effect lasts 10 sec**

 - **When used with an earth shield, the shaman's attacks have an x% chance to restore health equal to the charge of the shield . When triggered, a charge is consumed. If a shield is equipped: additional block chance increased by y%. This effect lasts 10 sec**
You can change the mana values, make them a separate value or a shield charge, but overall the idea is to use deep interaction LS with shield in this way. It's also pretty solid idea to make a pretty common block CD for us.

That was about mana

As for other abilities
We still need a defensive CD. A real defensive CD, not a set bonus, not a totem item. All tanks have em, but not us. What it will be depends on how the devs see the shaman tank. Will it be just a linear damage reduction or some mechanics. All this will depend on the chosen design of the spec and how the developers see it and what place it has among other tanks.

Also some suggestions based on the last few days of gaming experience

- **Totemic Alignment** from 30/60% to 60/100%.
 - Totems are really expensive, and the threat received is incomparably small right now

- **Rockbiter** from 30% to 40-50%
 - Cuz right now with pre raid its generates less threat from white attacks than it did before the patch. Yes, it allows us to scale better in the future, but even so, the current 30% is clearly not enough

- **Flurry** change from 5/5 to 3/3 or change the %.
 - The idea of ​​changing this talent is pretty ridiculous. But even so, the current 5 points required for it is just even more ridiculous

- **Spirit Armor** move higher. Also increases armor gained from rings, necklace and trinkets by 100/200%
 - It's a good talent to upgrade our armor. But I think it should be move higher in our tree to make it usable at lower levels, cuz we really lack armor, especially with leather; and for other specs for pvp. It should also make better use of some current feral-oriented itemization

- **Lightning Strike** has a 10y range now
 - Combination of this ability's interaction with shields makes it's a key ability in pvp now. 10y range will make shaman less kiteable and adds some survivability
Well, to be honest, it looks like it just has to be like that  happy_turtle_head

- **Ancestral Knowledge** increases your highest stat by 2/4/6/8/10%
 - I'm sure I'm not the first to suggest this. It's much more logical and balanced than a useless increase in base stats
