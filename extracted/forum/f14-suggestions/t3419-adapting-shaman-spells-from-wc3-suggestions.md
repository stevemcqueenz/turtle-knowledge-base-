# Adapting Shaman Spells from WC3 Suggestions

## Post 20705 by Dean — 2022-07-30T21:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20705#p20705 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I thought it would be a good idea to write down some ideas I have for the Shaman class. Some spells are still missing from WC3 so I wanted to have a crack at it and present some ideas for how they could be implemented into Turtle WoW.

I am not a WoW expert so don't be surprised if my ideas are unbalanced and absurd but, these ideas still could help give an idea for a more balanced version if they are OP so please have a read and reply what you think.

Original Earthquake Text from Warcraft 3
Earthquake (Ultimate)
Makes the ground tremble and break, causing 50 damage per second to buildings and slowing units by 75% within area of effect. Lasts 25 seconds. 90 Second Cooldown.

An ability from Far Seer Hero Unit, this spell would give Shamans an AoE spell similar to Mage's Blizzard spell which would be ideal for Elemental/Enhancement Shamans.

Earthquake Rank X
X mana
Channelled (8 seconds) 30yd Range
Makes the ground tremble and break, causing X Nature damage per second and slowing units by X% within area of effect. Lasts 8 seconds.

I also thought about two talents that could help all four roles for this skill that would also affect the new spell in game, Earth Shield.

Sharp Earth 0/3
Earthquake now also causes Bleed, dealing X Bleed damage per second. Additionally, Earth Shield deals X Nature damage to enemies within 8 yards when a charge is consumed, causing a high amount of threat.

To compensate for having slow naturally in Earthquake, the damage in Earthquake should be less than the Mage's Blizzard spell but, can compete with it by putting the same amount of talent points into this talent. Since technically this adds another debuff, this would prevent Elemental Shamans from taking this talent since debuff slots are limited just in case the Turtle WoW staff are worried about hybrid classes doing the same dps as pure damage classes in PvE.

Earth Shield talent change would help Shaman Tanks get AoE threat and be able to heal themselves with Earth Shields natural ability, causing even more threat due to the healing.

Resilient Earth 0/3
Increases Earth Shock and Earthquakes damage by X%. Additionally, Earth Shield reduces incoming damage by X% for 2 seconds when a charge is consumed.

Another idea is to just give a flat increase in damage to compete in dps with Mage if the worry about dps isn't there and to give reduced damage for Earth Shield, that way it benefits all Shamans roles but still gives the main benefit to Shaman Tanks.

Original Ethereal Text from Warcraft 3
Ethereal Form
Turns the Spirit Walker ethereal, making him immune to physical damage, but unable to attack. Ethereal units can only be harmed by Magic attacks and spells.

An ability from the Spirit Walker unit, this spell is incredibly powerful but, since two of its abilities are already adapted to Turtle WoW, we can adapt this one.

Ethereal Form Rank X
X mana
Instant / 5 Minute Cooldown
Turns the Shaman ethereal, making him immune to all damage and spells and discouraging enemies from attacking you for X seconds but, cannot attack or cast offensive spells. Once protected, the target cannot be made invulnerable by Divine Shield, Divine Protection, Blessing of Protection or Ethereal Form again for 1 min.

The idea behind this new text is to give the Shaman a mixture of Fade and Divine Shield. Originally in WC3 the downside of being able to go into Ethereal Form at any time without cooldown was not being able to attack during this form and take increased damage from spells. I adapted the weakness of no offensive abilities during Ethereal Form into this so it can only be used as a panic button and allow Restoration Shamans use of their heal spells still while reducing threat.

This should also have multiple ranks to give a range of seconds the user wants to remain in Ethereal Form, starting from 5 seconds and ending in 12 seconds, same as Divine Shield. This way Enhancement/Elemental PvE Shamans have a low mana, threat reducing ability while Restoration and PvP Shamans have a high mana but long duration invulnerable shield.

Original Stasis Trap Ward from Warcraft 3
Stasis Trap Ward
Summons an invisible and immovable ward that stuns enemy units around it. The trap activates when an enemy unit approaches. The trap lasts 150 seconds. The stun lasts 6 (2.5) seconds.

An ability from the Troll Witch Doctor unit, this spell would allow the Shaman to have a stun and we can turn it into a totem for the Fire category since it lacks utility and would allow combos with other totems/spells.

Stasis Trap Totem Rank X
x mana
Instant / 1 Minute Cooldown
Tools: Fire Totem
Summons a lesser invisible Stasis Trap Totem with 5 health at your feet for 1 min. It will stun enemies within 10 yards for x seconds the next time a enemy passes near it.

Taking inspiration from Hammer of Justice, it has a similar cooldown and range but, it isn't on demand like Hammer of Justice and can do AoE stun similar to War Stomp.

Another idea is to adapt Fire Nova Totem to have a stun attached to it if this idea is too OP.

Fire Nova Totem Rank 1
 95 mana
Instant / 30 Second Cooldown
Tools: Fire Totem
Summons a Fire Nova Totem that has 5 health and lasts 5 sec.  Unless it is destroyed within 4 sec., the totem inflicts 53 to 62 fire damage to enemies and stuns them for 2 seconds within 10 yd.
