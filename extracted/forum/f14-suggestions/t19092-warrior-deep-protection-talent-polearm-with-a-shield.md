# Warrior Deep Protection Talent: Polearm with a Shield

## Post 129773 by Terragoth — 2025-05-05T17:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129773#p129773 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The current changes to the deep Protection tree are wonderful, making it more rewarding to play deep Protection as a tanking warrior.  However, Protection is still a bit lacking.  Principally, commenters generally see *Defensive Tactics* as still carrying the bulk of that appeal for deep Protection.  The talent itself also receives criticism as inorganic or unflavorful.

To that end, another talent can be added towards the bottom of the Protection tree to decrease the burden of Defensive Tactics in carrying the value of deep Protection:

> **Lancer**
>
>  **“Polearms are considered one-handed weapons as long as you are wearing a shield.”**

This talent could be viable for several reasons:

 **1)	The code to implement such an idea already exists through various souces.** *Titan’s Grip*, introduced for retail in WotLK, allows the use of a two-handed weapon in each hand, so a similar idea is not unprecedented or unimplemented.

 **2)	The Spear & Shield combo is one of the most historical mainstays of defensive combat.**   Also, though *Titan’s Grip* was often criticized as silly, a talent such as **Lancer** is highly flavorful and organic.

 **3)	Adding this functionality means creating another instance where polearms are useful.**  Polearms are in an odd spot for most classes.  They are most useful for two classes: hunters, who often find the +skill useless, and for feral druids, who only find true usefulness in polearms that provide +AP while shapeshifted.

 **4)	Adding this talent reinforces warriors as the “master of arms,” making polearms useful.**   Despite this title, warriors find little appeal in most polearms for either PvP or PvE due to stats and other considerations.  However, this would change with allowing polearms as tank weapons.

 **5)	Polearms provide great functionality for tanks.** The stats on polearms, generally high in agility and +skill, are great for tanking both for threat and mitigation.  Polearms’ slow speed is also great for *Whirlwind*’s AOE damage, reinforcing the utility of Defensive Tactics.

 **6)	By requiring a shield, the talent can even be added higher on the Protection tree while remaining inaccessible to the “Fury Prot” dual-wield style.**  This reinforces the role of shields-style tanking in an organic way that doesn’t inadvertently benefit dual-wield min-maxers.

 **7)	Putting it into a talent allows for future adjustment for balance and functionality.**   Polearms are generally slow weapons, which can be awkward for tanking.  This can be compensated by having the talent adjust their speed while maintaining the same average dps.  An example would be halving both the wielded polearm’s swing time and damage-per-hit.  (However, the full weapon-damage contribution to talents like *Whirlwind* should likely be maintained.)  Other adjustments can also be more easily implemented within the talent for balance.

 **8)	The functionality of the range increase on polearms provided by *Master of Arms* would be great for positioning as a tank.**  The range increase aspect of the talent currently sees little benefit in PvE or PvP.  That functionality could instead be put into the Lancer talent, deep into the Portion tree.

EDIT:
 **9) Polearms could provide Protection warriors better weapons options outside of the Thunderfury supremacy.**  Thunderfury, a T1 raid item, isn't challenged in single-target threat until Gressil and The Hungering Cold, weapons on the last boss of T3.  It remains the best AOE weapon into T3.5.  The legendry's sheer power is unlikely to be totally surpassed even in T4.  However, the interaction of Polearms with *Whirlwind* allowed by *Defensive Tactics*, as well as the larger stat boost of the 2H weapons, are both big boons.  Thus, this talent could give options for those who do not feel like committing to the hundreds of hours farming Thunderfury, currently a class-defining weapon that boosts shield tanks threat far beyond what any other weapon can do for the slot.

All taken, this seems like a fun, organic talent that would add incentives for deep Protection in an easily-implementable but flavorful way.
