# A List of Future Improvements

## Post 106566 by Goldenspatula — 2024-11-05T16:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106566#p106566 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello! I've been a player on TurtleWoW for around three years now and, while I've always loved the server, there's always room for improvement. I have compiled a list of possible QoL improvements, while trying to avoid repetitive ones such as the questlog expansion or character list expansion (which would both be amazing changes, but should become possible with the TWoW 2.0 client, as it was stated before.)

 **Request stop** Introduced in Cataclysm, this small button on the UI allows you to stop at the next flight master available. It's always annoying to missclick a location and having to log off and back on so the flight will stop at the next stop.

 **Calendar** Introduced in WotLK, this allows to see events such as the DMF, Christmas, Brewfest and many other custom Twow events! This could also include a guild calendar that can be edited for custom events. The player could also include notes in the game calendar, and create their own events which will be marked on their own map and reminded by a system message, if they want to.

 **Addon Dependency** Addons have always and will always be an important part of WoW, but let's not forget a lot of them are there to fulfill things that should be in the base game. Things like DPS meters, threat meters or an addon marking all the ore nodes in an area are unnecessary, maybe even undesired as a base function, but some others are to be considered. As a few examples, consider the following:

> **Better Character Stats**
>  This addon improves a very much needed function of the game, which is the Character Screen. Vanilla has a very broad, basic display of stats that skips a lot of important aspects such as dodge, hit rating, spell power, attack power... This addon allows you to see all, as well as swtich between small windows such as Defense, Spells, etc for a much tidier display. This should 100% be in the base game (and was in fact added in TBC and later improved in WotLK.)

> **PfUI/Shagutweaks/Other UI Addons**
>  Tagged as *essential* addons in the official wiki. It took Blizzard two decades to give the player the ability to shuffle bars and icons around. A player's UI is very important and allowing them to move it around as they like should be a function. Moving or removing the menus (talents, main menu, etc), the bag icons, resizing them, making the map a different size or a different shape... This could even include presets that cover all of the looks of the UI addons (such as a more minimalistic approach like Shagutweaks, something more pragmatic like PfUI, more modern like the Dragonflight UI or even something more oldschool, inspired by the WC3 UI. It's very common for UI addons to bug, to cause headaches on the player, clip with other icons, etc.

> **Some other QoL addons**
>  Addons that, amongst many functions include: a key to sell all your trash to vendors, autodismount when pressing any key (as a toggable option), improved interface (some changes have already been added, like the map not being fullscreen anymore) and quest tracking. TBC added a quest tracking system that was helpful, yet unprecise enough not to break the immersion. Because, let's be honest, almost everyone uses some sort of "Questie" addon. A lot of quests are vague, and that's why a quest tracker is necessary to close the circle a little, while still being broad about the general area (for example in TBC, the quest icons were numbered and would mark the general area with a blue overlay shape, instead of placing specific dots with the exact spawn locations like, for example, pfQuest. Let's not forget these are tagged as recommended leveling addons in the wiki.

 **Addon-friendly Features**
Yes, I did say addon dependency was to be avoided, but addons will always be an important part of the game and, let's be honest, we cannot expect TWoW to add every little feature! Though it'd be ideal. For this reason, I present to you the **Addon Manager**, hooray! There's already a similar program that allows you to enter the github links and you can download updates once they are available, which definitely improves it. Not even WoW fully solved this issue. I am not talking about CurseForge, I mean a fully-integrated Workshop within the client itself that lets you download, manage and upload addons. This is, of course, very ambitious and could only be realized in the TWoW 2.0 client, and still I don't believe it would take a high priority. Still, having to manage folders is always a big deal-breaker for most people and I would like TWoW to bring the comfort of modernity and the classy vibe of Vanilla together.

 **Macros**
Addons such as Roid-macros improve and expand a lot on the macro language and usage, improving limitations. These improvements started in TBC, which saw the biggest overhaul, and continued all the way up to MoP, and still suffered some changes in future expansions. People love macros, and I'm no exception! Perhaps these limitations are due to the client (which then again TBC improved. As much as I disliked the expansion, it improved a lot on technical issues and client limitations, and this was 20 years ago!). More words, more conditionals, more simplification

 **The Combat Log**
A client modification, SuperWoW, has introduced a sort of patch that allows for lots of functions that were introduced in the client improvements of TBC (proper target tracking that follows ID and not unit name, proper debuff display). This proves that this is not a limitation of the client and can be added, though I would understand if the developers would rather wait until the new improved client comes out before adding this. But sooner or later, this should be added   turtle_tongue

 **Others** Come on, guys. Nobody likes that items don't have vendor prices displayed... Every item should! You shouldn't need an addon for that! Focus targets are also very nice, and I see no reason not to add this function. Also, let players mark objectives, friendly or otherwise, without the need of being in a group!

Fix the Hardcore chat. One of our best players and addon-makers, Lexie, made a quick addon to fix it, but even after months, no fix has come out. This is imperative and should take 5 minutes to fix.

As TWoW said, 2.0 will not and should not be *TBC 2: Now It's Personal*, but a different line of work that follows the line of what WC3 settled. That being said, there is no harm in noticing and mimicing some of the improvements that future expansions brought, and discard the less... pleasant additions. I apologize if this post comes as a TBC praise - it's not. I disliked the expansion, *a lot*, but it brought a lot of good things to the table on the technical side.

The list seems endless, but I think a lot of these issues can be approached slowly as the game continues to flourish. I want to thank the TWoW team for all their efforts, and to many more years of continuing the Vanilla spirit!
