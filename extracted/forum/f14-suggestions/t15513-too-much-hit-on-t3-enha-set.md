Additional suggestion regarding the 6 piece set bonus :
 -The duration of the buff is a bit low because it counts on us putting prio on Shocks over SS and LS which would be direct dps loss. I would increase the duration to 7-8 seconds that way we cant benefit from 2 bonuses (no time to use two shocks) and we can delay our shock if LS or SS are coming off cd at the same time.
 -The fact that it only gives a bonus to a different shock depending on which one we use makes it very inefficient to use on bosses that are immune to one of the 3 schools. For example. Sapphiron, BWL bosses. I would give bonus to any shock school, that way we dont lose the 1/3 of the bonus on the resistance fights.
