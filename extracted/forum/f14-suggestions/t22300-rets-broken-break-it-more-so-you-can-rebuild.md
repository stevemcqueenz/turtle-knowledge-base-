# Rets Broken, Break it more so you can rebuild

## Post 154790 by Icedcream (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-08T00:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154790#p154790 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

There is a fundamental issue within Twow with paladin scaling. Until these items are removed it would be impossible to balance the game. There is one paladin (unnamed) 20% higher than the rest, but don't balance the game on him. He has double shaman.. champion, and just about every possible buff. Also some how... like 800 AP higher while having all the same gear as us. Not saying cheating.... but something. Paladins otherwise general perform mid to low in DPS in by Kara 40.

 1)	The Nerfs

 1.a) Consumes:
Within spell ret builds there are no ways to address scaling issues with items that exist pre-MC content and they make up too large of a proportion the paladins base raiding power level. This causes problems in later game content  Kara40 where paladins do not scale well and there is no mechanism to address this.

Flask of Supreme Power – 150 SP
Arcane Elixir – 35 SP
Dreamshard Elixir – 15 SP
B. Wizard Oil – 36 SP
Judgement of Crusader – 140 + 33 (talent) + 21 (Libram)
Wrath of Cenarius  - 132 SP, conservatively 75% uptime on a boss fight.

All these items are available day 1 of raid. Nothing too wrong with the regular elixirs. No other melee walks into raid with a flask that impacts their damage this way. By end of Kara 40, 60% of your spell power are coming from judgement, proc, and consumes still. That means you open up in MC with a lot of strength and scale to a limited effect by the end since the game seems balanced around AQ40 content.

I recommend Flask of Supreme power maybe only effects range spells.

 **1.b) Procs and Items**
Late game 5% total damage come from Sigil of the Ancient Accord Proc (requiring use of T2 set). For whatever reason this works with this item and procs off the 4 damage divine strike (2 increments per swing – SoR+auto). As well, 5% total damage coming from Ashbringer proc since it has 100% spell power scaling. These items cripple paladin item selection. However, there is another factor which is paladins need the 4 dmg proc from some source since our class is balanced around consecrating (which is another problem seen further down), which needs judgement of wisdom procs to sustain our mana usage based on the cost of abilities.

Just nerf dragonbreath out of the game and make other foods buff consumption instant. No one has time to sit 10-15 seconds to get +10 str or stam. We have to eat oranges and we aren’t sitting around for 30 seconds then 15 seconds. Nerf would take away 2-3% of paladin dps.

I believe the ultimate goal should be to end procs of random effects (usually original unintentional) and build in proper scaling mechanics.

 **1.C) Abilities**
Before anything can be balanced we need to address the following abilities.
Consecration – Paladins only source of AOE and actually pretty decent (140-155 dps end game) single target addition into the rotation. The ability is unreliable if targets move since it burns the ground in a spot for 8 seconds. The ability mana cost is unreasonably high for a ret paladin at about 10-15% of their mana pool every 8 seconds, so they require these special gear mechanics to regen mana. A problem being that again the spell power mechanic is broken with too much power not related to gear scaling. Put consecration into the tank tree deeper down, and lower the mana requirement. This will however decrease ret paladin dps by 8-12% on single target (give rets a divine storm like ability instead, that doesn’t share a CD with stirkes).

Holy Strike – This ability should just be used by Prot for high threat for tank and heal for holy, and crusader strike by ret paladins for attack speed and higher damage. The Strength mechanic cripples the paladin into keeping things up which sounds fun, but you have to scale up your damage and this is ineffective on 95% of the fights that last sub 45-60 seconds.  Take the block effect of CS and put it base in the prot tree and put the strength buff into the ret tree.  Having no holy strike requirement would scale up paladin dps by about 4-5%.

Judgement of Crusader – remove spell power since it doesn’t scale.  Give them… 2-3% crit chance to target as a replacement.

 **2)	The buffs**
 2.A) Paladins have too many attributes they require. Give Divine strength to prot tree, make it 10% strength, 5% stamina (tier 1 of tree).
 2.B) move conviction to the first tree ret (every other melee has crit first lvl, why are we lvl 5)
 2.C) Paladin power scaling tied to holy power only with SoR is bad, and it doesn’t crit so it doesn’t scale that way with gear. I bet (and could do the math if required) that you could make SoCommand tied to base damage + holy power scaling and just up the proc rate. Guess is end game on average a minor dps increase (depends on increased proc rate) and early content a decrease in damage.
 2.D) No one wants rets taking Str gear from warriors so make a talent that gives 30% Spell power as strength (Holy Strength). Powered by your faith in the light your spell power increases your strength. This will replace the holy striking mechanic and give a (example 400-500 holy power = 120-150 strength). This is really a similar effect, maybe a little weaker, but 100% uptime on shorter fights.
 2.E) Divine storm – 70% auto damage to primary target and 35% damage to surrounding targets. Coefficient is 50%. This could be a cap stone, and the substitute for consecration. It would now scale with the paladins gear and not their consumes as much. Ability would enter the paladins single target rotation at a fairly equal level, but also benefits from sunders  on a primary target.  Fundamentally it will be weaker on surround targets, but may get more out of general mob aoes. Should crit on hit based on melee crit rate. Mana 150 mana instead, 8 second CD… so it would be more sustainable.   I think you may still be the weakest aoe’r after druids with this ability. – guess is 12-14% total damage on single target
 2.F) repentance – BAD, very variable based on boss attack speed, and 60 second CD. Give them a maybe burn effect dot that scales with AP/SP, 20 second CD.
 2.G) Make hammer of wrath instant, reduce range to 5 yards, scale weapon damage (110% weapon damage + 200, based on melee crit). Scale the power lvl with the % and CD as you balance.
