# Improve Discipline Priest Playstyle

## Post 152102 by Aloeve — 2025-09-17T14:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152102#p152102 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello friends,

I am new to Twow and started to play because I got rly interested in Twow’s discipline priest.

While I find the ideas of what was implemented amazing, I do believe two changes could benefit the playstyle by increasing skill cap and thus fun (i.e. feeling “how we play the priest actually makes a difference in our contribution”).

I would like to humbly put them up for discussion here.

 ***Problems:***

- Mana issues in dungeons without big buffs/consumes.

- Enlighten is just another 30min buff to keep up (we already have enough of those) and does not contribute to the playstyle.

- Chastise on GCD is usually not worth it in PvE fights because you lose 1,5sec cast time of a smite. Particularly true for raids.

- Non-priests complain about too high random burst in PvP.
 ***Solutions:***

 **Changes to Enlighten:**

After choosing the talent, the priest builds up “Enlightening Discipline” charges whenever he casts Holy Fire, Smite, Holy Nova, or Power Word: Shield. At 5 charges the priest can use the “Enlighten” ability. Using the ability consumes all 5 charges and restores 2% of the priest’s mana pool.

“Enlighten”: Burns the target for 4% of their health over 3 seconds. Enlightens the target increasing both the priest’s and the targets spell damage and healing by 5% for 12 seconds. If used on the priest spell damage and healing is increased by 7% instead. Instant cast without GCD.

Optional idea: Add different “Enlighten” abilities such as “Enlighten Body”, “Enlighten Spirit”, etc. with other effects. For instance, a movement speed increase for 20% for 4 seconds, mana regen, etc.

 **Explanation:**

- Building charges and adding a usable ability adds things to do and removes another “boring 30 minute buff”.

- Enlighten abilities off global cooldown means that priests can in fact use them during fights.

- 2% mana restore effect when consuming the “Enlightening Discipline” charges helps with mana but only if you actually use the abilities.

- Decreased damage buff from 10% to 5% (15% to 7%) to reduce random burst in PVP and account for higher up time.

- Increased duration from 8 to 12 seconds to force priests to sometimes use the buff on multiple other players as 5 charges can hypothetically be build up in under 12 seconds during e.g. Smite spam.

- Adding other “Enlighten” abilities would add further variety. Movement speed e.g. would help tanks pull, mages kite, or pvp group members move, but at the cost of 5 to 7 % dps.

- Making the 4% burn a DOT that ticks over 3 seconds avoids spell pushback.

- The sound effect of Enlighten is AMAZINGLY SATISFYING. We would get to hear it more often.

 **Changes to Chastise:**

I propose 3 changes…

- Remove GCD: This will make it worth using in fight as you don’t loose (smite) casting time.

- Remove damage when used on enemies completely: Nerf to reduce burst in PvP.

- Change damage on friendly units to a DOT that ticks over 3 seconds: Avoids spell push back when used on caster friendlies.
I would love to hear what others (and turtle staff in particular) have to say about these suggestions. Feel free do adjust numbers if sth is too nerfed/buffed.

Cheers,
