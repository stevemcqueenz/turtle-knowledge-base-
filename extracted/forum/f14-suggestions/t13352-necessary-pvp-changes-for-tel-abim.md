# Necessary PVP changes for Tel'Abim

## Post 89792 by Degenlife — 2024-03-09T19:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89792#p89792 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been playing tel'abim since server launch and daily pvp for past several months. The pvp scene is good but there are a few pain points that could be easily addressed for a better experience.

Blood Ring is not incentivized enough. It's a lot of fun when it pops, and the rep rewards are great, but it never pops on non-BR day and doesn't pop often enough on Blood Ring bonus honor day.

- Suggestions: Add Same Faction Blood Ring (alliance vs alliance, horde vs horde, not cross-faction teams). This would be a lot of fun and would result in constant BR pops on blood ring day. Would also be great for NA players as currently, battlegrounds often stop popping during NA evening times (02:00 ST) as there are not enough NA horde pvpers.

Add 2v2 Blood Ring. This could either be on a separate, new bonus honor day (my preference) or just an additional option that can pop when queue numbers are too low for 3v3 (e.g. 2v2 will pop if there are not enough players for 3v3 60 seconds after the queue threshold for 2v2 has been met.

Add Blood Ring leaderboards (separate for solo queue vs party queue). This will incentive players who are already exalted to keep playing. Title reward for top players or something like that.

Consider Buffing Blood Ring honor gain slightly. Currently, nobody queues BR on non-BR day, and even on BR day lots of people play WSG/AB for honor instead.

BUG: Blood Ring matches sometimes end prematurely as soon as 2 players from one side die. This prevents a potential outplay 1v3 or 1v2 scenario.
Nobody plays Sunnyglade Valley.

- BG either needs a revamp and big honor boost to incentivize playing it, or just removal from the bonus honor day rotation.
Re-vamp Ranking/RP gain system

- Increase Minimum Pool Size. Currently there are only 2 spots for bracket 1 and 4 spots for bracket 2. Increasing pool size to make these brackets a little bigger would be to everyone's benefit and would reduce the downsides of the current "Bracket Stacking" meta.

Consider adding a Maximum Weekly Honor Cap (e.g. 400-450k). Everyone who reaches this cap would receive maximum RP gain that week. This would eliminate the current Bracket Stacking meta and reduce the overall length of the pvp grind to a more manageable, but still very grindy amount.

There are a lot more problems with blood ring that make people not want to queue for it. I don't think any of your suggestions would really help with the availability other than allowing to fight the same faction. That being said, allowing so might cause friction between players especially on a tight knit community like Tel'Abim who heavily relies on PuGs to fill raids.

The reality is that most people don't queue for BR because:

 - The majority of vanilla PvPers are actually really bad at the game. They are selfish and are used to getting carried in battlegrounds. Arenas exposes how terrible they actually are.
 - Massive gear disadvantage due to no matchmaking/rankings. New players get globalled.
 - Allowing consumables is beyond broken
 - Composition imbalance due to not allowing to queue as a group. Sometimes you get lucky, sometimes the game is already over as you step in. It's also impossible to synergize with other classes that might fill some weaknesses.
 - The obvious (druid, ret paladins) class imbalance
 - The "Z axis" of blades edge arena is horrendous and those 2 pillars make hunters virtually immune to rogue saps.

All in all, I wish the reputation was drastically buffed. I also think that losing should not yield any rewards. It just encourages bad players to not improve their skills and just lose farm it.

Additionally, the gear weirdly overlaps with the blue tier honor one. While it is theoretically much faster - compared to the honor system - to gear up which should make it the preferred way for a new player, arenas are also a lot harder than battlegrounds and don't really allow newbies to "learn" to pvp. It is also very unpleasant as a seasoned arena enjoyer to deal with green geared players.

By the way, there is no premature ending bug. It's just that as soon as 2 are dead you can /AFK out without getting deserter debuff. Thank god for that as it would be too easy to grief your own teammates as a stealther (force them to afk out as punishment). Perhaps as an alternative you could allow dead players to leave - and count as a loss - without affecting players that are still alive.

~~

As for sunny glade, the situation is absolutely aberrant. Buff the additional honor reward to make it a no brainer to queue for it.
