# Endurance Mode

## Post 98638 by Cyklon — 2024-07-06T07:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98638#p98638 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Always thougt that running all the time in wow should be "nerfed". Why not add a challange to endurance.

Simular to a focusbar of sort. Scaled with level maby, the higher level you are the longer you can run without falling out of breath and have to stop and rest, or walk. Mounts might also have endurance.
The endurancebar will slowly fill up when walking at a ~50% rate, 75% wile standing and 100% wile sitting.

The exact rates and such is maby up for debate but i think that would add to the game, especially with slow & steady.
