# TWoW RPvP realm when?

## Post 59469 by Xa1amandor — 2023-08-28T01:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59469#p59469 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Blizzards' original World of Warcraft was the pinnacle of the franchise - until Turtle WoW.

The Turtle team have done such an amazing job reimagining & elaborating upon the beloved vanilla gameplay/experience that it's no mystery why the server has exploded in popularity like it has. It is the superior vanilla-era(+) WoW experience, unrivaled by anything that anyone else is currently providing or has before, including Blizzard themselves.

They've sated our nostalgic & creative appetite on so many levels save but one: an (R)PvP realm.

It's no secret that many players here are (R)PvErs that prefer this manner of gameplay. I'm not proposing that TWoW change the current RPvE realm to RPvP but WHY NOT open & host a concurrent RPvP realm to provide the historic realm-type dichotomy for players to choose from (or to play on both)? MANY players that play on the current RPvE realm would prefer an RPvP variant and even some of the (R)PvErs would surely appreciate an alternative to occasionally play on as well. Wouldn't the implementation be as simple as copying all the RPvE realm data (including player characters, etc) and pasting it as a new realm - only changing the realm type to RPvP? My technical naivety must be showing but I can't imagine it'd be much more complicated than this. And I understand that TWoW has other priorities with an upcoming patch and all but the eventual release of an RPvP realm must be on their calendar.

It would be a MASSIVE missed opportunity for TWoW not to do this. Eventually the demand for a vanilla/vanilla+ (R)PvP realm will be met - why shouldn't TWoW answer this call with their superior product before someone else does?

Blizzard originally offered different realm-type options because they knew that the MMORPG community is diverse and deserves the choice. (R)PvE & (R)PvP playerbases are both legitimate and substantial and have their place.

Since we've all long since fled from Blizzard, our mutual place ought to be here, under the TWoW banner.   smiling_turtle_head
