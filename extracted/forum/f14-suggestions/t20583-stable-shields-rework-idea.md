# Stable Shields Rework Idea

## Post 142324 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-31T10:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142324#p142324 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!
The current shaman talent in enh tree does this:
Increases the shield charges by (2-4-6) and increases proc CD by 1 sec.

It is a very useful talent, but I have a problem with this.
Enh DPS/tank is ideally expected to swap between shields for different procs and LS payoff.
A talent which adds a bunch of extra orbs rewards you for not doing that since LS and ES are kinda expensive, and you have a bunch of shields on the stack.

I'd propose this:
Reduces shield mana cost by x% (and if balance requires it, increasing proc CD by 1 sec).
If shields are really cheap and you always have a few, this gameplay gets rewarded.
As an enh DPS, you can switch anytime for a quick WS mana regen and super cheaply get back to LS.
As a tank, ES swap won't feel like a mana drain anymore, or an LS swap for threat boost would be much more affordable.

This change would be annoying for those who keep up one kind of shield type but it can be easily macroed.
But it would force enh for quick and rewarding decision making and would make shields more interesting than tank->WS, enh -> LS unless mana is low.
