# Paladin 2h Healing?

## Post 115067 by Kicken — 2024-12-31T22:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115067#p115067 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello!

I play on Turtle WoW and have a suggestion that I think would be incredibly exciting to explore in the game!
I believe it would be great if they could introduce a melee healing variant for paladins, as this makes sense both from a lore perspective and in terms of traditional fantasy characters.

Many classic paladins are often depicted as frontline healers wielding large 2H weapons or maces while also healing.
So why is it that only staves have +healing stats in the game? Why not have epic 2H swords or maces with healing attributes?

I think it would be amazing to play as a paladin who is a true frontline warrior, wearing heavy armor and wielding a large 2H weapon, while also healing in a unique way. With the introduction to healing only Holy Shock, and Crusader Strike resets the cooldown this would also make more synergy for the specialization.

So in other words i am willing to sacrifice the shield — meaning less armor and the ability to block, perhaps even a few stats — in exchange for looking cooler and hitting a bit harder in melee.

What do you think? Should Turtle WoW explore the possibility of adding 2H weapons with healing stats?
Or is this a balancing issue that might be difficult to implement?

Cheers
Kicken
