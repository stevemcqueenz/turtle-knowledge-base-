# Playable Draenei - Horde

## Post 76755 by Aloofsage — 2023-11-10T08:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76755#p76755 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Title? Makes no sense, yes?

 1) Background, Train of Thought

However, I think it does - I suggest adding the Draenei to the Horde as a playable race in the future.

In Retail WoW's timeline, the Draenei were heavily retconned. They became these blessed spacefaring advanced beings of light. To the point that the shamanic traditions seemed like something of a stretch (You empirically see what amounts to literal angels guiding your civilization on the daily away from actual demons, yet you still dance around with the spirits and elements).

Before this, though, they were really a "nothing" race, merely these primal and tribal inhabitants of Draenor than wandered through the Dark Portal into the Blasted Lands and Swamp of Sorrows. All of them were what we would later know as the Broken. They didn't have any really known history with the Orcs, at best being an insular and generally hostile group to outsiders. A perfect blank slate in a way.

It seems like, before the retcon, they had much in common in their traditions with the Horde. And in a progressing timeline I do not see how such a group wouldn't find itself on common ground with the faction, especially in response to the Alliance's approach to dealing with Draenor's original inhabitants.

 2) Lore

Since they are such a tabula rosa, it wouldn't be a retcon per se to just expand on them a little bit in a way that is consistent with the broader WC3 lore.

They could easily be written as a downtrodden, broken race in dire need, who's ways are in conflict with the zealous missions of the Alliance, and what is perceived to be the racist ways of groups like the Kal and Quel dorei. Their shamanic traditions and common history with the Orcs brings them to a shaky but passably safe refuge around Stonard.

 3) Game

This is where it gets tricky to a degree - After all the broken we see in the southern EK are the models we know. I don't see why, however, the later models cannot be used with proper edits.

Instead of the adorned blue models of TBC, they could simply be given more earthen toned recolouring of their skins, among other minor edits.

Their classes can be Warrior, Hunter, Shaman for certain. Possibly Warlocks, Rogues? (I'm not too familiar with the lore around them and fel magics, and rogue lore is totally lost on me).

I don't find this in conflict with their existing models, after all, nothing prevents one from simply acknowledging that this could "just be how some of them look". But such models are so unappealing to the average player I doubt without backporting the newer ones anyone would substantively play them, nor must they have a wide set of animations.

Finally, restating here, I don't see how the Draenei, given the WC3 lore, actually ever fit into the Alliance to begin with in Blizz HQ. It seems like they were heavily forced that way, when in reality they were a much more natural fit in the Horde.

This does ask the question of what an Alliance race should be to match, though this is beyond the scope of the post. I would welcome such speculation though in the thread.

Hi there. :)

Several things: As far as I've understood the Lore around the Draenei, they weren't shamanistic "out of the blue". They were a light-devoted race. But those Draenei who were altered by the Magics of the Dark Portals opened by Nerzhul and therefore been known as the "Broken", they were no longer able to wield the Light and therefore turned to the Elements, discovered shamanistic ways.
On top of that: Even though the Orcs are no longer the ones from Draenor, they still slaughtered the Draenei. And I doubt that the Draenei will either forgive or forget was has been done to them. So (to me) Draenei on Horde side seems absolutely out of question.
Since we still want to maintain the faction-uniqueness through Paladins/Shamans, I'd say that the Broken (who discovered the shamanistic ways), seem out of question. If we go for the TBC-Model of course they shall NOT have shamans. Then Paladins only.

Btw. an interesting fact: Everyone's talking about "Draenei being retconned". Interesting enough, that we barely had ANY story or cultural background about the Draenei prior to their appearance in "WarCraft III - The Frozen Throne". And even what we got in WC3TFT was just a mere glimpse on an almost extinct and ruined civilization on a broken world. So talking about "heavily retconned" might be an overstatement. :D

On top of that: With the TBC model we actually got a connection to Kil'jaeden and Archimonde. So if we'd go with the TBC-Model, they might be similar to the Undead on Horde side: starting on pure Neutral with everyone else, since they might be mistrusting to a race, which is looking like demons. (Especially the Elves might be mistrusting, since they knew the Eredar demons from their history in the War of the Ancients)
