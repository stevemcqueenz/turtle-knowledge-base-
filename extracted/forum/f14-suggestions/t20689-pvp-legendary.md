Before anyone says this is too easy I'd like to pre-emptively point out that this chain 750 gold total and 55,600 cumulative Honourable Kills with the weaker versions of your weapon wannabee legendary equipped!

---
Level Requirement: 60
Starting NPC: Master Smith, located in Orgrimmar (Horde) or Stormwind (Alliance)

 **Quest 1: Gathering the Materials**
Objective: Collect 100 Thorium Ore, 50 Dense Stone, 20 Enchanted Leather, and 10 Arcane Crystals from the world.
Description: Master Smith Hammerfall requires high-quality materials to begin forging a weapon worthy of a battlelord. Thorium Ore and Dense Stone can be mined in high-level zones, while Enchanted Leather and Arcane Crystals demand crafting skills or resourcefulness.
Reward: Unlocks the next quest.

 **Quest 2: The Unblooded Blade**
Objective: Pay 50 gold to the Master Smith to receive the Unblooded Blade (white quality weapon).
Description: With the materials in hand, the Master Smith crafts a basic weapon for 50 gold. This "Unblooded Blade" is the foundation of your legendary journey, but it must be proven in combat.
Reward: Unblooded Blade (white quality, level 60 common weapon).

 **Quest 3: Blooding the Blade**
Objective: Equip the Unblooded Blade and earn 100 Honourable in battlegrounds.
Description: the Master Smith instructs you to "bloody" the blade by wielding it in battlegrounds, infusing it with the essence of your foes. Earn 100 Honourable to temper its steel.
Reward: Unlocks the next upgrade.

 **Quest 4: Enhancing the Blade**
Objective: Pay 50 gold to Master Smith to upgrade the Unblooded Blade to the Bloodied Blade (green quality).
Description: The bloodied weapon is ready for enhancement. For another 50 gold, the Master Smith reforges it into a green-quality "Bloodied Blade," increasing its power.
Reward: Bloodied Blade (green quality, uncommon weapon).

 **Quest 5: Tempering the Blade**
Objective: Equip the Bloodied Blade and earn 500 Honourable in battlegrounds.
Description: The blade thirsts for more combat. Wield it to earn 500 Honourable further proving your prowess in PvP battles.
Reward: Unlocks the next upgrade.

 **Quest 6: Reforging the Blade**
Objective: Pay 50 gold to the Master Smith to upgrade the Bloodied Blade to the Veteran's Blade (blue quality).
Description: With the blade tempered by hundreds of kills, the Master Smith can enhance it further for 50 gold, transforming it into the "Veteran's Blade" of blue quality.
Reward: Veteran's Blade (blue quality, rare weapon).

 **Quest 7: Honing the Blade**
Objective: Equip the Veteran's Blade and earn 5000 Honourable Kills in battlegrounds.
Description: A true veteran’s weapon requires a monumental effort. Take the blade into battlegrounds and earn 5000 Honourable Kills to hone its edge.
Reward: Unlocks the next upgrade.

 **Quest 8: Empowering the Blade**
Objective: Pay 100 gold to the Master Smith to upgrade the Veteran's Blade to the Champion's Blade (epic quality).
Description: The blade, now steeped in the blood of thousands, is ready for a significant upgrade. For 100 gold, the Master Smith empowers it into the "Champion's Blade," an epic weapon.
Reward: Champion's Blade (purple quality, epic weapon).

 **Quest 9: The Final Tempering**
Objective: Equip the Champion's Blade and earn 50,000 Honourable Kills in battlegrounds.
Description: Only a legendary warrior can complete this task. Wield the Champion's Blade and earn 50,000 Honourable Kills, a testament to your dominance in PvP.
Reward: Unlocks the final upgrade.

 **Quest 10: Forging the Legend**
Objective: Pay 500 gold to the Master Smith to upgrade the Champion's Blade to the Battlelord's Fury (legendary quality).
Description: With an unparalleled kill count, the blade is ready to ascend. Pay the Master Smith 500 gold to forge the "Battlelord's Fury," a legendary weapon feared across Azeroth.
Reward: Battlelord's Fury (orange quality, legendary weapon).
