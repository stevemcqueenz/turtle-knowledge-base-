Please seriously consider making tier 1 and tier 2 gear sets swappable for the different specialization sets in Ratchet - exactly how tier 2.5 and tier 3 are in Ratchet.
It does not make sense having to farm complete sets of these tier sets again for another spec after gear progressing through the tiers on your main spec. Having these previous tier items that have been changed from their drop to a different spec rotting in the bank.
Instead of being able to raid as an offspec utilizing these items I replaced on my main spec, I would need to continue raiding in tier 3 and rolling on tier 1&2 items against people who are starting their gear progression when I have the changed pieces already that I could swap to my focused offspec.
Please change this!
