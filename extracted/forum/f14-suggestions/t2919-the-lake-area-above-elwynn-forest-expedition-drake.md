Hi

I think we should do something about the lake area in the mountains above Elwynn for the next patch, making another mountain passage going through Elwynn to the drake fighting mountaineers. In the process, I guess the old obsolete guild area there could be removed or remade into a general vendor camp.

A quest or two could be added as a start telling the player to explore to the border of The Burning Steppes, now being directly enterable from this small mountain passage (also add a ramp from The Burning Steppes to make it possible to enter from there).
The q could be a lowbie one simply to give the lowbies a cool view of The Burning Steppes, without taking them close to the drake. Say, put a quest giver in the camp asking you to talk to a scout within sight of the drake, though not close to it, and take his/her report and bring it back to the camp, after which you are asked to take it to further to General Marcus at the SW entrance.

For Horde, the new passage would also ease the path to the secret entrance to a certain lvl 60 dungeon in Elwynn. There could also be a high lvl quest asking the player to scout the Alliance and mountaineer area to see what they are up to.

Of course there could be more advanced features, but I always think within a realistic scope of what the Devs have time for with there small team as well as the fact that there are more important features to be added.
