# Profession Revamp Ideas

## Post 133363 by kalevala206 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-28T20:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133363#p133363 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

These are some suggestions I have for some tweaks big and small to professions that I think would help improve the game. Please call out any ideas you like here, I think most of these individually would be great, and if multiple were out out there, even better.

 **In General A Note:**
In general one of the main issues IMO is the lack of low-end materials inside of higher-end recipes. Everything is so linear in the game. The economy on the AH would be vastly improved in late-game recipes still called for early-game crafted or gathered materials. Why can't a lvl 60 potion call for Peacebloom in addition to Arthas' Tears? In general, I'd ask that as the Turtle Team develops new potions and recipes, that instead of inventing new mats to add another layer to this linear development- instead, please take a look at what mats could use more demand on the AH (especially things that AH  for less than they vendor), and try to incorporate those into new recipes or turn-ins.

Alchemy
 **More Higher End Recipes**
There needs to be more recipes that use higher end herbs. Arthas’ Tears is only used in 2 recipes in the entire game, and yet there are tons of nodes for it all over the place. If more useful items used these herbs, the economy of the game would function better

 **Add Grenades aka Thrown Potions**
Alchemy does fine with being useful for raiding, but it’d be more fun if you could also make more items that are fun utility and/or deal damage kind of like Engineering. Something like potions that you throw, creating gas clouds or flame bursts. If damage is too similar to Engineering’s gig, then you could have various AOE buffs/debuffs coming from the effects of the thrown potion. The idea is to help Alchemy have a more active role, and would interact well with the addition of Thrown to the game. The ability to make thrown ammo, or even Hawkeye-style trick arrow ammo that leaves gas clouds, any of this would be amazing.

 **Brewmaster Specialization**
Lets Alchemists specialize in making consumable drink items. Take water and drinks from in-game, brew your own alcohol, and mix ingredients together to make new mana-gaining brews! Combine Bitters (made from Herbs) with various waters collected in vials, or existing in-game drinks or fruits. Keeping it in Alchemy lets you keep the effects meaningful and impactful.
Hops (x2) + Refreshing Spring Water (x1) = Basic Hop Water (skill level 1)
Hops (x5) + Barley (x2) + Yeast (x1) + Refreshing Spring Water (x1) = Cheap Lager (skill 25)
Goldleaf (x3) = Goldleaf Bitters

Cooking
 **Feast Items**
At skill 300 you can go on a quest, as a reward you get an ability that lets you summon a large cauldron or pig-on-a-spit. If 4 other people (5 total) with 300 cooking help summon, then you can make Feasts. Feasts give 30-min raid-wide buffs that do not stack with each other, but do stack with elixirs and potions.

 **More Reputation Recipes**
Give us some Cenarian and Thorium Bros (and Zandalar, etc) related cooking recipes that involve ingredients you find in their related raids/dungeons, possibly with bonuses that will help progression in those areas. Have bosses drop rare cooking mats, giving cooking more prestige. BRD’s tavern is dying for more food quest content (which could scale for all BRM related dungeons- maybe they all give fire resist plus other buffs), and who doesn’t love silithid egg omelets or silithid honeycomb crunch?

 **Upcycling Low Level Food**
Introduce new recipes that allow higher-level cooks to use lower-level ingredients in new ways. A mid-level cook could use low-level meats and mid-level herbs and/or spices to create mid-level Sausages. Or a max-level cook could take large amounts of lower-level fish, and turn them into Oil that can either be directly applied to weapons, or used as an ingredient in other cooking recipes (a whole chain of Garum consumables at varying levels would work great and reinforce Cooking’s synergy with Fishing). The idea is to give more value to lower level ingredients, so players want to use them over a longer level range, which keeps the economy of those items moving instead of being overflooded with supply to the point where the AH is selling them for cheaper than what a vendor will give you for it.

 **Seasoning**
Let Cooks take herbs and convert them into Seasoning blends, which “enchant” foods. You can only enchant foods that give no bonus other than HoT, and the seasonings do things like give you +stats if you eat long enough. This would also help give low level herbs more reason to be bought and sold on the AH.

Enchanting
 **Make Vellum**
Made from some combination of Paper, Leather, Cloth, and Shards/Motes. Vellum is used to receive enchantments, which can then be sold on the AH, and applied to weapons/armor by the user. This would be an absolute game changer for Classic WoW. It doesn’t fundamentally shift balance, it just makes the process of getting and giving enchants 1000% easier, and is a huge QoL improvement. Enchanting is already a horrible profession from a user experience point of view, and this would make it a lot more viable without tweaking any balance.

 **Can Disenchant Magical Constructs**
Mana Wyrms, arcane elementals, and so forth, can now have their remains disenchanted after being defeated, if the enchanter is of the appropriate level and skill. This gives a skill up and appropriate mats based on level.

 **Cloth Upleveling**
Let enchanters turn Silk into Mageweave, Mageweave into Runecloth, and Runecloth into Felweave, by expending enchanting mats as well as the lower level cloth. They literally add the mage to the weave, and the runes to the cloth.

Engineering
 **Can Disassemble Robots**
Any mechanical construct can be disassembled by an Engineer, just like Skinners can skin Beasts. Examples of this are the Mechanical Harvesters in Westfall, or the various robots in Gnomeregan. This mostly gives you gray vendor trash (makes you want to have a repairbot handy) but can occasionally give you engineering components.

 **Silver-Plated Bullets**
Uses Silver, and higher-level versions use Truesilver, to make special bullets that give +damage vs undead and worgen.

First Aid
This is the skill that should have Tents, and they should be called Triage Tents. This can also take the Survival version of a campfire.

 **Boot Insoles**
Can now craft soulbound insoles for shoes, which allow a movement speed increase as you go up in tier. These last for a # of meters moved instead of for a timed duration. Goes away upon mounting, does not stack with other movement speed buffs, cancelled by combat.

 **More Medicines**
It’d be nice to have some more limited or rare recipes that are found in-world or that you have to hunt down custom trainers for, that would allow you to negate specific effects in game. While priests can cure disease wholecloth, these would be specific antidotes to specific diseases or poisons (like the disease at the start of Strat).

Fishing
 **Make Nets**
Using Rope and Cloth and sometimes Leather, you can make consumable Nets that immobilize units up to a certain level for a short period of time.

 **Make Equippable Gear**
Angler’s Wading Boots (leather, boots, +10% unmounted run speed in water)
Angler’s Fish Slipper Flippers (cloth, boots, -50% run speed, +50% swim speed)
Angler’s Bucket Hat (cloth, head, +50 fishing, equip: can track fish schools)
Dwarven Fishing Pole (gun, +10 fishing)

Herbalism
 **Plant Monsters**
Herbalism is in need of many more nodes. They have fewer nodes than mining, and mining allows you to harvest from a node 2-5 times. By allowing all plant monsters to be harvested after death (like skinning does to beasts) by herbalists of the appropriate skill, you add a decent amount more potential nodes for them. This isn’t needed just for the extra materials, it’s mostly useful to help herbalists level up, as currently lack of nodes is a large issue in that regard. Plant monsters include Lashers, Treants, Bog Monsters, etc.

 **Wood Gathering**
A new set of nodes for Herbalism, this would ask for various Branch nodes to be added to many of the trees around Azeroth. By having a high enough Herbalism, you could harvest the branches, which Woodworkers can turn into planks of wood, bows, and staves. This gives herbalists new nodes to harvest from and an exciting new type of materials to sell or use themselves with a new paired skill.

 **Planting**
Take this away from Survival, and put it with Herbalism where it makes more sense. You don’t get skill level-ups from Planting. Honestly, I don’t think this needs to exist, but maybe some people like it? Change it so that consuming items like Watermelons and Pumpkins gives you seeds.

 **Harvest Food Nodes**
All existing fruit and vegetable items in the game are given spawn nodes that only Herbalists can harvest. Everything from Shiny Red Apple to bananas, watermelon, pumpkins, cave mold, various mushrooms, and more.

Mining
 **Increase Silver Nodes**
This is a known issue with skill progression, and can easily be fixed. Darkshire has reason to have many silver mines and deposits, with its themes of fighting against darkness- especially undead and werewolves. We could spread these around in certain zones that currently lack mining anyways.

 **Rock Monsters**
All rock monsters should be able to be mined for Stone of an appropriate level. There’s not that many of these so it wouldn’t be that impactful, but it’d help verisimilitude.

 **Cracking Shells**
Let miners use their mining ability on dead Crabs and Turtles, with the reward being meat of the appropriate type. They can’t improve their skill this way.

 **Mining Picks**
Let the equippable weapon style mining picks (like the one from Gilijim’s Island) be used as a substitute for the default mining pick, and don’t make me equip it for the bonus, just to require me to have a normal mining pick in my bag.

Skinning
 **Finkle’s Dagger**
Fix this so that it doesn’t need to be equipped for the bonus. It should just replace my Skinning Dagger, having to carry both is a bit pointless.

 **Fix What’s Skinnable**
Worgen are skinnable, but furbolgs, murlocks, yeti, and naga aren’t. Scorpions are skinnable, but spiders and crabs are not. Sometimes dragons aren’t skinnable sometimes they are, same with silithids (there seems to be no logic to it). Sharks should be skinnable and lake monsters too. Honestly, we don't need more skinning nodes, but the inconsistencies are irritating.

 **Retuning**
Maybe it’s just because I’m on Tel’Abim, but leather (especially up to Medium) gets sold on the AH for less than you could sell it to a vendor. That means that the supply far out strips the demand. We either need more ways to spend leather goods, or tuning to the drop rates to make this more viable. Skinning has sooo many nodes compared to other gathering professions, and you can always find more because mob spawn rates are much higher than herb/mining nodes. Maybe adjusting drop rates so more often skinning results in Scraps could help solve this?

Tailoring
 **Cloaks**
Drastically expand the recipe list for cloaks made by tailors. Make them more available for classes at all levels. Focus on adding element resistance as it makes sense for certain dungeons/zones leveling up.

 **Battle Standards**
Allow tailors to make Battle Standards, which are consumables that create an AOE centered on the banner, either a buff or debuff. Banners last 10 mins and may be placed at a rate of 1 per hour. Enemies can interact with a banner to eliminate it. Banners should look like your faction, or possibly your guild tabard?

 **Pillows**
These are cloth and feather one-time-use (or charge-based) consumables that allow you to regain more MP while sitting. Pillows are an ingredient to some Woodworking furniture recipes.

 **New Bags**
Taking inspiration from Ammo Pouches, let Tailors make bags that give passive bonuses while equipped. Reagent Bag: Holds only reagents, gives 1% increased spellpower.

 **Harvest Spider Silk**
When in spider areas with visible spider silk, allow for Spider Silk nodes to appear that only Tailors can harvest. Harvesting does not give a skill increase.

 **Rebalance Existing Recipes**
Right now there are a lot of lvl 50-60 craftable gear that is just not usable given the stats on it, especially not worth it for the high amount of materials it takes to craft these pieces. They either need material cost reduced or they need better itemization.

 **Survival**
Even though I don’t think this skill should exist as-is, and think that its parts should be split up into other professions, if you were to keep it these are things I’d do to change it:

 *Grappling Hook*
Pulls an enemy up to a certain level towards you.
 *Traps and Snares*
Lets the user craft snares and traps that work similarly to Hunter traps. Decoys, slows, etc.
 *Repellant*
These are consumables which reduce your threat radius for mobs up to a certain level.
 *Food Bags*
Bag that only stores food. Gives you a minor “well fed” buff even if your food is generic.

New Profession
Woodworking
Uses wood gathered by herbalists from trees with nodes on them.

 **Staves**
Uses jewels and enchanting mats as well. You can choose this as a specialization. Gives casters a reason to want this profession, but recipes should probably lean Druid-heavy.

 **Polearms**
Uses metals as well. You can choose this as a specialization. Great for Hunters, maybe some Warriors or Paladins.

 **Bows**
Uses Spider Silk as well. Woodworkers also craft arrows, which are taken away from Engineers. You can choose this as a specialization. Obviously great for Hunters. Engineers keep crossbows.

 **Survival Items**
Take torches and fishing boats away from Survival and put them here.

 **Furniture**
Start with a simple Wooden Chair, these are all “Toys” that you can craft. Sitting on Furniture causes you to regen HP/MP at a better rate than sitting on the ground. Many furniture recipes call for Pillows, Cloth, or Metals as well as Wood to make.

 **Milling**
You take wood from branches and turn it into paper. This does not give level-ups. The paper is used to make Vellum and Books.
