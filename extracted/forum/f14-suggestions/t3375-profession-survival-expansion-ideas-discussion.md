# Profession: Survival. Expansion Ideas (Discussion)

## Post 20534 by Seaofgioy — 2022-07-27T18:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20534#p20534 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good day, fellow turtles.
Let me preface this with this: I don't know what the Turtle team has in mind for Survival, but at the moment is would seem solely centered on off-hands, a few items that help exploration and campfires. I seem to remember traps were mentioned once.

I would propose to expand it slightly, with a bit more flavour with items such as  **Fishing Poles**! Starting from a simple "Makeshift Fishing Pole" with a +3 modifier to fishing and crafted using some Simple wood and a Stranglekelp, or a Coarse thread.
As your skill in Survival increases, you might be able to craft fishing poles from Iron rods or some such as well. We could even craft some bows in much the same way!
I think it would make for an interesting game loop to have Logs drop from Treant elementals across Azeroth, maybe having them have to be cured with salt (or ash, but I think we should give priority to not cluttering the game with one-off items instead of perfect realism) and have these logs used for higher level fishing poles, or different looking tents, with the same effect, but maybe use wool cloth to make the white pirate ones, or silk for the High Elf variant, each Outline given by respective rep/mobs. (Could go the extra mile and add a rare mob that drops it, but I believe that to be too much)

How cool would it be to have a **"Animated fishing rod"** +20 fishing **It seems to know where the fish's at** ?

We could have something like a "Shadow Infused Bow" with some bonus shadow damage on hit, but I feel that **arrows** would be more fitting. Have a Cured Withered Log combine with some Shadow oil and Shadowgem for Shadow-Tipped Bolts. Or the same for frost, which would work similar to other vanilla weapons that had a FireDamage component, ignoring armor for that 2-5 damage.

And for last, we could have something to work on the "area of effect buff" theme that lanterns have: Incense burners, or Flares: the first is an off-hand that consumes a specific reagent to give a very small buff to close by players. The second is a consumable, made with, as an example, Silversage, Buiseweed and fine thread that, when used consumes it and gives a very tiny buff around you. I do not want to see these used in raids, but they could help out groups of players.  This might be bordering on feature creep, I'll end it here before I go on a deep dive on using Crawler Claws and Beady Eye Stalks to craft fish glue.

Let me know what you thought about this suggestion, is it too much feature creep? Too far from our beloved vanilla?
Personally, I believe Survival, being a secondary profession, should work with materials available to everyone, just as the other three do. I'll await anxiously your comment and critique!

Heres a couple of fun ones.
Grappling Hook - Lets you throw a hook and pull yourself to it. Useful for climbing up small cliffs and obstacles. Crafted with 1 Iron Hook and 2 Rope. 20 Yard Range. Does not require line of sight (this might be a maybe part), has a 1 second cast timer. Idea comes from Legend of Zelda hooks and the Outlaw Rogue from retail, and think it could be fun to have some form of 'climbing' ability for all players to get a hold of.

Repelling Line/Anchor - Lets you slow your descent. Not quite a parachute so that engineers still have those. 1 second cast time gives a 15 Second Slow Fall buff, must be target cast within range of a solid structure. Crafted with 1 Iron Hook and 2 Rope. I feel this will help the survival profession's description of "Allowing a Survivalist to create tools to overcome obstacles...".

Simple Critter Trap - Makes a simple trap for critters for when you really need some food. Crafted with 1 Rope, 1 Scrap Metal, 1 Trap Trigger. Critters that step on it will be killed and can be looted for Morsel Meat. Morsel Meat can be right-click cooked at a Bright Campfire to make Cooked Meat, a basic low level food equivalent to tough meat or some other low level food youd buy from a vendor.

Simple Remedy - This may belong in First Aid or even Gardening and not Survival. Allows you to create a Simple Remedy that removes a single disease (as we really only have poison removal in professions). 1 Minute Cooldown. Requires Health Giving Herbs (purchasable/found in the wild?).

Water Bucket - Allows you to collect water from any water source (right click while standing in water, creates 'Filled Water Bucket'), then right-click to boil it over a Bright Campfire for sanitary reasons. Requires 1 Metal Ingot. Produces 5 Refreshing Spring Water (a very low level drink).
