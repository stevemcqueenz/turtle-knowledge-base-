# CC2 Feral needs help in PvP.

## Post 119127 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-06T08:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119127#p119127 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Before CC2 Feral Druids were one of the strongest specs in PvP, they had good damage in cat form, okish damage in bear, which was fine as they were tanky while in Bear Form.

They had a great survivability tool for deep feral (Frenzied regen) before the nerf was deep ferals only way at staying alive if you were taking heavy damage, if you didn't run a Nature's Swiftness spec for the instant healing touch.

After class changes 2, every class does alot more damage especially casters, so bear gets nuked down really fast, and now Frenzied regeneration is nerfed into the ground you can't keep up.

The old Frenzied regeneration was abit overturned in CC1, but if you stopped attacking the bear they wouldn't gain rage to carry on healing. Nerfing the healing and cooldown at the same time was too far.

Also they have nerfed bears damage this patch(for tanking reasons) and because of that we suffered in PvP.
Most classes damage was buffed, cats got no real damage buff either just another bleed playstyle which isn't reliable.

Problem with feral atm is while in cat form you do OK damage to casters, but paladins and shamans are near impossible to kill, you can be destroyed in a few seconds in cat if you get stunned cause it's too squishy and you can't get into bear form.

Also feral really struggles against flash bombs, it's a 10 second fear consume that anyone can use on a 1 minute CD that doesn't break on damage if you get bombed in cat form this is a nail in the coffin if your pvp trinket is on cooldown.

If you go bear you basically just a punching bag that does no damage, even less now thanks to the side effects of the damage nerf because of tank threat in CC2.

Hopefully they can look over this soon, every class seems to be scaling like crazy but ferals scaling isnt great, will probably be worse after Kara.
