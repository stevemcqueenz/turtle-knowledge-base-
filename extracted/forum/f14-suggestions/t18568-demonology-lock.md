I focus more on the flavour strengthening. Two alternative ways.

The first is making all warlocks master demonologists, who specialise in dealing differnt styles of damage, such as DOT or fierce impacting magics. The original position of demonology spec can be replaced with a melee-oriented one, making the warlock able to tank. In general, this notion is strengthening the demonic influence and flavour on all warlocks.

The second choice is remaking current non-demonology specs. For example, affliction spec can be redesigned into necromance, involving undead control. The English word warlock is typically a dark/evil magic caster, which is inline with the necromancer unit's style in historic Warcraft games. It's worth mentioning that cononical deathknights were the creation by orcish warlocks.

Small aside: why can't we single target buff our summoned demons now? Works on hunter pets..

Anyhow, the main change I would like to see on demonology warlocks is pet-preeminence. Specifically for a
having a true threat tank in the voidwalker. It doesn't need to deal a lot of damage, but it should be able to do its one role well: keep threat. Would love to have the Demonology capstone be something like

"Transfers 50% of threat from warlock to voidwalker" instead of the 20% bonus to healing. This would, at the very least, make it the first threat dump target instead of the warlock when we out-threat the actual tank.

The other minor thing I'd like to have for the Succubus pet is for its Seduction to be non-channeled. This would make it a great way to interrupt spellcasting, and allow the pet to actually dps while still doing its CC utility role in normal group play.
