it would be cool if attacking mobs of the opposite faction, you would be flagged for pvp, it would be very in the spirit of the RP component of the server, leveling in Hilsbrad I often noticed the picture when a horde player kills packs of alliance mobs and I as an alliance hero would like to help the poor suffering, but all I could do is just watch them die, and even worse when the undead player eat those poor people to heal himself  xD, same situation i saw in Ashenvale, when horde players raid small elves camp with their brave horde NPC friends, here at least i can kill those NPC orcs but not the player. It is not in the spirit of the RP server, immersion is a key here i believe.

I think such change could revitalize the world pvp and not harm pve players, I know that some hardcore and pve people will be against, but world is full of quest with neutral mobs to kill, and there is tooltip exist that show if mob belong to hostile faction.
