# [QOL] Add dummy buffs for Warlock's Enslave Demon and Unleashed Potential

## Post 135910 by Suspicious — 2025-06-16T09:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135910#p135910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Demonology warlock is pretty fun, but managing demon timers is unnecessarily obtuse. Enslave Demon has a 5 minute cooldown, but there's no way to accurately tell how close to running out you are. Likewise, Infernal, Felguard, and Doomguard summons are on a 3 minute duration before they break free. The Unleashed Potential talent also lasts 20 seconds, but there is no way of telling that it is about to expire.

I suggest the following additions:
 1) Give the Warlock a 'dummy' 5 minute timer buff while Enslave Demon is active, cancelled when the demon is dismissed or breaks free of control.
 2) Give the Warlock a 'dummy' 3 minute timer buff while a Greater Demon is summoned, cancelled when the demon is dismissed or breaks free of control.
 3) Give the Warlock a 'dummy' 20 second timer buff whenever they have an active Unleashed Potential buff on their demon, refreshed/stacking up to 3 whenever their funnels tick or they crit.
