> Patch changes
>  World of Warcraft: Cataclysm Patch 4.0.3 (15-Nov-2010): Removed.
>  World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King Patch 3.3.2 (2010-01-02): Damage done by Shadow Bolt increased by 2/4/6/8/10%, up from 1/2/3/4/5%.

>  World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King Patch 3.1.0 (14-Apr-2009): Now increases the damage done by your Shadow Bolt spell by 1/2/3/4/5%, and causes your target to be vulnerable to spell damage, increasing spell critical strike chance against that target by 1/2/3/4/5%. Effect lasts 30 sec.
>  World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King Patch 3.0.8 (2009-01-20): Changed from applying a debuff to the target to a self-buff on the Warlock.
>  World of Warcraft Patch 1.11.0 (20-Jun-2006): Periodic damage spells and non-damaging spells will no longer consume charges of Shadow Vulnerability.
>  World of Warcraft Patch 1.10.2 (02-May-2006): Will now proc properly after landing a critical strike with Shadow Bolt.
>  World of Warcraft Patch 1.2.1 (21-Dec-2004): Added a limited duration to the effect.
>  World of Warcraft Patch 1.1.0 (07-Nov-2004): Changed from increases the next 1/2/3/4/5 sources of Shadow damage dealt to 4 sources.
