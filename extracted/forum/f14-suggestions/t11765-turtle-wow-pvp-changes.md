The problem with moving Holy Strike deep into the ret tree is that tank paladins need it to generate decent threat.

Holy Strike uses both spell and melee crit - doesn't sound super busted at first but because paladins have by far the best int-to-spellcrit ratio of any class by a huge margin (29.5 int = 1% spell crit, whereas all other classes require ~59-60 int for 1% spellcrit) means that paladins get a huge amount of free crit chance for Holy Strike. It also helps for Holy Shock crits when Divine Favor is on cooldown. You might say "but paladins require 54 int to get 1% spell crit!" Not on Turtle WoW, this seems to be an undocumented change.

Holy Strike, like Seal of Command Procs, can "double dip" the bonus damage from Vengeance. Basically, it will calculate the weapon damage components with that 15% bonus and then give the final damage amount another 15% bonus as it is a holy damage spell. The end result is that Seal of Command procs and Holy Strike do ~22.5% more damage rather than 15%.

Even without the aforementioned factors, just the concept of an ability that converts the entirety of your weapon damage into holy damage and gives it a 45% SP ratio is a bit absurd. Seems like it was designed with RIghteous Fury in mind for prot paladin threat generation but with a complete blindspot towards how much unmitigateable burst damage this could do with a ret focused build.

Tank paladins currently have much worse threat generation compared to bear druids and furyprot warriors. The Turtle devs are going to have to figure out a way to nerf Holy Strike without making tank paladin even worse than currently is.
