## Post 67482 by Skattberg — 2023-09-28T21:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67482#p67482 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've played on this server for almost a year now and I absolutely love it. Mostly i go for the pvp content and there are a few things i wonder about/think should be changed to balance:

Paladins - my seething hatred for this class can't be stressed enough, but let me give some constructive criticism - in classic wow their strength was their utility - they had almost everything but a high dmg output. Now that they have an insane capability for damage and most of all - nukes - they are absurdly unbalanced. They can cleanse magic, poison and disease with relatively low mana cost and its spamable, the change to divine shield is most welcome but is not enough imo, they have strong heal, even if they are retri, blessing of freedom and on top of that Lay on Hands right before holy strike for 2k non crit no crusader strike buffs.
Its just so frustrating to play against a paladin with mid to decent gear - not to speak of full naxx geared, their only weakness is that they don't have a hard interrupt/silence
Give Cleanse a 4 sec cooldown?
Make Cleanse only useable on friendlies? But also make it remove curses?
The damage makes them viable, i get it. But remove some of their utility to balance it
I also think Cleanse affects Mind Flay, as in it removes the effect completely, i don't think this was the case in classic or it is an intentional change on Turtle Wow

Alterac Valley
It still baffles me that the battleground is the way it is. It is so unbalanced in favor of the alliance side. They still have a backdoor, there are too many npcs making horde advances difficult while allies can breeze through after first gy cap. No choke points, nothing. While 4 or 5 allies are enough to hold bridge to ally base, due to bunkers and npcs quick respawn rates.
I saw another thread with the suggestion to mix up the teams, so that you are randomly assigned to either horde or ally side. I dont really want it but by this state im almost up for it when allies win 90% of games.
OR why not make npcs stop respawning after gy/bunkers are caped? For both sides ofc

Arathi Basin
WHY -  WHYWHYWHY are the icons not there? What is the point? To force the teams to communicate? Please don't. It is such an unnecessary and annoying detail that doesn't help at all.

I'd like to end with that this rant comes from a place of love. Turtle wow has done something amazing in expanding the feeling of classic wow but still keeping it true and not too over the edge!

## Post 67522 by Jstansberry (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-29T06:16:00Z

Adding a cooldown to cleanse is a bit much - it's paladin's only real answer to not being kited (Blessing of Freedom gets instantly purged by Shamans, I'd know because my favorite char rn is my Shaman), but either the burst damage or utility of paladins does need to be nerfed. Paladin is extremely strong in PvP right now and the scaling on holy strike is insane. A naxx geared paladin outscales a naxx geared arms warrior in PvP burst damage right now and that's unacceptable tbh. Holy Strike is a very overtuned ability and Crusader Strike should not be as spammable as it is - I think that Crusader Strike would be a better candidate to give a cooldown, maybe 2.5-3s. Holy Strike scaling should also be reduced. Turtle devs should find other ways to make paladin dps viable in pve - or don't and have them suffer the same fate as enhancement shamans. Balancing burst capability for pvp and consistent dps for pve is not easy - although that's only part of the reason why paladins are so cracked in pvp.

Even the archers in Alliance bunkers have serious advantages over Horde tower archers because bunkers are just better. AV is indeed Alliance-biased and the backdoor to the Horde base should be blocked to Alliance but the other things about it that make it Alliance-biased would require some serious changes to the layout of the Alliance and Horde bases that are likely not worth the dev time in comparison to cooler things the dev team could be working on.

AV sucks compared to other BGs, anyway. The real fix is to either nerf the honor gain from AV or buff the honor/rep gain from other BGs to incentivize players to do something else other than spam AV and semi-afk through the match and play it as PvE content to get ranks. As it is now AV provides the most honor gained per hour and it has created a meta where if you aren't spamming AV and trying to base race and end it as fast as possible you will take much longer to grind out the same amount of honor as people who do. I'd even be happy if they took it out of the bonus BG day rotation entirely, but I know that's a bit extreme.

I'd like to see them make AB/WSG more appealing by making the rep grind more comparable to AV (significantly faster) and by increasing honor gain from AB/WSG objectives. WSG might be kind of tricky because 3-0 matches are much more common than 3-2 so honor gain should be improved baseline rather than just from flag caps in that case - having all the rep gain be from flag caps as it is now is also very unappealing compared to AV's easy guaranteed rep gains.

One note to devs - when one class is uncharacteristically chosen, it is going to be because it’s overturned. Every YouTube and Forum post is saying paladins are the meta. Druid is a mid endgame tank (or at least that’s what the discourse says and there is a lack of them) which leaves warriors. So Horde is struggling to put teams together for PVE. Which means they struggle to be geared for PVP. Match that with a 4k+ average health gap between the alliance and horde side. It’s just not balanced. I say nerf the paladin or at least boost the shaman. Shaman (lore speaking - looking at you Thrall) could wear plate. You boost a talent for their defensive strat. Now you are looking at a more balanced game. Each faction 3 tank options. Though mechanically very different,  shaman and paladins both with the same health range would make a much more balanced game and shift meta conversations. — also eliminate alliance meta chaser overflows (meaning more horde players and less alliance) and you functionally would eliminate the need for cross faction PvP.
