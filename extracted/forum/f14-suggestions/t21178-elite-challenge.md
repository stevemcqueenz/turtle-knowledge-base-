# Elite Challenge

## Post 147065 by Potatoedream — 2025-08-19T19:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147065#p147065 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are players who would like to play hardcore but hate the fact that they lose the character if they die. Others would like to have a different challenge but with same level of difficulty of Hardcore.

Here I bring you an idea from another server: Elite challenge.

<https://prnt.sc/MIsxBzaHVKEH>

As you can see in the picture, the challenge transforms for you and only for you, all enemies into elite enemies. Increasing their difficulty.

In the picture I used a level 1 rogue to fight against a level 2 wolf, tried to use the final movement only at 5 actions points, I was able to use it 2 times and before the death of that wolf, it left me as you see in the picture, about to die!

Usually, you can even defeat a level 4 enemy being you level 1, but in this challenge, defeat alone a level 3 is impossible.

Here are some features with this challenge:

 - you and other players with the challenge active will see enemies as elites. Enemies in dungeons and raids will stay as they originally are.  There are some dungeons with tons of non elite enemies and if you transform them into elites, it could become, probably, impossible to defeat without a wipe, specially if they swarm on you.

 - other players will keep seeing enemies as normal, and here I offer 2 options: since they see enemies as normal, if you attack one and then a normal player attack that enemy too, that player could kill that enemy easly for you. To avoid this, players without this challenge active can not attack the enemy you fight if you attack it first, but if you attack an enemy another player attacked first, you can help that player but the damage you dealt by you will be the one received by that enemy as an elite.

Second option is, any player can help you but if you attacked it first, at the moment they attack it too, those players will see that enemy as an elite enemy, and only that enemy.

 -all elite enemies grants you x2 exp. If you use a tent, it doubles the base experience, so if an enemy gives you 100 of exp, you will receive 300 of exp. Slow and steady challenge would grant you 100 of exp.

 - all drops required from quests will have its chance to be dropped increased to 50% if they chance is below 50% and to 100% if the original chance was 51% or more. No changes to other type of drops.

 - this is optional: in open world, the distance of dectection of enemies will be reduced to half its original number and enemies in group of 2 or more will not help their "partners" unless you are 1 level above of them or more.

 - under your name you will have a title like "Elite challenger" or "Elite hero", instead of "still alive" from HC, or something similar.

This challenge allows players who love have difficulties to have "better" the experience of doing quests together. Trying to kill hard enemies with other players in a "chilling" experience like Twow does is something pretty enjoyable and great!

This idea, as I said at the begining, is to allow players have an optional difficult challenge and not only hardcore.
Mix hardcore and this new challenge could be possible? But I do not know if it would be better have it as an exception (you can not active HC and Elite challenge at same time). Would you preffer being able to active both?

This idea can be modified and you, players, can add, modify or remove any feature or thing I detailed by posting it in the comments section!
