# Endless/more important levelling — an old dream of this Turtle

## Post 19750 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-07-06T21:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19750#p19750 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I love leveling in WoW, the concept of "endgame" doesn't seem to match the vanilla philosophy.
How do you think could be levelling made even more significant?

Here are some ideas I've had:

 1. Once max level is reached, an option to reset to level one again endlessly, but with some of the progress preserved, details & possible rewards are debatable.

 2. No max level! And for example XP gain greatly reduced after 60, or xp reseted to 0 of the current level after each death.

 3. Current and future "max level content" spread out through lower levels, for example naxx being at 60, but previous raids sooner, or new ones added to lower levels.

These are crude basic concepts feel free to improve them and share other ideas how to make the levelling experience even more important in Turtle WoW.

I like the idea, i would propose something like:

Once you get to lv 60 a quest unlocks in order to reset your lv

Reseting will give you a passive spell much like the hardcore or slow&steady
Getting to lv 60 with that passive will give you some rewards (ie turtle coins) and increase the rank of that passive (so rank2, rank3 etc.)
Reseting your lv will also reset your quests done so you can do them again for different rewards.
People who reseted their lv (aka the ones who got the passive) will have the option to scale down their lv temporary
Quests reward items won't be equipable until said quest is done again in order to prevent a lv 1 with no lv requirement questing gear from lv 58 quests

Ofc that's a lot of work and scripting, but hey, one can dream

It also gives people the option of getting different rewards from quest which offer multiple high value items (IE "in dreams" quest line). It's also another way to gain rep with a faction without having to grind for mob drops or run raids over and over. This would also help disperse population across more level brackets and increase availability of players for low level dungeons
