# Dragon head buffs

## Post 159250 by Fayz2 — 2025-11-11T23:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159250#p159250 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Could we get a repeatable quest for each dragon head that opens after you have completed the story quest?  This repeatable quest does not pay out any rep, gold, or items, it only queues the head buff.  This would stop dragon heads from often being deleted or left on the boss because no one can use them, and would have the benefit of allowing the buff to be available for levelers more often.  To go along with this, updating the CD to 1 hour to account for the additional heads would keep players who get one that offers them an item from being locked out for so long with the influx of new heads.  As World buffs are not allowed in raids, this would also not change anything for the endgame.
