Lately i noticed that some dungeons are underpopulated because other dungeons are better or easier to access (for example cresent grove underpopulated because scarlet monastery is too convinient)

My idea to motivate people to visit a diversity of dungeons (join several queues) is to create a explorers league daily quest with the objective to kill for example 3 or 5 bosses from different dungeons, or loot historically important relics from different dungeon locations and in return get rep, xp, fashion coins, food/potions or other balanced rewards, maybe the explorers league could have explorers items, its own reputation and reputation rewards!

I think i have to clarify something on my mind. My approach for a solution isnt to make it easier to get to dungeons, because its definitely against the philosophy of the game and the travels and walking, tho it might seem annoying to players, is one of the most important aspects that gives the game its immersion and perception of scale.

The thing with quests is, that it is easy to implement and give it sense in the world. i agree it shoudnt be the daily quests, no one likes them, but there could be an epic quest chain around the dungeons, especially now that there are so many different options and more and more coming, i think it would be great to conenct them in a adventuerer-guild type organisation!
