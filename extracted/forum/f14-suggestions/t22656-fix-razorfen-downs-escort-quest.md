# "Fix" Razorfen Downs escort quest

## Post 157759 by Clintonm — 2025-10-29T21:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157759#p157759 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Yes, I know the behaviour in Turtle WoW matches Vanilla.

For those who don't know, there's an escort quest in Razorfen Downs, that is started inside the dungeon. The problem is the escort quest isn't just one quest, it's a two quest chain. Firstly, one must talk to [Belnistrasz](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=8516/belnistrasz), accept the breadcrumb quest [Scourge of the Downs](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=3523/scourge-of-the-downs), and then immediately hand in that quest to him. THEN, and only then, is the quest [Extinguishing the Idol](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=3525/extinguishing-the-idol) available, from the same NPC: [Belnistrasz](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=8516/belnistrasz).

Here's the problem. Usually when there's an escort quest in a dungeon, all that's required is for one group member to accept the quests, and as long as the other party members don't have a full quest log, upon one group member accepting the quests all other group members will get a popup saying they can also accept the quest.

This is great.

But this doesn't work with this Razorfen Downs quest.

Because what inevitably happens is that someone in the group that doesn't know the peculiarity of this quest will just see a quest marker in the dungeon, and pick it up thinking it's an ordinary dungeon quest. Then they'll see a question mark and immediately hand it in. Then they'll see an exclamation point again and pick up the new quest, not realising it's an escort quest.

If this second quest wasn't an escort quest this would be all fine, all the other group members could do the same thing and pick up the second quests. BUT, because it's an escort quest, the game immediately tries to share the second quest with all other group members. But because the other group members haven't completed the first quest, they can't receive the second quest, i.e. the escort.

As a result, the rest of the group is pissed off, because once this happens and the escort starts it can't be restarted. The other group members that didn't get the quest can't be bothered doing the escort because they didn't get a chance to do the quest, so the escort mob just dies, and everyone is annoyed for the rest of the run.

Yes, this is true to vanilla. I don't think it's good gameplay.

What one has to do to complete this escort quest is to get EVERYONE in the group to collect the first breadcrumb quest, hand it in, and then STOP! Don't accept the second quests. And then when EVERYONE has completed the first quest, one group member can start the actual escort quest and ONLY THEN will it get shared among group members.

Instead, when I ran Razorfen Downs, someone didn't know this, despite the leader saying something on chat, just before the mob appeared to NOT accept the quest, yet someone did, and ruined picking up the escort quest for everyone. There was some swearing and it just ruined the vibe for the rest of the run. Fortunately it wasn't me that screwed it up for everyone but it may well have been because I had completely forgot about this quirk since playing Classic years ago.

What I suggest to anyone doing Razorfen Downs is to explain this to everyone BEFORE even entering the dungeon, and make sure everyone understands before going in. But it's quite complicated to explain, takes a bit of time because it's the only escort quest that has this annoying design, and someone might still forget.

But what I also suggest the Turtle WoW team do is just change the behaviour of this quest from the frustrating vanilla behaviour of two quest to just be a single quest that starts the escort. Just include the breadcrumb quest text and few hundred experience in the main escort quest rewards. Yes, this is less Blizz like, but who cares? This behaviour just results in situations that ruins the vibe of the run, and is unfun for everybody. This is an easy QoL fix for everyone and I can't imagine who would actually miss the experience of being the person blamed for ruining the escort quest and it's nice Stamina ring reward for everyone or alternatively being on the receiving end of not being able to complete a quest in a dungeon due to an over enthusiastic clicker.
