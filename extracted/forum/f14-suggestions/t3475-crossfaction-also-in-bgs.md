I saw the slippery slope that turned vanilla into retail, players looking for even more convenience each time.

- Gathering is boring, why am I getting killed by other players on a PvP server? > Flying mounts.

- Walking to a dungeon is boring, why do I have to talk to other people? I just want loot! > LFG + instant teleport to dungeon.

- Why do alliance has paladin and not horde? > Blood elves (ignored lore and destroyed horde identity).

- Hybrid classes are not 100% efficient > Streamline every classes (granted they're trying to bring back class/spec identities now)
Just a warning about the slippery slope because once you get your shaman dwarves and forsaken paladins, tauren paladins and goblin shamans will be next and it'll only end once every race can be every class like in retail.

Like Moebius said, cross faction PvE makes sense and is interesting but cross faction PvP would make 0 sense lorewise (this is a RP server). Battlegrounds are faction themed, WSG: Nelves vs Orcs, AB: Humans vs Forsakens, AV: Dwarves vs Orcs.

An Illusion system could work like you said but because of shaman and paladins being faction locked it can't work atm. And yes I understand you want this lock removed but imo faction locked classes are good, it helped forge horde and alliance conflicting identities.
