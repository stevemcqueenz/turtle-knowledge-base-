# Rethinking Glyph Scribes

## Post 12326 by Merikkinon (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2021-09-18T01:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12326#p12326 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Recent update removed the gaming glyphs from Speedy turtle companion, and put them with the Scribe in each starter zone. I can grasp the want to have Speedy just be about the Donation Shop items.

I think this is a bit of a mistake in implementation. Or a half-measure. Solution offered at end, but here are the arguments:

There are some problems with the glyphs that represent inconsistent nature of 'having' them in your possession.

 1) **Diplomacy** only works if it is in your bags/keyring.

 2) **Twinking** works regardless of where it is - bank, bag, keyring.

 2) **Exhaustion**, and **Turtle** and **HC** (I believe) only works if it is in your bags/keyring.

 *The kicker is 'Twinking'. It is ALWAYS ON.*

Previously, players could throttle their accumulation of XP by taking the glyph from Speedy for a bit, then ditching it. This allowed for a decent coverage of all quests if people were wanting to get max rep as they went through leveling up. This doesn't happen anymore.

Why is the Twinking glyph in the starter zone and not in the main cities (where most people will be frequenting later in the game when they would want to move into perma-Twink status). NO ONE does that at level one, right?

 **SOLUTION #1**:  make the 'Twinking' glyph only active if it is in your bag/keyring (NOT bank)  <-- PREFERRED

 **SOLUTION #2**:  have the Starter Zone 'Junior Scribe', and create 'Master Scribes' in the main cities with all of the glyphs.
