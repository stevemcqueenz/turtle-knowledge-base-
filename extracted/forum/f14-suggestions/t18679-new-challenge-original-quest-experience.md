# New challenge - original quest experience

## Post 127147 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-17T12:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127147#p127147 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently you get xp from quests that are 25 levels below your character level.
It leads to meta in leveling then you pick all available challenges and outlevel all quests.

Proposal is: new challenge that forces you to complete only quests of your character level and above. They are marked as yellow-orange-red in quest log.

It will be fun to see completionists that pick all existing challenges, but in the same time can't cheese this challenge by killing grey mobs.

It's because of how changes to pre-existing things (Spells, Talents) and new implementations (overtuned Mobs in places like Gilneas, custom +X% to Mobs' Stats I forgot how much, to battle "power creep", essentially hurting the leveling experience). This Challenge would immediately not be doable (in any remotely fun way, at least) for anyone who is Melee (especially Warriors and Rogues). They tackle everything on, only to do Quests up to 25 CLs and fight Mobs at a minimum of 3 CLs below themselves, because it's a nightmare otherwise (I still see that as totally lame and contradictory of the point of a uh, y'know, "challenge").

If for instance the changes to, say, Slam being weaker and slower, and Hamstring having a CD, and Improved Hamstring being gone with Counterattack sucking, and Execute having a CD that can only be circumvented by taking up an early-mid-Tier Fury Talent, Mortal Strike also being weaker, and many more -- were reversed? The Warrior leveling process alone would be ten times better. Tinker with overtuned custom Mobs? Even better. Revert the lazy Mob scaling and instead properly tackle Class/gear imbalance at endgame as should have been done? Splendid.

Until then, it's just going to keep being a mockery and people overburdening themselves with more Challenges than they can handle will continue to look for cheap ways to keep progressing.

But personally, I'm not against the idea. It's just that... it wouldn't be popular. It wouldn't work well. By itself? Yeah. But in conjunction with others? Less and less, the more you add on top. People would just try and look for more ways to cheese, then.

Honestly, this just makes me think of having a Zone-locked Challenge, like in Elden Ring that some people there do. But that would be so annoying as well, here.
