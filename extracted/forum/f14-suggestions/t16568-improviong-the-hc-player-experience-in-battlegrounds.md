Dear Turtle Team,

I would like to propose a suggestion regarding the participation of hardcore players in battlegrounds.

Currently, hardcore players do not engage in PvP, for obvious reasons.

 **Suggestion:** Allow hardcore players to participate in battlegrounds without the risk of permanent death. Implementing this change would encourage more players to queue for battlegrounds, significantly revitalizing the low-level brackets and enhancing the overall PvP experience for both hardcore and non-hardcore players alike.

Thank you for considering this suggestion. I would be happy to discuss any feedback or refinements you, or anyone in the community may have.

Sincerely,
Kagrot/Kagrott
