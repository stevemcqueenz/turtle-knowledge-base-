# Transmog for weapon enchants

## Post 129340 by Arraris — 2025-05-02T05:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129340#p129340 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To the TWoW devs, gods, and fellow yurtles   smiling_turtle  ,

I would like to humbly ask that an option be added to the transmog menu: either the ability for the weapon transmog to hide the visual appearance of any enchants on said weapon - or the ability to transmog the enchant visual on your weapon to other enchant visuals you have previously collected.

As a raider who also loves to RP, it is a little disappointing and frustrating that my RP transmog options are restricted to either using weapons that already have a visual effect (and thus the enchant visual is suppressed) or only using appearances and aesthetics that match the enchantment.

I beseech the powers of yurtles on high; please consider this humble request for a future update. I know my humble self would greatly appreciate, and I assume some other RPers would also enjoy the additional options and flexibility.

Amen.
