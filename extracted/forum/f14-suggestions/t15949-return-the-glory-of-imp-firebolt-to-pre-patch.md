# Return the glory of imp firebolt to pre-patch

## Post 108595 by Suspicious — 2024-11-19T01:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108595#p108595 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In the most recent patch, the Succubus and Imp talents were combined. However, imp definitely got the short end of the stick. It already doesn't see much use, but nerfing the baseline talent from reducing Firebolt by 1 sec to 0.5 sec is actually a huge nerf.

Imp doesn't have a normal attack, so making their only offensive option go from a 1 second cast to a 1.5 second cast when talented into just kills the viability, especially considering the other buffs to Demonology.

I hope in the CC2 patch, this is addressed, but if not, I hope it will be considered.
