# New Tank Stats (reduced crit dmg taken)

## Post 149832 by Buuka — 2025-09-02T05:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149832#p149832 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We have this cool new stat that reduces crit damage taken that isn't being utilized. It needs to be better and more accessible. Bear tanks do not benefit much from defense rating, and it makes them homogenous with the other tanks. Bears should have reduced crit dmg taken instead, to keep their identity as a wall of fur that soaks damage.

Fury tanks could benefit from the stat as well, since their talents rely on them being crit. They could opt to stack this new stat instead, so they can afford to take some crits without exploding.

It would be useful on shamans as well, considering they have very little access to defense rating and are prone to exploding.

I would strictly make it reduced crit dmg taken, maybe give it a cooler name like resilience, buff it, put it on more gear, and offer it as enchants.

This would broaden the options tanks have, reduce the competition for defense rating, and make the hybrid tanks tanky in their own unique way.
