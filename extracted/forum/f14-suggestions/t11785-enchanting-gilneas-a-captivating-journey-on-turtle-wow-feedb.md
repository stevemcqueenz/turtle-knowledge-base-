# Enchanting Gilneas: A captivating journey on Turtle WoW [Feedback]

## Post 77901 by Whtmstttv — 2023-11-18T03:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77901#p77901 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am enchanted by the Gilneas location on the Turtle WoW server! Delving into the exploration of this amazing place, I have discovered numerous delightful aspects that I would like to highlight.

• First and foremost, the splendid execution of the location is awe-inspiring. Every corner is imbued with attention to detail, creating an incredibly realistic and captivating environment.

• The second undeniable positive is the music accompanying you. The sounds harmoniously blend with the atmosphere, creating a unique impression at every moment.

• Having traversed the entire location, I cannot help but note that it is impossible to separate the location from the main storyline. It is not just a custom location; it is an integral part of the WoW universe.

• Particularly impressive was the magnificent and expansive Gilneas City instance with engaging bosses, including Genn Greymane.

• *The only downside that brought some disappointment is the penultimate boss of the instance - Harlow Family Chest. The issue is that the loot from this boss does not drop directly from the boss but is found in a chest. During four visits to the instance, in three of them, it was announced in advance that the loot is in the chest, and a roll was suggested to determine who would open it. However, despite this, there was always a player who rushed, opened the chest, and automatically claimed the loot. This nuance undoubtedly became the only significant drawback, causing some disappointment.*

 **Overall, I want to express immense gratitude to the Turtle WoW developers for their tremendous work. Thank you very much for the fantastic experience and for the effort invested in creating this location.**  smiling_turtle_head
