# Need a Buff & Debuff Cap Increase... Again!

## Post 107139 by Nazzydragon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-08T18:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107139#p107139 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With 1.17.2, we're seeing more debuffs and buffs than ever before between new skills and talents.

In a 40 man raid, there's between 90-100+ debuffs that can (and should!) be thrown onto a boss easily, so many get pushed off... not good.

In my experience with feral druid, you're hitting that buff cap easily with consumables and new talents/tier buffs and will get stuck in your shapeshift form during raids (until you relog). This one is.. very problematic. I'm talking to warriors and paladins (mostly tanks) also easily hitting their buff caps and having buffs pushed off.

I think we need another raising on the buff & debuff caps before Kara40, ideally. Pretty please!
