# Caverns of Time suggestion

## Post 53199 by Whalemilk (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-12T16:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53199#p53199 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Caverns of Time has allowed us to travel back in time to experience key events in the Warcraft lore. With
Escape from Durnholde Keep, Opening of the Dark Portal, Battle for Mount Hyjal, etc. we got to have fun doing dungeons & raids.

So how about this for an idea. Travel into the future.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/eEHwt5e.gif)

Although we are all about vanilla here, we have to concede that there are some good dungeons/raids that were made in BC and WOTLK. Personally, I never experienced WOTLK content, but I heard it was pretty good. It would be cool to incorporate these dungeons/raids into TurtleWoW in a lore friendly way via Caverns of Time.
