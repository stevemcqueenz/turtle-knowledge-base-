# Demonic Sacrifice for every warlock

## Post 49433 by Seltsamuel — 2023-06-09T08:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49433#p49433 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Demonic Sacrifice is such a weird talent. It is in the talent tree to make your demon more powerful, just to get rid of it and make all points spent there rather pointless (baring Master Conjurer, Demonic Embrace and Demonic Aegis).

My proposal is to remove demonic sacrifice from the demonology talent tree and make it a base ability that can be trained at level 36 or 40. To compensate for the easy access of this ability, lower the fire and shadow damage bonus for the sacrificed imp and succubus to 5% fire and shadow damage (and maybe lower the mana and health replenishment of the other two demons as well?)

To compensate for the vacant spot I propose a new toggled ability called Dread Command, which lowers your damage dealt by 3-5% but gives a 15% of your stamina and intelligence to your demon and grants it 20% attack and 10% spell power from your shadow or fire spell damage, whichever is greater.
