You get dragonslayer from your faction capital if your faction drops dragonslayer. I feel like that holds a pretty decent sense of consistency and cool factor. Getting "Hey our cool friends at the alliance beat up the big dragon guy!" instead of what you normally would might feel... Weird. And even if the message stays the same, how do you solve the issue of an alliance hero being revered and celebrated as a hero of the horde? Like I said, consistency.

I also feel like we should try and preserve that faction specific feel and add more faction specific things to compensate some for the cross faction grouping. I personally love cross faction in spite of thinking myself a vanilla purist but it does detract some from that "Alliance Versus Horde" feel that a lot of people praise so much.

And like everyone else said, you REALLY don't need to be levelling with world buffs. They're a fun little bonus gift you get for travelling to your main city often, not a requirement to play the game. If you treat it like that, you're going to have less fun. I'm (not) sorry, but it's just how things work.
