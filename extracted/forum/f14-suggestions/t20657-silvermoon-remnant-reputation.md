# Silvermoon Remnant reputation

## Post 142981 by Daereidd — 2025-08-03T17:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142981#p142981 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
I'd like to ask for a more convenient way to get reputation for Silvermoon Remnant, currently, High elf player will barely reach honored after finishing the starting zone with almost no way to get it up to Revered without spending large amounts of gold for Runecloth. I would be okay with Revered requiring like 10-20 stacks of Runecloth to finish, but it takes around 500 stacks to reach Exalted i believe.

The easiest solution would be to add Daily quests for Alah'thalas zone, such as repeating the Wretched quest that wants the player to collect Arcane crystal boxes. Another very plausible quest would be to use the undead valley to Plaguelands, players could receive few quests from Halduron. Kill x amount of skeletons, collect x amount of plants, kill multiple types of undead mobs etc. The possibilities are many and I'd love a way for a fresh lvl 60 player to get the rep gear without grinding hours - might as well run few UBRS, Scholo and Strat runs and get better gear regardless.

Thanks for reading!
