Mana is one of the issues, but overall gameplay wise it's rather awkward. Baited relies on a crit from your pet, meaning you can't initiate with it and it also makes it rely on KC too much. KC on the other hand suffers from the same issue as Bestial Wrath, as it's both an offensive and a defensive CD.

Next is Coordinate Assault. If you play Survival, the talent is fine, but if you play ranged then you run in the following issues. Baited Shot shares a CD with Aimed Shot and Arcane Shot, so you can't use either to trigger the talent. Additionally, since it triggers from Steady Shot, it means that the optimal PvE BM build is yet again a Steady Shot spammer. And it also means that Frenzy, a talent that has historically been a 2 point investment for PvE, remains the same

I do think the devs had good intentions with the redesign, but for me at least, gameplay feel awkward
