# Request for PVE only glyph.

## Post 15202 by Capsacin — 2022-01-03T05:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15202#p15202 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to suggest a glyph that is the opposite of the new Warmode.

My thoughts are that it would be a 5% xp decrease and the inability to be flagged/take part in any pvp action. Perhaps allow buffs to be placed on pvp players that are not in combat but no ability to heal someone that is pvp flagged while not in your group.

My reasoning is such: I enjoy buffing just about everyone I come across while questing but with the new glyphs I now have to be extra careful to avoid buffing flagged players so I am not also flagged. This is also an issue in areas like the arena when placing fires and tents as I have been flagged for pvp while attempting to help others just to end up getting repeatedly ganked.

I have less than zero interest in the pvp side of this game and figured if the pvp players got a glyph then perhaps the carebears like me might also attempt to get one.

Thank you for your consideration.

All within the first few days mind you and i doubt this behavior is getting any better when more and more people join the server, because it's now kind of a pvp server.
