# Alterac Mountains Major Update

## Post 148123 by Teerack (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-25T07:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148123#p148123 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I always thought Alterac was one of those zones where blizzard kinda fumbled and didn't finish. There just wasn't anything hardly in the zone and there is a lot of history in that region, and somehow alterac valley is meant to be the zone, but there is just nothing in the zone that reminds me of alterac at all, so I went through and just tried to tie it more to the battleground lore and also thought a bit more snow would help it out.

It also doesn't do the zone any favors that the map for alterac has alway's been very simple when it comes to the ruins even tho there are many buildings there. I overlaid the zone map with the in game 3d renders of the map so everything should fit and be to scale using mostly just areas with no use at all(hills between zones) so that none of the old quests would need to be removed. the only thing I changed in terms of lay out is I made Dalaran a bit bigger to feel more grand, but dev's can really just do their own thing for dalaran. I also moved the entrances to Alterac valley to locations that actually make sense.

Alterac Mountains
---
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/CEY3W1p.jpeg)

Unchanged Locations
---

- **Ruins of Alterac** - Would be the same just needs the map to represent it better.
- **Strahnbrad** - Would be the same just needs the map to represent it better.
- **The Uplands** - Unchanged just with a more snowy texture.
- **Slaughter Hallow** - Unchanged
- **Crushridge Hold** - Unchanged
- **Growless Cave** - Unchanged
- **Old Dalaran** - Mostly unchanged ruins of dalaran just shifted over to make room for bigger dalaran.
Altered Locations
---

- **Dalaran** - Rebuild up and made bigger so that it could also have a dock.
- **Alterac Refugee Camp** - This used to be the alliance entrance to Alterac that now has a wall and some tents and functions as a refugee camp for people from Alterac.
New Locations
---

- **Violet Citadel** - A raid behind Dalaran City now on a small cross shaped island like in WC2.
- **The Frost Den** - A proper home and questing hub for the Frost Wolf Clan. Horde Alterac Valley entrance is here. Between Frost Den and Storm's Pike would be a place of orcs and dwarves fighting.
- **Storm's Pike** - A proper home and questing hub for the Stompike Clan. Alliance Alterac Valley entrance is here. Between Frost Den and Storm's Pike would be a place of orcs and dwarves fighting.
- **Winteraxe Village** - A village of frost trolls led by their leader Korrak the Bloodrager.
- **Snowblind Forest** - A forest in the mountains home to the Snowblind Harpies.
- **Wildpaw Hollow** - A small town for the Wildpaw gnolls.
- **Whitewhisker Cavern** - A cave full of the Whitewhisker kobolds.
- **Irondeep Cave** - A cave full of the Irondeep troggs.
