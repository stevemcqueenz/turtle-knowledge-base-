# Shop cosmetic - Verdant Spheres (Blood Mage roleplay)

## Post 20520 by Adunai (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-07-27T14:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20520#p20520 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My suggestion - add to the shop a purely cosmetic item that would only affect the High Elf race (and maybe restrict it to the Mage class, if technically possible). It would place three orbs of green fire above their head, as can be seen wielded by Kael'thas Sunstrider in WC3 or TBC (or the Warlock class in MoP-WoD).

Reasoning? As I am coming from Warcraft 3, and am a great fan of Thalassian culture, it is natural to think of any of the Alliance Elves in terms of Kael'thas as presented in the beginning of his campaign in TFT - a survivor that seeks any power to save his people.

Of course, the Quel'dorei culture as presented on Turtle is different. Politically, they neither courted the Naga nor the Burning Legion. Class-wise, they don't even have access to Warlocks. But I do feel like something is missing. In my view, their desperate desire for power made them interesting in TFT - and this little cosmetic feature would allow those similarly inclined to bring it to their characters.

We already have a Blood Elf cosmetic appearance (not sure what it does, as I'm new to the server - just the green eyes?). I have heard, there are plans for a Quel'Thalas patch - then it would be a perfect moment to introduce it. It is easy to imagine a High Elven member of the expedition picking up a few tricks from their foes, fighting fire with fire. Or one could even roleplay a renegade Blood Elf rejoining the Alliance!

(Of course, this suggestion is my coping with the fact High Elves can't be Warlocks. But with the 6 class limit reached, there's likely nothing anyone can do at this point.)

P.S. And if anyone retorts with the "Verdant Spheres were Key of the Three Moons" argument - I will say that it's:
 1) a retcon from 2014 (the *Blood of the Highborne* novel);
 2) when we say Kael in 2003, it was immediately recognisable as felfire, akin to that used by Doom Guard or Infernals - and even his Flamestrike spell had a green tint!
