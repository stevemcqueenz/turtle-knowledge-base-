My ignore list remains nearly empty, and I've been here for over two years :D

Personally, I find people with overflowing ignore lists to have something fundamentally wrong with them. Nobody spams world chat THAT much, and you could always ask them to tone down first before ignoring them, or just ignore them temporarily. The player base is huge, lots of people want to say something.

As for the linked post, that's also not a good idea. People could purposely wait for someone else to say their message and then push their message up after to ensure their own message stays visible.

The whole point of world chat has been for people to see your messages. You can always leave world chat if you dislike it, and check LFG channel, trade channel, general channel.
