# I just started leveling a Shaman - Totem Feedback

## Post 108490 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-18T14:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108490#p108490 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I just started leveling my own Shaman to try out the new changes etc.

I've just hit the 30's and done a few dungeons, one thing I have noticed is how often you have to replace the totems in a dungeon group, then I realised why.

The range on the totems is just too small.... 20 yds.
The problem with this is that you are always moving from mob pack to mob pack, so this means replacing the totems very often, literally every pack.

This leads to 2 problems:

Not using them due to annoyance (I must admit the GCD Seperation is great here and has helped alot)
Going OOM putting them down every pack

I for one didnt realise how crap the range was on them until I started taking notice of other party members while putting my totems down, they literally outrange them straight away.

After playing my Paladin where you just set and forget your blessings for 10 minutes (thanks to the new duration increase).... I feel like Shaman should get a range increase on their totems.

I would suggest either increasing the baseline range to 30yds , then the talent in resto being 40yds.

This should be enough in most cases, as your totems feel gimped in enhancement spec, and isnt enhancement supposed to revolve around totems ?

I would also suggest making Totemic Recall available at level 10.

The range of totems does feel a bit limiting it's true. Especially since many of the buffs (but not windfury) are on the meek side. It gets worse when everybody is spread out or is moving rapidly. I wouldn't mind a range increase to 25 or 30 yards.

I definitely second the notion of learning totemic recall at level 10. It's a core spell that players new to the shaman class should integrate into their playstyle as soon as possible. Both for mana efficiency and to avoid drawing in unwanted attention from patrols and such. Your party will thank you.

Note that the latest patch folds a 25% mana reduction into a talent at the bottom row of the resto tree. This goes a long way. Restos should always have this. It's easy to obtain for elemental too, but doesn't mesh well with an enhancer build.
