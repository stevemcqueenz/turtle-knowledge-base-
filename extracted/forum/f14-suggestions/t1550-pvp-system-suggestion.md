# PVP system suggestion

## Post 9198 by Jimmicz (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-01-21T15:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9198#p9198 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was just talking to people on World Channel and apparently, AV rep is impossible to max out so that got me thinking about what could be done to improve the experience on Turtle WoW and make all the PVP rewards available for everybody who wants to get them.

Weekly Wargames
We could have a discord channel with planned wargames the same way raids are being planned. My Idea was that the BG which would be logged would correspond with the PVP holiday schedule. And the week without any rep bonus could be decided by voting.

Example:
Wargames would be announced 3 days in advance on the discord Wargame channel. That way people could log certain BG without having to spam world.  the first Wargame could be the 23rd of January.

WSG Token exchange
As people mostly do WSG it would be nice to have a vendor who could change WSG tokens for AV / AB tokens so we can get more rep by turning the PVP quest in "For Greater Honor"
