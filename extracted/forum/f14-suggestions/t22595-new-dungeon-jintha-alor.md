# New Dungeon: Jintha'Alor

## Post 157228 by Voltaxian — 2025-10-27T00:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157228#p157228 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello! Yes, i know this has been mentioned before.
In 2021: [viewtopic.php?p=11275&hilit=jintha%27alor#p11275](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11275&hilit=jintha%27alor#p11275)
In 2022: [viewtopic.php?p=18396&hilit=jintha%27alor#p18396](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18396&hilit=jintha%27alor#p18396)
And more recently earlier this year: [viewtopic.php?p=116061&hilit=jintha%27alor#p116061](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116061&hilit=jintha%27alor#p116061)

I do realize the dev team may not have this too high in the priority list, but it really would make a region that is rather overlooked or considered a burden, more interesting. Let us analyze this more in depth:

 1. Context: Jintha'Alor is right now both a zone of interest and boredom. People who insist on dealing with the quests of that place because they find it easy to group up with others do it and get the respective rewards. Others who prefer to simply get to higher end content downright ignore the effort and time it takes to find or form a group to run it. Loot is not incredible and many quests in it are not entirely worth it, it takes quite a bit of time that could be spent questing elsewhere or running instances with real rewards.

 2. Lore: This bit can be extracted from wowpedia. <https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Jintha%27Alor>. In short: This is the second largest forest troll settlement. Size-wise, it is the scale of Zul'Farrak, just that it is more compact. It is the largest source of troll presence in Lordaeron and has a lot of influence in the lore of the whole region. Larger in scale than other already existing instances and only second in all other aspects to Zul'Aman.

 3. Questlines: There are, at least, 10 quests between alliance and horde that can be taken, completed or continued in there. Likely a few more since there are many questlines in other regions that send you to hinterlands and some of them to jintha'alor. It is even the source of the Mallet of Zul'farrak, which is necessary to summon a rather important boss of that instance (Also required to obtain the Carrot on a Stick, which is something i can confidently say everyone wants). As well as the quest to trap none other than Hakkar, the Soulflayer, last boss of Zul'Gurub, in an egg relic that dates to the time of the ancient troll empires before the Sundering, aka that egg has been there for way more than 10.000 years, even before the elves existed. (Of course we are talking about Hakkar's spirit, before it gets summoned by the trolls of the Sunken Temple, unlike the gurubashi trolls who do partially succeed at summoning him)

 4. Turtle WoW Storyline: In turtle wow, the Revantusks have a more prominent presence as a sub-faction. They went from just a single flypath stop on a difficult to access location, to a full on newborn faction with lategame effects. To the point that they were given their own region in Stonetalon Mountains, Amani'Alor. Making Jintha'Alor a dungeon would complement them more, as it would be a dungeon made of their nemesis, creating the possibility for more Revantusk interactions and storytelling.

 5. Difficulty: Jintha'Alor is a tough place. A single level 60 even with the correct gear may find it hard and even die if overwhelmed by even 3 or 4 mobs of the higher levels. A full party of level 48-52 characters can and will wipe if they pull carelessly. The density of mobs is rather high, the slimes deal a lot of nature damage and both named mobs have more than a couple of spells.

 6: Instance: Hinterlands lacks an instance and Jintha'Alor is already compressed enough to allow it to become one. It would be an instance available right after Gilneas City in terms of level and location, and good enough as previous instance to, for example, Blackrock Depths, much how Zul'Farrak is. The mobs are already there, there are some clear bosses already and it would be very easy to design new ones based on what each level of Jintha'Alor is about. It already also takes a full group with tank and healer as well as a good amount of time to clear all the conditions for the quests in there, not shorter than some other instances already.

 7. Modifications: Of course, turning an area into an instance can't be an easy task. I can only personally, in my ignorance, assume the dev team already has some experience with it after seeing the new instances as well as the modifications made on existing ones. But in this case, it would be necessary to create new bosses, loot table and a main gate that would work as instance entrance, which could just be an extension of the external wall, or even a main gate right after the double stairs at the entrance. It would open the opportunity to also create new quests, deepening the storylines of both Wildhammer dwarves and Revantusk trolls as it is their common enemy.

 8: Conclusion: It should be a very doable project, which would greatly complement the experience of players in the 45-55 bracket alongside Zul'Farrak, turning it into a sort of sister dungeon. Hinterlands is already frequented, yet many times overlooked due to the lack of organization to deal with a non-instance instance and the difficulty to LFG for "Elite quests". Making it a dungeon would drive more people to the region and make troll content richer as it is quickly forgotten past that point. It would also create the potential of pre-requisites if, in the future, Zul'Aman will be recreated as well.

Well, there you have it, all summed up. I am open to discuss about it anytime, I love troll lore as a whole and can very easily expand on this if further arguments are required. Cheers!
