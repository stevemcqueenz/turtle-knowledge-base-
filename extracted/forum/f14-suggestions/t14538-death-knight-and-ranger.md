Ok, let me explain:
I've been thinking for months and I have this idea, make the Shaman a class for both factions and leave the death knight as an exclusive class for the Horde while the Shaman is very limited to one Alliance race, while the Ranger to a single Horde Race.

It would look like this:

Shaman:
Alliance:
 -Dwarf

Horde:
 -Orc
 -Tauren
 -Troll

The Death Knight can be dealt like this:
 -Orc
 -Undead
 -Goblin

The Ranger like this:
Alliance:
 -Human
 -Night Elf
 -High Elf

Horde:
 -Undead

This way the Alliance keeps the Paladin as their faction OP class and the Horde gets their dark version so to speak, and both factions get a new almost special class.

Death Knight:
Created in the first war by the Dark Horde using the corrupting magic of the Void, the death knights have risen once again, using the bodies that fall in the constant war between the factions that devastates Azeroth, the Horde has decided to resort to once again to this obscure technique.

Ranger:
These skilled archers are dedicated to protecting the lands of their hometown, using lethal aim combined with a variety of techniques and poisons applied to their arrows, facing one can be your worst mistake.
