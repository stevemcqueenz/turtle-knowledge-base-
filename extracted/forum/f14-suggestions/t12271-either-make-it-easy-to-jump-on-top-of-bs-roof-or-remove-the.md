# Either make it easy to jump on top of BS roof or remove the ability to

## Post 81694 by Scelus12 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-19T03:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81694#p81694 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I almost always agree with the turtle wow devs on their design choices, but leaving the hidden wall jump up to the BS roof is the exception.

Having the roof as accessible high ground is fun. Even though BS is already the most valuable node and should be the priority, I like the idea of giving even more of an incentive to capture it first. But I just watched two of my teammates spend minutes trying to unsuccessfully jump up there in the middle of a bg. It's bad game design as-is. You shouldn't need to fiddle with a complicated, hidden ledge jump while you're being shot in the face. Make a clear, two-box jump that's actually represented by the in-game assets and it would be awesome. Not a 1 inch unmarked spot you need to magically land on in the middle of a battleground fight. Or just patch it out like they did in retail because it was unintended in the first place.

We can leave it because it's nostalgic and people who know the jump like to keep it, as long as we all agree it's bad game design.
