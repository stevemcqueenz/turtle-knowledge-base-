# Lockpicking skill leveling

## Post 16532 by Yevv908 — 2022-01-31T17:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16532#p16532 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So typically in order to level Lockpicking you have to go to some obscure locations after you have out leveled the mobs there and then grind through a bunch of boxes. I mean just getting to 110 you sit in redridge or the barrens with a movie on and click boxes for an hour. To get to 150 you repeatedly glitch your lvl 20 rogue quest, because that's the fastest way to grind through this mundane task. And you are thankful that at least you aren't swimming somewhere.

It struck me last night as I did Gnommer, why don't the junk boxes I pickpocket from mobs that are my level give me any skill ups? Couldn't all of this boring box grinding be solved if the lockboxes we pickpocketed just level accordingly. Even the lockboxes at my level don't give me a skill up. Instead I have to go swim around in Desolace for 2 hours at lvl 50.

For example I am at 105 skill right now at lvl 30. My max Lockpicking available is 155. I pick picket lvl 30 mobs and they just give boxes that are grey or green in terms of getting me a skill up. It seems like a really easy fix. I love pick pocketing and it integrates perfectly with role playing as a rogue, instead of just standing in front of the same chest and opening it over and over again.

Thanks for listening! Let me know your thoughts.

It is very easy to be flagged as bot during leveling lockpicking. Recently there was a thread from individual who was banned for "botting" or "anti-afk". Can't find this thread though.

How exactly it can be implemented?
 1. More rogue-specific quests to gather intelligence from all kinds of hostile humanoids all around the world. Those humanoids are usually led by someone. Those leaders usually write orders, letters, maps, recipes. Simplest form of reward is single-time quest for faction capital reputation. Some papers can be used to discover (spawn by on-use) secret couriers, chests. Some papers can be just fun read. Picking lock can trigger alarm with guards.
Discovering and reading all "fun" papers can lead to personal achievement with some title.
Those quests might not feature any significant reward. Stealth run to the fresh place and picking lock suitable for your level is good reward alone.

 2. More boxes with valuables. Add more chests to dungeons which had to be lockpicked.
More small boxes with simple rings, trinkets and amulets in the world could be added too.
