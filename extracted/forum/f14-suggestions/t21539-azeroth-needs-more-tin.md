# Azeroth needs more TIN!

## Post 149237 by Snoodydood (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-29T21:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149237#p149237 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The early requirements for 3 separate professions require a high amount of both coarse stone and bronze, needing tin ore and Tin is by far the lowest spawning common node out in the world. The competition for it is so fierce particularly because of HC characters who can't use the AH and are forced to farm it themselves making it very hard to come by in low level zones (even new players with no gold have a hard time buying it as the prices for coarse stone have went way up because of JC), meaning the only effective way to level up through these professions until you get to iron and mithril (of which there is an abundance of) is to level up then go back to zones like westfall and silverpine where you can safely avoid or kill mobs to get to them, which then makes it nearly impossible for the actual people of level in those zones to farm unless they get lucky and find one before someone manages to take it. Because Tin & coarse stone is such a large requirement to get these professions leveled at low levels, there should really be more out in the world  in low level areas with higher spawn rates to make up for it. I suggest adding it to starting zones like elwynn, dun morogh and durotar ect. to make them more widely available.
