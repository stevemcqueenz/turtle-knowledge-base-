Hello, back in the day i used to sell mercenary services to end game dungeons (maraudon princess and higher) for unwanted items for me to DE and some gold.

one example was scholomance: 5gold, 3 unwanted blues and 3 unwanted greens. The usual way to get them was the following: wait until everyone rolls, if no one hits need it's considered unwanted item and then i grab it with need, otherwise pass. Once i got all my payment i just hit greed to all like everyone else.

If people needed the upgrades and at the end of the dungeon didn't get enough items it was on me, from hundreds of times i did this only 2 times got less than requested items, which are compensated by the many times i got extra tips for a job well done or for helping completing specific quests.

Then some rules update came and while it was still allowed to sell mercenary services i wasn't able to ask for items anymore, so my prices had to go up in order to make it worth my time(25g or more). That was when the time chinese flooded the server and started some crazy RMT runs.

Would like to know if this can be revised, in case i can go back in bussiness, since it was a gold farm i enjoyed while socializing at the same time.
