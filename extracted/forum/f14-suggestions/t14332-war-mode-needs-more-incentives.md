# “War Mode” needs more incentives.！！！

## Post 97759 by Shyler — 2024-06-24T19:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97759#p97759 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After reaching the maximum level, the significance of War Mode is almost lost. In comparison, Hardcore Mode continues to offer unique titles, maintaining its value. For players who no longer need honor points, War Mode holds no real worth. Similar to Hardcore Mode, which carries high risk and thus high rewards, War Mode should ideally offer substantial incentives as well. I suggest adding an extra 10% gold bonus from quests and drops. That would be fantastic!

## Post 98151 by Daedalus007 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-06-29T16:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98151#p98151 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Considering the absolute gankfest of War Mode already (cowards, grifters, and assholes who do nothing but go to starter zones to gank lowbies and quest givers) I don't feel this is really necessary.

If anything, I feel the exp bonus should be removed and the honor bonus should be increased to 50% or more.  There isn't an 'incentive' to keep War Mode always-on at 60 after getting your R14 gear and items because there's no need to maintain a constant treadmill.  There are other games to play and it doesn't have to be 24/7 WoW all the time.

If there is only an honor bonus incentive then only the PvPers will take this glyph (as was intended) instead of having it be an 'leveling-meta-required' glyph for levelers to take because reasons.
