Entitled tanks (and sometimes healers) can be quite a pain.

However, the whole group needs to keep in mind what the tank is actually capable of and that level and gear alone are no reliable indicators for that. A high level beginner tank might not feel up to the task to tank a pack of 4 mobs, even if you've seen experienced lower level players pull these things off.

Having tanked a few dungeons myself on various chars, I know that dps players can indeed make a tanks life very hard: no use of cc, running away from the tank when they draw aggro, no focus, no interrupts, "helping" by unnecessary off-tanking when it's not needed, pulling, not waiting for aggro build-up and whatnot.

If a "good" tank encounters these players, they must choose between:

A) playing along, hoping to make things work with a bad group and risking a few heartattacks. This might not always work and end in frustration for everybody.

B) firmly but politely ask people to adjust the way they play in this dungeon according to the tanks needs. That's what I do when the dps are making the game too hard for me.

Still, as mentioned above, we all occasionally run into "jerk tanks", but I haven't seen that happen on turtle as often as on other servers

However, I often make my own groups by whispering to and inviting people instead of waiting for a queue to pop.
