The highest raid - the more game feels like matrix. More addons for UI and tracking casting spells, buffs, debufs, phases.

I suggest to add for each boss precast and casting animation. For each special debuff - color changing or/and size changing. Phases - fog changing. additional animation on the borders of fighting room.

For example - medivh:
Doom of medivh - target under debuff 5% bigger each stack
Corruption mefivh - target is green and has THIS animation as a radius of effect ![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/37993-cloud-of-disease.jpg)

Frost nova. When he casting - reverse animation of frost nova
Flame strike - while casting 1-2-3-4 fire pillars apeears around him.
Arcane blast - while casting 1-2-3-4 orbs appears above his head.
After each interruption medivh - glowing rune on floor appears, so raid can see how much interruptions was done during the fight ![Image](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcStlieYY6K-IF7MYviCPciZ88qTsxoirPYHlQ&s)

Please we need native indicators in game, instead 100500 UI addons

While i dont want to see over convoluted animations for "kick here please" signalling, i have to agree that some kind of readable representation is mandatory. A boss that has a hard requirement to use an addon is just not a well designed boss.

Medivh has threatening spells, and so does Kel'Thuzad. And in classic there are no target castbars out of addons. But one is a 5 meter tall behemoth who displays his glowing hands towering above the melees head, easy to see for any attentive raider with an interrupt.

Medivh demands certain spells be kicked at certain times and commits the cardinal sin of requiring an addon to play it.

So whilei dont care about the exact animations used, i absolutely agree with op that he should have a easily readable cast animation that can also be read by casters or healers who night have to chip in on interrupts
