# More Fountain Coins

## Post 29660 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-11-22T18:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29660#p29660 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Occasionally in water fountains around the world there will be a coin you can loot bearing someone's name and a little bit of flavor text. Some of these are for lore characters and most are for players, typically those that have been on the server for a very long time and might not even be around anymore. There are 42 in total.

Would it be possible for notable server personalities, guild leaders, or the like, to be given their own lootable coin from the fountains? I don't know if it would take much to make them or if it's not important, but I always found these to be a nice little addition to the world that gave it a bit more flavor and could lead to some interesting player stories about the people whose name the coin bears.

At the very least, I'd assume the notable contributors to the project, the devs and GMs, would have their own coins. Some already do like Kazgrim, Tinyfin (kinda), and Dragu, but there are probably others who aren't memorialized yet who could get something. Maybe Tamoko and reference the infamous anime furry transformation incident, or Elexxi from early Macrochelys and the Thomas Miller genocide (ongoing), or Shieldmaiden's crusade in perpetual HC 59. These are just some off-the-top ideas and I'm sure there's more, certainly, that would be positively immortalized in this manner.
