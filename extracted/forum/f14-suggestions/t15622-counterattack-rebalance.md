Hello there, im trying out warrior new talents and im having some doubts on counterattack:
While its clearly a leveling talent (no use in pvp since you barely parry in most matchups, and no use in lategame since you dont tank in raid) the balancing seems a little bid odd. Ap scaling makes it so it feels so weak to use in early game (20 rage for a 80 dmg ability in some cases), it should scale with weapon damage like overpower.

I make this suggestion ignoring the design philosophy behind it so i may be wrong in something.
Thank you in advance

I agree that it should be weapon damage, in general Warrior is a weapon-dependent class so every skill should be scaling with the weapon in my opinion. The 20 rage cost is too expensive for the dmg it does. In my opinion it should be 10 rage with current dmg it does. In addition in current state I think this talent is bugged since the mobs I use it against don't stay in place for longer than 1 sec. I didn't test it in PVP.

What I disagree is the fact that you don't parry enough in PvP. When you fighting rogue you parry so I assume this talent is made to hold them in place. I would also like to see that Warrior is a true master of arms, and when picking up this talent the parry chance could be further increased by 2% on top of 5% from the previous talents.
