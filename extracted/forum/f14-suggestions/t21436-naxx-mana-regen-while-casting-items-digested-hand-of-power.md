<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148598#p148598 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I have a small suggestion about some Naxx gear that got reworked last year.

I really like the changes made to the MP5 gear from Naxx, "Pendant of the Forgotten Names" and "Ring of Spiritual Fervor", giving them 10% mana regen while casting instead of being just useless MP5 items. They don't seem op since mana management doesn't seem to be that big of a problem anymore for healers on twow compared to the old game. Still they have uses when u want to go super mana efficient, or at least they are much better than before.

I am suggesting to make a similar change to the Off Hand "Digested Hand of Power", since that still has just the MP5 on it. Or is that too much Mana regen for everyone?
