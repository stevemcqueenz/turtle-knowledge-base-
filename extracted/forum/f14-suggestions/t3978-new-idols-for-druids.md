# New Idols for Druids

## Post 24072 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-08-31T17:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24072#p24072 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Thanks to the new changes, druids largely are more capable in all areas than before, especially balance spec, and even have a new healing spec to push for. However, their last item slot hasn't really been able to keep up with this with eight total current options; two enhance healing touch, one increases rejuvenation healing, one to decrease bear ability costs, one to decrease cat ability costs, one to decrease shapeshifting cost, one to increase moonfire damage, and one to increase thorns duration. Overall, you have exactly one option for each spec except classic resto (2 HT options, tree uses the only rejuv, powershift feral has one, straightline cat has one, straightline bear has one, balance has one, and thorns is useless). None of these are available until Maraudon, and a few options to spice things up towards different playstyles would do wonders.

With that, I'd suggest a few new ones to consider. I don't know the full extent of how balanced these would be, but I feel they'd at least add a bit more viability to current specs or increase the available playstyles, although I have to admit having at least one to support each variant already is a good start:

Balance:
 - Starfire cast time decreased by 0.5s (Idol of the Stars)
With the new mana reductions from moonkin and ability to cast innervate and decurse in moonkin form, their overall mana and ability to last through a fight has increased dramatically. I rarely need to use mana potions in fights, usually just for the last boss in each raid, but the rest are not an issue. This is true in all raid tiers, at least for me, and could probably be further increased by a couple tweaks on my gear for new custom items like Pearl of the Tides. However, boomkin dps is still low compared to every class and spec except Retribution Paladins, and even then it's not too far off. Increasing casts-per-minute while keeping spell damage the same should help on this front and have the least effect on pvp versus just increasing raw damage on starfire or wrath.

 - Hurricane cd reduced by 20s-30s (Idol of the Maelstrom)
Druids are the only caster that has legitimate difficulty with crowds. Mages and warlocks are of course the kings, but shamans, paladins, and even priests have an easier time killing large groups and causing AoE than druids, partly because druid is the only one with a minute-long CD on it's only damaging ability without a cap on the number of targets. Lowering the cd, or possibly increasing the damage via scaling to 0.1, would give another more viable crowd option for damage instead of defaulting to moonfire tab-targeting.

Feral:
 - Reduced rage cost of Feral Charge to 0. Increase cd by 5s-10s (Idol of Barbarity)
The current bear idol is great, and honestly there is little to compete with it for threat generation and tanking capability. So, offering an alternative playstyle, one that allows more freedom of movement/location in PvE or PvP, would give them a bit more flexibility during fights or even allow them to charge and swipe for immediate crowd aggro. Of course the downside of reduced maul/swipe rage is a tough one to combat, but the idea is to simply give an alternative rather than a strict improvement.

 - Add a 20%-50% chance to generate an additional combo point when using a finishing move in cat form (Idol of Savagery)
Cat form has two great idols. One decreased costs of their bread-and-butter, the other turns mana into extra melee attacks via powershift rotations. Offering another way to generate combo points or keep them going might provide an alternative playstyle to the above, but I am torn on it's effectiveness. Ultimately, just providing a bit of choice, especially for leveling cats, would be a boon.

 - Reduce Dash cd by 1min (Idol of the Fox)
Honestly, just an alternative for PvP or leveling cats to have a bit of flex and try to compare to rogue's reduced sprint cd later on. Druids already have great movement abilities with travel form, feline swiftness, and shapeshift breaking soft CC. This would just be another piece that proves useful depending on the situation that isn't another attempt at simply increasing damage.

Resto:
 - Increase duration of Innervate by 2s-6s (Idol of the Crow)
The main druid healing abilities (Rejuvenation, Healing Touch, Regrowth) are in a good spot overall, and HT and Rej both have relevant idols to boost their raw effectiveness. Increasing the abilities of a different support spell for druids might help a bit more in their secondary role of supporting other healers as the healing class that should last the longest with their mana in extended fights. Innervates in raids tend to go on other classes as a supplemental mana potion, and the T3 2-piece bonus can further enhance this by giving even more mana regeneration to others. Leaning a little further in towards the caster-support role of druid healers could give just that little bit more flex, especially for tree druids who can't use Healing Touch and only have one idol choice at the moment. I do realize this might also work for boomkins, but I'd doubt they'd go for this over a starfire or wrath idol. Even the moonfire one has a lot more viability for them in my opinion.

 - Reduce the cd of Tranquility, Innervate, Barkskin, and Nature's Swiftness by 30s (Idol of Renewal)
Nothing special, just a possible boon to the longer resto CDs to give them a bit more use. Could see the spells pulled into individual idols and the cd reduction of each tweaked for more fairness as well.

 - Reduce the cd of Rebirth by 10min-15min (Idol of Revivication)
Battle res is amazing, but also only used 1-2 times per raid, and generally less in dungeons. Giving 5-mans a bit more use out of it so druids aren't bottom-of-the-pile healers and are able to more effectively recover a near wipe would be a huge boon while leveling and give raids a bit more flex while not breaking any single encounter.

I apologize for this huge wall, but hopefully some of these are at least worth some deeper consideration. Feel free to criticize and let me know how broken or how worthless some or all of these are, but I personally feel expanding the 17th item slot would be a big help to the class and a boon to groups using druids of any size.

I can only really speak to the proposed moonkin changes.

Hurricane is an EXPENSIVE spell, far moreso to rain of fire and blizzard... and I believe if a moonkin player wishes to completely dump their mana with hurricane, they should be able to. I don't think this should be done via an idol, or else we'd have to make macros to switch to the hurricane idol on trash, and switch to moonfire or w/e on boss. That seems wrong to me, or maybe it's not wrong as you'd be switching trash and boss gear anyway due to hit% optimization.

A starfire cast time reduction idol would ruin the tradeoff it shares with Wrath. Right now, with lock curses disregarded:
Wrath is high DPS, med DPM
Starfire is med-high DPS, high DPM
And yeah starfire would become permanently the better choice with such an idol, were wrath to not get a -0.25s counterpart. Doing this via idol would be a shame, as your choice of whether to use starfire or wrath on a fight would come down to which idol you picked before the fight, and the switchup cannot be made mid-fight.

The other concern with cast speed reductions is that currently, 3 starfires, 1 MF 1 IS perfectly make up a 12second cast cycle. NG throws a wrench in this as you'd then go for 4 starfires 1MF 1IS but lose uptime on both dots. If starfire were made to be 2.5s, you could go for the 4:1:1, this way getting 2 NG procs means that you now are able to keep full uptime on that 4:1:1 and you dont feel the negative "feel" of going from 3 to 4 starfires before dots. This is indeed something to think about.
