Lets be honest with this one, Warmode-PvP isn't for actual PvP, if it was it would function differently. Ganking and griefing lower levels is the patent expected behavior with a high level smashing a low level.

My suggestion of a fix, Keep open world PvP the way it works, totally fine with it actually, PvP in the open world should be encouraged. To encourage that however, I would recommend the below.

Warmode, can only attack the other faction target If: < Target is within 3 Levels above or below current level of self. Example: level 35, attack able targets 32-35-38, outside this range, target cannot be aggressed towards, if your in a party with someone who is lower level and engages in PvP within their level ranges, you cannot assist, Dueling rules apply where you cannot heal 2 players locked in a duel.

This would encourage grouping and team play with people your same level or near to watch each others back.

Anyone against this is just looking to grief and not actually perform Player Verses Players. its a stomp your looking for, not a fight.
