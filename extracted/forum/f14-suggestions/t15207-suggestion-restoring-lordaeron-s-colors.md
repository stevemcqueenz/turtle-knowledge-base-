# Suggestion: Restoring Lordaeron's Colors

## Post 103165 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-10-16T16:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103165#p103165 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Most of the military or paramilitary Lordaeron-affiliated NPCs are town militias in Hillsbrad Fields and Southshore, as well as a few militiamen also in the Tirisfal Uplands. However, both Hawk's Vigil in Wetlands and the Remnants' Camp in Tirisfal have actual soldiers in armor; they bear the sigil of Lordaeron, but they also wear the blue armor primarily associated with Stormwind.

My suggestion would be to switch them to the white-colored Theramore guard armor to match with their nation color from Warcraft II being white. While it's true that, like with the blue armor being associated with Stormwind, the white armor is currently associated with Theramore, the latter was primarily founded by the refugees of Lordaeron, which would logically cycle back to Lordaeron's national color being white. Keeping the tabard also gives it a nice pop of blue color to tie it all together. While being at it, I suggest giving Captain Silas a tabard to match his men.

Stormwind/Azeroth remains blue, Stromgarde is red, Kul Tiras is green, Gilneas was given its black coloration when it was added to Turtle WoW in 1.17.0 (with a brown recolor for the Gilneas Brigade/Ravenwood Rebellion), and I think that Lordaeron should be given its white color once more to match.
