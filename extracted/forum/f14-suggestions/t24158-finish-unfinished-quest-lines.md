# Finish unfinished quest lines

## Post 169954 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-03T18:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169954#p169954 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

1. Missed diplomat
 2. The Essence of Eranikus

 1. Missed diplomat ends suddenly at the most interesting point. Jaina tells us, "Thank you, and go your own way." As we know from the comics,
Logosh was a captured gladiator in the ogre arena, and with amnesia. He later managed to escape their slavery. He befriended Valira Windrunner and gradually regained his memory over the course of his journey. He accepted his true name—Varian Ryn—and not the one the ogres gave him.

And as many might have guessed, Onyxia is connected to the kidnapping, and she has been destroying the kingdom from within for a long time.
But where is the quest for Onyxia's Lair and where is the lost diplomat? There's an eternity between them and dozens of pages of missing content.

A similarly sad situation with the Essence of Eranikus. The quest starts and ends immediately, and there's no story or connection between Shade of Eranikus and the Emerald Dream.

At first, Blizzard didn't have time to finish it, and then they just got lazy and left it as is.

I really want to see the conclusion of these two quests. Especially to continue the search for the lost diplomat, to follow in his footsteps and ultimately find all the answers and unspoken things.
