I wouldn't call it useless but rather situational. Want to use a lot of mana and not annoy your healer? Sacrifice felhunter. Want to do more AE damage? Sacrifice imp. Spam shadowbolts -> succubus.

Demonic sacrifice adds a lot of versatility to the class. You must also consider that having a pet is not always convenient, e.g. when you're in a wallhugging dungeon group. Sacrifice lets a demolock lose less power in these cases.

If I were to make a change to the demonology tree, I'd rather condense improved imp/succubus/voidwalker talents into a single one.
