## Post 29638 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-11-22T09:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29638#p29638 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Getting revered or even exalted with warsong at 19 is possible, but problem is that you don't need to engage warsong anymore since you do that. I already mentioned this in [other thread](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29318#p29318). But this thread looks like suitable place to post.

I disagree with huge bump of reputation for battleground factions. Because bg reputation is farming spot which supposed to sink time of players.
In the same time, normal levellers are not supposed to stay at 19 for several months. I think that player should be able to get all weapons from battlegound faction in two weeks of playing every evening for 2 hours.
Thats why I propose to split single "battleground" reputation to multiple reputations for each bracket.

Probably instead of single warsong reputation there should be multiple reputations. For example:
Apprentice Warsong Outriders for 1, 2-9, 10-15 and 16-19
journeyman Warsong Outriders for 20-29
Expert Warsong Outriders for 30-39
Artisan Warsong Outriders for 40-49
Master Warsong Outriders for 50-59 and 60

All items available now from single reputation should be distributed to different reputations of different brackets.
Apprentice warsong outriders allows you to buy items only for lvl 18. Journeyman reputation allows you to buy items only for lvl 29.

Imagine you are lvl 19 and win wsg. You should get like 300 reputation with Apprentice faction, 100 reputation with journeyman faction, 25 reputation with expert faction and 5 reputation with artisan faction.
If you are lvl 29 and win wsg, then you should get 250 reputation with journeyman faction, 50 reputation with expert faction and 10 reputation with artisan faction.

This way you:
 1. Get exalted of your current bracket faster and you get items for your level from pvp
 2. You progress with higher level faction too, but slowly. It is meaningful for you to participate in higher level warsong

Additional restrictions may apply. Like "max rep with Artisan faction on lvl 19 is friendly".

Right now there are following reputation rewards for Arathi:
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=15127>
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=15126>
and warsong:
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=14754>
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=14753>

How much reputation one BG should give?
Blue gear suitable for levellers require no more than revered reputation. I think it is enough for a milestone.
According to <https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Reputation>
Neutral-Friendly is 3k rep
Friendly-Honored is 6k rep
Honored-Revered is 12k rep

 21k rep total for Neutral-Revered.
I assume playtime 2 hours per weekday (total 10) and 8 hours per weekend (total 16). Grand total is 26 hours per week.
I expect that goal "neutral-revered" for each bracket should be doable in 2 weeks of playing or 52 hours of playing. Assuming wsg is played 15 minutes, it means 208 wsg to get Neutral-Revered.
I also assume 50% winrate. Because it is good average for PvP. Assuming that you get all reputation for win and nothing for lose, you need 104 wsg wins.
 21k/104 = 201.9 rep per win.
It is actually very close to current reputation gains. 35 per flag+50 for turning marks which is 155 rep per win.

It turns out that it is very possible to get to revered with wsg in 3-4 weeks of persistent playing even with current reputation system.

So the actual problems are:
 1. Normal players face a bunch of twinks in low level PvP
 2. If your teams are equal, then WSG will be almost endless and very tiresome.

I think devs should solve those problems first and then change anything regarding reputation rewards.
