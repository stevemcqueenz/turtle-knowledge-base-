**Cosmetic Spell effects**

Introduction:
Hi everyone. New to Twow and im enjoing the experience ALOT. Amazing to see what a game can become when love is put into it. Had this idea for a while please feel free to give ideas/input or critic :P
I have no personal experience in modding or programing like our Amazing Admins, so i dont really know if this idea is technically possible, but ill throw it out there either way.

Idea:The idea is to give an midgame and endgame "chase" outside of the regular Item/mog farming.
Something different that can expand upon both roleplaying and feel of classes.
. To paint a picture for you i saw a post from Error404 that  made an amazing mod swapping the reqular demons for undeads, thats exactly the type of Flavour this "mechanic" can bring. Imagine youre playing a class fantasy of a Priest of elune, imagine if we could make the spell effects more bright white moon inspired, or switch the regular yellow paladin light to a bright red or deep purple for a "fallen" paladin.
I know the glyph system often only changed 1 ability but what if it was possible to change up a whole kit, surely its alot of work and strain but imagine how cool it could be to personalise your class to yout RP idea.
The system that would be closest to this "idea" is donation rewards system thats implemented in the Twow game today or the glyph system (that i think only exist in Wotlk).

As i said in the introduction i have no idea of how either modding or coding works, so please remember this is just an idea. Either way ill write some class fantasys that could be hella cool down bellow, if your know any threads thats been posted on the forum before this that has the "same" idea please Link them in your reply.  - All love, Gstac.

an item or spell that changes the look of your spells.
How to get:
