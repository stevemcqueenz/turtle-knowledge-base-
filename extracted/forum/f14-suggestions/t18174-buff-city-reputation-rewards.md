City reputations at current are… Unequal to say the least at current. However, this is not a problem that Turtle WoW cannot easily solve while inspiring the player base to engage in more transmog creations as well as roleplay!

The Quel’dorei reputation vendors in Alah’thalas offer a slew of level 60 items, as well as lower level items for their reputations which are MC/ZG raid ready. For doing the starting zone, and for reaching 60 which is no easy feat, they have access to usable weapons and armor from the get go regardless of class, weapon, or armor specialization.

Other groups are not as lucky. For the Kaldorei in Darnassus as an example - we get mail gear and a sword prior to exalted (which grants a tabard everyone can use). Mail is only accessible to 2 of our 5 playable classes, and that number only rises to 2 post level 40 when Hunters gain access to mail and Warriors gain access to plate. Priests and Druids cannot use swords either, so that means for many of the players we will never have any usable rewards from our faction regardless of the amount of zones we do (Teldrassil, Darkshore, Ashenvale, and more) we will not see any usable rewards until exalted which often comes at the price of 200+ bolts of cloth on top of our leveling zones.

 **This is not to say that the rewards given to the Quel’dorei are a problem. In fact, I think it is beneficial to have rewards given for engaging in the reputation system and questing which can easily be bypassed through mob farming and boosting.**

I propose adding more rewards such as pets, cloaks, armor, and more which fits the races theme for multiple armor groups. Some of these could be recolors of other pieces (which the Quel’dorei have) or later expansion armor which fits the vanilla model. This allows for more customization in transmog, and perhaps an increase in fashion token purchases as a developer standpoint.

Some recommendations I would make for the Kaldorei are…
 **1. The City Cloaks with their faction logo**
• This would be an easy addition to each faction in my opinion, and useful for guilds centered in the Horde and the Alliance. I could see guilds such as the Shu’Halo using the Thunder Bluff cloak, and Wrath of the Forsaken using the Forsaken Cloak - so this would not be an Alliance specific request. <https://www.wowhead.com/item=64887/cape-of-darnassus>
![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/small/194761.jpg)

 **2. The Conquerer’s Nightsong Headpiece for Druids**
• One of the most iconic druid pieces, I feel this would be heavily used in RP and inspire more to level. Similar items for other races could do the same. [https://www.wowhead.com/wotlk/item=461 ... -headpiece](https://www.wowhead.com/wotlk/item=46184/conquerors-nightsong-headpiece)
![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/135459-conquerors-nightsong-headguard.jpg)

 **3. The Crescent Wand**
• In the name of Elune, I shall punish you! <https://www.wowhead.com/item=72871/crescent-wand>
![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/264117-crescent-wand.jpg)

 **4. The Moon Bow**
• I can understand this one potentially being a texture issue as it was introduced in wod, but may be low poly enough for those not on the HD pack. <https://www.wowhead.com/item=213160/kaldorei-moon-bow>
![Image](https://pm1.narvii.com/6361/3b9018236a3e692d3abf92df8383baa817a592d2_00.jpg)
