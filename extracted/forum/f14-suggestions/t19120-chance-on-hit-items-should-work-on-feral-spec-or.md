As fun as weaponprocs in Catforms would be, they fall in the same category as weaponprocs with the old (now thankfully reverted judgent of the crusader)

In order for a weapon to be balanced against all other weapons, it needs to have a similar powerbudget. Weapons with amazing poccs (Felstriker, Ironfoe) are punching way above their paygrade, but eventually their lack of itemlevel and therefore weapondamage makes them outclassable by better weapons.

If we give druids the ability to procc these effects within animal form, the low weapondamage or suboptimal swingspeed of those weapons is much less relevant. This means either we have to severely neuter some of the awesome procs that exist in the game (particularly felstriker, Ironfoe & other swing modifiers come to mind)

There is a very real chance that ironfoe beats out even atiesh and other endgame weapons in performance or at least gets close to them in performance.

Which just isnt a fun characterprogression experience.
It also ensures future weapons have to be checked for their abusability when wielded by a druid. Severely limiting what kind of poccs we can see.
As cool as it would be, it creates just soo many design problems
