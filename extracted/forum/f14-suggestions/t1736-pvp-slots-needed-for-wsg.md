The fact Warsong can proc in a 2V2 lead to some very boring aspects of the game.

(just keep in mind the honor progression actually on twow is really slow, compared to other realms and making 250K honor in a week is something quite impossible)

 - The lack of interest in a such great surface.
 - The fact that only rankers are doing pvp. making the content even more repetitive.
 - The time to make proc warsong isn't really affected, it doesn't encourage at all randoms to join.
 - The problematic of constant dodgers (even if they just forgot they were tagged since hours and are now dungeoning), leading to some super fast games with easy honor, compared to other games
 - A bunch of other problematics.

The easy way to make a warsong proc is now affecting all the other battlegrounds existing, because that's meta, no others battlegrounds since months have been made.

The only way to change this, is to keep the others battleground on the existing pvp slots needed to attract players to do something else than warsong. But set the proc for warsong on 3V3 or even 4V4 for at least a minimum. i'd rather see more players in warsong, or a few in arathi basin. or Alterac valley.

 **What we loose if these changes are done?**

Nothing.
