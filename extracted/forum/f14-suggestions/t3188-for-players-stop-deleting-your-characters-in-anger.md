# [For Players] Stop Deleting Your Characters in Anger!

## Post 19428 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-06-28T15:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19428#p19428 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi.  What's up?

I've been playing *Turtle WoW* for just under two months.  It's been pretty fun and the community has a lot of interesting people in it.  Something I've noticed, though, is that a surprising amount of people seem to "get angry" or "get frustrated" and straight up **delete their characters**.  Well…
  **Stop deleting your characters in anger!**
 
I understand that Vanilla *World of Warcraft* – which *Turtle WoW* is based on – is a much more challenging game than Retail *World of Warcraft* has been since, I'd say, part-way through *The Burning Crusade*.  The AddOn scripting is archaic, there is no "phasing" technology, and the quests are largely catered to group play.  Some of the "solo" quests – ones *not* marked "Elite" – tend to put players into seriously *stressful* situations, forcing them to fight two… three… sometimes even *four or more* mobs all at one time.  Even for the most *experienced* player, this can be very overwhelming.
On top of that, there are certain ways of playing which seem to have a generally *negative* impact on gameplay.  The **War Mode** challenge, for example, permanently flags players for player-vs-player combat, but gives a boost all gained Experience Points.  Just like in Retail, there will always be higher-level players who go out of their way to destroy lower-level ones despite how pointless it is.

I suppose some players hit a wall, at some point, and decide "enough is enough".  And when that point hits, they delete their character.  This isn't some "fluke" or some "accident", mind.  Players deciding to kill their character need to go to the character menu, click "Delete", *type* "Delete", then click "Confirm".  That seems like a *conscious decision*, to me.  But then… later, they decide they didn't *want* to delete their character and ask for them to be restored.  I guess that makes sense… except in the cases of players deleting their *inventory* beforehand.  That feels a bit more *permanent*.  Yet, they still ask for a *revival and roll-back*…

I guess I haven't hit that point yet, but I don't exactly understand why someone would delete their character and then ask for it back.  Or more specifically, I don't get what deleting their character in frustration accomplishes.  Is that their way of showing the game "who's boss"?  'cause, to me?  That's the player *giving up*.  And then, they go and beg the GMs to *restore* their lost character because "Oops, I got angry"?  That's poppycock.

There are *always* better ways to deal with one's frustration than by taking it out on their characters.  Take a break.  Stop playing.  Do easier quests.  Turn War Mode / PvP Mode off.  Maybe even *grind mobs* to raise the character level.  Nuking your character may *feel* like a good idea when you're infuriated with the challenge of the game, but really… if you're doing it purely for that reason, you're probably doing it for the *wrong* reasons.  Reconsider.  And **stop wasting your time and the time of GMs.**

Rant over.  Thank you for reading.
