## Post 15866 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-01-15T17:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15866#p15866 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I wish it would be nice to have a way to let users control the weather. There were tools for future versions of WoW called "WoW Machinima Tool" they allowed you to change the skybox, the fog (you could make the peaceful Elwynn Forest have the grim fog/skybox of Eastern Plaguelands or Felwood or vice versa - make Eeastern Plaguelands or Felwood look a bit more peaceful like Westfall or something), it allowed you to hide trees, set time and time speed, unlock the camera from the character and change camera speed so you can fly around the map and see places you can never reach with your character.

This was a tool that used the game client's settings, it did not affect the server at all and it also did not provide any advantage to players. When I played on WoTLK servers, I used to to make every zone's fog/sky look like Westfall and keep the game time at 11:00 AM at all times.

I think having such an add-on implemented into the game will be nice.

Last night I watched a video of some brand new Chinese Wuxia-themed MMORPG and in the video, the player showed roughly the same tool built into the game's UI that allowed him to change seasons (in winter snow would build up on the surfaces, but that's off-topic), if a MMORPG studio would consciously build this into their game and intend for players to use it for cosmetic preferences, I don't see the problem.

As Turtle WoW is a Vanilla+ server and it's aimed to provide a cozy and customizable environment for its players, I don't see why not allowing this into the game.

===================================================
My main reason for wishing for such a feature is that at most times I get to play in the evenings of EU time so whenever i can play, it's always gloaming or night and I never get the chance to experience the joy of a sunny day and I believe such a tool/feature will be great to have.
