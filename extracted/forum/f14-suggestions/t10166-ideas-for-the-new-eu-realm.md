# Ideas for the new EU Realm

## Post 66439 by Bazog — 2023-09-22T23:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66439#p66439 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone.

First I have to say I really enjoy Turtle WoW, the community is nice and the fact the team is passionate about the warcraft universe and adds new exclusive content is what made me play here in the first place (the sunnyglade teaser with the warcraft 2 soundtrack convinced me).

I heard the news about the plan to open a new EU server, it got me thinking and conjecturing about what kind of server it could be. The main thing currently lacking in Turtle WoW for me is a good PvP environment. Many people don't like PvP here, and it's fine, players are all different and don't always agree on what's fun or not.

Warcraft, in my opinion, have always been about gathering armies and destroying the ennemy base, that was the case in warcraft 1,2,3 and even in the canceled point and click Lord of the Clans where Thrall end up invading the Durnholde Keep.

I started playing WoW during the open beta test prior release and witnessed very big changes from version 1.1 to 1.12. Early vanilla was so much different than late vanilla.

In the beginning, there was much more world PvP, because it was the only kind of PvP available, battlegrounds were only added in 1.5. And I have to say it was the funniest time I had in WoW, you would sometimes encounter full raids of players in the open world invading quest hubs and capitals. It made the world feel "alive" in a way. That was the world of warcraft as I envisionned it.

I didn't care if the NPC that gave me the quest had been killed, I would call my guild to come retake the town and it created stories and content by itself.

And then battlegrounds and dishonorable kills were added, I think it was the end of an era. Everybody would grind honor in battlegrounds and never joined world pvp raids, unwilling to risk getting dishonnorable kills. I enjoy playing battlegrounds, but they really don't feel the same as joining a Southshore VS Tarren Mill battle or a big scale Stormwind/Orgrimmar invasion.

If the Turtle WoW team plans to release a PvP realm, I think it would be great to provide another option than instanced PvP to grind honor. It would be great if the same amount of honor could be obtained just doing world PvP.

Here are some ideas to achieve this:

 - Dishonorable kills do not affect PvP ranking.
 - Defeating Capital leaders grant higher amount of honor points.
 - Killing a player in the open world grants more honor than in a battleground.

I agree, anything that is integrated in the open world is much more immersive than instanced gameplay, it enhances the roleplaying aspect of WoW. Open world activities are closer to the essence of Vanilla WoW and Warcraft.
Open World PvP should be definitely encouraged.

Dishonorable kills are an important part of PvP,  there could be however pvp events; attacks against any Horde or Alliance town, with adequate rewards, you could join the NPCs of your faction that are carrying out the attack or defense. There could bd repeatable quests ment to reach a collective objective - for example to fortify the towns, each player could contribute to reach the final cost (gold/materials) of for example building a (destroyable) watchtower that would shoot at attacking players, similar to the Warcraft 3 towers, different based on the race, constructing a building that trains and equips the NPCs (granting them buffs) or other collective costs inspired by Warcraft 3 gameplay. Some buildings could be attacked and would need to be repaired. Towns could be occupied and would need to be reconquered. It'd reduce the gap between players and NPCs, make the game feel much more alive and dynamic. Professions would gain more significance.
Capital Cities would be like PvP raids, while Towns and Settlements would be like PvP dungeons. The collective effort to upgrade the Towns/friendly NPCs, and attack/defend your factions settlements would incentivize socializing and would increase immersion. and bring more of Warcraft into WoW.
Tying together open world PvP, PvE and RP.
