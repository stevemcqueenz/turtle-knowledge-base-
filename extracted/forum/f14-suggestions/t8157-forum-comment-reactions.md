## Post 52516 by Samsara (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-07-07T08:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52516#p52516 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The way I see it adding a laugh or anger smiley would be a decent way to show disapproval without the risk of fueling drama in some instances. While giving hearts and smilies without replying can also be used to show support without replying with nothing other than approval to say, keeping the posts more concise overall.

The real issue with networks like Youtube, Facebook and Instagram or even official WoW forums is their reactions do not allow a whole range of emotions and therefor skews the reactions in a biased unrealistic way with toxic expectations. There are plenty of forums in contrast that allow their entire list of emojis to be used and it bothers no one, because it is not forcing some reactions in particular.
