# Spirit Walker class concept

## Post 137392 by Mrkrissatan (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-29T09:21:00Z

**Spirit Walker (Tauren-Only Class)**
![Image](https://cdna.artstation.com/p/assets/images/images/054/322/144/large/patrycja-winniczuk-spirit-walker-hearthstone12.jpg?1664280451)
Mail Armor – Hybrid Role
Primary Resource: Mana (scales with Spirit)
Secondary Mechanic: Spirit Echoes (charges used to summon ancestral spirits)

Class Concept
Spirit Walkers are Tauren mystics who commune directly with the spirits of ancient warriors. Rather than anchoring themselves with totems, Spirit Walkers call upon these ancestors to walk beside them, offering aid in healing, protection, and battle. These spectral allies are semi-autonomous, mobile, and act like temporary summoned NPCs that mimic a party member role.

Unique Mechanic: Ancestral Spirits
- You summon 1 of 4 types of spirits, each lasting 10–20 seconds.

- Spirits move with the Spirit Walker, unlike Shaman totems.

- They share a portion of the Spirit Walker's stats and scale with Spirit.

- You can have one active spirit at a time, with talents eventually allowing two
.

- Spirits generate aggro, heal allies, deal damage, or restore mana depending on type.

- Spirits are summoned using Spirit Echoes, a charge-like resource that regenerates over time or via talents.
 **Ancestral Spirits**

 **Ancestor of the Hunt**
Damage
A ghostly warrior who strikes with ethereal spears, dealing physical and nature damage in bursts. Gains bonus damage if summoned during Ethereal Shift.

 **Ancestor of the Earth**
Tank / Distraction
A towering spiritual guardian that taunts nearby enemies and absorbs damage. Great for solo play or off-tanking.

 **Ancestor of Renewal**
Healing
A graceful spirit who pulses healing energy to nearby allies, prioritising the lowest-health target. Can channel emergency AoE healing.

 **Ancestor of the Sky**
Resource Regen
Calls down spirit winds that grant Mana regeneration or reduce ability cooldowns for nearby allies. Excellent in group content.

 **Talent Trees**
 **Spirit Bond (Healer)**
- Focuses on powerful group healing and spirit synergy.

- Improves Ancestor of Renewal's healing power and duration.

- Talents increase healing done while near your spirits.

- Final talent: Echoing Rebirth – your next spirit instantly revives a fallen party member with partial health.

 **Storm Voice (Damage Caster)**
- Offensive caster focused on elemental damage and spirit manipulation.

- Unlocks damaging spells like Lightning Spear and Spiritflame.

- Spirit summons cause extra nature or fire AoE upon entering/leaving.

- Final talent: Stormhost – Summon all four ancestors briefly for a coordinated attack.

 **Ancestor’s Will (Defender/Support Tank)**
- Focus on survivability, threat, and support.

- Your Ancestor of the Earth taunts multiple enemies and gains health-sharing link.

- Gain Passive Spirit Armor: resistance to shadow, nature, and arcane damage.

- Final talent: Guardians of the Path – periodically summons mini-spirits to shield nearby allies when you’re attacked.

 **Core Abilities**
- Summon Ancestor (10s CD): Calls forth one spirit.

- Ethereal Shift: Enter ghostly form for 6s, taking reduced physical damage, becoming semi-transparent, immune to roots.

- Spirit Mend: Heal over time spell scaling with Spirit.

- Dispel Spirits: Remove one magic effect from enemy or one curse from ally.

- Rites of Remembrance: Channel for 6s to regain Spirit Echoes faster.

- Spiritual Convergence: All active spirits pulse their effect once, even if on cooldown.
