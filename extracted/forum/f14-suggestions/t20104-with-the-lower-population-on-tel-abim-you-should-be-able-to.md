# With the lower population on Tel'Abim, you should be able to start the Shimmering Flats races by yourself

## Post 137627 by Apav (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-01T00:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137627#p137627 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We should be able to initiate a race by solo queueing on Tel'Abim for both Mirage Raceway: Gnome and Goblin quests as well as the Race Against Time quest.

I'm trying to do the gnome and goblin quests daily, but with the server population as it is, every day I have to just idly sit there for over an hour or more for someone to come by and queue. Most of the people who come by don't queue, as they're mostly Horde who do not understand what I, an Alliance player, am saying when I /point to the quest givers. I understand it's less of a race that way, but you're already on a timer and it doesn't matter who wins since both players get credit as long as one completes it. It's still better than not being able to do it at all because of the lack of players in the area. Also, the pre-requisite quest before the Goblin and Gnome daily quests on the test track already let you start by yourself.

In the exceedingly low chance that someone else is looking to queue as the same time as you, I also suggest adding a ~10 second timer before the race begins for the second player to queue, and when the timer runs out without another player queuing it starts the race with the one player.
