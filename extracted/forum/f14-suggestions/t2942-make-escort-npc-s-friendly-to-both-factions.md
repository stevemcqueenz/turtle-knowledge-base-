# Make escort NPC's friendly to both factions

## Post 18327 by Arishkegal — 2022-04-24T04:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18327#p18327 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Context: I joined turtle WoW because it was labeled a PvE server.

Situation: Certain quest NPC's such as the Defias traitor are camped by twinks: Just before finishing the quest, the NPC gets killed. If you try to save the NPC, you get flagged for PvP and get killed yourself by a twinked out char.

My opinion:
 1.  This behaviour only serves to grief the player doing the quest. This player chose not to flag for PvP. Therefore this has no place on a PvE server.
 2. This started with warmode as this brought in PvP crowd and toxic PvP behaviour.

My proposol:
Make escort NPC's friendly to both factions to reduce griefing.
Alternatives seem worse: giving the NPC's more HP can be abused (kiting them away). Making the NPC's hit harder makes the quest trivial (NPC solo's the quest). Removing Warmode reduces the amount of things the PvP crowd has to do and will lead to even more griefing.  So now that PvP-cat is out of the box, please reduce griefing by making certain NPC's not interactable (friendly to all).
