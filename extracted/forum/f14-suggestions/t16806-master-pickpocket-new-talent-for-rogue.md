# Master Pickpocket - new talent for Rogue

## Post 114829 by Ahlen (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-30T08:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114829#p114829 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Custom talents were added for Distract and Blind in the subtlety tree, I thought it would be a nice idea to add something related to pickpocket skill. My suggestion is:

Add a new talent in the 6th row of the subtlety tree called: "Master Pickpocket"

 1/3 Increases the amount of gold stolen while pickpocketing by 50% and gives you a 5% chance to loot an item from the enemy loot table
 2/3 Increases the amount of gold stolen while pickpocketing by 100% and gives you a 10% chance to loot an item from the enemy loot table
 3/3 Increases the amount of gold stolen while pickpocketing by 150% and gives you a 15% chance to loot an item from the enemy loot table

The part about looting an item would not work on Bosses and Elite enemies, only on regular mobs.
