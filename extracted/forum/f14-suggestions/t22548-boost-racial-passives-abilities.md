# Boost racial passives/abilities

## Post 156811 by Untakenusername — 2025-10-24T05:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156811#p156811 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Some races really lag behind in what they offer players and classes, while other buffs feel far too 'modest' where you don't really realize the effects, or the bonuses skew heavily towards one or two classes in the case of attribute bonuses. Bonuses to professions are nice, but also might as well just be 'flavor'.

I do love many actives, and with things like the buff to the Troll's regeneration passive, i'd rather like to see racial passives being more pronounced for a bit of increased flavour and personality... but if that isn't your thing, feel free to ignore those suggestions.

Do note that for some suggestions, I'm not assuming that ALL of them be added.

 **Dwarf**
Dwarves really only have one racial in Stoneform. They should have one or two more!
 - +10 to mining and smithing. Why they didn't have these bonuses boggles my mind.
 - Reduced effectiveness of curses, poisons, and frost slow effects. Either a 10%-15% reduction in the debuff/damage, or duration. (Replaces frost resistance)

 **Gnome**
So seldom seen I forget they exist. It feels like they need a bit more.
Expansive mind: Rather than boosting int, just boost mana by 10%... and max rage and energy by a small amount!
Tiny: Hostiles are less likely to notice gnomes! Gnomes are treated as 2 or 3 levels higher when determining how close they can get before an enemy aggros.

 **Night Elf**
Wisp Spirit is a reward for sucking at the game. It might as well not count as a racial and should just exist entirely for flavor.
 -Double (or triple?) the Attack Speed, Casting Speed, and Dodge chance buffs.
 -Increase the movement speed buff to 4 or 5% (Even if you don't boost the attack and casting speed, consider this!)
 -Resistance abilities are kind of boring and many people seem to loathe them. I'm not really bothered if Nature resistance is removed, even if it is to counterbalance what's listed above.

 **High Elf**
They're allright I guess? They feel undercooked.
 -Replace Agility boost with a +2-4% crit chance boost for melee, ranged, and spell attacks.
 -Quel'dorei Meditation: Kind of ass. Make it instant cast.

 **Human**
They've always been kind of bland bonus-wise. Perception feels like it should be a passive. I can't think of a decent active even if the CC cleanse in wotlk was clutch.
 -Make Perception a passive which increases stealth detection slightly.
 -Diplomacy: add "Buffs are 5-10% more effective and last 10-20% longer"
 -The Human Spirit: add a slight boost to rage and energy generation? (Granted, the boost to maces and swords might benefit melee classes more than i'm assuming. Boosted energy regen for rogues might make them too op)

 **Orc**
Blood Fury is pretty baller even if its now got a heal debuff. Command is too specific for me to suggest buffing it. Heck, if the other races are buffed, maybe they can lose the heal debuff and we call it a day.

 **Troll**
The buff to regen was pretty cool and what inspired this post.

 **Forsaken**
I've no complaints!

 **Tauren**
Tauren are another doozy. War Stomp is top tier, but you'd also think they'd be more resistant to things like stuns on account of their size. To avoid double dipping, i'm not gonna suggest stun resistance.
 - Remove nature resistance and boost their health bonus by 10%?
 - Endurance: Tauren are less likely to be dazed when attacked by enemy mobs.
 - Add 3-5 to their Defense skill?

 **Goblin**
Exit strategy is cool, but the other bonuses are kind of bland. Add one or two of these!
 -More money when completing quests!
 -Boost vendor discounts by 5% regardless of rep.
 - +10% healing/duration from/to potions/elixirs

Issue is if you make them this strong while being that varied, you're going to run into an instance where because you didn't do your research, guess what, it turns out your race class-combo is unironically the equivalent of a Classic human priest. Stuff like that sucks.

What I do agree on, is a standardizing of power level and making racials beneficial to all classes.

I think the Nelf +Haste passive is basically the perfect example of power level that isn't excessive while still being something that all classes desire. Contrast that to Helf where only one half of the classes it can be cares about one of its racials.
