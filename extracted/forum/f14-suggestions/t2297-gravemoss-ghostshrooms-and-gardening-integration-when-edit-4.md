I think OP's post was rather bloated, but consider again the grave moss situation.

Grave moss is a seriously strange bottleneck that has always made max level players spend countless hours farming it. LIPs, FAPs, speed pots are similar lower level potions that see use in raids, but a leveling population is more than enough to sustain the demand, but grave moss? nowhere near as much.

With 2 to soon 3 raid teams up against loatheb, we're already seeing the strain on limited grave moss open world availability.

Usually instance farming is a fallback to alleviate open world unavailability. On classic wow with hundreds of raid guilds per server, people were forced into SM GY to farm, but at least on classic they had 2-3 nodes per SM GY instance. Here, it's 1 or even 0 last I checked.

I don't think anyone considers this kind of strange, ephemeral, lower level grind to be critical to the enjoyment experience of progressing and farming loatheb and cthun.

I personally would have suggested Scholomance trash mobs dropping shadow oils, but wine's ideas work too, with some tweaking.
