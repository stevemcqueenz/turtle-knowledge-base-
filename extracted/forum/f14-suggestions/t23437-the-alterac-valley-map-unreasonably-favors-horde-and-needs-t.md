# The Alterac Valley map unreasonably favors Horde and needs to be fixed

## Post 164025 by Familymaker — 2025-12-30T00:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164025#p164025 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The layout of the map makes it very easy for the Horde to rush the Stormpike Graveyard and other areas, and very hard for the Alliance to rush the Horde areas, due to the layout of the map which has curved paths. Not to mention the choke point right before the two last Horde towers (people can literally sit on top of the overhead above the exit of the pre-area), while the Alliance only has a bridge the Horde can quickly cross over instantly.

To make matters worse, once Horde are inside the Bunker, it is very hard to get them out, as they can all go sit around the top and fire down. I mean.. the Bunkers are meant to make it easier for the defenders, not worse. Horde towers do not allow defenders to shoot at encroaching enemies within the tower, and attackers have to get past NPC's before they can cap it, whereas Alliance defenders are outside and will not attack cappers.

And by fixed I mean add some NPC in certain areas to make it more balanced, as I know Turtle Wow will likely not have the time to change the map itself. But small things here and there could fix the offset. Maybe more hp on the big tree? Or more impactfull attacks from the Alliance NPC's? And most importantly: Add frequest spawning NPC's on the inner upper floor, who can shoot at encroaching Horde attackers. That would also make it harder for Horde rogues to cap the Alliance towers, whereas Alliance rogues have to kill the NPC's as they have to basically walk over them to get past them on the narrow ledges.

This would also keep it thematic: How the Horde has used the elements to adjust the terrain in order to make it harder on attackers, whereas the Alliance would have better equipment and a more powerful tree monster, as well as better defended bunkers, and thus would have its own strenths when compared to the Horde.

The reason why Horde wins most of AVs (at least on Nordanaar) is because almost all PvPers from Horde queue for it while PvPers from Alliance don't. The outcome is that Alliance has mainly people who farm rep and some people without much interest or ambition to win the match. Alliance PvPers tend to not queue into AV because they see how shitty it is for honor/hour, they would be better playing WSG or AB. Horde PvPers, on the other hand, win 90% of all AVs so they naturally join AV for optimal honor gain. That's it. Do you really think that the same Horde players wouldn't be able to swarm their own base if bases were replaced?
