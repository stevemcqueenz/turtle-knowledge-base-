# Voicechat as RP Addon?

## Post 38578 by Elkepwn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-22T10:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38578#p38578 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello friends ![🙂](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f642.svg)

While observing two role players, I had an idea. How cool would be if you could not only talk in writing or hangin in the Discord channel but also via a kind of open voice chat?

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/18sjb73/Voice-Chat.jpg)

Voicechat has been around in retail for some time, but I find a version like in the GTA RP much better.

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/hpeDd3rr_1E]

The further away you stand from a person, the quieter you hear the person. This would be much more realistic in roleplay and can lead to random funny situations.

Of course, you can also turn off the voicechat or mute individual players to avoid harassment.

How do you think the idea and how easy/difficult would it be to implement? Maybe also as an external turtle addon like the shop or LFT?
