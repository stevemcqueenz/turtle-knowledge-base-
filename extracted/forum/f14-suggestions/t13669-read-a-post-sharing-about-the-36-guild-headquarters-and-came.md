## Post 92897 by Fight4feng — 2024-04-17T02:09:00Z

Hello developers of Turtle, I think the guild headquarters system is really great, although it's currently stopped due to some reasons. However, I have some suggestions, hoping that more new players can use and discover these surprises in their adventures, and participate in these headquarters systems.
 1.Introducing an NPC for Each Guild Headquarters
It would be great to have an NPC dedicated to introducing each guild headquarters. They could provide a brief overview of the headquarters, its functionalities, as well as some information about the guild's culture, slogans, etc
 2.Can players be hired as mercenaries for a guild, allowing them temporary access to the headquarters' functionalities? For instance, providing a temporary teleportation token or allowing them to be mercenaries for a week, wearing the guild's battle garb to access the headquarters' functions.
I'm sorry for my limited English skills. I hope you can understand what I'm trying to say. Also, thank you very much, Turtle WoW! I'm looking forward to new updates and game modes. Good luck!  turtle_in_love_head

I'd like to add that, to align with role-playing gameplay, when a player first discovers and enters a guild headquarters, an NPC could extend an invitation to join as a mercenary. As mercenaries, players could undertake tasks for rewards or provide resources to help maintain or expand the headquarters. In return, they would gain access to certain functional conveniences. Yes, you guessed it right—the much-desired tabard hearthstone system.
