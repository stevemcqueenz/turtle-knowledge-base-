# More faction-specific classes suggestion/idea/opinion

## Post 17743 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-03-26T12:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17743#p17743 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Horde-only class - Necromancer**

Why only Horde? Sure, if you go into the Plaguelands, you will see Human Necromancers, but why are they there and not in Elwynn Forest? Most likely because they were either cast out or escaped prosecution.

Necromancer traits:

 - Armor proficiency: cloth armor
 - Weapon proficiency: staves, wands, daggers
 - Special/Relic slot: Necromancy tomes
 - Talent trees: **Summoning** (skeletons, zombies, golems), **Curses** (debuffs, DoTs, utility, CC), **Spirits** (single target, DPS, AoE)
 - Specifics: in order to summon, needs to have a corpse first, summons disappear after logoff

 **Alliance-only class - Bard/Minstrel**

Why only Alliance? There is technically little reason for there to not be Bards/Minstrels in Horde society, but with their tribalistic ways, that role would fall to a chieftain or some elder in the tribe/village, who just stay there, whereas Bards/Minstrels travel the lands.

Bard/Minstrel traits:

 - Armor proficiency: cloth, leather armor
 - Weapon proficiency: staves, daggers, 1H swords, fist weapons (only because they are really underused in the game), bows, crossbows, thrown weapons
 - Special/Relic slot: lute, harp, flute, etc.
 - Talent trees: Healer, DPS, Buffs
 - Specifics: class is a hybrid between melee and ranged, having an equal number of both melee and ranged abilities

My idea for some faction-specific classes.
I'm not really suggesting anything because I can't imagine how insane it would be to make a custom class that meshes well with the chemistry of Vanilla WoW, and this is all just fun to think about.

 **Alliance-only class -** [Battlemage](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_mage)

Powerful melee spellcasters who combine the arcane abilities of a sorcerer with the ferocity of a warrior.

 **Battle Mage traits:**

 - Race: Humans, High Elves
 - Armor proficiency: Cloth armor, Leather armor, Mail armor (Plate armor at level 40)
 - Weapon proficiency: One-Handed Swords, One-Handed Maces, Shields, Wands, Off-hand items, Staves (Can learn Two-Handed Swords, Two-Handed Maces and Pole-arms at level 30)
 - Talent trees:
 **Vanguard -** Intellect-based Arcane tank, can specialize into 1H+Shield talents, defensive 2H stave/polearm talents, or 1H + off-hand items. Uses powerful defensive arcane magic to protect self from large groups of enemies

 **Spellsword -** Agility and Intellect-based Arcane melee DPS, learns to dual wield upon level 10, but can learn to equip Two-Handed weapons at level 30. Prefers strength over Agility when specializing into Two-Handed weapons. Fights off foes with powerful destructive spells and weapon enchantments.

 **Warmage -** Intellect-based mid-range DPS,  prefers one-handed weapons, off-hand items, wands and staves. Talents encourage cloth armor. These highly mobile mages specialize in close range and mid-range spellcasting, but also possess useful melee utility to defend themselves in close range.

 **Horde-only class -** [Witch Doctor](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Witch_doctor)

Spiritual spellcasters who call upon the shadow magic Voodoo through dark rituals. Primary stat is spirit.

 **Witch Doctor Traits:**
 - Race: Trolls, Orcs (the explanation for Orc witch doctors can be explained through apprenticeships and exchange of culture.)
 - Armor proficiency: Cloth armor, Leather armor
 - Weapon proficiency: Staves, Wands, Daggers, one-handed maces, one-handed swords, Off-Hand Items
 - Special/Relic slot:  Fetishes (Humanoid Skulls, Animal bones, etc), Idols (Voodoo Dolls, Carvings, etc)
 - Talent trees:
 **[Potion Doc](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Potion_doc) -** Spirit and intellect-based support/healer with some DPS. Mainly uses Staves, Wands, 1H Weapons and off-hand items. A magical alchemist who infuses his concoctions with powerful voodoo magic to create potent effects. Specializes in creating powerful buffs and potions to keep himself and his allies alive. Also can create powerful poisons, plagues and brews with potent negative effects on enemies. Rather than directly healing or damaging an ally/enemy, spells create potions, elixirs, brews, poisons and plagues in your inventory which in turn can be used while targeting an ally/enemy. Some potions can be traded to allies to be used by them instead. Some create AoE effects that instead instead of targeting an ally or enemy, target the ground below. Collects mojo from all corpses (excluding mechanical), a resource used in most potions.

 **Hexer -** Spirit and intellect-based ranged DPS. Mainly uses Staves, Wands, 1H Weapons and off-hand items. A powerful spiritualist who performs complex rituals ranging from chanting to dancing to curse and debilitate his foes. Spells range from but are not limited to; Mind control, fears, damages over time, transforming your enemies into lesser creatures, and the like. Can create a voodoo doll, increasing all damage to that class of enemy. Hexes get more potent when standing near corpses (because of spirits in the area), this effect stacks up to five times.

 **Shadow Hunter -** Spirit and agility-based physical DPS with shadow spells some healing capabilities. Mainly uses daggers, 1H weapons and staves. Has a level 20 talent to learn Throwing Weapons. Has a level 40 talent to learn bows. Masters of the voodoo, the Shadow Hunters are especially blessed by the Loa, and channel the blessings of many Loa at once. The Shadow Hunter would cycle through many Loa (stances) which grant various boons ranging from melee prowess, ranged prowess, offensive magical prowess, and healing prowess.
