# Disable Slow and Steady xp death penalty when killed by neutral town guards

## Post 163021 by Apav (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-17T20:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163021#p163021 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I play on the PvP server, and any time I'm in a neutral town there's about a 50/50 chance that someone will attack me, either in town or on the boat. Even if I don't do anything, the guards will still attack and kill me once I am attacked, and when I die it subtracts 5% of my xp with the Slow and Steady challenge which is a lot in the later levels.

I don't do a single point of damage to them which is strange, I don't think the guards should be attacking me if I don't defend myself, unsure if that's a bug or not. But regardless if it is or not, I don't think guard kills should count towards the xp penalty. They are effectively pvp kills (albeit indirectly) and pvp deaths don't trigger the penalty. Avoiding neutral towns entirely is not a good solution either as they are major quest hubs.
