# Prestige Classes

## Post 127788 by Mrkrissatan (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-21T10:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127788#p127788 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The idea here is that upon reaching level 60 you'd undertake a quest similar in length and complexity to the The Eye of Divinity quest line. These prestige classes would augment your existing class with new or changed availability's.

 ---

Death Knight

Theme: A corrupted warrior wielding unholy power.

Available To: Warrior, Paladin, Warlock

Key Abilities:

Death Coil: Augments execute/holy wrath/shadow bolt into a ranged shadow-damage finisher.

Unholy Aura: Reduces disease resistance and slightly slows enemies in range.

Runeblade Mastery: Enhances 2H weapon damage and converts some into shadow.

 ---

Spirit Walker

Theme: A mystic who walks between the spirit world and reality.

Available To: Shaman, Druid, Priest

Key Abilities:

Spirit Form: Temporarily shift to the spirit realm, becoming untargetable and healing nearby allies.

Ancestral Echoes: Causes heals or damage to “echo” for 25% effect after 3 seconds.

Totem of Passage: New utility totem that allows brief phasing through terrain.

 ---

Warden

Theme: Night Elf-style magical jailers, agile and precise.

Available To: Rogue, Hunter, Warrior, Druid

Key Abilities:

Glaive Throw: A bouncing ranged attack that applies bleed or slow.

Shadow Blink: Short-range teleport with a cooldown.

Prison Seal: Temporarily roots and silences a target.

 ---

Demon Hunter

Theme: A fel-infused warrior, trained to hunt demons.

Available To: Rogue, Warrior, Warlock, Hunter

Key Abilities:

Fel Sight: Detect stealth and invisible creatures.

Immolation: Short-range AoE fire damage, scales with attack power.

Warglaive Mastery: Increases crit with dual-wield.

 ---

Dark Ranger

Theme: A forsaken archer who uses shadow magic.

Available To: Hunter, Rogue, Priest

Key Abilities:

Black Arrow: DoT that weakens healing on the target.

Haunting Shot: Slows and reduces target's attack power.

Raise Companion: Raise your fallen pet as an undead version.

 ---

Blade Master

Theme: A disciplined swordmaster from the East.

Available To: Warrior, Rogue, Shaman

Key Abilities:

Mirror Image: Creates illusory duplicates that confuse enemies.

Critical Focus: Increases crit chance after dodging or parrying.

Wind Walk: Short invisibility and speed boost.

 ---

Necromancer

Theme: A caster who raises the dead and manipulates life energy.

Available To: Warlock, Mage, Priest

Key Abilities:

Raise Skeleton: Summons a temporary melee undead.

Death’s Grasp: Pulls a weakened enemy toward you.

Plague Cloud: AoE DoT centered on undead minion

I agree with Cyborg, a whole new class that you have to learn awkwardly at 60 is not great, and it's an extreme amount of design and balancing work for the devs.

I like the general concept, though. It should just be simplified. Ie: implement officially the mod that someone made which makes paladin spells look like DK spells.

Could be applied to other classes, so that the warlock can become a necromancer, again like that other mod.

Shaman becomes a witch doctor, mage becomes a lich or blood mage. Hunter becomes a dark ranger, etc.

You know, things like that where there isn't much balancing work to be done, maybe add a special ability or two, but nothing major, so it only becomes a matter of, basically, reskinning a class.
