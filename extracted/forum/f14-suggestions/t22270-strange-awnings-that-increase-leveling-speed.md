# Strange awnings that increase leveling speed.

## Post 154562 by Amadey2216 — 2025-10-06T06:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154562#p154562 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Seeing yet another "meat cube" of players at the entrance to the capital, I wonder if this is really what the developers intended when they introduced these awnings?
While traveling through the game world, I sometimes come across picturesque tent camps, cave encampments, and other places that look as if the resting adventurers just recently left. Or maybe they were devoured by something, leaving no trace, ugh.
Also, in strange places, you can find inns where you can rent a mount and even open a bank. A fair question arises: Wouldn't it have been better to create proper inns at crossroads, where adventurers could rest, sell junk, rent a mount (a bank is a bit much for me, but why not?), buy water and food, or demand it all from that mage in the corner—after all, that's literally what he's there for? And all of this under the protection of neutral bouncers.
And in truly remote areas, those same scattered tent camps around the campfire could have filled this role.
Right now, these "meat cubes" don't add anything good to the game; on the contrary, they ruin the entire atmosphere.
And sometimes I've seen some players try to interact socially with these monsters, but a meat cube is mute and deaf by definition.
Moreover, in those same capitals and some cities (Terren Mill?), it wouldn't hurt to add taverns. Spacious, brightly lit, and cozy, with crackling logs in the fireplace and cheerful music playing in the background.
Just think about what the guards would do to you if you lay down to rest on the pavement in the middle of the square?!
There should be designated places for rest!   crying_turtle
