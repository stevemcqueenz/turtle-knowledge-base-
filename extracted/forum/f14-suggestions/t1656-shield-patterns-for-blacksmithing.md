Instead of a Jewelcrafting profession I would prefer to add rings and trinkets to regular professions and I would do that in a manner which respects their rarity and difficulty to optain them. Rings should be produced in a raw form by Blacksmith like for example: Bland copper ring, Bland iron ring etc. and then refined by enchanters with random enchantments from a list of lesser effects learned from trainers and greater effects learned from patterns. Specific rings should require rare ressources for both their raw form and their enchanted end product.

Which actually makes sense from an rpg perspective as an item crafted specifically to hold enchantments will do so better than a form which is just massproduced.

Trinkets should be tied to transmutation and thus be closed off behind the transmutation receipt. Less magical trinkets are the engineering domain.

Allways remember that rarity and effects are gated by costs in both quantity and difficutlty in optaining the needed ressources. To balance those things will require a boatload of data and intense testing so even adding one receipt that is too easy to optain will break the immersion. Since only one is disappointing we could only get one try to add them in else we ruin the experience for everyone.
