As a healer, I cannot heal players of the opposite faction when instanced/partied.

Or add a fix/workaround for the healing bug/glitch? If it's a PvP flag situation, we could disable players' PvP flag once instanced with a party. And once they exit the instance, if they were previously PvP flagged, re-enable the flag? Just a few suggestions. I don't know if these are good solutions but it got me thinking.
