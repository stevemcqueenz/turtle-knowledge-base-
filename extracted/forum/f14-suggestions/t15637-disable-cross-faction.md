Will not happen. Turtle is still spiritually an RP server (like it was in the early days) and being able to communicate with eachother does wonders to the RP community and fosters character interactions from both sides. Plus the server's been like this for years and people like being able to play whatever race or faction they want without being cut off from their friends or guildmates.

Also, the argument that "it doesn't make sense lorewise" doesn't make sense at all since Common is the universal language of Azeroth, not a human-specific language. Orcs have been enslaved by humans for decades and thus it makes sense that orcs would know common. Forsaken have canonically known common in lore because, they are humans, and in fact they used to speak common really early on in retail WoW but Blizzard removed it for gameplay reasons. The rest of the Horde races would know common because Common is spoken across Azeroth. What sense does it make that a Furbolg would know common, but a Tauren would not?

And even ignoring that, and instead saying "The factions hate eachother or at war", that's not exactly true. The Horde and Alliance are at "peace". That's what the lore even said in retail vanilla. A lot of the alliance versus horde interactions would be unofficial skirmishes by people who hate eachother, rather than official military battles. Think of it from an RP or lore perspective. Why DOES say, a human rogue HAVE to hate and attack an Orc shaman out in the middle of no where like some cave? There's good reason to hate them, but he's not bound by any obligation to hate or kill that Orc Shaman. They're both stuck in a Yeti cave and both team up with eachother to get the hell out, share the goods, et cetera. That's what it is. Your character is generally not some Stormwind Guard or Orgrimmar legionairre or whatever. He's just an adventurer out in the world of Azeroth and it's on him whether he refuses to work with the opposing faction.
