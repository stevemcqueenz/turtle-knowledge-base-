Hey guys I brainstormed some possible changes or additions that could be added to the existing modes in the game let me know what you think. If you have anymore suggestions feel free to reply to the post

 1. Get rid of Character deletion on death on hardcore mode. Instead opt players into being able to resurrect, but they're kicked out of <still alive> and put into <mortals>

 2. Hardcore players that reach 60 should be put into an <immortal> guild or given an "immortal" title.

 3. New mode called either "Hardcore Ironman", "Nightmare mode", or "Permadeath". In this new mode it has all the properties of Hardcore and the addition of all glyphs being permanently activated at all times. Dying in this new mode will delete your toon.
