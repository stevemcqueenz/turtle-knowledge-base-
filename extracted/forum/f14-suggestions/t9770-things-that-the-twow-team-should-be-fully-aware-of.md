# Things that the twow team should be fully aware of

## Post 63832 by Eastvoice — 2023-09-14T01:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=63832#p63832 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the recent flooding of Chinese players in twow, I firmly believe that twow itself is facing unprecedented opportunities. However, besides the already chaotic situation bugging every sides, the upheavals and traumas looming ahead should not be underestimated by the twow team. To fully aware of the situation, the twow team should reevaluate two unfortunate yet critical facts: the innermost value of most Chinese players and the destructive power of Chinese wow workshops.

At least 90% of the Chinese players currently in the twow EU sever are extremely loot and prize oriented, and their only goal in this game is to level up to 60 with full speed and join their favorite raids to acquire prizes from dungeons, to replicate what they had done in the official server before Blizzard broke up with Netease, which I suppose is against why twow came into existence in the first place. These players are choosing none HC mode, (some even on war mode), swarming to every corner of each map, hunting down monsters that meets the eye, thus making things tough as hell for HC players as these poor lads stand alone on the side witnessing armies of those who they can not team up with rushing in and out, leaving nothing behind. On top of that, after decades of brainwash, a huge fraction of Chinese, especially those received minimum education thus too blind to see the real world, still bear irrational hatred to the west. They are either typing hanzi or pinyin in public channels to humiliate and insult the European society, or fighting back with all the filthy hanzi or pin yin beyond imagination when hearing someone say “Chinese causing lags” or “I hate Chinese”. This is no doubt causing more discrimination and racism in the server. I chose twow simply because of the harmonious environment, the challenging yet exciting HC mode, and the all the brilliant original stuff created by the twow team, not to behold the endless crossfire between the east and the west. Making matters worse, there are leading voices in twow related Chinese communities and groupschats not only boasting their conquest, outnumbering the EU players, but also threatening to launch server attacks or any other means necessary if any sorts of regional banning or ousting occurs. Some believe that the twow team will bend their knees in front of money and the whole vast number of cashcows from the east. Those who argue against these voices are labeled as traitors of mother China.

Chinese wow workshops are notorious for their horrific measures and multiple overwhelming victories against Blizzard and Nestease in Chinese and Asian official servers. They bloom and prosper wherever populations of greed and inpatient booty chasers(sadly many Chinese are) numbers up, sucking the juice out off the healthy environment by all means necessary. Bad news is that they are now invading twow as well. Instead of traceable rmt, offline trading are sprouting not through wow gold but via cold hard cash. Bunches of none HC lv60 accounts are now on the production line and on the market expecting  buyers. Several work shop raid teams are developing preparing for potential clients in the foreseeable future. It is not hard to anticipate that it won’t be long before everything from linens to Thunderfury will have its price on Taobao and Xianyu, as long as population of real player, not players plus workshop IDs, can maintain its current level. Moreover, the sea server plan might go south, not to mention the current appalling conditions. Indeed, rumors are on the street that the so called twow Chinese team was a treacherous workshop itself. Therefore, many Chinese players have no faith in sea servers as they believe that they will get backstabbed sooner or later, so EU sever is still their best choice.

The reason why so many Chinese players are able to play on the EU server is because of the the Chinese version client and addons. Banning hanzi even in all channels will be far than enough as players can still communicate fluently via pinyin as an alternative. Forbidding all contents in Chinese might be a better option to prevent further deterioration of the environment. Many Chinese players will leave the EU sever on that account, since they don’t read English with a limited vocabulary, and a complete banning of the language is what is worried the most and discussed among players and workshops. At the same time, those few who can blend in and truly embrace twow features and rules can still continue their journey in one of the last wow sanctuaries on the planet.
