# Rework Chat Eligibility (QOL)

## Post 68157 by Brisspvp — 2023-10-02T19:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=68157#p68157 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Under the current rules you are unable to speak in /Say, /Guild and /Whisper until level 5. This is a great rule, and I support it. But as someone who does low level twinking, could we have a rework to the rule?

- Once you have a character on your account that is level 5 or above, all characters on your account can not use all chat functions.

- If you join a guild other than one of the two default guilds you can use chat functions immediately.
