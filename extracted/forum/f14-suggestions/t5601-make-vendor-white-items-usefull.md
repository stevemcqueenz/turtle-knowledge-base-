Vanila wow itemisation was designed around strengh/agility/stamina/intellect/spirit.
Vendor items could have small amount of other stats.
Strategy 1. Stats which can be replaced with basic stats and useful in PvP: melee attack power, ranged attack power, just health, just mana, hp5, mp5, weapon damage, spell damage, spell healing, shield block value.
Strategy 2. Stats which help you to do quests of higher level and Useless in PvP: defense, weapon skill, chance to hit, chance to spell hit, decrease magic resistance of targets along with small amount of regular stats str/agi/stam/int/spi.

i would suggest a 50 to 75% nerf to the price of white armor/weapons from vendors. I would also suggest stats on them, roughly -50% of what a same ilvl green would offer, so if a lv 60 green is offering 20 int 20 stam, a white should offer 10 int 10 stam. Most players still wouldn't use them, maybe a HC who got unlucky with one or two gear slots, but yeah this content would go from unused to sometimes useful, and thats a big win I feel.

The fact that whites could only ever offer increased armor, just flies in the face of many classes being designed around not being hit, and gaining their armor through spells, kiting, pets, and shields. And so vendor gear being armor based always felt like a relic of 2000-2002 wow development before class and stat design came into fruition. A fix to this would be based.
