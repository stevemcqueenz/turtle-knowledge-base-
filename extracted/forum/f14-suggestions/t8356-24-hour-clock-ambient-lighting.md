# 24 Hour Clock & Ambient Lighting

## Post 53845 by Hirovision — 2023-07-17T18:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53845#p53845 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am really enjoying my experience on Turtle WoW. I wanted to make a couple suggestions to make things even better and more immersive. I am a HUGE fan of the Darker Nights mod, however, since WoW has a 24 hour clock, it would make many of us who are only able to play at certain times, only know Azeroth in total darkness. Fun for a hardcore challenge playthrough, but not so, for the long term experience. I would love to see Turtle WoW develop a normal day/night cycle that goes through 1 day in, say 1-1.5 hours. I think this would have so many more people using cool addons like darker nights.

The other suggestion is ambient lighting. I love the way character models look when you hover over them. NPC & PC alike. They brighten up and the colors really pop. It would be awesome to have an ambient lighting toggle that would make all players & npc light up in the world, as if you were hovering over them. Combined with the HD pack that is on the forums, and even darker nights, this would be an amazing experience.
