# Feral Druid Cat issues

## Post 161226 by Soulraziel91 — 2025-12-01T14:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161226#p161226 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello I would like to raise some design issues with Feral Cat and how to perhaps address them.
 1.Tiger's Fury & Reshift- Reshifting was clearly added so that powershifting is easier and it has always been a core part of playing feral cat in vanilla, however powershifting drops tiger fury's dmg buff and thus you powershift to spend mana in order to gain energy only to then have to spend that energy to regain the dmg buff you lost. This is very awkward and IMO reshift should not drop the Tiger's fury dmg buff especially since its massive given that most of the dmg comes from AA.
 2. Bleed build - bleed build suffers a lot from bosses and whole raids that are immune to bleeds, unfortunately that makes a lot of sense flavor wise, however there can be added effects to druid's bleeds (rip, Rake) that add different effect when used against bleed immune targets, with the aim of having Open Wounds talent interact with bleeds even if target is immune. The issue to solve here is that the optimal bleed build does not include the shread energy cost reduction talent, thus you need 2 dps builds for bosses that are immune to bleeds.
 3. No Aoe - Swipe should be usable in cat, or have another AoE spell.
