# A talent that gives Hunters bonuses for going petless

## Post 157161 by Troggyboy — 2025-10-26T12:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157161#p157161 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Warlocks already have something similar with their Soul Entrapment and Demonic Sacrifice talents. I don't know if these are even viable tbh, especially since it'll cost you 4 talent points in total but this isn't even the main reason I like these talents - it's that they allow players that like the Warlock class overall but don't like pet management in particular to play the class the way they like without it feeling like a straight up downgrade.

So could we maybe have something similar for Hunters? In my research I stumbled over a "Lone Wolf" rune in Season of Discovery that gave you 25% damage bonus while no pet is under your control. That one kinda seemed broken but if it was tied to spending talent points like for Warlocks and the numbers would be tuned down a bit I feel like it would be fine.

What do you guys think?
