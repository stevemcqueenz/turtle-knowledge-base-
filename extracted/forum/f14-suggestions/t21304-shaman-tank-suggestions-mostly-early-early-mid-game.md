# Shaman Tank Suggestions, mostly early, early-mid game

## Post 147753 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-22T21:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147753#p147753 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I was considering the current talent/ability lineup and wanted to make some suggestions regarding shaman tanking.
My goal is not to buff a build mindlessly.
I will always argue why I think a change would be beneficial at any given point.

 **Early game mitigation**

Now, that elites are stronger, early, early-mid game shamans are at a serious disadvantage at tanking.
First, lemme list the tools and talents which help with shaman mitigation.

 **1. Stoneskin Totem**
This is basically a demoralizing shout. However, it has problems. You can always use one earth totem at a time.
Certain encounters require tremor (think about half of Wailing Caverns) or more AoE threat, specially if the mobs have high fire resistance.
What I wanna point out: the shaman version of demoralizing shout cannot be always used when needed, because sometimes other earth totems take priority.

 **2. Healing Stream Totem**
It basically gives a weak HoT for the party. It should never be strong, since it would be unfair against HoT casting classes, but it had to be mentioned here, since on paper it helps with mitigation.
As a water totem, its spot is frequently overtaken by mana spring totem or cleanse totems.

 **3. Earth Shield**
A good tool, but not relevant for early, early-mid game. Learnt way too late.

 **4. Ancestral Guardian**
A really strong talent but it gets really relevant after level 40.
Leather items are not famous for having an interesting amount of armor.

 **5. Spirit Armor**
A super strong talent and compensates for many armor amount issues. It is however, not really an early talent (and should not be) and it gets better as shields get better, meaning a scaling as we level up.
The focus is still early, early-mid game.

 **6. Elemental Weapons**
Basically gives a defensive stance mitigation bonus to rockbiter.
Not bad, but comes late, not relevant for early, early-mid game.

 **7. Grace of Air Totem**
Comes late, usually overtaken by Windfury based on party demands.

 **8. Shield Specialisation**
I left this beauty to the end.
To understand the problem with it: unlike warriors and palas, shamans will never go crit immune, they are basically bears with parry and block. On top of that, there is no ability to temporarily raise block chance for similar reasons (think about shield block or holy shield).
What it means is this, if I get this right: this talent basically replaces 5% normal hit with 5% block, not touching the really hurtful crits and cruching blows. On top of that, the talent at early game basically gives a slight (!) damage mitigation with 5% more probability for a build in leather and with no def CDs.
There is a reason almost everyone, including tanks go for Ancestral Knowledge.

Having this out of the way, what I'd recommend:
 **1. Give a scaled-down earth shield and water shield much earlier, preferably by up to level 15.**

 **To explain this:** shaman tanks do not struggle with threat at the early game.
Rockbiter, earth shock are both disgustingly effective and there is also a taunt and the totem threat.
But being forced to use lightning shield means:
 1. No way to switch damage mitigation mode (earth shield).
 2. No way to switch mana regeneration mode (mana shield).

As a result, the early shaman tank generates a disgusting amount of threat, loses HP like a melee DPS and either drinks all the time, or refuses to use half of his abilities to conserve mana for later, auto-attacking with rockbiter instead (!).
In short, lightning shield being the only option pushes shaman tanks where they are already OP (threat), while negating the options which they desperately need to be valid in a stronger dungeon-environment (mitigation, mana management).

 **tldr:** More Water Shield and Earth Shield levels please, starting as early as level 14-15.

 **2. Remove Shield Specialisation talent and give a valid tank talent to the enhancement tree.**

The current setup is like a defense talent for bears, arguably worse.
There are many ways to do this.

 1. Remove that talent, add something like: raises your HP by 10%.
 2. Remove that talent and move the 10% damage mitigation from Elemental Weapons here, unless it breaks PVP.
You can be fancy, if needed, not sure about the proper balancing.
For example: 10% damage reduction / 10% damage reduction and +5% HP. Whatever is fair for 5 talent points.
If you go this way, you could also add something interesting to rockbiter under elemental weapons.
More effective water shield?
Reduced crit chance?
My favourite would be a 7/14/20% chance for a WF proc if WF totem is down. It feels bad that the only melee who can never experience WF procs is a shaman, specially in a world where even bears can proc it.

___________

 **With these two changes what we achieved:**
 1. The early shaman being much better at mitigation whenever needed (earth shield), and switching to water shield at easier packs to get mana. Smoother gameplay, happier healers.
 2. Added defensive talents to the tank build which arrives via leather armor and has no defensive CD.

___________

Two more change ideas **but these two are not proposals.**
They are way less realistic, but wanna include them as **unfinished** ideas.

 **1. Rework Bloodlust so it can be both a threat and a mitigation CD.**
However you wanna do it.
Honestly, It is not procced for the party buff effect, as an option that could be replaced with a damage reduction entry.
But any way works.

 **2. Include Healing Stream Totem under Enhancing Totems.**
Honestly, only tanks use that totem sometimes. Within that talent you already provided secondary effect to a totem (stoneskin and block amount), so I can go the same way here.
If healing stream totem is enhanced, let it give a party aura effect which increases healing received by 5%.
Would be much more beneficial and would result in clever comp build-ups.
As a balance, you can get rid off the extra blocked amount from stoneskin any time (without a block increase ability, the whole concept is clunky).

OR

 **3. Stoneskin Totem and Enhancing Totems**
As an option, you could replace the extra blocked amount to something like increased earth orb effectiveness. I think stoneskin could easily synergize with earth shield and it is much more flavorful than buffing blocks in a build which doesn't have active block increases.
