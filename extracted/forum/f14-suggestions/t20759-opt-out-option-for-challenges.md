# Opt out option for challenges

## Post 143995 by Xerilin (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-08-09T13:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143995#p143995 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In short, I would like to suggest reintroducing an option to turn off challenges. Whether this is by putting glyph masters back into the game or by some other means, I don't care.

Here are some of my reasons:

- When most of us started their challenges, we could still opt out by talking to a glyph master. For example, I have several characters with slow and steady mode and when I started them, I was under the impression that I could turn it off, if I didn't want it anymore. This is especially true for Level 1 Lunatics, who can't level at all, but other challenges are affected as well.

- The 1.18 changes to slow and steady rewards make some players not want to do the challenge anymore, because they were mainly after one of the illusions, for example. I know of some people in my guild, who are thinking about just deleting some characters over this.

- This is not hc, where no opting out is kind of part of the point. Being able to opt out of a challenge along the way is not devaluating the challenge. Apart from S&S, you don't get any rewards, if you quit before 60. You just have a harder time until then.

In relation to Lunatics:

From my understanding their intent is to never allow opting out again which I am fine with, but they should rewrite the challenge to not hint at that. This is due to these 5+ titles not being available to already established 60s.

Personally I am a fan of this change for the fact that this is a seperate endgame for a "niche gamemode".

This prevents many players from just making one to get Arena Grand Master or Horde Exalted Durotar Labor Union and then not actually participating in the community, which is a good thing to keep Gurubashi less contested for Lunatics and people who actually want it to participate.

For other challenges I can understand, like Artisan, S&S, Boaring, Vagrant. Just letting you know that from what I have heard in relation to Lunatics is it is no longer an intention.
