# Shen'dralar Goldsmithing trinkets

## Post 157565 by Isvya (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-10-28T18:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157565#p157565 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello,

Im pretty sure most of you are familiar with the Dire Maul class trinkets acquired by turning in the books looted from the abandoned city.
Just one of the trinkets in case anyone curious:
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/741354490959233044/1432789703488180264/image.png?ex=690254e5&is=69010365&hm=9bd74d32055e8048c054b04f05fe4893b6b59d730b26cc87b511804c80da80ab&)

they are decent for a fresh 60 but replaced quite easily with upgrades, some of them are considered pre raid bis items.

Aight, next up we have goldsmithing, which i find is a bit lackluster. There are some good items yeah sure, mostly for casters. Compared to other crafting professions its not fully fetched yet.

Next up, we have the newly added shen'dralar reputation. So, why not add a recipe for each of these class trinkets to upgrade them, of course make the trinkets goldsmithing locked. Increase their power level to somewhere between BWL/MC.

Make the recipes use some mats like fiery cores, arcane essences, herbs and such.

Just an example for the warrior trinket, minor stat increase and an additional effect.
 15 fire resistance
 10 defense
 40 attack power
 1% haste // 1% hit // 1% crit // you gain 5 extra rage on a melee critical strike // 10 stamina // your heroic strike deals an additional 15 damage
pick any, just some ideas.

thanks for reading
