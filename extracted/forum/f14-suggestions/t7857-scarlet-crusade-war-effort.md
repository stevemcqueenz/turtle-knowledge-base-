# Scarlet Crusade War Effort

## Post 50417 by Dogfight — 2023-06-17T21:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50417#p50417 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We know that sometime in the future there is a Scarlet Crusade raid coming. I think it would be cool to have some sort of war effort to build up excitement before the release of that raid. Perhaps not as extensive as AQ War Effort, but something that still would need the community on the server to cooperate to complete it. I do not have very broad knowledge of lore in general, so I apologize if there's any conflicts with the lore here.

 **Background**
Before the release of Naxxramas there was a Scourge Invasion event. Undeads would invade towns and areas even outside Eastern and Western Plaguelands. We had to fight them back and then assault Naxxramas and Kel'Thuzad. Now Kel'Thuzad has been defeated and we can assume the Scourge has been weakened. This opens an opportunity for the Scarlet Crusade who also have been fighting the Scourge. They now take control over Western (WPL) and Eastern Plaguelands (EPL). Undeads are pushed back to Stratholme and Scholomance as their last strongholds. The Scarlet Crusade takes control over towns and areas in WPL and EPL that were formerly controlled by the Scourge. Powerful Scarlet Crusade patrols roam the WPL and EPL (Like the mounted ones in EPL). Rumours of a Scarlet Crusade offensive towards Tirisfal Glades/Undercity and Southshore appears.
The Alliance have set up at blockade at Chillwind Point. The Horde are holding their ground at the Bulwark. This is where the War Effort begins.

 **Phase 1**
 - Objective is to take back control of WPL and prepare for an offensive into EPL
 - Gathering of mid level materials and equipment to supply the war effort.
 - Quests to take back control of Andorhal, the farms in WPL and take down the Scarlet Crusade patrols
 - Patrols possibly elite groups quests and patrol commander drops high quality gear and possibly other items needed for the war effort.
 - Phase 1 ends when enough materials have been gathered, Andorhal and farms have been reclaimed, patrols have been defeated and the Scarlet Crusade have been forced back to Hearthglen. Hearthglen is their final stronghold and becomes a short 5-man instance of 3-4 bosses.

 **Phase 2**
 - Objective is to take back control of EPL
 - Gathering of high level materials and equipment to supply the war effort
 - Quests to take back control of Light's Hope Chapel, other important areas and take down the Scarlet Crusade patrols
 - Patrols are group quests also here. Perhaps even more difficult, 5-10 players and drop improved loot and important items for the War Effort.
 - Phase 2 ends when Light's Hope has been reclaimed and the Scarlet Crusade has been pushed back to Tyr's Hand. Tyr's Hand is their final stronghold and becomes a 10/20man raid.

 **Phase 3**
This is the assault on the Scarlet Raid Instance. Perhaps a short timed event like AQ gates opening as a final push towards the entrance. Rewards for participating in the war effort and completing a questline from the start to finish of the war effort. High level rewards, perhaps some gadgets/strange looking items that will give you an advantage, but not required, in some of the encounters if used at correct times. Up to players to discover for themselves.

 **End note**
This would probably delay release of a Scarlet Raid even further, but we don't know how far away from release they are. So perhaps it is possible to do some of this if not all. I think it would be a cool way to introduce the new raid and make the community work together towards a greater goal.
What happens after the Scarlet Crusade has been driven back from the Plaguelands? Who knows. There are always threats lurking in Azeroth. Maybe the trolls from Zul'Mashar and Hinterlands want to reclaim some terrain? Perhaps the Plaguelands become green, peaceful and prosperous areas, or maybe someone else comes up with a continuation for this.

I'm on board with your ideas. Just some points for the scaling/level thing: This is not as stringent in Vanilla as you make it seem. A Lich is an iconic and very powerful enemy and you have them from lvl 42 (Amnennar the Coldbringer in RFD) to literally the final boss of the game (KT in Naxx). RFD is roughly the same level as SM Cath, so why shouldn't a Scarlet Crusade leader be an ultra-endgame raid boss?

And I would argue the opposite. That you encounter Scarlets so early should make it more likely to encounter them in the endgame as well. It's this idea of grand story-lines. There are a few really big storylines in Vanilla:
One is Silithids in Kalimdor. From very early on (Barrens) you keep encountering them and their hives (Thousand Needles, Feralas, Tanaris, Un'Goro), you investigate, you worry about these seemingly alien lifeforms until it all culminates in Silithus with the Gate Opening event and the AQ raids.
Another are Dark Iron Dwarves which you get from lvl 5 to lvl 60 - and the force behind them leads you into MC.
The corruption of Stormwind starts very early (Solomon in Redridge asking for help, Defias, Missing Diplomat) and ends up leading you into Ony and BWL.
Scourge/Plague keeps you busy from lvl 1 to Naxx.

Scarlet Crusade is a perfect candiate for a similar grand story arc. You have them from the foot soliders in Tirisfal at lvl 5, through SM into WPL, EPL, Hearthglen, Try's Hand, Strat Bastion. Why not a raid to cap it off? I too think the story should involve some twists to make it less boring, but it wouldn't feel like a "retcon" to me if it were just a straight head-on conflict with the Scarlets. They've always been there, they're well established as formidable enemies to the player on all levels even though lore-wise they haven't been super lucky with their campaign.
