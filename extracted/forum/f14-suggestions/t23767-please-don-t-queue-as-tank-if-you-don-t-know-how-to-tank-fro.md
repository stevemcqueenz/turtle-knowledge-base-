# Please don’t queue as tank if you don’t know how to tank (from a healer)

## Post 166680 by Nuninho32 — 2026-01-27T23:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166680#p166680 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I’m leveling as a healer and honestly getting pretty frustrated with dungeon groups lately.
This isn’t about being perfect or knowing every pull — it’s about basic tanking fundamentals. Too many people are queueing as tank just to skip LFG time, while clearly not paying attention to their surroundings or understanding the role at all.
As a healer, it’s exhausting to deal with:
 - Warrior not using def stance or pala not using Ritheous Fury
 -Chain pulling without checking the healer´s mana
 - zero awareness of patrols or positioning
 - mobs constantly running loose because of no threat control
 -LOS pulls where the healer gets blamed afterward

If you’re new to tanking, that’s totally fine — everyone has to learn. But please:
 - Say you are learning
 - Ask questions
 - Start Slow
 - or queue as DPS until you’re comfortable

What’s not fine is wasting 4 other people’s time by queuing as tank just to get instant groups and then expecting the healer to brute-force mistakes with mana and stress.

Healers are not miracle workers. We can support a tank who’s learning — but we can’t compensate for someone refusing to pay attention.

Turtle WoW has a great community overall, and dungeon runs are way more fun when people respect the role they sign up for. Please just queue honestly and play your role — it makes everyone’s experience better.

The tank abilities for each class do massive threat while leveling for each class respectively (Shield Slam, talented Holy Strike, talented Bear), and will massively out-threat any DPS even if they have world buffs.

I leveled my paladin as holy, leveled 2 priests as holy and leveled one warrior as protection. I could always do dungeons at the minimum level if the other role was also dedicated spec for the dungeon. People really underestimate how fast tank leveling can be, since you'll always get instant dungeon pops.

I highly recommend people to try out designated tanking specs if they want to do dungeons. You will hold aggro no problem, be able to tank dungeons much earlier and level up decently fast if you do dungeon spamming with worldbuffs. It might not be as fast as hunter leveling or AoE farming, but it's pretty fast.

I recently had a LFG group for DM North and the tank was geared (had some dps T2 at least) but didn't even attempt to hold aggro on more than 1 mob. It has been quite a while since I died that much as a healer.

This was the first and so far only time where I had the distinct feeling someone who had no interest in tanking was queuing as tank for the faster pop. And I can see how this kind of stuff makes you despair and write forum posts.   dead_turtle_head

There is no shame in wanting to learn how to tank but being inexperienced and undergeared. We all started out somewhere. But the people who are serious about wanting to learn will probably start their journey on an instance they outgear so they can start building their mindset, train their reactions, form some habits and muscle memory for their tank setup/hotbars. Say you are 60, have some decent gear for dps, and now you want to learn how to tank. How about jumping into Sunken Temple or BRD or Hateforge Quarry? For someone completely new to tanking there is plenty of challenge in handling those instances, but the stakes for the party are much lower.

The most important thing to bring to a tanking job is your mindset. Paying attention. Trying to hold aggro on all mobs. Coming to the rescue of your party members. A tank mindset is non-negotiable.
Everything else is icing on the cake - gear, spec, even character level. It helps, it does make a difference. But you can overcome those deficiencies with skill, while you can't really overcome a lack of basic interest in doing a decent tank job with anything.
A long long time ago I had an LFT group for LBRS. The tank was 57 and I rolled my eyes, because I don't really view the instance as a levelling dungeon. That person turned out to be one of the best tanks I've ever run a dungeon with. Methodical, precise, focussed. By their own account they hadn't run LBRS in years (first time on TWoW). Their gear was mediocre. They were underlevelled. They lacked instance knowledge. But they had a full tank spec and more importantly, they had the right mindset. That one run many years ago completely changed my mind about tanks and the importance of being over-levelled for dungeons.

I have a lot of patience for tanks who lack levels, lack gear, lack experience. But I have no patience for people queuing as tank who don't care to tank.
