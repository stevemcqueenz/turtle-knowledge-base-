I got a suggestion on how to perhaps bright up the world at night, especially for people with the darker nights patch! It's something me and my friend used to do back on another server.

The suggestion being:

If it's possible to put a light spell on an invisible (morph or be it a spell being applied to them) NPC in the Vanilla engine, such as the: <https://www.wowhead.com/spell=230942/moo> for example in Legion. GMs could spawn them easily in and out of the game with no issues! Maybe it's possible to make the lights come in different colors? This would be especially handy with the Scourge's blue-campfires and or torches.

I hope the suggestion is a clear read for you and if there's any questions about the concept I'm happy to answer it!
