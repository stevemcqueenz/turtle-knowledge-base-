This is almost exclusively a lvl 60 dungeon problem when ppl become more loot focused. Personally, I've had zero toxic interactions leveling using group finder across multiple toons.

About half of my queue pops for popular ran dungeons leads to leader hard reserving or me getting kicked due to my class's loot conflicting with the party stack queuing on group finder. And almost ALL of my queue pops for nicher dungeons lead to hard reserving items,class kick,leavers. There should be a checkbox for each player utilizing dungeon finder if whether or not they want the same class as their own in their party. This change alone would reduce so much toxicity.

Hard reserving anything while using dungeon finder should be bannable offense. Use world chat.

Everything below is a can of worms rant. Above are my actual 2 suggestions.
Leaving a newly formed group-finder group after the first x-minutes should lead to a +1hr queue ban with a lovely 2x multiplier each repeat offense within a refreshing duration of 72 hours. If someone tries to dodge penalty by afk'ing until kicked should simply be perma ban. If leader is afk dodging, gm ticket. For 99% of players, irl emergencies don't magically happen after hitting yes to a queue pop. And if by rare circumstances they actually did, submit a ban appeal. They should just automatically be unbanned if the account had zero strikes. If there were strikes prior an actual gm would need to appeal it.

Declining a queue pop is the only fair way of avoiding a dungeon that shouldn't have any penalty associated with it (unless consecutively). The player accepting a queue pop has already been informed the dungeon's title, slap on an estimated time to clear average to the queue pop menu and by that point the player has agreed to the terms and their fate is sealed. do the dungeon that they signed up for.
