# Revenge of the deep does not give reward for a mage

## Post 108679 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-19T12:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108679#p108679 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Quest [Revenge of the deep](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41158) gives as a reward: gun, 1h sword, dagger, mace. But if you make goblin mage, you can equip nothing from this list.
It is not very important, but feels like oversight.
