# Arcane power

## Post 103637 by Ougawa — 2024-10-18T13:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103637#p103637 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,
I'm playing on Turtle WoW for a year and a half, and as for now I really enjoy your work.
I think most of classes changes are good, but I have a problem with one change : Arcane power
My opinion is shared by some other mage players discussing about changes : in that state, arcane power will certainly not be used most of the time.

 -> It doesn't seem worth the risk to use this spell. You get drained 40% of your mana after 20seconds, you lose 75% of mana regeneration, and you can't go under 10% of your max mana. It's currently too much, and I don't think it suits well with what this ability want to make us do : unloading our DPS. How can we unload our DPS if after a dozen of seconds, we'll be limited and won't be able to use our mana if we don't want to die? It currently sounds like a bloodlust with a too big cost in return. And it wouldn't be fun to wait the end of the 20seconds to be able to use abilities again.
Also, does it really increase DPS at the end if you have to stop using your abilities until arcane power stops? It's a debate.

 -> This spell is UNPLAYABLE in HC. Please, don't be in the mentality "if it doesn't fit for HC, don't play it", we want to be able to have fun with the new changes too. A lvl 40talent is supposed to radically improve a gameplay, and it doesn't improve it right now since the risk of death is too high.

My suggestion would be :
 -> Instead of death, why wouldn't you put a debuff on a character who goes under 10% of his mana? It could be a long silence, or a stun for example. It would still force players to play well to not suffer from this debuff and not lose DPS in a fight. But it would make it playable by HC players, and certainly more viable too in general.
 -> It wouldn't be weird in terms of lore. Being silenced or stunned because you unloaded too much magic and your character can't control it would still feel lore-wise.

I wish you guys take into account my recommendation. I know you liked the idea of death around this ability, but I'm not sure it'll make the ability played a lot. It doesn't feel worth it.

Thanks for reading me!
