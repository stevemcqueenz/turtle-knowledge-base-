# Suggestion: Add Bow & Claw Recipes to Leatherworking

## Post 154715 by Kosasa — 2025-10-07T12:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154715#p154715 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I have a quick suggestion for the Leatherworking profession. Right now, plate classes craft armor + weapons via Mining + Blacksmithing, and cloth classes make armor + wands through Tailoring + Enchanting—all using their own profession pairs.  satisfied_turtle_head
For leather classes, we already have Skinning + Leatherworking (but Leatherworking only makes leather armor).   scared_turtle_head
I hope Leatherworking can be slightly expanded to craft a small number of not overly powerful bows and claw weapons.  turtle_tongue_head
This way, leather classes can also make both armor and weapons with their own profession pair (like other classes do), adding utility to Leatherworking without breaking the game’s balance (thanks to the weapons’ modest power).
Thanks for considering this!
