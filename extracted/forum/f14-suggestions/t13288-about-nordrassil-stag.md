## Post 89342 by Theexpell — 2024-03-04T07:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89342#p89342 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It takes more than a texture update to save this monstrosity of a model. The same model is used for the druid alternative shapeshift of the travel form. It is beyond ghastly to look at and was clearly never meant for anything but killable NPCs.

I have been diving deep into the subject of modding this .m2 file. The issue is that there are no good updates for the stag/deer in WOTLK. They finally did get updates in Legion, but of course, as "HD models," which do not fit within Turtle-WoW's "vanilla WoW" aesthetic. This model will need an artist, which significantly increases the production time.

My suggestion is to make your own .m2 with textures for the entire line of mobs that use the same .m2. Put it out for everyone to view in the modding community. Turtle-WoW is a community-driven project, and if everything just clicks, there might be a way for Turtle-WoW devs to actually put it into the game.

Together, we can build a better gaming experience.
