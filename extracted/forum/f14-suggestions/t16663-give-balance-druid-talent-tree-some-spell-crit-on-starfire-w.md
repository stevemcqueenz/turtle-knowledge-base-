# Give Balance Druid talent tree some +spell crit on Starfire/Wrath

## Post 113823 by Halfgeek9 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-22T10:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113823#p113823 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Its the only hybrid now which lacks crit bonus talent on its main nukes. Shamans get +11% crit rate on their Lightning spells, Priests get +10% for Smite/Holy Fire etc. These also have strong talents that synergize with crit spells, such as instant Smite or 2x crit dmg which Balance Druid also have.

Moonkin's Aura is a group wide 3% crit buff, thus not really good for self, being 10+3% > 3%, and given Druid has Nature's Grace for faster spell casts & 2x crit dmg for spells, they really need spell crit.

Suggestion: Moonfury talent also increases critical chance of Wrath & Starfire by 2/4/6%.

This will make Balance leveling spec more fun (big crits more often) & efficient (better DPM), and a small boost to end-game Moonkins, who still give their 3% aura to the grp but have a 6% buff for self with its two main nukes, Wrath & Starfire.

If its end-game dps is too high, reduce the spell bonus coefficient by a tiny % to compensate for more frequent crit.

Overall it will make the spec synergize better.
