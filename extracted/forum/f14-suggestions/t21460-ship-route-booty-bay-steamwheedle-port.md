# Ship route: Booty Bay <-> Steamwheedle Port

## Post 148756 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-27T20:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148756#p148756 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sup everyone,

Steamwheedle port is a port as it says in the name. It even got a dock. Yet no ships are docking here ever.
I was thinking that the neutral goblin towns could be a bit more connected.

It would make the trip to Gadgetzan less annoying. As rn if you are in the Eastern Kingdoms as Alliance you have to go to Menethil use the ship to Theramore and then fly to Tanaris.
As Horde you have to either use the Zeppelin to Orgrimmar or the ship from Booty Bay to Ratchet and then also fly down to Gadgetzan.

So why not also have a ship going from booty bay to steamwheedle port?

It seems like a good and covenient shipping route a lot of people would use.

Also while I am making this thread I was thinking that Goblin ship routes should get their own unique ship model.
In Warcraft 2 there were goblin ships... well Giant Turtles with goblin stuff mounted ontop. Seems like a good fit!

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/CpVOQ5b.png)

Kinda like this but less attacky and more transporty.
