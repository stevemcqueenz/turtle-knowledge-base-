# Just remove 2x EXP from Tel'Abim . . .

## Post 174339 by Troggyboy — 2026-04-07T14:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174339#p174339 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I know this isn't exactly what everyone wants but I feel like it's one of the main reasons the server's population is so low.

Presumably it's on here because the devs think PvP players don't care that much about grinding and want to spend less time doing it and more time just fighting other players in the open world or BGs. But honestly, this isn't the reason why I joined Tel'Abim. For me, it's the thrill and the action of engaging with other players while questing and being in the open world, it's the salt for the stew, without which it would be rather bland.

But if you're that brand of player, Tel'Abim isn't ideal. Since so many people only care about getting to 60 on here, people quest faster, there are fewer people around to begin with AND the population is kind of split between WPvP players and BG players, this drastically reduces the amount of players from the other faction you can actually run into in the open world.

You can technically turn off EXP gains for yourself by doing the Slow and Steady challenge or turning on .xpmode but in reality that doesn't quite work either. For some reason it ignores the EXP gained from quests which means that those still give you too much, leaving you overleveled anyway, especially early on. This has been known for a long time and gets complained about by new players finding out in world chat pretty much every other day but it still has yet to be adressed and I honestly believe it has cost Tel'Abim a lot of players who would've stuck around otherwise.

But personally, I'd go even further and just remove those EXP gains entirely. The game has been designed with normal EXP rates in mind, both the classic content aswell as all of the new turtle stuff. It's entirely too jarring and imbalanced when the EXP gets doubled and it just takes away from the game imo, and I truly believe the population is so low because many other players feel the same and NOT because people dislike PvP servers.

With that being said, there are of course people who still want to have the 2x EXP and come to the server for that specifically. But there are other ways of letting them have it, like perhaps adjusting War Mode to serve their needs without it affecting the entire server, just to mention one idea.

This is not the only issue the server has - for one there's the aforementioned split between WPvP and BG players which gives the server a somewhat schizophrenic identity, making it hard to adress the needs of one side without also inadvertently making it worse for the other. Or the problems PvP servers have always had, like adressing excessive griefing without taking away from the spirit of the server or gaining you more from engaging in WPvP than just doing it for the love of the game. But adressing the 2x EXP issue or at the very least just fixing .xpmode and Slow and Steady would already be a great start.

Alright that's all I wanted to say, thank you for reading everyone.
