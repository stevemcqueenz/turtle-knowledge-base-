# World Buff Removal/Stacking Buffs Comment

## Post 26315 by Riktor — 2022-09-28T02:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26315#p26315 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My suggestion: Have Blessing of Might affect ranged attack power as well as melee attack power.

As a hunter, I'm not really for the WB change. The reason is we don't really gain much from the change where as every other melee class is getting a huge buff.

Stacking buffs/totems would provide hunters access to Blessing of Kings not being overwritten by Strength of Earth totem.

We will never get windfury over warriors/rogues due to how groups pretty much have to be constructed. You can't stack a hunter group because Trueshot Aura doesn't stack and the only reason you stack them in TBC is the 5% damage buff from pets.

This will give hunters a small boost and something that will minorly replace the loss we sustain from losing WBs and we will get some stats back from actually getting blessing of kings to stick.
