# The Tree of Life form is incomplete.

## Post 70298 by Alexanbin — 2023-10-12T06:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70298#p70298 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really appreciate the changes made by the developers to Druids, but there is one issue. In The TBC expansion, Druids were designed as healers specializing in HoT spells, setting them apart from other healing classes. They were given Lifebloom as their main healing ability, replacing Healing Touch. That's why we couldn't cast Healing Touch while in Tree of Life form. However, now we can't use Lifebloom or Healing Touch, and it feels really frustrating. I have to give up the Tree of Life form, even though it looks great. If the developers truly want to reintroduce the Tree of Life form in the Vanilla version, please consider giving Druids back Lifebloom.

Additionally, there is another issue，why hasn't the stacking of HoTs been fixed yet!!!!!!  unhappy_turtle
