poe as well as runescape especially are also mmos.

Runescape also offers an hc mode and an ironman(ssf) mode which can be paired together for a long term challenge.

runescape offers multiplayer content, guilds, leveling, professions and many more similarities to wow so i dont think, additionally to the popular hc mode, this idea is far fetched and should work out nicely within wows ecosystem. difference being that dungeons and raids are soloable in runescape.

you could argue that having a hardcore mode is against the mmo spirit as well since it limite certain interaction within the game.

the game mode has found great success within poe as well as runescape, again with old school rune scape sharing many similarities to vanila/classic wow.

the core idea here is that the gear you obtain is profession based and made by yourself, being self sufficient. there are multiple ways to achieve this but having access to all professions without impacting the market is mandatory so a Trading/market restriction would have to be in place which already is the case with hc. then perhaps quest rewards/mob drops would have to be altered to suit the challenge but that mainly comes down to design choice.
