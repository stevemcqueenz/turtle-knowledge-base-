# What Feral needs.

## Post 121092 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-02-27T07:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121092#p121092 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After trying some beta of a different V+ server there are some glaring issues with the feral druid or druid in general on TWoW, I know most of it is down to the ancient client so we are at a disadvantage at turtle but I think some of it can be done!

 1: Direct form shifting.

In the 'other' server, say you're in Bear Form for example. All you have to do is click once on your Cat Form on the action bar or key bind, and it will switch directly to that form. This feels 10 times smoother, and feels alot better for druid shape shifting fantasy, having to go back to caster form first just feels janky and awkward. This might not be possible for 1.12 client, but please consider this as a priority for the Unreal client.

 2: You can interact with objects and talk to Npcs.
One thing that annoys me in turtle, expecially in Warsong Gulch is that I can't return my teams flag while I'm shapeshifted in any form. This puts you at a huge disadvantage in WSG as a feral because 90% of time you will be in your animal form, which delays your reaction at returning the flag. The same goes for picking up the enemies flag if they die. On the 'other' server you can just pick and return it while shifted.
Also talking to NPCs, while some consider RP flavour not being able to, puts an unnecessary restriction on the class, plus when I talk in /say the speech bubbles appear over my head, and some NPCs in the world speak in a Bear Form anyway.

 3: Weapon procs work in form

Stuff like Crusader and Icy chill work in forms, would this really be that game breaking if we just adjusted the ppm to the speed of the form you are in? Seems to work really nice on the other server and doesn't seem op. Just adds more options to playing a feral druid. They fixed stuff like sharpening stones working here in form, atleast the 2% Crit and consecrated ones. So the RP flavour of not effecting claws seems silly when we have broken that rule anyways.

 4: The tier sets are better statted.

The custom tier sets on the other server seem to have alot better stats for the spec they're meant for.
E.g the feral sets have strength agility, Crit and stamina.
In pve a feral doesn't really need intellect on their gear because the mana pool is sufficient to perform their popping out of form for a quick b res or innervate or quick heal. The overstating of intellect on the turtle stats is just plain wasted.

The set bonuses are also better, on turtle we have something like increases your defense by 25 while Frenzied regeneration is active.... I mean this one is just stupid and basically does nothing.

Id also consider making claw build and shred build equal on the sets, as the new TWoW sets seem to favour claw and not shred. Having both styles supported on the sets is a must.

 5: Rework lackluster talents on TWoW.

All of the talents on the other server seem well thought out... Scrolling through them I didn't see one and think 'that seems useless or underpowered' I'm looking at you 'Feral Adrenaline' and 'Ancient Brutality' these talents need reworking or just condense them.
As we all know dodge is kind of useless for a Bear Druid in vanilla.

I could go on all day comparing the two, but at the end of the day I want to see turtle succeed and I think competition is a good thing and both servers can learn from each other.

I do realise turtle are at a significant disadvantage with the ancient 1.12 client compared to 3.3.5 so hopefully the unreal client they are working on can close the gap.

Thanks for reading.
