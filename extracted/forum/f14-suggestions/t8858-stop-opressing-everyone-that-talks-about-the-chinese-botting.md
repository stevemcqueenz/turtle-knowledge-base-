## Post 57174 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-13T22:48:00Z

Where, exactly, did you see Stopchyna (… okay, but hear me out) accuse the **entire nation of China** of 'botting?  They simply said to stop "kissing China's boots".  To that point, it seems like you green guys *do* go out of your way to squelch or outright erase any and all posts that even *hint* at being, for a lack of better terms, "racist".

As I've pointed out in countless posts, there's no hard evidence that the general player base has access to that the 'bot problem stems exclusively from one location or another.  Well, except for that one guy who extended an olive branch – which you heartily accepted.  That's how this "Chinese server" thing came up, in fact.  Honestly, I was under the impression you guys just handed a copy of the server files over to these folks and said "have at it; go be self-sufficient".  If you still have to do all the work, like translations and stuff, but don't get any of the rewards, I think you guys got a *raw deal*.  But, I digress.

There's a definite uneasiness around the server and its people, these days.  You've got 'bots, you've got players who can't communicate with one another (communication *is* key in some dungeons…), you've got player queues that can range from minutes an **hour** or beyond…  You're working on a Chinese server, new game modes, new content, and trying to moderate the game, the forums, and a *Discord* server all at the same time.  You've got people asking questions you don't have answers to, you've got moderators and staff [lashing out at the user base](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56577#p56577), and most importantly, you've got an overwhelming amount of misinformation, confusion, and just plain frustration to deal with.  Server performance is at an all-time low and auction prices are at an all-time high.  Lowbies can't level up, high-bies (?) are getting ninja looted, and mid-bies (???) are, well, stuck somewhere in the middle.  PvPers are unhappy, RPers are unhappy, PvEers (that's a terrible word) are unhappy.  It's a trying time for everyone involved.  So, what's the solution?  Well, as the Critical Drinker often says…

"*Don't knooow!*"

And that, right there, seems to be the biggest point of contention.  Players *don't know* what's going on and staff hasn't given any indication they know what to do about things, or any concrete evidence that they actually *are* doing anything beyond making mock-up screenshots on test servers and talking about plans.  And, of course, no one has any solutions to anything, whether it be reasonable suggestions or temporary Band-Aids™.  So, I propose the following:

Have a town hall meeting.

Open yourselves up to questions, comments, and criticism via live chat, preferably on a stream of some kind.  Address some of the concerns of the people.  Let them speak their minds, semi-uncensored.  And for the love of Metzen, **do not** waffle if you can avoid it.  If you don't have an answer to a question, **tell the people**.  I know that's not how it works in the business world because a company *simply can't* be held accountable for anything, but you know… if you try it once, you might find that your players will respect you for your honesty.

Remember the old saying: "You can't please everyone".  But, you know what?  There *are* things you could be doing that would improve the situation in some form or other.

Cheers,
J
