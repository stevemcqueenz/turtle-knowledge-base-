# Don't make Silvermoon completely a raid

## Post 138003 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-04T01:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138003#p138003 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know it sounds silly, but I wouldn't want to see Silvermoon as a Raid, at least not entirely.

I would like to see some explorable Overworld within the Overworld, and for there to be an entrance to the final Raid of the patch. I think it's a waste of lore and a zone to make an area like Silvermoon a raid entirely. We've seen the potential of a city like that in retail.

The same thing happens to me with Gilneas. I would like to see some explorable areas in the Overworld, with normal enemies perhaps a bit stronger than the rest of the area.

So it's more of a request: please leave a portion of Silvermoon in the Overworld for exploration or roleplaying.
