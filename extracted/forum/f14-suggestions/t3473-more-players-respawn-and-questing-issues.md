## Post 21079 by Citizenkane — 2022-08-04T16:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21079#p21079 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A huge factor in all of this is that hardcore and non-HC players cannot group up to share quest credit. Although the server population isn't as huge as some others, the abovementioned issue effectively doubles the server pop (at low levels). To make things worse, there are other considerations, many of which are hot topics which I shan't repeat.

What I shall touch on, are my observations as to how this situation came to be. Back when the server pop was low, competition for quest mobs and items was correspondingly low. When the population began to rise (this was even before the class changes), tensions between HC and non-HC players rose. I personally experienced many instances where HC players would farm mobs in much lower level zones, effectively boxing out lower level players. This standoff created a situation where mobs would be tagged the instant they spawned, with no regard as to whether or not they were actually needed by the player who tagged it. As a result, both sides only stood to lose, like in the case of a prisoner's dilemma.

I feel this is the turning point in the server climate. Small altercations built up, and over time, boiled over into the general scene. This is when quest NPC killings, tent blocking, chat spam etc started. The whole PvP vs PvE debate got lumped in because PvE'ers were now at the mercy of PvP'ers, without even being flagged.

The regrettable part in all of this is that many of the issues mentioned prior could have been solved rather easily. For example, the chat spam could have been solved by introducing a 10 second timer between messages (might break LFT tho), and the tent blocking problem could be solved by using a tent model without collision.
