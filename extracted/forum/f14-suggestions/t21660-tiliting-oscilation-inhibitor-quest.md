# Tiliting Oscilation Inhibitor quest

## Post 150054 by Eyeburn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-03T14:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150054#p150054 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's easy enough to buy fused wiring for an alt, but this it's a mistake to put this as a quest objective on such a low level quest.  It's not realistic that a level 24 is going to farm mobs that can drop fused wiring, and as an endgame reagent for raids, it's pricey for a fresh level 24 to buy it off the AH.  Even making a bunch of target dummies to farm wiring is pricey on any fresh character on a fresh account.

In general, all non elite quests should be able to be completed solo and in a reasonable manner, meaning no slush fund required.  This would be a perfectly fine thing to ask at level 35-40, but not at 24.
