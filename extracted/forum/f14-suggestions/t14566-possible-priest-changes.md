# Possible Priest Changes

## Post 99402 by Midoriko — 2024-07-14T07:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99402#p99402 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone! Just an old priest sharing their Mind Vision in no particular order that makes sense. Kind of like when you get sent a vision from any overwhelming power, it may not be clear, but you can try to understand it :)

 *You feel your mind has been dominated, but as you have lost your free will, you are un-able to become angry.*

 1) Priests should be able to use bows:

Tyrande uses a bow. Many priests use bows in the books.  All that would truly be achieved is swapping magical wands out for a physical attack, adding ammo, and setting up for a "Monk" style build using agility.  Night-Elves Elune's Grace returns and burns mana based off of total agility, and with natural 1% dodge.

 2) Priests should be able to use 2h mace, and chain mail armor:

Before you steak me with fire, hear me out.  It would require removing all armor received from the existing buff inner-fire, but keeping the old-school attack power bonus, and keeping the 20 charges.  Altering the disc tree to remove the armor bonus and to add attack power or holy damage that scales with some combination of strength + agility divided by two.  Priests would be able to wear chain mail, leather, and cloth for healing, spirit, and mp5 gear. Widely altering their existing in-game gear options while not changing actual armor-values given to the player too-much.  No sets would need to be altered.  A melee priest is still very close to a monk, even if they do not obtain 2h maces, they still have staves like Friar Tuck, and zero attack speed bonuses.  Much like the hybrid tax paid by all characters, mostly pointing towards enhancement-shaman gear, any priest that would gear out to be melee, would have their casting power diminished by the missing spell-power now being melee.  They would still provide a support-raid-healer style of build, while being able to focus on dispelling and utility.  Shadow priests would be able to become a new style of PvE tank, caster tank.  Like the shaman but perhaps operating as good as a druid in their new chain-mail gear.  Alter the shadow agro reduction talent to increase agro. Alter improved fade to be an AoE taunt.
