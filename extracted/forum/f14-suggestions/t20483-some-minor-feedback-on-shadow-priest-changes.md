# Some minor feedback on Shadow Priest changes.

## Post 141544 by Yamino8888 — 2025-07-26T10:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141544#p141544 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shadow Priests have a very narrow stat focus, which often makes haste and crit gear go to waste. This results in poor gear synergy and frustrating itemization. It’s recommended to add haste and crit scaling to their talents, similar to Affliction Warlocks and Arcane Mages, to improve flexibility and gameplay depth. In addition, Shadow Priests are in desperate need of an AoE ability — this is extremely important. Currently, their performance and experience in multi-target fights are both severely lacking.

## Post 141809 by Yamino8888 — 2025-07-28T03:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141809#p141809 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since Turtle WoW has introduced the Haste stat, every class should be able to benefit from it, rather than excluding certain ones. Both Warlocks and Mages have received new talents that allow them to gain value from Haste, and I believe it's just as necessary for Shadow Priests.

While Shadow Priests offer a lot of utility, they also have inherent drawbacks — such as shorter range and weaker mobility. These are part of the class's identity and are fine. However, the new 40-man raid Karazhan includes many encounters that heavily favor AoE capabilities — such as the crow swarm on the first boss, the dragons on the second boss, the imp swarm on the final boss, and even high shadow resistance (200) on the final boss. Some mobs are even immune to shadow damage altogether. These mechanics severely hinder Shadow Priest performance and have even led some groups to exclude them entirely.
