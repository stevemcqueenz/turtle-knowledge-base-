# NO CROSSFACTION PVP IS A TERRIBLE MISTAKE

## Post 99876 by Splyntrskul — 2024-07-19T10:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99876#p99876 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am not going to continue to play battlegrounds pvp if I and one other ally character come head to head with 6 horde characters.
Battlegrounds are a numbers game more than anything else in this game. without equal teams the fun is taken out of the experience.

I recently rejoined turtle wow after about 16 months of not playing for this exact reason, thinking the devs must have changed this by now, but STILL not!

Developers.
How can you justify the decision to keep battlegrounds the same shitty system vanilla had them where teams were constantly unbalanced, unfair and unfun.
Coming from a team player and an avid PVP player in all games, especially ones involving teams, I find this unacceptable. How can you even successfully farm gear, if EVERY time you get your ass handed to you by horde?

For now I am quitting because is the sole reason I was playing but sadly you still haven't fixed this very simple, elementary issue. Without crossfaction PVP that balances gear and contestant numbers battlegrounds will remain dead.
And its a damn shame too, because CLEARLY the developers don't lack the skill to make these changes as they have done so effectively and efficiently with the PVE aspects of the game already.

I implore of you. Consider these factors. This server would lack nothing if battleground PVP was ACTUALLY a thing people could enjoy here. Teams must always be balanced and I really doubt it is beyond your skill to implement these changes.
