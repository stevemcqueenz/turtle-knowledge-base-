# Revert to the honor system from 20th - 24th

## Post 172724 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-03-27T13:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172724#p172724 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

When the patch first dropped the honor system seemed in a decent place, you could rank up at a decent pace and no one had lost their ranks etc.

When Wednesday's maintenance hit (25th March) suddently everything started falling down.

• People lost their ranks, even Rank14 characters had now been set to Rank 0.
• Most of my alts under 100 Hks, have been set to Rank 0, Not a big deal but annoying.
• Honor gains were much slower and tedious
• People werent even gaining Rank/RP from kills.
• The Level cap was put back in for Certain ranks (A step backward) People enjoyed this new system and thought it was a fun change for lower levels.

Whatever change was made after maintenance I would suggest in reverting it, and going back to the system when the patch first launched.
