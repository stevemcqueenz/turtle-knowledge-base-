Changing the speed of the weapon is a good solution to making it better for Prot, which is likely the only spec that actually uses it in a raid environment, though if you were trying to DPS with it it would also be an improvement. Here's a screenshot of the current Pally TPS calculator (that's pretty widely accepted by the Prot community here on TWoW) you can see what the TPS would look like at different speeds.

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#a/mJX7o13]

The stats supplied are those of someone who has gear from AQ20, ZG, and some Pre BiS options, all of which are extremely accessible to anyone who has CC Exalted rep for the weapon.

Gearset: [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/ ... sp=sharing](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1br6EP3rn9I7WYeBom3Fc0n3spjnM-dZvR83x4Uwq14s/edit?usp=sharing)

You can see that the 1.9s speed is one of the worst possible speeds, this is because it's fast (which means your SoR is doing less D/TPS) and because the 1.9s speed causes you to gain a large delay (1.4s) on your holy strike. You can see in the "HS TPS" column, that even a 1.7s swing speed weapon will deal more D/TPS from Holy Strike, despite doing less actual damage per Holy Strike (due to having less damage per swing); this is due to the fact that 1.7s causes a significantly lower (0.2s) second delay on your holy strike. Using the "JoW TPS" column you can also see why some fast weapons will deal more **TPS** (not DPS) overall because of their increased TPS from JoW procs, this is part of why the 1.5s speed version and the 2.1s speed version have such similar TPS. The Holy Strike TPS from the 2.1s version is significantly higher because though they both have a marginal (0.5s) delay on Holy Strike the 2.1s speed version will do more damage per swing, therefore doing more damage per Holy Strike. However, due to the faster weapon getting more JoW procs simply from swinging more, your ending TPS ends up the same (though your DPS would be higher with a slow weapon).

While it is currently quite good, only a 2.8% difference in TPS (at least at this gear level) from Lok'amir, changing it to a different speed that is a similar TPS but works better with Holy Strike would be a good decision. If it were changed to either 2.3s or 1.6s it would *feel* significantly better to use, while gaining a marginal improvement in threat in either case (though from different sources). Changing it to this speed would make it a 2.5% difference in TPS from Lok'amir.

Worth mentioning that the best swing speed on a 1H weapon for a Prot Paladin would be 2.6s, which has a low delay on Holy Strike and is slow enough that your SoR is doing good D/TPS. I do **not** think it should be made to be 2.6s, as this would make it significantly stronger than the other AQ20 weapons; if it were changed to 2.6s it would be a mere 0.003% difference in TPS from Lok'amir.
