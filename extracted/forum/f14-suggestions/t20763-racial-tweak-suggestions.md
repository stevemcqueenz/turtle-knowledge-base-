# Racial tweak suggestions

## Post 144025 by Krulltak — 2025-08-09T17:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144025#p144025 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First off I think its the right direction that racials have been made less important to the game overall, but they are also fun additions that should add to immersion. Below are some suggestions for small rebalances.

First off cooldown time on active racials should be standardized across the board except for perhaps shadowmeld. For all the rest, make them either 2 minutes or 3 minutes.

Weapon racial rebalancing was the right choice for melee, however for +bow/+gun 3 extra skill does not even add up to 1% hit, effectively it is no racial at all. Since ranged weapons only have to account for hit and not glancing blows, I do not think it would be too strong to make these +5 extra skill.

Buff warstomp and escape artist slightly by making them instant casts. Tauren in particular have one of the weaker sets of racials. I suggest +3 defense be added to their health bonus.

Dwarf treasure finding, effectively useless. Suggestion: +2 or 5% money picked up from humanoid enemies.

For the orc racial, the healing debuff is unnecessarily large, reduce it to 15 seconds in addition to making the cooldown 3 minutes. Likewise hardiness was nerfed too strongly, buff slightly by making it a 20% reduction to stun time.

Berserking was also hit quite hard. Please remove the inexplicable mana cost on it, and either make it a flat 15% boost (making it more useful to classes that aren't getting hit often), or make the duration 15 seconds.

To thematically match their bonus to JC, perhaps give goblins 5% extra stats from rings and necklaces.

Meditation for helves should be boosted to roughly be the mana equivalent to cannibalize as an active racial that restores a resource. Their agility racial is so weak as to effectively not exist. Unfortunately I can't think of an appropriate replacement, but I think it should emphasize their magic use, as rangers are already represented by the +bow skill racial. Perhaps a small boost to stats from enchantments, which would be useful to more classes than just rogues and hunters.

For Will of the Forsaken, instead of immunity for 8 seconds, why not be effective for removing only one instance of fear/charm/sleep before the protection goes away?

Even with recent nerfs, Horde racials still tend to outperform their Alliance counterparts—both in terms of active abilities and passive bonuses. I understand that some of this may be intentional for population balance reasons, as Horde races often have more monstrous appearances that might be less appealing to some players compared to the generally more "attractive" Alliance races.

However, I’m still puzzled by the decision to buff Undead with a 2% increased damage bonus against Humanoid and Undead targets. This makes them extremely strong in both PvP and PvE scenarios—arguably too strong.

On the Alliance side, I think the racials for Humans, Gnomes, and Night Elves are in a good place after recent updates. That said, it might be worth revisiting the racials for High Elves and Dwarves.

For example, the ranged weapon skill bonuses are  weaker than melee weapon skill bonuses, Additionally, the rest of their passive racials are wat weaker or totaly usless.

For High Elves, consider increasing their Agility bonus from 2% to 3% or even 4%, since their base Agility is relatively low to begin with.
For Dwarves, perhaps give them a bonus to Axe or mace weapon skill, or consider a 2-3% bonus to Strength or Stamina to better reflect their lore and physical nature. Especily sense Stoneform geting nerfed with next pach.
