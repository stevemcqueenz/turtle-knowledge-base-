Hunter Traps ... as items.

Rather than being Trinkets that need to be equipped, you could rig them as being more like Engineering Bombs (so something you use from inventory) but follow the established vanilla rules for Hunter Traps and share the same cooldowns with Hunter Trap spells.  The items themselves would be infinitely reusable, since the shared cooldown on Traps is the limiting factor, so a Hunter would only need 1 such item to enable use (no need to buy more than 1).

The real trick would be finding ways to allow the Survivalist talents to modify such additional Traps so as to integrate them as seamlessly as possible.  Of course, ideally you'd want to make such additional Traps as spells that go in the spellbook, but that's a different conversation.

Limit the items to being Hunter Only and call it a day.

- Lightning Trap (Nature damage single target)
- Poison Gas Trap (Nature damage AoE)
- Paint Splatter Trap (think Faerie Fire prevent Stealth debuff effect in an AoE, does not draw Threat to the Hunter, no damage)
- "Death" Trap (Feign Death AoE)
After being set, only a Hunter PC can trigger the trap (by stepping onto it, does not need to be the caster!). When activated, the Trap will cast Feign Death on all PCs in the triggering Hunter's party within a radius of 10 feet of the "Death" Trap. Only the Improved Feign Death talent in Survivalist will improve the chances that this effect will not be resisted by hostile foes.  Great for "wiping" the party without actually needing to wipe the party!
