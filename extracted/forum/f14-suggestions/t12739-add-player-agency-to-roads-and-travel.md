# Add player agency to roads and travel

## Post 84876 by Zigfryd81 — 2024-01-16T09:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84876#p84876 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I wish roads and paths would increases running & mounted speed boosts. This would be incredibly useful for lower level characters that have no mounts and would increase player agency by actively strategising which road to follow or run through wilderness, or traverse marshes.

Main idea:
- While running or mounted, major roads increase speed by 20%.

Experimental ideas that need polishing:

- Raptors increase traversing rough, marshes, bogged down areas with a 10% speed boost.

- Tigers increase speed in hilly and mountain areas by 10%.

- Horses (including skeletal undead horses) get additional 10% increases while using the roads.

- Any other mount types, like wolves and kodos could use an universal speed buff of 5% to outdoors areas or something. Also other mount types - pls post your ideas as I can't think of any at the moment.

## Post 84907 by Ishilu (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-01-16T14:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84907#p84907 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The main idea looks interesting. Not saying necessary, but definitely interesting.

I'd add some limitations, though, like "works only when you've been out of combat for x seconds", definitely should not stack with other speed increases maybe should be disabled up to level 20 or so.

 20 is the magic number for me because some classes get some kind of traveling buff there and some don't. Just give low level priests and warriors some love here. Of course, everybody can just get the turtle mount at this level.

About the experimental stuff: why not consider the climate of a zone and why only buffs and no debuffs? E.g. a raptor could work normally in "temperate zones" (Elwynn, Ashenvale Silverpine etc), get a buff in tropical areas (STV, Un Goro) and get a debuff in cold zones (Winterspring, Alterac Mountains), while things could be just the other way round for a furry mount. However mount buffs could have a severe impact on (world-) pvp, so I'm not a huge fan of pursuing this further.
