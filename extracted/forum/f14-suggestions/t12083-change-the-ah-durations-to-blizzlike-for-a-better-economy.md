## Post 80123 by Twospooky — 2023-12-06T23:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80123#p80123 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
I have a suggestion for the game which I believe should be implemented.

The duration of auctions on the AH should be blizzlike. 2hour, 8 hour, 24 hour.

Having longer auction durations might sound good on paper: less fees, convinience.
In reality is makes the AH stagnant. There is no window for price resets. I look at the same set of auctions for days, waiting for them to end and then they dont and then another player undercuts and it sucks. They're taking huge chunks out of their margins for quick cash, and the auctions stay up so long they've farmed more stacks which makes it worse.
Get the trash off the AH. It's slowing the economy.

I have been manually cancelling and re-posting every 2 hours on the items which have 1copper undercuts.  They are good competitive items and the AH is running out of those.
If they're logging off for 2 days then stuff their bags full of their items, we want the gold to be in the game getting played with.

Think of the auction refresh rate like FPS, it really sucks when it's not fast and there's a throttle on this one.

I agree that the original justification (having a 2-3 digit population) is no longer relevant, but I still appreciate the longer durations. A 3-day cycle is just so much more convenient than having to relist constantly.

Stagnant is good for the consumer, prices should be somewhat stable with long-term trends. And I say that as a frequent seller as well. Sure, it's fun to gouge prices, but it's not really good for anyone but yourself. Also there are so many people playing the AH and listing stuff that we're talking about very small windows, the price usually approaches the long-term mean very quickly even if a reset happens. People have addons or use that website, they know what the price should be and move there very quickly.

If you want to gamble with resets you can focus on some of the rare items. But for common stuff I think the longer durations have a solid benefit for everyone but hardcore price gougers. If you think profit margins are too low, you should simply not farm/craft those items. It's the market correcting itself via supply and demand though price discovery.
