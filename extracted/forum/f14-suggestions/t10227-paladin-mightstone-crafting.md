# Paladin Mightstone crafting

## Post 66759 by Danibalim — 2023-09-25T02:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66759#p66759 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This idea is that paladins will recieve a crafting tab akin to rogue posion tab.
Where and when you get this, is from the lackluster level 50 paladin chain, Instead of reciving the "mightstone" from the quest, you aquire the skill to craft mightstones, as the quest giver just did it infront of you and showed you how. This fits with the lore, especially considering how the ashbringer was created from a evil artifact which was "healed" or made pure, and then intigrated into a weapon, this is also what you do in the quest to craft the mightstone itself, of course you dont get a sick undead melting weapon from it.

So how does this work? Pretty simple, the paladin needs to gather materials and or artifacts from great evil forces (dungeon/raid/world bosses) to craft their mightstones, which they then can one-time apply to their weapon, if you get a new weapon. You will need to gather the materials again. Anyhow, what effect these stones should have, would be based on the powerful being you gathered the materials/artifacts from. Atleast thats my idea of it.

Lets say the paladin ventures into stratholme and defeats balnazzar. Balnazzar then drops a corrupted artifact. you then need to aquire the materials required to cleanse the object. When done, you  get a "mightstone" which you can apply to your weapon, giving it extra effects. Now, you cannot have windfury and weapon applied consume at the same time, so maybe it would be possible to find a way to make these work together, possibly the mightstone could work as an enchant? But competing with crusader is gonna be tough.

Give me your thoughts. Could this be done better, or is it just a bad idea?
