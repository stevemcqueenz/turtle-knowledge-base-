# Eyes of Beast and Three directions

## Post 147341 by Ulukay (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-20T18:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147341#p147341 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Can anyone explain why the developers left the talent [Eyes of Beast] unchanged?

They introduced new talents that increase the pet's attack power by a percentage of the character's ranged attack. However, when using this talent/ability, the benefit from ranged attack no longer makes sense, as the character is inactive during this time. The talent itself adds 30% DPS, but if the character is active, it would provide 2-3 times more DPS.

I don't understand why they tied pet enhancement to ranged attacks. It's like they're forcing me to be a ranged attacker if I want to enhance my pet.
It would be better if they allowed pet enhancement through magic. This would also help heal and buff the pet. This would lead to three paths for the character: magic power (beastmaster), ranged power (ranged attacker), and melee power (melee attacker).
