# Discipline Priest Global Cooldown on Instants

## Post 161092 by Vincensososo — 2025-11-30T11:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161092#p161092 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Greetings everyone,

I’ve been playing Discipline Smite Priest for quite a while now, and overall I really enjoy the talent tree – it fits extremely well into the gameplay. However, there is one specific mechanic that feels like a major flaw in practice:

 **The issue with Searing Light**

For a Smite Priest, Searing Light (3/3 → 100% chance on crit with Holy spells → next Smite is instant + 50% mana reduction) is one of the most important talents.
But the value of the instant-cast proc is much lower than it looks on paper.

- My Smite cast time is ~1.9 seconds (low Haste).

- The global cooldown is 1.5 seconds.

This means that even if Searing Light gives me an instant Smite, I’m still forced to wait 1.5 seconds doing nothing afterward. In a long fight, the “instant” cast is effectively a 1.5-second cast, not a burst accelerator.

On top of that, Searing Light cannot proc again for 3 seconds after it triggers.
So even if I cast Holy Fire during an active proc, and HF crits again, the proc is just overwritten instead of generating a new instant Smite. There is no way to chain procs or accelerate your rotation.

 **Result:**

The proc mostly saves mana.
It barely increases DPS.

 **Suggestion / Question**

Would it be possible to adjust this mechanic in one of the following ways?

- 1. Allow Searing Light to chain proc (i.e., allow a new proc even if the last one was triggered recently).
→ This would make crit-based gameplay meaningful and rewarding.

- 2. Reduce or remove the global cooldown after casting the instant Smite.
→ This would make the instant really feel instant and provide an actual DPS increase.

 **Comparison: Warlock Nightfall**

Nightfall gives an instant Shadow Bolt (normally ~3 sec cast).
Even though the Bolt is instant, the Warlock can cast again after only the 1.5s GCD → effectively ~50% faster throughput while the proc is active.

For Priest, the throughput improvement is only ~26%.
This is a huge difference, making Searing Light feel underwhelming despite being a key talent.

 **So my question is:**
Would allowing chained procs or reducing the GCD after the instant Smite be feasible from a design perspective?

Thanks for reading – I’d love to hear thoughts from other Smite Priests or devs.

I’ve only been playing Turtle WoW for about three months, so I only know the modern talent tree. My complaints are mostly based on the PvE experience. On Ambershire, the tradeoffs between Hit, Crit, and Spell Power are extremely noticeable right now. When you try to build a balanced set, Searing Light often ends up feeling less like a real DPS gain and more like a mana-saving effect with a downside. And the “GCD stunlock” you described is exactly how it feels — the Smite is instant, but effectively you still stand there doing nothing for 1.5 seconds while everyone else keeps casting or spamming abilities.

In PvP I don’t see a huge issue with the way it currently works, mainly because Smite Priest plays around those 50-second setups anyway. Your burst windows determine so much of the outcome in 1v1 that even if Searing Light could chain proc, it probably wouldn’t change much in practice.

But as I already mentioned, I’ve only been playing the game for about three months, so I’m definitely not the most experienced player. This was simply a flaw I noticed based on the way I play the spec, both in PvE and PvP.
