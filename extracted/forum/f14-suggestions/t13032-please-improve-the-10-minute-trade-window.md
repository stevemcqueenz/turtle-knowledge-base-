# Please improve the 10-minute trade window!

## Post 87302 by Sarenrae — 2024-02-08T16:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87302#p87302 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While the current trading system works fantastically on items it functions on, there's a few raid-tier items which this does not yet expand to...and I think they really should.

To start off: tier tokens are untradable. I don't know why they should be untradable when the other items on the boss can be. They lead to the same type of loot, after all. It really sucks to have to apologize to someone because of a mis-loot that cannot be rectified.

I believe this should also extend to world bosses, as well. Their items are of raid-tier strength, with bosses intended to be downed by a raid team on rare occasion. In the event of a mis-loot, it is currently impossible to actually rectify properly, because there is no trade window for these raid boss items.

Thank you for your time, developers!   satisfied_turtle_head
