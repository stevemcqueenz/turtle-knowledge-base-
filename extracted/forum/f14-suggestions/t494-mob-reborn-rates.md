## Post 2580 by Hipi — 2019-04-18T07:54:00Z

Hi,

It seems that the time after which the enemies are born after the killing has become really fast. I only play alone with all types of characters. One of the toons I play is the protection warrior. A few weeks ago something changed dramatically, and nowadays the enemies are re-born so fast that I can't kill a new enemy before my already earlier killed enemy is born again. This makes it impossible for some areas to play at the right level, because I cannot move towards my final goal. I have to kill the same 3-4 enemies over and over again. This destroys the feeling of realism and the game of me. The same effect can be seen when I play in my holy priest, or even in the rogue, but of course they are a bit faster to kill, so the problem is not the same.

Since this server is called "turtle wow", I found this server a bit more peaceful and also suitable for slower gamer, those who want to really enjoy the IRG (RP) with time and explore everything (like me). Also, there are not so many players on the server that enemies need to re-born quickly. There are enough enemies in the game to complete the quests. During the three months I've been playing on this server, I have not seen another player to perform the same quest at the same time with me. The number of enemies to complete quests is appropriate without their rapid re-born rate.

My solution is:
 1. Change the rate of re-born to the original 15-30 minutes (closed cave or a tight path might be even slower, open fields and plains may be faster), just like in the early days of the game.
 2. Make the following change that was in use at least in the Outland times (I no longer remembered whether this was like this also in the original game): If the enemy's body is NOT looted, the enemy will be born again after a longer period of time, but if the body is looted, then the time for re-born may be faster.

I also have the feeling that the miss values ​​have somehow changed. While I kill enemies of much lower levels, I miss many times, it is not fatal, but a bit annoying because I lose the rage and result is that the killing of the enemy takes even more time and the enemies that I have already killed have time to re-born again.

The last announcement: Silver elites are no longer very rare, it does not feel right, rare means rarely occurring, not common but one that is difficult to kill. Silver "rare" is far too easy to find today. Some of them are even located in a place that really effectively prevents the given quest from being executed when the Toon is in the correct level. For a character, a difficult or impossible rare enemy is standing next to a quest object. A good example of this is the silver elite in the SV over a bridge leading to the fountain. There is no voluntary way to get to the fountain, the only possible way to get the job done on the right level is to let the enemy kill your character and then be born again behind the enemy. This does not seem very realistic.

Best regards:
Hipi, Nupukka, Pyssynies, Varas, Lasaretti, Ruutiukko and few rarely played Hordie toons.

Tested a bit more:

Looting or non looting the bodies does not effect the resp counter.

Example Whiterbark Hideskinner, Hinterlands. Corpse non looted or even looked what dropped, corpse de-spawn after 5 minutes from kill and new mob resps after 6 minutes of the kill.

Same mob killed and looted, looted corpse de-spawn after 2 minutes, but new mob resps according original timer at same time after 6 minutes of original kill.

I strongly recommend that you set these counters starting after each others. Desp counter starts from looting, few minutes, mentioned 2 is enough, then mob resps according timer, but if corpse is not looted, corpse stays a bit longer (I recommend 15 minutes) and resp counter will start only after corpse is de-spawned. In my books, this change has no effect on Lore, but it will make possible to play solo with Prot warrior and Holy Priest.

Voila, everyone is happy, even we slower players :)
