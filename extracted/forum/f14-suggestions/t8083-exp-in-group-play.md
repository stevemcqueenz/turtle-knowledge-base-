# Exp in Group Play

## Post 51970 by Bebbop — 2023-06-30T23:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51970#p51970 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hear me out,

Wow is an MMORPG where playing with others and friends is a big part of the game. However, throughout Wow's history group play has been punished with slower leveling speeds (typically) especially outside of dungeons.

I think currently exp for group players is shared evenly up to a total of 100%?  For example if there's 2 players each get 50% EXP and if there's 4 players each person gets 25% of the exp.

So my suggestion is that instead of each person getting 50% exp in a 2-person group. They get 10% more  exp (so like 60% each player) to encourage more grouping, group questing and group play.  This could be adjusted depending on group size. I don't think it would be game breaking, but it would more a fun experience so that people who want to group together aren't punished significantly. Cause we all know that leveling by yourself is almost always much faster.

Thanks for listening,
 -Bebbop

Experience is fine as it is. You already level way too fast in this game to be able to enjoy all the of-level content available without turning XP off yourself for, say, dungeon runs. This game and this server specifically is not about how fast you level up. It's about living in the moment.

In the current state of WoW, experience (EXP) gained from defeating enemies and completing quests is shared among group members. This change was implemented to encourage and reward group play, ensuring that players progress at a similar pace regardless of whether they choose to adventure alone or with friends.
