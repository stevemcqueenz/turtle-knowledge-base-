# Possible buffs for feral

## Post 46442 by Critter — 2023-05-18T19:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46442#p46442 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all,
Today I offer some suggestions to give feral gameplay that extra oomph to feel like its still not a second-rate spec in PvE. This may not feel like the time additional buffs are needed, but when that time comes, please comeback and consider any one or all of these. Feel free to adjust the values/cooldowns/levels to make their implementation not obscene in PvP scenarios.

Omen of clarity added effect: clearcasting allows Rejuvenation and Insect swarm to be used in Bear form, Dire bear form and Cat form. Consumes clearcasting effect if used.

Justification: Warriors are hardier tanks as they stand. Casting a random proc HoT and DoT with additional 2% enemy miss chance should help smooth out the faster drop in health bar that bear tanks currently receive. This would be also unique gameplay so bear tanks can have their own tanking identity. Not asking for any kind of bonus healing or damage scaling, just the base values and secondary utility.

Feline swiftness > Feral swiftness increased chance to dodge by 2%/4% and increased movement speed 4%/8% in Bear Form, Dire Bear Form and Cat Form. Increased movement speed in cat form by 15%/30% while outdoors.

Justification: Bears in the real world can run 25-35 mph. Tanking would feel much better with that extra speed.
The 2%/4% dodge is more needed by tanking bears than in cat form.

Predatory strikes added effect: Increases attack rating by 2/4/6

No other way to increase weapon skill rating in classic WoW as a feral druid as far as I know. It would be a nice buff, but not the most needed on the list.

Additional 21 point talent: Blood Sport
Delivers a devastating bite to the target from an advantaged position dealing 120% normal damage and healing you for 50% of Damage dealt. Can only be used after dodging. 9-second cooldown. Requires Bear form, Dire Bear, or cat form. Cost 10 rage or 20 energy.

Justification: Feral tanking feels like it could use one more active button and conditional seems like the reasonable implementation. The healing effect is again to smooth out the rate of health bar drop when compared to warriors.

Additional 31 point talent: Mindful Beast (active)
When activated, allows the druid to cast barkskin, innervate, rebirth, abolish poison, or remove curse while in bear form, dire bear form, and cat form. 1 charge. 20 second cooldown.

Justification: The vast majority of perks to bringing a druid into a group or raid is lost when the druid is the main tank. Lets give access to those utility spells with some constraints such as a 20 second cooldown so they are not abusable in PvP. The talent tree placement discourages flag running druids that are hard to kill from picking this talent because they lose access to nature's swiftness.

Most of these changes should make feral feel more distinct and uniquely valuable instead of playing like a step-behind warrior or rogue. Please feel free to improve upon anything. If you do not like a proposed change, please expound for us why feral druids don't need/deserve that particular change. Thanks for reading!

With regards to bear survivability, adding the ability to use barkskin and insect swarm in bearform to a deep-feral talent would be a boost to bear survivability, and while having a significant rage cost (say 50 rage or more, so you have to tank some damage or do damage yourself for a while so you can't just shift directly into bear and fire off barkskin) would also mean pvp resto druids who wanted the ability would be required to spec deep into the feral tree. The effect could be added to LOTP for example. The effect could simply be that in addition to it's normal effect, the LOTP talent now allows the use of barkskin and insect swarm in bearform, possibly at significant costs of rage.

A third option could be to give bearform parry ability, also from a deeper feral talent.
I'm thinking something like heart of the wild(new tooltip would read): Increases your intellect by 4%. In addition, while in Bear or Dire Bear Form you gain the ability to parry attacks, parry is increased by 1%, stamina by 4%, and while in cat form your strength is increased by 4% pr point.

That way taking 5/5 HOTW would give bearform the ability to parry, and a base parry of 5%.

This would beneficially combine with various leather, ring and trinket items that have +parry%. Here I'm thinking of, for example, the infamous [Thick Qirajihide Belt] from AQ40 which is the best bearform tank belt, but has +1% parry which druids can't use. Rogues usually pass on the belt anyway since it is really only of mediocre pve value for them.

A trinket like counterattack lodestone, which gives 22 attackpower and +1% parry, or the tanking trinket from the onyxia head quest (which gives fire resist, defense, +1% parry, and health regen), now suddenly becomes a viable option for feral tanking.

other options: Feral itemization was always poor in classic. In particular druids were restricted in their choice for helm and leg ZG enchants. I say give druids access to the rogue ZG head and leg enchants, which give dodge and attackpower: This would simultaneously benefit bear and cat.

Another infamously poor item is the warrior Royal Seal of Eldre Thalas, which gives 200 armor and 10 fire resist. Basically close to useless to warriors who preferred defense trinkets over armor. That trinket would be *excellent* for bear tanking.

Then there's the terrible [wristguards of stability] from molten core, with 24 strength and 9 stamina. Far from ideal stats for both cat and bear. I suggest just making it a bear item. Subtract some of that massive strength and put the itemlevel points into more armor, a bit more stamina, and some agility. It's now a very good bear item through and through.
