# The Diplomat Glyph

## Post 97696 by Nals — 2024-06-24T00:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97696#p97696 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Idea I had after joking around with some friends and slowly making my way back through the game.

Probably impossible frankly. It’d need a lot more work than the other glyphs. But could be a neat idea?

Short version. Glyph Chooseable at Level 1 Only. Has its own NPC similar to the Hardcore NPC. Has its own set of warnings that you won’t be able to get GM help similar to the Hardcore NPC.

Glyph sets you as Neutral/Friendly with all Alliance and Horde factions. So you can quest on both sides.

However it’ll also need to come with severe drawbacks to make it an actual challenge/Glyph run.

First off, you are still flagged in other faction cities/contested quest hubs. Could make it so either you can’t fight back, or getting a Honor/Dishonorable kill breaks your Diplomat run.

Can’t queue up for BGs. At all.

Quest/Kill exp is extremely reduced, forcing you to complete basically every quest across both factions to level. You have to constantly put yourself in danger to keep up with leveling.

Guards and City NPCs won’t attack you, but players fully can. So questing in say Stormwind is possible for an Orc. But you’ll need to actually socialize and make friends and take back paths to get from quest NPC to quest NPC. You’ll need to be a Diplomat.

Reward for hitting 60 is the Title the Diplomat ( or something cooler, I dunno ), and maybe a mount?

Lore wise it’s built off what we already see in the Lore. The Battle of Hyjal ended the conflict between Aliance and Horde for a lot of people. Yes you’ve still got the diehards, but you’ve also got a lot of folks, especially younger Orcs who don’t care about continuing this conflict and want to see the world.

However the world may not be as welcoming to their attempts, and they’ll have to walk a fine line.

Difficulties off the top of my head. Whatever unlocks quests for both factions may cause issues with Neutral factions like Goblins. Some NPCs do call out race and obviously that’ll likely bug out as an enemy faction race. Could lead to a lot of griefing of people trying for their Diplomat title, or inadvertent city PvP. Some folks might not be happy seeing Horde in Stormwind or Alliance in Orgrimmar.

Thoughts? Suggestions? Outright saying it’s not possible?
