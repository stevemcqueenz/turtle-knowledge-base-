# New Dungeon: The Tower of Ilgalar

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134953#p134953 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Tower of Ilgalar (Levels 25–30)**

 *A 5-player, Classic WoW–style dungeon set in Redridge Mountains. Ascend the ruined tower to face warlocks, orcs, gnolls, and the rogue sorcerer Morganth.*

 **The Tower of Ilgalar** is a crumbling mage tower in the **Redridge Mountains** that has fallen under the control of the rogue sorcerer **Morganth**. Once exiled from Stormwind for dabbling in shadow magic, Morganth has now allied himself with renegades from beyond Redridge – including *Blackrock orc* warlocks and *Dark Iron dwarf* spellbinders – to build a base of operations for spreading chaos across Lakeshire. Under Morganth’s command, a small army of *Shadowhide gnolls* led by the vicious Snagglespear has been raiding local caravans and hoarding stolen treasure. Meanwhile, Blackrock summoners perform fel rituals in the tower’s halls, and Dark Iron mages experiment with volatile arcane powers. All the while, Morganth draws upon a fire elemental core stolen from Blackrock Mountain to amplify his fiery magic, making him a threat far greater than the lone warlock he once was.

This dungeon thematically links the local perils of Redridge to the greater evils lurking in Blackrock Mountain. The presence of Blackrock orcs and Dark Iron dwarves shows the influence of the wider conflict in the Burning Steppes (Blackrock Spire and Blackrock Depths) reaching into Redridge. In fact, the Blackrock clan here are agents of the Black Dragonflight, having been subjugated under the black dragon Nefarian’s rule. Throughout the tower, players will find hints of draconic influence – dragon bones and skulls adorn the interior walls– suggesting that Morganth’s newfound power and allies were not acquired by chance. Players must ascend from the base of the tower to its summit, battling through this collage of foes and ultimately face Morganth himself before he can unleash his fiery chaos upon Lakeshire.

 **Overview**
 **Recommended Level:** 25–30
 **Location:** Tower interior in Redridge Mountains
 **Format:** Linear four-floor vertical climb + 1 optional mini-boss
 **Theme:** Dark Iron spellbinders, Blackrock orc warlocks, Shadowhide gnolls, fire & fel corruption, Black Dragonflight influence

 **Dungeon Layout & Atmosphere**
Entering the crumbling mage tower, players first fight through gnolls, then Dark Iron casters, then Blackrock summoners, culminating in Morganth’s sanctum. Rising floors transition from torch-lit shadow to fel-green halls to blazing fire-charged observatory. Dragon bones and skulls throughout hint at draconic influence.

 **Boss Encounters**
 **1) Chieftain Snagglespear (Ground Floor)**
 - Shadowhide gnoll warlord with cleave, enrage at 50%, net toss, and reinforcements mechanic.
 - **Loot:** Shadowhide Boots (Leather), +Agility, +Stamina, +5% Movement Speed.

 **2) Gor’ashan the Spellbinder (Second Floor)**
 - Dark Iron caster channeling a stolen Fire Elemental Core. Uses Blast Wave (AoE knockback), Arcane Bolt, Polymorph, and overload phase with lava eruptions.
 - **Loot:** Darkforge Ember Staff (Dark Iron 2H caster staff).

 **Mini-Boss – Unbound Ember (Mid stairway)**
 - Lesser fire elemental that radiates AoE and casts Firebolt. Death causes an explosion.
 - **Drops:** Elemental Fire reagent and XP.

 **3) Razorbash the Summoner (Third Floor)**
 - Blackrock orc warlock with summoned Fel Infernal. Two-phase fight: phase one involves kiting and killing the Infernal, phase two is against the warlock with Shadow Bolt Volley, Curse of Tongues, and final wave of lesser Infernals.
 - **Loot:** Razorbash’s Felshroud (Cloak, +Int, +Spirit, +10 Shadow Resist)
 - **Quest Item:** Encoded Dragonflight Letter (leads into higher-level dragon story)

 **4) Morganth – Corrupted Sorcerer (Top Floor Final Boss)**
 - Combines shadow and fire magic: Shadowflame Bolt, Flame Wreath (fire rings), Summon Fire Elementals (at 75% and 40%), Dragon’s Breath cone, and an Inferno Core enrage at 20% (multi-target, rising environmental fire damage).
 - **Loot:**
 - *Embercore Talisman* (trinket, +Fire Resist, absorb fire damage)
 - *Pendant of Shadow* (quest/maybe wearable neckpiece)

 **Trash & Mob Types**
 - Floor 1: Shadowhide Gnolls (Brutes, Trappers, Mystics)
 - Floor 2: Dark Iron Arcanists + Arcane Constructs
 - Floor 3: Blackrock Summoners + Fel Guards + Infernal Servitors
 - Ambiance: Cracked runic walls, flickering fel-green lights, scorched floors, draconic bones

 **Sample Quests**
 **Quest 1: Morganth** (Alliance, lvl 27) – Given by Theocritus in Azora Tower. Objective: Defeat Morganth, return Pendant of Shadow. Ties into existing Classic questline.
 **Quest 2: Shadowhide Schemes** (Alliance, lvl 25) – Given by Bailiff Conacher in Lakeshire. Recover stolen caravan chest from Snagglespear.
 **Quest 3: Draconic Orders** (Alliance, lvl 30 elite) – Looted Encoded Dragonflight Letter from Razorbash. Turn in at Magistrate Solomon. Foreshadows Black Dragonflight.

 **Notable Loot**
 - **Darkforge Ember Staff** – 2‑Hand Staff; +Intellect, +Stamina, +Fire Spell Power
 - **Shadowhide Boots** – Leather; +Agility, +Stamina, +5% Movement Speed
 - **Razorbash’s Felshroud** – Cloak; +Intellect, +Spirit, +Shadow Resistance
 - **Embercore Talisman** – Trinket; +15 Fire Resistance, Use: absorb fire damage
 - **Pendant of Shadow** – Neck; quest item for Theocritus

Dungeon also drops Dark Iron staves, gnoll agility gear, and general gear appropriate for levels 25–30.

 ---

Together, the Tower of Ilgalar dungeon and its quests provide a rich, lore-infused experience for players in mid-level Azeroth. It connects the humble Redridge questing zone to the epic narratives of Blackrock Mountain and the Black Dragonflight. As adventurers clear each floor, they witness the alliances between disparate enemy factions and uncover clues of bigger threats at play. By the end, players will have vanquished Morganth – removing a regional menace – and also stepped onto the trail of the black dragons’ influence in the Eastern Kingdoms. It’s a classic-style 5-player dungeon that not only offers exciting boss fights and loot for levels 25–30, but also enriches the WoW storyline by tying into existing lore and questlines.

 **Prepare to ascend the Tower of Ilgalar, brave heroes, and confront the sorcerer Morganth before the flames of his ambition consume Redridge and beyond!**
