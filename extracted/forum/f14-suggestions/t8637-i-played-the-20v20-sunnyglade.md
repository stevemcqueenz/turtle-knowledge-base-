# I played the 20v20 Sunnyglade.

## Post 55645 by Jammyxx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-03T12:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55645#p55645 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I've joined the new SGV now it's 20v20, after a few weeks of fixes from the Devs.

Feedback as follows

 20v20 is not good, the BG hardly pops now even on its bonus days.

Suggesting 12v12.

 1: The problem is that middle is too easy to defend, there is one entrance to the fort, which is a choke point.

I'd suggest expanding the fort, make it the size of WSG Alliance or Horde base, where the flag is on the roof, but make two entrances to get up there, right now that puny fort looks pathetic.
Maybe use the Tiragarde keep layout and size as the Fort in the middle?

Adding the leaf power up was a good idea... I'd like to see more of these if the base becomes bigger, maybe a berserking buff or speed boots too.

 2: Make the Horde and alliance camp look a little better.
Right now it just looks lacklustre and the bosses don't really stand out, maybe make a big tent where the boss stands in, and the NPC's guard outside.

 3: Make the towers useful.... Atm they don't do enough, few passive sparks here and there, what I'd suggest is a 10% haste buff from each capped tower.

 4: The Trinkets are abit too OP... Great idea to include these but I think some of them need tonign down, like the charging one.

 5: The NPC's do add a touch of flavour but they are still a nuisance and I think something more could be done with them. Maybe a way to make them follow you after killing the grunt/footman they are fighting or sending that back to your boss once you've helped them, for more cannon fodder.

I think  20v20 and middle are the problem atm, it's just too hard to take back, so like I've mentioned the scale needs increasing, adding more entrances and making it not look pathetic.

Cheers
