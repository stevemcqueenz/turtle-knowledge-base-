One more problem about pvp quests like "kill 10 players in zone y" is that this zone could be totally empty and you will never be able to complete them. It is like critical mass. If people know that Tel'Abim is low population server, then people won't even pick that quest. Why bother if there will be no enemies to complete it?

Other situation: there might be only 1 player of other faction there and it will look like you'll need to kill that 1 player 10 times to complete the quest.
Killing quest should feature small amount of player kills. 1 or 2.

It should be possible to complete "pvp" quest without presence of enemy faction at all.
But in this case, this quest should require a lot of time. If you will spend 60 minutes doung stuff in one place, then chances to meet other player is higher than if you spend 10 minutes there.

This pvp quest should also be loud on start. System message like "battleground start" are very important to attract more players who sit on their alt or in other location doing own business.

And last, but most important. This pvp quest should not be available in "grey" state. Currently, quest grant xp and reward up to 25 levels above quest level. This level difference is HUGE.
People will complete this quest being on top end of its bracket. Like if quest is yellow for 20 level, then people will level to 44 and only then come to complete PvP one. Because people will use every advantage. And advantage by level difference is easiest one.
