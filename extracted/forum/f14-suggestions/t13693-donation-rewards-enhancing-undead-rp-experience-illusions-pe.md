These draw inspiration from the existing **Skeletal Warrior/Scourge** illusion. Similar to the Skeletal Warrior, **these models offer a small color variation palette**, and the core design provides a significant visual distinction, enhancing the variety of character appearances available to Undead players.

It's acknowledged that some undead models may lack dedicated jump or swim animations. However, as demonstrated with the successful implementation of the Skeletal Warrior illusion, I think it wouldn't be an argument against it here either.

Furthermore, all proposed models **possess the capability to hold weaponry** and exhibit appropriate animations for a wide range of in-game activities, as evidenced by characters like Devlin Agamand and Karrel Grayves/Samuel Fipps from Deathkneel.
The introduction of these illusions would undoubtedly bolster roleplay opportunities for players delving into both "Scourge" and "Independent Undead" narratives. This proposal has garnered support within my guild, and several members, myself included, would be enthusiastic about contributing financially to support adding them.

If privately I were to consider the scale of "importance" of these illusions, I think Skeletal Mage is the most necessary thing here. Skeletal Warrior fits the use of all melee characters, while not counting, happily, the already existing Ghost, Shade and Banshee, what is missing here is a magical counterpart for our skellington.
And I'm more than sure Skeletal Mage is the reasonable answer there.

 **Bonus suggestions, the possibility of which I doubt from the technical side, but I think that including them in this post as part of the completion of this undead-focused list is a good idea.*

- Skeleton Skin for Undead
I'm aware that Azeroth at War server is adding Skellingtons as an alternative to Undead race when creating character. For that, it seems their devs somehow have cracked down on the technical limitation of skeletons to be able to carry equipment on them. I think this is a very good idea on their part, and if technically possible, could be a cool addition for Turtle to consider, at least within Donation Skins. Personally, I wouldn't consider "Noggenfogger Elixir" as a good enough option for it, as it's locked under certain level and using it constantly in long-term is a costly business.
Being the skeleton is to be, not sometimes to be.
