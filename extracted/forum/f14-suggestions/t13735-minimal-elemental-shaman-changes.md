# Minimal elemental shaman changes

## Post 93440 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-24T22:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93440#p93440 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I apologize for spamming but had an interesting thought experiment.
Wanted to make elemental shaman PVE raid-viable with as few changes as possible. I don't think that the result is ideal but I think it makes elemental shaman mosty viable for raiding with only minor changes.

 1. Healing grace -> Tidal grace
Reduces the threat generated by your [healing] spells by 15%.
Removed the word 'healing'. Helps with threat. Still inferior to the ones f.ex. mages have but it's something.
 2. Lightning mastery ->
Reduces the cast time of your lightning bolt and chain lightning spells by 1 sec (and reduces the chance that your opponents resist these spells by 10%).
Elemental relies on nature damage, it helps a lot.

These changes are minimal but would answer 2 of the 4 big ele shaman problems: threat (somewhat) and nat resistence.
Mana problems and no specific utility compared to better shaman builds are still an issue but I wanted to change as few things here as possible.
