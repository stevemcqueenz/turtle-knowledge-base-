# Dragonmaw Retreat Feedback

## Post 156262 by Elean — 2025-10-20T07:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156262#p156262 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Having ran the dungeon several times as tank, healer & dps, I have some feedback:

 **1) LFG BUG!**

There's currently an issue that if someone DCs or leaves, and you use the LFG tool to find a replacement, after the new person joins everyone inside Dragonmaw Retreat will be prompted with a 'you're not a part of this instance, hearth in 1 minute'.

This happens ONLY in Dragonmaw Retreat as far as I have experienced, and considering how long the dungeon is / how far the run is for Horde Players, this should be fixed asap.

 **2) Orc Trash**

 **2.1)** The amount of Orcs is unreasonable from both lore and gameplay standpoint. This is supposed to be the lasts stand of A SINGLE CLAN OF ORCS from a war they had lost, why's there more Dragonmaw Orcs in the dungeon than Dwarves or Thrall's Horde Orcs in the rest of the Open World???

 **2.2)** While I don't mind mechanics, every other pull requiring corner-pulling casters gets exhausting by the end due to just how long the dungeon is, and how high their HP / Damage is relative to an average (non-twinked out) leveling player's output / survivability.

 **2.3)** Dragonmaw Soldier Cleave being 360 degrees (should not hit targets behind them to have some melee counterplay) + Dragonmaw Wyrmguard / Veteran damage. If they were always a part of 2-3 mob pull, that'd be fine, but level 28-32 groups that the dungeon is realistically going to draw simply don't have reliable tools to deal with 4-7 mob pulls in the last third of a full run with 1/2 minibosses in each.

Is it impossible? No, far from it. But it feels bad for healers to run OOM 2/3rds through every pull and hope and pray things just work, because even twinked out 34 tank I once ran with could not reliably handle the damage of 2 veterans + soldier + whelps + 1/2 more orcs.

It feels like trash damage was tweaked for plate-wearing tanks (45%+ damage reduction), when at that level mail-wearers hover around 30-35%, with druids possibly reaching 40%, but lacking block / parry still making them take a lot of unsmooth damage spikes.

In addition, non-paladin tanks don't have the means to hold aggro on that many targets.

Just use CC, you say. What CC? The only reliable CC at this level is mage sheep, and you're not guaranteed to find a mage at that level bracket.

Main suggestion for this is to reduce the amount of mobs in latter packs -- especially pre last boss pack -- to be 3-4 mobs, not 4-7. Reduce hp / damage of wyrmguard / veteran by 10-25%. And make cleave consistent with other cleave abilities (60-120 degrees, not 360).

...And to remove the 3-ghost-dwarf pack. Everyone skips it because it's a guaranteed wipe due to a combination of fear spam + anti healing curse. If it was an endgame dungeon it'd be doable but leveling players lack reliable tools to deal with it, especially if you hadn't been lucky with finding a decurser / tremor shaman.

I love the atmosphere. The dungeon as a whole is well crafted. I honestly think just tweaking trash amount & difficulty (And maybe buffing EXP per mob by 10-20%?) will make it perfect.
