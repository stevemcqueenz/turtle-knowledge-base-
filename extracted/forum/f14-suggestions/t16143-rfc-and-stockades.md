## Post 110144 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-27T08:54:00Z

It can go wrong in many ways.
At the beginning we have two options:
 1. This device porting you inside the dungeon.
 2. The device porting you near the dungeon entrance.
Option 2 will never work. You wanna raid SW/Org? Just mass LFT to RFC/Stockades with your lvl 60 PVP friends and suddenly there's an enemy army in the middle of the capital.
Option 1 has smaller problems but still has problems.
 1. RFC and Stockades are very early dungeons. Let's say you are a newcomer to the server and you just get used to ports to far-away dungeons. You'd become a saltmine the moment you realize that after these dungeons you have to do the walking. Game gave a comfort option at the beginning and took it away, pleople don't like already provided qol being revoked.
 2. The same idea worked for Gnomeregan, but it worked because of your higher level. You could do some quests, travel a bit and get to a place at a neutral city where port was provided. As a level 13 ally boi, your options are much more limited. The game would have to explain why the Alliance has a teleport to THE MIDDLE OF ORGRIMMAR in Elwynn or in Westfall. You can't put it anywhere else really, maybe another starting zone. Def not neutral cities, at level 13 you shouldn't be able to access those.
 3. It gives some replayability. Game is already pretty unique but the fact that a dungeon can only be done by 1 faction means some extra motivation to try out the other side.
