# Talent cost respec - early price reduction

## Post 112003 by Ahlen (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-10T09:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112003#p112003 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I've heard that on TurtleWoW the maximum amount of gold you need to pay for talent reset is capped at 5g which is great. TWoW also introduces many changes to the talents and I believe at the beginning of our journey people should have the possibility to test them out :)

For that reason I would like to suggest giving early levelers a little more freedom by adding additional thresholds to the talent respec cost.

 1. 10 s (new)
 2. 25 s (new)
 3. 50 s (new)
 4. 1g
 5. 5g

Currently, the first respec is 1g which is a lot if someone has just joined the server and is experimenting with the class.

Thanks
