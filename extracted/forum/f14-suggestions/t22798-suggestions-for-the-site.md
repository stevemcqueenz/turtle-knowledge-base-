Hi,

I've noticed a few things that probably need fixing on the site:

I have always wondered why password length is sometimes restricted. In principle, passwords are stored as hashes in the database, meaning that their actual length does not affect how they are stored: whether a password has 15 characters or 200, the resulting hash remains the same size. It would be beneficial if more developers adopt stronger security practices in the future to better protect users.

![Image](https://iili.io/Km3Hx99.jpg)

On the page : <https://turtlecraft.gg/profile#profile-overview>

It appears that the displayed last login date is slightly inaccurate.

![Image](https://www.zupimages.net/up/25/46/mlql.jpg)

Additionally, it might be better to remove the <br> tag found here. It doesn’t seem to serve a functional purpose and may negatively affect the layout.
