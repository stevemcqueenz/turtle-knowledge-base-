# Cosmetic Spells?

## Post 35572 by Andromath (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-21T05:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35572#p35572 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Don't know if it should be something available in-game through some kind of new Glyph Challenge or just another donation reward, but as a boon to the RP community, how about cosmetic spells?

There are plenty of spell animations in the game not used by players, and some which they do use but have short durations (Warlock summon animation or Mage conjuring portals animations).

A no-damage, purely cosmetic fireball, shadowbolt, smite, etc... that you could target on a random player just for the animation and nothing else. Feels like it'd add something pretty interesting, especially if one could learn them regardless of class. Maybe your Druid was a Highborne back in the day, but gave it up after the Sundering, but more recently finds him/herself in a position where they really need to use that old power? Maybe you're a mage using arcane magic to emulate druidism? Maybe you're a Warlock who was one a mage before finding the faster path to power? Heck, maybe you're a warrior who believes in the Light, but just doesn't have a strong enough connection to be an actual Paladin.

I'm curious what others think.

## Post 35613 by Andromath (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-21T13:26:00Z

To be clear, I wasn't suggesting replacements.

So, let's say you have your usual spells, and then a new tab for cosmetic spells.

[Arcane Ritual Channel (Directed)]
Channel Animation
Lasts until Cancelled

What this does is let your character use the same animation mages use when casting their portal spells. But, that's all it does. If you move, start an attack or spell/skill, take damage, etc... then the cosmetic spell effect ends. It only exists for show.

For RP'ers, this would be a great addition. Imagine you're in a Night Elf guild, and your group of Druids are performing some kind of cleansing ritual. They could use a [Nature Channel (Omni)] so that they all *look* like they're casting something. They're not reliant on using their actual combat spells for visual effect (which have short cast times or durations), and the visual effect can last through multiple people typing a response, rather than only a couple of seconds, making the scene that much more immersive.

The idea here is not to replace or overlap existing spells/skills. It's to add purely visual effects specifically for RP. Disabling them in Battlegrounds also seems fair, honestly. I mean, who RP's in a battleground, for starters? For another, yes, it could get very confusing.

About the only abuse or exploitation here that I can see would be griefing by running into a RP event and just start flinging around cosmetic spells to break immersion, but I'm pretty sure the server already has rules safeguarding RP so... yeah. Not a big deal? Report, ignore, and let them enjoy their vacation.
