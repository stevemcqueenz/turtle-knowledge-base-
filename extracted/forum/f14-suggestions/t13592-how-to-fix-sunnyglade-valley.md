# How to fix Sunnyglade Valley?

## Post 92182 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-05T23:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92182#p92182 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**How to fix Sunnyglade Valley?**

Let's say it clearly: Sunnyglade Valley is the least played battleground in Turtle WoW. This thread concerns only Nordanaar because I assume this battleground is even more dead at Tel'Abim PVP server. In recent months it doesn't even pop when SGV is battleground of the day. I had been trying to play this battleground for months but couldn't find any games! Note I tried at different times: European evenings and even at Chinese hours. Couldn't find a single game. This is why recently I organised Sunnyglade Valley event at European evening when SGV was the BG of the day. I advertised the event for a few hours before the event so to increase the chance of it to pop. And we managed to do it! Finally forgotten battleground was played after long time. The event officially lasted for 3 hours but we played a bit more so we've got about 10 matches overall. These games finally gave me knowledge to see what's is wrong with this battleground - I'm going to point out all the issues I noticed and suggest optimal changes to it. I will also report these bugs about the BG on bug tracker after I finish this post.

 **Too many people are required to start the game & queue doesn't help at all**

The core issue of Sunnyglade Valley at this point is that the game requires too many players to be in queue from both teams. From my experience (and experience of others, please share your opinion if you have found any Sunnyglade game recently) this battleground doesn't pop at all even when SGV is the BG of the day with bonus Honor. I had to organise event in order to revive this battleground because it seems game requires 5 people from each team in queue to start which is very unlikely to happen in current population numbers. This requirement is pointless at the moment because as I said this battleground is completely dead. My suggestion is to reduce these numbers to 3/4 from both teams. Before you say it's weird to have only 3 players from each team playing, consider that WSG requires same number of players and what's interesting is that both WSG and Sunnyglade Valley seem to have similar size of map. And with current enemy's boss HP scaling I can assure you even 3 man party can kill the boss. Map is small enough and can handle 3 vs 3 no problem.

The other thing that certainly doesn't cause Sunnyglade Valley to pop is Vanilla's queue system. In original Vanilla WoW you had only 3 battlegrounds, thus you could only queue up for max 3 battlegrounds at once. This has changed in Turtle WoW since you can queue up for 3 original battlegrounds but also for Blood Ring and Sunnyglade Valley. The problem is: we have 5 battlegrounds we can queue up to but Vanilla's limitation for signing up remains still 3 - that means you can only select 3 battlegrounds when signing up in Battleground Finder. Guess what, nobody prioritizes Sunnyglade Valley - this is why 3 original battlegrounds pop more often than custom ones. At least Blood Ring pops because it doesn't require as many players to be in queue as in Sunnyglade. Now you see the other reason why this battleground remains dead. If it's possible, please add possibility to be in queue for more than 3 battlegrounds at once - this would increase the chance for SGV to pop because there are people who would want to sign up for every BG, not only 3.

 **Poor Honor gained & no incentive to play Sunnyglade**

This is my empirical experience from playing these 10 matches of Sunnyglade Valley when it also was BG of the day. Luckily I played in Horde with more experienced players so we managed to win all 10 games in a row. Here are my observations:
- the average match lasted 15-20 minutes

- the average victory when it's SGV day in that time usually gives 1300-1600 bonus Honor - 1200 for killing last boss and the rest from collected sparks

- (I'm not sure about this, correct me if I'm wrong) the average defeat when it's SGV day in that time usually gives 400-600 bonus Honor - 300 for end of the game and the rest from collected sparks
Note that the Honor described above was still good because we avoided very long matches. If SGV goes longer than usual then average Honor per hour is even worse. Now compare Honor gained from SGV to Arathi Basin - average time of both battlegrounds is nearly the same 15-20 minutes. Though AB offers MUCH MORE Honor gained per hour. When AB is the BG of the day, if you win, you will get about 5000 Honor! And usually if AB match is balanced, losing player end up with about 3000 Honor gained. Compare these numbers to SGV again: ~1500 for victory and ~500 for defeat. Arathi Basin is at least 3x times more rewarding battleground now. By the way I think generally AB is superior battleground compared to others in terms of Honor farming in current patch and should be nerfed. Conclusion: Sunnyglade Valley Honor reward is very poor. If you want to promote this battleground, the Honor gained should be increased. If you don't increase the bonus Honor, this will give signal to players that this battleground isn't efficient at all to farm Honor in - farmers will avoid this battleground making it even more dead.
My suggestion is to increase bonus Honor for achieving victory/defeat by 3 times to at least match AB standards: instead of 1200 winners will get 3600 bonus Honor, losers also should get 3x more bonus Honor than now after game ends.

The other reason why Sunnyglade Valley remains dead is that the reputation from battleground is the same as the one gained in Caverns of Time dungeon. Every other BG (even Blood Ring) has its own separate reputation. People have no incentive to farm reputation in the battleground if forming a group for Black Morass is more convenient way. People who want to have Exalted reputation with everyone don't have to even join Sunnyglade Valley once, they'd be better farming it inside Black Morass.

 **Bugged Quests & Trinkets**

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1205456103757054004/1225565097364160612/trinkets.png?ex=66219771&is=660f2271&hm=28aa45994934aa4c6e6bba9f2b40c18564ddf832b938a5dece5e8d7eb2603d59&)

Currently in the game stackable buffs given by 3 of these trinkets can be taken outside Sunnyglade Valley until their time runs out. Though buff that gives AP lasts only 1 min and buff that gives spirit lasts 2 min, the buff given by the health trinket lasts 5 min! This can be potentially abused to boost main tank with extra 5000 HP for important fight like Patchwerk in Naxxramas.

Before I desribe second bug about these trinkets, you must know that AP trinket drops from Horde's quest NPC and Spirit trinket drops from Alliance's quest NPC. However, both teams can acquire all of these trinkets. If you want to get AP trinket as Horde player, you accept the quest from your Horde's quest NPC in which you must kill enemy's faction quest NPC (the one that drops Spirit trinket). The bug is: you can complete this quest once in your life. This quest isn't repeatable. That means once you complete quest for AP trinket as Horde's player, you will never get it in the next SGV match ever. I haven't tested how it looks like on Alliance side but I assume the quest is also not repeatable.

Another bug or intended mechanic is that the NPCs that drop these trinkets seem not to respawn at all (at least this is what I've observed in these 20 min games) once they are killed. And trust me: after game starts, usually all of these NPCs are killed within 3 minutes. If someone joins the battleground later, they will have no chance to acquire any of these trinkets. These NPCs should respawn in short time.

Probably another unintended mechanic is that once someone obtains the trinket, there is no way to lose it upon death. So if someone wears bonus Health trinket, they will keep it until the game ends. Considering the fact that players lose all sparks and give it to enemy that killed them upon death, these trinkets should work the same way. You die - your enemy inherits your trinket.

Also, these trinkets are soulbound for no reason. They should be tradeable in Sunnyglade Valley. Why the hell can Healer Priest use the trinket that gives AP? Why not give it to the melee class? Team should cooperate in this case.

The other broken thing about Spirit trinket is that you can stack it too easily. As a priest I could spam Renew Rank 1 to myself or whoever had this trinket equipped to boost my/their Spirit by 1000 in 30 seconds. The buff lasts 2 minutes so I can reapply it any time. I was generating about 300 mana per tick out of combat with Spirit stacked this way.

 **Terrain Core Issue**

In the map below I marked terrain obstacles (hills) which can't be reached going straight with Red color. The water obstacle was marked with Blue. Yellow dots are spots in which you fight enemy's faction boss.

Now as an example look at another battleground's map and find out what's wrong with Sunnyglade Valley terrain.

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1205456103757054004/1225918022884261990/warsongterrain.png?ex=6622e021&is=66106b21&hm=97bec05e44e668e5b270241bdde300a0651b32d4e61cb5025b514398472958d6&)

All original battlegrounds that were in Vanilla didn't have such boring open area as SGV. Here you have open zone where you can reach every point on the map by simply going straight forward. The awfulness of current terrain can be observed when one team is going to kill enemy's boss after gathering enough sparks. Bosses are marked with yellow dots on that map. Then everyone moves straight to the boss - there are 0 hills on everyone's way. The fight takes place on flat terrain hidden by some trees literally in the middle of the forest. Places where bosses stand are not unique! I observed last boss fight in all 10 matches and I've seen confusion of enemy team - most of them didn't even know where we were fighting even though their boss was close to their graveyard.

My suggestion to fix terrain issue is shown on this map:

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1205456103757054004/1225923101460529172/terrainsgv.png?ex=6622e4db&is=66106fdb&hm=718065ea6caf6d5723603d743f9c1b4b04e9822bfa8ca003bcb6e5e790426a8c&)

Impassable hills must be added in bases so reaching the last boss for attackers would be harder - it would also be easier for new players in defending team to find out where they should defend. Bosses should be scripted not to be kited further than 40 yards. My another suggestion is to activate this loudy notification sound that is in every BG when something important happens (for example someone takes flag in WSG). When boss is engaged, the sound should activate.

Another terrain issue inside Sunnyglade Valley which I find very annoying is a castle in the middle. The idea of controling the castle in order to collect sparks is nice one. However, there is one little problem - there is only one narrow entrance to it. Very narrow entrance. This place is amazing to defend as a Hunter with traps, Mage with improved Blizzard or even priest with Mind Control. Castle is crucial objective in this battleground, thus it shouldn't have only one entrance especially if it's in the middle of the map. Warsong Gulch base has 2 entrances, there are also 2 paths to every point in Arathi Basin. My suggestion is to add second stairs to the castle in the middle so the attacking team could have alternative way of going there instead of path through Blizzard Rank 1 spam.

 **Boss HP scaling works terribly wrong**

This issue can be classified as unintended mechanic or simply a bug. Note I'm not an expert who knows every formula in this battleground. I rely on my empirical observations. Little trivia:

These changes were introduced to the game almost one year ago. I suppose players' feedback back then was like "the boss is too hard to kill, let's nerf him" so devs decided to make boss easy to kill. My friend who was playing this BG back then told me that bosses used to be way harder to kill than now, he claimed to see boss having 1 000 000 HP. The boss HP now depends on how big is difference between number of sparks of your and enemy team. Such big HP was nerfed but now it's way too easy. I observed that if difference of sparks is big enough, the boss HP scales down to normal unit's HP. Because boss is elite mob, his HP in one game was only 7800 HP IIRC. The funny thing is that the boss can be potentially killed solo by geared Paladin by 3 of their swings xD. Nevertheless, the situation I observed was awkward too. Our team decided to kill the boss, we started the fight and killed him within 5 seconds because his HP was also 7800. What's interesting is that enemy team actually tried to defend the boss, however how is it possible to keep alive NPC that has only 7800 HP? This nerf was wade wrong, the weird thing is that nobody has reported this "bug" yet.

My suggestion in this regard is to set minimum HP for every boss. In my opinion 50k HP should be fine - it's more than Commander but less than Galvangar/Balinda in Alterac Valley. Scaling of HP should remain dependable of spark difference between teams. Also I don't mind if bosses hit harder or do some CC.

Because I touched the topic of bosses, I also suggest to buff mini bosses that drop the trinkets. They have 5-7k HP which is very little - these bosses can be killed solo easily, at least I could kill all of them as Smite Priest. Buffing their HP to 15k should be great. Then people would have to go there in groups in order to get powerful trinkets.

Summary, the features which would help Sunnyglade Valley

- Reduce the number of players needed to start Sunnyglade Valley match

- Allow to be in queue for more than 3 battlegrounds

- Increase Honor gained in Sunnyglade Valley

- Consider adding new reputation just for this battleground that would have interesting items to farm

- Fix bug that allows you to take buff from Sunnyglade Valley outside of battleground

- Fix quest for trinket to be repeatable

- Allow mini bosses to respawn (faster)

- Trinkets should be looted by enemy upon death

- Trinkets shouldn't be tradeable inside battleground

- Make terrain revamp in faction boss areas according to my map above

- Add second stairs to the castle

- Add BG-notification sound when your boss is engaged by enemy team

- Set faction bosses' HP to minimum 50k, buff mini bosses to 15k HP, add some CC spells to faction bosses

- Introduce Mark of Sunnyglade to the game according to 1.17.0 promises
