Cheerio!

I'm not sure whether this already counts as a bug report, sry if so, I know that it should then be on github instead of the forum. I will move it there if told that that's where it belongs.

When I read the tooltip of The Wolf Horn of Orgrimmar stating a chance to stun an enemy struck in combat, I thought this would not depend on the way of how that enemy was struck. From the database I could find, though, that the effect has a 14 [edit: it's actually 5] yard range. I experimented ingame and also only ever witnessed a proc when striking with a melee weapon.

Thus I suggest considering to change the word "combat" to "melee" in the tooltip if the behaviour is intentional, or change the behaviour otherwise.

Thanks for your time and best regards!
