# Add Shirts + Tabards to Transmog, Change How Tents Work or Remove Them

## Post 20073 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-07-17T13:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20073#p20073 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, what's up?  I have a couple of unrelated suggestions that I figured I'd lump together.
–

Add Shirts + Tabards to Transmog
What I propose is that **Shirts and Tabards be added to the transmogrification options**.  Simple as that. While collecting every unique set of "Level 1 Gear", it came to my attention that Shirts and Tabards are not currently available for transmogrification.  That's kind of a shame.  At present, there are somewhere around 70 different Shirts and about 70 different Tabards available on *Turtle WoW*.  I could see someone creating a "wardrobe alt" just to house some of these, but I doubt many people would be willing to have every single shirt stashed somewhere on their characters.  Some of these are also Unique and a handful are Bind on Pickup which means that they can't be transferred to other characters.  I can't imagine it would be that hard to do.  Granted, it took Blizzard until v7.0.3 to add Shirts and Tabards as options, but still.  It seems pretty common sense, looking back.  Right?
[small]And of course, "Hide" items for these should be made, too.  Hee hee.[/small]

 
Change How Tents Work or Remove Them
Ooh.  Controversial opinion, *innit*?  But, yes.  I would genuinely like to **see tents either changed or removed**.  I have good reasons, too. (This one's a long one, so buckle up…) Simply, tents encourage people to *idle*.  And I mean that in *spades*.  Have you taken a look at Goldshire?  Or how about the Crossroads?  Even the Gurubashi Arena (up until recently) has become a loitering spot for lazy people looking to shorten their rush to Level 60.  There is even a **'bot** in the Gurubashi Arena specifically there to *tell people* how many tents are in the Arena.  Is this really how people want to play the game?  Begging for people to erect dozens of tents, then sitting and idling while their Bonus XP gauge fills?  And more importantly, is that how *you*, the "Turtle Team", want players to interact with each other?
On top of that, tents encourage some rather undesirable behavior.  Namely, players who are flagged for PvP become sitting ducks for bored players of the opposite faction to prey on.  Again, take a look at Goldshire on any given day.  It's a jolly *mess* with corpses and skeletons littered all over the place.  And mind, I'm not saying "PvP bad, fix PvP".  PvP is a choice on a PvE server like this.  I'm just saying… the opposing faction would probably be less likely to pop in unannounced and start wrecking house if they didn't know, with certainty, that there's always going to be a nice little cluster of yellow / red nametags bunched together, completely defenseless and likely not even paying attention.
There's also another bad thing about tents that I haven't seen mentioned.  Since the tent is a physical object, it could possibly lead to some rather undesirable functionality.  One such example would be erecting a tent in Battlegrounds to get into places the player isn't supposed to.  Another example might be using tents to bypass certain checkpoints or certain areas.  It may even be possible to make a tent to avoid triggering town guards or, worse, someone making guards cluster in one spot, unable to attack but frozen in place.  This could effectively render a small town completely defenseless against players of the opposite faction.

I'm sure that very few people would agree with me on the idea of outright **removing** tents from the game.  However, I do feel that at least a handful more people would agree that the functionality *of* tents should be modified.  As it stands, tents are actually **significantly** more effective than staying at an inn -- which makes absolutely no sense at all.  Staying in a nice, warm building with a soft, cushy bed only gives players about a "bubble" (5%) of "Rested XP" per 8 hours, but a tent, which is open to the elements and doesn't even come with a *bedroll*, gives players a "bubble" of XP about every *five minutes*!  How does that even *make sense*?!
In my opinion, tents should work *exactly* line inns: you put one up, you log off, you get rested XP for as long as you're logged off, and that's that.  Keep the 30 minute limit for the tent object – that's fine.  Other people *should* be able to share your tent.  It's a *tent*, after all, not a sleeping bag.  But again, treat it exactly like an inn.  Also, make it a completely non-physical object.  That solves both problems with as little hassle as possible.

–

And that's all I have to say about that.  Please, take these suggestions under consideration and thank you, anyone and everyone, for reading.
