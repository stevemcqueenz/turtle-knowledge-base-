# Reduce crafting time for professions

## Post 29542 by Darkgestalt — 2022-11-20T10:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29542#p29542 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So far some reason, I'm not sure what that is, as a player makes higher level items the time to craft them increases to quite absurd lengths. I was just grinding some skilllevels by making run-of-the-mill items in large quantities and it took forever.

I can appreciate the anticipation of making fancy high level items, and its fine for rare or epic items to take more time to simulate the increased difficulty. But maybe tone it down a bit, especially for basic skillleveling stuff like commons.

I suggest that crafting time is based only on item quality and NOT itemlevel. So for example this:

Basic items that crafters make in bulk
Common - 3 seconds
Uncommon - 5 seconds

Fancy items that crafters only make 1 or 2 in a row
Rare - 10 seconds
Epic - 15 seconds

Superduper items that the whole guild is waiting for in anticipation
Legendary - 30 seconds
