# Itemization problems

## Post 143383 by Reiker — 2025-08-06T13:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143383#p143383 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I started playing paladin a few months ago and was disappointed to learn that the T3 and T3.5 sets are bad for my spec, and everyone uses T2 and T2.5 instead. It seems that good itemization for my spec/class kinda stops past AQ40.

The T3 set is inexplicably missing a core stat (SP) even though the T0.5, T1, T2, and T2.5 sets all give SP. All of a sudden the T3 set gives a completely different stat spread for the same spec & class? Why was it designed that way?

Surely the itemization team would see this, and improve the T3 (and 3.5) sets to be something for people to look forward to, right?

Well apparently the response to this problem was to instead nerf the SP from the T3 tank set (???) and then nerf the tier bonus for T2, T2.5, and T3 by 50%.

So to clarify, players were disappointed that their T3 and T3.5 sets were bad. The solution that the turtle wow itemization team came up with was to just nerf all of these sets, and somehow they think that will solve something?

Well these changes seem to actually be making the existing problem even worse. Now paladins are talking about going back to their pre-raid T0.5 set, just because it isn't a target of these nerfs. And some paladins seem to be completely screwed because they've already vendored this armor (who could predict that this pre-raid dungeon stuff would ever be relevant to Naxx+ raiding?) and Turtle WoW won't restore this armor they've also chosen to suddenly make relevant again.

So one more time: the problem was that the gear from late tiers was statted poorly. The solution by the turtle wow dev team was to just nerf every raid set, which now has paladins going even further back to non-raid gear.

The whole point of this game is to progress your character. People want to upgrade their gear. They don't want to go into Naxxramas knowing that none of the tier sets is an upgrade for their class. Now Turtle WoW is trying to make every tier set a bad option. Are paladins meant to just use their dungeon set all the way to Grim Batol?

The itemization team seems wildly incompetent. Everything else about the game is good.

> **Cheruscan wrote: Wed Aug 06, 2025 2:23 pm**
> you got it twisted, the idea from the get go was to move Ret more towards AP to avoid overlap with protladin, its an ongoing progress since devs need data and playtesting is to low of a sample size.

I have no idea if this is true, but it just sound like further evidence that there's a major disconnect between the itemization team and whoever else is designing the game. What makes ret paladin fun for me are the differences from warrior (which I've played a lot of already), so why redesign itemization to push ret paladins towards playing more like warriors?

Also, AP Ret seems good too. I raid with someone who uses Seal of Command and their DPS is competitive with everyone else. It's actually nice that we're both the same class & spec yet we go for slightly different items in raids. It also allows for stuff that normally gets disenchanted to become useful, like Shadowstrike. Why would the itemization team see this as bad design?

"Avoiding overlap with protladin" is also nonsensical. Ret paladins and prot paladins are the same class with the same abilities so obviously the same stats are going to benefit both specs. Why is this a bad thing? I enjoy having the flexibility between playing dps and playing tank. Isn't this what turtle wow is supposed to be about?

But anyways, this isn't even really about paladins in particular. The broader point is that tier sets should always be desirable. It feels bad to hit an early progression ceiling and not have things to look forward to from raids. To start seeing targeted nerfs to a particular class's tier sets feels like there's some core problems with design that could perpetuate to other classes.

My concern is that this feels like a slide towards live WoW's inclination to cycle through having certain winner and loser classes/specs instead of trying to keep the classes feeling equally fun to play.

> Both Spellret and Protladin are currently quite overtuned with their dps output, also patch reintroduces dmg component of holy shock

My perspective is someone progressing through the raid tiers and seeing half of my upgrades getting nerfed, and the other half not even providing useful stats for my spec. I'm not sure what things are like at the high end, but from looking at parses it seems like a lot of warriors and mages are on top with the "off" dps classes like paladins, hunters, etc. a tier or two behind.

It actually seems fairly balanced, much more balanced than vanilla WoW usually is. But this data would suggest that if anyone should be seeing nerfs it would be warriors and mages. But again, this post isn't about nerfing classes.

Regarding Holy Shock, I have my doubts. You have to lose talents from Ret run an extra +3% hit on your gear to have enough points to reach Holy Shock for what seems to be a mediocre damage:mana ratio. But I'm waiting to see what the general consensus is after the patch.

Protladin has been doing way too much dmg for a tank specc and simply nerfing Prot Tier sets would have just resulted in Prots going for the Ret sets instead. As it turns out its quite hard to balance a spellschool with no natural resistances against.

Paladin plays fundamentally different than warrior, yet in order to not make items not only usable by one class/specc theres gotta be a middle ground, you can still collect spellpower gear and combine it with Melee dps pieces to make your own hybrid setup, Tier sets were just too well optimised that they pushed alternatives out, thus the nerf, atleast that how I see it.

The lost spellpower on gear will therefor just be made available again through these new consumables.

Holy Shock will have a major impact on PvP, since it has a 50% crit chance through talents already and can be reset by CS (if you talent for it) so Holy Paladins can be quite bursty if they pick up some Spellpower pieces, or just a CD trinket, like ZHC or Ephemeral Talisman.
