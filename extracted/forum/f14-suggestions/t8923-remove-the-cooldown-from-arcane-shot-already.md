Hunter was an unfinished class in Vanilla and barely made it in. This is why it primarily scales off RAP but also has these abilities with SP scaling like Arcane Shot, Serpent Sting and Volley while originally also having some that didn't scale at all like traps or Mongoose Bite. There's also the mediocre scaling overall from stats that the class suffers from.

So the solution is to first finish the class by giving proper scaling (I do feel bad for any SP hunters out there) to what needs it and then build from there.

Auto-shot, Steady Shot and Aimed Shot having a cast time makes sense but what's the logic behind Arcane Shot being instant, specifically, and not Multi-Shot, for example? Clearly, another unfinished feature of a class that had to use Focus originally and ended up using mana, which then became a problem in every expansion until Cataclysm came about.

To put it into perspective, Arcane Shot costs around 175 mana at level 60 *with* the Efficiency talent. Your average hunter will have around 2500-3000 mana unbuffed before hitting raids. Using AS is a waste of both damage and resources. Spirit mana regen is pointless at max level as well due to the fact that ranged hunters spam Steady Shot.

A common advice given in TWoW is that the mana issues become way more manageable when acquiring T3 so an obvious path to explore is tweaking hunter mana costs and giving them mana recovery of some sort, perhaps tied to specific talents and logical for each tree (e. g. your pet critting gives you mana as BM and the pet gets focus when you crit, your special crits give you mana as MM and your melee special crits give you mana as SV).
