# Thorn Gorge - Dead on Arrival?

## Post 173081 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-29T21:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173081#p173081 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone,

I wanted to share some thoughts on Thorn Gorge BG that was recently added in 1.18.1 While it’s exciting to see new content, I feel like this BG is struggling and might be dead on arrival. Here are the main reasons:

No Bonus Day
Unlike the original three BGs, there’s no bonus day for honor. This makes it less appealing for players to queue up regularly.

No Reputation Rewards
BGs traditionally help players by rewarding rep gear at various levels, the lack of a faction reputation removes a key motivation to participate.

No Marks of Honor for Turn-ins
There’s no way to exchange Marks of Honor for XP or additional honor like the other bgs, which feels less rewarding.

Level Restriction
This BG is locked to level 60 only, which prevents low levels from participating.
(actually they enabled it at 30+ but you cant queue from the minimap so effectively 60 only still)

Queue Limit Issues
Players can only queue for three BGs at once. For most people, that means the usual choices are AV, AB, and WSG. As a result, this new BG will rarely, if ever, be queued, further decreasing its viability.

Declining Activity
From what I’ve seen, the BG was moderately active for the first 2–3 days after launch but now sees almost no activity, making it difficult for players to find matches.

Overall, these factors make it hard for the BG to gain traction. I’d love to see the devs consider adding incentives, broadening level access, or creating a way to make participation more rewarding, so it doesn’t fade into inactivity and become SGV 2.0 , which was never played.
