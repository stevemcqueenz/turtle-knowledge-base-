# Rogue Disguise - Making it fun!

## Post 24818 by Lewka — 2022-09-11T07:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24818#p24818 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, i love the idea behind rogue disguise, but i think this state is only the surface. Disguise should not fall off when we engage in combat, mounting, flying, etc... The current disguise is only a tool for RP, like dancing in the town, or i dont know. But giving some function, like some cool new rogue visual, where we can dress up as a member of a gang, and remain in that state, for like next death, is something that i think every rogue would love.

Beside that, i would give a little hidden extra to disguise. Every npc gang could have an informator - or the suspicious (disguise recipe vendor) npc could be the informator too.
The informator tells you about a vendor, who can be found in that current area, but hidden, and only can be interacted while disguised as the current gang. Those vendor could sell 1 item only. Some fancy mask, throwing, stealth increace /detection tool, some creative useful item.

That would be a cool extra rogue experience, and some content to the disguise spell.
