# BG Join Loading Screen Tip

## Post 49040 by Jammyxx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-06T12:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49040#p49040 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One great feature the Devs have added over classic is you can queue BG from anywhere in the world and be ported there and back when the game ends, this is great while questing to just do a quick BG.

The only problem is not many people know about this, I'd leveled my char to 60 and I was still queueing from the Battle Masters in town until a few days into 60 I found out.

So I feel like alot of the newbies to the server don't know about this amazing qol feature for queueing BG's - I do think if they knew low lvl BG's would pop FAR more.

So my suggestions is to add a custom loading screen tip saying "You can queue for Battlegrounds from the minimap icon anywhere in the world"

I think this one change alone wil help people realise they can join BG's anywhere, I'm currently leveling an alt and would love more low level queues to pop.. Yes I'm aware 10-19 is full of twink but the other brackets haven't been too bad.
