## Post 44140 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-02T17:22:00Z

I know this is a common request, but I've always felt the Eranikus questline has already been finished in Vanilla when they picked it back up with the AQ40 opening event (specifically <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=8733> and follow-ups). You have a nice talk with Malfurion Stormrage (what an epic lore moment to meet him if you're playing a druid!) about Eranikus and eventually end up bringing his fully-powered version into Kalimdor with the epic Moonglade event ( <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=8736> ). Sure, the transition between the Chained Essence and the endgame questline is non-existent, but it's clearly addressing the same lore issue (the corruption of Eranikus and the Emerald Dream in general) and bringing the Eranikus stuff to a conclusion. I don't know how much room there is for custom content here, since the outcome has already been written by Vanilla. I can see maybe adding 1 or 2 follow ups so it isn't *that* abrupt, but in the end the questline will have to pause until you're in BWL one way or the other.
