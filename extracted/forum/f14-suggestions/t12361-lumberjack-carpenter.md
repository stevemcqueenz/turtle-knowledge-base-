# Lumberjack / Carpenter

## Post 82267 by Spot (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-22T09:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82267#p82267 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Lumberjacks would equip tree cutting axe and gather wood from tree nodes.  The trees would be already existing trees in Azeroth.  You could tap a tree and then that tree couldn't be tapped for a wile.  Lumberjacks could have a tracking skill that highlights trees that can be tapped at the moment.  Gather different level wood from zones.  Refine them into useable boards.  Much like cloth to bolts.  Make specific types of wood boards needed for crafting with Carpentry.

Carpentry would give players the ability to make gun shafts, bow shafts, wood parts for engineering, lockboxes for rogues, spears, shovels, and arrows.  With enchanting mats added to the requirements perhaps give them the ability to make the enchanted broom pet.   And the ability to sell sawdust to rogues for better blind duration.  This is just off the top of my head.

I never understood why Blizzard took us into Gem cutting.  Another skill that pulls from mining.  The game is missing a lot of wood items the player has to buy from vendors.  Why they didn't add these professions is beyond me.
