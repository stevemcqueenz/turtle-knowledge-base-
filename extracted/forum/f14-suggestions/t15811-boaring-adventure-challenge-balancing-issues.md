# Boaring Adventure Challenge Balancing Issues

## Post 107783 by Redsalamando — 2024-11-13T03:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107783#p107783 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Now that it's been out for a while and I've met plenty of familiar faces in the limited amount of areas to really travel to, I've leveled to 30 killing only Boars in HC, ran RFK's pit several times, and planned the next future 30 levels to 60, and I have some complaints about the entire challenge.
I understand it's a challenge and intended to be difficult with varying degrees of difficulty depending on what class you pick, but most are class neutral issues that kind of plague the whole challenge. These are mostly complaints based on my opinion on what I've had to go through as HC, which may make some of these complaints sound niche or unwarranted to anyone not doing this challenge, or not playing HC and can just send gold to their alt. That issue also rears it's head later, allow me to get into it:

 **1. Most of the new boars don't drop ANY loot.**
The most you will get is coppers worth of Boar Meat or Boar Intestines, some with maybe a silvers worth of tusks. There is also another problem that kind of happens with these new mob additions: their loot tables aren't fixed yet, so all they drop is level 8 greens. I've spent maybe a week in Hillsbrad now and I'm poor as hell because killing exp-less Spiders for silver to train for abilities is actually something I have to do. The highest amount of money to rarely expect from a boar is a 2silver green cloth bracer and I expect this trend to follow into the Arathi Highland boars as well.
To put it really into perspective: you're only killing boars, so quest greens are your primary source of gear, because boars only drop pennies and level 8 cloth bracers with +1 spirit. So you'd have to be going out of your way to do something suboptimal to gear back up to speed. **I'm not wholly against having to take a break from exp to do professions or a quest or two to get a nice gear slot** (that's part of the fun of the challenge!), but being able to afford 1 new spell's worth from boar gold every two levels is legitimately annoying.

Solution: Fix loot tables please to not drop greens from 1-10 zones. Maybe add super rare grey drops (0.5% or so? Something literally only someone killing ONLY boars would ever loot. [Pristine Boar Tusk] that sells for 50s or would help a lot) to make "primarily killing boars to level" not completely gut every other aspect of your character.
The silver from selling greens should fix this issue though tbh. I'm just asking for a little more excitement of loot as a chance.

 **2. Some of the boar placements are downright laughable.**
"New boars" to level with and we thought 2 (total) boars at the elevator of Thousand Needles was enough for the level 18-22 bracket? They're also in between several level 25 stealth cougars and other level 26 mobs. Wait, aren't these the boars for people leveling 18-22? Why is there only a handful of them?? Why are the level 30-40 Boars being guarded by a level 50 Alliance Harvest Golem??
Is the "challenge" of this challenge supposed to be killing level 22 boars for exp in between level 26's? What about exp from level 33's in between a level 50? Why are these difficulty barriers only for Horde?? It really shouldn't be some secret Hard Mode to be Horde for this challenge.

Solution: Move the Mesa Boar (21-22) to above The Great Lift, making it level appropriate in the Barrens, and not in 1K Needles for some reason. There's no reason they should be scattered around a level 25 zone. (This boar also is completely nullified by the existence of the Tirisfal boar, who bridges the gap of level 17-22 to make it to Hillsbrad boars, making the Mesa Boar obsolete and pointless to attempt farming.)
Solution 2: Make 'Chaser' the harvest golem friendly to Horde to even the balance between factions. Horde shouldn't have to worry about using aoe in an area favored by Alliance for the sake of a challenge. I think the Turtle Team forgetting this quest NPC was even there before this challenge existed is a reasonable thing to happen, but leaving it hostile to Horde makes this challenge look a bit lazy, with the added lack of foresight to this and the following (and probably most important) issue.
Otherwise the safest route for the level 30-40 bracket is to repeatedly log into my higher level character, kill this quest NPC of the Alliance (bannable because it's griefing a quest npc), and logging back into my Boar character and get 5 minutes of free time before repeating the process, just for the sake of a challenge. And nothing is really stopping me from doing this btw, I can and will if this doesn't get changed. That's how glaring of an oversight this is.

 **3. Some level gaps plain suck and were not fixed.** (Focus on 40-45 primarily.)
 **I post this assuming that we as a community still haven't found the level 40 Boars yet.**
Where am I supposed to realistically farm at level 42. Be honest.
The Arathi Highland boars go from 33-34, which means the highest I can get, even if I only kill the level 34's while the 33's are grey, is level 43. The Ashmane Boar in Blasted Lands is level 48-49 which is a 5 and 6 level gap. The only other "reasonable" location for boars is RFD, which is small packs of non-elite boars in three packs in a group of 5 players. I know you watched the Youtube video, but did you actually look to see that the count during that phase of leveling rises from 4000 to 10,000 just for that level bracket? 6000 weak boars just for a couple of levels to skip to Blasted Lands. Imagine that many RFD groups. Lets be real, nobody wants this, and being Hardcore just makes this infinitely harder and tedious.
Now trust me I've done my scouting for this challenge in every level 40 area I can think of that may have a level 40 boar. But nope, this level bracket just sucks this hard, which also leads me to my most prominent evidence that this level bracket sucks: The highest level boarers right now hate these brackets so much that they just brought in their high level alts to kill those boars *for* them. This is pretty prominent evidence that this bracket sucks hard to power through, opting into the path of least resistance. During scouting I've seen this practice at least 3 times.
I don't care if what they're doing is "cheating" or whatever, what I care about is that this is what we're all going to become when level brackets are this spaced out.

Solution 1: A bit of a backtrack, but this bracket really is glaring when you get there. Give the Horde another zone to bridge the gap between Tirisfal Boars (17) to Hillsbrad Boars (23), which would be placing the Mesa Boars above 1K Needles at a comfy level 21-22.
Solution 2: Give us some decent boars to bridge Arathi Highlands's maximum level to Blasted Lands.
What's the highest I'd want to stay in Arathi? 40 or so, right? But Blasted Lands is too strong at level 47-48. Let's add some boars in lower end 40 zones like Tanaris or Hinterlands or something. I really did check everywhere. There's plenty of creative spots to hide them, make someone have to do research if they go into this blind. Put them in the road to WPL from Hinterlands that nobody uses, put them on the edge of Sandmoon Ruins looking over Shimmering Flats on the ledges, put them around the cliffs of Feralas overlooking the water.
Seriously though if we just all missed these level 40 boars then feel free to call me stupid but I did ask around

This challenge really is a breath of fresh air and has created several guilds dedicated to the journey, but it's kind of left me disappointed when these issues were overlooked so hard, as if nobody on the team had even attempted it and only made it because it would be funny as a joke. Hardcore is pretty much already baseline for any new characters I create, because it allows me to utilize and observe my options with gear and quest locations and not cheese the game through alts I already have, but this challenge is insanely limited comparatively. There is literally no other options than X, Y, and then Z, almost railroading the challenges experience instead of crafting your own. I really only created this thread to bring attention to these issues, not to criticize the challenge of it. I look forward to continuing my Hardboar character and hope some sort of remedy to these issues is sooner rather than later.
