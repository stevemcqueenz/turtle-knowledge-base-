Hello,  this is my first post here and i have been reading alot the last few days here but i wanted to give my 2 cents as well so here it goes!

Crafting:

As a long time crafter in most if not all MMOS I have ever played this is something I really enjoy doing and it’s sad that not many games have crafting as a viable playstyle. I get that in level based games it’s hard to do but that does not mean that you can’t learn from what makes crafting in other games fun.
One of the thing I think could be a fun edition to turtle wow and a boost for crafting/rp and even not that far from the vanilla spirit imo is when you craft items there is a chance that they become “exceptional” I’m using this word since that word used in Ultima Online which was my first MMO back in -97. And it’s basically crit in crafting, it makes the item a little better. Nothing major but lets say for a normal copper dagger [5-10] DMG 1.50 speed lvl req 6, and the “exceptional” one would be exceptional copper dagger [7-12] DMG 1.50 speed lvl req 6 or exceptional copper dagger [5-10] 1.30 speed lvl req 4. As for green, blue and even epic recipes maybe a 5-10% inc in stats as well as the above mentioned or even giving them a chance to gain in rarity green  blue  epic  just more stats. Nothing game breaking but still adds a bit more fun for crafters and the rp community.
These changes are more for the Armor/weapon professions, alchemy and enchanting might be a bit harder since I feel they might have a bigger chance of being game breaking if you do something like adding extra stats to enchants or better potions like extended duration or even higher stats like Stonescale potion does 2500 armor instead of 2000. But maybe some new slayer enchants with cool glow effects on, like beast slaying but the like undead, elements, demons etc.
Cooking could just be extended buff duration, as for fishing that’s a totally different thing *cough* ashbringer… ![😉](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f609.svg)

Hero classes:

Back when TBC was about to first be released there was a fake leak I think saying there would be hero classes that made me so hyped! Can’t really remember the details but the desire to play hero classes has stayed with me ever since. I’m all for the when you make a hero class you “sacrifice” a char to evolve so to say in to said hero class. This might not be for everyone but it’s my opinion. After you have turned lvl 60 there will be questlines available from certain NPC, that represent that hero class, like for horde hunters,  rexxar for beastmaster hunters (melee and pet focused hunter) more on that later. After doing the questlines and proven that you have what it takes to follow the path of xxxx class. You will either reset your levels and be sent back to your race starting area you will have a new talent tree (just 1) and start levelling again, or you can keep your levels but gain the new talent tree and start doing quests that give new skills and talent points or something like that.
After reading around here in the suggestion forum I have seen a few nice things but I will give my thoughts as well. Ofc I know these are really game breaking things and would take ages to balance but hey! It’s all fun and who knows there might be a few gold nuggets hidden in here :P

Hunter:

Beastmaster: (Orc, Tauren, maybe Nelf?)
A melee and pet focused class that can still equip a bow but only keep autoshot and huntersmark but it now gives melee ap instead of range ap bonus to target.
Weapon focus on either twohanded polearm/axe or 2 one handed swords/axes.
Still uses mail armor and pet healing is a HoT and not channelling.
 2 pets, still uses mana but get more attacks, bleeds and able to use traps in combat.

Ranger: (Helf, Nelf, Human)
Loses the pet and only focus on range, lose the 8 foot range cap, can use the bow all the time.
Keep wingclip lose all other melee attacks. Can equip the same weapons as normal hunter.
Still use mail armor. TS aura as baseline normal as an aura like pala not a 30 min buff.
Better at keeping enemys at range, slow, movement speed + etc.

Stalker/Night Ranger: (Nelf, Human, Troll)
Loses pet, range focus, can use stealth and uses leather armor. Can also use traps (snare, poison).

Darkranger: (Undead)
Same as ranger but shadow themed.

Shadowhunter:  (Troll also a path for troll priests)
Melee and shadow magic focus. Can use shadowform, and focus on debuffing and dots while using a spear/polearm/throwing spear/Axe. Some shadow healing and ress ability.
Leather armor.

Priest:

Highpriest: (Human, Helf, Dwarf, Undead)
Holy focused, lose all shadow/arcane abilities (even racial once). Buffes holy DPS and grp focused healing. GRP renew or leave a low lvl renew after prayer of healing.

Moonpriestess: (Nelf)
Arcane focused DPS and healing, more buff/absorb focused.
A few new dps spells same as druid starfire etc but other names.
Can equip bow and summon and owl that acts as a flare on CD.
Tyrannda inspired from WC3. Maybe shot magic arrows from bow no quiver needed.

Voodoo Priest: (Troll)
Shadow/nature based dps can still heal can use Hex like polymorph. Loa based abilities.

Paladin:

Spell breaker: (Helf also path for Helf warriors)
High magic resist and high lower magic dmg taken, can steal buffs from others.
Equip plate, shields, swords/glaves. Taunt tank spec.

Crusader: (Human, Helf, Dwarf)
Sword and shield DPS.

Lightbringer: (Human, Helf, Dwarf)
Holy heal and range spell DPS.

Warrior:

Blademaster: (Orc)
Cloth armor evasion only 2 handed weapons.

Warden: (Nelf)
Magic plate user melee and insta spell casts, slows and snares. Debuffs.
Blink, teleport.

Deathknight: (Undead, Orc)
Melee shadow, debuff plate tank and dps.

Mage:

Archmage: (All mage races)
A single tree that buffes all schools of magic and some new spells.

Necromancer: (Human, Undead, Orc, Troll, Goblin, Helf)
Pet shadow/frost magic dps.

Warlock:

Necromancer: (Human, Undead, Orc, Troll, Goblin, Helf)
Pet shadow/frost magic dps.

Cultist: (Human, Gnome, Orc, Undead)
Void like shadow magic user.

Rogue:

Demonhunter: (Nelf, Helf)
Cloth/Leather armor magic dual wield dps.
Fel/shadow magic.

Pirate: (Goblin, Human, Orc, Tauren, Gnome)
No sthelth, cloth/leather dual wield + gun dps. Evasion+ when drunk.

Spy: (Gnome, Goblin)
Stealth++ daggers only and better poisons, blink when hidden.

I suggested in another thread that hero classes could be an offshoot of an existing class, rather than an entirely new class.  This would allow them to be implemented with minimal interference with the classes that already exist, preserving balance.

So for example,  beast master would be attained by a hunter player who perhaps completes a certain quest chain, unlocking some new talent options that they can choose from.  Perhaps they get 1 or 2 unique spells that traditional hunters don't get, and their official class title is changed to beast master.
Overall, it would still function 95% like a normal hunter would, so it would be easier to add to the game.

Same with Troll Witch Doctors, allow them to acquire a few unique talent options that if chosen will change the characters class name to Witch Doctor, so if a troll priest goes down that path in their talent tree they can make the shift while still playing predominantly like a traditional shadow priest does.
