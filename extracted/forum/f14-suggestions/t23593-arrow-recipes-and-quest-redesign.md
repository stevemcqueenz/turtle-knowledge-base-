# Arrow recipes and quest redesign

## Post 165140 by berdal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-14T14:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165140#p165140 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

For low-level twinks, it's a pain to dish out large amounts of gold for consumable arrows. Which often has ludacris prices. Namely [Feathered Arrow]/[Exploding Shot].

My proposal is:
- Make the quest reward Profession Recipies instead of the actual ammunition as reward.
Alternatively, just give the quest reward projectiles a level req of 30 and be done with it.

This is the quest: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1370>
