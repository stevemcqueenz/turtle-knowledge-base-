As you know, Mehlar Dawnblade in the original time line was a Blood Elf with a big hate against Uther Lightbringer, and one of the main quests with him was "Defile Uther's Tomb", cause he hate him for train Arthas in the way of the Paladin.
Plus, he offers some quests exclussive for Paladin Class in the Western Plaguelands that makes sense with the Turtle lore, maybe only needs small retouches.
I guess could be nice add more High Elf quest givers in the plaguelands, I can't see Elves in the Argent Dawn or with the Alliance forces, and you know, the elves are deeply attached with the plague and the undead.
<https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Mehlar_Dawnblade>

Changes:
Name: Mehlar Dawnblade
Race: High Elf
Lore: He was an apprentice of Uther Lightbringer, he saw how Uther trained Arthas un the way of the Paladin, and saw how Uther was killed by Arthas, he get away of the battle before the start by order of Uther and now hates Arthas for that, now he is a Paladin trainer and a Quest giver in his objective of erradicating the scourge of Azeroth.
Location: Chillwind Camp
Main quests: Still pending.
