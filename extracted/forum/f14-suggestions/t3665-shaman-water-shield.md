# Shaman Water Shield

## Post 22176 by Frederick — 2022-08-13T02:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22176#p22176 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Today, I tanked a dungeon on my Shaman while leveling. I noticed that as I drank frequently, my healer would often be around full mana. Likewise, I noticed that Water Shield wasn't available until level 60.

I suggest that Shamans, like Paladins, get a crucial tanking ability at level 20. Paladins get Consecrate, so Shamans should get Water Shield at level 20. This will make tanking as a Shaman more attractive while leveling as it makes for smoother gameplay when the tank isn't constantly going OOM (Earth Shock is expensive).
