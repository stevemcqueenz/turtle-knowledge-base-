# Druid feral talent mis-design

## Post 161183 by berdal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-01T11:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161183#p161183 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey, I spotted a case of the bad game design. Probably done on accident, but here it is:

Feral 2nd row talent [Open Wounds]* should definitely NOT be on second row. Put it on 4th row or swap its location with [Blood Frenzy].

Reason is Cat form (and thus Rip), is attained at level 20. Meaning it serves no purpose on second row for a leveling feral druid. If it's by design (early access if you're down another talent spec), then it should have some effect in Bear form as well. Like for instance Maul could leave a an weak bleed effect (or poison like parasite infestation that lasts longer but does much less damage). Just any effect in Bear. Or simply move it down to 4th row.

 *<Increase the damage of Rip by 5%. In addition, increases the damage of Claw by 10% for each active Bleed effect on the target.>

Thank you for listening to my TED talk.

An idea I'd have there.
Current version:
Increases the damage of Rip by 5/10/15%. In addition, increases the damage of Claw by 10/20/30% for each active Bleed effect on the target.

Potential version:
Increases the damage of Rip by 5/10/15%. In addition, increases the damage of Claw by 10/20/30% for each active Bleed effect on the target.
Your Thorns also cause your target to bleed equal to  33/66/100% of the damage dealt for 3 seconds. Cannot stack.

Why?
Flavorwise I like it because hitting a thorn makes your hand bleed.
Designwise it would make the talent relevant for bears and would help with the bear weakness of multitarget tanking without overbuffing their ability to do so.
