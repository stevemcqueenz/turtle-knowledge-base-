# Ambershire: Is Cross-Faction still necessary? Vote!

## Post 142632 by Captain4k (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-01T23:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142632#p142632 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When it comes to WoW private servers, there's often valid debate surrounding cross-faction and how it impacts the authenticity and gameplay experience of 2 opposing factions at war. With the success of a new realm being uncertain, It's not uncommon for server admins to allow cross-faction gameplay. This is done as a fail-safe measure in the case of a population decline that would hinder players ability to form groups and farm content.

The TWoW team has just announced Ambershire, their new upcoming RP-PVE realm releasing later this month with cross-faction enabled. With Turtle WoW's established success in the Classic+ market and having a thriving population of players, is cross-faction still necessary for their new realm?

I want to know how the community feels. Do you prefer the traditional Warcraft experience with no cross-faction, or should cross-faction be enabled as a fail-safe precaution?

Submit your vote [HERE](https://strawpoll.com/LVyK29Re5Z0) and feel free to express your thoughts and opinions below.
