If devs nerf these donation pets, people will (rightly) cry on the forum. You remember what happened when devs nerfed Goblin Brainwashing Device in random weekly update. While I support your suggestion, it's unlikely to be changed for a few reasons. People have already spent their real money just for one of these specific pets. Items that they spent money on SHOULDN'T be changed no matter what. These pets are actually P2W elements, imagine farming lashers or anything else without them, your bags would be full after a few minutes. Nerfing them would be fair but at the same time they would become less desired. Less desired = no more $$$ for devs. Devs certainly don't want to lose money.

I agree these pets were badly designed from the start but nothing can be done about them at this point without pissing off the players.
