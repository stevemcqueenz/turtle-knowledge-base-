# Make it so that the Shatter talent adds a duration that the Ice can't be destroyed

## Post 162793 by Familymaker — 2025-12-14T18:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162793#p162793 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So I love the shatter trait, but for raids and dungeons it is useless as the frozen condition quickly gets destroyed.

So, how about each point of Shatter not only increases the critical strike chance against the frozen target, but also makes the frozen state unbreakable for a few seconds?

Perhaps 0.6 seconds for every rank of Shatter? Up to a total of 3 seconds that the shatter will not be broken through damage? That would really help out the talent a ton for dungeoneering and raiding!

EDIT: After some more brainwashing, I wanted to instead recommend the following:

 *Add to the Shatter trait that 0.4 second per rank after the ice shatters, that the target also provides the increased crit chance as if they were frozen*.
