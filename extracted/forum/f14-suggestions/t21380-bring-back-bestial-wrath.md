Some people do not want to press 20 buttons, or have a 10 button rotation. I understand for classes like Mage, Warlock, but a major appeal of Hunter and specifically BM Hunter is that the focus is on your pet. Some people who play BM just want to chill and not get exhausted trying to keep up a rotation, including my wife. Please do not go in the direction of Ascension CoA for example... That is completely unplayable, exhausting, and boring at the same time. Bestial Wrath was the only thing that differentiated BM visually from other specs. Please bring it back into the game. My wife is devastated with this news.
