# Give Theramore a Cloth Quartermaster

## Post 139916 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-16T15:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139916#p139916 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Theramore is a faction you can gain reputation with.
The quests you do in Duskwallow Marsh are enough to get you friendly.
So far the only buyable reward is the Theramore Tabard for which you need to be exalted.
As far as I am aware there is no other way currently, other than doing the quests, to gain reputation for Theramore.

Thats why I think Theramore needs a Cloth Quartermaster so you can get exalted with them.
Or give them dailies at least.

Also this suggestion may only focus on Theramore now but if there are other factions that need this they should get it aswell.
