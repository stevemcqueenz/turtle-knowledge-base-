# Dreadmaul Skull Bounty Change

## Post 46823 by Towdon2 — 2023-05-21T03:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46823#p46823 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The quest "Dreadmaul Skull Bounty" has you obtain 30 "Dreadmaul Skull"s from the Dreadmaul Ogre's in Blasted Lands. However only "Dreadmaul Ogre"s drop the skull, ignoring mobs like "Dreadmaul Ogre Mage", "Dreadmaul Brute", or even "Grol the Destroyer". It logically doesn't make sense that none of these ignored mobs don't drop their skulls for the quest.
