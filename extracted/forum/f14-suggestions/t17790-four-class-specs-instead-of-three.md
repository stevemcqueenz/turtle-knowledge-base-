# Four class specs instead of three?

## Post 121262 by Na'Tali — 2025-03-01T05:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121262#p121262 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Like how retail had a split to Feral druids so that bear tanking and cat DPS are completely different specs, but also a change to other classes to have 4 specs? The specs we have feel a bit squished together with 3...

 **Rogues** could have a proper tanking spec (I've tanked as a rogue during Cataclysm and dabbled with the available survivability talents during Wrath, so I am pretty much THE person to ask for this one) instead of having all of their defensive abilities scattered around the 3 specs. The tanking spec would require a "stance" ability that locks you out of Stealth & Vanish for its activation, and for 1 minute after it is dropped. (because **NO ONE** wants to go up against a rogue that is both Stealthy AND Tanky ***AT THE SAME TIME!***)
Also Combat could possibly be removed and replaced with a ranged DPS option based around throwing knives/axes. (that one is just spitballing)
I had just put together this talent tree earlier today as a replacement for the Combat spec, but it could be used as a baseline for the new possible tanking spec: <https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/_0rWKXyORw>

 **Shamans** could have a proper split for Enhancement so that 2 handed & dual wielding DPS would be its own thing, while shield & totem based earthly resilience tanking could be its own thing as well.

 **Mages** could have a chronomancy spec that heals and does the Time Warp (mage bloodlust in retail) ability as its final talent ability. Heal Over Time and damage-absorption-and-later-taking-it-over-time style abilities for this spec.

 **Priests** could delve into Necromancy, or even do stuff like how Trolls call upon the Loa. Damage spec.

 **Paladins** could have a proper "Shockadin" ranged spell DPS spec.

 **Druids** could have the same as retail; 4 specs, with Feral being the cat DPS spec and bear tanking being its own spec.

 **Hunter** could have a proper Master & Pet mixed tanking spec with the pet getting a portion of the defensive stats of the Hunter; and the abilities would be more focused on melee tanking survivability, with switching to the Pet for ranged support.

Admittedly, I do not know what **Warlock** and **Warrior** would get for a 4th spec... probably a mixed tanking spec akin to Hunters or a pet-oriented healing spec for Warlocks, and maybe a weapon throwing based ranged/melee mixed DPS spec?

Druids, Shamans, and Paladins could be the only true All In One classes with a spec for each kind of situation!   turtle_in_love_head
Balance Druids, "Shockadin" Paladins, and Elemental Shamans for spell damage.
Protection Paladins, Guardian (Bear) Druids, and (classic & turtle style shield) Enhancement Shamans for tanking.
(classic style 2h & retail 1h dual wielding) "Enhancement" Shaman, Feral (Cat) Druids, and Retribution Paladin for melee damage.
Restoration Druids & Shamans, and Holy Paladins for healing.

And if **Demon Hunters** and **Death Knights** were to be added further down the line:
A spell DPS spec for Demon Hunters, and I have no clue for a 4th spec on that one... (maybe healing?)
A spell DPS spec for Death Knights so that Paladins do not get ALL of the spell plate.   turtle_tongue_head
