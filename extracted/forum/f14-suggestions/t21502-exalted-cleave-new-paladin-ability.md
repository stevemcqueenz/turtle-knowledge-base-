Okay so after thinking about it for a while.  I'd rather not start a new post for this edit so here goes:

Exalted Cleave (Rank 1)
 30 Mana, 6 Second Cooldown
Instant Cast, 5 yd range
Requires melee weapon
Consecrates your weapon with holy power, inflicting
 10 - 20 holy damage plus 30% of your melee attack power
to the target and their nearest ally.
Exalted cleave also grants you a 5% bonus to your block
chance for 20 seconds.
Exalted Cleave shares a cooldown with holy strike and
crusader strike.

Rank 2: 20 - 30 Holy Damage +30% MAP, +7% block chance. 60 mana.
Rank 3: 30 - 40 Holy Damage +30% MAP, +10% block chance. 90 mana.
Rank 4: 40 - 50 Holy Damage +30% MAP, +12% block chance. 120 mana.
Rank 5: 60 - 70 Holy Damage +30% MAP, +15% block chance. 150 mana.
