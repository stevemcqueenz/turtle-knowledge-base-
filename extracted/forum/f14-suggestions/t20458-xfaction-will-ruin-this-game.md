# Xfaction will ruin this game.

## Post 141248 by 987004590 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-25T09:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141248#p141248 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know some people are trying to solve the issue of unilateral battlefield domination this way, but the truth is it won't work. Even with Xfaction, if one side has pre-made groups, the outcome is still obvious. The next step in balancing it can only be to eliminate prefabrication.
The final battlefield will be a chaotic free-for-all between two uncoordinated, disorganized, and utterly incompetent mobs. When that happens, true PVP enthusiasts will definitely leave the game. What will follow is the demise of PVP.

Unlike PvE, a thriving PvP environment should focus on the needs of advanced players, encouraging ordinary players to progress towards higher skill levels, rather than catering to casual players by diminishing the experience for top-tier players.

I believe the most ideal solution for the current PVP environment remains cross-server battles. To make it more viable, one side of the battle must consist of players from the same server and same faction, which would eliminate the possibility of cross-faction or cross-server trading. This also gives both sides of the battle a stronger sense of belonging and honor. Only in this way will players strive to become stronger, instead of complaining about how strong the enemy is every day.
