This is a lore-directed suggestion to help keep immersion and class fantasy planted in the spirit of Twow.

I shouldn't see Night Elves chopping down trees, especially druids.

I'd recommend changing their logging animation to the 'healing touch' or perhaps something where they interact with whisps or natural energy.

Im not sure the Tauren policy on logging, but Im sure its safe to assume druidic taurens understand the natural and environmental implications.

Source: WC3 Nelf Campaign.

Small details like this is what ensures world building, class fantasy, and lore all remain a perfect trinity for the end user experience.
