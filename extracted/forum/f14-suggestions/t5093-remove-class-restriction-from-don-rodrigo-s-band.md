# Remove class restriction from Don Rodrigo's Band

## Post 30852 by Snakeman (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-12-17T00:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30852#p30852 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As someone about to step into AQ40 on my elemental shaman, I'm left in a bit of a panic over spell penetration. The nature resistance of every boss in AQ40 is unfathomably high in comparison to earlier raids' bosses, and I have nowhere near enough spell penetration available to me to compensate for this. Our tanks don't have Thunderfury, and I currently only have around 30 spell pen available to me on the gear I currently own, not to mention gaining zero benefit from warlocks' curses. I hate being made to feel useless in a raid setting, and I really don't feel comfortable being shoehorned into healing, especially not this late in the game.

I could remedy this somewhat by getting my hands on [Don Rodrigo's Band](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=21563) from AV rep - 20 spell pen on a ring is pretty fat! - but it's locked to priest/mage/warlock only. Because of course it is. Shaman mains aren't allowed to have nice things ever /rant

My suggestion, simply, is to remove the class restriction! As far as I can tell it doesn't seem to have been suggested before, or I'm sure it would be gone already. Laser chickens and shockadins could also make use of it, even if only for the spell crit. Would make the AV grind actually worthwhile for me as an ele shaman, considering I already have way better offhands than what's available from exalted rep.
