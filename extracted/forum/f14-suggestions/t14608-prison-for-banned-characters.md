# Prison for banned characters

## Post 99706 by Pngu — 2024-07-17T00:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99706#p99706 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It would be interesting if banned characters would be teleported to an instanced prison that would function essentially just like a prison. You would be able to:

 - Gamble with other banned characters for some form of currency;
 - On the yard zone they could brawl to force other players to hand over their currency (and by doing that risk getting additional ban time from the npc guards if they catch him);
 - The currency could be traded (from an npc prisoner inside a specifc cell) for a shiv, reduced ban time, visitors time (which would allow for smuggling white items into the prison);
 - Not banned players could watch the banned ones inside and gamble the outcomes of fights etc;
 - On the yard zone other players could pass by and throw stuff on the banned people just for fun;
 - On the last day of prison time, before the player can leave the prison, he would have a walk of shame around his capital city where everybody could just make fun and laugh;
 - This instance should be slightly annoying to discourage people from getting banned, and slighly fun that would encourage people to still log onto their characters just for the laughs;

I think it's a pretty funny idea   happy_turtle_head
