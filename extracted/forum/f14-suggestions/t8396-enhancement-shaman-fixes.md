## Post 54120 by Regaldandin — 2023-07-20T12:13:00Z

New 31 point talent for enhancement - TotemCraft

 1 minute cooldown, 30% base mana
- Consumes your actively placed totems to create a Totem Golem

- Totem Golems last until killed or dismissed minutes

- You may have one Totem Golem active at any time

- While the totem golem is alive, you cannot cast any totem abilities

- Totem golems can be commanded as a pet, and come with the standard pet bar abilities of passive,defensive,agressive, attack, stop, stay. along with additional abilities based on the totem golem created

- Totem golems can be right click - dismissed

- The totem golem changes based on the totems used to create it
