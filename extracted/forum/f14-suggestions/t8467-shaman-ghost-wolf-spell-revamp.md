# Shaman Ghost Wolf spell revamp

## Post 54565 by Thegr8equalizer — 2023-07-25T08:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54565#p54565 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

I am a bit tired of the state of shaman on turtle wow, especially compared to other hybrids like druids or paladins. A lot of the members of this community been suggesting and brain storming changes for the class but alas none have been taken into consideration so far and the last changes touching shaman class are nerfs to some of their gearing options...

On this topic I **want to talk about Ghost wolf spell and how I think it should be change and more specifically buffed into order to shine against other types of "increased" speed spells.**

First of all, **Let us compare the spell to other similar options**, the criterias are easy:
 1) the spell should be **infinite duration**
 2) the spell should be **available while in combat** (no mount, no plainrunning)
 3) the spell should **not have any CD**
 4) the spell should **increase run speed on ground** (no swim speed/mount speed only)

Considering these criterias, only 4 other spells in Wow fit:

A) **Aspect of the cheetah/Aspect of the Pack**: 30% increase movespeed, instant, 40 mana lvl 20 (cheetah), 100 mana lvl 40 (pack), dazed 4 sec if you take dmg. Can get 6% increased speed with BM talent (2/2) at 10 points.

B)**Cat form with Feline swiftness** (10 pt feral 2/2): 30% increase movespeed OUTDOORS, instant, 55% of base mana, lvl 20, Shapeshifting in and out removes all snares/roots effects, immune to sheep.

C)**Travel form**: 40% increase movespeed OUTDOORS, instant, 20% of base mana, lvl 30, Shapeshifting in and out removes all snares/roots effects, immune to sheep. can get up to 61% increased movespeed with PvP set bonus.

Now let's see **Ghost wolf**:

 40% increase movespeed OUTDOOR, 3 sec cast (nature school), 100 mana, lvl 20. Can get up to 61% increased movespeed with PvP set bonus. Can get cast time down to 1 sec with Imp GW in enha tree 10 pt (2/2).

Now comparing these spells I cannot help but feel like there is a major point of difference between ghost wolf and all the other same type speed boost spells: **THEY ARE ALL INSTANT**.

Meanwhile, **baseline Ghost wolf is the SAME cast time as a MOUNT**.

 **On top of that, its on the main spell school of shaman, the same spell school that includes their heals, interrupt, purge, and main dps spells** (CL and LB). Meaning its extremely risky to put it out in combat.

Meaning between the slow cast time with spellpushback + the fact the spell is on the main spell school of shaman, you will almost never actually cast it when you need to (for example when you wanna chase or kite something).

This makes the base spell pretty much useless outside of extremely niche stuff (like shaman fcing/ fishing with water walking) after you get mount at lvl 40. Meanwhile, **hunter cheetah and druid travel form are staples of their classes and shape their identity and playstyle in both pve and pvp making them extremely good kiters**.

Now the other issue is also that, on top of being **instant**, both cheetah and travel forms have other MAJOR strengths that put them a whole category above ghost wolf. For travel form, it acts as a **full root/snare dispel** on demand, and makes you **immune to sheep**, making the druid extremely hard to pin down/cc.For cheetah, its **available INDOORS** making it extremely versatile as it's the only speed spell of this category that allows that.

Now what does ghost wolf have over the two others? Nothing. Its actually even worse, because you can DISPEL ghost wolf, whereas you cannot dispel cheetah nor travel/pack aspect.

You can now begin to see how unfair and gimped ghost wolf is. Probably because it is a relic of Alpha wow that was never modified as vanilla patches came one after the others...

This is why I would like to introduce 3 changes to the spells:

I)**The school of the spell should be "physical"**, meanings kicking it would not kick you out of nature school spells, meaning you may also cast it while silenced.

II)**The spell cast should ignore pushback or alternatively, should be 1 sec baseline**. As I said earlier, there is no reason why ghost wolf has to have a cast time (and one that's super slow) when you compare it to all the other similar spells, especially considering the fact ghost wolf can be dispel and does not have built-in cc immunity/removal.

III)To mirror other forms of speed spells, Ghost wolf should have a special "unique" property. I would suggest that **ghost wolf form prevents snares from lowering shaman runspeed below normal runspeed, meaning ghost wolf would at worst give you 100% runspeed, you'd still be vulnerable to roots**. This would massively help Enha spec which lacks gap closers or anyways to stick on the target for more than one auto attack while also helping healing shamans that lets make us remember have no defensive cd and no hard cc either baseline.

IV)Imp ghost wolf should be revamped to: **Make ghost wolf instant and gives shaman a moderate burst of speed upon shifting on a cd similar to charge/blink/feral charge**. Again, the idea is to help enha with closing the gaps with their target without turning them into unstoppable godlike machines with unmitigitable true dmg and full invulnerability (ret paladins).

Now for a little conclusion, I also though about the lack of "flavor" of ghost wolf in vanilla and I though of a few more changes that could help that issue:

Since the very beginning of wow, players in ghost form have been able to walk on water, they naturally stay on top of water and run on it. Aside this point, Ghost mobs in wow tend to float in the air, meaning they are "lighter" than your average physical body. Since ghost wolf is incorporeal, it would make sense that like the ghost of dead players, it would float naturally over water.

For this reason, I suggest two things:

 1) ghost wolf should **allow to water walk baseline without having to cast any seperate spell**.
 2) ghost wolf should be **available to cast for free when shaman is in ghost form (after releasing spirit when dead)** (think of it like a shaman "Wisp spirit" spell).
