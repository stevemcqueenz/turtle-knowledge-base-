# Player made quests + Players can be quest givers!

## Post 535 by Qesley — 2018-10-07T12:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=535#p535 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

☼ All quests must be tied to the lore of the zone
☼ All quests must be pre-Approved

The greatest potential for this idea is that some quests may use another player as the quest giver and other times the quests can be read from a board / signpost. This will further embed RP into the game even deeper! Imagine having to conversate with a real person who is unpredictable instead of an NPC that always behaves the same. The possibilities are endless!

If a player decides to do this they should be rewarded gold and xp just like the other participants (maybe a little more) and because the player is the quest giver they should remain in the quest giving spot a certain amount of time. Maybe the longer they wait there the more XP they get.

Anyway I think its worth discussing if this idea could be implemented and how it should be implemented, so please let me know what you guys think.

Usually this is done in the form of a storyline in roleplaying, where the reward is usually a fun experience but rarely there can be tangible rewards as well. I can see the system also being exploited by people to powerlevel, and it being pretty hard to code in the first place.

If you want to send people on quests, I would recommend finding something unique (like maybe a rare mob) and having some rewards to pass out, then advertising it in character. It doesn't always work, but if it does, it can be pretty fulfilling.
