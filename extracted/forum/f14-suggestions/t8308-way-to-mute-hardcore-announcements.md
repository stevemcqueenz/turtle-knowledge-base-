# Way to mute hardcore announcements?

## Post 53543 by Pnpgamer — 2023-07-15T14:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53543#p53543 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello
Is there a way to mute all the notifications of what happens to hardcore characters, ie. "A character [name] has reached level 20!" or "tragedy has occurred, a character died at level 35"?

## Post 53546 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-15T15:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53546#p53546 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Type in chat: .hcm 70
.hcm sets the level threshold where you get these hardcore messages. Setting it to 20 will already cut out most spam, 40 removes most uninteresting messages, 70 turns them all off.
