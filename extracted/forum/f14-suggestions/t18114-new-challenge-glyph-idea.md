# New challenge glyph idea

## Post 123275 by RMan360 — 2025-03-22T03:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123275#p123275 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Was playing through with a mate and talking about the challenge glyphs and thought that a 'hardcore soul-link' mode where you bind to another player in hardcore would be neat. At the most basic level of implementation it would just be that when one of you dies, the other does too. Would be really cool if you could also link quest logs and xp/rep gains but I'd understand if that's an impossible task. Reward could be something like a simple title or maybe as extravagant as a 2 person mount?

## Post 123369 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-23T05:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123369#p123369 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I believe 2 person mounts aren't allowed in this client. They were introduced in WotLK.
Linked quest log is interesting feature. Though it require more thought.
For example, will both players in party be able to turn quest items for reward?
Imagine quest to collect 10 heads of murlocks.
It might cause problems when player A collect 10 heads and player B stay in town and talk to quest giver. Will this quest be completed?
Each player in duo should get 10 heads (total 20) or only one player in duo should get them (total 10).

Next situation: player A is in town and player B somewhere far in the field. Player A turn in quest, who will get experience: both players or only A?

Right now, quest items (like heads of murlocks) are distributed in round-robin rule and they are not tradable. So getting 10 heads for the quest for each player will be as hard as it now.

If both players should get enough quest items, then game should not allow player A (with 10 heads) to complete quest while player B (with 0 heads). I see explaining issues here. Like "I have 10 heads, why can't I complete it?". There should be clear indication of progress for both players.

So in the end, idea is interesting, but devil is in details. Lets just keep it vanilla way.
I also don't know any other game with cooperative quest progress, except session-based games like Deep Rock Galactic.
