**[Glyph of Hindrance]**
 1 Copper
 *(Available at all levels)*

"Age or injury has taken its toll. The limbs do not obey as they used to.
You accept your condition as it is, until you are freed from its hindrance one
way or another. The mounts you own still carry you faithfully without exception."

 **What does this glyph do?**
 -Running speed is reduced by 50%, walking speed remains the same. Running appears like a faster walk animation to give a hobbling effect.

 **Why implement this glyph?**
 -To sell the act of roleplaying an elderly character or an injured warrior after a noble battle or Mak'gora.
