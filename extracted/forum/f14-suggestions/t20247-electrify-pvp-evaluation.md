# Electrify pvp evaluation

## Post 138752 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-11T16:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138752#p138752 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

DISCLAIMER: This is coming from an elemental shaman who primarily plays for pvp

So after 8? months of CC2 and the supposed nerf of shaman burst through the Electrify talent, lets take a look at the result:

- First, the gameplay. Lets say fight start with a LB cast. Unless you want to spam rank1 LBs to keep stacks up, you then need time for either a 2sec cast and 1-3 sec airtime before LB lands, or 1,5s if you cast LC which has a 5s cd. In the first scenario, that means you effectively have a window of 10-12 seconds to do other hybrid things - heal, totems, shocks - before you have to cast another LB or CL to remain dmg relevant. It is awful gameplay. Also consider this spell design means resists, immunes etc dont generate a stack = if this happens, you lose your stack(s). This scenario ofcourse does not account for the (very likely) chance that you get attacked or CC'd in this situation - which ofcourse usually also leads to losing the stacks.

- Did it nerf shaman burst? It made us slightly weaker in the initial stages of a fight but made the whole gameplay dependent on intervals of casting LB and CL if we want to stay dmg relevant. This often leads to a forced choice of helping teammates or trying to keep electrify stacks up. If we miss a spell, we need to start over and build stacks again. Completely awful gameplay.
Lets take a look at what the patch also introduced:
- First, 2 stacks grants an additional 10% damage.
Second, Stormstrike gives an additional 20% for 2 spells.
Third, the 8/8 T3 ele set makes shocks give stacks and adds 10% dmg increase to shocks for each stack. This is significant gatekeeping for players who are fresh or don’t have access to Naxx.
Add the BWL trinket to this combo and I can understand why people cry about shamans even more now. Twow devs in their efforts to compensate for Electrify created something entirely worse.

These changes did not fix elemental damage for pvp. The only thing they did is make the first two spells slightly weaker and lock players into a uniquely weird playstyle (keeping stacks up) to remain damage relevant. CC2 has for good reason been accused of increased burst and its things like these - thinking you fix pvp but instead create new monsters and painful playstyles - that are the issue.

I suggest:
- Remove Electrify, reinstate Elemental Fury and remove the 10% bonus

- if yes to above, move Stormstrike further down the tree so elemental shamans can not spec 30/21

- if yes to above, change the naxx 8/8 set bonus into something else. 8set bonuses giving game-altering abilities like these is bad design to begin with. It’s a pve set - give mana discount on offensive spells or something.
Tldr: Electrify was very poorly designed and has created a wonky gameplay that is unique for elemental shamans in a bad way. Bring elemental fury back in, and remove access to the %dmg bonuses, particularly stormstrike. This will honestly likely be a slight nerf to shamans in the current dogshit pvp burstiness but it will atleast improve (restore) the gameplay incredibly.
