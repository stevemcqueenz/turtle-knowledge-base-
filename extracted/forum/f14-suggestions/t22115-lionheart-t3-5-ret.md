 1) Crusader strike procs a crusade (5.5-6s cd from CS cd)
 2) After hitting 3 stacks, cannot refresh. After expires 15 s CD.

Example: CStrike> 5.5 sec of 1 stack (15 sec duration max) > Cstrike> 5.5 sec 2 stacks (refreshes 15 sec duration) > holy strike, does nothing> CS > 11 sec after last CS, 3 stacks now (lasts 15 sec). After expires ICD 15 seconds (or what ever you want). 5.5 (1 stack), 11 sec (2 stack), 15 sec (3 stack), 15 sec (no stacks). Averages 3.1% increase in speed - can be tuned with ICD Just keep in mind break points 5.5 or 6 second for strike cd.
