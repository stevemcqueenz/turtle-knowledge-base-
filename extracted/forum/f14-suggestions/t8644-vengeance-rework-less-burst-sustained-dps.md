# Vengeance rework - less burst, sustained dps

## Post 55699 by Isvya (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-03T20:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55699#p55699 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We know that paladin burst is a big issue atm partly due to vengeance double dipping (partly due to HS armor pen - ignore this part now pls)

The idea would be to rework vengeance similar to the TBC one.

Make it stack up to 5 times, each stack increasing your holy and physical damage done by 3%, lasting 15 or 20 seconds.

This would reduce the pvp burst potential by not having 15% double dip straight away and would help pve specs to maintain a steady uptime on vengeance.

This is coming from someone who plays a paladin btw.
