# Can we buff some niche items while waiting for Set Changes?

## Post 88922 by Verdict — 2024-02-28T11:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88922#p88922 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey guys, this a little bit of a Paladin Tank post more than anything but I believe it'll be a good talk not just from my perspective but also from everyone else's and their classes.

I am currently using items that are of such far tier behind what I'm currently raiding in and my spec truly forces me to use them because I have no other choice, but in reality better options are available. I need to provide more context.

 **Patchwerk Fight is a good motivator for this post.**

Patch is a crazy sensitive Armor to Health ratio fight, my biggest issue there is that I have to use high stamina/armor items which sacrifice so much threat/avoidance all for the sake of simply surviving the fight. I know this will be addressed probably with Set changes etc, but it's a simple example of the current state of the fight as a meme spec.

 **In reality what I aim with this spot is probably an ilvl buff of some paladin tank items that could use some love and we all use:**
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19912> - **Overlord's Onyx Ban**d is a blue item and it's set are literally a Paladin dream.
 - The only thing that breaks this set is if you require high Stamina gear (Spell Damage/High Damage fights) or get a better ring like Ring of Emperor Vek'lor to break the effect. The reality is that this is THE ring on paper, but you cannot use the best ring in the game because of low stamina numbers and in higher raids, it's not a good choice.

<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=91777> - Araxxna's Husk.
 - Yes this is a druid item... a druid item that I must use on Patchwerk-like fights simply because of the stamina, without it I create a situation where I might get one shot even with full preparations. You never know when the Hateful + auto will clip bad and kill you.

AQ40 Set 3/3: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=redeemed+proph>
(2) Set: 14 Damage and Heal
(3) Set: 2% haste and 12 defense.
 - this will soon become useless in it's entirety. It's not really used at all and I believe a good reshuffle/re-budget of the set effects will make it quite good. Personally I'd like an UP in the stamina numbers, get rid of the Set effects for damage and re-purpose it for tanks as no Ret would use it, neither a Spelladin, nor a Healer.

Hero's Brand and Zandalari Set effects: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19588>
 - Honestly just look at Warrior's alternative will make you wonder why not. Yes it's used by Rets in PvP but still, there's perhaps some work on it that could be done.

 **Just some final remarks and probably an advice for the devs on Paladin Set reworks:**
A tank that doesn't care about being Crit is a bad meta to enforce Defense as a stat to tanks. In reality Paladins need Stamina and Avoidance on good Hybrid Pieces. Defense is considered quite meme and with Resilience items being in the game the moment, it's even furthermore solidified as such.

Thanks for the read!
