# Give Dissolvent Poison Flat Armor Pen

## Post 170385 by Kakarotto — 2026-03-09T11:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170385#p170385 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Rather than give rogues' dissolvent poison 100% armor penetration, give it a flat number like 2000 armor pen which should be able to ignore most mobs armors after being sundered/exposed armor without bypassing the only damage reduction of high armor wearing classes like warrior. This change would allow rogues to perform their same DPS in raids/dungeons while not being too overpowered in solo content or in PvP.

## Post 170388 by Cfishet — 2026-03-09T12:21:00Z

Honestly an idea like this would be much healthier, especially in PvP. At the very least it would be much more healthier than the meta-dominant Assassination Dissolvent Poison build you see in PvP right now. I could also see a chance of it revitalizing other rogue specs and poison combination builds.

Obviously some fine-tuning like armor penetration number values would be needed. But this would still be far better than the current design we have. I don’t think the current design can be fixed by simply tuning numbers, since the poison is phyiscal dmg, spammable, unmitigable and guaranteed to proc, is overpowered by its core design rather than its numbers.
