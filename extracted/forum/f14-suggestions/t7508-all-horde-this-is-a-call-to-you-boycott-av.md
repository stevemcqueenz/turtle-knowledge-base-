At this point I know that the people whining have no interest in actually ever improving and would rather just keep whining, blaming their failures on others and demanding to have the BG just turned into a free win for them, but here goes anyway.

Here's a simple example strategy that will almost always put the Horde in an advantageous position, though it does assume the entire Horde team is playing instead of the usual 10+ AFKs

![Image](https://image.noelshack.com/fichiers/2023/22/2/1685451158-1.jpg)
The first step is to use both IB's notoriously easy to defend location as well as the Alliance attempts to rush to it (as they're used to the Horde never trying to win) to the Horde's advantage. A properly defended IB will not fall, at least not permanently, and SH is rarely actually defended with the exception of the odd gigachads trying to stop a rush game from happening (which leads to a completely different set of strategies, but also means IB will go defended)
Considering the proximity of a GY and the cave, numerical inferiority will not be an issue at IB so your team can safely send a handful of people to tag SH, will will be important for our next step
![Image](https://image.noelshack.com/fichiers/2023/22/2/1685451158-2.jpg)
With SH tagged, any Alliance death at IB will be permanent as they'll be sent back to the north of the map. if IB was holding until then, you'll wipe the Alliance relatively easily. If it was tagged, you should still be able to grind the alliance that's still there down eventually as they no longer have respawns available. Once IB is secured, the bulk of the Horde should move to SHGY, SH bunker can be dealt with later once the Horde has a reasonable foothold at IW. The small team from earlier must hold the flag at all costs until the rest of their allies arrive (this is why it's a good idea to send your best few ahead in the previous step)
![Image](https://image.noelshack.com/fichiers/2023/22/2/1685451158-3.jpg)
Once SHGY is fully captured, the Horde can now move slightly forward and hold the infamous IW high ground position. This is extremely difficult to take back for the alliance and also cannot be circumvented. From this spot, the Horde can prevent any advance from the Alliance as long as they wish to.

This doesn't give you a win, but it puts you in a position where you have full control of the BG and how it goes from there. This is also very easy to execute and will work easily 90% of the time with the current meta, but it does require an entire team actually playing the game which you simply won't get as the Horde
