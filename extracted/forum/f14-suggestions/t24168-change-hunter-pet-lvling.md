# Change hunter pet Lvling

## Post 170049 by Koilos33 — 2026-03-04T14:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170049#p170049 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**The current pet lvling system is as follows:**
You, a lvl 60 hunter, want to try out plainstriders as a pet. They got a new skill this patch, so you want to see how it goes in raid. So you go outside to tame a plainstrider. The highest lvl plainstrider is lvl20 (a rare btw, the rest are max lvl19), so you tame him. He remains lvl 20 once tamed, and you have to lvl it up. For your new pet to gain any EXP at all, you need to kill mobs that would give **you** EXP, which you now have to do without a big part of your dmg output, and tanking yourself, since a lvl 20 pet dies in 1 hit and can't land any dmg. Keep farming for days and if you don't give up or die of boredom, you can now test that pet in raid.

 **Suggestion:**
What I suggest is to change to the blizz system that was implemented in later expansions, so pets instantly get to playerlvl - 2 when tamed. Either that or make it so you have to kill npcs in your pets lvl range instead of yours so it's easier.

This is a small change (in my opinion) that would allow hunters to try out new playstyles and pet skills, hunt for rares or unique appearances, or pets with specific attack speeds without it feeling like a prison sentence.
I recently tamed the rare "Bayne" (lvl10) on my hunter alt (37 at the start of lvling) because I wanted a pink darkhound. I've been "forced" to change talents to Survival and fight alone, and now I'm 41 and my pet is still 10 lvls below mine. The system in place tries to dissuade you from that kind of change for no real reason, which kills a big part of the fun of being a hunter.
