# Concept: Making All Professions Feel Unique in Late Game

## Post 163997 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-29T15:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163997#p163997 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi there,

In my experience, all professions besides Engineering feel dull in the late game, and I think the reason is a lack of identity. What I mean by that is the absence of everyday items—like explosives for Engineers—that actively improve gameplay and constantly remind the player of their profession. Engineers are always aware that they are Engineers, because they use unique items that only they can use.

With other professions, this feeling is mostly missing. As a Blacksmith, for example, I sometimes forget that I even have the profession. I crafted the items I needed while leveling, and after that it mostly turns into a gold-making tool or a one-time utility like “yes, I can craft an Arcanite Rod.” There is very little reason to actively use my profession in daily gameplay, since nearly all consumables and enhancements can simply be bought from the auction house.

Because of this, I would like to suggest giving every profession stronger uniqueness by adding profession-specific bonuses or items that can only be used by characters who actually have that profession.

Enchanting
> **Enchanting Mastery: Shoulder Enchantments**
>  *Passive*
>  Allows the application of any armor enchantment to your shoulder slot for your own gear at Enchanting skill 300.

Blacksmithing
> **Balanced Sharpening Stone**
>  Use:
>
>  One-Handed Weapons: +4 damage and 0.5% critical strike chance.
>
>  Two-Handed Weapons: +8 damage and 1% critical strike chance.

Leatherworking
> **Swift Armor Kit**
>  Use:
>  Permanently enchants chest, legs, hands, or feet armor to increase attack speed and casting speed by 1%.
>  Requires Leatherworking (300).
>  Only usable by Leatherworkers.

Alchemy
> **Alchemist’s Health Potion**
>  Requires Level 55
>  Use:
>  Restores 1250 to 2050 health.
>  Only usable by Alchemists.

> **Alchemist’s Mana Potion**
>  Requires Level 55
>  Use:
>  Restores 1550 to 2450 mana.
>  Only usable by Alchemists.

Tailoring
> **Tailor’s Bags**
>  Passive
>  Requires Tailoring (300)
>  Adds 2 additional slots to each equipped bag.

Jewelcrafting
> **Gemcraft Mastery**
>  Passive
>  Requires Jewelcrafting (300)
>  Increases all stats by 2 for each gemstone applied to equipped gear.

Herbalism
> **Herbalist’s Momentum**
>  Passive
>  Requires Herbalism (300)
>  Gathering herbs has a 20% chance to increase movement speed and mounted movement speed by 5% for 5 minutes.
>  Effect stacks up to 25%.

Mining
> **Miner’s Resolve**
>  Passive
>  Requires Mining (300)
>  Gathering ores has a 20% chance to grant immunity to dismount and daze effects for 5 minutes.
>  Duration stacks up to 25 minutes.

Skinning
> **Skinner’s Nerve**
>  Passive
>  Requires Skinning (300)
>  Skinning beasts has a 10% chance to increase resistance to charm and fear effects by 10% for 5 minutes.
>  Stacks up to 50%.

Cooking
> **Gourmet**
>  Passive
>  Requires Cooking (300)
>  Increases health and mana regeneration from cooked food and drink by 20%.

First Aid
> **Field Medic**
>  Passive
>  Requires First Aid (300)
>  Increases bandage channeling speed by 20%.

Fishing
> **Snatching Fish**
>  Active
>  Requires Fishing (300)
>  2 min cooldown
>  5 yd range
>  Instantly catches half to all fish from a fish school you swim through.
>  Usable while shapeshifted.

Survival
> **Survivalist**
>  Passive
>  Requires Survival (300)
>  Increases stealth detection, movement speed, and swimming speed in the open world.
