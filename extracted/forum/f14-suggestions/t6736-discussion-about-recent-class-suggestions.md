Hello,

First of all I'd like to inquire how does suggestion system in Turtle WoW actually work. Is there a process of admins/developers listening to players' ideas and suggestions? Are there any polls? Who decides what will be implemented/changed?

Recently I've found an increasing number of topics about class alternations which dangerously mimic those of retail WoW. In general - erasing uniqueness of classes, balancing them/making them fair.
I want to express how dangerous and disturbing this is, in my opinion.

E.g. there have been ("very loud") threads about paladins wanting to have tanking abilities as effective as warriors in addition to their AoE tanking powers. In one sentence "gimme warrior taunt already!".
Another thread about nerfing druids' shapeshifting etc.

There has been many more.
My plea is to avoid making classes bland by adjusting their specific strengths/weaknesses.

I hope I'm not alone who is disturbed by these louder and louder demands.

Thank you.
