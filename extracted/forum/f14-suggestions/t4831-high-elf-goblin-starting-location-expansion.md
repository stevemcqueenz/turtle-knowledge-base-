# High elf & Goblin starting location expansion

## Post 29035 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-12T12:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29035#p29035 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

I would like to suggest two things:
 1st one is allowing other Alliance classes to do the HE starting area quests (or at least some of them).

Secondly, I suggest expanding the areas a bit with a few more quests, bringing the leveling experience, at least for the HEs up a level or two. Ideally, to level 10 so Loch Modan is also an option instead of Elwynn, but I guess that is too much work. The goblin area needs some love. Since the beginning, it has been an amazing take on a starting area. Blends in with vanilla completely, but at the same time unique in how you are working for a villainous faction and ... no spoilers. However, the area should be enlargened a bit, so it doesn't seem like a side-platformer which is kind of the feeling right now. Also the same goes for a few more quests as with the HE's.

Thanks for reading!
