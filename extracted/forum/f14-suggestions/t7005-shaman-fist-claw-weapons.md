# Shaman fist/claw weapons

## Post 44376 by Skjaldbjorn — 2023-05-04T03:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44376#p44376 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Pretty simple suggestion.  If we go back to WC3, a single fist/claw weapon was the iconic weapon of the shaman.  I would love to see some fist weapons (preferably of the claw style) added to the game with stats appealing to shaman.

Most fist weapons seem to be designed for DWing, which I really don't want for shaman.  They are also fairly niche and not commonly used by the classes that can DW.

Elemental and restoration would be simple, claws with +healing/spell damage.

Enhancement has some room for creativity.   Maybe set pieces with an OH that buffs the main hand by adding damage, debuffs, etc.  This part is probably asking a lot.  More than anything I would just like for shaman to get fist weapon designed for them since it is such an iconic weapon for them and not something widely used by any other class.
