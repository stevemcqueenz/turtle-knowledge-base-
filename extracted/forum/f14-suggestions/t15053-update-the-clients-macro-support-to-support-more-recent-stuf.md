# Update the clients macro support to support more recent stuff.

## Post 102343 by Fugus (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-10-02T00:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102343#p102343 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know this would basically just be a new addon but it is one that would help plenty of people out given how the macro addons each seem to be missing important stuff that the newer versions of the client supports. Example:

#Showtooltip
/cast [mod:shift] Prowl; [stealth] Pounce; Claw;

There is not a single way I can find to get every component in it to work.
#showtooltip works on CleverMacros but doesn't with Roid
[stealth] works in Roid but not Clever and so on.

Or maybe
#showtooltip
/cast [swimming] Aquatic Form; Travel Form;

Haven't bothered to try Stance macros yet to see if they work but having to choose whether I want to see the macro icon or actually not have to gut part of the moves because the tags aren't registered is frustrating to say the least.

As an extension of this.

When the 2.0 client is released, I STRONGLY recommend you guys include your own macro setups into it while blocking many of the current addons that are allowed now.

You are in some weird situation where basic tags or commands are limited but you can also do a full lua commands that allows you to automate entire rotations and priority lists.

So macros are both too limited for basic stuff and too open for the most advanced stuff possible.

Having a macro detect if you are swimming, in stealth, in combat, or if your target is friendly or not is understandable.

If you have a macro that checks their debuffs and applies them in order if they are missing and checks your mana/rage/energy and combo points and does different moves depending on how much the resources you have and applying the important buffs you have and all, is too much and just shy of botting at that point.

Same with the old totem stomping macros that hunters used to do, not sure if they work here but betting they are.
