How about possibility to put bounty on players. A Bounty Hunters Guild.

You pay gold in that will be reward for the kill and specify character's name. If another player accepts the contract in the Bounty Hunter Guild then the target will be pvp flagged for him/her. Faction doesn't matter so can kill allied players. Kill, get the reward.

Could require quest chain to become Bounty Hunter. Any players can place a bounty, but only hunters can complete.

 1 bounty put per day per player, and level requirement (like 10)
Can't accept bounties from alts.

Special skill: tracked target will appear on minimap
