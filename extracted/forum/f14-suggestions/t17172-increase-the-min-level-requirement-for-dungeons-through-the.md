# Increase the min level requirement for dungeons through the LFT!

## Post 117168 by Ashwhole (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-18T16:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117168#p117168 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi!   satisfied_turtle_head

Why is it that the LFT allows for such low levels to join dungeons? This is the case for just about every dungeon.

But for example in SM LIB, the LFT allows ppl to join that are lvl 29 while the last boss is lvl 37.

Thats a whole 8 level difference.

This does nothing good but to to ruin the experience for the lowbie joining, as well as for the other ppl who then has to carry the lowbie through the dungeon.

As a dps the lowbie might have a bad experience because he wont be hitting much of anything, he will aggro mobs from far away. As a tank he wont be getting much threat at all and he will die very quickly.

This will cause the dungeonrun to go slower, it might cause wipes and the other grpmembers might be annoyed at the lowbie.

Many lowbies joining have no idea that they are far to low lvl for the dungeon, because they had just ''Joined the LFT''. So now they might end up having a bad first experience with the dungeon for no reason.

I general dont have much problem with the minimum requirement to join the dungeon to be set low, as i understand some ppl want to boost their characters at an early level.

But please, increase the minimum lvl requirement for dungeons through the LFT. I think it should be no lower then 4 levels below the level of the last boss.

So for example, in SM LIB the minimum lvl requirment to queue through the LFT should be lvl 33.

Another reason for increase the min lvl requirment is that the lowbie wont be able to pick up the quests if he queues at too low lvl and he also wont be able to use the gear yet that drops.

I have been in many dungeonsruns were ppl have left, or the grp have disbanded just because they joined through the LFT and they didnt know that they are actualy too low lvl for the dungeon they queued up for.

Developers please change this!
