## Post 25131 by Massie2 — 2022-09-15T21:40:00Z

As someone who does fish frequently, my only gripe is like the first post says, a lot of "bites" are weighted towards the very last second of the cast. This means, despite normally having 3-4 seconds of time to click on your bobber after a bite, a lot of times you're left with less than a second to click.
This doesn't really make it particularly difficult, but it means what's otherwise a chill side-activity now becomes lowkey stressful as you notice the bar reaching its end and you are concentrating on reacting to a bite before it runs out.

Conversly, I recall another private server randomizing bites starting from the moment you cast Fishing (normally, you can't have a bite in the first 3 seconds of the channel). Additionally, bites were weighted more towards the start of the cast, rather than the end. This meant it was actually faster to fish by spamming the cast each GCD, and hope you get an instant bite within each 1.5 second of cooldown. And it also demanded attention, otherwise you might lose a fish because you cast again after a bite.

While this made fishing more rewarding if you were actively playing, it also kinda spoiled the vanilla fishing mood of watching TV for a while and waiting for a bite somewhere in the middle of the channel.

My fair suggestion is to normalize the time it takes to bite more towards the middle of the channel, so you can be more chill about clicking the bobber.
