# DM trinkets

## Post 102140 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-29T03:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102140#p102140 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I always thought about the DM trinkets as a good late-game reward on the hardest-to-fill equipment slot.
Some of them are really cool but imo many of them would need some readjustment. I feel it unfair that a quest like that gives a class a really good boost when collecting items to raid while for some others it's just all bad.

I'd add a hit chance to the warrior one. Would make it good enough that tanks consider it and could also help DPS warriors when building up hit cap.

I heard that rogue one is bad but I am not familiar with rogue gameplay enough. No idea what to propose here.

Hunter one is extremely strong. Only supports range hunter (only saying this because no idea if melee hunter will be a thing soon) but imo that's okay.

Warlock and shaman ones. They are good but for whatever reason they have the same effect. Just feels weird, every other trinket is unique. Would decrease SP and probably add some MP5 for shaman one because they struggle with mana at prebis phase and a spell hit or spell crit to the warlock one.

Priest one supports healer build extremely well while having a minimal benefit for shadow. Fine imo.

The druid one is weird. Extremely good for resto but unlike the other cases dru is 'the' hybrid class. I can ignore the priest one focusing on healers or the hunter one focusing on ranged gameplay because to me they feel like the base class fantasy. You can deviate from them but the trinkets support the core design. But here... There is no main dru class fantasy or playing each form is the fantasy itself. Would probably turn this into some kind of generalistic trinket.

Similar with pala. If we slightly reduce the healing and turn it into SP then it's okay for prot, so-so for ret and still ok for holy. Armor+sp or armor+spell crit feels the best combination here specially with the new pala talent which gives healing power based on armor value (with new changes).

Mage one seems weak to me, mages are usually good with mana. Another SP trinket might be boring. Maybe some spell haste, specially that, if rumors are true, channeled spells will be affected by spell haste.
