# Changes to a few Druid Set Bonuses

## Post 117385 by Darter — 2025-01-20T22:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117385#p117385 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have played a druid for thousands of hours, and personally love mostly all the changes TWoW has made to them and their armor, but I came across somethings that seemed out of place.

The first one is current Feral T1 (3) set bonus which is: Reduces the cooldown of Faerie Fire (Feral) by 2 seconds.

It just seems pointless to me. Maybe I am over looking something. If casting it could proc Omen of Clarity it would be much better, but it doesn't. So once you cast Faire Fire on a mob it lasts 40 seconds. If it is resisted, which is very rare waiting 6 seconds (normal CD) to reapply is very reasonable. And the reduction of armor is very minimal at 595 armor which is just a few percent. So why is this even a set bonus? There can be much better options.

Some I had are:
Increase the melee damage by 3,4, or 5%
Increase critical strike by 2%
Increase critical strike damage by 10%
Reduce the cooldown of Berserk by 1.5 minutes (5) set bonus, and change the current (5) set bonus to (3) bonus: Reduces the energy cost of Tiger's Fury by 5.

 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The current ZG (3) set bonus is : Increases the duration of Faerie Fire by 5 sec. This is just not necessary and shouldn't be a bonus. The 40 second duration on Faerie Fire is plenty of time.

Some bonus ideas I had are:
Increase Damage and Healing done by spells and effects by up to 20.
Increase the damage of Wrath by 4 or 5%.

 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
And one minor this I noticed in the Dreamwalker Healing set I saw was the (4) set bonus: Reduces the mana cost of your Healing Touch, Regrowth, Rejuvenation, and Tranquility spells by 3%.

I just think that 3% might be a bit to little for a T3 set bonus. Why not 5%?
