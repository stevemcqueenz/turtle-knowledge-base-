# Request: Please make it so that Quel'Dorei meditation has increased mana generation while out of combat

## Post 160051 by Familymaker — 2025-11-19T11:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160051#p160051 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So first of all, I absolutely LOVE Quel'Dorei meditation for the high elves! It is perfect for warriors, who are rewarded for giving up an attack with its 1.5 sec cast by getting a burst of rage, and rogues similairly gain a burst of energy to make some fun combos! I also like how in combat the 10% mana generation for spellcasters has a good balance, as it can help in a pinch, but isn't too strong that it outshines the other racials.

However, as I am playing, I often find myself in situations in between combat (such as during escorts, or while your group rushes through a dungeon) where I had to use Quel'Dorei meditation as a spell caster to regain my mana, and it is honestly barely noticible. Don't get me wrong, it did help, but it didn't feel that worthwhile to do in those situations. As such, I wanted to make the following suggestion for Quel'Dorei meditation:

How about making it so that while high elf spellcasters are in combat the Quel'Dorei meditation functions as it does right now and generates 10% of their total mana over 5 seconds after a 1.5 sec uninterruptable cast, but while they are out of combat the racial would instead generate additional mana? Perhaps 100% or even 150% increased mana generation while they are out of combat, to make it feel more rewarding for PVE?

That way most high elf spellcasters will likely keep using it in combat for raids and dungeons, but also have the option to use it outside of combat in between killing mobs, giving it another utility while keeping it balanced!

At least, that is my honest opionion in this regard. Overall, I have to say that I absolutely love Turtle Wow, and I'm really happy that I found it :)
