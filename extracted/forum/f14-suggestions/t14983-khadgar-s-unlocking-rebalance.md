# Khadgar's Unlocking rebalance

## Post 101938 by Khornet — 2024-09-26T10:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101938#p101938 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Khadgar's Unlocking was a Mage spell removed in WoW's 0.9 Beta patch.
Because it was removed even before a proper release of the first iteration of WoW, it was never really balanced, or even considered for balancing.

I was pleasantly surprised to see that TWoW decided to return this nifty little gimmick to mages.
I was unpleasantly surprised to see that the values of the skill have been completely untouched.

Let's summarize: at level 54 we get a spell that is equivalent to a lockpicking skill of 195. This allows us to open a Steel Lockbox at BEST. <https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=4637/steel-lockbox>
Steel Lockbox is a level 40 item, dropped by mobs most commonly around the level 45 bracket. They require a lockpicking skill of 175 to open.
By the time the mage is going to be finding those lockboxes, they will NOT be able to open them, as Rank 3 of Khadgar's Unlocking is equivalent to a lockpicking skill level of 135.
By the time the mage is going to be finding Mithril, Thorium and Eternium Lockboxes (level 50+ bracket), they will ALSO not be able to open them, as Rank 4 of Khadgar's unlocking is equivalent to skill level 175, not 225.

Do you see the problem? **Khadgar's Unlocking becomes obsolete the very moment the mage learns it.**
This makes the spell almost utterly pointless and confusing, as the mage is unaware of the exact skill values and has no clear idea why he can't open that Steel Lockbox within his level bracket. I am certain these are the main reasons for why the spell was outright removed in WoW 0.9.

If we don't consider the option of removing Khadgar's Unlocking again from the game, I implore you to consider rebalancing it somehow. Either by modifying the effective skill values (bumping rank 3 to 175 and rank 4 to 225 should be the simplest solution), or by adding new tiers of Khadgar's Unlocking, which seems like the hardest option, because Rank 3 and 4 spell books start quests to acquire the spell proper.

If that is considered TOO overpowered by rogue players, who can still open all lockboxes without reagent cost, the option to increase the reagent cost for these spells slightly is also on the table.
