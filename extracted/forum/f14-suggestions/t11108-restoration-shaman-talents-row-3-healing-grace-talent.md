Greetings,

I want to suggest a small change to the talent **[Healing Grace]**.

 **How it works right now:**  Reduces the threat generated by your heaking spells by 5% 0/3. 15% 3/3.

 **My suggestion is:** Reduces the threat generated by your spells and abilities by 3% 0/3. 10% 3/3.

Basically, make threat reduction valiable for Enhancement and Elemenal builds too.

Thank you!
