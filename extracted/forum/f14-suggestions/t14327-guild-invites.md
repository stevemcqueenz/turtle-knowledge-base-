# Guild invites

## Post 97719 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-24T09:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97719#p97719 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I made many alts here and there's something I always found annoying. Usually I keep my alts without guild until I'm sure I'm enjoying the class, experimented a bit with them and then I join the guild where my main is.
But people see a lvl 10 toon without a guild, people spam the toon with invites.
In better cases at least a dude is whispering to me that blablabla good guild please join. That's actually kind of fair.
But usually what happens is: I farm, quest or go to a low-level dungeon and midfight a random popup comes that join xxxx guild. Who are they etc, no idea, they didn't even bother to ask if I'm interested in joining a guild. I wouldn't write here if it only happened once or twice. It's regular. There are some guilds who just prey upon lowbies without guild and spam invites.
I think there should be a way to control this, like a character setting which autocancels all guild invites.
