# Two suggestions: PVP & Race/Class Changes

## Post 22794 by Heimdallr (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-19T09:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22794#p22794 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

I wanted to propose two things that keep bothering me since I've started to play here.

 1. Race/Class changes

When I wanted to get back to roots, to most immersive and as close to original World of Warcraft server, I lost hope to find any with online...and then I found two servers. Two servers that either started as one project and got split...or some similarities are just random. Both servers have things that I urge to have. This means I am split between two servers, picking the better one, yet I cannot play any, not longing for possibilities from another.

I am talking of course about Turtle as the "better" one, and Vanilla+ as the one that has features that I would really like to see here as well.

The general idea of turtle, the lore, world built etc, are in my opinion supreme to the latter server (this probably explains why the latter is close to dead) however I perceive we could learn a lot from that server, or perhaps suggest merge? I am not doing any promotion nor anything like that, I am just fascinated how such good servers can sexist split, instead of benefit and become the ultimate server, just like Warmane became for WOTLK or Ascension for character building.

Both have custom dungeons, bgs, world changes, class changes etc. If not merging...let's learn:)

Building characters is pretty important and cool stuff, allowing you to create what's in your mind, whether it's RP, PVP, PVE or combination of these. For example, on Vanilla+ You can have shaman tank, because shields are reworked for him. You could have a shaman with OFFHAND that is ONLY a fist weapon...first thing I thought about is - Ok, I want to cosplay a frostwolf shaman, with claws'n'shiet - Warrior that could be built under HP regeneration? another awesome thing. I don't want to talk much about it, but it's really worth checking and trying implementing similar things, or try to just build one big, supreme server

 2. PVP - I was dealing with TONS of PVP issues in RP games, namely Ultima Online servers and Neverwinter Nights 1 & 2 servers. The general rule is that there will always be a prick making other's game harder. But preventing or limiting pvp too much, always results in degradation as it is also a big part of RP(unless you live in some  sort of utopia)

I've seen enough people crying they were killed by high levels, twinks etc. Why not encourage pvp and support it mechanically AND with guidance of team + players?

it's just a suggestion but discussion could be big on it. I think that twinking is important, as it is basically your, let's say warrior, preparing best he can, to pursue his goal. Whether it's protecting his piece of land, urge for blood or other reason - It's still valid. How to make it balanced? Well...first of all wow is tied to various zones and levels. Let's use that to our advantage here.

On Vanilla Classic, we made RP NE guilds based on existing NE factions. Each faction was protecting different part of Ashenvale from the horde, we were communicating, roaming, fighting and roleplaying and it was hell of a fun, especially when horde created a huge Ashenvale raid, I think it's recorded somewhere on YT from when Classic started.

We couldn't efficiently move ourselves to Ashenvale if needed. On Turtle, it seems we can. There is City Protectors mechanic. Why not expand that to make region protectors? teleport to certain regions to protect them from players etc. Could be fun; ]

Absolutely prevent killing others with level difference that puts players in a such disadvantage, they cannot even compete. Better turn this this way at least till better solution could be found (this creates a different issue, you can be followed by opposite faction and guys can just report your position etc...but as I said, temporary it's better than now)

limit twinks to certain item level? Twinking is a way to survive combat vs multiple opponents, but if gone too far, you can just afk, log out, or quit because simply there is no chance if they sit on you all the time.

By saying that we could also use some guidance from team and players, think about it - many people rather cry than even try doing anything, it's common especially in fluffy RP community. How about set messages on channel, that in addition to "bg started, someone died" etc, they would advise that there are guardians ready to help you, call for help when your place is attacked etc. Just  slightly push people showing what options they have.

Goal of all is to turn warmode on (perhaps not for HC:D) or at least make higher risk higher reward areas. BG is fun, but it's actually not if you think about it. Blizzard tried to limit game to endgame repeatable content - you log in, you farm. You log in, you kill. You get bored, make break, repeat. in all games I tried PVP, the natural, free pvp is better, than grind to achieve certain level, to then repeat that endlessly, because it is simply boring. Different people, on different zones, on different builds, act differently, and that's what makes it fun, to roam the land, find opponents, make traps etc. But it will never be fun, if you can be killed by people with too big level difference, or gear difference that it makes it impossible to win 3v1, even 2v1 is sometimes undoable

Perhaps my writing style is bad, but it's some sort of free thought. I'd like to see tons of PVP and people not being afraid to call for help, organize raids to certain zone, try to capture cities, some forts if possible...just a living breating WARCRAFT
