Lot to unpack here:

Stormstrike not benefiting Frostshock and Flameshock is frustrating, but the issue reaches much deeper than just "one of your abilities benefits from it and the others don't". Not every element is equally useful in a PvE Raid environment. Flame and Frostshock benefit from CoE while Earthshock doesn't. If we just buff Stormstrike to include all shocks, you just use Frostshock and forget Earthshock even exists.
Instead, let us have Stormstrike be the debuff equivalent to CoE for nature. Then you are truely free in the use of shocks, as all get the same damage bonus. Earthshock still remains the bread and butter for Solo & Dungeoncontent, but in a raid environment that fixes the T3 wanting you to use all shocks and it fixes Elementals dreadful state.

Im absolutely not a fan of this "you wear a shield therefore you tank, or you don't therefore you never will want threat" mentality. There are good reasons for a shaman, particularly during levelling to want to be higher in threat than other classes. Not necessarily just when playing with shields. It also absolves Rockbiter from having any realistic use outside of Elemental weapons as a talent. As in early level Flametongue largely outclasses it, and in higher levels Windfury outclasses it.
Additionally, any scenario in which someone can spam Frostshock while running and hold threat with it isn't exactly something the game needs to actively encourage. Yes you struggle with nature resitant mobs. The other tanks suffer from mobs with higher than average parry chance. (Aka Dragons) Outside of maybe chromaggus whose entire gimmick is that spell resistence and a couple bosses in AQ, nature resistant mobs while tanking is at worst a bit of a nuisance, but not something that requires a remedy.

The hybrid scaling on imbues i tend to agree with. Imbues were originally designed to deal comparable dps while levelling, with minor upsides for each. Due to Twow largely being played at a very different gearlevel than what the game has been designed for, it would be lovely being a bit more free in the choice of imbue. Especially Flametongue and Frostbrand are feeling half-baked. Frostbrand is amazing, unless the mob is immune to slow, at which point it becomes absolute trash. Flametongue on the other hand is only really existing to buff Firetotem AoE. Something enhancer can't take good adventage of on account on it tanking your single target damage.

As for bloodlust being a defensive CD. That design is awful. Not only does it tie a grp DPS cd in with a tank lifeline (Creating conflicts of interest, where you wanna keep the CD ready for an emergency, while your party wants that sweet sweet haste), It also has no mechanical tie ins with the ability as it currently is.
If shaman needs a defensive CD (Not getting into if he needs one, as i am indifferent to that personally) - then just give him one. But don't bastardise Bloodlust. A spell that is already doign too much while feeling it doesn't do anything.

Old T1 bonus can not be restored to how strong it was. If a setbonus is so strong, it vastly overshadows complete sets not just one but two tierlevels higher than itself, its a flawed design. We should want to grab setbonuses, but one of the core appeals to wow is for players to get better gear. If that journey stops for 8 out of the 15 gearslots the second you step into molten core, that is a massive fail for the game (and for shaman itemvariety). Heck it never made sense for shaman of all classes to have that bonus to begin with. All it does is it turns healing wave into chainheal. Shaman absolutely needs a reason to press any other healspell than chainheal, but turning your other healspells into chainheal is not the solution the class needs. If other classes are indeed to strong, the issue needs to be remedied, not allowing shaman to abuse a crutch.
In the end this is the same discussion as with MCP for druids all over. Why are people so adamantly defending such a broken interaction?)

Spirit link lastly is a spell 90% of the shaman playerbase absolutely doesn't understand. No matter how much a priest heals while he pops ascendence. His tank has only however much HP he has. If the tank drops from 100-0 in between two heals, he is dead. Spirit link on the other hand allows its target to live spells you would die from even at full health with a priest shielding you. It is a very very potent spell.

That being said, i wouldn't mind its CD be lowered to 5 minutes. It feels a bit excessive with how little CD other potent healer CDs like swiftmend and Transcendence have.
