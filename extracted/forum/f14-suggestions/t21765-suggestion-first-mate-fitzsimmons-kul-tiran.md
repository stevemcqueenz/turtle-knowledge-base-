# Suggestion: First Mate Fitzsimmons, Kul Tiran

## Post 150751 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-08T07:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150751#p150751 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is a very small, flavorful change. In a similar fashion to how [Lieutenant Benedict and the Tiragarde Keep garrison of Durotar](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=63&t=10667) had their faction tags changed from Stormwind to Kul Tiras, I suggest that [First Mate Fitzsimmons](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1239), sole survivor of the Doomed Kul Tiras Third Fleet currently found in Menetil Harbor in the Wetlands, also have his faction tag changed from Stormwind to Kul Tiras as to properly reflect his nationality in-game.
