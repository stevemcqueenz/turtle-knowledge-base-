## Post 166205 by Abolaboboom — 2026-01-24T04:16:00Z

There are numerous posts on the forum complaining about Ravenholdt's reputation, with various suggestions being made. I propose changing the requirement for turning in Heavy Junkboxes to turning in Precise Lock Cylinders, which can be obtained by unlocking Heavy Junkboxes. This change is simple and respects the original gameplay (Pick Pocket and Lockpicking).Lock cylinders are obtained After unlocking and can stack up to 250.It is reasonable to use a large number of lock cylinders to gain recognition from a thieving gang. The key is that it no longer requires players to occupy a huge number of slots, which is an abnormal requirement.
