Some models sure, but others are a bit too high poly and clash with existing models. This is especially true for gear. Though I have a different concern, and that's Blizzard's looming eye...

Project99, an Everquest private server, has evaded all legal threat from Daybreak Games(current owners of the IP) by strictly offering the base game and its first 2 expansions, preserved as they were back in the day. That's something Daybreak themselves cannot offer due to the engine change of the game, and would require a completely separate client, instead of a separate server. Plus the lack of new content makes that private server a non-threat.

Now why did I go over that. Well, Turtle has the possibility of lowering legal interactions with Blizzard by a mutual agreement on what content can and cannot be done. This will of course limit the TWoW devs a bit, but the main demand from Blizzard I can expect is to not use any content (models, animations, music, etc...) from future WoW expansions. And the other thing being features or content (items, dungeons, raids) that don't clash with Blizzard's development roadmap for Classic+, as to avoid comparisons.

For obvious reasons, the devs can't discuss these things openly, but I won't be surprised if such a discussion has already happened or is currently happening. Partially because of the very specific questions in the Classic+ survey, asking for things like using UE5 for Classic+, which was on the Turtle WoW pipeline.
