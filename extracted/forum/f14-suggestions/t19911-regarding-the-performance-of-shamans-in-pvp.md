## Post 135983 by Flexdraw — 2025-06-16T20:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135983#p135983 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The responses seem a bit disingenuous. Yes, Elemental has always had huge burst and worn a shield. No, it didn't used to invest 17 total points into the enhancement tree for a 15% armor bonus, 6% dodge, and 30% bonus to armor gained from wearing a shield. All of this is from two talents, accounting for 5 of the 17 invested points in Enhancement.

I often see shamans try to justify this by pointing out that they're missing out on other options by taking these talents, but the reality is that these five talent points essentially give them dire bear form. *Every* shaman is taking these talents in PVP. In combination with lightning shield, you will die of reflected damage before killing a shaman 1v1 as melee. In combination with earth shield, they can essentially ignore you if they're feeling nice. This is all just hypothetically annoying, because in reality they'll just kill you in a single global and walk away.

If we're being charitable, these talents were put at the top of the tree so that low level shamans could tank dungeons. But it's been over half a year with no fix in sight. There has to be a way to let the four shaman tanks on the server have their fun while not allowing it to stay as oppressive as it is in PVP.

The upcoming change of percentage based physical damage/magic damage/healing/absorption reduction won't help with this issue at all. Unless it's something crazy like 5% physical damage reduction and 50% magic damage reduction (don't worry, it's not), this is going to be another huge shaman *buff*.
