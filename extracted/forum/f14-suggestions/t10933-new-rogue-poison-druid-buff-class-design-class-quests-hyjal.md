# New Rogue Poison. Druid Buff. Class Design. Class Quests. Hyjal.

## Post 71235 by Xfraz — 2023-10-16T14:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71235#p71235 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Rogues and Druids must work together in order to make 2 new poisons. Starting at level 24 the Rogue will embark on a quest which takes him to Hyjal via a teleport from Darnassus or ThunderBluff to learn these new poisons. Then he or the Druid must gather/purchase the materials to make them. These new poisons made by the Rogue are however useless due to needing a Druid to Activate them.

I propose changing the spell druids learn via their poison mastery class quest which is entirely useless due to learning a much better spell just several levels later. Instead Druids learn the spell Activate which can either have two uses or come in two forms. One being to activate the newfound poisons which the Rogue has made, the other I will talk more on later.

So the first of the new poisons is used by the Rogue which causes a stacking debuff on the enemy when attacked. At 5 stacks the poison explodes dealing AOE damage and removing the debuff.
(edit) To be clear this poison would be for dealing with large groups of enemies, think 5 or more. If applied to each weapon then you achieve 5 stacks sooner meaning more DPS. It should deal similar damage to Instant Poison but of course in an AoE with a higher proc rate of 35-40% and a radius 5-6 yards.

The other poison is unusable by a Rogue and required by Druids. Druids can use this poison just like a Rogue which again applies a debuff to the target of the Druids attacks. This debuff deals damage over time for each stack up to 5. At 5 stacks the DoT should be around the same DPS of Insect Swarm.

The other use of the Activate spell is to then deal instant damage per stack applied to the enemy at the reset of all stacks. The Druid can apply the poisons via auto attacks in ALL forms however being a Balance ability only in Caster or Moonkin form can the Druid *activate* the poisons.

The purpose for Rogues is to obviously give them a little extra AOE. For Druids However it is to breath new life into the class for both feral and Balance specs whilst bolstering the melee potential of Balance Druids.

Yes I said MELEE Balance Druids but wait.

Priests, Mages and Warlocks have their wands for dealing free damage. A Shaman has his weapon imbues and a Paladin  their seals. A Balance Druid however must rely solely on their big stick if they want to deal some free damage and tap into their Mp5 playing conservatively.  With their incredibly high armour from Moonkin form and to synergise with talents such as Natural Weapons and Omen of Clarity which most Balance Druids are already taking, I believe my proposal of a SP melee proc effect with a spell activation will help round out the spec whilst giving a welcome buff to Ferals.
