# Starting zones need to be more equally attractive for players

## Post 76140 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-06T12:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76140#p76140 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Almost everyone who levels an alliance character goes to Elwynn Forest. I created a gnome hunter and while there were people around its like a ghost town compared to Elwynn forest. And it doesn't surprise me! Though there are some new quests in Dun Morogh the quest rewards you get in Elwynn are great.

I don't exactly know how it is for Horde since I barely play on the Horde side but zones like Dun Morogh and even Teldrassil to some extent must be improved a little so the players are equally distributed among the zones.

My suggestion? Just like Goldshire got a few new buildings with quest givers why not do the same to Kharanos and Dolanaar? Same also applies to horde locations though as I said I am not to sure how it is for you guys.
Kharanos could use a quest dealing with the wolves that are all around the place. Brewnall could be expanded(maybe some wooden palisades?) and give you more quests regarding the trolls. Then at the quarry they could also lead to a few quests regarding the dark iron dwarfs? Maybe even a quest item drop like the ring you get in goldshire from the murlocs.

I don't know a lot about creating custom content but maybe this could be part of the new player experience patch that is coming soonish that focuses on High elves and goblins.

Edit: I always had the impression that the gnomish reclamation facility was some sort of high level zone for quests for gnomeregan but it turns out it is a fantastic zone with tons of quests for the starting area experience that covers pretty much everything I wanted.

Disregard this post entirely unless similar problems exist on the horde side.
