I leveled a shaman tank after CC2 but tbh I didn't do any professions, mostly just spammed dungeons.
In my experience the dungeon items more or less cover you during later leveling. Mostly not tanking ones but if they have stam on them, preferably with some other stats, that's good enough,

There are many reasons for not being totally screwed after lvl 40 as a shammie tank:
 1. I don't advise going all leather after lvl 40 but having 1-2 leathers here and there if they give good stats, it's totally okay.
 2. Spirit shield starts to become relevant. 30% armor bonus when armors start to hit 1000-1500 on shields makes you feel like almost wearing a plate.
 3. That lvl 33 stamina trinket. That solves a lot of problems.

After that, during dungeoning, my prios always were:
 * Item with a big stam on it
or
 * Item with a lot of random stats (you still need some int etc to properly tank)

And most impotantly: shield had to be as good as possible.

That's a bit of extra effort but you can pay some coins for cheaper stam enchants and armor kits so you won't even notice you wear leather/mail.

But back to the point: if TWOW added a mail tank, TWOW has the responsibility to provide enough mail tanking items.
