# Idea for class-specfic glyphs

## Post 111200 by Itorch100 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-04T00:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111200#p111200 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Here's a fun idea, it would be pretty cool if we got additional class-specific glyphs that impose additional challenges on the leveler (it would be extra interesting for Hardcore in particular)

A few that come off the top of my head:
Warrior - berserker (leather armor, no shield allowed)
Mage - arcanist (only arcane spells)
Hunter - lone wolf (no pet)
Druid - naturalist (no forms)

Those are the same challenges that were present in Classic WoW hardcore, during the addon era, but I think it would be neat if those were to be included on Turtle for those that would like to spice up the leveling and add an extra layer of challenge.

Would be very hard to balance this out.
You're basically asking a warrior to stay DPS while leveling which is kinda easy. At the same time you exclude shapeshifting from druids which is a big blow.
My other concern is how restrictive it is.
In your example you took away warrior tanking and feral DPS. BM hunter is excluded, mage has to be arcane. A good challenge doesn't grab half of the possible specs and throws them into the trashcan.
