# Lasher Resistance Change

## Post 91064 by Jce — 2024-03-21T15:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91064#p91064 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the recent changes to lashers, and it being a more gold transfer farm, and appearing to be something encouraged by T-WoW as they have changed it in favour of it providing herbs, can the Whip Lashers see an update to make them more conductive to every class having an even time farming them.

In this instance, I am referencing removing their nature damage resistance. Druids have no quick and sustainable way to keep up with other casters who have better AoE Capabilities.
If I were a boomkin never mind the fact hurricane is a 1 minute cooldown, the mobs are immune and will take 0 damage from the spell.
Additionally, if I were to go feral, and try swiping and reflecting them to death, they're immune to thorns damage along with Talisman of Binding Shard.

So the suggestion is quick and dirty, remove the nature damage resistance on only these mobs, and make it a more fair time for classes other than paladins or mages farming the mobs.
