# Flying Mounts with certain conditions

## Post 88015 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-02-17T15:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88015#p88015 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't know if this is a good or bad suggestion, but I thought about it and I think they would be a curious and perhaps useful addition.

What is it about?
Simple, taking the goldenshire griffin and the Razor Hill wyvern I thought of the following.
Allows people to purchase flying mounts and use them ONLY under certain conditions.
For example, to buy it you must discover ALL the flight points available on both continents and you can only mount and dismount in the Flight Masters.

The mount would only allow you to view Azeroth from above and fly at will but not land at will, similar to how the Goldshire and Razor Hill mounts work.

It's just an idea I had.
