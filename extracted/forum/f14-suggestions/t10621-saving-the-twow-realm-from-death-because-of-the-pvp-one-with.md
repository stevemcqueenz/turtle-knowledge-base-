# Saving the twow realm from death because of the pvp one with 1 easy fix - after a long time, allow transfers up to lv 40

## Post 69220 by Vaikaris (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-06T18:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69220#p69220 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**The problem**

 1. After the announcement of the new pvp realm, hype is massive. Most people who were considering leveling alts or starting new characters have basically stopped, opting instead to wait for the fresh realm.

 2. The old realm is experiencing a very sudden drop of lowbies/newbiews actively doing things in-game (not sure if numbers show yet, because there's nowhere else they can go, but activity is less). Over time this would grow exponentially as less newbies/lobies => leads to even less. This is not a MAJOR issue, the realm has and still can survive with 100 people online, but ***it doesn't have to***.

 3. The pvp realm will suffer from a very serious case of "you think you do, but you don't". Pvp is not for everyone and griefing can really wear you down, but the hype will drag you in. Twow risks losing its feel of fun, chill, not problematic atmosphere.

 4. Currently leveling alts is very pointless and there's about 20 days left that it feels that way.

 5. I forgot this one, editing it in - while the initial rush of players and filled to the brim zones are part of the experience, there's a certain level of sometimes having to play in a rush when you don't want to, just so you don't get left behind. This would have the effect of splitting the playerbase a bit. Thus also avoiding GIGANTIC queues because of hype.

 **The solution**

After a good amount of time, maybe a month, maybe two, or maybe when a certian treshold is reached such as 1000 level 40s on the pvp realm - allow transfers of characters up to level 40 on the pvp realm and back to twow.

 **Justification**

Basically, by allowing people to transfer characters up to that level, you make leveling alts more worth it here, since you're not losing out and you dont have to do it again. You also reinvigorate the current realm with tons of new characters and newbies. In the end, after a while, they can decide to stay because they prefer the chill atmosphere, leading to no cases of "you think you do, but you don't. Level 40 is enough to get a feel for that. On the other hand, you also have people who get swept up in the hype of the pvp realm, don't like it, then come back to twow - also contributing to a healthy population of newbies.

Level 40 is about a midway point where, on a pvp server, you start getting ganked/ganking more, while on a pve you start doing more dungeons etc. It's a great cut-off point to decide where you want to go.

 **Possible problems and solutions**

 1. Characters get transferred with an unfair advantage - gold is easy, a level 40 tends to buy their mount and have something like 10g left. So allowing a maximum of 10g to transfer is reasonable. This means you can buy your character a mount and give him some pocket change.

 2. Busting the economy with mats/trade goods/pots - simply scrub the whole inventory clean except quest items. Maybe only allow 1 bag of green gear up to level 40. That's not really a huge advantage, even vendoring full bags of level 40 gear is what, 10 more gold? You're getting somewhat of an advantage by leveling in a pve server with ample dungeons and less ganking, an empty inventory feels like a good trade-off

 3. People get an unfair advantage by not being ganked - here's the deal, this isn't true. If you want to level without being ganked it is ABSOLUTELY possible on a pvp server. An alliance played in Kalimdor can more or less avoid issues, and you can grind where enemies aren't anyway. So what's the difference between choosing to do it on twow or not? In addition, let's face it, you're gonna level in warmode. OR you can add a solution where only players in warmode can transfer. That way, you're facing just about the same risk of being ganked - as you're also in a server full of ganking level 60s.

And that's about it. I think this fix will end up being necessary eventually anyway, but I'd just hate to see the potential damage of the pvp server and I think this is an easy, painless fix.

If you consider it, please announce it ASAP so people can start leveling again :)
