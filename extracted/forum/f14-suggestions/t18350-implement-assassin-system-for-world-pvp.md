# Implement Assassin System for World PvP

## Post 125267 by Zerzah — 2025-04-03T14:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125267#p125267 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle WoW Team,

I’d like to suggest a feature that could bring more excitement to world PvP and encourage more interaction between players: the Assassin System, inspired by a mechanic from bfa.

How it works:

When a player kills 10 enemy faction members in a row without dying, they are marked as an Assassin.

Once marked, their location becomes visible on the world map to all players of the opposing faction.

This gives others the opportunity to track and hunt the Assassin down.

If the Assassin is killed, a small bounty chest drops at the location of their death, which can contain some gold or minor rewards.

Benefits:

Encourages organic world PvP encounters.

Creates dynamic risk-vs-reward gameplay.

Adds a new layer of challenge and prestige for skilled players.

Fits well with the immersive and community-driven philosophy of Turtle WoW.

I believe this feature would be a great addition to the game and could provide memorable experiences for both PvP lovers and casual players alike. Thank you for considering it!

Kind regards,
