# Whirlwind rework

## Post 37623 by Bloodline1x9 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-14T03:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37623#p37623 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This AOE ability deal dmg only from main hand without off-hand.

And you ask me "*So what? The warrior is the strongest dps*". Yes, there is a problem. Players use a macro to switch to a two-handed weapon for this skill. This is an ill-conceived broken mechanic that only creates mechanical obstacles for warriors.

However, the warriors are forced to take slow weapons in their hands for the sake of a stronger Whirlwind also not a very good idea. I suggest reworking the [Whirldwind] so that its *damage depends on* DPS x 3 from main hand or common weapon dmg if 2h weapon equipped.

Thus, the ability adapts to the equipped weapon and deals damage in one of two ways. With two-handed weapons, the damage will be a little higher, but not so much that you constantly switch to two-handed

The purpose of this change is not to strengthen Fury warriors, but to make their play more comfortable by closing the need for abuse.

Those who have never changed their weapons will, of course, increase AOE dps a little. But really, Furies are weak in AOE dps right now. And since they do not have Sweeping strikes, they will never be able to compete with Arms in AOE.
If need to reduce the damage inflicted by fury warriors so that other classes can compete well with them, then you need to pay attention to their direct damage, and not to make the Whirlwind weak. Because as long as the possibility of abuse exists, people will use it. Plus, they still claim 2-hand weapons. Anyone fury can roll 2-hand weapon and saying "this is for me to swap".

It's not a healthy mechanic when a strong dps class can deal more damage using a macro with a weapon change.
I think that this rework Whirlwind will on the contrary affect the top dps of fury warriors. They will see that without a two-handed swap, their aoe dps will decrease a bit. But not so much that they want to engage in this masochistic abuse by changing weapons back and forth every 10 seconds.
