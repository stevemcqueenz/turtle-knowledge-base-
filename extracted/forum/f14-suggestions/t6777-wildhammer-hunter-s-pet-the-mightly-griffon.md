**Wildhammer Dwaft And His Companion**

So I was thinking last night, how cool would it be, to be able to tame a Griffon/Gryphon as a Wildhammer Dwaft.
I've made a new Hardcore Character with the skin of a Wildhammer, and I'm very happy with it and im not dead yet.
But for **Lore** and **RP** it would be amazing to have a unique quest added maybe in the hinterlands or an outpost closer to the starting zone with a Wildhammer NPC in the taming and the training of a griffon and this would be a quest only open to dwafts and maybe down the line other races that can be hunters could have unique pets also.

 **e.g**
Undead Hunter > Giant Bat
Orc Hunter > Wyverns **[Wind Rider]** [http://classic.battle.net/war3/orc/unit ... ider.shtml](http://classic.battle.net/war3/orc/units/windrider.shtml)
NightElf Hunter > Hippogryph
Will have a think about others races later, any suggestions are welcome.

I really enjoy class quests, I know this could take some time to develop, but here more information.

 **Features and Skills:**

Growl, Cower
Bite, Claw
Charge, Dash
Screech
[Unique Griffon Skill] need to have a brainstorm about this, but it can't be too powerful, maybe something like:
 **Furious Howl**
 *Party members within 15 yards receive extra damage to their next physical attack. Lasts 10 seconds.*

Use as a land mount? Not flying mount cos this is the Vanilla world.
Adds a discount to flightpaths 10% off?, e.g. cos you're riding your pet but using Alliance airlines? A nice way of saving coin.

The model is in Vanilla wow: <https://www.wow-petopia.com/npc.php?id=2658>
Maybe the Griffon you tame is a young one, so in gameplay it makes sense why you can't fly on it freely.
Your weight could be a factor, or it hasn't come of age to be able to deviate from flightpaths

I think the quest should be a chain, not as simple as the hunter normal pet quests from the starting zone, but something with mutable levels to get that bond locked in and see the relationship grow with your new companion.

Also, maybe you have to spend some talent points into this feature?

Please fill free you add your view on this.
