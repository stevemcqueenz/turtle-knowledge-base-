# Creature Respawn Time Changes

## Post 36943 by Kinria — 2023-03-06T05:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36943#p36943 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is a post mostly focused on specific mobs that have a long, or lengthy respawn timer.   Some enemies in the game have quests tied to them that cannot be completed without killing them, and I wanted to focus on a few enemies and get people's suggestions to rework the respawn timer on.

- Blackmoss the Fetid:  Lv. 13 Rare in Teldrassil
Blackmoss the Fetid has a very lengthy respawn timer.  And has the Moss-Twined Heart quest item that is the final quest for Teldrassil.  Most people do not complete this quest due to how significantly rare this mob is to find alive.

- Snufflesnout: Lv. 4-5 in Northshire Abbey
This quest creature is highly sought after and has about a 5-10 minute time delay for groups to kill him. This proves to be especially annoying for new human players to get the kill, taking turns or fighting to get a tag in.

These are two enemies that come to mind, if there's others that people ought to look into, or have problems collecting on.  It should be considered to help speed along the process for these types of enemies.
