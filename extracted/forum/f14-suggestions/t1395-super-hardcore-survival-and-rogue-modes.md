# Super Hardcore, Survival and Rogue Modes

## Post 8513 by Himyname — 2020-11-24T10:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8513#p8513 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Super Hardcore
Have a chance of getting character deleted when you die. When you reach level 60 you merely lose all your items when you die and transported to your capital city. Level at 1x rate. Dying in dungeons merely drops your XP down at 10%. Dying in battlegrounds merely removes you from the battleground and puts a deserter debuff on you, consider it an honorable discharge. If you were hurt by any enemy faction in world PvP after 5 minutes you will not suffer any penalties getting killed by mobs because people could abuse the system by attacking enemy faction and watch them die to a mob.

Rogue-Like
When you die you lose all your items and transported to your capital city. Level at 1x rate. Dying in dungeons merely down at 10%. Dying in battlegrounds merely removes you from the battleground and puts a deserter debuff on you. If you were hurt by any enemy faction in world PvP after 5 minutes you will not suffer any penalties getting killed by mobs.

Survival
You require food, drink and rest. Like survival games, you need to upkeep your needs. It has the same rules as Super Hardcore but with food, drink and rest required. Neglecting in them could cause you to die. Respawning makes you recover about 10% of those needs so you have to get them back up as soon as possible. Food and drink are replenished using the food/drink items in the game. Resting is something you must do at an inn, survival tent etc. The spirit stat has added bonus of reducing the speed of loss of said needs. Hence getting food buffs with spirit will aid in slowing down loss of needs. Logging off will halt the loss of your needs, logging off in a capital city will replenish your needs so when you come back after an hour or so you will be at full food/drink and rest. Cooking and fishing will become just that much more important
