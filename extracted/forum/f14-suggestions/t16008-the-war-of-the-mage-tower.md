# The War of the Mage Tower

## Post 109055 by Visionmoon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-20T21:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109055#p109055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The explosion of the Moonblade caused the werewolf curse to infect the Twilight Forest. To remove the curse, the arcane mages of the Kingdom of Stormwind studied the werewolves, which led to the creation of the hyenas. The wild and uncontrollable spells of the Wild Druids could no longer be fully traced, and the transformation effects seemed to be permanently fixed in those who were infected.

In Azura's research report, it was proposed that if the spell effects were weakened, it would better control the strength of the werewolves and make them more suitable for labor. In the Tower of Irgalar in Redridge Mountains, Morgans, dissatisfied with the nobles of Stormwind enslaving the hyenas, recklessly cast a spell to control the hyenas, but the spell went out of control, leading to the birth of even more hyenas.

At the same time, the mages from Stormwind assisting the mages of Gilneas returned to the isolated kingdom of Gilneas with their research on the energy of the Moonblade, which was sealed in the archives of the spellcasters. When the exiles, for the sake of the subjects outside the high walls, embarked on a sinful journey, they brought out these secret archives, studying the curse of the werewolves to combat the undead invasion.

Under the ruthless and fearless research of these mages, the Moonblade was slowly being repaired. The secrets of the Moonblade were deciphered, and wars broke out between the mage towers over its possession. Algalon, the new master of Shadowtooth Castle, to increase his leverage, had to secretly manipulate the conflict between the Horde and the Alliance, leading to the fall of Redridge Mountains once again. This war resulted in the capture of High Marshal Windsor and also caused a lack of defensive forces for Varian’s delegation to Theramore due to this conflict.

Involved Factions:

LM Azura's Tower
BL Shadowtooth Castle
Neutral Hostile Force - Tower of Irgalar
