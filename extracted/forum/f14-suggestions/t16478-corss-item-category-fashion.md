"Gandalf, the great wizard normally use a two-hand"

In the books Gandalf uses Glamdring as a one handed sword, his staff is usually in the other.

In the movies it is a hand and a half sword - a one handed sword that has space for a second hand if needed. In the case of Glamdring the second hand is mostly on the metal hilt-pommel junction and not on the leather bound part of the hilt.

The Lodestone Blade is the only hand and half sword that's vaguely suitable, I'd go with and Azuresong Mageblade.
Off hand staff options are less varied, Sanguine Star, Geomancer's Rod or Highborn Star would be my choices...none of which are good. It's a shame there isn't a longer version of something like the Runic Cane, it would at least be simple enough without just being a stick.
