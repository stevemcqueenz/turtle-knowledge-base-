I would love if we could get Shamans for the alliance and a way to make that acc to lore is give Dwarves the ability to also be able to create shaman characters.

The Wildhammer dwarves of Aerie Peak and the Hinterlands were known for a strong connection to nature and elemental spirits, while they were not a playable class, lore implies many Wildhammer spiritual leaders acted very much like shamans — communing with the elements, calling on storms, and working closely with gryphons.

In order to balance this you could also give horde the ability to create Undead Paladins.

Examples of undead paladins in lore:

Sir Zeliek

Found in the original Four Horsemen fight in Naxxramas (both Classic and Wrath versions).
Was once a devout paladin so pure that even after being raised into undeath by Kel’Thuzad, he still could channel the Holy Light.
Lore says it burns him from the inside every time he uses it, but his willpower keeps him going.
He’s essentially the textbook case of an “undead paladin.”

Also undead has undead priests that, in lore, there are Forsaken who still call on the Holy Light, suffering constant agony while doing so. While these aren’t usually called paladins, mechanically and thematically they are close — armor and hammer aside.
