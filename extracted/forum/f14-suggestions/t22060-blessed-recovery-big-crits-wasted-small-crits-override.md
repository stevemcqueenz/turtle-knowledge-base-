# Blessed Recovery: Big Crits Wasted, Small Crits Override

## Post 153010 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-24T15:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153010#p153010 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So Blessed Recovery (3 points) is supposed to return 25% of a physical crit as healing over 6 seconds. The problem is that the effect doesn’t stack – it just gets overwritten.

Example: You take a 2k crit from a rogue and start getting a nice HoT, but then they immediately follow up with a tiny crit, and that weaker tick completely overwrites the stronger one. Instead of stacking like you’d expect, you end up with almost nothing.

As a priest, we’re immobile and can’t just run away, we rely on every bit of sustain we can get. On paper this talent looks like a great survival tool, but in practice it barely heals and ends up being more of a buff slot filler than actual recovery.

Question: Is this intended design? Wouldn’t it make more sense for multiple crits to stack their heals, instead of overwriting?
