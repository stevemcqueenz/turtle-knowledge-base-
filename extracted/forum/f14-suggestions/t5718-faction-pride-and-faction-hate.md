# Faction pride and faction hate?

## Post 35798 by Element19090 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-23T12:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35798#p35798 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One thing that I think really lacks in turtle wow is Faction pride / hate.  I know it's a PvE server and I know things are largely cross faction but Is there anyway we could get Faction only chats and maybe even PvP events that give daily or maybe weekly awards to the victorious faction? the complete lack of language barrier between factions is also a bit odd. Could we at least have an option to limit cross faction interaction to guilds, dungeon groups, and guild recruitment channels?

A big part of World of Warcraft, especially Vanilla was Faction pride and loyalty and the rivalry with the other faction. When that is taken away it really changes the entire feel of the game. Even from an Rp perspective it really just does not make any sense.

Am I alone in this thinking? Perhaps people are just to used to total cross faction communication at this point but I really feel like it takes more away form the game then anything else. I think adding more distance between factions would help to incentivize PvP.
