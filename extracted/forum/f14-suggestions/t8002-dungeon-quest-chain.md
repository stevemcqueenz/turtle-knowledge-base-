# Dungeon Quest Chain

## Post 51450 by Cacuba123 — 2023-06-27T04:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51450#p51450 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Recently with the introduction of INFERNO Mode/ LV 60 HC a very neat feature of community bonding has occured.
This bonding is in the form of a TBC style Dungeon/raid Quest series with the reward being a pretty significant trinket.

I would love to see something similar to this being implemented for Normies as well, with different loot/rewards.
This could either be an attunement questline for Lower kara/emerald dream, it could be a seperate questing with a different type of reward.
I would love it to be a sort of thing that gets everyone from Dungeon blues to T3 to say, Oh i need to go run those.
To hit off this, a great example is that the INFERNO questline requires you to run Dire Maul West, one of the most hated dungeons in WOW. Incredibly dangerous for HC to do as well.
Something for Say Lower kara would be building Medivhs key, You run a strat live to recover one piece, strat undead for the other, Kara crypts for the loops, and then you have to go to the dalaran mages to have it crafted.

Option 1: This is an attunement quest, which much like the rest of the raids in existence, do exist and only give you niche resistance items and access to raids.
Option 2: you recieve a consumable...I would love to see a Re-usable Consumable be the reward. Something that would NOT destroy the Market, but would allow people to save on consumes. Maybe a Re-usable Light of Elune style/LIP consume. Once per 24Hrs you get complete immunity for 10seconds but cannot attack or use spells. (IE dropping threat on bosses)( could also be a consume that you need to run back to the NPC to get another)
Option 3: You recieve an Appropriate Item that is not T3 BIS, but could last you a significant period of time: blackhands 2% crit trinket would be the best example. Consider having 6 different items as options to cover all specs/skill sets.
