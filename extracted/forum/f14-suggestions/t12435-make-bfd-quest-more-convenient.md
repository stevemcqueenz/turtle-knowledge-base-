# Make BFD quest more convenient

## Post 82840 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-26T21:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82840#p82840 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The quest I'm talking about is named "Twilight Falls" - alliance quest taken from Darnassus that requires you to obtain 10 Twilight Pendants that drop from Twilight cultists in Blackfathom Deeps.
Quest link: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=1199>

The problem with this quest is that only one pendant can drop from each cultist and can go to random person who has the quest active. Obtaining 10 pendants is very hard task because simply there aren't enough Twilight cultists inside BFD. Supposing that everyone in 5 man group has the quest, nobody will be able to complete it after one dungeon run. Most of people will abandon the quest because there isn't much point in doing BFD second time just to complete one quest that gives green reward. It doesn't make sense for a low level dungeon anyway. I've just finished my BFD run with 3 other guys - we did it 4 man. And still only one person managed to obtain all 10 pendants.

My suggestion is to decrease number of Twilight Pendants to 5 or 6. This should make the quest more convenient.

I normally don't play alliance but I've seen the problem and it's similar to issues I've seen with troll tempers in ZF or deviate hides in WC. The turtle team fixed these, too.

While I'm normally opposed to QoL suggestions, I think making team members compete for quest items in a leveling dungeon (and it feels like rogues and warriors normally get their stuff done while the healers are drinking or just running to catch up) is bad game design and can even lead to drama in random groups.

It's another thing if people have to rerun lvl 60 content for a quest, but a dungeon like BFD (which I actually like more than many others, in spite of or because of the remote location) should allow for a full quest run while the mobs are still yellow.
