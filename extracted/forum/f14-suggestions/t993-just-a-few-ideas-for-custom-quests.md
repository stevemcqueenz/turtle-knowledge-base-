# Just a few ideas for custom quests

## Post 5613 by Volitare — 2020-04-04T16:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5613#p5613 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I just played through the custom quests in Elwyn, and I had some ideas for other starter zones :)

 **Dun Morogh**
 - Helm's Lake is largely unused with the exception of two paladin quests, so it would be really cool if all dwarves would have a reason to visit and smack some dark irons! Recover stolen beer, kill some spies, etc.
 - Collect lost rams around Amberstill Ranch → kill some troggs that've been trying to steal them → get a ram pet!

 **Teldrassil**
 - Pirates have attacked Rut'theran Village and stole a bunch of hippogryph eggs! Sentinels managed to defeat the pirates, but some eggs sank to the bottom of the sea and brave adventurers have to recover them!
 - The harpies of oracle glade captured a friend of the enchanting trainer - a fairy dragon → you must recover a key and free the dragon → reward: a simple enchant?

 **Durotar**
 - A boar farmer on the outskirts of Razor Hill needs some help with a strange disease that struck his swines → all the clues lead to the a Kul Tiran warlock, who cursed the boars → defeat the warlock → collect some mats for a troll priest → lift the curse
 - Fight club. → profit

 **Mulgore**
 - A local fisherman requires your help cleaning out some trash the Venture Co. has been dumping into the river. → You find out from the druid trainer that multiple animals have been drinking this filthy water and got sick → heal them → get a companion, right?
 - A baker asks you to deliver some bread to Thunder Bluff cooking trainer → make another delivery to the alchemy trainer → she asks you to help her concoct a potion that is supposed to cure the Forsaken (if you read her banter, you know it's her goal) → the potion isn't succesful and you get an equippable reward (something like the cowl and cloak from Elwyn)

 **Tirisfal Glades**
 - A forsaken woman who lives on the outskirts of Brill demands of you to kill her brother, who has joined the Scarlet Crusade and threatened to kill her when she tried to reason with him → He is friendly to you and explains that he is only a part of the Scarlet Crusade because SC would otherwise take his stead and probably kill him. He explains he only chased his sister way for her own good, gives you a letter for her → she in turn sends him a ring or something → get a reward.
 - A man gives you his plaguehound pup and you run around Tirisfal looking for "potent zombies". These zombies drop some sort of a concetrated plague juice, which you can feed to the plaguehound → with each dose, the hound grows bigger, more demonic and twisted → return the bloodthirsty hound to the man → get a plaguehound companion

just spitballin' here
