I get the idea, but from a logical standpoint, it doesn't make a lot of sense for being good at skinning to magically manifest a collar around a creature's neck, and a torn pelt is the opposite of what a good skinner should be able to provide. Tails and paws make more sense, but that's more dissection than skinning. Also, Skinning already provides leather, which is used in fashion quests.

I think what would make more sense is to add a couple more quests to the Fashionista Apprentice, with different turn-ins. Divide it so that each one favors loot available from a different gathering profession (excluding Fishing, since anyone can take that).

I'd also personally drop items from crafting professions from any of the tiers, since those professions don't need as much in the way of extra value.
