# Tabard of the Scarlet Crusade

## Post 158268 by Jcarrill0 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-03T19:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158268#p158268 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi everyone, *(been a while since I posted here)*

I wanted to suggest a way to make the old Scarlet Crusade quest chain more meaningful, especially for lower-level characters. It could be nice to bring back the option for low-level toons to earn the [Tabard of the Scarlet Crusade](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=7725), similar to how it used to be. I know this quest was removed over time due to abuse with the faction system, so that’s not what this discussion is about. This would be a new quest just for lower-level toons to get the Tabard, nothing more.

For higher-level players, the [Tabard of the Scarlet Crusade](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=23192) could still drop from [Scarlet Trainee](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=6575)s in Scarlet Monastery: Armory, keeping the old-school method intact while giving newer players a chance to experience the faction in a meaningful way.

This change could help new characters feel more connected to the Scarlet Crusade lore, encourage roleplay opportunities, and make the quest reward feel useful rather than purely cosmetic.

I’d love to hear what others think about this idea!

— JC

context of the old questline
> **Mac wrote: Thu Apr 25, 2024 1:32 am**
> It was possible years back to become friendly with the Scarlet Crusade. If you were a human (and only if you were human), there was a quest you could do in Tirisfal to get a ring that would make you friendly with them as long as you wore it.
>
>  This ring wound up being abused by non-roleplayers (who now massively outnumber the roleplayers on this server, but back then much less so). They would put on the ring and farm chests in places like Scarlet Monastery. They tried nerfing the chests' contents for a while which was also disappointing and unfair for players playing legit. And with plans for a Scarlet raid on the horizon, ultimately the decision to remove the ring from the game was made. You can actually still see the remnants of the idea. There's a Scarlet quartermaster in the outside courtyard of Scarlet Monastery who can sell to no one, and here's an ancient forum post asking how the ring worked back when it was still in-game: [viewtopic.php?p=7229](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7229)
>
>  Not saying this idea is inherently bad or shouldn't be done but rather that you have to consider there are going to be players who abuse it, especially if you're seriously proposing it on this server where it's been tried. Concerns like this should be considered and addressed. Personally, I'd like to see it happen.
