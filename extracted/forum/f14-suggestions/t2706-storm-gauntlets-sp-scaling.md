# Storm Gauntlets SP scaling

## Post 17011 by Andrejus — 2022-02-13T13:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17011#p17011 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The bug with Spell power scaling showed us, that some items can be really good, if they have SP scaling. I am talking about Storm Gauntlets and Blazefury Medailon. When I used it, it made me to change my gear a bit, and it was such a flavourful thing to have. It also makes classes like Enha shaman and Ret paladin to not just stack Attack Power and Crit, but also have some spell power. Maybe think about adding scaling to thoose items. (To not make it overtuned you can add scaling just to Storm Gauntlets, or make thoose two items to not have separated damage (Instead of (2+(SP * 0.1))+(3+(SP * 0.1)) go for (2+3)+(SP*0.1)) to add new interesting Flavors to game. ![🙂](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f642.svg)
