# Feral Adrenaline Talent - Feedback for Devs

## Post 106609 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-06T00:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106609#p106609 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is just some feedback about the new Feral Adrenaline Talent - I think it needs another look over, the issue is it's very weak for a 3 point investment, every Druid I have spoke to just skips this talent as it doesn't do anything really.

The main issue:
The cooldown is 30 seconds.
Imo this is the main problem with the talent.
Just stop and think, is getting 45% dodge chance every 30 seconds worth 3 precious talent points? (that's IF you get Crit) I don't think anyone can argue it is.

What's more is that, the dodge isn't even good for a bear, you cannot push crits and crushes off the table with a dodge.

But even with that considered it might be worth investing for something other than tanking raid bosses if the cooldown was reduced to say 8 seconds. Or 15 seconds and 100% dodge chance.

You could even go as far as adding a secondary effect, like when you dodge an attack after using up your feral adrenaline charge you gain a bonus:

Cat Form: 20 energy restored.
Bear Form: Attack speed increased by 30% for 6 seconds....

I think that matches the theme 'adrenaline'

The talent as a whole is just completely lacklustre... Hence no one picks it. If we incorporate some extra effects it might see the light of day!   smiling_turtle
