# The new survival weapons

## Post 172883 by Paidian — 2026-03-28T12:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172883#p172883 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

First of all, I'm super happy about the survival prof. changes and additions. I actually have a reason to level it to a higher level now, rather than just leveling it to the tent level and be done with it.

I'm especially super happy about the low level bow crafting options. I'm playing a traveling craftsman/vagrant challenge hunter right now and I'm finally able to get rid off the bow my character has spawned with.

That being said, and this is a very subjective point of view, but I'm a bit upset about the lack of any other white bow crafting options for higher levels. I saw some one-handed white weapons (Which, again, super appreciate it. It's difficult to find one-hand swords or axes outside of dungeons, most items in the base game are main-hand weapons so gearing a dw fury warrior while leveling is not fun) but the only two high level bows that I could see were green.

Why don't we get more white bow (or other weapons) crafting options in-between those levels, so that the profession fills a niche that, in my opinion, needs to be filled in order for these challenges to be more immersive (rather than just crafting all your stuff early on and/or straight-up buying white gear from the vendors always for the case of vagrant only challenge runs)

Thanks
