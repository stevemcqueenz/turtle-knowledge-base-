# Assortment of suggestions

## Post 166420 by Seltsamuel — 2026-01-25T12:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166420#p166420 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello! Here are some suggestions about some things.

 **Classes**

Change *Improved Ghostly Strike* of the rogue. The 5 second speed for party members feels like it was just tagged on to make players know they play with a subtlety rogue. I dont see a scenario where that could be useful. A possible change could be 1/2/3% bonus dodge for the whole party for the same 5 seconds. The same weirdness is at the warrior with *bloodthirst*. Do they really want movement speed?

Explain *Blade Rush* in the combat tree of the rogue. How does it scale with agility?

Explain *Holy Shocks* chance to reset its cooldown. Same with Blade Rush but there isnt even stated how it can reset?

Add ranks to the passive *Daybreak* of the paladin so it doesnt heal that much when you unlock it and gains power back when at 60.

 **Locations**

The new areas, Balor and Grim Reaches, look and feel interesting. A nice addition wouid be added lockboxes for rogues to train on instead of drowning in Desolace. The custom dwarven forts in Grim Reaches or the ship warft in Balor could be a nice place for them. Also add junkboxes to the dragonmaw and random fish to the murlocs or a loot table for pickpocketing in general. The mobs in Balor have one, why the Grim Reaches not?

Connect Kargath with the Shatterblade Outpost flight path. Currently you fly a to Hammerfall first, which is a huge detour.

Add cooking fires and smithies to the new areas. Most fires in the new zones can not be used to cook and some outposts lack the local ability for the NPC weapon vendor to actually make his crafts, unless they are all imports.

 **Professions**

Remove one of the bind on pick up conditions from the *Pattern or made Dragonmaw Gloves*. The Stormreaver Gloves or pattern off from the other dungeon have neither.

Give the *Mining* profession a one to one recipe to grind stone down to one of the lower quality. or add *Rock Nodes* to areas that drop stones of the areas mining level and lower.

"Finish" enchanting with adding enchants Greater Absorption and lesser versions of Crusader and co, while restricting the bigger versions by level, similar to armour kits and ring/neck enchants. Keep the old items as legacy items, though.

 **Factions**

Sprinkle *Raventusk* faction quests around the horde areas. Similar to the High Elves, they are a faction that has a goody for low level characters. Unlike from the High Elves, getting the [cloak](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80605) around level 30 is an achievement I can not fathom to ever get. It does not need to have a low level questing area but how about some Revantusk trolls sending you on quests to gather voodoo ingredients or other troll thingies in the Barrens, Silverpine and so on?
