# Abuse of disabling PvP mode during the battle for the capital (Tel'abim)

## Post 138339 by Nkarie — 2025-07-07T10:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138339#p138339 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Have a great day, dear Turtle's developers and honour pvp players!

During raids on capital cities, the attacking side often encounters an abuse of PvP mode by the defenders—whether it’s an attack on Orgrimmar or Stormwind.

PROBLEMS:

 1. Defenders enable PvE mode and run through the enemy raid to regroup, gaining a strategic advantage.

Example: An horde raid of 40 players sieges Bolvar in Stormwind, guarded by 15 aliance defenders, while another horde raid of 20 players cuts off reinforcements to the throne room (a strategic move). At this moment, defenders from outside enable PvE mode and run unscathed through both Horde raids, and suddenly Bolvar is defended by 50 players—causing the assault to fail."

 2. Defenders use PvE mode to attack.

Example: An Alliance raid of 40 players fortifies themselves in Orgrimmar’s throne room and sieges Thrall. Part of the raid guards the entrance. At this moment, Horde defenders activate PvE mode, run a group of 30 players direct in the Alliance raid, then—on command—switch back to PvP mode and wipe the raid from the inside.

This abuse completely ruins the strategy of capital city sieges, making the activity unfair and unfun.

POSIBLE SOLUTIONS:

 1. Remove PvP toggle in capital cities for non-hardcore players while maintaining it for hardcore mode.

Adventages:

 - The real idea of ​​a PvP server is that danger is everywhere.
 - The Inevitability of Payback When a Bounty Is on Your Head (Roleplay)
 - Defenders will have to defend the capital, and not just turn on the PvE mode and run to the auction

Disadventages:

 - Weak-minded players will kill everyone in a row, including low lvl(The main negative thing)
 - During a siege, it will be impossible to run through the enemy raid for players focused on PvE. On the other hand, Tel'abim is a pvp server

 2. Set the cooldown for changing pvp/pve mode to several hours. Changed mode or attacked - received a debuff for 2-3 hours.

Adventages:

 - The defenders can't spam PVE/PVP mode exploit during the siege of the capital. This will already give a lot to the attacker, you can retreat, regroup and not worry that the defenders will run to your rear in PVE mode
 - This will liven up world pvp during leveling.
Example.
In alliance locations, a lvl 25 paladin sees a lvl 27 shaman who beats mobs. By definition, a shaman cannot attack a paladin, but a paladin can attack and then re-enable the PVE mode. If the paladin attacks, he gets a debuff on returning to the PVE mode for, say, 2-3 hours. In this case, it will be impossible to gank players with impunity and re-enter the PVE mode.

Disadventages:

 -There are no downsides to this approach that come to mind. Everything is fair, if you want to calmly level up in your home location, you don't touch the enemy, if you decide to attack, then you will be ready for the subsequent war.

What you think guys? Please leave your comments.
