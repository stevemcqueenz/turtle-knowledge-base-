# 'Almost HC mode'

## Post 111303 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-04T11:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111303#p111303 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

It's my personal take on the HC challenge, proposing something similar but different.
First I wanna list my problems with the current HC:
 * It encourages you to play super-safe, questing in zones you're not supposed to be in your level.
 * Server is not the most stable. One shutdown and a monthly progress might be discarded.
 * Disallowing mixed parties makes early-level questing, specially with respawning targets a pain.

The HC I'm thinking about should be one that:
 * Encourages you to take risks, to have a real challenge instead of slaying mobs well below your level.
 * Giving you a limited amount of chance to avoid factors outside of your power negating a monthly progress.
 * Allowing a more flexible questing experience.

How I'd imagine the challenge.
Get the current HC challenge with the below changes:
 * Remove gray-questing. In fact, disallow 'almost HC' players to gain any XP from gray or green mobs or quests.
 * In exchange give them buffed XP if they kill orange or red targets (and extra XP for elites) alone in the world or as a party within an instance. Mobs give no XP if you farm them in a team in the open world.
 * 'Super Mario mode', giving you 3 lives. You die 3 times, the challenge is over. But one server instability won't grief you.
 * Allowing them to party up with whomever. Less waiting time for mob spawns. Level restriction applies though to avoid carry.

Same boosting-restictions apply (bannable).

This challenge would force you to go up against harder targets to level up (I think more in flavor for HC), no more lvl 30 questing in Westfall.
It would also allow you to join normal dungeons. There are chances you'd quit after a wipe though you have 3 lives after all.
