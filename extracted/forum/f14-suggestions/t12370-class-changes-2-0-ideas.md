Also a class 2.0 change idea:

Change kara shield to be on-use, 5 min CD, 1337 spell dmg shield, lasts 10s (instead of 3% spell reflect....woohoo less than 1 of 33 spells.......), 30s cd starts on-equip like trinkets.

New shield is now more skillful and viable for shield users.

It would help balance out a lot of classes:
Pally because there is some counter to their instant spell dmg for classes that can't kite them as easy as say a lock/mage
Warr actually using a shield ever (% time of warr using shield at 60 has to be sub 1%)
Enh Shamans more inclined to use 1-h and shield (less offensive, more defensive)

This and any type of shield balance would also make PvP less blow-up which is needed in a full progressed vanilla server.

You must admit that no one using shields hardly ever (besides ele shaman for the armor boost) does point towards things being wrong.

## Post 82354 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-22T20:57:00Z

Priest main here. I have been playing as Shadow and Smite both in PVP and in raids. I didn't heal in raids just for your information. It would be cool if other players in this thread would share opinion about their most familiar class.

Generally I think Priest is in good spot both in PVE and PVP and doesn't need many changes.

Shadow in PVP: Good. Maybe a bit overpowered. Pain Spike spell scales too well. I would nerf it.
Shadow in PVE: Good. Doesn't deal as much damage as other dps classes but their healing and mana restoration compensate it. Doesn't need any change.

Current weaknesses of the spec: doesn't have any AOE damage. And I think it's fine. No spec should do everything.

Smite in PVP: Good. With 3 pieces of ZG set and some talents spent in Holy tree, Smite specs gains unique PVP trait which is 42 yard range of your Smite spell making this spec great sniper in open area. Doesn't need any change.
Smite in PVE: Good only in short fights due to constant casting which makes you out of mana fast. The biggest issue is that this spec doesn't bring anything unique to your raid and also doesn't deal as much damage as other casters in long term so some guilds don't want any Smite to be in the raid group. My suggestion is to add debuff to enemy hit by Holy Fire (currently barely used spell) that increases spell damage taken by enemy by X% for Y seconds.

Current weaknesses of the spec: has mana problems, doesn't bring any utility to the raid group, requires up to 16% spell hit from items, has 1 button rotation - you spam only Smite and when you are OOM you spam Holy Fire rank 1.

Now the specs I don't play but I know others' opinion (correct me if I'm wrong)

Holy in PVP: Good but not as Discipline I think.
Holy in PVE: Good spec for healers without much healing power.
Current weaknesses of the spec: I don't know

Discipline in PVP: Good.
Discipline in PVE: Good healing spec for BiS geared priests that can also support any players via Power Infusion.
Current weaknesses of the spec: I don't know
