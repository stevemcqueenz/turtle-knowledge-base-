Hello Turtles!

First of all: I absolutely adore Turtle WoW, what the devs have been able to create is amazing, hats off to you all!

I know Transmog is a thing, and I really like how it is implemented in Turtle WoW.

 **However I would also love to have the option to colour/paint/dye armor pieces**. I despise having to wear armor that is so vastly mismatched (even while leveling), as I am one of those players whose true endgame is fashion.

Playing is just much more fun for me when my character actually looks awesome and not like he crashed through an Armoury on a motorcycle like in a classic cartoon, and is just wearing what stuck.

Would something like this even be possible? I know some other MMOs like Warhammer Online or Guild Wars 2 have it, but I have no idea if this would be something that is technically possible or even desired by the wider community.

Sorry if this has been asked before, search function wasn't very fruitful for me.

Would love to hear your thoughts!
