# Item budget math is the reason of burst meta

## Post 156069 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-18T14:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156069#p156069 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Items in wow designed with stat budget in mind. They can be adjusted a bit after this math, but basic values always follow that logic.
from [https://wow.allakhazam.com/wiki/Itemiza ... _%28wow%29](https://wow.allakhazam.com/wiki/Itemization_Formulas_%28wow%29)
> The complicated part of this process is that everything must be raised to the 3/2 power and then added together, and should equal the budget (also to the 3/2 power.) So:
>
>  Itemization Points 3/2 = Attribute Cost #1 3/2 + Attribute Cost #2 3/2 + ...
>
>  So the epic neck from above (which had 46.9 itemization points) might have: +40 spell damage +20 intellect +28 stamina
>
>  This would work out as...
>
>  46.91.5 = (40*0.7)1.5 + (20*1)1.5 + (28*0.67)1.5
>  321.19 = 148.16 + 89.44 + 80.64
>  321.19 (budget) = 318.24 (cost)

If something raised to the 3/2 power, means "the more of this stat on the item, the less can be added on top of it".
 5 stat value = 11,18 stat budget
 10 stat value = 31,62 stat budget (+20 for 5)
 15 stat value = 58.09 stat budget (+26 for 5)
 20 stat value = 89.44 stat budget (+31 for 5)
 25 stat value = 125 stat budget (+35 stat budget)
 30 stat value = 164.31 stat budget (+39 for 5)
 35 stat value = 207.06 stat budget (+42 for 5)
 40 stat value = 252.98 stat budget (+45 for 5)

It is easier to get multiple stats increasing damage, than to get high value of single stat.

What stats do we have to increase our damage?
They are:
strength
agility
attack power
crit
hit
armor penetration
(school) spell power
spell crit
spell hit
spell penetration

What stats do we have to increase our survivability?
stamina
armor
(school) resistance
resilence
(there are things like parry, block, dodge, but they don't really useful to non-tanking people)

So my take is - it is much easier to get a lot of "damaging" stats than "survival" stats on the items. So it lead us to situation where people have a lot of damaging stats with few defensive ones.
