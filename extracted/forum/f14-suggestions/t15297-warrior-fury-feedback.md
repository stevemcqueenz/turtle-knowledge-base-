<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104410#p104410 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I also think fury is a bit starved for talent points, since they added in some new mandatory talent nodes. For fury PVE at least 6 more talent points are needed, so you can get both "Improved Shouts" and "Unbridled Wrath".

The problem with these new changes is that it lowers the skill cap for fury dps, and introduces more RNG with the enrage proc.  Heroic strike and Hamstring increased the complexity of the rotation, but with the new changes that is gone.

I think enrage should not be RNG, there should be a way for warriors to enrage reliably. Maybe make it so you enrage after hitting 70 rage, this will also help fresh 60s without much crit/hit rating. You can put a cooldown on how often it can proc if it's too OP, maybe like every 25 seconds for a 50% uptime.

I think fury warriors should get a new rage dump ability, that cost 25-30 rage, and doesn't replace weapon swing, since heroic strike has been nerfed, and hamstring isn't spammable.

These changes would add more complexity/fun and less RNG to the warrior rotation, rewarding warriors who manage their rage correctly,and punishing warriors who spam their rage dump too much.
