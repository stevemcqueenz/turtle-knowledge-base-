# shaman

## Post 106627 by jmwg — 2024-11-06T02:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106627#p106627 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Suggestion: When a shaman has a petrified weapon, the use of Hemophagia will reduce damage by 30%, and the team will reduce damage by 5%.

When a wind rage weapon is used, the use of Hemophagia will accelerate by 30%, and the team will accelerate by 5%.

When a fire weapon or ice weapon is used, the use of Hemophagia will increase the power of the spell by 30%, and the team will increase the power of the spell by 5%,

so as to solve the problem of Shaman not having active damage reduction
