# Raid Mechanic Idea - F.O.E.

## Post 153481 by Sagabal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-27T19:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153481#p153481 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Roaming nigh-unkillable boss (think F.O.E.s from Etrian Odyssey) with a long/complicated patrol around the whole instance.  If you engage it before killing all the other bosses, or someone goes AFK in a weird spot or just walks into it not paying attention, then it's probably a wipe Hakkar-style.

## Post 153483 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-27T19:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153483#p153483 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It punishes both casuals and try-hards.
Casuals: Guy goes for a smoke and follow is either forgotten or breaks (happens) and there's a chance everyone dies.
Try-hards: If patrol route is unfortunate, ruining the whole speedrun attempt at minute 2 with something they had 0 control over.
