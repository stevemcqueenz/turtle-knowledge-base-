# Suggestion: Expanding Roleplay Features in Turtle WoW

## Post 153612 by michaelarrington — 2025-09-29T02:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153612#p153612 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone,

First of all, thank you to the Turtle WoW team for building such a unique and immersive Vanilla+ experience. I really enjoy the slower pace, the custom content, and especially the emphasis on roleplaying.

As someone who loves the RPG side of World of Warcraft, I wanted to suggest the idea of adding more roleplay-focused features. For example:

Class-specific RP quests that give small cosmetic rewards (titles, outfits, or toys) to deepen immersion.
Expanded emotes or RP tools, like sitting on chairs, lying down, or interacting with objects in inns and towns.
Seasonal or community-driven events where players can roleplay their characters’ stories together.

I think these additions would strengthen what already makes Turtle WoW so special: the focus on community and storytelling.

What do you all think? Would other roleplayers here be interested in more immersive tools or events like this?

I totally agree.
I'd also love to be able to do some things that usually is only provided by npcs, such as set myself up as a shop, where people could click on me to buy things im selling from my inventory.
I love going back to starter zones and giving some gear to new players for cheap, so to be able to set up a stall and do that would be lovely.

I'd also love the ability to create my own quests, similar to how it was possible in that Star Wars Galaxies MMO.
It could breathe so much new content into the game, and blur the lines between players and npc's, making the world feel more alive.

I would totally act as an npc if they let me xD

Maybe also, some limited time quests would bring players together. One such example, when starting as a Dwarf or Gnome in Coldridge Valley, eventually there is a point where players have to fight through the enemy infested tunnel of Coldridge pass to get out into the next town of Kharanos. It's well set up already, with many npc's waiting around in front of the tunnel, saying they're waiting for it to be cleared in a few weeks or months till they go through.
But I'd love if there was a limited time mission where a group of Dwarven NPC's gather together and proclaim it's time to clear a way through, and form a convoy of sorts with the civilians in it which players have to escort as they fight their way through the pass.

There's a few other questlines in the game which would be good opportunities for stuff like this.
