# [War Mode] Yet Another "Glyph of War Revamp" Topic

## Post 45863 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-14T20:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45863#p45863 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hiya.  Ready for *yet another* redundant proposition about the always-controversial **Glyph of War** – aka "War Mode"?  *No*?  Too bad~

In my relatively short time playing *Turtle WoW*, I've noticed that there seems to be a trend regarding War Mode and World PvP in general.  The general concensus is "if you PvP and get ganked, it's your own fault".  I *agree* with this mentality; a player can go the entire **game** without ever flagging themselves for Player-vs-Player combat*, much less enabling War Mode.  However, this does tend to create some… less-than-favorable backlash within the community.

Level 60 players camping and slaying lowbies…  Entire teams of players assembling to go harass the other faction just for kicks and giggles…  For better or worse, this is all part of the MMO experience and something that even official servers have to deal with.  Still, in the case of War Mode, I think it could be done better. (Yeah, me and a *hundred* other people…)  Here's what I propose.

(* Yes, some quests *require* PvP interactions, so these must be skipped for the sake of "passivity".)
—

Glyph of War (Current)
- You can no longer disable PvP.
- Experience gain from all sources is increased.
(30% increase.)
Glyph of War (Proposed, Edited: 05/15)
- You are **permanently flagged** for Player-vs-Player combat.
(Different wording, same meaning.)
- **Honor gain** from all sources is **increased**.
(33% increase.)
- Removed Concepts
 - You can **no longer directly interact with unflagged players**.
(No parties, no buffs, no trading, and they can't sneak-attack you.  However, the Auction House is still an option.)[/color]
This idea was a bad one from the start.  It's **never** a good idea to separate the community even further than it already is.

 - You can only attack players with a **±5 Level difference**.
(Excluding Battlegrounds and PvA zones.)
This one just isn't feasible – especially if PvA were a thing.
 
I'd also like to take a moment to propose the following additional glyph…

Glyph of Anarchy (Edited: 05/15)
- You are permanently flagged for **Player-vs-All combat**.
(Yes, that's right.  You're out for blood and you don't care whose.  Just like in Gurubashi Arena.)
- **Honor gain** from World sources is **disabled**.
- **Honor gain** from Battlegrounds is **reduced** by 50%.
- Killing other players **5 levels or lower** than you will result in a **[dishonorable kill](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Civilian)** and **honor penalty**.
(If you go ham on other players and make them miserable, then you deal with the consequences.)
- **Disabled** in Battlegrounds.
(For obvious reasons.)
- Removed Concepts
 - **No level limit.**
No longer necessary since there would be no level limit for War Mode PvP.

 - You can **no longer directly interact with unflagged players**.
(Same as above, but PvP players *can* attack you and flag *themselves* for PvA (for 5 minutes).)
Same reason as above; community division is bad.
If it's possible, I'd also like it if, when a PvA player kills another PvA player within sight of town citizenry or guards, they get attacked by guards.  However, it doesn't seem like that's a thing that happens even when the opposite faction comes to call, so never mind.

—

Anyway, thank you for reading.  I hope you enjoyed my proposal.

So, taking into account the "anti-exclusivity" changes, it would go like this:
- **All** players, regardless of "Mode" (or lack of) can **attack** enemy faction PvP players, **buff** same-faction PvP players, and attack **all** PvA players.  Attacking / buffing a "War Mode" PvP **or** "Anarchy Mode" PvA player will flag the attacker / buffer for PvP or PvA for 5 minutes, like normal.
- "War Mode" PvP players can **attack** enemy faction PvP players, **buff** same-faction PvP (or PvE) players, and attack **all** PvA players.  Attacking / buffing an "Anarchy Mode" PvA player will flag the attacker / buffer for PvA for 5 minutes.
- "Anarchy Mode" players can **attack** enemy faction PvP players, buff same-faction PvP (or PvE) players, and attack **all** PvA players.  They **cannot** attack PvP players of their own faction, nor buff PvA players unless in a party.  This mode is **disabled in Battlegrounds**. (Not very RP-friendly for traitors and the like, I know.)
