# Warrior tweak ideas

## Post 156762 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-23T18:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156762#p156762 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey!

I want to list my warrior tweak ideas here.
Will always explain why I don't like something and how I'd change it.
It is all theorycrafting, obviously no testing was possible with the changes I made up.

 **Baseline changes:**

 * Sunder Armor and Revenge threat scales decently with AP.
 **Reason:** I don't want Revenge to be a dead talent late game, specially now, that you can invest 5TP in it under the Prot talent tree.
Regarding sunder, at late dungeons I see the following many times: prot warrior tries to build up 5 sunders at a boss as expected, eating up 5 GCD time. Most DPS easily overthreats the warrior during this time, resulting in a tanking rotation which leads to the tank losing the threat. Can be avoided by DPS going soft on damage for 5 GCD but let's be honest, it rarely happens and it is unfun to wait 5 GCD per boss by doing auto attack.
 *Regarding sunder I'll list an alternative solution later.*

 * Tactical Mastery is baseline.
 **Reason:** The beauty of playing a warrior is stance-dancing, when needed. Going to battle stance for a mocking blow, going to berserker stance for fear immunity etc. Forcing them to pay 5TP or be punished for this feels bad.

 * Charge can be used in Def Stance.
 **Reason:** Tanks usually either LoS pull or rush in. Punishing prot warrior for choosing one of these options (rage penalty via stance change) is bad design in my opinion. Flavour-wise I see no problem. An army commander rushes to the enemy lines, it is fine.
 *This is much less important if Tactical Mastery becomes baseline.*

 * Thunder clap now has a 2x threat modifier.
 **Reason:** Warrior should never become an AoE tank. But this change gives a defensive ability with abysmal damage and 4 sec CD at least some merit in keeping a group of mobs at the tank. DPS could still use it, honestly, threat wouldn't be anything special even after this.

 **Talent changes:**

 **Arms:**
 * Improved Rend rework.
 **Reason:** Now, to get to Impale, you are also expected to waste 2TP on an absolute dogsh@t ability.
I'd either give improved rend a damage increase debuff on bleeding enemies or would just remove it from the Impale chain to free up 2TP.

Arms would lose 5TP via Tactical Mastery and maybe 2 more via Improved Rend.
In exchange they could receive Iron Will from Prot for the sake of PVP-ers.

I'm mostly happy with Fury.

 **Prot:**
 * Improved Revenge and Reprisal Rework.
Improved Revenge would lose stun but would keep the CD reduction and receive the rage refund from Reprisal.
Reprisal would give Revenge a 50/100% chance to hit an additional target. This plus the decent threat scaling of the ability would give prots a spammable cleave.
 **Reason:** Dungeon tanking. Again, prot wouldn't become an AoE tank but would be more reliable with packs. Imagine bear with swipes but 1 less target in exchange of more threat.

 * Improved Intervene would also turn Intervene from a rage spender to a rage generator.
 **Reason:** If you tank as a warrior, chances are that 1-2 mobs run to an overthreating caster. Intervene would become much more interesting if it turned into a 2 for 1 repositioning and rage generation tool. This ability is more relevant to dungeons, exactly the places where warriors rage starve more anyway.

 * Improved Disarm is garbage but let's keep it to not overcomplicate things.
 * Defensive Tactics is too controversial. I keep it as it is.

 * One-Handed Weapon Specialisation would also give your main-hand weapon attacks up to 25% chance to apply a stack of sunder.
 **Reason:** Let's face it, the number of warriors per raid comps is way lower than in Vanilla. There are many other viable specs here. We used to see around 1/3 of the raiders being warriors and nowadays the ration based on my experience is around 1/10. Problem is that we expect the same sunder duty from much less people.
What used to be easy: pressing sunder once by every second warrior and each target was on full stacks now requires many GCDs and at many times you can't even sunder up each target.
Making sunders more consistent would help here and would give prots a unique ability: sunder-spammers.

 * Concussive Blow would also automatically grant 3 stacks of sunder on the target if it hits.
 **Reason:** Same as above. Also, this would free up many GCDs for the prot warrior at bosses, starting normal rotation earlier.

Prot would lose 5TP because of losing Iron Will.
Possible replacement talents:
 * Up to 100% of the shield's armor is being ignored when calculating rage generated when receiving damage.
 * Extra rage generation after receiving a critical hit.
 * Or even Adding a mocking blow-effect to arrows, ensuring that the DPS can shoot at the pulled mob, it would still approach the tank.
 * Mocking Blow CD reduction would also be an option to fill up TP.

 **How would warriors look like after this?**
Each warrior would stance-dance more, probably. Besides:
Fury mostly the same.
Arms also mostly the same, maybe with a Rend-related melee-debuff, and with an extra PVP talent.
Prot would benefit the most. Their threat would be okay with Shield Slam/Revenge rotation, Concussive Blow would shorten sunder times and they'd receive some added mobility.
Some more sunders here and there and one more benefit based on the talent we'd choose to fill up 5TP.
