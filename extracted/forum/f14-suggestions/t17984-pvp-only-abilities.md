## Post 122482 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-13T14:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122482#p122482 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

That is interesting question.
I do admit that PvE only abilities exist. For example Taunt-like buttons or Feint-like ones or Tranquilizing Shot or Pickpocketing.
I feel like 60-80% of abilities are already there for PvP.
Raid content is typically immune to CC abilities: slow, interrupt, root, stun, poly, fear etc. Raids are more immune than dungeons.
Poison-related skills are more PvP-oriented than PvE.
Mortal strike is pure PvP ability because there are other alternatives for PvE for that cost.
Blink/charge-like abilities are mostly for PvP. You don't need that much mobility in PvE.
Repentance before CC2 was PvP ability and I don't really like that "beasts repent their sins" after CC2.
Mana-drain/burn related abilities can be used in PvE, but this setting usually has insane amounts of mana, so it effectively useless. But in PvP it shines bright.
Disarming abilities are very useful in PvP and I don't really know how they work in turtle raid setting.
Stuff which work "after being victim of critical strike" is mostly for PvP or for tanks.

I don't think you need to technically grey-out abilities to prevent their use in PvE. Mobs can just be immune to stuff and players remove those abilities from hotbar.
I think it is totally possible to invent new type of CC if it helps balancing (like Death Coil is different type of Fear).
Also I think it is sad that wow has rich variety of abilities and it is reduced to DPS race in raids.
