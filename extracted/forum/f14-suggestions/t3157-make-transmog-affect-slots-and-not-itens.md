# Make transmog affect Slots and not Itens.

## Post 19250 by Melkormarkov — 2022-06-19T18:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19250#p19250 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have seen this in a different server and i think it's a great ideia. By making let's say my hand have a certain look of an Iten every time i place any gloves there instead of a certain glove you make the experience much more enjoyable for everyone, mainly because you can now enjoy transmog since level 1 pretty much. The way transmog works here it only rewards lvl 60 players with BIS gear, which i find kind of a waste of potencial. People love transmog, and it makes the game more enjoyable, especially if you are leveling with slow and steady, you can enjoy your char during the whole process rather than once in like 3 months or something.
