# New PvP gear should have it's own custom models

## Post 170618 by Dbl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-11T22:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170618#p170618 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I was and still am really excited about the upcoming 1.18.1 patch Nightmares of Ursol and the new PvP changes but upon watching the trailer I was left disappointed. It seems that new PvP gear won't actually be "new", it will just have different stats. I was really hoping that there would be new armor models (especially after introducing the new honor point system) that would make playing PvP even more worthwhile,but sadly that doesn't seem to be the case. Please give new custom PvP gear akin to how Wotlk and TBC PvP gear was implemented. Ypu already have a wonderful team and the new Timbermaw sets are perfect, use it more! This patch is a step in the right direction but we need more custom PvP rewards like new gear, mounts, pets etc. and I think everyone can agree on that, thanks for the hard work
