# Warlock Legacy Tier 2 Tweaks - Focus on Pets

## Post 83002 by Krotux (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-12-28T14:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83002#p83002 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We are getting new versions of warlock tier gear that focuses on destruction. So it doesnt make sense for legacy sets to also focus on destruction and just do a poor job at it.

Let's change the legacy bonuses to just focus on demonology instead of just being a worse version of the new one. Or consider making it another option.

For tier 2:

Change (3) set bonus from increased SP to +15% critical chance buff to pets

Change (5) set bonus from 20 stam and 130 resist to pets to 20% attack speed and 130 resist to pets

Change (8) set bonus from -20% threat on destruction spells to +50% damage to pets.
These seem like massive buffs but remember that warlock pets are not that strong to begin with and don’t scale. So this would be a way to make them scale with some gear while still maintaining the classic feel of it all.

Maybe tier 3 can be similar?
