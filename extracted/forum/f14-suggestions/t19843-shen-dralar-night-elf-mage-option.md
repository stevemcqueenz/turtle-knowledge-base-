# Shen'dralar / Night elf mage option

## Post 135499 by Winterstar — 2025-06-12T17:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135499#p135499 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there

I've been playing on this server for a while now and absolutely loving it. I'm a huge fan of night elves and their lore, have been since I was a kid, read all their books (from WOTA trilogy to Illidan) but there's a specific part of their lore that I'm extremely fond of and that I feel is often overlooked: the Highborne, especially the Shen'dralar. I know their presence can be controversial, especially considering their history with arcane magic and the general stance of the Sentinels. But I really believe there could be room for them here, especially for roleplayers and fans of their niche.

Uncorrupted Shen'dralar do exist. While they're rare, they offer a unique perspective within the kaldorei. I'm not suggesting they be made fully accepted by Darnassus or placed within the mainstream society, lore-wise, that wouldn't make sense. But I do think there's potential to include them as a fringe or secretive element, and I have a couple of ideas for how it could work:

First idea (more feasible):
They could exist as hidden individuals, night elf mages secretly practicing arcane magic, much like human or orc warlocks. Arcane magic was once a central part of kaldorei society, so it's believable that some might still preserve it in secret, operating from the shadows.

Second idea (more ambitious but very cool):
They could be offered as a sub-option or alternate path within the night elf character creation. Instead of Sentinels, you'd choose "Shen'dralar" as a cultural variant. They could have slightly different customization, jewelry, hair color, less feral and more fancy facial markings, to set them apart visually. They could start in a small custom camp zone in Feralas (perhaps near Dire Maul), have the classes like Mage and Warlock, and remove Druid and Priest. Their status could be that of outcasts or exiles, starting with an “Unfriendly” reputation with Darnassus. After completing some quests, they could be teleported to neutral or friendly zones like Elwynn or the high elf areas to prove their worth.

This server has been an amazing experience for me, and I'm truly grateful for everything the devs have created. The only thing I feel is missing is the presence of Highborne/night elf mages (which is what I main in retail honestly), which I've always been a huge fan of (I personally love queen Azshara and the night elf empire times). I hope this suggestion is considered, I think it would add a lot of depth, variety, and RP potential for those of us who love this side of kaldorei history, if not, I appreciate if at least you read it, thank you for your time!

It's 100% true most kaldorei rejected the arcane, but not ALL Highborne were corrupted or reckless. The Shen'dralar specifically survived the Sundering by isolating themselves and preserving their knowledge.

What I'm suggesting isn't about rewriting the lore, but exploring the gray areas, like outcasts, secret practitioners, or even Shen'dralar exiles trying to find a place in the world again. Given that the server already includes demon hunters or necromancer skins, archetypes that the kaldorei would arguably find even more offensive, adding a Highborne/arcane element doesn't feel too far-fetched.
