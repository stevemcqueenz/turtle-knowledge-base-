# Bael Hardul FP

## Post 108353 by Harkonnan (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-17T18:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108353#p108353 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I love the addition of Bael Hardul in Stonetalon, in addition to the Flight Point there.
Problem is, it has some really whacky flight paths.

Flying to Ratchet, as an example, which is a common neutral path for most players to get to STV as well as other areas. It literally takes you in a full circle of the entire Stonetalon zone, almost going back over Bael Hardul, and up through ashenvale...it's just crazy long and makes no sense. There should be a direct flight to ratchet.

I have a feeling that adding new flight paths is more difficult then just adding a new flight point, but it's really inhibiting to want to quest in that zone knowing that the flight will take almost as long as flying down the whole continent.

And I'm sure there are some other Flight paths that could use some QoL. And while were at it, how about some FP's that were added to TBC for some very good reasons? example: the flight point at the Rebel Camp for Alliance is sorely needed.
