# Holy Paladin 2h

## Post 129208 by Soymage — 2025-05-01T01:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129208#p129208 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, the ironclad talent limits holy paladins to using shields, which they weren't previously limited to in classic. There are a number of two handed maces/swords in the game that buff healing power. It would be nice to change the Ironclad talent in the Holy tree to the following:

Ironclad
"Increases your healing power by 2% of your armor. In addition, increases your healing power by 80% of your two-handed weapon's damage per second"

This calculation would give a two handed weapon like Fist of Cenarius about equal healing power to a shield of the same level.
