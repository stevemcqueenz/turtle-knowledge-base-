# Please Make Old Jonah Untargettable

## Post 134212 by Timmykaido — 2025-06-04T05:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134212#p134212 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Basically, most Alliance-affiliated mobs (that are not guards) in Northshire Valley are untargettable and do not attack you / cannot be attacked. Old Jonah (the Hunter Trainer) is the one exception. This is annoying for two reasons. The first is that he's just kinda annoying to be attacked by as a Lunatic. The second (and significantly more important reason) is that it is comically easy to grief Human Hunter players out of a trainer / quest. I do not intentionally grief players by killing Old Jonah (I ignore him most of the time--while griefing rules only state that it would be only be griefing if I'm farming him for a long period of time, I'm not enough of an asshole to kill him every time he attacks me), but sometimes I just get annoyed at him (inflicting dazed on me being the main reason) and want him gone so I kill him. I've actually had people flag themselves to attack me when I do this. Making him completely untargettable, which is the solution that twow has gone with for literally every other important mob in every other starter zone, would fix both of these issues.
