Can it not be an option to revert back?

I don't want to be ungrateful, as there are so many changes TWoW has made to Vanilla which have been very welcomed, changes Blizzard should have been the ones to implement, or implement sooner, instead of so many catastrophes it chose instead:

 - Adding High Elves and Goblins!
 - Adding Gilneans and Kul'Tiran's to Vanilla, even if not exactly the way Blizz did it.
 - Vanity items to help with RP!! Blizz really could have done so much for RP and they really dropped the ball.. so Thank you!
 - Radio Stations? ha! Awesome!
 - Challenges like slower leveling, etc! Brilliant!
 - Giving us new secondary professions? Fantastic!
 - Giving us the option for faster rested XP, but making us work for it, the Vanilla way? LOVE IT!
 - Giving Goldshire (maybe elsewhere too) a quick boost in speed via a work-for-it griffin ride? Love it so much!
 - And this latest patch, adding Karazhan?? Of course!! Thank you so much for that!
 - etc

You guys really came up, and implemented so many great things. It's easy to see why it's been so popular!

However, please, don't change the classes? Until this way done, one could still mostly follow all those Vanilla guides many, many people went out of their way to create, even in TWoW despite all the great changes. I know I was really looking forward to reliving what I had done back then, and what my friends got to experience on their classes too.

Can we not put them back? Or have the option to revert back to it? Some of us have very busy schedules, and cannot play often enough to have accomplished this by now - heck, I only found your server this past year!
