## Post 16631 by Daemonicas — 2022-02-02T13:05:00Z

I like being a rebel.  I like the pirates life. I like to get drunk and sing songs or shove a shiv into someone's back when they try to steal me wench.  I don't mind being wanted Kill on Sight by the hypocrite wussies of the Steamwheddle Cartel.  Its the price you pay to live truly free without the constraints of law or government.

That being said, the Bloodsail Retreat is lacking the proper infrastructure to truly be independent.  We have no profession  trainers (a big deal if you need recipes from Steamweddle allied towns).  No flight path (a pirate forced to swim is embarrassing)!   No mail box, auction house, bank (there's an anvil but no forge)?!?  Lastly I'm a Captain of a ship that cannot go anywhere.  Allowing this ship to show up unexpectantly at ports and vanish just as fast would be great for the lore of piracy.

I can see what the developers are trying to do and I applaud that.  Please keep us sea rats in mind and consider these humble additions along with more quests in the future to truly realize what it is to be a Bloodsail Buccaneer!

Captain Daemonicas
