# Making SWV more interesting

## Post 140102 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-17T16:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140102#p140102 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I wanna write about why I think SWV is much less popular than BM.
Foreword: creating a level 60 dungeon is always a bit tricky.
Target audience is narrow and they overgear what you can offer very fast.
Still, a level 60 dungeon can work, as proven by BM.

 **1. Problem: Accessibility**
SWV position is optimal for one faction, awful for the other.
Can't be really placed elsewhere if we keep it as Stormwind Vault though.
A direct teleport would be catastrophic, just imagine the gankfest if horde could just casually port mid-Elwyn any second.
I think the solution is the Gnomeregan way. Add an underground entry to the outside of the dungeon where you gotta descend. Then allow a port inside, from where it would be impossible, or at least extremely hard to get to the surface.
Finally, enable the second teleport in Booty Bay next to the goblin guy, which is currently just for show.
Booty Bay is a prime access point, very close to Grom'gol zep destination.
Horde could access the place much more easily.

 **2. Challenges vs rewards**
BM is a glorified gear-check. If tank has enough armor and HP + healer is awake, the dungeon is successful.
It is incredibly hard to ásspull. There are some mechanics but nothing too harsh.
SWV is vile, though. Move wrong, pull in a wrong order, forget the mobs hidden in a room and death is probable. There are much more mechanics going on, making the dungeon (imo) better-executed than BM.
But you get rewarded with meh items. Maybe some tanking prebis.
BM has arguably better blues, has many epics, has a bunch of arcane essences... and rep.

 **3. BM rewards you for revisiting**
You want the dragon? You want some cool epics for os? Or a big bag?
BM has its own rep which you can turn into valuable stuff.
Also, you run it maybe 3 times and you get an insta-port right to the entrance.
BM rewards you for attending. SWV lures you into a trap room and kills you with rats. *Every time.*

tldr:
I think SWV needs a Gnomeregan-like port system which also prevents easy access to outside-Elwynn.
Some interesting epics. Possibly a shield (a dungeon purple shield would be awesome) and maybe a healing trinket.
A valuable material dropping every now and then from trash, like righteous orbs.
(Or something else if it would make Strat Living obsolete).
And possibly a rep system. This would be challenging though since you can't just tie it directly to Stormwind.
Orcs and trolls being exalted with SW would be messed up. With some lore we could add a faction 'overseeing' this prison. Or just link it to an existing faction, accessible to both Alliance and Horde.

PS: It's more of a personal opinion but I don't think it is a good design if one class just becomes super-OP in a dungeon environment. Half of the place was built with warlocks in mind. Changing some elementals to f.ex. dragonkins (?), undeads (?) (whatever) would nerf banish a bit, specially at last boss.
(And don't get me started with the imp-fiesta.)
