# Benediction

## Post 98249 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-30T20:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98249#p98249 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

There are two things regarding Benediction which bug me.
 1st: If you fail the event, you are closed out for 2 hours. If you are failing and ask a nearby friend to join so the reaper comes and you die, you can retry it immediately. This logic rewards you for cheezing the event.
 2nd: ES made Benediction (talking about the healer part mostly) irrelevant. The claw, which drops every time gives a better mace, not even mentioning the staff which admittedly has a lower drop rate. Benediction is a very unique priest item but ES makes it too irrelevant too fast. (Honestly, if you do the ES attune, including the 90G payment for the rod and have late blues, you can easily find a PUG for normal mode because most guilds are healer starved).

In short:
 1. Benediction quest rewards you for cheezing the event.
 2. ES normal mode can be entered at early raid gear as a healer and you immediately get an upgrade over Benediction.

What I'd propose:
 1. Either remove the 2 hour waiting if you do the event as designed, or give the 2 hours punishment (maybe even more) if you wanna cheeze it.
 2. Either buff Benediction or nerf ES healing mace.

PS: I don't mean that ES healer mace by itself is better than Benediction. But it's a 1H. Realistically that mace + any low-raid OH is better.
