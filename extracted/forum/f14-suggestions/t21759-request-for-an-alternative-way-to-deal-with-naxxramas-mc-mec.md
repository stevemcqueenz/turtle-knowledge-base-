# Request for an alternative way to deal with Naxxramas MC mechanics

## Post 150713 by Lolbreegg — 2025-09-07T22:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150713#p150713 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title suggests I'm proposing the addition of an alternative way to MC Faerlina's worshippers and Razuvious' students. This could simply be in the form of an item purchasable from the argent dawn at a specified rep, that is only usable in Naxxramas and only works on the two types of adds that are required to be mind controlled to complete the encounters.

I believe this change would be beneficial in general, preventing situations where a raid team fails to get off the ground because of lack of Priests. It would also give raids more comp flexibility, and the ability to allow Priests to retain their focus on healing if the raid team is seriously lacking heals. I feel this change is in line with the spirit of TWOWs implementation of raid scaling, which seems to be aimed at making raids more accessible for smaller groups and growing raid teams.

I thank you in advance for your time, and I hope you give this some consideration. It may not be a common situation groups find themselves in, but it is rather unfortunate (and I've always though it was poor design choice to begin with) that Naxx is so heavily gatekept behind a single class with no alternatives.
