# Allow Artisan challengers to take all professions at once

## Post 115212 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-01-02T09:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115212#p115212 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The idea of the challenge is nice, but not fleshed out enough in my opinion.
Currently you are only able to take 2 professions, and one will always be blacksmithing (or one that gives you a weapon), the other would be for your armor (tailor, lw, bs), which really restricts players from picking up the more interesting professions like the new Jewelcrafting, enchanting, not to mention gathering professions, forcing them to farm gold to buy mats, which is painful and against the spirit of the challenge, which is to make your gear from start to finish. And don't get me started on "juggling" professions, it's simply insane and not viable unless you have 16 hours playtime per day.

Here is my suggestion to fix it - let people with this challenge take ALL professions. No limit. Let them mine their ores, forge their weapon, skin and tan hides for armor, sew their bags, craft their jewerly, engineer their trinkets and gadgets, pick their own herbs and brew their own potions (prohibit potions and food that aren't crafted by them), enchant their hard-earned gear to make it more powerful. Add recipes to cooking, so they can (and must) make their own mana drinks. Make a craftable fishing rod (from survival?), so they can catch their own fish and cook it for dinner. Make it the ultimate "self-made, self-found mode".

And the restriction would be that all mailing and trading with other people would be prohibited. If they want to disable the challenge mode (which stays active even at 60), they would have to abandon all professions but two. This means you have to farm your own materials, but is also there to protect the economy, preventing max levels to have all professions just for their CDs and flooding the market.

It's not really solo though. You can still group and do quests and dungeons with other players, you just can't trade with them.

I'm afraid that's the tradeoff for being a true artisan, since without this restriction, the econony will suffer if people were to suddenly have all professions at once and be able to sell their CDs (mooncloth, arcanite, cured hides etc).

Besides, this mode already is pretty much as self-found as it gets, because you can only equip things you make yourself. Why not take it all the way?
More playstyles will suit more types of people and I do believe that is a good thing, no?

I know I'd love to give Artisan a try if I could craft everything from all professions, but as it is now, it feels very half-baked and unfinished.

EDIT: I guess an alternative solution would be to disable the cooldown recipes for Artisans and let them trade, so they can buy any arcanite, cured rugged hides and stuff they might need.
