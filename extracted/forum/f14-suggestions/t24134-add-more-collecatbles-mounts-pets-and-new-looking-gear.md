**True endgame:Collecting Mounts, Pets and Transmogs**

I know alot of classic people will hate me for this but I believe that the true endgame to almost any game is looking cool and collecting rare stuff. This is backed up by Blizzard selling more and more mounts and pets and transmogs each expansion, why? Because love it or not people are engaging the most with collecting stuff because when you finish all your raids and grind up all the rep and got multiple alts what now? I really think that they should continue adding more different armor sets ([viewtopic.php?t=24146](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=24146) like these ones for example), mounts and pets but the twist here would be that they are not flashy and are still in the classic feel, turtle has already many cool custom armors and mounts but I feel like the number should be increased and that the custom gear should be made for lower levels too to keep things exciting . There is also a huge RP community tied to the server and it's pretty important to provide as much options as you can to make your character unique, also it's pretty immersion breaking entering SW/ORG and seeing everyone transmog the same 2 - 3 endgame sets just because there's nothing ''cooler'' to wear. I promise you that it will increase the lifetime of the server significantly. I'm really interested in what do you all think about this, don't hate me lol

P.S Also as a side note classic has the least amount of gear and seeing as many are class or type specific it reduces the possibility of looking cool/the way you want to significantly.
