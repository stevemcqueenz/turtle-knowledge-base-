# CC2 Sunk Cost Fallacy

## Post 111710 by Neechy (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-07T18:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111710#p111710 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear turtle team,

Please don't give into the sunk cost fallacy with CC2. To be clear, what I mean by the fallacy in this context is:

 *"We recognize the player base is largely unhappy with the degree of class changes, HOWEVER we have already invested 5000 hours collectively into this endeavour therefore we can't go back at this point or take a different approach".*

I am obv not in communication with devs so I don't know to what extent they may give into this fallacy; but I do know it is a common mistake that teams and businesses make.

From my discussions with players and reading many forum threads, once turtle wow makes the decision to introduce changes that deviate from vanilla wow they very rarely, if ever, decide to revert said changes, even if community feedback suggests disapproval.

In the context of post-CC2 feedback, we are seeing this as well. The devs have made some adjustments on racial nerfs, but they have not just reverted nerfs entirely. The devs have made some adjustments on talent changes, but have not just reverted talent changes entirely. The devs have made some adjustments on new abilities or removal of old abilities, but have not just reverted those ability changes entirely.

This is what I mean by sunk cost fallacy in action. The devs have introduced all these changes, many of which have been shown to be problematic, but because so much time was allegedly spent on designing these changes, devs would rather just keep them (with maybe some minor tweaks) than admit that it is the changes themselves that should be reverted.

Most people came to this server with a vanilla+ expectation that would be focused on new content, expanding the vanilla world horizontally, with just minor adjustments to classes to increase viability of weaker specs. If the dev team realized this more going into development of 1.17.2, perhaps much of those 5000 hours could have led to fleshing out content instead of overhauling entire specs and class identity that players have loved since 2004. Did vanilla wow have class issues? Yes. But despite those issues, it was still a very well designed class ecosystem - because if it was really so bad, tens of thousands of people would not keep coming back to vanilla wow 20 years later.

In conclusion, the turtle team has done an amazing job with the design of new content - this is clearly their strongpoint. And while we appreciate the efforts at class improvements, the reality is vanilla class design is very delicate and any changes should be introduced very gradually and minimally. CC2 has been a massive overhaul, and it would be a fallacy to think that the team absolutely has to keep digging itself down this path. Moreover, those 5000 hours do not have to been as in vain. Even if much needs to be reverted, changes can be held on the backburner until a time when they may be reintroduced more strategically. Please consider the possibility of reverting changes and see if the team can release Kara40 etc. in the meantime, while it continues to work on this issue.

Once again, thanks for all the work you do, and I hope this post can resonate. It will probably be my last post for a long while, as I don't think there's much more to be said on this topic that I and others in the community haven't already, and it's unclear what kind of impact this feedback has at the end of the day.
