# Add place on discord for HC partners.

## Post 44886 by Drifnur — 2023-05-08T07:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44886#p44886 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

On the discord there is a specific hc section of channels.
There's the lounge which is more for general chatter and questions.
Gallery for pics, dungeons for forming dungeons groups and trade for trade.
Pretty self explanatory.

It would be nice to have a place where people can look for duo-trio runs.
To do so here's your current options:
 1. post in #hc-lounge. The issue here is that it can very quickly get lost in the chatter since it's the busiest hc channel. Compare that to the dungeon or trades one which move slowly.
 2. post in #lf-group. The issue here is that it isn't intuitive to post here considering there's the hc section on the discord. If I want hc centric stuff I go to any of the channels above. It's weird that if you lfg but for a dungeon there's an hc channel for it, but other types of lfg, such as a partner, make you go here.

Solutions:
 1. Make a new channel called hc-LF-partner. This would be the very granular and specific solution. Much like the dungeon channel, there's no confusion to be had. If you post here you're looking for a leveling partner. The downside is an extra channel.
 2. Rename the hc-dungeons channel to hc-lf-group. It would essentially be a copy of the lf-group channel but hc specific; broadening it's range to more than only dungeons. The downside is more activity on the channel, but given the channel sees little activity as it is, the impact will be minimal.

TL;DR  if one is called ⁠hc-dungeons why is the other called ⁠lf-group and not #lf-dungeons. Just make ⁠hc-dungeons into #hc-lf-group.
