# 3.3.5 Client

## Post 163497 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-12-22T16:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163497#p163497 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I know this represents a huge undertaking in terms of both time and resources, but the truth is, almost two years were wasted waiting for an Unreal Engine client to be released, only for it to fail completely for one reason or another.

Now, I'm here to reiterate my proposal. Stop denying that Turtle WoW needs a new client or that the current one is fine, because the old client is on the verge of imploding after so much added content.

Advantages of Client 3.3.5:
 - Improved optimization: This client was the peak for Classic, finally adding real-time shadows, a longer draw distance, and better visual effects.

 - More modding possibilities and improvements from both developers and the community: If I'm not mistaken, this is where the original HD project, later backported to Turtle WoW, originated. It increases the number of polygons and the texture resolution the client can support.

 - Achievements: Do I need to explain? There's a lot of potential here for PvP, both World PvP and Battleground PvP.

 -WMO: I've been asking for improvements to Stormwind and the capital cities for years. There are projects like Open Azeroth that greatly improve the capital cities and are open source; they can be easily integrated into Turtle WoW.

Among many other improvements, stop denying that Turtle WoW needs a better client, because it clearly does. And if two years were invested in something that led nowhere, it's only fair to listen to the players and work on something that has a future and will cost much less than a client built from scratch.
