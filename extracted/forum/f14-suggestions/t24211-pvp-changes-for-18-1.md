# PvP changes for 18.1

## Post 170312 by aöü — 2026-03-07T22:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170312#p170312 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Dear Twow team and community,

I carefully read through [PvP in 1.18.1](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=24132) and I am definitely looking forward to it.
Twow is a free game and imo the best WoW version out there. So read everything that follows as critique on a high level. The game as it stands right now is very enjoyable, and I've learned to trust the Twow team to only add to that.

_____

I have talked to a lot of players about the upcoming changes and we've compiled 3 points with great potential to enhance the Twow PvP experience:

 **- Add skill-dependent rewards** (gear, or just cosmetics like pets, mounts, titles or tmogs), with players only being compared to other players of the same class.

Here's why, and what is meant by that:

The current system only has time-dependent rewards: A single player's impact on winning or losing a bg usually is relatively small. And even if a player carries a bg on their own, it only nets them a little more honor/time. In 40-man raids, the impact of a single player usually is also relatively small. For most players, being good at your class just saves a few minutes of raiding time and does not decide if you get an upgrade or not.

I wish for a change that awakens the competitive spirit in players. A field of battle where you would have to push yourself to your limits and beyond to succeed. A challenge that rewards you for actually being good at your class (independent of where your class would currently rank in comparison to other classes). A place where the amount of LIPs, FAPs and protection potions in a player's bags does not matter, and where it doesn't matter for how long you play, but **how** you play.

I am aware that, with the Conquest Points for high arena ratings, the outlined changes already are a small step into this direction of skill-dependency. But as it's currently phrased, a high arena rating only gives you conquest points faster, as you can easily put your hands on them by joining wPvP events or Call to Arms bgs. I am also aware of the fact not all players want to play Arena. But it just represents the best ground for competitive play within a closed environment.

Why class-internal skill assessment? Because I know there will always be a class having an edge over others in arena, even if it's just with compositional advantages. The game can't be perfectly balanced. So I would love a system where, in the grand scheme, you only compete against other players of your class.

The introduction of the Gladiator title in Vanilla tbc flourished the pvp scene back in the day, even though some players quickly realized they happened to have picked the wrong class to have a decent shot at it. Despite this fact, for many pvp lovers, this time was an absolute highlight in WoW history. And at the start, it was just a title - imagine for example a blood-red wolf given to the highest rated top 2 % of players of each class. Something like this would revitalize the competitive scene. And how awesome would it be meeting an opposing player with a mount, item or title like that? Not because they queued bgs 24/7. Not because their class is the strongest. But because they're actually good at the class they enjoy playing.

 **- Change the planned t1/2/3 PvP gear Item Levels from 71/77/92 to 71/80/89, respectively.**

Comparing different set strengths by just Item Level numbers can be tedious, so to better illustrate the current and the upcoming state of affairs I created this little graphic:

[![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/MHsMqJ3Q/pvp-gear-change.png)](https://postimg.cc/p5FXJ4HW)

t1 PvP set: A PvP set for starters with Item Level 71 makes perfect sense to me. Even if players are very active in PvP after hitting 60, by the time they get access to the current rare set (at PvP rank 7, 8 and 10, with an Item Level of 68), the gear they get from raids already makes the rare PvP set obsolete for the most part. Giving it a good kick in strength (and maybe making it available a little earlier) is a sensible change. In my eyes, with the planned Item Level of 71, the Twow team hit the bull's-eye.

t2 PvP set: Although the planned Item Level of 77 already is buff for the first epic PvP set, it would still be pretty much on BWL level in strength. It might make sense to push this close to AQ40 gear in strength (at least on Nord and TA). Raids are progressed so far that it's not easy to compete against many players, even if you have been active in PvP for weeks or months before. **Assuming this set will probably already cost a good amount of honor to acquire, it should be a valid purchase and give you, with good play, at least a decent chance against Naxx-geared players.**

t3 PvP set: **Setting the Item Level of this set too high is a very bad idea for two reasons:**
 1) So far on Twow, the message to the playerbase was "If you want to reach your best gear for PvP, go grind raids for months and you might eventually get there, even if you don't particularly enjoy raiding." I know many PvP lovers who are in the process of doing exactly that for quite some time now. **Upping the gear quality obtainable through PvP by whopping 18 Item Levels highly devalues all these players' dedication** to gear up. Keep in mind, if you compare a PvP set and a PvE set with the same Item Levels, the PvP set usually outclasses the PvE set by good margin when used for PvP. This means that a huge part of the effort PvP players have put into raiding would turn out to be in vain with 18.1.
 2) Most PvP players I know take great pleasure in theorycrafting and being faced with a multitude of possible gear combinations. They try to combine set bonuses from different sets to match their playstyle, their talent builds or overall goals in PvP. **With an ilvl 92 PvP set, this whole part of gear planning is destined to die out.** The gearing process would dumb down to "Buy best PvP set. Enchant it. You're done!". Currently, for most classes, the best possible gear for PvP consists of roughly 80-95 % K40 items. (For most classes it's good to mix in a few items for their effects or procs, or 2 pieces of the current ilvl 74 epic PvP set for the first set bonus.) While this percentage is too high in my eyes (PvP lovers should at least come relatively close to their bis gear for PvP by just doing what they love: PvP), the upcoming change could completely revert this balance. I would love a world where PvP bis is roughly 50% of both worlds, and where you would actually have to think about how to combine sets from different sources to maximize your influence in PvP.

 **- Keep set bonuses relevant and interesting (both for upcoming and existing sets)**

I have read from many classes in the forums that some set bonuses are either weak, uninteresting or just downright useless.
One example is mage's T2.5 3-set-bonus "Your Blizzard spell has a 30% chance to be uninterruptible." Not only is it unreliable, in most scenarios it also doesn't even add any value at all.
In my eyes, "+20 [any stat]", albeit at least being relevant, is also a meager set bonus. In relation to what I wrote above, good set bonuses make you think about how you play your class. An example of a better set bonus is warrior's T2.5 3-set-bonus: "Decreases the rage cost of all Warrior shouts by 35%." It instantly raises some questions to the reader. "How often do I use Demo, Intimidating and Battle Shout in the average scenario? How valuable is the given rage cost reduction?", and maybe even "When it's important to use a Shout, how often do I lack the rage required?" More of these type of bonuses please! I also love it when set bonuses impact both PvP and PvE.

_____

tldr:
Add reward(s) for highest rated X % of each class.
Rethink PvP Gear Item Levels to keep T3.5 gear relevant but not op.
Keep set bonuses interesting.
