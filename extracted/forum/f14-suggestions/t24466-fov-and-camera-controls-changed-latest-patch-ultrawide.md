# FoV and camera controls changed latest patch (ULTRAWIDE)

## Post 172132 by Svnflow — 2026-03-24T12:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172132#p172132 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

This patch changed several things in the camera controls, and I really miss the view and feel of the previous patch.
Note I am playing on ultrawide monitor (5120x1440 resolution).

I'll try my best to describe the issues:

The FOV doesn't feel like the previous patch, even though it's set to the same value before in the launcher, I can't explain it but the whole game looks very claustrophobic since the update.

Another note on claustrophobic feeling, it is visible everywhere, but especially bad in indoor scenarios like the Deadmines dungeon - my character model seems more centered and blocking the view than before.

The camera controls when mouselooking are erratic and almost impossible to control. It happens constantly, what feels like sudden burst of short-duration infinite mouse sensitivity, so my camera looks 100% up or down and spins around like crazy, even though I barely moved my mouse, it's giving massive headaches and it's quite dangerous in hardcore during hectic situations I might add. Other up-to-date private servers suffer from similar mouselook sensitivity issue on ultrawide - **but Turtle didn't have this problem until this recent patch!**

The new OPTIONS tab has no settings to address the above issues, trust me, I've tried every combination.
