# Suggestion; 'Cosmetic' RP Sets

## Post 2859 by Soreka — 2019-05-15T06:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2859#p2859 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, a post was made about this awhile back I noticed. A link to it shall be provided here:
[viewtopic.php?f=14&t=75](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=75)
This is not me attempting to steal this man's idea, rather to expand upon it, and to hopefully have it get more attention.

This suggestion is basically what it says on the title; adding in cosmetic / RP sets into the game. To note, when I say cosmetic, I personally mean just that. Sets which are there for looks, with no real armor rate or stats to speak of, as well as being cloth to be used by all classes.
These sets would be to look cool, and be for RP. They could be available from vendors scattered about the world as individual items, costing gold, or through the Turtle Shop.
It would make sense to lock certain sets behind factions if this was ever implemented.

There are a fair amount of sets on NPCs which cannot be achieved by players, and I believe adding in these sets will at least be a minor boost to the RP scene on this server. Some examples of sets shall be posted below.
