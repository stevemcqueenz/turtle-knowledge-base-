# Ideas for item drops in the new dungeon

## Post 3485 by Qixel (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-07-21T06:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3485#p3485 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I dunno if you guys are looking for ideas for items to add to the new dungeon, if it would even be feasible to do some of them, or even if this would be the right place to post them, but I wanted to get this in writing before I forgot like so many other things. xD

I think a cool drop idea for the Caverns of Time dungeon (or anything else in the future, really) would be a cycle of class-specific trinkets that do something interesting, either passively or as an active (although I'm personally more fond of passive, aha).

I drew up a quick idea for each class below, but obviously some are more inspired than others. Numbers are just to give an idea, I don't know how to balance things.

 **Timeworn Signet of the Arcane**
Mage Only
Effect: Increases damage done by Arcane spells and effects by up to 50% of your total mana.

 **Timeworn Signet of Burning Light**
Priest Only
Effect: Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Holy spells by 100%.

 **Timeworn Signet of the Nether**
Warlock Only
Effect: While your pet is active, you and your pet will regenerate 3% of total health every 10 seconds.

 **Timeworn Signet of Subterfuge**
Rogue Only
Effect: Reduces the chance enemies have to detect you while in stealth mode, increases your movement speed while stealthed by 3%, and gives you a 10% chance to return to stealth mode after using your Sap ability.

 **Timeworn Signet of the Cycle**
Druid Only
Effect: The cooldown of Rebirth is reduced by 50%.

 **Timeworn Signet of the Companions**
Hunter Only
Effect: While your pet is active, you and your pet both deal 3% more damage.

 **Timeworn Signet of the Elements**
Shaman Only
Effect: The radius of your totems that affect friendly targets is increased by 50%.

 **Timeworn Signet of Justice**
Paladin Only
Effect: The duration of your seals is increased by 25%, and their mana cost is reduced by 50%.

 **Timeworn Signet of Command**
Warrior Only
Effect: The duration of your shouts is increased by 50%.
