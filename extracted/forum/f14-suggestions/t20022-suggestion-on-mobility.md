## Post 136957 by Cosmico (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-25T10:51:00Z

Greetings!

One thing you must think in order to make the game attractive for people to put their money into it is how much they find it beauty. Yes people gets engaged with stuff that looks nice!

Characters capable to "pass" through the others is a little bit outdated. The game might look outdated that's part of the magic but it doesn't have to be obsolete.

If you give just a little of work into how the characters "stumble" against the others when they walk, then character's movement would appear more realistic. This isn't about changing the capacity of trespass above the other's, just to note there is no animation on characters when they go along. In fact they could move aside or walk by side when they get close to another character to "seem it's avoiding or clearing the way for the other" even if you don't move at all.

I know the game already has the technology for this animations because they are used in several resources, like the eyes watching from the fel crystals in blood elves zones, portals of many types and so on.

This isn't a urgent thing to attend. Although it might be interesting for somebody.
