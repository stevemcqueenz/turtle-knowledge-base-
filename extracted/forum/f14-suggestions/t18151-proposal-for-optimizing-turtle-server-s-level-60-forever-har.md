— Risk Compensation Mechanism to Enhance Player Retention & Guild Activity

I. Core Problem Analysis
Team Attrition Crisis
Hardcore players face "High Risk - Zero Reward" dilemma after reaching Level 60:
No resource acquisition through standard dungeons
Strict team composition requirements causing recruitment bottlenecks
​Critical Issue: Team disintegration risk from member deaths (unable to replenish members promptly)
Data Validation:
Post-Level 60 content desolation: Only "Prison" raid remains available

​II. Innovative Solution: Compensation System
 1. Dynamic Risk Compensation System
Trigger Mechanism:
Players receive soul-state option upon raid death
Meeting criteria → Rebirth with performance-enhancing buffs
Buff Standards:
​Base: +1 All Primary Stats (Str/Int/Agility/Stamina) per 10 levels if retaining original class name/clan identity
​Stacking: +2 All Primary Stats per 10 levels on 2nd death. Max ​7 stacks (configurable by developers)

 2. Social Incentive/Anti-Cheat System
Guild Honor Wall:
Death records displayed as "letter-like credentials" in Guild Bank interface
​Rebirth Requirement: Collect ​20 validated guild member signatures

​III. Expected Benefits
​Retention Boost: Hardcore mode adoption rate expected to surge significantly
​Guild Growth: ​300% expansion in Level 60+ raid-focused guilds
​Social Reinforcement: Mechanism prevents intentional suicide farming while strengthening guild cohesion
​Content Revitalization: Sustainable endgame through risk-reward mechanics

​IV. Long-Term Planning
Economic System Integration:
Gold recirculation via paid signature collection system
Server revenue enhancement through donation-based guild funding model
In-game currency ecosystem linking risk-taking with tangible rewards

Ah. Chinese. That does explain things. Translation makes it sound like it's for a different game entirely and with confusing terms.

Personally I feel Hardcore could use its own Layer. No separate Realm, no further custom changes to make people feel more awkwardly separated-but-also-not from normal players, no other concepts - - just make a Layer, have it be available only to whoever is detected as a Hardcore/Inferno Character, and since already people cannot group up like HC + normal, there is already one potential method of abuse gone in advance automatically (Layer hopping, HC boosted by non-HC, etc.). Channels would still work (including non-Hardcore; you could do trade ads, softcore player sees, has interest, logs on Hardcore, bam he's now on the same separate Layer as you). Once you're sixty you are tossed back with the others, or you can stay and choose Inferno. This on paper feels like it would be more proper separating the players without making it awkward or with custom limitations to try and battle potential abuse, divide the same Mobs, Mining and Herb Nodes etc., instead make it like that.

Layering has had its notoriety for having negative effects both in gameplay and socially, but for once, this might be a rare exception in which such an implementation (just one separate layer) might be good.

The idea of a separate HC-only Realm also exists, but it could be made better and therefore redundant with a separate Layer in current Realms instead, as well as give players the option to go back to the main Layer after HC without wanting Inferno.

Despite all that has been said, I would definitely prefer such an idea to first face extensive testing and perhaps a PTR-style Realm for public testing purposes to detect, report and iron out possible bugs (leftover abuse, maybe random Spell hitting across Layers etc. whatever might exist), and then have a test implementation of the Layer on a pre-existing Realm (the PTR), to be sure that should it hit say Nordanaar or Tel'Abim, nothing should go wrong, because it would have already been tested and proven.

This would also then in retrospect work with additional challenges such as War Mode (you would only be attackable by other HC-ers, so the stakes are there for both ends), and should even work well in a PvP Realm such as Tel'Abim (because HC there is basically suicide, especially with the global System Message milestone announcements that practically tell griefers that you can be hunted by them as non-Hardcore).

I feel that further changes and attempted optimizations to HC would only make it feel too different and separate of a mode from normal play, rather than just as few and as faithful changes as possible where it's merely just a "you live only once" mode and that being that - - of course with your Mount, Tabard, Title etc. to signify you did something cool.

This of course is merely a quickdraw idea and on paper it might sound alright but me seeing and argumenting only the positives could be making me blind to some negatives that could be dealbreakers.
