I was curious if theres any plans to ever release an "Elixir of Greater Frostpower" just like Fire already has an "Elixir of Greater Firepower"? In oiginal WoW, an "Elixir of Greater Frostpower" never existed and Frostmages had to wait til the burning crusade for an "Elixir of Major Frostpower", which had +55 and was higher than the ongoing "Greater Firepower".

Right now, we are stuck with the procession
 - Elixir of Firepower +10 for 30 minutes
 - Elixir of Frostpower +15 for 30 minutes
 - Elixir of Greater Firepower +40 for 30 minutes
so the next one should be Elixir of Greater Frostpower +40? +45?

And the mats could easily be Icecaps and Mountain Silversage, as both are not used for a lot of consumables and would fit.

While Warlocks and Shadow Priests have
 - Elixir of Shadow Power, which already gives +40 for 30 minutes, without being the Greater version.

and with Emerald Sanctum, the Elixir of Greater Nature Power got released, which already has +55 Nature Power for 1 hour, which is on the same level as Burning Crusade Elixirs.

On the other hand, there is no such thing as an "Elixir of Holy Power" for smite priests and paladins and no "Elixir of Arcane Power" for moonkins and arcane mages, while the "Greater Arcane Elixir" becomes more and more useless, when using a "Flask of Supreme Power" as they do not stack. And no consumables for healers with +healing exist in the game. its either spelldamage only or mana regeneration only.
im pretty sure, that some of them simply dont exist, because there was not enough herbs back in original wow and all herb combinations were used already, but this is turtle-wow. why not give the moonwells more jobs aside from felcloth to mooncloth. why not have an option to cleanse plagueblooms to sageblooms, which would open a large variety of options to be used, without having to implement its spawn locations and it would make the sacred chalice from the donation shop more relevant.

this suggestion has already been made back in 2023 and it never got an answer. its 2025, so could we reconsider this?

Correct me if I'm wrong, but I think the [Elixir of Greater Nature Power] being a +55 has to do with the fact that Elemental Shamans and Boomkin, who are the only ones to cast nature spells, cast spells of other magic schools (fire, frost, arcane). For the rest, +40 is enough. And there exists a (rare) frost power pot that gives +15.

(1) I don't know how of Boomkin damage comes from arcane and how much from nature, but either way, I don't think a slight (consumable) buff will hurt the Boomkin.
(2) A frost buff will not buff Elemental Shamans.
(3) Frost mage aren't played in raids, this won't hurt.
(4) Arcane mages are already very strong, and don't need buffs, but does it make sense to have a pot for every magic school but arcane?
(4) Same for Holy, no good reason against. +40 would makes sense, just like shadow and fire.
(5) I am not convinced healers need more buff pots. They can focus on mana regen and TWoW offers [Dreamshard Elixir] on top of classic WoW. I think it's enough.

Also, by the way, the +20 holy gloves enchant has been in the database since forever <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=70002> and it's still nowhere to be found. I hope it will be in Kara40.
