# Low level (30+) spell power Necklace for Horde.

## Post 76290 by Nixors — 2023-11-07T12:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76290#p76290 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The first Necklace with spell power for Horde that I was able to find is Scorn's Icy Choker.
(<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=23169>). Scorn is a scourge invasion boss, meaning this item is no longer available to obtain.

Equivalent to this level 30 item is Magnificent Necklace, obtainable at level 18 from an Alliance only quest.
(<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81418>). So, Alliance get an Necklace at level 18 equivalent to a level 30 vanilla Necklace.

Furthermore, Alliance get another one at level 42.
(<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=51756>).
Meanwhile the first spell power Necklace Horde could get at level 30 is not actually obtainable. Making the very first obtainable spell power Necklace for Horde at level 45.
(<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60735>).

Not to mention this level 45 Horde necklace only has 2 spell power more and 2 intellect less than the Alliance necklace they get at level 18. . . . .

This absolutely stinks, and it'd be nice if this was equalized.
