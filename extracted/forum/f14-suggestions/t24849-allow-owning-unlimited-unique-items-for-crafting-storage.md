# Allow owning unlimited Unique items for crafting/storage

## Post 175177 by Alexianon — 2026-04-14T09:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175177#p175177 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I suggest allowing players to own Unique items in any quantity for crafting and storage purposes, keeping the restriction only on equipping them.

Currently, you cannot have more than one copy of such an item even in your inventory. And if an item is already soulbound, crafting a second one becomes technically impossible until it is sold or destroyed.
