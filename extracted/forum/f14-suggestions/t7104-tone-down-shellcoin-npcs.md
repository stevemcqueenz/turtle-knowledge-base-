# Tone down Shellcoin NPCs

## Post 45194 by Hcdeaths — 2023-05-09T19:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45194#p45194 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd like to put forward a suggestion to tone down the Shellcoin advertising from NPCs in the game.

The Shellcoin ads are very frequent and detract from the game experience. Shellcoin also creates confusion for players who associate it with the donation shop and perceive the server as a profit-oriented venture.

I think that reducing the ads and giving the players more choice over when and how to engage with the NPCs would make the game more enjoyable and appealing.

I hope that you will consider this suggestion and improve the game for everyone. Thank you for your attention and hard work.
