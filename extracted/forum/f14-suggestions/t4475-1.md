## Post 26781 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-10-05T17:37:00Z

I am sorry to say that I don't think you gave this suggestion enough thought before posting it. I would like to make an advisory to any suggestion maker in any game that exists online ever, in that once you give players something in a game, it will be a very controversial move to remove it depending on the impact of the feature, how widely it has been accepted and enjoyed, and for how long it has been accepted and enjoyed.

Removing an entire playable race from the game, especially one that the server was largely promoted on falls far beyond the line you do not cross as service providers. Yet I have seen that suggestion made frequently. I understand you, OP, are not making this suggestion, but stepping it down a bit, you must then consider the wisdom in removing a certain race's playable classes, and for what purpose? Religiously strict lore, which is itself somewhat subject to interpretation among this community? Not a good idea.

It's alright to be enthusiastic about contributing to the server, but be sure that you're considering all of the implications of the changes you have in mind and how the community will receive them, let alone whether Staff will consider them.
