# Northwatch Hold and Ambermill Village Suggestions

## Post 100527 by Taxaztion — 2024-09-02T18:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100527#p100527 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to suggest that both Northwatch Hold and Ambermill Village be turned into low level Alliance Quest Hubs for levels 10-20 as doing so will provide Alliance players even more options for Leveling as well as allow players the opportunity to complete Alliance Versions of all the Horde Quests available within the Crossroads , Camp Taurajo and The Sepulcher

Northwatch Hold and Ambermill Village could also contain Breadcrumb Quest chains for Ragefire Chasm and Shadowfang keep which currently isn't available for Alliance players while Horde players could have their own Breadcrumb Quests for Deadmines within The Crossroads as well as a Breadcrumb Quest chain for the stockades in Orgrimmar

Turtle wow already has plenty of different areas for players to level however you can never have too many Leveling zones and doing this would give Alliance players the opportunity to complete quests that are otherwise unavailable to them
