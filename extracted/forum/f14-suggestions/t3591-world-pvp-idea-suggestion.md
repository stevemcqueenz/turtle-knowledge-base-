I don't think it's a good idea either. City raids happening more often because there's something valueable for everyone to get would get old very quick. The reason I and I suspect many others like vanilla style WoW is because a lot of things are more intrinsically rewarded rather than everything having some kind of dopamine hit or collectible to gather. If people want to raid cities they should want to raid cities for the act of raiding cities.
