# Leveling in battle grounds

This might sound like whining but I do think it would be healthy for the PvP community.

I suggest that twinks that are above a certain ilvl should be grouped only with people the same ilvl and above. This way the community can actually level well during PvP during each bracket without getting utterly stomped.

One of the selling points for me for Turtle Wow was the fact that you can level in PvP.

What do ya'll think?

## Post 151709 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-14T17:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151709#p151709 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I played Ambershire in a time when there were no twinks. I mean without full blues and without high level enchants. There were only some guys with stopped xp and some gear from dungeons.
And in my opinion, leveling on the battleground was not really possible. **Brackets are too wide**. As a level 1 vs level 9 you have no chance. Mostly because of spells and character stats, but also because of questing gear. Same stuff about lvl 12 vs lvl 19. On higher levels I expect the same.

There is also technical bug that you don't get xp from flags if you dead. It probably the same for xp from winning, but I am not sure.

There are also troubles with getting competitive gear. You need Revered with WSG to buy weapons and probably revered with AB to buy full set of AB gear.
Not sure if this particular thing should be fixed though. Turtle have cool expanded world and improved professions on low levels. So you can level gear-crafting profession and equip yourself in something basic.

So I agree that twinks kinda ruin battleground for people, but removing them won't solve the problem. From any possible nerf, I think that nerfing high level enchants on low level gear would be most fair. [link suggestion thread](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4499)

I thought about this problem for like 4 years. Lol. So far best solution with minimal changes will be overlapping brackets. [link to suggestion thread](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=21897)
