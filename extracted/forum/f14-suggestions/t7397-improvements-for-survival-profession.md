# Improvements for Survival Profession

## Post 47080 by Kashchei — 2023-05-23T01:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47080#p47080 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While I don't think torches or bright campfires are an inherently bad idea, they definitely have the limelight stolen from the overwhelming power of the mighty tent.

I think a few more consumables are a good idea and I think I know to implement them.

Have a series of other deployable items created that not unlike the tent increase the benefit the longer you stay under them, how do we do that you might ask? well we make these items provide buffs that increase in duration for the longer you are within range or channelling on them.

The buffs could range from basic spirit / stam increases, all the way to a possible 5% move speed increase, or possibly even increasing the rate that you can craft items while in proximity to a certain item to promote players to have downtime tasks instead of just going afk in front of a tent.
