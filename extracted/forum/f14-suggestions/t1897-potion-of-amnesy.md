Hi Everyone!

Function:
This potion lets you to unlearn the abilities you start the game with. It could be acquired from Speedy and used only as a lvl 1 character. The unlearnt abilities can be re learned in the trainers for free.

Example:
For example mage starts with Fireball and Frost Armor. If you use this item you will lose these abilities and you can re learn them. So let’s say you want to play a mage without even having a fireball you won’t learn that skill back and have only frost in your spellbook.

This would be a small self restiction roleplay stuff. I would like to hear your toughts on it!

So the cure is not that potion, but starting to create some allowance for things being they way they may be (such as in the spellbook) and practice not getting activated by it.
