Whenever Blizzard released a new expansion pack, they added a new mage portal to the final zone there. Now Turtle didn't exactly increase the level cap, but with the upcoming t3.5 gear and the class changes, as well as the harder than vanilla zones, like Hyjal and Tower of Karazhan, we are somewhere between Vanilla and TBC.
