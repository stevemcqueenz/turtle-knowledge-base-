# Some Hunter QOL Changes.

## Post 173692 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-04-02T00:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173692#p173692 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I think these could be good additions without being too OP.

Aspect Switching while Mounted.

Paladins can change their auras while mounted, and we hunters can change what we track while mounted, with the animation, I think it would be nice to be able to switch aspects while mounted before you jump into the fight, not big or OP , but just a nice fluid change imo.

Dimiss Pet Cast

It takes 5 seconds to cast this, in which time your raid could have ran ahead without you, just a simple change to reduce the cast time to like 2-3 sec while out of combat would feel less clunky when you have to dismiss your pet due to pathing issues.

Improved Feign Death Talent

Currently this only reduces chance its resisted, and doesnt do anything in PvP, I'd add a 5/10 sec cd reduction on it, as its Survival hunters only way of 'interupting' a cast. plus you're survival, it fits the theme.

Show Quiver on back.

I noticed your quiver only shows on your back when you have your bow out, (also other players cannot see this)
When you get your melee weapon out it vanishes.
It looks kinda cool and makes you 'look' like a hunter.
Adding a toggleable in the same place as helm/cloak would be a nice addition while making it shown all the time while unchecked.

Anyway I wont get too much into actual balance changes, just thought these were some decent QOL features.
