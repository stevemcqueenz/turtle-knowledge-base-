# Druid forms

## Post 143026 by berdal (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-04T07:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143026#p143026 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is basically two slight tweaks I think would provide a more fluent and natural (and consistent) way of playing the Druid class.

(1) That the 30% movement speed boost (in feral cat form) only works outside seems... weird from a logical sense. I think it doesn't make sense that the cat suddenly isn't able to run as fast if there's a roof over its head.
(2) The Swift Travel Form is currently the only form that has a cast time. Can this be removed (in line with the other forms)? It could have other restrictions like not usable in combat.

Of course if it would be completely broken and imbalanced it's definitely a no-go (unless the brokenness can be fixed and compensated elsewere)

## Post 143086 by Profgiggles — 2025-08-04T13:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143086#p143086 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm guessing that the 30% speed boost not working inside is to make the class a bit more on the same level as the others namely in caves and dungeons. A permanent 30% speed buff that applies in those instances would be very powerful. Unfairly so to the other classes I'd day. A big point of those instances I think is that if you screw up, getting out and away does require making the correct actions and having some knowledge about the environment. The mobs are faster than you, and that is a really big component. If a class can run with a buff indefinetely and be faster or as fast as the mobs, you remove the major threat that the mobs pose to you.

I've never had the swift travel form, but from my understanding its the equivalent of the fastest moving mount speed. I think having a cast time for it is fine.
