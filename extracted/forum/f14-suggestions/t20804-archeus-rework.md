# Archeus Rework

## Post 144421 by Bl4ckk9 — 2025-08-11T07:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144421#p144421 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, Archeus, Morgan Ladimore's Sword.

The buildup to Mor'ladim is well established, finding a disturbed grave off to the side of Raven Hill gets you to question who Morgan Ladimore is. Then being told the legend of Mor'ladim and being given a book that tells the tale of Morgan Ladimore's experience and skill, and eventual descent into despair and madness culminating in a fun boss battle with an emotional note at the end of the story where you put his spirit to rest is excellent.

But the reward feels... well.  Off.

An uncommon two-handed sword with sort-of-decent damage that procs for *Arcane* damage of all things?  Doesn't feel like the weapon of a Paladin, which the quest kind of feels like a Paladin's quest.

While it's not my place to say what the numbers should be if there is a rework, here's my two cents:
- Make it a rare item.
- Give it a decent strength bonus, or strength and spirit.
- Have it trigger on-hit Holy damage, instead of Arcane.
- Make the damage on par with other rare two-handed swords.
- Make the weapon glow, like the weapon Mor'ladim is wielding during the fight.
Now you have a really neat, very fun, thematically fitting weapon for a Paladin putting a wandering, tortured spirit to rest and being rewarded for their kindness despite the terrible acts Morgan Ladimore did in life.
