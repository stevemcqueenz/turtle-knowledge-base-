# One-Time Dungeon TP for Opposite Faction

## Post 9600 by Vernalsole1355 — 2021-03-01T00:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9600#p9600 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have a suggestion. Very simple. I think Turtle should provide a way to allow for opposing faction's players to TP to certain dungeons in enemy territory, such as Alliance being able to TP to RFC. You could buy this privilege using in-game currency or IRL money. You would get an item in the mail allowing you a 1-time teleport to the entrance to the dungeon, and providing you a five minute immunity to guards attacking you so you have a few minutes to get setup with your group once you show up.

Thoughts?
