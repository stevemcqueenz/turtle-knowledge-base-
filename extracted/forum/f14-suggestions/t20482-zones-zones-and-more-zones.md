# Zones, zones and more zones!

## Post 141528 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-26T08:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141528#p141528 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Adding more zones is imo the way to go.
People like asking for more class combinations, which i like aswell, but adding more zones is more important.
I like that they are adding more and more with every major update, however, here is a list of zones which I would really like to visit in a classic+ setting.

 **1. Kul Tiras**
This one is quite obvious because everyone know Kul Tiras even from Warcraft 3 and onward.
Kul Tiras is also not just one island. There are several islands close to it that can become questing areas aswell.
These are ***Crestfall***, ***Zul'Dare*** and ***Tol Barad***.

Kul Tiras and the surrounding islands should NOT be an alliance questing zone only. In the lore the Horde rescued some forest trolls from Zul'Dare(warcraft 2) which is why it has a trollish name. In Kul Tiras, the horde should have their main quest hub on Zul'Dare. Crestfall could be interesting aswell. In current WoW its an island expedition and you find pirates and orc spirits there(Kinda like Balor Island)

Tol Barad could potentially be a dungeon.

 **2. Southern Silithus**
Yesterday I watched a [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B9OK-6BZGBE) from Jediwarlock. He talked about the secrets of Silithus and then showed the unused Southern Silithus region. That region is not even inside the raids. It has ruins, hives, all kinds of things but was completely unused by Blizzard. This could be a nice extension for Silithus which doesn't have a lot of quests anyway. Needs some questlines, camps and some terrain fixing and ofc a gate that is open to reach it. Could be a lvl 58-60 zone like Tel'Abim. Theme is quite obvious I suppose.

 **3. Isles of the South Seas**
 ***Plunder Isle*** and ***Hiji***. Quite a bit is known about Plunder Isle. The bastion of the bloodsail buccaneers. Giant bats, giant turtles, giant squids. Probably Naga and Murlocs. Hiji is very rarely mentioned, only visible in a few concept maps but placed right next to Plunder Isle. Could be a nice place to get more rep for bloodsail buccaneers or kill them. Hiji being a minor quest area of the Plunder Isle which is a lot bigger.

 ***Darkspear Islands*** could greatly expand on darkspear troll lore and give the Horde something similar to the Gnomish reclamation facility but for the Trolls. Imo the trolls really deserve an area entirely focused on them. Could be a Horde only questing area like Northwind will be.

 ***Kezan*** and ***Undermine*** could be interesting for Goblins but I haven't thought much about it. I'd honestly rather see the other islands I mentioned first but its always an option.

Feel free to add more suggestions for entirely new zones below. I'd love to hear what other areas I might've forgotten that could fit into Turtle WoW.

Also its really important that some already existing zones get more quests, both Horde and Alliance. Just wanted to mention that because I can already tell someone will comment "Why add new zones when a lot of existing zones still need some love?"
