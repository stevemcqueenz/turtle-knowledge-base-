# War Parties

## Post 5915 by Heroclastus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-05-10T19:48:00Z

Hello,

in the recent Thread

PVP and XP Boost Suggestion
[viewtopic.php?f=14&t=1041&sid=9c8117e38 ... 70877ae252](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=1041)

we were asked if having PVP mode on should increase XP gained. I would suggest something similar but different
which IMO would fit the server model better and doesn't offend players that dont want to participate in PVP. The suggestions main flaw would be everyone turning PVP on with low-risk (because of low-pop)and reaping the rewards which goes against the 'take it slow' philosophy of the server .

My Suggestion:

War Parties (Mode) or other name :)  [mad_turtle_head

Main idea here is that this could be a game mode like hardcore mode which makes you PVP flagged permanently but gives you something in compensation. Things that come to mind are Rep Bonus or Honor Bonus and/or (PVP) Items for landmark level (20,30 etc.).

Two options of how it could be implemented :

 1. Make pvp mode item-bound (keyring item) just like hardcore mode is now up till you reach level 60 where you then can swap freely.

 2. Make it item-bound but make it interchangeable with diplomat item at any time (with CD perhaps) , this would prevent item rewards for pvp mode being a thing though (which is no big deal tbh).

Parameter changes (only suggestive, numbers or parts of the suggestion may be left out ofc if overall better) :

- War Party Mode and Diplomat are mutually exclusive

- Party Leaders in War Party Mode can only invite players into their group +3 or -3 levels (or perhaps up to 5 levels) of their own level and of their respective faction to prevent high levels carrying

- Group size is maxed at 5 people (till level 60 where it works as default ), perhaps make BGs an exception here (or not to encourage small scall skirmishes till level 60)

- if possible make it hard for several war parties to just gather to avoid zerging (e. g. Debuff when too many players with war party mode on are in the same area,  e. g. attract opposite faction Guards with high damage (think kazzak cleave).

- Enchants possibly would have to be only workable on items of a certain level (like in TBC) to prevent over-use of twinking since enchants make up a great part of over-powered low-levels-->e.g. novice and journeyman enchants no item level requirement, expert item level 45, artisan item level 55. This would have to be changed in general though not just pvp mode related then

- Players not in War Party Mode who toggle PVP on (e.g. to kill a low-level War Party player) have to wait a lot longer (in-game time) for the PVP-flag to wear off (several hours) so they really have to think about participating in PVP (e.g. helping ppl of their faction)  since they will be vulnerable themselves for a long time

- At level 60 one could freely get an item from the diplomat (warmongers medal/declaration of war or similar etc.) to toggle on the rewards of the mode (rep and/or honor bonus) BUT with a CD (1 day or so) for changing between diplomat item and war declaration item

- Possibly nullify honor rewards for kills in open world after the 3rd kill to prevent unfair behaviour (corpse camping etc.) or nullify them in general in the open world and make pvp quests reward some honor

- also possible: make the rep bonus only affect the bg and city factions (e. G. Killing player in ashenvale/barrens - Silverwing sentinels/war song outriders rep gain, in darkshore - darnassus rep gain etc.

- reduce repair costs for war party players since pvp can often be exploited to letting pve mobs kill the other player (fearing into mobs e. g.)

- perhaps also increase the number of brackets for bgs (a level 21 player won't have a lot of fun in a 20-29 bg, e. G. 10-14,15-19,20-24,25-29 etc.), doing BGS imo shouldn't reward XP to make questing mandatory to level

- to enable level 60 to gain the rewards one could allow them to get the milestone level rewards by killing a certain amount of opposite faction players with war party mode on in the open world or in a certain contested area (perhaps as a time-limited event when introducing the feature)

What this could mean

- Players who want to PVP and enjoy PVE are encouraged to form war band/parties and quest together (strength in numbers)

- Players with War Party on can earn rep/honor rewards quicker but with the added risk of being a victim of PVP, for people who just want to do PVE in its current (cross-faction form) nothing changes. Also - as a sort of disadvantage - War Party Players cant be carried through dungeons because of the level restrictions in the party

- Better immersion/more identification with your faction and more cooperation

- Hopefully attract players that like both aspects (PVE and PVP) of the game (like me) and avoid toxic players (xp rate is retained, unsocial PVP behaviour minimised through above parameters)

- rep farming groups are encouraged to turn on war party mode to accelerate rep farm process, fights for rep farming areas(silithus wind  stones, timbermaw hold rep farm camps etc.) possible

- give players an incentive to play in an sub-optimal spec (e. G. Rogue in sub spec) during leveling to be prepared for pvp participation

Those are some ideas from the top of my head. As always, I love to hear from you !

Addendum:

Unfortunately not a lot of people bother to write here. After some time in world chat, opinions about this vary greatly from enthusiastic support to downright rejection.
Problematic points seem to be:

 1. why reward pvp players for pvp when doing PVP alone is incentive enough ?
 2. fear of toxic chat in world channel
 3. why not just put /pvp on and have some pvp time ?
 4. why advocate for PVP when this is clearly a RP/PVE server ?

 1. Everything in WoW is built around work and reward. Without a reward, there's nothing to drive you further: you level to get better skills and items, you raid to get better items and get the satisfaction of progression etc. As I pointed out in the OP the rewards could be only small and PVP-centric in nature too and of no further interest to PVE players like increasing standing with Defilers when killing players in Arathi Highland (perhaps also only up to a certain reputation) so playing arathi basin for the items isn't obsolete. Other suggestions were
- Increase gold gain from slaying mobs: IMO not the best option as this is interesting for PVE players too since gearing for raids etc. can be very costly

- Get gold from slaying players: IMo also not a good option for the same reason as above

- Increase XP gain from killing Mobs: this is also problematic as others have already pointed out in other threads, offering XP weeks/gains does't create long-term invested players. Not increasing XP gains could hinder toxic (pvp) behaviour as players who have invested time into their character will generelly want a better standing in the community more than a player who has boosted to level 60 in a few days.

- Add War Party quests: perhaps the best but also probably the most difficult to implement. Having unique PVP quests could offer nice experiences which should be reward enough in themselves

 2. One way to solve this is to make Players in War Party Mode unable to enter the all-faction world chat - perhaps by making entering world chat require you have the diplomats item equipped. They would have to resort to the faction-specific world chat. This would at least hinder toxic remarks in general world and make the faction world chats useful as they aren't relly used ATM

 3. PVP also lives from the unexpected. If you can turn on PVP one minute, kill someone and have it off after 5 minutes (waiting somewhere safe) there's no danger and no need to form the described war parties. Things like players organizing or banding together after being attacked someplace where they want to quest won't happen. Also turning pvp on puts the player who starts off without it on at a great advantage when he sees someone flagged as he can decide if to engage and when to engage.Also putting PVP on and have some PVP on this server in the current state more closely resembles duels and not real open-world encounters. A lot of people answered that they actually enjoy real open-world encounters  and this could be a great way to attract more people to Turtle WoW. Remember that this is entirely optional in my suggestion, people who don't want to PVP don't have to in any form.

 4. One of the main problems of the server is population, the RP-PVE flag is quite probably one of the reasons for that. It saddens me greatly as this server is obviously done with great commitment and striving for quality. I'm only trying to find ways to make the server more attractive for more people. Wouldn't it be great if the sever could cater to PVE as well as PVP community where both PVE-players can go on their business just like before and PVP-players can have the excitement of open-world PVP without one interfering with the other ?

Thanks again for the feedback.
Perhaps I should have named the entire thing differently, war party or war bands just stuck. Of course, you can stick to single player, I never stated it to be mandatory to form groups - I just wanted it to be fair IF you decide to group thus all the points in the OP.
Perhaps we can agree on the simplest and most basic solution.

A new player who starts a new character is in a neutral state, hasn't picked neither diplomacy nor pvp mode. When visiting the diplomat he/she can chose between war and diplomacy item thus changing the mode.
Neutral mode players can only join faction specific world chat and can flag for PVP just like you can ATM

Choosing diplomacy:
- you can understand opposite faction and join cross-faction parties

- you can enter cross-faction world chat

- you can't flag for Pvp AT ALL, shouldn't be able to attack pvp npc targets

Choosing war:
- permanently pvp flagged and can attack pvp targets

- can't join cross-faction world chat, restricted to faction specific

- can't understand other faction and can't form parties with them

Now the reason I chose to make neutral and diplomat players unable to flag for pvp is simple. I played on a tbc server which was a pvp server but with cross-faction, which leads to some strange behavior. e. g.   horde 1 attacking a alliance player who is in a party with horde 2. Horde 2 would be able to help /buff/heal the alliance player and horde 1 can't do anything about it.

Free swapping between the modes at the diplomat, perhaps add a small fee or CD.
