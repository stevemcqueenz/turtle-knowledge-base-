# More availability of old(er) models and motions

## Post 100055 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-07-21T15:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100055#p100055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is an old post for suggestions for halted TWoW 2.0. I edited and added a few points.
> Posted on 21st July, 2024.
>  To make what I mean concise, I only use imperatives and phrases below.
> - 1, **Reserve player character models with original motions** and **include player character motions in post-Cataclysm expansions as optional alternatives**. (e.g. Unlike the ugly standing gesture of male nIght elf as the only choice)
>
> - 2, **Reserve old facial designs' aesthetic styles** rather than thoroughly replacing them with Holywood-cartoon-styled faces in Blizzard's latest expansions.
>
> - 3, Make even older models, such as those red-masked men we can find in Deadmine and crouching female trolls, optional for players.
>
> - 4, Make some other miscellaneous revisions, including modifying wildkins walking like an ogre and doing ogre dance, and making gnomes' mounts of regular sizes, or making gnomes smaller.
>
> - 5, Enable vertical jumps in place for player characters while mounted.

Some older models, for 1.12-based game, can be reused. We already have some early beta (pre-vanilla/classic) models for human and dwarf characters, namely those red-masked defias rogues, Ironforge reputation vendor, etc. On this basis, older troll models, especially the crouching female troll model, can be reused as regular player models. Female rolls standing straight up are way too elegant and different from their male counterpart, which make me think they are two difference species/races. I do not mean replacing the classic straight up troll female model but adding the older ones for diversity. Other races' old models would also be conducive to both immersion and attracting more players.
