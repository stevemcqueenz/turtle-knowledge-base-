# Ship a working version of LFT with client

## Post 61307 by Niralthas — 2023-09-07T17:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=61307#p61307 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings Community and Turtle WoW Team,

This suggestion is the result of several questions and understandable misunderstanding from new players who have downloaded the latest client version from one of the available mirrors.

The LFT addon shipped with the available client archive is not only out of date with <https://github.com/CosminPOP/LFT/> but in fact is no longer fully functioning as expected.

The confusion some of these new players experience is very understandable, especially when they have little to no experience with the 1.12.1 client. To me it seems like a small effort to make sure the master archive includes at the very least a functional version of LFT. Small change with big gains. It also adds a certain level of professionality to the delivered product which I feel we have come to expect from Turtle WoW.

Would like to propose to make this a suggestion towards the Turtle WoW team.

A recent example of someone who was referred from #support to #addons and possibly clarifies this use-case better can be found from Meryl here:  [https://discord.com/channels/4666224558 ... 0182819841](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/466627627554439178/1149394410182819841)

With kind regards,
