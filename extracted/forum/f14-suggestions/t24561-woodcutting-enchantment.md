It feels odd to have superhuman strength and a raid-tier axe that can cleave a man/demon/dragon in half holstered while using a basic woodchopper to whack a tree.

I suggest adding the following:
 -a survival recipe analog to the engineering goblin rocket fuel recipe that teaches a woodcutting enchantingrecipe
 -a woodcutting enchanting recipe that reads "this axe can now be used to chop trees; its speed (and/or yield) scales with the damage of the item (and or your strength)"
