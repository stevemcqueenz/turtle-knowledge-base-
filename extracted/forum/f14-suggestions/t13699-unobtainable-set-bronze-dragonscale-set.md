I think it will be a godsend for people who want to try tanking with their shamans to have an easily-accessible craftable starter set with good stats and set bonuses.
Current mail tanking pieces are very scarce, and the epic ones are almost unobtainable, because they come from World Bosses.
We already have all other colors of dragonscale sets for different specs, give us the tanking one! The farming of bronze dragonscales would require pissing off the bronze dragonflight, but there's nothing people won't do for profit if there's demand.

That being said, I think there should be a 4th or even 5th piece - Shoulders and/or Boots, with more interesting set bonuses. The current ones are rather lackluster and boring, give it some interesting procs like the other dragonscale sets have.
Here's a suggestion, given that the bronze dragonflight are guardians of time:
"(4) Chance on being hit: Undo all damage and debuffs done to you in the past three seconds.", with some flashy bronze-y glowy thingy when it procs.   happy_turtle_head

Also some spellpower on the pieces would be appreciated instead of the haste, since we rely mostly on earth shock for aggro.

I think the reason it wasn't added might be that there isn't a base "bronze dragonscale" crafting material item in the game, and if there was one, there are only three attackable bronze dragons, that being Occulus, Tick and Chronalis, making it extremely difficult to reliably farm.

A workaround could be fashioned by making infinite dragonflight members in the Caverns of Time dungeon drop "Infinite Dragonscales" when skinned, which can then be purified at the Hourglass of Time in the Caverns into proper bronze dragonscales, which then can be used to craft armor. Additionally, bronze dragonscales could also be made to drop from the various Chromatic creatures in Upper Blackrock Spire and Chromaggus in Blackwing Lair.

It still won't be something easily farmed compared to the blue dragonscales and the green dragonscales, but given that they'd be a semi-reliable dungeon drop/item conversion I figure that it might be easier than the red dragonscales, which drop almost exclusively from the red dragonflight members guarding Grim Batol (which are levels 60+ elites) and in Upper Blackrock Spire's Chromatic creatures and Chromaggus.

"Farmable evil dragon flight" (more "hostile/antagonistic" than "evil", as outside of the corrupt green dragons and the black dragons there aren't really that many evil dragons, being that they are driven by their Titan-given purpose)  wouldn't really work, because the *only* place where the Bronze dragonflight actually operates is the Caverns of Time, because that is their domain. There are less than a handful of bronze drakes in Silithus, but they're only there to keep an eye on Ahn'Qiraj from the skies, and Chromie is a lone operative in Andorhal, but there is nothing more.

The Greens go wherever the Emerald Dream or Nightmare is. That's the four portals where the Dragons of Nightmare hang around, the Sunken Temple party that was also corrupted, and a small clutch of whelps with Itharius.

The Reds are watching over Grim Batol, guarding it and trying to keep anyone from opening it up and unleashing whatever evil lies dormant in it.

The Blues seem to primarily hang around places connected to magic, magical artifacts, and the leylines. That's their home in Winterspring, Lake Mennar in Azshara, and the ridge in Badlands that was added in Turtle WoW.

The only working equivalent for the Bronze are the Infinite dragonflight, but they've also been corrupted and thus, like the Chromatics, likely wouldn't give actual bronze dragonscales when skinned. Thus, introduce Infinite dragonscales as a product of skinning them in Black Morass and have them be able to be converted back into Bronze dragonscales when exposed to a more "pure" time magic at the Hourglass of Time. Further adding Bronze dragonscales to the skinning loot tables of the Chromatics will also make it lore-accurate (as all flights were experimented on by Nefarian to create the Chromatics; you can see Corrupted Bronze Whelps in the suppression room in Blackwing Lair) and help introduce them to the economy in other ways as well.
