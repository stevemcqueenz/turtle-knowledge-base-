# New PvP news feedback

## Post 135800 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-06-15T13:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135800#p135800 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This post relates to recent announcement of PvP changes written by Jamey [viewtopic.php?t=19804](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=19804).

In my opinion there were announced some good and bad (or weird) news for PvP.

Let's start with good news.

---

> Patch 1.18.0 will also:
> - Add a **New Arena Map**.
>
> - Add the return of **2v2 Arenas**.
>
> - Add **Rated Arena Teams**.

That's fine change for any Arena enjoyer. People seemed to love old 2 vs 2 arenas.

---

> - Rework **Rewards & Gear Progression** so that PvP will be a truly viable gearing path.

We don't know much details but idea is good. Current PvP gearing progression is outdated and matches the timeline of BWL of original vanilla.

---

> Partially rework the current **Honor System** to reduce the brutal race against the clock at high PvP ranks.

This is in my opinion the best change of everything in the announcement. Again, we don't know the details but I suppose you want to remove infamous need of playing 10 hours a day for weeks to level up rank.
I'm happy to see that it's "partial rework" because (maybe it's unpopular opinion) vanilla honor system was quite good if you turn a blind eye on a unhealthy time needed to spent to progress rank (without way to stop!). Fortunately, it's likely going to be fixed. Good job!

---

Now let's move on to bad or irrational changes...

---

> Beginning with Patch 1.18.0, every player who enters a Battleground or Arena will automatically receive a universal buff/debuff that:
>
> - Reduces incoming **Physical Damage** by x%.
>
> - Reduces incoming **Magical Damage** by x%.
>
> - Reduces incoming **Healing** by x%.
>
> - Reduces incoming **Shields**  by x%.

This isn't bad change in general - it aims to reduce impact of burst since CC2. However, I see it as a lazy fix...
You guys should acknowledge that the problem of burst doesn't come MAINLY from (as many people think) Naxx, Kara40 and Grim Batol gear.
The problem skyrocketed MAINLY after CC2. Naxx/Kara40 gear and some unbalanced items like Scythe of Elune are also a problem but not as impactful as reckless changes to gameplay that especially affected PvP.

The problem is that you allowed some cancer talents to exist that ruin PvP. I main only one class so I can give only one example - I won't try to pretend that I know every class.
I suggested nerfing Searing Light talent for Priest because it ruins PvP with insane burst. You can read more here: [viewtopic.php?t=18918](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=18918).
It's only one example, I know people also complain about Shaman's burst and Paladin's instant heals. These are real PvP killers that haven't been fixed yet.

Instead of applying lazy fix to OVERALL damage in battlegrounds that also has some weird and irrational side effects, you guys should fix class balance.

---
As an aside...

New scaling damage reduction is hidden buff to units in Alterac Valley.
Do I understand it correctly that NPCs in AV aren't hit by this debuff? That means Champion units that have a lot of HP and are already pain to break through in bases in AV will become even harder to kill. Even now in 20 vs 20 games it's nearly impossible to capture enemy base with Champions units in game unless you have stealther in team who might eventually ninja the banner.

That's also buff to other stronger NPCs like Commanders, Lieutenants, Marshals, Galvangar, Balinda, Vanndar, Drek'Thar, Korrak, Lokholar the Ice Lord, Ivus the Forest Lord but that's ok - in my opinion they have been already very weak.

---

> Introduce a stacking debuff to anyone holding the flag for an extended period of time in **Warsong Gulch**.

I know what you wanted to fix by applying this change. Warsong Gulch can be prelonged if both teams control enemy flags and don't want to die. The effect is that WSG lasts very long leading to low honor per hour...
However, maybe these games aren't honor efficient but I know people who love them! Stagnation in WSG requires risky strategies.
By giving debuff to flag carrier, you remove this part of gameplay because people would likely be able to kill flag carrier with short combo. It alters WSG gameplay a bit.

Maybe it's needed change but I see not everyone could be satisfied. After all this game is not about virtual honor numbers... but fun.

---

> Players who are standing in spots that we deem **safespots** (as described by our rules) will now receive a Damage over Time debuff that will last as long as they are in such a position. These positions will be continuously updated by the team.

This is irrational fix. Why apply this debuff instead of adding invisible walls to these places so they can't be reached at all? I don't understand.

---

> Several new **Open-World PvP Events**

The idea isn't a problem here. The problem is that there are a few World PvP objectives currently in the game that are dead. Western Plaguelands towers... Silithus...

Instead of adding new WPvP content that will kill above content even more, you should fix the WP towers and Silithus WPvP...
Turtle WoW focuses on horizontal progression, not vertical one. You guys forget this isn't TBC.

---

> A **Brand-New Battleground**.

Same thoughts as in new WPvP events.
Fix current custom battleground first before you try realising another crap.
You guys promised in one interview and on Reddit Q&A that Sunnyglade Valley will likely received a revamp. It's not even in the announcement... but new battleground is on the horizon!

I've written solid suggestions explaining everything that went wrong with Sunnyglade here: [viewtopic.php?t=15147](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=15147).
This battleground is so dead that we must organise events for it to pop. This is just sad.

In other words, announcing new battleground before the first is even fixed isn't serious. Because how can we be sure that this new BG won't turn out to be just as unfinished as the previous one?

---

That's my thoughts on the new announcement. Have a great day!
