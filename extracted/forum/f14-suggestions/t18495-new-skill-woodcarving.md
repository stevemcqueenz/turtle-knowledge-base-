# New Skill, Woodcarving

## Post 126196 by Eckland — 2025-04-09T18:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126196#p126196 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ever wondered why we’ve never been able to harvest one of the most iconic resources from Warcraft III—Wood?

It’s time to fix that.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/J7y9WfXf/9900074a-ebd6-473b-a8c6-d449ae1e2400.png)

Introducing: Woodcarving, a brand-new profession idea that finally brings Wood into the world of Azeroth as a gatherable and craftable resource. It’s long overdue, and fits perfectly into the classic fantasy feel of the game.

 **What Woodcarving would offer:**

Harvest wood from trees (maybe special marked ones, to keep it immersive)

Craft shields, bows, crossbows, throwing spears, wooden trinkets, staves, Baskets, clubs and even decorative RP items

Possibly combine with other professions (like enchanting or leatherworking) to make advanced gear or relics

Expand RP and racial fantasy—imagine Trolls making ceremonial spears, Night Elves crafting ancient bows, or Dwarves shaping heavy oak shields!

Why hasn't this been done yet? Who knows—but Turtle WoW is the perfect server to pioneer this addition.

Would love to hear what others think. Let's bring wood back to Warcraft!

Stay crafty
Eckland

Cool idea. It had been suggested for some time now  but even Blizz was very reluctant to add new professions to the game. Post TBC it’s only inscription and archeology?! Jewelcrafting was added in TBC I tink.

The thing is, it’s already hard to keep existing crafting professions somewhat relevant.
The profession system itself is flawed. There’s no gear degradation system. With the rules of supply & demand applied, only professions that produce consumables (non-permanent items) can be profitable in the long run (Alch,Eng,Cooking pretty much). All other professions (producing permanent gear) are heavily reliant on the influx of new characters.

Additionally 90% (or more) of craftable items are already without purpose, both levelling and endgame phase. The tmog system adds some cosmetic utility back, agreed, but that doesn’t fix the underlying issue.

Now one could add a new an interesting profession like mentioned above adding more flavour to the game. Yes, but resources (Devs) are scarce and going back to the last paragraph. How relevant is it in the current state of the game to add another crafting table bloating up further the stack of unusable items.

If at all, wouldn’t it be more efficient to add your suggestion to existing crafts like BS, Ench or LW (as specialisations f.e.)? Then or before rework the existing systems (I have no idea how that would or should be done atm, it’s a vanilla+ server after all) and crafts?
