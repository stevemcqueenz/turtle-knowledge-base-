# Sub-60 World Bosses for richer world building

## Post 146739 by 231232sadasd — 2025-08-18T11:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146739#p146739 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Imagine a hexxed oracle in one of the troll villages in STV as a say lv 35 world boss. The cool thing with that is that "world bosses" sub 60 can be designed in a fitting way -- i.e. non-powerful superbeings. That way you could enhance the feeling of just being another adventurer.

It would enable a deeper integration into questlines e.g. as a culmination, as well as a new activity to pursue while levelling.

A valid counterpoint IMO could be that it is a novel idea and not found in vanilla and thus could feel maybe retaily? What do you guys think?
