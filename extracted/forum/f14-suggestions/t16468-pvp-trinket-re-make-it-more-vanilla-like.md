## Post 112589 by Adqw — 2024-12-13T15:14:00Z

The identity of vanilla pvp is about sacrifices & anticipation.

Some classes overpower others but you always can do a trick with a consumes, with a skull, with eng.
Or simply play around your team. The goal is to capture the flag or a base, not 1v1 middle.

Here some examples (but there are plenty plenty others) :

As a hpal for example, you can equip skull, but you sacrifice shield.
As a rogue, you sacrifice second poison off hand.
As a hunter, you sacrifice 2H weapon... and so on.

As a rogue/hunt/..., you can anticipate an incoming helm/blind with a skull, anticipate an intercept/rogue with a fap.
As a pala you can anticipate incoming sheep with a skull/sacri, incoming fear with shadow reflector/bs trinket.
As a druid you can anticipate blind with your abolish poison... and so on.

You have to anticipate what eng trinket you equip for what situations you'll face etc, and play your class perfectly.

Now, at the current state, you almost never switch trinket since it's 3min CD. The next trinket you would equip, will be equiped for only the next 2min30. You can make mistakes, and still trinket em to make it even.

I see that we're here for vanilla+, so I was expecting changes when I joined this serv.
But I'm just asking to not go too far.

Pvp trinkets were fine, they didn't need rework in my opinion. I guess you wanted to make the pvp more casual friendly, then just add remove stuns on all trinket and it will be okay.
