# Suggestions for tasks and rewards in Hardcore Mode

## Post 92233 by Linkewow — 2024-04-06T18:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92233#p92233 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The repetitive purgatory tasks that come with each death and the creation of a new character can be frustrating, discouraging many players who would otherwise be interested in this mode. I hope to streamline the purgatory task process, rather than requiring players to complete seven dungeons over two days of intense gameplay.
"We hope to have unique titles and mounts, just like regular level 60 ascended players. Compared to regular ascended players, the rewards for us purgatory players are too few. This is not fair treatment. We hope to have our own rewards."

tl;dr: get rid of inferno quest

Or - and hear me out - we could get rid of the inferno quest altogether.

Back when it didn't exist, people either dinged to 60 to get their rewards or stayed 59 to keep playing hc. At some point the devs announced that they'll add an inferno mode, so players could remain hc and still level to 60. There's a huge benefit to be 60 instead of 59, max rank spells, epic riding, max level items, no handicapped hit%, glancing blows, crushing blows math because you got 300/300 weapon skills/defense.

Tons of people re-leveled toons they had leveled to 60 before the announcement and this time stayed 59 in anticipation. When hc at 60 finally arrived, it was rather disappointing to see that the game mode was locked behind the completion of a multi dungeon quest chain. I'd argue that hc attracts solo players to a much larger extent than it attracts regular people, so I found it odd that these solo players now had to bend together and run multiple dungeons at 59 just to be allowed to keep playing hc. Or alternatively remain handicapped at 59.

When all it takes to start hc is talking to the mysterious stranger in noob valley, why does it need an entire quest chain to remain hc at 60? Just let us talk to the dude again to opt in for 60 hc. Done and done. No trinket reward necessary .

Check the hc scoreboard on the website. Now it only shows the top 50 of every category and so I can only speculate how many level 59.99 toons are around, just parked because those players don't want to ding to 60 and be non-hc after, yet also don't want to or can't do the inferno quest for whatever reason. But they exist and my guess is it's about 500-1000 toons. Yeah maybe they moved on to other servers/games. Maybe they leveled a different class, waiting for the introduction of a proper non-gatekeeping to way to play hc at 60.

There's zero benefit to the community to keep people at 59 when they want to play hc at 60.

Fire away: but there's nothing to do besides raiding at 60 and if you want to raid, you need to bend together and prove that you can do it, so a multi dungeon quest chain is a good start to show that you're worthy. Why do you need hc at 60 when you don't want to raid? See above, max rank spells, epic riding... and there is things to do besides raiding at 60, please don't dictate how I have fun in the game. You might not see why hc at 60 is relevant for non-raiders, but that doesn't mean people don't want it or need it. Again: there's zero benefit in keeping players at 59. And it's also not a handout. Remain hc at 60 is a setting. Add a console command ".hc remain" to let people stay hc.
