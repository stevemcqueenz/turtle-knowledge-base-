# Introducing a new challenge - Dungeon Delver Challenge

## Post 157574 by Senzei — 2025-10-28T18:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157574#p157574 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Dungeon Delver Challenge

Description:
Your character’s growth is bound to the dark halls of Azeroth’s dungeons.
From level 20 onward, you may still complete any quests and activities you wish — but only dungeons and dungeon-related quests may grant you experience.

The surface world offers no more strength — only the depths can make you stronger.

 **Rules**

You may level normally until reaching level 20.

After level 20:

Experience may only be earned from:

Dungeon mob kills

Dungeon quests

You may freely explore the world, complete quests, craft, gather, or participate in PvP — but **none** of those actions will contribute experience toward leveling.

Dungeon quests are defined as any quests that require you to enter a dungeon - quests marked with (dungeon)

Grouping is allowed.

Combining this with other challenge modes (e.g., Hardcore, Slow & Steady) is optional but encouraged for extra glory.

 ----
Background:
I used to level purely through dungeons while running Slow & Steady and Hardcore, and it was always such a blast.

But ever since the August change to Slow & Steady (reducing dungeon experience to 0.5%), combined with the Inferno questline becoming somewhat obsolete as a means of reaching level 60, I’ve noticed a big decline in players who run dungeons on Hardcore as they progress.

As someone who’s always loved that dungeon-centric playstyle, it really feels like those good old days are slipping away — especially past level 40. Finding people eager to dive into the depths together has become much harder.

I think introducing a challenge like this would greatly revive dungeon activity — not only for dedicated dungeon dwellers like me on hardcore, but also for players who enjoy cooperative, slower-paced leveling.

It could help address the issue of finding groups in the mid-to-high level range, and overall, make the journey to 60 more engaging and social again.

As for rewards — I’m not too focused on that part. There are plenty of possibilities, but I’d rather leave this suggestion as it is and see what ideas the community and devs might come up with.

I genuinely hope others can see the potential in this idea.
And besides… it just sounds like a really fun challenge! - Hopefully ![😄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f604.svg)
