# Vagrant's Challenge Currency Scarcity

## Post 48140 by Turtlegaming200 — 2023-05-30T22:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48140#p48140 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**The Scarcity**

- When a player is going through the Vagrant Challenge, there could be certain specific imposed restrictions on trading to avoid making easy money from the equipment you're stopped from using.

- The scarcity of money would make the journey more challenging and therefore very rewarding. Stopping by in a town's shop to buy your new falchion will be a much more meaningful step in a wanderer's adventure.

- This could also allow a few merchants to be overhauled, having more items in stock of varying usefulness to make the experience less frustrating and monotone.

- Thus, the existence of unique utility trinkets that can only be used by Vagrants in their campaign would be a very interesting mechanic.
 *What do you think?*
