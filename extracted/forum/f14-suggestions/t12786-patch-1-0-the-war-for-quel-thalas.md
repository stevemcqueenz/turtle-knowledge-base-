# Patch 1.??.0: The war for Quel'thalas

## Post 85267 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-19T04:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85267#p85267 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Quel'thalas, once the magical land that housed the great capital of Silvermoon, pride of the noble race of high elves that at the same time contained within it the greatest magical power in all of Azeroth, the Sunwell.
For thousands of years the region enjoyed an eternal spring, inside the magic of the High Elves enveloped the place, protected and fed by the Sunwell.
Until one day the plague, led by the Traitor Prince Arthas Menethil, devastated the place, most of the High Elves were killed in their homeland and the few that remained fled, abandoning their ancient home at the mercy of the Undead and the destruction.
Now, years after its destruction and with the High Elf people partially recovered, they are preparing to fight again for their former home, unaware of the horrors that have been created within the once magical city of Silvermoon.

 ***NEW AREA: Quel'thalas***

The ancient home of the High Elf race that they shared hostilely with the Amani Trolls, in one side, the ruins of the City of Silvermoon, converted into the largest bastion of the Scourge in the Eastern Kingdoms, in the other side, the great Zul'aman, the last bastion of the Amani Empire.

 ***Sub-zones:***
 ***DeathSong Forest:*** Once called Eversong Forest, today it is a corrupt place with the plague of death and rot, the once golden autumn trees of the forest have absorbed the plague and have become twisted and dead trees, among its trees the Abominable creations of the plague roam and lie in wait for any unwary who venture without caution into the cursed forest.

 ***Quel’danas:*** The Isle where was located the Sunwell Grove and the Dath’remar’s Tomb, here the demons have concentrated with the years…no one knows what they’re planning, someone had said that they are looking for restore the Sunwell…

 ***Deathborn:*** Located nearly at the entrance to the forest, The Bastion that Arthas Menethil established in his attack on Silvermoon City seeking the Power of the Sunwell today serves as surveillance so that no one enters or anything leaves the forest.

 ***The Blood Watch:*** An outpost stablished by the Blood Elves aligned with the Alliance nearly to the ruins of Silvermoon, they have planned to retake the city from the hands of the undead, this will serve as the base of the Alliance in the area.

 ***Amani’watha:*** An outpost stablished by the Amani trolls nearly to the ruins of Zul’aman, with the help of the New Horde, they have planned to retake the city from the hands of the undead, this will serve as the base of the Horde.

 ***New Dungeons:***
 ***Sunwell Grove:*** The location of the High Elves beloved and shattered magical fountain, destroyed by Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider, is now a lifeless hole spewing corruption and fel magic as the demons roam freely looking for a way to resurrect the source to summon their demonic leaders.

 ***Dath’remar Tomb:*** The tomb of the first king of the High Elves, Dath’remar was resting in peace until the attack of the scourge, after the attack, the undead have raised his body to the curse of the undead, now, without any memories of his past, he has become a powerful Death Knight, slave of the Lich King.

 ***New Raids:***
 ***Silvermoon Ruins:*** The Glorious City of the High Elves, now a gigantic bastion of the plague, death and corruption roam its streets in the form of the Undead, while the souls of the Quel'dorei fallen in the Invasion of the Plague wander wailing in its alleys and abandoned buildings, hordes and hordes of endless Undead emerge from within.
The Horde and the Alliance have resolved that, to root out evil, they must assault the once great city of the High Elves and exterminate whatever is creating these abominations of the Lich King.

 ***Zul’aman Ruins:*** The capital of the Amani Trolls, located in a remote corner of Quel'thalas and guided by Zul'jin, the Trolls happily watched as the High Elves were slaughtered by Arthas and his abominations of death, eventually the Undead horde began to attack the citadel and the Trolls were forced to abandon it, now, with the help of the New Horde they have planned an incursion for recover it, not knowing what they will find inside.
