# Easy & effective Druid fixes

## Post 173112 by Halfgeek9 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-30T03:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173112#p173112 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Treeform is so strange, as a healer Druid, you enter the shapeshift which is supposed to boost your healing potential, and you lose on your strongest heals. HoTs are nice, but limited in scaling and use, making Resto Druids not desired in PvE & PvP. HoTs should be an option, not the *only* power for a deep Resto Druid.

Fix: Treeform no longer restricted to HoTs. -25% mana reduction to all healing spells. Aura grants +10% to healing received to grp. Treeform also grants 25% mana regen during combat.

Give Resto talent tree a proper out of combat Rez. This is not just for Resto Druids, it's for every other players who can now form groups with Resto Druid, and aren't punished because the main healer has no rez. It's a buff for everybody!

 ----------------------

Balance Druid is still bottom tier DPS and it's not even close.

It also suffers hugely due to RNG Eclipse mechanic. Boss phases, random need to move, severely punishes Balance dps. Lack of procs feels terrible.

Please just remove it.

A few ideas for effective fixes:

 1. 31pt talent "Elune's Fury" (respecting the lore and the talent "Elune's Grace")

 -> Casting Wrath gives the Druid a buff, stack 4 times, each buff: reduce cast speed & mana cost for Starfire by 25%.
 ----> 4 Wrath = 1 instant free Starfire (a dps buff, and mobility option).

 -> Casting Starfire give the Druid a buff, 4 separate stacks, each buff: +25% dmg to Wrath.
 ----> 4 Starfire = 4 casts of empowered Wrath.

Thus, the Druid rotates through Wrath & Starfire as intended, but it is CONTROLLED by the player, not RNG dependent.

OR

 2. Buff Moonfury 12% -> 20%. Replace 31pt talent with "Summon Treants" from TBC.

This gives the player an on demand DPS cooldown, that fits the lore and the numbers from treant dmg can be tweaked to control the overall avg DPS. Fun ability to have in both PvE & PvP.

Part of me isn't really big on interrupting my magic ball cast spam with an instant spell that breaks the flow. Maybe a case of 2 stacks of wrath casting bringing you down to 50% cast time reduction on starfire? So the rotation looks vaguely like -> Applying dots on pull and whenever necessary during the fight, then wrath wrath starfire wrath wrath starfire rinse and repeat.

> I'm not sure how I feel about instant cast starfires, because in theory it could be OP in a PvP context

It's quite hard to be hard casting 4x anything in PvP, so its not that OP. Nothing like Smite Crit -> Instant Smite. It would be a small buff for PvP, which is fine considering how trash tier Balance druid is.

It's a Starfire DPS buff for PvE, and a mana saver built in. Not that huge, since an instant Starfire is still 1.5s gcd, meaning you only "save" 1.3 - 1.5s every cycle.

Empowered Wrath is needed bc Eclipse generally offers 25% dmg buff. So this approach gives the Eclipse effect and cyclic play loop without the bad RNG.

Ofc, the numbers can be tweaked to allow Balance to meet a certain target DPS in raids. Easiest being Moonfury passive +12% currently can be altered.

In general, having a free instant Starfire is a "feel good" dopamine reward, then a short lull of 3 Starfires, to return to the dopamine +25% empowered Wrath spam. It should be quite fun.
