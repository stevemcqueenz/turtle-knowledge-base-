This talent is really bad for lower levels trying to enjoy demons. Without enough crit you cannot stack and maintain this and it’s the bulk of your spec as a demo warlock. I propose either allowing the pets own criticals to apply stacks as well, or giving it the demonic precision treatment (for the same reason), where it has a good flat chance to proc and then scales with stats. The pets just don’t do enough work to justify having a really slow ramp up time (at low end gear/levels particularly). Also I’d like to note that this is most noticeably an issue in PvP, but demo is close to perfect . Thanks!
