# Please add a Cloth harness

## Post 90336 by Zeran (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-03-16T14:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90336#p90336 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There exists a harness chest armour for every armour type, except for Cloth.

A lot of the time, I personally don't wear robes, as they hide many of the other items I wear. I enjoy a more "lighter" transmog feel and harnesses look great. Unfortunately, wearing one on a Clothie such as a priest is impossible.

The only way to wear one currently is to roll a warrior, who will spawn with a [[Brawler's Harness]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6125), and then mailing it to your Clothie. BUT that goes into the Shirt slot and not the Chest slot.

In the game files themselves, 2 Cloth harnesses do already exist, but they are unobtainable:

- [[Sen'jin Guard's Harness]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=50409)

- [[Demon Hunter Harness]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=50034)
If the Sen'jin Guard mons can wear them, then I'd like to be able to wear one too, please   turtle_tongue_head
