# Enchanting Specializations

## Post 23867 by Bricius — 2022-08-28T21:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23867#p23867 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Of the Creation Professions we have in the game the only one in Classic that doesn't seem to have one is Enchanting. Given this servers love for making custom content and recipes, I'd like to suggest a possibility of three different specializations that could help this profession feel a bit more unique per character and open up a few missing niches, along with a brief explanation to how the Horde and Alliance could on each side.

Ancient Enchanting
Description - Originating from Ancient Elves, and values the potency of its magic above all else.
Quest - Starts in either Capital city, the Enchanter goes to the Elves in Dire Maul to talk to  the Elves of the Shen'dralar. This starts a simple quest chain (much like other specializations) with an NPC in the Shen'dralar library. The NPC gives the Enchanter a task to collect 3 Golden Pearls, 3 Black Pearls, and 3 Blue Dragon Scales from the Blue Dragons in Winterspring. When you return you are given a staff and told to enchant it with your power (requiring a certain enchanting level, right click item).
Benefits - Access to Ancient Enchanting recipes that give higher stat bonuses and allows you to craft Rare and Epic quality Staffs.

Runic Enchanting
Description - Originating from the Titans, but further used through history by Dwarves and Tauren to create Glyphs of great power.
Quest - Starts in either Capital city, the enchanter is then led to Ironforge/Thunder Bluff. NPC then has you go to Uldaman (outside the dungeon, not in) to collect relics of the Titans for study. Then when you return you are sent to Blackrock Depths/Hateforge Quarry to get a ledger from the Dark Iron Dwarves called "Recent Findings on Titanic Language". Return to the quest giver in Thunderbluff/Iron Forge and you learn Runic Enchanting.
Benefits - Access to Runic Enchanting recipes that give specific on use/activated effects (but NOT towards stats) and allows the creation of Rare and Epic quality trinkets. Examples of such enchantments would be "Fast Run/Mount/Swim Speed", Damage vs X-Enemy-Type Increased, Increased Gathering Speed with X, Higher Chance of finding more resources per resource node, etc.

Spirit Enchanting
Description - Originating from races with deep connections to their ancestors like Dwarves and Trolls, these enchantments allow you to call upon such beings to aid you in battle.
Quest - Starts with a quest giver in Hinterlands (Wildhammer Stronghold for Alliance, Raventusk Village for Horde), your character is required to go to three different locations around the world and speak to  an NPC before being required to beat that NPC in combat. The first NPC is in Azshara where you speak to an NPC who gives you a riddle, then regardless of the answer you speak with them again to fight them. This NPC represents the Ancestors of the past, reaching all the way back to before the Well of Eternity was broken by the greediness of the Elves. The second is in Deadwind Pass/Duskwood, where the NPC tells you of the horrors that happened there and then fights you. This NPC represents the present and how even now we must pay respects to those who have moved on and effect our lives. Lastly the third NPC is in the Eastern Plaguelands, where an NPC "Tells you of the future" (totally not metagaming) and how this land will play an important role to all in the future (referencing the Death Knights, taking back Quel'thalas, the recolonization of the Northern Eastern Kingdoms), before fighting you. This NPC represents the future and how even now we must consider how we want to be remembered to the future as an ancestor.
Benefits - Allows access to battle themed enchantments which proc frequently, giving a bonus to stat X/Y/Z every so often or when hit in combat such as haste, defense, mana per 5s, etc, and allows the crafting of Scrolls for short term buffs and utility effects (just like the in game scrolls, plus minor things like Slow Fall, Water Walking, and so on)

Like I said with my Druid Form post, would be more then happy to help on this if it gets accepted :)
