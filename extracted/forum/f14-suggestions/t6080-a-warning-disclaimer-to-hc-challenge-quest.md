# A warning disclaimer to HC challenge quest

## Post 38205 by Maesus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-19T08:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38205#p38205 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It seems that every single day, someone posts something along the lines of "my HC character died due to bug please res" or "my HC character was killed in PVP"

These people didn't read the rules, because the rules are sort of hidden in the Our Features section of Turtle WoW webpage. They heard about the server, they liked the HC challenge, and so they jumped on it. Then something happens and they go on the forum, because they think their death was unfair.

So, please put up a warning disclaimer that would pop-up when a player tries to complete the **Stay Awhile and Listen** quest, which would quickly notify them of the no-ressurection policy.
