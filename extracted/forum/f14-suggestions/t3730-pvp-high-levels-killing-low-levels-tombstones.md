## Post 22468 by Thol (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-08-15T18:56:00Z

Someone suggested to build upon the new dishonorable ranks system and ultimately make a ganker hostile to his own faction at Pariah, they couldn't go to their faction cities anymore and would be hunted by their former allies.

I myself suggested to copy retail system of applying a bounty on the ganker head after 10+ kills, players would be rewarded for taking them down.

There are a lot of creative, RP-friendly ways to go about fixing this, instead of restricting or even disabling WPvP, which has been a staple of WoW Vanilla since it's inception.

In the end it'll be the devs choice, they could absolutely get rid of WPvP flagging altogether and focus on RP-PvE (WPvP is RP for me, but that's another discussion), it would solve the problem of HC players getting griefed and it would allow players to get +30% XP without any drawbacks as they wished.
