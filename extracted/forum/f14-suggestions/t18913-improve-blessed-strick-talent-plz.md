# Improve Blessed Strick talent plz

## Post 128659 by Abolaboboom — 2025-04-27T04:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128659#p128659 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Unfortunately, we found that Holy Paladin's Blessed Strike talent did not actually run throughout the entire Karazhan development process. Please improve the Blessed Strike talent as a deep healing talent that should truly work, rather than just a gimmick.
For example, increasing the range to match the Judgement, throwing the weapon and being self disarmed for a short period of time, or enhancing Weapon Chain, sacrificing some healing ability in exchange for disarmed immunity, and so on.
Or, increasing movement speed or reducing damaged or controled in a short time, better escaping danger at close range, or resisting crises at close range. The first principle of Healer is their own safety in order to heal others.
In short, i hope that this talent can have a wider applicability, rather than still being a remote caster before 1.17 v.

## Post 128689 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-27T11:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128689#p128689 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Let me put it in numbers:

Holy Strike with blessed Strikes
 293-325 Holy Damage +43% of your spellpower
 180 Health + 30% of your Addheal and +90 Mana for yourself and 4 other targets in melee range.
Totalling 900 + 120% of your Addheal in Health and up to 360 mana restored

Its usable while silenced and Its an AoE heal, something paladin desperately lacks and it can be combined alongside hybrid playstiles that aim to proc weaponeffects while supporting their party. It is also smart and will preferably target allies who are hurt.

To compare with spending the same time casting Flash of light(Rank 7) with Healing Light and Illumination
Heals a friendly target for an average of 515 Health (+115 from blessing of light if present) + 43% Addheal for an average of ~130-140 mana

Plenty of bosses allow for paladins to hit in melee no problem. Including relatively hard fights such as Sapphiron, Thaddius, Vaelstrasz and other AoE encounters usually lack the kind of threat that prevents healers from going into melee range.

As much as melee healer as a full on playstile doesn't work, blessed strikes ain't the problem. that talent is amazing if you look at the value it gives and plenty of paladins sleep on it.
