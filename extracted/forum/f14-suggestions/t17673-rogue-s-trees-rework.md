First of all I want to make clear the forum name isn't a plea, a sleight to anybody, my intent is to "Help Rogue, Maybe?", felt like it could be misintrepreted after creating the forum account :).
To the post now!

People on forum complain about Rogue, people in game complain about Rogue, I myself find Rogue to be not in a great state.
I'm not a fan of the Subtlety rework but I don't mind it, it's an interesting take, and seems to be working as intended, providing enough boost to the raid to be considered a good spec to bring in the roster.

Combat Rogue seems to overtake Assassination by a large margin in DPS, which to be fair, isn't weird at all, was like this in Vanilla also.
My "rework" approach looks into class identity, and tries to balance the difference in DPS between the specs without altering anything other than the positioning of the nodes in the first rows, the ones accessible to all specs.

Subtlety :

Improved Eviscerate :
Added in the first row.

Camouflage replaced with Hightened Senses, and becomes a built-in tool :
Looks into movement speed and stealth level being a QOL improvement while moving around.
Stealth Detection increase is mostly useful for PvP scenarios, and whichever is in the talent tree will be picked from PvP players, both are a must.

Serrated Blades replaced with Improved Expose Armor :
Suits the support Rogue fantasy to have the talent in its tree instead of that of the Assassination.

Combat :

Lightining Reflexes moves to Setup place, Setup to Dual-Wield Specialization place, DWS moves to LR place :
These talents have been moved around to put DWS in a reachable row to the other 2 specs, will provide some damage without hindering that of the Combat spec. No idea if this placement would create problems for lower levels balance, but I believe this talent should be reachable as Assassination, and also Subtlety, as all 3 specs are dual wielding, doesn't make much sense for only one to be "specialized" in the only fighting style they all adopt.

Improved Backstab replaced with Improved Blade Tactics :
This is more of a "identity" change, Slice And Dice and Flourish, suit the combat fantasy more than the Assassination.

Close Quarter Combat : Removed from Combat tree.

Assassination :

Ruthlessness moved in the free spot after moving Improved Eviscerate to Subtlety tree.

Improved Blade Tactics replaced with Improved Backstab :
This is more of a "identity" change, Improved Backstab suits the Assassination fantasy more than the Combat.
Still obtainable by Combat Daggers/Fist Weapons Rogues.

Serrated Blades takes Ruthlessness previous spot :
Identity change; seems to be in line with "Taste for blood", Assassination becoming a bleed/poison focused tree.

Close Quarter Combat replaces Improved Exposed Armor :
This talent will provide Assassination and Subtlety rogues with some more damage, and is still reachable to a Combat Rogue that prefers to opt for daggers/fist weapons instead of swords/axes.
