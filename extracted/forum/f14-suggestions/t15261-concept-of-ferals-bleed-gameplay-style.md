# Concept of Ferals Bleed gameplay style

## Post 103894 by Templar85 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-10-19T19:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103894#p103894 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Concept of Ferals Bleed gameplay style:
It's damage not affected by the armor amount of the enemy for like normal attacks do, but it takes time until it is reaches it's full potential.

Positive side
- There is no huge upfront threat from this type of attacks. It is a damage over time.

- It is good against high armor enemies such as raid bosses.

- If the initial application of the bleed not get resisted then the later ticks will not get resisted either.
On normal attacks each attack has a chance to be Miss, Dodge, Parry.

- It is good when a druid can not stay continuously on a target due to a mechanic.
The bleed damage still ticking on the target.
Negative side
- Dots can not crit.

- It is limited for cats only.

- It does not scales as good as a normal type of attacks. As the raid can place more and more armor reducing effects on the boss their dps will increase proportionally but the bleed damage staying the same.

- It is already takes time to set up bleeds for their maximum efficiency and as the boss dies faster that mean less time for bleeds to do their damage.

Dungeons and Open World pros and cons:

Pro
Less geared tank can hold aggro still without druid ripping it without frontal burst damage.

Con
It is not effective to setup on low health mobs. They don't live to long if the druid is geared.

PvP

Pro
It can be good against high armor enemies such as warriors and bear druids.

Cons
Dots can not crit which gives opportunity to be outhealed.
Current form of PvP without resilience is about cc and burst. Dot types of damages need to be high enough to be considered useful instead of a instant or burst attacks.

Conclusion:

- It is nice to have more playstyles for cats, more options are always better then less. Bears should have too bleeds.

- It is most rewarding in Raids against Bosses. It is helpful in dungeon bosses but only while the player is not geared enough and the fight takes time.

- Because it is take much more time and effort to set up then do normal attacks the end result aka its damage should be much higher than normal attacks. Like 30% higher.

Developer insights which are wrong in my opinion:

> Current powershifting dps is fine for cats, don't need to improve it because cats shouldn't do as much damage as warriors.

Cats Powershifting shouldn't be on pair with them but that doesn't mean that their current dps is fine. I think they need some dps increase because they have only single target dps. Around 15% increase

> Bleed style of feral play should end up same damage then current powershifting style.

On Discord in druid channel it has been theorycrafted that it will do around the same or less dps if the rotation is not perfect. It should end up in higher damage if it takes more skill to do it. I think it needs to be higher dps like by 20%-25% if rotation played out perfectly than Powershifting.
