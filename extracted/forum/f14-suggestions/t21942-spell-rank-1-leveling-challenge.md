# Spell rank 1 leveling challenge

## Post 152039 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-17T08:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152039#p152039 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you pick that challenge, then you can't learn spells from trainers.
All your available spells/skills are learned automatically once you level up. Exceptions are spells learned as a reward of quest: bear form, defensive stance, voidwalker etc.
Only first rank of each spell is learned.
Pet spells included. You can't train features of hunter pets after rank 1 like increased health from trainer. You can't buy grimoirs of ranks higher than 1 for warlock.
