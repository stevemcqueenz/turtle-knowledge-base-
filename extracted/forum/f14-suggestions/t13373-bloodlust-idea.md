# Bloodlust idea

## Post 89949 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-12T09:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89949#p89949 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I'm thinking about a change regarding bloodlust, what do you guys think about it?
I really like the addition of bloodlust in TWOW and I think it was implemented+nerfed properly to a Vanilla+ environment.
However, having some problems with it:
 * Enhancement is a low-damage melee build, using the BL on CD on the top DPS or on the tank is a feels bad thing since it is your ultimate.
 * Half of the resto shamans give up on half of the resto tree to get this ability - I think if guilds want bloodlust, they should have an enhancement shaman for it.

My idea -> Changing bloodlust into a fire totem (still, enhancement tree ultimate).
Current BL: 5 min CD, target gets 20% haste for 20 seconds.
Proposed BL totem: 5 min CD, gives 20% melee haste for party members for 10 sec in a 10 yard radius.

Why would I propose this:
 * 10 yard radius is very hard on healers, they don't want to be at that range from the melee during raids fights.
 * Would allow the enhancement shaman to enjoy the effect of BL.
 * Would make BL stronger while adding some limitations to prevent it from being broken: only 10 sec, only for melee and party members have to be really close for full effect.

My desired result: guilds wanting 1-2 enhancement shaman specifically. Resto shamans staying at resto tree because 10 yards radius is not something that healers can usually do (+ they wouldn't feel the effect of BL, unlike enhancement shamans who do melee).

While it's a nice idea, resto shamans won't stop dipping into Enh tree, unless there is something more compelling added to the Resto tree. I can absolutely see Resto shamans diving in melee range to drop the BL totem. And "they won't feel the effect of BL" is a weak argument, because they currently also don't, they use it on the top DPS.

I see two solutions to making BL not suck, and both require the Resto tree to have something that would hurt Resto shamans to miss out on:

 -Make BL affect both the target AND the shaman at the same time (you can still buff just yourself if you're selfish or solo)
OR
 -Nerf BL and make it party-wide. Basically your suggestion, but not in totem form.
Totems are very clunky, especially if their range is low.

I'd totally agree to buff the resto tree to 'convince' resto shamans to pick those talents.

My main problem here is how the shamans were designed: at raids, resto shaman healing is 90% chain heal which is a baseline spell and barely has upgrades via the talent tree (I tend to experiment to have something more similar to a healing rotation but the less chain heal I use the worse my healing output is). While talents like healing way are nice, they target a healing barely used by restos in a raid environment.
The bottom of the resto tree (somewhat) handles mana management problems but TWOW seriously buffed shamans in this regard: the tea, the bunch of '5% mana regenerates normally whikle casting' and the druid buff are much bigger helps than the slight buff on manaspring and the existence of manatide.
My resto raids until AQ40 (true, I have no Naxx experience) and having no real mana problems with proper downgrades. By default having 15-20% mana regen while casting with the druid buff and can go until 35-40% with mana regen set for fights like Twins.
It's weird to say this but manatide after this buffs is not good enough as an ultimate to keep restos in the resto tree.
My dilemma here is that resto shamans are already very potent, big buffs on the tree could make them too good (nowadays both at PUGs and at guild raids, I tend to see a resto shaman at the top of the healing chart).

I'd think about slightly buffing manaspring and getting rid of manatide totally. In exchange, would consider adding cloudburst totem (a TWOW version, not a copy-paste from retail) or a buff to chain heals as an ultimate, since resto raid healing = spamming different grades of chain heal.
