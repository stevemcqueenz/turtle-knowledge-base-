# Unique Warlock mounts for each race

## Post 9377 by Totalmxo — 2021-02-15T21:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9377#p9377 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Howdy everyone! Ranthos here. TABC and I were thinking of some ideas and Daave brought up a great point. Orc warlocks on the Felsteed look silly. This made us start thinking of ideas to fix this. After much talk, Kratos suggested that I should make it a formal suggestion so here I am. We came up with unique mounts for each race that can play as a warlock. To start with, humans and undead can keep the felsteed because it looks normal for them (also each of their race mounts are horses anyways). For Gnomes, maybe a flaming MechaStrider or one with black and red flames would be really cool! Next is Orc's. A felwolf would be a really cool mount for them. Lastly, goblins should get a flaming motorcycle (kinda like ghost rider). This is not super important, but would be really cool if this ends up getting put into the game.
