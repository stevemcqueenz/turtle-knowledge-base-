# Frostbrand build in PvE Endgame

## Post 132276 by Minco — 2025-05-21T07:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132276#p132276 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
I'm new to Turtle WoW but I do have something on my wish list for enhancement shamans that could make them more fun (for some) and solve current mana issues (as I've read in different posts on here).

The rework of the talent "Elemental Weapons" gives potential for all sorts of different, interesting, builds. However as it is right now, the Frostbrand part of the talent is more or less irrelevant (in comparison to Windfury 2h) in lategame/endgame PvE content, since the Frost Shock debuff doesn't apply on bosses/some mobs. Some people enjoy the Windfury iteration of Enhancement shaman, where you just wait for big bonks on procs. On the other hand we have people like me, who enjoys more stable and "faster" dps, with lower hits.

I've seen that this has been somewhat discussed in other posts but I just wanted to give my two cents on the matter. Don't get me wrong, I am really excited to see reworked talents like "Elemental Weapons", I just want to see them getting some more attention since it can give people different build options that are not close to replicated from "regular vanilla".

Some suggestions (some taken from other post);
 1. Stormstrike increases the next 2 sources of Fire, **Frost**, or Nature damage by 20%.
 2. Frostbrand is guaranteed to critical strike for 10 seconds after casting Frost Shock.
 3. Makes bosses immune to the slow component of Frost Shock but not the actual debuff.
