# Make Horde World Buffs reach the entrance of Org

## Post 114315 by Ennv — 2024-12-25T18:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114315#p114315 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Why don't the Horde world buffs reach the entrance of Org like the Alliance World Buffs reach the entrance to SW? This might seem like a minor detail, but it actually makes a pretty big difference. On Alliance I could park myself at the entrance, gain rested XP, and likely catch a world buff. On Horde I can't do that, and have missed out on several hours of world buffs as a result. It feels kind of unfair, and it's a big enough issue for me that I won't play Horde for my next characters. I'm standing well inside of Orgrimmar where the Tents get dropped, the world buffs should reach me where I stand, but for some reason they don't. To be clear, I'm not standing outside of Org, I make sure that I always stand within Org and that my current zone specifies Org. The tents give their rested XP in a relatively large radius, so even if the Tent is outside of Org, I can stand inside Org and still get the Rested XP-- but for whatever reason I don't get the world buffs. ![😦](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f626.svg)

I know this might seem kind of dumb to whine about, but the factions are relatively balanced. This one thing, for me, tips the balance to Alliance by a fairly large degree as leveling with world buffs is fun, while constantly missing out on them just feels bad.
