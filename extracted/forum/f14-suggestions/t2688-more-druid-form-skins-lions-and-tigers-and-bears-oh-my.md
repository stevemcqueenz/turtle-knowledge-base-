# More Druid Form Skins! Lions and Tigers and Bears, Oh My!

## Post 16890 by Katty — 2022-02-09T11:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16890#p16890 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I love the store skins for the druid forms, especially the bear and cat. They're gorgeous wintery skins, and the models are even bigger than the original druid form models. However, I feel it's quite limited. I think there could be even more!   turtle_in_love

Particularly, *I* would love to see a lioness skin for the cat form (savannah huntress) and even an orange (or white) tiger skin, as this is the year of the tiger!! I think it would be fitting for Tauren especially. A panther skin and a striped panther (moonstalker) skin would be fitting options for Night Elves. For something extra spicy, the unused icy blue cat skin and the the untameable orange-striped green cat would be exceptionally unique! <https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/family.php?id=cat>

Perhaps even the spotted black cat (nightsaber) and spotted white cat (snow leopard) could be alternate travel form skins, for those who love the original cheetah form but want more colors.

There's not a *whole* lot of possible other bear skins, but I'm sure many (myself included) would love a grey, brown/dark brown, or black bear option as well! It would be fun to spice up the druid forms with different colors, so that numerous druids can look like how they might imagine themselves in their said forms. ^^
<https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/family.php?id=bear>

I hope you like some of my suggestions, thank you!   turtle_tongue
