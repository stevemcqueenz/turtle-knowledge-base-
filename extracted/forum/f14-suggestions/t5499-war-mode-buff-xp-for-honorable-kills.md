# War Mode Buff - XP for Honorable Kills

## Post 33703 by Ashstache (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-01T12:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33703#p33703 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

War Mode is attractive for its XP bonus, but in its current state, it mostly just makes bait out of PvE players solely interested in the XP gain. This doesn't really do much for WPvP, but does produce an extensive amount of salt. Whatever.

Add XP gains for honorable kills, and you have a reason for players to seek out WPvP. Give it declining rewards for repeated kills of the same individual to prevent cooperative farming, exactly the same as honor gains. What's not to love?
