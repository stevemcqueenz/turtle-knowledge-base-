# Hunter itself needs a QOL rework; not just MM.

## Post 170203 by Tuskaneer — 2026-03-06T06:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170203#p170203 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Being on the PVP ranking grind; I can say that the overall balance for Hunters has completely been ignored in lieu of gimmick abilities or "refreshing" gameplay changes might improve specific damage aspects for marksmanship, but doesn't improve the overall quality of life when compared to every other class. Currently if you want all the "survivability" PvP traits; you have to have a mixture of every tree spec; which leaves you as below average at everything, and master at nothing. Some of the issues are pretty blatant when it comes to PvP and the upcoming class balance doesn't address these issues but instead decides to put a small bandage on a gaping wound pushing Hunters to be an exclusive PvE only class.

 1. Global cooldown tied to every "passive" ability completely guts most reactive options in regards to survivability.

The skills in question are all aspects, tracking abilities, Trueshot Aura, and even Hunter's Mark. Every time you cast one of these; it puts the remainder of your unused skills on a 1 second cooldown. This completely guts any counter play for any spec when engaging in PVP fights or reacting to incoming damage/stuns (Swapping to Monkey/Cheetah isn't possible if you have to use an ability such as Scatter Shot, Wing Clip, or even any offensive ability leaving you at a severe disadvantage to disengage or mitigate damage; often giving opponents a much easier time to get free damage or daze when attempting to reposition. Hunter's Mark with GCD is a just a giant warning message to the target telling them that a Hunter will attack soon given the windup delay for any auto/ranged skill. This is also worsened due to passive abilities being reset on resurrection, causing MM (and soon all specs) 2-3 seconds of setup minimum before leaving your graveyard. Classes such as Warriors don't have GCDs applied to stances allowing them to instantly combo abilities with ease or prep engagements even when mounted, and similarly with Shamans with having no GCD tied to totems. Trueshot Aura is going to be even more problematic when it is expanded as a base ability seeing how there is no way to activate it individually when the aura is already active in your party forcing you to leave the proximity of another Hunter decreasing prep agency in BGs.

 -An easy fix is to just remove the GCD from these abilities; allow Trueshot Aura to be casted while mounted if unable to activate while overlapping.

 2. Mana regeneration isn't the problem; the high burst cost of abilities combined with low base mana is the culprit.

This has become an increasing problem with the introduction of Lacerate in light of removing talent base damage all while introducing another GCD. While the upcoming MM rework can be seen as a much needed change, Aspect of the Viper isn't going to fix the problem of Hunters running out of mana mid fight especially when you are introducing Arcane Shot and Aimed Shot as required rotational abilities for anyone who isn't wearing T3+ gear or permanently mana flasked. The experimental ammunition playstyle also seems a little forced given the reduced talent cooldowns for Arcane/Aimed shot. Hunters would still be forced to FD/Drink mid-fight to band aid fix this issue in party/raids, sacrificing 10-15+ seconds of only auto attacks to continue damage rotations in extended fights, or just permanently stay in Viper aspect; reducing overall DPS effectiveness. Currently for short skirmishes you'll see MM Hunters with roughly 25-40% remaining mana versus every other class sitting at 55-70% after full ability rotations, and that's before they have to resurrect their pets if fallen in combat.

 -Consider adjusting base mana costs for abilities; or increase talent mana cost reduction percentages.

 3. Crowd control abilities have completely fallen behind every other class both duration and agency wise; combined with conflicting talents which defeats the original purpose of using those abilities in the first place.

Most other classes are given a guaranteed ranged stun, slow, immobilize, or pure CC ability which lasts a minimum of 5+ seconds with zero chance of those being broken from additional passive casts. Both MM and Survival talent trees force you to use either go pure damage tied with heavily unfavored coin flip talents and pray that CC lands on your target; all while further increasing the chance these CCs will instantly break due to the added attack or dot damage roulette. Scatter Shot and Freezing trap suffers greatly from this when combined with Stinging Nettle, Piercing Shots, Endless Quiver, and even self inflicted poison food buffs. All this combined with most classes not having diminishing returns applied to CC spam abilities or an extremely low chance to break their own CC's with dots puts Hunters at a huge disadvantage especially when combined with the MM deadzone. For traps itself, this issue is further compounded with server instability/rubber banding causing players to either run/jump over traps without triggering, failing to place while eating the CD/GCD, being placed 1-2 seconds behind the moving player causing them to be useless in an active fight, or activating 10 yards past where the initial trap was placed allowing the enemy player to get a free melee combo before being CC'd.

 -Increase Scatter Shot duration to a 5 second disorient at minimum; while adding a spell interrupt lockout for x seconds due to compensate the short 15 yard range (21yd if considering Hawk Eye talent) as MM. Possible talent rework to allow the ability to stun or add a .5/1 second duration; or decrease to CD duration.
 -Rework the 5 TP (20% coin flip) Improved Concussive Shot and change it to either stun for 4 seconds after a ranged ability crits and/or if the target has Hunter's Mark/trap effect applied to them.
 -Rework Stinging Nettle to apply the damage immediately and Piercing Shots after 3 seconds rather than 8, or make both Scatter Shot/Freezing Traps immune to passive bleed/poison tick breaks if unchanged.
 -Rework Improved Wing clip to remove the coin flip immobilization, giving it a guaranteed chance to immobilize if target is under the effects of any trap. Giving it an increased x chance to avoid being dodged or increasing the total slow duration upon successful immobilization would be a good QOL due to Warrior's Hamstring cooldown being removed.
 -Give Frost and Fire trap categories their own separate cooldown pools; or apply a lower initial CD to the category not used allowing for flexibility for all specs if tied to above reworks.
 -Give Survival a talent at the 25 point mark to cause Freezing Traps to also spell lock for x amount of seconds in lieu of sacrificing trap damage (if dual cooldowns aren't implemented.)
 -Improved dodge/block enhancement talent for pets exclusive to deep BM tree (add spell lock to Intimidation upon successful interrupt via Spirit Bond talent.)

 4. Stings and immolation traps are far behind damage wise when compared to every other class dot (and often instant cast) ranged spells even after factoring in the 5-talent damage bonus of 30% sting/Survival trap bonus.

There's a reason why almost every Hunter rarely uses Serpent sting in PvE/PvP before the T3 set bonus; the damage is often too negligible to use in most fights due to the high mana cost while doing half as much damage as every other class dot ability; the only real use for SS is when applied as a Rogue "tracking" spell in fights or as a cleanse bait at Rank 1. Viper sting is also rarely used due to the 6 second CD while barely putting a dent in most mana user's bars (the only exception being when used against another Hunter.) The same goes for pre-fight Immolation trap for non-survival specs; it does 600-800 damage overall all while having to predict where your target "might" move to. Survival does a little over 2k all while requiring you to be in melee range and exposing you to both physical and magic damage. Most other classes can easily meet or exceed these numbers given their ability to crit and have extreme ease of use by letting them cast at max range instantly with shorter CD timers. This gives an extremely unsatisfying gameplay loop and even worse when comparing the longer CD, non-existent "burst" damage, and ease of the dot being dispelled/inability to be reapplied.

 -Adjust Serpent Sting base damage to match 70% of other classes average dot damage output with minor AP scaling to assist through tier gear transition; move Improved Stings talent deep into MM tree to increase it to 100%. Tweak the upcoming SS experimental arrows buff from 100>50% if needed.
 -Increase Viper Sting's base mana drain by 25%. Increase can be tied to the talent mentioned above.
 -Give the chance for immolation traps to crit or convert part of the overall Survival damage into dealing xx% on the initial tick; increase overall damage for non-survival specs.

There are more issues at hand with Hunters (and Druids) with bleed immunity, the deadzone/windup delay auto/ability when tied with server instability, projectiles putting the user instantly in combat, but not the target causing a 1.5-2 second delay when attempting to interrupt and thus at a disrupt disadvantage compared to any other ranged class, etc. but I hope at least #1 and #3 are addressed before throwing out mitigation compensations in an effort to "balance" out the classes when it comes to PvP.
