# Hunter. Rotation improvement

## Post 149134 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-29T12:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149134#p149134 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I like the idea of ​​a close connection between the pet and the hunter, but I don't like 2 extra spells. Spell overload is one of the reasons why I hate WoTLK and prefer classic. And I really wouldn't like to see classic become a crooked imitation of WoTLK. MM Hunter has now turned into a one-button turret, like from a mobile game, the rotation of the spec is completely broken

The following are the suggestions:
General:
 1. Steady shot - available to everyone and learned from trainers
 2. Aimed shot - a feature of MM and has improvements in the tree aimed at more frequent use, and not the ridiculous Steady shot turret as it is now

 **MM:**
 1. talent Steady shot -> Aimed shot
 2. talent Improved steady shot -> Adjust fire
stacks 3 times. each steady shot hit reduces the duration of the next Aimed shot cast by 0.4 sec on the same target and increases the hit chance by 1%. If miss - the effect gone.

 **BM:**
 1. Add a pop-up notification about the possibility of using Baited Shot, similar to Overpower, Revenge, Shadow trance. So that you don't have to keep one eye on the spell icon all the time. So that it pops up when the spell is fully ready for use. Not on CD and the pet made a crit
 2. Instead of an additional extra spell, add the Baited Shot effect to Arcane shot with an internal CD, or remove the shared cooldown between them.
 3. Coordinated assault. reduce internal CD to 2.5sec And when Swift aspects are triggered or Rapid fire is turned on, remove the CD altogether. Guns and crossbows with 3.5+ began to appear en masse with tbc. in classic, taking into account all the stats and the speed from the quiver, between shots less than 3 seconds. BM hunter needs to either slow down and keep a rhythm of about 3 seconds between Steady shots or lose 1 possible effect from Coordinated assault
 4. I would like to have mana regen somewhere. for example from pet crits after Baited shot
