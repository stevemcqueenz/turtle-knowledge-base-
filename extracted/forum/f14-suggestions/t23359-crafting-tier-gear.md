# Crafting Tier Gear

## Post 163409 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-12-21T05:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163409#p163409 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

In an effort to make crafting professions like leather working and blacksmithing relevant *to most late game players*, adding a piece of equipment to a new tier set (or really any top level set) would be a huge boon to crafting as a whole.

For example, perhaps a T4 leather set must have its chest piece be crafted.
Or perhaps one of the small sets like those from AQ could include a weapon made by blacksmiths.
I’m not suggesting to change anything existing, just giving examples of what could been done as a way to suggest what might be done in the future.

I know there are crafters out there, myself included, who would enjoy these kimd of recipes. And it could breathe new life into some of the more neglected crafting professions.
