# Please consider item crates for level 60 BG's

## Post 174160 by Piggiepig — 2026-04-06T09:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174160#p174160 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Mark turn-ins dont come close to covering the basics - food, bandages, potions etc. As it stands it could be more interesting/profitable for some to stay on 59 and enjoy the benefits.

A small reward could incentivize more players to participate, for people to keep playing after they have reached their honor goals. Some gold gain will allow players to stimulate the market and to enhance the gameplay with interesting new consumables (such as from survival proffesion).

With the high activity of battlegrounds there is not a lot of potential to farm between.

While leveling you gain XP, honor, valuable gear and item crates. On 60 its honor, valuable gear and less financial gain (20 silver per 3 marks or so)
