Hello turtles!   smiling_turtle_head

I have been thinking, it would be cool if, Shamans had a quest that would let them change the color of their lightning!
Once you have gathered all your totems at level 30, you become more in tune with the elements and can start tapping into their intent at level 40.

This would be chosen in the form of spirits. These are the spirits i've come up with so far.

Spirit of Fury: Red lightning
Spirit of Tranquility: Green Turquoise Lightning
Spirit of the Sun: Yellow Lightning

These spirits could also be race locked to Orc, Troll, and Tauren (in that order) for RP reasons.
I think this would be a cool way to customize your shaman and i also have a shaman named Redshock and red lightning would look very good on him. Think about it! and let me know what you think.
