# Allow [Enchant Weapon - Lifestealing] to affect spells too

## Post 70300 by Xa1amandor — 2023-10-12T06:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70300#p70300 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In light of all the new Vampirism and "x% of damage dealt is returned as healing" armor & weapon effects, I propose that TWoW adjust the Lifestealing to weapon enchant to allow it to work for spellcasting too!

Would this not be a welcome & interesting change that fits well with the aesthetic of the recent patch update?

Please comment below to share thoughts, criticisms and support.   smiling_turtle_head

## Post 70597 by Delsin — 2023-10-13T13:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70597#p70597 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

They could either add the spell effect to Lifestealing, add another enchant recipe for spells specifically, or add a new recipe that affects both so it would be more enticing for hybrid classes. If a new 'both'/pure Vampirism recipe was added it would probably be slightly more expensive in mats, but not too much more.

As a side note, Vampirism does present some very interesting opportunities for fun and creative procs on equipment or even talents. Like say a 'Bloodthirster Sword' that has a conditional Vampirism/Lifesteal, where it gets x% Vampirism based on the number of Bleed effects on target (up to a limit of course). Or fist weapon that has a chance to proc a high % melee damage specific Vampirism for a few seconds. Vampirism could even be a defensive proc, like 'upon being reduced to 40% or less of your maximum health, gain y% Vampirism for 8 seconds, this effect can only occur every 30 seconds'.
