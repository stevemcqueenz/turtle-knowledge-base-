# Improving Hunter combat experience

## Post 126217 by SantiagoGM20 — 2025-04-09T21:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126217#p126217 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm a relatively a **new in WoW**,  I just couldn't ignore that playing as a **Hunter** in this version of wow **feels really awkward**, specially when it comes to autoattacking from a distance.
Going straight to the point, i'd like to give some ideas on how to improve it, keeping in mind some of the retail mechanics and general stuff that just make sense to me to have as a distance dps class:
 **1.** Beeing able to **autoshoot while moving** would be great, in order to balance it, this could be a talent in the marksman talent branch.
 **2.**(The one that considere most important) **Remove deathzone on distance autoattack**, it really feels bad to be forced into meele being a distance/bow/shotgun class and there is already an option for meele playing style with the survival spell branch.
 **3.**Finally just adding the **possibility of replacing meele lvl1 autoattack with autoshot or any other spell for distance-autos**, this could be done by modifying the main hand slot to hold bows, crossbows or shotguns, and would make up for players to choose how they want to play based on where they equip weapons.

I think this would make hunter to feel more responsive and comfortable in both meele or distance playstyles.
