# The Ripper — Proc damage should be buffed to match epic quality and 1% drop rate

## Post 172757 by Worried — 2026-03-27T16:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172757#p172757 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Even with the proc bug fixed (see my bug report #21259), The Ripper's proc damage of 19-31 is dramatically weaker than every other epic proc dagger in the game. For a 1% drop rate epic from Stormwind Vault, the weapon severely underperforms its rarity tier.

Gut Ripper (Epic, Lvl 40): 95-121 physical damage proc — can crit
Shadowblade (Epic, Lvl 48): 110-141 shadow bolt proc
Alcor's Sunrazor (Epic, Lvl 58): 75-106 fire blast proc
Barman Shanker (BLUE, Lvl 50): 100 bleed damage over 30s
The Ripper (Epic, ~Lvl 60): 19-31 nature damage proc (1% Drop chance)

Gut Ripper at the same level range does 4-5x more proc damage. Even the Barman Shanker, a blue quality dungeon dagger, outperforms The Ripper's proc significantly.
The current Corrosive Poison version (Spell 3396) tries to compensate with:

Armor reduction by 60
 10 Nature damage every 5 sec for 30 sec (DoT)
 0.1 SP coefficient per tick (0.6 total over 30s)

But even with spellpower scaling, this falls far short. A player with 100 SP would add only ~60 bonus damage across 30 seconds still vastly weaker than competing epics that deal their damage instantly and can crit.

Suggestions (pick one or combine):

A) Keep the instant damage flavor (Fast Acting Poison) but increase the damage range to something like 75-110, putting it in line with Alcor's Sunrazor and Gut Ripper. This matches the tooltip's "fast acting" theme.

B) Keep the Corrosive Poison DoT concept but significantly increase the base tick damage, armor reduction, and/or SP coefficient to make the sustained damage competitive. For example: armor reduction of 200+, tick damage of 25-30 per tick, and a 0.3 SP coefficient per tick would make it a compelling spellpower-melee weapon with a unique niche.

C) Hybrid approach: Keep the instant 19-31 hit but add a meaningful DoT or armor debuff component on top, giving the weapon a unique identity as a "death by a thousand cuts" proc dagger for hybrid builds.

The Ripper drops from a custom dungeon boss at 1% — players farm Stormwind Vault for weeks or months to get it. The proc should reward that investment and feel epic. Right now it's weaker than most green "of the Monkey" daggers' raw stats alone.
