Hi,

With the addition of the crit reduction gear, general buffs to boomkin gearing, and the changes coming the the mace of unending life, I think a boomkin tank build is closer to playable than realised. With sacrifices to dps, getting a weird hybrid thing going on where you weave in MF and IS between weapon swings could be made.

The biggest thing lacking is ability to hold ago. In the same the way feral attack power in twow includes moonkin form, if the talent Feral Instinct also included moonkin form to add the 15% threat that it would go a long way into making it work.

I'm a big fan of small changes in the right direction than going to far and having to scale back so I think just this alone would get it to a playable although not optimal state which I think is fair, fun, and something inline with twow. :)

tl;dr: Please change the talent "Feral Instinct" to include moonkin form.
