# Make pvp gear non-unique

## Post 93855 by Kurtosmurtos1 — 2024-05-01T23:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93855#p93855 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings I would like to suggest removing the "unique" limitation on the pvp gear, the ranking and the blood ring gear. Just like how for example tier 1 and tier 2 pieces are not unique, meaning players can have more than one of each piece on them or in their banks. This allows players to make some fun enchanting combinations, one enchant on one tier 2 helm and another on the second, and so forth. This was just an example. The PVP gear should be no different. It would be a small QOL change that many players would probably never even notice. This would in no way worsen (the already horrible, but that is an issue for many more posts) pvp scene on turtle wow.
