Hello.

One suggestion I would like to make is to add NPCs to the Action House, Bank and a Mail Box in the Dwarven District in Stormwind, this would help reduce the flow of players in the central part of the city, with the high number of players in the center the game cannot handle, reducing performance to a poor state, this change would cause part of this movement to go to another location in the city.
