## Post 14458 by Afaslizo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-12-09T03:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14458#p14458 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After reading the OP's posting history I am not surprised by this kind of mentality. Seeing that you can already display your progress through both earned titles and earned gear I fail to see the need for achievements. You know yourself what you have gained and those are your personal experiences. It would lessen your personal journey if you are just in a grit with everyone else. Achievements measure game time over player enjoyment by rewarding having more free time spend on the game instead of your families, friends, other activities, work, eating and sleep. It is not healthy to subscribe to such a system while at the same time enslaving everyone to play the game in only a few ways and not the way you are able to. Some classes f.e. are not made to achieve certain damage requiredments for achievements.

Enjoy your personal journey where you experience your individual achievements. It will make for your own story and helps define your characters in ways an achievement system never will.
