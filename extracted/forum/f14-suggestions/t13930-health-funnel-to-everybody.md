I think it would cause some identity problems.
Sounds cool but:
 1. A warlock is a being of self-service. Warlock buffs are never direct. He gives you the stones and the pet gives the buff, they don't come from the lock directly. This change would give the most selfish class a selfless spell.
 2. Healer warlock would be something like tank shaman and tank rogue. Would divide people but those who like it would start to demand talent changes etc to make it viable. From a weird niche (and interesting utility) it would become a cornerstone of a build which is against the Vanilla+ spirit (just like we don't have warlock tanks or mage healers).
