# Priest Reskins

## Post 20192 by Hir3 — 2022-07-21T23:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20192#p20192 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If the dev time is there...

The basic idea here is that priests are your archetypal healers, and there exist priests in every society as lawful faith administrators. As an in game class, this is represented poorly, for example giving Darkspear trolls, described as having a hatred of all things human in old game manuals, casting Human-centered priest spells.

One would be to "racialize" priests as a unique class, opening them up to every possible race, with consideration that each race has a conception of faith and what a priest "is".

In this vein, priests should have reskins of their abilities and spell naming depending on the race they are of.

Examples:

Humans - Holy, Discipline, Exorcism
Dwarves - Holy, Discipline, Mysticism
Night Elves - Holy, Discipline, Moon
High Elves - Holy, Discipline, Sun

Orcs - Ascetic, Elder, Spirit
Forsaken - Devout, Discipline, Shadow
Tauren - Ascetic, Elder, Spirit
Trolls - Ascetic, Loa, Shadow

The exception is Gnomes and Goblins, who's religious practices are nonexistent or overly transactional and secular.

This theming would reinforce the priest similarly to warriors, rogues, and hunters: Representing an archetype while respecting race lore. Even if a name is in common, such as in the case of Night Elves and Humans for "Holy", the ability skins should be as unique as possible (Though a troll shadow and forsaken shadow can share, in a counter example)

The main change here is the shadow and holy specializations: Shadow Word Pain can be the arcane-lunar Moonfire in Night Elven priests, and Greater Heal can be a teal-grey skin, "Ancestral Blessing", in an Orc or Tauren priest.

Spells can be recolored or graphics altered where needed. RP and Lore can be respected. Each race that has a priest will now have two religious classes, with similar approach to how Paladins/Priests are offered in Humans and Dwarves. (Exception - Forsaken). This avoids most demands for weird classes such as Witchdoctor, for example. And is crucial to the these aforementioned RP elements.
