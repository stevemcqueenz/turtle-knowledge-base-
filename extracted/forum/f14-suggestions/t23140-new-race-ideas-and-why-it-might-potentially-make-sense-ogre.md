# New Race ideas and why it (might?) potentially make sense Ogre + Worgen

## Post 161592 by Redmoon — 2025-12-04T22:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161592#p161592 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The addition of the high elfs and goblins definitely gives turtle wow a more warcraft 2 feeling in contrast to a TBC feel and is great

Expansion past this is always a big risk and feels rather unlikely to begin with but if ever to happen I feel like ogre and worgen make the most sense

Ogre simply works given they were highly present in warcraft 2 and 3 and were already planned for classic but lacked a female model so we got the jungle trolls: problem is of course the female model

The alliance is much more of a grey area for 6th slot in contrast with suggestions usually ranging from furbolg / lost one draenie / worgen

None are fundamentally as solid as the ogre is for the horde but here's why I think Worgen would fit the slot the best

Worgen can't be shamans which lost one draenei and furbolg hand that does contribute to the classic spirit of paladins alliance shamans horde

All lack female models but I've a wotlk model for a female worgen concept that I think fits fairly well

The worgen and furbolg would almost invariably have to be druids creating a imbalance of 2 alliance druids : 1 horde that could probably be solved via having trolls be druids

Worgen and there gilneas storyline also represent a bridge between the eastern kingdoms alliance and the kalimdor night elfs in a way that is lacking for the furbolgs

Draenei in TBC were a method to get the alliance a more exotic race and I believe worgen could fill this role as well without the shaman problem

Perhaps instead of getting there own capitals both worgen and ogres could get city districts in darnassus and orgimmar respectfully and this would further serve to more of equalize orgimmar with stormwind in the present

This does recreate a shaman to paladin imbalance of 3 paladins to 4 shamans but a 2:3 already existed prior to the addition of goblin-high elf but doesn't add a shaman to the alliance of paladin to the horde
