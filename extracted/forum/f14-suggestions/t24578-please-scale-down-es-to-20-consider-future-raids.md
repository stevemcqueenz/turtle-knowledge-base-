# Please scale down ES to 20 (& consider future raids)

## Post 172943 by Phx — 2026-03-28T23:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172943#p172943 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Please scale down ES (and potentially other raids in the future) to 20. For starters, there's a reason 20-man has been the mythic standard for retail for over 10 years now. It's just balanced and feels really good to play.

It's also a complete logistical nightmare for raid sizes to increase dramatically on content releases. I'm speaking on behalf of Ambershire here, but filling rosters to 20-25 for ~6 months just for ES to soft-require an extra 10 players is making its mark on a lot of guilds. Not only do you have to out-recruit natural attrition/player loss but also you're pressured to recruit players at the same time every guild is trying to do so. It's clogging world chat and causing drama within the guilds who have to resort to hosting SR pugs, inviting players they might never see again just to clear ES.

I can't express my gratitude enough that I've been able to do MC and now BWL with 20 players as the base scaling. My entire guild loves how good the raids feel with this few of players. You can just add or subtract players (trials, callouts, etc.) willy-nilly and the raid feels fundamentally the same. So complaints aside thanks for that. But looking forward I think I speak for a lot of the community when I say ES should be the same as the other raids that are available.
