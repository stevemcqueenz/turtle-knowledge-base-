# DRUID FORM CUSTOMIZATION: Stances

## Post 171812 by Floopeez — 2026-03-22T17:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171812#p171812 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

So this might seem like an odd suggestion, as many of us are drawn to Druid because we love being able to play as a Cat / Bear / Chunky Space Chicken / Sentient Tree, but more customization is always great, and this is something I cannot to this day, blows my mind that its not in retail WoW.

 **"Stances"** in this regard means **"formless forms"**. Essentially, Astral Form form for Balance, but for every shapeshift. I think this would be an enormous hit, just to "change it up" from time to time or flex a terribad transmog. There are so many ways it could be done as well;

- I) You could simply allow the Druid to maintain their regular form when they transform (the low effort route)

II) The "astral" treatment, this could be a trail of leaves for Tree form, a slight recolor to give off a thick lair of "fur" effect for Bear / Cat form etc.

III) The "TRUE" stance experience. This could be as simple as replacing your weapons with a specific pair of Fist Weapons and Boots for Bear / Cat stance. No showing off your weapon and boots, you're still part aminal!

IV) The "Zul'aman boss" route. This, would be a TON of work. This would be a form that gave you a custom animal "head", as well as other features like tails, paws etc.
I'm partial to option III, but this is a custom private server, so anything, would be a win. Really, any form customization is welcome. Even a bunch of recolors would be neat. Purple and Green buzz light year bear, edgy black kitty form with red eyes, I'll take what I can get!
