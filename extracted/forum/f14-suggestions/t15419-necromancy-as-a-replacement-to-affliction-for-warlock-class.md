# Necromancy as a replacement to Affliction for warlock class

## Post 105461 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-10-31T04:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105461#p105461 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Blizzard's original warlock specs are more inclined to three different styles of the class as a demon summoner, which in fact is a system varying only on the extent of demon's participation in combat. In other words, the affliction warlock is merely a lesser demon summoner than demonological warlocks. In my opinion, that sounds like a controvercial identity of affliction warlocks regarding whether they are dark spell casters who can also summon demons or demon summoners who use weak demons (or a shadow priest who uses demons).

My suggestion is transmogging affliction to necromance by replacing demon minions with undead creatures. It doesn't feel odd that a warlock (dark caster) can control skeletons or ghouls. They can only operate in shadow, away from the public, and necromance has been extant in Warcraft prior to the founding of the Cult of the Damned, so the change would cause no contradiction to the basic plot.

Destruction spec, just like Affliction, could be revamped. Into a Old God-affiliated summoner maybe.

I went back to review again the themes associated with the Affliction specialization and interestingly your case is actually stronger than you might think. I would even say that Affliction IS Necromancy and there are many elements made by Blizzard themselves that suggest this is the original design; that lore-wise the death magic used by the Scourge is the same magic as the orcish warlocks of the Old Horde for both draw their power from the same dark well.

 ***Thematic Spells of Affliction***
 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If we look at the abilities of Affliction in our spellbook outwardly the impression it gives is that it's simply about curses as the name implies; you afflict curses on others. However what caught my attention was the other non-curse abilities that revolves around manipulation of life energies;
- Life Tap

- Drain Soul

- Drain Life

- Drain Mana

- Death Coil
Within the Affliction talent tree we find two more abilities of the same theme.
- Siphon Life

- Dark Pact
Most notable is Death Coil, an ability of the Death Knights of both Warcraft 2 and 3. From Warcraft 1 to Warcraft 3 the abilities of the [Necrolytes](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Necrolyte_%28Warcraft_I%29), [Death Knights](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Knight_%28Warcraft_II%29), [the other Death Knights](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Knight_%28Warcraft_III%29) and [Liches](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Lich_%28Warcraft_III%29) all carry themes of Death in their spell descriptions, from *raising the dead* to *summoning great cold winds of the underworld* and *drawing upon negative energies to cast fields of decay and either sacrificing minions or empowering them*.

All this is evidence of continuity, made stronger by their histories; the first Death Knights are the reanimated Necrolytes, the Liches are the reborn warlocks that followed the shaman-turned-warlock Ner'zhul and Scourge Death Knights are servants granted power by Ner'zhul who himself was remade into an unwilling servant of the Legion by the Legion.

Furthermore the nature of curses and other spell and talent descriptions can be reinterpreted to fit this narrative of Death easily. After all Death itself can be described as a curse with a **Grim Reach**; over time you will grow **Weak**, **Exhausted**, your body in increasing **Agony** as you live in **Fear** and **Terror** of that slow, impending **Doom**

With this connection in mind, the Affliction spec should have strong aesthetic themes towards death, decay, unholiness and chilling winds.

 ***Regarding Demonology and Destruction***
 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Since we're on the subject I might as well put out my thoughts on the other two specs. As for Demonology and Destruction I find them very straightforward; the former is about the summoning of demons through the act of soul-trading, the latter is about the violent destruction of civilization - a cataclysmic ruin that brings all to hell. I believe these themes are great and complementary with inevitable demise (Death) as they as a whole solidify the Warlock class' core theme of Entropy, the very thing the demons of the Burning Legion symbolize.

The Old Gods, while similar in goals are not of the same themes. Rather they are much more closely related to the themes of Primordial Chaos and dark arcane mysteries as originally presented in Classic prior to the oversimplifying retcon of being solely creatures of the Void.

Where the demons are about the process of both order and life coming to a complete end, the Old Ones are about the state before ordered life existed. Perhaps the creation of a new class is better suited for this archetype or be used to reinvent the Mage if seen fit or necessary, though I doubt the latter.

Instead, I would like to put forth the idea that a summoner class need not always have a creature following them. With two specs already having summonable pets as a core part of their playstyle, why not consider a spec that uses demons in a different manner?

 ***Reinterpretation - Demonology***
 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rather than simply summoning demons with soul shards to do your bidding, the master demonologist takes it a step further; making a pact with a demon to be their host body, summoning and binding their souls to yours before dominating it into submission by willpower and magical binds in order to attain their abilities. In other words, a Demon-Hunter.

I envision this Demonology playstyle to be a melee one, empowering attacks to consume mana/buffs and having Metamorphosis as a powerful ultimate ability. Defensive cooldowns can include Evasion and AoE being Immolation. To make it interesting, your utitly and other non-rotation abilities are dependent on what demon you have summoned within you. For example if you make a pact with a succubus you have a powerful mass-seduction ability for crowd-control. To change the demon within, we can have a RPG mechanic where you must perform the swap at a ritual circle or consume a prepared demonic soulstone when in the middle of combat.

 ***Reinterpretation - Destruction***
 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same as before but this time they get to summon demonic masses now as part of their rotation, the Warlock's hellfire abilities' main purpose is to weaken, damage over time and burn away enemy defensives to leave them vulnerable to their minions. They can have access to powerful cooldowns such as 'Abolish Magic' which can remove absorption effects for example, targets that take damage from your hellfire brands recently take more damage from minion attacks for a short while with 'Summon Infernal' being the capstone ability.

 ***Summary***
 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Affliction] Necromancers
 *Wielding necrotic energies and the cold winds of hell, these unholy defilers raise the dead to do battle once more.*
- *Spellcasting DPS playstyle revolving around drains, debuffs, slows, AoE and a permanent undead pet of choice*
[Demonology] Demonic Warriors
 *Making pacts with demons, these flame-wreathed warriors charge into the fray with unnatural speed and strength.*
- *Melee DPS playstyle revolving around mobility, cleaves, utility, burst damage, retaliation and Demonic Pact system*
[Destruction] Hellcallers
 *Drawing upon the Twisting Nether, these summoners call forth a burning horde of destruction and hellfire.*
- *Spellcasting DPS playstyle revolving around DoTs, focused attacks, dispels and multiple temporary summonable pets*
