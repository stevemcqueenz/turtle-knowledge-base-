# Please Give Melee Hunters a Party Buff

## Post 128988 by Piccolo1833 — 2025-04-29T14:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128988#p128988 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have been playing a melee hunter for a couple months now and from what I've experienced, they are the probably the specialization that needs some kind of party buff the most. Right now the only two classes/specs that don't bring a party buff are rogues and melee hunters, and people would rather bring a warrior or rogue to their raids because they do more damage or they don't have to worry about giving them different blessings like SV hunters. You can argue that they bring the ability to pull or tranquilizing shot, but it's really annoying for them to do so compared to a ranged hunter. I feel like SV hunters are having the most difficult time to find a raid spot right now. At least they can do good damage, if it wasn't for that, I don't think anyone would want to ever bring one to a raid.
