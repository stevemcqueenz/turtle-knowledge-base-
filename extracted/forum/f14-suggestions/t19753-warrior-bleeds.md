# Warrior Bleeds

## Post 134593 by Melfrato1 — 2025-06-07T03:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134593#p134593 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings Fellow Adventurers!

I would like to make a suggestion for Warriors to be specific to Bleed damage.

Currently I don't feel like Rend is Particularly worth to keep up, when I do its does around 1-2% of my dps and I have to switch stances which sometimes can cost rage to do, so its just not worth it for the little damage it does, even with scaling.
Compared to other DoT's the Damage can even be called pitiful.

I fully realize that most suggestion for changes for warriors are perceived as unneeded because "warrior op" which I can accept. I suggest class fantasy based on more Bleed damage, at least for Arms warrior.

There are multiple ways I can imagine doing that:

Change the Rend AP scaling to something like 10-20% (this will probably buff fury as well which could be a problem)

Bake Bleed damage increases into one of the Talents Deep into the Arms tree (Bleed dmg increased by X%)

Make Rend besides the scaling deal %weapondmg on top of scaling (More value from 2h)

 ----

Besides these I find it frustrating that Deep wounds "resets" if you get lucky with crits multiple Times, there I would love to see a somewhat "stacking" mechanic similar to that of the Fire Mages Ignite. This could also be made into a talent or just baseline.

If those changes would make the warrior "broken" or smth, offset it with a baseline reduction of Auto Hit dmg, I find it more interesting to press buttons instead of seeing that 50% of my dmg comes from "auto attack".

These changes would incentivise Stance Switching, and Skill expression in my mind because you have to be mindful not to dump your Rage on a switch.

Thanks for reading!
