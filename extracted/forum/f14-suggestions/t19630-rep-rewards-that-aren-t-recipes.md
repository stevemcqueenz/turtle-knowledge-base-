PvP reps all give gear, same as Gelkis, Timbermaw, Magram, Dalaran, Argent Dawn via token turn ins, Bloodsail, Wardens of Time, and more. The suggestions I see getting taken seriously and sometimes (rarely) implemented into the game are ones where the OP has some specific things they want to get changed and makes a proposal. If you have ways you want a certain rep or set of reps to showcase more items, perfect time to give some ideas.
