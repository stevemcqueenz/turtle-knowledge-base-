# Suggestion: Fix Holy penalty in Blackwing Lair

## Post 19746 by Velite (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-07-06T19:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19746#p19746 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When AQ40 was released and Blizzard realized they wanted to humor the idea of ret paladins in raids, they realized that when they created the twin emperors fight paladins would not be able to deal damage to the melee emperor because he is immune to all spells, while the spell emperor is immune to physical damage. Ret paladins do most of their damage as spell damage in melee. Accordingly, Blizzard made the fight such that the Melee emperor would be immune to all spells except holy spells to allow ret paladins to deal damage.

If we go back to the previous raid content, Blizzard made no such exception when they implemented chromatic creatures like <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=12461>, <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=12460> and <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=14020>. These 3 mobs have the unique ability which gives them 75% spell damage reduction <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23646>, but they will be given a random weakness from the 5 schools (frost fire shadow nature arcane) which makes them take more damage by 1100% <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=22278>. However if you notice, Holy damage is not covered. This means that they will always take -75% Holy damage and paladins and priests are not effective against them. This is especially an issue for chromaggus where a paladin is only able to deal threat by casting blessings. Since we want to move away from using blessings as threat, I fear that without adjusting these mobs you will weaken paladins in Blackwing Lair. I propose that the spell effect include an increase of 75% for Holy Damage so that they only take normal Holy damage always instead of the -75%.

This is within the spirit of vanilla, because Blizzard was willing to make an exception in AQ40 after they changed their design philosophy to recognize ret paladin as a raid choice. As turtle is doing the same it would only make sense to follow up on this oversight.
