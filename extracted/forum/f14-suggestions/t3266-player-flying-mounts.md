# Player Flying Mounts

## Post 19940 by Papachino — 2022-07-12T16:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19940#p19940 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Now before any of you reading this immediately dismiss the idea of the possible addition of flying mounts, take a moment to listen to my proposal. Flying mounts, a majority of the World of Warcraft Vanilla fanbase heavily despises this major feature that was added in The Burning Crusade Expansion Pack. So I have come up with a possible and potentially viable solution for Vanilla flying mounts.

 1. For the player to be able to use a flying mount, they have to first purchase a Flight License. This can be purchased in either Stormwind, Booty Bay, or Orgrimmar from a new NPC called a Flight Captain for roughly the same price as the riding skill, being 20 Gold. This will allow the player to fly in any zone they wish to fly in.

 2. Insitute a player flight height limit. Whatever this height would be is entirely up to what the Turtle WoW Development Team can settle on.

 3. Flight can only be used outside of major cities. This would mean you would be unable to fly in a major faction city the same as how riding mounts inside of Booty Bay is not allowed.
