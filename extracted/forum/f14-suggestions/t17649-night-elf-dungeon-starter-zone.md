# Night Elf Dungeon (Starter zone)

## Post 120425 by Oweantv — 2025-02-19T18:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120425#p120425 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A starter area dungeon similar to ragefire chasm but for night elfs in Teldrassil lvl 10-15, with furbolgs and moss creatures, spiders and spirits etc. Suggesting it be located either on the empty southern hill over Lake Al'ameth or in the southwest portion of Gnarlpine Hold. It has some ideas to explore the Nightmare and its corruption, with few extra quests that will tie in nicely with already set quests in area.
