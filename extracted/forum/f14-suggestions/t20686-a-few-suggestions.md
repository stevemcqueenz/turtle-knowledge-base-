# A few suggestions

## Post 143230 by Dogfight — 2025-08-05T11:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143230#p143230 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Consumables**
Currently we are struggling in raids because we hit buff cap. Too many consumables, some of them niche, will put an expectancy on people to go out there and farm all of them to min/max. This is tedious and may exclude some players from raiding because it is time consuming and expensive. I’d imagine it would also be easier to balance classes with fewer consumables that can be used in raids. Here are some suggestions:

- Reduce amount of consumables that stack

- Don’t let SP consumables stack for example. Same for strength/AP consumes. Allow only one active at a time and people can prioritize themselves what they want to use.

- Don’t let Spirit of Zanza stack with Flask of the Titans. Reduce rep requirement to get Zanzas and thus make it a “cheap” titans as an alternative.

- Make a Chronoboon for consumables. Having 1hr left on a flask after a raid and knowing that it will just go to waste unless I log out is not a good feeling to have. This encourages raid logging to not waste flask time and reduces social interaction. Make a chronoboon that stores all consumes that normally persist through death.
 **General**
- With more and more raids coming out, the raiding schedule of guilds become very busy. Even with efficient clearing times, it will be unsustainable to do all raids on a weekly basis. At the top of the pyramid, guilds that have cleared all content lose interest in lower tier raids, but still have to do them to gear up new recruits to cover the turnover. This is a challenge for Twow to do something about. Raid scaling so they can be completed with fewer people is a must. More catch-up raids to skip some content may be helpful too. I don’t have the answer, but this is something that needs to be looked at.

- Add some head and leg enchants to factions/reputations connected with high level dungeons. Enchants that may rival ZG enchants. ZG enchants feel like a chore every time you acquire a new head or leg item. Make alternatives for tanking, healing, dps, resistances etc from more factions and make them require a token that drops from bosses in dungeons connected with that faction. That will keep lvl 55-60 content still alive and replayable because players would often come back to get their enchants.

- In general add more level 60 content. Reputation rewards, questlines etc to develop characters that have completed most of the game. Currently it feels like most of the focus is on levelling content. That is all well and good, but there are also people quitting Twow because they have nothing more to do that motivates them.
