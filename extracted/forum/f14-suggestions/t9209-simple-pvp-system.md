# Simple PvP system

## Post 59837 by Uldra — 2023-08-30T00:06:00Z

In the spirit of Turtle WoW were you don't rush, takes things slow, it would be nice if the pvp system would receive a update. - It's just a bad system inside an amazing game.

Side note: Blizzard just released a new honor system, definitely worth taking a deeper look at it.

At the beginning of 2023, BR 1 was around 400k
Currently the BR 1 honor cap is set to 950k for both factions.

 - it has gone out of hand!

Most people within the PvP community agree that with the current system R14 grind is just an afk fiesta of burned out players trying to grind their sanity away.

I asked  20 top Rankers, they all said they easily spend over 60-100 hours inside BGs depending how the games go and what the bonus days are grinding between 6-10k honor per hour, 8k on average.

~ 80 hours / week.

Let's not balance the game around 1% of the player base that might get an "unfair" advantage by living inside bgs.
Every ranker I asked agreed that they would probably farm 200 - 500k / week if this ranking system would be implemented.

Rank 14 would still take 375 hours, with the 8k average honor gain.
Probably still by far the longest grind in classic WoW ....
but at your own pace!
