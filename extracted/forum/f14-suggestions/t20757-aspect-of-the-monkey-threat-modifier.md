I'd suggest adding a threat modifier to aspect of the monkey.
At PVE, melee uses wolf anyway.
At PVP, threat doesn't matter.

It would enable a niche build for hunters (or just those guys who really want to run a dungeon and can't find a tank) with 0 drawbacks.

Design-wise fits. You want the mob to focus on you and you raise your dodge at the same time.
Flavor-wise passes, too. Monkey can be pretty annoying, perfect for distraction.
