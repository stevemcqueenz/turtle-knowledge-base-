# Changing the pet stance Aggressive to Assist from Cata

## Post 64567 by Gravint — 2023-09-15T21:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64567#p64567 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It is VERY annoying to manually click the attack option for your pet whenever you play. You could make a macro but a ton of people don't know how to do that. You could also press the key bind for it, but there are people who didn't know that there was a key bind for it. I have been playing WoW for years and I only found that out a few minutes ago. I would just put the pet attack button on my side bar, but you can't move it from  the pet bar. A macro would solve that, but once again there are people who don't know how to make a macro. The Aggressive stance also has no good uses that I have ever heard of. The only thing it does is make your life harder by pulling random mobs. What about HC players who accidentally had the Aggressive stance on? It may pull a random mob and kill the player. I don't see one good reason to keep it in the game. The Assist stance however, it makes your pet attack what you want when you want. None of that "pull everything within the zone" crap that Aggressive does. So I am begging you, please for the love of god swap it out for Assist for every Warlock and Hunter.
