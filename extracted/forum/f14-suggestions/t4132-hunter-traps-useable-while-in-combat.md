# Hunter Traps - Useable while in combat

## Post 24797 by Brokehoof — 2022-09-10T21:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24797#p24797 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While I love the trap line for hunters, I have to say their implementation in Vanilla isn't the most useful. I rather like how they are currently on Live, where they can both be used during combat and also thrown at a distance for near immediate triggering.

I would love to see an in-combat useable trap system made available on Turtle. Either through talents in the survival tree or as a new kind of default. If you can throw them too, that'd be great!

Maybe the ideal setup is making the default trap useable in combat and the talent tree option could be to make them throwable. How awesome would that be?
