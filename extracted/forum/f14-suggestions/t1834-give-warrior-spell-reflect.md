[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/6H52h8c/Screenshot.jpg)](https://ibb.co/2vBw2S0)

I do not agree that warriors are weak in pvp.
The thing is that in vanilla wow, at least, every class has a counterclass. In other words, there are classes that are harder to beat than others for warriors. I can tell you straight away that paladins struggle tremendously against shamans and priests.
Other than that, gear difference is always a factor. If your enemies are packed in t2-3 level gear, then you will have no chance in preraid BiS.
Finally, personal skills in pvp can make a big difference too. Some people invest a lot of time to make macros, learn professions (like engeneering) and make consumables for battlegrounds. That can often turn the tides in their favor.

Having said that, maybe a spell reflect for warriors would be a good idea as a custom turtle ability, with certain limitations. for example, needing shield to use it and only available in defensive stance. Maybe it will only have a 25% chance of reflecting a spell and other 75% you just resist spell damage. Add a several minute cooldown on it and it could be a thing for warrior tanks. Not to say that warrior tanks aren't doing great already though.
