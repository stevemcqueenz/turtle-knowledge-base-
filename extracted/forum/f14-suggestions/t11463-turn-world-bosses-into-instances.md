# Turn World Bosses Into Instances

## Post 75901 by Ayesha (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-04T17:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75901#p75901 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My suggestion is to allow more players experience world boss content by turning world bosses into Onyxia or Emerald Sanctum type of Instances.

Right now there is one active coalition that does World Bosses. You can have access to world bosses if you agree to their loot rules. This sounds like a monopoly.

World bosses were designed for PVP environment, not PVE. There is no PVP for world bosses, the only competition is being able to recruit enough players who will do this content, and older guilds have an advantage in this regard.

My suggestion is to create an instance with random rotating world bosses like the ZG Edge of Madness event to allow more guilds from different time zones as well as PUGs enjoy this content and set their own loot rules. There will be more choice in terms of what community, leadership and loot rules you want to see when doing this content.
