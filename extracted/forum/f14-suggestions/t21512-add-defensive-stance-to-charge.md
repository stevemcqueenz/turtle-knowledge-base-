# Add Defensive Stance to Charge

## Post 149068 by Eyeburn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-29T05:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149068#p149068 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Charge being restricted to battle stance kneecaps dungeon tanking, and for no real reason.  I can understand leaving Tactical Mastery as a 5 point talent for PvP build reasons, even if it should probably be baseline or require less talent points.  What doesn't make sense is keeping charge as a battle stance only skill, requiring dungeon tanking warriors to sink points into Tactical Mastery just to function properly in rapid pull content, like a dungeon.  It blows hard ass.

Turtle WoW is cognizant enough to put defensive stance onto tclap, because it purely improves dungeon tanking and allows warrior tanks to manage their own debuffs without affecting any other aspects of PvE or PvP.  Letting warriors charge in defensive stance accomplishes the exact same thing.  It vastly improves dungeon tanking, particularly while leveling, and it doesn't have any wider implications for PvE or PvP beyond that.
