 **Add Rona Greenteeth to the Darkmoon Faire**

[![Image](http://dl7.glitter-graphics.net/pub/727/727097kjhd9gidla.gif)](https://www.glitter-graphics.com)

Rona Greenteeth <https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Rona_Greenteeth> is a Forsaken NPC who was added to the Darkmoon Faire during the Cataclysm expansion. However, the wares she sells do not need to be excluded from Vanilla + content for they fit perfectly well within the brutal lore of the Warcraft games.

Rona sells food items which are hinted to be harvested from fallen adventurers. There is a very good joke to be made here about HC players with her dialogue text, which could be a funny tie in too. She could cycle alongside the Faire, placed in the food booth for things to much on. I can see Scourge, Undead, and Troll roleplayers interested in her wares which include…

• [Forsaken Foie Gras]
 *"Why yes, there ARE ducks and geese in Tirisfal, sweetling."*

• [Green Ham & Eggs]
 *"From those rough, brutish Durotar swine, hee hee! And gull eggs."*

• [Mulgore Meat Pie]
 *"THERE'S the beef, sweetling. Hee!"*

• [Troll Tartare]
 *"Troll-STYLE tartare, that is, my dear. Hee-hee!"*

• [Blackstone Gumbo] (Suggested Addition for Goblins)
 *”Blackstone Isle is known for savory characters and their… I mean, its flavors, sweetling.”*

• [Beer Basted Short Ribs]
 *"BEARD-basted...? I mean BEER-basted, hee-hee."*

• [Stormwind Surprise]
 *"Hee! Hee hee hee! Heee-he- *hack* *cough* Ahem. Hee!"*

• [Teldrassil Tenderloin]
 *"Of COURSE it's venison, sweetling. What ELSE would it be?!"*

• [Gnomeregan Gnuggets]
 *"Breaded, bite-sized, and mechanically inclined. I mean, processed!"*

• [Silvermoon Steak]
 *"Ah, the cattle of Eversong are ESPECIALLY lean and tender!"*

Each could be available at 5 for 1 ticket, and provide a level 50-60 food buff related to the racial stats or abilities of each group. **You are what you eat, after all.**
