Hello, I'll jump straight into it.

World buffs have been removed near the start of 2023 and we are halfway through the year, let's see how many guilds are being compensated by fully utilizing shaman + pala stacking today, using the latest log I could find from them.

I'll be mentioning 3 important distinctions about the shamans/paladins: 1. Number of shamans in the raid, whether that fits all groups that could benefit from a shaman, 2. Roles of shamans/paladins. (if I know Whether the paladins/shamans are alts or freshly brought in just for their buffs, ill mention).

To reach a Clear for the compensation, the Paladins need to provide at least 3(ideally 4) blessings and the shamans need to buff every physical damage group in the raid.

I couldn't find any other guilds that cleared naxx recently on Legacyplayers (most likely due to the recent bug)

 **What's painfully obvious is that while most guilds did not struggle to find all the paladins that cover raid buffs, shamans are covered in 1 of these guilds, and that's NOT INCLUDING CASTERS compensations, as the there are but 2 totems that casters can benefit from, so in a shaman starved server they cannot get either (Mana spring, Tranquil Air)**

It's clear that Shamans aren't fun to play, most guilds run between 2-4, where ideally you'd want 5-6 for all physical DPS groups, that's 6 if you're including giving Elemental shamans a caster group for Atiesh/Boomkin/SP aura.

 **What can we do?** My suggestion is: Use
[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/63SjRB8NuWA?start=4056]
Wallcraft's code which enabled him to change party wide buffs (on Vmangos) to be all nearby players for  **not all, just most the important totems for the sake of server wide raid balancing** That'd include: Strength of the Earth totem, Windfury Totem, Tremor Totem, Grace of Air totem.

Things that shouldn't be made raidwide to encourage class stacking: Mana spring, Mana tide, Poison Cleansing, Nature/Fire/Frost Res, Tranquil Totem, Flametongue totem, Stoneskin Totem, Healing tide totem.

New totem: Skyfury Totem (Party wide) that gives spell crit based on 1/2 of the Shaman's spell hit chance, 31 point in Elemental, next to Elemental Mastery. (So elemental shamans can be placed in caster groups, and theres a reason to bring an elemental shaman to raiding without just having Resto shamans respec)

Reasoning: I'm not a player that won't get a shaman in raids. I'm not someone that has to ask to be swapped to a shaman's group as a warrior/rogue, my performance makes me not have to worry about shamans.
My reasoning is entirely based on seeing new players across multiple guilds be judged "unworthy" to receive a shaman in raids, never having an opportunity to "go all out" and always doomed to be under "the best" DPS. This is unnecessary toxicity in raiding that has no right to exist on a casual private server. I ask for this change not for myself but the hundreds of "low dps" warriors/rogues/rets that are on the server being judged before getting a chance to prove themselves.
