# New Class discussion: Necromancer; Monk; Bard?

## Post 116236 by Rayken (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-11T15:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116236#p116236 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Necromancer
This class would be a good addition to the game as a base class just in general and I think would be one of the most lore appropriate classes.

They would obviously be more covert than even Warlocks, in lore, but their ability to spread plague/raise undead minions AGAINST the scourge would be a pretty cool plot point. Not unlike what the Forsaken did.

A core mechanic could be;
Plague
Plague has a percent chance to spread target to target; damage is increased against plagued targets.
Plagued enemies that die can rise as skeletons or zombies under the control of the Necromancer (limit 1 per maybe 10 or 20 levels)

And I think maybe a Gargoyle or Abomination "tank" pet.

A lot of mechanics can be taken from both Frost Mage, Warlock, and the unused Death Knight. No Death Knights in TWoW; but Necromancers could be a cool way to bring that flavor and many of those spells.

Monk
This class obviously already exists in WoW, but I think it would need to be reworked. The way Blizzard introduced Monk was a mistake, it was basically a joke/drunken brawler class. They butchered the lore to make Pandaren Brewmasters work. Monks would actually be much more stoic and focused, they should be more of a mixture of Paladin and Shaman (in tune with the natural harmony of elements). I know that sounds weird but it's closer to a true WoW Monk.

Bard
This is the one TWoW could have the most fun with and could introduce a new slot; for instruments. I can think of Bards as using Int Leather or Int Mail/Int Plate like Paladin and strumming a lute in battle.

Mix of Paladin/Hunter/Rogue, using Poisons, with "Aspects/Auras" being songs, etc. They could use their music to do any number of things.
