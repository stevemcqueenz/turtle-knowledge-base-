# WC3-style flying mobs

## Post 58544 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-08-23T06:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58544#p58544 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In warcraft strategy there were a lot of flying units who can't be hit in melee by foot soldiers. For example Flying Machine, Gargoyles, Dragons, Hippogryphs, Wind Riders, Gryphons, Harpies.

WoW has a lot of mobs like this in the world: [Young Chimaera](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4032), [Chillwind Chimaera](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=7448), a lot of harpies all over the world, owls,
vultures, gryphons.
But all those "flying" mobs fly in melee distance just above ground. I think it would be much more immersive to have them flying 10 feet above ground. So you couldn't charge to them and attack in melee.
They should be killable with bow, ranged spells or some device (probably provided with quest) to bring them down to ground. They should also probably fall down if stunned by HoJ.

Now behavior like this can be found in Durotar, harpy den. [Dustwind Storm Witch](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3118) often positioned in inaccessible places and can attack with ranged spell down to you.
Though this harpy attacks you in melee when mana ends. I think it should fly above player schreeching something until mana regenerates for other Lightning bolt.

Flying beasts without ranged spells could throw stones on players head. Chimaeras could breathe acid or whatever it has.
