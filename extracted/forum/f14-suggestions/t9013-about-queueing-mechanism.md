# About queueing mechanism

## Post 58357 by Edgarfam (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-22T01:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58357#p58357 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After the latest update of queuing mechanism, my account started to queue yesterday. While waiting I observed the queue and have some speculation and suggestions.

My guess is that now the queuing mechanism has arranged every account into a priority sequence by how veteran the account is(we don't exactly know the algorithm determining it, factors could be register date, character lever, etc.). When the server is full, everyone trying to login are automatically placed in queue according to their priority sequence instead of queue time sequence.

This could explain:
 1. When different accounts start to queue at the same time, their queue number could very a lot.
 2. When an account has a low priority, his queue number could be increasing because there are more people(has higher priority) joining the queue than people leaving the game.

So, if my guess is true, I think this a good mechanism with the current overcrowded problem, and I'm absolutely supporting it.

However, my suggestion is, you have to pay attention to the possibility of players playing against it.

Because if players with low priority realized they can never queue into the game, their only way to play in peak time(could be their only time to play) is never logging off. **As far as I know, there are already many players keeping their characters online all day without actually playing. This could a waste of resource and make the situation worse.**

I think you should be monitoring this and counter it. When this kind of behavior is confirmed, I suggest to: 1. downgrade their priority sequence. 2. ban the account if it's priority sequence is already low.

Thank for looking into it.
