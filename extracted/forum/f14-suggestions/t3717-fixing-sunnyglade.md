# Fixing Sunnyglade

## Post 22394 by Ugoboom (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-08-15T05:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22394#p22394 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Aighty, so the server has maybe had Sunnyglade Valley pop maybe what, five to ten times in the last half a year? Every time we enter, everyone for 3 minutes goes "Whoa cool", but then immediately after get confused and frustrated over what in the world we're supposed to be doing.

We enter the bg, and there's hamlets and mines and towers, which basically seem to do nothing? I know there's some chests with sparks, but we had no clue if they respawn, and if they do then when. Maybe stagger the placements of the spark chests, and put the timers on the map? Like we'd see on the southwest, a hamlet chest is up in 10 seconds, and on the east mine we'd see a chest spawning in 30 seconds. This way, teams can know where on the map to congregate, call enemy movements, and good group battles can form over the hamlets and mines over the impending chest spawns. It would play out like a variation of AB, but with only a few flags at a time in such a way that travel time between spawns is juuust not enough for 1 group of players to grab them all, so teams are encouraged to make groups of 3-5 or so.

The towers??? I don't know, they just seem completely pointless as they are right now. Maybe have them generate sparks like AB bases would, but only a significant amount if both are captured. Also, with them being right next to the enemy graveyard and leaders, perhaps just during the final battle, add a substantial dps boost to the team that has capped their enemy's tower, so that offense players will want to capture that forward position and defend it alongside the team killing the boss. Losing it would just mean a dps down, so losing it sucks but it's not totally game over if this position is lost.

The npcs everywhere do absolutely nothing as well, except stick onto you and annoy you for accidentally AOEing them. Perhaps reward a spark each for killing them? But its also bizarre when killing them, that your friendly NPC does 0 damage to help you. they don't tank at all. Perhaps when you start attacking the enemy NPC, the friendly one starts doing actual damage, and has a preset threat so that lesser geared players could let the NPC tank the mob instead of themselves.

And for the end bosses, do not punish the lower spark team THAT much. If team A gets 100 sparks and team B gets barely any, their target boss should have at most 2x more health as punishment, still putting them at a significant disadvantage but still give a fighting chance, instead of us seeing 10x more health and dying of laughter at how we are completely screwed and at that part people defending our own boss becomes basically trolling.

These are my suggestions on how I think this game was meant to be played. Perhaps i missed a few things in the design cause yeah most of us were completely baffled by this battleground, and not in the good way.

Oh and don't forget, put in a BG weekend and make sure the honor rewards beat if not exceed WSG and AB when played ideally. And nerf AV honor or this bg will never be queued again.
