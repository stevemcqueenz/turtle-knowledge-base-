# PVP gear for healers!

## Post 160278 by Moomoobro — 2025-11-21T21:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160278#p160278 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Turtle has already introduced spec-based variants of Tier 1, 2 and 3 PvE gear so that each class can obtain items that fit their intended role (e.g. Holy/Prot/Ret for Paladins). It feels like a missed opportunity that similar spec-based variants for PvP gear do not exist.

A Holy Paladin who commits to the long grind for epic PvP gear should be able to obtain plate that supports a healing playstyle through healing power and spell crit, etc.

In a similar way, there should be more Rank 14 weapon options to accomocate such a change. E.g., a shield with healing power

Introducing such additional Rank 14 items together with role-specific PvP armour would make the entire PvP journey far more appealing. I believe this would attract more people into battlegrounds which would make matches more enjoyable for everyone. Healthy participation always improves match balance and helps the whole system feel more dynamic.

Why this would be beneficial
• It brings PvP rewards in line with the existing philosophy used for PvE gear.
• It rewards healers and other specs for the significant time and effort required to push high PvP ranks.
• It strengthens battleground balance by supporting all roles properly.
• It creates clear motivation for more players to queue as healers which improves gameplay for everyone.
