# Improve Rend & Class Sub-Specialization=

## Post 5108 by Snigery (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-02-25T19:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5108#p5108 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, new player to the server here. I'd like to start off with a disclaimer stating that I do not have any endgame experience in classic WoW and only recently had I gotten my first warrior to level 80 on another Blizzlike WotLK server. I was dissatisfied with feeling like I missed much of the game and rushed to get there and I found the rotation at lvl 80 (when it comes to min/maxing) overwhelming. Regardless, I love the feeling of the Arms warrior in that expansion; That I was a skilled swordsman manuevering (overpowering) around the enemy striking vital points compounded by prolonged bleed effects.

Coming to classic, the slower pace and increased challenge is a refreshing joy, yet I find myself missing the playstyle I was used to with [Rend (Rank 1)](https://classicdb.ch/?spell=772) lasting 6 seconds less, doing effectively half the damage, and lacking weapon swing modifiers of the [Rend (Rank 1)](https://wotlkdb.com/?spell=772) in WotLK.

Anyway, I believe Blizzard's intentions in later expansions improved in some significant areas in providing classes a unique feel; Rather than largely changing the game, I think it would be neat if classes had more options to tweak and improve their playstyle in more noticeable ways, yet as to not affect the balance of gameplay.

In my case, I would find it very interesting if Rend DoT would scale the longer its' effect is prolonged without interruption ([Trauma](https://wotlkdb.com/?spell=46855#)), perhaps with another secondary effect such as increasing other melee damage slightly ([Blood Frenzy](https://wotlkdb.com/?spell=29859)). I think this would harmoniously incorporate changes in later expansions into a single concise mechanic which provides more utility.

To maintain the balance, the goal would not be to incorporate all the changes of later expansions into a single spec, but provide them as sub-specs of a spec, and limit it to one sub-spec at a time; Furthermore the idea is not to bring special abilities back (Bladestorm, Titans Grip, etc) but allowing for the playstyle and simulated experience.

I hope this doesn't come across as hoping to only get my way, this in response to the dread I feel as I read guides stating largely that certain playstyles are just not viable and the struggle of lack-luster leveling playstyle; I found it interesting that such suggestions are considered, and even the existing additions to the game are quite impressive. So I provide this suggestion in the hope more will share their ideas.

To muck with the talents is a big can of worms. Warrior is fine. All of this requires a custom patch anyway.

Risky = Muck with working class specializations (Fury / Arms / Protection Warriors, Frost / Fire Mages etc.)
Moderate = Muck with under performing class specializations (e.g. arcane mages, some druid builds, some priest builds etc.)
'Safe' = Muck with broken class specializations (e.g. Retribution Paladin, Melee Survival Hunter, Tank rogue / shaman etc.)

I would start with adding talents in broken class specialization that have meme / joke status in raids like melee / survival hunter, retribution paladin [5 point talent below Pursuit of Justice to make holy strike instant, -0.2 seconds per point] etc.)
