# Replace Mage's Oranges With Ice Cream

## Post 122679 by BlueBionicle — 2025-03-16T09:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122679#p122679 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

That's it, just replace the mage's oranges with ice cream. Let mages summon a big bowl of ice cream and let other players recieve delicious frosty treats instead of boring oranges. Why oranges in the first place, do all mages have an affinity to gardening or some sort of secret citrus obsession? Scared of getting scurvy? Ice cream is so much better lore-wise, plus there is a shortage of candy-related items in game, so it would be a great addition to the consumables catalog. Just imagine players asking not for old boring oranges "oRanGes, pls!1", but instead for frosty delicious fruity ice cream "Oh noble mage, please let us taste some of that marvellous ice cream!". So much better, so much more flavour.
