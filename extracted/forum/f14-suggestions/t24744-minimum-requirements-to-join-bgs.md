 1. Just because it’s large‑scale and objective‑heavy doesn’t make it ‘not real.’ It’s part of WoW’s PvP DNA.

 2. Forcing players to queue WSG and AB in equal numbers would be a disaster. WSG has always been the least‑popular BG, and forcing people into content they don’t enjoy doesn’t improve PvP, it just increases AFKs and resentment. If a battleground needs mandatory participation to stay alive, that says more about the battleground than the players.

 3. Making movement exploits a requirement to queue? That’s backwards. Jumps and wall‑tech are fun tricks but, you’re basically suggesting that engine quirks from 20 years ago should gatekeep who’s allowed to PvP. Battlegrounds should reward actual gameplay skill, not punish people for not knowing niche terrain exploits.
