# Reduce the CD of normal juju buffs

## Post 164047 by Lane13 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-30T11:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164047#p164047 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Juju buffs have a CD of 1 minute when used. Normally this is fine since the players will just buff themselves. However, hunters have to buff themselves and their pet and so this CD makes the whole process clunky, especially due to Juju Might having a lower 10-minute duration.

Therefore, I suggest to reduce the CD of jujus to 5 or 10 seconds, similar to elixirs, with the exception of Juju Flurry (and Juju Escape I guess?). On another note, Juju Flurry should not incur a CD on Rapid Fire and vice versa so hunters can use it more effectively.
