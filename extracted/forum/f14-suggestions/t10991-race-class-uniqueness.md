# Race/Class Uniqueness

## Post 71928 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-10-18T17:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71928#p71928 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The addition of unique spells for each shaman race was a good idea (Feral Spirit, Hex,  Spirit Link).
As well, the racial spells for priest are good for the same reason, they make add more heterogeneity to the game.
For example, High elf mages could summon a phoenix like the mage unit in Warcraft 3, and Night Elf rogues could summon Avatar of Vengeance that the Warden used in Warcraft 3

What else could be done for the other classes to increase this?
Druid: Tauren, Night Elf
Hunter: All races
Mage: Dwarf, Human, Gnome, High Elf, Orc, Troll, Undead, Goblin
Paladin: Dwarf, Human, High Elf
Priest: Dwarf, Human, Night Elf, High Elf, Troll, Undead
Rogue: All races, except Tauren
Shaman: Orc, Troll, Tauren
Warlock: Human, Gnome, Orc, Undead, Goblin
Warrior: All races
