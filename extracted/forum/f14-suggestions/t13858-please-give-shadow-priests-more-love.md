Sharing my take.
Make shadow mend more spriest-like instead of it being a slow heal. For example a 1,5 sec cast time shadow mend with flash heal-like numbers which in turn puts a dot on the target wich damages him by the total amount healed via a 10 seconds DoT.
F.ex. you get healed 1000 and then you lose 100 life every sec for 10 sec. Would work as a 'damage delay' at big spikes and would play in the spriest fantasy where even healing leads to pain.

Would consider giving them a chain lightning-like ability on CD. Not aoe but something which can spell cleave every 10 seconds.

Vampiric touch with mana does similar things as arcane mage in TWOW. Would avoid buffing it to avoid overlap. Honestly I'd just remove the mana aspect of vampiric touch and would turn that talent point into something which buffs the healing factor of vampiric touch, like more healing or area-based healing instead of party healing.
