# Food Buffs and feeling full!

## Post 174095 by Cathius — 2026-04-05T19:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174095#p174095 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Have you ever sat down to a delicious feast, whether cooked on a stove by a skilled chef, or thrown together over a campfire with some friends, and after all becoming well fed, a mere fifteen minutes later feeling hungry again? No? Exactly. Let's bring food buffs in line with *reality*, because Azeroth and WoW are real.

 **My humble suggestion would be to make food buffs 1 hour to keep it in line with most other consumables durations.** It should retain loss on death (we all know what happens when you die...), but I feel that this is one of those more "unfun" things from an older, and more dated system of gaming.

I think it should just be a flat change, but perhaps in the interest of making it something to unlock or work towards, it could be something tied to a cooking quest or skill level or something like that? Early game foods (less skilled cook), shorter food buff duration, then late game it's the full hour. Something like that. Whatever works! I just think this would be an easy and positive change. No, I'm not giving thoughts to how this would effect balance, more so that I just feel it makes sense, deep in my heart (and stomach!). Thanks for coming to my TedTalk.
