You could see as a legacy the knowledge you've previously acquired about the most efficient routes to the flight points, which ones are worth getting, your knowledge of how to earn more gold in order to afford them, and the friends you've made that can now help you get there safely. All that cuts down the travel time, the alts don't have the exact same start like the mains do for that reason.

I think heirlooms are quite immersion-breaking in an online environment and go against the servers philosophy which focuses on levelling, not only endgame.

One thing I see possible, since you've mentioned that it could have some form of fair offset, is ocassional travelling carriages on the roads that could speed up the travel to flight point towns, but would cost premium gold that one could only afford if one already has a high level main (or is very good at making gold).

Same for 15 agility to white gloves.
When you find something better, you can just unequip them and pass to other character.
