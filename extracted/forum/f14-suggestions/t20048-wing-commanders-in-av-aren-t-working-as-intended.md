# Wing Commanders in AV aren't working as intended

## Post 137153 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-06-26T22:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137153#p137153 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have written it already in my long AV post but the suggestion was probably buried. Nevertheless, I find it great QoL change and I believe it's also easy to fix for devs.

Wing Commanders are special NPCs you can find across the Alterac Valley. They are supposed to accept multiple quest items from the players - and after they have enough of these, players can send them to the battle. They are one of the few NPCs in the game that can attack from the air - that makes them unique, especially it adds flavour to the Alterac Valley.

The problem is...

...Wing Commanders were barely ever seen in action on Turtle WoW. As far as my experience is concerned, I spent hundreds of hours on this server's AV and I have seen them only once. That's not because they are bugged (at least I hope so). The problem is number of turn ins needed to send them to the air that is detached from reality. Here are numbers which are likely valid for Turtle WoW's version of AV since there wasn't any mention about changes made to Wing Commanders in Torta's post 2 years ago:

- 120 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Soldier's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17326) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Soldier's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17502)

- 60 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Lieutenant's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17327) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Lieutenant's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17503)

- 30 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Commander's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17328) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Commander's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17504)
In reality, even in long turtle-games these Wing Commanders never serve their purpose. The objective is never fulfilled because the game is too short. Moreover, even if people loot these items, they usually don't turn them in because the reward for doing so is only reputation (likely rankers are Exalted already).

To make things worse, these items drop practically only from other players (technically you can kill one civilian in enemy base but nobody really does it).
The item that players drop depends on their actual PvP rank. I don't know the details but noobs drop one of these 120 and people with high rank drop the one of 30.

Suggestion:

- Decrease these numbers by half:

 - 120 -> 60 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Soldier's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17326) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Soldier's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17502)

 - 60 -> 30 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Lieutenant's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17327) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Lieutenant's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17503)

 - 30 -> 15 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Stormpike Commander's Flesh](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17328) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Frostwolf Commander's Medal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17504)

- Add slight gold reward to the repeatable quest to incentivize doing this objective. 5 silver for the most common item, 10 silver for the mid one and 15 silver for the rarest one should be fair.

- Make all of these items tradeable within Alterac Valley. I remember devs changed Armor Scraps, Stormpike Soldier's Blood and Storm Crystal to be tradeable one year ago and the effect was brilliant - PvE objectives became more common to see. But somehow this change didn't affect these quest items. Probably an oversight.
