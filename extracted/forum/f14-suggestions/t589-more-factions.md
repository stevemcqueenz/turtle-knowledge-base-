# More Factions!

## Post 3064 by Rudi2 — 2019-06-04T05:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3064#p3064 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings all! This is my actual, original suggestion. Hope you enjoy.

It is usually the antagonising factions of a story that seem most pleasing and fun (and sometimes relatable) to play when it comes to RPGs and contending games made in this generation.

Case in point, this exact game we play and even roleplay in we sometimes **dream** to play characters which interact with certain factions to suit a story, or sometimes even as your main character. These factions are not the ones that you can discover and grind for rewards or rep points. Far from it. They're the punching bags for your quests that you sometimes feel bad for even questing for.
For example, the Syndicate. They are a faction you can discover ingame and you are tasked with to take care of. You can get around this by mindlessly killing NPCs who are affiliated with the Ravenholdt faction, but you are not rewarded, let alone even tasked with killing in the first place!

The Druid of the Fang, the Scarlet Crusade, the Defias Brotherhood, the Syndicate, and even a few others I haven't even discovered in my time playing vanilla all have one thing in common.
 **They're antagonists.**
They're something that Blizzard puts us on the spot with being tasked to kill. Even if you're roleplaying as one, you cannot set foot in said faction's territory without being either ripped to shreds or being beaten on while you're trying to act natural in your respective costume or outfit in roleplay.

That is why I propose that we add "antagonist" factions to the list of ones that we may discover in the game and give new life to these sites which base some of the game's most notorious foes.

 **As an example:**
Upon targeting an NPC/enemy designated with the faction ID relative to who they are affiliated with (example: Brother Crowley in the Cathedral of the Light in Stormwind or any enemy labled "Scarlet"), you will be notified in the chat "You are now friendly/neutral/unfriendly/enemies with the *insert faction here*. It is just like discovering an NPC affiliated with Booty Bay or the Darkmoon Faire.
There are many ways in which you could grind reputation points with these factions: by speaking with an NPC and getting quests which will lead you to being unfriendly/neutral with said faction and work your way on from there (with the addition of faction-related quest rewards), killing NPCs of a rival faction, or doing the age-old daily quest of commissions by grinding mats and getting reputation as a reward **in order to join that faction outright.**

 **There is one optional addition to this suggestion which I believe could be beneficial, which can be redacted from the suggestion if a majority believe it to be too extreme.**

If a faction is notoriously evil or outright dangerous in lore which you have decided to ally yourself with, you will be automatically set to Neutral with all racial cities. It will be difficult to use vendors and be able to repair at these cities, due to the lack of proper discount that you receive when Friendly, Revered, Honoured, or Exalted. You still receive the set rate of price when using the auction house, or buying bags from the bank. This does not bar you from entering the city outright or interacting with players who have not aligned with a neutral faction.

As a trade-off to those outrageous conditions, your faction's bases and other settlements will include vendors of their own and any commodities that may be proven difficult to use when in a city.

Of course, it is race-specific on what race may join what faction, to suit the universe that the factions are of course set in. As for the required level, a player which cou

- Humans (and Dwarves?) for the Scarlet Crusade. Their capital is Tyr's Hand (or the Scarlet Monastery), with bases in Hearthglen and the Scarlet Palisade.

- Night Elves and Tauren for the Druid of the Fang. Their base is the Wailing Caverns.

- Orcs and Humans for the Syndicate. Their base is Dandred's Fold.

- Dwarves, Tauren, and Humans for the Defias Brotherhood. Their base is inside the Deadmines.
These are just a number of examples with how factions can be expanded. It doesn't have to be restricted to those with just labels. It could also include the Scourge (probably not), Old God worshippers, so on.
