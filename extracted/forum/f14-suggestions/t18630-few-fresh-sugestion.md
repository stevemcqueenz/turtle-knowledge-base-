Hi, I've been playing vanilla for 20 years, and I have some thoughts that could positively impact the game.

So...:
 - the ability to jump off the fly mount at any point;
 - two-person mounts - it might work the same way as sitting in a chair - an extra spot on the mount where you can click and then your character will sit there;
 - a five-person epic mount that could quickly transport the entire team from point a to point b. Of course, it would have to cost an appropriate amount of gold, e.g. 1000 or 2000, or some epic quest. just try imagine when four that mount just arrive to Onyxia :) 20 ppl on 4 mounts.
 - Added glyphs from the Catalyst expansion that worked to change the skill's behavior (Minor, major, and prime glyphs) - for example:
A) - your SWIPE abbiliti now will attack 4 targets, (normal 3) e.t.c.
The characters would be more diverse. Right now, everyone's path is very similar, and there aren't many options.
