# Hunter deadzone fix

## Post 20153 by Luclar — 2022-07-25T22:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20153#p20153 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since turtlewow is the representation of a vanilla+ with new content, balance and bug fixes, may I remind you that the hunter dead zone was no other than a temporary fix to an bug related to poor internet options and lag which was fixed on patch 2.3 with better technology(better options than dial up).

I remember hearing Kevin Jordan mention that the dead zone for hunters was only there because of system capabilities back in the day. They weren't able to have the game automatically distinguish when a hunter would default range or melee attack. So they put in the dead zone to force players to make the manual change from melee to range.

As the dead zone is easily exploited by root, maybe this is your chance to fix it in this progressive vanilla :) Cheers!
