# New Faction-specific Classes

## Post 15868 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-01-15T17:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15868#p15868 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think there need to be more faction class identity.

Alliance needs a new Alliance-only class and Horde needs a new Horde-only class. This was the one thing that made Vanilla WoW shine and it was destroyed in TBC.

Here are some ideas:

 - Alliance-only, Dwarf-only - Brawler - a Fist weapon class who can use alcoholic beverages to 1 hour buffs. With this comes a new application to Alchemy and Cooking - crafting special drinks for Brawlers.

 - Horde-only, Troll-only - Shadow Hunter - a Fist/Dagger weapon class who is something of a hybrid between a Shaman and a Rogue, this one also benefitting from Alchemy, where they also use poisons for their weapons, but also craft some potions with various buff/debuff effects.
