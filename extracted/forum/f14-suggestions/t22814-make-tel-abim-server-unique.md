# Make Tel Abim Server Unique

## Post 159140 by ForumGentleman (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-11T00:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159140#p159140 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello everyone,

With the recent introduction of cross-faction Battlegrounds on the PvE servers, Tel'Abim now stands out more than ever as the unique option for Turtle WoW PvPers and those who value a true faction-war experience.

However, the server's lower population means we need to leverage its strengths to make it truly shine. Tel'Abim has the potential to be a different and unique experience for players seeking a world that feels alive with conflict, rather than just a queueing hub for instanced PvP.

Here are my suggestions, to make Tel'Abim the definitive world PvP server.

 **1. Short Term - Easy Wins (Quality of Life & Incentives)**

- - Arena Masters in Capital Cities: Place Arena Battlemasters near the Auction Houses in Orgrimmar and Ironforge/Stormwind. The current location is out of the way. Having them in high-traffic areas would constantly remind players to queue and significantly increase participation. and/or Restore Instant Arena Queuing:

- Weekly PvP Quest: Introduce a weekly quest, "Veteran of the Warsong/ab etc," (that includes custom BGs) that requires winning a set number of Battlegrounds. Rewards should include:
 - Standard PvE weekly gold/items.

 -  A bundle of PvP consumables (Major Healing Potions, Free Action Potions, Grenades).

 -  A powerful, stackable buff upon completion (e.g., "Warfront Veteran's Resolve," increasing Honor gained by 10% for 2 hours).

 **2. Middle Term - Making the World a Battlefield (World PvP Quests)**

- **Bounty Quests:** Add daily or weekly quests from your faction's war command to thin the enemy ranks. "Kill 10 Orc Players" or "Kill 10 Human Players" in specific contested zones like Stranglethorn Vale, Ashenvale, or Hillsbrad. Only kills that grant honor are counted.
Rewards could be honor, gold, and unique transmog gear.

- **Sabotage Missions:** Small, dynamic quests that encourage infiltration. Examples: "Destroy Stormwind Dock Cargo," "Assassinate the Orgrimmar Apothecary," "Burn the Night Elf Sentinel in Auberdine." These would create constant, small-scale hotspots and add a layer of guerilla warfare to the server.

- **Caravan Escorts:** Occasionally, a trade caravan of NPCs (with a few guards) departs from a low-level zone (like Dun Morogh) heading to a high-level one (like Searing Gorge). The opposing faction's goal is to ambush and destroy the caravan before it arrives, while the owning faction must defend it

 **3. Long Term - A Living, Breathing Contested World (Capturable Strategic Points)**

Building on the capturable tower system in the Eastern Plaguelands, we should expand and improve this to more zones. The goal is to create persistent, faction-wide incentives to fight for control of the world itself.

Imagine small, capturable camps, towers, or outposts in key locations. Controlling a point would grant two things:

- A Local Buff: A small, personal buff for players in the zone (similar to EPL).

- A Faction-Wide Bonus: A significant, server-wide bonus for your entire faction that makes controlling these points a matter of pride and power.

Some ideas:

- **The Barrens - Gold Mine:**

Local Buff: "Miner's Fortune" - Chance to find extra ore and stone.
Faction-Wide Bonus: +5% Armor (in raids/dungeons/world) & 20% Reduced Repair Costs

- **Stranglethorn Vale -  Troll Temple:**

Local Buff: "Hunter's Cunning" - +3% Agility and Spirit.
Faction-Wide Bonus: +3% Damage in PvP Combat.

- **Tanaris - Merchant Port:**

Local Buff: "Trade Prince's Favor" - Reduced prices at all Goblin vendors.
Faction-Wide Bonus: -25% Auction House Cut & reduced cost on mount training by 5%.

- **Blasted Lands - Nethergarde Chapel:**

Local Buff: "Netherward" - +10 Shadow Resistance.
Faction-Wide Bonus: +2% Damage against Demons (works in raids and dungeons).

- **Hillsbrad Foothills - Tarren Mill / Southshore Mill:**

Local Buff: "Heart of the Conflict" - +10% Honor gained from player kills in Hillsbrad.
Faction-Wide Bonus: Increases mounted speed in the Eastern Kingdoms by 5%.

- **Ashenvale - Nightsong Grove (or a Warsong Lumber Camp):**

Local Buff: "Spirit of the Forest" - +5% movement speed while outdoors in Ashenvale.
Faction-Wide Bonus: +1% Haste (all stats, works everywhere).

- **Arathi Highlands - Faldir's Cove (or a Highland Stable):**

Local Buff: "Sailor's Vigor" - Breath lasts 50% longer underwater.
Faction-Wide Bonus: Increases the duration of Well Fed and other food buffs by 30%.

These changes would transform Tel'Abim from a server defined by what it lacks (population) into a server defined by what it offers: a truly dynamic, faction-driven world PvP experience. It would give players a reason to log in beyond daily quests and a powerful, unique identity that sets it apart from the PvE servers.
