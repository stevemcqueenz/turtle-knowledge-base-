# Appearance change tokens should be reusable just like skin tokens are.

## Post 136641 by Voldakir — 2025-06-22T15:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136641#p136641 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Why not? You're paying 16 euros worth of tokens to get a simple beard/tusk/gender/hair change, i think it would be completely fair to have the token be reusable since the skin tokens are reusable. I mean, there can't be any immersion reasons since you can just swap skin tones on the fly with the skin tokens.

Imo it would incentivize buying the appearance token because you get much more value from it. You're paying 16 euroes for a single character to get a pass to be able to change it's features.

The types of people who buy appearance change tokens are indecisive; kind of like me. That is why we buy the tokens. Because we care so much about our appearance and want to freshen up our looks.

If the token was reusable, i would instantly buy it on all 3 of my characters. Since i know they are one-time use, i am much more hesitant and won't buy them since i don't want to end up regretting that change down the line and buy another one, that is just too much waste of money.

TLDR; title. More people would buy them because more value, and it's specifically an item for indecisive people who enjoy having the peace of being able to change simple features like tusks or hair. Spending 16 euros on a single character is reasonable to get a pass to do so, as many times as you want.
