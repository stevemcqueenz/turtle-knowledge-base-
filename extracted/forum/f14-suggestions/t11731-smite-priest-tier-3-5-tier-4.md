Smite spec is not meme on turtle, nor should it be.  It is situationally superior to shadow.  The mage frost -> fire analog is fitting.  Shadow is a good budget raiding spec, but smite should be part of any DPS priest's repertoire as they progress.

Seems reasonable for priests to have 3 viable playstyles.
 1) Healing
 2) Shadow DPS
 3) Holy DPS

I'm not going to separate disc healing from holy healing since it doesn't affect your gearing, or raid role as much.

Given shadow is already a more PVP oriented spec, smite spec should reasonably get just as much attention in PVE itemization as moonkin, ret, ele, or any other talent specialization.

Ideal? Questionable. Current DPS of smite is not a problem. Of course smite priest won't deal as much dmg from crits as warlock or mage but unlike them there is no Holy Resistance in the game so if you have enough spell hit you will always hit the enemy with full damage. This compensates for the loss of crit damage - thus there is really no need to change it.

The only smite's problems I can think of are:
 -mana issues in long fights - smite consumes much mana and you will be OOM in a short time compared to other casters
 -high spell hit cap (15% I think?)
 -no utility to other raid members - I doubt this can be fixed via set bonus anyway

Tier 2.5 has only 2 bonuses and in the nearest future it's going to be the only smite set so it would be cool to fix both of these issues. My idea: (3 set pieces) - Reduce the mana cost of Smite and Holy Fire by 10%, (5 set pieces) - increase your chance to hit with spells by 3%)
