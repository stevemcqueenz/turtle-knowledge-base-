# Un-nerf shaman trash loot from Naxxramas

## Post 106774 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-06T23:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106774#p106774 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In the 17.0 itemization list, the following items were nerfed:

> - Girdle of Elemental Fury: [+301 Armor, +20 Stamina, +21 Intellect, +29 Spellpower, +5 MP5] -> [+285 Armor, +19 Stamina, +20 Intellect, +26 Spellpower, +5 MP5, Shaman Only restriction added in line with other pieces.]
>
> - Leggings of Elemental Fury: [+468 Armor, +26 Stamina, +27 Intellect, +32 Spellpower, +2% Spell Critical] -> [+443 Armor, +25 Stamina, +24 Intellect, +29 Spellpower, +2% Spell Critical]
>
> - Pauldrons of Elemental Fury: [+401 Armor, +19 Stamina, +21 Intellect, +26 Spellpower, +1% Spell Hit, +1% Spell Critical] -> [+379 Armor, +18 Stamina, +20 Intellect, +21 Spellpower, +1% Spell Hit, +1% Spell Critical]

Source: [viewtopic.php?t=8230](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=8230)

Now, with the significant inflation in stats following CC2 and tier set changes, I suggest you revert the changes made to these items and restore their original stats.
