# Add More Mid-Tier Enchanting Options

## Post 136825 by tr00dudu — 2025-06-24T11:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136825#p136825 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We need more affordable mid/end game enchants on some slots. As it stands, some slots like bracers have a wide variety of enchants from early game to late game, while other items - like caster weapons, you got either a +3int that is worthless, or +30sp that goes for 150g+.

It seems to me there are two solutions:

 1) I think the Turtle team made an interesting design choice with Jewelcrafting, where you got the expensive +5sp gem, then alternatives limited to a specific school, that are several times cheaper and even a bit stronger. (Same thing for stats)

So one solution could be adding JC-like specific alternatives to enchants (like school specific sp school enchants on a weapon, specific +single stat on chest, etc.).

But that's just for specific enchants and doesn't completely fix a weird issue with enchanting, so...

 2) Alternatively, there is already the minor/lesser/normal/greater/etc enchant scaling system for some enchants, that blizzard seems to have forgotten to apply to others (Cloak: Minor Agility??).

Adding at least lesser variants of existing end game enchants would not break balance as you're only adding weaker versions of enchants that already exist. But it would add to variety and QOL because people would have affordable options and variety while levelling and in the end game.
