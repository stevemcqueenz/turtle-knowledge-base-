# Probably a stupid idea...

## Post 116593 by Snigery (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-13T16:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116593#p116593 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am not a HC main, I don't like being barred off from the community, even if you can still interact with other HC around your level. I like that you can still cast buffs between the two, even if you can't party together, you can still help with an aggro'd mob. But what about allowing someone to ressurect a HC player who died, obviously within the same level range restrictions, and also applying a timer of like 5-10 minutes. Whatever timer that would keep someone from traversing the entire world, but allow an opportunity on the off chance should a nearby healer be available.

I think it would be neat to give some more weight to a healers ability to resurrect someone from literal deletion, and still allow some interplay between the two. Heck even restrict how many times a HC *can* even be revived this way, either permanently for their play, or with a long resurrection-like sickness.

I'm not trying to ruing it if this upsets anyone, but I thought it could be neat. Maybe only even allow the ressurection to work if the player actually has a majority talents specced into their restro tree? Idk, just ideas.

I just saved a HC player from an annoying spawn that aggrod them, was just thinking of how that still could have been a cool interaction should they have died and I GRANTED THEM LIFE AGAIN!!!

Just an idea.
