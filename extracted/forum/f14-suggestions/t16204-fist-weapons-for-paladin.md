# Fist Weapons for Paladin

## Post 110508 by Zsielous — 2024-11-29T13:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110508#p110508 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title suggests, I think it might be a fun idea to allow Fist Weapons to extend outwards to Paladins.
While not as potent/used as sword and board, or big weapons, the idea of a Paladin deciding to just beat people up with their fists is a genuinely humorous concept.
Beyond that - it could also make them a bit like a proto-Monk, and also give people investing in the Craftsman challenge some useful kit before they get their shields.
