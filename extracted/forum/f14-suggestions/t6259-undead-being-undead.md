## Post 39391 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-28T08:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39391#p39391 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think such a change is way to polarizing for the way classic 1.12 differs from alpha.

A passive fear/poly immunity massively changes how certain encounters can be played. It trivializes bosses such as Nefarian or Gluth if the tank is passively immune to fear.
Its also extremely polarizing for PvP, as paladins are already strong as is. Giving them more burst against undead players would for sure be pushing the Winrate even further into alliances favor. While the Undead only get stronger against Warlocks

RP-wise i think it would make sense, but imho it is way to impactful for race choice. Especially considering Paladin is right there to REALLY ruin your day if you ever step into PvP
