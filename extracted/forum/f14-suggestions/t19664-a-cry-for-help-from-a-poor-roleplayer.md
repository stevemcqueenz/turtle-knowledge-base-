## Post 133997 by Corpsembo7 — 2025-06-02T17:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133997#p133997 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Long ago, around 2019 give or take, you used to be able to buy various costumes such as a demon hunter costume, a necromancer costume (with the necroknight garb from Naxxramas), and so on. At some point, this was removed and only "added back" relatively recently in a way. Making things such as the necroknight garb no longer acquirable by donation makes sense in a way, even if it makes necromancer roleplay a harder challenge, but it can drop from any trash mob in Naxxramas. Farming any mob in Naxxramas is kind of easy due to the nature of them being raid elites and not overworld rare elites. Compare this to the demon hunter glaive in Blasted Lands where you need to farm a rare ELITE mob for 0.5% chance of dropping it, which is a hard fight that basically requires you to get the help of a friend to farm a mob for your own glaive. I remember hearing how people would instead pay gold to ancient TWoW players to get the glaive transmog in a far easier way because of how extreme this was. Now, let's compare it even further to the Righteous Crusader Helmet where you have to farm a more difficult ELITE rare mob in EPL for 0.2% chance of acquiring it. Meanwhile, Admiral Barean Westwind just casually drops an epic pirate coat at 15% drop chance. What?

For some reason, Scarlet Crusade roleplay is the most difficult uphill battle imaginable. Once upon a time, you merely do a quest in Stormwind to get a ring where you become friendly to the faction as long as you have it on. The ring has been gone for years now because of exploit concerns, the same kind of exploiting that people can do with Corrupted Ashbringer instead now. It IS possible to make players unable to interact with chests (go to Bael Modan as Alliance, it won't let you loot a chest that horde players can loot, I checked this years ago). It IS possible to reduce concerns of players exploiting friendly status to freely heal without risk (make the item reduce your healing power by 99%, no one in the world is going to get carried by Greater Heal Rank IV doing +17.2 HP). It IS possible to reduce concerns of people abusing the friendly status for PvP (same logic for damage, reduce it by 99%). You can keep all these debuffs 10-30 minutes after they take off the ring too, you can do a lot! There can be many more ideas related to debuffs, timers, so on, but instead we are left in a state where I need to get Corrupted Ashbringer to talk to the innkeeper in the backyard of Scarlet Monastery (not to be confused with Scarlet Crusade guild base). What?

I feel like I am fighting tooth and nail to merely larp as a Scarlet Crusade dude despite how much easier it was and could be. There was once an attunement questline for a Scarlet Raid that would've been the answer to this post (even if having to raid to dress as Scarlet Champion is a harsh challenge like having to farm Naxx mobs to be a necroknight garb necromancer, but so be it), but now that the raid is postponed, we are still desperately farming the same rare mob in EPL for only one part of Scarlet Champion armor set.

I have a lot of thoughts on SC rp, but at bare minimum, I think at least letting diehard Scarlet Crusade fans merely DRESS UP as their faction would be nice. It could be obtainable as drops, it could be on the donation store, I just think anything would be nicer than hoping you can beat the entire rest of the server in farming a helmet on some guy in EPL where you probably need to quickly arrange a group of friends before anyone else can arrange their own group of friends to fight it.
