## Post 41664 by Jannus — 2023-04-15T14:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41664#p41664 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey guys,

Since the goblin race is locked into an area that you cannot escape from until you finish all of the quests within that area, and there is no inn or a mailbox placed, you are unable to simply leave the area and move onto the next area like every other race is able to - humans can leave Northshire Abbey and go to Goldshire, night elves can go to Dolanaar, Orcs/trolls can go to Razor hill, etc, and I believe high elves had a mailbox at their starter location (correct me if I'm wrong) - but even if they don't, they can walk to Thelsamar (and probably die a few times along the way) but it's still possible.

So my suggestion is simple - place a mailbox in the goblin starter area, or make it possible to leave it with a one-way portal that drops you into Durotar (for example).

Thanks.
