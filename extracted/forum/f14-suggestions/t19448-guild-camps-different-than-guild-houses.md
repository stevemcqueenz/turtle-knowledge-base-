# Guild Camps (Different than Guild Houses)

## Post 132261 by Spirittap — 2025-05-21T03:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132261#p132261 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Would it possible to suggest the game receive a couple of camps built around the world in secluded areas that consists of perhaps a small tent or two and some chairs + campfire with an innkeeper where guilds can set their guild base?

Cross-faction guilds do not have a lot of options for guild bases right now and I love the theme of a guild base. But this could be a very simple and fun idea to allow people to have a nice RP hangout spot shared with other guilds.
