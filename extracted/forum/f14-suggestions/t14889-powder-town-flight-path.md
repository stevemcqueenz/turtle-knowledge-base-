# Powder Town Flight Path

## Post 101377 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-17T02:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101377#p101377 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Not much else to say 'sides the title. I've been doing all the quests in this nice little custom town on my hunter alt and my only complaint with it is a lack of a flight path. I know the distance between this place and sun rock retreat isn't too large, on paper, but with how twisty turny the way between them is, you're kinda travelling a lot more than it would normally seem. Would be nice to have a flight path between them just for the sake of convenience.

## Post 101381 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-17T03:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101381#p101381 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This seems wholly unnecessary, just like the Dun Agrath flightpath for Alliance.

There's a path east out of Sun Rock Retreat that takes you to the main road, which you can follow for maybe 30 steps before coming across a ramp that leads up to a mountain pass north of Sun Rock that looks down into the town and cuts right across the ridge leading into Powder Town, north of Charred Vale.

It's a 5 minute run with no mount at most.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/3RNJHpB.png)

Generally there should be a minimum threshold for travel time and inconvenience before considering flight paths to avoid the Wrath-onward mistake of putting FPs everywhere.  A good candidates, for example, is Steepcliff Port in Tirisfal.

Theme also has to be looked at, as gameplay convenience shouldn't always supersede how much sense it makes for a gryphon or wind rider handler (experts that have to be requisitioned, mind you) to be sent out to those places.  This is why Rebel Camp in STV should never get an Alliance FP.
