# Bugged animations - Hunter Pet - Eagle

## Post 64074 by Profgiggles — 2023-09-14T20:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64074#p64074 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!

A bugged animation for a hunter pet came to my attention recently.

The tameable eagle hunter pet defaults to its stationary animation while flying besides you. I have observed that this is the case for both the Amani Eagle found near the Stonetalon Mountains, as well as the Hinterlands Eagle from near Aerie Peak.
These are the eagle NPCs which can be tamed and become a pet with the bugged animation.

Amani Eagle - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=80815>

Hinterlands Eagle - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=80816>

Furthermore its minimum / default altitude seems to be really very low to the ground. It flies at the hunters leg height.

I really love the models for the eagles and would like to use one for my hunter pet, but the combination of these two issues makes it really irksome to look at, and it does turn me away from it. I would love to see it recieve some adjustments.

I found that this bug has already been reported on the bugtracker since July.

[https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow- ... ssues/3928](https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow-bug-tracker/issues/3928)

I am making this post to highlight that this bug persists, and I would love to see it fixed.   smiling_turtle_head
