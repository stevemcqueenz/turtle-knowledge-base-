# Restore the Viability of the Shockadin

## Post 122639 by dushaka — 2025-03-15T16:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122639#p122639 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings, Turtle WoW team and community!

Like many others, I returned to Turtle WoW hoping to relive unique gameplay experiences that only this server can offer. However, I was surprised to find that the Shockadin, one of the most beloved and creative Paladin builds, has been practically removed due to recent changes.

We understand that modifying Holy Shock to function solely as a healing ability was intended to balance the game, particularly in PvP. However, this change has completely eliminated a playstyle that many players enjoyed in both PvE and PvP. The Shockadin not only provided a viable and fun alternative to the traditional healer or tank roles but also allowed players to explore a hybrid playstyle without being overpowered in any area of the game.

 **Why Should the Shockadin Be Restored?**

 1. Gameplay Diversity: Turtle WoW is known for innovating within the framework of classic WoW. Maintaining a wider variety of builds and playstyles enriches the experience for everyone.
 2. Balanceable Without Removing It: Instead of making Holy Shock healing-only, adjustments could be made to its damage coefficient, mana cost, or cooldowns to prevent PvP issues without sacrificing its viability in PvE.
 3. A Community That Wants It: Many players have expressed their disappointment with this removal. Restoring the Shockadin (even with necessary adjustments) would show that the developers listen and respond to the community.
 4. Consistency with Paladin Fantasy: Paladins have always been holy warriors, wielding the power of the Light to both heal and smite their enemies. The Shockadin embodies this fantasy, and removing it makes the class feel incomplete.

 **Possible Solutions**

Instead of removing the Shockadin’s viability, alternatives could be considered, such as:
![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Adjusting Holy Shock's damage coefficient in PvP without affecting its PvE performance.
![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Implementing an optional talent that preserves the hybrid identity without breaking balance.

Turtle WoW has consistently shown that it listens to the community and makes decisions that benefit as many players as possible. We believe there is room to bring back the Shockadin without compromising the game’s vision or overall balance.

We appreciate the development team’s work and dedication in making Turtle WoW a unique server. We hope you consider this suggestion and find a way to allow us to once again enjoy this playstyle without disrupting the game’s balance.

Support This Suggestion!

If you agree that the Shockadin should return, leave a comment and share your thoughts. The more voices we have, the more likely the developers are to reconsider this change.

For the Light and for Turtle WoW! ![🔆](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f506.svg)

It’s great to see so many players engaging in this discussion! A few key points have come up, and I’d like to highlight some thoughts to keep the conversation productive and focused on solutions rather than just arguing about the past.

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Balance concerns – Yes, old Shockadin had the potential for high burst, but outright removal instead of a balance pass was an extreme solution. If the issue was PvP damage, simple tuning adjustments (damage coefficient reductions, mana cost tweaks, or PvP-only limitations) could have kept it in check without deleting an entire build.

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) "No one was complaining about Shockadin" – Several people have pointed out that most of the complaints about Paladins were focused on Ret, not Shockadin. If Shockadin wasn't breaking the game and had a dedicated player base, why remove it completely?

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Preserving class identity and variety – One of the things that makes Turtle WoW great is its willingness to expand and refine Classic’s identity rather than just copy-pasting later expansions. Shockadin fits perfectly into this philosophy. It wasn't some weird exploit or unintended gimmick—it was a valid hybrid build that fit the Paladin fantasy. Removing it feels like cutting out an entire spec.

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Other specs were adjusted, not removed – Many builds got reworks in CC2, but Shockadin is the only one that was outright deleted. This is what frustrates many players. Tweaking it? Fine. Reworking it? Fine. But removing a playstyle that many enjoyed—especially when Holy Paladins are now struggling—was a poor design choice.

If Turtle WoW is truly about expanding Classic, why not find a way to keep one of the most iconic and beloved hybrid playstyles alive?
