# Occasional Uncapped Battleground Event For Fun

## Post 18297 by Jambiya (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-04-22T00:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18297#p18297 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Okay so hear me out. This would be a seldom thing allowed just for the sheer novelty of joyous chaos it would provide. If possible I'd propose for a day the devs remove the player caps for both character level and player amount from a specific battleground, say WSG or AB. This means for 24hours there would be a 100 vs 100 (or higher) battleground bracket that can be qued from any level 1-60. I'd exclude AV since the high level mobs will prevent low levels from participating.

Purely for fun and wild abandon this would just be a novelty event for community enjoyment. Having this event announced once in a while should allow people to save the date and schedule their participation. This would greatly help keep the sides relatively even. At the end of the day however we can't expect much balance other than tapering off the player limit if one side is stuck around a certain amount (if 40 vs 70 show up, put a cap of 50 vs 50 to even things out)

A possible way of implementation would be to create an 'uncapped' clone of a battleground that will suddenly become available on the day of the event. I would suggest giving it a different name as to sperate it from normal bgs for those wanting to gind rp.

Feel free to critique or brainstorm humorous, alternative battleground names below.
