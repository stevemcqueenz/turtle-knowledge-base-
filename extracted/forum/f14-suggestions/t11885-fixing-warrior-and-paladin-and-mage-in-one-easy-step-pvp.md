Its well known that Paladin is the most overpowered class in PVP.
There are other classes/specs that are somewhat OP and could use minor nerfs but Paladin is the prime candidate.

The devs don't do anything about it likely because Horde have OP racial abilities in PVP.
So it kinda sorta evens out overall... statistically speaking. (I'm guessing)

ANYWAY... despite the common myth that "Warriors totally rock in PVP, bro":
 **Warrior is (in actual fact) AWFUL in general PVP scenarios.**

The reasons are too numerous to explain quickly.
ONE THING: is that they are designed to become powerful over long periods of combat (gathering Rage).
They are busted in PVE as boss fights last a long time.
They suck ass in PVP as PVP is 90% of the time a BURST DAMAGE race -- fights are over in literal SECONDS.

Blizzard themselves were smart enough to understand the obvious weakness of Warrior in PVP which is why in TBC they gave Warrior "Spell Reflect".

Yes, T-WoW should copy this and give them Spell Reflect as well.

HOWEVER someone just gave me the EXCELLENT idea of also **giving Warrior the WoTLK ability "Shattering Throw".**

This ability only affects PVP and will indirectly nerf Paladins by popping their bubbles early.
AND it gives Warrior something to do against Frost Mages (extremely common OP PVP class/spec) (who BTW ANNIHILATE Warriors with ease) which is BREAK THOSE DAMN ICE BLOCKS that everyone hates).

Indeed, Warrior is also greatly lacking in Utility -- they are a deeply selfish class that offers nothing to their teammates in PVP and DEMANDS constant baby-sitting from healers to even have any effectiveness whatsoever.

If they had Shattering Throw people would actually be GLAD to have a Warrior on their team -- to help combat the endless Paladin armies.
