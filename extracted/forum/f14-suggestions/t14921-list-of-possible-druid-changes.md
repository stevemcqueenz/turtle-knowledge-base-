# List of possible druid changes.

## Post 101562 by Fugus (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-09-20T21:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101562#p101562 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I originally posted this as just a few suggestions, but decided to make it a little more comprehensive.

 1) Allow druids to be able to use the Tauren Racial "Warstomp" without breaking form. Its weird that a cow can stomp but not a bear or cat.

 2) Allow feral druids to be able to actually use potions, open chests, and use quest items without breaking form. These get very frustrating. And some flavor limitations that blizzard put in were effectively broken by design when they implemented them, 3 is another example of such limitations.

 3) Remove the "Outdoor Only" requirement to the roots and speed. It really sucks to have your class balanced around abilities and then to lock them out in almost every instance you go into and half the locations in battlegrounds and world PvP. Giving druids access to Entangling Roots indoors wouldn't be balance breaking in the slightest nor would allowing feral to actually run in the house.

Those are the BIG ones that I really think need to seriously be considered. The next ones are more of a "Depending on the overall design ahead" setup.

 4) Feral Charge: Moved to become the new capstone but changed to be usable in Cat or Bear forms with no resource cost or dead zone. This would give both forms a gap closer and an interrupt which it lacks. This makes the capstone usable in both cat form and bear form in both PvE and PvP and making sure not other spec gets access to it. Also, a good PvP set bonus would be to reduce its cool down.

 5) Where the old Feral charge is, replace it with Tiger's Fury but instead of it boosting damage the way it currently does, change it to work similar to a Warriors Sweeping Strikes or a rogues Blade Flurry which would give both Cat and Bear access to an AoE cooldown which would help bear form with AoE threat some when combined with Swipe while Cat form actually gets an AoE at all.

 6) Modify Moonkin form so that when Hurricane is cast while in Moonkin form, it's debuff and cool down are removed. This would give Moonkin a spammable AoE like other casters while still locking other specs out of it and if they want to use the debuff, they can just drop Moonkin form at which point they are also putting it on cooldown.

 7) Berserk: Never was a fan of how Berserk was gutted on retail before it went live. Originally, it was designed similar to a Warriors Bladestorm or a Rogues Killing Spree or a Hunters Beast Within where you couldn't be stopped unless killed but then was nerfed into a fear only thing which killed it for any big PvP as the opponent would just hit you with any real CC the instant you turned big and red and their CC cooldown was much lower than your berserk cool down. And back then feral PvP all but ignored Bear form and was just DPS racing for instant cast procs to keep going which isn't the case here. My suggestion would be to either give it back its CC immunity it had before it went live or lower the cool down to something that isn't so long. Doubly so if you also made the Tiger's Fury change above which would impact single target DPS.
