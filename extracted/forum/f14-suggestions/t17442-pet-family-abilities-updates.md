# Pet Family Abilities updates

## Post 119085 by Mistermadigan — 2025-02-05T19:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119085#p119085 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Retail has done a lot of ridiculous things with pet abilities.  My main was a hunter and my favorite pet has always been the Tallstrider, and over the years the Tallstrider family got like 6 different pet abilities - it was stupid.

Now, each pet family having their own unique ability does bring a lot of fun design choices depending on how you work it.  Crabs having a unique ability that can Disarm enemies is hilarious in PvP and PvE, and sets them apart from other pet families.  The problem is always going to be - which pet family does the most damage - though that question is mostly answered by having unique attack speeds for certain rare pets when tamed.

Anyways.  I think there is room in the current system for certain pets to have currently existing pet abilities - I know you don't want a homogenized mess where all pets are interchangable.  I love that pet families still have those hidden values for Health, Armor, and Damage.  I want all that kept as is - but I see a few pet families that might benefit from an expansion of their talents:

Tallstriders/Hawkstrider - They need the Screech Pet Ability, they're already famously loud pets.  Giving them Screech makes so much sense.

Crocolisks - I honestly think the Prowl Pet Ability would fit the theme of Crocolisks well.

Raptors - Charge from the Boar pet family would be great for Raptors.

Hyenas - So, the problem is that Hyenas share the wolves +5% Armor bonus, so you don't want to give them Furious Howl because you want to keep them distinct.  But Hyenas are also famously sneaky, so I think Prowl might fit them the best.

Spiders... The fact that Spiders don't have a web or a venom Pet Ability really irks me.  I think Scorpid Poison needs to be renamed to Arachnid Venom or something so that Spiders can gain access to an ability they should have always had.

Bears - Bears getting Furious Howl would work, but Bear sounds are more like roars or growls.  But you can't just give them Demoralizing Roar like Druids... (vsauce music plays) or can you?

(Okay, the truth is I don't care about the other Pet Families, just give Tallstriders/Hawkstriders Screech okay?)
