Hey folks!

 **Please bring back some love for priests**. The latest nerfs were way too hard.
Playing dps priest does not feel good at the moment, neither in pve nor in pvp.

my suggestions:

 **1. Rework the tier gear** - T2.5 is only +holy dmg. T3 and T3.5 is only +shadow.
Please change these to normal +spell power or add a 3rd set. This way all spell schools can benefit from tier gear.
Other hybrids like druids and shamans got these sort of tier gear as well.
Its a hybrid class, so please give us hybrid gear.
In both pvp and pve we often need to stop dps and cast some emergency heals. With this current +holy and +shadow gear we cant fit our hybrid role. Just give us hybrid gear like druids and shamans got.

 **2. Rework smite spec** - Why did you nerf the mana reduction on Searing Ligtht?
Our mana sustain is very very bad now. In addition we are currently bottom tier dps.
These chain smite crits gave this spec some identity and mad it fun to play.
At the moment it just feels like a low dps mage. Give us some love back. Maybe some +crit damage talent like other casters have. Furthermore i suggest to rework chastise. At the moment there is no real use for it in pve.
In most cases its just an overall raid dps loss.
In pvp the 2sec disorientation is a meme. With GCD you have only 0.5s left to cast something. Maybe increase the stun duration and decrease the damage, so people dont cry about it.
And please give us some T2.5 bonus back, that effects the offensive part of chastise or anything else we can benefit from. Even the 5s "stun" with old T2.5 bonus wasnt that overpowered, because it breaks anyway if the enemy gets damaged. Other classes have these kind of spells with way longer durations like Sap, Polymorph, Seduction etc.

 **3. Minor fixes for shadow spec** - The cast time on Vamipiric Embrace just feels awful in pvp. Please undo that.
Why did you move Blessed Concentration out of the discipline thee? Against melees most of the time mind flay only ticks once, because of the spell push back. Please give us atleast a 70% push back immunity talent like other casters have.

I hope you agree with my suggestions. Our turtle priests would be very thankful.

ps: sorry for my english, im not a native speaker.

## Post 111950 by Caracole — 2024-12-09T20:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111950#p111950 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

DPS priests are lacklusters in pve but we need ZERO pvp buffs. This I am certain, other than pve buffs inadvertently buffing pvp priest's ability.
I don't think Chastise needed to get a triple nerf all at once. Nerfing the 3T2.5 bonus (not to the point of killing it though) and its range were enough. It's not a stun either. But I can make sense of it, if they want to keep its short cooldown. But friendly (pve) Chastise doesn't make any sense. This is where the focus should be.

=== CHASTISE IN PVE ===
If a target scales perfectly on haste (doesn't happen), a chastise will increase its damage by 0.15*6*dps = 0.9*dps. If I chastise, I waste 1.5*dps of my damage (1.5s = gcd). So, why would I ever do this? How did we come us with this number of 15% haste for 6 seconds?
 6s, or even 8s on crit, is EVERY SINGLE TIME a loss for raid dps, assuming you have average raid gear. It is not a buff. Chastise is a dps transfer, with an overall loss. The only time it makes sense is on pull on the tank if they have trouble generating threat. That's it. And this is our capstone spell.
If I am a rdps receiving Chastise, I can AT BEST cast 3 spells of 2s within a 6s window, these will last 3*2s*0.85 = 5.1s. That's a gain of 0.9s, while the priest pays 1.5s. And now, healers have to heal the mage. How much sense does this make? It doesn't.
=== NO UNIQUE SUPPORT ===
Enlighten (which is good) is not the smite priest capstone spell, and healer priests can very well play it, given how champion is also a bad capstone.
Smite priests are supposedly a support dps (otherwise, why do we have 3/4 the dps of a mage?) but they have NO SUPPORT ABILITY of their own. Warlocks offer better support.
The solution is: boost Chastise baseline length and/or make it synergize with Enlighten and make it enticing for priests to play it nearly on cooldown in raids. Basic suggestions: (1) Have it buff the target and the caster and/or the enlightened player. (2) Make it 10 seconds. If this is too long, make its cd equal to 50s (preserving the same 1/5 ratio we currently have which 6s for a 30s cd) but bring back 3s disorient to even things out.

=== NO IDENTITY ===
Pre-CC2, smite priests had the unique ability to do threatless AoEs. This was taken away.
CC2 brought the great idea of using crits to compensate for the two major problems with smite priests: mobility and mana management.
Before the last patch, we had very average dps. Now we are the weakest dps spec after moonkins and survival hunters according to the last patchwerk logs (and anyone playing endgame content) only that, we know the top smites are self-enlightened and therefore, offer exactly ZERO support to their raid, while moonkins offer amazing support, in comparison. So much for the overtuned smite priests.
Chain searing light does not make an outstanding difference either (reminder, we were low-average dps) unless one gets to bis gear + full consumes in raid conditions to reach very high crit %. But it offers us an identity, an itemization that makes sense.
Crit chance makes us cast 29% more smites (1.94s/1.5s=1.29 with talents). Having searing light reduce mana cost by 20% means that crit is now NOT saving us mana, but costs us additional mana. What was the point of this nerf?

=== NO ENDGAME SCALING ===
This follows from the previous point: crit is not good anymore. The more we stack it, the more it loses its value in terms of damage (searing light losing its value as we start to have successive crits), while it should be the opposite, crit being the stat that makes a spec viable endgame (besides haste), unless we start to increase spell scaling greatly (e.g. mind flay for shadow priest, which was the only alternative solution). It also makes us spend more mana.
Searing light is what gave smite a chance to do well endgame. Now that this was removed, all our remaining talents are flat and do not scale. I don't know why we are getting 3% haste from talents.
I agree with bringing smite damage back to vanilla level, but let us cast it freely (no internal cd), it's not like we have talents to burst at will in pvp,  it's pure rng, unlike other specs, which btw can crit at 200%, we don't. We are not asking to be top dps, just let us have an identity (by committing to the cc2 design and giving us actual useful support abilities) and a chance to do well endgame.

=== TIER SETS ===
Why does the smite priest only have a T2.5 set? If they are to only have 'off sets', why don't they have a T3.5 set? All the other dps specs have 2 to 3 T3.5 sets. Why don't we have a T3.5 for smite spriests? Just change the color (or keep it, doesn't matter) and slap some stats on it.
DPS priests are the only hybrid dps specs whose tier sets do not include healing power, and a single school spell damage. Aren't we hybrid like Moonkins and Elemental shamans? Why can't we also have that?
If we can't have smite tier sets for some reason, why can't we use the 'shadow tier sets'? We'll overlook bonuses. Dps priests always leaned on off tier pieces, but having tier sets being forbidden to us is just not right.

=== SHADOW PRIEST IN PVP ===
We have a burst-based meta. Spriest were never good at that, they have no big cooldown abilities (same for smites), and go in the opposite direction of having a strong sustain and quicker smaller attacks (dot, 1s-tick channel, 1.5s cast). And reminder, they are 'hybrids' but they cannot heal themselves (i.e., they're not). Shadow priests got a nerf on blackout, painspike, health and mana regen via VT. Please keep their original design and give back their instant VT. We have seen its impact, it's overkill.
Also, no idea how did they distribute stats for the T3 and T3.5 shadow sets. First of all, their stats are clearly below that of other cloth rdps (mage and warlock), why is that? Second, they don't make sense. Why is there so much hit and crit on them? Spriests can't use crit, and they have way too much %hit from the rest of their gear. Other classes have viable stats. I don't understand.

Anyway, I don't think I/we are asking for anything gamebreaking here. Just commit to a gameplay that makes sense, and be desirable (useful support abilities) and viable (better scaling) in raids. Tier sets are a minor point, we can leave that for now.

Priests
While I see you claim Priest specializations are performing well, I find Discipline dps scales too poorly into Naxx while Proclaim Champion feels too lackluster for a single target capstone buff. And current discipline is not a support spec, with a pure +holy damage tier set, and high mana consumption together with the healing nerfs for specs without spiritual healing there is no off-healer role. Chastise is lackluster as a support spell, and Enlighten while ok is miniscule compared to how other specs buff eachother.

High Elf
Increase the duration of Grace of the Sunwell to 15 sec, but also increase the cooldown to 10 min. The current spell is arguably worth the global cooldown, but this kind of buff feels much better in play when it is a long cooldown with big impact when you press it. Arguably it could even be buffed to 20 sec duration with 15 min cooldown and it would be great.

Holy Problem
Proclaim Champion mana regen for the priest is currently powerful if you are the priest who getts to place it on a main tank. But mana management is a joke with current kill times, especially with demonic runes, tea, and mana potions all being on different cooldowns.

The benefits for the person receiving Proclaim Champion are small and in a raid environment with full buffs they don't even stack (from what I understand).

Empower Champion is small and forgettable and again doesn't stack with full consumes.

Holy Solution
Proclaim Champion should make the main tank feel like a champion. It should be a buff tanks ask for/desire similar to how they would ask for Blessing of Kings, or dps ask for Blessing of Salvation.

Buff Proclaim Champion grant raw mitigation like a plain takes 5% less damage taken instead of the current +armor and +resistances buff. Alternatively make the +armor much bigger (like +700 armor) and allow the +resistances to stack with paladin auras etc.

Buff Empower Champion to give 2% Haste and +5% Threat instead of the current AP + SP.

If it feels like this is too much, nerf the mana regen from Proclaim Champion back down to 1%. It is more important that the Champion feels good about his buff than the Priest. Priests who want big numbers themselves can spec Enlighten
instead.

Discipline Problem
Instant Cast smites is "too" strong in pvp/unfun to play against. While current Disc is nerfed enough to be fine in pvp this makes it less fun and potent in pve and makes balancing problematic.

Discipline is strong while leveling and as a fresh 60, but scales poorly into Naxx and beyond.

Discipline mana sustain is terrible compared to basically all other mana dps.

The support aspects like Resurgent Shield and Chastise feels bad to use.

Smite "needs" to be in a group with Sanctity Aura, but Ret paladins want to be in melee groups while Holy paladins often don't spec Sanctity Aura for raids.

Discipline Solutions
Remove or nerf the size of the +spellpower buff from Inner Fire. This free spellpower gives too much of a buff while leveling and as a fresh 60 in lower raids, which hampers scaling for dps priests into higher raids.

Have Searing Light cause the next Smite to cost 40% less mana, and let it increase critical damage bonus of Chastise, Smite, Holy Fire, and Holy Nova by 17/34/50%. Critical scaling is required for a dps spec to scale nicely into Naxx.

Change Purifying Flame to give the +10% Holy damage to yourself, and grant an additional +5% Holy damage buff to you and each group member. This way we get synergy both ways between priests and paladins so both desire to be grouped together and buff eachother. Also Holy Fire basically cannot be cast in pvp, so damage bonuses here won't distort pvp balance much. If buffing party-members (paladins) is too much of a hassle to deal with, just increases the damage bonus from Purifying Flame while nerfing the base damage of smite to weaken smite in pvp while strengthening it in raids.

Resurgent Shield should give significant amounts of +Spell Crit instead of +Holy Spellpower. Maybe like 0.8-0.9% +Spell Crit for each point of damage absorbed. For dps this would make it more interesting in pvp, as you hard cast while shield is up, then fish crits with holy nova -> instant smites when shield is broken and buff is up. In pve this might allow for some meme-play as a healer where you shield + spam flash heals for high Inspiration uptime.

Give Chastise back the 1.5 sec global, nerf CD to 45 sec, and increase the buff duration to 12 sec (15 sec with t2.5 set bonus). It feels much better if the button is impactful to press but with longer CD, and having Chastise be weaker in pvp helps allow for buffs elsewhere.

Give Holy Nova reduced threat, ideally I would prefer the glory of old 0 threat days but alternatively have Silent Resolve double the threat reduction for Holy Nova. Holy Nova is an incredibly fun spell for Smite dps, and basically carried the spec before cc2.

Fix tier sets. Pure holy damage/shadow damage feels bad, lackluter set bonuses makes it feel even worse. As suggested above 3-set T2.5 should be +3 sec Chastise haste duration, while like 5-set T2.5 should increase the damage bonus from Enlighten by 5% or something to make it desirable, instead of a weird Chastise CD reduction. Discipline/Smite also really needs its own T3.5 set with upper kara, or the ability to share a set with Shadow (aka +spellpower instead of pure shadow damage on all pieces).

Shadow Note
I don't play shadow much, so how you should tweak shadow is something I would leave to shadow priests. However removing +spellpower from Inner Fire would nerf shadow and should be compensated for, probably by increasing the base damage of Mind Flay and Mind Blast. Support wise I think shadow could get back 2/4% scaling on mana kickback with nerfs to mana elsewhere (Demonic Rune, Mana Potion, Tea shouldn't all be on different cooldowns).

Important Final Note
When buffing/nerfing specs and how they feel to play, ready a big tool box of possible tweaks but only apply them 1-2 at a time so you can get playtest and player feedback. It feels terrible when you get nerfed along 4 different vectors at the same time like what happened to Smite after the initial cc2 release.
 1. Nerfing Chastise disorient duration
 2. Searing Light getting internal cooldown on instant smites
 3. Searing Light giving less mana cost reduction
 4. Smite/Holy Fire gettingg lower base damage values
All of these nerfs at the same time is just plain killing the spec. Like any of these changes in isolation was possibly a deserved nerf, while the initial cc2 Smite was incredibly fun to play compared to the current it was clearly also unfun to play against. It would be better if they were not all applied at a rate of 1-2 per patch with a review if you achieved the desired result or if more nerfs are required.
