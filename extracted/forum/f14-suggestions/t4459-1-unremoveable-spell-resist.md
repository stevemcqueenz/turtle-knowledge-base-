The 1% spell resist is supposed to foster emergent gameplay and give a need to cooldowns without pre-emptive use. At least, that's the idea behind things like this, or getting crit by a boss 2 times in a row, statistically unlikely things. That's the idea behind RPGs, encounters have unpredictable elements that you have to react to, not be proactive.

As a player I hate when it happens. As a designer I'm going to go the other way and say we need more systems like this.
