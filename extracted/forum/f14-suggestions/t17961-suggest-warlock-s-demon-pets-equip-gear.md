# Suggest:Warlock's Demon pets equip gear

## Post 122315 by Johnkw — 2025-03-12T03:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122315#p122315 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle WoW Developers,

I have a suggestion regarding warlock demon pets that I hope will interest you and bring more enjoyment to players:

We know that mercenaries in Diablo II can equip gear. Could we explore allowing warlock demon pets to equip gear as well, thereby enhancing their survivability and combat capabilities?This mechanic could offer the following benefits and challenges:

‌Benefits:‌
 1、Players could utilize diverse gear styles to create more varied pet builds, significantly enriching warlock demon gameplay and improving pet utility.
 2、A large amount of "useless equipment" generated after max level could be repurposed. Increased circulation of such gear would foster more player interaction，and make "useless gear" to be usefull.

‌Issues & Solutions:‌
 1‌、Balance Concerns:‌ Gear-equipped demon pets might disrupt game balance.
Solution: First, offset Demon power by nerfing existing demon talents. Second, restrict demons to equipping only Green-quality or lower gear to limit stat scaling.
‌2、The Gear Lost:‌ Players may hesitate to equip pets if gear is lost upon the pet’s death.
Solution: Return equipped items to the player’s bag upon demon death, similar to soul shards. If technically feasible, allow pets to auto-re-equip gear upon summoning (Diablo II-style).
‌3、Hunter Player Complaints:‌ "Should my pet also equip gear?"
Solution: Hunter pets (beasts) should not equip player gear. Their power remains tied to pet skills and Beast Mastery talents.

‌Additional Details:‌‌ Should gear-equipping be innate or tied to talents? Both options is OK. A 3/3 talent could unlock Green/Blue/Purple gear tiers, depending on balance needs.
