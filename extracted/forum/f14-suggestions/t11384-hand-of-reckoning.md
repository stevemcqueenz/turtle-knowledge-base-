# Hand of Reckoning

## Post 75273 by Mirick — 2023-11-01T16:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75273#p75273 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please consider adding the Paladin skill "Hand of Reckoning" to the game.  The current system of relying on Seal of Justice to taunt is not fun.  Not only are you forced to utilize a sub par seal (Which is probably the point) you are also forced to rely on your Judgement to initiate the taunt.  Judgement should be part of every paladins regular rotation so more times than not, it's going to be on CD.  This feels so inefficient and again...not fun.

Simply replacing improved Seal of Justice on the Talent tree with "Hand of Reckoning" would do wonders.  Give the skill the same chance to taunt 33/66/100 to avoid skill point excess.  I would happily spend the 3 talent points for "Improved Hand of Reckoning".  Spending 3 talent points on "Improved SoJ" feels bad.

Thank you for taking the time to read and consider this suggestion.
