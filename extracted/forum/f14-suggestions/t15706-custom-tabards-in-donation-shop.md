# Custom Tabards in Donation Shop

## Post 107174 by Wooglyboogly — 2024-11-08T22:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107174#p107174 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since there are tabards available in the donation store, it would be nice to have the option to create a custom tabard for cosmetic purposes   smiling_turtle  . This could either be a donation shop item or a vendor item.

The tabard customization could be the same as for guild tabard customizations. It would be nice to not have to join a guild for a custom tabard, and I also wouldn't want a guild name above me for other people.

If someone wanted to change their custom design afterward, the tabard could be "re-dyed" to change the customization of it (maybe costing gold or needing to go to a special location).
