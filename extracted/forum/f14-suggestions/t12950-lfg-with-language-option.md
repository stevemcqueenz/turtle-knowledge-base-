As you might have guessed, I'm one of those whispering types. Being quite an altoholic, I play lots of leveling dungeons and it's not that I assume anbodys spec, it's that I just don't care ;-). It's widely known that e.g. warriors of all specs can tank any leveling dungeons as long as they don't wear only rogue leathers and they keep a shield and 1-h weapon available. Same goes for Spriests with healing, enhancers, and so on.

I try to be polite about the whole thing, though. Just because we're playing an online game doesn't mean we don't need to be polite. Some form of greeting and full sentences with mostly correct punctuation and orthogaraphy as well as relevant information (e.g. "we can share quests" or "we have a mage for decursing and free drinks") make - in my opinion - a huge difference to "Yo, cum heal RFK". I woudn't respond positively to the latter, either. They way people epress themselves is one filter for me.

Character names are another filter and I simply don't bother with some players for varying reasons of my own. I can do this because I don't rely on an automated tool to group me up with random strangers and my dungeon experiences are mostly pleasant.

Finally, I'm not really concerned about pinpointing locations. My thought is mostly an extension of the concept that we should keep real life politics out of our gaming environment. Spoken language hints at ethnicity, cultural background and the political climate a person lives in. Not conclusively, but someone on the internet is always stupid enough to assume otherwise and this can lead to unnecessary friction, especially when it's pushed into peoples faces.
