# Alterac Valley fixes (Unbalanced atm)

## Post 54566 by Kriegbe — 2023-07-25T08:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54566#p54566 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

As it stands Alterac Valley is completely broken/unbalanced because of a number of things that could be easily fixed.

First problem : Alliance can backdoor like it's nothing. The Alliance can backdoor by doing an easy mount jump over a hill. This could easily be fixed by adding a fence or even an invisible wall and would solve a BIG problem that this bg has atm. I have seen games end under 20 minutes because they can use this backdoor strat (exploit??) while some wsg games even take longer. While you're at it also remove the horde backdoor (has no use anyways -> see third problem)

Second problem : Queue pops too soon. As it stands AV queues will almost insta pop for horde players, this is not a bad thing but Alliance spam this bg because of easy honor and we get a lot of games where the numbers are just unbalanced from the start untill more horde join. Sure we get a speed boost but that is worth nothing. Also what is happening is that we start games with under 20 players on both teams which makes it extremely boring / useless to play untill more players join because of the big map with a lot of objectives. AV was designed as a massive 40 man bg so why would you let a game start with 11 players... Please up the needed number of players before a queue can pop (30 min on both sides or just 40 like it was intended).

Third problem : Guards in Frostwolf keep vs Dun Baldar. What makes the Alliance backdoor effective and the horde not is that the horde has almost no guards at the Frostwolf relief hut graveyard and the alliance has A LOT of guards at the Stormpike aid station graveyard, I would suggest toning down the alliance guards or adding more horde guards in their bases because even without the backdoor the Alliance base is just 2 packed with guards vs the horde base on certain objectives. Or just remove / lower the respawn timers when you kill them so we can actually pvp in the base without grabbing aggro from 10 different npcs.

Hope you guys can review this and try to fix this bg, because atm I think Alliance win 95% of this bg (no joke).

Thanks for reading !
