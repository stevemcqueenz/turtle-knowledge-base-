# Togglable /PvPAll Mode

## Post 128968 by Mayson (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-29T10:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128968#p128968 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Core Suggestion:** Make the Gurubashi **neutral** PvP state toggable on the fly for everyone, like the normal faction-based one, with the command line "/PvA".

 **Rationale:** I play a few characters that are neutral or don't fit the mold of Alliance or Horde. And I'd love this option. I'm aware that this would increase the likelihood of getting ganked "unfairly", Warmode style. However I wouldn't mind taking a few Ls for the flair.

There is no need to alternate any NPC behavior or other systems. It is fully opt-in for outsiders. Players toggled with normal PvP and PvE cannot be attacked by **Gurubashi PvP players**. This means, any interaction with neutral PvP is decided by the non-PvP tagged players, giving them the opening move if they decide to do anything. I'd call this more than fair. There is zero risk of someone exploiting this to impact others in a harmful manner.

This could be the catalyst for a many fun moments and it can also be used in events among people who do RP-PvP. I look forward to opinions.
More options to engage, riskfree. What's not to love?
