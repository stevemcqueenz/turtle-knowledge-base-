There is a different underlying problem you are not taking into account and that explains why any cap won't work or be accepted from the dev team or the community.

 ****What is essentially missing on Turtle WoW is a way for Hardcore players to connect and team up easily.****

We have no LFG Tool we can use nor do we have a simple means to identify Hardcore players in /who. Meanwhile the Hardcore chat channel is so overcrowded one can hardly follow it.
This is one major reason it is so attractive for players to join a big mass HC guild which is clearly housing Hardcore players. It makes it easier to team up and often comes with additional perks.

It is an Oligopoly of a few big guilds by Design as the underlying structure encourages big groups forming.

I am also not so happy with this. Imho, what needs to change for other HC guilds to thrive equally like non HC is the way we can team up and communicate with one another as hardcore players. Currently, we can see mass guilds being somewhat of a workaround to a major shortcoming in the design how players can interact.

This is what needs to be addressed to push for a change in how many guilds are forming.
