Hello dear developers,

I'm eagerly awaiting the release of the updated Turtle game on UE, and in connection with this, I would like to suggest some improvements:

 1. Add a drop list for items as a separate button next to highlighted monsters. For example, there could be an icon on the left or right side that displays a bag when clicked, showing the list of items and their drop rates. This will enhance the gaming experience under the slogan “No more addons or websites, everything is at your fingertips in the new world.”
 2. Introduce player achievements similar to those found in the expansion pack "Wrath of the Lich King," where players earn points for completing tasks that can then be spent on cosmetic changes such as outfit modifications or acquiring vehicles and other non-balance-affecting items. This feature helps retain players longer in the game. Personally, I know over ten players who would love such a system.
 3. I'd also like to see new PvP armor sets whose stats are comparable to t3.5 armor sets. Perhaps you could introduce a dedicated open-world PvP location where players can fight each other while completing daily quests or simply battle for monster loot.
 4. Finally, please add summoning stones for dungeons and raids. This won't affect the spirit of Classic at all.
