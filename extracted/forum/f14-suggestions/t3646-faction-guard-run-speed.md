<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22068#p22068 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My suggestion is that they run normal speed.. but perhaps have like a 3 second sprint ability which makes them as fast as they currently are, but not permanently.

Seems a bit ridiculous that a faction guard can catch up to me in travel form...

Maybe it makes sense in Horde/Alliance controlled areas, like Ellwyn forest, or Crossroads.. But in contested areas like Hillsbrad, where i have to run past Tarren mill (as alliance) to head north for quests, it shouldn't be a thing...

I have died to Tarren mill death guards pretty much every time I've ran to kill ogres north of Tarren mill.. I give a wide berth, in travel form literally hugging the mountain. Normally, pulling aggro would be fine, they can chase me, i get away. No problems. But now that they run like hussain bolt, I have no chance.
