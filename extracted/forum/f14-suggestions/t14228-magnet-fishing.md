# Magnet Fishing

## Post 97013 by Ibux (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-06-14T17:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97013#p97013 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Reading about all the weird things people find with magnet fishing in reality made me think that it might also be of interest to players here.

Magnet fishing would involve players using a fishing pole attached with a magnet to fish up metal objects, treasures, and other unique items. Similar to how traditional fishing works but with different metallic rewards.

New item, Magnetic Lure, which players can attach to a fishing pole to start magnet fishing. This item could be crafted or purchased from specific vendors.

Items fished up through magnet fishing could be used in various professions. For example, blacksmiths could use metals to craft new items, or engineers could for unique gadgets.

Example Items:
Rusty Dagger: A low-level weapon that can be reforged.
Ancient Coin: A very rare item that starts a quest for a special reward.
Metal Scrap: A crafting material for blacksmithing.
Pirate's Booty: A chest containing random valuable items.
Valuable or junk items that can be sold or used in crafting.
Rare trinkets, weapons, and armor pieces.
