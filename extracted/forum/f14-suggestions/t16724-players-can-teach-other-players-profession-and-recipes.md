# Players can teach other players profession and recipes

## Post 114246 by Elenar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-25T14:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114246#p114246 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If they share same profession players could teach other players recipes, standard trainer price. Also could upgrade profession (to expert,master etc) or teach it if other character doesn't have it at all.
Reputation discount would apply depending on teacher's race.

Not sure about unique recipes learned from scrolls.

No, teacher fees is one of the few goldsinks the game has. Players teaching other player would cut that off completely.

There are a couple recepies like the goblin rocket fuel which can be teached from one player to another, but crucially it requires the use of some store bought reagents.
