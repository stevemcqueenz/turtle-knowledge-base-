# Add loot to World raid quest Bosses without loot

## Post 36949 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-03-06T09:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36949#p36949 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

"The calling" is a vanilla sillithus "world" boss summon quest that can be summoned at any time without the need of special mats or long respawn timers.
Blizzard originally decided to have no loot on these kind of bosses (compare Nathanos the blightcaller also no loot, thunderfurry wind elemt raid boss also no loot). The reward is only the qeust.

With ostarius on twow, we have diverged from this, since he too can now be summoned at any time and killed multiple times in a row which is confirmed not a bug (there used to be a cooldown on summon that is gone) . But unlike the other vanilla summon "the calling" he has loot additionally to completing his qeust.

Suggestion:
can we have lvl 60 blue loot on the calling and Nathanos too please? Would make it alot more intersting to do these again, after completing the quest and not just once per character and never again. This would also help alot in filling those raids if there are some intersting loot to convince people with.
