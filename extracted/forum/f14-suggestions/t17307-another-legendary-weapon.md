I would really like to see a legendary version of ironfoe, its hard enough to get it but then it gets outclassed by other endgame epics. My suggestion is to make a questline for it only obtainable for those who have the item. A legendary orb with low drop chance found inside a Dark iron ore vein which starts a quest. The quest obviously will require a lot of time and resources and a quest chain of at least 10 hard quests. The reward would be a legendary version of the hammer which gives double the proc chance it already has as well as adding increased dps, furthermore fury of forgewright would add 20 or so extra fire damage to all attacks for its duration.
