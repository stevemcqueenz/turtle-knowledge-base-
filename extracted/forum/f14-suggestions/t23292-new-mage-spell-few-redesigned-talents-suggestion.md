# New mage spell + few redesigned talents suggestion

## Post 162804 by Witchblade — 2025-12-14T19:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162804#p162804 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

hello guys, me and a couple others were brainstorming some ideas and we came up with the following

 **1. Arcane Ward: 920 absorb, 30 second cooldown, 5 ranks, follows the same scaling as frost/fire ward**

Mages get fire ward for bwl/mc, frost ward for naxxramas and i think it would be cool to follow this idea up with adding arcane ward for improved survivability on kara40 trash and bosses such as anomalus.

 **2. Reworking Wand Specialization: Successful wand attacks have a 100% chance to restore mana, but this counts as a wand "special" attack and will have a CD e.g 1st attack will grant mana, anything after that for 10 seconds will just be normal wand hits**

Wand spec is an absolutely garbage talent that has 0 use in raids, the proc rate isn't nice (tested by a large amount of experienced raiders) and even in levelling it isn't very good as most mages do not wand level, we are not priests, mages go deep frost for the ease of levelling, quality of life and AoE farming or they go arcane and nuke the target with rupture > missiles before it even reaches them, changing the talent to the proposed idea would also improve down time during levelling by granting mana on-the-go and would make this a much more desirable talent choice for a leveller too and could unlock new tech for raiders to learn as a mage.

 **3. Moving Improved Fire Ward in the fire tree to tier 1 row**

Improved Fire Ward has no place more than half way down the tree, acting as a tier 4 talent, it practically does the same but WORSE as Frost Warding which is a tier 1 frost tree talent and this would give arcane players something to take instead of a useless 5/5 fireball that they'll never use. You could also potentially look at reworking the functionality as reflecting is completely useless in PvE and even in PvP its extremely niche, could add extra damage it can absorb.

 **4. Adding more flavor to Magic Attunement by allowing mages to cast wards on raid/party members at 2x CD or 50% less efficiency**

Currently, Magic Attunement is not a fun talent to take, theres no fun in using it and is limited to extremely niche uses such as tanks on Patchwerk or melee on sartura, by adding my proposed idea this would breathe some life into this talent and could give mages some nice utility every player would appreciate. **To make up for this change the talent tier with improved mana shield could be swapped**
