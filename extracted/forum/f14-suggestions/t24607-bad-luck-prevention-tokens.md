# Bad Luck Prevention Tokens

## Post 173114 by Tanilion — 2026-03-30T05:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173114#p173114 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

My suggestion is the addition of tokens to raids that can then be turned in for gear from those raids, and they would work thus:
 - One Epic token per raid, per player, to be dropped from the final boss
 - One Legendary token per raid, to be dropped from the final boss
 - Tokens can only be used for the Epics or Legendaries from that raid
 - Epics and Legendaries would be priced by rarity and impact

This system would allow guilds to pool Legendary tokens to a chosen player, thereby guaranteeing that player their eventual legendary item without dramatically decreasing its rarity.

This system would also allow individual players to acquire Epic loot which refuses to drop for them.

Both of these forms of tokens would keep players feeling motivated, as there would be a visible indicator of progress being made toward a goal.

There is no good reason why players who complete the content regularly, week after week, month after month, should find it impossible to acquire certain pieces of gear simply due to bad luck.

 - - - - - - - - - - - -

Story time:
Story One: I run raids with two guilds. One has been running MC every week since October, and has yet to see a single Binding drop. The other guild has had five Bindings drop, and one or two Eyes of Sulfuras.

At this point, if each Binding took 12 full clears to earn via tokens, the former guild would only just this week have completed its first Thunderfury, while the latter guild would only have gone from five Bindings to seven, or to six and an extra Hand of Rag.

Story Two: My first level 60 character on Ambershire was a Hunter, specced Survival. I've had Deathbringer SRd for 18 weeks now, and have yet to see it drop a single time on either of my raid characters.

Worse than that, I haven't had even a single weapon I've had on SR on either of my raid characters drop in 24 weeks of raiding (24 for the Hunter; 8-10 weeks for my Shaman). It's not even a matter of losing rolls, they haven't even dropped. The only weapon rolls I've won on these characters have come on weapons I didn't have on SR, that literally no one else rolled against me for. Almost all of my gear comes from being the only SR on an item, or from gear dropping that no one else needs. The only notable exception I can recall would be my Band of Accuria, which I won with a high SR+ over two other players.

With Epics from raids available for a varying number of tokens based on rarity and power, I should imagine that after this many weeks of raiding Onyxia I would at least be able to purchase a Deathbringer from the raid vendor by now. And with newly released content for Ambershire, I would at least know that I was working toward eventually acquiring an upgrade.

 - - - - - - - - - - - -

Would I be here posting this suggestion without the above experiences? No. But even so, I would have welcomed the tokens I am suggesting. I have seen, all too often, players in Classic/Vanilla, TBC, and even WotLK get stuck in loot hell, where they're essentially permanently nerfed by bad luck. It's one of the least enjoyable aspects of this game, and Turtle possesses the means by which to address this longstanding issue.
