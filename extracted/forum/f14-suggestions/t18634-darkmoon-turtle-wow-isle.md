# Darkmoon Turtle WoW Isle

## Post 126851 by Siquexy — 2025-04-15T08:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126851#p126851 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings good people!

I love TWOW and there are almost no things I dislike about it. But on my adventures through the world, I can't look back and with sigh and smile remember Darkmoon Isle. You may might like it or hate it, but I think it would be nice to have version of it here in TWOW.

I always loved this Isle, as it was nice place where to take your friends to just chill or RP. The soundtrack is so great, awesome blend between sinister and jolly. The whole theme of park is great, right spot on for two dramatic masks, where one is joy and second sad.

Vanilla Darkmoon is bit bland, it's just turn in for repeatable quests and it lacks the atmosphere! In Darkmoon Isle I was able to spend whole evenings with friends and always couldn't wait when it opens it's gates again and we will uncover tiny details and mysteries hidden in it.

I don't mean to do exact port, it wouldn't fit the aesthetics of TWOW, devs have their own take on things and I love how they portray already established locations. But, would I would love to hope that in future patches we will see this dark and mysterious place to open it's gates for us with shooting fireworks on the sky and sinister looming around us.

And maybe we could also get **Inky Black Potion**?   turtle_in_love_head
