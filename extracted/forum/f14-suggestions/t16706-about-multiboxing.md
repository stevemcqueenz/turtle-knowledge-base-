In my opinion, a multiboxer should ask others if they are ok with that before the dungeon run starts, however, I can't imagine someone doing multiboxing in end-game content. It's ok in lower instances which are pretty easy.

But in general, I believe multiboxing should be allowed only for miscellaneous features like:
 1) Mage to open portals for your other alts
 2) Mage to make food/water for your alts
 3) A class with enchanting to let you enchant your other classes
 4) Warlock for summoning purposes
 5) etc.

Traveling in that game also takes a lot of time so for that reason I like to multibox. While I walk from Crossroads to Thunderbluff, during that time I can skill up some profession on another character.
