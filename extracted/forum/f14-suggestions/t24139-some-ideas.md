## Post 169782 by Lightburn — 2026-03-02T10:51:00Z

Okay, guys, this might be a really hard one to balance. This is a game design I was pondering for a while... With the release of custom content and hardcore challenges, this might be a valid one as well:

 - Item durability loss -

In a world where durability loss may matter more, finding a place to repair your shield or robe might be tricky... Some items are very rare, therefore it takes quite a bit of coin to repair it, or a long travel to find such a skilled craftsmen.

I would imagine a whole team working for quite some time to balance such a world and economy, one where durability loss is taken very-very seriously. It might go well with the rest of the challenge list out there, but I would imagine generating a separate realm for such a world...

Good job with your server and community here, it's quite a success!
