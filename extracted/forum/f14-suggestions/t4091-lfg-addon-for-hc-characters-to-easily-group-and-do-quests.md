As the title suggests why not create a similiar addon to current TWoW LFG but for HC characters to be able to queu and join in an easy way people of the same lvl gap to do quests or level together. Being the search paramaters that the players have to input the current zone their playing and type of questing being it general quests or an quik match up for elite quests, the addon will pair up through player input -> zone and type of quest and automatic level gap calculation.
