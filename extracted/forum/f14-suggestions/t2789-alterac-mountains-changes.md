# Alterac Mountains changes

## Post 17421 by Darklightening — 2022-03-06T21:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17421#p17421 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

i've come up with some changes for the Alterac mountions which include a faction base for both the Alliance and Horde, a new region within Alterac mountains and a new Battleground

new Faction bases- Windrunner outpost and Mage's Retreat
new Zone- the Blighted lands
new Battleground- Dungeons of Dalaran

Windrunner outpost- the Forsaken have turned their eyes toward Alterac Mountains and now seek to take the region and use it as a staging ground for further assaults on the Alliance lands below. to this end a Military outpost known as Windrunner outpost has been established

the Horde Quests for Alterac, strangbrad and the new region known as the Blighted Lands will be located here

Mage's retreat- the wizards of Dalaran have established a foothold here in order to try and retake the Alterac Mountains for Dalaran with support from the Alliance

the Wizards here offer the Alliance Quests for Alterac, strangbrad and the new region known as the Blighted Lands

new Alterac mountions zone- the Blighted Lands

located within Alterac Mountains to the North of the city of Dalaran which would be known as the Blighted Lands and will allow players to Quest within the zone seen during the warcraft 3 mission Under the Burning Sky where lord Archimonde first entered Azeroth following being summoned by the Scourge

the zone will be totally controlled by the Scourge Forces and will include 4 diffrent areas

Gate's ascent- a large Scourge citadel located within the center of the region where the Hill used to summon lord Archimonde is located.

the hill is surrounded on all sides by Scourge buildings and troops

Keal'mag Village, Magicbreeze Village and Mage's front

the three villages where the Forces of Dalaran fought to disrupt the summoning of Archimonde. each one of these villages lies in Ruins and
is controlled by the Scourge

i also came up with a new Battleground known as the dungons of Dalaran that were seen during the Blood elf campaign in warcraft 3

the Battleground pits the Alliance forces who are trying to Prevent the Forsaken from taking the dungeons and bypassing the dome that protects Dalaran

comments and suggestions are welcome
