# PVE raid weekly event

## Post 147394 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-21T00:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147394#p147394 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Every week the same raids. In some they dress up newbies, in other - dress up themselves. And then what? CD until next week.
I suggest weekly raid events like a competition.
The idea is taken from DeepRock Galactic - Deep dive and Elite Deep dive, and another low popular game.

"Nozdormu dormamu dragons need your help again. The dragons of time are tearing the fabric of time. You must quickly put an end to their crazy experiments. The opportunity to live, to exist further is already a great reward, but we will not remain in debt and will generously reward all of you"

 **Main idea:**
The essence of the weekly event is to clear all the bosses as quickly as possible.
 **Reward**. At the end of the week, the guild with the least time receives Gold in the GB.  (no, it won't ruin the economy, players could spend the same time farming lashers, gardening fishing, herbs, and potions are not for free)
You can go through it several times to achieve the best result (3 Reset is enough, I think).
 **Duration** +- for a 40 min - hour and a half. For raids 20ppl (15 min).
 **Two difficulties**. 1 - equivalent to the Temple of Ahnqiraj, 2 - equivalent to the Tower of Karazhan.
Instead of items, only materials drop (nexus crystal, various essences)

 **What will you have to go through? Several options:**
 1. The same raid dungeons, or big dungeons - but scaled to the appropriate difficulty.
 2. An arbitrary map / part of existing raids or large dungeons, with arbitrary mobs from different raids, with arbitrary bosses from different raids.
