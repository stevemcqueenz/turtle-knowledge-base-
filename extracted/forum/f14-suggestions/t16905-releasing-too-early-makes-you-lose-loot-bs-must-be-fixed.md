# 'Releasing too early makes you lose loot' BS must be fixed

## Post 115452 by Grinface — 2025-01-05T01:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115452#p115452 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I just ran through Stratholme UD for the Shadowchaft pants from the last boss (ofc it's the LAST boss) and the fight was going to the crapper. All of us except for one mage died, the boss disappeared during the fight, and I thought that was it and released my soul to run back to the instance. Only for the mage to miraculously survive and kill the boss and, of COURSE, my pants dropped but, guess what; I can't roll, let alone loot them. Because I released too early.

Needless to say, I'm upset that I'm have to continue farming these stupid ants for the Darkmantle set until they drop again. Why is this a thing? That you can't roll for loot from the boss even if you participated in the fight just because you released before the fight was over. Maybe it's a client limitation, I don't know. But I'd hope this mechanic, if you can call it that, is changed if not on this client at least on Unreal Engine 5, cos this is bullshit.
