I suspect the devs would be open to combining rods, but it's not an easy thing to do. The server-side approval is not the problem. The problem is that your 20 year old 1.12 client has to sign-off on you being able to cast the spell, before it will even send the cast to the server. And that Blizzard-made closed-source client uses a data structure where every spell has up to 2 tools (called "totems"). And either you have the item specified as a Totem, then you get to cast the spell. Or you don't have the item, then you don't. There's no room for "alternate tools". Only the 2.0 client has a new data structure that can store and check "TotemCategory" where multiple items can enable the same spell.

So the proper way of handling multiple rods would be to duplicate the enchanting spells for every rod. Then you would have 5 entries for "Enchant Bracer - Minor Health" in your enchanting window, each for a different rod, and you would have to manually pick the right one that matches the rod you carry. That's also kinda ugly, so I get why the devs don't do it.
