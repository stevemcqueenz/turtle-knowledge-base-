# Temporary Level Reduction for Questing/Dungeons with Friends or BGs

## Post 99790 by Mechsoap — 2024-07-18T15:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99790#p99790 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, i think there is sometimes a problem with trying to level with friends, or you get friends that come later to the game, its hard to be around the same level range, because of differences in time schedules or motivations to play wow.

I've often been in the situation where there comes a disreprancy between levels, and it becomes hard to do content again, without either waiting for however long on your main character, or alternatively using a lot more time to level a new character.

Sometimes you also have friends who start playing wow later, but you like your class and don't want to make a new of that class or similar. or you just want to run a dungeon at a lower level with your character with a guildie or friend, maybe even a bg at a lower bracket.

I would think it cool if turtle wow made it possible to temporarily de-level, here are some ideas about the mechanics of it:
You can only wear, consume, and use items and skills that someone in that level bracket can use, so you start with taking off all your gear, then de-level, and then you can equip level appropriate gear.
Buffs and similar from higher level potions ought to be dispelled through it, or to simplify it, all buffs are dispelled upon doing it.
You're locked out of using skills or ranks of skills of a higher level than you have, melee classes would automatically have them de-ranked while casters would have to adjust a bit.
Your talents are rolled back in the order you selected them to fit with the talent points available at that level.

I think that would be a cool idea to create a way to run especially mid-level dungeons or bgs. i would also suggest restrictions like so:
You can not gain experience points or honor points that count towards your normal level or similar, so you can't delevel and get xp from lower level mobs.
Quest xp would still go towards it as turtle wow iirc removed the xp decay of doing old quests, so that wouldn't matter much, it can be debatable if one ought to be given honor for completing bgs or be able to get marks of honor from bgs.

Maybe this is not possible on the current engine, but maybe it could be possible on the upcoming unreal one, i think though this would fit well in the spirit of turtle wow of going slow and steady, enjoying lower level zones for what they can offer and their challenges without much reward.

I don't think personally it would discourage levelling new characters, as i don't think many people level new characters solely to do a mid-level dungeon with a friend, then never play it again. Maybe twinks would be less levelled, though i dunno if the twinking community is large or if the wider community would particularly suffer from this. in that at least there could be more people in bgs at lower brackets that are not only there while levelling or a handful of twinks.

Thats all, thanks for reading.
