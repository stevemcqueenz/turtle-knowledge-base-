# Spammable slow get nerfed and I like that

## Post 103997 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-20T16:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103997#p103997 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[Free Action Potion](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=5634)
> Free Action Potion
>  Requires Level 20
>  Use: Makes you immune to Stun and Movement Impairing effects for the next 30 sec. Does not remove effects already on the imbiber.

Before the patch 1.17.2, this potion was the only way to counter enemy slow for every class. Other way was to be paladin or to have pocket paladin, which is not the case for horde.

Typical situation in WSG. You pick the flag and run. Some warrior charges you or some hunter shots you with Concussive shot and come close. What they will do next? Hamstring or Wing clip.
How would you counter that?
Blessing of freedom and Free action potion.

As a tauren I could slow attacker back, warstomp, trinket and run away. But that option is not for every warrior or hunter.
As a warrior I can bloodrange+disarm opponent on contact (to prevent him to use slow) and then slow him down.

Warriors/Hunters trinket is useless because the second you used it, that hunter/warrior attack you with slow again.
Druid shapeshift was not very useful too. If you just shift out and shift in, that warrior/hunter will apply slow again.
If you stun and shift in shift out, then you get another charge one second later.
If you use talented Nature grasp before shifting, then they just trinket it out and keep following you.
High parry/dodge chance won't help. In next GCD I will be slowed again.

I personally followed a druid which used almost every trick he had to escape and druid failed to escape. All I needed to do was to spam Hamstring and occasionaly charge/intercept him.

If I see enemy warrior charging on me, I was 100% sure that I will be slowed.

How it will look like after the patch?
First of all, I can parry/dodge. It means that rogues could use Elusiveness. Hunters could use Aspect of the Monkey. High avoidance stats will help you *with some chance* not to get slowed in first contact.

Second, I will be able to trinket out of slow and slow attacker back. I may succeed or may not. But that option exists and for 6 seconds I could use something else.
I should not be tauren to make use of my anti-slow trinket.

War stomp got nerfed, so I am not sure if I will be able to do this trick after the patch.
Still I will be able to disarm+slow to prevent.

In conclusion. CD on hamstring/wing clip is good thing because it open more options to deal with slow.
