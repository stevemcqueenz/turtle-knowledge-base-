## Post 169996 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-03-03T23:47:00Z

Calling consumes pay to win on a server where RMT is a bannable offense and nothing from the shop offers you direct power over other players is... A little silly, if we're putting it lightly.

Anyways, no. I'm not really into the idea of nerfing/removing the use of consumes. They're an integral part of vanilla gameplay. Stacking 10 different buffs from potions, food, class buffs, scrolls, chugging potions that make you invulnerable or immune to entire methods of crowd control, throwing stun grenades that turn the tide of battle, chugging chili that makes you breathe fire, etc. etc. so on and so forth... It's part of that old RPG charm that I feel like a lot of modern games lack. It all more or less just gets boiled down to: Health potion, mana potion, melee dps buff elixir, magic dps buff elixir, tank buff elixir, healer buff elixir, and maybe a food option for those roles if you're lucky.

I'm aware half of this tangent really isn't necessary or even relevant but I feel like the spirit of the game itself should probably be taken into consideration. Classic is a very "just a bunch of dudes making cool shit" kind of game when it comes to consumables, because like... There was a lot of cool shit. Sometimes it's not very cool to be on the receiving end, but it definitely feels cool to drink a LIP, I'll tell ya that.

Also yeah a magic damage equivalent to LIP would be cool. Let's make one of those instead.
