# More options to hide certain slots for Transmog

## Post 23218 by Vyntoras — 2022-08-23T13:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23218#p23218 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yo, Turtles.

Today I'm just wanting to drop the idea of potentially adding more items than just the Hidden Shoulders. I, personally, find it quite troublesome to sometimes find fitting Bracers/Gloves/Belts/Shoes and wouldn't mind building a set around a character not wearing any or some of these. It also gives the player more options to work with!

Night Elves are, for example, a race that is often depicted without shoes, yet there isn't an option to just hide those. Belts and Wrists can sometimes make building sets really tough unless you manage to farm every single fitting piece so you don't look strange, etc.
