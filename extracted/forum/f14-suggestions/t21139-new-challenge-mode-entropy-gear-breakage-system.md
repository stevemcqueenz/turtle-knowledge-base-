# New challenge mode: Entropy (gear breakage system)

## Post 146884 by Froggystyle — 2025-08-19T01:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146884#p146884 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In this mode, gear doesn’t last forever. 

Rules:
- Every repair slightly lowers an item’s maximum durability.
- Bigger repairs lowers item's maximum durability more.
- High-end gear wears faster (because they are so finely crafted at the time of creation, ordinary repairers can't keep the item's durability up as high--maybe there can be high level repairers who can make repairs with less maximum durability loss)
- When maximum durability reaches zero, the item is broken.
- In rare cases, an item can break before reaching 0 maximum durability during extreme conditions (e.g., a crit from a boss mechanic, environmental hazard, etc).
- Every raid should result in almost all gear needing severe repair. Some dungeons, world bosses, skull level mobs may also be hard hitting.
Why play this mode:
- Keeps all levels of gear valuable, not just BiS.
- Creates a more active, ongoing economy for crafters and traders.
- Adds a strategic layer of resource management.
Expected impact:
- Entropy should be tuned so most raid groups participating in the challenge should see about one piece of gear break per run on average.
- Smart upkeep and strategic swapping can extend an item’s lifespan.
- Almost every dungeon/raid drop and craftable weapons/armor gain use value.
- Role balance consideration: threat-generating stances/forms like Defensive Stance, Righteous Fury, or Bear Form provide a buff to gear durability to accomodate tank roles
Possible rewards:
- Leveling: +20% xp gain from combat
- Lvl 60: +20% rep gain from all sources
