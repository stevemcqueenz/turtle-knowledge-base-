# Rework of Searing Light talent

## Post 133702 by Erousagi — 2025-05-30T18:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133702#p133702 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Despite insta cast is cool and feeling good, Searing Light is hard to almost impossible to balance with it and I think for greater good it needs to be redesigned.

The adding of ICD is justified, ppl who played back then know how stupid op it was in PVP, however the nerf of mana reducing doesn't make any sense, Disc already had mana issue in any PVE fight slightly longer before the nerf and any AOE would deplete your mana pool easily. For comparison, clearcasting for shaman is -60% and mage is -100% mana cost while Searing Light is clownlike -20% and only apply to Smite.

Damage wise, a Searing Light proc can be roughly seen as ~33% dps increasing for your next smite however due to the icd, the benefit is discarded for chain crits, and due to the limit of gcd, it also neglects any bonus from haste.

Since the design philosophy for Disc is incentive around crit, the easy solution is just to give it a +100% crit dmg talent like many other specs but that's boring and doesn't really touch the mana issue. I'd suggest to redesign Searing Light, remove the instant cast part and make it a stackable buff that each stack increases crit dmg and reduces mana cost of all Holy and Disc spell. The values will need to be tested but the general idea is to remove the slot machine and make it a buff needs to be carefully maintained, and by doing so both crit and haste are beneficial to help the uptime, and to address the mana issue for both single target and aoe scenarios.
