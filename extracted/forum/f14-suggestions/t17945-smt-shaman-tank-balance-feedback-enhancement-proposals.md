# SMT (Shaman Tank) Balance Feedback & Enhancement Proposals

## Post 122228 by Lanning2023 — 2025-03-11T05:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122228#p122228 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle WoW Developers,

As a dedicated Shaman Tank (SMT) on the Asian server, I’ve successfully led raids through Naxxramas and Emerald Dream speedrun challenges with my guild **<North & South>**. While SMT is a thrilling role, I’d like to respectfully propose adjustments to address current limitations:

 ---

### **1. Survivability Issues**
 **Current Challenges**:
 - Compared to Paladins, Warriors, and Druids, Shamans lack reliable **active mitigation tools**.
 - In critical situations, we can only rely on **Stoneclaw Totem** (easily destroyed) or **Earth Shield** (limited absorption), leaving SMT overly dependent on healers during high-damage phases.

 **Proposed Solution**:
 - **New Talent: Enhanced Earth Shield**
 - **Effect**: Extend Earth Shield’s CD to 10 minutes.
 - **Damage Reduction**: Earth Shield reduces incoming damage by 30% when triggered.
 - **Synergy Bonus**: If triggered by **Lightning Strike**, increase its healing effect by 500%.
 - **Design Goal**: Provide burst survivability without overshadowing other tanks’ uniqueness.

 ---

### **2. Gear Availability Constraints**
 **Current Challenges**:
 - **Missing Tank Gear Slots**: Wrist, Hands, and Waist slots lack viable mail defense gear.
 - The only SMT waist item (*Stoneplate Waistguard* from world bosses) has a **4% drop rate**, creating excessive RNG dependency.
 - **Stat Balance**: To reach **400 Defense Rating**, SMT must sacrifice Stamina, resulting in only ~7,500 HP (below 40-man raid standards).

 **Proposed Solutions**:
 - **A. Introduce New Mail Defense Gear**:
 - Add Stamina/Defense Rating-focused items for Wrist, Hands, and Waist slots (e.g., crafted *Earthwarder Gauntlets* or raid drops).
 - Suggested Sources: Crafting recipes (Blacksmithing/Leatherworking) or mid-tier raid bosses (e.g., updated Zul’Gurub).
 - **B. Defensive Totem Additions**:
 - New totems providing raid-wide Defense Rating or Stamina buffs (e.g., *Totem of Unyielding Earth*).

 ---

### **3. Long-Term Vision**
Balancing SMT would:
 - Encourage more players to explore hybrid specs, enriching class diversity.
 - Align with Turtle WoW’s philosophy of empowering underutilized playstyles.

 ---

### **Closing**
Thank you for your incredible work in expanding the Vanilla experience! I’m passionate about perfecting the SMT role and would gladly provide further testing/data if needed.

Best regards,
 **Sweetheart Milk Bunny (甜心奶兔酱)**
 **Server**: Turtle WoW Asia
 **Guild**: North & South (南北)
