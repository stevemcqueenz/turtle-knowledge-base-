# New lore Historical Quest chain

## Post 14955 by Robertscotty — 2021-12-28T21:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14955#p14955 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it would be cool to have a caverns of time Quest chain that will teach new players who might not have played any of the stand alone games the History of Warcraft through a series of dungeons

The dungeons could include some that already exist in retail wow as well as ones that weren't in world of Warcraft based on historical events in the games

Below is a list of all the dungeons included

 1. Horde siege of Shattrath
 2. Horde Siege of Karabor
 3. Opening of the Dark portal
 4. Horde siege of Stormwind keep
 5. Culling of Stratholme
 6. Scourge Attack on Silvermoon city
 7. Siege of Dalaran
 8. Siege of the burrow deeps- where Tyrande freed Illidan stormrange during the third war
 9. Battle for the Skull of Gul'dan- where Illidan consumed the Skull of gul'dan
 10. Battle of Mount hyjal

Each Dungeon will have a Quest requireing players to go back in time and participate in the battle

Completing all the dungeons will reward the player with a new title called Historian of Azeroth
