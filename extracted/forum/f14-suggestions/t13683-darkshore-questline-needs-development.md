# Darkshore questline needs development

## Post 93019 by Rurin — 2024-04-18T11:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93019#p93019 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of Turtle WoW's fundamental goals is expanding questlines that Blizzard never bothered to finish. Some great examples of this we can see in Ashenvale and Dustwallow.

Yet, Darkshore also has such questline and yet I see no talk about it. It would really love some development.

The iconic white-dressed night elf Gwennyth Bly'Leggonde is involved in total of 11 quests regarding the investigation of beached sea creatures on the zone's shores. Almost everybody completes them, as they are basically free exp by simply clicking on these dead creatures on the shores.

However... the investigation doesn't provide any answer. It's only suggested that naga are involved, "possibly" pushing the creatures south to unknown ends. The quest giver says the night elves will analyze the data you provided... but nothing ever comes of it. Not a single further quest, or even a mention in any other zone.

I think this is a perfect place to further expand on the game's lore. Just a suggestion :)
