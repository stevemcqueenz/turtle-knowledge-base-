# End-game Guild PvPvE content: Add more viable "Raid-style" open world bosses

## Post 75228 by Stinkethrateth — 2023-11-01T13:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75228#p75228 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey folks,

This is for all my PvPers out there that love the PvPvE contesting.

This was a major feature in Aion, another MMO that I loved at endgame (not so much the levelling)

Hear me out...I know there are a few Open World Bosses at the end game but i feel they get overshadowed by better items from raids etc.

The idea is to have world bosses that spawn on a timer that gives Guilds another activity to compete for outside of Raid content, providing some liveliness to the world and better interaction between factions.

Idea:
 - World Bosses on a timer with interesting mechanics - think outdoor raid boss
 - Put them in a zone that has chokes and bottlenecks, where guilds have to strategise to access it, and there are trash mobs etc that need to be killed before you can get to it
 - Make the items viable for end-game - unlike the world bosses atm which I believe after a few raid releases become obsolete (or maybe just buff their items and abilities?)

What do you guys think?

I know one concern will be faction balance, and that's fine, people will always whine about faction balance you can't do anything about faction balance lol - the only thing you can do is add content that motivates those still active in a faction to seek out that content.

I think it will require some careful balancing so that faction imbalance doesn't hurt one faction too much, but I don't see this as a blocker.

Let me know thoughts!!
