# On ninja looting

## Post 119367 by Greenaniline — 2025-02-08T20:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119367#p119367 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Having gotten ninja looted in my first ever raid on vanilla wow server, I propose the following system for punishing ninja looters I have seen on a server which I won't name.

Make it possible to report ninja looters (perhaps via discord). If a player is identified as a ninja looter, whenever they will join a group, the players will be announced by the system that a known ninja looter has entered the group. I believe it went something like "Warning: Player X is known to be a ninja looter. Proceed at your own risk.".

I assume this won't be popular due to increased GM demand. However, it is a method that works and won't have the playerbase lose their shit about it. On repeated offenses the player is banned. (Also, the ninja'd item is restituted to it's deserving owner. I know GM's don't want to get involved with player items, so consider this optional.)
