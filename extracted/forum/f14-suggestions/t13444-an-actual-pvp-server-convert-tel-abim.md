# An actual PvP server - convert Tel'Abim

## Post 90651 by Spacew0lf — 2024-03-19T10:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90651#p90651 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I could see a future for Telabim if they basically eliminate leveling (start at 60), and give you starter competitive gear and play at 60, instantly being able to roam the world with a decent chance at survival, with capturable towers/towns and other world pvp objectives. Imagine a PvP server that focuses on making PvP accessible and semi balanced from gear perspective, then the low population matters less.

Turn Tel'Abim into a real PvP experience, get rid of the leveling and massive gear grinds to be competitive, keep some end game gearing / transmog rewards for some motivation, but not such a gear gap that makes PvP pointless for the new and ungeared. True PvP experience, thoughts?

## Post 90652 by Spacew0lf — 2024-03-19T10:47:00Z

Towers could be capturable with some method as simple as arathi basin or warsong gulch flag method.

Rewards could be something as simple as a counter tallying up how many tower/town captures you were a part of (in the same party or raid as capturer).
Like the honorable kill counter.
Maybe too ambitious short term, but instant and decent geared 60's option would go a long way to increasing population for battlegrounds and world pvp raids/defence. Tricky problem, but one that could show MMO giants a better way to treat PvP respectably, while keeping the world vibe that games like LoL, HotS miss out on.
