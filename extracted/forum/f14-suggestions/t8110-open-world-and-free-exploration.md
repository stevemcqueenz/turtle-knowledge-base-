# Open World and Free Exploration

## Post 52162 by Weichenxi911 — 2023-07-03T09:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52162#p52162 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle World of Warcraft Development Team

First of all, thank you for creating Turtle World of Warcraft, which has brought me a lot of happiness. Then, as a player from China who has been playing this game continuously for 15 years, I would like to offer some suggestions about World of Warcraft. I believe that World of Warcraft should be a slow paced open world exploration game, where every player can enjoy the vast continent of Azeroth, whether they are a fisherman, miner, doctor, or blacksmith. However, with the continuous updates of official servers, these things have basically disappeared without a trace. In response to these issues, I think I have some ideas to improve.

 1. The design of the existing flying point reduces the time players spend exploring the map. Imagine using a carriage instead of a griffin, when players move from one camp to another, they will encounter attacks from bandits and monsters. This requires players on the carriage to work together to repel these attacks.

 2. The territory being contested is a very important setting in World of Warcraft, perhaps you can refer to the competition setting in another game, "Planet Margin". The bulletin board in the main city should be a refresh point for real-time missions, as the alliance and tribe are at war, which puts some campsites in danger. The king or chieftain should issue a notice to look for warriors to support these campsites. This method can stimulate players' enthusiasm and allow more players to be online at any time to protect these campsites in the midst of war.

 3. Guilds should take on more player social tasks, such as some difficult guild tasks, where the chairman receives tasks from the king, such as escorting the king's supplies, causing trouble in enemy camps, or donating materials within a specified time limit. Completing these tasks can earn some reputation rewards for the union.

 4. Increase the playability of exploration, such as placing some items in hidden corners of the world. Of course, these items are floating and if not found for a period of time, they will disappear. However, when adventurers find these items, they can apply for a reward from the Explorer Association. When players find these props, they will bring endless surprises to them.
