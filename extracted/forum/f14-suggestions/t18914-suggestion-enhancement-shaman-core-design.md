# Suggestion: Enhancement Shaman Core Design

## Post 128668 by Johnkw — 2025-04-27T08:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128668#p128668 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To the Turtle WoW Dev Team:
Thank you for continuously adjusting class designs to bring them closer to their intended identity. Since Patch 1.172, Shamans—especially Enhancement—have seen significant improvements, moving closer to the "melee spellcaster" archetype. However, it still feels like some significant step is missing.

Looking back at Blizzard's adjustments, until WLK, the spell and physical scaling were unified, finally realizing the Shaman's true potential. While Blizzard's direction was correct, their adjustment was overly complex and balance implications.

Proposed Adjustments:

Class Identity:
Enhancement Shamans should lean more toward being a spellcasters,even as melee DPS or tanks, rather than purely physical attackers.

Weapon Enhancements Scaling:Rockbiter Weapon and  Windfury Weapon should scale with  Nature Spell Power while retaining their  physical damage type.
 1、Rockbiter's AP bonus scales with Nature Spell Power.
 2、Windfury's AP bonus scales with Nature Spell Power.
(This maintains physical damage but rewards spell-focused gearing.)

Hit Mechanics:
Modify Elemental Devastation (Elemental Talent) to:
+3% Spell Hit, and convert Spell Hit into Physical Hit

Critical Strike Mechanics:
Rework thundering Strikes (Enhancement Talent) to:
Merge Spell Crit into Physical Crit (Total Physical Crit = Spell Crit + Base Physical Crit).
Remove the flat +5% Physical Crit bonus (to account for baseline Agility/Intellect, gear stats, and hybrid itemization).

at last for Shaman Tank:
Modify Shield Specialization to:
the amount blocked by shield scales with Nature Spell Power, and keep increase the chance to block by 5%.
Modify Ancestral Guardian to:
the armor value increased scales with Nature Spell Power.

Design Philosophy:
Enhancement Shaman, as the name suggests, refers to using spell power to strengthen items such as damage and armor. This aligns with the game mechanics  before vanilla level 60 raid content.
These changes aim for minimal disruption to core mechanics while refining Enhancement's hybrid nature.
Feedback is welcome.

1) Windfury is a problematic ability to begin with because of its burst dmg. Attempting to improve Shaman dps through this ability will result in uninteractive one shots while simultaneously having minor improvement to dps overall because WF is a chance proc. For the change you proposed to be remotely balanced, its base value would have to be nerfed, and ultimately scale back to its current value more or less, and be adjusted everytime new gear is added. Its simply adding complexity where there needn't be any.

 2) I like the idea of Elemental Devastation's proc granting you % hit based % spell hit, but I will always be in favor of this talent being reverted to grant crit instead of hit chance. A part of endgame progression is based around getting hit chance to increase dps on mobs 2-3 levels higher than you. Having hit chance innate in the talents circumvents this pve progression and feels counter-intuitive to preserving the classic design.

 3) I think they can help fill in the spellhance fantasy by implementing a mini-maelstrom effect instead. Maybe make Lightning Strike instead of just proc a lightning orb on hit, it allows you to instantly cast a half-dmg chain lightning at the cost of 3 charges of lightning shield.

Current Problems:
Pure Physical Playstyle:Feels like a "dumb hammer-swinging simulator" with almost no incentive to cast spells.
Lost Identity:Lacks the hybrid "elemental warrior" fantasy—mostly just auto-attacking with Stormstrike.

Proposed Changes & Benefits:
Spellpower Scaling:
Lightning Bolt, Healing wave, and other spells now benefit from Spellpower without sacrificing physical damage.
Maintains current melee strength while adding  versatility

Tactical Flexibility:
Ranged Situations: Use Lightning Bolt when melee isn't viable.
PVP/Solo Content: More self-sustain with stronger heals.
Hybrid Utility:Can contribute emergency heals in raids.

Philosophy Behind the Changes:
This isn't about making Enhancement overpowered,it's about restoring its hybrid roots.The spec should feel like a melee shaman,not just a warrior with totems.
