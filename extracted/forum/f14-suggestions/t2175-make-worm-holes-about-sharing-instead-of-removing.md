# Make Worm-holes about sharing instead of removing!

## Post 13320 by Ramue — 2021-10-29T22:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13320#p13320 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the removal of certain modes of transport in the coming patch I feel the one that has the most potential to be a benefit to the culture of TWoW is the Wormhole.
I know exploration and being in the world is the direction turtle wow desires to pursue.
However one of the things the server also bills itself on is sharing and a extremely friendly atmosphere.
So what I think should be done with wormholes is make them more of a gesture of sharing rather than spammable personal teleports.

So what I purpose to make the worm Holes more about sharing rather than spammable transport is a few things:
 - Give the wormhole a much longer CD.
 -make the CD to generate new WHs trigger not only when you cast a worm hole, but also when you just use one someone else casts.
 -The cool down however should not be extended if you enter other people's wormholes while yours is already on CD.

This makes it so that a wormhole is a very special thing and something you will want to share with others as to help anyone else that is still on CD.
But also de-incentives frivolous travel and just using them on a whim, thus keeping more people traveling in the world.

That is just my idea on the topic, thank you for reading! I would love to hear what anyone thinks can be done to "fix" the worm holes rather than scrapping the idea. ^^
Hope you are having a nice day! <3
