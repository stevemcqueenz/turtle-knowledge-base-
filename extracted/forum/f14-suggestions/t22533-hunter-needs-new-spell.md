Less is more. Just because marksman has no relevant dot doesnt mean he has to.

The marksman playstile is all about weaving spells with autohits. as such, any spell for hunter has to work within the confines of attackspeed. A dot on the otherhand demands you keep it up perfectly to maximize its damage. These two concepts clash and the shorter the dots duration is, the worse it gets. Lets say i have a 2.5 second attackspeed thanks to haste and i am weaving a 6 second dot in. I am consistently either loosing 1-2 seconds of the dots duration due to applying early, or i wait for it to expire proper and loose an auto attack or wait another second or two beforei can reapply it.

In both cases i loose a significant portion of the dots damage.

A 6 second dot on hunter is absolutely cancerous for gearing reasons as you need specific setups of weapon and haste gear or you loose a lot of its power leading to a tightrope walk for balanace at best and toxicity around hunterdrops at worst

Having more spells than just steady shot all the time would be great, but this one might as well just be a steady shot cast instead
