# Game mode which turns off Mob XP?

## Post 40869 by Clintonm — 2023-04-09T08:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40869#p40869 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Playing through on classic, I noticed, even when not grinding mobs (i.e. just killing ones required for quests) I was almost always 5-6 levels above the quests I was doing, with lots of quests left over at level 60.

I understand combining "slow and steady" and the "glyph of exhaustion" effectively reduces mob XP by 75% (as I generally would quest rested as I'm a fairly causal player), but I'd prefer a simple 0 mob XP along but with no death penalty that comes with "slow and steady".

Considering Turtle Wow comes with extra quests I would _think_ reaching 60 without mob XP could be doable.

I could emulate this mode by turning off XP except when handing in quests, but this would also turn off exploration XP (which I'd rather not do) and I think I'd find it annoying without hacking a mod to do it automatically (if that's even possible).

Could something like this be added? In this thread [viewtopic.php?f=3&t=6491](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=3&t=6491) where I asked whether this feature was already in the game, *Maesus* suggested a glyph of *Slower & Steadier*, which I think is a cool name. But even a variation of on the "Slow and steady" game mode, reducing mob XP to 0 but removing the death penalty, could work well.
