# Changes I'd like to see for the Hunter Class [BM Version - Part 2]

## Post 71980 by Steelx — 2023-10-18T19:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71980#p71980 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My intention with this post is not to pretend that every spec in every class should be a viable raiding spec or a  "class within a class" of it's own.

I understand that the vanilla feel of classes is something very unique and that it must be preserved at every cost. And that any changes made to any given class must fall under this line of development, first respecting the Lore elements of the Class and The World around it as well as respecting it's own vanilla feel.

Having said this, over the years players have figured out the most efficient way of playing almost every class. Along with items, talents, what abilities to use and when. With this knowledge at hand and it being so widely spread, a player might feel that classes don't need "fixing" and that they already fulfill their niche within the game and that the game can be "beaten" without the need for "changes".

However by doing this, we fail to realize all the potential that's been left behind with many classes. Things that weren't developed to their ideal potential back in the day. Such is the case for a couple talents that I want to present you as having an unexploited potential and that in their current state are widely overlooked and discarded as useless or too weak to invest in.

The first talent I want to bring light is "Bestial Discipline"

At rank 2, this talent
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/MyYkEpH.png)
(reads: "Increases the focus regeneration of your pets by 20%.")
At first glance it doesn't seem that bad, after all 20% is a pretty big number.

First we have to evaluate what is the base focus regeneration of hunter pets.
And that would be: 25 focus every 5s, therefore an increase of 20% would signify 5+ focus every 5 seconds.
Still not that bad? Let's do some more math.
Let's take for instance the most common pet skill (apart from growl): Claw.

- Claw costs 25 focus, and at lvl 57 a hunter is able to learn Rank 8 Claw, which does 43-59 damage before damage reduction by armor.

- Let's say it does an average of 51 damage per cast on a target with no armor and that can't parry nor dodge and can't be missed against.

- After taking 2/2 talent points into Bestial Discipline, a player would get an extra cast of Claw every 25 seconds. (+5 focus every 5 seconds *5 = +25 focus).

- An extra cast of Rank 8 Claw at lvl 57 would signify (51dmg / 25s= 2.04 dps) a whooping 2.04 dps increase in a best case scenario where every cast of Claw lands and the target has no armor.

- Doing this same calculation, on a Wind Serpent with rank 6 Lightning Breath and taking 2/2 Bestial discipline: The dps increase would be (106dmg / 50s = 2.12dps) a 2.12dps increase.

It doesn't matter how you look at it, this talent is no good. Even for aggro an extra cast of Growl every 15 seconds is nothing.

What solution do I propose? Well, I have a couple.

Let's start with the simplest one.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/7To9uAP.png)
(reads: Bestial Discipline Rank 2: "Increases your pets' focus by 50 and increases their focus regeneration by 100%.")

I call this simple because it's a number buff, it doesn't imply a significant gameplay shift from the current state, pets simply will have more resources. The numbers are not arbitrary, it is thought so that at rank 2 of Bestial Discipline a pet would have 2 extra casts of Claw initially or 1 extra cast of Lightning Breath initially before going "oof", and would get 2 casts of Claw instead of one every "tick" of focus and 1 cast of Lightning Breath every tick instead of every 2 ticks.

The second one that occurred to me, and that is a bit more complicated is inspired by the talent added in The Burning Crusade expansion "Go for the Throat". This talent regenerates 25 focus for your pet every time you would get a critical strike with ranged attacks. I changed it a bit to fit more the BM tree and it would look like this:

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/UeibC2X.png)
(reads: Bestial Discipline Rank 2 "Increases pet focus by 50 and your melee and ranged attacks have a 30% chance to regenerate 25 focus for your pet.")

This second alternative came to me after taking into account the playstyle of Hunters that go deep into the BM tree both while leveling and in PvP. They prefer a faster weapon speed as it makes harder to take aggro from the pet by an unexpected big critical strike and to interrupt casters in PvP as much as possible. And I think that is good that faster weapons have a place in WoW and be encouraged to use.

I understand that both of these "solutions" don't mean a lot either when it comes to a pure DPS perspective. Still most damage from pets comes from normal "white" attacks and Pet damage is only relevant in PvP at early stages of the game when people don't have gear, and then it's downhill from there. That pets start to become just a nuisance that's easy to deal with in PvP by your enemies, and often a reason for terrible accidents in dungeons and raids where hunters are often requested to dismiss their pet in order to avoid such accidents.

But, as I said at the beginning, my idea is not to hugely buff pets' damage or to make BM hunter a class of it's own or to make BM hunter "the perfect raiding spec", simply to improve on the class parting from what we have now and to make justice to what certain talents were intended for. In simpler words to polish the class. That is all.
