# "RP"/Empty Mode Dungeons

## Post 147387 by Serijas — 2025-08-20T23:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147387#p147387 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Could it be possible to set up a Mode (Like would with heroic etc over your character's frame) that makes it so that the leader of a group/raid can convert a Dungeon into an empty place to RP in? There are a lot of beautiful spots that need you to clear out the space at first to get to it. You can just "skip" that part and use a dungeon for example as a Guild Headquarter etc.
