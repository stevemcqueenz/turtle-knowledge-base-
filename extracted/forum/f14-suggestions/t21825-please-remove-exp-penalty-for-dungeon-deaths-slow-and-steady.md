# Please remove EXP penalty for dungeon deaths (Slow and Steady)

## Post 151141 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-10T14:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151141#p151141 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Now that we have 50% exp rates for mob kills in dungeons, the 5% exp loss in dungeons makes them too risky for slow and steady players.  Too many times, I've had tanks that pull ridiculous amounts of mobs, then I die and lose 5%, or a healer doesn't heal me and I die and lose 5%.
To make it worse, when I die, and the group quits so that we don't finish the dungeon, I don't get to regain that lost exp.
As well, when the group is starting to be overwhelmed, I'm stuck in a difficult position: Do I run now while I have the chance, and screw them over? Or do I stay and help because we might scrape by and kill this mob pull?  There shouldn't be an incentive for any player to abandon there party early, but there is now.

Out in the overworld, where a death is predominantly your fault,  I agree with the exp penalty for slow and steady players who get themselves killed; however, instances have too much room for chaos.  There shouldn't be a factor discouraging people from doing instances, but now there is.  I'd rather just solo level as slow and steady than take the risk of instances.
