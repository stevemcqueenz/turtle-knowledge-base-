I have a suggestion for a slight update to the race icons. I have done some small changes and made a new high elf male portrait based on the blood elf one, but tried to adjust the smirk and some edits to make the look more in line with the rest of the portraits (less details etc.).

 **Changes**

 **Dwarf Female**
 - Adjustments to nose and shadows

 **Gnome Female**
 - Adjustments to silhouette and shadows

 **Goblin Male**
 - Adjustments to the leather clothes to be brown, rather than red. New nose, teeth and eyes to be closer to the in-game model, but still respect the style of the icons.

 **High Elf Female**
 - Adjustments to eyes, hair, highlights and neck

 **High Elf Male**
 - New portrait based on blood elf male, but with less details to be closer to the classic look.

 **Night Elf Female**
 - Adjustments to nose and shadows

 **Night Elf Male**
 - Adjustments to shadows (removed the hars, black lines)

 **Tauren Female**
 - Tiny changes to shading to make the nose more distinct

 **Troll Female**
 - Adjustments to eyes

 **Troll Male**
 - Adjustments to eyes
