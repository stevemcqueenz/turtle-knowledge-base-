# New arcane spell for mages: Arcane Bolt.

## Post 35478 by Starweaver — 2023-02-20T18:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35478#p35478 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/83609-arcane-bolt.jpg)

"Arcane Bolt
 90 Mana	40 yd range
 1 sec cast
Requires level 20
Hurls a magical bolt at an enemy, inflicting Arcane damage."

Is it just me or would this not be an awesome and very welcome addition to make a purist arcane approach to mage gameplay more viable?  Of course, the spell stats above could be adjusted for balance, (e.g., 1 sec cast -> 2 sec cast, mana cost scales with rank, etc) but I think this would serve as a tremendously useful direct damage ability for the pure arcane mage that beautifully ameliorates the currently existing deficit to playing such a build. We have the archetypal Fire Mage and Frost Mage.. having a proper Arcane Mage only makes sense!

I know that there are new spells present here on Turtle WoW so surely this one could be (and ought to be) added as well!

What do you guys think?

## Post 35492 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-20T19:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35492#p35492 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I do think it would be good to do more pure arcane, but I don't think it should be in the form of simple single shot spells. I know this is an Arcane spell that NPCs get, but I'm not sure it fits for Arcane Mage.

One thing I realized about Arcane messing around with it, is that the offensive side of it seems designed around multi-hit. You only get two offensive spells, but what are they? Arcane Missiles fires off several shots. Arcane Explosion is an AoE. Both have the problem of a high cost, and your talents are focused on spiking damage even more, by burning more mana. Blizzard seems to have gone back and forth on this, but Clearcasting is a chance to trigger per *hit*, and I'm inclined to believe that it was supposed to favor Arcane by virtue of their offensive spells being multihit, and so giving more chance to proc.

That said, that's all just my speculation on how Blizzard might have envisioned it working. In practice, Arcane ended up being a "dump" category for all the utility spells, with only two inefficient attacks, and Clearcasting ended up so good with *any* spec that it didn't really matter.

If Turtle *were* to give Arcane Bolt to Mages, here's my thought. The NPC spell is called [Charged Arcane Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=16570/charged-arcane-bolt). So...maybe a spell that you channel, and then cast again to fire, dealing more or less damage depending on how long you charged it? That would fit with Arcane's "spend more to do more" thing (as lacking in fleshing out as they are).
