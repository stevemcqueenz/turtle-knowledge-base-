Got off one once without jumping and--sure enough--ploop!--fell right into the water.  It doesn't happen every time, but happens often enough that it's safer to jump, which is 100% reliable.

Admittedly it's a fairly minor problem, and also admittedly movers like ships are usually a bear to deal with in just about any game.  Don't want them getting hung up in geometry so perhaps it's easier/more reliable to keep them slightly away.
