# Scourge Invasion Dungeon Bosses

## Post 31661 by Lanimil — 2022-12-27T19:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31661#p31661 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So... I've been thinking about the bosses that spawn only during the Scourge Invasion in dungeons around Azeroth. Since the scourge invasion is kind of non-existent on this server, I have a few suggestions for how these bosses could be implemented here on Turtle wow. Before this though, I'll list all the bosses and where they spawn:

Sever - Shadowfang Keep
Scorn - Scarlet Monastery Graveyard
Lady Falther'ess - Razorfen Downs
Balzaphon - Stratholme
Lord Blackwood - Scholomance
Revanchion - Dire Maul

All of these bosses drop great loot for their respective levels which I think would be a great addition to the custom nature of Turtle wow. Some of the items I would even consider contenders for pre-bis gear.

My suggestions are as follows in order of personal preference:

 1. As rarespawns.
This one is pretty self-explanatory. Just have them spawn in like any other dungeon rare in their original locations.

 2. As invaders.
Spawned in a similar manner as the elemental invasion.

 3. As open-world rarespawns.
Pick custom locations for them to spawn. Could be great custom open-world content for level 60s and levelers alike. You could even add custom loot or adjust the existing items.

 4. As regular bosses.
Also pretty self-explanatory. Just spawned in every instance they appear.

I think this would be a cool addition to Turtle wow "custom" content. Appreciate any feedback or additions to what I've said!   smiling_turtle_head
