A thing that always bugged me is that WOW have thrown weapons but basically no one uses them.

What I propose is a rework of some talents for rogue and hunters (which are the staple throwing classes in wow, think abvout troll headhunters for example) to make thrown weapons more appealable.

EXAMPLE:

HUNTER

Savage Strikes: apply crit % to thrown aswell
Deterrence: add "each time you parry a strike while under deterrence, you respond with ((thrown weapon damage X 25) /5) to the attacker.
Trap Mastery: rename it "veteran skirmisher": reduce the chance your opponents will resist traps effects by 3% and increase your chance to hit with thrown weapon by an additional 3%. Additionjally , thrown weapon can be used in melee aswell.
Wyvern Sting: requires melee or thrown weapon now. Make it usable in combat.

 **ROGUE**
Thrown specialization: increases chance to hit with thrown by 3%
