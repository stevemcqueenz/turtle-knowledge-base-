# Inheritance

## Post 40951 by Styopa1 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-09T19:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40951#p40951 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Maybe if you die as HC (or not?) you could implement an inheritance process?

Something like when you die as HC, your next HC toon could be their inheritor.  You can only have an inheritor if you died at 20+

If your HC character dies before 60, the inheritor gets:
 - 10% of their liquid cash
 - 1 item from their inventory as a family heirloom (it's un-soulbound  if it was soulbound)
 - both of these are only available to the inheritor when they reach 21.

Personally, I think the remaining cash would determine how big a headstone the dead toon gets in a graveyard somewhere in Azeroth, as long as that toon wasn't deleted.  Maybe the inheritor would have to find their ancestor's grave to get the inheritance?

## Post 40964 by Liatris — 2023-04-09T21:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40964#p40964 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I feel like if this were implemented, this system would be abused by much of the HC community. The idea of an HC character going to the site of a previous character's death is cool from an RP standpoint, but if they were to retrieve an inheritance from the old character, I feel it's better to keep that item soulbound. Otherwise, hardcores could trade dungeon items pretty easily, which would eliminate the whole point of doing dungeons as HC.

I can understand spending hours playing a character and not wanting all that time to feel like a waste. Though, that should be motivation towards being careful with that character and ensuring they don't die. Having something to remember them by is a nice thought, but that's kind of what the gravestones are for.
