# Adjust cod mail abuse

## Post 97317 by Ravenhide — 2024-06-19T09:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97317#p97317 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, Turtles

After being victim of players asking to buy items cod, only to intentionally not accept, thus keeping said item hostage for 30 days, I'd like to propose making a change to the way cod works.

Here's my suggestion for removing option for trying to troll/gain advantage on AH market this way by essentially removing other sellers items for 30 days:
 1.) Give the player who sends cod the option to cancel the cod mail, if the other player hasn't accepted it.
 2.) Make cod time limit 20 min. If the buyer don't accept within time window, cod cancels and item is returned to seller.

Hope this makes sense! ![🙏](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f64f.svg)![😁](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f601.svg)

Not sure if this solution is a good one, but problem of trolling exists.
COD limit of 20 minutes will harm those players who often place "buy orders" in world chat like "will buy all runecloth for x gold per stack". They usually provide good deals for sellers.
You can just send items to them anytime and they eventually buy them out. With 20 minute limit it will ruin this type of trade.

So I think there should be ability to cancel COD mail or to set specific waiting time.

Meanwhile - don't COD expensive items. Sell them in SW/Org by direct trade.
