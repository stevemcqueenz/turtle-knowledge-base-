# Add faction requirement to wsg rep gear

## Post 93017 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-18T10:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93017#p93017 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Current situation:
Items sold by warsong quartermasters are visible only when you have appropriate reputation
Kelm Hargunth <Warsong Supply Officer> <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=14754>
Illiyana Moonblaze <Silverwing Supply Officer> <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=14753>
But items themselves don't require any reputation to equip.

Proposal is:
Items should require reputation to equip, but visible to everyone

I believe most people doesn't even know that wsg has reputational gear for lvl 18 because it is not visible for them.
I expect that visibility of rewards will slightly increase chances that people join battleground
