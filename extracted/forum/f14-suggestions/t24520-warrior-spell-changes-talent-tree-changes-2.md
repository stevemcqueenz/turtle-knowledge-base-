# Warrior Spell Changes & Talent Tree Changes 2

## Post 172546 by Kyrsie — 2026-03-26T13:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172546#p172546 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Here are my suggestions for Warrior PvP, tanking, and ease-of-use around abilities like **Overpower**, as well as making the 30 minute cooldowns feel like core abilities that the class can use frequently. I made a similar post months ago which looked a little too custom. These are smaller changes that shouldn't majorly affect PvE DPS for Fury Warriors, but could slightly increase the lower end.

Talent tree visual for reference: [https://www.talent-builder.dev/calculat ... Bk&class=1](https://www.talent-builder.dev/calculator?t0=9IIwKvtahU&t1=-wIEvE8uBq&t2=tEWrTV9-Bk&class=1)

"Every class has its bonus. If it's a Warrior complaining about a Mage, well guess what; the Warrior is in the raid and the Mage isn't."

- **Improved Disciplines** is now a passive spell learned by all Warriors at level 48 for Rank 1, 60 for Rank 2
Reduces cooldowns of **Retaliation**, **Shield Wall** and **Recklessness** by 10 and 20 minutes respectively.

- **Improved Disciplines**: Renamed to “**Battlefield Veteran**”. Effect changed to “Reduces the cooldown of your **Retaliation**, **Shield Wall**, and **Recklessness** abilities by 3/5 min.
 **Improved Shield Wall**: No longer reduces the cooldown of **Shield Wall**. Increases the effect duration of **Shield Wall** by 3/6 secs.

- **Improved Intervene** → **Wrecking Ball**
Grants a 50/100% chance to gain immunity to movement impairing effects for 3 secs after using **Charge**, **Intervene** and **Intercept**.

- **Improved Overpower**:
Increases the critical strike chance by 25/50% and reduces the cooldown of your **Overpower** ability by 2/4 sec.

- **Tactical Mastery**: Learned by all Warriors at level 12, and level 36.
Rank 1: You retain up to 15 of your rage points when you change stances.
Rank 2: You retain up to 25 of your rage points when you change stances.

- **Tactical Mastery** and **Deflection** talent positions swapped.

- **Tactical Mastery** renamed to “**Deadly Calm**”
Effect changed to “Your **Overpower** ability grants a 20/40/60/80/100% chance to cause your next **Berserker Stance** or **Defensive Stance** to retain up to 50 Rage points.”

(With the spell’s conditions not lining up with the rotation often, this makes **Overpower** a more reliable button to press.)

- **Rend**: AP scaling reduced/removed. Now scales with your weapon damage.
(Name a Dual Wield Fury Warrior who presses Rend)

- **Improved Heroic Strike**: Removed, **Improved Thunder Clap** takes its place
 **Heroic Strike**: Rage point cost reduced from 15 to 10

- New Talent in the place of **Improved Thunder Clap**: **Improved Hamstring**

- Gives your **Hamstring** a 5/10/15% chance to immobilize your target for 5 secs.

- **Unbridled Wrath**: Effect changed to “You gain 5/10/15/20/25 Rage points when dealing damage with an attack or ability. This effect has a 30 sec. cooldown per enemy.”

- **Intimidating Shout**: Damage from **Rend** and **Deep Wounds** no longer have a chance to break the Fear effect.

- **Master Strike (Maces)**: Damage from **Rend** and **Deep Wounds** no longer break the Disorient effect.
