# Ret Paladin Calculations & Change Suggestions

## Post 161927 by Icedcream (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-07T16:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161927#p161927 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I have created an excel sheet two google sheets calculating expected peak ret paladin performance now, and with suggestion reviews.The math is based on a 2 minute fight, boss standing there, no game mechanics. No paladin achieves peak numbers. Generally players only achieve 65- 70% of peak because of game play factors. A review of max damage and average damage suggests the math is accurate and many numbers were checked by the community. I've reviewed the numbers version game logs at various raid progressions.

I think a core problem is paladins require every stat to perform (sp, ap/st, ap/crit, melee hit, sometimes spell hit). The game cannot be balanced around procs. Ret paladins have been locked into using very specific gimmick items. There are some additional ways to fix this. To avoid competing with warriors for gear there could be a SP to Str conversion stat or talent. However, if you take this path you have to be prepared to work on a long list of itemization options that focus on creating balance so paladins go for plate options (or paladins will just start wearing cloth lol).

Current paladins can only achieve about a 42% increase in damage out put from pre-mc to end of Kara 40. The top rets out at 1050 dps in raid boss encounters.

My change suggestions goals were:

 1) Increase scalability
 2) Bring paladins into a mid / competitive position at end game raid
 2) Increase item selection
 3) Address HP issue
 4) Address mana useage Issue
 5) Soften effects of raid consumes
 6) Address dependency on WoC, Accord, Ashbringer, UTB, T2.5 (3pc), and Spell strike
 7) Blend AP and SP ret, but creating a blending scaling of stats and abilities. Trying to keep itemization different than warrirors where possible.
 8) Remove Spell strike mechanics
 9) Create further differences between Ret and Proc

 **Change Log:**

 1)	Nerf - Flask of Supreme Power - Minimum Spell Range
"Change to allow FoSP to only affect spells beyond a typical Judgement, seal, consecration, shock, or other shaman melee spell range

Comment: Allow for melee to use Flask of Titan's as they will never use it if the choice is Survival over DPS. Reduce power scaling of early game from consumes and rebalance Coefficients to distribute power"

 2)	Nerf - Wrath of Cenarius  - Reduce chance to proc by paladins
"Remove proc chance from divine strike, spell strike, and seal of righteousness. Leave consecration chance maybe...

Comment: Too much power level comes from this quest item that isn't even raid dependant. At a minimum this has a 75% uptime if not greater. It may be the single most important item. Having this item remain in it's current state creates 100% dependance for spell ret, ap ret, and prot paladins on this item. Adjust other characteristics to compensate for the loss. """

 3)	Solo Nerf / Raid Buff - Judgement of Crusader
"Remove 196 (improved/fervor) holy power version. Top Rank replace with 25 Spell power. Talent improvement 1%/2% crit chance.

Comment: Too much of the paladins total spell power comes from this judgement. It doesn't benefit the raid, and affects future paladin scaling."

 4)	Nerf - Sigil of the Ancient Accord - Proc
"Remove ability to proc off divine strike

Comment: Forces paladins to use T2.5 (3pc) all the way to end game."

 5)	Nerf - Weapon proc life steal - Spell power Coefficicent change
"Reduce procs from 100% SP Coef. to 25%

Comment: creates degenerate behaviour and locks paladins into using very specific weapons"

 6)	Nerf - Untamed Blade - Proc Rate with spell strikes
"Nerf ability to proc off paladin seals, and spell strikes

Comment: community estimates 50 second up time per 60 seconds. 300 str. Totally destroys scaling of all other weapons comparatively. May even been a contender for best weapon unit end game. Even may be better than Shar't for AP ret with SoCom."

 7)	Nerf - Dragonsbreath Chili - Proc Chance and SP
"Just make it proc like evey other melee and reduce spell power coefficient to 0%.

Comment: open's paladins up to stamina buffs, str, and A/S buffs. Less RNG gaming. It is currently pretty ineffective 1.5-2% total damage."

 8)	Nerf - Vengeful Strike - Remove Holy Might
Comment: Create new "Righteous" buff to fill this gap see below. Although it makes you think about rotations, it doesn't add anything to the game play. It reduces strength up time which cripples the physical scaling if not managed 100% effectively. Further, reduced overall average output in a fight based on fight duration. Example in a 1 minute fight it may not be applied till 18 seconds.

 9)	Buff - New Ability - Righteous Purpose - Buff - Top rank below
"Only one ""Righteous"" buff can be active at a time

 25% increased strength, 25% increased agility, 25% threat reduction"

 10)	Buff - Divine Strength
"10% increase strength, 10% increase stamina

Comment: Improve paladin survivability - both ret and prot. May be a tier 1 prot talent. "

 11)	Change - Sanctity Aura
"Move to tier 2 or 3 Retribution tree

Comment: was a prot ability, and still remains a prot ability. Could make better talent flow for ret and prot paladins."

 12)	Nerf - Consecration - Move ability to Prot Tree
"Adjust spell power scaling to 85% (from 66%), or increase threat generation by 15% to make up for reduced SP.

Comment: Open ret paladins for new cleave ability that is more mana efficient and reactive to target location. Consecration is the source of Ret paladins mana ineffeciency and a key reason they always have atleast 1 spell strike effect for 2-4 dmg to proc more wisdom. Otherwise, they'd mana out 100% of the time. Poor efficiency also plagues ret in high pace raid environments."

 13)	Nerf Ret / Balanced for Prot - Improved Righteousness - Talent Move
"Move to prot talent tree. Change from 6/12/18% damage increase and 10/20/30% threat increase.

Comment: Ability has been isolated by ret paladins because it scales better than SoCom. With SP this should shift, but the ability is essential for prot threat. Scaling up the damage gain and threat should help offset part of the change. Move talent from holy to Prot. Overall, talent is nerfed via nerfs 1,2, and 3. Requires buffs to remain effective for prot paladins."

 14)	Buff - Righteous Fury - Talent
"Update improved Righteous Fury to also include 20% increase threat from physical (as well as what it already does for holy threat). Allow to make up lost threat gain from SoR.

Comment: Further help their threat generation which is heavily tied to holy damage. Should provide some scaling."

 15)	Buff - Seal of Command
"Change double hit check system  (Currently hit/miss/dodge checked at auto, then checked again on seal proc.)

Change PPM from 6 to 8 (about 48% chance on a 3.6 weapon speed)
Change SP Coeff from 24% down to 20%

Comment:  This will be further buffed by items below for increased scaling. This is the correct path for ret because they benefit from SP, they benefit from AP, Str, and most importantly they scale with crit and attack speed as well.

There are some other leavers - to choose, example sp scaling, phy% scaling, crit % (higher or lower with higher or lower PPM)"

 16)	Buff - Judgement of Command
"Scales off Strength 100%, no scaling AP, Scales SP 40%.
Reduce initial damage top rank to 50

Comment: AP scaling is a little too flat due to Battle Shout and Might. Even Str scaling is kind of affected since Strength totem adds a massive 96 str. However, best of a bad situation."

 17)	Buff - Vengeance Talent (Rename Divine Purpose) - Application and %
"Passive 20% increase damage by all

Comment: Remove of visual buff slot. 1 minute fight we currently see 90% uptime of 3 stacks. Just streamlines this and reduces scaling up time. Buffed % scalability of all factors in raid"

 18)	Buff - Two hand specialization
"Change from 6% to 10% increase damage
Still includes 3 weapon skill 2 h"

 19)	Buff - New - Holy Warrior - Talent
"Spell power increased by 25%

Comment: Reviewed many options, and this encourages paladins the most to still choise items with SP, just helps SP scale better with item efficiency"

 20)	Buff - New Capstone (Lights Will? I don't know) - Talent
"Instant Attack, doesn't share CD with Strike, 8 Second CD, test at 135 mana cost

Primary Target: Holy damage = 50% weapon damage coeff, 12.5% SP Coeff
Primary Target: Procs seal @ 50% effective seals on primary target (100% chance)

Use of ability procs active seal at 35% damage (SoR or SoC) on to up-to four enemys around target. Or proc seal (wisdom/light) at 25% effectiveness per target hit.

Primary attack: Physically check for hit, crit, miss. Cannot parry/dodge
Seal procs: Shares characteristics of a typical seal
"

 21)	Buff - Burst of Light - New Ret Talent
"Crits increase all damage done by 2%, stacking up to 3 times to 6%. 4th Crit discharges a burst of holy light (single target) equal to 50% spell power. Stacks last 8 seconds if not refreshed.

Shares crit chances with melee, cannot miss/dodge/resist, 100% crit damage. Does not build up from spell strike crits.
Only works with Auto, New cap stone, SoCom crit, JoCom crit, HS, CS, crits

Comment: Provide a bit more damage scaling, SP scaling, and add paladin Twow flavor"

 22)	Quality of Life -  General
"Allow seal to presist for 1 second after casting judgement. Seal fades if another seal is replaced if new seal cast before the end of the 1 second.

Comment: Avoid seal twisting. However, improve player experience due to GCD lockout, and service latency checks."
