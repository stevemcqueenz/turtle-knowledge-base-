# All the custom islands feel very...samey to STV?

## Post 138898 by Nazrudin — 2025-07-13T02:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138898#p138898 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I enjoy the custom content and the life the TWOW brings to our Azeroth (Gilneas was an amazing immersive experience!), but sometimes i just get kinda bored to go out there and explore/do the quests in the islands because it feels like it's Stranglethorn Valley...all over again...

For example, the majority of the assets/creatures in these islands are straight up the same thing from STV (creatures having some exceptions like Tel'Abim having wind serpents in a jungle biome and the unique chimpanzees). But wouldn't it be cool to add more variants of these creatures? Like what you guys did do Gilneas and the brown/grey worg in there, which if i remember correctly it wasn't available in the original game. Imagine some tropical-colored wind serpents, parrot owls (like the ones you can buy in Booty Bay), something to make these places more different!  smiling_turtle_head

Just something to make these zones stand out a little more and bring more life to the game. What do you guys think?

I agree, they feel like extensions of Stranglethorn Vale. Maybe they need some ancient ruins where Gnomes, Dwarves and Goblins are excavating, or some other group.

Those two islands - Gilijim and Lapidis feel like they lack some overarching story to keep the interest going. Maybe some mystery and some underground ruins with some big bad boss and a dungeon. Where both islands have the same main story.

Each one has some underground ruins that maybe connect under the water and you have quests with normal mobs, but deeper into the ruins is an entrance for a dungeon where you do the final quest.

I think just changing the assets to something else isn't going to do much. I can compare Swamp of Sorrows to Dustwallow Marsh and how one is yellow/orange and the other is red/brown with different trees and creatures, but the story in each is completely different too. Same with Winterspring and Dun Morogh - the only major difference is one has blue sky and the other pink and the buildings in one are dwarven and in the other elven, then the story is also different so it really sets them apart.

But for Gilijim/Lapidis and Stranglethorn Vale, I don't see much of a difference. I only completed them to experience the new content, but to me playing there felt more annoying than fun. Tel'Abim, on the other hand was much better and so were the quests.

 1. Kul Tiras: Temperate climate. Like the UK irl.

 2.1 Crestfall, temperate climate, some dragon activity
 2.2 Zul'Dare, temperate climate, probably has troll ruins

Further tropical islands:
 1. Plunder Isle, tropical, bloodsail buccaneer home
