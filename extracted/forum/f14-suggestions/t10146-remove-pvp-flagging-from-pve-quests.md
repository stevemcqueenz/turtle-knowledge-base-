# Remove PVP flagging from PVE quests

## Post 66358 by Darkcovenant — 2023-09-22T10:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66358#p66358 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Certain quests currently flag the player for PVP, many of these don't even involve attacking npc's of the opposite faction. They leave hardcore players vulnerable to grieving by those who enjoy spoiling others gaming experience; this situation is very different to killing a hardcore player with warmode enabled. It is also in the interest of standardising the questing experience, many quests which involve attacking the other faction do not flag the player.

Some of these quests which currently flag players include:

 *"The Attack!"*(Seal of Wrynn quest chain)
 *"The Missing Diplomat"*(Both the Stormwind & Dustwallow stages)

There is also the custom quest in Swamp of Sorrows called *"Avoiding Detection"* which requires the killing of 8x Stonard Scouts. The same Stonard Scout npc's often interfere with the vanilla quest called *"Galen's Escape"* by attacking the escort as it makes the way through the Broken Draenei camp, as the escortee is so weak the player either has to flag and kill the scout themselves or fail the escort.

The plethora of Horde quests in Hillsbrad, Stonetalon Mountain's, Ashenvale, the Barrens and the Hinterlands which require killing Alliance npc's do not flag players for PVP whether it is killing Darnassus faction Dryad's on Stonetalon Peak; Stormwind faction Peasant's in Hillsbrad Fields or Kul Tirasian Marines at Anchor's Edge.

There are probably other examples of levelling quests which can flag players for PVP and I urge other posters to mention them.
