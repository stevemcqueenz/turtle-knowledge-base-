# Feedback Regarding Music Selection on the Warcraft Radio

## Post 138966 by Softly — 2025-07-13T17:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138966#p138966 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Everlook Broadcasting Co.

I hope this message finds you well. I wanted to share a bit of feedback regarding the recent music selection on the Warcraft Radio stream.

While I truly appreciate the effort that goes into curating a diverse and engaging playlist, I couldn't help but notice a recent inclusion of Spanish love songs during the broadcast. As much as I respect the genre and the cultural richness behind it, I personally feel that this style doesn't quite align with the atmosphere and immersive fantasy world of Warcraft.

The game’s musical identity is typically epic, atmospheric, or thematically dark — whether it’s background soundtracks or community-driven content. Hearing romantic ballads in Spanish tends to break that immersion and feels somewhat out of place in the context of Azeroth.

Of course, this is just my perspective as a long-time listener and fan, and I completely understand that musical taste is subjective. I simply wanted to share this in hopes of keeping the station’s vibe consistent with the tone and spirit of the Warcraft universe.

Thank you very much for your time, and for all the work you do to keep the community engaged.

Best regards,
Softly
