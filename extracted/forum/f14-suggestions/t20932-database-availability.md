# Database Availability

## Post 145594 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-15T09:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145594#p145594 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtlewow has a tradition of hiding new items in the Database whenever a new patch comes out. The idea is so people can explore the new things the game has to offer when the new patch drops. And thats a noble goal.

But can we please for the love of god not disable the database entirely whenever this happens?
 90% of items, quests and spells in the game remain unchanged and we cant look up things because of the complete database blackout. In older patches you could at least use wowhead to get the needed baseline informations.
But twow by now has so much custom changes to existing content that this option is no longer feasable.

Please leave the database online and just not update it for the days you want the information blackout on the new content to last.

For someone who likes theorycrafting and who has to figure out what gear to wear now with tank pally t3 being made into a joke, this is extremely frustrating to be limited to ingame atlasloot and db sniffing with shagudb.
