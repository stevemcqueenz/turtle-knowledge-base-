# Tonal Stones

## Post 15952 by Silverstruck — 2022-01-17T13:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15952#p15952 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to start by congratulating the Turtle WoW staff on their great advancements and the hard work. Many people consider this server to be quality over quantity and it's quite true. What I personally enjoyed a lot was the simple addition of this Tonal Stone item. Really not something that makes any difference in gameplay, but such a nice touch. I would love to see more of these tonal stones scattered across all areas, each playing different tunes. Aneka Konko, or maybe some of her brethren should travel to some remote locations throughout Azeroth and players could find them and hunt for their musical gifts. Personally, I find that the koto and shakuhachi tunes are gorgeous, but could be expanded onto western classical music as well. Some harp, mixed with piano and violin - true immersion right there. I'm not that knowledgeable as to provide actual names, but I'm sure there is plenty of stuff available in the public domain.
