PALADIN/SHAMAN
I'm personally iffy about the idea of breaking the faction barrier set on Paladins and Shamans, but if I had to pick races for them to be lifted, it'd be Undead Paladins and Dwarf Shamans, with Troll Paladins and Night Elf Shamans being my second picks for each, albeit at more of a stretch.

Zeliak is a singular exception when it comes to Undead Paladins in canon, true, but he IS proof that it can theoretically be done. Undead can also be priests, and the RPG lore (if it's considered canon for this server) describes Undead as being able to wield the Holy Light, just at much physical pain to themselves. Not to mention the Forsaken are the reanimated people of Lordaeron, where the Order of the Silver Hand was literally founded. It could work, with some good writing.

Wildhammer were described (also in the RPG) as the best source of shamans within the Alliance. While it's true the Clan as a whole has not fully committed to the Alliance in WoW canon by this point, there could certainly be individuals, and Turtle could explain a greater number of them coming to the Alliance's service similarly to how they did Forest Trolls for the Horde. Even without Wildhammer trainers, one could, at a bit of a stretch, point to the dwarves' study of their titanic origins and their connections to the Earthen as a possible opening for Ironforge Shamans as they delve deeper into their ancestries and the old magic of the world itself.

Troll Paladins are, as I said, a stretch. But theoretically, any race that can be both Warriors and Priests could learn to be Paladins. These would not be Silver Hand Paladins, but a new, Troll-specific order. Even without relying on BfA lore, the Zandalari were always established as the religious heads of the old Troll Empires, it wouldn't be too much of a stretch to say that the Darkspear rediscovered (or were given access to) old Zandalari traditions that mechanically match the concept of a Paladin.

Similarly, Night Elf Shamans would less so be traditional shamans in the sense that the Horde would use the term, and moreso a different flavor of Druid. Perhaps ones devoted to the Dragon Aspects who gain their powers from the flights as opposed to the elements. The RPG used to have things like the Sect of the Dragon and the Dragonsworn - this lore could be adapted for a new order of NElf shamans.

OTHER IDEAS
Orc Druids - According to the RPG (my catchphrase for this post, I know!), the Mok'nathal were almost as prominently druids as the Tauren are. With the inclusion of the Rexxar-based Mag'har/Mok'nathal skin, it'd be kind of cool to be able to play a Mok'nathal Druid, or at least an Orc that was instructed by a Mok'nathal.

Troll Druids - Pretty easy lore, there. Something something Loa, something something trolls should probably have always been druids, tbh.

Human Hunters - Self-explanatory. Look to the Human Crossbowmen of Warcraft I if you really need a precedent.

Tauren Rogues - Almost comical, I know, because of their size, but the existence of the Grimtotem makes a solid case for the idea of Tauren Rogues. They were always described as pretty shifty and unscrupulous by Tauren standards, and (at least by Cata times, not sure about prior) they even incorporated the use of poisons and Combat Rogue abilities with some of their NPCs.
