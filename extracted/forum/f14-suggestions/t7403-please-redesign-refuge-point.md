# Please redesign Refuge Point

## Post 47139 by Mechsoap — 2023-05-23T15:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47139#p47139 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Something that often bothers me when i go to the Arathi Highlands, is that supposedly the elite remnant of Stromgarde are holed up in Refuge Point, a hole in the ground, that is extremely susceptible to ambush attacks.

It would be nice if it could be redesigned to be perhaps a tad more level, and not a big hole in the ground, and some defensive works put up around the perimeter of the camp, because as i understand it, they have been there for 4 years, but they look like they fled yesterday.

It would also fit the theme if it became a more proper military camp, that actually looks like it can hold it's own. some small houses, maybe a wooden watchtower, and basic services like a mailbox and HS spot would also be appreciated for gameplay reasons, as the nearest hub with those things is Southshore for alliance.

Unless there is some deep lore reason as to why they are holed up in a hole, instead of say, using the holy as a defensive moat for one side of their camp, i simply don't understand why any military retinue would think it a good idea. There is only maybe one Guard every 20 meter around the hole, and they would be susceptible to being ambushed as said with some orcs/syndicate/undead shooting down on them.
