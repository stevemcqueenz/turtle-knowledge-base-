# Keep War Mode On at 60

## Post 152341 by M3te0r — 2025-09-19T06:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152341#p152341 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Right now, War Mode is activated by talking to a PvP Warmaster NPC, and it works just like the other “challenge” options on the server (like hardcore leveling or only exp while drunk). Because of that system, War Mode automatically gets turned off once you hit level 60. After that, you have to manually walk back to a Warmaster every time you log in to re-enable it.

The result of this technicality is that world PvP is basically dead. Even people who want to play with War Mode don’t keep it on, because it’s just too inconvenient to have to re-toggle it every single login. While leveling, War Mode is always active and that creates fun risk/reward gameplay, but at 60 it essentially vanishes.

I think the server would benefit if War Mode simply stayed on after you enable it, instead of resetting at 60 or after every relog. It’s not that players don’t want world PvP (Lots of people do). It’s that the current system discourages it. This change would breathe life into open world PvP and make War Mode actually function the way it was intended.
