It was probably harder in original Vanilla period. Not because of the numbers like HP of bosses etc. But because raid knowledge wasn't widely accessible. Back then you didn't have videos that explain each fight. Even some must-have addons like BigWigs weren't used back then. World buffs weren't widely used, too. Internet connection was a problem especially for casters because it had big influence over your casting pauses.

Now we have tons of addons that help you with raiding. Plenty of guides, too. Game became much more easy with same numbers of HP on bosses, their damage etc. Devs probably wanted to adjust level of difficulty to current standards - we have no connection issues now and raiding addons, input reduction time mods are used probably by everyone to boost everyone's performance. Correct me if I am wrong, MC received 2 buffs: indirect and direct one. Direct one was about making some mechanics of bosses more difficult/extended. Indirect one is general increase of HP of every monster in the game.

While MC received 2 buffs, other raids received only 1 buff - the indirect one. That's what OP wanted to say if I understand it correctly. For example Kara40 received 1 buff and 1 nerf which is scaling to 35. MC used to be on tier 1 level, now it's tier 1.5. MC seems to be overtuned.
