# New hunter pets for each race

## Post 149167 by Phinderblast — 2025-08-29T16:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149167#p149167 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello testudine friends. I post this in Reddit, but I'm giving it a shot here too. I'm leveling a hunter in the new patch and I'm enjoying it so much. I love the pet system in Vanilla that incentive you to hunt and learn about the mobs to learn new skills and to get the skin you like the most. The new pet families reignite this thrill and the new skills are so flavorful and in the spirit of turtle that I get myself wondering: **what if we get an exclusive pet for each race?** You know, some races are deeply conected with some beasts that is just natural to pair then. Dwarves and gryphons, Orcs and Wyverns, Taurens and kodos, and so on and on. in the spirit of turtle, of getting flavorful aditions that are lore friendly, I think of the possible combinations of pets and races.

Before someone get the pitforks and call for my head to propose something so disruptive to the balance, I'm not proposing for then to be something like the Exotic pets introduced in wrath, but just "reskin" of current pets, so the adition could be only aesthetic e not mechanic. Something like a Gryphon could only be a reskinned owl, with the same status, skills and stuff. But disclaimer aside I'll make a list and you all can discuss it, maybe you have better pairings, maybe you dislike it, maybe you don't. Anyway, I make the list for the easier to harder to think in candidates.

 **Night Elf**

 *Hippogryph*- Pretty standard choice. They are their racial flight mount, They are a unit in Warcraft 3, they are really close to their culture and continue to be in Vanilla and even in further expansions. They can be, maybe, an Owl reskin

 *Chimaera* - They are too a unit in Warcraft 3, but in wow vanilla they were absent as a cultural thing for then, albeit turtle revitalize it with the Feralas quests. They can be a Wild Serpent reskin

 *Fairy Dragon* - Unconvenional, as they are not beasts, but dragonkin. But they are too a unit in Warcraft 3 and are loosely tied with NEs. They dont see to be in the same level of inteligence of a dragon, but as some sort of animal level of dragonkin, so I think it's fair. I cant think of a perfect match of reskin, maybe owl, maybe wild serpent, maybe tallstrider.

 **Orc**

 *Wyvern* - Another standard choice. There is a whole map in warcraft 3 about orcs taming then, they are prevalent in horde areas, they really fit in the vibes. They can be a Cat Resking

 **Tauren**

 *Kodo* - A no brainer, as tallstrider is now a common pet. Lore friendly, lots of kodos in Mulgore and Barrens, tightly weaven in tauren culture, there is no better choice here. They can be a Gorilla reskin.

 **Dwarf**

 *Gryphon* - Wildhammers are famous for the relantioship to then, gryphon riders are an all time favorite unit in Warcraft 3. They already tame and use then in lore, it's just a matter to implement the mechanic, really. They can be a Owl reskin.

 **High Elf**

 *Dragonhawk* - They dont exist in Vanilla, but they do in Warcraft 3 and High Elves use then as mounts. The lore is there, it's just a matter to sort it out. They can be an Owl or Wind Serpent reskin

 **Human**

 *Dog* - Not much to work here. Humans have hyenas in Scarlet, albeit they call then dogs. In later expassions they change the hyena models to new hunting dogs, so there it is. Maybe they can import or make their own version of dogs and let then be human exclusive. They can be a wolf or hyena reskin.

 *Sheep* - Ok, that's a bit of a joke sugestion, but sheeps are a big thing in human campaign in Warcraft 3, the are plenty of then in Elwynn. Not a really strong case, I know, but it is what it is. They can be a bear reskin.

 **Forsaken**

 *Undeads* - I'm not talking about human zombies, but the undead dogs and bears that Lordaeron and the plagelands have. Easy choice, but flavorful one nonetheless. They are their respective reskins, of course. Undead dog is a wolf, undead bear is a bear and so on.

 *Slime* - Ok, now hear me out. Forsaken have a really deep conection with the plage, the concotions, the slime river in their capital. I'll not be shocked to see a forsaken with a pet slime running around, do you? To be even more flavorful you can make then eat grey itens, the same one they drop in droves when you kill then. Honestly they can work as a reskin of many pets, but I would put then as hyena reskin, a slime that spawn another slime is just perfect.

 **Gnome**

 *Mechanostrider* - Now things get tricky. Gnomes dont have a deep conection to any beast, they are absent in warcraft 3, there is not much to work here. I put the mechanostrider here because it's easy to just make then a mecha tallstrider and call it a day, but honestly any mechanical contraption of then work, like a mechanical chicken, mechanical dragon, etc. You can even make then eat some engeneering parts or a Flask of Oil.

 **Goblin**

 *Harvester?* - If gnome have little to work, goblin have even less. I dont think I can suggest for then to tame a cart full of dynamite, even less a miniature zepelllin. Goblins in Deadmines summon some miniature version of golem harversters, so Idk. I'll leave this for you too give a proper suggestion.

And this is it. I have not hope that this will ever be implemented, but it was a fun exercise for me and I would like to share. This is much more an atempt to suggesting something more flavorful and tied to the lore, and to discuss the possibilities and lore of the races, than to a really strong and firm suggestion, to be fair.
