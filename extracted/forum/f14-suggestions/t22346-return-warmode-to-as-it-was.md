# Return Warmode to as it was.

## Post 155177 by Stalinist — 2025-10-11T17:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155177#p155177 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello everybody

Our guild is an RP-PvP guild based in Stranglethorn Vale. It's entire premise is to terrorise and dominate the Vale, scaring away our enemies and ensuring that we are it's masters and rulers. Open world PvP played a significant role in that because we would RP with people in the Vale who were flagged, threaten them and then if they did not comply with our roleplayed out demands we would kill them. This added a flavour to the wPvP and gave everybody an introduction to RP.

Similarly, wPvP was more active throughout all levelling areas because more people had warmode on and fought. When I levelled my first and main character from 1-60, I had fights in almost every contested zone from Stranglethorn onwards. I thought it was great and really enjoyable. I did get ganked a few times, but it was not as bad as it's made out to be.

In my opinion the changes to warmode have ruined wPvP on Nord completely and the 20% experience bonus should be returned.
