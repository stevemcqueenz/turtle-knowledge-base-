# Expand Questlog

## Post 32756 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-01-19T07:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32756#p32756 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle Wow is fantastic for many reasons. One of which is the plethora of new quests in many zones. With each new update more zones receive more quests.
I noticed that I often reach the Questlimit of 20. This forces me to abandon some quests that surely I will do later on if I remember them.
It would be fantastic if the questlog could be expanded to 25 total quests up from 20.
I know this isn't Vanilla but I think it wouldn't really take from the Vanilla feel overall.
And yes this is mostly for people still leveling but not only are there always plenty of people questing but also HC keeps the population in the lower level zones high at all times.
