I don't mind needing an anvil so much, but I'd love to see more anvils and smelters placed around settlements for convenience.  For instance, having to run all the way to the Dwarven District in Stormwind is something that bugs me whenever I'm down that way.  With all the random odds and ends stashed around the alleys and such, an occasional anvil around larger settlements doesn't seem like it would be out of place.  The Great Anvil in Ironforge is conveniently central, but Ironforge also seems like a place where there would be plenty of additional anvils around the city.

P.S. Not to say that I'm against the idea of some kind of special portable anvil either, even if it does seem a little goofy.  With all the ridiculous stuff we're able to carry around in our multiple backpacks, one of them being an anvil doesn't seem especially immersion-breaking.  If it really bothers people, make it some kind of magic anvil or something.  Or a Dwarf racial power: Summon Anvil!
