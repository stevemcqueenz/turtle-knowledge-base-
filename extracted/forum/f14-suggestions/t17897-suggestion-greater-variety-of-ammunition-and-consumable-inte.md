# Suggestion: Greater Variety of Ammunition and Consumable Interaction for Pets

## Post 121982 by RPGnerd (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-03-07T17:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121982#p121982 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As the title suggests, there could be a wider variety of ammunition for Hunters—arrows and bullets made from Arcanite, Dreamsteel, Enchanted Thorium, and perhaps other materials like Silver or Truesilver.

Another suggestion is that when feeding a pet, it should receive the Well Fed buff if the food has that characteristic. Furthermore, pets could also be "fed" potions (Elixirs and Flasks) for additional effects.

We can always dream, can't we?
