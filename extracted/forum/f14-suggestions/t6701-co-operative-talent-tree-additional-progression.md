# Co-operative Talent Tree - additional progression

## Post 42192 by Telandris — 2023-04-19T13:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42192#p42192 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone,

One of the greatest things about a Classic/Classic + era server is the way in which it naturally incentivizes people to play together to overcome the dangerous open world and instanced content.

Regrettably, in the retail version of the game, Blizzard put a tremendous focus on the 'individual power' element of progression.

While there is nothing inherently *wrong* with this approach, I think it can detract from the potential of 'shared power growth' and this is something I feel could be addressed in a meaningful and creative way here on Turtle WoW.

Our talent trees are largely focused around increasing our own, personal power. But what if there was an additional element to this?

A secondary talent tree designed to increase interplay and shared power between one another not only heavily leans into the MMO side of group play and co-operation but would allow for additional methods to achieve greater class balance without introducing potentially unsustainable individual power increases.

Let me give you a series of examples:

 1. Rend is a notoriously weak Warrior ability by itself. One solution could be to buff the scaling co-efficient of Rend. Another could be for Rend to empower the abilities of other classes. If we take the idea of 'co-operative talents' a Hunter might be able to take a talent in their secondary tree that causes their pet to do more damage or an attack speed increase to targets afflicted with Rend.

 2. Consecration for Paladins might relate to a Priest secondary talent to increase the casting speed of Holy Spells while standing within the consecrated ground. Layering Consecrations may allow other Paladins to benefit from a secondary talent that grants small increase to attack speed and Holy Damage from Seals.

 3. Warlocks and Shadow Priests could develop an affinity for bouncing off each others Shadow Spells. A Warlocks Curse of Shadow might allow Shadow Word Pain to tick faster or Mind Flay ticks could give Curse of Agony ticks an increased chance to crit.

There is an incredible amount of potential here that would facilitate really interesting, unique interactions between classes while alleviating the need for X number of % buffs when it came to class tuning. I'm interested to know what people think about this sort of progression mechanic and whether or not it would improve the game moving forward.
