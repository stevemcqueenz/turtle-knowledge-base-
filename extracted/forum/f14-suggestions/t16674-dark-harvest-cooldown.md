# Dark Harvest cooldown

## Post 113928 by Jannus — 2024-12-23T01:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113928#p113928 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey all,
I feel like Dark Harvest is a pretty fun ability and I like using it a lot. The mechanics on it are real novel (speeding up the dots applied on a target). That being said, I feel like 30 seconds as a cooldown can be a bit too much, so I was trying to think of ways we could improve that cooldown timer.
So I have came up with the following ideas:

- Make it so casting Life Tap reduces the cooldown of Dark Harvest by 1-2 seconds. Could either be assigned to a talent or a native addition to Life Tap. You'd need to make sure this only works with the highest rank of Life Tap though, otherwise people can spam level 1 Life Tap to get it earlier with the least amount of health spent. I feel this is in line with the Affliction Warlock class fantasy (trading life for power);
- Each tick of Corruption lowers the cooldown of Dark Harvest by 0.5-1 second. That way, you can both hasten the cooldown by having Corruption up while you channel Dark Harvest, but is also in line with the overall Affliction Warlock gameplay loop and rewards for casting Corruption on multiple targets instead of just opting for Hellfire / Rain of Fire AoEing down;
- Killing a target that grants XP or Honor while channeling Dark Harvest resets your Dark Harvest cooldown - this would allow you to use the spell as an 'Execute' type ability and there is some risk vs reward, since you want to time it right and not get interrupted to receive a small reward of being able to recast it on the next enemy without having to wait 30 seconds.
The above suggestions can be mix-matched in some way (although combining all three might be a bit overkill). Any suggestions or ideas from both warlocks and non-warlocks are welcome.

Cheers!
