> Solution 1: Give LFT option to look only for your faction members or mixed
>
>  Solution 2: If a mixed group has a single PVP tagged player, the whole group goes PVP and alliance members become attackable by alliance and horde to horde.
>
>  Solution 3: Remove all PVP possibility from all mixed group members. They can not attack, and they can not be attacked.

 1) Takes away a good bit of charm for me and would further divide the playerbase that already has problems with long queue times.
 2) Is a terrible suggestion. As a healer I don't really have a choice whether I flag or not. Most dungeons have elite trash leading up to the instance portal, which means overworld combat happens, which means I have to heal my group. If someone (especially the tank) is flagged (or even warmode) I have no choice but to become flagged myself. Why should I become a target for both factions because of something outside of my control? It's bad enough that party members greedy for XP can force me to become a target for one faction.
 3) Seems like the only sensible solution. Forbidding PvP is understandable (it does lead to unfair situations when I can cross-faction heal but they can't engage me), but the attacker from outside the group cannot know that PvP is forbidden for the group. So enforce it on a technical level. Cross-faction groups should not be able to attack or be attacked in PvP. For the reasons mentioned under 2) I would like that for single-faction LFT groups too, but that is beyond the scope of this topic.
