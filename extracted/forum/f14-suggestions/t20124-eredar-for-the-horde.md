# Eredar for the Horde

## Post 137803 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-02T06:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137803#p137803 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There's been a lot of talk about what new race to include in the Horde. Basically, the Alliance is already the dream Alliance we never had at retail.

But the Horde, honestly, still has a lot to offer in terms of races, so many that I don't even think there are enough options in the Alliance to create an equivalent.

But many have said Ogres, the vast majority, although we all know the big problem is that a proper female model has never been seen.

But we have the infamous Draenei, included in The Retcon Crusade. They're a race of muscular, sexy space goats who came from outer space and joined the Alliance, their main meeting place being the Lion's Pride Inn in Goldshire for their not-at-all-weird business.

Well, the goal is, the Eredar, the evil and red Sorcerers of the Burning Legion, several have seen Sargeras' Crusade fail many times in trying to invade a simple planet and have decided to desert, trying to hide from their former slavers, have arrived on Azeroth, the Alliance has clearly rejected them for their corrupt and demonic past, but in the Horde they have found a race with a similar past, the Orcs have understood them and have decided to welcome them and all others who decide to desert the Legion in order to fight in the name of the Horde against everything that threatens their new refuge, Azeroth, while fighting against that faction that refuses to accept them, the Kaldorei being their main enemies and opponents, considering them a very serious threat and a new reason why the Horde increasingly becomes a very big threat to the Alliance and the planet in general.

I think that since we have the opposite of the Blood Elves, the High Elves (yes, I know the lore and everything there is with the Elves, they are my favorite race but you understand what I mean by "opposites"), we could have, now yes, the evil and corrupt but repentant version of the Draenei, the Eredar for the Horde, and create a symbolism that Turtle is the opposite of what was done in TBC.

## Post 137807 by Nilay (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-02T07:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137807#p137807 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In the lore, they are full-fledged demons, fully committed to the deeds of the Burning Legion and often used as officers by Sargeras.

From what I’ve understood, the lore of Turtle WoW is mostly a continuation and expansion of the Warcraft III and Vanilla universe, while drawing some solid inspiration from BC and WotLK. That’s what made me come here.

Turning them into “repentant” demons would require a complete narrative overhaul — Retail-level destructive. I would honestly stop playing if it ever came to that.

I’m here for a true Scourge and Burning Legion threat: dangerous, uncompromising. I have no interest in seeing another forced redemption arc trying to sanitize the original canon.

In term of dislike, I would prefer a stupid useless Gnoll or Murloc in the Horde than a damn seid of Sargeras.
