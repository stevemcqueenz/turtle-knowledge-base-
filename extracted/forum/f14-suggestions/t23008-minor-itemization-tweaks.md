# Minor Itemization Tweaks

## Post 160589 by M0thra — 2025-11-24T20:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160589#p160589 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello, I noticed over the last couple of months that a handful of nice itemization tweaks have been made to various low-level equipment, which gives me hope that more changes like that might be considered down the line.

I've been meaning to do this for a while, so I thought I'd start a tally of items at lower levels that feel either improperly tuned, redundant with other gear, or outright useless. Would love to see some love for some of these. Some of them are Blizzard's handiwork, some of them are the TWoW team.

This will be updated as more of them pop into my head or as I come across them.

So without further ado:

 **[Ancient Leggings](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=58045) vs. [Quilguard Leggings](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80734)**
Same stat-spread, but quilguard is much worse despite being a higher ilvl. In this case, quilguard is a little undertuned, but if you're going to tune it to the level of ancient leggings, I think the stat spread should also be differentiated slightly. Maybe int or spirit instead of agility.

 **[Wreath of Worgen Blood](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61420)**
An ilvl 42 quest reward with roughly level 30 stats. The belt reward from this quest also seems undertuned.

 **[Mutant Scale Breastplate](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6627) vs. [Avenger's Breastplate](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=1488) vs. [Shining Silver Breastplate](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=2870)**
These 3 rares are all linear upgrades 1 level after another and feel like essentially the same item. It would be more interesting if there was a bit of diversity in this range, or at least inverting the values on the middle one so the stamina was higher.

 **[Slaver's Gauntlets](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41726)**
ilvl 33 item with roughly level 26-27 stats.

 **[Dragonmaw Helmet](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=58099)**
I don't necessarily know if this item actually needs to be changed, but melee classes get 40 attack power from this item at level 29. That's probably BiS for a dozen specs until mid-40s. Just drawing attention to that lol

 **[Batwing Mantle](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6697) vs. [Feline Mantle](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3748)**
Int/Spirit/Agi is an unusual stat spread that no one is really looking for, so it's especially strange that the next rare that upgrades Feline Mantle has the exact same spread (and is a little undertuned to boot)

 **[Cook's Apron](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81015)**
I'm not sure how you rate hp5 on gear, but my guess is a little too high in this case. ilvl 46 item with somewhere between level 30-35 stats.

 **[All of the "Knife Eared Stalkers" Quest Rewards](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=70025)**
I think the rewards are a couple levels undertuned for the quest level.

 **[Robe of Divinity](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80791) vs. [Robes of the Dukedom](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=58142)**
Only 4 ilvls apart, but 19 stats and 2 spellpower differential. Robe of Divinity is a little undertuned at least, not sure if Robes of the Dukedom need to be changed.

 **[Brigand's Pauldrons](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3765) vs. [Enforcer Pauldrons](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6747)**
Identical stats, ilvl, armor. Seems like a missed opportunity

 **[All of the Heart of Darkness Quest Rewards](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41787)**
These items not only do not match the database, they also do not match the patch notes. In fact, I'm not convinced they are even working as intended--the numbers on the dagger seem especially egregious in-game.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/eN4DXRU.png)

 **[Cloak of Draconic Madness](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41565)**
Slightly undertuned. ilvl 34 item with roughly level 30 stats.

 **[Drakeflame Girdle](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=58103)**
Rare instance of a slightly overtuned BoE. ilvl 30 item with roughly level 40 stats.

 **[Helmet of Dun Kithas](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41955) vs. [Enduring Circlet](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=14765)**
Helmet of Dun Kithas is slightly overtuned. Compared here with an item 3 levels higher, it's quite a bit better.

I don't really follow the logic. Some items will always be better than others based on your stat preferences, based on how hard those items are to obtain, and based on their ilvl and rarity. There are really only a few outliers as listed above.

Having easy to obtain items that are too good or hard to obtain items that are not good at all doesn't add anything to the game's depth. In fact, it narrows build diversity by making everyone pick the obviously better choices.

Adding more negative stats to items has potential, but there are limits to how much of that sort of min-maxing can be allowed while still maintaining the integrity of the game. If there was a way, for instance, for mages to sacrifice strength and agility to gain more int and stam, I'm sure everyone would do it and the end result is simply a net-positive on useful stats.
