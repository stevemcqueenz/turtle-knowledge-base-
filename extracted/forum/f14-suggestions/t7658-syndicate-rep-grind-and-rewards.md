# Syndicate rep grind and rewards

## Post 49059 by Houseperenolde — 2023-06-06T14:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49059#p49059 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I just finished grinding Syndicate reputation to friendly which I believe is the highest it can go. The addition of a small questline from Lord Falconcrest was a great addition and I am enjoying my orange bandana from the quartermaster. Now, on to my thoughts about changes that could be implemented:

 **Ravenhodt**
The grind has three problems, 1, it's monotonous, 2, it's not challenging once at level, and 3, it's a little lacking in drops.

Problem 1 can be fixed by making the mobs an interesting challenge in their own rights. They have a poison vendor, why not give them a poison attack? Wolfe isn't much on his own but how about making him linked to any elites that are attacked so that it is best to put him down first? On that note...

Problem 2 comes down to there being only one elite mob when really Lord Ravenholdt and Fahrad (or whatever his name is) should also be elites. Maybe give them an interesting ability or two and the extra rep reward for them being elites.

Problem 3 is solved simply by giving these guys a rudimentary loot table. Like, the fact they give exp and money when pickpocketed is a step up from when I did this grind in Classic (yes, I've done this twice) but criminals committing mass murder need more incentive.

Chilling Guard: OK, I've killed the lone Ravenholdt guard overlooking Chillwind Pass a hundred times now and at some point I started feeling sorry for him. He is on guard, alone, in a very exposed area. The least that can be done is give him a stool to sit on #justiceforchillwindguard

 **Alterac Valley**
Some people might know that there are Syndicate NPCs in AV. The quest they give has a meh reward in terms of securing victory (should be improved but that is another post). Many quests in AV give multiple types of rep and I think this one should give some Syndicate rep. This would give non-rogue classes a way to increase rep without destroying Ravenholdt rep. Disclaimer: it didn't give Syndicate rep back in the day and I assume it still doesn't but I haven't been able to confirm yet.

 **Questlines**
As stated, the Falconcrest questline to get you through neutral to friendly is a great addition. Aiden Perenolde should be added to the faction (currently factionless) and given quests to get you above that.

 **Rewards**
Okay, now we get to the meat of things. Suggested items:

Syndicate Emblem - The quests Falconcrest gives you include an item that makes you hostile to SW NPCs but only in Arathi. A reward at Revered to make this a permanent worldwide item would be interesting.

Alterac Banner - Similar to the Stormpike and Alliance banners and sharing a cool down with them. Reward for exalted. The item model is already in the game so it wouldn't be too hard to add.

 **Lowbie rep**
You start this rep grind pretty far into hostile and with Ravenholdt members being the only rep gain option it makes being 50+ highly advisable. Despite this, the questline and zones are more configured for a high30s low 40s character. Another means of getting rep at lower levels would open up options. I think the attack on Ravenholdt is essential to the Syndicate image so I'd cap this rep gain at unfriendly which is high enough to get yourself out of war status with the Syndicate and makes the Ravenholdt section not be too long. As for what this lowbie quest could be I think it could be a turn-in to a friendly Syndicate-aligned NPC. As it happens there is one already: the merchant at Durnholde Keep.

 **Concluding Remarks**
I've always liked Alterac's lore and I'd love to see more done with the Syndicate as well as Isiden Perenolde (wherever he got off to).
