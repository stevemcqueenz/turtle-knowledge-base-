# Hunter (& all others) Pet Collections!

## Post 8211 by Laguntz — 2020-10-20T15:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8211#p8211 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Fun concept imho, expanded pet stables & portable stable item... yes more than one.

STABLE 1 - Hunter only...
Hunter pet standard stable: An expanded stable for pets would allow for play testing of "best pets" for individuals without having to empty an already shallow pool of pets. This would also allow collection of pets as a new "fun thing for hunters".

STABLE 2 - Hunter + all others...
 1. Collection or "Out to pasture" Stable: A stable you can archive old pets in that are only usable as a follow for fun (nostalgia/collection) pet. Would have ability to trade out of?
 2. Pet trading might be a way to give pets to others to collect that aren't hunters, I realize this one may be a huge effort. This might be a monetizer option from the turtle market for donations? I imagine giving a crafted item "Collar and Leash" to another player.

Portable Stable Master/Item:
Sort of a Portable Wormhole for pets... would allow more flexibility for parties with hunters to chose trait specific pets like tanking, ranged dps, melee. Situational but would offer huge flexibility and desirability to a DPS/CC only class. Another donation item to help Turtle wow server!
