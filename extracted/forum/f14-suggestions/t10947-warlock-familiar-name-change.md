I'm not sure if this is wow-specific lore, so please correct me if I'm wrong. I always understood that names have power over demons. To know a demon's name is to control it and have power over it. Part of the binding process is finding out its name. Thus, changing the name of the demon wouldn't really make sense and could even threaten the control you have over it. They are real, conscious creatures. They are not a beast that you lure into loving you so that you can give them whatever name you want.

Maybe a lore appropriate way to get a different name could be to visit a warlock trainer and ask to bind a different demon. Maybe you'd have to give up some soul shards to do it. Then you could re-do the binding quest and get a different random name because you summoned a new random demon.

Or maybe you could utilize a demon hunter npc (like the one in Felwood) and tell him you are after a demon with a certain name. You pay him some gold or soul shards or something he'll make a glyph that you can take to the warlock trainer to summon a demon with a specific name.

Of course, this being a new and different demon, you would have to buy the books from the demon trainer to teach him the higher levels of the spells.
