# Make the Ivus the Forest Lord/Lokholar the Ice Lord blue gear easier to obtain

## Post 58600 by Forbearance (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-23T11:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58600#p58600 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Both [Ivus the Forest Lord](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=13419) and [Lokholar the Ice Lord](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=13256) in Alterac Valley drop unique blue gear that's highly useful and not obtainable anywhere else in the game. The problem is that these two bosses are rarely if ever summoned by players in the battleground. You will also have to roll against 39 other people at most to win the item that drops. These equipment pieces are therefore exceedingly rare.

I suggest making these items obtainable elsewhere, either by crafting professions or by having exalted reputation with your Alterac Valley faction.

The unique blue items that these two bosses drop are:

[Cold Forged Blade](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19110)
[Deep Rooted Ring](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19109)
[Frost Runed Headdress](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19105) (could easily be pre-raid BiS for a frost mage)
[Frozen Steel Vambraces](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19112)
[Winteraxe Epaulets](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19111)
[Yeti Hide Bracers](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19113)

These gear pieces are really quite good and they deserve more distribution than Blizzard originally gave them in my opinion.
