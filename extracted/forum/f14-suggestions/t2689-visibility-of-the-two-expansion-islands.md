Hello there,

I´ve come across many players through these past many weeks who didn´t know that the new expansion brought an entire new zone with it - Lapidis and Gillijim islands. I think you, the developers, should add a quest to Stormwind/Ironforge & Orgrimmar/Undercity leading to the quest hub for each faction on these islands. It could be a recruitment officer, gossip from an innkeeper or whatever you prefer. The npc would ask you to "take a gryphon/bat to FP master of Ally/Horde quest hub west of Stranglethorn Vale." The minimum lvl should be 49 and the quest ends by talking to the Flight master in the quest hub after landing. That should do the trick.

The main point is that the new zone, which you have spent lots of time creating, is currently very neglected and I only see a handful of people going there. In the case of many players it is simply because they don´t know about the content. With my suggestion implemented this could be improved.
