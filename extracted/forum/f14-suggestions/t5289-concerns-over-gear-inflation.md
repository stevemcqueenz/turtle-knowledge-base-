# Concerns over gear inflation

## Post 32149 by Fidget16 — 2023-01-05T23:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32149#p32149 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I understand the desire to flesh out the world and incentivize players to participate in custom content, but some of the custom gear is really strong for its level.

A couple examples:

The lv18 quest ring that was added for, I think its the new Kul Tiras area, is better than the Gnomer ring and similar high 20-mid 30 rewards.

Garden shovel at lv20 is basically a 2hander.

The NElf rep set is better than anything around its level.

The Rogue head from CG, a mid-high 30s 5man, is on par with T2.

Those are off the top of my head.

My concern is that these OP items trivialize leveling, and especially the very strong solo rewards depress interest in 5mans and professions and contribute to the game becoming more single player.

I understand Blizz vanilla had some wonky items in this regard as well (Crowd Pummeler, Wolfshead helm) but adding more of such things doesnt make the game better.
