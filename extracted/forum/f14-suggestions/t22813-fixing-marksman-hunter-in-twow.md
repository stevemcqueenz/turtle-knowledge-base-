# Fixing Marksman Hunter in TWOW

## Post 159132 by ForumGentleman (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-10T23:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159132#p159132 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello everyone,

I'm writing this post after extensive playtime as a Hunter, with a particular focus on Marksman in both PvE and PvP.

Feels to me that Marksman is one of the specs that has received the least amount of custom changes on Turtle WoW. While other classes and specs have been revitalized with new tools and synergies. This has left it feeling lacking and uncompetitive, especially in PvP.

Let me break down the issues:

 **PvE Problems:**

 - Aimed Shot is Underwhelming: The core identity of the Marksman spec is its powerful Aimed Shot. However, in its current state, it's simply not worth the cast time and mana cost compared to spamming Steady Shot.

 - Mana Starvation: Unlike other mana users who have received sustainability tools, Marksman Hunters still struggle significantly with mana.

 **PvP Problems (The More Pressing Issue):**

 -  **Outdated CC Chain:** The Hunter's classic CC chain has been nerfed to the ground. Scatter Shot sharing a Diminishing Return (DR) with Freezing Trap is a big nerf in a meta where every class can use PVP insignia to remove the first trap. So it comes down to the second trap, which is nerfed heabvily by sharing DR with Scatter shot.

 -  **Lack of Impactful Tools:** While Hunter fundamentals  are still strong in open-world or large BGs like Arathi Basin, in duels and Arena, we are bottom tier.  We lack the "punch" and utility that other specs have received. Dead zone, lackluster CC, LOS and limited kiting space make MM a bottom tier spec in Arena and duel (i have almost 3 chars exalted with arena so I speak from experience)

 - **Damage Doesn't Shine: In Battlegrounds**, Yes, we can be a nuisance with ice traps and mana drain, but on the damage front, we are just not there. We are often outshone by other pure DPS classes that have received more significant improvements.

 **Proposed Solutions:**

 **1- Fix the Core PvE Rotation - Rework Aimed Shot:**

Option A (Steady Synergy): Casting Steady Shot grants a stackable buff that reduces the cast time of your next Aimed Shot, potentially making it instant after a few stacks.

Option B (Aimed Empowerment): Add a talent that causes Aimed Shot to empower your next 2-3 Steady Shots, significantly increasing their damage or critical strike chance.

 **2- Critical PvP Fix - Remove Scatter Shot DR:**

Brain dead fix. Scatter Shot should not share a DR with Freezing Trap. In a environment where traps are instantly trinketed, having our two primary CC abilities share a DR is a ridiculous and unfair penalty.

 **3- Give Marksman a PvP Identity (One of the following):**

 - Improve Aimed Shot's Utility: Give Aimed Shot an additional PvP-oriented effect. A 1-2 second silence or a significant armor reduction on the target would fit thematically and give the ability meaningful impact beyond raw damage.

 - Replace Trueshot Aura with a New Capstone Talent: Trueshot Aura is a bland PvE-focused talent. Move it elsewhere in the tree and introduce a new, impactful PvP ability as the 31-point talent. For example:

- Whistling Arrows: *Silences the target for 2 sec and increases their casting time by 20% for 10 sec. (30-40 sec cooldown).*

- Explosive Shot: *Fires a shot that lodges in the target, exploding after 3 sec for 300-450 AoE damage and knocking back the primary target. (1 min cooldown). This would provide much-needed area control and burst.*
 **4-  Quality of Life - Address Mana Issues:**

Add an effect to Trueshot Aura where successful ranged attacks from party members have a low chance to restore 1-2% of their base mana. This would give the aura unique utility and help with the Hunter's (and their group's) sustainability without being overpowered.

Thank you for reading. I'm curious to hear what other Hunters (and players of other classes) think.
