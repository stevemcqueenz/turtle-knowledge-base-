"Morality" is already supported by the social systems as your soft reputation across the server. The reputation was thought as an emergent property of the game systems by the designers.
A more mechanical part of this idea are the honorable/dishonorable kills and reputation faction system.

Entangling moral choice into the quest system is so hard (apparently because of the way the quest system was programmed) that retail can't do it to this day.
