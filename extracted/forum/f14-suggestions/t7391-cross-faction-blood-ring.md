# Cross-faction Blood Ring

## Post 47055 by Tendies (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-22T20:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47055#p47055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

During Dave's livestream Shang said cross-faction battlegrounds destroy character immersion.
This is understandable for WSG, AB and AV but what about cross-faction Blood Ring? When in Blood Ring you already get the free-for-all flag instead of your faction flag as in Gurubashi Arena in STV. Blood Ring and Gurubashi should be these rufian places where anyone can fight for sport. Any race should be able to team up against two other contenders.

On a practical note cross-faction Blood Ring would also fix the ghost queues created by premades since they wouldn't be able to know if they were on the same team or against each other before entering.

EDIT: I realise premades can still create ghost queues by one player entering and using /who if the other team fills (so player one is still alone) then and only then will player two of the premade enter. Still I think cross-faction BR would reduce the number of ghost queues.
