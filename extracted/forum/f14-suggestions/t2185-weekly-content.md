## Post 13401 by Diademuertos — 2021-11-03T13:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13401#p13401 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The one thing I think Turtle wow needs more then anything are 2 Different weekly content creation teams for PvP and pve

Let's face it the People who run turtle wow are extreamly passionate about the server so you can expect a long wait time for new content as they want to make sure all the new content put into the game lives up to the player's expatations

This leaves the need for the 2 teams I mentioned earlier . a team for both PvP and pve who's only job will be to add a fresh Quality world event for both PvP and pve players every week. Doing this will ensure that level 60 maxed out players never run out of things to do while waiting on the next batch of content to be released

A few examples for PvP would be a Storm the keep event where players have to storm the keep belonging to the opposite faction and kill the commander or king of the hill where players have to capture a certain hill and defend it for a certain amount of time

A few examples for pve would be a battle for Light's hope chapel style quest chain where players must defend light's hope from a massive Scourge Attack or a quest chain where players have to prevent a assassination on the faction leaders
