# Oscilation Inhibited Disk Needs to Change

## Post 27010 by Arraris — 2022-10-08T18:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27010#p27010 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know this is just one small new quest, but having done it now I am in the firm belief that the reward from this quest chain needs to be changed or buffed. Quite simply, in it's current state this quest isn't worth doing at all, which I think is a shame.

At this time, the items required for the quest [Tilting Oscilation Inhibitor?!] cost far too much for the reward. Fused wiring is a Gnomeregan drop, aside from a few other rare lvl 37+ mobs in STV, and currently goes for about 1 gold. 6 Strong Flux cost 1 gold 20 silver before reputation discounts. Ignoring the other profession items, this means that you need to do pay over 3 gold to make this shield, or pay at least 1 gold and do Gnomeregan.

Now I want to state that I do not think these item requirements should change. Instead I suggest two things that will make this a more interesting and rewarding quest for those who can find it in the middle of nowhere in the Wetlands.

First, this quest should become a Gnomeregan dungeon quest and should have it's and XP reward bumped accordingly (maybe 3575 XP as a suggestion). This dungeon association sets up the proper expectations about the difficulty/gold sink required for the quest, and will let newer players better understand where Fused Wiring comes from. It will also hopefully drive a few more players to Gnomeregan, which is always a good thing.

Second, the final reward itself should be buffed or moved to a different slot. Even for a lvl 24 shield, the Oscilation Inhibited Disk is not great. It's stats are fairly lackluster for the difficulty to acquire, and it is worse than the shield you can get out of Gnomeregan (Thermaplugg's Central Core).

For this second point, the easy fix would simply be to buff the Oscilation Inhibited Disk to a similar stat lvl (say, 765 armor, 18 block, 6 stam, 5 agi, 5 nature resist. But, I think alternatively, a more interesting and worthwhile option is to turn Oscilation Inhibited Disk into a trinket. It would obviously need to be weak, but perhaps something like: 2 stam, 2 agi, 10% chance to inflict 5-7 Nature damage when struck by a melee attack.

This allows the quest reward to stay tank-oriented, but offer a much more interesting and worthwhile reward for the difficulty to find the initial quest giver, dungeon association, and gold/crafting required to actually finish the chain.

I know this should require the Horde side to get a similar equivalent trinket, which I also think would be great! low level trinkets are really neat and feel amazing to acquire for the player. I love the ones currently added to Turtle Wow   turtle_in_love  and think even more options and variety would be really cool.

Anyway, that's my suggestion. Looking forward to people's thoughts!
