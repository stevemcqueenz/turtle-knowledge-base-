# Underwear modification

## Post 173141 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-30T08:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173141#p173141 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

A transmogging or modifying mechanism can be added in game, just like the options of nail decoration. For example, the bikini wear's colour and shape can be changed without interfering with the combat stats. I'm not expecting a vulgar/porno mechanism, which would have detrimental effects to the game IMO. But, while playing the game, we do need to take off gears sometimes, for avoidance of reducing equipment durability or occasionally a quick wipe. So, the petition of diversifying the underwear's look should be considered legitimate.

By the way, attributes on shirt should be removed. Shirts and underwears are purely detrimental elements. Making them exploitable for stats accumulation would hinder players motivation in decorating their characters. My suggestion is simple. Just make underwear available to modify (you can choose whether it's displayed when a tube or backless top is worn), like the nail art system.
