# Suggestion: More Unique Boat Models for Travel Routes

## Post 42882 by Syrathegreat (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-04-24T17:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42882#p42882 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle has made an amazing addition in repurposing the Darnassian Transport Ship model for Night Elf travel routes and I think it would be worth expanding this concept to different sea lanes for extra immersion!

This can further be carried forward into any new sea routes that will be added, especially with new ones being suggested in the [Patch 1.16.6](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=63&t=5391) WIP preview.

I'm using [this list of models](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Boat#Boat_types_appearing_in_World_of_Warcraft) with various models up to and including the MoP **Alliance Battleship** already having been added as static models in Turtle (the Battleship for example appears in Anchor's Edge in the Barrens recoloured to be Kul Tiran).

- **Rut'theran Village (Teldrassil) > Auberdine (Darkshore)**: *Darnassian Transport Ship*

This route currently uses the Darnassian Transport Ship and I think this is perfect as it the route travels between two Night Elven settlements.

![Image](https://imgur.com/nI55PIv.jpg)

- **Auberdine(Darkshore) > Stormwind:** *Alliance Transport Ship*

This route is currently sailed by a Darnassian Transport Ship, same as the previous, however with the Night Elves' famed isolationism and rather unenthusiastic membership in the Alliance, it would stand to reason that they would be reluctant to facilitate the transport of Alliance adventurers to their lands, even if they allow it. I would propose that this ship be reverted to the standard Alliance Transport Ship to reflect this. Additionally this original change has made the Night Elf ship more prominent than the normal Alliance ship since it now only remains for the less travelled (while early-mid levelling) Theramore route, as well as the Booty Bay route (which will be discussed later). I do appreciate however that from a gameplay perspective it does visually help new players in Stormwind immediately understand where that boat is headed.

![Image](https://imgur.com/X1aKpQR.jpg)

- **Menethil Harbour (Wetlands) > Theramore (Dustwallow Marsh):** *Alliance Transport Ship*

I had originally toyed with the idea of using the [Alliance Battleship](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/e/e1/Human_frigate_WoW.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20121010012134) (flying Theramore colours) as the transport to Theramore since it is ostensibly a well fortified military stronghold, however eventually I decided the current Alliance Transport Ship makes more sense to carry adventurers. However, Turtle please add the battleship model to the Theramore docks please! You even have a pirate ship there yet no military vessels??

![Image](https://imgur.com/C5ovmM2.jpg)

- **Booty Bay (Stranglethorn Vale) > Ratchet (Barrens):** *Pirate Frigate*

Actually the inspiration for this post, with the Goblin territories being fairly lawless lands where money is king, why does the transport have to be an Alliance Transport ship? I understand in original Classic/Vanilla when this was the only option for the transport ship, but since the Darnassian Transport has now opened the door to new models being used, it would be so much more thematic to have a unique model for the Booty Bay boat. The deck is higher than the original transport ship, however vanilla Booty Bay has shown that we can easily use ramps to compensate for this with that city having a downwards ramp to reach the lower transport ship. Additionally with the current replacement of the Auberdine>SW ship with the darnassian one, this means that the most prominent use of the Alliance Transport is between Goblin settlements, which just feels weird to me to be honest.

![Image](https://cs10.pikabu.ru/post_img/2018/09/14/9/153693795813424928.jpg)

- **Feathermoon Stronghold (Feralas) > The Forgotten Coast (Feralas)**: *Darnassian Transport Ship*

This currently uses the standard Alliance Transport but should 100% be Darnassian - this is literally a Night Elf fort in Night Elf territory!
 *(Thanks **Lasershadow** for pointing out I'd missed this one!)*
Please let me know if I've missed anything! And any of your own suggestions! It would be just great flavour to have this variety!
