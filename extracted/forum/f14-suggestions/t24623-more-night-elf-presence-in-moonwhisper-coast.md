Also each faction can bring different aspects and culture.

Talking about culture, this what turtle WoW can fix, playing WC3 or looking at night elves concept art for that game and you can see how much WoW ruined their culture.
They gone from wild and savage protectors of the forest to something more peacefully and that because they mixed the neture of male and female night elves, and there is a WC3 concept art that showcase the huge difference between the male and female in night elf society.
Female are hot headed, fierce warriors, wild protector of the forest, they are the main army of night elf society and do most of the physical work.
Male are more calmer, they are the spiritual leaders of the society, they focus on Druidism and taking care of nature.
For sure not all males or females are the same, we seen cases like Illidan who is not a druid and more like a warrior.

Overall there is savagery and wildness to the night elves culture, turtle wow can bring that back if they expand the night elves lore, just look for WC3 night elves concept arts and you will see how many things can be done with them.
