# Hunter Talent "Swiftshot" Rework

## Post 156159 by Kowys88 — 2025-10-19T09:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156159#p156159 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**The Problem**

The Hunter Talent "Swiftshot" (Reduces CD of Arcane Shot and Aimed Shot by 1 sec) is pretty much a "dead" talent. No self-respecting Hunter takes it in either PvP or PvE.

In addition, there have been many complaints from the community about MM Hunter being underpowered in PvE raiding.

There have also been criticisms about Aimed Shot being severely underutilized to the point of not being used at all in entire raids or battlegrounds, primarily due to its long cast time making it not worth using over Steady Shot.

This suggestion proposes to address all of these issues with a single talent rework.

 **Proposed Solution**

Rework the talent "Swiftshot" to the following:

 **Swiftshot (Row 6, 2 points. Requires Improved Steady Shot)**
"Every cast of Steady Shot reduces the cast time of your next Aimed Shot by 0.5/1 sec."

 **Rationale**

By reducing Aimed Shot cast time down to Multi-shot levels, it gives it a place in a MM Hunter's rotation. This has several advantages:

 1. Makes the MM rotation more interesting and engaging. In addition to giving MM hunters an additional button to press, it introduces new possibilities and choices to the rotation. Would you wait to use Aimed Shot until it is instant cast or do it early while it is at the same cast time as Steady Shot? How does the optimal rotation look? Where does Multishot fit into this?

 2. Brings up MM Hunter's dps in PvE raiding where it is currently underpowered compared to other classes.

 3. Revives a previously "dead" talent in the MM talent tree. The name "Swiftshot" doesn't even need to be changed as it still fits thematically with the cast time reduction.

 4. Revives a previously underutilized ability (Aimed Shot).

All of that, with a single talent rework. Please consider it, thank you.
