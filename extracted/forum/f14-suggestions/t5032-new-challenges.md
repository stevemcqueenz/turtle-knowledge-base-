I think it's a nice idea, just needs a bit of refining.. A few years ago, I made myself a tauren hunter and allowed myself to only ever wear white gear, aside from magical ones that I crafted myself, while my bows (uncraftable weapon type) were allowed to be quest rewards.

It was challenging until about lvl 20, because by then the blues and greens you get from leatherworking (+blacksmithing and tailoring) are really good and very often much better than ordinary quest rewards. Now, if I was actually in a guild, as you suggest, and other people were able to craft everything I was missing, everything would be just as easy as normal gameplay..

 **So, Ironman Challenge glyph? Yeah, but it would have to be like Hardcore on steroids..**

- No magical gear of any kind (except enchantements from other players or yourself)

- No talents allowed

- Only one life, once you die, you're dead

- Only one profession, either primary or secondary (would drastically encourage team play)

- Can only group with players -5 or +5 levels than your own level (to discourage people from boosting their low lvl Ironmen with their 60)
