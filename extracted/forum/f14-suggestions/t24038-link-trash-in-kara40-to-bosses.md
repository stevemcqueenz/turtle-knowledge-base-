# Link trash in kara40 to bosses

## Post 168980 by Lyssebabz2 — 2026-02-20T07:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168980#p168980 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Since the trash isn’t linked to the bosses, skips save so much time that you basically have to do them to compete for #1, which is unfair to guilds that want to clear normally, a reasonable fix would be lightly linking certain trash to bosses (especially the ones before Rock of Desolation) without overdoing it so casual guilds aren’t punished.

Gnarlmoon = All worgen type mobs in the start

Incantagos = Manascale Overseer

Anomalus = Arcane Overflows - Or a new mob since these appear before and after Anomalus

Medivh = Lingering Arcanist/enchanter - Or a new mob since these appear before and after Medivh

Chess = Crumbling Protector for example, they don't appear afterwards and are mechanical like Chess

Ima'Ghaol = Make Orb of Teleportation unusable without him dead

Sanv = Could be some of the Broken Draenei packs, not to many tho.

Rupturan = This I am unsure about, could be Infernals but they dont fit to well imo.

Kruul = We have linked mobs
