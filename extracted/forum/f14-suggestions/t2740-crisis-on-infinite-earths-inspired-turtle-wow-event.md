# Crisis on Infinite Earths inspired Turtle wow event

## Post 17164 by Superuniverse — 2022-02-21T02:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17164#p17164 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

i just finished watching the crisis crossover event on Supergirl and it got me thinking maybe the Turtle wow team could create a similar Crisis on Azeroth event for players to participate in

the main backstory will be a new cult of Warlocks and mages comprised of Magic users from both the Horde and Alliance who call themselves the children of the Night have unleashed a deadly Anti-light Shadow wave across azeroth which will Destroy the world unless Stopped

the event will be divided into three separate patches each with a unique quest chain

the first patch will open with the first signs of the Anti-light Shadow wave as it causes environmental destruction across Azeroth such as Earthquakes and tsunamis resulting in several towns catching fire and or being swept away away by the rising waters

on top  of that a force of water Elementals, imps and felguards summoned by the  children of the Night have emerged to sweep across azeroth to weaken the mortal defenses against the Anti-light Shadow wave

the first patch has players working with the argent dawn in evacuating and securing the most devastated areas with a unique Quest chain comprised of 20 quests available for both the eastern kingdoms and Kalimdor

the second patch opens with a demon/elemental Attack on the Horde and Alliance capitals resulting in all the faction leaders being abducted so that their life-force can be used to fuel the Anti-light Shadow wave

the Argent Dawn once again rallies the horde and Alliance in a Assault on the Fortress of the Night which will be a new dungeon within Darkshore

players are tasked with Fighting through the fortress and rescuing the abducted faction leaders before the ritual can be completed and the Anti-light Shadow wave becomes stronger as a result

the Fortress will be divided into 2 sections

the lower level will house Magni Bronzebeard, Gelbin Mekkatorque, Sylvanas windrunner and Cairne Bloodhoof

the Upper level will house Anduin Wrynn, Bolvar Fordragon, Thrall, Vol'jin and Varok Saurfang

the final patch will comprise a extremely long and exiting unique Quest chain where players go on a epic Quest chain to learn the nature of the Anti-light Shadow wave, create a Device to Stop it while also Destroying the Children of Night in the process

there will be two new Dungeons

the Fortress of Night located in Darkshore where life draining rituals are conducted

the Temple of Nightfall where the Anti-light Shadow wave is being powered and channeled from.

i think a special set of armor and weapons should be made available from the Argent quartermaster as a reward for completing the event and there should be a set for each class

i would really appreciate any comments on this

Superman and Supergirl are my 2 favorite comic book characters and I really loved the crisis on infinite earths storyline which is where i drew the insperation for the Crisis on Azeroth event
