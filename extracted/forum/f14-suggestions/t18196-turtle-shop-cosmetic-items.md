# Turtle Shop Cosmetic Items

## Post 123987 by Rowdy Beerchugger — 2025-03-26T18:16:00Z

Greetings!
As a long time RPer and someone who's deeply in love with transmogging. I want to suggest to the devs or whoever is in charge of the turtle shop to bring back certain items which were buyable before.
As an example, I intended to buy the blue mountaineer hood for my character, but sadly it was removed.
I understand the need for a dynamic shop, but non-seasonal items shouldn't be removed from it. Hallow's End items should be available during the halloween event, same with Winter's Veil etc.
However, low-fantasy items which an everyday RPer would love to buy, display and use for their perfect transmog should stay in the shop at all times.
