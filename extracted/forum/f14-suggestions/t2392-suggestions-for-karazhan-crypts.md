# Suggestions for Karazhan Crypts

## Post 14969 by Haroone — 2021-12-29T07:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14969#p14969 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are some aspects of the new dungeon that feel somewhat unfair. I want to articulate my thoughts properly and not sound like I'm whining, but these things are frustrating to deal with and could actually improve over time as people get accustomed to the dungeon more. BEWARE OF SPOILERS AHEAD!

 1. Parts of the dungeon have the graveyard upon death, set to Raven Hill Cemetery, Duskwood. It occurred in the underwater section of the dungeon. I believe the GY should be adjusted to the Deadwind Pass GY that the rest of the dungeon uses.
 2. The bats at the entrance and the skeletons after the big drop have very short respawn timers. When coupled with the Duskwood GY run, the respawning bats can't be helped. If one person dies and can't be revived (opposing faction released spirit), the whole group needs to help them get back, but can't climb back up to the entrance, so they have to die too. This could be alleviated to varying extents by changing the following;
Increasing the time before the mobs respawn, fixing the GY spawn from Point 1, and/or reducing the number of bats spawned since it's hard for classes with little to no consistent aoe to kill all the bats.
 3. When leaving the water section to fight Corpsemuncher, there are 4 skeletal guards and 1 frost spectre. It feels unfair with how much damage they do when all are aggro'd and we were able to abuse the swimming aggro reset after killing the spectre.
I like the skeletal guards' ability, but I think they do too much damage to have 4 there all at once and requiring the water reset abuse. It makes it harder as mana users can't drink during this time without some form of water walking.
 4. The water section that allows water-breathing feels inconsistent with the rest of the water sections.
I don't really have any recommendations for this, but perhaps some decorative clams on the bottom of the floor providing air would make more sense for that small section only being breathable.
 5. Mob placement seems random and lacks structure. Stealth mobs aside, the skeleton and zombie placements are pretty strangely positioned in the main corridor after the water section and the skeletons in the beginning after the drop are stretched across the room very strangely. I don't have much issue with the stealth mobs since once you're familiar with them, you should be more wary on your advancement through the dungeon.
The amount of skeletons placed randomly feels like they shouldn't be elites or as durable as they are and running around the wall's edge feels like it's cheating almost, to get through the rooms. If any decorative pieces could be added to create pathways and mob positions could be rearranged into proper mob packs, I think it would feel less messy in those larger areas, although I'm not entirely sure if the decoration idea is possible or would make sense.
 6. The bosses feel fun and while I didn't get to the last few rooms due to my previous points bogging down the experience and increasing the run time, those fights were still enjoyable. I do wish the Guard Captain had some kind of loot, even if it was a couple of possible greens, since he's unique compared to the other skeletons and ghouls.

These are my thoughts and suggestions on my experience through the Karazhan Crypts. I think it's a got a great theme and ideas and the boss fights are a lot of fun, but the current execution of the non-boss encounters and traversal makes the experience feel like a slog. Hopefully things get better from here, but I still appreciate everything the Turtle Team has done for the expansion so far and I'm having a lot of fun with the new additions.
