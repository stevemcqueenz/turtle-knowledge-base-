Germany has a reputation for strict IP enforcement.

German copyright and trademark laws are aggressive, and Blizzard (or any rights holder) can get court injunctions quickly.

German ISPs and hosting providers are often legally required to cooperate fast when presented with a DMCA-equivalent or Abmahnung (legal warning letter).

UK, while not lax, tends to have a slower and more expensive legal process for cross-border IP enforcement.

A cease-and-desist still has teeth, but getting court orders, enforcing them, and seizing data often requires more back-and-forth, especially when the claimant is outside the UK.

Result:
Germany = “swift shutdown” risk.
UK = “slower, more costly to enforce” for Blizzard.
