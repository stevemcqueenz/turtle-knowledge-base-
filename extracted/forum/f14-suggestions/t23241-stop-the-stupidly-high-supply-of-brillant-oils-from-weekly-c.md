> **Drubarrymooer wrote: Thu Dec 11, 2025 11:15 am**
> Is it deflation or inflation.  The op says it's both.  I'm with Escaton on this one.  The less it costs to consume, the better.  There are plenty of ways to make gold in this game.

High pop realms have inflation due to the world nodes not supplying enough materials and due to the excess of players grinding stuff and generating raw gold from questing and vendoring greys.

to curb this inflations measures were introduced, like replacing gold generating drops on lashers with potion materials, adding a lot of gold sinks like weapon quests etc. in short: artificially increase materials and consume supply and reduce the amount of gold in the economy to stabilize prices.

On an average to low pop realm where world resources can sustain the population and where there isn't a pressure on players to grind stuff, this creates the exact opposite:
the average player is inundated with consumes and materials, everything that can be produced by players is cheap, the average player makes less gold but the gold sinks and prices for repairs, vendor materials and reagents is still the same. that's Deflation.

And that leads to a bad kind of subsistence economy where you'll need to make everything by yourself cause trade in the AH is dead: you can't buy the cheap shit because you can't sell shit.

My consume bill went down from 400g to 200g but somehow it's not cheaper because I'm making less gold yet cost of repairs and reagents is still the same. And now i have to do extra grinds i didn't have to do before to basically mint gold when raw gold farms are actively being nerfed.

We're at the other edge of the balance where we don't need consumes, we need raw gold.

New weekly quests that reward raiding consumes and important trade goods is what has really corrupted this economy.

Before this change was made, players could only obtain these items through professions (and maybe lasher farm to some extend). It was natural chain of economy:
- Herbs -> Alchemy -> Consumables
So basically, all herbalists were at the bottom of this chain.

 1.18.0 has added new weekly quests that work like this:
- Do something in game -> Consumables
So now consumables ultimately don't come only from herbalists. Now every player that completes this quest, generates consumables out of thin air.
