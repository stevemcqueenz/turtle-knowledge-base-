This is something multiple people have asked for before, and I would like to ask it now: Please give more varients to Naga Illusions from the donation shop.

Right now, there is a single color variant for female naga illusion, which is the least desirable one, imo - yellow with purple fins.
Naga found all over the world exist with at least four different color variants: most common form of naga siren is IN FACT: teal one with orange/yellow fins, not the yellow one - see Spitelash Siren in Azshara zone for reference.

Another siren variants are:
Stormscale Sirens in Darkshore - who have blue/yellow (scale/fins)
Hatecrest Sirens in Feralas - dark green/purple
Daggerspine Sirens in Hilsbrad Foothills - blue/green

Please consider adding these as option.
