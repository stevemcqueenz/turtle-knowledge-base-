# Scaling Dungeons

## Post 174734 by Afraido23 — 2026-04-10T20:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174734#p174734 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

You know what would be cool? If we could go to low level dungeons as level 60 and the dungeons are scaled to level 60. Loot stays the same but we get weekly crests and fashion coins. Most people will never try the new dungeons at their level, so it would be an amazing opportunity for people to try new content with some challenge and small reward.

## Post 174735 by Zeran (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-04-10T20:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174735#p174735 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

There's a possible side effect here: people won't level any alts and will just do all content on their level 60 chars, which will increase Q times for everyone.

Yes, maybe it other 60's are also Q'ing the same dungeon it won't be so bad, but there's no guarantee they will be Q'ing that exact same dungeon. Whereas when you are in e.g. a level 14-20 level range, there are only a few dungeons you can do, so it narrows it down and helps Q times.

So yeah, in the end, it increases Q times.
