# Return of Crusader Strike ideas and calculations

## Post 17045 by Hugswithcuddle7 — 2022-02-15T21:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17045#p17045 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone whoever decided to read this, hopefully you are doing well today.
This is my suggestion/idea for the return of  "Crusader Strike". It was implied that it will finally be brought back, so just some thoughts are written here. I did some calculations.

For those who didn't know, the original "Crusader Strike" was an ability of WoW paladin before Vanilla. This ability was officially removed from the game on the patch that released WoW. It dealt damage based on rank (static damage) and increased Holy damage taken by the affected target by amount based on rank. It stacked up to five times, so it was similar to the "Sunder Armor" skill of warriors. Another similarity between CS and SA is that both didn't have cooldowns.

Here is information on the ranks of Crusader Strike that the paladins might regain after the update is complete, with rank, mana cost, static damage, amount that holy damage taken is increased :

Rank 1: 25 Mana, 12 Damage, 6 Bonus Holy damage per stack. × 5 = 30 ;
Rank 2: 40 Mana, 27 Damage, 10 Bonus Holy damage per stack. × 5 = 50 ;
Rank 3: 55 Mana, 46 Damage, 15 Bonus Holy damage per stack. × 5 = 75 ;
Rank 4: 70 Mana, 69 Damage, 22 Bonus Holy damage per stack. × 5 = 110 ;
Rank 5: 90 Mana, 103 Damage, 30 Bonus Holy damage per stack. × 5 = 150 .

The " × 5 = " is to show how much Holy damage taken by the affected target would be increased, if the CS was applied five times. That way some ranks of it seem to correlate very well with the ability that replaced it in the original game, Seal of the Crusader.

"Seal of the Crusader" ranks in Classic WoW by its  "Judgment" effect and mana cost :

Rank 1 at LVL 6 = 20 holy damage plus, mana cost = 25 ;
Rank 2 at LVL 12 = 30 Holy Damage plus, mana cost = 40 ;
Rank 3 at LVL 22 = 50 Holy Damage plus, mana cost = 65 ;
Rank 4 at LVL 32 = 80 Holy damage plus, mana cost = 90 ;
Rank 5 at LVL 42 = 110 Holy damage plus, mana cost = 125 ;
Rank 6 at LVL 52 = 140 Holy damage plus, mana cost = 160 ;

We have been informed that "Crusader Strike" debuffs probably won't stack together with "Judgement" from "Seal of the Crusader". So I did some recalculations for if the Holy Damage debuff was just removed from the ability, making CS just a spammable spell for damage.

Crusader Strike baseline mana cost recalculated without debuff that increases Holy Damage taken by affected target :

Rank 1 = 20 mana cost for 12  damage ;
Rank 2 = 27 mana cost for 27  damage ;
Rank 3 = 37 mana cost for 46 damage ;
Rank 4 = 45 mana cost for 69  damage ;
Rank 5 = 58 mana cost for 103 damage .

Fair amount of static damage if mana cost stays the same as originally, but the debuff effect is removed :

Rank 1 = 25 mana cost for 16  damage ;
Rank 2 = 40 mana cost for 40  damage ;
Rank 3 = 55 mana cost for 68 damage ;
Rank 4 = 70 mana cost for 107 damage ;
Rank 5 = 90 mana cost for 160 damage .

Because their other main damaging abilities tend to have noticeable cooldowns (Judgment 10, 9 or 8 second; Consecration - 20 second; Holy Strike - 8 second), a "spammable" damaging attack might seem very out of place for original WoW's Paladin. However just adding a cooldown would be a terrible idea, due to its low damage. So I did calculations for its damage if the cooldown was between 3 seconds and up to 3.75 seconds.

Calculation of "Crusader Strike" damage if it had a 3 or 3.75 second cooldown and if the level calculations based on former debuff are also correct :

LVL 12, Rank 1 = 35  damage ;
LVL 22, Rank 2 = 75  damage ;
LVL 32, Rank 3 =  144 damage ;
LVL 42, Rank 4 =  215 damage ;
LVL 52, Rank 5 =  300 damage .

"Crusader Strike" is a melee attack that deals physical damage with the amount of it being based on its rank. That is why it should be nice for leveling Paladins, it could improve the gameplay and class fantasy while doing so.
The damage of "Crusader Strike" doesn't increase based on the Paladin's weapon or stats, so at the time of raiding they should be outclassed by classes that are meant to outclass them, such as Warriors who scale very well with gear.

Paladin ability that scales on the weapon and stats of the Paladin is known as "Holy Strike". It converts the whole damage of next melee attack into Holy Damage and adds some more, this means that with a good weapon gear and stats, a Paladin can happily use only it's first Rank even at later levels. It also ignores armor and is enhanced by “Judgment” from “Seal of the Crusader”. That's not the same with CS. The damage for CS would have to be increased a lot for it to share a cooldown with HS, but even then it would be a bad idea.

Anyways, hopefully this was fun to read. I hope to know your thoughts on this.
