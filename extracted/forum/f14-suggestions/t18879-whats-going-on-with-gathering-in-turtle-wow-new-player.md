Hi i just leveled up to lvl 60 in Turtle WoW and this is my thoughts.

So my 2 cents, Gathering with mining and herbing is just sad in Turtle WoW, i have played in Vanilla/classic/SoD and now Turtle WoW and i must say its boring as hell with the gathering. The respawn rate is terrible and the Rich Thorium Vein is almost all gone replaced with Small Thorium Vein. That was the jackpot when you find one and the Gemstone Deposits isnt just that exciting to be honest. Same goes for fishing school respawn time, and the numbers of them.
