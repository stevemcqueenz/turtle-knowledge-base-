# 4th talent tree for each class

## Post 127816 by Mrkrissatan (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-21T12:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127816#p127816 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ok, I've rethought my original idea of prestige classes and come up with this.

Warrior — Warlord

Core Fantasy: Battlefield commander who buffs allies and seizes objectives.
Role: Hybrid melee DPS / raid utility.
Mechanics:

Tactical Orders (resource): Generate “Orders” by dealing damage; spend to grant nearby allies bonus damage/resilience for a short time.

Stance Toggle: Switch between Aggressive Command (increases ally damage dealt) and Defensive Command (increases ally damage reduction).
Signature Abilities:

Rallying Cry: Converts 3 Orders into a massive Morale buff, increasing party haste and movement speed.

War Banner: Deploys a banner that pulses nearby allies with 5 % crit and reduces slowed effects.

Shock Offensive: A leap that deals damage and applies an aura, granting allies further AoE damage.

 ---

Paladin — Inquisitor

Core Fantasy: Zealous crusader rooting out corruption and dealing righteous damage.
Role: Melee DPS with targeted utility.
Mechanics:

Zeal Stacks: Build up Zeal by landing consecration or judgment effects; spend to empower your next hammer strike.

Combat Judgments: Multiple judgment runes you can “brand” enemies with for on‐hit bonuses.
Signature Abilities:

Brand of Penance: A consumable judgment that causes the target to take increased Holy damage over time.

Hammer of Wrathful Light: Consumes all Zeal to smash enemies in a cone, dealing massive burst.

Sanctified Ground: A ground‐targeted aura that heals allies when they strike enemies within its radius.

 ---

Hunter — Stalker

Core Fantasy: Predator who blends stealth, traps, and lethal melee strikes.
Role: Melee/ranged hybrid DPS.
Mechanics:

Predator’s Mark: Automatically applies a stacking debuff that you can detonate for bonus damage.

Trap Mastery: Carry three specialized traps (snap, net, smoke); resetting on kills.
Signature Abilities:

Shadow Pounce: Teleport to your marked prey for a guaranteed critical strike.

Crippling Net: Immobilizes and silences the target, empowering your next melee finish.

Vanish into Mists: Enter stealth instantly and restore a charge of all traps.

 ---

Rogue — Phantomblade

Core Fantasy: Spectral assassin who phases between this world and the next.
Role: Melee burst / mobility.
Mechanics:

Spectral Energy: Build by attacking; spend to phase—becoming untargetable for 2 sec and leaving behind a phantom clone.

Dual‐plane Attacks: When phased, your next attack deals bonus damage and applies on‐hit effects twice (once in each plane).
Signature Abilities:

Wraith Strike: Strike deals damage in both planes, generating large Spectral Energy.

Blade of the Beyond: Throw a ghostly dagger that returns, damaging enemies both ways.

Ethereal Ambush: A cooldown that immediately phases you and teleports behind your target.

 ---

Priest — Soulbinder

Core Fantasy: Weaver of souls—drawing power from life and death to bolster allies or weaken foes.
Role: Hybrid support / shadow DPS.
Mechanics:

Soul Fragments: Generated by healing or damaging spells; spend to empower next ability.

Essence Channels: Toggle to convert a portion of spell healing into Shadow damage and vice versa.
Signature Abilities:

Life‐Leech Grasp: Heal an ally and siphon a portion back to you as mana.

Soul Rend: Consume 5 Fragments to rip enemy souls, dealing AoE damage and silencing.

Ethereal Shield: Shield an ally; when it breaks, releases a wave of shadow that damages nearby enemies.

 ---

Shaman — Spiritwarden

Core Fantasy: Guardian of the ancestral spirits, balancing elemental and spiritual forces.
Role: Off‐tank / support.
Mechanics:

Spirit Bonds: Place up to three bonds on allies or totems; each grants different bonuses (healing, damage, or defense).

Ancestral Echo: Your healing and damage spells recharge at reduced cost when cast on bonded targets.
Signature Abilities:

Spirit Totem: Summons a totem that cycles through healing, damage, and shielding pulses.

Ancestral Bulwark: Consumes bonds to grant a strong damage‐absorbing shield to you or an ally.

Echoing Wrath: Unleash ancestral spirits to stun and damage enemies in a line.

 ---

Mage — Spellbreaker

Core Fantasy: Arcane knight who shatters magic with martial prowess.
Role: Melee burst / anti‐caster.
Mechanics:

Anti‐Magic Charge: Build by parrying or dodging spells; spend to empower melee strikes to interrupt and dispel.

Arcane Residue: When near enemies you’ve interrupted, you gain small percent buffs to damage and movement.
Signature Abilities:

Shatterstrike: A two‐handed strike that dispels one buff from the enemy and stuns them if it hits a caster.

Arcane Breaker: A ground AoE that steals a portion of incoming magic, converting it to health for you.

Countermagic Stance: For 6 sec, your melee attacks have a chance to nullify a spell effect entirely.

 ---

Warlock — Dreadlord

Core Fantasy: Master of forbidden pacts who summons Legion‐touched horrors.
Role: Caster DPS with pet control.
Mechanics:

Demonic Pacts: Three pact runes—each gives a different pet augmentation (damage, utility, survival).

Corruption Echo: Your DoTs linger on death, spawning mini‐imps that seek new targets.
Signature Abilities:

Felconic Invocation: Summon a dreadwalker that absorbs damage from you and retaliates.

Soulrip Chains: Roots multiple enemies; if they break free prematurely, they take heavy damage.

Pact of Ruin: Sacrifice health to grant your pet and spells massive haste for a short window.

 ---

Druid — Wilderkin

Core Fantasy: Primal shapeshifter channeling the raw fury of nature.
Role: Melee/off‐tank hybrid.
Mechanics:

Primal Rage: Stack by dealing or taking damage; spend to empower your next form change.

Form Infusions: Each shapeshift form (Cat, Bear, Moonkin, Tree) can be “infused” by spending Rage to add a secondary form’s passive.
Signature Abilities:

Feral Fury: In Cat form, consume Rage for a flurry of blows that each apply bleed and slow.

Stonebark Charge: In Bear form, charge an enemy, stunning and rooting them while you shift into Tree form.

Celestial Blossom: In Moonkin/Tree hybrid, bloom a massive area that heals allies and damages enemies.
