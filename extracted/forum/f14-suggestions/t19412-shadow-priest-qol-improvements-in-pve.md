# Shadow Priest QoL improvements in PvE

## Post 131963 by Keds (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-19T15:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131963#p131963 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Taking a look at all the changes made for all classes and with PvE in mind, many got some nice first row talent changes, paladin with improved blessings, shaman with ancestral knowledge, hunter with improved slaying and swift reflexes, warlock with soul entrapment, and so on...

While the wand specialization is nice for leveling, late game in raids its not really useful, and this is the same for Spirit Tap, since for it to trigger you gotta deal the killing blow, so it's kinda impossible in a raid environment, and the alternative first row talent is Blackout, a PVP talent, so not really a good option either. Mental agility for shadow priests seems weak compared to other classes, now that Vampiric Embrace is not instant, it doesn't benefit from it anymore.

I have some suggestions:

 1 - Swap positions Mental Agility and Silent Resolve.
or
 2 - Combine Shadow Focus with Piercing Light.
or
 3 - Move Shadow Focus or Improved Mind Blast to be on the first row.

We got a new interaction with Holy Nova, but it's a weird mechanic and also kinda "wasting" 10 points on the holy tree just to get this is a real pain.

New talent ideias would be:

 1 - Reduce pushback, maybe combine this into shadow focus 5% per point, total of 25%.
 2 - Revert the Holy nova change and add a Shadow Nova
