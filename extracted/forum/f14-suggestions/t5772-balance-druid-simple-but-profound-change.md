# Balance Druid simple but profound change

## Post 36229 by Kub — 2023-02-26T21:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36229#p36229 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all,

Much love and hope you're all doing well. I recently discovered Turtle WoW and I love the thoughtful and detailed discussions on what changes could be put in place to improve the game. I wanted to participate with a suggestion to address how balance is (imo) still very far behind other specs in terms of being viable for both leveling and end-game.

I have a very simple change that I think not only gives them a small bump, but also introduces a much more interesting element to their gameplay and itemization goals. Here it is... Either an item (perhaps from a druid quest), talent, or a skill, acquired early on, that allows the balance druid to auto-attack while casting.

It's important that this is acquired early on as this would greatly improve the leveling process.

Impacts:

 -Class fantasy/archetype: Classic Balance druid spec has always seemed like it was an unfinished product. There were a few directions (or combinations of these) balance druid could have gone if it had been fleshed out more. 1) a relatively tanky caster 2) an actual tank caster (imagine if they had introduced some higher threat and more mitigation) 3) a caster/melee hybrid 4) a very supportive caster.

It seems all of these possibilities were abandoned and instead balance was left being next to worthless in every regard. Even the moonkin aura is so miniscule when put up against the amount of damage lost by swapping a moonkin with any other ranged dps (okay except sp). In my opinion, the best approach today would be to pursue option 3.

 -Itemization: this small change would make gearing balance druids dramatically more interesting. Suddenly the tier 0, 0.5, 2.5, and many other items that don't see a lot of play would be quickly become highly valuable to the balance druid. Balance druids would likely always have 2 2hers, one with greater caster stats, and one with greater melee stats that could be swapped in when mana is low and they hold off on casting. Items like Herald of Woe, Maul of the Redeemed Crusader, Fist of Cenarius, Hammer of Divine Might, etc. all go from borderline useless items to highly sought after by the Balance druid.

 -PvP: Balance druid is terrible in pvp currently, this would not put them over the top at all, but it certainly would improve their lot overall in pvp.

Anyways, I really hope if you enjoyed this suggestion if you took the time to read it. I'd love to hear feedback and also if it's possible, I'm curious if the devs have a way of prototyping this? I'd gladly get on and play it in greater detail to test how it feels.

Much love

There's a lot being said in the thread I disagree with but in spirit I do agree balance still needs something.

Feral does well on turtle at the moment when properly geared and your rotations are properly executed. Resto is fine, a bit behind other healers but overall not bad at all. Anyone saying otherwise hasn't been raiding here beyond MC. Balance is also good in pvp with proper positioning since it can 2 shot anyone, it's just not busted. With a crit, I've 1-shot more than one person in pvp, but standing still for 3 seconds isn't exactly a powerful move when fighting a team.

The tier sets aren't really suitable for other specs but TWoW has mentioned they're making "tier-adjacent" raid content which most likely means we're getting balance and feral gear with set bonuses in the new raids coming out. I'm not worried about the gear problem and they've added some fantastic items in the custom dungeons and world bosses for boomies specifically that greatly help and I'm certain their new items will continue this trend and add some new BiS items that don't all compete with warlocks and mages for priority.

The big issues for leveling boomie are what they always were: long spell casts that are mana hungry and not a lot of help from gear. Omen of clarity greatly helps the longevity in fights and gives you a way to actually still do something when you're oom, but overall it just isn't enough while leveling. Mana issues would need to be resolved for leveling balance to be anything other than a teeth-grinding exercise.

When raiding, it's the opposite. I don't really run into mana issues raiding anymore with the new changes and, using easy-to-acquire raid consumes like tea and weaksauce, it takes quite a while to oom. Instead, the issue is overall damage which is hampered by a 3-second cast being virtually the only spell used. If boomkins want to compete with other casters, a change to the raw damage in some way for raids is needed. Maybe add a proc like nightfall gives to boomkin spell casts on crit, or allow Faerie Fire to also reduce magic resistance to nature/arcane of the target by some amount. Maybe Insect Swarm can be specced to add a secondary DoT that stacks up to a cap. Just anything that won't affect pvp really but will have a profound effect in raids and dungeons. This can only really be done through buffs to spells not commonly used in pvp but are more viable in raids. Nothing would be stopping a druid from using these spells in pvp, of course, but it probably wouldn't be as viable to spam debuffs on targets than to just root -> starfire -> starfire/wrath.
