# Yet another PvP suggestion - Leaderboards

## Post 12522 by Adelaidde — 2021-09-24T06:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12522#p12522 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Recently I got really into PvP, always trying to be on top, trying to go for the City Protector Title, and i caught myself inspecting other players to see their honor very frequently. I think i'm not the only one who's interested in seeing the standings so I thought of a way to display it. How about we put a scoreboard/leaderboard in the capital cities, (just like the bounty board) that would display the top 5 people in the standing so everyone can easily check instead of running around inspecting and guessing? I think it would only do good for the PvP community encouraging competitive spirit. Maybe the Leaderboard could reset daily, but if it cannot be implemented to show real-time standings, it could show who was the top 5 standing for last week and it would reset when the usual reset is, on Wednesdays. Thank you for the consideration.

Opinions?
