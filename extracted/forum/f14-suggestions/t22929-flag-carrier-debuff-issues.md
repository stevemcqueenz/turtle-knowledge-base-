# Flag Carrier Debuff - Issues.

## Post 160086 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-11-19T16:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160086#p160086 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Okay so, this mechanic was implemented to stop people standing in GY and all turtling the FC preventing long games.

I guess it sort of solved that issue but one big issue had arisen for us in a game last night:

we got up to 15 stacks, and both flags were returned, the score was 2 : 1, the opposing team decided to troll us for about 30 mins by just turtling in the Flag room, the problem here was, as soon as anyone picked up the flag, they had straight away got the 150% dmg debuff, so it was impossible to even move anyones flag out of the FR.

So in the end, this mechanic solved NOTHING, games were still turtling just in a different way.

What needs to happen is the debuff needs to be reset when the flags are returned back to the base, or it creates an issue where one person defending can stop anyone even leaving the FR if you''re taking 150% dmg soon as you pick the flag.

Also I would change the damage buff to a healing debuff instead, this gives good skilled FC's a chance to actually do something ,rather than just be Oneshot at anything over 5-6 stacks.
