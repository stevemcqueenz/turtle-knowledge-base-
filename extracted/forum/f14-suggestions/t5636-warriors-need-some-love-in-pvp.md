## Post 35195 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-19T00:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35195#p35195 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shield Wall reduces damage taken, but doesn't make you stop being almost dead.

Last Stand is temp HP, so it's more of a "Buy time for someone else to heal you." or "I just need a few more seconds to finish this."

Lifegiving Gem is another temp HP.

Frenzied Regeneration is a fixed amount of healing rather than a percent, over time rather than all at once, doesn't clear status effects, has a much shorter cooldown, and most importantly is a Druid ability. The only relevance I'd give it here is as evidence of a precedent for "Rage to Healing" abilities.

Design wise, I wrote out Second Wind with the concept of a Paladin's Lay On Hands that you get at a later level, requires setup to get the full healing from, and can only be used on yourself, but in exchange gets a reduced CD (still much longer than most abilities) and some semi-niche status removal.

Also, I'd argue that self-heal is less valuable to raid tanks than other specs, because they're already accompanied by healers who have them as a priority target.
