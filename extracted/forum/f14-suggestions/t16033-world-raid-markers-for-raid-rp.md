# World Raid Markers , for raid/rp

## Post 109230 by Aleyar — 2024-11-21T15:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109230#p109230 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am aware that either Guildmasters or someone with a an item is able to place world makers for raids. (runes on the ground?). But for the rest of us it seems sadly there is nothing as such that I am aware of.

These tools are extremely useful for RPers and raiders alike.

If i am running a pug group and want to place markers I have sadly seemingly no way to do this or for that matter in RP. In RP its very useful for people running events to indicate where an "NPC" is without having to request the direct support of a GM ( as awesome as it is, at times it might be unavailable or might be the spur of the moment)

My suggestion is:
Give the tool to place raid markers for RL that lasts until cancelled or moved too far away. That is -only- visible to raid members. That be either the floating style, rune on ground or anything really. Just something to indicate a position in the world.

It wont bother anyone else. Very useful for raiders and RPers alike.

Note: If I am just not aware of such a tool, then i have just missed out.
Its pretty much the only feature I miss from retail atm. :/
