# [Discussion thread] toward a new twow honor system

## Post 92189 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-04-06T07:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92189#p92189 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Authoring this thread as an average pvp enjoyer and Tel'abim lover with some experience from ranking on classic and twow. I mean for this to be a discussion thread where we can provide input for the dev team so they can eventually, hopefully, revamp the honor system. I am aware twow wants to keep the 'vanilla' experience, however with the class changes and new dungeons the servers are already well past that. It is time to give attention to the honor system too.

So, my initial suggestions, which are far from perfect and will benefit from input:

 1) In general it will be important get a recognized classic pvp player/streamer to join the team in some form and oversee the development. A plus side of this is it may draw outside attention to the server/project.

 2) Honor system: Take the TBC recipe and make honor a currency. The grind to full rank14 gear could take just as long, with items costing in the ballpark of 1-3 million honor. But rewards along the way will help incentivising the grind and attract players who may only want 1-2 items. What keeps players away from ranking is the well-known grind; Twow, with an alternative system, could attract players who are thrown off by this.
 2b) If incoming alternative tier sets make honor sets redundant, you could have the tier tokens as an alternative reward. Better though is to update the honor sets instead.
 2c) Pvp titles: Keep them by providing a quest or similar. Once you have the full r12-13 set you can obtain the title of Warlord. Once you have that and a r14 item you can obtain the title of High Warlord. And so on. Or, leave the titles and let the players who suffered through to r13-14 until now keep it as a badge of honor.

 3) World pvp: Triple the honor gained from wpvp kills. Also I have suggested some changes in previous threads:
[viewtopic.php?t=13320](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13320)
[viewtopic.php?t=13479](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13479)
 3b) Weekly quest to kill a player of a certain race ('collect the head) from the opposite faction in wpvp. Basically, same quest as in AV, only in world. Reward: Honor and some gold.
 3c) Weekly quest to kill 50-100 players of the opposite faction in world pvp. Reward: More honor, more gold, and a lucky coin (why not).

 4) Experience from pre-60 BGs and wpvp kills. To incentivise leveling through pvp.

These are some ideas, I might have more later and as a disclaimer I am certainly no expert on the underlying mechanics of the ranking system as it works today. Now I encourage people to add well thought through, constructive, and well-written ideas. I ask everyone to refrain from mentioning class imbalances and keep this thread about the honor system specifically. Class changes are already coming. Further, I recognize that some of these suggestions are likely already suggested on here in some version or another - consider this a summary of some ideas I've collected on here. Now let's help the twow team!

Thanks again for hosting great servers. Tel'abim has a great community of players and deserves to be a top pvp destination.
