The thing about War Mode is that you mistakenly think it's meant to be a means to emulate choosing a PvP server. I thought so too for a while.

It's not. It's a challenge, with a tangible reward associated with sticking with it. It's not meant to be fair, or even necessarily fun if you're not into the adrenaline rush of Solid Snake'ing past opposing faction players that could breathe on you and turn you into ash.

Like PvP in general here it's just a means to an end, not a pillar of the game's structure deserving of much respect.
