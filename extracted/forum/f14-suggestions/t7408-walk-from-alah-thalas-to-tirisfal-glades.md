# Walk from Alah'Thalas to Tirisfal Glades

## Post 47161 by Kanto123 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-23T19:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47161#p47161 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The walk from Southshore to Scarlet Monastery is a deterrent for a lot of Alliance players who want to do the Scarlet Monastery dungeons, and if there isn't a Warlock in the group to summon it can be very inconvenient.
It would be helpful if a walkable path was created between Alah'Thalas and Tirisfal Glades so that it is easier for Alliance Players to reach the SM.  Essentially just being able to travel across the shoreline north of Western Plaguelands that dumps you into north eastern Tirisfal would do the trick.

You can literally do what you described. There is swimming, but it’s a safe and clear route between Alah’Thalas and Tirisfal Glades with no mobs. I usually take this route since it’s safer and often faster depending on where I am. It’s absolutely the fastest way as a mage; teleport to the Stormwind mage tower, go to Alah’Thalas, and swim.
