# Item rarity collection counter

## Post 123627 by Scr — 2025-03-24T19:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123627#p123627 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, my idea is that a player gets stats for how many unique equipment items of a certain quality they have collected.

It would be a neat avenue of extra collection right beside grabbing item tmogs, it could be separated into different counters by quality and/or by item type. All of this should theoretically have groundwork laid out for it via the tmog pane, since items in the tmog pane show their rarity color (I am assuming that they have some identifier to them). This stat panel could be implemented into transmogrify in some shape or form.

I personally want the stats because I am playing as a permanent vagrant (playing with the vagrant rules past level 60) and want a bunch of counters that say 0 as I continue raiding past BWL and MC/Onyxia.

Thanks for reading!
