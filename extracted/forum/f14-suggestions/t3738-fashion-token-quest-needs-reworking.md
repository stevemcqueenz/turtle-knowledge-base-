# Fashion Token Quest needs reworking

## Post 22501 by Vyntoras — 2022-08-16T04:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22501#p22501 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, fellow Turtles!

Like many others, I've joined a few days/a week ago to join this hype project, though I've run into quite the problem as a person who likes to level faster than most casual people. The Fashion Token quest.

The areas 10 to 19 and 20 to 29 are completely fine, though the latter level brackets become unbearably expensive for just a single token. Needing relatively valuable/expensive items for just a single token feels like being punished for leveling up. I, as a Newbie, did not know that the cost for the Tokens spikes up rather harshly after Level 29 and now I've missed out on the opportunity of gathering Tokens to make my character look cool for RP or just my own comfort.

You may say 'Just get to 60 and farm Endgame Dungeons' but the flaw in that is that you will have to spend multiple hours for each dungeon run just to compete with 4 others to get a single token. People should earn their Tokens, sure, but the Quest shouldn't require such expensive materials and should be more accessible for Newcomers too, since the people in the 10 to 39 brackets are more worried about getting their mount at 40 or just don't even know about the Quest in the first place before it is too late.   unhappy_turtle_head

Hope this is something that gets looked at in the future, as it is quite the bummer to be gated by huge gold costs for just a single Turn-in since you need a Token for each slot you want to transmog.
