# Another idea for content PvP-E players

## Post 136368 by Cosmico (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-20T08:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136368#p136368 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/b5J0kTTK/3.png)](https://ibb.co/23N9Txxk)

With the scene of my favorite zone of all in the game so far, and no intentions to lose any time into the matter allow me to present...

"Garrisons. The vanilla way".

Features:

> Fully destructible to demolish camps, forts, strongholds and citadels.
> Specific coins and resources for this content.
> Very valuable and rewarding amount of value for this resources.
> Places that requires the player guilds to "grind" resources in the area, also other areas.
> Buildings takes long in order to be built opening the chance for more ores and objects to grind stuff.
> Multiplayer gathering "MG" reduces the amount of resources needed depending on how many players contributes.
> Possibility to create new secondary professions (Construction, Demolition, Transport of goods, Scavenge of ruins...)
> Give new life to wide zones "abandoned" in the map which becomes unnecessary after late game.
> Size of buildings set a cap according to how big the map is going from camps to citadels.
> Chance to introduce a new portal system also Garrison heartstone.
> Makes possible to propose and create new mounts, armor tiers, and transfiguration gear beyond story.

> Content that requires having fulfilled all raid PvE and hard PvP content of the game to get in!

> Features to make possible new objectives and gameplay to involve your specific race and class capabilities.

Don't say it can't be... just say that can we make it possible.
