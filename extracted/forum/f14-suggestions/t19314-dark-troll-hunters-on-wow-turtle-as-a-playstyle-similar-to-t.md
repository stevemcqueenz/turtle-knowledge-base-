# Dark Troll Hunters on Wow Turtle, as a playstyle similar to the Priestesses of Elune

## Post 131216 by Natremar — 2025-05-15T13:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131216#p131216 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

*I'm sorry, I don't know English well, so I translated my message to the developers through a translator. I hope for your understanding.*

Good afternoon, dear developers and players! With patch 1.17.2, the priests of the night elves were given the opportunity to wield bows, based on the story that each priestess of the moon of the night elves, in addition to her priestly abilities, is also an experienced hunter. I really liked this change, and it also inspired me; your team can really implement the bold solutions that wow turtle players would like to see.

I also have a suggestion for you. The priestesses of the moon are really good with bows, based on their history, but the priests of the black spear trolls also combine the skills of a hunter and a priest, and representatives of this style are called dark hunters, which are Vol'jin, the leader of the black spear trolls, as well as the hero of the Horde Rohan, and many other trolls who meet as the game progresses. These characters possess the magic of Loa spirits, thanks to which they are able to heal wounds and relieve ailments, but also cause harm with the help of dark magic. They reinforce their magical power with excellent bow and throwing skills, as well as the trolls' natural agility and agility. Considering that the racial ability of the troll priests "Hex of Weakness" is rather boring implemented in classic wow, I would like to suggest that the development team slightly redesign this ability: first, add the ability for trolls to shoot arrows, throwing weapons, based on their history and culture, and racial abilities, then introduce a new ability "Shadow Shot" or something like that, which will deal dark damage with a ranged weapon shot and cast a "Hex of Weakness" curse on the target. This change will connect the gameplay of the troll priests with their history and culture, and open up new opportunities for playing the character.

In order to improve the experience of using bows by night elves and trolls by priests during the leveling process, I would suggest that the priests of these races be able to disable the dead zone of bows during the shield, since nothing threatens the priest while he is acting - this change will open up a new leveling style for the priests of the respective races, and will correspond to logic. Also, due to the fact that there will already be two races of priests using bows in the game, it would be advisable to add intelligence and spirit bows to the game as loot specifically for night elves and trolls.

The use of bows by priests of night elves and trolls should not develop into a full-fledged method of dealing damage, as other races will be impoverished in gameplay terms.

My message is sent to the developers in order to draw attention to the troll priests, whose history suggests that they, like the priestesses of Elune, combine priestly magic and ranged weapons, which is just as tightly woven into their culture and image. And personally, I don't see any obstacles to introducing these changes to Wow turtle.
Please write your opinion on this issue, and suggest your own options for innovations. I have tried to convey my thoughts to you, and I hope you will hear them.

Данные персонажи владеют магией духов Лоа, благодаря которым они способны исцелять раны и снимать недуги, но так же наносить вред при помощи темной магии. Свою магическую силу они подкрепляют превосходными навыками владения луком и метательным оружием, а так же природной ловкостью и прыткостью троллей.

Данное изменение свяжет геймплей троллей жрецов с их историей и культурой, откроет новые возможности для отыгрыша персонажа.
В целях улучшения опыта использования луков ночными эльфами и троллями жрецами в процессе прокачки, я бы предложил ввести жрецам данных рас возможность отключить мёртвую зону луков во время действия щита, так как пока он действует жрецу ничего не угрожает - это изменение откроет новый стиль прокачки для жрецов соответствующих рас, и будет соответствовать логике. Использование луков жрецами ночных эльфов и троллей не должно перерастать в полноценный способ нанесения урона, так как другие расы будут обеделенны в геймплейном плане.
