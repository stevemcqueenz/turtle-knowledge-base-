# Guild banner working in world

## Post 137503 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-30T07:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137503#p137503 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just a cool idea I thought about when pvping in BRM.

It would be cool to have the ability to plant a guild banner in world.
The mechanics+item already exist in the pvp banner (which sadly only works in BGs).
This version would only work in world (so not dungeons or BGs) and would only need to give a X yard boost of 10 stamina or something if the BG banner stats are too op. It would be more for RP reasons than giving an actual in-game benefit.

Guilds would be able to purchase and, importantly, design it themselves for X gold. Maybe limit the use to Officer rank and above.

Sorry for the poor writeup, but I think you get the idea. An easily implementable change that should be more for the visual effect than the stat benefits.
