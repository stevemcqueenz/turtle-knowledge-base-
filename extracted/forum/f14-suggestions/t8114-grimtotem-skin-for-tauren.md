Alright.  Well…  There's a problem with your suggestion, Bluecow89.

 **That option already exists as a default skin.**  It is, in fact, "Skin 0" for Tauren characters.

If you take a look at any Grimtotem Tauren, their defining trait is that they have dark, black fur and dark, black horns.  That's it; that's how they looked in Vanilla.

Now, I notice that when Blizzard did the big "model refresh", they added more NPC options to pretty much everyone – including [the Tauren](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Grimtotem_tribe#Appearance).  Looking at *Wowhead*'s Dressing Room, there are three distinct "Grimtotem" skins with paint on their faces and armos: gray body with red paint, gray and white, gray and blue.  This could work, but if Turtle Team did that, they'd probably have to update all 30-some Grimtotem NPCs / mobs to incorporate the war paint.  Which, honestly, would be kind of cool.

You know what?  I'm throwing my hat in on this.  **+1 to the idea** – let's see some Grimtotem love!
