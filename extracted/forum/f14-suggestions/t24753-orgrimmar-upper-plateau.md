It's lore-loyal to have that plateau exloited. Controversy may occur when the discussion is about what/who would occupy that sector in Orgrimmar. If devs pick a reckless choice, that might be noxious to lore preservation.

The Forsaken is neutral to Thrall's New Horde and their relation isn't like an alliance or partnership. In other words, the Forsaken is a dependent faction. Silvanas will never become the Warchief of the Orcish Horde in Turtle WoW. A safer option is making that plateau stay inline with the city's orcish style. Due to its altitude, I agree that a flight hub should exist on the height, but how the out-of-town ones in classic game is dealt with is still a question.

In addition, Orgrimmar should stay in the pan-barren land. It should not have a geographical link with Azshara and the Sea, which is defined in WCIII.
