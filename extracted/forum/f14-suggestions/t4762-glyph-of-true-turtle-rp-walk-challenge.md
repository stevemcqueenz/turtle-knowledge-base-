# Glyph of True Turtle (RP Walk Challenge)

## Post 28510 by Khalthul — 2022-11-05T10:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28510#p28510 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think it would be interesting to have a Glyph that keeps the player in a permanent rp walk status for as long as the Glyph is active. For lack of a better name, I call it the Glyph of True Turtle.

So why have this Glyph?
It could make for some interesting roleplay... whether its for an old man or orc, a disabled veteran of war on a limp, or for someone with an over-encumbered sickness. It could also make for an interesting challenge, for anyone patient enough to go for it.

How would it function?
Exceptions to the RP walking status of this Glyph could be when the player mounts up, swims, or as a ghost returning to its body after a wipe.

What's in it for me?
If there was any sort of benefit to pay off the cost for all this slowness... I think being given a second hearthstone or the ability to portal to the city would make leveling more bearable... (and maybe you get a discount on flightpaths).

Why not just toggle the walk function normally?
I figured it'd be interesting to have a glyph that further supports the "Slow and Steady" narrative of Turtle WoW, and that rp walking alone without the portal to the city function is ultimately what made Tinytraveler (An RP Walker who made it to 60 and beyond on Grobbulus) choose Mage for this challenge after playtesting Druid, Hunter, Warlock, and Mage. The ability to teleport back to the city, that "movement power," was the ONLY factor that kept this player from going with a different class. So, if this Glyph existed with that power... then every class would be viable for this challenge.

Additional thoughts:
Granted that having a second hearthstone or portal to the city could be abused by grabbing the Glyph and getting rid of it, I think both should be tied together to prevent a possible exploit. Other names for the Glyph could be... Glyph of the Ancients... Glyph of the Overencumbered... Glyph of Patience... Glyph of Exhaustion (although fitting it is taken (maybe make the current Glyph of Exhaustion into the Glyph of the Restless)). Speaking of Glyphs, Glyph of War should give an honor bonus rather than an experience bonus as well as giving players that kill you an honor bonus.

I look forward to your thoughts, comments, and concerns,
 -Khal'thul, a Shaman of Shadows
