# TWoW, please fix human priest racial spell Feedback

## Post 52433 by Xa1amandor — 2023-07-05T20:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52433#p52433 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello TWoW community,

I'm here to suggest that, if possible, the spell Feedback be fixed.

The original spell info reads as follows:

Feedback
# Mana
Instant                                                3 min cooldown
Requires Priest
Requires level #
The priest becomes surrounded with anti-magic energy.
Any successful spell cast against the priest will burn #
of the attacker's Mana, causing # Shadow damage for
each point of Mana burned.  Lasts 15 sec.

The issue is that this spell is technically classified as a Discipline school spell, therefor, despite causing Shadow damage, it cannot apply the Blackout talent, nor benefit from any of the other Shadow Magic talents found in the Shadow talent tree.

The original spell info for Blackout reads as follows:

Blackout
Talent
Requires Priest
Gives your Shadow damage spells a #% chance to stun
the target for 3 sec.
(Proc chance: 10%)

Does this not seem to be a clear example of oversight and mistaken spell categorization by Blizzard?

The original design and purpose for Feedback seems to have been an attempt to counter the trolls' racial ability of Shadowguard; spell info reads as follows:

Shadowguard
# Mana
Instant
Requires Priest
Requires level #
The caster is surrounded by shadows.  When a spell,
melee or ranged attack hits the caster, the attacker
will be struck for # Shadow damage.  Attackers
can only be damaged once every few seconds.
This damage causes no threat.  3 charges.  Lasts 10 min.
(3.5s cooldown)

Again I ask, does this not seem to be a case of oversight by Blizzard and therefor worthy of being fixed by TWoW if possible?

I propose that TWoW recategorize Feedback into its' intended Shadow Magic school, thus allowing it to reap the benefits of the myriad talents that bolster Shadow magic spells such as Blackout, Darkness, etc.

Please share your thoughts, support, criticisms, etc. below!

Thanks!
