# Firework devices and vendors in most crowded areas in cities

## Post 168702 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-18T05:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168702#p168702 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As it is arranged in classic WoW, Lunar Festival is mainly celebrated in sectors like The Park in Stormwind where druids gather. In some other places like The Trade District, only decorations like red lanterns can be seen. The Valley of Heroes has some launching devices, but it is less popularised than the Trade District. So, I recommend adding complete facilities for festival celebration in all trade zones or most crowded places in major towns in order to involve more players with firework display.
