Pain Mode

Pain Mode is a proposed challenge mode which can be activated by talking to the Mysterious Stranger NPC in your race's respective starting zones. Pain Mode can also be permanently deactivated by talking to the Mysterious NPC again. Pain Mode can be played on its own, or it can be played with other challenges such as Slow & Steady, War Mode, Exhaustion, or even Hardcore Mode. Pain Mode players will NOT be restricted from grouping with, trading with, or playing with regular characters (or regular Hardcores if playing in Hardcore Mode), and have full access to things like Mailboxes and the Auction House (if not playing on Hardcore mode). Pain Mode will not have a unique guild, and Pain Mode players will automatically find themselves in Newcomers or Still Alive, respectively.

In Pain Mode:
- All physical damage the player receives is increased by 150%. (This percentage can be changed by the developers for balancing if necessary).

- All magical damage the player receives is increased by 100%. (This percentage can be changed by the developers for balance if necessary).

- Most debuff timers are doubled, such as DoTs, curses, diseases. Some timers are unchanged, like ressurection sickness

- Weapon and Armor durability deteriorates faster than normal.

- Weapon and Armor durability loss upon death is increased to 25% (up from 10%).

- Weapon and Armor durability loss when resurrecting from a Spirit Healer is increased to 100%.

- Rare and Epic Weapons and Armor disappear upon reaching 0 durability. Poor, Common and Uncommon quality items do not disappear.

- The player will no longer passively regenerate health or mana outside of combat.

- Health and mana regeneration can only come from consumables, items, consuming food, consuming drinks, using player abilities, talents, buffs, mp5/hp5 effects, etc.

- Every 10 levels the player will be rewarded in a similar way to how Slow & Steady players are rewarded, though with different rewards.
Upon reaching level 60, the challenge is marked as complete and automatically deactivated. Players who complete the challenge will be awarded a Title, a Mount, a sum of 100 gold (subject to rebalancing as the developers see fit) as compensation for the amount of money spent on Food, Drinks and armor repairs while leveling.

I liked this mode, but i have some setbacks.

 1) Instead of making the gear breaking faster, i would suggest the repair being more expensive. Besides that, the idea of permanent breaking gear is unatractive, because some loot are so rare that the ones that loot it would never use it to save it.

 2) Mana regeneration, for priests for example, their whole mechanic is all about mana regeneration. That would need a tweek.

 3) I would suggest that in the pain mode you cant revive in the spirit healer, or even run back to your body, you can only come back if someone else revives you. (not applied to dungeons/raids/battlegrounds).

 4) This +150% on damage taken i see that could be compensated, like 50% more damage taken and less 50% damage done. We must think that the longer fights the healers would suffer more. And also making the game more tactical.

 5) You to only get XP from yellow and above mobs (and completing quests), and besides that, if the mob was killed/attacked by someone higher level than him no one gets XP. (that would stop boosters)
