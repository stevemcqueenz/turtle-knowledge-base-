Just a suggestion that may help the community when the realm goes live, I would suggest that you make a "world/global" chat channel that every player auto-joins.  There is currently a /world channel in beta with a couple of users but I think this is a player created channel as I had to manually join it and use the /chatwho (5) to see if others were using it.

I think having a global chat will certainly help build the community and help players to set up RP style events.  I suspect that at launch the community will not be massive, and players will quickly spread out regardless of the exp values and this could be a useful tool.

HTH

PS, the current beta system of allowing instant 60 has a small issue.  While you have auto-enabled all flight paths the slight issue is that you can use both factions flight paths not just your own factions.  I just flew into crossroads as an alliance, and while it has no impact I thought it was worth mentioning.

Quick update, to whom it may concern: we can't force the client to join custom channel without modifying it or creating an addon. It's only possible in WotLK 3.x.x+ clients via "chatcache hack". I won't go into technical details but it's a real pain in the shell in Vanilla! Client maintains its own list and will ignore all server-side changes.

Sorry!
