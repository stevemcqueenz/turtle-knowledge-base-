# Add better enchants for PvPers

## Post 109436 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-22T20:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109436#p109436 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After recent talk about "damage of all classes is too high" I looked on my 2 year old "dream" thread [Nerf high level enchants on low level gear](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4499) from a different angle.
What if high survivability of twinks on lvl 19 is a norm?
I mean pvp players on lvl 1-29 collect survivability first until they reach some comfortable cap.
Then they can sacrifice some to get more power, but getting worldbuffs is usually enough. Boots are always enchanted with speed, because without speed any amount of health is not enough.
Gloves and weapons can't be enchanted with stamina, so they are enchanted with power.
Belt enchat for stamina is small (2pts) against other enchants (6 agi or 10 int)
Bracer enchants is usually stamina or +healing, which essentially survivability too.

People could enchant head and legs with anything except 100hp, but it is most straightforward option. 100hp is 100 hp,
Alternatives are 8 str/8 agi/12 SPD
 8 strength is 16ap=~1DPS =~ +2 damage to 2.0 weapon or ~+3 damage to 3.0 weapon.
 12 SPD is 12 damage to long casting spells and some very minor damage bonus to DoTs and short casting spells.

If 100hp enchant on lvl 19 is a norm, then same 100hp enchant on lvl 60 is absolutely joke.

Suggestion is: add 1000 hp enchant for legs and head of level 58 and above.
alternative - add -15% damage from players for legs and head of level 58 and above.
