# More Guild Tabard design options

## Post 171046 by Aegle47 — 2026-03-17T08:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171046#p171046 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi everyone,

I wanted to suggest an update to the Guild Tabard customization system to give players more creative freedom and better representation of their guild identity.

Currently, the available tabard designs feel quite dated, and the selection of icons and shapes is fairly limited. While they’ve served well over the years, expanding the options could really enhance guild expression and immersion.

Suggested Improvements:

 1. Expanded Icon Library
Add a wider variety of icons, including:

 -More abstract shapes and patterns

 -Item-based icons (weapons, armor pieces, tools)

 -Fun or thematic icons (e.g., fruit-themed designs like bananas, apples, etc.)

 2. Profession-Themed Icons
Introduce icons that reflect different crafting specializations and professions, such as:

 -Dragon scales (e.g., Dragonscale Leatherworking)

 -Chestplate icon (Armorsmithing)

 -Alchemy symbols, enchanting runes, engineering gadgets, etc.

This would allow guilds to visually represent their focus or identity in a meaningful way.

 3. Classic Warcraft-Inspired Designs
Consider incorporating iconography inspired by Warcraft I and Warcraft II. These would:

 -Stay lore-appropriate for the pre-TBC setting of Vanilla WoW

 -Add nostalgic value for long-time fans

 -Expand the visual diversity while keeping within the game’s aesthetic

Why This Matters:
Guild tabards are one of the most visible and iconic ways players represent their guild. Expanding customization options would:

 -Encourage creativity and uniqueness

 -Help guilds better express their identity and theme

 -Modernize a system that hasn’t seen much evolution

Thanks for taking the time to read this, and I’d love to hear what others in the community think!
