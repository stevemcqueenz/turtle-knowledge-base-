# New Glyph Suggestion - Glyph of Walking

## Post 59070 by Morphomisc — 2023-08-25T22:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59070#p59070 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Introduction
First off, I'd like to commend the team for the innovative ideas brought about by the glyphs system. These self-imposed challenges not only test our gameplay limits but also transform our experience in Azeroth, breathing fresh life into familiar terrains. The likes of *Glyph of Exhaustion* and *Glyph of the Turtle* cater to those among us longing for a deeper, more challenging, and immersive WoW experience.

Introducing an idea I believe could beautifully complement these glyphs: the **Glyph of Walking**.

Mechanics

- On activation, this glyph restricts your character to a walking pace, whether on foot or mounted. The ability to run or gallop is disabled, emphasizing the deliberateness of each journey.
- Every in-game decision—from dodging patrols to selecting quests—becomes consequential. The world feels more expansive, distances gain significance, and every journey becomes an adventure in its own right.
- Combat abilities that inherently involve rapid movement or change in position, such as Charge, Sprint, etc., would need to be evaluated on a case-by-case basis. This ensures that the essence of the glyph is maintained without overly hampering combat efficacy.
Requirements & Rewards

- The glyph is available only at level one, ensuring players make a conscious commitment from the outset.
- The reward shouldn't drastically alter gameplay mechanics. Thus, hitting level 60 with the "Glyph of Walking" bestows the player a unique title, such as "The Patient Wanderer", "Endless Walker", or "The Tenacious Trekker". It's a nod to their incredible journey, without giving any competitive advantage.

Why this Glyph? And its impact on Roleplay

Beyond the allure of challenges and achievements in WoW, it's the journey that often leaves a lasting impression. "Glyph of Walking" compels players to delve deeper into Azeroth's intricacies, making every accomplishment all the more gratifying.

While not everyone might opt for this mode, those who do will undoubtedly enhance the roleplay atmosphere of the server. The deliberate pace could inspire more spontaneous interactions, story-building, and a sense of community.

Similarly, as the "Vagrant" glyph prompts us to reevaluate gear strategy, the "Glyph of Walking" reshapes our perception of space, distance, and choices in the game.

I hope this idea resonates and finds consideration.
