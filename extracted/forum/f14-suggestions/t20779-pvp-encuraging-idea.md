# PVP encuraging idea

## Post 144140 by Slashignore (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-10T09:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144140#p144140 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This event is not ment to be something crazy, giving tons of honor.
Its ment to be a fun event to attend - to encurage world pvp.

 **![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) World PvP Beacon Event – Fight for Glory & Triple Honor! ![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) - Level 50+ event**
Every 2 hours, a powerful Beacon of Power will spawn in a random contested zone in Azeroth.
Its location will be announced in World Chat, and you’ll be able to spot it from a great distance by its glowing light — red for Horde control, blue for Alliance control.

This is your chance to rally your faction, crush your enemies, and earn TRIPLE HONOR!

 **How it works:**
Finding the Beacon
Every 2 hours, the Beacon spawns at a fixed location within the chosen zone.
The spawn location changes zone every event, so camping one spot is a waste of time. (A zone can have more than 1 fixed position)
Locations wont be close to a faction city/hubs

 **Charging the Beacon**
Your faction must have at least 5 members within 60 yards of the Beacon to start charging it.
Charging always takes 15 minutes, no matter how many people you bring.
If your faction drops below 5 players in range, charging stops until you meet the requirement again.
It will never drop - only stop or reset.
If you die, you cannot contribute to charging again for 2 minutes after you resurrect.

 **The Disruption Aura**
A 90-yard aura surrounds the Beacon.
If you die within this aura while defending, you gain a -5% HP, Damage, and Healing debuff (stacking up to -95%).
This debuff lasts until the 15-minute defense is completed or the enemy takes control.
The debuff encourages smart positioning and makes drawn-out defenses riskier over time.
If you decide to leave the zone the debuff will last for 15 min or until the attacking faction takes over.

 **Changing Control**
To take control, the attacking faction must reduce the defenders to fewer than 5 players in range who are not debuffed.
Once control changes, the 15-minute timer resets and all debuffs are removed from both sides.
This allows for back-and-forth fights, but constant deaths will eventually make a team too weak to hold it.

 **The Rewards**
If your faction successfully charges the Beacon for the full 15 minutes, you get TRIPLE HONOR for all PvP kills made during the event.
Example: Earn 5,000 Honor during the event → get an extra 10,000 Honor added after victory.
