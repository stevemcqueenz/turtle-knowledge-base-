# Making the Whirlwind Mace and Sword viable one-handed alternatives to the Axe

## Post 156872 by Troggyboy — 2025-10-24T11:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156872#p156872 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As of right now, the Whirlwind Axe is the obvious choice anyway and the other two options (sword or mace) never get taken. And since the Whirlwind Axe is a good two-hand, it essentially makes it feel really bad for those warriors that wanna specialize in either a sword and shield or dual wielding.

The obvious choice here imo is to make the other two options one-handed, but perhaps with different stat spreads or extra effects to make them good for the aforementioned builds. What EXACTLY this could look like is not that important to me, as long as it's good enough to not just funnel Warriors into two-handed arms specs as much as the game does already.
