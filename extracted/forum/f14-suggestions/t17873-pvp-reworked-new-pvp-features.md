# PvP reworked/new PvP features

## Post 121826 by potatodreams — 2025-03-06T00:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121826#p121826 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This suggestion includes new features + changes in how does work pvp. The goal is make pvp more appealing for all players, even those who hate it.

Usual problems which make some players hate pvp:

 - can't fly because a toxic player do not stop killing the npc which allows you to fly.
 - can't accept/deliver a quest for the same reason
 - can't do quests because they are being camped/killed the over and over again by high lvl players.
 - can't fight/escape from high lvl players.
 - etc.

features and changes:

 1. imagine a lvl 30 player, if that player goes to a level 1-10 zone, all his/her chatacter stats, equipment stats and skill stats are reduced to the maximum possible for a lvl 10 player with the best lvl 10 equipment. That player moving to a level 40-50 zone (for example) do not have his/her stats increased.

 2. imagine the same player. If that player tries to kill a level 24 (or less) player, that low level player will be killed almost for sure. That low level player instead of be killed, he/she will survive with 1HP, can not be attacked by the same player again for 10 minutes and can not be attacked by any other player unless he/she recovers 100% of his/her life 100%. can be attacked by other players after have 100% of his/her HP again.

 3. NPCs which allow you to fly can not be killed, NPCs with missions can not be attacked but can not attack too.

 4. players killed now can be looted, but only 1 random item of their equipment can be looted. How does this work?:

 4.1. You can only loot players of your same level and 5 levels under you or above you. (after kill one, his/her body can be looted).

 4.2. Players with 1 to 5 level under your level have a 25 (or 20%) chance to be looted. Players with your same level 50% (or 35%) chance to be looted, and 1 to more levels above you 75% (or 65%) chance to be looted.

 4.2.1 Imagine you are a lvl 30 player and you find a lvl 60 player about to die and you kill him/her and you get one of his/her items (what a trash of player you are in that case, haha, and players who love to camp low lvl players are trash to! >:c ). That lvl 60 player now will be able to permanently see you in the minimap and only in the minimap (could be in the global map but I am not sure about this) whenever you and he/she are in the same zone but with a lag of 30 seconds to 2 minutes (maybe?). if that player finds, and kills you, he will obtain 70% of the value of the looted item in the AH.
Now, lets say you kill that player, you loot him and the item looted has a value of 1000 gold coins in the AH, and lets say you decide to sell the item for 1 gold coin. That player will permantly see you in the minimap (or global map) until he can loot from you (or from your bank, in this case the coins will be automatically moved to his/her bank) until he can get  70% of 1000 gold coins which is 700 gold coins. (so you  can get a final proffit of 300 gold coins less the taxes related to the AH).

 4.3 People in party can only loot players with the same level or above but with the next exceptions:
 - If you are in a party of 2 players, you can only loot single players with 5 levels under or above the member of your party with the highest level, Parties of 2 members and above.

 - If you are in a party of 3 players, you can only loot parties of 2, 3 or more players and only if those players have 5 levels under or above of the player of the highest level in your party.  you can imagine how does it work with parties of 4 and 5 members.

 - yes, you can attack and loot a single player with a party of 3 to 5 players but all members of the party will not be able to attack the same player for atleast 20 minutes after that and that player can not attack you during the same period of time. only 1 item can be looted from the killed player, not 2, or more if your party has 2 or more members.

 - in figths between raids, all members can be looted but only 1 item per player killed and rules above do not work. This is because we assume and accept we are doing a "massive" war and we are accepting the pros and cons of participating in a "war".

 4.5. The item looted has it's durability permanently reduced by 10% and can not be repaired. This mean the item can be sold a max number of times of 9. after the 9th time, the item is instantly destroyed. This means those objects can be traded in the AHouse, for example, with a price according to its max. durability. This also helps to not have so many copies of the same item in the game. This also helps to encourage players to do pvp instead of doing dungeons over and over again to have a desired item.  If you are questing and notice a player with an item you desire, an item that can also be found in a dungeon, why do the dungeon when you can kill him/her and have a chance to get that item? and last but not least, imagine how much would increase activities related to farming resources, creating items, duneoning/raidings, etc. Also, would be more easy get certain items to do certain content, bringing more acces to those contents for more players, without risking the economy of the server because those items eventually will be broken permanently.

 4.6. The player who deals the final attack to kill another player will see in the screen, above, a message which will say "do you want to loot player's name"? if the killer press "No", that player will receive honor x 2.

 5. To encourage even more players, you can earn exclusive borders for your character avatar (as if they were the silver/golden border of elite enemies) according to the number of players you loot or decide to loot. Maybe after 100, 200, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000 and 10000 players looted? and if you decide to not loot players, you can also earn titles/borders related to not loot lootable players killed by you, after 100, 200, 500, 1000, 5000 and 10000 players.  the last one, in both categories, will be lost if you decline to loot a player in the first case, and if you accept to loot a  lootable player killed by you until you decline to loot again a player.  if you loot 2 players, you must decline to loot other 2 players to recover the last title/border.

And this is for now. I have forgotten more ideas but I hope atleast 1 of these ideas could be tested in this server becasue as far as I know, there is no WoW server with this feature and I am 100% sure that these ideas could bring even more players (specially pvp lovers) from a lot of servers to try Turtle WoW!

It will discourage people from pvp very much.
Reason - obtaining gear in wow is very time-consuming. You can grind for some item for years.
There is one game with this feature - Albion Online. You lose your gear in pvp death. The easier you can lose items, the easier you can get them. If you are in established guild, you can just craft full set of gear pretty fast if your guild has enough materials and infrastructure. Or you can just buy top gear from AH.
Another game like this is Eve online. You lose your ship if you die, but you can craft yourself new one. Crafting is not that easy, but the higher the power, the higher the stakes.

Also, full drop of items will be source of raid gear trading. It will mean that new players will be engaged in farming gold more than in participating in dungeons or raids. Why bother with any tactics and raid schedule if you can just grind gold and buy everything?

Anyway. Dropping items will completely change how the game looks like. It might be interesting to play, but on some other private experimental server.
