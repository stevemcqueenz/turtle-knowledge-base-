# Diplomacy Opt-Out Toggle

## Post 16783 by Snakeman (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-02-06T16:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16783#p16783 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello! Apologies if this has been suggested before; didn't see one while I was skimming.

I and most (all?) of my guildies in the <Blacktooth Grin> are active PvPers on this server - we run BGs, we raze pinkie towns, we deal with those with the gall to attack our guardsmen in the Swamp of Sorrows - and, as ingrained as diplomacy is on this wonderful server, I think returning the ability to opt out of cross-faction grouping and communication would not necessarily be unwelcome.

I do absolutely think diplomacy should still be the default per the 1.16 update, but personally I would love it if, say, there could be a glyph implemented that you can toggle in main faction cities to disable diplomacy temporarily. Having world-PvP'd on live servers many times before, I'm beginning to dislike that I can actually understand what the pinks are yelling at me (and that they can understand what I'm yelling at them) - and having people whisper you as you gank them doesn't sit right with me either, regardless of whether they're slurring at me or my guildies because they got PvP'd while they were *flagged for PvP.* I'm not "mad cause bad" or anything, I just find it queer to be whispered by someone fighting on the Alliance side in the middle of a battleground?

My first thought was to bundle it with war mode, but grouping cross-faction for dungeons etc is far too useful to only have it be a one-time opt-out. So, I think a separate glyph or toggle would be the best way forward - could even be implemented similarly to the twinking glyph in the latest content update, where you can just right click your portrait to toggle as you please. I think I prefer a main-city-only toggle for this, though.

Something to consider, anyway. I welcome anybody else's input on this. Cheers, and much love to the devs! <3
