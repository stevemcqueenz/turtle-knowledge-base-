I've been playing here since octobre 2021 and i noticed a lot of people really enjoy the ideas behind the Disguise and Khargar's Unlocking abilties, yet find them underdevelopped in a few aspect.

Here you'll find my suggestion for what could be done to improve them. Any feedback on those ideas is more than welcomed and i hope you will enjoy them !

 **Rogue's Disguise Ability :**

The idea of a rogue being able to disguise himself and go through the enemy lines to carry out an assassination or steal some valueable item is obviously very thematic and fitting lorewise with the class. Yet, - for the moment - it's purely cosmetic and have no real impact gameplay-wise outside of some niche RP event.

My suggestions:

 - Create some hidden/secret quests for rogue that require a particular disguise to accept and/or carry out the quest.

 - Add some secret vendors with unique rogue only items (like a low level mace from a trogg vendor in loch modan ; or a pirate hat / scimitar from stranglethorn ; a parrot/raven pet or something)

 - Don't make disguise able to be used in combat (it's already like that in the live server.). Disguise should be about stealth. The quest NPC and vendors mentionned above should be reached without entering combat (use your distraction, disarm trap, stealth, sap and ofc disguise abilty to reach them). If you are discovered while using a disguise, the NPC in the area become more vigilant so you have a 5 minutes debuff where you can't use the vendors or talk to the quest NPC.

 - Add some content to Ravenholdt and Syndicate faction, add a Ravenholt guard disguise (Syndicate diguise is already ingame). It would be nice for rogues to pick one of those faction and have them carry out hostile action toward each other. Maybe some RP-PVE/RP-PVP objectives ?

 - Add more disguises.

_______

Mage's Khadghar Unlocking ability

i was a bit disappointed when i figured out mage equivalent of lockpick was capped at a lower level than the rogue's one. And it's perfectly understandable : you don't want to spoil rogue from one of their thematic ability and class-tied unique profession.

My suggestions :

 - Make khadgar's unlocking not work on normal lockboxes. Make it a proper profession, up to level 300.

 - For each type of uncommon quality lockboxes, create a "magically sealed" equivalent.

 - Those magically locked boxes would be obtainable by the same means as the normal one : dropped by npc or pickpocketed by rogues. The only particularity is that they would only drop or be pickpocketed from magic users NPCs (make sense).

 - Only mages could unlock them (provided they have reached the approriate khadgar's unlocking level).

 - Those boxes loot table would be fitting for magic users : Enchantings mats ; Staves, wands, sword and dagger with caster oriented stats ; tailloring patterns ; enchanting formulas ; caster armor pieces.
The droprate of BoE epics would remain unchanged and non caster BoE epics could still drop from them. Of course, the quality of this loottable would be appropriate for the boxes level.

__

I personnaly think those changes would make both ability more enjoyable, useful and class-flavored than before. Though i am not a developper and i have no idea if that's even possible within the limits imposed by the vanilla client or how much time it would take to implement those ideas or how difficult it would be.

I'm really looking forward for feedback, so again, feel free to post your thoughts.   smiling_turtle
