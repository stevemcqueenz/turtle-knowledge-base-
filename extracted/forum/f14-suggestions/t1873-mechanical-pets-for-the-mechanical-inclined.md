## Post 11223 by Jirry — 2021-07-12T12:19:00Z

Greetings, fellow Turtles and Turtelettes.
As Hunter of the gnomish sort I don't feel particularly inclined towards furry uncontrollable Beasts that can chew me up in one swoop, I'm sure others of my kind would tend to agree, even those goblins with their contraptions, or those that only begin to learn the Arts of Steam and Cogs.

It came to this Gnome's Attention that adding additional Pet Families would proof difficult to do.
My Suggestion, learn from Nature that Technology often imitates!
A Techbot can become as sturdy as every Bears thick Fur.
A Bomb-O-bot can throw bombs that hit as hard as a Tigers claw,
and a mechanical Spider, well can do Spider things.

If we have the technological modeling capabilities we could even return to the age old food delivery System:
