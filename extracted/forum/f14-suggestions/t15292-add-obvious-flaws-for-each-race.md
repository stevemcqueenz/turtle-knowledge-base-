# Add obvious flaws for each race

## Post 104256 by 987004590 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-10-22T03:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104256#p104256 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is just a crude idea, and in order to make roleplay more realistic, races should not only have superior talents, but also inferior talents. The change in direction of CC2 is basically to reduce sudden damage, which makes it likely that there will be a situation on the battlefield where neither side can do anything. Racial disadvantage may be an opportunity.

Since I am not familiar with the history and background of each race, I am unable to propose specific and effective changes. Do you have any ideas?

## Post 104259 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-22T03:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104259#p104259 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I like this as an idea but it wouldn't work in practice.
Balancing positive racials already makes things really difficult to the point that people don't really agree if balance properly exists.
And by the nature of things, people are more interested in buffs than nerfs when playing a char. Meaning that while racial nerfs can be fun (or very flavorful) they would cause much less hype.
So as a developer you have a certain amount of 'creative force' which you focus on this topic. You get more 'audience score' at the end if you focus on racial buffs, not nerfs.

But as a game I like the idea, here are my 50 cents.

Goblins are shady, noone really trusts them. Harder reputation gain.
Orcs, while having legendary spellcasters, have the zugzug stereotype for a reason. Reduced intellect.
Undeads have increased holy damage received from any enemy source.
Taurens are big and slow, they get a haste penalty.
Trolls are chaotic, they'd have a small chance that their attacks/heals etc target a wrong (valid) unit.

Gnomes are weak, they deal less physical damage.
Night elves are really dependent on their forests which get decimated by the enemy's flames. Added fire damage taken.
Dwarves are masters of itemcrafting (specially metallic items) but they are also maximalists in this area. Added repair cost (in the pursuit of perfection).
High-elves having slightly more expensive mana-requiring abilities because of their obsession with magic and adding more to it than needed for art/perfectionism.
Humans would receive increased shadow damage to represent Guldan's invasion and their ongoing bouts against the scourge.
