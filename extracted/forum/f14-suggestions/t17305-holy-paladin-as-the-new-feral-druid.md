# Holy paladin as the new feral druid?

## Post 118175 by Seltsamuel — 2025-01-28T13:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118175#p118175 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the recent class changes, the Holy paladin tries to house two play styles in its talent tree which feels just like feral druid with cat and bear form. Whats even weirder is its removal of the damage component of holy shock while still keeping things like Sanctity Aura in its talent pool. The aura is just there as a tax for Retribution paladins and maybe to solo monsters.

What I would propose is to change the t1 6set bonus to something that benefits both, the ranged healing and melee healing paladin. As it stands, the whole set has 0% hit chance to allow holy paladins actually using their melee tools to actually heal where it counts. Then add a talent to the holy paladin talent tree that gives him this ability like:

---
 **Righteous Healing** 15 points in Holy
Reduces the physical damage of the paladin by 25% and gives him a chance on every melee attack to heal 5 nearby allies for X-XX. This ability is exclusive to Righteous Fury.
---
with something like that, the melee healing can be achieved, it is not desirable for Protection or Retribution paladins and as it has no mention of yourself, it does not heal the paladin himself to not give a class with strong self sustain even more of it.

Also the holy damage or spell paladin could be brought back by removing the cooldown removal on Holy Shock and giving it back its damage component. Then add a talent like

---
 **Holy Resurgence** 25 points in Holy, 3 points in Holy Power
After getting a critical effect from your Flash of Light, Holy Light or directly healing an ally with Holy Shock, reduce the cooldown of Holy Shock by X
---
 **Blessed Strikes** could then be unlocked from Holy Shock and its deign could follow the talents in the Protection or Retribution tree, granting Crusader Strike a bonus per stacks just like the others. (Maybe something like x% of your holy damage heals one nearby ally per stack). Buffing Holy Strike by a ton with a single talent feels weird as well. So shifting some healing to **Healing Light** instead and further modifying it with this talent would allow the melee healing idea to come online earlier but would also benefit Protection or Retribution if they have the points to spend to increase the health sustain with Holy Strike

 **Ironclad** could also support the spell damage paladin, by adding a 20+ talent in Holy, changing the healing power into healing and spell power.

Discipline priest and holy paladin were probably meant to be in melee while healing when you consider the talent trees and spells they had. The start of the holy paladin tree has offerings to do something else than healing, while the other healing trees start as early as the starter talents.

Now comes the question about the class identity for said holy paladin. Shamans and druids excel at group healing with chain heal and HoTs. The new discipline priest buffs and also is the only healer with shields. Holy priest is jack of all trades and with the champion spell, also pretty good at single target healing. Then comes the holy paladin who is mostly single target healer as well in his ranged variant that should still be fully viable.

With that, the melee variant holy paladin could also gain the role of group healer. Holy Strike already hits multiple allies with its healing. By adding something else, like converting the t1 6set bonus to a talent/spell would allow that more easily. With that, he could also heal his allies in an aoe silence and would not rely on a shortened holy light every ten to eight seconds.

I'm replying to this as a feral main, mainly because the idea of a paladin melee healer has piqued my interest.
I think the comparision works but also kind of doesn't. Feral is unique because it's two distinct roles compressed into a single spec/build, meaning you don't need to change talents when switching between Tank and DPS and only somewhat have to change your gear.
Holy Paladin is somewhat different because you're essentially still performing the same role, just a different variant of it. A better example would be differing resto druid builds like Regrowth/NG compared to Tree (51 resto or moonglow/tree). Both builds focus on healing, but in different ways. This is great as it incentivises bringing multiple of the same class/role, but utilising different builds.
Looking at the current melee hpala build (without having played it much), one of the main problems seems to be that it doesn't provide any kind of valuable niche that other builds can't do better. For using debuff weapons like Nightfall, Annihilator or Darkspear it's still better to use a Ret, and for raid healing melees shamans still win by a mile.
I think melee hpala needs some kind of unique groupwide aura or debuff that they're the best at applying. Something that warrants maybe bringing 1-2 per raid (out of a larger hpala roster) for certain situations.
They could copy spirit link and add something like Martyrdom Aura: 5% of all damage taken is redirected to the Paladin instead.
