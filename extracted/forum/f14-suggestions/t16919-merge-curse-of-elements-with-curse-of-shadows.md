# Merge Curse of Elements with Curse of Shadows

## Post 115510 by Forbearance (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-05T16:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115510#p115510 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Due to the lack of warlocks (being the least played class alongside shaman) and also the introduction of scaled down raids, I propose that Curse of Elements should be merged with Curse of Shadows, as Blizzard did in TBC.

The expanding of specs like fire mage/warlock and arcane/frost mage are hindered by the lack of warlocks, leading to everyone in a raid having to stick to the spec that gets buffed by whatever curse happens to be available in a raid at the time. For example, I'm sure that many people would like to try out frost mage in raids, but they might not have Curse of Elements readily available to make it competitive against arcane.
