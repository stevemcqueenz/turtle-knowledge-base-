-Add guards to barracks.
 -Add waves of npcs that attack from those barracks to enemy capture points and allow them the ability to capture them if they are improperly guarded.
 -Make it so when those barracks are conquered by players the waves of npcs stop flooding from them (aka barracks destroyed no more npc back up for conquering control points)
 -Add a behavior to guards so when a player attempts to capture a capture point (graveyards/bunkers/towers) they immediately aggro onto whoever is trying to capture the flag at those points.
 -Make it so bunkers are not skippable (final boss can still be challenged but I doubt they'd be able to successfully kill them without claiming the various control points along the way)
 -Increase how much the mines generate reinforcements so they actually hold value in AV.

Note: NPCs may have to be beefier for both health and damage for this to work so players don't just rush past anyway due to npcs being too weak to actually be a threat to their progress.

This is to incentivize players clashing in no man's land because losing those npcs could cost them if the enemy is making strides across their control points due to npcs helping them as well as to incentivize defending barracks and bunkers alike so it isn't just BUM RUSH BOOM DONE AV OVER   wary_turtle_head

Most of these changes would only emphasize the problems AV has by adding more NPCs players have to fight through and making them unavoidable on top of that. Personally, the idea of giving the guards and archers in AV even more HP and damage makes me shudder. I would never play AV on this server again.

I agree that players ignoring enemy players in favor of rushing to kill each other's bosses is pretty lame, but please for the love of god Turtle devs do not listen to this man. DO NOT BUFF THE NPCS PLEASE.

People hate AV because it is either a PVE base race or a mutual turtle that neither side can end.

To fix AV, I'd make two changes:

Remove upgrading of NPCS, no more Seasoned/Veteran/Champion upgrades. This would greatly reduce 5 hour long turtle matches.

Greatly reduce the bonus honor gained from killing initial Captains/Lieutenants etc so players are not incentivized to rush through the PVE content and base race while ignoring each other. Move the bonus honor to capturing the towers/bunkers, with the towers/bunkers inside the main bases having the most bonus honor value. Players would be incentivized to defend at least until they have taken the towers/bunkers in the other team's base.

Honestly though, the only reason most people play AV is because it gives the most honor by far. AV itself is inherently unfun and fixing that would require reworking it entirely. The real solution is to nerf AV honor gain and buff AB/WSG honor gain.
