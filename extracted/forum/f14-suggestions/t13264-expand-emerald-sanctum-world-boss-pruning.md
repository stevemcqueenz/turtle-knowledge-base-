# Expand Emerald Sanctum/World Boss Pruning

## Post 88896 by Piacrt (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-02-28T04:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88896#p88896 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are many world bosses in turtle wow, and most will never see them because they aren't in a circle of guilds that camp them 24/7.

My suggestion is that Emerald Sanctum be reworked/expanded to add the 4 nightmare dragons to the instance similar to the Nightmare raid from the Legion expansion, perhaps there can be portals from the Dream to instanced versions of the portals where we fight the dragons.

If this change were to occur the 4 nightmare dragons would be removed from the world boss group, bringing the count closer to vanilla numbers. This makes Emerald Sanctum more worthwhile to get attuned, especially for guilds looking to progress into AQ40.
