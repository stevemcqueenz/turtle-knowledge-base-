# Lore-friendly Goblin change

## Post 25725 by Kindredsoul — 2022-09-22T07:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25725#p25725 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Goblins are into blowing stuff up so give a bonus to using guns. At the very least a Goblin playing the Hunter class should start with a gun like how Dwarf, Tauren & Gnome Hunters start with a Blunderbuss rather than the Shortbow a Gobbie starts with now. A Blunderbuss may not be appropriate but if so some other gun-powdered weapon could be. Quest givers offered a gun once in the goblin starting zone & immediately after which was awkward as I couldn't use them.

Next, Goblins should have something to reflect their love of profit and ruthless pursuit of it. Some barter or treasure ability similar to Dwarves could fit. It could be (borderline) useless but have cool flavor.
