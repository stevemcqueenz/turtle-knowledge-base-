# Elemental tweaks

## Post 29344 by Snakeman (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-16T15:06:00Z

I've seen a lot of "curse of the elements including nature would fix elemental" around, which I do agree with to a degree, but we're all forgetting about the fact we also don't have a nature damage pot or glove enchant like mages and warlocks do. Yes, [this](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=70000) does exist, but seemingly only in the database. Giving it a chance to drop from the same bosses that fire/frost/shadow power drop from as well as adding a recipe for an Elixir of Greater Nature Power to fit with the fire/frost/shadow damage elixirs I feel would be helpful in the attempt to bring elemental up to speed with fire mages and warlocks in raids.

(arcane power for arcane mage/boomkin would be cool also but i don't play those so it's not at the front of my mind rn lol)

Of course, there's still the glaring ele mana issue to address, but we'll worry about that another day
