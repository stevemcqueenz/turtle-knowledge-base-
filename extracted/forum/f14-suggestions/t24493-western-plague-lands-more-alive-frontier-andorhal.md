# Western plague lands, more alive frontier Andorhal

## Post 172318 by Giorgioslatori — 2026-03-25T10:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172318#p172318 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Please consider the following:
 - opposing army lines at the gates, like at entrance high elf starter zone.
 - Some quick made up wooden palisades, arrow/sword crates and other war like attributes that give the feel of an endless exhausting frontier warzone
 - field hospital at Chill Wind camp
 - on turtle wow website, at the south of sorrow hill cementary, old mass graves with burned bodies are described, from past scrouge invasion. In-game no such open pits can be found.
 - In lore and warcraft games Andorhal has been described as having walls around the city, and heavily damaged by conflict. Unfortunately, no walls present in-game.
 - some death kights at Andorhall patrolling the lands or at the frontier lines
 - Some Palladins at the frontier lines. This should be their battle ground.
 - improvised blacksmith, cooking area, etc.

Bullwark, same thing. Make it feel more alive
Hearthglen, same thing, a little frontier at the gates as well?
 - a small mountain pass from Hearthglenn to the Scarlet Monestraty (how else are they commuting inbetween, instead of walking through a deathzone?)

Although Arthas is retreated, the scrouge should still feel like an endless hungry plague, always taking more.

Anyways, I am just thinking out loud. Love all the work so far, thanks!
