# Proposal; Subscription donation system

## Post 63536 by Feetlover444 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-13T09:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=63536#p63536 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi playerbase and devs that might read this,

With the current player situation and everyone wanting to jump on the turtle wow hype I have a proposal!

I propose a recurring subscription model for players that want to support the devs monthly.

In return, anyone that has a recurring subscription active should entirely get to skip the queue or get priority I propose the monthly fee to be somewhere within 5-15$.

This way those that want to play after work get to do so without having to wait 4 hours in queue and going to bed after checking your mailbox, those that are unable or unwilling to subscribe are still free to play nothing changes for them.

It's a win-win situation as it'll reduce the player base tension and pressure the devs receive, and it'll allow a portion of the playerbase to get playing quicker than ever before! The extra funds generated by those that are subscribed can be used to expand server capacity/expand the team/open new servers or whatever the devs need immediate funds for!

And because its a recurring subscription model the devs can budget for it, because its much easier to calculate passive liquidity compared to arbitary donations!

Naturally the normal donation system wouldn't change and would co-exist with the new recurring subscription model.

One simply bypasses the queue
And the other functions as the in game store for cosmetics.

And that's the end of my proposal!   happy_turtle
