# X-Faction Blood Ring

> TL;DR:
>
>  There's been a lot of talk about Blood Ring and its development going forward, specially with the announcement of a 3v3 bracket. Cross-Faction is a popular idea that many players support as it could shorten queues, create more game data for class balance, and allow players to team up with friends from different factions. Most of the community seems to be in favor of Cross-Faction based on posts and messages observed across the board, however, it is not an accurate representation of the entire community's opinion. The goal of this post isn’t in any way meant to impose views on others, but rather to start a discussion for an eventual positive resolution.

Title says all.

There's been a lot of talk lately about Blood Ring, specially now that 3v3 bracket was announced, on how we can keep changing it for the better, and although the player base seems torn on what should be allowed/changed (or not) something we all seem to agree on is Cross-Faction. Cross-Faction would play a big role on Blood Ring's development not only by making queues shorter for everyone, which means more games are being played and therefore more data is being created we can later analyze and use for further class development/balance but also since Cross-Faction is one of Turtle WoW major selling points, and present in every other type of content you as a player engage in (be it running Dungeons, Raids or simply joining that friendly Guild you saw post in /world), you're bound to make yourself some "*inter-faction*" alliances this change would allow you to play with your newfound friends (regardless of what faction they are) therefore increasing the amount of players willing to try this type of content.

I've scoured over 2,000 posts/messages on these forums and on our community Discord regarding this topic, and although compelling arguments could be made supporting each side, I feel like the census goes **in favor of Cross-Faction**, about 60-65% are in favor of Cross-Faction and around 35-40% are against it **(keep in mind that is based only on the messages and posts I reviewed and should not be taken in any way as a true representation of what the community actually wants, so take that with a grain of salt)**.

So, what's the point, and therefore the **ultimate goal**, of this forum post/thread ? Well to bring awareness to the developer and the player base as a means to create and promote a healthy discussion within our community to keep supporting and improving what is essentially the biggest community-driven Vanilla Plus server.

In the end this is purely speculative and based solely on my own personal views and opinions, I'm not here to tell anybody they're wrong or in any way try to skew someone else's view in order to align with mine. This is simply a rant and nothing shorter of a conversation starter so we can eventually get to a resolve (hopefully) everyone's happy with.
