# Buff/Debuff limit makes feral dps change meaningless

## Post 107788 by Glowingfrom — 2024-11-13T04:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107788#p107788 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

From the new feral dps talent change, you can clearly see the devs is trying really hard to push powershifting out of feral dps rotation. With increased lasting time and damage of Tiger's fury/Blood Frenzy and losing TF when shifting out, claw + rake + rip combo can easily outperform  shred + powershift, even with Improved Shred. But, this happens only when :

 1) Rake and Rip can actually land on the target
 2) You have the 2 buff slots for TF and BF

However, in a 40man raid like 10+ Warriors dotting deep wounds, and numerous pally buffs, temporary healing buffs,  the above situation just does not exist. So, basically druid still need to shred/powershifting, but this time, without MCP.

To be honest, I really cannot understand the logic of pushing druid to use bleeding rotation without increasing/removing buff/debuff limits. So, Why change? Why not just delete feral dps from the game?
