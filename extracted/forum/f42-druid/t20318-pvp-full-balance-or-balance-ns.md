Don't listen to Maximage, there are two variations for moonkin pvp.
First one is the usual classic 30-0-21  [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?po ... DCIAAAAAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=pgCDYCFYBIAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAooADJDCIAAAAAA=)  I use this most of the time, you dot up your opponent, kite him, stop him with roots and occasionally throw a few wraths. You can move 4 points from Nature's Grasp to Omen.

Second version is  19-11-21  [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?po ... DCIAAAAAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=pACbQKAAAAAAAAFACYBAAAAAAAAAAooADJDCIAAAAAA=)   You get increased stun, armor and charge but lose moonkin form and other spell bonuses for a more "upclose" gameplay. I rarely use it, usually when i play in a team with guildies.
Thats pretty much it, moonkin's gameplay is basically a hit-n-run, you dot up enemies, throw a couple of wraths, kite and root, while keeping up your hots.

Never try to play moonkin as a pure caster, nobody with half a brain will let you just stand there and cast away your starfires, then again if you're playing vs alliance you might actually get away with it.
