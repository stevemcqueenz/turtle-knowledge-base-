## Post 42410 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-04-21T06:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42410#p42410 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

That person played too much on buggy private servers.

The only weapon enchants that work for druid in animal form in classic are stats (15/25 agi & 15 strengh)

Neither crusader nor striking nor fiery weapon will have an effect.

What will however work is proc items THAT ARE NOT THE WEAPON. Hand of justice, darkmoon card heroism and so on will proc even in animal form
