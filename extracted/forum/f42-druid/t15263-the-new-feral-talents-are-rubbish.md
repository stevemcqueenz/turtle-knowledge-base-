# The new Feral Talents are rubbish.

## Post 103902 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-19T21:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103902#p103902 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone, and thank you for the staff for your continued work on this patch.

I have seen the new talents and taken my time going over them in my head and finding a use for them.

But the thing is, I really wanted to fit these talents into my character. BUT.... I just can't see a use for them at all

 1:
Open wounds:
Increases damage of Claw by 30% if the target is afflicted by Rake, in addition your Rip ability increases your Claw damage by 5% per combo point for 5 seconds.

OK so, the issue with this is that no one uses claw, because even with all these buffs to it and the set up of 5cp rip and rake, this still does less damage than just spamming Shred... I mean I get what the Devs were going for here, but it just falls short massively. Someone on the discord druid channel has been working the maths out on this since release of the new talents, and that's the conclusion he came to.

This talent used to include shred, but was quickly taken off, because it 'nearly did as much damage as a warrior' - now I find this rather silly to be honest.... What exactly is wrong with doing NEARLY as much damage as a warrior? As a cat you have leather armor, alot more squishy NO AOE /cleave , and your heals suck in full feral gear as you have 0 healing and a low mana pool. So this I can't understand.

Anyway moving on to the next talent.

'Feral Adrenaline'
Suffering a physical critical strike while in Bear, Dire Bear or Cat Form, increases your chance to dodge by 45% until you dodge the next physical attack against you. Sounds nice right? Until you read the last part.... 30 second cool down.... I mean what.... 3 talent points for 45% dodge every 30 seconds IF I get Crit?

I'm not sure what the Devs were going for on this as if you dodge you only push hits off the table, not crushes or crits... So honestly I can't recommend anyone take this talent... I think it would need to be 100% chance or change the cool down to 10-15 seconds with 45%.

Last new talent:

Ancient Brutality.

Dodging an attack while in Bear or Dire Bear Form imbues you with the spirit of the ancients. Returning 15% damage dealt as healing and generating 3 rage per second for 5 seconds. Cannot occur more than once every 6 seconds.

Ok this is probably the best of the 3 new talents. But even then I can't see this being used in a raid.... The 15% vampirism won't help much vs a raid boss... Maybe it could have some niche uses in a 5 man with a good geared druid idk... The rage on dodging is the best part about this but as a druid I'm rarely rage starved anyway in bear form...

I honestly think I'd rather just take thick hide than this one

Conclusion:

I think most people are upset because all the other classes got some really nice cool abilities where as feral it feels like we didn't get anything useful .... Just an alternative play style for cat form which requires more effort than power shifting(imo) for less damage? It doesn't make sense to me really.

Not to mention the extra bloat now in the feral tree! There are now 9 more talent points to spend and you can't get them all! Even as a dedicated Bear tank only.

Alot of people like the fact druid in vanilla is a combination of both cat and bear, and you don't have to choose unlike wotlk.... But these new talents took a step in the wrong direction....

Thanks for reading.
