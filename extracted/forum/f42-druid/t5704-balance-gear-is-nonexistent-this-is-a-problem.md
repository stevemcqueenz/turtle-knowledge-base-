# Balance gear is nonexistent. This is a problem.

## Post 35702 by Datadragon — 2023-02-22T07:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35702#p35702 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have been leveling as a balance druid, mostly because it got buffed and as an experiment. It's been challenging but interesting. Omen of Clarity helps a -lot-, moonkin mana cost reductions are huge, etc.

But the only gear that supports the playstyle is 'arcane wrath', 'nature's wrath', and cloth pieces with blank spellpower.

I don't just mean 'the only easily accessible gear'. I mean the -only- gear. There is not a single piece in a dungeon that is leather and has +spellpower on it. There is not a single craftable leatherworking piece with +spellpower on it. Big dumb statstick pieces (+int/stamina), yes. +healing pieces, yes.

But as best as I can tell, if you want to wear leather and have your spells do more damage, you can either:
 - use X wrath pieces with no other stats off the auction house, neglecting either arcane or nature
 - use all cloth pieces, on a caster really designed to weave in melee attacks
 - wait until Molten Core and tier 1

This is pretty horrible. As best as I can tell, moonkin are on the weak side on this server even after the buffs. I can see the gear added in dungeons designed for ele shamans, feral druids (already the best leveling spec), and various clothie casters. Even smite priests have a world drop, and they can use the clothie options just fine.

Where tf is the gear for balance? Am I missing dozens of pieces because I'm new and don't know where to look, or was the spec just, entirely forgotten other than as a 'pick up scraps other casters use better' afterthought?

Ironfeather gear is the sort of gear I'm looking for. It rewards leveling the crafting profession and it supports the playstyle. I'm happy to learn about the buffs to that one. I wish it had more spellpower and less int, but it's at least trying.

I would like similar gear to exist in other places and at other levels.

I could write a dissertation about why int and especially spirit are essentially blank, but other people have done it better than me. Armor is useful to a point, stamina is useful, int is...about on par for agility for warriors? And spirit is an almost complete waste of stat budget.

Where warriors and feral druids have strength and hunters have agility, casters have spellpower. Vanilla WoW has -very- limited options for spellpower while leveling; it's a flaw in the original design, and Turtle has definitely added some pieces to fill the gaps. I'm happy to see that! But they did not add anything other than buffing Ironfeather for leather spellpower specifically (that I can find).

The staff and shoulders from SM are not bad, but they're quite expensive rare drops (as opposed to boss loot or quest drops), and they're not leather.

For comparison, a warrior trying to wear +strength gear has an overwhelming number of options, from most quests and bosses and rare drops and anywhere they care to look. A hunter looking for agility has multiple crafted mini sets (Nightscape, Wicked Leather, Dusky, etc), quest rewards, and dungeon drops. Casters have fewer, but by chaining together things like the Azure Silk set into dreamweave into frostweave into 60 dungeon loot, they can find options. Elemental shamans look like they struggle here also, and I'd like to see more gear for them also, but they have a few mail +spellpower pieces.

Balance druids have the ironfeather set, or x wrath, or cloth. Also, just to pile on more challenges, their Dire Maul trinkets and ZG enchants don't help them.

I'd like to see more there.
