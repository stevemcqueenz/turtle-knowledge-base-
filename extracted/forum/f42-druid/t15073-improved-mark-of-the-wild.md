Improved Mark of the Wild, for 5 talents is 100% NOT worth it...
We pick it as Balance/Resto PvE, because the other option is EVEN LESS useful.

Also the calculations Grizb37 made are wrong:

 **- Armor:** who is going to benefit from that + 99 armor? the casters? the melee dps?  NO.
Tanks are those who get hit constantly and so will benefit from an armor boost (even tho 99 extra armor when you already got 10k is almost nothing).
So the CORRECT value should be:
between +200 to +400, depending on how many tanks you have in your specific raid.

 **- Stats:** most classes ONLY benefit from 2 Primary Stats and 1 Secondary out of the 5 stats total.
That ofc varies from class to class, but generally DPS/Tank/Healer classes need 3 stats.
So the CORRECT value should be +12 (stat points) for each Raid member:
 4 (stat points) x 3 (stats affected) x 40 (raid members) = 480 (stats points)

 **- Resistances:** in the majority of raid fights there are no aoe spell damage mechanics and, UNLESS a fight requires everyone to equip Elemental res Gear OR the Raid Boss does multiple spell damage aoe effects during the fight, a +7 is useless.

So to summarize: for 5 talent points spent you get:
 - Armor:    POOP.
 - Stats: +12 per raid member up to 480 total.
 - Resistances:    POOP.
