# The state of druid in 1.18.1 (all 4 specs)

## Post 173102 by Noephix (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-30T00:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173102#p173102 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

**CAT**: Cat sucks because item devs made a mistake, refused to acknowledge and doubled down on it.
Cat is great dps spec at low gear levels - but in current endgame raid it's one of the worst.
Cat doesn't get a big full-slot item upgrade in Naxx, unlike literally every other DPS spec. But for some reason items devs thought that it did and designed cat gear in Kara40 to be only slightly better than it's previous BiS (which is basically AQ4 gear with ~3 Naxx items), resulting in Kara40 cat gear having tons of dump stats (roughly over 50% of stat budget) and cats falling far behind on gearing (since Naxx is the highest jump in item quality through the entire game). Then they attempted to fix it by buffing cat's talents, resulting in cat becoming OP DPS during AQ40 (cause that's where al the good cat gear comes from) while still falling off a cliff in Kara40.

Also unsuccessful attempts at fixing cat dps resulted in bleed rotation coming into existence, which deals more damage than oldschool powershifting but many bosses are immune to it, so cats end up spreading skill points between 2 specs and having not enough for any optional/QoL talents, resulting in those talents being dead. Cat needs to be lvl 62 to take all the mandatory DPS talents without a single flex point. Too bad lvl cap is 60.

 **BEAR**: bear sucks because threat was the only thing going for them and now it's gone.
Bear mitigation sucks. Bears take over 2x the damage while having only around ~20% more HP than warriors. Extra damage comes from the fact that bear can't parry or block and is a subject to crits and crushing blows (which isn't something that happens to a half-competent warrior or paladin tank). Bear snap threat is way worse than paladin's, while threat over time isn't really an issue for most tanks, be it warrior, pala or bear (not sure about shamans). Now there's literally no reason to bring a bear tank over warrior or paladin.

Also item devs strike once again, making bear t3 choke-full of useless and low-value stats like intellect or defense (bear cant parry or block, remember?). So the best current bear set in game by far is.... PvP t3. Because pvp sets are naturally made with proper stats. Yes, bears have to spend months upon months on BGs to get their pve BiS set.

 **MOONKIN**: Moonkin sucks because the math isn't mathing. Yeah, the rotation is kinda fun (even if some people object), items are OK - but the numbers are just too low. 1.18.1 moonkin rework was supposed to be a slight buff - but ended up being ~15% dps nerf to already low-tier spec, resulting in moonkin becoming the worst "proper" dps spec in the game. Below even cat. Also talent overload is a problem once again, too many mandatory talents.

 **RESTORATION**: resto sucks because there's only 1 slot for a resto druid in 40-man raid. Maybe 2, if they coordinate. But more than 2 - and they start griefing each other by overwriting each other's heals. Dev team's attempts to fix the problem didn't really help.
And resto also sucks because holy priest does resto's "speciality" (heal over time) much better.
