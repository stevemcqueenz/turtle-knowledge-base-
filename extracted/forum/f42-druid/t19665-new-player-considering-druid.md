Here's my feedback as an endgame raider:
Boomkin is good. Could probably use some DPS buffs, but is generally in a good spot.

Cat is a hot mess. Too many mobs are immune to bleeds for bleed playstyle to be consistent. Oldschool powershifting is just too weak compared to all the new toys other damage dealers have. Which means you have to switch between 2 different rotations during raid depending on bleed immunities.
Also cat gearing kinda stops in AQ40, while every other spec gets a full slot upgrade between Naxx and Kara40, which doesn't help either.

Devs promised some sort of cat buff in the next patch - but it's unclear what would that be.
