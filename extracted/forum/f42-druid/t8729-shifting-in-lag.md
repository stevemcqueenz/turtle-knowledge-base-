Shifting Feels Jank

If you don't have perfect latency, which is nearly never nowadays with this population shifting feels very janky, especially when you're powershifting, you'll be using your powershifting macro and you'll appear in caster form for a second and hit them with your normal wep and maybe miss an energy tick.

Would love the devs to make shifting to cat-cat possible without the caster form appearing, this would help feral DPS and people with high latency.... Or make the 31pt talent in feral also allow bear-cat cat-travel etc etc without having to go into humanoid form first.... Kinda how it works in Wotlk and Classic wow client.
