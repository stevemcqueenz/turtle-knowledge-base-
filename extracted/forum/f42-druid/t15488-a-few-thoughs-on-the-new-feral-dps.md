# A few thoughs on the new feral DPS

## Post 106016 by Romy — 2024-11-02T19:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106016#p106016 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So no more MCP. So TWOW has given us some new damage boosts.

Will the new boosts actually help with our DPS in raids? I'm not sure it will. Here are my thoughts.

Previously, feral did not use claw, rake, or rip during most raid encounters. The rotation most ferals use is shred to 5 combo points then ferocious bite. Neither shred nor ferocious bite were buffed at all, so the question is, will claw, rake and rip now be used in a dps rotation during raids. If the answer is yes, Rake/Claw/Rip rotation is better than Shred/Bite, that is great news. I don't think this will be the case though.

So what's the problem? Feral will be ridiculously overtuned in pvp if Rake/Claw/Rip are that powerful. This is similar to a time in vanilla, I believe it was the very beginning of Lich King that ferals were bleed gods for about 2 months before blizzard nerfed them to the ground again. You could literally pounce, mangle, rake, claw, rip and then walk away and watch any non-healer die from the bleed damage.

I am not sure so early in the patch if Rake/Claw/Rip will out damage Shred/Bite, but if it doesn't then feral just got the nerf stick, and if it does, I can't wait to bleed the heck out of everyone in PVP.

Anyone with after patch raid experience, please let me know if Rake/Claw/Rip is out dpsing Shred/Bite.
