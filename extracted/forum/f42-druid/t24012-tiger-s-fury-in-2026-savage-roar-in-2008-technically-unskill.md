# Tiger's Fury in 2026 ≈ Savage Roar in 2008 = Technically Unskillful Design

## Post 168796 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-18T18:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168796#p168796 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

To begin with, savage roar in WotLK is a clumsy skill, which significantly enhances the user's all phisical damage by 30% without a cooldown. The issue is that this skill, just like ferocious bite, consumes combo points while all other melee attacks require savage roar to be activated for efficiency. In other words, the design involves a weakened set of basic skills that need to be enhanced by using savage roar. To illustrate that, a feral druid's maximised ferocious bite and rip must land on the target before savage roar's effect elapses, which means that the druid needs to make five combo points, confirm rake's bleeding effect, land a rip or ferocious bite and make at least one combo point for the next savage roar. All these must be done in about 35 seconds. If you fail that, you are considered under-geared or incompetent. That was a time of inconvenience and frustration, no matter how high the DPS could be, because the loop of skills was relatively more complicated and difficult to sustain. I dislike this skill and it deepens my disfavour of the whole expansion.

In the later expansion Cataclysm, the effect of savage roar is optimised into increasing only automatic non-skill melee damage, but that still blindly complicates the mechanism as most damaging skills are influenced so players still need to cover the loop with savage roar for efficiency.

Back to TWoW's discussion, the name savage roar has never existed in TWoW, but Tiger's Fury (in early 2026) is the de facto savage roar skill, with a zero-cooldown and nearly universal enhancement for other melee skills of cat form.

How can designers modify or replace the current tiger's fury mechanism? A possible change is making this skill more like its WotLK version, adding an instant energy recovery with a long cooldown. Also, the attack boost from using the skill should be replaced with a passive attack increase merged into the basic panel data.

By comparison, warlock's Improve Soulfire talent is scientically much more acceptable. Why? Unlike savage roar and tiger's fury, soulfire itself deals great damage and it has a long cooldown. The universal damage boost only applies on fire damage. Equally importantly, the enhancement functions even if soulfire fails to land. As a destruction warlock, I feel casting soulfire a natural, amusing and persuasive choice. It returns a soul shard and never consumes precious combo points and energy - it can be casted without preparation and no wait is needed before casting following damage, like immolate and conflagrate.
