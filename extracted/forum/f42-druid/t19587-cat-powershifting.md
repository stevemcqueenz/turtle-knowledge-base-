# Cat Powershifting

## Post 133301 by Slade1994 — 2025-05-28T11:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133301#p133301 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey team, I am a cat player from SEA server.

Just wanted to bring up an issue that’s been affecting Feral (Cat) Druids on Turtle WoW — and it’s something that was actually confirmed and fixed by Blizzard during TBC Classic.

Right now, energy regen for Cat Form is bugged: if you shapeshift right before a 2-second energy tick (like at 1.9s), the regen timer resets and you lose that tick entirely. Over the course of a fight, that’s a huge DPS loss.

This isn’t how it’s supposed to work. By design, Cats should get 40 Energy on shift and then 20 Energy every 2 seconds, regardless of when the shift happens. Blizzard acknowledged this exact problem in TBC Classic and pushed out a fix — after that, shifting no longer reset the tick timer. You’d still get your energy right on time, even if you shifted just before the tick.

Players even found workarounds back then (like using Troll’s Blood Elixir to “trick” the timer), but obviously that’s not sustainable or intended.

Can we please get this fixed on Turtle WoW too? It’s a confirmed bug with a known solution. Feral DPS already struggles in some areas — we don’t need bugs making it worse.

Thanks!
