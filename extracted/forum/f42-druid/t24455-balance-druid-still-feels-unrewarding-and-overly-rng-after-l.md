# Balance Druid still feels unrewarding and overly RNG after latest changes

## Post 172020 by Auralys — 2026-03-23T21:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172020#p172020 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
I’m posting this as a K40-geared Balance Druid (with Scythe of Elune), and even after the recent changes to Starfire crit chances, the spec still feels really off to play.

The main issue is that the gameplay just isn’t rewarding when you play it correctly !

Right now, when an Eclipse procs, you’re often forced to stopcast the spell you just started because letting it go through is a DPS loss. Since Eclipse windows are so short, you basically have to cancel your cast and immediately switch to the buffed spell.

At the same time, you’re expected to maintain your DoTs on the target, but you have no control over Eclipse procs, which makes some 12-second windows feel almost wasted.

That means optimal gameplay = constantly interrupting yourself.
It feels clunky, unintuitive, and frustrating.

On top of that, the spec feels way too RNG-dependent. More crit just means more procs, but also more situations where you have to react instantly or lose value. You don’t really gain control over your damage, it just becomes more chaotic. Threat generation also becomes very inconsistent because of this.

At the end of the day, you’re putting in more effort than most casters for equal or lower results. And unlike others, we don’t even have a real burst cooldown to stabilize our damage or create a strong window.

Another big issue is how inconsistent procs are with Balance spells.

Moonfire and Insect Swarm ticks don’t proc trinkets (Scythe, Clackora, Sigil), and Hurricane doesn’t either. Meanwhile, other classes can proc everything very easily with instant or low-commitment abilities (Arcane Explosion or Holy Nova, for example).

This makes DoTs feel underwhelming and AoE even worse, especially considering Hurricane is already harder to use properly (channeled, positioning-dependent, and punishing if mobs move).

I recently got the Clackora trinket, and honestly it feels awful on Balance, even though it should be a strong item. Even trying to stack it with rank 1 Wrath feels extremely inconsistent and unreliable.

I don’t think the spec needs more crit. It needs more consistency.

A few changes that would already improve the feel a lot:

Let Eclipse apply to the current cast instead of forcing stopcasting
(for example: the next Wrath after an Eclipse proc benefits from Arcane Eclipse, and the next Starfire benefits from Nature Eclipse — this would feel much more fluid and rewarding)
Allow DoTs (Moonfire / Insect Swarm) and Hurricane to proc trinkets and effects
Add a meaningful cooldown to give the spec some control over its damage
(for example: a cooldown that extends the current Eclipse by ~12 seconds every 3 minutes)

 **Right now, the spec feels clunky, RNG-heavy, and less rewarding than simpler alternatives.**

Curious to hear if other Balance players feel the same, especially with raid gear and logs to compare.

Thanks for reading.

As a fully BiS Ambershire Balance Druid main (aside from the newly released BWL and ES) I feel the exact same.

First of all lets discuss the elephant in the room in the form of the recent change to balance of all things. It feels awful and performs just about as awful.

For the mana reduction part of wrath I don't have any actual complaint. I can't speak from a nord perspective but from an Ambershire perspective and even with the longer fights in bwl mana isn't an issue if you have innervate, mana potions, tea/demonic rune/dark rune unless there was a chaotic fight where you were forced to both tranquility and combat ress someone and perhaps assist healing in more forms. With that being said we still have way more mana issues than any other caster.

Now the moonfire/starfire change feels absolutely dreadful and in my opinion this is why:

 1- Previously you could stack 0.5 sec reductions before a boss fight. It wasn't broken, it didn't mean we were gonna outdps any of the other casters it just gave us a bit more control over what is a very RNG dependant spec that even when all the stars align is outdpsed by a mile by every other caster while bringing very little to the whole group.

 2- Lets look at a standalone example. A lot of the times it takes you 3 maybe 4 or maybe more wraths to get a lunar eclipse. And once lunar eclipse starts realistically you are playing with a 8-9 moonfire on your target which you don't want to reapply because doing so is actually a dps loss and you'd rather spend the entirety of your lunar casting as many starfires as possible except for the last few seconds where you might want to indeed reapply  moonfire if there's not enough time left for your starfire to hit. With the previous iteration even if you had the exact same thing happen and even if you didn't start the boss fight with pre-stacks for your cast they are still stacking up as you fish for your eclipse.

The buff to moonfury was a welcome one because it's a consistent buff but at the end of the day balance druid is still fighting for spots against every other caster and giving 15% crit spread across 5 players is a dps loss for the raid compared to bringing another mage or a warlock which by the way bring their own benefits to the raid while doing a lot more damage. Mage has the same decurse and brings arcane intellect and waters and amazing aoe. Warlocks bring curse of elements and shadow, healthstones and soulstones.

I don't particularly understand the reluctance to buff the spec in a meaningful way when it's so clearly far behind everything else. If pvp could become an issue with those changes well now you have dampen so if you feel like balance druid is where it should be in pvp you buff it in pve and add a bit extra dampen to make up for it. It does seem like a poor excuse as that doesn't seem to be the case either.

But even if we took the dps aside the spec just feels unrewarding to play. In fact I'd argue crit is the opposite of what we need. We'd rather have more consistency and then if we get the ocasional big crit it feels much better.

And by the way changing balance of all things to 3 talent points... It's...nice? But the extra 2 points aren't going into anything particularly meaningful. The sheer amount of spellhit we require compared to other casters is equally atrocious. If you are afraid to leave ferals needing 0 melee hit maybe change natural weapons to give the same 3% melee hit but 10 % spell hit. And yes I am saying 10 and I do not think it's outrageous to ask for that.

Also gatekeeping some of the spec's potential behind specific tier bonuses is another bad policy. Right now on Ambershire it's Tier 2 time and guess what? The tier 2 bonuses are all inconsequential in pve.

In sum you could give us spell hit cap from talents, infinite mana and even reduce starfire cast by 0.5secs by default and not only would the spec still feel unrewarding to play but we'd probably still be behind other casters.
