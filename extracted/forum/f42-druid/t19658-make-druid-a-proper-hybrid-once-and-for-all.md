# Make Druid a proper hybrid once and for all

## Post 133774 by Forumdweller (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-31T13:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133774#p133774 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Druid was my first ever class and I mained it since I first got introduced to the game by a family member back in 2006.
Back then in my eyes the Druid was a versatile class that can perform any function and had access to tools that nobody else did. I wasn't a feral, balance, or healer druid. I was just a druid that could adapt on the go with the many baseline tools I had at my disposal. This is quite a farcry from what the Druid class currently is, and I know that it's both a massive change to what we have currently have and on the extreme end of the never going to happen on Turtle spectrum. But I haven't enjoyed this class since mop (after the novelty wore off) and if this inspires even one person to open their mind to the possibilities of this class then I will have succeeded. Here's what I propose.

The existing no cooldown stance system needs to be changed. Forms should not operate in the exact same fashion that warrior stances do.  Instead they'll be extremely powerful buffs that last no longer than 10 seconds and tied to long cooldowns. They wont turn you into animals anymore either, but they'll provide various visual cues depending on the inhabited stance similar to this guy in LoL (no I don't play it). <https://gyazo.com/dafecf65ced84c6c880f06d5ac7f6e22>

Want to use bear abilities? Activate bear stance, grow enormous oversized paws (as a human/nelf whatever) and slap people to death.

Follow the same logic with cat (being a fast, reflexive stance that can dodge attacks)

strengthen your connection to the stars temporarily and become attuned to astral magics for a short duration with long ranged attacks and aoe damage

This encourages the 'not a feral, balance nor a resto druid' mentality that was intended with the OG druid. You're just a HUMANOID druid that can temporarily take on the aspects of various beasts.

I tried leveling as moonkin but it's so damn boring, no amount of 'go oom' slower talents are going to make this enjoyable. What's enjoyable for me is being versatile, dotting everything up, kiting them around while I rejuvenate and cleanse myself of poisons and diseases and weaving auto attacks in between. Not the boring spam wrath and moonfire as a fat abomination chicken that balance has become.

Again, not going to happen but atleast it's out there now and hopefully someone who cares about this classes identity sees it and does something with it, however insignificant.

It's been over 20 years since druid was released and nothing innovative has happened with this class. I think it has went backwards. If blizzard had the tools we have today back then I think druid would be much closer to the vision I'm creating above. They wouldnt've just made them specialize in 1 form like we have now. Atleast go all the way and make it a fun class like they have in TWW where they cast super fast and summon trees and faerie dragons and melt everything around them. Right now its like you've just went 5% of the way (in the wrong direction) and given up.

Sucks because I know that you guys are really capable of achieving something amazing like you've already done with the custom pve content in the form of dungeons, zones, music.

I’m fully in favor of bringing back the hybrid identity of the druid (and other hybrid classes too). One of the great things about original WoW design was the flexibility to adapt and fill in where needed, instead of being locked into rigid roles.

The issue isn’t that hybrid gameplay doesn’t work — it’s that the current group-building and role system is completely closed off. When forming a party, you’re forced to choose one of the “holy trinity” roles: tank, healer, or DPS. There’s no real option for a hybrid role, or for dynamic party compositions.

But the reality is, most dungeons (outside of raids) could easily be completed with more creative setups, such as:

Two druids who both heal and contribute damage.

Two tanks who share pulls and rotate mitigation.

Groups with no pure healer, but several classes sharing utility healing and support.

These kinds of comps actually worked back in Vanilla, but over time they've been sidelined by modern expectations and rigid role tools — even on old-school servers like Turtle WoW.

Reviving the druid’s hybrid playstyle wouldn’t just be true to the class fantasy — it would actually enhance gameplay. It would promote creativity in group composition, reduce the “LF healer/tank” bottleneck, and make content more approachable for non-meta builds.
