## Post 155593 by Lynxkitten — 2025-10-14T21:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155593#p155593 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I main a resto druid, in a guild that is raiding on Ambershire.

In group content, a resto druid is ok, but we cannot compensate for an undergeared tank, where other healers sometimes can. This is because we have less up-front healing options, and our mana sustain can be tricky.

In raid content, resto druids are amazing - as long as we are the *only* resto druid. There is no discernable use to a second resto druid, compared to that raid slot being filled by virtually any other healer. But that one resto druid is a game changer, and no raid should be without.

## Post 155621 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-10-15T04:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155621#p155621 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

There's a few different restored healer routes. Moonglow HT is still a thing and Aessina's Bloom really makes the throughput feel great, especially if a tree is already HoTing your target. I regularly get high or on top of the meters in raids up to naxx because of it.

Tree is good but limited due to not having a solid repeatable heal for tanks. They're solid for a raid but I'd hold off using them as dungeon healers unless you're stacked and your HoTs alone can keep the MT up. The issue of multiple trees was alleviated somewhat with early HoT renewals granting some instant healing but it's not sustainable manager wise.

I still recommend tree over moonglow early in raiding since there's not a lot of competition for non-set healing pieces with high spirit (1spirit is 0.2 healing to every member of your party, so in a way every point of spirit is 1sp, very strong even if your personal logs don't reflect it) but be open to switching if there's already a lot of trees or there is a strong need in your team for direct heals on the tanks.

There is a third spec but I'm still evaluating but looks promising, going deep restored without tree or moonglow. If it holds up I'll post here unless someone can give their own evaluation first.
