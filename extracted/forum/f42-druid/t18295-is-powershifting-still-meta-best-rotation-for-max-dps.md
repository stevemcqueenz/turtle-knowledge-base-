## Post 124903 by Crake (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-01T12:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124903#p124903 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The turtle wow team have done their best to offer feral druids alternatives to power shifting. I can't speak to itemization or meta at end game, as my druid is currently only level 56, and im doing the vagrant challenge, so i can't equip any gear, but in terms of playability, gaining 10 energy per bleed tick has been a huge quality of life upgrade, along with the increased duration of tiger's fury, and it granting an attack speed buff (which is lost upon power shifting btw)

However, powershifting druids do also have support, in the form of a damage buff to shred for 10s upon entering cat form, so they aren't trying to eliminate power shifting as a play style.

That said, having played both powershifting in the past, and this current iteration of changes that went live today, I must say I vastly prefer the bleed build.
