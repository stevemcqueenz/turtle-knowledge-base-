It's "pretty mediocre" even against [blues from the first boss of a 5-person instance](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83440) (and even if TUF were to get FAP this relationship would remain, since Boneshatter Maul is the same ilvl and rarity doesn't matter for FAP).
