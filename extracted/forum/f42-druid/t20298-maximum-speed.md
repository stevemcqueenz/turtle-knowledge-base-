Dash gives us +50%. I've read that Feral Swiftness doesn't stack with dash and this is sad.
But does it stacks with Swiftness potion so you get +100% speed?

## Post 141031 by Profgiggles — 2025-07-23T19:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141031#p141031 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Speed buffs never stack. If you have more than one speed buff active, the higher value buff is the active one. 100% speed is your default movement speed. Feral swiftness brings you up to 130%. Dash or swiftness potion takes you up to 150%. Using dash + swiftness potion will still have you moving at 150% speed, however you can use a swiftness potion after your dash runs out to prolong your speed buff.
