So to echo what was said here, Maul is the more threat per rage and damage per rage option for general play.  However, SB is always better on an Omen proc.  There is a break point where SB will do better damage per rage, but that is at very high gearing levels that probably aren't even realistic.

Maul will always due better threat per rage as it benefits from the bonus 75% threat on the ability and SB does not.  If they add the 75% bonus to SB it will get much closer, but still worse at most gearing levels for threat per rage.

> **Takinsur wrote: Tue May 02, 2023 1:53 pm**
> Also worth considering that maul takes your auto attack rage generation

 100% this.  However even with this Maul is more rage efficient compared to Savage Bite.  For example, even taking the rage loss into account on Maul, Maul does 2.5 times the threat per point of rage at my gear levels.  My gear is only level 60 dungeon blues and such with no raid or crafted gear as a point of reference.  The only time that Savage Bite is clearly better than maul is on Omen procs.

The more interesting measure is comparing Swipe to Savage Bite since they are more similar to each other as non-auto attack based skills.  At low gear levels, the threat per rage measure is actually close between the skills on a single target fight, however Savage Bite is FAR better at damage per rage.  Savage Bite is generally better as a filler even at low levels of gear but they separate quickly with increased attack power.
