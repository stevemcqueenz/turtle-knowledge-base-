## Post 147629 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-22T03:04:00Z

Not possible on 1.12 client. Even with a macro it's 1 action per press.

So the first press will make you leave cat form then you have to press the macro again to go back in.

The only way around that in 1.12 is the new Reshift spell but unfortunately that is unlocked at level 60.
