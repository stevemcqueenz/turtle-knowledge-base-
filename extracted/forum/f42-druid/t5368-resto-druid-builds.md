Its interesting build to say the least but i gotta say its probably not suited if you're just getting into raiding. That build requires alot of intellect ( to have bigger mana pool for more casts and also a little bit of crit chance) and spell crit ( the more the better) . In other words you're itemizing just like a paladin. Your playstyle would resolve of spamming regrowth and whenever it crits your next cast would be quicker. The spell regrowth is faster casting compared to healing touch ( even if talented ) without including nature's grace proc.
To sum up :
Pros: its excellent raid healing spec and playstyle since regrowth has very quick casting and nature's grace would proc approximately 2/3 times.

Cons: This spell - regrowth delivers half of its healing done as a result of casting it, and another half as HOT ( healing over time) with 15+ seconds duration. That being said, if you spam regrowth over and over - two things will happen. Firstly, you're wasting about 50% of the value regrowth provides since you'd want to cast those quick healz as soon as possible, and second - not only wasting mana/decreasing value of the spell, but in general regrowth is expenzive spell ( mana per healing done) regardless of talents its just like that.

Conclusion: Spamming regrowth with this build would enable you to heal multiple players at the same time providing a quick healz castings which is druid's weakness in my opinion ( since healing touch is 3.5 seconds cast) , but at the cost of increased mana consumption. Im pritty sure you could oom yourself in less than 12 seconds. If you obtain a few good pieces from zg/aq 40 it might be better build than what most druids do however :)
I would drop 3 points of imp. Regrowth and slot them into moonglow ( still exactly the same playstyle of spamming regrowth, just less crit chance but better mana cost of the spell until you get a couple purple pieces from raids that is

Old healing druid specs are still the way to go, even with talent changes.
Tree of life is very good when you're the only healer, it becomes very bad the more healers in the group due to its slow heals.
Moonglow spec is very effective if you struggle with mana, once you get used to it, regrowth spec offers better healing output. On both cases you spam healing touch when the group is topped off or there's low damage
