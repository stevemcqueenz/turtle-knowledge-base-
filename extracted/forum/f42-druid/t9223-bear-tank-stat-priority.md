## Post 60018 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-30T12:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60018#p60018 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't think there is a general stat priority. A lot depends on the role and context - are you tanking a 5-person dungeon or a 40-person raid, how many healers are there, is the incoming damage physical or magical in nature, how geared are your dps players, etc etc.

Stamina is always good as it provides general survivability for all incoming damage. Beyond that I tend to look at stats in 2 broad categories:
 1) Physical Mitigation - your armor, your dodge (whether directly or from agility), your defense.
 2) Threat - your hit, your crit (whether directly or from agility), your attack power (whether directly or from strength), your haste.
And depending on the task at hand you need to (re)balance those two. Broadly speaking in 5-person instances you lean heavier towards mitigation and if a dps pulls aggro you tell them to cool it. While in a raid where you have your own 5 healers you can slack on mitigation and go heavier into threat to beat the encounter quicker. This balancing act is the same for warriors btw, a lot of raid tanks are fury/prot and sometimes dual-wield while tanking (no shield) to stay ahead of dps on threat.

Just a few pointers on your stat list:
 *) Armor is an iconic bear stat but once you get half-way decent gear you'll be drowning in it. Hitting the armor cap is quite easy to do on Turtle, even in pre-raid gear. Do pick up all the good armor accessories (rings, trinkets, etc), but don't be afraid to swap them out for something else if physical mitigation is not a huge priority for the fight.
 *) Defense seems much too high on your list. It does very little for Druids (we don't block and we don't parry) making it an incredibly expensive way to boost dodge and add a little crit mitigation. I would not go out of my way to seek gear with +defense on it as a bear tank.
 *) Haste is completely missing and actually one of the best threat-generation stats. Our main threat skill is Maul which is on the auto-attack timer (which is at a crawlingly slow 2.5s). Item budget wise 2% melee haste is equivalent to 1% melee crit and you can get a lot more threat out of 2% melee haste than 1% melee crit if we're talking about single target tanking (ie raid bosses).
 *) Crit seems too low on your list, it's certainly not the worst offensive stat. Melee crits double the damage and all our main threat skills are implemented as multipliers to damage dealt (where warriors just get a flat threat value added to their damage if they use a skill). So a crit actually means double threat on that attack for us. And the synergy with Primal Fury is always nice should you happen to have rage generation issues (great for multi-targets where you Swipe on cooldown and you get 3 chances for some rage refunds).
