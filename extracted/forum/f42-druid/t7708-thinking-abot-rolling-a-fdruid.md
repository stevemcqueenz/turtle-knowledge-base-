## Post 49399 by Portholes — 2023-06-09T01:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49399#p49399 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Druid is an acquired taste (especially in 1.12.1).  It's kinda clunky and off-putting at first, but you can fix a lot of the problems with addons such as Roid/Super Macros.  During leveling you'll do great damage and have a great time learning to use your forms for certain situations, but by end game your damage isn't too impressive.  As a Feral you're a jack of all trades (you ain't too good at any one particular thing), but when the time comes to pop out of form to off heal, or shift into bear to off tank in a dungeon, thats where you shine.  With current changes made to Druids on Twow they are better, but they still need some work.  Pvp is kinda ok I guess as a Druid, I tend to stay away cause of all the dam Paladins.

If you're not 100% certain on Druid, I'd highly advise against it.  It'll be really depressing watching your brother have a hell of a time on the Rogue, getting weapon upgrades left and right while you're left with a stat stick.  But if your into the "struggle" and "heartache" go for it.
