# Feral tank stat priority

## Post 115628 by Greenaniline — 2025-01-06T15:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115628#p115628 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, does anyone know which is the "agreed" general stat priority for bears?

I see a lot of players, mostly fresh 60s going by what i assume is the Icyveins stat priority that being Armor>Stamina>Defense>Agility/Dodge>Hit.

The other bears i see, generally with bis gear, go full agi, str and stamina. Completley disregarding defense. I understand that it may depend on the boss fight, but generally, is it better to aim for offensive stats rather than defensive?

## Post 115691 by Drubarrymooer (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-07T04:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115691#p115691 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You actually want a fair amount of dodge because of ancient brutality.  Outside of naxx, you generally don't worry about defense.  Even then, you're probably not going to hit cap unless you go out of your way to do so.  I would say go armor till cap/stam/hit/agi+str.  Now that the tier gear has multiple sets, it's a lot easier to gear.

I'm not the best druid out there though.  Some might have better advice or different thoughts on stat prio.

## Post 115706 by Reploidrocsa (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-01-07T08:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115706#p115706 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Depends on the fight, most of the bosses can be done with a cat set and tank jewerly, so

hit until cap, agi and stam

become the priority. Then there's bosses in raids who hit you hard or you're not sure if they hit hard. On those cases you go

hit until cap, defense, stamina, armor.

Some bosses in bwl can hit you very hard if you aren't careful, so does twins in aq40 and bosses with limited healing like Erennius, maexxna or loatheb

go for Agility if you have to
Attackspeed is really, really good for Bears because more Attackspeed the faster you hit with Maul

Hitcap and good amount of Crit for Threat

Armor
Hit
Attackspeed/Crit
Agility and Strength
Defense/Dodge
