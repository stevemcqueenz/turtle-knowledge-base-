# Allow Genesis to effect bleed dots as well

## Post 134898 by Justbe — 2025-06-09T15:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134898#p134898 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Druids are my favourite class, bar none.

When I saw genesis added to the game and that it increased damage over time abilities the wheels started turning on how I could level a new way that would be unique and would allow me to use more of my kit.

Then one of the devs on discord told me that genesis only worked with magical damage over time abilities, meaning bleeds were not affected, I was completely disheartened.

I would love if genesis would apply to bleed effects as well. A leveling rotation for this was extremely fun and satisfying applying dots to multiple enemies and watching them all melt while you kept yourself topped off.

I don't even believe this would change much in terms of the end game, as genesis is so far down the resto tree no feral dps would go that far just for a small increase in bleed damage.

Was this ever tested? What is the rational behind not allowing Genesis to apply to bleed effects.

Please make this happen as it would give druids an extremely fun, different way to level that was boomkin or 100% uptime cat form.
