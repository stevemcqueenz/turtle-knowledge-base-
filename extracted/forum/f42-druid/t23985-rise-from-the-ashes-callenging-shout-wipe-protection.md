This would be fun,

Rise From the Ashes (30-minute CD)
 6 second cast
Druid takes the form of a Pheonix. When shifting into Pheonix player lets out a screech causing all enemies in a 20-yard radius to attack them for 8 seconds. The Phoenix burns causing 300 fire damage to all enemies in a 10-yard radius every 2 seconds for 8 seconds. The Phoenix combusts into ashes at the end of 8 seconds but is able to self-resurrect. 100% movement speed while in Pheonix form.
