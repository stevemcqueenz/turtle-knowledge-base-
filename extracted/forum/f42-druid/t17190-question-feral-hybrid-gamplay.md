I have been away for awhile waiting for the devs to finish what they are doing to decide what I want to do (No point in being a Tauren if the Night Elves outclass them in every way as a druid).

But it appears they are trying to destroy the hybrid gameplay of Feral and trying to force us to specialize like they did in WotLK which destroyed much of the fun for me.

Are they REALLY trying to destroy one of the core draws of being a feral druid like that or are they still trying to address it later? Because I just took a look at the talent trees and it is bloated to the point you can't really play as a proper hybrid like you could originally.

Can understand getting rid of our reliance on the manual crowd pummeler, I could even agree with them getting rid of our reliance on powershifting if they wanted. But being able to go from Cat to Bear without having to respec was one of the most fun parts of it for me.

In the old TBC days I loved going into raids alternating between cat and bear as the encounter dictated, I loved going in as a DPS only to drop out in throw off heals when things got rough or go bear and hold the boss if the main tank went down to finish it. I actually had setup a pure DPS set, a pure Tank set, and a hybrid set where I could be decent as both. I might not have topped the charts when I wore the hybrid set but I definitely pulled my weight and saved countless wipes and when I went full cat, I could compete and even top the DPS charts (until endgame where our scaling hit a wall) and in pure tank was actually enjoyed by the healers (except in pugs where the DPS couldn't help but AoE out the gate even knowing the bears were crap for AoE threat).
