# Hybrid Revive for the Hybrid Class

## Post 165025 by Captain4k (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-13T10:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165025#p165025 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

This post is a part 2 solution to my [Give Druids a Revive Spell](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=20827) thread.

I recently came up with an idea to give Druids a classic+ revive spell that still respects vanilla design philosophy. My idea is to hybridize the [Rebirth] spell by giving it dual functionality. It would keep its reagent cost and would be usable both inside and outside of combat. If used while in combat, the spell is a 3 second cast (meaning it can be silenced or interrupted) and has a 30 min cooldown. If used outside of combat, it has a regular cast time of 10 seconds with no cooldown, but a heavy mana cost.

This is the spell's original vanilla tooltip:
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/28G9VCrn/Screenshot-2026-01-13-at-3-20-18-PM.png)

My updated spell description would look something like this:
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/FHN6FG8N/Chat-GPT-Image-Jan-13-2026-04-33-56-PM.png)
This change respects the overall theme of the druid and its hybrid class identity in the vanilla world. It also stays consistent with the other hybrid class, shamans, who have their own dual revive ability to rez other people as well as themselves. So this druid version would allow the revival of teammates both inside and outside of combat with adequate consequences for both.

Looking for community feedback. How do you guys feel about my hybrid solution? Why or why not?

Let me know your thoughts in the comments.
