I'm not specced for pure dps or tanking, but this here is what I'm working with, as a sort of reflect tank build that pairs well with <https://wowclassicdb.com/item/21488> This is my talent setup at the moment. <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid/1O3F6-UIE93EDV-5>. Now, your best bet with pure DPS itemization is probably going to be getting 9% hit as soon as possible. So three points in natural weapons, and then % hit gear. This is important for making sure your attacks don't miss (as far as I know) literally everything. You'll also want crit chance, so % crit and agi gear are important. Agi is good both for PvE and PvP as it not only increases your crit chance, but also dodge chance and even armor by 2 points for each point of agility. Strength is useful since it grants attack power, but it won't be as important as agility. Now, all of what I've mentioned is my PVE knowledge, and unfortunately I wouldn't be able to help much with PvP, as most of my advice would probably end up being bad.
