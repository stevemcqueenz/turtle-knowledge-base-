# Resto druid could be more fun

## Post 121596 by Synlapse — 2025-03-04T12:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121596#p121596 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Great server and great class changes for the most part! I have more fun playing feral and moonkin than in classic. The same cannot be said for resto druids, unfortunately. This is not a post about power, only about how it feels to play. While HoT healing is definitely the way to go for druids, the tree of life form brings it all down imho.

In order of importance:

 **Slow**
One reason I went with druid is because its mobility. however the tree is slow. It feels like having a constant hamstring on and its just not fun. Wasting mana to shift out just to not fall behind your dungeon group is not fun gameplay. In pvp it's a death sentence.

 **Visual**
Some people might like looking like a tree, but i appreciated that the healing druid was the one druid spec where you could see your gear and your char. It would be much better to make the tree visual an optional glyph for those who like it.

 **No offensive spells**
It might not matter in raids, but in dungeons its no fun to be cut off from all damage abilities. We can't even wand. All we can do is stand there or try to melee, which is of course a terrible idea. Especially strange since the talent Genesis, required for tree form, improves dots.

 **Limited spells**
Tree of life cuts off healing touch when there already are so few healing spells. I don't mind making hots the main/best way to heal, but how does this artificial limit make the class more fun? Also tranquility is now on such a long cooldown it feels like it does not exist, further limiting the healing arsenal.

My proposal - change tree of life talent to:

Lifegiving Grace
While this aura is active the healing power of nearby party members is increased by an amount equal to 20% of your spirit and the mana cost of heal over time spells is reduced by 20%, however the mana cost of healing touch is increased by 20%.
OPTIONAL Glyph: tree of life

From my understanding (at least when it came to raiding) druids were never meant to be the big healers. On Blizzard Servers with TBC and the ability Lifebloom, the Druid established itself as a sort of "Support-Healer" who was filling the gaps between the big heals from priests and paladins/shamans. I raided as tree-druid during TBC and I enjoyed my spot.
Unfortunately: Yes the Resto isn't really fun here on Turtle.. at least not yet. I'm still a strong advocate of adding Lifebloom here, which, at least in my opinion, is a Must Have for the Tree of Life, since its only 2 spells are rejuvenation and regrowth (and Swiftmending), since Healing Touch isn't an option for this form.
Though I'm more of a Vanilla player, TBC was imo the definitive version for resto druids.
(afair Lifebloom got heavily nerfed with WotLK, rendering the resto druid kinda useless again).

I'm not pro player and don't calculate everything through, i merely give my opinion on how the class "felt" from playing.

Regarding the visuals: Someone suggested here on the forum to add a glyph to shop similar to the "Glyph of Stars" for the Moonkin form, but instead a "Glyph of Emerald Dream" with a green shimmer for the Tree form. I'd like to see that. We would have a visible caster form (while technically still in tree form) and Turtle could earn a bit more for the server. Win-Win.
