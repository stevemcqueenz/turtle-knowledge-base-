# The problem with T3.5 gear set bonus RDruid

## Post 172927 by Froizerk — 2026-03-28T18:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172927#p172927 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

So i have been playing resto druid for quite a while, when raiding in the Upper Tier Raids i see a very obvious problem with the set bonus in whole.

 - ***Blooming Bud***
Blooming Bud why is it an issue as a set bonus? Blooming Bud is a set bonus that tries to help swiftmend do more  impact but fails. Previously when Blooming Bud had infinite linger it was a good set bonus to buff before raid to make the raid healthier. Entering the new patch blooming bud now has a 20s Linger which only realisticly effects the tanks to get the proc (240 healing once getting hit).

 - ***Blooming bud as a buff***
It being a buff makes no sense, in the current raiding phase buff slots are very limited In raids above naxx for clarity but its workaroundable.

 - ***The FIx for Blooming Bud***
The set bonus of T3.5 mostly focuses on lower Swiftmend CD for more blooming buds, considering they do little to no impact they don't work together. The solution to this would be reworking blooming bud to be something entirely else for it to work/ make blooming bud a buff thats worth having on your buff slots.

 - ***Some Blooming Bud Ideas***
Your swiftmend applies blooming bud which does an explosion vfx and heals people in a 10yard range (1200 healing power scaling needs tuning) healing split into 10yard explosion (that heals people by 1200 / people count)
 - ***Some T3.5 5set replacements***
Your rejuvenation ticks now have a 5% chance to grant you elunes favor
 1st variant Elunes favor: Increases the healing done by your next rejuvenation by 20%
 2nd variant Elunes favor: Increases the healing done by your next regrowth by 30%
(these numbers should be adjusted to the strength of druid at patch)
