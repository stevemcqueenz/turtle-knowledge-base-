For entry level, healing touch builds are viable and very mana efficient. Once you get some raiding gear, i reccomend going tree and replace healing touch spam with regrowht spam. Unlike dungeons, raids have more widespread damage and single target heals are less valuable (that's why paladins excel in everything else, no raid heals). With proper consumable management and balancing healing power with mana regen gear your mana can last the entire fight.

Max rank for when you need to top someone up fast. Rest is downranked regrowths.

Which one? Depends on your gear, but as a rule of thumb, spam regrowth with full mana and no buffs/consumables. If your mana last 45 seconds or more without any external help/no innervate/ no potions you're good for 90% of the fights with that rank. If your mana lasts more than 1 min 15 secs use a higher rank and repeat until you're between 45 secs and 1 min 15 secs
