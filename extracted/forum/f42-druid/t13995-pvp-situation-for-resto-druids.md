# Pvp situation for resto druids

## Post 95318 by Elleshar (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-05-20T22:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95318#p95318 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would say for the most part druids is overall in a decent spot when it comes to pvp, we win some matchups and we lose others. Rock, paper, scissor the way classic wow has always been. Turtle wow has improved druid somewhat when it comes to feral especially versus it's sad "classic" state which I appreciate, making it viable to play. I haven't tried lots of boomkin but it's alright too from the limited testing I have done.

What I think hasn't gotten any love however is resto druids, specifically alliance resto druids. Why you might ask?

Compared to horde druids we have on alliance side two classes that have instant dispels/purges, making all our HoT spells basically useless. Even if you fake cast rank 1 spells making them go oom is basically non existent, shamans for example here with no gcd totems and near free water shield makes it a moot point to try to outlast or win a mana war against them. When you take in all these factors all resto druids have to rely on is then nature swiftness and healing touch, one of the healing spells with the longest casting time in the game. Good luck not getting silenced when trying to cast it!
Of course you have swiftmend too but you will never get off against a decent priest/shaman. Even warlocks felhunter can easily eat HoTs too.

Now the solution and the way blizzard balanced this in tbc and later expansions was with the introduction of a new spell - Lifebloom. A smaller rejuv basically that heals for a bit if it gets dispelled or finish it's duration. It would solve a lot of problems but if twow doesn't want to implement that I would also suggest some talent that made HoTs have a higher chance to be resisted or not dispelled so easily. This was also introduced in later expansions for druids to deal with this problem.

The reason I bring this up now is because looking at the twow roadmap there might be some future pvp changes in the future, and if they ever bring up rated bgs or rated arenas it would be nice if resto druids were a viable spec to play.

I hope this gives some consideration or discussion for those interested.
