## Post 126107 by Tarsis — 2025-04-09T07:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126107#p126107 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Well cat is not "better" is faster/efficient: kill 2-3 normal mobs quick, self heal, than cat again and repeat, very efficient BUT you struggle vs Elite mobs due to your low mana pool.

Moonkin for me is the most enjoyable to level, because as long as your enemy is not immune to Entangling Roots, he is dead.

So my suggestions are:
- early game (until you past lvl 20 if you get some good gear) will be: hot yourself and fight in bear, if you go in dungeons you can easily dps/heal or even tank depending on your gear quality

- pick any talent that saves/restore your mana

- 2 + enemies: root any melee and wither them with dots while you kill the others

- dont strees about leather gear, pick anything with int/spirit

- don't worry about "Vengeance" or "Nature's Reach" for now (you will respec at 60)

- i suggest only 1 talent in "Owlkin Frenzy", you just want the mana regen, you dont want to cast in melee anyway

- after you took all the talents you want from the "Moonkin Form" row, i suggest to go resto to take the 15% mana regen while casting, you should have quite a lot of spirit from gear by then

- if you like alchemy, mana potions are good to always have at hand, but whatever profession you feel like is fine.
So be mindful with your mana, ROOT anything that moves and enjoy your ABSOLUTE dominance over any NPC.
