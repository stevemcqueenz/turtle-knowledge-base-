# Why Tier Sets for Feral dps provide so much intelligen

## Post 114200 by Jaffcover — 2024-12-25T09:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114200#p114200 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all, I think intelligen is not a necessary attribute for feral dps druids, and it will have almost no direct impact on dps during the raid process.
Secondly, the large proportion of intelligen leads to a lack of endurance in some equipment. This will lead to a decrease in the viability of the RAID process. As a melee, my max health is even lower than that of a rdps.
This makes me almost afraid to wear T2.5 in raid, as the lack of endurance could lead to my little kitty dying at any moment.
If intelligence can provide more power-shifting possibilities and can be distinguished from some TAQ pieces,
I think at least the proportion of intelligence in the set should be reduced, and the quota of this attribute should be increased to endurance, strength, or agility. So much intelligen is a waste

> Powershifting is amazing and I hope it stays forever.  There are a lot of people who enjoy it.

No, it isn't amazing.  It was a fun discovery that let feral dps be relevant next to the horde of warriors in vanilla.  Here's what actually happened:

 - Bear tank underrated, but multiple tanks are necessary in vanilla
 - Dual spec doesn't exist and multiple prot warriors aren't good when not needed for this or that fight
 - Bear is a actually a hybrid spec, and a hybrid cat does more damage when not tanking than a prot warrior
 - Cat dps still isn't great, but then we found MCP and wolfshead powershifting

Now our offtanks are bears that can do respectable damage when not tanking.  This was fun and cool, and it caused the clearly spec breaking exploit that is powershifting to be overlooked.  Not least of all, it was overlooked because cat dps wasn't exactly top tier even with powershifting and MCP.  Fast forward many years later to pservers, and now warriors are fury/prot, which ultimately just meant that bears and warriors were equally strong as off tanks without sacrificing much raid dps from boss to boss.

Powershifting doesn't have any depth.  It's exploiting an overlooked combination of wolfshead and furor to create energy from mana.  The problem is that it's a broken effect.  Pressing Shred is always a top priority.  Any complexity in powershifting is just spending your sub 60 energy value, if possible, before shifting again to press Shred.  And this interaction is broken.  It was broken enough to prop wolfshead helmet as bis all the way through TBC.  Let that sink in, a level 42 rare helmet remained bis over the strongest level 70 headgear in the game in TBC.

Holding onto powershifting ruins anything that could be done with feral dps.  There is no way to buff anything that feral dps can do in a way that doesn't just make powershifting stronger.  That's why it's currently breaking feral dps, and try to realize that "breaking" doesn't mean that feral dps is massively ahead of everything else.  A "broken" mechanic is one that is unhealthy for the class.  In the case of feral druid, spending every GCD to hit shred breaks the spec.
