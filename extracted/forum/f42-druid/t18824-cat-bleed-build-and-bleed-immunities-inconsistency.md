## Post 132703 by Rmoruser — 2025-05-23T17:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132703#p132703 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Bleed/Shred Cat is decent with the right gear and talents.

The Bleed talents that generate additional energy combined with Idol of Laceration (which gives your finishing moves a 20% chance per combo point to refund 15 Energy on your next Shred) allow a rotation of Rake + Shred + Rip to deliver a fairly good and consistent DPS. However, several problems make this hard to realize:

 1. Many mobs, as others have pointed out, are immune to bleed effects.

 2. In raids, bleeds become almost useless because, even if you manage to apply them, with so many debuffs on the target it’s simply impossible to track and keep your rotation going. Other players applying their own debuffs or warriors’ Wounds effects constantly overwrite and remove your bleeds. You can’t keep bleeds on the boss, so the bleed talents and their energy regeneration become ineffective.

 3. Ferals lose a significant amount of damage to glancing blows. Watching just 10 seconds of a raid fight, you’ll see many glancing hits. It would be great if ferals had a unique skill to reduce or remove glancing hits or if weapon skills could influence this.

 4.AoE damage is weak for ferals. You can always use Swipe in bear form, but if you’re not a tank, it’s pretty useless because you can’t generate enough rage to make it effective. Also, ferals don’t have the spell power needed to be effective with Hurricane on packs, and leaving cat form to do AoE just isn’t true feral gameplay.

Suggestions to improve feral gameplay:

 1)To make the bleed spec playable, bleed talents should allow bleeds to bypass bleed immunity, similar to the Hunter talent "Stinging Nettle." I believe this effect could be added exclusively to the feral cat DPS bleed talents.

 2) To solve the second issue and give bleeds a better chance to generate energy, I believe ferals need a talent that applies a Rake-like bleed effect automatically—similar to Warrior’s Wounds. Critical hits with Shred or Claw would apply this bleed to the target. This would also combine well with the earlier suggestion to let bleeds bypass immunity via bleed-specific talents. The bleed would restore energy as Rake currently does, allowing cat DPS to focus on building combo points and maintaining their Claw/Shred + Rip/Ferocious Bite rotation, while providing a more reliable source of energy regeneration during raids.

 3)In long fights (cats can’t jump higher than their own heads), it’s not uncommon to see a less-geared warrior out-DPS a feral druid in the last 20% of the encounter.

Almost every class has burst skill with cooldowns on a 2–3 minute timer, while Berserk is a 6-minute cooldown. For example, in Molten Core or Blackwing Lair, I can’t use Berserk on every boss, while other classes can use their burst on nearly every pull.

First: Please address the issue of glancing blows for ferals. On some bosses, cat DPS ends up being equal to bear DPS, which feels unrewarding for a spec focused on melee damage.

Second: Consider splitting Berserk into two separate abilities — one for bear form and one for cat form. A 6-minute cooldown is acceptable for a tanking defensive ability, but it's far too long for DPS. Reducing the cat version of Berserk to 3 minutes (like Warrior’s Death Wish) would make it more viable in raid environments. Alternatively, you could introduce a talent that reduces Berserk’s cooldown by 1–2 seconds per critical hit.

Also worth noting: Berserk only increases energy regeneration and doesn’t directly boost abilitys damage or white attacks. This means that even during Berserk, druids remain limited by energy regeneration, stats, and the global cooldown.

 4)It’s pretty sad that Raka’shishi, Spear of the Adrift Hunt’s effect doesn’t work for druids. Warriors and Hunters are already top AoE classes, so this weapon ends up situationally useful for Warriors and very strong for Hunters.
