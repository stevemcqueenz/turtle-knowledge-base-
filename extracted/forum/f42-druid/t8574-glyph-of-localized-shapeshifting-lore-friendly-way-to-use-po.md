# Glyph of Localized Shapeshifting: lore-friendly way to use potions in form

## Post 55250 by Beefchalupa3 — 2023-07-30T19:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55250#p55250 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think druid players all agree that not being able to use consumables in form is a very annoying and unfun mechanic, even if it was originally intentional. Because of that, I think it deserves some kind of change. When something is just clunky and unfun, and changing it won't massively disrupt game balance, it usually is a good idea to change it.

I think a high-level druid questline that eventually awards a lore-friendly way to use consumables in form would make sense. There are probably other things you could name it, but my idea is to have some kind of "Glyph of Localized Shapeshifting" that grants Druids the ability to shapeshift just their hands, so that they can use potions in-form. Alternatively, you could call this "Glyph of Opposable Thumbs" for a slightly more humorous option. Make it a decently hard questline or something to balance it out. It's not that crazy that a skilled and experienced druid could alter his form to have paws and limbs that could grasp things. Besides, we can swap out armor and weapons in form anyways, which doesn't "make sense."

I don't think this would break the game or anything, it just makes druid feel much better. It's frustrating not being able to pop out and use a healing potion while tanking, and even if we could, warriors would still be better. Admittedly, this ultimately would be a buff for PvP, but let's not pretend like PvP balance is a top priority anyways. Also, since PvP involves more constant shifting between forms anyways, there are plenty of opportunities to sip potions in PvP so this change wouldn't be as drastic there. Or just straight up make it not work in battlegrounds if we are concerned about that.

Thoughts?
