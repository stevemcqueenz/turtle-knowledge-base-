[Calculator itself is a Google Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IACK4CltzOMmULWtprWE9lV86zl2La3fUZufOw6we9g/edit?usp=sharing)
You need to do File->Make a Copy to be able to feed it with your inputs

Right now it works for one specific rotation, assuming you keep it ideally, and does not account for ramp up period or rotation breaking up due to bad luck or extra luck with crits. This is what you should get on average during long static fights like Patchwerk.
Six pieces of T3 considered to give static 1% dps increase, but in reality it is more complex and i don't have enough time/courage to improve it. How did i get 1% though - assuming we keep both dots on a target, chances that debuff was up during last 6 seconds (3 ticks from IS + 2 ticks from MF = 5) is 1 - (1-0.08)^5 = 0.34 On avarage
 34% chance to have 3% dmg boost is 1% dmg boost.

But main value of this calculator is to get some estimation on a stats value, which i think it is doing just fine.
