## Post 47171 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-23T21:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47171#p47171 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't know of a list, but isn't the research half the fun? Most juicy feral loot comes from the new instances, but there are a few pieces from quests or added drops here and there. Some pre-raid items from memory:
Thornroot Maul from Crescent Grove is a good early weapon for cat form <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83221>
Thornclad Warhammer is a new BoE zone drop from Maraudon and excellent for bear tanks <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80751>
Almanac of Savagery is a new off-hand making 1h weapons more viable for bears <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60431>
Hatereaver Cog has replaced Smoking Heart of the Mountain as a Best-in-Slot trinket for tanking <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60559>
Ring of Authority is a new tank ring <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=50189>
Tank necklaces Taskmaster's Tag <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60569> and Amulet of Warding <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60420>
Tank chest Damien's Sorrow <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60421>

Caverns of Time: Black Morass (new instance) has some good stuff but is currently closed, like Gavel of Aqir Superiority <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61044>

There is a very good (and very expensive) elemental leatherworking epic set called Convergence of the Elements. You can see the items on the database like <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=65024> but not the set-bonus which is where it gets really nutty.

Speaking of really nutty, there is some insane feral loot from world bosses, but good luck nabbing one of those against the established raiders. The items are literally 2x or 3x better than the next best option for that slot, for instance Venom Covered Cloak <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=51731> and Failsafe Activation Key <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83481>
