 **In short how this build works:**
It uses a somewhat standard powershift rotation while also utilizing Tiger's Fury to keep up Blood Frenzy. When Tiger's Fury is activated it will also give you an addition buff for 12% increased haste called Blood Frenzy (Talent). When shifting out of Cat form, Tiger's Fury will fall off but Blood Frenzy will stay up for its entire duration.

Now for the fun part, every time you strike the target (may it be with Shred, Claw, Auto-Attack, Hand of Justice Proc) you will also hit the target for:
+2 fire damage (Gloves)
+2 fire damage (Shoulders)
+2 fire damage (Boots)
+2 fire damage (Incendosaur 2 Set Bonus)
+3 nature damage (Offhand)
+3 nature damage (Cloak)
+2 fire damage (Neck)
+5% chance to deal and additional 15-25 fire damage (Incednosaur 3 Set Bonus).

Each melee hit does an additional 7 **separate** spell hits to the target, each of which has a 10% chance of proc'ing Blade of Eternal Darkness for +100 shadow damage and restoring 100 mana to the caster.

Note: These hits do not have a spell coefficient, so stacking spell power does nothing to these "additional hits" nor does it affect the Blade of Eternal Darkness's shadow dmg proc

Soloing Princess is actually easier on Twow compared to Classic/Vanilla servers.  As long as you kite Princess at max range she will stop and channel "Dust Field" every 4-5 seconds (as long as you stay out of range of her Boulder 20-30 yards).  You can stealth all the way up to princess without having to fight anything.  Once you're there you can just kite her in a circle around her room casting moonfire/insect swarm.  Every 4-5 seconds (count in your head as your running) she will stop and channel "Dust Field", you can throw out a starfire if you want to kill her faster.  Think it averages about 7 min per run so you'll hit lockout timer within a little over half an hour.

Note: if you get hit by the boulder and get knocked down just keep running.  She wont be channelling her "Dust Field" for a bit, but will decide to start using it again in about 10-15 seconds. Always stay max range (I suggest getting a Distance/Range finder addon).
