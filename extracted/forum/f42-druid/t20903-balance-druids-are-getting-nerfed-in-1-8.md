# Balance Druids are getting nerfed in 1.8

## Post 145279 by Halfgeek9 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-14T02:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145279#p145279 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Are they doing too much dps? Nope. Mediocre currently.

In 1.8, two Balance Druid mechanics are nerfed:

Balance in All Thing Starfire cast time reduction nerfed from 0.8s to 0.5s. A big nerf to Starfire cast time. Instead of 2.2s its 2.5s. Or 2s with the Starfire idol, to 2.3s.

Eclipse nerfed from flat 30% damage bonus to 10% + 60% of spell crit rate.

T1 geared Balance Druids have around 20% spell crit. T2 geared around 25% spell crit. T3 geared around 30% spell crit.

 10% + (60% of 20%) = 22%

 10% + (60% of 25%) = 25%

To reach 30% Eclipse dmg bonus, a Balance Druid must have 34% spell crit.

Balance Druids do not have +spell crit talents like other casters, they only get +10% to Moonfire. Moonkin is grp 3% spell crit buff.

But what about Wrath/Starfire Eclipse proc buff?

Wrath has 30% chance to proc. That's about the crit rate of T3 Moonkins Wrath.

Starfire has 50% chance to proc Nature Eclipse, okay, that's a buff. But Arcane Eclipse is where Druid dps is good, because of Curse of Shadows in raids. Wrath being nature doesn't benefit, and get higher partial resists.

So we don't really get into Arcane Eclipse more often. And when we do, its less than the current 30% dmg bonus, and Starfires cast slower.

WTF why nerf Balance Druids?
