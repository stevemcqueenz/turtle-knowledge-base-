Can confirm that feral is very good, and fun, for leveling up. You switch between bear and cat form depending on the situation, but the bear form regen was heavily nerfed recently. Druids on TWoW can also train Polearms, which will help with those feral stats.

On the other hand,  more than a few folks have mentioned on Discord that Moonkin spec is now viable for leveling. If I leveled up a Druid again, I would go that route. It has something to do with more mana efficiency, but you'd have to consult the experts for more info.

When you have time, poke around on the TWoW Druid Discord channel. We do bite.

I'm currently leveling a druid as boomkin and am at level 37.  Very fun, there are times where you feel the damage isn't quite there, but when you crit you destroy things.  Mid 30s the spec starts coming together very nice.  Up to that point I would open with starfall, wrath,  moonfire and insect swarm and then either melee or wrath some more.  Hard mobs use entailing roots and you can use rank 1, don't need to use max rank.  1 point into owlkin frenzy is good enough for the mana back.

You can also pop into cat and dps if mana gets low, just save enough to shift.
