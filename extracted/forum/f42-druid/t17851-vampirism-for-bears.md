## Post 121720 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-05T06:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121720#p121720 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a tank in raids you should never prioritize vampirism. You need to be as beefy as you can. Its others job to keep that healthbar full.

As a tank you typically will deal somewhere in the 500-800 dps range. While often taking  800 or more DPS yourself.
 50-80 healing per second is a drop in the bucket and you should only get it if you are tanky enough and have no threat problems already, or if you have literally no alternative gear.
