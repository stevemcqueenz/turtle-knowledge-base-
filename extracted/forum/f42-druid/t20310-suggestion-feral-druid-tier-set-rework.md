# Suggestion: Feral Druid Tier Set Rework

## Post 139785 by Decavi — 2025-07-16T04:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139785#p139785 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I’d like to propose a rework or reconsideration of the itemization on Feral Druid tier sets. As it currently stands, it feels highly inefficient — and honestly, disappointing — to see so much Intellect on gear that is supposed to be tailored for Feral Druids, especially when it's intended for tanking or melee DPS roles.

Intellect is largely a wasted stat on Feral Druids. Even if the justification is to support shapeshifting with a larger mana pool, having 100+ Intellect on a set that is meant for a form that doesn’t use mana or cast spells feels excessive and impractical. A much better approach would be to replace Intellect with useful offensive (Strength, Crit, Hit) or defensive (Stamina, Armor, Defense) stats.

It’s also strange that main tank sets (like T2 and T3) are given to Feral Druids when, in most cases, non-set pieces with the right stat distribution are far superior for bear tanking. On the other hand, Feral DPS Druids get Tier 2.5, which is ironically outclassed by other loot in the same raid. And yes, those other pieces often don’t carry Intellect — making them more viable for Feral builds.

The newly introduced Tier 3.5 (from Kara40) seems to fall into the same trap: it gives around 120+ Intellect, again, on a class/spec that does not use mana nor spells in combat. I believe most players would agree — it makes as little sense as giving Intellect to a Rogue.

It’s unfortunate to see such a unique and flavorful spec like Feral being overlooked or misrepresented in itemization. I hope this suggestion can serve as constructive feedback and encourage a more tailored approach in future tier designs.

Thank you for your time, and for all the hard work on Turtle WoW.
