# ECLIPSE... WHY ?

## Post 126009 by Tarsis — 2025-04-08T15:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126009#p126009 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have always liked druids, from D&D to Warcraft 3 and when Blizzard released WoW... well... i immediately made a Balance Druid and i kept playing it until Blizzard fucked it up and ruined it with ECLIPSE...

Eclipse is the definition of "ADDING COMPLEXITY FOR COMPLEXITY SAKE"...
It doesn't make playing the class more enjoyable, it doesn't add "flavor" to the class, it doesn't add flexibility to your playstyle, it adds constrictions and more buttons to press in a specific order, or you will perform WORST than before...

Eclipse is a NERF disguised as a BUFF.

Before i take my pants down to dump on Eclipse, let me say that the class update has been REALLY GOOD.

I was especially impressed by the Destruction Lock changes, they did a great job with it, removing Shadow Bolt from the fire spec, making Soul Fire and Searing Pain viable, they understood that if someone plays destruction, he probably wants to shoot Fire shit to enemies and not Shadow, WELL DONE.

I have many more compliments about the changes (and few eyebrow raising stuff too), but this is the Druid forum and i want to talk about Eclipse so... why is Eclipse bad?

Well to put it simple, Eclipse is a chore that penalizes you if you ignore it; is like having to dust your house or take out the garbage.

Plus all of this:

- Every other class got their damage improved, but Balance (in order to keep up with the others) has to rely on a short time buff window that triggers on a randomly generated spell crit...

- If you have to dps at specific moments during the fight, or you want to use some damage boost items, you have to cross your fingers and pray to the RNG gods for their favor, and they are shitty gods...

- If you are victim of a CC during your DPS window, you loose big DPS.

- If your target is Immune/heavy resistant to one of your two schools, you loose big DPS.

- If you have to move around during your DPS window, you loose big DPS.

- If you make a mistake and screw up your rotation, you loose big DPS.

- Forcing a class that already struggles with mana issues, to use more spells in the rotation, is sadistic.

- Gear Restrictions: forcing us to use both Nature and Arcane damage in the rotation prohibit us from using those items that buff Nature/Arcane specifically.

- PvP NERF: well is pretty obvious, if you want to heal and is not arcane damage boost time your opponent doesn't have to choose between interrupting your damage or wait for your heal/cc to go off, he just interrupts your nature school and you are stuck with the arcane school that now does 30% less damage (because remember every other class got buffed) FUN.

- TOO MUCH SHIT TO FOLLOW ON SCREEN: the Druid already has a shit ton of buttons to remember in PvP (normal spells plus Bear & Cat abilities, items, consumables, etc...) plus you have to track all other players around, enemies that need cc, allies that could use a heal or mana, and NOW i also have to constantly check which eclipse i'm on and how long does it lasts...
Now, you might be a 16yo Korean pro gamer, but i'm an old fuck with a job that doesn't have the brain capacity to process all that after 10 hours of work.
 ***ECLIPSE... My Assessment? UMGAK.***

There are many other ways to boost Druids, just on top of head, you can give us a Trent pet (or Furbolg, or whatever):

- Base abilities: Wrath (1.5 sec cast), Healing Touch (2 sec cast), no mana cost, 5 sec CD on both.

- The pet benefits of 1/3 of the owner Stats like HP, Armor, Spell DMG/Heal, 100% of owner crit/hit.
The pet has 3 fighting stances that can be swapped OUTSIDE of combat:
- Melee Stance: gains HP, Armor, Att Speed boost, a pet like Taunt, and a Gorilla pet like Stomp.

- Caster Stance: No CD on Wrath.

- Healer Stance: No CD on Healing Touch
Than just replace Eclipse with talents that gives Balance Druid 3 Treants but weaker, or the one Treant gets stronger.

Or whatever you feel like, almost anything is better than Eclipse.

I really love this server, everything you did with the world and the classes (you did a much better job than those idiots that sits inside the Blizzard building right now) and i would love to play my favorite class and have fun with it on Turtle WoW.

So i politely ask to PLZ reconsider the ECLIPSE talent for the Balance Druid.

I hope that this post reaches the devs and convince them to rethink the Eclipse introduction, or maybe you guys disagree with me and your vision of Balance Druid is completely different, and if that's the case well i will have to find a way to deal with that.

 **I wish everyone a nice day, peace.**
