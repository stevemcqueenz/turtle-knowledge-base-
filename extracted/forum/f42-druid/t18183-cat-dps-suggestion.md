# Cat DPS suggestion

## Post 123857 by Slade1994 — 2025-03-26T03:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123857#p123857 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi EU Turtle WoW community,

I’m a Feral Druid main from the SEA server. I’ve been playing Cat DPS since Nostalrius, through Classic 2018, and now on Turtle WoW. I really appreciate all the changes made to improve Cat DPS, but I still have some suggestions to further refine Feral Druid gameplay.

Rake: Direct damage remains unchanged. Bleed duration increased to 18 seconds, and bleed damage is relatively increased.

Rip: Bleed duration increased to 18 seconds, and the bleed damage per combo point is increased.
New effect: Ferocious Bite refreshes the duration of Rip on the current target.

Ferocious Bite:
•	Remove the energy-to-damage conversion effect, OR significantly improve the energy conversion ratio, as the current effect is too weak.
•	New effect: If the target is affected by Rip, Ferocious Bite will refresh Rip’s duration.

Tiger’s Fury:
•	Damage bonus increased by 50 points. The buff should persist in Humanoid Form until it expires or until the player shifts into an animal form other than Cat Form (e.g., it should not carry over to Bear Form).
•	Reasoning: The 20% attack speed bonus from talents persists outside Cat Form, so why shouldn’t the damage bonus? Additionally, since Feral Druids still rely on powershifting, the damage buff should remain.

Pounce:
•	This skill has seen little use in recent patches. Due to the “Open Wounds” effect, it’s sometimes used in solo elite as well, but the overall effectiveness remains poor. Suggested changes:
•	Increase bleed damage.
•	Base stun duration increased from 2 seconds to 3 seconds.
•	Remove the positional requirement (no longer requires attacking from behind).

Claw:
•	Currently capped at Rank 5, while Shred is already at Rank 6.
•	Given the current feral design, we improving Claw’s damage through bleed effects, should there be a Rank 6 Claw to increase its base damage accordingly?

⸻

Dash:
•	Currently, the buff disappears immediately when shifting out of Cat Form.
•	Suggested change: The buff should persist and continue to provide its effect when shifting back into Cat Form.

Auto-attacks:
•	Feral Druids are the only Melee DPS class unable to counter the glancing blow mechanic.
•	According to current talents and test results from raids, auto-attacks now contribute the highest DPS for Feral Druids in PvE, surpassing Shred.
•	Suggested change: Allow Leader of the Pack or weapons with Feral Attack Power to grant Druids their own weapon skill, resolving the issue of glancing blows. (Even Beast Mastery Hunter pets have weapon skills!) ![😭](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f62d.svg)

Ancient Brutality
•	No changes for Bear Form.
•	Energy regeneration from Cat Form bleeds is currently too low.
•	Suggested change: Increase energy regeneration to 4/6/8 to help Feral Druids reduce reliance on powershifting.

I understand that class balance is important—I’m just sharing my thoughts, and hopefully, some of these suggestions can help. Thanks for providing this platform! I love Turtle WoW!
