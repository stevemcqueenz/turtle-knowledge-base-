# Feral Feedback after raiding in 1.18

## Post 147482 by Snkx (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-21T12:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147482#p147482 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The new changes have been mostly positive for Feral.

However the spec still has some flaws.

#1 - Bleed Immunes
While not as devastating as before, they are still a big DPS loss, also you still have to change your playstyle entirely (to Powershifting), yes Tiger's Fury now grants 10 energy per 3 seconds, but this is still around a 20% dps loss compared to powershifting, people who enjoy the non powershifting variant dont want to feel screwed over on bleed immune.

#2 - Powershifting itself.
Even on non bleed immunes, the Bleeds Regen + TF vastly outperform powershifting, so you will only use Powershifting as a "Plan B" as mentioned above. - I think the gap is still too big and needs closing more, Maybe a buff to Furor to grant 50 Energy instead of 40 could help.

#3 - Talent Point Issue.
We feel really starved for Talent points, heck ive got FOUR Feral specs just in my Brain Wash device, one for Tanking, one for PVP, one for DPS, and one for DPS outside (with feral swiftness and feral charge)

In raids I mostly DPS as Cat, so I have zero room for any bear talents, I cant take Charge, Feral Instinct (Threat Talent), Thick hide (minor) or Feral Swiftness (dodge), the devs freed up 2 talent points by merging Savage Fury and Predatory Strikes, which was great.... But then they added a new 2 pointer which took these points up anyway, so this didnt really help.

Personally I'd like to see atleast 3 talent points freed up so I can atleast pick up Feral charge and Swiftness in the DPS Cat Spec, after all when you look at Ep*chs talent tree you can take both Tank&DPS talents, which is how feral should be IMO.

#4 - The Tier sets / itemization.

Okay I think everyone can agree that the new Tier sets designed for Feral Cats, have a tad too much intellect on them, this really hurts our dps, and alot of people are rolling on the leather chest from Meph in Kara (Warrirors, Shamans, Rogues, Hunters), which we also want, even more so that we dont have a great tier option.
Personally I'd like to see some intellect removed, and restatted into either strength or agility, also cats dont gain much from +% attack speed, thats more a Bear stat for threat.

#5 - Consider just removing the restriction from form entirely.

You have now removed potions and jujus from the restricted in form list, which is great, but it just still feels inconsistant, if a Bear can crack the cork out of a potion and drink it, why cant he throw a grenade or sapper charge? it makes so sense, on another Server I can do all that and pick the flag up in Warsong and talk to NPCs etc, it just feels alot more fluid .... You've done 75% of the work, why restrict the rest of it from being used?

Anyway thats all I have for now. - I think the feral changes have been great, but I still feel like more could be done.

Ty
