Some raids will take a boomy all the way and there's at least 4 that I know of currently in Naxx including some clearing KT. There are guilds that will definitely take a boomkin and let them actually roll on gear. In fact, the only one I know that doesn't take boomkins is <Macrochelys> since every other Naxx team I know of has at least one boomkin in it. <Thunder Ale brewing Company>, <Hard Knocks Society>, <The Blackwing Guard>, and I believe <Doom Turtles> all have at least one boomie in their core raid teams.

That said, your raw dps will not compete with other primary dps toons. This isn't calculating the addition of your faerie fire, crit aura, or great utility both during and between combats, but it needs to be said that your overall dps contribution will be less than other raw dps classes on paper. In action, it can very easily be a different story and I've seen multiple boomkins get top 5 dps on raid bosses in AQ40 and Naxx several times over.

If you want to compete, you must get your gear enchanted, you must use every consume available to you, and you need an alternative set, most likely resto, available to you. Otherwise, you'll justifiably be looked at as a wasted slot.
