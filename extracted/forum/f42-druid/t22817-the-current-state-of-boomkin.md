# The Current State of Boomkin

## Post 159149 by Scattyau — 2025-11-11T01:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159149#p159149 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I posted this to Reddit and was told I should crosspost it here. Yes, there are some issues with my haste comments and I added an edit, but didn't change the original content, so grain of salt that part. TLDR, Haste isn't as bad as I say, but Crit is still better. Also, I don't hate Fire Shaman, it is just a very apt comparison. My points would be the same in a vacuum if Fire Shaman was not how it is right now.

 -------------------------------------------------------------

Hey all. Scattyshifts here - 9/9 K40 and newly Scythe-touting Balance Druid Main here. Today, I'm here to tell you why Boomkin isn't rewarding to play. Its cool. Its viable. When you factor in raid comp, its great. But it doesn't feel rewarding.

The Competition
It would not be right to write this without context. So, I will be writing this knowing that Fire Shaman exists in its current state. For anyone unfamiliar, Fire Shaman has two buttons - Flame Shock, which fully snapshots trinkets etc, and Molten Blast, a 2s cast with excellent damage, double proccing from Rekindled Flame, and it refreshes your DOT duration on every single cast. You shock, then you Molten Blast for the whole fight, and you win DPS on Patchwerk. It is not engaging, but given its absurd simplicity and resistance to RNG, skill issues, or anything else, it massively overperforms and will almost definitely get a nerf. Arcane Mages, various flavours of Warlocks, even nature-based elemental Shamans all are in great places, and they play in ways that are rewarding and fun, but I need to call out Fire Shaman specifically because it overperforms with NO RNG and NO SKILL required. Which brings us to...

Performance
A full-BIS Boomkin, playing a perfect rotation with perfect 100%-chance Eclipse procs and lining up the next spells perfectly to benefit from Eclipses, standing still against a target dummy, will do LESS DPS than an equally geared Fire Shaman who used trinkets for their shock and have leaned on their Molten Blast button. To be clear, a Druid with 100% proc chance on their RNG rotation-defining talent, perfect play, practically impossible to achieve, will do less damage than the '1' spammer Shaman. This is important context as well - people will overlook a LOT of issues if meters/parses say #1. Unfortunately, we're not top, so we need to actually have a rewarding playstyle to make up for it. Lame, right? But the problem with that is...

RNG Punishes
For many classes, RNG will give you a proc that you can take advantage of. A new button lights up, and when you press it, bigger numbers happen. Warlocks get Nightfall - if they need to move, they can instantly throw their nuke for some free bonus damage while repositioning, or just use it anyway since its still a good bonus. Neato!

The Boomkin rotation is full of RNG, and all of it is punishing.

Firstly, Eclipse. Eclipse procs 50% of the time on a Starfire, or 30% of the time from Wrath *hits*. Given that, as casters, we are chain casting, if you are in your "fishing for Eclipse" phase, you're chain casting one spell. So, when you get the notification that your Eclipse has actually procced, you're already casting your next spell. This can often mean that if you are hard-casting a 3s Starfire, and your last cast procced your Nature Eclipse, it is better to CANCEL the cast, write off the whole 1.5s GCD, and instead begin chain casting Wrath. It feels SO BAD to literally cancel a cast because it isn't worth finishing it, and you have a buff ticking down that you need to make the most of. On the flip side, your Arcane Eclipse procs off the Wrath HITTING the target, and it has a hell of a travel time. So you will almost always lose at least 1, often 2-3, seconds of your 15 second buff because you were GCD locked into your "fishing" spell when the payoff landed.

Next, DOTs. Eclipse's damage buff is so high, it is mathematically worth clipping your DOTs at a certain point to refresh them with the Eclipse bonus. However, we have two DOTs with practically identical durations, and given the chaincasting nature of Boomkin, you really want to refresh them together. Optimal play means refreshing one with Eclipse out of sync, and so it feels gross to play. You're often clipping DOTs, or going without one briefly, because your casts and your lunar cycle don't play nice with DOT durations.

Next, Balance of All Things. There is so much to hate here. Firstly, they recharge randomly and unpredictably, with a 30% on each DOT tick. Second, the Starfire buff is great, reducing the cast time, but with 3 charges stacked, it is gone in 6 seconds of back-to-back crits and now you're back to hard casting 3s spells. The Wrath bonus is... well, a mana discount is welcome, but it objectively doesn't affect how you play. Neither of these really affect your decision making, the Starfire one feels good for the three charges but the odds you will recharge one before your Eclipse ends there are around a coin flip, so you're definitely spending the rest of the Eclipse and subsequent fishing phase on 3s casts, sans crits. But also, it can just NOT proc, and then it feels TERRIBLE to enter Arcane Eclipse with a 3s hard cast. It is absolutely possible to get only FOUR Starfires off in a whole Eclipse, less if you need to re-DOT. Also, our 5p T3.5 bonus, the best set in game right now, increases this lacklustre effect by 50%, so you can feel so little, slightly more! Drop your Wrath down to 37 mana, 3 times!

Also, Haste. Druid Atiesh gives Haste, our set is loaded with Haste, and our T3 8-piece bonus is Haste. Haste affects ONE of our FOUR spells. Wrath is GCD capped with 0 haste, and the two DOTs don't scale with it. So, Starfire gets Haste. Feels Bad Man, especially when Crit triple-dips by increasing damage per cast, proccing Nature's Grace for far better haste, and also increasing the Eclipse multiplier. My calculations put 1% crit equal to around 4-5% Haste. It might even be worse. But it is hard to avoid it.

 *EDIT: Moriena pointed out, Haste's GCD is 1.0s base, 0.5s with talent. So it still benefits from Haste. So this point is less pronounced, but Haste is still significantly worse than Crit but it is thrust at us from multiple places. Happy to be wrong, but it doesn't make it all better.*

Oh, and our Best Idol? Two choices. One, the best by my calculations, is a BOE that gives 17% Moonfire damage that scales with Spellpower. Just insane. Moonfire is around 22% of our damage, so increasing it by 17% is close to a 4% total DPS gain from the Idol slot. The other, Ebb And Flow, reduces Starfire cast time. The one spell that gets Haste, gets a bit more. It isn't bad, but given Starfire is our 4th best spell and we'd rather cast anything else, it is nowhere near as good as it FEELS. It FEELS great casting faster Starfires, but the actual damage impact is less than Mr 17% Moonfire over there, who sells for a lick under 20g right now and is BIS forever.

Overall, every single RNG talent feels like you have a ticking clock that is wasting away while you're locked into casting something else, or they're inconsequential until you just happened to NOT get them, then it just feels bad. There is no "oh yay, I got X, I can do this now", it is only "Fuck, of course it procced now..." or "where the hell is my X?"

Talent Bloat
This would not be complete without discussing talents. Firstly, Genesis in Resto is too strong to pass up, so you have 13 points in Resto. Thankfully this is improved from last patch, where we needed 18. However, we're still bloated with 38 points in Balance.

Firstly, you need the Core Talents. Everthing that increases damage, hit, range, crit, cast times, Moonkin Form, BoAT, Eclipse. That is 35 points. So, we have 3 points to flex, between interruption avoidance, improved moonkin form, gale winds, or any utility cross-tree. Improved Moonkin Form is a one-point wonder, since the only scaling is the cooldown going 30/25/20 seconds. So, two points, either gale winds or avoiding interruption. Gale Winds is amazing raid utility, but it adds no damage, so most go with interrupt avoidance, but it still brings you to 76% during an enraged owlkin proc, so you still get pushback.

Solutions
 *"Players are normally almost always right about problems, that's the thing I always find. They're almost never right about the solutions."* - Sean Murray, No Man's Sky creator.

With that in mind, the goal should be making it so when an RNG proc happens, you can take advantage of it without feeling that it is being wasted, and it should feel impactful.

First, Talents. Remove Moonfury - it is flat damage early in the tree on a tier with 10 other points. That frees up 3 points to get either all the Balance Tools, OR to hybrid into Resto further. Roll the damage bonus into Moonkin Form, or just increase the baseline spell damage. Moonkin Form gives the aura and armour, with a small mana discount, it is much less impactful than every other form that gives new abilities etc, or Tree with its massive healing bonus.

Balance of All Things should be something you want to see saved up, and spend the charges. This would actually much more be suited to the flat damage bonus Eclipse currently gives - save up bonus damage charges, and use them on demand when the lunar cycle is right, giving you more control over your playstyle and conscious payoff.

So, Eclipse. Right now, Eclipse can happen after any cast, often at terrible times, and you immediately begin wasting it. So, control or at least predicatability over WHEN it happens is important. Then, rather than just a flat bonus to damage, this is a better place for some interesting mechanics. Make an Eclipse-buffed Wrath have a 0.5s LONGER cast time, but have more crit/damage/something. That way, it benefits from Haste during your Eclipse. Starfire in Arcane Eclipse could have a big crit bonus, to help chain Nature's Grace together. Buffing Crit for these also increases the suggested BoAT damage bonus if it calcs the way current Eclipse does, with crit multiplier.

DOTs need something. We either need something to help keep them up (like Molten Blast, but less brain dead) or to make them less dependent on Eclipse and clipping them. Right now, they feel important to keep up, but hard to sync and never optimal. Maybe, having Eclipse-buffed spells hit a target extend that DOTs duration by a few seconds each time?

I'm not going to say my suggestions are good. But I want to focus on the principle issue - currently, they're unfun punishing RNG mechanics that make you bleed your already terrible optimal damage. Turn them into fun, engaging, celebratory events that give you a payoff, not just wasting your opportunity to keep up.

And remember, we're competing with Fire Shaman here. If we're gonna juggle double the DOTs, double the spells, incredibly lopsided itemization, RNG swing mechanics, RNG charges, and still not give the group Windfury, then I would hope we at least can match them in DPS if we play well. Right?

TLDR
For how many balls we're juggling, we don't do enough damage. The rotation is punitive, RNG based, and riddled with chances to LOSE damage without opportunities to capitalize and do more. Boomkin is constantly saying "oh for fuck's sake..." when procs don't proc, or proc at the worst times. Nerf Fire Shaman. Thank you for coming to my TED Talk.

I found this topic interesting and made a theorycraft for the boomkin talent tree.
Do you think the below changes would help?
I give my reasoning for every change I made.

<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/OhVxO-NNto>

 1. Balance of All Things
I found the original unreliable, specially for DoTs which are usually not spammed left and right.
Wanted to give a stackable buff instead (or two in this case). In the short term, they increase damage done. After the third stack, you get a free DoT.
Can be played strategically: you either swap between nature and balance to keep up the damage buffs as long as you can or you go for free and consistent DoT upkeep.

 2. Eclipse
The problem is the fishing nature of it you've described.
Turned it into a resistance decreaser + spell crit giver.
My reasoning is that a spec requiring two schools of nature to be consistent has to have a way to deal with mobs having some resists. Specially since no curse exists for nature.
Moonkins also want a bunch of spellcrit. Aura gives 3% but it's really low compared what others can get.
This can be maintained by one boomkin, swapping between nature and arcane spells or by several boomkins, making sure the target gets hit by both schools of nature periodically.

The reason behind these change ideas:
 1. Wanted to give buffs which feel rewarding and not punishing like the current ones.
 2. Wanted to make the effects more reliable.
 3. Wanted to smuggle in some spell penetration and spell crit.

PS: These changes wouldn't make casting DoTs redundant as you frequently want them from the beginning of a fight, not after 3 casts of both wrath and starfire.

I greet you my fellow druids!

I've been a balance druid ever since my time as a Tauren Greenhorn in Vanilla Classic. In this time I was fortunate enough to see everything from family like noob guild to tryhard semi and actual pro gameplay.

The endgame playstyle for druids on Turtle WoW I can only judge from videos so far so this is a post that combines my experience and general impression about what is wrong with the ways of balance druidism.

In my opinion, the (current) state of boomkin is broken ever since Eclipse was introduced. I've been despising this talent ever since the rise of the scourge lead by the Lich King.
Why? It is in conflict with the very essence, the nature of playstyle of druids which is flexible, adaptive, hybrid. Druid gameplay is Freedom or let's say it was. Now my fellow druids care about being #1 in dps or timing eclipse, charges n stuff, instead of being the druud that holds stuff together with a big occasional heal or situational wrathful burst.

Sure it's great to finally be on top of the meters and enjoy big numbers here and there but at what cost? Have we been fallen to the curse of power like the Nighelves a long time ago?
Don't get me wrong! I also enjoyed my fair share of being a top tier battle chicken wielding the arcane and power of nature. However, the restricting nature of Eclipse always lead me to explore other roles and tbh citical resto in raids or balance bear leveling is so much more fun than focusing on what's my current state of Eclipse and therefore what is being dictated to do next..
This is why I will continue to boycott Eclipse for the sake of the Vanilla Experience (which does not go along well with Eclipse, imho) and if this means that I will be playing casually for the rest of my days. So be it.

Eclipse makes balance druid gameplay feel static, clunky and I can not think of any implementation that was truly fun, didn't feel restricting and forcing a certain playstyle and rotation upon me.

 ****For the sake of the Vanilla spirit, Elune, Cenarius and everything that is sacred to Druids in the World of Warcraft.****

Why do you still care so much about Eclipse?! Did you ever experience the freedom of pressing Wrath and Starfire whenever you wanted to, save a fellow heroes ass or just contributed enough to make you feel complete at the end of the raid day?

I wish the Turtle WoW Team and Community would rethink if Eclipse is truly Vanilla and has its place earned in a game that aims to carry forward the spirit of the old days.
