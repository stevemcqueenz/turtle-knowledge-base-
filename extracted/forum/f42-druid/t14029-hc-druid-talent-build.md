## Post 95535 by Hctwowfan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-05-23T22:11:00Z

I'd go feral to Feline Swiftness so you got a nice +30% cat movement speed at 21. Then switch to balance to get Omen of Clarity asap. Then either pick 3/3 Improved Thorns or switch right back to feral an all the way down. Depends if you wanna herd up 3/4 mobs and swipe em down in bear or 1v1 front shred mobs in cat. Furor in resto ain't exactly helpful while leveling, so I'd skip that.

Your talents would look like this more or less.
<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid/1O3F6-Z0K3FEDV>

Feel free to pick feral talents in whatever order they become available after you got OoC. And obviously skip (or pick late) Improved Shred if you don't front shred and/or don't do lots of group content in cat.
