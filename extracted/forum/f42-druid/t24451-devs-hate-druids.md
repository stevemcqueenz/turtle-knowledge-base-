I will share 4 cases as case studies on events that happened to me today, doing outdoor pvp.

Case 1 - VS Ele/ Enh Tank Shaman

So I found this guy, he had good gear, but not better than mine. He had 5k+ hp, and so did i (with buffs).

I opened the fight and i did a crit shred on him. I did 350dmg, again and again, even with rake and rip on my dmg was simply pathetic. You'd think ok, he is a tank and that's fair, but then let's go to the damage HE did on me. The fight took roughly 15 seconds, he casted 3 earth shocks on me all three crits averaging 1800 EACH. 0 skill, just pure insanity. It's like fighting 3vs1. Then the dude proceeded to kill some elite, even though i never attack someone that is not full hp or in a fight, i went for it, and surely he just melted both me and the elite no issues, he was still above 70% hp...

Case 2 - VS Assassination Rogue

I opened him, did shred crit of about 1k, before he could do any dmg on me i got him to 50%, then he kidney shotted me, i trinketed, eventually i killed him, but he got me to 30% hp in less than 5 seconds. Latest patch supposedly nerfed the stupidly broken poisons, but in fact, they got buffed. Dissolvent poisons used to hit me for about 200, now instant poisons hit me 220-250/hit and he hits like crazy, its like 700dps from poisons only.

Case 3 - VS Demo/Affliction lock

I opened him, he coiled me, he didn't even bother to fear me, just 3 dots and until coil was over, and i run back to him, i was dead, even with Zanza and pot. His dots tick for about 600/tick + soul drain its like 1000 dps. Then he /w me from another character telling me that I am noob because i could heal and decurse... I mean you can't make this shit up.. his Agony was ticking for 130dmg not worth going in and out of cat form to decurse because the whole fight took 5 seconds. It's just impossible not only to kill, but to get him to 50% even when i open, and even then he didn't even bothered to sacrifice Void, or use healthstone, and he still had soulstone on.

Then a hunter saw the fight and joined, the lock killed him as well, and he was still above 50% hp without sacrificing Void...

Case 4 - VS Discipline Priest

This happened in AB, i open him, get him to 50%. All he had to do was jump around spamming holy nova, and insta smites. I was dead within 5 sec, he did x2 crit insta Smites for 1400 each + the holy novas.

All these classes got insanely broken buffs, while feral druids are getting nerf after nerf after nerf, even thought they completely underwhelmingly underpowered. In order for me to do proper dps on them, i need to play litteral piano building dots, and hopping for all the starts to align and even then the best i can hope for is to get them to 50% hp? With all of their CDs still untapped?

Even Shred, which is the most powerful ability I have is completely crap when compared to any of their abilities, not to talk about skills like savage bite, which should rather be named kitty's cuddle. It cost 25 rage, and does what 300dmg (today i **critted** an arms warrior for 141dmg with it).

Are you also equally frustrated with how miserably unbalanced PVP is and how devs keep making it worse in every patch?
