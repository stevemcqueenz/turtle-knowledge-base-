# Resurrection Flexibility for Resto Druids *Suggestion*

## Post 58810 by Tieku — 2023-08-24T10:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58810#p58810 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What would we think about giving Resto druids the *option* to pick a traditional resurrection instead of the battle-res? The way I can see it is that out-of-combat resurrections are purely a convenience factor other than some niche uses in PvP and I can't see how allowing Resto Druids to access this would disrupt balance in any meaningful way.

Allowing Resto druids to choose a traditional resurrection as a talent also allows Feral/Balance/Hybrid druids to still have battle-res keeping the class identity intact as well as giving resto druids a little bit more choice in their playstyle.

Ultimately I can't see anyone really being against this, everyone who isn't a resto druid would rather their healer could resurrect like normal in PvE content and I imagine that majority of resto druids would prefer that they don't have to force the group to corpse-run everytime the unfortunate happens. (Potentially making more healers available, I know I for one do not like healing 5-mans as a druid for this reason)
Making this a talented option deep in the resto tree preserves class identity and unless I am mistaken does not disrupt class balance in any way.

It sucks being the only healing class without an unlimited res, but I don't really feel it's a big problem to be honest.

What's a realistic scenario where it would really matter to have an unlimited out-of-combat res available?
- whole party wipe - nope, out-of-combat res makes no difference

- you pull heal aggro and die - nope, out-of-combat res makes no difference

- one person randomly dies due to a confluence of bad circumstances - nope, just spend your battle res (I've run a *lot* of dungeons, I've used BR maybe once in actual battle to bring someone back - usually if shit hits the fan you have other worries than blowing 1k mana on a res)
So an out-of-combat res makes a difference when multiple people die and there are no shamans, priests or palas left standing while a druid is still alive - that seems like a really low-odds scenario to be honest. If the druid is the tank or the healer, chances are they died. Statistically most parties will have a shaman/priest/pala since the roles (dps/tank/heal) are so open on Turtle.

Furthermore, you specifically suggest an out-of-combat res only for resto druids. If you're the healer of the group, just get good enough that people don't die. With a good tank and a good healer people seldom die. And with a bad tank and a good healer the first person to die will usually be the healer, which makes a res pointless as well.

Again, I'm not categorically against it. Just that from my experience running a few hundred dungeons on TWoW as a resto druid I can count the times where it would have made a difference on one hand.

## Post 58934 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-08-25T00:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58934#p58934 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

And a Paladin not being able to pull from range is a perfectly fine shortcoming in class design, as they are primarily AoE tanking in their niche, and thus running into a group from just the right angle to not chain pull and being right there in the middle of the action is not inherently detrimental to their ability to tank. It simply means that they must pull in more creative ways, and then slowly drag the group back to the party to position it properly. All of this is within acceptable parameters and, again, does not impede the tank's ability to tank.

Not having a baseline taunt, however, when a boss has a literal threat erase ability that involves a kick or a stun or something that prevents even a prepared tank from immediately reacquiring aggro when heals are still going to be happening (and then will happen more as the healer has to keep squishies or themselves alive for the 1 or 2 attacks that are on them and not the Paladin) and DPS is still going to be trying to do its job... That's a problem. The tank is then throwing every last bit of mana they have just to regain aggro desperately because asking everyone to stop doing everything for 2 or 3 seconds every 15-20 seconds is not a reasonable demand, and then will have no mana to retain that aggro, creating an incredibly frustrating experience for that tank.

THAT is bad design, where a tank cannot do its job because of circumstances beyond their control that are built into the game. No one wants classes to be exactly the same. No one wants classes to not have their quirks and identity. But identity comes from strengths and weaknesses, and the lack of a basic necessity for countering game mechanics such as aggro is not a weakness. It's a game design weakness.

Admittedly, having an out of combat res is not nearly as fundamentally important as taunt for a tank, but it nonetheless does not have any meaningful reason for continuing. I have yet to find anyone who can provide a reason that cites principles of good UX design, game and class design, or objectively measurable criteria for why Resto Druids, at the very least, should not have an out of combat res.  I fail to see the fundamental value added to the game by the lack.  I fail to see how saying "flavor" or "class identity" serves to somehow exonerate this design decision, as if throwing buzzwords around is somehow itself adding value to the discussion, yet often I see precisely that in discussions such as this one.
