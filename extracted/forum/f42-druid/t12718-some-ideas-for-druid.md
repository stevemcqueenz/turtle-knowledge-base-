Druid Class Turtle wow ideas.

Fist weps for druid
Can visually see them on the druid in forms.
Maybe slow the druids movement speed down due to bulkiness. This allows chance on hit and enchants and wf  totem can proc. I know this would favor fist weps over all else so a work around could be conjured.

New markings or tattoos on the druid  skin as an enchant or maybe a rune in the world. Large items marks with ancient runes to buff the druid or shaman. Different runes will give different tattoos and different buffs- for example a fire rune will give fire resistance and give red markings on the bear/cat/boomkin forms. There could be hand in requirement's for the runes and the buff only lasts so long.

Capturing fish in aqua form, if the druid is swimming through the fish spawn rings it could add some fish to the inventory. Tauren get striding which is stacked on herb gathering so this cant be to op.

Bark skin usable in form.

Swipe hits 5 over 3 targets while in bear form and can be used in cat form, maybe keep it at 3 targets in cat form

Rebirth has short cd when used outside combat. Long cd when used as a cb res.

Polearms would be nice to use

Travel form speed increases with level like the turtle mount speed? It is funny to see tauren and turtles out running a cheetah lol. Will max at 60 percent. Could increase with riding skill.

Symbiosis was a move added sometime after wotlk where the druid can trade a spell with another class. This is a great idea and could be added with low tier spells to add flavor and lore.

Spirit bomb would be great, ask the world for it energy and sacrifice your allies life force to nuke an enemy. The more energy, the bigger and stronger it will be... Kakarot..you can do it! lol

New forms that take over existing forms,  A few ideas for forms could be..

Cat Form ideas: spider, wolf, crocodile, raptor, hyena,

Bear form ideas: kodoo, TURTLE,

Boomkin: gnolls, those horsemen things, dragon,

Keep the moves simple and things will carry over for both abilities and talents. Say spider for example has a poison over time move called sting but it a simple renaming of rake with a new picture with poison instead of bleed damage. Rename fb with furious fangs. Just pick animals that can stealth and again rename and change the pics of the ability.
A raptor and wolf would do very similar moves to the original cat form. This is a work in progress of course but is meant to spark ideas that could b implemented and fun. limit it to 1 different form at a time, so you can only have cat or spider, not both.

This can also be compounded in the shop, the player can learn the form through a quest and can have skin changes via the shop.

Make a slower larger travel form that can be mounted by an ally for traveling. Like..a TURTLE!

These are just ideas and are food for thought. What do you all think
