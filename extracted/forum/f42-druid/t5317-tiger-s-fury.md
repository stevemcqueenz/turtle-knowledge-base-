## Post 74041 by Tomberry — 2023-10-26T21:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74041#p74041 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think as a fresh 60 it's not that bad.
Your equip and damage are not very good, so the extra attack and the 40 extra DMG per hit from TF really kick in.
That's 8 whitehits plus 1 extra attack plus 3 shreds/Bite plus Omen shreds getting the bonus in these 9 sec.

Later, when your equipment improves, your Energy to Damage ratio gets better and the extra energy from shifting gives you more damage than the flat 40 DMG per hit.
And whitehits are less effective against raid bosses due to glancing anyway, so the yellow damage from energy becomes even more valuable.

But I can imagine that as a green lvl 60 TF is equal or better. Should be viable for dungeons when you gear up for raids.
