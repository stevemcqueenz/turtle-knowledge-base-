## Post 58705 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-23T15:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58705#p58705 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Druid tanking hasn't been changed too much mechanically, but there are some nice tweaks (1 new rage dump, 1 new "oh shit" button, greatly buffed Frenzied Regeneration). More impactfully there is a lot better gear, more game knowledge and more accepting people. Druid tanking for 5-people instances is super viable and completely normal. Druids main tanking for 20-people raids is still completely viable and somewhat common (I've done it myself). For some fights druids are actually the ideal tank (can't be polymophed/hexed, can't be disarmed).
For MC and BWL it becomes more a social problem than a mechanical problem - geared and experienced warriors sign up as tanks and you have to find a spot. I have off-tanked in BWL, but at least our raid has so many main-spec tanks that I don't really consider signing up as a tank myself when there's other roles to fill that benefit the raid more.

With pre-BWL tanking gear I have more HP (and naturally way more armor) than our BWL-geared tanks, but there are still some obvious draw-backs: I don't have the -10% damage reduction against magical damage from defensive stance. I don't have a good spell interrupt like shield bash (many boss mobs are stun immune). I cannot stance-dance to avoid fears (a mechanic prominently featured in the Ony and Nef fights). Turtle has Berserking which lets me avoid 1 fear, but with a 6min cooldown, which is not really viable for these certain bosses (apart from Nefarian's druid call pretty much entirely preventing druids from tanking him anyway).

When it comes to Naxx... I haven't been myself, but from what I've heard from other druids there are some select fights where bears have their place. We still have the most physical mitigation and if geared right can actually output the most threat-per-second since our main threat skills are multipliers applied to damage (while warrior skills generate flat threat values per use, ie don't really scale with gear). I have not heard of a druid main-tanking all of Naxx but that doesn't mean it doesn't exist.
