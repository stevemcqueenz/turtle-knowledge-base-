# Resto Druid PvP Healing: Lack of Quick Heals?

## Post 156271 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-10-20T08:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156271#p156271 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Lately I’ve noticed something in BGs that’s been bugging me. In clutch moments, once I’ve already applied Rejuvenation and Regrowth, used Nature’s Swiftness + Healing Touch, and Swiftmend is on cooldown, I’m left with basically one option: spam Regrowth.

The problem is, Regrowth spam absolutely drains mana, and Healing Touch is way too slow to rely on in active PvP fights. Is there really no way around this? Are resto druids just designed to rely on other healers for those fast 1.5-second burst heals like priests, paladins, and shamans all have?

On my priest, for example, I rarely even touch Greater Heal because it’s too slow, but at least I’ve got Flash Heal to keep people alive in emergencies. It feels like druids just don’t have that equivalent until WotLK, when we finally get Nourish as a proper 1.5-second direct heal.

Curious how other druids handle this. Do you just pre-HoT and pray, or is there some trick I’m missing?
