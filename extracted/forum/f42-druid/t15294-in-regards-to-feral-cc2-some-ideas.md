# In regards to Feral CC2, some ideas.

## Post 104327 by ForumOLV — 2024-10-22T12:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104327#p104327 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I want to start by saying that I appreciate the team's efforts with all the class changes, including those for Druids. However, I feel that **Feral Druids** are somewhat lacking compared to the improvements other classes have received.

 **Why Do I Say That?**
We’ve been given a new talent called **"Open Wounds"**. While it seems weaker than the current **Powershifting** gameplay on paper, after running some numbers, it could offer a decent alternative for players who don't want to engage with Powershifting. It introduces a new playstyle and makes **Claw** a relevant ability, which I see as a positive direction. I hope that the numbers will reflect it as a viable option.

 **What I Like About the Talent**
What excites me most about this talent is that it makes **Claw** relevant again. That said, there are still a few other abilities that hold very little importance in PvE. Specifically, our **stealth openers**:

- **Pounce**
- **Ravage**

 **Stealth Openers in PvE**
In most PvE situations, Feral Druids have little incentive to use their stealth openers:

- **Pounce** deals negligible damage.
- **Ravage** is outclassed by simply using two **Shreds** in terms of damage output.
 **My Recommendations**

 **Pounce**

Pounce is valuable in PvP for its **stun** component, but the damage portion is extremely low. I noticed that Paladins' **Repentance** will have a PvE component where, if the target is immune to the effect, it still takes damage. I'd like to propose a similar change for **Pounce**:

- If the target is **immune to stuns**, allow the damage from Pounce to stack with every tick (similar to **Curse of Agony**).
- **Damage Scaling Suggestion**: Pounce deals 90 damage every 2 seconds. I suggest a cap of 300% (270 damage every 2 seconds), with the damage increasing by 10% per tick. This would require 60 seconds to reach the cap of 30 stacks (300%).
Since **Pounce** only lasts 18 seconds, I would recommend the following talent tweak to accommodate this:

 **Brutal Impact (2/2)**
- Increases the duration of **Bash** and **Pounce** by 0.5/1 second.
- **Claw** increases the duration of the **Pounce bleed** on the target by 1.5/3 seconds.
With **1 point**, Pounce will drop off over time unless you use finishers and **Clearcasting (CCg)** procs on **Shred**. With **2 points**, you can extend its duration past the global cooldown (GCD), allowing more flexibility to use finishers and CCg procs.

In PvP, this tweak shouldn’t affect balance much, since it only ties to the damage component, which activates when the target is immune. You **could** plan around using it on a stun-immune target, but unless you stay in Cat Form using **Claw** constantly, you wouldn’t gain the full benefit.

 **Ravage**

Let's be honest, Ravage is just a heavy-hitting, frontloaded ability. After I ran the numbers on why Shred was removed from Open Wounds, I found that it was doing as much damage as Ravage, which was interesting to see. Since my Pounce idea is made to accommodate the Open Wounds playstyle of bleed uptime, I would make Ravage the go-to ability for Powershift players.

As such, I have the following idea: Since Powershifting accounts mostly for resetting your energy to spam Shred (and soon, to some extent, Claw, Rake, and Rip), Shred should have an interaction with Ravage. Ravage hits quite hard and would be a bit too much to be able to spam. But what if we rewarded players who Powershift properly with some heavy-hitting hits?

 **REMOVED:** Improved Shred

 **New Talent: Chase the Prey 2/2**
Ravage applies **Chase the Prey** on the target for 20/40 seconds. Using **Shred** on a target afflicted by **Chase the Prey** buffs the druid with stacks of **Ravenous**. Each stack has a 1.5% /3% chance upon **shapeshifting into Cat Form** to allow the usage of **Ravage**. Additionally, this talent reduces the energy cost of Shred by 6 / 12 energy.

 **The idea is the following:**
Reward the druid who starts with Ravage with a chance to use Ravage during the fight. To keep up the debuff, you need to focus on Shredding and Powershifting. Ravage hits very hard, and proper gameplay would allow you to leverage this talent much more often. For PvP, it wouldn't be optimal to use as you would have to maintain Cat Form for a long period of time and Powershift inefficiently for a chance to be able to use Ravage.

With all of these recommendations in mind, I would love to know the thoughts of others on this topic or what else they think Druid needs or would be nice to have in light of the soon upcoming changes. I would appreciate if your thoughts were shared in way that's constructive for the conversation, we have #druid on Turtle's Discord for dooming and memes.

In regards to your post I also have some ideas in regard to the CC2 changes to feral druid, both tanking and damage dealing. I’ll give my review and thoughts on the new playstyle in PvE point of view only. I’m by means an expert on feral druids, but not completely new either.

 ****Cat DPS****
Right now, the way for feral DPS seem to be power shifting and the rotation goes roughly like
- Shred till 4-5 combo points and then Ferocious Bite, while power shifting to refill your energy.

- Occasionally throw in a Tiger’s Fury for the 12% haste if you got the chance.
With the new class changes you get the option to use “bleed build”. However, if you want to continue as before. The only thing we get is like 18% more haste in your rotation and the combo points follows if you switch target That’s it.

“Cat Form’s Combo Points will no longer vanish upon switching targets, but instead reset when applying combo points on a new target.”

If you want to try the new “bleed build” you got a few more things to look out for in comparison to powershifting.
- You need to keep track of 3 uptimes
o        Tiger’s Fury - 12 sec.
o	Rip - 12 sec.
o	Rake - 9 sec.
I did some testing self-buffed (no consumes) with 28% crit chance and not power shifting, and it was not easy to keep all 3 abilities active at the same time, and I surely did not manage to weave in Ferocious Bite.

One of the perks though is that we don’t have to think about hitting the back.

Don’t get me wrong, I absolutely love an active playstyle, but I also like to perform well in raid and pull my own weight. I think one of the reasons some classes perform better than others are because of its simplicity.

When I play my “boring” mage I just tab from target to target and cast frost bolt. There’s not much to look out for beside mana and aggro.

My suggestion to bleed build is to automate at least some of the abilities. Either by:
- Crits or ability crits refresh the rake bleed.

- Finishers refresh Tiger’s Fury.

- Or something else…
Warrior’s impale talent automatically apply bleed, their Flurry talent gives them the 30% haste (for 3 swings)

 ****Feral Tanking****
Right now, most of our threat comes from MCP and thanks for removing that item. But then we are down to 30% haste from enrage for a shorter duration.

In comparison to feral DPS, tanking is a very passive playstyle that mostly reply on your gear rather than your skills (of cause it matter a lot to know the boss mechanics, but I’m talking about our buttons to press)

We got Maul, Swipe and Savage Bite

- Maul consumes our rage and auto attack for more threat, but also uses our white swing that provides us with rage.

- Swipe got buffed a bit but is used to keep aggro on more target.

- Savage Bite is also buffed with more threat and can be clever used with clearcasting proc as it cost a bit more (25 rage).
So besides managing our rage we don’t got much going on for us. We don’t have an active/reactive ability to avoid attacks. My suggestion would be to add something to that playstyle, so it doesn’t only come down to how good your gear is.

With the new class changes we are going even further away from an active playstyle with Frenzied Regeneration increased from 3 to 6 min. and Barkskin from 1 to 10 min.

I think it’s interesting that dodge has become somewhat useful, but if as some people mention that druids can become crit immune, then talents like Feral Adrenaline become redundant.

Obviously I haven’t tested Ancient Brutality so it’s hard to comment on, but I think it’s nice to get something from dodge.

What could change towards a more active and skill-based tank play? I don’t know, but you could leave Barkskin as it is and let it be used in forms? You trade some threat for survivability. Nice for enrage phases or if you get low on HP and healers are dead and IF you have enough threat to sacrifice.

 **Items**
I don’t know how the new tier set are going to affect us, but out playstyle shouldn’t be tailored to a specific item, we saw that with MCP and Wolfsheart helm.

And while we are at it. I think it would be a good idea to move the Wolfsheart enchant to idol slot or just make it baseline part of the Furor talent. So we don’t miss out on a head enchant.

 **Talents**
A last thing about the changes. It seems like we need 3 specs in feral now which I think it’s a shame. The devs should try to combine some of the talents so we don’t need 3 different feral specs.

- Feral tanking (and we don’t even have talent points enough for a full tanking spec)

- Raid Power Shifting spec (as it still seems to be our best option)

- Single target farm spec (I find myself using claw a lot when farming mobs in the open world. I think this could boost our farm potential)
