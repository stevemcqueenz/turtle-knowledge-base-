# Balance Druid Ideas

## Post 33189 by Watchman777 — 2023-01-26T15:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33189#p33189 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just had thoughts on some small changes to help Balance:
First allowing Roots indoors is a must.
Second is bumping Insect Swarm to 24 seconds as the damage is negligible anyway and it saves a little mana reapplying.
Third is some sort of interrupt as shifting into Bear is simply not viable especially if kiting and not having points in Furore.
Finally Barkskin feels very off. While having 20% DR on a one minute CD is nice enough the spell and melee slow down feels punishing and feels counter intuitive with the spell pushback protection. Personally I'd rather a buff to the DR, longer CD and no castspeed and melee increase to make it a decent defensive CD then it's current state.
These changes for me would help flesh out the spec both for levelling and endgame without being too radical.
