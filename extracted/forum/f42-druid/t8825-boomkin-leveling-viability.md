I had a level long phase where I decided to respec balance around early 50 somethings, and in all honesty, it's... Fine? Mana issues and all aside, you've got great survivability, your kill times are comparable to feral except with more control over situations due to roots and being at range. You have SLIGHTLY better healing capabilities with the moonglow talent? (I might be wrong about the name but I know there's a talent that reduces mana cost on some spells including healing ones.) All in all, the spec is definitely viable to level as, despite feral being much better in many ways. I look at it like this. You want fun? Pick whatever's fun for you. You want better? Pick feral.

## Post 57086 by Citramate — 2023-08-13T12:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57086#p57086 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've found that it's not that bad if you keep a decent staff and melee a lot, abusing omen of clarity procs to basically have no downtime. Similar playstyle to a priest. Just need a swing timer to know when to use moonfire to not delay your attack. Basically Starfire/wrath -> moonfire -> insect swarm into melee attacks with the occasional moonfire on omen procs or a big heal to restore HP on an omen proc. The solo kill pacing isn't actually that bad and omen proc healing is completely underrated for healing dungeons, i probably only need to drink 1-3 times per dungeon due to omen procs bypassing the 5 second rule.

It's still nothing compared to a tigers fury & front stabbing feral druid for soloing but it's not bad compared to classes like paladin, shaman, warrior or rogue.
