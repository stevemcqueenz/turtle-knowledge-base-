# Feral druids should be able to...

## Post 51268 by Kothorix — 2023-06-25T17:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51268#p51268 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This would just be a small quality-of-life change, but as someone who identifies as a cat and/or bear (and occasional mangled walrus thing), it would greatly alleviate the stress of clicking one button to shift out of feral form if I could talk to NPCs as bear/cat.

You could make some kind of roleplay argument that "cats and bears can't speak human words" but just think about it for a minute. If you were a vendor and a cat-form druid dropped a bunch of garbage at your feet and started screaming and caterwauling, you wouldn't know that means "give me silver for this"? Or if you told someone clad in animal skins, antlers, twisted branch staff, and other weird druid crap to kill a Satyr and then 20 minutes later a cat-form druid ran up to you carrying the bloodied head of that SAME Satyr; you wouldn't know it's the same guy? Give me a break.

Turtle WoW seems pretty keen on making changes in the form of quests - and I like that. So here is my suggestion for a quest:
Level 20ish druid quest. You go to giant-bearpaw-man in Darnassus and he's like "Ah yes, young [character name], surely you've noticed by now that normies can't talk to animals like we can. It can be quite inconvenient. There is, however, a way to alleviate this. The furbolgs of Ashenvale are hoarding caapi vines - go raid their stores and roll around in it for a while. The ayahuasca fumes should allow anyone near you to speak to animals, allowing you to converse while in cat or bear form."

In all seriousness though, this would be a simple change and make questing as a feral druid a little less annoying. Every second I spend outside of cat form is an eternity in hell.

Believe it or not, even human teeth can break most common rocks (look it up). Large cats and especially bears have jaw power (and tooth hardness) many times that of a human. Then consider that druid forms use their teeth and claws as weapons to fight giant monsters (who are often made out of rocks and/or metal). On top of that druids are given magically enhanced items and abilities which make them even stronger. How much attack power does it take to crush rocks with your teeth? I don't know, but if I can do it in what is essentially grey/white gear IRL, then my druid with gear granting hundreds of AP can certainly do it in-game. Needless to mention, things like copper and silver are MUCH softer than the rocks surrounding them.

All of this means that your cat or bear forms wouldn't only be able to mine, but are very capable of tearing copper nodes out of the ground and refining them into bars without a smelter like they are chewing bubble gum.
