****Here is a couple of useful tips for your druid endeavor****   satisfied_turtle_head

 **Macro Spells** For each of your physical attacks I recommend to make a macro like this. It triggers an autoattack before the ability and will auto target the closest enemy in front of you. It's a basic for every melee. Code: Select all

```
/startattack
/cast Swipe
```

For talents:
 - **Thick Hide:** don't forget to spec into for the extra Armor. Healers will thank you and its generally useful. Many cat druids are squishy for missing out on it or forgetting to upgrade their items
 - **Nature's Grasp:** in Balance is worth one point for the escape utility
 - **Sylvan Blessing:** if you need to heal yourself often or if you catch yourself drinking after getting bear form, consider a single point
 - **Feral Instinct:** One point should be enough in the beginning - put more in only if you struggle with holding aggro

On lower levels I play like this to speed things up:
 - range pull 2-3 mobs with a DoT like Moonfire or Insect Swarm
 - switch to Bear and Swipe to cleave em down

Here is a light talent suggestion to start:
<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=BB-FAJYRQ->

Make it your own and adapt to how you play like

As a tank main (Bear, paladin, warrior) since 2008, here's my opinion.

I 100% agree with this:
> **Chev03 wrote: Wed Jan 21, 2026 9:46 am**

> /cast Swipe
> ```

But he forgot to mention that you need addons such as "****CleveRoid Macros****" in order for these macro inputs to work in the vanilla 1.12 client.

> 1- Night Elf or Tauren ?

For tanking, Tauren is always the better option due to their racial ability that grants 5% extra Stamina, which are tanks most important stat. Tauren also have a much bigger bear form model than night elves. So it adds to the immersion of the tank fantasy.

> 2-  I want to level up as feral and tank dungeons at the same time, can you give me a talent build?

[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?po ... DQQIFQB-Ao](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=BSAaAI-FAYYRaDQQIFQB-Ao)
This is the meta tank build for the current patch of class changes. A lot of people ignore [Sylvan Blessing] because it is worded weirdly. But it's a really important talent that helps keep you from going out of mana when you heal yourself in between mobs. This minimizes your down time, so you don't have to stop and drink often while leveling.

> 3- Omen of clarity and Furor first or go straight full feral ?

Always go [Omen of Clarity] first by Lv20, then into [Furor] by Lv25. The clearcasting procs very often and gives you tons of threat when tanking from all the free swipes you get in bear form. It also gives you free claws/shreds in cat form, so you kill targets faster while questing and maximize your up time. These two talents provide a much smoother and more efficient leveling experience.

bonus advice as a long time druid player: Grinding dungeons doesnt help you level faster. At all.
