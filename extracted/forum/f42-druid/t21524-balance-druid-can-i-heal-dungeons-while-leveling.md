## Post 149149 by Mcnair (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-29T14:37:00Z

Good day.
As long as you have an Intellect-heavy gear for healing (which you'll have if you plan on being Balance), you can surely heal all the instances as you level, unless your tank is underleveled or badly undergeared (e.g. fury warrior without a shield and in mostly leather DPS gear).
This in general true for all healer/hybrid classes, be it paladins, druids, shaman, or priest, they all can heal the instances as they come in any spec (or even without any talents) as long as they have an Intellect-heavy healing gear.
Just make sure to either have a mage or buy drinks before the start, as without a constant supply of drinks the downtimes will be rather insufferable. Also, do not try to be a miser with the said drinks, drink whenever you get out of combat and you have missing mana, as sadly many tanks tend to chainpull in instances disregarding healer mana.
This starts to change around level 50 or so, when the instances with higher mob density and overall higher damage start.
