# Taco Form

## Post 47272 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-05-24T15:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47272#p47272 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Firstly, for those lore elitists out there, this would make perfect sense, as tacos have lettuce in them which is a plant. Now that we have that out of the way, let's get to theorycrafting. The taco form will be an entirely new spec that uses spiciness as a resource, which works like rage. A taco has 100 spiciness total and this resource is generated through using certain abilties. that blends ranged and melee abilities in a play style that focuses on utilizing the various ingredients found in a taco to deal damage and occasionally support and heal allies. To start, let's look at some basic abilities. I may post more spells and possibly even talent ideas later, but for now we'll keep it basic.

 *Hot sauce: Splashes the target with a spicy sauce, dealing minor damage over time for 5 seconds and reducing armor for the duration. Has a 20 second CD and costs 10 spice.

 *Mmm, taco: Share a bite of yourself with an ally, healing them a large amount but dealing minor damage to yourself as punishment.

 *Sizzling rage: Goes into a delicious frenzy, the beef inside of you generating 15 spice and then an additional 20 spice over 25 seconds. Has a one minute CD.

 *Inspiring scent: Tempt your allies within 30 yards with the smell of taco, making them really hangry and increasing damage by 5% for 5 seconds. Has a 5 minute CD.

 *Beefy Slap: Bonks your enemy with your taco body, spilling a bit of ground beef, dealing moderate damage. Has a 6 second CD.

 *SPEEN: Spins wildly, tossing random ingredients everywhere within 15 yards and dealing aoe damage. Also buffing an ally in the area of effect depending on the ingredient tossed. These buffs also affect the caster Beef: Increases attack power by 5% Lettuce: Gives a small percentage of mana/energy/rage. Hot sauce: Increases attack and casting speed by 5%. Cheese: Heals for 5% HP.  Has a 1 min 30 second CD.

 *Tacoblivion: Spends all your spice and deals a base amount of damage, plus extra damage that scales off of how much spice you spend.

Shell Stab: Breaks a piece of your shell off on an enemy, making your next auto attack deal slightly more damage. Generating 5 spice.

Talk-o: Speaks...? Intimidating enemies and confusing them, making them hesitant to attack you, decreasing all damage they deal to you by 5% for 10 seconds. 2 minute CD.

These are just some basic ideas, and probably wouldn't come together well as an actual toolkit. But I figured I'd share my completely random and stupid idea
