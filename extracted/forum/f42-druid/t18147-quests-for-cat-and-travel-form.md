## Post 148388 by Mactnite — 2025-08-26T12:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148388#p148388 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the things I loved about druid was the different quests to get their different forms.
I like that part of old MMORPGs, the part where you have to take a break from leveling and questing and grouping and just go do an important quest line by yourself.
It gave you a feeling of having to prioritize your character over gaming the leveling system and it made the experience feel more "lived" and I regret that it is almost completely gone from MMORPGs (and this is also why I haven't touched an MMORPG until I discovered Turtle WoW very recently).
It's nice to see their attention to the lore and their attention to the experience, as much as the game.

So, I would vote YES to add a cat form quest for druids, and I'd just allow anyone who already has the form to do any new quest for fun, even if they don't need to.

________________
As for travel, I've always felt cheetah form was a cheap reskin and I felt an avian form - owl (night elves) and eagle (tauren) - (no z axis movement, just head height flying along the ground) would've been an awesome addition. Medivh shifts into Raven form throughout Warcraft 3, and so while he was a mage, it makes sense in lore to allow such forms. If they implemented such a reskin, I'd also be in favor of a quest for that as well.

________
And finally, there are a lot of potential animal form skin variants that should be in the donation shop.
This is a huge miss for the team.
