<https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/Roid-Macros>

Then do:

/cast [nomybuff:Bear_Form] Bear Form
/cast [nomybuff:Dire_Bear_Form] Dire Bear Form
/cast [nomybuff:Cat_Form] Cat Form
