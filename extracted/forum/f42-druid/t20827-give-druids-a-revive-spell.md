# Give Druids a Revive Spell

## Post 144701 by Captain4k (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-12T03:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144701#p144701 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Druids deserve a base resurrection spell. I understand that they did not have a revive ability in vanilla. But this is vanilla+ and the whole point is to correct all the mistakes and design flaws of the original rushed, unfinished game that is classic vanilla.

All tank classes should have access to a taunt ability and all healer classes should have access to an out of combat resurrection ability--period. There's no debate to be had. These spells are integral to the identity of their respective roles. Tanks taunt, healers heal and revive fallen comrades.

I'm on the opposite side of this request. Why?
 - Players often call for more immersion with class-specific experiences, and i agree wholeheartedly.

In raids (and likely dungeons too), we usually have 1 or more paladins or Priests in the group. Why not use their ability to resurrect fallen players out of combat? Combat resurrection is where druids truly shine.

Based on years of experience as a healer, I know some might disagree, but:
 - Druid healers are the MVPs for emergency healing, thanks to instant casts like Swiftmend (15s cooldown), and the instant cast HoT's.
 - They have major mobility compared to other healers.
 - Vanilla dungeons and raids feel slow because many players make them so with a classic mindset.

My 2 cent,:
 -If you want to boost druid healers impact, add Wild Growth, as how it was done in retail’s SoD. Or more in the style of Turtle WoW: remove the need for last-hitting to trigger Sylvan’s Blessing talent.

 -To make druids more desirable in groups, shorten the Combat Resurrection cooldown or make it cost less mana to ress with Rebirth.
 --Or, like in retail, increase their damage in healer spec. More damage means faster kills and less healing required, which might be too much of a competitive meta mindset for chilling in Turtle, but nevertheless..

But most of all:
 -Please avoid making all classes in the same like retail has done. I’ve played there for many years and watched my class(es) change into a generic framework with different colors.

I understand the argument that “all healers should be able to rez,” but that mixes two completely different concepts:
 - Abilities essential for a class to perform its *core role in combat* (like taunt for tanks)
 - Out-of-combat convenience tools that are not required for the role to function during the fight

A druid’s battle rez is actually far more impactful than a normal resurrection — it can turn a wipe into a kill mid-fight, which is far more valuable than simply skipping a corpse run afterwards.

In retail, Blizzard added mass resurrection because “it’s out of combat, so why not make it easy for everyone?” The result was that corpse runs became trivial and death lost part of its intended penalty.

Death in WoW, especially in Vanilla+, is meant to have consequences.
Corpse running forces players to slow down, regroup, and recover — it’s a moment where mistakes are acknowledged, and the entire group shares responsibility for keeping each other alive. If someone dies far from the graveyard, it should feel like a setback, not just a minor pause while someone presses a button.

Adding a standard rez to druids would not fix a broken mechanic — it would only make certain dungeon runs slightly faster when no other rezzer is present. The game is designed so that not every possible party combination can ignore that downtime, and that’s part of the intended challenge.

You seem to be missing the key trade-off here.
Druids have *battle rez* at the cost of not having a baseline out-of-combat rez. That’s the balance. Battle rez is fight-saving and available to all specs — not just a convenience spell after combat.

To address your points:

 **“Priests and druids too alike”** – My comparison wasn’t about making them identical, but about the fact that priest’s *Proclaim Champion* is a far more limited version of battle rez. Druids can use theirs on anyone, with a much shorter cooldown in practical terms, and without talent-tree restrictions.

 **Cooldown length** – Yes, in absolute numbers it’s not “short,” but considering its power — bringing someone back mid-fight — it’s an extremely strong tool. Reducing that cooldown for Restoration could be a discussion, but replacing it with a normal rez removes part of the druid’s class kit identity.

 **Baseline for all healers** – Having a healer tree doesn’t mean all baseline utility should be the same. Each healer brings something different to the table:
• Paladins can DI someone at the cost of their own life, and have very fast single-target heals with excellent mana efficiency.
• Shamans have Ankh to self-rez and return to the fight, plus very strong area spells and mana-regenerating totems.
• Priests have Proclaim Champion in their talent tree — a weaker, more limited form of battle rez — but possess a very strong and diversified healing kit.
• Druids have battle rez for anyone, but no baseline out-of-combat rez, Tranquility healing that can save a raid, very strong HoTs, and solid single-target healing.

This trade-off is intentional.

Also, on this server, druids are already in a very strong spot in terms of DPS, HPS, and mitigation/threat. They don’t need an additional baseline rez to perform their role effectively.

Giving Restoration a baseline rez at the end of their tree could be a reasonable middle ground — but adding it to all druids just for convenience would be a solution in search of a problem.
