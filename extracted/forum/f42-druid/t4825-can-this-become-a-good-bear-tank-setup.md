Feral Aggression and Blood Frenzy are not skilled for a bear spec since they only benefit the cat playstyle

Improving the attackpower debuff of Demoralizing roar sounds nice, but most mobs dont even have that much ap you could reduice. And on the once you can its only about a 4dps damage reduction.

Extra damage for Ferocious bite sounds really good on the other hand until you realize that you dont have skilled improved shread which is massive for your combopoint regeneraton.

Since you skilled bloodfrenzy i assume you want to also play cat effectively with your spec.

i would take these 5 points from feral aggression, put 3 into feral instincts, as it helps immensely with aggro and one into feral charge, as its massively useful to quickly get into tauntrange and kick caster. Also its really really fun to click!

The last point i would put in improved shred.

Additionally i would take one point from thick fur to max out improved shred. Extra armor is nice but 3% arent really needed and shred is important for cat (I tanked with my bear in BWL in season of mastery without thick fur and it was fine ;))

That leaves you with: <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid/1O3I6-UIC8FEDV-53>

The cookiecutter druid tank build most players use is: <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid/1O3I6-UIK8FEDV-5>

I personally think going for natures focus is the weaker of the two options, but you pick what fits your playstile. You can heal with eiter spec in dungeons.
Heart of the wild massively boosts your manapool. Natures focus shouldnt be necessary if the rest of the group is mildly competent.
