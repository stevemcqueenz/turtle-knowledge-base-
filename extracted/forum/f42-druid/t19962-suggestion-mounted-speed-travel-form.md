# Suggestion: Mounted-Speed Travel Form

## Post 136445 by Quetzy — 2025-06-20T19:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136445#p136445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really appreciate the addition of the improved Travel Form that counts as a mount — it’s a great step toward making the druid feel more unique and connected to nature.

That said, I’d like to propose an idea that could take this even further while staying true to the druid’s fantasy and class identity.

What if, once the player learns the level 40 riding skill, the druid could unlock an upgraded version of Travel Form through a class-specific quest? This form would:

Only be usable out of combat

Require a short cast time (just like a mount)

Move at standard mount speed

Keep the flavor and immersion of shapeshifting instead of summoning a mount

This would allow druids to remain in form — enhancing the fantasy of being a creature of nature — while not breaking the game balance, since it would functionally act like a mount with the same limitations.

There are many ways this could be implemented:

A druid-only quest involving natural spirits or wild zones

A learned skill only usable in Travel Form at level 40 if you’ve learned riding

A talent path exclusive to the class

I think it’s a great opportunity to reinforce the uniqueness of the druid, and give players a more immersive alternative to traditional mounts — without giving them any unfair mechanical advantage.

Would love to hear what others think!
