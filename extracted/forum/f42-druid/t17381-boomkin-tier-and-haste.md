# Boomkin Tier and Haste

## Post 118694 by Noobikscube — 2025-02-02T08:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118694#p118694 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In CC2 Boomkin received some changes that breathed a lot of life into the CC1 1 button spec. These changes have been well received by the players and have made the class more interesting, more fun, and have a higher skill ceiling. The most impactful talent added being Eclipse.

Boomkin flavor has always been about crit. They provide a large crit aura to fellow casters, they have strong crit modifiers, whenever they crit they get .5 seconds off their next cast and then in CC2 Boomkin got Eclipses proccing upon Wrath and Starfire crits. Making crit an even more valuable and necessary stat. Literally living and dying by the crit.

When we look at this new play style that lives and breathes if they are critting or not, one would hope that these new alt tier sets would help support this new iteration of boomkin.

When looking at tier 3 and 3.5 balance sets they do not appear to be on the same page as the class direction itself. We have haste stacked on multiple items. Is haste useless? No, but apart from spell power the Boomkin stat prio is Crit>Hit>Haste.

Haste was also added to other alt tier sets, like arcane mage, and affliction lock, but their new kits actually scale with haste and is a sought after stat. Please consider removing the haste and making room in the stat budget for more crit.

## Post 118750 by kierova — 2025-02-02T18:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118750#p118750 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the things that has been communicated to me in the past via the devs (and by example with the new and recent changes to our boon system in Balance of All Things), is that the developers seem to think that haste will compensate for our ultimate and utter lack of crit.

While haste will indeed, mathmatically speaking, allow us to caste a greater number of abilities, there is no failsafe nor bad luck protection when it comes to the all-too-reliant crit that we need in order to do comparable damage to other casters. For example, just last week on Grobbulus, my first Wrath crit occured at approximately 50% of the boss's HP. I did not receive another meaningful crit until the boss was at 4% HP, while Arcane Eclipse was well off cooldown, and then the boss was dead. I was able to use one 15 second window of my Eclipse buff on a fight that lasted over a minute. This feels absolutely awful, and no amount of haste will prevent this from continuing to happen time and time again.

Yes, haste is not a meaningless stat, but it is by far the least valuable stat for the current Balance kit, and it is both disappointing and concerning that the developers continue to not only ignore, but argue against and laugh about the current state of both our current tier gearing options, but future tier sets as well.

Give us the crit and hit we are constantly starved of. Give us a form of bad luck protection with a cooldown that can force proc an eclipse (why do shamans have Elementual Mastery, but we don't have something similar?). And stop pretending that the "hybrid tax" is real, and treat Balance like the proper caster our new cc2 kit wants us to be.
