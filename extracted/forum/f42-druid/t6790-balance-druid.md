## Post 42946 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-04-25T03:20:00Z

In raids a feral can very viably tank almost any raid boss and is even ideal in some circumstances. Most raiding guilds on the server end up using at least one bear tank through their progression and farming.

Cat dps is hard to pull off but some of the best raid dps on the server was actually done by a cat. You need to fully utilize the custom content put in place, but I've seen two cats now get top 5 overall in naxx.

Resto is a little better than it was in vanilla since you can get swiftmend and still go moonglow spec. They're still perfectly viable and I've really only seen one raiding guild show preference against resto druids for other classes when it came to gear. Ultimately your ability to play your class should outpace most any advantages other healers might have if you're better than them.

Balance is far far better here than retail, but it still isn't up to par. It's a novelty still but at least with the changes if you're not slacking on consumes and buffs you can show up on the meters. Balance mana issues are greatly reduced at max level raiding now thanks to the free casts off Omen and reduced costs from Moonkin Form, but I still used a Darkmoon Card: Blue Dragon and a Flask of Distilled Wisdon to help carry me through many raid fights until I got enough mana from gear.

TL;DR Boomkin isn't good but it's not terrible, every other spec is better and bear or cat is completely viable
