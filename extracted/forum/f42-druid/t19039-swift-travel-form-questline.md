Swift Travel Form

 1. 'Mount Hyjal In Turmoil' Speak with Loganaar in Moonglade and travel to Nordanaar.
 2. 'Shadowed Spectre' Archdruid Dreamwind wants you to investigate the abnormality ****west of Darkhollow Pass**** in Hyjal.
 3. 'The Son Of Cenarius' Bring 20 Brigth Dream Shards to Keeper Remulos in western Moonglade.
'4. Tethered Memories' Gather the Bough of Cenarius from Dreamstalker in Bough Shadow, Ashenvale; the Crystalline Ray of Moonlight from Venethas in a cave north of Everlook, Winterspring; and the Jade Scale of the Dreamer from Solnius in the Emerald Sanctum raid for Keeper Remulos.
 5. 'The White Stag' Return to the Spectre west of Darkhollow Pass and defeat it, return to Keeper Remulos afterwards.
 6. 'Woven Dreams' Gather 10 Viridian Mushrooms from Jademir Lake in Feralas, as well as other materials for Keeper Remulos.
 7. 'The Eternal Sleeper' Travel to Seradane in the Hinterlands and convene with the spirit of Vithekus.
 8. 'Under the Vibrant Moonlight' Gather friends in the Twilight Grove of Duskwood and receive the blessing as your reward.
