 1) Make RIP ability have a Slow effect (based on Combo points or same regardless of CP)

Can be done as a X pieces bonus of some Druid Set (PvE or PvP or LW)

 2) Make a CUSTOM DPS set BoP (bind on craft) from LW!!

Make LW feel special by adding recipes that are BoP !! So that it is appealing to play and lvl something else then ENG on Turtle WoW! :)
