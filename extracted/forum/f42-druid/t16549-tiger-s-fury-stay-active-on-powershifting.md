## Post 117231 by Eyeburn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-19T08:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117231#p117231 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Powershifting has its own charm, but it ultimately holds back feral dps by making it extremely reductive, just spam shred.  It's not a good basis for rebalancing feral dps on this server because it is based on an oversight.  Turning wolfshead into an enchant is an easy solution, but a long term solution should seek to eliminate the playstyle as the only viable one.  What this server has is effectively TBC's cat rotation, and it's very dry.

Wrath cat was terrible, but there is a way to give cat a rotation that isn't endless bleed and buff management.  Powershifting disregards any intended rotation to spam Shred.  If Tiger's Fury and Rake are worth the energy and combo point costs, you can build a real rotation for cat.  Letting feral benefit from 'on hit' effects would also be easier to balance.  Vanilla cat damage can very easily sky rocket with small buffs due in large part to powershifting exaggerating their effects.
