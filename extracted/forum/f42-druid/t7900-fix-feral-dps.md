I've never done powershifting and I have no interest in it. It feels stupid and gimmicky to me. But cat druid is pretty different from rogue. Cat has virtually no stuns, more bleed, ability crits give double combo points, Ferocious Bite dumps your energy bar leading to completely different rotations, no Slice & Dice finisher you have to keep up, cat is mechanically like a 2h weapon not like dual wield so you have to itemize completely differently, Strength is almost as powerful as Agility for cats leading to even more item divergence. It shares the same big picture ideas (automatically regenerating "mana" bar, combo points), but they actually did a remarkably good job giving it its own identity - something you only appreciate after you play both feral cat and rogue for a while. Because admittedly - from the outside it looks darn similar.
