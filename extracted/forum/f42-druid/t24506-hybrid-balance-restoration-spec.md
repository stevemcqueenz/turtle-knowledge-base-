So for dungeons being the difficulty they are at, it is viable but honestly its 10x better to specialized, the only balance resto spec really is the 30/0/21 for pvp (which is the best balance spec for pvp). Most healers will be perfectly fine by themselves, you already can emergency heal as regular balance, and you will be wasting a dps slot by not going eclipse moonkin etc. Dot tick crits also don't proc Nature's swiftness.

But if you do wanna try this.
 1. Take Swiftmend - 1 pt, its very strong and will let you get an emergency heal off very quickly.
 2. Take Nature's Swiftness - 1pt, again very strong and will let you get an emergency max rank HT off.
 3. Your build is missing moonfury which is big for dot damage.
 4. Subtlety 100% not needed.

You can go just the regular Healing touch build and throw dot's in while queing as healer. (This is a viable non-tree healing spec - that uses downrank HT spam).  Trade out wrath points for NT if u wanna solo and stuff.
