I think Lightning Shield, if it were reworked, should keep the charges. In Warcraft 3, the spell made a unit do AoE damage to enemy units around it and could be cast on friendly or enemy units (i.e., you could lightning shield a soldier in the middle of your enemy's army and it melts all his units). I think Thunderhead could add that functionality-- give the spell a pulsing AoE that does damage constantly, and then gives a bigger chunk of damage when the target is attacked, and then it could be cast on an ally standing in the middle of a pack of enemies for AoE damage.

## Post 53918 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-07-18T12:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53918#p53918 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Thunderhead is conceptually a great talent, it allows you to deal a bunch of damage while generating threat for someone else. Shaman despite not being a great DPS has threat problems, so it fits the bill of allowing him to do more damage without ripping threat and buffs your tanks abilities in a group.

That being said, lightning shield is prohibitively expensive to cast. Its high spellscaling means you can get some use out of spaming lower ranks on a paladin tank, but with a Warrior or Druid tank who doesn't typically have spelldamage statted, it is just horrible.

Lightning shield is a conditional spell. The person with lightning shield needs to be hit for it to proc, and only one proc can occur every 2 seconds. It can't crit either. So i would strongly suggest heavily reducing the manacost of the spell either baseline or with the improved lightning shield talent (which currently is horrible as is)

Another option to consider would be letting Elemental focus apply on LS. Its an elemental spell, but for some reason it is not considered offensive and therefore doesn't get the cost reduction. Damage comparison between earthshock and LS truely make LS look horrible when considering Elemental Focus & Conviction lowering earthshocks manacost to 225 mana for 517 - 546 dmg (2.36 dmg per mana) while the conditional shield with no additional effects and 4 talent points exclusively buffing itself dealing 683 dmg for 296 mana (2.3 dmg per mana).
