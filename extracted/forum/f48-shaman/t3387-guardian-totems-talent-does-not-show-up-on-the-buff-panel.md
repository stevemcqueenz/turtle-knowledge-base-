# Guardian Totems talent does not show up on the buff panel

## Post 20605 by Darkgestalt — 2022-07-28T18:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20605#p20605 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please, provide a clear description what the bug is:
The Guardian Totems talent increased damage reduction for Stoneskin/Windwall does not show up on the buff panel for your character. It shows the unmodified amount.

Steps to reproduce the behavior with as much detail as possible:
 1 Put 2 talent points in Guardian Totems.
 2 Confirm that the tooltip for the Stoneskin/Windwall ability has increased the damage reduction by 20%.
 3 Place a Stoneskin/Windwall Totem.
 4 Mouseover the Stoneskin/Windwall buff on the buff panel, it shows the unmodified amount.

Expected behavior. Describe how it should work:
The buffpanel should show the correct values of the respective buffs. It is very difficult to assess whether or not the talent does anything in general because of the fluctuating damage numbers.

Additional notes:
The Cooldown reduction of the Grounding Totem is working properly, so again it is difficult to notice if the talent is broken or simply the buffpanel.
