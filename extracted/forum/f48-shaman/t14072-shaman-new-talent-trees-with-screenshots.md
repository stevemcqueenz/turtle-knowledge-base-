Objectives :

Elemental :
 - Buffing PVE damage without impacting PvP
 - Helping sustain with elemental focus stacking

Enhancement :
 - More threat and damage totems taking a huge tanking buff from rockbiter weapon
 - Better sustain with Elemental Shock and new stormstrike giving mana
 - More scaling with Critical Synergy from physical crit
 - Bloodlust now fits the gameplay of the spec avoiding the BL-resto abomination

Restoration :
 - 31 talent is now a choice between mana and health, and will be a good button to use in PvP too
