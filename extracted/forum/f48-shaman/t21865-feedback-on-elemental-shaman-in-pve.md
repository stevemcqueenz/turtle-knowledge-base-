# Feedback on elemental shaman in pve

## Post 151552 by pagu — 2025-09-13T11:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151552#p151552 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Want to share my thoughts and suggestions about current state of elemental shaman in pve
My experience is raiding as enhancement shaman up to nax and ele shaman (nature spec) up to aq40. Didnt try fire ele but there were shamans that spec in my raid so i made some conclusions

So, ele shaman has 2 ways to play now:
Nature spec classic ele - has low single target dps and good aoe/cleave. has 4 actively used situational spells, shocks, totems. pretty complicated rotation. need to track clearcasting procs not to be oom instantly. and even with perfect clearcasting management ooms fast. need to track fire shock duration to effectively cast molten blast. need weapon oil.
Fire spec ele (with elemental weapons) - has good 1 target dps. (escepically if can afford t3) and average aoe. has 1 spell, shocks, totems. dont need to track fire shock duration. dont need to track clearcasting. no rotation, just spam 1 button. hard to oom because no expensive mana cost spells. dont need oil. disadvantage - alot of fire immune bosses in mc/bwl+ony, so it cant be used as the only spec to play in raids. also may have threat issue but idk

For me nature ele feels clunky. Complicated rotation that easily breaks, is procs and cd based, sensitive for resists. Most of rotation is long casts. Mana. Pushbacks.
 1) mana.
enhancement and resto shaman have mechanics to better utilise water shield. why ele has only clearcasting?
clearcasting needs crits to work. if you give it even -100% cost to spells, its very unreliable. i have with very good gear (33%+ lightning bolt crit chance) and sometimes i have very long streaks with no crits at all. in a very bad situation i had almost 0 crits on onyxia and was oom in the middle of 2nd phase, eating big mana potion. do we have any another classes with such mana troubles?
and what makes situation even worse - 99% of time ele shaman is in party with melees or tanks to give wf. so no moonkin aura and shadow priest mana regen.
leveling as ele shaman means drink half of you life
 2) eye of the storm.
the worst antipushback for pve and even so it is nerfed in 1.18. that is brain cancer...
there are alot of packs and bosses where all raid gets damage. and shaman casts constantly pushed back. as ele shaman rotation is mostly of long casts that is so annoying, its just may halve dps.
 3) improved molten blast.
bad desing. i understand devs want to make molten blast good as spammable ability for nature immune targets and at the same time good for rare use on other targets.
but if you want to use it effectively, you need to constantly track fire shock duration. even with addons there is no good istrument to do that. and if your molten blast resisted, rotation is broken - you need to watch it and recast fire shock.
my suggestion for improved molten blast: molten blast has +100% crit chance but when it crits, you get debuff for 10/6 seconds that disables this bonus. if you already has this debuff and get another molten blast crit, dont refresh debuff duration.
that will make rotation more stable and predictable, dont break by resists, debuff is easy to track, helps to get more clearcasting especially for low geared shamans.
for those who cry about 100% crit chance is op - molten blast base damage is pretty low
 4) earthquake.
bad desing. it helps to get more dps in aoe situations but has alot of problems.
devs want it to be good for 1 targen and aoe, but for 1 target it is worse than ligntning bolt. it is 2.5 sec cast and has no crit bonus from talents. the only advantage it does 3 hits so more chances to get clearcasting. but if 1st hit is resisted, there are no 2nd and 3rd hits.
for aoe its useful on fat packs. but there are alot of aoe encounters with fast dying mobs. 2.5 cast is already too much, half of pack is dead when cast is finished. but if your target dies before cast finished, its canceled, so you just stay and do nothing. what a joke... even fire totems are more comfortable to use.
my opinion it needs to be revorked. if you want it to be castable aoe, make it area cast like dynamite. if you want in to be 1 target, make it instant.
but honestly i would change it to some passive effect. may be proc on chain lightning.
or give us elemental overload instead of this, for example. i know arcane mages have it now but so what?
or something like if you cast doesnt crit you get +10% spell crit. stacks until you get spell crit. ele shaman is extremely crit dependent spec. no crits - dps suck and you are oom
or may be shamanistic rage cd with strong mana regen and defensive buffs
 5) elemental mastery.
look at elemental mastery, then at bloodlust, then back at elemental mastery and laugh. need to be improved, duration extended, may be add spell crit or haste and buff party when shaman land a crit.
 6) searing totem.
please make it not attack controlled targets or targets out of combat
with extended range so much risk it pull extra pack, so i use it very rare, only on bosses or in safe situalions
 7) weapon enchants.
not sure may be i want too much... but it will be great to get spell power/healing shaman weapon imbue
 8) need some buff for caster party.
shaman is utility class but all it have for caster party is ridiculous mana spring totem or tremor in some cases
make wf totem to have elemental overload effect for party? for direct damage spells and/or heals. hm... looks very interesting but may be too strong. wf totem is very strong for melee party so why not to give equal for caster party
or make air totem that buffs spell hit/crit

All above is just my thoughts, i dont expect all of that will be implemented but may be devs notice that and make some changes for smoother ele playstyle

I think that the WF part cannot be helped.
Strictly speaking, WF totem was already nerfed but further nerfing it or locking it behind only certain builds would affect all melees in the game.
The problem with WF is that:
 1. it is so overpowered it determines how shamans are placed
 2. it doesn't benefit shamans (weapon imbues have prio, PVE casters and healers won't melee)
 3. it affects half of the playerbase so any nerf would mean an uproar from rogues, hunters, palas, warriors etc

In my opinion we have to accept that shaman at Vanilla is by design a melee support class which on top of that base can perform all four roles to a degree.

On paper you could put eles to a caster group, tranquil air totem has some form of stacking with salvation and you have water totems, too. But those benefits are not comparable to WF+ strength + FT.
The only positive thing I can state is that if a raid has too many shamans, eles usually have a priority to be put in a caster group. It is rare though.

Regarding the other points.
I think fire ele is an abomination. Molten blast should be a backup spell for high nat rez enemies, not an entire playstyle. Just something to press when mob says nope lol to lightning bolts.

For mana, improved water shield talent helps for passive regen but regen is weak.
Resto has a good regen with access to both improved shields and improved water shield but enh and ele struggles there.
There should be a way for those two to be more consistent at mana.
My suggestion would be making mana spring totem mana regen equal to max rank blessing of wisdom and make the totem cheaper since it has to be spammed (which is not only expensive kinda but it keeps interrupting full spirit mana regen).
Maybe just add a new AQ20 book for it.

Regarding weapon enchants, I am against it.
Weapon imbues are a speciality of enhancement shamans and all have a melee effect at Vanilla.
I think turning them to a generic, build-dependent buff would be against this identity.

Searing Totem: I agree that it should by default target the same mob as you do. Asking for a talent point for that is stupid.

 8) need some buff for caster party.
I am against this because of what had already been said.
Shaman by default is a melee support class. Weakening WF would cause an uproar. Giving shaman an equally potent caster buff would be too OP.

 5) elemental mastery.
No real opinion but please note that mastery, unlike bloodlust is not a capstone talent.

Earthquake.
I have a unique problem with that. In my opinion it should be an ice-based spell.
Hear me out.
Shaman weapon imbues can deal fire or frost damage.
Shaman shocks can deal nature, fire or frost damage.
Shaman casted abilities can give fire or nature damage (2 kinds).
Shaman totems each give fire damage.
The most underrepresented spell type is frost. If I wanna imagine elemental shaman as a master of elements, I want it to have certain amount of symmetry between those elements.
Adding another nature damage into the mix just makes the scales of damage types more imbalanced.
Let earthquake be avalanche or hail.

 2) eye of the storm.
Here I wanna defend the devs. Ele can use earth shield for 30% negation. Sure, some other casters have more but not each of them.
This 30% ensures that resto can go 100% for healing via talents.
But adding more to ele would badly hurt PVP in my opinion, since ele is a caster (at PVP) with a shield, a shitload of armor and slows. Allowing this bulky caster to self heal without interruption (for example) would be too much.
