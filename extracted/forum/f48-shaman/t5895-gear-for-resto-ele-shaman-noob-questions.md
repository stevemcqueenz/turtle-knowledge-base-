It's not straight forward in the sense that they're limited items that give you spell damage and healing until later in the game, but that's fine.

You will be able to find Leather and then Mail with Intellect, Stamina, and perhaps some Spirit on it and that should suffice. You will be able to heal any dungeon below level 50 quite comfortably in any spec as long as you have int gear and are willing to heal, and your group isn't misplaying

Not sure if you're referring to floating combat numbers or the numbers on the unit frames. The latter definitely exists.
