# Mastering the Elements quest bug

## Post 31524 by Darkgestalt — 2022-12-25T10:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31524#p31524 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The questline to Da Voodoo quest, the sunken temple classquest, has 2 paths you can follow. You can either get Elemental Mastery from Beram Skychaser or Sagorne Creststrider OR you can directly go to Bath'rah the Windwatcher who gives Mastering the Elements.

Both Elemental Mastery (8410) and Mastering the Elements (8411) require you to hand in elemental air, fire, wind and water. Afterwards, you get the Spirit Totem quest (8412) and merrily continue on.

However, Mastering the Elements does NOT make you eligible for Spirit Totem. You can complete the quest, Bathy-boy takes your 4 elemental items, you get some XP (citation needed) and he even shows you the quest description for Spirit Totem. But if you press Accept, you hear a error sound and a message that 'you are not eligible for that quest'.

Has anyone else ran into this problem? For now I recommend all shamans to strictly go to Beram or Sagorne to get Elemental Mastery instead and avoid going to Bath'rah directly!
