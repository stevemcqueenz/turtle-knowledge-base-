Crackling thunder used to be really good, now its numbers are pathetic. 10%chance on lb or 15 % chance on ls

Results in roughly 3.2% haste for LB and
 1.6% haste on LS
So for ele, due to some weird math with how haste scales with lb, if you deal ~625 dps or more with straight lb casting, crackling thunder starts to overtake storm. But beware this only works to its fullest effect if you have 9% or less combined haste on the rest of your gear and buffs
And thats the break even point dps wise, you still pay extra mana for haste based dmg increases

As for enhancer, full t2 instant lb is a meme playstile, if you wanna do it go for it, but then your primary goal aint dps anyway.

Without doing the exact math since it geta really complicated thanks to ap scaling on shocks, wf and base haste from flurry, the example from ele suggests stonebreaker is probably the better choice
