# Let's talk about Stormstrike

## Post 78815 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-11-26T17:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78815#p78815 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ahh, Stormstrike, the Enhancement Shaman's only attack. And it turns out, it's totally not worth it. Here are the problems with it:

First off, the mana cost. At Rank 1, it costs 21% of base mana, which is extremely expensive for what it is. I've been so excited to pick it up, and since picking it up I've used it around 5 times, because it's so damn expensive. Maybe it's going to get better once I get Rank 2 (12% base mana). But something has to be done about its mana cost, it's not worth it to use it, since for the same mana you can cast a lightning bolt or shock and do way more damage (especially for 1-hander users like me).

Second, which is actually the bigger pain point for me, is its secondary effect:
"The next 2 sources of Nature damage dealt to the target are increased by 20%."
And they really mean ANY sources of nature damage. If you or ANYONE else in your party has any item that has "Adds 3 nature damage to attacks" (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=16615> , I have 2 of those by the way), or god forbid the tank is buffed with thorns, kiss those 2 debuff stacks goodbye before your GCD is done, for a total increase of 1 damage (if that, lol). Actually with 2 of the aforementioned items, the debuff doesn't even show up, because the attack caused by stormstrike consumes them. This makes Stormstrike really trash tier if you're relying on these effects to proc Clearcasting.

TLDR:
So here are my suggestions to fix the skill:
 1. Reduce the mana cost, especially for Rank 1. I propose 15% base mana for Rank 1 and 10% base mana for Rank 2.
 2. Make it so the nature damage debuff can ONLY be consumed by the shaman's own spells (Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, Earth Shock or Lightning Shield).

The shaman consortium and the elements thank you for your time and consideration.   smiling_turtle
