## Post 131896 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-05-19T10:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131896#p131896 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Stable shields is an awful talent for pvp.
You increase the the charges of lightning shield from 3 to 9 but you slow the time you can expend charges substantially. While with regular lightning shield, you get 100% of your spellpower against your target in 10.5 seconds at best, with stable shields that time is extended to 13.5seconds and only after 18 seconds in combat do you gain any value off of the talent.

How often do you have a pvp fight that lasts more than 18 seconds without you having an oportunity to recast shield?

Stable shield is a nerf to your shield and you should only ever skill it if you grab it for lightning strike.

And lightning strike want you to wear slow weapons. Hand of edward has a chance to give you a decent lightning bolt afterwards, while any "Good" weapon just ensures the lightningstrike itself at least hits hard.

It is quite bad im afraid.
The cookie cutters specs are B-Tier and Battle Caster is C-Tier

But I will admit it is amusing to try it out when you get bored of the meta/cookie cutter specs.

HI, besides the other ppl saying it sucks i will tell u a different story. IMO its the strongest build for ele. For better understanding, iam waring full t2.5 got huge stam ofpices from quests like ring and neck witch get me up to 5.4k HP. against casters i swap to reflect shield from k10.

U are beefe as hell with alot of armor from shield and talents. Your Lightning strike hits really good with lightning shield up to 1k all sources of dmg combined + the dmg enemy takes from hitting me (~350ish).

For talents specific i go with:
elemental mastery for those big crits.
short CD on shocks 6s->5s
NO totem talents like nova etc
Lightning strike ofc
all the armor i can get
Ghost wolf

For the play style, u want to stay back as far as u can and cast as much ass possible like normal ele dose. if u face any class that can silence or kick u u get in shock range and only rely on ur shocks and LB R1
Lightning Bolt R1 is ur favorite spell u press it every time to keep ur electrify stacks up (if u know warrior has no more kick or silence is gone u can also chain lightning).
When a melee gets at u before u got any casts of, stay with earth shield and cast 2 LB r1 swap to Lightning shield can shock lightning strike till u get away or (more likely) target dies.
The healing u benefit from earthshield isnt bat ether and if u are really oom u get more out of water shield.
improved elemental shields are really nice for push back reduction as well 3 charges are gone asap

The improved ghost wolf can be really strong as well when chasing some one or needing to go somewhere fast u can swap to blood guard boots and u are almost at 60% mount speed.

Downsides of this build is the short totem range, u have to be carefull to not outrange totems, same goes for team support.
u are still ele so u prio SP and ur heals are still super strong but no more nature swiftness instant heals. u also miss 5% crit.

upsides are high armor (u beat most warriors rouges hunters and palas)
more stam witch is always good
more utility fast ghost wolf
melee attack that ignores silence and most of its dmg armor.

If u want to heal most of the bg stay back and be support play ele/restro.
if u fing ur self alone on the BG or just want to play more offensive go ele/enhance

i hope this helps u :)
