# A small tweak to the Stormstrike buff

## Post 106160 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-11-03T15:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106160#p106160 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, I love how Stormstrike was changed in 1.17.2, so it procs only from your own sources of damage.
However, it can still get consumed by effects like "+3 nature damage on melee hit" from gear, which makes the spell useless if you have even one of these effects on your gear. Is it possible to exlude this effect from consuming Stormstrike's buff stacks?
Maybe limit it to the shaman's own spells - Earth shock, lightning bolt, chain lightning, lightning shield?
