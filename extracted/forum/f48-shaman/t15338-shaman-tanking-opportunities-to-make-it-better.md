# Shaman Tanking - Opportunities to make it better

## Post 104705 by Azuredrak0 — 2024-10-24T17:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104705#p104705 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Quick shout-out to the devs.  While there is a lot of dooming going around, I personally enjoy the proposed Enh changes, and there is a lot to be said about making the life of Enh DPS and possibility of Shaman Tanking (Warden) much more realistic.

I used to be a Shaman tank, but I achieved my goals for the class and moved on to another class having been satisfied with how far I could push it.  Interestingly my issue with continuing was not so much the durability of the tank, it wasn't even the notable lack of threat, it was the constant requirement of the group that I purposely nerf my tanking capabilities to boost the DPS of the group - i.e. I needed to always drop Windfury totem (and twist it if possible).  Windfury totem does not benefit the shaman in the slightest, it simply benefits the group.

So, here we go, a suggestion - is there a way to provide synergies that benefit the shaman when a totem that does not benefit the shaman is dropped?

Here's an example - when one of the team members triggers Windfury, the shaman gains either 3% block and/or 10 SP (stacks up to 5) for 5 seconds.  The benefit can be baked into Totemic Alignment talent and depend on whether the shaman is wearing a shield or not.  Something different that's along these lines can be done for Flametongue.

This can serve as an additional incentive for consistency of the totem placement.

Alternatively, Windfury Totem can simply stack with Windfury Weapon and both can be tuned to work together.
