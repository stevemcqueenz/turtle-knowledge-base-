Unless you're in a long fight over an AV graveyard or AB point there's basically no way you're ever going to get that buff to 5 stacks in PvP early enough for it to matter. PvP fights just aren't that long most of the time. Elemental Mastery giving 2 stacks feels like a strange choice to me given that forcing a crit with it is the time when having that damage bonus matters the most.

I wish they'd return the old elemental fury talent, and replace the crit damage buff in electrify with something like reducing spell resistance or allowing mana regen while casting. Basically give you a choice between "do you want to do burst damage" vs "do you want to be able to keep up sustained damage during a long boss fight".
