## Post 24223 by Fishi — 2022-09-02T09:50:00Z

Hello fellow Shaman enjoyers!
There is a lot of discussion and speculation about changes for shamans, and as someone who has been playing enhancement since forever, I'd like to advocate for my most beloved spec here.
These are just my personal opinions, and although I am not a person of math and mechanics, I still firmly believe 3 simple fixes would make shaman soar a little higher in raids and dungeons already!
 1.) Please allow shaman and paladin buffs stack.
We are playing on a crossfaction server, which I adore, but a soft mutual exclusivity barring my two favourite classes apart from each other breaks my heart, and to an extent, my gameplay. I do not believe that allowing totems and blessings to mix would make much of an impact negatively - it would further allow sustainabilty regarding mana towards classes that need it the most, and the most overu- i mean, popular dps specs are already broken to hell and back, an extra teaspoon of crack wouldn't stir up much water in their direction. If anything, it could be a change that would welcome both dps / tankadins and ele / enh into raids.
 2.) Stormstrike debuff stack removal
It would be such a lovely thing to give to boomkins, assassination rogues and eleshams, among others, a lovely flat nature dmg increase! It already takes a debuff slot, so why not make it beneficial to the entire raid? Nature damage is already wet noodle tier, this way at least it could make a sizable impact, potentially, which brings me to my next point -
 3.) Curse of the Elements to include nature damage
Quite self explanatory, and would help us a lot, especially spellhancement / ele / boomie friends.
I do not think that these changes are in any way overly excessive, they could add a lot not only to enhancement or shamans, but to raids and other classes as well, without directly empowering shamans, therefore making a bit less of an impact in PvP, in which I believe shamans already are quite fine at.
Please share your opinions, and if you agree with any of these points, make some noise!
Thank you very much for reading. Have a wonderful time, fellow turtles!

My personal suggestions are
 1.) Buff the shit out of Mana Spring Totem. It's currently garbage and due to cross faction, it should at least equalise Blessing of Wisdom like it did in WOTLK.
 2.) Make Stormstrike a stacking debuff with a duration for Arcane/Nature. 5% per stack with a 20 second duration. This buffs Arcane mages, balance druids, other shamans and dagger rogues as well as people who have nature based damage ontop of that.
 3.) Make Earth Shock only do the threat multiplier when Rockbiter is applied. It's silly otherwise because it means Stormstrike is nothing but an extra swing to Enhancement Shamans and the entire point is to buff Nature damage, which most of which comes from shamans in the first place. This isn't game breaking and allows shamans a slight improvement.
 4. Make the +15% Lightning Shield talent into a +15% any elemental Shield talent. The talent to cast it on another target can remain as LS but for a tanking shaman at 60, you will likely be using Earth/Water Shield over Lightning Shield and that means the talent is boring and avoidable. 15% healing or mana restored wouldn't break the balance since they aren't overly strong anyway.
 5.) Totems to have 30 yards baseline other than fire totems.
Also, reworking of Shaman T1 and T2 so there's no spirit and a more focused statline. T1 should be healing and T2 should be caster focused.

Tier sets definitely need to a rework.
I would also suggest making Ancestral Knowledge into something useful - instead of a +5% max mana, I'd rather have a +X% mp/5 that works during casting. That would make the talent feel less like a waste / nearly useless gateway to Flurry, and would boost leveling immensely.
Every spec would benefit from it, and I think it fits with the theme of shamans way more.
Some folks have suggested making the various weapon enhancements into 'stances', for example rockbiter for tanking, windfury for melee dps, flametongue for ele, and frost for healing. While personally I don't really agree with that direction, it can definitely have a positive impact on simplifying some of the playstyle issues shaman has, especially tanking, which several shaman players seem to be very fond of.
