How do you play your ele shaman ?

For fun/solo memeing:
Use Flame Shock more often (30 yard range)
engage via melee attacks more often (lightning strike easy to get)
Use Rank 1 LB to get Electrified easily (less than .5 sec cast time)

For actually winning Battlegrounds:
Play as a conservative supporter:
 1. Spam Spam Spam Purge.
 2. Provide key targets emergency heals (you crit quite often. these crit heals also activate Clearcasting btw)
 3. Interrupt Spell casts
 4. Provide Totems (such as Grounding)
 5. Use Chain Lightning on groups (especially weakened groups)

Note: Ele Shaman was nerfed as this was considered "Fair"
