# Rating totems

## Post 126280 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-10T10:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126280#p126280 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I wanted to create a totem tier list with some added notes next to each ranking.
Tiers are:
 * Nerf or rework is needed
 * Strong
 * Mid
 * Weak
 * Buff or rework is a must
 * Not applicable

Let's go in an ascending order!

 **Not applicable Tier:**
 * Sentry Totem - This totem has some PVP niche but for a class who already has far sight, it's a weird addition. Has no PVE use but has a weird niche for creating group selfies.

 **Buff or rework is a must Tier:**
 * Windwall Totem - Lowering ranged attack damage on the same element where WF exists? The only case where this would in theory be used is a custom instance where we have to fight off an army of archers somehow. Only limiting its effect to ranged damage makes it useless.

 * Healing Stream Totem - It is a very weird totem, arguably giving shamans a precedent for HoT usage. But for what it does, it barely ever worths it, even if it is upgraded via 5 talent points. Its scaling is way off, putting it down is usually just wasting mana.

 **Weak Tier:**

 * Nature Resistence Totem - On paper all resistence totems are equally strong but their element is an important thing to look at. You barely ever use nature resistence totem even at high nature damage places because of windfury. The only time I remember using it is in AQ40 before CC2 where the frost oil prevented WF from going off at Viscidious and at Huhuran, via totem twisting. But twisting is no more.

 * Stoneskin Totem - Not a bad idea for a totem but the design is badly executed. Even as a tank I barely ever use it. The problem with it is the lack of scaling. You are usually way better off by giving strength to the party and kill things faster.

 * Searing Totem - The effect itself would put it in the Mid tier but it is extremely unreliable unless you spend talent points to ensure it doesn't pewpew down sheeps and patrols (?). Because of this I lowered its rank by one.

 **Mid Tier:**
 * Earthbind Totem - It has a decent utility for kiting and during PVP against a melee but most opponents can destroy it from afar or can charge to it - and it has an internal CD so it isn't spammable. Also, shaman is not the best kiter in the world.

 * Tranquil Air Totem - The effect itself is strong but very limiting. It never really sees play when you play alone or go to dungeons. It is very strong in raids in a caster group but barely any shaman goes to a caster group at raids. It has its potential for late game but windfury ensures the shaman's placement in a melee group.

 * Flametongue Totem - The current iteration is weird and personally I don't like it, but I have to admit, it is a straight buff to the melee 'for free' and the shaman is usually in the melee team.

 * Frost Resistence Totem - Better then the nature one because you don't have to sacrifice WF to use it. But its use is kinda limited, would benefit from more frost magic-using enemies.

 * Mana Spring Totem - Not bad, if the party stays in one place for a longer time. Its mana recharge is below blessing of wisdom but is still respectable. The problem is its initial cost. If your team moves fast and you spam mana spring, chances are, you just burn up your mana to give some small mana ticks to party members. Even after 5 talent points of investment.

 * Fire Nova Totem - It definitely has its uses but its higher skill ceiling (you gotta make sure that the enemy gets in the range way before it goes off) and its CD limits it from a higher tier.

 * Magma Totem - Does okay damage despite not having the best SP coefficient in the world and is a staple for shaman dungeon tanking. Has no CD. But it's extremely expensive, limiting its usefulness.

 * Stoneclaw Totem - Besides dungeon tanking, also has some limited use but it mostly gets its point as a dungeon-tanking tool. Arguably the best of the bunch but that's all it's really useful at. The fire aoe totems also help during PVP or as a DPS. Stoneclaw's non-tank utility, while exists, is extremely limited.

 **Strong Tier:**

 * I think this is our best resistence totem. Uses water element which we can 'sacrifice' quite easily and there are many instances where the enemy casts fire spells. From BRD up to BWL, super useful (but not broken at all).

 * Grace of Air Totem - The favourite of all kinds of enhancement shamans but never used by enhancement shamans. This agility buff is extremely strong (above leader of the pack with Kara10 relic!) but the totem happens to be in the same element as windfury.

 * Strength of Earth Totem - Its buff is very useful for melee and is almost always used. While being very strong, it is not broken, if we compare it to other buffs (for example, blessing of might).

 * Disease Cleansing Totem - Super limited use but when its needed, it's awfully effective. Turns out that having its effect work even during etb ('enters the battlefield') comboes well with the fact that it doesn't have any CDs, allowing it to be spammed. Usually useless but if diseases happen, this totem alone negates the whole thing for the entire party.

 * Poison Cleansing Totem - Same as the disease one but poisons happen more often in my opinion. An absolute beast in ES.

 * Tremor Totem - Its use is very limited but when it is needed, it steals the show. Tremor makes encounters involving fear (charm, sleep) way more trivial. I kept it in strong only because of its limited use and because it is a bit unreliable (uses area-based pulses every 4 second to dispell, if not used with experience, can go wrong).

 * Grounding Totem - I agree with the GCD nerf, this totem was (and is) a PVP menace. Giving a class which can interrupt every 5-6 seconds a totem which nopes a targeted spell every 15-12 seconds is extremely strong. It has some PVE uses and has a good skill sceiling to know when to use it. Its internal CD and GCD usage limits it from being higher up on the list. But to be honest, it would never reach windfury anyway.

 **Nerf or rework is needed Tier:**

 * Windfury Totem - One totem (without GCD) giving your 4 melee party members 20% chance for a free hit every time they hit is 1) beyond broken 2) scales with the equipment of the party members really well. WF is so strong, it warps common sense. Level 56 shamans are invited for entry-level  raid pugs (I'm not sure if the game still allows this) just to spam this one totem. Summoning this totem suddenly 'almost creates' a melee character out of nothign if we add together the DPS increase it causes. Tranquil air totem never and agility totem barely sees play because of this bad boy. This totem ensures that all shaman goes to the melee party.

Also I would like to point out that Healing Stream Totem is literally the most mana efficient heal in Shaman's kit by a massive margin and is, matter of fact, impactful. It just doesn't FEEL impactful. It's a low profile form of sustain in the same vein as Stoneskin Totem is a low profile dmg mitigation. Especially together, these totems provide a sizeable amount of survivability in dungeons essentially allowing leather and mail wearers tank on its own, and even applicable survivability in raids.

The flat number reduction, which is made even stronger with the Enhancing Totems talent, ranges between 1%-4% mitigation from melee dmg in raids, and has higher value than armor upgrades in some cases because of DR on armor dmg mitigation, and obviously has highest value on aoe pulls where many auto attacks are coming in.

Again, the issue is that what it does is passive and quickly erased by healing in comparison to dmg numbers which are constantly popping in your face, and that these buffs typically only help a single individual who has threat as opposed to everyone who does melee dmg, or everyone who uses mana.
