Honestly it's obvious before or very eyes. Shaman already had the entire kit to tank apart from one technical detail: totem's aggro table is their own.

Just make it into a talent to make totem's aggro revert to the shaman!!!

That way Magma is AoE sustain threat, Flame Nova is AoE snap, while Stoneclaw is our taunt!!!

I can imagine how technically in terms of code this might be hard to implement but prior to play a shaman I had to be told/read in a guide that totem's threat wasn't shaman's, instead after as its own! As in it's counter intuitive.

Bonus points if you decided to make all totem's effect be selfish, as in after activating the talent for totem's aggro, you'd also make party wide effects to be centered: stoneskin instead of providing armour to the party, got let's say 3x times armour to the shaman (plate to mail differential gone), water would provide a either higher mana/health gain for the shaman (I know "it's too similar to paladin's seal of light/wisdom, but c'mon it's just too make your shammy engaging). In terms of air, something in terms of of spell redirection (grounding) and give autoattacks some built in AoE/cleave (windfury)

C'mon make it happen! It's time until October's next patch I'll help! No downsides! No PvP balance affected it's all good!
