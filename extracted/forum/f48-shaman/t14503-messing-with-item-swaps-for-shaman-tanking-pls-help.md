Mid combat weapon swap for stormstrike costs you too much time to be worth it, and leaves you insanely vulnerable against stronger enemies such as bosses. For that 1.5 sec window

I would not recommend it.

You can armor swap while pulling as far as i know, but you start each fight with significantly less hp, due to the casterset having less stamina.
Probably doable but seems somewhat pointless to me.

Enhancement shamans pullthreat is one of the few things about the spec thats actually decent. I kinda struggle to believe equally geared dps need these shenannegans of you to not rip threat. And its all solved by giving you 1-2 seconds headstart on oull
