# Ele Sham should get same treatment as other caster hybrids

## Post 104831 by Froghijab420 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-10-25T18:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104831#p104831 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Other caster hybrids (Balance, Smite + Shadow priest) are getting lots of love in the new class changes patch.

Here are some small QOL suggestions/buffs from the 1.17.2 changes.

 1.
> Elemental Fury has been replaced by Electrify: Your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells charge you with electricity, increasing your Nature damage dealt by 2% and the critical strike damage bonus of your offensive spells by 20%. **Lasts 20 sec**. Stacks up to 5 times.

Please change the duration of this from **20 seconds** to something like **40 or 60** seconds.
As this current change stands, this will make you lose your spell damage buff that you use to have baseline, between every dungeon pack pull, or drink breaks.

 2.
> Move Lightning Mastery [0/5] up in the talent tree
>  Reduces the cast time of your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning by (.2, .4, .6, .8, 1.0)

This will allow players more choice is choosing what spec to level with, this talent being so far down is the only reason why elemental is only viable for leveling at 39ish+; with the enhance changes - even if this talent is moved up - it will still most likely be the much better leveling spec.

 3.
> Elemental Focus: Mana cost reduction Increased from 40% to 60%.

Elemental Focus reduces 2 spells by 60% after a crit, **change it to reduces 1 spell mana cost by 100%**.
On regular classic wow, Elemental Focus added chain lightning into single target rotation, it would be nice to still have it included in the rotation - even during mana conservation phases.  I miss using chain lightning while leveling, etc.

 4. Better pushback talent.

Some very very small changes that would make elemental much more enjoyable.
