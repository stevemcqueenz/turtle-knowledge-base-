# Shaman Chain Lightning & Swing Timer Change

## Post 130400 by karuu — 2025-05-10T03:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130400#p130400 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Quick repost when I first submitted in suggestions.

Got some inspiriation after seeing that the devs added a SP coeffiecent (5%) to Lightning Strike, and I propose two very small changes:

1. **Revert the cast time of Chain Lightning to 2.5 seconds to fit in-between swings smoother.**
2. **Allow the swing timer to continue while casting Chain Lightning.**

Promotes build diversity (spellhance), adds to skill expression (timing, positioning, resources), and is thematic to the shaman fantasy (chain lightning=cool).

Chain Lightning is self-regulating, in that it has a CD (6 sec) and a high mana cost (lame). Could even make it a talent, though wording would be a disaster.

Not sure if this should be extended to Lightning Bolt as well, you'd have to crunch the numbers.
