Please give Elemental shamans some flavour mechanically,heck even resto. One button rotations are just uninteresting,the facts that we provide utility via totem shouldnt overshadow dps performance. Imo the spec should be given one of the following:
Lightning overload - given to arcanes in the form of cascade which makes the spec fun,exciting and worth the long cast to proc extra effects.They also get PoM - an instant cast option built in naturally in their talenting,while we have to go resto for that
