## Post 69716 by Jstansberry (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-09T08:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69716#p69716 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

PVP trinkets break different CCs depending on your class. Shaman trinket breaks immobilizing effects like frost nova, stuns, and slows. Shamans can not break sap.

And yes, Tremor Totem must be placed prior to being feared/charmed/slept. It will not break the fear instantly, either. The Tremor Totem "pulses" every 4 seconds to remove fear/sleep/charm effects from those in range, so if you place it right before being feared, you will still be feared for ~4 seconds. A better answer to Warlock fears is Grounding Totem, place it right before they finish casting fear. Tremor is your only answer to Priest Psychic Scream fear, as that is AoE and Grounding Totem will not intercept it.
