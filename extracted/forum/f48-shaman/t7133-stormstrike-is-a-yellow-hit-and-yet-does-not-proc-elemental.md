## Post 45434 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-05-11T05:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45434#p45434 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yellow doesnt automatically mean spell.

Stormstrike is a melee based ability, not a spell. As a rule of thumb, if  you can cast it while silenced, its not a spell.

Other abilities in that category are
Heroic strike
Sinister strike
Aimed shot

This is why stormstrike doesnt proc elemental devastation.
