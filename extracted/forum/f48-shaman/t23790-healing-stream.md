Back with another controversial opinion:
Shaman healers need healing…pun intended
Nerf after nerf to shaman healing is not placing them in a strong mindset, it use to be one of the best healers, often times best parsing healer for no-overheal in classic wow.
I was always impressed by chainheal go BRRRTT.
Now shaman heals sit in this category where holy paladins out perform them in any situation that allows a paladin in melee. They have better mana sustain, cheaper spells, better at heal sniping, and finally instant cast spells.
Shaman literally peaked with t1 8/8 for mana sustain( gone now with 1.18.1)
So now they are stuck being a healbot/totemthot amusing spiritlink to save tanks and getting all the chain heals sniped.

Well now introduce Healing Stream…not the totem
A literal stream of healing power
Imagine magic missles, but for healing.

Healing stream: cost 20% of base mana ( 800-1000mana) 1 minute CD
Directs a stream of healing power towards a target for 10 seconds of channeled time. During this time a tick of 350 healing per second with 0.33 Sp coefficient with be directed at the primary target. Any healing to the target above maximum health will be redirected to a nearby target below 100% health.
