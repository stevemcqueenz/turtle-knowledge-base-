# How to fix Bloodlust ?

## Post 25723 by Rafale (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-09-22T06:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25723#p25723 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Bloodlust is badly designed right now :

 - It costs too much mana for enh shamans which lack it
 - You always need to give it to MT to secure threat at beginning of fight
 - You can be a resto shaman and use it, which is absurd when we look at the talents in that tree

Solution :

Bloodlust (**no mana cost**, 3 min cd)
Increases melee, ranged, and spell casting speed by 20% for a party member within 20 yards. Lasts 20 sec.
 **This ability can only be used after scoring a melee critical strike. Each time you gain a melee critical strike, the cooldown of Bloodlust is reduced by 5 sec.**

Benefits :
 - Only real enhancement shamans will be able to use bloodlust. No resto shamans will have to get it.
 - Reactive gameplay and blocks the "give to MT at pull".
 - Real scaling for enhancements which will have to build their gear on crit (synergy with Flurry, elemental focus, elemental devastation)
