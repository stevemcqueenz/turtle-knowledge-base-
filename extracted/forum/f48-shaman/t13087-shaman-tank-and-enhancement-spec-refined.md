Hey there guys just wanted to see what other people think of this subject. I personally think enhancement as DPS is pretty awesome on t wow and a lot has been done to make it truly worth while during late game. Another idea I had is if we could cast other shields on targets besides lightning.

My main concern though,  is the tanking aspect of shamans. We have everything we really need from gear, to talents. All we are missing is a true taunt ability. Yes we have high threat and we can keep agro. Despite that that all goes to the way side without a true actual taunt. It destroys are ability to truly tank in end game and raids. We have rock biter weapon and earth shock. Which state high amount of threat. Which ends up being worthless with the simple fact we don't have a taunt. You did a wonderful job giving us our shields "water shield and earth shield " All we need is an extra shock that's a taunt, or something for that matter.  With that ability, then we will be underway for true viability as the next tank class and no longer be a meme speck!
