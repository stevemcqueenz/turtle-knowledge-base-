## Post 32082 by Thegr8equalizer — 2023-01-04T13:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32082#p32082 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Totem twisting is a vital part of enhancement gameplay and a very fun game mechanic.
The current situation makes it very mana intensive to totem twist while mostly gimping enhancement dps without providing much of anything else for themselves to compensate. The idea is to allow enhancement shamans to totem twist and rip the benefits of iit.

Taking into consideration the shortcomings of enha dps itemisation and the lack of mana sustain this is the talent I have come with.

Totemic Current - 5 Row - Requires 20 points in Enha tree - 3 talent points: "Whenever you have an air totem, an earth totem, a fire totem, and a water totem active at the same time, you gain a 10 sec buff that  gives you 40/80/120 spell power and 3%/6%/9% spell crit and hit.You also regen 10%/20%/30% of your base mana in 10 sec (this has a 20 sec ICD),the buff will be reapply upon summoning another 4th totem and will instantly cancel upon casting non-instant spells"

Promote totem twist gameplay, while providing mana sustain mechanic to support the heavy mana usage of totems and giving a good buff to promote spellhance gameplay without creating issue if ele/resto try to cheese it because of the non-instant spell rule.

The values are placeholder and could be change :)

Tell me what you all think about it! If you like it, if you think it's useless or need some tweaks!

"I would rather like him be balanced and desirable based on other mechanics" Kevin Jordan
source: [Vanilla Dev Explains Shaman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iH7xKE0W7Pc&t=15m56s)

Totem twisting was never a design feature and the original creators tried their best to not make it a thing. They only tolerated it because shamans were not popular picks for raids (outside of healing) and so they allowed it to "get him in the door".

As such, on a server that wants to keep classic design philosophy paramount, I don't think actively encouraging it is the way to go. Now, since shamans are still not first picks for dps it can be left as is for now. I'm with Kairion on this one if there ever will be a fix for shaman totems.

For anyone else interested, that interview also has some amazing insight on enhancement in general.
