Fistly, water shield is free. Even if you dont plan to get hit, it does pay to have it active just on the odd chance you might get hit.

Earthshield offers pushback protection. Against some mobs that deal aoe thats a great boon to have even if they dont hit you specifically.

There is also a totem from black morass anda talent in resto that give some mana for having watershield active.

Tldr: always have a shield active. If you dont think you get value out of youe shield, just keep watershield active since it doesnt cost any mana anyway
