# Shaman talent trees own take

## Post 110595 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-29T21:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110595#p110595 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

It's just a thought experiment. My take on the current shaman trees/what I'd change there.

Red means that I'd make changes in the talent.
Green means I'd move it.
Blue square refers to a new talent which I'd add there.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/TwbqKs00/shamantalents.jpg)

So.

Elemental

Earth's Grasp
Now: +HP to stoneclaw totem and +range to earthbind totem
New: CD reduction to stoneclaw, +range to earthbind, +5% threat reduction to tranquil air totem

 **Call of Thunder**
Now: +spell crit% to lightning bolt and chain lightning
New: cast time reduction to lb and chain lightning but also damage reduction for them

 **Lightning Mastery**
Now: cast time reduction to lb and chain lightning
New: +spell crit% to lightning bolt and chain lightning, removing damage reduction from CoT

What I did here is to enable faster spellcasting earlier to make ele more interesting pre late 30s without making them too strong. Was basically a talent swap with some extra steps.

 **Eye of the Storm**
Now: Gives you focused state after getting a crit
New: Gives you focused state after dealing a spell crit. Focused state caps at 75% (25, 50, 75)

Call of Flame and Flame Guidance go up by 1 to help shaman tanks.

 **Flame Guidance**
Only change is to making searing totem follow flameshock a baseline behavior. Feels like all searing totem should behave that way.

 **Electrify**
Now: Gives 2% spell damage and 20% crit damage bonus, stackable 5 times.
New: Gives 3% (6, 10) spell damage and 33% (66, 100) crit damage bonus, stackakble 3 times.

 **Blue square:**
Spell damage totem (fire element)

 **Enhancement**

 **Ancestral Knowledge**
Now: 10% stat boost to each main stat
New: 5% stat boost to each main stat

 **Shield Specialization**
Now: 5% block chance and 30% amount blocked buff
New: 5% block chance and 15 defense (arguable between 10 and 20)

Amount blocked is a good stat but for it to work, it requires an on-demand block-chance increase ability which shamans don't have. Getting defense is better for them. More reliable.

Below two are just fun ideas. I share them less seriously than the other things:

 **/wacky ideas start**

 **Totemic Alignment**
Now: 60% of totem threat is yours
New: 60% of totem threat is yours and you get some mana back based on the threat generated by the totems (possibly with a cap)

This one might not be necessary but I like the idea. This, clearcasting and water shield together should make mana management very consistent.

 **Improved Ghost Wolf**
Now: 2 sec faster ghost wolf
New: Would change what ghost wolf does. Would turn it into a 1 min CD, 2 sec cast time spell which turns you into a ghost wolf for up to 10 sec, even in combat. Damage has a chance to turn you back. Improved Ghost Wolf would make it an instant spell.

 **/wacky ideas end**

 **Flurry**
Now: Crits grant 15% haste for the next 3 melee hit.
New: Crits grant 15% haste for the next 3 melee hit and remove 0,5 sec from SS and LS CD.

 **Stormstrike**
Instead of 20% to your next 2 nature spells, I'd give 20% to your 2 next shocks. As an enh you'll probably cast shock for it and opens up frost shock and flame shock usage if they are needed.

 **Enhancing Totems (was supposed to be red)**
Now: Strength and agi totem +15%, grounding -2 sec, stoneskin +30% damage reduction and 30% blocked amount increase.
New: Same but +10 defense instead of the blocked amount increase. Same logic as by shield specialization.

Blue Square: A defensive CD
Ancestor's Communion
Basically a self-cast spirit link.

 **Restoration**

 **Totemic Reach**
Now: 30 yard radius to totems.
New: 35/40 yard radius (helps at raids and at high movement).

 **Healing Way**
Now: HW has a 100% chance and CH has a 33% chance to make your heal bigger on the target next time.
New: Would remove CH but would add LHW with 100%.

Tidal Surge was marked as an error.

 **Improved Chain Heal**
Now: CH cast time reduced by 1 sec.
New: CH cast time reduced by 0,5 sec.

 **Undertow**
Now: HW and LHW have 50% chance to restore a water shield or if full, to consume it.
New: Your **max rank** HW and LHW have a 100% chance to restore a water shield (consume it if possible) and make your next 2 chain heal spells 0,5 sec faster.

With these 2 I basically kept chain heal cast time as usual but made a condition: you have to strive for a healing rotation to benefit from the really fast chain heals.

I'd like to also add that shamtanks are (as far as I'm aware) the only tank with a ranged "taunt" in the form of earth shock, making their initial pull threat and the regaining of threat somewhat more manageable than, say, a warrior which would have to run and taunt or swap stances to intervene/intercept. Don't even think about using ranged, 'cause that shit hits like a wet noodle and has almost zero extra threat. Earthbind totem also exists to slow a mob's movement, as well as frost shock, but you should probably be earth shocking to get threat back I assume.
