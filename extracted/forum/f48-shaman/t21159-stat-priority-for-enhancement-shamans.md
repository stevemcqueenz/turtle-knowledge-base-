## Post 147005 by Taralej90 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-19T13:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147005#p147005 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

> Weapon Skill (Up to a maximum of 5. Beyond that it's useless.)

That's not how weapon skill works at all, not on default vanilla and most certainly not on Turtle-WoW.

Read [here](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_Skill) and [here](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16790). If you're a melee class, then you want to go for the hard cap of 315, period.

> Hit Chance (Up until 5 which should make me hit the hit cap combined with talents. I'm a bit confused, as I think the hit cap used to be 9, but it seems to be 8 now.)

Correct. The hit cap on T-WoW is 8 instead of 9 because the useless hidden 1% from vanilla has been removed.

> Crit Chance (Is there an upper limit to this?)

There is, but you won't reach it. Crit cap is your maximum hit chance minus glancing blows, misses and dodges.

> Strength (Or for every strength, x2 Attack Power)

Almost correct, but Str > 2 AP due to it scaling with talents and buffs.

> Agility (I've heard some people say this is more important than Strength.)

At early gear levels, you severely lack crit to keep Flurry and Clearcasting up, which are the bread and butter of your DPS. Beyond T2 you should just go for Str. People who claim that Agi is just overall straight up better than Str across the table are bad.

As for EP values, I can't give them to you and I doubt anyone can. I've tried playing around with ChatGPT to calculate them for me, but it took a long time, many corrections and brought unsatisfactory end result.
