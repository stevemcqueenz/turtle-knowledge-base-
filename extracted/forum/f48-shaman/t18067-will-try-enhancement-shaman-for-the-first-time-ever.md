Mining and blacksmithing is far from "optimal" for tank sham. You'd likely want engineering more than anything, since up until 40 you can't even use the mail gear you get from blacksmithing.

If your concern is viability, the playstyle has been "viable" since CC1 (CC2 was the most recent big class balance overhaul) in the sense that it's usable for almost every raid. Now, it's much smoother to do content with.

I can't exactly help with rotation all that well and what I share will be very simplistic fundamentals you can likely figure out yourself, but- here they are anyways.

Always keep rockbiter on.

Use your highest rank of earth shock as a "pseudo taunt" for snap threat or initial threat rather than your constant on CD main threat generator. It can burn through mana like no tomorrow if you're not careful and leave less flexibility with using other tools like totems. Your second highest rank should be your steady threat generator, with rank one being used solely for interrupts if you wanna be super optimized.

For multiple targets, use fire nova totem and tab target smacking to keep threat. I'm not sure if magma totem is better since I haven't gotten that on my sham but experiment between the two if you can't learn which is better. I would imagine they both have their purposes. I imagine you'll want stoneskin over strength of earth for mitigation despite how much your melee DPS might want it.

At lower levels, like in RFC (and possibly WC-ish?) you can safely heal yourself mid fight with rank 1 healing wave so long as the number of enemies is low and since it comes out so quick while giving a decently substantial amount for the level and also technically being "aoe threat" since you generate threat through healing. Maybe you can squeeze in a rank 2 with slower attacking enemies but that's probably stretching it. Also, ALWAYS remember tremor totem, especially in WC since the druids sleep you a lot. After that, though, I can't help. It's up to you to discover things for yourself or keep asking around.

Hey!

In my experience tank shaman starts off as extra squishy (healer has to pay more attention than usual) until around early 30s. From that it's easy (for dungeons). Main challenge is maintaining mana.
You can make early game 100x better for yourself if you use some potions, armor kits etc to reduce squishiness.
Downgrade your spells to save mana when it makes sense.
