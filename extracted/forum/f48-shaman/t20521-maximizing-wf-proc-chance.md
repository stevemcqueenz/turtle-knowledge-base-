## Post 141835 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-28T06:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141835#p141835 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The CD of Stornstrike and Lightning strike are generally short enough to where its not worth holding them.
If you hold a stormstrike unil wf is ready, you on average get 0.2 autoattacks worth of damage. Every time you cast the ability you gain ~1 auto attacks worth regardless of wf cd.

So its never worth holding yout stornstrike or lightning strike for more than 1/5 of their cooldown. Considering how crowded your rotation is, holding em also have knock on effects.

So i would genuinely advise against it for dps. For pvp where burst is more important than raw dps this might however be different
