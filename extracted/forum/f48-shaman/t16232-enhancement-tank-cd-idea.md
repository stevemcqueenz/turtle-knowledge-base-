# Enhancement Tank CD idea

## Post 110676 by Reivaj — 2024-11-30T13:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110676#p110676 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is an idea that goes with the identity of the class, and it could be intended as a way for the shaman to drop the pressure of an encounter into an elemental. The cast time allows players to interrupt the shaman, probably diminishing its application into PvP. To acquire this ability I think it should be deep into the enhancement tree or make a questline for the shaman class. Although the later expands the idea to different specializations.

The idea:

 **Guardian's Call**
 *Cooldown: 35 minutes.*
The shaman starts shifting into the astral plane (cast of 2 - 3 seconds) once shifted, the shamans body goes into the astral plane reducing physical damage received by *n%* and makes it immune to certain effects (venoms, sickness, curses, bleed... *apply ideas here*). While shifted the shaman can't move, attack, or cast spells.
The shaman mixes his spirit with an earth elemental, transferring his threat into the elemental and granting control of the elemental to the player for 10 seconds, granting certain skills:
- Taunt
- AoE damage area
- Single target damage

---
 *Possible extension of the idea:*
The CD could be something expandable to other applications (other elementals) based on your weapon's imbue or it could be made a questline for shamans to acquire the ability to ascend into different spirits. We could assume natural elements related with the flavor of the class like fire, wind, earth or ice.

## Post 110687 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-30T16:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110687#p110687 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I usually follow Occam's razor when theorycrafting.
This ability sounds very flavourful but way too complicated for what it does (take into account how many new animations, mechanics etc it needs).
The other problem is the cast time. Unless the tank somehow gets 100% spell pushback, it wouldn't really work since ideally many mobs are hitting the tank.
I'd welcome a defensive CD but the simpler the mechanic is, the more plausible it is.

## Post 110701 by Reivaj — 2024-11-30T18:57:00Z

It is definitely a complex mechanic, I agree Akos. In terms of animations not so much, since many of them could be reused and applied in simple terms. The elemental control part is tricky, it will have to be something as "eyes of the beast" spell from hunters. As of making the player's character immobile, pretty sure that for certain boss encounters it won't work well. But there has to be some sort of tradeoff between value and inconvenience, specially for certain niches like PvP. That's why I thought about the cast time. Overall I like the whole idea of shamans taking control or becoming an elemental for a short period of time. I think on the long run it goes very well with the shaman class, and it could even be beneficial for certain encounters / environments where elementals can be handy enriching the game mechanics a little bit more.
