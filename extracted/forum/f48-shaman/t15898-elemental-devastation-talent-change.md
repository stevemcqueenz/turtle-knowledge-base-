# Elemental Devastation Talent Change

## Post 108264 by Zeena — 2024-11-17T09:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108264#p108264 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello fellow adventurers,

I've been reflecting on the recent change to the Elemental Devastation talent and I’m struggling to understand the reasoning behind it.

Previously, this talent granted +9% melee crit chance after a successful spell crit. This made perfect sense within the Elemental talent tree, as it synergized beautifully with the ultimate talent that provides 100% spell crit chance. The intent seemed clear: encourage a hybrid playstyle where shamans alternate between ranged spells and melee attacks, embodying the hybrid nature of the class.

Now, Elemental Devastation provides 3% base spell and melee hit, plus 9% spell hit after a melee crit. This feels like the complete opposite of its original design. Instead of promoting the hybrid playstyle, it now pushes us towards stacking hit, which we already achieve through base talents and gear.

Questions:

What’s the rationale behind doubling down on spell hit?
Wouldn’t crit-related bonus align better with the name Elemental Devastation? If it were about hit, it would have been called Elemental Precision.

Wouldn’t a more balanced approach be 3% base hit (spell and melee) plus 9% melee crit after a spell crit? This would maintain synergy between ranged and melee damage, allowing shamans to adapt fluidly in combat—a hallmark of the hybrid class fantasy.

I’d love to hear from the community on this. Am I missing something? Does anyone else feel this change undermines the hybrid shaman playstyle?

The old elemental devastation was very good for tank shaman's mana efficiency especially in conjunction with flurry (increased attack speed after crit) and elemental focus (mana reduction of shocks after crit). As a tank you ideally used a fast weapon so you had crazy good proc rate on elemental devastation. If you played it right almost every earth shock got the mana cost reduction.

Now we have lightning strike to trigger an empowered water shield charge plus the new stable shields talent which ups the number of charges to a whooping 9. That also frees up a lot of global cool towns. Don't know whether mana efficiency is better now but from my limited play time today I would say: yes.

Note that during Bloodlust you still get up to the old attack speed you had with pre-nerf flurry.

Edit: shamans now essentially gain 5 talent points because they no longer have to "waist" 5 points on the first row of restoration to get to the 3% hit chance talent. Seems like a net positive.
