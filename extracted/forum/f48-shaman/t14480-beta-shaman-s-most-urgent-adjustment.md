I think that adding to the enhancement combat rotation leads to a dilemma.
Every +melee hit you grant them is a potential WF proc which can loop into itself. Honestly, in PVE I wouldn't find it unfair since fury warriors usually have access to WF in raids and them spamming hamstring is not frowned upon. But in PVP it could be disgusting.

Adding new shock-like effects wouldn't help imo. Enh shamans can already shock every 5 sec and allowing a melee class to have a shock-like effect every 2-3 sec would be oppressive.
I see two design spaces as workarounds.
 1. Give them a melee haste talent, for example 10% haste for 5 tp. Sounds extreme but it would only have 2 effects: enh shamans would autohit every 2,7 sec not 3 sec with a 3 sec 2h weapon -> somewhat less downtime and tanks would be better with threat since they wanna spam rockbiter procs on targets.
 2. A raptor strike-like effect which makes your next auto-attack stronger. Would feel like doing something instead of spamming auto-attack on a slow weapon, would increase DPS and this ability would only enhance one strike, meaning that WF procs won't become disgustingly powerful.
