# Petition to give Ele Shamans some PvE dmg pushback reduction

## Post 43506 by Voodoochile (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-04-27T20:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43506#p43506 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm proposing a change to Elemental Mastery (31 point talent) to add % dmg pushback resistance either passively for a low amount, say 20% (becoming 50% w/ Earth Shield), or up to 70% for a short duration after casting Elemental Mastery or perhaps after using the proc (since you can hold the buff indefinitely) becoming 100% w/ Earth Shield.

I propose this on Elemental Mastery so it may not skew pvp by still making you choose between it and Nature's Swiftness.
