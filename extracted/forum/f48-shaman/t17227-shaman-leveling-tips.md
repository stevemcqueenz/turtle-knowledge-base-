## Post 117662 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-23T11:16:00Z

Totally viable. In TWOW you can find a raidspot wit basically any build unless you really meme one.
On the other hand, shamans are the biggest on-demand class there is because of how their buff works.
You ideally want 1WF totem for each melee group, maybe 1 agi totem for a hunter group and very maybe 1 tranquil air totem for a caster group.
Mostly WF totem carries, though.
End result is guilds taking any kind of shaman just for the buff (but secretly hoping for a resto).
From raid pov while all shaman builds are wanted because of the buff, the builds are from different tiers:
 * Resto is close to being broken. Huge mana pool, spammable, quick chain heal. Imagine old T2,5 resto shaman stealing prot pala's mana management. Raids love them, they are a bit too OP probably.
 * Enh is much worse as a DPS than resto is as a healer but the have their own niche. They are designed as mostly single-target focused, burts DPS, meaning that in shorter windows they can output disgusting damage. Very good for farmed contents, not really effective for progression.
 * Ele is okay. You really want a thunderfury tank. You're probably forced to use WF totem and you are kinda boring imo but they work.

You can easily find raid spots with any of these, except for high-end speedrun guilds where (usually) resto has a high prio and (maybe) enh has some for the farm speed. Ele is less successful there if I know well.

Only exception is tank shaman.
As a tank shaman you can't expect raid spots. First you need a chill and friendly guild. With them, you can raid as a tank shammie but it's rather a favor from the bros, not an on-demand spec. On paper they have their own niche (spell damage resistance) but in practice they are objectively the weakest tank at raiding level.

For resto, best is probably 0 8 43 (getting the extra shield to combo with improved water shield talent).
Unsure aout the others atm.

I level as enhancement, and after the last content patch it is a lot of fun.

With the first 11 points,  get the elemental focus talent from the elemental tree. Focus on agility gear to improve crit chance. When you clear cast, cast your shock.

Then go down the enhancement tree. When you get elemental weapons, use the frostbrand imbue paired with frost shock to improve your chance for crits.

This play style revolves a lot around landing crits, which in turn gives you flurry and clear casting.
