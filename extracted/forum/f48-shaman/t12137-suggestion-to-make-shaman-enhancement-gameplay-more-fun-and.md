# Suggestion to make Shaman Enhancement gameplay more fun and "complex"

## Post 80580 by Educhagasfarias — 2023-12-10T22:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80580#p80580 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to suggest to the developers to place an effect right after activating the Shaman's weapon enhancements, for example, when using windfury, you will have an increase in attack speed for 10 seconds, when using flametongue, your first auto attack will cause a fire damage in the area, things that encourage not to leave just a single effect always active that we only have to worry about renewing when it ends, it could be balanced by putting a low cooldown of 3 or 4 seconds, obviously the numbers are things that should be studied in depth, however this gameplay that encourages using various elements in the weapon in a fight would make the specialization very dynamic.

## Post 80584 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-12-10T22:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80584#p80584 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It seems Blizz intended Shamans to be "swapping" their Enchantments around often (like one second DPSing a heavy armory mob with Flametongue then quickly switching to Frostbrand to kite a dangerous mob)

Thats why they only last 5 mins.
Like Paladin buffs they were supposed to "swapped" around to fit the situation.

However no one uses them this way. You just stick with your 'favorite/ideal' one and have to refresh it (annoying) every few minutes.

Turtle should either increase the length to 10+ mins as this 'mechanic' is a failure...

or change them to have one for healing and one for spell dmg (then people WOULD actually do as Blizz intended and proactively/strategically swap them around on the fly)
