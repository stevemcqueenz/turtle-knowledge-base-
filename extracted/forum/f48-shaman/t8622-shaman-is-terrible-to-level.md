# Shaman is terrible to level.

## Post 55534 by Derrderr — 2023-08-02T03:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55534#p55534 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I took the time to level both shaman and paladin to level 40, trying Ele and enh as opposed to paladins ret. i can say with certainty that shaman is just as boring to level in classic. i don't understand why paladin feels smooth and was given nice ability's to melee whilst shaman feels like a high cost slog for low reward. i was at least expecting shaman to get some useable melee ability's early on like crusader strike, but it doesn't. its just a boring auto attack slog whilst you have to rest constantly after using mana. it just doesn't flow well and seems frustrating, i took shaman to 60 on classic and really was hoping turtle wow would give it some nice balance early on i wouldn't even care if some spells were removed, just to make it feel smooth.

Previously i also asked several 60's on both classes about their perspective and it seems that shaman in general is just considered a very boring class to level, even at 60 you are only guarantied a raid slot if you are a healer, no one seems to want to waste time leveling a class that feels boring.

Please give shamans spec variants of crusader/holy strike. enhance leveling still uses elemental attacks that chunk its low mana pool. maybe something such as an ability that will use up a shamans rockbiter enhancement to deal damage to the enemy, not much but also lowering their armor, or an equivalent for flametongue which gives a dot to pair with flameshock, something that will synergize.
