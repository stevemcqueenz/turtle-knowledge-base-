# Thunderhead: A Beautiful Failure

## Post 99338 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-07-13T03:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99338#p99338 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Thunderhead is a custom T-WoW Talent that:
"Allows Lightning Shield to be cast on one friendly target. Also reduces Lightning Shields cost by 20%"

Whoever created this should be hailed as a 200 IQ champion of Turtle WoW and the Shaman Class.   turtle_in_love_head
They understand precisely how Enhancement Shaman is meant to be played and how it should be designed.

Reasons why "Thunderhead" is S-Tier Game Design:
- 1) Instead of being some corny/broken new ability -- it simply takes an existing ability and allows it to be used in a fresh way. This is clever, conservative, and reduces ability-bloat.
 2) It gives the player an actually desirable reward for investing 3 Talent Points into the normally god-awful 'Improved Lightning Shield".
 3) It gives Shaman a "normal" Magic-type buff to apply. It's amusing game-play variation from the often tedious normality of being 'tied to totems' if you want to buff anyone. (The Water Walking/Breathing buffs dont count.)
 4) It doubles down on the class fantasy of "Enhancing Others" and being "A Supporter". **Based!**
 5) It addresses a very common complaint of 'not getting value from Shields unless personally getting hit'.
 6) It addresses a very common complaint of 'Lightning Shield costing too much'.
 7) It addresses a very common complaint of 'Having nothing to do while waiting for Stormstrike CD.' You will be refreshing this on the Main Tank often.
 8) It synergizes with your Stormstrike debuff (if you are afraid to use Earth Shock due to threat).
 9) It has unlimited range (very bizarre but bemusing to buff someone from 500 yards away. KEK)
... yet despite all this -- 'Thunderhead' has been a (nearly) complete failure. Why? Simple:
It got mega cucked by the newly added Water/Earth Shields.   crying_turtle
These fresh faced custom competitors are excellent and do NOT require a 4 Talent Point investment.

(also I will admit -- Shaman already have Mana issues so keeping Lightning Shield constantly refreshed was unrealistic)

However; it was at least not an 'Epic' Fail because it still saw some use from level 20-59 Shamans (mostly just for the 20% reduction)

Anyway this post is a loving tribute to this 'soon to be removed/replaced' ability/talent.
(Its getting swapped in favor of a new Melee attack that is basically Holy Strike Jr. -- Devs are caving to the endless whining for 'moar deepz' and making a Shield into an ability that you actively attack people with.)

Good night, sweet prince. You were too pure for this World (of Warcraft).   dead_turtle_head
