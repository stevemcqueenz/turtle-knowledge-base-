Both fire elemental and regular elemental shaman can easily sustain their mana-needs with manaspring totem, potions and tea even in the longest of fights without usage of watershield and other shenannegans.

Being oom after a fight is not an issue. Being oom in a fight is. And elemental shaman with gear appropreate for any raid encounter does have enough mana for that. With the possible exception of the aq20 gauntlet at the second boss. But thats hardly something that needs fixing.

I speedrun kara and naxx on my elemental and potions + tea keep me going well enough for 1h clears.

That being said, i am convinced the team in charge of shaman changes is adjusting the class out of missguided faith over cold hard maths. First, we got a spamable firespell added to our talent tree. In the same patch that gave us +60% firedamage from talents. It takes an absolute genious to figure out that thats a winning combination. On top of a bug that doubledips the damage amplification on top of everything.
But the nerfs that have been announced so far miss the forrest for the trees. The reason fire elemental gets played is simply because due to imp. scorch, curse of elements and scythe of elune which is more common of the fire variety than the nature variety, fire is on average just dealing 30% more damage than an equivalent spell of the nature school in endgame raid scenarios. Thats not even considering exotic fire buffs like the cla'okra ring or dragonlings.

Molten blast with basically no supporting talents is comparable to lightning bolts in such scenarios.
(For comparison, the equivalent benefit value of lightning mastery, molten blast has just from being fire.)

What makes me chuckle especially is the fact that "regular" shaman is nerfed next patch too with the totem switching nerf impacting them way more than fire ele, and the spec is already underperforming in raid boss dps outside of maybe 5/5 t3.5 specifically.

The insistence to keep a meme spec alife (melee fire shaman) and not another meme (ranged fire shaman) is creating needlessly wordy and over specific talents.

Just rip the bandaid off, get rid of elemental weapons boosting spells entirely or get rid of molten blast if we are not meant to use it. Or better yet, give nature equivalent support to fire. So we aren't just 30% worse off than someone using shadow or fire as their primary attacking element in raids.

Hillariously enough, the one buff we did get makes us even better at pvp. Peak patch design, missing absolutely every potential patchgoal
