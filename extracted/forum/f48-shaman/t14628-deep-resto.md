# Deep resto

## Post 99806 by Spacepony — 2024-07-18T17:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99806#p99806 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This post is about ideas to adjust the restoration tree, especially the bottom of the tree.

Is it possible to improve the bottom of the tree while:
 1) not making resto shamans overpowered in PVE
 2) preserving the unique strengths and weaknesses of different classes
 3) making deep resto more desirable than deep enhance etc for shamans who want to heal

I find it hard to reconcile these 3 goals.  One idea is that deep resto has a lot of room for improvement in PvP and it would still stay mediocre.  I would like to see a talent like Nature’s Guardian that was introduced in TBC:

“Whenever a damaging attack is taken that reduces you below 30% health, you have a 50% chance to heal 10% of your total health and reduce your threat level on that target.  X second cooldown.”

Then at least a deep resto build would gain more survivability in PVP which seems like a fair trade off for eschewing the huge damage boost of deep elemental.

I could also see talents that support earth shield being a good avenue to pursue, as this seems like it would help Shaman healers in some content without buffing chain heal  output in raids.

Curious what others would suggest.
