Lightning Shield

Lightning shield is fine if it’s the only spell you’re casting during combat. Mixing other spells will quickly drain mana.

Good for single mob combat.
Less effective for multiple mobs.
(A pity Earth Shield and Water Shield are learned at level 60)

Thunderhead talent pulls threat off of the friendly target. Not recommended to cast on tanks.

Stormstrike debuff, on target, doesn’t react to Lightning Shield. Don’t know if this was intended.

Most of my attention is on my Hp and what the mobs are doing so recasting it can be overlooked at times. Even the floating text indicating Lightning Shield as faded can be missed as well as the spinning orb. A lot of the time I’ll go through a fight and forget to recast it.

Suggest having a sound effects when a Shield losses a charge. Maybe more special effects.
