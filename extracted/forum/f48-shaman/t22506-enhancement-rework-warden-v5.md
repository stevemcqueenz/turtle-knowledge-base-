# Enhancement Rework: WARDEN v5

## Post 156329 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-10-20T17:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156329#p156329 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/xhy9EGb.png)

Generally quite pleased with the current state of the class.
That said-- some CC2 things are a little corny/OP for my taste. So I am calling this 'Class Changes 1.5'.

This moderate rework of Enhancement is primarily to improve the health/balance of PVP.
(Code for 'Nerfing Shaman' in PVP)

Note: Shaman is a fundamentally 'tank/support/caster' class and should not be obsessed with 2hander Melee DPS.
Leave that to the Warriors and such.

> BASELINE SHAMAN CLASS CHANGES:
>  - NEW: Totemic Alignment
>  90% of the threat generated by your totems is transferred to you.
>  (Instant cast. Aura. Lasts until cancelled.)
>  - Lightning Shield now has the ability to critically hit (Earth Shield has always had that ability).
>  - Lightning Shield damage reduced by 10%.
>  - Purge cooldown reduced to 6 seconds.
>  - Grounding Totem no longer triggers the global cooldown and will not be destroyed by non-damaging spells.
>  - Stoneclaw Totem now has a 50% chance to (1 sec) stun enemies that attack it.
>  - Sentry Totems health increased by 200.
>  - Feral Spirit (Orc Racial) is now instant cast. (Currently instant when 1 Talent Point put into Improved Ghost Wolf)
>  - New Passive (flavor) Ability: Fire Walking -- reduces damage taken from Boiling Lava and Bonfire sources by 75%.

Looking fine, i would just change 2 talents:

Ancestral guardian:
remove the 5/10/15% armor increase, add -1/2/3% chance to be critically hit while keeping the 2/4/6% dodge.

Spirit armor: armor gained from shields increased to 30/65% (from 15/30%)
Threat generated while wearing a shield increased to 20/40% (from 5/10%)
+ Reduces all damage dealt while wearing a shield by 10/20%

Reasoning for the 2 changes: you became extremly tanky without any penalties to your offensive capabilities, creating a monster in pvp. To balance it, you only gain extra armor while wearing a shield, but then you do less dmg while having your threat output increased to compensate and overcome that penatly.
To compensate a little for nerfing ancestral guardian you gain 3% crit reduction which helps with the poor itemization of shaman tanks.

Baseline changes:
Rockbiter weapon: instead of the crazy flat attack power this weapon imbue now increases your attack power by 75% (and perhaps a small flat amount)
Reasoning: ele+ resto shamans cant abuse it for increased shock dmg in pvp
