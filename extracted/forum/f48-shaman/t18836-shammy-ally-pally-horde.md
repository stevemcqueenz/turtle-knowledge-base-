> - Rework **Rewards & Gear Progression** so that PvP will be a truly viable gearing path.

>  We feel that if a player who has geared mainly through PvP progression wants to dabble in PvE raiding they should be able to with the gear achieved in PvP without starting back at zero with stats that are mainly useless, and vice versa for PvE -> PvP.
