# Electrify requiring 5 stacks is too much - for PvP

## Post 107648 by Ryken — 2024-11-12T10:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107648#p107648 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Five stacks of Electrify in order to obtain 100% crit bonus to offensive spells, which was previously a passive, is too much of a nerf for Elemental PvP. Fights are too short and CC-heavy to make this regularly achievable.

Elemental Mastery giving 2 stacks isn't enough to bring us back on par to what we had as default, and is only available every few fights and is attached to a clear 'quick, CC me!' animation. The 10% extra bonus damage at 5 stacks also isn't that useful the stacks can't be maintained in the first palce.

For PvE, change seems fine (better for longer fights like bosses, though annoying for trash/shorter fights).

But for PvP, I've been playing regularly since the patch dropped and the frustration is immense.

Players are actively trying to CC you, and since shocks don't add to electrify (yet they also suffer the crit penalty from the changed talent), in active combat you are dead before 5 stacks can be reached.

The only success of getting 5 stacks is if you are able to free-cast while the enemy is focusing on your teammates, and the enemy in that case is dumb and would die even if there was no crit bonus anyway.

The other likely chance is building up 5 stacks using rank 1 on a nearby enemy pet, then hoping your 20sec doesn't drop off before you get into combat (it often does). This creates a very frustrating mini-game, without the fun, to constantly monitor and manage your stacks.

I agree with concept behind trying to reduce burst non-interactive PvP (I guess that is over to arcane mages now), but please consider a slight adjustment such as making it a maxium of 3 stacks of electrify with 33% bonus each is much more viable and still addresses the burst concerns.

As it currently stands, Ele is extremely frustrating to play in PvP and I'm close to retiring the spec alltogether, which was the main reason I leveled this class to begin with.
