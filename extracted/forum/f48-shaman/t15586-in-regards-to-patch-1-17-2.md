# In Regards to patch 1.17.2

## Post 106453 by Voodoochile (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-05T04:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106453#p106453 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been very critical about the patch from the moment I started reading it, and I want to start by saying I didn't really bother to read all the changes to all the classes; only for the classes I play, being Shaman, Warrior, and Druid. I've been caught up in my emotions saying my piece in regards to how I believe the patch diminishes the Shaman's class identity and role in the game, and it has led some to believe that I simply hate change to #muhclassic. And I want to clarify my position:

I believe changes to Vanilla Shaman are necessary for the game to improve, and that pre-1.17.2 Shaman was only marginally better than Vanilla and still needed to be iterated on. But the changes proposed, which are now live, in particular affecting Resto talents and abilities and builds, serve in my opinion only to attempt to make each Shaman spec semi-viable at the cost of their class identity, namely around the changes to the Talent Trees, Totems, and Healing, ie: The outright removal of Mana Tide Totem, the nerfing of Mana Spring (and Enamored Water Spirit by extension), the increase in Talent Point costs to reach key Talents that were previously accessible to those cross-speccing, and the nerfing of Chain Heal cast time.

And, while I believe that the removal of existing abilities is taboo to a Vanilla+ experience, I do not believe this option can be wholly forgone if what replaces it preserves the identity of the class, while improving poor aspects of the class. But the removal of Mana Tide Totem to be replaced with personal mana regen serves only to diminish the Shaman's identity as a Support Class, or to be a bit more accurate, an Enabler, which I personally believe the curtailing of is antithetical to the idea of a Vanilla+ Shaman. This in tandem with baseline Chain Heal being nerfed for no good reason only to be BUFFED in the resto tree, and moving improved totems in the Enh Tree from 2nd & 3rd Tier Talents to **5TH TIER** re-enforce their move to prop up spec identity at the cost of class identity.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

I wish to provide a more constructive aspect of my thoughts on what I believe Shaman changes should look like that would preserve their unique class identity as a Hybrid Group-Support Class, and which changes I believe were good for the class.

Firstly, I love that Spirit Link is no longer Tauren exclusive as it is an amazingly synergistic ability with Chain Heal and Healing Totem that emphasizes a specialty of Shaman: AoE Healing, and more generally AoE buffing.

Secondly, I believe Bloodlust was also reworked in a very tasteful way that makes Enhance feel good for the Shaman playing it while still being beneficial to their party without being too strong AND not just preserving, but enhancing the Support Class identity.

Thirdly, I am a little disappointed tho in that the Elemental Tree didn't get anything meaningful aside from marginally better mana efficiency, while also nerfing Shaman's ability to burst and forcing them to build up damage. Many encounters in the end are already very melee-centric because their damage is front loaded with no wait time, leaving casters in the dust for encounters with short kill times, and this change actually only makes Shamans even worse in this aspect in PVE from a personal performance stand point, but ontop of that they simply don't do shit buff-wise in comparison to Enhance and Resto, and especially because the only caster-centric buff at their disposal is Mana Spring.

I would've liked to see something like the addition of Totem of Wrath or Wrath of Air Totem in the Tree, maybe make the Keystone Talent give additional effects to all the Totem Types so Fire gets a Totem of Wrath bonus, Wind gets Wraith of Air bonus, Water could maybe gets a Mana Tide effect if it REALLY needed to be removed from the Resto Tree, and idk for Earth Totem.

But another thing that urks me about the lack of Elemental changes is that Elemental just feels awful to level. You spend a majority of your time in melee playing a worse Enhance shaman that does no damage when you are oom, and the lower end of the talent tree offers nothing to make this experience any better. If NOTHING else got changed in the tree, I'd atleast ask to make Lightning Bolt a 2.5 second cast baseline and nerf Lightning Mastery Talent to give only .5 seconds off the cast for a net even cast time of 2 seconds with the talent so leveling feels a little less shit. Going back to my argument for the ability for Shaman to be a Hybrid, I believe making Lightning Bolt have a lower baseline cast speed would improve that.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Now for my unhinged ideations of a potential version of Shaman:

I need to admit upfront that, as I have stated earlier, I did not read the entire patch notes for the changes to most of the other classes. This being said, I have not read the Paladin changes, nor do I play Paladin. But I have read people complaining about Holy Paladins now being Melee Healers, and let me say that sounds fucking badass. In my mind, BOTH Paladins and Shamans should fill the same fantasy of a Group Support, but more over that they should be melee focused no matter the spec.

At this point, I've played nearly all versions of WoW excluding MoP and WoD. And although I did not end up liking WotLK, I LOVED Spell-hance Shaman in Wrath. I dislike DW for Shaman, but I believe the idea of a melee Shaman casting Chain Lightning and Chain Heals while popping buffing his group with Totems and Bloodlust is absolutely fucking peak GigaChad Who-Gives-A-Fuck-If-I'm-Not-On-Top-Of-The-Charts-I'm-Fucking-Thrall-Bitch.

And obviously we couldn't be that strong in Vanilla for obvious reasons, but in my mind being a melee-enhancer is peak shaman fiction. And with that in mind, I believe that it would be better served to have Spirit Link if not a baseline ability, then a 21 Cost Talent in Resto instead of 31 Cost so it can serve as Shaman Defensive CD for Tank Shaman, or for Ele-Shaman who opt out of Elemental Mastery. For Tank Shaman in Particular, I believe that simply leaving them as simply another viable tank is not serving to preserve their identity and have them stand out from the other tank options. With Spirit Link as a Defensive CD, Tank Shaman can in effect become support Tanks, who even if they are not the Main Tank in raid, they can provide their Main Tank extra survivability based on their own health pool that allows additionally Resto-Shaman to be more useful on Solo-Tank Pulls.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Alright, thats pretty much all that I had to say for now. Ultimately I just don't want to play SoD again or TBC or Wrath or Cata again. I want to play Vanilla+, and I want Twow to stay true to that and as of now I am very disappointed with the design they have chosen regardless of viability.

I am a Naxx and ES geared Shaman who's played here for over a year and was excited for Kara. I have spent plenty of money to support the project. But I simply have no desire to play my Shaman anymore due to the changes, and I find it hard to log in that I cannot play my favorite class across all of WoW.

Greetings,

To start, while I have not always agreed with your opinion on all things shaman, I have always appreciated the fact that you take the time to post like this with a background and explanation of where you are coming from. Additionally, I too am in the "melee healee sure sounds niftee" camp and do play some paladin myself. I feel like you have some good points about the feel vs the utility of Restoration and Elemental and are touching on something I have been thinking about in regard to Enhancement, more specifically tanking Enhancement. I shall lay these out below.

As the base of all my thoughts on the shaman changes, I believe that a more basic form of spirit link should have been made baseline to the shaman (Note: I am not so far into the calculations of the game as to include numbers so I will place variables for all).
This would be in the form of a single target link between your target and another player based not on how close they are but on who has the highest armor and health other than the target within a set range. The base version would split X% of damage between the two per usual with the addition that each of the trees would have a modifying talent for the skill (Note: a possible change I would add for the spell to improve utility would be a chance to avoid spell knockback for a linked caster).
This basic version would allow any shaman to provide a minor form of assistance to a party member in trouble. Examples would include a tank during a high damage portion of a fight or a healer/DPS being assaulted by adds.

Class specific additions.

Restoration: (My secondary play style)
I will start with the Restoration variant of the spirit link. I would trade the Spirit Link talent with the talent 'Restorative Totems', it would add an additional target to Spirit Link with the effect that after the spirit link wears off each person effected would have a HoT placed on them that healed x% of the damage they received during the link which scales off of x% of the shaman's Sp/Hp.
I would modify the 'Restorative Totems' into a capstone talent called 'Totem of Restoration' Which would place down a totem that restored x% of the shaman's mana and health to all party/raid members within X distance.
While I am not sure the effect this would have in a raid situation it feels better thematically and restores an aspect of the mana tide totem that many shaman healers seem to miss.

Enhancement: (My primary play style)
The variant of Spirit Link I would add here would be folded into the talent 'Spirit Armor' at the second talent point. This variant would change the behavior to always link the shaman with another player but increase the resistances of both players by X times the shaman's level amount and their armor by X% of both the shaman and linked player's total armor.
I would modify the 'Stormstrike' talent to have a chance to proc 'Lightning Strike' (If available) that cannot crit for free, with slower weapons having a higher chance to proc the attack (Note: This may be too much, but I am very underwhelmed by cost/benefit of both 'Lightning Strike' and 'Stormstrike).
I would also modify 'Lightning Strike' to burn two charges of the current shield with a commensurate increase in effect if it lands a critical blow.
Finally, to improve their overall defenses I would increase their block scaling in relation to their defense skill and change the effect to the Stoneskin totem in the 'Enhancing Totems' talent so that it would redirect the extra critical hit damage to the totem destroying it instead of increasing the damage resistance (Note: I would keep the block amount increase).

As I play a shaman tank, I have perhaps over tuned these changes, but I feel the spirit link adds a needed defensive cooldown and the general increase in block along with the ability to mitigate a critical hit at the cost of the totem would go a long way in making a fun tanking play style. I also think that with the egregious increase in mana cost for the strike attacks melee damage dealers would feel better with more synergy there.

Elemental: (My least favorite play style and therefore probably completely off base, I'm guessing here)
This variant would get added into the 'Eye of the Storm' talent with it having the unique distinction of being the only variant with multiple tiers. At tier one the variant would flip the equation for the link making it link the shaman and the player with the highest mana total and mana regeneration and sharing X% of the mana used/man burned between the players but adding X% of the players combined mana regeneration as MP5 to both. At tier 2 both players would receive the focused casting proc chance and increase the % of the mana regeneration given. At tier 3 the mana cost of spirit link would drop by X% and further increase the mana regeneration bonus.
I would baseline the nature damage portion of the electricity mechanic to the lightning spell and then add "allow X% of mana regeneration to continue in combat for each stack of electricity" to the 'Electrify' talent to help with the mana starvation complaints I have heard.

As I do not regularly play Elemental I can understand if this portion is just ignored outright or if i have failed to think of the potential secondary effects these changes would have.

Thank you for reading I hope I didn't bore you.
