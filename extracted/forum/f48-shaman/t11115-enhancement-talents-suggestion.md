# Enhancement Talents Suggestion

## Post 73047 by Ryo3000 — 2023-10-23T15:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73047#p73047 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was thinking a bit about the whole debate on suggestion about Enhancement buffs from dual wielding to outright buffs to Enhancement as a whole (which wouldn't be bad at all if that's the way it goes)

But there's a lot of room for improvement on Shaman's skill tree without necessarily just changing the class identity (like dual wielding)

First reworking "Improved Lightning Shield" to

"Improve Elemental Shield"
Increases the effects of your Lightning Shield, Water Shield and Earth Shield by 5/10/15%

 15% more mana from water shield and 15% more healing from earth shield help every shaman across the board

Then replace thunderhead, it's a fun idea but it doesn't really work.
You get all the aggro from the damage, can't use it on tank if they're alliance and it's a bit bugged because you can use it to override other shamans elemental shields, even if they're better than the lightning shield you're casting.

We keep the pre-req of level 3 "Improved Elemental Shield" but replace the effect with something that Shamans kinda need, mana regen

"Storm's Attunement"
While you have an elemental shield on, critical effects from spells and melee attacks restore 1% of your maximum mana.

And for Enhancement Capstone

"Aftershock"
Whenever you use a shock spell increases the attack speed of your next attack by 100%.

I really believe these changes would help the class without fundamentally changing it, the focus is still on crit effects and the class really want those heavy weapons for Aftershock, still useful for the team of course but they're more meaningful than just dropping totems.

What do yall think of the ideas, good? Bad? Too strong? Too weak?

I thought about that too, the talent is not great looking from a tank's perspective.

On the other hand if it requires an actual shield makes it so only tank shamans get to use it, and all specs of shaman could use the in combat mana regen.

But that really depends on if TWow has an actual interest in shaman tanking or not, if they do there are a few ways to make this more attractive

 1 - The effect is active if you have a shield equipped or an elemental shield, but at that point I'd be concerned if that one talent isn't doing too many things at the same time...

 2 - Make it an active ability like druids get clear casting, make a self buff that lasts maybe around 30 min with the effects of getting any crit restores some mana. Not dependent on elemental shield, not a bad thing necessarily.

 3 - Keep the talent like that but make the other elemental shields available at earlier levels and having ranks.

Add in Earth Shield 1 at level 16 together with Lightning Shield 2 (so we'd have earth shield 1 - 6)

And Water Shield 1 at level 24 with Lightning Shield 3 (so we'd have water shield 1 - 5)

The skills would restore mana and health for the same amount that lightning shield would deal damage
Earth shield costs the same as lightning shield and water shield could be 25 mana cheaper than the equivalent lightning shield so it pays itself back on 2 procs.

As an example we'd have a shaman at level 40 having

Lightning shield 5 - 180 mana 3x80 240 (3x92 276)* damage
Earth shield  4 - 180 mana 3x80 240 (3x92 276)* healing
Water shield  3 - 155 mana 3x80 240 (3x92 276)* mana

 *(numbers in parenthesis are with the 15% increase of enhanced elemental shield)
