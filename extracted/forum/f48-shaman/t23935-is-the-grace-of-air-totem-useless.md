Hello, to answer your question, no, the Air Totem isn't useless. While the Wind Fury Totem is a significant DPS buff useful for melee classes, it's not for all classes:

 - Hunter (already mentioned),
 - Combat Rogue (who has a talent that increases their energy generation based on their agility),
 - Tank Shaman (to increase their critical strike chance and dodge ability),
 - Tank Druid (for the same reasons).

If you're raiding and have these classes, you can make a group with :
 - 1 Tank Shaman,
 - 1 Tank Druid,
 - 1 Combat Rogue,
 - 2 Hunters, with at least one Marksman for the Trueshoot Aura.

You can also make the same group with an Enhancement Shaman, which will allow them to benefit from additional buffs, since the Wind Fury Totem doesn't provide them with any.

I hope that answers your question :)

As a bear tank I would strongly disagree. I maintain that the biggest buff to bears with CC2 was the Shaman class change giving us access to Windfury.

A bear's main threat engine is Maul, which is a queued skill replacing your next auto-attack. So a bear played properly will *never* white hit. You *always* want to have Maul queued.
But baseline (without talents) you don't generate any rage from yellow hits, only from white hits.

That meant that before CC2 bears were largely rage-capped. Dodge was seen as a very detrimental stat, because you relied on getting hit for your rage generation. Some bears even deliberately wore less mitigation to take more damage and generate more rage (which is rather counter-intuitive for a tank imo).

WF in bear form fixed all of that in an instant. You keep Maul queued 100% of the time and WF will still provide white hits inbetween, ensuring you have ample rage to spend even if you dodge a couple of times in a row. Having ample rage to spend means you can use Savage Bite and Swipe on CD. Using those instant cast abilities on cooldown, provides more triggers for WF, which means more threat and more rage. It's a beautiful snowball where you get to press buttons on cooldown without having to sabotage your avoidance and mitigation.

Grace of Air is not bad for bears (and probably superior for some fights). But WF fixing rage starvation and trivializing the rage economy is much *much* stronger 9/10 times in my opinion.
