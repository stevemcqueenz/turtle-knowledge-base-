## Post 111836 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-09T01:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111836#p111836 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Does some damage based on your weapon's damage, some nature damage and:
 * more nature damage if you have lightning shield
 * gives mana if you have water shield (with good gear, it makes it cost nothing)
 * gives health if you use earth shield

If you enh DPS ->
Usually use it with lightning shield unless you really need to save mana (water shield then).

If you tank ->
Usually use it with water shield unless threat is an issue (lightning shield) or you are about to die (earth shield).
