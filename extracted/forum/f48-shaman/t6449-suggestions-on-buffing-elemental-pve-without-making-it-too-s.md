# Suggestions on buffing Elemental PvE without making it too strong in PvP

## Post 40621 by Beefchalupa3 — 2023-04-07T04:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40621#p40621 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, elemental is clearly great in pvp already, but it's lackluster in pve for a few reasons, primarily for two reasons:
1. mana inefficiency
2. lack of nature resistance reduction
For mana inefficiency, I think it's pretty straightforward. But you could fix it through talents or just a straight mana cost reduction. I think the clearcasting talent reducing the next 2 casts by 40% is strange, it could easily be 50% or 60% instead.

Now, the second point is interesting because we now know from Blizzard classic that endgames bosses actually had just 23 nature resistance. Turtle's values simply being overtuned and not blizz-like are a massive nerf to elemental shaman. That being said, the common suggestion of adding nature resistance reduction to curse of elements would be a great alternative.

Instead of adding more utility to Warlocks though, here are my suggestions of potential new talents and other changes to the elemental tree:

Rolling Thunder (0/3): Your Lightning and Chain Lightning reduce your target's Nature Resistance by 5/10/15 for 15 seconds. Stacks up to 5 times. (This could also just be 1 point at 15 resistance reduction, since it's so late in the tree).

Similar to a Priest's Shadow Weaving talent, this will apply a stacking debuff to the target. I think this stacking buff is the best way to go about this change, because this will increase PvE effectiveness while not affecting PvP much, where it's all about burst and a slow stacking debuff like this will never really be useful. More importantly, this will give exactly 75 nature resistance reduction, the same amount as curse of elements gives.

(alternatively, this could provide a personal BUFF to the player that increases their own spell penetration for 15 seconds, to make sure it doesn't affect anyone else, or have a situation where just 1 shaman is forced to take this talent for the rest of the raid).

In order to fit this talent into the tree, I propose this: Move Lightning Mastery up 1 or 2 spots on the tree. It never made much sense getting cast time reduction so late anyways. Then, slot in Rolling Thunder where Lightning Mastery used to be, and make Lightning Mastery a prerequisite. Finally, we should condense some talents similar to what was done to druid talents like natural weapons and some feral talents, where they got 5 pointers reduced into 3 pointers.

Recommendations on condensing and streamlining the talent tree:

Reverberation:  Getting only 0.2 second off cd per point just feels bad, even though it's quite a decent talent. It kind of feels bad to put points in, I think this could be condensed into 3 points at 0.33 seconds each.

Call of Thunder: I actually think this talent should have its location swapped with Lightning Mastery regardless of any other changes. It makes more sense to get cast time reduction earlier in the leveling process like all other spellcasters do, and then get crit chance later. Because this maxes out at 6% crit, which is weird for 5 points, this could also be condensed into 3 points at 2% each. This way we have space for adding points into Rolling Thunder. You could also throw in another 6% mana reduction here.

Call of Flame and Improved Fire Totems: I think it would make sense to combine these into one talent, since they are both about fire totems. The fire element in general also seems lackluster, maybe you should add a part of this talent, or baseline, that gives flametongue weapon some useful bonus like 2% spell hit.

I'm not necessarily recommending ALL these changes be made, but I think adding in the nature debuff Rolling Thunder talent, along with 1 or 2 other talent point shrinks would put elemental into a much better place.

Finally, I think all of shamans final talents in every tree are pretty lackluster. Other classes get build-defining, exciting skills and we just get utility stuff. Give us totem of wrath or something, or some kind of cool ability that has to do with the elements.

I think a fire elemental totem would be a very cool addition as the final elemental talent. This would be both flavorful, very lore appropriate, and not affect pvp much because the totem can just be killed instantly by other players. And we need a cool CD to use that isn't just a free crit every couple minutes.

Anyways, just wanted to get the discussion started. I might also make a post about revamping the resto tree, since that tree is so bad that resto shamans will go enhance just to get bloodlust lol.

I remember having the idea at some point (either while playing Light's Hope or maybe retail classic TBC) that since Shaman seem to often rely on probability that it would be interesting to have a mechanic that interacted with their various elemental shields. I'm not sure how they would all work, but having an Elemental talent that would increase the number of stacks of water shield downlinked to a talent that would consume a globe and restore the usual amount of mana on spells landed (perhaps only on LB and/or CL).

If the probability and/or amount of mana restored were adjusted appropriately, it could provide some relief in terms of mana efficiency between crits and force the shaman to occasionally change up their rotation to reapply water shield.

Comparing Elemental to other caster specs, much could be done simply and elegantly by provided a flat %-based reduction to enemy resistance a la Elemental Precision or that early Shadow talent for priests.

Other changes could include rebalancing Elemental Fury to be a 5-point talent like Frost mages get and having that 21-point slot be something else (e.g., the water shield talent or move Elemental Mastery up and invent a new 31-point talent).

I agree that getting cast time reduction so late is odd, but I'm guessing it's that deep to make Enhancement and Resto hybrid specs more difficult to turn into dps-lite type things. I think it's possible to meet somewhere in the middle.

Reverberation seems more like something Enhancement would naturally benefit from relatively early in their tree.

I think those are most of my random thoughts from over the years (about Elemental, anyways).
