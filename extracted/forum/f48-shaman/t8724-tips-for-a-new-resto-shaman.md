# Tips for a new resto shaman

## Post 56102 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-08-07T08:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56102#p56102 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi!

Respecced to resto shaman around a week ago, didn't play healer before.
Checked some guides and I have a general grasp about how to play it but having some questions.
If you have any idea about these topics, please help me out.

 1. Warlock in the party
I have a hard time with warlocks, they die much more frequently than anyone else in my groups.
My problem is that they always lifetap to barely any health and one punch usually kills them from there.
If I can I heal them up but during a fight I usually spam heal tank or prioritize melee DPS chars.
My other problem is I never know if they being attacked or they just started lifetapping mid fight.
I usually realize the attack too late and don't have the time to quick heal them.
Do you have any tips regarding warlock healing?

 2. Warlock and soul stone
I usually have ankh. I know SS can give more mana and HP upon resurrection but should I still ask for SS for added mana and + healing spells or should I ask the warlock to give SS to someone else so that 2 guys can be rezzed if needed?

 3. Lesser Healing Wave
Most guides state it is for PVP only as an emergency healing tool. I still use it for emergency heals in dungeons because nature swiftness has a large cooldown and sometimes I don't have the time to use healing wave. Is it a skill issue or lesser healing wave for emergency scenarios is justified in partys?

 4. Hex as crowd control
It transforms the target for only 4 sec. I keep it on my action tab as an emergency tool but mostly because I love the spell. Do you use hex if a pull is too big?

 5. Totem usage
My totem selection somewhat changes based on comp and dungeon but I have a select few from each element - except fire. Searing totem can be okay but if there's a danger of pull, I skip it. Fire resistence is way too situational. Do you have a fire totem which you frequently use?

 6. Damage dealing
Most non-shaman healers I saw did some damage between heals using wand. As a shaman I don't have wands and I'm in a dilemma regarding fights. Damage spells like lightning bolt eat up too much mana while going there and punching can be risky. Are you using a spell to contribute the party's damage output or just stand still between heals?

 7. Effective mana restoration
If I don't meet a friendly mage, a good chunk of my loot money goes to buy a lot of drinks. Is there a way to circumvent that? I try to be economical with my spells to save mana but I end up using a lot of consumables.

 8. First aid during fight
Is it okay to patch up non-melee dps during a fight to save mana? I know that being hit stops the effect, that's why I'd use the bandages on casters. Is it a viable way to save mana?

That's all, if anyone has a tip, I'd be grateful.

## Post 67001 by Blaketj — 2023-09-26T14:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67001#p67001 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm a little late to the party but maybe you'll still find some use from my 2c.

> **Akos1896 wrote: Mon Aug 07, 2023 8:01 am**
> 1. Warlock in the party

Talk to them and reach some sort of an agreement. If they mercilessly lifetap to fuel some super thirsty destro/demo build in a dungeon that they can't reasonably sustain otherwise, and they absolutely refuse to rotate say life drain, first aid or less demanding spells to balance it out, and if they drain your mana to the point of endangering the tank and the group as a whole? Let them die.

> **Akos1896 wrote: Mon Aug 07, 2023 8:01 am**
> 2. Warlock and soul stone

Ankh costs money and it's there if the SS runs out and no one notices. No reason it shouldn't be on the main (only) healer at all times.

> **Akos1896 wrote: Mon Aug 07, 2023 8:01 am**
> 3. Lesser Healing Wave

If you can save the party? Yes, it's absolutely viable. Should it be used over the significantly cheaper cookie cutter lower rank healing spam that's aimed for sustained mana conservation, proccing certain effects or at least crits? I wouldn't but if you can make it work that's your choice.

> **Akos1896 wrote: Mon Aug 07, 2023 8:01 am**
> 4. Hex as crowd control

I'd use it as a pure panic button if you personally take aggro. It's far too short a duration to work as a reliable cc.

> **Akos1896 wrote: Mon Aug 07, 2023 8:01 am**
> 5. Fire Totem usage

There's Flametongue.

> **Akos1896 wrote: Mon Aug 07, 2023 8:01 am**
> 6. Damage dealing

Do you want additional aggro? Do you want to stand in melee range wearing your cloth healgear set? If you're going as healer do your job man. Of course if you're going as DPS nothing wrong with helping your healer out with a spell or two if you see them getting overwhelmed.

> **Akos1896 wrote: Mon Aug 07, 2023 8:01 am**
> 7. Effective mana restoration

Work on your effective mana usage first and foremost. If 2 rank 1 single heals can keep the tank topped up no need to use one rank 4 spells for several times the cost. Stand in your Mana Spring Totem, have appropriate gear. Bottom line is that if the tank is taking damage you're going to use your mana but unless it's a borderline boost and the tank is a God walking among mortals you're going to have to sit down and have a drink after most harder encounters.

> **Akos1896 wrote: Mon Aug 07, 2023 8:01 am**
> 8. First aid during fight

Why wouldn't it be? It would be silly to run up front and start healing with an effect that breaks on damage or to expose yourself to damage you wouldn't receive otherwise but there's nothing wrong with reducing downtime with FA.
