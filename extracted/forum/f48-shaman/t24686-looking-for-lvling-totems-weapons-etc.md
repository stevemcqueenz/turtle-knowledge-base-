## Post 174099 by Riiya — 2026-04-05T20:29:00Z

Yes mana deficit is very real for early Shamans, but it gets easier with Water Shield. Also, yes there's a distinct point where 2H damage becomes much better, but I found myself and other shamans prefer 1H + shield for soloing all the way up to 60. The better damage just does not compensate the time lost for getting mana back which can be used for shock damages, and the risk for going low on hp.

For the same reason, you will find the Water Shield helps you solo faster than Lightning Shield or Earth Shield. (However, test it for yourself because the Water Shield was nerfed slightly in 1.18.1)

Searing totem is very nice in itself. It's mana efficient, it's off your other CDs, and since shamans land big attacks than dots, small damages from Searing Totem helps finishing off mobs with low hp. However, the con is more about the management. Shamans tend to finish fights quickly, so you'd move on to new targets before you reap the benefits. So It's nice if you can manage it, but it's also not a must.
