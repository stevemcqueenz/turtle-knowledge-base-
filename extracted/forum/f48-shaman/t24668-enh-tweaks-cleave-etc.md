# Enh Tweaks (Cleave, etc.)

## Post 173678 by Phx — 2026-04-01T23:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173678#p173678 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Enh Shaman remain relatively weak in the grand scheme of DPS'ers, particularly on Nordanaar with late-game scaling. They received some minor tweaks in 1.18.1 in the form of a flat % buff to WF as well as random bursts of haste from Elemental Weapons, so time will tell if this is enough to make them really feel relevant.

Besides that, they remain one of the only DPS without real AoE. There's no scaling on your Fire totems unless you're Elemental or some Spellhance hybrid, which generally sacrifices single target (or should vs. pure Enh).

A simple solution would be another tweak to Elemental Weapons to allow Windfury to do a % of it's damage as cleave damage. That talent has seen major changes every patch so it's no surprise if it gets more changes/additions at this point.

Another issue is that your damage output doesn't match the threat output. In other words, you're doing too much threat for your DPS; it's not difficult to pull threat even if you're, say, not even top 3 on the meter. Just quickly comparing the threat reduction talents to their Ret counterparts, they appear to have an additional 5% threat reduction on top of the fact ours doesn't include Earth Shock.
