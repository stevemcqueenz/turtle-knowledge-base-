# CC2 has been great for Shaman! ^.^

## Post 108342 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-11-17T18:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108342#p108342 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Preface: Don't really know/care about Raiding tbh

But from a PVP perspective -- CC2 has been **great** for Shaman!
Well done!

Elemental was somewhat overpowered...
And also boring to play (spam Lightning Bolts).
This was successfully addressed via various new hybrid spec / gameplay options.

Enhancement simply hit for rather lame, weak-sauce damage (if you played my favorite: Tank/Resto combo)

or it was a gamblers meme spec of Windfury 2hander (terribly unreliable)

or it was just 'Elemental Lite -- where you aim for huge Shock crits to win'

This was all successfully addressed.
You are now properly a 'scrappy jack of all trades brawler with spells'.

Deep Resto is now viable (at least in AV) as you can be a proper Chain Heal master.
And you save key targets with 'Spirit Link'. Probably great in Blood Ring.
Mana Tide Totem was GOD AWFUL and unplayable in PVP.

P.S. I often mocked "Lightning Strike" as "Holy Strike Jr.'
This was unwarranted.

First of all, Lightning Strike doesnt hit nearly as hard (wise)
Second of all, you have to spend Talent Points to get it (wise)
Thirdly, theres actually counter-play options for enemies (they can dispel the Shield buff -- neutering the attack)
Fourthly, it feels fun to use. Sounds and looks cool. Shaman vibes are strong.
Fifthly, the longer lasting Shield buffs/orbs are a great quality of life change.

Thunderhead, i loved thee but this new shit is actually incredibly well designed. I'm impressed.

P.P.S Purge is still overpowered however and should be nerfed.
