Greetings, turtles,

With the release of Ambershire and the recent class changes, I see renewed interest in a list of Elemental Shaman BiS gear you can acquire before Molten Core and Onyxia are released.

I made this list for myself some months ago, back when South Seas was in phase 1. I felt it was necessary after I saw immense potential in some alternative gear added by the four Turtle-exclusive late game dungeons. Moreover, a lot of content normally kept for later phases is already released on phase 1 in Turtle (Dire Maul, for instance). And I practically had to browse all Atlas-Loot tabs for the new crafting recipes too. Recently, I've upated it with the Scars of the Past (1.18) content so I could share it here.

First option in each equipment slot is just my personal pick, based on my experience as main Elemental through the first few phases.

You are all more than invited to suggest changes and additions!

I've avoided/given up including PvP stuff there, but it is an alternative to a spell crit% or a spell hit% ring.

It does apply to all five schools of magic. I don't have a source for any of this but what people say is that non-immune bosses have 15 default nature res, and reducing it affects the chance to land spells. Here in Turtle, it also helps with glancing (half-resisted) hits. This is something you need up to the point Lower Karazhan enchants are available or you have a Thunderfury tank.

The other accessible PvP item that you guys might want to take a look at is Arathi Basin's Mindfang, which I see as the BiS one-handed weapon up to Nefarian's Lok'amir.
