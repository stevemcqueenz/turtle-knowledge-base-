Amanagor has a point in a sense. Most guilds want shamans but they'd prefer said shaman to be resto. Still, you can easily find raid spots as ele or enh.
Usual resto setup is 0 8 43. Get ancestral knowledge and stable shields from enh (stable shields synergize with improved water shield talent).
At the resto tree, pick most things, what 5tp to leave empty is mostly your personal decision. F ex I'd skip nature's grace and improved reincarnation. After CC2 overaggroing as a healer should be really rare with the buffed tanks imo and improved reincarnation is only really helpful if you plan dying way too often.
