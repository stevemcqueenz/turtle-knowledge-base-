## Post 174001 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-04-04T15:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174001#p174001 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Firstly let me start with the most important thing:
Every single class and every single specc is powerful enough to beat all the content the game throws at you. So if you want to play elemental shaman, you can certainly do so. Doubly so on shaman. We are always in demand and almost no raid ever said "damn bro we have too many shamans"

That being said, elemental unfortunately is a very weak spec in raid environments. Most casters have support from curse of elements and curse of shadow. Only elemental shaman and to an extent boomkin lack this support and are worse in raid dps for that reason alone.
Even with that downside, in aoe encounters, we can mostly hold our own. But when it comes to single target dps, you will never be in the top 10 if your raid knows what they are doing.

So if your personal performance compared to your raid is important for you, save yourself some frustration and pick another caster. Warlock offers much more output, while mage, particularly arcane offers a way more forgiving rotation that still outperforms shaman in most encounters.

As for gearing, regardless of class, preraid dungeond are never a bad place to start for gear, but particularly on nordrannar, getting into kara10, zg & mc is easy often even in just questing greens. And that gear is much stronger than many dungeon items.

As for what to focus on, in the beginning, you are happy to grab any raid you get an id for. On shaman particularly, you wanna focus on kara10 and zg early on, as kara has plenty of good caster items (minds key especially) and from zg you can get a great cloak and reputation for your shoulder enchants and zanza. Both of which you need in higher raid.

Other than that, you can only enter most raids once a week. Ideally you take all of them, but if you have to choose, pick the highest tier raids if possible.
