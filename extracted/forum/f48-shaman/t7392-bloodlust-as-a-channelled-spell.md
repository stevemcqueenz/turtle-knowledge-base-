# Bloodlust as a Channelled spell

## Post 47056 by Ravenstone (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-22T20:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47056#p47056 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been thinking about Bloodlust as a channeled AoE buff for the party, BUT allowing the Shaman to still be able to move and use melee attacks. Any spells cast would break the channeling. This would firstly dissuade Restos from taking it, but secondly also allow the Shaman themselves to benefit.

The numbers would have to be toned down but I think it would lead to some interesting gameplay as you'd have to think about the timing more, so as not to lose duration on the channel from stray hits.

I don't believe there is anything like this in vanilla, but it isn't too far away from the new Decisive Strike implementation, and is a bit more involved support gameplay wise than simply throwing a buff on someone. Most of all though it just sounds interesting and fun to me.

Thoughts?
