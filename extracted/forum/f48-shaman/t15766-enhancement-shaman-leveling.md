Can't really go that wrong with Shamans as long as you grab both Strikes. It means you have 3 instant attacks by level 30 that all do decent damage (Shock, Lightning Strike and Stormstrike) and since they nerfed the hell out of WF, Flurry and Elemental Weapons, even focusing primarily on the more defensive talents to leverage tanking talents is a decent way forward.
You just need to decide whether you want to rush for Stormstrike first or go into Elemental for Elemental Focus and your 3%  hit first since both are extremely good to have.
I'd personally go the tanking route so I have that option since there's always tanks in demand and the new talents make it way stronger than it used to be with Rockbiter affecting ALL threat now rather than just white damage.
