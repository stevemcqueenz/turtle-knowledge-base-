Hey!
With macros and addons I can't really help. Just uding PFUI which has a 'show totems' element so I always know which totems are up and for how long.
For totems I just group each element to a button and using shift and ctrl for 2ndary and 3tiary totem of that element. It is more flexible.

Can give some tips for leveling though:
 * Rockbiter weapon and earth shock are cool but they give increased threat. If you aggro mobs too often, consider using other shocks, f.ex.
 * From the moment you have WF weapon, a slow 2hander is your friend.
 * Mana will be a problem, only put down totems you really wanna use.

Have fun!
