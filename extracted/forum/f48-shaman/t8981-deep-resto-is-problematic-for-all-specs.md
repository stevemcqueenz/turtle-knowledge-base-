# Deep Resto is problematic for all specs.

## Post 58069 by Healingrain — 2023-08-20T03:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58069#p58069 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The emergent raiding meta talent build of Bloodlust+Resto showcases a serious problem. Deep Resto builds are so lacking in impact that we can afford to effectively dump 30 points of filler for one talent, a talent likely intended to increase the attractiveness of Enhancement DPS to a raid group. The design space of deep Elemental and Enhancement talents that increase their raid viability will forever be limited by the fact Resto Shamans are relatively talent independent until it's changed.

Deep Resto is only attractive for healing at the 5 man level as it augments Healing Wave. Shamans do not utilize Healing Wave in raids on the overwhelming majority of encounters because their action economy is better spent on the overwhelmingly powerful and unique Chain Heal. It's just better to let Shamans stick in a raid healing lane and have other healers spend action economy on single target tank heals instead where possible. The most powerful buffs to Chain Heal lie not in the talent tree, but on 3pc T2 (a set so notoriously busted and scaling so well it was nerfed in the Sunwell patch) and 5pc T2.5.

I do not want Resto Shaman buffs, they're already extremely valuable in raid. My suggestion is simple, redistribute the very powerful T2 3pc set bonus to a new 25pt or even 30pt talent in Restoration. 20% can go to this new talent and the T2 3pc can be nerfed to only 10% so it's still 30% with the 3pc. It would be trivial to get rid of a few points of bloat in the tree to compensate for this as well, dropping five pointers to three or what have you.

This means deep Elemental and Enhancement builds get to keep their things and it makes deep restoration feel distinctly powerful. It also means we do not strictly NEED to farm BWL and other T2 bosses to really come online as an endgame healer and opens itemization in a number of slots. T2 3pc can also be a real itemization choice to utilise or not.
