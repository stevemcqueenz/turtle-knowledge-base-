# 'Frost Shock' Should Affect 'Raid Bosses'

## Post 160218 by Avalok — 2025-11-21T11:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160218#p160218 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The Shaman's Frost Shock debuff should affect Raid Bosses,
but with little to no movement speed reduction effect on them for balancing reasons.

This way, the shaman's "Frostbrand" imbue can always critically hit Raid Bosses more often if she has the
"Elemental Weapons" talent with her. The said talent allows Frostbrand imbue to always critically hit so long as the taget is under the Frost Shock debuff. This makes the Frost Shock + Frostbrand combo for Enhancement Shamans more viable in raids, specifically against Raid Bosses.

This also makes it more 'mana conservative' for Enhancement Shamans when fighting against Raid Bosses, due to the critical hits of the Frostbrand imbue activating their "Elemental Focus" talent, which gives the Shaman two (2) stacks of clearcasting buffs that reduce their spells and abilities' mana costs by 60%.
