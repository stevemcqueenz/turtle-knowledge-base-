# Clearcasting Talent Affectation

## Post 126836 by Avalok — 2025-04-15T07:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126836#p126836 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Clearcasting talent of Shamans now apply to abilities, and not just spells. This means that melee abilities that do damage are now affected by the Clearcasting buff, such as Storm Strike and Lightning Strike.**

I do not admire this new implementation, simply because the melee abilities mentioned to be affected by Clearcasting have no practical or reasonable benefit. the melee damaging abilities of Shamans have little requirement for mana as opposed to any damaging spells they have. This is especially sensible as an Enhancement Shaman, for one of their main damage and threat generating outputs come from Shock spells, which are of high mana requirement. If the damaging melee abilities were to expend a charge of Clearcasting, it would really be a waste if they weren't instead be expended on shock spells.

I would like to suggest now that The Clearcasting buff of Shamans be reverted back to its older version of just affecting spells instead, and to not affect melee abilities as well.

On that note, I would like to say that I am happy with an adjustment they made on Shaman Clearcasting regarding the percentage of mana reduction it has. It has now been increased to 60% as opposed to the earlier amount of only 40%. This change makes it feel more delightful to play as an Enhancement Shaman.
