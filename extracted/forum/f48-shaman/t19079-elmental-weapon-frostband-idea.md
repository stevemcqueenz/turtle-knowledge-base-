## Post 129702 by Laysson (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-05T06:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129702#p129702 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really like the new talent rework and the gameplay possibility with frost shock and frostband combination, to change from the windfury gameplay, but this have some big weaknesses :

 - It doesn't benefit from the 20% StormStrike buff
 - If your FS gets resisted, you suffer a huge dps loss
 - A lot of mobs (like bosses) are immune to the debuff

Solution :

 - Make apply a guaranteed debuff that ignore immunities (like Survival Hunter Stining Nettle talent) so that it work on immune mobs and bosses

## Post 137278 by Markka — 2025-06-27T21:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137278#p137278 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In addition to many mobs being downright immune to the Frost Shock effect, which completely gimps the damage of Frostbrand, it is also anti-synergistic with certain classes and specs which makes it worse in group play.

Melee hunters that spam Wing Clip, or frost mages that inflict a slow effect either block Frost Shock from being applied, or even downright override the effect which completely cripples your damage. I have already had to ask people to not use their slowing abilities just so I can do damage. It's a really bad oversight.

If the current implementation of Elemental Weapons is intended to stay, Frost Shock has to be functional on enemies that aren't immune to frost, even if it doesn't slow them. Frost Shock also must not get blocked or knocked off by a similar or stronger effect. Alternatively, it would have to be reworked a bit like some of the earlier suggestions.

Also, it is strange that Frostbrand does not override Flametongue Totem and Windfury Totem effects without there being any mention of that in the tooltip(s). I appreciate that I can actually make use of 50% of my totems, but why do the other weapon imbues not get the same treatment?
