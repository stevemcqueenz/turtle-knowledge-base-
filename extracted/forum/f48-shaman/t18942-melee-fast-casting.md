i really liked the enha shaman in wotlk. with all those maelstrom stacks and instant casts. it just fits thematically. hand-to-hand combat and quick lightning casting.

which leads me to the idea of ​​​​somehow doing it here. of course without copy-paste and a lot of changes. I know that there is a possibility for that right now but somehow a lot is lost on both sides, on ele and enha to achieve that. and still i does not fulfill the class fantasy.

i'm really interested in your thoughts on this idea.
