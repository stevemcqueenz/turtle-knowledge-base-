# Yet another shaman suggestion post

## Post 84707 by Blumtoff — 2024-01-14T17:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84707#p84707 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey hey,

I’ve been thinking a lot about shamans and tanking, and I’d love it if they were more accepted and had a better toolkit. I came up with a few ideas that I think are viable and wanted to know what you all thought.

First change would be to alter the “spirit shield” talent in the 5th line of the enhancement tree. It could be something along the lines of increase armor from shield by 10/20/30% and increase threat generation from all sources by 15/30/45% (insert appropriate percentage here, that was just a suggestion) while a shield is equipped”. This would add on to earth shocks and rockbiters already high threat multipliers, making them more effective without changing the gameplay much. Bonus points if it swaps places in the tree with ancestral guidance to ensure the toolkit starts rounding out earlier.

The other significant change would be to separate earth shocks cooldown from the other shocks. With the increased threat from the previous suggestion it would effectively become our taunt for adds or when we lose threat, while frost and flame shock, along with smacking things with rock biter, should generate enough threat to hold aggro once we have it.

For aoe threat (this is much less thought out than the others) there’s been a lot of talk about making all totems redirect threat to the shamen, but I think would change other specs threat too much. So I propose a talent that increases only stoneclaws threat generation (if necessary) and redirects it to the shamen, leaving all the others as they are. You could keep the cool down but make it last only 10 seconds or so for new packs of mobs or for when things start getting messy.

A final change is less groundbreaking but I think would add to the toolkit a bit. That would be to change the talent improved lightning shield to add 1/2/3 orbs as well as the increased damage, meaning we cast it less so conserve mana and maybe get the mp5 for once, and helps add a little extra threat to larger pulls.

Just some thoughts, I think the first two are pretty solid but I’m not precious about any of them. Let me know what you think!

Those are not bad ideas, but all angles must be looked at when implementing ideas such as these.

Increased threat gen from Spirit Shield is not a bad idea.  Rounding out toolkit too early is not really necessary as in the lower gearing brackets, threat from Rockbiter and Earth Shock should be sufficient to hold agro

Separating Earth Shock into separate cooldown would likely cause PvP balance issues, as this increases shaman's already significant burst damage, especially vs clothies.

Totems producing threat for the shaman seems like a great idea, especially if it was linked to a "tanking" talent.  However, I'm not sure if this is an easy thing to implement on the client side.  All totems appear to be separate entities, like pets and they don't' transfer threat to owner.  It's a wonderful idea though if it were possible.

I like the Lightning Shield suggestion.  It's a highly underutilized shield at level 60, and it would be wonderful if there was a way to offset it's mana cost some way.  I heard it produces on average about 80 tps, and that's nothing to sneeze at.  However, in it's current itteration, it's mana prohibitive to use comfortably.   And since we already have to manage mana, I can't justify using it instead of Water Shield or Earth Shield.

## Post 84737 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-14T23:17:00Z

I've written about this earlier but I think that with some minor tweaks shaman tanking could be possible.
Shaman's enh tree would look like druid's feral tree, meaning that melee dps and tank should share the same tree.
Imo, these are the biggest problems with the shaman tanks today:
 * No aoe threat
 * No taunt, only high-threat single target spell
 * Bad scaling of rockbiter
 * Kinda squishy in mail
 * They need too many different stats (int, agi, stamina, strength) and gearing up is hard
 * No real CC (frost shock and earthbind totem sound cool but try to use them as CCs if you pulled an additional pack and 'the house is burning')

Some of these problems can easily be solved based on others' suggestions:
 * A final level of earthshock could be introduced which has a taunt ability.
 * Rockbiter threat-scaling could be re-checked and modified.
 * Shield block value can be further augmented via talents. Something has to give armor to shaman tanks and if we just generally buff shaman armors via talents (even further), shamans would just have plate without calling them plate-wearers. I'd make them more resilient with an emphasis on their shield.

Since melee dps is at the same tree (as tank shaman in theory is), sharing their biggest problems, too:
 * Hard to get reasonable hit chance, too many white hits
 * No real CC
 * Low DPS
 * They need too many different stats, hard to gear

There are some overlaps here. Enh tree should answer the problems of lacking CC (or shamans in general should receive a CC).
Also, shaman sets should not include spirit on them because no shaman talent synergizes with it. Int and stamina are always on them, an agility or strength (it sometimes appears) could actually help shamans.

For overlapping problems, I'd add a melee aoe ability in the enh tree, just like whirlwind for warriors.
For melee dps gameplay can become more diverse -> +1 tool to handle enemies.
For tanks it can be an aoe threat option since weapon usually has rockbiter on.

Melee DPS ideas mostly ->

Moving nature's guidance talent to enh would also help a lot. If we also make it a 5 part talents resulting in a 5% hit chance bonus, it is a huge advancement.
Buffing weapon mastery from 2-10% to 3-15% for added damage.

These two changes could make enh DPS a better damage dealer and a more rewarding experience. However, this is not strictly related to tanking.

Melee DPS ideas end.

Lastly I'd change stoneclaw totem, making it invulnerable for 3 sec after which it disappears (on a CD, of course). Could be a tanking utility and also a pseudo-CC.
