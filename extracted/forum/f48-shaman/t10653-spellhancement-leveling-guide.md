# Spellhancement Leveling Guide

## Post 69447 by Hartmut — 2023-10-07T20:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69447#p69447 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello fellow Lovers of the Elements,

this guide covers most of what you need to know about the most efficient and fun way to level your Shaman on Turtle-WoW Servers! First of all, why Spellhancement?

The advantages over traditional Enhancement Shamans are as follows:
 - Way better use of the new talent Elemental Focus
 - Amazing synergy between the Talents Elemental Focus, Elemental Devastation, Thunderung Strikes, Flurry, Stormstrike and Elemental Fury
 - Better survivability

The advantages over taditional Elemental Shaman:
 - Way Way better mana efficiency
 - The possibility to tank dungeons

The Spellhancement Build focuses more on the Spelldamage side of the Shaman while still engaging in melee combat.

 **Talents in Order**:
Level 10-20: 5/5 Convection -> 5/5 Concussion -> Elemental Focus
The Points in Concussion can also go into any of the Row 2 Talents, its not as important, depends on your taste and playstyle. Elemental Focus is the most powerful leveling talent for the Shaman on Turtle-WoW so get it ASAP.

Level 21-30: 5/5 Shield Specialization -> 2/2 Improved Ghost Wolf -> 3/5 Thundering Strikes
Improved Ghost Wolf is a very Powerful Mobility Talent. Be aware of the 5-second-rule when using Ghost Wolf. Once Thundering Strikes is taken, the Synergy with Elemental Focus starts to kick in since it also procs from Melee Attacks.

Level 31-41: 5/5 Thundering Strikes -> 3/3 Improved Lighting Shield -> 1/1 Thunderhead -> 4/5 Flurry -> 1/1 Stormstrike
Lighting Shield in combination with Thunderhead improves the damage per mana value of Lightning Shield a strong margin. Flurry again synergyzed very well with Thundering Strikes and Elemental Focus. Stormstrike gives free Clearcasting Chances as well as a damageboost to your clearcasted Earthshocks. You can also take Ancestral Guardian over the Lightning Shield Talents if you decide to tank dungeons more frequently.

Level 42-51: 5/5 Call of Thunder -> 3/3 Elemental Devastation -> 1/3 Elemental Warding -> Elemental Fury
With Call of Thunder you improve your DPS as well as the Chance to proc Elemental Devastation on the Lighting Bolt pull. Elemental Fury completes your Talent Synergy while boosting the Crit Damage of your spells as well as FIre Totems and Flametongue Weapon.

From there on the Talents are really up to your own personal preference. You can go down in Enhancement for a bit more Flametonuge and Melee Damage or go down in Resto for the 3% Spell and Melee Hit.

 **Basic Rotation**:
Before the pull make sure you have Lightning Shield up and Flametongue on your weapon. Flametongue is the best Imbue by a long shot because it increases your Chance to Proc Elemental Focus while providing solid DPS and it can also proc Elemental Devastation which is vital to your talent synergys. You Start the fight while using max rank Lightning Bolt to pull the mob. After that you drop a Searing Totem, use Stormstrike and cast Earthshock as soon as you get a clearcasting proc. You will leave the fight with a clearcasting proc a lot of the times, so be aware of that and use it for your next Lightning Bolt pull if possible.

 **Gearing**:
All stats are usefull to you, but there are some rules you should follow: use a fast Onehanded Weapon and a Shield to proc Elemental Focus as often as possible as well as increasing your survivability by a strong margin. Agility increases your Flurry and Clearcasting uptime which makes it a very strong choice while leveling.
Try to save some Money before you reach Level 21, you will unlock a very strong tool at that point. In Sparkwaterport there is a vendor that will sell you the Thunder Lizards Hide for just under 8 Gold which is a big investment at this point. But a very worthy one. The Cloak provides you with a Lightning Damage proc on every Melee Swing which can also proc Elemental Focus for you.

After this brief explanation I hope I covered most of the information that you might need, otherwise feel free to ask away below. I'm looking forward to your suggestions and will keep adding to and improving this guide.

Cheers,
Hartmut

I leveled 1-60 with this build (on turtle mode) and here are my findings:

 -Get as many items with +3 Nature damage (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=166150>) and +2 Fire damage (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=7711>) effects as possible. Early options are Flameskin Gloves (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81267>), Leggings of Fiery Temper (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61505>), Thunder Lizard's Hide (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81308>), Lavadrenched Chainmail (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80704> - you can solo RFC at 40 when you're able to wear mail) and Repaired Electro-Lantern (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=65030> - I recommend shield over this one if you have at least 2-3 other effects). Later on get ring from Gilneas and craft Storm Gauntlets, and farm the Incendosaur Set.

These will proc your clearcasting so you can shock away at a discount. Other than that Improved Lightning Shield (3/3) and Thunderhead are an awesome source of passive damage with unbeatable DpM (damage per mana).
Stormstrike is absolutely useless with this build if you have even ONE +3 Nature damage item, because it eats your stacks. I really hope they fix that. Also it's way too expensive mana-wise and doesn't do anything with a fast 1h weapon. I'd say go for Elemental Devastation (3/3) and Elemental Fury before going into the Enhancement tree for Flurry (5/5). After you got everything (at lvl 50+), you can get Nature's Guidance (3/3) from the resto tree for the hit rating, it's huge.
Also always use Windfury over Flametongue - it's just better DPS-wise and gives you more procs from on-hit effects.

Gear-wise I went more heavily into spell gear. Melee gear could be viable too, I don't know. But spellpower and especially spell crit improves a lot of things in this build.
For weapons, ideally try to get 1.30 speed weapons whenever possible (filter daggers and fist weapons in the database by speed). Try not to get anything above 1.50. An honorable mention is Satyr Claw (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80834>) - the fastest weapon ingame with 1.20.
At 57 you have the option of getting Hand of Edward the Odd (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=2243>) for around 300g from AH. Especially if you're going for the spellpower route, DO IT! It procs nearly every fight, sometimes twice or thrice in one fight. You can cast instant lightning bolts, chain lightning, phat heals that crit for more than your entire HP pool, or even insta-cast Astral Recall (lol, get that, bubble-hearthing paladins!).

The other weapon choice at 60 is Blade of Eternal Darkness (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17780>), you can farm it from Mara final boss with 1% drop chance if you really, really hate yourself. I procs like crazy from all the on-hit effects we've stacked on our gear, and you basically become an auto-attacking damage machine. Druids usually do this build, and I think it's rather boring (not to mention suicidally boring to farm), so I stick with my Edward mace.
The other option is Ornate Bloodstone Dagger (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=65004>), crafted from a bunch of arcanite and usually going for 900-1000g on AH, its proc is similar to Blade of Eternal Darkness, but instead of giving you infinite mana, it will kill you. Use at your own risk.

The blue dragonscale set (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20295> - set bonuses aren't updated in the DB, link it ingame to see them) is pretty good and somewhat affordable if you can snipe tons of blue dragonscales off AH at good prices. Has tons of spell damage and intellect, but no stamina, so you become somewhat of a glass cannon. Its 4-piece bonus SOUNDS like the effect of Blade of Eternal Darkness, but it DOESN'T (as of this patch) proc from on-hit effects like the dagger. There is an open github issue about it, go pester the devs for me please.
Still, the set is really good for starting out your max level journey, and the effect still procs from your shocks and (insta-) lightning spells.
At 60, get Coif of Elemental Fury (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=21804>), it's really good and dirt-cheap on AH! You could also get Earthfury Bracers and Belt, they're usually pretty affordable.

Black Grasp of the Destroyer (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=22194>) is interesting for its mana-stealing effect and it does add a bit of sustain to your manabar, but I don't know if it's worth replacing an on-hit effect (like Storm Gauntlets) with it.

At max level, this build is really interesting and fun to play, even if it's not on-par with the meta specs in terms of damage. I'm having a blast farming and questing with the insta-cast procs from my mace like I was a shaman from WotLK :D
Needless to say, get PowerAuras to track the insta-cast procs and your clearcasting stacks (and if you're missing a shield buff and weapon enchant buff).

I went all "Gotta catch them all!" mode, surely you don't need them all. I'm just listing options for people to choose from. However, there IS a real benefit from stacking them all, the first of all being that you can drop Flametongue in favor of Windfury for greater DPS (yes, even with fast weapons), the second is that these 2-3 damage per hit really stack up and start doing a respectable percent of your overall DPS (and they apply to the extra hits from WF and SS too).
