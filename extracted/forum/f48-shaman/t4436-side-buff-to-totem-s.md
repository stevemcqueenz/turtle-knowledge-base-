# Side buff to Totem's

## Post 26496 by Klouther — 2022-09-30T13:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26496#p26496 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Recently I started levelling a shaman after casually playing a pally for quite a few months and the first thing that I found problematic was the Totem System.

Totem's ought to be the counterpart for paladin Blessing's yet they are quite inferior to them: you need to do a few questchains *to get all the basic totems and the reagent to use them*, their uptime cost is far higher and depends on positioning, resistance totem's use slots for WFT, STR, AGI or healing totems making them less desirable to use and the base range of the totem's is pretty limiting on itself.

So here's my proposal; change the totems(the item's you need to carry around to cast Totem's) to grant self buffs.

Earth Totem-> Increase armor and resistance contribution from your gear by 5%.
Fire Totem-> Your auto attacks, Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning deal 10% of your stamina as extra fire damage.
Water Totem-> You receive 5% of your spirit as hp+5 & mp+5.
Air Totem-> Increases your hit and spell hit by 2%.

These are just suggestion from someone with only leveling experience as a clasisc shaman so far so feel free to point out better alternatives or ones with a better "classic feeling". This post is mainly to highlight a possible path to buff the base strenght of shamans without the need of a patch.

The pros-> my proposed change would be an item database change which is quicker to do from a dev standpoint and easier to tune(or scrap), players will get something useful of the totem's taking bag space instead of it being a reagent you need to drop Totem's and therefore provide utility for yourself or group, it would make completing the totem questchain feel more rewarding instead of "now you can use half your skillset", possible addition of PR elements.

The cons-> if implemented, it would add another layer to balancing the class, it might upsept PvP balance, the buffs might appear too weak/strong and players will probably whine about it and ask to buff/nerf it and take away dev's time, other classes might feel entiltled to ask for something similar

While I am not a big fan of the specific suggested changes, I do agree that totems as a counterpart of blessings and paladin auras, are inferior at least in some ways.

Considering totems' stationary, range and duration conditions, they should be stronger buffs in most aspects compared to blessings, who do not share any of those limiting factors (to the same extend anyway). In their current state totems are weaker in almost every aspect in cases where paladins have a blessing or aura, which does the same thing.

Totems do have one thing going for them, which are unique effects like those from Windfury, Grounding, Cleansing Poison/Disease, Earthbind & Healing Stream on top of my head.
