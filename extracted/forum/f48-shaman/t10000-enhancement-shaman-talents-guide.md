For leveling enhance I would do this <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/shaman/0-5H00Z01>

From there, I suggest going into elemental so you can have cheaper shocks. Take note that elemental focus gives clearcasting on MELEE critical strikes now too. my build for pvp with a slow 1h + shield using windfury and earth shield
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/shaman/0 ... JI-UC3V0O6](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/shaman/0-BH0C5IJI-UC3V0O6)
