# Experiences after leveling shaman on this server

## Post 44792 by Darbo — 2023-05-07T09:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44792#p44792 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just some quick observations and suggestions for the team:

First of all great job shaman is better than on classic. However some things could be tweaked.

Thunderhead talent is useless. When I buff for example a tank with it the agro goes to me. So it really has no use other than wanting that 20% mana cost reduction. If it was change to affect all shields that would be much better. If you removed it I wouldn't notice. I like that I can make shaman more tanky with the new talents. Remove it and add another ghost wolf talent slot for instacast.

Extra shields. Are very good but I would love to have had them at around 20-40 range. At least the mana shield one. If you added them earlier shaman would have a much nicer leveling experience.

All in all it's better in PVP but same-ish in PVE. I like the new zones and quests they really fill out the void in leveling past certain level. There is also much more mp5 gear I noticed and that sure does help with the healing dungeons.
