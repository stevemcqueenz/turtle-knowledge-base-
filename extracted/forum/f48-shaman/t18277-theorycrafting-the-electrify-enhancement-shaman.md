# Theorycrafting: The Electrify Enhancement Shaman

## Post 124808 by Franktank — 2025-04-01T00:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124808#p124808 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey Shamfam,

Been wanting to submit a post on here for a while to discuss some theorycrafting around an Ele/Enh hybrid. I've been messing around over the past month, and here's the build so far (still WIP). Apologies if this is a repost, but I haven't found anything like it yet, and I am not active on the Discord. All negative/positive feedback is greatly appreciated.

The core of this build is a basic Enhancement Shaman while capitalizing on the Electrify talent to further increase crit damage and reduce mana consumption with the constant uptime of Elemental Focus from crits. With the changes the devs have made, it feels like they’re opening up a lane for Shaman to be a viable melee caster. It’s kind of weird, but there is some synergy there.

 **Talents**
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/shaman?p ... AAAAAAAAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/shaman?points=FgAYNQAABAAAAAFAFYIIFALIAoAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA=)

 * 1st tier
 5/5 Convection: Shaman is mana-hungry
 5/5 Concussion: higher damage with CL and LB to build stacks for Electrify
 5/5 Ancestral Knowledge: flat increase to stats

 * 2nd tier
 3/3 Elemental Devastation: +3% hit for raids and +9% spell hit for shocks/lightning
 5/5 Thundering Strikes: higher crit chance = more EF procs/DPS
 3/3 Stable Shield: easier mana sustain with Water Shield OR higher DPS with Lightning Shield. Without Water Shield, mana can be a problem, but since storm and lightning strike work with clear-casting its a bit better.

 * 3rd tier
 1/1 Elemental Focus: This build crits almost constantly, so this is a mandatory talent for mana sustain
 5/5 Reverberation: Shocks are spammed on CD
 1/5 Call of Thunder: burner point for the next tier, though you could swap points from Concussion for higher Lightning crit%—totally up to you.
 1/3 Calming Winds for lower threat OR 1/3 Ancestral Guardian as a filler point. I'd go for less threat when raiding
 1/1 Lightning Strike: more hits for crits + it benefits from weapon/Nature/SP scaling + procs Lightning or Water Shield.

 * 4th tier
 5/5 Flurry: higher attack speed for more crits, procs, and DPS.

 * 5th tier
 1/1 **Electrify**: Like many of you, I scoffed when I first saw this new talent, but at 2 stacks you get +100% crit damage to offensive-spells (Shocks, Frostbrand procs, Lightning spells). Especially fun when Hand of Edward the Odd procs a lot and the RNG gods are in your favor. +10% nature is nice too for earth shocks and lightning spells
 1/2 Enhanced Totems: small stat increase for Air/Earth totems
 3/3 Elemental Weapons: this makes Frostbrand proc A LOT. You could still use WF for extra attacks or against Frost-resistant mobs, but Frostbrand feels more consistent overall right now.
 1/1 Stormstrike: another attack for more procs + 20% increased Nature damage is welcome

 * 6th tier
 5/5 Weapon Mastery: more damage.

Now, you could sacrifice Electrify for Bloodlust for higher group DPS, but that would make this build feel pointless—at that stage, pure Enhancement would be better.

 **Stats**
Currently prioritizing Attack Power > AGI > Spell Power > Hit/Crit% since these all affect our main damage sources:
Auto attacks (AP/Agi)
Shocks (AP/SP)
Frostbrand procs (SP)
Nature spells (SP)

Still testing, but AP seems king after the Shaman rework.

 **Gear**
Running a mix of T1/T2/epics at the moment.
Hand of Edward the Odd was made for this build—fast attacks = more Frostbrand procs + instant CLs. I have mine ench with SP since it'll increase many sources of damage, but crusader is fine too. An off-Hand that provides more SP is nice. With the threat this thing rolls out you could slap on a shield and go OT/bruiser playstyle.
Two-Handers still provide amazing damage. Nightfall works pretty well too since that 10% increase will affect your shocks and frost-brand procs. So many different set-ups here. Cast Rank 1 LB's to keep electrify up.
Haven't experimented with t3 sets yet but hopefully there are some tier set synergies that work with this playstyle.

 **Rotation**
 1. Chain Lightning or Lightning Bolt opener for Electrify stacks. In fast fights, I usually just do one CL and move on. You can also cast 2 Rank 1 LBs to build 2 stacks in 3 seconds as its only 1.5s cast time
 2. Frost Shock for guaranteed Frostbrand crits (or Earth Shock if running WF). Since shocks scale with AP now, it should hit like a truck when it crits.
 3. Spam auto attacks, Lightning Strike, Stormstrike.
 4. If using Hand of Edward the Odd, spam CL when it procs, especially with Electrify & Stormstrike up. Without Hand of Ed, use CL/LB as you see fit between Shock/Strike cooldowns to keep Electrify up.
 5. Keep spamming Shocks/Strikes/CL as they come off cooldown.

 **Cons so far**
 - CL/LB cast time feels awkward mid-fight—if you time it well, it's fine, but still a hassle.
 - Frostbrand is useless against Frost-resistant mobs.
 - Very RNG-dependent—some fights, you top the meters, others, you don’t. Classic Shaman experience.
 - Threat Magnet - beg for salv

Anywho, I’d love to hear all of your guys’ thoughts and input.
