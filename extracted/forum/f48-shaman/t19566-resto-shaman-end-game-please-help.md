I mostly agree but with the caveat that spirit link is best used with some raid experience when you are more comfortable with your positioning.

It has a range of 15 yards and a cast time of 2 sec. In practice, if you are a new healer, you'll probably follow other healers and play from distance to be safe. Totem effect is 30 yards, heals are 40 -> By default you are around 30 yards away from your team to be safe and to also give them totem support as a resto.
The problem is that spirit link is best used on tanks. So you gotta know when to play it not so safe, be 15 yards away from tanks at max and stay there for at least 2 sec for casting the spirit link.
When you just learn the raids you'd find spirit link mostly useless, running with the healers and 'never being able to reach' the ones you wanna reach.
The ability becomes really good the moment you pre-emptively know when to start a fight closer than usual, specially if those fights have heavy damage.
