# Comprehensive DPS test of different weapon imbues

## Post 126159 by Taralej90 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-04-09T11:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126159#p126159 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Test done with pre-raid BiS gear + some epics, using Nightfall as a signature Enh weapon. 308 weapon skill from orc racial + book quest, 31.22% crit, 758 AP (from Ony buff). Only auto-attacks.

 **10 min vs 60lvl dummy:**

 - **Rockbiter** - 178 DPS
 - **Windfury** - 220 DPS
 - **Flametongue** - 178 DPS
 - **Frostbrand** - 178 DPS
 - **Frostbrand + WF totem** - 230 DPS

 **10 min vs Boss dummy:**

 - **Rockbiter** - not tested due to threat issues making it non-viable
 - **Windfury** - 167 DPS
 - **Flametongue** - 138 DPS
 - **Frostbrand** - 151 DPS
 - **Frostbrand + WF totem** - 167 DPS

 **Disclaimer:**

 - FT and FB use spell crit and only crit for 50% dmg
 - FT and FB crits can proc Clearcasting but not Flurry
 - FT and FB are both affected by Nightfall debuff
 - FT can be partially resisted (for 25%, 50% or 75% of the dmg)
 - FB cannot be partially resisted but can be fully resisted
 - FB is the only weapon imbue that interacts with WF totem
 - FT and FB do not scale (Rockbiter also doesn't scale)
 - Effect of Elemental Weapons on FB is unusable because bosses are immune to the effect of Frost Shock
 - Effect of Elemental Weapons on WF diminishes significantly as you gain more weapon skill.
 - WF attacks can be glancing blows. I'm taking 308 (racial + book) as the baseline. Obviously, with more weapon skill (AQ40 gloves) the DPS of WF will go up.
 - Autoattack and WF crits cannot be glancing blows.
 - WF attacks can proc Flurry but do not consume Flurry stacks
 - WF itself does not scale (like Rockbiter), but the 2 extra attacks scale with the rest of your gear
 - During a WF proc, you have a small window of about 1 sec where you gain the increased 323 AP from the WF proc. Any attacks (or last-rank shocks) done during that 1 sec window will benefit from that 323 AP.

Interaction between weapon imbues and "strikes" (Stormstrike and Lightning Strike):

 - FT and FB both proc from strikes. They only proc once from LS. Rockbiter is a flat AP increase, so it affects both strikes significantly, as well as the elemental shield proc of LS (which scales with AP). WF can proc off strikes, but doing so resets the AA timer. Because of this and because of the way WF works (last point in Disclaimer), it is best to delay your strikes for just after your AA.

 **Conclusions:**

 **Windfury** comes out on top in almost every situation. It also scales with the rest of your gear, unlike all other weapon imbues. However, it is also the most severely affected by glancing blows, making the orc racial(+3), skill book(+5) and specific items(+6/7/8) extremely important. Frostbrand being unable to be partially resisted makes it better than Flametongue, but Flametongue is buffed by 45% by T1. Even with that buff and adding Searing Totem into the mix (from Elemental Weapons increasing Fire spell + totem dmg by 30%), Flametongue would be barely equal to Windfury, and only in raids where enemies aren't resistant to Fire dmg. By the time you have full T1, Windfury would just be way too far ahead for Flametongue to catch up. The fact that bosses (and many raid mobs) cannot be affected by Frost Shock debuff means Frostbrand cannot show its true potential in most cases.

As you gain more gear, more crit and more weapon skill, Windfury completely overshadows the other three weapon imbues, mainly due to the fact that they don't scale and only crit for 50% dmg. Should these two things be changed, then Enh Shaman may see usage of FT and FB beyond memes, PvP and leveling.

Rockbiter remains the best weapon imbue for **leveling** due to its consistency and raw output. The 10% dmg reduction from Elemental Weapons is also always active and requires no management or mana spent. Windfury remains the best end-game weapon imbue.
