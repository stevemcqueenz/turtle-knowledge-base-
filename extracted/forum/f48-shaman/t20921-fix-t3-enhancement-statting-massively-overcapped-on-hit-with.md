do you deal with any of the itemization at all? Why do we have 6% hit on our enhance t3? If we were a dual wield spec, it would make sense. But just assuming we’re using t3 and and our talents, no jewelry or trinkets, we’re at 9% hit. Not factoring in weapon skill either. It’s dead weight for us. Most shamans don’t care to go above the 4 set or 2 set bonuses because both the gear and the set bonuses are kinda garbage. (Side note: 6set and 8set bonuses dont work)

Clearing Naxx (endgame minus k40) should reward the best (minus k40) drops available, based on natural raid/itemization progression.  With current statting, Enhancement shamans avoid tier3 mostly. This is flawed design.
