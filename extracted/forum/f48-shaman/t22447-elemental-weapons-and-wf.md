# Elemental Weapons and WF

## Post 155922 by Chanchoberto420 — 2025-10-17T17:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155922#p155922 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The Elemental Weapons talent is one of the most interesting reworks done to the Enhancement talent tree. It sets out to give each weapon imbue bonus features to accomodate for different playstyles and make each one more relevant within the Shamans toolkit.

Nevertheless, the bonus given to Windfury weapon is quite lacking compared to other imbues. Given that they were recent changes to the bonus, with equally lukewarm effects, it seems that the developers are not sure how to provide bonus to an echant that is already very powerful and the main choice for endgame Enhancement DPS.

What do you guys think could be an appropiate bonus for WF?

I got a couple of ideas:

 1) In line with the current bonus, gaining extra attacks increases movement speed AND chance to dodge attacks. This goes well with the wind theme and provides a defensive aspect thats useful for leveling, similar to what RB does with damage reduction.

 2) Extra attacks that crit damage up to 2 nearby enemies. This would give some additional AoE dps which the spec lacks. I also like the whirlwind cleave as part of the wind theme.

 3) If Im allowed to dream: Extra attacks that land have a chance to reduce the casting time for Lighting Bolt and Chain Lightning by 25%, stacking up to 4 times. I love Maelstrom Weapon from later expansions, and would allow Enh Shaman to add those spells to their rotation. Having instant CL would add AoE dps capabilities as well. I would leave the healing spells out as it would be too op in pvp I think.

I would love to hear your ideas about this.

The current bonus isn't movement speed.
It's 20% haste on your next swing if WF attack is dodged, parties or misses.
The talent calculator is wrong and the in-game talent tree states the correct effect.
It's still not a great effect for a DPS, as you shouldn't see parries or misses at all as a DPS but it's fairly good as you level and much better for non-PVP.
