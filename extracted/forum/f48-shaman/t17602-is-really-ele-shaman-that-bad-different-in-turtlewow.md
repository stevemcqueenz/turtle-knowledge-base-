## Post 120206 by Bjorn88 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-02-17T13:46:00Z

Hi, unfortunately you are making a good observation. The "electrify" talent is a significant nerf to ele pvp, particularly when the latest patch also increased the burstiness of other classes, even creating new ones such as the disc priest.

Electrify effectively means you start every combat at 50-66% of your power level. If you are in endgame/tier3 gear you can still manage, however as relatively fresh you will likely struggle to get there at all. I am confident this aspect was not part of the twow devs' calculation when they designed this sad talent.

As you've noticed, Electrify has been one of the most criticized talents since the announcement of CC2. It has already been quasi-buffed once - initially you needed 5 stacks instead of 2 to gain the 100% boost. But there seems to be a lot of pride in CC2 so I doubt we will ever see the needed reversal back to Elemental Fury.

Ele is in an interesting spot. You still got a bunch of utility + for a caster, you can get disgusting amount of armor.
BUT
Class changes made many other builds more bursty while ele became less bursty. It's still kinda bursty but they are nerfed in this department.

Earlier you had the 100% crit damage upon crit as a passive.
Now your landing (!) bolts and chain ligtnings build up up to a stack of 2 (shocks don't), each one adding 50%. At the bright side you get some passive damage increase with the stacks (up to 10%).
But this means les burstiness. You running around, seeing a guy and starting to attack means no instant giga-crits.
But you still have totem support, heals, big armor, 5-6 sec CD interrupt, slow and purge.
