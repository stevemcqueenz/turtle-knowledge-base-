## Post 154870 by Chanchoberto420 — 2025-10-08T16:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154870#p154870 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I wanted to suggest a couple of things regarding mana efficiency. Im currently leveling an Enchancement Shaman. I specced for Elemental Focus talent, which I think is vital for mana efficiency.

First I think it would be good if Lightning and Earth Shield would benefit from Clearcasting. Elemental Focus improves our mana efficiency greatly but refreshing Lightning Shield is expensive. It would then become a choice for the player if they want to expend a proc of Clearcasting by casting the shield.

Second, I feel that the mana restored by Lightning Strike while using Water Shield to be very low. The only time I see a considerable mana increase is when using Rockbiter imbue and casting Lightning Strike with a Clearcasting proc. It would be nice if the mana restored by default covered the mana cost of Lightning Strike on top of the mana recovered by the orb. Maybe at endgame it scales better, but while leveling it seems like we only get a free strike that does like 50% weapon damage.

What does everyone think about this?

'Tank shaman is the least role with mana issues, since Mana Shield pops on incoming hits'

I have to disagree there.
The 'shaman mana engine' is improved water shield talent + stable shield talents.
Only resto can reach both, which gives them a lot of passive mana regen:
 9mp5 + 18% full mana regen while casting if all 9 orbs are active.
Plus healers downgrade spells all the time.
Resto is actually in a pretty good place with mana.

Enh DPS and Enh Tank are super JoW-dependent.
LS with water shield on is kinda bad even if it is scaling. Passive water shield orb procs are a nice supplementary but can't carry mana regen.
Played both pala tank and shaman tank. While both use mana, shaman tank mana management is infinitely worse. It has many reasons:
 * BoW is superior to mana totem. Mana totem kinda has to be spammed, not cheap, and it is below max rank BoW in mana regen if I remember right.
 * Water Shield LS is not comparable to JoW.
 * Water Shield passive is somewhat comparable to block mana regen pala talent.
 * Shammie tanks spams usually max rank abilities for rotation. Palas have to spam less abilities for the rotation (consec is area-based DoT, holy shield and holy strike is not comparable to constant SS, ES, LS spam + totem upkeep).

It is not an anti-pala post, just showing how a build with bigger mana spendings have worse mana maintenance, causing problems unless JoW is maintained by someone.

Enh DPS is in an even worse spot because optimally their rotation requires lightning shield and LS through that, saying no to mana regen.

Ele can in theory get either improver water shield or stable shields but not both.
Improved water shield alone is not a good mana regen talent, because it really scales with the number of shield orbs you have. If it is kept at 3, it won't help that much.
Also, the talent combination would be less effective with melees (orbs more likely to be consumed and on avreage enh has less spirit from gear). But still, it alongside mana totem, and LS would do the trick.

Enh DPS is a bit peculiar, but they had the option to switch between resource management mode (water shield) and DPS mode (lightning shield) so I think it's fair.
It would also allow enh tank to go for other shields more often, allowing DPS spiked (LS) or a more defensive setup (ES).
_________

I'm arguing for making improved water shield more accessible, because (unless we retweak a bunch of other stuffs), the shaman mana engine come from the combination of that talent and stable shields. If it is affordable for each serious build, this problem gets resolved.
