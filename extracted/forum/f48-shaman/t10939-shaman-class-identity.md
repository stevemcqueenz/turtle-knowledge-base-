# Shaman Class Identity

## Post 71306 by Blondboombox — 2023-10-16T17:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71306#p71306 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been doing a good amount of reading about Shamans as well as playing them extensively, mostly Enhancement. They're an incredible support class with huge amounts of versatility and utility. I think fundamentally the thing that needs to be changed is how Shamans inherently lose personal buffs, dps, survivability at the cost of their support ability. Ex. Bloodlust not being cast on shaman in addition to party target, WF totem being useless to the shaman personally at the expense of not having Grace of Air or Grounding, and of course Thunderhead, storm strike applying a debuff which can be triggered by party members nature damage.

Things id love to see as fixes for this, (besides better scaling) provide additional personal buffs to the shaman. Thunder head should at least make it castable on yourself AND a party member, same with Lust, Stormstrike should be a personal buff instead of a debuff, Flametongue and WF totems could apply an increased personal buff to the shaman, Grace of Air and Strength of Earth could provide increased AGI and STR to the shaman.

Shamans identity as a support isn't one i want fundamentally changed, but instead be able to be singularly capable instead having to sacrifice so heavily to provide for party or raid scenarios.
