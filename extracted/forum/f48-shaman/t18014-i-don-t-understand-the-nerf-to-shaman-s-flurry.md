Since the introduction of dual-wielding for Shamans in *Wrath of the Lich King*, the Flurry talent was nerfed. However,
"In Turtle WoW, the Shaman's Flurry has been nerfed to 20%."
, especially when Warriors still retain a 30% Flurry effect. Granting Shamans dual-wielding to address the current overabundance of hit rating would be a fantastic improvement.
This reflects a specific balance change on the *Turtle WoW* private server, where the Flurry ability's attack speed bonus was reduced to 20%, contrasting with its historical values (e.g., 25-30% in official versions) or other
Wrath of the Lich King (2008–2010): Dual-Wield Era Overhaul**

 - **Dual-Wield Synergy**: Frequent crits from dual-wielding improved stack stability, boosting burst potential.
 - **Talent Tree Position**: Remained a core Enhancement talent (5 points to max).
