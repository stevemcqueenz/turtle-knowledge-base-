# Sensible Shaman Tweaks

## Post 55599 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-08-02T19:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55599#p55599 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

they 'tricked out' paladin a bit too much (resulting in truckloads of paladins)
generally I dont like it when private servers go 'over the top' with changes so i try to be conservative.

lets address Shaman:

- running out of mana and devolving to mere auto-attacks is not fun.
 - Running out of mana and having to constantly drink is not fun.
 - Constantly refreshing rockbiter/wep enchantment is not fun.
 - Taking at least 6 full seconds to set up Totems (just to swiftly move out of their mere 20 yd range) is not fun.
NOTE: in the upcoming patch they will **thankfully** remove the global cooldown on totems (making them much easier/smoother to place down)

my suggestions to fix:
- Reduce the damage AND mana cost of shocks. (Frost Shock is infamously overpowered)
 - Reduce the mana cost of 'Lightning Shield' by 20% by default (Thunderhead can be changed to apply ANY 1 shield to any 1 player)
 - Increase the duration of rockbiter/wep enchantment to 10 mins (to match Shields duration)
 - Change 'Stormstrike" debuff to have an effect based on the type of shock/spell used. (its boring being limited to Earth Shock)
 - Allow 'Ghost Wolf' to be used while 'Water Walking'
RADICAL IDEA:
- Give Enhancement shamans a mediocre physical AOE attack similar to 'Thunderclap' (ironic warriors got this and not Shamans)
I would add it as a Talent on the 3rd row of their tree (just as Paladins had Consecrate as a talent)
 - Give Elemental/Resto shamans a Talent that gives them a weapon enchant that provides some minor mana regen/spell power.

Changing the category of damage type caused by the displacement effect from magical to physical would be an ideal change imho.

Any thoughts on this?
