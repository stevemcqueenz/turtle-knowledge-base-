# [AddOn] ShamanAid - Update

## Post 125987 by Ptr1ck — 2025-04-08T13:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125987#p125987 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

ShamanAid is a lightweight addon to notify you if your elemental shield or weapon buff is active. Originally created by Jumpee about 20 years ago, I updated it to include Water Shield and Earth Shield. I also added a text pop up for when those buffs fade. It also tracks if a weapon buff is active; I did not touch that functionality.
