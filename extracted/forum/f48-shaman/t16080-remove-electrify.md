# Remove Electrify

## Post 109484 by Neechy (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-23T06:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109484#p109484 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Turtle Wow Team,

I write this on behalf of the shaman community as you have likely seen (I hope) the majority disappointment with CC2 changes to shaman elemental spec in particular. And it is primarily due to replacing the iconic Elemental Fury talent with the highly flawed Electrify talent. While the community is of course happy with a number of aspects of this update, this was not one of them.

To quote @Ladhihs who captured the issue perfectly on a separate thread:

> **Ladhihs wrote: Wed Nov 20, 2024 3:36 am**
> The fundamental issue is that the developers don't play the game.
>
>  When the shaman discord was discussing [Electrify] before it was released and how it will kill elemental pvp, Haaxor:
>  didn't understand why shamans "can't just get 5 casts off" to get the full buff
>  didn't understand why shocks are important to elemental burst
>  insisted shamans use the new Elemental Mastery to make Electrify less painful
>
>  Not only is it bad game design to be pigeonholed into a talent to get around another talent that only exists to nerf your potential, it goes against vanilla's design. It's intentional that shamans have a choice between EM and NS; do you want a guaranteed crit or be more versatile? Turtle is trying to remove that choice as a roundabout way of further nerfing shaman.
>
>  **Shaman was designed as the Horde's offensive answer to the Alliance's defensive bulwark that is paladin. As paladin had the best cooldowns and utility but no damage, shaman was given damage but no cooldowns, mobility, or utility. Its sustain was also poor. By nerfing shaman's niche the class is effectively dead and faction balance is now heavily skewed towards Alliance.**
>
>  It is obvious that they are approaching class design in a class by class basis. They have no vision of how the classes are meant to interact with each other. This is why EF got turned into Electrify, while Ruin/Arcane Potency didn't get changed. It's why Enhancement's Flurry got nerfed and Warrior's didn't. No other class has to "ramp up" their damage and they retained their kit. Hell, most of them have only gotten better with this patch.
>
>  As of CC2 shaman is now objectively the worst class and I wouldn't recommend it to anyone.

With all that said, we do understand that the rationale for Electrify was to reduce shaman burst (because apparently no other classes have crazy burst in PvP right?). So if there is no budging on that then here is...

My suggestion:
Updated talent (1 point): Elemental Fury
"Casting your Shock spells invigorates you with Elemental Fury, increasing the critical strike damage bonus of your Fire, Frost, and Nature spells by 100%. Lasts 30 seconds"

 **What does this suggestion address?**
 - It is still a nerf from the original Elemental Fury talent because it requires having to "attune" yourself by casting a Shock first, slowing down the burst that you would normally have from opening with a guaranteed lightning bolt crit.
 - Does not affect totem crit damage as the OG Ele Fury talent, only spells the shaman casts. As such, TWoW's Flame Guidance talent is still viable and encouraged.
 - Still has a duration (although increased from 20 s -> 30 s), meaning the Shock charge up will be a regular part of rotation between fights
 - Does not have the +10% nature damage that came with 5 stacks of Electrify, because increasing damage that wasn't part of the original vanilla specs is leading to power creep, an issue that a lot of players are rightly concerned about.

In conclusion, if the dev team is unwilling to bring back Elemental Fury as before (e.g. due to the ele spec in context of other class changes) then there has to be an overhaul of the Electrify talent that can respect the shaman spec identity and ensure its viability in both PvP and PvE.

I provided one possible solution and the rationale, I am happy to hear thoughts on this. And more than that, I think the shaman community would like to hear from the dev team to know that our feedback is being seriously considered and addressed.

Sure.

Unlike CC1, TWoW's approach with CC2 has been radical overhauls of time-tested class design that has forced players to deal with clunky, unpolished changes on the live server.

The issue with number tweaks is they don't address the fact that the real issue may *actually* be the new talents/ability changes themselves. The way CC2 was released has simply led to a neverending set of revisions that has disrupted the playerbase, dev resources, and the rollout of new content including the long-delayed Kara40 raid.

Anyways that's my rant. Let's keep advocating for what we feel is best for the long-term health of the server. Thanks everyone.
