## Post 30863 by Darkgestalt — 2022-12-17T08:57:00Z

When it comes to Kairions suggestions, I dont think adding another button to press is really the right solution here. Shamans already are quite the piano-fighters as it is. Now that they added a racial spell and bloodlust to the mix, I feel focussing on tweaking the class within the established toolkit is the best option.

Reducing the innate threat of Earthshock is vital, both for dps shamans and tanks. As Downfall made clear, dps shamans of any variety are unable to use Nature shock damage without needlessly maxing threat which is just stupid. And for tanks, as soon as an enemy is resistant, or worse immune, to nature damage they lose 50% of their threat generators! For a shamtank all shock-types should be able to generate additional threat.

For pvp, it is my personal opinion that not all specs for all classes need to be equally competitive. Obviously there needs to be a good pvp option, but its not like the warrior class has full protection spec be a pvp build. Nor do you expect a subtlety Rogue to outdamage Combat in a raid. Same with Enhance, it allows the shaman to dps in raids and solo, and tank in instances. I feel thats fine for now.

I suggest we follow Trymv1s idea and make Stormstrike a self-buff, getting it away from the debuff limit on bosses. Making Lighting Bolt instant would be insane burst damage with regular hit, WFproc, Stormstrike AND lightning bolt. Thats a bit silly.
Instead, just have it give its bonus on all spell damage (any elemental type) and removing the max 2 limitation.

Then change the wording on Rockbiter and Earth shock to this:
Rockbiter rank 1
Imbue the Shaman's weapon, increasing melee attack power by 50 and allowing melee attacks and elemantal shocks to cause additional threat when using that weapon.  Lasts for 5 minutes.

Earth shock rank 1
Instantly shocks the target with concussive force, causing 19 to 22 Nature damage.  It also interrupts spellcasting and prevents any spell in that school from being cast for 2 sec.

Many suggestions have already been made.
I'd like to try and give additionnal imput here.

There are talents that comme directly from xpacs that could adresse the mana issues if implemented :
 - Shamanistic Focus : After landing a melee critical strike, you enter a Focused state.  The Focused state reduces the mana cost of your next Shock spell by 60%.
 - We could imagine an Improved Elemental Shields (from WOTLK) which buffs all shields and which would give access to a Static Shock (again from WOTLK) like 1 point talent in place of Thunderhead which would increase the number of orbs and would give a chance to apply the effect on melee hits. It would solve the issue of Lightning Shield when not being hit, improve its mana efficiency and give a chance to proc Water Shield and regain mana
There are some other existing talents that would do the job, but we don't want to bloat the tree too much and these are the ideas that I like most.

Like other people suggested, regarding spell hit chances, it could ne included in Elemental Devastation as a two way talent : spell crit would give melee crit, melee crit would give like 5% spell hit.
Then we could add some other 5% elsewhere in the tree, in place of Ancestral Knowledge for exemple or combined with Weapon Mastery.
Like that, the spell miss chances would be reduced to 4-7% which seems to be acceptable.
