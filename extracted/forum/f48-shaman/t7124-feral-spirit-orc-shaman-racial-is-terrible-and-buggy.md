## Post 54617 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-07-25T14:51:00Z

At first I thought they were terrible due to their short duration and huge mana cost.
They are surprisingly useful though!

Their damage is enough to be noticeable.
They will target whoever you are currently smacking with your weapon or shooting with a spell.
They interrupt (slow) spell-casters.
They will CONTINUE TO FIGHT after YOU die! Pretty funny in PVP matches to see them chase someone that just killed you.

Best of all though: they are NOT on global cooldown. So casting them is smooth as silk.

That all said: they are not gonna 'carry you to victory' but they are a fairly useful little bonus racial ability.
