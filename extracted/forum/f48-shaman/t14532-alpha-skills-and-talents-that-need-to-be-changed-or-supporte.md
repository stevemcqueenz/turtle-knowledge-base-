SKILLS:
 1. Stoneclaw totem， The threat posed by the Stoneclaw totem should be transferred to the shaman.  This gives the fire totem a safe working environment, otherwise the fire totem would be completely useless.  ( Although the Stone Claw totem is used as a shaman's escape ability at the beginning of the game, it doesn't work very well, so instead of using it as a shaman's group taunt ability. it is better to give the shaman a more reliable escape ability. for example Ghost Wolf [viewtopic.php?t=14480](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=14480))

 2.Frostbrand Weapon ,  I don't have any good ideas for it right now, but obviously this skill is completely useless right now. Maybe we can replace it with the maelstrom weapon.

 3.Sentry Totem，This ability is also completely useless at the moment, and may be given the ability to sneak and anti-submarine.

 4. Flametongue Totem， The present is almost useless, and the shaman should have the right to enjoy it. [viewtopic.php?t=14123](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=14123)

TALENTS：
 1. Moving talent Nature's Guidance at resto 2 line *( Increases your chance to hit with melee attacks and spells by 1%/2%/3% )* to the position of talent  Elemental Warding at ele 2 line. and removing talent  Elemental Warding *( Reduces the damage taken by Fire, Frost and Nature effects by 4%/7%/10% )*.

 2. Moving talent Ancestral Healing at resto 3 line to resto 2 line *( Increases your target's armor value by 8%/16%/25% for 15 sec after getting a critical effect from one of your healing spells )*.

 3. Improving talent Ancestral Knowledge at enh 1 line *( Increases your maximum Mana by 1%/2%/3%/4%/5% )*.

 4. Modifying talent Lightning Shield at enh 2 line *( Increases the damage done by your Lightning Shield orbs by 5%/10%/15% )* to be compatible with Earth and Water Shields.

 5. Modifying talent Thunderhead at enh 3 line *( Allows your Lightning Shield to be cast on allies and reduces its mana cost by 20%. Only usable on one ally at a time )*

 6. Improving talent Earth's Grasp at ele 2 line *( Increases the health of your Stoneclaw Totem By 25%/50% and the radius of your Earthbind Totem by 10%/20%. )*
