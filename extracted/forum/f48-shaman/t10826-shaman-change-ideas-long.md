# Shaman change ideas - long

## Post 70467 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-10-12T23:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70467#p70467 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey guys!

Note from later: the 3 builds I was talking about were ele, enh and resto. Did not cover resto going deep into enh for additional bloodlust.

I wanna share with you how I'd change the shaman builds to make them more viable without giving them the paladin treatment. I might be wrong, this is a personal take.

My problem with the builds is the following:
 * Ele is mostly a PVP build which suffers in PVE. Very mana dependent and easy to oom. Usually outshadowed by mages.
 * Enhance is mostly sought by guilds having a strong melee core who really wanna bloodlust at bosses. Suffers both in PVE and in PVP. Has no real crowd control or escape mechanism and this hits really hard considering they don't have plate armor.
 * Resto is mostly fine, chain heal is OP. Resto shaman is in raids (mostly) to chain heal and set up wf totems for melee.

I think the main problem lies in how the shaman class is designed. The class is 60% utility and 40% anything else. They can be healers, melee and rdps but they should not be as good in these roles as classes with less utility because their added value would make them too OP in comparison. For example an enhance shaman should never be as good as a rogue as a melee dps because the shaman provides many additional tools for the group which the rogues do not.
If both of them had the same dps prowess, enhancement shaman would be an objectively better class because of versatility.
In my opinion the core of the problem is that shamans have to be made viable without making them equal in power level to classes which have much less utility.
Of course mages are vendors, they decurse, crowd control and port, warlocks give hs, ss, summon, aoe, fear, banish but a shaman gameplay mostly revolves around these utilities even if you play as a melee.
Poison cleansing, disease cleansing, redirecting spells, kiting, damage reduction, team buff, slow mana refill, purging and healing even if you are on paper a melee guy.
Shaman utility is based on versatility and they are expected to support the team if any of the situations arise for which they have a tool. Shamans have a tool for most situations.
This is another point I'd slightly change, making some talents considerably stronger or weaker in performing certain utility stuff. This can help shamans focus more on their actual on paper role like dps or healer and
this could make the need towards different shaman builds more prevalent among guilds. Now if a guild is at least somewhat serious it's go resto or go home unless they really love bloodlust.
I wanna offer some changes which would in my opinion make all 3 main build desirable while they would all be filling different niches.

What I'd change

Enhancement
I'd start the buffs with a nerf. In my opinion base wf totem should be much weaker while improved wf should be as strong as it is today. This would mean that guilds would actually want enhancement shamans if they want
real wf buffs.
In turn I'd give enhancement shamans an efficient crowd control ability (you can't expect the mail gear guy to rely on frost shock, earthbind totem and prayers when it's time to run against a bunch of enemies). This would add survavibility.
A mass fear, a mass stun, a sleep effect or just making the shaman much faster for 10 sec to run away (even indoors), I don't know. The point is to give them an oh shít button because their gear is not strong enough to warrior they way out of a really bad pull. Ancestral guardian talent is useful but not enough.
I'd take away ancestral knowledge from their talent tree and put it to ele (later explaining, why).
I'd put elemental devastation in enhancement talent tree but slightly reworked because enhancement has much less probability to spell crit. Might link the melee crit increase to having less than a certain percentage of your life.
I'd also add a stun chance for improved lightning shield procs to further improve enhancement shamans a bit (maybe 10% stun chance per proc, stun lasts to 2 sec). This would also make thunderhead better since enhancement shamans could give stune chance to tanks.
These changes would free up 2 additional talent points in the enhancement tree where the crowd control ability could be inserted.
I'd integrate nature guidance's 3x1% melee hit into thundering strikes talent (I'd remove guidance from the resto tree). Since this talents has 5 levels, I'd add 0,6% plus hit chance per level.

This wh nerf would ensure that enhancement shamans are sought after in guilds. Passive +melee crit at low health would add a bit of power to them while the crowd control spell would give much desired survivability to them.
In my opinion an enhancement shaman would still perform worse than a rogue as a dps but his added utilities would balance things out and would make enh a valid pick. Specially since real wf would only come from them, no more
resto shaman wf spamming. The idea that we take one build and expect it to bring everything good a shaman can (except bloodlust or even with bloodlust in case of enh+resto hybrid which in reality almost only has healing roles) has narrowed down things too much leading to resto or nothing ways of thinking by guilds.

Elemental
Elemental is in an interesting spot. They are very good at PVP but at PVE there are problems. Mana problems. Randomly stopping dps and starting to chain heal to make people survive then having to explain why dps was so low.
The main problem here I think is the tidal mastery talent. You are kinda forced to go 21 talents deep in resto to be a good ele. This is not a problem now because a lot of elemental talents are kinda irrelevant (like elemental devastation
from earlier). But if you wanna make the ele talent tree more interesting you have to make tidal mastery go up in the resto talent tree making it easier to obtain. Let it switch places with healing focus freeing up a bunch of ele talent points then make the tree a bit more interesting! Elemental devastation was already taken out and ancestral knowledge was brought in. Delete earth's grasp (spread it's bonuses alongside the enhancement tree for slight enh buff) and we are at balance for now.
Instead of improved fire totems give ele shamans either two spell penetration talents or two talents for passive mana regen. At the same time I'd include shocks in the storm reach talent so that ele can use most of its offensive arsenal without any awkward repositioning.
I'd change flametongue totem, too.
Melee enhance is an enhancement shaman's job and improved wf will always be the number one choice for that. Flametongue totem currently lives in a world which has no place for it.
I've read it somewhere and agree that it should be changed to a spell damage totem. To remain consistent, flamestrike weapon could add a stackable spell damage buff for your next spell after each melee hit (would help
enhancement shamans with their shocks). With a limit of course, let's say max stack is 5. Flamestrike totem could be improved via call of flame talent. All shamans could add a bit of party-wide spell damage but only ele flamtongue totem would actually make a real difference (don't make the added spell damage too big but make it considerable).
This would help ele a lot. No need for all those resto talents (healing should be rarely their job anyway, only in emergencies). In exchange more mana, added mana regen or spell penetration and guilds would look for ele shamans to buff their casting party. What wf is for the enhancement melee group, the same could be flametongue (almost) to a caster group.
I'd also integrate nature guidance's  3x1% spell hit to call of thunder (guidance would be removed from resto tree). Since this talent has 5 levels, I'd add 0,6% plus hit chance per level.
I think ele shamans should not outperform mages. If they stay slightly below them, balance is reached because they add more extra utility to the group.

Resto
I promised to explain why I'd put ancestral knowledge to ele and not resto. My way of thinking is the following: resto is usually needed for chain heals and wf besides the usual utility. Without wf they need some kind of rebalancing which is even more important because resto is currently the only well-balanced shaman build in my opinion. If we take away wf, we have to give something. My take is health and mana regen. A potent mana regen totem would make up for the lack of ancestral knowledge at resto tree.
I'd change for resto the following: make restorative totems give a much higher buff for mana and life totem. Everyone could use the base ones like now but improved mana totem would have much higher restorative abilities.
I'd also buff mana tide. Make it 3 minute cd and slightly higher mana output.
There's also a nerf I'd add here which could help resto being more distinguished from non-resto builds. I'd change healing wave and chain heal base casting time to 3,5 sec instead of 3 sec. In exchange improved healing wave talent would reduce 1 sec instead of 0,5 sec if all talents are spent there. I'd put the talent a bit deeper into the tree but not much deeper. I want to make a build talent sacrifices to get this talent but if it is put too deeply, resto shaman leveling is seriously harmed. I think resto should be able to learn improved healing wave 5/5 until early level 20s. This way we'd define the different shaman builds' utility a bit better. Builds not visiting the resto tree deep enough could mostly operate with lesser healing waves in an emergency situation because chain heals would be too slow. DPS shamans without resto talents would be less expected to become near perfect healers in a sec but they could still help in (ineffectively) if it is really needed.
I'd remove nature's guidance from the resto tree and would increase the base cooldown of cure disease and cure poison from 1,5s to 3s. Then I'd a 3 part talent to the resto tree, each one decreasing cure desease and cure poison cooldown by one second, making it an instant cast if the talent is fully learned. This can further differentiate the resto build from the others and would make resto better in one specific utility. In a raid if a party doesn't have a shaman for cleaning totem, enh and ele can still help in the poison or disease clearing but this would be suboptimal in case of a mass poisoning or mass disease. This would change resto shamans from the wf+chain heal guys into a chain heal guy with good mana+health regen totems who is a specialist in raidwide poison and disease removal.
As said before, I'd also integrate nature guidance's 3x1% melee hit into thundering strikes talent and 3x1% spell hit to call of thunder. Since these talents have 5 levels, I'd add 0,6% plus hit chance per level.
This way a guild would take resto shamans for (good) chain heals, some utility, to mass-cure poison and disease (faster than ever) and for considerable passive mana or life regenerations during fights (I think life and mana totem should be seriously buffed to balance out the lack of potent wf).

With these changes each build would be (in my opinion) viable without being overpowered and each build would be seeked by guilds for different reasons.

Other things I'd consider to change:
 * Give stoneclaw totem a bit more HP.
 * Give (not improved) healing stream a bit better healing abilities. Highest version of it would become a meme among rank 20 players as it stands.
 * Slightly buff windwall totem because noone is using it.
 * Make searing totems cast fire 1,5x faster and possibly make their damage a bit lower. They are there partially for damage dealing but mostly to ping casters. If you ping them quicker, spellcasting would take even more time.
 * I am unsure how to nerf base wf but an idea: wf totem still grants the extra attack with the same success rate but instead of giving extra attack power to it, the extra attack would damage 1/2 as much as the melee character's normal hit.
__________

The differences I think this would make:
Elemental
Less emphasis on healing or mass poison/disease clearance. Buffing casters including itself instead of wf spam. Larger mana pool, shocks are easier to use. More focus on doing actual dps work, would do additional things like being a healer less often because of the nerfs. More oriented into the caster dps direction. Welcome by guilds wanting to buff caster parties. Less oom. Can't really support a melee party anymore and would make a bad healer by himself.

Restoration
Minimal emphasis on wf, mostly seeked by healer/caster groups. Clear role in disease and poison cleansing, providing extra mana making its caster group oom later, including himself. Life regen totem would almost feel like a renew almost giving the resto shaman a heal over time ability, too.

Enhancement
More survivability, added crit chance at lower health and a new form of utility with thunderhead. Less emphasis on healing (emergency only). Clear choice for guilds wanting a wf totem.
