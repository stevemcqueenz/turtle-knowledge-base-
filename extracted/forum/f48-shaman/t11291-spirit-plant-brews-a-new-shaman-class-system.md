# Spirit Plant Brews | A New Shaman Class System

## Post 74717 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-10-30T02:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74717#p74717 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shamans have various interesting class systems, like the Totems.
I propose an additional one, that could be used to deliver changes/fixes, as well as flesh out further the unique differences depending on the race of choice;

That system is **Spirit Plants**

An ability Shamans would be able to level as a class profession, akin to the Rogue Poisons, and it'd allow them to make mind-altering entheogenic brews from spiritual plants and mushrooms, like Shamans have been doing since forever in real life in all cultures.
In fact it has always been an integral part of Shamanism.

The plant/mushroom ingredients could be picked by Shamans in the world or by all herbalists.
 *(or sold at vendors, but profession-class interaction is good for immersion and economy, poison reagents should also be coming from herbalists/rogues picking them instead of an NPC vendor)*

There would be brews that'd be race-specific.

 **Orc Shamans**

Drinking a brew from spirit plants that facilitates the communion with the spirits of the elements. The Orc peers beyond the Elemental Plane, gleaning visions of an ancient past where manifestations of volatile energies once raged unimpeded across primordial Azeroth deepen the Shamans connection to the Spirits, reducing temporarily the mana cost of Elemental spells.
(making Elemental tree at long last considerable for levelling compared to Enha)

Enhancement Shamans concocting potions from spirit mushrooms, fueling these ferocious elemental-powered fighters into a blood-chilling frenzy, making Enhancement better dps/tank. It could be given also to other melee classes by fellow shamans, most fitting would of course be fury warriors.

 **Tauren Shamans**

Drinking a brew from an entheogenic cactus that could be found mainly in Barrens either by herbalists or only pickable by Tauren Shamans, allowing them to commune with their ancestors, inspired by
[spirit plants used by native north american shamans.](https://www.notesfromthefrontier.com/post/hallucinogens-native-american-spirituality)
They'd assume temporarily:
Ethereal Form
an ability of Spirit Walkers from Warcraft 3
![Image](https://i.ibb.co/0sn8TVQ/BTNEthereal-Form-On.png)
Turns the Tauren Shaman ethereal, makes him unable to attack, reduces physical damage taken but increases magical damage taken by x%.

Tauren Druids are, unlike Night Elven Druids, in contact with Shamanism and used to be Shamans before learning about Druidism from the Night Elves, they could have a racial ability allowing them to drink a brew made by fellow Restoration Shaman players from a [sacred vine](https://medium.com/@SandeepPrakash/dreams-are-nature-nature-is-eternal-jung-and-individuation-in-light-of-ayahuasca-shamanism-8dc22347fdc6), facilitating them to enter into the Emerald Dream. It'd explain why they could keep up with the much more experienced Night Elf Druids.

 **Troll Shamans**

That are Witch Doctors (there is always a cauldron near one) making brews that'd require also macabre non-plant ingredients, allowing them to deal (Shadow) Voodoo magic damage, apart from elemental.

Zuvembi brew is arguably a witch doctor's most insidious concoction. Driven by the cauldron master's strength of mind, Zuvembi brew takes the will of those who breathe its fumes. It makes them compliant and easily manipulated. It is low magic, relying upon ancient cantrips and recipes known only to witch doctors of a certain skill. The witch doctor can add drops of the Zuvembi brew to any potion. The brew creates a powerful suggestive state in the imbiber that he can trigger and control. After drinking a Zuvembi brew potion, the imbiber can be dominated by the witch doctor.

The arts of the witch doctors originated with the trolls, and they make up the vast majority of those who practice these arts. Some of these witch doctors have agreed to take on the occasional non-troll apprentice, usually from among the orcs.

The witch doctor is an alchemical and spiritual master, skilled at not only simply brewing potions and alchemical goods, but at awakening the spirits of the ingredients that go into his goods. A witch doctor at work can be unsettling, for he croons and chants to the herbs and animal parts that go into his brew, shaking a rattle to awaken them from their slumber, and sometimes even bursting into dance to appease them and make them favor him.

The witch doctor's seething cauldron contains a hallucinogenic mixture of herbs, poisons, crushed insects and other noxious materials. He may add drops of this Shaka brew to improve the efficiency of any potion or alchemical item he creates. The Shaka brew maximizes all variable, numeric effects of the witch doctor's potions or alchemical creations. Drinking a potion treated with the Shaka brew leaves the imbiber with a euphoric buzz. The Bambe brew is a secret recipe that extendes the effectiveness of magical potions. Drinking Bambe brew potions leaves the imbiber with a faint crawling sensation over their skin.

Troll Priests could be given a brew from fellow Troll Shamans that'd allow them to commune with the Loa and temporarily shapeshift like Troll Loa worshippers do.

Feel free to suggest more entheogenic shaman brews made from Spirit plants.
I'll later add more examples for potentially new Shaman races, like the Dwarves, Vrykul & Ogres.

I love the idea! Let shamans get high!

However, I think us cows are getting the short end of the stick. Trading the ability to attack for physical damage resistance is somewhat lackluster, because if you're receiving physical damage, it means you have aggro. If you have aggro, it means you're in melee, so being unable to attack is not ideal. The only thing you CAN do (I assume) is to cast spells, but those will get pushed back like crazy while you have aggro. So maybe we could add an additional effect to the Ethereal form:
"You have 70% to avoid pushback from physical attacks while casting spells."
Assuming a 5-10 sec duration, it should allow you get a heal or two off unless interrupted/stunned.

OR make it an escape tool by adding a movespeed increase, like the ability had in WC3.
