# SHAMAN FIXES

## Post 80037 by Chappinhas3000 — 2023-12-06T12:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80037#p80037 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shamans and Paladins stand out from the other classes since they are thematically support classes, this is to say that they shine greater when in a party. However, given the recent changes to paladins and the subsequent power boost they receive it is apparent that once more shamans have been left outcasts and the player base shows that latent issues prevent the class from reaching it's true potential. I have made a list of changes and tweaks i believe could be implemented that may flesh out the meta and playability of the shaman class.
Before i engage in the crunch, i find it important to determine what exactly defines a support class and how best to understand the shaman's role. From where i see it, paladins and shamans have the uncanny ability to improve overall dps of a party and aid both in combat and healing, furthermore, the shaman class distinguishes itself for the use of magic in favour of physical damage. This factors lead to a class that must have some downtime within it's rotations so as to focus those moments on support, be it the application of totems, the occasional support heal or simply a moment of repositioning. Additionally, the shaman class has always flirted with tanking capabilities by shield and threat even tho it does not use plate, this as often been cause for contention and excitement among would-be tanks. The following propositions seek to adress these issues without crossing the shamanistic spirit.
Generally speaking, should shaman tank, damage mitigation should arrise from stoneskin totem like effect, additionally talents that may increase chance to be missed or less chance to be crited should be favoured, this is due to the armour factor forcing the shaman to control more thuroughly the incoming damage, the mana factor also takes a great toll on this playstyle and should be resolved thru the use of totems and elemental shields has it would have class RP.
On the enhancement factor, the greatest deal arises from the weapon enchantment commitment that prevents the shaman from using consumable and enchants to empower his melee attacks, additionally the mana issue remains, albeit to a smaller degree.
The elemental playstyle is dull and repetitive showing innovation only in a shield melee build not adequate for late game content, an over relliance on castable spells and limited toolset prevents the spec from reaching further.
restoration, despite it's great advantages and suficient healing lacks luster and can prove equally dull at times, point in case, the lack of interesting spells on the spec tree.

To solve these issues the following is proposed:

 - Base Spirit, and agility increase
 -Rockbitter weapon : the addition of damage mitigation ( akin to stoneskin totem) and possible healing spell power, to allow both resto and shaman tanks to use it in diferent forms, additionally it could incentivize the tank shaman to heal himself as means of accrueing aura threat.
 - Flametongue weapon : the addition of spell power to generally increase elementall damage numbers.
 - Frostbrand weapon: a flat increase in damage to counter it's relation with flametongue and the adition of mp5 buff.
 - Totems: a yard increase to 35yards, allowing for more interaction and less placing of totems
 - Shields: an increase in charges ( possibly by enhancement talents) and inner cooldown to prevent the fast deprecation of the same, this would make them more mana efficient.) additionally passive effects while the shield holds, mana regen for water, damage reduction for earth and crit for lightning ( possibly only accrued at lvl 60) giving them more versatility for team situations. Additionally water shield should come at lower levels to aid shamans with early mana issues.
 - Flame Shock: allow for the dot to crit
 - Lightning bolt: reduce the cast time by 0.5 sec, allowing for LB weaving in between attacks when using a very slow 2hand weapon
 - Off-hand Fist weapons: a niche and possibly pointless change that would add variety in playstyles and be thematically cool, these could either be enchanted by shaman weapons or force the shaman to seek shadow oil's or frost oils for his magical needs. As a counter to tradicional dual weilding, this offhand useage could ignore dual wield hit penalty and be counted as typical 1 hand 5%.
 - Bloodlust: make it so you always buff yourself and an ally when you cast it and reduce the cooldown to match it's duration allowing for perma use of the spell, reduce it's effect to compensate for the permanent presence of the spell, this would allow the shaman to aid an ally without compromising itself.
 - Enhancment tree should have a talent, maybe on dodge effect that allows for 15 % mana regen to continue while in combat, high enough in the tree that it could be picked by resto and ele.
 - WFT and FTT Should not count as weapon enchants and be considered buffs instead, aditionally they should stack with the shaman's own weapon provided it is not the same as the totems.
 - Allow for enchanting shaman weapons.
 - Slight increase in stonekin totem damage reduction
 - Threat transferal from magma totem
 - Increase in stoneclaw totem health and threat generation
 - Decoupling of fire nova, allowing it to be cast at all times emanating from both the current fire totem and the shaman alike.
 -Add a water nova effect, capable of being cast at all times emanating from both the current water totem and shaman alike ( possible resto talent), small heal and mana recovery and cleanse effect around 20 sec cooldown
 -Add a sand blast effect, capable of being cast at all times emanating from both the earth totem and shaman alike, providing enemy armor reduction and chance to miss
 -Add a wind nova effect, capable of being cast at all times emanating from both the wind totem and shaman alike, providing allies with a slight haste bonus and movement speed buff.
 -Add a ele talent to incentivize shock weaving, something as simple as next diferent shock damage increase
 -Add a special effect to enhanc shaman block based on current elemental shield, forked lightning( low damage high threat) for lightning, rejuvenation like effect for earth and mana regen for water.

I believe these changes could aid the class significantly and allow for new ways of making shamans, in conclusion, the damage mitigation is ensured by direct flat vaklue damage reduction, the dps increase by buff spell flexibilitiy and the mana issues i believe can be countered by less totem usage and some increase mana regen from water shield and dodging ( for would be tanks)
