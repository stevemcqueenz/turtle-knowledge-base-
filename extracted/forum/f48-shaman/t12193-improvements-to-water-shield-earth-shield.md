# Improvements to Water Shield / Earth Shield

## Post 81082 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-12-13T21:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81082#p81082 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings, fellow element connoisseurs!

I recently hit 60 with my shaman and was exposed to the awesomeness of Water Shield and Earth Shield.
However, I believe that these can be further improved.

As a start, they should be acquired at earlier levels. They would have helped my leveling journey tremendously if I had access to them. Rank 1 of Lightning Shield requires level 8. So Water Shield should start at level 20-25, and Earth Shield could start at 35-45.

Furthermore, I think they should be affected by talents, specifically "Improved Lightning Shield" - it should be renamed to "Improved Elemental Shields" and apply its 15% bonus to the healing and mana restore of Earth and Water shield respectively.

The capstone talent "Thunderhead" should also be reworked to "share" (copy) your current shield type to your target ally (without removing it from yourself). It will have a 1-2 min cooldown. This can be used as a CD to heal, give mana, or shield an ally. It will also still have its mana cost reduction, but applied to all 3 types shields.

A different idea for a capstone talent would be a way to pop your own shield orbs yourself somehow. Perhaps on every spell crit (including healing crits, so resto can also do it)? Maybe we could include melee crits in there as well, so enhancement can make use of it too?
Personally I like the idea of sharing shields more, because this one was done in WotLK and wasn't very interesting to play with. Sharing would fit better into the vanilla shaman design of a support class better.

And finally, visuals!
It would be super cool if we could visually see how many stacks of a shield we have by the amount of orbs circling around our characters. Their spawn animation already includes all 3 orbs, so just make them stay instead of having only one.
