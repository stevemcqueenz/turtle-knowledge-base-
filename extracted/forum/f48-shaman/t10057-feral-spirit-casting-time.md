# Feral Spirit casting time...?

## Post 65862 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-09-19T23:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=65862#p65862 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Orc Shaman racial ability "Feral Spirit" has been (for me) INSTANT cast (and did NOT trigger global cooldown) for hundreds of hours of gameplay.

For MONTHS it was instant cast for me.
Was this some bizarre bug?
Has anyone else had it be instant cast?

It now (out of nowhere) *correctly* has a .5 second cast time.   crying_turtle
I know its supposed to be that from a video showcasing it on youtube from years ago.

Theres no patch notes indicating this bug/fix.
As a bonus odd thing: the website says its a 5min cooldown when its actually a 4 minute CD.

UPDATE:
Some random guy on here just taught me whats happening:
'Improved Ghost Wolf' talent actually affects the cast time of this Racial Ability.
So even 1 point will make it instant cast.

Bizarre. Unknown if intentional or a bug.
