# elemental tragedy

## Post 108094 by amanagor (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-16T09:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108094#p108094 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Elemental shaman was in a bad state before the patch, and is now in an even worse state. In pve, it is a bottom of the barrel dps spec,  provides no utility over other shaman specs, and so is not taken in end tier raids. This issue is likely going to be aggravated for Karazan.
The spec still has clear mana issues, as the changes to elemental focus and water shield do not compensate for the nerfs to tea, and do not  allow elemental to use any other spell than lightning bolt in their rotation without ooming quickly.
On top of that, even without ooming, the spec under performs compared to other dps specs.
Shaman is also the second least played class in the game for dps in naxx. (behind only priest)
Somehow turtle devs think this is acceptable, as they state they are happy with  how elemental performs, as they use this statement to justify how little the spec was touched in 17.2 (other than destroying it in pvp).
On the subject of pvp, Elemental is now objectively garbage, as it needs multiple casts to start providing useful damage due to the new electrify talent. The only scenario where elemental is viable in pvp, is in a group fight where the opposing team is incompetent, and completely ignores the elemental, letting it hard cast for 10 seconds
Against competent players, the elemental just gets focused before it has time to build up stacks, and doesn't have the utility, or mobility to survive.
In a 1V1 scenario, don't even think of building up stacks of the buff, elemental is literally a free kill at this point.
Currently There is no aspect of the game where elemental performs well. The spec is roughly equal in strength to its classic version, in regards to pve, and significantly worse for pvp.
The question i'm asking the devs, is if they are planning on making the spec viable, or if people that want to play elemental should just give up and go play somewhere else?

I've always mainly played elemental in PvP. The class identity to me was mostly about burst damage, with some extra party support in longer fights where the burst doesn't matter as much. Balance druid has always had better mobility with travel form being instant cast and the ability to get out of snares. They're also less threatened by mages due to having two damage schools, and moonkin gives them better survivability overall. Now balance also does more burst damage than elemental which leaves my favorite class kind of lacking an identity?

I stand by my previous suggestion, return elemental fury as it was and change electrify to stack something more meaningful to the PvE longer-form fights that it seems intended for, like maybe allowing mana regen while casting. I don't raid so someone more experienced could suggest something better. The idea is that you choose to spend your points in burst damage or sustained damage, and it'd be costly point-wise to do both.

The fundamental issue is that the developers don't play the game.

When the shaman discord was discussing the talent before it was released and how it will kill elemental pvp, Haaxor:
didn't understand why shamans "can't just get 5 casts off" to get the full buff
didn't understand why shocks are important to elemental burst
insisted shamans use the new Elemental Mastery to make Electrify less painful

Not only is it bad game design to be pigeonholed into a talent to get around another talent that only exists to nerf your potential, it goes against vanilla's design. It's intentional that shamans have a choice between EM and NS; do you want a guaranteed crit or be more versatile? Turtle is trying to remove that choice as a roundabout way of further nerfing shaman.

Shaman was designed as the Horde's offensive answer to the Alliance's defensive bulwark that is paladin. As paladin had the best cooldowns and utility but no damage, shaman was given damage but no cooldowns, mobility, or utility. Its sustain was also poor. By nerfing shaman's niche the class is effectively dead and faction balance is now heavily skewed towards Alliance.

It is obvious that they are approaching class design in a class by class basis. They have no vision of how the classes are meant to interact with each other. This is why EF got turned into Electrify, while Ruin/Arcane Potency didn't get changed. It's why Enhancement's Flurry got nerfed and Warrior's didn't. No other class has to "ramp up" their damage and they retained their kit. Hell, most of them have only gotten better with this patch.

As of CC2 shaman is now objectively the worst class and I wouldn't recommend it to anyone.

I think there are really two problems here. The first issue is that PvP fights are *fast*. The only time you'd ever get five hard casts off in a one-on-one encounter at all is if you're fighting an especially tanky opponent. Especially for a glass cannon build like elemental shaman, five hardcasts is just not ever going to happen in any PvP fight except for an extended fight over an AV graveyard. This is really different from PvE, especially raiding, where fights last longer and that five hardcast on-ramp is much less of an issue. The average player has 4k-5k hp, if you're dealing ~750 damage per lightning bolt and doing nothing else, your target is almost guaranteed to be dead before getting 5 hardcasts off.

The second issue I have with it is that it doesn't quite fit the class identity of elemental shaman. The spell is LIGHTNING bolt, lightning strikes fast, zapping an unlucky area with a blast of sudden energy that's gone in a blink. In the original classic design lightning bolt and chain lightning hit at the same time for burst damage, giving the playstyle that sort of lightning-fast explosive feeling. That's why the "shock" spells are instant cast.

> **Ladhihs wrote: Wed Nov 20, 2024 3:36 am**
> didn't understand why shocks are important to elemental burst

I am admittedly less clear on this one. I've always used shocks either to confirm a kill if the initial burst doesn't do it, or more for their utility.

> **Ladhihs wrote: Wed Nov 20, 2024 3:36 am**
> insisted shamans use the new Elemental Mastery to make Electrify less painful

You talked about it a bit in your post but I think it's worth emphasizing. There's a real choice to be made between Elemental Mastery or Nature's Swiftness. On one hand Nature's Swiftness is an exceptionally versatile skill, you can instant-cast a lightning bolt to add more damage to a burst, or use it on a heal to save a party member in a tight spot. Compare to Elemental Mastery which is all damage, it lets you control exactly when you will get a burst so you can time it for effectiveness, or use it as a type of ambush. There have been, and continue to be, constant arguments over which one is better in PvP. It's a really compelling, difficult choice in the normal vanilla talent tree that doesn't really exist anymore in CC2.

If you're going with Elemental Mastery the guaranteed crit is **the most important time** to have your crit damage bonus at 100%. This isn't currently possible unless you've already done three hardcasts, which not only restricts your ability to time a burst but also makes it effectively impossible to ambush someone at full power. For someone doing a long PvE fight using EM to keep mana costs down and score some extra free damage it's not an issue but this change makes the ability barely worth using in a PvP scenario.
