# Enhancement Suggestions

## Post 66156 by Jstansberry (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-21T07:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66156#p66156 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Flurry attacks should restore 1-2% max mana. This would address the issue of enhancement shamans constantly being OOM in PVE and would also make itemization much more flexible.

Bloodlust should apply to both the shaman and their target. The biggest issue with enhancement in pve is that there is no reason to give them gear because other classes will use it 10x better. Why would the devs choose to reinforce this pattern by making Bloodlust a single target buff that'd obviously be better used on anyone OTHER than the enhancement shaman? We should just make enhancement not awful instead of making them a "support" class whose only notable strengths don't scale with gear.

Nature's Guidance hit rating talent from the restoration tree should be swapped with Guardian Totems in enhancement. Having the %hit bonus talent in restoration is cruel.

Trying to understand the rationale behind Improved Lightning Shield -> Thunderhead... Sure, maybe it could be useful for giving a tank more threat, but it's definitely not worth 4 talent points and also reinforces the "support class" concept that the devs seem to be going for that is also causing enhancement to be a dead spec. Retribution got buffed to the point of being competitive with rogues in DPS charts, what's keeping them from doing the same for enhancement?

Stormstrike should work for the shaman only - for the sake of moving away from being strictly a support spec (rogue poisons, boomkin Wrath, and ele shamans currently benefit more from Stormstrike than enhancement shamans do) and also so that it doesn't take a debuff slot. 20% more damage from the next two shock spells as a buff to the shamans rather than 20% vulnerability to nature damage as a debuff on the target would be helpful. You're not going to be casting lightning bolts/chain lightning after a Stormstrike and using Earth Shocks is not realistic when WF procs on their own can already get you in trouble threat-wise. This also allows for more flexibility as most raid encounters have a lot of nature resistance. Or just remove increased spell damage debuff component and replace it with a massively increased proc chance for on-hit effects, similar to what they gave paladins. This could potentially be busted for pvp as this implies that Stormstrike would be a guaranteed WF proc but obviously the devs aren't prioritizing pvp in balancing decisions, paladins and feral druids have become more viable in pve at the cost of becoming absolute menaces in pvp.

A threat reduction talent. This would ideally be in the first 2 tiers of either the enhancement or elemental tree.

A lot of enhancement's issues stem from the fact that it's few strong points in pve do not scale with gear at all. Bloodlust and (marginally) better totems is all enhancement brings to the table. I understand that enhancement having the potential to instantly blow someone up in pvp causes some difficulty in balancing, but that didn't stop them from buffing retribution paladins and rets could also do that before they got buffed (not even referring to reckbombing  - Seal of Command proc crit + Judgment of Command under HoJ will oneshot most players). Retribution and enhancement both absolutely sucked in pve and were fine in pvp, and were very comparible as hybrid class melee specs. There should be no hesitation to buff enhancement if they were comfortable giving retribution as many new tools as they have. Maybe give enhancement bonus on-hit proc chance as part of their kit, as well? It would make sense both thematically and from a gameplay perspective. Enhancement can't even be the dedicated Nightfall carrier anymore, as retribution paladins do that better, too. The 1.17 targeted nerf to the Nightfall/Seal of the Crusader interaction is not going to make a difference in that regard, either, the problem is moreso that ret can spam r1 Crusader Strike indefinitely to proc things (including Windfury Totem and Judgement of Wisdom), but that's a bit off topic. Not asking ret to be nerfed (though it is overtuned in pvp), just buff enhancement and make it scale with gear.
