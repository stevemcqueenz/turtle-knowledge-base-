As Griz mentioned, druid is your best bet for world PvP most likely. Levelled like 20-60 with warmode and i still have it on. Of course, I'm still shit at the game and running it on shittier hardware, so sick PvP maneuvering and positioning type stuff is essentially impossible. But even with all that in mind I'd say the class is undoubtedly kitted for outdoor skirmishes with lots of space to run around and hide.

As for shaman? I don't really know. I'm told elemental is nutty but I'm fairly sure that's specific to endgame. Enh is just hoping for windfury procs and crits, and resto is... Hardly talked about. Of course, chances are this is way different at lower levels, but I think it's best to do your own research/practice one way or another.
