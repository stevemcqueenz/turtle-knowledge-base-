You are not forced to use lightning strike. In fact, with your talent suggestion it might be better NOT to use it, so the shield charges dont expire faster.

Because contrary to paladin you dont loose charges on your success state, you loose them on failsate. But even at maximum depletion rate (1 Globe every 4.5 seconds from being hit, + one on CD with LS, you will at worst be down 4 globes by the time you can refresh water shield.

Considering you come with over 20% innate parry and dodge chance before gear (block not included(5% parry and dodge innate, 4.4% dodge from grace and 6% from ancestral guardian)) without this talent, you will easily have over 50% chance to completely avoid auto attacks. meaning your likely only gonna drop a single charge during the full 9 seconds without using lightning strike, this is basically passive 24+% dodge chance.

Speaking of the gear
T2.5 would be ~5% dodge from agi + 1.5 dodge from defense & 1.5 parry from defense, Elementium Threaded cloak is 2% dodge and another 1% from the enchant. as well as 2% dodge on head and leg enchant each and another ~1% dodge from agility on gloves. ~2% dodge of qiraji belt and heck why not throw in 2 vigilance charms for another 4%

WIth that little setup tossed together, no neck, rings weapon or consumables /other classes buffs considered, you end up with 70% combined parry and dodge.

That is absolute overkill. I think you haven't quite grasped the essence of the tank shaman.

It doesn't matter if a tank shaman is more tanky than a warrior or paladin because the answer will always be no, since the class wasn't designed for that.

However, it's a spec with incredibly high threat generation, which allows DPS to unleash their full potential in both single-target and multi-target situations, in addition to providing significant buffs for melee characters.

Therefore, if tomorrow a tank shaman could have the same tankiness as a warrior or paladin, it would be the end of other tank classes, which would be a real shame.

P.S. Regarding tank shaman gear, there's quite a bit available in Tier 1, it's just not always obtainable through raids.
