# Elemental Devastation change suggestion

## Post 31152 by Mzgnkh — 2022-12-20T09:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31152#p31152 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One change I'd like to see is Elemental Devastation in the elemental tree giving a stacking casting speed buff to the entire group rather than a melee crit buff to the shaman. Right now, elemental shaman doesn't really bring anything unique compared to both enhancement (bloodlust) and resto (mana tide), which doesn't sit well with me since shamans are a support class. AFAIK no other class buffs casting speed, so it would be a unique perk of the elemental spec. The talent would have to be moved to tier 5 or 6 to make it out of reach of the other specs and the percentage it gives would likely have to be adjusted to make sure it's not over- or underperforming, but it would be great if elemental shamans could become a viable caster group buffer like they were in TBC without outright copying the TBC hit/crit fire totem.
