# Enh shaman Talent

## Post 79857 by Jhonnyronnin — 2023-12-05T06:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79857#p79857 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

*Hello everyone*, I would like to show you my idea of talents to see if you like how it is presented. I made these talents without modifying the original talents so much, some of the ideas raised here such as combining two imbue is to not have the need to put dualwield and thus have the benefit of being able to do more magic damage (Since we are not a warrior with wf ). Another thing is not to leave aside the tank shamans, and I gave them a taunt which would do magic damage and escalate with it. The other skills are necessary for the Enh shamans, to recharge mana, to have spellhit (Since shocks are part of the Enh shaman's rotation). The devs could use these talents so they don't have to give as much spellhit or mana recharge to the new tier that they plan to release in the next patch and also make tier bonuses that are increasing the damage of the frostbrand or flametongue and not leaving these skills behind (Since they are not very used after level 30). I hope you like it and you can suggest changes and new things that I will gladly read. And sorry for my English  scared_turtle_head

 *I edit the improved shield, guardians totems and Unleashed rage. Ty for u suggestions<3*
