# Totem Twisting is a remedy to a bigger problem, not a gameplay style.

## Post 44920 by Keds (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-05-08T14:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44920#p44920 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm doing some research on the forums and asking people ingame about the currently Enh Shaman gameplay, and I've come to the conclusion that most people don't seem to realise. I've seem many players suggestions about reducing totem mana cost, about giving shamans a way to refund their totem spent mana based on their duration, about combining these two totems and so forth... But if you think about it:

 **Totem twisting is ONLY a thing because we can't compensate the agility DPS bonus of 4 others melees on our group....** And that's IT.

My suggestion, change Stormstrike.

> Stormstrike   (Rank  2)
>  XZY Mana
>  Instant
>  Gives you an extra attack. In addition, the next shock spell you cast have it's damage increased by 20% and gives nearby party members (Agi,AP, or whatever) X amount. Lasts 6 seconds. (Doesn't stack with Grace of Air totem)

This way, we would be able to use all of our shocks, have a nice rotation without relying on stupid gameplay remedy issues.
