# [alpha]Improving utilization ratio of  Earth，Light, Water Shield

## Post 99259 by 987004590 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-12T11:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99259#p99259 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is no doubt that these three shield skills are extremely effective and practical. Because of this, you must always keep an eye on the BUFF column during combat to ensure that it can be replenished when it disappears. If you reuse it before it is exhausted, a lot of mana will be wasted. unfortunately, this spell is consumed and used very frequently, This causes you to either constantly stare at its consumption, or frequently waste a lot of mana, which makes it unbearable.

I think there needs to be a way to make them easier to use. My suggestion is to delay the mana cost of this spell until the time the skill takes effect, not when the skill is used.
