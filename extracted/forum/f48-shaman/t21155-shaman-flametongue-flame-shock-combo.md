# Shaman: Flametongue + Flame Shock Combo

## Post 146958 by Avalok — 2025-08-19T08:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146958#p146958 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**HELLO!**

I would like to make a suggestion here regarding the Shaman's Flametongue weapon imbue, along with her Flame Shock spell. The suggestion I post here now emphasizes on a fair amount of increased damage done (DPS), and mana conservation as well, regarding the two Shaman abilities I have mentioned.

My suggestion is that, if a target is affected by Flame Shock (from any source: Own/ Other Player/ NPC?), each hit of the Flametongue imbue of the Shaman on that target should have an increase in critical hit chance by an X amount, so to improve the Shaman's DPS with this combo, and have a better system of conserving mana, specifically from her talent, Elemental Focus, which provides two (2) stacks of the Clearcasting buff to her whenever a "spell" critically hits; in this case, her Flametongue imbue.

Maybe the additional critical hit chance of the Flametongue imbue provided by the Shaman's Flame Shock spell should be at around 20 to 40 percent. In my opinion, it should be 30 percent. This will improve the DPS provided by the Flametongue weapon imbue quite well, and also increases the Shaman's capacity to conserve mana, and will make her feel less burdened in casting her spells, specifically shock spells since this is mostly about Enhancement Shamans.

 ----------

The reason for this suggestion is because, the Shaman Class already has a "combo" similar to it.
I am referring to the Frostbrand + Frost Shock Combo. Frostbrand weapon imbue is guaranteed to always critically hit its target if the latter is affected by Frost Shock. Since this is the case, and that this combo deals damage very well, and with a decent mana conservation system with it, I send this suggestion to the Forums now to have a proper "variety" in the Enhancement Shaman's playstyle.

If the Flametongue + Flame Shock combo suggestion that I have sent here would be implemented, this means that Enhancement Shaman Players, including myself, can use other Shock Spells as well along with the Flame Shock. That means the Stormstrike's additional nature damage increase buff can be much more properly used in terms of DPS, and with better mana conservation included.

That is all!   turtle_in_love_head   Thank you for your time!
