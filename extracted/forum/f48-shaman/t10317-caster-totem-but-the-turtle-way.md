# Caster totem - but the Turtle way

## Post 67386 by Schwarzschild (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-28T13:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67386#p67386 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shamans are great in buffing melee groups, but healer and caster support is pretty lacking. Specifically Elemental Shamans fall short of supporting a caster/healer group, which is counter-intuitive. TBC solved this problem by intruducing an air totem for flat spellpower and a 31-points fire totem for Elementals with spell hit and crit. That made it an absolute no-brainer which totems to put down as an Elemental Shaman in a caster/healer group (unless a situation required a anti-poison/disease or tremor totem).

While some aspects of TBC can make sense to introduce in Turtle, this should be kept at a minimum and not be considered a go-to solution. Turtle needs a unique approach, that feels both "Shaman" and "Vanilla", i.e., that integrates itself well into the game.

 ****I propose to augment the exisitng tier-4 Elemental talent "Improved Fire Totems". This 2-point talent now also improves the Flametongue Totem, so that it imbues the main hand weapons of party members not just with the known fire dmg proc, but also with a 2%/4% haste buff.****

Naturally the value 2%/4% haste should and would be dependent on testing. It might be more reasonable to scale it down to 1%/2%. Alternatively, if it would become obvious that melees scale too well with this change, a change from haste --> spell haste could be considered instead.

 **Reasoning:**
 1) Haste is an attribute already associated with Shamans (Bloodlust).
 2) No completely new spell or talent is introduced.
 3) The Shaman has to make a conscious decision when to put down his fire dmg totems vs Flametongue. Nevertheless, with the shorter Fire Nova delay also granted by this talent, the Shaman can easily put down a Nova totem and put the Flametongue one back up before its weapon enchantment runs out (sort of a mini-totem twisting).
 4) Healer/caster party members would now face the same, very vanilla-esque dilemma as melees who want Windfury and therefore cannot use poisons/sharpening stones etc.. Likewise, healers/casters cannot apply wizard/mana oils on their weapon if they want Flametongue. Like with Windfury, 4% haste is better than any of the consumable effects for caster weapons in most cases. This will therefore make further use of the newly introduced weapon cleaning cloths by the survival profession.
 5) Elemental/Enhancement hybrid builds including Elemental Devastation, which also is in the 4th talent tier of the Ele tree, will get more interesting.
 6) Hunters will finally have a 2nd Shaman totem (in addition to the Agi totem) that can buff their damage output. Likewise, pets, who already benefit from the Strength totem, cannot benefit from Flametongue, thereby helping this change to not put Hunters over the top.
 7) Feral Druids, who already cannot benefit from Windfury totems, cannot do so from Flametongue either (assuming no changes will be made to how Druid feral "weapons" work). However, with Flametongue up in their group, Ferals can at least more reliably benefit from their Shaman's Agi totem, as it will not have to be twisted with Windfury. In raids, this is further enhanced by the fact that Shamans with the suggested haste buff-Flametongue totem would tend to get grouped with Ferals and Hunters, who need Agi instead of Windfury totems anyways.
