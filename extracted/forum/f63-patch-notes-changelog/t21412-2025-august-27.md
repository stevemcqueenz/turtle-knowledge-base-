 **Molten Core**

With Patch 1.18.0, we've boosted the overall power of creatures in the open world, dungeons, and raids. This was long overdue: over time, class updates steadily made players stronger, and a fair challenge is essential to the Vanilla WoW experience. Overcoming obstacles through strategy, knowledge, and proper gearing is what keeps the game fun and rewarding.

Raids should stay engaging and require teamwork. For too long, Molten Core has been almost a free loot source—understandable on a progressed server like Nordanaar, but it was too easy for new guilds or players, which made preparation and coordination feel less important. Sure, the gear was nice, but it didn't teach the fundamentals of raiding, causing issues in later content.

However, while open world and dungeon tuning has been going as expected, Molten Core has sparked a lot of discussion. This week, we're altering numbers of some boss fights while keeping the core challenge intact.

With the server restart, the following adjustments will be applied:

- **Molten Core** Boss Health Adjustments:
 - **Lucifron**: Reduced health by 20%

 - **Magmadar**: Reduced health by 10%

 - **Garr**: Reduced health by 15%

 - **Baron Geddon**: Reduced health by 20%

 - **Shazzrah**: Reduced health by 20%

 - **Sulfuron Harbinger**: Reduced health by 20%

 - **Golemagg**: Reduced health by 25%

- **Rune of Detonation** and **Rune of Combustion** now fade from players when **Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan** is defeated.

- Reduced **Magmadar**'s Lava Bomb area of effect duration to 20s and 40s (previously 30s and 60s).
Keep in mind that on the previous maintenance update, Majordomo and Ragnaros encounters also received mechanical and tuning changes.

Looking ahead, we're also exploring the idea of hard modes for existing instances, since there's clearly a lot of interest from the community.
