# 2025 — July 23

## Post 140939 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-07-22T22:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140939#p140939 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-07-23_ENG_2.jpg)

- Fixed an issue where **Gloves of Manathirst** granted more disenchanting materials than intended.

- Fixed an issue where **Choker of Terminal Arcanum** could not be disenchanted.

- **Broken Tooth** will no longer cast **Fireball** instead of **Rend** and **Enrage**.

- Fixed an issue where **Old Jonah** (Human **Hunter Trainer**) could be attacked without flagging **Horde** players for PvP.

- Fixed multiple scripting issues with **Sandfury Witch Doctor**.

- Spells that do not deal damage, such as **Distract**, should no longer trigger procs from **Blade of Eternal Darkness**.

- Fixed incorrect requirements for the quest **Spirit Totem**.

- You can no longer use the item that begins the **Battle for Darrowshire** while in **Andorhal**.

- Fixed an issue where **Healing Ward IV** was not actually healing.

- **Arugal** should no longer appear in the incorrect position.

- **Frostbrand Weapon (Rank 5)** no longer incorrectly stacks with **Windfury Totem**.

- The **Core of Arc'Tiras** will now spawn on the ground once the boss is defeated, instead of being lootable from his corpse.

- Made scripting fixes to various mobs in **Razorfen Kraul**.

- Fixed a spelling error in the **Kel'Thuzad** Hardmode encounter.

- Fixed an issue where **Corrosive Poison** could bypass invulnerability effects.
