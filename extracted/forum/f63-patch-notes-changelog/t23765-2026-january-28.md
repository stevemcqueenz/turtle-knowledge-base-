# 2026 — January 28

## Post 166661 by Shadowsong (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-01-27T21:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166661#p166661 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2026-01-28-ENG.jpg)

- The **Mulgore to Durotar** transport is visible again.

- **Arcane Elemental**companion pet will no longer act as a guardian. The guardian tab visually will remain till the next client update.

- **Alternative Tier Sets** and **Tier 3.5 Sets** will no longer be unique.

- **Sanv Tas'dal**will no longer attempt to drop Totems for Alliance groups on PvP realms.

- **Command: Take Position** will no longer sometimes have the pet walk to the destination.

- Fixed **Shadow Sight** stealth detection.

- Re-added **Rose Petal Pink** hairdye for High Elves to the barbershop vendor in Stormwind.

- Moved the **Cultist's Firestick** to Taragaman the Hungerer's loot table.

- **Ash‑Covered Tunic**: Armor value increased to 26, Stamina set to 1.

- **Fiery Cloak**: added 1 Stamina and renamed to **Ash‑Covered Cape** to avoid duplicate naming, moved to Bazzalan's loot table.

- Added a new item to **Jared Voss**'s loot table: **Defias Longbow**.

- **Defias Mage Cape**: Armor value adjusted to 10.

- **Murloc Eye on a String** is now enchantable and grants +1 Spirit.

- **Broken Stonemason's Guild Signet** now provides +1 Spirit.

- Added a new item to Edwin VanCleaf's loot table: **Tattered Defias Rags**.

- Added a Tabard to the **Summer Collection of Unseen Fashion**.

- For the quest **Junkboxes Needed** players must now gather 5 **Precise Lock Cylinders** (dropping from Heavy Junkboxes) that stack up to 20 per slot, allowing rogues to save bag space while working on their Ravenholdt Manor reputation.
