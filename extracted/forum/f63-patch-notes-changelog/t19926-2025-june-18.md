# 2025 — June 18

## Post 136087 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-06-17T19:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136087#p136087 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-06-18_ENG.jpg)

- Fixed **Sorcerer Ashcrombe** in **SFK** not opening the door.

- Players who still didn’t have any underwear on have been graciously gifted some pants — except some gnomes. They’ll have to cover their bums with the next client patch.

- Creatures will no longer cast **Mana Burn**-type spells on targets with no mana.

- **Shamans** should no longer damage themselves with their own **Lightning Shield** under any circumstance.

- Players should now be unflagged for FFA PvP after leaving **Blood Ring**.

- Fixed **Islen Waterseer** not offering to give players more **Water Sapta**.

- Fixed the book's name "Solution To Arcane Resonance".

- Fixed unminable **Ooze Covered Mithril Vein** in **Shimmering Flats**.

- You can now interrupt spells cast by **Warpwood Moss Flayer** and **Razormane Seer**.

- Race changing will no longer turn **Royal Seal** into **Insignia of the Alliance**.

- **Touch of Darkness** undead skin will no longer change your face and features.

- **Rake** now scales fully with **Tiger's Fury**.

- Fixed race requirement for **Furen's Letters** quests.

- **Anubisath Guardians** corpses now despawn after default time or on boss pull.

- **Ragnaros** is now immune to Freeze effects.

- Reduced all thrown weapons' buying price from 4× the selling price to 1.5× the selling price. This change should reduce repair costs of thrown weapons by 62.5%.

- **Hellfury Shard** is now correctly immune to Bleed effects.

- Fixed an issue where **Saltbeard**, **Blackeye**, and **Ironhoof** wouldn't have threat tables or reset properly.

- Fixed **Oozing Bag**'s loot table.

- **Naxxramas**: Fixed magnetic pull threat swap.

- The **Scythe of Elune**'s on-use effect now shares a cooldown with other on-use trinkets that increase spell damage.

- **Defias Mage Drape** is now Bind on Pickup.
