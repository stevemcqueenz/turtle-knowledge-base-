# 2023 — June 21

## Post 50762 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-06-20T22:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50762#p50762 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Increased the drop chance of Righteous Crusader's Helmet.

- Made various grammar fixes for custom quests.

- Fixed an issue where Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer and Warchief's Blessing buffs were not properly applying to players in all parts of the city.

- Mr. Bigglesworth will now attack nearby maggots.

- Changed Lady Katrana Prestor's respawn time to no longer be 30 minutes when she despawns for the quest The Great Masquerade.

- Winterax Trolls near the banner and directly on the other side of the bridge to the cave will now despawn once players capture Snowfall Graveyard.

- Made the Dreadlord in Blasted Lands immune to Enslave Demon.

- Changed General Drakkisath's Flamestrike to target a random player instead of always targeting the tank.

- Reworked the Midsummer Fire Festival Event for better functionality.

- Added a new donation reward: Guild Name Change token.

- Fixed the quest category of Warsong Gulch care package quests to "Warsong Gulch" instead of Ashenvale.

- Fixed the quest category of select Arathi Basin mark turn-in quests to "Arathi Basin" instead of Arathi Highlands for consistency.

- Adjusted the reputation requirement for Take 5 Bases Arathi Basin quests to Exalted-500 for quality of life improvement.

- Added a missing +10 reputation reward for several WSG and AB care package quests.

- Removed exclusivity from care package quests, allowing all three to be claimed for quality of life improvement.

- Changed League of Arathor reputation reward for Capture Four Bases from 800 to 1000 to match Horde's counterpart.

- Increased the hourly mail limit.
