# 2024 — February 14

## Post 87728 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-02-13T21:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87728#p87728 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_feb14.png)

- Moved Moroes back to the stage.

- Removed broken and deprecated script from Bebri Coifcurl in Sparkwater Port. No other races have their own barber available, and we aren't planning on adding them. This NPC was added during the earlier days of development when we wanted to provide a quick fix for those players whose model got drastically altered in one of the updates.

- Blackstone Sea Shell will now correctly drop from Blackstone Sea Shell objects for the quest "She Sells Sea Shells."

- Dying during Hardcore Challenge will no longer remove you from your guild.

- Sent Klockmort Spannerspan to Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility.

- Mechanical Dragonling pet HP changed from 3184 to 400.

- Mithril Dragonling damage is increased to 41, and now it casts "Flame Breath" from time to time.

- Arcanite Dragonling damage is slightly increased to 71.

- NPC Vilewing Bat, Vilewing Batling Greater Vilewing Bat: The bat is tamed correctly and eats food appropriate for the pet's class.

- NPC Gelwig Darkbrow has the correct name #5790.

- Quest: Danonzo's Tel'Abim Surprise has the correct short description. #5584.

- Quest: A Flickering Flame has the correct main description.

- Solid Chest in Tanaris (75.3, 46.1) is now available for looting.

- Battered Chest in Tanaris (71.4, 76.6) is now available for looting.

- Maexxna will now enrage if taken out of her room.

- Made changes to Thorium Vein pools to try and solve issues with them.

- Reworked Wind Stones in Silithus.

- Invisible walls around Kel'Thuzad's throne will now be removed once the boss is killed.

- Implemented yearly reset for seasonal quests, like those part of the Lunar Festival event, so that players can complete them again each year.

- Fixed the possibility to bypass the minimum HP threshold of creatures below which they should be invincible if the attacker has on-hit / on-cast procs that deal damage.

- Reworked the script for The Missing Diplomat quest involving Private Hendel.

- Scarlet Monastery pathfinding will be more aggressive; kite exploits based on previous mmaps will not work anymore.

- BRD arena mobs will start chasing players once spawned. Previously, they were only able to walk to a specific point and go back.

- Valentine Pledge and Gift items are now Bind on Pickup.

- New donation item: Pet Rename Token (Hunters).

- Battered Viking Shield is now classified as Uncommon.

- Adjusted stats of Fallen Crusader Legguards: Increased Strength from 17 to 22, Added 10 Agility and 1% Physical Hit, Removed 28 Damage to Undead.

- Adjusted stats of Wing of the Time-Lord: Reduced Haste from 2% to 1%, Agility and Strength from 12 to 11 and Stamina from 10 to 8. This change was done due to this cloak performing on-par with Naxxramas cloaks while being acquired from a dungeon reputation grind.
