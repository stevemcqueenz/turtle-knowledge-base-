# 2023 — October 25

## Post 73511 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-10-25T00:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73511#p73511 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This week another week full of bug fixes and some QoL improvements. Thank you for reporting all issues encountered.

- General fixes:
 - Fixed a long-standing respawn bug with Silithus Wind Stones.  All Lesser Wind Stones now respawn after 5 minutes, Wind Stones after 15 minutes, and Greater Wind Stones after 3 hours, as per Blizzard respawn timers.

 - Linked an errant bonfire in Silithus to the Midsummer Fire Festival event so it doesn't remain year-round.

 - Item Wisdom of the Sages will now drop for everyone in the party.

 - Fixed sound effect of the item Gemstone of Ysera.

 - Fixed sound effect of item Arcane Goods and Pendant of Arcane.

 - Fixed the vendor price of the gloves of the Unknown.

 - Alah'Thalas civilians should no longer be skinnable. (Yes we know, horror)

 - Allowed mounting in Emerald Sanctum.

 - Allowed mounting in Booty Bay.

 - Fixed a mixup of stats between Dreamthread and Dreamsteel Mantles.

 - Fixed Emerald Sanctum hardmode Erennius not attacking sometimes.

 - The command "guildname" used to rename guilds now allows spaces.

 - Added safeguards to prevent Lord Blackwald II from respawning. **Players who have previously exploited this bug will have the loot from exploited kills taken from them.**

 - Corrected the melee damage schools of multiple creatures.

 - Mana Stalker will no longer cast Mana Burn on targets without mana.

 - Fixed exploit with Antu'sul summoning too many creatures.

 - Fixed the Nordanaar Inn moonwell.

 - The cooldown on Thornpod will now show correctly after changing maps or relogging.

 - Fixed equip effect of Uldum Construct Stompers.

 - Corrected movement type and waypoints for multiple creatures.

 - Various quest fixes by QA.

 - Fixed PvP not being disabled on flight paths.

 - Corrected Auberdine Sentinel waypoints.

 - Removed low-level item drops from various Gilneas NPCs.

 - Deeptide Mystic should now properly cast its spells.

 - Fixed an issue with Moroes not being interactable in Lower Karazhan Halls.

 - Fixed an issue where Shaman's Far Sight allowed the player to drop combat.

 - Fixed an issue where the Druid spell Emerald Blessing would decrease mounted speed.

 - Fixed some issues with Armor Penetration on items.

 - Fixed missing armor class on Regenerating Robe.

- The mid-patch itemization update is here! check [viewtopic.php?t=10971](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10971) for details.

- We've heard your feedback on Hyjal and have made various changes to the zone and its content:

 - All Hyjal collectible quests reward a bright shard instead of a small one.

 - All Hyjal quest items for collectible quests no longer require the quest to be picked up in order to get the quest item.

 - Some collectible items drop rates increased to match other quests of similar difficulty.

 - Increased droprate of small dream shards from the elite trolls of Shadeflayer Tribe.

 - Increased droprate of small dream shards from Emerald Sanctum trash.

 - Increased droprate of dream shards and dreamscale from Erennius and Solnius in Emerald Sanctum.

- We've updated the ingame radio with all sorts of new tracks and tunes.
Please don't forget to delete your WDB folder!
