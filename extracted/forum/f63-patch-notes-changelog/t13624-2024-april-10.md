# 2024 — April 10

## Post 92466 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-04-09T19:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92466#p92466 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_april9.png)

- Fixed an issue where the last charge of Eye of the Dead did not increase healing.

- Corrected a problem where negative damage taken bonuses, such as Dampen Magic, were not functioning correctly.

- Added bandages (hands, shirts) to the inventory of vendors Balai Lok'wein and Deneb Walker.

- It is now impossible to bypass killing all the captains in the Rajaxx encounter in AQ20.

- The Gothik encounter in Naxxramas will now be terminated if the number of living players per side falls below 10 before phase 1 begins.

- Fixed the Natural Shapeshifter talent not reducing the mana cost of Tree of Life.

- Fixed an issue where the Berserk aura was removed when switching between Cat and Bear forms.

- Addressed an exploit that allowed the summoning of engineering pets immediately upon learning the profession without needing the corresponding item.

- Sunnyglade-specific buffs can no longer be taken out of the battleground and retained until they expire.

- Quests to defeat mini-bosses in Sunnyglade will now reset upon joining, allowing them to be completed in each game.

- Increased the total health of faction bosses in Sunnyglade.

- Introduced a notification sound when engaging the final bosses in Sunnyglade.

- Increased the honor awarded at the end of Sunnyglade.

- Special trinkets can now be looted from players defeated in Sunnyglade.

- Lowered the minimum number of players required to start Sunnyglade.
