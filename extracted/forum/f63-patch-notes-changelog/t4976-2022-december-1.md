# 2022 — December 1

## Post 30093 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-12-02T00:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30093#p30093 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Removed mana cost from Crusader Strike debuff.

- Judgement of the Crusader can now trigger weapon procs.

- Fixed Fey Dreamcatcher.

- Added buy and sell prices to Dark Iron Residue.

- Reduced respawn time of quest object Ancient Treasure Chest.

- Slightly buffed higher ranks of Elune's Grace.

- Added a small chance for high level herbs to contain Black Lotus.

- Improved various NPC scripts.

- Fixed an exploit that allowed to set a wronk client-link as DnD or AFK message.

- Dreadmaul Skull now can stack 30x.

- Fixed various grammatical oversights in custom quests.

- Fixed various reputation gains for custom quests.

- Hargul the Hardy is now correctly typed a giant instead of a Humanoid.
