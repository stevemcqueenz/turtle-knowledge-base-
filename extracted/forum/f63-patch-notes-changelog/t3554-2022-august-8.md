# 2022 — August 8

## Post 21351 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-08-07T23:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21351#p21351 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Your game will automatically update to Patch 1.16.2 tonight.

Please read the change logs of the upcoming update:

- Compact Harvest Reaper will now break after 10 minutes.

- Compact Harvest Reaper can no longer be healed through HoTs.

- Banish will now prevent Faerie Fire.

- Added missing Bubbly Fissures for the druid class quest.

- Wyvern Sting will now break on damage.

- The resistance portion of Sanctified Command works.

- Mana gained from Sanctified Command will show up in the combat log.

- Added missing progress text for the Remember Alterac Valley quest that confused players.

- Fixed cast time for Decisive Strike not matching the tooltip.

- The crafting time of some professional items is now reduced with increased skill as is blizzlike.

- Fixed respawn time of Chromatic Elite Guard.

- Implemented Warlock's Demon Portal which summons a Felguard pet.

- Scourge Footsoldiers spawned during the Priest class quest should no longer evade prematurely.

- Hunter's Entrapment talent should now work.

- Battery Powered Crowd Pummelers are now implemented and can be crafted.

- Remove Curse is now usable in Tree of Life form.

- Fixed issues with The Empty Stables quest in AV.

- Warrior's Retaliation will now work.

- Mage's Ritual of Refreshment will now work.

- Shaman's Feral Spirit will now work.

- If you have multiple guardian pets summoned at once, they will now spread out around you, instead of all stacking on top of each other.

- Fixed client crash in the Gnomeregan area of Dun Morogh.

- BG Finder now requires a left click instead of a right one.

- Excluded mandatory sound in the background modification.

- Improved Sap will now work.

- Summon Felguard will now work.

- Brilliance Aura will now work.

- Fixed bug with the guild creation charter requiring to be in the main bag.

- Vampiric Touch now restores correct mana percentages.

- Added scaling to Shadow Mend.

- Improved description of Battery Powered Crowd Pummeler.

- Fixed not being able to use First Aid during Divine Shield.

- Updated visual effect and sound for Felguard.

- Removed spell pushback on damage from Slam.

- Fixed hit chance of druid Natural Weapons talent.

- Increased stats of all guards in the world.

- Added prevented measurements from Horde ganking Defias Messenger.

- Fixed Warlock's Ritual of Souls.

- Fixed returning player to the position he entered from after leaving BG.

- Fixed Demonic Aegis warlock talent.

- Increased spell power scaling for Consecration.

- Fixed Searing Totem bonus coefficients.

- Fixed Judgment of Righteousness bonus coefficients.

- Added condition for earning Lorekeeper title.

- Added rested bonus to the Karfang Hold.

- Armored Azure Drake has been replaced with Immortal Champion's Charger in order to preserve the unique spirit of flight-less Azeroth.

- Added /road sounds to all races.
