# 2025 — February 26

## Post 120933 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-02-25T20:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120933#p120933 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2025-02-26_4.jpg](./download/file.php?id=136) <dd>Changelog_2025-02-26_4.jpg (448.56 KiB) Viewed 16459 times</dd>

- Fixed **Libram of the Faithful** proccing when it shouldn't.

- Spells that are enabled or triggered on partial resists will no longer do so when the damage makes the target more vulnerable.

- Killing players connected from the same IP address as you no longer grants honor.

- **Nelly Cogwheel** will no longer fall through the tower.

- There should no longer be multiple copies of Demetria and her bodyguards spawned at the same time.

- Fixed an issue where new battlegrounds could be created while ongoing ones still had pending invitations.

- Fixed an issue where battleground queue positions were ignored when determining which players to invite.

- **PvP Battle Standards** can now only be used by their respective factions.

- **Korrak the Everliving** and The **Return of Korrak** are now repeatable quests. However, the **Skull of Korrak** is no longer a party drop.

- In addition to regular Warlock demons, greater demons can no longer be buffed.

- Korrak's health has been increased since there are now more incentives to kill him with the quests being made repeatable.

- Debuffs that reduce spell casting speed can no longer affect **Chromaggus** and **Sapphiron**.

- Fixed Ouro's Dirt Mounds not doing anything.

- Fixed **Level One Lunatics** not being able to speak despite fulfilling the requirements.
