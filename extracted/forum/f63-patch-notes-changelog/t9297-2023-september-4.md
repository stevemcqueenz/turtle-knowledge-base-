# 2023 — September 4

## Post 60730 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-09-04T10:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60730#p60730 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- To ensure compliance with our public channel language rules (<https://turtlecraft.gg/#/rules#chat-abuse>), we have enforced English as the mandatory language for communication in the General, Trade, World, and /yell channels. This measure aims to minimize the need for GM intervention.

- The respawn timer on Poppies in Redridge Mounts have been properly lowered, for real this time!  They should now have a 90 second respawn timer.

- The Spirit Pyres in Amani'Alor will now only burn for 15 seconds for parity with the Alliance version of this quest.

- The Cartographer title no longer requires Scarlet Basilica.

- Thorium Greatsword can now be disenchanted.

- The quest "Stealing A Core" is now properly labeled as a Dungeon quest.

- Fixed a bug that allowed tents to be stacked in close proximity of one another.

- Reworked the Mirage Raceway event to fix issues with players getting stuck unable to move or keeping their increased speed indefinitely.

- The Winterfall Ambushers spawned after turning in the Winterfall Runners quest will now attack the player.

- Removed statically spawned Hive'Ashi Ambushers near the Hive'Ashi Pod in Silithus as part of the "Hive in the Tower" quest.

- Scarlet Recruit and Vladeus Springriver are no longer aggressive to prevent accidental death of Hardcore players passing by the bridge.

- The Giantstalker and Dragonstalker Armor 5 piece set bonus no longer prevents the player from being unflagged for PvP.
