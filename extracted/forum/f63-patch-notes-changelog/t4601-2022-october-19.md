# 2022 — October 19

## Post 27514 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-10-18T22:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27514#p27514 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- **Azeroth is round! Kalimdor and Eastern Kingdoms are now in different time zones with an eight hour difference for increased immersion. Take that, flat Azerothians!**

- Fixed an ancient bug that caused permanent periodic damage auras on creatures, like the disease cloud on abominations, to start ticking faster and faster over time, eventually one-shotting players that go near the creature if server is online long enough.

- Abominations have returned to Eastern Plaguelands.

- Flare will now correctly prevent enemies from casting Stealth while standing in it, instead of only removing it upon first entering the spell radius.

- Added appropriate skinning loot to Ishamuhale.

- It's now possible for rogues to disarm traps by right click, instead of having to first click the spell and then select the trap.

- Druid's Enrage now correctly reduces armor.

- **You can now buy back items sold to vendors even after logging out. Items will remain in the list until you sell enough other items to push them off the buy back page.**

- **You can now buy back blue or higher quality items you've destroyed from a special vendor. No need to bother the Game Masters with tickets about item restorations! Seek out Gaston the trash collector in cities.**

- Players should no longer wrongly take fall damage when stepping off ships. Hopefully.

- The warrior Improved Disciplines talent no longer wrongly decreases the cooldown of Whirlwind.

- The order in which items are displayed on vendors has been corrected, and multiple missing items have been added to various vendors across the world.

- Fixed issues with shaman's Elemental Focus talent. The buff should no longer go up to 4 stacks, and Chain Lightning should only consume 1 stack no matter how many targets it hits.

- The leash range of Xalvic Blackclaw has been increased.

- Removed level based resist chance from Holy spells. Not to be confused with chance to miss.

- Ferocious Rage Scar should no longer cast Rend Flesh on far away targets.

- Removed low level items from Fire Roc's loot table.

- Corrected issues with stealth auras on several mobs.

- Dampen Magic can no longer reduce magic melee damage below half.

- Multiple fixes for grammar issues reported in our bug-tracker.

- Multiple fixes loot/creature/quests inconcistencies reported in our bug-tracker.
