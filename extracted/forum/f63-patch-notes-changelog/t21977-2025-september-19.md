# 2025 — September 19

## Post 152301 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-09-18T18:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152301#p152301 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- **Verdant Rune** now disappears once all of its charges have been depleted.

- Removed problematic combat bugged NPCs from **Stormrought Ruins**.

- Items required for the quest "**Pestering the Pesterhide**" will now drop once again.

- **Brangar's Journal** is no longer available to the Horde.

- **Webwood Hatchling** and **Wildthorn Hatchling** will properly drop from their correct spiders again.

- Fixed an issue where the Horde version of "**You Reap What You Sow**" did not teach the player to craft the Simple Wooden Planter.

- **Baited Shot** now stacks across different hunters.

- Fixed an exploit that allowed players to gain an unintended advantage in **Alterac Valley**.
