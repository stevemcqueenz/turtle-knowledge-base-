# 2025 — July 16

## Post 139703 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-07-15T21:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139703#p139703 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-07-16.jpg)

- Fixed several ranged weapons using the incorrect hand to hold the weapon, including: **Farstrider Lodge Protector's Bow**, **Quilflinger**, **Silvermoon Bow**, **Balanced Light Crossbow**, **Lodestone**, **Crackling Zapper**, **Diathorus' Claw**, **Burning Torch**, **Cryptwatcher's Call**.

- **Elune's Rage** now grants increased spell critical strike chance instead of melee, and its bonus Fire damage now triggers from spell critical strikes.

- Fixed an issue where some triggered spells were improperly reflected in the combat log.

- Fixed a harpy that was stuck underground in **Feralas**.

- Fixed an issue where the **Slow and Steady** challenge would incorrectly affect other group members.

- Fixed an exploit in the **Mograine and Whitemane** encounter which allowed one of the bosses to be repeatedly killed.

- You can no longer level **Lockpicking** by opening **Benedict's Chest**.

- Fixed missing armor visuals on **Otherworldly Spaulders**.

- Fixed a bug where mini-pet companions would sometimes appear on mounts or have incorrect auras.

- Fixed an issue where **Twilight Shadowmages** were not casting **Dominate Mind**.

- Moved two **Peacebloom** nodes to accessible areas in **Redridge**.
