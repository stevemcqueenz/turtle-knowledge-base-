# 2025 — May 7

## Post 129934 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-05-06T22:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129934#p129934 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-05-07.jpg)

- Restored the internal cooldown for **Ornate Bloodstone Dagger**.

- **Pick Pocket** can no longer trigger any procs or on-cast effects.

- Restored the functionality of **Totem of Flowing Water**.

- Fixed an issue where **Idol of Evergrowth** was not functioning correctly.

- Fixed an issue where **Stinging Nettle** (Rank 2) did not function correctly with **Immolation Trap**.

- Fixed an issue where the spell hit chance reduction component of **Smoke Bomb** was not working correctly.

- The temporary pet summoned by the **Blackblade of Shahram** can now attack neutral targets the player is attacking.

- **Black Broodling** and **Peculiar Broodling** now share spawn points.

- Reduced **Cho'Rush the Observer's** mana regen.

- Increased aggro radius of **Infinite Rift-Lord**.

- Updated several items to no longer require only direct damage/healing landing spells to trigger their effects. These items can now proc from successful harmful or helpful spell casts, respectively:
 - **Breath of Solnius**

 - **Blood Tiger Harness** (2/2 set bonus)

 - **Bindings of Contained Magic**

 - **Vestments of Faith** (8/8 set bonus)

 - **Loop of Unceasing Frost**

- Tooltips for these items will be corrected and clarified in the next client patch where applicable.

- **Wrath of Cenarius** can once again trigger multiple times from a single cast, such as when area of effect spells hit multiple targets.

- Talking to **Darius Ravenwood** will now complete the quest **The Rebellion** even if you're in a raid group at that time.

- Open world version of **Sanv Tas'dal** in **Swamp of Sorrows** will no longer attack players first.

- **Cla'ckora's Crustaceous Claws** is now a frontal cleave ability.

- Fixed an issue where **Improved Shred** did not provide the expected bonus damage.

- Fixed an issue where **Druids** could switch shapeshift forms while affected by **Involuntary Transformation** during the **Nefarian** encounter.

- **Wandering Faerie Dragon** no longer drops **Green Whelp Scale** when skinned.

- **Thunderbrew Specialty Mug** can no longer be used as a weapon.

- All creature packs in **Crescent Grove** now pull together when one of them is attacked.

- Corrected missing and wrong pickpocket loot tables for **Groveweald Furbolg**.

- **Master Raxxieth** now stuns everyone when he reaches half health.

- Updated **Crescent Grove Quest Rewards**:
 - **Grol's Band**: Increased Agility from 2 to 3, Added 3 Intellect.

 - **Cloak of Denatharion**: Increased Intellect from 7 to 9, Increased Spirit from 4 to 5, Increased Healing Power from 9 to 11, Renamed to "Groveweave Cloak".

 - **Chain of Denatharion**: Renamed to "Sentinel Chain".

 - **Epaulets of Denatharion**: Renamed to "Epaulets of Forest Wisdom".

 - **Mask of Denatharion**: Renamed to "Bushstalker Mask".

 - **Chain of Loruk**: Increased Stamina from 15 to 16, Increased Strength from 9 to 10, Reduced Spirit from 6 to 4, Renamed to "Outrider Chain".

 - **Cloak of Loruk**: Renamed to "Strongwind Cloak".

 - **Shoulders of Loruk**: Renamed to "Foreststrider Spaulders".

 - **Hat of Loruk**: Increased Intellect from 10 to 14, Increased Spirit from 6 to 9, Increased Healing Power from 11 to 22, Renamed to "Hat of Forest Medicine".

- Updated **Azshara Revamp Quest Rewards**:
 - **Flaxwhisker Sash**: Reduced Intellect from 20 to 17, Increased HP5 from 3 to 6.

 - **Wobblefree Fizz-rifle**: Reduced DPS from 28.7 to 27.4, Added 10 Attack Power.

 - **Turbo-Charged Fizz-Ring**: Increased Intellect from 8 to 11, Increased Spellpower from 6 to 8.

 - **Bloodfist Vambraces**: Increased Stamina from 6 to 10, Replaced 1% Parry with 5 Defense.

 - **Crown of Unstable Nature**: Increased Intellect from 13 to 15, Increased Spirit from 5 to 7, Increased Spellpower from 8 to 16.

 - **Blademaster's Binding**: No longer has 5 Stamina and Strength.

 - **Gigno's Overalls**: Increased Strength and Agility from 8 to 17, Added 9 Intellect.

 - **Gigno's Spanner**: No longer requires 200 Engineering to use.

 - **Legplates of the Raider**: Increased Strength from 10 to 19, Increased Agility from 18 to 19, Increased Intellect from 13 to 14.

 - **Mantle of Dag'grak**: Increased Strength from 11 to 12, Increased Stamina from 4 to 6, Replaced 13 Spirit with 12 Agility, Added 1% Haste.

 - **Spiritual Mitts**: Increased Spirit from 11 to 15, Increased Stamina from 5 to 8, Replaced 6 Agility with 8 Intellect, Increased Healing Power from 7 to 13, Added 15 Spellpower.

- **Lighthouse Keeper Boots**: Replaced 3 Strength with 6 Agility, Increased Spirit from 4 to 6.

- **Centaur Stompers**: Reduced Agility from 12 to 10.

- Adjusted armor for **Bloodsail Eyepatch** and **Crab Catcher's Hat**.

- Cla'ckora is now properly Level 63 and can no longer be Mind Controlled or Polymorphed.

- Level requirements have been restored to **Ragefire Chasm**, but **Level 1 Lunatics** can now bypass it. This applies to **Deadmines** as well.

- Increased bag sizes for various endgame bags:
 - **Bottomless Bag**: 18 > 20 Slots.

 - **Panther Hide Sack**: 18 > 20 Slots.

 - **Supply Bag**: 18 > 20 Slots.

 - **Onyxia Hide Bag**: 18 > 20 Slots.

 - **Mooncloth Bag**: 16 > 18 Slots.

- You can now get credit for the quest **Making a Statement** even while in a raid.

- Updated **Swamp of Sorrows Revamp Quest Rewards** and **Vendor Stock**:
 - **Scepter of Aka'sha**: Reduced Intellect from 10 to 6.

 - **Talisman of Kar'noom**: Reduced Stamina from 8 to 7, Reduced Spirit from 8 to 4.

 - **Outland Exile Hood**: Increased Stamina from 2 to 5, Increased Spellpower from 7 to 11.

 - **Draenic Wanderer Harness**: Replaced 7 Spirit with 7 Strength.

 - **Harborage War-Mace**: Replaced 2 Spirit with 6 Attack Power.

 - Adjusted DPS for **Sorrowguard Shortsword**.

 - **Lightgraced Mallet**: Increased Intellect from 6 to 7, Replaced 4 Healing Power with 6 Spellpower.

 - **Sorrowguard Clutch**: Increased Stamina from 8 to 10.

 - **Truthkeeper Mantle**: Increased Strength from 8 to 10, Increased Intellect from 7 to 10, Increased Spirit from 4 to 5.

 - Adjusted armor for **Swamphunter's Eye** and **Swamplink Harness**.

 - Updated Swampguard Girdle: Replaced 3 Intellect with 1% Dodge.

 - **Swampseeker's Kilt**: Replaced 7 Stamina with 3% Meditation.

 - Added 3 MP5 to **Leather Chef's Belt**.

- Forked Mudrock Tongue drop chance has been increased from 25% to 50% for the quest **"Mudrock Soup and Bugs"** and it now drops for all party members.

- **Mirefin Head** for the quest **"Hungry!"** now drops for all party members.

- When a raid boss is killed any dead group members outside will get bound to the instance as well.

- Increased the cast time of Mephistroth's Shards of Hellfury.

- Fixed an issue where Onyxia's loot was incorrect.

- **Tel'Abim** will now have access to same-faction race change tokens in the shop.
