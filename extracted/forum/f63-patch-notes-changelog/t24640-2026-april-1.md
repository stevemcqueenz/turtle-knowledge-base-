# 2026 — April 1

## Post 173490 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-03-31T19:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2026-04-01-ENG.jpg)

- Added kill reputation for **Timbermaw Hold** to **Blackroot Furbolgs** in **Moonwhisper Coast**.

- Reduced reputation gained from completing the quest **Glowing Draenethyst Cluster** to 250.

- Removed experience gained from completing the quest **Core of the Elements**.

- **Emergency Parachute** can now stack up to 5.

- Restored seasonal conditions for **Winter Squid** and **Raw Summer Bass** in **Moonwhisper Coast**.

- Fixed rare dead catches while fishing in **Moonwhisper Coast**.

- Assigned minimum fishing skill requirements to the following areas: **North Sea**, **Ruins of Nendis**, **Whispering Shores**, **Vysnagosa's Rest**.

- Fixed an exploit in the **Ezzel** encounter in **Blackwing Lair**.

- Replaced **Sunnyglade Emissaries** with **Thorn Gorge Emissaries**.

- **Orgrimmar Grunts** will now correctly direct players to the **Survival Trainer**.

- **Totem of Calm Cascades** now scales with 15% of the player's Healing Power.

- Fixed **Cairne Bloodhoof**'s gossip.

- **Rushing Winds** and **Flurry** (Shaman) now stack.

- **Earthquake Aftershock** now benefits from **Elemental Fury**.

- Increased the proc per minute of **Rift Tear**.

- **Rift Tear** and **Midnight Comet** can no longer trigger spell effects.

- Receiving loot from a **World Boss** will now prevent further loot until the weekly reset.

- Corrected out-of-bounds creature spawns in **Moonwhisper Coast**.

- Players can now enable 'At War' with **Draenei Exiles**.

- Fixed an issue causing **Earthen Ring**-aligned NPCs to be uninteractable for Horde players.

- **Soul Entrapment** no longer increases damage when controlling a Greater Demon.

- Added missing Survival supplies to **Rufus Hardwick**.

- Removed stackability from several new **Well Fed** buffs.

- Reduced the respawn time of **Rotvine**, **Grammon the Ageless**, and **Tidelord Rrurgaz** to 10 minutes.

- **Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread** and **Dur'angor Melon** are now significantly more lethal when consumed.

- **Dur'angor Melon** now shares a 5-minute cooldown with **Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread**.

- Reduced the minimum level requirement of the Artisan Survival quest **To Survive in the Jungle** to 35.

- Fixed the sheathing position of **Prestor's Rod of Command**.

- **Ambassador Vortalus** will now properly lose **Engulfing Vortex** if the encounter ends prematurely.

- Fixed an issue preventing **Ceremonial Belt Buckle** from being applied to belts.

- Increased the drop chance of **Blackroot Totem**.

- Increased the price of **Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread** and **Dur'angor Melon**.

- Chests are now distributed according to group loot rules.

- **Pattern: Astronomer Raiments** is now properly consumed on use.

- Slightly increased the health of **Karrsh the Sentinel**, **Rotgrowl**, and **Kodiak**.

- Reduced the health of **Loktanag the Vile**, **Ormanos the Cracked**, and creatures spawned during the **Ormanos** encounter.

- Reduced the damage of several abilities used by **Loktanag the Vile**, **Selenaxx Foulheart**, and **Ormanos the Cracked**.

- **Trioch the Devourer** is no longer immune to bleed effects.

- Fixed minor issues in the **Archdruid Kronn** encounter.

- Fixed an issue with fixate mechanics during the **Ursol** encounter.

- Adjusted the secret encounter in **Timbermaw Hold**.

- Corrected the visual effect of **Glyph of the Moon**.

- **Insom'ni's Chest** will no longer spawn **Leni, the Silent Blade** unless players are on the associated quest.

- Fixed several **Timbermaw Hold** set bonuses not functioning correctly.

- **Lower Karazhan Halls** and **Hateforge Quarry** maps will now open correctly.

- Fixed tiny points of interest on the minimap.

- Fixed an issue preventing the **Transmogrify** UI from opening in some cases.

- Fixed **Survival** missing from auction house filters.

- Fixed errors when inspecting talents.

- Fixed missing enchantment text for **Facetted Crystal Scope**.

- Fixed minimap battleground finder level requirements for **Arathi Basin** and **Thorn Gorge**.

- Fixed a regression affecting chat frame docking.

- Increased the yield of the cooking recipes **Squid Eel Skewer** and **Deep Sea Stew**.

- Increased the damage of **Mar'kali, the Midnight Star** and reduced its internal cooldown.

- Fixed a possible crash in **Timbermaw Hold**.

- **Loktanag**'s boss area is now slightly brighter.

- Fixed visuals of leather, mail, and plate **Timbermaw** tier boots.

This maintenance will be followed by another **launcher update** to support fixes that require client‑side edits.
