# 2023 — May 24

## Post 47173 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-05-23T21:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47173#p47173 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Stability and performance improvements have been made. However, we advise hardcore gamers to exercise caution as these may not necessarily be improvements.

- Added one missing hair dye item for humans.

- Fixed a typo in Kelly Vaulstone's gossip.

- Fixed some items not playing their sounds in the player's inventory.

- Fixed the wrong Black Hair Dye color for goblin males.

- Reduced XP from killing Starving Bonepaw in Desolace.

- Marked the quest Treant Muisek as Elite.

- Marked the quest Army of the Black Dragon as Elite.

- Added a second cooking fire to Caelan's Rest tavern.

- Fixed Reindeer getting dismounted in water.

- Fixed Ivory Tallstrider getting dismounted in water.

- Fixed chest opening animations.

- Attempted to fix character creation issues.

- Feral Spirits will now always attack the same target as the Shaman and will partially scale with the Shaman's damage.

- Syndicate Shadow Mages now use a different version of Curse of Thorns.

- Changed the respawn time of all Expedition Officers to 5 minutes.

- Reduced polearm training cost to 10 silver.

- Added new gossip text for Thultash.

- A-Me 01 will now actively attack nearby enemies during his escort quest.

- The short scripted sequence that plays after turning in the quest Making Sense of It now plays when accepting the quest instead.

- Fixed some DoT auras wrongly being affected by armor.

- Opening some chests will now cause nearby mobs to aggro.
