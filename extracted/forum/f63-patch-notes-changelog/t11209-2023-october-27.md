# 2023 — October 27

## Post 74058 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-10-26T23:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74058#p74058 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Added missing drops to NPC Shadeflayer Goliath.

- Made various grammar fixes for NPCs, items, and quests based on community member reports.

- Increased Nordanaar Herbal Tea charges to 3.

- Improved scripts for Grol, Allistarj, and Sevine in Blasted Lands.

- Corrected waypoints for all Auberdine Sentinels.

- Deeptide Mystic, Razarri Mystic, and Razzari Priest will now cast spells.

- Players will now be properly unflagged for PvP when beginning a taxi flight.

- Made various improvements to the Counterattack quest.

- Miss Dana and her students will no longer get separated.

- Items won through group loot will no longer become free-for-all if the roll winner''s bags are full. Any rare or higher quality items that are still unlooted upon the corpse despawning will be sent to the winner by mail, instead of being lost forever.

- Fixed issue with Improved Seal of Justice incorrectly buffing Holy Shield damage instead of Seal of Justice proc chance.

- Fixed Thorium Spurs not increasing mount speed.
