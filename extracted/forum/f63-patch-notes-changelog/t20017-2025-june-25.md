# 2025 — June 25

## Post 136891 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-06-24T21:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136891#p136891 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-06-25_ENG.jpg)

- **Arch Hydromancer Lapidis** is back to being an Elite, as he should be.

- **Warsong Gulch Marks of Honor** can now be turned in below level 10 as well.

- Placing **Greater Blessings** on a pet will no longer override buffs on players and vice versa.

- Some versions of **Lizard Bolt**, **Dominate Mind**, **Frostbolt**, **Entangling Roots**, and **Wither** spells cast by mobs can now be interrupted when they previously couldn't.

- Leaving the instance now removes any debuffs applied by **Mephistroth**.

- Fixed an issue where completing the escort quest **Suntara Stones** would flag players for PvP.

- Made all **Kruul**'s spells ignore line of sight.

- Changed the drop chance of **Ephemeral Pendant** to 20% on all encounters that can drop it.

- Changed the drop chance of **Formula: Eternal Dreamstone Shard** to 1.5%.
