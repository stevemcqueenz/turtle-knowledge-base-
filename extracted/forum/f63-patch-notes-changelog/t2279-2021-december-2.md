**Greetings wonderful community!**

As you might remember from our announcement of **Class Changes**, one of our goals is to alter or enhance certain existing aspects of the game to uphold current standards, this also extends to items aswell.

Unique item design is one of the strongest parts of Vanilla, finding that item with the prefix "of the Bear" amongst the piles of rubbish has always felt good, feeling truly rewarded after getting that one item you grinded a dungeon for days. Even if we want to preserve this feeling there are items that are too weak for their qualities, that end up sharded whenever they drop.

This is very prevelant on crafted items too, easily getting replaced by dungeon or raid drops, ruining the feeling of accomplishment of gathering materials and forging your equipment with your own hands.

We intent to fix atleast some of these problems with this line of changes, improving niche epic world drops, epic items that require epic tokens from Upper Blackrock Spire, crafted items and more! And the best part, these changes are applied **right now**!
 **You can find the list of changes below.**

Love most of the changes, honestly. A lot of them were very needed, especially the mooncloth ones actually making the items at least make you question whether the price tag is worth it. Been seeing a few of those crop up on fresh 60s here and there for the first time ever.

Any thoughts on a possible new druid relic to make Hurricane usable for actual AoE dps instead of the spell you use to keep AoE tanks alive on a big pull? Currently the spell is horridly expensive mana-wise, the damage is middling, and it has a 60 second cd, meaning aside from the unique drop in mob attack speed it's the worst AoE spell that hits everything in an area. Not saying it should outshine mage or lock AoE, but when even holy nova on a holy priest can outshine hurricane dps I feel something could stand to change.

Maybe a relic that drops the cd in half, or maybe makes the mana cost cheaper, you could have the glyph increase it's duration, possibly add a movement slowing effect, or even just a buff to it's unique aspect of attack speed debuff, anything to make the spell worth casting in all but an incredibly sparse number of cases.

I've so far only ever used the spell in raids, never dungeons or solo, and in all cases but exactly one it was because I didn't have another AoE option, and even then I usually just revert to single-target wrath spam. The one other case was to give a paladin tank damage mitigation on Fankriss when tanking his little adds. That's literally it. There's so many places where AoE could be used in dungeons and even solo leveling and farming, but the downsides of the spell make it prohibitive on multiple fronts from doing what an AoE damage spell should do in my opinion.
