# 2023 — April 26

## Post 43089 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-04-26T00:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43089#p43089 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Removed Gold Veins in Western Plaguelands.

- Changed display of Foremand Silverhand.

- Fixed categories for various quests.

- Corrected the NPC type for Spirit of Palkeote.

- Added a few extra quests to allow becoming friendly with the Syndicate faction.

- Fixed Purple Tallstrider getting dismounted in water.

- Vengeful spirit will no longer flag players for PvP.

- Corrected reward XP for various quests.

- Corrected grammar for various quests.

- Fixed Improved Sap taking you out of stealth.

- Various performance improvements throughout the core to handle more players.

- Did various core stability fixes.

- Idol of the Moon will now increase total damage.

- Grimlok and his adds are now grouped and will have a smoother aggro radius.

- Added a new axe to the game as a proper quest reward for Deep Blue Sea in Durotar (replaces Orcish Cleaver, an item that was stolen from Thragomm).

- Removed erroneously-applied Required Level to Equip from numerous quest reward items.

- Updated the appearance of Kheyna Spinpistol.

- The chance for 5 of the 6 bosses in the final chambers of Scholomance to drop boss loot has been raised from 30% to 100%.  Previously, only Lord Alexei Barov had a 100% chance to drop any items from the shared 20-item drop table (4% chance per item), while the other 5 had a 30% chance (1.11% chance per item).

- Fixed a bug that caused the recently changed pet summon trinkets to have finite charges.

- Added Glowing Cat Figurine to the list of summon items that have been turned into trinkets.

- Convergence of the Elements Crafted Sets items have seen some adjustments to their stats due to an oversight on the item budget, you can see the changes below:

 -  Flamewrath Leggings: 28 -> 18 Strength, 18 -> 25 Agility, Removed 1% Critical Chance
 -  Depthstalker Helm: Removed 1% Dodge and 3 MP5
