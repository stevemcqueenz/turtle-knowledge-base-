Below is a mini changelog that will mostly include bugfixes of 1.17.1 with some othes fixes too:

- Improved NPC flee behavior.

- Removed Fine Free-Range Alterac Hide from vendors.

- Fixed a lot of model issues with various shop skins and illusions.

- Fixed durability visual problems on some new fashion gear.

- Fixed new Hunter quest objective for High Elf tame quests.

- Removed the items Red Hem Embroidered Waistcoat and Formal Mint Waistcoat from players and refunded their gold. These items have broken models and will return at a later date.
