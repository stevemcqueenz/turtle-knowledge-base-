# 2025 — January 1

## Post 115021 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-12-31T17:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115021#p115021 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2025-01-01_3.jpg](./download/file.php?id=107) <dd>Changelog_2025-01-01_3.jpg (433.49 KiB) Viewed 12144 times</dd>

- **Snowball** now only requires Apprentice Riding to learn.

- Slightly reduced **Harvest Golem Arm**'s damage values.

- Added missing reputation rewards to some **Durotar** quests.

- Renamed the original **Warlock Tier 3 Leggings** to **Plagueheart Pants** to resolve outfitter addon issues.

- Rhahk'Zor's Hammer is now Bind on Pickup.

- Corrected the sheathing of **Rudeus' Focusing Cane**.

- Lightwell is no longer usable while crowd controlled.

- Added a new donation item: **Famous Fashionista Glitterglam**, a stylish companion transmog NPC ready to help you look fabulous before battle.

- Fixed not being able to complete the quest **Set Them Ablaze** while on the Artisan challenge.

- Fixed Kanati Greycloud and Kaya Flathoof not attacking mobs during their escort quests.

- Fixed the disenchant output of the following items: **Moonlight Staff**, **Pendant of Arcane Radiance**, **Spellweaver Rod**, **Golden Jade Ring**, **Mithril Blackstone Necklace**, **Blackthorn Gauntlets**, **Ravenshire Gloves**, **Banshee's Tear**, **Signet of Gilneas**, **Ravenwood Belt**, **Dark Footpad Belt**, **Knife Juggler Gloves**, **Spaulders of Insight**.

- **Bulwark of the Righteous** now uses melee hit and critical strike chance for its effects.

- Fixed **Nefarian** and **Ragnaros** dropping too much loot.

- The Warlock talent **Improved Drains** now affects total damage rather than base damage.

- The Warlock **Felstone** effect now heals enslaved and greater demons.

- Fixed an issue where the Priest Tier 2 8-piece set bonus was not affected by talents that improve **Renew**.

- The damage bonus for **Claw** provided by Open Wounds has been corrected.

- Fixed an issue where **Arcane Surge** could not trigger **Resonance Cascade**.

- Fixed multiple issues with **Arcane Missiles** and **Accelerated Arcane**, ensuring their duration and amplitude are correctly calculated.

- **Overpower** is no longer unexpectedly enabled upon parrying.

- Fixed the Shaman's **Clearcasting** charges being consumed both on cast and landing.

- **Arcane Instability** no longer displays visuals on the Mage.

- Druid's **Eclipse** effect no longer triggers when the opposite effect is active.

- Fixed an issue with **Judgment of Command** where damage scaling from attack power was incorrect.

- Fixed the 4-piece bonus of the **Restoration Dreamwalker Set**, which now correctly reduces the mana cost of **Healing Touch**, **Rejuvenation**, and **Regrowth**.

- The Shaman T1 Battlegear 3-piece set bonus now reduces the mana cost of **Purge**, **Bloodlust**, and **Calm Elements** as intended.

- **Greater Blessing of Light** will now correctly increase healing of **Holy Shock**.
