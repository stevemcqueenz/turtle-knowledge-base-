# 2024 — March 6

## Post 89510 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-03-05T21:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89510#p89510 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_march6.png)

- Corrected the Seeker of Knowledge achievement text to accurately refer to a count of 2800 quests.

- Ignite no longer provides an additional tick upon initial application.

- Ignite no longer resets stacks when refreshing the debuff between the final tick and debuff expiration.

- Reflective Shield now correctly functions when shielding other players.

- Blink should no longer teleport the player downward when used in water.

- Fixed a potential crash due to Alzinn's script.

- Fixed a potential crash caused by Web Wrap's script.

- Fixed Vampiric Embrace and other procs that involve a debuff on the victim, ensuring they trigger on the killing blow.

- Fixed some triggered spells, like the damage component of Judgement, not displaying any message in the combat log if they miss.

- Ranged attacks will no longer incorrectly trigger Retribution Aura or Thorns.

- Reworked the Father Flame event in UBRS involving Solakar Flamewreath.

- Resolved the issue with Grobbulus poison clouds, preventing players from being kept in combat.

- Fixed wind stones breaking and becoming permanently non-interactable if two players click the gossip option at the same time.

- Chance on Hit effects, such as rogue poisons or shaman weapon enchants, will no longer cause you to unsheath your weapon and perform a casting animation when they proc.
