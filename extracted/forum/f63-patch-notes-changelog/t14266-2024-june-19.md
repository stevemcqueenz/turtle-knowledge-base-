# 2024 — June 19

## Post 97269 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-06-18T20:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97269#p97269 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_june19.jpg)

- Yeh'kinya's Bramble is no longer unique, allowing you to start the quest again even if you previously failed and put the item in your bank.

- Fixed an issue where flying mounts would completely break if you were jumping at the moment you clicked the option to become mounted.

- Fixed Fallen Soldiers occasionally rising from the dead and attacking totems.

- Fixed the current resource count on war effort collection NPCs not updating when players turn in quests.

- Fixed players getting stuck in combat with the pets of creatures.

- Fixed Mages not swapping their portals upon race changing.

- Fixed Druids being stuck in particular Glyphs.

- Fixed an exploit in the Gothik encounter.

- Stitches should no longer aggro players from abnormally high distances.

- Added missing skinning loot to some creatures.

- Fixed an exploit in the Kel'Thuzad encounter.

- Fixed a master loot exploit that allowed stealing items without being the designated master looter.
