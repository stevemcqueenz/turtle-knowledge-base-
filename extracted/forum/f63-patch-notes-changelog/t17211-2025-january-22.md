![Changelog_2025-01-01.jpg](./download/file.php?id=111) <dd>Changelog_2025-01-01.jpg (419.06 KiB) Viewed 46698 times</dd>

- Temporary skill bonuses are no longer considered when attempting to meet the skill requirements for equipping an item. ([Bug Report](https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=4179))

- Persistent area auras, such as **Blizzard** and **Consecration**, will now correctly deal all ticks as intended. ([Bug Report](https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=8542))

- Decreased the call-for-help radius of **Cursed Marines**.

- **Lord Valthalak's Amulet** will now be provided to players upon accepting the quest **Mea Culpa, Lord Valthalak** as expected.

- **Infectious Ghouls** and **Plagued Ghouls** will now only cast **Rend** when in melee range.

- Opening chests will no longer alert **Sapped** mobs.

- Shellcoin-related quests can no longer be completed by Hardcore players.

- **Purge** no longer consumes a charge of **Stormstrike**.

- It is no longer possible to store certain quivers and pouches inside other quivers and pouches.

- **Nibu** will now exit his cage once freed.

- Fixed an issue where Totems were unable to critically hit their targets.

- The unintended **Aura of Fear** mechanic has been removed from the creature **Dissipating Specter**.

- Fixed an issue where **Enslave Demon** would break early even with **Soul Link** active.

- Fixed an issue with **Thick Obsidian Breastplate** where it would incorrectly trigger from periodic spells. It now also has the expected cooldown.

- It is no longer possible to receive healing from triggered spells after death, preventing players from ending up in a semi-dead state.

- You can no longer disarm targets that have no main-hand weapon.

- Fixed a regression where creatures could use their melee abilities from a greater distance than intended.

- Auras that ignore armor based on weapon type will only function when the corresponding weapons are equipped.

- Fixed the **Siphon** used by **Ravenclaw Servant** dealing too much damage.

- **Kolkar Drape** now makes the proper sound when moved in inventory and is no longer invisible once equipped.

- Fixed **Crusader Strike** not triggering **Zeal** under **Divine Shield**.

- Eating and drinking will no longer generate threat.

- The **Everlook Broadcasting Co.** has been hijacked by an unknown assailant! Are you the one who can help **Zappo Zapblast** restore Azeroth's beloved radio?

- Fixed an issue with the **Frostfire Vestments** 6-piece bonus not being applied correctly.

 **New Tavern Feature: Guild Quarters Coming Soon**

Starting next week, guilds will have the ability to rent any tavern in the game that includes an Innkeeper, using either gold or tokens. These guild quarters can be located in Horde, Alliance, or neutral areas for cross-faction guilds. However, in cross-faction guilds, only players from the same faction as the guild leader will be able to use the guild teleport if the rented tavern is in a faction-restricted area.

In addition to the guild tabard teleport, these rented taverns will provide an extra rested bonus for guild members who are stationed there.

This system is being introduced to ensure all guilds, new and old, have fair access to the guild housing feature. Previously, older guilds had exclusive access to guild housing which created gameplay advantages newer guilds couldn’t share. Some older custom bases occupy space needed for upcoming zones and content expansions.

To meet the increased demand for housing, we will also be adding more taverns across Azeroth in the future.

 **Important Notes:**
- Pre-existing guild bases can apply to have an Innkeeper added to their base. We may approve or deny the application as some guild bases will be subject to removal for future content. Do note that this Innkeeper would allow any guild to set a teleport to that base.

- Setting your guild's base is a one-time cost.

- There is no limit to how many guilds may use one tavern.

- These taverns will serve as a new location for Guild Tabards to teleport to.
Stay tuned for more updates and details!
