## Post 101418 by Reynolds (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-09-17T20:18:00Z

![Changelog 2024-09-18 3.jpg](./download/file.php?id=11) <dd>Changelog 2024-09-18 3.jpg (412.47 KiB) Viewed 11551 times</dd>

- Charging now brings you to the outer edge of the target's combat range, rather than to the center of the unit.

- Fixed an issue where grouped players appeared ungrouped to themselves after re-logging into the game.

- Anti-flood chat restrictions no longer affect the LFG system.

- Applied various grammar and style improvements to quests and items submitted by players and editors.

- Doubled the loot chance for Wartorn Chain Scraps.

- Resolved an intermittent issue that prevented Alterac Valley from ending after completing the main objective.

- Newly created level 1 characters no longer impact faction balance restrictions.

- Tel'Abim PvP has entered Phase 4 – the Scourge event is live!
