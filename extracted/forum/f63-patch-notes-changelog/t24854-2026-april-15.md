## Post 175230 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-04-14T21:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175230#p175230 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2026-04-15-ENG.jpg)

- **Lophanel, Dusk of Calamity** will no longer be visible while sheathed.

- **Chimera Chops** and **Squid Eel Skewer** can now stack with other buffs.

- **Elixir of Brute Force** and **Juju Power** can no longer stack.

- **Enraged Roar** can now stack with other buffs.

- Picking up the **Thorn Gorge** flag is no longer affected by haste.

- Increased honor gains from **Thorn Gorge** by 50%.

- Reduced the damage of **Warleader Kentoza**.

- Fixed an issue where players could be saved to **World Bosses** without notification.

- Fixed issues with **Gri'lek the Wanderer**.

- **Presence of the Wild God** and **Blessing of the Bear God** will now be removed upon leaving combat or exiting **Timbermaw Hold**.

- **Peroth'arn's Dirk of the Beast** AoE component now ignores line of sight.

- Fixed visuals of leather, mail, and plate **Timbermaw Hold** tier boots.

- Fixed an issue preventing players from queueing as a group for **Arena Skirmish** and **Thorn Gorge**.

- Added rested bonus to the inns in **Moonhoof Village** and **Earthen Ring**.

This maintenance will be followed by another **launcher update** to support fixes that require client‑side edits.
