# 2023 — April 8

## Post 40776 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-04-08T14:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40776#p40776 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Greater blessing should work on non-grouped players again.

- The damage bonus from the Moonfury talent now applies after spell power.

- The damage bonus from the Improved Curse of Agony talent now applies after spell power.

- Sap will no longer proc damaging weapon effects or enchants.

- Hero of the Stormpike / Frostwolf has been added back into the game, but is the same as the Legend of Korrak quest.

- You can no longer attack players with the Steam Tonk's Mortar and Mine abilities.

- Warsong Outrider is no longer a civilian.

- Fixed the name of Darlthos' Jewelry Box.

- Fixed quest relations for The First Settlement, The Lost Tablets, and The Shadow Well.

- Reduced XP rewards from Welcome to Amani'Alor and To Amani'Alor quests.

- Head of Speaker Ganto will no longer drop from Witherbark Venomblood.

- Fixed some Venomflayer Serpent spawns respawning too quickly.

- Ornate Dagger of Jalvan now gives positive intellect instead of negative.

- Several performance fixes regarding big guilds.

- You will now no longer dismount when mounted in water, this goes for ALL mounts. (Their swimming speeds are unchanged)

- Hardcore players will no longer be flagged for PvP when accepting a PvP quest.

- Fixed low respawn time on some Venomflayer Serpent spawns.
