# 2023 — April 1

## Post 39845 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-04-01T07:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39845#p39845 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixes and improvements for the raid trading system.

- Various grammar fixes for quests and creatures from QA and bug-tracker.

- Added party loot flag to Ancient Thalassian Tablet.

- New script for Rift Spawns.

- Reduced the vendor price of Adventurer's Belt Pouch.

- Assigned correct creature type to the Teldrassil Owls.

- Assigned correct skinning loot to the Shadow Charger.

- Increased charge count to 10 for Scarlet Illusion.

- Reduced respawn time of Ancient Burial Container.

- Fixed issues with messed up guild rank on the creation of the guild.

- Fixed non-damaging abilities not triggering weapon procs.

- Added missing items to Bloodsail Quartermaster.

- Fixed quiver speed-bug with wands.

- Ostarius cooldown will now reset if summoned but not killed.

- It's now possible to fish out the Ashbringer, as it was meant to be.

- To address the recent performance issues, we have decided to relocate our infrastructure to Antarctica. This is because the colder climate will hopefully prevent our hardware from overheating.
 *Please note that the revision has been postponed until after the weekend due to potential stability issues.*
