# 2024 — July 10

## Post 99050 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-07-09T21:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99050#p99050 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_july10.jpg)

- You can no longer use a Goblin Brainwashing Device if another one is already nearby.

- The anti-afk Inactive debuff in Alterac Valley will now be removed upon interacting with banners.

- Canceling the Druid Enrage buff will also cancel the armor reduction component.

- The Reflective Shields talent will no longer trigger from self-inflicted damage.

- The "Invalid Target" error message will no longer appear on screen when absorbing damage from a dead player with the Reflective Shields talent.

- Fixed an issue where world buffs were not always applying to pets.

- Fixed an issue where the Ice Bear skin caused players to get teleported to enemies in some circumstances.

- Fixed an issue where Tel Co. Enforcers were not acting like guards.

- Fixed an issue where Janela Stouthammer was no longer flagged as a quest giver after the event until she respawned.

- Removed bleed immunity from training dummies.

- Alterac Valley will no longer erroneously have unbalanced teams.

- The bonuses from Demonic Sacrifice will no longer persist through swapping talent specs if the new spec does not have this talent learned.

- Renamed the crafting material Iron Belt Buckle to Iron Buckle to prevent confusion with the Blacksmithing belt enchant with the same name.
