# 2024 — July 3

## Post 98410 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-07-03T00:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98410#p98410 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_july3.jpg)

- Increased the drop chances of endgame custom dungeon Bind on Equip rare drops from 0.0031%-0.01% to 0.02%. This includes Caverns of Time: Black Morass, Stormwind Vault, and Karazhan Crypt.

- Fixed guild bank tabs and item issues.

- Players trying to complete a non-raid flagged quest while in a raid group will now get a red warning message on their screen.

- Janela Stouthammer will now properly turn in quests again.

- Fixed bosses in Sunnyglade becoming stuck in an unalive state.

- Slayer's Crest Use effect will now properly stack with other Attack Power buffs. (Stacking buffs/debuffs is an ongoing effort)

- Fixed Kor'gan becoming uninteractable.

- For Nordanaar: Players will no longer remain flagged for PvP after a battleground ends.
