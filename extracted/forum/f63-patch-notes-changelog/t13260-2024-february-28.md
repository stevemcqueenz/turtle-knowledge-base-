# 2024 — February 28

## Post 88858 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-02-27T17:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88858#p88858 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_feb28.png)

- The spell resisted status should now be correctly sent to the client in all cases.

- Retaliation will trigger Overpower if the target dodges it.

- Several quality assurance fixes have been implemented for Hyjal quests, including converting repeatable material turn-in quests into auto hand-ins and adding chaining behavior to numerous quest chains.

- A typo in the name of Ivory Hawkstrider has been corrected.

- Miniature Cannon Balls and Doomshoot are no longer Bind on Pickup and can now be traded and sold on the Auction House.

- Uldaman Stone Keepers will now self-destruct upon death.

- Calm Elements will no longer aggro mobs if it misses.

- Incorrect loot has been removed from Icepoint Whiteclaw.

- The Four Horsemen will no longer wrongly summon far-away players when they are already dead and have become ghosts.

- Additional invisible walls have been added to the Horde base in Alterac Valley.

- Eye of Divinity and Ancient Petrified Leaf now drop as intended.

- Klockmort Spannerspan will no longer teleport to Ironforge when someone turns in the quest Iron Coral.

- Hardcore death messages will no longer appear twice for priests with the Spirit of Redemption talent.

- Zaman's Claw is now BoP.
