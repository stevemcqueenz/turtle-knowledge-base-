# 2024 — January 03

## Post 83488 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-01-03T00:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83488#p83488 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Added a max leash range for Snowball.

- Fixed a bug that allowed classes other than Mages to learn Polymorph: Cow.

- Introduced Wildhammer reputation on kill for several Turtle custom Witherbark Trolls.

- Rectified several typos in the Gilneas Lighthouse quest chain.

- The Wavecrest Scale quest item is now lootable for the entire party.

- Assigned a sell price to the Garden Shovel quest reward weapon.

- Enhanced the minimum and maximum damage on Diathorus' Claw and Starcaller wands.

- Addressed some of Moroes' bugs in-battle; the fight should progress smoothly now.

- Rectified Venomhide Ravasaur's missing poison aura.

- Casting Blessing of Protection or Divine Intervention on the flag carrier in WSG is no longer possible unless the flag is dropped manually first.

- Marshal Reginald Windsor in BRD will no longer attack Horde players.

- Added missing cloth, scraps, and scrolls to Hateforge Chemist's loot table.

- Fixed The Black Shield quest chain.

- Empty Cursed Ooze Jar and similar items will now return an error when your inventory is full, instead of letting you waste the charges.
