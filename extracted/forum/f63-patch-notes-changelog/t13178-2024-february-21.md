# 2024 — February 21

## Post 88271 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-02-20T22:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88271#p88271 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_feb21.png)

- Added missing gossip to Field Marshal Chambers, ensuring the quest can now be completed.

- Klinfran's respawn time is now intended at 15 minutes.

- Ghost Wolf form speed updates instantly upon equipping Blood Guard's Mail Greaves.

- Corrected the misspelling of Earthen Dwarf in the shop.

- Shell coin price change announcements are now formatted instead of showing in copper.

- Fixed The Unforgiven in Stratholme.

- Some city guards previously not part of the Love is in the Air event can now receive love tokens.

- Fixed a bug preventing players neutral with an NPC's faction from giving love tokens.

- Kwee Q. Peddlefeet now buffs players who blow him a kiss as intended.

- Fixed an issue where Darkmaster Gandling's loot was inaccessible to the whole group.

- Pyroclasm now procs from Hellfire as intended.

- Item "Ironbark Branch" is now an off-hand frill.

- Reagents & Poisons vendors in AV now sell Crippling Poison II.

- NPC "Deepmoss Lurker" now contains loot.

- Fixed an issue where Silithus Windstones allowed summoning multiple bosses from the same stone.

- Decreased spell power scaling of the Equip effect of Idol of the Emerald Rot.

- Non-Latin characters are now allowed in guild names on SEA realms.

- Applied autoscaling to Vaelastrasz' health.

- Players are now teleported to the instance start when going out of bounds in Black Morass.

- Prevented Patchwork Golem's Cleave attack from hitting the caster.

- AoE abilities' reach now includes the target's combat reach in PvE, ensuring more consistent landing for big NPCs.

- Tome of Portals: Theramore and Stonard now actually drop from trash mobs in Lower Karazhan Halls.

- Spirit Shield rank 1 - 3 will now update armor when grabbing the talent.

- "Remove Insignia" will no longer be interrupted if the player releases the corpse.

- Players will now be automatically dismounted when mind controlled in any way.

- You can now turn in quests to Kwee Q. Peddlefeet during the post event phase.

- Added missing gossip text to Kwee Q. Peddlefeet.

- Removed wrong pick pocket loot from Hazzuri Hexxer and Speaker.

- Removed all gold veins in the Burning Steppes.

- Recipe: Major Mana Potion can now drop even if there is no alchemist in the group.

- Shredders in AV will now use their Armor Melt ability.

- Blacksmithing trainers will no longer offer to learn a specialization you already have.

- Made several anti exploit fixes to Heigan, Gothik and The Four Horsemen in Naxxramas.

- Totem of the Calming River will no longer generate threat when you are at full mana.
