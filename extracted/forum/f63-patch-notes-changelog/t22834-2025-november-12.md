# 2025 — November 12

## Post 159221 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-11-11T20:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159221#p159221 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-11-12-ENG.jpg)

- Fixed an issue introduced recently that affected the scaling of spell damage and healing effects.

- **Zebrian the Mad**’s path start has been adjusted so he no longer crosses phasing boundaries and disappears forever.

- Added missing **Stealth** and **Invisibility Detection** to the **Qiraji Gladiator**.

- **Druid’s Rip** and **Ferocious Bite**, Hunter traps with the **Untamed Trapper** talent, and **Rogue’s Eviscerate** and **Rupture** now also correctly scale with Attack Power bonuses against specific creature types.

- Fixed an issue where the **Warlock’s Malediction** talent did not function against creatures immune to physical damage.

- **Whirlwind** now correctly benefits from the Warrior talent **Impale**.

- **Taurens** will no longer lose **Plainsrunning** in Cross‑Faction Battlegrounds.

- Fixed an issue where sometimes **PvP items** would vanish in Cross‑Faction Battlegrounds.

- Fixed an issue where leveling up in Cross‑Faction Battlegrounds would modify your stats.

- Fixed an issue where you could not use **Reputation‑locked items** in Cross‑Faction Battlegrounds.

- Fixed **Kinriel’s Scalpel** having weird damage values.

- **Gilded Scarlet Charger** and **Beige Riding Scorpid** now require only **Journeyman Riding**.

- It is no longer possible to turn in **Onyxia’s head** a second time after changing your faction.

- Attempted to fix loot issues with the quest **Draining the Soulflayer**.

- **Plainsrunning** will now be unlearned when you change race from **Tauren** to another race.

- It is no longer possible to enter the **Cow Level** if you have not completed the required quest.

- **Bolvar** and **Tyrande** are now properly marked as faction leaders.

- **Battleground Queue System** will now prioritize matching you with your own faction before considering a Cross‑Faction swap.

- Fixed an issue where players could relearn toys and illusions that were already known.
