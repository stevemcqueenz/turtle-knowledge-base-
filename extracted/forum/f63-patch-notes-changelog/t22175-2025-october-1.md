# 2025 — October 1

## Post 153822 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-09-30T17:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153822#p153822 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-10-01ENG.jpg)

- **Mind Soothe** can no longer trigger any procs or on-cast effects.

- **Poison Spit** and **Immolation Trap** can now stack.

- **Distract** proccing offensive triggers has been fixed.

- Fixed an issue where the proc from **Spellwoven Nobility Drape** could not be triggered by periodic spells.

- **Chief Rnarl** should drop **Paw of Chief Rnarl** with 100% chance.

- **Belgar** should drop **Paw of Belgar** with 100% chance.

- **Guild Quarters Deed's** sell price has been removed.

- Inconsistent minimal levels for some chain quests in **Northwind** have been fixed.

- A crash in the **Battleground** code has been fixed.

- **Guild Quarters Deed** will not be refunded on death if already used.

- Reverted the fix for **Mana Attuned** due to identified regression.

- Fixed for a crash in the **Guild Bank** system caused by excessive gold storage have been implemented.

- Localization updates for Spanish, Portuguese, German, and Chinese locales have been applied.

- **Burnt Monk** is now classified as **Undead**.

- Reduced **Spirit** of **Udor's Pendant** from 8 to 7. Removed 4 **Stamina**, Added 3 **Strength** and **Intellect**.

- **Bishop's Reverence** now correctly includes the missing **Spirit** addition from last changelog.

- Reduced **Spellpower** of **Nathrezim Mantle** (Affliction) from 46 to 42. Added 1% **Haste**.

- Added missing dungeon quest tag to **The Farraki Ancient**.

- **Apprentice Fishing** can now be learned at level 1 by both Horde and Alliance.

- Quest **Bonescythe Ring** will now correctly appear under **Naxxramas** category in the quest log.

- **Hyjal Cub** item is now properly Soulbound.

- Added **Gravelweave Pantaloons** as a reward in the quest **The Redbrand Lie**.

- Adjusted the size of **Speedy** and **Lil’Ragnaros** minipets.

- Added the option to set your guild house to **Thomas Arello** at **Honey Drop Inn**.

- Added the option to set your guild house to **Brundah Cliffbrow** at **Hardul Distillery Inn**.

- Opened access for **Level One Lunatics** to **Wailing Caverns**.

- Added new drops exclusively for Lunatic-only parties when defeating **Lady Anacondra**.

- Players participating in leveling challenges **Vagrant’s Endeavor** and **Artisan’s Quest**, will no longer gain experience at the end of **Battlegrounds**.

Hey Torta
As always, thanks for the updates. Appreciated.

Regarding BAITED SHOT still being in a sorry state, could you at least give us a sign of acknowledgment?

First, the spell is currently BROKEN
 - Shares CD with arcane shot
 - Doesnt not proc from Spell abilities like Wind Serpent Lightning Breath or Gorilla Stomp
 - Doesnt proc from Coordinated Assault crit

Secondly, even if it were not broken, I think this ability is really bad both in terms of gameplay and in terms of the Spec's identity.
 - Not usable in melee which is often a playstyle chosen by BM hunter
 - It creates a weird 3-steps setup (first, kill command to have a crit; then baited shot; then only you get the bonus dmg on the target for the next attacks). it is impractical, barely usable in PVP and the setup/reward ratio is honestly bad.
 - Doesnt fit the Spec's identity.

Suggestion: replace it with more flexible BESTIAL COMMAND:
 - Your pet uses an ability based on its category: DPS, HYBRID, TANK
 - Similar philosophy as Warlock Master Demonologist talent whose effect depends on the minion used or as the Warrior's Master's strike effect which is based on the weapon you use
 - Widens the various BM gameplays and respects the class identity

What could the effect be? Here is my suggestion:
BESTIAL COMMAND (1m30sec cd)
 - DPS family: good old Bestial Wrath (probably would need to be a bit nerfed)
 - Hybrid pets: Intervene: Your pet runs at high speed towards you, intercepting the next attack made against you and regens 10% of your mana over 10%
 - TANK family: either a actual taunt or a Last Stand ability
