# 2025 — March 19

## Post 122937 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-03-18T22:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122937#p122937 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2025-03-19.jpg](./download/file.php?id=148) <dd>Changelog_2025-03-19.jpg (532.18 KiB) Viewed 22447 times</dd>

- **Greater Demons** summoned by **Warlocks** will no longer be impacted by raid size scaling.

- **Seal of Righteousness** can now trigger melee ability-based proc effects, such as **Storm Gauntlets** and **Fiery Plate Gauntlets**.

- Summer Collection of Unseen Fashion is now Bind on Pickup.

- **Summer Collection of Unseen Fashion**: **Leggings** will no longer hide your underwear.

- Fixed mana regeneration effect on **Deep Sapphire Circlet** to work correctly while the item is equipped.

- Removed durability values from cloak items that had them for some reason.

- Fixed an issue where city guards would not attack hostile players who engaged in combat without moving in neutral cities.

- Fixed an issue where passive outdoor auras would not correctly reapply after the condition of being outdoors was fulfilled.

- Fixed an issue where attempting to cast **Icicles** or **Arcane Missiles** on a target out of line of sight would silently trigger the global cooldown despite the spell failing.

- Added the Shaman counter-part of **Again into the Great Ossuary** for players who have completed the quest **The Darkreaver Menace**.
