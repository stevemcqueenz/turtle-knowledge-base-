# 2023 — August 27

## Post 59286 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-08-27T10:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59286#p59286 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed several issues with the Baxxil quest.

- Added scripts for the quest Troubled Spirits of Kel'Theril.

- Amplify Curse should now work with Curse of Exhaustion.

- Added a new script for the quest The Calling.

- Players for PvP inside dungeons, assisting group members, can no longer be flagged for PvP.

- Increased the respawn time of Lady Katrana Prestor to 10 minutes. She will continue to respawn faster if she leaves as the part of the Onyxia's Lair attunement scripted event, this is to prevent duplicated spawns.

- Foul Effigy will only drop for Alliance.

- Improved War Master Voone script.
