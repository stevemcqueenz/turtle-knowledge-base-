# 2025 — April 9

## Post 126056 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-04-08T20:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126056#p126056 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-04-09_2.jpg)

- The quest "**The Terrors of the Webweavers**" now properly ends at **Charlie Riley**.

- Fixed an issue where relogging while dead would cause some talents to become non-functional until relogging again while alive.

- Fixed an issue where pets would unexpectedly unsummon when teleporting within the same map.

- “That creature is already being tamed” will be displayed if the beast is currently being tamed by another player.

- **Ironfist** now sheathes properly.

- **Bloody Brass Knuckles** are now Bind on Pickup.

- **Choker of Terminal Arcane** now should (hopefully) be enchantable.

- The quests **Backup System Activation** and **High Energy Regulator** are now sharable.

- **Ardent Custodian**: Replaced 100 Armor with 10 Stamina.

- **Kaldorei Side-Blade**: Removed 4 Stamina and 5 Nature Resistance.

- **Captain's Moonglaive**: Reduced Agility from 6 to 4, Added 4 Stamina, Removed 5 Nature Resistance.

- **Priestess of Elune's Staff**: Increased DPS from 22.3 to 23.7, Reduced Spellpower from 16 to 15.

- **Bow of the Night Huntress**: Adjusted DPS to properly match ranged weapons of equal item level.

- Fixed **Thunderstrike** and **Shadowstrike** buffs not being properly added.

 **Tower of Karazhan**

- **Anomalus' Arcane Prison** can no longer be reflected or redirected.

- Some trash has had their damage, health & spell timers slightly adjusted.

- Added extra clarity via raid wide announcements to a few key spells.

- Slightly modified **Incantagos Affinities** health depending on their school (with small adjustments for PvP realms).

- Fixed a bug where the **Chess Bishop** would not remain stationary.

- Fixed a bug where some trash was not skinnable while they should have been.

- Fixed a bug where some trash did not drop enough gold.

- Fixed a bug where **Keeper Gnarlmoon** sometimes did not stop moving while he's immune.

- Fixed a bug where some beneficial spells were not working while fighting **Keeper Gnarlmoon**.

- Fixed a bug where the raid incorrectly would have reset too early.

- Fixed some spurious bugs with the **Chess fight**.

- Slightly changed stats on **Pawn Chess pieces**.

- Increased hitbox radius for bigger sized bosses.

- Added more clarity for some key spells.

- Fixed a bug where **Echo of Medivh** would start walking more than expected.

- Specific school wand damage should now work against **Affinities**.

- Disabled **Arcane Aura** damage spell pushback on **Anomalus**, small health change.

- Increased cooldown on some of **Echo of Medivh**'s spells.

- **Anomalus** will no longer target **Spirit of Redemptions** with some spells.

- Pets and Totems can now damage ravens if their owner has the correct Aura on **Keeper Gnarlmoon**.

- Fixed an unintended change regarding restorative Teas.

- Fixed some Trash spells being casted more often than intended.

- Fixed some Trash not being able to be Shackled.

- Slightly increased **Medivh's** Health.

- Temporarily drastically reduced expire time on skeletons of dead players.

- Changed a few Chess fight mechanics.
