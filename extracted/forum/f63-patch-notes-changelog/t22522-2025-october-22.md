# 2025 — October 22

## Post 156509 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-10-21T20:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156509#p156509 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-10-22-ENG.jpg)

- **Mana Attuned** is no longer removed from a player after a long‑distance teleport within a battleground.

- The root effect of **Icicles** on its caster can no longer trigger negative effects.

- **Kel'Thuzad**'s **Frostbolt**, **Frostbolt Volley**, **Frost Blast**, **Shadow Fissure** and **Mind Control** now share a 5‑second global cooldown.

- Fixed the missing money reward for the quest **A Dear Friend**.

- Fixed an unintended quest completion for the quest **Storm, Twilight and Hammer**.

- Added missing spawn points for **Bookie Herod** in Stranglethorn Vale.

- Player‑vs‑Player damage reduction no longer applies when one of the participants is under **Mind Control**.

- **Searing Infernals** are now immune to bleed effects and will no longer occasionally hold weapons.

- Open‑world creatures with **Mind Control** abilities will now avoid targeting Hardcore players, preventing griefing.

- Reduced the respawn time of **Saltscale Murlocs** in The Vile Reef.

- Illusions can no longer be used inside battlegrounds.

- Corrected the item icons for **Mana Jade** and **Mana Agate**.

- Mage's **Ignite** no longer triggers from non‑Mage spells or abilities, such as grenades.

- Shaman's **Totemic Mastery** now correctly affects **Stoneskin Totem**.

- Fixed an issue where a tamed **Sewer Beast** would not respond to attack commands.

- Increased the chance to obtain an additional **Black Lotus** on high‑level herb nodes to 6 %.

- Bloodmage Lynnore’s quests in the Blasted Lands now require six fewer **Basilisk Brains** and **Vulture Gizzards**.

- Weekly **War Supplies Cache** now offers increased material and consumable rewards.

- Weekly **Molten Supplies Cache** has been modified and now provides higher rewards.
