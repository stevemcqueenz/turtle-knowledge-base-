# 2023 — August 9

## Post 56424 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-08-08T22:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56424#p56424 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Made more attempts to fix transport issues.

- The ships should now be visible from all continent instances.

- You can no longer cast Lightning Shield on other players if they are flagged for PvP and you are not, to prevent players with the Thunderhead talent from accidentally flagging themselves.

- Reduced the healing bonus from Tree of Life aura to only 20% of Spirit.

- Removed an incorrect immunity spell from the Skeram encounter.

- Pulling Mordresh Fire Eye will now cause nearby skeletons to aggro as well.

- Fixed spectators in BRD arena not becoming friendly after the fight.

- Increased the time it takes for mobs in the Alliance base in AV to respawn.

- Added invisible walls to block backdoors in AV.

- Reduced aggro radius of Anchor's Edge mobs so they don't attack players through the walls.

- Removed double drop of T2 helms/leggings from Onyxia and Ragnaros.

- Fixed Timbermaw Ancestor not healing the player.

- Extended the brief post-duel damage immunity to both players.

- Conjured Mana Orange can now be used to feed pets.

- Reduced the respawn time of Gurubashi tablets in Stranglethorn.

- Made pet autocasting more responsive.

- Mor'Ladim will now be affected by the dynamic respawn time reduction.

- Spells that chain to multiple targets should no longer target flagged players if the attacker is not flagged.

- Faerie Fire will now trigger Omen of Clarity.

- Fixed a lot of issues with NPC pathfinding and their evading in custom zones. This is an ongoing process.
