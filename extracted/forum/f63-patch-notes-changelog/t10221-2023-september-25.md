# 2023 — September 25

## Post 66741 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-09-24T23:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66741#p66741 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed blast radius of the cave in in the Gnomeregan Emi Blastfuse event.

- Fixed intermittent issue with turning in Mother's MIlk to Ragged John.

- Implemented the UBRS Stadium Event upon use of the Seal of Ascension to summon Vaelastrasz.

- Decreased respawn time of mobs in Blasted Lands required for Bloodmage quests.

- Decreased respawn time of furbolgs in Winterspring.

- Slightly increased droprate of Winterfall Firewater.

- Isalien will now unsummon his guardians upon evade, instead of collecting more adds with each wipe.

- Added a Horde version of the Stormwind Vault quests.

- Elemental Sharpening stones will now work when applied to off-hand weapons as well.

- Fixed bonus honor and reputation during the Arathi Basin weekend.

- Added Trueshot to Hunter trainers that were missing it.

- Fixed extra attacks being lost when you have only 1 stack of Reckoning.

- Reduced minimum player count required for Sunnyglade to start.

- Reduced the respawn time of Eris Havenfire to just 15 minutes.

- Smokey LaRue will now offer to give the player more of his special compound during the quest "When Smokey Sings, I Get Violent" as the quest text implies.

- All priest champion spells are now affected by Inner Focus.

- Increased detection range of Ratchet Bruisers and added an extra spawn near the town center.

- Grace of Air totem will now stack with other agility increasing effects.

- Fixed the Egg-O-Matic and Curgle Cranklehop's gossip options related to it.

- The taunt effect from Improved Seal of Justice should no longer have a separate chance to miss.
