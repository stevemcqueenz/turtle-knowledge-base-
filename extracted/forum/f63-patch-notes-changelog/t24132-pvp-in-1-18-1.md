# PvP in 1.18.1

*(This thread will be continously updated.)*

Hello everyone and welcome to the PvP Update Post for **[Patch 1.18.1: Nightmares of Ursol](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=22214)**

This post will go over some of the bigger changes coming to PvP in 1.18.1.
All of these changes are set to go live shortly after the release.

 ****PVP GEAR PROGRESSION****

- **PvP Rewards**

The old **2-tiered PvP Armor Set Rewards** will be sunset and will no longer be obtainable.
 **New Armor Set Rewards** will consist of 3 tiers for different armor types and different roles:

 - **Tier 1 Rare - Item Level ~71**

 - **Tier 2 Epic - Item Level ~77**

 - **Tier 3 Epic - Item Level ~92**
The old **PvP Weapons** will remain the same with the addition of an extra Tier under the current ones.
Various new Off-set pieces and miscellaneous items will be obtainable through various PvP events. (See below)

- **Honor**

 - The ways of gaining **Honor** will remain the same, killing players, doing PvP objectives etc.

 - **Honor** gained through these ways will now be credited to the Player immediately.
This also implies that you will no longer be "fighting" for your spot to gain Honor/RP based on weekly standings.

 - **Honor** will now be a currency to buy various new PvP gear and miscellaneous items with.

 - **Honor** gains throughout all activities that grant Honor will be reduced to 10% of previous values.
Costs of **Honor** for the aforementioned items and gear will reflect the new gains as well.

 *As some Players were gaining hundreds of thousands of Honor a week the above change is simply to keep numbers in line and purely a visual change.*

 - **Honor** gains will be capped to **20 000 Honor per week**. Resetting every weekly maintenance.

- **PvP Ranks**

 - **PvP Ranks** remain in the game. Every time you would gain **Honor** points you will also gain a portion of that amount as **Rank Points**.

 - **Rank Points** are immediately credited as well. Having enough **Rank Points** immediately promotes you to a higher Rank.

 - **Rank Points** decay every week, as before. You can't drop **Ranks** from this decay as before as well.

 - **Rank Point** gains are not capped.

 - Most PvP Rewards that can be bought for **Honor** will also have a **Rank** requirement. Buying these items will not consume or drop your **Rank**.

- **Conquest Points**

Some of the strongest PvP Rewards will, on top of requiring a specific PvP Rank and an Honor cost, also have a Conquest Points cost.

Conquest Points are gained through various ways:
 - Achieving a high rating in Ranked Arena and being active for that week (Distributed on weekly flush)

 - Various PvP World PvP Events (Distributed immediately)

 - Call to Arms Battlegrounds (Distributed immediately)
On the weekly maintenance the system will distribute Conquest Points for Arena Teams and decay inactive ones.

- **Old System Consequences**

To streamline the process of going from the old system to the new system the following actions will be taken:

 - Players keep their current **PvP Rank**, it will NOT be reset.

 - Based on their **PvP Rank** before the new system, Players will gain a one-time Honor Points bonus upon the launch of the new system.

 - The old PvP gear sets will no longer be obtainable.

 - Players who own any of the gear sets will be able to keep playing with them. (As the newer gear is consistently stronger we don't expect this to last long)

 ****PVP DAMPENING****

As already discussed on our main post, the **PvP Dampening Debuff** introduced in 1.18.0 will now have dynamic reductions of Magical and Physical damage based on your class / spec instead of a flat number for all classes.

 ****RATED ARENA RESTRICTIONS****

Note that with the start of the new system we will also restrict some of the strongest cooldowns that players have access to in Arenas. Please stay tuned for an inclusive list of everything restricted in the near future.

 ****BATTLEGROUND UPDATES****

As previously already shown in our main post [here](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=22214) **Thorn Gorge** is coming to Turtle WoW. Defeat foes and defend bases to lead your team to victory!

Also note that **Sunnyglade Valley** will be disabled upon the rollout of this system while we update the Battleground to better reflect our current standards.

 ****PVP WORLD EVENTS****

Various new PvP World Events will take place throughout Azeroth:

- Assault your enemy's faction base and Defend your own in **Southshore vs Tarren Mill**.

- Capture & hold the Flags versus your adversaries in **Redwall Keep**.
Make sure that you join in on the action as these events will grant **exclusive rewards** such as Off-set pieces and a good portion of **Honor** and **Conquest Points**!

## Post 169725 by madferret — 2026-03-02T02:22:00Z

Overall these sound like great changes at first sight. I guess without seeing the details it's a bit hard to assess completely, but they seem to be headed in the right direction -yay WPvP!- , pending finetuning.

However two things worry me:

 1. **tl;dr make max pvp gear itemlevel 88 (naxx equivalent) instead of 92 (k40 equivalent).**
Max tier pvp gear being on par with k40 gear seems excessive. The highest PvE gear available should remain a notch above PvP gear, that was always the case in vanilla, and for good reason. PvP requires nolifing to achieve the top, nobody will argue that, but it is something that can be achieved solo. "Beating the game" in PvE requires the collective effort of 40 dedicated players, and then you, the individual, being rewarded for your contribution. It just makes sense that such accomplishment (that cannot be achieved solo) stays a tad above PvP rewards. And yes, I am aware that pve items' stat distribution isn't always useful for pvp and vice-versa, but my point still stands.

 2. Class/spec specific dampening -WHAT??? **tl;dr this has potential to be an absolute nightmare to balance and to introduce immense frustration, griefing, and a loss of interest for pvp.**

Flat dampening was a shock to the community but after months playing in it I think the consensus is positive. It has made pvp more interesting overall: fights last longer and require better decision making, allow for skill expression, etc. The fact that the dampening is the same for everybody is a key part of that: you can't be that upset if you didn't win a fight where you and your opponent are burdened with the same limitations. But if the dampening is uneven... How will players feel? Satisfactory gameplay is key to keep people motivated to pvp. Giving certain specs a crutch and others handcuffs will just trigger "f this bs" moments left and right and push people away from pvp.
Certain specs will always be stronger than others. Such is the nature of WoW in ANY expansion. And that's okay! As long as they are playable and have a unique class identity.

Not to mention, to find the goldilocks zone for dynamic dampening to work fairly, the amount of PvP insight and expertise the devs will need is enormous -and even then it will probably still feel unfair and frustrating to many players, as there is no universal consensus and many people have different opinions on what is "broken" and what is "useless".
No offense to the devs, you're trying your best and it is VERY appreciated, but we get 1 set of pvp changes every 6 months at best... Who here can say they trust them to know what to nerf and what to buff, dynamically, as issues arise?

It seems to me like a high risk small reward decision.
