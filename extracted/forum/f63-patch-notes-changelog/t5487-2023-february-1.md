# 2023 — February 1

## Post 33639 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-01-31T21:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33639#p33639 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- It's no longer allowed to leave raids while in your character is in combat. This is a measurement implemented to prevent raid groups from exploiting the first boss multiple times.

- Fixed Muglash not being attackable.

- Made Garments of the Oracle set bonus heal on periodic heals.

- Yet another attempt to prevent channeled auras from skipping ticks.

- Removed buy and sell price from Magic Mushroom.

- Grug'thok the Seer can no longer be tamed.

- Added new loot items for the new Commander Andreon boss in Karazhan Crypt:

 - Deathforge Belt

 - Ring of Authority

 - Boots of the Holy Mentor

 - Frostchain Gauntlets

 - Band of Arcane Dampening

 - Gravewalker Pauldrons
