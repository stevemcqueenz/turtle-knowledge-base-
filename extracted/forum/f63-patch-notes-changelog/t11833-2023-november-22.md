# 2023 — November 22

## Post 78235 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-11-22T00:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78235#p78235 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed an issue where lockboxes opened via the trade window by Rogues other than the player would re-lock when logging out.

- Addressed a problem in the Black Morass instance where players were being kicked outside the northwest portal in Caverns of Time instead of the southwest (Black Morass) portal during logout or /camp events.

- Corrected the stats on Sorcerer's Leggings and Beastmaster's Gloves.

- Increased the stack size of Corrupted Sand from 200 to 250.

- Reduced the minimum level requirement for the quest to purchase the Goblin Brainwashing Device with in-game gold from 50 to 30.

- Added missing waypoints for various creatures.

- Included missing gossip for Archmage Tarsis Kir-Moldir.

- Fixed Zandalarian Hero Charm not always consuming charges.

- Eye for an Eye will now correctly trigger from fully absorbed crits.

- Addressed exploits related to flying mounts.

- Line of sight checks for GameObjects now consider the actual height of the model.

- Fixed the inability to cancel Eye of Kilrogg.

- Corrected the behavior of the Grace of Earth trinket to reduce enemy threat as intended.

- Added one additional graveyard to the Hyjal zone.

- For Tel'Abim and CN Network realms: added the missing Arcanite Rod to the vendor in Hyjal.

- The quest "Rival Presence" is now properly marked as Elite.

- Fixed line of sight issues with profession and spell trainers, preventing money from being taken without the corresponding spell being taught.

- Corrected typos in several quests with the help of the community.

- Adjusted the behavior of Patch of Elemental Water pools in Azshara.

- Made a lot of performance improvements.
