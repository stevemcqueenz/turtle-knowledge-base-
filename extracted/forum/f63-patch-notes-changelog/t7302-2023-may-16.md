# 2023 — May 16

## Post 46277 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-05-17T15:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46277#p46277 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Grammar fixes for quest An Unholy Alliance.

- Parity fixes for Gadgetzan Times quests.

- Unchained incorrectly chained Warlock Doomguard quests.

- Properly categorized quest Rooting Out Evil as Crescent Grove.

- Increased stack size of Golem Core for quest Discovering Golem Secrets from 1 to 3.

- Corrected quest category / zone for Rooting Out Evil.

- Lowered the respawn time of more gameobjects to 5 seconds for parties.

- Fixed Battleground scoreboard updating after the battleground has ended (players will no longer be removed from the board as they leave).

- The level based miss chance cap and spell resist cap now only applies to players.

- Reduced Swarmguard Needler damage to match Classic values.

- Bloody Pick and Crossguarded Blade should now be able to drop.

- Burning Blade Adept should no longer cast through walls.

- Reduced Deathchill Servant damage to match Classic.

- Warlock's minion stats have been adjusted to match Classic values.
 * Some of the fixes are not in game yet and scheduled for the next restart.
