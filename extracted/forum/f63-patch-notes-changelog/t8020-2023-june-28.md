# 2023 — June 28

## Post 51551 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-06-27T22:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51551#p51551 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Stormwind Vault quests should now properly be available to both factions.

- Made Lurking Shadow evade after 100 yards.

- Wasteland, The Spirits of Southwind, Hive in the Tower quests should now properly be available to both factions.

- The quest A Husband's Revenge is now available to the Horde.

- The Tower of Lapidis X is now available to the Alliance.

- Added Captured Flame to Horde's Fire Festival quest.

- Fixed mistakenly deleted Felix's Chest.

- The quest The Fire Festival is now available to the Alliance.

- Added an item level to the Bloodstone Warblade Blacksmithing Plans.

- Reimplemented Orcish War Leggings Blacksmithing Plans, replacing the spell cast on the player from the quest The Old Ways for parity with the Alliance version of the Blacksmithing quest.

- Assigned disenchantment loo to the Mithril Scale Gloves.

- Increased the drop chance of Wavecrest Scales.

- Korrak's health has been reduced once again, to make it easier for players to kill him when the rest of team doesn't want to help. It's now a quarter of what it was originally.

- Wormscale Stompers, Runed Mithril Hammer, Arena Belt and Sentinel's Pauldrons disenchants have been corrected.

- A Light in Dark Places should now see that you used the correct bonfire.

- A Thief's Reward should now be obtainable.

- Crate of Elunite should now all spawn above the ground.

- Panther Hide Sack drop rate has been adjusted to match classic from 12% to 18%.

- Witherbark Bloodling should now have the correct Health values and will no longer drop items.

- Quests The Graveyards of Alterac and Towers and Bunkers should now correctly reward reputation on completion.

- Raw Nightfin Snapper drop rate from fishing as been adjusted to match classic values.

- Festival Dumpling should now restore the proper health and mana during its duration.

- Transfer Documents for the quest Protecting Fresh Blood can no longer be interacted with unless you have the quest.

- Re-scripted quest Of Forgotten Memories.

- Hakkari Shroud can no longer drop from creatures.

- Karfang Grunt is now PvP flagged.

- Intimidation and Bestial Wrath buffs will no longer be saved when you dismiss your pet.

- Sun's Embrace and Grace of the Sunwell books can no longer be used on other players.

- Dragons of Nightmare trash mobs can no longer be kited indefinitely.

- Transmogged items will now remain soulbound after logout, even if they are of white or grey quality.

- Extended the duration of the Midsummer Fire Festival by 1 week.

- Equalized respawn times of Winterax trolls in Alterac Valley. They now range from 15 to 30 minutes. Previously some spawns respawned quickly, while others never respawned.

- Marlos Catos will now yell less often than before.

- Corrected the spawn positions of some Cenarion Hold Infantry.

- Lurking Shadow can no longer be kited indefinitely.

- Boulderclaw Geomancer will now cast spells.

- Corrected position of various gameobject spawns in WSG.

- Schools of Fish in Darkshore should now be the correct distance from the shore.

- Schematic: Portable Wormhole Generator: Orgrimmar and Schematic: Portable Wormhole Generator: Stormwind require 125 to learn up from 120. Made this change due to it requiring crafted items that require level 125 to learn to make.

- Jordan's Ore Shipment and Jordan's Refined Ore Shipment is now indestructible.

- Dream's Herald now correctly only lasts 12 seconds.

- Corrected the turn in npc for the quest The Hunter's Path (80330).

- Baelfyr's damage values match more closely to what is expected of level 41 elites.

- Evil Twin effect will no longer interrupt with summons.

- Added an Innkeeper to Caelan's Rest' tavern.

- Lowered the reset timer for the crypt door in the goblin's starting zone.

- Blessed Oils and Consecrated Sharpening Stones are now tradeable.

- Removed the one hour long cooldowns and the 5 item limit from the Blasted Lands consumables.

- Removed the 5 item limit from the Zandalari Tribe Zanza consumables.
