# 2026 — January 14

## Post 165064 by Shadowsong (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-01-13T21:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165064#p165064 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2026-01-14-ENG.jpg)

- Fixed an issue with the quest items for **Pricolich Lycan**.

- **Loop of Unceasing Frost**'s proc will no longer hit the Shaman.

- Fixed **Whitemane** becoming unkillable after **Mograine** dies.

- Reduced respawn time of **Warbringer Dar'nakk** to 5 minutes.

- Fixed **Abiding Crypt Keeper** casting while moving.

- Disabled gossip for used objects for quest **A Deal Worth Taking**.

- **Triage** quest will no longer give group completion credit.

- Fixed gnolls falling below the ground in a cave in **Northwind**.

- **Deadwind ogres** will now share spawn points with each other.

- Fixed incorrect **Fishing loottables** for the areas **Ursolan** and **Timbermaw Hold** in Azshara.

- Players can now request another **Amulet of Draconic Subversion** from Myranda the Hag should they lose it during the **Onyxia Attunement** questline.

- **Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread** now has a 5 min cooldown.
