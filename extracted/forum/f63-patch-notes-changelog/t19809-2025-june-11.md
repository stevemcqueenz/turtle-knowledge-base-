# 2025 — June 11

## Post 135177 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-06-10T20:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135177#p135177 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-06-11.jpg)

- It is no longer possible to create a guild name that differs only in letter capitalization from an existing one.

- Fixed **Anvilrage Overseer** pathing.

- **Rip** now refunds Energy when it misses.

- Added an **Alchemy Lab** to **The Repository** in **Tower of Karazhan**.

- Added an additional way to loot **Core of Arc'Tiras** for the quest **The Enemy Lays**.

- **Murky**, **Terky**, **Gurky**, and **Pebblemoon** will now groove with you when you dance.

- Granted **Enraged Cave Rumbler** immunity to Bleed effects.

- Fixed an issue where the effect of **Magispark Leggings** stopped working after death.

- Fixed **Maraudine Wranglers** casting **Net** too frequently.

- **Net** can no longer be cast on targets already affected by it.

- Removed **Disarm** immunity from **Engineer Figgles**, **High Foreman Bargul Blackhammer**, and **Har'gesh Doomcaller** in **Hateforge Quarry**.

- Fixed **Decoded True Believer Clippings** not always having loot.

- **Orb of Deception** now cancels your active roleplay disguise.

- Removed unsupported symbol from the item name **The King's Conviction**.

- Added disenchant loot to the **Priest T1 Shadow Pants of Prophecy** variant.

- Fixed typos in **Keeper Marandis**'s dialogue and the quest description for **Manifestations of Nightmare**.

- Fixed an issue where the final tick of periodic spells would not occur if the caster despawned (e.g., **Magma Totem**).

- Fixed **Cloak of the Bloody Ravager** not properly adjusting bleed durations.

- **Haywire Battlechickens** are now immune to **Rend** and other Bleed effects.

- Relocated several underground harpies in **Mulgore** back onto the surface.

- Marked quest **Gilnean Pricolich** as a Dungeon Quest.

- Fixed **Lawbringer Bracers** not granting MP5 on equip.

- Changed model of **Steel Plate Boots** to match the set.

- Changed models of **Grunt's Pauldrons** and **Grunt's Anklewraps** to match the set.

- Fixed **'Water Rat' Jorgy** not giving XP.

- Granted **Widow of the Woods** and **Shambling Dead** immunity to Fear.

- Reduced **Giant Softshell Clam** respawn timer to 15–25 minutes.

- Restored correct models for **Dragonkin Illusions**, including a missing blue variant.

- **Antnormi** in **Black Morass** is now correctly marked as **Dragonkin**.

- Assigned missing skinning loot to **Packmaster Ragetooth**, **Regent-Lady Celia Harlow**, and **Regent-Lord Mortimer Harlow**.

- **Dawnbright Cuffs** will correctly disenchant into **Soul Dust** or **Greater Astral Essence**.

- **Emberstone Idol** will correctly disenchant into **Dream Dust** or **Greater Nether Essence**.

- Allowed mounting while under **Worgen** or **Gilnean Worgen Illusion** effects.

- **Merchant Kryillos** at **Kalkor Point** on **Gillijim's Isle** is now selling correct ammunition.

- Restored the original entrance level of **Ragefire Chasm** to level 8 for non-Hardcore players.

- **Twilight Marauder Morna** and her party now aggro when players approach.

- Removed duplicate spawn of **Arcanist Sovatir's Torn Notes**.

- Renamed gardening **Watermelons** to differentiate them in Auction House searches.

- Fixed grammar in the quest text of **Golden Elves of Feralas**.

- Updated **Two-Head Ogre Illusion** to use models with animation variety.

- Fixed **Behind the Wall** quest being available to **Horde** when it should be **Alliance**-only.

- Renamed **Camouflaged Tunic** to **Camouflaged Vest** to avoid item name duplication.

- Assigned missing models to the **Blood Elf Illusion**.

- **Zinfizzlex's Portable Shredder Unit** no longer counts offline time in its duration.

- Added a missing option to restore **Mage Talismans** from **Al'tabim**.

- Increased drop rate of **Wartorn Plate Scrap** in **Naxxramas**.

- Fixed unshareable quests and quest level display in **Gilneas**.

- Fixed an issue with **Special Water** not working on **Blast Powder Kegs** in **Booty Bay**.

- Removed Bleed immunity from **Garr**, **Golemagg**, **Sapphiron**, and **Kel'thuzad**.

- Fixed **Captured Arko'narin** not starting escort quest or respawning in her cage.

- Increased respawn acceleration of some open-world profession nodes based on player count.

 **Scythe of Elune**

- **Elune Infusion**: Reduced damage from 650–500 to 500–375, and reduced spellpower scaling by 25%.

- **Elune's Twilight**: Reduced Mana Burn from 200 to 100, damage from 150 to 120, and spellpower scaling by 20%.

- **Elune's Radiance**: Reduced healing from 150 to 130.

- **Elune's Ire**: Reduced damage from 350 to 275 and spellpower scaling by 20%.

- **Elune's Grace**: Reduced damage from 250 to 200, damage reduction from 10% to 8%, and spellpower scaling by 20%.

- **Elune's Rage**: Reduced damage from 400 to 325 and spellpower scaling by 20%.
 *These values will be displayed correctly after a client update.*
