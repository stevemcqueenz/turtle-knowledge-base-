# 2023 — May 10

## Post 45215 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-05-09T21:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45215#p45215 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Pets will no longer chase their target if it becomes invisible to them.

- Several spell interrupt flags have been fixed.

- Fire Shield will now be removed upon entering instances.

- Fixed a case where successful interrupt by Shield Bash or Counterspell did not prevent some spells, such as Entangling Roots.

- Assigned a dungeon type to the quest The Stockades Search.

- Grammar fixes in the quest Tower of Lapidis all parts.

- Grammar fixes in the quest Arugal's Folly.

- Grammar fixes in the quest The Old Church of Westfall X.

- Grammar fixes in the quest Tesala's Word.

- Grammar fixes in the quest A Friend of the Tribe.

- Grammar fixes in the quest The Depths of Karazhan VII.

- Grammar fixes in the quest Da Unforgiving Lands.

- Grammar fixes in the quest Goblin Engineering At Its Finest!

- Grammar fixes in the quest Willing to Serve.

- Grammar fixes in the quest Blackrock and Roll.

- Grammar fixes in the quest Blackrock Slayers.

- Grammar fixes in the quest A Broken Oath.

- Grammar fixes in the quest Azsharan Front.

- Rumbling Elementals no longer drop coins.

- Fixed incomplete Progress text in the quest Special Forces.

- Fixed misleading description of the spell Moonkin Form.

- Fixed a typo in the item Tyrant's Crown.

- Removed description from the trainer's spell Savage Bite.

- Fixed a typo in the farming mushroom's growing stage.

- Fixed Tier 3 quests' Progress and Completion texts.

- Removed Glowing Cat Figurine's vendor price.

- Gave Dim Torch a 1 copper sell price for easier disposal while leveling Survival.

- Minimum player level to use chat has been reverted to 5.

- Increased incredibly low leash range for mobs in custom zones such as Lapidis Isle and Tel'Abim.

- Tomes for higher levels of Khadgar's Unlocking now have unlimited stock on the vendor.

- Living Bomb no longer has a chance to crit.

- The level based spell miss chance is now capped to 10 levels difference, making it possible to hit high level players in PvP.

- Kor'ghan's evil twin has been removed from Sen'jin Village, and two quests attached to him (810 & 811) have been removed.

- Car Key Trinkets from Mirage Raceway are now Bind on Pickup.

- Added a title for having completed 2800 quests: Seeker of Knowledge.

- Added equipment repair services to Argon Halmantle at Steepcliff Port in Tirisfal Uplands.

- All E'ko items have had their party loot removed and their drop chance increased to 60%.

- Survival tents have had a change in appearance to prevent griefing.
We'd like to thank the contributions of players who have helped improve mistakes in quest text.
