# 2024 — July 17

## Post 99696 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-07-16T22:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99696#p99696 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_july17.jpg)

- Fixed Labor Union Defender not being considered a shield.

- Fixed an exploit where the towers in the Eastern Plaguelands gave more Honor than intended.

- Fixed inconsistencies with the White Riding Talbuk and Dark Riding Talbuk models, item, and spell names.

- Removed armor and durability values from ~80 cosmetic items, previously preventing them from being repaired.

- Renamed Armored Golden Steed to Armored Swift Palomino to correctly reflect the in-game model.

- Fixed Scythe of the Unseen Path being invisible while sheathed.

- Added disenchantment loot for the Hollowbone Choker.

- Added low-level fishing loot to the Amani'Alor.

- Fixed Windtalker Cape, Fisherman's Shawl, Glaymore Shawl, Quagmire Shawl, Drape of Flickering Flame, Horse Rustler Drape, Ash Shawl, Skyfall Mantle, and Operative Cloak not properly displaying as cloth, preventing them from being eligible for enchanting.

- Fixed armor penetration effect on the Golden Scale Cuirass.

- Dream's Herald can now be disenchanted and sold on the auction house.

- Fixed text inconsistencies in the quests: Disturbance in the Air, Bloodfury Bloodline, Donning the Red Flag, Draconic Presence, Satisfaction for Shak, The Price Of Information, and Scarlet Aid.

- City Protector's Teleport now has casting time and cooldown.

- Fixed the glitch that allowed multiple City Protector's Medallions to be received at the same time.

- Deepmoss Spiders will no longer drop clam meat.

- Removed some dungeon loot from NPCs that weren't supposed to drop it.

- Fashion Coins have been added to the loot tables of the bosses in Lower Karazhan Halls.

- Fixed Mind Control threat reset on Razuvious adds.

- Characters from banned accounts will be treated as if they gained no honor for every week that they remain banned.
