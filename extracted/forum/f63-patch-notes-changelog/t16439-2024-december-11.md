# 2024 — December 11

## Post 112126 by Reynolds (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-12-10T22:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112126#p112126 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2024-12-04.jpg](./download/file.php?id=91) <dd>Changelog_2024-12-04.jpg (427.74 KiB) Viewed 15947 times</dd>

- Fixed the issue preventing spell damage buffs, trinkets, and consumes from stacking.

- **Blade of Eternal Darkness**'s added proc cooldown has now been removed.

- Fixed an issue where auras providing invulnerability, such as **Limited Invulnerability Potion**, would not cause the **Warsong Gulch** flag to drop.

- Fixed Tier 2 gear not dropping from **Nefarian**.

- **Fashion Coins** will now stack up to 200.

- Removed **Nert Blastentom** due to new narrative changes.

- Using **LFT** inside a dungeon no longer drops the current Dungeon-ID.

- Creatures that are immune to other creatures will no longer attempt to aggro them, avoiding a permanent aggro-evade cycle.

- Creatures with pets will no longer dismiss and resummon their pet if the pet is enslaved.

- Stabled pets will no longer get summoned after being revived in battlegrounds.

- Swapping quest rewards through **Feid Rota** will now destroy only 1 copy of the item instead of all.

- Changed the category of the quest **Turning Off The Tap** to **The Deadmines**.

- **Amani Eagles** now have **Screech Rank 1** when tamed.

- Fixed an exploit with **Shallow Graves** in **Zul'Farrak** that allowed summoning infinite zombies.

- Removed level requirement from **Tome of Tactical Escape I**.

- **Codex of Searing Shot V**, **Tome of Polymorph: Cow**, and **Formula: Eternal Dreamstone Shard** can no longer be used to teach other players.

- Older **Tauren Shaman** characters should no longer be missing **Totemic Slam**.

- **Flametongue Totem** damage will now scale correctly for Druids in feral form.

- Fixed an issue where the Shaman talent **Flame Guidance** did not consistently provide the expected range increase for **Searing Totem**.

- The Shaman talent **Call of Flame** now affects the periodic damage of **Flame Shock**.

- Fixed an issue where the Druid talent **Guidance of the Dream** would not stack with the Druid Balance T1 5-piece set bonus.

- The Shaman Restoration T2.5 3-piece set bonus now provides the correct mana bonus for **Reincarnation**.

- Fixed an issue where the Priest talent **Spiritual Healing** unintentionally extended the duration of periodic healing effects.

- Magical damage will no longer consume stacks of the **Hemorrhage** debuff.

- The debuff priority of Paladin **Judgments** have been raised.

- Damage from the **Repent** debuff will now be dealt by the Paladin who cast **Repentance**.

- All ranks of **Repentance** now share a cooldown.

- Fixed issues with the Warlock talent **Malediction**.

- The Retribution and Protection **Judgment** set 2-piece bonus can now trigger other auras, such as **Judgment of Wisdom**.

- Fixed the Shaman Enhancement T1 5-piece set bonus, which now applies the correct bonus to **Flametongue Weapon**.

- The Druid T2.5 "**Genesis Harness**" 5-piece bonus now correctly increases periodic damage of **Rake**.

- Improved **Ghostly Strike** and **Evasion** can now stack.

- **Winter's Chill** effect no longer has a separate chance to miss.

- Loop of Unceasing Frost's fire damage increase can now stack with **Improved Scorch**.

- **Enlighten** will no longer trigger **Corrupted Mind**.
