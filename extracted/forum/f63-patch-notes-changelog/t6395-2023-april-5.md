# 2023 — April 5

## Post 40307 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-04-04T22:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40307#p40307 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Prevented Arch Druid Renferal and related mobs from staying at the graveyard.

- Reduced mana cost percent of Travel Form and Aquatic Form.

- Removed wrong aura state requirements from Swiftmend.

- Removed GCD from Savage Bite.

- Made class wide buffs affect pets.

- Revantusk Troll quests update.  Horde can now attain Friendly with Revantusk in a single day and access the level 10 greens for parity with Allianceand Silvermoon Remnants rep. Look for Warleader Zol'majin in Amani'Alor for a new quest, and seek out the new Speaker of the Revantusk Tribe in Grommash Hold. NOTE: A new quest has been added to grant access to the Amani'Alor teleport. If you previously had access to the teleport, you will need to do this new quest to regain access. Keep this in mind before making a ticket about not having access to Amani'Alor.

- Fixed an issue where Master loot would sometimes not allow players to trade in the 10 minute window.

- Added a hard-cap for player model's scale.

- Fixed master-loot target not getting new non-soulbound item update client-side.

- Fixed power' reset at start of Blood Ring.

- Fixed Wolfshead Helm giving rage twice.

- Lowered Benedict's Chest and other reported quest GOs' respawn timer.
