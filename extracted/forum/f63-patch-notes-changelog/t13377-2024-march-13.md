# 2024 — March 13

## Post 90014 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-03-12T22:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90014#p90014 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_mar13.png)

- Hyjal Nectar is no longer available for purchase from drink vendors who mistakenly had it in their offerings.

- Attempted to address issues with temporary flying mounts that allowed players to continue flying after their expiration.

- Resolved the issue of the mount aura icon being invisible when mounting up after reaching the buff limit.

- Removed the 40-yard forced leash from Gillijim's Isle mobs.

- Fixed the guild tabard not appearing on the character selection screen.

- Disabled the alliance version of Barov Peasant Caller as well.

- Jaedanar Adept will now cast Blink when stunned.

- The Emerald Rod will no longer function as a secondary weapon.

- Fixed the summon position of regular Wind Stone mobs to prevent them from getting stuck inside the crystal.
