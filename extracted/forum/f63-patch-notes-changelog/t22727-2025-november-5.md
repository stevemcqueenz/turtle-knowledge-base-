# 2025 — November 5

## Post 158364 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-11-04T20:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158364#p158364 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-11-05-ENG.jpg)

- Players can no longer learn talents while dead. This resolves intermittent issues where such talents did not work as expected until the player relogged.

- Effects from items such as **Runeblade** of **Baron Rivendare** now properly reapply after resurrection.

- **Totemic Slam** no longer triggers unintended effects, such as **Totem of the Corrupted Current**.

- Fixed an issue where summoning the **Eye of Kilrogg** could cause the warlock’s pet to become lost.

- Abilities and spells that scale with Attack Power now correctly include bonuses against specific creature types.

- Fixed pathing issues near **Hearthglen**.

- Fixed pathing issues in the outer areas of **Blackrock Depths**.

- **The Blazing Pan** has been slightly buffed to deal better damage than the easier‑to‑obtain **Half‑Eaten Mutton Chop**.

- **Serena Bloodfeather** will now always drop loot.

- **Crossfaction Battlegrounds** will start to be active on our PvE realms. This means that you might get sorted into the opposing faction to fight for instead.

We will first enable this on Warsong Gulch and enable the other Battlegrounds throughout the week.

 **Please use the launcher to apply the small client patch required for cross‑faction battlegrounds.**
