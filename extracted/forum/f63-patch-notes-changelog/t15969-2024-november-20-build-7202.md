# 2024 — November 20 [Build 7202]

## Post 108746 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-11-19T21:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108746#p108746 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog-2024-11-20-7.jpg](./download/file.php?id=80) <dd>Changelog-2024-11-20-7.jpg (507.59 KiB) Viewed 27842 times</dd>

This week’s maintenance will bring a small client update with interface fixes and the first round of mid-patch class balance tweaks.

 **For the class changes, read here:** [Updates | November 20 2024](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108776#p108776)

 **General**
- Cavernweb Broodmother is now larger.

- Captain Salt Tooth's Head will now always drop.

- Changed **Orb of Deception** and **Carved Ogre Idol** to normal items, allowing them to be added to the toy collection without trinket cooldowns.

- Delarion Featherwing can now teach players Herbalism.

- Scourge Soldiers in the corridor between Eastern Plaguelands and Thalassian Highlands no longer grant experience.

---
 **Spells**
- **Earth Shield** is no longer usable in Ghost Wolf form.

- **Calming Winds** now correctly decreases threat.

- Fixed **Holy Power** not increasing **Flash of Light** crit chance.

- **Shadow Weaving** and **Improved Shadow Bolt** effects can now stack.

- Fixed an issue where **Efflorescence** would trigger from Healing over Time effects of yourself.

- Hunter pets no longer inherit unintended immunities.

- **Aggressive Negotiations** now correctly increases skill with two-handed maces.

- Fixed issues with poisons and **Icicles** not stacking between players of the same class.

- Fixed an issue where the **Dark Harvest** periodic haste effect sometimes caused the last tick of periodic debuffs on the target to be lost.

- Fixed an issue with the talent **Improved Rend**.

- **Crusader Strike** should no longer benefit from Vengeance twice

---
 **Quests & NPCs**
- Fixed PvP flagging when attacking Bartleby during "Beat Bartleby" (Warrior quest).

- Corrected quest rewards for **Trousers of the Oracle**.

- Changed the **Pyrewood Ambush** quest objective to fix completion issues.

- Fixed missing and incorrect quest texts for **Prince Thunderaan** and **The Binding** (Warlock quest).

- **Thalassian Fox** is now classified as a Fox instead of a Wolf.

- Fixed an issue where **Mastery of Guns** did not grant the intended skill bonus.

---
 **Items & Crafting**
- Fixed **Aquamarine Pendant** and **Rough Truesilver Ring** tool requirements.

- **Heavy Gritted Paper**: Updated material requirements to Mageweave Cloth.

- **Solid Gritted Paper**: Materials reduced to 3 Silk Cloth and 3 Solid Stone.

- **Dense Gritted Paper**: Materials reduced to 3 Runecloth and 3 Dense Stone.

- **Solid Gemstone Cluster**: Updated materials to 3 Mithril Ore and 3 Enchanted Gemstone Fragments.

- **Dense Gemstone Cluster**: Updated materials to 3 Thorium Ore and 3 Pristine Gemstone Fragments.

- **Tea with Sugar** now gets consumed properly.

- Crafting **Rough Truesilver Ring** now requires 2 Truesilver Bars.

- **Totem of Crackling Thunder: Reduced attack and casting speed increase to 8%, increased Lightning Bolt proc chance to 10%, and added 15% proc chance from **Lightning Strike.****

- ****Having the **Crusader** enchant on both weapons may result in two Crusader effect stacks.****

---
 **Dungeons**
- Fixed mobs falling through floors in Hearthglen towers.

- Resolved crashes in Scarlet Monastery Library.

- Improved mob pathing in Shadowfang Keep and Wailing Caverns.

- Players teleported by Heigan should no longer fall through the floor.

- Gothik should no longer reset unexpectedly.

- Players are no longer kicked from dungeons when finding replacements through LFT.

---
 **Visuals & Sound**
- Added new visuals for **Warlock Power Overwhelming**, **Shaman Spirit Link**, **Priest Enlighten**, and **Priest Chastise** spells.

- Updated visuals for **Druid Eclipse**, **The Face of Death Shield**, and **Misplaced Servo Arm**.

- Added an **Eclipse** sound effect when triggered.

- Fixed missing Dwarf Female Mining Sound.

---
 **UI & Miscellaneous**
- Disabled notifications when players join or leave chat channels.

- Added localization for hardcore player death announcements.

---
 **Tooltips & Descriptions**
- Corrected damage descriptions for all ranks of **Crusader Strike**.

- Updated **Surprise Strike** to clarify it cannot be blocked, dodged, or parried.

- Fixed tooltips for **Elemental Focus**, **Tidal Surge (Rank 2)**, **Soul Link**, and **Envenom**.

- Adjusted **Malediction** spell description.

- Added a passive indicator to the ability **Heightened Senses** in the spellbook.

---
 **Profession Frame**
- Recipes that were not displayed despite being known now properly appear in the recipes list.

- Search results now include headers (collapsible sections of the recipes list).

- Search results update dynamically when changing the active item in either dropdown menu.

- Re-opening the tradeskill window now resets any previously applied filters.

- Fixed frame overlap issues.

- The recipes list scrollbar range now adjusts based on the number of search results.

---
 **Radio**

- The online broadcast of our in-game radio is now easier to access: <https://turtlecraft.gg/radio>

- Introducing the **Everlook Broadcasting Co. Airtime Voucher**! This new donation reward allows players to submit custom advertisements for their guild, event, or dungeon partner search, read by the talented Everlook DJ team. Mail your voucher with your pitch to "Everlook" and get ready to hear your ad on-air!
