# 2023 — June 14

## Post 49944 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-06-14T00:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49944#p49944 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed wrong disenchant loot from many custom high level blues.

- Fixed all Tallstrider mounts to behave like normal mounts.

- Removed faction requirements from raid loot that only caused issues.

- Added new scripted sequence for Melris Malagan to reflect the current reality in the shellcoin investment world.

- Warlocks will now have their Soul Shard refunded upon manually dismissing their pet.

- Last Year's Mutton now requires level 50 to equip.

- Companions Gurky, Murky and Terky will now dance with you if you dance with them.

- The Cow King will now drop Tome of Polymorph: Cow.

- Drysnap Crawler will cast Frost Shock less often.

- Reduced Korrak's health and increased the amount of loot he drops. In addition, Korrak's blood aura slightly increases the melee attack speed of players who participated in killing him.

- Corrupted Ashbringer and Neretzek, the Blood Drinker's Drain Life now scales with spell power.

- Priest's Shadow Vulnerability and Blackout no longer have a separate chance to resist from the spell that caused them to proc.

- Corrected the stats of some trash mobs in AQ40 to match classic.

- Anubisath Sentinel now uses the correct Knock Away spell.

- Stitched Spewers can now be interrupted.

- Race Against Time is now a little more forgiving with checkpoints.

- You will no longer be invited to battlegrounds in the middle of flight paths, in order to prevent some exploits.

- Totems will no longer be targeted by Chain Heal or any other positive AoE spell.

- Fixed The Tower of Lapidis X having different race requirements from the previous parts.

- Arathi Basin Runecloth Bandage no longer drops from any mobs. It can only be obtained from Care Packages.

- Added missing text to Defias Dockmaster when he detects somebody sneaking nearby.

- Ironforge Gauntlets can now be disenchanted.

- Maraudine Scout is no longer elite.

- Priest's Feedback will now proc even if the damage is absorbed.

- Attempted fix at Ouro's spawn.
