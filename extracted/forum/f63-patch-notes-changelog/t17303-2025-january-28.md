# 2025 — January 28

## Post 118168 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-01-28T12:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118168#p118168 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2025-01-29.jpg](./download/file.php?id=116) <dd>Changelog_2025-01-29.jpg (565.15 KiB) Viewed 39507 times</dd>

- Removed deprecated quests to kill the most honored or dishonored player of the week.

- Spells and abilities with additional effects beyond disarm, such as **Riposte**, will no longer undergo strict checks to verify whether the target has weapons.

- **Enlighten** now triggers when casting any Holy or Discipline spell, as intended.

- Combat log entries for damage-splitting auras, such as **Soul Link**, **Spirit Link**, and **Hand of Sacrifice**, have been corrected.

- Fixed teleporting to Horde cities from **Moonglade** during the Lunar Festival.

- Fixed **Lunar Festival Heralds** and **Emissaries** offering all the quests at once, instead of only the one for the city they are in.

- **Soldier of the Frozen Wastes** and **Soul Weaver** will now use their abilities once they attack their target. These NPCs follow regular threat table mechanics rather than targeting the closest player, as originally intended.

- The Lunar Festival will continue until February 12, giving everyone plenty of time to join the celebration!
 - Seek the Spirit of Wisdom in Dustwallow Marsh.

 - Recover a **Lunar Token** from the most powerful foes in Azeroth’s dungeons.

 - Defeat **Omen** to earn unique festive rewards!

- Purchase the **Guild Tabard Deep** at your local Guild Registration office in any capital city to secure headquarters for your guild.

- Buffed **Lower Karazhan Halls** bosses’ damage and health values to better match the rewards given, offering a more rewarding challenge.

- Players with the **Boaring Adventure** challenge enabled will now receive money from quests at level 60 without disabling the challenge.

- **Cleave** will no longer consume multiple charges of **Flurry**.

- The heal effect from **Holy Strike** can now target PvP-flagged allies inside dungeons, even if the caster is not flagged.

- The 1-1 BG bracket has been removed. Level 1 characters will now participate in the 1-9 bracket for **Warsong Gulch**.

- Removed deprecated visual aura "Entering Battleground" from the PvP tabards.

- Players can no longer use **Illusions** and **Portable Mailboxes** in Battlegrounds.

- Fixed broken **Lunar Festival Lanterns**.

- Tents are no longer able to stack. This behavior was unintended and has been corrected.

- Fixed the **Fishing Boat** not giving skill-ups correctly.

- Unlearning a profession will now interrupt any cast or craft in progress.

- Fixed an exploit related to **C'Thun**.

- Fixed **Curse of Recklessness** not stacking properly with **Demoralizing Shout**.

- **Searing Whelps** are no longer incorrectly marked as Elite.

 **Mid-Patch Itemization Changes**

 ****Mage Tier Set****

 *Developer Comment: We've taken a second look at many of our Arcane Mage alternative tier sets and converted the majority of normal spellpower into arcane spellpower, along with converting some spell hit into haste. This adjustment better fits the specialization and makes the gear more useful since Arcane already gains substantial spell hit from its talent tree.*

- **Arcanist Circlet**: Added 1% Haste, removed 9 Spellpower.

- **Arcanist Vestments**: Increased Stamina from 11 to 14, increased Intellect from 15 to 19, removed 8 Spellpower.

- **Arcanist Handwraps**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 11 to 14, increased Stamina from 8 to 11, removed 5 Spellpower.

- **Arcanist Cord**: Replaced 12 Spellpower with 13 Arcane Spellpower, increased Stamina from 6 to 8.

- **Arcanist Trousers**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 16 to 23, removed 6 Spellpower.

- **Arcanist Slippers**: Replaced 14 Spellpower with 16 Arcane Spellpower.

- **Netherwind Circlet**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 30 to 34, increased Stamina from 12 to 18, increased Intellect from 16 to 22, removed 12 Spellpower.

- **Netherwind Epaulets**: Replaced 1% Spell Hit with 1% Haste, removed 7 Spellpower.

- **Netherwind Vestments**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 26 to 30, increased Stamina from 14 to 17, removed 8 Spellpower.

- **Netherwind Wristbands**: Added 6 Stamina, removed 6 Spellpower.

- **Netherwind Handwraps**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 21 to 26, removed 6 Spellpower.

- **Netherwind Cord**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 16 to 21, removed 6 Spellpower.

- **Netherwind Trousers**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 22 to 26, removed 6 Spellpower.

- **Netherwind Slippers**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 24 to 30, removed 7 Spellpower.

- **Enigma Crown**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 28 to 36, removed 9 Spellpower.

- **Enigma Epaulets**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 24 to 30, removed 8 Spellpower.

- **Enigma Vestments**: Increased Stamina from 17 to 20, increased Arcane Spellpower from 30 to 36, removed 11 Spellpower.

- **Enigma Trousers**: Increased Stamina from 16 to 19, increased Arcane Spellpower from 32 to 40, removed 12 Spellpower.

- **Enigma Slippers**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 24 to 31, removed 9 Spellpower.

- **Frostfire Crown**: Added 12 Stamina, increased Arcane Spellpower from 36 to 40, removed 13 Spellpower.

- **Frostfire Epaulets**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 26 to 33, removed 9 Spellpower.

- **Frostfire Vestments**: Added 14 Stamina, increased Arcane Spellpower from 36 to 41, replaced 2% Spell Hit with 2% Haste, removed 13 Spellpower.

- **Frostfire Bracers**: Added 10 Stamina, removed 9 Spellpower.

- **Frostfire Handwraps**: Increased Arcane Spellpower from 26 to 33.

- **Frostfire Cord**: Replaced 1% Spell Hit with 1% Haste, removed 9 Spellpower.

- **Frostfire Trousers**: Added 1% Haste, reduced Spell Crit from 2% to 1%, increased Arcane Spellpower from 30 to 36, removed 11 Spellpower.

- **Frostfire Slippers**: Replaced 1% Spell Hit with 1% Haste, increased Arcane Spellpower from 25 to 29, removed 9 Spellpower.

 ****Warlock Tier Set****

 *Developer Comment: We’ve shifted our approach on the Affliction alternative Tier 2.5 set, converting some extra armor into stamina to make it more generally usable.*

- **Doomcaller's Crown**: Armor reduced from 250 to 100, added 19 Stamina, reduced Intellect from 29 to 28, reduced Spell Hit from 2% to 1%.

- **Doomcaller's Leggings**: Armor reduced from 277 to 107, added 22 Stamina, reduced Intellect from 33 to 29, removed 1% Spell Hit.

- **Doomcaller's Raiments**: Armor reduced from 288 to 133, added 18 Stamina, reduced Intellect from 30 to 23.

- **Doomcaller's Slippers**: Armor reduced from 192 to 82, added 15 Stamina, reduced Intellect from 24 to 21.

- **Doomcaller's Spaulders**: Armor reduced from 189 to 89, added 12 Stamina, reduced Intellect from 24 to 20, removed 1% Spell Hit, increased Spellpower from 29 to 30.

 ****Shaman Tier Set****

 *Developer Comment: Restoration Shaman Tier 2.5 had pieces stronger than their Tier 3 counterparts. We’ve rebalanced them for better consistency and improved stat allocation.*

- **Earthfury Sash**: Reduced Haste from 2% to 1%.

- **Gloves of Ten Storms**: Replaced 1% Haste with 1% Spell Critical Chance.

- **Earthshatter Boots**: Reduced Intellect from 25 to 23, increased Healing Power from 37 to 44.

- **Earthshatter Belt**: Reduced Intellect from 25 to 23, increased Healing Power from 42 to 48.

- **Earthshatter Handguards**: Reduced Intellect from 28 to 25, increased Healing Power from 35 to 42.

- **Earthshatter Headpiece**: Reduced Intellect from 30 to 28, reduced Healing Power from 68 to 62, added 1% Haste.

- **Earthshatter Legguards**: Reduced Stamina from 28 to 25, added 1% Spell Critical Chance.

- **Earthshatter Spaulders**: Reduced MP5 from 6 to 4, increased Healing Power from 42 to 51.

- **Earthshatter Tunic**: Reduced Intellect from 32 to 29, reduced MP5 from 12 to 10, increased Healing Power from 59 to 62, added 1% Haste.

- **Earthshatter Wristguards**: Reduced Intellect and Stamina by 2, increased Healing Power from 33 to 40.

- **Ring of the Earthshatterer**: Reduced Intellect and Stamina by 3, reduced MP5 from 6 to 5, increased Healing Power from 37 to 51.

 ****Druid Tier Set****

 *Developer Comment: We’ve updated several Tier 2.5 and Tier 3 Balance Druid pieces to include stamina, moved some spellpower into spell hit, and adjusted other pieces, like the Tier 2 Feral Combat belt, to better suit bear tanks.*

- **Genesis Helmet**: Added 14 Stamina, reduced Spirit from 26 to 16.

- **Genesis Treads**: Added 12 Stamina, reduced Intellect from 18 to 10.

- **Cenarion Girdle**: Replaced 1% Haste with 1% Physical Crit.

- **Stormrage Girdle**: Replaced 1% Physical Crit with 1% Dodge, increased Stamina from 16 to 19.

- **Dreamwalker Girdle**: Reduced Strength from 18 to 14.

- **Dreamwalker Wristbands**: Increased Spellpower from 24 to 26.

- **Dreamwalker Sash**: Added 1% Spell Hit, reduced Spellpower from 46 to 36, removed 10 Spell Penetration.

- **Dreamwalker Mantle**: Added 12 Stamina, reduced Spellpower from 32 to 26.

 ****Warrior Tier Set****

 *Developer Comment: We’ve updated Tier 1 and Tier 2 Arms/Fury gauntlets to better compete with alternatives like **Sacrificial Gauntlets**. Additionally, stamina has been added to several Tier 3 Arms/Fury pieces to make them less "glassy."*

- **Gauntlets of Might**: Added 12 Agility, reduced Strength from 19 to 18, removed 1% Physical Crit.

- **Gloves of Wrath**: Added 1% Physical Crit, removed 12 Agility, reduced Strength from 21 to 20.

- **Dreadnaught Gloves**: Added 11 Stamina, reduced Spirit from 18 to 10, reduced Strength from 29 to 28.

- **Dreadnaught Pauldrons**: Added 12 Stamina, reduced Agility from 24 to 22, reduced Spirit from 16 to 10.

- **Dreadnaught Sabatons**: Added 15 Stamina, reduced Strength from 26 to 19, reduced Agility from 23 to 18.

 ****Paladin Tier Set****

 *Developer Comment: Some pieces, like the Tier 2 Protection greaves and Tier 3 Retribution bracers, were stronger than intended, while others, like the Tier 3 Retribution pauldrons, were weaker. Adjustments focus on rebalancing while moving some stats into stamina without sacrificing offensive power.*

- **Judgement Handguards**: Replaced 1% Physical Hit with 1% Block.

- **Judgement Greaves**: Increased Strength from 9 to 14, reduced Block from 2% to 1%, reduced Defense from 7 to 5.

- **Avenger's Helmet**: Removed 1% Physical Hit.

- **Redemption Pauldrons**: Replaced 25 Intellect with Strength.

- **Redemption Bindings**: Reduced Haste from 2% to 1%.

- **Redemption Gauntlets**: Replaced 16 Agility with Stamina.

- **Redemption Leggings**: Added 18 Stamina, reduced Agility from 26 to 17, reduced Intellect from 23 to 15.

- **Redemption Sabatons**: Added 20 Strength and 18 Stamina, removed 60 Attack Power.

- **Redemption Greaves**: Increased Spellpower from 12 to 18, removed 14 Block Value.

 ****Non-Tier Set Changes****

- **Stratholme Holy Water**: No longer causes any threat.

- **The Black Book**: Damage and armor bonus reduced from 100% to 50%.

- **Halycon's Muzzle**: Reduced Intellect from 22 to 17, added 10 Spellpower.

- **Wyrmthalak's Shackles**: Reduced Intellect from 9 to 7, reduced Spirit from 15 to 14, added 12 Healing Power.

- **Demonfork**: Increased health leech from 50 over 25 sec. to 125 over 10 sec., increased PPM from 1 to 2.

- **Tattered Hakkari Cloak**: Removed 6 Stamina and 6 HP5, added 1% Physical Crit and Vampirism, reduced Strength from 7 to 6.

- **Faded Hakkari Cloak**: Removed 5 Stamina and 6 MP5, added 1% Spell Crit and Vampirism.

- **Chillpike**: Increased PPM from 1 to 1.8.

- **Sphere of the Endless Gulch**: Reduced stacks required to activate from 10 to 8, reduced cast speed increase duration from 15 to 12 seconds.

- **Skyfury Helmet**: Increased Physical Crit from 1% to 2%, replaced 2% Spell Crit with 1% Haste, replaced 12 Spirit with Intellect.
