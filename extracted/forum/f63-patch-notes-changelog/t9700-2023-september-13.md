# 2023 — September 13

## Post 63377 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-09-12T23:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=63377#p63377 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- The Smuggled Procurements game object in Theramore for the quest "Kul Tiran Provisions: Special Goods" now has a reduced respawn time from 5 minutes to 5 seconds.

- The minimum level requirement for the Stormwind Vault quests "The Tome of Arcane Intricacies and Magical Phenomenon IX," "Recovering Vault Shackles," and "Ending Arc'Tiras" has been increased to 58 to match the minimum level required to enter the instance.

- Fixed the description of Old Church of Westfall VII quest.

- Added a timeout to /who for improved performance.

- Your position in a queue will now be retained after being disconnected.

- Updated the script in Stormwind Vault to close the door to Arc'Tiras after he is engaged.

- Glyph of Exhaustion will no longer grant rested bonus while the player is offline.

- Fixed the detection range, bounding radius, and combat reach of Hateforge Quarry mobs.

- [Delayed due crashes] Made the Hanzi filter for public channels more strict, now affecting /say as well. *

 *Please note that full support will only be cut when transfers to SEA realms are functioning properly. We are currently preparing more space for these transfers to be possible in the first place. We understand the frustration caused by overpopulated realms, but installing server infrastructure across half the planet is a challenging task. We ask for your patience as we complete all technical preparations.*
