# 2024 — April 17

## Post 92848 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-04-16T18:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92848#p92848 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_april17.png)
- Removed stock limitations on all patterns, recipes, plans, schematics, and formulas from vendors.

- Resolved issues with the quest Guardians of the Altar.

- Sweeping Strikes will no longer target PvP-flagged players nearby if you are not PvP-flagged.

- Retaliation will no longer trigger while you are under crowd control that prevents attacking.

- Fixed Calm Elements — it was doing the exact opposite of calming.

- Fixed the drop conditions for the item Tome of Portals: Theramore.
