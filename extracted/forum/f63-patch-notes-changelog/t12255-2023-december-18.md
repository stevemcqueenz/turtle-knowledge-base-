## Post 81562 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-12-18T01:02:00Z

- Void-Linked Satchel is now Bind on Pickup.

- Made Snowball mount Bind on Pickup.

- Lowered Snowball's respawn timer to 5-6 hours.

- Fixed issues with Moroes not being able to shift through his phases.

- Added item level and disenchanting to Evermoon Staff.

- Fixed the wrong hand for Blackflame Wand.

- Fixed the wrong sheathing for Nature's Gift.

- Removed Cenarion Circle reputation reward from Race Against Time! quest.

- Fixed a sheath-display issue with the item Nature's Gift.

- Blackflame wand will now properly display as a Wand.

- Corrected the iLevel and disenchant loot for Evermoon Staff.
