# 2024 — May 1

## Post 93817 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-04-30T21:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93817#p93817 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_may1.png)

- Pets will automatically stop moving when ordered to cast a channeled spell.

- Corrected the size of Kiri Starstalker.

- Fixed Longtooth Runner being stuck in a tree.

- Spells that do not benefit from spell power will no longer consume charges of the Zandalarian Hero Charm.

- The Haunting Phantoms curse will no longer summon phantoms outside the dungeon to prevent griefing.

- Stranglethorn Raptors will now have a consistent respawn time of 5 minutes.

- Turning in Baxxil to Hizzle will now complete the quest for the entire group.

- The Ground Scorpok Array buff can no longer be cancelled, preventing accidental loss by players using the ShaguMount addon, which misidentifies it as a shapeshift due to its icon similarity with Moonkin Form.

- Rumbling Earth Elemental will now drop loot appropriate to an Earth Elemental.
