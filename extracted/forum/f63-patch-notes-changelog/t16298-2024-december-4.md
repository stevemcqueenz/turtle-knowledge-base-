# 2024 — December 4

## Post 111130 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-12-03T19:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111130#p111130 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2024-12-04_color.jpg](./download/file.php?id=86) <dd>Changelog_2024-12-04_color.jpg (415.7 KiB) Viewed 16260 times</dd>

- Fixed players being At War with the generic **Alliance**/**Horde** faction after a race change.

- Fixed inconsistent behavior in the **Spirit Armor** talent.

- **Deadly Poison** and **Corrosive Poison** are no longer mutually exclusive when applied by different Rogues.

- Fixed **Marshal Magnus Greystone** not always dropping loot.

- **Razzashi Serpent** will no longer put itself to sleep instead of the victim.

- Fixed multiple scaling issues for Paladin spells, including **Crusader Strike** and **Holy Strike**.

- Fixed incorrect calculations for negative haste effects, such as **Curse of Tongues**.

- Removed unavoidable instakill mechanic from **Damian the Ripper**.

- **Warlock Greater Demons** will now despawn if the warlock dies and cannot attack players other than the warlock.

- **Mage's Fire Vulnerability** and **Warlock's Improved Shadowbolt** debuffs are now guaranteed to hit.

- Fixed **Nefarian** sometimes dropping less loot than he should.

- **Reginald Windsor** is now unattackable by players.

- Fixed the Paladin T1 3-Piece set bonus only working the first time you cast **Consecration**.

- Damage done by the **Eye for an Eye** talent will no longer exceed 50% of the paladin's health.

- Fixed an issue where it was not possible to equip two **Emberstone Idols** at once.

- **Amber Ring** will now grant either **Strange Dust** or **Lesser Magic Essence** when disenchanted.

- Restored the correct model for **Illusion: Wraith**, now resembling the standard **Eye of Naxxramas** appearance.

- Players using **Teleport: Stormwind** will now appear facing the exit portal of the **Mage's Tower**.

- Greatly reduced the repair cost of all throwing weapons.

- **Staff of Alarus** will no longer appear upside-down when sheathed.

- **Everlasting Firework** now adds directly to your toy collection on use.

- Fixed an issue where **Elemental Precision** did not apply to **Icicles**.

- Fixed an issue where **Elemental Focus** would stop functioning after equipping **Totem of Static Charge**.

- Fixed an issue where **Searing Shot** was unable to trigger the **Searing Light** talent.

- **Amplify Magic** and **Dampen Magic** no longer affect healing from **First Aid** and similar effects.

- Fixed an issue with the **Affliction T3 2-piece set bonus**.

- The **Druid T2.5 Genesis Harness** 5-piece bonus will now correctly increase periodic damage of **Rake**.

- **Judgement of Righteousness** will no longer proc effects on the caster.

- The buff from **Drain Soul (Rank 5)** will no longer remain on you after the spell is resisted.

- The buff from **Improved Ghostly Strike** will now be applied even if the ability misses.

- Fixed an exploit that allowed you to summon infinite **Crimson Hammersmiths** from the Blacksmithing Plans in **Stratholme**.
