# 2025 — May 28

## Post 133235 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-05-27T21:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133235#p133235 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025_05-28_2.jpg)

- Fixed an issue where **Sapphiron's Iceblock** did not block line of sight.

- **Mind Vision** can no longer trigger procs or on-cast effects.

- Disarming an enemy **Hunter**'s trap will now flag the **Rogue** for PvP.

- Fixed an issue where **Chastise** could not trigger the **Searing Light** talent.

- Dual-use spells such as **Dispel** and **Chastise** can now trigger either positive or negative procs based on whether the effect is helpful or harmful. For example, using **Chastise** on a friendly target can trigger **Breath of Solnius**, but not when used on a hostile target.

- **Coast Crawl Snapclaw** is no longer an Elite creature.

- **Southsea Pirates** in **Gilijim** and **Lapidis Isles** can now drop **Volatile Rum**, similarly to other pirates.

- **Axe of the Wildtusk**: Increased item level from 35 to 40, increased DPS from 18.0 to 22.1, and reduced Agility from 5 to 4.

- Warrior and Warlock quests to swap Tier 1 and Tier 2 items in Blackrock Mountain are now repeatable, like other classes.

- Reduced damage values of the NPC **Corrosive Backup**.

- Added the new **The Great Goo'dini Elixir** to **Greenix Oiljuices** in **Thousand Needles** (slimey).

- Fixed an issue where **Durotar Labor Union** faction guards would not attack **Alliance** players in the Goblin towns.

- Fixed an issue where **Battle Boar Horrors** had no armor and did not grant XP during the **Boaring Challenge**.

- Fixed the item sound of **Red Owlbeast Feather**.

- **Totems** and **Librams** should no longer drop for the wrong faction on **Tel'Abim**.

- Fixed an issue where **Hammer of Wrath** did not benefit from melee critical strike chance as intended.
