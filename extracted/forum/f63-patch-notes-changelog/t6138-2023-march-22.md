- Fixed issues with quest Breaking the Felstar.

- You will no longer be invited to another battleground while already in a battleground.

- Added missing scripted event for quest The Blackwood Corrupted.

- Reduced respawn time of Dreadmaw Crocolisk.

- General improvements to the battleground queue system.

- The debuff of Crusader Strike should no longer have a separate chance to miss.

- Fixed issue causing Kel'Thuzad to reset.

- You will now be notified upon login if your account has received a warning.

- Adjusted positions of some creatures in world.

- Fixed an issue with Gurubashi Axe Thrower.

- New quests:
