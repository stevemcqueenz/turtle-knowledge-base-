# 2023 — July 12

## Post 53097 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-11T23:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53097#p53097 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- All reputation gains now give 100% of their intended value, regardless of the level difference between your character and the quest/mob.

- Power gains of Rage/Energy/Mana (procs, energize ticks, potion use, etc) now properly generate threat as intended.

- Melee spells will now put you in combat even if they are dodged, parried, or blocked.

- The quest "Gusting Vortexes" has had a few grammatical errors fixed.

- The quest "The Price of Leadership's" quest text should no longer be cut off.

- The NPC Randal Portman is now correctly affiliated with the Undercity.

- AQ20 and AQ40 entry portal message now reflects the correct required level.

- The NPC Yig Oilfuse's gossip text has been revised.

- Removed excessive gold drops from Winterax Trolls.

- Improved server performance and stability.

- Pet spell casts will now be interrupted upon switching to Passive mode.

- Pets can now buff other pets.

- Pets will no longer break off attacks against stealthed or invisible units if the target remains visible to their owner. (Hunter's Mark)

- Reduced Shard of the Fallen Star spell power scaling.

- Dragonbreath Chili should now be exclusive with other food buffs.

- You can no longer cheese the Ostarius fight by standing somewhere he can't reach.
