# 2023 — October 6

## Post 69006 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-10-05T20:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69006#p69006 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed <error> message on Maws' death.

- Various fixes for grammar/loot/creature/item DB inconsistencies in 1.17.0.

- Anniversary party presents are now in the game!

- Argent Dawn Shoulder enchants will now require reputation and a quest to unlock.

- Elemental weapon damage spells can now proc again.

- Fixed non-working Eternal Quintessence.

- Fixed faction and skinning loot of the Soothhounds NPCs.

- Added missing Nightmare Effigy drops.

- Fixed race of Mossheart in Black Morass.

- Drifting Avatar of Sand can no longer be stunned or frozen.

- New display ID for Shard of Eternity.

- Added missing Gilneas plate drops.

- Lowered respawn timer for Vilemusk NPCs.

- Enforced English in /h.

- Fixed spell triggers on Finkle's Lava Dredger.

- Fixed respawn timer of High Energy Regulator.

- Set frog pets to Uncommon rarity.

- Fixed low respawn timers in The Black Morass.

- Fixed decay rank issue caused by the faulty honor update last time.

- Removed cloth from Relentless Infernal's loot and added stones.

- Made Nordanaar Guardian act like guards.

- Made new teleport books self-cast.

- Made a number of community-contributed typos, grammar fixes, and general language improvements for various quests.

- Added command to leave HC chat: .hcchat.

- Made The Lower Binding of Xanthar drop for all party members.

- Updated Shame Never Again quest rewards.

- Fixed Cleaning Cloth Outline skill requirement.
