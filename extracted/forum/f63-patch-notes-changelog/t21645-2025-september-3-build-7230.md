# 2025 — September 3 [Build 7230]

## Post 149960 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-09-02T22:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149960#p149960 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-09-03_ENG.png)

- Fixed a glitch with the **Survival Skill** window.

- Adjusted the damage of **Northwind Guards**.

- **Razorscale** is now tameable and has proper armor values.

- Fixed the icon of **Turtle Meat** and applied the new **Soothing Spice** icon to the correct item.

- Increased the drop chance of **Dragonmaw Bracers**, **Dragonmaw Greaves**, **Dragonmaw Hauberk**, **Dragonmaw Leggings**, **Stormreaver Boots**, and **Stormreaver Belt** from 0.11% to 1.35%.

- Removed **Scarlet Bracers** from **Stormwrought Ruins** creatures.

- **Stormreaver Boots** are now Bind on Equip.

- Fixed missing changes for the **Chromatic Cloak**.

- Fixed an issue where mounts could not be used as a **Dryad** or **Worgen** while under the effect of **Path of the Wild**.

- Reduced **Aku'mai Snapjaw**'s damage by 25% and health by 18%.

- Updated the display of **Flamescale Pike**, **Nature's Call**, **Jadestone Protector**, and **Mallet of the Awakening** with custom-made, more fitting models.

- Added 7 missing rare Bind on Equip trash drops to the **Castle** section and 8 missing rare Bind on Equip trash drops to the **Caverns** section of **Stormwrought Ruins**.

- Fixed certain cases where **Dampening** of healing and absorb shields did not function correctly in PvP.

- Fixed the quest reward for “**Excessive Regulations**.”

- Fixed the model of the **Twilight Unicorn** mount.

- The following recipes have had their reagents updated:
 - **Concoction of the Dreamwater** no longer requires 6x **Sungrass**.

 - **Concoction of the Arcane Giant** no longer requires 6x **Purple Lotus**.

 - **Concoction of the Emerald Mongoose** no longer requires 6x **Wildvine**.

 - All three now require 1x **Imbued Vial** as a crafting reagent.

- **Molten Core**:
 - Reduced the health of trash creatures by up to 20%.

 - **Golemagg** will once again enrage at 10% health.

 - **Ragnaros** no longer blinds the raid when struck by **Arcane Shot**.

 - **Golemagg's Core Hounds** now move 25% slower.

- **Level One Lunatics** are no longer bound by the no-multiboxing rule in challenges.

- Added the **Onyxian Drake** as a drop from **Onyxia**.

- Added the **Emerald Drake** as a drop from **Solnius**.

- Added missing particle effects to the **Stormwrought Deathsteed**.

- Fixed a visual issue with the **Stormwrought Deathsteed**.

- Fixed an issue where the quest tracker overlapped with debuffs.

- Fixed visual issues with the **Shadowhorn Stag** mount.

- Rescaled the model of **Tor Nagaz**.

- Added music and ambience to the entrance and exit of **Dragonmaw Retreat**.

- Increased the proc chance of the **Idol of the Forgotten Wilds Sproutling** to 15% and its duration to 12 seconds.

- Fixed an issue with flickering NPCs in a tower in **Azshara**.

- Fixed a rare crash in **Dragonmaw Retreat**.

- Fixed a rare crash in **Stormwrought Ruins**.

- Fixed an issue with flickering NPCs in the **Scarlet Enclave**.

- **Baited Shot** no longer shares a cooldown with **Arcane Shot** or **Aimed Shot**.
 *Note: this update will include a small client patch that will be delivered through the launcher. Please be aware that the changes will take effect approximately 7–8 hours after our usual honor update restart.*
