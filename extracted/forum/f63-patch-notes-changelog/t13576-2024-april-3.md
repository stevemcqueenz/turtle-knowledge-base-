# 2024 — April 3

## Post 91958 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-04-03T00:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91958#p91958 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_april3.png)

- NPCs Solnius and Erennius have been made immune to horror and disorientation effects.

- Lost Excavation Pickaxe has been removed from NPC vendor Valentino Westwood's inventory.

- NPC Healing Ward IV will no longer drop loot.

- NPC Tamaala Bloodhoof now correctly appears as a female Tauren.

- Addressed an issue with Brightly Colored Egg producing empty loot.

- Fixed a bug with Argent Dawn Commission Auras occupying two slots and bonuses failing to apply after death.

- Hakkari Frostwings will now move around and share spawn points with Hakkari Sappers.

- Items obtained from chests can now be traded with raid members, consistent with loot from creatures.

- Flash of Light will no longer consume two charges of "Talisman of Ascendancy" and "Eye of the Dead."

- Corrected an issue where AoE healing from spells like Holy Nova was being incorrectly blocked by LoS.

- Agitated Rock Elemental now shares the same immunities as other Rock Elementals.

- Fixed an issue with some mobs dropping Scourgestones without the required trinket.

- Blackhand Assassins will no longer cast spells while under crowd control effects.

- Updated waypoints for various mobs across the world.

- Buffs above the 32 client cap will now always properly function even if there's no aura slot available.
