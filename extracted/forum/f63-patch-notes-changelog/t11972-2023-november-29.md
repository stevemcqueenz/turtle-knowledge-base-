# 2023 — November 29

## Post 79223 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-11-29T07:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79223#p79223 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Due to recent unfavorable legislation in Stormwind and Orgrimmar, Shell Co offices have been closed, and Marlos Catos has relocated his investment business to Booty Bay.

- Leaving Razorgore's room in BWL is no longer possible before completing the encounter.

- Adjusted the proc rate of Brood Affliction: Bronze to 25%.

- Made some tweaks to Chromaggus' script.

- Added many missing types of spell immunities to various creatures.

- Fixed the Southshore Crier event.

- An error message is now displayed when attempting to create chat channels with an invalid name.

- Adjusted timers on abilities cast by Emperor Vek'nilash.

- Fixed the equip effect on Beastmaster's Gloves.

- Fixed Veil of Nightmare being unable to be enchanted.

- Fixed Corrupted Sand having its Unique count set to 250 instead of its stack size. Corrupted Sand can now stack up to 250 per inventory slot without limits.

- Other bosses in BWL will now only spawn after completing the Razorgore encounter to prevent cheating.

- Vagrant's Challenge restrictions regarding enchants no longer apply once you reach level 60.

- The effects of Totem of Crackling Thunder and Battery-Powered Crowd Pummeler can now stack.

- Escort NPCs following the player, and unattackable by enemy players, will no longer assist in PvP.

- Fixed the inability to loot Whipper Root Tubber.

- Onyxia and Ragnaros will now drop 2 tier set pieces as intended on Tel'Abim PvP.

- Kylanna Windwhisper can now train Lesser Healing Potion.

- Fixed line of sight issues with a Barrel of Sweet Nectar in STV.

- The Fire's Glow buff can now stack with other spirit buffs.

- Added equipment to Ragnak Helmcleaver, Vi'cha Spirithowler, Elder Orgrun, Rahna Longstrider, and Witchdoctor T'zabagesh.

- Changed stats of Chromie's Broken Pocket Watch, replacing %2 Spell Hit with 10 Spirit. Dev Note: This is a temporary measure until the haste proc is changed to be a negative effect in next next client update. (Patch 1.17.1), ss it is currently greatly outperforming its ilevel.
