# 2022 — August 24-30

## Post 24112 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-09-01T08:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24112#p24112 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

• Reflective Shields will now work against creatures too.
• Pain Spike now benefits from Mental Agility.
• Gravestones will now look different depending on the player level.
• You can no longer send your pet to attack a target that is not in line of sight inside dungeons, in order to prevent exploits that allowed you to pull bosses through closed gates or walls.
• Fixed the possibility of duplicated add spawns if you wipe at Majordomo.
• Fixed not being able to open some Hidden Strongboxes due to a line of sight bug.
• The Making a Statement quest will only reduce your Booty Bay reputation, instead of all goblin reputations.
• Chests can now contain money.
• The malfunction effect of Goblin Rocket Boots no longer applies the debuff that is intended for the Gnomish boots.
• Meshlok the Harvester is no longer immune to Gouge.
• Increased the drop chance of Eidolon Talisman.
• The quest Thandol Span has been fixed.
• Judgement of Light healing will now be done by the unit that's attacking as is blizzlike. This means the paladin will no longer get flagged for PvP when another flagged player attacks the same mob.
• Fixed a typo in the The Pumpkin Thieves quest description.
• /played command will show correct completed quest count.
• Lowered respawn timers for several quest objects in starting zones.
• Corrected base mana for Tauren Druids.
• Multiple fixes for the content issues reported to our bug tracker.
• Removed riding requirement from the Immortal Charger (vote on the mount type is still happening very soon).
• Thandol Span quest should now be working for real.
• Blacksmithing will no longer get dropped or bugged when unlearning a specialization. You will retain your current skill level, but you will have to re-train any recipes that are learned from trainers.
• Far Sight and Eagle Eye are no longer prevented by line of sight.
• The 3 piece bonus of the Shaman Tier 2 set now also increases Chain Lightning damage. This change will not be reflected on the tooltip until the next client patch is out.
• Fixed issues with the Stormwind Vault Rat Trap.
• The mana cost of several Felguard abilities has been reduced. This change will not be reflected on the tooltip until the next client patch is out.
• Artist Renfray is no longer invisible.
• Fixed the requirements of the Preventive Strike quest.
• You will no longer get kicked from your group for engaging in PvP.
• Earth Shield will now scale with your bonus healing.
• Remove Curse can now be used in Moonkin Form. This change will not be reflected on the tooltip until the next client patch is out.
• Shaman's Spirit Link has undergone some minor changes and it finally works. Damage taken by the target will be split among nearby group members, instead of chain bounce targets. This change will not be reflected on the tooltip until the next client patch is out.
• It should now be less likely for the position of players under Fear effects to get desynced.
• Charging players should no longer appear to teleport sometimes.
• You can now turn while dancing without interrupting the dance.
