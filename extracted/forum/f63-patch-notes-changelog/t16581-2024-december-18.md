# 2024 — December 18

## Post 113088 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-12-17T22:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113088#p113088 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2024-12-18_2.jpg](./download/file.php?id=92) <dd>Changelog_2024-12-18_2.jpg (427.39 KiB) Viewed 29947 times</dd>

- Quest **Avenging the Fallen** is no longer incorrectly marked as repeatable.

- When a hardcore character dies, they will now see the message explaining the reason for their death, even if they are too low level for others to see it.

- Quests **Preventing Poison** and **Curing the Sick** now use different quest items to prevent charge-related issues when both are accepted.

- **The Four Horsemen** now enrage after 20 minutes, as intended.

- Fixed an issue where the walking speed of creatures with a shapeshift or stance aura was permanently reduced by slow effects if the aura was applied or removed while they were slowed.

- **Repent** will no longer trigger multiple times from a single attack, such as Cleave.

- Normal creatures now have primary attributes and can be affected by buffs and debuffs that modify them.

- Fixed a regression where off-hand damage was lower than intended.

- Moved 29 transmogrification items to the fashion vendors, which were previously in the donation store.

- **The Idol of the Apex Predator** now provides the advertised rage cost reduction.

- **Atiesh's** aura will no longer be canceled upon the character's death.

- Fixed some creatures being aggressive when they previously were not.

- Fixed a bug in the **LFT** system that teleported some players to the **RFC** dungeon unintentionally.

- Corrected the damaging spell for **Searing Totem Rank 1** and fixed its functionality with **Flame Guidance**.

- **Moll-E Terminal** now has a 5-minute cooldown.

- The Winter Veil boss **Snowball** now has double the HP, no longer casts a deadly AoE when receiving frost damage, and has a respawn time reduced by half.

- Fixed players not being flagged for PvP when assisting another flagged player in **Winter Veil Vale**.

- Corrected some incorrect item prices at the fashion vendors.
