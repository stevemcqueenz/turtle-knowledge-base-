# 2025 — November 26

## Post 160700 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-11-25T21:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160700#p160700 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-11-26-ENG.jpg)

- Fixed player scale changing while in **Astral Form**.

- Fixed ghost visual remaining permanently if you cancel **Glyph of the Moon** while in Moonkin Form.

- **Reshift** can no longer be used with **Involuntary Transformation** or any other temporary/debuff form.

- **Flamestring Bow** is now soulbound.

- Fixed broken flight transitions between routes going from **Slickwick Oil Rig** through **Gadgetzan**.

- **Chained Essence of Eranikus** can no longer be sold.

- **Daphne Stilwell** should no longer get stuck when starting her quest.

- Custom race/class combinations and several **Human** classes now use the correct stats templates.

- **High elves** and **Goblins** now have unique starter stats.

- **Disassembler** (racial passive) now functions as intended.

- Attempting to channel a spell that would be interrupted by movement will no longer cast and will display an error stating you cannot do that while moving. This resolves issues such as players being flagged with **Recently Bandaged** even if the spell was immediately canceled due to movement.

- Queued next‑melee spells and abilities, such as **Heroic Strike**, will be interrupted if the player has insufficient power (Rage, Mana, etc.). This can occur if the power is depleted by other means after the spell or ability has been queued.

- Fixed an issue where **Rabies**, **Cadaver Worms** and similar spells did not affect health regeneration from food.

- Fixed an issue where creatures, pets and demons were not affected by health‑regeneration auras, such as **Strider Presence**.

- Fixed several raid downscaling issues, especially in the **Tower of Karazhan**.

 **Ambershire RPPvE**

The Field Duty Silithus quests were erroneously made available before their scheduled time. The quests have now been disabled, and players who completed them will have their rewards temporarily removed. Those rewards will be restored a few days after the designated phase opens. We're sorry!
