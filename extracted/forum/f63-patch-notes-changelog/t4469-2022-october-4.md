# 2022 — October 4

## Post 26723 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-10-04T18:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26723#p26723 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Please read the changelog for the upcoming server update and the maintenance:

- Fixed the stamina bonus of Druid's Frenzied Regeneration.

- Shaman's Elemental Weapons talent now properly increases Flametongue Weapon damage.

- Pattern: Boots of the Wind can now drop from mobs in Feralas.

- Some slight adjustments to loot tables of Overlord Ramtusk and Agathelos the Raging.

- Slaver Vilegrip can now drop other stuff in addition to the quest item.

- Warrior's Improved Disciplines talent will now reduce the Retaliation cooldown. This change may not be reflected on the tooltip until the next client patch.

- Your keybinds and macros will now be saved on the server, so you won't lose them if you reinstall the client.

- The Clearcasting buff from the Shaman Elemental Focus talent should no longer have both charges consumed by a single cast.

- Corrected the PPM rate of Jeklik's Crusher.

- Fixed a typo in the description of quest Mystery of Lake Mennar.

- Fixed Illusion: Scarlet Crusade not working. You may be unable to see the buff until the next client patch.

- Whirlwind Shredders will no longer move around out of combat.

- Fixed Helboars being flagged for PvP.

- Fixed Druid's Moonfury talent not increasing Wrath damage.
