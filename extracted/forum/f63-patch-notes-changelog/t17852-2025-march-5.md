# 2025 — March 5

## Post 121678 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-03-04T22:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121678#p121678 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2025-03-05.jpg](./download/file.php?id=139) <dd>Changelog_2025-03-05.jpg (419.63 KiB) Viewed 22204 times</dd>

- **Barber shops** are back! You can now change your hairstyle for 3 Fashion Tokens.

- **Hair dye vendors** have returned to Stormwind and Sparkwater Port.

- **C'Thun** and his tentacles are now immune to Feign Death.

- **Sacred Chalice** and **Blazing Forge Kit** will no longer stop functioning after 5 minutes instead of the intended 10 minutes.

- Added disenchantment loot to the **Glittering Ring**.

- Completing the quest **Four Dukes** now yields 100 reputation points with **Cenarion Circle**.

- Added disenchantment loot to the **Bronze Scepter**.

- Fixed an issue where **Improved Shadow Bolt** could incorrectly proc on the caster.

- Fixed the zone category for **Devils in Westfall**, now properly listed under Westfall.

- Added **Crusader Strike Rank 1** to the High Elven starting trainer **Andalideth Suncaller**.

- Fixed an issue where **Blackout** did not proc from the intended Shadow Priest spells.

- **Dragonbane Pauldrons** will now drop Large Radiant Shards when disenchanted.

- Fixed **Dream Herald**'s movement reduction effect.

- **Irontree Heart** will no longer disappear from **Eridan's Supplies** on logout.

- The dismiss button now works with **Eye of Kilrogg**.

- Power regeneration (including mana) is now subject to stochastic rounding, fixing edge cases where effects like **The Human Spirit** provided no bonus at lower regeneration rates.

- The quest **First Mate McGillicuddy** now only requires his head for completion.

- **Pounce** now grants combo points and applies the bleed effect even if the target is immune to stuns.

- **Bloodpetal Pest** can no longer be skinned.

- Fixed an issue where certain spells and items could not be triggered if the target was a demon.

- Added a way to recover **Dartol's Rod** if players lose it while doing the questline **Raene's Cleansing**.

- **Prelate Ironmane** and **Spectral Clerics** are now properly categorized as Undead.
