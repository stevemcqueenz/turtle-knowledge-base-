# 2022 — November 7

## Post 28636 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-11-07T16:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28636#p28636 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- You can now refresh Eyes of the Beast while channeling without interrupting it.

- Seal of Righteousness is now allowed to proc off other procs.

- Patrons will now be temporarily stunned after drinking Dark Iron Ale, and will not cause others to aggro if killed before the stun expires.

- Respawned all chests in the open world. Some spawns have been removed, while others have been added.

- Open world chests are now grouped per zone, and only a set number will be spawned at the same time.

- Added a minimum level requirement to open chests.

- Removed threat on miss from last ranks of Mind Soothe and Soothe Animal.

- Multiple grammar fixes for the issues reported in our bug-tracker.

- Updated Field Repair Bot 75B's model to something less loud.

- Fixed Whiteclaw Pelt drop from Icepoint Whiteclaw.

- Fixed ordering for Triggered! chain.

- Fixed Tirisclaw Ambusher loot table.

- Fixed Daphne Stilwell holding a cube instead of a gun.

- Fixed gossip display for Young and Foolish quest.

- Plans: Rune-Etched Breastplate are now corectly consumed on use.

- Added missing spawns for Spire Spider Eggs in BRD.

- Fixed Insect Swarm ranks and trainers (requires a client update).

- Fixed Fifth Mosh'aru Tablet's respawn timer.

- Fixed High Elf starting weapon inconsistencies.

- Made performance improvements regarding how entities in the world are found and updated.

- Fixed a bug where Hardcore characters would get old Auction House items mailed back to them.

- Fixed a bug where players were able to summon Ostarius multiple times during a fight.

- Fixed a bug where items weren't added to the  available Transmogrification list upon obtaining them.

- Fixed a bug where the Cartographer title wasn't always properly rewarded.

- Added the Tauren shrinking potion.

- Fixed a bug where Improved Drain Soul would grant its buff upon a different NPC dying than the one you are draining.

- Fixed a bug where Hunter's Viper Sting stacked.

- The Riding Turtle mount is now able to swim in water, retaining 50% of its movement speed bonus in water.

- Fixed a bug where Conflagrate did not trigger Pyroclasm.

- Fixed a bug where you would sometimes be teleported into the void when getting into a Mirage Race.

- Fixed a bug where Priest's Improved Shadowform was unlearned everytime a talent reset happened.

- Fixed multiple bugs with AoE auras not ticking their proper amount of times.
 **PvP-related:**

- Added 10-19 bracket quests for Warsong Gulch for Horde and Alliance.

- You will now automatically leave the battleground queue if you out level the bracket.

- You will no longer drop to your death if you accept a battleground queue while flying.

- Reworked some parts of the rewards for BGs and premature timers. See #pvp-news on Discord for more information.

- Fixed a bug where the Mirace Raceway abilities allowed you to flag someone for PvP.

- Fixed a bug with some guardian pets that allowed them to attack non PvP opposing faction players.
