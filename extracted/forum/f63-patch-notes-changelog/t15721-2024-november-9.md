## Post 107253 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-11-09T15:29:00Z

![Changelog_2024-11-09_2.jpg](./download/file.php?id=78) <dd>Changelog_2024-11-09_2.jpg (463.13 KiB) Viewed 16955 times</dd>

Contuining our rapid iterations of bug fixes. Below is a list of changes that are currently live.

There are other fixes that concern client issues however they can't immediately be reflected on the live servers because they require client updates. A midpatch fix update is slated to roll out somewhere next week, once this is done these hotfixes will also be listed.

- Bulwark of the Righteous now shares a cooldown between ranks.

- You can no longer get dazed in Swift Travel Form from ranged and spell attacks.

- Fixed the nature damage buff of Stormstrike not working, but it will be invisible until a new client update is released.

- Corporal Keeshan should no longer get stuck climbing terrain.

- Fixed an issue where Resonance Cascade did not scale down the spell damage bonus for each duplication as intended.

- Fixed an issue that sometimes caused the Paladin's global cooldown to get stuck.

- Fixed an issue where Rapid Fire and Quick Shots were not stacking.

- Steady Shot critical strikes now correctly trigger Piercing Shots.

- Players will no longer get PvP flagged when fighting Private Hendel during The Missing Diplomat quest.

- Fixed an exploit in AQ20 that allowed players to fight General Rajaxx's troops without starting the event.

- Fixed an issue where Feid Rota would not properly swap some Priest T2.5 items.

- ZG trinkets are now swappable through Feid Rota.

- Fixed an issue where debuffs were dropping off before the 64 debuff limit was reached on a target.

- LFT is back up and functional again.

- Added XP to Money conversion at max level for many quests that were missing it.

- Added localization to Feid Rota.

- Bile Sludges in Naxxramas should no longer multiply to infinity.

- It should now be possible to train Serpent and Fox pets.

- Resolved stacking issues between some set bonuses and talents.

- Removed deprecated version of Spirit Link from players that was causing their talents to get reset on each login.

- Fixed an issue where some NPCs would erroneously drop Jewelcrafting crafts.

- Kel'thuzad no longer drops the equipment ring items and instead drops ring tokens with the same class limitations as the other gear tokens.

- These new tokens can be turned in at Light's Hope Chapel into an original or alternative tier ring for no additional cost and Rogues and Hunters now have new quests to turn the ring tokens for their tier rings due to them not getting one with the release of Tower of Karazhan as they do not have any alternative sets to swap into.

Some quick math to show what I mean -
Let's say you're a druid, and using my previous naxx composition, there was 2 druids total.

Given there's 9 classes, KT has a 8/9 chance of not dropping your ring. He's guaranteed to drop 2 rings so the chance of him not dropping a druid ring is (8/9 x 8/9) = 64/81. Therefore, the chance he drops at least 1 druid ring is: 1 - 64/81 = 17/81, or ~21% chance at least 1 druid ring drops. Now you have to win the roll, which for simplicity sake given its just 1 other druid, is 50/50. 17/81 x 1/2 = 17/162, or ~10.49% chance 1 druid ring drops and you win the roll.

Now using the new system, KT now drops 3 tokens with the original class groupings. Instead of the druid ring dropping 1/9 its now 1/3. Again, KT drops 2 tokens, the chance no druid ring drops is now (2/3 x 2/3) = 4/9. Therefore, the chance he drops at least 1 druid-useable ring is 1 - 4/9 = 5/9, or ~56%.

Using the same naxx comp as above, instead of competing with 1 other druid, you now compete with - 1 druid, 1 hunter, 5 paladins, and 5 shamans (12 other people). Your odds of winning the roll, assuming equal again is 1/13. So your chance of at least 1 usable token dropping and you win the roll is 5/9 x 1/13 = 5/117, or ~4%.
