# 2025 — December 10

## Post 162251 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-12-09T23:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162251#p162251 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-12-10-ENG.jpg)

- **Ectoplasmic Distiller** can no longer be used in major cities.

- Creature‑type‑specific Attack Power bonuses (e.g., from **Mark of the Champion**) now apply to auto attacks.

- **The Stormhowl** 3/6 set bonus now works as intended.

- **Hunter's Mark** now correctly applies to auto attacks, fixing the regression.

- **Flametongue Weapon** – damage and scaling calculations have been corrected.

- **Earthfury Battlegear** 5/8 set bonus now scales correctly.

- Fishing in **Lapidis Isle**, **Gilijim's Isle**, **Icepoint Rock** and **Undercity** now yields fish again.

- **Icy Menace** quest now requires the correct targets.

- Missing **Winter Veil** cosmetic items have been re‑added.

- **Krampus** and **Xandivious** are now immune to **Enslave Demon**.

- Periodic persistent auras such as **Hurricane**, **Flamestrike** and similar effects now correctly register all ticks in the combat log (previously missed/resisted ticks are now shown).
