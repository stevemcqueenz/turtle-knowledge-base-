## Post 116739 by Reynolds (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-01-14T20:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116739#p116739 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2025-01-15.jpg](./download/file.php?id=110) <dd>Changelog_2025-01-15.jpg (463.32 KiB) Viewed 16407 times</dd>

- The Tier 1 Paladin Retribution 3-piece set bonus now correctly affects **Judgement of Righteousness** and **Judgement of Command** as intended.

- Moved one Battered Chest out of a no-LoS area in **Elwynn Forest**.

- Fixed an exploit that allowed trading loot inside gift boxes when opened inside a raid.

- **Mudrock Borer** can now be skinned.

- It is no longer possible to understand opposite faction messages in the **LocalDefense** and **WorldDefense** channels.

- **Curse of Agony** should no longer remove other players' **Curse of Doom** due to the **Malediction** talent.

- **Sir Zeliek's Holy Wrath** will no longer target Totems.

- **Shaman's Bloodlust** no longer unintentionally shares a cooldown with items such as **Juju Flurry**.

- Corrected the waypoints of **Fozruk** in **Arathi Highlands**.

- Fixed **Vangros** not respecting threat.

- Fixed an issue with the Human's **Diplomacy** (Racial Passive) where it did not correctly apply to small reputation rewards.

Arcane mage T3 6piece bonus not working after patch!please check!

The 6-piece set bonus of the Mage Arcane T3 gear restores 3% of maximum mana on a critical hit during Arcane Power. It was functioning normally before, but after this week's update, the mana restored on critical hits has significantly decreased. I have a total of over 6000 mana, but during Arcane Power, each critical hit only restored 48 mana, far less than 3% of the total.
Through testing, I discovered the issue. The description of Arcane Power states that it reduces mana regeneration effects by 75% during its duration. After this update, this effect has been incorrectly applied to the Arcane T3 6-piece set bonus and the Arcane Absorption talent. This causes the mana restoration effects of the 6-piece bonus and the talent to be nearly ineffective during Arcane Power. I believe this is an error introduced during the update.
The root issue might be that the Arcane Mage T3 6-piece set bonus, "Arcane Power no longer reduces your mana regeneration," is not functioning correctly after this update. Please address this promptly, as it is a critical effect for the Arcane Mage build.
