# 2022 — December 7

## Post 30302 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-12-07T05:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30302#p30302 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Multiple fixes to Solenor the Slayer.

- Added missing Redstone Crystalhide spawns.

- Added missing Redstone Basilisk spawns.

- Slightly increased the spell power coefficient of Arcane Missiles to match advertised value.

- Fixed a bug where some NPCs didn't take the players height into account when they would aggro. Fixing multiple pull issues.

- Fixed Enslave Demon attacking players that aren't flagged.

- Fixed random movement of certain NPCs in locations without proper movement maps.

- Fixed a possible Guild exploit that allowed double invites.

- Fixed the target of a Pet's attack being the combat aggressor. This fixes Booty Bay guard responses for pets and the like.

- Fixed Hunter Pets not always being flagged for PvP when they should be.

- Fixed Pets sometimes being deleted upon logout.

- Fixed some miscellaneous at-war issues.
