## Post 102784 by Reynolds (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-10-08T21:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102784#p102784 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog 2024-08-28 (1).jpg](./download/file.php?id=21) <dd>Changelog 2024-08-28 (1).jpg (419.07 KiB) Viewed 9588 times</dd>

- General Marcus Jonathan will no longer aggro Scourge Invasion skeletons.

- Watcher Biggs will no longer drop any loot.

- The patterns for Glacial Wrists, Polar Bracers, Icy Scale Bracers, and Icebane Bracers now require Argent Dawn reputation instead of Hydraxian Waterlords reputation.

- Fixed an issue where Concavius and Ostarius did not consistently drop items.

- Fixed an exploit that allowed players to reset a dungeon while remaining in the same position inside the new instance.

- Added a missing event after completing the quest Cycle of Rebirth.

- Players will no longer be flagged for contested PvP when attacking enemy players in battlegrounds, preventing neutral city guards from becoming hostile upon exiting.
