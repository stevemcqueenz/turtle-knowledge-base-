# 2023 — July 26

## Post 54660 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-25T22:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54660#p54660 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Pyrewood Village vendors can now repair gear.

- Empower Champion should now apply ranged attack power.

- Added weather effects to Tel'Abim, Gilijim's Isle, Lapidis Isle.

- Simone the Inconspicuous will now have waypoints again, instead of standing still.

- Limited the frequency of announcements about new battleground matches starting to reduce spam.

- Fixed rotation of Zul'Gurub madness event bosses.

- Fixed a crash related to blinking at the edge of the map.

- Removed The Black Morass requirement for Cartographer for now.

- Made an attempt to fix the issue with totems of warmode players not giving a buff.

- Ostarius pedestal should no longer be stuck in an uninteractable state if the player who turned in the quest leaves.

- Malor the Zealous will now always drop a blue item.

- Flamekin Torcher and Flamekin Rager will give experience on kill again, but less than other mobs for that level.
 **Sunnyglade Valley**

- Added an introduction sequence that explains the premise and objective during the preparation period before the battleground begins.

- Reduced the health scaling of bosses, making them easier to kill.

- Footmen and Grunts will now use their Battle Shout / Demo Shout less often, and it's a lower rank than before. They have also been nerfed to level 55 instead of 60, and have less health now.

- Regular NPCs will no longer increase Wardens of Time reputation when killed by default. Instead, some of them will secretly be Infinite Dragonflight agents in disguise, who will reveal their true form when attacked by players. Only those that transform into drakonids will give reputation. They will also give reputation to the whole team, not just the killer.

- Sparks now actually look like sparks instead of chests.

- Added smaller spark spawns in empty areas of the map, which will contain only 1 spark.

- Turning in sparks will now give reputation, and the amount of reputation rewarded at the end of the battleground has been reduced to compensate.

- Added 1 mini boss per faction and 2 neutral mini bosses in the mines, which will drop Sunnyglade-only trinkets with unique effects that will disappear once you leave the battleground.

- Changed the player limit from 10 to 20 players.

- Added a healing leaf in the empty room of the keep.
 **As a disclaimer, this update might get delayed until the morning.**
