![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-12-03-ENG.jpg)

- **Resilience** now reduces the entire critical damage taken, not just the bonus critical damage.

- **Calming River** now adds 8 mana per 5 seconds to regular mana regeneration while **Water Shield** is active, replacing the separate periodic timer.

- **Gardening** crops can no longer be watered by players who didn’t plant them.

- The boars of **Northwind** have eaten enough and are now neutral.

- Fixed an issue where fishing in **Tel’abim** yielded no fish.

- **Human Hunters** now use the correct stats template.

- Fixed some **Corrupted Songflowers** not spawning a cleansed version after turning in the quest.
