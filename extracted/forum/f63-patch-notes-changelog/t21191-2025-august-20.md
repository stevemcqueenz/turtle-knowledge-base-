# 2025 — August 20

## Post 147116 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-08-19T23:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147116#p147116 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-08-20_ENG.jpeg)

- **Lacerate** and **Immolation Trap** dots can now stack with each other.

- Lowered the respawn timer for **Stolen Horse Feed Sack**.

- Lowered the respawn timer for **Autumny Leaf Pile**.

- Increased the drop chance of **Wolf Tongue** for the Quest '**The Woods Have Changed**'.

- Corrected the text of **Sir Amberwood's Report**.

- **Nadia Geringt** now sells horses only to players **Exalted** with **Stormwind**.

- **Ravasaur Egg** now correctly contains **Nearly-Hatches Ravasaur Egg**.

- Removed unintended pet spells from **Ashpaw Hunter** and **Ironfur Patriarch**.

- Fixed an exploit with **Stormwrought Ruins** and **Dragonmaw Retreat** which allowed you to reset the dungeon and remain at the same position inside it, but in a fresh instance.

- You will no longer respawn in place if you die at specific places in **Molten Core**.

- Added missing equip spells to **Gem of Nerubis**.

- **Ighal'for** will not die prematurely before summoning **Mergothid**.

- Reduced the amount of gold dropped by **Smoldaris** and **Basalthar**.

- Added gold to **Incindis**.

- It is now possible to enable and disable **War Mode** as many times as you want.

- Pets will no longer teleport to you after you outrange them.

- **Mana Attuned** no longer fades upon death.

- **Rethevus'** spells now ignore **Line of Sight**.

- **Faerie Darters** are now also susceptible to **Physical damage**.

- **Faerie Dust** is no longer resistable.

- Fixed the **Cartographer** requirements.

- **Undertaker Gordo** no longer identifies as a **gryphon**.

- Fixed the reward choice for the quest **The Redbrand Lie**.

- The **Gullwing Crew** on **Balor** is now safe and can no longer be attacked.

- **Waterlogged Footlocker** no longer contains the heirlooms of the **Crawford Family**.

- The visiting pandaren tourists have concluded their vacation and left **Darnassus**.

- Opening **Benedict's Chest** will no longer summon more than 1 **marine** every 2 minutes.

- Fixed an issue where several **elixirs** were not correctly stacking with each other.

- **Shaman's Elemental Flurry** and **Flurry** can now properly stack with each other.

- Fixed an issue with **Chain Heal** bouncing.

- Some **buff foods** no longer stack with each other, meaning you can only have one **Well Fed** buff active on you at a time.

- Fixed an issue with Armor above 75% DR.
 ****Molten Core Changes****

- Reduced **Ragnaros'** health by 15%.

- Reduced **Majordomo Executus'** **flamewakers'** health by 10%.

- Reduced **Son of Flame's** **mana burn** amount by 25%.

- Massively reduced the amount of periodic **fire damage** dealt to the raid by **Ragnaros**.

- Fixed an issue with **Ragnaros' Elemental Fire** damaging the target twice.

- Reduced **Majordomo Executus' Teleport** stun duration from 3 seconds to 1 second.

- **Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan's Rune of Detonation** and **Combustion** are now less punishing when their mechanics aren't done.

- Reduced **Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan's Arcane Barrage** knockback.
