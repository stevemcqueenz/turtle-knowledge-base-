# 2024 — November 6

## Post 106599 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-11-05T22:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106599#p106599 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2024-11-05_4.jpg](./download/file.php?id=77) <dd>Changelog_2024-11-05_4.jpg (310.03 KiB) Viewed 16836 times</dd>

- Holy Nova now generates normal threat instead of no threat.

- Robe of Faith quest now grants the correct reward.

- Toy spells now have consistent cooldowns, with missing cooldowns and cast times corrected.

- Serpent’s Shoulders are now Bind on Pickup (BoP).

- Quest Item Swap system functioning as intended.

- Fishing nodes now despawn as expected.

- Missing ranks of Counterattack added to Warrior trainers.

- Missing Rank 1 and 2 of Seal of Wisdom added to Paladin trainers.

- Missing Dual Wield training added for Warrior trainers.

- Crusader Strike can now critically hit.

- Blessed Strikes now resets the cooldown of Holy Shock (Rank 4) as intended.

- Using Chastise in PvP mode no longer enables PvP on a target with PvP disabled.

- Holy Strike now initiates auto-attacks as expected.

- Exploit with Paladin talent Righteous Strikes resolved.

- New Insignia no longer causes players to drop the Warsong Gulch flag.

- Blessed Strikes no longer crashes the server.

- Enchanted Gemstone Oil now appears in the crafting window once the recipe is learned.

- Blood Craze now correctly procs from self-inflicted damage of Bloodthirst and Bloodrage.

- Improved Bloodrage no longer increases self-inflicted damage from Bloodrage.

- Seals now trigger correctly from extra melee attacks.

- Holy Shock (Rank 4) now benefits from the Illumination talent.

- Holy Might buff from Crusading Strikes now triggers even if Holy Strike fails to hit the target.

- Earthshaker Slam now benefits from spell hit chance instead of physical hit chance.

- Blood Fury now increases ranged attack power as well.

- Enlighten will no longer trigger multiple times from a single spell cast.

- Shamans and Paladins will learn One-handed Axes on the next login. This will be visible in the interface after the next client update.

- Certain Fire spells no longer incorrectly trigger the Hot Streak effect.

- Bloodthirst now only damages the user for 10% of the damage dealt to the enemy, unaffected by modifiers like armor.

- Fixed chance on hit proc with Drake Talon Cleaver.

- Holy Strike now benefits from auras that increase Holy damage taken, like Judgement of the Crusader.

- The Mysterious Stranger no longer speaks in binary.

- The new Insignia now also removes Blind effects.

- Resolved issue causing Looking for Group Tool (LFT) to crash the server.

- Resolved issue causing Lightning Strike to crash the server.

- Limited the mana gain proc of Proclaim Champion to visibility range.

- T2 Holy Paladin 8-piece set bonus now only triggers on critical healing effects, as intended.

- Increased the reward count for the "Tea with Sugar" quest to 10.

- Poisons at max stack now properly take Envenom into account.

- Seal of Wisdom Rank 1 now requires level 18, and Rank 2 requires level 28.

- The fight against the Dissipating Spectre in the Swift Travel Form questline has been reworked and is now less challenging.

- Warrior Trainers now offer Dual Wield at level 10.

- Goblin Land Mine is no longer usable in Druid Feral forms.

- Arcane Focus now correctly reduces resistance chance on Arcane Rupture.

- Casting Spirit Link while moving is no longer possible.

- Endless Quiver now correctly triggers from Auto Shot.

- Whirling Whizz-Bot, Mudpaw Brute, and Mudpaw Mystic are no longer incorrectly marked as boars.

- Piercing Shots can now stack with Serpent Sting.

- Pumpkin and Biscuit are now bound on pickup.

- Echoed Thunder no longer removes all stacks of Lightning Shield.

- Handbook of Lethal Poisons can now be used even if you know one of the spells it teaches.

- Slice and Dice can now be cast even if targeting someone other than the target on which you have combo points.

- Scarlet Sentries now respawn in 5 minutes.

- Netherwind Cord, Trousers of the Oracle, and Ring of Flowing Leylines are now bound on pickup.

- Enlighten will stop functioning if you reset your talents and do not relearn the talent.

- Searing Light can now proc from Chastise.

- Enlighten no longer triggers multiple times from a single spell cast.

- Chastise no longer causes pushbacks on spell casts.

- Swift Recovery now affects all Priest healing spells as intended.

- Elune's Lantern has a cooldown again.

- Dark Harvest's periodic damage calculation issues resolved.

- Void Zones are no longer tiny.

- Arcanum of Rapidity now also boosts spell haste.

- Omen of Clarity proc chance adjusted.

- Increased health and damage for Warlock's Infernal.

- Improved Steady Shot no longer affects Aimed Shot.

- Sunder Armor can now trigger Windfury Totem.

- Mana costs for Stormstrike and Lightning Strike reduced.

- Water Shield’s effect from Lightning Strike now scales better with attack power.

- Training costs for Seal of Wisdom corrected.

- Mastery of Fist Weapons now properly increases Unarmed skill.

- Shaman Tier 1 Earthfury Set Bonus (3) no longer disappears after casting an instant spell.

- The bug causing some Warriors to generate an additional 10 rage on every single attack has been fixed.

- Scaling for lower ranks of Imp’s Firebolt adjusted.

- Seal of Righteousness scaling reduced to intended values.

- Chastise can now target friendly raid members only.

- Repent now triggers only when the target performs a melee attack.

- Water Shield’s Attack Power scaling with Lightning Shield reduced from 20 AP per mana to 10 AP per mana.

- Stormstrike’s mana cost reduced from 12% to 10% base mana (tooltip update pending).

- Lightning Strike’s mana cost reduced from 15% to 12% base mana (tooltip update pending).

- Fighting Ouro outside his room is no longer possible.

- New pet families now benefit from all relevant talents.

- Swift Travel Form no longer cancels upon entering water.

- Dazing no longer applies in Swift Travel Form if facing the attacker.

- Holy Shock cooldown issues resolved. It now has a chance to remove the cooldown for its next cast, rather than the current one.

- Despawn timer of NPC Cla'ckora changed to 30 minutes.

- Lady Sylvanas Windrunner gossip bubble added.

- Items Thunderbrew Specialty Mug and Hornfang Specialty Mug are now equippable by all classes.

- Missing new Jewelcrafting recipes have been added to drop tables.

- Dissipating Spectre: Spell rotation corrected.

- Quest The Harvest Golem Mystery: Short description fixed.

- Arcane Resistance Recipe quest can now be completed with NPC Pietro Paraveno.
