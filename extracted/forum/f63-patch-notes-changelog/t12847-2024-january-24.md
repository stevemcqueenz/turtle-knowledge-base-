# 2024 — January 24

## Post 85725 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-01-23T19:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85725#p85725 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Corrected an issue where certain totems summoned by mobs were not properly labeled and lacked area-of-effect immunity.

- Resolved the problem of Firebrand Invoker casting Fire Nova while out of combat.

- Damian will now inflict damage with a spell instead of instantly killing the highest threat player at low health.

- Fixed incorrect disenchant loot assignments for several item lvl 56 greens.

- Ornate Bloodstone Dagger is no longer capable of triggering multiple times from area-of-effect spells.

- Players controlled by creatures will no longer be flagged for PvP if the AI forces them to attack another player.

- Expert Training Dummy should now be immune to all crowd control effects to avoid relocation.

- The PvP flag should now properly activate upon landing in a contested zone. (PvP Realms)

- The slime in Naxxramas will now accurately apply a debuff again.

- Addressed an issue with Xalvic Blackclaw, preventing him from excessively casting his knockback when the target is immune.

- Fixed incorrect immunities from Blackwing Spellbinder, Anubisath Sentinel, and Plagued Ghoul.

- Fixed Sapphiron facing the wrong direction during his engage animation.

- Plaguebloom is now found growing within Stratholme. However, it's important to note that these herb nodes will not respawn, as is typical for instanced content.

- Stormstout PvE: Resolved a longstanding performance issue affecting the realm.

- Mysterious Anti-RMT botting tweaks have been put into effect.

- Outline: Cleaning Cloth has been re-added to Rufus Hardwick's vendor stock in Nesigwary's Camp.

- Creatures will no longer interrupt their current spell cast to Enrage when they reach the required health threshold, and will instead do so as soon as they are done casting.

- You can no longer hit the battleground spirit guides with traps and other AoE spells.

- You will no longer drown if you use your hearthstone underwater and don't move afterwards.

- Fixed an exploit that allowed players to reach 100% crit chance by swapping enchanted weapons.

- Improved Anubisath Defender's script.
