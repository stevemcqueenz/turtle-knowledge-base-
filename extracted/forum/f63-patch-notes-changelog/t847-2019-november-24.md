## Post 4490 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2019-11-24T05:48:00Z

**22.11.2019**

- Win-trading is forbidden.

- Item/XP vendor Dyah Owon is no longer available.

- Honor rate inside the Battle Ring has returned to normal values.

- Bloody Coins now can be traded only for temporary buffs.

- Warsong Gulch requires only 3 people  on each side to start.
 **23.11.2019**

- Honor gains in the open world has been decreased to 25%. This change is temporary. Arena is included.

- Your spell cooldowns now will reset if you join Battleground or resurrect on Battleground.

- Plainsrunning has been disabled on Battlegrounds.

- Character power restoration has been increased during the BG pre-start period for 30 seconds.

- Character power restoration has been increased upon resurrection on the Battleground for 10 seconds.
 **04.12.2019**
- Experimental feature ⁠— [Bounty Boards](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=859) has been implemented.
