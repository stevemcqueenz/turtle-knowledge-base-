# 2024 — December 22 [Build 7206]

## Post 113782 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-12-22T00:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113782#p113782 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2024-12-22_2.jpg](./download/file.php?id=94) <dd>Changelog_2024-12-22_2.jpg (901.7 KiB) Viewed 17569 times</dd>

This server maintenance is accompanied by a client update in the EU region, with SEA following in a few days. Join our Discord for support if you're experiencing troubles updating the game.

 **For the class changes, read here:** [Updates | December 20 2024](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460)

 **General**
- Normal creatures now have primary attributes and can be affected by buffs and debuffs that modify them.

- Added Nordanaar and Ravenshire point of interest icons to the world map.

- The Minimap Clock now displays Zone, Server, and Local times. *Currently not supported by Shagu Tweaks.*

- Improved the GM Ticket interface.

- The loot window will now clamp to the screen.

- Fixed an issue where some players experienced low frame rates in **Scarlet Monastery Armory** and **Library**.

- Several updates to the profession frame, including dynamic search results and better filter resetting.

- Restored buff and debuff type labels on auras.

- Tauren, Troll, and Undead males now have a special dance emote, /DanceSpecial.

- Changed summon sound effects for multiple mounts to better match their appearance.
 **Spells**
- Fixed multiple issues with pets' **Poison Spit**, including incorrect damage and power cost calculations.

- Fixed the tooltip on the **Searing Light** talent to correctly show the mana cost reduction for the next **Smite**.

- Added new visuals for **Warlock Power Overwhelming**, **Shaman Spirit Link**, **Paladin Reckoning**, **Shaman Electrify**, **Priest Enlighten**, and **Priest Chastise** spells.

- Added a new sound effect for **Eclipse** triggering.

- Updated visuals for **The Face of Death Shield**, **Misplaced Servo Arm**, and **Druid Eclipse**.

- Added a new **Druid Glyph of the Penguin**: While active, your Aquatic Form will appear as a Penguin.
 **Quests & NPCs**
- Fixed Kanati Greycloud and Kaya Flathoof not attacking mobs during their escort quests.

- You will no longer be flagged for PvP during the quest **A Rogue's Deal**.

- Quests **Preventing Poison** and **Curing the Sick** now use different quest items to avoid charge-related issues.

- **The Four Horsemen** now enrage after 20 minutes, as intended.

- Amani Eagles now have **Rank 1 Screech** when tamed.
 **Items & Crafting**
- **Shadow Oil** and **Frost Oil** no longer scales with spellpower.

- Fixed a regression where **Mithril Lockbox** contained Double Mail Vest.

- **Plans: Emerald Monarch's Glow** and **Plans: Blue Starfire** now display the "Already Learned" message when you know the recipe.

- Corrected reagent lists for crafting **Heavy Gritted Paper** and **Solid Gritted Paper**.

- **Sphere of the Endless Gulch**: Buffed effects and reduced activation requirements from 10 to 8 stacks.

- Updated icons for the **Mechanical Auctioneer** and **Field Repair Bot**.

- Alternative T1 and T2 now have unique, custom icons.
![icons.jpg](./download/file.php?id=96) <dd>icons.jpg (327.61 KiB) Viewed 17523 times</dd>

 **Launcher**
- Fixed cache issues occurring after a launcher update.

- Refined handling of unsupported install directories.

- Prevented forced borderless fullscreen when gamma settings are changed.

- Fixed an issue where window drag functionality was not working correctly for some users.

- Updated fetch client with improved error handling.
