# 2023 — July 19

## Post 53989 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-18T21:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53989#p53989 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Effects like Thorns will no longer trigger if the attack is absorbed or the target is immune.

- Bloodthirst now correctly uses weapon skill.

- Made an attempt to fix ghost queues in Blood Ring.

- Fixed a bug that allowed Gelkis and Magram to be killed without reputation loss beyond a certain reputation threshold.

- You can no longer trade soulbound raid items to players who were not inside the raid when the boss died.

- Made an attempt to fix Tabetha becoming uninteractible.

- Staff of Eldara is no longer indestructible.

- Fixed auto-cast of some pet abilities.

- Fixed a typo in the quest Twilight Marauders.

- Magister's Letter, Paladin's Letter, and Priest's Letter now have the correct corresponding text when read.

- Whitewhisker Geomancer and Whitewhisker Vermin can now drop Armor Scraps.

- Serpentbloom should now always be contained in its object.

- The Horde now can get the correct Rope to wrangle those pesky Gnomes in AV.

- You can now continue to turn in sparks after the bosses have been summoned in Sunnyglade, and it will affect their health accordingly.

- The bosses in Sunnyglade now properly transform into a black drake at 30% and their abilities change.

- Added appropriate abilities to all other NPCs in Sunnyglade.

- Killing soldier NPCs in Sunnyglade now rewards 10 honor.

- There is now an announcement when somebody turns in sparks in Sunnyglade.

- Fixed issue with Holy Strike when duration of Judgement wasn't updated correctly.

- Shadow Mend is now affected by Healing Focus, Inner Focus, Shadow Affinity, and Spiritual Healing.

- Fixed armor reduction effect of Enrage.

- Fixed Prototype Shredder X0-1 Schematic now always drops.

- Bramblethorn Boar, Plateau Fleshripper and Ravenous Darkhound are now tameable.

- Added battleground holiday events for Sunnyglade Valley and Blood Ring.
