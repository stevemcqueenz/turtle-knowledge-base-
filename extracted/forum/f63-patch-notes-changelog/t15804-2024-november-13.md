# 2024 — November 13

## Post 107724 by Reynolds (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-11-12T21:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107724#p107724 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2024-11-13.jpg](./download/file.php?id=79) <dd>Changelog_2024-11-13.jpg (368.83 KiB) Viewed 24487 times</dd>

- Fixed an issue where Libram of Divinity incorrectly boosted Flash of Light healing.

- Corrected the functionality of Ferra's Collar.

- Magistrix Ishalah is now designated as a portal trainer.

- The Scrambled Brain debuff can no longer be dispelled by invulnerability effects.

- Revised the mechanics of the Shadow Priest T3 4-Piece Set Bonus to prevent triggering a Global Cooldown (GCD).

- Resolved an issue where attacking with Lightning Shield would cause all charges to be expended if the attacker also had Lightning Shield active.

- Corrected Magistrix Ishalah's omission of the quest "In Search of Solar Knowledge."

- Added and fixed the Glyph Vendor in the High Elf starting area.

- Corrected dialogue and options for Ironpatch.

- Improved grammar in various quests.

- Groups where all members have been offline for over a week will now be automatically disbanded.

- Tier 3 rings can now be swapped correctly.

- Fixed enchanting issues with items: Cuffs of Evocation, Frostfire Signet, Choker of the Emerald Lord, Ley-Kissed Drape, and Felforged Nathrezan Veil.

- Addressed a glitch causing quests to reward experience instead of gold at level 60.

- Removed "Plans: Gorgeous Mountain Gemstone" and "Plans: Malachite Ring" from creature drop tables.

- Ensured Desecrate Tokens can now be properly disenchanted.

- Fixed Efflorescence to now function as intended.

- Avoidance will no longer occupy Demon spell slots.

- Corrected the spell power scaling of Wrath.

- Fixed an exploit allowing War Mode to be removed multiple times.

- Updated the duration mechanics of time-limited items to continue depleting even when players are offline, leading to their eventual disappearance if not used.

- Fixed the Sheath position of Serpent's Coil Staff

- Fixed an issue where Forest Boars would deal Fire damage

- Fixed an issue with Amplify/Dampen Magic spells when the Magic Attunement talent is active, which allowed them to be used on unflagged enemy players.
