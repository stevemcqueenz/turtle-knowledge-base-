# 2023 — November 8

## Post 76368 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-11-08T00:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76368#p76368 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed missing effects on Lion Horn of Stormwind.

- Fixed an issue where Sentinel Glensha was not offering quests to players.

- Corrected the pyre count for the Revantusk daily, which had been reverted.

- Removed disenchant IDs from equipment sold by the Desolace centaur tribes' reputation vendors.

- Temporary enchants (such as fishing lures, Rogue Poisons, Shaman weapon enchants, and totems, as well as blacksmith stones) should now work for Vagrant Mode characters.

- Implemented measures to prevent Corrupted Crystal from becoming unusable.

- Addressed an issue with some Rich Thorium Veins in Dire Maul that could not be mined.

- Updated the Wind Stone respawn time override in the core to match the database.

- Talisman of Ascendance will now only proc once per cast, instead of per target hit.

- Implemented various optimizations to improve core performance.

- Made fixes for Ivus and Lokholar in Alterac Valley.

- Reverted Nordanaar Tea to have a single charge but stack in groups of 5.

- Quest "Nordanaar Herbal Tea" will now reward 5 items.

- Corrected incorrect stats for some elites in Dustwallow Marsh and Swamp of Sorrows.

- On Tel'Abim, properly locked some quests from being taken based on phase.

- Fire Rune will now only scale with 5% of spell power and will only proc from damaging spells.

- Fixed the sheath of the Bland Dagger, The Dark Knife, and Pious Blade.

- Removed the buy and sell price of Aliattan Anderson's Journal.

- Added gossip options to receive the messages for quest "A Diplomatic Mission."

- Removed non-quest loot from Captured Sprite Darter.

- You can no longer click off your buffs to remove them while being Mind Controlled.

- Predatory Strikes now only affects the intended forms.

- Corrected the wrong level requirement being displayed on Razormane Orders.

- Removed extra spaces from the names of Spell Power and Healing Power enchantments. This change will take effect after a new client patch is released.

- Sticky Ooze-Tar will no longer drop for players who are not on the quest.

- Borrowed Time now increases cast time instead of reducing it. The spell text update is only available after a client patch.

- Fixed the armor of Crimson Whelp to vanilla values.

- Addressed bugs related to Moroes.

- Blade of Eternal Justice: Returned 40 Spellpower and updated DPS in accordance to the item level increase.

- Hookblade Cleaver: Replaced 3 Spirit with 9 Strength and 5 Agility.

- Added hard-earned titles for Inferno players.

- Fixed a typo in the description of the quest "They Call Themselves Illidari."

- The appearance of transmogrified items will no longer revert back to the original upon durability changes.

- Murkdeep should no longer become invisible when attacking players.

- Rewards for "The Seeker's Demise" have been adjusted. Players must now choose a reward instead of receiving both.

- Vault Armory Equipment will now spawn after killing Arctiras, not Damian.

- Deathstrike Tarantula should no longer randomly evade due to bugged terrain.

- Mobs in Emerald Sanctum will no longer evade when their target in unreachable, instead they will instantly summon players into melee range.

- Fixed an issue where Transmogrified item texts would turn garbled.

- Implemented part 2 of the new Brainwashing device - see here [viewtopic.php?t=11434](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=11434).

- Fixed an exploit where players would be able to attack and use abilities while mounted.
