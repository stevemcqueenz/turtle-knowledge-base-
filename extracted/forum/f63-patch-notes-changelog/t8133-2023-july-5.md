# 2023 — July 5

## Post 52323 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-05T00:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52323#p52323 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed incorrect chaining of Shimmer Stout quests.

- Chilton Wand now has a level 48 requirement and attack speed has been changed to 1.5.

- Cinder Bracers are now a reusable trinket like other guardian summoning items and no longer has an expiration timer.

- Reimplemented repeatable version of A Light in Dark Places quest for the Fire Festival event.

- Removed improper quest chaining from the Horde Blacksmithing quest "The Old Ways".

- Fixed Gelkis Rumbler not giving reputation to Magram for each kill.

- Added 5 reputation per kill for each kill of Maraudine centaurs to BOTH Gelkis and Magram. Happy Insane in the Membrane hunting!

- Slightly lowered the reputation rewards for the Maraudine Bracer turn-in quest to offset the new reputation on kill values.

- Removed EXP from the Gelkis and Magram repeatable quests.

- Corrected race requirements for level 5 Priest robe quests.

- Fashion Coin quests are now standard repeatable quests that no longer occupy the quest log.

- Fixed grammar issues in NPC Marty Moonshine gossips.

- There should no longer be a chance for Darkmoon Faire Fortune to contain nothing.

- Added missing gossip option to Fallen Hero of the Horde to restore quest item.

- Spectral Defender and Spectral Betrayer will now engage in combat with each other.

- Shard of the Fallen Star will now scale with spell power.

- Fixed Stoneskin Gargoyle not facing it's target in combat.

- It's no longer possible to pull Baron Rivendare outside of his room.

- Fixed the Darkmoon Faire cannon.

- An attempt has been made to fix the issue with players not being resurrected in Alterac Valley, but the cause of the issue is still unknown.

- Rogues can no longer dodge or parry attacks by Raid bosses if they are attempting to solo it.

- Decisive Strike (rank 1), Decisive Strike (rank 2), Water Shield, Stormstrike (rank 2), Feral Spirit (rank 2), Earth Shield, Calm Elements, Bloodlust (rank 2), Mana Funnel (rank 1) and Mana Funnel (rank 2) is no longer available from the starter zone trainers

- Venomous Jungle Serpents are now skinnable

- Holy Fire and Holy Nova will now have the correct component scaling as stated on our class changes page

- The item Ragehammer should now Increase damage done by 20 instead of Attack Power

- The Raging Oceans Blue is now correctly marked as a elite quest

- Mobs in AQ20 have had their reputation gain corrected

- Emperor Vek'nilash and Emperor Vek'lor in aq40 have had their reputation gain corrected as well as their beetle friends

- Gizzard Gum and Cerebral Cortex Compound no longer have the 5 item limit

- Blasted Lands consumables now stack to 20

- Zandalari Tribe Zanza consumables now stack to 20

- Quests Priest's Letter, Magister's Letter, and Paladin Letter now give the correct letters

- Corrected the drop rate for Shadowforge Torch

- Tradesman Laz no longer assumes your gender during the quest Supplies to the Port!

- Fixed typo in the Tinkermaster Oversparks Response letter

- Grammar has been fixed for Willy's Letter

- Name fixed for the item Finkle's Accelerator

- Bobbing Apple, Plump Country Pumpkin,

- and Winter Veil Cookie now give the correct stats when well fed

- The quest The Scales of War no longer requires friendly reputation with Kul Tiras.

- Lowered restock cooldown for Fizzy Energy Drink.

- Grammar fixed in various custom quests. (Thank you text-edit contributors!)

"Rogues can no longer dodge or parry attacks from Raid bosses when attempting to solo them."

This change appears to be biased.

Even if a player was able to farm 3000g per hour, they likely acquired specific gear and enlisted the help of others to farm it. If three people are soloing AQ40 Trash and earning 3000g per hour, it amounts to 1000g each. It's possible that they sell Idols or Greater Scarab Coffer Keys, but the market will eventually become saturated, making it difficult for them to maintain that income.

I recall when people farmed mana-draining trash as a 5-man group, utilizing non-mana classes and a healer who regenerated mana outside the instance. I believe this strategy is still viable for you, but it seems unfair that rogues with unique specifications or difficulty finding a group are affected.

It's frustrating that I can't queue as a tank, and the threat caused by agitating poison is too low.

The ability to farm solo or with 1-2 companions was a primary reason for me to play as a roguetank in the first place.
