# 2025 — March 32

## Post 124813 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-04-01T02:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124813#p124813 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![tex2t.gif](./download/file.php?id=165) <dd>tex2t.gif (37.07 KiB) Viewed 8367 times</dd>

- Removed the on-hit chance effect of [Thunderfury, Blessed Blade of the Windseeker] to encourage diverse itemization choices.

- Warrior main tanks will no longer suffer from bleed effects.

- Removed Herobrine.

- Druids will now lose all their fur if they take Fire damage.

- Implemented Attack on Pandaria attunement questline.

- Reverted all CC2 changes to Arcane Mage to encourage more specialization diversity.

- Removed all Stamina from Warrior Tier 3, as their passive Spirit regeneration keeps them healthy enough without it.

- Removed the Hunter class weapon skill for Bow and Gun weapon types to encourage higher Aspect of the Wolf utilization.

- Fixed a bug which caused certain NA raiders to be unable to reliably use Invisibility Potion

- Hunters can now tame Druids who are shapeshifted into either Cat or Bear form.

- Fixed the bugged implementation of Druid's Hibernate, which was only lasting 6 months, rather than the intended 8 per the previous **March 32nd** changelog.

- Fixed queues for the Battleground **Sunnyglade Valley** which were seemingly bugged and had not popped for the past year.

- Improved client download speeds by removing the unneeded **WoW.exe** file.

- Fixed the issue where players reportedly found a Cow level. This does not exist.

- Increased server performance by deleting a random VM named **Tel'Abim** — we have no clue what this was.

- Soul Shards now take up 6 bag spaces given the emotional weight of carrying such an item.

- Added the ability to whisper yourself. Taking this action too many times will result in the title of "the Insane."

- Theme music from the movie **Jaws** now plays when a Rogue is invisible near a player of the enemy faction.

- Cooking skill can now be lost if you burn the food.

- Being ganked by 60s while leveling now grants the **Welcome to Vanilla** achievement (achievements pending Turtle WoW 2.0 release).

- Herbalists can now be attacked by angry Dryads if they damage too many flowers in a row.

- The Deeprun Tram now features ads for imaginary games like **Warcraft IV**.

- NPCs now have a 5% chance to ask you for a quest. Declining lowers your rep with them, eventually earning the title **The Disappointing**.

- Shaman totems can now be stacked into stylish totem poles. Purely cosmetic, extremely unstable.

- Resurrection now brings players back slightly taller. It’s cumulative.

- Players who spend more than 6 hours in the Molten Core will now be invited to **Lucifron's LinkedIn** network.

- **Ostarius** has been renamed to **Logoutstarius** to properly reflect what typically occurs priar to his spawning.

- Upgraded client to Warlords of Draenor functionality, except we only ported the Garrison portion.

- Added a second Dark Portal somewhere after a developer mistakenly placed it. We know there are two of them now. We cannot find the second one.

- Thousand Needles now includes slightly more needles.

- Resolved issue where players in **Maraudon** forgot why they were even there.

- Removed invisible walls. If you fall off the world, you deserved it.

- The Great Lift now has a 50% chance to just not be there. Good luck.

- Night Elves now flip 20% more often.

- All users who use the R.O.I.D.S. buff are automatically diqualified from the strongman minigame at the Darkmoon Faire

- Removed a game-breaking .wmo named karazhan40

- Warriors who cast Shout spells too many times in a quick succession gives players the Sore Throat debuff, making them unable to cast them any further.
