# 2024 — June 26

## Post 97844 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-06-25T21:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97844#p97844 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_june26.jpg)

- Fixed the issue where certain quest items could not be looted when master loot was enabled.

- The Chilled slow effect of Improved Blizzard no longer overrides other longer-lasting slows when applied.

- Added missing waypoints to some elite mobs in Silithus.

- Hateful Strike now has a finite range, and Patchwerk can properly summon targets from a distance.

- Fixed all Attack Power reduction spells to correctly reduce NPC Attack Power.
 - *Developer Note:* This bug had been on our radar for some time but we were unable to reproduce it in our test environments, which significantly delayed the resolution. The root of the issue was that on specific OS and compiler versions, the code responsible for reducing Attack Power behaved differently, resulting in inconsistent behavior and making it difficult to track and fix the problem.

- Removed a deprecated safeguard that would kick in when a Rogue attempted to solo a world boss.

- Both positive and negative Attack Power modifiers will now be displayed correctly when hovering over your Attack Power on the interface.

- Added the missing spell visual when Spirit of Redemption is activated, as well as the death animation once it expires.

- Void Zones should no longer move and attack players.

I did some testing of the AP debuffs and unfortunately they aren't fixed, only reverted to the state before the Demo Shout disaster happened.

If the target has 250 AP, and you apply CoR + 5/5 Improved Demo Shout, the target should end up having 250+90-204=136 AP, however:
 - if you first apply Curse of Recklessness then Demo Shout, the AP increase from curse will be completely ignored and the target will have 250-204=46 AP
 - if you first apply Demo Shout then Curse of Recklessness, the AP decrease from shout will be completely ignored and the target will have 250+90=340 AP

Also Screech doesn't stack with Demo Shout, it's exactly as it was before.
