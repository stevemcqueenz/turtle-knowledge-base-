## Post 27955 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-10-26T09:03:00Z

- Fixed some racial skill bonuses not applying.

- Fixed Retaliation not working.

- Urom Doomhowl will no longer despawn once summoned.

- Fixed Overlord Wyrmthalak not awlays dropping items.

- Fixed Gate Constructs not evading after player dies.

- Reduced size of training dummies.

- Fixed mobs summoned in some Ashenvale scripts never despawning.

- Fixed Sun's Embrace rank 2.

- Combustion will no longer proc from AoE if no target is hit by it.

- Added a command to clear your saved game settings on server: .account cleardata

- Corrected base player stats for multiple levels.

- Fixed some procs that should have had a chance to resist never resisting.

- Fixed typos in Report on the Defias Brotherhood.

- Removed Pattern: Volcanic Leggings from Gor'dosh ogres.

- It's no longer possible to get quest credit multiple times from Screecher Spirits if you click it multiple times quickly.

- Removed some wrong spawns in Bael Modan.

- Pain Spike will now benefit from talents related to shadow spells.

- Warlord Krom'zar and the Counterattack quest are now marked as Elite.

- Fixed Mind Blast threat being lower than intended.

- Fixed multiple grammar issues reported in our bug-tracker.

- Fixed multiple NPC/Quest issues reported in our bug-tracker.

- Reduced XP gains from neutral mobs in AV.

> **Torta wrote: Wed Oct 26, 2022 9:03 am**
> - Reduced XP gains from neutral mobs in AV.

AV being faster to level in than normal Outdoor content was kinda busted and needed a nerf.
But I find it a bit sad that the XP rates were completely gutted (from 700 XP base to 100 XP base per elite mob) , because for a single day, AV mines were turned into a mini-dungeon and was thriving with low levels who essentially grouped up to clear the hard-hitting elite mobs.
And you weren't just a useless burden for the people playing to win, because you'd get tons of Armor Scraps to hand in for the map-wide buff and to upgrade your backbone NPCs for defense (who already hit pretty hard).

But because XP rate was basically completely nuked, we are back to mines being completely empty and non-existant again, which is kind of sad considering restored-AV added engagement for all players, but this is an instant step back.

Instead of giving mobs insanely high XP values, maybe add **reasonable** XP to the repeatable quests, so low level characters have an incentive to do those otherwise useless quest (seriously, most of them just give you 1 AV rep as reward, because the main "benefit" is the BG effect such as spawning elite units, which never happens, because no one does the quest so it's a vicious cycle).
This would also indirectly balance out the flaw of shared mob XP where a guy solo-clearing gets a ton of XP, but the moment other people show up (including 60s), the XP gets shared and you barely receive anything anymore.
By tying the XP to a quest instead of mobs directly, you're heavily promoting the AV features and also giving people a very good incentive to do those quests which benefits the entire team.
