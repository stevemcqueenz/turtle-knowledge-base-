## Post 159994 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-11-18T20:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159994#p159994 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-11-19-ENG.jpg)

- Fixed an incorrect cooldown category for **Windwall Totem (Rank 2)**.

- Fixed attack‑power scaling for **Judgement of Command (Rank 3)**.

- Fixed inaccurate damage stats on **Rufus' Trusty Tankard**.

- **Idol of Evergrowth** received a new effect: **Demoralizing Roar** now deals Nature damage equal to your active **Thorns** effect to all nearby enemies.

- Fixed **Champion Razjal the Quick** not always dropping loot.

- Added further information to the **Goblin Brainwashing Device**, stating that spending all talent points when setting a specialization is required for it to save.

- Rented mounts are now faction‑specific and can no longer be used in combat.

- Implemented adjustments to address resilience stacking issues.

- Fixed an issue related to the item bonus of **Greatblade of Quel'Danil**.

- Fixed an issue where **DoT effects** would deal 1 damage in PvP under very niche circumstances.
