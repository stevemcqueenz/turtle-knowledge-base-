# 2024 — June 5

## Post 96352 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-06-04T23:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96352#p96352 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_june5.jpg)

- Removed Mageweave Cloth from the loot table of NPCs Gibbering Ghoul and Scourge Warder.

- Item Earthclad Bracers now has a vendor price.

- Freed the NPC Moonstalker Matriarch who was stuck in a tree in Darkshore.

- Item Recipe: Elixir of Minor Agility can now be stolen from relevant NPCs.

- Item Nightsaber Fang no longer drops from NPC Crimson Lynx.

- Added a new loot table for NPC Leprous Worker.

- NPCs Wavecrest Siren, Wavecrest Tidefinder, Spitefin Wavecreaper, and Spitefin Tidehunter now drop money.

- Forlorn summit scourgestones are now dropping correctly.

- Removed the low-level loot table from NPC Rage Talon Dragon Guard.

- Unique items specific to Scarlet Monastery have been removed from the Crescent Grove loot table.

- Added loot tables for NPCs Foulhide Shaman, Foulhide Overseer, and Foulhide Miner.

- Reworked fall damage handling to address issues of players randomly dying from minor falls.

- Sailor 'Deepmug' no longer sells Volatile Rum due to its use in reputation turn-ins.

- ‘Ale Saint’ Grida now accepts various rums in exchange for Bloodsail Buccaneers reputation.

- Introduced a new frog NPC to commemorate a level 59 Hardcore player's unique demise at the hands (or tongue) of a level 1 frog in Ashenvale.

- Race Change feature is back in the shop, currently available only on PvE realms on the EU and SEA network.
