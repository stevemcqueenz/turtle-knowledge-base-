# 2025 — September 24

## Post 152865 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-09-23T19:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152865#p152865 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-09-24_ENG.jpg)

- Fixed an issue where dead players would not receive experience at the end of a battleground.

- **Mana Attuned** now reduces mana cost of some spells and abilities while inside battlegrounds.

- **Cloak of Draconic Madness** can now be enchanted.

- Corrected sheath position for **Scepter of Nazjatar**.

- **Operative Cape** can now be enchanted.

- Removed **Charged Servo Arm** from Lord Pythas loot.

- **Elixir of Demonslaying** now also increases ranged attack power.

- Fixed hiring rented mounts if you have less than 100 copper in the bags.

- Fixed respawn rate of **Grave Moss** in **Northwind** to be 30 minutes.

- **The Redbrand Lie** quest will no longer grant all three rewards at once.

- **Steel Belt Buckle** can now be applied on other person's belts via trade.

- Fixed issues related to rounding of damage and healing values.

- **Bindings of the Forebearer**: Increased Intellect from 2 to 5, Reduced Spirit from 5 to 2.

- **Kindling Signet**: Replaced 7 Spirit with 7 Stamina.

- **Gnoll Hide Cuffs**: Removed Nature Resistance, Added 4 Strength.

- **Sash of Flamebinding**: Replaced 4 MP5 with 17 Fire Spellpower.

- **Stonelink Leggings**: Removed 2 Defense, Increased Stamina from 5 to 9, Added 30 Additional Armor.

- **Chainmail of Writhing Miasma**: Replaced 1% Physical Crit with 1% Spell Crit.

- **Stolgaz Shoulderpads**: Removed 1% Physical Hit.

- **Trashtalon**: Fixed damage values to match its item level, Reduced Agility from 12 to 7.

- **Magistrate's Brim Hat**: Reduced MP5 from 7 to 5.

- **Defiler's/Highlander's Leather Boots (Level 60)**: Fixed double 20 Attack Power and added back increased running speed.

- **Footwraps of the Oracle**: Replaced 3 MP5 with 1% Spell Hit.

- Changed the name of Tier 1 Priest Shadow pants to **Trousers of Prophecy** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed the name of Tier 2 Shadow Gloves to **Gloves of Transcendence** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed the names of Tier 2.5 Discipline Helmet, Chest and Boots to avoid duplicate names.

- **Rod of the Churning Hourglass**: Reduced Haste from 3% to 2%, Fixed sheathing.

- **Stormrage Handwraps**: Reduced Spellpower from 30 to 23.

- Respawn time for quest objects ('On the Powers of Blood', **Spirit of Silverpine Charts, Waterlogged Letter, Eternal Flame**) has been reduced.

- **Dragonclaw Gauntlets**: Reduced Fire Resistance from 15 to 10, Added 10 Agility.

- **Bow of Quel'Danil**: Reduced DPS from 21.7 to 18.8, Fixed an issue with sell and buy prices.

- **Dagger of Quel'Danil**: Reduced DPS from 29.2 to 23.7, Reduced Spellpower from 7 to 5, Reduced Intellect from 6 to 5, Fixed an issue with sell and buy prices.

- **Greatblade of Quel'Danil**: Reduced DPS from 35.6 to 31.2, Fixed an issue with sell and buy prices.

- **Siege Bomber**: Fixed an issue with sell and buy prices.

- **The Dark Knife**: Reduced DPS from 22.9 to 18.2, Fixed issues with sell and buy prices and disenchant output.

- **Pennyworth Cane**: Increased Intellect from 3 to 4, Fixed issues with sell and buy prices and disenchant output.

- **Stunning Crossbow**: Reduced DPS from 16.6 to 14.3, Fixed issues with sell and buy prices and disenchant output.

- **Shard of Beth'Amara**: Added item level.

- Changed name of Tier 3 Warrior DPS Shoulders to **Dreadnaught Spaulders** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 3 Warrior DPS Boots to **Dreadnaught Boots** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 3 Warrior DPS Ring to **Signet of the Dreadnaught** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 3 Shadow Priest Ring to **Signet of Faith** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 3 Shadow Priest Boots to **Slippers of Faith** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 2 Affliction Warlock Boots to **Nemesis Boots** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 2 Warrior DPS Boots to **Boots of Wrath** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 2 Warrior DPS Shoulders to **Spaulders of Wrath** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 1 Warrior DPS Bracers to **Bindings of Might** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 1 Warrior DPS Shoulders to **Spaulders of Might** to avoid duplicate names.

- Changed name of Tier 1 Warrior DPS Boots to **Boots of Might** to avoid duplicate names.

- **Bloodmoon, Sickle of the Murderous Flight**: Increased PPM from 1 to 1.7.

- **Sash of the Grand Betrayal**: Reduced Attack Power from 52 to 46, Increased Physical Crit from 1% to 2%.

- **Ring of the Cryptstalker**: Replaced 6 MP5 with 1% Physical Hit.

- **Raka'shishi, Spear of the Adrift Hunt**: Attack speed reduced from 3.5 to 3.7, DPS remains the same.

- **Bishop's Reverence**: Reduced Stamina from 25 to 18, Added 15 Spirit.

- **Giga-Charged Arcane Reflector**: Now properly has Arcane Resistance instead of Nature Resistance and now has a different icon to differentiate it from the Nature Reflector.

- **Ancient Threshadon** should no longer leave the Fetid Lake inside Stormwrought Ruins.

- The quest **Heart of Darkness** now requires Level 32 to accept.

- Talen no longer drops crafting materials.

- Fixed skinning loot for **Firemane Scalebane and Firemane Flamecaller**.

- **Mudrock Spikeshell’s Barbs** now deal the correct damage.

- **Tranquilizing Poison** can now be removed by effects that dispel movement speed reduction.

- **Shredded Muscle** can now be removed by effects that dispel movement speed reduction.

- Fixed an issue where **Blind** was still not considered a physical ability in some cases.

- Fixed boat model for **Icepoint Rock** route.

- Group Leader Assignments will now be randomized when using LFT.

---

- **In loving memory of someone who cherished the game deeply and always helped others enjoy it to the fullest, a High Elf named Nat has been added to a fishing spot near Crystal Lake. His kindness inspired so many, and those memories will continue to cast a gentle line of connection within us all.**

For base stats it's easy. Int provides a certain amount of crit, and spirit is just a flat 0.25 per point. For hit/crit you just equip no items other than a single item the gives 1% of whatever stat being tested. These values also float based on gear, so I've done pretty extensive testing in Naxx, particularly on Patchwerk. This does however mean that my stat weights assume ~naxx level gear. Haste is a bit more complicated as it inversely scales with crit. (every smite crit you get effectively GCD-locks you, making haste only affect holy fire. This is why self-cast Chastise is a dps loss, as is Grace of the Sunwell if not pre-cast.). Because of this I used it's old value of 12 (same as hit) and halved it because it's only useful on every other smite and a holy fire every 5th global. It could be slightly more or less, but half the value of hit seems to be a decent rule of thumb. Mp5 is in a similar situation where it's not zero value, but also very hard to quantify. I've listed it as equal to 1 spellpower. This is probably a little high, however the amount of it on gear are so small that even halving this number doesn't change the position most, if any, gear sits on the list.

All of these weights assume you are running the current Smite meta spec. (31/20/0) I've seen some other people try out leaning more into the Holy tree and not taking Chastise, as it's only use-case is to cast it when forced to move and you don't otherwise have an instant smite.

The numbers here are *mostly* tested, although not extremely thoroughly. I haven't sat at a target dummy for 10,000 casts per stat because frankly it seems silly to be that autistic about a spec that maybe 6 people play at the highest level.
