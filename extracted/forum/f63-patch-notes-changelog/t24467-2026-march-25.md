# 2026 — March 25

## Post 172139 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-03-24T12:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2026-03-25-ENG.jpg)

- **Earthen Bulwark** will build up from melee abilities as well.

- Fixed damage calculations for stackable spells (e.g., **Scorpid Poison**, **Deadly Poison**, etc.).

- **Danonzo's stove** in the **Tel Co. Basecamp** on **Tel'Abim** is now functional again.

- Fixed **Holy Strike** not triggering **Mending Light** in all cases.

- Fixed missing bonus healing scaling for **Mending Light**.

- Fixed trigger conditions for **Flurry (Shaman)**.

- Fixed respawn timer of **Idol of Neptulon** object.

- Fixed **Elune Infusion** not triggering.

- Fixed targeting of **Lightwell's Splendor of Light** being too precise.

- Fixed **Lightning Strike** ranks being missing from trainers.

- Updated the names of **Woodcutting Trees** for better immersion.

- Fixed an issue where players who left their guild remained in guild chat.

- Added missing armor to **Vest of the Beastkeeper**.

- Summoned **World Boss** corpses will now despawn after 30 minutes.

- Attempted a fix for broken fishing catches in **Moonhoof Village**.

- **Totem of Thundercall** and **Libram of Ardour** will no longer drop for the wrong faction.

- **The Wrath of Malgan** now rewards **Thunder Bluff** reputation as intended.

- Fixed broken quest chaining for the **Rumors of the Deathtotem** quest chain.

- Readded **Furbolg Medicine Pouch** and **Furbolg Medicine Totem** to the vendor **Meilosh**.

- Fixed some **Survival** quests not properly rewarding items.

- **The Hallowed Note** quest for **Tauren Priests** can no longer be accepted by other classes.

- Changed the model of **Demonbane**.

- Fixed **Star of Maras'ethil** always saying you're out of range when attempting to shoot.

- Fixed issue with block not working as intended.

- Fixed issue with **Zealous Defence** not working as intended.

- Fixed survival skills not being categorized properly in trainer UI.

- You can now cast **Abolish Poison** and **Abolish Disease** on demon pets.

- **Cartographer** no longer requires areas inside instances.

- **Tideblade Mistress** will no longer cast **Frost Nova** while stunned.

- Changed health of **Onyxian Hatcher**, **Vaelastrasz the Corrupt** and **Ezzel Darkbrewer**.

- Players who have completed the quests **The Great Iqa'quluk**, **Don't tell the Others** and **Restocking for Vacation** and haven't gotten their rewards are now able to complete these quests again.

- Added missing gossip text for **Ranshalla**.

- Significantly reduced health of **Onyxian Hatcher** (Hatchers can sometimes be too tight of a DPS check).

- Increased health of **Vaelastrasz the Corrupt**.

- Increased health of **Ezzel Darkbrewer**.

- **Thorn Gorge**: Spirit healers should only spawn when the specific faction captured the point.

- Fixed binding type of **Handwraps of Faith**.

- **Lower Karazhan Halls** and **Hateforge Quarry** maps will now open correctly.

- Fixed tiny points of interest on minimap.

- Fixed not being able to open **Transmogrify UI** sometimes.

- Fixed **Survival** missing from auction house filters.

- Fixed errors when inspecting talents.

- Fixed missing **Facetted Crystal Scope** enchantment text on item.

- **Thorn Gorge**: made the spirit healers visible for the correct faction.

- Players will still get half the old honor system flush RP from standings for this week as compensation for the mid-week patch release to the new System. This is the last and final week. The RP Decay changes have already been shifted to the new System. Please take this into account when your Rank after the flush is different from what you expect.

- **Thorn Gorge**: added speed, berserker & heal power-ups.

- Added a 3 second cooldown to **Gunfire Blast**.

- Reduced Min Level for **Thorn Gorge** to 31.

This maintenance will be followed by another **launcher update** to support fixes that require client‑side edits.
