## Post 169350 by Shadowsong (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-02-25T00:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169350#p169350 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/IMG_20260224_150418_745.jpg)

- **Bodley** can now restore **Lord Valthalak's Amulet** or pieces of it if players lost them.

- Resolved some bosses not dropping loot in cross faction groups.

- The sleep from **Green Whelp Armor** will no longer work on targets above level 50.

- Fixed an exploit with **Blink**.

- **Spitelash Naga** will now use their abilities in combat.

- Fixed an issue causing **Glowing Arctic Grayling** to not be catchable.

- **Tree of Life** aura healing bonus will now periodically update based on the caster's spirit instead of snapshotting forever.

- **Shard of Nightmare** will correctly sleep targets below Level 61 again.

- Corrected the targeting of **Chain Heal**to no longer target Guardians or Totems.
