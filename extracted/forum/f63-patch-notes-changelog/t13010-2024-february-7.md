# 2024 — February 7

## Post 87117 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-02-06T22:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87117#p87117 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/1132334947865210880/1204579108101689344/Changelog_07-02-2024_4.png?ex=65d53eb7&is=65c2c9b7&hm=ffada12215a47bf8d088b95f8c0d717070014688dbfcb82471c23b87653d0ae5&=&format=webp&quality=lossless)
- Introduced Guard Didier and scripted his quest.

- The quest "Whispers of Autumn" now offers a choice between two rewards.

- Unearthed a Copper vein in Durotar (60.1, 61.8).

- The quest item for "The Seeker's Demise" is now accessible to the entire group.

- Updated greeting text for non-hunters for NPC "Twinkie Boomstick."

- Enabled looting for Solid Chests in Tanaris (93.8, 66.2) and Hillsbrad Foothills (75.0, 41.0), as well as Alliance Chest in Silverpine Forest (63.3, 58.2).

- Revised greeting texts for NPCs: Commander Anarileth, Magistrate Solomon, Tanalla Sagebranch, Senior Foreman Nazz Firecracker, Nelson the Nice, Brother Neals, Kelvar Greybrew, 'Slip', Talom Stridecloud, Orin Stonefury.

- Corrected typographical errors and capitalization inconsistencies for NPCs Solnius and Erennius.

- Implemented a blizzlike gossip menu for NPC "Ralo'shan."

- Added new quest "Testaments Of True Love."

- Adjusted Thorium Vein spawns, with a 90% chance for Small Thorium Vein and 10% for Rich Thorium Vein.

- Spores now despawn when Loatheb evades, removing their buff from players.

- Players outside Maexxna's room at the start of the encounter will be teleported inside.

- Prevention of spawning Winter Veil trees inside instances to avoid exploits.

- Installed invisible walls around Kel'Thuzad's throne.

- Added an invisible wall in WSG to deter climbing on top of a tree.

- Introduced invisible walls in Gothik's room to prevent players from climbing on bugged pathing spots.

- The .guildname command to rename a guild is no longer restricted to Latin characters on SEA realms.

- Increased movement speed for Erennius, Solnius, Brood Queen Araxxna, Lord Blackwald II, Clawlord Howlfang, Grizikil, and Moroes.

- Designated Erennius as a boss, eliminating slowdown on low health.

- Fixed Thurman Mullby's gossip.

- Reduced respawn times of Rhok'delar quest bosses to 15 minutes.

- Resolved an issue with auras modifying max health percentage, preventing an unliving state where players have 0 health but are technically alive and unable to release spirit.
