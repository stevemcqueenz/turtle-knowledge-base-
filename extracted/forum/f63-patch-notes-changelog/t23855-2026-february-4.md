# 2026 — February 4

## Post 167358 by Shadowsong (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-02-03T23:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167358#p167358 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/5B94A0B8-DD33-47A8-A385-F5EA2E9884DE.jpg)

- **Pets** will now always move at their run speed when commanded to take a position.

- You can now see **Battleground announcement messages** like "X has picked up the flag" concerning players that you've ignored.

- **Grand Inquisitor Isillien** should no longer fall through the tower and get stuck after he spawns.

- The **Enhance t3 6pc** bonus **Flame Shock** damage increase proc now affects the DoT component of **Flame Shock** too.

- **The Comb**and **The Master's Cellar** are no longer required for the **Cartographer** title.

- **Scarlet Friars** will now heal less often.

- Increased the chance for **Gahz'ranka** to drop **Middle Piece of an Ancient Idol** by 10%.
