# 2024 — January 31

## Post 86423 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-01-30T22:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86423#p86423 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_jan31.png)

- A group of NPCs, consisting of 2 Obsidian Nullifiers and 1 Anubisath Warder in Ahn'Qiraj, can now be pulled simultaneously.

- The "Anthion's Parting Words" quest for Horde mages is no longer available for the Alliance.

- The item "Schematic: Unstable Mining Dynamite" now drops from the loot tables.

- Fishing is now possible in the "Oily Blackmouth School" fishing pool in shallow waters north of Stranglethorn Vale.

- The Black Riding Boots item is now suitable for repair.

- Items "Lodestone," "Skullrattler," and "Blackflame Wand" are now displayed in the right hand.

- Corrected double parry and applied the proper stats listed in the 1.17.0 changelog for Kargron's Leggings.

- Crystal Sword of the Blossom is now properly displayed when sheathed.

- Gyrofreeze Ice Reflector should no longer be listed as Trade Goods in the auction house.

- Viewing Room Key now goes into the keyring.

- Fixed issues related to the Meditation stat not stacking with similar mana regeneration in combat talents.

- Players who die in Stormwind Vault will be sent to the graveyards in Elwynn Forest.

- Added invisible belt transmog items to the Fashion Apprentice NPCs.

- M-0L1Y's respawn timer has been adjusted to be consistent with other rares and now properly drops items unique to her.

- Any players outside Kel'Thuzad's room when the encounter starts will now be teleported inside.

- You can no longer start the Gothik encounter if all players are stacked on the same side.

- Heigan and The Four Horsemen will now evade if pulled out of their rooms.

- Fixed grammar on Vambraces of Kin'Tozo, Ebonmere Vambraces, and Bloodfist Vambraces.

- Fixed the wrong weapon proc on Bloody Pick.

- Fixed numerous text and grammar typos in quests, items, spells, NPC names, and more, thanks to community contributors.

- Corrected the wrong Immolate spell used by Lord Alexei Barov.

- Added missing alternative spawn positions for Bijou's Belongings.

- Your two-handed weapon will now be automatically unequipped when you right-click to equip a shield in your inventory.

- The missile visual of Consecrated Caduceus is now a holy bolt instead of a shadow bolt to reflect the fact that it deals holy damage.

- Fixed a floating Thalassian Daisy.

- Channeled spells now properly display their animation after being recasted.

- Fixed an issue with auras that modify max health percentage sometimes leading to an unliving state where you have 0 health but are technically alive and can't release spirit.

- [Changed the functionality of consumables in dungeon and raid environments.](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=12931)
