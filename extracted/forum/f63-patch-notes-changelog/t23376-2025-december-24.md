# 2025 — December 24

## Post 163576 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-12-23T21:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163576#p163576 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-12-24-ENG-02.jpg)

- **Astral Insight** will no longer deal damage to you when proc effects such as **Lightning Shield** trigger.

- Fixed **Gnomish Alarm‑O‑Bot**.

- Server time will now be consistent for all players (except on Kalimdor where it is offset by 12 hours) and won't change when you relog.

- **Undercity Guards** will no longer attack the **Summoned Voidwalker** for the quest **The Binding**.

- It is no longer possible to **Blink** through closed doors.

- **Father Lycan** will now evade if moved farther than 100 yards from his spawn, and his adds will aggro players in combat with him regardless of distance.

- **Supervisor Laggle** will no longer drop **Gelwex's head**.

- Fixed every 10th item not being shown in **Feid Rota**'s list of swappable items.

- Ranged mobs trying to move away from their target will no longer pass through walls or travel absurd distances.

- **Warlock pets** will no longer end up with incorrect spells.

- **Battle Standards** no longer trigger a cooldown on **Warlock Healthstones** when used.

- Restored the **Hillsbrad Tailor** to his intended location.

- **Magmadar** should no longer damage himself with his own **Conflagration**.

- **Young Pridewings** now fly higher above the ground instead of clipping the terrain.

- You can no longer buff **Engineering Target Dummies**.

- Added sell values to **Glowing Arctic Grayling** and **Barnacle Covered Key**.

- Movement of flying creatures with waypoints now looks much smoother.

- Increased hitbox of **Rupturan** and **Mephistroth**.

- Alterac Valley supply officers now sell items again.

This is the last changelog we'll publish in 2025. Thank you all for following our progress throughout the year, and we wish you and your families a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year! May the coming years be filled with warm and positive moments for everyone.

We've added a new vendor in **Winter Veil Vale** who sells the warm wishes you submitted in [this thread](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=23146), grab one in‑game and give it to your favorite person online!
