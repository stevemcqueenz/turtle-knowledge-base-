# 2025 — June 4

## Post 134147 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-06-03T21:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134147#p134147 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-06-04_ENG.jpg)

- There is now a 20-second timer before **Free-For-All** is disabled after leaving the arena, to prevent safespotting on arena boundaries.

- Fixed an issue where **Ruthlessness** and **Seal Fate** granted more combo points than intended.

- Enemy team factions should no longer be revealed to players. For example, if you are an **Alliance** player, clicking an enemy NPC in **Alterac Valley** will no longer cause the **Frostwolf Clan** to appear in your Reputation tab.

- Fixed an issue where **Endless Quiver** dealt less damage than intended.

- Fixed an issue where **Surprise Attack** could not trigger **Seal Fate**.

- Fixed an issue where **Call of Flame** did not increase the damage of **Flametongue Weapon** and **Flametongue Totem**.

- Fixed an issue where the mana cost reduction from **Robes of Insight** was not consumed correctly in some cases.

- **Hunting Spear**: Replaced 17 Stamina with 17 Agility.

- Mephistroth's **Shackles of the Legion** no longer triggers from jumping or falling.

- Fixed an issue where changing into **Moonkin Form** from another form did not grant **Moonkin Aura**.

- Added missing **Arcane** damage immunities to creatures in the **Tower of Karazhan**.

- Added missing **Bleed** immunities to various creatures.

- **Blast Powder Kegs** should no longer be interactable unless on the related quest.

- **Agitating Poison** will no longer scale with spell power.

- Fixed an issue where the poison bonus from **Band of Ancient Lethality** did not stack with the **Rogue** talent **Improved Poisons**.
