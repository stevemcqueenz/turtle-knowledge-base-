# 2023 — May 31

## Post 48093 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-05-30T17:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48093#p48093 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed the Saltjaw Basilisk in Gilligim's Isle dropping items from the basilisk in Blasted Lands.

- Fixed random movement not working for some swimming creatures.

- Fixed many creature spawns that were stuck below the ground.

- Marked the quest Breaking the Felstar as Elite.

- Elemental Devastation can now proc from Flametongue Weapon.

- The Preparation cooldown will now be reset upon entering the Blood Ring.

- Resurrection spells can no longer be cast in the Blood Ring.

- Messages sent in the Local Defense and World Defense channels can no longer be understood by the opposite faction.

- The Atiesh aura can now stack with the Moonkin aura.

- Grammar fixes have been made for the quest Jasperlode Infestation.

- Corrected Moonstone to Lesser Moonstone for the quest Preparation for Divination.

- Grammar fixes have been made for the quest Matter of Trust.

- Fixed an objective typo in quest The Maul'ogg Crisis I.

- Replaced the quest They're In The Way! reward item with money.

- Set a 5-second respawn timer on Wazlon's Toolbox.

- Fixed the Ironforge Chain disenchant ID.
