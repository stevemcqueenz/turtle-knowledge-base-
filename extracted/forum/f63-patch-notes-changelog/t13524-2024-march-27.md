# 2024 — March 27

## Post 91478 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-03-26T18:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91478#p91478 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_march26.jpg)

- Corrected an issue where Major Pakkon accidentally aggroed Colonel Zerran's mobs in AQ20, causing both waves to merge.

- Resolved a bug preventing Goggeroc from aggroing after spawning.

- Players will now automatically be dismounted upon exiting a battleground.

- Seal of the Dawn, Rune of the Dawn, and Mark of the Champion will now display the same visual indicator aura when equipped, consistent with the Argent Dawn Commission.

- Introduced a verification check for cooldown lengths upon login to address issues of players facing incorrect cooldowns due to partially written data after a server crash.

- Removed experience point rewards from repeatable quests involving Armor Scraps, Stormpike Soldier's Blood, and Storm Crystals in Alterac Valley.

- Adjusted the drop count for the item "AZURE WHELPLING" to 1.

- Set drop chances for items "Recipe: Greater Frost Protection Potion", "Recipe: Greater Nature Protection Potion", "Recipe: Greater Shadow Protection Potion", and "Recipe: Greater Arcane Protection Potion" to blizzlike standards.

- Removed the "Cottontail Rabbit" pet from innkeepers' sales lists.

- Lord Falconcrest's Syndicate quest chain is now fully accessible. NPC "Otto" will no longer attack players who have a reputation with the Syndicate.

- Relocated a blocked "Mithril Deposit" in the Western Plaguelands (35.80) to a nearby location.

- Fixed the quest "An Audience With the Prince" to ensure the quest giver is always available for interaction.

- Added missing traps to Warpwood Pods in Dire Maul.

- Fixed an issue with the Haste 1 spell effect granting 2% spell and ranged haste instead of 1%.

- Implemented checks in the 4 Horsemen encounter script to prevent pulling them out of the room or aggroing only a single horseman.

- Hunter pets will now use Dash/Dive more intelligently.

- Succubus pets will now use Seduction more intelligently.

- Adjusted respawn timers for Young Goretusks to prevent them from respawning too quickly.

- Addressed grammatical errors in The Key to Karazhan VII quest.

- Corrected the subclass for the Ring of the Deep Sea, allowing it to be disenchanted.

- Fixed the sheath position for the Sickle of Unyielding Strength.

- Corrected the skin index for the Night Elf: Necromancer skin token.

- Rogues can now be seen easily by their fellow raid members.
