# 2025 — February 19

## Post 120347 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-02-18T20:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120347#p120347 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2025-02-19_6.jpg](./download/file.php?id=124) <dd>Changelog_2025-02-19_6.jpg (385.68 KiB) Viewed 15199 times</dd>

- Fixed an issue preventing players from training with rogue trainer **Loriel** unless all quests from the NPC were completed.

- **Verog the Dervish** should no longer be summoned multiple times at once.

- Damage transferred from **Hand of Sacrifice** is now reduced by **Blessing of Sanctuary**.

- Fixed an issue preventing players from deleting **Jordan's Refined Ore Shipment**.

- The **Celebrate Good Times** buff is now removed upon entering raids.

- Fixed the gossip option for **"Where is Elder Snowcrown?"**.

- Fixed mobs inside **Dustwind Cave** being unable to move.

- Fixed an issue preventing guild homes from being set at **Joalar Solar**.

- Players who die inside the **Gurubashi Arena** will now be revived at the nearby spirit healer.

- **Shellcoin** items are now **Bind on Pickup** to prevent RMT activities.

- Fixed **Traveler Tents** appearing in the wrong tab.

- Fixed **Love Tokens** not being consumed when talking to civilians during **Love is in the Air**.

- **Icicles** can now trigger **Master of Elements**, restoring 2/4/6% of Icicles' base mana cost per missile, up to a total of 10/20/30%.

- Reduced **Ouro**'s **Sand Blast** radius to 40 yards.

- Blackstone Island adjustments:
 - Added 4 new weapon quest rewards to **War on Mudpaw** and **War on Venture Co.** to bridge the gap between upgrades, adding two to each.

 - Reduced **Whirling Whizz-Bot** and **Haywire Battlechicken** health, damage, and armor to match creatures of their level.

 - Changed **Whirling Whizz-Bot** level from 1-2 to 1.

 - Changed **Ashfeather Scavenger** level from 2-3 to 3.

 - Reduced **DV-500**'s armor from 192 to 147.

 - Changed **Stonetail Lasher** level from 5-7 to 6-7.

 - Changed **Muckfin Tidecaller** level from 6-8 to 6-7 and reduced stats accordingly, substantially reducing its armor to match the values of other nearby murlocs.

 - Slightly reduced **Stoneshell Scrabbler**'s damage to account for its **Pierce Armor** ability.

 - Reduced **Mudpaw Brute**'s **Rend** ability damage.

 - Reduced **Mudpaw Mystic**'s **Lightning Bolt** spell damage.

 - Reduced how often **Venture Co. Thug** uses its **Sunder Armor** ability.
