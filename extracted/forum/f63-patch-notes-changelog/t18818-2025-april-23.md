# 2025 — April 23

## Post 128053 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-04-22T23:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128053#p128053 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-04-23.jpg)

- Magus **Palon** will no longer chase players forever.

- **Dwarven Tinder** is no longer Unique, so players are not prevented from doing the **Nek'rosh's Gambit** quest if they misplace the quest item.

- Fixed mobs continuing to spawn again during the **Thandol Span** quest if player does not leave the area quickly enough after the ambush.

- The 5 instances per hour limit is now separate for **dungeons** and **raids**.

- Added some readable books to the **Tower of Karazhan**.

- Fixed an issue where **Natural Weapons** did not provide the correct increase to spell hit chance.

- **Swordsmith Specialization** will now be lost upon dropping the **Blacksmithing** profession.

- There is now a raid warning message when **Mephistroth** begins to cast Shackles of the Legion.

- Fixed mount model of **Plagued Riding Spider**.

- Fixed an issue where **Improved Drains** did not function as intended with **Drain Life** and **Drain Mana**.

- **Mephistroth's Shackles of the Legion** now grant affected players immunity to knockback effects. Additionally, **Shackle Shatter** can no longer critically hit.

- **Lightning Strike** will now trigger **Elemental Shell** for **Water** and **Earth Shield** as well.

- Fixed not being able to feed pets.

- Fixed line of sight checks all over the place failing because the width of units was reinterpreted as their height.

- **Whiskers, the Wizard's Cat** now resides in the **Dustwallow Marsh** together with his owner.

- Removed entrance level requirement from the **Ragefire Chasm** in **Orgrimmar**.

- Pyroguard Emberseer now plays his RP Event properly instead of directly attacking upon summoner kills.

- **Companion pets** are no longer valid targets for buffs or healing effects.

- Players will no longer get invited to **Blood Ring** while they are AFK.

- Fixed not being able to collect radioactive fallout from some mobs for quest **The Only Cure is More Green**.

- **Affinities** in the **Incantagos** fight will now despawn upon death.

- Removed the **Bow Specialization** racial from **Goblins**, as they are not intended to have it.

- Fixed an issue where **Kruul's** attack range would unintentionally shrink when he became enraged.

- Fixed an issue where **Brand of Karazhan** could not be enchanted.

- Added a few flavorful additions to **Ragefire Chasm** loot tables, some of the drops available only for **Level One Lunatic** parties, running dungeon without any help.

- Plenty of performance fixes.

- **Rupturan's** adds will now attempt to avoid targeting the same player with their abilities at once.

- Fixed an issue where **Priests** would sometimes retain incorrect racial traits after a race change.

- Fixed a bug where **Sanv Tas'Dal** adds were left up after the fight.

- Fixed a bug where a player would sometimes remain charmed after leaving combat in **Karazhan's Chess** encounter.

- Modified the damage done by **Ignite Rock** in the **Rupturan** fight.

- Implemented realm first raid clear announcements for South American realms.
 **Tower of Karazhan Item Balance Changes:**

- **Medivh's Hindsight:** Added 9 Stamina.

- **Royal Seal of Greymane:** Increased Stamina from -15 to 15.

- **Bishop's Reverence:** Added 25 Stamina, removed 32 Spirit.

- **Gloves of Leyline Convergence:** Added 16 Stamina, Removed 19 Spirit.

- **Girdle of the Faded Primals:** Added 15 Stamina, Removed 15 Spirit.

- **Desecration:** Added 2% Haste.

- **Wristwraps of Exiled Radiance:** Increased Intellect from 10 to 19, Reduced Stamina from 13 to 12, Removed 11 Spirit, Increased Healing Power from 53 to 57.

- **Worldbreaker Girdle:** Added 17 Stamina.

- **Thunderfall, Stormhammer of the Chief Thane:** Stamina penalty reduced from -13 to -8.

- **Censer of Soulwarding:** Removed 15 Spirit, Added 12 Stamina, Added 12 Intellect.

- **Turalyon's Hope:** Removed 22 Intellect, Added 22 Stamina.

- **Cloak of Rapid Regeneration:** Removed 9 Spirit, Added 9 Stamina.

- **Khadgar's Guidance:** Increased Stamina from -15 to 15.

- **Memory of the Last Guardian:** Increased Intellect from 7 to 10, Removed 13 Spirit, Added 10 Stamina.

- **Netherwrought Bracers:** Added 13 Stamina.
