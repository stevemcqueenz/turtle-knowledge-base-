 **Itemization**

With the last server restart we've touched upon some of our new dungeons, here's a list of what we've changed:

Karazhan Crypt creatures and some Stormwind Vault creatures has been nerfed by 5-10% in health values to provide a better dungeon delving experience. We've also touched on most item drops found in the Karazhan Crypt to make these drops more enticing and more relevant to the general dungeon diver, you can see the comprehensive list of item changes below.

In addition Commander Andreon finally got promoted to a proper boss position with new abilities, health and damage values and now features 6 new item drops! (These items will arrive with the next server restart.)

We hope that this will make these instances more enticing without being too troublesome to clear with rewards worth hunting!

- Splintercage Breastplate - Increased Stamina from 10 to 12, and added 10 Attack Power

- Bonewall - Increased Stamina from 12 to 14

- Boneshatter Maul - Increased Strength from 32 to 35

- Clutch of Hivaxxis - Added 7 Agility and 1% Haste

- Amice of the Webweaver - Added 10 Stamina

- Darksoul Band - Increased Spirit from 9 to 12

- Soul of the Spider - Added 25 Nature Resistance and the item is now Unique

- Venom's Touch - Increased Agility from 8 to 12 and added 2 Strength

- Consumer Shackles - Added 5 Defense

- Rotworm Grips - Added 1% Hit

- Wormheart - Added 7 HP5

- Tome of Shivering Secrets - Increased Frost Spell Damage from 20 to 22 and Increased Intellect from 10 to 12

- Shroud of Haunted Torment - Added 2% Haste and Increased Spell Damage from 14 to 16

- Miasma Walkers - Increased Agility from 20 to 24

- Marrowclaw Knuckles - Increased Agility from 13 to 14 and Increased Strength from 6 to 7

- Cadaverlink Vest - Increased Agility from 19 to 29 and Increased Intellect from 10 to 13

- Baneforged Leggings - Increased Strength from 23 to 26, Increased Agility from 10 to 16 and Increased Spirit from 7 to 10

- Shroud of the Archlich - Added 10 Spell Penetration

- Corpsekeeper's Charge - Added 12 Shadow Spell Damage

- Cowl of Mivax - Increased Intellect from 9 to 15, Increased Spirit from 5 to 24 and added 5% Mana Regen in Combat

- Windbreaker - Increased Agility from 5 to 12

- Deathrune Leggings - Increased Shadow and Frost Resistance from 5 to 10

If you fill a dungeon with gear for the "meme" specs, you just create a dungeon where groups of only these meme specs will form. Any dungeon should have a little something for every class inside.

Many of the classic dungeons already fall into this trap and provide loot that is not desirable for all classes - and much less all speccs. UBRS is famously undesirable for Hunters while most casters only get scrap from strath UD.

There is no rule against adding hybrid loot to old dungeons and making the loot in custom dungeons also appeal to mainstream classes.

But in Crypts case i noticed a distinct lack of decent casterloot. Stats, Castspeed & Manaregeneration are nice, but will not outclass raw spelldamage in most cases. Currently even with the changes, i think only the cloak and the extremely rare shadow shoulders are worth it for casters.
