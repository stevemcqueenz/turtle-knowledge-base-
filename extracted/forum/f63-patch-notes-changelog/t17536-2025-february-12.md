![Changelog_2025-01-28.jpg](./download/file.php?id=118) <dd>Changelog_2025-01-28.jpg (476.98 KiB) Viewed 24458 times</dd>

- It's now possible to set your guild house at Calandrath in Silithus.

- Hunter's Pet **Avoidance** is no longer unintentionally affected by talents such as Unleashed Fury.

- **Mongoose Bite** will no longer reset the auto-attack timer.

- Over the past months we've had multiple revisions of Class Changes 2, each update bringing every class and spec closer to our intended design & performance. As good as this is for the classes and specs that weren't seeing a lot of play we've also noticed that some level brackets of NPCs don't quite match the power compared to the updated classes.

Therefore,  NPCs in the mid-to-endgame (level 10 ~57) will be receiving a minor buff to damage and health values, these will range from 4-7% depending on their current values compared to the standard.

- We will roll out a new **Battleground Queue System**to combat ghost queuing and team imbalance. This rollout will be done on Tel'Abim first and will go live on Nordanaar soon after.

- New mount: **Plagued Riding Spider** has a very low drop chance from Kel'Thuzad.
