# 2023 — April 11

## Post 41180 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-04-11T21:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41180#p41180 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Moved Shell Co. office in Stormwind to a different house.

- **Added an additional opt-out for War Mode at level 60.**

- Fixed possible crash on player disconnect.

- The chicken quest CLUCK! is now repeatable.

- Adjusted spawn times for Soul Weavers in the Kel'Thuzad encounter.

- Added a limit to how small players can become.

- Improved Weapon Totems talent will now apply to party members as well.

- Eye of Kilrog can no longer attack.

- You will no longer be teleported straight to Isillien when he turns hostile if you are reachable.

- You will no longer be able to interact with the Dirt-stained Map in Mulgore if you've done the quest.

- Removed high-level drops from Dun Morogh wolves.

- Corrected a resapwn time of the wreckage near Steamwheedle Port.

- Quest In Dreams: Now spawning guards into the tower instead, to prevent players from going there. Pathing is broken there, and the quest will break if player hides inside the tower. Isillien must be able to aggro the player for next part of event to be triggered.

- Fixes quest drop of the item Head of Speaker Gan'to.

- Various grammar and creature fixes from QA and players.

- Added cloth drops to Kul'Tiras Expedition NPCs.

- **It is no longer possible to build Traveler's Tents too close to each other. However, the rested bonus given by a single tent has been increased to 5x. Please do not waste all your tents at once. We hope that this change will encourage more immersive usage of this feature, and we look forward to seeing happy camping parties all over the world, not just in several popular locations.**
