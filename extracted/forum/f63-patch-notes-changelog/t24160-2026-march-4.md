# 2026 — March 4

## Post 169965 by Shadowsong (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-03-03T20:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169965#p169965 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2026-03-04.jpg)

- **Anomalus** and **Sanv Tas'dal** can no-longer be fought outside their room.

- Items that summon guardian pets such as **Remains of Overwhelming Power**can no longer be activated in arenas.

- **Love Fool** dummies can no longer be buffed just like other target dummies.

- **Sha'ni Proudtusk**will now stay for longer after spawning.

- **Bulrug** and **Temma of the Wells** are now the same size as other tauren.

- The buff from the trinket **Molten Emberstone** can now stack with the orc racial **Blood Fury**.

- **Ride of the Wild**: you can no longer mount a player who is ignoring you.

- The elusive **Glowing Arctic Grayling** should now be catchable again (three Gnomes were sacrified to make this happen, please make it count...)

- Fixed an issue causing **Glowing Arctic Grayling** and **Barnacle Covered Key** to not be sellable to vendors.

- **Agitating Poison** now correctly uses charges as intended instead of lasting indefinitely.

- Fixed a bug where weapons were not copying sheath position of a new appearance when transmogrified. This change is not retroactive.
