## Post 124967 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-04-01T19:55:00Z

![Changelog_2025-04-02_3.jpg](./download/file.php?id=167) <dd>Changelog_2025-04-02_3.jpg (359.61 KiB) Viewed 15138 times</dd>

- Fixed incorrect values for **Shaman's Bloodlust**.

- Fixed an issue where the **Throwing Weapon Specialization** talent did not allow **Deadly Poison** to apply **Agitating** or **Instant Poisons** from the off-hand weapon.

- **Glyph of the Forest Stag** will no longer change the look of **Swift Travel Form**.

- Fixed an issue where **Curse of Agony** triggered by **Malediction** would incorrectly consume mana.

- Fixed issues with **Improved Seal of Righteousness**.

- Fixed issues with **Judgement of Righteousness**.

- Fixed issues with threat when both **Righteous Fury** and **Vengeance** were active.

- It won't be possible to start **War Mode** challenge on **PvP realms**.

- Removed **Pet Rock Star** title from players.

- Fixed the **Enlighten** buff only increasing healing on the Priest and the target.
