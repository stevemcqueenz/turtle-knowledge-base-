 **RAID RELEASE: TOWER OF KARAZHAN**

This raid will feature the new Tier Set 3.5 and challenge 40 players to gameplay even more difficult than Naxxramas, allowing the truly determined to prove their strength against daunting foes.

 **Class Changes**

This patch comes with the next round of class balancing. Please visit this thread to learn more about the changes:
- [Updates | April 1 2025](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504)

 **General**

- The client will now launch in **Windowed Widescreen** mode with improved default video settings on first launch.

- Added an additional mission to the **Level One Lunatic** challenge: complete 100 quests to earn a special title.

- You can now fish while seated.

- Updated the **Eastern Kingdoms** and **Kalimdor** loading screens to improve Gnome representation and add Goblins and High Elves.

![Gh_2mOgWMAEmJ8y.png](./download/file.php?id=132) <dd>Gh_2mOgWMAEmJ8y.png (480.14 KiB) Viewed 55116 times</dd>

- Updated original dungeon loading screens that have gotten an extension to feature the **Mysteries of Azeroth** logo.

- It's now possible to transmogrify tabards and shirts.

- Ported over camera skip glitch fix from **Vanilla Tweaks**: [https://github.com/brndd/vanilla-tweaks ... in.rs#L252](https://github.com/brndd/vanilla-tweaks/blob/master/src/main.rs#L252)

- **Turtle WoW** will now show up in your Discord Activity list.

- Fixed issue with set bonuses not showing correctly on transmogrified items.

- Select categories now have subcategories for easier filtering of shop items.

- Items available during holidays are now shown first in the respective categories and will have a special badge.

- General performance improvements - the store should feel smoother now (especially the fashion category).

- Updated the main game menu (Esc) layout.

- Added a new button to the main game menu dedicated to the donation store.

- **Leveling Challenges**are moving to the character creation screen! You will no longer be able to get a glyph at the glyph master mid-leveling, however, you can still toggle **War Mode** at your local battlemaster.

 **Lapidis and Gilijim Isles Soft Rework**

- Adjusted the damage, health, and armor values of most creatures in **Lapidis** and **Gilijim's Isles** (more than 65 creatures!), putting them more in line with other creatures of the same levels and fixing issues of several creatures having too little armor, too much armor, too much damage, and caster creatures not having the appropriate stats (less armor and health than normal melee creatures).

- Adjusted the levels of a few creatures in **Lapidis** and **Gilijim's Isles**.

- Rebalanced all quest rewards and changed **Insomni's** questline rewards to Rare quality, refer to the Itemization changelog for details, shown above.

- **Destroying the Deeptide (Horde)**now has the same item rewards as **Drowning Deeptide (Alliance)**.

- **Glasseye**, **Ruins Creepers**, **Seer Jang'zo**, and **Tanglemoss** now drop normal loot alongside their quest drops.

- **Seer Jang'zo** no longer unintentionally drops **Lard's Lunch**.

- **Seer Jang'zo** and **Speaker Ujuwa** now have a 100% chance to drop **Tainted Mojo**.

- **Chief Imaz'ul**, **Seer Jang'zo**, and **Speaker Ujuwa** now have slightly more health and damage.

- **Tanglemoss**, **Chief Imaz'ul**, **Seer Jang'zo**, and **Speaker Ujuwa** now have proper abilities.

- **Ruins Creeper's** **Creeper Root** drop chance increased from 30% to 40%.

- **Ruins Creeper** now can cast **Entangling Roots**.

- **Bengal Tigers** and **Junglepaw Shadow Panthers** now can cast **Rend**.

- **Gor'dosh Ogres** no longer cast **Torch** and instead can cast **Knockdown**.

- **Gor'dosh Butchers** now can cast **Cleave**.

- **Gor'dosh Brutes** now can cast **Strike**.

- **Forgotten Crew** now can cast **Drowning Death**.

- **Drowned Souls** can now cast **Frost Nova** and are now smarter, walking away from enemies they've just frozen.

- **Forgotten Swordsman** now can cast **Strike**.

- **Southsea Pillagers** now constantly cast **Fireball** instead of going into melee and hitting their targets with their staves.

- **Arch Hydromancer Lapidis** is no longer classified as a Boss.

- Reduced the amount of speaking trolls in **Zul'Hazu**.
