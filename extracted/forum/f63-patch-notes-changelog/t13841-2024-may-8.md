# 2024 — May 8

## Post 94237 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-05-07T21:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94237#p94237 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_may8.png)

- Fixed the Ritual of Refreshment so it now works in battlegrounds.

- Adjusted the levels for the quests Money Down The Drain and Secure the Cargo.

- Corrected the issue with Improved Drain Soul not applying the Soul Siphon buff on the player if the target dies from the last damage tick of Drain Soul.

- Fixed an issue where the Drain Soul buff remained on the caster if the channel portion of the spell was resisted.

- NPC Darrow Shademore is now a vendor.

- Extended the 10-minute grace period for trading Soulbound items in raids to include loot from trash, provided the items are of rare quality (blue) or higher.

- Fixed the Seal of Ascension quest to correctly interact with the Orb of Draconic Energy instead of autocompleting.

- Unstable Mining Dynamite can no longer critically hit.
