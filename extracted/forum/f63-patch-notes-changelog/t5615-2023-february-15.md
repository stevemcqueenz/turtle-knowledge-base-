# 2023 — February 15

## Post 34787 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-02-15T01:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34787#p34787 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed yet another crash in Ostarius script.

- Fixed cooldowns of Scarlet Archmage spells.

- Preventing mobs from getting stuck with PetAI if they die during charm.

- Grammar overhaul for various quests and NPCs.

- Changed Ring of Authority: now unique and armor is 100.

- All current and future Epic Bind on Pickup profession crafts now require the skill that's required to craft the item to equip. This has been done to avoid meta scenarios where the player could make these items which were intended to be the personal bonus for leveling up the profession and being a reason to not outsource all crafts, swapping profession completely defeats this purpose and further increases the gap between equipment-centric and consumable-centric profession power level due this completely nullifying the equipment power side of the former type of professions with this profession swap.

We plan on making these professions more on par and worthwhile to personally pick up with these personal crafts with upcoming content, with one such example being the **Alchemists' Stone**, coming with 1.16.6.
 **P.S**. Sorry for adding this change late into the changelog as its an important one, it wasn't our intention to obscure this information from the players, it was due to an oversight on our end.

- Set respawn timers to newly-spawned Leprous Workers in Blacksand Oil Fields.

- Fixed some issues with quest The Attack.

- Corrected money rewards for Silvermoon daily quests.

- Defined targets for Curative Animal Salve.

- Fixed Theradras' Shed Tear icon.

- Removed broken effect from Chillwind Armor.

- Corrected respawn timers for various quest objects and NPCs all over the world.

- Returning player to queue position if on flight path when entering bg.

- Changed Shellcoin Promoter texts to say instead of yell.

- Shellcoin Event page now got second weekly graph: <https://turtlecraft.gg/#/shellcoin>

- Prevent interacting with Durnan Furcutter while Scalding Mornbrew Delivery quest turn in event is running.

- Fixed and added more stats to Alchemists Stone.

- Added new reward for quest Gnoll Bounty to replace Gnoll Punisher.

- Disabled some War Effort related spawns.

- Added fishing loot on Tel'Abim.

- Ouro shouldn't retry submerge in 2 secs. Rather reset the counter back to 90s.

- Made `Corruption` Bind on Equip, there's currently no special occasion that mandates that it stays a BoP, its not even an epic.

- Added item level and disenchant ID to Gowlfang's Vest.

- Fixed quest requirement of Memory of An Ogre.

- Hedania (Strange Bedfollows) corpse will now despawn in 30 seconds.

- Corrected base player stats to match original data.

- Fixed not being able to attack some targets with enslaved demons.

- Restored missing School of Tastyfish spawns.

- Fixed broken graveyard for Crescent Grove.

- Fixed graveyard for Deadmines non-instanced area.

 **T2.5 changes: Spirit removed from all pieces of the Warlock, Hunter and Shaman sets, the following buffs were added to compensate for the following:**

Hunter:
- Striker's Footguards: Spellpower increased from 6 to 11

- Striker's Leggings: Spellpower increased from 9 to 19

- Striker's Diadem: Spellpower increased from 12 to 21

- Striker's Pauldrons: Spellpower increased from 6 to 9

- Striker's Hauberk: Spellpower increased from 9 to 20
Warlock:
- Doomcaller's Footwraps: Spellpower increased from 28 to 29

- Doomcaller's Trousers: Spellpower increased from 34 to 37

- Doomcaller's Circlet: Spellpower increased from 33 to 35

- Doomcaller's Mantle Spellpower increased from 28 to 29

- Doomcaller's Robes: Spell Critical Chance increased from %1 to %2
Shaman:
- Stormcaller's Footguards MP5 increased from 4 to 5

- Stormcaller's Leggings: MP5 increased from 4 to 7

- Stormcaller's Diadem:  Added %1 Spell Hit and Spellpower increased from 32 to 33

- Stormcaller's Pauldrons MP5 increased from 3 to 4

- Stormcaller's Hauberk: Added %1 Spell Hit
