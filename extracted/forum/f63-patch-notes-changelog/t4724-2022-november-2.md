# 2022 — November 2

## Post 28269 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-11-01T20:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28269#p28269 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Nerfed all guards back to their original stats.

- **Your settings will no longer be saved on the server. Back up your WTF folder if you don't want to lose them when switching clients.**

- Fixed quest-related spawns not working because previously spawned mobs haven't despawned.

- Fixed Elemental Focus not getting refreshed if you crit while its active.

- Renamed Gryphon Chew Toy to match the associated item.

- Quest Zalezane's Apprentice will now give Darkspear Trolls reputation.

- **Implemented daily Call to Arms rotation. Battleground holidays will change every day to promote playing all battlegrounds.**

- Fixed Elemental Invasion bosses despawning before they can be looted.

- Fixed voice lines in Crescent Grove not playing.

- Corrected stealth auras used by Fen Creeper and Bleakheart Shadowstalker.

- Corrected timers for spells used Deathknights in Naxxramas.

- Fixed bug when it was possible to summon multiple instances of Ostarius boss.

- Multiple grammar fixes for custom quests and creature texts.

- Fixed loot tables for Icepoint Whiteclaw.
