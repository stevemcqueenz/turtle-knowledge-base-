# 2024 — September 11

## Post 101054 by Reynolds (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-09-10T20:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101054#p101054 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog 2024-09-11 (1).jpg](./download/file.php?id=10) <dd>Changelog 2024-09-11 (1).jpg (394.18 KiB) Viewed 12049 times</dd>

- Fixed an issue where the quest reward for "War on Worgen" was displayed twice.

- You will now be automatically dismounted when speaking with a flight master.

- Fixed Crimson Hawkstriders not being skinnable.

- Fixed a bug where Alliance players who died in Swamp of Sorrows would respawn in Blasted Lands.

- Fixed an issue where Kel'Thuzad's Frost Blast could target non-player entities.

- Mavel Brightwood will no longer aggro players.

- Fixed an issue where the Clever Traps talent did not increase the duration of Freezing and Frost Trap effects as intended.

- Fixed an issue where the Deathknight Understudy failed to reset threat after regaining control.

- Reverted last week's "Blocking is no longer possible while in the unarmed sheath state" change due to unforeseen side effects.

- Updated last week's changes to LFT. Party leaders are now able to kick players again, just not while loot rolls are still active. Group loot (and no longer Need before Greed) is enforced.

- Reworked instance ownership rules to prevent exploits when resetting dungeons.

- You will no longer be automatically removed from the group upon logging out.

 *Developer's Note: The grouping and dungeon instance handling system has been made to work just like Classic now. This means that the old methods of resetting a dungeon you are currently inside, no longer work. Instead, the methods that worked on Classic now work here:*

Doing a 5man dungeon, and want to teleport everyone to the start to clear again? Player 2-5 can log off, and the player 1 group leader needs to kick these players the moment they go offline. When they log back in, they will find themselves outside the dungeon. Once this is done, player 1 invites the others back in the party, passes lead to a player who then re-enters the dungeon, and kicks player 1 when they re-log, who will now also be outside. All 5 players can then walk right outside, party leader hits "Reset all instances" on their right-click portrait, and enjoy the next instance.

Doing some solo dungeon farming, are deep inside, and want to reset your own instance to go again? Invite a 2nd player or your alt, and pass them the leader. Log out on your main character, and the 2nd character needs to hit "Reset all instances". The main character can log back in finding themselves outside, and enjoy the next instance.

Mid-week hotfix:
- Fixed a regression affecting the Priest spell Proclaim Champion.

- Fixed another exploit in the dungeon reset system.
