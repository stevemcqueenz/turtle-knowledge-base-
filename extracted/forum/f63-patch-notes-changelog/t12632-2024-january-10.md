# 2024 — January 10

## Post 84147 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-01-09T23:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84147#p84147 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- You can now use stealth while carrying the flag in WSG. However, casting it will cause the flag to drop.

- Addressed an issue with Earth Sapta in Shaman quests.

- Dolores and Daisy's races can now occur simultaneously. The gossip text now indicates which side requires a player for the race to start.

- Rescripted the Mograine and Whitemane fight in Scarlet Monastery Cathedral.

- Rectified summoning issues with Lord Valthalak.

- Lightning Shield will no longer remove the Corrupted Mind debuff.

- Fixed the cannon for the quest "Death From Below."

- Enslaving a demon with a pet will no longer render the pet unattackable.

- Adjusted waypoints for the Defias Traitor during the escort to be more accurate.

- The option to block trades in interface settings can now be disabled properly, without breaking the ability to trade until logout.

- Corrected gossip options for Ralo'shan the Eternal Watcher.

- Scare Beast is now appropriately marked as a single-target spell.

- The following creatures are now immune to disorient effects: Brood Queen Araxxna, Lord Blackwald II, Clawlord Howlfang, Grizikil, Moroes, Master Blacksmith Rolfen.
