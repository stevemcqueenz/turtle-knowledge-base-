# 2023 — December 13

## Post 80839 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-12-12T15:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80839#p80839 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Gaston has been reintegrated into the game.

- Destroyed items restored by Gaston will now be soulbound.

- You can no longer loot Favor of Erennius, Cache of the Firelord, and the Four Horsemen Chest if you weren't in the instance when the chest spawned.

- Players on temporary flying mounts will now be dismounted upon being teleported.

- Added missing Ironbark Protectors.

- Made changes or fixes to the following items: Veil of Nightmare, Quel'dorei Guardian's Warhammer, Revantusk Watcher's Maul, Quel'dorei Ranger's Spear, Revantusk Shadow Hunter's Striker, Mantle of the Fallen Prophet, Ebonmere Vambracers, Foul Effigy, Chromatic Gauntlets.

- Assigned skinning loot to worgen in Gilneas.

- Nerfed Thorn Valley spell power scaling to the ground.

- Dishonorable kills will now be given for killing players that are grey to you, not for killing civilian NPCs. Rejoice, war criminals!

- You can no longer use trinkets that summon guardian pets in Blood Ring.

- Mana will no longer be consumed for spell casts before the battleground has started.

- Health, mana, and soul shards are now restored to full upon leaving a battleground.

- Removed the duplicate spawn of Magus Tirth.

- Fixed the mana cost reduction amount of Improved Shadowform.

- Removed incorrect drop sources for Idol of Brutality.

- Fixed the Large Solid Chest in the Dreadmaul Rock cave.

- Fixed player hitbox when using Dream Panther skin.

- The pet summoned by Harvest Repear Kit will now always attack the same target as you.

- Added missing aggro texts to the pets summoned by Barov Peasant Caller.

- Removed on-kill reputation from Risen Lackeys.
For more information on the changes coming this week, please refer to the announcement thread here [viewtopic.php?f=2&t=12138](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=12138)
