# 2023 — October 18

## Post 71678 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-10-17T21:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71678#p71678 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Added 1% Spell Hit Chance to the Ring of Nature's Duality. The previous nerfs in the last changelog were excessive, so we have decided to restore some offensive power to the ring.

- Decreased Haste from 2% to 1% and increased Stamina from 10 to 13 for the Sanctum Bark Wraps.

- The Nemesis Gloves should now have the reworked stats as specified in the patch changelog.

- The Strength and Stamina values on the Thick Obsidian Breastplate have been swapped.

- The Magus Blade no longer deals Arcane damage on swings.

- Decreased Strength from 31 to 26 and increased Stamina from 4 to 13 for the Dreamsteel Boots.

- Changed 14 Agility to Stamina and 1% Vampirism to 1% Physical Hit Chance for the Dreamsteel Leggings.

- Decreased Intellect from 26 to 19, removed 5% Meditation, and added 19 Spellpower to the Dreamthread Mantle.

- Reduced strength from 33 to 30 and changed agility to stamina, increasing it from 12 to 15.

- Removed 1% haste and 12 attack power, and added 1% critical chance to Dreamsteel Mantle.

- Shoulders crafted from Dream materials (Dreamthread, Dreamsteel, Dreamhide) now require a profession skill of 300 to equip, as they are much stronger than other pieces in their sets.

- Fixed damage values on Axe of the Wildtusk.

- Band of Infinite Possibilities now provides a flat 18 spellpower instead of a range of 1-36 spellpower. This change was made because the spellpower value is randomized per equip, not per cast.

- Added a way to quit the Vagrant's Endeavor challenge if it becomes too unbearable.

- Temporarily removed the Cleaning Cloth from the vendor. The item will return in 1.17.1.

- Fixed the issue with Mystic Guay'Jin not offering the "Welcome to Amani'Alor" quest (for real this time!)

- Added proper quest chaining to the Way of the Spiritwolf quest chain for Orc Shamans and changed the quest category from Orgrimmar to Shaman.

- Updated quest text to mention the Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility for quests that still referred to Tinker Town NPCs being in Ironforge.

- Improved Wildhammer Clan reputation handling for Troll Tribal Necklaces.

- Corrected the item requirements for the "Plans: Ornate Bloodstone Dagger" quest from the Steamwheedle Blood Ring.

- Added missing experience and max level reward money for a Karazhan quest chain.

- Fixed the faction requirement of "The Black Waltz" quest.

- Made various fixes for the "Chocolate Fish" quest.

- Fixed the missing prerequisite for Azuregos's Magical Ledger.

- Fixed the Stormwind Vault entrance in Mirror Lake.

- Increased Erennius' damage by 25%.

- Modified spell "Call of Nightmare" in Emerald Sanctum to ignore Line of Sight (LoS).

- Adjusted Deep Wounds to only trigger from physical damage.

- Implemented dynamic and randomized respawn timers for ores and herbs in custom zones.

- Decreased cooldown of consumables in Blasted Lands.

- Fixed correct display name in /who for Stormwind Vault.

- Added creature group for Defias Dockworkers and implemented formation movement.

- In Lower Karazha Halls, spawn Lord Blackwald II if Dark Rider Champion is dead.

- Removed "cannot be destroyed" flag from Recipe: Alchemists' Stone.

- Fixed issue with inability to repair Wolfblood.

- Added a long cooldown to Shellcoin ads.

- Add a wandering behavior to Hollow Web spiders.

- Adjust the reputation rewards for Troll Necklace Bounty to ensure they are fair and balanced.

- Change the guild master designation on hardcore death from Leader to the oldest invited Officer.

- Correct the duration of the Idol of Evergrowth to no longer add 60 seconds to Tiger's Fury.

- Adjust the respawn time of the Dark Rider Apprentice to ensure it is appropriate.

- Change the damage type of the Magus Blade to better align with its intended purpose.

- Summon the Eye of Kilrogg at the caster's position, rather than a random location.

- Ensure the Ironhand Guardian only casts Gout of Flame when Magmus is engaged in combat.

- Fix the feral attack power bonus on the Grand Marshal's Glaive and High Warlord's Pig Sticker.

- Correct the Arcane Vulnerability hunter set bonus to function as intended.

- Resolve any issues with the Invocation quests and spells.

- Address any client lag caused by Vampirism spells.

- Remove the deprecated Scarlet Monastery quartermaster.

- Increased drop chance of Black Lotus.

- Increased drop chance of Arcane Crystal.

- AoE effects and their visuals that do not deal damage will now properly show on units.
Please delete your WDB folder!
