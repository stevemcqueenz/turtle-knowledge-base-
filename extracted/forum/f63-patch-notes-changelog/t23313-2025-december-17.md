# 2025 — December 17

## Post 162943 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-12-16T22:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162943#p162943 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-12-17-ENG.jpg)

- **Magus Ariden Dusktower** will no longer become permanently uninteractable.

- **Snowball** will no longer become enraged when hit with frost spells.

- Fixed an issue with the **Enhancing Totems bonus for Stoneskin Totem**. Damage reduction is now a flat 15/30 bonus, and the provided block amount is 15/30%. The next client update will reflect this change in the talent tooltip.

- Fixed an issue where **Shadowbane Darkcaster** could cast Darkbolt while moving.

- Fixed an issue where **next-melee spells and abilities** were unqueued if the player had insufficient power, even when cost reduction buffs like **Clearcasting (Omen of Clarity)** made the ability free. Abilities with their cost reduced to zero by such effects will no longer be unqueued.

- Fixed an issue with **Paladin's Two-Handed Weapon Specialization talent** where swapping weapon types, such as switching from an axe to a mace, would result in losing the bonus weapon skill.

- Fixed an issue where **Force Reactive Disk** would not reapply after being revived from death.

- **Gnomish Battle Chicken and similar pets** no longer aggro players that heal them.

- Fixed the **Elemental Strength buff from Totem of the Stonebreaker** not stacking with the Orc racial **Blood Fury**.

 **The Lava Incident**

Last week an incident occured on Ambershire where lots of players, normal and Hardcore players alike, were suddenly teleported to certain locations on the map. The most lethal of them being the lava in BRD.

After investigating we found out that by using certain tools some players were able to trick the server by sending a normal movement "packet" but changing the target player to someone else.

By cleverly disguising this packet the server wrongfully accepted this request and thus it teleported players who weren't meant to be teleported.

We've since fixed the problem and because of incredibly exceptional circumstances we've rolled back player deaths that occurred due to this.
