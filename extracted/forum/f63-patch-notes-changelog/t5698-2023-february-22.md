 **Dragons of Nightmare Encounters:**

- All dragons' health has been reduced from 1.2 Million to 832,750 health. This was done due to the latter health value being the right value according to Classic.

- Mark of Nature now last 6 minutes down from 15 minutes.

- General loot item level increased to 75, Dragon specific loot and quest reward ring's item level increased to 76, same as the three drakes in BWL.
