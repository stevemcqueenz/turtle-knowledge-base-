# 2024 — July 24

## Post 100214 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-07-23T22:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100214#p100214 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_july24.jpg)

- Tree of Life will now properly remove polymorph and movement impacting effects when shapeshifting, just like the other Druid forms.

- Mind Flay is no longer removable by any type of dispel magic.

- The unattackable Venture Co. Machine Smith in Cragpool Lake has been moved to a more accessible location.

- The foxes in Thalassian Highlands will now be categorized as wolves when tamed.

- The locations of Draconic For Dummies: Volume 5 and Volume 7 have been swapped to match their intended positioning.

- Corrected the objective for The Hunter's Path quest to direct players to the correct NPC in Alah'tHalas.

- Fixed grammatical issues in the quests: Preventive Strike, Delivery to Archmage Xylem.

- Fixed scaling issues for the Tauren appearance of Horde Guard NPCs.

- Fixed incorrect sheathing for the Scythe of the Harvest.

- Panda Cub will join you in napping if you type /sleep while having your companion targeted.

- It's now possible to tame NPC Bayne, a rare level 10 Darkhound.

- The radius of Shazzrah's Arcane Explosion ability has been corrected from 30 to 20 yards.

- Guards in the Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility can no longer be attacked by Alliance players.

- Elixir of Fortitude will no longer require level 25 when learned from the trainer.

- Band of Piercing Shadows will now drop reagents appropriate to its item level upon disenchantment.

- Set appropriate respawn times of 5 minutes for Battered Footlockers across the world.
