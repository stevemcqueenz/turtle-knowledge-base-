# 2023 — December 5

## Post 79953 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-12-05T21:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79953#p79953 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Relentless Strikes can now trigger even if the target dies from the finishing spell.

- Fixed a dungeon reset exploit involving resurrection that allowed players to reset the instance and then teleport to the same position where they had died within the new instance.

- Stormstrike can now trigger weapon enchantment effects like Flametongue Weapon.

- The Will of Shahram buff from Blackblade of Shahram can now stack up to 5 times.

- Players charmed with Gnomish Mind Control Cap will now use their spells.

- Warlock pet spells should no longer get lost if you were teleported while channeling Eye of Kilrogg.

- Added race restrictions to Stormwind and Orgrimmar's Portable Wormhole Generators for Engineering to prevent cross-faction abuse.

- Increased Spirit on Netherwind Belt to 13.

- Corrected the name of the Tome of Portals: Theramore.

- Removed reputation on kill from Qiraji Scarabs.

- Quest item Foul Effigy is now Unique in line with other quest-starting items.

- Fixed script for Ambassador Flamelash in Blackrock Depths.

- Corrected Bubbly Fissures in Lushwater Oasis and The Forgotten Pools.

- Rectified incorrect immunities for Battleguard Sartura, Guardian of Icecrown, and Zevrim Thornhoof.

- Added waypoints for Harb Foulmountain, Siege Golem, and Witherheart the Stalker.

- Resolved an issue with cavalry assaults in Alterac Valley not functioning the second time after the NPC has died and respawned.

- Enabled melee attacks with Razorgore while mind-controlling him.

- Vampiric Touch's mana restoration to party members no longer generates threat.

- Fixed the inability to fish up Nightfin and Sunscale in Kalimdor.

- Removed 5 groups of creatures in the Emerald Sanctum to make the raid feel like less of a slog.

- Tel'Abim Battleground start numbers have been reduced to match Nordanaar.

- Non-raid dungeon respawn timers have been normalized across the board for Quality of Life:  All standard dungeon trash now has a 5 hour respawn timer instead of 2 hours, with the exception of low-respawn patrols (Eye of Naxx, Wandering Eye of Kilrog) and special spawn rule mobs (Scarlet Monastery Graveyard undead are still 15 minutes).  This should alleviate common complaints regarding dungeons such as BRD and LBRS, but will also accommodate struggling groups in difficult dungeons.  Bosses, as always, do not respawn.
