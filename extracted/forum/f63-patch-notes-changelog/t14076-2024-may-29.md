# 2024 — May 29

## Post 95911 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-05-28T21:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95911#p95911 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_may29.png)

- Grand Widow Faerlina's Followers/Worshippers will no longer lose aggro if the initial target does.

- Added a loot table for the Brilliant Wind Serpent.

- Rocket Cars are no longer classified as beasts.

- Disconnecting while mind-controlled will no longer result in a non-functional mind control debuff upon relogging.

- Fixed a rare issue where a dead player could be healed, causing them to get stuck in a liminal state between life and death.

- Golemagg's core ragers now expire concurrently with Golemagg's death.

- [Field Duty] Set the health of Hive'Regal Hunter-Killer to 320k.

- [Field Duty] Removed CC immunity from Hive'Regal Hunter-Killer.

- [Field Duty] Set the respawn times for Krug Skullsplit, Merok Longstride, and Shadow Priestess Shai to 2-3 minutes.

- The Aged Wooden Chest now has a respawn timer of 10 seconds.

- The Brainwashing Machine can no longer be used during combat.

- Fixed a bug that allowed players to assign loot via master loot to players who did not participate in raid encounters.

- The Dream Frog is now classified as a rare quality item.
