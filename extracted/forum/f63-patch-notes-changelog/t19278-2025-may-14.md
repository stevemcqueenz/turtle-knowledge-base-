# 2025 — May 14

## Post 130900 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-05-13T22:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130900#p130900 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-05-14.jpg)

- Fixed swapped models for **Naga Explorer** and **Naga Siren**.

- Re-enabled hair dye vendors. Barbers remain broken. Please stop asking.

- **Earth Shield** criticals can now proc the **Ancestral Healing** talent.

- Fixed an issue where **Dismiss Pet** did not decrease pet happiness.

- **Inner Fire** should now stack with the effect of **Draconic Infused Emblem**.

- The quests **Legend of Dawnstone**, **Behind The Wall**, **Legacy of Perenolde**, and **The Wall Comes Crashing Down** are now shareable.

- **An Old History Book** and **Unpopped Darkmist Eye** should no longer drop from unintended mobs.

- **Unpopped Darkmist Eye** from the quest **"Jarl Needs Eyes"** now drops for all party members.

- It is now possible to mount while in **Pandaren's Disguise**.

- Fixed bug preventing illusion auras from persisting through death.

- Fixed **Illusion: Scourge Warrior** not displaying any visuals.

- Added missing **Armored Gnoll Illusion** to the rotation.

- Replaced one incorrect **Murloc Illusion** in the rotation with the correct deepwater version.

- The server’s global cooldown (GCD) calculations now take world update ticks into account.

- You can now set your guild home at the **Steepcliff Port Inn**.

- **Rage Scar Yeti Hide** will no longer drop if the player does not have the corresponding quest.

- Dungeon quest **Rampant Weeds** is now shareable.

- **Blue Dragonspawns** in **Azshara** should now be skinnable.

- PvP potions **Superior Healing Draught** and **Mana Draught** now have a 2-minute cooldown down from 5 minutes.

- Raid and dungeon death-persistent auras will now remain active after logging out while dead.

- **Arcane Rupture**’s secondary effect no longer triggers other effects.

- Usage of **Mudskunk Lure** is now limited to the **Pagle’s Pointe** area.

- Added several flavor **white** and **gray** items to **Deadmines**, exclusively for **Level 1 Lunatics.**

- Fixed **Innkeeper Shyria’s** vendor list – food is no longer limited to 5.

- Fixed an issue where **Distract**, when used through **Blinding Haze**, unintentionally removed stealth from targets.

- Added **Razor Arrows** to **Chylina’s** vendor list.

- Increased healing coefficient of **Holy Light (Rank 1)** to 0.33.

- The **Paladin Tier 3.5 Retribution (5/6) set bonus** no longer affects the entire raid and instead applies only to the Paladin's group, as intended.

- **Ossirian's Binding**: Reduced Stamina and Agility to 16, Added 6 Polearm skill.

- **Black Brood Pauldrons**: Removed MP5, Added 14 Strength, Added 7 Two-Handed Sword skill.

- **Slime-coated Leggings**: Removed Intellect and Attack Power, Added 27 Agility, Added 1% Melee Crit.

- **Flameguard Gauntlets**: Reduced Armor, changed subclass to Mail.

- **Toughened Silithid Hide Gloves**: Added 5 One-Handed Sword skill.

- **Taut Dragonhide Shoulderpads**: Reduced Stamina to 28, Added 15 Agility, Added 5 One-Handed Mace skill.

- **Torn Wings of Midnight**: Reduced Intellect from 15 to 8, Reduced Spirit from 19 to 14, Reduced Meditation from 10% to 3%, Added 23 Spellpower and 1% Haste.

- **Ravenkeeper’s Frenzied Embrace**: Removed 24 Intellect, Replaced 18 Stamina with 20 Strength, Replaced 2% Physical Hit with 2% Haste.

- **Crown of the Wildpack**: Removed 16 Spirit and 46 Attack Power, Reduced Intellect from 19 to 11, Added 40 Agility, Replaced 2% Physical Crit with 2% Haste.

- **Leysteel Legplates**: Reduced Strength from 37 to 32, Increased Stamina from 32 to 37, Increased Defense from 11 to 13.

- **Jewel of Wild Magics**: Increased Spell Crit from 2% to 3%.

- **Magispark Leggings**: Reduced Intellect from 29 to 26, Reduced Stamina from 26 to 23, Increased Spellpower from 27 to 33.

- **Remains of Overwhelming Power:**Minor Arcane Elemental no longer takes AoE damage, can no longer miss with its attacks, and its health has been doubled. Spellpower granted has been increased from 45 to 55 but no longer applies to your entire party. The Spellpower increase will be reflected in the description with the next client update.

 **Tower of Karazhan Balance Adjustments and Changes**

- **Keeper Gnarlmoon**: Slightly reduced the damage of **Blood Boil** and **Phase Shift**.

- **Ley-Watcher Incantagos**: Slightly increased the channel time before **Guided Ley Beam** deals damage.

- **Anomalus** can no longer get stuck.

- **Echo of Medivh**: Significantly reduced the damage caused by dispelling **Doom of Medivh**, **Corruption of Medivh** will now trigger a raid warning message when cast.

- **Chess Encounter**: Reduced the frequency of abilities cast by **Knight** and **Rook**, reduced the damage of **Ghastly Horseman**.

- **Desolate Invader** and **Desolate Destroyer** are now immune to **Enslave Demon**.

- **Sanv Tas'dal:** Curse of the Rift will no longer be resistable.

- **Rupturan:** **Felheart** now generates less mana and has reduced maximum mana, and is now vulnerable to **Viper Sting**.

- **Kruul:** Now attacks another player in its range if his main target is too far to be hit by **Remorseless Strikes**.
