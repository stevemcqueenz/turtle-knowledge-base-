# Patch 1.12.1+ — Chronoboon Displacer, Mage Table, and Warlock Soulwell

## Post 10502 by Xerron (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-05-10T06:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10502#p10502 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!

This week's update will include some quality of life features we've been working on.

 **[Chronoboon Displacer](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83000)**
The purpose of this item is to store world buffs pausing their timers to use the remaining duration when you please.

- Costs 5g
- Unique and Soulbound
- Bought from [Chromie](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=10667) in Ruins of Andorhal, Western Plaguelands
- Consumed on use, turns into [Supercharged Chronoboon Displacer](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83001) if you have world buffs, storing them for later use
- Cannot use in combat, battlegrounds, arenas, or during raid encounters
- Once consumed you can buy another one from [Chromie](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=10667)
- Buffs stored: [Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=22888), [Spirit of Zandalar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=24425), [Mol'dar's Moxie](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=22818), [Fengus' Ferocity](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=22817), [Slip'kik's Savvy](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=22820), [Songflower Serenade](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=15366), [Sayge's Dark Fortune of Damage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23768), [Sayge's Dark Fortune of Agility](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23736), [Sayge's Dark Fortune of Armor](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23767), [Sayge's Dark Fortune of Intelligence](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23766), [Sayge's Dark Fortune of Resistance](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23769), [Sayge's Dark Fortune of Spirit](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23738), [Sayge's Dark Fortune of Stamina](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23737), [Sayge's Dark Fortune of Strength](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=23735), [Warchief's Blessing](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=16609)
 **[Supercharged Chronoboon Displacer](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83001)**
The purpose of this item is to restore any world buffs stored by the [Chronoboon Displacer](?item=https%3A//database.turtlecraft.gg/83000).

- Consumed on use
- While you have world buffs stored you cannot benefit from them and any new world buffs you get while having this
- item will fade from you if you already have them stored
- Cannot use in combat, battlegrounds, arenas, or during raid encounters
 *Please note that displaying what world buffs you have stored on the item's tooltip is a game limitation that we can only get around by using a client side addon that will be posted later on.*

Here's a short video of [how it works](https://streamable.com/hdwtqp).

[Refreshment Table](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83002) — Mage
Begins a ritual that summons a Refreshment Table. Requires 3 players to right-click the portal and not move until the ritual is complete. Once complete players can use the Refreshment Table to acquire [Conjured Mana Orange](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83004). Requires 2x [Arcane Powder](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17020).
Visit [Archmage Malin](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=2708) (A) or [Bethor Iceshard](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1498) (H) to get started on your journey to learn this conjuration.

Here's a short video of [how it works](https://streamable.com/y0e6sp).

[Soulwell Ritual](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83003) — Warlock
Begins a ritual that summons a Soulwell. Requires 3 players to right-click the portal and not move until the ritual is complete. Once complete players can use the Soulwell to acquire a Healthstone. Requires 5x [Soulshard](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6265).
Visit [Gakin the Darkbinder](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=6122) (A) or [Carendin Halgar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=5675) (H) to get started on your journey to learn this ritual.

Here's a short video of [how it works](https://streamable.com/zip37i).
