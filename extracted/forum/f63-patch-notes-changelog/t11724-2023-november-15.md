# 2023 — November 15

## Post 77428 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-11-15T01:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77428#p77428 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed the low respawn time on Small Thorium Veins inside the Hateforge Quarry dungeon instance. Nodes will now have a prohibitively high respawn timer, similar to other Vanilla dungeons (Wailing Caverns, Blackrock Depths, etc.).

- Fixed Infinite Dragonspawn in Black Morass not giving 5 reputation per kill up to Friendly with Wardens of Time.

- Lower Karazhan Halls will no longer allow dead players to re-enter if a boss fight is ongoing.

- Nature Protection Potion and Elixir of Greater Nature Power can now stack.

- Assigned race requirements to A Crumpled Note to make it clear it starts an Alliance quest.

- Zandalarian Hero Charm and Eye of the Dead will now proc once per cast, not per target hit.

- Fixed Deathstalker Feaerlina becoming uninteractible if the quest is failed.

- Proclaim Champion and related buffs will no longer be removed from players upon changing instances.

- Another attempt to fix Windstone respawn times.

- Goblin Hunters will now start with an ammo pouch instead of a quiver.

- Removed duplicated effect from Blackhammer Pauldrons.

- Corrected the wrong spirit of human rogues at some levels.

- Mages can now see how many points they've spent per tree on saved specs, similar to all the other classes.

- Added missing flavor text to some quest givers.

- Corrected waypoints for Forsaken Courier.

- When a boss puts the whole instance into combat, it will now impact pets too, not just the players.

- Corrected the way Life Giving Gem works.

- Fixed Cloak of the Dark Veil not being able to drop.

- Horde characters can now accept the Crawling for Meat quest from Inunquaq in Darkshore for access to the Icepoint Rock zone.

- Khara Deepwater will now sell only 1 Strong Fishing Pole instead of an unlimited amount.

- Daisy's Hot Pants can now drop from Daisy's Gift Box.

- Fixed Thornpod no longer dropping.

- By Any Means Necessary III is no longer marked as an Elite quest.

- Carrion Swarmer now gives 10% of the XP normal mobs of that level give.

- Hargul the Hardy should no longer evade prematurely.

- On Tel'Abim - Made T0.5 available. This was an oversight from the previous week as we locked down quests that weren't supposed to be available for this phase.

- Fixed an issue where the Goblin Brainwashing Device sometimes reset specs. Also gave 2 free talent resets to people affected.
