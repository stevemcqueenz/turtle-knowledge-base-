- **Ride of the Wild** now properly allows riders once again!

- Loot is now tradable from non-boss enemies within a 10 minute period.

- Fixed incorrect fishing loottables of **Hyjal** and **Burning Steppes**.

- Fixed cost and requirements for the mount **Frost Ram**.

- Fixed **Paladin** and **Shaman** Tier 2 leggings having lower chance to drop than those of other classes.

- Reduced aggro range of **Hillsbrad Sentry**.

- The quests **Honoring the Earthmother**and **Day of Remembrance** have been added for the **Day of Remembrance** event, available until **March 20th**.
