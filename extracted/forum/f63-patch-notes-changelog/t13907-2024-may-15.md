# 2024 — May 15

## Post 94874 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-05-14T21:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94874#p94874 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/changelog_may15.png)

- Fixed a loot exploit involving Blackwing Technicians.

- Corrected the delayed shoot animation of the Darkmoon Cannon.

- Players can no longer be invited to a battleground after it has ended (score screen displayed).

- Relogging will no longer remove players from battleground queues.

- Reworded hardcore death messages for improved clarity.

- Speaker Lazoko will no longer repeatedly recast his channeled spell.

- The Statue of Lyvdia Dawnbird is now only interactive when required by the quest.

- Red Summer Shirt and Blue Summer Shirt now have uniform sell prices.

- Duskpelt Stalker now utilizes a different Stealth aura that reduces its movement speed while stealthed.

- Zetharia is now immune to Fear.

- Sandfury Axe Thrower now inflicts increased damage with throws.

- The shaman talent Thunderhead has been updated to allow buffs on pets.

- Sealed Crates will now always contain an item.

- Lokholar the Ice Lord and Ivus the Forest Lord can now attack NPCs.

- Burning Adrenaline cannot be taken outside of Blackwing Lair.

- Eternal and Aqual Quintessence no longer share cooldowns.

- Moved the quest "The Evil Made Me Do It" to the Gilneas City category.

- Quests from Kex Blowmaster are no longer sequential.

- Added knockback triggers to the top of Dun Baldar to prevent players from climbing.

- Raid items will no longer be tradable after being enchanted.
