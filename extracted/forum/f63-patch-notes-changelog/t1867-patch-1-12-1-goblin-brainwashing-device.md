# Patch 1.12.1+ — Goblin Brainwashing Device

## Post 11156 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2021-07-04T08:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11156#p11156 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Goblin Brainwashing Device** will allow you to save two talent configurations as primary and secondary. Just spec into what config you want and click Save Primary or Save Secondary. Next time you use your machine you can click Activate Primary or Secondary to quickly switch between your saved configurations.

Since the device won't restore your action bars we recommend using <https://github.com/firenahzku/ActionBarProfiles> that saves and restores your action bars layout.

Additional notes:
• Lazy pig addon has a silly feature and it will quickly click on Reset my talents, was fixed in this release: <https://github.com/CosminPOP/_LazyPig> or get it from <https://turtlecraft.gg/addons>.
• Cost of activating is still the same as a talent reset.
