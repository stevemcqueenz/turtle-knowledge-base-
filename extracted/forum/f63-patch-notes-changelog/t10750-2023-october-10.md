# 2023 — October 10

## Post 70096 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-10-11T00:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70096#p70096 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed Rune-Etched Blacksmithing recipes not being consumed on use.

- Lasher's Whip now properly binds to the player upon pickup.

- Fixed double Intellect stat on Ancient Jade Leggings.

- Axe of Dormant Slumber, Vampiris Kris, Ring of Nature's Duality, Vial of Potent Venoms and Medivh's Foresight are now Unique.

- Reduced Intellect on Dawnstone Hammer from 32 to 25.

- Fixed Armor value on Leggings of the Shrouding Mists.

- Reduced Strength and Stamina on Anasterian's Legacy from 16 to 12.

- Fixed Dark Iron Reaver's equip spell to properly function and have the proper effect.

- Reduced the Haste of Wing of the Time-Lord from 2% to 1%. Increased Strength and Agility from 11 to 12 and the Stamina from 8 to 11.

- Fixed Spellweaver's Turban stats as per told in the patchnotes.

- Unyielding Gust and Unmelting Ice should now have the proper use spells to change them into their respective recipes.

- Fixed sheathing on the Spear of the Endless Hunt.

- Changed the model of Venomweave Mask to fix a clientside crash.

- Fixed grammar on Pierce's Instructions, Shield of Mathela and Sword of Laneron descriptions.

- Slightly buffed Shield of Mathela and Sword of Laneron.

- Fixed block value on Marshal's Blocker.

- Fixed the unusually high armor value on Boarskin Cloak.

- Dwarven Battle Bludgeon's stun effect has been changed to a proc instead of being an on use effect.

- Fixed the unusual proc ability on Fleshslasher.

- Fixed a stat type for Fiery Chain Shoulders.

- Reduced the Agility from 22 to 15, reduced the Strength from 19 to 15 and reduced the Stamina from 20 to 18 for Nordanaar Guardian Spaulders.

- Changed Claw of the Infinite to have an attack speed of 1.7 seconds. The damage values have been adjusted to keep the DPS the same value.

- Removed 1% Spell Critical Chance and 5% Meditation from Ring of Nature's Duality.

- Increased the PPM for Annihilator from 1 to 2 due to an anomaly in the average proc rates on the weapon.

- Change the quality of the recipe for Powerful Smelling Salts from Uncommon to Rare.

- Broken Blade will now proc its effect properly.

- Fixed Gnome and Goblin Hunter quests not being able to tame nearby Beasts.

- Improved the Thrash mechanic of Firemaw to be more consistent.

- Fixed incorrect chaining of quests in the Dead Until Dark chain for Horde in Gilneas.

- Fixed missing Revantusk reputation quests from Mystic Guay'Jin and Warleader Zol'Majin.

- Fixed prerequisite quests for Horde's Anchor's Edge quest chain.

- Removed broken Engineering recipes from trainers.

- Fixed Libram of the Faithful effect stacking from multiple Paladins.

- Fixed Firemaw's Thrash charge "storing" behavior.

- Fixed Rune-Etched blacksmithing plans not having a cast time or being consumed on use.

- Fixed bosses in Kara10 not being immune to Horrify effects.

- Fixed Gnome Hunter Beast Taming quest item not working on Large Crag Boars as intended.

- You can no longer enter Emerald Sanctum while a boss encounter is ongoing.

- Raid Scaling: [viewtopic.php?f=2&t=10748](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=10748)
