# 2025 — May 21

## Post 132195 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-05-20T20:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132195#p132195 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog_2025-05-21_ENG_02.jpg)

- Merged the physical and spell hit chance effects for the **Rune of the Wildhammer Clan** and **Rune of the Guard Captain**.

- Fixed an issue where **Loop of Infused Renewal** did not function as expected.

- Fixed an issue where **Mind Vision** triggered hostile effects on the target.

- **Shieldrender Talisman** now functions correctly while shapeshifted.

- Removed **Enlargement Balsem** from the game.

- Fixed an issue where players could mount while using illusions that do not have a mounting animation.

- The quests **The Grimtotem Plot**, **Provisions for Thalanaar**, **Woodpaw Disarray**, **The Ruins of Isildien**, **Cleansing the Roost**, **Harness of Chimaeran**, **Feeding the Younglings**, and **Chimaeran Task** are now shareable.

- Fixed several bows that did not benefit from **quivers** as intended.

- Fixed **Anub'Rekhan's Impale** not dealing damage correctly.

- Creatures in **Gilneas City** will no longer drop **Rare** items from **Uldaman**.

- Fixed an issue where **Pain Spike**'s healing component did not restore the correct amount.

- Fixed an issue where the healing component of **Pain Spike** could be resisted.

- Fixed an issue where the **Scythe of Elune** prevented the bearer from being summoned.

- On **Tel'Abim**, **Shaman** items should no longer drop for Alliance groups, and **Paladin** items should no longer drop for Horde groups.

- The 5-piece set bonus from **Doomcaller's Attire** now summons a **Lesser Doomguard** guardian instead of replicating **Curse of Doom**.

- Fixed an issue where certain NPCs were scaled down more than intended.

- **Alarus** will no longer respawn after being killed.

- Fixed an issue where certain game objects, such as **Mailboxes** and the **Arena Treasure Chest**, were incorrectly blocking line of sight.

- Increased the drop chance of **The Scythe of Elune**.

- Players will no longer learn **Portal: Alah'Thalas** when faction-changing to Alliance, unless they are a **Mage**.
 **Tower of Karazhan**

- Added a mini-pet to **Sanv Tas'dal's** loot table.

- **Kruul** will no longer teleport players if he cannot reach them.

- Reduced the armor bonus from **Shifting Rock** to 3000.

- **Rift Shifted** is now dispellable.

- Increased the attack range of **Ima'ghaol, Herald of Desolation**.

- Fixed an issue where **Lingering Magus' Lingering Polymorph** could target players immune to polymorph effects.

- **Mephistroth**
 - Will no longer enter the add phase if any **Shards of Hellfury** are alive.

 - **Doom of Outland** and **Dread of Outland** are now cast slightly more frequently.

 - Reduced the health of **Doomguards** spawned from dispelling **Doom of Outland**.

 - Removed knockback from **Doomguards** spawned by **Doom of Outland**.

 - Slightly increased the damage of **Vampiric Aura**.

 - **Hellfury Shard** is now immune to all crowd control effects.

- **Anomalus**
 - **Unstable Magic Zone** will no longer spawn multiple visuals, and players will no longer see multiple debuffs while standing inside it.
