# 2023 — December 20

## Post 81778 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-12-19T19:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81778#p81778 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Patch 1.17.1 release: [viewtopic.php?t=10910](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10910)

- Fixed Eagles' model and animations.

- Fixed 6 Female Forest Troll skins.

- Fixed 40+ helmet visuals for both male and female Goblins.

- Fixed numerous Goblin casting animation issues.

- Fixed sheathing animations for both Female and Male Goblins.

- Fixed Male High Elves 2h Animation Special.

- Fixed Lost Defender's Helmet not hiding hair.

- Nightmare Dreamrunner now has proper mount animations.

- Fixed Anasterian's Legacy black texture and enchanting visuals.

- Fixed Larvae of the Great Worm black texture.

- Fixed Guardian of Icecrown black texture.

- Fixed Anub'Rekhan black texture.

- Fixed Thunderfury black texture.

- Fixed Stygian Buckler texture.

- New zones are now properly discoverable! This addition is initially rolled out with Thalassian Highlands, Blackstone Island, and Icepoint Rock. Other zones will be addressed in a later patch.

- Gilijim and Lapidis Islands' general map appearances have been updated to better fit the actual shape and location.

- Added Anchor's Edge, Thalassian Highlands, Blackstone Island, and Icepoint Rock to the world map.

- Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility's lighting has been updated for a better look.

- Added hover-over Points of Interest for all custom locations on the zone maps.

- Restored vanilla map's Quel'thalas section.

- Inns should no longer display Caelen's Rest Inn in places where it shouldn't.

- Fixed most, if not all, pathing issues. Notable places include southern Gilneas beach, Deadwood Village in Felwood, Feralkin Cave in Feralas, and Anchor's Edge in the Barrens, where these issues were most severe.

- Bloody Belt Buckle should (finally) be applicable to other players via the trade window.

- Alah'Thalas flight path is now routed through Light's Hope Chapel for a more scenic flight.

- /wave should no longer play an incorrect voiceline.

- Karazhan Crypt should no longer bug out and send the player to Redridge Mountains on death.

- The stun from Deadwood Pipe can now be removed and prevented by effects that remove stuns.

- Players who are still ghosts upon being forcibly teleported out of Stormwind Vault due to no longer being part of the group will now be automatically revived.

- Casting Starshards on other players will now properly cause you to enter combat and be flagged for PvP.

- Made adjustments to Anubisath Warder ability timers.

- War Mode has been changed to give a 20% bonus honor from all sources but reduced to a 20% bonus experience, following this schedule: [viewtopic.php?f=2&t=12138](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=12138)

- Fixed invisible NPC attacking players during the Sapphiron encounter (hopefully).
 **Important notes:**

- Vertex Animation Shaders & Smooth Shading options are MANDATORY for custom models like the new Goblins.

- Players who use PfUI addon must update their addon to see new zone maps: <https://shagu.org/pfUI>

- Our main realm, Turtle WoW RPPvE, is getting a new name. With the client update, we will try to make the required changes to your clients to keep your addon settings intact. If it doesn't work, just go to the folder ../WTF/Account/YOUR_ACCOUNT_NAME/****Turtle WoW**** and rename it to ../WTF/Account/YOUR_ACCOUNT_NAME/**Nordanaar.**
