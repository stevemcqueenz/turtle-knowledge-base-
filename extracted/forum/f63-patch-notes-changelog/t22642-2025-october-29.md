# 2025 — October 29

## Post 157583 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-10-28T21:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157583#p157583 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-10-29-ENG.jpg)

- **Blooming Bud** healing now correctly avoids consuming resources or triggering other effects.

- **Cloak of Flowing Fire**, **Tattered Orcish Shawl**, **Tattered Crimson Shabrack** and **Drape of Groldan** can now be enchanted.

- Inventory type corrected for **Shadowblaster**.

- **Krug Skullsplitter** will no longer wound himself with **Mortal Strike**.

- **Nippsy's Precision Rifle**, **Standard Grade Rifle**, **Beasthunter's Blunderbuss**, **Zan's Leperification Blaster**, **Slag Slugger** and **Waterlogged Blunderbuss** now benefit from ammo pouches.

- Illusions will now be removed upon entering a battleground as well.

- Fixed the position of **Mal'adaris** so he no longer clips the cart.

- Fixed mobs being stuck inside **Widow's Nest**.

- Fixed several **Corrupted Songflowers** that were not functioning properly.

- Leaving the arena queue will no longer cause you to leave all battleground queues.

- A confirmation dialog now appears when changing **blacksmithing** specializations.

- New transmog items have been added to the shop and to NPCs **Karlos Fieldings**, **Ostantino Villantes**, **Rixi Lamour**, **Riccarda Thillamore**, **Vivi McGoldings** and **Stella Verve**.

- The cannon used in the quest **Death From Below** will no longer kill players during the naga attack phase.

- **Dark Harvest** no longer affects Affliction spells cast by other Warlocks.

- Pet Command **Take Position** can no longer be used while mounted.
