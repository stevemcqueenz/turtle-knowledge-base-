# 2025 — April 16

## Post 126946 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-04-16T00:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126946#p126946 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Changelog_2025-04-15_1.jpg](./download/file.php?id=168) <dd>Changelog_2025-04-15_1.jpg (459.65 KiB) Viewed 11125 times</dd>

- Undercity Bucket Hat now looks like an actual bucket. A perfect helm to exit your starting zone.

- Corrected models for Corrupted Feral Raptor.

- Fixed an issue where Curse of Agony dealt incorrect damage while Dark Harvest was active.

- Fixed an issue where Resonance Cascade would not always trigger correctly under certain conditions.

- There is now a scepter in Kel'Thuzad's room that will speedup Phase 1 of the fight if used.

- Fixed an issue where pets and guardians would sometimes engage distant targets unexpectedly.

- Lightning Strike will now trigger Elemental Shell.

- Mobs spawned during the The Tome of Valor quest should no longer respawn after death.

- Renamed Sweet Mountain Berry (Agility) to Sour Mountain Berry.

- The quest 'The Boran Family' is no longer shareable.
 **Tower of Karazhan**

- Increased the drop chance of Ephemeral Pendant from 4% to 10% for all Outland bosses.

- Increased drop chance of various items for various trash.

- Decreased HP of various boss encounters.

- Increased loot drop chances on the Chess encounter and Outland bosses.

- Increased cooldowns on abilities for Kruul, Rupturan, Chess & Mephistroth.

- Reduced the requirements on the Anomalus encounter.
Please note the loot related changes will go into effect after this lockout ends.
