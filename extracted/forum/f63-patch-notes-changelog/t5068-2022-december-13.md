# 2022 — December 13

## Post 30582 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-12-13T15:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30582#p30582 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- It's now possible to Taunt a Banished creature.

- Scripted the Felhound Tracker pet for quest Azsharite.

- Corrected the drop chance of Scarlet Belt.

- Corrected respawn time of buffs in WSG. Food Buff is 20 seconds, Berserk Buff is 120 seconds, and Speed Buff is 150 seconds.

- Creatures will no longer aggro your pet while you are mounted.

- Your pet will no longer slow down if you enter combat while mounted.

- J'eevee for the quest Imp Delivery no longer enters combat.

- Increased the priority of the Annihilator debuff so it's less likely to be pushed off.

- Removed internal cooldown from Hand of Edward the Odd, but reduced the chance for the effect to be triggered by Judgement of the Crusader.

- Chain Lightning can now jump to targets not in the caster's line of sight, but within line of sight of another target.

- Grounding Totem will now work even if the caster or victim are not in line of sight.

- Fixed a bug with Illumination where down ranked heals could refund mana appropriate for a higher rank of the spell.

- Elwynn/Durotar flying gryphon and wyvern will be likely less bugged.

- Remnants Farmer and Remnants Militia will now aggro players that go near them.

- War Mode will correctly increase experience gains from discovering new areas.

- ZG quests Paragons of Power will correctly require level 58 instead of 60.

- Far Sight should no longer break if you go too far with it.
