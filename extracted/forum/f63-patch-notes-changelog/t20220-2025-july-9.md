# 2025 — July 9

## Post 138497 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-07-08T21:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138497#p138497 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-07-09-1.jpg)

- Fixed a regression where **Endless Quiver** was incorrectly consuming ammunition.

- The transformation effect from **Embrace of the Wind Serpent** no longer applies to targets immune to **Polymorph**.

- **Vanish** will no longer be removed by spells already in flight.

- Fixed an issue where some usable items could not be activated due to an incorrect "**No charges remain**" error.

- Fixed an issue where the 3-piece bonus of **Vestments of Pestilence** did not correctly refund 15% of **Renew**’s mana cost.

- Fixed an exploit related to **Eye of C'Thun** mechanics.

- **Mephistroth** will no longer attempt to summon **Shards of Hellfury** while **Rain of Outland** is active.

- Fixed spell power scaling for **Elune's Twilight**.

- Fixed an exploit involving **Death Talon Captains**.
