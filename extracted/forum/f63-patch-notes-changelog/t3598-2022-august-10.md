Thank you very much for submitting your client data for us to investigate random game crashes. We were able to find and isolate the issues that were causing your game to crash and close.

We will push yet another, hopefully the last one, emergency client update with a hotfix for this during today's maintenance!

Changelog for the upcoming server update:

• Fixed random client crashes around the world.
• You now can lookup a total amount of completed quests when you type /played
• Fixed some dungeon's homebind teleport if you are kicked from the group.
• Made Retribution Aura benefit from damage taken bonuses.
• Fixed Heart of the Crusader.
• Southsea Sash should only drop when on the quest.
• Fixed Omen of Clarity not proccing on spells.
• Fixed interrupted message when learning companions.
• Added gravestone spawn on death of Hardcore characters.
