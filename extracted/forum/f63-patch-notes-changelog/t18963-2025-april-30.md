# 2025 — April 30

## Post 129028 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-04-29T22:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129028#p129028 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2025-04-30.jpg)

- **Sweeping Strikes** can no longer crit.

- Mephistroth is no longer affected by **Curse of Tongues**, **Mind-Numbing Poison**, or similar cast-slowing effects. To compensate, the cast time of **Shackles of the Legion** and **Nathrezim Terror** has been increased to 2.5 seconds. In addition, **Shackles of the Legion** no longer affects pets or summons.

- Fixed an issue where **Grounding Totem** was non-functional during duels when opponents were in the same group.

- Dual-use spells such as **Dispel Magic**, **Chastise**, **Dampen Magic** and **Amplify Magic** (when used with the Magic Attunement talent) are now correctly considered by **Grounding Totem**.

- Added **Dwarven Battle Loaf** to the bread vendor in **Ironforge**.

- Father Lycan's portals now despawn upon his death.

- Ragnaros is now immune to Freeze effects, allowing **Flash Freeze** to properly trigger on him.

- Fixed an issue where **Hunters** and **Warlocks** couldn't complete "**The Stones That Bind Us**" due to the Stone of Binding not working.

- Added **Kul Tiras Tabard** back to the donation store along with new tattoos and faction cloaks.

- Returned **Victorious Alliance Steed** to the donation store.

- The Recipes for **OG/SW Wormhole generators** are now Factionlocked.

- Fixed **SM reset exploit**.

- Mages that faction change from Alliance to Horde will now unlearn **Teleport: Alah'Thalas** and **Portal: Alah'Thalas**.

- Race changes within the same faction will no longer cause your faction-specific spells to be replaced with those of the opposite faction.

- There is now a text cue when **Incantagos** casts **Guided Ley Beam**.

- Nether Infernals will now despawn when **Kruul** dies.

- Witherbark Tusk, Witherbark Medicine Pouch and Rock Elemental Mastery: Compendium III now drop for all party members.

- Reduced drop chance of **Plans: Frostbound Slasher**.

- Fixed **Farwell Ring** to properly look like a ring.

- Fixed an issue where the Rogue talent **Smoke Bomb** did not function as expected. #12429

- Updated several items to no longer require direct damage/healing landing spells to trigger their effects. These items can now proc from successful harmful or helpful spell casts, respectively:
 - **Possessions of the Lost (set bonus)**

 - **Blade of Eternal Darkness**

 - **Ornate Bloodstone Dagger**

 - **Pure Jewel of Draenor**

 - **Wrath of Cenarius**

 - **Staff of the Qiraji Prophets**

 - **The Scythe of Elune**

 - **Sphere of the Endless Gulch**
Tooltips for these items will be corrected and clarified in the next client patch where applicable.

- Fixed an issue where spells such as **Mind Control** and **Mind Vision** would not correctly interrupt automatically if the target resisted the spell cast.

- Echo of Medivh is now immune to **Shackle Undead**.

- Removed incorrect Bleed and Poison immunities on some mobs in the Tower of Karazhan.

- Fixed an issue where **Drain Soul** would cancel immediately after a subsequent cast.
 **Drain Soul** no longer occupies a visible buff slot on the caster.

- Fixed an issue where players couldn't craft at an Anvil in the Badlands.

- Fixed an issue where a **Dabyrie Laborer** would clip onto a nearby roof after reset.

- Fixed an issue where **Ribbly** and his Cronies in **BRD** would attack **Hurley Blackbreath**.

- During the Quest "**Set Them Ablaze!**" in the Searing Gorge, the Towers now lit up in Fire.
Due to ongoing issues with our payment providers during the spring sale, we've extended it until **May 6**, inclusive.
