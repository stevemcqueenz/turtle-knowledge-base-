# 2022 — November 16

## Post 29281 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-11-15T23:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29281#p29281 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Fixed a bug with double skill gain from ranged weapons.

- Attempt to fix mini-freezes.

- Spirit Link will work as intended now in combination with Nature's Swiftness.

- Fixed avoidance for Warlock pets. Grimoire spell still needs a client-side fix.

- Bonespike Shoulders should proc on every crit starting today.

- Fixed having a net debuff on login after when Gnomish Net-O-Matic is equipped.

- Additional adjustments to Slow & Steady XP bonus during dungeon runs to equalise all XP gained.

- Anubisath Defender, Sentinel, Warder, Obsidian Eradicator & Obsidian Nullifier will all drop Large or Small Obsidian Chunks for mining upon death.

- Increased Fel Guard regen + he should be allowed to enter water now.

- It's no longer possible to put zone-bound items in bank.
