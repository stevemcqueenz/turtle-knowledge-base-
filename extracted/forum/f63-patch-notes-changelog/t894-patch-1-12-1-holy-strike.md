# Patch 1.12.1+ — Holy Strike

## Post 4894 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2020-01-17T16:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4894#p4894 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/images/turtlewow_holystrike.jpg)

Greetings!

Today we bring some good news for Paladins!

Speak to **Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker** in Stormwind or **Brandur Ironhammer** in Ironforge.

The first rank of Holy Strike is granted for completing a new class quest, **Piercing Light**. Other ranks are distributed via Librams. To upgrade the spell's rank, one can find the vendors **William Halford** and **Braelnor** at your class trainer's locations in Stormwind or Ironforge.

 **Please, install this client's patch to fix the position of the spell in your Spell Book as well as its name (zzOLDHoly Stirke to Holy Strike). [Download](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/466627627554439178/667904597041348608/Patch-H.MPQ) the patch and copy it into your Data folder. Credits: Reven#4362**

Note: You don't have to do this if you don't want to, the changes are cosmetic only.
