# 2023 — August 16

## Post 57503 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-08-15T21:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57503#p57503 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

- Adjusted the position of invisible walls in AV to fix the gap in the alliance base backdoor.

- Added a script for Private Thorsen. He will now only offer his quest if he survives the ambush.

- Attempted to fix Keeper Remulos deserting his post and going to other zones.

- Fixed the formation movement of Winterfall Runners so they won't stack up anymore.

- Rewrote the script for the quest Staff of Eldara to fix issues.

- It should no longer be possible to accidentally target another player with spells like Multi-shot if you are not flagged for PvP.

- Increased the honor rewarded at the end of Sunnyglade.

- Prevented players from falling below the floor in Blood Ring.

- Fixed a crash related to groups of creatures evading together.

- Fixed Vehena in Orgrimmar phasing out.

- It is no longer possible to kill players in duels by reflecting their own spells.

- Swapping weapons no longer delays your next attack if you are not actually in combat.

- Improved stability and performance.

- Mages can no longer open a portal inside a Battleground.

- Fixed faction-specific quests with race restrictions set so that High Elves and Goblins should now be able to accept quests restricted to the Alliance and the Horde respectively.

- Increased the number of Poppy nodes in Redridge Mountains for the Silvermoon daily quest "Tears of the Poppy" from 15 to 41, and lowered the respawn time from 5 minutes to 1 minute.

- Increased the number of Lake Toads for the Revantusk daily quest "The Means to Heal" from 21 to 45, and lowered the respawn time from 5 minutes to 2 minutes.

- Fixed the Spirit Pyres in the Amani'Alor tomb to behave like proper game world objects. Previously, the pyres could be clicked rapidly for multiple credits towards the quest objective.

- Fixed a rare bug that caused guild bank deposits to go missing.

- Retroactively restored guild bank items that had gone missing.
To combat excessive Auction listings on the AH there will be 2 changes to the Auction House going forward:
- The maximum amount of open listings per account is now 120.

- The listing fee will now scale depending on stack size and maximum stack size of an item. In short, this means that you will pay a higher listing fee per-item if you list lower amounts of stackable items individually versus in bulk.

Thank you, it's a start. The scaling fee is an interesting approach but will obviously not do anything for items without fees - like all enchanting materials, a group of items mass-listed in singles about as often as cloth.
Since the devs might be listening it's a good time to bring up another dodgy AH practice: Fake bid-listings. Someone lists an item for an obscenely low bid with an overpriced buy-out. The low bid puts them at the top of the listing (sorted by price). People bid on it, but half a day later the listing always gets cancelled. This tries to get fools to accept the overpriced buy-out because it's the top listing while wasting everyone else's time bidding and making price discovery harder since the seller never intends to actually auction the item.
This is unfortunately quite prevalent with enchanting materials (again due to zero listing fee, so cancelling doesn't lose you anything). Some days I get several pages of refunded bids due to auction cancelling in my mail. Like mass listing single items in general, this is only really manageable by sellers automating the process with addons. Since it benefits nobody except the seller but wastes everyone's time, I think it should be looked into.

So listing pages of singles even hurts honest sellers who have good offers and good service - it's really just a scummy move hoping buyers will give up flipping through the AH pages and just buy your overpriced single.

Big brokers create overabundance of certain goods in a given niche of the market. To stay in business they have to fix price on the commodity in question, which in turns leads to price on any adjacents commodities being fixed as well. And when this broker goes out of business - the entire niche collapses as supply no longer meets demand which leads to a run on the given item, further hiking up demand while supply stays the same.
For example exactly that happened to arcanite bars a couple months ago: I used to keep price at 25g/bar and bought my crystals at no more then 15g/crystal. By stocking up on crystals I could afford to wait around until price on them stabilized at ~15g, thus keeping 25g/bar ratio profitable and having arcanite CD to sell meaningful.
