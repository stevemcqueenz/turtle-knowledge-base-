# 2026 — January 21

## Post 165755 by Shadowsong (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-01-20T23:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165755#p165755 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/changelog/Changelog-2026-01-21-ENG.jpg)

- **Poisonous Mushroom** and **Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread** has now 5 minutes shared cooldown.

- Fixed multiple issues with the Troll Shaman racial spell **Hex**.

- **Soothound** will no longer attack Hateforge Watchers.

- Fixed both lanes of the **Deeprun Tram**.

- Swapping warrior stances will no longer proc **Windfury Totem**.

- **Moonfeather** can now be tamed.

- **Talisman of Ascendance** will now only proc from damage or healing as the description says.

- The **moonwell in Talonbranch Glade**now allows crafting Mooncloth like other Moonwells.

- Dying in **Anasterian Park** in Thalassian Highlands will now send players to a Spirit Healer in Alah'Thalas.

- Added **Rose-Petal Dawn**, pink hair color for the Night Elves to the barbershop vendor.

- Added 10% swimming speed bonus to the **Admiral Grumbleshell** turtle mount.

- Fixed missing inventory sounds for **Spiced Chili Crab** and **Spider Sausage**.

- **Formula: Enchant Boots - Major Intellect** will now be properly consumed on use.

- Alliance players will no longer be sent to Darkshore upon dying in **Amani'Alor**.

- Dying in **Caverns of Time** will no longer send players to the graveyard in Tanaris.

- **Demon-kissed Sack** will now always drop at least one item.

- Healing from **Pure Jewel of Draenor** can no longer critically strike.

- Fixed an issue where the Hunter talent **Pathfinding** provided more bonus than intended in some cases.

- Fixed an issue where **Sweeping Strikes** benefited twice from damage modifiers.

- Fixed an issue where the Shaman talent **Improved Molten Blast** dealt more damage than intended.
