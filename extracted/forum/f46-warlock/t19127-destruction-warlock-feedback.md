# Destruction warlock feedback

## Post 129960 by Vrankz — 2025-05-07T01:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129960#p129960 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is based on my experience from playing classic for the first time as of phase 1.

ive chose destruction warlock to play because i wanted to cast spells but the problem is theres no point to it ,

in pve specifically molten core i deal more damage using corruption and soul drain rather than casting shadow bolt (also it costs more mana to use shadowbolt than souldrain) even tho im specced into a pvp destruction build. I know spamming shadowbolts only is bad but theres not even a point for it

in pvp its even worse

 1. destruction locks have no sustain your mana is gone after a couple of casts and theres no siphon life or drain life equivalent nor using lfietap is viable i dont think there should be so that every spec should be unique but you basically kill things in a rotation and a half or run out of resources, i dont think buffing the damage or the cooldown would be fair considering the nature of conflagrate and shadowburn but something needs to be done.

 2. you cannot cast  more than one spell that have casting time and usually only at the beginning of the fight or maybe after deathcoil and that spell usually its immolate, almost all classes have ways to prevent you from doing so . in every fight im basically a insta damage affliction lock where you only use spells that are insta cast as everything else is going to be interrupted or enemies hitting you will delay the cast, theres talent only for fire spells and its 70% any feral hunter or warrior is going to hit fast enough to not let you cast assuming they outright dont use interrupt skills. The whole concept the spec is based around straight up doesnt work. you cannot even cast searing pains its just better to use souldrain similar damage per tick , faster instantaneous tick that cant be delayed

Whats worse affliction lock can just use curse of tongues and  invalidate anything you should be able to do with a destruction build. There should be talents that mitigate casting time  delays or interruptions i don think making them instant is the way to go as it would be too broken. Frost mages can lock enemies in place while also slowing them
right after with frostbolt not to mention they can instantly prevent enemies from casting spells for up to 10 seconds if they were casting

 3 Defenses in PvP, the warlock has multiple summons but you cannot have them all at all times so that means you are stuck playing with a void walker for the sacrifice skill , the game assumes you have them all at all times and gives you no compensations whatsover for only being able to summon 1. mages can spam their shield whenever its off cd and even use ice block to wait for the cooldown dispel debuffs and so on, warlocks can only use it once per combat ,and its basically only some more hp, as resummoning the void during it is unrealistic . Mages get counter spell and dispells warlock need a felhunter for that but if you get it you are losing on 2k hp+ from the void shield and will most likely die.

 3.2 the only true defense the destruction warlock can get is curse of exhaustion without it anyone can just run up to you and 2 shot you and even then it still happens as most classes have ways to close the gap. this means destruction warlocks have to spend all their remaining points into affliction just for this curse, which makes everything worse as affliction locks can spend their points in the demonology tree and get master conjuror for better health stone demonic embrace for 15% more hp and talents that allow to quickly resummon demons so not only most of their spells are instant they can mitigate interruptions and can heal themselves while dealing damage they also have the best defenses of them all

 3.3 Fear. Fear is supposed to be the thing that makes warlocks good but it isnt as you cannot finish casting before you die. Im playing alliance and most people play undead so they can dispel it twice with the trinket too, warriors can use their kit shamans have tremor mages counter spell the moment you try and so on. its just not viable. Deathcoil has global cooldown so you have time to cast immolate or use 1 instant spell before it runs out forget about using fear twice in a row too youll get interrupted.

as it stands now destruction warlocks are bad mages mages have tools for every situation and their kit is extremely similar only that in every comparison the mage kit is way better, Frost bolt is better than shadow bolt, frost nova is better than howl, they have blink and counterspell while warlocks have nothing and the felhunter which has to be at melee range which it will never be, magic missles is better than the drains arcane explosion is better than hellfire evocation is better than life tap.etc

 4 Gear. Destruction warlocks rely on short burst of crits to kill but most of the gear doesnt have chance to crit affix so theres even more incentive to play affliction as it scales way better

if you have a destruction warlock in your team if not targeted it can do stuff, assuming you get lucky as non crits deal as much damage as a fire shock from a shaman, but not better than other classes if its targeted its just going to die  in a 1v1 against most classes at least as in the phase 1. Beast hunters for example can just send the pet enrage it and you cannot do anything about it  you are going to go low without being able to do anything about it. If it does the same to a shaman the shaman is going to 3 shot the pet from 4.5k hp or higher it doesnt matter

to summarize destruction lock need a serious rework.
