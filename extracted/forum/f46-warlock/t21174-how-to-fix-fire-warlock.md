# how to fix fire warlock

## Post 147054 by Afraido23 — 2025-08-19T17:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147054#p147054 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all I want to say that I don't care about PvP. Fire warlocks are not bad overall but are definitely behind SM/Ruin. In my opinion it needs a buff and devs need to create greater synergy between fire warlocks and fire mages. Fire mage buffs fire warlocks and fire warlocks should buff fire mage in return.
How I see it:
- Improved Immolate (talent) should also have additional feature: Immolate gives 10-15% debuff to a target that increases all fire damage the target takes.

- Put fire spell hit in Aftermath talent. Now it has 3 points but nobody takes it. Put 1% fire spell hit in one point so fire warlocks get 3% fire hit when they have 3 points in Aftermath.
So far that is all I can suggest. Please give some feedback <3
