Warlock is an easy class to level.

Personally I don't think you need Nether Studies. This is a new talent and lock had no issues with threat on their VW even before this.

The best vanilla spec to level for warlock is called "Drain Tanking" and it assumes going deep into Affli. Personally I always do an adaptation and branch into Shadowburn and Nightfall cause i like more offense.

With the new changes and faster ticks from Affli i imagine drain tanking would be even stronger now for leveling. Dark Pact is lost but tbh i always preferred to life tap and drain life of a new mob.

If you are new to warlocks entirely i would go for [something like this](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock?points=FoASFYSARIAAAAAoTAAoAbAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA=).

Start with affliction. prioritise imp corrution, imp drains and fel concentration.
You can play around a lot with this spec and tweak as you see fit. The differences are not too big and it boils down to preferred play style as well at the end of the day.
The general rule is Affli/Demo mixes are best for leveling, but if you know what you're doing you can easily go 11 points into destruction as well for Shadowburn.

A link to the original drain tank spec as well if you're curious: [https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qdUs22 ... GIh7q/view](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qdUs229_XvkR1NCeTrJkybL5BDXGIh7q/view)

## Post 121816 by Browntown22 — 2025-03-05T22:47:00Z

I started leveling a new lock to try out the changes and one thing in particular caught my eye, the tier 3 demonology talent Demonic Sacrifice. I know it seems stupid to give up your pet but sacrificing the voidwalker is actually incredibly good while leveling.

You get a buff that heals for 3% every 5 seconds, doesn't sound like much but you only need a little stamina to start passively out healing the damage a single mob can do to you. It gets really crazy if you get power word fort or any kind of bonus hp like from a world buff or something.

I'm level 36 right now and this is totally a viable way to level. My hp regens so fast that I can life tap for infinite mana. I can easily pull 2 or 3 mobs at a time, dot them all up, tank all the damage while wanding them down, and then go do it again and be full hp by the time I reach the new mobs.

I won't lie the dps loss from not having a pet kind of sucks, but its not like warlock is really lacking damage and being pretty much immortal is a great trade off.
The best part is if you need to solo an elite you can just summon voidwalker, have it tank until its about to die, and then sacrifice it anyway to get the hp regen buff anyway.
