# Safe to assume that Demon Hunters won't be playable...

## Post 111386 by Silverleafeon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-04T18:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111386#p111386 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So in that case, what if Demonology Warlocks got reworked in a way that more resembles a Demon Hunter?

I've always liked the Warlock aesthetic, but I've never really enjoyed playing one. That is until MoP / Warlords, when the Demo tree was reworked. Suddenly Demo warlocks didn't just study and contol demons, they delved deeper into the dark arts and almost became demons themselves. This was so cool and fun to play! Demonology Warlocks quickly became my favorite class.

And then Legion hit. Great expansion, but it totally gutted Warlocks so that Demon Hunters could be added to the game. One of the biggest tragedies in WoW history, IMO.

But back to the topic, I think it would be a really great idea to do this. It would make sense lorewise. Warlocks are fueled by ambition and a desire for more forbidden knowledge. I think at some point, it would be the natural course of a Demonology Warlock to realize that to really study demons, they must become a demon.

Of course, they woudln't totally lose themselves either, or else they'd just be joining the Legion. But sorta like Void Elves and Demon Hunters, they do a precarious balancing act between the light and the dark.
