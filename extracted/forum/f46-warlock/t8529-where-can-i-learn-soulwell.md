Had the same problems with my warlock. It's a bit tricky.
First go to Blasted Lands. Talk to that guy (western part of BL, surrounded by demons).
He will teach you how to summon a Doomguard via a long and boring quest chain.
Do it.
After that immediately he wont do anything.
Me, as an ally warlock toon, went to SW and talked with the warlock trainer who had a new quest.
Quest sent me to the same guy at Blasted Lands.
He sent me to kill Hakkar and loot it's essence.
Did it, gave the essence to him -> Soulwell.

One thing to note, which can be confusing.
If you have improved hs talent, soulwell will ignore it and give normal hs to the party.
If you do hs the normal way, it is still improved of course.
But game handles these 2 hs as different. So if you hotkey the improved hs to your action bar, do a soulwell and action bar shows 0 hs, it's because of that. You can still find soulwell hs in the bag. Putting up both to hotkey can help.
