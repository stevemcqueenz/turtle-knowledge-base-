# Troll racials in 1.17.2 – what do you think?

## Post 116092 by Niefalt — 2025-01-10T08:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116092#p116092 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

***Greetings, everyone!***

I’d like to hear from more experienced warlocks about your thoughts on the recent changes to Troll racial abilities and how they impact our class.

For those who might not remember, here’s a quote from patch notes 1.17.2:  – **Berserking** now grants between 10% - 15% attack and casting speed depending on how badly you are hurt instead of between 10% - 30%.
– **Regeneration** now increases health regeneration by 20% instead of 10% and allows 25% of total health regeneration to continue in combat instead of 10%.

In my opinion, the [Berserking] ability was nerfed specifically because of warlocks, and now it doesn’t seem as powerful for our class as it used to be.

That said, the [Regeneration] ability received a buff, and I’m curious to understand if this makes playing a Troll warlock more appealing. How noticeable is this ability at higher levels? Does it significantly improve the survivability of the character?
