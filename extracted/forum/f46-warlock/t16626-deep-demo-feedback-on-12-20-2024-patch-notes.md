# Deep Demo Feedback On 12/20/2024 Patch Notes:

## Post 113531 by Krotux (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-20T09:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113531#p113531 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Greater Duration:**
Greaters should be 5 min because:
 3 min sucks for progression, good for speed clear, hard to balance. Additional duration doesn't buff the speed clears since boss dies fast
Feels stressful to need the tank to pull ASAP because you know clocks ticking.
A lot of times the pull is cancelled or have to jump on the "reset spot".
A lot of fights are simply over 3 min, potentially more coming in kara40.
Infernal has nice AoE so would be nice to use for trash.

Having the demon attack you when the boss is at 25% hp sucks, and then you're a terrible version of demo/ruin.

Alternatively, you could change master summoner to reduce cooldown by 70% instead of 50%. Making the cooldowns 3 min, 9min, and 18 min for infernal, felguard, and doomguard respectively. I would prefer 5 min tbh tho.

TLDR: Infernal should always be mostly accessible, Felguard/Doomguard serving as CDs.

 **Greater HP:**
This isn't that important but hp being about the same as normal pets feels bad flavorwise. There was like 1 doomer (who hates all of demo/pets anyway) who complained about infernal HP. Now that duration is longer, there's plenty of time to kill them off with power overwhelming. It's not a problem in PvE or PvP and I can provide proof why if you want.
Increased Infernal's health from 3544 to 7500
Increased Felguard's health from 3832 to 8500
Increased Doomguard's health from 4517 to 9500

 **Doomguard left out:**
Doomguard should probably also have a master demonology effect. Maybe shared with Felguard?

 **Infernal Debuff is wild:**
Infernal: Healing taken reduction increased from 50% to 75%
This seems a bit excessive, no? It's the main pet for SL. It is 15% in live. I would have expected like 33%?
