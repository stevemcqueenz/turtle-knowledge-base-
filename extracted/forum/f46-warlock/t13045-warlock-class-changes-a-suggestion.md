# Warlock Class Changes, a Suggestion.

## Post 87438 by Grayhammer — 2024-02-10T02:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87438#p87438 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, Its cathy again. Been a while since I've made a forum post.

I saw we had a new drop of a Roadmap with more class changes on it. Riveting stuff. Love what you did with the Tier sets, Bloodvine had it coming.

Here are some changes I think everyone could get behind.

 1. Take Healthstone off of CD with Potions/Tea. Implement Master Conjurer into Cookie Tables. Tea is great, however it overshadows both Healthstones (Warlocks) and ManaGems (Mages). It invalidates a part of a kit, taking away player importance over a consume for convenience.

 2. Change Spellstone & Firestone to work like Soulstone, and have them share a CD. Make it so you have to debate between giving someone a revive, a Firestone Buff or Spellstone Buff. This would give warlocks more options as well as give Firestone and Spellstone a reason to exist.

 3. Improved Lifetap, make this Baseline or first row. Does not matter if you are Demo/Affliction or Destro. Warlock kits are centered around high mana cost and everyone will need to take this at the top end of PVE or PVP. Its just a QoL change.

 4. Make Master Demonologist a raid wide buff, like Paladin Aura's just demon flavored. Otherwise the only raid wide buff we have is Imp bloodboil.

I will stop here, since otherwise I would go down the entire talent tree of Demonology and express my love for some things, how certain things should be combined/rearranged. How certain things should synergize in the tree and changes that I know will be shot down. The changes I've recommended are the most reasonable and I've had time to talk with my guild, and fellow warlocks on what they would think is a good change, thus far these are it.

anyhow, thanks for reading my ted talk. toodles.
