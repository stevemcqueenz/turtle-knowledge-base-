## Post 102794 by Eversongwoods (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-10-09T06:35:00Z

They are similar but affliction is definitely better for leveling if you play it right. Demo is bettter for soloing elites and the better choice for hardcore as it is more survivable, being able to quick summon your demon is a life saver. With affliction you can multi dot and drain tank a mob yourself while your void is dealing with another, keeping siphon life up on multiple targets. I always go 12 pts affliction, all ten pts in the top two tier talents/lifetap then go 8 into demo for the stamina and void talent so he holds aggro. I would just go all affliction all the way to shadow mastery if you mostly kill mobs at your lvl, i typically go for mobs 1-3 lvls above.

## Post 102809 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-09T11:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102809#p102809 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think affiction has better leveling talents. For example reduce enemy chance to resist your spells, refund mana after kill, slow curse (almost every elite can be killed by kiting).
trading spirit for stamina is not really viable if you have low attributes overall. Also I suspect that this talent works only for *base* stats, not for improved with gear.
Reduced cooldown for shadowbolt in is good option. Though, mobs of your level will successfully die from your dots. If you want to DoT and spam shadowbolts, then you probably waste your mana (because dots won't tick their full duration).
Also up to lvl 40, your best damaging spell is wand. You don't need to spend mana on shadowbolts if your wand has better DPS and don't suffer pushback from mobs.

## Post 102810 by Zulnam (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-10-09T11:49:00Z

the main difference between Affliction and Demonology for leveling is that one is good and the other is bad.

Affliction gives you a lot of tools for leveling that will make your life easier.

In contrast demonology gives you very little and is only ever useful for:
a. raiding dps spec (0/30/21) (and even then it was like a 5% increase in dmg over 30/0/21 iirc, which is solid pve and pvp)
b. pvp if you are new to the class and want to be a bit more defensive (20/31/0)
