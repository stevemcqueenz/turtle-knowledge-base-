# Problem & Solution CC2 Demo spec

Issues with current cc2 proposal for Demonology:

 1.Hard to balance
Deep demo is going to be either complete garbage in PvE or insanely op in PvP. You simply can't have an OP succubus dpsing down players on top of the utility she already has. And if it's not OP, it will suck in PvE.

 2.DS talent is pointless in Demonology spec
It makes no sense for a talent to be useless for the spec its in. Imagine a fireball talent in frost that frost can't use. It makes no sense. Originally, DS was meant as a way to get rid of your pet and focus on Greater Demons.

 3.Janky and Inconsistent
The reliance on Fel domination and having to resummon demons is janky. Master Demonologist randomly excluding Doomguard is inconsistent. Soul Trap requires "no demon", while DS requires  "no common demon". Both should just be "no common demon" for consistency.

 4.Lame (Opinion)
Other warlocks already make use of Imp & Succubus extensively. Why make deep Demology the same? You got 3 greater demons.

 5.Other issues
There are a couple of other minor issues here and there. For example, "The Black Book" will likely make the Felguard/Infernal OP since they do all their damage in a short time. It will likely lead to lame bandaids like these demons becoming immune to buffs.

 **Solution**:

Greater demons should be the focus in PvE for deep demo (no Ruin). Greater demons are impractical in PvP, allowing you to easily balance PvP and PvE. They're also waaay cooler than an imp. Hard to see the demonology fantasy when you're using an imp 99% of the time.

What is needed:
Remove Felguard/Infernal buffs from "Master Demonologist" and increase the duration of greater demons.You can also add a penalty for maintaining control of these demons, suggesting that you are pushing your limits as a warlock.

 **New Proposal:**
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/DdGVZxj.png)

“Master Demonologist”
Greater Demons: +10% damage and mana cost
(Common demons remain the same)

New Keystone next to Soul Link:
Greater Demonology
Increase Greater Demon control duration by 5 min, but your damage is reduced by 25% while you control it.
(can be higher up to like 60%, or lower, depending on balance)

This completely optional talent lets players access a whole new type of gameplay, where you focus on supporting your demon and your raid with health funnel and Improved Shadow Bolt.
