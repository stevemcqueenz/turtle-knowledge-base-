The sad reality is still that way too many people think pve and pvp are having no overlap and they suck at both.
While the old spellstone was definitely not super overwhelming, blocking ~1k spell damage on demand still had its uses.
Be it as a extra layer of safety against general spelldamage or a specific tool to survive bossmechanic (such as an extra mark on horsemen, stay in longer on firemaw etc.)

But people always get by in pve with just the core abilities that are expected of them without using the full depth of their potential toolkit.

Thats why these spells often get decried as useless, while they absolutely do have uses in cases where the full width of your toolkit matters. Such as in duel or small group pvp scenarios.

Treating pve & pvp as different games and designing only for pve doesnt just ruin pvp. It also makes pve more and more one dimensional.
