# warlock tanking (via pets)

## Post 74657 by Spriggit (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-29T15:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74657#p74657 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Here's an idea I've long wished to have seen in WoW - warlocks tanking via their pets.

I don't mean the standard way of summoning a voidwalker and then having it sic a mob, then casting on the mob until the voidwalker's threat gets outpaced. I'm thinking something more drastic. Specifically for allowing the pet to retain mob aggro even with well-geared players wailing on its mob. This would be through either outright possessing the voidwalker (like a hunter's Eyes of the Beast) and upping its damage and threat, or via some kind of damage/threat tie-in ability like soul link.

The closest implementation of this kind of idea that I've seen was in Dark Age of Camelot via the Necromancer class. For those who haven't played Daoc, Necromancers were a pretty ineffectual cloth casting class until they summoned a minion, which they would then possess until slain. Essentially they were undead forms like druids have animal forms in wow.

Now i accept this may be too radical a change even for Turtle Wow - maybe the next best option would be an ability that changes your damage/aggro, somewhat like Soul Link currently does. I'm thinking specifically for utilizing this with a voidwalker but in theory every pet could have its own niche application.

Here's how it might work. The ability - let's call it "Fel Pact" just to call it something, would grant an indefinite (or at least 1 minute) duration buff on both the warlock and demon pet. On the warlock side, it'd reduce damage output by something like 50% (or more), while transferring all or nearly all of the lock's generated threat to its pet. On the demon's side, it'd be 'granted' the warlock's threat while also boosting its in-combat health generation and possibly also upping  its damage mitigation. Doesn't have to be absurdly high, maybe something like 5-15% outright mitigation like the TBC talent "Demonic Resilience" offers.

If people think this would make the pets too good of an aoe-threat tank compared to our other meatshield options, then maybe the transferrable threat could only come from certain spells, like shadow bolt, searing pain, or curse of agony/corruption/immolation etc.

I'm not asking for the best tank in the game to be available to warlocks. It'd just be a really cool way to get a low-tier (but somewhat functional) group tank into the hands of demonology spec-d locks. Tanks are always in high demand but low in numbers so this would probably be an asset to everyone's group delving experience. Locks wouldn't be gaining dps over the destruction/affliction schools(due to the personal damage nerf) when using this talent so it wouldn't be a go-to choice for the class.

Oh, and also, please fix demon pet's lack of Avoidance, thanks!
