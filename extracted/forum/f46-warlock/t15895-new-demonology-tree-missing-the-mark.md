# New Demonology Tree missing the mark

## Post 108251 by Spriggit (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-17T06:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108251#p108251 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While I like the logic applied by the Twow team with the new talent changes, especially to Demonology, I think they missed a big opportunity with the master demonology talent and Voidwalkers.

The whole point to using a voidwalker is that it should draw attention and soak up mob aggression, not provide "pushback resistance."

The physical damage reduction buff is fine, but instead of pushback resistance, it would have made a lot more sense to have warlock threat transfer to the voidwalker in somewhere like the 40-50% range. This way we could use it as a reliable aggro buffer (the only one warlocks get) at the cost of having slightly less DPS. Maybe even as a budget tank when none other are around.

The new enhancement shaman talent that transfers totem aggro to the caster is an example of this idea that works well.
