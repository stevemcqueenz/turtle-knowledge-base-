Tracking pet buffs on this server is a bit of a nightmare, since the pet buff bar only appears when you hover over it. It becomes even more difficult with Unleashed Potential.

Warlocks can keep Unleashed Potential active through Funnel, but there’s essentially no way to monitor the duration.

A simple solution would be to give the Warlock a corresponding buff—purely as a visual timer—without adding any extra effects.
