Hello.

This goes out to TWoW dev. team that changed the talent tree of Affliction Warlocks.

I have been playing TWoW now for a couple of months and i as a Vannila and Classic WOW  Warlock player, i have to say that changes made in talent trees are making Affliction builds way too strong and gimping other ones.

I know that Destro build isnt the one you should go from the start, but comparing deep affliction with SM/Ruin or pure Fire build, just isnt close to comparison. With my current gear, which has few greens in it and lacking 3% hit to being hit capped, i am pulling 450 DPS easy as a Dark harvest affliction warlock on elite lvl 63 dummy.

Other day i specced SM/RUIN just to try it out and i was hiting 250 DPS.

Same gear, way different outcomes. You made affliction warlock way too strong, by changeing certain talents.

For exp.: Shadow Vulnerability isnt anymore crit triggered and it gives 20% shadow buff to all shadow damage.
This alone favors deep affliction then SM/Ruin...

I know that there should be some drive to go for other talent trees, which are dependant on gear. But if you need to get 16% hit chance as fire warlock and on top of that at least 20% crit, amount of good quality gear needed for that is insane and frankly not worth the time.

What i want to know is how other warlocks feel about this and if there are any plans to "balance" these builds out?

Thank you, Wraithbound.

## Post 169207 by Spritzbube — 2026-02-22T16:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169207#p169207 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The problem isn’t that Affliction is too strong — the real issue is that the other builds (SM/Ruin and Fire) are simply too weak.

Fire has some potential, but Searing Pain is just a bad filler spell, and the threat generation is way too high. Some fire warlocks are even casting Shadow Bolts in a fire spec, which is honestly ridiculous. On top of that, getting hit-capped without any talent support is extremely difficult unless you already have specific items from  MC/BWL/AQ40/Naxx (and yes you need to farm all this raids for specific items). That makes Fire mostly a PvP or very late-game option.

SM/Ruin, on the other hand, is just in a terrible spot right now. It doesn’t perform at all and feels completely underwhelming.

Affliction is harder to execute properly, but gearing for it is much easier thanks to the 10% hit from talents. All you need is 5-6% hit and as much haste as you can get and it performs really well. That said, I think Dark Harvest should simply function as a stronger version of Drain Soul. Managing a proper rotation with Dark Harvest alongside all the DoTs is already extremely demanding, and compared to other classes, Affliction is more than challenging enough to play effectively.

In my opinion, Fire should get a hit talent and a buff to Searing Pain. For example, swapping the Searing Pain crit talent to a hit talent and increasing its spell power scaling (as they’re apparently planning in the new patch) would already make a big difference. But without a threat reduction talent I feel, it would still perform worse than affliction.
