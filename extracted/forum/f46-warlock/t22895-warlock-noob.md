## Post 159958 by Justgreens123 — 2025-11-18T05:21:00Z

When leveling most people recommend focusing on Affliction or Demonology.  Voidwalker works great as a tank when solo, and in groups imp works well.  Succy if you need the sleep or want to do a bit more dmg than void.

Generally rotation is CoA, Corruption, siphon life, soul drain.  Can corruption first if you want nightfall to trigger.

For leveling could go for: [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock? ... AB-CoDAB-F](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock?points=FoASaAKDRBFAB-CoDAB-F)

For later raids may eventually go for this, as pet will die to AOE:
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock? ... B-CrAAAB-F](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock?points=FoASaAKDRBFAB-CrAAAB-F)

Could also focus on demonology/greater demons but I don't know that myself.

For gear, you want stamina when leveling, int is a bonus.  Spirit about worthless, as you get 0 regen in combat (unless troll).  Later levels, spell power starts to be a factor.  Until 60 try to balance with stats to not get 1-shot.
