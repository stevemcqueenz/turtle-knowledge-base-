I think this absolutely the expected outcome here.
You're lower level than all of your party members, by up to 5 (!) levels. That's going to make a *massive* difference.
Depending what the druid was doing you might have been the only caster, so your resource management and cast times are in a different league than the other players.
You might have played with alts of people who dropped a few gold on green gear.
Most importantly - did you check the other characters for world buffs? If any of the melee had the Dragonslayer world buff they were about 4x as strong as their level would indicate.
You say you're playing "demo", and at level 14 that probably means your first-row talents add zero offensive power.
As mentioned, pre lvl 20 dungeons you're missing some of your key skills, certainly any kind of aoe.
And yes, warlock isn't super strong in a group context early on.

Hard to tell without knowing more details, but your experience feels pretty average to me as the lowest level caster in an early dungeon. That alone doesn't mean you're playing it wrong.
