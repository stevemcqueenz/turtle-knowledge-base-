# Some suggestions for Warlock as a Warlock main

## Post 127471 by Forumdweller (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-19T08:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127471#p127471 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Most of these changes are pvp-oriented suggestions, please check out the pvp thread devs <3

I'll preface this by saying that I want everyone to feel like the game is as fair as possible. I play many classes and have done so across many private servers and my classic wow career. That being said I know it's not possible to please everyone and that it's probably a good thing that you can't. Please refrain from being toxic in this discussion and come up with legitimate counter arguments/praises and explain your reasoning. I want the game to be fair, just like you. But we all have our own individual experiences and perspectives. All the changes I suggest don't have to come together in a package, one or two ideas can be implemented and the others can be discarded, keep that in mind.

First off I think curse of exhaustion is WAY too strong as it currently stands. Specifically for destruction. Perma control & enormous burst damage is not a good combination to have from a pvp perspective. It's better for affliction since they lack burst damage and have to wait for their dots to ramp up or pray for nightfall procs.

I don't think curse of exhaustion for destruction is necessarily a bad thing, but 50% baseline for 3 points that early in the affliction tree is insane. To put it into perspective you can literally curse someone on a 100% mount and they'll never catch up to you even as you're running (this actually looks bugged TBH. something is off with how movement speed is calculated in regards to curse of exhaustion and mounted targets)

I suggest reigning it down to like 20% and bring amplify curse back (maybe make amplify a deeper talent that wont let you take it with conflag?). That way we can double it to 40% but ONLY ONCE on a long CD (2-3min). It's a very good counter to a rogue after they pop sprint or vanish for example but can't permanently be exploited to keep a warrior at 36 yards while hardcasting soulfire or shadow bolt.

The next suggestion is for aftermath. If this talent is incorporated you may choose to discard curse of exhaustion completely. With max points, shadowburn and conflagrate has a 100% chance to daze the target. This would be fair because shadowburn is a low range ability with a mid-long CD, and conflag can only be applied after casting Immolate (i think someone said that conflag can now be casted without immo? that should definitely be reverted.. imo).

Non soul link Warlocks have always lacked defensive options against melee classes. It would be nice if demon armor gave slightly more defense but that would depend on if we actually had a way to get away from them excluding a 3 min CD in the form of death coil. And I know there's a talent in demo now to increase the effect of Demon Armor but it's hilariously negligble and will never be worth taking it over grim reach, nightfall, or fel concentration. Basically off the table.

Fear should be completely reworked. This spell is a god damn nightmare for every party involved. It's practically only castable against other casters. You can't fear most targets because of the short range (which is fine). It's basically our only defensive but it's unusable 90% of the time because it takes 6 seconds to cast it against melees or classes that can hit you quickly (including hunter). But if you do cast it there's a good chance the target runs around uselessly for a minute. Fear is especially useless in uncoordinated and coordinated group PvP alike, where you manage to fear someone but it breaks instantly because they got hit by someone elses direct damage, which is basically a death sentence to the warlock and it's completely out of their control. Or it breaks from the first tick of your corruption which can also happen more often than you think.

I have a few suggestions for how fear could instead function.

The first is to leave fear as is. Since fear is already very good against casters we leave it. HOWEVER we modify howl of terror instead. Howl of Terror now comes with a baseline baked into the ability (or a point early in a talent tree) which prevents casting from being interrupted by damage. This would make it a silly choice to cast against casters (unless they're really cocky and come up to your face) but gains a viability against melee while maintaining a long CD and a longer cast time than fear (howl of terror is already a 2 second cast). As of right now it's unusable without a pre-emptive lip which is quite toxic.

This gets abit complicated now but what if fear applied a debuff that prevented it from breaking immediately from direct damage for 2 seconds (excluding shadowburn and nightfall procs to prevent exploiting). However after the two seconds it has a high chance to break from ANY damage. Think of it like a pseudo-disorient (not a stun) that can also be used as the infamous method to control one target while you deal with another. This could even be a talent that can be gathered in the destruction tree or applied to a destruction set bonus/pvp gloves. Also this additional 2 second debuff could have an ICD of 10-20 seconds so you can't spam it and the normal functionality of fear is maintained

The last option and probably most OP is to simply change the current warlock pvp glove bonus from searing pain to compatibility with fear instead. I.e 70% chance to resist interruption from damaging attacks while casting fear.

Let us cast fear (unless we're kicked) it's our signature spell!!

Finally I think the Insignia changes should really be looked at. Imo they should retain their class specific features but they could definitely do with a relooking at. Imo the purpose of pvp insignias should be to help alleviate class counters. A warlock insignia should remove stuns (since we already suffer greatly from CC especially against melees who are naturally ingrained with many stuns) while a mage shouldn't remove stuns because they counter practically every class that can use them by simply blinking or popping ice block.

A non PvP suggestion: bring back curse of doom for demonology. I'm gonna be honest I don't know much about the new raiding specs for lock but deep affliction is bis now right? It would cool to make demo an alternative like in WoTLK. curse of doom can only be used against non players and ticks for large damage every 10 seconds for 30 seconds.

 **I'll do a small Q&A here because I feel like people are gonna keep making the same misrepresentation of these changes.**

 **Doesn't the change with 2 second grace window make fear even more op?**

No. In many ways it becomes weaker overall, but it becomes more usable for skilled players who know how to fake cast their abilities or juke spells. This is because fear would now break from ANY damage after the 2 seconds debuff, meaning it can no longer function as a mini 20 second stun which don't break even as dots are ticking. It encourages warlocks to not keep you CC'd permanently while dealing damage or putting up their dots willy nilly. It's more rewarding now but is less toxic for the people who rightfully complain about how OP fear can be (remember I said this would be a destruction talent or set piece so affliction warlocks can still play the game)

The point is to make it so that it doesn't make it break immediately when you're fearing someone in pvp which can either happen when you're 1v1'ing from the first tick of your corruption - if it ticks within the 2 second 'grace' window - (this happens more often than you might think) or because someone else is slamming them. It's basically a death sentence most of the time and it's completely out of your control. To make it even more clear what I'm trying to convey here, the disorient effect only lasts as long as the 2 second debuff fear would now apply and breaks from MOST, if not all direct damage from the warlock but will NOT break from dots or other players for 2 seconds. The best thing the warlock would be able to do in this window is either run, drain life (2 free ticks), or cast a free searing pain (1.5s cast) without fear breaking.

 **What about the change to the pvp glove bonus with fear adding a chance to resist interruption from taking damage while casting fear?**

As for the change with replacing the pvp glove bonus of searing pain to functionality with fear, fear is exactly the same... now you can just cast it versus the classes that keep you 'soft-stunned' permanently. To put that into perspective, fear can take upwards of 6 seconds to cast vs warriors or rogues. It's so absurd that a skilled player wont even bother kicking fear because they know you wont even manage to even get it off. How is this fair?

Psychic scream is a instant cast aoe CC. What do you think is better. Fear or psychic scream? Priests can also heal themselves. I think we'll be fine with these changes.
