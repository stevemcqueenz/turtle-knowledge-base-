Master Demonologist:

 **Second**, a re-balance to common demons so that succubus isn't always the best:

Felhunter's tainted blood should change to be a stacking dot, and then change MD:

Master Demonologist:
imp: 15% mana cost reduction and +5% haste
succubus: -10% threat and +5% damage
voidwalker: -10% physical dmg reduc & pushback resist
felhunter:  60 magic resist & direct damage has a chance to apply new tainted blood dot

Imp should be decent overall, and even help the raid with buffs (blood pact, fire shield).
Succubus should be best if your focus is on the warlocks damage and threat management.
Voidwalker is a niche pick when getting push back.
Felhunter is ok, but best if you don't have to worry about threat or buffs AND it's a long fight.
