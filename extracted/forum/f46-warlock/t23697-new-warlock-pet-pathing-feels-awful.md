# New warlock pet pathing feels awful

## Post 165906 by Sharmaine (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-22T02:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165906#p165906 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

About two weeks ago there was a change in enslaved demon behavior, including greater demon pets (infernal, felguard, doomguard).
They now lag behind you as you go. They wait for you to move, then think for a second or two, and then maybe follow. Or maybe they don't.
You can't be sure now that your pet comes with you. He is not by your side, he is several yards back if you walk. If you ride, he might be 100yd away. Have to look around and check if he is here.
Demonology has talent Unleashed Potential that you need to update every 20 sec using funnel, and it has 30 yd range. You hop on mount, ride for 15 sec, dismount and use funnel... but pet is not here. He is not even in your sight, he got lost somewhere on the open terrain. UP stacks drop.
Apparently pets now use some server logic to check if path is available, and this logic doesn't catch up with player movement. The logic is spammed, overwhelms pet, causes hundreds of tiny delays with each new player position. It just doesn't work well enough.
If possible, please revert this change. Whatever goal you were pursuing, Turtle wow team, by introducing this change, it isn't worth making game feel so much worse.
