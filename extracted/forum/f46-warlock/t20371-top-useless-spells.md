Here is a top useless spells that u can forget for pvp and 99% pve

 1. Drain mana. Spend 310 for to get 700 in 5 sec and no scaling by SP. And channeled means u have to stay 5 sec. in the original vanilla blizzard tried to fix the uselessness of talent in the affli tree by adding a dmg to it *( caused 30% of mana drained as a damage)*. But it was stiil too smal lame effect.

 2. Mana funnel. Channeled, 63 mana per sec to your demon. I mean LOL. if you a demo wl just strangle your demon by Power overwhelming ans summon another with full mana. More effective than just stay 10 sec and losing dps.

 3. Spell stone. 10% (15%) decreases threat by spells. For demo and destro firestone much better, afli hasn`t crits and it is much easier to control the threat. Just instal TWT treat meter, ask pala about salvation (20% threat reducion), u also can use Soothing kiss and forget about spellstone.  rip . but in original vanilla [spellstone](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=17728/create-spellstone-major) has usefull effect in pve and pvp . I don't understand why this nerf was needed and why it couldn't be made consumable like healthstone with CD

 4. Curse of doom . 3200dmg and chance to summon doomguard looks cool. but u have to wait 1 min, and chance near 6%. And also for non demo specs doomguard under control 10-30 sec. After 10-30sec he starts attacking you, and you cannot re-enslave him here on twow. Just lame spell

 5. Ritual of Doom (for afli and destro). You need 1 Demonic Figurine, you need 2 smart enough party members that understand they should NOT move. And, and you have same doomguard for 10-30sec. Still cannot be re-enslaved. Lame spell

 6. Curse of weakness. -15%(20%) of attack speed. It mean autoattack speed. In pve in some situatuions when tank low geared it could be usefull, maybe. But much better to kill all mobs ASAP. In pvp - lame spell. -15%(20%) means that Warrior with 3.3 attack speed will hit by autoattack 3.8(3.96) but rogue and hunter`s pet with 1.3 AtSpd will hit 1.5(1.56). LMAO. Its almost nothing, no any effect to phys ablilities. Reducing phys dmg by 15(20%) would be much better but. We have 6th lame spell

 7. almost forgot. Eye of Killrog. Low speed, small time. Worse than other scout spells like Hawk eye. Idk where to use it. Just increase view distance and forget about this lame spell

 8. Drain life. due to weak scaling it has a very situational use in PVE. The heal per second is less than from Vampiric Touch Shadow Priest. But at the same time you dont do good DPS. 99% of the time you will not use it in PVE. Only in rare situatins when healers cannot heal you and Death Coil on CD. Not lame, but not so often needed
