# Warlock talent and spell reworks wishlist

## Post 156057 by Szalord — 2025-10-18T14:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156057#p156057 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello folks!

So i've been trying to compile some sort of wish list for some changes for warlocks with a few other very active warlocks in the community. The point of this list is to express some opinions of the warlock community in regards to its state, and offer some suggestions in order to try to push the class towards a better spot in the game overall, because, as we probably know, currently, warlocks are struggling in raiding environments overall compared to other pure damage classes that tend to get a much better deal out of an easier gameplay loop on top of it.

Moreover, i know not everyone would always agree with every suggested change, and i myself in no way am trying to pretend to be the ultimate expert at the class, i certainly have a lots of things to learn from much better players than me. All that to say, feel free to disagree (respectfully!) with anything in this list.

So, here are the suggestions we came up with:

 1. Sinister Pursuit: removed. Comment: there are enough filler talents in the affliction tree as is, this talent has no practical purpose at all in any area of the game. Edit after comments: instead of this talent, make it a Grimoire to be bought from the demon trainers that permanently increases the demons' movement speed by 10 or 15%.

 2. New talent (Destruction tree, row 1): a 3 point talent that reduces the warlock's (and the demon's) generated threat by 10% per point. Comment: most of caster classes / specs have threat reduction talents (except elemental shamans and moonkins, and that fact is also sometimes an issue for them), while warlocks have only some small threat reduction effects scattered between Voidstone's 10% reduction, and sacrificed succubus' 10% reduction, both of which options reduce the build diversity quite a lot if they get mandatory to have.

 3. As a followup to #2, rework Create Voidstone to now grant 2% cast speed.

 4. As a followup to #2, rework the Demonic Sacrifice's talent's bonuses. Suggestions: Imp: Increases the casting speed by 4%. Succubus: Increases spell critical strike chance by 4%.

 5. Aftermath: removed. Replaced by: 3 point talent, grants 2% spell hit per point for destruction spells. Comment: Aftermath is a very poor talent that offers very little to Immolate's damage over time only, and very inconsistent CC, so both of these effects can be moved elsewhere; destruction warlocks have a harder time than affliction gearing up in earlier raids with no spell hit talent support, this would help them in that regard. Rebalance a few T3.5 destruction gear pieces by removing some spell hit and incrasing spell damage or adding spell crit instead.

 6. Resilient Shadows: buff it from 5% chance for affliction spells to not be dispelled per point to a much higher value, like 20% or 25% per point. Comment: affliction warlocks' damage is based on damage over time effects and they tend to get totally shut down when facing a dispell class. If necessary, move this talent in a deeper row in the Affliction tree, for example instead of Grim Reach or Soul Siphon.

 7. Shadow Mastery: reworked. Now instead of increasing the damage dealt or life drained by shadow spells by 10%, increases the damage over time of Affliction spells by 25% (5% per point). Comment: Affliction's damage over time effects should deal noticeably more damage than non affliction trees, which historically has not really been the case.

 8. Curse of Agony: now deals constant damage over time on every tick, instead of ramping its damage per tick. The total damage dealt by the curse remains the same. Comment: see the suggested Dark Harvest's rework, right below.

 9. Dark Harvest: reworked. Now, haste increases the cooldown recovery of this spell. Moreover, instead of accelerating all damage over time effects by 30% during its channeling, this spell instead refreshes all damage over time durations of Affliction damage over time spells on its last tick. Comment: this spell in its current implementation has received too much of a mixed reception in regards to its power, with the overwhelming opinion of it needing to be a better capstone spell for Affliction.

 10. Health Funnel and Mana Funnel start their first tick instantly. Comment: the first tick should happen instantly instead of after 1s as it is currently, we already have 1.5s GCD that limits the spammability of these spells, as well as health and mana initial upfront costs.

 11. Rain of Fire: increase the spell damage scaling.

 12. Hellfire: increase the spell damage scaling, each tick of this spell can now be a critical hit, similar to how Arcane Missiles' channels work for mages. Comment: the steep mana cost, the damage taken, the fact the caster has to stay immobile and the fact the caster has to stay in melee range of the enemies to affect them with this spell is enough reasons to make this spell deal significantly more damage.

 13. Improved Soul Fire: instead of increasing your fire damage by 10% for 30s, now increase the damage of all destruction spells by 10% for 30s. Comment: the destruction tree focuses too much on fire damage, while Shadow Bolt is part of the Destruction spell school and hence, should also count for this talent.

 14. Emberstorm: instead of increasing the damage of fire spells by 10% for 5 points, instead, increase the damage of all destruction spells by 10% for 5 points. Comment: see comment on #13.

 15. Searing Pain: increase its spell damage scaling coefficient in order to make this a better filler option. If necessary, increase its cast time by 0.5s.

 16. Intensity: instead of giving Fire spells only a chance to not lose casting time when taking damage, make it apply to all Destruction spells. Comment: see comment #13.

 17. Master Demonologist: Greater Demons: reduce the healing received by 40% for 5 points except from the Warlock's healing effects. Comment: Felstone and vampirism effects should be a good option to heal greater demons, and not also get nerfed by 40%.

 18. Ritual of Doom: reworked to now not need additional summoners anymore.

 19. Master Summoner: now also reduces the cooldown of Ritual of Doom by 50% when 2 points allocated in the talent.

 20. Curse of Doom: reworked to explode after 15s instead of after 1 min, remove its cooldown. Scale down its damage accordingly.

 21. Fel Stamina is now a 3 point talent, rather than a 5 point talent.

 22. Demonic Aegis: removed. Demon Armor's baseline bonuses are buffed. Instead, Demonic Swiftness (from destruction tree) takes its place.

 23. New talent in demonology tree, 5th row: Your Curse of Doom can be used alongside other curses. Applying Curse of Recklessness, Curse of Shadow or Curse of Elements also afflicts the target with your highest rank of Curse of Doom.

 24. Nightfall: also reduces the mana cost of Shadow Bolts from this effect by 50%.

 25. Create Firestone: instead of granting 2% critical chance to your Fire spells, grant that to all Destruction spells. Comment: see comment #13.

 26. Unleashed Potential: also grants the warlock a debuff that does nothing but that lasts for the same duration as the Unleashed Potential's buff duration on the current demon. Comment: demonology warlock is notorious for its lack of quality of life when it comes to managing the pets, and the tracking of the Unleashed Potential stacks's duration is part of the problem. If the warlock gets a debuff with a proper duration, this would help alleviate the issue considerably.

 27. Bonus addition: add a new Greater Demon to the pool of Greater Demons to use as demonology warlock, which can cast spells from range (similar to the imp), and that has a powerful cooldown-based AoE (like Felstorm for Felguard). How about the Observer demons (the big floating eyed with tentacles kind of demons)? 30 min baseline cooldown, gets reduced by Master Summoner to 15 min. This would replace the Felguard as a powerful burst AoE Greater Demon, and a powerful option for a ranged Greater Demon.

Additional changes from edits in this thread:
 28. When you summon a greater demon, the "enslaved demon" debuff on it does not show the correct 3 minutes duration, i think it still shows 5 minutes duration. This needs to be fixed. Alternatively, for all greater demons or enslaved demons, make the warlock get an appropriate debuff so we can track the remaining duration easily.

 29. Make the greater demons remember the states of auto toggles on each of their abilities, so people don't have to rely on macros or addons to do that.

 30. Create Wrathstone: this spell now increases the threat generated by all Destruction spells by 40%, instead of only of Fire spells. Comment: this change opens up some flexibility when it comes to warlock tanking, especially since Shadow Bolt would also get 70% anti pushback with the suggested changes. Searing Pain needs a crystal clear identity alongside a buff in its damage however, so it still has a place in the warlock's arsenal.

 31. Cataclysm is now a 2 points talent, instead of a 5 point talent.

 32. Improved Stones: moved to row 2. Also now increases the Voidstone's casting speed bonus by 50/100% per point.

 33. Soul Funnel: moved to row 3.

 34. Unleashed Potential: now a 2 point talent, down from 3 points (the addition of the talent for Curse of Doom requires us to take a talent point off somewhere else, otherwise the talent distribution gets a little odd).

 35. Improved Searing Pain is now a 2 point talent, instead of 5.

 36. Improved Immolate is now a 3 point talent, instead of 5.

I'd like to add a few additional points to address some more warlock issues, notably when it comes to speedrunning and the fact warlocks have to respec in some raids quite a lot to stay optimal:
 - All warlock demons now can be summoned in 6s as a baseline, down from 10s.
 - Master Demonologist now reduces the summon time of warlock demons by 1 extra second per talent point (down from 2s).
 - Consuming a Voidstone, Felstone, Wrathstone or Fire Stone is now instant (like Healthstone), instead of requiring a 3s cast.

Extra bullet point that concerns all the classes as a result, but that would have a huge positive impact on warlocks:
 - Reduce the cast time for deployment of the Goblin Brainwashing Device to 5s instead of 10s.
 - Reduce the duration of the Scrambled Brain debuff to 8min, down from 10min.
 - Reduce the cooldown of the Goblin's Brainwashing Device to 8min, down from 10min.
 - Reduce the mana exhaustion from respeccing to 25% of the total mana pool, instead of getting all the mana wiped out.

I was having a conversation about Warlock tweaks that would be nice and decided to chime in on this topic rather than make my own.

 - I agree with ideas regarding the use of Stones needing to be instant cast.  Keep the creation of them the same, but being able to swap between Stones should be something situationally rewarding to Warlocks.

As for the specific suggestions I have:

 1. Firestone giving essentially a Fiery Weapon enchant to melee attacks is weird.  There is something there, but you need a rework of Firestone.  Keep the +Crit to Fire Spells, but perhaps give Melee Crits a chance to make Searing Pain Instant Cast - but to make that possible you would also need to have Firestone give Warlocks Melee Hit equal to their Spell Hit.

 2. Spellstone.  Spellstones share a CD with Healthstones.  Spellstones absorb incoming Magic Damage, but the damage they absorb is less than the health that a Healthstone heals.  So being a better player and anticipating incoming damage is not rewarded as much as being braindead and healing after taking damage.
 - Spellstone suggestion 1 - Spellstones restore mana equal to 50% of spell damage absorbed.
 - Spellstone suggestion 2 - Spellstones give a 30 second buff of 30 to magic resistance of the type of magic damage absorbed (absorb fire damage, gain fire resistance) - might be harder to code this one though.

 3. I like Szalord's idea about Voidstones giving a 2% bonus to casting speed.
 - A second suggestion I might have would be that while using Voidstones Warlocks are immune to magic reflect causing their spells to come back at them.

 4. Wrathstone - I kind of like the double-edged sword of Wrathstone.  It's great to be able to deal more threat, and it's also great to take less damage from hellfire - but Hellfire threat is dangerous enough without Wrathstone - with Wrathstone it's downright deadly.
 - Maybe Wrathstones should synergize with the idea of Fire and Threat and have their own version of Malediction, where casting Immolate on a target also applies Curse of Weakness.

 5. Felstone - I think there is room for more Demon Synergy here.  Because Felstone heals the Warlock and their Pet for damage done by the Warlock - but perhaps that buff should apply to the Demon as well.

As much as I would love to see a Demon Within build added to Demonology, I know that's kind of unrealistic - but the function of Warlock Stones really needs some work if we want them to be more than just "situationally useful".
