# My POV on warlocks CC2

## Post 115748 by Yggd — 2025-01-07T15:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115748#p115748 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really enjoy playing warlock, it's always been my favorite class to play in all versions of WoW since 2004, even though it's not my main raiding class. I've played alot of warlock over the last couple months and I think that while the current state is better than what it was pre-Christmas, it's still a bit off. My comments:

 1. Affliction

Right off the bat we face two massive issues where DoTs have to be reapplied rapidly and you have to have to apply them on all mobs in order to do significant damage. It is not realistic gameplay; especially not when Hellfire is a simple cast that ticks on every mob, every second. Other than bosses in raids, no pack of elite mobs will last a sufficient amount of time either for a warlock to have DoTs up on every mob, or for a significant amount of time. So AoE with these spells is completely out of the equation, leaving single target as the only place it might shine. However, as much as the new capstone talent is an interesting concept, you're always limited by GCDs and refreshing DoTs which cost alot of mana/lifetaps cost a GCDs also. The set bonuses aren't good, and the damage is lacking, even when you can cast a few SBs with the (very rare) Nightfall procs.

Overall: C-

Suggestions, ideas:
 - Through late talent points, it could be interesting to have the opportunity to refresh DoTs when applied, or a chance for casts to apply said DoTs to targets
 - Another option could be similar to WoTLK Death Knight 'Corruption' spell that cast all DoTs at the same time on the target and spread to another target every 3 seconds; the same could be done but with CoA, Immolate, Corruption.
 - The capstone talent could not only make DoTs hit faster, but make them tick for AoE damage as well.
 - Nightfall could be a talent that cannot be reachable if you put points in Ruin, and then greatly improved. Have it be a 15% chance to proc after 3 talent points, and make it cost no mana when it procs.

 2. Demonology

I like that DS and it's follow-up only require a total of a dozen of points in order to be obtained, leaving the warlock 30-ish talents to spend elsewhere. However, I think it removes the identity of the warlock to not have a pet. I wish there was a way to benefit from said bonuses without sacrificing the pet - you don't really see hunters sacrificing their pets, no one even questions it. I think Power Overwhelming hit the nail on the head where pets can finally deal decent damage, however it's still limited* (see further comments below). Unleashed Potential is a good idea, however no warlock will want to waste a GCD to proc the refresh, considering the warlock's damage is already greatly diminished by going demonology spec. That talent needs to be reworked completely.

Overall: B-

Suggestions, ideas:
 - Pets HP needs to be revisited, or the way they take damage, negate damage, etc. In a raid environment where most bosses have at least 1 AoE effect, all pets will die to that AoE effect right away. Even moreso if you used PO beforehand and the pet sits at 60% HP or less. In late WoW versions, pets could not be hit by PvE AoE spells; that might be the play here, leaving them still vulnerable in PvP. I had a boss fight where I started with a succubbus, she died within 2 seconds, I used my Fel Domination talent to spawn another one, died even faster - used my felguard, he died within 6 seconds to AoE and then used my infernal and it also died within 5 seconds. The boss wasn't even at 70% at that point, all my options were depleted for the fight  and the next 5-15 minutes.
 - Felguard and infernal should be capstone talents. Or at least make them both 2 minute duration spells with a 5 minute cooldown. Wasting so much time spawning a succubus between using both spells is severely impairing the warlock's dps, especially considering how weak she is, as mentioned above.
 - For that matter, please make Demonic Figurines and Infernal Stones stack to 20.
 - I think Power Overwhelming should be a passive proc from the pet's attack. The warlock already has enough to manage with 2-3 DoTs, Immolate, Shadowbolts, Hellfire, Lifetaps and a pet to move and it's spell bar - doesn't need to add another spell to its rotation. Make it a 5 proc per minute passive ability, which is the equivalent of 5 x 12 PO which the warlock is currently doing.

 - Destruction

I always felt like Destruction was intended to be the most powerful spec with that name; everything gets destroyed. While ISB talent got changed recently again, it's very lackluster and the fact the tooltip does not mention how much is the 'chance is higher on critical hits' doesn't help bring confidence if it's good or not. The tree is mostly the same, which to me has always been underwhelming, yet mandatory because of Ruin, regardless of the spec the warlock wants to play. Fire spells other than Hellfire are very weak, including Conflagrate. The fact that no other fire spell such as Incinerate (from TBC+) was added again leaves the fire spec undesirable and with no flavor.

Overall: D

Suggestions, ideas:
 - If the effects of Ruin are to be mandatory for every warlock, regardless of their spec, then it shouldn't be a talent at all. Either make it a passive (too strong), or make it an entry level talent with 5 points, so the warlocks and go spend their talents elsewhere after.
 - Hellfire should crit. You see mages' AE getting massively buffed and hitting for 900 per mob, there is no reason for the warlock's AoE not to crit, especially since it also hurts the warlock and the mages' AoE does not. Make it tick every 1.5s so that it's like a mage's GCD spent on spamming AE, but the damage is just not there.
 - Fix the tooltip on ISB so that we know what we are working with, talents shold not be vague.
 - In future version of WoW, warlocks had a (PVP) AoE floor talent called Havoc, on a cooldown, which made it that all mobs in the zone would be hit by the single-target spells the warlock was casting. Couple with Chaos Bolt*, it was a very interesting combination of a spell.
 - I think you should move on from trying to make a Fire spec as the main source of damage altogether. The warlock does not have sufficient tools to make it a main spec. It would be like making a shaman's main source of damage being fire.
 - Conflagrate and/or Soulfire could be changed into something more interesting like Chaos Bolt. A massive 4 seconds cast, single target spell that is unresistable and always puts ISB for 6 seconds on the target it is getting cast on.

In all honesty, the Destruction tree and suggestions are probably too hard to make. It's a flawed tree that doesn't provide much outside of Ruin. The changes and suggestions to Demonology would be rather simple and easy to make, and some in the Affliction tree as well. I think they should be strongly considered to bring back warlocks on par with other pure-dps classes.

Finally, something needs to be done about warlocks hit for their main spell, Shadow Bolt. Every single other spec for DPS has something that either reduces creatures chance to resist a spell, or straight up improve the chance to hit, including affliction spells. A 3-points talent to improve the hit would go a long way. For that matter, Supression could be reworked into a talent that gives 2/4/6% chance for Affliction spells not to be resisted as well as an additional 1/2/3% chance for all spells to hit.
