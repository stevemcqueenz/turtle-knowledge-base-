It's very clear from any testing that this wasn't well thought out or tested at all. I've been making tons of suggestions because I already knew this would happen. It's time to listen to the players that actually want to use these tools.

 1. Infernal and Doomguard are literally just the same weak NPCs from cc1, with timed durations. They are not worth summoning at all. Felguard is ok but doesn't really merit its 1 min duration.

 **Just make the duration of all Greater Demons 5 minutes, with a very very high chance to break early unless you get Soul Link. Also buff Infernal, even if it lasted permanently it sucks. It's worse than the Imp.**

Infernal would be the go-to pet, with Felguard and Doomguard being longer cooldowns. Common demons would serve as backup when your pet dies mid-combat, and for demo/ruin builds and any build that doesn't take DS.

 2. Common Demons are completely worthless except Imp. Even if you tweak the numbers, there will still always be a "best pet" unless you make some changes. The pets themselves need balancing, but also MD needs to be changed to avoid there always being one best pet all the time.

Master Demonologist:
Imp: 15% mana cost reduction and +5% haste
Succubus: -10% threat and +5% damage
Voidwalker: -10% physical dmg reduc & pushback resist
Felhunter: 60 magic resist & direct damage has a chance to apply new tainted blood dot
Greater Demons: +10% crit and -10% haste or -20% healing received etc
Or just remove all greater demon buffs, or add Doomguard

More Info:
[viewtopic.php?t=15370](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=15370)

Also, pets are still dying in raids due to mechanics. They could use way more hp in general, specially greater demons if they last longer. Like 10-15k hp for an Infernal. It would also make them harder to kill if you mess up.

 3. Could use a new tier set, or at least make the affliction set not affliction-only. Set bonuses should be a bit generic not boost stuff like dark harvest...
A fix for this would be to make pets scale with your crit, via the talent Demonic Precision.

 4. Power Overwhelming is Underwhelming. 20% damage is cool, but 40% HP loss is a bit harsh. Maybe add 20% attack speed or crit as well. Then it might be worth always using that and using health funnel to keep up your Infernal, assuming it lasts 5 minutes.

I feel like Affliction and maybe firelock was your focus, with demo spec being something you were forced to tweak at the last minute.
