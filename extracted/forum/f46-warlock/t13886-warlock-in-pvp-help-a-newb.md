I agree with your observations on stat difference for different classes, but can't agree with final conclusion.

PvP is not PvE, survivability is better than raw damage. So everyone stack on stamina until they reach some comfortable amount to not die instantly from any ambush.
If you get enough survivability, then you can go to AH and search "of shadow wrath".
Chest with +23 shadow spell damage costs 3g, Boots with +17 SSPD cost 1g, bracers +7 SSPD cost 30s, boots +19 SSPD cost 25g etc. Prices are different, but you can stack spell damage if you want.
You can go dungeons and get gloves with SPD, dagger with SPD, shoulders with SPD.

 30-39 is the first bracket where you start gearing in +spell damage. Turtle team also have added a lot of +damage items from lower brackets.

Even with this, I see enough twinks 19 with green gear of school spell damage, which play casters. It means it is totally possible to play there as caster.
Yeah, you probably should learn how to survive without Death Coil. But it is not the only spell in your book.
