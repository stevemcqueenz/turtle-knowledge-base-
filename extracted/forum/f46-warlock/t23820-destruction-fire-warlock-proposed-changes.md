# Destruction Fire Warlock Proposed Changes

## Post 167117 by Afraido23 — 2026-02-01T09:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167117#p167117 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

1. Firestone can now proc from fire spells. Added small spell power scaling.

 2. Soul Fire rework: Inflicts damage to selected target and the three other targets nearest to it. Each successive enemy struck receives half the damage of the previous one. Base cast time reduced to 4 seconds, Bane reduces it to 3 seconds. Spell power scaling set to 125%.

 3. Improved Soul Fire (talent) now reduces Soul Fire cooldown to 20 seconds and the buff provided by it stacks up to 2 times. The buff still lasts 30 seconds so you can have +20% fire damage on extended fight.

 4. Searing Pain base damage increased on all ranks to match with new 2 seconds cast time. Now also deals additional x Fire damage if the target is affected by an Immolate spell.

 5. Improved Immolate (talent) - Additionally, Immolate debuff apart from damage now also increases the critical chance of your Fire spells by x. This change is designed for Fire Warlocks to buff other fire damage classes - mages and shamans.
