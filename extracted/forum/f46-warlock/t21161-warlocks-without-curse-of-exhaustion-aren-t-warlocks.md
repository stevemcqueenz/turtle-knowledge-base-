# Warlocks without curse of exhaustion Aren't Warlocks

## Post 146987 by Lisander — 2025-08-19T11:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146987#p146987 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To those on the Alliance who decided to strip Destruction Warlocks of Curse of Exhaustion: you really don’t get it. This debuff wasn’t just a spell — it was the backbone of control and strategy on the battlegrounds.

Without it:

Destruction Warlocks can’t weaken enemies before engaging.

Their damage, which relied on slowing and reducing enemy resistances, takes a huge hit.

The tactical depth of “maximize every moment of the fight” is gone; now it’s just “stand there and die.”

And this is on the Alliance, which already can’t fear 40% of the Horde, and now you’ve taken this away too.

A Destruction Warlock without this curse isn’t fulfilling its role — it’s a shadow of the class it was meant to be. Think twice before nerfing core tools; PvP balance isn’t just about weakening things, it’s about keeping the class identity intact.
