# Demonic Sacrifice - a revamp

## Post 116344 by Digitalmage — 2025-01-12T13:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116344#p116344 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since the new class changes update, Demonic Sacrifice has been demoted to a tier 3 talent, resulting in a loss of 6% damage when it comes to the Imp and Succubus sacrifice effects (even when combined with the tier 1 talent Soul Entrapment). The Warlock is a pet class and for a talent ability that changes the gameplay of the class, removing its pet-controller status, it is simply lackluster. I think that Demonic Sacrifice needs to be revamped to such a degree that it makes it an interesting spell, not simply a self-buff in order to not have to manage a pet (if you did not want a pet, why roll one of the two pet classes?). Obviously, this will require tuning warlock demons in order to make them sturdier to raid boss AoE effects and mechanics, so they are not dead for the majority of a boss fight.

Anyway, my suggestion is turning Demonic Sacrifice into the following ability:
> **Demonic Sacrifice**
>  *15% of base mana*
>  *Instant* *2 minute cooldown*
>  Sacrifices your summoned common demon, temporarily infusing you with its demonic strength. For the next 18 seconds, you gain bonus spell damage equal to 30% of your sacrificed demon's Intellect and an additional effect based on the common demon that was sacrificed.
> - Imp: The critical strike chance and mana cost of your direct damage Fire spells is increased by 15%.
> - Voidwalker: You regenerate 2% of your total health every second and you take 25% less damage from all sources.
> - Succubus: The critical strike chance and mana cost of your direct damage Shadow spells is increased by 15%.
> - Felhunter: You are surrounded by anti-magic energy, making any successful damaging spell cast against the warlock burn the attacker's mana equal to 25% of the damage dealt, further causing 1 Shadow damage for each mana burned this way.

This way, Demonic Sacrifices changes from a stale, passive ability into an active, interactive ability that can be part of the Warlock's arsenal and rotation, with uses in both PvE (as a burn phase self-buff mechanic for example, or a finisher) and PvP (the Voidwalker and Felhunter DS effects act as valuable defensive cooldowns for a class that currently has none). Because it's much stronger than the currently-implanted form of Demonic Sacrifice, this will require moving it further down the tree. Another option would be to simply remove it as a talent all-together, and make it a base level 60 ability shared between all warlocks.
