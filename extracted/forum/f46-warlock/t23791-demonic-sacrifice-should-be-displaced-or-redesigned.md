# Demonic Sacrifice should be displaced or redesigned

## Post 166824 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-29T06:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166824#p166824 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

This talent, Demonic Sacrifice, has a long history. Initially, or in early game, it is a relatively deeper talent that exists as the prerequisite passer-by investment for the more significant Soul Link. There have been a number of strategies or popular talent builds involving the effect of demonic sacrifice since 2004. And now in early 2026, TWoW has moved this talent to a shallower deck, with a deck 1 talent as its prerequisite. These two talents stack as a 10% increase to all warlock spells' damage when the imp is sacrificed.

The first problem is still about its strategic inclination - discarding a demon and fighting alone, which is contradictive to the minion-based style of Demonology spec. I think its initial form in vanilla is a better design as a minor option that can be adopted when in need.

The second problem is that this talent is actually too essential with an effect similar to some much deeper ones like Shadow Mastery and Ember Storm, which also requires at least 5 talent points for 10% of a specific spell damage (shadow or fire). As a DPS in a Naxx raid, a Affliction or Destruction warlock typically needs this talent to maximise the damage. Ironically, a demonology warlock doesn't. In other words, if you want to benefit the raid with a blood pact from your imp, you need to reduce your max damage by nearly 10%. If you want to tank Vek'lor, you can take the imp for higher HP, but your threat will be reduced.

The possible redesign: make this talent split into corresponding demons' skill lists, such as binding it to the voidwalker's damage absorbing shield and making it an burst damaging skill and you would never need the effect of this talent all the time.
