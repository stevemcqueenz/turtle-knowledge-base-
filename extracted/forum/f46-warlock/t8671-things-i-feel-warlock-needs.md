Shadow burn - no longer cost a soul shard instead it rewards one when kill a target. deals more damage and is baseline

Corruption deals additional damage everytime target is hit by your own shadowbolt. meaning when cast shadowbolt while corruption on target you proc shadow damage

Life-Siphon - deals little more damage can also proc nightfal

Soul Shatter - Deals Damage - Removes all threat u have caused soul shard - In destruction tree were shadowburn was

New Talent - Soul Shatter - Now Deals Shadow Damage. to target reduces all warlock threat to 0 instant cast 60 second cd

Cripple - what cripple will do is take all curses currently on the target and consume them. then reduce targets movement speed 50% Attack speed by 20% All Resistances reduced by 25 take 10% more damage and receiving 10% more damage and deal a damage over time effect. like bloodlust this has 5-10 min cd and last 30 seconds.whil this is active no longer able to put curses on target or other warlocks until it finishes

Soulfire Baseline - Procs with Nightfall - but gains gains 30% second cooldown and stuns for 3 seconds.

Improvements to demonology -

Unholy Frenzy - Increases your pets damage by 20% and damage it takes is reduced by 20% and gains 50% attack speed bonus. the cost is during duration your pet continueless take damage this effect lasts 12 seconds while frenzyed your pet cannot be Crowd Controlled has same cooldown as hunters berserk

New Talent Burning Hellfire - Reduces amount of hp taken using Hellfire and will deal additional damage over time. every time it hits this can stack up to 5 times have like immolate effect
Demonology warlocks can summon infernals and doomguards without the need to enslave them again and they getting hostile to your team mates

Pet Improvements -
Attack Speed on pets are improved little bit
Damage on Succubus and Felhound is increased depending on spec
