Mixed feelings. In practice, Affliction is awesome for soloing, fine for dungeons, and bad for raiding (though dark pact sucks for all). To respond specifically to a couple items on your list:

Improved drain soul significantly increases your uptime if used well. Lots of extra mana.
Improved curse of agony: Do you really think other curses outperform in dungeons? Raids, yes (and debuff cap makes it irrelevant), but putting curse of agony on all the secondary targets will give you a ton of damage that rarely pulls aggro because the curse damage is end-loaded.
When you can dot several targets, instant cast improved corruption is a pretty big damage increase. But yes, it's worth very little on dungeon bosses.

It's hard to solve affliction's weakness in raids because a spec themed around dots is inherently bad when dealing with a debuff limit. That said, replacing dark pact could close some of that gap, as well as offer something interesting in other situations. I see three approaches:

 1. Offensive utility: Replace it with an improved curse of shadows/elements. You might still sit low on the DPS meters, but other players will want you around.
 2. Defensive utility: The thematically appropriate option would be "Drain life overflow". When you are at max health, life you drain goes to the party member with lowest health (possibly stack a raw drain power increase here, as well). In hard fights, this could give the healers a larger margin for error and save a little on mana. Again, you won't top the DPS meter, but a warlock who knows when to start draining would be extremely valuable.
 3. Raw Damage on a cooldown: A little boring but still a step up from shadow bolt spam.

I wanna answer from a theorycrafting perspective. Not realistic changes but changes I think would be awesome.
In my eyes, lock currently has 3 raiding builds: SMDS SMRuin DSRuin.
In my opinion there is a design problem here: all 3 branches of the talent tree have a very strong talent near the end but not at the bottom, making it possible to combine 2 of them. The only question is which kind of hybrid you want.
My problem with this is the lack of difference at gameplay. You can be either of these 3, you will do the exact same thing in raids and you'll barely feel any difference in gameplay. Like having 3 hybrid options for warlock on paper, having 1 in reality.
From a theorycrafting POV I'd put these talents to the bottom of their respective tree. It would weaken all warlock specs a lot but it also enables to power-up each of the three branches to reach or slightly exceed the powerlevel of the original hybrids. This would also result in more distinctive lock builds since you don't overlap half of your talent tree with the other hybrids.
What I'd add for affliction: better CoE and CoS, threat reduction for shadow spells and an ability to use 2 curses on a target. Keep in mind, in this theorycrafting affliction locks have lost DS or ruin, there is a power gap to be filled.
Gameplaywise it would be slightly less boring (but still boring). Now you can play with DOT twice as much before shadowbolting.
Another option would be to improve drain life and drain mana so that you feel like you have alternatives to shadowbolt during a raid fight.

+1
I think that drain soul and drain mana could be combined. Drain soul is regarded as a necessary bad: you cast a barely damaging, channeling spell for a soul shard to collect them. Drain mana is barely used outside of some bosses like Moam.
If we combine the two, drain soul would feel much better against mobs with mana, adding a piece to warlock rotation where you start channeling mana mobs with low health and you not only gain a soul shard but also added mana forn the next fight.
