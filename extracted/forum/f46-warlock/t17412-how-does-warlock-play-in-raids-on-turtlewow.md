I play a Warlock, and I am not speaking for all, but I always ask the tank/s if they mind AOE, most are cool with it as long as you give a few seconds to build threat.  In the meantime, I cast affliction on the targets, then for 3 or more trash mobs, it's AOE Rain of Fire or occasionally as the opportunity arises (usually based on the circumstances) I will Hellfire the trash (make sure the healer is aware).  For bosses, I will afflict, then immolate, hen depending on if the target can case shadow damage, I will use modify the affliction accordingly.  Use your Shadow Ward when you know the boss can cast.  I almost always cast Soulfire (costs a shard) when the target is about half health.  I also use buffs to reduce threat (costs a shard).  Make sure you are always working on your gear and stats.  It goes without mention, shadow bolt, shadow bolt...   Lastly, your talent points.  I spec mine mostly in destruction, with about 30% spread across Affliction and Demonology.  I always take my Imp in Dungeons and Raids, Blood Pack buffs all.
