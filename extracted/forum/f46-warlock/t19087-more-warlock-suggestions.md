# More warlock suggestions

## Post 129752 by Forumdweller (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-05T15:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129752#p129752 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

These are things I'd like to see to improve mostly the QoL for warlocks in cc3

First one is gonna sound abit wild to the sceptical mind but bare with me.

Fel domination should be baseline for all warlocks (following this, the old master summoner demonology talent is baked into the spell). However, it's going to be unusable in combat and will not reduce the mana cost for summoning demons. The master summoner talent is now going to be changed so that it makes Fel Domination becomes usable in combat and *does* remove the mana cost from your next summon. This will be very helpful for non demo specced warlocks in both pvp and pve without necessarily changing their playstyle or class tier. It just allows us to access our toolkit, which the original clunky vanilla demon summoning system doesn't allow. Basically in pvp you only use Voidwalker because it's the most versatile pet but it completely locks you out of using the other demons and makes gameplay more stale for everyone. The idea is to give the warlock a chance to summon a more ideal pet before combat begins. I.e you see a rogue running towards you so you quickly switch from felhunter to succubus. Or you just respawn and you quickly need to summon a new demon without being helpless for the next 30 seconds. Again, without being specced demo Fel Domination / insta summon isn't usable in combat.

Next I'd like to ask again that the old spellstone that absorbs magic damage makes a come back... This is my and many other warlocks favourite class-flavoured ability and it's so sad to see it gone from the game   scared_turtle_head

I wanna see something that makes Demonology a unique, standalone spec. Right now there's still no use case for it outside of enslaving stuff in Hyjal or Dire Maul. I propose that it becomes more of a support spec which empowers the existing demons at the expense of the Warlock's own capabilities. Think stuff like talents that reduce the cooldown on Felhunter's devour magic, devour magic can now heal friendly target's if it removes a magical effect, a new capstone talent that allows banish to be used on humanoids (it could last for like 4 seconds in pvp). You could go crazy with it. Succubus can cast lesser invisibility, keeping her target invisible for 10 seconds while she channels the spell.
