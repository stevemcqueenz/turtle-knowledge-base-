## Post 48162 by Grubi450 — 2023-05-31T00:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48162#p48162 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Join a guild that is looking for a warlock and you will get the bloodvine set free of charge if you bring the mats.

Do Sunken Temple once while leveling for the Soul Harvester, it's almost as good as pre raid bis but very easy to get.

When you reach 60 spam Stormwind Vaults since you can enslave the fire/frost imp which will deal more damage then your entire party with his no cooldown aoe attack, just spam health funnel and get your bis trinket, wand and ring (reward from the tome quest for Alliance) as well some random caster gear.

Do LBRS once as a 10 man raid to get the Eye of the Beast quest, start your MC/BWL/Ony attunement, finish UBRS for attunements and Eye of the Beast and the rest of the gear does not matter since everything else is easily replaced in raids which as a warlock will be easy to enter.

Green Felcloth and of shadow wrath armor is insanely good for how little it costs, for example I am still wearing green shoulders of shadow wrath since I didn't get a drop from BWL yet.

Farm gold instead of hunting for every pre raid bis piece, not slacking on consumables will make your raid leaders appreciate you a lot more.

If you are low level you can complete every quest for Raventusk Trolls or High Elves and get an amazing staff, wand and some armor pieces like this at revered <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80541>

At 60 it may not be worth it since you are looking at 500+ gold in runecloth turn ins.
