# Demonology Warlock Improvements/Problems

## Post 141432 by Jumadforum — 2025-07-25T18:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141432#p141432 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there, I have been a warlock player since vanilla and I've always loved demonology. I have decided to post some of my thoughts today as I believe there is a lot of room for improvement from the fun/quality of life/feel of the class. None of these are intended to increase/change the DPS for whatever the devs intend it to be, just make the class feel better to play. People much smarter than me can play around with numbers or whatnot to balance things, I just can provide feedback on how the class plays, to me.

Problem: Greater demons attacking at end of enslavement doesn't feel equal to the power gained. Warlocks to me have always been about things having a cost - health for mana, soul shards for summons, etc. The greater the power the greater the cost. With greater demons, it feels like it misses the mark. Greater demons are better, and so have a steeper cost of needing a reagent but the tradeoff is that they last only last a few minutes as a dps cooldown. (Fun temporary demon concept). However, it was fun for about a day flavorwise having the demons turn on you and attack. It doesn't feel good, especially in a raid setting, when you are interrupted by a rampaging demon. If anything, it should just go back to the twisting nether upon ending. To let it die using power overwhelming near timers end requires communication to the healers not to heal it which feels bad. However, if the choice is that demons stay to attack you, they should be reenslaveable.

Problem: Demon Gate summon is interrupted when taking damage so it feels really bad when you see a bad situation incoming, decide to summon him with the plan to survive for him to arrive, only for the cast to just stop from damage. It should behave like any other cast with pushback instead.

Problem: Greater summons ability states are not saved. Pet ability management feels really clunky. Not only do you have to pay all of the other summon costs, you then have to toggle the abilities on each time you summon them, as there is no persistence (likely due to them being coded not as pets).

Problem: Orc passive and 3pc aq20 don't work with greater demons. Again, likely a coding limitation, but feels really bad when it doesn't work as written (or clearly explained otherwise).

Problem: Staying at max range to damage means you often can't heal your pet because of AI positioning or boss mechanics, which feels bad. This should hopefully be solved in the upcoming patch with the increased range to funnels.

Problem: Stones (self-buffs) can't be used in combat which doesn't make sense, also seems to be solved in next patch.

Problem: Demon health management doesn't feel super good. In part from trying to find the right range to heal the pet while avoiding whatever mechanics are ongoing, and the cost is very steep. Between the change to power overwhelming to base health and heal range this might be fixed? But it also feels like you are trading off damage to heal, desperately trying to keep this temporary thing alive. Healing should feel more satisfying, maybe through increased attack/cast speed while a channel is ongoing or some other secondary tweak. DPS then would be more evenly distributed rather than highs into lows.

Problem: With base ui it is virtually impossible to keep good uptime on unleashed potential pet buff, or to keep track of enslaved demons about to break. For the former, it leads to significant dps losses. For the latter, it exacerbates the aforementioned cost problems of the greater demons.

Problem: A pure dot with a cast time (corruption) doesn't feel good. Immolate feels fine because there is an instant damage portion, so as long as corruption has a cast it should be treated the same. A 1.5s cast for purely delayed damage just doesn't feel satisfying. Affliction could have its own addendum to make sure it balances out with the 0 cast time and maybe no upfront damage, or make it purely like a shadow immolate that can be instant cast with talents.

Problem: Doomguard clickable looks like soulwell clickable, so its not a very obvious clickable thing especially if people already have healthstones and thus requires additional communication. The additional requirement of 2 people, significant prep time, and communciation, although better than having 1 sacrificed, does not line up with the strength of the demon. At its current strength (balanced), it should just be summonable like the felguard/infernal.

Problem: While Doomguard is casting rain of fire I can't cast my own spells.

Problem: Any fight that fears your pet bugs the pet out, and other fights the pet goes underground or otherwise becomes unresponsive until you recall resend to try and workaround. This doesn't feel good, and complicates a very management heavy spec as you are trying to troubleshoot jank in addition to all the things you are required to do in a raid (watch pet buffs, watch your buffs, avoid fire, position for damage, position to heal pet, watch pet health, move pet, make sure pet doesnt hit unintended targets with aoe, do your own max dps, etc.).

Problem: Infernal feels kind of bland as the only pet without any abilities. Even if its fire aura was toggleable so you could walk through areas with passive enemies without aggroing them would feel better, without any real mechanical changes.

Problem: Voidwalker doesn't really hold aggro as you get stronger. Hunter pets seems to hold aggro better in my observation. Some better scaling on the threat generation would feel better.

Problem: You can't stop the felguard from spinning if circumstances around him change which can lead to him hitting unintended targets. If you recall him, he should stop spinning.

These is probably more I may think of later. I can also think about/provide suggestions or straight changes if its wanted, but these are problems based off my experience.
