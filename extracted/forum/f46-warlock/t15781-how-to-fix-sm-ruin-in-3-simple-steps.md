# How to fix SM/Ruin in 3 simple steps

## Post 107593 by Delalama — 2024-11-11T21:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107593#p107593 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

SM/Ruin is undertuned. See [viewtopic.php?t=15780](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=15780) for data

How to fix SM/Ruin in 3 simple steps:

#1. Make ISB proc on 100% of critical strikes and make ISB proc on 15% of regular hits.

This change increases the uptime of ISB on trash. It also "feels good" to know critical strikes apply ISB.

#2. Make nightfall lower/eliminate the GCD of the next SB, exactly like how conflag has a shorter GCD.

The frequent nightfall procs makes SM/Ruin have a clunky feel. Lowering this GCD will make the spec play more smoothly and increase DPS.

#3. Fix Avoidance

Pets are unusable.

 ---

These three changes will make SM/Ruin much closer to a competitive DPS spec.

I think the boat has sailed on SM/Ruin. Work towards buffing affliction, fire lock and demo. The devs won’t play along with sm ruin, they don’t want us to be a shadow bolt spammer again.

Destro Fire lock seems to be the way forward. But it needs a lot of changes to improve the feel and performance of the class.

 1. Potion of quickness the only dps consume that has an active effect for casters is virtually useless for fire lock due to searing pain being a 1.5 sec cast. We need another active dps consume, or searing pain needs it’s cast time increased and its spell coefficient increased to compensate.

 2. Chastise being a haste active buff and only applicable to targets with 80% or more health - could this be anymore penalising to any other class other than lock? When is a lock not life tapping or hell firing. Also haste issue is the same problem as in point 1.

 3. No fire mages, fire mage was nerfed slightly and arcane wayyyyy overtuned. If fire lock was balanced around scorch, it means we were gimped right out of the box. No mage is going to help our dps when the consensus pre patch was that locks were curse slaves to mages. There has to be some sort of incentive for mages to help locks out.

 4. Soulfire not applying amp fire buff if it misses - huge glaring issue. If you’re not giving us hit talents this feels highly punitive. Also the amp needs to be improved 8% is too low and does not provide enough incentive to spam the spell on cool-down. Also consider reducing the spell cast time it feels clunky having to hard cast it during a boss fight.

 5. T3.5 having hit instead of crit, this seems counterintuitive to our class and talents for fire lock, ruin is all about crit yet you force us to go for hit (I’m guessing this was to ensure soulfire hits) but this gimps our ruin talent and reduces the overall potential for higher dps.

As you can see there a lot of problems lock have atm that need addressing.
