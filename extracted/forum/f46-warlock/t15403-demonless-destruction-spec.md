# Demonless Destruction spec

## Post 105265 by Rudyraccoon — 2024-10-29T22:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105265#p105265 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Looking at the upcoming talent changes to Warlock, and seeing Demonic Sacrifice being moved to being a Tier 3 talent, and with the new addition of the Soul Entrapment which gives you 3% additional damage dealt when you have no demon active, it got me thinking, would it actually be a viable spec to play as a Warlock without ever using a demon?
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock? ... VIAoBAA%3D](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warlock?points=AAAAAAAAAAAAAACTDAABAAAAAAAAFAFYRoSBVIAoBAA%3D)

Molten Core fire resists aside, I guess it would be most viable for dungeons and higher raids for obvious reasons, but if you were to use this for levelling and PvP, I don't know, I just thought it seemed like an interesting spec that might be very challenging. Understandably Destruction Warlocks on their own are not quite like Fire Mages since Mages tend to have more utility spells to keep mobs at bay with Frost Nova and Polymorph, while Warlock only has Fear which is a double edge sword of a spell, so it probably makes sense why the voidwalker is the best demon for levelling, as it can tank and hold the aggro away from you.

## Post 105313 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-30T07:57:00Z

Question is: what buttons are you going to press in this spec?
I assume you going to start with Soul Fire (to get fire buff) then immolate, then Conflagrate. If you have free time, then you spam Searing Pain or Life Tap.

In that case, you don't need improved shadowbolt.

I am not sure if it is viable for PvP, because it will be hard without Succubus Charm or Felhunter Dispel.
It means that this spec is for PvE. Then I am not sure if you actually need Pyroclasm.
That free talent point can be invested to Fel Domination, because it is useful and you have required talents to get it.

Next question is: how would you gear up with this spec?
You obviously need to reach spell hit cap. What then?
Assuming that Soul Fire has additional benefits from spell power and Immolate can crit only initially and I have no idea can Conflagrate crit or not. then it is better to go full to spell power.
Crit is not that important.
