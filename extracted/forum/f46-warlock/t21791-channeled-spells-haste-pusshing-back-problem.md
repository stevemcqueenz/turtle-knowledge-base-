# Channeled spells + haste + pusshing back problem

## Post 150902 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-09T01:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150902#p150902 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Pushing back as usual drops cast time -1sec,-0.8,-0.6 .....
But with Channeled you lose your left time and ticks.
 -1 sec = -1 tick, -1.8 sec = -2nd tick etc
So the main and biggest problem. that at least with 1% haste you will lose 2 ticks for first pushing back

For example.  Each tick drain soul = 0.91 sec. First pushing back - minus 1 sec. its full one tick and one unfinished, so its 2 ticks lost. 2nd pushing back - minus 1.8 sec in total, all fine, no additional ticks losing. But if you will have tick per 0.89sec  2nd pushing back will third tick less.
I think pushing back should depends on haste too
