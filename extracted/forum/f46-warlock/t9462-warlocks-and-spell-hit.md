# Warlocks and Spell-hit

## Post 62052 by Maladron — 2023-09-09T11:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=62052#p62052 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a Warlock main who raids enjoys Classic WoW+ raiding I always disliked the idea of being under hit-capped throughout the journey, unless I sacrifice so much of my options to get to 16% hit chance. I am aware that Warlocks have their strengths and weaknesses, however I cannot but feel inferior in raids in comparison to other casters, and having to wear Bloodvine and certain items just to be competitive in DPS. I am also aware that certain items of T1 & T2 will have bonus 1% spell hit, but that still does not cut it for us Warlocks, especially with the Bloodvine nerf. To make this more coherent, I'll compare other classes with us.

 **Mages.** 3/3 Elemental Precision: (Fire/Frost) receive 6% bonus hit from talent. And Arcane spec receives 10% from talent.
 **Shadow Priests**: 5/5 [Shadow Focus]: 10% Shadow Spell hit.
 **Warlocks**: [Suppression 5/5]: 10% Affliction Spell hit only. No additional hit rating in Destruction or Demonology. I'm positive Warlocks' main DPS rotation revolves around Shadow Bolts, which as mentioned, receives no spell hit from talents.

Zul'Gurub Enchants (Head/Legs):
Mages receive 18 spell damage and 1% spell hit.
Warlocks receive 18 spell damage and 10 stamina.
Personally I do not mind this, as it is the same with Rogues/Hunters and other classes, although it would be better if we receive hit chance instead of 10 stamina, as if we are supposed to be tanking. Don't want to make this seem like a rant, so I'll stop here.

In conclusion: Give Warlocks destruction/shadow spell hit chance instead of affliction-spell-hit only.
