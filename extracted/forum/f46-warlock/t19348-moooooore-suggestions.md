## Post 131454 by Forumdweller (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-16T16:26:00Z

Some more spell concepts for ya to make Warlock's less boring/useless. No need to implement them all, some things would perhaps be OP together like giving fel domination & dark pact simultaneously.

Fel Domination: (Spell)  We make this spell baseline for all Warlock's so they're able to summon a demon quickly after respawning or prepare for the next incoming fight. We're literally just opening up their existing toolkit without changing how a fight would play out. You may also want to add an additional gimmick to the spell, adding a 20sec cooldown once the Lock leaves combat so he isn't able to kite/CC and resummon a pet instantly when he drops combat.
![Image](https://gyazo.com/d9fc622ed44527a9a58b50001823da60.png)

Master Summoner: (Talent) This now takes the place of the old Fel Domination talent, allowing warlocks to summon a new Demon **during** combat. You'd probably have to add more talents to Demo now since 2 of them have already just been removed.
![Image](https://gyazo.com/696077394fca3cd6a21e2107dbb62e40.png)

Master Conjuror: (Talent) This is such an awful talent in the current patch because it has 0 scaling. Good in MC when the max hp limit is like 3.5k for locks. Less so in Kara where you're expected to have around 5.5-6k
![Image](https://gyazo.com/a2c25fafa24b0578db7aea2e83f9ac42.png)

Void Calling: (new Demo talent)
This one is spitballing but Demo is so boring currently. Adding perma enslave to this spec isn't going to cut it. It needs some proper utility or 'fun' talents. I propose adding a flavour talent that makes you feel like a badass when you kill a bunch of players in quick succession, summoning a Doomguard for you to wreak further havoc (probably oom and nearly dead at this point anyway). Doomguards are an awesome pet that currently can only be summoned through pure RNG with Curse of Doom getting the killing blow but it has a super nice toolkit (Devour Magic, War Stomp, maybe replace rain of fire with hellfire).
![Image](https://gyazo.com/7d25c9a88146f9c68463582c4a46b129.png)

Dark Pact: (New Spell)
I've said it before and I wont stop saying it. In this new hybrid meta where spellstone has for some reason been butchered warlock's need a new defensive to keep up. Let us sacrifice our existing non-enslaved Demon's for a dispellable mediocre bubble effect that is still outshined by a single priest renew/PW:Shield
![Image](https://gyazo.com/465b657645749a2ba5d21c3fa828466f.png)

Since we're basically getting another voidwalker sacrifice bubble, voidwalkers existing one can either be replaced with an updated version akin to TBC OR give Voidwalker a 3 second disarm or Stun. Or in addition to the sacrifice.
![Image](https://gyazo.com/6cafb55d8ab1a167efedac3ba86c5e8c.png)

Dark Simulacrum (new Felpuppy spell)
Another tool with gimmicks that help us in this hybrid caster meta. Basically a weak version of Dark Simulacrum from wotlk DK's.
![Image](https://gyazo.com/7ba5f13d7f69c2ccd3a0e2aa6052bee6.png)
