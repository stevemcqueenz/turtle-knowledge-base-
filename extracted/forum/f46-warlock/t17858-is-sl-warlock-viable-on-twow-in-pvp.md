## Post 121777 by Dannyp92 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-03-05T15:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121777#p121777 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Unfortunately, SL Warlock got a few nerfs that hit very hard:

 *Soul Link dmg reduction nerfed by 5 %
 *No Spellstone absorb shield
 *VW Sacrifice shield is much weaker now that it can no longer be buffed by talents
 *Fel Domination effect is now interruptable as it now results in a 2 second cast for a new Demon instead of the 0.5 second cast it used to have

On the other hand, they also got buffs:

 *Soul Link increases all damage by an additional 2 %
 *Soul Link can't be dispelled
 *Felstone (and similar) is now a buff and gives a significant vampirism effect (but can it be dispelled?)
 *Your demons' power  scales with your own, which means they will eventually be more tanky than before
 *Specific effects varying with your Demons, so if you have VW out, you have increased healing (benefiting Drain Life and possibly Felstone effects, Healthstone etc.)
 *Access to Curse of Exhaustion, which is very useful to reach an enemy or to run away from one
 *Demon Armor can be slightly buffed, so more armor and HP regen

... so all in all it's a bit difficult to say what the end result is. But I think the weaker dmg reduction, weaker Sacrifice shield and interruptable summoning are terrible.
