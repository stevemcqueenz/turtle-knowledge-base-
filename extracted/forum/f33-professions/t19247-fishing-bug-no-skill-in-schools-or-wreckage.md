# Fishing bug, no skill+ in schools or wreckage

## Post 130752 by Carlito1 — 2025-05-12T18:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130752#p130752 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When fishing in open water such as in Stormwind or anywhere else, I achieve fishing skill up points at the rate expected for my level of fishing.

If instead I fish in pools or schools of fish, I receive no skill+ at all.

To test this I achieved 225 fishing by fishing in open water. I did the Nat Pagle quest to achieve 225/300 fishing.

I have since gone to Stranglethorn and fished over 50 Oily Blackmouth, 15 Stonescale Eel, and countless Iron Bound Trunk and Mithril Bound Trunk, from the pools around the coast.  I am still 225/300 fishing. Not a single skill-up in probably 100+ successful catches.

I asked in guild and world chat about this, and nobody seems to have encountered it. But then again, nobody really fishes.

This is 100% a bug with fishing, I'm very surprised it hasn't been raised before now.
