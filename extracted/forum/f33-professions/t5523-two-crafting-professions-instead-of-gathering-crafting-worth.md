## Post 33938 by Massie2 — 2023-02-03T19:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33938#p33938 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Gathering professions are 100% covered by just having gold. If you have gold, they provide no bonus to your character. And odds are, if you are in a pumper guild, gold is not an issue for you anyway at that stage.

Crafting professions are required by certain items and sets (i.e. literally the entirety of Engineering and stuff like Tailoring Bloodvine set), therefore providing a substantial boost in power level.

WOTLK somewhat addressed this flaw by adding minor statboosts to gathering professions (crit rating for skinning, stamina for mining).

The tldr is really just, if you have gold, you have no more reason for gathering professions.

I usually don't pick crafting profession during leveling, except alchemy and engineering (because of consumables). You can ask other players to craft almost every piece of gear for yourself.
Crafting profession on high level is useful only when you have specific recipes. You probably won't craft multiple items for yourself. So you don't need to gather raw materials all the time.
The exception is crafting writ quests. In this case you can buy mats or ask people to gather mats.

The idea is to trade with other people. Thats why you are not self-sufficient. It is MMO after all.
