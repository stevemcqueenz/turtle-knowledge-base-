## Post 51366 by Oothe — 2023-06-26T15:05:00Z

What if you could learn a tent design that's indicative of every race at certain places throughout the world? So say in Orgrimmar you can get a "Horde Tent", in Stonetalon a "Goblin Tent", Durotar an "Orc Tent", etc - have all the tents functionally the same but different purely for the sake of roleplay aesthetic, similar to how Rogues can quest around for their Disguises. It'd incentivize players to explore more, add immensely to the roleplay community, and flesh out more items in the profession. You could even have them incorporate different dyes to feel different at least, and have them use slightly different materials at most (like using Simple Leather and Copper Bar in lieu), which would diversify the economy even more. I could also see the Horde and Alliance Tents using a "Private's Tabard" as a focus in addition, like how said Rogue Disguises use different costume pieces for their various choices, as a reward for those who PvP.
