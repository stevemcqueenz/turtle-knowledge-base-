Well, first we need to see what your goal is.
With Blacksmithing, you can make plate items, which is great for you since you’re a warrior — it’s useful for crafting your early-game gear and basic items.

If you want to go for something more unique, then you should pick Engineering. It gives you access to pets, bombs, and gadgets that are useful in both PvP and PvE — like dummies, mechanical pets, and lots of other cool stuff. I personally think it’s really fun.

If your goal is to make gold quickly, then you should definitely go with Herbalism.
