## Post 132443 by Thermanshs — 2025-05-21T21:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132443#p132443 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is a major design flaw in gardening right from day I started doing it.
Any 1 lvl player can ninja the final stage of one's production by just carrying Refreshing Spring Water on him. I got it done many times by ignorant players before and had minor losses not worth mentioning. Today was maliciously camped by player already reported thru the in-game ticket system and got threatened /who "my name" and camp that place with 1 level characters and waste all production. If we are talking large scale mushroom farms that could be several hundred gold worth of loss and also several hours of play.
GM's handled the case by just messagint the player and giving a warning.
Fixing the player is not a permanent solution
Fixing the profession design is. Any 1 level character carrying 5 silver worth of vendor items can ninja several hundred gold worth of stuff
I dont think this kind of design is any benefit and very susceptible to trash player behavior like that I experienced twice today. I dont think this should be encouraged and not even allowed. Gardening loots should be looted only by the producer himself or people in his party at worst

Just my 2 cents there to make things fair and decent for everyone playing this game
