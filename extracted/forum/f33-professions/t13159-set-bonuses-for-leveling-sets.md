# Set Bonuses for leveling sets

## Post 88180 by Mistermadigan — 2024-02-19T22:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88180#p88180 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is going to be a general sort of suggestion for professions to get Set Bonuses.

Personally, I like Set Bonuses because they really make you think about replacing one piece of gear with a Dungeon Rare drop which might be better but may make you miss out on a set bonus - I like that feeling of weighing pros/cons of itemization which happens most often at max level and not enough while leveling.

I have 3 excellent candidates for "Sets" that could gain bonuses, as well as my suggestions for them.

Tailoring - Black Mageweave Armor "set" (Levels 36-41)
(2) Set: Increased Wands +5
(4) Set: Increases damage and healing by magical spells and effects by 12.
(6) Set: +14 Intellect

Leatherworking - Tough Scorpid Armor "set" (Levels 39-45)
(2) Set: +2% Movement Speed
(4) Set: +10 Agility
(6) Set: Increase Attack Speed by 2%

Blacksmithing - Steel Plate Armor "set" (Levels 40-45)
(2) Set: +100 Armor
(4) Set: +5 Defense or +5 Parry
(6) Set: Chance on hit (1%) Stun target for 1 sec

I don't think these are too overpowered.  It's just that after the Armor of the Fang - there really aren't armor sets until max level and I think it'd be interesting if there were more item sets.
