# Suggestion: Mail Armor with Fire Resistance

## Post 109240 by RPGnerd (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-21T16:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109240#p109240 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Introduce reputation-based recipes for crafting mail armor with Fire Resistance. As Shamans are evolving into tanking roles, adding these recipes would provide valuable gearing options tailored to their needs. Additionally, this would elevate the significance of the Leatherworking profession, offering players more meaningful crafting opportunities.
