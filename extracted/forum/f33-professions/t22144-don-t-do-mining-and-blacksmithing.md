All professions that produce gear suffer from the same problem. They are flawed at the most fundamental design level.

>>There is no irreversible wear on equipment in WoW.

As a result, there is no naturally (supply and demand) regulated economy for equipment. Demand for equipment is artificially stimulated by new characters. No new characters, no new gear.

The situation is different for consumables. As the name suggests, these are consumed.

This means that on a permanent server like Twow, all prices for gear will eventually settle at a low level, while all the cash that is free floating in that economy can flow into the other professions or other gold sinks. Turtle wow tackled some of the issues with more „enchant“ type consumables but the imbalance remains.
Dunno if my theory holds , because enchanting is still kinda expensive (but less than I remember tbh)
