Came here to make a post about this. Goblins have a shield that's only learned via a recipe that drops from their starting zones, and it sucks.

Aaaaaaaaand that's it. No other shields at all.

As for suggestions - shields are typically made using wood (for the base), metal (for the rim and umbo) and leather (for the handle strap, optional). Shield handles are made of either wood or metal, but I suppose for QoL purposes it would be best to just ignore them and assume they're made of whatever material the shield itself is made. The wood is already available through Enchanting suppliers for wands, although if T-WoW are willing they could add separate woodworker NPCs (since Enchanting wands are only for low level).

As for stats:
"buckler" shields could lean towards agility, as they are lighter
"tower" shields could have extra block
"full metal" shields could have extra armor at the cost of agility (these should be rarer)
"kite" shields would be the standard ones

Tanking shields
Tanking shileds are made from blacksmithing. They focus on stats needed for protection warriors and paladins such as stam, str, def rating and block. The armor and block valutes are considered to be average for shileds on that level. For max lvl there is possibility to make a new BLX spec from armorsmith which could be called Master Shieldsmithing.

Casting shileds
Casting shilelds are made from Leatherworking. Stat focus of this shileds is focused for holy paladins, elemental and resto shamys such as intel, stam, spirit, SP + heal or MP/5. Since they are made mainly from leather, the sileds here have lower armor an block rating then normal game shields and BLX creations. Max lvl new spec is called Protector leatherworking
