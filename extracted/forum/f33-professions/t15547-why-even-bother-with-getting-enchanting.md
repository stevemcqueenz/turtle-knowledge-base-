# Why even bother with getting Enchanting?

## Post 106246 by Rudyraccoon — 2024-11-04T00:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106246#p106246 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Well, here are the things that really bother me about Enchanting.

 1. It's the biggest money sink profession.
 2. Can't sell enchantments on Auction House, have to advertise for it on trade chat
 3. People only want the best enchants, meaning no-one wants the lesser enchants.
 4. Harder to sell when more other people are selling the same thing on large servers
 5. How do you set prices?
 6. Requires raiding.

Personally this profession has always rubbed me the wrong way, like I've never gotten a character to max level and probably would struggle to do raiding, so I get the sense that this profession is probably not recommended for a casual player such as myself, and it requires a lot of salesmanship. I think I'd rather pair skinning with tailoring for any cloth class since some of the tailoring recipes require leather.

## Post 106273 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-04T06:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106273#p106273 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

7. To earn money with enchanting, you don't need to level it.
The only way to earn gold by enchanting is to pick it on your bank character and send him items for disenchanting. You have to install Aux and scan auction for "Shard", "Essence" and "Dust". You also should enable "disenchanting value" and "vendor price", always compare those prices and do action with highest benefit.
IIRC, Dream Dust has highest value, because it is used for high level consumables in demand. Everything else, including Illusion Dust don't profit you as much as selling green item to vendor.

In some other enchanting-related thread was a point "if enchants will be tradable on AH, then their price will go down below cost of materials".
On high level you can spend some time searching for spefic character who can enchant you best enchant.
In the same time, on low levels people don't always have lots of gold and gear is not so good to enchant it with "super best enchant". So I think that top enchants should stay as they are, but low level enchants should be made in form of items like Buckles or Armor Patches. This way, people will be able to quickly buy low level enchants from AH, without search for enchanter.

 8. I also think that all enchants should have required item level. Assuming that low levels can enchant high level enchants, then there is no demand for low level ones.
But assuming that turtle devs added Lunatic challenge and did nothing with regular enchants, I assume current status is totally fine for them.
