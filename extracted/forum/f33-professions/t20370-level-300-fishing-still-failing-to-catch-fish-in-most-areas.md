## Post 140390 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-18T22:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140390#p140390 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The highest bracket of fishing areas (Bay of Storms, Winterspring, Zul'Gurub, etc) requires 330 skill to cast, and 425 skill to guarantee success.

If you have BiS fishing gear (+35 from rod, +5 from line, +5 from hat, +5 from boots) you can get away with +75 lures, otherwise you have to use the +100 lures to guarantee success. There should be +50 from deploying a fishing boat, but I've seen many reports that it's bugged right now.

If by "failed casts" you mean an empty loot window (or rather just the sound of a loot window opening) that's a different issue. You passed the skill check, but some fishing loot tables don't add up to 100%, so you can roll empty loot (happens on Tel'Abim as well for instance). It's technically a successful fishing attempt, it just didn't award you with an item, hence why you don't get an error message.
