## Post 149215 by Blysk990223 — 2025-08-29T20:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149215#p149215 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you're asking about professions in the game, it's not the best, but if I were focusing solely on making money from them, I'd choose mining/herbalism + skinning and sell everything I acquire. You can always focus on killing specific enemies, such as Elemental Earth (Badlands, Arathi Highlands) or Essence of Fire (Un'Goro). Not every character class allows for this type of income, but you can focus on farming solo AOE instances like Mara Princess Run or ZF. If you're a mage/paladin, you can boost people for gold. I've been playing for 20 years and have a lot of experience, so my favorite way to make money is the Auction House (AH) and trading. Buying low and selling high (flipping), observing trends (e.g., potions rising in price before raid night), and monitoring the market for rare materials (e.g., Mooncloth, Black Lotus). Playing on the AH isn't for everyone, as you need a budget to get started. There's also the risk of spending a lot of gold on materials that will ultimately cost you money. However, I believe that if someone has a good understanding of the game, understands how certain systems work, understands the decreasing or increasing demand for a specific item, and can generate an item deficit on the AH, they'll be able to snowball their gold very quickly. I currently have 918g at level 56.
