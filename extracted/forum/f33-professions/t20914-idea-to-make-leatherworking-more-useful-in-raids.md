# Idea to Make Leatherworking More Useful in Raids

## Post 145389 by Singran — 2025-08-14T12:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145389#p145389 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone, and hello dear game designers,

I would like to discuss the imbalance between Leatherworking and other professions. In the next patch, both Blacksmithing and Leatherworking will receive some new recipes, but Leatherworking still feels less effective in end-game content compared to other professions.

Currently, Leatherworking has some recipes for Kara40 and a kit for spell damage.

Could you please take a look at this small issue and perhaps consider adding a few new features — for example, Drums that could give a 5% casting speed boost to the party for a few seconds, or other small bonuses that wouldn’t be game-changing for high-end content but would make Leatherworkers more useful in raids.

I believe this would add some fresh flavor to gameplay and make high-end content more varied.

P.S. Thank you for all the changes you’ve made for us — it’s a great job, and you’re still giving us so much more to experience in a game that’s over 20 years old.
