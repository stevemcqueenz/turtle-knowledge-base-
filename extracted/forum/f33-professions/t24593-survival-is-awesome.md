# Survival is awesome!

## Post 173031 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-29T15:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173031#p173031 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

With all the negativity going around, I'd like to talk about something that I really enjoyed about the most recent patch - Survival!

- Available to everyone - no FOMO, no having to delete established professions.
- Has fun toys, but also useful products.
- Ties into other professions well, even into secondary professions.
- Secret pets.
- Adds a fun gathering activity without having to expend a profession slot.
- New trees fit super well into the established world.
- And all of that actually integrates into the player economy!
Professions have been hurt time after time across the years, most egregiously by the weekly lootboxes introduced with the 1.18 patch. Instead of tackling the complex problem of fixing the player-driven economy suddenly we had materials and finished products spawn out of thin air without even having to level any professions, which utterly ruined some professions and made farming unattractive.
I'm very happy to see Survival go the other route again. Wood was removed from vendors and added to a gathering activity. How lovely! One of my favorite aspects about WoW is riding around, stocking up on resources and feeling you're meaningfully contributing to the community by providing a service to those who can't or don't want to gather themselves - while at the same time also lining your pockets and furthering your own character development.
And not only that, Survival can even boost your herb gathering and mining through special trinkets! This is the correct way to tackle something like Black Lotus scarcity - make gathering more rewarding instead of just conjuring mats from unrelated activities.

Survival has easily been the best part of the patch for me and it is very much a step in the right direction for the server. Thank you for that!
