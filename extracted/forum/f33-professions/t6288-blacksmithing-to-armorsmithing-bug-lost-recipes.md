Tried a few searches through the forums and on google to see if anyone else had this issue. Been playing TWoW for a while and loving it. Picked blacksmithing as my main profession. Hit level 300 and starting to read up on armor and weapon smithing.

Long story short: When I trained and got armor smithing I lost probably 60 or more recipes from the black smith trainers. They show up as a purchasable training from the different trainers.

Long story long: I picked armor, got the quest in Org and turned in the requisite pieces. Excited I picked up my two new items that exist in turtle wow! Great. Went to go unlock a lockbox I got from farming mithril ore and it said I didn't have the level for it. Odd. Tried up to my Arcanite key with the same issue. Looked at my list and noticed it was missing a lot. Went to a trainer and sure enough, a bunch of available recipes were there. All the ones I had purchased before.

Anyone else get something like this before?
