## Post 102124 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-28T20:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102124#p102124 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

High Elf starting zone has some serious Line of Sight issues with the trainer placements.  Try making sure there's a clear line of sight between you and the trainer with no furniture or anything else.  The trainer has to "cast" the spell to teach the profession on you.

Why they didn't just remove LOS requirements from Profession Trainer Teach spells is beyond me.
