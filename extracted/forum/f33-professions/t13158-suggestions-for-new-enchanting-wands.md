# Suggestions for new Enchanting Wands

## Post 88177 by Mistermadigan — 2024-02-19T21:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88177#p88177 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've always found it sad that there are no "max level" wands for Enchanters to make, the max wand Enchanters make is [Greater Mystic Wand](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=14810) - which is "yellow" at 195 Enchanting Skill.  I believe there is enough "room" in the profession to create wands similar to how Blacksmithing is able to make high-end weapons via Weaponsmithing.  I'm not suggesting having "Wand Crafting" as an Enchanting specialty, I'm just "for" the inclusion of new wands.

Issue #1 - New Wands would likely require the inclusion of a new type of Trade Good Wood.  [Simple Wood](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4470) and [Star Wood](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11291) are both used for 2 wands each, and I don't feel that Star Wood is sufficient to cover the higher level wands I am suggesting.
Solution a - Use Truesilver Rods as the basis for max level Wands.  They are already used to create Scepters of Beckoning, which shows a certain... versatility in design.
Solution b - Create a new Enchanting item - Rune Wood - created by Enchanters, similar to Enchanted Leather/Thorium
Ingredients: Savage Frond and Large Brilliant Shard
Solution c - Create new Trade Good item - Rune Wood - sold by Enchanting Suppliers/Trade Good vendors.

Issue #2 - While it is true that there are far fewer max level wands compared to other weapon categories, the reality is that wands are supplementary weapons to casters, as they get most of their stats from Staves or Swords/Mace + Off-Hand.  So, what would new Wands bring to the table?
Solution - Make new wand recipes stronger than ones found in dungeon drops, but make the ingredients costly to compensate.  Make Max Level crafted wands recipe drops as wild in comparison to [Force-Reactive Disc](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=18291/schematic-force-reactive-disk).

Itemization Niche -
The Reality is that for leveling - Rare Dungeon drops should be superior to crafting greens - this is the rule of thumb in the game.  So for the Crafting wands I have suggested, my intention was not to "supplant" notable Blue Wands - but to snipe at uncommon world-drop green wands - which can still technically be better with their random enchantment bonuses.
That being said - there are a couple of "usual" weapon bonuses that are surprisingly missing from the Wand weapon "family".  The first is a +HP/Second effect, the second is a Haste effect - I think Enchanting Wands could cover both of these.
