Hey everyone,

After the dev team allowed us to access the database again I went out to search for the location of the new belt buckles, see if I can farm them.

To my surprise, there was a new one I didn't know about, Plans: Dragonscale Belt Buckle, problem is, the current drop rate is like 1 in 4000 kills.

I went out and killed like 200-300 dragons for it, thinking the drop rate was an exageration and with the passing of days the drop % will change in the database but it hasn't.

At this rate I have to ask, what's the point of such extremly low world drop rate?

There are lots and lots of blacksmith and enchant plans I know about that have around 1 or 2% chance from specific world npc's but I have to admit it's the first time I see a drop rate this low.

Why?
