## Post 164697 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-01-08T17:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164697#p164697 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As you figured out already, blacksmith itself isnt offering too many useful recepies.

Arcanite Reaper sadly is barely worth considering these days, as there are many very easy to get weapons that can compete with it or surpass it.
Axe Smith does however offer nightfall as a craftable weapon, probably one of the best and most usefull crafted items ever.

This is technically a raid weapon, but with how cheap mc mats have gotten due to the weekly caches you can easily get the mats on the auction house.

Armorsmith by comparison offers some mc tier resistence items, fiery plate gauntlets, demonforged breastplte, as well as the decent but massively overpriced enchanted thorium pieces.

Both choices have their draw, i suggest pick whichever you find more appealing. After all, the profession items are for the most part tradeable and whatever you end up not being able to craft someone else can get for you
