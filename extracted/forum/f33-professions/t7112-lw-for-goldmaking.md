## Post 45339 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-10T14:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45339#p45339 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's hard to make money as a crafter on Turtle. The server is very mature, there are people with literally every recipe in the game who've also stopped caring about gold a long time ago. So they will offer their services for free even on recipes that cost 1000g or entail a year-long rep grind.

My experience: I've never seen anyone ask for Dragonscale items or crafts. But the scales sell well on the AH so someone must want the items. Elemental has the very cool and desirable Convergence set, but getting the recipes is hard and/or costly. But I often see people ask for Devilsaur items from Tribal (both ready-made and to-craft). That is a basic recipe that you can start selling fairly quickly. If you can get your hands on the Hide of the Wild recipe you have another major seller in Tribal that most healers will eventually want.

Like mentioned above - the bigger value is always in the ingredients (Devilsaur Leather, Cured Rugged Hides, etc) but I think Tribal has the most accessible opportunities to make some money on the side. But I would honestly pick whichever you think fits your characters or which is most convenient for you (crafting items you might use). I don't think your LW specialization will ever measure up to just your basic skill 250 saltshaker cooldown in terms of money making.
