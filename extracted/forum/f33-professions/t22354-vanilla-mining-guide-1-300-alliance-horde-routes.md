# Vanilla Mining Guide 1–300 : Alliance & Horde Routes

## Post 155215 by Retaz — 2025-10-11T23:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155215#p155215 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Alliance Mining Routes:**

 **1–65 (Copper):[/size]**
- Elwynn Forest: Stick to the outer ring for dense Copper veins.

Dun Morogh: Southern hills and small caves offer good Copper spawns.
 **65–125 (Tin & Copper):**
- Redridge Mountains: Follow the mountain ridges for Tin, Copper along rivers.

Loch Modan: Hills and cliffsides have a mix of Tin and Copper veins.
 **125–175 (Iron & Gold):**
- Arathi Highlands: Hillsides and cliffs, some Gold near rivers.

Thousand Needles: Canyon walls and mesas are great for Iron and occasional Gold.
 **175–245 (Mithril & Truesilver):**
- The Hinterlands: Loop around mountain areas for Mithril.

Tanaris: Desert cliffs and rock formations are rich in Mithril.
 **245–300 (Thorium):**
- Un’Goro Crater: Outer edges, cliffs, and caves for Thorium.

Eastern Plaguelands: Light’s Hope Chapel to Plaguewood circuit.

Winterspring: Mountain ridges near Everlook for final Thorium veins.
 **Horde Mining Routes:**

 **1–65 (Copper):**
- Durotar: Valley areas between Razor Hill and Orgrimmar.

Mulgore: Circular routes around Thunder Bluff for dense Copper.

Tirisfal Glades: Hills and rivers near Brill, low competition.
 **65–125 (Tin & Silver):**
- The Barrens: Follow paths between the crossroad and Fort Triumph.

Hillsbrad Foothills: Hillsides and riverbanks for Silver and Tin.
 **125–175 (Iron & Gold):**
Arathi Highlands: Hills and cliffs for Iron; Gold near rivers.

Thousand Needles: Canyon walls provide good Iron spawns.

 **175–245 (Mithril & Truesilver):**
- Desolace: Cliffs and mountains for Mithril.

Tanaris: Desert rocks and cliffs rich in Mithril
.

 **245–300 (Thorium):**
- Un’Goro Crater: Outer cliffs and caves for Thorium.

Eastern Plaguelands: Circuit from Light’s Hope Chapel to Plaguewood.
Felwood: High-level Thorium nodes in corrupted forests.
