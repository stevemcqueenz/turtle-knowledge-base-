This is how herbalism works on Turtle.

There are a whole lot of potential spawn points for herbs. As a herb is picked, a new herb spawns on a random spawn point.

Therefore, herbs will tend to stay where people don't go, but will be picked quickly along popular paths.

Additionally, if you are doing a circuit "behind" another herbalist, they will pick the herbs in front of you, and the respawns will happen somewhere random ... leaving you walking thru picked-clean areas, wondering where all the Plaguebloom went.

As well, if people are "cherry picking" by - to pick a *completely random* example - leaving the Arthas' Tears and collecting the Plaguebloom, then the random spawn will be Arthas' Tears some of the time and be left as well ... and almost no Plaguebloom to be seen.

Therefore, try and go where most people dont, and try not to be following another herbalist.
