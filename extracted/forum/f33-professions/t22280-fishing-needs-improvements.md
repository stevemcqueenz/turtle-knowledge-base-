# Fishing needs improvements

## Post 154637 by Farehorn — 2025-10-06T20:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154637#p154637 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey

I am a huge fan of fishing in blizz wow and have been fishing also here in Turtle. But the biggest thing that bugs me with this is that it is not rewarding and it is really monotonious. There are actually no rewards but the actual fish after you reach 300 and win STV contest. Sure some of the fish sell well, but its still just... casting.

I really think fishing needs some love as a trade skill. Here are couple of ideas:
 - Quests that require fishing
 - New fish, maybe even uncommon, rare and epic fish
 - New lure types (eg. lure out day fishes during night and vice versa, etc.)
 - Chum, that you can make with survival to throw in water to summon a pool of fish suited to area. Different animal meats would produce different type of chum. Would work nice with survival skill as a support for fishing.
 - New contests
 - Guttable fish which produce items for recipes and contains sometimes items that the fish has eaten, like jewellry or gems.
 - Improved loot from lock boxes (it really sucks currently!)
 - New fish like Deviate fish that can be used to do cosmetic stuff
 - Include fish in more alchemy and other recipes
 - etc.

It really should be revamped. It is now too plain profession with nothing but casting to do.

Oh, and the leveling bug should be fixed.
