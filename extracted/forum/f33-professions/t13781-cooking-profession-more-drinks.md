# Cooking Profession - More Drinks

## Post 93746 by Valari (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-04-29T16:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93746#p93746 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://images-ext-1.discordapp.net/external/OykEJuWZR6ZuoGapg0xTIYLVslht3OB7BcRYu3dKmoM/%3Fcb%3D20170131221206/https/static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowpedia/images/8/8e/Thistle_Tea.png/revision/latest)

Having reached 300 in cooking, I was saddened to see there was not much left available for custom Turtle Wow cooking content.

Something that was briefly touched in Classic Cooking was the inclusion of dinks I’m cooking recipes. We see these in Captain Rumsy’s Lager, Thistle Tea, Hot Apple Coder, Egg Nogg, and Goldthorn Tea. While most of these are alcoholic in nature - Thistle Tea provides an energy boost and Goldthorn Tea provides an alternative to vendor or mage water as a mana replenishing drink.

![🌙](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f319.svg) <https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Goldthorn_Tea>

I would personally like to see more drinks added to the cooking section, which would create more of a demand for herbs than just Alchemy on the auction house market. They could both provide extra mana, and potentially buffs which would be beneficial to mana users. This would encourage more to focus on the cooking skill at large.

Thank you for reading!
