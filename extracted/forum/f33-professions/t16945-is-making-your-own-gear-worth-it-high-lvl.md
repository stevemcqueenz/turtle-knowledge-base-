Can't agree that endgame recipes are money-makers.

On the topic, I think that crafted gear is your catch-up gear if you want to equip in full blues/epics but don't want to rely on RNG of dungeons, but have ton's of gold.
Really good recipes are coming from raids, so strategy "farm recipes and make your own gear" will be harder than "farm gold and buy crafted gear". Effectively, while you spend time on farming recipes, you probably get second-grade epics and fill your items slot just fine.
