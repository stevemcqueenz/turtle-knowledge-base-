# Wolfshead Helm needs an improvement

## Post 133133 by Pheero — 2025-05-26T22:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133133#p133133 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Wolfshead Helm from Tribal Leatherworking needs an improvement. Its stats are lacking.

It has 10 Spirit and 28 attack power for Druid Forms and requires lvl 40.

Compared to that Leatherworking offers "Grifter's Cover" with 9 Str, 13 Agi and 3 Stamina at lvl 38, which is 31 attack power in Cat Form and additional crit chance through Agi.

I get why the Wolfshead Helm was changed. It was too op for ferals at lvl 60. But at lvl 40 when you can acquire it, this iconic item is sadly usless as a better version is already available without the need for Tribal Leatherworking.

The Wolfshead Helm should needs a stat boost: 10-13 Agi at least or straight 1% crit chance.
