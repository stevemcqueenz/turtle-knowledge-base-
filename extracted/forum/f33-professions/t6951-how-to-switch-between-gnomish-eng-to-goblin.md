How to switch to Goblin Engineering from gnomish Classic/Vanilla?
Just north of Steamwheel there is a hut. Go inside and click on the book on the table. It will allow you to switch to goblin. In Classic/Vanilla Wow to learn a new or old engineering spec you need to click on a book in steamwheedle port in tanaris, this doesnt cost any gold or items for quest hand in etc.

Try this:

“ To switch between Goblin Engineering and Gnome Engineering, bring up the “Professions” tab in the “Spellbook & Abilities” interface. Within the “Engineering” section, on the top-right side, there will be a button for your current specialization. Immediatedlty to the left of that button will be a small red circular icon. You can click that icon to remove your current specialization. Important: Do not click the similar icon that is to the left of the green skill bar since that would completely remove your Engineering skill (and not just the specialization).”

[SOLVED] I ran into several problems, but I got them solved eventually.

 *The book in Tanaris that was used to switch specialization doesn't work in Turtle WoW (confirmed by a GM).
 *It seemed the game got bugged when I un-learned engineering, as the game still remembered I had Gnomish Engineering (it was visible in the Spellbook), and Expert rank etc. so it wouldn't allow me to train higher as my engi skill got capped at 75/75.
 *Due to this bug I un-learned yet again, but made sure the Spellbook was cleared of all references to engineering, and that it got removed completely from the "Skills" tab. I also deleted the WDB-folder and restarted the game.
 *Once I got my engineering back up to 200 again, the quest for Goblin Engineering appeared for me in Ratchet

Just remember to make everything you want to keep from the previous specialization before un-learn to swap!
