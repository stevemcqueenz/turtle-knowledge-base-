# "Predicting" Jewelcrafting

## Post 99097 by Moongirl — 2024-07-10T14:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99097#p99097 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone! The Turtle Wow Team has announced a new profession a while back: **Jewelcrafting**! I'm quite excited for this, and I'm gathering as much gems as I could for when it releases.

Now, I still have some questions about what is actually in the list of recipes. Of course, we won't really know until it is out, but I would like to know your opinion on what is likely to appear! Here is my questions:

 **Gem Prices**: Guessing how Turtle Wow likes to make things a little difficult, I think most, if not all, recipes will need at least a gem. The team also said the "Gem Rush" (which doubles chances to find gems) will last *until* the patch. How would this affect the auction house? What about Mining Nodes? There are already 2 other professions that require mining. I feel the smart thing to do would be to add more Mining Nodes, unless the goal is for Jewelcrafters to just buy them off the auction house.

 **Dreamsteel**: Dreamsteel Bars are a type of mining bar that (I think) the Turtle Wow Team added. After looking at the database, I found out it is only used for Blacksmithing. Do you think this can be a chance to add Dreamsteel necklaces or even staves? I quite like the idea of a Dreamsteel Epic Quality Staff... (maybe because my main is a priest.)

 **Soulbound Items**: I think what some people have problems with is this: "Look, I can finally make a <item> for my alt! (Crafts <item>) Time to go to mailbox-OH NO! IT'S SOULBOUND!" (At least, that happened to me before...   turtle_tongue_head  ) I understand why the developers wouldn't want an epic item to sell on the Auction House. But Recipes? Maybe for someone like me, who barely has time for dungeons, recipes can be hard to find. I'm guessing that some recipes will be found in dungeons or even raids. Do you think they'll make the recipes soulbound as well, or just some items?

 **Jewelcrafting Companions**: I have to be honest and say, in MoP expansion, Jewelcrafting got kind of odd because you can make... gem mounts. Gem mounts that fly, nonetheless. With Turtle WoW, however, you need to have some Enchanting to make some recipes. (I'm pretty sure they said that?) Mounts are strange to me, but don't you think a little tiny gem owl following you around will be cute? What do you think are the chances for those?

Of course, this is just what I think might happen. What do you think is going to be in this new profession? I'd like to hear what you want to see or what you think is possible. Thank you for reading, and good luck on your journey!   smiling_turtle_head
