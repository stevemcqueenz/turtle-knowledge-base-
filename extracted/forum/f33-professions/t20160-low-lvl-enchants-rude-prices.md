## Post 141905 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-28T16:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141905#p141905 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You’re absolutely right to feel the weight of the early-game economy. 15g for basic enchantments when you're trying to enjoy Gilneas in the 40s is steep, especially when quest rewards barely cover a few silver. It’s discouraging when you want to immerse yourself, to feel like your character matters in this world — but you’re met with a wall of inflation, high-level-driven pricing, and a player economy that doesn’t seem to make room for newcomers.

That said, here’s another way to look at it:

Turtle WoW isn’t just “retail in disguise” — it’s a community sandbox, and economies in sandboxes take time to find balance. Yes, many players are 60. Yes, the market is flooded with high-level gear, gold, and expectations. But that’s not necessarily a flaw of the system — it’s just a phase of its life. You’re stepping into a world that’s already living and breathing. That can feel alienating, but also — it’s an opportunity.

The economy wasn’t built for levelers anymore, but guilds, communities, and even crafting groups exist precisely to patch that gap. Turtle, unlike retail, gives you time. Time to find people who value helping lowbies, time to meet crafters who’ll enchant for free or cheap just to support someone enjoying the journey. Not every slot needs enchanting right away — and maybe that’s part of the charm. You’ll get there piece by piece, not instantly — and when you do, it’ll mean something.

Also, the cost of enchantments doesn’t have to be an expectation — it’s a choice. Leveling can still be engaging and powerful without every piece of gear glimmering with stats. In a way, that’s the anti-retail heart of Turtle: the stats don’t make the story, you do.

So yeah — it’s frustrating, but also, you’re not alone in that. Many had to wrestle with the same economy, and many have found ways to thrive by leaning into Turtle’s slower, communal pace.

Don’t give up on the world just because the auction house is cold. Gilneas is waiting. And so is the journey that doesn't need +4 spellpower to start meaning something.

What exactly do you mean by "have"?
What specific enchants you were trying to acquire?

Leveling part of the game can be completed without enchants. Only twinks enchant their gear below level 60.
Twinks are often done by high level character, who can afford high level enchants.

If you buy green gear from AH just to disenchant it, keep in mind that some gear is just good and cost more than other pieces. Like "of the wolf" +spirit and agility costs less than "of the monkey" +agility and stamina.

Next thing to consider is that green items can be just sold to vendor. I usually do this during leveling, because raw gold is more liquid than enchanting materials.

Also keep in mind, if raw materials are expensive, then it is more profitable for first-time levelers of the server to get gathering professions instead of crafting.
