## Post 146939 by Metaalverf (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-19T07:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146939#p146939 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Is ammo restricted by the challenge?

Leatherworking has  its own mail armor pieces beyond skill
 200 I think. Most of those recipes drop in tanaris. So it could be worth sticking to LW slightly longer.

Do you feel like using the AH for materials? If not, I would start out with skinning and mining. Get the ore for a ranged weapon and a scope from engi. Drop mining, level engi. Potentially, if you have some gems for jewelry (not familiar with this prof), you could Drop engi and get jc for some neck pieces and rings.

Bags are not a part of the challenge, but if you feel like they should, you must get tailor early on.

Blacksmithing can get you decent melee weapons, even for hunters.
