Between the two, I'd say you should drop Leatherworking.  Yeah, there's some fantastic patterns for Dragonscale and especially Tribal Leatherworking, but most of them, if not all of them, pale in comparison to the Tier 1 and Tier 2 gear from end-game dungeons and raids.  Meanwhile, you can focus entirely on skinning mobs and selling their Leather and Hides on the Auction House.

I wouldn't recommend going for Mining in today's economy.  Yeah, you can make a killing at Auction, but leveling up the skill to 300 takes an absurdly long time due to the fact that there could be "7,100+ players" on at any given time – most of them being under Level 10 or at Level 60.  Competition is simply too high and Mining nodes too scarce.  But then… I suppose the same could be said for Herbalism.

I dunno.  I don't have a character higher than Level 20, right now, so I could be wrong.  I just know that none of my characters who have Mining can get to those sweet, sweet Copper Veins in time.
