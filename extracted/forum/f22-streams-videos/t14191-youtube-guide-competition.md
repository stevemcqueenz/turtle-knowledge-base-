Calling all creators!

Hello all! Today we are launching our first **YouTube Guide Competition**.

To enter:
- Upload a Turtle WoW related guide video to YouTube.

- Include "Turtle WoW" in the video's title.

- Post it to the Streams & Videos forum category.
The Rewards:
- 500 tokens given to 3 winners chosen by the Turtle WoW Team.

- 250 tokens given to 2 winners chosen by the most likes on their videos.

- High quality entries may also receive an invite to the Turtle WoW Creator Program (More information coming soon!).

Submissions are due by June 30th 11:59pm GMT. Good luck!
