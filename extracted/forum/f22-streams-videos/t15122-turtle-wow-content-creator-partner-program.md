 **Calling All Creators!**
We are excited to announce our content creator partner program, allowing us to better reward creators for creating and publishing Turtle WoW content! We on the team love to see all the exciting content produced by our community, and believe it is extremely valuable to our network.

We are additionally hoping to expand our internal media team in the near future, and will use this program as a scouting grounds of sorts of additional members of our internal team. If a role within Turtle's content creation team interests you, your best bet is building a portfolio and getting rewarded along the way!

This is also the only way to post in the **#content** channel in our Discord!

 **Current Rewards**
- **15 tokens** per Short/Reel

- **50 tokens** per full-length video (5 minutes or longer)
These rewards are **doubled** upon reaching 1500 views!

 **Joining the Program**
Interested in joining the Turtle WoW Creator Partner Program?

[Click here](https://discord.gg/yuqVBa8WSQ) to join the program's Discord server and verify yourself as a creator. Please do not join without social media channels created, as the server is locked behind a channel verification. We welcome all forms of content, including art, shorts, and videos.
