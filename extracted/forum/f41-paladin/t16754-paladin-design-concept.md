Since the last CC2 patch, I saw a lot of complaints about several changes to Paladin, one of the main problem is the removal of playstyle, something that shouldn't happen.

I wasn't sure about showing it publicly, I know people on the internet tend to give harsh criticism but from what I saw, Turtle community is pretty chill so, here I am.

The doc is in 4 parts, the first one focus on the baseline changes while the 3 others focus each on one spec.

> **Sylveria wrote: Fri Dec 27, 2024 10:29 pm**
> "Instant gratification"...?
>
>  Wanna talk about how much instant gratification other classes have?

I’d love to, since there isn’t much to go around for most classes until at least level 20. Warriors don’t get anything as impactful and rotation-shaping as Cleave or Mortal Strike/Bloodthirst until 20 and 40 respectively (by the way, I think lowering Slam from 30 to 14 is a mistake too), Mages don’t get proper AoE kiting until 20 with Blink and Blizzard, and Rogues don't have the entire Poison system until 20.

Crusader Strike is as defining to a playstyle as those abilities are, and 10-19 isn’t about introducing fleshed-out DPS rotations to the hybrids (with the possible exception of Shaman, but they were meant to be the offensive counterpart to Paladin's defense and even then it's a basic caster). 1-10 introduces the basic foundation of what your class does and what its roles are, while 12-18 fleshes out your utility and reinforces those roles. You’re *supposed* to feel like something important is missing during those early levels to give players a motivation beyond new places and bigger numbers to keep leveling, and to make the moment of filling those mechanical gaps that much sweeter.

> If it's for you guys, you'd propably roll back Paladin back to vanilla with only 1 button every 10seconds. :P

My man, I already said Holy Strike is fine and I even welcome it. I’m not calling for Crusader Strike to be removed, just for raising the required level (and maybe another eventual rework, Zeal stacks encourage a bizarre momentum that doesn’t feel fitting for the class, but that’s a lot more subjective and I don't have an obvious solution for it, I’m much more focused on the early game impact). Paladin was intended to be an accessible and simple to play class, and that goes double for the early game. They were specifically designed for new players to learn the game with a more passive playstyle and not needing to worry as much about optimal sequencing.

Raising the floor and ceiling for the class isn’t an inherent problem, but doing so this rapidly and this early muddles the pacing and learning curve of the game. Personally I feel like Crusader Strike would be best as a level 40 Retribution capstone, I’m just trying to be realistic with expectations and compromise with the suggestion of baseline at 20.

> I could count down all the active abilities other classes have to push during battle (PRE Level 20).

Honestly I’m sure you could considering there would only be like 3-5 per class

> I really like the paladin, but only 1 or 2 buttons are just not fun to play. so having 2 strikes made the paladin finally fun here on turtle.
>  And sorry, but Seals are just buffs. They are not fun. Not even in the slightest. Class Balancing aside.

The Seals are more than just buffs like the Blessings are, you’re meant to actively use them and switch between them in combination with your Judgments while timing those abilities with your auto attacks. If it isn't worth it to do so and better to just stay with Righteousness the entire time (because yes, sticking to only one Seal is indeed boring) then that’s an issue of tuning and incentive.

I’m not saying no new buttons ever, just that having only Holy Strike for the 10-19 bracket would be a happy middle regarding early game engagement and complexity for players who are still trying to learn. There's a whopping *two levels* between learning Holy Strike and Crusader Strike and those two levels go by *fast* since it's only 8-10, I think it would be better to spend more time with just Holy Strike before unlocking the second Strike. Having access to both Strikes doesn’t even give you more buttons to press since they share a cooldown, it's only convoluting a level bracket that's meant to be simple. If you’re actively using your Seals and Judgements with the rest of your kit then I honestly believe there are enough buttons to press and choices to make early on, especially for someone new to the game. Again, if there’s no motivation to play like that then that’s something that should have been addressed instead.
