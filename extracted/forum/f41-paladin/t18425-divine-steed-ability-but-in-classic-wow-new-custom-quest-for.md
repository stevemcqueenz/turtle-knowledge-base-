# Divine steed ability but in Classic WoW (New custom quest for the Argent Dawn charger)

## Post 125725 by Fortheking — 2025-04-06T12:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125725#p125725 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Here is my proposal for the new custom quest for a paladin. First of all I have to say I foreshadow some toxic commentaries and sarcasm but I don't care. I've created this topic with hope that someone from Turtle WoW developers glances to this my suggestion. Also I have to mention that this is my very first topic ever and I never did it before and not experienced in this that much (so if it's made not professionally I bring my apologies). Also I will be glad if this topic isn't missed.

Here is the issue. I'm not the first one and not the last one who says that Paladin is terrible at PvP in case of lack of abilities that allow you to shorten the distance between you and your enemy. Paladin doesn't have these kind of abilities at all. And in purpose to compensate it I offer to create a new custom quest for a paladin which will be allowed since level 60.
This ability already exists in retail WoW (I'm not sure if it still exists or has been removed because I haven't played retail for a long time but I took inspiration from the Blizzards themselves so I haven't come up with this idea myself). The ability is called "Divine Steed". The main point of it: you're riding your horse in the heat of battle at some particular amount of time and during this time you're protected of being stunned or slowed down and at the same time you're allowed to keep using your regular skills (basically how real medieval knights did it, they didn't fight being dismounted). For those who don't know how it works here is the explanation from the wowhead:
<https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190784/divine-steed>

Now let me share my own thoughts how can it be implemented without breaking Vanilla WoW spirit. The early Blizzards were obsessed with idea of earning something really worthy after dealing with lots of trials and struggles (so this is basically what Vanilla WoW was about — the spirit of adventure). It can be compared to the shaman quests chain.
Here is how I see it: The quest chain starts at the Light's Hope Chapel in the Eastern Plaguelands. During this chain you will be fighting with battalions of undeads and scarlet crusaders. It leads you through both Eastern and Western plaguelands and aftermath brings you right to killing some elite mob or picking some item in the raid.
After proving your devotion to the Argent Dawn you receive the ability (the Argent Dawn charger itself) and the very important thing — relic.
If this ability will be OP? I believe if this ability is binded with wearing a scpeacial soulbound relic earned for completing this chain and is working only with wearing this special relic — this ability will not be over powered at PvP.
How should the ability work: I think it's up to the Turtle WOW game devs but I would like to see it exactly how it was in the official game.

This is what I think the horse itself should look like:
[https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowwi ... 1105124619](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/wowwiki/images/7/7e/Crusader%27s_White_Steed.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/732?cb=20091105124619)

P.s. I ask not to judge this my offer very strictly because Turtle WoW is a Vanilla + project opened to the different ideas. Also I hope at least someone reads it and finds this idea awesome.

And precisely for pvp, this spell is absolutely misplaced in classic. It works in modern wow because all the classes paladin might run at have many more abilities to gain distance themselves or defensive utilities to prevent the paladin from just bursting wm down.

You might say, but warrior has a charge, rogue sprint, shaman ghost wolf and druid has even multiple ways to get into melee range quickly. All except druid can be slowed. Having someone who makes themselve unslowable with blessing of freesom just to rush at you with MOUNTSPEED while packing enough burstdamage thanks to their significantly buffed offensive potential has no place in classic or anything that wants to be classic adjacent.

Modern wow is a fun game all by itself, but if we end up retroporting steed, we have to retroport ever, classes mobility tools. At which point we can just play retail instead
