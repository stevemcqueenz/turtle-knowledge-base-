# (Updated 8.0) Possible improvments to the current Paladin Talent trees.

## Post 112689 by Papum69 — 2024-12-14T13:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112689#p112689 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the current changes to all paladins the Holy paladins can now be in the heat of battle for extra benefits, yet to hit with melee and spells in high end pve without much hit rating is not that good for an Holy paladin wich lacks severly in that department. Also some other ideias for the other paladin trees.

 **Suggestions:**

 **1 -** Now that '***Holy Shock***' has been changed to only heal, leaving only '***Holy Strike***', '***Crusader Strike***' and normal seal Judgement's/melee swings to do holy damage it is not probably worth having '***Sanctity Aura***' in the holy tree anymore for just an +10% holy damage increase. Instead move it back to the Retribution paladin tree (as its more beneficial there for the sake of spending less unnecesary or forced [if using the 'Seal of Crusader' talent point] talent point and by so having more to spent on chosen usefull ones) merged into the '***Improved Retribution Aura***' stating 'Increase the damage done by your 'Retribution Aura' by 15%/30% and increases Holy damage done by party members within 30 yards by 5%/10%.'.
And in place of '***Sanctity Aura***' create an new 1 point talent called '***Sanctity***' that states 'increase your weapons skill with one handed weapons by 2 and increase your chance to hit with spells by 2% also extends the range of you Holy Strike and Crusader Strike to 8 yards'.
This will help Holy paladins with hit chance for both melee and spells and not be in the middle of all the melee's yet also not be in the back with all the casters, instead create an new middle zone for them so they have an increased reach to those that are in the back or further away as well as those in melee range.

 **2-** Add to the '***Benediction***' talent the ability to reduces the chance your Seal spells will be dispelled by an additional 18%/36%/54%/72%/90%.

 **3-** Add to the '***Improved Blessings***' talent the ability to reduces the chance your Blessing spells will be dispelled by an additional 2%/4%/6%/8%/10%.

 **4-** Make '***Blessing of Kings***' baseline for all paladins.
This way Holy paladins will have more talent points to spend elsewhere instead of having to dump a lot on useless points just to get to one talent, also all other paladins will have easy access to it aswell.
Now whit the removal of the '***Blessing of Kings***' talent there remains an 1 point talent void in the Retribution tree for a new talent at tier 3.
It could be an talent to help out the Retribution and/or Protection paladin in what they still lack/needs, possibly a gap closer from an new 1 point talent called '***Crusade***' that extends from the '***Pursuit of Justice***' talent and is an activated ability wich multiplies the speed % amount of '***Pursuit of Justice***' temporarily by 400% = 40% speed from the normal 8% (can be used while mounted and won't dismount you) for 4 sec. with an 30 sec. cd.
It can have an mechanism wich prevents it to be used as an direct escape tool wich is that when activated it will mark an target within 25 yards for 3/4 sec., and the paladin will only benefit from the extra movement enhanced speed (the normal 8% while remain active regardless for if you decide to run away from the marked target during the duration of '***Crusade***') if moving towards that target.

 **5-** Add to the '***Seal of Justice***''s judgement the ability to reduce the movement speed bonus of the target to 0.

 **6-** Add to the '***Seal of Command***''s judgement the ability to do the extra damage to targets that are immune to stun or incapicitation aswell.
Helps making this seal a bit more viable in pve raid encounters aswell.

 **87-** Change '***Precision***' & '***Guardian's Favor***' to tier 1 of the Protection paladin tree in exchange of '***Improved Devotion Aura***' going to tier 2 of the same tree.
This will give accessability to some more much needed hit rating to the now changed Holy Paladin and also Retribution paladin wich now struggles to get to that talent as it previously did not.

 **8-** Make '***Improved Devotion Aura***' more appellative by changing it into '***Improved Defensive Auras***' wich now states 'Increases the armor bonus of your 'Devotion Aura' and the resistance bonuses of all your resistance auras by [20%/40%/60%/80%/100%].'.

 **9-** '***Hand of Reckoning***' ability seems more like 8 yards than what the tootltips describes it, wich is 10 yards.

 **10-** Extende the normal '***Blessings of X***' from 10 to 30 min. and greater blessings to 1 hour.
Helps in a way to conserve mana and also gives a longer period of time while in an dungeon/raid the need to re-bless and to other players that you bless while pve'ing in the world a longer benefit from the blessings while they go there own way, like the buff's other classes give others while pve'ing in the world.

 **11-** Add an interrupt (and closing for an couple of sec that school of magic) with an reasonable low cd wich is only useable in close combate like what was added in retail in WotLK the ability 'Rebuke' .
This helps the paladin not to be so sujectable to mana drain till being oom in an couple of seconds and/or being cc'd till death (paladins may have an divine intervention to break out o it yet you deal less 50% damage while in it and it has an 5 min cd so while it comes back the paladin is almost an sitting duck).
One could argue that the paladin has 'Hammer of Justice' yet that has an 1 min. cd and an simple usage of an trinket can remove it, one could also say an retribution paladin can also have repentance and that would be true but the same as above it is on an 1 min. cd.
So in resume a paladin can have 2x 1 min. interrupts against and trinket removal and taking the other cc one till it wears off and if the paladin was not able to finish the target off (because the paladin now has mainly to build up it's steady damage for an couple of seconds) so after that the opponent just cc's the paladin and/or oom's him till death.

I like this one a lot, although it might make Judgement of Command a bit too powerful, since it already has almost not drawbacks to it (can't be dodged, parried, blocked, uses melee hit/crit, does double damage on crit and acts as a melee swing).

I would make just one adjustment to your suggestion. Make it so the extra damage required the target to be stunned, incapacitated or afflicted with the repentance debuff (I think its names Repent or something like that). This way it would still work in a similar way to what you're suggesting, but would require you to be deep ret to pull it off, and it would limit the amount of times you can judge with extra damage to the duration of the debuff (20 seconds out of every minute).

Might be even easier to implement since the skill just need to check if the target is affected with the debuff or not.
