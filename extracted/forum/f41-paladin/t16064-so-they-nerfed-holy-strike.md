# So... they nerfed Holy Strike.

## Post 109414 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-11-22T18:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109414#p109414 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My hate thread about (old) Holy Strike was a massive viral hit with countless replies and nearly 9000 views.

They massively nerfed this fan favorite ability and yet I must admit I am surprised/impressed that there has been hardly any whining whatsoever from Paladin players.

However, I suppose it was a 'favorite' simply because of how overpowered the damage was -- not because it was fun/interesting to use.

By contrast; Shaman Mains have been crying endlessly ever since CC2 was released.
This is especially ironic given that Shaman was provided with a brand new (fucking sweet) melee attack that hits for at least 700 damage in crappy blue gear.

So is the new/improved Crusader Strike really so good that it's a satisfying replacement for Holy Strike?

## Post 109418 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-22T18:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109418#p109418 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What you have to understand is that holy strike under ideal scenarios (raidbuffed bis gear) "only" ever critted for 3. 5k ish maximum. And that is already factoring in the 2x 15% dmg increase from the doubledipping vengeance. This while replacing an auto attack that would itself deal about 1.5k ish damage under those circumstances

Realistically you would hit only for about 2.5k in good gear on a BG. Any hit bigger than this was due to a bug when it comes to how berserking (and vengeance) is applied to holy strike.

It first applies the damage bonus to the white hit, and then again to the holy strike ability. So with berserking and vengeance you had a whopping
+110% damage bonus. With ampliers like this, almost any ability would have hit hard.

Considering the old holy strike would only give +130ish holy damage and replace an auto attack aside from armor pen and this buggy amplification, the new holy strike/or the instead casted new crusader strike is strictly better than the old one except with this one bug being considered.
Thats for the PvE play

When it comes to pvp aside from the bug, the new crusader strike in conjunction with an auto attack is about equal in value to the old holy strike against any target with 45% or less armor (e.g. everyone that isnt bear)
So ye for the most part, this is a buff to paladin. And all it cost us was an instant ability without cd that would regularly hit for less than 100dmg.

But turns out, pvpers only tend to complain about the flavor of the month oneshot
