There have been quite a few heated discussions about the new seal of the crusader, but that's a bit besides the point here.

In this case, seal of command is the one you'd use for a more normal playstyle with it doing extra damage and making mana management easier. Seal of the crusader is specifically built for proc weapons, you use it if you're more interested in a kind of "weapon collector" playstyle where you'd amass various weapons with strong proc effects specifically in the aim of getting easy procs with seal of the crusader, and even guaranteed ones with its judgement

## Post 42907 by Ravenstone (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-04-24T19:20:00Z

SoR is the most used Seal, regardless of if you are Tank or DPS since it has been overbuffed. SoR will give you more DPS and mana back than SoC due to both the white and yellow hits triggering JoW, as well as anything else like WF/oils etc. SoC only gets used on a couple fights.

New SotC/JotC is basically to be a Nightfall bot.

Imp Seal of the Crusader is now called Heart of the Crusader and it buffs the new Crusader Strike spell. It is the +Holy Damage stolen from the old JotC and put into its own spell to allow it to stack with the other Judgements.
