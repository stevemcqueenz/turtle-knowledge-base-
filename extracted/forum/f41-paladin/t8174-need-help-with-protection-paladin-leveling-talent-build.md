## Post 52593 by Aeternusdoleo — 2023-07-08T03:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52593#p52593 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One option you have leveling as a pally tank is to put points in Holy initially, and go for full Spiritual Focus. That allows you, in combination with the Concentration aura, to self-heal while tanking. You'll need a decent mana bar, and this strategy really starts to kick off when you get access to Seal of Wisdom, Sanctuary and Holy Shield. A shieldspike on your shield also helps. Your damage reflection will wittle down melee enemies like a slow, persistent AOE.

It is much slower in soloing, but in terms of early tanking it works well. Once you're at 5 Concentration, start the protection tree with Redoubt, Precision, 2 ranks of Toughness. Then Sanctuary (this will be your main blessing on yourself) Shield Specialization and Improved Righteous Fury (assuming you go tank stuff). Improved Seal of Justice is a must as soon as possible as it's the only Paladin taunt. Then Holy Shield. Reckoning, Onehanded Specialization. Finish Toughness and Anticipation. And 2 points in Unyielding Faith.

You'll probably want to respec once you go raiding (or whenever class changes hit) but this build will do well while leveling. With Concentration aura on your heals won't ever be interrupted (short of enemies with spell interrupt effects, watch out for those), and you can gain additional aggro on enemies by self-healing mid combat while your holy shield and sanctuary keep damaging the enemies. A fast weapon and Seal of Wisdom on whatever enemy needs to go down first then allows you to regen mana.
Soloing, this build has a slowish TTK in one versus one, but it can stand up to a lot of enemies and elites singlehandedly. Endurance over firepower. The only thing it struggles with is elemental ranged enemies that are scripted to run away (such as those wizzies in Strahnbad) because this build is melee and does not have much in the way of stun-and-hold. Pair with engineering to compensate for this is a good option.
