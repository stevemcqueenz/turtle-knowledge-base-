Stat priority are as follow for survival:
Stam until you can survive the tier you are in (also depend on healers in your guild)
Defense is nice but not mendatory just imagine 5 defense is 1% mitig, its almost as good as 1% dodge for ex.
agility is also very nice, 18 agi is about 1% dodge and 1% crit in raid scenario (coze of kings) so i usualy go 15 agi>5def but i pref the def over lower amount of agi (personnal pref)
block chance until you reach crush cap with holy shield is nice to have (healers will thank you)

Threat stat prio:
Spell power
agi because of crit
strength (but its the worst stat you can get at the moment and its only Attack power)

but realy there arent too many things you need to pay attention to, go for tier gear 1 after the other, and for some mitigation on the side like the helm of emerald sanctum for example. And if you realy want to be the chad, then you farm Wrath of cenarius in silithus, and you go every week to MC to get your thunderfury.
