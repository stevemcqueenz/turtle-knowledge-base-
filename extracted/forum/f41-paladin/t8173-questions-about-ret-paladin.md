Ret does fine technically. A low dps just means you pull less threat. Sure, you won't have as fast clear times if you replace a full bis skilled fury with a full bis skilled ret pally. But are you going to wipe because of it? Of course not. Probably don't go prot to fix the issue, as you're only going to be wanted (And actually preferred) for five mans. And if you actually care about DPS or big numbers and performing well? Just be holy, they're great healers from what I hear and only push like one button.
