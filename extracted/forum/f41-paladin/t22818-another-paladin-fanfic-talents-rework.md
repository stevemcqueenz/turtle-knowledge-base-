## Post 159180 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-11-11T11:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159180#p159180 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Lets start with the ideas i like.

Evil is a point of view is a great talent both flavorwise and gameplaywise if you ask me. As it taps into the more sinister aspects of blind faith, befitting a ret paladin.
It should however be limited to damaging abilities. Paladin should not gain access to a widely useable fear spell. Particularly not in conjunction with any other of the massive buffs that you intend for the class.

Consecration being useful as a healspell would be a great addition to healpaladin, as they struggle from not having enough buttons to press. Making consecration be useful for them would be great. However it definitely needs some tweaking. As "all friendly targets" in some fights can mean upwards of 1k hps per paladin.
Id rather have it say "up to x friendly targets inside the area get healed for half that amount". Keeping its usefulnes while limiting the abuse potential of just stacking the raid in a ball and dropping some consecrations. (Especially since tank and retpalas can come and pick the talent uo with very little downsides).
Or simply limit it to the paladins group.

Searing light might be fun to enable a more spellcaster type paladin. But with the numbers given, it is undoubtably a meme. Which i think is good. Paladin has some playstiles. He doesnt need to be good at range too.
Hereim just worrying about tankshaman syndrome where over time it will get buffed to an unreasonable degree because people adore a meme spec. Creating a lot of problems in the future in other game areas (particularly pvp as was the case with shaman and pala in the past)

Now for the stuff i dont like at all
Bullwark of the righteous
Is hitting people with your shield cool as hell? of course!
Is it also brutal and slightly dirty and undoubtably a warrior ability? Also yes!
With the current threat and dps numbers, tank paladin does not need to pilfer the one cool thing prot warrior has in his arsenal.

Northshire abbey training replacing precision and the removal of imp hand of reckoning leave paladin with wanting 17% spellhit for their taunt. Warrior and Druids both have secondary taunt effects (aoe taunt/mocking blow) in a pinch if their taunt misses. Paladin (and Shaman) dont have that. They need their taunt to be more reliable. And the respective spellhit talents are vital for them being reliable at taunting.

Radiance & the change to divine shield i am absolutely not a fan of. It gives tank paladin access to the single hardest hitting aoe ability in the game.
And at the same time it takes away ita debuff cleansing properties. The best thing abour current divine shield!

Then there is the mess that is conviction into holy cleave into vengeance.
Why am i forced by prerequisite talent to improve them both? These abilities are sharing cd. If they have separate talents, I should have the option to invest in one and not the other!

It also is a 15 pt chain that you basically have to take towards the end of the tree. Ensuring you have to invest far more than 31 points and overinvest into the tree (something that has sadly become all too common with the reworked talent trees)

Another one that ruffles my feather is the buff to imp. Hammer of justice. Base cd is 60 seconds. With the talent it goes to 40. With the pvp setbonus a further 10 seconds and libram of the justicar nabs another 5 seconds.

As 25 second cd 20y ranged stun that lasts 6 seconds... No thanks
When suggesting talents please ensure you are aware of what interactions that are already in the game you are impacting.
