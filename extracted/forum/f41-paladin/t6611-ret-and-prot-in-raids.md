The answer is yes Prot Paladins can tank.
Do they have their weaknessess? yes.
Do they still need more work? yes.
Are they better than they were in vanilla/classic? yes.
Would most still prefer a geared warrior or bear tank on most fights? yes.

Paladin is good for fights like Nefarian because he will do a "class call" and affect classes differently:
Druids are forced into  Cat Form for 30 seconds by  Involuntary Transformation.
Hunters have their equipped ranged weapons instantly broken. You can counter this by equipping a different weapon right before a Class Call comes out, then equipping your real weapon after the call passes.
Mages will polymorph raid members into animals like giraffes, cows and worms with  Wild Polymorph.
Priests are afflicted with  Corrupted Healing, causing the targets of any direct heals they cast to be debuffed by  Corrupted Healing, dealing 44 to 56 damage every 1 sec, stacking up to 50 times.
Paladins receive  Siphon Blessing, forcing them to continuously cast  Blessing of Protection on Nefarian.
Rogues are teleported in front of Nefarian and immobilized, leaving them open to his Cleave and breath attacks. Tank repositioning usually saves Rogues from this call.
Shaman will be under the effect of  Corrupted Totems, summoning powerful versions of the Shaman's various totems that will buff Nefarian and damage the raid.
Warlocks will be forced to  Summon Infernals, summoning two Corrupted Infernals per warlock to start attacking the raid.
Warriors will  Berserk, forcing them into a modified  Berserker Stance, increasing damage taken. After the call is over, Warriors must manually return to their desired stance.

So Paladin tanks don't really get much of a debuff on this fight like Druids (forced cat form) and Warriors (Berserker Stance)

And just as a note. Most things on this server are viable, but thats a very subjective opinion.  Some think that optimal and viable are synonymous so they'd disagree.  Something like ret, are you going to be at the top of the meters? most likely not but that depends on the player, gear, consumables, and who you're competing against.  Just remember that your strength as a pally mostly comes from your buffs.

First things first.
The Video of Tanking Chromaggus has to be taken with a big pinch of salt. Worldbuffs are gone as is the weapon that is likely used to tank him in this footage. This weapon was essential to tanking mobs with damage reduction against holy spells. As it dealt your physical damage as holy.
Since this can no longer be done, the Threat generation for paladin in this fight is atrocious and you REALLY want a druid/warrior tank for chromaggus.

Mana sustain i'm a bit of split opinion. On one hand you are prone to going oom mid bossfight without support. But you are in a 40 man Raid. Getting BoW, JoW on the target and WF support as a tank is not too much to ask for. A Shadowpriest or a Arcane mage are also possible solutions for the manaproblems. As is using Innervate or tea with sugar.

For 5 mans mana sustain via blocking and jow is totally sufficient.

Reploidrocsa surely raids in a different guild than i do. We successfully employ multiple paladin tanks in all raids. Sure we can't tank Fear bosses or Chromaggus but otherwise our threat is sufficient for all encounters.

TLDR: Paladin surely ain't as good as warrior for raid tanking, but i'd take a good paladin over a bad warrior any day of the week.
