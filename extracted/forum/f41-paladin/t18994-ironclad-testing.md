<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129265#p129265 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The healing bonus doesn't show for some reason, but I can confirm the bonus is quite noticeable. It is definitely worth it to wear plate items. At 8,000 armor you should get extra 160 healing power which is easily attainable with a shield. In a raid, it's quite common to be able to reach 9 to 10k armor. You can also drink the potion that gives another 2k armor for 3 minutes. That's another 40 bonus healing :)
