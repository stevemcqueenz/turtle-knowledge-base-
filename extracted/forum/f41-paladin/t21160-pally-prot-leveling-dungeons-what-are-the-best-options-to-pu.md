## Post 147010 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-19T13:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147010#p147010 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My recommendations:
 1. Keybind the skull mark or a mark of your choice and let dps know they should pull that. As long as the lobs run trough your consecration you ahould easily be able to pick em up.
 2. Get familiar with your characters bodypull range. If you walk carefully the need for range pulls is remarkably low.
 3. If you have to have a range pull of your own, items such as explosive rockets or ez-throw dynamite can be used by even non engineers to pull savely.
If you dont wanna use consumables, you can als o use the torch of holy flame from the morbent fel questchain <https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=55/morbent-fel> (but personally i find ez-thro dynamites much more convenient)

As of 1.18 using holy shock for range pull is also once again an option. While dungeontanking its not necessary to be prot to do your job as a tank, spending 21 pts in holy is totally worth trying out. I personally didnt spec out of holy shock until i was raidtanking
