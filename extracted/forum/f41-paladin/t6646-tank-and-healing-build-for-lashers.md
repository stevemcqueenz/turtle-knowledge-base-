Armory doesn't update all too regularly.

In terms of talents. Every baseline lasher builds use this framework: <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin/UU-5ID3> The rest you can pretty much distribute to your liking. I prefer to max out the holy tree for an extra pulltool/emergency heal in holy shock and a crit heal to top myself off with divine favor.

But going for Holy shield if your gear is particularly bad or Pursuit of Justice & improved ret. aura for better killtimes are also options.

You can pull it with pretty much any gear, but of course additional sources of reflect damage or spelldamage improve your killspeed.
