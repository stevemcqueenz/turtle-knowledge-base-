# A New player has lot of Questions about design choice.

## Post 37907 by Enceebe (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-15T20:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37907#p37907 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I am new to the server and only reached lvl 35 on my Paladin so far.
I was really happy seeing that Paladin got some love.   turtle_in_love_head  but

Here are my mayor problems the Seal System in General, Seal of Justice/Taunt and some Talents.   [mad_turtle_head

 1.  In my opinion the mainpoint of Dmg speccs of Hybrid classes was improving full DD classes like Mage, Warlock, Rogues and Hunter and tanks and healer also helping the group like with resource management. But how?

Seals have 3 categories of Spells an active effect on the player, an instant effect on casting judgement and/or a Judgement debuff that can be refreshed with Auto Attacks. Lets be honest the Seal System was always bad.

For example if you need mana you could use Seal of Wisdom and use it for like 30 sec. or cast judgement to turn it into a debuff on the enemie that gives mana and recast a Seal. Or Cast Seal of Wisdom cast directly Judgement than recast Seal of Wisdom. I never understood why judgement consumes a seal, when I have to rebuff a new seal anyway all 30 sec or after using Judgement or when changeing the seal.

To put it simply.   smiling_turtle_head

Step 1 cast a seal
Step 2 cast judgement that insteadly deals damage or wait 30sec or if you cast a Judgement that causes no debuff return to step 1 or if your Judgement causes a Debuff continue to Step 3.
Step 3 Use Autoattacks to refrech the active Judgement on the enemy and return to Step 1.

My Idea is why not a Step 4?   happy_turtle_head

Step 4 Use spells that can provit from the Active Judgement on the enemy depending on the Judgement or allow spells to be cast only against enemies with your or an active Judgement. Or make Spells that consume your Judgement on the Enemy.
For example for Seal of Command to also have an effect on a boss that can not be stunned or incapacitated let it have an increased dmg when a Judgement is on the target.

 2. The Seal of Justice always felt more like it should be its own spell outside of the Seal-System. In retail it was changed in later addons into a long range spell that simply debuffs enemies. Making it impossible to run faster than 100% or flee. So you could also "slow" players that were faster than 100%. (I have heard cats and mages that carry flags and people especially gnomish ones who like being faster then the allowed speed limit of 100 hate this trick).

Why not give Improved Hammer of Justice or Repentance also the effect of Judgement of Justice debuff for 12sec. or make the debuff into its own spell that stops enemies from fleeing and make it impossilble to move faster thant 100% and simply remove Seal of Justice in its current form from the game.   insidious_turtle

Why not make it so that casting Judgement in general or Holystrike "Taunts" enemies when Righteous Fury is an active buff.

 3. The other talent that is in my opinion bad is Reckoning and redoubt. If your equipment improves the chance to get crits from enemies also decreases. And your chance to get Reckoning procs decreases. Like getting better equipment makes you worse. And Redoubt is unreliable. But the chance of getting Crushing Blows stays the same.

Every Mob with a "Weapon Skill" of 15 or higher (all mobs 3lvl above you) than the base defence skill of a Player can cause a Crushing Blows to a player that is 150%  DMG. Why not let Crushing Blows trigger Reckoning and/or Redoubt for 100%. Or dropping to low health triggers these talents.

The end...  dead_turtle_head
