# The many pros and cons of new Paladin and how to fix them.

## Post 107349 by Vidnar (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-10T09:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107349#p107349 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The long awaited class changes finally dropped, for our favorite armor clad bringers of salvation, freedom and retribution and hings look... Wierd.
For those who just want tldr: class changes for paladins feel like someone had an idea how to fix them but someone else forced their crippling for "balance reasons". Like half cooked stew or half baked bread.

Seals and Judgements

The basic mechanic that have seen a bit of tuning for 3 most popular seals (command, wisdom, rigthnouess) but the rest were either ignored (Justice, Light) or just thrown into trash (Crusader).
Command finally got that AP/SP corelation so you wont feel like you shaft yourself going for either or both.
Rigthnouess for spelladin got its talent finally working and is worth taking! Hurray!
Wisdom got its proc chance improved to 100% and buffed so it itsnt just some measly investment, requiring 10 sec to make even.
Melee skills such as Holy Strike and Crusader Strike proc Judgements on the target.
Now for Crusader, Justice, Light and Wisdom.
In terms of damage, they are worthles and im utility not far behind.
Wisdom requires you to stand there and hit the target for half a minute straight to go from zero to full mana but in the meantime you cant atack or use skills as they dont proc seals (more on that later). Who wants to sit in the raid for that long and fall behind on their contribution to killing a boss? Forget about PvP, that's like a death sentence. During leveling it might be okay if you get only one mob on yourself. Dungs will be a slog as mobs will die too fast to even bother, maybe on a boss?
Light is just... Dull. Triggering doesnt effect the group to justify its low health return and now it will be just easier to pop SoW and use flash or two.
Justice lost all its usefulness with giving paladin a baseling taunt (and thank God for that, the talent was awful to use), relegating it to a skill you learn and never use.
Crusader... Oh boy. The previous iteration of it forcing procs sounded good on paper but then every single weapon proc has been nerfed, not only reducing viability of that seal but also hitting with a nerf bat other classes such as shamans and warriors. And now we are back to increasing holy damage target takes with judgement (which you will never use, mana issues for later) or seal that decreases your damage dealt by 60% and doesnt even give you anything substential in return.

Crusader Strike, Holy Strike and holy sh*t

At long last paladin had a chance to something ressembling a rotation. HS doing moderate damage and always having a buff depending on the spec, CS giving you ramping up buffs to boost your melee and casting haste, meler strikes finally refreshing judgements on target, removing ungodly and frankly retarded inconsistent damage of HS ranging from nuke to a wet noodle was a good call.
What went wrong?
The only reason you use HS is to refresh a buff if you have one, your melee strikes dont proc your seals and on top of that they have shared CD. Why. Just why? Cause of WF Totem? That's like a percentage of a percentage of a game. So you just stand there and auto atack like its 1.12 again.
Also, who in the world thought that forcing paladin into melee healer was a good idea, without giving them any kind of defensives against cleave, silences and stuns just didnt thought it trough. And the removal of holy shock damage component? Great even less more buttons to press as DPS, cause that's what hybrids want, less options yet still being hybrid taxed aka paying for nothing.

Itemization, oh how we missed you.

We got them. Finally after so long we, boys in holy plate, got our alternative tier sets, so we can finally be Saviors, Protectors and Retributors!
But holy crap have they butchered them. Holy is mostly okay, being simple int, hsp, stam with a bit of crit.
Prot got something better than Kharazan 10man set so they wont feel like half of the gear they need to steal from warriors.
But retribution? Why in the hell does it have stats like spirit or haste? We already buff our haste with new CS and spirit doesnt scale with anything so why is there? You have less mana and hp in full T2 ret than in its holy variant by almost 1k of mana and hp.

...but path to Redemption is always there.

All the ranting and pointing to flaws is done, now we can start thinking about how to fix that new mess. Give your suggestions, ideas, maybe new info that came out i didnt seen. We are all here to make it work, so in spirit of jolly cooperation, lets get to it, starting with baseline.

Seals, as our bread and butter should not be wasted when you cast judgement OR make their cost reasonable to promote their changing depending on the situation. Maybe add some bonuses to encourage that?

Crusader should be our go to cleave seal. Its damage reduction could be capped at 30% but have us hit two more targets with our atacks (auto, CS, HS). Maybe let Ret cut down on damage penalty as capstone? Have Holy deal, well, Holy damage?

Justice could be CC seal as its effect has chance to stun. Maybe let it work as innterupt if you judge the target with it? Improve running speed while its on? Heck, or let it keep enemy at normal speed like in later expansions.

Light and Wisdom as utility seals in concept are great! You smack someone and benefit your party that way. Make them have some scalability so they dont fall off in later parts of the game. Or maybe have it work on whole group, leaning that way more into support aspect.

And most importantly ALL the seals should have some form damage component. SP, AP, Hybrid, anything.

Holy and Crusader Strike should be separate CDs, simple as that. And more importantly, let melee skills proc seals. As it stands there is no reason for them to not be on separate CDs.

Now, to fix the glaring issue of paladins ranged options (we are the only class in game that Has a problem like this and nothing to compensate) i suggest to give us a new ability akin to flame shock called Righteous Brand.
Simple x sec CD ability for short range that deals damage and puts a dot on the target. And as a Holy, let Holy shock be an upgrade, giving back the shockadin option back, ret much needed ranged option in pvp and tanks a ranged pull.
Other suggestions would be to make excorcism more worth it to use, maybe have its effectivness be that of 50% on other targets?
Hammer of Wrath having an option to be turned instant?
At least that's it for now. Lets see what you come up with.
Deus Vult!
