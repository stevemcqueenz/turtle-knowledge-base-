Good day.
Welcome to Turtle! :)
One of the good things about WoW is that you are truly free to play the game as you see fit. While there will always be a 'meta' way to play, that tends to be rather boring and there are enough people out there doing it already. With the recent changes to paladin, your play style is definitely doable and even encouraged - if you want to be a healer that swings around with a nice 2-hander sword. There are multiple talents in the Holy talent tree that helps with yours heals if you are also actively fighting, so it might be worth for you to check these talents and aim for them.
As for professions, mining and blacksmithing is a nice pair to have as a paladin. While it might not be your cup of tea to go farming, I would recommend to spend a bit of time to level these skills up a bit more - go around just mining for copper and tin (definitely doable at your level) until your bags are full then just spend the time creating the items with the least costs that are still orange or maybe yellow. At your level you could reach about 175 skill level if you happen to also find a bit of silver. I know this could be a bit daunting task as a new player, as knowing where to look for veins could be a bit tricky, but you'll learn that soon, or you could also get some addons that have built-in maps for such things. If you keep your blacksmithing level roughly parallel with [5 x your character level] you'll see that it can provide you with amazing items that will help you a lot in your levelling.
