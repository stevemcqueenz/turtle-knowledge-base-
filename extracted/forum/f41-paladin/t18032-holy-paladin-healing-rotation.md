<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122802#p122802 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hit 60 over the weekend and respec my talents for healing.  I am working on getting gear in 5 man groups.  What is the normal healing rotation?  I have been waiting for tank to get about 3/4 health or so depending on their total health and hitting them with holy shock to get daybreak.  I use Flash of Light mostly but will use Holy Light if they are taking heavy damage.  I then pop holy shock whenever it's ready (and necessary).  Mana is really not an issue in boss fights unless the tank didn't give me time to drink (full mana) or they don't pull the target into a good spot expecting me to just heal through their laziness.

Is that the same rotation most of you use for healing?

You play classic, here we have no "rotation" for healers

With weak gear, you spam flash of light, switch to holy light if you need more raw output. But thats very mana inefficient.
If your gear gets better, you more and more rely on flash of light.

Holy shock is foremergencies or when on the move.

You can use holy strike & judgement if there are healing downtimes, but thats about it
