# A few ideas based on paladin history

## Post 24006 by Gromdred — 2022-08-30T17:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24006#p24006 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As an old player, since 2005, I have experienced the paladin class in its ups and its downs. I have raided all content, including full AQ40 and part of Naxx, back in 2006. The highest PvP rank I got was rank 10, in Vanilla and Classic.
Here on Turtle Wow I am raiding all content as Ret and Holy and I am working my way to rank 10, once again.

I will propose a few ideas for the class, nothing overpowered or broken, just a few fractions of its history and identity, pieced together.

Most players will agree, that prior to 1.8 patch, before the big paladin overhaul, the totally different paladin trees back then, gave a sense of choice and diversity, rather than being a one-way street.

The old talent trees:    [https://classicwowtalents.appspot.com/i ... =1124125_2](https://classicwowtalents.appspot.com/index.html?talent=1124125_2)

Then came 1.9 and brought significant changes, some good, some bad.

My idea is to combine the best of these 2 and a couple more ideas from TBC, as it's been done already with Sanctified Command.

Firstly, Vengeance, Two-Handed Weapon Specialization and Seal of Command would have to move to a higher tier, as they were before, with Vengeance, additionally becoming a 3 point talent.

Secondly, Reckoning would have to move up, to Improved Seal of Justice's place, becoming a 3 point talent.

These changes will once again allow, having benefits of Vengeance and Reckoning for PvP, while having to choose between Reckoning and Repentance.

Also, remove Sanctity Aura and bake that 10% into abilities, while making the talent Improved Retribution Aura, grant some group benefit for all Auras, such as minor % Haste or minor % Damage Dealt for the whole group.

Additionally, give a  Ret talent , which makes Crusader Strike 100% weapon damage with a 6" cooldown, while also removing the debuff. Thus allowing to choose between PvE and PvP version. That would have to be placed where Seal of Command now is.

Further, give a new ability: Spell Ward, which works like Blessing of Protection, but for spells and is affected by Forbearance of course.

Furthermore, a give talent where Vengeance currently is, which will allow to get Spell power and Healing through Strength or Attack Power and will make Exorcism and Holy Wrath Usable (being baseline instant cast) on any target with a shared 10" cooldown.
This could be named Art of War ^^

The last suggestion, will solve the problem of hybrid tax on gear stats. Spell Damage and Healing would have to be removed for Tier 1, 2 and 2.5, aswell as from Naxx trash paladin items and both PvP sets and PvP offparts, granting important base stats, such as Strength and Agility instead.

It will also provide some more steady PvE damage, while adding the element of choice between single target and aoe damage. It also won't be available with Reckoning, thus having the player making a crucial choice for his PvP build.

Thank you for reading.

A word on the hybrid tax, as blizzard originally envisioned it, it's absolute nonsense.

A warrior or rogue only needs 3 stats to improve the dps of all their abilites:
str, agi, and hit.

Hybrids such as ret, enh, even hunter, need both physical and caster stats to improve their dps:
str, agi, hit, spell hit, spell crit, spellpower

For hybrid gearing, they should be given full value of all of these stats. For example, if warrior bis is Legplates of Carnage that gives:
 42 str
 2 crit

Then rets and enh should have access to something like:
 42 str
 2 crit
 42 spellpower
 2 spell crit

Itemize the class locked Tier gear like this, which is class restricted anyway, so that warrior tank thunderfury damage minmaxers dont try to snipe this up

Anyway, I agree with the spellBoP idea, very cool and seems strange this was missing.

There are many rebuttals to almost all suggestions I and others have made to improve the state of ret paladins on twow. Some of them are unfair however others do make good points.

Changes and suggestions all need to consider the following:

 - ret burst in PvP is already quite good with seal of command in its current form. Any changes or additions to rotations and buffs have to consider the fact that if ret acquired any more burst than they presently have PvP will become unfair in their favour, and the devs will never go for it.
If seal of command, judgement of command, and holy strike remain as they are you cannot give ret any ability with meaningful damage on a cooldown like tbc crusader strike because they will global people in PvP.

 - twow devs have a philosophy in place for ret paladins which most of the community agree with: they are a support melee class which does damage by fishing for procs. Any debate or idea around trading some or all support for damage or changing abilities to operate in the same way warrior ones do doesn't fit the philosophy and moves them closer to warrior-lite.

 - any shift towards melee/physical focused damage risks paladins competing for gear with warriors. Even though I personally have no problem with such competition the majority of the community will not go for it.

 - ret is dependant on windfury totem to be viable in raid. Others may find this acceptable, personally I find being dependent on a single ability from another class which makes all other temporary weapon enchants obsolete stupid and unbalanced class design.

Remove seal of command and replace it with the following spell:

Weapon Command
 200 mana
Instant cast

Imbues the paladins weapon, causing it to have a chance to strike for 70% weapon damage as holy damage. Lasts 5 minutes.

In order for ret paladins to have reliable sustain in raid (and sometimes dungeon) settings they need windfury weapon for extra swings to proc judgement of wisdom. The purpose of this change to seal of command is to provide the Paladin with a means of performing this without the dependency of a shamen and specific group configuration. It's operational mechanics work the same way seal of command does however it moves the effect to a temporary weapon enchant.
I want to make it very clear that this is not intended to increase Paladin dps in PvE or PvP , it is a quality of life change which encourages the optimal play style for ret in the game's current form. If the damage is considered too high then lower it to 60%.

I like creative and practical ideas, but I believe we should not forget the vanilla class identity factor.

It would be very simple, to just add similar tools with the ones warrior have (various strikes, aoe, interrupt, snare, gap closers etc), but that no what paladin was about in vanilla.

Swapping talent rows and utilizing existing abilities, seems like the way to go, rather than making vast changes,
which could, potentially, make the class unrecognizable. Remember, untill patch 1.8, you could have Reckoning + Repentance + Vengeance + Seal of Command (scaling with 100% weapon damage, instead of 70%) and no dependendy on Spell Damage.

The only new spell I would very much like to see, is Spell Ward (BoP for spells). Would be very justified, since paladin lacks offensive tools, snares, gap closers etc and is extremely vulnerable to fear and other crowd control effects (outside of Divine Shield). Crusader Strike, Exorcism and Holy Wrath could be reworked and utilized differently, as I mentioned.

The changes I proposed, would make the paladin, still feel like the vanilla class we love, only upgraded.
