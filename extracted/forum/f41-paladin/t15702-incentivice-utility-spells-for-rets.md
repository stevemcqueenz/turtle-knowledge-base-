# Incentivice Utility Spells for Rets

## Post 107148 by Cheruscan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-11-08T19:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107148#p107148 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello fellow Light enjoyers and anyone interested, since the patch hit I have observed many opinions about the new paladin good and bad.

Regarding the bad, SP Ret has been severely kneecaped with the nerfing of key items and Hybrid suffers as a consequence (still ok just feels like a novelty rather than a choice to have SP on our equipment)

Most newer Ret pallies were already drawn towards AP since pre raid gearing is quite one sided for us, except for the T0.5 set but that requires gold and luck with the drops (ie time). This results in many Rets suffering from a condition I like to call „warrior brain“ they simply forget that they play a hybrid class and refuse/forget to use utility abilities like BoP/BoS/BoF or Cleanse.

Furthermore there have been complaints about our rotation being to simple atm, which isnt unwarranted since we press 4 buttons, 2 of which are on the same CD. Consecration isnt used by AP ladins outside of trash and even there it eats our mana away very rapidly, Hybrids (which often rely on WoC) therefor use Rank 1 but this still means a heavy strain on mana if you want to keep the uptime 100%

I propose a simple solution, add a new talent to the 1st row of prot tree that goes as follows

„Your Flash of Light/HoS/HoP/HoF give you *buff1* your next 2 crusader strike/consecration cost 25/50/75% less mana, lasts 7secs. Your Cleanse gives you 2 stacks of *buff2* your next autoattack is empowered with 3pt holystrike, lasts 30secs stacks up to 10 times.“

Maybe like this as a 3 pointer or maybe a bit different as 5 pointer.

This would incentivice using utility spells in our rotation, so more buttons to press, also it makes consecration cheaper to use, which helps SP/Hybrid rets. Opens up more plays for our melee healers  aswell and makes the mana drain of cleanse a bit easier to manage.
