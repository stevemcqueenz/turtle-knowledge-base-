# Protection Paladin Simple Talent Improvements V1.02

## Post 67967 by Partyboyza — 2023-10-01T20:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67967#p67967 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone, I play as protection paladin (Belriose) I will present a simple vanilla like protection paladin talent rework.
The protection spec should mostly use deep protection based skills and talents as it's intended.

I will try to offer some of my opinions on how to improve the build without breaking the game or the raiding tank meta: Warrior->Druid->Paladin
I will highlight the areas where the protection paladin talent tree is lacking in proficiency:

 **1. Damage mitigation**
 **2. Mana regen**
 **3. Small health pool**
 **5. Viable, cheap and simple to use taunt**
 **6. Range pull**

The protection paladin should have around 9% damage reduction from all sources, as to increase the survivability and mana burden on healers and this is how I suggest we go around this:

 1. **Improved Blessing of Sanctuary** (new row 20 talent, prerequisite Blessing of Sanctuary) 1 talent point:
 1 point, a new spell with 10min cooldown that can stack with blessing of sanctuary, increase dmg reduction by 3%
No explanation needed, simple to implement, it also has benefits in raids.

 2. **Improved Righteous Fury**, 3 talent points: add 1% damage reduction from all sources for each level

 3. **Seal of the Defender** (new row 30 talent)  Holy Shield as prerequisite, move ardent defender to the right in the talent tree.
Fills the paladin with holy spirit dealing  2x 30 extra holy damage attacks on weapon swing roughly same amount of holy damage as Seal of Righteousness after each hit:
 -while the seal is active, reduce all damage taken by 3%;
Judging the seal will cause same amount of holy damage as Seal Righteousness.
Judging the seal will add 2 holy shield charges. (to help with Reckoning, see below)
We need extra attacks to avoid using non tanking items that have +2 extra dmg to proc judgement of wisdom.
This solves that issue, and also helps the prot paladin land more weapon procs for extra threat.
With this change the mana problems will completely disappear.

 4. **One Handed Weapon specialization** same place in tree, 5 point talent, and each talent point increases all damage by 1% / 2% / 3% /4%/ 5% and stamina by 1% / 2% / 3% /4%/ 5% while one handed weapon is equipped and a downside, decreases all damage done while two handed weapons are equipped by 2% /4% / 6% / 8% / 10%.

 5. **Reckoning**, 5 point talent, 10%/20%/30%/40%/50% chance on block to add an extra weapon attack, on your next weapon swing.

 6. **Viable, cheap and simple to use taunt** (row 25 talent) (5 talent points)
 **Ardent Defender** : (1 point row 30) Change passive from When you have less than 35% health->50% health, damage is reduced by 25% -> 2%/ 4%/ 6%/ 8% / 10%, and now can be activated to taunt for 3 seconds 3yd range 10 sec cooldown.
No protection paladin in their right mind would miss this after this change.

 7. **Improved Devotion Aura** remains 5 point talent, but now it also increases defense skill by 1 per talent point.

 8. **Toughness** all around good talent point, should stay the same

 9. **Anticipation** somewhat weak, but it gives the prot paladin an opportunity to get some defense.

 **10. Improved Hammer of Wrath** (5 talents)
Reduces cast time by 0.2 /0.4 / 0.5 / 0.6 / 0.8 / 1 second,
only usable at enemies that have less than 36% / 52%/ 68% / 84% / 100% life,
and reduces damage by 15% / 30% / 45% / 60% / 75%,
and increases cooldown to 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 seconds.
And reduce mana cost by 15% / 30% / 45% / 60% / 75%.
Basically our hammer of wrath will become our main range pull with:
Instant cast (to be used while tanking as well) damage reduced by 75%, and a cooldown of 30 seconds (as to not be spammed and mostly used when pulling packs every 30 seconds), and used when the enemy has 100% life.

 11. Everything else should stay the same.

 12. Remove **Improved Seal of Justice**

In conclusion this build we will get:

+9% damage reduction out of which 3% can be redirected towards someone else.
+5% increased all damage
+5% extra stamina
+15 defense skill
+4 holy shield charges from judging **Seal of the Defender**
+50% chance for extra swing on block, on your next melee attack
+2 extra holy damage attacks to proc judgement of wisdom and replace non tanking items with +2 fire damage
+1 on demand low cost taunt
+1 range pull 30yd range, instant cast, reduced damage by 75%

I hope you liked it, this has a very vanilla/tbc flavor to it, and I think it will fit perfectly for Turtle Wow and it's great community.
I'm open for any suggestions. And I will continue to update the build.
If the devs need help implementing this build or altering I will gladly help.
