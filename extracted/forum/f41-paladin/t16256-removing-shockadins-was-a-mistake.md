# Removing shockadins was a mistake

## Post 110786 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-12-01T13:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110786#p110786 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In general, outright removing ANY playstyle or build that some players enjoy is not good design. Sure, it might have been overpowered in some cases, but by outright removing the ability to use Holy Shock offensively, you ruined the playstyle for each and every player that had this build, and of everyone who wanted to try it.
Shockadins were unique in that they were an offensive healer, and other classes can not replicate such functionality easily. So in my opinion, removing this build was a mistake, because you remove a unique playstyle.

My suggestion is to return the ability for Holy Shock to be cast as a damaging spell on enemies to the baseline spell, however, remove it if the paladin has one or more talent points into divine favor:

Divine Favor: "Holy Shock critical strike chance is increased by 6/12/18/24/30%, but Holy Shock can no longer target enemies."

This gives players choice whether to specialize into pure healer for stronger shocks, or keep the damaging option, but not crit as often - which is the main complaint about shockadins - their crit burst.
The amount of damage on the damaging component could also be balanced accordingly, so it's not overpowered, it doesn't have to be the same as the healing component, or even have the same SP scaling.

It is way better to balance something that players enjoy and keep it in the game, rather than removing it outright.
