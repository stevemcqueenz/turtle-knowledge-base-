# Paladin Seals Rework

## Post 63652 by Invokersama (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-13T13:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=63652#p63652 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1-Seal of Command

Every time Paladin hit target deal additional Holy damage equal to 50% of normal weapon damage.  Only one seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time.  Lasts 30 sec.

that mean Seal of command will deal damage in hit not in chance like older V,  making the seal less frustrating and random to play with.

 2-Seal of the Crusader

Fills the Paladin with the spirit of a crusader for 30 sec, granting 326 melee attack power. The Paladin also attacks 30% faster, Only one Seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time.

Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy for 10 sec, increasing the melee attack speed on target by 10% by any attacker.  Your melee strikes will refresh the spell's duration.  Only one Judgement per Paladin can be active at any one time.

most paladin only use Seal of Command or Seal of Righteousness to deal damage Ignoring Seal of the Crusader because the backoff that come with it " less damage 5% every time you hit target" making use the seal more annoying to use compare to other seals,  that why i change the seal to 30% more faster attack speed with granting 326 melee attack power, in intention to make paladin do less damage then fury warrior and rouge.

in my opinion i think Seal of the Crusader should be valuable way to deal damage next to SoC and SoR

Seal of Righteousness scale with SP for spellpower builds
Seal of the Crusader scale with AP and attack speed for pure physical builds
Seal of Command scale in both since it's 20 point seal

i think Judgement of Crusader will be more fitting this way, not only helping the paladin to do more damage but also his team mates.

 3-Seal of Wisdom

Fills the Paladin with divine wisdom for 30 sec, each melee attack will restore mana equal to Paladin's spirit,  Only one Seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time.

(1 spirit stat = 1 mana generation ) so if you have like 78 spirit stat you will generate like 78 mana

i think seal of wisdom need rework to make it worth use it more then it's own judgement or other seals

 4-**Seal of Light**

Fills the Paladin with divine light for 30 sec, each melee attack will heal the Paladin equal to his spirit, Only one Seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time.

(1 spirit stat = 1 heath generation )

just like Seal of Wisdom but instead of mana, it will generate heath based on spirit

Plausible idea for SotC:

Keep JoW/JoL the same as now. Revert JotC back to the original version and remove Crusader Strike. Slight buff to the Holy DMG bonus of JotC if need be. Have the Paladin's SoW/SoL effect the whole group aka heal/mana the group when it procs.

Reduce the DMG reduction of SotC by a little, not a ton, boosting it slightly. Add to it, a 100% chance to proc JoW/JoL and increase the chance to proc Weapon effects. Make SotC the semi-passive regen/utility Seal it was always meant to be.

Plausible idea for Crusader Strike:

TBC version. Ret End Talent. Gains physical DMG bonus from +holy so it all works together. Replaces HolyStrike. Move Rep a level up. I know there considerable QQ about Ret in PvP right now. I don't see this making that go away at all. What it will do it balance out the physical and holy dmg, give their rotation a better feel, and bring SoC back into a more usable state.

Edit: Like I've said in other threads, TWoW likely needs to fix Prot first. Since so many go deep Ret to tank, messing with Ret will impact current PvE tanking meta. We likely want to fix the broken meta and then repair Ret.

To the OPs point, you more or less never use seals besides SoR (even when trying to regen mana/health). The Judges are switched up, which more or less means the Judges are ok but the Seals are all unfinished. Having SoW/SoL proc for the group really hammers home the Group Support role for Ret and maybe even gives Holy a reason to be wearing plate.. Having the TBC Crusader strike refreshing the Judgements on the Boss brings another element of synergy among Group/Raid Paladins and keeps Ret's raid utility high.

Reworking SotC (solo mana/health regen and weapon procs) and JotC (group/raid holy dmg bonus) give those a very designated and purposeful space and removes the awkward and spammy TWoW Crusader Strike implementation. Remember one thing the original JotC does is allow Priests to flex to some DPS if healing is not needed. IMO there is a HUGE reason Smite and SotC have the same icon.

I know some dont like it but I dont mind the taunt on JoJ. SoJ could have a chance to interrupt if the current stun based implementation is simply non-functional,and the IMP talents in Prot could increase the chance along with add taunt on JoJ.

Seal/Judge is a VERY unique element to the Vanilla Paladin and really each should have a use and it would be ideal if every paladin, Skirtadin or not, used them. They should be the Alliance 'totems'.
