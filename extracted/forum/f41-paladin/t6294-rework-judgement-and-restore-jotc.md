# Rework Judgement and Restore JotC

## Post 39654 by Ravenstone (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-30T16:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39654#p39654 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Rework Judgement**
My Judgement proposal is simple. Take the damage from Judgement of Righteousness, and apply it to the Judgement spell so that every time you judge, regardless of your Seal, you deal Holy damage. JoR and JoC would need to be reworked, but I'll get to that in a bit.

 **Restore Judgement of the Crusader**
My second proposal is to restore Judgement of the Crusader and it stems from the fact that Paladins now feel completely unvanilla. The new Judgement of the Crusader is increadibly unthematic and has had its original purpose entirely been replaced by an awful spell in Crusader Strike. The new JotC currently makes me feel like some rare weapons collector, carrying around different weapons just for their special effects, and has already led to a whole exercise of nerfing specific weapon interactions just to force the change to work in a more balanced way. It also pidgeonholed Paladins into becoming Nightfall bots which I think is a terrible direction to take them.

Crusader Strike is OP in PvP. Its spammable, which means it can be abused in AV/AB to prevent anyone from capping a flag in conjunction with Divine Shield for a full 12 seconds. It also provides 5 quick, separate instances in which you can proc Vengence, which in conjuction with Holy Strike double dips to deal insane damage bypassing armor entirely, which means you can run at a flag carrier surrounded by his team in WSG, Divine Shield, spam Crusader Strikes and then blow him up with a Holy Strike/Judgement combo. The only area Crusader Strike works for is PvE, but at the expense of the vanilla JotC, and PvP as described above. Crusader Strike was a mistake and should be removed.

 **Tying them both together**
With my first proposed change, it shouldn't feel as bad to use SotC as you will be dealing Holy damage each time you Judge. This will be a massive improvement already from how it was in Vanilla. Part of the problem around vanilla SotC is that it looks like a DPS seal, but its Judgement is only usable once, so it was much like the other Judgements just applied once and forgotten about. This led to the Seals feeling inconsistent and basically split into two categories, the two damage ones, SoR and SoC, and the rest being utility ones. With this change, it shouldn't feel so punishing to switch to a different Seal during battle as you can still maintain Judgement damage.

I think it still has some drawbacks, notably that only one person would be able to really use SotC/JotC, but I believe it is a step in a better direction than what we currently have. SotC could still do with a rework IMO but I don't really want to discuss that here if possible.

 **Judgement of Command and Righteousness reworks**
Back to the judgements, obviously, we don't want SoR and SoC to be doing double damage, so they'll need to be modified. SoC is simple and can just deal its extra damage when the target is stunned so that its basically the same damage it is now. For SoR I'd propose the Judgement deals extra threat if to help Paladin Tanks maintain single target threat a bit more reliably. I don't think this is anything too drastic and SoR is the tanking Seal anyway.

I don't think the Judgement change really impacts PvP all that drastically from what we already have, although I acknowledge it is a buff. I think the benefits out weigh the negatives there though. I believe removing Crusader Strike is a well deserved nerf to the current Paladin in PvP, though Holy Strike still needs to be fixed from one-shotting everything, however, I'd like it if this thread didn't get derailed too much from Holy Strike discussion though.

Thoughts?
