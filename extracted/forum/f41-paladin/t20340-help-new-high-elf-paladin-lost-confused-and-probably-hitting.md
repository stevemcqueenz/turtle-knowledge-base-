technically prot is bis for leveling with shield spike/eagle gear and dmg reflect but thats is more advanced territory so i would go ret.

Buy a green or blue 2hander  every 5-10 levels on the auction house. Buy "bear" modifier gear with stamina and strength on the auction house, it beats 70% of the spirit-packed blues; it will allow you to tank mobs(stamina) and strength will let you push out heavy damage.

As far as seals go just use the basic seal of righteousness until you get seal of command in the ret tree. Technically you can try to seal twist but that is more advanced and for pvp/raids, this is normal pve so i would just auto attack and crusader strike

Do make sure you get crusader strike( i think you need to kill hogger as a class quest). Save your gold for gear(the greens beat blues and dont cost 15x) dont buy stupid stuff, use tents for rested exp.

Discard the guides, embrace the **reading**.
Talents? Just **read** what these will do for your before you pick any out of all 3 trees you have available.
Gear? Same thing: you should just **READ** what your stats do for you and what you benefit from the most efficient for you playstyle.

Seals? You may not believe me, but the best thing you could do is **READ** what these seals do when you cast them and when you judge them. Otherwise you just rob yourself from the best part of this game - learning stuff yourself.

Most important part; never rush. Do it slow and steady, just like the challenge suggests.
