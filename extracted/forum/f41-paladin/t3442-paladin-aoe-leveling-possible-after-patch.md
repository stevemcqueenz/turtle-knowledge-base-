No real need for the Holy talents. Your priority is Blessing of Sanctuary and Shield Specialisation then down in ret to Imp Retribution Aura. Then you either drop the rest into Retribution for Vengeance for greater AoE/reactive damage in general or into Prot for greater survivability and tanking viability.
Just remember to get an iron/mithril shield spike and make sure to know when to bother grinding out a new shield in a dungeon/investing on AH for something like Green Tower.
Some other toys you can get is the trinket from Crescent Grove's furbolg boss for self buffing thorns, a fast weapon with the Badlands weapon buff and anything with a touch of reactive damage like Naglering or Heart of the Scale.

[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin/ ... J06-5CUU0C](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin/0-5KVLUJ06-5CUU0C) For a more defensive build, using bubbles to bandage.
