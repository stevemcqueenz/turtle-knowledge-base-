People do care, which is why these spells had to be coded to not proc extra effects. A big reason for this is burst damage. Paladin is already one of the burstiest classes as is.

Currently, Auto Attack + SoC procc + Crusader Strike can already almost kill questmobs with a bit of critluck in one go. If you add another Proc to it there is a good chance your questing, farming and pvp encounter end in the first second you get into melee. This is exasturbated once you  get swing increases such as wings of the timelord, hoj or other similar effects. Especially with WF in the mix. That might be fun for a few hours as a milestone to reach, but it considerably dulls gameplay down afterwards.

To give you an example of how much (by current balance figures unnecessary-) power that is:
You could increase the damage of ret paladins seals by 50%(!) and it would still be less strong.

On hit modifiers such as wf & seals dont mix well with instant attacks. Which is why shaman and pala are nightmares to balance, and always will be.
