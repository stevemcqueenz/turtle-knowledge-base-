I leveled my paladins prior to the latest class changes, but I'd go like this:

Retribution up to 35ish
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin? ... AAAAAAA%3D](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin?points=AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAoYAAAAAAAAAAAAoAoFCAAAAAAAAA%3D)

Then switch to Prot and AOE grind 3-5 mobs at the same time
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin? ... AAAAAAA%3D](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin?points=AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAoYFBYAYBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA%3D)

So that your talents would eventually look like this
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin? ... AAAAAAA%3D](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin?points=AoCAAAAAAAAAAAAoYFBYAYBAAAAAAoAoVCAAAAAAAAA%3D)

Most importantly always have improved ret aura and Blessing of Sanctuary up, switch to conc aura if you need emergency heals (which you shouldn't). Keep up Holy Shield and if necessary Consecration. If you're low on mana after fighting a group of 3-5 mobs, fight a single mob with Blessing of Wisdom and judge Seal of Wisdom and attack with Seal of Righteousness.
