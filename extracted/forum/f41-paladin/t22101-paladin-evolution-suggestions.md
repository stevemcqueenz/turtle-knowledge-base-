# Paladin evolution suggestions

## Post 153320 by Dogfight — 2025-09-26T13:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153320#p153320 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Paladin on Twow is in a good state imo. All specs are viable and enjoyable to play, Twow has done a great job here. However, class balance is always ongoing and classes are still evolving. So here are some suggestions for paladins.

 **1. Ranged Pull**
I know, I know, we do have gimmicky items, dynamites, our BFF hunter to pull for us etc, and we make do. But most of the items and dynamites have a 1min cd minimum, which is too long cd when you're chain pulling, and/or shares cooldown with other items you'd want to use in combination with the pull (Sappers i.e). And when you have a milisecond to pull that patrol before it is too late, then it's too late to ask the hunter. It would be convenient to have our own ranged pull. So if Twow decides to give us a ranged pull, here are a couple of suggestions:

 **a) Mark of the Crusader**
 30yd range, 10 sec CD, puts you in combat and generates a tiny amount of threat.
Puts Mark of the Crusader on the target for 10 sec, increasing Holy damage taken by up to 140.

 - CD reduced by 1/2 seconds with Imp Judgement talent
 - Holy damage bonus increased with current Imp SotC talent

Remove Seal of the Crusader and remake Judgement of the Crusader into Mark of the Crusader as a ranged pull. Seal of the Crusader isn't being used very much imo. Judgement of the Crusader on the other hand, is very much used in dungeons, raids and perhaps even by some levelers. Unless you're leveling weapon skill on a target dummy, then I'd say most of us won't miss not having SotC.

 **b) Mark of Justice**
 30yd range, 10 sec CD, puts you in combat and generates a tiny amount of threat.
Puts a Mark of Justice on the target for 10 sec, preventing the enemy from fleeing.

 - CD reduced by 1/2 seconds with Imp Judgement talent

Judgement of Justice is seeing little use and is very niche. Seal of Justice is also niche, but has a clear usecase against casters. So I see this as a bigger sacrifice than losing SotC. You could perhaps make JoJ a 30yd range, but then you'd have Judgement on cd for your threat opener - which is not ideal. All in all a worse alternative than Mark of the Crusader.

 **2. Hand of Sacrifice remake**
Hand of Sacrifice was mostly used for breaking CC in PvP and PvE. Now that it doesn't work anymore it is more or less useless. Suggestions for a remake:

a) Let the damage transfer scale with healing power and reduce the duration to 10 sec. Possibly cap the damage transfer at XX % of the paladins health. Could be a useful tool for holy, prot and SP/Hybrid rets.

b) Remake it into an Argent Defender type of spell. The next attack within 5 seconds that brings the target below 35% health will have 30% of its damage transfered to the paladin instead, up to a maximum of 50% of the paladins health. Or just not have a maximum damage transfered at all for some light comedy when someone actually sacrifices themselves and dies to save others. Numbers as an example and it would need a fairly long cd. Possibly 30min so you have something to do while waiting for LoH to come off cooldown again, right?

c) Possibly let HoS reduce the duration of CC effects by XX %? Doesn't really fit namewise, but it is what we mostly used it for - preventing CC. Could be a standalone effect or in combination with alternative a). Not sure if I like this personally, but throwing it out there.

 **3. Gap closer and better mobility?**
Mobility is obviously a weakness of paladins. Not saying we must do something about this, because classes should be unique, but here is an idea anyways.
In TBC we had 15% run speed increase through PoJ to function as a gap closer and improved mobility for us. How about if Guardian's Favor reduced the CD of Hand of Freedom, but also increased run speed of the target by 4/8% for the duation. Stacking with other run speed increasing effects ofc.
That would make it a utility spell both for ourselves and others.

Let me hear your thoughts.
