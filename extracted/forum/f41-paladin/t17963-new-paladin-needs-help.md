## Post 123166 by petersonj — 2025-03-20T18:10:00Z

As a holy paladin, I like another paladin as the main tank in 5-man groups.  We are able to use multiple buffs that benefit the DPS and each other.  It does take a skilled player to optimize playing a tank paladin when it comes to seals and when to use what.  A good tank will know each encounter and be able to place targets to minimize group damage.  It is also appreciated when a main tank marks targets and keeps an eye on healer/dps mana levels.  These issues are not limited to paladins...
