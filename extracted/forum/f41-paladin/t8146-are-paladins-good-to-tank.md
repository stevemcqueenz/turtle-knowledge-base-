## Post 52427 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-07-05T19:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52427#p52427 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tankadins are pretty crazy on this server, even while still having some shortcomings that will most likely be addressed in the next round of class changes. From level 20 on, they're solid dungeon tanks and can even 1v1 elites at level 38+ once they have Seal of Light / Seal of Wisdom. You will thoroughly enjoy your Tankadin leveling, just be prepared for working harder as a mana tank. There are some tricks to optimize your tanking rotation and behaviors to be mana-efficient, but it is not uncommon to drain on mana by the end of a dungeon pull if you aren't careful.
