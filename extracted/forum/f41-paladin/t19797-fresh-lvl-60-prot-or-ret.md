prot is a much better spec to level since it's more mana efficient than the other two specs.

if you are fighting melee mobs with no fancy abilities (stuns) then you can easily fight 5 at a time, sometimes even more depending on gear and mob level.

the 1h mace from ZG is much better than the epic sword, since it procs a inferior thunderclap, which is great for aggro and also increases your survivability when farming.

prot only struggles against caster mobs, which there are not many of.

also makes it very easy to join dungeons and prot paladin is probably the easiest dungeon tank out there.
