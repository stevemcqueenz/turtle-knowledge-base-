# New Vengeance talent as an example of bad design

## Post 107720 by Khagrim — 2024-11-12T20:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107720#p107720 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the main draws of Vanilla WoW is that hybrid classes are exactly that - hybrid. Paladin's base kit allowed them to fill any role in a leveling dungeon group just by swapping some gear. Talent specialization was a cherry on top and enhanced your main playstyle. But you were still able to heal or even tank most pre 50 dungeons as a Retribution paladin. I know because I've done it both on TWoW an Era. One could argue that due to Vengeance talent deep Retribution could produce threat comparable or sometimes better than Improved Righteous Fury.

And now we come to the problem with the paladin rework. Vengeance now has a built-in threat reduction. And ret absolutely needed threat reduction! But by baking it into Vengeance the hybrid potential of the class is diminished. Which brings the design closer to the later iterations of WoW and further from Vanilla. So if you are leveling as Ret you can't just put on a shield and fill a tank role. Thus limiting options and making tank shortage worse.

I understand it's not a problem at endgame where most people have respec devices, but as a leveling and HC enthusiast I feel that it is a problem worth looking into.

One possible solution would be to add an additional condition that threat reduction only works while wearing a 2h weapon. Similar to Warrior's Defensive Tactics that increases threat in battle and berserker stances but only if wearing a shield.
