# Usefulness of 'adds damage to melee attacks' for paladins?

## Post 150736 by Lunattic13 — 2025-09-08T05:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150736#p150736 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone, new player here. Looking through guides and topics on this forum I found this in an (outdated) guide:

"Paladins should make use of (Equip adds X Damage to melee attacks) as these all trigger Judge of Wisdom (JoW). These can also CRIT based off spell crit and proc Vengeance."

Is this still the case? Is it true that adding +damage to effects can trigger judgment of wisdom and vengeancce twice each attack? And if they can  proc on-hit effects, can they also help trigger additional seal of command hits or such?

Thanks in advance for any advice.

## Post 150774 by Cheruscan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-09-08T11:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150774#p150774 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yes they still trigger JoW and Vengeance, but Vengeance is now active for 30secs so having low crit isnt a dps loss anymore

On hit effects could never be triggered by the +dmg effect.

The only ability that can trigger additional on hit effects is SoR

+dmg effects are still super useful for solo play/dungeons and for low geared 60s

And if you want to spam max rank consecration +dmg triggering JoW an additional time is a must if you dont want to sip teas on CD
