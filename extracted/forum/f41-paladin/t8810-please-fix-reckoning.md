<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56788#p56788 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Reckoning only being able to store 1 extra swing only is extremely stupid.  It’s a 5 talent point skill. If you want to make it 1 swing only, make it a 1 talent skill.

The excuse of needing it to be nerfed to balance for PVP is insanely stupid.  “Balance” really is a slur when it comes to RPG.  Each class is supposed to have its own quirk.  If you had to balance it so badly then I ask you, as a paladin main since vanilla in 2004, where is my wing-clip or rend? Where is my “zealots charge” that acts like a warriors charge?  Paladins literally have ZERO closers. Anyone losing an open PVP fight to a paladin is already playing their class wrong.

The outcome is to have completely ruined the vanilla “cookie-cutter” pally build of AOE grinding.  You’re supposed to be able to pull a pack, consecrate, heal your way through the damage while reckoning stacks, and then release it to mow the mobs down one at a time.

Even if you increased proc chance to be like redoubt, where it procs off any damage, the one swing limit still makes it useless so you can’t AOE grind, because at the end of your endlessly interrupted heal, you still only do one swing, so you’re relying on the pittance of damage from consecration.

If you want to change the meta of paladins so dramatically, you need to keep the AOE grind ability by dramatically increase damage of consecration, and make it so that Blessing of Sanctuary reflect a % of ANY damage taken, as opposed to the current pittance of a chance of holy damage only you block.

Please, why did you break the paladin so badly? Why turn paladin from the C-tier levelers they were to F-tier?
