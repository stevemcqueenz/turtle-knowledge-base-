# Paladin 2.0 Nerfs and Reworks

## Post 52343 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-07-05T06:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52343#p52343 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I thought its time for an updated List for a definitive fix all paladins related issues. i mean not just buffing paladins, this has significant nerfs too.

 1. bubble changed from -50% attacksped to -50% all dmg like in wotlk
This is a needed change to compenaste for paladins higher dmg output on twow

 2. Holy Strike and Seal Of Command double dip formula removed
SoC double dipping is blizzlike, the formula has always been
((70% weapon_dmg  ) + spellpower bonus dmg ) *1.15, but the weapon dmg itlsef is already incrase by 15%, therefor benefitting twice from dmg multipliers on attackpower.
But this has become an issue with the addition of holy strike. The weapon_dmg in the fomula needs to be adjusted to be without dmg multipliers. This will be a MASSIVE nerf to paladin burst in pvp, and full physical ret paladin dmg in pve and should not be done in a vacuum.

 3. Seal of Command procc dmg increased by 5% per rank
Currently higher SoC ranks only incraes the judgmenet dmg. Often times you can just use rank 1 to safe mana.
With this change rank 2 would be 75%, and max rank 5 would be 90%, making it benefitial to acutally go for the max rank as inteded. This buff offsets the double dip nerf not completly but buffers it. Paladin dmg would be more consisten but without extreme double dip burst

 4. Crusader strike rework to be less frustrating
 4.5 second CD added (no more degenrate and unfun cs spamming, fishing for wf proccs, which is a huge nerf in raids, no more cs spamm under bubble which is a huge nerf in pvp)
the 103 dmg it does turned to holy (more threat for protection pala)
added a small sp coefficient (dmg slightly increases with stacks from itself, it will still do only around 150 dmg and now with cd, overall a nerf)
libram and pvp bonus spellpower applied only once on the first stack and not 5x giving paladins hundres of sp from these items (unbalanced design making thse items ridicolous strong, but this will super nerf paladins in pve)
you will need more time to stack the 5 cs because of the cd. paladins working together to get 5 stacks fast will be much intersting gameplay. in pvp it wont be possible to stack to 5 quickly and mega burst.
Lastly I think the ability should be able to crit, everything can crit besides dots. This is a blatant break of vanilla wow conventions on what to expect how abilities work. It woild be OK after the CD is introduced.

 5. Judgmement of crusader restored
Just remove that forced proccing custom mechanics. noone rly likes it, everysone complaints about it, its a balacmne nightmare. its not worth the trouble.
while we could go and find some new effect, the easist and most straightforward is just a revert to its original form everyone is familair with.just make it stack with crusader strike again.
The only reason the mstacking was a problem before and had to be removed was because of the librams and pvp gloves bonus being applied 6 times in total then with 5x cs and 1x from judgement.
after that is nerfed to 1x from cs and 1x from judgemnt (see crusader strike nerf above) it would be totally fine to stack.
This will mean that paladins in raids can stay somewhat competetitive after the crusader strike nerf aslong as you got 2 paladins in raid, one for judgemtn of cursader one for jugment of wisdom.

 6. Holy Shield made use able while silenced
Change holy shield definiton form spell to ability, so that you can use it while being silenced just like holy strike already is and Warriors shield block is too. this will make tanking for paladins fairer. Note that warr sheild block is also of GCD while paladin holy shield requirres a GCD.

 7. Vindication is bugged and does not work currently in pve
This talent reduces the targets strenght and agiltty when procced. the problem is, on cmangos npcs do not have strength or agllity unlike in classic where they do have it. he reuslt is the talent only works in pvp in twow but its not even worth using there.
 --> Make it reduce the targets attackpower or attackspeed instead since npcs do have attackpower. this way it will be finally atleast working in pve, would be a workaround if you dont want to fix the acutalg bug of no agi and streght values on npcs
 -->An alternative suggestion could be that it reudces the targets chance to crit with meele by 1/2/3%, which would allow for intersting tank theorycrafting and help offset paladins inabillty to get crit immune in raids

And that is it. no absurd paladin buffs. a list of small tweaks that will make it more balanced and more fun, both for paladin haters and paladin enjoyers

Now come the more experimental reworks and buff after we fixed the pvp mess with the changes above:

 8. New Argent defender 31 prot talent, 12 second CD active ability: strike your target with your shield dealing X base dmg + 300% block value + 33% sp as holy dmg. triggers redoubt on use.
This allows to keep redoubt up more reliable and with holy shield you can become crush immune, jsut like warriors allways could reliably with their 75% shield block. It also gives paladins anotheer holy trheat ability with sheild to make up for losing vegance. currenly all prot palas are acutally ret talented isntead

 9. make reckoning 3 talents instead of 5, so 33%/66%/100%
This change is needed because twow nerfed reckoning. on classic in can store up to 5 charges and then you can mega burst. on twow you are capped to 1 extra hit. noone uses this talent. non in pvp not ib pve. it is worthless. make it ateaslt cost 2 talent points less to make up for it.you also cant get Seal of Command + Reckoning on twow which finished of this pvp specc compeltely.

 10. anticicaption talent made usefull, changed to reduced dmg by 1/2/3/4/5%
currently it gives 2/4/6/8/10 defesen with 5 points. its a total noob trap. it scales horribly into endgame too, noobody uses it in raids ever.
New effect is to make up for paladins not having defesnive stance which reduces dmg by 10% but also has the tradeoff of lowered dmg by 10%. now its an intersting option for raid tanks to take instead of the shield specc talent that gives mana on the same line.

 11. Improved seal of righeousness talent fixed
Currently it increase the BASE dmg by 3/6/9/12/15%. That's nothing, no ones uses it. Another noob trap because they don't know it does not incresec the total dmg as the tooltip suggests.
Change it to apply after bonus sp like you did for warlocks curse of agony talent and druids moonfire idol. but change it to 2/4/6/8/10% to keep balanced.

 12. Exocism add 1.5s castime, make it usable against all targets, with a 10% bonus cirt chance against undead and demon.
Everytime I step into stratholme I feel like, this is it. This is how paladins should be. With a range Pull and extra holy dmg threat.
Adding it just as it is as instant spell woild be broken thought in many cases. A cast time will ensure it not totally abusable in pvp and we have enough pvp nerfs above anyway. Bonus crit chance on demon and undead preserves it's original flavour.
