# Ideas for fixing paladins

## Post 101550 by Perka123 — 2024-09-20T11:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101550#p101550 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Holy:
Holy Shock: Removed from talent tree and replaced with Light's Mercy
Light's Mercy: Heals your target for X amount of damage, and splashes four nearby allies for 25% of the healing done.
 1.5s cast time, 30 seconds cooldown.

Protection:
One-Handed Specialization: Your one-handed weapon attacks deals 2%/4%/6%/8%/10% of it's damage dealt to nearby targets as holy damage.
Holy Shock: Put it in deep in the prot tree and make it force the target to attack you for 4 seconds (not a taunt, more like Mocking Blow)

Ret: Replace Repentance with Holy Strike. Repentance can become a level 40 baseline spell.
Holy Strike: Reduce Critical strike damage by 20%, cooldown increased to 14 seconds.
Crusader Strike: Cooldown increased to 4 seconds, damage increased by 20%

Bubbles:
Divine Shield: Removed or maybe a deep Holy Talent.
Divine Protection: Cost changed to all mana up to 50%. Increase cooldown from 3 minutes to 10 minutes cooldown.

 **WHY THO?**  mad_turtle
The problem is what every one is complaining about; the burst damage and survival capabilites of paladins in PVP.
By placing Holy Strike at the bottom of the the Ret tree it is no longer combinable with Holy Shock.
"But it still hits to hard". Yes, that why I propose a nerf to it's critting. To make up for it we buff Crusader strike a little bit, to remove it's boring spamming and giving some meaning to it.

Now, Holy Strike is an important attack for Prot pallies, so we give them Holy Shock from the holy tree to make up for it. I've seen a lot of people having hard time adapting to the taunt baked into the Seal of Justice, but instead of completely removing it I opted to make Holy Shock force the attackers attention to help out a little bit.
O-H Specialization I don't really have any argument, I'd just thought it be a more fun talent than just a flat damage increase on single targets. Might not be necessary due to Retribution Aura and Consecration.

To fill the empty spot at the bottom of the holy tree we slap Light's Mercy in there, a fast and powerful panic splash heal worthy of replacing Holy shock.

And lastly, bubbles. I've also seen a lot of people complain about the survivability of paladins with their plate armor, HoF and bubbles.
By nerfing the bubble without taking away it's core principle we make it a life saver that share cooldown with Lay of Hands. This allows paladin to still be able to become immune to damage but at a greater cost.
