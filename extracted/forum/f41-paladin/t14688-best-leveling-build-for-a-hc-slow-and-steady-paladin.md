## Post 106484 by majorunpleasan — 2024-11-05T08:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106484#p106484 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For a solo, Hardcore Paladin with Glyph of the Turtle, go Protection for tankiness or Holy for healing and sustain. For professions, take Mining and Blacksmithing for crafted gear or Herbalism and Alchemy for potions and elixirs. Retribution is an option if you’re confident with faster kills but be cautious. Adjust as needed if AoE farming becomes critical at higher levels. Play carefully!
