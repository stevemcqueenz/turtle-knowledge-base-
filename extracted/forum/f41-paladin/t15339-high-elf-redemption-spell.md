## Post 149255 by Jeanierose — 2025-08-30T00:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149255#p149255 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Pick up the quest "Tome of Divinity" from the Paladin trainer in Goldshire.
He sends you to a trainer in the Cathedral.
He gives you the Tome of Divinity.
Open your bag, click on the Tome of Divinity: "This item begins a quest."
Talk to the Cathedral trainer (whose name I can't remember.)
He gives you a quest to find Stephanie Turner in the Trade District. (Have 10 linen cloth in your bag.)
Go back to the trainer, click, he gives you a Symbol, talk to the wounded man near the altar.
THEN go find the man you need to resurrect at Hero's Vigil.
Fight the bad guys there until you get the Script.
Return the Script to the wounded man in the Cathedral.
Talk to the trainer. You get Redemption.
Level 13, by the way. Maybe 12, I don't know. Took me forever to figure this out.
