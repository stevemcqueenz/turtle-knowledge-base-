## Post 153775 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-30T12:13:00Z

Prot pally is really all about stamina and strength. Once you hit level 20 and pick up Seal of Wisdom, mana just isn’t the issue people make it out to be. You’ll regen enough through SoW that you don’t need to stack Spirit. If it’s on a piece you’re already using, that’s fine, but it’s not something you should go out of your way to hunt down.

What actually pays off is stamina for survivability and strength for threat and block value. Some folks like going heavier into intellect for a bigger mana pool, and that’s not wrong, but it’s more of a comfort choice than a necessity. By design, your kit already handles your mana problems, so what really matters is staying alive and holding aggro.

And if you happen to come across gear with spell damage, that’s worth considering too. It’ll give your threat output a nice boost, which makes life easier for you and your group.

It directly boosts the effectiveness of several key spells that form  your threat generation and utility. Consecration, for example, scales with Spell Power, making it far more potent for AoE tanking and holding packs. Holy Shield benefits as well, not just by increasing your block chance, but by amplifying the reflective damage you deal every time you block, which translates into passive threat. Holy Strike is another important ability that scales with Spell Power, offering reliable single-target burst and synergizing well with a caster-oriented tank setup. Seal/Judgement of Righteousness also scale with Spell Power, which is especially valuable since Prot Paladins don’t rely heavily on weapon damage. While Crusader Strike does have some Spell Power scaling, I wouldn’t recommend using it in a Prot build as it's a mana waste and doesn’t offer the same threat or utility as the other abilities mentioned.

That’s not completely true. While it’s right that your main aggro tools (SoR, Judgement, Consecration) scale with spell damage, strength still has an indirect impact. It increases your block damage, and block damage directly boosts the damage from Holy Shield.

I tested this swapping between strength-heavy gear and gear without it, and you can see the difference, every block with Holy Shield hits harder when your block value is higher. Since that’s holy damage, it benefits from Righteous Fury as well, which means more reliable threat when you’re tanking packs.

So no, strength isn’t on the same level as spell damage for raw threat scaling, but it’s not useless either. It helps with Holy Shield, which is a big part of holding aggro as a prot pally.

Go Mail/later Plate with Stam, most of the time you will got Strengh, sometimes there are "...of the Eagle" Greens with Stam/Int, take them. But Stamina and high Armor is all you need. Later you will find Items with +Defense or +Avoidance (Parry/Dodge/Block).
Later on Plate there is +Spelldamage and in the late 60s Dungeons you will finally find Items with Stamina + Intelligence + Spelldamage + Defense.

Untill then just run for Plate with Stam/Def-Stats: Survivability is more important than Threat.

With no Int you will have a poor Manapool, so Regeneration through Seal and Judgement of Wisdom is important. Therefore allways use fast weapons (with +spelldam as a nice to have)! Weapon Damage is not important for threat, only Crusader Strike scales with weapon damage. But try to keep Zeal up! Faster hitting=more Mana! Thats why +attack speed is also a nice stat. And try to keep at least one item on you with "adds +dmg to your attack"(<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=16615> or <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=7711>). This extra damage on attack triggers Judgement of wisdom! Game changing!

Another notable thing is <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17774> and other "when struck" items. You got hit a lot.
