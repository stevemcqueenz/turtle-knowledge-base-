> Retribution Repentance: Repent effect applied on immune creatures now scales with 8% of your Attack Power per tick.
>  Seal of Command: Procs per minute increased from 7 to 9.
>  Crusader Strike: Rank 5's weapon damage percentage increased from 90% to 100%.
>
>  Developer Comment: Retribution paladins that leaned into attack power have been underperforming for the last few patches. These three changes should bring them closer in line to their hybrid counterparts.

These are the patch notes from the current class change logs. Definitely some welcome buffs to AP ret, but the last line says the goal should be to bring AP in line with their hybrid counterparts, but ignores the fact that hybrid dps is close to dead last on the meters in top end raids right now. If you look at the rankings for k40 dps, aside from the one obvious outlier ret dps isn't even in the top 350 spots. Other than priest dps and MM hunter (both getting some nice buffs), it's the least represented class on the damage charts. There are only 2 pallys total in the top 350 spots.

I know a lot of people think hybrid dps classes should not be up near the top of the dps charts, but shaman, warrior and druid vastly out perform ret right now (along with almost everyone else). If it's not too late to make class changes, can the devs take a look at ret right now and give them some love?

The real issue here is that Retribution isn't suffering from 'low numbers' - it’s suffering from disruptive and anti-synergistic mechanics. Tweaking a few percentages here and there won't change the fact that the spec is fundamentally fighting itself.

I'm relatively new to Turtle WoW, but I've spent an absurd amount of time on WoW in the past 19 or so years. Looking at the current state of the class, even with the custom changes, this feels like an underpowered version of the TBC ret pala, which had its fair share of troubles even back then.

Beyond the numbers and the horrendous amount of micro-timers we're forced to watch and maintain, the combat flow feels sluggish, heavy, and definitely not engaging in terms of fun. We are stuck with an archaic resource management system and a lack of real synergy between our strikes, making the gameplay feel cumbersome rather than rewarding. We don’t need 'more' of a clunky system; we need a few surgical mechanical tweaks to make the ret pala actually enjoyable to play and, ultimately, more viable for those of us pushing the AP-based holy fighter.

I’ll probably open a dedicated thread soon to share some of my ideas on how to address these bottlenecks, hoping for some healthy community discussion/constructive criticism

I checked these numbers some time ago and you're dead on correct about no ret DPS being in top 350. Kara40 heavily favors classes with cleave abilities. Ret paladin not only doesn't have cleave but it also can't afford to change target often because of needing wisdom to sustain mana, this goes double for AP ret which has even less intellect and mana sustain.

Numbers wise AP ret can pull MAYBE 1750 DPS as of now on Patchwerk which is quite a lot behind of other classes, and on multi target fights (Majority of Kara40) it is dumpstered hard.

In addition to doing poorly, it also offers little utility. Enhancement shaman for example brings totems, better totems than their restoration spec and bloodlust on top of that. There is no utility a ret paladin brings to the raid that a holy paladin doesn't bring.

Ret paladin definitely needs some number adjustments, could be in several ways. Either it does really good single target DPS but we are fine with it being bad on multi target fights, or we give it some sort of cleave ability, or as a third option we make them useful to have in a raid group. This could be done by turning sanctity aura from 10% holy damage increase to 3% damage increase to everyone in the group similar to the feral druid buff or trueshot aura.
