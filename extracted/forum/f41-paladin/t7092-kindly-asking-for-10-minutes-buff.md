Hi,

I really, really, really enjoy what you Turtle guys did with this version of vanilla. :) It fixed soooooooo many issues that I hated in WoW and kept all of the good stuff.

But I beg you: pleeeeeeeease, make the regular pala buff last 10 minutes. Even Blizz realized - even though it was very late - that the 5 min version is not only exhausting, forgettable and stressful when you have to buff according to skill trees and not only classes. So I hereby ask you to implement that. I cannot imagine that this should be a hard thing to do or destroy balancing or so. Everyone would benefit from this: less forgotten buffs during an instance, no asking for buffs, no waiting for rebuffing etc.

It would be more enjoyable to everyone, not only Paladins!
