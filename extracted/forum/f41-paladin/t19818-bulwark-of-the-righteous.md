# Bulwark of the Righteous

## Post 135283 by Melfrato1 — 2025-06-11T11:12:00Z

Greetings!

Playing Paladin Tank rn and I kinda feel that Bulwark of the Righteous doesn't feel very good as an "ultimate" you can only press every 5 minutes, therefore I would suggest a change, and you lot just tell me what you think about it.

Get Rid of the 30% less damage for 12 Seconds on the Ability.

Make it 8 second cooldown so it bascially becomes the equivalent to the Warriors Shield Slam and a constant Threat Builder.
Make it increase the Block Value by 10% for 10 Seconds. (Unneeded probably)
Equalize the dmg it does for the low cooldown, so its not too overpowered in Threat.

Add a 1/1 Point Talent to the left or right to that talent that turns your Divine Protection into a 5 Minute Cooldown that does the 30% less dmg for 12 Seconds. (Similar to warrior shield wall but weaker and on lower cooldown)
