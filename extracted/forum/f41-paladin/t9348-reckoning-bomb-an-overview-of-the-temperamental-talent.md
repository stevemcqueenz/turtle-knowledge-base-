# Reckoning Bomb- An overview of the temperamental talent.

## Post 61165 by Ironfoe101 — 2023-09-06T12:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=61165#p61165 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’ve seen a lot of conflicting, and after testing, incorrect information about the Reckoning talent on Turtlewow.  So I set out to compile a list of what does and does not lose your charges, to help people who want to use Reckoning get better use out of it.  Also to help sniff out exactly what is buggy for bug reports since ‘does not work’ is not helpful.

Most of these I’ve tested multiple times to try and ensure I got consistent results.  However practical applications in World PvP, BGs, and Duels carry a great deal of variables that can make it feel like it doesn’t work at all.  Hopefully by breaking it down bit by bit we can better understand why it breaks.

How to gain Stacks
 -Get crit.
 -Yes this includes crits from spells.
 -Also you can sit crit if you time it right.
 -Seriously.
 -You can get full 5 attack stack.  Please understand you still can.

Things that do NOT lose your charges.
 -Spell casting (Like healing yourself or starting a seal)
 -Being interrupted (Kick)
 -Being Stunned
 -Being Incapacitated (Gogue)
 -Being Disoriented (Blind)
 -Using a buff trinket (Zandalarian Hero Charm)
 -Using a head item (Like Goblin Rocket Helm)
 -Having an Auto attack started (You can stack Reckoning charging while attempting to auto attack a target, as long as you do not swap targets, or stop the auto attack attempt)
 -Swapping targets while not Auto attacking
 -Dismounting
 -Attempting to mount. (If you start the cast and cancel it.)
 -Entering a Duel

Things that DO lose your charges
 -A Duel ending.  (Win or lose.  Concede/Flee/HP loss)
 -Mounting (As in completing the cast for mounting.)
 -Swapping weapons.
 -Starting an Auto attack and ending it.
 -Starting an Auto attack and swapping targets.

Please suggest further situations that can be tested, and if you find something contradictory with your personal experience please share.  If you do, I would be grateful if you shared what addons you were using so strange interactions can be found.

Edits: Clarified that you can get full Reckoning stacks.
 -Changed Mounting as I did more tests related to it.
 -Added entering a duel does not lose charges.
