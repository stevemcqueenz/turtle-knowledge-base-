<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35416#p35416 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A slight reajustment so y cn have an better spending for both retribution and protection paladins:

 - Merge [Sanctifeid command] into the 1 point talent [Seal of command] (by so releasing the unnecessary 2 points spend to get that improvment while you already spend 1 point in the [Seal of Command] talent).

 - Reduce protection talent [Precision] to two talent points (by so both protection and retribution paladins cn rearrange better there spending points).

 - Reduce protection talent [Improved Seal of Justice] to one talent point (by so you will losen up some talent points to be spend elsewhere).

 - Reduce protection talent [Improved Righteous Fury] to one talent point (by so you will losen up some talent points to be spend elsewhere).

- Seal of Command gives a lot of value even without Sanctified, so merging both ranks of that with it would definitely be too much. Maybe just merging the ranks of Sanctified, but that still might be too much for one point. I'd say changing Sanctified Command to a 1-rank talent that only gives 45% dispel protection, but gives the 20% mana refund seems about fair to me, but I really don't think it needs changed.
- Hit chance is a valuable stat for a talent to give if you aren't capped, and Precision not only does melee hit, but spell hit as well. Besides which, once you *are* hit capped from gear, you can refund these points anyway.
- On the fence about this one. Stun chance *can* help with trash mobs, but the stun isn't really as valuable as the effects of some other seals even at max chance, and it does nothing to most bosses. With the taunt judgement and it being in the Protection tree, this is clearly meant to be a tanking talent, but it feels meh for tanking, and really more PVP spec. My gut is to say yeah, make it one point for guaranteed taunt, but not improve stun at all. Instead, give the seal a base 30% chance, and add a new book that gives you a Rank 2 version with 60%.
- Strong, but since one of the things I keep hearing about Paladin tanks is how they struggle to keep aggro, I can't say it would be broken.
All of that said, I personally haven't found having enough points to go around to be an issue, but I'll admit I haven't tried Prot spec yet.

## Post 37030 by Mekunekud (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-07T01:54:00Z

Roll Sanctuary into Imp-RF. The blessing itself isn't all that special and the base effects can be put into Imp-RF without unbalancing the class. In it's place, a ranged attack style (so it's not spell hit based) taunt that does a small amount of non-scaling damage.
Make RF just affect all threat at a smaller amount, rather than just holy damage. There's not enough extra spell damage in the game after a point aka 60, to keep paladins scaling well, especially since you have to sacrifice spell damage for tank gear that is primarily statted for melee damage and taking caster weapons costs you the extra defensive stats that a tank item would give. When you are stuck using the tank gear from bosses like Ossirian, Hakkar and offset pieces that are all Strength, Stamina and Agi, you have no real option to raise your threat and as your avoidance rises, your threat drops due to how blocking and reflective damage works.
Just remove Reckoning. It doesn't work right. Give it as a side talent for Holy Shield that either increases charges and the block chance of Holy Shield  OR forces a Redoubt proc on use. That way, Holy Shield is now a decent anti-crushing blow tool on raid bosses. 2 charges and 10% block per point would allow paladins a better in when tanking at higher levels and the number of times I've seen a Holy Shield go through its entire duration without a block and thus no mitigation or extra threat is more numerous than it should. Relying on both Redoubt and Holy Shield to proc at the same time for the same crush immunity is bad design but increasing the block chance of Holy Shield through a forced redoubt proc or a higher base block chance would help alot.
Make One Handed Specialisation just give a flat 10% physical/holy damage when you use a one handed weapon. This gives a competitive advantage to Vengenace, which is the current leader for most tanks due to how poor Argent Defender and One Handed Specialisation is and how much better Imp-Ret Aura and the rest of the Ret talents scale.
Don't know how to balance Argent Defender. It's not very appealing as is. Maybe make it an on use defensive minor shield wall on a shorter cooldown? Something like 20% damage reduction for 5 seconds with a 5 minute cooldown that also applies Forbearence on the paladin.

For Ret, change Vindication to something actually useful. Maybe a debuff on mobs affected by your holy effects that lowers AP or attack speed? Would give Ret some more utility as a debuffer through Consecration/judgements.
Sanctified Command should just be a judgement effect rather than specifically Seal of Command.
Make Kings the 21 point talent so it requires more investment in the spec to get (11 points is too little for how good it is) and Seal of Command back to 11 points, especially since Paladins feel horrible now without SotC being useful until you grab a Ravager. Giving back Seal of Command means the levelling becomes less mundane.

For Holy, nerf the damage on Holy Shock and have it trigger the C/D on Holy Strike and vice versa. Other than that, its fine.
