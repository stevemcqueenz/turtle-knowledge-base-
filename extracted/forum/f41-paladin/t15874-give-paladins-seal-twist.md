Blizzard included it in Classic for historical purposes, because they removed batching, which would have otherwise made seal twisting impossible, but it is 100% a bug. Classic was intended to be an archive that allowed players to play the game as it was back in the day.

Seal twisting was entirely a bug that stemmed from the way spell batching worked, in the same way that two warriors could charge each other, or two mages could polymorph each other. It was not intended gameplay by the original vanilla devs at all.

As an aside, anything that requires precise timing on a server where people could have upward of 200ms ping that may vary from moment to moment, and does not have spell queuing is 100% bad game design. The only way I would approve of seal twisting is in the form of a mechanic that actively allows you to have two seals up for a short period of time, removing the requirement to effectively play a rhythm game at 200ms ping. An example of this would be the way SoD handled it, by including a set bonus on ret tier gear that allowed them to maintain a second seal for 6 seconds after casting a new seal:

> T1 6pc: Your Seal of Command, Seal of Righteousness, and Seal of Martyrdom now persist for 6 seconds after you cast another Seal, or until you cast a third Seal. Your Judgements can now trigger multiple Seals if active.

This would also give you breathing room to be able to use other abilities such as strikes and consecration
