In very simple terms:
T3 > T2.5 > T2
Thunderfury > Weapon with big spelldamage number > Weapon with small spelldamage number
For jewelry ring and cloak, Spelldamage or blockchance depending on the needs of a fight. (If Ou fight raidbosses and are dying you should aim for 98% combined dodge parry block with holy shield active.
For a shield, roughly bigger armor number better. Bonus points if it has + blockchance

It pays to have a max mitigation gear if you ever offtank on patchwerk or tank stuff such as loatheb. But the vast majority of times of the time you wear 98% mitigation and then as much spellpower as you can (and yes Noephix is right, exactly its 97.4% mitigation, not 98)
