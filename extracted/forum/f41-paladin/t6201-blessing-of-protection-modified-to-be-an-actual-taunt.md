# Blessing of Protection modified to be an actual taunt

## Post 39030 by Ravenstone (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-25T15:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39030#p39030 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was reading this post [viewtopic.php?f=14&t=6159&p=39009#p38982](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=6159&p=39009#p38982) and started to wonder whether there was any merit in Paladin's taunt being based on their Blessing of Protection. Obviously just a new spell not attached to anything would be the easiest and simplest fix but thought I'd at least see peoples opinions.

Basically, my idea is to add a proper 10 yard taunt debuff when casting BoP on an ally, much like Challenging Shout. I'm imagining the AoE debuff centred around the person you casted it on, though I guess with some work it could also originate from the Paladin instead. Obviously in both cases the Paladin would be the one to receive the threat.

This would give Paladin's a way to regain aggro without going through the current Justice Seal/Judge awfulness. The Guardian's Favor talent would be a much better pickup, and it'd be something that all Paladins could use in a pinch. I don't think  the loss of a blessing on someone was too big a deal for the scenarios I'm imagining.

I'm not sure if there are any abusable things I'm overlooking, but it would have minor impact in PvP.

For a negative, I could imagine it being a bit finicky with the range finding on the AoE vs the distance you cast the blessing from, and if out of range it could effectively cause it to miss the taunt entirely.

Anyway, I'd like to hear peoples thoughts on the idea.

## Post 39047 by Bob022 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-25T19:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39047#p39047 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I can live with the judgement-based taunt.  The mana cost is steep--its main drawback--but otherwise it does its job on the relative handful of memblur-type encounters where a taunt is necessary.  Otherwise, the class's core problem isn't its taunt so much as its inadequate single-target threat and mediocre damage reduction.  Tank Paladins should not feel obligated to go 30 points into Retribution because they can't hold threat otherwise.  Likewise, Ardent Defender is a nice one-point talent, it's a no-brainer if you'd want to go thirty into Protection anyway, but it's looked down on because it's defacto a ~7 point talent due to the weakness of the late Protection tree.

What I'd do?

Make 1H specialization talent significantly increase threat generation of normal melee; this fixes single-target threat and makes deep Protection more desirable.  Add fulltime 10% spelldamage reduction to Ardent Defender's other part-time effect; this makes AD desirable even for raiding players fighting things that can leapfrog its normal effect, and brings the tank Paladin's spell damage reduction up to on par with what a flippin' Shaman can access--but keeps it too far down in Prot for the Ret or Holy versions of the class to access.  I do not ask for additional physical damage reduction too--although we'd all like it--because I recognize we have no chance of getting it due to the traditional WoW anchor conventionally known as PvP balance.  Defense gear would remain an issue, as it has been ever since Defense gear was nerfed in 2005, but that can be fixed over time with new gearing options.

I don't ask for a proper AE taunt because we already have Blessing of Protection as-is and we have Consecrate.  I see that as the trade-off for no conventional AE taunt, and I accept that.
