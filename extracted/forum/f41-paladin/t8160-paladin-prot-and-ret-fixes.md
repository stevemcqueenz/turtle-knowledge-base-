# Paladin prot and ret fixes

## Post 52489 by Rof — 2023-07-06T15:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52489#p52489 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

i've been thinking about these changes for a hot minute now but i think the way to fix paladin is this

Removed Holy Strike as a baseline ability (this ability is just too strong for every paladin to get for free, its straight up as good as some classes 31 point talents meanwhile ret and prot 31 point talent is dogshit)

Repentance - Moved up one row

New Ret 31 point talent - Holy Strike (its as good as a 31 point talent so why not make it the 31 point talent, also fixes prots stupid wep gearing, and holy's burst in pvp)

Two-handed wep spec - now increases ALL damage by 2%/4%/6% and reduces threat generated by 5%/10%/15% (this is the threat reduction talent rets are missing compared to other classes, its weaker than those other threat reduction talents because rets are tankier than most classes and it does more than just threat reduction)

on to prot

Precision - Increases Physical hit by 1%/2%/3% and increases spell hit by 2%/4%/6% (this is to help increase the effectiveness of taunt and exo landing)

One-handed wep spec - Increases ALL damage by 2%/4%/6%/8%/10% and increases stamina by 2%/4%/6%/8%/10% (this helps solve the stam issue prots have and to atleast slightly off-set the loss of veng)

Ardent Defender - moved up one row - now 5 ranks - Reduces damage taken by 25% while below 30%/35%/40%/45%/50% health

New 31 point talent - Divine Exorcism - Removes the targeting requirement of Exorcism (this is to replace Holy strike as the main threat ability while also making prot more spell based versus rets wep based gameplay)

Exorcism should remain the way it is. The devs lean heavily on inspiration from WC3, which limits their baseline holy blasting potential to undead targets. In it's current form, exorcism accomplishes this pretty well.
What ret needs is a button they can press with a reasonable cooldown that does some damage. This isnt for the purpose of increasing their dps (although it would be nice for them to be somewhere other than bottom-mid) but to make the spec more fun to play.

A combination of the above would allow ret to be competitive for mid-range damage in most raids, then be competitive for higher spots in undead raids like Naxx. It fits thematically and rewards the player for making it all the way to the end game raid.

But due to the design of Holy Strike and Seal/Judgement of Command, ret is trapped in a corner with no way of adding a damage output ability that doesnt break pvp even moreso than it already is.
