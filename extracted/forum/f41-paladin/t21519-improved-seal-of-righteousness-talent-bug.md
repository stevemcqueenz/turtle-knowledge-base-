# Improved Seal of Righteousness Talent Bug

## Post 149110 by Avalok — 2025-08-29T10:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149110#p149110 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**HELLO!**

The issue regarding the Paladin's Improved Seal of Righteousness (SoR) talent is that the additional holy damage of the spell effect in terms of melee attacks do not increase, and only the Judgement of SoR's damage increases.

I noticed this detail as I was applying ranks to the mentioned talent while also checking the spell's tooltip with each rank added. The additional holy damage of the SoR spell in terms of melee attacks did not increase at all as I applied ranks on the talent, Improved SoR.

I also tested this issue out with mobs in Silithus while I was farming there, as all existing Player Characters' talents were reset when the new update 1.18 was added in-game.

Apply the SoR spell effect on the Paladin and attack an Apprentice Target Dummy with melee auto attacks. Now, add any amount in rank of the Paladin's Improved SoR talent, re-apply the SoR spell effect, and attack again the Apprentice Target Dummy. The additional holy damage of SoR per melee hit (the yellow damage) does not increase at all with any rank applied on the Improved SoR talent

Each rank of the Improved SoR talent must increase the additional holy damage done in terms of each melee attack based on the percentage provided of said talent.

As of now, the tooltip of rank 8 of the spell, SoR, describes additional holy damage per melee hit by 21 to 75 based on weapon swing time, and this does not yet include any ranks of the Improved SoR talent. With each rank I added to the said talent, the additional holy damage per melee hit of the spell effect based on the tooltip did not increase at all, and remained the same (still 21 to 75).

This also includes actual 'tangible' damage done to a target. the actual damage done of the spell effect in terms of melee attacks do not increase at all with each increase in rank of the said talent.
