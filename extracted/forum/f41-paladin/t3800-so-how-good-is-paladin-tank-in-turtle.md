## Post 23920 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-08-29T15:11:00Z

I've also found it's not in the least bit necessary to use Crusader Strike while tanking. It's too much extra mana drain for an unnecessary and small increase in damage/threat. Crusader Strike really feels like it was made more for Ret and Shockadins. Leave it off your bar as a Tankadin. Seal of the Crusader is similarly not necessary unless you just have some extremely aggressive DPS.

I usually open a fight by Judging Wisdom on my mob, popping Seal of Righteousness, and immediately starting threat with Holy Strike. Ret Aura on, Blessing of Sanc on, if there's 1 or 2 melee, Holy Shield on. Don't bother with Consecrate unless you're tanking 2+ mobs. I've seen people drop Consecrate for a single enemy, and that's where all their mana is going.
