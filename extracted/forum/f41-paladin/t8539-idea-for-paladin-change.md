## Post 56129 by Rudezlad — 2023-08-07T12:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56129#p56129 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yeah, Reckoning, as it currently stands, is flawed in two major ways, in both pve and pvp:

 1) As a tanking talent, you strive to become immune to crits, as in, have defense 390 for pve. The more defense you have over your current lvl x 5, the lower the crit chance against you is, with base value being 5% chance to be crit by enemies of your own level. So when that 390 defense gets achieved, you can no longer benefit from reckoning in pve.

 2) The effect of reckoning proc is not tied to weapon speed and/or type, which makes it most effective for slow 2h weapons, and least effective for fast one-handed weapons. But since it requires you to be hit (and crit), you can't really benefit from it as a dps unless you are actually getting hit (or solo), and in my experience when you do get hit as dps, the actual tank will instinctively try and make the enemy stop hitting you and hit them instead.

 3) In PVP, chances are that reckoning would proc a lot, since player character melee dps tend to have a higher crit chance than pve enemies. A fix to address reckoning's flaws might make it less useful with regards to pvp.

In TBC, reckoning was changed in following ways:

 1) Its proc chance was made to be 2% per talent point, so at 5/5 it would have 10% chance of proccing when being hit
 2) Its effect was changed to give (and overwrite whatever reckoning stacks you previously had) you 4 stacks of reckoning for 8 seconds, and every autoswing you make removes 1 stack of reckoning from you while making your autoswing hit twice.
 3) While the proc chance is lower in pvp against high crit players, 10% proc chance for double autoswing effectiveness for 8 seconds makes up for it.

This fixed the talent with regards to striving for crit immunity, and with regards to weapon speed benefits. A fast weapon would hit 4 times in that 8 second time window, whereas a slow weapon would hit 2 times, maybe 3 times. This fix would be ideal, although it might be a bit strong in comparison to other vanilla talents, so maybe adjust that 8 second timer down to 4 seconds and nrof stacks to 3 to keep it in line with the power of other talents. Either way, having a talent that no longer works when you become uncrittable is a very bad idea.

Reckoning is also not just about damage, but also about regaining mana (or health) through judgement effect being on the target you hit twice with reckoning. It is basically a threat increasing effect either way, since mana regain / heal also generates aoe threat, and the additional payload of damage is single-target threat. And with the additional mana you regain through working reckoning, you have better mana economy for keeping consecrate and holy shield up at all times.
