## Post 101405 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-17T12:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101405#p101405 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Slow weapons generate more threat and slightly more dmg, fast weapons generate vastly more mana with judgement of wisdom

Generally speaking for your role as tank, slower weapons are much better. 2.6 is ideal, slower than 2.6 is decent, 2.1-2.5 is okey and 1.3-2.0 should be avoided unless soloing
