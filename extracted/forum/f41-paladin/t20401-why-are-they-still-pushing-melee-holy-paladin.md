You're assuming that melee healing Paladins are strictly for "noobs," but that's a narrow take. The melee healing archetype isn’t inherently bad—it just has a higher execution requirement and demands proper encounter design and team synergy. Yes, they take more damage and face more interrupts, but they also bring powerful utility to the frontline: instant spot healing, blessings, and the ability to weave in melee attacks for mana sustain or proc effects.

The idea that ranged Paladins are the only "pro" option ignores the fact that some top players have optimized melee healing builds and found success in coordinated groups where positioning, interrupts, and utility management are tightly controlled. Not every healer should be a turret at 40 yards spamming heals.

As for gear—yes, the Kara 40 set seems built with the melee-healing playstyle in mind, but that doesn't mean it's "for noobs." It simply supports an alternative build path. And frankly, diversity in viable builds is good for the game. A ranged-only healing meta creates stagnation. Giving Paladins both a melee and ranged option isn’t just catering to skill level—it’s offering playstyle choice. And the best players will make either work depending on comp, fight mechanics, and strategy.
