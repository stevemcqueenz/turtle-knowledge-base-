# Small QOL Vengeance's threat reduction

## Post 117910 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-01-25T16:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117910#p117910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The new Vengeance talent is great, but its threat reduction is undesirable in some situations.

I'm levelling as ret. However due to the nature of levelling, and thanks to the flexibiliy of my class, I am sometimes in a situation where I equip a shield and turn Righteous Fury on to tank for group quests. This will likely also happen once I'm 60. The nature of paladin in general means that whatever your specialized role is, you'll often find yourself in situations where doing a different one, even briefly, fits the situation best.

My suggestion is to remove the threat reduction on Vengeance if Righteous Fury is on.

Old description :

> Gives you a 1/2/3% increase to all damage you deal and decreases threat you generate by 2/4/6% for 30 sec after dealing a critical strike from a weapon swing, spell, or ability. This effect stacks up to 3 times.

New description, with adjusted grammar to clarify that Righteous Fury would only negate the threat portion, not the damage portion (also clarifying that only offensive spells can proc Vengeance, not all spells such as heals) :

> Your melee and offensive spell criticals give you the Vengeance effect, which increases all damage you deal by 1/2/3% for 30 secs. Vengeance also decreases threat you generate by 2/4/6% unless Righteous Fury is active. Stacks up to 3 times.

Thanks for reading !
