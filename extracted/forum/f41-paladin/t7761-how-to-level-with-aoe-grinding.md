There's no shame in learning from others as well.

I'm still a newbie in many ways when it comes to pallying, but playing a protection pally in PvE (it's slow, I'll grant you that), I'm usually doing:
 - Concentration aura. With the other spell interrupt reduction talents, your self heals will never be interrupted by damage.
 - Righteous Fury, just because.
 - Sanctuary blessing on.
 - Seal of wisdom. Target the group of mobs I want to attack and body pull them. Keep attacking one to regain mana.
 - Tap on Holy Shield. Enemies start damaging themselves on Holy Shield, Shield Spike and the retaliation from Sanctuary.
 - Line of Sight pull: Walk backwards to a convenient spot, behind some terrain to force ranged enemies to close and bunch everyone up.
 - Consecration. Then Holy Light or Flash of Light to start self healing.
 - Judgement on the Seal of Wisdom to increase mana regain. Re-judgement whenever an enemy dies as you target the next one.
 - Holy Shield again as soon as it's off cooldown.
 - Keep self-healing, Holy Shielding and whacking the enemies until they are all dead. If there's a stronger one in the group of you find you have abundant mana you can use Crusader Strike and Holy Strike for additional damage.

If you picked engineering, you can add a few cheap explosives for more damage. I like to chuck an iron grenade as an opener. The stun gives you some breathing room too. If you find yourself running out of mana frequently while doing this, switch to a faster attack speed weapon, even if it means sacrificing a bit of damage.
You'd be much better served though with a build like this by teaming up with a dedicated damage dealing class like a mage or hunter.
