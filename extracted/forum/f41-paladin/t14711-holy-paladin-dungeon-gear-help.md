Good day.
In vanilla holy paladins rarely wear plate gear before they get their hands on the tier (1-2-)3 pieces. Stats wise, aim for +healing, +spell crit, and +intellect.
One of the most lucrative place would be Scholomance in my opinion, if for nothing else, then for the libram. I excluded from the lists the tier0 set, Lightforge, as alone it does not offer that great stats for a holy paladin. If you plan on doing the upgrade questline it becomes a quite nice hybrid set.
Kirtonos - Loomguard Armbracers
Jandice - Royal Cap Spaulders
Rattlegore - Rattlecage Buckler
Ras - Maelstorm Leggings
Minibosses around Gandling - Ghoul Skin leggings (alternative to the Leggings above) and, most importantly, Libram of Divinity
Gandling - Don Mauricio's Band of Domination (of course it is nice to let caster DPS get this first, but the spell crit is really good for a holy paladin)

Another nice place could be of course Stratholme undead side. It is only fitting to gear up a prospective paladin in the undead infested instances.
Barthilas - Crimson Felt Hat (again, it is nice to let caster DPS get this first)
Ramstein - Animated Chain Necklace
Rivendare - Robes of the Exalted

Other items worth noting:

Blackrock Depths:
Argelmarch - Second Wind
Chest of The Seven - The Hammer of Grace

Dire Maul:
The Light and How to Swing It (quest starter for the class trinket)
East
Alazzin - Gloves of Restoration, Whipvine Cord, Merciful Greavers
West
Ravenoak - Padre's Trousers
Immol'thar - Cloak of the Cosmos, Eyestalk Cord
Tortheldrin - Emerald Flame Ring
North
Kromcrush - Boots of the Full Moon
Cho'Rush - Insightful Hood
King Gordok - Brightly Glowing Stone, Bracers of Prosperity, Harmonious Gauntlets

As you can see there are some overlaps, so based on your own preferences and drop luck, you have choices to make. This list is by no means a final word on what you must get, just sone ideas of what I would think as nice gear for a holy paladin aiming at dungeons. I only focused on a handful of instances that I think are frequented enough to easily find groups for them.
