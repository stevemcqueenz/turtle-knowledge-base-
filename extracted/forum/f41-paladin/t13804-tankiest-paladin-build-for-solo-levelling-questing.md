Heya, for the talent tree:
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin/ ... 331-5CUU0C](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin/5U1-5IV331-5CUU0C)
Would probably be best for solo play, if you want to tank dungeons you should take 3 points from Toughness into Improved RF and 3 points from Improved HoJ into Improved SoJ if you feel like you need taunt (for dungs you probably do not tho).
While leveling, go for Shield Spec and Blessing of Sanctuary in prot tree first, then either Holy Shield or Improved Ret Aura, leaving Holy tree for last, the +10% holy buff and 70% interrupt res is very nice but other stuff makes farming faster.

Regarding gearing, try to look for mail and plate armor with Stamina, Int and Spirit, if you find some with spellpower it's also nice, but it's usually easier to find spellpower on rings, amulet and cloak. Speaking of, you can get very good one for High Elf rep (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80505>), it's enough to do their starting location iirc, and you can get ones for lv 45 and 55 too :D. Also look for retaliation equipment, like <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81005>, it's very good on pally ^^. For the weapons look for proc 1h weapons, like <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=1482>, <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=9651>, also you can enchant your weapon with Fiery Weapon and use other items that give additional damage source, like <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=12631>. The reason for that  is your Seal (and Judgement!) of Wisdom procs of damage sources, so if you have a +4 fire damage and 2 procs, it can give you hundreds of mana per hit :D. Also - remember to ask a blacksmith for a shield spike, it's also nice to have :D.

AoE farming is easy, buff up BoS, use Ret aura, pull 3-5 mobs (or more if you can handle them) use Holy Shield and SoW, autoattack and Consecration if you need it :D.

For single target, like elite mobs, use BoS and Sanctity Aura , prepare Seal of Wisdom, pull with Judgement, cast Seal of Righteousnes and Holy Shield. Then just spam Crusader Strike rank 1, use holy shield and Holy Strike on cd and you are golden ^^. Remember to use Divine Shield and/or Lay on Hands if you are dying.

Good luck!
