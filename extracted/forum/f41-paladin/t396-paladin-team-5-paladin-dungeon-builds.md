# [Paladin] Team 5-Paladin Dungeon builds

## Post 2078 by Jcarrill0 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2019-02-13T02:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2078#p2078 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So back on "old" project servers, I enjoyed doing end-game dungeons using all Paladins.

we had a group of 5 Paladins that was Talent-Spec'd to compliment each other.

while the runs were all successful, I wanted to see if it could be done again.

Here is the 5 Paladin builds:

[The Tankadin](https://classicdb.ch/?talent#sxVzZVhxIxzVoV0L) - Protection Paladin. Judgement of Justice, Blessing of Sanctuary, Resistance Aura (Fire/Shadow/Frost).
[The Healadin](https://classicdb.ch/?talent#sVxubgstgMt00h) - Holy Paladin. Lasting-Judgement of Light, Improved-Blessing of Wisdom, Improved-Concentration Aura.
[The Reckadin](https://classicdb.ch/?talent#sZVGtzc0xZVf0tRfk) - Hybrid Reckoning Paladin. Improved-Judgement of Crusader, Blessing of Kings, Improved-Retribution Aura.
[The Ret Pally](https://classicdb.ch/?talent#sE0zZVhZgf0trcqo) - Retribution Paladin. Improved-Judgement of Crusader, Improved-Blessing of Might, Sancity Aura.
[The Shockadin](https://classicdb.ch/?talent#sEVurxstxhZV) - Spell Power Paladin. Lasting-Judgement of Wisdom, Blessing of Light, Improved-Devotion Aura (With Libram).
please note: these Talent-Specs were designed only for the purpose of clearing all the end-game dungeons and while they were all successful and fun, it's not recommended for Hardcore Raid or Hardcore PvP.

the end-Goal was to get a joint 10-Man UBRS using the Paladins as well, but that never happened.

Thoughts? Please reply with you comments/Concerns/Rants below.

Keep it civil please!
