## Post 170296 by Draykomanuel — 2026-03-07T17:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170296#p170296 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello, I'm a WoW player with over 20 years of experience as a Holy Paladin. I've played the class since 2005 through all its expansions. I'm a PvE and PvP player and have seen all the class changes over the years. Having said that, I'd like to give my humble opinion regarding the melee Holy Paladin. I see that some want to force the Holy Paladin to heal in melee, and I understand that there might be pressure from players who don't know the class or want to innovate with it. The Holy Paladin never worked and doesn't work in melee; it's a healer like any other, with ranged abilities that you have to know how to use. I know you want the Holy Paladin to heal in an area, but for that, you only need to give it the one ability that I think is the best for a Paladin to heal in an area without losing its essence. The ability is called Beacon of Light. This ability was added in the Wrath of the Lich King version, and it was very good, allowing the Paladin to maintain its essence and heal multiple members of its party simultaneously. That's speaking about PvE. Now... Speaking of PvP, they should restore the full damage of Holy Shock. Many of us want to play Shockadin in PvP, and it's not fair that other classes can do their full DPS while Holy Paladins can't be Shockadin. I hope you'll consider my small opinion and that it can bring about a change for the better for Holy Paladins. Thank you all! And may the light bless you.
