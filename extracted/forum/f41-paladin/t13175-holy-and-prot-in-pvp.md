# Holy and Prot in PvP

## Post 88242 by Anythinggoes — 2024-02-20T14:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88242#p88242 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The first class I ever played in WoW was a paladin because I liked the lore. It was long after that, after I played classes like a mage, that made me realize how boring and repetitive paladins really are. They lacked the tools to do even basic stuff, unlike some other classes whose design was to be envied.

Ret in vanilla was auto attack casino class that could do no dmg in PvE.
Prot was a tank spec that could not tank.
Holy spammed 2 heals + Holy Shock for an entire raid and did pretty much the same in PvP.

There was one paladin I really liked back then, though - Zalgradis. Unlike the rest of the samey PvP videos where the ret got some lucky crits on unsuspecting targets, Zalgradis played prot and he made some pretty enjoyable footage.

The game has evolved for 20 years. Players have gotten better. What worked before can easily be shut down now. My question is, with the changes done to paladins in Turtle, can some form of prot/holy hybrid work in PvP? I don't care about ret at all, I am only interested in the kind of playstyle where you can outlast your opponent. This is how I believe this class should be played, instead of lolret>dead.
