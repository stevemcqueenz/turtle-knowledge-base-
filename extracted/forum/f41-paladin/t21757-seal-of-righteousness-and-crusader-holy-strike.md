I tested with Seal of Wisdom and both Crusader and Holy Strike make Seal of Wisdom proc and give mana every time. I am not sure how this works with Seal of Command as I do not have it. ## Post 150817 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-08T16:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150817#p150817 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Its a purposeful design choice. Paladin is already an insanely burst heavy class and that is causing bad blood in pvp as is.. Giving crusader strike a ~40% chance with slow weapons for another 1k+ dmg from proccing a seal of command would be absurd.

Furthermore, seals are considered physical attacks. So if CS & HS would be proccing seals, you would be milking procs such as ashbringer or sulfuras for even more damage.

Tldr: you'd have a realistic chance of 100-0 oneshotting pretty much anybody in pvp and any questmob or instantly dying to burstthreat every time you ligned up a cs with an auto attack and got two soc procs
