Disclaimer: i Love To Play Paladin, i have 3 Paladins, but i'm No theorycrafter and dont use any Guides. I try Things Out and Take the path that Suits me the best and also feels good "To Me"! So dont expect my advise to be the best Option. ^^

 10seconds for a kill 1v1 (vs a mob of the Same Level) IS actually ok. I dont know what exactly you expect. ^^ If you have Problems with more than one mob, than you might be lacking either stamina or the Skill.

 1. I can actually only Show my own ret Talents. If it's the Most efficient one? Idk. I dont tend To use any Guides. but i went well with it.
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin? ... AAdIFAAAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin?points=FAAoBAAAAAAAAAFAIAAAAAAAAAAAFoQAFKAAdIFAAAA=)
Eniugh To say: Take all 3pts in Precision (Prot Tree) as long as you're Not hit capped.
I am Hit capped with my ret so i dont need as many pts in Precision anymore.

 2. I tend to use Seal of Command as my Main Seal and usually use Crusader Strike To keep my Zeal Stacks Up and use judgment Just to use Up my Seal before it runs Out. Holy Strike becomes interesting later with the "Vengeful Strikes" Talent, that gives me another strength Boost. So as soon as i got my 3 Zeal Stacks Up, i use Holy Strike for the strength buff and Switch between both Strikes, To keep both buffs Up.
I dont rely on Consecration, since it Just Burns Up my mana.
If my mana gets Low during Fight and i know the Fight isnt over soon, i try To get my mana Up quickly with Seal of Wisdom (Not Judgment of Wisdom!). Holy Strike helps with Seal of Wisdom To regain mana Quickly.

 3. as Long as your this early in the Leveling Stage you shouldn't think too hard about what stata you build on, but later i'd say
Hit (until capped) > Crit > Str > Agi > Stam > Int

Obviously it also depends on what Kind of ret Paladin you Play, since there's also the Spelladin who instead of Seal of Command builds on Seal of Righteousness. With that one i cant Help though, but i assume they need more spellpower and Int.
