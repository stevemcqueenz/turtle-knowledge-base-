# Rework holy strike

## Post 25599 by Geojak (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-09-20T15:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25599#p25599 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Holy strike is in a weird sitaution.

It adds massive burst especially in pvp but still doesn't solves rets bad dps in raids.

Why is that? Let's investigate first.

Holy strike transfers your physical hit to holy dmsgse, that is effectively 100% armor pen. This massively helps to against high armor classes like bears of warriors and shamans in pvp.

They keep complaining because of this.

In raids in the other hand armor penetrstion is applied to bosses for example by warriors. The relative effect of paladins having thisc100% armor penetrstion is alot lower since other physical classes also have it. This means paladins can't capitalise in raids on this.

The class cnages nerfed the CD of holy strike from 8 to 10. It didn't not touch the dmg of holy strike, meaning the burst is Stil las high in pvp, but the dps in raids was even nerfed

An early holy strike crit in raids is often a quick death for the raiding ret. Less burst but more consistent dps would be to the paladins advantage.

This spell is both up and op depending on setting. Let's fix this!

Suggestion:
First add to the talent that increase pala movement speed the additional effect to decrease the CD of holy strike by 1 point per talent (meaning back to 8s with 2 talent points)

Second nerf holy strikes to turn 50% of your dmg to holy dmg (this part scales with sp) and the other 50% does physical dmg. This will massively help at drying horde pvp tears.

This is in total a paladin pvp nerf, one that I as a paladin myself see as reasonable despite our classes overall poor dps.

But pvping has me conviced that this burst nerf is required if we want rets to actually do more consistent dps.

P. S. Rets need meele haste (not burst) like all other meele classes to get up to pair in raid dps without making them op in pvp.

P. P. S the amount of burst paladins can do is widely exaggerated by horde. I heard claims of 3k crits in preraid bis. This just isn't true. I half epic equipped and my epic av mace crits for just 2k. You need ful ret bis for 3k crit and then this isn't all that op considering what other classes can do in full bis
