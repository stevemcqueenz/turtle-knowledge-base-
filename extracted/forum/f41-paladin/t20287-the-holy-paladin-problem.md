# The Holy Paladin Problem

## Post 139025 by Soymage — 2025-07-14T05:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139025#p139025 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

Let's jump right into the issues I see with holy paladins right now:

 ***THE IDEA OF HOLY PALADIN***

Conceptually, holy paladins seem to be an **"Endurance - Tank Healer"**. I have a big mana pool. My flash of light is very efficient. I can smack with seal of wisdom during downtime to stay at full mana. No other healer is nearly as efficient with mana usage and mana generation. But mana efficiency really does not matter 90% of the time because... if you or your other healers are not ever running out of mana (only happens a few times a raid in my guild, Kara40 and Naxx) it's just a wasted stat. It doesn't matter if I have 8k Mana if I have 50% remaining at the end of the fight (which happens often). Which brings me to my second point:

The healing is just sub-par to every other class, mainly shamans. Granted, the tank healing is superb. But there are so few fights where tank healing really matters... and here's the kicker: In 40 man raids, tank healing is mostly irrelevant anyways when you have 8+ healers spamming the tanks. When my guild needs to field 7/8 raid healers because of such heavy raid damage, we obviously keep those same players/raid healers on tank-healing-intensive fights, and there is really no noticed deficiency. We do not suffer for having more raid healers than tank healers.

 ***THE NUMBERS***

The REAL reason I am so frustrated with holy paladin is not really due to the concepts I just described. It's just down to the numbers. I am going to compare Paladin healing to Shaman healing since both classes tend to spam one ability (Flash of Light | Chain Heal):

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/gGNaUKT.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/4NYXl4q.png)

Looking at these two spells, flash of light seems like a much more powerful and efficient spell. But we need to factor in a few things. Shaman's talent *Improved Chain Heal* reduces the cast time of Chain heal by 1 second. Chain heal is a 2 second cast, and it hits a second target for 50% healing, and then a third target for 25% healing.
Spell Coefficients: Flash of light has a 42% healing power coefficient (1.5/3.5). Chain Heal has a 85% healing coefficient (3/3.5). When you also factor in potential 2nd and 3rd target hits from chain heal, the heal is more like 964 - 1102 (148% coefficient). This is just insane.

If you "normalize" these two spells, that is, calculate how much they both heal in, say, 30 seconds, this is what you get:

Flash of Light: 428 - 494 (42%) * 20 casts = 8560 - 9880 + 840% healing power coefficient
Chain Heal: 964 - 1102 (148%) * 15 casts = 14460 - 16530 + 2220% healing power coefficient
Chain Heal (Single Target Only): 551 - 630 (85%) * 15 casts = 8265 - 9450 + 1275% healing power coefficient

Now, I understand that getting a perfect line-up for 3 full chain-heals 15 times in a row is unrealistic... but it does happen frequently, literally any time a strong AOE hits the melee dps or the whole stacking raid (Think Kruul, Anomalus, or Chess). And it is a smart heal, so it automatically selects people to heal for you. I could go on and on about this, but I'm actually not here to bring down Shamans. I hope I'm illustrating how far behind Holy Paladins are from other raid healers. Even if some of my math is wrong or im leaving out substantial talents/effects (even with a near-perfect setup of the holy talent Daybreak for 20% healing is not going to cut it), This gap is enormous. A holy paladin is getting more than doubled by a Shaman at raid healing. And in single target healing, the shaman is STILL healing the same if you factor in Daybreak for the paladin. The coefficient issue is the main problem here.

 *source - <https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Spell-Coefficients>*
(Note: the coefficient listed for chain heal at this link is likely incorrect. The cast time was 2.5 in classic, but it was increased to 3 sec in twow, then the improved chain heal talent was added. This would mean a higher coefficient. Even if I am wrong and the coefficient is still 72%, it won't change the idea of what I'm depicting here)

Shamans, Druids, and Priests all have ways of "breaking through" the standard spell coefficient. That is, they have something that makes their effective spell coefficients more than 100% healing bonus for every 3.5 seconds of casting. They do this through cast time reduction, HoT healing, or AOE healing. Paladins only have Daybreak, and that is clunky as hell to use.

 ***POOR ITEMIZATION***

Looking at the secondary stats that benefit healers (Healing Power, Crit, Cast Speed, Mp5), Paladins do not scale well with gear and are straight up BORING to itemize.

 **Healing Power** -> (No issues here. A great stat for everyone.)

 **Crit** -> Crit has never been that good for healers. It simply does matter when you crit and overheal. Overhealing is a huge problem especially with 7 or more other healers. The paladin's benefit for a crit is 60% mana refund and applying Daybreak for 20% extra healing on your target for 12 seconds. I already explained that mana gain as a paladin is not that good. I rarely ever get close to low mana anyways. Seal of wisdom smacking makes mana efficiency not that great. I will talk more about Daybreak later.

 **Cast Speed** -> Cast speed is normally an amazing stat... but unfortunately, flash of light is sitting right on the global cooldown (1.5 seconds). This makes cast speed pointless. There is no point in casting flash of light at 1.1 seconds if I am still locked to casting every 1.5 seconds.

 **Mp5** -> Mp5 is not great for the same reason discussed with crit. I don't need more mana.

So, as a paladin, you can't look forward to getting any of these stats because they are all just so clunky.
Shamans benefit heavily from all of these stats. They do not have an infinite mana gain like paladins do. Crit is great on shamans due to proccing Ancestral Healing on your tanks, and the fact that chain heal's second and third hits are much smaller than the main hit, you don't really risk overhealing on a crit.
Druids benefit more than paladins, but their hots lack the ability to crit or speed up with cast speed, so they are also slightly problematic in regards to these stats.
Priests also benefit more than paladins with these stats, but their flash heal is also at the global cooldown of 1.5, so they are in the same boat as paladins with cast speed.

As a side note, the Kara 40 holy paladin set bonuses are garbage. (3) buff to Holy shock, (5) buff to Holy strike. Really? I shouldn't even need to explain why this is bad. What a disappointment.

 ***WHAT I WOULD CHANGE***

Looking at Turtlogs.com right now, out of the top 30 healers for Kara40, only two are holy paladins. I won't dive further into this because statistics like these require a ton of nuanced discussion. It's not enough for me to say that there should be more paladin representation in statistics, but in my personal observations I have not seen many holy paladins topping the charts in any of the pugs or guild raids ive been in.

I don't believe in changing boss fights to fit holy paladins. We don't need to add more heavy tank-healing fights like patchwerk just to boost holy pallies ego. I don't believe in giving holy paladins an AOE heal. I want classic to stay true to classic. I think most of us want to avoid the homogenization that retail suffered from making every class able to do everything. That being said, here's what I suggest:

 **Create a global cooldown modifier.** Make it so that cast speed can reduce your global cooldown below 1.5 when your spell is faster than the global cooldown. This is good for all classes, not just paladins. I don't think this would interfere with classic's integrity.
 **Rework Daybreak.** Make the talent apply off all spell casts and reduce the bonus to 15%, or make it last 30 seconds, or make the healing bonus apply to everyone within 10 yards of your daybreak-ed target with one allowable target at a time, with in internal cooldown of 8 seconds, so Daybreak won't just swap around constantly (this is my favorite. I think Daybreak could have a large light effect like Beacon of Light, but I do not wish to add Beacon of Light to classic).
 **Rework Holy Shock** I really don't know how, but it sucks. Give it a lower cooldown. Make it deal damage again. Give it more range. Make it less reliant on crits and randomly resetting cooldown. It is seriously unfun when it either doesn't crit or doesn't reset itself.

I hope you all enjoyed my rant. My paladin is Soyknight on Nordanaar.
