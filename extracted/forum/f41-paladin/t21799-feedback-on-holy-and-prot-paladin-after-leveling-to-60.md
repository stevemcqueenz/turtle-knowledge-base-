# Feedback on holy and prot paladin after leveling to 60

## Post 150949 by Tankmeister — 2025-09-09T09:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150949#p150949 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

(*this post was formatted using AI for better readability)*

I’ve played Paladin since retail Vanilla, through Wrath, and into Cataclysm. First, I want to commend the Turtle WoW team - this is by far the most fun version of Paladin I’ve experienced in WoW. After leveling to max and spending a lot of time in dungeons (and a few raids), here’s my feedback:

Protection

 **Positives**

Seals and Judgements finally feel impactful. Choosing the right seal/judgement in the moment feels somewhat strategical.

The Reckoning redesign is fantastic. Having it proc immediately, and on block, makes sense thematically and feels rewarding.

Having a taunt is just good.

Converting some blessings into hand spells is a welcome quality-of-life change.

 **Mixed Feelings**

Bulwark of the Righteous is a great addition with both offensive and defensive utility. That said, a 5-minute cooldown feels like a lot. I’d personally prefer a slightly weaker version that could be used more often.

Holy Strike and Crusader Strike are excellent concepts but currently feel underutilized. Right now, I just use Crusader Strike to keep Zeal up, and otherwise Holy Strike. I’d love to see more meaningful choice regarding these two abilities:

Holy Strike: a supportive option - perhaps by providing a short party buff (e.g. a 6-second damage boost). Or just significantly increasing the healing done.

Crusader Strike: a survival tool, enhancing my defenses (also for 6 seconds).

Right now, the Holy Strike heal is simply too weak to matter and Crusader Strike just feels like a chore you have to keep doing.

 **Negatives**

Eye for an Eye should be available to Prot again. It was the only real tool against casters in PvP, and it fit the Paladin fantasy very well.

Holy

I’ve never really played as a healer in WoW before. Standing at the back spamming heals never appealed to me. But Turtle’s melee-healer design tempted me to try Holy, and I’m surprised by how much I enjoy it.

 **Positives**

The melee healing playstyle is fun and engaging. Even if I’m not dealing huge damage, being in the fight and seeing damage numbers alongside heals just feels better.

 **Mixed Feelings**

The interplay between Holy Strike and Crusader Strike works better here than in Prot. Timing Crusader Strike to refresh Holy Shock vs. using Holy Strike for healing/mana management feels meaningful. But again, Holy Strike’s heal (even with Blessed Strikes) is far too weak. I’d prefer if Holy Strike had a more impactful heal, and compensating with lowering healing from other spells. That would add a conscious choice between “healing one vs. healing many” to the gameplay.

 **Negatives**

Daybreak feels underwhelming and uninteresting. It could be much more engaging if it were an activated ability. For example, alongside its current effect, it could make your next heal a guaranteed crit. This would also synergize nicely with Holy Strike, since the only time it feels impactful is when it crits and procs Daybreak.

Closing Thoughts

Overall, I think Paladin on Turtle WoW has had a lot of great improvements. Fun, unique, and closer to what I always wished the class had been in retail. However, it leaves a lot more to be desired and improved upon, and I hope the dev team continues to realize this hidden potential.
