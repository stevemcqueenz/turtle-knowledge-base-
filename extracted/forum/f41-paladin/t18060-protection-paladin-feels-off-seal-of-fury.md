Yes. I respect what you want to do, I like to theorycraft, too but there are two things here I'd have a problem with.

 1. Prot paladin is already extremely buffed. They are the best dungeon tanks and they are perfectly fine to tank all contents (except for some raid bosses who really punish mana using tanks). If you further push them, you could push them from 'very strong' to 'game breaking'.
Unless the other builds get a new wave of buffs and pala would need ways to catch up, I see no options to buff them unless we give some and also take some. But this leads to my second point.

 2. It is true that palas don't have an aoe taunt but they are still the 'aoe specialists' among tanks. They are just the best at it. You want to buff them with something they are already really good at, instead of giving them something they lack (for example range pull or a rush-like effect). I don't say palas should have access to these but further buffing something they are currently the best at feels like a 'waste of design points'.

Under design points I mean an imaginary number. Optimally different builds get around the same level of investment with talents, abilities, buffs they bring etc. Every new ability or every new buff is a further investment into a build.
But pushing the same thing they are good at over and over again gets diminishing returns. The same 'amount of buff' at a place they struggle - or if we wanna keep blind spots for builds, in which they are mediocre at - gives a much bigger push to the build.
