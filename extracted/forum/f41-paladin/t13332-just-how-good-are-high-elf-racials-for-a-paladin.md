# Just how good are high elf racials for a paladin?

## Post 89646 by Anythinggoes — 2024-03-07T20:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89646#p89646 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Most of the races that can be paladins actually have pretty terrible racials.
Dwarf Stone form can be good vs rogues and probably if you want to tank.
Perception sucks big time, but the bonus expertise can be good in PvE, I guess.

Then come the high elves, with their meditation. Probably not bad if you are a healer needing mana, yes? Is it very impactful?

I was thinking of trying human or high elf because dwarves are too short for my taste. Unfortunately, high elves look so feeble when wielding a weapon. It is as if they will break a limb trying to swing something heavy.

What about PvP? Can the high elf meditation make a difference?
