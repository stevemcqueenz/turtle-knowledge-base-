## Post 13265 by Lahire (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2021-10-27T10:06:00Z

I think there are good reasons why K. Jordan, the class designer, converged to the 1.12 tree instead of the 1.1 one. They are a bit junky init?

I agree with you that the ret 1.1 tree is far more appealing and cool. But on the other hand, it contains all the core paladin fantasy abilities, which is not optimal. Consecration, King, Precision and SoCommand in the same tree is quite bloated from a fantasy pov: what particularities are left for the 2 other specs? Not much. Perhaps the designer corrected course a bit too much (he says so himself), but the 1.1 paladin has design problems too, if not the same than the 1.12 one.

Paladin suffered during production because it never had 3 fully realised fantasy. It had 2 : big dude with big mace on the frontline doing smack and off-heals (ret) ; big dude with big shield giving back the damage he endured (prot). Holy suffers from a lack of vision, it is a function-spec : "ima the healer i guess". And its performance emerged from players and not from the designer (K. Jordan said he was a bit surprised when paladins began to stay away from melee and wear robes like priests). Because Holy lacked clear vision (and because the healing plate gear was bad), the players made it into a priestly experience.

Each other 1.1 class than paladin is less appealing than their 1.12 version imo. The absence of Moonkin is a big no-no. Manatide totem, Innervate, as 31 talent... wtf? The 31 Survival talent "Lacerate" is the funniest meme ever put in WoW. Lots of lesser stuff, you can feel it's less polished than 1.12.

All in all, we're good with the 1.12 version.
