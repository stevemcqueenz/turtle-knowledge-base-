Depends a lot on what your goals are.

 5 man and casual content and no plans on alts? Mining with some profesion that uses minerals (blacksmithing, jewelcrafting or engineering). This gives you a source of materials and can craft some items better than dungeon drops.

Endgame raiding? Consumable crafting professions. Alchemy and herbalism are a safe pick, since you'll use potions and elixirs on a daily basis.

PvP? Engineering with mining. Engineering gives you extra tools and CC consumables. Mining again to supply with materials.

You're thinking on staying here playing regularly for years and do some serious raiding? Enchanting will take a lot of time investment, but once you get the rare recipes you can semi afk in a capital whispering anyone needing a enchant you have "pssst, i can enchant what you're looking for if you bring me the mats and some tip". Very low gold income but almost efortless once you got the recipes. Pair it with jewelcrafting specialized in gemology to enchant others people jewerly for the same deal as enchants. Perfect if you can have the client open all day semi afk.
Remember that without mining you'll have to level up an alt or buy the materials from the AH

Planning on leveling alts? Get double crafting profession on your main. Engineering is very useful in both pve and pvp (mostly on pvp) and another profesion of your liking. Engineering also allows you to craft dynamites which can be used for ranged pulls if you ever need to do so. For now, most important materials you might need can get gathered in the world, so you can have a dedicated miner/herbalist for your daily needs while your main enjoys more crafting stuff
