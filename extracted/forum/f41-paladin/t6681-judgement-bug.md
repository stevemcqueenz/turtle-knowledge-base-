Not sure if anyone else noticed but there is a bug with judgement ... there are times when it simply does nothing other then consume the seal you had on you when you activate it.

I first noticed it when I was using judgement with seal of righteousness during leveling but I ignored it at the time.

Right now I've been testing it with seal of the crusader and frost oil applied to my weapon. As you know, frost oil has a 10% chance to proc... so seal of the crusader + judgement should activate it all the time at a whooping 300% chance. Sometimes it works 5 times in a row, sometimes it doesn't... it's extremely inconsistent and I have no idea why it does that

As you can see in the screenshot I've provided, It worked the first time , followed by a second time where it simply consumed my seal and nothing happened, followed by a third use of judgement where it worked again
