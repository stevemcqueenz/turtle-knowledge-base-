I wanted to give my impressions as someone who’s been leveling ret and is level 34 now, so take this with a grain of salt because I haven’t been leveling too long. But after some trial and error, I’ve found a solution that works for me: just don’t level in the meta way (auto attacking, never using crusader and holy strike, and rarely judging to keep mana) instead, try and stack up on intelligence when you can along with strength and stam, spec into heart of the crusader as well, during rotation actually use crusader strike 1-3 times depending on mob health, and use judgement and holy strike when you can and when mana allows. Is it gonna be the most efficient best leveling spec? Nah. You will have to stop and drink every 4 mobs or so as well, but it’s actually fun to level, it’s actually an interesting somewhat engaging rotation that doesn’t just have you auto attacking and spacing out. To be clear, it’s NOT meta, but to me it’s much more worth it for the leveling to actually be fun, keeps me playing for longer and makes me not regret playing paladin. Plus with heart of the crusader and 1-3 crusader strikes on a mob, it’s fun to see the extra damage, even if small, and holy strike hits for big numbers, and big numbers feel good. And IMO it feeling good is more important than it being 100% optimized and efficient. Just my two cents though.
