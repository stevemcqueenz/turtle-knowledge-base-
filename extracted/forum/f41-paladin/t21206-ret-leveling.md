So I'm not a "professional Ret Leveler" but I've leveled them many times. These are what my talents might look like at 30 & 50:
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin? ... FoAAVCIAAI](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin?points=--FoAAVCIAAI)
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin? ... oAAVCYYFIF](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin?points=F--FoAAVCYYFIF)

Useful tip to know is that Sanctity is -not- worth it until 60. Improved Retribution Aura helps you deal more damage overall since you don't have enough big numbers on Holy Damage, even on single-target mobs.

As for CS & HS: If you're able to keep your killing streak going, it's great to have 3 stacks of CS, then keep using HS. CS will do more damage than HS will but as you mentioned, drains mana. Just do CS once in a while to keep it up.
If you feel like you run out of mana during it, don't sit down and drink. Pop Seal of Wisdom and spend time on one mob to get your mana back, then carry on. I also recommend using "Blessing of Wisdom" as that will keep you going between fights.

If you can obtain the ring from Gilneas City that adds a "+3 Nature Damage" attack, great! That thing is considered it's own attack, meaning if you cast Judgement of Wisdom, you now have an extra proc chance on the Wisdom - and the proc chance on that judgement is very high.
