# Bubble Cleanup and Bulwark fix

## Post 119267 by Jrobz (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-02-07T18:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119267#p119267 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My $0.02

First off the new End Talent of Prot is good, but still seems very uninvolved with the spec given it's downtime. An End talent should be Spec defining. Look no further than the Warrior for how to do End Talents. I like the idea of a tanking defensive cooldown but not as an End Talent. Here's a idea on how to clean this up and hopefully add spice in a balanced way.

Bubble: There's 3. One is completely redundant - Divine Protection. Let's update that to reduce dmg by 50% for 8 seconds - that's it - only PvE usable - shield equipped and under RF (aka tanking). Also, let's increase the 60 seconds shared bubble CD to 10 or 15 minutes. I think the Pally Bubble can be taken town a few pegs. This gives all 3 bubbles a  useful place.

Bulwark: Reduce the Cooldown to 4s and remove the defensive element (that is handled by Divine Protection). Change the effect to consume a Holy Shield stack (and cost 0 mana) to shield bash the target for Holy Damage = X% of SBV + Y% of HOLY SP. This gives the paladin a way to consume unspent Holy Shield stacks and adds a neat element to gameplay. It scales up with SP and SBV, and gives the Paladin a way to use their mainstay ability (Holy Shield) in a more generalized way.

This would be that spec defining ability that weaves nicely in with the Seal and Zeal systems.
