# [Tel Abim] Stop asking for WF, start demanding a Firestrike Sharpening stone.

## Post 119141 by excin (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-02-06T10:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119141#p119141 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Firestrike adds additional magic damage to your melee strikes, typically fire.

Each proc is considered as separate spell cast, and as such, each proc will have the same chance to trigger judgement of wisdom as any other spell hit.

This procs on you Crusader strikes, Holy Strikes, Melee hits, and even seal procs. Practically doubling your Judgement of wisdom output, helping you sustain more higher rank consecrations.

On my last Nax run it procced 907 times, for comparison I only dealt a total of 437 auto attack hits.

Getting it:
The effect is present on a couple of items of different levels of gear and magic school flavors:
[[Flameskin Gloves]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81267) - Fire - lvl 23 World drop
[[Ring of Electrical Binding]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61332) - Nature - lvl 43 GC
[[Incendosaur Skin Pauldrons]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60572) - Fire - lvl 49 Hateforge
[[Incendosaur Skin Gloves]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60582) - Fire - lvl 49 Hateforge
[[Incendosaur Skin Boots]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60568) - Fire - lvl 51 Hateforge
[[Fiery Plate Gauntlets]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=12631) - Fire - lvl 53 Blacksmithing
[[Storm Gauntlets]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=12632) - Nature - lvl 54 Blacksmithing

All the above will be a significant stat gimp (except for the incendosaur stuff at starter gear) so go for the ring.

The better stuff would be
 - T2 Ret 3bonus set - Holy damage (which will work on all encounters but lock 3 gear slots)
 - Embergem Cuffs - Jewelcrafting cloth bracers - 4 Fire damage 13 Str 13 Stam (what I currently use)

And ultimately, from Lord Kazak
[[Blazefury Medallion]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=17111) - fire

Now it being like minor WF, in the sense that you need to have it. It's Gimpish itemization and will always be locking 1-3 slots of your gear and cause a significant stat sacrifice, until you get that long queued for medallion with the mediocre stats.

 **What I suggest is Making it available through a consumable, perhaps a Sharpening stone.**

 **Since we already get +7 melee damage as a base, and a whopping 2% crit at top tier. I don't think a +2 or +4 [magic damage of your choice] sharpening stone (1.8 dps) would break the game.**

The only opposition coming to this would probably be some folks from the old realm who've gotten a Blazefury Medallion and see it as a rite of passage.
