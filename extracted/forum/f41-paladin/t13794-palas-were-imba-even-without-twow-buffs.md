## Post 93870 by R0ck3rhe4d — 2024-05-02T03:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93870#p93870 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Paladins fulfill three primary roles.
Healers were well-balanced previously and remain so. They require solid equipment to perform effectively, but once equipped, they excel at maintaining the health of all players in the game.
DPS Paladins required enhancements. They didn't match up to vanilla enhancement shamans, a significant comparison. Despite adjustments, they still don't excel in PvE content, but they wield significant power in PvP scenarios, being resilient and inflicting high damage quickly. However, in raids with bosses boasting immense health pools, consistent damage output and threat management take precedence, areas where they struggle.
Tank Paladins needed attention, received some, yet could benefit from more. They suffice for most tasks, including raids, and can serve adequately as secondary tanks. However, further adjustments are necessary for them to truly excel.
