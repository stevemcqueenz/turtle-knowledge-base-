# Seal of Righteousness downranking.

## Post 4069 by Chlothar (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-09-24T19:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4069#p4069 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have something you holy warriors could be interested in. Seal of Righteousness rank3 has a better scaling with SP than other ranks. So if you stack some Spellpower rank3 will do the same dmg per hit as rank8 for much lower mana cost. At some point you will even do more dmg with rank3 SoR. Only downside is that the judgement will do much less dmg than rank8 SoR-Judgement ofc.
