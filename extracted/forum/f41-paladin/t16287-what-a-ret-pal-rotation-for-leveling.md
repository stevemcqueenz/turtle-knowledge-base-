## Post 111024 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-03T08:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111024#p111024 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Judge wisdom if stuff takes more than 5-6 swings to kill. Otherwise dont use a debuff judgement, keep command up while swinging and click either crusader strike for fast killspeed or holy strike if you wanna conserve more mana while killing.

Do not use hammer of wrath if you wanna keep chainkilling. It costs too much mana

Buff yourself with either wisdom or might. Kings is only worth while levelling if you have good gear

Make sure to wear a SLOW weapon (anything faster than 3.0 you should only consider if its a big weapon upgrade as both crusader strike & soc heavily benefit from slow weapons)

Ifyou cant decide between two weapons try them both. So you get a feel for it
