Seal of the Crusader is definitely terrible and I've made my own thoughts on it quite known: [viewtopic.php?f=41&t=4177](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=41&t=4177)

However, using TBC/Wrath as a comparison to vanilla design philosophies is a mistake. There are a plethora of options that can be taken without dipping toes into the ideas that furthered WoW's decline. That is why we play a vanilla style server is it not?

I think the biggest issue here is having a clear design intent for paladin. Vanilla leaves paladins pretty lacking in direction, especially after patch 1.9, as the game wasn't built with their overhauled design in mind. Hell, the spell coefficients weren't even adjusted to be decent when the base damage is terrible. And personally I think the existence of holy strike hamstrings further changes to the class. It really is too much. Same for CS.

Want paladins to fulfill the fantasy of being anti-undead instead of being lackluster at is supposed to be their thing? Remove the cooldown on turn undead. Halve the cost and reduce the cooldown of exorcism to 5 seconds. Halve the cost and reduce the cooldown of holy wrath to 8-10 seconds. It literally affects nothing but PvE content and its not even in an overpowered way.

Want stock paladin to perform better in pve with minimal pvp impact? Halve the mana cost of HoW and allow it to not to reset the swing timer. Allow JoC to do double damage against naturally stun immune creatures. These are just examples and there is a lot more that could be done to minimally change the fundamental design while also minimally impacting the game without borrowing from the expansions.
