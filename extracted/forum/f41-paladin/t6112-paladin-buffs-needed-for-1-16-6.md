Hey guys so I think we're really undertuned, epecially in PvP (not enough burst damage)

My changes purposed as below:

Holy Strike - Just flat out underpowered and not worth using. Cant even one shot an 8k hp warrior with it
I suggest to increase the damage by 500% atleast or remove cooldown.

Divine Shield - Only lasts 12 seconds. This needs to just last about 30-40 seconds, we arent immune for long enough to one shot the entire horde pvp team in BG in 12 seconds. Also reduce cooldown to 2 mins if possible.

Lay on Hands - Absolute pointless... 1 hour cooldown for max hp? I've never used this... It should restore hp and mana and grant +200 strength for 2 minutes... Also remove cooldown.

Crusader Strike - This needs to proc seals and do more damage like in TBC.. maybe 350% weapon damage?

Hammer of Justice - its ok , but i think an extra 10 seconds would do it... Actually no need because ive normally globaled my target in PvP anyways.

Blessing of Freedom - Garbage... this needs to be the same effect as Free Action Potion but last 60 seconds.

Holy Protection Potions - These really screw our class over, it absorbs up to 3250 Holy damage, this is a huge problem, it takes me an extra 3 seconds to kill my target... please ban from pvp

I think thats it for now, but this class seriously needs help and I hope the devs buff Paladins, because in their current state we may aswel roll shaman (who are borderline useless too)
