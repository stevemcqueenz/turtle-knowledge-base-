# Can Paladin just get a normal taunt like the other tanks?

## Post 51837 by Getplucked (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-29T22:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51837#p51837 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Druid is a "hybrid" class but still has a regular taunt spell, so I don't understand why Paladin is stuck with this weird janky setup that requires 3 talent points and also makes you use a GCD and judgement CD just to taunt.

Since using this taunt also deprives you of one of your two on-demand threat abilities, often times you taunt something and then the DPS immediately pulls threat on it again because your only threat after the taunt is an auto attack. Like imagine if taunting as a Warrior prevented you from using Sunder/Revenge for the next 8 seconds. That'd be dumb. But that's effectively what Paladin is stuck with.

It's a creative solution to giving Paladins a taunt in vanilla, but in practice it's just bad and clunky as hell. Can we just get a normal taunt spell like Warrior and Druid have?
