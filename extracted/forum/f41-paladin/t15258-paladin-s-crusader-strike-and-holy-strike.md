Currently the rotation looks like this for prot/ret Paladin:
Holy Strike - 6 sec, Crusader Strike - x3*6 sec,then ret/pal renew buff Holy Strike and spam Crusader Strike.
Paladin's defense has a similar principle.

I suggest removing Holy Strike from the general book and adding it to line 5 or 7 of Holy Paladin, removing the overall cooldown, or adding a trigger from blessed strikes.

This solves the problem of removing the damage component of Holy Shock that matches your vision

Developer Comment: Holy Paladin’s damage right now can be quite bursty at times, thanks to Holy Shock. With the new talents allowing for it to reset, we felt like the ranged burst could become too dangerous. Overall Holy damage should be higher thanks to the new strikes, but will require you to remain in melee.
While we understand that the “shockadin” specialization is beloved by many, it does not fit our vision for the class.

and we can now remove the Holy Strike components from Blessed Strikes, Righteous Strikes and Crusader Strikes and give Crusader Strike as a buff. let's call, for example, holy power that gives buffs to talents of the same name.

I would also like to say something about the Stronghold of the Righteous.
It's worth reducing the damage and increasing the CD. this is too strong an ability for a tank in 3 directions Damage/Aggro/CD.

For comparison:

New Spell: Barkskin (Feral)
Learned at Level 44 with new ranks at Level 52 and 60.
Requires Bear Form, Dire Bear Form, Cat Form
The druid's skin becomes tough as bark, reducing the damage of the next 5/10/15 melee attacks by 50%. Movement speed is reduced by 20% during its duration. Lasts for 12 seconds.
(10 Minute cooldown)

Shield Wall (warrior)
Instant	30 min cooldown
Reduces the damage taken from melee attacks, ranged attacks and spells by 75% for 10 sec.
Improved Shield Wall moved from row 5 to row 6. Now reduces the cooldown of Shield Wall by 5/10 min and increases its duration by 1/2 sec - from talent

New Capstone Talent: Bulwark of the Righteous (7th Row Capstone)
Requires Shields.
Bash the target with your shield, dealing 556 to 601 Holy damage and reducing damage you take by 40% for 12 seconds.
(3 Minute Cooldown, 200 Mana Cost, 5 Yard Range, has a 43% Spell Power scaling coefficient for all ranks.).
