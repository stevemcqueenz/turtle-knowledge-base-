- Seals are indefinite buffs that are consumed upon Judgement.

- Seals can only unsealed normally through just Judgement.

- If a paladin breaks a seal, they incur repentance (with a flavor*)

- Seals are sturdy and very resistant to dispel.

- In case the pesky voodoo guy is persistent, and the seal is broken, **they** incur holy wrath.
