Besides, there are better solutions than exorcism.

 1. Move holy shock to 3rd row of holy tree (or baseline) and nerf it's damage/healing, then make the final holy talent un-nerf it.
 2. Make a deep prot talent to extend the range of judgement
 3. Make a Libram with an on-use ability that hits for a very small amount of holy damage with a range of 40 yards.
 4. Make a deep prot talent which reduces the damage of hammer of wrath by 90% and removes the targets health requirement.

Some things to keep in mind about Paladin spells/abilities:
 - judgement of righteousness, holy shock, and exorcism are spells which means they use spell hit and spell crit. Paladin has no spell hit items decent for them so using any of these abilities has a high chance to fail.
 - hammer of wrath and judgement of command are abilities which use melee hit and crit. For suggestions to improve ret damage or add reliable prot range pulls these spells fit better.
 - the reason why adding any damaging ability to ret breaks PvP is because ret can perform a holy strike + seal of command proc + judgement of command before even spending a global cooldown. If the target is stunned that combo alone can global most moderately geared clothes, before even considering weapon procs' or shadow oil. Adding any damage on top of that breaks PvP further. Seal of command is the broken part and needs a rework.
