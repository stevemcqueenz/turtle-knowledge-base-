Hi, Freddofooz, and welcome to Turtle WoW.   hiding_smth_turtle_head

The design behind Turtle WoW's continued development of vanilla are additions or minor changes to classes to help fill the gap and give underdog specializations a fighting chance at endgame.

These two sources may help you come to your decision about playing retribution paladin in the December 27 1.16 Mysteries of Azeroth patch:

Talents: <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin>
Class changes: [viewtopic.php?f=2&t=2197](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=2197)

Consecration (which is now baseline), Judgement of Righteousness, and Retribution Aura will now scale better with spellpower. Crusader Strike is being added into the game, but it is mana intensive and uses a debuff slot. As it stacks unlike Judgement of the Crusader, it might be best used to help protection paladins tank dungeons and raids (which will still not be easy) and likely rare. I would think of Crusader Strike as more of a paladin Sunder Armor rather than more direct damage. Holy Strike is also having its cooldown increased by 2 seconds. While consecration is now baseline, Sanctity Aura will takes its place at 11 points into Holy tree. Retribution is now receiving Blessing of Kings again (like in patch 1.1). I imagine Turtle raids even with higher population wouldn't mind having one ret paladin for Kings, BUT that's assuming you're willing to put in that extra work. So while paladins as a whole are getting buffed, Ret I can't imagine will be in that much better of a place in raids.

HOWEVER, as a paladin, you are a hybrid class. I think you will get the most fun out of your class if you treat it as a hyrbid. Why not run dungeons and raids with something like this build:
<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin/Z5JCW6V-0-Z00V>
Wear some tier gear with a fast two hander, deal holy damage, and judge your targets to help benefit your group (Holy Shock is getting a buff too). When you don't need to heal, you're dealing damage! This can be done in raids too, and I'd hope to see more players embrace hybrid classes. While this may not scratch your retribution itch, DPS/Healing/Support hybrid playstyle is far easier to get into groups with, and will teach you more about the class overall. I know far too many people who thought Arthas was cool, and decided to spec full ret with limited understanding of their class.

The Turtle Team has mentioned in the past that these changes are not set in stone, and if they find a way to buff Retribution further without upsetting the balance of the game, they likely will in a future update.

I hope this helped clarify some things about the upcoming patch, and give you an idea of what you want to play.
satisfied_turtle
