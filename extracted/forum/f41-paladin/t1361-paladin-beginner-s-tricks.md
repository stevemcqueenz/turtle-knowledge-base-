# [Paladin] Beginner's tricks.

## Post 8305 by Unangwata (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-10-30T13:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8305#p8305 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For optimal combat performance I would recommend few techniques a paladin could learn.

Opening a fight.
Usually paladin just opens fight with Judgement at close range. This is not very effective, because it's followed by weapon swing that has no Seal effect on, so it doesn't have chance to proc additional damage. It's efficiency loss.
The solution is to learn to use Judgement at maximum range and then cast Seal before swing is made. This requires some practice, because if you press judgement to soon then it won't be cast, and if you follow it with Seal you will just lose mana. You can look at range indicator at your quickbar, but it's inconvenient. If you just practice judging the distance with your eye you will soon learn to do it with no problems.
Adding Holy Strike. Even better way to open a fight is prepare Holy Strike, you will use it immediately when fight starts, so you can use it quicker again. Prepare Holy Strike and then open with Judgement at distance and follow up with Seal before swing is made, this results in Holy Strike attack and chance to proc Seal as well. This is especially effective when opening with Judgement of the Crusader that increase holy damage of following Holy Strike.

Fight.
Important thing during a fight is to time your Judgement well. If you do it just before weapon swing happens then you will not have enough time to cast Seal again before the swing hits, which results in a hit without Seal on and loss of damage potential. Observe your weapon swing and Judge only when you still have time to put Seal back on.

Gear.
During leveling always value Intellect. It's more useful for you than stamina is, because you are paladin and you can heal. Potentially Intellect grants you more health than Stamina does.
Also value Spirit. This support stat will let you skip drinking and eating between fights, unless these are really long fights.

Talents.
It's always your personal preference. However, if you like 2h weapons and plan to go Retribution, consider dropping that path right after you get Seal of Command or after you get 5% crit bonus, and go Holy Tree. This is very beneficial. You get plain 10% str increase, resistance to interrupts while healing (which is way more valuable than it seems when solo leveling), and my personal favorite Divine Favor -it grants your heal a crit effect with no mana lost, which works with Holy Light almost as if you have Lay on Hands every 2 min, and you can use it as life saver or mana conserver every cd to heal.

These are my few starting tips for pals.

edit: Another mana conserving trick is to use Divine Shield/Blessing of Protection + bandages to heal during a fight. It won't get interrupted and u will save lots of mana. Level up your First Aid and use the best bandages for that purpose.
