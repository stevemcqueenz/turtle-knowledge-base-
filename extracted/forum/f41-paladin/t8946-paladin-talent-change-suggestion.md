# Paladin Talent Change Suggestion

## Post 57793 by Jonyhand — 2023-08-17T20:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57793#p57793 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1. Changing improved seal of justice to improved exorcism that allow you to use exorcism at all sort of creature which have an extra taunt effect, it still only do damage to undead or demon.
Firstly, you can choose whatever seals to judge instead of saving cd for justice seal, it will improve the playability. besides, 30 yards spell range can make up the paladin short legs drawback.
Secondly, greater mana cost and longer cd(15-30s as designer want) make it impossible to replace warrior and druid main tank status, but its ok, the long range taunt and sacrifice spirt can save the raid in emergency.

 2.  Changing redoubt talent back to be victim of critical attack or spell and improve the block chance.
They have the ardent defender talent to avoid dying in one sec. without defense level stacking, the stamina ,armor, and block value can max the defender effect.
Newly-added hitting by critical spell mean you have more chance to trigger redoubt, that's same with reckoning trigger condition. In total, this is still a nerf compared with 10% hitting chance.

 3. Changing improved command seal to recover only your 5-10% base mana when the seal do any extra critical damage include command seal trigger and its judgment.
In level road, as your melee critical odds nearly equal 0, using the harmer of justice or repentance for 100% judgement critical may be the only way to trigger it. as gear upgrade, you will find the growth and potential, that may become your motivation to keep playing it.
My bis ret Pala is that you should always monitor the rest of mana, when continuously command seal critical happened, you have enough mana for holy strike in the next several auto attack, it cause lots of additional damage.

Critical make Pala possible and impossible, healing Spell critical make holy Pala stand between priest and shaman, as well as prot and ret, at least that's what I thought.
