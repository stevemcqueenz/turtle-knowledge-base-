I'm new to the TWoW PvE Server, and due to the differences compared to the "other game," I'm not quite sure how to spec properly with all the tweaks here.

I'm aiming for a single spec from level 1 to 60, as respeccing is quite expensive and I’m starting with 0 gold. My goal is to have a survivable build that doesn't require much drinking between mobs.

From what I’ve read on forums and Reddit, some players recommend investing 7–8 points into the Holy tree first for the Intellect bonus and pushback reduction — which can be life-saving.
However, from what I understand, these talents might not be as crucial at early levels due to the server changes. For example, Holy Strike already feels like a nice QoL improvement in terms of kill speed (currently level 7 ![😄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f604.svg)).

My current plan:
Focus only on Protection until level 30, taking Holy Shield as the final Prot talent.

Then switch to the Retribution tree until level 42 to grab Improved Run Speed.

At level 49, switch to Holy, pick up the Intellect and pushback talents, then finish off the Protection tree and take Kings last.

For gear:
I’ll be prioritizing:
Spirit > Intellect > Spell Damage (and if possible, more Spell Damage).

Does this approach make sense for a smooth leveling experience — basically becoming an unstoppable crusader? ![😄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f604.svg)

For leveling there are essentially 2 ways: deep ret and damage focused on 1 foe. This one is the most standard way of leveling, doing quests and such.

Deep prot with spiritual focus from holy tree. This will give you less single target damage since you playing with mh + shield but allows you to slowly kill 5 enemies at once, which can be very effective if you want to focus on killing mobs and kill mobs related quests.

In any case, grab any free +int gear you can get (never ninja in groups, ask 1st) so you can go and heal in case you're needed. You'll do just fine with +int gear in dungeons until you reach your mid 50's
