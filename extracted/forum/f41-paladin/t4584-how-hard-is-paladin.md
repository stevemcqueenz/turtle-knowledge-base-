Paladin was changed here, it's no longer seal + auto attacks only, they added crusader strike and holy strike so it's a bit more dynamic. I don't think it's the easiest class, hybrid classes (paladin,druid,shaman) are a bit more complex since they can fill all 3 roles (healer/tank/dps). Mage is the easiest for a new player I would say, you can only use one ability if you want and still be effective. Ret Paladin in PvE is lacking even with the changes, so I wouldn't recommend it, you'll be oom most of the time and will have to drink more than a healer. Healing spec is the most viable but in 5man it requires awareness and can be stressful. Tank spec is viable as well but it's not an easy role to fill.
