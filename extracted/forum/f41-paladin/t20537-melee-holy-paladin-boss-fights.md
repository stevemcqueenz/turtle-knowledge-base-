Certain mobs have mechanics which affect melee only.
Not strictly regarding bosses, imagine f.ex. the Scholomance tutors doing the aoe silence.
Standing in melee range means you won't be able to heal the crew for a while.
Some mobs are doing aoe stun or haste penalty.
Beside that, the cleaves and extra damage you take (risking losing a healer + needing to target you with heals instead of others who get damaged anyway).

## Post 144592 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-11T17:20:00Z

Blessed strikes indeed incentivizes you to stand in melee range, but there are a few limiting factors.

The range on the Holy Strike healing is melee only. Depending on how many targets are in closeproximity to the mob, you might put yourself in harms way for no extra healing. Especially in dungeons where shield slams, silences or fears are being cast, thats a gamble that can backfire spectacularly.

Blessed strikes is mainy for raidhealing.

There it is vital that you know the bosses, as it is really good in some fights and trolling in others.

Bosses where you should usually avoid it:
MC: Gehennas, Garr(boss is ok, never stand close to adds), Shazzrah, Sulfuron Herald (ae stun), Majodormo (the firenovas hurt you more than you gain), Ragnaros (he targets manaclasses with an aoe knockback that tends to kill a lot of melees if it hits someone close to them)
BWL:razorgore(melee cc abilities), lashlayer (knockback), Chromaggus (the only target taking dmg is the tank anyway),  Nefarian (Fear)
Aq: Bug Trio(fights too chaotic you loose too much time moving), Sartura, Twins(can mess the tanks up and can get you killed if you dont know the fight in and out), Cthun (chance to kill melees on chaining)

Tldr: know the boss mechanics and only go in if you know for certain its an upsidd because while it is better to have the option to use blessed strikes, if you do it in the wrong fights you kill yourself AND others
