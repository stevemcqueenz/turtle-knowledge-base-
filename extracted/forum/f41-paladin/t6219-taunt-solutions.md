# Taunt solutions

## Post 39138 by Enceebe (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-26T12:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39138#p39138 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a newcomer, I've noticed there's been some debate about the Taunt with SoJ in the game. However, I have to agree that it currently feels underwhelming, especially compared to taunts from druids, warriors and pets or compared to Hammer of Justice when it comes to crowd controll. While the melee swing from SoJ can be useful for interrupting enemy actions or stopping them from fleeing, it doesn't quite live up to the Taunt function that it's supposed to provide.

One potential solution could be to tie Taunt to the Righteous Fury ability. For example, when using Judgment with Righteous Fury active, it could automatically Taunt enemies as well. Alternatively, SoJ could be reworked to function more as a debuff that not only stops enemies from fleeing but also Taunts them. This way, Taunt could become a more practical  ability to use in combat.
