Hey

I want to give my Feedback on Ret Paladin:

First of all, this post does not care about any numbers. Numbers can be tuned. I do not excatly know why, but playing Ret is not so much fun atm/anymore. My guesses when I think about it are the following:
Youre only looking on timers to not fuck stuff up.
- You need to watch a swing timer, for Judging/reapplying the Seal

- You’re constatly on a hastle to keep the CS stacks up. Its just so bad when they fel off between groups. Remember: 18 seconds and 3 gcd to build them up.

- HS is another timer to keep up for the buff
To add on the Timers, the Rotation itself feels empty – espacially outside naxx. In Naxx you got a usable Exo now and thats fun to use, but outside youre just looking on timers.

I was playing a lot of Enh sham too since patch and boy is it fun. Simple mechanic – Stormstrike buffs your next Lightning Strike and earthshock. So a Prio list and not a fixed rota. Handling diff shields lets you actively decide between mana and dps at all time, throwing totems in there…. Its fun.

Why do I bring up the sham? With the downtime after Judgement and CS or HS, Ret Pala reminds a lot of Enh Sham b4 CC2 (the dead time, not the numbers) – but with additional timers you have to check every second. xD

My Suggestions would be, cut off some timers and add other usable stuff in the toolkit.
- I know, you dont want us to have a good aoe for 0 mana, so consec prob isnt the button

- Exo outside undead/demon would loose vanilla charm

- But we still have a completly useless button: Hammer of Wrath. Maybe a complete overhaul. Ive done a suggestion for it in discord and copy it to the end of this

- The changes with SoC are cool, it feels nice to press.

- HS maybe feels good, when it does not hit like a wet noodle – you only press it for another timer.

- CS itself is cool and I like the idea of getting stronger with each hit. Very paladin like. But if it uses a timer, it should last longer.
Here an idea, to replace a timer HS for Example:
With one of the attacks you could build stacks that last and dont reset after X seconds. Each stack buffs you in some way (paladin like). After X amount of stacks, you get an empowered version if the attack that has stacked it and the stacks get deleted.

I hope it gets read
cheers

Hammer of Wrath suggestion:
It could be just a Range Weapon attack with X% of Wep Damage on a 30 sec to 1 min CD. Without any restriction on how much HP the NPC has left.

This would massivly help Tanks to Pull stuff and gives Rets an option to do anything in Range (except Exo, but thats just undead). With the condition as Weapon attack, you can make sure it will not get too strong with SP as a tank. And a long CD should help with any PvP issues

I've been tempted to write my own feedback on the changes for the class overall, but my biggest annoyance at the moment is that I don't like how Crusader Strike and Holy Strike works, especially as they share cooldown. While one button is useful for one spec, the other just ends up being a chore you have to manage. Yes, as Ret, it's a lot better than spamming the Crusader Strike every second for Windfury buff, but could still be better.

For Ret: Crusader Strike is your go-to choice for damage. Also gives a nice attack speed boost! But you need to use Holy Strike purely for the strength buff (And it's a shame since using attacks to heal/restore mana to the group is something I wanna do, even as Ret).

And while not Ret in specific, still issues with them for the other two specs.
For Prot: Holy Strike for damage, some regen and the extra threat. Crusader Strike is purely for the buff. The annoying thing though is that unlike Ret with HS, this has to be used 3 times for full effect and if that drops off... Well, have fun getting your stacks back.

For Holy: I don't have much experience with it but I never much bother Crusader Strike to refresh Holy Shock, so it's a dead ability on my end and just use Holy Strike.

Overall, I feel like we could get rid of one of the buttons, add some of their functions to Judgement, and use the CS/HS Talents on each tree to instead buff the one remaining and Judgement.
