# Protection Paladin Tier set bonuses V1.0

## Post 71623 by Partyboyza — 2023-10-17T19:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71623#p71623 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I main a protection paladin (Belriose), and with the implementation of the new spec Tier sets I would like to create a threat where we can post our suggestions to help the devs with ideas.

I would like to invite everybody to offer suggestions and opinions on what do we think the best affixes would work for each Tier set.
I've presented each set bonus with what I consider to be the most valuable stats a protection paladin would need:

 **2 Item sets:**
 1. +2 T1/ +3 T2 / +4 T4 Holy Shield Charges
 2. +3% T1 / +6% T2 / +9% T3 Block chance
 3. +40 T1 / +80 T2 / +120 T3  Block Value
 4. +10 T1 / +20 T2 / + 30 T3 holy Damage on weapon swing
 5. +3% T2 / +6% T2 / +9% T3 Dmg Reduction
 6. +20 T1 Defense ( no point in adding +defense on T2 and T3 since that will be easier to acquire)
 7. Holy Shield charges return mana +50 T1 / +75 T2 / +100 T3  to the party when consumed.

 **4 Item sets:**
 1. +2% T1 / +4% T2 / +6% T3 Dodge Chance
 2. +15% T1 / +20% T2 / +25% T3 SoR Dmg
 3. +5% T1 / +7% T2 / +10% T3 Consecration dmg
 4. +2% T1 / +4% T2 / +5% T3 chance to hit
 5. +20 T1 / + 25 T2 / + 30 T3 damage reduction from all sources (like Blessing of Sanctuary)
 6. +100% T1 / +200% T2 / + 300% T3 increased effect of Blessing of Sanctuary (epic effect for AOE farming)

 **6 Item sets:**
 1. +1 T1 / +2 T2 / +3 T3 enemy targets affected by your Judgements 5yd range
 2. +10% T1 / +10% T2 / +15% T3 Consecration dmg
 3. +15 T1 / +25 T2 / +35 T3 extra stamina to everyone affected by you Devotion Aura ( or auras in general) - like a priest buff
 4. +10T1 / + 20 T2 / + 30 T3 Devotion aura also reduces damage from all sources (like Blessing of Sanctuary, only that new is an aura)
 5. -15% T1 / -20% T2 / - 25% T3 reduced attack speed to anyone affected by your judgements

 **8 Item sets:**
 1. +20 T1 / +30 T2 / +40 T3 extra stamina to everyone affected by you Devotion Aura ( or auras in general) - like a priest buff, doesn't stack with each other
 2. -10% T1 / -15% T2 / -20% T3 reduced attack speed to any enemy affected by your consecration
 3. T3 only Your Divine shield affects your entire party 30 yd range (might be too OP but cool nevertheless)
 4. T1 / T2 / T3  Your divine shield reduces dmg taken by 60%
 5. T3 only When Holy Shield is consumed it damages all nearby enemies 5yd range for half damage
 6. +20mp +25hp T1 / +30mp +40hp T2 / +40mp +50hp T3 to all targets affected by your consecration
 7. +3% T1 / +6% T2 / +9% T3 damage reduction to anyone affected by your devotion aura
 8. Your Holy Shield will taunt an enemy for 3 seconds when used, can also be used without a target
 9. -5% T1 / -8% T2 / -10% T3 damage reduction for 8 seconds after using Holy Shield

Choosing the right set bonuses for each set will be hardest part.

These are just some of my ideas for the Tier set bonuses.
Please give me your opinions and feel free to add your suggestions, I will try and update with your suggestions.
I hope this helps the devs to get some ideas on what would benefit the protection paladin the most.
