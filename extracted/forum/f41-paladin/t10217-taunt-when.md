This is such gross misattribution of game mechanics. "Taunt" is not an invention of World of Warcraft, of Warriors, or of Bear Druids. This is an ability as ancient as tabletop RPGs and exists for an express purpose to fulfill an explicit need in mechanical systems that design enemies with an "I don't fucking care how good you've been playing, fuck your healer now" mechanic, which World of Warcraft absolutely has. You clearly have never played Paladin as a tank for an extended period of time if you think their current, piss-poor implementation of Taunt is "good enough".

For starters, the baseline taunt from Wrath onward has ALREADY been ported and just put behind some incredibly questionable mechanics, so this starts your argument on shaky grounds. Other than the fact that you have to put 3 talent points at levels 25, 26, and 27 into an otherwise terrible talent that affects a terrible Seal, actually executing your Taunt is worse than shifting out of Bear Form or switching stances and dumping all of your Rage, because at least in those scenarios you can get Rage back. Prot Paladin, if you didn't know, relies on very finite amounts of mana (as they aren't stacking copious amounts of Intellect) and very costly spells (not even talking about Consecrate) to generate threat outside of reactive threat and sustain their mana. This comes in the form of Judgement of Wisdom and Seal of Righteousness, both locked behind an 8-10 second cooldown Judgement, which also costs mana.

Imagine if you will, that you are operating at roughly 1000 mana or less in the middle of a pull.  This happens QUITE frequently given the nature of the build, as I have described before.  God help you if you are fighting things you cannot block to regenerate mana, such as casters or Elementals. All of a sudden, you need to taunt. Now you need to spend 170 mana on a seal no one wants to use, spend 75+ mana on a Judgement you don't want to use, and then you need to spend another 215 on Holy Strike and 170 on Seal of Righteousness to try to HOLD that aggro and build up threat again which just got dumped.

Consider that in 8-10 seconds, you are either not regenerating mana or generating threat as you must choose between Seal of Wisdom or Righteousness, so you also need to be ready to spend another 170 mana to get Seal of Wisdom back on, so that you can judge that, then put Seal of Righteousness back on, and hope that the threat dump mechanic hasn't happened again in that time.

What you end up with is a tank tapped out of mana incapable of generating further threat unless the enemy is glued to you and triggering blocks, which relies on, you guessed it, having aggro. All of this because having a baseline taunt is "too similar". Consider this perspective: Warrior and Bear don't have Taunt because it was designed explicitly around their class identity, they have Taunt because Taunt, in a vacuum, is a necessary part of a tank's toolkit.

tl;dr If your class doesn't have a baseline taunt in the context of World of Warcraft NPC combat mechanics, your class is not an actual tank and the design team should not attempt to lean into it being a tank. Prot Paladin's current taunt implementation cannot work unless the Paladin has more ways to regenerate mana than having the enemy's attention at all times.
