The prot tree has always been bad in vanilla.  What og vanilla had for warrior tanks that's been changed in TWoW is a complete monopoly over tanking due to a lack of options.  The raid tanking options in vanilla were so severely limited that using a 31 fury build, furyprot, took over the role.

This gives players the misguided conclusion that warriors were strong tanks in vanilla when in reality it was always trying to do the most with the extremely limited options.

Why does bear excel in TWoW?  Not only do they have outright better tps numbers, they have better options for generating tps.  Swipe is on the gcd, Maul is on next swing, savage bite is ogcd.  This means that bears can potentially deliver 3 hits on any given gcd.  That is excellent parry/dodge protection.
