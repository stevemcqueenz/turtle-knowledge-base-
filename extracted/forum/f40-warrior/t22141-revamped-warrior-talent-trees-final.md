# Revamped Warrior Talent Trees (FINAL)

## Post 153548 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-09-28T15:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153548#p153548 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/H6PojEa.png)

My goals were to address deeply unfair/unfun PVP gameplay problems --
Whilst being as conservative as possible.
I also know T-WoW devs are highly adverse to TBC and WoTLK so I tried to avoid copy/pasting.
Special thanks to 'Kyrsie/Willwonka' for providing framework/inspiration.

 **BASELINE CHANGES:**
Charge now has a 1.5 second stun.
Intercept damage is based off Attack Power.
Revenge has a 45% chance to stun for 3 seconds.
Rend's Bleed Damage increased by 20%.
Hamstring snare duration is reduced to 12 seconds.

>>[**LINK TO NEW TALENT TREES**](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator?t0=Nb8LXgLHY3&t1=kM2pVBwMNI&t2=tai_ojUqFw)<<

 **[ARMS](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/Nb8LXgLHY3) (Brief Notes):**
Given more control over enemies (lower CD Hamstring and chance to root).

(Conditionally) increased self-healing from Healing Potion...
and all other sources. Actually makes a Warrior a desirable healing target.

Sundering Strikes gives you a reason to wail on Tanks (commonly seen in T-WoW PVP).

Shattering Slam sometimes (requires luck) breaks an Immunity Shield (Bubble/Ice Block/LiP/etc)
(these are deeply problematic in PVP mostly cuz of having 0 direct counterplay options).

 **[FURY](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/kM2pVBwMNI) (Brief Notes):**
They are already in a decent spot so I primarily gave them more flavor.
Specifically -- 'crazed maniac zoomie mobility'.

 **[PROT](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/tai_ojUqFw) (Brief Notes):**
Bloodrage will allow a Warrior to gain Rage whilst beating on Immunity Shields and 'Absorb Shields' -- things such as PW: Shield, Ice Barrier, various trinkets, etc.
Increased support + mobility in one talent. (Speed boost for Warrior + partner)
More resist/survivability against all spells.
Can outright cockblock certain types of spells (Projectile).
