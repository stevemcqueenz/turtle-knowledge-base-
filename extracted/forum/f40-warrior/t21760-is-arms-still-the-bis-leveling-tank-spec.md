## Post 150752 by Oomentaloo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-08T07:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150752#p150752 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You can tank with every spec. I like to take Tactical Mastery, so I can play around with all the tools of the warrior. The mobs don't hit hard enough so you don't get much out of Prot imho.
So if you are tanky enough for the dungeons you need threat and rage. you get threat and rage through damage and threat spells that cost rage. So as long your are not a too heavy burden on your healer dw or 2h tanking is good and maybe even easier.
But when the mobs are hitting too hard you have to have a shield to mitigate some dmg.
On Edwin Van Cleef or Verdan the Everliving in Wailing Caverns I would probably put on the shield, but trash mobs can mostly be tanked without.
TacMastery lets you do stuff like Charge -> Def stance -> ThunderClap -> Demo Shout.

So macros for weapon/shield swap and macro for shield block that has the shield swap built-in is a nice thing to have.
