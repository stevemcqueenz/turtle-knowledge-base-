## Post 134748 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-06-08T14:16:00Z

You can download Atlas loot in the launcher if you want an addon to help with your search. There's a good chunk of new dungeons so you'll have plenty to choose from in regards to gearing and looking through the list of stuff can help you decide where you want to go for upgrades. One of my recommendations is probably going to be hateforge Quarry. It's got one of the few pre-raid trinkets that have hit on it, on top of armor pen and nature resist for raids that need that. There's other stuff besides that that you'll want but it's one of the highlights for me considering trinkets are a bit rare.

## Post 135325 by Noephix (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-11T13:44:00Z

If you' Alliance - consider farming a crapton of runecloth and getting Revered with Silvermoon Remnants. they have many amazing pre-raid blues for warriors.

Horde has Revantusk trolls - but instead of Runecloth turn-ins those have a daily quest. Which is waaaay slower (but also cheaper). You might still want to grind it for a bear mount, though.

Early tier raids are very easy and don't require any good pre-raid gear. Hop in as a fresh 60 and get free upgrades.
