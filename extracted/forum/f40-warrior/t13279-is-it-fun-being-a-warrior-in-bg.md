## Post 89058 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-02-29T15:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89058#p89058 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You will be kited to oblivion and most if not all classes will toy with your ass unless you bring consumes, play to your fullest and spec engi. Many classes have better cc, better mobility, better damage and better self sustain. In solo fights you're kinda weak. Most classes deal magic damage to at least some degree so your armor is essentially worthless in a lot of situations. On top of that, being a physical damage dealer means other player's armor hinders your damage (especially paladins and other warriors, as well as druid to some degree)

Honestly, if you want braindead big numbers unbeatable performance and flashy piss colored instant win buttons, go paladin. If you want a challenge and a sense of accomplishment for every big play and highlight, roll warrior. They do make pretty good team players as far as I hear and heals does bump them up a bit.
