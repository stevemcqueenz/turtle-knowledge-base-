## Post 103027 by Zulnam (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-10-13T19:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103027#p103027 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

druid can tank most bosses in raids.

The only real downside when compared to warrior is that you need to already be somewhat geared, where as warriors are the go-to tank for progression raids. even in mostly blue gear they can still tank most of the MC bosses and their tier set is specifically built for tanking, making it much easier to gear up and progress the raid.

In contrast druid tank gearing is still a bit awkward (leaps better than vanilla tho), so you will have to run some raids first as OT or dps and gear up that way.

Also your main raids for gearing are different: where a wariior can get pretty good tanking gear from MC, druid has to focus on AQ20. And many of your BiS items for tanking are still blue items (see discord druid channel pins for list of gear).

As for hitcap, warrior can probably reach it easier than druid imo. If you go human you will also get like 3% hit from the weapon skill but keep in mind these are subject to change in the upcoming major patch, so i would not choose a human warrior now for the weapon skill racial alone.
