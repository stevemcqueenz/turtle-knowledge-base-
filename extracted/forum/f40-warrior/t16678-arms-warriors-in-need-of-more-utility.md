# Arms Warriors in need of more utility!

## Post 113971 by Feetlover444 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-23T10:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113971#p113971 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently as of latest patch and every patch before that warriors got clowned on by literally every other class.

There's no matchup a warrior is strong against, literally none especially with latest patch, any class can easily dispatch a warrior.

The dps of a warrior is fine its not super amazing or anything but due to being a plate class I'm going to give it a pass.

But what a warrior severely lacks is being able to counterplay against all the other classes.

Wotlk gave us shattering throw to counter ice block/bubble.
TBC gave us spell reflect to counter all kinds of spells to which we are super weak against.
We also got second wind in TBC that helped us with sustain (A warrior should have good sustain due to not having any heal on demand in his kit)

Normally being a plate class you'd think you'd be the longest standing throughout a fight, but spells of course bypass any and all your armor and just disable you in 2 seconds either way with the burst heavy meta in pvp.

It makes no sense that a warrior can't hold his ground against any other class in a 1v1 situation, arms is severely lacking in utility being a PVP class foremost it needs more utility in its arsenal.

My proposal is having a good look at tbc warriors and perhaps bring some of that tbc utility to twow warriors. If you guys ever end up releasing rated arena's warriors in their current state won't be able to compete at all, it'll just be insane ccfest all over the place.

Another proposal I have is nerfing polymorphs stuns saps and fears, super long cc's are broken especially in the burst heavy meta we have. Make all of them have diminishing returns and maybe increase their cd's and slash their cc durations.

Tbh at current patch every other class or needs massive tuning down, or warriors just flatout need more utility in their kit to keep up with these overtuned classes.

There was a time in life where I'd been completely convinced warriors were dogshit in PvP, but I think it's safe to say the whole "Warrior is great with a healer!" take isn't as half baked as me and like two other people at best thought.

Think of it like this.

A warrior at max rage is dangerous. Big damage and mortal strike debuff can be pretty debilitating.

A warrior who can't be stopped is also dangerous. Faps are key in a lot of fights and having support from a paladin to remove CCs can be huge. Pally freedom is niche and not going to be readily available especially for the hordies (No fweedom, orc sadge) but ANYONE and everyone can use fap. Warrior just benefits from it more since it allows them to play their class better and crush face without being slowed/rooted. It covers their biggest weakness in the form of being kited.

These two factors can be achieved through one thing. Sustainability. If your warrior is getting heals, and they know how to play (free CC-begone button not required to do that surprisingly enough) they can be the sole cause for a close win in a team engagement. Want an important target debilitated and made easier to kill? Chase that healer (don't forget your fap) and mortal strike them. Keeping topped off will be infinitely more difficult and if you manage to hamstring or someone has them CC'd it'll make getting away more difficult, ultimately securing a kill.

Also- rogues. Rogue is warrior's one particular 1v1 "counter". This isn't to say a warrior will dominate a rogue, but they're definitely pretty advantaged in comparison to matchups with other classes. I will admit warriors are pretty mid in terms of duels and in the wild PvP encounters, but vanilla isn't meant to balance every class to be good in every niche. That just wouldn't be smart. Warrior is already the best DPS class. Apparently they're not the best tanks anymore but even besides that the fact that they're the de-facto best at anything proves there's some semblance of balance. And again, team fights. Warr fills a niche in BG PvP with the purpose of being a spearhead and enabler. If that warrior isn't getting clowned on then chances are someone would snap their focus right on the poor troll hunter who just wanted to spam steady shot and fight the evil alliance paladins.

For reference, none of this is me saying warrior is "OP" or already super busted- their kit isn't particularly potent by itself. What *is* busted though, is consumables, and strength in numbers. Warrior truly shines when they're enabled and given support. They have a niche, and require coordination. Imagine if arcane mages or pre-CC2 ele shams needed to pop a consume before they raw casted a fuckload of burst onto someone, or if paladins needed someone else to cast their bubble for them. These are exaggerations, of course, but the basic concept is the same. Warrior is bad. They need help. But not changes.
