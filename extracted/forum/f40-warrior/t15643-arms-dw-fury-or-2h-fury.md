Now I'm not an expert on Warrior but I imagine that all these different playstyles are viable for levelling, and I'm trying to decide myself between them. And I get that they can be situational, like Arms has more of an advantage for PvP, and Fury is usually the dps spec for raiding. So for what I understand 2 Hander is easier than dual wielding because of the off-hand hit penalty but dual wielding is faster and tends to generate more rage than you would with a slow 2 hander, but a 2 hander tends to do more big hits.

Then there's the differences between Arms and Fury, Fury benefits you with talents when you're a victim of a critical strike, and are faster in hitting, while Arms gives you Mortal Strike, and in the recent patch you can have improved slam.
