In patch 1.17.2 devs have added talent in protection tree "Improved Intervene", which grants 50/100% chance when casting Intervene to gain immunity to movement impairing effects for 3 sec.

So, good news - warriors have something to break roots. Bad news - you need to invest talents to protection tree.
This talent is in 3rd line. It is not very deep, so you can get Mortal Strike and Improved Intervene.
