## Post 28796 by Philandros — 2022-11-09T05:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28796#p28796 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To my knowledge no one has publicly posted a T-WoW pre-raid bis list.

From what I have gathered, the philosophy on T-WoW is not to chase meta lists, but to capture the excitement of WoW when it was new and people were still finding and developing their character sheets and strategies. They want you to explore the game, get excited, and scratch your head when you find a new piece of loot.

To answer your question, you can either use their database or the T-WoW atlas loot addon, and scour the items to your playstyle.

I would also say you are probably pretty safe following a vanilla or classic guide online, but beware, some original WoW items have been changed on here.

Good luck hunting!
