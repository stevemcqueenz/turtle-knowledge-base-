## Post 164933 by Ellifta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-01-11T23:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164933#p164933 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Warrior tank got the short end of the stick with CC2. Deep prot was buffed and was meant to be itemized around shield block value and crit, but none of the warrior tier sets have both of these stats in the same set. I'm not saying within the same item, I'm saying within the same entire tier set.

Fury prot was nerfed, and that spec didn't use traditional warrior tier sets anyway. Warrior tank in general got pushed to worst tank spot from first spot. You should never ever wear full T1, T2 or T3 if you want to hold threat against people with purple gear.

I don't have high hopes, but let's hope the 1.18.1 fixes protection warrior itemization and their outdated sets. For now I suggest sticking with T2 shoulders, which are actually pretty good. T2 legs and boots are also strong. The rest of the items are situational at best, and you should use off-pieces like ES helmet instead of T2 helmet or T2 DPS chest instead of T2 tank chest.

I have a suspicion that deep-protection warrior, the spec that is meant to use these items, is the least played tank by a long shot, so developers don't have any data nor receive any criticism or suggestion about them.
