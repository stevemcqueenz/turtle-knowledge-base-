## Post 101834 by Czasku (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-24T16:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101834#p101834 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The short answer is simple; Big weapon = big whack on puny hummie.

The long answer: When you level up there's lack of critical strike for warriors, you usually run with 5% which isn't much. But at lvl 30 you can take Axe Specialization which is additional 5%, the mace specialization isn't very good tbh because in vanilla it doesnt generate any rage (in tbc they buffed it to grant you 7 rage when it procs making it the decent alternative to axe spec) and the sword spec is 5% flat extra swing chance (it surpasses the axe spec when you have at least 25% base chance to crit because the extra swing can also crit) also when you get lvl 45 you can get Cruelty that adds another 5% giving you flat 15% chance to critically strike making your Impale and Deep Wounds proc more often.

When you're lvl 40 you get Mortal Strike, your only one instant damaging ability on demand (you wont have enough rage to also use Whirlwind, trust me.) so you want it to hit hard. The dps of 2.6 and 3.6 weapon speed may be the same, BUT Mortal Strike has 6 seconds cooldown, so its damage will be bigger with slow weapon than with fast weapon. Blizz stated that they wanted people in vanilla to change they playstyle based on weapon speed, thats why there's so many "next melee" abilities in vanilla.

So, in summary:
Whirlwind Axe is boosting your only one instant damage ability and gives you extra source of rage and damage from Impale, Deep Wounds AND potential strike from Overpower.
Whirlwind Sword boosts your Heroic Strike and gives you potential extra strike (which is obviously extra source of rage) its also probably better for 2h fury.
Whirlwind Mace is something between, but without extra source of rage generation. It also gives you swag because no one is using it.
