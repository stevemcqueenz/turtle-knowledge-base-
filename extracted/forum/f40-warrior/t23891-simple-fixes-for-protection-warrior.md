# Simple Fixes for Protection Warrior

## Post 167684 by Ellifta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-07T16:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167684#p167684 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It's common knowledge by now that warrior tank is severely lacking compared to other tanks in threat department. Arguably also in mitigation department, since taking the defensive tactics talents to do more threat requires leaving defensive stance and foregoing 10%DR that comes with it.

The forum is full of many protection warrior suggestions, but I think they reinvent the wheel by revamping the protection tree or making grandiose changes that don't fit much with the vanilla+ . Therefore, I wanted to offer my thoughts on very simple fixes that developers could literally implement overnight with the experience I have from having played warrior with tanking only for 155 days and 13 hours.

 1 - Add Scaling to Revenge
Currently a lot of warrior abilities like sunder armor and revenge have fixed threat values that don't scale with any of your stats. This means there is no difference in threat done between a fresh 60 and Kara40 geared warrior. The solution is quite simple: Add 20% AP scaling to revenge. A Kara40 geared warrior tank will have between 1500-2100 AP depending on their gear and raid group. This would turn the ability into a decent filler whenever it pops since it would now deal quite a bit of damage against bosses once they get 5x sunder + FF + CoR. In PvP this would change absolutely nothing, since revenge is physical damage and is reduced by armor. Revenge should also be available in battle stance if the talent "Defensive Tactics" is taken.

 2 - Some Number Tweaks
A lot of people don't know that warriors have an inherent 0.8 multiplier to their threat in battle stance and berserker stance. This is why, despite the massive 180% threat multiplier listed on "Defensive Tactics" talent, the actual threat ends up being almost the same. To fix this, the developers can simply increase the threat multiplier granted by "Defiance" and "Defensive Tactics" even higher. As of the time of this post, next patch will increase Defiance from 15% to 20%, but I think 25% is even a better number. Additionally, the 180% threat multiplier from Defensive Tactics should be increased to 250%.

 3 - Minor Talent Tweaks
The deep protection talent "One Handed Specialization" should reduce the chance of being parried/blocked/dodged by 1% for each point, up to 5% reduction on boss avoidance. A lot of people don't know that bosses have a 14% chance to parry attacks from front, then a 5% chance to dodge on top of that. This would minimize the chances of getting screwed over by RNG at the start of boss fights where you get "parry, parry, block". It's also deep enough in protection tree that only deep protection tanks can take it.

Shield block charges gained from shield slam and shield block should stack. You should not have to play piano and watch whether boss managed to hit you or not to be crush immune in defensive stance. Currently, they cancel each other out. It's incredibly stupid and pointless. I know the point was to prevent Fury prot from being crit-crush immune, but if you have the shield slam talent, you are already not fury-prot, so there's no need for these bonuses to not stack.

And that's it. That's all that would take to make warrior a great tank again. Warrior doesn't have to be good at AoE, have the best threat or mitigation amongst tanks, but it should be the most reliable single target tank. The warrior tank already foregoes 10% DR by switching to battle stance if using the talent "Defensive Tactics". It also lacks AoE the paladin tanks have. It just makes sense that they should have the second best single target TPS after a bear.

Protection tank in battle stance should be just under a bear tank in terms of threat and above the rest. Protection tank in defensive stance should be on-par or slightly worse than a paladin in single target threat.

First of all, Turtle WoW isn’t Vanilla+. Yes, it’s the closest thing we have to Vanilla+, but as I remember reading, it is *“a fan-made expansion story for Vanilla World of Warcraft that delves deeper into exploring the original lore of the game”* (literally the first sentence on the website).
So please don’t react to ideas with the mindset of this being Vanilla+. Allow it to be its own ****EXPANSION****, and with that, allow space for changes that are actually bold.

Your improvements aren’t bad, in my opinion, even if they mostly just tweak things a little.

 1. It doesn’t really matter that much, but yes, I think in general all abilities should scale in some way.
 2. Yes, more threat is always nice; that’s pretty much what we’re being promised in the next patch anyway.
 3. Check out the talent in my custom talent tree for Prot Warrior (my little dream), which directly addresses the issue with Shield Slam by changing Improved Shield Slam:
[[https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c ... Z89v8fans4](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator?t2=Z89v8fans4)]([https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c ... Z89v8fans4](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator?t2=Z89v8fans4))

I don’t really understand why you think a deep Prot warrior should be worse than a paladin in single-target threat. I strongly disagree. In my opinion, deep Prot should have roughly the same single-target threat as a druid tank, since both are primarily single-target tanks, while paladins and shamans should excel at group threat.

I also question whether we should always continue giving Fury/Prot more threat. Maybe it’s enough that Fury/Prot does more damage, but has the same or even less single-target threat, or shifts its strength toward group threat and add control instead of pure boss threat. That could be a way to give warriors the tools to act as a kind of Fury/Prot AoE tank, while deep Prot clearly excels as the single-target tank.

Since warriors effectively have two tank specs—unlike other classes—this could be a strong new identity: three distinct roles within one class framework—DPS, AoE tank, and single-target tank.

Just my humble opinion.
