The current answer is probably better rage generation still.

But in Vanilla and before CC2 (may it be damned) the reason why you would use an OH faster than your MH is that Heroic Strike uniquely bypassed the OH bonus inaccuracy while it was queued up. Meaning that having an OH that would consistently get swings between your MH swings got more value out of this Heroic Strike mechanic, which translated to more rage and more damage.

But they got rid of that mechanic because of.... reasons, surely.

I believe the rage generation of a slow 1h is gonna be more per hit, but about the same assuming the a fast wep of the same dps. Technically fast weps have the advantage because of how many hits they get means that the chance to miss will average out closer to your actual hit chance than fluctuate from the low amount of swings of a slow weapon (meaning if you have 10% miss chance on your OH, a slow weapon might end up hitting 96% of the time or sometimes 75% of the time depending on how swings happen simply based on rng. While a fast wepon would probably more often land somewhere between 88-92% hit rate)

But aside from that, the unbridled rage talent also generates 1 point of rage on HIT regardless of damage. Meaning thatyour OH will generate a bonus point of rage more often if your OH is faster.
