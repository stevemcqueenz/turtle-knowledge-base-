## Post 54896 by Armet — 2023-07-27T21:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54896#p54896 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just a few ideas on how to improve the warrior protection tree and make deep prot relevant again. Make revenge deal weapon damage in addition to its normal damage. Remove improved revenge. New tier 6 talent. Onslaught requires 5/5 in one hand weapon specialization makes revenge usable in any stance and gives normal melee attacks a 5%/10%/15% chance to grant the use of revenge. Change improved sunder armor to 2/3/5 rage point cost reduction. Add an execute component to shield slam making it deal an additional 1 point of damage for each rage point consumed. I think these changes wouldn't break the game and just makes deep prot a little more viable while keeping it pretty balanced.
