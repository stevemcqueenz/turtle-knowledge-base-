# 10-19 warrior build 1.17.2

## Post 105917 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-02T11:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105917#p105917 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Not going to pretend for perfect build. Just my thoughts to figure out build for myself.
I will list talents from best to worst with my reasoning.

Bad news. All classes can remove slow with Insignia trinket. It used to remove slow only for warrior/hunter/shaman.
Hamstring has CD. We can't spam it to proc Fiery Weapon/Crusader. Sunder Armor and Rend doesn't seem to proc fiery weapon.
Wing Clip has CD 3 sec. We will probably be kited by hunters. Though we already were kited, because 40% slow of Hamstring vs 50% slow of Wing Clip allows them to get away.

Good news. We no longer need shield to interrupt enemies! Pummel can be learned at lvl 18 and it is available in Arms.
Hamstring has CD. We are no longer stuck in melee when we engage other warrior.
We have plenty of rage. I did few duels and my rage bar was full. On lvl 19 white attack for 100 damage grant ~18 rage, white attack for 60 damage grant 10 rage.

Talents

 1. Tactical Mastery
> You retain 5/10/15/20/25 of your rage points when you change stances.

It is on 1st row, so we don't need to choose between 3 bad talents in Arms.
I surely pick Tactical mastery atleast 4 points to be able to use Disarm (20 rage) when I need it.

 2. Booming Voice
> Increases the area of effect of all shouts by 12/24/36/48/60% and the duration of your Battle and Demoralizing Shout by 12/24/36/48/60%

Our Demoralizing Shout can break rogues stealth. It is very typical situation when I approach to rogue, he stealthes. I use Bloodrage (to prevent Sap) and then Demoralizing Shout to try to break stealth. Sometimes it is successful.
Basic radius of Demo Shout is 10 yards. With 1 point it is 11 yards. So I guess with 5 points it will be 10+60%=16 yards. Now when we don't need to invest a lot to get Tactical Mastery, we can invest in Booming Voice.

 3. Improved Charge
> Increases the amount of rage generated by your Charge ability by 5/10.

Require 5 points in Arms and you can reach them with Tactical Mastery.
Rage generation was changed, so I am not sure if it is good idea.
Default Charge generates 9 range. Most of our abilities require 10 range. But you usually immediately deal melee attack on that enemy after the charge.
So at least 1 point in this talent will be useful to be sure that you get enough rage for Hamstring or Pummel if your miss/dodge/parry/absorb your initial melee attack.

 4. Improved Bloodrage
> Increases the instant Rage generated by your Bloodrage ability by 5/10.

Default Bloodrage grants you 10 rage, which is enough for macroses like "Bloodrage+Pummel" or "Bloodrage+Shield Bash" or "Bloodrage + Hamstring".
This talent is just better than Shield Specialisation to reach Iron Will.

 5. Anticipation
> Increases your Defense skill by 7/14/20.

The only good thing that this talent work regardless of your weapon. 20 of defense skill is -0.8% to be critically hit and 0.8% to block, parry, dodge.
It also better than Shield Specialisation to reach Iron Will.

 6. Iron Will
> Increases your chance to resist Stun And Charm effects by an additional 3/6/9/12/15%.

There are a lot of stuns on 10-19. Hammer of Justice, War Stomp, Grenades, Charge stun, Improved Concussive Shot, Impact (though fire mages are rare find), Blackout (though I rarely see priests in shadow).
So I think it is useful talent. The only downside of it that it requires you to spend all 10 talents to stun resistance, so you don't get Tactical Mastery.
You lose ability to quickly switch to Disarm. Though you can use it after Bloodrage and any melee attack.
You also lose additional 5-6 yards of Demo Shout.

 7. Cruelty
> Increases your chance to get a critical strike with melee weapons by 1/2/3/4/5%

 1% of crit per point. Plain simple useful.

 8. Deflection
> Increases your Parry chance by 1/2/3/4/5%

Parrying the attack allows your next melee attack to land earlier. You need to face enemies to parry. It is hard to successfully play with it in real situations.

 9. Improved Thunder Clap
> Reduces the cost of your Thunder Clap ability by 1/2/4 rage point and increases the damage by 20/40/60%

It costs 20 rage and deals 17 damage untalented. Might be a good button to debuff enemy melees. Reduced cost allows to press it earlier.

 10. Dual wield Specialization
> Increases the damage done by your offhand weapon by 5/10/15/20/25% and your chance to hit with your offhand weapon by 2/4/6/8/10%.

The only reason I will equip offhand is to get immune disarm effect from Steel weapon chain while keeping Fiery Weapon enchant on main hand weapon. This situation occurs only when I charge to another warrior. I don't think I need damage nor hit chance for offhand in this situation.
I don't think it is useful for pvp.

 11. Unbridled wrath
> Gives you a 15/30/45/60/75% chance to generate an additional Rage point when you deal melee damage with a weapon. The effect is doubled for two-handed weapons.

You will have plenty of rage in PvP without this talent. I don't think it is useful.

 12. Shield Specialization.
> Increases your chance to block attacks with a shield by 1/2/3/4/5% and generates 1/2/3/4/5 rage when a block occurs.

I don't think it is useful for PvP at all.

 13. Improved Heroic Strike
> Reduces the cost of your Heroic Strike ability by 1 rage point.

It is "Next melee" attack, which increases damage of next strike by 32. Might be useful to dump rage, but I don't think it is reasonable to spend talents here.

 14. Improved Rend
> Increases the bleed damage done by your Rend ability by 10/20%.

Rend costs 10 rage, deals "28 damage over 12 sec". It is 4 ticks on rank 2.
Rend also scales with attack power. On lvl 19 I have 50 strength and 137 attack power without gear. Untalented Rend deals 13 damage per tick (52 damage total). When I equip my (not perfect) gear I have 96 strength or 229 attack power. Rend ticks for 18 damage (72 damage total).
+10% of damage will be equal to 18+10%=19.8=19 or 18+20%=21,6=21 damage.
I usually use Rend on rogues after Hamstring to get away from them and force them to stay out of stealth.
 1 or 3 additional points of damage does not look like good reason to spec into it. At least for PvP, at least on 19 lvl.
I will probably use it more than before, because I have spare GCD, but not always because it break sheep.

 15. Toughness
> Increases your armor value from items by 2% and the amount of damage absorbed by your shield by 3%

In my (not perfect) gear with shield I have 1452 armor. It is 41.9% to reduce incoming physical damage.
Additional 150 armor will reduce incoming physical damage for 2-3%. I don't think I notice the difference on 10-19. It will be like 1 or 2 damage points.
Other downside is that if you get Toughness, you don't get Tactical Mastery, Booming Voice and Iron Will.

Conclusion
I end up with two builds:
 1. Tactical Mastery+Booming Voice [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warrior? ... AAAAAAA%3D](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warrior?points=FAIAAAAAAAAAAAEAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA%3D)
It has ability to switch from Battle Stance and immediately use Disarm.
It allows to Hamstring even if your melee attack miss.
It has improved area to break rogues stealth.
 2. Iron Will [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warrior? ... AAAAAAA%3D](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/warrior?points=AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAQYFAAAAAAAAAAAA%3D)
 15% to resist Hammer of Justice, War Stomp, Grenades and Improved Concussive Shot proc.
