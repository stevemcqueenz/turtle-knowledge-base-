## Post 152740 by Snoof — 2025-09-23T01:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152740#p152740 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

"Useless" is being dramatic imo. Warrior is very good at a couple of things in PvP — pressuring healers, killing squishy melees and surprising mispositioned casters with high burst damage. The big problem with the class is just that it''s really, REALLY overreliant on things that aren't in its kit to function in PvP, be that consumables or the help of a support class.

Warriors are the only class that doesn't have any spammable general-purpose defensive abilities to use in PvP; that's by far the biggest issue if you ask me. The closest we get are retaliation/shield wall/recklessness and intimidating shout. The first option **is** super powerful in the right situation, but the cooldown is so long you only get to cast it once every other BG. Intimidating shout is also a pretty long cooldown, and as a fear it's some of the most unreliable CC in the game the way things are right now — especially if you're alliance going up against Will of the Forsaken and tremor totems. We struggle too much to pin targets down while being extremely easy to kite, and we die too quickly trying.

The other two big problems I see are shields and LiPs. Not being able to generate rage on shields is absolutely crippling, and it's complete nonsense to boot. I'm convinced it was never a deliberate balancing decision to begin with. imagine how stupid it would be if rogues stopped generating energy while hitting a shielded target, or ret paladins had to play the start of every fight against a shielded enemy without a mana bar; that's playing warrior when priests are around. I really can't overstate how much it sucks to deal with. LiPs meanwhile are just bullshit in general; whether you like the vanilla WoW brand of consumable spam pay-to-play PvP or not, I don't see how anybody could make a reasonable case for a potion that gives everybody a paladin bubble against phys damage classes being okay. There should either be an equivalent potion against magic damage, or they simply shouldn't exist in PvP.

My dream changes for warrior right off the bat would be:

 - Charge available in combat.
it's extremely easy for caster classes to cast some nothing spell like pain or counterspell at max range to get you in combat and deny you the ability to enter the fight with any kind of momentum in a way that feels much more like abusing a technicality than intelligent counterplay. And yes, I know that intercept exists; it costs rage that you don't have at the start of a fight and deprives you of all mobility for the rest of the encounter if used as an opener. You need both to have any hope of a mage even noticing you're there.

 - Slow immunity for the duration of Berserker Rage; Improved Berserker Rage moved to a more accessible place in the fury tree.
Warrior badly needs some kind of counterplay to CC built into its kit — Improved Berserker Rage is a good idea, but right now it's so deep in the PvE spec that the arms warriors that actually need it can't access it at all.

 - Homogenize Master Strike.
Master Strike is infinitely more useful than Counter Attack was, but it's still an extremely inconsistent ability. I'm sorry to say it, but trying to make an ability do that many different things is just not a good idea; the game's not deep enough to support it. What you've ended up with is the same exact thing as with Master of Arms: axes being the 'correct' option. Swords? Warriors already had disarm. Maces? Disorient's just worse CC for a class that auto-applies DoTs when it hits you. Polearms? Excessively niche, only useful against targets that **aren't** trying to fight you. Daggers, fists and staves? What even is the point? We don't get to use these weapons, and none of the effects are good enough that anybody's gonna bother carrying around extra weapons to use them. If they **were** that good they'd be OP. Trying to swap weapons situationally with primitive classic era macros completely sucks, and practically nobody bothers. Keep the root and scrap the rest.

 -Defensive Cooldown Rework.
Nerf the effects, dramatically shorten their cooldowns. Make shield wall usable without a shield (name change?)

 - Rage vs shields.

I really think the whole idea of warriors being this amazing duo class comes from the fact that if you get a 2v2 between two DPS & Healer duos the duo with a warrior in it tends to win because of mortal strike.
