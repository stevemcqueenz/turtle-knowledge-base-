# What is our identity in PvP

## Post 141635 by Zamera — 2025-07-26T23:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141635#p141635 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Warriors at the moment are just glorified target dummies in PvP. We provide no useful utility, crowd control, and our damage is mediocre at best. We require multiple other specific classes/specs to perform at a baseline level. The upcoming patch addresses nothing, specifically for Arms PvP. I always saw Arms warrior as a tactical juggernaut, but none of this fantasy is felt in the game.

The upcoming patch provides a 10% buff to Mortal Strike and Slam, this is nerfed in instanced pvp combat and is really a win more buff. We will not really feel this. This is really a compensatory buff for PvE due to the execute changes.

Master Strike is very mediocre. While it is interesting to swap weapons in and out depending on the situation, not all weapons are created equal and acquiring multiple high end weapons seems like a tall tale to ask all warriors to do. Combine this with the rage cost and swapping weapons imposes a GCD penalty and resets your swing timer.

Some suggestions:

Unlink our big 3 cooldowns, Retaliation, Recklessness, and Shield Wall. It feels ancient that these are all the same cooldown still. Impose a 2 min cooldown when you activate one of them. Preferably I would like the big 3 cooldowns to be about 15 mins. Remove the Improved Disciplines talent from the game. It is border line useless with all the restrictions/other talents we can take.

Remove the shared cooldown on intervene and intercept.

Create a new spell, War Banner. Let this provide some sort of buff utility. Potentially we could see 3 of these for each spec/stance or we could limit it to one version. Let the banners be placeable and warriors can intervene them like an ally.

Arms Banner: Group movement speed increase, movement speed cannot go below x%, maybe reduces incoming crowd control (not CC that has already been applied)

Fury Banner: Some sort of group crit buff/crit dmg modifier increase?

Prot Banner: Mimics your health/armor, increases threat generation, pulses threat, if you cast taunt or maybe if you taunt it, it aoe taunts mobs onto it for 2s. Gives you the threat when it expires/dies.

Spell reflect or some sort of spell block.

Second Wind- health/rage when crowd controlled/immobilized

Victory Rush- medium damage- heals for 20% of max hp, resets on kill/hk (and assist) 20 sec cd

Holmgang- preventing most attacks from reducing your HP to less than 1. Duration: 10s chains a target to the warrior (stolen from ff14)

Nascent Flash- grants a high amount of vampirism for 10s (maybe like 50%) (stolen/similar to ff14)

Please note I am not asking for all this, these are suggestions in a way the game can go for Warriors (arms mainly).

Please comment on what you would like see/improve for Warriors or the direction you would like to see them go for us.
