I'm new to turtle wow and I'm really enjoing the experience, I just hit level 44 with my warrior and I'm having a lot of fun with the new zones, dungeons etc... So first of all, good job Turtle Team! You guys are nailing it!

So I love PvP. Personally I think it's wow's true late-game, and where it really shines. But I'm noticing that warriors have been nerfed a lot in Turtle WoW. And that's not a bad thing, they where too OP on classic wow. The thing is warriors have been nerfed out of existence?

I pick the meele class because I want to beat other players at meele, but then a shaman comes along and I die in 2 seconds??? I can deal with rogues alright. Some people are complaining about them but they are manageable. In comparison I've taken bursts of 2000 damage in less than 2 seconds from a Shaman. **And that's all my health**. Yes I've died in 2 seconds.

They walk up to you and just delete you.

I mean, it's gotten to a point where the battleground team with more shamans win? At least that's what I'm feeling whenever I enter a battleground. But of course shamans being broken is another topic, and I'm sure the Turtle Team knows this.

The thing is, warriors should be the strongest meele class. Shamans are casters, they shouldn't delete you up close, they should delete you at a distance. But if you get up and personal, that's warrior territory! You shouldn't get up and personal with a warrior!

I think the problem with shamans sums down to meele magical damage. My armor does nothing against magical damage. So when a shaman bursts me at meele, I just die.

This is how I feel whenever a shaman gets near me:

![Image](https://media.tenor.com/RBrUuN4z0ewAAAAe/guess-ill-die.png)

I think shamans need a nerf, make some of that magical damage into physical, or give the warrior a method to handle taking bursts of damage in pvp. That would be even cooler, and tie into the 'Master at Arms' talents about switching weapons! Maybe a shield talent for arms that allows you to block some incoming spells with shield block? That way the warrior still has to have the skill to switch to the shield, and go defensive mode until the shaman's mana gives up. And the shaman can keep his absurd deleting damage.

I have been having a blast with Master Strike. Like goddamn what a cool ability! I use it to root, dismount and disarm and I keep changing my weapon acording to what I need. Amazing idea! Just amazing!

PS: I noticed whirlwind is a level 36 ability, but improved whirlwind is a lvl 31 talent?
