## Post 95013 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-05-16T22:04:00Z

Some people do, seen some, but only for fun. Sweeping strikes can be nice but I think it comes from the DPS mindset.
Specially the warriors. If you roll a warrior and you decide not to be a tank, chances are you wanna grind up to lvl 60, farm ages, buy 10000g of consumables and all this to see your toon at the top of the DPS charts. Fury takes you there, arms will not.
If arms had a unique raid utility, maybe some people would consider it but as things stand you have the option to be a better DPS warrior or a worse one for a class which overfocuses on big numbers.
