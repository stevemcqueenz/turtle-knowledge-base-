## Post 122628 by Grizb37 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-15T13:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122628#p122628 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In 1.12 client macros can only do 1 action with 1 click. So you have to click the macro twice.
