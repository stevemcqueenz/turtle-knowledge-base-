I use outfitter, works very nice. You can either swap to defstance and then it automatically uses shield and 1h.

I use mousewheel up for bersistance. Mousewheel down for defstance. Click mousewheel for combatstance.

Shift + mousewheel up for dps gear for bosses. Shift + mousewheel down for defensive gear.
Shift + click mousewheel for dps trashmob gear.

For pvp I change all of that accordingly.

Especially in pvp i often just wanna equip a shield and 1h and not swap to defstance bc loosing rage would actually pretty bad.
