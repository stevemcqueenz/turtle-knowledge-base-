This is a build I thought up on a whim for the heck of it. I am in no way a warrior expert and my highest level one on the server is 27, so this will likely have flaws, but I'm into the idea. I'll go through the basic concept.

My main goal with this is to play as a PvE dps spec. As for how it works, the basic idea of your rotation will be as follows:

Build rage with dual wielding, in berserker stance, and swap to a 2h weapon to dump all your rage with heroic/mortal strikes. Repeat this process throughout the whole fight. Your rage dump stage will obviously change to whirlwind/cleave if the situation calls for AoE
I'm sure there's many ways this can be expanded upon, but anyways, here's my talent setup.
