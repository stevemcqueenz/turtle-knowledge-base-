# Warriors in PVP (Updated for CC2)

## Post 103527 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-10-18T02:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103527#p103527 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

View my first thread for context: [viewtopic.php?t=10856](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10856)

My primary personal recommendation to elevate Warrior was to give them Spell Reflect + Shattering Throw.
This would have brought Warrior from an 'F' grade up to a 'D-'.
That would technically be a passing grade.
Note: Warrior is already extremely popular and dominates in PVE. It's fair for it to be mediocre in PVP.

Anyway, obviously those abilities were not implemented.
So what important skills (in regards to PVP) **did** Warriors receive?

Arms:
 1. 2 yards extra melee range. (Polearms exclusive)
This is excellent -- allows a "Master of Arms" to actually clown on other melee.
Previously they just went toe-to-toe against other melee and still lost 50% of the time. It was a farce.
Now you Hamstring then keep at 7 yards distance to literally dunk on them.

 2. Having Pummel in Battle Stance is much needed.
Previously had to use Shield Bash which was not realistic for obvious reasons.
Not sure how the 'daze' mechanic (if Talented) is going to be useful but whatever -- an interrupt is a blessing!!

Note: Counterattack is awful. Does poor damage and the effect is useless.
You will only use this attack against other melee so the 'root' is somewhat irrelevant.
I guess with Polearms you can troll them at 7 yards distance as I explained. Kek.

Fury:
Blood Craze has been buffed to WoTLK levels of power-creep.
This may be the number 1 most impactful change as this allows the solo Warrior actually noteworthy amounts of self-sustaining healing.
Staying alive longer is absolutely essential for Warriors success.
Longer life = more rage = more kills = more wins

Protection:
 1. Generally they have had their god awful damage somewhat increased.
 2. And they can (reliably) dispel a Magic Effect.

TL:DR:
Warriors got a very conservative/modest power boost so the status quo will remain largely the same.
They are going from F grade to F+

Still Trash Tier i'm afraid.   maintenance_turtle
Sorry Warrior mains!

Additional Notes:
At least Paladin (which 'Hard Countered' Warrior) was hugely nerfed. So you will no longer auto-lose these fights.
Hamstring was OP due to its extremely long 15 sec debuff duration. It's fine that it has a 6 second CD now.
Orc 'Anti Stun' Racial nerf means Charge/Intercept are slightly less awful. They might actually serve their function!!
