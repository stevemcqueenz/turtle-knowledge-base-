I'd say stick with troll or tauren personally, but tauren might be your best option defensively.

Warstomp gives some breathing room and comes out fast enough that having multiple mobs isn't much of an issue, and endurance is just a flat out great bonus for any class/race and especially warrior as well as tanks. Nature resist is near negligeable but helps with WC and BFD even if you won't be able to notice it most likely.

Troll has Berserking, which gets extra mileage out of you being the centre of attention, and the health regen racial is... Something that exists. Beastslaying and throwing/bow expertise is also niche as heck, but still fine.

You're kinda just getting shadow resist and WOTF from undead, which is fine. Cannibalize exists I guess.

Only good orc tanking racial is weapon skill, and that's only for axes, also weapon skill isn't as important since everything should be around/below your level. Well- actually, I guess there's hardiness, but like other resist stuff it's kinda niche for PvE.
