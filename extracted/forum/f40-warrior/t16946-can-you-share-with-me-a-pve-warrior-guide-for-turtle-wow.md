## Post 130489 by Berettadin — 2025-05-10T19:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130489#p130489 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My basic advice for leveling is choose Fury. The most important talents are about longevity and reducing downtime, and that means Blood Craze. You're getting critted every fight; 6% back makes a real difference.

Basics are Cruelty 5/5, Dual Wield Specialization 5/5, Blood Craze 3/3, Improved Shouts 5/5, (Demoralizing Shout is a staple and you're reducing damage taken), and Enrage 5/5. After that comes things better for your role as dps on in a team like Reckless Execute and Death Wish (or PvP with Battlefield Mobility), but for PvE leveling Flurry is excellent.

On the Arms side Improved Heroic Strike helps since HS is your primary rage spender. Improved Rend is a talent tax for later abilities -Rend itself is okay because it's cheap and can be used all the time but it's obnoxiously weak and not worth buffing. Tactical Mastery is only worth 1 thing: having enough Rage for Disarm. Improved Charge is ok, but it won't always work and DW's Rage gains even out not having it. Improved Overpower is actually quite nice since you'll generate a lot of enemy Dodges but it's not cheap.

Opening is Rend, Demoralizing Shout, Sunder Armor, and HS when you can afford it. You'll get a feel for what to do when. There's not a lot of complexity 'til endgame and besides forgetting to Hamstring things that run off and come back with friends there's not a lot to go wrong.

Professions: First Aid and Cooking are absolutely essential. To reiterate for emphasis downtime and survival are your weaknesses and you are the weakest class in game for buffs. Take the time to level Cooking.

Beyond that I like Alchemy and Herbalism since being able to heal and buff yourself is crucial. Late game this will mean things like Flasks and making money selling Mongoose Potions. Alternatively do whatever; anything except Enchanting is worth your time.

Finally Warrior is an expensive class. Heavier armor costs more to repair. You have more weapon skills to train then anyone. You are also more gear dependent so you will grind for upgrades more and spend more money. Late game this will be actually good as when you are finally fully upgraded you'll be the best or near-best tank or dps there is. It's just a loooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooong
journey. If by chance you make friends with a Priest to level with you'll both fly upwards with how much you compliment each other.

The rest is persistence. Good luck.
