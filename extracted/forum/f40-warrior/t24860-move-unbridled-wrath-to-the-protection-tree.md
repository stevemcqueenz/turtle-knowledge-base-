# Move "Unbridled Wrath" to the Protection tree

## Post 175303 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2026-04-15T10:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175303#p175303 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hey there, I've been thinking how to improve the protection leveling experience, without introducing more damage and without affecting late game too much, and the following idea came up.

Replace ****Iron Will**** in the Protection tree with ****Unbridled Wrath**** and apply the following changes:

 1. Update the name so it fits the protection tree better. "Focused Rage" maybe?
 2. Update the effect to: "Gives you a 15/30/45/60/75% chance to generate an additional Rage point when you deal melee damage with a weapon. The effect is doubled for two-handed weapons****or while using a shield**.**"
---
 **Pros:**
1. Warriors using a shield will have better rage generation during the early stages of the game.
2. Rage through Unbridled Wrath is counted as power gain, so it causes a small amount of threat (minor but flavorful addition to the tank oriented spec).
 **Cons:**
1. "Unbridled Wrath" is currently the talent choice for 2h Fury warriors. This means they'll either have to take Booming Voice or the substitute talent must accommodate that playstyle.
 ****Thoughts and neutral points:****
1. Moving "Unbridled Wrath" to the protection tree won't affect Fury-Prot.
2. For leveling, duel-wielding warriors skip on "Unbridled Wrath", because  "Duel Wield Specialization" provides significantly more rage due to more successful and harder hitting offhand hits. And the talents from row 3 and onwards are too good to skip out on.
3. Currently, during the early-mid game, Shield Specialization does not generate enough rage to make it on par with Duel Wielding or 2H Warriors.
4. Tanking in classic and especially in Turtle WoW as a warrior is extremely easy. You don't need to invest a single point into the prot tree until late game, and a lot of encounters can be handled without even using a shield. So why bother improving Prot then? Well, a lot of players like custom challenges, roleplay or just enjoy the shield focused playstyle. So the aim is to improve the leveling process for them
 **Concerns and open topics:**
1. What do we do with "Iron Will"? Should it be placed on Row 2 of Fury, in place of "Unbridled Wrath", or should it be merged with another Prot talent?
2. If "Iron Will" gets merged with another prot talent or outright removed, then what should the "Unbridled Wrath" substitute in the Fury tree be? Imo it should still be something that favors 2h warriors.
