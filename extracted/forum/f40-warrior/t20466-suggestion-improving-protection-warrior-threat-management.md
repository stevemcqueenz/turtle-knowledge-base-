# Suggestion: Improving Protection Warrior Threat Management

## Post 141341 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-25T14:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141341#p141341 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Protection Warriors still struggle to keep up in threat generation, even after recent changes. While they lack strong AoE threat tools — and that's fine — they should at least excel in single-target threat, which is currently not the case.

---
 **![🛡️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6e1.svg) Key Suggestions:**

 **1. End-Tier Talent: Improved Defensive Stance**
Introduce a final-tier Protection talent that increases threat generation by **at least 30%** while in Defensive Stance.
This would give Prot Warriors a solid identity as single-target tanks and help them fulfill their intended role more reliably.

 **2. Make Defense Skill Contribute to Threat**
Tie **Defense rating** or skill directly to threat generation.
This would add depth to gearing choices and reward tanks who invest in defensive stats — both thematically and mechanically.

 **3. New Ability: Opportunity Attacks**
Inspired by Pathfinder and D&D 3.5e, this would be a reactive ability exclusive to Prot Warriors:

- Triggers an **automatic melee attack** when the warrior *loses threat* on a target
Deals *low damage*, but generates **high threat**
- **Immobilizes** the target for **1 second**
This would give Prot Warriors a unique, skill-based mechanic to recover threat in clutch moments, reinforcing their role as disciplined, control-oriented tanks.

---
These ideas aim to **enhance single-target threat generation** without making Prot Warriors overpowered in AoE or altering their core identity.
The class fantasy is strong — it just needs tools to stay competitive in modern encounters.

Protection warriors need single target threat. PERIOD. They do dumpster DPS, so at least allow them to maintain threat on a single target. This is the #1 issue with protection warrior. Ways to address this:

 1) Add a flat threat modifier to Improved Taunts (i.e. +500 flat threat and 1 second reduced cooldown) for each point

 2) Increase scaling for Shield Slam (i.e. increase AP scaling from 0.15 to 0.3)

 3) Add scaling for Revenge (i.e. add AP scaling of 0.15 for revenge)

 4) Add scaling for Concussion Blow (i.e. add AP scaling of 0.15 for revenge)

 5) Increase flat threat modifier for Concussion Blow (i.e. double the current flat modifier)

 6) Add a new talent 'Devastate' which adds AP scaling to Sunder Armor

Any or ALL of the above would be massive fixes to aid single threat target generation for protection warrior. None of these would make protection warriors better damage dealers than furyprot tanks or defensive tactics tanks, but they would allow protection tanks to be a non meme spec and do what protection warriors want to do (i.e. mitigate damage and mantain agro on a single target)
