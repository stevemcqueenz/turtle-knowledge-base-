# Warrior Spell Changes & Talent Tree Changes

## Post 152711 by Kyrsie — 2025-09-22T20:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152711#p152711 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

"Every class has its bonus. If it's a Warrior complaining about a Mage, well guess what the Warrior is in the raid and the Mage isn't."

I decided to make a forum post to show some changes and talent trees I made for Warrior, because I want to see every Warrior spec do better in PvP in a way similar to WotLK Warrior. My reason is because the Classic+ goal of every class/spec dealing enough damage or being able to tank in the highest level raid content in Turtle WoW has been achieved. I think Warrior should now receive changes aimed towards being less miserable in PvP without an extreme amount of consumable usage and a pocket healer.

[https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c ... Vb&class=1](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator?t0=x1SNUNtNkf&t1=ETXHFzUP3T&t2=s1HwuVS2Vb&class=1)
Credit to Willwonka for several ideas from his talent tree

- **Shield Wall** will persist after unequipping a shield.

- **Recklessness**, **Shield Wall**, and **Retaliation** no longer share a cooldown with each other. Activating one of them will now put the other two spells on a two minute cooldown.

- **Hamstring** no longer has a cooldown, and can no longer grant **Flurry** or proc weapon effects.

- **Improved Overpower** now grants 25/50% critical strike damage bonus on Overpower instead of 25/50% critical strike chance.
New Spell: **Spell Reflection** (Rank 1)
Learned at Level 54
Raise your shield, reflecting the next spell cast on you.  Lasts 5 seconds.
Requires Battle Stance, Defensive Stance
Instant, 25 Rage, 10 second cooldown
