# Warrior Envy is Nonsensical [Rant]

## Post 112511 by Captain4k (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-13T06:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112511#p112511 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

How do people hate on warriors out of all 9 classes? It's the equivalent of being born in a privileged class and hating on the successful guy that came from nothing and earned everything he has, despite not having the same advantages in life. And it's clearly people with a very specific type of entitled ego that envy warriors because anyone who's levelled a warrior knows that it's one of, if not the most difficult class to not only reach level cap with, but to play exceptionally well due to how heavily gear dependant they are.

We all know that TWoW is strict on the preservation of Blizzard's blueprint of class identity. What is the warriors class identity? Simple answer: A high risk, high reward raging berserker. Tanking aside, high damage is LITERALLY the value that warriors are expected to provide to groups and raids. Because if a warrior is not providing high damage benefits to your party, what else are they useful for? So if damage is the main expectation of the class, OBVIOUSLY it's going to exceed exceptionally well in that role, *as intended by the designers of the class.*

Okay, since warriors are "so overpowered" and this is clearly the oppression olympics, lets compare and contrast warrior benefits to every other class in the game:

Can a warrior have the highest damage performance- Yes
Can a warrior heal themselves with spells?  - No
Can a warrior heal others for a short time after death? - No
Can a warrior resurrect a fallen teammate? - No
Can a warrior resurrect themselves? - No
Can a warrior summon other teammates to the location? - No
Can a warrior make portals to teleport themselves or teammates? - No
Can a warrior shape-shift and fulfill every role in the game? - No
Can a warrior stealth at will or easily escape combat - No
Can a warrior AoE farm by making it rain icicle spears or fireballs from the sky? - No
Can a warrior banish or polymorph targets? - No
Can a warrior set traps for targets? - No
Can a warrior grant levitate or slow fall to themselves or others? - No
Can a warrior walk on water or grant underwater breathing? - No
Can a warrior turn into a cheetah or wolf for increased movement speed? - No
Can a warrior grant Aspect of the Pack or Cheetah for increased movement speed?  - No
Can a warrior tame pets or summon demons to aide them in combat? - No
Can a warrior grant a variety of buffs to party or raid members? - No

So then what significant value does a warrior provide to a party, you ask?  The answer is damage. High freaking damage. And yet people hate on the warrior class for shining in the one single area that it excels at, despite it lacking in virtually every other aspect of class benefit comparatively. With that being considered, if every class were to be equal to warriors in terms of damage, then wtf would be the incentive to play a warrior over any other class?

P.S. Don't even try the "Warriors are the best tanks" argument. Warriors **WERE** the "best" tanks because vanilla WoW was released half-baked due to the time constraints of its development cycle. It wasn't intended for them to be the only viable tank class. TWoW has since rectified that issue with the Class Changes 2 patch. So all tanks are now viable and each uniquely excel in areas that are specific to their class.
