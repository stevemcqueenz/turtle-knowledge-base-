## Post 114307 by Turbosaxophonic (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-25T17:34:00Z

With that reasoning Prot should also be getting Fury’s Shout bonuses and recovery from being critically hit since that would benefit tanks as well. Warrior’s a tank/DPS hybrid and their talents should show that.

Each tree gets defensive options so Prot gets to keep building a tank in other trees after 40, but they’re also there to make early game tanking smoother if you aren’t specced Prot. Warrior as a whole is supposed to do well at defense even if they don’t specialize to tank, it’s important for each tree to have their own flavor of damage mitigation. In the case of Arms with its balance of offense and defense, they get Parry for their mastery of weapon technique since you can only block with a shield, this contrasts with Fury’s “bring it on” attitude to taking damage. Warriors are supposed to be able to stand toe-to-toe with their enemies in each spec.

I really hope this isn’t something that happens, CC2 moving Slam and its talent to Arms because “it’s the 2h spec” diluted the intended mechanical crossover with these trees and I don’t want to see more steps in that direction. I think it’s better when the talents don’t push you to build in one direction so much.
