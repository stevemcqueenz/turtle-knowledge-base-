## Post 152842 by Eyeburn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-23T17:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152842#p152842 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Defensive state is a normally invisible buff that you get while a dodge/parry/block proc'd skill is available for use.  Warrior's get this buff to allow the use of Revenge.  Go through your addons for buffs and uncheck anything to do with "hidden buffs" or "invisible buffs".
