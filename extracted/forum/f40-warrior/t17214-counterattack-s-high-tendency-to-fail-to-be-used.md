Unlike Overpower, which can be smoothly pushed down and casted as it is described, using Counterattack, even though it is properly pushed and timed, most probably triggers "the skill is unavlailable" or something like that in the middle of the screen. That is annoying.

Last night, I tanked in a Windsor rescue run and confirmed the feeling in my experience of using this skill. It may generate some threat when it is successfully casted, but its low damage and efficiency could make it fail to meet players' expectation.

For being a tank, I am considering a respec with Counterattack replaced by some more effective choices.
