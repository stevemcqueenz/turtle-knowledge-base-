# Thoughts on Proc Warrior Rework – Identity, Skill Ceiling, and Build Diversity

## Post 166545 by Sylbrosa — 2026-01-26T14:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166545#p166545 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

With the upcoming proc warrior talent rework, I wanted to start a thread to share some thoughts and hear the community’s opinions about the current gameplay and the warrior’s identity.

I’ll post my own views here, and I’d really like to see what other people think as well.

First of all, the warrior has always been a high-skill class, and a big part of that identity is stance dancing.
Switching between stances to get the most out of your toolkit is what makes warrior gameplay unique and rewarding.

Because of that, I think Turtle WoW made a great decision by putting Tactical Mastery in the first row of Arms and by adding Defensive Tactics, giving proc warriors more reasons to stance dance.

That said, this also makes warriors harder to play. Right now, warriors need to do a lot more work to get similar results to what other classes (like druids in Bear Form) get just by existing in their form.

For this rework to be successful, I’d really like to see three things:

- “Easy Mode” Talent Build
A way to build proc warriors where you don’t need to stance dance to tank effectively.
This would make the spec more accessible, help bring more warriors into tanking, and keep it balanced with other tank classes.

- “Hard Mode” Stance Dance Build
A build that still rewards active stance dancing, similar to what we have now.
It’s very fun once you get the hang of it, and having a high skill ceiling is a big part of the warrior’s identity.

- A Viable PvP Tank Build
Warrior tanking in PvP is already very fun thanks to its survivability and Shield Slam’s dispel effect, and I’d really like to see this playstyle become more viable and better supported by the new talents.

I also hope more attention is given to the first three rows of the proc warrior talent tree, so PvP players have meaningful choices instead of feeling forced into the same path every time. Piercing Howl is great and pretty much mandatory for PvP, but because of that, everyone ends up taking it. At the same time, talents like Last Stand just don’t feel very impactful in PvP, so they’re rarely a real alternative. It would be nice to have stronger defensive or utility options in those early tiers, so choosing talents feels like a real decision instead of an obligation.

Overall, I hope the rework keeps both accessibility and depth, so new players can enjoy proc warrior while experienced players still have room to master it.

Looking forward to hearing your thoughts.

I agree and disagree on a few things. To start, I think Defensive Tactics as a deep prot talent was a mistake and arguably bad game design. The idea that a prot warrior needs to leave their tanking stance in order to do their job effectively sounds antithetical and illogical. Unless it's to execute spam a weakened target, it should be a high risk decision to leave defensive stance when tanking. Defensive Tactics would make more sense if they removed the shield requirement and placed it in the fury tree to assist the fury tanking playstyle. But for a deep prot warrior, it doesn't make much sense and doesn't fit the sword and shield tanking style.

But I heavily agree on making prot pvp more viable. Prot has so much pvp potential that could be realized with a little bit of talent support updates in the protection tree. For example, I've always been of the opinion that talents like Improved Disarm need to be in the first or second row of the tree for easier access.
