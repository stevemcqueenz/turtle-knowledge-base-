Hello,
May I suggest :
Currently anticipation talent is at the start of the prot talent tree. It would be good if one handed weapon spec which is further down the prot tree, be swapped with anticipation.

Most of the guides on the net recommend one handed spec for better melee dmg and thus threat generation, and anticipation as 5 def points  increase isnt really good and nobody on the net suggests taking it. It would balance the talent point taking gradually deeper in the tree and would be available later in the tree for those that really think adittional 5 points mean something to them.

The result would also be that less points would needed to be invested in the prot tree thus saving them for other talents.
