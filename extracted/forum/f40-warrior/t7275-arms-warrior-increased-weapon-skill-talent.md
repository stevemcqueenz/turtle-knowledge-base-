# Arms Warrior - Increased Weapon Skill Talent

## Post 46100 by Ravenstone (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-16T21:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46100#p46100 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm aware that there have been several +weapon skill increasing items made available to us, including some specifically for 2h weapons, which are great and a step in the right direction to giving more balance to weapon skills. Loving them. However, I feel like some specs should get additional help in the talent trees. Arms Warrior being one of them.

I'd like to propose weapon skill be provided to all 2H Weapons via the Two-Handed Weapon Specialization talent. I'd suggest 2 per talent to a max of 10 since I feel it wouldn't make too much of a difference build wise.

Weapon Skill doesn't affect BG PvP [at all](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_skill) (see Player attacking a Player section) so this doesn't buff the standard PvP Arms Warrior, and since it'd be limited to 2H weapons won't affect DW Fury DPS either. 2H Fury is the only real beneficiary of this proposal, which could use a little help.

Thematically, I've always thought it was incredibly odd that Warrior Arms spec didn't include any way to increase Weapon Skill much like Rogue's Weapon Expertise talent. I always imagined Arms as a master of their weapons, having trained extensively and as such having those specific Weapon Specialisations. I also thought this change could potentially be extended to Enh Shamans and Ret Paladins, as they both have real PvE damage concerns, but I feel there is more of a case for Warriors to get this.

Anyway, let me know what you think, or if I've missed a glaringly obvious problem here.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/O8KXBgz.png)

This is the impact of Weapon Skill for melee strikes.

Weapon skill is specifically advantageous and desirable for dual wield melee. While +8 weapon skill on items such as Obsidian Edged Blade are a boon, 5 talent points for 10 skill would not likely be super attractive (against the value in Fury), nor improve the PvE preference for Arms Warriors.

 10 skill will save the 2H warrior the need for 3% gear hit, and a modest portion of their damage (white glancing blows) will improve by 30%.

If you want to improve the Arms warrior's lot in raid, it may be best to allow their utility (reducing healing by 50%, stronger AOE potential) to shine in the raid and dungeon environment. Either by making more healing mechanics (even making those existing mechanics affected by Mortal Strike when they are not), Or improving the uptime of Sweeping Strikes and adding some value to Battle Stance Offtanking (probably by Buffing Mocking Blow uptime/utility).

Warriors, as you have probably heard, have few issues in vanilla. They do have some, and they are worthy to improve upon:
- Odd order of talents in the Prot Tree
- Unimaginative utility in Arms
- Lacking a "Bleed Build" featuring Rend
Some people would also rather Arms simply be a PVP spec.
