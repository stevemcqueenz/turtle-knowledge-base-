## Post 73184 by Tomberry — 2023-10-23T21:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73184#p73184 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You don't need the 9% immediately.
This is the end-value for lvl 63 (Raidbosses) and ensures that your skills don't miss. Whitehits will still miss, but that's unavoidable.

For lvl 60 dungeons ~5% are fine. No need to min-max here.

If you respecc to fury while leveling, don't fight mobs that have a higher level than you. You will miss a lot, what is a bit frustrating. But as a warrior you shouldn't do that anyway.

But the most important thing is fun. Play what you like more.
