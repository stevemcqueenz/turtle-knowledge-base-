## Post 122609 by Wolf of Rage (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-03-15T06:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122609#p122609 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You should be in Berserker Stance all the time to benefit from the extra Crit in spite of the additional Damage intake, as Battle Stance offers nothing and Defensive Stance instead lowers your output.

Overpower is also only available if ever your opponent performs a Dodge on any of your attacks - - else, it is unusable.

You should be using Whirlwind off Cooldown and having Slam be your filler whenever both MS and WW are unavailable, you have no Overpower to use, and there is nothing to Execute.

Furthermore, you should Slam relatively the same as you would in original Vanilla; sure, the Swing Timer here continues rather than pause (post-Vanilla) or reset (Vanilla), but the increased Cast Time of 2.5s (untalented) or 2s (Improved Slam 2/2) means that you will almost always have to use it just about immediately upon swinging. You may have enough time for one Ability (1 GCD, 1.5s wait time), but that may vary a lot and takes a lot of discipline to keep consistent.

You should also consider using Hamstring as an instant filler, despite its atrocious custom 6s CD, because it can still be used as a means of fishing for procs for Sword Specialization, Hand of Justice, and Windfury Totem, as well as whatever other chance-based effects you may have on top of all of that combined. Personally, I run without Improved Slam, and generally don't use Slam itself all that much, instead cycling MS/WW and fishing with Hamstring or stacking/refreshing Sunder, then using something else like Cleave (if AoE), and that is after initially activating Sweeping Strikes at the start (also if AoE), so I usually do not have downtime, and even if I do, it's better to just wait a short while to swing normally and get back to the rotation.

You should only ever really bother with Heroic Strike as a last-resort Rage dump if you have nothing available and your Rage is at 70 or more; implying 15R (untalented) as well as not producing Rage from your next swing, followed by MS and then WW - - that should spend all your Rage (implying no OP proc or Execute phase, no swing procs to immediately generate extra Rage).

Of course, this is how I've learnt to do it, and it has worked quite well for me; others may do it differently to their own benefit.

## Post 122819 by Wolf of Rage (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-03-17T18:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122819#p122819 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Note that for ordinary enemies (standard Mobs and weak Elites that die quickly), you should consider using Sunder only up to 50% remaining Health.

Additionally, Sunder Armor can still be used to proc certain effects, but not nearly as many as using Hamstring can: it can for example proc the Crusader Weapon Enchantment, but will not trigger Sword Specialization as an Arms Warrior.

The effectiveness of Sunder Armor's Debuff depends on how much Armor the Target has to begin with: if it has a lot, the benefit won't be big, as there will still be a lot of leftover Armor, but some is better than a lot more (especially with more Damage Dealers with you who rely on Physical Damage as their main source); if, however, the Armor is little as it is, the returns are larger, and by removing that amount, you end up restoring more of the otherwise-reduced Physical Damage you'd be dealing to the same enemy. You must however exercize caution with exceptionally low-armored enemies (not Bosses but other Mobs, and some Player Classes), as you cannot reduce Armor below 0 -- attempting to stack Sunder more than you would need would only be a waste.

Still better than using Rend starting from about Level 20, though, so indeed, use it more frequently and train yourself to know when and how to best use it, depending on the scenario in front of you.

Yes, and you should prefer to use your biggest Cooldowns during that period (Human Racial for extra Crit OR Recklessness; Bloodrage if you're running Enrage but that's unlikely to have in PvE), and time the Executes so that you're getting the most out of the Rage that you use up -- swing and then use Execute, since you might get a Crit for fat Rage (plus a Sword Spec which could be its own Crit), likely capping you out, allowing a max-burst Execute, then Precision Cut will give back tons, and then either do it again (especially if Execute procs Hand of Justice or Windfury Totem or Sword Spec for more Rage right back again), or wait for another swing and then Execute again.

Get a good rhythm and become comfortable with adapting it dynamically, and you should see some good numbers.
