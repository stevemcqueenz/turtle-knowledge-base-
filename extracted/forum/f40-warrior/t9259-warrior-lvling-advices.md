Best tip i can give you:
Ignore the protection talent tree.

The talents in the protection tree are really bad. your spells baseline are plenty good enough to tank anything before raids no matter your spec. Just skill damage and keep a onehander + shield around for when you wanna tank some dungeons.

Once you ding 60 and are more familiar with the class, you can always respec into a protection spec if you so choose
