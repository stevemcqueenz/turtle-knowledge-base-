## Post 118658 by Arkaris — 2025-02-01T21:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118658#p118658 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've never seen any value in the shield wall talent.  An extra 2 seconds, every 20 mins, seems like you could do a lot better with other talents.  If you are using Heroic Strike as a tank, you are dumping rage, so making HS cheaper defeats the purpose of rage dumping.

In my opinion the free rage revenge, +50% damage, 1.5 second cool down on revenge seems extremely valuable.

But really if your healers can take it, learning how to fury tank is the way to go until you start getting toward Naxx level gear.

Personally I always preferred Protection tank, but furytank is better for threat generation and just getting the raid going, again if your healers are able to get you through it.

In the end play what you like to play and enjoy the game.  Unless you are trying to be in an extremely hardcore raiding guild play what you enjoy and you can clear all content in time.
