# Master Strike and Precision Cut

## Post 166667 by Licata — 2026-01-27T22:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166667#p166667 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Master Strike and Arms Execute(by association Precision Cut) feel like running into a brick wall. Seeing the warrior changes for 1.18.1 I thought I'd try to show why I dislike these two talents and give suggestions on how they can be improved.

 **Master Strike:**
 7 impactful effects on a short cooldown and accessible to any spec, sounds amazing. Except at any given time you have the wrong type of weapon for 6 of them, and worse still you don't even have control over which one is available because weapon stats are so important I'll be using the weapon with the highest numbers, not the correct weapon type. There's no guarantee you'll have a weapon of the desired type to switch to that's close or that you'll have the weapon skill items for it, or that a weapon like that exists at all - if I as an arms warrior with a Kruul axe am fighting a boss I want to disarm with Master Strike my closest downgrades are 15 weapon dps down. If I could pick which effect is available on demand before I go into a bossfight that'd be great, but I can't without taking weapons away from others, handicapping my damage or both so I resort to in combat weapon switching.
So to use our swiss army knife talent we have to:
 1 - as you're using your previous ability(as long as it's not casted like slam, which you use a lot with a 2hander) you switch weapons, with a separate keybind and occupying a bag slot just to use one of many Master Strike variants, retriggering the gcd and resetting the swing timer
 2 - use Master Strike, doing pitiful damage
 3 - in the gcd after master strike switch back to your main weapon, resetting the swing timer again
You're doing 3 things, none of which do real damage, not autoattacking for 3-5 seconds depending on dual wield or 2hander for what, a 3 second disarm? The boss actually gets his weapon back and swings it before I do mine. It's easier to go into def stance and use the normal Disarm for an effect that lasts 3 times longer. And that's a debuff that bosses aren't outright immune to. How often do you immobilize and why wouldn't the monster turn and slap you as the only target in melee? Or disorient or silence? 2 second knockdown? Barely lasts longer than master strike's own gcd. Staff parry which is removed if you switch to another weapon? Is this more useful than pulling a shield out?

The problem I see stems from being tied to the mechanics of switching weapons, thematic sure, but incredibly clunky in practice. Trying to stay close to the idea of flexibility above even what other, unspecialized, warriors can do I suggest these:
 **1** - Turn Master Strike into an off-gcd buff to be used in combination with one ability to give it an extra effect. That way you can split the different effects into skills which fit them. My choices would be, starting with the obvious:
 - Hamstring also immobilizes for 4 sec on top of its 15sec slow
 - Shield Block also grants the 10 sec parry buff on top of the single block charge
 - Sunder Armor also disarms for 3 sec on top of applying the armor debuff
 - Thunder Clap knocking down for 2 seconds on top of applying the attack speed slow. I'm hesitant to make it multiple target, but it's a skill that does 100 damage at max rank without any scaling, and gets reduced by armor, and gets resisted, and the knockdown will be almost over by the time you can use your next skill
 - Whirlwind dismounting on hit. I do not hesitate to make this multiple targets because if it was on a single target skill is dismounting a single person really better than immobilizing them? Drop the extra 100% damage when dismounting unless you want warriors to explode packs of mounted people with Sweeping Strikes
 - Silence - I'm not sure. Adding it to Demo Shout fits as being loud enough to disrupt spellcasting around you, and the radius can be additionally specced into to make it even bigger. I'd rather not have it on an interrupt because that defeats the purpose of speccing a second ability that stops spellcasting.

My aim was to attach it to baseline abilities any spec can use on demand without relying on conditional skills like overpower or revenge. Slam would be a fine choice for knockdown as well. If a strict damage option is required to make it attractive for pure dps pumpers in raids add the 100% weapon damage on hit effect to Rend.

 **2** - Assign Master Strike's effects to stances rather than weapon types, they're on a separate gcd from the rest of our abilities so the process of setting up your desired variant and then using master strike flows naturally like the rest of warrior's stance restricted abilities. Since there's 7 effects and disorient as a cc overlaps with some of the others while also ending on damage I'd drop that and pair the rest up like so:
 - battle stance: disarm and silence, shuts down enemy's offense.
 - defensive stance: 10sec parry buff and immobilize, trap an enemy with you to give you parry haste.
 - berserker stance: knockdown and dismount, the risky stance getting the best cc and forcibly dismounting someone fits them not falling on their feet.

 **-------**
 **Precision Cut**
Execute being limited by the target's hp requires it to be worth more than our other skills, second in effectiveness only to the spec's main skill - Mortal Strike, Bloodthirst, Shield Slam.
Under 20% hp Fury stops using filler skills and spends all its surplus rage on Execute instead of dumping with Heroic Strike and using fillers for Windfury. There's a talent deep in Fury that helps that by reducing the base cost so even with the misses inherent to dual wield you can Execute your rage down to 0 and still be able to use it again after 1.5sec of autoattacks.
Prot gets a lot of rage from being hit, but to tank effectively it needs the threat bonus from Def stance. There's a talent deep in Prot which lets you retain that bonus in stances which can use Execute so you can stop using other filler skills and chain Executes instead of Heroic Striking to dump rage.
Arms can't do that. It generates rage once every 3+ seconds so, barring Windfury, it can't Execute more often than that. Even in the Execute phase it is forced to use fillers. So surely there's a talent deep in Arms which solves this, maybe through some sort of refund to let you Execute twice between your white hits. No it just makes it do more damage, except not enough for to be more effective than the filler skill you use all the time - Slam. That's ok, it's still not a dead gcd, you start doing Mortal Strike/Slam + Execute between your swings - you're still Executing in the Execute phase.
Except after you Execute you have 0 rage and if you're unlucky your next swing doesn't crit or proc windfury, rolls low on weapon variance, gets glanced if you lack weaponskill and you end up at 29 rage or less. Not enough to even salvage with 2 Slams. You don't have Enrage, Flurry and Death Wish to boost your rage generation during the Execute phase, you don't have Recklessness on every boss God forbid you're still progressing and use it on a wipe attempt.
The new 1.18.1 Precision Cut will give you more Execute damage bringing the surplus rage to ~26dmg/rage. In my gear Mortal Strike is ~45dmg/rage, Slam is ~70dmg/rage, it's not close. Even the most effective part of Execute - the base damage is lower at 40dmg/rage. With less gear it might compare better, but with less gear you're generating even less rage. Unless you're about to start overcapping your rage or the target is dying right NOW it's better to use Slam, even during the only time Execute is available, even if you've spent 3(please do not make Boundless Anger a prerequisite and make it 6) talent points on it.
Even when I play around it like the 1.18 patch notes mentioned - I'm at high rage from the last white hit, I'm casting a Slam first and Executing second so the next hit gives me rage to keep going - the game still often outplays me by proccing Windfury on the Slam, overcapping my rage and immediately spending it all on an Execute just after my swing reset so I sit there watching the game for 3 seconds before I'm allowed to press buttons again. To pass the time I look at Bloodrage and remember 10 rage doesn't let me Slam or Execute.
It's not only during Execute phase on a boss, it applies to adds as well. The scenario where Execute feels like it did the exact job it was supposed to - quickly dumping lots of rage from a white swing with no cast time to kill an add, freeing the ranged to switch to something that might not die before they finish casting - leaves me with no rage to intercept to the next target. I can't Battle Shout to rebuff, I can't use my increased Demo Shout range to fill downtime as I walk to the target. But say that next target is close and I don't need to intercept - I don't have the rage to Sunder it. I can't interrupt if needed. I can't even REND to maximize the number of ticks. I can only sit and wait for the next autoattack in 3 seconds like a level 14 warrior. Should I, as the Execute class specced into Execute, not Execute adds because it grinds my rotation to a screeching halt?
Whenever Arms Executes it risks ragestarving and you would have to make the scaling rage bonus 150% for a full 100 rage execute to compensate for missing the 1000 damage you could get from one Slam in the next swing. The refund solved this by ensuring we have enough to keep the rotation going in sync with our white swings while in Execute phase. Why was the refund removed and the extra rage scaling now coming back in 1.18.1? A 30% refund without bonus scaling still makes it a DPS gain over Slam when dumping close to full rage, the only time you want to use it even with the scaling bonus anyway, because it doesn't risk being unable to Slam afterward. It wouldn't make Executing twice at 100 rage more effective than Slam + Execute either.

Enough complaints - here's four suggestions I've come up with for Precision Cut to make Arms feel smoother:
 **1** - Return the 30% refund, lower or remove the extra damage scaling if absolutely needed. Encourages high rage Executes to avoid overcapping without making following low rage Executes effective enough to compete with Mortal Strike/Slam for the refunded rage to prevent chaining. Seems easiest as that's how the talent previously worked. Negatives are Execute gets used less often and possibly hits no harder than it does for other specs.
 **2** - Make Precision Cut a stacking buff you get on melee hit, stacks up to 3, gets used on Execute to boost extra rage scaling by 25% and refunds 10% of the rage spent per stack. First Executes are still big due to setting them up with white hits/Mortal Strike/Slam/Sunder/Overpower/Whirlwind for multiple stacks and whatever else. Following Executes are less rewarding to discourage chaining. Negatives are this being a third layer of setting up on top of needing a big rage bar and the target hitting Execute range.
 **3** - Keep the extra Precision Cut scaling, put a cap on the talented Execute for total rage spent that matches the damage of an untalented 100 rage Execute, should be almost 65 total rage with 75% extra scaling. Same Execute damage for less rage, you get rage left to work with afterward, a second Execute is less dmg/rage than Mortal Strike/Slam to discourage chaining. A full Boundless Anger bar lets you max out twice if you want to spend 3 talent points. Negatives are rage cap sounds buggy and Execute doesn't max out higher than other specs, only hits harder when compared to equal rage spent.
 **4** - Keep extra Precision Cut scaling, put a cap of 100 total rage spent on baseline Execute so at the very least we can use Boundless Anger to give ourselves leftover rage without changing anything for other specs. I hate it because you need to wait for 100+ rage to have anything leftover at all, but it's better than dropping to 0 rage with no counterplay.

Precision Cut has to pull double duty - compete for talent points with Enrage and support the Arms Execute phase so it doesn't get completely demolished by Fury stacking Enrage and Death Wish for more rage gen + more Execute damage on top of Flurry haste. When I say "hits no harder" in reality Fury's Executes will hit harder and more often even as 2h Fury because it has cooldowns to use for the Execute phase.

Finally, please do not make Boundless Anger a prerequisite. Right now I can take 3/3 Precision Cut, 2/2 Battlefield Mobility and Piercing Howl, that's perfect. If Boundless Anger is added to the cost of Precision Cut it leaves me with 2 points to spend on Improved Disciplines or Enrage and I've never felt 2 points in either of them are worth it. Dropping utility and wasting points to get to the talent meant to make Arms Execute work is the opposite of fun.

TLDR:
Switching weapons for Master Strike sucks, use stances instead or make it empower specific abilities.
Having 0 rage after Execute feels terrible no matter how hard the actual Execute hit, please let us refund or keep some rage.
I am begging you do not make Boundless Anger a prerequisite to Precision Cut.
