# Ideas for Warrior in PvP

## Post 166846 by Sylbrosa — 2026-01-29T14:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166846#p166846 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I feel that Arms Warriors are currently falling behind in PvP damage.

I once saw a post describing warriors as a boulder: if you hit correctly, you get the kill; if you miss, you just stand there like a rock, doing nothing. That analogy feels painfully accurate.

Master Strike gave warriors some much-needed versatility, but even with it, the class still feels behind others. Most ranged classes naturally have the upper hand, and we often lack the damage or tools needed to finish off Shamans or Paladins. Rogues are the only class I feel warriors can somewhat handle, thanks to Overpower and Rend — and even then, if I don’t have a trinket to break stuns, I'll just die.

Right now, Arms Warriors feel like sitting ducks in PvP.

It’s supposed to be rock-paper-scissors, but it feels more like rock-paper-scissors where you can only lose.  crying_turtle

So I’d like to suggest ways to bring better balance. If we follow the rock-paper-scissors idea, warriors should have an advantage against melee and physical-damage classes, while being slightly disadvantaged against casters.

Let’s group the classes like this:

 **Fighters**: Warriors, Paladins
 **Mid-range**: Rogues, Hunters, Feral (Cat) Druids, 2H Shamans
 **Casters**: Priests, Balance Druids, Mages, Warlocks, Elemental Shamans

And the intended matchup flow would be:

Fighters beat mid-range
Mid-range beats casters
Casters beat fighters

I’m intentionally not including healers in this breakdown. While healers are absolutely crucial in group PvP, my goal here is to discuss class-to-class balance and individual matchups.

I also know there will be comments saying, *“But warriors have Mortal Strike, you need to factor that in because it’s huge against healers.”* And I agree — Mortal Strike is extremely important in healer interactions. But that’s a separate discussion about PvP objectives and healer balance, not about how different DPS classes perform against each other in direct combat.

And yes, I also expect the classic *“warriors are great if you have a pocket healer or two”* argument. But let’s be honest: every class performs better with dedicated healing support. That’s not unique to warriors, and it doesn’t really address how the class feels or performs in standard PvP encounters or smaller skirmishes.

Here are some ideas to improve the Arms Warrior PvP experience by giving the class better tools against Rogues, Hunters, and 2H Shamans — without breaking class identity or making warriors overpowered.

 **1. New Ability — Nachreisen**

Effect: Becomes available after **the Warrior's attack get's parried**. Deals weapon damage %.

Casters won’t be parrying warrior attacks, so this ability would mainly affect matchups against fighters and mid-range classes. It would give warriors extra pressure where they are supposed to be strong, without impacting PvE damage.

It also keeps the warrior’s high skill-cap identity: the player must recognize when the ability is available and against which targets it’s effective, making it a reactive, skill-based tool.

 **2. Victory Rush / Momentum After a Kill**

The warrior is a boulder: once it gets rolling, it should keep rolling.

If free damage from Victory Rush is not desirable, the same idea could be implemented in other ways — for example, resetting Intercept on kill, allowing the warrior to quickly move to the next target.

This still keeps warriors disadvantaged against casters, since Intercept would not be available to deal with Blink, roots, or crowd control unless a kill has already happened. It would play similarly to how Charge works at the start of an engagement. If you want to make sure the warrior is directly responsible for the kill. Bake it into Execute.

 **3. Improved Deflection for Two-Handed Arms**

Add a skill-based parry mechanic for two-handed weapons, similar to Shield Block, deep in the Arms tree — possibly even combined with Sweeping Strikes.

Ideally, it would have its own cooldown separate from the global cooldown, making it more skill-based and reactive. This would improve survivability against fighters and mid-range classes, without affecting matchups against casters.

 **Notice that abilities #1 and #3 don't interact.**

I believe these three changes would greatly improve the Arms Warrior PvP experience without making the class overpowered.

Most importantly, abilities #1 and #3 would give developers tuning knobs that affect only PvP matchups against fighters and mid-range classes, without impacting PvE balance. By adjusting these tools, they could fine-tune warrior performance where it matters, instead of globally buffing damage.

And since they **don't interact** you can fine-tune DPS or survivability separately. Although if these are implemented. You can expect players to complain about them not interacting and saying they should. Because everyone wants a powerspike  hiding_smth_turtle_head

I’d love to hear other ideas as well. **Please comment and upvote**  so the developers can see these suggestions and the discussion around them.
