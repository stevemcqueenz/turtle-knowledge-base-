> **rindexxx wrote: Tue Aug 12, 2025 11:44 pm**
> im 44 as fury right now and dual wielding is bad, i miss half the stuff, no rage, small dps and if i dont crit im in trouble. HOWEVER if i have a shield and 1h equiped im tanky and dmg is the same. whats going on here? should i switch to arms at 50 when i get sulthraze

DW has a massive hit penalty on white damage while skill damage hit cap remains the same.  DW adds a 19% chance to miss on both hands, combined with the base 5%, that's 24%.  The new talent in fury increases ONLY your OH hit chance by 10% as a compensation for fixing HS queuing.  While leveling, your chance to hit with white damage on anything at level, or 2+ levels ahead of you, is going to be hot dick cheese.

I knew a rogue who didn't train dual wielding until he hit 60 specifically to avoid the hit penalty, which I think for that class occurred even with an empty OH because of the punching.  Warriors can throw a shield on to disable the OH as an attack.
