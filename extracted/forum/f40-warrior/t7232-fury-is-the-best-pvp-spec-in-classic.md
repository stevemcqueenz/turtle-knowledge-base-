# Fury is the best PvP spec in Classic

## Post 45809 by Bloodline1x9 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-14T03:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45809#p45809 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Arms is considered a pvp spec. But it started with TBC. On classic Arms as pvp spec is worse than Fury.
All benefits received by Arms came from the Fury spec.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/JhLxstG9/fury-tbc.png)

 1. First of all, Death Wish. A warrior who can be feared in the battle or defensive stance, or after 10 seconds, when Berserker Rage ends, is not a warrior.
 2. -10 second intercept. 30 vs 20 sec cd. One and a half times more mobility.
These both key talents comes from Fury.
Аnd the final and most important is "Second Wind". This is not in Arms or Fury on the Vanilla. It appeared in Arms and made it the best spec for pvp.

But at this stage, Arms does not have all these advantages. When you choose Arms you get Mortal Strike instead of Slam (DS). But you lose a lot. Including Flurry +30% AS.

The reason Arms is better is
#1, MS debuff is very OP
#2, You don't need as much AP to do damage since you scale off weapon damage more, this allows the warrior to equip more stamina heavy gear without major sacrifice since you can get carried by your weapon.
I tested this a lot on other 60 pvp servers, as much as i loved the intercept CD reduction, arms consistently felt better in 95% of situations.

I would say that Arms is the best PvP option for Wars, mostly because of the MS debuff, the improved Hamstring and the fact that you don't really need to be stucked to your target, you may have a critical on your MS and chunk half the HP of your target within a second, also in 1v2 situation you are better suited in arms with Sweeping strikes.

However I also think that Fury is not as bad as people make it to be in PvP. Fury has some potential to deal massive damages and be more "sticky" with the improved Intercept and the Death Wish fear immune.

Also I think that a Fury war may have more potential in a 2v2 scenario WITH a heal on ur ass. Fury is known to be by far the best PVE dps spec, they cannot be good at everything
