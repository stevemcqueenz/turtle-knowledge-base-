# Warrior is Inherently Bad (in PVP)

## Post 139157 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-07-14T18:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139157#p139157 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Warrior class is fundamentally doomed to failure in general PVP scenarios.

The reasons are countless but i'll give a couple examples:

First of all -- Rage is balanced around PVE content.

Dumbass NPC mobs hit the Warrior with generic melee attacks allowing him to get rage without dying too fast -- as a healer tops off the Warriors HP.

Competent players will just ignore/CC a Warrior -- cucking them of Rage -- and clapping said Healer instead.

Important Note:
Every single class starts a fight (respawn from GY) at their most powerful -- with full resources available (AKA Mana/Energy).

Warrior is the sole exception -- they begin every fight at their absolute weakest and most useless: 0 Rage
Warrior is known for Charge ---> Graveyard (due to no survivability/escape tools) so they will be constantly in a state of pathetic impotence.

In PVE -- AKA Raid Boss Fights -- long slow paced battles are standard. Giving ample time to build rage.
In PVP -- battles are over in a matter of seconds. No time at all to gain Rage. Simply Die too fast.

Secondly -- Armor is balanced around PVE content.

 90% of NPC only attack using generic physical hits -- this makes Heavy Armor/Shield very useful and viable.
Warrior essentially has no defense/survivability in PVP because roughly 66% of Players are using Magic Damage only.

Conclusion:
Warrior will continue to be hopelessly inept and dead weight in PVP Battlegrounds (and WPVP) unless massive reworks (or additional tools/abilities) are implemented.

However -- Turtle WoW is specifically designed/intended to be conservative in nature.
The unfortunate result?

Warrior will NEVER be viable. It should simply not be played in PVP.
I'm sorry. I really am.   sad_turtle
