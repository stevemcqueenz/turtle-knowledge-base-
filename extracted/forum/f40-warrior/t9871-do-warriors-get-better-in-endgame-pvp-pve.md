## Post 64561 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-09-15T21:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64561#p64561 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Warrior (At least fury and fury/prot) warrior is the best DPS and best tank at endgame with gear. And even without it you're very solid. Arms is actually a decent spec for DPS in endgame simply due to warrior scaling as well as it does, but fury is your best bet. Now, PvP wise, you're kinda fucked. Warrior, even as arms, the best PvP spec for the class, you'll be pretty underwhelming in most situations especially without help. Sure, you can pump big numbers and hit hard, but that isn't worth much when most classes can either kite you, or other melee classes have some sort of way to counter or disable you. (Rogues usually have the opening and stunlock until you're dead. Paladin is just... Paladin... Druid can bear and frenzied regen, or kite. I'm not sure on shammy, but they can kite a bit against warrior too.)

## Post 64581 by Reploidrocsa (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-09-15T23:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64581#p64581 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You get a huge power spike the moment you get 9% hit so your abilities won't miss.

Landing hits generate rage
Also the rage generated is proportional to the damage caused, so once you start stacking crit and attack power you'll have more and more rage so you'll be able to spend in more and more abilities.

The 1st step, 9% hit, won't come until you're lv 60 with some hit gear from dungeons and 20 man raids

Warrior is a class that needs to group up, alone they're kinda bad, no sprints, starts the combat with 0 resources, need to eat constantly, etc. That's why they excel in group pve and are very destructive in pvp with a backup healer
