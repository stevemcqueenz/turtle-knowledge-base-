# Sub Rogue needs some love

## Post 146058 by Jinkarasu — 2025-08-16T05:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146058#p146058 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

TL/DR in bold.

The changes and additions to the sub support tree seem pretty cool! I really like Shadow of Death and I think the consolidation of Imp Sap and the blinding debuff makes it a really solid talent.

 **The issue is the tightness of the Tricks of the Trade talent. At 12 seconds duration, this is unreasonable**. If a single hemo fails for any reason (miss, block, dodge, parry), or the finisher does for any reason, you drop the stacks. Additionally the timer requires you to spend 4 Hemos and 1 finisher (160-170 energy) every 12 seconds.

 12 seconds is 120 energy, accounting for Relentless strikes giving a refund, you're asking sub pve rogues to spend 160-170 energy with an energy income of 140. This means the energy talents must make up for it. Maybe in a highly optimized group for a raid, getting 5 energy every 1.5 seconds will actually pan out, and assuming every frame the cooldown is up there's a party physical crit to grant you the energy, this increases your energy income to 180.

Is the intent that this spec only feels good to play in a hyper optimized environment for raids? And only once you've acquired a minimum baseline of gear to ensure that nothing goes wrong rotationally? 4% crit isn't huge, but the upkeep on the buff being on such a tight timer really conflicts with the rest of the kit.

This barely holds together just using hemorrhage and expose armor and slice n dice. You gave us shadow of death, but the 30 energy cost means dropping the tricks stacks. Using distract means dropping the tricks stacks. Using smoke bomb drops the tricks stacks. Gouge drops the stacks. Blind drops the stacks. Kicking a spell drops the stacks.

You gave us support tools and then made the timer on this so tight that we're not allowed to use our support tools.
What's more is this talent REQUIRES ruthlessness and relentless strikes to be functional.

 ***Please make it last 30 seconds. Maybe 20 seconds minimum.***

Maybe gear fixes this, but I find it hard to believe that they want such a high gear check for an inconsistent 4% crit buff.
