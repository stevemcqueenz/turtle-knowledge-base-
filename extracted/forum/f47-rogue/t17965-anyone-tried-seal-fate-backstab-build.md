Hi,

like maybe a lot of rogues out there I am a bit disapointed by most of the changes to the Rouge talents. They did make me experiment a lot with new combinations though. I've come up with a bit of a odd build I'm wondering if someone out there have given a try:

[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?po ... AAAAAAAAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=aoaAIoAAYIAoAAaADoAAAAAAAAAAAoAYAAAAAAAAAAA=)

Image:
[https://photos.google.com/share/AF1QipP ... diLU5PZ3RR](https://photos.google.com/share/AF1QipPndrj0HFr5GPqwbISVn_Xt_UE7KNjIIuneIVpfEDudLf5Yb93VsNfBbvot7eufzw?key=c3NYUk1xYVIwRjBTWk10cFNfVzNGbWdiLU5PZ3RR)

The idea is:
 - maximum backstab damage + up to 3 combo points per backstab from Backstab talent and Seal Fate
 - Garote is normal boss opener, Cold Blood + Ambush is still an option for openers
 - keep up Rupture on boss and Eviscerate for spare combo points on finishing moves
 - we have some talent points to play around with - Ive taken Remoresless Attacks for extra crit chance during questing and dungeon pulls, Blade Flurry is another good option

Feel free to give your feedback.
