# Pros and cons being aliance/horde rogue

## Post 99343 by Gottabe (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-13T05:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99343#p99343 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Would be interesting to hear other people opinions about this, just my personal thoughts, but overall i think you shouldn't choose race or faction based on some superior perks, just enjoy esthetical aspect of game. Generally speaking about PVP:

As Horde rogue. most people choose undead or orc.

People choose orc for stun resist and bloodrage, which are amazing skills for pvp, especially stun resist... would help against other rogues or paladins, which you gonna face a lot, but it's kinda lottery thing :) but could save your life. If you got stunned by pally or other rogue, high chance that you're dead :) Only thing could help - consumables, but many players not willing spent money on them, especially during gearing process.
The other thing is facing human ability - perception, have to keep some distance or use vanish. Human rogues will have advantage finding you first.

Undead rogue, of course  WOTF, and generally esthetical aspect. Cannibalize also very underestimated skill :) WOTF amazing ability to counter fear. But you'll face all factors i mentioned above, forgot to mention dwarf racial ability, you always have to keep it in mind when use blind :)

As Aliance rogue most people choose human, dwarf or gnome.

Being human  is easier to detect stealth, but not always help with orc rogues, when your stun got resisted, could be total game changer, of course not in your favor :) Other good thing you don't need worry about paladins ) Most thing you have to deal is orc stun resistance racial.
Dwarf - good racial ability to remove bleeds, poisons and diseases, including blind from other rogues.
Gnome - good ability to escape slows and movement impairing effects.
Overall most annoying thing for alliance rogues is orc racial :)

I just mentioned some common situations you gonna face in bg's and world PVP, of course some things like engineering, consumables etc gives you huge advantage, but it's other story ;)

In favor of the vanilla races not mentioned,

Night Elves racial Shadowmeld effectively give them one extra level in the Master of Deception talent from the subtelty tree making it rank 6/5, like Humans, they will see other stealthed Rogues and Druids first,
Night Elves also have the highest starting agility, 1% higher dodge rating and access to wisp form.

Trolls get Berserking which is a racial that increases your attack speed, unlike other racials this one costs energy, they can deal good burst in short time in conjunction with Slice & Dice and Poisons,
Troll racial Regeneration also heals in stealth much faster.

All races are good at something, pick what you like cosmetically.  happy_turtle_head
