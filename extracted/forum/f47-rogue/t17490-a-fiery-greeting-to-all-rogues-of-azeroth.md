# A fiery greeting to all Rogues of Azeroth!

## Post 119376 by Resnica — 2025-02-08T21:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119376#p119376 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don’t know how it came to this, but after the latest patch, the situation for you, my fellow Rogues, is truly dire! I’m really sorry that our once-amazing PvP class has been turned into a disgraceful shadow of an assassin. Yes, it still kills, yes, it still controls… but I assure you, compared to all the upgrades, nerfs, and buffs of other classes, the Rogue has remained almost unchanged—or even weaker than before!

The idea of a support Rogue is interesting… but that’s no longer a true Rogue! Especially in the Horde and especially in the late game, the PvP situation is awful! The balance in PvP is completely off.

That being said, I truly appreciate the dedication, vision, and hard work of the Turtle WoW team—I just hope Rogues get the attention they deserve!
