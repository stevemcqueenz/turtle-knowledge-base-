Orc:
[Axe Handle]
Passive:You can wield 1Hand axes
Editors note:
Even tho i kinda think that axe is not rogue like weapon. giving orc rogue ability to wield axes would not break the game. This would also active Orc racial ability +5weapon skill to axes wich is realy good

Undead
[Cloak of the undead] 3min cooldown
Instantly removes all existing harmful spell effects and increases your chance to resist all spells by 30% for 5 sec.  Does not remove effects that prevent you from using Cloak of undead.
Editors note: Cloak of the shadows realy fits for rogue but giving it to every race would be overpowered. imo one race having it would be great fit

Troll
[Finishing Regeneration]
Finishing move that consumes combo points on any nearby target to restore 3% of maximum health every 3 sec. Lasts longer per combo point:
 1 point  : 6 seconds
 2 points: 12 seconds
 3 points: 18 seconds
 4 points: 24 seconds
 5 points: 30 seconds
Editors note: giving rogue heal would be overpowered but giving one race heal would be okay. also i think it fits greatly for troll

Goblin
[Time is money Friend] 5min cooldown
Finishing move  that throws money on the air  and gives you and your party 20% attack speed
more money and longer last per combo point:
 1 point  : 1copper and 3 seconds
 2 points:  1silver and 6 seconds
 3 points: 10silver and 12 seconds
 4 points: 50silver and 18 seconds
 5 points: 1gold and  20seconds

Nightelf
[Improved shadowmeld]
You can cast shadowmeld on combat. Also you can switch shadowmeld to stealth with out losing conceal
Editors note: Shadowmeld kinda useless on nelf rogues. This would give nelf rogues extra "feign death"  caster interupt

Dwarf
[Sinister shot]
 25 energy	8 yd range
 45 sec cooldown
Requires Gun
A short-range shot that deals 50% weapon damage and disorients the target for 4 sec.  Any damage caused will remove the effect.  Turns off your attack when used.
Editors note: Scatter shot but for dwarf rogues. would fit well with dwarf race because Gun mastery and gun vibe i get from the race.

Gnome
[Stolen Invention from grandmaster engineer] 5min cd
You have stolen engineering gadget from grandmaster engineer you get random gadget
 - battle chicken
 - Turret that shoots
 - target dummy
 - big sapper charge
editors note: i think gnome rascial ability should be engineering based. it just fits to the race.

Human

Passive: your pick pockets can give you item [usefull information] you can turn these at any capital runecloth donor for reputation
editors note: i think human have realy strong racials and giving them more damage would be too much imo. this also fits with diplomacy.
