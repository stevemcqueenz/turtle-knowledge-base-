## Post 148542 by Jinkarasu — 2025-08-27T02:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148542#p148542 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So the largest, most consistent benefit is the 4% physical damage increase that hemorrhage grants the raid. As long as you're spamming hemo, that should never really fall off. Additionally the improved access to Improved Expose Armor means you have the largest armor drop in the game, to boost physical damage yet further.

Then for your specific party, you have Tricks of the Trade that grants 2-4% additional crit chance depending on if you have the gear and raid comp to get a full 5 point combo off every 12 seconds.

Additionally you have Mark for Death which buffs you and your party by a % of your attack power.

Defensively, sub has some interesting abilities. Blind is a 10% damage mitigation if talented for it. Distract lowers enemy accuracy. Having access to improved sap makes some dungeons way easier.

That said, sub feels like ass to play until you have the requisite hit cap, a sufficient amount of crit baseline to power the energy procs, a full 5 man melee party in the raid group to additionally facilitate said energy procs, and possibly shadowcraft's energy proc bonus. If you don't meet all those requirements you'll be stuck with the 2% crit buff and never able to push any buttons other than hemo, expose armor, and slice and dice.
