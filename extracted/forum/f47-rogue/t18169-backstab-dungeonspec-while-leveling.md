camo to get faster and reduce stealth cd then you can kill next mob more quickly.
For dagger rogues during leveling, the main source of damage comes from stealth ambushes. Therefore, it's crucial to approach the next monster for an ambush more rapidly within the duration of remorseless attacks. Usually, when all attacks are critical hits, one ambush followed by one backstab and then a Eviscerate can kill the monster. However, the cooldown of stealth often doesn't reset in time, and you have to stand still and wait for the stealth cooldown to finish.

Camo is great to have while leveling imo. It's a lot of bang for your buck in twow, since it:
 - increase speed while stealth
 - reduces stealth cd
 - reduces chance to be detected

In the open world it makes manoeuvring around mob camps (and pick pocketing) much easier.

Idk how great daggers is for leveling solo. If you are teamed with a class that can taunt the mob then it probably works, but if not swords + riposte is probably a better approach. Otherwise you are putting a lot of points into improving skills you rarely get to use.
