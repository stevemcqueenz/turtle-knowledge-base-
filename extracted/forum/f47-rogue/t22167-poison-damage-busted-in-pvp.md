My issue is people who have clearly never played an energy class before completely blowing the whole thing out of proportion.

First and foremost, poison damage scaling has not actually changed between now and the last patch, the primary difference is noxious assault and envenom being added to the talent tree. However in a pvp scenario, a rogue is unlikely to have the spare time, energy, or combo points to pull up envenom, so that is basically off the board entirely.

Additionally, if a rogue is specced into noxious assault, that means they DONT have dirty deeds OR preparation. This means their cheap shot is 60 energy, not 40.

So in a full stunlock combo, they will AT MOST, manage to get off 3 noxious assaults in a cheap shot/kidney combo:

 60 energy 4 second stun cheap shot -> 4 second stun = 2 energy ticks, so they get to 90 energy (sin rogues have 110 energy) meaning they can pop 2 noxious assaults, however then they wouldn't have any energy left over for a kidney, so they need to cheap shot -> noxious assault -> kidney shot at 4 combo points, meaning they lose out on a 6 second kidney, and instead only get a 4 second. That's 2 more energy ticks, with an 80% chance to refund the kidney, so they get at most another 40 energy up from the 45 they were at, giving another 2 noxious assaults, and being left at 3 combo points.

Now each noxious assualt does approximately 300-400 armor-affected damage, plus two lots of 250ish poison damage (probably less due to envenom not being up), so about 800 damage total against an armored opponent. You get three of those off in a full cheap shot/kidney combo, if you leave yourself 0 energy afterwards for a blind. That's 2400 damage, plus auto attacks. Since the stunlock is 8 seconds, and we assume 1.3 speed daggers, that's 12 autos at 30% proc chance each, so about 3-4 more poisons, so another 750-1000 damage.

So you'll end up taking ~3150 - 3400 damage total. Yes that's a lot. Except the rogue is now at 0 energy. And you're not stunned. And if they blind you, you can trinket the blind. Because they're noxious assault spec, and don't have prep to re-blind. Also, you're not slowed, because they have dps poisons in both hands.

Now, I'm not really arguing for or against poisons and their balance in pvp, what I'm saying is: get your facts straight. Have a look at the dps meters and death summaries, because 9/10 when I die super quick to a rogue in pvp, it's actually due to ambush/backstabs, not poisons. They have a MUCH higher crit strike chance, and they can offload their energy way quicker, giving you little to no time to even register what's going on. However, since poisons are the big change for rogues this patch, I think people are just assuming that it's due to poisons. And backstab builds can easily spec into prep as well, AND they can pick up improved sprint for extra tools for breaking out of snares.

Tl;dr: Poison rogues are a pve spec. They pump damage, but are terrible at control. It is the equivalent of getting beat up by a combat spec rogue back in vanilla. If they outgear you, they will destroy you, but if you can survive the burst, they will flop instantly.
