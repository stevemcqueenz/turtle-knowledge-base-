# Tauren Rogues...?

## Post 67417 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-09-28T14:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67417#p67417 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just for clarification, this post is almost entirely for the sake of memeing. I'm not here to recommend, ask for, or demand any significant changes or changes at all, just some neat theorycrafting that may or may not be either underpowered or broken.

Starting off, why the heck would tauren become rogues? Well, anyone can wear leather armor and (attempt) to be sneaky or agile. 'nuff said.

Now, I think that obviously, given their stature, and build, tauren are not the best race for sneaking. With this in mind, I've decided to completely remove the ability to do so altogether! Now, you're probably asking "Wait, what? How do they use stealth abilities then?" Well, the answer is simply to give them a 'stealth', but allow them to be seen in this state. They will retain the slow move speed, and have a longer cooldown than regular stealth, but there could be some sort of compensation. Buffing the effects/damage in comparison to other rogues, or something similar comes to mind. I'm thinking of calling this "stealth" something like 'poise', wherein they prepare themselves to strike, like a bull kicking his hooves back.
