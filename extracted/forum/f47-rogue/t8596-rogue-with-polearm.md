## Post 55417 by Bloodline1x9 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-01T00:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55417#p55417 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**[Impale]**
 *80 energy*
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/sDgwJd2L/impale.png)
Requires: polearm
Deals 100% weapon dmg. Awards 2 combo points.
Deals 125% if behind the target and stuns for 1.7 seconds.
Always deals 125% and stuns if target already stuned or incapacitated.

Incapacitate means sap, gouge, blind, poly, fear.

Why it stuns for 1.7 sec? It's short stun but it's more the 1 global cooldown. The energy cost will not allow to use this skill often (The normal energy regeneration is 10 energy per second.)

What do you think?
