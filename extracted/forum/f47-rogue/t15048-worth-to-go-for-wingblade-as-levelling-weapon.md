# Worth to go for Wingblade as levelling weapon?

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102305#p102305 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Trying to follow along one of Shadowpanthers Rogue guides and they list [Wingblade](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6504) as the best weapon in the 15-25 level bracket. Think it's worth to go for it or to buy it from theAH if available?

## Post 102306 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-01T11:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102306#p102306 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all, it is main hand weapon. So you have to level as combat spec without ambush.
Almost every dungeon in game features quest to get blue sword. For leveling rogue, it might be a problem, because you need to find party or make one.
Amount of time you might spend on gathering/waiting a party might be higher than amount of time you might spend on grinding gold for blue sword from AH.

I think best tactics is - queue LFT and level with whatever you can find. Don't treat this sword as must-have weapon.
