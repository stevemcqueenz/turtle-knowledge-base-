# The many issues with Subtlety

## Post 153929 by Tankmeister — 2025-10-01T14:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153929#p153929 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is mainly from a PvP perspective. I find that several deep sub talents are very lackluster and/or totally messed up in their current state...

 - Smoke Bomb -
I was excited to try this one out. I like the idea of the sub rogue being elusive and harder to hit, which fits the archetype imo. However, I just cannot make good use of this ability. I think the problem stems from the fact that it affects ALL within the area, not just allies. Furthermore, it has a FIVE MINUTE cooldown!? It's extremely situational at best.

 - Cloaked in Shadows -
Make the absorption shield not break stealth when absorbing damage - that would fix this talent. Right now, the shield for you and allies isn't terrible, but it isn't something you're gonna pop vanish for.

 - Blinding Haze -
It lasts only for 6 seconds and reduces hit change by 6%... I dunno, I think this one just sucks. This just needs a numbers tweak to make it good.

 - Shadow of Death -
This one is just a mess in my opinion. You're supposed to spend all your combo points on an ability that requires you to do damage in the next 6 seconds? This shouldn't be a finishing move - it makes absolutely no sense. As it stands you're heavily dependent on teammates doing the damage, and it doesn't feel right. It has the potential to do good damage but it isn't enough to justify the finicky nature of it.

 - Tricks of the Trade -
As someone else mentioned in another post - this one sucks and isn't worth spending 5 points into for anyone. Numbers are too low.

 - Mark for Death -
This really confused me when I tried it out for the first time. I totally expected it to put a debuff on an enemy (judging from the name + description and the fact that it is an attack), but it just gives everyone a buff instead. Sure, buffing everyone is more powerful but thematically it is a bit of an absurd design in my opinion. What I would like to see instead, is a move you can cast from within stealth, that puts 2 combo points and puts a strong debuff on a target. That would just make a lot more sense and would fit the playstyle better and would be more fun I think.

Overall, I think the sub tree is a bit all over the place in terms of vision. I don't mind the "support" direction, but how it is implemented now just feels awful. One problem is that you depend too much on allies (Shadow of Death, Tricks of the Trade) and that really isn't fun. I think a sub rogue can be supportive in other ways that ARE fun, and I think the team should lean more into stealth, elusiveness and CC much more. I'm imagining a rogue that is difficult to lock down, that disables the enemy, and can support the team with e.g better designed smoke bomb and mark for death.
