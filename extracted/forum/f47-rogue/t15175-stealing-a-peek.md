## Post 103039 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-13T23:42:00Z

My answer will be very limited because I only did PVE with my rogue. No raiding either. Troll boy is currently around level 26 because I found the PVE gameplay boring and I haven't touched him for months. It means that I had less tools than a raiding rogue, what I saw was limited.
But telling what I heard and experienced.

PVP might be fun, I heard. Farming is okay, specially if you have some bandages. Dungeons were boring. Before making the rogue, I was imagining moving in the shadows, sapping mobs before pulls, being a utility and kick powerhouse, kinda a ninja. In theory, you can try sapping mobs before pull but unless you go to the PVP build, you'll probably lose stealth, everything attacks you and you are forced to use a reagent+CD ability to get back to the shadows. Tanks won't wait for your little show anyway, they'll just pull.
Once pull happens, you run to the mob, seek his backside and you start a mini game of energy, combo points and finishers. Sounds fun but was boring. You make sure you maintain the haste buff on yourself, maybe do some eviscerates, everything else is only there if you're fancy. Can expose armor but if there's a warrior in the party, they'll just automatically overwrite it with sunder all the time.
So you're hitting the backside of the foe and make a rotation 90% focused on keeping up a haste buff. Sometimes you can kick which is cool. But as I experienced, most of the rogue's fun is suppoed to be stealthing around and doing huge damage from a well-planned opening. Try this in a dungeon where the tank feels like being on cocaine and the hunter somehow pulls half of the region. At the end of the day dungeoning felt really boring. Looking for mobs' backside, auto-attacking. Sinister strike, auto-attack, keeping up haste, LF other enemy backside.
And your own professions... On paper sounds cool. In practice you need a reagent or material for everything. A rogue wants to shít, he needs a reagent to do it I swear. For example poisons are cool. But you make your doses in advance using materials and they expire super fast. On my enh shaman I just selected the weapon enhancement I liked and pressed it every 5 minute. You want to use half of your abilities? Reagent. You want to look like an orc peon? Materials. Every time you make the costume. Only lockpicking is different which just requires some tools to be in your bag. And also spending one of your Friday nights in a ship in the Barrens, interacting with a set of chests.

People also kinda boo internally every time you show up at the dungeon. Good damage, sure. But palas/shamans bring super buffs, a mage gives int, water, sheep etc, even a hunter can use traps. As a rogue you go to a dung 'bufflesly' and also rival most tanks at items. (I mean palas and warriors at weapons, drus at some leather items).

From raiding, I only heard about it. In theory it's chill with an easy rotation. But also, rogue is the only class which has no real upside at raids after BWL. From AQ40 a fury warrior is almost always better.

Again, for PVP I have no idea.
