# Simple Fix for Rogue Tank Threat Problems

## Post 167928 by Lomikare1 — 2026-02-11T05:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167928#p167928 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Dear developers,**

I’ve been playing the unconventional Rogue tank specialization for several years now — yes, there are still players who enjoy it because of how unique it is.

Rogue tanks currently face one major issue: the poison that boosts our threat deals Poison damage, and a surprising number of enemies are immune to it. These enemies become extremely difficult, almost impossible, for us to tank. It’s comparable to Paladins suddenly losing Righteous Fury or Warriors losing their Defensive Stance threat bonus on a large portion of bosses or mobs.

The fix is simple and consistent with previous adjustments to rogue poisons: Agitating Poison should deal Physical damage instead.

 **Proposed change**
 *Agitating Poison → renamed to Itching Poison: now deals Physical damage instead of Nature damage.*

Thank you for taking the time to read this, and I wish you smooth progress in developing this wonderful game.
