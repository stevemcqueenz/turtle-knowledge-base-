# the 2006 history rogue tanking could have had

## Post 49177 by Derrurak — 2023-06-07T11:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49177#p49177 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When people see rogues tank on turtle wow some people think its broken and not how wow used to be, i shall now demonstrate with 3 boss examples that rogue tanking has always been viable but just not practical enough to see more than niche use in 2006(razuvious was in fact killed the way i am about to describe)

So, to start with heres why it works so well on turtle wow, flourish grants 20% parry chance which gets you from 80 to 100% avoidance
Heres what you could have done on true 1.12: ghostly strike(15%)+evasion(50%)+preparation(cd reset)

with full mitigation gear(no custom items) my rogue is currently about 0.8% above cap without insect swarm, with bis gear it wouldve been over 1%
i reach cap without scorpok=>2.9%
there is no insect swarm=>4.9%
mongoose does not stack with agi totem=>6.7%

so since flourish has 5% more avoidance than ghostly strike a true 1.12 rogue tank using ghostly strike would still have 1.7% mitigation above cap
this assumes kings+grace which wouldve been possible with mind control, however alternatively they could have grabbed worldbuffs for the same if not greater effect - not practical, but possible

a single rogue with a cd reset rotation of ghostly=>evasion=>ghostly=evasion=>ghostly has 51 seconds of immunity
however the average boss would take 9 seconds to actually land a hit and kill the rogue afterwards, its even possible for him to not hit for 13sec at which point another ghostly of 7s can be used, including that and accounting for varying swingtimer speed the life expectancy is about 62 seconds or so - with 3 rogues used in that manner you have bought over 3 minutes of time, which is enough to kill razuvious but not patchwerk/loatheb

however this isnt the most ideal way to kill loatheb and patchwerk

patchwerk:
for patchwerk one would simply have 3 rogues rotate ghostly strike, when one rogues ghostly strike expires the next gets a stam buff and uses his-3x7=21s duration 20s cooldown technically doable and would save a raid 6 healers, hateful strikes give free threat solving that issue as well

loatheb:
loatheb doesnt hit that hard, so with armour+stamina consumables a rogue can survive getting crit by the boss especially if they have worldbuffs, the rotation would be ghostly strike on cd=>wait to get hit=>if no ghostly evasion get healed up=>ghostly strike on cd=>preparation ghostly strike get healed up =>get hit=>if no ghostly evasion=>ghostly strike=>get hit and healed up=>ghostly strike on cd=>get hit=>ghostly on cd heals not ready get crit die=> 2nd rogue takes over repeat

loatheb hits fairly slowly, so if we assume it takes the boss on average 14sec to land a hit, so half the time we get ghostly strike ready again and half the time we dont
this is gonna depend on rng, but a ballpark estimate would be:
till first hit its gonna be ghostly+13s+ghostly+6s=33s, evasion 48s, ghostly+13s+ghostly+6s 81s, preparation ghostly+6s+evasion+ghostly+13s+ghostly+6s+6s =148s or 2 and  a half minutes per rogue, 2 rogues on average would be enough to tank loatheb with a third being safety, thanks to spores threat is a nonissue 1min into the fight

conclusion:
as you can see the 3 typical rogue tank fights in naxx out of which some are among the hardest fights in the instance have always been possible to tank using rogues, so why did this not happen? for one it requires more thought, preparation and good gear, but in particular not having portable respecs meant that you already had 10 healers for loatheb, you already had a bunch of tanks for patchwerk, having 3 rogues with garbage dps specs the entire raid just so those people can be useless wasnt the most practical

what turtle wow changed wasnt making rogue tanks viable-it was making them practical, and the biggest change that enabled rogue tanking wasnt flourish but portable respecs. if people had the ability to respec on demand rogue tanking wouldve seen much more use in 2006 even without flourish

its not that a broken spec was created using flourish, rogues are now able to utilize a niche spec thats always been viable but simply wasnt worth using for the entirety of the raid thanks to portable respecs

disclaimer: i do think flourish should be rebalanced however that should be in the context of a rogue tank rework which should happen because as ive demonstrated, rogues have always been capable of tanking-they were just lacking quality of life features such as respecs, portable scorpoks etc to make it worth bothering with. theres also a lot of talents in the rogue trees aimed at tanking which is why it makes much more sense to build on that filling the gaps to make rogues more well rounded n proper tanks as opposed to trying to destroy something that was viable in 1.12 all along

its already a thing and always has been.

last time i checked meme specs were those that didnt perform well, which means rogue tanking isnt and wasnt a meme as it has superior mitigation

as for improving weak specs, the point of this post was not to make suggestions. its as the title describes- what rogue tanking couldve accomplished in 2006 already. its to highlight a piece of content that has always been present somewhere but never really been seen which is pretty much what turtle wow is all about. however rogue out of all classes has the worst trees with hemo and assassination being mostly unplayable and combat is just an inferior version of fury, but since you want suggestions here they are:

my suggestions for future class changes would be:
 -to allow rogues to disable the inherent threat penalty as all other tanks can
 -to introduce a fok type aoe because rogues are the only class without any baseline aoe and to differentiate rogues from warriors 2 target cleave playstyle and present an alternative to bladeflurry
 -rework assassination into a spec that is worth using with lots of crit like the seal fate talents suggest with a worthwhile 31 point talent
 -add more damage talents worth taking in the sub tree, someone playing hemo takes practically no damage increasing talents for the first 15 points
 -fix abilities that dont scale nearly well enough for what theyre meant to be such as rupture eviscerate scaling less than an auto attack with attack power when theyre meant to be big chunky 5cp finishers and poisons not scaling at all
 -to rework flourish into a cheat death/stagger or similar type ability as right now rogue mitigation is certainly too much of an all or nothing thing
 -minor fixes such as combopoints and not being able to vanish boss fights

its worth noting that rogue tanking doesnt really need buffs other than the 20% threat reduction being removed, because the primary issues with rogue tanking are fundamentally rogue dps problems such as the lack of aoe
