# Rogue spec identity discussion

## Post 148387 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-08-26T12:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148387#p148387 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So both in theme and gameplay what are they?

Judging by the talents alone they're like this for vanilla:

 **Assassination** - Boosts to Combo Finishers and Poisons. Thematically, the Poison rogue.
 **Combat** - Boosts to combo starting abilities out of stealth, with some PvP utility.
 **Subtlety** - Boosts to combo starting abilities while in stealth, with some PvP utility.

in TWoW it's mostly the same, with **Subtlety** being changed to have support utility as well.

Now that's a very high level overview, but these have always felt off (to me), and only in Wotlk did each spec start feeling more distinct and with a more focused intent. What I mean by this is, which is the PvP and which the PvE spec? Is it at all 3 or each has partial benefits to help with PvP and PvE. It's obviously the latter, but why? Doesn't that only make balancing each spec harder?

And as far as identity, why does Assassination have both Poisons and Combo Finishers as its gimmick? Wouldn't the Combo Finishers fit Combat rogue more? Their whole playstyle is pressing their 1 combo generating ability and spamming a finisher, so why not enhance that? Likewise, Assassination benefits from fast attacking weapons to proc poisons, so why not give dagger specialization to them instead? Or better yet Sub, since daggers are a "subtle" weapon opposed to swords and maces. Or what about Improved Backstab. That fits Sub's theme of sneakily doing damage a lot more than it does combat. And in terms of how damage is done, how are they defined? Assa is changed to be more DoT heavy thanks to Taste for Blood, but Serrated Blades is in the Sub tree. In the last patch, you could play Backstab Assa, and still pickup Serrated Blades, but with the talent changes that build path is significantly weaker

My own interpretation for ideal gameplay identity of these specializations has been:

Assassination = DoT damage and additional effects based on applied poisons.
Combat = Combo damage, expert in Swords and Maces.
Sub = Burst damage, and a lot of PvP utilities.

Of course, that's entirely my own point of view, and I'm not suggesting any changes in this thread. But I really want to hear your opinions on these specializations' identity. Be it gameplay or theme
