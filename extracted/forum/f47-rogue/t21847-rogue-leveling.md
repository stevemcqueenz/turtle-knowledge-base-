Lv 10-19
 2 points in Camofaluge
 3 points in Improved Gouge
 3 points in Improved Ambush
 2 points in Remorseless Attacks

Lv 20-29
 5 points in Opportunity
 3 points in Improved Backstab
 2 points in Camofaluge

Lv 30-35
 1 point in Camofaluge
 5 points in Precision

From here on out you can experiment with other talents. I recommend specting into things that further boost your Ambush so it will consistently slash away huge chunks of the enemy's HP. So things like Malice, Murder, Lethality, Serrated Blades and Initiative. For dungeons, some extra points in Improved Blade Tactics can be quite useful

Your rotation is very straighforward: Ambush -> Gauge -> Backstab -> Eviscerate. If the target is not dead, use Sinister Strike or repeat the Gauge -> Backstab rotation.
