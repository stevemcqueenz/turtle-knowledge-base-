# The role of Subtlety

## Post 124849 by Unsilentstorms — 2025-04-01T07:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124849#p124849 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

as a sub rogue player, I thought I'd address some things that I noticed in both the talent trees and from the statements in one of the patch notes listed:
 ------------
"Rogue has a specialization that works fine aside from a few pain points, one that’s spot-on flavor but falls short gameplay-wise and one that lacks proper identity and tools especially in a raiding environment"

"This specialization lacks both identity and falls off in performance in raiding environments due to not being able to use their openers after their stealth and vanish are spent. We’ve considered abilities that would solve this such as Shadow Dance but after lengthy talks the conclusion was that Rogue did not need another flavor of dealing damage, and we’ve gone ahead with quite a different approach: a specialization that focuses on supporting their allies."
 -------------
The issue is that people are trying to look at the specializations in terms of an "identity" and not for what they actually are. Each class in the game have abilities comprising of 3 different categories. In this case, execution based abilities are assassination, toe-to-toe combat and defensives are 'combat' where as subtlety revolves around enhancing underhanded trickery and its efficiency.

To me it seems the talents in subtlety are mildly neutered, with core talents removed and made baseline with other talents placed at the convenience of an assassination / combat rogue for more DPS. but subtlety was not about just catering purely to some PVE play style, since it had PVP emphasis taking into account the nature of the game world and the various playstyles that can emerge to account for. It's about having a set up advantage, and emphasizing benefits from it's ability to exploit its foes (by way of getting openers, better stealth, dodging/avoiding, and preparation to get an extra set up) which is why making Heightened sense base line and merging camouflage with master of deception is not a good idea, and explains why it lacks identity.

And then at the expense of the spec, they allow easier access to improved ambush, serrated blades and hemo. That just sort of minimizes what subtlety has to offer by giving people an easy 5 points and another 3-6 to get all the main benefits out of the spec. -- where as if I want to get the most benefits out of assassination, I need at least 10 points. it would be like just dropping lethality/relentless strikes, or vile poisons into the second tier.

The idea as asssassin or combat is favoring opportunity to get camouflage so that you have the better CD and speed with more damage with less effective stealth.

Removing the Setup talent also nerfs the whole rogue tanking concept similar to a D&D style Duelist that you'd get paired with combat talents focused on a more regular use of ghostly strike with riposte and dodge talents. but this obviously could clash exploiting an abundance of dodges through abilities like smoke bomb + evasion + ghostly strike to build Combo points.

Hemorrhage actually boosts all sources of bleed damage to a target, even by group/raid members, and is where sub is meant to see its PVE DPS enhancement. and deadliness increased 10% attack power which increases your bleed damage

heightened awareness should remain as a talent with ghostly strikes and setup. there should be no dust of disappearance, smoke bomb or tricks of the trade or irritating agent, blinding haze, improved ghostly strike, honor among thieves. just seem pointless ineffective abilities no one is going to take anyway to play a sub raid spec or PVP spec when they can just get all the benefits of being combat or assassin with hemo and serrated blades improved ambush. and they also purely rely on a party.
 ------------------------------

Imo Feint needs to be improved. There should be more functionality around it and the talent sleight of hand. like making dodge chances during feint reduce your chance to be critically struck, or chance to avoid AoE or reduce it's damage like in retail - also similar to TBC's enveloping shadow talent. Improved sap should get similar functionality to Dirty Tricks talent.

Alternatively to irritating agent you can have sap/blind fades causing enemy to miss more often for a short duration.

now I don't know what DPS ratio's and rotations are looking like in this server yet. but I think the cata rotation had a nice feel. you set up to get in with the energy regen from slice and dice, enhanced damage on your bleeds, and chances to refresh your rupture on eviscerates.

In a similar way, the honor among thieves talent should be replaced with a similar energy regen without a party requirement, like either from bleeds ticking, damaging bleeding targets, or a slice and dice fairly balanced. this could also incentivize sub to go for an improved slice and dice for convenience.

The point overall is that some of these decisions don't seem very sensible and I think this approach can be revisited

Yeah. and there's a number of ways they can deal with this. I for one, like the shadow dance concept but I don't think the execution of it.

Shadowdance itself is based off the D&D Shadowdancer subclass that uses "Hide in Plain Sight" (also shadow clones) where periodically you can use this ability to re-enter stealth and get an attack off. but the issue with this is rogues already have it (vanish) and to further support it with preperation for a second use. and imo playing around with this feature could potentially make it a useful DPS rotation skill.

For example, you can have a raid talent that works similar to honor among thieves, but lowers the cool down of preparation so that you can get more vanish procs in a raid fight (although this could interfere with group PVP).

Or you can simply have a shadowdance ability that only let's you stay in stealth for 1-2 seconds without breaking combat so you can't sap with limited time to execute an ability, without the entering and re-entering stealth multiple times mechanic as it originally did, and make it only proc occasionally on CD from certain conditions.

Another alternative is an ability like shadowstep where it can have a talent proc to automatically cast ambush out of stealth similar to shadowstrike. making it so you'd want to save your shadowstep use for those procs.

These all seem to me pretty simple ways to get the benefits of the in stealth set up rotation into longer fights while limiting their use so that they are not too heavily abused for PVP situations
