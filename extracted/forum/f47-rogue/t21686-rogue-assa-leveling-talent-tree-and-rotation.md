The true assassination build doesn't really come into it's own till level 43, and better if you hold off till your 50's (IMHO) and even then you wouldn't have the talent Precision +5% to hit (at level 43) very important to have.
 50%+ of your damage is coming from your poisons and if you are missing more because you don't have Precision, you damage is going into the toilet.

Let's say you don't go with Precision first, and just start putting points into the Assassination tree. Any meaningful damage wont start until level 28-33! when you can start putting points into Vile poisons and Improved poisons again 50%+ of our damage comes from POISONS.
Build without precision: <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=CoYDIgDY-->

If you take precision: Any meaningful damage wont start until level 38-43! when you can start putting points into Vile poisons and Improved poisons

Build with precision: [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?po ... IgDY-AoAo-](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=CoYDIgDY-AoAo-)

The main combo point builder for Assassination is the last talent in the tree Noxious Assault (Just Crazy I know)
which would mean you will have to run sinister strike or backstab. Running Improved Backstab would cost you another 3 talent points if you go that route.

Glad you are enjoying it

Regarding poisons: Poisons are situational really. (jmho) Use 2 fast daggers and don't use any damage over time poisons ie. (Deadly, corrosive)

If you are fighting animals, beasts and the like, just go double instant until you get Dissolvent poison.

If fighting humanoids, orcs, trolls and the like, I would run instant on main hand and crippling on off hand.

In dungeons, it's your call, depending on what mobs it is that you are fighting and how spaced out are they from each other.

I'm sure you have notice that humanoids have a tendency to run when they get low on health, and by George we do not want them running away agroing more mobs lol.

Dungeon Rotation: Rotation is simple, you are going to try and open up on a mob from ambush,( since stealth is on a 5 sec cooldown, you should have time before each mob pack in dungeons to re-enter stealth). from there it's backstabbing till you get to 4 combo points then slice and dice and repeat.

Open World

I don't like sinister strike unless i absolutely have to use it.

Poisons: See above

What I do- Ambush into gouge, pool a little energy and pirouette to the back side and backstab.
I now have 3-4 combo points.

 1. how much health they have left, not much? Eviscerate, call it a day, get back in stealth and do it again.

 2. They have more health than I like, Check to see if crippling up on mob, rupture and kite (while lol-ing) as soon as rupture runs it course, gouge, pirouette to backside and backstab. if their not dead you should have 2-3 combo points to eviscerate to finish em off or rupture again and keep lol-ing :) so much fun being a rogue.

As rogues, we have alot in our tool box to use, like, blind, Kick, Gouge, Sap, using Vanish offensively ( don't use Vanish offensively when solo, it will rest the mob back to full health)

while kiting, feel free to work on your range weapon skill :)

A lot of stuff I have covered here [1.18.0 Rogue leveling spec](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=20993%5B/url)
