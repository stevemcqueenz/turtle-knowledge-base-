Is some buff on you when your target dodge and you able to apply Surprise Attack  ?
For [Roid-macros addon](https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/Roid-Macros) I can propose such macros
/startattack
/cast [nomybuff:Seal_of_Righteousness] Sinister Strike
/cast [mybuff:Seal_of_Righteousness] Surprise Attack

replace here Seal_of_Righteousness - with name of your buff with underscore

> **Snaire359 wrote: Wed Mar 26, 2025 1:08 pm**
> hi, is there a single macro for sinister strike and surprise attacks ?

/cast Surprise Attack
/cast Sinister Strike

simple as that. It will cast SA if available, otherwise cast SS. The only downside is you give up control for when SA is up but you actually want to SS, but why would you want to do that anyway.
