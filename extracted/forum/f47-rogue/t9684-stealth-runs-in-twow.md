## Post 64820 by Gantulga (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-09-17T01:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64820#p64820 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Custom dungeons have mobs with broken stealth detection where your stealth levels do not mean anything so you're seen easily even if you're 10 levels above. You gotta be extra careful there and vanish is often times necessary to get past some packs.

With the total gutting of Flourish next patch, your soloing potential is also greatly diminished.
