Well as assassination the only requirement imo is being 40 for noxius assault and you MUST have poisons on weapons. The rotation is simple you usually choose to spend combo points in eviscerate or rupture depending if the mob is going to be alive for 10+ seconds or not. The perfect situation would be keep rupture on enemy, slice and dice on yourself and only then use eviscerate, but I never managed to do that. Lot of energy starvation in classic

In combat/sub you can go any weapon you like build differs slightly.
I usually go dagger sub (with few combat talents for backstab) when doing dungeons (and if i raid too) since I can always backstab enemies easy. The improved backstab talent (combat) enhances crit chance and gives a chance to get 2 combo points I think it's is huge but then again if you are solo leveling it's kinda shit to frontstab enemies.

With non-dagger sub you definitely want to focus on hemo talents and ignore improved backstab and improved ambush for obvious reasons.

In any case I do not recommend going full sub if you are solo leveling some big talents rely on your party or are better in party (tricks of the trade, shadow of death, honor among thieves, mark of death). You could build sub until hemorrage and then swap to combat talents for example
