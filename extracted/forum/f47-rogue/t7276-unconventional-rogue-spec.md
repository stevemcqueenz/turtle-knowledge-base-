I'm going to assume you are not using daggers as you didn't pick up improved ambush or improved backstab.

You do not need opportunity if you are not going daggers/backstab. Those points should be moved to master of deception, if you don't take master of deception for pvp you are going to have a bad time, stealth is your advantage and not something that should be tossed because you want some extra bleed damage on garrote.
Setup could be dropped and put into serrated blades giving you the ability to pick up hemo which costs less energy then sinister strike and combos very well with dirty deeds reducing cheap shot energy cost.

For close quarters combat and dual wield spec you don't really need those unless you are in a super prolonged fight, even then going close quarters combat is kinda odd considered you have 5% crit in the assassination tree that leads it self very nicely to other talents that are extremely helpful. I don't understand the point of going that deep into combat if you are not going to pick up riposte as well which can be very good in pvp. if you don't have enough hit % on gear then sadly precision is needed till you do. You also do not need endurance since you have prep.

If you have enough hit% on gear I recommend <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue/KXC6U1-I-U2X02K86>
If you need hit% still <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue/CX06O-IC5-U2X02K86>  drops coldblood and other such things. If you are using daggers and you are using sinister strike then I'm going to have a fucking stroke.
