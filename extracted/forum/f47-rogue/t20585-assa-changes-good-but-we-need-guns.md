> **Kerenis wrote: Sun Aug 24, 2025 9:21 am**
> Noxious Assault does more damage with slow weapons thoug

Not true. It doesn't use weapon damage values at all. It's a % of your total attack power. If your attack power is the same with two slow 2.5+ speed weapons as it is with two fast 1.5 speed weapons, it will hit the same damage. Attack power normalization does not apply to spells that do not use weapon damage in the formula.

Which means that fast weapons are actually optimal for assassination because the weapon speed doesn't matter for noxious assault, but it DOES matter for how frequently you can apply poisons through auto attacks. More swings = more procs = more damage.

And assa is like 50%+ poison damage.
