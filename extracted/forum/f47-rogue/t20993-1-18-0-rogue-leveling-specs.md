If I were to say what talents to get:

lvl 10-11: Remorseless Attack, (after killing a mob, 40% increase in crit to ambush, backstab or sinister strike) lasts 40 seconds. Huge!
lvl 12-16 Camouflage ( get back in stealth in 5 seconds and increased movement speed while in stealth, Huge!
lvl 17-19 Improved Ambush ( 45% increase to crit for your ambush) Huge!
lvl 20-24 Opportunity ( Increases the crit damage to ambush and backstab by 20%) Huge!
lvl 25-27 Improved Backstab ( 30% to crit on backstab, plus 45% chance of getting a combo point) Huge!

<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=C-FAD-FAY>

Your cool down on stealth is 5 sec. You lickity spit to your target and ambush (45% chance to crit) you sinister strike 3-4 times and it dies. You hit stealth and again lickity split to you next target but this time you have the remorseless attack buff which adds 40% crit to your already 45% ambush crit buff.   turtle_in_love_head   Muhahaha! And let's not forget the 20% increase to damage to your ambush from the talent Opportunity.

 1.18.0 - Rogues got a 7% increase to there run speed (A Skill learned at level 30). Which make you 7% faster in stealth. If you can get a pair of boots and put Minor Speed Enchant (gives you 8% increase speed) which makes you 15% faster now ( Holy Smolly!) compared to the last release.

Any Boot! (even grays or white boots) and save them! Why?
You can switch gear as long as you are not in combat.

You make a macro and when you press stealth, The macro will contain /Stealth and /equip name of boot, the macro will equip the boots with Minor run speed (you are 8% faster, Holly Smolly!)

You make another macro for Ambush. This macro will contain the the line /ambush and /equip the name of the boot you want to switch to (you higher dps boot), right before you get into ambush range you press the macro and it switches boots.

Make the same macro for vanish and be 8% faster (Vanish takes you out of combat) and get to your next target or get out of dodge. :)

or a couple post down, I put out a Stealth/vanish macro, so just add the line /equip (your name of boot with the run speed enchant)

This method will save a bit of gold so every time you upgrade boots you don't have to put minor speed enchant on them.

I don't like sinister strike unless i absolutely have to use it.

Main hand instant, off hand crippling

What I do- Ambush into gouge, pool a little energy and pirouette to the back side and backstab.
I now have 3-4 combo points.

They have more health than I like, Check to see if crippling up on mob, rupture and kite (while lol-ing) as soon as rupture runs it course, gouge, pirouette to backside and backstab. if their not dead you should have 2-3 combo points to eviscerate to finish em off or rupture again and keep lol-ing :) so much fun being a rogue.

while kiting, feel free to work on your range weapon skill :)

A rogues energy regeneration (Baseline) is 20 energy every 2 seconds so, going from 0 energy to 100 energy will take 10 seconds. (baseline no buffs)

Blade rush as it states lowers the time of those ticks equal to the amount of agility.

(If I read this correctly):

A rogue with 500 agility will lower the time between energy ticks by .5 seconds or (half a second).
Allowing the rogue to gain 20 energy every 1.5 seconds.

A rogue with 1000 agility will lower the time between energy ticks by a full second
Allowing the rogue to gain double the amount of energy within the same time period as the baseline amount.

Putting 2 points into Blade Rush only affects the attack speed element of the talent and not the energy tick regeneration.

Lifesteal is a simple enchant: get resisted or not, deal 30 dmg, heal 30 hp and create a 60 health difference.
 1. (can be resisted)
 2. Off hand has a higher miss rate than main hand (off hand has lower proc chance)
 3. Does not scale as you level, does NOT scale with any buffs
 4. There are no talents that benefit from this enchant

Agility enchant another simple enchant: 15 Agility= .5% crit, 15 attack power, adds armor and dodge.
 1. Can't be resisted
 2. Does not rely on off hand hit rating
 3. Does not scale with level but DOES scale with buffs
 4. Seal fate talent does benefit from having agility

Both enchants look like that cost about the same depending on server
