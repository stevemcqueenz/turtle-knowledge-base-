# ...of class fantasy & niche concepts (Rogue)

## Post 149749 by Unclecatjam — 2025-09-01T14:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149749#p149749 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So..
I wanted to start a *(hopefully civil)* discussion about your ideas, concepts and thoughts in regards to class fantasy or identity, especially the niche ones. Examples for that would be something like the outlaw rogue, as a swashbuckling, pistol wearing subclass or SoD's time twisting, temporal healer mage subclass or maybe even the survivalist, trap-throwing meele hunter subclass.

This is soley meant as a fun little discussion and exchange of ideas and not a suggestion for TWOW *(even though, some ideas might be actually cool to see coming to life)*.

I'd like to start with the rogue forum mostly because I've always thought the talent trees were lacking in the department of identity and even after outlaw made it to retail, it never hooked me the way I was hoping for.

 **Assassination:** Out of the three available talent trees assassination is the only one that, in my opinion, has a real tangible class identity as the sneaky, murderous and backstabbing assassin. There's not a lot I'd change class fantasy-wise, other than moving over a lot of the subtlety talents to assassination (like camouflage, improved ambush and similar, sneaky, backstabbing talents).
 ***Assassin***

 **Lore Implementation:** Not needed, it's an Assassin - Ravenholdt or maybe SI:7

 **Combat:** Combat always felt weird to me, mostly because of the word in itself. Combat, to me at least, just doesn't feel rogue-ish enough, unless it is meant in the swashbuckling way. *"Cheating"* in open combat by throwing dust in your enemies eyes or leaving them flat-footed through deceit and feints. The retail version, namely the outlaw talent tree always felt too cartoon-ish to me. While rolling dice and shooting a blunderbuss point-blank seemed cool, to me it felt half-hearted and sluggish *(or maybe I'm just too stupid to play the class..)*.
Having said that, I'd always wanted the dueling type of swashbuckler (similar to the DnD 5e subclass) - that excels at 1v1 by blinding, taunting and parrying their enemies or throwing oil-filled bottles on the ground to catch them off-guard or otherwise cheat their way to victory. There's a lot of talents or skills that already hint in that direction, like riposte, blind, vanish, cheap shot, kidney shot, feint, distract or kick *(a kick to the groin style)*.
 ***Swashbuckler***

 **Lore Implementation:** Well, easy to implement.. Pirates are already a thing: Bloodsail Buccaneers, Blackwater Raiders or anything Booty Bay.

 **Subtlety:** Now here comes the niche idea that's been haunting me for quite a while because subtlety to me is just a small aspect of assassination, at least from my point of view... therefore it could wander over to the assassination talent tree and cut out the rest, that doesn't fit.
Let's talk about throwing weapons, which never got a fair chance. Often you read about the concept of a ninja talent tree which of course, as a class fantasy, sounds really fun, but... I personally could never figure out a way to implement them lore-wise that felt fitting and true to warcrafts lore, without just being a weeaboos wet dream *(no shade - love me some weeb-dom)*. So I had an idea and it ties in with the darkmoon faire of all things - let me introduce: the Jester, but more about that in the Lore Implementation section.
Gameplay could revolve around throwing weapons, which of course would have to work like bows, guns or wands first and foremost. Daggers or other weapons, would be statsticks and only used similar to the hunter meele weapon: only meant to slow, cripple and trip anyone that comes near you, so you can create distance to throw your knives at them.
You could, if the assassination tree would be filled to the brim, even implement the poison aspect here, but that's up for debate. Next to Deadly Throw, skills or talents like Fan of Knives, Ricocheting Throw or Poison Dart could be implemented as either finishers or combo point generators. This is of course a very vague concept, but I always liked the idea of a fully ranged rogue talent tree and not the half-assed version that is the retail outlaw.
 ***Jester***

 **Lore Implementation:** I am anticipating that I will lose a lot of you with this one, if I didn't already with the Swashbuckler, but nevertheless it is a niche concept that I personally really like: The (Court-)Jester or the Darkmoon Faire Specialist. Why the darkmoon faire? Well, throwing knives seems very fitting also (extended) lore wise, it wouldn't be too far off. Silas Darkmoon, the leader of the Darkmoon Faire is according to the comic Legends a fair and passionate leader, that isn't afraid of criminal activities if it means to protect his found family of weirdos and outcasts. Considering the story of the comic legend implementing the jester could look something like this:

> The Darkmoon Faire started as a gathering of misfits, lead by Silas Darkmoon. During the events in Southshore, where strong-woman Kerri Hicks was accused of murdering Cedrick Fallrooks brother, Erik Fallrook, who in fact was murdered by Cedrick himself, Silas took matters into his own hands by not just freeing Hicks out of jail, but also burrying Cedrick Fallrock alive. From this moment forward Silas swore to protect not just his own people, but all the common folk from injustice and the meddling affairs of nobility. His new found resolution led to the founding of the underground organization commonly refered to as "The Pale Moon Vigilantes".

This is all very far fetched, the name just pulled out of my arse and there's a million different ways to implement them or somthing similar (and maybe it's not even relevant that it's called the jester) but I really wish there would be a fully ranged rogue talent tree - as I've said multiple times already!

Well, this is already long enough and that's where I want to close my ramblings.

I'm curious and would love to hear about your ideas, concepts or class fantasies in general that stuck with you over the years warcraft has entertained all of us *(sometimes more, sometimes less)*.

So long and tudelu,
