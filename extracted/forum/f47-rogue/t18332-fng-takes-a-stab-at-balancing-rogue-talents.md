# FNG takes a "stab" at balancing Rogue talents

## Post 125088 by Ergoeht — 2025-04-02T13:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125088#p125088 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey all, long time WoW player, new to Turtle-WoW, and relatively inexperienced rogue offering his opinion that nobody asked for. I've seen a lot of legitimate concerns about how Rogue talents have been built. Ok here goes:

 1) Swap positions of Improved Blade Tactics and Improved Backstab.

Justification: IBT strikes me as more sustained DPS (or evasion) and IB is bursty with combo point gen. They're in the wrong trees. Swapping them is pretty easy: same row, same point investment. Combat rogues that dip into Assassination and Assassination rogues that dip into Combat probably won't notice a difference, but Subtlety rogues that dip into either will find choices that fit the tree themes better.

 2) Move Taste for Blood into the Subtlety tree, replacing Irritating Agent (more on that below). New talent: "Poison Injector. Your poison damage is increased by 5/10/15% of your melee attack power." 3 Points.

Justification: I've constantly seen Rogues mention that poison damage does not scale very well, so this deep talent is to address that. 5/10/15% might be the wrong values, but it's the right concept. A single bleed-based talent in assassination seems out of place.

 3) Change Improved Ghostly Strike's effect to: "Your Ghostly Strike increases your chance to dodge by an additional 2/4/6%, and your party members' change to dodge by 2/4/6% for 7 seconds."

Justification: While I like the concept of the "support rogue", there shouldn't be talents for rogues that don't also help themselves. Also 3% is a pittance, considering GS's cooldown, a max of 6% party benefit seems reasonable. This might make them a little too dodgy in PvP though (I'm not much of a PvP'er).

 4) Change Smoke Bomb to either not affect ALL targets, or to be a throwable AOE effect.

Justification: Either a lazy QoL improvement, or a more controllable skill shot if you place it well. Both fix the problem of it currently being just a mass "slowww it down here boys" effect.

 5) Get rid of Irritating Agent (mentioned above) and replace it with Taste for Blood from the Assassination tree.

Justification: Subtlety has other bleed-boosting talents, and could really use a good source of damage increase for the  spec.

 6) Change Blinding Haze to: "Targets affected by your Distract are 1/2/3% more likely to be hit, and 1/2/3% more likely to be critically struck, for 5 seconds. This effect is guaranteed to be applied"

Justification: A small damage boost for openers, helping those who aren't hit-capped yet a bit more, and still giving a little to those who are hit capped. The loss of defensive benefit is already compensated for in the changes to Improved Ghostly Strike above.

 7) Add the following to Mark for Death: "For the duration, abilities that ordinarily require stealth may be used against the target. May only be used against targets at least 3 levels higher than you." Remove the combo point generation. For flavor, perhaps rename the talent to "Slay the Goliath".

This gives a few windows in long fights to reapply garotte, hit ambush a few times, and reapply distract. Since you'll likely be getting a ton of combo points from openers, there is no need for it to generate points itself. It's unclear in the current tooltip whether to rogue benefits from the 15% damage boost themselves, but if they do, I don't think it's unreasonable to have a juiced opener spam mode in their rotation once every 3 minutes. The "3+ levels higher" restriction is so that it isn't horribly oppressive in PvP, although it does give sub rogues an interesting anti-overleveled-ganker niche in world PvP, no?
