# Little suggestion regarding normalizing certain bonuses

## Post 94787 by Gantulga (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-05-13T20:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94787#p94787 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So right now we have multiple sources that boost certain attack skills but not others. It is very inconsistent and doesn't make much sense.

Lethality: boosts everything except Riposte.
Remorseless Attacks: doesn't work with Hemorrhage or Riposte.
Cold Blood: doesn't work with Ghostly Strike, Hemorrhage or Riposte.
Revealed Flaw (T3 bonus): doesn't work with Ghostly Strike, Ambush or Riposte.

Riposte is the main outlier which is sad since it's a very conditional skill, but it's inconsistent all around.

Wouldn't it be nice if all those bonuses were normalized to simply affect all instant physical damage abilities?
