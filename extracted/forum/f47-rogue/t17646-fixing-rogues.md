# Fixing Rogues

## Post 120417 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-19T16:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120417#p120417 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd like to preface this with the fact that I do not play rogue, and I haven't studied the class much beyond other "Rogue bad waahhh" posts and all I know is that they're underperforming and why that is. No we're not talkin' about PvP here. I'd just like to bring more attention to the PvE side of things and help y'all get the publicity you need there. Here's the deal.

Combat and Assass are more or less pure DPS specs with practically zero utility. What special things they do have can be done by other classes. Warriors and shamans have low risk interrupts to replace kick, mages have polymorphs and warlocks have banishes to replace sap, sunder armor, fearie fire and certain weapons trump expose armor, you get the idea. What's rogue's counter?

A support tree that requires you dump all your talent points into it to fill the lack of support tools. This same spec deals the same amount of damage as tanks. Did I mention you have to spec into it?

You get the idea. Even when the devs try to deviate and make rogue fill a different niche it loses out on so much trying to do that. The solution(s)?

Make assass and combat do better ST damage. AOE is not necessary if they can fill the niche of being top performers in single target centric fights, which is pretty much all of them in raiding. Putting rogue in the upper echelon of this niche will give them, well- a niche... Which they need. Badly.

Bake some damage stuff into subtlety talents to make it so you're not poking at patchwerk with destructive power comparable to a gnome in pillowy battle armor wearing squeaky toy brass knuckles.

Thanks for coming to my TED talk. 5000 hours baby.

I give my 2 cents too about this.
Rogues have many special 'quirks' but they translate badly to raiding:
 * stealth and stuns don't really help
 * venom is a weird sharpening stone at OH (MH is supposed to have WF)
 * small gimmicks like trap disarm, misdirection or expose armor help but they are not consistently good enough to be called a utility kit
 * at the end of the day combat rogues are single-target fury warriors who refuse to tank

How I'd change it:
Combat and subtlety are easy. All combat needs is a number tweak with some extra caution. PVP still exists in this server, you have to figure out how to raise combat's damage profile without making a cancer in PVP. Note, they should be at least on par with fury warriors who among otherscan spam execute at low enemy health.
Overall raise of rogue DPS might be too toxic for PVP, giving a kind of execute to rogues might be toxic, too. They need an increase in the damage profile but this increase has to be done very cleverly.
Subtlety is easy too, since we know what TWOW Team wants to achieve.
But.
Reagents are for rare and situational abilities. I refuse to believe that a standard rotation should force you to use reagents.
Damage is also way too bad. Hybrids show us that in this server having utility doesn't have a big tax. Sub should perform below combat but should way outdo tanks.

Assassination is the interesting tree. Rogues go there to buy some talents but I refuse to believe that assassination builds, mostly relying on this tree actually exist. Maybe some theorycrafters here and there but that's all.
In my eyes, assassination is the only remaining tree in the server which doesn't provide a clear picture of what it does. Sure, you get some crit and venom synergies, cool. But I've yet to see assassination rogues in raid, read about assassination-specific rotation etc.
They need an identity first.
I'd go the middle way and make them a half-utility tree. More damage than subs. Utility coming from unique venom types - some could be equally impactful as a warlock curse, making raids happy if they have 1-2 assassination rogues.
