Atlas Loot addon is a great place to start and you will be looking for gear that has:

 1. Agility
 2. Crit
 3. Haste
 4. Hit Rating ( you only need 4-5%, you are doing dungeons not raiding)
Oh. and may be a wee bit of stam (lol! Who am I kidding, screw the stam and more crit plzzzz!) Muhahaha

The [Stormshroud Set](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?itemset=142%5B/url) is awesome for Assassination/Dagger rogues.
and since you said you were grinding gold you might just want to pick up
[Nightslayer Bracers](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=16825%5B/url)

[Mongoose Boots](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18506%5B/url) Another good AH buy

Weapons double [Julie's Dagger](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6660%5B/url) is great but head to Dire Maul and get [Distracting Dagger](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18392%5B/url) for off hand

Another great drop from Dire Maul is [Mugger's Belt](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18505%5B/url)

Dire Maul: [Tarnished Elven Ring](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18500%5B/url)

These are just a few

Regarding Haste: I have 1.3 Daggers and with haste on gear. speed is at 1.25
With Slice and dice running weapon speed is at .97 and when the [Stormshroud Set](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?itemset=142%5B/url) procs, my dagger speed is at .87

Like all stats there are diminishing returns

Have fun murdering stuff
