# Thoughts on Going Assassination at Level 40

## Post 158547 by Sslushee — 2025-11-06T02:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158547#p158547 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey folks — just wanted to drop a quick note for anyone thinking about going Assassination around level 40.

I’ve been leveling daggers all the way up, with a short detour into Combat Swords. For context, I’ve been playing WoW for about 20 years now — my first toon was a rogue back in 2005 — so I’ve been around the block with this class.

That said, I’m only level 40 right now, so this post is focused entirely on leveling performance, not endgame.

![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) What I’ve Found So Far

 **Combat (Swords):**
Easily the weakest option I’ve tried — and it’s not even close. Leveling as Combat felt rough enough that I swapped back to daggers after just a few hours. It’s nowhere near the powerhouse it was in vanilla.
Rogues themselves are strong… but this spec just doesn’t hold up while leveling.

 **Daggers (Sub/Combat Mix)**
This setup was decent, but not great. My basic flow was:

Ambush → Gouge → Backstab → Eviscerate

If Ambush or Backstab didn’t crit, the fight dragged on forever and usually ended with me bandaging. It worked, but felt inconsistent — solid burst, weak sustain.

 **Assassination** (Level 40 Respec)
When I hit 40, I was on the fence about switching — I thought I’d really miss Precision. Took the risk anyway, and man… I’m so glad I did.

Mobs are melting. I don’t miss Precision at all.

This build isn’t crit-dependent in the slightest — I even skipped Remorseless Attacks and have only two points in Seal Fate, which is working fine since the whole build revolves around poisons instead of crits.

Here’s the gist:

Grab all the poison talents + Efficient Poisons

Use two daggers (avoid swords, maces, axes). Faster = more poison procs.

Rotation looks like:

Cheap Shot → Envenom → Slice & Dice (off the Ruthlessness point) → Noxious Assault twice → Dead

Result? I’m killing mobs about 75% faster than I was with my Ambush/Backstab build. I’m also topping DPS meters in every dungeon since the respec — before this, I was usually mid-pack.

Build at 40:
![👉](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f449.svg) [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?po ... gDYZIQQB--](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=AoYCIgDYZIQQB--)

If you’re on the fence about trying Assassination at 40 — do it. You won’t regret it.

Note: After extensive target dummy testing, double Instant poison (at least at 40) trumps both instant/deadly and deadly/deadly. Instant poison is hitting so fast that the 5 stack cap on deadly ends up hindering your dps.

Also, I'll challenge all of you to drop 5 points in Seal Fate, and pickup Duel Wield specialization instead (31/20/0).
