Bind backstab to mouse wheel up and down and keep at it. Your rotation should be: pick pocket, bs, bs, bs, bs

Rush to Blade Flurry at 32:
<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue/2-0X5C0U1>

Pick up all backstab talents after (Opportunity, Lethality)
<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue/2U30U-0X5C0U1-5>

Dual Wield Specialization after and feel free to do whatever at that point.

Most controversial 5/5 Lightning Reflexes over imp gouge and imp SS, I just dont like imp gouge, it's a stupid talent imo for pve. Yes, you get 1 more bandage tick in but with 5% more dodge you straight up dont take that damage in the first place and it's just always up 100% of the time, not in some niche cases where you need 1 bandage tick. Feel free to disagree.

Edit: Drop improved eviscerate, use combo points on slice and dice and move on to the next mob or dont use em at all and just finish a mob with another backstab. Pick up Precision asap, a major part of your damage between backstabs still comes from regular white hits. +5% hit is a huge damage boost, especially for your offhand.
