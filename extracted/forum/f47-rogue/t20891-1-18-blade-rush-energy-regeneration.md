Blade Rush: Increases your melee attack speed by [2/5]% and reduces the time between energy regeneration ticks
by the amount equal to your agility. More effective than Blade Rush one.

A rogues energy regeneration (Baseline) is 20 energy every 2 seconds so, going from 0 energy to 100 energy will take 10 seconds. (baseline no buffs)

Blade rush as it states lowers the time of those ticks equal to the amount of agility.

(If I read this correctly): ( best Guess)

 1 agi = 1ms makes the most sense
anything smaller just wouldn't be worth putting points into. IMHO

From talent " and reduces the time between energy regeneration ticks
by the amount equal to your agility."

A rogue with 500 agility will lower the time between energy ticks by .5 seconds or (half a second).
Allowing the rogue to gain 20 energy every 1.5 seconds.

A rogue with 1000 agility will lower the time between energy ticks by a full second
Allowing the rogue to gain double the amount of energy within the same time period as the baseline amount.

Putting 2 points into Blade Rush only affects the attack speed element of the talent and not the energy tick regeneration.

At a certain point there will be diminishing returns, there is only so much agility you can pile on and ( for combat Daggers) Going over crit cap has to be monitored more closely than combat (unless your are running close to BIS gear)

There will be at times, adding attack power will be more beneficial than agility based on gear level.

IMHO (in my humble opinion) It's a must have talent for combat, regardless if you go swords, maces or daggers.( Out side of the Talent Adrenaline Rush ) it's the only way (not including gear) to get more energy regeneration back which = a smoother rotation which = more damage.

JMHO but I think that diminishing returns are already baked into it in the way of not wanting to be or go over crit cap.
once you start getting above level 50, dungeon gear blues will start dropping for rogues that have a crit stat on it and on top of that are the talents that boost our crit.

ie Dagger rogues will get +5% crit putting 2 points into Close Quarter Combat, and another 5% crit for the talent Malice. If you went Backstab combat rogue you would then get another 30% increase to crit from Improved Backstab, and we haven't even started talking about agility on gear

If I remember right 1% crit is = to 29 agility, so 10% crit from just those 2 talents would = 290 agility

The gear I'm wearing now is the [Stormshroud Armor](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?itemset=142%5B/url) which has 4% crit total on the entire set.

If you look at [Shadowcraft Armor](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?itemset=184%5B/url) it comes with 157 agility which = 5.4% crit

And we haven't even started adding other gear like rings, neck, enchants.
This addon [Better Character Stats](https://github.com/pepopo978/BetterCharacterStats%5B/url) does have your boss crit cap already figured out based on your hit rating, expertise and other factors.

and here is the formula if you wana play around with it.

 100% - (base miss % - your hit %) - (base dodge %) - (base parry %) - (glancing blow %)
This only factors a 300 weapon skill, a higher weapon skill and the glancing blow % would change

I'm not sure if anyone can hit the crit hard cap on turtle wow. I do know at level 57 and the gear I had running Combat daggers at the time (Assassination now since 1.18) I was at my crit cap of 28%,( without buffs) If I was to get double agility enchants for my weapons it would have put me over then factor in + Kings buff + mark of the wild.

it is a balancing act with all the stats the higher your level and better your gear gets.
