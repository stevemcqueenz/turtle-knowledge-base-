# Combo Points

## Post 107026 by SSJGary — 2024-11-08T04:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107026#p107026 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Is anyone elses combo point not carrying over to a new target? It seems my combo points disappear after switching targets.

## Post 107038 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-08T07:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107038#p107038 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Combo points are kept on Target A when you switch to Target B, but unlike vanilla, when you switch back to Target A, the points are no longer lost.  You cannot transfer your Combo Points to Target B, and if you apply a Combo Point to Target B, your points on Target A will be lost.
