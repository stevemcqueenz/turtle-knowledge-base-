# Sinister strike/Auto attack macro

## Post 55660 by Psytr0n — 2023-08-03T13:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55660#p55660 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

/cast !Attack
/cast Sinister Strike(Rank **?**)

 --------------------------------------------------------------------

Change the **?** to the rank of Sinister strike you have eg.

/cast !Attack
/cast Sinister Strike(Rank 3)
