# Envenom Raiding could use some love

## Post 122847 by Bigbosc — 2025-03-18T04:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122847#p122847 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have been raiding on every spec rogues can field for the last few months, and testing thoroughly on end game content.  I have near bis for almost every set up, and have been clearing the end game for around 2 years now.

With that experience I have come to see that Envenom builds are not quite up to snuff with Combat builds, and both can certainly field Improve Expose Armor, which is our best buff, so there isn't much reason to play Assassin over Combat.

With the great change to poisons to have a scaling component, the gap between the two shrunk, Assassin got much better!  Awesome change, and another buff was given to Relentless Strikes to add a stacking finisher move damage buff.

Envenom spec (especially using backstab), can't really use Rupture or Eviscerate often.  I find the best DPS comes from 4 or 5 point Envenoms and 4 or 5 point Slice and Dices, which doesn't leave much room to push Rupture or Evsicerate almost at all, even during long fights.

This is a shame because the Rupture talents are really cool (taste for blood, serrated blades), and the change to Relentless Strikes is really cool too.  But if you can't really afford to push Rupture much at all, you get no benefit from this damage effect, and can barely get value from taste for blood or serrated blades rupture boost.

This is less of a problem if you are using sinister strike with your envenom build, but it still is a problem.  Its even worse of a problem if you are using Expose Armor for your raid benefit, you then simply can't ever push a damaging finishing move.  So all those cool talents are useless, and the relentless strikes buff doesn't affect you at all.

So I suggest we make a change to the Assassination talent tree.  Efficient Poisons says it reduces your chance for a poison resist, and gives a chance to not lose a charge.  A bit of an underwhelming talent, I suggest giving the resist part of this talent to Vile Poisons, just add it on, and give the charge part to Improved Poisons.  This is nice as the chance to not be resisted makes sense if your poisons are VILE!  It also has good synergy, if you take improved poisons your poisons trigger more often, and eat up charges faster, so making that talent also improve charge use is nice.

Next I would add a new talent where efficient poisons was "Singular Focus : Whenever you use a finishing move on a target afflicted by Envenom or Slice and Dice, you have a 33/66/100% chance to refresh the duration of those abilities.".

TL;DR  Assassination lags behind combat in DPS especially in cleave, and lags far behind Sub in terms of what it brings to a raid.  Give it some more fun buffs that help it in longer single target fights.  Adding a way to refresh the duration of Slice and Dice and Envenom, will free us up to be able to press expose armor, and damaging finishing moves more often.
