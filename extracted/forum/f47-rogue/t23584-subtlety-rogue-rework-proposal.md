# Subtlety Rogue rework proposal

## Post 165103 by Lomikare1 — 2026-01-14T06:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165103#p165103 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**Greetings to all players and developers of this amazing game!**

I’ve put together a proposal for changes to the Subtlety Rogue spec that puts a stronger emphasis on stealth‑oriented gameplay. Subtlety has always been the spec most closely tied to stealth, ambushes, and powerful opening moves, and I believe it’s a shame that the current talent setup doesn’t fully reflect that fantasy.

Using a tool that allows custom talent‑tree creation, I built a reworked Subtlety talent tree. I then reached out to several experienced Rogue players, including dedicated Subtlety mains, and refined the proposal based on their feedback. The result should be fairly polished — but if you notice anything that could be improved, I’d really appreciate your feedback! O:)

 **LIST OF INDIVIDUAL TALENT CHANGES**
 **1. ROW**
Improved gouge: the talent no longer increases the duration of Gouge, but instead reduces its Energy cost. This change was made to improve its synergy with other talents in the Subtlety.

Camuflage:  now has 3 talent points instead of 5. The effect stays mostly the same. The difference is a 1‑second reduced cooldown on stealth abilities, so overall it’s a buff. The increased movement speed in Stealth now has a much stronger impact in PvE encounters as well.

 **2. ROW**
Improved ambush:  its position was moved slightly to the right for visual clarity. The effect remains unchanged.

Blackjack: moved from the 4th row to the 2nd row, making it more accessible early while leveling and adding more diversity in PvP.

 **3. ROW**
Gouging shadows (NEW): Gouge is gaining a new effect that gives Subtlety Rogues better access to stealth and stronger opening moves. The cooldown on Gouge is intentionally increased — partly for PvP balance, and partly to make the overall rotation feel a bit less frantic.
Note: For PvP, Cheap Shot would need a small rework — specifically adding a cooldown. Having a free Cheap Shot every 20 seconds could break PvP. Ideally, Cheap Shot should share a similar cooldown to Kidney Shot.

Dirty deeds:  moved from the 5th row to the 3rd row, placing it alongside Initiative. It now has 3 ranks and reduces the cost of Garrote and Ambush (but not Cheap Shot). Cheap Shot is already strong in PvP.

Initiative: no longer grants a bonus combo point on Cheap Shot openers. As mentioned earlier, Cheap Shot is already very strong in PvP.

Smoke Bomb: removed. It was a cool idea, but far too difficult to implement properly in both PvP and PvE. If it absolutely needs to stay in some form, it should only exist as a class‑specific consumable (similar to Rogue Thistle Tea, Blinding Powder, etc.).

 **4. ROW**
Ellusivnes: moved to the 4th row and now has 3 ranks. It’s buffed: with 3 points invested, it reduces the cooldown of Blind and Vanish to a total of 90 seconds. This allows Vanish and Blind to be used twice (or three times with Preparation) per boss encounter. Elusiveness becomes even stronger thanks to Stealth Surge.

 **5. ROW**
Stealt surge (NEW): new talent that reinforces the subclass fantasy of a stealth killer and increases the importance of Vanish and Elusiveness.

 **6. ROW**
Honor Among Thieves: swapped position but remains in the same row for cosmetic reasons. It’s buffed, as it was previously the weakest energy generator. It can now also be triggered by the rogue himself.

Deadlines (NEW): It increases attack power, which is the best stat for Subtlety rogues. This is why it’s placed on the same row as Tricks of the Trade. Sub rogues now have to choose whether they want to be more supportive or focus on personal damage.
