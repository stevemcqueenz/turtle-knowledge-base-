Im only having limited experience as my rogue is still levelling. But here is my best guess whats happening and how you can deal with it.

Turtle wow serverticks are luch much faster than they were in classic wow (0.04seconds vs 0.4seconds)

That means in classic, even if the mob was technically so close he could spot you, foe up to 0.4 seconds he wouldnt yet react. On turtlewow you arent afforded that luxury. Similarly, If a mob notices you close but hasnt qute spotted you yet, he turn for you and will attack you if you dont back away quickly. This period of time seems to be much shorter too.

How do you stealth efficiently with this knowledge?
 1. Never stealth close to a mobs face. Their stealth detection facing forward is stronger than you expect because you have no grace period
Approaching from behind yields better results
 2. Use distract against mobs that might spin while you approach them
 3. Dont dick around in melee range. Shiftloot pickpoket and opener. Dont give the mob time to react.
 4. Never run trough the hitbox of a mob. No amount of stealth level hides you if you stand inside a mob
 5. Some mobs have very high stealth detection. Learn to avoid them. Typically packdogs/hyenas, mobs that dont rely on vision such as bats or mobs with tracker in their name have drastically increased stealth perception to the point where stealth can be entirely useless.
