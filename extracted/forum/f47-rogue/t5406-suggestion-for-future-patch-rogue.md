Not sure if there are any plans already stated for rogue development but I would love to see rogues get some form of AOE ability.

Suggestion:

Fan of Knifes: Finisher that does AOE damage based on combo point

Improved Blade Flurry (ver 1): Increase the amount of additional opponents hit for duration of buff. IE 3 or more extra targets hit.

Improved Blade Flurry (ver 2): Able to cleave around you for the duration at a slight reduction in damage.
