# Analysis on why Alliance loses 90% of the time.

## Post 136534 by Ellifta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-21T15:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136534#p136534 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It wouldn’t be a hot take to say Horde wins the majority of BG matches on this server. When playing on WSG day followed by AB day, as a holy paladin in Kara40 gear I won 7 matches out of 52 I played. Everyone’s welcome to say that I must be really bad at the game and it must be me causing all that, and I’ll gladly take the blame. I got 217k honor last week and that put me at standing 38 for Alliance, which might be interesting for Horde players who are trying to rank up to see how wildly different honor to standing ratio is for the other side.

People tend to open threads in this forum when they notice one specific problem that leads to massive Horde winrates, and are quickly shut down by whataboutism (Example: I got one shot by shaman/ But what about druid T3.5 gear???) and people who don’t know how to refute points or argue. It doesn’t help that Alliance players open these threads without an intention to argue with, but to vent their frustrations, so I decided to make a neutral toned and objective analysis as to why Alliance is losing so often in BGs, from the perspective of a healer in BGs who only plays soloque.

 **1- Paladin vs Shaman Core Design**
The biggest reason I can think of is how bad the average paladin player is and how shaman counters paladins on top of that. For starters, purely from a design perspective a shaman counters a paladin. Purgeable seals and purgeable buffs including freedom give shaman a massive edge over paladin who can’t afford to auto attack shaman’s totems with a slow two handed weapon. Secondly, the counter to a shaman are kicks and silences. Paladins have stuns, but the average paladin player is really bad and only uses HoJ as an opener to start doing their PvE rotation on the shaman while the shaman will just facetank the initial 6 seconds and then wreck the paladin. Because the majority of Alliance plays paladin, on the bigger picture you’re looking at shamans hardcasting a lot of the time because paladins don’t have kicks and that’s what people are playing. It’s actually harmful that people are playing paladin so much, the situation wouldn’t be as bad if Alliance had more casters and rogues. In terms of bursty DPS niche, both elemental and enhancement are far more bursty than AP or SP ret.

 **2- 17 Enhancement Build**
The elephant in the room is the uncounterable 17 enhancement and rest on elemental shaman build which is what a pre-CC2 shockadin could dream of being. This specific build gives shamans massive armour from their gear and their shield and dodge while they’re still able to do 3.5k in one global with guaranteed crits. Anyone with a shield is simply less prone to being critically struck, these shamans are still able to heal decently because they have SP gear. So you end up with a build that is really tanky, has a shield, deals massive ranged damage and can heal. Alliance has no counterpart or counterplay to this other than watching their mortal strikes hit for 450. I know people like to complain about getting one shot by soulfires and whatnot, but to me this is the only objectively overpowered spec in PvP right now. Holy paladin would be a good second contestor, but purely because no one plays healer on Alliance side this goes unnoticed. Holy paladin is incredibly tanky and has insane single target healing, but because it lacks any offensive abilities at all unlike 17 enhancement build, people don’t play it. This build alone accounts for the vast majority of Alliance players quitting BGs due to how annoying it is, which leads us to my second point.

 **3- Gear and Player Discrepancy**
Since Alliance loses the majority of the battles, people who PvP on Alliance side do it mostly to rank up to their desired rank and stop playing. They aren't exactly enjoying the game, but grinding their teeth until a desired rank. They don’t stick around and are quickly replaced by people in greens. I’ve seen people as low as 2.3k HP with kings on Alliance side, people with empty ring slots, people with missing helmet and shoulders, people in level 40 spirit AH gear… In any match, a sizable portion of Alliance players will be walking health packs for Horde players. This wouldn’t be so bad if 50% of Alliance weren’t retribution paladins. A less geared healer is infinitely more useful than a less geared melee DPS. A less geared caster is infinitely more useful than a less geared melee DPS. But as everyone probably notices, Alliance only plays ret paladin. Since good players don’t really stick around, you’ll be stuck healing these people who will just quit after getting one tapped. It’s bizarre to me that as a class that can do 2.7k heals every 1.5 second, I can’t keep some of these people alive against one shaman.

 **4- Melee vs Caster Damage**
Post CC2, it’s safe to say that a lot of casters got quite bursty while melee classes except enhancement shaman and melee hunter didn’t receive any buffs to compensate. When we look at it from the bigger perspective again, Alliance has a lot of melee players in the form of retribution paladins while Horde has a lot of caster players in the form of elemental shamans. Melee works great against clothies, when I equip my Kara40 weapon on my retribution spec (I very rarely play this) I can one-shot low geared horde casters and do serious damage to even high geared casters. However, the fundamental problem is never the priests, warlocks or mages. It’s always this particular 17 enhancement build which reduces my crusader strike damage to 550 on a non-crit and 1000 on a crit. Suddenly as a melee DPS I’m against a caster that deals me 700 in return from their shield against my 550, then deals 3.5k damage to me. I’m literally killing myself playing melee against this particular build, the water shield or whatever it is will wear me down from my own auto attacks. Caster DPS ignores all armour at the cost of being soakable by potions, but guess what, shamans can purge your nature protection potions. In the end, you are in an extremely unfavorable matchup against a shaman as melee, which sadly the majority of Alliance is.

 **What is the Solution?**

There’s unfortunately no short term solution to this extreme discrepancy. I don’t think the recently announced flat damage reduction or healing reduction will solve any of these issues. Things need to be done for the currently low-geared Alliance players to rank up and enjoy the game so hopefully there’s an equally skilled and geared playerbase on both sides in the future. Several things come to mind

 **A) Nerf the 17 enhancement build -** This is a no brainer, it’s equally cancerous as shockadin. No caster should be able to do 3.5k damage in one global and have 6k armour + dodge + shield. Moving these talents past the 20 point in enhancement tree can fix this problem to a degree. Shamans will quickly point out to a similar build Paladins can use by taking points in holy and prot tree, but this build has nowhere the same offensive abilities as an elemental shaman does. All you’re doing is trading healing for 10% DR, which can be desired or not, this doesn’t give you any offensive abilities.

 **B) Make Paladin seals undispellable - No brainer, the equivalent of this would be paladin cleaving all shaman totems every GCD.**

 **Unfortunately while I’m fairly confident on things I’ve said until this point, I’m not about the following.**

 **C) Give paladins a seal that ignores armour but deals X% less damage.**
A seal that can ignore armour by turning all damage dealt to holy damage but reducing overall DPS would work and give the equivalent of enhancement shaman capabilities to retribution paladin. To ensure this doesn’t disrupt PvE balance, make it so that the X number is high enough that SoC or SoR deal more in their corresponding gear against targets with 2k or less armour. Ideally this seal should deal more damage to targets above a certain armor threshold but less against targets with less armour such as casters compared to SoR or SoC.

 **D) Buff direct combat abilities of warriors, feral druid and rogues so that people are less likely to flock on Paladin or Shaman classes.**
I think people play paladin because playing warrior is kind of a suicide and other classes don’t seem interesting enough. Instead of tinkering with paladin or shaman, perhaps buffing others to make them interesting could be a choice, but this has the obvious problem of powercreep.

 **Possible criticism that I foresee:**
You can argue that in organized play, paladins are stronger due to ability of chaining stuns and ranged gouges. This is true. However, majority of BGs are random people queueing for a few games and logging off. Competitive, serious games do not represent the majority of the gameplay and in those situations bans and restrictions can be manually placed. Yes, Alliance sucks and rarely wins outside of premades, doesn't mean we should let them suffer.
