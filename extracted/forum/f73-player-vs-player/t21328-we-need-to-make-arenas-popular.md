How to actually fix BGs and Arenas:

 1: Normalize levels and stats:
Everyone is boosted to lvl 60
Everyone is given normalized gear for their class.
Everyone has a choice between all class weapons with normalized stats for their class.
A selection of core, standardized trinkets.

 2: Pool all three servers into a single matchmaking pool
This increases the matchmaking population.
This allows for cross server interaction
This allows for leaderboards and ranks not limited by server selection

 3: This kills twinking & T3.5 complaints:
With stats normalized, no one have an advantage
With stats normalized everyone no matter their server is equal. Grind time and server age doesn't matter.

 4: Incentives to encourage pvp engagement:
Special ranks and titles
Special cosmetics for players and guilds
Earn rested exp while playing bgs and arenas
