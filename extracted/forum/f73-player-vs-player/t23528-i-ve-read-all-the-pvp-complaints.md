# I’ve Read All the PvP Complaints...

## Post 164676 by Bau — 2026-01-08T12:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164676#p164676 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Dear PvP friends of Turtle WoW, this message is for everyone who enjoys PvP, whether you play it seriously or just for fun and not for competition.
I have read many topics that mostly reflect individual opinions, and I have to disagree with them because they often lack fairness. Since the talent and skill update patch, every topic seems to talk about the same things over and over again. One player says that all classes are too strong except hunters.
Another says that every other class is ridiculously overpowered and that paladins deserve more attention. Another claims that priests have no space to play because all the other classes can do everything better. And so on, you can easily imagine the rest.

Then there are people who try to make class rankings that are sometimes mostly fair, but they still end up denigrating the classes they clearly do not play themselves.
I am not trying to be the voice of truth or someone who must be right, but take a moment to think about this. It is useless to throw dirt on every class except the one you play.
A more honest approach would be to admit that the patch, which was meant to improve PvE and actually did so, has had too much impact on PvP balance, which was already fragile before.
Unfortunately, when patches are designed to balance damage, healing, skills and mechanics for PvE, those changes inevitably spill over into PvP.
Like many of you, I believe it would have been better to design PvP-focused changes that then affect PvE, because for PvE players the difference would be minimal.

Why do I say that it would not change much? It is simple. If you are like me and played the original vanilla before private servers even existed, you know exactly what I mean. PvE has always been a complex strategy of numbers, consumables, calls and rotations.
No matter how much damage your class did, whether too little or too much, almost no one ever complained that a boss was too hard and needed to be nerfed, except in very rare or specific cases.

In PvP, however, the experience is completely different. Those who played old vanilla know that even if it was poorly balanced, players always found their own way to play against other classes. I am not saying that if you are good you automatically win, but I am saying that in some ways it was better when it was worse. Complaints existed back then too, but they were far more constructive. Now I mostly see people saying only that other classes are stronger than theirs.

Saying things like that leads nowhere if we all apply the same reasoning to every class.
In the end there are only two possibilities.
Either we accept that the classes are fine as they are and end the discussion, or we admit that all classes, and this is my personal opinion, have been overbuffed.
I would rather go back than continue down this path. At least before I knew what kind of game I was playing. With the current patch, I honestly do not know if this is a poorly functioning TBC or a damaged version of vanilla plus.
