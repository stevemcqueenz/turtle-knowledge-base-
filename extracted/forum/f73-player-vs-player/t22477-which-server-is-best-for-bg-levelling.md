# Which server is best for BG levelling ?

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156187#p156187 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello, I'm interested in levelling whilst doing BGs, including arenas. Basically I want to always be in a BG queue while I level, and join whenever they pop, questing only during the downtime ; however, ideally, I'd like questing to be the smaller part of my XP: the more BGs the merrier. If you're familiar with that, I'm thinking a similar experience as when Tel'Abim opened : I reached rank 4 before level 10 because arenas were constantly popping, and that was AWESOME !!

I'm especially interested in a server than doesn't have too much twinking, as they immediately defeat my interest in BGing at that bracket. I would much rather fight, and cooperate with, other players that are, like me, simply BGing to level. Of course, running into a twink every now and then is fine, but for instance, it's my understanding that the 10-19 bracket of Nordanaar is infested with twinks, which simply isn't my cup of tea.

I played for about an hour on both Ambershire and Tel'Abim and did not see a single BG start for any level bracket. Have announcements for BGs been removed, was I unlucky, or do the English-language servers simply not offer the possibility to level through PvP ?

I also speak Spanish so the SEA servers are an option, if they would fit that bill ! :)

Thanks for reading !
