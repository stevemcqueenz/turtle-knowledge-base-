# 30% Dampening on Casters is Well Deserved

## Post 174184 by Ellifta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-04-06T12:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174184#p174184 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

For the entire duration of 1.18, the outcome of a BG could be more or less guessed based on how many casters one team had versus the another, assuming gear was mostly equal. Warlocks, elemental shamans and scythe mages were blasting people from full HP to 0. People were joking about apologizing in advance if you joined a BG as a warrior.

If anything, in an ideal PvP setting physical damage should have 0 dampening except for rogues. The way this dampening was implemented was bad, it should have been announced by developers and made explicit instead of letting the community figure out the numbers, but the number itself is actually good.
