Please understand. If you don't pvp at all or very little you should decay overtime back to 0% progress of your current rank. But why in the HECK if I max out my honor gain to 20k and continue to pvp should my progress decay? I literally lost 2 days of pvping to a 5 minute server reset. For what reason?  To waste time? To make you grind out that next rank by the end of the reset? For the lols? It feels really bad to have all that time wasted it's why I stopped ranking in the first place.  unhappy_turtle
