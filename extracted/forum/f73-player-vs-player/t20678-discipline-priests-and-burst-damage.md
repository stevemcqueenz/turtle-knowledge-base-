# Discipline Priests and Burst Damage

## Post 143148 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-04T20:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143148#p143148 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Priests have traditionally always added moderate offensive support. In arenas and battlegrounds they could throw out instant casts on the move and, if they weren’t healing, they could still pressure with Smite, DoTs or Mana Burn. Leaving a priest unchecked was always a mistake because they would slowly turn the fight in their team’s favor. The twist was that priests are the least mobile class. Once a melee was on you, you were stuck unless your team peeled.

On Turtle WoW, healing priests now deal burst damage that rivals or even exceeds pure DPS specs. This comes mainly from Searing Light giving instant-cast Smites on critical hits. You often see a Smite cast followed by an instant Smite, both critting, which can delete an opponent in seconds.

No other healing class comes close to this level of damage. This playstyle always, always trumps traditional pressure like Mana Burn. At the start of my PvP journey against priests I thought, “Are you really going to damage me with Smites while I mana burn you?”

Guess what? Deleting an entire health pool beats removing small amounts of mana with a three second cast time. That spell is no longer even accessible in the Discipline tree for the 2.5 second reduction, making Mana Burn a niche option compared to just blowing someone up.

I've also started abusing rank 1 holy novas when getting targetted by melee classes to fish for instant proc smites, which usually generates a considerable amount of instant smites, letting me kill melee classes with ease. It feels very wrong to do this.

Instead of pure burst power, I’d rather see more skillful tools added back to the priest arsenal:

 * Add a self-damage spell like Shadow Word: Death to help avoid CCs (either introduce a new spell or revamp Mind Spike by letting it have this mechanic + reduce its CD to 10 sec)
 * Return Improved Mana Burn to the Discipline tree for strategic play
 * Improve mana management, because priests spend 90% of their playtime drinking, which is ridiculous

 * Making mana burn viable as a pressure tool
