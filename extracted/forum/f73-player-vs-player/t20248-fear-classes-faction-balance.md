# Fear Classes & Faction Balance

## Post 138758 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-11T16:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138758#p138758 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’ve been playing Alliance priest on Turtle WoW for a while now, and I’ve also played both Vanilla and TBC on retail from way before. I’ve reached a point where I really want to open up a proper discussion about fear-based classes and how the Horde racial Will of the Forsaken (WOTF) interacts with them.

 **Core issue**

If you're a priest, warlock, or even a warrior relying on fear, playing against Undead Horde players just feels off. Your primary control ability is often rendered useless on a huge portion of the enemy faction. Fear, Mind Control, Howl of Terror, Seduction, Psychic Scream, they’re all shut down instantly by one racial.

WOTF is not just strong. It’s everywhere. In most BGs, it feels like half or more of the Horde team are Undead. The odds that your fear will be ignored are incredibly high. Even Undead hunters now get WOTF, which is worrisome considering they’re already hard to pin down.

 **Racial comparison**

I understand there are strong racials on Alliance too, like Stoneform and Escape Artist. But there’s a key difference:

- **Stoneform**
breaks Blind and bleeds, but rogues still have tools like Vanish, Gouge, and Kidney. You counter one ability, not their entire kit.

 **Edit 250915:** Stoneform no longer removes blind.*

- **Escape Artist**
is great against snares, but mages have more than just Frost Nova. They have Blink, Polymorph, roots, slows, and kiting tools.

WOTF, on the other hand, deletes the central mechanic of fear classes. It’s not situational. It’s a hard, reactive counter with no cost or downside.

 **Fear Ward is baseline**

Previously, Fear Ward was one of the few tools Alliance priests had to counter WOTF and give their team a shot. Now that it’s baseline, even Undead priests have it. That means a class that already has WOTF now also gets to shut down your fears on others too.

A fear break, a fear immunity, and the ability to deny your fears, that is too much on one character.

 **Perspective from the other side**

What makes this feel worse is knowing that Horde fear-based classes get to use their abilities normally. If you’re an Undead warlock or priest, you don’t have to worry about your fear being broken by a racial every other fight. As an Alliance player, I often don’t even cast Psychic Scream unless I’ve already seen the racial used or know it’s on cooldown.

Every once in a while, I face a Troll or Orc rogue and they actually stay feared. It’s such a strange feeling because it reminds me how these abilities are supposed to work. But those cases are rare.

 **Proposed solutions**

I’m not asking for a nerf out of frustration. I genuinely think there are ways to keep the spirit of WOTF without making fear classes feel worthless:

- **Make WOTF proactive**

Instead of breaking fear, it could give a 5 second immunity to fear, sleep, and charm effects. This would require skillful timing and couldn’t be used as a panic button.

- **Introduce counters or vulnerabilities**

If Undead are going to keep this strength, there should be a meaningful tradeoff. They could be vulnerable to spells like Turn Undead, Shackle Undead, and Exorcism. This wouldn’t break PvP, but would add a layer of counterplay and decision-making, Undead players would have to weigh the benefits and risks before using WOTF.

It would also enhance faction flavor, adding a unique dynamic to facing Undead opponents. Paladins would gain a situational tool that feels thematic and rewarding, not something they can spam. And priests would finally have a reason to use Shackle Undead in PvP.

Of course, this wouldn’t override other CC. Polymorph, stuns, slows, and normal mechanics would still apply. This is about making Undead a little more interactive, not introducing more immunities.

- **Change WOTF to a flat duration reduction**

Similar to the Orc racial, reduce the duration of fear/sleep/charm effects by a certain percentage rather than fully breaking and blocking them.

 **Final thoughts**

Racial flavor is one of the best parts of the game. I love that my priest can shoot a bow and actually deal good damage and that a crit can proc an instant Smite. I love that Starshards got a damage boost and now feels like a real, meaningful spell. These are great examples of how racial identity and class synergy can feel rewarding without being broken.

A lot of people talk about skill, and I think most of us genuinely enjoy pulling off clutch, well-timed plays. I doubt most Undead players feel “skilled” when pressing one button to negate a core mechanic of another class. If anything, introducing counterplay or requiring proactive timing (like the 5 second immunity idea) would actually increase the skill ceiling.

These changes would let us talk about outplaying, not just out-racing. And that benefits everyone.

Would love to hear other players’ thoughts, especially if you’ve played both factions and seen this from both sides.

 **Update after more playtime**

Since posting this, I have also realized that Feral Druids with Berserk are too strong in the same way. They already deal incredible damage and when you fear, they use Berserk, you use Chastise, they trinket and you die.

If Berserk did not remove fear, Druids would actually have to make a choice. Either stay feared or break it and risk being crowd controlled by something else. Right now there is no choice at all, just an instant reset.

What makes it worse is that Feral has no trouble closing the gap on targets, unlike classes that rely on uptime management. From a PvE perspective I can understand why fear removal exists. Warriors have it too. But Warriors are not a hybrid class with stealth and off healing. And in PvE you are expected to bring teammates who handle those mechanics such as Priest Fear Ward and Tremor Totems.
