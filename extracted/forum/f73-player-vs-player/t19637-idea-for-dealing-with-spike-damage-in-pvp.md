# Idea for dealing with spike damage in PvP

## Post 133621 by toloth9 — 2025-05-30T09:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133621#p133621 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am not sure if this has been suggested or not, but why not have a stat that works like a Brewmaster tank. For example.

Grit: X% of the damage a player takes in PvP gets taken over Y seconds as a damage over time.

Make the damage you do take from the DoT portion a little more than the damage you would have taken, so that players can choose how they want to build their characters. It also changes the PvP meta a little because certain classes will be better or worse or change their spec depending on how much Grit they decide to stack.

For example: Warrior has 15% grit, so he takes 15% less spike damage, that's the upside. The down side is he takes 5% more damage over the 10 seconds the damage is distributed too (so 15% + 5% more damage). Thus the warrior is taking  a little more damage overall, but its less spikey.

Another warrior Might decide he wants 5% grit. He takes more spike damage than the first warrior, but because he chose less grit, the DoT he takes is only the 5% damage he took.(so 5% + 0% more damage)

Warrior 3 has 25% grit, its almost impossible to spike him down with damage, but the the damage over 10 seconds is say 40% , a 15% increase in the damage done overtime. (25% + 15% more damage). This means the warrior is going to take much more damage, but he can hang on longer to secure a kill, or run a flag.

This creates a situation where players can build around the choice of spike damage vs overall damage, and no playstyle suffers more than another.

If players can add grit to any item like they would and enchant, they can choose how much grit they want. The length of the dot effect can be changed to balance, the amount of damage reduction and the amount of damage you take for the reduction can also be balanced as needed, all without affecting PvE or creating the need for 2 gear sets like resilience did. (assuming you can have it stack with existing enchants.) Turn in a warsong gulch mark, and get an enchant for 1 item that say adds 5% Grit.

A few things to consider:

There would need to be no way to negate the damage, for example it would need to damage pallies through bubbles, otherwise all pallies would just use a bubble to negate the large Grit damage DoT.

Depending on how powerful being a healing class is against this damage, an MS style anit heal effect may need to be part of the DoT Debuff. (ie. heals are 10% less effective when you have it, etc).

Just thought I would throw the idea out.
