I support reverting Hand of Sacrifice to its original CC-break behavior.

From a class-design standpoint, Paladins rely on reactive support mechanics to remain competitive. Removing the CC-break made HoSac strictly weaker than equivalent tools available to other TWOW classes:

Warriors keep Berserker Rage (fear/charm break)
Priests retain Fear Ward + instant defensives
Rogues / Hunters maintain reset mechanics (Vanish / Feign Death)
Mages still have layered CC + self-cleanse

HoSac was Paladin’s only ally-oriented anti-CC trigger, giving tactical depth in PvP and coordinated play.
Without it, the spell becomes a net loss: damage transferred to the Paladin with no compensating utility for the target.

Technical recommendation:
Restore the CC-break on damage,
or introduce a controlled variant (e.g., internal cooldown on CC-break activation).

This preserves Paladin support identity and aligns with the defensive tools other classes retain.

– Sybril Dawnbreaker, Knight-Champion of the Light, Nordanaar
