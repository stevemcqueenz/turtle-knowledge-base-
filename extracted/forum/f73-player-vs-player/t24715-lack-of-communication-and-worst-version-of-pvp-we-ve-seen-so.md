# Lack of communication and worst version of PvP we've seen...so far

## Post 174012 by Alfredostfu — 2026-04-04T16:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174012#p174012 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

The much awaited pvp patch has been around for 2 weeks now and yet the state of pvp has been the worst it has ever been. Please note that the points below are seen from an Ambershire perspective. Some will no doubt be in line with Tel'Abim and Nordanaar but some issues could be different or be different degrees of the same problem.

From a purely transparency perspective:
 - World PvP event and Ranked Arena progession were announced for the 1st of April. They have not come out as of today and there doesn't seem to be any effort to at least communicate there have been setbacks even if twow couldn't provide an ETA at this point.
 - Regular Arena Skirmishes have also been disabled for over 2 weeks even though they should be an entirely separate thing from ranked arena progression and leave the player base with 1/2 less bonus honor days.
 - Dampening has been in effect for 3 days now and once again no communication on it nor on the values applied. The playerbase is once again left to figure out on their own what's going on.
 - As for the new ranking system. The previous one lacked transparency as well but at least the playerbase had it figured out. Right now there has still been no communication on exact decay values. Vague replies given by devs on twow disc state that 62k honor is what you need from r13 to r14 yet that doesn't make sense when the people at r13 are decaying all the way to 0 after hitting even past their 20k honor per week.

PvP sets:
 - Some specs got unusable sets with dreadful stats or single sets even though the playstyle and stat requirements of their different specs is entirely different.
 - Prohibitive cost of the sets particularly for the classes that got 2 or more sets. This would standalone be an issue but once you add a blocker on weekly farmed honor it becomes even worse. The only certainty is that by the time you have somehow managed to accumulate enough honor to buy the full set there's a good chance that new content has been released that overwrites it entirely.
 - Everyone and their mother having a r14 weapon transmog. Before this would be a beacon of pride, sweat, tears and most likely a lack of showering. Now it's something you do within 1 AB day only to be awarded with what is now the new pre-bis MC weapon entirely unusable to anyone that has been here since server launch.

PvP itself:
 - Thorn Gorge. Visually? Fantastic, I would daresay it looks better than Eye of the Storm ever did. Shame noone plays it because it doesn't give marks of honor, it doesn't have a bonus honor day, the map is bugged and sometimes you can't see who or if anyone has a particular base, Warriors and Druids are easily charging under the battleground to their death, Shifting to Travel,Cat, ghost wolf forms drops the flag, players able to cap bases in stealth, there are invisible blockers in certain areas of the map that you have to jump over even though visually they aren't there(such as the bridge on the right side), flags that randomly switch to horde/alliance banner even though they have been fully captured by the opposite team - These are only the issues off the top of my head and that I've experienced within the few opportunities I had to play it. Feel free to add more below so that hopefully we can get some traction and hopefully fix what can potentially be a fantastic addition to the battlegrounds.
 - As mentioned earlier arena skirmishes aren't working which leaves players with 2 bonus honor day removed entirely.
 - Dampening. Damagewise it doesn't feel like a terrible change although it's still difficult to understand why the nerf is bigger for casters than for melee but that isn't the main issue. The lack of any apparent new restrictions to healing is particularly concernening specially at a moment where healers are getting a 15% silence resist and 40% interrupt reduction with the new sets.
 - Massive queue times are back. Again, this comes from an Ambershire perspective and from what I've been able to ascertain next to Nordanaar players it hasn't been an issue there. But after an announced "fix" to the queue system that started this week it appears that from time to time the queues on Ambershire are over 20 minutes even with enough players to open the battleground and this is for solo players and not from a full premade perspective.

There are certainly many more issues and there are naturally class/spec specific issues I did not address at all but I've tried to mostly avoid those because they are not the core problem of what has been going on for the past 2 weeks nor do I play enough of every class to give an informed opinion on them.

Most of all it's the lack of communication and transparency and one random message tossed every once in a while by a dev in between the ruckus of twow discord's pvp-general channel isn't really providing clarity to most if any of the playerbase.
