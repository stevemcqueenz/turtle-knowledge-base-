# Decurse Feels Outdated, Here’s How to Fix It Without Nerfing Warlocks

## Post 161541 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-12-04T14:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161541#p161541 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

In battlegrounds, curses often go untouched and it’s easy to see why. The current Decurse ability feels underpowered and impractical compared to other dispel tools in the game.

 **Suggestions for Improvement**

To bring Decurse in line with other dispel mechanics:

 * Allow Druids to Decurse in Feral Form – So Ferals can contribute without crippling their damage.
 * Introduce “Abolish Curse” to Druids and Mages – A short-duration buff that removes curses over time, like Abolish Disease/Poison.
 * Intoduce Decurse to more classes that have a natural affinity to support their teams directly

 **Why It’s a Problem**

Affliction Warlocks can apply two curses in one GCD (e.g., Curse of Agony combined with Curse of Recklessness, Shadows, or Elements via talents). They also have a talent that reduces the chance their curses are removed by 15%, meaning each decurse attempt has an 85% success rate. In practice, this often requires multiple GCDs to clear curses, especially in BGs where several allies are affected. Meanwhile, oppressive effects like Curse of Tongues can shut down healers and casters for entire fights with almost no counterplay. A single Warlock GCD can cripple an entire team’s chance of winning by gimping their healer.

For instance, Warlocks spend 1 GCD for two curses on one target, that’s about 2.36 GCDs of decursing. Now imagine you're having a whole team that are getting spammed with these curses.

 **Classes that can decurse face major trade-offs:**

Mages: Limited instant casts; decursing means sacrificing damage and control.
Boomkins: Same issue as Mages, losing pressure for utility.
Feral Druids: Decursing is practically impossible without destroying their damage output.
Restoration Druids: The best decursers due to HoTs, but even they lose healing time and often fail multiple times due to dispel protection.

Effectively, only one spec (Resto Druid) can realistically handle decursing as part of its gameplan. Everyone else pays too high an opportunity cost.

 **Comparison to Other Dispel Mechanics**

Priest – Dispel Magic: Removes two magic effects per cast.
Paladin – Cleanse: Removes magic, poison, and disease in one GCD.
Priest/Druid - Abolish Disease/Poison: Provides ongoing protection against reapplication.

 **My Experience as a Healer**

I often play as a Discipline Priest and team up with Paladin healers. When we’re together, it feels incredible. Between us, we can handle almost every type of debuff: magic, poison, disease. It’s like having a complete toolkit for keeping our team alive. But there’s one glaring weakness: curses. The moment we face a Warlock team, everything changes. A single Curse of Tongues can completely shut down our healing, especially for Paladins who rely on fast casts. Suddenly, our strength disappears, and there’s no reliable counter unless we have a Restoration Druid on the team.

What makes this worse is how much fun it takes out of the game when you’re impacted by this and it’s almost never removed. For Alliance players, this problem is amplified because we generally have fewer Warlocks ourselves due to racial balance, Horde has WOTF, Tremor Totem, and Fear Ward, which makes Warlocks far more attractive on their side. So not only do we face more Warlocks, but we also lack the natural counters they bring.

That’s where the problem lies. Resto Druids are rare in PvP, and they’re one of the least developed healing specs in TWOW. When they do show up, it feels amazing. I love that as a Resto Druid I can cleanse Viper Sting from Priests, throw out an Innervate when someone is running dry, and remove curses. That kind of utility feels rewarding and impactful and it makes me feel like I’m contributing something unique to the team. However, for pure healing output and to actually keep your allies topped, just having one Resto Druid is not enough - you need another healer to consistently make sure your friends don't die.

And let me be clear: I love that Warlocks have so many tools. Don’t take that away from them. Their design is great, and their curses add depth to the game. The issue isn’t that curses are too strong; it’s that other classes simply don’t have enough tools to deal with them. When one ability can cripple an entire healing core, the counterplay shouldn’t hinge on the presence of a single rare spec.

The only other class that can apply a similar debuff is the Rogue with Mind-numbing Poison. But for that to work, they need to coat their weapons with it, a huge opportunity cost, and then land melee hits on every target they want to affect. That’s not realistic in a team fight, especially when a Warlock can comfortably sit in the backline and apply Curse of Tongues instantly to multiple targets without risk.

Adding more options for curse removal would create a healthier, more dynamic experience for everyone. It would spread the responsibility across classes instead of leaving it all on one spec and that means more teamwork, more strategy, and more fun for everyone. :)

You probably know that Warlocks have DoTs in their toolkit that are magic in nature. None of the decurse classes can remove all Warlock debuffs, since they’re a mix of magical and curse-based effects. So the idea that these classes could “just dispel everything” doesn’t hold.

With Affliction, Curse of Agony is often paired with Curse of Shadows, meaning you get two curses in a single GCD. On top of that, both have around a 15% chance to resist dispels, which makes decursing unreliable. In practice, a Restoration Druid may need to spend more than two GCDs just trying to remove them, which makes it inefficient and often not worth it.

The biggest issue, though, is Curse of Tongues. It heavily disrupts classes like Shamans, Holy Paladins, Priests, and other casters. It lasts a long time and significantly slows gameplay in a way that feels frustrating rather than interactive. One Curse of Tongues on those classes effectively takes them out of the game instantly. The only comparable effect is Rogue’s Mind-numbing Poison, but that requires actual commitment and can’t just be spread freely across a 30–40 yard caster radius.

On top of that, it even affects communication, you literally start typing in demonic, which is a fun flavor detail, but in practice it makes it harder to coordinate with teammates in-game. That ends up pushing players toward external tools like Discord just to function properly, gameplay should never force that.

If decurse mechanics aren’t improved, then Curse of Tongues needs a heavy nerf. It should have a meaningful cooldown (something like 5–10 seconds) so it can’t just be spammed, and its duration should be significantly reduced. The same goes for Curse of Shadows, the duration is excessive.
