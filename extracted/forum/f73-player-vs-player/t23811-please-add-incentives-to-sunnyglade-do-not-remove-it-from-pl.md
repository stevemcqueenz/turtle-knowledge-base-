You are correct, the incentive to queue Sunnyglade is still very low.

Around 1 year ago I suggested increasing the honor gain because it was so bad. Devs increased it but the change wasn't satisfying. Now on Sunnyglade bonus day, winners receive 1.5k honor and losers 1k. Average game lasts about 15 minutes. I think WSG is a bit longer on average but awards twice as much honor (I might be wrong, I don't know exact numbers).

Reputation farm in this BG is a pain, you get 150 rep by winning if I remember correctly. Black Morass is way better.

The truth is most of the players that queue into battlegrounds do it for honor farm. They don't care about how it is played, they do care about efficient farming. If the incentive to play specific battleground is low, they won't queue for it. I think that current Sunnyglade Valley with all its faults could still be widely played if the incentive would be high enough. 2k honor for losing, 3k honor for winning (on SGV bonus day) would be OK. Also, 500 rep for winning and 250 for losing.

Another reason why Sunnyglade isn't played as often is requirement of 12 queued people. In comparison WSG requires only 6 or 8 people to maintain the game.

BTW they remove Sunnyglade Valley because they can't open the map in Noggit. That means they can't edit the map. Further edition of the map is impossible so only complete rework can save it.
