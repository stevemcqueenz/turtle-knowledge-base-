The "PvP: What's on the Horizon" thread is bad imo.

The current OP situation is only in some cases , not every class every spec are in OP state.

From my experience , first wave change as follows:

 1. insignia should also remove silent effect.
 2. any extra attack from item proc/talent/skill will be no long capable making a crit.

 3. mage
arcane missile and icicles will no long cause cast time pushbacks of your target ,and both of them will remove any movemoemt impairment of the target.
talent "resonance cascade" only duplicate 50% of normal damage even if it crits.
(mages almost kept the damage ,but gives opponent the chance to counter)

 4. rogue
expose armor has more effective on high armor but lower on low armor .(for example , 5 combo reduce 1700 armor on 8000 armor target ,but only reduce 700 armor on 3000 armor target)
increase magic armor (mage/priest/warlock) value at some points.

 5. feral druid
reduce bleeding time of pounce from 18 seconds to 9 seconds .
talent "open wounds" should be 5 points instead 3 points , the effect of full points could remain the same.

That's for now that I think would solve most unbalance issue but not big compact to pve.
If you counter more ,please add to discuss.
