## Post 158217 by Ellifta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-11-03T14:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158217#p158217 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Back in summer I shared my experiences PvP'ing on Alliance side and explained how we'd lose 80% of our matches due to several factors, but mainly due to lack of player skill and gear on Alliance side, overpowered shaman specs and discrepancy of caster ratio on Alliance vs Horde side.

Since the announcement of cross faction battlegrounds, suddenly Alliance is winning a lot more than before. Sometimes with big score differences too. I would go as far as saying my experiences have been flipped playing the last 2 WSG and AB day. Alliance still is majority melee, but seems like a lot of good Alliance players made a come back.

Was this cross-faction announcement in itself enough to tip the balance in favor of Alliance? Obviously my experiences in last 2 weeks as one person doing moderate amount of PvP (150-200k honor) is not a big sample size. Yet, I've seen the typical Horde premades not playing anymore and straight up giving, suiciding when they realize they aren't fighting 2.6k HP retribution paladins anymore. It really blew my mind to see people who used to pubstomp a few months ago now AFK'ing in graveyard when they lose the first flag in WSG, without even attempting to fight back.

Are shaman players that used to auto winning every game with their broken specs that, the chance of meeting another one scared them bad enough to stop playing or even trying altogether? Are they afraid of having a green geared protection paladin with 2.8k hp on their team instead of 7k hp shaman? If someone has any other explanations as to why suddenly Alliance is stomping in WSG and AB, please say so, otherwise it seems like sealclubbers scattered away like the rats they are upon fighting equally geared and skilled opponents.
