I don't disagree with anything you said, but I think you're looking at it from the wrong perspective. Cross faction PvP makes PvP more fun by spreading good players around. It also takes care of melee-heavy Alliance and caster-heavy Horde we're so used to. I don't think it was meant to make classes more balanced. All it does is put up new scenarios that didn't exist before (shaman+paladin in same team) and massively shake up the usual players you'd see on one side.

Weak classes will still stay weak, but now it's less likely that you will get curbstomped due to class imbalances. Your team is as likely to have strong classes/players as the other team. This doesn't mean it will always work, but beforehand it was heavily in favor of Horde with both better players and stronger classes. Now people can't pile up on Horde with race changes and whatnot.

I did about 12 battles so far, both have been pretty... unique so to say. At least it's a fresh experience and entertaining for now. It seems extremely chaotic at times, but I've yet to make a conclusive comment about scenarios like shaman getting healed by paladin while fighting another shaman scenarios.

In other words, what you are criticizing wasn't the intent of the change so I think your criticisms are not valid.
