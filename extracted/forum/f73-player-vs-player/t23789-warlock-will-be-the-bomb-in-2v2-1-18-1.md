# warlock will be the bomb in 2v2(1.18.1)

## Post 166815 by Solaire0726 — 2026-01-29T03:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166815#p166815 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

This content is about the strength of the Warlock class in PvP on the Turtle WoW server and the consequences of the rated arena opening in version 1.18.1.

In the current evaluation of 2v2 arenas, the Warlock class is the strongest, for the following reasons:

 1.High Health Pool: A fully geared Warlock can reach over 5000 HP (those prioritizing damage typically have around 5000 HP, while those stacking Stamina can even reach 7000). For example, on Nordanaar, I encountered a Warlock named Tapkalol with 6.8k HP. Before a 2v2 arena match begins, the Warlock can sacrifice their Voidwalker to gain a shield, effectively adding even more health. Combined with a Healthstone, this grants the Warlock exceptionally strong survivability. PS: Only a Rogue might be able to quickly take them down, and even then, they must be careful not to be detected by Detect Invisibility.

 2.Reliable Crowd Control Chain: In 2v2 arenas, Warlocks can summon a Felguard, which has an Axe Toss ability for reliable crowd control. This pairs with the Warlock's own Death Coil to form a control chain. Within those few seconds, they can quickly burst down a target with over 5000 HP. Additionally, the Felguard has an ability to break crowd control and a high-damage Shadowstorm skill, making it overwhelmingly powerful in 2v2 arenas. Even if they forgo the Felguard for a Felhunter, the Warlock remains a caster killer, though it feels less oppressive than facing the Felguard and causes less frustration.

 3.Consistent and High Damage: The Warlock's damage over time (DoT) effects, combined with Shadow harvest (or Soul Harvest), are instant casts like a Mage's Arcane Missiles. Once applied, they can only be stopped by interrupts, and during this time, your health drops rapidly. Paired with their crowd control, this creates an unparalleled sense of frustration—you enter the arena ready to outplay your opponent, only to be locked down and drained to death.

These three factors make the Warlock a monstrous presence. If no adjustments are made after the introduction of rated arenas, I believe a Warlock + Holy Paladin composition will dominate everything. I hope the Turtle WoW class designers see this post. If a single class possesses all three of these advantages, it becomes nearly impossible to counter or contain. (For example, the Flame Shaman in version 1.18.0 disrupted the PvP environment with high armor and burst damage, but it could still be controlled through crowd control. With the changes in 1.18.1, Flame Shamans may no longer be viable in PvP, as the condition for melee to trigger damage buffs is too demanding.)

If adjustments are to be made, I hope the development team will consider limiting powerful summons like the Felguard in rated arenas, while also reducing the damage of Warlock DoTs combined with instant nukes in PvP. This would address the issue of dealing massive damage without any cost (such as casting time). Alternatively, the survivability of the Warlock itself could be nerfed. I hope we can discuss this rationally.
