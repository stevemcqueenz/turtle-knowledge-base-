# Tried AV after changes

## Post 149657 by Matches (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-31T22:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149657#p149657 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tank, 60, 5k health, 423 defence, mostly the best epics you can get from crafting/AH.

The PvP nerfs have not made a difference.  It seems to me players have come to be doing *so* much damage that a 20% nerf doesn't make a noticeable difference.  I was killed from 5k by a mage casting arcane missiles twice - died half way through the second cast.  His missile crits were doing 808 damage - I guess before they were doing 1k damage.  I was takingi 404/808 damage per tick, with a lot of crits.  That 20% reduction made no difference to the outcome.

Seems Warmasters are a lot more powerful.  Played as a 60 rogue.  Taking 2k hits *normally* from them and their cleave.  Two hits and I died.  Was ressed, instantly hit by a passing cleave, died.

It seems to me I'm being insta-killed by players as usual, the nerf has not changed this, but now Warmasters are *also* insta-killing.

On the face of it, the existing problem is not fixed, and now there's a new problem.

Also, a lot of games, Horde are using Korrak dagger to get the entire team into A base very early.  AV is already dominated by H, the top half of the kill list is always Horde, *and also* they're often teleporting the entire team in A base.  A team have no chance to win, the game ends long before they can get into H base.

Guys, devs, this seems just fucked up, all ways going.

The game in one sense always has had a huge power range - we start at level 1 and progress to level 60 - but the game is arranged so players meet mobs of their own approximate power range (as they move from zone to zone, levelling).

When you end up with a large power range at the same level (level 60), then you have a problem when all of those players - with their large power range - come together in the same zone (AV).  The NPCs are either too strong or too weak, the other players are either too strong or too weak.

The more TWoW has added high end gear, the more AV (and other BGs) has become unbalanced, regardless of any other problems regarding power creep.

Separately, I'd say right now AV where it's unbalanced between A/H, where H get to K first and where H normally dominate in combat, AV can't tolerate the Korrak dagger.  For now, remove it from the game.
