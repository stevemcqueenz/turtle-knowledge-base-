# Restrict Premades!!!

## Post 157806 by Odlnn — 2025-10-30T11:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157806#p157806 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello everyone,

You all know that feeling: you wait in a 30-minute queue, only to lose the game in just 5 minutes. When this happens, it just makes you want to stop playing.

How is this fair? We're all here to farm honor, and when we get matched against players who get a free win, it feels like a huge injustice. Allowing premades in random battlegrounds corrupts the entire ranking system.

 **Objection: "A five-person premade can't have a huge impact in a 10v10 or 15v15."**
This is wrong for many reasons, but the most important one is that battlegrounds are often unbalanced. We frequently play 5v5, 7v8, or 6v8. Even in a 15v15 battleground like Arathi Basin, a premade can bring 3-4 healers while we have one, or even none.

 **Objection: "Just create your own premade."**
Premade games are an entirely different type of game. It's not the same as a random queue. You have to form a team, set up a Discord channel, inspect everyone, assign roles, and so on. The way you play in a premade is completely different.

But I don't want to do that. I just want to play the game after work. I want to play, not lose 5-0 in Arathi Basin in just 3 minutes.

In conclusion, I want to say this: I am not food for worms. I would gladly wait five times longer in a queue than go through this experience. Many people feel the same way. When we face the same premade over and over, we just stop playing. And when this happens, it kills the battlegrounds activity. Please notice this.

 **My Suggestions:**

 1. Restrict premades by making them only play against other premades. This might mean they never get to play, but you could create a separate ranked battlegrounds system with bonus honor to accommodate them.

 2. Restrict premade groups to a maximum of 2 people. Even two coordinated players can be tough to beat in a situation like 4v6 in Warsong Gulch, but it is at least acceptable.

I know many people enjoy playing in premade groups, but on our server (Ambershire), it has simply become a tool for farming honor unfairly.

If we’re going to argue that premades in battlegrounds are unfair and should be restricted, then we need to apply that same logic to guilds raiding together. Guilds are essentially long-term premade groups with massive advantages: they coordinate strategies, assign loot, optimize class balance, and run content on a schedule. Compared to pugs, guilds can farm BiS gear with far greater efficiency and consistency. Pugs wipe more, struggle with communication, and often get locked out of progression entirely. If fairness is the priority, then allowing guilds to raid together is just as “unfair” as premades in PvP. But we don’t ban guild raids, we accept that teamwork and organization lead to better results. Battlegrounds should be no different. The real issue isn’t that people play together, it’s that the system doesn’t account for the imbalance. Let’s fix matchmaking and honor incentives instead of punishing players for forming groups.
