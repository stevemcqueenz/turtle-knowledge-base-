To queue to Arathi Basin at lvl 19 you should:
 1. Make a group of 5 people (or whatever actual number you need to start)
 2. Send leader of the group to Arathi Highlands to battleground entrance.
 3. Enter battleground entrance by foot. Bg registration window will open.
 4. Leader of the group should press "join as group".
Group will be queued.
