# The best battleground I ever had in my life (10-19)

## Post 138834 by Odomiah — 2025-07-12T14:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138834#p138834 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/imgur.min.html#a/EocTUJ3]

What you see above is utter domination by horde.
What you see above is complete obliteration of your opponent.

 *But what happens when your opponent just won't quit?*

This battleground lasted 1h and 36minutes. I carried the flag for over 1h at least.
This entire time my patience was tested to the fullest.
Countless of runs, countless of calculations of where to go, what cooldowns they have so I can escape...
It was mental breakdown at some points.

We had a fight versus party of geared horde 19s. 3 shamans and 2 priests.
 99% of people would give up after 10 deaths and pointless tries to outdamage the healing potential of their 19s.
But I kept flag.
I kept flag and "cowardly" ran away every time they tried to catch me.
I used all knowledge of map layout I had. Used every single tiny bit of shortcut I can to make the gap between me and a chaser.

No offense to horde, but with three shamans you should seriously do better with totem positioning. I used their lack of battle IQ to fullest and stalled until my warrior guildmate eventually grabs a kill or two.
We even had 2 man down at some point as ppl simply had to leave. Battleground was over 1h at that point.

Still I ran...

They cought me 2 times. But as scoreboard says, I managed to get them two times too. Both crucial times! Got our flags back with, eventual, capping follow up.

Our 15lvl rogue played perfect counterplay when they got me second time! Separated himself from group, went straight to retake their flag. They managed to get him killed but I was fast enough to forsee it and spring my way to him while he dropped the flag. I took it from him and made his effort count!
I will never forget that moment, Barbetruc!

Also our lv15mage Atmena did amazing job with "sheep" spell. Countless times he prevented them from using either heals or fears against us.

Their kill counts are huge but our willpower was bigger.
It would take me 3 pages to write you all epic scenarios that we had in this Warsong Gulch battle, but biggest one happened in our minds!

I want to pay homage to all my comrades in this battleground for not giving up after this slaughter they did to us!
I want to congratulate them for sticking with me on it and carefully playing out plans and actions.
(keep in mind, we did it via bg chat, not discord)

We killed them few times, but all those times were when we planned it and wanted it!

Last fight happened when they lost it in their heads. They went full in our graveyard. But their mistake was that they went in 2 by two... 2 priests... 2 shamans.
Still you'd think they'd have a bigger chance, but people underestimate me. I have 850 hp but my damage compensates for it. Glown's few heals were just enough to give me edge over their entire roster. They made mistake to focus me, while mage was there just conveniently sheep-ing them.
They started going out of mana, heals were fewer on the horizon and they eventually started dying 1 by 1.

We killed them all and left their flag carrier last. Eventually I killed him too, got flag back and used my sprint only to meet their shaman Ypernyteatr running towards our flag. When he saw me, he placed totem to slow me down but my jump was bigger than the gap between  us and I capped final flag.

I don't know how they feel after such a loss.
But we, as total underdogs... we felt amazing!

This bracket surely has something others don't.

Welcome to 10-19 Warsong Gulch.
Antler Song awaits you!
