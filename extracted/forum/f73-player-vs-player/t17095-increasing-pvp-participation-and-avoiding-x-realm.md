# Increasing pvp participation and avoiding x-realm

## Post 116590 by Thorngraze — 2025-01-13T15:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116590#p116590 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am a Tel'Abim player, and this primarily concerns the situation on that realm.

I strongly oppose cross-realm PvP (and cross-realm features in general). I believe it weakens server community by matching you with players you never encounter outside battlegrounds. I like the fact that I'm participating in battlegrounds with the same people I run dungeons with or see idling in cities, as it enhances the world-building experience.

It still remains, there is a problem with PvP participation on Tel'Abim. While level 60 activity is decent for Warsong Gulch and Arathi Basin, there is an absence of matches for Arenas and Alterac Valley and participation at lower levels is practically non-existent. To help resolve this a really helpful feature would be displaying the number of queued players from both factions for each battleground.

This change would likely have a psychological effect, encouraging more players to queue when they see others waiting. This would particularly benefit lower-level battlegrounds and less popular options like AV and Arenas. Currently, I mainly queue for WSG, AB, and AV, but if I saw three others queued for Arena, I would queue knowing a match would start soon.

 **TLDR: Avoid cross-realm features! Instead display the number of players queueing to improve battleground participation.**

Have the devs given any indication of introducing cross realm play? To my knowledge, they have not. If anything, they've reiterated that they have no plans for it.

Tel'Abim's issue and it being a PvP realm has little to do with the battlegrounds but more to do with the players and the lack of **world** PvP structured scenarios. You go into battlegrounds and their intricacies, but the simple fact is that there is literally no reason to be on Tel'Abim for battlegrounds; just play on Nordanaar if those are desired. In fact, a bunch of people have already figured that out. Even the scenario you described of going to dungeons and idling in cities with the same people you PvP (battleground) with exists just as much on Nordanaar.

If you want to increase PvP participation, then you have to do what groups that advocate for it do. For example, guilds such as the Korkron Enforcers. And they do it *because they love pvp* rather than loving battlegrounds specifically. And press the devs to give an actual reason for Tel'Abim to exist.

I'm saying this as a fan of PvP realms. I started WoW some 20 years ago on a PvP realm and my only big experience with PvE has been on Nordanaar. Unfortunately, Blizzard gave next to no reason for world PvP in vanilla. The *only* world PvP that is structured in game that I can think of would be the towers in Eastern Plaguelands. So one scenario and extremely niche. If Turtle gave thought to adding features like this to pre-existing zones, for example Stonetalon Mountains, and made such features exclusive to the PvP realm, PvP participation might increase. And the scenario of world ganking, which is what we all know world PvP usually is, would be even more wasteful.

But if it's just battlegrounds, then Nordanaar exists to fulfill those desires.
