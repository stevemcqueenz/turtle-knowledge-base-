Saying X-Faction isn’t about classes ignores the elephant in the room: racials and faction skew. Paladins and Shamans are not symmetrical, and Alliance doesn’t have anything like WOTF or Tremor Totem.

Many roll Horde precisely because they don’t have to deal with WOTF or oppressive Shaman utility. That’s not about ‘skill,’ that’s about avoiding disadvantage.

If PvP is supposed to be competitive, then fair matchups should be welcomed, not feared – speaking of fear, I look forward to watching Horde Warlocks and Priests get shredded by Undead Rogues. Maybe then we’ll see a sudden willingness to discuss much needed nerfs to racials like WOTF.

I fear that Xfaction will cause players to lose their sense of belonging and honor. While it appears to be one team fighting another, in reality, individuals will feel no attachment to the team, nor will they have any desire to win or lose. At least now, everyone has a common enemy: the opposing faction. Even so, many players don't care about winning or losing. But if this common enemy no longer exists, I don't know what players will be fighting for on the battlefield. When there is no desire to win, the level of combat in the battlefield will decline sharply. Of course, this feeling is not based on any evidence and is unreliable, but that is my concern.

When the battlefield is non-Xfaction, You will face a stable group of "enemies" with a stable group of "friendly parties". But when the battlefield is Xfaction, "friends" and "enemies" cease to exist, and you'll find no meaning in the fight.
