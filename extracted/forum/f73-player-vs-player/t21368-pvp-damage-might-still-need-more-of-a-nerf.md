## Post 148135 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-25T09:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148135#p148135 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a Priest with the following PVP-centric gear:

 * Almost maxed resilience
 * 5.5k HP w sta buff
 * 40%  armor mitigation w inner fire

I would have to say that I survive a lot better when getting focused by geared players - casters or melee. In TBC with maxed resilience I could often survive against two players for quite some time, but not here. I think it's fair to go down vs two DPS.

However, mana is a big issue behind this spec. Mana runs out quickly and this is usually what kills me. On the other hand, I see a lot of raid gear healers without this mana issue, like other Priests and Druids. On the other hand, they die like flies when focused. Paladin and shaman healers seem to be absolutely unkillable tho if played correctly and they seem to have very little mana problems.

Also, I do think that LOS is an extremely important aspect here vs casters. If you face casters in the open, your odds of winning decrease substantially. So, you need to be around LOS-objects in order to mitigate a lot of the damage. This is not always available tho.
