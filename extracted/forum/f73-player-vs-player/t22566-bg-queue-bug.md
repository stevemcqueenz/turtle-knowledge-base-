Hello!
When you queue for two battlegrounds at once (AB and WSG) and finish the first one, you can receive an instant invitation to the next BG upon exiting. However, the invitation is not sent to the entire group, but only to the players who left the previous BG first, causing the rest of the team to lose their place in the queue. Please fix this bug. The invitation should be sent to the entire team because we queued "as a group"!
