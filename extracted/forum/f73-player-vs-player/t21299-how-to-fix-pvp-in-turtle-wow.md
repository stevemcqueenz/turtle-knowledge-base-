# HOW TO FIX PVP IN TURTLE WOW!!!

## Post 147715 by Hammerfall — 2025-08-22T16:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147715#p147715 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

right now twow pvp feels like its lacking all kinds of love. its been years that this server has been out and we're JUST NOW getting some kind of pvp update for arenas and gear changes ect...i really hope the devs see this and take it into consideration.

HOW TO FIX PVP

ARENAS
 ---
 1. add arena queue system to the global list where players can queue up ANYWHERE around the world. not just in a vague hidden room inside the capital cities. there were many times players would ask and wonder "where can i queue arenas?" thats bad. make it to where players and queue ANYWHERE.

 2. if you dont plan to have conquest points be a thing, PLEASE make it to where arenas give GOOD honor gains so it gives players an incentive to do them. theres a reason your new custom bg never gets queued because there is no incentive to even do it. if your new custom bg gave alot of honor per win/loss players would wanna do it. and same applies to arenas. make it to where arenas give GOOD honor. not just 200 per win and 50 per loss...should be maybe like 2000 per win and 1000 per loss as an example. you just need to make sure whatever it is you want players to do for pvp there is a good reason for them to do it. if the only way to get pvp gear is honor. why waste your time doing something that gives little to no honor?

 3. add a global leaderboard for the top 2v2 and 3v3 teams that players can see to add more competitive feel to arenas. maybe add the leaderboard somewhere noticable in the main city or as a ui component people can check at the bare minimum.

BATTLEGROUNDS
 ---
 1. battlegrounds seem ok there isnt much that needs to be changed for bgs. BUT it would be nice to see a new bg that is smaller in scale like maybe a 5v5 bg so its more skill based rather than numbers based when it comes to fights.

WORLD PVP
 ---
 1. consider maybe adding objectives around the map that give good honor gains for players. because right now the only thing players do for honor is bg bg bg...we need something different besides spamming bgs. some ideas for objectives could be maybe honor/marks of honor airdrop system that drops a couple of airdrops randomly around the world where players from each faction could compete to capture for rewards, and the loot goes towards whichever faction loots or captures the airdrop ect

 2. maybe also increase the amount of honor gained from kills in the open world compared to bgs and arenas so players feel a better reason to get honorable kills in the open world rather than only in bgs and arenas.

PVP GEAR
 ---
a big issue with pvp gear right now is that its generally weaker than pve gear in most cases. and we dont even have good pvp off pieces like rings, capes, trinkets ect that are better than pve off pieces which forces alot of pvp players that DONT wanna raid or pve to start raiding in order to have "BIS" gear...and it sucks. pvp players dont log in to pve...they wanna pvp. thats it. so here are ways to fix that.

 1. we need to adjust the pvp gear stats. i know some people complained that adding resil to gear will cause a divide and now pve players wouldnt be able to compete much in pvp ect with their hard earned tier gear. so an easy fix is to NOT have resil and instead adjust the pvp gear stats to match tier gear. pvp gear should have the stats of tier gear in pvp and weaker in pve. for example a rank 8 chest piece should have the same base stats as a tier 2 chest piece and the equip and set bonuses should only count towards pvp, so to compare...a rank 8 chest piece equip effect gives +50 healing done in pvp just like how a tier 2 chest gives +50 healing done BUT in pve the pvp armors equip effects dont count much because it only works in pvp so they arent as strong in pve. or instead of making the pvp gear equip effect and set bonuses not work for pve you can make it weaker in pve like +50 healing in pvp and only +15 healing in pve ect

Rank 7-10 rare blue pvp gear/weapons should have the base stats of tier 2 gear and the equip and set bonus power level of tier 2 gear ONLY towards players in pvp.

Rank 12-13 epic purple pvp gear/weapons should have the base stats of tier 3 gear and the equip and set bonus power level of tier 3 gear ONLY towards players in pvp.

if you impliment this system, pvp players can focus on farming ONLY pvp gear without being forced to raid..and still have an even chance against pve players with tier gear. and PVE PLAYERS will also have a chance to still compete in pvp if they want with their tier gear because there is no resilience involved ect.

PS: PLEASE ADD PVP RINGS, NECKS, CAPES, AND TRINKETS with this same system i just mentioned above in mind, rank 7-10 pvp off pieces should also be as strong as tier 2 pve gear and rank 12-13 off pieces should be as strong as tier 3.

THATS ALL. DO THIS AND PVP WILL IMPROVE DRASTICALLY ON THE SERVER.  turtle_in_love_head

PS: if you need any advice on balancing or any other pvp stuff im willing to help the dev team with those ideas for pvp aswell.

I really like the ideas already mentioned, and I’d love to expand with a few more that could add variety and depth to PvP without breaking the classic feel:

- **Outdoor/Open-World PvP objectives that grant BG reputation**
Hold towers/banners, flip supply depots, or secure crossroads checkpoints in contested zones. Rewards: a small amount of WSG/AB/AV rep (plus modest honor/marks) so world PvP feeds into BG progress. *Safeguards:* per-day cap, diminishing returns on repeat flips, and a short “hold” timer to prevent ping-ponging.
- **Daily/Weekly PvP quests**
Examples: “Win 3 WSG,” “Earn 25–50 HKs in contested zones,” “Escort a supply caravan,” or “Disrupt an enemy objective.” Rewards could be honor, BG marks, rep, or light consumables. *Anti-cheese:* level-appropriate HKs only and account-wide lockouts for the weekly.
- **Smaller BG designed for lower brackets (no mounts)**
A tight 5v5 or 8v8 map for 19/29/39/49 brackets with 8–12 minute rounds and simple objectives (King-of-the-Hill or a short flag run). No mounts; emphasis on utility and skirmishing so newer players learn objectives before larger BGs.
- **Faction Supply Runs / Escort Missions**
Periodic caravans moving between outposts; both factions get a zone-wide ping when one spawns. Attack/defend for honor/marks/rep, with shared credit to encourage grouping. Scales to zone level so lowbies aren’t griefed.
- **Rotating “Call to Arms” zone**
Each week, one contested zone offers bonus honor/rep/objective credit. Moves weekly to keep hotspots fresh and spread the action around the world.
- **PvP currency-sink vendors**
Cosmetics and fun utility purchased with BG marks/honor: faction banners, tabards, camp standards, duel flags, city recall pennants, etc. Purely cosmetic/QoL to avoid power creep while giving long-term goals.
- **Profession-linked PvP missions**
Engineers craft temporary barricades or gyro-turrets for world objectives; Alchemists deliver field rations/medkits; Tailors/Leatherworkers turn in supply crates. Lets non-combat roles contribute meaningfully to PvP efforts.
- **Underdog honor scaling**
If one faction is under-represented in BG participation that week, grant a small, temporary honor/rep bonus (that tapers as balance returns). Helps queues without punishing the majority.
