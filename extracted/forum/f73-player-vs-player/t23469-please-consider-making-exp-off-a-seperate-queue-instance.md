Disabling xp in bgs benefit normal players more than twinks.
Twinks have luxury of being carried through all dungeons without killing all thrash.
Normal players have to kill all thrash and they inevitable get xp from it.

There is three proposals to improve situation:
[Nerf high level enchants on low level gear](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4499) because enchants are most imbalanced factor.
[Overlapping bracket](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=21897) to force twinks to fight with enemies of higher level.
[Remove World Buffs from battlegrounds](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=18167) because WB makes stong people even stronger and weak people lose those buff too easily.

Anyway, PvP at level 60 is much much worse in terms of power differences. Viable twink for lvl 19 costs about 400-1k gold. BiS is about 2-3k gold.
Viable lvl 60 pvper is several weeks of raiding. For less dedicated player is might be several months.
