PvP clearly hasn’t been tested enough, and it really shows. The mechanics aren’t what they used to be, where fights involved a lot more strategy, even in 2v1 or 3v1 situations. Now you just get smacked way too hard, even if you’re skilled.

## Post 163620 by Snoof — 2025-12-24T12:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163620#p163620 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

1 - Pretty much. Not necessarily OP in PvP (although those poisons **ARE** messed up right now), but their CC spammy playstyle is the gameplay equivalent of giving anybody who runs into one 1v1 crotch rot.

 2 - Beyond broken. It's really quite ridiculous for the best support class in the game to also be able to 1-shot everybody with their basic rotation, and between their high armor class and stoneskin totem they're practically immune to physical damage while doing it. I think the devs need to make a final decision about whether they want enhancement to be a DPS spec or a tanking one, because as long as it's allowed to be both you're just not gonna be able to balance this class for PvP.

 3 - Every bit as bullshit as Shaman for many of the same reasons. Practically unduelable, they have a spell to answer nearly every situation, they're extremely good at supporting other classes when they can be bothered to (although at least they don't do it just by existing to the extent that shamans do) and they can whack you with one of the longest stuns in the game from range in spite of being a melee class. They also get high burst that ignores armor on top of all that because why not, I guess. Only class in the game I can't think of a single bad matchup for. Tools to break bubble need to be introduced to the game if you want to balance the class without making Retri unviable in PvE.

 4 - Not bad, but the barrier for entry warriors have to be competitive in PvP is astronomical since gear is absolutely everything for them. They're also pathetically reliant on teammates and consumables to do basically anything that isn't bursting an isolated Priest, and have the survivability of wet tissue paper without dedicated PvP gear. Rotten duelists, too. The CC vulnerability is very much still there; there's a talent that lets warriors cleanse roots with berserker rage on TWoW, but it's largely irrelevant in PvP since it's so deep in the Fury tree that Arms warriors can't access it.

 5 - Curses are extremely obnoxious and affliction warlocks can absolutely ruin the day of any class that doesn't have self-healing without even fighting them because they're able to apply multiple dots with one spell cast, but overall I don't think they're unbalanced. I do wish the duration on curses was reduced on player targets though; still having debuffs on you from a Warlock you fought 5 minutes ago is absolute cancer.

 6 - Generally fine, although the fact that mages' channeled spells (icicles, arcane missiles) can't be broken through LoS and seem to stretch their range to infinity while being cast makes them extremely unfair to fight against in some scenarios. Frost mages might be just a touch *too* good at fucking over melees with constant frostbite procs, but they get dunked on by other casters so I guess that's a kind of balance - not what I'd call a good one, rock paper scissors is a pretty dull formula for PvP IMO, but a kind of balance.

 7 - Yeah Hunters just suck in PvP. Everything a ranged hunter can do a casters can do better, and survival hunters are completely unviable as melees due to not having any reliable gap closers to get onto the ranged and being too low armor to square up against the other melees. I'm sure there's something they beat, but I'd generally rather be on a team with no hunters.

 8 - Obnoxiously good at carrying flags in WSG, but the same thing that makes them really good at that one thing makes them pretty mid at everything else. Druids in PvP are made to kite and run away, but since there's usually very little incentive to chase a Druid around the map in any non-WSG flag carrier scenario, it's a pretty mediocre strength to have. Balance druids' damage takes way too long to come out, feral druids die at lightspeed in Cat form where they do damage but are invincible in Bear where the most they can do is be kind of annoying. Resto is not too bad, but their reliance on shifting to bear for survival makes them pretty bad under pressure compared to other healers, especially if you're not a Tauren with access to war stomp.

 9 - Priests are generally pretty good and have a lot of unique tools that make them extremely valuable to have on your team, but they're balanced to rely a little too much on fears - probably the least reliable form of CC in Turtle PvP - to stay alive in group fights and tend to melt very easily under pressure. I think they'd be in a good place if some of the more ubiquitous fear cleansers like Will of the Forsaken and Tremor Totems were nerfed for PvP.
