Diminishing returns in Turtlewow are very much a thing.

But classic wow behaves quite differently from wotlk in many ways. One of which is CC.
Short form CC's usually have a fixed duration (Cheapshot, Death Coil, Intercept etc.) and if you are hit by multiple of the same category, their duration is halfed on the second application and quartered on the 3. application until it resets after 15 seconds. So far, thats the same way it did in wotlk afaik.

But the longer from CCs like Polymorph and Sap behave different. Instead of having a fixed duration, each second you are in these CC effects, the game rolls you a chance for the effect to end prematurely. That chance increases and without diminishing returns usually breaks around 9-12 seconds. But if you get rotten luck, its possible to sit in CC for up to 20 seconds from a single CC spell.
Diminishing returns on repeated long form CC of the same type increases the chance to break out prematurely. Its less likely you will sit in CC for 10+ seconds, but its not impossible.

Additionally, different forms of CC do not share Diminishing return. If you get Sapped into Sheeped into Ice Trap, those effects are not sharing DR. So you will likely be CCd for longer than 30 seconds.

But, if you sit in CC for long periods of time, that is usually something that can be remedied by better play. As a ranged character, you want to keep close to cover, so if someone wants to sheep you, you can duck behind cover. If you suspect rogues nearby, try to buff someone who is in combat to also get you in combat so you can not be sapped and so on.
Dealing with CC and utilizing your own CC is a large part of what classics PvP is all about.
Additionally, make sure, once you unlocked rank 3 in pvp (should be doable in a couple hours playtime) to visit your factions PvP vendors located in Orgrimmar and Stormwind respectively and grab the Insignia. Compared to other classic servers, on turtlewow the insignia cleanses all CC effects on you uppon using. Greatly saving yourself frustrations every 3 minutes
