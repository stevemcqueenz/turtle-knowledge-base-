# Sooo... AV NPCs

## Post 150730 by madferret — 2025-09-08T03:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150730#p150730 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Are we ready to admit that fully upgraded Champion NPCs stats, combined with their insane 1min respawn rate, is just unbearable to play? Once fully upgraded, turtles turn into purely bot pvp, where you're just trucking against these insanely fast respawns while also dealing with the defenders of the opponent team.

I didn't like the Resources mechanic introduced in later expansions, felt like a cheap way to solve the issue of eternal turtles, but I do think that there is room for improvement. Here are a couple suggestions off the top of my head, feel free to shoot them down and add yours:

 - The obvious one: increase the respawn timers for base NPCs such as roaming guards and archers. GY guards are more manageable and they play an important role in strategy, but the roaming NPCs do not add anything to the experience and feel more like a chore than a mechanic. A horribly dull chore at that.

 - Alternatively, remove Champion upgrades, and limit them to Veteran rank. That is still an incentive for players to upgrade NPCs, but the result is more manageable. I still believe the respawn rate is the main issue though.

 - Another one could be turning off the respawn rate altogether in very specific circumstances -turtling related ones. For example, if both towers/bunkers are burning, and still there's no progress being made, and HKs do not yield honor anymore, make base NPC guards gradually stop respawning, the same way archers stop respawning in destroyed towers/bunkers. Maybe the trigger could be "X amount of defending players die in their base", similar to Reinforcements, but in a way that losing all your Reinforcements doesn't automatically win the game. This wouldn't remove turtles on its own, but it would allow for the deciding factor to be player vs player combat, and not this nonsensical race against the respawn timer of both NPCs and players.

IDK, call me naive, I do not care about ranking up efficiently, and I like moderate turtle AV more than flat out "ignore each other and full on PvE race to the finish line", but the amount of NPCs in Twow AV, their power, and their respawn rate just make turtles plain unfun after 1h~
