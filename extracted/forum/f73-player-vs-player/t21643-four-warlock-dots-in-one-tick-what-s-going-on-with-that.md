I think dots need a significant overhaul in pvp in general. They are too oppressive, and deal far too much damage for such a tiny action economy investment.

I can think of a few ways to do it. Significantly reduce dot duration on players, kinda like how sap and sheep were made to only be half duration vs players, but give it a slight dps boost to compensate for the added need to recast more often. This would make it so that dots require actual intent to maintain on someone rather than a fire and forget tab dotting of the entire enemy team.

Alternatively, limit dots to a single player, if you cast a dot on a new player, all instances of your dot on other players are immediately removed.

Neither of these changes would affect pve either, so no complaints from the pve crowd for that.
