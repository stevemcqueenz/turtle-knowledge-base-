# Resto Druids Are Bottom Tier – They Need Love

## Post 153169 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-25T16:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153169#p153169 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have been running a lot of arenas lately on both my disc priest (geared) and a resto druid alt (bad gear but still playable with the right teammate). And honestly resto feels like the weakest healer choice right now.

 **From my priest’s perspective it is a free win.** Priests strip every HoT. Shamans used to purge them dry, and even with the new purge CD (which helps a little) they still have plenty of ways to interrupt casts and negate CC. Paladins are even worse, because BoF and Cleanse remove every bit of CC and control a druid has. On top of that the recent buff to Blind means Abolish Poison no longer clears it. That was one of the few skillful counters druids could pull off and now it is gone.

 **TWOW has already taken plenty of abilities from later expansions.** Shamans got Water and Earth Shield. Priests got Chastise and Fear Ward baseline. Paladins gained buffs and quality-of-life improvements. Meanwhile resto druids got nothing. They need some form of unique advantage that makes it worth playing them, because right now there simply is not one.

 **In battlegrounds it feels even worse.** Facing mass locks stacking DoTs, I cannot understand why HoTs are not stackable. Why? If a warlock dots a team against a druid he should be worried that HoTs will negate that pressure. Instead druids crumble. Warlocks have too many tools. Endless DoTs, pets that silence, death coil, fear chains, curses. Meanwhile the druid toolkit offers nothing in return.

 **Back in TBC arenas I hated facing druids with Lifebloom and Cyclone.** They were oppressive. But in TWOW it feels like they actually need those tools just to have value. And honestly I think it would even be healthy for the current meta. Right now everything is so bursty that matches often end in mindless tunnel damage wiping people instantly. Cyclone would pause that flow, forcing players to think twice before charging headfirst into a druid team.

 **Some ideas that would make resto viable**

 * Cyclone for resto (and maybe balance) so they at least have one reliable CC and a way to slow down the burst meta
 * Barkskin usable while CC’d so they are not helpless vs deep fury warriors
 * Abolish Curse to deal with lock spam the same way Abolish Poison deals with poisons
 * Swiftmend tweaks like not consuming a HoT or being usable off the GCD
 * Another HOT like lifebloom + talents with anti dispel embedded (why do locks get this for their dots but not druids for their hots?)

And if I would be so bold, give druids a unique tool entirely. Something like a stun ward, akin to Fear Ward for priests, that would remove or prevent a stun once. Rogues are out of line right now, and a stun counter would be a perfect way for druids to carve out a unique identity.

Resto druid is meant to be the HoT healer but in the current bursty meta with constant dispels, warlock spam, and rogue stun chains this design just does not work. Unless they are given something to stand out they will remain an afterthought which is sad for a spec that should feel unique and rewarding.

Then there is the gear situation. The honor set is feral. The arena PvP sets have no leather healing options. I was planning to gear up my resto druid through PVP, but with the current state of the class it just feels pointless. Every other healer has a PvP path and druids got left out.

 **What do you guys think, have you managed to make resto work or is it really as bad as it seems?**
