The thing is tho, Warrior fear has a lot of counters:

 * Tremor
 * Wotf
 * Fear ward

Without these counters, yes, Warrior fear is extremely powerful. However, this is usually not the case.

Blind on the otherhand, it almost always secures 1n1 wins for Rogues since if you trinket kidney, you are blinded and the Rogue resets. Not to mention in group plays where blind can be used to shut down other players, and where the rogue can make other hack plays like vanish + sap them since they will probably be out of combat.

As a Priest with maxed survability (almost max resilience, 40% armor mitigation with innerfire, almost 5.5k HPjust waiting for R12 now which will unlock even more stamina + more resilience), geared Rogues hit extremely hard still and are impossible to fend off myself, especially if they are Undead. Having this amount of oppressive DPS along with extremely potent CC and with arguably the best survability in the game (stealth which allows you to choose favorable positions) makes for a really powerful mix.

However, on the other hand, balancing has always felt like they try to balance melees vs frost mages. From that POV, I understand a lot of the Rogue changes, but this means that it makes Rogues even more oppressive against immobile classes like Priests. Again, its a team game I know that, but I do feel like its important to lift these things. Personally, I would not mind this change as much if they gave other classes more tools like:

 * Priest Pain suppressions
 * Priest / Warr iceblock/divine shield mass dispel
 * More instant heal casts (Prayer of mending, lifebloom)
 * Shadow word death mechanic where you can skillfully use it to get out of polys (or sap)
