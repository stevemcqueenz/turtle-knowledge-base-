## Post 124261 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-28T17:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124261#p124261 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Unless your target wears gear with +defense (which noone does in meaningful quantities) 5% hit is all you need.

If you are dual wielding you will still miss plenty of swings past this point due to dualwields massive hit penalty, but its generally not worth overcapping hit. As after 5% all your abilites are hitting.

Normally it doesnt matter, but check if you actually have 300 weaponskill trained on your weapon of choice. But that should only affect pve hitchance. Worth investigating tho if nothing else helps.

Other than that, its worth noting that hitcapped does mean your attacks cant miss, but your oponent atill can parry, dodge and block them. Since those are separate mechanics
