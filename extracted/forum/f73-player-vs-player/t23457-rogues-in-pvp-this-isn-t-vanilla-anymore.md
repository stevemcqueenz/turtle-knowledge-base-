# Rogues in PvP: This Isn’t Vanilla Anymore

## Post 164118 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-12-31T11:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164118#p164118 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**TL;DR**

 * Rogues’ new poison talents push their burst far beyond “high” into “unhealable.”
 * Priest PvP is now literally unplayable against them.
 * Druid tools don’t fix it — even with detection, they kill you instantly.
 * Their kit has too much: CC, damage, mobility, ranged kick, unremovable Blind.
 * They’re overtuned in PvE and PvP.
 * If rogues delete people in 1–2 seconds, the entire concept of stealth loses meaning.

This needs to be tuned. Something needs to give.  **Suggestion:**
Just revert this new poison spec or tune its damage to match the other rogue builds. Right now it’s overtuned to the point of breaking PvP. I know the ongoing lawsuit must be taking priority and PvP isn’t the main focus right now, but please just retune rogues so the game becomes fun to play again.

 **Priest journey in PVP**

I’m fully aware that vanilla WoW was never a balanced game. Gear matters, skill matters, racials matter. Every class has its highs and lows. But throughout all of WoW’s history, rogues have been the outlier. It's the class that performs well even without gear. Fine. That’s part of their identity. What we’re dealing with now in TWOW is something completely different.

I started PvP on my Night Elf Priest. I got destroyed at first, naturally.
But over time I collected PvP gear, resilience bonuses, raid gear etc. Eventually I reached that point where I could tank a rogue if I played perfectly - and kill them if they werent UD. I still felt immobile, lacked defensive cooldowns, but at least I could play the game.

Not anymore.

Right now, the moment a rogue makes contact with any target; cloth, plate, bear, whatever.
Unhealable deletion. As a Priest I can’t heal through it. As a Druid I can’t heal through it.
I can’t save my teammates. I can’t save myself. At this point, if the enemy team has a rogue, my Priest may as well AFK at the GY. That’s how useless the class feels.

And yes, everyone knows me. I’ve been around long enough. I’ve played through Horde’s overtuned racials, through WotF/Tremor/Horde fearward nonsense, through a meta that has always been rough for Priest. But this isn’t “rough.” This is unplayable.

 **“Just reroll” and so I did. And guess what?**

I rerolled Druid. More mobility, more utility, stealth detection, Faerie Fire. The tools you’d expect to counter a stealth class. But detecting a rogue changes nothing.

People see me pop a rogue out and go “omg he’s dead.” No. He’s not. Because:

 * he still has all cooldowns
 * he still has absurd reach
 * he still reaches me regardless
 * and I’m dead in 1–2 seconds anyway

Even being the counter-class doesn’t counter them. This kit is doing way too much. The new poison talents pushed it over the edge. A rogue now has:

 * absurd damage
 * too much CC
 * a ranged interrupt that also slows
 * Blind that can’t be removed anymore
 * the best opener in the game
 * the best escape in the game
 * the best ability to pick any fight
 * and the best ability to avoid any bad fight

If you’re going to give a class THIS much damage, then something else in their kit needs to go.
Right now, they do everything better than everyone. If they kill everything on contact… why do they even need stealth? Genuinely. With my FF's they still run around killing people. Others in this forum act as if them getting FF'd destroys them completely. If the moment they reach a target that target is dead, what’s the role of stealth anymore? What’s the counterplay? What exactly are the rest of us supposed to do? Right now the only viable thing to do is to bring your own Rogues and have them stunlock enemy rogues to death.
