# Vanilla was bursty by design

## Post 129346 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-05-02T07:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129346#p129346 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Lot of people complain about CC2 and its "burst" meta. But lets be honest, burst was good even before CC2. PoM-Pyro mages, enh shamans, ele shamans, shockadins, seduction-soulfire warlocks. CC2 just added burst to more classes/specs.
Some also might notice that resilence in later patches made heals stronger and fights longer.

Here is my point: vanilla pvp was bursty by design

Imagine wow without bursts.
In this game, group pvp will look like DPS race. Your team inflict damage, enemy team outheal this damage. If damage > heals, then your team wins.

You might say "but we can CC healer". Right, but all CC can be dispelled in one way or another. What would healer do in the next second after releasing from CC? Right - he will heal.
If enemy team can CC, then your team can CC too. Damage dealers can be CCed and their target can be outhealed during that short period of inactivity.
If played right, your team can outheal any inflicted damage taken. See "turtling" in WSG base. At some gear level, team can just stay near graveyard and fight endlessly.

Vanilla devs came to conclusion like "to prevent long boring fights with DPS race, we allow some classes to inflict heavy damage in short timeframes".
As a result, in Vanilla your class had burst or some pressure against healing: Mortal Strike, Viper Sting, Wound Poisons.

If you look at pure pvp games, you might notice that there is few ways to restore health, but there are always some way to nuke enemy player.
Team Fortress - you need to exit "front line", travel to specific point and pickup health. Skilled engineer can make turtling at some points of the map. But there are classes with huge burst like Sniper or Soldier.
DotA - healing fountains are present in base so you need to exit "front line" and travel back in order to heal. Very few classes can heal others in combat.
Mortal Combat - no heals at all.
For Honor - you can heal at some points of the map. Though, fights here are bursty like in Mortal combat. You can't heal full during fight.
Counter Strike - headshots say "hello".
Hell, even chess are "bursty". If one figure attacks other, defender can't survive at all. It just dies and get removed from table.

So in conclusion, bursty pvp is a norm. It is design proven by multiple other games.

The intro is almost a false equivalence. Just because Vanilla had burst doesn't mean it's fine for Turtle to be even more bursty. Nuance matters. The extent, accessibility, and frequency of burst is clearly much higher in Turtle now, due to CC2, new gear, and general power creep.

Besides, even though Vanilla was bursty, it almost always had trade-offs. Sure, you could kill someone with 2x Pyroblasts, but you were limited to a certain spec and setup, could only do it every 3 minutes, and you weren't always able to pull it off. Yes, Shamans/Paladins could one-shot you if the stars aligned, but otherwise their damage was fairly low and they were susceptible to getting shut down. Etc. etc.

Furthermore, I believe the old PvP-sets had especially high Stamina on them to counter burstiness, but as far as I know the new endgame gear in Turtle will have power that far outpaces that Stamina.

I'm also not convinced the PvP trinket change was a net positive. The original design leaned more into a rock-paper-scissors meta, where you couldn't trinket everything. That gave us better tools to shut down burst through strategic CC; now it's harder to do so.

Even with some classes burst windows getting intentionally or unintentionally nerfed (spellbatching for frostmage and elemental shaman crits being removed, reckoning reverted, Raptorstrike Critchance significantly lowered)

The massive influx of gear and class changes for higher average DPS have substantially changed the pvp landscape. What used to be freak oneshits like tripple crit windfury of shaman or reckoning bombs that required long time in combat to stack up has traded places with on demand burst.

Shamans have a over 60% chance to see their windfury in the first 3 seconds of combat. Arcane mages have a spell that hits substantially harder than Pyro (thanks to 220% crit multiplier and spell windfury for up to 185% normal damage) on a 2.5 second casttime that can even be shortened substantially due to their haste interaction.
(Yes its possible for that single spell if stars align to do 5-6k burst damage)
Paladins next to remaining similarly unkillable as they were boast holy strike which hits about as hard as holy shock used to. With a third of its cooldown. While SoR scales 2-3x as well with spellpower as it did back in the day.

There are choice examples of classes whose burst got worse, but the general trend is very clearly up. And while classic wows pvp was always bursty, it was never that intense. And the trend only is getting worse as more Kara gear with high offense and low stamina makes its way around.

And there is one giant problem with oneshot pvp. It is not fun to be on the receiving end of it repeatedly. And with all the powercreep what used to be a rare occurance has become ever more commonplace. If i wanna have engage times of 2-3 seconds id play a shooter where im back in action 20 secs after i died. In wow that process of rezzing buffing and riding back to the front takes minutes.
