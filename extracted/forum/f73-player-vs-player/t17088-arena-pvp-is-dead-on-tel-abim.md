# Arena PvP is DEAD on Tel'Abim

## Post 116567 by Winrath — 2025-01-13T11:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116567#p116567 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's sad to see this but gradually we see that there is no incentive for people to do Arenas anymore.

People rather queue battleground for their honor instead of Arenas.

Arena PvP needs a serious revamp. My suggestions are:

 - Introduce  2v2 bracket
 - Introduce Rated Arenas
 - Update the Arena Gear (change the way the gear can be obtained)
 - Increase the arena honor gained substantially
 - Allow matchups between teams of the same faction
 - Spawn consumes on map to reveal invisibility to avoid griefers
 - Optimize the Queue so groups won't have to wait for solo queuers.

The introduction of rated arenas should be only for Tel'Abim, being a PvP server. This change will bring an influx of well needed players.

People that have the Arena gear on Tel'Abim took advantage of a bug when the server was launched and got their gear even before hitting 60. The fact that is almost impossible to get it now with the current system and queues on Tel'Abim makes the situation quite regrettable.

I know doing this comes with implications of balancing and gearing.
I know the team has already their plate full of TODO tasks.

BUT
Every single PvPer on the server would welcome these changes.
It will bring more diversity of things to do in the game.

Thank you
