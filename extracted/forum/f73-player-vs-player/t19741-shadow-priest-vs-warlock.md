## Post 134460 by Halfgeek9 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-06T01:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134460#p134460 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you ask Spriests, they will say Warlocks far better. More mobile, can kite endlessly with CoEX, can shut down casters with Curse of Tongues, has a pet that can dispel magic on you, while you're stunned or CC by magic like Paladin hammers, and Warlocks can get Ruin for huge crits to instant gib people.

If you ask Warlocks, they will whine that Spriests are better Affliction locks, with more defensive tools, a better dispel, and can always pop out to heal.

IMO, having played both, and main Warlock since 2003, Spriest is a one trick pony. A Warlock has multiple good specs, and hence, playstyles. Spriest gets old & boring real fast.

Plus, Turtle nerfed spriest with VE. While Warlocks got buffed with CoEX, and 2 curses active on one target. ie. Agony damages while you can Shadow, Tongue, CoEX.

Edit: To add, Turtle nerfed Spriest further with Blackout 3s stun -> 2s. It's even more difficult to peel melee on top of you, and Warriors in Turtle gain rage hitting Shields absorbs. But Priest have a viable Smite build when you get ultra geared, just lacking huge fat crits since spell crit only 1.5x not 2x like other casters.
