How to enter Alterac Valley? [CUSTOM CONTENT]

On Turtle WoW you can access AV from any place in the world by clicking Battleground Finder icon (it's somewhere near the minimap by default). Level 51 is required to join.

How to win?

To win Alterac Valley battleground, your team must defeat enemy's faction boss: Vanndar Stormpike or Drek'Thar. Sounds like an easy job but it is not.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/t5Xm7NY.png)

The boss sits in the building surrounded by his elite guard at the core of enemy base deep in enemy's territory. He has more than 100 000 HP and hits very hard.

The boss is guarded by Marshals/War Masters in his building. Practically, slaying boss without killing all of these guardians is impossible. So before you attack the boss, first you must lure out Marshals/War Masters and slay them.

But before you do it, you should first capture the graveyard at enemy's base - without respawn your team can't do much. The way to graveyard is filled with numerous NPCs so you won't have an easy job.

But it's not all. The base is also guarded by 2 Towers/Bunkers where the archers shoot anyone on a line of sight. Without these points burnt, the base will be hard to capture.

And before all of that, you must travel a long way to enemy's base without dying.

This guide will delve into all the details later.

Armor Scraps to do: image of armor scraps icon, blacksmiths location

Armor Scraps are the quest items you can get from almost any enemy NPC in Alterac Valley. You can turn in large quantities of these to permanently upgrade your team's NPCs.

You turn them in to blacksmith NPC in your base (Murgot Deepforge in Alliance and Smith Regzar in Horde).

There are 4 levels of strength of your NPCs:

- 1. Normal - game starts with this level of troops.

- 2. Seasoned

- 3. Veteran

- 4. Champion
Each next level grants your NPCs more damage and health massively increasing your team's defense.

 500 Armor Scraps are needed to upgrade troops to the next level - 1500 total. After turning in 500 Armor Scraps to the blacksmith, the new dialogue will appear. Click it so new stronger units will join the game.

After upgrading your troops to Champion ones, Armor Scraps become pointless because the Champion units stay in the match forever.

Good places to farm Armor Scraps are Coldtooth Mine and Irondeep Mine as well as surrounding of Captain Balinda and Captain Galvangar. Armor Scraps drop from Troggs, Kobolds and enemy NPCs.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) Lokholar the Ice Lord and ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) Ivus the Forest Lord to do: blood and crystal image, shaman/druid location, summon location

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/esZ0GsQ.png)

Lokholar the Ice Lord and Ivus the Forest Lord are probably the most iconic and powerful NPCs in the entire Alterac Valley. Each faction can summon their own mighty elemental that will lead their team to the victory.

 **How to summon?**

Horde can collect Stormpike's Soldier Blood and Alliance can collect Storm Crystal from any enemy dead player or NPC. These items have 100% drop chance though only one random player nearby can loot these. If farm the items for yourself, it's recommended to loot enemy corpses away from your team mates.

Your team must bring 200x of these items to ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) Primalist Thurloga in the cave in the Frostwolf Village or to ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) Arch Druid Renferal who is located behind the Dun Baldar North Bunker.

You can ask these NPCs via dialogue to find out how many items are still needed to start the ritual of summoning. They won't directly tell you the number but below is the approximate formula:

First dialogue "still much work to do" tells you that your team has collected below 100 items, second one "I've made contact" informs you that your team has already turned in more than 100 but less than 150 items. Last dialogue "summon is near" informs you that there are less than 50 items needed to start the summon.

After turning in last item, the Shamans or Druids start moving to the place of summoning. Be ware. If they die to enemies during the escort, the summoning will fail. These locations are: ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) small hill north of Galvangar's building or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) at the north-eastern edge of Field of Strife.

When Shamans or Druids are there, everyone in your team can click the circle to be part of the ritual of summoning. In order to summon the elemental, your team needs 10 clickers at the same time. When 10 people simultaneously click the circle, after a few seconds the Lokholar or Ivus appears and moves to the middle of the map.

 **The route**

They stop in the Field of Strife for about 3 minutes.
They soon start moving into enemy's territory. Their next stop is ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) Stormpike Graveyard or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) Frostwolf Village.
Next check-points are Aid Station or Relief Hut though usually enemy players kite them to the boss room so it rarely happens. Lastly they go to the Vanndar's or Drek'Thar's room.

 **Loot**

When elemental dies, it drops some unique [items](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13419). It's worth to note that [Cold Forged Blade](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=19110) is the only item with this look in the entire game.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/SytENJh.png)

 **Tips**

- Elemental Lord is very good choice to break stalemate.

- Good spots to farm Stormpike's Soldier Blood and Storm Crystals are Irondeep Mine and Coldtooth Mine supposing enemy team has captured these points. NPCs there have low HP and this area isn't crowded.

- Once your team summons the elemental, you can buff them.

- Entrance caves are good spots to kite elementals - your friendly NPCs will help you much.

- Healing the elemental should be priority for every nearby player that has healing spells.

- Hunters can kite the elementals. If your elemental is being kited by enemy Hunter, you should focus on the Hunter and stop him from ruining your team's effort.

Cavalry to do: cavalry banner in the title

Alterac Valley is the place where swift Frostwolves and mighty Alterac Rams dwell. These animals are used by Frostwolf Clan and Stormpike Guard as loyal mounts for their trained troops. Your team can tame these animals in order to summon the cavalry that will pierce through enemy defense.

 **How to summon?**

In order to summon the cavalry, your team must meet 2 conditions:
- Tame ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) 25 [Frostwolves](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=10981) or 25 ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Alterac Rams](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=10990)

- Turn in ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) 25 [Alterac Ram Hides](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17642) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) 25 [Frostwolf Hides](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17643)
 **Part 1:**
First, you must take the quest to tame Frostwolf or Alterac Ram. It can be picked up from ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Frostwolf Stable Master](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13616) located in the stables east of Frostwolf Village or from ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Stormpike Stable Master](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13617) who stays in the base of Dun Baldar.
You will receive the item that allows you to tame the animal. Use the item on the animal and after taming is complete, escort it back to the stables. Talk to NPC and turn in the quest.

 **Part 2:**
Second, you must turn in ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) 25 [Alterac Ram Hides](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17642) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) 25 [Frostwolf Hides](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17643) to ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Frostwolf Wolf Rider Commander](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13441) or to ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Stormpike Ram Rider Commander](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13577).
Unlike in the first part, you don't need to accept the quest to loot these items. You can loot all 25 items and then turn them in to that NPC.

Beware!
- There is no way to find out exactly how many animals are still needed to be tamed. However, every 5 animals you tame, the animals appear in the stables. When stables are full - that means there are 5 more animals needed to be tamed. It's visual bug.

- There is no way to find out exactly how many hides are still needed for your team to complete the objective. That's why it's recommended to turn in all 25 at once.
When both conditions are fulfilled, talk to the same Commander that gave you the quest for hides. Special dialogue will appear. Click it and observe how the cavalry charges and destroyes everything that stands in their way.

 **Tips**

- Riders move very fast and their route goes by all graveyards. Make sure to follow them and capture the graveyards along the way.

- Although Riders don't as much damage as upgraded Ground Assaults, they stick together and are very tanky. If they all focus on one target, they can still one-shot an undergeared player.

- The best strategy to fight cavalry is to slow and separate them.

- Summoning cavalry is one of the easiest things low level players can do to influence the outcome of the game.

- All Riders can be buffed.

- It's possible to kill enemy's tamed Wolves or Rams that sit inside the stables to stop enemy from summoning the cavalry.

Infantry [CUSTOM CONTENT]

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/RkJLzTu.png)

Infantry - also known as Ground Assaults - is the elite group of ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) Reavers or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) Commandos that are the strongest soldiers of Frostwolf Clan or Stormpike Guard. They are possibly the strongest summonable units in the Alterac Valley.

The infantry consists of 11 NPCs that unlike the cavalry hit extremely hard and are more numerous. Their power scales with the upgrade level of your troops. Units with no upgrade have much less armor and deal significantly less damage. Thus, it's recommended to summon the infantry only if you upgrade the troops first.
Comparison:

- Stormpike Commando (no upgrade):
 - Level: 56 -- 9986 HP - Damage: 335-432 - Armor: 2 699 - Resistance: 0

- Champion Commando (Champion upgrade):
 - Level: 61 - 11632 HP - Damage: 559-720 - Armor: 4 091 - Resistance: 5

 **How to summon?**

There are 3 steps your team must do in order to summon the infantry.

- 1. Complete ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Irondeep Supplies](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=6985) quest or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Coldtooth Supplies](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=6982) quest 4 times. [CUSTOM CONTENT]

- 2. Activate dialogue and receive special item.

- 3. Turn in the item to ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Warmaster Garrick](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13449) or to ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Field Marshal Teravaine](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13446).

Step 1:
First, you must accept the quest from ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Frostwolf Quartermaster](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=12097) or from ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Stormpike Quartermaster](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=12096). You have choice between going to the Coldooth Mine and Irondeep Mine - always select the Mine that is further away from your base. That's because completing "harder" quest once makes as much progress as completing easier one 4 times. It makes much difference if you want to save time. But if for some reason you wanted to farm supplies from the closer and safer Mine, you would have to complete the quest 16 times. That's a bit boring if you ask me!

[CUSTOM CONTENT] It's worth to note that these numbers are different from WoW Classic where 7 and 28 quests respectively are needed to summon the infantry.

Then, you must delve into the Mine and if it's not controlled by your team, you must kill enemy leader inside. Once it's done, you are able to grab supplies from the boxes. Go back to the Quartermaster once you grab all 10 supplies.

Step 2:
If you complete 4 turn ins, special dialogue will appear when you talk to the Quartermaster.
After clicking it, you receive ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Frostwolf Assault Orders](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17442) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Stormpike Assault Orders](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17353). The item has 15 minutes duration so make sure not to hesitate and go to the next step.

Step 3:
After you receive the item, infatry's leader will spawn. You must bring him the orders you received from Quartermaster. You will find ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Warmaster Garrick](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13449) north-west of Captain Galvangar's building and south of Snowfall Graveyard and ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Field Marshal Teravaine](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13446) to the south of Stonehearth Bunker at the ballista. Complete the quest and infantry will spawn. Then, it will automatically move through the all enemy graveyards up to the last boss.

 **Tips**

- You can buff all of these units.

- Running across the map takes much time. To save it, use ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [Frostwolf Insignia](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17690) or ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Stormpike Insignia](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17691) to at least swiftly return to your base.

- Infantry (if upgraded) is the most powerful summonable force in Alterac Valley. It's shines as stalemate-breaker.

- Currently there is a bug on Turtle WoW that doesn't let you summon infantry more than once in the same match.

- When you fight infantry, it's good to separate the units and kite them to your NPCs so they can help you.

- Summoning can be synchronized with cavalry or elemental - combined power might be unstoppable.

Ryson's All Seeing Eye [CUSTOM CONTENT] to do: Syndicate image, orb in troll's cave, turtle's AV map to note where syndicate hides and where is trolls cave

[CUSTOM CONTENT] Ryson's All Seeing Eye is side objective that was originally in Alterac Valley in patch 1.5 but was later removed and is unknown by most WoW Classic players. Turtle WoW restored this objective.

East of Field of Strife and behind Gnoll encampment there is a hidden cave in which Syndicate has a hideout. Their boss: [Syndicate Master Ryson](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13151) gives you a quest to recover [Master Ryson's All Seeing Eye](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17354) - powerful relic that was stolen by Winterax Trolls.

The item can be found inside Winterax Hold cave. Once you find the Eye, you must carry it back to the Master Ryson. The carrier of the eye, however, is cursed. You start to glow and your appearance will surely attracts other players' attention. Though you also get extra buff: with the Eye on you, you see invisible units from a distance. It's very useful buff if you want to detect enemy Rogues or Druids.

When you go back to Master Ryson and turn in the Eye, you will unlock new dialogue options.

The first one grants you 30 minutes buff that allows you to detect invisible units from a distance - without glow effect. This effect persists through death.

The second one is continuation of this objective. Master Ryson can give you a [Ryson's Beacon](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17362) that you can place in enemy base courtyard. Once placed there, it must be protected for one minute. When it's completed, Ryson's falcon spawns over enemy base and allows you to have view on enemy from your own base. This part of the objective is practically useless but it flavours how Syndicate can help you in fighting over Alterac Valley.

 **Tips**

- The quest can be completed once per AV match.

- If you carry the Eye and mount up, you drop the item - then it can be repicked by anyone.

- Sometimes it's not worth to complete the quest and keep the Eye for your team. For example to detect enemy stealthers inside your base.

- When you die carrying the Eye, the Eye is dropped and can be repicked by anyone.

- There is only one Eye.

Korrak the Bloodrager [CUSTOM CONTENT] to do: korrak image

[CUSTOM CONTENT] Korrak the Bloodrager was originally added to the game in patch 1.5 but was later removed and is unknown by most WoW Classic players. Turtle WoW restored his presence though with a few modifications to the original.

[Korrak the Bloodrager](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=12159) is a powerful Troll that guards the Snowfall Graveyard and the bridge to Winterax Hold. He drops unique loot for example [[Mark of Deflection]](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=17108). His loot table is also shared with other world bosses so technically Korrak can be treated as a world boss.

Loot, however, isn't the only reason to kill Korrak. This mighty Troll is the objective of the quests: ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [The Legend of Korrak](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=7181) and ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Korrak the Bloodrager](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13841). Blue items from these quests are one of the best items you can get in the game without raiding - and the quest can be completed at level 51!

[CUSTOM CONTENT] These quests also give you [[Winterax Ritual Dagger]](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=50743) that is completely custom item made by Turtle WoW team. Moreover, once the quest is completed, you get access to repeatable version of this quest that gives you only the dagger: ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Tt49ZgY.gif) [The Return of Korrak](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=7381) and ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif) [Korrak the Everliving](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=7382).

These quests can be picked up from NPCs in your base in Alterac Valley: [Warmaster Laggrond](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13840) and [Lieutenant Haggerdin](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=13841). To complete the quest, your team must kill Korrak and loot his skull. However, only one person can loot the skull each match - the drop goes to random person who has quest active.

Let's delve into how [Winterax Ritual Dagger] works. The description is mysterious and doesn't tell us much about the item.

First of all, the item can be activated only in Alterac Valley. When you use it, one effect is certain: you will die. The other effects depend whether you have Korrak's debuff on you. The debuff is named [Bathed in Blood](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?spell=46437) and you have it applied if Korrak dies close to you. The debuff passively does nothing and lasts 1 hour but is required in order to activate other effects of using the dagger.

- If you use the dagger and you don't have debuff on you, you will die and nothing happens.

- If you use the dagger and you have debuff on you:
 - if there is no friendly player close to you, you die and everyone in your team is summoned to the location you used the dagger.

 - if there is at least one friendly player close to you, they all get bonus 30% damage dealt buff for one minute.

 **Tips**

- [Skull of Korrak](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=18148) drops to only one person in one game.

- The dagger can summon whole team to your location. Rogues and Druids can stay in stealth to safely travel to enemy base and there summon their whole team. This way you can drastically shorten the length of the match and guarantee your team easy win.

- If you die, the debuff fades from you making the item useless in that match.

- You can stack the daggers in your inventory from multiple games. If you already have one dagger in your inventory before you kill the Korrak, you don't need to immediately return to your base to get next dagger - this can make your action quicker.

- Winterax Ritual Dagger, in good hands, can deeply influence the outcome of the game and even let weaker team win the match.

- Currently there is an exploit in the game that allows any ranged class [to solo kill Korrak using bad pathing under the bridge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UjwCpH5npVg).
