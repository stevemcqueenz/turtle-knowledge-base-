## Post 130154 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-08T06:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130154#p130154 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I agree that enchants should be ilvl-restricted, but unfortunately it's probably not happening for two reasons:

 -Items that already have high-level enchants applied to them would have to be removed manually, which is a huge undertaking. You would basically have to un-enchant almost every item on every twink character. It's likely possible to automate this.

 -How would current twinks be compensated for their lost enchants? Imagine dishing out several thousand gold to set up your twink, and logging in the next day to find all your enchants gone. That would be really shitty, and would cause a huge backlash and wave of whining.

If TWoW implemented enchanting vellums that could store enchants in consumable item form, they could possibly just add those to the twink's inventory when removing it from their items, so they could re-sell it on AH and recoup some of the cost. But enchanting vellums are probably not happening either.

An alternative would be to just add the current AH value of all mats used in the enchants to the twink's gold total, but that has its own problems - like ever-changing AH prices, and enchants that can't be valued accurately like the Naxx enchants.

It's not impossible to see this implemented at some point, but I don't see it happening any time soon.

If, however, it were to happen, I would love to try out twinking. The upfront cost of endgame enchants has always put me off from trying it. I imagine more people are in the same camp, so it could revitalize the twinking scene with new blood.
