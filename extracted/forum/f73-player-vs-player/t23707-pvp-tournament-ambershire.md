PRIZE 600-1000G

FREE REGISTRATION

BEST OF 3

Hello everyone! This is the first-ever event for this server, and it will coincide with the BWL (Blackwing Lair) release.
This is just the beginning! Moving forward, we plan to host events every 1 or 2 weeks. While the prize pool for this first event is 600 gold, it will likely increase to 1000 gold for future editions.

Our GMs have officially given their consent to organize these events, so I am here to ask for your support: please sign up as soon as registrations open!

Help Wanted: Feedback & Ideas
The rules are currently under development, and I want this to be a community effort. I am not looking for gold or personal rewards; I am looking for your input. I’d love to hear your thoughts on:
Rule suggestions to keep things fair and fun.
Location ideas within the world to host the games.
General event concepts or creative ideas.
If you’d like to help or share your thoughts, please reach out to me in-game: Asgaren.

Thanks to everyone for your support!
Your Mage, Asgaren :)
