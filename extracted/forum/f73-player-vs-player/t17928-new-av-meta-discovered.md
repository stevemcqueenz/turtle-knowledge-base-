# New AV meta discovered

## Post 122137 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-09T15:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122137#p122137 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One week ago devs finally fixed one custom quest that is obtainable in Alterac Valley. Before the update, this quest actually didn't work properly: it could be completed only once.

- **Korrak the Everliving** and The **Return of Korrak** are now repeatable quests. However, the **Skull of Korrak** is no longer a party drop.

The quest can be taken from Warmaster Laggrond or Lieutenant Haggerdin in your faction's base in AV. Your objective is to kill Korrak the Bloodrager and loot his skull. However, skull can be picked up only by one random player near Korrak. If you are the only player with the quest, you will have 100% drop chance.
It's important to have debuff applied when Korrak dies near you. This debuff is essential to properly perform the ritual in the future. It has blood icon and 1 hour duration.
When you obtain the Skull of Korrak, go back to your base and turn it in to the quest giver. Then, you will receive [[Winterax Ritual Dagger]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=50743)

Winterax Ritual Dagger is the item you can click to perform the ritual. You must have the debuff on you - otherwise the ritual won't work. When you click it, it can do 2 things based on whether you have any friendly players nearby:

 1) if there is NO a friendly player close to you:
- You die. Everyone in your team in AV receives the summon to the place you are in.
 2) if there is a friendly player close to you:
- You die. Everyone close to you gets 30% damage buff and movement speed bonus for one minute.
This video is self-explanatory why I think this item becomes the new meta in AV.

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/xqTsfb9fRIU]

As you can see the carrier of the Winterax Ritual Dagger can possibly summon everyone to the last graveyard and end the game in next 10 minutes.

EDIT - Patch 1.18.0

It seems the dagger was nerfed in patch 1.18 although it isn't noted anywhere in patch notes. Now to receive the summon, you must have "Bathed in Blood" debuff on you - the debuff you get when your character is close to Korrak's death. Practically, it means not everyone will receive the summon.

So in theory this meta can still be exploited but it requires much more team work. When the game starts, everyone from your team should go to Korrak instead of Stonehearth/Iceblood GY.  That is needed in order for your team to be summoned later. It's hard task because usually people in AV aren't well coordinated - everyone joins solo and some people don't even read chat.
