# Warmode still doesnt make leveling with pvp viable

## Post 169087 by Biggamehuntard — 2026-02-21T04:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169087#p169087 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey guys, Ive never made a forum post but id like to point something out:

On Nordanaar, With nobody using warmode to level in the open world and all the actual juicy xp tied to objectives in bgs and twinking, theres nothing to actually allow for the average joe to get ANY xp from the pvp system. The world pvp scene is completely dead and in bgs, all xp gain in tied to objective completion if you disregard the tiny sliver you get at the end of it for winning or losing. Its like 10% of the total potential value, worse yet, you get things like level 19,29, and 59 twinks that sit and camp honor at their bracket preventing you from ever actually getting objectives done. AB is your best bet and even then if you dont have any twinks of your own, you're not getting any through the tiny trickle of resource points that you'll get as the guy in several thousand gold of gear slams you again and again.

So, my proposal is pretty simple. Reduce the xp gain on objectives and add a small amount for pvp kills in bgs. that way when you gang up and kill enemies, you can actually still make up for objectives being insurmountable again our really expensive friends without removing twinking as a whole from them. WE all know nobody is getting the pvp kill xp in the world anymore and with everything being moved to bgs anyway, why isnt that feature already present?

Additionally, Allow me to suggest pvp quests for getting honor kills in bgs, objective capture, and maybe even something like "capture 3 flags in warsong gulch yourself" or "play a bg without dying once" or something that also rewards xp and is on quartermasters for pickup.

I dont think these are too big of an ask and it wouldnt impact much of anything. Thanks for reading! :D
