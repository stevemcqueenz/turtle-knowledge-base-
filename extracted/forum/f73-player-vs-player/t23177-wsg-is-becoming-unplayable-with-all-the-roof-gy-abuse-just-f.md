## Post 161908 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-12-07T13:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161908#p161908 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**In the spirit of WoW Classic PLUS, can we please remove this crap and make WSG fun again?**

Classic Plus is supposed to be about **better gameplay, better design, and actually moving the game forward**, not about who can abuse tunnel roofs, glitch through the Horde GY, or phase through walls the best.

Right now WSG feels less like a battleground and more like a competition of:
 **“who knows the most broken geometry exploits.”**
That’s not skill. That’s not fun. And it definitely isn’t **Classic PLUS**.

WSG should be about **teamplay**, strategy, coordination, clutch runs and clutch returns, not chasing someone who reached a spot only accessible through unintended bugs from 2005.

If Classic Plus is really about improving the game experience, then **fix these exploits**. Close the walls, patch the GY jumps, stop the roof glitching. Make WSG fair, fun, and competitive again without needing a library of exploits to keep up.
