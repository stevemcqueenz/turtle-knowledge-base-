# PvP Reward Overhaul & New Gear

## Post 141512 by Stayclose — 2025-07-26T08:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141512#p141512 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

PvP Reward Overhaul & New Gear:
 * New exclusive Arena sets and weapons.

 * Existing rank-based PvP sets and weapons requirements for acquisition will be reduced by 2 ranks:
 -Lieutenant's/Champion's blue boots/gloves will become available starting from Rank 5.
 -Field Marshal’s/Warlord’s epics will become available starting from Rank 10.
 -Weapons will become available from Rank 12.

 * Resilience: All PvP gear (Rank sets, Arena sets) will feature the Resilience stat, reducing damage taken from players.
 * Lieutenant's/Champion's blue(rare) Battleground sets will become as effective in PvP as epic pve sets due to Resilience.
 * Field Marshal’s/Warlord’s (Epic) sets and new Arena sets will surpass all PvE gear in player-versus-player scenarios.

These changes are desperately needed. The current PvP gearing system is absolute madness compared to PvE. Lieutenant's/Champion's (Rare) boots/gloves require weeks of daily BG farming for just two pieces!
Reducing rank requirements (Rank 5 for Rare sets) will finally let regular players obtain basic PvP gear.

Hi all

I'd like to add a suggestion that builds on what has already been raised.
Turtle has already introduced spec-based variants of Tier 1 to 3 PvE gear so that each class can obtain items that fit their intended role (e.g. Holy/Prot/Ret for Paladins). It feels like a missed opportunity that similar **spec-based variants for PvP gear** do not exist.

A Holy Paladin who commits to the long grind for epic PvP gear should be able to obtain plate that supports a healing playstyle through healing power and spell crit.

In a similar way, there should be **more healing focused Rank 14 weapon options**. A shield with healing power for example would make the Rank 14 grind far more rewarding for dedicated healers. At the moment the climb to Rank 14 is not especially attractive for a healer because only a few of the final rewards are suitable for healers.

Introducing healer oriented Rank 14 items together with role specific PvP armour would make the entire PvP journey far more appealing. I believe this would attract more healers into battlegrounds which would make matches more enjoyable for everyone. Healthy healer participation always improves match balance and helps the whole system feel more dynamic.

Why this would be beneficial
• It brings PvP rewards in line with the existing philosophy used for PvE gear.
• It rewards healers for the significant time and effort required to push high PvP ranks.
• It strengthens battleground balance by supporting all roles properly.
• It creates clear motivation for more players to queue as healers which improves gameplay for everyone.
