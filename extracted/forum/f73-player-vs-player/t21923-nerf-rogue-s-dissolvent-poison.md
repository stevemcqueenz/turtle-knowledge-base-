Dissolvent poison by itself isn’t really an issue — the problem is how the spec works. You are gona use Envenom to spent combo points, which gives you +30% poison proc chance. That’s why everyone is using fast weapons. But the funny part is that 1 CP = 12 seconds. It literally requires zero setup — you open with stun, ambush, or garrote, and you already have a 12+ second buff. In that time, you can kill anyone.

I respect team for trying something new with Assassination, but this is just a bad idea. When your passive ability does over 60% of your total damage, there’s clearly a problem. You just can’t design it that way.

The simplest and fast “nerf/fix” before reworking the spec. would be to make Dissolvent deal physical damage — but that wouldn’t solve the main issue.

Poisons should be supportive, not your main source of damage.They should rework Envenom to be like in TBC/WotLK — I mean a buff that increases your poison proc chance based on combo points, 1s+1 second per CP. If rogues want to use Dissolvent after that for more passive damage, that’s fine. But for more damage over time or in PvE, you’d use Deadly Poison like in TBC/WotLK. Envenom can still hit hard, but it should require proper setup.
