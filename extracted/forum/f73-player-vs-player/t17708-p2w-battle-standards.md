# P2W Battle Standards

## Post 120712 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-23T12:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120712#p120712 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Devs,

I understand that you don't pay much attention to PvP on this server.
However, there is one small issue that requires urgent fix because it makes the PvP "Pay To Win".

A few months ago Race Change Tokens returned to the donation shop.
There is one bug that allows you to buy both Battle Standards that can be used in battlegrounds simultaneously.
This bug is only accessible to people who pay their real money to the server.

This Battle Standard works like Shaman's totem: it has 1500 HP and it increases your party's total health by 15% for 2 minutes.
This item existed by default in Vanilla.
Actually, there were 2 items: one accessible to the Horde and other one accessible to the Alliance.
However, in Vanilla you couldn't change your faction by changing race because it's Turtle WoW custom feature.

With return of the Race Change Token in the shop, players can purchase for example Horde Battle Standard with their first Orc, then switch to the Dwarf and purchase Alliance Battle Standard.

Then, these players are able to place both of these Battle Standards in battlegrounds at the same time.
The bug isn't still well known, however, it has been reported already on a bug tracker.
Here you can find all the details: <https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=10869>

What deeply concerns me though is that the issue was reported 3 months ago.
I wouldn't be writing this post if you fixed that bug. It seems to be easy to fix.
I don't assume that you intentionally let this bug be to intentionally allow P2W on the server so the people interested in PvP spend extra $$$ on your server.
In contrast I assume you tend to ignore important PvP bugs without even understanding them.

By the way, it's not the only bug related with PvP that leaves its content broken.
There is one bugged quest in Alterac Valley that doesn't let you buff your team more than once per character (<https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=6475>). Bug is 1 year old and still not fixed.
A few days ago we finally got queue bug fixed after 2 years.
About 1 year ago there was new meta to play Alterac Valley discovered that relied on using backdoor that was accessible to only one faction - it was fixed after a few months of abusing instead of days.

What I mean is the PvP issues are being (if ever) fixed after unreasonably long amount of time.

Please fix Battle Standards bug as fast as possible. I think everyone doesn't want such P2W to be available on Turtle WoW.
