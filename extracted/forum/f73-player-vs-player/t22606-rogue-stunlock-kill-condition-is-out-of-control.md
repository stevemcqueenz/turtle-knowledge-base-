# Rogue stunlock kill condition is out of control

## Post 157314 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-10-27T14:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157314#p157314 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It has reached a point where the stun-lock kill condition from rogues is completely disproportionate. If a rogue opens with Cheap Shot into Kidney Shot, many classes have no functional counterplay. The fight ends before the opposing player is allowed to act. That design philosophy removes skill expression from the interaction.

If rogues are allowed to maintain this instant kill pressure while the opponent is stunned, every class needs significant buffs to protection mechanics. Examples include:

• Nature’s Grasp or Bear Form should trigger while stunned.
• Warriors should be able to activate a shield while stunned.
• Priests should be able to apply shield while stunned and/or get tools like pain suppression.
• All classes should have at least one reactive survival tool that is not disabled by the stun itself.

The stealth advantage remains the strongest opener in the game. If the design team considers it acceptable that rogues can erase someone from full health during a stun lock, the inverse must also hold true: when a rogue loses stealth and is caught, the kill condition should be fully reversed onto them. Fairness requires symmetry. If they get to delete others with zero interaction, they should be just as vulnerable when the situation flips.

The intent here is not to demand a direct nerf, but to demand balance in how combat initiations and crowd control function across classes. Either reduce rogue opener lethality or bring all classes up to a level where counterplay is actually possible.

If the developers truly believe this level of one-sided stunlock gameplay is acceptable and does not require adjustment, then at least provide an optional battleground queue without rogues. Those who enjoy stealth-based hide-and-seek can fight each other and express their “skill” through stunlocks, while the rest of the player base can participate in PvP without being instantly deleted before pressing a single button.
