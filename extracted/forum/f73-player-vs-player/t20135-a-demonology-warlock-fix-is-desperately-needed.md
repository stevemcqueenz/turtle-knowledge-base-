# A Demonology warlock fix is desperately needed

## Post 137910 by pillowed — 2025-07-03T02:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137910#p137910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The spec that is supposedly the defensive warlock spec is simply impotent. There is no other way to describe it.

Problem 1:
Fear and Howl of Terror are both near un-castable when being attacked by anyone with a twig with greater than 2.5 attack speed. The primary two defensives that a warlock possess are just unreliable. This problem is exacerbated if you are an alliance warlock that basically has to cast 3 fears for one to have desired effect because of trinkets and racials. This is even more ridiculous when you look at the fact that warriors and priests have a more reliable fear than the class known for fear. With the amount of damage most melees are putting out, 1.5 second cast time on a spell that you will probably need to land 3 times is simply too long. In any scenario where you would want to cast "Howl of Terror", a cast time of 2 seconds may as well be a 30 second cast time. And "Death Coil" is simply on too long of a cool down to only affect 1 player.

Problem 2:
The "Fel Domination"/"Master Summoner" combination needs to net to an instant cast summons. The main reason for this spell is to on-demand summon a voidwalker to sacrifice for an emergency shield. The current values resulting in a 1.5 second highly interruptible cast time, that can also be stunned resulting in the loss of the "Fel Domination" buff, means that the emergency void sac is essentially unusable. The same situation applies as above, 1.5 seconds is too long of a cast for a defensive that... well that leads to.....

Problem 3:
The voidwalker sacrifice absorbs far too little damage. If, and it is a big if, you do somehow get a sacrifice off, if you didn't already have a voidwalker summoned, you are rewarded with a shield that absorbs about 1.1 second worth of damage with the absurd amount of damage classes are outputting now. The shield is simply too weak to provide any meaningful or significant relief, especially given that it took you 8-10 seconds and 3000 hitpoints to execute if you are being attacked. Warlocks simply do not have enough mobility to have a shield that does absolutely nothing of significant value. Fear and shields are our only defensive and both are essentially worthless. Especially when you see priests running around with on demand bubbles, and instant cast aoe fears. Mages with blink and a complete10 second immunity that removes all debuffs. Shamans..... well lets not even bring up the absurdity of a class that can burst for 5k+, tank 3 or 4 people while healing themselves through it, and insta-gib people.

Problem 4:
The design of the warlock pets, lesser and greater demons, is just not well thought out. I don't think I'm alone in saying that it would be better to have a permanent pet somewhere between the current lesser and greater demons than to be completely feeble except for every 5 or 15 minutes. Especially when as soon as you throw out a greater demon it becomes a bat signal for everyone to either burn the greater demon down or simply burn the immobile warlock down. It was a good idea to try something new but the power gap between the two is way too large. Valiant effort on reimagining demonology("soul link") warlocks but this is simply terrible in practice. Waiting 5 minutes for a pet to be insta-deleted, banished, or being swarmed as soon as you summon it is just not a good practical choice if you intend to have class balance. Either add more impactful damage modifiers for the lesser demons deep in the demonology tree or reduce the damage output of the infernal or felguard and make one of them a permanent pet for soul link warlocks. Before you say it.... Unleashed Potential is simply bad for pvp. Fishing for crits is not a reliable strategy as a demonology warlock because to get unleashed potential you simply don't have enough points to dump into improved searing pain, the best option to fish for crits when fully talented, to get to the full 10 percent increased crit chance on searing pain. Not to mention, it effects spell damage and as an alliance warlock the preferred pet, the fellhunter, gains absolutely zero benefit from that talent.
