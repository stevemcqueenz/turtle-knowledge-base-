<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161614#p161614 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Poisons have always ignored armor.

I need a copypasta about how a noxious assault rogue can't even get a full stun combo out without gaps, not to mention they can't put up all the buffs they need while keeping someone stunlocked, and they also don't have the energy to do more than 2-3 noxious assaults in a full combo, of which they get about 3-3.5k damage in tops. Mages one shot you for more damage than that, and that ALSO ignores armor

Also, cheap shot lasts 4 seconds, and kidney lasts 6 if done at 5 combo points.

 **A noxious assault rogue's opener would look like this:**

Cheapshot (60 energy) -> Noxious Assault (45-4 energy for poison procs)
At this point the rogue has 9 energy, and needs to wait 3 energy ticks to recoup enough energy for another noxious assault + kidney. This means the rogue must either kidney at 4 combo points, losing 2 seconds of the stun, or they can barely squeeze in another noxious assault to get to 5 combo points, but leave a gap for a retaliation (psychic scream, mortal coil, stun, some kind of dot on them to make resetting more difficult, eating the poison mushroom so you can trinket kidney and break the blind, pop a fap etc) before they get the next energy tick for a kidney. IF THEY'RE LUCKY they might get enough energy from poison procs, but without envenom, their proc chance isn't amazing.

Lets say they get a full kidney off cause you were bad. They now have 28 energy and 6 more seconds, which means 3 more ticks. That is a whole 2 more noxious assaults, and 3 total combo points on you with 6 energy left in the tank, meaning you get ANOTHER chance to retaliate before they have the energy to pop a blind on you and try to go for a full reset.

so the full combo looks like this:
Cheap shot -> Noxious assault -> Noxious assault -> stun gap -> kidney shot -> noxious assault -> noxious assault -> stun gap -> blind -> reset

You get 2 chances to save yourself if they're going full zug. If they're NOT going full zug, they you should be able to live through the 2 noxious assaults unless your gear is absolute dogwater.

 **On the other hand, a hemo rogue's combo will look like this:**

Cheap shot -> hemo -> hemo -> full kidney (no stun gap) -> hemo -> hemo -> blind -> if you counter or retaliate, they prep and reblind, or if you're low, they just vanish -> cheap shot -> cold blood -> eviscerate. All that with no gaps, and no energy issues.

Oh, and they're also auto attacking you with poisons on their weapons exactly the same as the noxious assault rogue the whole time.

 **tl;dr:** if you're dying to a noxious assault rogue, its because you got pve zugged, and that's a skill issue. Pvp specced sub rogues are still way more deadly, and they've been playing the exact same for decades at this point.
