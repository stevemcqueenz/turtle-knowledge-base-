Yo, i have a couple of ideas i would like to share with you.

First it is 2v2, and 3v3 duels. Dont think that would be too much work to put into the game, and it would be insanely fun!
Second is a Gating/Ranking system for PvP, where basically there is one BG that says you require to be below a certain rank to join, and one BG where it requires you to be above a certain rank to join.  One BG where it requires you to be below rank 7 to join, which would ensure there is a noobies BG for new players, so they can have fun, and also its lessens the population of noobies on other BGs, so the more experienced can have more fun. And also a BG which requires to you be Above Rank 10 maybe, or Rank 7, i am not sure, but this would be like the most serious BG players. I am not sure if the server population is high enough yet for this to be implemented, but when Twow 2 comes, i think this is needed.
