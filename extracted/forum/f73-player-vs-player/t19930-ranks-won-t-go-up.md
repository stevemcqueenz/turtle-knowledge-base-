## Post 136157 by Membrane (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-18T12:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136157#p136157 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's not about how much honor your make, it's about how much honor you make compared to everyone else that week. If you're farming honor and your rank is not progressing, it means everyone else around you is farming more honor.

From your post, I gather you're trying to rank up multiple chars at the same time. At rank 9-14 you're not going to be able to do this, the people around you will be getting in excess of 300,000 honor per week.

Download the addon HonorSpyTurtle and you'll see your standing along with everyone elses.

<https://github.com/gwetchen/HonorSpyTurtle>

This is not a TWoW thing, this is how it was in Vanilla WoW back in 2005 / 2006. However TWoW has reduced the burden slightly as you cannot go down in rank here, in 2005 if you had a few days / week off, you would lose your rank.

Patch 1.18 has announced some PVP changes, specifically something to help the Rank 14 grind a little less painful, what that turns out to be though currently remains a mystery.
