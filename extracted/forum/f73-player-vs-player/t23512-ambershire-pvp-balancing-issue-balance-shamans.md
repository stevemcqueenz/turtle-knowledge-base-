# Ambershire PvP balancing issue - Balance Shamans

## Post 164548 by Firewi33 — 2026-01-07T01:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164548#p164548 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

When Arathi Basin regularly ends up with 2 premades running 5 Shamans, it’s clear there’s a balance problem. This pushes more players to reroll Shamans and hurts class variety in PvP.

In large fights, Shamans can quickly kill even 6k HP defenders with shocks and burst damage. When several Shamans do this at once, defending objectives becomes nearly impossible, no matter the gear or positioning.

If this keeps going, PvP will become predictable and people will stop queuing.

Balancing Shamans the same way Paladins were adjusted feels like the logical next step. Ignoring this might be ok in the short term, but it risks long-term damage to PvP and the overall health of the server.
