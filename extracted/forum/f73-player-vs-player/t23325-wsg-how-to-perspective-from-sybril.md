# WSG – How to: Perspective from Sybril

## Post 163063 by Maxe71 — 2025-12-18T10:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163063#p163063 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

When I enter Warsong Gulch, I am not thinking about kills. I am thinking about time. Time is rank, and rank is discipline. Every action that does not move the flag closer to a capture is wasted effort.

The first thing I look for is the Flag Carrier. If we do not have one assigned before the gates open, we are already behind. In Classic, the Druid is king. Mobility decides everything. Armor does not save you, damage does not save you. Movement does. A slow carrier is a dead carrier, and a dead carrier is a lost match.

Once the carrier is chosen, the healers fall into place. One healer belongs to the Flag Carrier and nowhere else. Not midfield, not chasing kills, not padding meters. Their job is to keep that carrier alive through shields, dispels, and positioning. If that healer drifts, even for a moment, the enemy will feel it and punish it.

The rest of the team is not there to fight for glory. They are there to control space. Midfield is the heart of Warsong Gulch. Whoever owns midfield controls the pace of the game. I want slows, roots, fears, and pressure applied there constantly. A Warrior keeping Hamstring up, a Mage freezing the ground, a Hunter tracking movement. This is where games are won quietly, long before the flag reaches the base.

At the start, we move with intent. The carrier pushes immediately with support. Two players stay back on defense, not to farm kills, but to delay. Delay is victory in small pieces. The rest of the team takes midfield and holds it. If midfield collapses, the match becomes a chase, and chasing costs time.

When we engage the enemy Flag Carrier, we do not rush. The first target is always the healer. Always. Crowd control, grenades, pressure. Once the healer is gone, the carrier falls quickly. Anyone tunneling the carrier while the healer stands free is playing the wrong game.

Defending our own carrier is about anticipation, not reaction. Dispel before the stun lands. Peel before the burst comes in. Use Free Action Potions before the choke, not after. Swiftness is for breaking contact, not for panic. A clean escape is better than a heroic last stand.

Recoveries must be fast and decisive. Rogue scouts ahead. Hunter denies stealth. Mage controls the ground. If a recovery takes more than half a minute, we have already given the enemy too much time.

Consumables are not optional. In ranked Warsong, they are part of your kit. Free Action, Swiftness, healing and mana potions, bandages. Engineering tools are standard issue. If you walk in without them, you are asking your team to carry you.

Communication stays short. Clear. Functional. No speeches, no arguments. “FC tunnel.” “Healer down.” “Cap in ten.” Anything more is noise.

Warsong Gulch is not about proving how strong you are. It is about proving you can follow a plan. Fast wins matter. Clean execution matters. Ego does not.

That is how you rank. That is how you win.
