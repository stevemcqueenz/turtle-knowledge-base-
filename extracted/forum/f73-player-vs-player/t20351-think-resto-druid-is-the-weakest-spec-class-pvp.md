Pvp at the moment is extremely bursty and thats definitely having a negative impact on all healers powerlevel.

And while i do think you have a point with barkskins cast time reduction, you reveal that you might have quite a bit to improve about your own gameplay by even mentioning treeform.

Pvp is a lot different from pve. If you just stand there "like a tree" you will of course get jumped. Healing in the current burst meta is not like healing in a pve mmo, the pure dmg output of enemies you wont overcome. But its healing like in a moba, where your heals can buy your allies enough time to hopefully unleash their own burst. So abilities like prowl are a godsend to ensure its your team getting junped, not you. And with a quick regrowth + swiftmend from an unexpected angle, you can save the burst target while giving the enemy not much time to counteract.

Also switching to bearform for that sweet sweet bonus health is vital if you see dangerous casts come your way. You wanna run something like this ans avoid people jumping you in the first place by being difficault to get a hold of
