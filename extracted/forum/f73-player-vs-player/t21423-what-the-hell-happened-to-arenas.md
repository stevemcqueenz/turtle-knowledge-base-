I haven't played arenas on new patch yet but I have seen they are in poor state. I mean, everything in this patch was high quality and tested except the PvP content.

On day 1 there was a bug that you couldn't kill a person from same faction in xfaction arenas. That means, the xfaction arenas weren't even properly tested in terms of "is everything working right?". If 2 teams joined Arena and they all were from same faction, the game couldn't be finished! Devs had to disable arenas for a short time because of that.

Rated arenas were also bugged. If your team won, the system would count it as a lose and decrease your rank.

These 2 bugs lead me to conclusion that the Arenas were added in rush and they weren't even in alpha state. They were literally unplayable. This is not quality I would expect from Turtle WoW.
