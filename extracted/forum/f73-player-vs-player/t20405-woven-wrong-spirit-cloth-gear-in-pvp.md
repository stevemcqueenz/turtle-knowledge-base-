# Woven Wrong: Spirit Cloth Gear in PvP

## Post 140788 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-21T21:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140788#p140788 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’ll never forget the moment I lived a Rogue opener with 80 HP left. One more tick of poison and I was dead, but I had just enough stamina to survive, fear the Rogue (thank god he wasn’t Undead), and heal myself + my teammate.

And when you’re farming PvP gear, you need survivability now. You're stepping into BGs half-naked, getting stunlocked by Rogues and nuked by Mages. Every point of Stamina helps you stay in the fight long enough to contribute. Spirit does absolutely nothing in that grind.

 1/ PvP cloth gear needs a rethink. Why? Because Spirit is eating up stat budget where we desperately need Stamina or utility stats like Spell Power or Resilience (a lot of discussions on this atm)

 2/ As a Priest, I’m going OOM anyway in most fights. Spirit regen doesn’t matter when I’m chain casting and trying to stay alive. It’s even worse when you’re still gearing up, every missing point of Stam hits hard.

 3/ Spirit makes more sense in PvE, where you get time to regen. But in PvP, it’s dead weight. It’s time to stop letting PvE stats dilute PvP performance.

 4/ If PvP gear is meant for PvP, then let’s make it work for PvP. Drop Spirit from cloth sets and give us stats that matter in actual combat.
