# Rated Arena Myth

## Post 124455 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-30T14:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124455#p124455 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If I recall correctly, some time ago one dev uncovered that the team has in plans to introduce "rated arena" system to Turtle WoW. The topic wasn't much uncovered but it's self-explanatory - the system will evaluate everyone's arena rating based on win/lose ratio in Blood Ring.

It might sound amazing for everyone. I have seen a few forum posts about PvP that asked for this change. What can go wrong then? Although it's generally desired change, people expect too much from this concept and I will explain why rated arena is overhyped.

I think there is nothing wrong with adding arena rating to the game. However, some people believe rated arena equals to matchmaking that selects players based on their arena rating. In other words, bad players would face bad players and good ones would face stronger enemies. While it's technically possible to introduce such matchmaking along with rated arena, this idea has one issue. This system actually shrinks the Blood Ring playerbase. Why does it matter?

Currently in Blood Ring matchmaking is absolutely random. Your gear doesn't matter, you face random players who also queued. This isn't fair but at least playerbase isn't fragmented.

I'm not sure how lv 60 Blood Ring scene looks like now but one month ago on Nordanaar it was nearly dead apart from BR days. You could have game after game only on BR day (if you were lucky) and usually there were only 0-2 arenas active simultaneously.
On the other hand, on non-BR days arena was completely dead like Sunnyglade Valley. You could be in queue for a few hours and not join any Blood Ring.

People think this kind of matchmaking makes sense because Blizzard has already implemented it on retail (and wherever else). But people can't see the difference between Blizzard servers and Turtle WoW. They can't see Blizzard has like 100x more players who play arena, moreover, players connect from several servers because cross-server battlegrounds are standard there.
People think this matchmaking would work here too because it works on Blizzard's servers completely ignoring Turtle WoW's reality. The worst thing is that it takes 1 second to type "/who Blood" to be enlightened how small arena community is on this server.

If the matchmaking was changed to select only players with similar arena rating, let's be honest, Blood Ring would be even more dead than now. Players with low rating couldn't participate in the same games with better players. I'm not sure how many "layers" of that matchmaking would be needed on Turtle WoW but mathematically let's say at least 2. That means the playerbase would be splitted at least by half. The threshold will be too high resulting in never-popping games.

This is why rated matchmaking will likely never be implemented on Turtle WoW. Devs wouldn't want to kill their own child.
