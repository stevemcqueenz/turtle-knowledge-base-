# Resilience changes are not thought out and are going to destroy what little is left of the PvP scene

## Post 135442 by Forumdweller (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-12T09:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135442#p135442 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There's going to be 3 or 4 specs that are absolutely monstrous in pvp dominating every single fight, the rest are going to be mid or even worse off than what we currently have.

Take feral for instance. Already extremely tanky but pumps out huge crits as cat and stops you from even casting your spells. So now we can't even burst them down which was previously your only way do deal with them as a caster cause not only do they do absurb damage but now they can tank all your damage without even shifting to bear. They're already as good as immune to CC excluding stuns. Plus they are now going to have an easier time escaping because there are exactly 0 limitations on their ability to freely maneuver and it'll be harder to finish them off before they escape.

feral here isnt geared at all and isnt even opening me as a feral usually would which leaves you at half hp before u even get out of pounce

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/cZ143gK8lKY]

Specs like destruction are extremely reliant on their burst and is practically our only way to win fights. Resilience doesn't help us in the slightest vs melee classes cause we can't cast spells in the first place due to spell knockback. Now our suffering is simply prolonged and melee classes yet again get to feel good about themselves hitting a defenseless child.

![Image](https://gyazo.com/b7786fa2737444d281ca99c4e8c777b1.png)

Vs rogues? Yeah resilience is really going to help us out when the problem isn't surviving but being stunned for eternity while we can't do anything. Warrior intercepts you and coil is now even less useful because they're taking reduced damage from you so you just stand there.. being more useless than usual? In vanilla as a non SL lock once a melee is already hitting you it's game over because you can't cast spells and one swing does half your HP. You can't get away again like a mage can (which is gonna dominate warrior even more now because warr can't get a lucky kill when they do connect XDDD)

There's going to be new class changes to compensate for this which brings us back to the question. Why add global resilience in the first place? A better move would be to give each spec their own values which isn't perfect either but is certainly one step towards fairness.

Idk, I think you've just successfully doubled down on turning pvp into a consume/trinket fest. Only way to survive ferals now is popping lips.

On an off note, cat should now be affected by all forms of CC including seduction.

TL:DR resilience changes are going to accomplish absolutely nothing except making 1sided matchups even more 1sided.

Pets are also going to need some sort of hp & resilience buffs to compensate
