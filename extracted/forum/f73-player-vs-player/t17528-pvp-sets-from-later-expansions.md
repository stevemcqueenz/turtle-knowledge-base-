# PvP sets from later expansions

## Post 119632 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-11T14:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119632#p119632 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was thinking, a lot of people complain that the PvP sets are way inferior to the ones you get in Naxxramas and soon Karazhan, and that it's the main imbalance in PvP.

I was thinking that including sets from later expansions as PvP sets here would be a good idea, as it saves them design time, just dealing with the stat balance to match the Raid set it will counter.

It could be the Dungeon 3 set or the PvP sets released in TBC, the style doesn't deviate much and fits perfectly, maybe a retexture to vary the design a bit.

I think that would be a great way to start, no?

This also leaves them more time to design the PvE sets which I think the look is more important with the lore.
