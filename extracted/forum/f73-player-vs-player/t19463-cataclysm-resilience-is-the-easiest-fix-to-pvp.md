Good points, but id hardly consider cata to be "the most balanced". In my book thats wrath, and both wrath and tbc didnt have all of that homogenization going on, and resilience worked well in those expansions to lessen the impact of overbloated damage.

My own points against resilience here, is that it really kills the gearing versatility of vanilla and segragates the two gamemodes, limits you with what you can wear etc, which is why if it is to be implemented, it should be implemented for all pieces of gear, with pve gear having lower values and pvp more of it.

Another minus to resilience, is that it never feels good seeing one of your abilities do 2k dmg to a mob, and then deal 1k to a player. These kinds of damage discrepancies just dont feel good, as you cant properly gauge how much damage you'd actually be able to do without proper knowledge and experience. For me this isnt a problem, but I know its a problem for some.

Then theres the issue of healing. Healing might become too overpowered. How do you address this? Lowering healing output on targets with higher values of a stat might work but it feels weird losing power this way as well, and has never been done in WoW, as its certainly open to weird unforseen exploits. At the very least hybrid classes will become unkillable gods if they don't have severe mana issues as counterbalance.

Imo the correct course of action is adding more stamina, armour and resists to most pieces of raid and pvp gear. It fits the vanilla aesthetic more, but it would also warrant PvE readjustments, which might be far too time consuming and nearly impossible without a large playtest. How are the devs supposed to organize 40 players to readjust raid damage intake with all those buffed stats?

For a similar reason simply scaling down damage is going to be tricky. How would it affect raiding if suddenly DPS drops down by 20%? Content becomes much harder. It wont be an issue for early tier raids, but naxx could become very difficult (might be a good thing).

Ascension tackled this problem by first having global dmg reduction, ontop of every spell having its own pvp modifier. Both of these lead to huge problems and created a ridiculous shitstorm. Spells would be forgotten to be updated, scaling would break etc.

Resilience isnt a pretty solution, but theres a reason blizzard took that route - it was an easy and cost effective solution that actually fixed the problem, at the expense of creating a diffent problem.

Currently its better to have at least some solution rather than nothing, so picking the lesser evil is preferable. PvP is disgustingly bad, its hardly a "classic" experience in that regard, and its putting off a sizable junk of the playerbase. A large crowd of the classic fanbase gear up so they can PvP as an endgoal. When PvP becomes a 2shot fiesta with next to no counterplay, gear is irrelevant in equal matchups, and playing is pointless if you dont have the gear to match up. You'd get more balanced and fun fights if everyone was buttnaked instead.
