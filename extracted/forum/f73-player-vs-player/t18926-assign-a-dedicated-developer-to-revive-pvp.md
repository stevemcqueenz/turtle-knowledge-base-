# Assign a dedicated developer to revive PvP

## Post 128789 by Whoopie — 2025-04-28T08:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128789#p128789 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Could we get a dedicated developer for PvP?

Someone who would monitor and adjust class balance, propose PvP sets and items, optimize gearing, honor, tokens, ranking systems, and more?

Currently, PvP is dead if we look at the number of players participating compared to the total player base. This doesn't have to be the case. There are hundreds of players willing to engage in PvP, but not in its current form, with all the burst damage, difficult gearing, and faction imbalances.

We would love to see a comeback of the PvP era. Many players, myself and my friends included, play the game mainly because we enjoy PvP.

Even just acknowledging these issues and communicating that the dev team is actively working on them would go a long way.
