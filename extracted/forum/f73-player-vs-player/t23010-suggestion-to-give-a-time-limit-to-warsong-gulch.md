# Suggestion to give a time limit to Warsong Gulch

## Post 160612 by Yoraeryu — 2025-11-25T07:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160612#p160612 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello all. I doubt this is the first time someone has brought this up, but since I couldn’t find the exact topic here easy I decided to post about it myself.

It has become increasingly frustrating how long Warsong Gulch matches can take. People “turtle” a lot because nobody wants to risk losing. I understand that, but it feels like we are wasting the time of many PvP players. Some are here for reputation, others for marks, and some just want to finish and either stop or move on to Arathi, Alterac or Arena without having to forfeit the match.

This doesn’t happen every game, but there are times when a Warsong match can go on forever. I think a 20–30 minute limit per match would make things much better, and would also help reshuffle players more often. Some say a flag debuff would be a better solution since with a time limit people could still turtle to win – but honestly, they already do that now, and matches last forever anyway. I would rather lose a match and be done than sit in a game that never ends. I don’t mind queuing again if I know there is actually an end in sight.

This idea could also apply to other battlegrounds where turtling becomes a problem, but I mainly wanted to talk about Warsong Gulch since that’s where it feels the worst. Whether people agree with the suggestion or whether it’s taken into consideration is another matter, but I wanted to put the idea out there.

Thank you for your time, and have a great day everyone!

Thanks for your comment. I’ll be honest though, the tone you chose kind of proves part of the issue. I made a suggestion about match pacing, and instead of keeping things civil you jumped straight into personal remarks (“listen sister,” “pee drawers”). That doesn’t strengthen your argument, and it reflects a defensive attitude that pops up a lot in PvP discussions. It’s very possible to disagree without getting personal. ![🙂](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f642.svg)

Anyway, onto the actual points:

 1 “Longer WSG = more fun” is only true for a specific type of PvP-purist players, and they tend to be the loudest.
Not everyone enjoys 45–90 minute stalemates. Some players want rep, marks, honor, or simply a match that actually ends, this  isn’t “anti-fun”, it’s just a different definition of fun.

 2 A time limit doesn’t stop good matches from happening.
Strong teams can still have intense, competitive games,  just without them dragging on forever because both sides are afraid to make a move or cant push. Eternal turtle games punish both sides equally.

 3 “Just leave the match” isn’t a solution.
Leaving punishes your team, wastes your queue time, and feels bad. Asking for a system that avoids very long lockouts is far more constructive. And this was simply a suggestion, no need for anyone to get salty about it.

 4 Not everyone queues for the same reasons.
Some people PvP for the thrill of it, some for rewards, some for progression or rep. These are all valid. Acting like there’s only one “correct” reason to play doesn’t help the discussion.

 5 Class balance and match length are separate issues.
Yes, class balance needs work. But that doesn’t affect whether matches should last 20 minutes or 90. Both problems can be addressed independently.

 6 Accessibility doesn’t kill PvP,  it keeps it alive.
You mentioned the community is old. Exactly. Older players have jobs, families, responsibilities, and limited play windows. Healthier pacing keeps battlegrounds populated instead of scaring people away with hour-long slogs.

Lastly:
I never attacked anyone for liking long games. I get that people enjoy them. I only pointed out that not everyone enjoys endless stalemates, that was my entire point. There’s no reason for the discussion to turn personal.

I PvP because I enjoy it, but I don’t enjoy when a match becomes a time sink with no progress. And I’m not doing it for leveling or “easy ranking”; I often lock my levels to play different brackets, and my 60s leveled mostly through quests. Please don’t assume who I am just because I made a suggestion.

Everyone has the right to offer ideas. People can disagree, that’s totally fine  but keeping it constructive makes the conversation better for everyone. The people above you shared their counterpoints clearly and respectfully, and even if I disagreed with some of it, I could fully understand where they were coming from.

Have a nice day!
