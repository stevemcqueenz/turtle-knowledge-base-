# NERF PVP DAMAGE, IT'S STILL TOO HIGH.

## Post 148804 by Hammerfall — 2025-08-28T01:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148804#p148804 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

ive played plenty of battlegrounds and i tested arenas for a bit when people were queueing. and one thing i noticed is that the pvp damage is still too high atm...people are getting killed in less than 3 seconds at times...its just way too high still. and its REALLY noticeable in arenas especially. damage is still just too high still and the pacing of pvp right now is too fast to where you cant even make much plays..its just zerg zerg zerg, no time to fake casts, no time to go for some cc ect dont get me wrong the pvp dmg nerf helped a bit but it needs more still.  sad_turtle_head

Recommendation.

Current pvp damage stats
 ---
All incoming Physical Damage from other Players is reduced by 15%.
All incoming Magical Damage from other Players is reduced by 20%.
While in PvP combat (in both Open World and Battlegrounds/Arenas) all incoming Player Healing is reduced by 20% and all incoming Player Absorb Shields are reduced by 15%.

i would say they needs to be changed to the following below...

New pvp damage stats
 ---
All incoming Physical Damage from other Players is reduced by 20%.
All incoming Magical Damage from other Players is reduced by 30%.
While in PvP combat (in both Open World and Battlegrounds/Arenas) all incoming Player Healing is reduced by 25% and all incoming Player Absorb Shields are reduced by 20%.

its not a massive change but i think if we went with these new stats pvp would feel better in terms of breathing room for making plays ect  satisfied_turtle_head
