<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127381#p127381 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The decreased buff cost has greatly diminished the effectiveness of dispel mechanics in PvP. Everytime you are now fighting people with 6k+ health even without flasks because buffs are so cheap to cast that they can be placed on every player every time. Having to spend 4-5 globals just dispelling buffs before you can actually get to the buffs you want to remove on every player is ridiculous.

The start of the game with no buff is cost is fine but bring back the original buff costs to either force players to drink more to buff everyone or players don't get buffs 24/7 in BGs.

For PvE I see no problems with the reduced costs as it just allows everyone to get ready faster.
