# Midpatch Itemization Changes Changelog

## Post 71699 by Dragunovi (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-10-17T22:31:00Z

Greetings! We're happy to announce that the **Midpatch Itemization Changes** (or M.I.C. for short) are now complete and went live with scheduled maintenance!
Do keep in mind we will be monitoring the items and their performances and might do future adjustments as needed. You can see the list of changes below:

 **Dungeon Set 2/Tier 0.5 Rework:**
All pieces of the set are now item level 60 epics. This affects the item level 65-66 blues and the item level 55 epic gloves for all sets, increasing their stat budget.
In addition, some other pieces have seen some slight tweaks to make them more worthwhile to acquire.

- Warrior:
 - Bracers of Heroism: 9 -> 10 Strength, 5 -> 10 Agility

 - Belt of Heroism: 9 -> 12 Agility, Added 1% Hit

 - Legplates of Heroism: 11 -> 16 Agility, Added 1% Critical Chance

- Hateforge Belt: Reduced Strength from 20 to 15. Changed 13 Spirit to 15 Stamina. Added 1% Haste.

When you change raid loot it nullfies peoples investment into the raid and gear they have spent weeks/months farming.

I think the final thing that doesn't get mentioned enough is that if you just change TOO MUCH stuff you waste peoples future time. Time spent farming an item that they knew from vanilla to be worthwhile to find that when it finally drops that it actually isn't the item they know anymore at all. These kind of changes aren't as bad on raid loot when you are working with a large group to farm them and have the hive mind of your guild to let you know when you're after an item that has changed.
