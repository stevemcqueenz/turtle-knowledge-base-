# 1.17.1 Itemization Changelog

## Post 79558 by Dragunovi (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-12-01T18:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79558#p79558 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! Here's the itemization changelog detailing all item related additions and changes coming in **Patch 1.17.1: Labor and Legacy**.

The list might seem a bit small but that's due to the majority of changes planned for this patch being pushed forward with the Midpatch Itemization Changes that happened sometime after the release of Patch 1.17.0.

 **Keep in mind that the changes and additions are subject to change and not final.**

 **Item Additions**
- Added 16 drops for the new rares found in Blackstone Island, Thalassian Highlands and Forlorn Summit.

- Added 7 new crafting recipes for the new starting zones: Maritime Gumbo, Gloves of Manathirst, Blast Shield, Volatile Concoction, Unstable Mining Dynamite, Lynxstep Boots and Enchant 2H Weapon - Minor Intellect.

- Added many junk drops to Thalassian Highlands.

- Added something fanatical to C'thun.

- Added Aspect of Seradane to Solnius.
 **Item Adjustments**
- Obsidian Mail Tunic now reduces spell damage taken by 3% instead of a flat 10 damage.

- Sigil of Penetration and Invocation of Shattering now grant 40 Armor Penetration down from 75.

- Invocation enchants from Lower Karazhan Halls should now function properly without needing a dedicated fix patch.

- Totem of Flowing Water's mana return has been buffed from 10 to 25.

- Totem of the Endless Flicker now has a new effect: "Increases the duration of your Flame Shock by 3 sec."

- Totem of the Calming Water now grants 8 MP5 instead of 5 MP5 and additionally grants 10 more mana when your Water Shield is struck.

- Idol of Evergrowth now has a new effect: "Your Thorns additionally gives a 3% chance when attacked to inflict 15-20 Nature damage to nearby enemies."

- Libram of Truth now has a new effect: "Your Devotion Aura additionally reduces all physical damage taken 2%."

- Staff of the Qiraji Prophets now grants you 3% Spell Hit when its effect is procced instead of 50 Spell Penetration, but its proc chance has been reduced from 25% to 15%.

- Drake Talon Cleaver's proc rate has been increased from 1 to 2 procs per minute.

- Neretzek, The Blood Drinker's proc rate has been increased from 0.8 to 1.4 procs per minute.

- Hyperchromatic Deflector and Towerforge Battlegear's 3 Set Bonus now has a 3% chance to reflect spells down from 5%.

- Thornpod's range has been reduced from 20 to 8 yards and now scales 0.05 from Spellpower down from 1.

- Chromie's Broken Pocket Watch's spell proc now properly reduces casting speed, the old stats have been restored and the description updated to be clearer.

- Fixed incorrect armor and block values on some low level custom items.

- Renamed the trinket version of Shard of Eternity to Temporal Anomaly to prevent confusion.
 **Emerald Sanctum Loot and Loottable Adjustments**

- Normal Mode Loot:
 - Fixed slightly off armor values for most pieces, netting an overall increase in armor.
 - Increased item level from 76 to 78, increasing overall power.

 - Robe of the Dreamways: 44 -> 46 Spellpower

 - Jadestone Helmet: 16 -> 18 Agility, 15 -> 16 Strength

 - Staff of the Dreamer: 106 -> 121 Healing Power, 4 -> 5 MP5

 - Shard of Nightmare: 36 -> 37 Spellpower

 - Veil of Nightmare: 30 -> 32 Attack Power

 - Sandals of Lucidity: 16 -> 18 Stamina, 17 -> 18 Intellect

 - Sanctum Bark Wraps: 14 -> 15 Agility, 13 -> 14 Stamina

 - Talonwind Gauntlets: 20 -> 22 Agility

 - Mantle of the Wakener: 26 -> 28 Spellpower

 - Mallet of the Awakening: 10 -> 11 Stamina, Increased DPS from 57.5 to 59.9

 - Scaleshield of Emerald Flight: 11 -> 12 All Stats

 - Ancient Jade Leggings: Added 9 Strength

 - Axe of Dormant Slumber: 8 -> 9 Strength, Increased DPS from 58.2 to 59.8

 - Ring of Nature's Duality: 51 -> 40 Healing Power, Replaced 1% Spell Hit with 1% Spell Crit (Hopefully the last change on this item.)

- Hardmode Loot:
 - Increased item level from 78 to 81, increasing overall power.

 - Choker of the Emerald Lord: 27 -> 28 Spellpower, 11 -> 12 Nature Resist, Added 1% Spell Hit

 - Crystal Sword of the Blossom: Increased DPS from 59.4 to 62.9

 - Naturecaller's Tunic: 18 -> 20 Stamina, 20 -> 21 Intellect, 13 -> 25 Spirit

 - Nature's Call: 5 -> 8 Stamina, Increased DPS from 46.8 to 49.0

 - Jadestone Protector: 12 -> 13 Intellect, 15 -> 18 Extra Block Value

 - Breath of Solnius: 10 -> 11 Spirit

- Claw of Erennius Quest Rewards:
 - Rewards buffed from Item Level 66 to 74, increasing stats alongside it.

 - Jadestone Skewer: 35 -> 39 Agility, Increased DPS from 62.6 to 72.8 and Feral Attack Power from 84 to 196

 - Jadestone Mallet: 8 -> 10 Stamina, 6 -> 13 Spirit, 68 -> 106 Healing Power

 - Claw of Senthos: 20 -> 24 Attack Power, Increased DPS from 48.1 to 56.0
In addition to the loot changes, the Solnius and Favor of Erennius (Hardmode Reward Chest) have seen adjustments, even completely redoing Solnius' loottable. Here are the notable changes:
- Smoldering Dream Essence, which is needed for the Druid's Emerald Blessing, now has a 35% chance to drop up from 20%.

- Solnius and Favor of Erennius are now guaranteed to drop a Fading Dream Fragment but Solnius no longer drops Small Dream Shards. This should make his overall Dream Shard net not luck dependant and overall higher if you can clear him in Hardmode.

- Bag of the Vast Conscious now has a 25% chance to drop instead of 15% chance from Solnius and you are now guaranteed a Bag of the Vast Conscious from the Favor of Erennius.

- Solnius' gear drop loot groups are now split into three which consist of the Physical, Magical and Weapon/Relic categories, making sure that he's unable to roll multiple of the same items from the same kill. This adjustment also fixes the edge cases where he had a very rare chance to not drop an item from one of the loot groups.

- Solnius is now guaranteed to drop a Dreamscale up from 48% chance.

- Enchant Gloves - Nature Power is now properly part of the crafting recipes loot group.

- Formula: Eternal Dreamstone Shard now has a 1% chance to drop, up from 0.85% chance.
 **Crafting Recipe Adjustments**
- Arcanite Belt Buckle's Arcanite Bar cost has been reduced from 8 to 3.
