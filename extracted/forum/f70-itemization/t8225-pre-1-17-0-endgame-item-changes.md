# Pre-1.17.0 Endgame Item Changes

## Post 52794 by Dragunovi (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-10T14:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52794#p52794 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! We have compiled a list of end-game itemization changes that have been done before the release of Patch 1.17.0: Beyond the Greymane Wall. Please refer to patch specific itemization changelogs for more recent changes and additions. This list primarily concerns items that already exist in vanilla. Any changes not found here like set bonus changes can be found in their respective topic posts.
This list will be primarily split into **two** major sections: **General, Raid Drops**, where the latter is also split into which changes done for which raids.

General:
- Eye of the Beast and Don Rodrigo's Band: Now usable by Shamans.

- Lei of the Lifegiver [+53 Healing Power, +3 MP5] -> [+9 Spirit, +40 Healing Power, +5 MP5]

- Elemental Focus Band: Increased Spellpower from 22 to 24.

- Earthern Guard: Armor buffed from 2468 to 2575, Block buffed from 44 to 47, added 1% Block.

- Windshear Cape: Increased Strength from 8 to 10, added 1% Haste.

- Gauntlets of the Shining Light: Removed 10 Spirit, 22 Healing Power -> 16 Spell Damage

- Death Knight Sabatons: 9 Spirit -> Agility

- Bloodmail Hauberks: 10 Spirit -> Agility

- Handcrafted Mastersmith Leggings: 12 Stamina -> 29 Stamina, 29 Strength -> 12 Strength

- Gift of the Elven Magi: 6 Spirit -> 8 Spell Damage

- Bludstone Hammer: 12 Spirit -> 12 Intellect

- Energized Chestplate: 13 Strength -> 8 Defense

- Backusarian Gauntlets: 9 Strength -> 14 Block Value

- Morlune's Bracers: 7 Stamina -> 12 Stamina, 12 Intellect -> 7 Intellect, 11 Spirit -> 14 Block Value

- Bile-etched Spaulders: 6 Stamina -> 17 Stamina, 17 Strength -> 6 Strength

- Grand Crusader's Helm: 12 Spirit -> 12 Intellect

- Righteous Orb: Critical chance increase has been increased from 3% to 5%.

- Grimy Metal Boots: 17 Strength -> 17 Stamina

- Wind Dancer Boots: 16 Spirit -> 16 Agility, 9 Intellect -> 9 Stamina

Raid Drops:
Zul'Gurub:
- Seafury Leggings: 14 Spirit -> 6 MP5

Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj:
- Boots of the Desert Protector: 14 Stamina -> 20 Stamina, Removed 4 MP5 and 12 Strength, Added %1 Block Chance and 14 Block Value

Molten Core:
- Added six [New](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81260) [Molten](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81261) [Core](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81262) [Trash](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81263) [Mob](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81264) [Drops](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81265)

- Increased the proc chance of Shadowstrike and Thunderstrike from 1 proc per minute to 1.5 procs per minute.

- Increased Health Regen of Sash of Whispered Secrets from 6 to 10 every 5 seconds.

- Removed 11 Spirit from Seal of the Archmagus and added 13 Spellpower instead.

- Removed 10 Spirit from Band of Sulfuras and added 12 Spellpower instead.

- Added 69 Holy Spellpower to Benediction.

Onyxia's Lair
- Removed Spirit from Dragonslayer's Signet, reduced Intellect and Stamina from 12 to 8, and added 18 Spellpower.

- Reduced all resistances of Ring of Binding from 10 to 5, Increased Defense from 4 to 6, Increased Armor from 60 to 100, and added 16 Stamina.

Blackwing Lair:
- Increased the fire delay of Dragonbreath Hand Cannon from 2.6 seconds to 3.1 seconds, the DPS remains the same.

- Added 5% Mana regeneration in combat to Gloves of Rapid Evolution.

- Helm of Endless Rage: [+26 Strength, +26 Agility, +29 Stamina] -> [+25 Strength, +25 Agility, +26 Stamina, +1% Hit]

- Legguards of the Fallen Crusader: [+28 Strength, +22 Agility, +17 Intellect, +22 Stamina] -> [+35 Strength, +20 Agility, +18 Stamina, +1% Hit]

- Removed 17 Spirit from Black Ash Robe and added 20 Spellpower instead.

- Replaced 17 Spirit of Herald of Woe with Agility.

- Replaced 15 Spirit of Primalist's Linked Waistguard with 15 Agility, also added 1% Haste.

- Removed spirit from Girdle of the Fallen Crusader, increased Agility from 10 to 16 and Stamina from 15 to 17. Also added 1% Haste.

- Removed spirit from Primalist's Linked Legguards and added 13 Spellpower instead, also increased Stamina and Intellect from 22 to 23.

- Removed spirit from Angelista's Grasp and added 11 Spellpower instead.

- Removed spirit from Therazane's Link and increased Intellect from 12 to 14, increased Stamina from 22 to 24 and added 1% Haste.

Temple of Ahn'Qiraj:
- Added two new items to Ouro's loot table: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=21613> | <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=21612>

- Reduced Stamina of Gloves of the Redeemed Prophecy from 20 to 16 and Intellect from 20 to 10, added 12 Agility and replaced 37 Healing Power with 20 Spellpower

- Reduced intellect of Gloves of the Fallen Prophet from 20 to 15, replaced 44 Healing Power with 23 Spellpower. Gloves of the Fallen Prophet have been added to the same trash loot tables as the Gloves of the Redeemed Prophecy as the shaman counterpart of their Prophecy set.

- Increased Spellpower of Scaled Leggings of Qiraji Fury from 36 to 39.

- Replaced 10 Spirit of Gauntlets of the Righteous Champion with Agility.

- Replaced 10 Spirit of Gauntlets of Kalimdor with Agility.

- Replaced 1% Parry on Thick Qirajihide Belt with 1% Dodge, Increased Strength from 10 to 13 and Agility from 15 to 18.

- Increased the fire delay of Blessed Qiraji Musket from 2.6 seconds to 3.1 seconds, the DPS remains the same.

- Swapped around the stats of Mantle of the Desert Crusade and renamed to Mantle of the Redeemed Prophecy: Intellect: 20 to 6 | Stamina: 17 to 20 | Strength: 10 to 17 | Agility: 6 to 10 | Swapped 44 Healing Power for 23 Spellpower and 21 Healing Power

- Reduced Intellect of Mantle of the Desert's Fury from 20 to 15, Increased Strength from 6 to 9, and added 9 Agility. Renamed to Mantle of the Fallen Prophet and got a new model.

Naxxramas:
- Digested Hand of Power: Reduced Intellect from 14 to 8, Reduced Stamina from 10 to 6, Increased MP5 from 10 to 12.

- Pendant of Forgotten Names: Reduced Intellect and Spirit from 16 to 13, Increased MP5 from 7 to 10.
