# Pre-1.17.0 Item Sets and Adjustments

## Post 52795 by Dragunovi (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-10T14:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52795#p52795 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! Here you can find all new item set additions, adjustments done to set bonuses and items that make up the item sets. This list only lists additions and changes that were done before the release of Patch 1.17.0: Beyond the Greymane Wall.

New Item Sets and Additions to Old Sets:

- New Dragonscale Leatherworking mail healer set: Red Dragon Mail
This set consists of the breastplate already available in Vanilla, boots, leggings and shoulders. Acquired via various recipes around the world.
(2) Set: +12 Fire Resistance
(3) Set: +26 Healing Power
(4) Set: Your direct healing spells have a chance to place a heal over time on your target, healing 240 over 12 sec.

- New Oddball leather set: Incendosaur Skin Armor
This set consists of boots, gloves and pauldrons that are extra fire damage per swing based. Acquired via drops in Hateforge Quarry.
(2) Set: Adds 2 fire damage to your melee atacks
(3) Set: 5% chance of dealing 15 to 25 Fire damage on a successful melee attack

- New Blacksmithing mail set: Hateforge Armor
This set consists of six pieces which are melee focused. Acquired via crafting recipes rarely dropped by trash creatures in Hateforge Quarry.
(2) Set: +20 Attack Power
(4) Set: +100 Armor Penetration
(6) Set: +2% Haste

- New Elemental Leatherworking leather set: Living Armor:
These pieces have previously existed in the original Vanilla version, we have turned it into a proper item set with bonuses. Acquired via various recipes around the world.
(2) Set: +24 Healing Power
(3) Set: Allows 10% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting

- New PvP Set | Bloody Gladiator's Regalia
Full cloth armor set accessible with Blood Ring reputation.
(2) Set: +20 Stamina
(2) Set: Reduces your chance to be critically hit by melee and ranged attacks by 1%.
(4) Set: +20 Spell Penetration
(6) Set: +23 Spellpower
(8) Set: +2% Spell Critical Chance

- New PvP Set | Bloody Gladiator's Vestments
Full leather armor set accessible with Blood Ring reputation.
(2) Set: +20 Stamina
(2) Set: Reduces your chance to be critically hit by melee and ranged attacks by 1%.
(4) Set: +100 Armor Penetration
(6) Set: +40 Attack Power
(8) Set: +2% Physical Critical Chance

- New PvP Set | Bloody Gladiator's Pursuit
Full mail armor set accessible with Blood Ring reputation.
(2) Set: +20 Stamina.
(2) Set: Reduces your chance to be critically hit by melee and ranged attacks by 1%.
(4) Set: %2 Haste and +10 Spell Penetration
(6) Set: +20 Attack Power and +13 Spellpower
(8) Set: +1% Physical and Spell Critical Chance

- New PvP Set | Bloody Gladiator's Battlegear (8) | Full plate armor set accessible with Blood Ring reputation.
(2) Set: +20 Stamina.
(2) Set: Reduces your chance to be critically hit by melee and ranged attacks by 1%.
(4) Set: +100 Armor Penetration
(6) Set: +40 Attack Power
(8) Set: +2% Physical Critical Chance

- New Tribal Leatherworking leather set: Warbear Armor
These pieces have previously existed in the original Vanilla version, we have turned it into a proper item set with bonuses. Acquired via Timbermaw Hold reputation.
(2) Set: +1% Physical Critical and Dodge Chance

- New Dragonscale Leatherworking mail set: Dragon Mail (3)
This set consists of gauntlets and breastplate already available in Vanilla and leggings. Acquired via trainer and rare recipe from dragonkin around level 40s.
(2) Set: +10 Stamina
(3) Set: +100 Armor Penetration

- New Temple of Ahn'Qiraj mail and plate sets: Redemption of the Prophecy and Fall of the Prophet:
This set consists of pieces already available in Vanilla and leggings. Acquired via boss drops and rare trash drops found in AQ40.
(2) Set: +14 Spellpower
(3) Set: +2% Haste and +12 Defense

- Added a new endgame Elemental Leatherworking leather set: Convergence of the Elements
Item set consists of 4 items: Tunic, Boots, Leggings and Helmet. Good luck on your efforts on discovering where the recipes are acquired, the mystery is half the fun ;)
(2) Set: +5 All Resistances
(3) Set: 1% Dodge and Hit
(4) Set: 24 Attack Power, 1% Haste and Increased Movement and Swim Speed

- Imperial Plate set now has a new set bonus achievable via the addition of gauntlets to the item set:
(8) Set: 1% Parry Chance

- Ironfeather Armor set now also grants 1% Spell Hit with its 2 Set.

Item Set Bonus Adjustments:
- Replaced the extra holy damage on judgement 8 set bonus with "20% chance to deal 300 additional Holy damage whenever you cause a critical hit in melee." for Paladin's Judgement set.

- Replaced Holy Light cast time reduction at 3 set with "Inflicts 60 to 67 additional Holy damage on the target of a Paladin's Judgement." for the Paladin Zul'Gurub set.

- Embrace of the Viper (WC Set)
(2) Set: 7 Nature Spellpower -> 14 Nature SP
(3) Set: +2 Staves -> 1% Attack Speed
(4) Set: Healing Power 11 -> 22
(5) Set: 10 Intellect -> 15 Intellect

- Defias Leather (DM Set)
(2) Set: 10 Armor -> 30 Armor
(3) Set: 5 Arcane Resistance -> 10 AR
(4) Set: Daggers +1 -> Daggers +3
(5) Set: 10 Attack Power -> 20 AP

- Chain of the Scarlet Crusade (SM Set)
(2) Set: 10 Armor -> 50 Armor
(3) Set: 1 Defense -> 4 Defense
(4) Set: 5 Shadow Resistance -> 10 SR
(5) Set: 15 Attack Power vs Undead -> 30 AP vs Undead

- Shard of the Gods (Onyxia + Ragnaros Set)
+(2) Set: 8 MP5
+(2) Set: 8 HP5

- Ironweave Battlesuit (Various High-level Dungeons)
+(4) Set: +15 Stamina
+(6) Set: 1% Dodge and 1% Spell Hit
(8) Set: +200 Armor -> +300 Armor
Old (4) Set: 10% Silence and Interrupt resist -> New (2) Set

- The Gladiator (Blackrock Depths arena boss drops)
(2) Set: 20 Armor -> 100 Armor
(3) Set: 2 Defense -> 6 Defense
(4) Set: 10 Attack Power -> 30 AP
(5) Set: 1% Crit -> 2% Crit

- The Postmaster (Stratholme)
(4) Set: 12 Spellpower -> 18 Spellpower
(5) Set: 10 Intellect -> 20 Intellect

Tier Set Item Adjustments:
Tier 0.5 Changes List (Questline)

Paladin
- Spirit converted to Agility.

- Soulforge Boots: +10 Spirit to Agility

- Soulforge Breastplate: +12 Spirit to Agility

- Soulforge Helm: +12 Spirit to Agility

- Soulforge Legplates: +10 Spirit to Agility

Warlock
- Added pet buffing effects to the epic pieces.

- Deathmist Mask: Now increases your pet's maximum health by 3%.

- Deathmist Robe: Now increases your pet's armor by 10%.

- Deathmist Sandals: Now increases damage dealt by your pet by 3%.

- Deathmist Wraps: Now increases your pet's critical strike chance by 2%.

Shaman
- Reduced amount of stamina and intellect and removed spirit on some items and added agility, added haste to some pieces.

- Coif of The Five Thunders: [+22 Stamina, +21 Intellect, +12 Spirit, +14 Spellpower, +1% Spell Crit] -> [+12 Strength, +12 Agility, +18 Stamina, +16 Intellect, +18 Spellpower, +1% Spell Crit, +1% Haste]

- Bindings of The Five Thunders: Replaced +9 Spirit with Strength.

- Boots of The Five Thunders: Replaced +10 Spirit with Strength. Added 1% Haste.

- Kilt of The Five Thunders: [+17 Stamina, +17 Intellect, +16 Spirit, +10 Strength, +11 Spellpower] -> [+12 Strength, +14 Agility, +12 Stamina, +18 Intellect, +12 Strength, +18 Spellpower, +1% Haste]

Zul'Gurub Mini Set Changes List
Paladin
- Replaced spirit with agility on all tier pieces.

- Zandalar Freethinker's Bindings: Replaced 11 Healing Power with 6 Spellpower and 6 Healing Power.

- Zandalar Freethinker's Belt: Replaced 26 Healing Power with 12 Spellpower and 14 Healing Power.

Druid
- Replaced 6 Strength to add 3 more intellect for all rare and epic necklaces

Shaman
- Replaced spirit with agility for all rare and epic necklaces.

Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj Mini Set Changes List

Paladin
- Blade of Eternal Justice: Increased attack speed from 2.30 seconds to 1.9 seconds. Removed 9 Strength,increased Intellect from 7 to 8, Increased Stamina from 11 to 16, Changed 3 MP5 to 40 Spellpower and 1% Dodge.

- Cape of Eternal Justice: Replaced 5 Mana Regen with 14 Spellpower.

Tier 1 Changes List (Molten Core)
Paladin
- Spirit converted to Agility, Healing Power now gives both Spellpower and Healing Power that equals to the original healing power value when combined.

- Lawbringer Chestguard: +13 Spirit  -> Agility, +22 Healing Power  -> +15 Spellpower and +9 Healing Power

- Lawbringer Boots: +10 Spirit  -> Agility, +18 Healing Power  -> +11 Spellpower and +7 Healing Power

- Lawbringer Belt: +8 Spirit -> Agility, +18 Healing Power  -> +11 Spellpower and +7 Healing Power

- Lawbringer Gauntlets: +14 Spirit -> Agility, +18 Healing Power  -> +11 Spellpower and +7 Healing Power

- Lawbringer Helm: +10 Spirit  -> Agility, +22 Healing Power  -> +15 Spellpower and +9 Healing Power

- Lawbringer Legplates: +18 Spirit  -> Agility, +22 Healing Power  -> +15 Spellpower and +9 Healing Power

- Lawbringer Spaulders: +8 Spirit -> Agility, +18 Healing Power  -> +11 Spellpower and +7 Healing Power

- Lawbringer Bracers: +11 Spirit -> Agility

Priest
- All pieces now give spellpower and healing power that are equal to the original healing power combined.

- Boots of Prophecy: +18 Healing Power -> +11 Spellpower and +7 Healing Power

- Circlet of Prophecy: +12 Spellpower -> +12 Spellpower and +11 Healing Power

- Girdle of Prophecy: +9 Spellpower -> +9 Spellpower and +7 Healing Power

- Gloves of Prophecy: +18 Healing Power -> +11 Spellpower and +7 Healing Power

- Pants of Prophecy: +22 Healing Power -> +12 Spellpower and +11 Healing Power

- Mantle of Prophecy: +9 Spellpower -> +9 Spellpower and +7 Healing Power

- Robes of Prophecy: +22 Healing Power -> +12 Spellpower and +11 Healing Power

- Vambraces of Prophecy: +24 Healing Power -> +13 Spellpower and +11 Healing Power

Tier 2 Changes List (Blackwing Lair/Ragnaros/Onyxia)

Priest
- All pieces now give spellpower and healing power that are equal to the original healing power combined.

- Belt of Transcendence: +26 Healing Power -> +13 Spellpower and +14 Healing Power

- Bindings of Transcendence :+33 Healing Power -> +15 Spellpower and +18 Healing Power

- Boots of Transcendence: +35 Healing Power -> +18 Spellpower and +18 Healing Power

Tier 2.5 Changes List  (Temple of Ahn'Qiraj)
- Spirit removed from all pieces of the Warlock, Hunter and Shaman sets, the following buffs were added to compensate for this:

Hunter:
- Striker's Footguards: Spellpower increased from 6 to 11

- Striker's Pauldrons: Spellpower increased from 6 to 9
