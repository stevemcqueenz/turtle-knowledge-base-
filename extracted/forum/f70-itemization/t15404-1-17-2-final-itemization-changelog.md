Greetings! We're happy to present the **final** itemization changelog detailing all item related additions and changes coming in **Patch 1.17.2: Tower of Karazhan.**

This patch has been a massive undertaking for everyone in the team involved, amassing over **1300(!)** new items for this patch alone, some with completely new effects such as the new relics and quite a few items from Upper Karazhan. This total number does not count the existing item changes, item set changes and item set additions such as the many new and unique set bonuses added with Alternative Tier Sets and Tier 3.5, a few off-set item sets from Upper Karazhan and a few item sets for Jewelcrafting.

Keep in mind all changes are subject to change and to not look at these changes in a vacuum, this is the massive class changes patch after all.

 **Summary:**
- Adjusted quite a few dungeon drops, especially changing Uncommon items in dungeons at or above level 40 to be Rare quality and buffed accordingly. We've also buffed the majority of Gilneas City boss drops to make the dungeon desirable outside of completing quests.

- We've turned the dial back slightly on most of the Moroes drops.

- Almost all of the Royal Seals of Eldre'thalas have been revamped to be useful for majority of specializations, exceptions being already good for all the specializations of that class.

- Quite a few old items were adjusted and a few new ones were added in accordance to the weapon skill formula rework.

- Moved the new items away from the original Ouro loot table and into a new one alongside with even more new items in it.

- Buffed most of the underperforming caster staves in tiers below Naxxramas more worthwhile to use, primarily buffing their spellpower to be near equal to one-handed weapon and off-hand frill/shield options.

- Buffed reputation rewards for Warsong Gulch and Arathi Basin, making the grind much more worthwhile.

- Buffed Atiesh for Priest to have spellpower equal to Warlock and Mage, and Atiesh for Druid to have more feral attack power, changed the aura granted to be useful for all roles and added spellpower similiar to the Priest version.

- Revamped or improved a few existing set bonuses, notably the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj Tier Set for Hunter and Zul'Gurub Tier Set for Warlock.

- Revamped itemization on a few original tier sets due to them no longer being split in power between multiple specializations thanks to the introduction of Alternative Tier Sets.
 **Tier Set Bonus Changes:**

Majority of these changes were made to integrate Survival Hunter and Deep Destruction Warlock abilities into existing tier sets, this was done instead of adding completely new tier sets due to these specializations requiring the same stats as the current sets provide. In addition to this we've polished and reworked a few underwhelming set bonuses to be much more useful and relevant.
As a specific note, the Hunter Tier 2 8 Set Bonus was converted to be a personal buff due this effect being way too powerful if it also included melee attack power and to make it a less mandatory effect as a raid debuff.

 **Stat Adjustments**:
- Meditation's stat budget has seen a lookover. In simple terms: Some items should see a small nerf while some items should see an overall buff from this change. If a Meditation item is not listed in the stat specific changelog section below then the item is unchanged.

- Resillience is seeing a complete rework. Instead of reducing your chance to be critically struck, it will instead reduce the damage of critical strikes and damage over time effects on you, making it much more oriented for PvP combat, as it was mostly utilized by PvE tanks to get rid of critical strikes from bosses.

 **The conversion rate is:** 1% reduced chance to get critically struck is turned into 3% reduced damage from critical strikes and damage over time effects. To avoid any confusion, anything in the changelog that lists resillience as 3% is for the new version of the stat, equal to 1% of the old version.

Please keep in mind that this change will go live alongside other class centric changes such as the addition of the Alternative Tier Sets and the second batch of Class Changes, don't look at this in a vacuum.

 **Naxxramas Tier Set Token Disenchanting:**
We have introduced alternate sets for Tier 3 (Naxxramas) and recognize that the scraps obtained from raid trash drops may not suffice for all the new gear that raiders will desire. To avoid excessive farming for a limited group, we are introducing an alternative method for obtaining scraps. Moving forward, you will have the option to disenchant tokens dropped in Naxxramas, with the following rewards available:

- Priest, Mage, Warlock: 7-15 Cloth Scraps

- Warrior, Rogue: 5-10 Leather Scraps, 5-10 Plate Scraps

- Paladin, Hunter, Shaman, Druid: 7-15 Chain Scraps, 1-5 Leather Scraps, 1-5 Plate Scraps
We hope these changes provide a more balanced and efficient way for all raiders to acquire the necessary materials for their Tier 3 sets without excessive grinding. Our goal is to ensure a smoother progression experience while still maintaining the challenge and reward structure of Naxxramas. Thank you for your continued dedication, and we look forward to seeing how these updates enhance your journey.
