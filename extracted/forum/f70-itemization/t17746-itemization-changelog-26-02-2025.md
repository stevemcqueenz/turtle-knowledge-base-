# Itemization Changelog 26/02/2025

## Post 120971 by Crite (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-02-26T07:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120971#p120971 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! We have made some minor adjustments to a few items to improve their quality and functionality.

- Redemption Crown: Added 17 Agility, Reduced Strength from 28 to 24, Redcued Stamina from 27 to 19.

- Redemption Chestplate: Reduced Physical Hit from 2% to 1%, Increased Physical Crit from 1% to 2%, Increased Attack Power from 64 to 72.

- Ring of Redemption: Removed 6 MP5, Added 1% Spell Crit.

- Band of Redemption: Reduced Agility from 20 to 15, Removed 1% Physical Hit, Added 1% Haste.

- Ring of the Dreamwalker: Increased healing power from 37 to 46.

- Dreamwalker Slippers: Reduced spellpower from 26 to 22, removed 2 haste, added 2 spellcrit.

- Earthfury Sabatons: Removed 1 Haste, Increased intellect from 10 to 16.

- Sabatons of Ten Storms: Reduced Strength from 18 to 15, increased intellect from 10 to 13.

- Earthfury Leggings: Increased Strength from 17 to 21, lowered agility from 21 to 14, lowered stamina from 21 to 16, increased intellect from 13 to 18.

- Leggings of Ten Storms: Reduced Strength from 25 to 21, lowered agility from 21 to 16, increased intellect from 13 to 17.

- Earthshatter Leggings: Removed 1 haste, increased intellect from 15 to 19.

- Earthfury Girdle: Removed 1 Physical Hit.

- Girdle of Ten Storms: Removed 1 Physical Crit, Added Physical Hit, increased agility from 15 to 18.

- Earthshatter Girdle: Removed 1 Haste.

- Earthshatter Breastplate: Reduced Strength from 34 to 30, increased intellect from 17 to 21.

- Earthfury Pauldrons: Removed 1 Physical Crit, added 1 Physical Hit.

- Earthshatter Pauldrons: Removed 35 armor penetratrion.

- Crown of Ten Storms: Reduced Strength from 20 to 15.

- Earthshatter Crown: Reduced Strength from 24 to 19, lowered agility from 20 to 16, increased intellect from 16 to 19.

- Desert Wind Talisman | Removed 15 agility, removed 1 Physical Crit, added 3 mount speed, increased haste from 1 to 2.
 **Updated:** Desert Wind Talisman | Partially reverted, reduced Agility from 15 to 10.
