Greetings! We're happy to present to you the Itemization Changelog for **Patch 1.18.0:** Scars of the Past.

Alongside changes coming with this patch, we've also included a **"Catch-Up"** changelog, this changelog includes all itemization changes done since the release of **Patch 1.17.2:** Tower of Karazhan.

As always, keep in mind all changes are subject to change.

 **Item Additions:**

We've added new items to the following sources and locations:
- Quest Rewards (Grim Reaches, Balor, Northwind, Slickwick Oil Rig, Earthen Ring)

- Rare Creatures in the New Zones (Grim Reaches, Balor and Northwind)

- New Dungeons (Dragonmaw Retreat and Stormwrought Ruins)
 - Epic Quality Items (3 in Dragonmaw Retreat, 2 in Stormwrought Ruins)

 - Rare Bind-On-Equip Items from Non-Boss Creatures

- Dungeon Extensions (Razorfen Kraul, Razorfen Downs, Blackfathom Deeps and Zul'Farrak)

- Raid Extension (Molten Core)

- Crafting Recipes

- Reputations (See Below)

- Secret Methods

 **Vampirism Adjustment**
The Vampirism stat will now heal for 70% of damage dealt by area of effect spells and abilities. This stat has become quite utilized in creature farming which we're glad that it has found its use, but its benefits have ended up much stronger than we've intended when damage is dealt to multiple creatures at the same time. We hope that this change slightly pulls back on this very strong use case and still keeps it useful.

 **Concoctions**
We're introducting a new type of Alchemy crafted consumable called Concoctions. Concoctions consist of two or more existing elixirs or long duration consumables, combining their effects into one consumable item. This is primarily to open up inventory and buff room for those that have to use these potions at the same time. Concoctions do not stack with the potions they're made from or with consumables that have similiar effects to the potions. We're adding the following concoctions with this update:

- **Concoction of the Emerald Mongoose:** Combination of Elixir of the Mongoose and Dreamshard Elixir.

- **Concoction of the Arcane Giant:** Combination of Elixir of Giants and Greater Arcane Elixir.

- **Concoction of the Dreamwater:** Combination of Winterfall Firewater and Dreamtonic.
