# Introduction and Fundamentals

## Post 52793 by Dragunovi (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-10T14:25:00Z

Greetings and welcome to the Itemization section of the Turtle WoW Forum! Here you will find dedicated forum posts that give details on additions, changes and reworks done on different parts of Turtle WoW. From massive profession craft revamps to new recipe additions, you can find details for it all here.

Now that the introduction is out of the way, I'll get into the Fundamentals. These changes and additions are deep rooted and their effects can be found in many parts of itemization and the game as a whole.

 **Reminder that anything slated to be added or changed is subject to change before release.**

 **New Stats:**
- **1.16.0:**  Haste: You can find this effect listed as "Increases your attack and casting speed by X%.". This effect passively increases you attack and casting speed, useful for classes that rely more on weapon and item procs on hit such as hybrids and dual wielding classes.

- **1.16.0:**  Meditation: You can find this effect listed as "Allows X% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.". Useful for most mana related classes, but especially Druids and Priests which have spirit in their Tier Sets and spirit related talents.

- **1.16.1:**  Armor Penetration: You can find this effect listed as "Your attacks ignore X of the target's armor.". Primarily for physical damage dealers, its effect snowballs as the enemy takes more and more damage with less armor, especially powerful if the target has armor reducing effects in a raid environment to stack the stat with.

- **1.17.0:**  Regeneration: You can find this effect listed as "Allows X% of your Health regeneration to work while in combat.". Especially useful for classes without self healing capabilities, allowing health sustain in combat.

- **1.17.0:**  Vampirism: You can find this effect listed as "% of your damage is returned as healing." which is pretty self explanatory, especially useful for outdoor content. Primarily found in Gilneas as the source of the magic for the lifestealing effects originate from it.

- **1.17.0:**  Resillience: You can find this effect listed as "Reduces your chance to get critically hit by X%". Currently only found in set bonuses for PvP sets, a few items in the Lower Karazhan Halls and a few other miscellaneous sources.
 **General Adjustments:**

- **1.16.0:** The stat budget for Health every 5 seconds (HP5) is massively reduced, its now mainly used for flavor purposes due to how weak it is and items that have had this stat in Vanilla have seen some slight buffs in compensation.

- **1.17.0:** Increased Chance to Parry stat cost has been decreased from **15.65 to 13.35**.

This essentially means items with parry now have room for a bit more stats. Parry Haste was valued too much compared to Dodge where the Increased Parry got its extra cost. We hope this makes the stat more viable to use in gearing without sacrificing too many other stats.
This change affects **40+ items**, we won't list all changes here for that reason, but you can expect small to medium increases in other stats the item utilizes depending on the amount of parry stat.

- **1.17.0:** Most Two-Handed Maces and Polearms now grant Feral Attack Power, granting 14 for every item level above 60.
Below are a few examples and not an extensive list of all weapons with Feral AP:

- Bonecrusher: 42 Feral AP

- Unyielding Maul: 28 Feral AP

- Boneshatter Maul: 70 Feral AP

- Nat Pagle's Fish Terminator: 70 Feral AP

- Finkle's Lava Dredger: 140 Feral AP

- Herald of Woe: 210 Feral AP

- Barb of the Sand Reaver: 238 Feral AP

- R14 PvP Polearms: 252 Feral AP

- Eye of Nerub: 322 Feral AP

- **Pre-1.15.0** All skills including Polearms can now be learned at Level 5. Polearm learning cost has been reduced to 10 Silver.

- **1.16.0:** Shamans can now learn Two-Handed Mace and Axe skills and the ability to Parry from trainers and no longer require a talent in the Enhancement tree to do so.

- **1.17.0:** Druids can now learn the Polearm skill from trainers.

- **1.17.0::** Polearms below Item Level 25 had their level requirements adjusted to be usable at a lower level. Back in Vanilla all polearms had to have a level requirement of 20 at minimum regardless of item level due to the skill learning level and no race starting with the skill, now that the skill is learnable at level 5 the proper level requirements have been instated.

Gargoyle's Bite: Level 18
Pearl-encrusted Spear: Level 16
Impaling Harpoon: Level 17

> What does that code represent?
Function RollMeleeOutcomeAgainst decides what will be result of attack of "this" unit against "pVictim" of attack.
It is used for any situation. I mean player vs player, player vs mob, mob vs player, mob vs mob.
Result of attack is one of the: miss, dodge, parry, glancing, block, crit, crushing, hit.

I assume you interested in situation when player attacks mob from the front because mobs doesn't parry from behind.
Your specific mob also have nothing to increase dodge, parry or blocks. I will check all kinds of level difference and weapon skill bonuses of player.

Default value for dodge can be found at line [https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/de ... .cpp#L2591](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/development/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp#L2591)
next line adds bonuses from equipment talents and skills. So I assume base chance to dodge of mob is 5.00%
Chance to parry can be found here [https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/de ... .cpp#L2623](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/development/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp#L2623)
Base chance to parry of mob is 5.00
Chance to block can be found here
[https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/de ... .cpp#L2653](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/development/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp#L2653)
Base chance to block of mob is 5.00

To simplify calculations, number 5.00 is converted to 500.

First line mentioning skills is [https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/de ... .cpp#L2115](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/development/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp#L2115)
It calls GetSkillMaxForLevel. It is implemented here [https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/de ... ter.h#L328](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/development/src/game/Objects/SpellCaster.h#L328)
It figures out level of attacker against current target and multiplies it by 5. It has complex logic when attacker is worldboss, but when attacker is player it just returns player level.

So if you are lvl 60 and attack normal mob of lvl 60, then
attackerMaxSkillValueForLevel = 60*5=300
victimMaxSkillValueForLevel = 60*5=300
here race and bonuses are irrelevant

If you are lvl 60 and attack worldboss, then
attackerMaxSkillValueForLevel = 60*5=300
victimMaxSkillValueForLevel = 63*5=315

After this, server takes into account weapon and defense skills [https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/de ... .cpp#L2118](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/development/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp#L2118)
Checks current weapon, checks current player skill for this weapon. For example when you leveled your skill to 80 out of 100.
Logic is in function [https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/de ... .cpp#L6119](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/development/src/game/Objects/Player.cpp#L6119)
Here you get permanent bonuses and temporary bonuses. I know that talents like Anticipation and racials like Axe Specialization works different than bonuses from Edgemaster's Handguards. But I don't know which are temporary and which are permanent.
Anyway, they sumed both.

So if you are orc with axe of lvl 60, leveled axe skill to 305 then
attackerWeaponSkill = 305
if you are orc with sword of lvl 60, leveled sword skill to 300 then
attackerWeaponSkill = 300
if you are orc with axe of lvl 60, leveled axe skill only to 280 then
attackerWeaponSkill = 280
if you are orc with axe of lvl 60, leveled axe skill only to 305 and wear Edgemaster's Hadguards then
attackerWeaponSkill = 312

Defense skill of mob depends of type and level. But we probably interested in two situations.
[https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/de ... .cpp#L2119](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/development/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp#L2119)
If you attack normal mob of 60 lvl
victimDefenseSkill = 300
If you attack worldboss
victimDefenseSkill = 315

After this, server calculates skillDiff [https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/de ... .cpp#L2122](https://github.com/vmangos/core/blob/development/src/game/Objects/Unit.cpp#L2122)
