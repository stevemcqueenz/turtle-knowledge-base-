Greetings! At long last, here is the changelog detailing almost all single item and item related addition and change arrived with the release of **Patch 1.17.0: Beyond the Greymane Wall**

 **Keep in mind that the changes and additions are subject to change and not final.**

Introducing Two New Stats:
We're adding **two** new stats with the release of 1.17.0, there might not be a lot of items with these stats currently but the amount of them will increase as we introduce more content, they're as follows:
- **Vampirism:** You can find this effect listed as "% of your damage is returned as healing." which is pretty self explanatory, especially useful for outdoor content.

- **Reduced Chance to Get Critically Hit:** Basically what it says in the name, this is primarily a PvP stat with some use to tanks.

- Haste stat now grants **double** casting speed of the attack speed bonus. Ex. Increases your attack speed by 1% and casting speed by 2%.
EDIT: This change had to be reverted last second due to overpowered synergy with healers, more powerful haste might return for casters as a seperate stat at a later patch.

- In addition, Increased Chance to Parry stat cost has been decreased from **15.65 to 13.35.**
This essentially means items with parry now have room for a bit more stats. Parry Haste was valued too much compared to Dodge where the Increased Parry got its extra cost. We hope this makes the stat more viable to use in gearing without sacrificing too many other stats.
This change affects **40+ items**, we won't list all changes here for that reason, but you can expect small increases in other stats the item utilizes.

 **Item Adjustments**
| As a (not so) small note on items such as Bloodvine, Titanic Leggings and Spellweaver's Turban and other similiar items that stick out compared the rest, keep in mind that these items were made as catch-up gear later into the lifecycle of Vanilla with the thought that earlier released raids were being ran much less than before and were made to skip gear progression by a long shot and get into the endgame much faster, but Turtle is different in this regard. All raids are being ran quite often and there's no end to the content development and to the lifecycle of the server of the server.

The existence of catch-up gear in an environment there is no need to catch up causes a lot of issues in the gearing flow the endgame at large, especially the early endgame where this affects the first few raids you conquer. Our intentions aren't to punish players by taking their power away, its to provide a healthy progression of items and for the players to get the enjoyment of acquiring new upgrades to their items at a decent pace and not get stuck with a single piece of item for the entirety of the game.

All of these nerfs were both compensated via the additions of new endgame gear with Lower Karazhan Halls and the Emerald Sanctum and via making already existing items powerful enough to act as a step in gear progression for players to enjoy (which you will see in this list of changes). Thank you for understanding.
