# New World Epics and Adjustments

## Post 52798 by Dragunovi (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-10T14:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52798#p52798 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! We have compiled a list of all changes and additions related to epics that are acquired as very rare world drops. This post will be split into two, showcasing the new additions to the roster and then a comprehensive list of changes that have been done to existing world epics.
Profession changes and additions, new relics, endgame itemization, item sets and new item drops are listed on different topics found on the Itemization section.

 **Keep in mind that the changes slated to release in 1.17.0 are subject to change and not final.**

 **New World Epics:**

- [Shieldbreaker Arbalest](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60782)

- [Orb of Purified Light](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60783)

- [Breastplate of Beast Mastery](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60784)

- Effect is being revamped to the following and will be contained in one spell in 1.17.0: Increases your pet's damage by 3%, maximum health by 5% and critical strike chance by 2%.

- [Choker of Cauterization](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60785)

- [The Night Watchman](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60786)

- [Scythe of the Harvest](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60787)

- [Sandals of Inner Peace](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60788)

- [Finkle's Accelerator](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60789)

- [The Wolf Horn of Orgrimmar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80793)
 **World Epic Adjustments:**
- Icemail Jerkin: New Effect:
When struck in combat has a chance of freezing the attacker in place for 3 sec.

- Stonerender Gauntlets: New Effect:
Decreases the magical resistances of your spell targets by 10.

- Ring of Saviors: On Use Effect cooldown reduced from 30 to 5 Minutes.

- Lifestone: On Use Effect cooldown reduced from 30 to 15 Minutes. 10 HP5 -> 15 HP5

- Lady Maye's Pendant: 10 Stamina -> 29 Healing Power, 19 Intellect -> 14 Intellect + 2 MP5

- Jeweled Amulet of Cainwyn: 10 Spirit -> 11 Spellpower

- Blade of Hanna: All Stats increased from 11 to 14, Added +1% Hit and Crit

- **1.17.0:** Cloudkeeper Legplates: On Use Attack Power cooldown reduced from 15 to 10 minutes.

- **1.17.0:** Lion Horn of Stormwind: Proc changed from: "When struck in combat has a 1% chance of increasing all party member's armor by 250 for 30 sec." to "When struck in combat has a 2% chance of increasing all party member's attack and casting speed by 5% for 30 sec."
