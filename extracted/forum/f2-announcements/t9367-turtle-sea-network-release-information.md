# Turtle SEA Network Release Information

## Post 61322 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-09-07T20:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=61322#p61322 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Greetings everyone!** We are here to share some more information regarding our SEA Network.

 **Multiple Realms**

Our SEA launch will include two PvE realms, **Ravenshire** and **Hogger**. This will ensure ample room for the population, and provide a low ping option for a wide variety of locations.

- **Ravenshire** will be located in Singapore, providing low latency for the OCE/SEA region.

- **Hogger** will be located in Russia, providing low latency for the Chinese mainland region.

 **Timeline**

Both realms are now privately online for final-phase testing. If no urgent issues arise, we will be officially launching **Saturday**, the **9th**.

 **Network Connections and Transfers**

We are planning to allow the **one-way** transfer of accounts starting Day 1 from our EU Network to our SEA Network. Higher-level characters will initially be locked and will be unlocked gradually as players level.

Unfortunately, it is not technically feasible for our Asian and European networks to be connected. This means we will not be able to have cross-network battlegrounds or any sort of shared infrastructure.
