Greetings (to yet another update),

The SEA network has gathered way more attention than we initially anticipated. This has lead to the 2 new SEA network realms being fully populated with enormous queues, on top of the already full UK main realm.

Opening another EU realm in the coming weeks. Exact location, date and type of realm will be announced soon.
Thank you for your continued patience and support!
