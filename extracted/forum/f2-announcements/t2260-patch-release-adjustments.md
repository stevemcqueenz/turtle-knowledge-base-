# Patch Release Adjustments

## Post 14083 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2021-11-23T21:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14083#p14083 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!

As we're coming closer and closer to release we as a team have realised that the amount of additions and changes that are coming no longer fit in the scope of a content patch. As happens with a passionate development team, more and more things got added over time which would mean calling this an unnamed patch release would undermine the work put in & the content released.

It is therefore that we have decided that the next release will officially be an expansion instead of a patch release and we have moved from labeling it **1.15.2** to **1.16.0**. This means that any previous spoilers & teasers announced to you as content to come in **1.15.2** will now be released as **1.16.0**. Our time schedule has not changed, this is only a version update.

And now, I would like to present to you: **[Patch 1.16.0 — Mysteries of Azeroth](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=2253)**
