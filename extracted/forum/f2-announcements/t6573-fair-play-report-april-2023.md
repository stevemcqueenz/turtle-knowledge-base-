# Fair Play Report — April 2023

## Post 41433 by Pompa (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-04-13T18:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41433#p41433 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!

As you may already know, we heavily monitor all gold transactions so we'd like to provide you some numbers regarding our fight against any form of RMT, botting, and unfair play in order to provide you a **clean and bot-free environment**:

In the last 60 days...

- ~150 gold-selling services advertisers have been banned.

- ~300 accounts related to gold-selling services (mules, banks...) have been banned.

- ~350 accounts of leveling bots have been banned.

- ~100 accounts of cheaters have been banned.

- ~120 player accounts that bought gold have been temporarily banned (and non-legit gold removed from their characters).

 *Please don't support individuals that are actively harming our server, the gold they offer comes in most cases from stolen player accounts. Be aware that if you are involved in these activities your account will be automatically flagged and eventually your ban will come.*
