# Hardcore Update — Inferno Mode

## Post 47793 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-05-29T03:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47793#p47793 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! Today we are pleased to announce our **Hardcore Update**! This update will come in two parts.

 **Part One, Coming on June 16th:**

 **Inferno Mode**
- Otherwise known as HC60, the long awaited max-level continuation of the Hardcore challenge! This gamemode can be taken by current or future Hardcore characters. Upon reaching level 55, an attunement questline will be unlocked which allows players to opt-in to Inferno Mode. Upon completion of the questline, players will gain a [Inferno] effect, allowing them to continue their Hardcore challenge at max level, and remain part of the Hardcore ecosystem.
 **Hardcore Guildbanks**
- Players with the [Inferno] gamemode will now be able to purchase and access Guild Bank tabs. Hardcore players without [Inferno] gamemode will still be unable to access Guild Banks.

 **Part Two, Coming in 1.17:**

 **[Hardcore] Channel**
- All Hardcore characters are now added to the [Hardcore] world channel by default.

- With this change, we will be retiring the <Still Alive> guild upon release.

- With the retiring of the guild, [Still Alive] will become the title given to all Hardcore players to distinguish them. It will be possible to hide this title.
 **New Titles**
- Still Alive - Title for all those who are in the Hardcore or Inferno gamemodes.

- Vanquisher of the Flames - Hardcore Ragnaros Kill

- Vanquisher of the Dragons - Hardcore Nefarian Kill

- Vanquisher of the Qiraji - Hardcore C'Thun Kill

- Vanquisher of the Scourge - Hardcore Kel'Thuzad Kill

- Ascended Champion - The deal is done, penned in blood. Did you really think you'd be able to back out now?

Unfortunately, <Still Alive> causes quite a few problems.

 1. GMs need to be in multiple public Guilds at the same time to moderate them. This is not possible.
 2. Lack of community building (Players are actively discouraged from joining guilds with friends, as they need <Still Alive> to access the HC ecosystem for groups and trades)
 3. Players who /gquit at level 1 from spam immediately lose all access to groups and trades.

List goes on, but the community damage one is the biggest issue. Guilds are, as a system, intended to be groups of people who want to play together and socialize with eachother. A guild of 10,000 people is very departed from this concept.

Folks will still be able to find groups easily with the [Hardcore] channel, they just now have the added ability to seek out meaningful community without being punished for it.
