Zug-zug friends!

Can you believe it’s our 7th holiday season together?

Thank you all for playing on Turtle WoW — it truly means the world to us.

Find **Frosty the Snowman** in your capital city to journey to [Winter Veil Vale](https://turtlecraft.gg/watch/event-winter-veil-vale) and join the festive activities!

 **The event runs from December 15 to January 15.**

- All quests have been reset for everyone.

- New snowball fight quest targets have been set — happy hunting!

- **New this year:** Find a tiny snowman in Winter Veil Vale and join the collective effort to build the biggest snowman in Azeroth, granting a world buff to everyone in the area! ![❄️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2744.svg)![☃️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2603.svg)

- Changes to the Snowball Boss encounter:
 - The boss now patrols the area, respawning every 50-80 minutes at random locations.

 - Anyone can tap into the boss fight, regardless of the party or raid that initiated it.

 - The boss itself won’t drop loot. Be sure to accept the daily repeatable quest **Peace Comes With a Price** for a chance to earn festive rewards, including a unique mount!
 **Presents from the radio team, airing this holiday season on the Everlook Broadcasting Co. station!**

- Make sure to listen to our new Winter Veil radio play, **A Very Arthas Christmas**!

- A special Winter Veil edition of **EatsMeats™** — festive feasts and holiday treats await!

- **Airtime Voucher:** the perfect way to send congratulations to your friends and foes alike!

- **It’s beginning to look a lot like Warcraft** — an old classic, now with a few bloodstains for the battleground enthusiasts!![🩸](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1fa78.svg)![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg)

- Good music, congratulations and festive tales from everyone

- **Lore Scrolls** are back on air!

Happy Winter Veil! ![🎄](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f384.svg)![✨](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2728.svg)![❄️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2744.svg)
