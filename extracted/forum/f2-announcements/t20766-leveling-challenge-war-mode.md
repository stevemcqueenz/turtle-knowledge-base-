# Leveling Challenge — War Mode

## Post 144045 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-08-09T19:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144045#p144045 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/patch_1180/Warmode_is_Hell.png)

Hello everyone!

With the upcoming PvP changes in Patch [1.18.0 — Scars of the Past](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=63&t=17688), we've decided that **War Mode** needs a redesign to align better with the new PvP philosophy.

Since battlegrounds will now allow players to earn experience and level entirely through PvP, we want War Mode rewards to better reflect the playstyle of those who truly enjoy waging war against the other faction.

Below are the updated rules and conditions for War Mode starting with the upcoming patch:

 **Permanent PvP Mode**
War Mode puts your character in permanent PvP. You can toggle it on or off at any time at you Battlemaster. The challenge will still end automatically when you reach level 60.

 *We may extend War Mode beyond level 60 once the PvP system supports rewards that don't interfere with the PvP ranking. Right now, we can't give exclusive Honor rewards to War Mode players at level 60, as that would put those without it at a clear disadvantage in the PvP ranking system. Right now, we don't feel that reputation rewards alone are meaningful enough to justify keeping War Mode active at level 60 in its current form.*

 **How to Enable War Mode**
War Mode can no longer be selected at character creation. We want newcomers to choose this playstyle with a clear understanding of the dangers of the open world, rather than blindly chasing an XP bonus. To enable it, speak to a **Battlemaster** in your capital city.

 **Experience for Killing Players in the Open World**
You now gain experience for killing a player, equivalent to the XP you would earn from killing two normal mobs of that same level. XP gain follows the same rules as honor:
- Repeatedly killing the same target yields diminishing XP.

- After 10 kills of the same player, you will no longer earn XP from them.
 **Bonus Reputation**
Gain +10% reputation from any battleground reputation sources.

 **Bonus XP — Open World**
Gain +5% XP from creature kills in the open world.

 **Bonus XP — Battlegrounds & Arenas**
Gain +20% XP on top of base XP from any experience source in battlegrounds or arenas: wins, losses, flag captures, objectives, or killing enemy commanders.

Base PvP Objective XP Values:

- **Warsong Gulch**
 - Win — 4.5% XP bar, **5.4% with War Mode**

 - Loss — 1.5% XP bar, **1.8% with War Mode**

 - Flag capture — 0.5% XP bar per capture (up to 1.5% if all three are captured), **0.6% / 1.8% with War Mode**. Losing teams still earn XP for captured flags.

- **Arathi Basin**
 - Win — 4.5% XP bar, **5.4% with War Mode**

 - Loss — 1.5% XP bar, **1.8% with War Mode**

 - Every 300 points gained — 0.3% XP bar, **0.36% with War Mode**

- **Alterac Valley**
 - Win — 6% XP bar, **7.2% with War Mode**

 - Loss — 2% XP bar, **2.4% with War Mode**

 - Enemy commander killed — 1% XP bar, **1.2% with War Mode**

 - Enemy bunker/tower destroyed— 0.5% XP bar, **0.6% with War Mode**

War Mode may receive further gameplay changes as the PvP system continues to evolve.

 *Note: If you already have War Mode enabled and don't want to continue under the new rules, you'll be able to turn it off once, regardless of whether you've already used your one disable before the patch.*

The changes to Warmode are well intentioned, but I'm afraid they miss the mark.

It must be understood that even though BGs and Wpvp both involve killing players, they are two vastly different aspects of the game and should not be forced together under the umbrella of one challenge.

One is a hyper-focused team effort to accomplish specific objectives in an isolated, neverchanging environment. PvP is the entirety of the experience here.

The other is a fluid, unpredictable experience that can play out in an infinite number of ways depending on where you are, what you're doing, and who else is there. There are no set objectives here, and PvP is a part of a greater, ever shifting whole.

There no guarantee whatsoever that a Wpvp enjoyer will want to spam BGs, and vice versa. I therefore suggest catering to each of these two separately. Here are a couple of quick suggestions:

For Battlegrounds:

 - BG leveling challenge: increased XP from battlegrounds and their associated quests, reduced xp from all other sources. Unique title / mount upon reaching 60.

For Warmode:

 - Leave the xp bonus as it was.

 - Make it so flagging for PvP in a contested or hostile zone keeps you in PvP for at least one hour. This is to combat the cheap practice of ganking lowbies then getting yourself unflagged by the time the cavalry arrives.

The claim that Warmode is hurting anyone is disingenuous. Here's why.

 1) It's an optional challenge. Yes, you get 20% more xp, but you're also at risk of getting PvP'ed, fairly or otherwise, at any point in time. It could happen while you're questing, gathering, or on your way to a dungeon. You could lose your precious world buffs. AOE farming in the open world, or even just pulling multiple mobs as a class that can cleave? HA. GOOD LUCK. All of the above WILL inevitably delay your leveling speed, putting you at a not-insignificant handicap compared to the average PVE'er. The 20% xp bonus fairly bridges that gap in my opinion, allowing you to both level at a reasonable pace AND experience world PvP in all of its wonders and terrors.

 2) It's the only challenge that you can actually OPT OUT OF right now. Constant PvP get too old for you? Too many ganks by high lvl players / twinks in your low level zone (not saying this isn't a bad thing, I will address it below)? No problem. Unlike any other challenge currently on the server, you may easily change your mind and OPT OUT.

This completely invalidates anyone advocating to nerf or shutdown Warmode for the sake of those who complain about being ganked on it, because they are frankly being entitled children who want their cake and to eat it too. After all, the risks are abundantly clear, and they have 1) the option to NOT take the challenge, and 2) the privilege to OPT OUT of the challenge if the risks prove too much. What more could they ask for?

Now to address the claim that all World PvP is toxic. As someone who has leveled several Warmode chars to 60, I have experienced equal parts fair PvP, as well as completely oppressive, one-sided ganks (most of which were concentrated around the first 20 or so levels). Everyone agrees that fair fights are fun, there's no need to talk about that.

But when it comes to hopeless ganks (60 vs lowbie, twinks), the experience was much worse for me here, having come from an RP-PVP server back on classic. Back there, there was a very elegant countermeasure to ganks: you called for help. 60s would rush in to defend against other 60s in low level zones, and larger groups of players would band together to hunt down level-appropriate twinks, stimulating larger scale glorious World PvP. Finally getting revenge / avenged was such a satisfying part of the experience. I myself couldn't wait to get to 60, so I could be among the first to return to low-level areas when they're in need of protection, as I once did.

But how does this play out on Nord in the current ecosystem? As soon as the level 60s arrive, or larger groups of lowbies band together to fight off twinks, the offending 60 / twinks simply let themselves get unflagged, and spams taunts and emotes at the now toothless defenders. Until the groups scatter again in pursuit of their leveling, or the 60s return to whatever they were doing, then they start again.

The result is that lvl 60s lose interest in fighting off gankers as a result of getting blue-walled, and level-appropriate players lose the interest in banding together in the open world, seeking instead to level in dungeons or simply end the challenge altogether.

This is why in my earlier post I proposed a much longer cooldown time on getting unflagged, so that there's a chance of fair counterplay in these scenarios. Someone mentioned that the downside of that is that people could accidentally flag themselves. To that I say we could implement the same rules that protect HC players from accidentally flagging, such that the only way to do it is to manually flag yourself.

I think the issue is two-fold:
 1) There really isn't any WPvP content outside of a few racial leaders.
 2) There is nothing to de-incentivize jumping another player that is significantly lower level.

This is why BGs pretty much killed WPvP in Classic; it was level-relevant content for PvP while also being objective-based content.

So the real question shouldn't be, how do we bribe more players into War Mode, it should be, how can we get players out of BGs and back into WPvP?

I think getting a Bounty system set up to cover point #2 would help alleviate a lot of the outlier stuff, and would likely help ease more players into WPvP.  But working towards point #1 is key; you really need to have a variety of WPvP content scattered throughout the world that is inter-mixed with PvE content to allow for that organic overlap of the two.

Two examples off the top of my head that likely have some flaws but hopefully come across as well-intended:

PvP World Boss added to Arathi Highlands or the Hinterlands that flags all players within a notable radius as PvP, with a 5 second warning to allow players who do not wish to participate to retreat.  The Boss drops an Artifact that acts like a WSG Flag (the player carrying it is visible to all, and drops it when defeated or when they stealth).  The Artifact must be returned to a new set of buildings on the outskirts of Southshore and Tarren Mill (while we want to foster WPvP, we don't want to hinder PvE).  Once placed, the first faction to hold the Artifact for a certain duration, gets a 1-hour World Buff that grants an additional +10% Honor and +20% Rep gain to All Players of that faction.  Once the buff ends, the World Boss respawns within 15-30 mins or so.

Add a New Zone with AB-like branching Supply Chains that lead to two opposing Fortresses.  Mines, Smelters, Blacksmiths, Forresters, Sawmills, Farms, Orchards, Mills, Meaderies, Bakeries, Slaughterhouses, Fisheries, etc.  Only players flagged for PvP can "capture" a location.  As supply chains are held, that faction gains the appropriate resources; when the supply chain is broken, those resources begin to dwindle.  As resources reach certain thresholds, new items become available at Vendors, and other amenities open up, such as free repairs and discounts.  Add a new Dungeon/Raid to this Zone that uses a new damage type, and give the Vendors both consumables and recipes* that protect against that damage type.  (* Recipes would use new resources only found in this zone, and would craft more powerful consumables as well as BoP gear focused on the Dungeon/Raid in that Zone.)  Now you're combining PvP, PvE, and Crafting into and entire zone-wide activity.  Heck, for S&Gs, throw in some Rep stuff so that the Artifact buff from my previous example can come into play.

Both of these are objective based.  Both of these could help incentivize players into doing some PvP from time to time.  Both of these funnel WPvP into more concentrated areas so that War Mode players would have more people to interact with, and would thus gain additional bonuses due to all of this being PvP-related content, so more XP, Honor, and Rep for doing WPvP.
