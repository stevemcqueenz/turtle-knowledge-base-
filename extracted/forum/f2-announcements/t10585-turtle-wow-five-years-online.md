# Turtle WoW — Five Years Online

## Post 68974 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-10-05T19:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=68974#p68974 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

On October 6th, Turtle WoW reaches an important milestone — 5 years of being around! We are incredibly grateful to every player in our wonderful community for choosing Turtle WoW as their home. It has been an incredible journey, and we are thrilled to be picking up speed!

Log in to the game during the next week to receive a special reward for dedicating your time to playing here. Please note that your character must be above level 10 to qualify for the reward.

 **Patch 1.17.0 — Beyond the Greymane Wall is Live!**

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/turtlewow_patchgilneas.png)

The long-awaited content patch is finally available in the game! Discover the mysterious secrets of Gilneas, explore the enchanted woods of Hyjal, face new challenges, and uncover hidden treasures! You can find the full changelog at <https://turtlecraft.gg/gilneas> For a detailed preview, check out Vrograg the Fishslayer's [Survival Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VUW8cuI7kbE).

 **Everlook Broadcasting Co. — Goblin Tech & A DJ on Deck**

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/zappo_zapblast.png)

Prepare for an bombastic radio experience in Azeroth with Everlook Broadcasting Co.! Featuring DJs Zappo Zapblast, Vrograg the Fishslayer, Dan "Leto" Hahn, and more, this new feature can be found on your minimap. Get ready to rock out!

 **New EU Realm**

We are excited to announce the launch of a new PvP realm on October 27th. Here are some important details:

- The realm type is going to be PvP.

- Hardcore Challenge will not be available on launch.

- Any crossfaction interactions, such as chatting, grouping, guilds, and trades, will not be possible.

- We will closely monitor faction balance to ensure a healthy ecosystem, using both manual and automated systems.

- Transfers from current realm will not be available.
Stay tuned for more information!
