# Beginner’s Guilds

## Post 2461 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2019-04-07T23:33:00Z

Greetings!

A few months ago, in an effort to assist new players, we implemented a feature called **The Beginner’s Guild**.

This feature is auto inviting players into a guild, helping them easily find players who are also just starting out fresh. Once players reach **level 15** they will be removed from the guild, and thrown back into the wild.

We hope this further strengthens the community, and helps make many new friends for your future adventures in Azeroth. Please, meet **<Newcomers>**!
