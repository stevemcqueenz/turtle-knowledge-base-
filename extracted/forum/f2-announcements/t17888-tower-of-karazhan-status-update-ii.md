**Karazhan Status Update II**

Hello /everyone! Today, we have another update on the progress of Karazhan 40's development. We are nearing the end of our **QA Phase**, after which will be the release! While waiting for this release, we have a few more pieces of information we'd like to share with you all.

 **Turtle WoW Logs**

With the release of Tower of Karazhan, we are looking to release the first public version of our own server-side logging tool, **ShellStats**! We're working on having a whole lot of features and data available, however we don't want to promise too much just yet. For now, enjoy two teaser images below!

Please note, the UI will change, this is just to show off a few cool features! The name may change as well, so toss us any suggestions you have!

![IMG_2097.jpeg](./download/file.php?id=144) <dd>IMG_2097.jpeg (327.1 KiB) Viewed 26091 times</dd>

 **Donation Changes**

While it's not a new feature exactly, we find it important to remind players: if you choose to support the project, please be sure to use the links at the bottom of <https://turtlecraft.gg/donate> ! This will allow the fastest turnaround time on donations, ensuring your points are delivered quicky and accurately! We have changed our donation processing methods recently, so please avoid using PayPal's "Send again" option.

 **Class Changes 2 Update**

We would also like to mention that we plan to release a small round of **minor** balance changes to help some underperforming specs, and address a handful of bugs requiring client updates. **This round of changes will be primarily focused on numerical tuning, with only a few design changes**, as our focus has been entirely on Karazhan 40. We feel it's important to continue to stress that class changes on Turtle WoW are not a one-patch topic—we will continue iterating and updating classes as long as we need, meaning there will be more updates in the future.

 **Tower of Karazhan**

Lastly, we would like to give one more update regarding Tower of Karazhan's progress. We plan to give an **exact release date in the coming weeks**. Until then, what we will say is that the testing has been going well. For now, enjoy two more teaser images below, one of the Chess Boss and one of a ”slightly bugged” Manascale Whelp.

Stay tuned for more information!
