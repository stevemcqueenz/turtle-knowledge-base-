# Crossfaction Battlegrounds are coming to PvE Realms!

## Post 157078 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-10-25T23:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157078#p157078 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![xfac.jpg](./download/file.php?id=174) <dd>xfac.jpg (270.13 KiB) Viewed 120415 times</dd>

As indicated prior, our PvE realms will soon have **Crossfaction Battlegrounds** enabled.

This means that players, depending on team imbalance in a given Battleground, may end up fighting for the opposite faction for that game to increase fairness and reduce queue times.

A small**laucher-only update** will go out somewhere next week that will be required for Crossfaction Battlegrounds to function properly. Then on the maintenance of **November 4** we will enable them for all PvE realms.
