## Post 158192 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-11-03T12:19:00Z

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/levelling_event_2025.jpg)

Hello, everyone!

Starting **November 5**, right after the maintenance restart, the **Grand Alt'Lympic Games** kick off again on the veteran realms: **Nordanaar RPPvE** and**Tel'Abim PvP**.

Put aside your dusty old epics and go on a fresh journey! Create a brand‑new character, race to level 60 by the end of the year, and earn a unique mount for your faction.

 **The character must be created on November 5 after maintenance, not before.**
