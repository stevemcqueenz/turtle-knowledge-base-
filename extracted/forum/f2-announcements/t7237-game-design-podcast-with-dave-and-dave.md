Hello! It's an exciting Sunday for those of you interested in the lore and stories of our expansion, Mysteries of Azeroth.

We want to invite you to join DesignerDave's podcast, where he will feature our Narrative team members as special guests today!

Dave was a Warcraft III development team member, where he focused on campaign stories and level design. As we aim to maintain the core essence of the previous Warcraft games in our modification of WoW, we hope this discussion will be of great interest to you.

While the WoW fan-based development community encompasses a wide range of aspects, such as emulators, addons, and game client modifications, we will focus on fan-written stories during this podcast. The stream will begin in just two hours, so don't miss this exciting opportunity to ask a question!
