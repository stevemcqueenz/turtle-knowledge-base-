# Ambershire (RPPvE) — New EU Realm!

## Post 142471 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-08-01T10:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142471#p142471 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/ambershire/ambershire_poster.png)

Hello everyone!

With Season of Discovery sunsetting, we've seen a surge in demand for Classic+ realms. Nordanaar has recently hit over 10000 concurrent players and even started to see occasional queues again.

With the release of **[Scars of the Past](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=17688)** on **August 15**, we're expecting another natural population spike — as always happens with a major patch, and we want to make sure that Nordanaar is not overcrowded.

 **August 17**, we'll be launching a new RP-PvE realm in the European region: **Ambershire (RPPvE)**.

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/ambershire/population.png)

 *Nordanaar RPPvE population growth (2025) — 7 years online and still growing! Thank you! ![💚](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f49a.svg)*

 **Why not launch Ambershire on the same day as the patch?**
The first 48 hours after a major patch are usually the most technically unstable. Giving ourselves a short window allows us to hotfix any unexpected issues before opening the new realm.

 **What if Ambershire isn't popular and fades over time?**
In that case, it'll be merged into Nordanaar. Nordanaar players and guilds will have name priority during the merge.

 **What if Nordanaar population declines over time?**
Then Ambershire will be merged into Nordanaar, whether you like it or not. Nordanaar is eternal.

 **Will there be content progression on Ambershire?**
Yes! Ambershire will feature a progressive release of both original and Mysteries of Azeroth raiding and PvP content.

We're giving raid progression a little more breathing room this time — but at the same time, we don't want to gatekeep the new realm from the content we've planning to rollout on the existing ones. Classic raids (like Blackwing Lair) will be getting content extensions along the way, and any new dungeons or lower-tier raids we release along the way will be available on Ambershire as well.

All raid releases are aligned with **Fridays**, so that the race to clear can kick off over the weekends.

The first raiders to complete each raid on the realm will receive permanent titles to honor their achievements:
- Molten Core: **Guardian of the Flame**

- Blackwing Lair: **Blackwing's Bane**

- Onyxia's Lair: **Dragonslayer**

- Temple of Ahn'Qiraj: **Champion of the Shifting Sands**

- Naxxramas: **Champion of the Frozen Citadel**

- Tower of Karazhan: **Conqueror of Desolation**

[![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/ambershire/ambershire_roadmap.jpg)](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/ambershire/ambershire_roadmap.jpg)

 **Is it a cross-faction realm?**
Yes, same as Nordanaar.

 **Any rule or policy differences?**
Nope! The same rules that apply to Nordanaar will apply to Ambershire.

 **Why PvE and not PvP?**
We already have a PvP realm — Tel'Abim and it still has plenty of room for new adventurers. If you're not a fan of cross-faction play and prefer open-world conflict, Tel’Abim is the place for you!

 **Release Time, Name Reservations & Launch Day Plans**

- The launch of Ambershire is locked in for **17:00 PM Server Time.**

- Name reservations open on **August 13 at 17:00 PM ST.** You'll be able to create your characters ahead of time, but the world won't be accessible until release day. Reservations will remain available throughout that period, with the exception of a temporary maintenance window on August 15 during the patch rollout.

- **[Turtle WoW Radio Crew](https://turtlecraft.gg/radio)** is going all-in for launch day! Our DJs will be live with special shows, cheering on your first quests, dungeon dives, and level-ups. Whether you're racing to level 10 or just vibing in the starter zones, tune in to the radio on your minimap!

Let us know if you have any questions and see you in Azeroth!

A server with only a few top-end guilds and a burnt-out general populace is not a sustainable model.

- **Strong Foundation:** A longer initial phase to let the community form and players to reach 60 without being rushed.
- **Meaningful Tiers:** Enough time in each phase to master the content, gear up, and explore other aspects of the game.
- **Clear Progression:** Distinct goals for PvE and PvP players in every phase.
- **Logical Pacing:** Weaving all content into a timeline that provides a steady and rewarding progression curve.

 **Proposed Duration:** 2 Months

- **System:** Honor System is active.
- **Goal:** Engage in world PvP and battlegrounds to earn Honor for the Rank 7-10 blue PvP sets.

- **Goal:** Guilds coordinate and progress through the first tier of raiding to collect Tier 1 gear.

 **A Quick Note on Phasing & The Advantage of Hindsight**
Some of you with a keen eye for the original progression may have noticed a significant change: moving Zul'Gurub and other mid-tier content to a phase before Blackwing Lair. This is a deliberate choice made possible by the greatest advantage we have when recreating this experience: hindsight.

When this content was first released, the schedule wasn't always optimal. Zul'Gurub, for example, was originally released after Blackwing Lair. While it was intended as a "catch-up" raid, the community has since learned that its itemization and difficulty make it the perfect bridge between the Molten Core and BWL tiers. Placing it earlier provides guilds with a crucial intermediate step. It allows them to acquire powerful gear and hone their coordination in a 20-man setting before tackling the much greater challenge that BWL presents.

Leveraging this hindsight is key to creating the best possible server. We have the unique opportunity to learn from the original release schedule's bumps and smooth out the progression curve. Re-ordering content like this isn't about arbitrarily changing the game, but about pacing it more intelligently to prevent the gear gaps and frustrating walls that guilds historically encountered. It’s about curating a more logical and rewarding journey for the entire community.
