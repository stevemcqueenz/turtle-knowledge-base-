# PvP: What's on the Horizon

## Post 134968 by Jamey (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-06-10T03:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134968#p134968 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings,

Not long ago we asked for your thoughts on potential PvP changes in [this thread](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=18254). First off we'd like to thank everyone who has taken the time to write constructive feedback and input.

Drawing on your suggestions and our own internal discussions, we're ready to share what's coming next. PvP updates will arrive in two stages across Patch 1.18.0 and Patch 1.18.1.

 **Patch 1.18.0**
---
 **REVISED | Dated: 15 August 2025**

Beginning with Patch 1.18.0:

- All incoming **Physical Damage** from other Players is reduced by 15%.

- All incoming **Magical Damage** from other Players is reduced by 20%.

- While in PvP combat (in both Open World and Battlegrounds/Arenas) all incoming **Player Healing** is reduced by 20% and all incoming **Player Absorb Shields** are reduced by 15%.

- While in an Arena or Battleground all **Mana Costs** are reduced by 20%.
Patch 1.18.0 will also:
- Add a **New Arena Map**.

- Add the return of **2v2 Arenas**.

- Add **Rated Arena Teams**.

- Introduce a stacking debuff to anyone holding the flag for an extended period of time in **Warsong Gulch**.

- Players who are standing in spots that we deem **safespots** (as described by our rules) will now receive a Damage over Time debuff that will last as long as they are in such a position. These positions will be continuously updated by the team.

 **Patch 1.18.1**
---
The follow-up patch focuses on broadening PvP's appeal:
- Rework **Rewards & Gear Progression** so that PvP will be a truly viable gearing path.

- Partially rework the current **Honor System** to reduce the brutal race against the clock at high PvP ranks.

- Several new **Open-World PvP Events**.

- A **Brand-New Battleground**.

---
 **Why a universal debuff instead of PvP-specific stats?**

When looking at what could be done to extend PvP battles & combat and reduce some of the volatile burst that exists now we've opted to go for a more general debuff rather than having PvP specific gear that does the same through set bonuses or adding on more of a resilience-like stat.

Our main reason for this is that once we start reworking the gear progression for PvP we want to make sure we can stat this new gear somewhat similarly to the current PvE tiers with a few more minor benefits to PvP. This avoids forcibly splitting up 2 communities based on the gameplay they prefer.

We feel that if a player who has geared mainly through PvP progression wants to dabble in PvE raiding they should be able to with the gear achieved in PvP without starting back at zero with stats that are mainly useless, and vice versa for PvE -> PvP.

---
Once we get closer to release this information will be updated with the latest changes.

See you in Azeroth!

I’d like to share some thoughts on the new change introduced in Patch 1.18.0:

> Beginning with Patch 1.18.0, every player who enters a Battleground or Arena will automatically receive a universal buff/debuff that:
>

>

If you apply these damage reductions without addressing the fact that elemental shamans can PvP using tank talents, it seems unbalanced and ineffective, for example. It simply won't work if the base isn't correctly working.

![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg)![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg)Here’s an alternative proposal keeping the same spirit![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg)![🔹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f539.svg)

Apply the damage reduction only when the player is under crowd control (CC) effects such as charm, stuns, fear, or specific procs (like C’Thun axe)... **NOT ON FLAG CARRIER IN BGS**

This damage reduction could be tied to specific PvP items, for example the PvP trinket or a ring rewarded at Rank 6 (or an equivalent milestone).

Why this is better:
The real frustration in PvP comes from being killed without a chance to react, and that is primarily due to CC—not raw damage.
If you can interrupt, stun, or line-of-sight the opponent’s spell, then the kill feels fair.
But being killed instantly under a 1-second stun with no way to respond is what feels unfair and frustrating.

Why this is not ideal:
Some classes can global you without CC & this wouldn't change that part.

Apply class-specific tuning: x% damage/healing adjustments per class.
This kind of system already exists in other games — for example, in ARAM (League of Legends), where some champions are nerfed or buffed to better fit the game mode.
Rather than broad changes or trying to balance everything globally, it’s often more effective to tweak classes individually with specific x% damage/healing buffs or debuffs.
It allows for more precise balancing without breaking the core design of each class.

Long time game dev, I think I can maybe provide some angle of thought that would be helpful here.

The amount that heal/shield power is debuffed should be greater than the damage resistance. In addition, I think limiting the debuff on heal/shields to outgoing (healing other targets) is the move. Self-healing not being reduced (or atleast not as much as heals from other sources) allows Vampirism to shine. It's a stat that is typically not very useful outside of PvE, so by orienting systems to make it viable you create the following dynamic;

 * Healers are able to heal themselves up easily, so that they can keep supporting frontline (albeit with healing nerfs)
 * DPS are able to self-sustain and get value off Vampirism, sustain is inherently more valuable with these changes
 * Players giving up some DPS for Vamp decreases the overall pressure on the system to reduce player damage.

Just one of my thoughts. There are a lot of angles you can take PvP changes, but this is one I've not seen mentioned that I would really consider exploring.
