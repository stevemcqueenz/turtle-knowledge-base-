# Guildhousing Concerns

## Post 56333 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-08-08T14:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56333#p56333 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! We would like to address the misunderstanding caused by our previous statement regarding the state of Guild Housing on Turtle WoW. To provide more clarity, we will provide additional details about our plan.

- The guild housing on Turtle WoW has always been a collaborative effort, built by the community and RP GMs. Unfortunately, new building activities have been discontinued in recent times. This decision was made because it significantly interferes with our development routine and we simply cannot accommodate the high demand for Guild Houses in the open world. The requirement for staff to keep up with these requests and the concern of overcrowding in the game world make it unfeasible for us to continue the building.

To summarize, it is no longer possible to construct new guild houses while the system still relies on RP Gamemasters building them in the game world. Another issue with guild houses is that certain teleport locations and NPCs provide gameplay advantages, while others do not. It is unfair that certain guilds and their members enjoy privileges that others cannot have.

- We will explore the possibility of introducing a genuine Guild Housing system that would be accessible to every guild. This system would allow guilds to create their own environment, showcase their achievements, and serve as a gathering place for the community. Additionally, we will implement a new system that rewards guilds based on the effort their members put in.

- **We understand that the current guild bases hold significance as RP hubs and are part of the server's history. We assure you that these locations will be preserved, and we will actively work to ensure they remain easily accessible and usable for RP purposes, rather than solely for gameplay enhancements.**

- It is important to note that no immediate or near-future changes will occur. We are outlining our long-term plans and explaining why we no longer support the current Guild Housing feature in its current form.

I'd like to address the 'advantages' that guild bases imply.

For PvE, PvP, AND RP - guild bases were built with their locations in mind, and we had to work with the RP GMs to get the base built within their regulations and our desires. It doesn't 'become unfair' just because newer bases are going to be built with a different standard. There might be ups and downs of both types of Guild Bases, but if the legacy bases are retroactively having their fantasy/intent misdirected into being simply random places in the world with no way of using them as a common area without a long trek, they are going to lose a lot of their appeal for RPers, PvEers, etc..

At least, that's my two cents.

Please, reconsider your stances on these things. Allow the older bases and newer ones to live in conjunction of each other, and keep whatever good or bad each had or has in the future going forward.

I have a question that no one seems to be asking.  What, exactly, is the point in "guild housing"?

Like, okay.  Yes, I understand that it can serve as a place for tired guild members to hang their hats after a hard day of raiding, or that it can serve as a roleplaying location for those who want it.  But at the end of the day… guild housing just seems like an *empty status symbol* more than anything.

There's [a very good post](https://www.reddit.com/r/Guildwars2/comments/39iida/mmos_and_guild_halls_a_discussion_of_design_who/?rdt=44003) on *Reddit* discussing the purpose and ultimately non-functionality of guild housing, and some people have made some very good counter-arguments and examples of good, non-intrusive guild housing in various other games.  One idea that wasn't mentioned was the siege-able guild bases in *Ragnarök Online*. Clan Wars were kinda fun, and that would certainly cater to the ever-growing PvP crowd of this server.  But, I digress.

Opinions No One Asked For I understand where Turtle Team is coming from if they truly intend to **wipe former guild bases off the face of the world**.  The message I'm getting from this topic and the last blunderous discussion **which is no longer available to read** is… "If we can't provide bases for **all** guilds, then **no** guilds should have them".  Plus, there's the argument of how *certain* guild bases provided a locational boon to certain guilds.  Even something like the one in the Wetlands, which has a **thirty-second rideable gryphon** (like the one in Goldshire), provides a distinct advantage to players of that guild and players who know how to get up there.  But I mean, what's the difference between going to a guild base for some R&R… or going to a main city?  A main city has all the amenities that your guild base does and more.  Yes, you have to "share the space" with others, but that's kind of the point of an MMO, isn't it?  To interact with others?  *I* don't because I'm anti-social, but a lot of people *do*.

Now, let's look at this another way.  Let's pretend *Turtle WoW* is a **[roleplaying server](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54528)**.  Do these extremely out-of-the-way locations, such as "Fake Brewfest" off the coast of Arathi, actually provide anything useful to the roleplaying community *as a whole*?  Honestly, I'd say "no".  Again, since places like these are just "guild bases" and generally not meant to be accessed by "normal" players, they're just kind of… *there*.  Sure, guild members can teleport to these places and have a jolly time… but without a Warlock handy, some of these places aren't readily accessible to non-guild members.

At the end of the day, do you know what these random guild bases are to the average player?

 **Landmarks.**  Landmarks to find, landmarks to visit, and landmarks to put on a map.

On several of my characters, I wanted to get a Tabard of the Argent Dawn.  These are free, on this server, and don't require anything but time to obtain.  And so, I went to the Thandol Span, jumped off, and swam around.  When I noticed "Fake Brewfest" on the coast of Arathi, I found myself entranced and investigated.  Then, I later found a Horde guild base in The Hinterlands.  I couldn't access it, but I was still **thrilled**!  These little do-nothing guild bases added some charm to the game and it got me excited to try and find more.
When I found the one in the Wetlands that has an active gryphon roost, I was even **more** excited… but I also paused to consider the possibilities.  How far could I go on gryphon-back?  Would this help me in any way?  Et cetera, et cetera.  And that's the kind of thing you want to **avoid** when it comes to making new landmarks on the world.  And that, perhaps, is a very strong reason as to why guild housing should be limited to a guild-wide instance rather than a physical location in the world.

 
So, what do I think about *Turtle WoW* threatening to erase its past by nullifying the guild bases from the world map or otherwise putting them elsewhere?  Well… while I understand the reasons *why*, I don't actually agree with the decision.  I think that everyone who has a guild base in the world should be able to **keep** their permanent mark on the map, but as has been suggested by others, maybe **disable all practical functionality** of the bases.  It's really unfair for one specific guild to have a base right outside of, say, Zul'Gurub, or one that's directly adjacent to Dire Maul, while other guilds have to struggle and travel for up to 30 minutes just to get ready for their dungeon journey.

And that's all I've got for now.

This isn't an outright objection to the notion of instanced housing as a thing to pursue, but just that, 'it's not fair' as an easy dig at an aspect of an uneven player landscape comes loaded with assumptions about what the objective of even being in the world is. If someone was looking for an advantage in number-grinding, they're not going to find it in a guild whose first priority is working on narrative, scene and character development.
