What’s a journey without style? It’s time to level up with flair!

Until **December 15**, Turtle WoW is all about FASHION and transmogrification. Here’s what’s in store:

 **Grand Fashion Event!**

Log in consistently for 7 days to earn Fashion Coins and unique transmogrification rewards:

- Day 1: 2 Fashion Coins + Half-Item Mutton Chop

- Day 2: 4 Fashion Coins + Black Top Hat

- Day 3: 6 Fashion Coins + Romantic Royal Suit

- Day 4: 8 Fashion Coins + Romantic Royal Gown

- Day 5: 10 Fashion Coins + Hallow’s End Suit

- Day 6: 12 Fashion Coins + Mid-Autumn Festival Hanfu

- Day 7: 15 Fashion Coins + Wizard Hat

 **Alt'lympic Games**

Create a new character and level it up! For every 10 levels, you’ll earn these rewards:

- Level 10: 15 Fashion Coins

- Level 20: Black Top Hat + 15 Fashion Coins

- Level 30: Scotty, Robot Minipet + 15 Fashion Coins

- Level 40: Adventurer’s Lucky Tabard + 15 Fashion Coins

- Level 50: Wanderer’s Pouch (20-slot bag) + 15 Fashion Coins

- Level 60: Tel’Abim Gorilla (mount) + 15 Fashion Coins
 *Notes:*
- *You can send the Tel'Abim Gorilla Mount to any character on your account, as long as they’re on the same realm!*

- *During this event only, the rest bonus gained from tents is doubled!*

- *Characters must be created after November 22.*

 **Sale**

The shop now features 68 new transmogrification items to customize your look!
 **All shop items are 40% off until **November 28** — don’t miss out!**

I think turtle devs decided to make this speed-leveling event for several purposes:
 1. To test leveling talents for every class. Now a lot of people go and level their brand new toon to 60. A lot of fresh leveling data can be gathered.

 3. Marketing reasons. My post is not about of them.

I think this test might produce wrong results.

Some levelers are probably just carried by lvl 60 hunters with Aspect of the Pack (+40% movement speed). Others can be carried by AoE mages. You can't judge how easy the world/how powerful the classes by looking at persons who get carried by lvl 60. I totally sure that those super-speedy levelers should be excluded from statistics.

Another thing is that leveling is about running first and killing mobs next. If you get speed boost (like aspect of the pack), then you move faster from one quest objective to another.
Assuming that lots of people play simultaneously, sometimes there are queues to kill some specific mobs. You lose time on this too.

I got ganked once, which made it really close to the wire, and I kid you not, I dinged 50 at midnight, right as the yellow "welcome to alt'lympics..." message popped up, which I think might have marked the turn of the hour. Later I messaged my ganker that he didn't ruin it after all xD. He felt bad, cause he didn't realize it was the end of the event, with some people still trying to get to their closest benchmark level to squeeze in one more reward.

I learned some interesting tidbits from my ganker, that might help for the next event, funny enough. Now, my thoughts on the biggest complaint I see on here:

I personally disagree with the FOMO complaint. WoW has always had timed and seasonal events, special items with low drop rates, and things that you had to really grind and work towards to achieve - it's the fact that something you got was limited, that made it so special. You see this all the time, with people mounted up on cool mounts that are hard to get, or wearing gear that takes time and work to grind for, and then standing around hubs to show off.

And let's be honest - there was no single game changing item you could get during the event, every one of them was either cosmetic, or practical but very much possible to get outside of the event in another way. Looking at other players having cool things, and comitting time to giving it your best shot to get them too, has been a big part of WoW in the first few expansions, and I think that the Alt'lympics was an amazing tWoW original event in the style of how WoW events used to feel like.
