# 44k players online, and all want the same copper vein!

## Post 146718 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-08-18T10:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146718#p146718 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone!

We're excited to share some stats from yesterday's launch day :) What started back in 2018 with just 100 players on a single realm has grown into a global community of around half a million active players worldwide, united by their passion  for classic World of Warcraft. Let's celebrate this incredible journey together!

Total peak yesterday: **44 000 players online**
Total active players across all regions (*unique IPs, multiboxing excluded*): **~500 000**

 **Patch 1.18.0 Post-Release Stats**

 **Europe**

- Total: 24 600 players online

- **Ambershire (RPPvE)** *(Launch Day)*:
 - Concurrent Population: **11700 players online**

 - Total characters created: 150 000

 - New accounts from unique IPs: 45 000

 - Items looted: 1 300 000 within three hours

 - Average world latency: 78ms

 - Maximum bandwidth *(world connections only)*: 2.3 Gbit

- **Nordanaar (RPPvE)** *(7 years online)*: ~10 000 players online

- **Tel'Abim (PvP)** *(2 years online)*: 3 000 players online. We're very happy to see that Tel'Abim's PvP community has been growing as well! This is our only EU realm that doesn't have cross-faction, so if you’re really into world PvP, it's the best time to join!

 **South East Asia**
- **Karazhan (PvE)**: 8 900 players online

- **Ravenstorm (PvE)**: 6 200 players online

- **Blood Ring (PvP)**: 1 800 players online

- Total: 16 900 players online (*expected to reach 20,000+ again in September with Gehennas*)

 **South America**
- **South Seas (PvE)**: 2.500 online (*not on Patch 1.18.0 yet*)

 **New Radio Record**: 4 000 listeners during our [Ambershire Launch Day](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=21061).
