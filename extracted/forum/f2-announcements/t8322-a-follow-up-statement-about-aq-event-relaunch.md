# A Follow-up Statement About AQ Event Relaunch

## Post 53657 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-16T10:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53657#p53657 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! After seeing both the concerns and excitement expressed by our community, we would like to inform you of a change in direction regarding the recently announced AQ event re-launch on an EU realm.

 **We have made the decision not to proceed with this plan.** Taking into account both the realm's history and lore, it simply does not make sense to wage the same war twice within the storytelling of Warcraft. This event is of immense magnitude and has far-reaching implications for the lore. Instead, we would prefer to preserve this event as a significant part of the server's history and focus our development efforts on creating more events that align with the linear story of Vanilla WoW.

Our objective is to uphold the integrity of the realm's community story, and we sincerely apologize for any confusion caused by our previous announcement.

We appreciate your understanding and support.

That is sad news. I would have loved to re-experience the AQ stuff. It would have tied really well into the nostalgia aspect of this forever-Vanilla realm. But I also completely understand. There would need to be major reworks for this to make sense, both lore-wise and mechanically.

The war effort seems kind of dull when the major rewards that the whole realm is supposed to push for (AQ20 and AQ40) are already available.

Finally - to me the big wasteful thing was always the invastion event. Even in Vanilla it lasted just 24 hours iirc.
