# Leveling Challenge — Slow & Steady

## Post 2459 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2019-04-07T23:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2459#p2459 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/patch_1180/have_you_seen_my_kids.png)

If you really love questing and leveling, pick the **Slow & Steady** leveling challenge when creating your character.

 **Slow & Steady Rules**

- XP from creature kills — both in the open world and dungeons is reduced by 50%. Quest XP stays the same, so completing objectives is still the fastest path forward.

- You'll lose 5% of your current level's XP on death, everywhere except battlegrounds and PvP.

> *Update 07/08/2025:*
>
>  With the release of **Patch 1.18.0 — Scars of the Past**, we're updating some leveling rewards:
>
>  The **Purple Tallstrider** will be removed from the reward list and won't return. If you've got one, it's now a unique, grandfathered item, hold on to it! **The Sword of a Thousand Truths** is being moved to the **Boaring Challenge**, where it was always meant to be. New rewards have been added in place of the removed ones: a new mount, **Admiral Grumbleshell** and the new unique transmogrification item **Turtle Shell Pauldrons**.
>
>  Additionally, with the next patch, XP gained from creature kills in dungeons will be halved. This change comes in response to a growing number of players using dungeon speed-leveling to exploit the donation system. We want to keep the experience fair and consistent for everyone.
