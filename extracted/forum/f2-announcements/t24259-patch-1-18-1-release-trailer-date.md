## Post 170603 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-03-11T20:18:00Z

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/-O2lcZGUQZs]

Hello everyone! The long wait is finally over, and we're happy to announce that our new **Patch 1.18.1 — Nightmares of Ursol** is going live on **March 20**.

The maintenance will begin at **9AM GMT** across the entire network and will last approximately six hours.

Please note that the **Timbermaw Hold** raid will open its doors on**March 27, 7PM server time**, a week after the release, to ensure that realms are stable and all critical hotfixes are applied.

We can't wait for you to see what we have been working on!
