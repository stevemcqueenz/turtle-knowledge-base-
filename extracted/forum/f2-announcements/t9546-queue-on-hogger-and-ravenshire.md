Greetings!

Our new realms, Hogger and Ravenshire, are currently placing new players in a queue, even if the server's player capacity hasn't been reached.

This is a network issue on our hosting provider's side. We are hoping to get it resolved by Monday. If the issue with queues persists, we are considering expanding our network by adding more realms or changing the provider for Hogger realm.

The decision will be made after observing the project's operation in the coming days. We ask you for a few more days of patience and understanding, thank you.
