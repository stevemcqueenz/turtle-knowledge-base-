Hello, everyone! Tomorrow, on **October 6th**, Turtle WoW celebrates its 6th anniversary! What a wild ride it’s been. We want to thank everyone for playing Mysteries of Azeroth with us, for guiding us through this journey, and for sticking with us through both the good times and the tough ones.

We hope the magic of the MMO spirit, along with the beautiful stories you’re creating in our realms, will continue to thrive for many more years to come!

 **What's a celebration without some brew?**

Adventurers, travelers and vagrants around Azeroth! A new festival has been organized in the small goblin town of Ratchet!

Come one, and come all to try ale from two of the greatest brewers in the world to compete for who has the best drink on **Sunday October 6th**, which just so happens to align with the anniversary of Turtle WoW! There are numerous brews and ingredients around Azeroth which need acquired, including many from dungeons!

Additionally, to celebrate, we’re offering a special promotion: a 30% donation bonus on tokens and a 6% discount on all donation rewards! This event will run until October 16th.

Download the anniversary wallpaper, a gift from us to remind you that the journey in WoW is all about making friends along the way: <https://twow.link/art> ![🎉](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f389.svg)
