# Steps we take to reduce the queue, and patch 1.17.0 release

## Post 64771 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-09-16T17:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64771#p64771 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Greetings!** We would like to provide you all with an update on our progress and a roadmap.

 **Today, September 16**
- Transfer of the SEA realm **Hogger** to better hardware to prepare for EU to SEA account transfers.

- Launch of SEA realm: **Emerald Dream, PvE**
 **This Week, September 16-23**
- Migrating **Ravenshire** to better hardware to allow more room for transfers.

- Opening one more realm in SEA, allowing more room for transfers.

- Offering transfers from EU to SEA.

- Enforcing an English-speaking environment on the existing EU realm.

- Implementing a new version of queue prioritization on the EU realm.
 **Next Week, September 23-30**
- Setting up new infrastructure for client updates.
 **Next-Next Week, October 3**
- **Patch 1.17.0: [Beyond the Greymane Wall](https://turtlecraft.gg/gilneas)** — European release!

[embed: https://turtlecraft.gg/watch/embed/d169db12]

 **Further information**

We understand that you all would like to hear about the patch release, but we also recognize that you are anxious about longer queues and the current new content not being as exciting as resolving an overpopulation crisis.

The exact details of our new EU realm are still under internal debate, and will be shared when finalized. We hear and appreciate your opinions and concerns.
