On **October 3rd**, the server will undergo a lengthy maintenance period to ensure a smooth distribution of the patch. We anticipate that our services will be down for an extended duration as we update.

Here are some additional things to keep in mind on the patch day:

- If you're playing Hardcore mode, stick to safe activities as the server will likely be unstable initially after the release.

- The attunements for the new raids will be available from the start. However, the Emerald Sanctum and Lower Karazhan Halls raids will open on **October 7th** to prevent any possible instability issues from affecting the raiding experience.

- Once again, with the release of another major patch, we are excited to embrace the spirit of discovery and adventure by temporarily locking down our online DB website. Embark on new adventures, face formidable enemies, and share your experiences with friends or through the explorer's channel on our Discord. Our database will be reenabled on **October 24th**.

- Additionally, we will have an additional server configuration in place to prevent datamining of items until they have been acquired by a player. Once acquired, everyone will be able to query and view that item.

- During the update, all your custom modifications will be deleted to restore the game files integrity checks. If you have anything important, please move it out of the data folder! If you are using the HD patch, do not save it, as you will need to download the newest version.
