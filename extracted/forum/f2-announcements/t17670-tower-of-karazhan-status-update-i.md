## Post 120531 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-02-20T19:29:00Z

**Karazhan Status Update**

Hello everyone! Today, we’d like to give a little more information regarding the progress of the Tower of Karazhan raid. Tower of Karazhan, aka “Karazhan 40” is coming very soon. We are currently in the midst of the **QA Phase**. In this phase, our team is having patches pushed each week to Karazhan after QA testing. We anticipate the raid being completed soon, however we cannot give an exact date just yet, as we are **absolutely** not willing to release the raid in an unfinished state. We want to catch every bug or issue we can prior to release.

Among the many Karazhan changes from the development cycle, we’d like to highlight that the title for **realm first** has been changed to **Conqueror of Desolation**.

To answer a few questions:

How much is done? Is anything else being worked on?
Every boss and encounter are fully implemented, every model is made, every room is already created. This means, besides developers handling bugfixes and QA, our team is working on other content—everything from events to 1.18.

When will we know a date?

We will be publishing a release date alongside the Tower of Karazhan raid release trailer a minimum of **14 days** prior to release. We're hoping to release this date soon!

When will next class changes be?
We will be pushing more class changes in the future, however our focus is currently on Karazhan balance and bugfixes. Currently, we are only looking at small changes to the biggest outlier classes/specs to bring them up a little. As always, please remember that we do appreciate feedback, and we expect everyone to follow our Terms of Service both in Discord and in-game.

Lastly, I'm excited to share that **the armory is back online!** While it still has a few bugs we're ironing out, we're excited to get the armory back online for everyone to use! The armory can be found at: **<https://turtlecraft.gg/armory>**

Look forward to more Karazhan screenshots this coming week!
