**"Why move the release forward?"**

After a great deal of community pressure, we changed our initial 3 month window into a 4 month window for Phase 1. However, we've opted to reverse this decision for a handful of reasons. While community feedback on the issue was mixed, we have seen a gradual dropoff of players over time. With this, and the complaint of "there's not enough to do," we've decided to take an aggressive approach to the attrition. We will be doing more EXP events in the future, and opening up Phase 2 content now, adding in 3 new raids and 3 new world bosses.

This will also allow Tel'Abim to align closer with Nordanaar for future raid tier releases, as we don't want a realm to be denied access to new content when 1.17.x or 1.18 release. While we do not have a set time for releasing any future patches—we want to ensure the phase lockout is not too extensive for Tel'Abim, if present at all.

We apologize for the suddenness of this release, however with more changes slated for work or release in the coming weeks, we want these stable changes pushed now. Stay tuned for more updates, both Tel'Abim and Nordanaar have more to come.
