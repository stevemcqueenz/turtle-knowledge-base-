# Transfers — FAQ, Details, and Restrictions

## Post 85872 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-01-25T21:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85872#p85872 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/transfer_cn_eu.png)
 *[点击此处阅读此公告的中文版](https://luntan.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=627)*

Greetings! We would like to provide some clarification regarding the newly released Transfer feature in the Turtle WoW account panel! This feature is available to accounts  created before **October 1st, 2023**.

 **Starting the Process:**Use of this feature will **freeze** your account, meaning you will no-longer be able to use it to play on **Nordanaar EU**.

 **Destinations:**You will be able to choose to transfer to either **Ravenshire SEA** or **Stormstout SEA**. These realms are based in Asia and operate using primarily Mandarin language.

 **Important Notes:**
- You must be logged out of the game to initiate the transfer.

- **Guilds** do not transfer. You will need to recreate your guild on your new realm.

- Your account will be **snapshotted** to prevent any loss of data in the transfer.

- **This is a one-way transfer: There is no option to undo your transfer.**

- Transfers are NOT available to or from the **Tel'Abim EU** realm.

- Transfers are NOT available **to** the **Nordanaar EU** realm, only **from** it.

- Donation Tokens and shop items **will** transfer.

- To prevent hoarding, players will be capped at:
 - 1000 Gold.

 - 40 of each "Trade Goods" material.

- Any items/gold in excess of this will be deleted.
