# World Bosses on Turtle WoW

## Post 111191 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-12-03T23:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111191#p111191 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![00000.jpg](./download/file.php?id=88) <dd>00000.jpg (545.79 KiB) Viewed 65098 times</dd>

 **Greetings!**

Over the years, we've received feedback on numerous different systems and their implementations both by ourselves and Blizzard's original design. One system that has been a constant source of controversy and complaint has been the **World Boss** system, where raid-level mobs would spawn into the open world and their loot would go to the first group to kill them.

The Problem

While this is an awesome experience, it does struggle to hold up to the optimization of modern gaming: namely, this system allows strong guilds/groups to take a chokehold over respawns. Our goal going forward is to preserve the fun experience of having these massive, looming threats in the **open world (not instanced!)**, while allowing more players to experience the fights and have a chance at the drops.

Our Solution

We are looking to implement the following changes with the Tower of Karazhan raid release:
- World boss **loot** will have a weekly lockout similar to instances.

- Players will be able to find items throughout the world which will be used by quests to summon certain world bosses.
Alongside these changes, please note:
- Summoned world bosses will **not** affect their normal respawn timer.

- The summoned bosses will automatically be tagged to the summoner's group.

- These bosses will remain **uninstanced**.

## Post 111657 by Possum — 2024-12-07T03:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111657#p111657 | page 2 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's good to see you (the devs) are thinking about ways to shake up what has become quite a static part of the endgame.

Why reduce the scope of social experiences available in the game though? Making the change outlined in the OP will reduce social experiences from world bosses to something already undertaken weekly by all raiders: *guilds killing raid bosses on their predetermined schedules as it suits them, with no social interaction outside the guild raid team.*

This needs further introspection about what makes Vanilla/TWoW so good. Is the game really just about the loot? Is it worth homogenizing a novel and unique end-game system in the name of loot? Is this world boss change moving the game further in the direction of a vanilla WoW experience or a modern retail WoW experience? Are other players "getting in the way" of the hamster wheel experience of collecting loot a bad thing in a social game?

Thinking back to slightly older times...

...before Dragonwatch existed, back in late 2022, there were a number of different groups that would organise to kill world bosses. There was no guarantee any one group would get a kill, it was always a contest. After officially forming, Dragonwatch was not getting every kill guaranteed. There were still rival groups contesting. It took a lot of time and effort from the players involved in Dragonwatch for them to end up in the position they are in. I think it's quite impressive what they do. As the devs you too should be impressed at what your players do, and perhaps think a while longer before completely removing the systems responsible for such a complex and unique social structure that has grown around your game world.

At the moment, there's no strong motivator for people in Dragonwatch to defect and form rival factions because it's arranged so politely and with such ease. If alterations were made to world bosses that increased friction between specific groups of players within Dragonwatch, it could lead to factures and a return to rival groups competing.

I'm not on the turtle dev team, and I'm sure you can cook up your own fantastic solutions to this that would maintain the novel and unique social experience that world bosses provide. I have written out some brainstorming ideas to get the ball rolling for you:

- The areas where world bosses spawn mark players for FFA combat like gurubashi arena, where only groupmates are friendly. Other groups could more directly compete for kills through PvP (and no griefing rules apply, mayhem ensues!)

- Increased world boss spawn rates 2x or 3x, so it becomes untenable for a single group of players to maintain 24/7 pressure on all the overlapping and frequent spawns without burning out or losing interest

- Combined with the above point, players are loot locked each week after their first kill so there is less incentive for the same group of players to contest every single spawn (not no incentive, but a lot less)

- When a boss is killed, it drops loot that's only available to a single faction - the faction of the player who initially tagged it on pull. This could encourage horde/ally to form separate groups and engage in more faction-centred conflict and give a way for devs to avoid enforcing the "no cross-faction group pvp" rule (regarding world bosses)
