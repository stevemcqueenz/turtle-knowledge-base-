# Tel'Abim (PvP) and Nordanaar (PvE)

## Post 74044 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-10-26T22:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74044#p74044 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Greetings,** everyone! We have a brief announcement regarding our new realm, Tel'Abim, as well as an update for our main realm.

 **Tel'Abim** will feature a small handful of rule adjustments due to the inherent differences between a PvE and PvP serve:

- The largest of which will be the complete banning of multiboxing on our new realm. The **only** situation in which people may multibox on Tel'Abim will be for summoning alts.

- Hanzi font support will be fully disabled on release.
Additionally, our progression calendar has been changed to the following:

- **Phase 1:** 4 months.

- **Phase 2:** 3 months.

- **Phase 3:** 3 months.
Our main realm, Turtle WoW RPPvE will also be receiving a new name going forward. This realm will be henceforth known as **Nordanaar RPPvE**. This update will not be reflected in-game until *Patch 1.17.1* due to the large number of issues which arise when changing a realmname.

 **Nordanaar RPPvE** will also soon have another update on the removal of Hanzi support, as upon the launch of Patch 1.17.0 in our SEA Network, there will be less roadblocks for transfers from the European region to our Asian Network. It is already disabled in public, moderated channels.

Yes, actually.  Glad someone asked about this.

How to Fix Your AddOn Settings after "Turtle WoW" is Renamed
 **Note:** Don't do this until the "Turtle WoW" realm is actually renamed! **Character Settings:**
1. Navigate to your *(Turtle) World of Warcraft* folder.
2. From there, navigate to **WTF -> Account -> AccountName**
3. Right click the "**Turtle WoW**" and click "Rename"
4. Erase "**Turtle WoW**" and type "**Nordanaar**", then hit Enter
You should be good to go.
—

 **SavedVariables:**
 **Note:** This one is **considerably** more *delicate* and one mistake *will* break that AddOn's global settings.

Using *Notepad++*
1. Navigate to your *(Turtle) World of Warcraft* folder.
2. From there, navigate to **WTF -> Account -> AccountName -> SavedVariables**
3. Go up to the address bar, highlight and copy **the full address**
4. Open *[Notepad++](https://notepad-plus-plus.org/)*
5. Either press **Ctrl + Shift + F** or click **Search -> Find in Files…**
6. Change the following prompts:
 - Find what: **Turtle WoW**
 - Replace with: **Nordanaar**
 - Filters: ***.*** or ***.lua**
 - Directory: **[your SavedVariables folder directory]**
7. Click "**Find All**" to verify which files are going to receive changes.  **This will not make any changes.**
8. If you clicked "Find All", re-open the "Find in files…" prompt and now, click **Replace in files**.  *Notepad++* will ask you to confirm this command.

All mounts have a base speed of 0.5*your level. So, if you were to acquire a store purchased mount at level 10, it would give you 5% mount speed. Acquiring the Turtle mount at lvl 20 would giv eyou 10% mount speed.

The reason why you'll see people riding the Turtle mount at much faster speeds is because they have bought riding training at either level 40 or 60.

So, at level 40 (with riding training), you'll have the normal 60% mount speed no matter what mount you are using. At 60 (with epic riding training), you'll have 100%.
