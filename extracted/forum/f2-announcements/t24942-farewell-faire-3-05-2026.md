# Farewell Faire 3.05.2026

## Post 176342 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2026-05-02T16:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176342#p176342 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

The time for final goodbyes draws near.

Starting tomorrow at 8 PM, we welcome you to join our Farewell Faire in Moonwhisper Coast, bringing everyone back together one last time, giving all a final chance to admire old friends and pay respects to worthy foes.

The Farewell Faire offers the space and opportunity to talk, drink, eat, and celebrate together, to share stories of the past, or plan adventures yet to come with the community and the team.

 **The main day of the festival is May 3, starting at 8 PM server time (in a day).** The event will run from tomorrow until the day of Turtle WoW's sunset. Join us one last time and let's make it an unforgettable festival!

Speak to a **Turtle Faire Barker** in any of the capital cities for an easy way to get to the Faire.
