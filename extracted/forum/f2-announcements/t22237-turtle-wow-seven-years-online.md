So the funniest and most memorable twow moment is something our entire guild still laughs and jokes about:

We where the first Horde guild to clear Naxx and got promised a title for that. However, it took some time. We tried reaching out to GM's and devs. But no luck. It kinda became a meme to keep asking twow staff when we would receive our title.

Then one day, Vrograg was streaming while answering viewer questions. We took the opportunity to ask him as well when we would receive our title. To which he replied that we are entitled little shits.

This turned into a guild meme. Some of us still hope we would actually get that title one day: Entitled Little Shits!

Funny enough, we recently cleared k40 as the first horde guild. And again we need to wait for weeks for our title. The 'Entitled little shits' meme is doing its round in our guild again ![😆](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f606.svg)

So yeah, I would say clearing these 2 raids as the first horde guild is the most memorable, but not as memorable as being entitled little shits going after their title ![🤣](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f923.svg)

Hey fellow twow enjoyers

My name is Slix, some of you may know me, either from spamming my free opening lockboxes macro in og, or by just running into me on random dungeon runs as i tend to do at nights when i have nothing better to do.

When i saw Turtle WoW asking for anyone's most special twow moment, i felt i had to share this one with you, as this is not only my most special twow moment but also my most special gaming moment ever, period.

I am not very good at something like and i am also writing this at 2:30am so i am plenty tired and also maybe a little drunk but i do not know when i will have time again until 6th october so bear with me.
First a little background:

I started playing on twow about 3 years ago and after successfully levelling my rogue to 60 as hc (*cough* third try *cough*) i found out there was a long running succesful guild on the server called traveler. Joined up with them and quickly found super awesome like minded friends to hang out with after study/works hours just shooting the shit and having fun while also progressing through naxx.

I played on most of the other historically well known p-projects like Nostalrius or Lights Hope and got to know a lot of awesome people and guilds. However this guild is different, this is the most awesome guild i ever had the honor to be part of and i am writing this post to commemorate the fact.

Quickly befriended a lot of the regulars as i had many times before with in other guilds. But this time it was going to be different.
One day, i believe was some day in february of 2024, i literally woke up to a discord dm from Newki saying hes planning to get the gang together irl and asking me if i was interested to come around.
You see, as Traveler is a multinational guild, this wouldn't be an easy feat and i had my doubts. But i was intrigued and i wanted to see if anything came of it. I heard many tales of such occurances from both people i know irl but also from a somewhat well known podcast i am sure many of you will know called "Drama Time" and heard all the kind of mayhem such a get together could bring.

As you are reading this, you can probably already imagine that Newki actually had the balls to go through with his plan and so he put in the work investigating where and how we could meet up, talking to everyone who would eventually get involved and also handling financial side of this.

In the end he found 10 people crazy enough to honor the guildtag <Traveler> and so we would all meet on Gran Canaria during summer to spend a week of vacation together.

Against all odds, there was actually no mayhem, just our usual banter, a lot of drinking (a lot!), discussing guild politics, and all in all having a really great time together.
Such an awesome group of friendly and helpful players, both inside and outside the game.
Some of us have since stopped playing actively and only show up from time to time, some of us have joined different guilds, but we still talk to each other and play and have fun together from time to time.

Thank you guys for all the great times we had, hopefully we'll have many more :)

Even though i am not a core raider in Traveler anymore myself, i would like to use this opportunity to let people know that Traveler is actively recruiting and looking to expand as they are currently building up a solid k40 roster.
If you are interested in joining a multinational, EU based guild that has naxx on farm and is looking for more people to join their raiding team no matter what gear level in order to start up on k40, i can definitely recommend giving Traveler a shot.

Ah yes and also, whoever took the nick Slix on the Forum: i hope the next time you take a dump at a highway gas station there is no toilet paper >:(
