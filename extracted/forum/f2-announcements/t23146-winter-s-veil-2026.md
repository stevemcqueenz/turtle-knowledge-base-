![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/Winterveil.png)

 **Winter Veil Is Back!**

Starting **December 7th**, Winter Veil will go live! To celebrate the holiday, we have a few events lined up.

 **Winter Veil Card Contest**

Artists of Azeroth, it's time to strut your stuff! Post your submissions on our subreddit! The team's top 5 favorites will be awarded 150 tokens each. The 3 most liked will also be awarded 50 tokens each. Submissions close **December 28th**! (Please, no AI submissions!)

 **Winter Veil Warm Wishes**

Have you been struck with the holiday spirit? Share the warmth with a memento in-game. Reply to this post with a message directed to the community (up to 180 characters long) for a chance to see it added in-game ![👀](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f440.svg). Don't forget to include your name and realm!

 **Seasonal Sale**

From **December 7 to January 4th**, donors will get 40% bonus tokens with every donation! We've also added a few unique cosmetic rewards, only available during Winter Veil!

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/GfbmmgTXcAA9ogl.jpg)

Merry Winter's Veil!
