Won't happen for one reason; Bugs. Fall damage was nerfed on hardcore because sometimes people would phase through the zeppelins, or sometimes when the zeppelin enters a new loading screen people would randomly fall through the zeppelin and die of fall damage, especially the new Zeppelin to Badlands. A common bug beyond the hardcore player's control that would guarantee death from fall damage. There were also issues with players falling through the elevators at Thunderbluff, Thousand Needles, and Undercity. The sensible thing was to nerf fall damage for hardcores so that people don't die of the various collision bugs and errors.
