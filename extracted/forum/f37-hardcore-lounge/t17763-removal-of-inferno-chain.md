# Removal of Inferno Chain

## Post 121114 by Thisisreallydumb — 2025-02-27T12:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121114#p121114 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there,

So, where to start. Lets start with some insight into Inferno raiding: Running and managing an Inferno guild is many times more difficult than softcore and takes a lot more time, effort and resources. In a softcore guild you pretty much just have to do recruitment, make raid signups and comps. But on Inferno you have:
 - You have to manage guild resources, guild farms and functional trading since no auction house where players can just trade for their raid consumes and stuff. And hardcore raiding uses A LOT of consumes.
 - In a softcore guild you don't need to spend any time thinking about dungeons when people can just independently queue LFG at no risk. But on Inferno you can't leave this up to chance, so you have to manage this internally in a guild by assembling proper people with a proper tank and healer who knows what they are doing. And can't just drag a fresh lvl 60 straight into raiding on Inferno without them first getting a bit of dungeon gear, unlike softcore.

I am probably missing some nuance since I haven't raided softcore for years now, but I cannot state it clear enough how much more difficult and time intensive it is to run an Inferno guild. It is A LOT, at least ten times and that is on top of the stress and difficulty of doing the actual raiding on hardcore (which should be the main focus of the game mode).

<<<<<<<<<< SKIP TO HERE IF YOU WANT WHAT THE POST IS ABOUT >>>>>>>>>>

That brings us is to the Inferno chain.
It was fun on release and the first few times (thanks for that, it was a good experience), but after 1.5 years it is a huge drain for Inferno progression and nobody is having fun any more with it.
So we are already running and managing two MCs, BWL, ZG, AQ20, ES, Ony, multiple Kara10s, AQ40. And we also have to plan and manage the attunes for these, where on softcore they are pug'able. And this is on top of having to manage all the consumes for these raids and get people dungeon geared to enter in the first place, and with how people some times get themselves killed, all of this has to be done over and over again.

The Inferno chain is: Mara -> LBRS -> Scholo -> DMW -> BRD & HFQ -> UBRS
And at every step you have to get people to the Mysterious Mailbox in The Master's Cellar under Kara which cannot be done alone by 99% of people.
And every single new Inferno player has to be carried through this and with the amount of raids we are running at AQ40 progression this is becoming untenable. Like, what softcore guild at this progression has to make signups every week for UBRS?

And here is the thing, whenever a new person joins an Inferno guild and starts the Inferno chain they become a **ticking time bomb**. Some, if the guild doesn't assemble a team of nine other people and get them through the Inferno chain within a few days then they just lose interest and choose to ding 60 to become normie and then all the resources that was poured into this person is lost.

And these dungeons almost never align with getting dungeon gear for people. This chain is something we have to run ON TOP of getting people dungeon gear.
When you want dungeon gear for people you assemble a team of ten who need and then you run that dungeon multiple times. You don't do this on the Inferno chain and if someone gets a piece of pre-bis loot while doing it, it is pure luck. You just cannot run the Inferno chain and switch out multiple people for each dungeon because someone needs gear from that dungeon and another person needs a piece from somewhere else, while trying to retain a functioning comp of healers, tanks, dispellers, decurser etc. it is a managerial nightmare, and running a dungeon only once sucks. Combining running the Inferno chain and running for dungeon gear is impossible. They almost don't overlap at all.

At this progression it is a huge waiting time for people sitting at lvl 59 to get through this chain if they miss a dungeon run or just can't be online outside of raiding time. And people complain we don't run UBRS every day, but like, we also have raiding and farming to do.

<<<<<<<<<< SKIP TO HERE IF YOU JUST WANT TO KNOW WHAT HAS TO BE DONE >>>>>>>>>>

The dungeoneering from the Inferno chain has to be removed.
I suggest that you simply become Inferno with the first visit to the Mysterious Mailbox. This provides enough grit to where someone doesn't accidently become Inferno. Going all the way to Karazhan and fighting through the Cellar is not something you do unintentionally and you're gonna need at least one person with you to do it.

And this change has to be done ASAP because it's getting critical.
I would appreciate if someone on the team would write back about the technicality as to whether such a change could be done in a normal server update or if it would require a larger client patch. Just so people know what to expect. Something to be done about Inferno chain has been brought up for at least around a year now and now it has to be done.

Inferno mode is and should be about running hardcore raids, not about being forced to run mandatory dungeons over and over again every single day. And if you say "then just don't do them" then guess what, there will be no people for raiding and then there will be no raids and then the entire game mode is dead.
You already take my screenshots and use them for your social media every time we do a server first clear of a hardcore raid. If you want that screenshot of server first hardcore C'Thun and Kel'Thuzad to promote your server then you really need to do this now, because the Inferno chain is not fun any more and has become a huge factor in burnout. And let me state again, how much more goes into running an Inferno progression guild over a softcore guild. It is a A LOT.
