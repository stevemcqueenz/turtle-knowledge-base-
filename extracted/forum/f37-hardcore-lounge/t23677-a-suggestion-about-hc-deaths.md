# A suggestion about HC deaths

## Post 165748 by Drakz — 2026-01-20T21:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165748#p165748 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone,

I just wanted to bring up an issue that’s been really frustrating for a lot of us in the Hardcore community. A lot of deaths lately aren’t coming from gameplay mistakes - they’re happening because of server disconnects, like the one on January 20th at 10:50 PM EET.

I think it would be a really good idea for the server to consider doing a small rollback in situations like this. Losing progress or characters due to issues completely outside of our control is super frustrating and honestly risks losing part of the playerbase.

Also, I’ve noticed some moderators on Discord blaming players for these deaths, even though it’s obvious we were all disconnected at the same time from different countries. It’s disheartening to be accused when it’s clearly a server problem.

I hope this can be considered seriously - it would make the Hardcore experience much less stressful and more fair.

Thanks for reading.

## Post 167107 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-01T03:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167107#p167107 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The core debate is whether our hardcore gaming should count server restarts and disconnection in as "regular" part of the game.

As some players claim, everything, including every incident like real-life wars, server crashes, game glithes and earthquakes should not be reasons for HC absolution. Even if other players think characters in game who die for those issues should be revived or reclaimed, the biggest question lies in how to identify those issues in order to prevent the mechanism being abused. If it is abused, GMs' workload and further complaints may also multiply because every player may say that server latency ruins his character.

My compromised suggestion is having dead HC characters moved and revived in a less active or unpopular realm/server, such as current PVP servers in Feb. 2026, as non-HC ones, which would compensate the players' dedication and help people stay in the game. Despite the frustration, they don't feel completely lost as their life, time and energy are not pathetically wasted.

Other than that, as TWoW's HC mode isn't Blizzard's HC servers, there have been several specific functions implemented, including preventing unwanted PVP engages. I think HC characters should be tagged for more such mechanisms, such as automatically teleporting them to an inn when the server restarts without warning. Such restarts typically occur with a rollback, so characters may find themselves among respawned mobs.
