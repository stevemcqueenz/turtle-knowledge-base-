Unstuck feature is supposed to save u, when server is down and u are in a middle of a cave!
It won't save u from DC or power cut

For power cut, i suggest a ups
For isp problems (that will last), i suggest to turn your mobile phone into a wifi hotspot (mobile date)
and immediately connect to it

All 3 options have saved me from time to time!
