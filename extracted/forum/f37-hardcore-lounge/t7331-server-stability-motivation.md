# Server stability = Motivation

## Post 46558 by Embero — 2023-05-19T01:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46558#p46558 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there Turtle / HC community!!

I wanted to express that I LOVE the built-in Hardcore challenge, it's some of the most fun I've had playing WoW in years! But I've been a little concerned with the massive spike in popularity over the past few months that playing on my Hardcore characters feels almost too risky with the increase in server lag, crashes and whatnot.

This isn't a criticism or a demand to fix anything since I know the Turtle WoW dev team are doing their best with what they're given, but I am feeling less motivated to play Hardcore in general because it feels like too much of a liability with the servers the way they are... Most of my characters are all Hardcore, but I've been thinking about making my main a non-HC just because I don't like fearing that lag or an outage could be the end of it.

I'm curious if anyone else has been feeling this way? A little afraid to play their HC toons as the servers came under more stress / the server got more popular? I'm suffering from their success so to speak...   crying_turtle
