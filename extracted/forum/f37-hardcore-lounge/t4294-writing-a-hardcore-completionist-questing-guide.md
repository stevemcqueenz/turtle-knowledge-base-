# Writing a Hardcore Completionist Questing Guide

## Post 25568 by Blueage — 2022-09-20T06:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25568#p25568 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I'm pretty new to Turtle WoW. It's been a lot of fun exploring all the new content, though admittedly I haven't gotten very far yet. I also fell in love with Hardcore Mode and can't imagine playing WoW any other way now.

Anyway, I decided to write a questing guide for HC players. While eventually I would love to make my own addon (I'm very new to coding), for now I am using the TourGuideVanilla addon. Hopefully there's some interest in this sort of thing.

At the moment, I've finished the guide for Goblins from level 1 to 10 (12 if the player completes all optional sections).

I explain most of this in the guide, but this is not so much a power-leveling guide. While it was a major goal of mine to optimize the quest routes, I am not perfect and there's always room for improvement. More importantly, I want to include as many quests as possible which may require that the player go a little out of their way occasionally. However, efficiency is a focus.

The addon uses coordinates in conjunction with TomTom Vanilla. The addon also works with Pfquest Turtle. I'd recommend turning off the Pfquest directional arrow and quest log (TourGuide tracks quests automatically), but the minimap modes are very useful.

I DID NOT CREATE ANY OF THESE ADDONS.
THE GUIDE ONLY WORKS FOR NEW GOBLIN CHARACTERS (for now)

Downloads

If you try out this guide, please feel free to ask any questions or offer any suggestions on how I can improve the experience.

Thank you
