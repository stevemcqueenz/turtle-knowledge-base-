## Post 11846 by Stenerjoe2 — 2021-08-27T08:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11846#p11846 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

TIps on top of my head, some might seem very obvoius;

Dont go out of your way for a quest.
Travel by roads - If you should have an accidential DC or lagspike.
Dont dive in water (same as above)
Dont focus on speed but survivabilty - Dont save gold for mount but spend it on spells you NEED and consums that makes you stay alive.
Plan ahead - Check which areas in the zones you are about to visit has which quests/mob-level.
Level cooking on your way.

Fight mobs that are 1-2 levels below you ( with rested XP from tents its defo not so bad )

Keep asking yourself this; What is more dull, to start all over or to keep staying safe.

List of things i comonly die to:
Stealthed mobs that ambush me when i've angroed another mob.
Mobs that have anoying repeatable CC that makes it hard to get away (Looking at you, Thornweavers in The Barrens).
Being overconfident and running thru terain instead of roads, meaning i get dazed and smacked to hell by 4 cougars with a 0.30 attack speed (feels like that).
Patroling elites on and off road because im unaware (Dont play when you are tired or really hungry)

Good luck
