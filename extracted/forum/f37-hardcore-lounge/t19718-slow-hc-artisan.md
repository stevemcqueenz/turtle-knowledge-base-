## Post 134304 by Winthrop (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-06-04T20:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134304#p134304 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So Im playing those challenges(hc, SnS, Artisan) + exhaustion as a frostmage, currently level 47.

I started with mining & Skinning up to Bronze(Loch Modan).
Then took JC to craft the best Ring(Trainer), neck(Trainer), Staff(Serpentcoil world drop recipe) and Trinket (shadoweye farmable in Loch Modan)

In the meantime I sold leather for Gold then switched to Tailoring. You will have lots of wool from the Shadoweye farm.
This means you will be running around for quiet a while without clothing.

From there on full invest in Tailor & Enchanting.

Great thing about the wetlands is the silkbolts you get through fishing!
Don't skip out on cooking for the juicy mp5 sagefish caught in Loch Modan.

Been a blast so far! Enjoy the journey.
