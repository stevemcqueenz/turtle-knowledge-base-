It's not a bug so much as a feature, probably added by blizzard to stop people abusing feign death to simply camp out an area or wait indefinitely for friends. It was very well known in the vanilla client; there are comments on it from 1.2-1.12 here: [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=5 ... h#comments](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=5384/feign-death#comments). Search "die" and the 6 minute timer before death is mentioned by people playing the game in 2005.

Dying at the end of the timer isn't a bug since it's intentional. However, most deceiving is that the death at the end of the timer isn't mentioned in the tooltip.

On any character, a biological emergency would be a reason to log out. Feign death, shadowmeld, or stealth never guarantee a safe return when coming back to the computer.
