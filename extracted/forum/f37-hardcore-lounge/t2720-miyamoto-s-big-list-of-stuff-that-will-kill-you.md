# Miyamoto's big list of stuff that will kill you

## Post 17070 by Boogie — 2022-02-17T08:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17070#p17070 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey guys, this is Miyamoto / Boogie here.

There’s plenty of generic advice on how to survive in HC, but I haven’t seen a good list of specific things that might kill you.

I’ve put together a list of all the things that have killed or almost killed me in my time on Turtle, or that I’ve seen kill others regularly. There’s a heavy bias towards under 30 content, because I haven’t survived past that point yet (all in the name of research for this post, of course. I could easily survive if I wanted :clownface:).

I haven’t bothered to list every rare in the game because most of them if you see them and you’re a level or two higher, it’s a fairly easy kill for most classes.

I also haven’t bothered covering things that are obviously dangerous like dungeons.

A few general things:

Murlocs are always deadly.

Groups of casters in tight spaces like Defias Pillagers are always dangerous..

Houses and caves with different vertical levels can mean aggroing mobs you can’t even see who tend to bring a whole train of friends with them.

NPCs with the Execute ability need to be handled with care. If your health drops too low it’ll be goodnight. Look out for anything with “slayer” in its name, and some carrion birds also have it.

Here’s the list.

Durotar

Captain Flat Tusk
An elite chilling with a bunch of normal pigmen. If you’re not alert you can accidentally aggro him and suffer the consequences.

Dark Storms
Fizzle has quite the rolodex of dead HC players at this point. Make sure you’re a decent level and clear the mobs around him as he patrols. If you don’t have a ranged interrupt you’ll probably need to run WAY back to pull him out of the danger zone so also clear a bunch of lightning lizards. On Turtle, if he casts his Soul Siphon and you don’t have an interrupt off CD, you’re pretty screwed. Do this with a buddy.

Gazz’Uz
This guy chills in the Burning Blade cave near Orgrimmar. If you get him at the right level he’s not too hard, but likely you’re going to be in there a bit lower level and might accidentally aggro him while fighting other mobs. Drops an item that starts a quest, which is nice.
Tirisfal

The Family Crypt
A densely packed tight space with a number of patrols who can link groups of mobs. You can get this quest at level 7,  but especially on weaker classes it’s best to come here at level 12+.

Teldrassil

Denalan
Don’t AFK near this guy on a low level. Handing in some quests to him spawns level 5 Boglings who will kill you.

Blackmoss the Fetid
Another one who is fine if you’re the right level (he’s level 13), but chances are you’ll be in this area before then. He hits pretty hard so be careful, especially if you can’t kite.

Relics of Wakening
Sure it can be done earlier, but if you don’t know this cave well I suggest leaving it until you’re over level 10. Some furbolgs can heal and they all run, so you can get yourself into a pickle pretty easily if you’re slow to kill.

Elwynn Forest

Defias Dockmaster
This goblin is an NPC for a rogue quest. Engaging him spawns 3 bodyguards, who kill make short work of you unless you’re overlevelled. He drops nothing of value. If you’re a rogue, pickpocket him and run (or better yet, do the Dwarf version of the quest for extra safety). If you’re not a rogue, just stay away.

Dun Morogh

Frostmane Hold
Not super hard but unless you’re really careful you’ll probably pull at least one or two groups here, they have the annoying combo of ranged abilities and they run, so you can get yourself into trouble pretty quickly. There’s also an elite in the deepest part of the cave who might surprise you.

Protecting the Herd
The elite here hits particularly hard, harder than some other elites you see in starting areas like Hogger. Bring a friend.

Those Blasted Troggs! / The Public Servant
This is fine if you stay around the cave opening. If you venture in deeper it’s very easy to get swarmed and die, so don’t get tempted by chests or mining nodes unless you’re overlevelled or have a friend or two.

Loch Modan

Silver Stream Mine (for the quest Filthy Paws)
Caster kobolds who run, move in packs and do decent damage. This cave has claimed many a HC. I recommend standing in the entrance and waiting for quest items to respawn rather than venturing deep inside, unless you do enough damage to not have to worry.

In Defense of the King’s Lands
More troggs in caves. Even the first part of this quest can get ugly quickly with the valley densely packed with troggs who have ranged abilities and run. Be careful.

Ol’ Sooty
An elite bear who looks pretty much like all the other bears in the area. Easy to accidentally stumble over and die.

Horde patrol
Just three cool guys cruising looking for other cool guys. These guys kill Bingles many times a day, and will likely kill you too if you accidentally aggro them. When travelling on the southern and eastern shores of the Loch, keep an eye out.

The Barrens

The Principal Source (Horde Druid quest)
Alliance has it so easy for this one. If you’re a Tauren, you have to fight your way to the top of Dreadmist Peak. When you click the flask, mobs will spawn and you’ll want to hoof it out of there. Definitely don’t click and go AFK, or click it when you’re low HP from fighting your way up. I personally wouldn’t rush to get this done as soon as I get it - no harm in waiting a few levels.

Alliance patrol
Another bunch of cool guys cruising - this time in the Southern Barrens. You definitely don’t want to run into these guys accidentally.

Dishu
Great skin on this cat, but when you attack her she spawns four cubs. Can be quite a shock if you’re trying to tame (or kill) her at around her level.

Counterattack!
You can be dramatically overlevelled, be really careful, and still have the mobs glitch and suddenly have 10 centaurs beating on you. Just don’t do this quest.

The Tear of the Moons
The NPC you need to kill for this quest is at the bottom of a Dwarven bunker (red flag #1) and he’s a few levels higher than everything else there (red flag #2). There’s also a dense group of adds with him and patrols who can bring others running (red flag #3). Do this one overlevelled or bring a friend.

Hagg Taurenbane
A 26 elite in an area with lower level pigmen, this guy can give you a nasty surprise. Especially because many of the pigmen around him are in stealth, so you can end up in a gangbang pretty quickly.

Sludge Fen
A location with a nice easy escort quest, you need to be very careful. There are three rares here who all can give you a nasty surprise. The one I hate the most is Engineer Whirlygig. He spawns a mechanical (as with many private servers, spawned mechanicals on Turtle are horrible) that can make the fight very difficult.

Boulderlode Mine
The scene of some unforgettable collection quests (Cats-eye Emerald anyone?) this mine features especially densely packed mobs and many patrols. Don’t go in here unless you can comfortably kill 3 level 20s at a time.

Silverpine

Sons of Arugal
Obvious but worth mentioning. There are three of these. One you probably will never encounter because it’s in the south where there are no quests, one who stands near the Sepulchre and will kill you if you try to take the tin node near it at a low level, and one who patrols from the beach along the mountain in northern Silverpine.

The Weaver
I love grinding Dalaran humans in Silverpine because they have nice drops and the Alliance Chests in the area are bountiful, but this quest involves some seriously densely packed mobs in a human town hall type setup. Don’t try it too early.

Ravenclaw Regent
Hits hard so nice to do with a buddy, but note that he mind controls and your buddy could end up lightning bolt critting you to death (happened to me on a warrior with my shaman pal).

Fenris Isle
Densely packed, multiple vertical level undead gnolls, multiple rares and a mid-20s elite named quest guy. Gross. This place is death.

Krethis Shadowspinner
To be honest I haven’t tried killing this guy on Turtle at a reasonable level because I’m so traumatised by my experience on another server. He has a crazy damage ability called Shadow Shock, and pops a pretty chunky shield on himself so it’s hard to kill him before he Shadow Shocks a second time.

Darkshore

Carnivous the Breaker
Seems like a normal level 16 but hits extremely hard. Easily kiteable, for melee I would stay away until significantly higher level (like 22+). On a 16 mage he could probably one shot you so still handle with care.

Shadowclaw
The biggest issue with this guy is that he’s stealthed and much more dangerous than the mobs around him, so by the time you realise you’ve aggroed him you can be in deep trouble.

Den Mother
This used to be easy as she was surrounded by cubs who wouldn’t aggro. Now (I hear) they are all linked. Make sure you can take them all before engaging.

Inside the Tower of Althalaxx
The mobs inside the tower are much higher level than outside. Eventually if you do the chain you’ll need to go in here to finish it. I recommend being significantly overlevelled - ideally the quest would be green or even grey - before you try.

The Blackwood Corrupted
To do this you need to kill a demon called Xabraxxis. Not too much harder than what you’d expect for his level (level 19 so don’t get suckered into trying this at 15 or something), but take care to clear the camp first as you may want room to manoeuvre.

Wanted: Murkdeep
Not quite as bad as Counterattack!, but this can be a little glitchy. Even if not glitchy, it involves swarms of murlocs, so you know how that goes.

Westfall

The Forgotten Heirloom
If you go into the house to do this quest, be aware there’s a level 12 mob and a level 15 mob waiting for you. Given you’re probably going to try to do it at a lower level, it’s pretty high risk. On other servers this can be done by going around the outside of the house and clicking through the wall - probably can be done on Turtle too.

Foe Reaper
Picture this - you’re a level 14 hunter, mindlessly grinding through Westfall, killing everything in your path, when suddenly a harvest golem ruins your day and your run. That’s Foe Reaper. He’s cool, he drops a 10 slot bag, but you want to be a decent level before you tackle him.

Westfall Beaches
I mentioned murlocs already but the beaches in Westfall are a nightmare of densely packed, netting mobs with pets who will chase you for eternity if you manage to evade the nets.

Deadmines quests
You might get tempted to try to solo the quests outside Deadmines when you’re decently high level. When you get to the undead mobs deep inside, be aware that the first pull has two skeletons chained to the zombie and they hit really hard. I did this one on a level 25 Paladin with a level 24 Druid and we narrowly escaped with our lives, mostly because we could both heal.

Carver Molsen
A new custom addition to the server, this is a 21 elite in a spot where you may not expect. He hits hard and chases forever.

Redridge Mountains

Lake Thresher
Redridge is a zone with many densely packed groups of mobs and elites, but most of them are pretty well obvious. Not these guys though - level 25 elite lake monsters just chilling underwater. So take care when you’re swimming across the lake, and for rogues levelling pickpocketing be extra careful with the boxes near these.

Stonetalon Mountains

Boulderslide Ravine
Just a really horrible cave with densely packed, running caster mobs and lots of terrain to glitch out on. The second part of the quest also involves an elite.

Taskmaster Whipfang
A 22 rare elite who has a long duration fear. He sucks.

Foreman Rigger
Another rare elite, this guy has a net ability and hits hard.

Gerenzo Wrenchwhistle
Another Venture Co guy with summoned mechanicals, so the usual comments apply. Make sure you’ve cleared around him before taking him on (including patrols). If it gets ugly just jump off.

Deepmoss Eggs
Once you loot an egg it will spawn small spiders, who can spawn bigger matriarchs. If you get two matriarchs from one egg you can be in real trouble very quickly.  One strategy is to loot the eggs and run, but it's not fool proof. Do this quest when it's grey.

Wetlands

A Grim Task
This and the quest you get along with it are elite quests so need to be treated carefully, but I find the dwarves and the named guy you have to kill hit particularly hard for their level and elite status. Have a decent group and a proper tank and heals unless you’re way overlevelled.

The Missing Diplomat
The part where you chase Slim out of the inn spawns adds who can easily kill you. Either do it overlevelled, or have a few friends with you.

Ashenvale

Supplies to Auberdine
This is a nightmare escort quest where you get ambushed three times. It’s very difficult to keep the NPC alive, and can also be a risk for the player. Definitely one to wait until you’re overlevelled.

Befouled Water Elementals
These water elementals hang out at Mystral Lake, and taken alone they aren’t too much of a problem. They do a debuff that increases frost damage taken by 50, and they also cast frost bolts. Where it gets dangerous is when you get 2 or 3 of these guys on you at once, their frost damage can overwhelm you pretty quickly.

Wandering Protector
Horde levellers need to keep an eye out for this guy. Level 35 elite who can cast roots, so if you think you’re safe being mounted and riding past and aggroing him, you’re wrong. Basically if you go anywhere near him without FAP or other roots-break, you’re probably going to die unless you’re lvl 35+.

Hillsbrad Foothills

The Rescue
This quest can get you in a few ways. Firstly, the Syndicate casters in the area cast a nasty Curse of Thorns that inflicts a huge amount of damage to melee characters. You almost have to sit and wait for it to fade if you’re unlucky enough to miss an interrupt. Secondly, the two jailors are a really deadly combo on Turtle. Every time I’ve done it they’ve both been in a really tight area with a bunch of other mobs (who run). One of them has a very frequent net and shoot combo that makes it a nightmare for melee. The other has an enslave ability. So even if you go here with a friend, you could find yourself in a situation where you are netted, your friend is beating on you, and the two NPCs chained another 3-4 adds onto you as well.

Hillsbrad farms
This area isn’t too bad but one thing to keep in mind is the human mobs here run when their HP is higher than usual, so you can accidentally chain groups together if you’re not really paying attention.

Duskwood

Mor'Ladim
Super easy to spot and avoid, skeleton walking around Raven Hill Cemetery with a glowing sword. He’s high level for the area and has made a mess of many a HC player, so take care in the area.

Stitches
Another easy one to spot and avoid. Abomination who mostly can be found on the road to Darkshire.

The Unseen
Invisible high level mobs that can be aggroed by any AOE. Easy solution is to take AOE off your bars while you’re in Duskwood. Or use a map to see where they are and avoid those spots. Or just don’t play Alliance.

Thousand Needles

Test of Endurance
This involves going into a disgusting harpy cave all the way to the end, breaking a box, then fighting three waves of harpies before the quest named mob appears. Seriously don’t recommend doing this as a yellow quest, unless you’ve got a group.

Fearing mobs
Some coyotes and scorpids in this zone have nasty fears that can get you into trouble if you chain a few together into other mobs. Make sure you're clear before engaging them.

Steelsnap
Not too hard by himself but has two other dogs with him and they all fear, meaning you can be chain feared and killed pretty easily. Do this when you're overlevelled or in a group.

Stranglethorn Vale

Venture Company Mining
Some of the goblins in the area you do this quest spawn mechanicals. I can’t reiterate enough how bad spawned mechanicals are. These ones will chase you forever, until one of you dies. Unless you can pump out a huge amount of damage, don’t try this quest.

Arathi Highlands

Singer
A rare NPC at Northfold Manor, chilling inside a barn with 3 other npcs. Hits like an absolute truck and with the adds is extremely dangerous, if you run into this barn without checking first.

Feralas

Fey Dragons
The ones in the cage that Alliance free for a quest can bug out and sit there in the Tauren camp. It's possible as Horde to accidentally aggro one and suddenly have them swarm you, so if you see them steer clear of the area. Note these are different to the neutral fey dragons just chilling closer to the road.

Dustwallow Marsh

Coral Shark
These guys swim around off the coast, including near where the ship lands. They also swim around the islands where murlocs are up north of Theramore. I've heard unconfirmed reports of them even coming up onto land. They’re mid-40s elites and will make a real mess of any HC who stumbles onto them - so don’t.

The Missing Diplomat (again)
If you survived the previous Wetlands gank, there’s another one here when you attack Private Hendel. Screw this quest.

Tanaris

Videre Elixir
This quest will straight up kill you in order to progress. So whatever you do, don’t drink the Videre Elixir in the graveyard in Tanaris.

Un'Goro Crater

Pawn Captures Queen
Don't do this quest. Silithid caves as a rule should be avoided on HC characters, but this quest in particular involves waves of spawns that will almost certainly overwhelm you. I saw a 57 pally die trying to do it - nuff said. Plus there are bugs around here that do one of the nastiest +dmg debuffs in the game.

Western Plaguelands

The Annals of Darrowshire
Loot the wrong book and it'll possibly spawn mobs that will debuff and annihilate you in seconds. Do your research beforehand on what the correct book looks like.

Eastern Plaguelands

There's a bugged abomination in the Noxious Vale that can do a crazy amount of damage very quickly, almost certain death for the unwary.

Of Forgotten Memories
When you try to retrieve the item it spawns a group of dwarves who are pretty deadly, I suggest just dropping the Tirion Fordring chain at this point unless you have a group.

Plaguelands

Blood of Heroes
These can be found and clicked on. Don't loot this. It'll spawn an elite that almost certainly will kill you.

Some bits from me:

Silverpine - Beren's List:

Aside from the fact that you fight undead melee mobs on a narrow path or inside a cave behind Dalaran human caster mobs an undead elite looking exactly like to armored skeletons you fight for the quests may spawn in the front half of the cave. Either skip till later or avoid completely.

Hinterlands - Lard's picknick

The quest spawns three 47-48 trolls on the tiny island with you after you click the quest item. Either be a druid, have a swimspeed potion, bring a friend or just avoid that quest.

Un'goro Crater

The easternmost Devilsaur patrol bugs and literally telefrags after reaching the river crossing in central eastern crater where it slides up and down the river twice before resuming patrol quite a bit away. You may aggro it if you do not have stealth or are far out of the way.

Stranglethorn Vale

Both eastern STV south of ZG raid entrance and the middle of STV north of Arena hold stealth shadowtooth panthers. Keep that in mind when you run around or fight other mobs (they share their areas with tigers in general).

Silithus

Silithus holds an elite Twilight Marauder pat who you might see from way back when you are in a flat area but beware as normal wildlife is able to charge-stun and poison you.

Eastern Plaguelands

The lowest type of bats got a fear cast, a Scarlet elite group patrols around plaguewood and Corrin's crossing is not safe at all as an undead hostile settlement on the way. Keep to the north and west of the road when doing the wildlife hunting quests of Fordring.

Hillsbrad Foothills

 **Helcular's Revenge (final part).**
Alliance guards. Enough said.

 **Dangerous! (Clerk Horrace Whitesteed)**
 **Battle of Hillsbrad (Magistrate Burnside)**
Both NPCs spawn in a town hall building, with casters nearby. Kill the guards first, one by one.

 **Dangerous! (Miner Hackett)**
 **Battle of Hillsbrad (Foreman Bonds)**
Found in a deep mine (red flag). Foremen patrol the area, so you can suddenly aggro multiple mobs.
Foreman Bonds, when badly hurt, summons two Dun Garok Soldiers - a nasty surprise when you almost killed him.

 **Battle of Hillsbrad (Blacksmith Verringtan)**
Not that dangerous as the town hall and the mine, but still be careful not to aggro too many humans.

 **Souvenirs of Death**
 **Battle of Hillsbrad (Hillsbrad Peasants)**
 **Battle of Hillsbrad (Farmer Ray and Farmer Getz)**
 **Elixir of Pain (feed the Elixir of Pain to Stanley)**
Also not that dangerous as other nearby quests, but mind the Hillsbrad Footmen patrolling the area.

 **Elixir of Agony (Mudsnout Blossoms)**
Be careful with those nasty casters.

Thousand Needles (The Shimmering Flats)

 **The Swarm Grows**
Those insects (drones) have chain aggro - touch one, and you will soon be surrounded by the entire swarm.

Final note: have a Hearthstone ready, if you venture into a deep cave (or even not so deep, if killing mobs on the way takes you much time). There's nothing more horrible than ploughing through multiple mobs and finally killing a tough NPC, only to be killed by ordinary guards suddenly respawning on your way back. Use HS to safely blink away instead.
