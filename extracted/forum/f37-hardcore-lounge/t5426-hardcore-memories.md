Belive me when I say, I will still know which faction gets a flightpoint in Nethergarde Keep on my death bed.

## Post 33169 by Ravenstone (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-01-26T09:46:00Z

My High Elven fire only 'blood' mage was in the mine in Northern Loch Modan. I had been happily blowing everyone up that I had come across with my fireballs so I was overconfident. I pushed deep into the mine only to realise halfway that some of the mobs were becoming immune to my fireballs through a spell. I panicked and realised I was too deep in the mine, and if I didn't escape soon I'd become overwhelmed.

I started to double back only to find the tunnel rats had already blocked my path. Throwing out a fireball at one,  I accidently aggroed another. Hastily casting a sheep on the second, a third spawned in as the polymorph applied, and soon I was fighting three mobs, who became immune to my fireball. Another soul desperately flailing around, tripping over fences and mine carts, to eventually be claimed by a mine and it's kobolds.

My first ever hardcore character, and first hardcore death was on my gnome warlock. Someone had posted in world chat that they needed help with an elite quest. The dwarf/gnome version of hogger. I've forgotten the name. He was a hunter and I figured his pet would have a taunt, so even if the pet died, we would likely be able to finish the job with no problem. To my surprise, this was not the case. His pet almost instantly lost threat, and the yeti began chasing him. The foolish dwarf began to run away, with me following close behind attempting to save him. I soon realized I'd also made a mistake, because as soon as he died, the furred giant turned to me, and began to chase me down, before mercilessly slaughtering my poor gnome boy. Leaving his imp without a master. You'll be remembered, Geeblik.
