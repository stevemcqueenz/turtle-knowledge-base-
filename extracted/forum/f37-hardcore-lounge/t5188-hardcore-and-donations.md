> **Borna wrote: Tue Oct 17, 2023 11:32 am**
> oh so it is a permanent purhase even if we play hc? that was the only thing stopping me from buyin stuff i thought it was gone when char dies in hc

There are 2 steps to the whole process and people seem to be getting confused between the two:

 1) Get tokens credited to your account.
You can complete the Slow & Steady challenge. You can "donate" real money (purchase #1 if you will). All of these steps are permanent and irreversible. I'm not aware of a refund policy.

 2) Get in-game rewards for specific characters.
You open the in-game shop, you spend tokens for cosmetics or helpful items (purchase #2 if you will). These purchases are generally permanent but not without exceptions. The biggest exception is that the death of a hardcore character automatically refunds all tokens spent on that character back to your account balance.

So yes, if you donate for 500 tokens and only play hardcore, these 500 tokens will not be lost, they stay with you through your various characters. That doesn't mean the tokens are entirely permanent (manually deleting a character does not refund tokens as far as I know), but the answer is yes - the death of a hardcore character will not permanently lose you tokens.
