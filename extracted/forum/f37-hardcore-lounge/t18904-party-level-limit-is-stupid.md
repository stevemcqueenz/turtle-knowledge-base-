## Post 128621 by Rachdingue — 2025-04-26T17:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128621#p128621 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I bet this is not the first topic in the forum but here I go

What is the meaning of the +/- 5 limit on party? This is totally ruining the experience. I guess the point here is to prevent boosting, but boosting still happens outside dungeons tho I've seen MANY. I rather have a 24 hour lock PER dungeon than this party level limit.

Im 37ish and a guildie is 39, we can't find tank to library. Maybe If we could get a 32-33 level tank but hey turtle wow team won't allow that. And I want to do SM so bad that I just disabled my exp gains because if I keep leveling I won't be able to do lower level dungeons. So I'm at the point of logging in, trying to find a party for dungeon and in 30+ min not finding shit just logging out cause I also don't want to level up. It is just stupid.

Same happens with trading. Veteran people have a character every 5 levels to be able to trade the full 1-60 range, what kind of joke is this? Why do I have to throw materials because I cannot find a guy on my level range to trade? What is the point of that. Why can't I give an item to my guildies just cause they are 10 levels below me.

In both situations this mechanic prevents one of the best feelings of vanilla wow, which is the social part of it.
