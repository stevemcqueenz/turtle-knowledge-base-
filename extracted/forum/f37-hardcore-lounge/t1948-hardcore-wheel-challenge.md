Hey adventurers !

 **Inspired by @Kanate I've decided to start a HC challenge too.**

I've done this challenge several times by the past so I wanted to have some fresh within it. I will play a retribution paladin on this run.

 **The wheel challenge.**  insidious_turtle

Rules :

 - Normal hardcore is enabled.
 - Every 5 lvl I have a new penalty that replace the older one.
 - Penalties start at lvl 5.
 - The penalty will be decided randomly on stream with a Wheel.
 - I have 3 re-roll (joker) to use during my progression. (It means that I have to spin the wheel once again and I have to take the next pick of the wheel.)
 - I can't roll the same penalty twice in a row. (For example I can't have the same penalty lvl 5 and 10 but I can have the same lvl 5 and 15. It ensure more "fun").

List of all penalties :
 - No healing spell for 5 lvl
 - No aura for 5 lvl
 - Delete a random piece of gear
 - Not allowed to group for 5 lvl
 - Throw 50% of my gold
 - No seal for 5 lvl
 - Bashing mob only for 5 lvl
 - I randomly drop one of my professions
 - I can't loot for 5 lvl (except quest loot and raw gold)
 - Can't equip anything that I loot for 5 lvl
 - Have to use a white weapon for 5 lvl
 - I have to throw my whole inventory except hearthstone, mount and quest item (This doesn't include the bank).
 - Have to take the glyph of Exhaustion for 5 lvl
 - Have to take the glyph of Turtle for 5 lvl
 - Delete all potions/water/food

 **This list is not exhaustive and might change before the challenge, feel free to give me any idea ! (Hard but not impossible please)**

If this Paladin reach lvl 60 on the first run I will give 100€ to the server. If this Paladin reach lvl 60 on any other run I will still give 50€ to the server !  happy_turtle_head

 **If anyone want to join me on this run feel free. The whole process will be livestreamed and will start Friday 20th August.**

Regards, Lindzei
