> if we were to accept the "stay a while and listen" quest would it immobile us from actually working together for the same common goal? How does DUOS work withen the challenge ?

If you're both HC characters it will not get in the way of your objectives

HC characters can group/trade with other HC characters so long as there's no more than a 5 level difference

But they may never use the auction house, so be aware of that if any of your plans involved making money with auctions

As a tip: Herbalism + Alchemy is considered to be some of the best professions for HC because consumables can save you in a pinch and provide great utility, if you're playing a duo it's probably a good idea to have one of you 2 as a potion crafter
