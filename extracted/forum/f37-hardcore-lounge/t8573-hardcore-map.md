## Post 55249 by Kissoncheg — 2023-07-30T19:11:00Z

Dear players! We continue to follow the world of wowturtle and today we are happy to introduce you a new improvement that will help you to be ready for any danger! Introducing the global map of dangerous monsters of our server, which is now available for everyone.
Since the last post on July 25 (link to the post: vk.com/hswowturtle?w=wall-219438565_535), we've been working hard to collect data and analyze the threats that may come your way, we've written over 1000 stacks of code. Now every player will be able to visually assess the dangers in each location and make the right decisions for a successful journey.
On the global map we have marked each location with dangerous monsters and assigned each of them a rank from 1 to 10, indicating the level of danger. The higher the rank, the stronger and more dangerous the monster is, and the more experience and rewards await you if you can handle the challenge. Note that the rank for each monster changes depending on the number of players killed by it.
Find locations with dangerous monsters on the global map and gather your team to tackle these challenges together. Remember that cooperation and coordination is the key to defeating the toughest opponents!
