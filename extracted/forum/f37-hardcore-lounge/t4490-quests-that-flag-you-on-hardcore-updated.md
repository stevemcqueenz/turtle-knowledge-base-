Part 11 The Missing Diplomat (FIXED) - Tapoke Jahn PVP Flagged, (Menethil Q ~lv 33), Check for Horde.

 - Fixed 2024 — April 24 viewtopic.php?t=13727
 - Players will no longer be flagged for PvP when attacking Dashel Stonefist for the quest The Missing Diplomat.
