## Post 31250 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-12-20T23:03:00Z

Alchemy is always viable on druids. Those who think it isn't viable since druids can't drink in form aren't playing druids well; good druids shift often (and ought to do so given the benefits of items like Wolfshead Helm<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=8345> and the Furor talent. This is obviously mostly relevant to feral druids, but alchemy is fine on the other specs as well since they can and should be shifting forms fairly often.

Leatherworking is nice and in flavor. Also, the leather armor pads (enchant slot occupiers) are best on a druid since they enjoy an armor multiplier when in bear and moonkin forms. On the other hand, my biggest gripe with crafting professions is that the craftable gear is often just a temporary stepping stone. Like the Grifter set, which is great, but has lootable or quest reward gear competing with it. The gear of course is good, but I generally find the perks it adds are often less notable than the benefits of something an elixirs would accomplish. For example, some people go nuts over gear that has what are objectively good stats, like the Deviate Scale Belt <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=6468>, but in the end provides less stats than something of equivalent ilvl like the Bristlebark Belt <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=14567> plus elixir of lesser fortitude, elixir of minor agility, elixir of minor defense, and elixir of giant strength. Of course the ideal would be to have it all, both the good craftable gear and elixirs, but the reality is that we rarely see players with both until level 60.
Ultimately, I favor alchemy since the elixirs always help regardless of gear and the healing/mana/etc. potions are always nice to have in reliable supply. But that's just my preference.

Enchanting wasn't mentioned by you, but it's always worth mentioning for druids since Smoking Heart of the Mountain is such a great trinket for them. In HC, people are generally poor and there is a lower supply of enchanting supplies than on normal, so it's both difficult to make money with and hard to get supplies for - including the rods crafted by blacksmiths. More importantly for Smoking Heart of the Mountain, it's exceedingly rare to find HC players willing to go as far in BRD as is necessary to craft it. So it's not a good reason to go enchanting.
