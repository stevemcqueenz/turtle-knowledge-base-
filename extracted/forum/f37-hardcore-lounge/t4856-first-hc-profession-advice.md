## Post 29282 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-11-15T23:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29282#p29282 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi! A warlock was my first immortal and I have a good amount of experience with various professions.

Basically, there are a lot of people that fall into think either alch/herb or engi/mining are the ideal HC professions. There are other possibilities, like tailoring/enchanting, but such wouldn’t help with survivability nearly as much as the first two combinations. I think alch or engi are the two best for most classes in HC

What a lot of people miss is that that the professions should lend themselves to playing to the strengths and covering the weaknesses of each class. For example, I went engi on my rogue (also immortal) in HC since they pickpocket more than enough health potions and sprint is better than a swiftness potion while also being on a different cooldown. Furthermore, the energy system means I can use bombs when out of energy in order to damage and stun simultaneously while energy regenerates. So engineering on my rogue was great.

As a warlock, I chose alchemy. This is because they don’t have sprint or other great escape mechanisms, so a swiftness potion is always appreciated. Troll blood elixirs when combined with demon armor provides a bunch of health regeneration. And the other elixirs were nice too. Engineering bombs would take time away from casting, so they wouldn’t be very useful. Target dummies are ok, but warlocks already have pets to help manage agro as needed. A lot of the other engineering items are nice but are best suited to PvP, which as an HC character is not a good area to invest in while leveling.

You said you’ll be soloing. This is another reason why alchemy may be the best choice relative to engineering or tailor/enchant. You already noted how enchanting rods can be tricky to come by if mostly a solo player. And you’d have to go to Uldaman sometime for high level enchants, which you probably don’t want to fight through solo. Engineering requires more trading with other professions such as leather workers for a lot of its more notable products, target dummies included. Meanwhile, alchemy is a profession that stands alone and independent in terms of needing the others. You can fish for certain ingredients. During the living process, alchemists don’t need ingredients from any of the other primary professions to make their most important leveling potions and elixirs.

In the end, I chose alchemy since it was fun and helped me feel witchy while playing my warlock. Choose whatever professions you’ll enjoy as you embark on your hardcore journey.

A note on trading in HC. Some people will say to go engineering and trade for alchemy potions. While theoretically possible, it’s impractical. While possible in the early levels, it becomes increasingly improbable as you level up. When I was in the 50 range, I knew 2 alchemists in my trading range. We generally weren’t in the same areas or playing at the same time, so we never traded. And the time I leveled as an alchemist, there also wasn’t a whole lot of trading happening at the high end. So there’s that to consider if you ever hear of it being easy to trade for things you don’t have.
