I see there is an underlying risk that should be mitigated as it would potentially lead to some downsides for us Turtles. However, why can't we explore this idea a bit further to weigh off good and bad? Maybe we can accommodate for the downsides if it means more upside on the other hand? One common fallacy in modern businesses to avoid is also that adding new features isn't the only thing or even always recommended. Some development should always be dedicated to improve the existing experience.

I also want to highlight that **enabling PvP is a feature request at the heart of many players** and if we look at it from a strategic lens it would for example give Turtle WoW even more of an edge against others who provide the Hardcore experience.

In the Hardcore challenge we set out to challenge the PvE environment in quests, open world and dungeons. While I understand there are more challenges involved to make PvP available in the open world, I see a much lower hanging fruit in enabling it for battlegrounds.

 **So what does it really mean for us to explore** (incomplete list):

Assumptions to check with Dev-Team for tech. feasibility
 - Can we disable Hardcore in Battlegrounds or add a temporary condition to make players revivable?
 - Can we access the code that causes players to be flagged for PvP after a battleground?
 - Can we touch this code safely or is there any possibility of a workaround?
 - Can we use the event of leaving the Battleground to trigger another event like a buff similar to desertion?
 - Can we protect Hardcore players in the event of them leaving the Battleground with a protection buff?
 - Can we make this buff/debuff exclusive to outside of battlegrounds?
 - Can we test this live with a disclaimer or an otherwise safe environment?
 - ..

Hardcore pinciples & ethics to explore
 - Isn't Hardcore more of a PvE experience and we should look at PvP from a separate lens?
 - Is there any usecase for Hardcore being enabled in Battlegrounds that is actively practiced? Is it worth overruling it or a possibility to stay mortal in battlegrounds?
 - Does never dieing in an instance specifically intended for PvP as a game mode really go against the Hardcore ethos that is more PvE focused?
 - What would be the impact on the Battleground & Hardcore Experience for players in various modes alike?
 - ..

Also here an example worth considering: Currently Hardcore players negatively influence the Battleground Experience for normal players in the 10-19 bracket with quests like Horde Defender Axe. Finding a way to accommodate would serve both sides.

Feel free to add any more angles or ideas to explore the possibility of providing accessibility to Battlegrounds for Hardcore players.

Personally, I'd be delighted if this can be implemented as I follow somewhat a completionist approach to Hardcore and also love to engage in Player vs Player.

Thanks for your consideration and bringing this thread to the attention of the Turtle WoW Team, specifically Product Management and Engineering.
