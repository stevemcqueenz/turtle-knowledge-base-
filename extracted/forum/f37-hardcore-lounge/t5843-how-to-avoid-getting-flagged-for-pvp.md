## Post 36922 by Bellybutton (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-05T20:37:00Z

Don't buff or heal players that are PvP flagged, because it will automatically flag you as PvP as well.
Green name - PvP flagged
Blue name - Not PvP flagged.

There are some (not a lot, but some) quests that require you to kill mobs that have a PvP flag on them (notably killing the Stonard Scouts in Swamp of Sorrows), avoid doing those quests because killing said mobs will flag you as PvP... ...And flagging PvP in Swamp of Sorrows is a terrible idea *grin*
