# Suggestion:  Add a "Solo Self Found" Challenge Option

## Post 134705 by Yandaemonium — 2025-06-08T03:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134705#p134705 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Given how Turtle has a ton of different, creative challenges built into its client, I am really surprised that they do not have this one when even official Blizzard HC does.

For those who do not know, the rules are quite simple:
 --No grouping up with anyone under any circumstances
 --No trading with anyone under any circumstances

It is usually played in conjunction with normal HC rules as an additional difficulty hurdle, but it can also be played on its own if one wishes to challenge themself without perma-death.
