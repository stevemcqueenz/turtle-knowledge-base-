Since I will be playing as Orc Shaman on horde side. There are several things that can proven useful during leveling:

It's okay to quest in other starter zones if you dont feel like grinding through boars and scorpids for that example, the zeppelins outside Orgrimmar will take you to both Thunder Bluff and Undercity (Mulgore and Tirisfal Glades) to grab quest rewards and even perhaps you have better luck getting 6-slot bags drops from the mobs

Start using primary and secondary professions soon as possible to max them out before next bracket

Fishing and Cooking are easy to max out as soon you dinged to level 5 on skill 1-75 and 75-150 on level 10. Don't forget to learn cooking recipes of the fish you have catch.

Speaking of cooking, there's custom quest in Razor Hill allow you to make crocolisk meat that gives you +4 Stamina/Spirit which is very good to acquire once you have reached level 10 that requires cooking 80 to learn the recipe

Because Barrens is quite unforgiving place that get you easily killed, you have two options once you reached level 10: You do quests in other starter zones (Bloodhoof Village and Brill) or you will grind crocolisks between Durotar and Barrens for level 10 and level 11 until you reach level 12, that way you get big supply of crocolisks ready to cook

Fear that you have to run back to Orgrimmar or Ratchet for storing the crocolisk meat for your bags? Worry not! There's bank NPC along the river nearby not far from Farwatch Post and there's no danger to reach them as you can store your items in the bank and vendor trash items for easy cash

level 12 is recommend level to start questing Barrens

Once you have got maxed out at skill 75 on all professions and dinged at level 10, You start by maxing out fishing first by Ratchet and learn second fish cooking recipe for you to level up both fishing and cooking up to 150.

There's another handful tip, when you have reached fishing skill 150, you can with +75 bait to boost fishing skill up to 225 is just enough to fish in Booty Bay when you take the ship from Ratchet.

Why fishing when you are already capped? Well the answer is retrieving Mithril Trunks from Booty Bay that give you little bit of silver and as well bolts of Runecloth and mageweave, Rugged leather and thick leather for gold making. You also get potions that is way above your level to make use of them, these can be sold as well. You should also keep eye open for rare fish catch that gives you gems like Star Ruby, Blue Sapphire and the like, these sell for very nice silver too. Do it often and you will be fishing for gold and got the money back you bought from the +75 fishing bait boosts.

Fishing go a long way, teaching you how to fish and you will be fed for lifetime ;)

Philosophy aside, it is important to have honored reputation with any friendly faction you come across, Ratchet is also one of the neutral factions which you can get benefits from. If you are miner and you have friendly reputation, you can get a nice mining upgrade of +5 that requires you to have 10x Light Leather and Ornate Spyglass made by player Engineers and ordinary mining pickaxe. Goblin players will find +10 mining bonus a very big advantage when they want to smelt tin, silver and iron early on compared to any other race

Crossroads also have recipe of thunder lizard cooking recipe that gives +6 stamina/spirit which is big compared to crocolisks cooking, so by the time you are strong to attack thunder lizards (level 18 recommended), you can vendor crocolisks meat and start using thunder lizard tail meals instead.

In my personal opinion, leveling your mining and engineering soon as possible allow you to craft stun bombs, goggles that gives stamina/spirit buffs and target dummies that prove to be much better damage sponge and taunt than Stoneclaw totem which makes for perfect opportunity to heal up, get out of here via escape route. Horde players also want Deadly Blunderbus for Ashenvale quest, it's good idea to make yourself useful through player trade and you will find friends that will help you with difficult quests in Barrens that are proven too difficult to solo if you are not good enough.

I can't stress you enough, avoid caves unless you are absolutely ready with potions, good gear and have good common sense, and above all else, do not panic when things doesn't go your way, there's always decision making that will save your hard work you have put through.

When you are done for the quest, simply use hearthstone in safe spot in the cave, do not attempt to exit the cave through the entrance or you will get caught by respawned mobs.

Avoid places like Boulder Lode Mine, any oasis filled with centaurs not any necessary than just the quests, Sledge Fen, Dreadmist Peak, southern barrens and Wailing Caverns. All the places I mentioned are filled with tight packed mobs in groups and even have rare mobs standing by that proves to be very dangerous in capable taking out players on their own, let alone fighting with other mobs will take out your hitpoints rather real quick.

Do not stay any longer than needed to be if you must, but when you quest together with someone else or more people and they feel that it's not feeling well for them, then its good time to get out before it's too late and says goodbye for your character

Hi! Long time no see. I have been inactive in few months however I am back in business.

Not only I am leveling orc shaman on horde side, but also dwarf warrior on alliance side with the same challenge goes by the name Powderbarrel

The first five levels is such a pain, why? Because the spirit stat is absolutely down and you can gain 1HP while out of combat early on, flat broke and food...this is tough choice to continue.

But also because I seek fights with bugged mobs like Juvenile Snow Leopard they hit fast and hard, fortunately after four fights surviving them, it wasn't easy task to do that. I survived because of potions and decent low level 2H axe I bought from Kharanos so I hit hard enough.

Remember I mention you should avoid caves? Well it is true but except I ignore my own rules and went into the wendigo cave and what do you know! I got +4 Strength 2H axe in a chest! No I am not joking, this was such a great upgrade I carried until level 11

Speaking of levels, the alliance side has reputation vendors like Ironforge and Darnassus, now the Night Elves vendor sells mail gear of level 25 and you're also required to have Honored Reputation in order to use them. So thats what I did by taking the boat from Stormwind Harbor to Auberdine on Darkshore and from here, to Darnassus/ Teldrassil and complete every available quest  I can complete there.

I can tell from the experience, it's not easy and fortunately this is a great place to level up your profession skills like Herbalism, Alchemy and Cooking (secondary) with spider kebab which is easy to gather and doesn't require spices. Teldrassil also has access to Mageroyals (which have lowkey chance of spawning swiftthistle. Potions with 4+ strength, 50 armor, HP2/5 and 27HP per 1 hour (total HP of 47) which can be quite lifesaver while you're also brewing low level healing potions, minor rejuvenation potion is a MUST while leveling in Teldrassil because it heals more than minor healing potion.

What are the dangers in Teldrassil? The whole mother nature: Spiders, moonstalkers, owls, harpies, furbolgs, timberlings, imps. Places like Shadowthread Cave, Starbreeze Village, Fel Rock, Ban'ethil Hollow, Lake Al'ameth, Wellspring Lake, Oracle Glade and Gnarlpine Hold are absolutely dangerous alone, it is recommended to group with someone, however great job if you pull this alone.

Shadowthread Cave is packed tight with spiders so you can potentially pull more than one and not survive this, there is also custom quest that gives you wand for caster classes which is nice if you run out of mana

Starbreeze Village is your first encounter with bear humanoid creatures, keep eye open for one rare in that place they can potentially ruin your day if you see them patrolling, they are not nice if you pull them, thats not all you also have to fight more of them nearby that village for the necklace's emerald gem.

Fel Rock is best avoided, however! If you have done the quest that gives you severed voodoo claws that reduce physical damage cast on the mob you use, that may be doable...but the problem is the Imp mobs that may accompany this Satyr mob required for quest and I believe there's also rare IMP inside there

(Reminder! Only use Severed voodoo claws against named NPC's that deals LOT of damage, this will save your life! By the time I came to Darkshore, I got 1-2 left)

Lake Al'ameth which is south from Dolanaar is your first encounter with elemental beasts called Timberlings which is spread all over the lake, do not approach towards the west side of the lake as the mobs increase in levels 8-9 that uses AoE spell and there's golden dragon portrait inside a cave which is ELITE quest, Oakenscowl hit like a truck and soak lot of damage, only HC chad warrior would welcome a challenge and live to tell the tale.

(REMINDER! DO NOT STAY CLOSE TO THE QUESTGIVER THAT MAY SPAWN TIMBERLINGS IN YOUR LOCATION!)

The other place where Timberlings can be found is at Wellspring Lake which is north-east from Darnassus the capital city of night elves and also close by Oracle Glade, The notable spells they use are Roots and Healing, KEEP EYE OPEN for level 13 rare which drops a quest, but you better avoid them because these are way worse than Oakenscowl, Kiting and CC is your best tools to keep them at distance. Later on you also have to kill named spider nearby

Oracle Glade have top level spiders, owls and moonstalkers nearby for this zone and the worst kind of mobs in that area, are definitely the harpies. The magic spells HURTS your HP points and certain types heal from the damage they caused, there's also a hidden quest that requires you to rescue Moonstalker companion for a sentinel NPC by the moonwell and there's interactable fruit plant for another quest

Now the real question is, can you survive Ban'ethil Hollow alone? Not recommended alone no, I did however survive at level 8 and the guild chat doesn't believe me.

So what I did is having good gear for that level, potions for buffing and healing, food buffed and potentially HP buffed by priest, and you're good to go for 30 minutes. What are the mobs? These are Furbolgs and there's plenty to list: Melee Warlocks that use debuff curse to make you hit less damage, healing shamans and normal ones. The most dangerous foe is level 10 Furbolg called Greenpaw and their lightning shocks can deal about 50-65+ Damage and they're always nearby with Augur if everything goes well

(REMINDER! You need to have minor rejuvenation potion and severed voodoo claw to debuff Greenpaw ready if you want to live, be sure you're very good geared for level like 8, Warriors will hamstring a lot of times inside the cave which prevent the fleeing mobs to pull more)

If you manage to defeat Greenpaw then congratulations you fought the most dangerous mob in that cave, the cursed druid is easy compared to Greenpaw and once you've done all the quests in here then the most difficult part of whole Teldrassil has yet to enter:

Gnarlpine Hold!

There's interactable fruit just like the one near Oracle Glade and this one in Gnarlpine Hold is right at front of their territory, so what do they have? The furbolgs once again and this time, they are upgraded with increased attack speed and lightning bolts that does hurts much, they are also tightly packed together as well...THIS is where I absolutely recommended going with a group to clear out the area for Ursal the mauler

However there's a custom quest that wants you to kill Avengers and Pathfinders, the mobs that are in Gnarlpine Hold in the area and rewards you with +3 STM and +2 SPI ring which is poggers and certified badge that you completed Teldrassil Hardcore!

So how far are we with Darnassus Rep after this: Well just about 5,5K or more which is few hundreds short from reaching Honored which you have to do Darkshore quests however once you reached honored rep with the night elves then congratulations, all you have to do is reach level 25, earn currency and STAY ALIVE as Alliance Warrior which gives VERY nice AP and STM stats for level 25.
