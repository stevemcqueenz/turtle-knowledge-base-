As the person who has both an immortal warrior and an immortal rogue I can confidently say: rogue is way easier than warrior.

Rogue has a ton of options to escape, warrior only has intimidating shout pretty much. Retaliation can help you to survive a tricky situation by standing your ground, but sometimes you just **have** to run. Got cornered by undeads that are immune to fear, and retaliation is still on cooldown? Time to think about your next character's name.

Next, Rogue is way less gear reliant than warrior. Well, if you spec into combat that's true, so make an Ambush build instead. You are going to crit for half of the mob health from Ambush with 100% crit chance due to the Remorseless Attacks and Improved Ambush talents. I am currently leveling a vagrant artisan rogue with level 5 copper daggers as my main weapons, and this build is still going strong at level 53, albeit it removes ~20% and not ~50% on Ambush crit at this level.

Of course, Rogue is not an auto win button. They are indeed squishy, that's true. If you want an auto win melee class, you should probably roll paladin. Rogues are also susceptible to hubris (I can stealth though those dense packs of elites no problems!..), but way less so than hunters and warlocks. And be wary that you can also screw yourself up royally if you place target dummy then vanish, that's a bad idea. For rogue, pick alchemy+herbalism instead.

Once you get to level 22 as rogue and get Vanish, you are set to success. Before that, be super careful.
And don't do your poison class quest before you get Vanish.
Hope that helps~
