## Post 59502 by Grantgrace — 2023-08-28T10:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59502#p59502 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Losing a character you've invested time and emotion into can feel like a real blow. It's natural to feel down, and even if you're surrounded by people, that sense of loneliness can still creep in. What I've found helpful is talking about it with fellow gamers who've been through similar experiences. Sharing stories and strategies for coping can make you feel understood and less alone.
Remember, there's no shame in reaching out for support. Sometimes, a [hotline for mental health](https://mentalhealthhotline.org/) can provide valuable advice and someone to listen to when you're navigating tough emotions. Taking care of your mental well-being is crucial, both inside the game and in your day-to-day life.
