> **Rokit wrote: Thu May 18, 2023 8:03 pm**
> If they drug Stitches in Duskwood to your AOE to have it aggro onto you, that is a reportable offense.

Wait.  It *is*?  Let's see here…

> **Fin wrote: Fri Oct 01, 2021 12:39 pm**
> **To keep the community friendly the following activities are strictly forbidden:**
> - Kiting a group of mobs into another player's (AoE) spells.

Huh.  Guess I forgot about– hey, *waaait a minute*…

Correct me if I'm wrong, but… a "group" is generally defined as "a number of things or persons being in some relation to one another".  Now, I know that Stitches is an **Elite** mob, but… it's still only *one mob*.  Ergo, the rule – **as it's presently written** – would not cover this particular type of griefing.

If the focal part of this rule is simply the AoE part, then I highly recommend that this rule be **rephrased** as such:
> - [No] Kiting mobs into other random players' (AoE) spells.

On a similar note…
> - [No] Dropping infernals and doomguards on a group of players in low level zones.

This is a very disappointing rule.  ):  Where's the harm in a little *uncontrolled chaos* from time to time~?
