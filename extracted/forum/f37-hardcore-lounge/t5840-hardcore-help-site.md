Hey everyone, my dad has recently started playing on our server in HC mode, and he came up with the idea to create a list of the most dangerous monsters on the server. Within five days, we collaborated to develop the website <https://hswowturtle.com>. On the website, you can check out the list of the most dangerous monsters and the players who died at their hands.

Please note that the statistics are only based on five days of data, and we already have clear contenders for first place in the future. One of them is ***Unseen level 51***, who lives in Duskwood and mercilessly kills players 30 level  with a single blow coming out of invisibility.

We hope that our site will help HC players avoid dangerous places and stupid deaths while trying to reach level 60.

Since we have only collected stats for five days and from two accounts, we kindly ask for your help by sending us your chat log file to *[[email protected]](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#adc5ccdfc9cec2dfc8ded9ccd9dedac2dad9d8dfd9c1c8edcac0ccc4c183cec2c0)*. To record the chat logs, you just need to enter the command **/chatlog** at the beginning of each game session. The log file is created in the **/root game/Logs/WoWChatLog.txt** directory, and it should be sent in its original format via email.
