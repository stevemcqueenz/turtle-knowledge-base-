I can't really speak on behalf of hardcore related stuff since I don't really play it much at all, buuuut

On my main, the big thing I like to do when I'm bored or just want to stock up on things are:

Just kill shit - for various reasons. My most common mobs are the scorpids, spiders and worms near cenarion hold in silithus for sandworm meat. I make smoked desert dumplings with those as a DPS food. The gray items from the mobs I kill around there actually make decent money when I vendor them.

Next are winterfall furbolgs. I think it's obvious why people kill these, but I might as well bring it up.
Beads for rep hand in, winterfall firewater for the attack power buff, and Winterfall E'ko for Juju power. That gives strength, I believe. Lasts a half hour and you get 3 per hand in so it's a great raiding consume.

The Hyjal Satyrs are also something I've done a few times but don't do often. There's always other people farming them- which is alright since drops are shared. Gold is good, and you can hand bright shards in for consumes and other stuff.

And lastly, level alts. That's kinda it. I know it's all hardly related, but I figured I'd share anyways.
