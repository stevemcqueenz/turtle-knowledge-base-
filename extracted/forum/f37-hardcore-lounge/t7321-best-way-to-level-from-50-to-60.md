There should be plenty of quests if you utilize tents at all. Have you tried the new zones? Lapidis Isle and Gillijim Isle?

The safest thing you could do is probably grind mobs. The nagas on the beach in Azshara are solid for your level and then once you reach 55 you could try moving to Winterspring and grinding the furbolgs / yetis near Everlook. Be careful though, the yetis have a stun.
