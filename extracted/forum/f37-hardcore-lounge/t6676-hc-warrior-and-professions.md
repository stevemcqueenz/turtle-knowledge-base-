## Post 42029 by Mcnair (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-18T15:06:00Z

Good day!
I think the chances that your profession choice would make or break your HC run is quite low. With the exception of engineering, all the other can be traded for from other players.
So I would recommend to choose something that you think would best fit your idea of the character or that you personally prefer.
That said, I chose blacksmithing on my troll warrior in HC, as I really liked the idea to mostly wear self-made weapons and armour.
