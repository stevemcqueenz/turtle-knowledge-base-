You got killed by someone "disguising" their flagged pet as a mob?  That's *hilarious*!  And hopefully, you've learned a lesson from your absentminded clicking.

To answer your question: the GMs [will never revive a dead Hardcore character](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=1636) for any reason.  *However*.  The person who killed you *has* **broken [the rules](https://turtlecraft.gg/#/rules%23griefing)**.

> **To keep the community friendly the following activities are strictly forbidden:**
> - Harassing a player by following them around and tagging mobs they need to kill for a quest or **tricking them into getting flagged for PvP without their intent**.

Hopefully, you have some sort of tangible evidence that this happened.  If you happen to have the character's name, rather than the pet, that would be ideal.  Screenshots and log files, of course, would also be very helpful.  Otherwise, a rough estimate of when and where this happened might suffice.  Whatever the case, open an in-game ticket clearly explaining the situation, and with any luck, something good will happen.  Just remember that this won't get your character restored, but at least you might get *something* for your trouble.
