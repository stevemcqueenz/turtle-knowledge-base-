I am just guessing here, but probably these players tried to flee from a warlock in PvP with the pet following them, and then they died outside the distance that the vanilla engine considers in pet-owner relations. In those cases the pets will be the one killing the victim, and it will not be attributed to the pet owner. This distance issue can be seen in other cases, e.g. when you find a shaman totem out in the wild with the shaman already far from it, it will show to you as a red mob without an owner.
Another case could be when a pet kills the victim without the pet owner doing any damage - in this case the kill credit goes to the pet, not the owner.
