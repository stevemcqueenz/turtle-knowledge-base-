> **D3mig wrote: Tue May 02, 2023 2:22 am**
> Had a 11 lvl Human Wariror. Was killed by Undead Walock ?? lvl in Elvyn forest. Just HC , no warmode. How is it possible?

You grouped up or buffed a player who did have Warmode on -- you can tell by them having a Horde/Alliance sigil next to their portrait - or, if you're using some addon that replaces the original unit frames, PvP people of your own faction always have green names rather than blue.
By grouping up or buffing them, you were automatically flagged for PvP, which that warlock took advantage of.
While playing HC, you need to pay attention to who you're interacting with and if you didn't accidentally flag yourself for PvP.
