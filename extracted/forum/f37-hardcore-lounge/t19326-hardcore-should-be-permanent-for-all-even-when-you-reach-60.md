# Hardcore should be permanent for all even when you reach 60

## Post 131279 by InfernoTooFewPlayers — 2025-05-15T22:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131279#p131279 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really enjoyed playing hardcore and I would consider supporting this project more if something was done about lack of players on inferno. Finding a group for dungeons becomes impossible when playing inferno and I have stopped playing as I cant find any players for dungeons.

Different suggestions how to fix lack of players on inferno:

a) **Merge Nordanaar and Telabim servers.** There are not many inferno players on Telabim (sorry if I don't got the server names right) but the few players there would unite the community if servers merged.

b) **Players who choose hardcore should no longer get the title immortal and mount at lvl 60, instead hardcore is permanent even when you reach lvl 60.** You can remove the "inferno" and simply call it hardcore. (This is the most important suggestion which should be done!)

c) **Make it easier and faster to go from hardcore to inferno.** I don't understand why you added extra steps which cannot be soloed to play inferno. This reduces the number of players who play hardcore at lvl 60 since the quest to become inferno is complicated for new players as you don't know if you are permanent hardcore or not (go to a mailbox below karazhan guarded by 20 lvl 60 mobs which just drags it out. Players wanna do dungeons, raids and other fun stuff instead of trying to get to a mailbox and escorting new players there).

d) **Remove level restrictions for trading and mailing items due to trade chain exploit.** If you create alts and level them up you can trade with anyone and it becomes a chore. Its hard to sell and buy items since there basically is no market. Keep the AH closed for hardcore, but enable only trading and mail. This means level 60s will be able to give petri flasks for low levels but it will increase number of players who are willing to play hardcore and inferno.

e) **Hide the dungeon finder icon when playing hardcore.** This is confusing for many players as to why it doesnt work when it is infact disabled in hardcore mode.

f) **Add stats how many succesful dungeons a player has completed when you shift-click a players name in chat.** This will allow experienced pug players to be trusted by guilds to play more together. Currently many guilds will only play dungeons with guildies because they think pugs are inexperienced and dont know how to pull packs, boss encounters, gear etc.

g) **Dire Maul should be 5 players max, and not possible to do 10 player raid.** As a tank it's hard to find a group for dungeons due to this, due to only needing 1 tank for 10 man raids in 5 man dungeons, it also becomes too easy removing the otherwise awesome feeling when playing hardcore.

h) **Hardcore players should not be able to create portable banks, mailboxes and repair bots which follow you.** This makes the game simply too easy and not "hardcore".

i) **Make the challenges in character creation screen bigger and easier to find.**  Many players dont know there is in fact challenges such as hardcore mode when you create your character as it is easy to miss the button that opens the submenu for it.
