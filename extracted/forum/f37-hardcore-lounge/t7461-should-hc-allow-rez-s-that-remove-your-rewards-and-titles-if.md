**PLEASE READ BEFORE YOU FLAME**

I have the utmost respect for anyone with the Immortal tag. Again, no disrespect intended.  Just a thought I had and wanted to get y'alls opinions on it.

## Post 48915 by Styopa1 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-06-05T15:25:00Z

I've thought about the same thing myself (somewhere between my 3rd and 4th HC deaths) but ultimately I think it slightly cheapens the HC experience.
I get it, completely.  And I wouldn't really object if it was implemented but it slightly takes away the "ALL IN" feel of HC which is the core element of the addiction.

If HC = 'every act may be consequential' your proposal then takes away a little bit of the sting if you can keep playing that character later, just as a 'normie'.
What it means, really, is that the colossal pain of dying say at...45+...is quite a bit less.

And that just ain't HC, to me.

What it would do, I would mention in counterpoint, is it means that lvl 20+ game is FAR more likely to see HC people willing to run riskier content like dungeons.
That alone might be worth it for the game generally.
