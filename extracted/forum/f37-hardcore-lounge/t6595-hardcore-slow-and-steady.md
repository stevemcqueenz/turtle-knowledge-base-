## Post 41574 by Portholes — 2023-04-14T22:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41574#p41574 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Done HC S&S and would highly recommend.  You can keep your professions up to date, you get little gifts in the mail every 10 levels (cause of slow and steady), and in the end you get the Murloc Illusion and Tallstrider mount (which is huge on a Tauren).  One thing for me when I'm playing normal HC is I tend to rush to keep up with everybody else.  With Slow and Steady I just kinda accept I'm going to be slow as shit and everybody is going to lap me so why push it.  You'll have better gear as your leveling due to completing more at level quests/crafting.  At least for me if I level a normal character, I wish i did it HC, and when i finish leveling a HC character, I wish I did it S&S HC.

Also it's not much different from playing Classic or Vanilla. It's not like they made all these crazy changes and you'll be lost on whats going on.  Just play normally and you'll find cool stuff along the way. The only thing i would note is to take advantage of "Tents".  Tents give you rested xp at a very fast rate and that's why you'll see people standing under them in places like Goldshire.
