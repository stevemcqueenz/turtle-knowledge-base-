## Post 150477 by Thule (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-06T10:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150477#p150477 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yes, the challenge ends when you reach level 60.
However, if you want to continue after reaching level 60, you must first complete a quest that will mark you as Inferno, and then the challenge will continue.
This quest is available from level 55, and you must complete it before reaching level 60.

The quest you'd need to complete before reaching level 60 is this:
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40914>

It starts from an item you'll get in the mail once you reach level 55. If you do not complete this quest, you will turn into a softcore character as soon as you hit level 60 and can die as often as you want.
