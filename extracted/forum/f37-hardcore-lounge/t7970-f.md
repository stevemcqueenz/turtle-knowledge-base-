I already wrote it in another, similar topic, but I think it's worth to spread the word - too many people do not know to _never_ use the 'Exit now' button!
If you press the 'Exit now' button during the countdown, your character does not stay in game for the remainder of the 20 seconds but for 3 full minutes, the normal DC-logout time! This is more than enough for any NPC to respawn and eat your (hardcore) character alive.
