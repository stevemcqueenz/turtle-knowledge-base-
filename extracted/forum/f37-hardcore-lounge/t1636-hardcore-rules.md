# Hardcore Rules

## Post 9678 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2021-03-06T15:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9678#p9678 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, weary traveler!

We're excited to unveil a brand-new mode coming to Turtle WoW — one that's not for the faint of heart.

Introducing **Hardcore Mode** — a brutal new way to play, inspired by Diablo's permadeath system and the old-school Hardcore challenges of Classic realms. This is the ultimate test of skill, caution, and sheer stubbornness: level your character to 60 without dying. Not even once.

The ultimate goal of this challenge is to reach level 60 without ever dying. Once you’re level 60, you can participate in raiding and PvP without a risk of losing your character.

To join the challenge, toggle Hardcore Mode when creating your character. While leveling, your character will carry the title **Still Alive**, letting the world know you’re doing it the hard way.

Reach 60 and you'll earn:

- Achievement title **<Immortal>**

- [[Tabard of the Immortal Guardian]](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80187)

- [**[Immortal Champion's Charger]**](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=80692)

 **Rules [Read it. Live it. Die by it]**

- When your character dies, the game ends for you. If you choose to create and play a hardcore character, you do so at your own risk: we will not restore any deceased hardcore characters for any reason including connection problems, bugs, global warming, your little sister, or any other reason whatsoever.

- Dead characters will remain locked on a selection screen unavailable for playing. As long as you don’t delete them, characters above level 10 will be displayed on our [Scoreboard](https://armory.turtlecraft.gg/hardcore).

- You will be able to trade items and gold only with other Hardcore characters as long as the difference between your levels isn’t higher than 5.

- You will be able to group up only with other Hardcore characters as long as the difference between your levels isn’t higher than 5.

- Hardcore characters can not trade, nor buy on Auction House.

- Hardcore characters can use mail, but with no attachments.

- It is strictly forbidden to multibox if either of your characters is undergoing a Hardcore challenge.

- Hardcore characters are not permitted to get assistance from players outside of their group.
