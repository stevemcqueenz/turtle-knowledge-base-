I routinely do dungeons and they're quite safe without voice chat. In fact, the worst dungeon experience I heard involved voice. The healer said, "Oh shit, oh shit," throughout the whole thing. That made it stressful for everyone. A less stressful (and safer) experience can be had by just overleveling for dungeons. For example, Deadmines and Wailing Caverns are generally done at 22+. Some people lowball dungeons and pay the price. I've been invited to group with some people and declined when I saw what levels were being accepted. And then I've seen the death notices. It's happened more than once, but not in the never in the dungeon groups I've been part of. So it all depends on the players. A well coordinated team generally doesn't need voice but instead simply communicates roles from the start.

Also, dungeons are generally safer than the open world if you follow these kinds of guidelines for dungeoning. This is because all the npcs in dungeons are known or can be known beforehand. The open world, on the other hand, has rare wandering elites, dangerous respawns (with some people dying to rare elites respawning on top of them), and a lot more dangerous bugs to potentially encounter. All my deaths, and there have been a decent number of them, were from the open world but never dungeons, despite running dungeons often multiple times each day.
