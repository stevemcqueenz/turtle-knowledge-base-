Here is Bing Chats Response:
I found a few addons that might help you achieve what you’re looking for:

Clique1: This is a simple addon that enables powerful click-casting and hover-casting on your unit frames and in the 3D game world. You can bind virtually any mouse or keyboard combination to a spell or macro1. It’s also available for WoW Classic and WoW TBC Classic1.

VuhDo2: This addon replaces your group unit frames with highly customizable ones. It also makes it easy to bind your spells to mouse clicks, similar to using mouseover macros2.

Click Cast Keybinds3: This is a new feature coming in Eternity’s End, allowing players to bind spells and macros to mouse clicks. You can bind spells to default mouse binding (left / right button) or create custom mouse bindings3.

Please note that you will be unable to make these changes when you are in combat due to limitations in the Blizzard API1. I hope this helps! Let me know if you have any other questions.
