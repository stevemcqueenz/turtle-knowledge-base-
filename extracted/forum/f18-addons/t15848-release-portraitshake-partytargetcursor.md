# [Release] PortraitShake & PartyTargetCursor

## Post 107980 by stoofin — 2024-11-15T00:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107980#p107980 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been leveling a priest and made myself a couple tiny add-ons for the vanilla UI.

[PartyTargetCursor](https://github.com/stoofin/PartyTargetCursor) points a a final fantasy 7 cursor at your target's party portrait. It moves smoothly around as your target changes.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/k07VSLW.png)

[PortraitShake](https://github.com/stoofin/PortraitShake) shakes a portrait when that unit takes damage.  Helpful to see who in your party is getting slapped, and also a goofy fun thing.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/cMlgEth.gif)
