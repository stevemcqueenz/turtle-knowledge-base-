I've noticed the [Corehound Belt](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19162) recipe from Tribal LW incorrectly shows 4 Rune Thread, when it should be 4 Ironweb Spider Silk

As for a bug report/feature request: If you use the sideways arrows at the bottom of the window to navigate between sub-tables it does not store the sub-table you are currently on. If you close the window and re-open it you always start on the first sub-table (or the last one you've selected from the drop-down at the top). I really like the arrows, it feels natural to use them to follow along with a dungeon. But then your position doesn't get stored and it ends up being pretty confusing.

Thank you for maintaining a very helpful addon! FOSS can be a very thankless job, so thank you for shouldering the burden for the community.
