# With Guda Bags you can replace your occupied bag with the new one

## Post 173807 by Vaitchild — 2026-04-02T22:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173807#p173807 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

No more manually emptying bags before replacing them, just drop the new bag on the slot and [Guda](https://github.com/vatichild/guda) handles the rest. A small delay while it moves your items, but it will ease your pain.
