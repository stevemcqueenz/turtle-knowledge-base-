# Mouse Highlight Circle Addon

## Post 121209 by Goamania — 2025-02-28T13:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121209#p121209 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey Turtle WoW community!

I’m excited to share a small but handy addon I’ve created: MouseHighlightCircle. If you’ve ever lost track of your mouse cursor during intense raids, PvP battles, or chaotic moments in the game, this addon is for you! It adds a pixelated white ring (with a transparent center) around your cursor, making it much easier to spot without cluttering your screen. Designed specifically for Turtle WoW’s 1.12.1 client, it keeps that classic Vanilla WoW aesthetic we all love. ![😊](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f60a.svg)

Why I Made This:

I noticed that addons like WeakAuras don’t work on Turtle WoW, and many of us struggle to keep track of the cursor in busy fights. So, I created MouseHighlightCircle to solve that problem while keeping things simple and true to the Vanilla experience. It’s been a lifesaver for me in raids, and I hope it helps you too!

Features
A pixelated white ring around your mouse cursor (retro Vanilla style).
Transparent center for a clean and unobtrusive look.
Smoothly follows your cursor in real-time.
Lightweight and optimized for Turtle WoW.
Simple slash commands:
/mhc show - Show the ring.
/mhc hide - Hide the ring.

Installation
Download the addon from the GitHub repo: <https://github.com/goamania/MouseHighlightCircle>.
Extract the MouseHighlightCircle folder into your World of Warcraft/Interface/AddOns/ directory.
Make sure the pixelring.tga texture file is included (it’s needed for the ring effect).
Launch Turtle WoW, enable the addon in the AddOns menu, and log in.
Type /mhc show in-game to see the ring in action!
Notes
The ring uses a single 64x64 pixelring.tga texture. If you want to customize the ring’s look, you can replace the texture with your own (just keep the same dimensions and transparency).
Tested on Turtle WoW’s latest client (1.12.1-based). Let me know if you run into any issues!
Feedback
I’d love to hear your thoughts! Does the ring help you in-game? Any features you’d like to see added (like a combat-only toggle or customizable sizes)? Feel free to share your feedback here or on the GitHub page.
