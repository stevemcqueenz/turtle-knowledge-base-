Hello everyone,

As a fan of Everquest I always felt like Wow lacked some immersion and sense of danger that Everquest so beautifully sets. The amount of information displayed to the player, in my opinion is the main culprit. You know how much health the mob has, where you are in the world at all times, the mob difficulty etc etc.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/9mWMgbxg/Wo-WScrn-Shot-031126-230923.jpg)
![Image](https://i.ibb.co/YTBxv4h0/Wo-WScrn-Shot-031226-232622.jpg)
Screenshot of the addon in action

With that in mind I (CHATGPT for the most part) developed a simple consider system integrated into a compass addon (mapofscars). Consider this addon a immersive hardcore experience of the game. It does a couple of things:

 - Adds a consider command "/consider" - if you target a mob it will display a message of how difficult it will be to fight that mob and it's reputation status with you. If you are not targeting any mobs/NPCS your character will instead consider its wounds and give you a prompt of its health status
 - you can make a macro with that command and bind it to the "E" key for example.

 - Hides player Frame
 - Hides minimap frame
 - Hides target frame
 - unbinds your world map key - you should use the compass for directions and quest objectives.

Known Issues:

Lack of support for debuff tracking (use the combat log you Everquest nerds)
Lack of support for combo point display (I recommend using NugComboBar for that: <https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/NugComboBar>

I hope this addon brings some Everquest neckbeards onboard and maybe revitilizes the world of Azeroth even more. Take care and see you in game :)

PS: Any feedback is greatly appreciated, this is a passion project from a guy that has no idea how to code so any input is amazing.
