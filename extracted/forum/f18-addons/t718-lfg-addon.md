## Post 4010 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-09-19T14:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4010#p4010 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the reasons some players play on the vanilla servers is they didn't like the effect the LFG tool had on the community when it was introduced. It's definitely a convenience, but that's because you no longer need to talk to people.

One of things I like a lot about Turtle WoW is the community. Being forced to /who is online when I want to run an instance and actually /whisper players to see if they're available to tank/heal/dps is part of getting to know other players. It's how I've met a lot of people and made a lot of friends here.

The LFG addon eliminates the need to talk to people and takes some of the socializing away from the game.

 **~Gifted**
