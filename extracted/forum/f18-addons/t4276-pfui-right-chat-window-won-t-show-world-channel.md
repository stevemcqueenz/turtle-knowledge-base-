Late response here:

PFui does nothing else but to create an extra channel for world/loot or whatever.

If I understand you correctly, all you have to do is create a new chat window (right click on the tab),
name it whatever you want, and then drag it out of the left box and put it onto the right chat frame/panel
that PFui offers you. Then simply tick/untick the channels that you want it to show you.

When you drag a tab out of your chat box, like combat log etc, it will create a new chat window.

The right chat panel in PFui isnt really an extra chat frame, it simply makes a new chat tab and moves
it into the panel.
