# Bagshui 🎒 Feng shui for your bags

## Post 114890 by Jx (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-30T20:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114890#p114890 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[Bagshui](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui) is a powerful single-window auto-categorizing and sorting inventory addon with customizable layout and rules, built specifically for Vanilla WoW.
Recommended if you like AdiBags, ArkInventory, Baganator, Baggins, BetterBags, EngInventory/EngBags, TBag.
And with “[OneBagshui](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/FAQ#how-do-i-switch-to-the-onebag-style-layout)”: Bagnon, Combuctor, Inventorian, LiteBag, OneBag3, SUCC-bag.

[![Image](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/images/Bagshui_Thumb.png)](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Screenshots)

Features

- Single window inventory for Bags, Bank, and Keyring.
- [Customizable layout and design](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Profiles) with automatic grouping and sorting.
- [Categorization](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Categories) via [rules](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Rules) and item lists.
- Per-inventory and account-wide [search](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Searching).
- Offline viewing of any character’s inventory and [item counts in tooltips](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Item-Information#info-tooltip).
- Identification of [profession reagents and crafted items](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Professions).
- Color tinting of unusable items.
- Badges to indicate stock changes, quality, quest items, and unusability.
- Empty slot stacking and custom graphics for profession bag slots.
- Automated bag swapping – no more manual item shuffling!
- Selling protection to safeguard valuable items.
- Clam (Open Container), Disenchant, Pick Lock, and Hearthstone buttons.
- [Colorblind mode](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki#colorblind-mode) to help identify item quality and unusability.
- Lots of other little niceties like item restacking, pfUI skinning, and more.

> **Secrett wrote: Sun Jan 05, 2025 2:30 pm**
> I'm trying to make my engineering crafts be in a seperate "Engineering" category

As noted on the [wiki](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Rules#available-rule-functions) and the in-game rule function menu **[𝗙𝒙]**, the following should work. At least in theory, assuming there isn’t a major bug I missed. Code: Select all

```
ProfessionCraft("Engineering")
```

Keep in mind the [limitations](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Professions#limitations) of this method.

Alternatively, you could try using the **PeriodicTable()** function. Check the [Item Information window](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Item-Information#item-information-window) to get a list of an item’s PT sets. Installing [AtlasLoot](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/AtlasLoot) and leveraging its data via **PeriodicTable()** may give you more accurate/up-to-date results than the built-in PT library.

I think the confusion may be coming from the fact that the built-in Categories are read-only, which is why the only option you’re seeing is to create a new custom one.

Custom Categories will have a lower sequence number by default and will be evaluated first, catching the items and preventing them from being matched by the built-in Categories. (You can even give your custom Categories the same names as the built-ins.)

Bagshui was designed this way so that built-in Categories and Sort Orders can be revised in updates without affecting any custom objects.

Also keep in mind that the Groups you seen in the inventory Structure (layout) have Categories assigned to them, not items. The wiki has a [diagram that visualizes Bagshui’s hierarchy](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Profiles#structure).

Hopefully this helps explain things!

> **RandoOnSteam wrote: Mon Mar 31, 2025 8:35 pm**
> Also is there a way to "refresh" its sync with outfitter. Sometimes items that were removed from sets stay in "My Gear".

There's no Outfitter integration in the default Structure; it's up to you to create a custom Category that uses the **Outfit()** rule function. "My Gear" has two assigned Categories, Soulbound Gear and Equipped Gear, so what I think you're probably seeing is items that you have previously equipped (which you can remove from the equipped item history using the Alt+RIght-click item menu).

If you want to have a Group that includes only items you have in an Outfitter outfit:
1. Create a new Category ("My Outfits" maybe?) with a Rule of: Code: Select all

```
Outfit()
```
2. Save the Category.
3. Go into [Edit Mode](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/Edit-Mode) and click the **[+]** where you want your new Group, then give it a name (can also be "My Outfits" if that makes sense to you).
4. Right-click the Group you just created and assign the My Outfits Category (or whatever you called it).
The same general idea applies even if you switch to the non-categorized layout, only instead of creating a new Group, you would just assign the new Category to the single **Inventory** Group and then all items that belong to any Outfitter outfit will be sorted together.

Hopefully all this is helpful and makes sense!

Aside from that, there's not currently an option to auto-organize when things change -- and that's by design. Early in development, Bagshui reorganized the window whenever anything changed, but I found that this made operations like splitting a stack of items feel somewhat disorienting. Sorry you're having difficulty! Bagshui is inspired by ArkInventory, but it's not a clone, so some of the concepts don't map 1:1. In Bagshui, Categories aren't active until they're assigned to a Group (in other words, just creating a Category isn't enough to make it actually do anything).

Maybe [the wiki](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki#customization) could be of some help (it also explains [the decision to make built-in objects read-only](https://github.com/veechs/Bagshui/wiki/FAQ#why-cant-i-edit-built-in-objects)).
