## Post 154360 by Morkahja (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-04T19:09:00Z

Hi yall!

I didnt find an addon that gives me a bit more detailed info about my resistances so i crafted one myself, very simple but it gives me a better understanding about my resistances! ANd since i think its an interesting tool i would like to share it here.

there are only a few /commands:
/buxres
Shows an overview of all Resistances and their % chance to resist non binary spells.

/buxres fire/frost/shadow etc
Shows a detailed overview about the respective resistance.

<https://github.com/Morkahja/BuxbrewResist>

Would love to hear your feedback and also if the calculations are correct!

Regards, Morkahja/Buxbrew
