Did you accidentally unbind targeting?
Luna supports Clickcasting, so the left mouse-button can do something other than target. I had a shift-modifier in some of my macros and while holding down Shift I just could not target on Luna frames. That's because Clickcasting interprets Shift+LMB as a different action than just LMB.

So head to the Clickcasting settings and look if you have LeftButton bound to target. If not, click on the "Click Me" button to let it know you want left click, then enter in the field beside it "target" (without quotation marks) and click "Add". Do that for all the clicks you want to use for targeting.
