From [https://vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com/ ... SetBinding](https://vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com/wiki/API_SetBinding)

It doesn't show up in the keybindings UI but these two script commands worked for me (copy paste and hit enter for each line seperately). Z is the key (change to your key), 1 is for account bindings (use 2 for character specific). Holding shift and this key loots bodies, yay!

/script SetBinding("Z", "TURNORACTION")
/script SaveBindings(1)
