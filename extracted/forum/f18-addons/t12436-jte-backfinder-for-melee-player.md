# JTE-BackFinder (for melee player)

## Post 82844 by Jettieyoo — 2023-12-26T22:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82844#p82844 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you are a melee player, I highly recommend installing this addon.

When you are attacking at the monster's front, there will be a voice reminder for you. The sound is like "Da Lian Le"
I have created a similar WA on WAGO, but turtle wow does not have any working WeakAuras, that is why I made this addon.

It's based on whether your melee attack (skill+swing) got parried or blocked. And it works pretty good.

The JTE addon also includes some small features that I personally use. Forgive me for not having time to separate Backfinder.

If you only want to use Backfinder without other sounds:
 1. go to the sounds folder: Interface/addons/JTE/Sounds/
 2. delete everything except dalianle.mp3

<https://github.com/Jettie/JTE>
