Good day.
If it was working for you before, then I am convinced you did not do the same thing now as you did previously:
The addon you linked is not for Vanilla, but for Classic version of the client. If you get Healbot for Vanilla, I am sure it will work for you.
The confusion comes from the fact that both addons have a version called healbot-classic, though they refer to different '-classic' things (WoW Classic from Blizzard in the case of the addon you downloaded and the other one refers to a private server that was called 'Classic' a few years before Blizzard's)
In short, try the one at <https://github.com/yogo1212/healbot-classic>
Or if you prefer legacy-wow as the source, then get the one at
<https://legacy-wow.com/vanilla-addons/healbot/>
