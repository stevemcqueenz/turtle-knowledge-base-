---
 **What is FlightTracker?**

Ever wonder how long that flight to Booty Bay actually takes, or how much gold or time you've spent on flights over your adventures? **FlightTracker** records your flight times and costs, displays them right in flightmaster tooltips, and gives you a full statistics breakdown. It also adds quality-of-life features like an in-flight timer and a route checklist for completionists. Some might remember this addon as FlightPath-Turtle in it's earliest form, but I decided to abandon that and restart fresh. This is the last of my older addons that still needed a proper forum post, only new addons here from new on!

---
 **Features**

- **Flight Time Tracking** — Records your flight times and displays them in flightmaster menu tooltips.
- **In-Flight Timer** — Shows your remaining flight time while you're in the air.
- **Auto Dismount** — Automatically dismounts you when you speak to a flightmaster.
- **Confirmation Window** — Shows a confirmation prompt before accepting a flight, so no more accidental wrong destinations.
- **Party/Raid Announcement** — Automatically shares your ETA with your group when you take a flight.
- **Flight Statistics** — Track your total flights, total gold spent, total time flown, and your longest flight ever.
- **Route Checklist** — A checklist of every possible flight route available to your faction. A completionist's dream.

 **Commands**
Code: Select all

```
/ft
/flighttracker
```

Opens the FlightTracker window.

---
 **Installation**

 **TurtleWoW Launcher:**

1. In the Addons tab of the launcher, click **Add New Addon**
2. Paste this .git link into it and install Code: Select all

```
https://github.com/tilare/FlightTracker.git
```
