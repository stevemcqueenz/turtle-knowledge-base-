# [Release] TurtleRoleplayingHelper

## Post 122081 by Goldenspatula — 2025-03-08T21:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122081#p122081 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[TRPHelper](https://github.com/vaaslite/TurtleRoleplayingHelper) is an improved version of the original Vanilla release of the RoleplayingHelper addon. Said addon has been improved to better suit TWoW by:

- Adding TWoW's custom races: High Elf and Goblin

- Almost doubling the amount of flavor text. Most class/race files were empty or had grammar mistakes- no more!

- Fixing minor coding mistakes

- Correcting spelling

- Others!
Some files are still lacking the desired amount of flavor text- it's a lot to write! If you have suggestions, reports or would like to provide your own flavor text, DM me on the_vaas (Discord) or send me an in-game message (Vaaslite). I am not an expert in LUA, so any coding help or advice would be very much welcome. I'd like to expand the addon in the future and make it much more accessible, as well as add more events.
