You need to copy the folder WTF\Account\ACCOUNTNAME\SavedVariables into your other account.

In this folder you will find all PFUI settings from your account

The SavedVariables are for every Server/Character unique in
WTF\Account\ACCOUNTNAME\Turtle WoW\CharacterName\SavedVariables

But first safe the old SavedVariables folder if something will bugging, just to be safe ![😅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f605.svg)![🙏](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f64f.svg)
