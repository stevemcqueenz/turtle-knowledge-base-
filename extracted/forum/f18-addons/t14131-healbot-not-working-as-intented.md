Hi guys, I've just started healing as a paladin, it's my first time healing in classic.

I downloaded the HealBot addon and configured it, but after joining a dungeon group I noticed that I could only heal alliance players because the horde players had pvp turned on. I am not sure if the AddOn is supposed to work like this. The version I'm using was downloaded from the forum thread with raid AddOn's.

## Post 96323 by Mcnair (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-06-04T13:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96323#p96323 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good day.
As far as I remember, there is a tick-mark among the options of this addon that says something along the line "Avoid accidental PvP flagging". If you uncheck that you'll be able to heal PvP flagged party/raid members. I would also suggest to make sure the addon allows you to heal unhurt targets, as many times you will have to pre-heal people for incoming damage.
