# FIX target sounds effects with custom unit frames

## Post 142416 by Phrxqt — 2025-07-31T22:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142416#p142416 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, just sharing something I ran into and ended up fixing.

When the default unit frames are replaced (like with custom UI addons), the usual target change sound effects often stop playing. I made a small addon to bring those sounds back:
<https://github.com/phrxqt/TargetSoundRestore>

I think most people don't miss them or even notice they're gone, but I couldn't find a fix when I looked, so figured I'd share in case anyone searches here.
