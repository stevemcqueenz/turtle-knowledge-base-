# [Release] PvPAlertTurtle - PvP Warning

## Post 154705 by Adhavar (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-10-07T11:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154705#p154705 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I’ve seen Hardcore runs end because players accidentally hit PvP-flagged NPCs and got flagged themselves. PvPAlertTurtle gives you a clear on-screen warning whenever your current target is attackable and PvP-flagged. Works for players and NPCs. (By default it triggers only on the opposing faction; there’s an optional fallback for NPCs with no faction info.) Enjoy:

<https://github.com/Kovy97/PvPAlertTurtle>
