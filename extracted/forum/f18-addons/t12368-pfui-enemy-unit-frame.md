## Post 82338 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-22T19:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82338#p82338 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You can't see any dispelable buff on your enemy because standard version 1.12 of WoW doesn't let you do it. As far as I know this was possible in Classic WoW by Blizzard but it was custom change made by them. So enemy buffs can't be seen by anyone in Turtle WoW. You can however spam Dispel button and if enemy has 0 buffs you will get info "Nothing to dispel" or something like that.
