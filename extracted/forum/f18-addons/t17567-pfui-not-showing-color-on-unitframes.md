<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120132#p120132 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

pfUI settings -> Unit Frames -> General/Player/Target -> scroll all the way down to the Overwrite Colors section -> check "Custom Health Bar Background Color"
