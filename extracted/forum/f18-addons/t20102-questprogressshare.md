# ⭐QuestProgressShare⭐

## Post 137619 by Dreambjornn — 2025-06-30T22:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137619#p137619 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

[QuestProgressShare](https://github.com/Dreambjorn/QuestProgressShare) automatically syncs quest progress with your party in real time. Designed for smoother and more coordinated group questing without the need for manual updates or chat messages.

Features
- Share quest progress

- - with party members

 - with yourself

 - with local chat

- Share all quest progress or only finished quests

- Share quest links instead of plain titles (only if pfQuest is enabled)

- Party progress tooltips - View party member quest progress by hovering over:

- - Quest log entries - Display party member progress for the same quest when hovering over quest log titles

 - Tracker entries - Show party member progress in pfQuest tracker tooltips when available

 - World objects/mobs - View party progress for quest-related entities when hovering over them in the world

Hover over quests in your quest log, the pfQuest tracker or world objects/mobs to see your party members' progress in the tooltip.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/ULAmX3f.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/xEutU0X.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/krxSmwz.png)

If you encounter any issues or errors, please let me know.

Note: pfQuest and pfUI aren't required for QuestProgressShare to work, but having them installed enhances the experience.

Credits: This addon is a fork of the original created by @graved.
