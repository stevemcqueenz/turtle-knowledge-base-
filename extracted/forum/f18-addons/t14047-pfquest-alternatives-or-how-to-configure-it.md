## Post 136864 by MadafakinRio — 2025-06-24T18:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136864#p136864 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Type /db config and play around with it, see what you like.
This is how I set mine up. You have visible available quests and dots for where you get the quest items, but no routes (I find it useless). I also don't like the crazy arrow. Btw you can type "/db query" and the addon will query the server for all the quests you've already completed and mark them as such, so you don't have to shift-click them manually. However if you get a crash or something like that it won't save your settings and marked quests, so you'll have to repeat it.

I played WoW without addons from 2006 until probably 2010. I learned the game from zero this way.

English is not native to me and my first playthrough attempts were more doing and less thinking/reading. I ran into a lot of problems such as being level 13 in The Barrens with skills trained only up to level 5, because I didn't know I had to go to Orgrimmar and I thought the game was too hard and boring.

Then I decided to really learn the game a few months later and to read everything and pay attention to everything. And with my inferior (at the time) English, I still managed to get things to work out with a Night Elf Hunter, even complete the Hunter pet quest properly and move on to explore the world.

I even played Turtle WoW back when there was no addon for the Turtle-specific quests and I was still able to complete them.

I think you need to play without addons at first to learn the game the way it was meant to be played. It's absolutely doable and you need just a bit of effort, patience and persistence.

In my opinion, addons are for people who have already played the game a lot and don't want to waste time thinking where to go and what to do. But in order to appreciate the power of addons, you first need to play it at least once from 1 to 60 without them.

As you say - addons spoil the experience, just go without them, you can figure things out easily. Quests are made with good descriptions so if you read them carefully, you will know where to go and what to do.
