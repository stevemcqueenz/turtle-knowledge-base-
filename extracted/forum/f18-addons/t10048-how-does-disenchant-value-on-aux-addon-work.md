It works by item lvl.

For example green armor item lvl 55 have a the same diseucbst disreobituono.
Smerhing like
 50% to get 1-5 dust
 1% to get a large shard
 49% to get 1-2 large essense

Then it takes the market values of those dust essense and shards multiplied with the probability to get the expected disenchant market value.

IF aux is showing you - then it means it is missing the, data for the dust and essence market values and can't calculate a value without them.

Go to the ah and scan
"dust"
"shard"
"essence"

Might take 10 minutes but then you should have seen all values once
