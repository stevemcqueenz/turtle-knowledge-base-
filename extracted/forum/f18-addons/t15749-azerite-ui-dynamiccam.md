Addons made for classic and future expansions don't work with turtle's 1.12 client. I doubt someone backported these in particular, so the best you can do is find alternatives like Vanilla Storyline (<https://github.com/tubtubs/VanillaStoryline>) and PfUI (<https://shagu.org/pfUI/>)

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/eJdhBwt.png)

You can see all turtle-suported addons on the wiki: <https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Addons>

Rumors are that after the UE5 launcher is released they would try and get classic addons working, but that's a long way away still.
