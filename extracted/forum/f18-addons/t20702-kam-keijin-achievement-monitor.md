## Post 143388 by Keijin — 2025-08-06T14:28:00Z

 **![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg) [Addon] Keijin Achievement Monitor (KAM)**
---
Keijin Achievement Monitor (KAM) is a fully standalone achievement addon for Turtle WoW.
It tracks progress, rewards exploration, honors combat achievements, and introduces a fresh goal system tailored to the Turtle experience.

Ready for both existing content and upcoming Turtle expansions (1.18+).

 **![⭐](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2b50.svg) Key Features**
---
Supported Achievement Types:

 **![🗡️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f5e1.svg) Kill Achievements**
 *Defeat enemies, named targets, groups or reach kill milestones (e.g. 10 / 50 / 100 kills)*

 **![🧭](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9ed.svg) Exploration Achievements**
Discover every zone in Azeroth – including Turtle-specific areas like Tel'Abim or Misty Isles
Support for Patch 1.18 already included

 **![🧍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9cd.svg) Character Progression**
Track leveling milestones and global account-wide development

 **![🔧](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f527.svg) Skill & Profession Achievements**
 *Reach thresholds in professions and weapon skills (e.g. 150 in Alchemy)*

 **![🤝](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f91d.svg) Reputation Achievements**
 *Gain reputation in 1000+ point steps across neutral, Horde, and Alliance factions*

 **![🌀](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f300.svg) Meta Achievements**
 *Unlock broader goals like "Explore all of Kalimdor" or "Reach exalted with all factions"*

 **![🔜](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f51c.svg) Planned Features:**
- Hardcore Achievements
 *Custom rewards for Hardcore players (e.g. reach level 30 with no deaths)*

- Quest Achievements
 *We're looking for iconic or memorable quests to feature – feel free to suggest!*

- Mini window with favorites
 *Track your favorite achievements live in a small optional side window*

 **![🖥️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f5a5.svg) UI & Technical Info**
---
Clean, lightweight, custom UI with minimal memory impact

Fully filterable view (All / Open / Completed / Searchable)

Segment system for better performance and clarity

Persistent UI position saved between sessions

Classic-style layout – no framesets, no templates, no backdrop bugs

 **![📦](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4e6.svg) Installation**
---
Download the latest version here:
![🔗](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f517.svg) <https://github.com/KeijinDE/KeijinAchievementMonitor>

Unzip the folder and place it in:
Interface\AddOns\KeijinAchievementMonitor

Restart your game or reload UI with /reload
