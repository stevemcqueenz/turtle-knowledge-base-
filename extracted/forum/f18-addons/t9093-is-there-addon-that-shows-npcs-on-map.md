pfQuest

Use /db *npc name*, click on the Mob & Objects tab at the bottom, then click on the NPC that you want to see on your map

Works for objects too like say, Black Lotus

Or you could /db herbs to show all herbs or /db mines 150 225 to show all only mining nodes between lvl 150 and 225

Or you could /db runecloth then click on the sword icon next to runecloth in the items tab and it will show you everything that can drop runecloth

Or /db simple wood then click on the bag icon to show every vendor that stocks simple wood
