# Tmog - The ultimate dressing room for Turtle WoW

## Post 117800 by Spitfire33 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-24T17:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117800#p117800 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Have you ever stared a a piece of gear, trying to rememebr if you have it in your collection or not? Do you wish to be able to see all items in the game and plan your future look? Tmog is what you need! With this addon item tooltips will show you if it's in your collection or not, you will be able to try on all items in game and make outfits!
Download it from GitHub: <https://github.com/Otari98/Tmog>

 **Important!**
If this is first time using addon you will need to cache your existing transmog collection, for that go to fashionista in Stormwind or Orgrimmar, click on every item slot, you need to do this for all weapon types that you have (swords/axes/maces, polearms, staves, daggers, shields, off-hand holdables, wands, crossbows, bows, guns)
For example if you have transmogs for daggers and polearms, you need to equip any dagger, go to transmog npc and click on dagger, then equip any polearm, talk to npc again, click on polearm. Repeat for other weapon types that you have. For armor slots clicking once should be enough.
