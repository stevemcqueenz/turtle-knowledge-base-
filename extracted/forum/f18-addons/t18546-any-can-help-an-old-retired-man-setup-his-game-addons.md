## Post 129439 by Zmarotrix — 2025-05-02T22:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129439#p129439 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Here you go. Just extract it into the Turtle WoW install directory (where the WoW.exe and turtle-wow.exe are).

<https://filebin.net/sr47m7l5yxodsf73>
(Originally a google drive link, but it detected SuperWoW as a virus so I had to update it. I requested review.)
This actually reminds me that you will likely need to setup your Turtle WoW directory as an exception in your windows defender.

This has:

Damage Meter - ShaguDPS
Quest Helper - pfQuest
Dungeon Helper - BigWigs
Loot Helper - I'm not sure what you meant by this. I installed a Grey Item seller.
Bag Addon - Bagnon

I've also included a few QoL mods that you might not consider for Vanilla WoW. TurtleMail to make mail act more like modern WoW, ShaguTweaks to modernize the utility of a lot of the UI and such. I've also included the most up-to-date versions of SuperWoW and Nampower as well as their config addons. I've uncluded the UnitXP config addon as well.

Ensure the launcher is closed when you extract this, then ensure the following are enabled in the launchers mod tab:

SuperWoWhook
UnitXP
VanillaFixes
nampower
(Whatever else you want)
