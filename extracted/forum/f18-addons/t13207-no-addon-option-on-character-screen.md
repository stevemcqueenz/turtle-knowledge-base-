Hello, I have just started with this server and unfortunately there doesn't seem to be any visible addon button at the character selection screen found at the bottom left corner. I have not incorporated any addons just yet (the usual unzip to addon folder inside the interface...etc etc), so I am seeking some help please. Thank you in advance!

## Post 88519 by Mellodia — 2024-02-23T04:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88519#p88519 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My apologies, I have resolved it.

I had the github addons, but I also noticed that the interface/addons folder is extremely picky, it can't have duplicate folder names for example having a folder such as altoholic-master (the unzipped version) and then within that folder have another folder of the same. On top of that, the unziped folders require to be renamed or delete "-main" or "-master": for example altoholic-master will require to be simply altoholic (no dash or master/main)

Hope this helps someone out there, once I did this, the addons button appeared without any issues at the bottom left of the character selection screen. Just to be safe, I recommend doing things outside the game without it running.
