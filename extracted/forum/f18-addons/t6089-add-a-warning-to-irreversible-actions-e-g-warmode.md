# Add a warning to irreversible actions (e.g. Warmode)

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38241#p38241 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good design would warrant a warning every time an irreversible action is about to be made. I turned on warmode forever by mistake. I know there's a warning text somewhere along the road, but I had forgot about that some weeks later. So please, when you turn warmode on irreversibly, have a warning window saying the action is irreversible. This will avoid a lot of unnecessary grief for unmindful players.
