**MessageBox**
| Modern Messenger-style whisper addon for TurtleWoW |
[![Image](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-MessageBox-blue?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/tilare/MessageBox)

---
 **What is MessageBox?**

Tired of whispers getting lost in your chat scroll? **MessageBox** is a modern, messenger-style addon that replaces TurtleWoW's default whisper experience with a clean, dedicated window for all your conversations, complete with full history, contact management, notifications, and customization. This is another one of my addons that has been around for a while, but I wanted to finally make a proper forum post for it.

---
 **Features**

- **Whisper History** — Every conversation is stored and displayed, so you never lose track of what was said.
- **Popup Notifications** — Get an alert when a new whisper arrives. The minimap button also displays a red badge with your unread message count.
- **Contact List** — All your contacts in one place, with a online status, search bar and per-contact unread counts.
- **Send Direct Whispers** — Message players straight from the addon window, with optional cascading popout windows — similar to WIM.
- **Intercept Whispers** — MessageBox intercepts whisper commands from the UI or chat, opening directly to the relevant conversation.
- **Chat Search** — Search through your full chat history.
- **Modern & Classic Themes** — Toggle the Classic theme for a more Blizzard-style look and feel.
- **Background Who Queue** — Optionally runs a background /who lookup on a 30-second cooldown to fill in info for contacts not on your friends list.
- **Right-Click Context Menu** — Right-click any contact to Invite, Target, Add Friend, Ignore, Delete, Pin, or Pop Out their conversation.
- **Color Picker** — Customize the UI colors to match your setup.
- **Linking Support** — Item links and web links both work inside the addon window.
- **Pin System** — Pin a conversation to keep it at the top of your list and protect it from being deleted.
- **Conversation Management** — Delete individual chats or wipe your entire history in one click.

 **Commands**
Code: Select all

```
/messagebox
/mbox
/mb
```

Opens the MessageBox window.

---
 **Optional Dependency**

 **Nampower** is optional but required if you want crash save backup functionality.

---
 **Installation**

 **TurtleWoW Launcher:**

1. In the Addons tab of the launcher, click **Add New Addon**
2. Paste this .git link into it and install Code: Select all

```
https://github.com/tilare/MessageBox.git
```

---
 **Recommended Addon**

MessageBox pairs perfectly with **[pfUI-MoreDatatexts](https://github.com/Arthur-Helias/pfUI-MoreDataTexts)** by Walter Bennet — an external pfUI module that adds extra datatexts, and one specifically for MessageBox.
