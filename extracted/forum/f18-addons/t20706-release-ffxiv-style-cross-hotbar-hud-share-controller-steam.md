 **Install**

- Download and install two addons: [Discord Action Bars](https://github.com/UndercityAddons-Vanilla/DiscordActionBars) and [Luna Unit Frames](https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/LunaUnitFrames)

- Download my profile [FFXIV-Profile](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1kEfL64t72IgFU7_OwaunPYb8uKTJ7G3v/view?usp=sharing)

- Unzip and open folder "*WTF \ Account*", rename folder *#YourAccountName* to you account name.

- Copy *WTF and Interface folder* to your game path.

- Open game,
type */luna menu* to open addon Luna Unit Frames , change profile to FFXIV.
type */DiscordActionBars* to open addon Discord Action Bars , change profile to FFXIV.

- Done! Now Remap your controller:
LT:Ctrl
RT: Alt
LT+RT: Shift
