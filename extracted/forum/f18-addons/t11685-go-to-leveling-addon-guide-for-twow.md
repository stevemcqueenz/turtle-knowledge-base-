Good day.
While sadly I can not help you in your search for such an addon, but maybe I can offer an alternative.
I would recommend to get lost questing. Pick up a quest that just looks nice. Follow to other places. Go to places to you have not been to yet and check if you find anything interesting. That was the original beauty of the game when the beta rolled out and still is the best part now that we can re-live a similar experience on Turtle. While many people think that there is a goal to reach 60 and then do stuff, but I think the alternative is so much more rewarding. The game is the journey from 1 to 60 in a world that is full of wonder and secrets for you to stumble upon. As someone wrote quite some time ago, "The struggle in itself must be the goal."
