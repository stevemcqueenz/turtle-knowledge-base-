Ah I have fixed the issue myself. Posting my solution for anyone who stumbles across this in the future.

I scrapped my old wow client, and unzipped a fresh one. I went through the same install process laid out on the turtle wow install page video with all the addons and tweaks. Except this time I downloaded and installed " vanillafixes-1.3.zip " and not " vanillafixes-1.3-dxvk.zip " nor " vanillafixes-1.3-dxvk-legacy.zip ". I then copied my old interface folder from the previous install over to preserve my addons.  This time the bug is gone!
