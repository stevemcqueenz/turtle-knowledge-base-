# [Release] Trailblazer

## Post 138357 by Fluchmeister6 — 2025-07-07T13:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138357#p138357 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone,

I'm excited to introduce my first addon for Turtle WoW:
 **Trailblazer** – A simple way to manage and summon your favorite mounts!

What does **Trailblazer** do?
 **Trailblazer** allows you to easily manage your mounts and summon them randomly – either from all available mounts or just your personal favorites. It's designed to be lightweight, character-specific, and intuitive.

 **Features:**
![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Automatic Mount Detection

All learned mounts are automatically detected and listed

No setup required – just choose your favorites and ride!

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Graphical Favorites Interface

Use **/trailblazer fav** to open favorites-menu

Select your favorite mounts using the checkboxes

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Random Mount Summoning

Use /trailblazer to summon a random favorite mount

If no favorites are selected, it will summon from all available mounts

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Ahn'Qiraj Smart Filtering

When you're inside Ahn'Qiraj (AQ40), only AQ-specific mounts will be considered

If you've marked AQ mounts as favorites, it will prioritize those

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Character-Specific Settings

Favorites are stored per character, so each toon can have their own list

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Simple Macro Support

Create a macro with /trailblazer and drag it to your actionbar

You can replace your regular mount button with this macro for quick and dynamic summoning

 **How to use:**

Type **/trailblazer fav** to open the favorites window

Select your favorite mounts using the checkboxes

Close the window

Create a macro with **/trailblazer** and place it on your action bar

Click the macro button anytime to summon a random mount (based on your selection and current zone)

 **Trailblazer** is a great tool for collectors and adventurers alike who want a smarter, more immersive mount experience.

Let me know what you think – feedback and ideas are always welcome!

Thanks for checking it out and happy riding ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)
