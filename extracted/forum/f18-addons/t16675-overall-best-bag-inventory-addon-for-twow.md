## Post 119524 by Tslnox — 2025-02-10T14:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119524#p119524 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I presume you found what you were searching for, but if not (or if anyone comes here searching for the same), go for BagshUI. That thing is... incredible. Can work either as AdiBags/EngBags (virtual bags per item categories) but better, or like Bagnon (one window with all slots together). It has beautiful design, you can do custom categories, custom sorting rules, it has account-wide directory of all owned items, it can remotely show snapshot of your bank and inventories and banks of all your other chars on the account.
