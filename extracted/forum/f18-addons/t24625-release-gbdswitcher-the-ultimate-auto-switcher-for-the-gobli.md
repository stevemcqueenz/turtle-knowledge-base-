# 🚀 [Release] GBDSwitcher - The Ultimate Auto-Switcher for the Goblin Brainwashing Device (Zero Dependencies!)

## Post 173336 by Holylance — 2026-03-31T07:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173336#p173336 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Tired of manually swapping your gear and action bars every time you use the Goblin Brainwashing Device (GBD)? Pushing multiple macro buttons or relying on heavy, resource-intensive addons like ItemRack or ActionBarProfiles just to switch your specs?

Meet GBDSwitcher (v0.1) – a lightweight, zero-dependency, standalone addon designed specifically for Turtle WoW (1.12) to automate your multi-spec life!

![✨](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2728.svg) Core Features
![🪶](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1fab6.svg) Zero Dependencies: Completely independent. It works out of the box without needing any other heavy profile management addons.
![🤖](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f916.svg) True Auto-Switching (Slots 1-4): Automatically loads your saved Gear and Action Bars the exact moment you select Spec 1-4 from the Goblin Brainwashing Device dialog. Talk to the goblin, click your spec, and watch your UI transform automatically!
![🎯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3af.svg) 6 Custom Profiles (Slots 5-10): Need a dedicated gold farming set? A PvP set? A roleplay outfit? We've got 6 extra, renameable slots that allow you to manually save and load specialized gear and bar loadouts anytime.
![⏱️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/23f1.svg) Smart Delay Locking: Action bars inherently have a server delay when swapping. GBDSwitcher lets you configure a custom delay (default 2s) to accurately snap your action bars into place right after the gear swap finishes.
![🛠️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6e0.svg) How It Works (It's super simple!)
Prepare: Equip the gear and arrange the action bars exactly how you want them for "Spec 1".
Save: Type /gbds in-game to open the interface, then click Save G (Gear) and Save B (Bars) on the Spec 1 row.
Repeat: Do the same for your other specs.
Automate: The next time you talk to the Goblin Brainwashing Device and click "Activate 1st Spec", your gear and bars will swap automatically without any extra clicks!
![💻](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4bb.svg) Slash Commands
/gbds - Toggle the config window
/gbds save <1-10> - Save current gear and bars into the specified slot
/gbds load <1-10> - Manually load gear and bars from the specified slot
/gbds list - Print your currently saved spec data to the chat
/gbds toggle - Turn GBD auto-switching ON/OFF
/gbds delay <sec> - Adjust the action bar load delay
![📥](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4e5.svg) Download & Installation: (<https://github.com/oplancelot/GBDSwitcher>)

Download the zip file.
Extract the GBDSwitcher folder into your Interface/AddOns/ directory.
Make sure to remove any -master or -main suffixes from the folder name!
Drop a comment below if you have any feedback, bug reports, or feature requests. Enjoy your seamless spec switching! ![🐢](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f422.svg)![⚙️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2699.svg)
