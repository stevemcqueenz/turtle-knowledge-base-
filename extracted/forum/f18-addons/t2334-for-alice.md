This is a list of addons and instructions on how to install them. It's meant for a friend who has trouble with modern things.

Links
- **pfUI: <https://github.com/shagu/pfUI>**

- **pfQuest: <https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest>**

- **pfElite Overlay(because i think you'll miss the dragons): <http://shagu.org/pfUI-eliteoverlay/> **download the 1.12****

- **Aux: <https://github.com/shirsig/aux-addon-vanilla>**

- **Imrpoved Mail: <https://github.com/shirsig/Mail>**

- **Atlas: <https://github.com/CosminPOP/Atlas>**

- **Atlasloot: <https://github.com/CosminPOP/AtlasLoot>**

 **Instructions on how to download and install stuff**
- **Create a folder on your desktop, name it ''Addons''**

- **To downlaod things on Github, click on the green''Code'' button and select download ZIP. Put everything you download on the ''Addons'' folder.**

- **Extract everything you've downloaded so far.**

- **Rename every folder who has the suffix ''-master''. For exemple, ''pfUI-eliteoverlay-master” would be ''pfUI-eliteoverlay''**

- **Copy all the addons files without the ''-master''**

- **Open your Turtle WoW folder and go  to  interface>addons**

- **Paste all your addons there!**

 **Setting stuff up**
- **When you first log into your account, Click on AddOns, on the bottom left corner of your screen**

- **Click on ''Script Memory (MB)'' and set it up to 999.**

- **Log into your character and set your pfUI to your liking!**

That being said,  pfUI can be really overwhelming for someone who's just starting. Because of that, I'll leave you a code with my settings for you to import.
I have a large monitor, if you're playing on a laptop stuff might be to big or feel out of place. To fix that you'll need to press ESC go to pfUI and learn how to change things. I'll help you out with that when you log onn.

How to import my pfUI settings
- **In game, type /import**

- **A window will appear. There, you paste all the text that I'll put on the end of this post.**

- **Click ''decode'' then ''import''**
