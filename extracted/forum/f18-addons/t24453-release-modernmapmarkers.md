 **What is ModernMapMarkers?**

Ever opened your world map and wondered exactly where that dungeon entrance was hiding, or which road leads to a world boss? **ModernMapMarkers** (MMM) brings modern convenience to TurtleWoW by placing clear, filterable markers directly on your World Map for every dungeon, raid, world boss, and transport route in the game — including TurtleWoW's custom content. This addon has already existed for a while, but I finally got around to making a forum post!

No more searching Wowhead, no more asking in trade chat. Just open your map and go.

---
 **Features**

- **Dungeon & Raid Markers** — Every instance entrance is marked on the world map, including level range info so you know at a glance if it's right for you.
- **World Boss Markers** — Never lose track of a world boss spawn location again. All world bosses across both continents are pinned on the map.
- **Transport Markers** — Boats, Zeppelins, and even the Deeprun Tram are all marked. Perfect for new players still learning the routes.
- **Find Marker** — Use the dropdown to instantly locate any specific dungeon, raid, or world boss on the map. No more hunting around.
- **Filter Markers** — Toggle marker categories on or off from the in-map dropdown UI. Use **/mmm** to show or hide the dropdown menus entirely.
- **pfUI Integration** — MMM automatically detects pfUI and skins the dropdowns to match. It also detects pfQuest and repositions the UI to avoid conflicts.
- **Localization Support** — Supports **esES**, **deDE**, **ptBR**, and **zhCN** clients in addition to English.

 **Commands**
Code: Select all

```
/mmm
```

Toggles the visibility of the filter dropdown menus on the World Map.

---
 **Installation**

 **ModernMapMarkers can be installed directly from the TurtleWoW launcher.**

---
 **Recommended Addon**

MMM pairs perfectly with **[InstanceJournal](https://github.com/Arthur-Helias/InstanceJournal)** by Walter Bennet — an in-game instance journal that complements the map markers with detailed information. Highly recommended for the best experience.
