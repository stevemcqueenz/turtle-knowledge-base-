Hi there!

pfUI is an amazing interface addon with tons os customizations...
Took me several hours playing with settings to start having a good understanding about it.

Let me see if I can help you...

Open pfUI Config, then select UNIT FRAMES > PLAYER
Now scroll down the  right side until u see  HEALTHBAR and POWERBAR .

Healthbar, by default, should be displaying your HP right?
So in the powerbar you can set it to display, in your case, your rage.
So click on right text and select mana-max. Since your class is a warrior and you dont have mana, it will display your rage (in this case in the very right corner).
