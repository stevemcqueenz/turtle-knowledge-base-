# [Release] Friend-O-Tron

## Post 143089 by Refaim — 2025-08-04T13:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143089#p143089 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**[Friend-O-Tron](https://github.com/refaim/Friend-O-Tron)**

A World of Warcraft addon for Vanilla WoW (1.12.1) and Turtle WoW that automatically synchronizes friends lists across all your characters on all your accounts on the same realm.

 *This cutting-edge friendship automation device has been meticulously engineered by Gnomeregan's finest tinkerers specifically for discerning adventurers who demand maximum social efficiency with minimum manual intervention. Each unit comes pre-calibrated with advanced mechanical enthusiasm algorithms!*

 **Features**

 - **Gnomish Over-Engineering Marvel**: A delightfully unstable friendship protocol powered by authentic gnomish over-engineering with entertaining mechanical personality quirks
 - **Automatic Friend Synchronization**: Keeps friends lists synchronized across all characters on the same realm with zero manual intervention
 - **Cross-Account Support**: Share friends lists between multiple WoW accounts using SuperWoW integration (if available)
 - **Cross-Faction Compatibility**: Works seamlessly with friends from different factions on both PvE and PvP realms, even when your account has characters from opposing factions
 - **Multi-Language Support**: Available in 9 languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional)

 **Download**

<https://github.com/refaim/Friend-O-Tron>

 **How It Works**

 **First-Time Setup:**
 1. Enable the addon on all characters you want to synchronize
 2. Log in with any character - Friend-O-Tron will catalog your existing friends with mechanical precision
 3. Log in with other characters - they will automatically receive the shared friends list and contribute their own friends to the collective database
 4. Any future friend additions/removals on any character will sync to all others upon entering the game world

 **Cross-Account Synchronization (Advanced Engineering):**
 1. Install [SuperWoW](https://github.com/balakethelock/SuperWoW) for maximum friendship automation
 2. Friend-O-Tron will automatically detect SuperWoW
 3. Friend lists will be shared between all your characters on all your accounts

 **Slash Commands**

 - **/fot help** - Display help information and operational status
 - **/fot quiet** - Enable stealth mode (minimal notifications)
 - **/fot noisy** - Enable maximum notifications (default robotic enthusiasm)

 **Known Limitations**

 - Synchronization only works within the same realm (as per WoW's friendship limitations)
 - Friend list capacity is limited by WoW's built-in 50-friend limit

Please submit suggestions and bug reports to the GitHub issue tracker: <https://github.com/refaim/Friend-O-Tron/issues>

 *Warning: This construct may occasionally display excessive mechanical cheerfulness when successfully synchronizing friendship data. This is normal gnomish behavior and should not cause alarm.*
