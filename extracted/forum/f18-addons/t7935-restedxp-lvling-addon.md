Weeell… if you're talking about [this thing](https://github.com/RestedXP/RXPGuides), then your main problem is that the AddOn is for *Classic* and not *Vanilla*.

Basically, going forward from *The Burning Crusade* (the original release) onward, Blizzard added a bunch of stuff that let AddOns do a much wider variety of things.  None of this is available on *Vanilla* 1.12.1 – which is what *Turtle WoW* is based on.

If you're looking for something to help you find and complete quests, try *Questie* or *[pfQuest](https://shagu.org/)*.  They're both incomplete and a little broken in some ways, but they do the job.
