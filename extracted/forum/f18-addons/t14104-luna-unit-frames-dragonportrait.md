# Luna Unit Frames Dragonportrait

## Post 96108 by Ace1226 — 2024-05-31T20:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96108#p96108 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Some might find it goofy, but I really like having a dragon frame on your player unit frame. Since Luna Unit Frames don't support that, I just extended the addon to allow exactly that (No base functionality is edited or overwritten)

So this edited version of Luna Unit Frames for WoW 1.12 allows you to add the Rare or Elite Dragonportrait indicator to your player for the player and target unitframe. Choose the Silver version for the rare and the Gold version for the elite background when downloading.

Gold Version:
![Image](https://i.ibb.co/phwhX1h/luf-gold.jpg)

Silver Version:
![Image](https://i.ibb.co/hMSY5V7/luf-silver.jpg)

To activate the dragonportraits go to the Luna Unit Frames options in the game. In the Player and Target section you will find a new checkbox for the Dragonportrait. Activate it and move and resize it as you prefer.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/dW8Q2fF/luf-options.jpg)

Download the version you prefer here:
<https://github.com/stan1226/luf_dragonport/releases>

To install just put the LunaUnitFrames folder into your Interface/Addons folder. If you have LUF installed you can overwrite it without problems.

Attention: When going back to the unedited addon, you might have to edit your *WTF\Account\<Account>\SavedVariables\LunaUnitFrames.lua* file to remove the saved ["dragonport"] values.

I put v1.1 to github. Just place the LunaUnitFrames folder into your Interface/AddOn folder and replace the older version.
