# Artisan - Compact window for your professions

## Post 117670 by Spitfire33 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-23T13:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117670#p117670 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey, here is my attempt to improve professions UI.
You can download it from GitHub: <https://github.com/Otari98/Artisan>

It has following features:
- Search

- Custom categories

- Quick access to all your professions via tabs

- Keybinds

- Shift-click to post required reagents in chat
Slash commands:
- /artisan - shows version and available commands

- /artisan auto - toggles auto accepting enchant replacement dialogs

- /artisan icons - toggles mini icons next to craft names
