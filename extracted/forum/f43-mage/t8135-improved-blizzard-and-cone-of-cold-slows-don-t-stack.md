**'Improved Blizzard' and 'Cone of Cold' Slows Don't Stack**
There's a problem with the spell "Cone of Cold". Its slow effect doesn't occur if the mob is under the effect of the slow of "Improved Blizzard". This causes Mages to get chased and hit by mobs after the effect of Improved Blizzard dissipates from the latter. This needs to stack for us Mages to keep on Kiting mobs, and avoid dying while doing AoE farming.

If the slow effect of Improved Blizzard cancels out Cone of Cold's slow effect due to the latter being greater by 15%, then at least make them stack, but this time the effect of Cone of Cold doesn't affect the mobs until the effect of Improved Blizzard dissipates from the mobs. I believe that's how it works in Classic Retail and Classic Expansions. This makes it easier for us Mages to Kite mobs while AoE farming a lot of mobs.

Improved Blizzard - 75% Slow Effect
Cone of Cold - 60% Slow effect

The Data above includes the maxed talent point of (Permafrost 3/3) and (Improved Blizzard 3/3)
