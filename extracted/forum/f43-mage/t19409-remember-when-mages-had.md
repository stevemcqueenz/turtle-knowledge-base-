Its no different than actual vanilla.
Kick a mage's frostbolt? They do scorch, fireblast (or blastwave if elementalist spec), poly, missiles, explosion
Kick a mage's fireball or scorch? They do cone of cold, frost nova, poly, arcane missiles, explosion.
Kick a mage's missiles? Lel block.

Them having access to throw out a different dmging spell isn't anything new, its why blanket silence exists. Against a mage the rule of thumb was silencing arcane as top priority if blink isnt on CD and he is safe to poly properly, otherwise silence his mainspec school. Silencing elementalist is trickier, but seeing as how there are no mages playing that particular hybrid spec on this server, its a nonissue.

Whats the difference here? That mages just do more dmg? Everyone else does.

Wheres the difference with interrupting a balance druid? Interrupt starfire and he does wrath. Interrupt wrath and he turborams his moonfire key.

Wheres the difference with interrupting a warlock? Interrupt his fear and he does immolate or searing pain. Interrupt searing pain and he does fear, coil, sbolt or w/e.
