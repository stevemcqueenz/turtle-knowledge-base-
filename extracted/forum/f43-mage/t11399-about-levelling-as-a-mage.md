## Post 76091 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-11-06T02:08:00Z

~ Blood Mage ~
My hc fire aoe build:
<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/F25C8-5U2JF3JV>
Focus only on Intellect, always use Mana Shield, aggro multiple mobs, then:
Flamestrike (lower rank)
Flamestrike (max rank)
Frost Nova
Blast Wave
 -repeat-
Flamestrike dot stacks only from different ranks. The aoe will trigger clearcasting, making the next spell free, but if the aoe spell crits at least on one mob (and it will), the mage gains 30% of its cost as mana (which is the mages health with mana shield), effectively making spells generate mana instead of costing. Between impact/blink and frost nova they are kept at bay, they go down before mana shield does, however feel safe to tank them with always more than 25% mana left. The gameplay feels like a Fire Blood Elf with spell vampirism or like a fiery battle-mage. On single target open with Pyroblast, then Scorch/Fire Blast (which should also be used to finish off mobs after most die in aoe). It can be risky, but also rewarding. **I don't recommend it, but for me it worked.**

An alternative route that uses rank 1 Blizzard/Nova to trigger Freeze and 40% crit chance to be used on fire aoe spells, gaining even more mana (life while mana shield is on) from master of elements + clearcasting, it sacrifices improved mana shield but has better kiting capacity:
<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/F05-5U2JC31-CIKI70O>
