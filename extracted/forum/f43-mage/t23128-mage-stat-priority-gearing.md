Hi Perkamow

As an ArcaneMage I use this createria:

Hit 16% (talent10%+6% from gear.  This is my 1st prio.

Then I use the following formula to see if a gearpiece is worh uppgrading:

 *20SP = 14 DPS
 *1 Haste = 9,1 DPS
 *1 Crit = 9,1 DPS

I never go over 16% hit total. But 1 Hit is = 9,1 DPS

Hope this helps.
