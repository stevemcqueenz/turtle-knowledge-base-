Arcane has huge dmg spikes, and I understand that this is a problem in PvP, but I want to add one thing to consider:
This is no simple crit, but a crit that duplicated two times (Resonance Cascade). Chance of duplicating is 20% with 5 skill points invested at the bottom of arcane tree.
