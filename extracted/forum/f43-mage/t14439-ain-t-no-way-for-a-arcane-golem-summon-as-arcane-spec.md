Here's how you do it as an Arcane Mage:

Start with a Frostbolt for the slow, then Arcane Missiles.

They will reach you, you Cone of Cold. The Cone of Cold slow is very very very very strong, usually if I'm just doing it to slow someone I will use Rank 1 Cone of Cold to conserve mana.

Run away a little bit, Arcane Missiles again. By the time they reach you, you should be able to Cone of Cold again and repeat the process.

Playing Arcane Mage isn't about spamming Magic Missiles the entire time, it's about using your full class toolbox to kill enemies and have Arcane Missiles do the bulk of your damage to trigger Clearcasting repeatedly.

Good luck!

> **Atreidon wrote: Mon Jul 01, 2024 5:45 am**
> Nobody plays pure arcane mage

Oh, but I do. In fact, I have never used a single frost ability, and with fire abilities Pyroblast was the only exception I allowed myself once I got full tier 2.

To me, arcane is in the perfect spot right now. Powerful damage, especially on multi-target fights, and exceptional mana support. While the rotation may not require a high APM to perform, and while there may not be a huge amount of dynamism in spellcasting by itself, there is still a lot of room for skill expression through the use of your other abilities and consumables, positioning, managing the mana of your entire party and following your regen ticks very closely to maximize time spent outside the 5-second-rule of mana regeneration.

While arcane may not be the top single target damage dealer in the game, it is very easy to maintain a position near the top of the charts over the entire raid. Clearing trash faster translates to clearing raids faster. Don't sleep on arcane, thinking the talents it offers are best used to support frost or fire builds. You will miss out on an immensely destructive force that can only be tamed by experience.
