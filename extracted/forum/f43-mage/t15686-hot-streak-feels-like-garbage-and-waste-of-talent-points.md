# Hot streak feels like... garbage and waste of talent points

## Post 107074 by Alsapko — 2024-11-08T10:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107074#p107074 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So the idea of making pyroblast an option is actually a good one. But the realisation... In Aq40/Naxx gear you have about a 30% crit rate with all buffs and potions. Ill make it 33% for the sake of easier maths. This is like 1 stack every 9 seconds. So to fire a 1.5 second pyroblast (which is not significantly better than a fireball), you need 1 minute and 21 seconds of fireball casting.
The stated goal of the talent was to make firing less of a 1-button pony. Is pressing another ability every 1.5 minutes (you use Scorch more lol) a solution? I guess not. For example, in Patchwork Fight I used it 2 times. That is clearly underwhelming.
You can increase the effect of a stack 3 times and limit stacks to 3 to make the mechanics almost the same and it will at least feel viable and wont affect dps any significant amount (a pyroblast is about 20% more dmg than a fireball and it will still take about 30 sec to recharge the fast cast) and include scorches in it. It will still be worse than some 2% fire dmg talents, but it will at least work.
