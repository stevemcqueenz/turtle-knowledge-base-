Tides of war and heroes of the storm are not Vanilla canon, they were released after Mists of Pandaria I think.
The turtlewow canon is warcraft 3, and in warcraft 3 Jaina is an Archmage unit [https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Jaina_(Warcraft_III)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Jaina_%28Warcraft_III%29)

With Arcane and Frost abilities. It's important to emphasize that lorewise almost all magic users have some arcane affinity. That includes warlocks, priests, druids. Even the most specialized frost mages will still use arcane magic cause it's a major "quality of life" school. Jaina opens portals and teleports people all the time.
