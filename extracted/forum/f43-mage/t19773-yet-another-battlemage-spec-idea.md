# Yet another Battlemage spec idea

## Post 134770 by Atolius — 2025-06-08T18:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134770#p134770 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello all,

I love battlemages and I just couldn't help myself from creating a subspec for them. Plus, they're an established part of the lore along with High Elves and not represented at all as being available to players in retail, which is a shame.

Here's a failed implementation using wand weaving which loses to scaling at around level 30, but is fun before then:

[viewtopic.php?t=19612](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=19612)

Below, I detail 3 talents in the Arcane tree which can be made to create a hopefully satisfying Battlemage experience while not stepping on the toes of other specs and classes due to imbalance. Let me know your thoughts while paying attention to the magical damage reductions that are outlined. Would this scale well? What needs to be changed?

If Turtle WoW ever adds additional classes or subspecs, and this is implemented, any ridicule now would be worth it.

Dragon Age: Origins fans may recognize the flavor of some of these abilities. This was my favorite implementation of battlemage in games, so I used it loosely as a model here.

NEW ARCANE TALENTS FOR BATTLEMAGES:

[https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c ... nc8yPO&c=0](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator/custom?t0=ki0KtKJpvi&t1=J21Cma0V44&t2=R4M5nc8yPO&c=0)

 1) Battlemage Training
Row 1 talent
 1 point

Channels magic inward to increase your physical ability at the expense of spell potency.

Replaces Strength and Agility with Intellect.
Can equip leather armor, mail armor, and shields.
Magical damage dealt decreased by 25% plus 2% per leather item, 3% per mail item, and 10% for a shield.
You can now parry and block attacks.

In addition, your melee attacks have a 5% chance to make your next Arcane Rupture, Fireball, or Frostbolt instant cast and cost half mana. This effect cannot occur more than once per minute.

 2) Magic Weapons
Row 2 talent
 1 point
Requires Battlemage Training

Grants access to the following four abilities:

Arcane (Fire, Frost) Weapon
Instant
 1% of base mana
Imbues your weapon with magic, causing 10% of weapon damage as additional Arcane (Fire, Frost) damage. This damage ignores reduction effects from Battlemage Training. Does not stack with other magic weapon imbues, but does stack with other weapon enhancements. Lasts 30 minutes.

Spellstrike
Instant, melee range
 10 second CD
 0.5% of base mana
Hits for 100% of weapon damage, plus 100% of weapon damage as spell damage, split between the nearest 4 enemies within 8 yards. The spell school chosen is determined by the active magic weapon imbue. Cannot be used without a magic weapon imbue. Can be used off global cooldown. This damage is not affected by Battlemage Training reduction effects and can proc Clearcasting.

 3) Arcane Warrior
Row 3 talent
 1 point
Requires Imbue Magic

Grants access to the following abilities:

Combat Magic
 3 second cast
 10% of base mana
Increases all weapon skills by 2, Spellstrike damage by 10%, and Arcane Weapon, Fire Weapon, and Frost Weapon damage by 50%. Cannot be used with Defensive Magic. Active until cancelled.

Defensive Magic
 3 second cast
 10% of base mana
Increases threat generated by 250% and reduces damage taken by 2%. Mana Shield now absorbs 1.33 damage per mana point, absorbs double the total amount of damage, and generates threat equal to the damage absorbed. Your Fire Blast now taunts an enemy to attack you if it is not currently attacking you. This effect ignores Fire immunity and has a 10 second cooldown. Cannot be used with Combat Magic. Active until cancelled.

Let's hear your thoughts.
