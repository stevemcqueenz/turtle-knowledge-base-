Hello there, welcome to Mage ^^
Frost does decent damage, but it is incredibly reliant on RNG Flash Freeze Procs due to Frostbite, which means you may end up in fights where you legitimately not cast a single Icicle, Frost's major damage spell, since you don't get a single proc.

Arcane is, by a large Margin, the strongest spec, Frost has (sadly) nowhere close to the ability to do damage as Arcane does. You want Cast Speed for this spec more than Crit or Pure Spell Power, but most of the good pieces that give Haste come from Molten Core. There are outliers from some dungeon quests, and Stormwind Vault though.

The biggest issue is, that Frost (and Fire) don't actually have any form of Tiersets that make a difference for them. Whereas Arcane actually does something, Frost and Fire are stuck with Boni such as "Do a bit less Threat" or "Increase the radius of Flamestrike and Blizzard by 15%".
That said, if you want to invest the time and gold into it, Frost benefits a lot from our Second Tier Dungeon Set. A 5% chance to trigger freeze whenever you take damage, which in turn causes Flash Freeze to proc.
It's kinda sad that Arcane is so much stronger than Frost, since Frost honestly feels more fun than Arcane does.

Fire is sadly not viable until after Blackwing Lair, since several bosses in both Molten Core and BWL are Fire immune, a relic of a time when that didn't make sense, but still was done. It should do better than Frost once it can actually do damage though.
