## Post 54081 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-07-19T18:41:00Z

> **Lapukas wrote: Wed Jul 19, 2023 11:59 am**
> Hello, I was wondering what are the best arcane talents for leveling (I know that frost is a better option for leveling, but I want to try out arcane).

[Level 14](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/0U)
[Level 19](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/0U5)
[Level 22](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/0U53)
[Level 27](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/2U5303)
[Level 30](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/4U53036)
[Level 41](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/4U53036-52I1)
[Level 45](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/4U53639-52I1)
[Level 50](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/4U5363BI-52I1)
[Level 54](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/4U5363BL1-52I1)
[Level 58](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage/HU5363BL7-52I1)

Last 2 points are up to you.  I'd personally recommend Improved Counterspell for PvP and PvE utility.  This is what I used, however, and it was a lot of fun and satisfying pew-pew all the way.  I usually ended up out-dpsing other mages just because of the total lack of wind-up or build-up.  Most things except bosses didn't stay alive long enough even 40+ for Ignite rolls to get going on Fire Mages.  It probably changes drastically at 60 once preraid and raid gear becomes available, but by that point you'll probably already  be married to how much joy this build brings you.

You could end up burning your entire mana bar before a boss is dead, or you could never drop below 90%.  It all depends on that Clearcast RNG.
