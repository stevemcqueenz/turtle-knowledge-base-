Hi,

I'm new on the server but wanted to give my impression of the change you made of this talent.

First point, the more you get stuff the less resist you will have, so the more useless this talent will be.
This statement applies also to the new spell Arcane Surge, while the new mecanic might be fun*, it doesn't give good tools to the mage, specially with that very short time to use it, less than 5 sec, so you can't even put 2 Frostbolt before using it, also the cd is pretty long for a spell on proc (same as Fire blast which has no condition)

Talking about CD, why the cd for Magic Absorption is twice what it was? It was 1 sec with a working mecanic, now 2 sec with a mecanic out of your control and less working as you get stuff.

Why I hate the change on that talent : As it was before, you could benefit from your stuff to gain mana, so if you go full Fire resist for example, then the talent works fine, you can make it work.
For farming, just now I'm in Felwood farming fire elementals, of course I have 0 regen mana with that talent, on official servers every resist was +500~ mana at 1 sec cd ... as it is now, the more stuff you have, the less it should work ... sweet.
I don't think the talent was perfect before, maybe we can improve it, but you took a total different approach and it doesn't make sense.

Also on an other subject, I saw the other topic talking about the nerf of Shatter 50% =>35% ... I totally agree, this is non sense, put it back at 50%. When you are in the Frost Nova or under Frosbite proc, it's normally a 100% crit ... I mean the talent wasn't +5%/10% ... 50% means that you better use all you have to go out because what is coming will hurt, it means Yeah, crit is coming, not "maybe". So 35% with the same 5 talents points is not going full on what you wanted, maybe nerf to 10% ? But this is just hurting mages for nothing.
