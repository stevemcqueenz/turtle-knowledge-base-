# Flash Freeze: The issue with Inconsistency

## Post 156219 by Eluaria — 2025-10-19T20:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156219#p156219 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Inconsistency has never been a good part of the game, Vanilla Time or not. I know a lot of people love to disagree with this statement, but they are usually stuck upon things like "No changes are better" and the like, despite it being quite obvious that it's not really holding much validity in that regard.

In the Mage Class, there is notably just 1 thing that really impacts it for PVE fight, being Frostbite. A 15% chance to trigger a freeze effect may not sound like a huge deal, but when Turtle WoW attempted to change up the way Frost plays (mainly to being forced to be a 1-Button Wonder), they essentially attempted to fix it via a mechanic that doesn't really implement well into the game. While getting a Guaranteed proc via Frost Nova is rather fun, in practice there is a decent chance that a fight goes over without a single Flash Freeze Proc happening during it. The issue stems from the chance to freeze being static in vanilla, whereas in modern games (including WoW) there are failsafes in place to not be entirely dependent on RNG.

While Vanilla technically doesn't have that massive of an issue, with other classes using their Procs more as a buff to damage, Frost Mages rely on those procs to do the majority of their damage rotation.

Since people apparently are keen on complaining about Mages in PvP - a fact that is quite funny to me, considering how incredibly easy they are to kill, especially for Feral Druids or Rogues - I would like to make a suggestion that doesn't impact PvP related performance positively, while ultimately helping out the issue of Flash Freeze Procs being entirely dependent on a 15% chance.

![Image](https://cdn.imgchest.com/files/fbfee755d4dc.png)

Instead of having to fish for a Freeze Proc during the entirety of a fight, this now enables you to have a form of guarantee during a lengthy fight to throw out Flash Freeze enhanced Icicles every now and then. It also makes it so that PvP people cannot complain about it making it stronger outside of PvE fights. Because, frankly, it doesn't actually do anything in PvP.
