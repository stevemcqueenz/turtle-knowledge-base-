# Arcane Surge and haste

## Post 145255 by Guldred — 2025-08-13T23:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145255#p145255 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

I was wondering if it is intended or an oversight that Arcane Surge (its GCD) does not scale with haste (and you can't improve by talents like Fire Blast). The problem with this being that while the spell is great at low haste value, due to the lack of scaling with haste you dont use it in burst phases.

As far as theorycrafting goes, the threshold lies at about 30% which you reach with Mind Quickening Gem or Arcane Power or the Sphere of endless Gulch (given you are at about 10% haste without the proc of the Sphere).

So when we go into burst mode, one of our 3 skills just falls flat and gets kicked out of the rotation.

Thats unfortunate since it is great fun to go after those procs at the last possible second, even canceling missiles for them sometimes.
