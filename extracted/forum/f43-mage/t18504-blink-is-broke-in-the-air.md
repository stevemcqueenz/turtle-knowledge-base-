# Blink is broke in the air?

## Post 126234 by niqht — 2025-04-10T04:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126234#p126234 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Blink is supposed to still teleport you when in the air and bring you to the ground. Operating in more of a 2D sense. Jumping off a zep, slow fall somewhere, jump into Thousand Needles... it only ever puts you back up higher, where you were in your fall :/

## Post 126267 by Turboman (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-10T09:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126267#p126267 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For this feature to work flawlessly you need a perfect surface beneath you (no collisions, not too curvy), if you appeared to be higher after blink that means that your "hit" some object on the ground in front of you or a surface curve.
Oh and also it works on certain height in my experience. If you press blink to soon, then you're get put higher with fall speed reset.

Its one of those lessons you just need to learn. Blink when you are low. Not high up
