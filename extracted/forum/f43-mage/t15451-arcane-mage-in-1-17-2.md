## Post 105841 by Kibuum — 2024-11-01T20:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105841#p105841 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

(Only talking about group PvE)

Arcane will lose Brilliance and therefore its role as a support caster, and on the other hand gain improved dmg capabilities, esp. Arcane Rupture and the synergy between Accelerated Arcana and Arcane Power.
Time will tell if this makes Arcane viable, utterly useless or OPTM, but since this forum lacks a discussion about the new skills and possible builds, I wanted to start it:

What to pair Arcane with? Fire, Frost, or a mix of both?
[My take atm.](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/mage?points=VAAoLIAaBKAoBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAQYDTBDQAAAAAAAA%3D)

Thoughts on Arcane Instability? Will it proc of every hit of Arcane Missiles [AM] like Arcane Concentration?
Is it worth the added risk combined with Arcane Power?

And gear! Will cast speed become more important than crit? What could be new BIS items? [Chromie's Broken Pocket Watch looks insane.](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61564)

Also: Will Rupture buff all AM-projectiles of a single cast, even if it runs out during the channeling? If so, then bringing AM channeling down to under 4,5 seconds would increase dmg of two full AM-casts. That would be possible with 12% casting speed increase, which is rather doable with Accelerated Arcana providing the first 6%.

And Presence of Mind: What is it for?
