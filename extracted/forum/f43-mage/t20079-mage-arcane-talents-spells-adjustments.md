# Mage Arcane Talents & Spells Adjustments

## Post 137409 by Avalok — 2025-06-29T12:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137409#p137409 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**HELLO!**

In this topic, I would like to suggest some adjustments on two talents in the Arcane Talent Tree,
and one Arcane Spell as well, for the Arcane Mages of Turtle-WoW! ---  happy_turtle_head

 1. ***Arcane Concentration*** (Talent)

I notice there has been added an "occurrence-in-time" mechanic on said talent. Just so you know, this mechanic didn't exist before. *What I would like to suggest is increasing the occurrence amount of the talent buff to twice (2), instead of just once (1), and also allow for the talent buff to be able to stack on the Mage for two (2) times as well.* You can also nerf the occurrence-in-time's duration to 10 or 12 seconds, instead of 8, if that would seem more fair.

The reason for this is because Arcane Missiles (AM) can actually proc the said talent multiple times in one channeling... Back then, when the talent, Arcane Concentration (AC), was not yet adjusted with an occurrence mechanic, the Mage can proc the said talent multiple times in one channeling of 'AM,' which seemed pretty silly, and kind of OP without the practicality included.

 2. ***Arcane Surge*** (Spell)

An important and subtle detail regarding this spell, Arcane Surge, is that it has an availability duration of what I believe to be four (4) seconds 'only,' after any other damaging spell hit of the Mage is partially or fully resisted by his target.

 *What I would like to suggest regarding this spell is to increase the duration of its availability to six (6) seconds, instead of just four (4) seconds.* It is because, sometimes, Arcane Surge (AS) will only trigger near the end of the channeling of Arcane Missiles (AM). By that time, it would have already been too late for me to be able cast 'AS' as I have already casted my next channel of 'AM.' After the duration of the channel, 'AS' would no longer be available.

 *Another suggestion I would like to give regarding this spell is to just remove its mana cost...* When using this spell, 'AS,' while having an 'AC' buff present on me would cause it to consume the latter, which is really just a bother... That 'AC' buff would have been much better off being consumed by a channel of Arcane Missiles.

 3. ***Temporal Convergence*** (Talent)

 *If it is possible, could you guys please adjust the talent, Temporal Convergence (TC), so that when the buff of said talent affects the Mage, it will not also consume an Arcane Concentration (AC) buff, if it is also present on him?* When an 'AC' buff and a 'TC' buff are both present on the Mage, both will be consumed after casting Arcane Rupture, which really feels like much of a waste to be honest.

Temporal Convergence is much like an Arcane Concentration buff, but it only affects one particular spell, which is the 'Arcane Rupture' spell from the Arcane Talent Tree. I personally do not like using Arcane Rupture, because I'm a bit too conscious with mana conservation. My rotation is simply just spamming Arcane Missiles, with the occasional Arcane Surge.

 *Maybe the said talent, 'TC,' could be adjusted to restore only half the mana it consumes instead, or some other mechanic that could go along well with it (nerfed if needed), so long as there are no 'AC' buffs consumed when it is active on the Mage along with 'TC' buff.* I hope that an adjustment could be implemented on this talent.

 ----------

That is all.   happy_turtle    Thank you for your time.
