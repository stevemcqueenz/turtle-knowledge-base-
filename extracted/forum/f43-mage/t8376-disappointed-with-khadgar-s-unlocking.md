Yes, Khadgar's Unlocking is pretty bad - probably intentionally so. You can crawl the database and look at the exact values.
Rank 1 does unlocking at skill 15 which just opens Ornate Bronze Lockbox.
Rank 2 does skill 75 which adds Heavy Bronze Lockbox and Iron Lockbox
Rank 3 does skill 135 which adds Strong Iron Lockbox.
Rank 4 does skill 195 which just adds Steel Lockbox.

That's it. Reinforced Steel/Mithril/Thorium/Eternium Lockboxes require higher skill than that. My guess is they wanted to add something neat that didn't upset the balance. Skill 195 for the highest spell rank is suspiciously below 200 where you could unlock the Gordok Inner Door for DM North tribute runs. Still need a rogue, a blacksmith or an engineer for that. The lockboxes you can carry to a rogue anyway, so I think Khadgar's Unlocking is meant to be used on those locked dungeon chests if you don't have a rogue in the party. But even there the weirdly low skill ceiling means it stops being useful after Uldaman.

Take it as class flavor, not as something terribly useful.
