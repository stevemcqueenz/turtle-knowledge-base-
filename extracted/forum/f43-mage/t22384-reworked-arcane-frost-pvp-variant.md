# Reworked Arcane & Frost (PVP Variant)

## Post 155430 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-10-13T15:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155430#p155430 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/A2kiBdg.png)

Blizz had deeply admirable concepts in regards to thematics and 'flavor' for Mage and its Trees -- these being very important in a fantasy Role Playing game.
...but overall they did quite poorly as there is a whole array of highly over/underpowered abilities and Talents.

Sadly, the T-WoW devs did not adequately address the mistakes of their forefathers...
AND went even further down a twisted path by adding ill-conceived, custom, Raid-brained DPS shlock.

> 'making Arcane Missiles viable in PVP' was HIGHLY commendable. they likely did this by accident however.

I reworked Arcane and made minor (but important) tweaks to Frost.
Yes, there will be (long overdue/well-deserved) nerfs im afraid.
This is to *primarily* improve the overall health/enjoyment of PVP.

I added a handful of new, 'Flavorful' abilities to "soften the blow".
My goal is to Make Azeroth FUN Again.
NOT obsess over numbers on a '40 Man Raid DPS Meter Add-on.'

 **BASELINE CHANGES:**
Arcane Surge has been reworked into '"Overload":
> (enemy players should NOT be heavily punished merely for having Resistances -- as they wisely should)

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/lciDbtL.png)

Polymorph cast time increased from 1.5 sec to 3 sec.
Blink CD increased from 15 --> 20 sec.
Remove Lesser Curse range has been reduced to 20 yards.
Early ranks of 'Frost Bolt' now have a 2.5 second cast time.

>>[**LINK TO NEW TALENT TREES**](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator?t0=aEakFcXgRS&t1=sEZF3OGjUa&t2=marVRCbsES)<<

 **ARCANE TREE IMPORTANT NOTES:**
 - 'Arcane Rupture' was problematic (infamous for 1 shotting in PVP) and is simply incompatible with my (and OG Blizz Devs) vision/philosophy for this Specialization.

 **NEW ABILITY:** 'Invisibility' from the beta of WoW! Its basically a free Invis Potion every 10 min. Tho in true 'goofball Magician' fashion its rather unreliable!

 **NEW ABILITY:** Quick-cast variant (Rabbit) of Polymorph which is intended for PVP usage (or escaping a mob).

 - Largely restored Arcane's original intended identity & character.
 - Returned (reworked) various iconic Vanilla Talents.
 - Reduced bonus damage to 75% as this tree was intended by OG devs not to dominate in raid DPS but to provide Sustainability/utility/survivability/amusing Magic Trick Gimmicks -- mostly in PVP.
 - "Arcane" was supposed to be a "Jack of All Trades" generic Magician so Fire/Frost are also slightly boosted in a few ways.

 **FIRE TREE IMPORTANT NOTES:**
No changes.  satisfied_turtle_head
Its the arch-typical 'glass cannon' spec and it seems reasonably balanced in my experience.

 **FROST TREE IMPORTANT NOTES:**
 - Icicles (infamous for cutting thru walls in PVP) has had its range reduced (and restricted) to 25 yards.
 - Reduced bonus damage to 75% as this tree was intended by OG devs not to dominate in raid DPS but to provide incredible AOE/control/survivability.
 - Slightly nerfed Ice Block (my Warrior changes would provide a direct counter to it).
 - Slightly nerfed Cold Snap as it ruins Arena PVP (requires investment to go back to 10 min CD)
 - Ice Barrier was buffed for survivability but "nerfed" for boosting damage (costs a point).

> **NOTE: If you want to see a more 'conservative/Raid-approved' version of Arcane --** [**CLICK HERE**](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=22267).
