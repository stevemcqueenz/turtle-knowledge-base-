# Mage: Arcane Talent Bug

## Post 34740 by Avalok — 2023-02-14T04:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34740#p34740 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Talent **Arcane Brilliance** gets to be available and gained as an ability when the talent, **Arcane Potency**, which is a three-point requirement for Arcane Brilliance, is only at one point. What I mean is one can get Arcane Brilliance with only one point of Arcane Potency, and I don't think that should be the case as of how the new talents were designed, so please, I'd like to ask Turtle WoW team to do something about that.

Also, **I'd Like to suggest** that maybe the talent **Arcane Focus** in the first row of the Arcane Tree could be **reduced from five points to three** instead. It would go like 4%/ 7%/ 10%. I think it would be a great talent point balance when investing talents to get those Arcane talents from the furthest row, like **Arcane Power** and **Arcane Brilliance**. I hope Turtle WoW team reads this and give consideration to my requests and suggestions.
