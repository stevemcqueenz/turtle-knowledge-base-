There are several things mentioned in the replies I want to answer to.

> Is subtlety rogue good, or beast master hunter? you have two good specs, just use one of them instead of frost

This is an absolute nil argument.

Just because other classes have the same problem does not make it a good point to throw out. If anything, this just means that Beastmaster and Subtlety Rogue need changes too.

Not to mention, Subtlety is amazing as a support class and adds a bunch of damage to a raid that outvalues a singular rogue's own, people just don't play it because they want to be the ones doing the big numbers. And Beast master is LITERALLY being buffed in multiple ways, both baseline and talentwise, in the coming patch.

Going "Oh, just play something else" is redundant and doesn't further anything in any form.

> I dont get it? Its still best control based spec there is for mage?

The spec isn't. The only control you get from it are slightly stronger slows and an ability to get a Slow on your Blizzard while Reducing Frost Nova's cooldown. And a 15% chance to freeze on slow effects, which only really applies to Improved Blizzard which you need to also Spec into, or a bare chance on Cone of Cold/Frostbolt, which is essentially gambling to have any effect.

Most of these are ADDITIONS to the control which Mage already has baseline. Cone of Cold, Frost Bolt, Polymorph, Frost Nova all exist baseline. They aren't what makes the Spec, they are just a baseline to the class.

This doesn't change anything about Frost having little identity in its own outside of "Slow, maybe sometimes freeze" and lacking both consistency and damage on top of that.

> I could be wrong but I think the devs want frost to be more of a pvp spec.

I'm not likely to be in the minority when I say that PvP is not and should not be the balance focus of a game that is 75%+ PvE. If they want Frost to be PvP spec, they can add Talents to it that make it better for PvP that don't help in PvE and vice versa. WoW has always done that, and balancing these two things differently, giving damage in PvE where it is missing while reducing it in PvP, shouldn't just be a suggestion but is by far the easiest way to handle it.

As it stands, Frost is okay in Arena PvP, but doesn't really hold up in BGs, considering Arcane and Fire just outburst and outsustain it by a lot. Frost's only saving grace is the utility it provides in PvP, and even then it is notably better to just kill someone quickly rather than trying to outsurvive them slowly.

If the devs really want Frost to be a PvP spec, they have not done a good job so far.

> My guess is that devs havent decided on mage specc directions yet, they fleshed out arcane but essentially made it a mono specc with no connection to fire or frost

I like this as an idea, however I fear it's not as simple, considering how Fire is very much holding up in Kara40 and is getting the QoL change it needs to fix their current issue with Fireblast dropping Ignite. The Turtle Discord even got one of the devs to answer to that specifically, but regarding Frost it has been dead silence for a while now.

> Fire mage does not see much play outside of coordinated mage teams rn since a solo fire mage will only serve as a fire shaman/fire lock buffer.

This is, factually, wrong. Some of the best Mages on the Nordanaar Server are playing Fire, and they aren't just keeping up with Arcane, they can outdamage them. The issue Fire has is its dependency on Crits, which you can not fully rely on, which is the main reason Arcane is played over it. Consistency in Classic is extremely powerful and Arcane just has those tools which Fire lacks. However, this does not mean that the spec is dead or doesn't see play outside of coordinated teams.

As such, you are not correct in assuming that Fire is dependent on other Fire Mages. Most Mage Players simply don't fully grasp that currently Fire Blast isn't part of the rotation unless you have a fully stacked Pyroblast ready, and that they need specific items to fully keep up with Arcane's consistency.

As it stands, Arcane is the best spec due to that aforementioned consistent gameplay loop, but Fire is entirely capable of being competitive and is preferred by multiple mages both on the discord and those not on it. The data for the fight turnouts show the same, where Fire Mages that do show up very much keep up in the damage ranges Arcane has, they are just heavily outnumbered.

This doesn't make Frost's place any better, and Frost doesn't need buffs in PvP and especially not follow-up backwards buffs with a "When Immune" mechanic that is so inconsistent as is that you can have entire fights without a single Proc coming from Frostbolt due to it being a static chance and the way chances are calculated in Vanilla. Frost needs either an overhaul or buffs in general, because while it may be the most used PvP spec, if you look at the Arcane and Fire mages who just make use of the Base Line abilities shared with Frost, you notice very quickly that these specs are favored in PvP as well.
