# Kamakazi Mages Disgust Me 🤢

## Post 101677 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-09-22T15:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101677#p101677 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Warning:** This is basically yet another (well-deserved) Frost Mage hate thread.

In PVP you very often see mages (usually Frost) mount rushing or blinking into a group of enemy players
Then they use Frost Nova, Cone of Cold, then simply spam Arcane Explosion -- usually this results in huge AOE damage and often finishing off at least a couple 'weakened' players.

The Mage class is intended to be a 'Glass cannon'.
Frost Mage is intended to still be a Glass Cannon but simply less so -- as they have improved control/survivablity.
And i feel that their damage was supposed to be worse to compensate (but Blizzard simply failed to make this the case)

In my personal opinion -- no Mage should EVER be purposefully and repeatedly charging into a group of enemies.
I simply view this as bad game design.
It doesn't fit the flavor of the class (Frost Mage should be hiding in the back -- using Blizzard for AOE) nor is it healthy for balance or fun to fight against.
Becuz there are times when you are forced to be bunched together and you basically can't avoid the inevitable mage bombs.

I deeply despise that Mages (especially Frost) have like 5+ Damage absorb/immunity Shields which essentially makes them more Tanky than a Naxx geared Warrior.
(they take so long to kill that this allows for this Suicide Bomber strategy to be extremely viable).

My 'solutions':
Make certain Shield/Absorb Buffs not 'stackable'.
Ice Block applies 'Hypothermia' debuff
Reduce Frost Crit Bonus Dmg Talent from +100% to +50%
Shattering Throw given to Warrior (to break Ice Blocks)
