# Arcane Meditation?

## Post 99456 by Stoneskin — 2024-07-14T22:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99456#p99456 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am new to mage but does the talent Arcane Meditation works with arcane missiles? because arcane missiles is a channeled spell so i was wondering if the mana regen works.

## Post 99457 by Hctwowfan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-07-14T23:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99457#p99457 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Fairly certain that it does work with Arcane Missiles because it simply allows 15% of your OoC mana reg to always be up, no matter what you're doing.

You'd need a fairly substantial amount of spirit though to have a noticeable effect. While leveling, it's rather disappointing, as it will shave off a tick or two of drinking post combat at best. That's 3 talent points that can be put to better use if you're leveling an arcane mage.

In endgame content with better gear and a priest's spirit buff,  I guess it could be more worthwhile. But then again, there's tea.
