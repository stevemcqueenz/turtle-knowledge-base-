## Post 171409 by Eluaria — 2026-03-20T11:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171409#p171409 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I cannot recommend playing Frost in Raids. Even Fire (with a Spell Penetration build) outdoes it in Molten Core and Arcane is simply miles above it. With BWL now up and running, Frost will be left in the dust, sadly.

Until the Devs actually rework and Buff Frostmage, it will not be playable outside of Open World Content.

It DOES excel there. The survivability is absurdly good to the point you can solo many Elite Quests that almost all classes have issues doing. However, generally speaking, it is likely better to run a Fire Build with a decent amount of Points in Frost for survivability, as it will be faster kills overall.

Also, Darkhann understates the nerf to Blizzard. We lost a ton of damage on it, while also losing Damage on Improved Cone of Cold and a Reduction of Improved Blizzard's Slow. It is better to utilize Flamestrike, Frost Nova, and Cone of Cold to kite and AoE farm, with some Arcane Explosions inbetween. For AoE farming, we don't use Blizzard at all anymore, the nerfs are simply way too hard.
