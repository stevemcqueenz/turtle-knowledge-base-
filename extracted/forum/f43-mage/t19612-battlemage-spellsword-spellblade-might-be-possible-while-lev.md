# Battlemage/Spellsword/Spellblade might be possible (while leveling)

## Post 133463 by Atolius — 2025-05-29T11:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133463#p133463 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi Turtles,

I'm leveling right now as an instant-cast melee mage, and it seems quite viable so far. I don't expect it will scale well, but am level 26 and having a blast. Its damage is competitive and survivability is off the charts with Mana Shield and Counterspell even with low stamina gear. Throw in the Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer buff for even more fun and heavy crits. It seems to outperform ranged Arcane in overall damage and lasting power with this buff.

Yes, I know Shamans exist. This is fully for RP flavor and those who are willing to experiment or wish to try out a melee spellcaster with an Alliance race.

The trick is to have the right speed of staff and wand, and to weave them appropriately with the instant cast spells. Right now, I have Staff of the Friar ([https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=34 ... -the-friar](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=3415/staff-of-the-friar)) and Thunderwood (<https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=13062/thunderwood>).

The staff speed is 2.6 and wand speed is 1.9. Wands on Turtle WoW don't reset swing timers. Hitting melee and then wand once the hit animation activates gets me to be able to weave one wand cast in between each weapon swing within the swing timer. It might extend it a very little bit, something like 0.1 seconds, but works well together.

Dramatically lower or higher weapon speeds than this may either not work well with the wand or be too fast to weave wands with the 1.5 second default GCD, so I'd aim for as close to those two speeds for staves and wands as possible. Swords and off-hand with similar speeds is also likely to work, though not as heavy in melee.

My gear prioritizes the stats as follows: Attack Power, Agility, Spirit, Intellect, Stamina, and everything else. "of the Wolf" (AGI/SPI), "of the Falcon" (AGI/INT), "of Agility", "of the Owl" (INT/SPI), and "of the Monkey" (AGI/STAM) gear names are sought after in that order. Agility increases wand and possibly also melee crit once you have enough weapon skill. Amber Rings give +6 AP and you can acquire them early and keep them for a long time.

Always, always have your weapon and defense skills at highest levels. You will notice a big change if starting out versus having these full.

My current rotation is this:

Mana Shield
Arcane Rupture
Wand once
Melee
 - Once the staff hits, Fire Blast
 - Wand once (this wand + FB will reset or extend the swing timer, I've noticed)
Melee
Wand once
Melee
 - By this time, mp5 will be active and FB will be off cooldown. Use FB or melee more for mana as desired.
Wand
When Clearcasting procs, use missiles.
Use Arcane Explosion and Cone of Cold anytime FB is on cooldown, in a separate swing timer interval, if you have the mana.

Etc.

With Arcane Meditation, regeneration is tripled below 30% mana. This is important to get even more casts of Arcane Explosion off. Lowest rank AE can regen fully under these conditions and proc Clearcasting.

I basically boost damage for instants and skip the resistance talents except for Magic Absorption because Arcane Surge at level 32 will be a powerful DPS boost for all variants, potentially.

Lastly, anytime Seal of Wisdom is on an enemy, you will regenerate a LOT of mana and can use far more instant casts. Try it out on a training dummy when a Paladin is using it, and unless you're using Cone of Cold a lot, Fire Blast and Arcane Explosion at highest ranks can be used perpetually. Below 30% mana with Seal of Wisdom on the enemy and Arcane Meditation, it doesn't matter what instant spell you cast. You can do it forever with the rotation.

I'm not saying this build is the best, but it's something to try out for fun and seems very good so far. Let me know your experiences with it and if anyone can post any DPS numbers/survivability impressions. This method also requires very little to any drinking and eating downtime if you time the spells well with mp5 regen intervals.

The last point I'll say is if they could make a baseline mage ability like the one below, it could probably work.

Battlemage Aura:

 - Decreases casting/channeling range to 3 or 5 yards while in combat (targets which then move out of this range could still be hit by that spellcast)
 - Increases melee attack power by a portion of INT or even AGI

A long CD for the aura could help force one playstyle or the other in cases where blending range with melee breaks balance.

Not sure what to do about the melee miss chance and glancing blows vs. the same and higher levels...
