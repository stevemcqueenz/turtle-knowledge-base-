# Change the Changes plz

## Post 108772 by Rethon — 2024-11-20T00:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108772#p108772 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ignite 4s > 6s:

Ignite was 4s because this prevented the mob from having a low ingite from a scorch on it before the fireball hits. The scorch triggers a small global cooldown + the fireball has 3s casttime + flies for about 1s = low dmg ignite runs out before a new one is triggered by fireball. That was perfectly coordinated. Increasing the Ingite to 6 seconds was a bad idea in my opinion. You could also enlarge the ignite duration so the mages can hit 2 fireballs before it runs out. This would really help maintain the ignite with the few remaining (one) firemages in the raid. But it's not ideal because of the reason I mentioned above. I strongly recommend setting the Ignite back to 4s. The change (ticking duration) also made the ignite too weak.
I understand the increase to 6 seconds and acknowledge that these help to keep the ignite up (especially if you are the only mage trying to keep it up with scorch after a pyro+fireball crit) but the 6s also cause the ignite to be reservated by low dmg scorches. I also understand that the ignite is too strong and therefore balance between fire/frost/arcane is complicated without giving frost and arcanemages an ingitelike ability. I have no idea what the designers were thinking with the firemage changes because rly no one who understands the changes likes them. Maybe there wasn't enough time. The arcane mage, on the other hand, has a really good concept in my opinion. Compared to the first one with the brilliance aura and stuff^^

Hot streak:

The problem with the hot streak is that it depends on critical hits and the fireball has a flight time of 1s. Assuming you have 8 stacks and the 9th fireball flies towards the mob, then you already start casting the 10th before the 9th fireball has arrived. If the 9th and 10th both crit you effectively lose a hot streak buff. If Hot Streak wasn't dependent on critical hits you could use 8x fireball > fireblast > pyro.

Both changes are not good and lead to speculation. Wasn't there enough time? Are the basic game mechanics not understood and then you intervene in a system that you don't understand? Reducing the manacost of the grpbuffs or solving the aggro problem of Ignite are both such godlike solutions compared to ingite and hot streak changes. People also LOVE oranges. But with the 6s Ignite and this hot streak you're really making people unhappy.

A class or spec that is more difficult to play should also provide more performance. No attention is paid at all to whether a seemingly complicated class can be made relatively simple using macros. That's difficult to keep in balance. You always need at least one melee and at least one range class that is easy to play so that the game remains inclusive, otherwise you will lose players who have limitations due to age or illness. The combat rouge in pve and the mage in pve were these inclusive classes.

The world is not just made up of young and healthy people. Us old people want to play too. We stabilize the markets and farms stuff. We improve the social climate and have patience to explain things to new players. We are an important part of the community. So please give us our 4s ignite back and destroy that hot streak ability.
