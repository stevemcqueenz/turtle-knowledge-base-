# Mage suggestions

## Post 158193 by Cheruscan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-11-03T12:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158193#p158193 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Playing a mage is very boring atm because arcane covers pretty much every aspect of the game for your class.

During lvling you best go arcane (low downtime, 0% spellpushback, highest hit chance from talents, etc)

During raiding you best go arcane (almost no arcane immune enemies, highest hit chance from talents, high dmg, easy to grasp rotation that is still a little engaging but never feels like chore)

During PvP you go arcane (high dps, channeled spell to ignore LOS, 0% pushback, instant cast pyroblast)

Fire specc is dead, it relies on multiple mages playing the specc in coordination, I know 2 mage mains and they both would love to play fire but they simply cant since mages are pretty rare and if there are other mages they are 95% arcane because its the most convenient specc

Frost specc is a meme you lose 15% of your dmg when you get hit... icicles feels like a worse arcane missile.

Pls turn arcane back into a support / gimmick specc make it have synergy with frost/fire again rather than being a standalone arcane dmg specc

Fire needs to be made more accesible, either rework ignite so a single fire mage can reliably keep it up or change the way the specc is supposed to do dmg, add more synergy with frost so a frost/fire specc can become a thing, hotstreak could count any spell crits no matter from which school, remove blastwave or give fire mages a real AoE playstyle using blastwave/flamestrike

In the lore fire magic is described as chaotic and volatile the capstone of fire should reflect this, maybe have the mage take dmg from his own firespells for a duration but buff your fire dmg at the same time.

Frost magic on the other hand is the prefered "safe and reliable" option for mages, the slows and shields are great but it lacks dmg. In PvE they could be given a stacking debuff on their target, smth like, stacks 6 times than explodes with frost dmg or just a dot akin to ignite but it doesnt need crits and it only works on enemies that are immune to the slow effects. Synergy with fire spells would also be great after 4 stacks the target loses fire immunity and will take 150% extra dmg from your next fire spell.

Arcane should offer talents for mana efficency and extra effects on all your spells in the first 4 rows, other people suggested giving poly and blink longer CDs and then have talents in deep arcane to bring them back to vanilla CD, for blink to problem, for poly it depends since its still a much needed tool, especially in dungeons. Deep arcane could still offer some arcane dmg, maybe add a chance to "empower" your next spell after hitting with arcane missiles, the empowered effect could be a debuff on the target or just simply "more" dmg but it should have different effects for different spells, easiest would be to "empower" by spell school ofc.

For example: your next fire dmg spell melts the targets armor increasing physical dmg taken by 10% for 5 seconds / your next frost dmg spell chills the target to its core reduces attack/cast speed by 40% for 4 seconds / your next arcane dmg spell ruptures the target dealing 200% extra dmg and increases arcane dmg taken by 8% for 12 seconds

Have all these on a seperate 15 sec internal cd so you cant procc the same effect over and over
