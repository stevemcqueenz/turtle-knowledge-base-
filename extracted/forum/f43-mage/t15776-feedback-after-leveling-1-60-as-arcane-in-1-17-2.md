# Feedback after leveling 1-60 as Arcane in 1.17.2

## Post 107572 by Schoop123 — 2024-11-11T18:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107572#p107572 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After reading the class changes when they first dropped a few weeks ago, I decided to level all the way to 60 with the Arcane changes to see how they feel, here's my feedback from that journey. It's pretty long, so if you decide to read it, thanks in advance!

 1. **The Good**:
First of all, I'm a huge fan of the ideas behind the changes, with the focus on mana management and the effectively 3 different crit effects with Arcane Instability and Resonance Cascade, it makes it feel like the power is hard to control and fits Arcane perfectly in my opinion.

I also really like the idea of the Accelerated Arcana talent, reducing CDs of all Arcane spells based on casting speed gives a great new way of using stats, such as using Arcane Power to reduce the CD of Blink for movement (though not much else while leveling, more on that later). It also made me look everywhere I could to find more casting speed, something I'm a big fan of, even if there aren't that many sources while leveling.

There's also a ton of talents that you want, which means there's a lot of different ways you could build and still play "correctly", which is cool.

 2. **The Bad**:
 2.1. **Arcane Power**
The obvious one here for me is Arcane Power - I think the idea behind it is cool, and I think it's clear that the idea is to make even fully raid geared Mages care about mana, but this way of doing it makes it so that leveling Mages will basically never want to use it at all. For example, if you want more power to kill an elite, you'll instead just go oom and die because of it. If you want to use it to kill general mobs faster you'll just have to drink more, effectively wasting time rather than the opposite. The only use I've found for it is to use it to cast teleport faster, and to reduce the CD of Blink.

It also seems like it's contrary to the design of the tree in general - while the tree as a whole seems to revolve around mana management, Arcane Power punishes you for having a lot of Intellect (*by draining more of your mana*) and drastically reducing the value of your Spirit, and making your own ways of restoring mana (*Evocation and Mana Gem*) useless for the duration (*note: currently only Evocation and your Spirit mana regen is affected, I'm not sure if this is intended*). I feel like it'd fit the tree better if you wanted to use all of your tricks during Arcane Power to not die.

Also, it currently gives 25% casting speed rather than 35% as stated in the tooltip. Not sure if this is a bug.

 2.2. **Burst**
The unfortunate side effect of having 3 different forms of crit is that when all of them happen at once the amount of damage you can do is absolutely crazy. Arcane Rupture hits pretty hard on its own, but with Resonance Cascade being a potential 1.75x damage increase, Arcane Potency making crits 2x damage increase, and Arcane Instability being a potential 1.25x damage increase, you can sometimes hit (*1.75*2*1.25=*) **4.375** times harder than a normal cast, which is pretty problematic in PvP I feel.

 2.3. **Suggestions**
If the functionality of making geared Mages care about mana is important to maintain, you could change **Arcane Power** to drain some of your max mana for each spell hit (*1% for each hit? including Cascades*), remove the reduced mana gains, and give an amount of **base** mana regen per second (*something like 5% per second, which is 60/s at level 60*). This makes it so that the spell is useful at all levels of content, makes you care about mana somewhat even in late endgame, and becomes useful while leveling in a different way from while raiding.

As for **burst**, I think the easiest solution is to make Arcane Rupture do a lot less damage and compensate by making Arcane Missiles better (*more on this later*). It won't matter that a crit can do 4.375x damage if your hits in general are fairly weak but numerous.

 3. **The Awkward**:
This section isn't really anything I can say is objective, it's just what I personally feel could be improved. First of all:

 3.1. **Intellect bad?**
While obviously yes, Vanilla class design in general doesn't really make any spec want to stack Intellect, I've always felt like if any spec should want it, it would be Arcane. With the current spec design, especially with Arcane Power draining a % of your max mana, Intellect feels *worse* for Arcane than for other casters. Until you can start stacking spelldamage and casting speed, it feels like Spirit is your main stat. Spirit being good isn't a bad thing, but I feel like Intellect should also be strong.

 3.2. **No great talents in the other trees**
While this definitely isn't a necessary thing to improve, I think it'd be great to pay homage to the roots of Arcane being a support tree to the other specs (*PoM Pyro, Arcane Frost*), by giving a reason to use spells from the other trees occasionally.

 3.3. **Arcane Rupture buff weirdness**
The fact that this buff lasts for 8 seconds and doesn't buff a cast that's started right before it ends but does increase the mana cost is awkward, it feels like you're punished for casting a second Arcane Missiles in the window unless you have enough cast speed to make a cast take 4 seconds.

 3.4. **Arcane Missiles still feel bad, especially early**
The spec is currently very reliant on either Arcane Rupture or the later talents for Arcane Missiles to feel better than the other main casts even with Arcane talents. Even with them though, the cast is just a little too sluggish to feel really good. I imagine this'd be fixed somewhat with raiding gear.

 3.5. **Suggestions**
To improve Intellect as a stat, I'd add an effect to a deep talent to do one or both of the following: 50% of your Intellect is added as Spirit, 20% of your Intellect is added as Spell Damage. The former at least makes Intellect relevant for leveling, while the latter gives some base Spell Damage that feels like it would be appropriate for Arcane. Intellect still wouldn't be the main stat for raiding, and I think that's fine.

To make other trees more relevant for offtalents, I'd add an effect that makes it so that every hit (*ie every hit of Arcane Explosion, every wave of Arcane Missiles, and every proc of Cascade and Instability*) of an Arcane spell increases the crit chance of your next Fire/Frost spell by 2%, stacking up to 50. I don't even know if this is possible with the Vanilla client, but I feel it'd do a lot to spice up the rotation without making stuff like PoM Pyro OP.

To both improve Missiles and to reduce the awkwardness of the Arcane Rupture buff, I'd reduce the time between each wave to 0.8 sec, 4 sec total for max ranks (*and reduce the scaling so it doesn't outscale Frostbolt/Fireball*). Though I'd like to see Arcane Rupture fully buffing any cast that was started while the buff was active regardless.

 4. **Final notes**
I think the ideas overall for the spec are great, and the changes have managed to make Arcane feel like a proper spec for sure which is impressive considering how little there was to begin with. The added stuff feels mostly appropriate for Vanilla while still being fun to use. While I've got a fair few suggestions in this post, I don't at all think all of them are necessary for Arcane to feel great, but rather they're just everything I've thought would improve the complete feeling of it.

If anyone read this far, thanks for reading!
