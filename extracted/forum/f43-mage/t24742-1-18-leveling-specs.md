Hey most folks go Frost but Fire got a lot of love on Turtle WoW

In leveling you can go for any spec you want really. I would always go for sustain (e.g. reduce mana of spell x) or reduce cast time (like Fireball) or survivability talents (change to stun from fire spells)

I am leveling a Fire Mage currently and its kinda fun. I would generally advice to keep your gear up 2 date for your level (get the recent dungeon, quest or profession items) as this will have the biggest impact on your leveling experience. Frost is a bit less gear dependent if I remember correctly. What matters is to have resources like mana up and reduce downtime to regenerate.

You can also choose to go Alchemy and Herbalism to always have mana pots and elixirs. Standard is Tailoring Enchanting.

Have fun with your mage :)

## Post 174948 by Eluaria — 2026-04-12T10:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174948#p174948 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Generally speaking, Frost wins out as an Open World Levelling Spec. The ability to freeze enemies in place and kite is incredibly useful and enables the spec to actually solo elite quests, which the other 2 specs can't reliably do. You can technically run full fire and use frostbolt, Frost Nova, and Cone of Cold without any enhancements to kite, but it is definitely harder. Arcane I can only recommend for Dungeons, Open World it is by far weaker than the other 2, although it will win out against Frost in late game content.
