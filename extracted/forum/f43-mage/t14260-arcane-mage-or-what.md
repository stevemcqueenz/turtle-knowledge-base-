I'm not expert at mages but based on what I have seen:
For leveling, frost is the best one because you can aoe farm and level up fast.
At PVP frost is very good again with the slows. Fire is a higher damage glass cannon, less ways to protect yourself.
At raid you can go two ways:
 * Damage -> Frost until BWL, respeccing to fire from AQ40 (fire has overall bigger damage but the leveling advantages and the early raids' fire rez usually mean a swap in spec around AQ40).
 * Support -> Arcane. Lower damage output (but still better than Vanilla) + passive mana regen for the party + the option to evocate. If your guild druid innervates you and you evocate at the exact same time, you basically refill the mana of the whole party.

BUT class changes are come very soon, we have no idea how the meta in 1-2 months will look like.
One of my guildies went to full arcane as RP and only changed it at full T2 set because he took pyroblast talent at fire so when T2 full set bonus procced, he'd got an instant pyroblast.

Play whatever you want and meta will change soon but rn I'd advise arcane if you are comfortable in sitting in a mana-intensive group, doing your channeled damage (or arcane explosions sometimes) and preparing for the moment when both evocate and innervate is up so you trivialize mana management in a long boss fight for the whole party. But as far as I see that's mostly it.
