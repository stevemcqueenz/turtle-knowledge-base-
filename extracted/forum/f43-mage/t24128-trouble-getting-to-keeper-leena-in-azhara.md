# Trouble getting to Keeper Leena in Azhara?

## Post 169690 by St0neh3ad — 2026-03-01T21:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169690#p169690 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

This is needed for at least a mage quest chain. Leena is up in a tower near 44.45, but you can't get to her that way. You need to get to the Temple of Arkkoran, entrance ramp is at 78,38. Then turn to the right twice, you will be in a round chamber where you click on a stone and voila. At this stage I assume that you already have the Staff of Eldara, that you will need to zap around.

Hope this helps :P
