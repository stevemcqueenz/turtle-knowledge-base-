These tweaks are intended to make 'CC2 Arcane' *tolerable* in PVP.
Whilst working within the framework of T-WoW devs Raid DPS obsession.

>>[LINK TO 'RAID DPS' VARIANT of Arcane](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/1awkwbFMT8)<<

Arcane Focus had to go because it meant Arcane attacks were virtually never resisted in PVP.

And it 'competes/conflicts' with other talents such as 'Subtlety' and 'Mana Cycle'

Magic Absorption was changed/nerfed to preserve its flavor and to reduce Arcane survivability in PVP.

Imp Mana Shield buff was also reverted to reduce Arcane survivability in PVP.

'Glass Cannons' should actually be FRAGILE.

The other changes are simply to nerf Arcane Rupture/Missiles (absurd RNG burst) damage in a variety of ways.

Examples:
'Rupture' damage reduced by roughly 20%.
'Resonance' only affects BASELINE spells so not Rupture.
