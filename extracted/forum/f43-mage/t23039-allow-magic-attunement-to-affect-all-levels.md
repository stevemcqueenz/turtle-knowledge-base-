# Allow 'Magic Attunement' to Affect All Levels

## Post 160751 by Avalok — 2025-11-26T07:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160751#p160751 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The Mage's "Magic Attunement" talent in the Arcane tree
should, I believe, affect targets above level 62.

This way, the spell buffs affected by this talent can be useful in Raids,
and not just in Dungeons, specifically against Raid Bosses themselves.

This causes the Mage's Amplify and Dampen Magic spell buffs
to affect NPC targets other than themselves and other players.

This way, Mages could have an alternative to what Warlocks
have which is their Curse of Shadow and Curse of Elements.

If there is some balancing need about this, then maybe reduce the
amount of effectiveness increased by the talent from 100% to 50%.
