# Cross-faction BGs confirmed

## Post 106403 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-04T21:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106403#p106403 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A few days ago Akalix confirmed that cross-faction battlegrounds will come to Turtle-WoW.
> Have you considered cross-faction battlegrounds? It would lead to faster queue times, more balanced games, and an overall better experience for those of us who still want to participate in PvP.

> This is something we have in the works currently. Coming Soon TM.

I'm just curious if actually majority of PvP playerbase want cross-faction battleground? I have always thought it would be controversial change and I've been always opposed to this idea but maybe I'm biased, maybe majority of people support this change?  Share your opinion.

As of mine, I think this is bad idea because of a few reasons.

- Perhaps the strongest argument against cross-faction if you think more about it. We all know how toxic some PvP players are. Without cross-faction we could at least hate only half of the playerbase. With cross-faction available we will hate everyone. I've made a lot of friends from Horde PvP scene on Turtle-WoW and I don't want to lose them only because I find out how annoying they are (and vice versa) when I meet them in opposing team.

- It's lore unfriendly so it shouldn't be added to the RP-focused server. For example Alterac Valley conflict between Stormpike Guard and Frostwolf Clan is indirect war between Alliance and Horde. Although formally there is peace between both factions, they officially support one side of the conflict. With cross-faction it would be weird for Orc to fight against Frostwolf Clan.

- In fact cross-faction doesn't shorten the queue time for both factions but equalizes waiting time for everyone. So currently the faction that waits longer for battleground to start will have shorter time but the other one will have longer time.

- There might be a problem with Shamans/Paladins being available for other factions which breaks Vanilla tradition of class exclusivness, however we don't know yet if devs will allow these classes to be mercenaries.

- Cross-faction will disturb faction balance even more. Currently PvPers were encouraged to join Horde because they usually had shorter queue. Without cross-faction the game autobalances itself.

- Some class/race combinations might become dead because of min-maxing if cross-faction is allowed. Why would you want to play Human Priest in PvP if you could choose Undead Priest that has one of the strongest class racials in the game and WotF on top of that?

I want to know if generally people on the forum support cross-faction. Let me know, please.

Note about small premades:
My only worry is small premade teams that are harder to track, I wish there was some addon or notification about premades joining(currently you can kinda tell if BG is empty until the game starts and suddenly 5 people pop in suddenly).

I think the only people who would not benefit from cross-faction are:

People heavily invested in keeping factions/classes in BG's seperate
Alliance players wanting a challenge/odds stacked against them
Alliance premades/honor farmers, they will now have to grind longer to get PvP gear and compete with horde.
Alliance who AFK to honor farm(now they gotta AFK longer due to honor rankings being merged)

Funnily enough Horde Premades would benefit from the change since they would have to grind less.
