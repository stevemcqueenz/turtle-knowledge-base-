> **Foobs wrote: Sat Aug 05, 2023 4:34 pm**
> When I try to rejoin lft as DPS I don't get the option to choose DPS anymore and I get told that DPS spots have been filled.

The "all DPS spots are filled" thing is a well-known long-standing bug that you can thankfully work around. When the role check happens the 3 DPS have to confirm their role first. Only when all 3 DPS spots are taken by the DPS players the tank and/or healer are allowed to confirm their role. LFT is sometimes double-booking roles, so for instance it can think the healer counts as a DPS and then it doesn't allow more DPS. If you book in the DPS first that cannot happen.
