neutral_turtle    |  **Chapter 1 : where it all started**

The core and key of this mystery is a non-aggressive NPC named <**Lord Rygel**> in Ashenvale. *[> Here's the T-WoW database's link about Lord Rygel.](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=62004)*

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/w9WE77u.png)

Why is this NPC so important ? What's makes him SPECIAL ?

• First of all, it is a ****Turtle WoW only NPC****, **it does not exist** in Classic WoW. Someone in the TWoW Team put a lot of effort in this.
• It's a ****rare mob**** with long respawn cooldown that does not have any drops except for some grey trash-items. *(I believe it spawns every 24 hours or so, I've seen it most of the time around 11pm.)*
• It is a ****level 59 elite**** mob located in Ashenvale, a zone for level 19-30 players. It means that any regular player questing here will most likely be unable to kill it.
• It is in the beast category, but it has ****a title**** : <**Frog Noble**> .
• It's name is very most likely a reference to **Dominar Rygel XVI** from Farscape (I'm not familiar with the license).
• When you do a */kiss* while targeting <**Lord Rygel**> , you're shapeshifted into a Frog and he says " Ribbit! "
• Last but not least, when I've looked into the loot table, I've noticed it has ****THREE unique items**** that drops ONLY from <**Lord Rygel**>.

So to summarize, this NPC is too high level for the zone it's in, it is not meant to be seen nor killed. It drops nothing of value, and nobody has really heard of it.
It could just be a fun easter egg added by the dev team. BUT I believe it's more than that. Why ? Because of the **loot table**.
