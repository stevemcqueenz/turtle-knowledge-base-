# Improve your RP feeling with playsoundfile makros!!

## Post 2925 by Chlothar (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-05-18T15:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2925#p2925 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is a way to play some ingame sound files (most are lines taken from raid bosses).
Bind them in a makro combining them with an attack or when poping a trinket.

Here`s one example soundfile:

/script PlaySoundFile("Sound\\Creature\\Ashbringer\\ASH_SPEAK_03.Wav")

You can choose from _01 to 09.Wav Check it out!! Not sure there is an existing list with all accessable playsoundfiles for vanilla.
