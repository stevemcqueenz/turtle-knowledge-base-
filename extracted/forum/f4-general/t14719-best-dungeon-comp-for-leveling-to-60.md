Good day.
Having a  warrior and a mage is a no brainer. Warrior is still a very solid choice for both as tank and as DPS. Mages bring good DPS with strong CC and water. The rest can be debated, but here are my 2 cents below:
I think during leveling druid wouldn't offer the best benefit as the healer. Since most of their healing repertoire is HoT-based, they can sometimes struggle with bursty encounters and/or quickly run out of mana using Healing Touch too much. If you are mostly after synergy, then nothing comes close to having a shaman healer. Good single-target heals with the amazing totems make them maybe the best choice for a dungeon healer position. Especially if you plan on having at least 2 melee classes in the party with a non-holy paladin there. Priests would also offer a strong buff with better healing during leveling.
Also the warlock seems like a strange choice to me, a hunter/rogue would give you another CC with better DPS (and optional pet offtanking from hunter). While the warlock curses are welcome, they shine the best in raids where they can affect the damage of much more people. If you take a shaman for the healing role you will not miss the soulstone since you will have already 2 wipe protections with the paladin and the shaman.
I won't question the choice of having a paladin there, if for nothing else, just the pure numbers of them makes them easiest to find to fill up your party.

Assuming dungeon spam 1-60 with no duplicate class, and no full AoEing.
Also assuming you don't need water/food, that you don't need to portal/summon to go to instances quickly.

Drood, war, rogue, pal, sham will have the best synergies by far (with no duplicate).

> 0 dependency on resources
> Drop WF, str, buff bless, shout, pack aura => auto-shot/cleave endlessly & brainlessly
> 3 shield holders, mail/plate => tanky af, can have your MT that dies np, you don't have to care about threat at all
> Pal can off-heal, off-dispel, rez. Sham can purge, spam kick, and ankh in case something goes very wrong
> Sham can drop tremor and grounding

You also get a 100% coverage on dispel diseas/poison/magic offensively/magic defensively/curse.
