## Post 70066 by Itorch100 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-10T22:06:00Z

Heya! Recently an idea crossed my mind and got me wondering, were there or are there any plans on rehauling the original established areas in WoW? (Talking about playable areas in particular).

One idea that comes to mind is Alpha Ironforge, which had a lot more verticality, having multiple levels to the city. I think it looks incredible, makes the city feel way bigger, Tinker Town made way more sense, and is a real shame it never made it live. Now, obviously it wouldn't have to be an exact copy, but I think the concept of multiple levels to a mountain city is really cool.

Seeing all the high quality polished custom content would have me wonder, if something of sorts was ever in the works? Cheers :)

I, too, think it'd be awesome to have some sort of overhaul of Ironforge. Since some of the old areas wouldn't work today, like the Deeprun Tram entrance, maybe some sort of amalgamation between old and new Ironforge would be great?

I especially love the outer and inner ring of Old Ironforge:
The outer ring is more like a living area of the dwarven folks, with some shops and the two-level-areas with the barracks and the temple of course.
What i really like about that outer ring is, is that's not a strict ring, but goes a bit down then up again, some turns here.. it just feels much more natural. :D
AND the poor gryphons don't have to endure the heat of the great forge. ;D

The Inner ring is more like a shopping mile.. at least partially, since the dark area with the rogue and warlock trainers' on that ring as well... i especially like, that THIS is the place with the bank. It always felt odd to me, that the Bank, where all the riches of the city are kept, was right behind the entrance of the city. This already screams: "Come enemies of the dwarfes! Raid us! Take all our riches!"

The Only thing about Old Ironforge that i don't like, is the Great Forge. In all honesty, it was just plain ugly. ^^ This is where some re-design or even amalgamation with new Ironforge can be done. :)
