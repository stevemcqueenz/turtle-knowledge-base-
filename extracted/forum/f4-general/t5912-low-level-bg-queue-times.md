## Post 37272 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-03-10T04:24:00Z

You should not only schedule bg time. You also need to remind people about it on daily basis. Use all possible channels: Discord, forums, world chat, Newcomers chat. Don't spam. It will only hurt your cause. One message per day. Be like that guy with "posture check, hydration check".
Weekly reports on the forums about number of games played could help too.
If you have enough dedication, after month or two, you will manage regular battlegrounds.

Try to assemble parties of 19 lvls to register for battleground. You'll need alliance/horde buddy for this.

Announce not only battlegrounds, but also dungeon parties and high-level quest parties. You should help people to get some gear. Check out my list of gear for 19 levels (including comments) [viewtopic.php?t=4714](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4714)

I think you should *NOT* make specific PvP guild. There is no meaningful benefit from being in specific guild. If you stay in Newcomers, your words will be heard by many people.

This is what I would do if I had enough time for this. Wish you good luck.

"Twinks" are barely alive. People play alot during gearing phase, but when they collect everything they planned, they usually do goldfarm on main character and don't login on twink.
