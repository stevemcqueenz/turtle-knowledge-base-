## Post 120677 by Zamnilisback89 — 2025-02-23T02:37:00Z

I think that warrior was talking about the weapon skill quests. You can find these things called Ancient Warfare Text, they're fairly cheap on the AH. Taking those to Fray Island where you learn Berserker Stance as a warrior you'll find a quest giver to hand them in to who will give you a quest to gain bonus weapon skill for a particular weapon class.

And as you know the more weapon skill you have the more hit it will provide(as well as dealing more damage on glancing blows and such).
