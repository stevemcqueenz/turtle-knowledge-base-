Good day.
Dishonourable titles are the same as better-known PvP ranks, just going the other direction. So if you earn enough honour in PvP to 'rank up', you'll get rid of your dishonourable title at the weekly honour reset.
If PvP is not your cup of tea, you can also spend some gold to disable showing any PvP rank. The NPCs for this are located in Orgrimmar (Valley of Strength inn) and Stormwind (Old Town inn).
