# Hardcore challenge characters are actually getting compensation when they die

## Post 133993 by Zwjwq5060 — 2025-06-02T16:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133993#p133993 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

@All members today, I regret to report that an unfortunate incident occurred in the game that caused the death of a large number of hardcore mode characters. Since the death adventurer characters are large and involve sudden problems, out of respect for adventurers' enthusiasm and persistence, although according to the server regulations, the hardcore character will not be resurrected once it dies, the development team still decided to give certain compensation to the characters who unfortunately drowned in this incident to encourage the drowning HC players to continue moving forward. (Compensation will be issued within 3 working days) World of Warcraft
