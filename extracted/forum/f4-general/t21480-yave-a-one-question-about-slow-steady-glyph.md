# Yave a one question about slow & steady glyph.

## Post 148881 by Qwinto — 2025-08-28T09:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148881#p148881 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there! I hope you can help me with some information about glyphs. I really enjoy clearing out locations from quests, and that's what I am interested in learning more about. In order to get experience while completing a quest, should I use a slow and steady glyph or is it not necessary? Do you only reduce experience for killing monsters, or do you also provide maximum experience for completing a quest regardless of the difficulty level?

## Post 148886 by Oomentaloo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-28T09:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148886#p148886 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You get 100% experience from quests with S&S glyph activated. In general quests give experience for longer than in vanilla. Reputation is also not reduced on green or grey quests.
When you die with S&S you lose 5% of your experience, but you can't level down.
Chose the S&S glyph when you want a bit of a slower pace and like to level though an entire zone.
The pace of most professions are also more in sync with your level when using S&S what I like very much.
