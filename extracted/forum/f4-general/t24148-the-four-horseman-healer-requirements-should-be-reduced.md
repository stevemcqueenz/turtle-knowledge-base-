# The Four Horseman healer requirements should be reduced.

## Post 169874 by Hysterics — 2026-03-03T03:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169874#p169874 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hear me out, please.

Naxxramus is no longer "the big bad". It's not the central focus of end game. With more content coming out that's only going to become more and more true. Yet Naxxramus even with better scaling than K40 has a more intensive composition requirement.

The requirements specifically for healers on Four Horsemen is very much a guild killer. It's hard to get to the "comfortable" amount of healers for Four Horsemen with an average group. I say average meaning people approaching Naxx that haven't been on the server for years on end. People with one character. People with one spec. People just reaching the point that they can pass Naxx gear checks. These players don't all have geared offspecs/alts that they can use to help out on specific fights.

The addition of scaling does help in general throughout Naxxramus, but it really falls short on Four Horsemen.

I want people to consider the impact of this. It's a huge knife in the back for new guilds. It creates a scenario where the guild ecosystem on the server is relatively top heavy. This causes more situations where people end up getting benched from raids, and potentially losing interest in the game because of it.

But yeah, if we can do the rest of the raid with 7 healers, needing nearly 50% more than that for just one fight is extremely rough. When we go in with less healers it also puts more strain on the healers that do show up, increasing their burnout. That can spiral fast.

That being said, I expect people will tell me to get better or say that I'm whining, and shoot the idea down-- but again this isn't even about me and my situation. It's about the health of the server and I've been here long enough now and in a position where I'm running a guild long enough to see that this is a real problem and as more raids come out it will become more of an issue. One single fight from one raid should not cause more stress from an organizing perspective than every other fight from every other raid combined.

The more pressing problem in my mind is that content is getting too easy. CC2 and all subsequent patches have introduced massive player powercreep. And I'm not talking about K40 items. Just the classes themselves are fantastically more powerful than in Vanilla, on top of all the new synergies that cross-faction raiding allows.

Naxx progression used to be measured in months. Now a guild entering Naxx for the first time can expect to down KT the very next week. I've seen it happen multiple times in recent months, and it's kinda crazy to me. Running a 1-day Naxx used to be a big achievement requiring earning gear and performing optimizations across months after your first KT kill. Nowadays it's the norm for the vast majority of all Naxx raiders within the first month of entering Naxx.

I don't think there is a need to make any encounters in Naxx easier. But this is Turtle. 20-year-old strategies require some adjustments. You don't need 12 healers that rotate with every mark. We run 4HM with 6 and it works well. During progression you might run more healers anyway, so 8 (10 at most) should be well enough to make 4HM go down safely, if you insist on sticking with the classic rotation strat.
With all the increased player power many guilds opt for a Thane-burn strategy where you nuke down 1 horseman at the start. Yes, with how powerful raids on TWoW are, this is now absolutely a progression strategy that people use from day 1.

This server is not Vanilla. Far from it.
This age is not 20 years ago. Far from it.
Don't feel enslaved to strategies that were designed for a bygone balance during a bygone age.
