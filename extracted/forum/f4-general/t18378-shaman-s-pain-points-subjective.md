# Shaman's pain points - subjective

## Post 125474 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-04T16:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125474#p125474 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

In this article I wanna collect the things which I feel are wrong about shamans in TWOW. Might be a flavour-problem, might be a gameplay one.
This is a personal take, while I'm trying to be objective, I might be wrong or controversial every now and then.
Will mention takes I don't agree with, too. Usually won't mention ideas about how to solve these, it's just a list of problems.

Let's start with the builds!

 **Elemental**
 * Very unrewarding levelling experience before late 30s.
 * Very straightforward/boring gameplay in PVE.
 * No unique utilities the other shaman specs can't bring to the table.
 * Mana (gets better with gear) - Ideally water shield barely procs on them in PVE and they don't have a water shield talent like resto does. Clearcasting has been buffed and it helps with geared eles with high spell crit but earlier mana feels like a problem.

 **Enh DPS**
 * Mana used to be a problem, now we gotta see how much SS's inclusion in clearcasting and shorter LS CD helped.
 * Threat. WF weapon gives a 20% chance for 2 free melee hits after every hit. Enh DPS can take over threat from tanks at every part of the fight.
 * People sometimes mention the lack of 'rush' the lack of CC or defensive CD but I think those might not be necessary. No class should have it all.

 **Enh tank**
Really depends on how seriously we take this build. Is this a dungeon tank by design? Then no concerns. Do we consider it a tank just like the others? Then there are some.

 * Unlike at Enh DPS, the lack of defensive CD is a problem here. Had a discussion today and got reminded about the Vanilla design of shamans being offensive utility and palas being defensive utility. But as long as we want a decent tank, specially in mail, a def CD is required. Also, here pala's offensive toolkit seems not to be half-bad.
 * Shield spec talent and stoneskin totem. Plus block chance is not bad but shaman tanks will not reach cap. Despite being a clear tank talent, it is barely used. Block amount buff feels random, too since shamans have no way to increase block chance on demand like the other shield-using tanks (holy shield, shield block). Stoneskin has a problem with scaling, too, it is barely ever worth it, specially since in case of a bunch of mobs, you usually prio stoneclaw. By the time it wears off, 15 secs passed, ideally you don't have enough enemies at that time to effectively use stoneskin totem.
 * Needs push at itemization for raid tanks who haven't reached T2,5 yet. Turning one set (like ZG or AQ20) into tanking would help a lot.
 * It's subjective but earth shield barely ever feels useful. Cost/effect ratio is bad, at least for PVE.
 * Arguably the effectiveness of aoe totems. They are either super expensive, have a CD or have a delayed effect. But personally I'm ok with it, aoe tanking is pala's speciality.

 **Resto**
 * Suffering from success. Chain heal is way too strong now.
 * Very boring gameplay.

 **Other problems**
 * Versatility: Shamans have so many random effects in the form of totems that people tend to be against further buffs on them. Some of these, like windwall totem serves 0 purpose and are just here to further bloat our totemic pool.
 * Windfury: It is so strong that it warps shaman gameplay. Being the only class MC pugs bring at lvl 56 - to spam WF totem - is not healthy. It binds shamans to melee parties (many ele complain about that, for them a moonkin aura would be huge).
The problem with WF is that - if we look at it from an individual PoV - it hurts each shaman spec at the cost of helping the party. Not using it is selfish, I agree. But designing an effect in a way that you have to play suboptimally to help the party feels bad.
Giving examples.
Resto would prefer a healer group with a nice tree and probably a tranquil air totem.
Ele would prefer a caster group with a moonkin and using a tranquil air totem.
Enh DPS is fine with a melee party but totem doesn't stack with weapon imbue, except for Frostbrand (don't ask, why). Them using WF means giving up on agi totem, losing 4-5% crit chance on a very crit-reliant build (clearcasting, flurry, ele devastation, bloodlust).
Enh tank is the same but for them agi totem also means 4-5% dodge and around 180 armor.

Just to be on the same page: since cc2 totem twist is impossible, you can't twist in the air totem you want.

I'm undecided here. I sincerely hate how WF warps the shaman gameplay but I have to agree the rules of a Vanilla+ environment.

 * Searing totem attacking random available targets feels like a bug. An imp won't blow up sheeps or target a far-away patrol. Making sure that searing totem doesn't go crazy should not cost a talent point.
 * Spirit wolves orc racial was heavily nerfed, now its only use is to harass casters. Could benefit from some AP scaling.
 * Frost shock and PVP. In Vanilla it used to be a real problem but I think it's more manageable here.
 * Planned to mention purge as a nerf target but got convinced otherwise today.
 * Healing stream totem is useless. Would either benefit from a buff or even a removal from the totem pool. It's way too bad.
 * Flurry talent. There has to be a reason why a shaman's 5TP is worth less than a warrior's 5TP. I'm okay with keeping the haste bonus as it is but in that case our Flurry should have a weak secondary effect.
 * I kinda mentioned this before but chain heal is way too strong now.
 * Water totem quest. I mean in exchange the air totem quest is a joke. But you could really put half of the water totem's questline into the air totem one, maybe with changes flavour-wise.
