# Snowball Event and PvP

## Post 113047 by Silhouette (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-17T15:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113047#p113047 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Snowball is a World Boss related to the current Winter Veil event and spawns every 50-80 minutes. The whole Winter Veil Vale is where the WB is, it is a small area filled with hundreds of players and there is no protection for people with PvP ON or Warmode ON which feels poorly designed.

With PvP or Warmode ON, of course people are going to attack you, and while PvP usually isn't an issue because you decides to enable it in the first time, it is with this event when people can easily kill you and prevent you from completing the quest related to Snowball, which imply to wait for another 50-80 minutes to **maybe** make it this time. It also is a visual clusterfuck to even PvP in such conditions (small area + hundreds of players). The thing is that Warmode is designed in such drastic way that you cannot disable it a second time, so if you are stuck with it, you have to deal with it and the current Winter Event completely ignore the PvP rules some players partake under duress.

Overall good and dense event, but poorly thought when it comes to the PvP playerbase. Kinda feels out of place to be so punitive in a festive event, as well as Warmode shouldn't be an eternal punishment, notably on such server with a PvE mindset.
