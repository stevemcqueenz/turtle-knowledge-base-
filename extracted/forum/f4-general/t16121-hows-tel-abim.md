Based on the subreddit and in-game questions:

 *It's more cozy and tight knit due to the low population where "everyone" knows each other so you don't want to get a bad rep.
 *The server has been growing slightly the past months. It went from 250 max to 700 max when the patch released; it's down to around 550 max prime time now.
 *Slight Alliance majority, so 55/45 ?
 *You can get dungeon groups going during prime time, but otherwise it's hard, is what I've read.

 *The Leveling Challenges have been implemented on this server too.

The server seems quite nice, it just needs more people on it.
