# All Winter Veil quests look daily, tons of free xp harmful to WoW

## Post 115232 by Matches (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-02T13:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115232#p115232 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I may be wrong, but it looks like the Winter Veil quests, all of them, are repeatable daily.

On a newbie char, level 7 or 8 or so, I went in and took about 5500xp in total for doing just about nothing.

I thought that was bad enough (and did it to see what was being given) but then it turns out this looks to be *daily*.

(There's also another quest which is about level 17 or so, which at level 34 gives 5800 or so xp, and this also looks daily.)

People play WoW for lots of different reasons, and I think one of those reasons is the feeling of satisfaction in a character, by the work necessary to build up and develop that character.

This reason is fundamentally undercut by giving away easy xp.

If after you've done the work to build up and develop a character, that work is changed to become trivially easy, then why did you bother?  what you have done has been made worthless, and the satisfaction you had is removed.

I may be wrong, but to my eye, giving away too much - xp or otherwise - is in a way like inflation.  It steals value from existing players, and this is profoundly and fundamentally harmful to WoW.
