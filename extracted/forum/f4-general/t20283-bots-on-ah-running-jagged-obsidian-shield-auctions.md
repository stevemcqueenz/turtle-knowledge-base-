# Bots on AH running Jagged Obsidian Shield auctions

## Post 138978 by Matches (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-13T19:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138978#p138978 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1. I went to AH to buy Jagged Obsidian Shield.

 2. About ten or so for sale, all different players, all a little bit more than 150g.  All are "very long".

 3. I buy the cheapest, then shortly after realize it's much cheaper to buy that mats and pay someone to craft.

 4. About ten minutes after buying, I go to AH to put shield up - there now is *one* auction on the AH (and it's what was previously the most expensive, at 157g).

It looks to me like my purchase of that shield caused all the existing auctions - except that one, which I suspect is the only genuine auction - to be cancelled.

 5. I put the shield up at 150g.

 6. About five minutes later, one new auction for the shield is up on the AH at 149g 99s 99c.

It seems to me this is not a common item for sale - people are not constantly crafting and putting Jagged Obsidian Shields up on the AH, such that so very quickly a new auction would turn up.  It seems to me this auction is from a bot, and it came up so quickly because there are other bots constantly scanning auctions and prices on the AH.

So I think I've just seen a number of bots co-ordinate price scanning, auctions, and auction cancellation - and if I have, it seems to me this is badly disrupting the AH, because normal players are being automatically undercut by automated systems.
