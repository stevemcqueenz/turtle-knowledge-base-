I'm not talking about the population quantity.
I'm talking about quality.

People would help each other, even across factions, and normal with hardcore players.

We have seen an influx in player quantity, some love it, some don't.

But we have seen a terrifying drop in player quality.

I've reported every one of them.
They are still online.

Guild chat now is all about what kobold biology and reproduction.

Are there plans to revert this? Is this the server the staff wants? Should we get accustomed to this or leave if we don't like it?

Can we get any kind of transparency on if this will be addressed or how?

I am not demanding anything. I just want to know if the server I love is completely gone, or if there is a discrete plan in motion to save it.

## Post 39111 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-03-26T08:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39111#p39111 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Setting up and enforcing anti griefing rules can be extremely time consuming. So as the population grows it gets less and less viable to do so. But griefers rely on their actions falling on fertile soil.

Make liberal use of the blacklist. You can always take someone off of it if you happen to meet in a dungeon or guild.

For people tagging your grouped up mobs, get an item or spell with reflect damage. This immediately tags mobs for you when they hit you once.

Or to be fair, if you are in an area with lots of players, dont do too big aoe pulls. Its a bit of a dickmove in itself.

Never ever lash out in wisper against people stealing your tag etc, they thrive off of it.

And lastly, find a guild. A lot of the annoying players tend to stick in the default guild for quite a while. Finding a nice community is still possible
