**Orc Racial Ability: Include Ranged Attack Power, Spell Damage, and Healing**
The Orc racial ability only affects their base melee attack power. This makes their racial ability useless for those classes who specialize in ranged attacks, spell damage, and healing. Please do an adjustment to this ability and include the latter.

I suggest that the 25% increase in base melee attack power also includes base ranged attack power. Additionally, the racial ability should also include and affect spell damage and healing by a lesser percentage of about 10%, or maybe 15%. High Elves have a racial ability that is practically an Evocation ability for their spell casters, and affects rage and energy of other classes as well, so why not make an Arcane Power racial ability for the Orc spell casters too?

Come on, Turtle. I know it may not be vanilla or blizz-like, or even fantasy-like for the Orc racial ability to include those I have suggested, but please allow the exception and make an improvement for which the originally designed one made by Blizzard lacks. I believe this is the only racial ability in classic wow to not be useful for all classes, compared to all the other races.
