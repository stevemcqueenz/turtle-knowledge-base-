So there's clases who has always been existed in lore but never made it to the game.

This is an idea of how it can be done. However, this is not a suggestion yet, what about you guys talk a bit about it and argue about how possible it is or isn't before suggesting it in the right section?

Class promotions.

You cannot trully put something into vanilla that will make no sense after, because vanilla isn't the antithesis of the expansions but how it trully is according to the original story. In this case the only solution for that is...

CLASS SKINS!

Warlock > Necromancers (The practice of Necromancy)
Druid > Warden (The wisdom of the Ancients)
Hunter > Ranger (The cavalry of Marksmanship)
Rogue > Pirate (The power of the Crew)
Paladin > Crusader (The champions of the Light)
Mage > Spellbreaker (The elite force of the Archimages)
Priest > Moon priest (The power of Elune's light)
Shaman > Dark shaman (The power of the Corrupted Elements)
Warrior > Gladiator (The might of Blood Rage)

In short a class promotion is an external visual change of character and spells, not a parallel specialization or class revamp.

It opens the land for end game content, epic class quest chains and more...
