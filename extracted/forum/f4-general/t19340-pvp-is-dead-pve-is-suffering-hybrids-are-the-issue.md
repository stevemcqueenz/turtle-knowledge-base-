Ret was looked at like trash unless you were lucky enough to get enough gear from offspec rolls. I was only allowed to play ret outside of raids. Pvp and dungeons only. Warriors, rogues, mages, hunters, and locks were the heavy hitters. Most of the guilds I were in wanted those. This is coming from the eyes of an alliance pve player not pvp. Turtle WoW fixed a lot of my vanilla pve raiding complaints by making pretty much every class viable.

The catch 22 is pve was the focus and pvp seems to have gone off the rails a bit due to the changes in dps for hybrid classes.
