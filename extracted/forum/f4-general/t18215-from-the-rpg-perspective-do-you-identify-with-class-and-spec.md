Hello everyone,
I am relatively new to twow and have only leveled an enh shaman to mid 54 so far. As the title says, I'm not primarily looking for help or a rant.

It's about classes, more precisely, actually about specialization identity. Using the shaman as an example, it seems to me that there are roughly 3+1 identities. Enh DD, Ele, Resto and an Enh Tank. They are also relatively fleshed out, which seems easier with a hybrid class than with a mage, rogue or warlock, for example, who only takes on the DD role.

Now you hear discussions that nobody would play Shadow in twow because Disp is so good and Fire Mage because Arcane is so much better. It seems to be even worse with Rogue?! I can't judge all that myself, as I said, I have little idea of the current state in twow.

Now to my question to the community. Wow is an RP game. Can you identify with your role through the specs in the current state of WoW? Does it really feel special for you to be a Fire mage instead of an Arcane mage? Does your spec have enough flavour let’s say to make it worthwhile?

## Post 124188 by Wolf of Rage (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-03-28T08:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124188#p124188 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As an Arms Warrior who specifically uses Swords, I like that I still basically have the weapons specialist vibe in order. The change to various Weapon Specialization Talents I see as a positive, because now as "Master of Arms" instead, it's been homogenized to combine all previous applicable Weapon Classes into one -- you no longer have to choose one, but instead, have all. I can already tell that if someone simply chooses to, they could stress on this by having one of every Weapon Class to switch to on the fly, just for the RP purposes. I find this awesome.

Unfortunately, that is the only positive I can give, as, due to other changes, I have lost the feeling of being a tactician who suppresses and doubles down on enemies whose weaknesses have been exploited, as now it's more about rushing to get to 20% HP to abuse the fact that Execute got buffed here.

Hamstring is nerfed. Improved Hamstring is gone. Counterattack is vastly inferior. You are kited more, and can kite less (Arms is a lot about Range "dancing" -- watch Laintime Vanilla PvP to see mastery of that). Proc fishing has nearly been killed off from this, too (Sword Spec Arms especially suffering from this because only Hamstring procs it, not Sunder or Pummel or applying Rend).

Various damaging Abilities are weakened. Rend has inferior scaling (Weapon Damage/Speed does not matter), and its Talent is nerfed. Mortal Strike faced a little reduction also due to a formula change (WDMG% + raw bonus > only WDMG% now with just slightly higher scaling). Slam is awkward now, and can hardly anymore be used in tight timeframes (like Intercept's Stun duration) -- even with the Talent. Improved Cleave is gone (your AoE from it is weaker, regardless of still having bombs like Sweeping Strikes + Whirlwind).

The above points are ignored due to the fact gear scaling power creep exists here, which hides all of these issues by inflating your Damage the moment you get to like T2 DD and especially T3 DD. And PvE, because much of this matters less in PvE -- and you're just an Execute bot everyone complains about being broken.

My Spec is now more about rushing to finish off someone already weakened, with not as much to do beforehand.

Makes me feel like a mentally-crippled Fury Warrior.

Basically, some changes do not match what I think of when it comes to Arms' identity (save for Master of Arms being a shining exception), and the balance has further made it feel mismatching.
