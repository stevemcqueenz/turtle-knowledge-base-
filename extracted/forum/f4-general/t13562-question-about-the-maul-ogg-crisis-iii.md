Just checked it by foot. In the northern part of Gor'dosh Heights ogre camp there is a broken cart and large root looking like arc and stone. Go into that root-arc from stone and move forward. From this point direction is west. You go up a bit to plant with light-violet flower. Then there be hill. Round this hill from north and keep initial direction. You will go down a bit and then up again. There will be tree in the mountains. When you reach the tree turn left and go up the hill. Soon enough you will find Seer Bol'ukk. He is directly above Tyrant King Brog'dosh.
