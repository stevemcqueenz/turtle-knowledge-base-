## Post 57516 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-16T00:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57516#p57516 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are 3 ways to queue for a battleground:
You can go to the battleground and queue through the portal.
You can talk to one of the battleground emissaries in the capital cities to bring up the interface.
Or you can just click the crossed swords icon near your minimap to open the queue interface.

Once you are eligible to enter the battleground you will get a pop-up asking you to join. Accepting will teleport to you the battleground and save your location. You will be returned to where you left when the battleground ends.

Some limiations: Devs have been flip-flopping whether joining a BG works during a flight. I think the current situation is you will never get an accept pop-up during a flight. Also Blood Ring has it's own ideosyncracies - you auto-accept the join message and instead of an instance portal you can queue by talking to someone at the arena.
