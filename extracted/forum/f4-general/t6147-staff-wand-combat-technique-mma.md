# Staff-Wand combat technique MMA

## Post 38625 by Elenar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-22T17:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38625#p38625 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I developed this style playing priest.

The technique is based on the fact that Staff and Wand have separate cooldown/timer.
Which means you can train to use both.

 -After casting a spell, there is delay before you start hitting melee, that's why start with wand.
 -after like 2 wand hits, attack with staff. You time it right before or after wand animation ends, the sooner the better, so after practice you will see two white damage numbers almost immediately, one from wand and one from staff.
 -after simultaneous hit from wand and staff, you will go on global cooldown so wait until it's gone and start with wand again.
 -staff is now on timer, so hit twice with wand again (so the staff is ready). Repeat simultaneous staff attack on 2nd wand hit.

The slower the staff the better for more impactful hit. Times and number of wand shots may vary depending on how fast the wand and the staff is.

Enjoy :)
