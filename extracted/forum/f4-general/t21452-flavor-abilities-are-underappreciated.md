# Flavor Abilities are underappreciated

## Post 148717 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-08-27T17:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148717#p148717 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The dedicated/obsession with fine-tuning for hardcore raiding (and sometimes PVP) has resulted in an abandonment/forgetting of the Pure & Simple Fun of Flavor Abilities

One of the many quirks of Vanilla/Classic WoW was that it was NOT try-hard. the blizz devs had fun with it
crazy and sometimes OP engineering contraptions for instance
and lulzy things like Huntards "Eyes of The Beast" so you could larp as a Pokeman **;3**

Rather infamously:
In Class Changes 1.0 the T-WoW devs gave Shaman 'Calm Elements' -- a rather useless but cute spell.

This addition was despised due to ppl getting salty that Shaman was "neglected".

My perspective was that it was good for the game and that it was in-line with Blizzards original mindset.

Anyone else think the game needs more flavor/fun and less "MUH RAID DEEPZ Chartz" sweat?

P.S. The devs just gimped a flavor ability "Far Sight" in PVP Arena due to it allowing you to see the enemy team.
This is an example of "Flavor" dying out in favor of "fine tuning".
