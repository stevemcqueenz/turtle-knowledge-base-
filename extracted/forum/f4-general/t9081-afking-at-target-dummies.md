I think you have a gross misunderstanding of how AFK flagging and target dummy fighting actually works. You would only discover this on target dummies since they can stay alive for more than 5 minutes without the player also dying, but if you are not actively pushing buttons and are simply auto-attacking, you DO still get flagged AFK and your character WILL sit down and stop attacking. This of course means you will eventually be logged off.

I can speak from personal experience on this, as someone who went away for a few minutes while leveling a weapon skill that was at 1. When I came back, my character was not attacking the dummy having gone AFK and sat down roughly a few seconds before I'd returned. You can rest assured that all of those players at the target dummies who are attacking are either actively pressing buttons, moving around, or otherwise keeping tabs on their client. They are not simply parked at the dummies and out at dinner or buying groceries for hours.
