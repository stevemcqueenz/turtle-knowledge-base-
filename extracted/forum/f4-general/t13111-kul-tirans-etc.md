I actually know the guy this npc is named after, I used to roleplay with him on Moonguard, in the <Kul Tiras Marine Corps>. He was a real character, always wondered what happened to him. Its really nice to see Kul Tiras get some love on this server, particularly because roleplay involving Kul Tiras has been a huge part of the time I have spent on WoW.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/QZ7Adv7.png)
Curious how Valdarion got himself noted here, funnily enough the Boxing he refers too was a part of his character history even on retail warcraft. If you're out there Vald, Furyforge says hi.

I dont want to imply that the guild had anything to do with the Kul Tiran zones in TurtleWoW, but I always liked that our guild had such a lasting history on Warcraft that, for a time you couldn't google Kul Tiras without seeing some screenshots of our guild on google images.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/xmmhuqn.jpeg)
Interestingly I noted that the various Marine NPC's on Caelans rest all have different boots on, as well as legplates, and it was something of an inside/long lasting joke that we constantly changed uniform requirements and it was usually only the boots. I wouldnt be surprised if theres an analogue there.

Anyway, just wanted to comment and give thanks from an old roleplayers perspective. Almost feels like home!
