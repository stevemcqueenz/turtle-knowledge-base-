## Post 52257 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-04T01:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52257#p52257 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

On this server, you can accept multiple different Challenge Modes at the same time.

As per your example, you can combine **[Hardcore Mode](https://turtlecraft.gg/#/hardcore-mode)** with with **[Slow and Steady](https://turtlecraft.gg/#/slow-and-steady)**.  Doing so would alter your gameplay experience as follows:

- If you die, **you cannot resurrect**. Period.  Technically, this is worse than losing 10% of your Experience per death from Slow and Steady, but that 10% penalty is a non-factor since you're not allowed to die.  d:
- For every 10 Levels you earn, the server will announce your progress and you will get a care package with various goodies.
- At Level 60, you will be **removed from Hardcore** and earn the in-game title of "Immortal", as well as earn **two** unique mounts for your trouble and one last care package with some very nice things in it.
Hopefully, that clears things up for you.  ![👍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f44d.svg)
