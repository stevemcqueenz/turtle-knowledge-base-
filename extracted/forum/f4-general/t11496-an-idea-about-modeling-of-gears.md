# An Idea about Modeling of Gears

## Post 76095 by Didimcdudu — 2023-11-06T02:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76095#p76095 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We all know that twow dev team has invested a lot of time and energy in this game, especially about those original gears and iterms. We respect all the work they have done. However, those gears could have better looks, and currently those gears are just old gears with different colors.

On the other hand, I understand modeling new looks can be time consuming, which could be costly. Therefore, is that possible to outsource this job to all players? For example, the twow team can hold a design competition for gears in the game. There would be proper descriptions for each gear so the style of that gear can be related to its corresponding raid. There could be a deadline so all the players in the community have their opportunities to provide their designs to the dev team, and the prize for this could be turtle coins (maybe also put their name in the description of that gear in a certain way? just a thought).

I believe there are many talents players in our community and many of them are willing to contribute.

I write this because that shield in Emerald Sanctum does look terrible...no offense.

## Post 76116 by Hooker34 — 2023-11-06T09:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76116#p76116 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While outsourcing the design work could save the development team time and resources, it's important to consider the feasibility and logistics of such an endeavor. Establishing clear guidelines, providing detailed descriptions, and setting a reasonable deadline are crucial for a successful competition. Additionally, the development team would need to carefully evaluate and select the winning designs, ensuring they align with the game's overall aesthetic and functionality.

As you mentioned, it may take some time to figure out a comprehensive instruction/requirement, but compared with spending the very limited time that the dev team has, this should be cost-effective, especially when the whole process has been well developed, all the designs for the new items in the future can be determined in this way.

If it works, fine, next competition will be held in a larger scale; and if it doesn't work, it will just prove that my suggestion is a dumb idea, which I don't care xD.
