Honstly, 150/450 for your level bracket is too generous. That's 1/3rd. You can probably assume 1/3rd at least are level 60, probably more, but lets be generous. That leaves 300 levelers, and if we assume "in your bracket" means +/-5 levels, this means 1/6th of the levelers will be in your bracket, assuming an even spread. But that's also not true, levelers are almost always concentrated at the lower levels. So if you're around level 40sih, you can probably expect 20-30 people at your level to do dungeons with.

That's not a TERRIBLE amount honestly, but it does mean that you need to be far more proactive when it comes to group forming. You can't just sit in the dungeon queue and wait and hope, you need to go out and whisper all the people in your bracket, asking them if they're interested, and hope out of the 20-30 people available, you can find 4 others willing to do the dungeon.

But as someone else who plays primarily during off hours, I would strongly recommend you just roll nord or ambershire. You will genuinely probably get more world pvp in warmode on either of those servers than you will on tel abim, but you likely won't ever get those big fights you're looking for during off hours, no matter which server you pick.
