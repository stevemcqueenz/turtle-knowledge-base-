# AQ20 and ZG

## Post 170826 by Ffnabep (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-14T20:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170826#p170826 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello,

I think there is value in keeping some content simple and accessible. However, in their current state, AQ20 and ZG feel *too* simple and far too easy.

For example, today we completed AQ20 in a pug with only 14 players, and it was done without any real effort or challenge. When a raid designed for 20 players can be cleared that comfortably with such a small group, it suggests the difficulty may be overtuned in the wrong direction.

My suggestion would be to adjust these raids to a **maximum of 10 players**, similar to how smaller raid formats work elsewhere. Geared groups would still find them manageable, but it would create a bit more challenge and engagement for everyone else.

Right now, AQ20 and ZG essentially function as participation rewards. You show up, go through the motions, and collect items with very little resistance. That kind of design can diminish the sense of accomplishment that raids are supposed to provide.

It might be worth revisiting the tuning or structure of these raids to make them a bit more meaningful while still keeping them accessible.

Curious to hear what others think.
