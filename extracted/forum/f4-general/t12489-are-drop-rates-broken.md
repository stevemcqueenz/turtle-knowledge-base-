# Are drop rates broken?

## Post 83110 by Toaonyk — 2023-12-29T17:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83110#p83110 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This will probably perceived as unfounded paranoia and/or whining by some, but it's nevertheless an issue I want to address because I am under the serious impression that the drop rates of certain items (t0 set pieces specifically) are either buggy or outright manipulated - speficially the warrior t0 aka Valor set. (But also other sets,more on that later.) And since the T0,5 is the only realistic option DpS warriors have for quite some time, I also can't simply skip it.

One indicator is that there whenever UBRS/Strat/Scholo groups are formed, I exclusively see warriors (and less often rogues) HR'ing their t0 pieces, but not other classes. Okay, admittedly this can be blamed on the unfortunate combination of warriors being far more common than classes like, say druids or priests (who can expect to get their items by default and therefore don't have to call dibs on anything) on the one hand and also not having the option to simply collect T1 instead if they want to do DpS (unlike pure DpS classes like mages, hunters, warlocks and rogues).

What can't be blamed on class design or the makeup of the server population however is that certain items don't even drop in the first place: I've never even *seen* the warrior t0 chest or legs drop, and it took me ages to get the t0 boots - and it's not for lack of trying on my part! I probably have a combined 60-70 kills for Rivendare, Drakkisath and Kirtonos under my belt, and only got exactly 1 boot drop. If we assume that every item has an 11%-drop chance (which definitely should be the case for heads and legs and as far as I know isn't that far off from the drop rate for boots), the odds of this happening is less than 0.05% - so either I am one seriously unlucky bastard or something is seriously fishy here. And I am far from the only one, whenever I inquire other warriors about their experiences, they've had similarly terrible luck for one or more pieces of that set and racked up a similar amount of runs (admittedly I didn't ask the entire population of twow, but it's nonetheless odd that I never ran into a guy who got his set in a reasonable number of runs), a phenomenon that certainly contributes to that Hard Reserve-epidemic on this server.

And it's not as if only the Valor set was affected, but I've also barely if ever seen Shadowcraft or Lightforge drop, whether it's the big three (head, legs, chest) or the two remaining off pieces (shoulders, boots) - while my groups constantly get Wildheart, Devout or Elements (which more often than not go unused), and somewhat regularly get Magister or Dreadmist.
And this makes me even more suspicious because there are a lot of warriors, rogues and paladins and comparably few shamans, druids or priests - it kinda looks to me as if the drop rates have been tinkered with to favor underrepresented classes at the expense of those that are dime a dozen. I can only speculate about why someone would do such a thing, but here we are.

Sigh...

If I roll a hundred-sided die and need a result of 11 or less in order to win, this means that I can expect to not win for 89% of all rolls.

The likelihood of me getting a number larger than 11 (and losing) in *one* roll is therefore 89%, or 0.89.

The likelihood of me *always* getting a number larger than 11 in 65 consecutive rolls is 0.89 ^ 65, or ~0.000513, which is roughly 0.05%. Which means that even if it *can* happen, it is so exceedingly unlikely that the ususal conclusion would be that the numbers don't add up.
(And as I said, the odds of getting boots should be somewhat better than 11%, since they are a standard item on a normal loot table and don't share a spot with 8 equivalent with a supposedly identical drop rate; so all in all the odds for such a string of bad luck occuring should be even less than 0.05%).

So please enlighten me where my calculations are wrong.
