# General attitude toward Multiboxers

## Post 30429 by Disgruntleddude — 2022-12-10T23:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30429#p30429 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

This is my first post of this nature, and perhaps the last I will make, too. I have been playing on this server for years, even when there were only about 100 people playing at a time and you couldn't get anything done. Nowadays we see around 1k players regularly, and I think that is great...except for one thing: The blatant and disrespectful hate the community seems to direct at multi-boxers.

Some within the community feel so strongly against multi-boxers they will go out of their way to stalk and harass these players. Recently, a new player came into the Newcomers guild and simply asked about multi-boxing and was immediately met with hostile and vulgar language. It is so bad that group leaders with other characters on /follow are being targeted and harassed.

Personally, I find this behavior pathetic, and if you are one of these people you should be ashamed of yourself. This is a free server that has rules and allows people to play with multiple accounts. Believe it or not, multi-boxers are people too, they are not losers, and they do not suck at life; in fact, there is an enjoyable challenge to controlling multiple characters. Additionally, benefits like pocket heals/buffs, more bag space(just to name a few)are appealing to everybody.

Now, go ahead and make whatever arguments you want. That being said, sure -maybe- there should be HC exceptions, but for the most part, this is a really disturbing and unflattering pattern I am seeing within the community.

Just my 2 cents, GLHF everybody.

Vast majority of the playerbase on this server is very casual and RP/Community oriented. People that play here are deliberately trying to get away from all the soulleeching concepts that were introduced with Classic/Retail - GDKP, RMT, Mboxing/-botting as example.

It shouldn't really suprise you that people will chew you out if they spot you on the worldmap, especially if the formation looks very jarring and immersion breaking: I.e.
 - lazy name convention "Mboxkillerone; Mboxkillertwo; Mboxkillerthree; Mboxkillerfour; Mboxkillerfive"
 - all follow chars stacked inside eachother
 - all chars casting at the same time (looking like a bot, even if you just have good window management and click on individual windows for each spell)
 - singlehandedly wiping out elite quest zones when a group of players was waiting

People will only give you shit, if you make it obvious that you're trying to overpower the game by stacking multiple characters under your control instead of banding together with real people.
The smart way to multibox is to not appear like you're multiboxing.
Let me give you a personal example:
I do have a set of 5 mbox chars - 3 hunters, 1 priest, 1 paladin. Yet instead of giving them completely apathetic and sterile names, I give the hunters good matching dwarven names, the priest a good elven name, and the paladin a fair meme name (Thrundgrim - Krogrim - Bryngrim - Quelnora - Chadovsky).
Now on top of that, whenever I'm near populated areas, I make sure to **conga-line** the characters on follow.
Those 2 factors already make a massive difference in public image, when you compare "MBoxCharOne-Five" stacked inside eachother, to reasonably named chars that are following in a sensible formation.
Now sprinkle a little bit of lighthearted roleplay when near other players, such as moving the dwarves into a firing line formation and giving the /fire order with your paladin, and **WOW** - would you look at that? People actually react positively to your setup, because in the best case they think it's a group of people doing funny roleplay, and in the worst case, it's a multiboxer using the characters for funny roleplay - on a roleplay server! People don't throw rocks at your head, telling you to go back to retail!

I also reckon that a lot of people have had enough of multiboxers dominating other private servers without restriction.
Me and my group I play with had enough of Warmane's fiasko of 40-man Wintergrasp Starfall druids and their staff taking their time lining their pockets with the 40-man BIS gear donations until eventually crumbling to the severe backlash from the average pop, forcing them to progressively restrict Mboxing entirely.
Granted this server does already impose two important rules of "No Mbox PvP" and "No Mbox software", but people still hold a deep grudge for every time they had been wronged/griefed by a Multiboxer.
Add to this the notion that blatantly Multiboxing a meta-party gives the impression that you have zero interest with interacting with the playerbase, and it shouldn't surprise you that people will push back against you hard.

People asking about "GDKP" and "dungeon boosting" in Newcomer guildchat get chewed out just as hard. Why? Because it has ruined plenty of servers including Classic. And in the eyes of your average casual player, Multiboxing is one step away from botting, considering they don't know if you are manually operating all characters, or "illegally" using 3rd party software.

This obviously doesn't excuse obscene toxicity and harassment - you can still report that.
But you **really** shouldn't be surprised that people are intolerant of multiboxing.
