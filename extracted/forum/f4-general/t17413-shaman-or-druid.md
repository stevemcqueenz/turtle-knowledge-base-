## Post 118919 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-04T15:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118919#p118919 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Druid is- objectively, with no competition, the best (and most fun) leveller out of these two.  I suppose the fun part is subjective but it definitely feels better/smoother if you ask me. Feral (the generic levelling spec) can tank, heal and DPS through dungeons, but lacks in aoe in regard to DPS. I will say that pre 20 the class can feel a bit incomplete, but hitting that milestone is more than worth it.

Shaman can do the same in the enhancement spec. Tanking is a bit tougher but more than doable, and you can heal with some spare healing gear like a druid can. Your aoe capabilities are stronger, too. I will say that shaman's early game like pre 20 is -very- strong, too- so if that's a deciding factor you should probably take that into consideration.

## Post 118924 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-04T15:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118924#p118924 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Are you also considering priest if you wanna be a caster with an option to heal?

Gameplaywise... I played both but had more experience with the shaman, I might be biased.

Raiding - You can easily find a spot with both, but if you wanna keep the option of raid-tanking open, go druid. Shaman tank is not so desired, for a reason.

Leveling - Dru all the way. The only thing in favor of the shaman here is that they get their movement form 10 levels earlier so less walking. DPSing with a shammie even now feels like going oom way too soon plus having too much threat compared to the damage. Tanks might not like you but they won't say it because WF totem is goated.

Flavor - Highly subjective, but I enjoyed shamans more. I prefer chaotic characters and shamans have a chaotic flavor. Also, a seemingly small thing which is actually important - do you mind if your gear/tmog can't be seen 90% of the time because you are usually a naked bear/moonkin? I honestly felt less motivated to gear up as a druid.
Also, sometimes camera movement is bad if you are a big bear in a narrow place like SFK dungeon. I played blindly half of it, same in SM.

Things I can list in favor of shaman:
Reliable rez. Sometimes people /moo if you join a dung as a resto druid and there is noone who can rezz. Might result in corpseruns.
Resto shamans don't have this pressure.
Also, since swipe nerf, shaman tank aoe threat >> bear aoe threat. If you wanna tank sometimes but you also mentally break down if mobs start to run around if the pack is big enough, then shaman (unless still, if you wanna raid tank -shaman raid tanking is doable but a challenge in itself).
Also, you have a totem for everything. If you learn them and learn how to use them, you'll start to feel like a swiss army knife.

I think druid has a more smooth playstyle if youre leveling feral, also note that you do get a +30% speed buff with feline swiftness talent at level 22, so the difference with ghost wolf is only 10% for lvl 22-30.

Being able to stealth is a very, very big bonus for the druid to solo content and general exploration and avoidance, also when dealing with gankers.

As druid you will usually instantly find a group as last spot as you can both heal or tank when having some casting gear while leveling. That being said, when healing as druid, it very nice to have a (dps/tank) shaman with you in the group in case you wipe.

As heart of the wild tank/healer spec 0/30/21, you have no problem to heal MC and tank up to ZG/AQ20 with a mix of raid and pre raid bis.

Resto druids suck to farm, you become a one trick pony, and the end game will be pretty boring in that regard. But this probably is true for shamans as well.
Solution: Spend some gold or cash on the brainwashing device to be able to dual spec.
