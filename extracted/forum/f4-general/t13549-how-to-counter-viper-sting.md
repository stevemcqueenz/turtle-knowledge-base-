Think of vipersting as an enrage timer. By the time your mana goes out, you need to have won the fight against the hunter. Similarly to how a warlock or shadowpriest puts a timer on you with his dots.

So if you cant be close to a dispeller class, try to end the fight against the hunter quickly. He benefits from you trying to draw it out.

Hunters in all but the best gear do have only a limited manapool themselves. So they also put their manapool under a strain. If you are a dispeller or have one nearby, it can be beneficial to let him spam it.

You are cc resistant while under viper sting, do not be afraid of freezing trap while under its effect.

Nordrannar herbal tea is quick and insanelyeasy to farm and gives you about 10 seconds more mana than a hunter would have to burn in classic. Dont bother with poison cleanse consumables. They just reapply it.

And lastly, it might be hard to admit it, but if he manages to burn you dry, he won. It might feel infuriating, but treat it no different from a shaman blowing you up or a rogue stunlocking you to death. Die, rezz and get him next time
