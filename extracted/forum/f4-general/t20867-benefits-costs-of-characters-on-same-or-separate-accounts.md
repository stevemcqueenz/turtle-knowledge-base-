> 1. Can you just log into the same account twice at the same time on turtle wow? (I presume the answer is no)

You'll be disconnected on the first logged client, as any regular WoW account.

> 2. Do separate accounts need different emails or just different usernames (I presume the answer is different emails)
>  3. Will the '+' email trick satisfy account, i.e. "[[email protected]](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#375a4e525a565e5b1c434245435b52405645455e584577505a565e5b1954585a)", "[[email protected]](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#167b6f737b777f7a3d626364627a73647971637356717b777f7a3875797b)" which I understand gmail just forwards to "[[email protected]](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#4a27332f272b23260a2d272b232664292527)".

You need a different email per account last time I checked. Also the trick with "+" didn't work last time I tried.

> 4. Is the mailing wait time difference between characters on different accounts and the same account?

The mailing wait time is instant or almost-instant (like slight delay) on all characters server wide, you don't need to be on the same account.

> 6. Any other downsides I haven't thought of due to having a separate account per player instead of characters all on one account (besides having to relog in to change characters)?

No. You're pretty much doing what everyone else is doing. Have your mules on a different account, an isolated mage level max to serve water/food/portals to all your characters, and you're good to roll. You could have 1 account per character, but realistically you're probably good with 2 or 4. I'd say the most important is to isolate your mage, but you could just get away with 2 accounts only, as long as you can group, cast your portal, and switch account/character.

You can think of leveling a warlock up to 20 (iirc) to summon your alt characters also whenever you feel like rerolling a character, but you need 3 accounts.

The only real hassle is to handle x accounts/passwords, but that's a very minor inconvenience.
