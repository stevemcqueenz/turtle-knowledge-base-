> **Donjn wrote: Mon Jul 08, 2024 10:01 pm**
> As a caster, do you still have to manage mana as much?

Yes, mana management will always be a core part of the vanilla experience.  That being said,  some classes have had their talent trees or kit tweaked to improve their mana sustain.  No one has infinite mana though, but Class Changes v1 (and the upcoming Class Changes v2) promise to add some quality of life to mana users that seemed deprived of talents other caster classes had access to, such as pushback resistance, mana upkeep on kills, clearcasting, etc.

> **Donjn wrote: Mon Jul 08, 2024 10:01 pm**
> While questing do you need to drink potions every other pull?

I'm not sure what class you've been playing that you felt that you needed to burn mana potions (maybe you meant sitting and drinking?) but see points above.

> **Donjn wrote: Mon Jul 08, 2024 10:01 pm**
> As a Warlock does the Voidwalker hold threat when questing? In vanilla he basically does not.

Yes, the Voidwalker is a decent tank for the Warlock now, though probably still not as a dungeon tank.  They have tweaked threat values slightly on the Voidwalker, and also added damage to their "growl" equivalent, which means more threat generated.  You will still have to be careful though, as Warlocks are great at generating threat; so it isn't EZ mode, just more tenable.

> **Donjn wrote: Mon Jul 08, 2024 10:01 pm**
> What are the costs for mounts? Are they reduced?

Mounts are the same as they've always cost.  For Warlocks and Paladins, the 40 mount is free.  For everyone else, training costs 90g and the mount costs 10g (100g total).  This is reduced by 10% if you are PvP Rank 3 (which now lasts forever once you reach it), and another 10% if you are Honored with the faction you're buying from, for a total of 81g + 9g (90g total) and 72g + 8g (80g total).

For the 60 mount, for everyone but Warlocks and Paladins, it's the same deal, except it's 900g (810g / 720g) for Riding Training and 100g (90g / 80g) for the mount itself.

On Turtle, however, you need not pay the mount costs, and only the Riding Training cost, as you can do a free quest at level 18 from Darkmoon Faire that gives you a mount.  This mount scales with your level (+0.5% movement speed * your character level) until you get Riding Training, at which point it becomes the usual +60% or +100%

> **Donjn wrote: Mon Jul 08, 2024 10:01 pm**
> Can you track ore and herbs at the same time?

No you cannot.

> **Donjn wrote: Mon Jul 08, 2024 10:01 pm**
> Were there changes to make leveling up professions easier?

Yes, a great number of crafting professions have been reworked to make not only the lower level "grinding prof levels trash" worthwhile, but there's more recipes available and some costs are a bit more relaxed.  There's also better distributions of ore and herb nodes compared to most other servers, though your mileage may vary.

> **Donjn wrote: Mon Jul 08, 2024 10:01 pm**
> I assume the Dustwallow and Ashenvalle Forest Song quests aren't in the game?

A number of new quests have been added to not just these two regions, but a great deal of regions that vanilla was observably lacking or "unfinished" in their quest design.

> **Donjn wrote: Mon Jul 08, 2024 10:01 pm**
> Are there enough quest "crumbs" to tell me to visit the new areas?

Turtle has been okay at making breadcrumb quests to new areas or even their newly added questing hubs, but it's not perfect.  You may have to snoop around the world a bit on your own.  You will definitely find enough quests throughout the game now, compared to base vanilla, that it will be impossible to do everything in all zones of your level range before they start to turn green or grey.  Plenty of character leveling / questing paths to take now so that each character can have a novel experience, if you're willing to experiment.

> **Donjn wrote: Mon Jul 08, 2024 10:01 pm**
> Are classes slightly more balanced for DPS? Ret pally for example?

Class Changes v1 attempted to remedy places where classes were sorely lacking, and Class Changes v2 aims to make all classes at least feel engaging and satisfying to play while fulfilling their vanilla-designed niches.  "Balance" is subjective, but I can assure you that there is no plan to let everyone do everything just as well as everyone else.  The core philosophy has been class fantasy and enjoyability over homogenization.
