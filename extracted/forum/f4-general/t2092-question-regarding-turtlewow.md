## Post 12750 by Sharpfang — 2021-09-29T18:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12750#p12750 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!
There are a couple of custom quests in each zone with more to come in the upcoming patch.

Some of the custom content you can check out right away however.

We have 2 custom races. High elf for alliance and goblin for horde. Each with their own chain of starting quests.

Each faction also have mounts for when you complete your reputation grinds. Not just each factions race but also factions that didnt have mounts in vanilla.  For example if you max your reputation with the wildhammer clan in the hinterlands you are rewarded with a Griffin mount.

The portable wormhole generator is obtained early in the game as well. You can start the quest from a goblin in razor hill or a gnome in goldshire.

Also dont forget to pick up our custom secondary profession, survival. With this you can make campfire, light torches, build fishing boats and craft tents that give rested experience.

Hope this helped and enjoy the server.
