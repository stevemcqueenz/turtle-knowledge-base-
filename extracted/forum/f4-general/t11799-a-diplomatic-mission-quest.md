Make a GM ticket in-game asking about it. Be absolutely sure that you turned in the Stormwind quest already. After accepting Diplomatic Mission, 4 other quests open up, each one for the Humans, Dwarves, Gnomes, Night Elves.

Once you turn in any of these quests to their respective leader, that specific quest cannot be done again, even if you abandon Diplomatic Mission. However, you should see this option if AND ONLY IF you have turned in the specific quest to this specific faction leader.  In your case, Bolvar, for turning in Appealing to the Alliance: Humans.
