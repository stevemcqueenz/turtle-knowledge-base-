# Level 60 only dungeons - why?

## Post 117151 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-18T10:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117151#p117151 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What is the purpose of the lvl 60 custom dungeons?
Don't get me wrong, they are fun. But from PVE pov late game dungeons serve two purpose: pushing 60 and prebis. A 60 only dungeon can only help with prebis but that's just taking away the relevance of the already existing late dungeons.
Don't get me wrong, I like a good CoT run but I never really found its place. You can only enter at 60 and you already outgear the non-epic loot after 2 ZG pugs.
