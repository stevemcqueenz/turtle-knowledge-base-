Pal prot is the king of tanking atm.

You get consecration baseline, taunt, and realistically infinite mana. You also get -10% damage taken from the tree, which is vastly superior to defensive stance from warriors. And you also get 10min blessings.

You still retain all your broken CD, bubble/LoH, and you can self dispel.

You're also the golden child of TWoW, meanwhile you'll get constantly buffed.

So yeah, roll pala. It's by far the best tank on TWoW.
