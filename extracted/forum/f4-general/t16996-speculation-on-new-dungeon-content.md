# Speculation on new dungeon content.

## Post 116012 by Bonado — 2025-01-09T16:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116012#p116012 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With the recent teaser for new dungeon content like we got in Wailing Caverns, The Deadmines and Scarlet Monastery being added to Zul'Farrak and Razorfen Kraul I thought it'd be fun to speculate/give my wishes on what I'd like to see as a on again off again constantly making new characters leveller.

 *Also please add more bosses and quests to Razorfen Downs and Blackfathom Depths, no one ever wants to run them even though they have amazing loot  crying_turtle*

 **Razorfen Kraul**
A previously undiscovered opening within the den has been discovered with a sect of Thornspeakers hiding in the shadows twisting the nature around them to build upon the dungeon itself.

Two all new bosses!

Sentinel Gnargi
An "anti-melee" fight with frequent roots and Thorns damage against melee hits.
Very little Mail Armor and good Melee Two-Handed weapons drop in this dungeon so seeing this boss drop a Blue Two-Hand Mace with a bash proc for level 29 twinks and decent Mail Boots would be great!

Thornweaver Naggarma
An "anti-magic" fight with medium-high nature resist, an AoE healing reduction curse and dampen magic totems.
Without the rares spawning very little caster gear drops in this dungeon, it'd be great to see a generic caster dagger non-mace users, high spirit cloth legs or boots and a Shaman Totem recipe for early level Jewelcrafters!

New drops
- Totem of Raging Rubble- Shaman Totem recipe for Jewelcrafters that give a little bit of armor (20 - 40) and increases Earth Shock damage and Threat generated by Nature Damage by 5%, make the Shaman Tank real!

- Spiked Whomper - Two-Hand Mace with a chance on hit to stun a tiny bit of Stamina on top, idk if this would even be good but I hope the PvP twinks enjoy it.

- Thornweaver Set - Low level dungeon sets are always fun and what better than a leather tanking set, though I'd like it to encourage Shaman it'll probably end up as more of a druid set. All gear would have some Agility and Stamina and a minor Nature Damage thorns effect (1-3 damage per item coming to a total of 20 damage with the full set). This would have the major pieces drop from both the new bosses as well as the rest of the set available as random drops throughout the dungeon like other dungeon sets.
 **Set Bonuses**
 - (2) - +22 Nature Spell Damage.

 - (3) - Deal 10 Nature Damage when hit.

 - (4) - Increase threat generated by Nature Damage by 10%.

 - (5) - +10 Stamina and Agility.

 **Zul'Farrak**
In the back of Zul'Farrak a dark force can be felt as wicked Farraki Trolls have begun rituals to defy death itself and raise their fallen. Though adventurers had seen glimpses of this previously with the Witch Doctor now they delve into the Farraki Catacombs to stop it from the source.

Again two new bosses!

Risen Leader Ma'kar
Imagine fighting a Warrior in a full Vampiric set, lifesteal on melee hits, interruptible Drain Life and a Charge on a random party member.
With very little Mail gear dropping in the Dungeon it'd be great to see this boss dropping Legs or a Helm with 1% Vampirism on or even a 2% Trinket for Jewelcrafters as we're now getting into the higher levels.

Zujo the Crafter
The creator of the Undead within Zul'Farrak! This fight would have very weak skeletons spawning throughout that die pretty easily to AoE but can also overwhelm you just as fast.
Though there is some good Caster loot in the Dungeon most of it is on a single Boss, having more caster loot would be an absolute plus.

New drops
- Zandazar the Beckoner - If Melee Sul'Thraze Casters should be allowed something equally cool. This would be a Staff made combining the Main-Hand Sword and Off-Hand Book with a skill similar to Blade of Eternal Darkness, dealing Shadow Damage and restoring a bit of Health.
 - Misery's Edge - A Sword mainly for Warlocks, this would have good intellect and shadow damage increased. This would drop off of Chief Ukorz Sandscalp.

 - Book of Sorrow - A Book again intended for Warlocks that would increase pet damage by 5%. This would drop off the new boss Zujo the Crafter.

- Beating Heart - A Unique Trinket recipe for Jewelcrafters that gives +2% Vampirism on Melee hits, more incentives to become a Jewelcrafter!

- Risen Wrap Set - While Cloth wearers get quite a few sets from Tailoring there isn't any that drop in dungeons before the end game hits, this fills in that gap of after the early Tailoring set but before end game with a focus towards high Intellect and HP5.
 - (2) - + 24 HP5.

 - (3) - + 14 Stamina and Intellect.

 - (4) - + 34 Spell Power and Healing.

 - (5) - + 10% Health Regen in combat and Movement Speed when below 30% Health.
And that's some speculation and what I'd like to see from the new teased dungeon content. I'd love to hear thoughts and opinions!
