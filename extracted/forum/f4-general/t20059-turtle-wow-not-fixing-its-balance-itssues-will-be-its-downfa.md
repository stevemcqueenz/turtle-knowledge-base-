Turtle WoW historically was always a PvE-focused network. This is no surprise or shocking revelation—we spent most of our life as a RPPvE realm, rather than the multi-mode network we are today. For many years, our development was nearly entirely focused on various forms of PvE content, however we've recently posted regarding [upcoming PvP content and changes](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=19804).

You're not wrong that various people with different levels of experience have given their input on what we "should do" for PvP. Almost all of these are players coming from their own limited scope of experience as given classes within PvP on Turtle. This is valuable feedback—but it's incredibly egotistical to imply that any one player knows PvP best. This is why we've been so openly asking for feedback, as well as individually approaching active, veteran members of our PvP community for feedback.

This is not to suggest that I believe our PvP is perfect—as an active ranker myself I certainly have some complaints.
