# What about Russian localization?

## Post 148955 by Dimastasc — 2025-08-28T15:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148955#p148955 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Turtle WoW team,

I’ve recently noticed that you released an update regarding German localization. That’s great news, but it made me wonder: what about Russian localization?

Have you forgotten about us? I completely understand that it takes time and resources, but the question still stands. As you know, there are a lot of players on your server who understand and prefer Russian.

I can even tell you from my own experience: if there was Russian localization, even more people would play on your server. For example, three of my friends don’t want to switch to Turtle WoW because it’s uncomfortable for them to play without Russian, since they are used to it.

So please, consider this request Russian localization would definitely attract more players and make the game more enjoyable for a big part of the community.

Thank you for your attention!
