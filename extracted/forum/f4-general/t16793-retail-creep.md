# Retail Creep

## Post 114779 by Biotek — 2024-12-29T17:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114779#p114779 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Everyone!
This will likely be a controversial subject/opinion for many but here we go (let's keep it short)!

Leveling Speed
The new quests and zones are nice, especially in places like Silverpine which have gaps for multiple levels.
Doing even a couple of the new quests in already fleshed out areas can jump a character ahead of the leveling curve - doing all of them speeds things up dramatically. We should consider that new content should only be to fill gaps or add to "endgame".

Crafting
This combines with leveling speed. It still takes a ton of materials to be able to make anything usable by the crafter while leveling. Now we level faster, crafted gear is even less useful. This is a shortcoming in vanilla as well, exacerbated by the new XP options, but especially in retail. Crafting is essentially useless until endgame recipes are available.

 **>> Skills & Talents**
This might be the biggest one. I'll start by admitting I'm not well experienced in every vanilla class - I rarely played priest or warlock, for example. The current changes to skills and talents are making certain builds feel more like retail. The need to constantly press abilities and fill every single GCD was really only a thing for some classes in vanilla. It's the standard in retail and it's not only mind-numbing but the abilities are weaker to compensate.

There is also an apparent need to make every talent tree good at raid level pve, if not at everything. This is really diluting the feel of vanilla, not because more classes are optimal (they've always been viable) but because talents are being removed/replaced/redesigned. I might be part of a minority here, but I think the old talents should be treated gently and with caution. Give them *minor* tweaks at best, do not replace them. Instead, add new talents as needed to fill the "gaps" we are trying to patch. Let players make the decision to min-max or pickup utility/flavor talents.

There is a **lot** more to WoW then raids. Not everything needs to be centered around them! There is more to content than ever upwards as fast as possible.

Please be careful not turn TWoW into the mindless spamfest that is retail!

This is a forum so feedback and discussion is welcome, but please keep it civil and constructive!
