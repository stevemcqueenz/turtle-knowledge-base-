## Post 78632 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-11-24T23:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78632#p78632 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My personal experiences with the various classes in general PVP scenarios:   satisfied_turtle

 **Warriors:** The worst players in the game. They are the weakest class in PVP and I see these clowns consistently charge in and die in 5 seconds while simultaneously claiming they are "GigaChads in PVP, brah."

They're deeply entitled and will whine endlessly "WTF heal me bro" and require constant babysitting to even survive for more than those 5 seconds.
Overall a deeply selfish and horrible 'ball-and-chain' class that is unpleasant to be paired with.

 **Mages:** Awesome players who are usually very clever and extremely useful in a variety of ways.
Mage (Frost especially) is overpowered in PVP they are just soo good I can't sum it up in just a paragraph.

 **Rogues:** Usually whiny unlikable douchebags but at least they are often skilled/effective. Sap is extremely useful/OP in PVP so that ability alone makes them somewhat desirable to have on your team.

 **Druids:**  Typically rather dull-witted and useless. Oftentimes vapid kiddies play this class not because they like/understand the shapeshift mechanics but because they think 'animals r kawaii'. Many will stay in Bear Form even tho its pure dogshit in PVP as it does no damage and offers no significant utility.

 **Huntards:**  They are basically as dumb and bad as Warriors except at least they can do some damage and last longer due to not having to 'charge straight into the death zone' to deal damage.

 **Shamans:** Cool funky hipsters that want to play the least popular class in the game. Incredibly supportive and versatile class. A delight to have in your group.

 **Priests:**  Soft-spoken, considerate, and wise players. Incredibly supportive class. A delight to have in your group.

 **Warlock:** Among the smartest players. Offer some decent utility like Health Stones. Fear is overpowered. Basically they are pretty awesome and a delight to be paired with. Also they are the only class that hardcounters the overpowered Paladin menace.

 **Paladin:** BUSTED... but many mouthbreathing kiddies play this class cuz they heard its OP. So you can outsmart many of them. HOWEVER a smart Paladin is absolutely terrifying and will utterly dominate/humiliate opponents. And yes they have AWESOME utility/healing so they are lovely to be paired with.

I barely PVP but this thread looks fun. Trying to give my PVE impressions.

Hunter: usually a nice guy not taking the game too seriously. Dungeon starts, the guy is doing 3 other things at the same time, sometimes randomly disappearing to make a popcorn or to play guitar. Not the best teammate, no hunter in the history of the game has ever payed attention to the pet's stance. If you suddenly get attacked by half the dungeon, be suspicious about that lion pet called Cat.

Rogue: it has the silent and the edgelord subtype. Both can play the game pretty well, though. Silent subtype feels like an NPC but God, it kills the mobs well. Edgelord subtype tends to ragequit dungeons to gank somewhere.

Pala: There are the old pala players who sticked with the class for 20 years even if the class was bad. They are usually the gentlemanly type. Good at the game, good to be around them. They are like the 5 percent of the playerbase. 95 percent flocked to this class because of the buffs. A lvl 25 tank joined a 25-35 dungeon with awful items, leeroying through the instance and complaining about dying? Almost surely a pala. They got so used to being OP that they can't understand that sometimes they have to play somewhat well to avoid dying. Respect to holy palas though, they are cool.

Priest: they have two types, the good guy and the good guy with edgelord tendencies aka shadow priest. Both are good teammates and fun (altough a bit silent).Shadow priests occassionally start compaining about needing some PVE buffs.

Shaman: fun weirdos. Good ones are a blessing, bad ones put down windfury everywhere (even to 4 druids), spam chain heal or bloodlust and pray that the others actually manage something. They usually like weird jokes. They complain by far the most about needing some buffs. Their looting can be seen as ninja because they respec all the time from healer to melee to sometimes caster. Deep down they'd burn every windfury totem down.

Mages: the ones who play really well but usually talk a bit too much. Mana classes simp them for drinks, teams love them for damage and utility. Most mages think /party is some kind of blog and spam like noone else. Arcane subtype is a god though, they embraced the memes and became godlike.

Warriors: they have two PVE subtypes. Most common ones are the yolo warriors who are DPS but come as a tank to each dungeon for a quick LFT. Sometimes they put on a shield but not always. These adrenaline-filled people are more cautious than the noob paladin subtype because repair cost for a warrior is hell. After a while all of them has a phobia of dying and they all start leveling mages to finance the main char. They tend to sh*t-talk a bit. The other type is the 'I protect you' warrior, playing as a tank from the beginning. Very cool guys, usually with a huge game experience. At late-game guild tends to finance these guys' repair cost.

Druid: a mixture of shamans and hunters. Lovable hipsters not taking the game too seriously, walking through Azeroth as a moonkin. Usually very fun teammates. Unlike the hunters they actually tend to pay attention during dungeon. Also, one thing I noticed. 99 percent of druids is a blessing but the biggest @holes I've seen this game are somehow that 1 percent category of druids.
