Hey!

I liked the pros and cons from the indoor root post, there were pretty good arguments from both sides.
Decided to bring up another topic: hybrid tax.
In a mostly PVE-focused server should hybrid tax exist? (F.ex. should a melee DPS hybrid have a lower DPS than a pure melee DPS?).

Personally I can see it go both ways.

+ A hybrid has added flexibility and (usually) utility - also having an identical DPS as a pure DPS is overdoing it.
 - On a mostly PVE-raiding-focused server hybrids barely benefit from being hybrids since they specialize in one role for raid runs (not counting some off-heals or brainwashing-spec changes).

Usually, if I were to use your example of Melee, a Hybrid is below a pure one.

If anything it would be better to instead look at what might be contributing to power spikes - - Abilities and/or Talents and/or Equipment - - and alter them to a degree where they feel more balanced, and not overnerfed.

This is the entirely correct take. Hybrid classes have the *potential* to switch. But once they've switched, they're mostly locked into their respective spec. And once the fight starts and you can't swap gear anymore, you're on equal footing with "pure" classes.
The hybrid tax is having to collect multiple gear sets to be able to fulfill multiple roles adequately. There should not be a mechanical hybrid tax beyond that.

I can get behind specializations though. No class should be the best at everything, because it makes the game boring and raid comps stupidly one-sided. The same class shouldn't be best at single-target damage and aoe damage. The same class shouldn't be best at dps and tanking. But I do feel that every class or hybrid-role should have a niche where they excel without any artificial drawbacks ("hybrid tax") imposed to keep them worse than others "just because". There should be encounters where druids are on top, and there should be encounters for every other class the same way.

Wanna share my own take, too.
What I see often is people washing 'utility kit' and 'being a hybrid' together.

Another thing I noticed is that 'DPS trees are homogenized'. A class having 3 DPS trees doesn't mean it only has 1 tree but in this debate a paladin vs rogue argument would refer to builds as 3 vs 1.
It is to a degree the game's fault in my opinion: if a class has several DPS trees, each should have a unique way of dealing DPS, being optimal for different scenarios.
F.ex. imagine a rogue being able to choose between the trees of 'high burst damage', 'high maintained damage' and 'utilities and debuffs'. These are 3 thematically and gameplay-wise different builds. The game should highlight this a bit more though.

I agree that in a non-raid environment hybrids have an additional utility: being able to switch to bear and be a new tank or jump back and spam heal on the warrior are both extremely valuable. At PVP it is borderline disgustingly strong even if you are not geared for a role. Free heals are free heals, free armor is free armor.
In raids everyone has 1, highly-defined role, this advantage disappears there. Question is wether we balance to end-game PVE or to anything else?
I believe Turtle is PVE/RP first and foremost and most PVE enjoyers end up raiding with their main anyway, which is an argument for no hybrid tax. But it inherently griefs the PVP landscape or some other aspects, too.

What I'd def want for DPS classes to have their 3 builds feel more unique and doing distinctly different kinds of damage. AoE? Build-up damage? Burst? Cleaves? So many options.
Since the server highly focuses on raids, this could at least create balance there:
While a hybrid can decide which build he plays for a specific encounter and brainwashes/changes gear for that, a DPS could decide what kind of damage profile he needs for the same fight and respecs accordingly.
Hybrids giving a width of choice, pure DPS having a depth of choice - a hybrid can't really change between how he does the damage, he goes to the 1 tree for it. A pure DPS builds up his damage profile with talents like a gourmet. At least this is my vision about this.
