Hey howdy! I did some napkin math on this, which inevitably turned into non-napkin math.

 **TLDR:** Hitting Revered before level 40 without doing Teldrassil isn't feasible. Doing all of the Darnassus reputation quests in Teldrassil + Darkshore + Ashenvale *should* put you on track to hit Revered at 40/in the early 40's.

Now, for the numbers:

| ZONE NAME | REPUTATION QUESTS | TOTAL REPUTATION | AVERAGE PER QUEST |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teldrassil | 45 Quests | 4105 [Dwarf/Gnome/Nelf] 4516 [Human] | ~91 [Dwarf/Gnome/Nelf] ~100 [Human] |
| Darkshore | 57 Quests | 4525 [Dwarf/Gnome/Nelf] 4978 [Human] | ~79 [Dwarf/Gnome/Nelf] ~87 [Human] |
| Ashenvale | 39 Quests | 3550 [Dwarf/Gnome/Nelf] 3905 [Human] | ~91 [Dwarf/Gnome/Nelf] ~100 [Human] |
|  |  |  |  |
Dwarves/Gnomes/Nelves will be at least **6,180/12,000 Honored**.
Humans will be at least **7,399/12,0000 Honored**.

Bear in mind, these are kind of "bare minimum" numbers. Actual reputation per zone may be higher via things like breadcrumb quests, quests that begin/end in one zone but take place in another (i.e. quests that start/end in Darnassus but are completed in Teldrassil). et cetera. My own math says that 4,105 is the minimum earned for Teldrassil, but my Nelf Druid has just about run out of quests and is moving on to Darkshore at 5,595/6,000 Friendly. Between custom quests & quests that fall between the cracks on places like Wowhead, you *should* be 2-4k below Revered by the time you finish Ashenvale.
