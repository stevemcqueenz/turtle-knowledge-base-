# Waste of potential at zone expansions!

## Post 149228 by Kaktus96 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-29T21:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149228#p149228 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The last patch, 1.18.0, added an area in Dun Morogh called "Rugfords Mountain Rest".
Now personally I didn't think Dun Morogh needed more quests but at the same time more quests are also always welcome.

Today I checked out this area and also did the quest you get at the southern watchtower for it.
It's a simple Elite quest that you do towards the end of your Dun Morogh journey.

But I can't help but feel underwhelmed. The area that was added looks fantastic. The chairs and desks are missing in the Inn building and are used as defensive palisades outside which I think is a nice touch.
BUT, all this for... **one quest**? Just one quest where you kill an elite for the belongings of the former owner of the inn.
Also the story makes no sense. It says that this is a very frequented resting post for mountaineers but its very off the beaten track? Shouldn't a road lead there?

No quest for killing 10 Dark Iron Spies? No quest for retrieving stolen supplies? 2-3 quests should've been easy to put there. Not just one.

This isn't the only time this happened btw. In Duskwallow marsh they added "Westhaven Hollow" which also looks absolutely fantastic. Fits right into the zone.

 **One Quest**. Killing a bunch of undead and the orc necromancer that raised them.
No burning of corpses to stop the orc from raising more. No retrieving of mementos to lay the dead to rest?

Why all this work to design fantastic additions to zones for this lousy amount of quests?

Also a little off topic but please, in future updates, make [Loch Modan](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=20146) a proper alternative to Westfall. Westfall is completely overrun and people would really appreciate it if they didn't have to compete this much for everything in Westfall.
