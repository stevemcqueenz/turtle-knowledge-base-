# Item Icon Differentiation Project

## Post 125017 by Vrograg — 2025-04-02T02:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125017#p125017 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ever look at a Limited Invulnerability Potion and say, "Hey you remind me of another consumable I know," only to realize too late you just reapplied a Flask of the Titans? Have you looked at your Hourglass Sand and wondered if it was actually Hourglass Sand, or Deeprock Salt, or Salt of the Scorpok, or Un'goro Soil, or Dust of Decay or...?

Hi I'm Vrograg Fishslayer, a proud orc, philanthropist (to RP guilds), Youtube (micro-niche) personality, and proud News Orc for Turtle WoW. About year ago to the day I started a project to make it easier for players to distinguish their Shadow Oil and their Greater Shadow Protection Potion. Because, let's face it, Turtle WoW is changing, so shouldn't our item icons change with us too?

I have been slowly gathering information and input on key items that needed icon updates, and some key factors became clear based on input from you, the community.

 1. Some icons are way too over used
 2. Some items having icons reused make things confusing
 3. Custom Turtle WoW Items reusing old icons feels lame

So I endeavored on a journey, slowly creating some of my own icons. I'm not an artist, but I learned how to do some basic color pallet swapping, and how to utilize layers in image software. Before long I had a small portfolio that I brought to the team to have integrated into the game. They were receptive, and even loaned me the skills of some of their artists, Dane and Legal. Some months later we presented our work to the team and it was integrated into the game... without letting any of you know about it... and just kinda figuring it out. Classic Turtle   wary_turtle

Anyway I could keep waxing poetic about how proud I am of the work the artists have done, or how it goes to show that our community can do anything, but let's get down to brass tacks.

 **What is this all about?** - I wanted items to stop reusing the icon art, and I kinda hyperfixated on it.

 **Why only Items?** - Items first as a proof of concept. Tackling buff and spell icons is more tricky because it often interferes with addons, and thinking about tinkering with a spell database makes me scared.

 **Is there any rhyme or reason to these changes?** - Largely yes. I endeavored to keep the most commonly seen or used items as they were, and make the lesser known or used ones different, to make the change less shocking. In cases where multiple "big" items were in consideration, I'd try to keep one of them the same and the other similar in some basic way like color or shape.

 **Is it just consumables?** - Largely yes that was my initial focus, as it was a pain point for players, but my research uncovered a veritable treasure trove of some criminally overused icons, especially in the BG food items.

 **Will this break my addon?** Unlikely, though anything is possible. I did some limited testing, and also these are items, not buff icons, which addons are more likely to read than items, so you're probably good.

Now to the part you are all probably even looking at this for to begin with: So, what did you have changed?

I am largely not a fan of the changes to so many icons.

We play TurtleWoW mostly out of nostalgia factor. There's many more convenient and more modern games. There's even lots of WoW versions that are more easily accessible, more polished on modern clients. But we're here on Turtle because of the nostalgia for a 20yo game.

So keeping stuff the way it has been for 20 years has value in its own. There should be a burden to argue why something needs to be changed, not the default understanding that everything inconvenient or slightly confusing should be removed and updated to better quality of life.

There were egregious cases of possible confusions with terrible outcomes. LIP and Flask of the Titans absolutely should not share the same icon. I'm 100% for changing that. But I feel those cases are 2-3 at most.
For everything else I cannot understand why it was changed, especially why baseline Vanilla items had their icons changed. These are icons we have been deeply familiar with for decades. By and large items sharing icons was not a problem at all. How often do you carry Salt and Hourglass Sand? And even if you did - you don't consume Salt, so the danger is showing up to Chromag with a small stack of a low-level crafting material and accidentally putting Salt on your hotbar? I just don't understand. There was no confusion to begin with, why even change something that was not a problem? Especially drops, foods, crafting ingredients, consumables - it's absolutely fine that they share icons because they have different properties and stack sizes. Nobody was confused by this because of how little overlap there is.

I also liked the visual simplicity. Now you get dozens of new flashy icons, some with really grating style differences. It feels wrong. Even some items with no icon overlap have been changed like the Gingerbread Men from the winter veil cookies.
