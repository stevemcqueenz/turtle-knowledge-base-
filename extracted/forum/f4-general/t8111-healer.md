All 4 healer classes are quite viable in their heal specs.
Priest & paladin are usually considered to have the superior raid dps spec to shaman or druid. But all 4 classes have an at least somewhat useful dps spec. Shaman notably can use the same gear (T2.5) for both Elemental and healing, so he has an easier time gearing for DPS than the others

## Post 52173 by Reploidrocsa (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-07-03T12:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52173#p52173 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shadow priests are very good support dpsers with big mana and health regen.

If you chug mana consumables like crazy you can do very respectable dps as elemental shaman.

Druid can either go melee or ranged dps, but performs a bit lower. Nothing gamebreaking and No need to hold your threat (ripping aggro from tank is #1 cause of wipes in raids).

Paladin is insane in pvp but goes oom very fast. Perfect candidates for Nightfall or other chance on hit weapon debuffs.

In general, classes who can heal excel at being good supporting dpsers
