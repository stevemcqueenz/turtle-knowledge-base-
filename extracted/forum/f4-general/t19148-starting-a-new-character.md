Hey there! Our PvE realm, Nordanaar, averages between 6,000–8,000 players online at all times, though this can vary depending on the time of day. There are more Alliance characters than Horde.

Our PvP realm, Tel'Abim, averages 600–1,000 players online at all times. It has faction balance, meaning there's a system in place that prevents you from creating an Alliance character if the server detects that there are currently too many Alliance players.
