# Thank you for the 12-hour difference at the continents

## Post 123411 by Amyloriley — 2025-03-23T12:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123411#p123411 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Kalimdor, the Eastern Kingdoms. They have a 12-hour difference.
When it's night in Kalimdor, it's day in the Eastern Kingdoms. When it's night in the Eastern Kingdoms, it's day in Kalimdor.

Surely I assume this is to get people from all over the world play being able to log on during daytime or nighttime as they prefer; and be able to catch the fish that only spawn during certain cycles of the day.

But as a roleplayer myself, each time I take the ship from Kalimdor to the Eastern Kingdoms or back, it really feels like I'm traveling to the other side of the world. The world truly feels bigger that way. And I really appreciate it.

Thank you for coding that little thing in the game. :)
