As far as I understand it the green items are randomly generated. There's several criteria for the stats however. For example items ..."of the eagle would be with stamina and intellect", "gorilla - strength and intellect" and etc. The actual end results are based on a dice roll depending on the item level ( very similar to DnD )
So to sun it up, green items do not exist in the database, theres just conditions to what prefixes ( or otherwise what combination of stats the item can be) and the value is dice rolled
