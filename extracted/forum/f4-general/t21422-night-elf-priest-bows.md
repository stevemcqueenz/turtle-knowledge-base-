I finally figured out what my problem was and I thought I would share in case it happens to anyone else. I still don't know if this is a bug or glitch or if anyone else has experienced it. It seems like it's just me or maybe I am dumb lol.

Anyway. me and a friend finally figured out that if I right click a mob to start shooting at them it puts me into attack stance or whatever you call it and I guess it only assumed I am melee attacking so I would try to auto shot and it would say that I am too far away. Similarly, when I would try to searing shot, the searing shot would go off but auto shot would not start.

However, I finally figured out that if I either left click or tab target the mob auto shot and searing shot both work exactly as intended. So I don't know if I am dumb but I thought or assumed right clicking a mob would not have an effect whether the attack was ranged or melee. But it's fine.

I'm just so happy I finally figured it out with the help of my friend and I can finally use my bow properly!
