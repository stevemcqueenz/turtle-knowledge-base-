I can't speak on high elf classes as I play horde.  I will say that dual spec is a thing but it does cost ~300v gold or a donation to get the respec device.  I will add that respects are capped at 5g here so if you don't want to spend the 300g, you don't have to.

My best advice, if you don't want to play high elves, would be to level a druid.  If your heart is really set on HE, probably paladin would fit you best.  Hybrids are very viable here.

Best of luck to you and I hope you enjoy your time here with us on turtle!!

## Post 157407 by Catyngla (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-27T22:49:00Z

It depends on whether you want to go meta or not.

High elves' racials are good for mana based classes, especially casters (since clothing/enchanting suits casters the best and you'd get +15 ench)

I can't say for heal, but levelling as a tank is possible, I'm doing it. We're in high demand, and it is easy to get into dungeons. I feel healers are not far behind. Just questing may get a bit problematic.

Since you're new, look out for the challenges. They are to be taken at the character's creation. You might like some.

Another tip: unless you wanna raid asap, take time to level up. There is much more levelling content (zones, dungeons).
