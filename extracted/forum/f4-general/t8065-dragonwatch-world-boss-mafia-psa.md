# Dragonwatch: World Boss Mafia PSA

## Post 51856 by Dragonwatch — 2023-06-30T02:09:00Z

Greetings from Dragonwatch, a coalition of guilds formed to kill world bosses. Others call us a "world boss mafia". We’d like to clear up some misinformation that’s been going around Discord and this forum about world bosses.

History
Throughout 2022, <Thunder Ale Brewing Company> held a near monopoly on world boss kills because they were the largest motivated guild. However, they only invited guildmates and friends. Dragonwatch formed on the 14th January 2023 with the goal of expanding the reach of world boss loot across the server to many guilds, at a time when Turtle WoW’s population grew quickly.

Mechanics
World bosses are the largest scale co-operative effort in Turtle WoW. That’s because they’re rivalrous by design, and will not stay up for hours at a time. If we don’t kill a boss in 10 minutes, someone else will in 15 minutes. Contesting a spawn means following a two-step process:

 1. Scout the boss spawns. 24 hour vigilance requires a large team comprising multiple time zones.
 2. Rollout. When a boss spawns, we raise an alarm and fill the raid as fast as possible. The only way to quickly form raids across any time zone is to recruit a huge roster.

Leaking a boss spawn attracts both griefers and competition. We mitigate griefing by dropping PvP flags, but the best defense is to kill the boss before anyone else knows it spawned. Our low profile surrounding world boss spawns has prompted comments like “overly secretive” and “shadow government”, but the methods we employ aren’t secret.

Dragonwatch takes no official stance on how world bosses should function on Turtle WoW. We work together to kill them using existing mechanics.

Membership
Game designers add world bosses to MMOs to encourage players to work together, and we recruit most guilds that apply. Since forming Dragonwatch, the server population more than doubled from 2,546 to 6,203, while we expanded the availability of world bosses from one guild to 20:

We team up to optimally slay bosses as implemented, and if you have different or opposing goals, then organize your own raid. We invite the competition.

Hey! Thank you for the in depth set of questions. I wanted to first dispel the notion that we've only just been created; Dragonwatch has been active and killing almost every single world boss on spawn for approximately six months. Our gaps and failures have been due to our own laziness, and comprise of the world bosses with the shortest respawn (thus also meaning occasional burnout on said boss). We've been making good on what we've promised to do since January, now: significantly expanding the access of world boss loot. Now, onto the three questions...

 1. Guilds do not 'call dibs'. If the boss spawns, all member guilds have equal priority on invites to the raid. Because we have so many people eager to secure a kill, the only time we are at a disadvantage is if we somehow missed the boss' spawn or others have a preformed group ready. It's much easier to secure a kill with a group of hundreds of likeminded individuals of varying time-zones than it is to convince one singular guild to be ready to camp a boss. We encourage individual competitors to do so.

 2. Guild representatives are both responsible for inviting trustworthy members of their guild and ensuring these members do not break our rules. We have had Hydra Dominatus betray us before. Notably, they no longer kill world bosses.

 3. Speed and experience. Like our first point, having twenty people familiar with the mechanics within five minutes is better than having thirty random pickups who find out too late and don't know what to do. If we secure a tag, we secure a kill. The same cannot be said for someone unprepared.

Hopefully this answers your questions sufficiently. Feel free to posit more.
