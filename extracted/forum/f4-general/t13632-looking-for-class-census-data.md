From my Census Addon. Just keep in mind that a full census now takes 2+ hours, so this information is not complete but it should give a good estimate. The data is from while I was taking a Census, so the numbers are very slightly off and the total sum does not match exactly.

Total characters: 5445 (2943 @ 60)
Druid: 475 (240) 8% (8%)
Hunter: 728 (334) 13% (11%)
Mage: 854 (561) 15% (18%)
Priest: 471 (253) 8% (8%)
Rogue: 608 (318) 11% (10%)
Warlock: 378 (166) 6% (5%)
Warrior: 793 (389) 14% (13%)
Shaman: 376 (175) 6% (5%)
Paladin: 785 (531) 14% (17%)

 29,9% is Horde, 70,1% is Alliance. Keeping in mind that shamans are Horde only, and paladins Alliance only, 19,6% of the Horde is shaman and 25,2% of Alliance is paladin.
