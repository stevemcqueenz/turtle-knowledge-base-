## Post 91749 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-31T08:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91749#p91749 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I did some warrior offtankin in raid but not much. Can give you a general idea but not sure that what I say is perfect.
+hit is your best friend, it has to go until 9% (maybe 6% with a +weapon skill class, my warrior was a tauren). Think about it: you generate rage and threat both with hits, you have to make sure that you hit.
After hit it's more up to you but I had more problems with threat generation than with survivability (specially if raid DPS likes to pump and they already have some purples). I'd prioritize stats helping you hit bigger and generate threat. +threat enchants are also a good idea.

So:
hit chance (objectively) > threat generation (I think) > survivability

Maybe use some consumes for added stamina etc (don't overdo it) to get an okay survivability but it's the easier part imo
