I think part of this is a LOT of people think that everyone who plays on a private server is a WoW veteran who misses vanilla.  For MANY players (myself included) that is not the case.  I play private because I didn't get to play WoW while growing up, and I want to see the sights and experience the content.

So, as a 'NOOB', I will say that some of us just don't care to do it at 100% efficiency.  I did WoW classic back in 2020, and I enjoyed the leveling experience, but was unable to find people to do group content with.  So, since I had an account, I decided to try retail.  I got to the first dungeon, and.... I got kicked for not being good at my class (Dwarf priest).  Now, I was playing fine in all of the PVE events, but in the FIRST group content that I experienced I was kicked, without any explanation.  I didn't know where to go, I hadn't healed a group before because I had only done PvE.  and the other players just talked crap and kicked me.  happened 3 times before I got to finish the dungeon.  and I promptly stop playing retail.

And for reference, I have healed in savage content in FFXIV and was a healer in a weekly group in SWTOR up through Shadow of Revan.  I -CAN- do the content.  But some players expect everyone to be perfectly geared and knowledgeable of every dungeon the first time they do it.  It is just not realistic.

So, I would suggest 3 things:

 1) find out if the people you are playing with even know the content.  If it is their first time, then expect them to want to look at everything.  They don't have memorized WoW muscle memory about how to do everything like veterans.    Don't make their first time a bad time by rushing them.  and if you DONT want to play with total newbs, ask before starting, and politely leave if needed (and say why, don't just give an Irish goodbye)

 2)if they are stating that they know the content and are making rookie mistakes (like the tank over pulling example), try to teach them, but if they don't learn/listen then tell the group why and either kick them or leave. but dont expect too much for everyone all the time.  I know in FFXIV I am a pretty good healer, but sometimes I drop the ball (everyone gets distracted).

 3) if you have the issue all the time of the dungeons going too slow/the group wiping, then step up and tank yourself.  the tank gets to set the pace, not a DPS who is impatient.
