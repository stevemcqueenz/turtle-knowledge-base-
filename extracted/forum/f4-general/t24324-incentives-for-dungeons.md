Because dungeons are rarely worth your time at most levels.

As long as the LFT doesn't teleport you to the instance, there are no incentive to run a dungeon, with some exceptions like the famous ones that are near capitals, and rather easy to access.

And even with all that taken into account, assuming that you managed to join/form a party, you're not even sure the dungeon will run smoothly, that you'll get any item out of it, or quest completed, or that one of the player will not just disappear for whatever reason, in which case you're totally f***ed because you can't just teleport a new player in, so the run is literally wasted, as well as your time.

Also, unless you have like a "magic item" that can be BiS for 40 levels or something, the rewards are 99% not worth spending your time in a dungeon. It will be literally obsolete the next day, after you grinded 5 extra levels.

Meanwhile, running regular quests and killing monsters is predictable, abundant, and has little to no variables.

Running a dungeon to level is just objectively a waste of time. The vast majority of people understand that instinctively.

I'm against instant teleports through a LFD system. The moment LFD was introduced, people stopped knowing where the dungeon entrance is or in what zone it even is. All form of player interaction went to shit, and the best example for that is retail.

LFD also sapped away a lot of the open world, as people would just queue up for dungeons to level up faster, so finding people for elite quests will be hard. You can use WotlK servers as a reference for that.

In my personal opinion, the smoothest way to appease both parties is the following:

- Characters need to visit and click on a Meeting Stone in order to unlock it.
- Once unlocked they can:
- [1] Be summoned to that meeting stone by party members.
- [2] Summon allies (that have unlocked the meeting stone) to it.

 **What we achieve with this is the following:**
 - Encourages people to explore the dungeon entrance.
 - While doing so, they may naturally find other people and form a group on the spot.
 - It doesn't take much time, and gives an additional optional objective for a given zone.
 - Once unlocked by the character, they can be summoned from anywhere on the open world.

The reason it's character locked and not account locked, is simply to encourage exploration with the newly created characters. Otherwise people will just rush this with their 60s and never both with it again, which defeats the purpose.
