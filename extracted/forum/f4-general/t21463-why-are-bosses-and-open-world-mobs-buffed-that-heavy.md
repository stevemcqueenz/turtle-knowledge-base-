## Post 148762 by Aloka — 2025-08-27T20:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148762#p148762 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey,

I know, here many try hard people who like to push challenges into the limit. I read that this is the reason why the last patch buffed dungeon, raids and open world NPC heavily.
But why there is no space left for casuals? I know, today it is a shame to not want a big challenge and like the calm casual chilled mode with easy quests and beginners content.

Since this patch launched, BWL bosses are quite harder than they where in SoD. Hosting a PUG raid without gearcheck or special requierments to the raid members, does not make fun this way. We spend 4 hours in BWL and killed 3 bosses...
Never made such an experience and I'm playing WoW since 2005.

Lovely Turtle Team, can you please let there be any easy mode space for older and casual players where the NPC are not buffed up to 10% or more in lifepool and damage output?
Or at least, give better /more loot or any other kind of reward for making everyone being faced into harder challenges by force?
For me as older casual player, it's just pure frustration to have no option going a chill mode and easily clearing the beginners content that Molten Core and BWL should be.

Maybe you can bring hardmodes for the pro gamers, like Blizz did in SoD. Than people have the choice to stay in easy mode or went for the challenge and receive some special loot for solving it.
But wiping 4 hours on totally out of control BWL bosses which drops the same 2-3 boring items as always, does not feel worth it.
