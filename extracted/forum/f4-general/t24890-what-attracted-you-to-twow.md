# What Attracted You to TWoW?

## Post 175638 by Captain4k (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-04-18T14:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175638#p175638 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Legendary is the word that I feel most accurately describes Turtle WoW. The day when Twow chooses to close its doors, it will go down in gaming history as the undisputed greatest WoW private server of all time. The amount of love, care, passion and enthusiasm put into this project is noticeable in every aspect of the game, and is much appreciated by it's playerbase. You can really tell the leadership and development teams are fans and players of the game.

The future is uncertain but this isn't meant to be a doom post. I want people to share their favourite things about the server. What attracted you to Twow and what made you stay and decide to make it your home?

I'll start.

What initially attracted me was the playable high elves and the custom leveling zones. I played a blood elf paladin on retail but I was always of the opinion that elves didn't thematically fit in with the horde. I had wanted to play as an alliance high elf ever since seeing them in Dalaran and at Trial of the Crusader in the Wrath of the Lich King expansion. I had also always wondered what those unfinished areas of the world map would look like if made available. When I saw that Turtle WoW provided both of these features, I was instantly sold.

But what made me stay was actually the community. The players were friendly in game and more than willing to help me feel comfortable and get back into the game after not playing for so many years. Every zone I went to I saw players farming, questing and having fun. Tents at the gates of Stormwind with people actually socializing. Not only was this server active, it was thriving.

There were community led events like transmog fashion shows and even a news orc on youtube that announced everything you needed to know about current and upcoming patches. I felt a nostalgic feeling I hadn't felt since I was a teenager, just getting into the world of warcraft for the first time. Almost like I hopped in a time machine and went back to 2007. That was when I knew Turtle WoW was my forever home.

It was 2021 and WoW classic was coming to an end.
My guild just killed Kelthuzad some weeks ago, they decided to give me the Corrupted Ashbringer that droped 2 weeks before TBC.
And at this time when everybody in the guild was hyped and joyful, I was extremely sad.

Firstly because I never liked TBC, was never attracted to it. I don't like the idea of a broken world in space. Don't like that it isn't Azeroth and that it shrinks the world. Don't like the too violent powercreep (green replaces purple). I hate flying mount. I wanted to de Naxx again.

Also, Blizzard was doing their shit once again : with TBC came level boost, paid toys and other marketing ploys they promised to not do in classic back in 2019.

Last nail in the coffin : my guild master sais "in TBC there is only 25 spots for raid, not 40, and raids are harder. We want to tryhard more. So we gonna rush the leveling part and then each people needs to be good enough to justify their spot in the raid."

I felt an immense fatigue. I didn't want to struggle to play with my mates for an expansion I didn't like. And I didn't want to see my chill guild, safe space for jokes, beer nights and friendship, become a "no talking allowed we serious we concentrate we hardcore people" toxic space. I liked them too much to see this change happen.

So the day of the opening of the Dark Portal, I accompanied them to the blasted lands and waved goodbye. I didn't explain much, just said I didn't feel like playing TBC and prefered to go to Era. Though I splitted my char in 2 (paid for the tbc copy) anyway.

I played in Era for a bit, but it was a wastelands. Everybody was gone to TBC because of the hype. Sometimes down the line, they did come back, when lots of them they understood TBC/WOTLK were less fun than the original experience, but for now I sat almost alone in Orgri. The game was broken and I was tired.

So I looked for a private server with the promise that it will be chill, RP, centered around leveling and not raiding.
I found turtle wow.

At this time, there was 300 players online, and only 3 modifications if i remember correctly :
 - you could put a tent down. We put it in gurubashi ring, and it was a meeting spot for all people as there was a tp to the ring. I loved it, what a perfect idea to bring people together and have chill discussions. And stranglethorn is beautiful.
 - there was a "turtle mode" and turtle mount. The mode wasn't called "slow and steady" if i recall correctly, and the mount had a name. You got it automaticaly, sent to your spellbook.
 - the first guilds had little homes in the world, and the tabard TP to it. After a long time playing alone, my first guild was a RP guild only for high elves. Its home campment was in Loch Modan, and still is ! I went there recently to check.

It was such a nice little server. I did long pauses, met people, leveled toons... but often disappeared for months because of work and life.

In 2025, I forced myself to finish the leveling of my immortal druid boomkin called Laitfraise. I always loved and played boomkin in classic, but as a second toon (cause it was not welcomed in raids). Here, I met Loredoria from the guild Smokey Tokers, who was friendly and said they needed a boomkin. Unusual but welcomed. So I came.
It was a return to the chill semi-core guild experience I loved all these years ago. Raiding was half chill/joke, half serious, so it motivated me to try, farm the consos once again weekly, and play regularly.

And I loved to see the server evolved from one tent one turtle to massive zones, raids, dungeons, gear innovations... you guys did so well.

Now we moved to another vanilla+ server as a guild, and it's cool.

Thanks Turtle, thank you Torta for this long journey.
