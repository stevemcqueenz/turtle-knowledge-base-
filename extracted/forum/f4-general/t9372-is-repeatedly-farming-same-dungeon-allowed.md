## Post 61404 by Aydea (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-08T05:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=61404#p61404 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

That is indeed allowed, there is no rule preventing you from running the dungeon you want over and over again. However, be advised that there is a maximum cap on the amount of instances (i.E. Dungeons) that you can enter per hour. **This is based on your account, not your character!**

Also, note that this does counts the amounts of times that you enter a dungeon even if you immediately exit again. You can test this if you like on a day you know you won't run any dungeons. Just go to a dungeon and go in and out again until it prohibits you with an error message saying that you have entered too many dungeons instances.

If that happens then you need to wait for a little while. This counter resets every hour I believe.

Hope this helps.
