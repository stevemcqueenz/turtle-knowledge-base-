Astalor Sunsworn is the patriarch of House Sunsworn and a current member of the Regency Council of Alah'Thalas. One of the surviving nobles of Quel'Thalas, Sunsworn is amongst those most opposed to Vereesa Windrunner, having aspirations to ascend to the throne himself and establish his own dynasty to replace House Sunstrider as rulers of the Quel'dorei.
