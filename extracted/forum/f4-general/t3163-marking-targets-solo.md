## Post 19276 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-06-21T10:58:00Z

Well, I know this isn't what you were asking, but /target [target] *does* help.  For example, if you're wandering around Stormwind Keep and you "/target Anduin", his name will appear over his head (if you "show target name" enabled in Interface Options) and a selection glow will appear under his feet.

This can also be used from greater distances, the side-effect being that the target's portrait only appears when you're within 100m of them, or so.

Hopefully, someone else can help you with an actual AddOn, though.
