 **Data Source:** Data collected using the Census+ addon. The data was over approximately 6 months. This report has been updated with more comprehensive data over time. his represents a sample of players who were online during the collection periods on the Nordanaar realm.

Total Characters Scanned: 52784. *(Slight discrepancy because of screenshot times)*

 **Introduction:**
This report analyzes a snapshot of character data collected using the Census+ addon on Turtle WoW's Nordanaar realm. While not a complete server census, the data gathered provides a significant sample (52784 characters scanned in total) that offers insights into the active player population's racial and class distribution, as well as progression towards level 60.

 **Summary of Key Findings (Based on Sampled Data):**
- **Racial Distribution:** This sample shows a significant preference for Alliance races, which collectively make up the largest portion of the characters listed (approx. 63.3%). Humans, High Elves, and Night Elves are the most numerous individual races. Goblins are the least represented.
- **Class Distribution:** Paladin appears as the most popular class in this dataset, closely followed by Warrior and Hunter, together accounting for over 41% of characters listed. Warlock (8.10%) and Shaman (8.44%) are the least frequently observed classes among online players.
- **Level 60 Progression:** The percentage of characters reaching level 60 within this sample varies significantly by race and class. High Elves, Humans, and Night Elves show high rates of reaching max level among races, while Goblins have the lowest. Mages, Paladins, and Priests have high Lvl 60 rates among classes, contrasting with lower rates for Warlocks, Hunters, and Shamans.

 **Analysis and Discussion:**
The Census+ data collected provides insights into the active player base on Turtle WoW's Nordanaar realm. The significant overrepresentation of Alliance races, particularly Humans, High Elves, and Night Elves, compared to Horde races, is a notable trend in this sample (Alliance constituting approximately 63.3% of the sample). The popularity of Paladin, Warrior, and Hunter classes aligns with typical preferences for accessible or strong performing roles. The varying Level 60 completion rates across races and classes may reflect differences in leveling efficiency, gameplay appeal, or the types of players drawn to them. The overall average Level 60 completion rate in this larger sample is approximately 63.8%. The data from this sample suggests a vibrant community with clear preferences within the available race and class options.

 **Conclusion:**
Based on this sample of **52784** Turtle WoW players scanned using the Census+ addon, the active population shows a strong concentration in Alliance races, particularly Humans, High Elves, and Night Elves, making up nearly **63.3%** of the sample. Paladins, Warriors, and Hunters are the most frequently observed classes, representing over **41%** of the class distribution. While overall Level 60 completion rates among sampled characters are around **63.8%**, there is notable variation, with classes like Mage, Paladin, and Priest, and races like High Elf, Human, and Night Elf showing a higher proportion of max-level characters compared to less popular options or those with lower completion rates like Warlock, Hunter, Shaman (classes) and Goblin, Troll (races).

 **Disclaimer & Data Evolution**
- This report is based solely on data collected using the Census+ addon over specific periods while online. The data presented here has been updated since the initial collection period (Currently sitting a little over 6 months now) to reflect a larger and more recent sample.
- The data collection is an ongoing effort, and this report may be updated again in the future as more comprehensive data becomes available.
- It's important to remember that this data represents a sample of players active during the collection times and is not a complete or official server census. Character numbers, distributions, and percentages can fluctuate over time due to various factors including player activity, server population changes, and the timing of data collection.
