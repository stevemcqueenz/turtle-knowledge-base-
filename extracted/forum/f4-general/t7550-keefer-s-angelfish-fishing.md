## Post 48260 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-31T14:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48260#p48260 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

That fish is part of the Strangelthorn Fishing Extravaganza, the weekly fishing tournament. You can only catch it on Sundays from 13:00-15:00 server time in the "School of Tastyfish" pools off the coast of STV. It is one of the rare catches that reward you items at [Fishbot 5000](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=15079). The drop chance is about 1:450, so depending on your luck it can take many weeks to get a specific rare catch.
