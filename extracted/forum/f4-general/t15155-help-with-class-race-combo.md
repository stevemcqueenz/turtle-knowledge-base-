## Post 102983 by Zulnam (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-10-12T20:03:00Z

I recommend prot paladin.

Easy to level, you can kill 4+ mobs at once, if they are melee, with almost zero downtime.

Easy to tank. In dungeons just go in the middle of a group, with retribution aura, and pop a max level consecration, then a lvl 1 consecration. that's it. you're set. you might have to run around occasionally when someone gets aggro but now they have a taunt as well via talents, so not a big deal.

Always wanted. Log in at every time of day, and odds are there will be a group needing a tank for a dungeon you're interested in.

In raids it is a different story and you will have to work a lot harder, but for dungeons paladin is probably the chillest tank.

As for race, if you don't care and just want whatever makes your life easiest, go human. The +5 sword/mace racial gives you a good increase to hit percentage. just keep in mind it will most likely change in the upcoming major patch as they announced sweeping changes in regards to weapon skill racials.

Second best is probably high elf, since its active racial allows you to quickly generate mana. useful in a pinch.

Dwarf is more suited for pvp or poison-heavy encounters. Although it is the sexiest one.
