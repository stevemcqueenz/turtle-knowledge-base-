# My strange thoughts

## Post 72550 by Xiaochichi1020 — 2023-10-21T09:57:00Z

I have some strange ideas about playing WOW for so long. It feels like everything revolves around changing the relevant settings in the game to reflect playability, such as talents, dungeons, skills, festivals, etc. So is there another way? For example, the storyline of game content, I think this is Azeroth's absolute strength. Enable players to participate more in the storyline process and push the game forward?

If the storyline of Azeroth continues to advance, assuming that the next expansion is still the opening of the Dark Gate and entering Outlands, can we set up a more epic scene before moving on to the next expansion. For example, categorizing the Dark Gate as an area rather than a dungeon. In this area, there are numerous small monsters and bosses, whose health, mechanism, skills, and difficulty completely surpass those of existing dungeons. To overcome these, most people who need this server and numerous guilds work together. Only by defeating them can we officially enter the next stage of the expansion pack, otherwise we will never be able to enter. Simply put, it means creating and setting up an expansion, but opening it requires the efforts of almost all players, otherwise it will be permanently sealed.

The motivation to attract players to complete these tasks is a more interesting and rich content. Of course, this will quite test the game team. Otherwise, players will settle for the status quo.

Many strange ideas cannot be fully expressed here, they can only briefly describe a certain general aspect. If you don't understand, we apologize.
