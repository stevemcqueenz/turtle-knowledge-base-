# Most likely controvertial PVP suggestions

## Post 71274 by Siflisk1 — 2023-10-16T15:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71274#p71274 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Suggested Change:
CC:
Polymorph, Fear, Sap, and Seduce: 75% reduced damage taken while under the effect of these abilities.
Stun: 25% reduced damage taken while under the effect of these abilities.

Getting blasted from full to dead while CC'd is not fun.  CC should take people out of the fight, not allow you to effortlessly kill them.

Level based resistances:
These should be removed for PVP, especially on a new PVP server. If the only thing keeping a group of 30s from beating a 60 ganker is non-stop resists, it shouldn't exist.  Lower levels should be able to outplay higher levels if they can.  This also helps with lower level battlegrounds.

Gear Stat Scaling:
Characters in PVP should have their gear stats scaled.  The BG should get the highest characters iLVL and the lowest iLVL and scale the lowest person up to 85% of their iLVL. Everyone else in-between being scaled accordingly between the two values. iLVL has a lower cap of 90% of players level to prevent abuse by equipping really low level stuff.

Example: Top Player 80 iLVL,  bottom player 60 iLVL.  Bottom gets scaled up to 68, everyone in-between gets scaled between 68 and 80.

PVP Items:
PVP gear should be bought with Honor.  You gain honor based on your rank.  You get a discount on items for your rank and lower.
For example:   Discount (50%)
Rank     Cost     Gain Per week
R14     10000     5000
R13      8000      4000
R12      6000      3000
R11      4000      2000
R10      2000      1000
R9        1000       800
R8         800        600
R7         700        400
R6         600        300
R5         500        200
R4         400        100
R3         300        75
R2         200        50
R1         100        25

Someone who caps out at rank 10 can get all their gear in ~52 weeks. Someone magically at R14 would take 6 weeks at fastest.  Numbers can be tweaked, but the concept keeps more people playing because they know they'll eventually get there.
