I don't know if you can get large bags early unless you know people who can help you with money or bags or you have a high level character and send yourself things through the mail. But I never had problems with inventory in the early content. I do get my bags full when you have 6-slot bags for the first 10+ levels, but I sell things whenever I get the chance and come upon larger bags right when it's  needed. I only spend money on AH for larger bags well after levels 30-40.

I think it's a problem only if a person is a hoarder or just lazy and doesn't sell junk often enough. I never saw this as an issue in WoW, I've seen it in different F2P P2W MMORPGs where inventory space is limited and you need to grind heavily or pay real money to unlock more inventory, but never in WoW.
