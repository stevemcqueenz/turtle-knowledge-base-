# Beta Conclusion & Final Rates

## Post 397 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2018-10-03T14:19:00Z

Greetings, Tortollans!

A huge thank you to everyone who participated our beta! You helped us to discover and close issues like:

- Transport movement issues

- Pathfinding problems

- A large amount of minor quest bugs

- Escort quest waypoint fixes

- Correction of invalid item sell prices

- Adjustment of XP rates
Bug tracker: [viewforum.php?f=16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewforum.php?f=16)

You will be hopefully satisfied with the results which we achieved together!

We came to the conclusion that the beta rates were exactly what we were looking for. Players were motivated to do quests to gain a bigger amount of experience.

The final rates will be the following:

- Quest rates: x1

- Creature kill rates: x0.5

- Rested XP bonus: x0.5

 **Further Achievements:**

- 1-9 PvP brackets are simply hilarious as we expected them to be! We can't wait for the first lvl9 BiS gear guides on our forums!

- We received a lot of applications and are currently reviewing those, our family is growing!
