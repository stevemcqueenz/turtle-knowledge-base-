[https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Stra ... travaganza](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Stranglethorn_Fishing_Extravaganza)

The tourney runs from 2pm - 4pm server time. The server time is UTC +0 and you are in UTC -8, so that is 6am - 8am for you. Do not be in Booty Bay when it starts. Go out on the shore somewhere so you can get to the fishing pools as quickly as possible. It does help to set your hearth to Booty Bay, then you can hearth back quickly when you get 40 Tastyfish. Turn them into Riggle Bassbait, he's a goblin wearing fishing waders standing on a wooden box.

Edited to add:  Don't forget to have plenty of bag space, the Tastyfish will need 2 spaces and you'll fish up other fish and junk also.

Good luck!
