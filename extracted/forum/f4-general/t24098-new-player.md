## Post 169449 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-02-26T13:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169449#p169449 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Class and spec isn't that big a deal. You can more or less play whatever you want and find some level of success. And even if you're not pumping like the best of them, the community is chill enough to bring along the less performant players, almost 100% of the time.

PvP? You can try it, but I'd recommend not putting much time into it. It's an unbalanced bursty mess and most people will vastly outgear you. Also, the only point you'll find PvP on the main server is typically at 60 in BGs, unless you go out of your way to queue in like... The 10-19 bracket, or pick up warmode. But warmode doesn't get you PvP. It gets you ganked by bored 60s or some random rogue while you're busy with a mob.
