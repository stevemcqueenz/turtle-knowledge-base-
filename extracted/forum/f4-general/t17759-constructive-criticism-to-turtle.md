# Constructive Criticism to Turtle

## Post 121082 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-27T05:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121082#p121082 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ok, I've been watching a certain open beta on a certain server that I'm not going to mention here or make comparisons, but I will point out certain flaws in the old content that Turtle gave us in patches prior to 1.17. 0, since while the content may be interesting, the development of the world feels... incomplete.
What do I mean by this?
That the terraforming and development of certain things are... half-baked.
Let's start with the most obvious: Stormwind.
What's the problem with Stormwind?
While it has the docks, it's noticeable that the backporting of Lich King to Vanilla gave several errors, the Barber Shop is there and has no purpose and also has some buggy animations, you can't sit down and the object that indicates the Barber Shop is poorly animated.

Next, custom zones that are old.
Which ones do I mean?
Tirisfal Uplands
Hawks Vigil
Dun Agrath
Ronae'thalas

Etc.
All of these zones feel incomplete and not very detailed, I won't deny that their lore may be good, but the detail of the world feels incomplete.
And to focus on Ronae'thalas, that zone was introduced in 1.17.0 and I understand that its development was left incomplete, being half-done and without any purpose.

The objective of this is to emphasize that although Turtle has introduced new zones, it is clear that the older ones have development problems due to perhaps inexperience or lack of tools that were already in place to develop Thalassian Highlands or Gilneas, which look in better condition compared to the older zones.

I think that a patch should be focused on being able to develop or polish the old zones better before developing new zones and thus get more out of the work that, although it has already been done, could be better today.

Last but not least, the minimap.
There is a very noticeable difference between the original map and the custom map, perhaps they could try to redo old areas of the minimap, since for example, when they introduced Alah'thalas, the difference was not as noticeable as in the Gilneas minimap, and the colors are better and it is clearer.

I've made this review based on what I've seen in the beta on another server and these are things I'd love to see in Turtle.   smiling_turtle_head

By the way... ahem, Orgrimmar could have some Zeppelin areas inside... just a suggestion.   hiding_smth_turtle_head
