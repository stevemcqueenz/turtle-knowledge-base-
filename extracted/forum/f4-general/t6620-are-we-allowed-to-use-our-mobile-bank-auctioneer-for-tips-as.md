It's most likely not against any rules.  When I started selling T0.5 Boss summoning services I had the same confusion as you on if it was ok to do so (the new rule changes are pretty much to prevent selling BOP items).  What I did was write a ticket in game asking specifically if it was against the rules or not and took a picture of the GM response.  If theres any fallout from selling services you have something to fall back on.
My GM response went as follows:
 **"Hello, Summoning Services and Selling BOP items are two different activities.  Selling Summoning Services is allowed. Selling BOP items is not allowed."**
