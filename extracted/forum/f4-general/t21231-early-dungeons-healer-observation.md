Probably depends a fair bit on the pace as well as the person playing the healer. I play druid, and I don't feel like I have any particular issues with running out of mana. So I think it depends on how efficiently you are spending your mana. If you only have your max rank of your healing spells in your cast bar and you cast a heal for 400 hp when you only needed to heal a 100, then yes you will run out of mana. Use lower rank heals to heal the chip damage and conserve your mana.
