Everything in Ascension is too convenient, too easy.

All of my characters have aspect of cheetah, have riding skill increases, can port, the open world might as well not exist.

It's too easy to run back to your body, night elf racial useless.

Warlock summons useless.

World buffs makes warlock underwater breathing useless, walk on water useless, that underwater form useless, undead underwater racial useless.

 3X XP gain, teleport to dungeon, super fast rides, too easy, too easy, too easy.

Soulshards removed, more homogenization, too easy.

Power creep.

Everyone's at endgame doing instances with gear well above crafted gear usefulness, this makes all professions useless.

They really dug a hole for themselves.
