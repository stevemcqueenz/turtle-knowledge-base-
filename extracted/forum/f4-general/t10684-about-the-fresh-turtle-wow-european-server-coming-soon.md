 1) Vanilla+. It's basically Vanilla, but a few cherries on top here and there, nothing crazy. You'll eventually discover a few new sub-zones, quests, items, recipes, talents, class abilities, leveling challenges etc. And rares seem to be buffed here.

 2) Yes, very. Turtle isn't perfect, but it largely keeps that Vanilla vibe, while expanding upon it. It has given me some of the best WoW-fun I've ever had. They thankfully also crack down on bots and RMT here, while actually engaging with the community. It will be interesting to see what happens next, however, because now Turtle has become so big that several sub-groups want to pull the game in different directions, which might annoy the original, tight-knit community.

 3) No one knows, but the devs claim their projections show both the main server and the pvp server will be 'healthy'. Fresh PvP servers tend to die off a bit after after a few months. My guess is that there will be a few thousand on both servers (3-5k) after some time, especially given that more Chinese are supposed to transfer off.

 4) Again, for PvE there's nothing crazy afaik. Enhance still struggle, while Feral and Paladin is in a better state. Shadow Priests no longer have much mana issues. On Turtle no one really cares what you play; just play what you want.

In PvP a lot of people are complaining about Paladins because they now deal decent damage and have a powerful burst; and they're very numerous on the Alliance so they can become annoying. Feral is also super strong, and Spriest is also good. The problem with buffing hybrids, which used to struggle in dps roles, is that they can become too powerful and flexible in PvP with all their tools, so we might see some of that here.

I'd urge you to NOT play Paladin and to support the underdog faction; thank you hehe :))
