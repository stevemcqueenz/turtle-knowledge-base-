Pepesmite actually explained some of their GMs’ processes in another post: [LINK](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=4&t=11589#top)

You’ll need to scroll a bit unfortunately- I couldn’t get a direct link on mobile. Here’s the most relevant bit though:
> After the GM provides a response, they review the chat logs to assess the content and manner of the conversation. If any violations are found, the severity of the violation determines whether the GM takes note of the player or imposes a punishment. If a player receives a warning, repeated violations may result in further disciplinary action.

In short: TWoW’s GMs *do* investigate logs after reports & issue appropriate punishments, even though the only part of the interaction that players see is “block this person”. I do think their GM responses may want to emphasize this more though.
