## Post 36001 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-02-24T19:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36001#p36001 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The best thing you can do to enjoy your time here is don't worry about "how to do X properly" or "where to go for best gear" or things of that nature. Turtle very much is built upon the idea that you should be enjoying the adventure, taking things as they come, exploring and discovering new things.

You will enjoy it most if you try to meet people and make new friends, and don't be afraid to try something different. The meta is primed to be shaken up. The goal of playing on Turtle is to have fun.
