The thing about Paladins is that they are meant to be Tanky even while Healing. And in PVP / BGs you are supposed to wear plate anyway.

So you are the most tanky healer but you are also capable of putting pressure on people just by swapping to a 2h weapon with all the offensive buffs that Paladins got.

So no, I would not say that all healers are equal in PVP.
