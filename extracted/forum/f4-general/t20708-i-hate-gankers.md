Before the lifeless PvPers come along and argue, "No, you can disable PvP whenever you want and save yourself the gankers."

I find it unfair that using a game mechanic requires you to tolerate some fat, lifeless guy coming to screw you over just because you have PvP enabled so you can face people of your level.

A few hours ago, there was a level 19 paladin—I was level 15—facing a hunter. I wasn't planning on attacking him until the paladin attacked and killed me. Then we started facing him off with the hunter. It was a fair and balanced fight, and it was fun.

It stops being fun when a level 60 rogue starts ganking you when you're level 15 and trying to kill mobs and level up. Those with War Mode complain about people without PvP enabled attacking them, but they also abuse this mechanic. When they go to kill others with War Mode enabled, they're simply leveling and are much lower level than them.

Unfortunately, I'll have to disable PvP so I can level up peacefully without anyone bothering me until I reach level 60 and then just use /PvP whenever I want to PvP, because honestly, gankers are a pain in the ass. I'd forgotten why I didn't enable it on my other characters. It's HORRIBLE.

I find it really silly that everyone uses the "Just disable PvP" argument, since they're defending all those lifeless idiots who ruin the fun for everyone else.

They should bring back dishonorable deaths so they're at least marked in some way so you can tell them apart. Not even I, at level 60, am going around killing lows and ruining the fun for others.

If you're one of those, FUCK YOU, touch some grass, get a life or whatever, and stop ruining the fun for those who want to play.
