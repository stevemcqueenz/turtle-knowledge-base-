I've been wondering that ever since I started playing on TWoW. SW is quite inconvenient compared to IF. It usually means adding 4-5min in travel time to any endgame content - be it 5man or raid. It feels especially punishing for Dire Maul.
Back in the days on my Vanilla WoW server IF was the center of everything and SW was deserted. It feels really weird to me being so far away from Southern Kalimdor. The Menethil-Theramore boat was already annoying (long wait if you barely miss it) without the added 5min flight from SW.
Admittedly the helf teleporter is extremely convenient for Plagueland stuff, but other than that I see only drawbacks of the TWoW Alliance transportation system. And that shortcut only comes into play once you can reach Light's Hope, meaning even mid-game staple dungeons like SM, Uld, ZF are universally 5min further away.

[This thread](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=6502) does a fairly good deeper dive into the disparity between factions when it comes to transport. For me personally most criticisms would evaporate if an SW-Theramore boat was added. Just a good way to access the center and South of Kalimdor would cut out so much useless wasted time. And I'm actually a proponent of flight-times and no dungeon summons. I want the world to feel big - but the 100th time tabbing out for 5min until I land in Menethil has long lost its charm.

I do have to say the daylight and ambiance of SW is nicer compared to the mostly feature-less giant dark cave of IF, but it's very hard to overlook how much time SW wastes compared to IF.

Back in the day when the server was only 20-50 people full and the RP scene was much more present, we all had a Stone to use for an RP hub. You can find some topics on that regard here even still. This was the moment of birth for those infamous bots we have nowadays: Tents.

Teleporting and Portals in general should be class skills only or a very rare quest and as less as possible. Reasons, I believe, should not be discussed here since the topic is on SW over IF reasons.

As others have said:

At launch, Ironforge was by default the only serious hub for alliance.  There were no world buffs and it had the only alliance auction house.  A lot of people, myself, included, were never fully happy with that because Ironforge is so dreary.  Once auctioneers were added to every city and world buffs became a thing, Stormwind took over as the popular "main base" pretty quickly.

On Turtle-WoW, Stormwind probably has the best overall location.  It has the Vault, it's close to Karazhan, both it and Ironforge are so close to Blackrock Mountain that the difference between them is moot, and connection to the High Elf area means Stormwind also has fast access to the Plaguelands region and its associated instances.  Dire Maul and Silithus are the main nuisances, albeit they tend to take awhile to get to even from Ironforge, just not quite as long.

I assume tents are usually set up in Goldshire due to Goldshire's history as a common "roleplay" hang-out location.  Sometimes, in gaming as in life, things stay popular for little reason beyond familiarity and tradition.
