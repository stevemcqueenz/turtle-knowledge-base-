# So, I'm Back

## Post 5105 by Breidr (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-02-25T08:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5105#p5105 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When I found this server I was super excited and couldn't wait to play. Then I started to slip back into my depression. "What's the point?" "It's won't be the same this time." "It's just nostalgia, and it's gone forever."

I just shut out all games. Haven't even logged into Destiny 2 or WoTLK with the clan. Didn't want to waste the time leveling something I'd be terrible at. I was a terrible hunter back on Vanilla through Wrath and didn't want to end up in the same situation. With things like difficulty being subjective there is no clear "answer" to this either.

I'd really like to take this server on. I've even set about convincing some others to roll alts here instead, and talked about hardcore mode.

I've got all these ideas and aspirations, but can't bring myself to roll a character past the starting area. What if it turns out I'm terrible at [class]?"  That's a lot of time to sink into a failed toon.

I love the idea of this server, I really do. I'm even eyeing some of the turtle shop stuff, but I just can't get off the ground. For me, WoW has always been about the shared experience, not the difficulty, and I remember Vanilla being a hard game for me to wrap my head around.

There's always something that slows you down before you achieve your goals. There's always people who'll say "This is pointless" or "you're bad at it, try something else."

Listen only to your own feelings. Trust no one as them Forsaken chant over and over.

Roll anything you like. The whole game was designed you'd find something interesting and funny. The whole game was made with every need in mind sated one way or another. All you need is just some friends nearby who won't crab bucket you down. You don't have such? Make new ones here on Turtle WoW. It's a bunch of like-minded folks here.

## Post 5113 by Sinrek (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2020-02-25T21:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5113#p5113 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The best choice is to catch that character you've created inner flow, emotions you would feel on the journey, reactions on the possible odds which will occur and perhaps you'll find something new while playing.

Also, if you haven't tried roleplaying yet then Turtle WoW is the best scene to start since almost all players here are more than welcome to have some interactions with roleplay in mind even if you don't have any extraordinary story to begin with. Most heroes begin as mere folks from some village. It's not like a rule or something like that, just an idea to start with.

I remember my first RP character was a merchant with "best clothes in town" and "most exquisite handcrafted shirts available for the fair price!" Funny enough people used to buy them just for the sake of interesting interaction they had. Most weren't even bothered by the price and just tossed gold coins instead of what I was asking for the goods. That was PvE server by the way. There were no RP servers at that time.
