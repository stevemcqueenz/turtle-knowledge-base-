# Vagrant’s Endeavor Theorycrafting

## Post 87563 by Brvtalrainbows — 2024-02-11T23:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87563#p87563 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was curious about the Vagrant’s Endeavor and decided to spend some time on the database theorycrafting and planning for the challenge. It got a little out of hand, so I decided to share - I hope people find it helpful.

The Basics
- The Vagrant's Endeavor is a challenge that forbids you from using any items that aren't poor or common quality, and forbids enchants on gear.

- Because this challenge is about items, that’s what most of this theorizing is focused on. You’ll have to look elsewhere for leveling advice or step by step guides.

- White/common quality items are always going to be better than gray/poor quality items of an equivalent item level, and are generally better than grays of a much higher level.

- Stats for whites are, as far as I can tell, normalized by item level. Any white dagger of x level will have the same damage/speed/ect as any other white dagger of that same level.

- While there are some noteworthy white drops, mostly these are sold by vendors. However, the best whites sold by vendors top out around level 45.

- The white vendor weapons are the best weapons available all the way through 60, with only a couple exceptions.

- In general, both quests and professions stop giving white items by level 20.

- Consumables are even more impactful when you’re not getting stats from your gear. Use them when and wherever you can get them!
