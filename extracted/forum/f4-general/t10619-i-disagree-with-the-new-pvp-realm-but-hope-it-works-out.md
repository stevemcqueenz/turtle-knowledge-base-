# I disagree with the new PVP realm, but hope it works out

## Post 69204 by Wafflecrusher (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-06T17:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69204#p69204 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I, and many others, have expressed the numerous reasons why we think a PvP realm is a bad idea. I stand by these ideas, and am anticipating the PvP realm being under-populated and immensely problematic, at least in the long-term.

That being said, for the sake of the community and Turtle WoW as a whole, I hope it is successful. I hope it attracts more players to the platform as many pro-pvp people stated. I hope it doesn't demand a huge amount of resources. I hope it doesn't split the community, and drag both realms down. I hope it doesn't cause the forums to be flooded with demands for fresh servers constantly, and PvP oriented class balancing that makes them all the same. I want this project to be successful and to survive another five years, and I hope the devs can make it work in a way that mitigates all of the potential problems. I hope me and other dissenters like me were wrong, and the PvP realm is successful.
