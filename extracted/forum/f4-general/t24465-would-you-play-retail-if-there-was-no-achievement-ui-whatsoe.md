# Would you play retail if there was no achievement UI whatsoever.

## Post 172130 by Rfelleto (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-24T12:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172130#p172130 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Ok, so I was currently gaslighting myself into MAYBEish playing retail. MAYBEish being a very important word here.

Then I just remembered all the grinds, mount farms, pet farms, exploration, catching up I would HAVE to do to "ENJOY" the game. And honestly I don't have this feeling in vanilla mostly because there is no ACHIVEMENT UI, and I think this is the main culprit of the current wow experience.

I don't know to organize my thoughts very well, but I can safely say that before wrath (when achivements were implemented) I didn't have this urge of do this, do that, farm this, farm that; that I currently have with the current version of the game. I would go as far to say that the achievement system has "addicted" me to a certain type of gameplay that is always looking for the next thing, for the next transmog that is going to become unavailable just so I can never use it, for the next mount I have to farm just to forget about it with the rest of the 500+ mounts I have laying around. This constant FOMO is what drives Wow in its current version and I think getting rid of the achievement UI and pop ups would be a good step in the right direction.

Let's take Turtle for example. When I start a new game session the goals I set up are set by me, not some artifical system saying this is important to do. If I want to run a dungeon great, if I want to level an alt great, if I have time to raid great. If I don't want to do any of this stuff and just run around leveling farming or survival thats great too. All systems are in place and will not go away and that is SO IMPORTANT to get my FOMO down to a level in which I can actually enjoy the game.

Just wanted to throw it out there to check if other people would feel the same. And interestingly enough there is no addon, currently (from I could find) that completely gets rid of the achievement menu/interface + pops up.

The difference between retail and classic in regards to ability design and rotation is that your rotation buttons and your utility and support tools are typically almost even in amount. Some classes have 2-3 damage buttons to press for personal throughput, and then maybe one or two for utility and whatnot. Think of warriors with sunder armor and battle shout. Warlocks with curses, shammies using their totems, paladins and their gajillion buffs, hunters with something as niche as tranq shot, the list goes on, and while it's not large, I find that classic's classes are pressing buttons that benefit their team instead of themselves more often than they do in retail, and this is special because a social experience should FORCE you in some capacity to play for your team to help them succeed as opposed to just mashing your rotation and flexing your big DPS as your class's greatest contribution. Which... In fairness, was technically what happened after everyone figured out vanilla WoW.
