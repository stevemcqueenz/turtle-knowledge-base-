Many options are valid but questing is probably the fastest if you play as a DPS.
Main things to focus on:
 * There is a custom item from survival secondary profession called tent. There is 1 usually in SW, Goldshire, Org and Crossroads, sometimes at other places, too. Doubles xp gain (if I remember well) and as you stand under a tent, it charges up, up to 150% of your level. Later you can craft your own if you go to STV for the survival skill. Some macros and addons list rested xp.
In short: make sure that you always have rested xp. Makes things much faster.

 * Warmode. If you're okay with being ganked sometimes, it also gives an xp bonus (20%-ish). Should stack with tent.

For money, fishing oily blackmouths used to be good, no idea about the recent gold meta, I barely play nowadays.

I agree with BigSmerf. Explore and have fun.
Wow classic (even this modified version) is about the journey, not the goal.
getting to 60 asap is not really as fun as just exploring the game and enjoying yourself on the trip there. Nothing wrong with being efficient, but Don't worry about "not" being 60 fast enough.

I'd focus on trying a few class archetypes if you haven't played WoW before. If you have, then start with a class your comfortable with. Most of them have had decent upgrades and balance changes, so have fun trying out the new talent trees and skills. if you haven't, then I'd try out some different Damage class types (melee, ranged, spell), a tank (paladin, shaman, warrior, druid) and a heal spec (priest, shaman, druid, etc) and see what you like doing the most.

Find a tent and hang out for a bit. It gives rested XP (just like logging out in a main town or inn), but at an accelarated rate. What does a tent look like? just head out to the entrance to the main city and look for a crowd of about 40 people crammed under a tiny thing. That's the tent. takes about 5-ish minutes to max out your rested XP, so good time to grab coffee or take a bio break.

Also keep bookmarks for the forums here, as well as the turtle database, and the turtle wiki. These will both help you allot as your getting used to all the stuff in turtlewow.
