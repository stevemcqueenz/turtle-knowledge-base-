# Turtle WoWs amazing growth since Warmode

## Post 27932 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-10-25T19:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27932#p27932 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm glad to see that I could contribute to this excellent server with the Warmode idea; which is basically having an option to play as if it was a pvp server within a pve server, pvp oriented people can do all they could on a pvp server, while the server remains to be pve by basic, and those that don't choose warmode won't be dragged into pvp against their will.

Before it there were ~200 people online, now there are 2000+.
First I've suggested in the world chat, there were arguments against it (that turned to insults when disproven), and I got a temporary mute (kind of unfairly, doesn't matter), but on the forum I could elaborate about it in more detail:
[viewtopic.php?f=4&t=2068&p=14078#p14078](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=4&t=2068&p=14078#p14078)
And it turned out to be a great addition.
Years ago there was a similar situation, back when Hardcore mode was what is today the Turtle mode, and I've suggested in the world chat that it should have perma death, something I've enjoyed a lot in Diablo 2, and people were saying that nobody would play a perma death hardcore mode. And years later it was implemented, people love it and it's one of the best wow communities I've played with.

As I've thought, Warmode (I called it optional pvp challenge) increased the population manifold, as many roleplayers consider the faction conflict to be an integral part of the warcraft lore and necessary for immersion. And with more players the donations to the server increased too, which ensures server longevity and capacity to create more great custom content.

There is this tribalism of PvP vs PvE players, but in my experience it's unfounded, I've met both decent and toxic people on both sides. I'm sure many Turtle WoW players that prefer PvE made friends with new players that like PvP.
It isn't about PvP, certain % of people are toxic, they tend to be loud too, and if the playerbase grows with it does all types of people, it really is a matter of perspective how one sees an influx of new players, most of them will adapt to the unique culture of Turtle Wow.
The servers growth attracted the attention of Activision, unfortunately. But that in turn served as an advertisement and led to even more growth.

Some things I'd change about the Warmode:
Getting flagged for PvP when buffing/healing someone in warmode, that can be avoided by simply paying attention to the colors, but there could be something in game that warns new pve players about this being able to happen, especially on hardcore it can be a problem if someone isn't aware of it.
And it shouldn't have an XP reward, because it incentivizes PvE players to use Warmode just for the sake of the xp bonus, and then they complain when they end up in a pvp situation, this is I've noticed, what is the cause of most people complaining about PvP.
If someone picks Warmode it should be purely for the sake of wanting to do PvP, that is a reward by itself, no gameplay enhancing compensation is neccesary.
What there could be instead is cosmetic rewards like in Turtle mode, titles at max level/throughout leveling. Preferably in the form of a warmode currency rewarded every 10 levels, which could be spent on an item of choice at a special vendor.
And something specifically for hardcore + warmode.
But definitely not a reward that affects gameplay.

Turtle WoW is the best server I've ever played on, exactly what I wanted for many years, I've been enjoying the server also with a lower population, but it's good to see it grow, and that more people know and support Turtle WoW.
