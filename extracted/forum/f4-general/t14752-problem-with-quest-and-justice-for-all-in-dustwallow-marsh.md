You probably need all 3 starter quests to unlock that.

The Shady Rest Inn chain has 3 starter quests, unlike other chains:

 - hoofprints at the entrance
 - soldier badge on one of the planks on the floor of the inn
 - mounted shield on chimney

you need all 3 at some point to unlock further progression.
