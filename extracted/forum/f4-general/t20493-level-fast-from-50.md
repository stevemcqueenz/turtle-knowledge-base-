## Post 141650 by Zulnam (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-07-27T07:46:00Z

Every level requires more XP than the previous, so it makes sense that 50-60 feels slower than previously, especially if you are playing casually.

Questing and doing dungeons is the best way to go without min-maxing. Quest in areas with lots of quests, kill all mobs around quest spots (not just quest mobs).

Gather loads of dungeon quests so that the first time you go into a dungeon you get a lot of XP from completing it.

burning steppes is a good zone for this level. Lots of quests and lots of quests for Hateforge Quarry + BRD.
