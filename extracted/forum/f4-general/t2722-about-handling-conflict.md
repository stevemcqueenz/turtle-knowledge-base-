# About handling conflict ...

## Post 17079 by Aydea (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-02-17T13:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17079#p17079 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This little guide is supposed to be an aid for those who find themselves involved in conflict, toxicity or the target of other non-sense of similar caliber. Why am I writing this?

I love the server and its community and what it stands for.

And because I haven't found anything of the like yet, if it exists, I am sorry to have written something redundant.

 *Introduction*

The first truth about conflict with another person is ... that it takes another person. Shocking, right? Conflict with oneself doesn't but usually people don't argue with one self. Usually. :D (Though, I suppose a healthy debate wouldn't be a bad idea... )

The second truth about conflict is that "it begets more of the same". Or "causes more of the same". Just like manure, where there's the smell, you can tell ...

Anyways.

 *What can you do?*

Usually when you have a conflict, you can separate people into two broad categories:

#1. Conflicted but ***willing to resolve.***
#2. Conflicted but ***unwilling to resolve***

So, naturally you have to accommodate either category in its own way ...

 *Category #1. Conflicted but willing to resolve*

People in this category are usually ***interested in a resolution***. You can usually tell within the first few moments, but not always. If someone is interested in resolving a conflict getting there is just a matter of making it happen. Common goals and all, right?
Its like a good trade. You give some, you get some. And usually either party gets most of what they want but not everything. An amicable compromise.

 *Category #2. Conflicted but unwilling to resolve*

There isn't really a whole lot you can do if someone is unwilling to resolve and work with you. So, what can you do? Remember those two truths from the beginning? That conflict with someone else takes someone else and that conflict tends to cause more of the same?

Stop talking to them. Its that simple.

It addresses both truths at the same time. You remove yourself from the conflict and unless you perpetuate it, you also cause the conflict to not perpetuate. If the other person perpetuates it, thats on them, but you can still ignore them.

 *Conclusions*

Conflict is an interaction and if an interaction is not productive or pleasant to you then YOU have every power and right to stop it. Why keep going to the circus if you feel clowns are creepy, right? Just don't.

Don't let people convince you that you have to interact with them or put up with their non-sense. You don't have to. You're free, you're an adult. So act like it and exercise your responsibility.

If a person is a productive and pleasant member of this community, people are going to be attracted to them.
If a person is a toxic and unpleasant, detrimental member of this community? They'll attract things too but mostly negative attention. And in the end they always end the same: Alone

Anyways, thats it. I hope this helped and happy gaming turtles <3

The problem with the second category who don't want to resolve is that they will always want to have the last word. Sometimes it's OK to let them have this little victory, but in other occasions it can be damaging.

Imagine they are arguing about some imaginary issue they have with the server, someone tries to discuss with them and point out that they aren't right, but they aren't having it, they want to keep arguing hoping that the other person will just get tired and give up leaving the discussion so they can have the last word and feel smug about it in their personal crusade against who knows what.

The problem is this leads to misinformation, if the topic of discussion is important and someone who is completely new find this and since they lack any experience, they can believe what has been said and decide they don't want to play.

This can be about this server or just about any other server or game out there. Such misinformation by toxic people who just want to cause drama can lead to potential players leaving before even starting and it can hurt any population, especially if the game is an online one and doesn't have a very large population and each person matters.
