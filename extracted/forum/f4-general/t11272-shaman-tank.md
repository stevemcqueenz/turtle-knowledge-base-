> **Insgrosky wrote: Sun Oct 29, 2023 10:37 am**
> Could anyone tell me if a Shaman tank would be possible, especially for a low skill player? Or if I'm being too worried about late game and should stick to Shaman healer. (which I have been enjoying)

In general the tank job in MMORPGs tends to be harder than the healing job in normal grouping.**   Trying to do the job well on a class that isn't fully designed for it becomes harder still.  In general I would only recommend the shaman tank role on T-wow to players who are already familiar and comfortable with the tank job because it's hugely beneficial to have experience and instinct doing the job when you're trying to do it without a full toolkit.

If you're familiar with normal "classic" or WoW 1.12, here's a comparison:  The Shaman as a tank on Turtle-WoW is inferior to a Paladin from stock 1.12 WoW.  In stock 1.12 Paladins are only barely considered tanks at all--and the shaman on T-wow is less of one even than that.  People can make the T-wow shaman work for the job, but it goes against type and takes determination both from the player and from the people he's grouped with.

If it's what you have to do to make your group function at all, then you do what you gotta do.  If you hate healing to the point where you'd rather quit playing, then don't do that--it's not beneficial to drive yourself to quitting outright.  But going back to your original question, the Shaman is a mediocre tank on T-wow, sort-of adequate, but never ideal and very often quite far from ideal.  Since you said you enjoy the shaman as a healer--I recommend sticking with that.

 **In raid settings the relative difficulty often flips:  Tanks often have only one assigned target to worry about, while healers have both more people and potentially more damage sources to deal with.
