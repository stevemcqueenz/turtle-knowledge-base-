So, in case of a progression raid or during a decent PUG there is a shared core philosophy: you have to help the team as an individual so that the team can surpass the challenge. In the process, you are rewarded with gold per bosses and potentially, items.
As a reminder, progression raid gold gains are mostly to cover expenses of the raid preparation.
Still, not talking about speedrun mentality overspending. A guy spamming jujus for haste and flasking (and probably spamming the chat with DPS logs) is probably an idiot.
The core philosophy is to be good enough individually so that the group can succeed together.

We have a 10 man raid, dropping half blues, half purples.
Not even giving gold after boss death which is the game's way of telling: don't overdo the consumes unless you are really undergeared.

Then we have ZG, still half blue drops, the purples being consistently inferior than most MC ones.
Then comes AQ20, still having many blues.

What I'm saying:
Loot quality and difficulty-wise MC is almost in the middle of the pack.
Sure, historically, it was the first raid (or Ony? I think MC), but realistically, a new, progression guild should not touch MC initially.
It has loots so good it almost invalidates 3 raids before itself.
Consequently, it is made harder (talking about this MC here), expecting players to have some experience, gear and willingness to consume if they want things like accuria or bindings.
And still, the gold reward of the raids covers the realistic expenses and item repairs from wipes.

 3. What I mean under being prepared, consume- and enchant-wise.
Some resistance potions during progression. From the moment it gets smooth, they are not needed.
Mana pots, healing pots, tea.
 1-2 role specific consumes. Let's say, keeping up a brilliant wizard oil and a mageblood as a heal.
Zanza much later. Usually by the time a guild's roster gets exalted at ZG, MC is easily on farm.

If you add these together, the raid itself covers the expenses and also some wipes.
Maybe tanks' situation is different but raid tanking, spec MT always meant extra sacrifices.

And once things get easier, they can be omitted.
But for a moment imagine a decent PUG getting wiped over and over again, because half of the healers have no tee. They basically sabotaged a run and made everyone's day bad by refusing to buy some super cheap teas at Hyjal so they went oom mid boss with blue gear.

Gears and enchants:
Will not mention MC for a sec, will focus on real entry-level raids like Kara10.
I wouldn't expect people to farm pre-bis or buy super expensive purple boes.
But getting to a raid has some innate seriousness. For the way better loot, you gotta respect the other's time and effort.
F.ex. if you heal, at least have blues above level 50.
Specific items which require farming and luck can't be expected unless you are an elitist but certain items can be.
For example Tirion quest chain, providing a huge healer ring. Involves no luck, just performing a chain which can be expected from someone wanting to heal a raid.
And some enchants on the better items. On ring of Fordring, a +12 heal for sure. It is cheap and by itself makes the ring a BWL-level item in a server where JC don't exist.

So I mean doing the quests giving good items, enchanting the better items with decently prized enchants, doing reasonable quests which give important items and get some consumes without being an overachiever. IF it's a progression or a fair PUG without hard-carries.
