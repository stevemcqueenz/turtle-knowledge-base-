 --Buffs--

Lower max rank wrath mana cost by... I dunno, 25? Enough to make it the more mana efficient, but less damaging spell. However you'd rework that.

Make moonkin form increase spell damage by something smallish. 30 works. Just a decent general damage increase for the more or less mediocre spellcaster.

 --Nerfs--

Thorns lasts 1 less second. (I'm not at all serious about this. It's just here because balance needs only buffs and the criteria for posting here demanded a nerf :3)

OKAY, time for feral.

 --Buffs--

Feral Charge deals 20% of normal auto attack damage plus 50. Maybe lock this behind an "Improved feral charge" talent but i think it'd be better just to have it baseline so as not to give us too many talents and not enough points. Figured this would be nice for some early threat grabbing or snap threat when a crazy ele sham goes ham, panics and runs the mobs away from you.

New Ability - Enduring Spirit: Requires bear or dire bear form, costs 10 rage, and decreases all damage taken by 1% for 10 seconds. Stacks up to 5. This is spammable but it is on GCD. Works well for a big rage dump when you've already got threat and damage isn't much of an issue. Plus gives some magic damage resistance that all tanks might need a bit more of. I think since bear has a reputation for being a very beefy meat sponge, their specialty should be in sustained resistance to or tolerance for damage, where warriors have a similar vibe, but less pronounced as they favor defensive CDs and big influential buttons like shield wall, last stand, shield block and so on.

Ferocious bite now deals 10% more damage to enemies affected by your bleeds. Gives reason to use rake/rip.

Tiger's fury is now free, but has a 20 second CD. Buff persists through powershifting. Not sure if this is a buff or nerf in the end, but the ability will at least feel less janky.

New ability - Barkskin (Bear): Costs 30 rage, has a 3 minute CD, and reduces all damage taken by 10%, slowing melee attack speed by 25%. Fire damage ignores Barkskin entirely and deals normal damage. Has -some- PvP counterplay, downsides, long-ish CD, and hefty rage cost. I'd say
