The dungeon issue on Tel'abim is a bit distinct, though not completely, from apparent lack of battlegrounds.

The double experience rates on Tel'Abim, which are effectively **quadruple** experience rates with tents, make it inefficient to level by dungeoning. Even if you get more experience while inside the actual dungeon, the waiting time and traveling time make it far more efficient to level by simply grinding - that 4x experience rate is pretty nuts.

While people reach 60 sooner, the boosted experience rate effectively kills world pvp since people can reach max level even casually with relatively few hours of play time. And if people are in a rush to reach 60, there is no reason to queue for battlegrounds since that will only slow them down. There are some dungeon groups, but again, doing dungeons also only serves to slow down reaching 60.
