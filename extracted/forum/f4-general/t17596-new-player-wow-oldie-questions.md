Hey there and welcome to TurtleWoW :)

 1. The Skill Trees had a major overhaul across the board, still: there are skill trees that need some tweaking here and there and of course the will never be actually equally. But most of the TurtleWoW Community goes by motto: Play whatever you want. Have fun here. We don't care so much about minmaxing.

 2. The population is actually quite great. And not just for end content. Yes, the crossfaction and Raidscaling was from times, when the population of TurtleWoW was much lower, and they actually wanted to revert the raid-scaling at some point, but the Community rose up and stopped that change, since also smaller guilds wanted to have the opportunity to rate with the scaling.

 3. Things you "should" know beforehand? Well. The only thing that would come to my mind would be: don't depend too hard on questhelper-Addons, if you're the type of player who uses those.
Because whenever a Major Update comes to TurtleWoW, the DevTeam does **NOT** update its public database. The Team wants us to figure things out ourselves first, find new Quests by ourselves, communicate about new stuff instead of just looking it up on the database. ^^

 4. Besides the Addons that are recommended on the addon-site of the TurtleLauncher, here:
<https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Addons>

You can still tank dungeons as any type of warrior but warriors don't have a privilege in tanking here.
If you wanna play Alliance and you want a character which can reliably tank dungeons, you can choose between warrior, druid and paladin. Each 3 can be a valid raid tank later and have other valid roles, too. You are not forced to become a tank with any of these at raid level.

Warrior is mostly as you'd imagine with better rage generation and thunderclap in defensive stance.
Pala is your aoe-threat specialist (with taunt!).
Bear received some dodge-gimmicks but mostly their strengths were buffed.

Also, PVE is cross-faction as you stated. When you raid, expect both pala blessings and shaman totems.

My data is kinda outdated, I'm pausing from actively playing since around Christmas. But Europe evenings we usually had several thousands of people (peak) with slightly lower numbers outside of it.

Scaling... Each class is stronger now and some dugneons and early raids feel trivialized. In a response, TWOW made a lot of mobs stronger lately.  *3) For me as a regular classic player: Is there anything from the top of your head that is such a vastly different experience, one should know about beforehand? Except for what one can already read on the homepage.*

Tents give rested XP. You stand under them for some minutes, XP loads up for 1,5 levels and you get double XP from mobs (maybe quests too) for that period. Many macros or addons can display your rested exp.
Old stereotypes of Vanilla classes rarely hold up. Retri paladin is not a joke, raiding druids are not necessarily healers, some hunters wanna punch from melee-range, a shaman tank in a dungeon is not (too much of a) meme... Don't have the old prejudices.
Respeccing has a price limit of 5 gold. First is free, later ones get more expensive up until 5 g. You can buy (also via in-game gold) a device which lets you save builds for free respeccing among them.
Server is filled with custom loots and areas. Even normal dungeons like Deadmines have custom parts.
Warmode is an option as a glyph at the starting zone. More exp and rep in exchange for being always flagged. Personally I don't recommend it.
There are also other challenges as glyphs at the starting zone. Look around.
Some MVP items from Vanilla got nerfed. Lionheart helm? Titanic leggings? You can easily get better items at raids.
Transmog is possible via fashion coins which tend to drop at lategame.
