# Shaman builds and WF group in raid

## Post 101269 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-14T19:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101269#p101269 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I wanna write a short summary about how much different shaman builds like to be put in a melee group for WF duty.
I understand the impact of WF in a melee group, my goal is to give the other side's perspective for prioritizing.
In the best case scenario this helps a raid lead setting up shamans more efficiently one day.

This of course only applies to pre-class changes, not sure how this will change.

So.

 **BL-resto**

The most optimal build for melee groups. Should have improved strength/agi totems and probably improved WF/FT, too. People going for BL-resto give up some healing power for added melee utility, they are the best choice for your WF group because they love to be there. Specially twisters.

 **Deep resto**

Deep resto would prefer to be in a group with a tree druid and maybe a mana regen caster like arcane mage or spriest but it's fully functional in a melee group. Has a big enough mana pool to reliably twist, too.

 **Enhancement**

Enhancement hates WF groups because WF weapon is stronger than the totem and only 1 buff can be applied to their weapon. They also highly need crit for clearcasting and flurry and improved agi gives 4,5% of that. They function the best in an agi+str group, surrounded by a feral druid with that crit aura and an MM hunter with trueshot aura. Prot palas also work but they require FT which means that the enhancement shaman won't use damaging fire totems (fire nova, magma, searing), so prot pala is okay but less ideal than feral druid and MM hunter.
There's also a misconception about enh shamans. They usually go kinda deep into elemental tree and don't pick improved WF/FT (sometimes not even improved strength/agi). Many raid leads put the enh shaman to the melee group assuming that their totem buff is the biggest but it's usually not true, that goes to the BL-resto. They usually also have a smaller mana pool making them a less ideal candidate for totem twisting.
But honestly enh shaman raid DPS output is so low at the moment that team necessities have to have a prio over the shaman's playstyle. It's a bit evil but they won't do much damage anyway.
Special shootout to Nightfall enh players. If they can reliably twist without resetting swing timer, they are maybe an even more ideal candidate for the melee group. They are absolute support builds but they switch up the chain heal spamming for Nightfall triggers.
Also, enhancement shamans can dia kinda easily for cleaves etc and a dead shaman removes the party's totem buff. Relying on the enhancement shaman's totem buffs is kinda gambling under some situations.

 **Elemental**

Unless you absolutely need all shamans in a melee group, don't put the ele there. Ele hates melee groups.
Unlike enhancement, they have a valid raid DPS (seen them near the top many times at Naxx - below fire mages and some fwars of course) but they really struggle with threat (and kinda need a TF tank for AQ40).
The elemental shaman wants his tranquil air totem and unlike enhancement, under an ideal setup, they are really good. Put them in the moonkin+top caster group if possible. Tranquil air totem and salvation work together (in a multiplicative way though), so an ele shaman in the big d*ck caster group allows them to go off much more while you also allow the ele shaman to normally play the game.
Totem-twister ele shamans can work in a melee group (WF and tranquil air) unless there's any tank or OT in that group.

 **Tank shaman**

If you went with the memes and allowed a shaman tank in the raid, the least you can do is to further compromise raid efficiency to allow the shaman tank to function. They need the agi totem and they despise the WF totem. Similar reasons as enhancement but also: WF totem allows the melee in the party to overaggro more easily; tank shamans also have a very limited mana pool (bad for twisting); agi totem's 4,5% dodge (if talented) and added armor are also a blessing for the tank. Never ask the tank shaman to WF for the party.

 **tldr:**
You have a bunch of shamans for the raid and wanna decide which ones should go WF duty.
 1. BL-resto willing to twist / Nightfall enh willing to twist.
 2. Deep resto willing to twist.
 3. BL-resto / Nightfall enh.
 4. Deep resto.
 5. Ele willing to twist tranquil air and WF (no MT or OT is in the group).
 6. Enh shaman willing to twist.
 7. Ele willing to twist WF and agi. (Ele overaggroing and dying also removes the totem buff for the remainder of the fight).
 8. Enh shaman.
 9. Ele.
 10. Tank shaman.

 **PS:**
Raid comp depends on many things but I prefer to put rogues in the twister group since they are the only melee class which receives melee AP from agi (too). WF+agi twist is not only the 4-4,5% crit chance like for the other melees, it's also 77-88 AP per rogue.
The other priority group is the tanks when it comes to twisting. That 4-4,5% dodge (plus some added armor) can really help them.

Also, if you wanna twist but have mana problems despite using consumes or a fight is just really long, consider using rank 1 WF totem for the twists.
