# Tested the new tank shaman

## Post 108375 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-17T21:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108375#p108375 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Spent the last days testing out the new tank shaman. Wanna give feedback about what I experienced.
To be fair: I only did high-end dungeons, only non-5 men instance was a Scholo/Strat 10 run with friends. My feedback is not punctual from the sense that I had been testing a build at dungeons while having overgeared the content.

 **The positives:**
 * People's attitude. I've mentally prepared for many fights at the start of dungeons, mostly melees demanding WF totem. Was not the case. I underestimated the politeness of the Average Joe.
 * Aoe threat. I felt like a prot pala. Stoneclaw totem has a 30 sec CD so mindless spamming doesn't work but having many sources to generate AoE threat feels huge. Sometimes using 1 oil of immolation here and there and the AoE threat is yours, guaranteed.
 * AoE threat allows you to just run into a pack and magically have threat on each of them, like a pala. But unlike them, you have several ways of pulling from range.
 * Mitigation. I admit, I overgeared the content but the high dodge, the added damage reduction to rockbiter via talent and the rockbiter buff via T2,5 really adds up. Armor is physical damage mitigation, adding 15% reduction next to the armor which is already buffed by talents like spirit shield is not bad at all.
 * Bloodlust feels like a very good on-demand party utility. Arguably the best party utility from a tank I can think of. Bloodlust is better for tank shamans anyway since your faster 1H weapon will have more crits, more or less having a guaranteed haste buff on the party all the time when bloodlust is up.

 **The negatives:**
 * Mobility: A caster pulls a mob or you get thrown to the other side of the room and all you can do is to run back. Shocks more or less work as a ranged 'almost taunt' even after the changes but the mobility element is honestly lacking. You get tossed out of position and chaos emerges since you need a bunch of time to get back to your place (no charges). Palas are similar but pursuit of justice helps which we don't have. Boot speed enchant can in theory help but despite the good mitigation I experienced I'm still sceptical with a mail user and I will enchant my boots with +15 stamina every time for a shaman tank.
Suggestion: A 'gust of wind'-like ability would help without breaking anything. Alternative is a wider use of ghost wolf but it could break the gameplay in much more ways. Another option is a +speed totem. I don't think that shamans should have access to charge but an inferior mobility option would be a huge qol difference for shaman tanks.
 * No defensive CD. If you're cautious to give a defensive CD talent to the enhancement tree because enh DPS could use the same at PVP, just make it require wearing shields or giving a massive damage reduction for a limited amount of time. I'm sure a defensive CD can be added without breaking enh DPS. Honestly, this is the one thing making tank shamans objectively a bad choice at raids. Only thing which holds them back.
 * Mana. Water shield and lightning strike help a lot. I only experienced mana problems during 10-men runs when many DPS went all out and I had to spam abilities and totems to keep aggro. So this is something I'm not totally sure about since I've done 0 raid testing. All I can say is that mana management was decent at 5 man but had to stop the run by drinking way too many times during 10men.
 * Itemization: T2,5 and fallen prophet sets overlap. By changing the fallen prophet items it would be possible to have 2 tanking-oriented item sets on you at the same time.
 * Blocked amount: don't get me wrong, it is a nice mitigation stat. But for shamans block chance is not high enough to be reliable so it is an unreliable mitigation buff. Talents giving +blocked amount would be much more useful giving something else, f.ex. defense instead.

PS:
Threat: Had a talk with a friend after tanking Ony q dragons for him. He observed that my initial threat was huge but it gradually fell off and it felt like maintained threat can be a problem. Honestly, I might have used my rotation badly, I can't state that the build has threat problems. All I can state is that I had a very weird threat curve (didn't notice elsewhere, dungeon enemies die too fast). If maintained threat is indeed a problem, it might be worth reconsidering why T2,5 has 0 strength altogether.

PS2 : Might be a consequence of me being overgeared but earth shield didn't really have a place. Lightning shield worked as a threat build-up, water shield worked as a mana sustain but earth shield is too expensive for what it does and LSing it is underwhelming. I'd consider slightly reducing earth shield cost and making LS earth shield give you a defensive buff instead (f.ex. 10 percent damage mitigation for 10 sec). That would open up a niche for it.
