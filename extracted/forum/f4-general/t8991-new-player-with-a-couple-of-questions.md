## Post 58184 by Aydea (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-20T16:55:00Z

Looks like someone has to do the honors, so here we go ...
You ask a simple question but answers rarely are simple. I hope you brought some time.

 *"Is this crashing normal?"*
Generally, no.
However ...
At the moment, the server is experiencing an notable over-population to the point of straining the game's internal code-powered engine to its limits. These crashes are most often caused by no one in particular and simply the sum of all players being online at the same time. So, for the time being you can expect that until the issue of overpopulation is addressed, these instabilities due to over population are likely going to occur. A fix is in the works and the situation is going to be addressed sometime soon (tm), so stay tuned. And be very careful if you play HC (Hard Core challenge).

 *"Server Status"*
At the moment you can find the server status on discord which you can find on one of the buttons to the right when you launch the client. If you don't want that, then you may have to wait until the next patch when the client itself is going to receive an update that shows (at the log in window) how many players are online. You might be able to see whether the server is online there as well (once the update is done).

 *"Tell me what to play!"*
This just never gets old. People asking others to tell them what to do. Personally, I would advise you not to let other people tell you what to do but hey what do I know amirite?
From my own experience I can tell you though that what matters most is whether you enjoy spending many hours of your life playing the class you select because that keeps you coming back regardless whether you are "in demand" or not. What you are also going to hear is "Roll a paladin" but that is an inside joke due to the class changes made here for the paladin class (there are too many paladins according to some).
It might sound a bit harsh but its better if a person plays what they truly enjoy and keep coming back, rather than play what they were told like a good little sheep and then get miffed and quit the game because someone has the audacity to tag their mob or steal their resource node because they never learned to take responsibility for their own actions.

All of that being said if what I said didn't put you off, welcome!! I hope you read the rules, enjoy your stay and don't feed the trolls. Except maybe Darktifa. But they don't need food, besides they'd use your cookie to troll you and it would be so good you might just enjoy it. That sorta stuff for mature audiences only though.  maintenance_turtle

> This just never gets old. People asking others to tell them what to do. Personally, I would advise you not to let other people tell you what to do but hey what do I know amirite?

OP didn't ask which class to play, he asked about which of 2 classes he wanted to play was more in demand. He's not asking you to wipe for him, just a general census, likely due to which is going to be better at getting groups.

Overall the advice is otherwise solid.

I should note that there are a lot of people. A lot, lot, lot of people, and every zone is fairly heavily camped almost everywhere. Instances are nice in that they are fairly reliable sources of content that isn't just running around hoping to be within sight range of something as it spawns, and you can tag it before anybody else. Everybody will be trying to tag any mob that spawns in almost every quest area.

Do you want to tank? Feels like there isn't that many tanks on the leveling treadmill up. If you want to tank, they are very much in demand, and if you are good you can probably gather a nice friends list of people to instance with.

If you are going to quest out in the world, priest is nice due to lower downtime. Healers do seem to be somewhat rare, not as rare as tanks but still rarer than dps. Pretty typical of mmos really.

I would probably suggest warrior to you if you are down with tanking, otherwise probably priest. If you do go warrior and start tanking instances, be sure to note the good players you enjoy grouping with and friend them for future runs. Lots of people in the LFT (group finder) are a mixed bag of extra suck and highly skilled team players.

Establishing a good social circle will likely help you enjoy the game more, as teaming up in the world with a friend or 2 helps a lot when there are so many people camping everything.
