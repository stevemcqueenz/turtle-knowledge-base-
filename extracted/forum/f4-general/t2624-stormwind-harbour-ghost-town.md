## Post 16422 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-01-28T03:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16422#p16422 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've thought of three things that could help liven up the harbor a bit:

- A quest that introduces you to the harbor by making you deliver a package to Auberdine. Helps settle in to the fact that it exists and get used to it instead of Menethil Harbor.

- Turn the harbor into a Hawk's Vigil-type questing hub. Could have a whole storyline of smuggling, Defias activity, corruption on the docks, maybe some stuff that involves the Stormwind Sewers (where do you think the Canals empty out to?). The harbor is big enough to have quests spanning the area all the way to the lighthouse. Could have plot hooks for South Seas content, [the island of Balor](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Balor), hooks to Kul Tiras proper, Crestfall, Tol Barad or [Zul'Dare](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Zul%27Dare?so=search) and the [Channel Islands](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Channel_Islands).

- Obviously, adding some basic NPCs would help a lot making it feel like an active capital city harbor. Could add dockside merchants (since it's the harbor it could have merchants from all sort of places), military and/or civilian personnel working on the ships and the siege tanks, more dockworkers, and maybe just people wandering around or looking to charter a ship some place.
