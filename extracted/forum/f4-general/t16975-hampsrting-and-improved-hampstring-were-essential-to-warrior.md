Counterattack is good for hunter. Very good, in fact. Normally you wanted it to root someone and make distance- which is sort of what survival's whole shtick was before CC2. Being slippery and making space.

Warrior? Not as much. They usually only benefit from a root because they can use it to prevent escape. Sure, it can also be used as an escape tool, but the hunter version was simply better because of one thing: Deterrence. An increase to dodge and parry chance meant that getting a counterattack off was super easy. It simply went with their kit so much more smoothly in comparison to how it works for warrior, and- part of that is why hunters are so outraged by the fact it got taken and half assedly slapped onto another class with nothing else to synergize with it. God forbid they do the same with deterrence...
