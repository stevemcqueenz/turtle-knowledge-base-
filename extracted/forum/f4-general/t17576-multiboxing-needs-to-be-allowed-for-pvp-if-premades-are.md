Honestly. Without some sort of large overhaul, pvp just isn't going to be fun. Back in the day, you could hop in a battleground while leveling and you could have some fun while getting your reputation up a bit so you can get some of the gear for leveling. Today, you have to deal with teams of people on hyper optimized characters with only the best race/class/spec/gear, infinite consumables, pvp addons, and they're all on discord. And they've got a scripted bot camping prince nazjak for his stun trinket.

The simple fact is that WOW, especially a private server like turtle, will never have the population needed to dilute the effect on the game that the sweatlords have. Now to compete you have to grind the best gear THEN have fun. Even then, without a team you're just going to get farmed in WSG for 30 minutes before their paladin healer/druid flag holder(who doesn't cap) get bored or have to leave for work. Frankly, it's far closer to feeling like a job or commitment rather than fun.
