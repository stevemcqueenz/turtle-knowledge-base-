# I was unknowlingly playing on 1024x768 resolution

## Post 107866 by Nib42 — 2024-11-13T18:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107866#p107866 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It turns out that my ingame resolution settings weren't actually doing anything. I'm using vanillafixes.exe so that might be why idk. If you are like me, check your config.wtf file in WTF folder and change your resolution to your monitors'. Also the framerate is actually uncapped and I was running the game at 400-700 fps the entire time. Use MSI afterburner with Rivatuner to cap your fps to save power!  dead_turtle_head
