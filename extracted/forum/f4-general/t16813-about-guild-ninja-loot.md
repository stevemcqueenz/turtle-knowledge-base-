# About guild ninja loot.

## Post 114855 by Yy38156642 — 2024-12-30T14:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114855#p114855 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’m a hunter, and today I joined an MC guild raid. The guild follows the SR > MS > OS rules, and after defeating the final boss, Ragnaros, I won a 2% hit ring on SR. However, Lootmaster didn't follow the usual procedure—Ring SR, then Ring MS, followed by Ring OS—and skipped over the SR phase entirely. This left me confused during the Ring MS phase. As a result, I didn’t claim it until the Ring OS phase, when I explained in Raid chat that I had SR'd it. By that point, a Fury Warrior had already rolled for it.

After about four minutes, Lootmaster, after verifying my statement, decided to award the ring to the Warrior. I understand that, as a DPS, I’m not playing at my best and don’t fully understand the tactics. As a retired player, World of Warcraft holds a special place in my heart—it’s a part of my youth and memories. Twenty years ago, I stumbled upon a colleague secretly playing the game in the office. Out of curiosity, I joined in, and it led me to play for five years. But with the arrival of WOTLK, I took a break. Now, after retiring, I’ve picked it up again. Perhaps WoW isn’t as pure as it used to be. Maybe the players have changed, or maybe it’s me getting older. The sense of nostalgia I had in the past seems to be gone from the current gaming community. It feels like it might be time for me to leave WoW. Thank you for the memories of my youth.

(I’m not blaming anyone, just sharing my feelings.)
