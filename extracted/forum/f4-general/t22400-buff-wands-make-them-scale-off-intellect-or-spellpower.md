# Buff wands; make them scale off intellect or spellpower

## Post 155544 by Springboards (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-10-14T10:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155544#p155544 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Currently, wands still scale off agility since they are just a lazy copy-paste of hunter autoshoot. This becomes especially obvious at 60 when suddenly even your epic aq40 wand starts hitting for only ~150 avg dmg while everyone else is swinging their weapons for 350-400 per hit. This is not fair,as tank shamans or paladins can easily hit you for 400+ dmg per white swing while casters cannot retaliate like that--our white hits are the wands basically. At least make them match each other for damage, pound for pound. The whatever dps it states it has is a total lie, but blizzard is known to have lying character screens since diablo 2

Make wands scale off Intellect and/or Spellpower instead of agility!!!

OR, make wands instant cast with a cooldown but allow us to use it on the move; remove the charge-up autoshot stand still mechanic, and just make it cooldown of however our wand speed is. They are near impossible to use in pvp, if a player knows how they work they can circle-strafe you forever with you being unable to ever get a wand hit off, especially if you have a slow wand.
