Rogue leveling is very fun.

Ambush -> Gauge -> Backstab -> Eviscerate and most mobs around your level will be dead or close to dying.

It's hard for me to recommend Hunter as a first character for classic wow, since you have to go out of your way to tame new pets, so you can learn their abilities, and then teach those abilities to your main pet. I find that aspect fun, but I can imagine it being very tedious and annoying for others. You also need to macro your pet, since pet pathing in classic is not very good, and they're much more prone to aggroing things around them. With that being said, I would still recommend hunter if it wasn't for the awkward spot he is in rn. A ranged physical dps specialized class, who's best spec is its melee version...

## Post 161056 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-11-29T13:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161056#p161056 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

1. Hunter is very fun for questing and leveling. Having a trusty compaion is always cool. Initially the pet can be a bit annoying: at lower pet loyalty level either you feed it all the time or the pet leaves you. Once you get used to that part and the pet grows more loyal it becomes super fun.
Hunter is less optimal if you want to be a tryhard in some game aspects like PVP but overall, really fun.

 2. Rogue... More PVP oriented and a really good damage dealer but for me the poisons were a turn off. You need specific items for a lot of your abilities or tools. I never really enjoyed playing them.

 3. Warlock is a bit like a magical hunter but with a demon. Can be fun and easy to level but you need soul shards for a lot of things (you get souls via a specific ability when killing a mob). Locks can turn into an annoying micro-management class eventually (making sure you teleport others etc) but the fantasy is fun.

No class is particularly hard but for leveling the easiest ones are usually the ones where you have a companion tanking for you (hunter or warlock).

If you used to play a healer I'd recommend classes which can be both healers and DPS so you can switch back for some nostalgia if you want.
Such classes are priest, paladin, shaman and druid.
