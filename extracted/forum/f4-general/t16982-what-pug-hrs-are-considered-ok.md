## Post 116316 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-12T09:28:00Z

Technically you can HR whatever snd however much you want, as long as you advertise it very clearly to everyone.

But be advised, the more you HR the worse your pool of players will become. Good and prepared players have options and wont come to a raid with excessive hr.

Also it should be said, if you pug Raids with HRs, you justify those HRs by doing the actual organizing. So having Resistance Tanks where needed, organizing Douses/Sand and actually leading the Raid is your responsivility.

The better you do these things the more people are okey with the HRs.

 **But if you are a raid noob and are oblivious to the raids callenges, do run in someones raid a LOT before you organize your own pugs with HR. If you dont know how raids work as a HR pug organizer, you can ruin your reputation royally**

## Post 127269 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-18T07:30:00Z

I mostly raided before CC2, things have changed a bit since then.
But for me the rules back then were like this:

Kara10 - Scythe or Legacy were okay but never both of them. Recently saw some people HRing Arcane Essence, that is NOT ok.
ZG - Maybe the caster cloak and 1 idol. Ideally 1 of them. Seen people HRing bijous before (?).
AQ20 - Crossbow is ok. Anything else was weird but as far as I see book prices and tier 1 boe prices went really up so nowadays people sometimes HR those. But I stand by my belief that book HR is a scam.
Ony - It was usually 1 T2 helm or Vishkag on HR. Maybe the bag. I find these fair as long as 1 is HRd.
MC - Usually legendaries, maybe accuria, maybe the caster neck, maybe cauterizing band, nowadays even the boe... It's a mess. I think legendaries are fine. Legendaries +1 item is okay if you actually lead the raid. Everything besides that and I suggest skipping that raid.
BWL - Mostly the trinkets and that's totally fine. There are some other goodies here but in my opinion for BWL HRing 1-2 trinkets is the only okay way.
ES - Not really sure, I wasn't there for a long time.
AQ40 and upwards - These are usually guild-based half pugs. Here usually the guild has a set of priorities for items and you can decide if you still wanna join for the run. BWL can sometimes be like this, too.

Two more things:
 * For me it's a big red flag if someone HRs items and has additional SR. No.
 * I'm much less tolerant with HR-ing raid organisers if they don't tank or at least heal. The main responsibility goes to the MT as always and having a DPS HRs just for being there, I don't like that. Exception is Ony when he creates a crew and HRs his head gear.
