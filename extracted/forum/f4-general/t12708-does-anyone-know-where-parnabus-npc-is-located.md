![Image](https://i.imgur.com/7GeA9Xs.png)

Looks like he's probably on one of those isles off the coast of Azshara.  What you do then is go to Azshara, off the coast of the southern "tine" of the two pronged landmass, and then open up your zone map, then zoom out to your continent map, then zoom out to your Azeroth map.  Check your coordinates and see how close you are to 38.6 - 41.1 on the Azeroth map.

Another thing to consider is that when the database says "Azeroth" for the map, it could also be "Eastern Kingdoms" or "Kalimdor". Another possibility is that it is an NPC in Gilneas.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/G8eMmBI.jpeg)

Depending on what quest you are looking for, use context to deduce which result is more likely. Whatever quest you are on, I would assume that it makes some mention of who or what you are looking for, and very rarely is it so vague that you cannot at least use deductive reasoning and context clues to hazard a guess.
