Hi fellow turtles,

So with the recent increase in popularity of the server we've seen some population growth, and of course, with that comes a few bad apples.

Over the past couple of days world chat has been visited by a few edgy attention-seekers, bringing their bigotry with them.  I've already seen a few of them make homophobic, racist, and anti-semitic comments, as well as try to bully roleplayers.

I've seen this on other private servers go unaddressed, and quickly spirals into full-blown white supremacist propaganda being spewed on global chat.  No thanks.  That toxic community aspect is the primary reason that I, and I'm sure many others, left other private servers and chose to make Turtle WoW our home.  For a long time, we were free of this kind of thing, and I'm worried that a few bad eggs are going to start sullying the atmosphere and discouraging others from starting up here.

I've personally already added a few new names to my ignore list.  Wanted to just make y'all aware, and advise that if you run into one of these, the easiest thing to do is just ignore and report.  Replying to them only gives them the attention they want, and they thrive on your reactions.

I hope the GMs are watching, and are ready to take action towards the individuals in question.

Let's keep Turtle WoW an inclusive, fun social environment for all.  Love y'all.
