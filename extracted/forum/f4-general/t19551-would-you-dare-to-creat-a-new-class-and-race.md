## Post 133121 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-26T21:25:00Z

If Ogres were to be implemented, I would be perfectly fine with just having male ogres, and not worry about creating whole new female ogre models. However, an alliance counterpart will need to be added. And then starting zones will need to be added. Faction inclusion in major cities or towns. Special quests, and so on and so forth. It's a lot of work, which I don't think should be prioritized anytime soon. Maybe in the far future, but not now.

On new classes I stand divided. Did I want Necromancers in WoW? Yes, a lot actually during Wotlk. But I think it will stray away too much from the vanilla+ feel, and dive into the fully custom client side, which people are already accusing the server of becoming. Regardless, even if artificial, I would like certain content to be leashed, as to maintain the feel of vanilla.

Tl;dr No for custom classes, and a Maybe for adding more races in the far future

## Post 133136 by Bellybutton (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-27T00:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133136#p133136 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd be down for custom classes and more races only if Turtle WoW were to balance it correctly, but such a task would be too momentous and there's a million different ways they could mess everything up.
I think everybody agrees, including the devs themselves that if Turtle were to add new races, Ogre for Horde is a no-brainer. The biggest issue is finding a race for the Alliance within the framework of Vanilla WoW and the lore of WC3/Vanilla/Turtle. The only... passable(?) option for a new Alliance race backed up by lore would be the Furbolgs given their close ties to the Night Elves, but Furbolgs clash extremely heavily with the aesthetics of the Alliance and are Shamanistic, which is a huge can of worms that nobody wants to open since  a lot of people, myself included are against Shamans for Alliance and Paladin for Horde.

As for classes, such an endeavor would mean an overhaul of so much of the game to make it fit seamlessly. I'd honestly doubt Turtle would even TRY, and I wouldn't blame them.
Personally I'd love to see a Witch Doctor class for Horde (Troll), and a Spellbreaker class for Alliance (High Elf).
Though honestly developing two classes for the factions would be a ridiculous undertaking; It'd probably be more resourceful to make a Machinist class that's Gnome and Goblin exclusive.
