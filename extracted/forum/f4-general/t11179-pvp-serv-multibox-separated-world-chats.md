## Post 73824 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-10-26T04:37:00Z

Don't mind Foobs.  They misunderstood the question.

The rules were written when there was only one realm and haven't been revised in quite a while.  It's true that, at present, there is no multiboxing **for the RP-PvE realm**.  However, the GMs haven't really said anything, one way or the other, about **multiboxing on the PvP realm**.

There are no challenge modes or Hardcore Mode on the PvP realm, either, so there's no real "advantage" to multiboxing beyond having someone who is consistently reliable at your back.  Plus, it would be *bafflingly* stupid if multiboxing on that realm we're only allowed in the first couple of zones.  So, if it's anything like Blizzard's official realms, then yes, you're allowed to multibox on the PvP realm while flagged for PvP.

Maybe, one day, a GM will actually weigh in on this and the website will be updated accordingly.
