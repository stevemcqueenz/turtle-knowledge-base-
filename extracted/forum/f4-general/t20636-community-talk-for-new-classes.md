## Post 142907 by Mekk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-03T11:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142907#p142907 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The thought of adding a new class is intriguing, especially when you look read up on all the things that changed during development, such as combining multiple Hero Classes into the Classes we all know and (mostly) love.  The problem with adding a new class, or any significant class/role addition, is that you can significantly affect the balance of the game, for better or for worse.  The benefit is that you can add in new gameplay mechanics, new themes, and even new roles to bolster what is already there.

One thing that I will say is that, while I like the "idea" of Hero Classes, they are much more limited in scope unless you start pulling from elsewhere.  The Death Knight, for example, was pretty much every Scourge unit rolled into one, because the Death Knight on its own could not fill three talent trees.  Couple that with the fact that most Hero Classes are fairly race-specific.  A Witch Doctor or Shadow Hunter, as a Class, are pretty much exclusive to Troll, just as Warden or Huntress is Night Elf.

However, going after something like the Runemaster, which only has a vague concept, or some other open-ended Class could be feasible, within reason.  Monk, for example, would require significant animation work, which may be outside the scope of what TWoW has available.  But something like a Bard could be doable if it was more of the melee/caster variant instead of the musician visibly playing an instrument (which would require new off-hand types and animations), but you could still utilize the instrument in the Relic slot or something similar.  Tinker may be limited due to limited mechanical assets, though I could be wrong (I know that they added enough by WotLK that you could create one without the need for custom assets).

The nice thing with bringing in an entirely new class is that its wide open in terms of design.  We know what a Bard is, but Turtle could create the Azeroth version of a Bard.  Runemaster is wide open for interpretation, filling any role that Turtle felt like giving it.  Likewise, Tinker could fit any role imaginable.  For my own project, I've got a Stormcaller that's mostly ready for testing (DPS/CC/Support class that uses lightning and wind), and I've been wrestling with a Thornweaver idea for a while now, which is a Nature-based Minion Bomber of sorts (that's likely more complicated than it needs to be). LOL

Now, as for a counter-thought, instead of creating a new class, how about adding a 4th talent tree instead?  This could be used to properly expand classes, such as separating Cat and Bear Druid into their own tree and giving Shaman Tank a proper tree.  Personally, I think this is where I would start, as these two classes could already greatly benefit from a 4th tree.  From there, you'd need to decide if there are any other classes that could benefit from a new playstyle, such as a melee Spellsword for the Mage or an offensive shield Warrior (like a hoplite, but maybe not limited to spears).  Or if you want to use this as a way to introduce some of the missing Hero Classes, such as adding Blademaster to Warrior, Shadow Hunter to Priest (yes, Shadow Hunter was originally in the Priest category), and Dark Ranger to Hunter?  Are there any other Classes that could benefit from an added role such as adding a Healer or Tank role?  And, the biggest question, do you use this to add an entirely new Role, such as a Support-oriented Bard Rogue?  For my own project, I moved melee over to the Beastmaster tree and turned Survival into a mid-range Support that utilizes thrown weapons (combining a bit of Headhunter and Shadow Hunter). :)  Obviously, these are just some random examples off the top of my head, and I'm sure others could come up with better ideas.

However, I still want to emphasize that adding something like this could upset the balance of the game and would need to be approached with caution.  Turtle is trying to stay very much Classic+, but I do appreciate them attempting to widen the Class options, such as fixing those that just weren't very good (*cough* Ret Pally *cough*), to opening up new roles as in the case with the Shaman (which was already kindof there in Classic, just not fully supported).  I do think there is room to expand, either with new classes or new talent trees, but Turtle will need to tread lightly in this area.

Also, you mentioned Summoner.  Interestingly enough, this was the only Gnome class early on and it was under the Warlock category, along with Occultist, Necrolyte, and Necromancer. :)
