I'm in the same boat as you, I hate having a raid schedule I have to adhere to. I have enough job schedules IRL, thanks.
However, I do think TWoW has enough content for people like us. The entire leveling journey up to 60 is amazing, especially if you turn on the Turtle Mode glyph (0.5 XP rate) and go for completionist - complete every quest in the zones, and especially in each dungeon along the way. AtlastQuest really helps pinpoint the dungeon quests, and collecting them all is an adventure in itself. Experiencing the new zones and dungeons for the first time is awesome.

If you want an even slower leveling, you can take the Glyph of Exhaustion (no rested XP) and avoid taking the bonus XP from Warmode (20%). You can even turn off XP gain completely from time to time if you're about to outlevel a content you want to complete.

At 60 there is still a lot to do without raiding. There are a lot of dungeons, attunements, long quest chains and things to work towards - epic mount, dual spec (getting alternative gear sets and experimenting with specs), professions, BoE and crafted epics, rare drops from dungeons etc. Especially awesome is the Dungeon 2 set quest chain - it's really long, interesting, hard and expensive, so it really gives you an alternative to raiding for getting epic-geared. It's still weaker than T1, but it's a lot of fun (and pain) to get.

To sum it up, there is a lot to experience even if you'd like to avoid raiding altogether. Even then, there are PuGs for lower-end raids, so you could dip your toes into it without having it as your "second job".

I agree. Classic WoW is split into three games.
 1. Leveling
Very much open world experience. The occasional dungeon. You can play even if you don't have much time. Just do a couple of quests or grind mobs. Level your professions. Journey to a new FP, etc.

 2. Pre Raid Gear
Mostly dungeon farming. Few open world experience (some high lvl quests, attunement quests and travelling to the dungeons). If you only have one hour time. Forget it. You need to find/start a group, travel to the dungeon and also do the dungeon.

 3. Raiding
Open world experience means farming for consumables and getting world buffs. You need a couple of hours time and you have to make sure, that you will not be disturbed.

Everybody knows, which phase is the best and most fun part. The problem is, you know, that you will eventually end in a state, where you can't progress anymore because the game needs you to invest several hours in which you can't be interrupted. If you have small kids. That means GAME OVER.
And that is very demotivating.

You can also find some very good epic drops at dungeons, including at custom ones, but again, not Naxx level drops.
I think you can totally enjoy this game and get a very decent gear without raiding if you accept that your gear will never be as good as the gear of high-tier raiders.
