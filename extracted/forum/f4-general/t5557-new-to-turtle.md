## Post 34318 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-02-08T07:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34318#p34318 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I wouldn't worry about missing out on anything. The best way to experience TurtleWoW as a new player is to set out on a path and abandon everything you know about "the standard leveling path" from vanilla WoW. Try to experience the game as a new WoW player again, chase quests that seem unfamiliar or interesting, give in to your compulsions to explore an area that seems unfamiliar, be willing to roll other characters who may take other paths that will eventually, in theory, lead you to seeing everything there (currently) is to see.

This will give the game a great deal of replay value for you, coupled with the different class experiences courtesy of class reworks.

I'd definitely say that Turtle RP is the definitive must-have roleplaying addon. Some people use My RP as well, but my experience with it was that it was buggy and threw error messages, so I got rid of it.

As others have said, play the game like you're new, and just see where things take you. There's a lot of differences from Blizzard's WoW, and plenty of new quests. Even extensions to old quest chains, so don't just focus on looking for the stuff you've never seen before.

Honestly, roleplaying is a *great* way to experience the new content, since you can just ask yourself, "What would my character want to do now?" and do that.

In essence, there is nothing wrong with "missing" something that may be optimal or best in slot for a level / level range or the like, as you can always go back and do it later usually for full experience, and just like the first time we all played WoW, you can then use that new information to inform what quest rewards you want to target on your next character.
