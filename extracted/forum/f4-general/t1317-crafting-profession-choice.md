## Post 8043 by Roxanneflowers (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-10-08T12:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8043#p8043 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Blacksmithing will allow you to craft weapons and armor for use as you level up ... you just need one Miner alt to feed your Blacksmith the materials.  Having Skinning on another alt for the needed leather materials for some of the weapons is a good choice or you're going to have to rely on the Auction House.

Short answer is that Blacksmithing will cost you less money to level up than it will be to use the Auction House as the source for your gear as you level.
