I can't say I've read much Warcraft lore other than one book about Medivh and Garona, I don't remember the name or much of the plot, and some parts of a comic book about Varian losing his memory and becoming an arena fighter in I think was Orgrimmar. But the latter gave me the impression that the world of Azeroth is poorly depicted in the game. While I think it's one of the best games I ever played in my whole life, I still think the world is kind of sterile and it creates a false impression that the Alliance and Horde hate each other so much that if they see each other, they have to kill each other on sight. But that's just the game having world PvP by default and when the game was still new, people from one faction would rarely miss the chance to fight those from the other faction. In the comic, lots of characters from races on both sides would meet in person and act friendly/neutrally to one another. That is if we consider these comics to be canonical.

Also while I don't know much of the lore in WC1 and WC2, I know a little of it from WC3 and I believe there the Alliance are just Humans, Dwarves and maybe Gnomes, while the Horde is just Orcs, Trolls and Tauren. The Forsaken, I believe were retconned for WoW and joined the Horde, because they needed a 4th race and same for Night Elves - they were made part of the Alliance, because they needed a 4th race. From what I know from developers' quotes, Gnomes and Forsaken weren't supposed to be in the game, Ogres would have been in the Horde and the Alliance would have had High Elves or something like that, but due to time constrictions, they had to go with Forsaken and Gnomes, because they had developed them already.
