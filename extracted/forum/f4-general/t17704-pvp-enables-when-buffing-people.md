# PvP "enables" when buffing people

## Post 120672 by warozell — 2025-02-23T00:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120672#p120672 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If an ally is pvp enabled (aka their name is green), then if I buff them, my pvp gets soft-enabled too. This doesn't mean that it's fully on, as I can't right-click my portrait and disable it.
As someone who loves buffing random people I pass by, and enjoying it when someone else buffs me, what is the point of this "feature"? I play on the rppve server with no intention to pvp in the open world, but there's always some [very non-vulgar word] lvl 60 horde traveling across alliance low-lvl zones, who one shots me for no reason, and only because I was being nice to other players by buffing them?

Imo this should be changed so that the pvp status doesn't get shared across people. Someone might argue that I could just not buff green-named people, but that would imo be a stupid argument.
Perhaps I'm just being salty, but I seriously have no idea why this is even a thing.

## Post 120717 by Tightpants — 2025-02-23T13:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120717#p120717 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is kind of intended behaviour because it's sorta classified under the same umbrella as healing, since you're improving an allied character's state.

Imagine a scenario where you can't kill a healer currently healing an enemy character. Now, that would be terribly unfair. So healing will also flag you for PvP.

I don't think this will change any time soon. You may just have to be careful who you buff.
