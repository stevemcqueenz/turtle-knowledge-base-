With Retail WoW would implement something like that for old raids, so you could actually do old capstone raids in their "revamped scaling leveling experience."  Anyway...

Since I don't remember where I left off, and because I'm going for a more "immersive" playthrough, I was going to make a new character. So I'm trying out a bunch of new characters, hoping to get more involved some day.  This was mainly a long winded post to ask "Where's Speedy?"  I remember lost turtles being everywhere, but they seem to have vanished, and there is no quest to get an apple.  I must have missed some change.
