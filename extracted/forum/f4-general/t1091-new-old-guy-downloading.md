Welcome to the server then, both of you! As it stands currently, the server is more or less just one very large guild. People seem to go out of the way to help each other here, and it's definitely in your best interests to get your Diplomat medallion.

Make sure you read through the Hardcore notes and FAQ, as there are some great additions to that system that make it more smooth and enjoyable. Just a couple for example: Gray quests still reward as much xp to you as if they were yellow, up to 25 levels under your own, and all dungeons still reward 1x xp to all monster kills to encourage grouping.

Have fun and welcome to Turtle!
