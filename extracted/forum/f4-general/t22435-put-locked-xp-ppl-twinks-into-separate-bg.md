I play Ambershire since opening mostly on lvl 19. I skipped like 1 or 2 weeks. All other times I try to play atleast 1 bg per day.
My observations are following:
There are always people with stopped xp. Even in first day people do preparion for fight. Even leveling to 9 and getting 4 Light Armor Kit is preparation. So in some term, twinks always exist.

I think that idea "level through bg" kinda dead from the beginning with brackets 10 levels wide. People come at higher levels. Low levels are hated for being dead weight. Brackets aswell could be 18-19 with same result.
It is true that more people have appeal to going bg during leveling. There were several leveling battlegrounds in early days of Ambershire simultaneously.

The more often you see people with Crusader and Fiery weapon, the less battleground overall pop. I still convinced that regardless of xp locking, high level enchants on low level gear should be nerfed [link to suggestion](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4714)

I totally sure that xp locking helps normal players more than to twinks.
As a twink you get luxury of being carried through all dungeons with like 0 xp.
As a normal player you have to clear all those dungeons and can't avoid all this xp.

Normal player with stopped xp still can be successful now. I play hunter with some green chest and legs without 100hp enchants. I do have Venomstrike+7 and have like 900hp. Can't say I am superhero, but it feels competetive enough.

Normal players with stopped xp are geared and are interested in going early dungeons. As a consequence, they provide "soft" boost to all other normal players because of good damage/healing or survivability.

I know that retail solution to twinking problem (all with stopped to separate queue) works.
It actually killed twinking at all. But it is bad outcome.
I don't have much time to level characters to 60 and just enjoy battlegrounds and getting into dungeons in random party with my different characters. At some point, WC, DM and RFC become very repetitive, but random party and ability to switch character in no time helps a lot.
I just want that power ceiling of twinks become much lower. It will be good for both normal levelers (they become competitive in bgs) and for twinks (battlegrounds will happen more often).

I don't care about power balance at lvl 60. Let people enjoy their epics. Low level power balance doesn't have to be the same. Having different options is good for MMO - it attracts different players.
