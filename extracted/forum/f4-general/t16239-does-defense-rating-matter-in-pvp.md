If I recall correctly +defense on gear works in PVP. But it's not worth it, much better to get other stats like +Stamina:
- defense does nothing against any form of magic

- dodge, block, parry only work when the enemy is attacking from the front

- defense doesn't apply when you are stunned or casting
Basically it's contribution is minimal in common pvp scenarios.

## Post 110820 by Bob022 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-01T18:34:00Z

Defense functions in PvP, if that's what the OP is asking, but in that envrionment it's typically not worth the opportunity cost in terms of stat trade-off.  In addition to the points made above, the main reason players stack defense in PvE--critical hit immunity--is virtually irrelevant in PvP because players raise their crit rates much, much higher than those of PvE monsters.  Simply can't stack enough defense for it to effectively do what it's best for, and it comes at a steep itembudget cost.

Truth be told defense can be questionable at times even in PvE.  It still does nothing against spell damage, and in all group content as well as the lower-tier raids the occasional critical won't kill a tank anyway.
