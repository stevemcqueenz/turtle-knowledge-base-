## Post 84049 by Toaomb — 2024-01-09T01:31:00Z

Hey!

I don't usually post but I just wanted to pop in and say I really enjoy the servers! I've been playing for about 2 years now. I have some 60's on the PvE server and a 60 on the PvP server, and this is the best experience I've had in WoW in years- if not ever :) I have made so many friends, had so many positive interactions and will continue to give out free enchants/items to new players to help their journey :)

The PvP server was an amazing addition for me, as I love to PvP and play the game more like Smash Bros (with a roster of characters to PvP with) than PvE. I do both, but this is my preference. I found the world PvP satisfying, made some good friends and tough enemies, and am excited to play for years to come :)

Remember that there is a large group of players who don't really talk on the forums and don't complain in World Chat who are having a fantastic time and whisper each other/trade all day long!

What I want to see now are some good publicity movements! Let's get some streamers in on this! Let's get some Youtube videos rolling! Get some eyes on the game and bump up some of these numbers :) Not everyone agrees, but I like making new friends and if you need something feel free to reach out :) You're all wonderful, passionate people, and I look forward to fighting you on the battlefield!

Peace be with you friends, be kind to yourselves and others <3

You can find me on Tel'Abim! **I LOVE THE PVP SERVER- CANNOT OVERSTATE!!**

And to the people that don't agree with me: That's okay :D! We can have different opinions, we can want different things, but having more options will attract more players, which will build a stronger community of passionate players, and I think we all want to see a stronger community :)

Good luck!
