## Post 24704 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2022-09-08T23:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24704#p24704 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Player-created channels (this includes /world or any channel that isn't General, Trade, LocalDefense, WorldDefense, LookingForGroup, GuildRecruitment) have an issue with the format of chatlinks and wouldn't show links before a certain vanilla patch. Prat has a function that was meant to rectify this, but in either a later patch or by private servers, this issue was resolved. As a result, this feature of Prat was deprecated but never removed, and causes conflicts.

Thus if you leave this feature on in Prat, all your links in World chat will be broken spammy messes no one can click on.
