## Post 58921 by Hctwowfan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-08-24T22:19:00Z

How do you make money at lower levels? You don't.

The fastest and most efficient way towards making enough money to buy your riding skill at 40+ is to just level. Quest rewards will bring money and more importantly trash loot from random mobs will be your major source of income. The faster you level, the faster you'll get access to more valuable trash loot. Everything else just falls flat because of cost of opportunity. You could follow some other path to make money but it will never be anywhere near as efficient in your lower levels than to just focus on leveling.

As said, don't play the game like a race. If you're one of those Classic WoW brains who will flay your own skin off if you don't reach level 60 in a week, you're going to be broke and incapable of ever affording anything.

If you play the game it was intended, leveling, doing dungeons, just losing yourself in the little mundanities and are playing at a casual pace, you will easily have the money for your mount before 40 (though you may need to work a bit for the 60 mount unless you want to go the extra mile).

Personally, I ended up taking a break from leveling around level 45 on my first character and started listening to music and fishing while watching / socializing with / shitposting in World chat. I maxed fishing and spent several hours over 2 days just fishing up boxes in Booty Bay, Steamwheedle Port, etc and got 14 slot bags for myself and alts, hundreds of Bolts of Runecloth/Mageweave and Thick/Rugged Leather to sell on the AH and made literally 100s of gold.

working solutions for lvl 19:
- sell copper bars/ores
- sell silver ores. bars are much cheaper
- fish for oily blackmouth on barrens/darkshore/silverpine coast, sell it on ah
- fish stranglethorn vale, sell stuff. could be more effective if you level survival to 75
- craft&sell deadly blunderbuss, trolls blood potion
- gather&sell briarthorn, swiftthisle
- pick enchanting, buy low priced high level gear, sell enchanting dust, essences
- craft target dummies, plant and loot them. sometimes it drop fused wiring. sell it on ah
Farmed enough gold on twink 19 to buy most of enchants for him.

P.S. if you want to actually gather some metals, you need to move to less crowded place. For alliance it is darkshore, for horde - silverpine forest.
