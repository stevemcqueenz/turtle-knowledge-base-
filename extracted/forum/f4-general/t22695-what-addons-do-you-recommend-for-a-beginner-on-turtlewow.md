lol I don't have a short list, I got a gazillion addons lmao but if I were to suggest, heres my short list

[PFUI](https://github.com/shagu/pfUI%5B/url) = All in one UI replacement that let's you adjust pretty much everything and move anything, has clique heals and mouse over macros using the command line /pfcast. If your fimiliar with ELVUI, it's kinda like that.

[Pfui Turtle](https://github.com/doorknob6/pfUI-turtle%5B/url) A small extension that fixes some issues that arise from Turtle's custom UI, class and item changes.

[PFQuest](https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest%5B/url) A lightweight quest helper and ingame database

[PfQuest Turtle](https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest-turtle%5B/url) A TurtleWoW DB extension for pfQuest. You need base pfQuest for this to work!

[Distance](https://github.com/Linae-Kronos/Distance%5B/url)  shows how far your target is away, based on the spell ranges your class has. If you like to charge, you don't have to look down at your action bar to see if you are in range. Great for all classes really

[Dungeon maps](https://github.com/Otari98/Atlas%5B/url) View maps of dungeons and raids

[Atlas Loot](https://github.com/Otari98/AtlasLoot%5B/url) AtlasLoot provides a loot browser for dungeon bosses, raids, world bosses, PvP rewards, crafting, and more. It integrates seamlessly with the game to help players find loot tables and item info quickly.

Daggers for Life

Pulled some info from my rogue leveling so ignore the whole Rogueish stuff. The rest is spot on.

A couple things that might help leveling.
caster
Have you ran into a situation, maybe a group dungeon or solo and you are fighting one caster but all the casters in the area look like they are casting? It happened to me in dungeons. I didn't know who to kick or gouge, it was so frustrating that I felt like I was letting the group down.
[Super Wow](https://github.com/balakethelock/SuperWoW?tab=readme-ov-file%5B/url) is a mod that fixes that mess. Now you will see only those caster that are casting.
Here is the installation guide [Super Wow Installation](https://github.com/pepopo978/SuperwowInstallation%5B/url)

Do you find yourself not knowing if you are going to pull agro off your tank, did you install a threat addon but can't find the time to look at it because of all the sweet, sweet action going on in the dungeon and all your backstab crits lighting up you screen? (sigh, I luv rogues)
[Tank Plates](https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/TankPlates%5B/url) must have [Super Wow](https://github.com/balakethelock/SuperWoW?tab=readme-ov-file%5B/url) installed as a mod.

[Tank Plates](https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/TankPlates%5B/url)

simply put it changes the color of the enemies nameplate.

Red - You don't have agro, keep on pumping you pumper.

Yellow - you need to ease up there ninja maybe use a [Feint](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=1966/feint%5B/url) or chat with the pretty priest, who knows, it actually might be a woman this time.

Green - OMG! Now you done it, you couldn't hold back and you pulled, it is time to [Vanish](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=1856/vanish%5B/url). Oh No! What you say? [Vanish](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=1856/vanish%5B/url) is on cool down? Hit [Sprint](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=2983/sprint%5B/url) little fella and scream like a little girl as your running for the portal at the beginning of the Dungeon. ( I have never done that, no really don't know what you talking about (cough)

Blue - The enemy is under CC (Kidney Shot, Blind or someone else's CC like Poly-morph) For all that's holy, don't break the CC.

I luv this addon, it's in my face with the enemy nameplates and not tucked away in the corner of my screen or in the middle of my screen blocking all my wonderful yellow crit numbers.

Also a must if you have a Tank or alt Tank.

[Trinket menu](https://github.com/S4V4GENZ/TrinketMenu%5B/url)

I'm a collector, ok maybe a hoarder. a trinket hoarder. I got alot of trinkets.

What's great about this addon is, it puts your 2 trinkets on your screen. You can mouse over and if you have another trinket in your bags it will, with just a click change your trinket (out of combat).

I have 3 trinkets ( I really have a whole lot more but let's stick to 3)

 1. is a straight forward attack power trinket, no on use effect

 2. is a straight forward agility trinket, no on use effect

 3. is a poison cloud trinket which does 50 damage every 5 sec for 45 sec with a 10 min cool down.

I join a group for a dungeon and of course the agility trinket is my bread and butter
the second trinket will be my longest cool down trinket - the poison cloud

on my screen it will show those 2 trinkets

when the tank pulls alot of mobs I can pop my poison cloud (aoe damage! Yes Plz!). the beauty of this addon is I can be in combat and mouse over the trinket I used and select another trinket.
as soon as I am out of combat it will equip that trinket I selected, which would be the attack power trinket.
