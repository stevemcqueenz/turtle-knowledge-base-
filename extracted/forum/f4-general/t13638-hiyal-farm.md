Farming shards used to be an annoying and tedious activity. The quest items were not shared in a group and they had to be turned in once the quest requirement was fulfilled.

This was then changed so that everyone in a (raid) group could loot the item from a killed mob without the need to hand in the needed amount from time to time. On top of that, the dynamic respawn system made it possible to farm insane amounts of shards during the first two weeks after the changes were applied.

So, we went from one extreme to the other. Since the current variant has been live for so long now (and the damage has been done already), I doubt that we will see any further changes at this point.
