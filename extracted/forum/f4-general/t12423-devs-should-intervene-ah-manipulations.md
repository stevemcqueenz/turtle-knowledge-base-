Market clearances just happen organically. I used to sell Rumsey Rum Black Label back when the fishing pools were still overpowered. A stack usually went for under 3g, but I always felt true value was more like 5g. So I kept up some 5g stacks just in case. Usually once a week, typically on weekends, 1-3 of my stacks would sell. Because 2 pages of cheaper rum had been bought up. That was not the work of scalpers, that's just natural fluctuations of supply and demand. Fewer people listed, more people bought. I sold stacks I had fished up, but I could have just as easily resold rum using the same idea. I don't feel like there would have been anything wrong with that? You take a little bit of a risk, investing some gold to buy at low prices and investing some more in listing fees. Instead of selling within 8h you gamble on selling within 1-2 weeks.
Of course now stacks go for upwards of 8g because of the fishing nerf, but that's a different story.

That's just to illustrate that stuff like all flasks selling out is just something that can happen organically. Especially around a very extraordinary time like Christmas.

I'm open to the idea of a scalping mafia. I would just like to see some evidence for that, because my natural assumption is it's an organic market movement. I'm not even talking about internal data that only the server knows - Were the cheaper stacks bought by one of the characters now listing them expensively (the original seller would know that)? Were all of the overpriced stacks created after the market sold out or have they been listed for a while before? (which is of course only circumstantial evidence - higher prices cause new people to enter the markets quite naturally) Has general supply been low the past 2 days or on-par with previous weeks? etc etc
If you really suspect foul play, there's a lot of investigation you can do. Without more data, I'm hesitant to assume it's all just manipulation.
