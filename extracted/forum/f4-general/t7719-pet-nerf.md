<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49603#p49603 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle wow's way of ensuring pets can't chase rogues through stealth was by making pets unable to attack anything stealthed full stop.

This has the unintended consequence of: Hunters can't command their pet to attack a target if that target is stealthed, even if they can see it.

This applies also in pve (most noticeable with big level differences, a level 60 hunter can't send their pet on a level 10 stealthed mob even if they can see it from 30 yards away.)
