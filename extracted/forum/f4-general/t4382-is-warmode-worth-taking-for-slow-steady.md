## Post 26117 by Thol (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-09-25T12:06:00Z

You don't lose XP if you die in PvP with Slow & Steady. Like you already surmised, most players use War Mode as a Rabbit mode for the +30% XP. There's also the rested XP from tents for mob XP. You can level quite fast if you wish, even with Slow & Steady ironically, most XP comes from quests and you still get full XP from those. Also even grey quests still give full XP (up to 25 levels range).
