# Balance Druid still feels clunky and overly RNG after update

## Post 172023 by Auralys — 2026-03-23T21:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172023#p172023 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hey,
After testing the recent Balance changes in raids (K40 gear level), the spec still feels really off to play.

Main issues:

Eclipse forces constant stopcasting → feels clunky and unintuitive
Too RNG-dependent → more crit = more chaos, not more control
DoTs and Hurricane not proccing trinkets (Scythe, Clackora, Sigil)
High effort for lower or equal DPS compared to simpler casters

It just doesn’t feel rewarding when played correctly.

Balance is extremely intense with no pay off. I think it needs to be less brain-frying? Some ideas on how to make it so:

 - eclipse could be a a cd that empowers both spells: increase crit for starfire by let’s say 17% and increase nature dmg by 20% for 20 sec with a 40 sec cd
 - starfire and wrath casts are guaranteed to create a stack of buff that empowers the opposite element. you consume a charge after casting a spell of that element
 - make it possible to specialise in either arcane dmg or nature dmg. casting 2 starfires increases your arcane dmg for 15 sec, casting 2 wrath increases your nature dmg.

I liked the idea of dots and hurricane ticks allowing for procs, since it can boost moonkin dmg. But I’m not a big fan of item procs overall, they feel gimmicky to me. I would prefer it if moonkin could perform well and better than they do now without proc items.

Just floating ideas because playing boomkin with the current eclipse rotation is frustrating and feels punishing. People want the crit buff but dtuids are tired and burnt out and don’t really have an incentive to play the class that doesn’t even do a lot of dmg.
