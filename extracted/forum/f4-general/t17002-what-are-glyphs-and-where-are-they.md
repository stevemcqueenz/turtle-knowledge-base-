## Post 116429 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-12T16:04:00Z

Glyphs can be bought from glyph vendors that are located in starting areas for each race. These areas are rather small so you shouldn't have trouble with finding them.
Using glyph activates the challenge you've chosen. You can find the details about these challenges at turtle web page in Leveling Challenges section.
