Yeah I quit 2-3 weeks ago too. Reached level 37 and then couldn't bear the slow leveling anymore and deleted my character, along with WoW and went to play other MMOs instead.

This server's first mistake was having x0.5 XP rate. It was somewhat fixed when they made it x1 XP so at least it was normal.

But after playing WoW for so many years, I realized how painfully slow and annoying leveling is in Vanilla. Even with tents on this server, it's still slow and annoying. If anything, the XP rate should increase as follows:

 - 1 - 20  -  x1 XP Rate
 - 20 - 30  -  x1.5 XP Rate
 - 30 - 40  -  x2 XP Rate
 - 40 - 45  -  x3 XP Rate
 - 50 - 55  -  x4 XP Rate
 - 55 - 60  -  x5 XP Rate

With a dead server such as this that barely passes 100 players a day and even allowing Horde and Alliance to group together, it still isn't enough for a healthy population so many things are impossible to do, like dungeons or whatever. I've spent 5-6 hours a day playing and see the same guy for 1-2 hours spamming in Global chat that he is looking for people for a dungeon, when nobody answers him after 2 hours he gives up and leaves.

Then there is the problem with how annoying and boring leveling is in Vanilla when your class uses 2 buttons and even questing and killing mobs becomes a huge pain in the neck. It's even worse when you have played WoW before many times and there is nothing new to see for the first time. If it was your first time playing the game, exploring the classes, the zones, the story, it might be bearable, but  when it's far from your first time, it's a bore on such a dead server.

What's worse is that the few other Vanilla servers that are slightly more alive are only alive because they are PvP.

I'm not keeping any hopes that the XP rates on this server will even increase more than what they already are. I also may be an exception and everyone else might be perfectly content with the leveling speed on this server, but the main reason I quit and deleted my character was because I couldn't take the slow and painful leveling in Vanilla anymore, it's just artificially slow and annoying and not fun at all.

Took me a few years of attempting to reach 60 on various Vanilla servers to find out I don't enjoy this one bit - in fact I hate it. I only enjoy leveling between level 1 and level 20, after that it becomes a huge pain for me and then I realized WoW is no longer a game I can enjoy. Even after 25,000+ hours spent in the game since 2006, even failing 10th grade and having to repeat it, because I only played WoW all day and nothing else. Even if I still love WoW, I also hate it just as much. Time for me to leave WoW behind for good and finally go enjoy other MMOs, which are less time-consuming for all the wrong reasons, like artificially elongated leveling process for no good reason at all and a disappointing and even more time-consuming end game that leads to absolutely nothing worthwhile.
