# Solving premade problem

## Post 98183 by Caos (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-06-30T09:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98183#p98183 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It is so dumb. Hordes don't even play and you have a premade of 10 allis caping flags for I don't know how many hours over and over again.
It is a braindead grind for the premaders, the hordes stay in bg without doing nothing or eat a deserter and it is bad for the allis not in premades as their standing will be worst.
You can tell they should be able to play with friends, the horde should premade also or whatever you want but the reality is what it is a brainded grind and the other faction pseudoafking.
This needs a solution either you match only premades vs premades or penalize honor for premading or something.
You could punish horde for afking or leaving against premades but I'm 99.9999...% sure this will completely kill bgs.
Even if the horde did premades half the times the premades wouldn't get matches togheter and the situation would be exactly the sime as right now but would get progresively worse over time (more premades basically)

The main Problem PvP currently faces is the lack of a PvP community in the first place. Punishing the one that does exist because their opponents don't want to coordinate with their team is unnecessary & cruel

IMO the best they can do is to allow xfaction battlegrounds, so you can actually form premades with your friends from the opposing faction (and if one faction currently is dominating with their premades, multiple of one factions premade can duke it out against one another if xfaction BG is a thing)

But punishing players for playing with others in a MMO is completely against the spirit of the game
