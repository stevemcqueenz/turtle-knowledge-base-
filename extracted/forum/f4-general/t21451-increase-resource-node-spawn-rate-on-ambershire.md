## Post 149074 by Mekk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-29T06:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149074#p149074 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Classic servers were balanced around 2000-3000 players.  Ambershire is over 10,000 players.  When numerous players are in the same area, mob respawn drastically scales from 5-7 minutes down to just a few seconds.  This means that leather and cloth acquisition is unaffected by player density.  More players = more mobs = more resources.

However, ore and herbs do not have a scaling respawn rate.  This means you have 10,000 players trying to work in a system designed for 2000-3000 players.  To make matters worse, Jewelcrafting added another crafting profession that's trying to get these same resources, exacerbating the issue.

To address this, I would look at doing two things.  First, I would have ore and herb nodes respawn 1-2 minutes faster (2 minutes for the low tier nodes, scaling down to 1 minute for the high tier).  Second, I would increase the node quantity by 25-50%.  Then I would see where things stand after that, with the option of doing another pass on node placement.
