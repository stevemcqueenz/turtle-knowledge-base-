<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114009#p114009 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You can become PvP flagged when buffing, healing, resurrecting another PvP flagged player/NPC or debuffing/hitting an enemy PvP flagged player/NPC. Be careful when using AoE abilities near PvP flagged players. Entering an enemy city/area will also flag you.

Avoid that.

When you get flagged, spend time in an allied city or go offline for a while.

It may not be what you want on a PVE server to be tagged for PVP because it exposes you to player attacks. "War Zones" and "Contested Areas" are two examples of the designated zones on many PVE servers where PVP is automatically activated. You might be automatically flagged by the system when you enter certain regions. Look at the map or ask other players to point out dangerous spots.
