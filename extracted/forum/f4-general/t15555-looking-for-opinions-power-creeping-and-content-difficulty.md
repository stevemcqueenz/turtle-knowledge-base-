## Post 106320 by Rumfrq — 2024-11-04T14:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106320#p106320 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Short answer: Yes, I feel the new patch has an impact on difficulty.

I've level'd alot of warriors through vanilla content on official Blizzards servers. When i started playing here on TurtleWoW, I didn't notice any significant changes to the warrior class. Therefore the difficulty felt about the same.

After the new patch (1.17.2) I can definitely feel a change in dificulty. Especially the rage generation (buff) which alow me to use my skills way more often than before. So yes, I guess you can say that it is "easier" to level now.

Is that a bad thing? No, not for me. I think it's way more fun and it opens up alot of new opportunities to build my warrior with the new talents/skills.

TurtleWoW offers alot of challenging and new content for me. It's not for everyone, and difficulty differs from player to player.

## Post 106578 by Renzor — 2024-11-05T18:49:00Z

Content has been too easy for a while now imo. We've already got shamans+paladins and scaling raids, + a fully progressed server with very strong pre-raid items, both crafted and from custom dungeons. Tons of additional and more powerful enchantments.
With the new class changes on top, which massively buffs dps, tankiness, threat and healing, everything just feels like easy mode, no matter which role or class u pick. It's fun right now, to test out all the new builds, but if there is no challenge I'll probably get bored quickly and stop playing. Hopefully they'll buff the HP of raid-mobs and bosses. Kara-40 difficulty remains to be seen. But its loot is very strong - especially the tank loot, which will make them almost invincible against physical dmg.
