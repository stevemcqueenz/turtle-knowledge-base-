It's a mistake to try to balance the game around both PvE and PvP.

This is something Turtle will never get around and in case they do - the very next change to any item/class/potion/trinket... will just break it again.

If people want balanced PvE and PvP - the very first thing they need to do is to enable a system where they can balance one without affecting the other and this is easy as 99% PvP is already instanced. Dampening is a first step but still far from perfect.

In any case, this is not the topic here. Rogues are dealing such an enormous amount of damage right now (most classes do more than what they should btw) to the point it feels bad to use +100g in consumables as a caster (Flask + all potions) just to get obliterated by a rogue with a couple consumables up and half the gear.

On the Flasks topic - they shouldn't be taken as granted when balancing classes either. If we don't like this we either remove them or add one for each class (simply adding an AP flask?) and balance around them - but you can't force a damage type to run a premium consumable to """compete""" against others (note the """, because right now Rogues are in late TBC dps numbers compared to the rest of DPS is not even a competition anymore)

Which brings me to another topic. Mob/Boss HP values are not suitable any more for the damage numbers classes have to the point that out of bosses players need to rely on instant cast spells - there's no chance a Mage will hard cast a Fireball during trash, not to mention there's 0 chance it will stack up Fire Vulnerability or build an Ignite - core mechanics for his spec to shine.

Turtle needs to take this seriously and actually spend time on this. Set a target DPS for each class at each tier and see where issues arise. We are not talking an small deviations in DPS here, some classes are dealing almost x2 times the damage of others.

For example, full T1 + best in slot for each spec. Put them into a dummy boss and see how they perform. Fire Mage is 50% over the rest? Check his scaling/gear/set bonus... Warrior is 40% below because its missing a lot? Add more items that help with that. I know this is very hard and time consuming - but you have to do something, the pace of class balance updates is so slow right now and are not that effective.
