# Is Herbalism overpowered profession?

## Post 89784 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-09T17:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89784#p89784 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I was doing some math tonight and I realised herbalism is the best gathering profession at the moment and possibly the best of every profession in the game.

Prices of high level herbs at AH change overtime, nevertheless current prices at Nordanaar are following:

- Black Lotus: 40g

- Dreamfoil: 0.25g

- Mountain Silversage: 2.5g

- Plaguebloom: 3g

- Icecap: 1.5g
Each node of Dreamfoil, Mountain Silversage, Plaguebloom and Icecap have 2% chance of getting extra 1x Black Lotus according to Turtle DB. If price of BL is 40g and each node gives 2% chance of getting it, that means each node of those herbs equals to extra 0.8g - I call it Black Lotus Bonus.

Each node of those herbs apart from Black Lotus gives you randomly 1-3 herbs so let's assume you get 2 herbs from node on average.

Formula of node's price:
((Herb base price) * 2) + Black Lotus Bonus

So let's estimate price of every herb node:

- Black Lotus - 40g

- Dreamfoil - 1.3g

- Mountain Silversage - 5.8g

- Plaguebloom - 6.8g

- Icecap - 3.8g
In comparison best open world mining node - average 6-taps-RTV gives:
 6x Thorium Ore, 4.8x Dense Stone 0.18x Arcane Crystal
which at current prices equals to 4.68g + possibly a few silver more if we count other gems so let's assume it's 5g.
I don't even compare herbalism to skinning because we all know it's not as profitable in open world.

It's worth noting that herbalism is strongly related to consumables and therefore raiding market. Turtle Team has recently presented 2024-2025 roadmap with incoming Tier 3.5 and Tier 4 raids so my assumption is that raiders will need even more consumables. I think this will increase the demand for herbs so herbalism is going to be even more superior than it is now unless developers make changes to consumables.
At this point I'm not surprised that AV is so popular for herbalists - each game spawns a lot of Mountain Silversage, Dreamfoil, Icecap nodes and even Black Lotus. Free gold lays on the ground.
