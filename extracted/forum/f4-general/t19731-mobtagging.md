i did it a couple of times, not the full 1-60 experience but more as a way to get levels when i am in a level bracket where no zones interest me.

downsides of mob tagging are:
 - pretty boring
 - nowhere near as efficient without rested xp and that goes by fast, even with 2 tents
 - bad way of gearing up or making money. if you rely on it too much you will need to have gold to buy some gear from AH.
