It does for hunters against mana users as the amount of damage Rogues do with their opening attacks. Blizzard designed Viper Sting to drain mana fast and remove or limit caster abilities. However, casters have several tools to balance against Viper Sting.

- **Dispelling or Purging the Debuff:** Classes with dispelling abilities can remove Viper Sting. For example, Priests with Dispel Magic, Paladins with Cleanse, and Shamans with Purge can remove Viper Sting from themselves or their allies.

- **Using Mana Regeneration Abilities or Items:** Casters can use various abilities, potions, or trinkets to regenerate mana more quickly, offsetting the drain caused by Viper Sting.

- **Crowd Control:** Abilities that incapacitate or otherwise control the Hunter can prevent them from applying or reapplying Viper Sting.

- **Spell Reflection or Absorption:** Some classes have abilities that can reflect or absorb spells. While Viper Sting is a nature-based ability, and not all classes have a direct counter, those that do can effectively negate its impact.

- **Strategic Positioning and Kiting:** Staying out of Hunter's range or effectively kiting can reduce the chances of being hit by Viper Sting.

- **Cooldown Management:** Using major cooldowns wisely to either burst down the Hunter or recover mana can be a crucial strategy.

- **Healer Support:** In group settings, healers can play a crucial role in cleansing debuffs like Viper Sting and aiding with mana regeneration.
Hunters won't consistently use Viper Sting if the target keeps countering it due to its moderate mana cost.
