I am going to say it straight to you all. There are several factors that prevent horde from having more players, in very different aspects of the game. I don't even have a 60 yet and I already see it. I am going to try and sum it up the best I can.

 1) the aesthetic:

It is no surprise that in here, alliance is the top choice for most players looking to play elegant, pretty looking characters. This was emphasized by twow Dev robbing the blood elves from the horde (which is btw, a very good, vanilla oriented decision) to give them to the alliance. The big issue being that the race that the Horde got in return, is not nearly as iconic.

The goblin model looks bad, yes, I said it. It is much lower in quality than the High elve model, and there is something very uncanny about it that makes it awful to play.Imo they should have stayed with the purely vanilla model rather than a mix of the cata model + vanilla model with custom changes.

Solution: I think it is vital to not surrender to the stupid decision that wow retail did, turning the horde into half a joke with the blood elves but rather add/build more iconic races that fit the horde.

There are LOTS of options that are vanilla lore friendly. The most obvious one is the ogre race, which imo should have been the first race to be added with the high elves for the alliance.

A second very good option, probably somehow easier. Is to give better options for the current races of the horde.

The troll model for example, where are the sand troll skins (yellow-ish)? The zandalari troll skins (pink-ish)?where is the buffed troll model that looks super good and was used from tbc/wrath on for the revantusk trolls?

There are already undead elves in Vanilla wow, why can we not be one?

Even as a horde player I cannot help but feel like the alliance races look much better on average and the new options seem more refined on alliance like the new dwarf male beards/wildhammer skin options.

 2) the leveling experience: the leveling experience is much much better on alliance. They have better quests rewards, better reputations items like entire sets at very low levels while the horde get two hats options (why?) A bunch of unique pet trinkets that are all located on alliance zone and a third tank that is much better than the third tank option on the horde (shaman tank lolilol).

Solution: easy to fix, add similar reputation rewards (sets) for the horde, linked to the UD or the orgrimmar rep, add better quest rewards and create more pet trinkets dropping from rare mobs in horde zones.

 3)The paladin situation: it is no surprise that Pve wise, the alliance has been meta for years, thanks to the power of the paladins as opposed to its horde counterpart the shaman. In twow, paladins have received major buffs and revamp in playstyle which made them even better in Pve and pretty much unbearable in PvP with a much better leveling experience than the OG vanilla auto attack afk simulator. On the other side of that, shamans have received extremely bad changes  (yes I said it) that do not fix the class issues and create even more problems for no reason (why add more tank talents if you don't put more support for it later in the game while also not fixing the DPS aspect of the enha spec?). All the racials are underwhelming, totems are bugged both in Pve and PvP, all the new spells are lvl 60 only for no reason while paladins get new tools as early as lvl 10 (why bother putting water shield in if it's lvl 60 only and you remove the mp5 part of it?). All the spells shamans get are heavily nerfed version of the real spells while paladins get tbc scalings and insanely op new mechanics with spells that can auto proc enchants/weapon procs.

Solution: buff the hell out of shamans, completely revamp the enha tree, make water shield available at low level and add the mp5 portion to it, add more gear options for enha DPS and tank playstyle or simply rework the tank talents into DPS talents. Change the level of the racials so that shamans get them at the same level as priests (why the difference?). Buff the racials so that they are not so bad to use (lvl 60 wolves should be doing as much DPS as a lvl60 summoned pet and scale with offensive stats). Add some of the alpha spells that shamans had that got removed like Molten blast (fire school scorch-like spell), lightning shock (the 4th shock), ghost wolf rank 2 (can be cast on friendly target and 100% speed), group astral recall, totem of invisibility, ethereal form (BoP shaman version), Add the tbc scalings on totems, remove/ consolidate the totems, having 3 different totems for resistance is bad gameplay when the shaman npc get a elemental resistances totem that does it all for free. Sentry totem is useless as it stands and needs a rework or needs to get gone. Calm the elements is a bad new spell that has no application whatsoever, bring the elemental summon totems from tbc.

The situation is already crazy bad and it will only get worse as classic players come from the new wow token situation. I would highly suggest a bandaid solution that would be fast to apply while working on a deeper and more though through one.
