# A question to the devs: release strategy of new zones?

## Post 85036 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-01-17T13:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85036#p85036 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

First, I want to make clear that Turtle Wow is the best Wow server, I've ever played on since 2005. Now for the question and criticism.

So being a fairly longtime player here, I couldn't help but notice that every new area has been released slightly unfinished and then later tuned.
The clearest example of this were Lapidis and Gilijim, where mobs were missing loot, there was no grass and a quest or two didn't work cause the NPC bugged out.

Tel'abim did not have quest gold rewards for a long time, meaning that many of us, curious to see the new island, basically lost, let's say, 50+ gold. Not a big thing, but still weird. It also had tons of pathfinding bugs.

With Gilneas, it was claimed a good amount of weeks before release that everything was now complete and ready for launch, yet it launched with certain areas being unfinished.

Finally, the new HE starting area lacked fishing loot and certain NPC's/ functions expected in a new town.

Now, I understand that development is no easy matter, but I am curious as to how you, the developers, structure the release of these areas. One thing is that bugs will always exist when something new goes live, but big oversights is something else.
Do you have a checklist to go through every time? If not, it would probably be a good idea, because releases have been pretty uneven. They always, in my opinion (with the exception of Hyjal, which still needs a makeover terrain design wise), end up being very high quality.

Could you maybe explain something about the process?
