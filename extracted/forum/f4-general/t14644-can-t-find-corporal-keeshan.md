[https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=349 ... n#comments](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=349/corporal-keeshan#comments)

> Go to the cave (33,07) at the very end of Render's Camp. Once inside turn a left as soon as you're able, this takes you through a narrow path that leads to an inside lake, this lake should be directly to your right.
>  Corporal Keeshan will be in a hidden nook directly opposite of where you are when you first came into the lake area. Keep walking around the lake, clear mobs as necessary, once on the other side of the lake it's an apparent dead end, but look to your left for a hidden pathway, Keeshan is there, alone with a growth of mushrooms.

Google is your friend.
