Love the new raid, it's fun, we've been having a blast.
I just think there's a slight issue with loot in Kara40 where bosses only drop 2 pieces of gear per kill, and only 1 piece of tier from bosses 5-9. I know there's a slight chance for a second to drop, but it's still pretty underwhelming considering that's going to be roughly 40 weeks to gear a team of 40 (most teams have more than 40 raiders), not counting the eventual 4-5 weeks in a row when you're going to see the same token drop and they probably will go to waste at one point.

I just think if 1 of each token dropped per kill would be a bit more reasonable, kind of like AQ40 does. It's still going to take time to gear everyone, but at least not *almost a full year kind of thing.

Any GM with an opinion on this, or the reason why it's only 2 drops per kill would be appreciated.
