# Lowbie guide to getting your tent

## Post 3708 by Grunow — 2019-08-13T21:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3708#p3708 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At lv 15 you can make a tent to gain rest very fast, to do so here's the steps
 1. from SW bank you talk to the goblin who will send you to the arena
 2. go outside and go south as far as you can go...die
 3. res at the graveyard that will be close to booty bay
 4. buy the goblin oil (45s) from the engineering guy at port authority
 5. get the flight path
 6. take boat to ratchet get flight path
 7. use meeting stone to get back to SW
 8. go back to the goblin and go to the arena
 9. go outside and go north carefully as far as you can go....die
 10. res at the graveyard and jump down and go across the river to the dock you can see
 11. go to the camp and get quest and buy the tent, buy the rope, pick up the wood on the ground
 12. I hearthed from there and leveled survival to 75 while in the inn
