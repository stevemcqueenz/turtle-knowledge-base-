If you are leveling, there aren't any important quests that you must do besides class quests you can get from your class trainer in every major city.
However, there is one quest that is worth doing on every character: the quest that gives you Turtle mount. The quest can be accepted at level 20 if I remember correctly from one of Darkmoon's Faire NPCs (if you don't know where it is, ask someone in the world chat). The mount is basically free and it's getting better every level. Turtle can be upgraded to epic mount as soon as you learn epic riding so it's basically free epic mount - that saves you some gold.

Unlike vanilla, you will never run out of quests on turtle wow, so just download quest addon and feel free to level whenever you like. Keep in mind gray quests here provide full exp.
Fishing is a nice profession to grab some starting cash for bags, if you play horde there is a fishing spot at shadowpray village (30+ location) with floating debris spawn every 3 minutes near the harbour.

Ezmode friendly class is hunter. As a hunter you can pick some important challenges like vagabond and clear the whole map with 37 lvl white bow and obtain donation bank mule at 60 as a reward.
Second one is warlock but a bit slower.

Dont pick challenge of exhaustion its bad + useless.

## Post 112628 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-12-13T22:26:00Z

I can recommend downloading Atlas & AtlasQuest (and AtlasLoot), and once you meet the level requirement for a dungeon, open Atlas Loot and grab every quest for that dungeon, then complete them all in one run. It's a fun way to level and you get some great gear that way. Maybe not the most efficient, but everyone has their own style of leveling.

I also highly recommend getting the Slow and Steady challenge glyph - leveling is just too fast without it, so you won't experience all dungeons and new zones/quests.

If you want to level faster, stand under a tent until you have 150% rested experience. Especially before a dungeon run.

Most of all, have fun. On turtle the leveling journey is a real part of the experience, not just a slog to powerlevel through so you can reach the "real game" as it is on newer expansions. Go slow and steady and enjoy the ride!
