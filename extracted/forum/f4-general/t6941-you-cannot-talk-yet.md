Hello again.

A while ago, I read that the level threshold for being able to use chat channels, whispers, and local chat was permanently raised from Level 1 to Level 5.  I started playing the game again about three days ago and, indeed, I noticed that I couldn't talk or use AddOns which sent to chat channels until I hit Level 5.

I took a couple of days off to do this-and-that, and when I came back, I noticed that I could no longer talk despite my specific character being Level 7.  When I logged onto a Level 12 alt, I asked World Chat if they knew what was going on.  No one responded.  So, I went back to my Level 7 (now Level 8) character and opened a GM ticket about it.  A short while later, I received this response from a GM:
> [13:34:26] <GM> [redacted] whispers: Hello! At the moment, you can write in charts *[sic]* starting from level 10

I was going to reply back, but since my character is below the minimum level for chatter… I couldn't.

I, myself, had plans to roleplay my way through Dun Morogh, but since the threshold was raised, that kind of ruined those plans.  So, here's my question: Did something happen in the last couple of days to force an increase in the minimum level threshold for chatting?  If so, what?

Thank you very much.
