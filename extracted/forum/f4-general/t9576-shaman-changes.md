# Shaman Changes

## Post 62704 by Rahaith — 2023-09-11T04:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=62704#p62704 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Loving turtle wow! Classic+ has always been a big fantasy of mine and I just wanted to share some thoughts on Shaman changes. I think a proper air totem quest would be banger, there's already a place in 1k needles where you can see the air elementals with a sapta, and it just feels so strange having the air totem quest be so.. short and dissimilar from the other totem quests. Another suggestion, maybe we could get an unleash elements spell that does something based on our current enhancement, so if we have rockbiter, it would be a taunt which would further enable shaman tanking, frostbrand could be a freeze root, flametongue could be a burst of fire spell damage which would make flametongue nice on an ele build, and windfury could be like a mini windfury proc but nature damage, that way we have a non-high-threat-generating spell to use on our stormstrikes. Lastly, if troll shaman's racial spell could maybe get reworked a bit, it's kinda the one that's the most lackluster, especially compared to orc's.
Absolutely love all of the changes implemented so far, this is the most fun I've had playing wow in a really long time.
