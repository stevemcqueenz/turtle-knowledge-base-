# Multiboxing and Challenges

## Post 162137 by Thickcolonel — 2025-12-09T08:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162137#p162137 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The rules state:

"It is forbidden to multibox if either character is participating in any Challenges, such as Hardcore, Vagrant, Slow & Steady, Boaring Adventure, Level One Lunatic, etc — characters found to be in violation of this rule are subject to being locked."

But they also say

"Multiboxing rules are not enforced within friendly capital cities, within reason"

Does this mean that it's permitted to play a challenge on account 1 whilst being online on a bank alt on account 2 in a capital city?
