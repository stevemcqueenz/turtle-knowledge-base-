# Roleplay issue: Lack of Empty spaces.

## Post 84534 by Grayhammer — 2024-01-13T16:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84534#p84534 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I dont know where to post this, so doing it here.

I'm a bit upset that empty locations are being filled in, rather than left empty for RP. Many shops that were empty, are now filled In SW. There is no 'empty' tavern anymore. The Blacksmith section in Arathi Highlands that was empty was a fond spot to RP at as a far-flung Blacksmith is now filled with NPCs rather than a PC that could fill that a player has a little spot in the world. Menethil Harbor ship is now filled with level 1 Pirates.

This extends to new content, Not a single small doc or building in Alah'Thalas is empty, I have not found an empty bench to just sit at. I get from a Lore point its overflowing; Player experiences however is not that great. Gilneas is another tragic example of this If I want to RP as a Gilnean I have to go to shadowfang keep in Pyrewood Village to find an empty house and a tavern that has no barkeep and clear upstairs and downstairs. I love the new content, I really do. I am ecstatic with what is being added. It however feels like RP spots that existed are being removed with no alternative.

This is not always the case, Dun Agrath & Hawks Vigil both have a wonderful spread of Content & empty spaces or less cluttered locations for RP and gatherings of players. Other zones just feel to closterphobic and have too much going on for any proper RP. at least from what I can see. I hope that someone sees this and moving forward adds some empty spaces for RPs to happen. Outside of guild houses.
