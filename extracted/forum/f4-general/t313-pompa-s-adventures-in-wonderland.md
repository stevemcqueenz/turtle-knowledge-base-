In one of my interdimensional shell trips, I ended up in Wonderland. There, I met two girls who loved our turtle world, so without thinking twice they decided to come with me.

To earn some money, they are selling us **[Magic Muffin]**, which will make us grow up to 15%, and **[Strange Bottle]** that will make us dwarf down a 15% as well.

If you want to meet them and buy them something, you can find **Agize** at the Orgrimmar orphanage and Alice at the **Stormwind** orphanage.

 *The effect will last until disconnection.*
