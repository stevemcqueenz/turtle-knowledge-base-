 **Survival Tools**
- The first tool that you will need is a Whittle you can get from a Survival Supplier for 50c.

- The next tool that you will need is a Woodcutting Axe you can get from a Survival Supplier for 50c.

- You will also need a Blacksmith Hammer and an Anvil to craft certain recipes. They can be found from Mining Suppliers in town for 20c.

- Cooking Fire is also needed for some recipes

 **Survival Leveling**

 **1-75**
- **1-10**
 9x Dim Torches - 9x Unlit Poor Torch

- **10-15**
 30x Bundle of Simple Sticks - 60x Simple Wood
 *We are gunna need these bundles later.*

- **15-35**

 *These melon seeds can be found from farms in the Barrens, Durotar, Elywnn Forest or Redridge Mountains*

- **50-75**
 25x Weak Healing Salve - 25x Refreshing Spring Water, 50x Remedy Herbs, 25x Simple Leaves

 **Journeyman Survival**
You can only train Survival past 75 if you learn Journeyman Survival.  Every Survival trainer will teach you this skill. (Requires level 10) and level 50 Survival.

 **75-150**
- **75-100**
 25x Crude Fishing Rod - 200x Simple Wood, 25x Fine Thread

- **100-125**
 30x Simple Fishing Lure - 30x Clam Meat, 30x Remedy Herbs
 *Save Simple Fishing Lures for next level range.*

- **125-140**
 60x Bundle of Bright Wood Sticks - 180x Bright Wood
 85x Sturdy Net - 340x Sturdy Rope
 *You need 45 Sturdy Nets for a few levels above and 40 Sturdy Nets to get from 280 to 300 later on.*

- **140-155**
 30x Blackmouth Fishing Trap - 60x Bundle of Bright Wood Sticks, 30x Simple Fishing Lure, 30x Sturdy Net

 **Expert Survival**
You can only train Survival past 150 if you learn Expert Survival. Not every Survival trainer will teach you this skill, you have to find an Expert Survivalist. (Requires level 20) and level 150 Survival.

 **Artisan Survival**
You can only train Survival past 225 if you learn Artisan Survival. Not every Survival trainer will teach you this skill, you have to find an Artisan Survivalist. (Requires level 35) and level 225 Survival.
