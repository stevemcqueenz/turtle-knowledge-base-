Good write up. Seeing the Alliance-bias written out like this is funny. 29s was always my favorite bracket, but it has felt a lot less balanced in TWoW than classic.

Horde did always have Windstorm Hammer, though it used to be a green item and it was never BiS for rogues because the top-end wasn't anything special. Some used it for offhand just for a little extra dps, but it's debatable whether it's worth it over the very fast Vendetta for crippling procs. It was good for shamans and Druids.

I would argue that the lantern hat is just as good for hunters or better than the listed BiS unless they are specifically survival and AP focused. Probably also true of FC/support druids.

I would also say that hunter weapon priority is like this:

Survival Spec Melee: Corpsemaker with Crusader
Other Spec Melee (and general use):  Ceremonial Dwarven Staff with 25 Agility. This might also be best item for ranged burst now as well, taking over the previous need to dual wield for max dps.

Alliance hunters might have an argument for Trashtalon mh, WSG Dagger oh with 15 Agi x2. But why weapon swap, waste good gcd time and spend hundreds of extra gold for a negligible difference.

Ranged sustain advantage still goes to dual wield, Zealot Blade (22 Int & 20 spirit) mh, Bloody Brass Knuckles oh (22 Int). Swap to staff either inside gun range or when your mana drops below 50-60% and you're still fighting. Swap to spirit when below 50-60% and traveling or pushing objectives.
