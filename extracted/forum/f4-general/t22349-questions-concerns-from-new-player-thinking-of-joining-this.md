## Post 155565 by Catyngla (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-14T13:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155565#p155565 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

If you enjoy levelling, go for "Slow & Steady" and "Exhaustion" challenges, at least these 2. You get rewards, and it'll slow down the progress enough that you can enjoy a lot of the content.

Kerenis is right, even if the classes are buffed, you will die if you go for a pack. I currently play a paladin protection, with plenty of blues from dungeons/quests, a spec known to handle packs better, yet I'll sweat with 3 mobs of my level, often having to pop a health potion, and I'll 100% die with 4 mobs.

Regarding the cities, alliance side, it's Stormwind the most populated, by a huge margin. Then Ironforge, decently populated in the evening, but it doesn't have the buzz of Stormwind. Darnassus isn't empty by any means, but it's the least populated. I don't think it has 50 people at peak time on either Nord or Amber. But I've never been on my own either. I think with the boat links in Auberdine, it's busier than Darnassus.
