# Adding two slots to a range of bags is not an optimal choice

## Post 130930 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-14T03:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130930#p130930 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am not blaming that bags are bigger. For accommodating more content in the game, enlarging bags is beneficial to both players and the longevity of the game.

The point is that the numbers of old bags' slots are balanced according to rarity and difficulty of how they are obtained. If adding two-slots to mooncloth bags is considered reasonable, should traveler's backpack and the one from Razelikh quest also be buffed?

My suggestion (although late) is providing a special skill or crafted item from at least one profession, such as patches or additional bags sewn to original bags from tailors or leatherworkers, an enchant enlarging the internal dimension in a bag or a magic making accommodated items smaller, and so forth. Doing so will not interfere with orignial balanced slot numbers but improve our experience in game.
