For situations like these when you are not able to pick up q items:

 1. Ensure you dont already have the q item in your bank
 2. Have no addon active that messes with picking up loot at the time of kill
 3. Try to re-open the lootwindow if it doesnt work
 4. Wait for everyone else to have looted the q item and try again

Unfortunately for you, you will likely just have to go there next id and try again
