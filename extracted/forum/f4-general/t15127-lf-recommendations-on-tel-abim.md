## Post 102822 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-10-09T17:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102822#p102822 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

People have said in full confidence that PvP in general is more plentiful on Nordanaar rather than Tel'Abim without a doubt, since Tel's only got like... I think 900 players total? So if the faction split is fifty fifty, then 450 people for you to play with active at peak times.

Nord is different in that- while people are less willing (or just not flagged all the time) to do PvP, they're also EXTREMELY more plentiful. There's loads of people levelling alts or playing for the first time, and a decent chunk of them have warmode on. Plenty of victims >:3

Around 5k folks peak times for Nord. All of which you can play with, including some limits ofc, like hardcore or faction specific quests. Gearing will be easier, you can play with classes of the opposite faction, making raid content easier, on top of people already being geared more than on Tel'abim, which means-- again, faster clears. Generally, the raiding experience will be much more lenient, forgiving, and easy to get into if you want to gear for PvP in BGs or WPvP.

TLDR: Nord has more purely due to population and a pre-existing community that's been around longer than Tel'abim by a huge margin.

I've noticed that Tel'abim is actually a decent bit more welcoming than most people might say. I've also noticed that some people can get a bit snippy and butthurt when you tell them the obvious truth. Don't just listen to me though, the place has actually been growing recently as it was actually lower in population than now, so business is booming. Hop in and give it a shot if you feel like it, I'm not saying it won't be fun, just not as easily accessible.
