There's a couple of things to consider between Turtle WoW & Blizzard's Classic WoW...

 1) Content - Turtle WoW has all of the 'true' classic content and more. The Turtle Devs have done a really good job releasing old/custom content, scripting events (Gurubashi Arena), and being present/responsive to the Turtle WoW community. Hard to say what the new 'classic' will be like with the talk of sharding and other changes. And I really doubt their developers will ever be as responsive as Pompa & Torta and their team.

 2) Community - The Turtle WoW community is truely unique. The slow-and-steady mindset of this server has created a very relaxed attitude among the player base. The larger private WoW server community is pretty toxic. The go-go-go attitude ends-up being a race to the bottom when people burn through content and then bail when they get bored. We saw this back in October of 2018 when Turtle WoW launched. Players raced to 60 in 2-3 weeks time then had nothing to do because there weren't enough 60s to do anything with and left the server for the next new server launch to try and be first to clear all the content once again.

I have the same mixed feelings with my love of what Blizzard originally created and my love of Turtle WoW now. But today's Blizzard is **NOT** the same team as back in the late 90s and early 2000s. I think Classic WoW is their test to see if they can revive the franchise to simply make more money without having to create new content.

Anyways, it's all speculation until Classic WoW launches. I don't intend to roll a new toon at launch, but I will be watching what the private WoW server community says about it. Things are different this time around because they all know there's FREE servers they can go play on at any time! Blizzard's going to have to offer something of greater value to keep players paying $15/month when they can get the same basic thing for free elsewhere...

Hi guys, I m Athena.

When wow started I was amazed not only by its fresh content, but most of all about the quality of the player base and the stellar community they created as they went along.

I was then, Gnomecrusher, the Troll shaman, a cool ,low level guy, trying to earn respect as a Hordie.

As time progressed I decided to be a resto shaman (healer) and tried to antagonize priests and to show that shaman, contrary to the popular belief, can be a viable alternative  to healing a party or a raid. I was able to do it, I earned my place in dungeons and raids and was happy about it.

But times changed. The game eventually lost its luster for me with the server mergers they tried, as the feeling of community was lost.

Tried many servers later on, other games, but could never gain the feeling of being a member of something important.

I wasn't trying to relive old times, I m wiser than that, but I was looking for something that appreciated the leveling procedure, a place where I got to earn my place by actions and where my actions had significance in the eyes of my piers.

And I found it in Turtle-Wow.

Will I move on with Classic?

Why?

Will I find a better community? Will I find better admins?

I don't think so and that's my answer.
