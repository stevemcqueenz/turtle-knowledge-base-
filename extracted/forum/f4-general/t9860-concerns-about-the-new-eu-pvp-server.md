# Concerns about the new EU PvP Server

## Post 64426 by Queenoona (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-15T14:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64426#p64426 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Howdy folks! I had a couple of concerns regarding the EU-based PvP server that the team is working on. I understand that the team is very busy at the moment and are likely considering many of these things already. The ultimate goal is to have a healthy server with longevity where people who enjoy PvP can roll and commit atrocities to their hearts content.

 1) What will be done to prevent the server from becoming numerically imbalanced in favor of one faction or another?

 2) Can expect to see new PvP content to promote this realms health? The addition of PvE content has always been a staple for turtle that keeps people coming back to see whats new. Without new PvP content, what will keep people coming back to this server?

 3) What will become of Warmode, on a PvP server it doesn't really fit since you're already flagged. Does this mean the EXPrates will always be higher for all players?

Overall I wish Turtle success with this server, but for a PvP realm the worst thing that can happen to it is population issues, whether that be low population to the point that BGs aren't firing, or imbalance that encourages people to pick the 'winning side' and thus further exacerbate the problem. Turtle's strong suit has never been PvP so despite my faith in the team I do worry that this server will not be as healthy as the PvE-RP realm, if you have any suggestions on how to combat these issues, feel free to drop a comment and it may help the team!

## Post 64431 by Queenoona (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-15T14:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64431#p64431 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My own proposals:

 1) What will be done to prevent the server from becoming numerically imbalanced in favor of one faction or another?

I'd suggest that the factions should stay roughly at a 60/40. This is a difficult decision to make because it can prevent people from PvPing with their friends in some cases, but forcing people to roll on the other faction for balance would help to keep things somewhat equal as far as player count goes.
Otherwise a new system would probably need to be added to allow for cross-faction PvP to help fight against this.

 2) Can expect to see new PvP content to promote this realms health? The addition of PvE content has always been a staple for turtle that keeps people coming back to see whats new. Without new PvP content, what will keep people coming back to this server?

Additional beneficial world objectives could be something the team could develop to encourage people to get out there and fight out in the world, they would need some rewards to encourage players to do them but that could even be something as simple as receiving caches of Potions and such for taking objectives and holding them for a certain amount of time. This would likely be achieved through a daily quest or something similar.

 3) What will become of Warmode, on a PvP server it doesn't really fit since you're already flagged. Does this mean the EXPrates will always be higher for all players?

Overhaul Warmode to be PvP benefit instead of a PvE one more honor from kills in the world seems like an easy change, or perhaps replace it with a new glyph that can allow you to fight not only the enemy faction but your own. This would obviously need to be unable to work in certain zones to prevent excessive griefing but it would add an additional dynamic to the choice of playing on a PvP realm.
