## Post 157757 by Reddguard — 2025-10-29T21:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157757#p157757 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I never played Vanilla WoW for more than a few hours, mainly because of the monthly subscription cost. Then one day, I heard about private WoW servers and that they are free to play, awesome! But until Turtle WoW, I had never found one with high quality and that “Blizzard-like” feeling I wanted (i.e. 1x exp rate and that the journey to 60 is an important part and stays that way).

So, while I can’t compare Turtle WoW to Vanilla WoW, I can compare it to all the other video games I’ve played in my life (and that’s quite a lot).

I loved Warcraft 3, especially the Rexxar campaign in TFT. It was so cool! Turtle WoW feels like that campaign, but a thousand times bigger and better.
By now, Turtle WoW is the video game I’ve spent the most hours on, by a huge margin (Granted, as an MMORPG, Turtle WoW naturally tends to accumulate more hours than most other genres). Still, no other MMORPG I’ve tried even comes close.

I’m still discovering new aspects of the game and enjoying all of them — the social aspect, PvP, dungeons, questing, farming.

I like to take my time and explore everything, I hit level 60 a few months ago, found a nice guild, and started running high-level dungeons. I haven’t raided yet, but I’m looking forward to it.

Over the years, I’ve watched the Turtle devs add so much to the world: new skills and talents that often fit well, and new zones and dungeons that are simply beautiful.

With so much /played, I’ve also started to notice some things that aren’t perfect.
For example, I love Alterac Valley, it’s such an epic PvPvE experience but in every game I’ve played (around 50), the Horde has won! At first, it didn’t bother me that one side always wins, because AV was so new to me and there was so much to do. But over time, it started to bother me. It’s just completely unbalanced, the outcome is always the same.
Or take some of the flight paths, they’re ridiculous! You have to fly through three other points even when a direct route would make much more sense.

With time, I likely see more things which need fixing. But all this time I have so much fun.

The Turtle devs pour so much energy into the game, which is free to play. New content keeps coming, and it really feels like they listen to the community.

Turtle WoW is my favorite game of all time — easily a 10/10 for me.

## Post 159107 by Beardozer — 2025-11-10T18:57:00Z

I love the fact that I've already spent 4 days /played and still have yet to see a single gold farming bot. When world chat starts crossing the line from pleasant shitposting to talking politics & religion, a GM steps in and tells them to STFU. The game is actually looked after, which is crazy after decades of playing neglected blizzard realms.

The custom zones and dungeons have been gorgeous and feel like all the gaps in the vanilla world have been filled in. The new quest text isn't written very well, mechanically, because they rarely tell you where you are going or what you are doing. Logging in to resume a quest chain that simply says "Kill this guy", without telling you where he is or even what zone he is in, is frustrating.

The radio is a feature I never knew I needed. The GTA-style in-game ads of fake products are great.

I'm having a lot of fun man. This is the best version of WoW I have ever played, and I've been playing for 20 years.
