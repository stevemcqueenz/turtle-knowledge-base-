## Post 126302 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-10T13:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126302#p126302 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shadow *used* to be desired for its mana/health regeneration from vampiric embrace but they nerfed that ability with a 2 second cast time. Hopefully that's fixed some day. Other than that shadow's damage is alright, but your utility is very clunky now and limits personal performance.

Druids only have battle res, bears are (always have been but even more so now) competitive with warriors as tanks, and the meta rotation for cats does revolve around powershift/shredding but they have added a talent that involves claw and bleeds which seems to be performing alright. You won't be optimal but your raid leader won't be calling you a useless subhuman failure for playing something suboptimal either. People are just chill like that here.
