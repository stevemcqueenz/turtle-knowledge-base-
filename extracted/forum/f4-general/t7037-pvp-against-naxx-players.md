You can build some glass canon mages with pre-raid gear.  Warlock and Shadow Priest are always a tough matchup too.

There is a lot of +nature gear available pre-raid too.  Might be able to roll an ele shaman and be a glass canon in pre raid gear.

EDIT:  If PvP is your focus you will be able to get PvP rare set pretty easily which is solid for most specs/classes.  ZG/AQ20 is run often in pugs and should have enough pieces to supplement/replace PvP rare set and have you gear well enough to compete in most cases.  I haven't seen a plethora of Naxx geared opponents in BGs.  Mostly others working on their rare/epic PvP sets.
