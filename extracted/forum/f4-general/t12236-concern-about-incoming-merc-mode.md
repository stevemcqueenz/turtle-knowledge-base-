## Post 81392 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-16T10:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81392#p81392 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Assuming that currently you have 90 alliance and 30 horde queued, then you will see queue from alliance side because too few horde players are there. Only 1 AV can start. First 30(40?) alliance players get in and next 60(50?) will wait another start.
If there will be 90 alliance: 70 true alliance and 20 mercs alliance and 30 true horde players, then you will see small queue from both sides.
It will heavily depend on priority of mercs over faction players.
If true hordes will be in priority, then you will instantly join. Then it might be 40 alliance vs 30 hordes and 10 mercs. Other 30 alliance and 10 mercs will wait for another bg to pop. Hordes will instantly join.
If mercs will be in priority then you can get AV with 40 alliance vs 20 ali mercs and 20 true hordes. 10 hordes will be pushed to next match.
If people will be prioritised by queue join, then there are lots of situations could happen. They will vary from extreme examples listed above.

If 90 alliance will consist of 10 true alliance and 80 mercs, then there will be no queues. People will jump to first AV available and play.

So the more accurate question is:
who will be in priority on match startup: mercenaries or true faction players?

Gameplay wise i think mercenary mode is a straight upside. Long que times are frustrating and cause a negative spiral (no bg pops, so noone ques so no bg pops).

While the faction that has less players can quickly get their rep/ honor etc done and lack reason to que again (causing - you guessed it - less bgs to pop)

Holding players hostage in ques just for their choice of race was bad design even all the way back in 2004.

I can see why flavor and lorewise why mercenary mode doesnt fit into the game. But gameplay wise, it drastically shortens que times and improves game quality for the majority of players no matter the faction (remember, you can also have no bgs to pop due to your own faction not meeting the threshhold or bestuck in 7vs10 wsgs)

It also finally allows us on the pve server to que up with our friends from the other faction
