# Rich Thorium Veins on Tel'Abim

## Post 88030 by Ramaan — 2024-02-17T19:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88030#p88030 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've noticed and received confirmation that the spawn rate on Rich Thorium Veins has been changed due to prevent bots of farming them.
On Github a dev said that each small Thorium Veins has a 10% chance to become a Rich Thorium Vein.

This change makes it extremely hard to farm Rich Veins for Arcane Crystals on the Server because of the low Population.

For example if you are alone in a Zone like Un'Goro and there are NO Rich Veins currently you, not only do you have to mine ALL small Thorium Veins and Truesilver but also pray to RNG that any of those Veins when they respawn also become a rich vein afterwards. This can result in basically Zero Rich Vein in several hours of farming because the population is rather small on Tel'Abim and it seems other people are not mining small veins because they don't need to.

Could you unfix these changes for a healthy method of farming Rich Veins on Tel'Abim, because as it is right now, noone can farm these veins anymore or even get any arcane crystals.
