# Karazhan Upper Halls scaling, new patch

## Post 105123 by Torqess — 2024-10-28T16:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105123#p105123 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone,

First of all, I'm really happy with this upcoming patch and want to thank Turtle WoW team for all the hard work they've been doing and plan to continue doing.

Current situation on PVE realm is nice and stable. But, I myself is a part of the PVP realm and with my guild we're actively participate in all world events and try to clear all existing pve content on a weekly basis.

Should be no surprise if I mention that average population on PVP realm is not the best at the moment and most guilds are struggling when try to gather full raids for those instances, which literally require 30+ geared and experienced people. We're talking about Emerald Sanctum and Naxxramas.

The issue of low population touched every guild, but who were hit really hard are those guilds which don't use English as a main language for communication. (Russian, Chinese, etc.)
As a result, guilds disband, merge, and their progress drastically slows and overall population on PVP realm decreases.

Yes, we try our best inviting English-speaking people to join our guild and try to communicate with them during raids when we have to.
But still, in the case of our guild currently we're unable to gather even 30 people to start clearing Naxxramas. (25 well-geared people is what we got on a good day)

Speaking of PVE realm, not only they have great online numbers, they also can gather raids from both Alliance and Horde factions, but on PVP we don't have this option.

And yes I'm talking about PVE content on a PVP realm. The reason is, not only we do enjoy both pvp and pve activities, but also that those raids such as Naxxramas, ES and Karazhan Upper Halls provide our characters with much better gear, than we can possibly get from getting pvp ranks 13-14.

So, on behalf of our guild (and PVP realm community in general) I ask you to consider adding a scaling to Karazhan Upper Halls, starting from 20-25 people to make it at least possible for us to enjoy all the hard work you have done for us so far.

P.S.
I understand that all Blizzard raids were designed for 35+ people, but in our situation with low popluation it's highly desirable and I guess is quite possible for your team to also update scaling for Naxxramas and Emerald Sanctum to make it playable on PVP realm for most of the remaining guilds.
