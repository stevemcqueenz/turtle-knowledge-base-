# High Elf Racial Ability Suggestion

## Post 11116 by Splyntrskul — 2021-07-02T08:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11116#p11116 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I just noticed that while using the High elf racial ability, it offers no advantage to High Elf rogues. A simple suggestion is if blood elf rogues upon creation receive an alternative racial ability that grants 60 - 100 energy instantly (much like a Thistle Tea). The reason for my suggestion is I can imagine no situation where a rogue would have 6 seconds up their sleeve mid battle to wait for some additional energy to be channeled over 6 seconds.

As far as the ability goes for mana users and warriors I think its not too bad, but certainly for High elf rogues this is something I would suggest, not sure how do-able it is, but perhaps it could be a simple resolution.
