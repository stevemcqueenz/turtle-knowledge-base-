## Post 52187 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-07-03T13:17:00Z

The way the ranking system works is just like in vanilla. Including the inhumane grind from r10 to r14

The system however assumes the PvP population is bigger than it actually is. This makes ranking up to r6-r8 super easy with just an afternoon a week being enough to get you there over 4-5 weeks. (at least it was when i ranked)
If less than 800 players are in the people who get rank points for the week, it will assume there were 800 and it gives points based on this inflated poolsize.

The top end of things doesn't really change here compared to other servers. If you want to get R13 gear you have to nolife hard, since the decay is as brutal as ever.
