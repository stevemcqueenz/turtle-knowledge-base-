## Post 118829 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-02-03T15:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118829#p118829 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you really want to use LFT (which I don't see why you would, finding groups in world chat or by whispering people around your level is way better), I guess you have two options:

 1. Level a tanking class, but as a weird non-tank turned tank spec. For example 2H Ret Pala tank, or helf-healing Holy Pala tank. Or 2H warrior tank. Or moonkin tank. Or some weird elemental/resto shaman spellpower tank thing?
 2. Level with a friend who plays a tanking class and queue as a group - They queue as a tank, you go as dps or healer, and you switch roles.

Still, it's way better to just form the groups yourself. Then you can make sure everyone is okay with your tanking experiment.
Good luck with your tanking adventures!
