# Warmode Challenge turning off

## Post 154899 by madferret — 2025-10-08T21:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154899#p154899 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Since the 1.18 patch, players wishing to keep the Warmode challenge while at max level have been denied that option: the challenge disappears from your character as soon as you log out. If you want to play with Warmode active, you have to activate it every single time you login. This is extremely annoying for those of us who: A) like wpvp and would like our character to be permanently pvp enabled without having to remember to type /pvp; and B) want to grind pvp faction reputation.

This bug has been in the tracker for a while, and it seems to me like it should be something fairly simple to squash, and yet there have been a dozen hotfixes and weekly patches released with no mention of this issue.

Are there plans to fix this, returning Warmode to its pre-1.18 state?
