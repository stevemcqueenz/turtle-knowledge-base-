# Thank you very Much Turtle WoW

## Post 171868 by Danythepig — 2026-03-23T05:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171868#p171868 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hi! This is a post directed to all the Turtle WoW Community.

I have been playing WoW for about 20 years, I started playing on WotLK and went all the road down until BfA first or second major patch, I don't remember well. I stopped playing retail because it wasn't fun anymore, it didn't feel like a MMO and more like a solo-grinder with some eventually random group-up to beat a dungeon/raid/battleground (with a very toxic community I could say).

So I decided to look for other alternatives for the game. I played a good share of many private servers, but the feeling was somewhat similar, people rushing to do last content, last patch, rush the dungeon in record time, kicking you out at the first mistake, etc, etc.

I gave up the game, told myself "enough of MMOs" . But the magic happend. Browsing on social media I found out this server, and decided to give it one more try. And good lord, what a good decision that was.

This place is magic. There is always people online (by far the most real populated server), they talk to each other, they help each other, they want to chill out and talk, go questing together, group up and discover new overhauled dungeons, take the time to do it right, no rushing, no toxicity. It's like all the stories I heard about vanilla/TBC era, when the game was something else. It made me come back and really enjoy once again gettin online and play.

This is possible thanks to 2 parts, the first one is the dev team. Guys what you are doing is awesome, the re engineering of the game, adding new content, telling new stories.

And the players that sticks around and actually play and enjoy the game.

It's 3 AM around here, and maybe I can be a little tipsy from some beer cans I chugged, but I just wanted to say "Thanks". This place is really great and I'm really happy to be a part of it
