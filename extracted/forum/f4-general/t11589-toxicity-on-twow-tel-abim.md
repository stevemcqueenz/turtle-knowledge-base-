The hardest part of PServer moderation is that there are simply never enough GMs for the amount of poor behavior people can engage in, and outside of paying GMs (which is not viable for a variety of reasons) you will never have enough coverage to catch everything.

I do want to emphasize what Pepesmite said earlier in the thread, though. Oftentimes if a GM tells you to mute, ignore, or block a player, it’s *not* the only action that GM is taking. In the case of inappropriate chat conduct, a GM has to check the logs & then make a decision based on several factors (number of infractions, severity of behavior, etc). However, suggesting that a player block the offender is an immediate solution that helps ensure they don’t bother you while that GM is checking things. Telling you to reach out again if the behavior persists (ie messaging you on an Alt, following you around in-game & griefing you by stealing moba etc) is to ensure that if this player’s behavior worsens, they can move them up the priority queue & escalate the punishment.

It sucks seeing people act like jerks, especially in massive channels like /World or /Trade! Mega-channels tend to be very hit or miss: see how Barrens Chat used to be in Vanilla, or /Trade has been on live for 2 decades now. The best thing you can do is ignore/block people you find annoying but harmless & report the ones who actively are breaking the rules. Keeping /Trade & /World in another window is good even discounting any bad actors: they’re very active/fast channels & mostly helpful for finding people, goods, or answers to specific questions.

You’re going to see a lot of opinions in here that PVP realms draw in more toxicity than PVE realms as well as anecdotes to reinforce that opinion. So if it’s any consolation to here: my experience on Tel’Abim Alliance has been fine. I’ve run a few dungeons, done a few Warsong Gulch, ganked & been ganked a bit in Ashenvale, and so far everyone’s been nothing but neutral to friendly with me.

I’ve only gotten to put a little bit into my Horde alt, but folks over there have been more or less kind to me too. I really hope you can find a group to vibe with on either faction: Tel’Abim has been a lot of fun for me so far and I’d like to see others get the same enjoyment out of it.
