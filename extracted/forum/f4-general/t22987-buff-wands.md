I really hope i am feeding a troll because if op is serious...

Lets start with what role wands fulfill in the game. Wands are exclusively wielded by classes limited to cloth armor. The "caster" classes. All three of which are capable spellcasters as long as the blue juice keeps flowing.
In low levels that manapool is still very much limited and can run dry easily. Wands serve as the easy to use option for those warlocks mages and priests who happened to misscalculate their mana.

Compared to going melee with your staff or wizarding dagger, wands have the upside of range. Allowing you to casually stay out of harms way. Firstly by keeping you out of the way of cleaves and the likes, and secondly not to screw you over by exposing you to the threat mechanic that isless generous to dps fighting in melee.

In that role they serve admirably exactly how they are designed now. They do suck gameplay wise because the game is teaching you that you shouldnt be in a situation where you have to wand for extended periods of time to begin with.

Now lets get to ops gripes with scaling
Cookies stiring rod has 20-38 arcane dmg. With an attackspeed of 1.3 resulting in 22.3 dps
Smites hammer on the other hand sounds mighty with his 55-83 damage. But thanks to its slow swingspeed it only ends up with 19.7 dps.

If we are calculating its innate strength into it as well, its still only 21.3 dps. Still worae than cookies.

But why end there, most mobs at that level range come with 20-30% dmg reduction from armor and an about 15% chance to either dodge parry or block.

Leaving you with about 15 dps actally hitting your target. You need 150Ap or more extra for smites hammer to compete. Which is coincidentally about what you would have at that level.

If you want the weapons to be comparable, you would have to nerf wands because they still have the benefit of range, strong talents on 2/3 classes and can be amplified further by dmg amp such as curses.
