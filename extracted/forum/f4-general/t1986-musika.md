Claire de Lune would be nice and it is probably not a copyright issue. However copyright I think is not an issue. The issue is that the raido stream remain personal, cozy and original.

There are many things that a broadcast station must take into consideration. We are scattered all around the world and finding the right time for the right music require a lot of investigating. You need to know which time what country is presently during raid times or levelling, grinding phases to best please the appetite of their musical hunger. Alas, that is but only one aspect of their work and but only a few songs in particular out of the many more that fill up the rest of the channel's daily playtime!

If we manage to gather a list of tracks that they can browse from then they will have an easier time setting up an entertaining show.

It is useful to link composers and albums of large variety a repertoire since if they find the free time for it then they may make the playlist themselves. However what would prove to be the most useful is to collect individual musical pieces, songs and soundtracks. Preferably from ancient times or the most modern that which has not yet seen the surface.

However the main aim shall be to gather the relevant Warcraft music from the earlier games and conjure a preferance list - a collection of the track titles in order of preference of the realm or the forum's collective userbase - since as far as I know the radio doesn't stream from old content and it would help them.

Even if you don't know all the original tracks by heart - like myself either, only a few - but decided to start browsing through them on a hinge. Stop occasionally and say "Hey, this one ain't bad!"  and post them in a list, it would help the cause!
