Dear Turtle Team Prime,

I understand your frustration with the user base, but if you expect people to stop talking about the server situations by repeatedly closing their forum threads and chastising them for expressing their concerns, then I have some bad news for you.

It's appreciated that you have a roadmap which is designed to resolve the current "crises", and I understand you asking people to "stay patient" and "let you execute all the plans" instead of "putting an additional pressure on the team", but you have to understand that people are irritated, annoyed, frustrated, sometimes entitled, and they need some way to cope with the stress of being unable to play the game that, in some cases, they've invested time and/or money into.  And some of them choose to make forum threads about their frustrations.

If a thread spirals wildly out of control and turns into an insult contest, then yes, by all means, step in.  Otherwise?  Don't try and silence people.  Let the people speak their minds.  Let others raise counterpoints.  And for Pete's sake, stop calling them "unnecessary".  That, in itself, is meaningless as a *lot* of forum threads are "unnecessary".  If you clarified *why* these threads are "unnecessary", in particular, then that would be different.  As it is, though, it just seems like you can't handle honest feedback about a heated situation.  You're **turtles**, for crying out loud!  Use that thick shell of yours to shield yourselves from "additional pressure" instead of needlessly *snapping* at people!

Thank you for your time,
J

To reiterate, people are whipping each other into a frenzy both on the forum threads and general channel on TWoW Discord server. It is right for the player base to express their dissatisfaction but, altogether, it's just a bit much, to be honest. Also, it's one thing for people to express their dissatisfaction, but a completely different thing to start theorising as to why the TWoW team are taking particular courses of action. This whole things seems to have brought out the worst in people.

I get it; people are very keen on playing TWoW and thus equally irked when their experience is hampered. However, at this point, there are just too many destructive posts/comments, which I'm sure felt very cathartic to create, but will most likely cause harm to this project. Remember, this is a small(ish?) team delivering TWoW especially when you compare it to something like Activision Blizzard. The whole relationship we have with TWoW staff, relatively speaking, is a lot more personal than what you have with big corporations. As such, each of these destructive posts/comments probably cuts deeper than you might think!

As it currently stands, TWoW is dealing with a 'nice headache' - it's becoming too successful and could potentially collapse under its own weight if it can't find the appropriate infrastructure solutions. But you know what might also be a breaking point for the TWoW team? Having to stomach too much vitriol from the player base! Imagine how tragic it would be if they just came out and said "sorry, we can't take it anymore... the conduct of the player base makes us just feel like the whole project isn't worth the effort anymore....".

So, my advice for anyone who is struggling to remain civil with their feedback, is just to.... do something else..! I have no idea why people are subjecting themselves to multiple hour long queues especially when it's combined with current server instability & restarts. Why not occupy yourselves with another hobby for 1-2 months and come back to a, hopefully, resolved situation? Even then, I would keep your expectations low. I know they've just released a timeline suggesting that things will be resolved when the new patch comes out but anyone who has been involved in Project Management knows that unforeseen issues crop up and the plan inevitably moves to the right!
