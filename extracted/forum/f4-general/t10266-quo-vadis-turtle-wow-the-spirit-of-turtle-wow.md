# Quo Vadis Turtle WoW? The Spirit of Turtle WoW

## Post 67047 by Oomentaloo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-26T22:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67047#p67047 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello my dear turtles and turtlelettes and especially dear developers.

The last days, weeks and month on Turtle WoW always left behind a weird feeling as something is not right.
I became a fan of the server over the last 2 years or so with breaks, but I always admired the vision for a lively world with a slower approach to game. In the last months there was a big influx of more and more players and it got a bit tumultuous and unfriendly.
Many chinese players seem to look for a well maintained WoW server because their official servers got wiped. But also new western players came in droves with a different view on WoW than let's say the old garde.

I think I am not one of the old veterans that shaped the very friendly and helpful community, but every time I play on the server I like to contribute to it with bad jokes and help with quests or mobs where I can with respect to my own time.

I felt now a new wind blows on the realm of Turtle WoW. New, fresh, PvP server? "Fresh Server when?"
I thought that was against the ideals of Turtle WoW?
Was cross-faction grouping really just a necessity and not part of the idea of world where the player chooses their friends without restrictions?

But enough rambling and to the point:
 **I am very grateful for the time on the server you created and I just want to ask the developers to give us your new (or not new) visions and goals for the Turtle WoW culture.**

Of course, you can lead the Turtle WoW server in the direction you want, but I and maybe others like to know where to. Maybe around the new patch would be a good time.

Yours truly.

The diplomacy system was a good way to make a point that putting aside the loyalty to our faction and seeing beyond the conflict on an individual level is an exception in the warcraft universe, not the rule.
And I like the fact that it is a choice Turtles make, to get together in a hostile world, you bend the lore in game with your actions, instead of outside of the game with retcons.

I believe that the new pvp servers purpose was to strengthen the english speaking community by attracting more people, also because of the upcoming patch.
But as we've both noticed, these not-so-turtlish folk are attracted to the idea like moths to the light, so having them move there is good for the original server.
I think with the right people, world pvp can be a very good experience, it has been on many occasions here, Turtle WoW players don't do world pvp like you are used to on pvp servers, I can tell that the conduct is more honorable.
And I can spot the other kind of players right away, I expect that to become less frequent once the new server opens.

They may not dictate the course of the server.. well at least for now, but the majority dictates the overall feel(!) of the community and therefore of the server. And the feel of the server definitly has changed. Not because of the content or because of balancing changes, but because of the people here.

So at this point I just hope, that the course of development will stay the same, 'cause I can't find something, that is really bad (at least not right now), but I'd wish(!) for the community to return to its old self again.

I think the "Fresh" thing isn't as bad as some might think.. but maybe my definition of fresh is another than everyone elses..? In my opinion, a fresh server is for those, who want to start anew alongside everyone else. Where you still have the possibility to form the economics and the community, since everyone else is in the same boat of being "new" and "low". I personally get the urge of starting "fresh" at some point.

Can compare that to Minecraft, or 7 Days to Die.. at some point you have reached everything you wanted to, and now you create a new, "fresh", save file, since you want to restart from scratch.. but not just alone, but with everyone else as well. I understand, that this isn't for everyone, but i understand the urge for it.
