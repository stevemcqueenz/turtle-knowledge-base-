Hunter, shaman and druid all have both ranged and melee options, so perhaps give them a try, and play around with the different specs.

Druid in particular also gives you a bit of insight into how both rogue and warrior play (before anyone complains, i said A BIT of insight), introducing you to both rage and energy mechanics, and then you have caster gameplay with boomkin, and healer gameplay with resto.

Shaman is the only other class able to play all four roles of melee dps, ranged dps, heal and tank, but is purely mana based regardless of spec.
