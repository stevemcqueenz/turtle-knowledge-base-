I'm not the best one to answer (fairly new myself) but since nobody else has:

 1. A lot of people are interested in battlegrounds (based on what I see in chat), but the server is massively skewed toward alliance. So I figure if you want to do a lot of BGs, going horde seems like your best chance.
 2. I see some people advertising for raids, but nowhere near as much as when I played classic. When I do dungeons, it's not rare to have people who've never been there before. Likewise, the new player chat has a lot of questions that suggest there's a significant population that's actually new to WoW...not just old timers playing on a private server. I think that reduces the amount of raiding, but it also helps with a more casual atmosphere.
 3. A lot of people talk up the pfUI addon, but I haven't tried it myself. Since so many people use it, it's probably the easiest one to get help with.
