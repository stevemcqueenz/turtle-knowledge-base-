## Post 159596 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-11-14T14:41:00Z

As far as Seal & Judgement go, the,yare independent of one another. The seal part only procs on your auto attacks, the judgement part can proc off of any direct damage someone does. Including spells, ranged attacks and some aoe abilities such as arcane explosion.
You as the paladin can benefit from both at the same time. But its only worth judging it on bosses. Single trashmobs die often way too quickly to bother.
This is true as long as your mana is limiting the party. If you have a shaman or paladin tank that is more mana starved than you, judge away.

As a rule of thumb, you use holy light only when the people take too much dmg for flash to keep up. Holy light is less mana efficient.
UNLESS you are very comfortable and the tank takes very little dmg. Then you can throw him a holy light every so often and keep meeleing for longer. But if yout goal is just healing as much/long as possible in 99% of cases, flash wins

Holy strike has a talent for added scaling at lvl 35-40. Before that it scales 5% with addheal per target (so basically it has no scaling)

Crusader strike itself costs mana, holy strike refunds its cost. Alternating CS and holy shock will get you oom extremely quickly. Rather stick to holy strike and flash for the meat and potatos of your rotation.

For auras: always go with ret aura in dungeond. It helps the tank a lot with threat.only switch to devotion if the tank is dying or at risk of dying a lot and only swap to conentration if stuff sticks on you.

Hand of Protction is a good emergency save for any dps. Yes it disarms them. But if they ripped threat of 3 mobs as cleaving dps, a little dps loss could actually be beneficial ;)

I like judging every time my cast speed increase for holy light is inactive. But otherwise, trashmobs usually dont need you to judge as a healer.

Holy strike doesnt scale with weapondamage, so swapping weapons isnt realy beneficial. Even if you have perfect weapons for healing/ holy striking. Swapping weapons incurs gcd. You loose 3 seconds swapping every time for a 6 second cd. Sont bother.

As far as actually hitting goes, it speedsup the dungeon and is always welcome as long as you can do so SAVELY! But healing is your priority. If you dont melee for 3 seconds nothing will go wrong. So i would t worry too much about it
