## Post 33269 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-01-27T07:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33269#p33269 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I like the mechanic of downranking. It fits that as your character gets better at being an adventurer (levels) he gets better at casting the same spells. But that shouldn't mean he can't cast a weaker form with less effort (downrank)

There are however some effects i think are a bit too good when downranked. The worst offender here is Seal of Command. The effect of the seal itself doesn't get better with higher ranks, so the max rank version is just a downgrade unless it is judged.

Frostnova for the most part falls in the same category and there are a few more spells that are a bit too good when downranked.

Having all these spells however as different spells rather than different ranks of the same spell does suggest to your character that all of them should end up in your toolkit and on your actionbar.
