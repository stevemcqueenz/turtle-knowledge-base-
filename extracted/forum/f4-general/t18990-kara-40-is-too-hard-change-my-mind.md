# Kara 40 Is Too Hard: Change My Mind

## Post 129227 by Healingrain — 2025-05-01T09:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129227#p129227 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Just want to preface this by saying I have done content on Retail that has been several orders of magnitude harder; this isn't about my personal ability. I am saying that tightly tuned raids like Kara 40 are a poor fit for the Vanilla game.

Wipes are too time consuming and too costly given the consumables in the game, which is really just time consuming again if you think about it. There's too many players with too many potential points of failure, too much disparity in raid composition, and too wide a skill disparity between players in 40-mans.  Merely finding good 2-4 hour timeslots for your 40+ roster to all log on and accomplish something can be very hard on a logistical level, especially for more niche time-zones or specialist guilds. There's also tons of relevant raid content all competing for attention for these timeslots.

The hardest content should involve fewer players, and if you really must insist on hard 40 mans; large changes should be made to trivialize consumable costs and hasten wipe recovery at the very least. I will also say that if you truly crave very difficult content the Retail game is unironically a better fit for you, the skill-cap is way way higher.
