There are two breakpoints in player resists you can consider (the occasional madman who runs in a full resist gear against your class should be respectfully avoided), you have the basic 20 to negate the benefits of Gift of the Wild, and then you have the beefier 80 to counteract Gift alongside the specific resistance buffs/auras provided by Priests, Paladins, Shamans or Hunters which are all 60.
