# Tour of the empty areas of Azeroth

## Post 107280 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-09T19:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107280#p107280 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You're probably thinking, "Turtle has already added a ton of content to Azeroth, so much so that I don't know if there's still room for further exploration of both continents," and I'll tell you, we still have Azeroth for a while.

With the thousands of areas forgotten in the lore itself by Blizzard, plus the ton of areas that are a complete blank canvas for the Turtle team to unleash their imagination and take us to new areas with their own lore, Azeroth Vanilla is a wealth of possibilities in terms of new lore and mysterious areas that can be explored and developed even further, all the potential that Blizzard wasted is there for Turtle to use in their new patches, let's list a few:

 1. We have this large area next to the Wetlands and Loch Modan, in the future Blizzard used it for the Twilight Highlands and then... it fell into absolute oblivion, well, it is assumed that Grim Reaches will be here in the Mysteries of Azeroth Universe. How big will it be? Nobody knows, but we will find out very soon.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1nCh5nuejt2AMF9ROB3HrAK3PgC3K8LMj/preview]

 2. Next up, we have this vast area located behind Stormwind, next to the Burning Steppes and behind Dun Morogh, Deathwing wiped it off the map in the Cataclysm leaving just a crater in Retail, but this is where Northwind is presumed to be located, will it be that big? Will Stormwind finally get a new entrance as access to this area? Nobody knows.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1aVfbmNRo5Gq4pUjQtjKRONej91jjGi8T/preview]

 3. Our next stop is a twin location of the previous one, located on the opposite side, between The Badlands, The Burning Steppes and the Swamp of Sorrows (What a bunch of names and areas to be in), completely wiped off the map during the Cataclysm by Deathwing in Retail, it has a peculiar shape, a peninsula and a large wasteland... what could be here? Who knows, but what is certain is that nothing good can come out of this place due to its location.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1PJQsxn9nrqgVwK3AZYX2VqRT169QfJ_1/preview]

 4. Next we will see a long coastal area that begins in the Blasted Lands and ends in Stranglethorn Vale, a large border on the continent of Azeroth.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/147lnUNvvqsEZK_h9x-IyX2Rwi1xxT4bi/preview]

 5. Arriving at the opposite end of the continent, where the history of the Human Race began, in the Arathi Highlands, lies an area hidden among the mountains, empty and unexplored.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1pN93hkuF5v-HhVpPFCE3qPBZzk030i2s/preview]

 6. Now we will move on to the area most awaited by many, devastated by the Scourge during the Third War, massacring the vast majority of the once glorious elven race known as the Quel'dorei, Quel'thalas today remains infested with Undead, its entrance blocked by withered and fallen trees, no one knows what has happened in the last 7 years behind those mountains that once guarded a formidable, prosperous, ancient magical kingdom.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1-eKNUiIEXbMt9FX2RYSiY0xt5x2Uq9E-/preview]

 7. Located next to the Plaguelands, hidden among the mountains next to the Scarlet Enclave, lies another unexplored area, forgotten next to the Kingdom of Lordaeron, who knows what horrors have been hidden among those mountains...or perhaps there is something else hidden there by the Scarlet Crusade?

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1yUGk53URPVb8DvqqYb9aRV2rus7xgHxw/preview]

 8. Now, sharing a border with many areas known for their infamous and merciless nature lies an unexplored valley that, if you enter, has a depth much greater than Un'goro Crater... and that's saying a lot. Who knows what things are hidden there considering its location close to Silithus, Un'goro Crater, Thousand Needles, Feralas and Tanaris...

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1LVnAdVYSRbiMYHwimwoZE_hXY3XcMLc_/preview]

 8. You thought the previous location could be horrible in terms of fauna? Well, what do you think of an area that shares a border with the former Fallen Kingdom of Ahn'qiraj? Horrible, right? Who knows what C'thun did with an area that is right next door, unprotected by anyone and so far from the jurisdiction of the Night Elves. It should be noted that in Retail, Uldum came out of nowhere here.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1bGo_z4JYDgillJTvgdJNny12aTehZEsp/preview]

 9. At the other end of Silithus lies a small empty area, shared with Ahn'Qiraj...yes, directly connected to Ahn'qiraj...

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/14hSEkLjRygiI0T-ZcsJmLktadvGebvx8/preview]

 10. Did you think you knew all about Mount Hyjal when Greymane's Wall fell? No, there's still an unexplored area... apparently a mountain that rises next to the World Tree Nordrassil. What could the Night Elves be hiding there... or perhaps it fell into the hands of demons?

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1uItXgVcCPd0MtaVcj0E4oBQr5-h3Noev/preview]

 11. Last but not least, hidden among the mountains next to the Night Elf Capital, Darnassus, is a small unexplored valley.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1ovsMhylvr49JAzXOTTHrjWDSTUOTRzC0/preview]

As you can see, there is still a lot of Azeroth that we don't know about, hidden areas that Blizzard forgot or simply erased from the map during Cataclysm, areas that Turtle can take advantage of and give a new lore to the world that they have been creating over the years... that's all for now, I hope you liked this little post I made to remember the many areas still pending to be used in World of Warcraft: Mysteries of Azeroth.   happy_turtle_head
