# Jewelcrafting Info ("Prospecting")

Good morning all!  I'm relatively new to Turtle WoW, but have been enjoying myself immensely in the past month that I've been playing.  I found Speedz guide for leveling Jewelcrafting ( [viewtopic.php?t=15560](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=15560) ) to be super helpful, and I wanted to contribute to the community and the forums by sharing a bit of info on what some people might call "prospecting" since that's what it was called in retail.  Turtle WoW Jewelcrafting does provide a way to get gems from ore, but it's a bit more involved than the process was in retail.

About your source: In game I'm Arxaegis a level 32 hardcore Paladin with Exhaustion and Slow & Steady.  I'm currently skill level 186 in Jewelcrafting, so I can speak definitively about things up to that skill level and have suspicions beyond that.

 **The Quick Version:** Prospecting takes cloth, stone, and ore.  All three of these items have tiers.  Getting one loot roll of tier x gems will require x tier x ore, x^2 tier x stone, and x^2 tier x cloth.  For example, if you're trying to get a Jade for that sweet +11 stam trinket, that's a tier 3 gem, so you'll need 3 Iron Ore, 9 Heavy Stone, and 9 Silk Cloth to create 1 Heavy Gemstone Cluster, which will have at least 1 tier 3 gem in it, which might be a Jade.

Tier 1: (You'll need 1 cloth, 1 ore, 1 stone for each cluster)
- Cloth: Linen
Ore: Copper
Stone: Rough
Gems: Shadowgem, Malachite, Tigerseye, Amber Topaz
Tier 2: (You'll need 4 cloth, 2 ore, 4 stone for each cluster)
- Cloth: Wool
Ore: Tin
Stone: Coarse
Gems: Lesser Moonstone, Moss Agate, Crystal Quartz
Tier 3: (You'll need 9 cloth, 3 ore, 9 stone for each cluster)
- Cloth: Silk
Ore: Iron
Stone: Heavy
Gems: Jade, Citrine, Aquamarine, Emberstone
Tier 4: (Speculation - You'll need 16 cloth, 4 ore, 16 stone for each cluster)
- Cloth: Mageweave?
Ore: Mithril?
Stone: Solid?
Gems: ???
Tier 5: (Speculation - You'll need 25 cloth, 5 ore, 25 stone for each cluster)
- Cloth: Runecloth?
Ore: Thorium?
Stone: Dense?
Gems: ???

I have been collecting data about this on a Google Spreadsheet.  Since it's currently just me, my sample sizes are VERY small, but this sheet gives a sense for both:
- the average yield of a certain mineral (how many of a certain mineral will come from a cluster on average - useful for determining how many you'll need to make if you want to get a certain number of gems)

- the likelihood of getting a certain mineral (your percent chance of getting that mineral from a cluster - useful for determining how many you'll need to make if you want to get ONE of a certain gem
