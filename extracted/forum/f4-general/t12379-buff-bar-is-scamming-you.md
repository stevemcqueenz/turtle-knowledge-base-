Greater Arcane Elixir, Dreamshard Elixir and Dreamtonic all stack on buff bar but Dreamtonic effect does not appear in Better Character Stats addon after drinking Greater Arcane Elixir or Dreamshard Elixir.

The question is: why the buff bar lies about certain buffs being active and lets them stack?

I know more examples of buffs on buff bar showing certain value that is not correct. If you have any buff that is upgraded with talents its value on buff bar will not be adjusted.
