## Post 112354 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-12T08:09:00Z

Hunter is a nice and beginner friendly choice if you can get over the hump of learning how to wrestle your pet
(I recommend going to the options and putting the pet action bar ability 1 (thats where pet attack lies) on a easy to reach button and doing so for the button that corresponds to passive mode on your pets actionbar. This allows you to send your pet in and call it back very easily.

If you look for a class that is decent at both melee & range you might also enjoy shaman. They are tanky and very flexible. So a good choice to learn the game on.

I second the shaman as an option, it's a very interesting class, with a lot of different ways to build it. There's not a lot of "weaving" between melee and range though, even if you go for the spellhancement build, you're mostly in melee.

If you want to weave, go for hunter. There are macros for managing your pet more easily.

Another interesting option is the paladin, with the recent class changes, it can be built as a melee healer, although this is not great for leveling, especially with the nerf to Holy Shock.

## Post 112357 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-12T08:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112357#p112357 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I agree, shaman (enhancement) can give you that 'battle mage' feeling. But prepare for a lot of drinking, they are not the best with mana, specially leveling!

Pala: Pretty good class, can do anything but lacks range. You can use magic but mostly to your immediate surroundings.
Warrior, Rogue: no magic, you prob wouldn't like them.
Druid: That's a valid option, too, maybe even better than shaman since you jump between forms and some forms don't use mana at all. You pull as yourself and turn into a cat or a bear, you heal, cast, tank, whatever you want. I'd propose them over shamans because it's a really annoying 'first experience' in WOW when you just gotta drink half of the time.
Mage, priest, lock - lack melee.
Hunter: Can work as melee hunter but there is deadzone (a distance where melee doesn't work yet but ranged doesn't work already). Can be a bit annoying and overcomplicated + they don't use magic, they use guns.

Tldr: What you're asking for is flavorwise an enhancement shaman. But in my opinion, based on what you look for, you'd enjoy druid, maybe feral druid the most.

For addons, PFUI is very good but requires a bunch of set up. Decursive if your class can remove negative effects. Threatmeter if you wanna tank. ShaguDPS maybe. I personally don't use it, makes me too competitive and a bit of an arse. Bettercharacterstats so your character panel will show more to you.
And of course:
<https://github.com/CosminPOP/TWOwenWilson>

Only choose addons that you truely need after playing the game without any addon gadgets for over a week. In my opinion, those addons are mostly garbage. I've seen some avatar addons that show insufficient information by replacing characters' look with a class icon. There is also a stupid addon that turns the breath bar into a pure number display in an HC death collection video. So don't use those addons that are functionally equal to the original. Sometimes a garbage addon may kill your HC character.
