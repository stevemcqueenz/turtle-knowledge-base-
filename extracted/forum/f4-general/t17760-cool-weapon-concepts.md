# Cool Weapon Concepts?

## Post 121083 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-27T05:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121083#p121083 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Been doing a bit of doomer posting and troll feeding lately. Figured I'd take a much needed break from that with something fun for myself and the community. More or less what the title says, but I'll write down some more specific 'rules' to this lil activity I'm cooking up.

The goal here is for everyone to come together and share an idea for a weapon (or multiple) with its stats, name, maybe a proc effect, etc. You can either be hyper specific with stuff like: Intended level range, every single stat, maybe even an icon or an existing model, etc. If anything looks "OP!!!" or "Broken!!! >:(" then that is probably my intention because it would be cool or funny.

Feel free to do the same with other items, too! Armor, all sorts of consumables, heck- even a mount or pet. Just cool stuff to have. Les get to it.

 -Kneecapitator-

Named this one after the character name of a friend's dad. Was a gnome, so- fittingly, this two handed axe does more damage in the hands of a gnome, goblin or dwarf. While I'm not sure if such an effect is possible, it'd be cool to have anyways. Something like an increment of 2% would make sense. Since it's meant to do big devastating dismembering blows, I imagine high agi and slow swing speed would make this for a devastating weapon that'll really hack those legs like butter!

 -Shiv of feasting-
A dagger that deals 2% extra damage to beasts, has some basic melee stats like strength/agi (but not a lot) and has a proc effect that steals some health from an enemy. Great for one handed tanks that prefer fast attacks or even just DPS for the proc effect. Last I remember shamans usually wore daggers or other fast weapons because rockbiter weapon had a base unscaling threat amount, which meant faster attacks would be better than slow ones since each attack generated the same amount of threat without taking damage into consideration. Might not be the case now though.

 -Bluemusic wizard sword-

If any of you have heard of the fabled Azuresong Mageblade you'd know it's an absolute godsend for any mages who get it in Molten core. While this sharp thing has a similar name, it's kinda just... Worse. Like- half the spell power, no spell crit, and slightly less int and stam. Maybe you could get it from a shady vendor after a rep grind/questline or something?

 -Hunter's Stave-
With stats like int, strength, melee attack power, and agi, this weapon is kinda supposed to be the epitome of a 'hunter weapon', with its special yellow word quote being: "Laden with magical energies that empower might and mind, this balanced fighting stick (the gem is for decoration) is the perfect tool for any experienced trap layer, beast tamer or sniper."

 -Peashooter-
A gun that does abysmal damage, and not much else. Literally just exists to be the WoW equivalent to something like the fallout game's BB gun. What this means is something absolutely worthless. There's just no point in having it. But that's what makes it funny to me, I guess.

That's kinda all I got for now. Stoked to see what y'all might have.
