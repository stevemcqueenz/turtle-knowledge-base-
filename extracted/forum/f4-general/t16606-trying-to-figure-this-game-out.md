I’d suggest looking for a newbie-friendly guild – there are lots on Turtle WoW that focus on guiding new players. You could check out the official Discord or use in-game channels like /world or /lookingforgroup to find a group. When you join, just be upfront about being new and wanting to learn – most people will be cool with explaining things.

For questing, if you’re looking for a buddy, try hanging out in starting zones like Elwynn Forest (Alliance) or Durotar (Horde), where you’ll find other new players. You can ask in /say or /general if anyone wants to team up for quests. For dungeons, just jump into a few casual runs to get the hang of it – places like Deadmines or Wailing Caverns are great starting points.
