# Top 5 Most Broken/OP/Annoying Abilities in PVP

## Post 100821 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-09-06T19:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100821#p100821 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**1. Holy Strike**

It's getting reworked/nerfed (better late than never) but I cannot stress enough how utterly disastrous this nonsense has been for PVP balance (not to mention various other aspects of the game's health).

 **2. Ice Block**

Frost Mages hit incredibly hard and can become literally Invincible (twice with Cold Snap) -- allowing time for their cooldowns to refresh. And stall for time (which is extremely important in PVP Battlegrounds btw)
You WILL waste time/mana/cooldowns shooting at this outrageously OP immunity shield as you cannot predict it.
There are no counterplay options whatsoever.
This ability was double-nerfed in TBC... and then triple-nerfed in WoTLK -- becuz it was STILL overpowered.

 **3. Polymorph**

In a 1v1 this ability is truly insane. It's a "you can't fire me -- I quit" button.
Mage starts to lose? Bad luck? Outplayed and outskilled? Doesn't matter. Just sheep and run.
Mage can just try again until they get lucky (with crits for example) and win.
No idea why Blizz gave this incredibly long CC a mere 1.5 sec cast time. It should be 3 seconds to match Mind Control.
Should be a desperate last resort -- not a cheap coward button to casually chain at ones convenience.

 **4. Purge**

There are so many Magic effects in Vanilla that you can spam this on people endlessly.
Even works on Melee players cuz 'Crusader' enchant proc is a 'Magic' effect!
 30 yard range and very low cost. Gear doesn't matter. You can be in Greens and harass Naxx gear Priests.

 **5. Viper Sting**

 35 yard range and very low cost. Gear doesn't matter. You can be in Greens and harass all enemy casters.

Thats my Top 5 most hated abilities in PVP -- but im interested to see your own lists as well.   maintenance_turtle
