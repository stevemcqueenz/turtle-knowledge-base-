## Post 136774 by Thexdylldolan — 2025-06-23T21:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136774#p136774 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Too many guilds are restricted from running this raid consistently for the sole reason that having exactly 40 players (no more, no less) is not possible on this server for many but a small number of large guilds.

If you are in one of the many affected guilds, please share your experiences below so the devs will implement raid scaling bringing new content into balance parity with existing content.

I have been playing TWoW for over two years, and I have always thought that raid scaling was a foundational turtle wow addition, allowing smaller guilds to play all content, whether if you had a few people on vacation that week, or even if you are still recruiting to fill out your roster. Scaling is a complete net positive for the growth of the server, and community at large.

Thank you for your time reading this post, and sharing your thoughts(if you choose to)!

I think scaling helps growing guilds progress. I also think that is a bad thing, because you never "grow into your shoes". You run a light roster and continue to progress until you hit Kara 40, then you're forced to completely change the dynamic of your raid composition and roster, which comes with its own problems of finding good players, weeding out bad players, new personalities, you name it.

If you were forced to grow into a 35 naxx roster with a jump to 40 man in Kara, it might be a lot easier. Instead, you have numerous guilds running a skeleton crew of 30 man rosters clearing Naxx to BIS.

I think it's a mute argument at this point anyway. Developers have made it clear that Kara is designed and meant to be a 40 man raid, with no intentions of scaling until Grim Batol. So either recruit more people (I'm sure you're doing this), combine with other guilds (some guilds are running combined rosters), or don't do Karazhan until Grim Batol comes out.

Been there before, roster boss sucks. I don't think scaling does anything for you other than try to de-value the difficulty of Vanilla, which is coordinating 40 people to cooperate together. Nothing about the raids or classes are hard, it is all about getting 40 people on the same page. There's more than enough players on this server to support that, they are just stuck in roster hell because scaling to 20 and 30 man allowed them to progress further than they should (think 8 groups of 30 man rosters versus 7 groups of 35 man rosters, and extrapolate downward to see my point).

That's just my opinion though. If it were my choice, scaling past T2 would not be a thing.
