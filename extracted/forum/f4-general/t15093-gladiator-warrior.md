## Post 102591 by Czasku (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-10-05T23:02:00Z

Season of Discovery tried this, but its actually not working, you gear like a tank and pretend to be the DPS. The problem lies in Vanilla giving no truly reason to play with shield except Armor boost. You can play 1h+shield fury only to see that your only advantage is using worthless Shield Block and Shield Bash instead of Pummel. The entire Vanilla Protection tree is garbage, and the only reason to be 1h+Shield, the Shield Slam ability is capstone of Protection.
If Turtle had to make Footman playable, they would have to rework entire Protection tree with optional Capstone talent "Gladiator Stance" working something like this:
"Dauntless Combat Stance replacing your Battle Stance. While in this stance you may use abilities requiring Defensive Stance and Battle Stance. Reduces your Armor by 30% but increases your attack power by 30%. Your Shield Slam now deals extra damage based on 25% of your attack power instead of Block Value and your Revenge is now Assault, in addition your Shield Block is now Shield Charge"
Assault - Instantly cause weapon damage plus 81 to 99 (at max rank) and apply Sunder Armor effect (5 seconds cooldown 15 rage. shares cooldown with Overpower)
Shield Charge - Charge to your enemy knocking him down silly (25 yards, 30 seconds cooldown 5 rage, shares cooldown with Intercept, 0.5 second cast).

Personally i think Turtle should experiment with giving players unique playstyles with cons and pros like main hand only Rogue. Spell Power DPS Paladin. Smite Priest. Melee Mage (Battlemage). Lone Wolf Hunter or Demonic Sacrifice Warlock.
