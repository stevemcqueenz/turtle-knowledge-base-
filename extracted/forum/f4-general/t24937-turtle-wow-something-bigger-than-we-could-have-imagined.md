# Turtle WoW, something bigger than we could have imagined

## Post 176285 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-04-27T14:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176285#p176285 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

I've been thinking...

This is bigger than we imagined.

Remember Nostalrius? Well, thanks to them, more decent Vanilla servers emerged than before, because they released the core.

Unfortunately, Turtle only goes up to version 1.17.2, missing the last two major patches. However, some servers have already appeared using that core; the most famous is Sandworlds, which has already started slowly increasing its player capacity. And who knows, as BigSmerf said, with enough support, it could be the next Turtle WoW.

This is undoubtedly bigger than we imagined, a fan project taken to the extreme in terms of content. Without a doubt, a worthy successor to Vanilla, which, honestly, I can no longer play as a Vanilla server without feeling empty because of the lack of all the Turtle content.

There's no doubt this will usher in a new era for custom content. No more random content; from now on, more than a few developers will try to make sure that what's added makes sense.

Cut off the Hydra's head... and two more will grow back.

The only thing confirmed is that our data and the server are doomed. A few more points or questions are listed below.

 1. Whether content in Turtle 1.18.1 update is lost, will be reused in another Turtle-based server or mergered by a non-Turtle-based server.
These are all technically possible. However, using the 1.18.1 content seems improbable for servers based on 1.17 code.

 2. Whether Turtle WoW itself is lost, replicated or mergered into some other servers.
Due to the gap between different source codes and lack of labour, other servers adopting Turtle's entire resource is also difficult.
More importantly, the core value of Turtle is its purist and fundamentalist orientation of lore, which I have never seen on any other products in market. So, new affiliated servers would probably transform into something disfavoured by veterans of Turtle WoW.

 3. The 1.17 source code and any server based on it are still subject to Microsoft legal action.
So, even if the banner of Turtle WoW is raised again by someone, it would be hunted down again by those people, especially when player base is growing big.
