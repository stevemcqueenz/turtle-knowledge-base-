## Post 124840 by DumbleJum — 2025-04-01T06:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124840#p124840 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle WoW has several ways to highlight quest objects on the ground, especially if you've installed "pfQuest."  Some suggestions:

 1. Check Addon Settings

 - Display the pfQuest settings in your game.

 - Look for quest item display choices.  Make sure relevant settings are enabled.

 2. Add Extras

Try other highlighter extensions if pfQuest doesn't work.  Some common choices are:

 - Questie: This addon tracks quests and highlights items.

 - TomTom: This navigation addon can increase item visibility with other questing mods.

 3. Configure Interface

 - Check the game's interface options for item visibility or highlighting settings.

 - Enable any quest item visibility settings.

 4. Use Macros

Create a macro to find quest stuff.  Even without visual highlights, it can help you find things.

 5. Community Forums

Consider asking at the Turtle WoW forum since you mentioned it.  From their experiences, the community may have unique answers or ideas.
