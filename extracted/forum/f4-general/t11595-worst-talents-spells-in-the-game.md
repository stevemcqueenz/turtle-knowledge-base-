# Worst talents/spells in the game

## Post 76707 by Blackduck (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-09T20:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76707#p76707 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Talk about the worst talents or spells or whatever in the game that you find to be mostly useless or heavily flawed.

 1. Blessed Recovery
> Blessed Recovery	Rank 3
>  Talent
>  Requires Priest
>  After being struck by a melee or ranged critical hit, heal 25% of the damage taken over 6 sec.

While this seems like a cool talent it is heavily flawed in that it overwrites itself.  So if you're constantly being crit by say a rogue with a high chance to crit the thing will just overwrite itself before you can get any healing.  It also doesn't scale with healing so you don't really get much back from taking it and it requires 3 talent points.  This talent would be better if it provided immediate healing instead of a hot.  Also I don't believe it has been buffed at all on Turtle. Unlike say the druids Frenzied Regeneration which has been buffed considerably compared to vanilla. So much so it practically makes druids invincible when they use it.

Also I really think this talent belongs in the disc tree and probably should be combined with Martyrdom since they both activate when you receive a melee or ranged critical hit and have the same duration.
