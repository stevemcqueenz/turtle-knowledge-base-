# Party comps at raids

## Post 96126 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-01T07:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96126#p96126 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I wanna share some interesting party comps which I've seen during raiding. At the same time, if you know about any, please share here. Goal is to help people making more effective team comps.
I won't mention everything, like putting a shaman into a melee group or moonkins into a caster group. Only lesser known ones.

 **1. SM/Ruin warlock in a tank group**
SM/Ruin is the only viable warlock raiding spec which doesn't sac the demon. You can put such a warlock into a tank group for the imp buff + the plus threat via the fire shield. Downside is that your tank group probably has a shaman and the warlock would not benefit from WF/agi+strength totems, unlike a melee which you can also put there.

 **2. Shadow priest in a tank group**
Similar as the warlock and the same downside. In TWOW I think this is the better solution since shadow priest is a valid raiding spec on its own. The passive health regen helps the tanking group a lot and the mana regen helps the shaman.

 **3. Arcane mage in a mana group**
Arcane mages provide some mana regen for the party based on their own. The trick here is to make them innervate targets and they need to evocate when they are being innervated. This fills up every nearby party members' mana bar. If you have a long fight, casters struggle with mana and they are positioned at kinda the same place, arcane mage can make sure that they are suddenly almost at full mana mid-fight.

 **4. Elemental shaman in a caster group**
Only if you have enough shamans for the melee. Ele struggles with threat and tranquil air totem stacks with blessing of salvation (I think not additive but multiplicative but still, helps further reducing threat). This means that an ele shaman in a big DPS caster group can make sure that those casters have to hold back much less + the ele is not forced to use windfury and can suddenly have better threat management.
