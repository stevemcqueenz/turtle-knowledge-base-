The issue with firemaw stems from disarming suppressors, if you disarm them all, then the pools can spawn in the back much further away. I recommend only disarming the frst 2 or 3 rows from lashlayer, that way the max range they can spawn at is fairly close.

A lot of poeople disarm them all by instinct, which in this case is the wrong move, especally since they added a shortcut so that is no longer necessary.
