## Post 125953 by Wolf of Rage (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-08T07:09:00Z

Both, honestly. Maybe more bias towards Tanks.

The situation is that whatever can do DpS or tanking, it's usually DpS.

Whatever can do DpS or healing, it's usually DpS.

Whatever can do all three, it's usually DpS or less likely, healing.

Tanking therefore should be less picked.

But as said, you can cover for both -- and even all three -- by going to the Gurubashi Arena, and buying the Goblin Brainwashing Device; you'll be able to store Talent builds and swap them around every once in a while upon reusing it and picking your choice. It's essentially a custom makeshift precursor to Dual Talent Specialization seen in later iterations of the game.
