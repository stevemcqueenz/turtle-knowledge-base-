# How does your build feel like?

## Post 106177 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-03T16:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106177#p106177 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I'd like to know how different builds feel like atm.
Only tried shaman for now, can give my 2 cents.
Restoration shaman feels unfairly strong but a bit boring. Old T2,5 chain heal spam required you to wear bad items for that chain heal bonus. Now, with added mana sustain and being able to wear actually good items, I always feel like being flasked. Went one BWL with the char, shaman healing numbers feel unfair compared to the others.
On the flipside, I think devs had the intention to make some kind of rotation for the shaman (undertow). This iteration of resto feels a bit brainless because chain heal is so good, you just won't really use anything else most of the time.
Also, flametongue coexisting with WF made totem setup even less of a choice. You get in your melee group, put down your melee totems (except for water) and you are only required to think if fear is a possibility. Even good old grace of air and tranquil air got retired 99% of the time.
In short: resto feels extremely strong but a bit braindead.

Wanted to try out shaman tanking, at least to see the rotation. Me tanking ZF with my high-end raid gear is not representative (guys needed a tank, I wanted to see how rotation works). LS helps with mana sustain while also doing something and biggest change compared to old shaman tanking was the fact that you usually have buttons to press.
Mostly feels good. I understand that it's been created for dungeon tanking so some aspects are missing, like a proper defensive though. Got some oom but I guess mostly because I'm in a learning phase and mismanaged mana.
Declaring and keeping up AoE threat seems easy but taking over many mobs if a DPS gets rogue and pulls 3-4 mobs on himself is a bit janky. Overall, I feel like it's an okay build for dungeons but we'll see if community wants more, like BWL tanking etc. It feels lacking at essential things for that.
Also, shield bonus (f.ex. shield specialization) feels weird. Prot palas are themed about big block chance, it's understandable for them and warriors can block on demand with shield block. For shamans having talents which increase blocked amount (with 2 talents) while block staying consistently low feels bad. Shaman tanks have 1 exceptional value, the dodge. I'd have used this theme rather since the two random added block amount talents feel out of place.
What I'm thinking about.
Shield spec (now): +5% block chance and +30% block amount.
Enhancing totems (now): Stoneskin totem giving +30% block amount (among many other things).
These effects are nice but out of place since the shaman can't block consistently and they mostly rely on dodge.
What I'd propose f.ex.
Shield spec: +n% block chance for m sec after a successful dodge.
Enhancing totems:  Stoneskin giving an additional +n% damage reduction for m sec after a successful dodge.
This way the main flavor of shaman tanking (dodge) is used here and it is used in a way which actively and reliably helps damage mitigation.

No idea about enh DPS, ele or the other classes, haven't touched my other chars yet.
What have you guys experienced?
