## Post 59161 by Wafflecrusher (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-26T14:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59161#p59161 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For the most part I disagree that veteran players will quit. Some may, but in order for the server to grow and thrive new players need to be able to play, and with veteran preference that means they will never be able to play. Furthermore, it's important that this issue is visible, especially to veteran players. They are typically the most vocal, and if they are placated it quiet calls for much needed change (whether that be HC servers, or an expedited Chinese server). Giving que preference is ultimately wrapping up an infection with a bandage.

Saying there is no room for new players is problematic. The devs rely on new players and their donations to keep funding the server. If new players stop playing, donations will dry up, and the people who will suffer ultimately will be those who have played the longest. However, if it is the case that there is no room, and the devs truly believe it, then they should shut down the ability to create new accounts.

I also disagree that the proposal that blizzard killing their game had to do with capacity. It was lack of moderation, failure to understand what people loved about the game, and generally all the bad behaviors manifested by corporate greed. Additionally, saying that giving veterans preference is good customer service is an issue as they are not guaranteed to actually be customers. However, following that logic, then players who donate should be able to pay to cut in line, as customers, a position which I fundamentally disagree with. Or players who have donated should be able to skip in line, again something I disagree with. We as a community should not be divided based on how long we've played, or how much we've paid.

Veteran que preference is bad for the game as it masks underlying issues, it stifles new growth, and creates a two tiered community. We all love this game, we all want this game to thrive, and Veteran Que preference creates many more problems than it solves.
