First of all, I will say that I have several lvl 60 chars on the Nord server. Always played with war mode. And one week on tel'abim gave me more fun and oldschool vanilla expirience then Years on Nord. In minę opiniion Playing on nord comes down to standing in the city and looking for dungeons or entering bgs. and that's it. This game is created for two factions fighting each other. and every enemy encounter in the world arouses great emotions, and low lvl pvp is probably more fun than 60s. the game is harder, getting equipment is harder, every trip is an adventure. and every blue object is a hundred times more enjoyable. Hardcore gaming has been added, which adds even more spice, as players defend each other. I think this is the best feeling of old good vanilla wow expirience. But we have one minus - populatio. 500-600. it's enough for dungeons, but a bit too little for world pvp. If we reached 1k+, this server would never have any competition anywhere. I just want to say that if you're starting a new adventure, or you're tired of spamming dungeons on Nord, this is the place for you. because here, when a human sees an orc, he attacks it, not hugs it!
