# Something needs to be done about consums, but for real this time

## Post 90462 by Yggd — 2024-03-17T14:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90462#p90462 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Current price of Plaguebloom: 3.38.99g / herb
Current price of Mountain Silversage: 2.98.99g / herb
Current price of Icecap: 1.49.99g / herb
Current price of Gromsblood: 1.45.99g / herb
Current price of Ele Earth: 4g /
Current price of Black Lotus: 49g / herb

They first changed some recipes to change the mats you need to craft them. That was a smart idea however they created new problems with some, or came short of solving some issues (i.e. firebloom instead of dreamfoil for GFPP, icecap instead of dreamfoil for GfrPP, no change to GSPP and GNPP which means prices remain insanely high). The fact that every single end-game elixir recipe asks for not just one but two plaguebloom - and not changing that - is a massive, massive fail. The economy adjusted and inflated prices accordinly, for the worst. They definitely came short of finding a good and permanent solution.

Then they changed the elixirs where they persist through death. A very good solution and welcomed one. Instead of spending 400g per night, you spend about 60-70g total. A really smart idea - but they didn't address the fact that this would affect consums prices, again for the worst. People jack up the prices where (Plaguebloom and MSS) prices are just astronomically and comically high to a point where you just can't buy them at that price, you're better off farming them. And I'm not even touching the aspect about Flask prices; the Blotus price is high, but it's nothing compared to the other mats required to craft them: 30 Gromsblood / Stonecale oils / Icecaps /MSS, that's just ridiculous.

However, you can't farm them because bots are sitting on nodes 24h/24, 7d/week, waiting for respawns. And then you have people who whisper you and tell you they'll pay you 300+ worth of Flasks in order to join one of your guild's raids just so they get one specific item. Can you imagine the amount of hoarding and market manipulation this causes if just one guy has 300+ flasks of titans ready to be spent on just one item?! That's insane, purely insane.

So what are we supposed to do? With (probably) 3.5 on the horizon according to the road map, how are we supposed to get gold / prepare for consumes in order to have decent progression, if nothing's done about consumable prices but make them much, much worse, every single day?
