Honestly? I wouldn't mind something like this if it was done in extreme moderation. Maybe once Kharazan comes out there could be a quest tied to getting fragments of keys that unlock a certain book or bookshelf which is also in the raid. Each one has knowledge on a certain stat. Simple ones like strength, agi, int and spell power. 10 for each of these stats. Since this is meant to be one of the highest raid tiers, they could be written as the very deepest secrets or minute details when it comes to understanding one's potential, and offer that extra little bit of 'final' knowledge or technique needed to naturally increase your capabilities without gear and trinkets. I think it'd be neat to have more player power tied to progression outside of gear, but- again. In moderation. No need to do very much beyond this.

Just one of a few routes you could go, but I wouldn't mind if this was the only way you could achieve this kind of permanent stat bonus. It would be more fun/flavor or an extra grind to do than for the purpose giving raw player power, because it's at a point where 10 of a stat is just a little extra you could really care less about. It's like a food buff or something.
