Being moved there automatically isn’t possible, but you can walk (for example, from Durotar to the goblin island). It’s a bit of a trek for whoever’s traveling - though not too bad if you’re a troll or orc.

Once you're there, you’ll be able to do the same quests together.

yes characters can travel to other starting areas at any level, even level 1. quite the journey, but doable.

make sure to get flight paths along the way and save to an inn so you don't accidentally teleport all the way back to your own starting area.
