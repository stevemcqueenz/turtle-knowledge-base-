# What is the best class for tanking?

## Post 38794 by Liatris — 2023-03-24T06:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38794#p38794 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been noticing that dungeon queues are lacking in tanks. I was considering the idea of rolling one, but it's been a very very long time since I've tanked in a group. The tank I played was a monk during MoP, so I have zero experience when it comes to tanking in vanilla.

I hear that warriors and druids (as bears) make really great tanks. I've also heard that paladins and shamans can tank decently as well. The options have me pretty torn. I know each class has their pros and cons, but I'm still unsure on what I should go for as a beginner.

## Post 38797 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-03-24T06:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38797#p38797 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First you need to decide what your strengths are in the hypothetical scenario where you're tanking. Can you multi-task well and switch targets frequently to distribute threat while managing your resource? Would you have an easier time just pressing a few AoE tank skills and managing your mana instead? Do you prefer complex gameplay over simple (more buttons versus fewer buttons)?

Each of the three primary tank classes come with their own strengths and weaknesses, some easier to supplement than others.

War can do tricks. Like berserker stance/intercept/defensive stance/taunt a mob that run after healer (you need talents that keep rage when switching stances for that), charge/thunderclap, aoe taunt (once a 10min) that sometimes is very handy when things go wrong, emergency Shield Wall, even Disarm like when Herod does aoe spin etc. Domoralizing Shout gives some aoe threat but also lowers damage income, Thunderclap does that too by slowing attack of all mobs. Put that on top of 10% damage reduction in defensive stance and you get some protection. Can interrupt spells with Shield Bash that has nice threat itself so you can use it on non-casters aswell.
It requaires some practice tho. There is lots of warriors because of myth that wars are OP created by fury warriors in raids (which apart from that warriors are not). Arms and tanking requires most skill of any class to really play properly, that's why lots of wars suck.
But pals are now awesome tanks for dungeons, also bears are chad like always. I would pick one that seems most fun.
