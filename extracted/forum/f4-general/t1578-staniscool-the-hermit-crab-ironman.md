This is Stan. My alt.

He is a Human. Only human. He needs no spells, no buffs, no food, no potions, no clothes, no armour, no weapons, and no oxygen. He needs no sleep and therefor is always exhausted. He needs no friends. If he dies, he will be forgotten and subsequently deleted.

Hello TW gang. It's been months since I've been on. And to debut a return and make the game a little more challenging for me, not that it wasn't already as a human warrior on ironman before 1.15, back last year.

I present to you, Staniscool. A level 5 Human Warrior.

These are the details of his quest.

 - He is an ironman. If he dies, I delete him. Only exception is to gankers and hostile hordies and roaming factional NPCs
 - He is to always be nude and have no weapons. Bare fists only.
 - Because he's just a human he will have no mana, no buffs, no training, and no talents.
 - He will not eat food or drink potions, or receive buffs. Any he is given will be sold and deleted, respectively.
 - He will not talk. I just came up with that rule writing this. No more talking. He will not join a guild either.
 - He has hardcore mode and exhaustion. .5 XP and no rested bonus.
 - Everything is to be sold on either AH or vendor.
 - No professions or secondary skills until level 19.
 - No quests until level 19 to allow buffer for quests lower than level 25. I would go for no quests at all, but milestones are necessary otherwise I'll die of boredom.
 - No mounts. I'm keeping the starting turtle though.

Fighting hostiles rather than neutrals will be difficult of course. That is why one of the optional rulings is to kill only neutral NPCs or stray hostiles and NPCs two levels or a level lower, or equal
