# Stormwraught Ruins - my new favourite dungeon

## Post 146243 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-16T18:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146243#p146243 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I explored new dungeon Stormwraught Ruins and I must admit it delighted me. It is no doubt S tier dungeon on my list now and probably the best dungeon Turtle has ever released (I haven't seen Dragonmaw Retreat yet though). Dungeon is huge! 12 bosses, many rooms and even a small puzzle. It has interesting lore combined with quests in Balor, everlasting feeling of uncovering mystery of what Stormreaver clan is doing behind the scenes.

I like the dark art style of the dungeon so much. It feels unique compared to other Vanilla dungeons and somehow it still looks like made in Vanilla era. Blizzard could learn from the artist who designed it.

The only bad thing about this dungeon is that it's made for low level characters. Why is it bad thing? Because this dungeon is so great that you want to do it over and over again. But you can't because you would eventually outlevel mobs inside and its gear stops being relevant later. I wish this dungeon was made for level 60... imagine farming your BiS gear in this masterpiece before going to raids.
