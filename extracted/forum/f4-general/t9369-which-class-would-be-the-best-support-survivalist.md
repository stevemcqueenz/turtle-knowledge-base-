Druid is your best bet if you want to support with solo capabilities and survivability. You're a great tank in almost all content from early dungeons to endgame, desirable melee dps with buffs and utility that a warrior won't bring to a group, a solid healer, and I guess the ranged DPS spec is a thing?  Also on top of this arguably one of the fastest levellers. In short, druid is probably the best class for flexibility, group synergy, and efficiency during the early game. People often shift (get it?) on how they perform endgame, but rarely if ever pay attention to how well they shine early on imo.
