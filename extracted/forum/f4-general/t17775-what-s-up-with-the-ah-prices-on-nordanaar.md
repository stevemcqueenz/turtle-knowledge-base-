# what's up with the AH prices on Nordanaar?

## Post 121177 by Stormthrash — 2025-02-28T01:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121177#p121177 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't know if i'm crazy or not, but i'm seeing a bizarre pattern on the auctionhouse for gear and trade goods. At first, i put up some gear I got from killing some things in the Barrens, and used the search results of said item for reference. I raised my eyebrow because the search results were not above the default auctionhouse prices. "Weird" I thought, but I chalked it up to people wanting to make a quick buck, even if it's a couple silver above the base price. I notice this pattern for quite a few levels, then I notice something that really had me in disbelief. My inventory was saturated with wool and silk cloth, and when I went to put it up, the prices that people were selling them at were BELOW vendor prices.

It's not just limited to cloth items either, it spans to gear, trade goods, consumables, etc. People are either selling at default AH prices or below vendor prices. I don't understand what's going on. Do people not know what they're selling, or are they just that generous?
