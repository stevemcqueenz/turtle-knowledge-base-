I am a Chinese player, and yesterday I saw some images in the Chinese community. These images show that there are groups of Chinese studios using scripts and other tools to create a large number of alt accounts to occupy Twow servers, in order to discourage Chinese players and redirect them to domestic WoW private servers. I am very angry and deeply sorry about this situation.

At the same time, I sincerely suggest that Twow's team establish a mechanism to check, handle, and ban such accounts. Otherwise, no matter how many servers you add, these studios will continue to use the same methods to make server queues so long that it's impossible to play the game normally.
