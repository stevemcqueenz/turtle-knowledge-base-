## Post 1018 by Tokka — 2018-10-30T15:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1018#p1018 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings / Mok'rah Adventurers of Azeroth,

Introducing the Portable Meeting Stone!

After seeing some of your input on Roleplay, we have decided to look into helping players with their Roleplay needs. We will be adding a "hub" for players looking to get involved with the realms Roleplayers, so they can connect with others looking for a way to engage in, or begin a storyline. We have taken the idea from this post: [viewtopic.php?f=17&t=111](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=111), and have selected two main hubs from your suggestions in this thread.

We will be implementing items to use like meeting stones in these two locations, which will be 2 amazing taverns to mingle with friends: Alliance, Stormwind, Oldtown -- Horde, The Barrens, Crossroads. These items/stones will be available for FREE, just talk to Speedy! Our staff is working hard to make these locations as cozy and enjoyable as possible as we speak!

Thanks for all your wonderful ideas, and helping to make the Turtle WoW community a strong one!
