# Not a suggestion but a petition - item level display without addons

## Post 140407 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-19T03:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140407#p140407 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At least at level 60 in Turtle WoW, gears differ in quality (colours and stats). The need for checking an equipment's item level is legitimate and it is at least a recommendation objectively based on the game's database when we are picking up raid members and generally compare players gears.

Unlike "gearscore", the item level system is an in-game layering mechanism initially implemented by Blizzard, the creators themselves, rather than from a third party. Now we have Emerald Sanctum and Karazhan-40's new gears, whose data still comply with Blizzard's concept of item designing, with clearly shown item levels in Turtle WoW's database. So, just make it unnecessary to install additional and complicated addons for this simple function. Just like the display of gold received for selling out an low-level weapon, item level is merely part of Turtle WoW and it doesn't bite you. If you insist that anyone inspecting item level display is a noob, you are showing a noob's bias and paranoia.
