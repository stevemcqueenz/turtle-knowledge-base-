Salutations, Turtles.
 -----------
I thought I’d hop on over to the forums to introduce myself before starting my adventure.
My name is Lantsei! I’ve been lurking about for a while, but I’ve finally decided to come on and join in the adventure as a Warrior of Mulgore.
My journey will not be a simple one though, no no.
I have decided to undertake, what I am calling, the
Hardcore Nomadic Challenge
This challenge goes as follows,
level to the highest level you can, following these conditions:
 -----------

- Using Turtle WoWs Hardcore Mode (before level 10)

- No Dungeons | Battlegrounds | Raids

- You’re only able to use gear you yourself have crafted, or bought from a vendor, no quest reward gear

- Auction house can be used to sell materials, that's it

- All buffs must be self-applied e.g scrolls

- All secondary Professions are allowed, including the Survival Skill

- No aid from other players (buffs, gear, etc.)

- You are given two lives, if you die twice, it is all over; may your soul walk the Halls of Valor
 -----------
If you'd like to attempt this challenge, know that you're free to tweak the rules. I've simply made it harder for myself. Any class can participate, if you're as mad as me to try it!
Throughout my journey, I'd love to meet you all in a roleplay setting. Don't hesitate to give me wave!

"The shaman's old callused hand gripped the wood handle of his issued mace. He dug his toes into the coarse rocks and sand outside The Den. He had submitted himself to retraining as a shaman. The elements of this world recognized him, he knew. They had saved his life in the mines of the barrens more than once but they would not answer when he called. So here he stood, among orcs a third his age and trolls learning the shamanistic ways. Being sent to kill boar as a test of his skill. He jogged off into the valley until he saw his first target. He reached up to beseech the elements for power and smile came to his grizzled face as he felt the energy crackling around his outstretched hand. He focused on the boar and thrust his arm forward, sending the lightning hurtling through the dry air."

"There was a time in this orcs long life when the lightning that flowed through him would incapacitate the wild beasts of his home world. He reflected on those times as the lightning pounded into the boar and the beast, barely phased, charged towards him in anger. The orc met the charge, a tusk digging deep into his leg as he brought the mace down and ended the boars life with a savage blow. Pain seared through his leg as he felt the dampness of blood roll down and into his ankle wraps. He felt something else. He felt alive. He called upon the elements to heal his wound and they did. He looked at the mace and with a smirk stuffed it into his bag. He would survive by the elements or he would die."

"With renewed vigor and a sense of purpose he'd not felt in years, the orc carried on. He killed the boar necessary, taking meat, hides and tusks back to The Den. He gathered cactus fruit for Galgar, helped a troll that had fallen to a large black scorpid, and cleared a den of demons that had gathered around a corrupt warlock." Rekgor leaned back against the wall in the Razor Hill inn, "Now young ones, my story is at a break." He tapped the ash out of his pipe against the wall before carefully tucking it into his pack. "After I've rested a bit I set out to Sen'jin to help the trolls there, and to see what help I can render and to see what the elements of Azeroth have still to teach me."

Rekgor smiled as the small crowd dispersed. His body ached now and his movements were wrought with stiffness and pain. Days swinging a pick and tossing rocks were nothing compared to running and fighting. Sleep came to him easily, swinging in the rope hammock. He dreamed of his younger days, prior to drinking the demon blood. The atrocities he committed while under the influence of that foul substance could not be forgotten but for the first night in years they were a side note to his slumber and not the focal point.
