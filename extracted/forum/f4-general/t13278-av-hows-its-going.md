While I have been playing only in Horde in TWoW so you can assume my opinion is bias I must admit Nitaya is actually right that currently there exists backdoor imbalance faction-wise. As far as I know currently there is no other way for ninja-taking Relief Hut apart from going there in stealth which is OK for me. On the other side 2 classes who have "Charge" ability are Druids and Warriors. Unlike the last backdoor that was fixed, this way is not accessible to literally everyone. To be honest I have never noticed someone from Horde was doing this charge-trick but Nitaya's story with screenshots seems to be true so I assume he is right - Druids and Warriors can do backdoor in Aid Station. It's still uncommon to see anyone doing this trick but I think it's a matter of time just like in every meta-game.

So the problem is Relief Hut can be captured by 2 classes (Rogue and Druid in stealth) while the Aid Station can be captured by 3 classes (Rogue, Druid and Warrior). Though this imbalance really exists let's ask ourselves the question: does it matter that much?
I mean, of course Warriors can charge toward one guardian in Dun Baldar but if there is at least one defender in Dun Baldar, they will probably notice it and can safely kill the Warrior from distance and if defender is melee, they can easily stop Warrior's action too so they won't be able to capture the Aid Station unless there are 0 defenders. How about 2 other classes? Druid and Rogue can go stealth so they won't be noticed - they have the same way of capturing graveyard as in Frostwolf Keep.

Supposing that units guarding both graveyards didn't get upgrade and there are 0 defenders, Rogues can clear packs on both sides (Commander can be sapped in Horde's base). However, while the Warriors and Druids can easily clear pack guarding the Aid Station, it's not the case for Warriors and Druids of other faction. Correct me if I'm wrong - I'm not Druid nor Warriors specialist. Druids are able to do it, just root the Commander, kill the guardians and capture the graveyard, it requires some RNG though because rooted Commander might break out. Warrior can't capture Relief Hut. So in that case, Horde has easier way of capturing last graveyard.

What can be done about it? More invisible walls should do their job so Warriors/Druids won't be able to charge in and use this backdoor. One patroling Commander could also be moved to guard the Aid Station and stay only in this place.

Btw I also think something should be done about pulling Commanders far away from their original spot - best example would be pulling Commander Mulfort down through Waterfall which is basically safe area to kill him for Alliance. Same can be done with Warmasters and Marshals - once I saw one hunter kited all marshals from Vanndar's room to Stonehearth Graveyard while we were having only one graveyard in our attack. That was weird and funny at the same time but we all can agree this shouldn't be possible xD
