I played Classic WoW TBC back in like 2022 and loved Grobbulus for it's PVP status. I found it very fun having to defend myself while leveling every once in a while, and calling for help when some higher level was causing troubles. But like 90% of my activities where PvE and I enjoyed them.

How is that experience in Tel'Abim? I have an Warrior already in Norandar with like 50+ hours played. But I'm thinking if I really want to keep leveling in war mode where I barely have any pvp interaction or go make a new char in Tel'Abim.

One thing that keeps me back is that I mostly like PvE content. I want to get my first character to 60 and try and joing guilds to do raids or just dungeons with others. But I did like how the world felt when leveling with world pvp,
Another concern is that apparently the population isn't really as big, and I'm in NA so peak times are when I'm not playing.
I know I could have 2 character but I don't play enough time to have 2.

My main activities are leveling by questing and dungeons, and my end game is mostly PvE content with a little bit of PvP once in a while. I'm pretty indecisive sometimes and I know whatever my choice I'll have a good time, but what do you guys think?

TL;DR I enjoy PvE, but like PVP once in a while, do I stay Norandar or go to Tel'Abim?

## Post 151646 by Sharmaine (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-14T02:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151646#p151646 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Join Tel'Abim, most players there are like you.
The truly dedicated wpvp crowd is minority. A few guilds to pvp events semi-regularly, most of the others are just PvEing. And 2-3k online gives much more breath in open world.
You may find it hard to find a dungeon group as a dps while leveling since x2(x4 with tent) exp means everyone levels too fast to grind dungeons, but raiding at top level is healthy, and there are no queues to pick a flower or dig a mine if you like that.
