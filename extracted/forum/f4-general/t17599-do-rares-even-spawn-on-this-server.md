Hi!

You have incorrect information about Rare mobs in the 1.12 game client.
The spawning system works as follows. No more than 2 rare mobs are allowed on a location at a time.
In Dun Morogh location there are Bjarn, Edan the Howler, Gibblewilt, Great Father Arctikus, Hammerspine rare creatures.
This means that the chance of Bjarn appearing is 1/5.
The next parameter that is taken into account is the population of the location.
Another not unimportant parameter is that rare creatures do not have a clear spawn time. It can be really 45 minutes and can be 8 hours.
There have been cases when players have been waiting for respawn of a rare creature for several weeks.
That is why these creatures are called rare. And to hunt them you need to have patience and luck
