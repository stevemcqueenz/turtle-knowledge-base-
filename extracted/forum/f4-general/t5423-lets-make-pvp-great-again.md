# Lets make PvP great again.

## Post 33141 by Somnam — 2023-01-25T14:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33141#p33141 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would have posted this in a dedecated PvP section of the forum, but I haven't noticed one. Maybe it's time to start one?

I guess the lack of PvP activity is the only big downside of the server, as it goes for me. I see BG's popping in the 10-19 and 51- 60 brackets. First one is a twink paradise, the later is catching up with players that maxed their characters. What about in between? Those are fun too.

I am currently leveling my first character here on Turtle and in the 20-29 bracket it's a rare occasion one can actually go on a WSG, maybe on a sunday evening.

It takes a team of three to carry a WSG, a team of five to make it playable. How about starting teams on the Aliance and Horde side? How about starting to make appointments for PvP events, heralded on the forum and discord so people can actually know when to quee up with a chance of playing some PvP?

How about making some custom events - for example RPing an extra boss in Hilsbrad Fields so horde from Tarren Mill has an extra challange  there with players their level.

What would you people suggest to make the PvP more live and attractive, so people start having it while leveling?
