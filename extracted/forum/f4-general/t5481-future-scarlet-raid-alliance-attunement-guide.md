# Future Scarlet raid alliance attunement guide

## Post 33610 by Valkin — 2023-01-31T05:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33610#p33610 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello there turtles! I took notes as I quested my way through the Scarlet raid attunement quest chain. At least it's supposed to be attunement quest chain but tbh I'm not 100% certain since the raid is not out yet.

 1)Talk to SW’s crier NPC. He patrols around, I found him in the trade district’s northern bridge. He will give you a letter
 2)The letter leads to Mathias Shaw. He sends you to Lakeshire
 3)In Lakeshire you have to talk to Foreman Olsow (by the bridge), Dorin Songblade (inside the stables) and Magistrate Solomon (Town hall). They will tell you that the town’s children have been kidnapped by the Scarlet Crusade. Report back to Mathias Shaw.
 4) Caravan Meets Caravan quest: you are told that a caravan hailing from the Wetlands towards Aerie Peak has been attacked. You are asked to investigate the remains of the caravan. After investigating the caravan, you turn the quest at Kixxle, the vendor by the intersection that turns north towards Arathi, from the Wetlands main road.

The caravan is north from Kixxle. The three clues are the dead mountaineer dwarf, the canister and a small wooden box.
 5) Thandol Span quest: you have to defeat the scarlet ambush and bring an NPC back to Menethil Harbor, specifically to Captain Stoutfist, who is inside Menethil Keep. You will be attacked by four scarlet initiates and then by the named NPC Vladeus Springriver.

ATTENTION: quest seemed bugged to me. After I defeated Vladeus and spoke to him to have him follow me back to Menethil, the four other scarlet mobs respawned and started chasing me. They have a Hammer of Justice stun, so be mindful they can interrupt your spells that way.

 6) Are you true to your nature? quest: you are sent to interrogate Vladeus, who is now in the dungeon beneath Menethil Keep. The quest is completed by interacting with the piece of paper on top of the table. You are offered two options: to persuade or to torture him. I went for persuation because I’m not a psychopath.
Just go through the dialogue options. Vladeus conveys that he and the other initiates were recruited by a person wearing a red hood. The Scarlet numbers are low and they are disorganized after the death of two of their leaders (as in, this quest chain happens canonically after the events of Scarlet Monastery). The last piece of information they give you is the name Abbendis. This quest is turned in at the same paper on the table from before.

 7)The Price of Information quest: it starts at that paper, and ends at Captain Stoutfist.

 8)Scarlet Aid quest: go to the basement of the Cathedral in SW and talk to the Scarlet ambassador guy, Brother Crowley
Crowley tells you in a scripted RP that Brigitte Abbendis, daughter of Abbendis senior, must be behind all this. Last she was seen was at Tyr’s Hand, but that this hidden scarlet training location must be a secret passed from the Ashbringer down the line.

 9)Donning the Red Flag quest: Brother Crowley will give you a Scarlet Crusade illusion item, which has 5 charges. You are sent to Tyr’s Hand, where you are to speak to Mavel Brightwood. She will only speak to you if you're friendly with the crusade. I believe but I can't confirm that Brother Crowley will give you a replacement illusion, possibly only if you're still on Scarlet Aid quest.

 10)Mavel will tell you to kill undead in the Plaguelands and get their brains, namely 30 of them. Diseased Flayers can drop the brains. So do Gibbering Ghouls. It seems that you need to kill zombie-type undeads only.

 11)Return to Mavel and next you’ll be asked to bring 50 Core of Elements and 50 Savage Fronds. I went to the AH because I’m not a masochist. All in all it costed me around 10-15g. Cores of Elements were about 3s a piece, but the fronds were more expensive, averaging 18s each.

This is where the chain finishes as of time of writing, 31/1/2023. You are awarded a Scarlet Crusade Insignia which is a rare quality item that goes in your keyring. The quest text describes you being given a map to the secret training location, but you are given no such item. That might be added later. There’s a chance the quest chain is expanded once the raid drops. Who knows...!
