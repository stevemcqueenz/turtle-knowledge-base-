## Post 36897 by Hankledorf — 2023-03-05T17:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36897#p36897 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I use a logitech G600.

Through the "Logitech Gaming Software" interface from the manufacturer, I have my buttons bound to Ctrl+1-9, and for the bottom three I use Ctrl+, Ctrl+. and Ctrl+/. For the "G-shift" settings, the binding when holding the third mouse key, I use Ctrl+Shift and all of the same bindings.

In-game you can bind those keys to any action bar like you normally would, and you can still use normal keyboard bindings and Shift keyboard bindings.

For myself, I use the "Bongos" action bar addon to make visual maps of the mouse buttons in my UI. In the addon I changed the action bar size from 12 to 5, so I get 24 separate bars I can move around. Using this I have visual maps on my screen of the keyboard and the mouse, and then bound the mouse buttons accordingly in the Keybindings menu.

Hope this helps!
