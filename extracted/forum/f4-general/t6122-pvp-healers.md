When it comes to PvP everyone wants to heal, usually noone heals themselves.
Generally on alliance side you'll probably get healed a bit more often just because a significant portion of the PvP playerbase plays paladin, so they at least have the "potential" to do it. But most of them are not specced into heal, so your mileage may vary.

If you are looking for support from a Priest you might find more luck on Horde, as more PvP Interested priests roll Undead in large parts for devouring plague. That being said, they do so for their Shadow spec, so also don't take their heals / shields for granted either.

Easiest way to guarantee heals in BG, find a friend who is willing to play heal and que up as group.

Horde side Discipline/Holy or Discipline/Silence specced Priests are highly sought after in group PVP not just for healing but their invaluable defensive dispels which is unique to them on that faction.

Alliance side you can also go Paladin. Holy/Prot is the great traditional "unkillable healer", and they can provide a little extra offensive pressure on this server when the situation calls for it with Holy Strike, buffed Holy Shock in combination with HoJ.

These are basically the best PVP pre-made classes that nobody plays.
