## Post 39287 by Mcnair (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-27T14:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39287#p39287 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While the ability is reminiscent of the 'Charge' spell Warriors have, these boar-type NPCs have their own ability. Or rather, abilities - different boars have some different spells when it comes to their 'charge' ability. Most of these have no minimal range, so they will cast it even if you start the combat at melee range. You can check in the database which spell your favourite boar uses and you can adapt accordingly - in this case you might want to stun/slow/gouge them when they cast their spell and kite a little bit as the buff lasts 3 seconds. Or just be aware of this and start the fights on full hp.
