GILLIJIM'S ISLE & ISLAND OF LAPIDIS: 48-52 (better start at 50)
CAELAN'S REST: Is a Town on Lapidis Island (A)
TIRISFAL UPLANDS: 14-20 (H)
SPARKWATER PORT: Is a City in Durotar (1-10)
HAWK'S VIGIL: is a City in Wetlands (20-30)
DUN AGRATH: is next to Hawks Vigil / Wetlands (20-30)
SORROWGUARD KEEP: is a Town on Swamp of Sorrows (35-45)

You can find more information here: [viewtopic.php?f=2&t=2253](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=2253)
