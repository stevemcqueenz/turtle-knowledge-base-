OK I accidentally found the answer when I made a High Elf to keep from leveling past 1 with my Orc while I figured this out.  It turns out there is a Glyph Master NPC in each of the starting areas that sells the glyph for only 5 copper which you click on in your bag to activate turtle mode.  So yeah Mater was a typo after all. He was easy to find in the High Elf starting area but I am going to have search the Orc area again to find him.
