I think the inevitable problem with questions like this is that each situation/context has its own tier list.

If you forced me to tier the specs/classes in PvP I'd want to break it up into contexts:

 - leveling wPvP
 - level 60 wPvP
 - BGs solo
 - BGs with a partner/coordination
 - Arena/coordinated team play wPvP

PLUS each of these lists would look different at different times in the content cycle and with different gear levels. For instance if you have access to whatever the highest available PvE gear, all the physical dps classes get much better.

I think you meant to edit in that second post, but I'm just gonna go ahead and give you my opinions based off of how cool a specs community is. I'm just gonna stick to PvE since I'm not much of a vanilla PvP guy (unbalanced and unrewarding. Doesn't feel good to me.)

S Tier:

Feral Druid (Tank) Objectively the best tank of all. With a reactive and rewarding maul spam playstyle, this is without a doubt the most fun you can have tanking. Dated main tanks Naxx too. 'Nuff said.

Feral Druid (Dps) is for those who want to put in the extra effort to succeed. Even if you're sidelined, even if you're not the best, you're TRYING, and trying harder than everyone else, and it pays off when someone finally recognizes.

Balance Druid (Laser Chickens, Boomkins, Oomkins, Boomies, secretly a meta tank spec) There's not much to be said here. Moonkin aura is busted and anyone who plays this is a certified gigachad and a maiden haver who mains the goofiest spec because he's the most chill, fun and lovable guy ever. Bonus points if you're tauren.

Enhance Sham (Tank) is a top tier tank spec because anyone who plays it is one who loves to avoid the norm, and do something challenging or impressive. No one gives a shit about a world first Naxx clear if the whole guild is fury warriors, holy paladins, dwarf priests, and fire mages. Give me a shaman main tanking Naxx just once and that'll be a hundred times more awesome.

Prot Warrior. Because fuck fury tanking, and fuck the meta. Shout out to Honorable who mains this.

Shadow Priest. Good DPS while you heal AND provide mana regen for your group? Shadow priests are awesome team players, and kick ass mind flaying all the while.

A Tier:

Enhance sham (DPS) It's okay guys, we know you're not a support spec. Just keep earth shocking and one day you'll be recognized as more than a bloodlust dispenser.

Beast Mastery Hunter: Normally either guys who haven't swapped out of their levelling build to start raiding, or a cool dude who just wants to nurture his beloved pet "Digbick" and see him outperform all those mean fury warriors who said that pet dps is bad and they die too fast to matter anyways.

Combat Rogue (Not outlaw fuck you retail) is a heavily underappreciated specialization, as they lack utility, buffs, and debuffs that warriors have despite being on par with them on DPS. Plus poisons are pointless in raiding for both factions now with windfury

Arcane Mage: This spec is normally for those mages who want to be humble, and just play for fun instead of topping meters and being the best ranged dps pick. Real cool dudes they are.
