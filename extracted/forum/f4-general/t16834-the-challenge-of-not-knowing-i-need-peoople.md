## Post 115013 by MimiPixel — 2024-12-31T17:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115013#p115013 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Okay.. has it happened to us that you are tired of always seeing guides, tutorials, having the best option to know everything about the game from minute 1 and that everything is perfectly calculated following guides and guides instead of exploring on your own? Here is my idea:

The challenge of knowledge is basic, it is to find people who know little or very little about Turtle Wow and the story and want to start from 0... but here is the trick...

 - You cannot use guides.
 - You cannot consume YouTube to search for hidden missions, lore among others.
 -You can't search for anything related to the game (guides, dungeons, etc. (knowledge)
 - You can't use wikis
 -No addons to see mission locations, mission tracking on UI screen and any addon that helps, the idea is to play in the most "pure" version of the game.
 -(optional for more immersion) while you play don't use anything else, not even titok, youtube, etc.. just the game itself to completely immerse yourself in the world, universe, and story.

It's just about discovering it... and the point is to find people who also want to explore the game this way, that everything is by word of mouth, entering dungeons without having any idea of ​​what they are like, entering areas and only having to be guided by the text of the missions, among others... maybe it's too late to find people for this...

But I being a completely new soul in turtle and classic have decided to do this, if anyone wants to join this adventure, feel free to comment, we could talk about it and create a community of ourselves looking for all the hidden secrets! maybe we find something that no one has seen before :P
