## Post 67038 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-09-26T20:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67038#p67038 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, there's this cool button in the character selection screen that says 'Create Character'. It does basically what it says. You get to make a brand new character of any race you want. Either Ally or Horde. (Which race change can't do.) You get to level them back up again and experience the wonderful experience of fighting for hours over quest mobs. And, as an added bonus, you get to keep the old character if you ever regret switching races! "But what if I have too many characters?" You may ask. Well, don't fret, my naive and simple friend, the delete character button lets you get rid of one so you can create another in its place! And, with the help of the turtle team, you can get your deleted character back if you wanted to as well!

Now, a service such as this sounds like it would cost quite the hefty price, considering how much it provides, right? Well, that's just the best part. It's completely free! THAT'S RIGHT, FREE!
