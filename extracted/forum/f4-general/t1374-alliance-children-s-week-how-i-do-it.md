# [Alliance] Children's Week (how I do it)

## Post 8384 by Roxanneflowers (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-11-07T01:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8384#p8384 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I often times read people in world chat saying that they're never doing the "long" travel of Children's Week on the Alliance side ever again.  You need to go to:

The Orphanage in Stormwind (start)
The Lighthouse in Westfall
The Dam in Loch Modan
The Bank in Darnassus
Jaina in Theramore
The Orphanage in Stormwind (end)

For some people, that's just too long a haul to get to all of those places ... but I've developed a pattern for running this in less than an hour (sometimes closer to a mere 30 minutes), which I'd like to share with everyone reading these forums.

Here's the travel pattern I use:

- Get Wormhole Generator from Bank (and any ice cream left over from previous week, if necessary)
 - Go to Stormwind Orphanage (start)
 - Buy Ice Cream (if needed) from the vendor near the Stormwind Auction House (very important!)
 - Fly from Stormwind to Westfall (99c cost)
 - Go to Lighthouse off the south coast
- Once on the Lighthouse island, while down at the water summon the Orphan using the whistle item
- Accept quests (main then the three)
- Run up the slope towards the Lighthouse, crossing the boundary that completes the quest with the Orphan out
- Complete Lighthouse quest
- Use the whistle item again to dismiss the Orphan
 - Return to the Westfall flight point
 - Fly from Westfall to Stormwind (99c cost)
 - Go to the Deeprun Tram in the Dwarven Quarter of Stormwind
 - Take the Deeprun Tram to Ironforge
 - Fly from Ironforge to Thelsamar (99c cost)
 - Go from Thelsamar to the Dam of Loch Modan
- Once on the Dam summon the Orphan using the whistle item
- Run to the center of the Dam, crossing the boundary that completes the quest with the Orphan out
- Complete Dam quest
 - ***HEARTHSTONE*** to Darnassus (***very important!***)
- Summon the Orphan using the whistle item
- Run to the Bank, crossing the boundary that completes the quest with the Orphan out
- Complete Bank quest
- Begin the Ice Cream quest
- Complete the Ice Cream quest with Ice Cream in inventory
- If you have excess Ice Cream in inventory, either put it in the Bank or mail it to your next alt who needs to do Children's Week
- Begin the Jaina Autograph quest
 - Go to the Rut'theran teleport
 - Fly from Rut'theran to Auberine
 - Take the boat from Auberdine to Menethil
 - Take the boat from Menethil to Theramore
 - Go to Jaina
- Get Jaina's autograph
- Summon the Orphan using the whistle item
- Complete Jaina's autograph quest
 - ***Use the Portable Wormhole*** to return to Stormwind (5s cost)
 - Go to Orphanage to complete quest
- Choose 5g bag reward
- Return to Stormwind Bank and log out

Total cash expenditures ... -7s97c and +5g ... for a net gain of 4g92s3c.
You also gain XP and Alliance Faction, so it's one of the few ways you can work towards Exalted status with Gnomeregan (if you really want one of their mounts instead of your own racial mount).

Now I know that sounds like a lot of traveling, but the Hearthstone to Darnassus and the Portable Wormhole to Stormwind really cuts down on the travel time for completing this quest chain (every week).  Once you learn the route(s) for this one, it's really not all that difficult to do or that terribly time consuming.  The biggest thing you need to worry about with it is that you want to have your Hearthstone set to Darnassus before you start and that you have your Portable Wormhole and some Ice Cream in your inventory before you leave Stormwind on your Orphan Tour.

Enjoy sightseeing with your Orphans my fellow Turtles!    satisfied_turtle
