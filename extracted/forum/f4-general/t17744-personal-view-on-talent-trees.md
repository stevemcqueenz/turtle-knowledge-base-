# Personal view on talent trees

## Post 120945 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-25T23:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120945#p120945 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I wanted to create a quick note about my personal view on the builds the trees currently provide. Nothing too in-depth, no maths involved, just a general design understanding from my end compared to what I see.
Will mention roles for different trees, most of them are trivial:
 * Healer is well... Healer.
 * Support is a bit subjective since everyone, even a combat rogue brings something to the table. Will use it subjectively where I feel that the tree's build's provided support value plays a crucial role in the build's identity.
 * Melee. I mean yeah, punch stuff until stuff is dead.
 * Ranged. Caster DPS, including physical ranged DPS, too.
 * Puppeteer. Builds which have pets and rely heavily on them.
 * Tank. Yeah.
 * Offtank. Build which can tank dungeons and can sometimes offtank raids but it's objectively less optimal for raid MT as tank builds.

Note: I'll focus on leveling and PVE, PVP is mostly an afterthought for me.

So.

 **Hunter**
 **BM (puppeteer > ranged)** - I like their place during leveling, maybe a bit too much even (most hunters I see leveling are BM). But I really miss a good BM build from raiding. I wanna see those pets really matter.
 **MM (ranged)** - I think they are at a great spot for raiding, but would love a bit more during leveling. Personally I find the aspect of buying your arrows annoying but I can respect it as a part of the class fantasy.
 **Survival (melee > puppeteer)** - I think TWOW missed the melee hunter flavor a bit. They went hardly on melee and missed the option of pet-synergies. In my mind a melee hunter's unique identity relies on the fact that your melee DPS output is highly dependent on the comboes you and your pet do together. I wanna see the pets being more involved in the rotation with setup buffs for example.
Small addition: hunter's mark is fine as it is but I'd prefer a rework where it's useful for each spec. Mark is the 'most hunter' ability in my eyes and it only supports ranged playstyle. Which is not a problem by itself, but TWOW pushed melee into existence.

 **Rogue**
 **Assassination (melee)** - Okay, combat goes inside the tree for some talents. But I have no idea what assassination is trying to achieve on its own, as a raid build which goes at least 31 into it. Would change it to a melee > support build where the poisons give very potent team utility while them, as a primarily DPS-focused build could maintain a high damage output. Kinda like a warlock.
 **Combat (melee)** - I miss some numbers. Or at least more raids with traps and stunnable key mobs. But mostly numbers. They barely have anything for the team and even their DPS is limited to mostly single target. If we keep it so, those numbers should really go up.
 **Subtlety (support > melee)** - I appreciate what TWOW is doing here. Just remove the reagents from the rotation and make them deal damage like a DPS. Not a top damage dealer, they shouldn't be that. But they should feel like a DPS.

 **Warlock**
 **Affliction (ranged > support)** - It's hard to explain, having the feeling of 'almost there'. Gameplay is almost there, as if there was 1 missing spell to patch up the whole thing. Similar with output. I don't say affli is bad, it just feels like a puzzle picture where the box was lacking 1 piece from the beginning.
 **Demo (puppeteer > ranged > offtank)** - I know, LFT doesn't allow it but with the new stone for added threat? Those bulky demo talents? I only put offtank to the third place as a very niche use. Warlocks, if played by a good player can tank dungeons now pretty well but improving it further like making them able to offtank raids would be a mistake. My main problem here is not even that. They should be the mana-using BM hunters. Demo pets should really count at raid DPS. BM beasts and demo pets should buff eachother to a degree. I want to see demo warlocks in raids relying heavily on demon damage. A big niche not filled in the game. Honestly, puppeteer as an archetype is a big niche not properly handled. Pets are given, but at raiding they are usually sacrificed or dismissed.
 **Destro (ranged)** - As you can see, I'd imagine affliction doing the warlock support hard-carry, after all, curses are affliction territory. In my mind destro is almost a purely damage-based build, including flavors of self-harm (even more than lifetap), sudden boosts, being fragile and a tendency to blow up (I'm kinda mad arcane mages got THAT talent, it feels so destro). This is how I'd want them. Big damage, low utility, having a tendency to die early unless you really pay attention.

 **Mage**
 **Arcane (support > ranged)** - I preferred the old TWOW idea, making arcane a lower damage but higher utility mage.
Fire and frost - Nothing to say really, mostly as I'd imagine them.

 **Warrior**
 **Arms (melee > support > offtank)** - I stand by the idea that arms should be slightly below fury at DPS but should have an arms-only debuff which is valuable to any party. Also, as a general note, would revise warrior gear-scaling (no idea how, though).
 **Fury (melee > tank)** - Almost wrote offtank but fury-prot still exists after all. Want them one of the biggest DPS builds but preferably not above combat rogue, unless the pack is big. Execute is another thing I'd redesign. Spamming it at raid bosses with lategame rage generation is too strong.
 **Prot (tank)** - I'm 99% fine with them. Would give them a slight help with aoe so that dungeon places like Stratholme zombie packs would become more manageable. Not a much, though, I don't want prot warrior to become prot pala 2. Liking them otherwise.

 **Priest**
 **Shadow (ranged > support)** - As Atreidon has once stated, remove painspike (end quote). It allows devs to properly buff them without breaking PVP that easily. At PVE they need help. What I hate the most about them is their range as a caster and their big threat spikes with mind blast (despite their usually low damage). Make them more fragile if that's needed for PVP balances but in any case, make them more interesting for raiding (this is mostly a PVE environment after all).
 **Disc (support > healer)** - Well, here I really differ from the mainstream but in my eyes turning disc as a holy magic-using caster DPS is not a good idea, specially that those spells are mostly buffed in the holy tree. I'd want them as proactive healers. Bigger emphasis on shields and HoT and a unique buff from deep into the tree. They support first and foremost, bring up healing numbers second. I might even remove the spirit buff from baseline and would add it to disc later.
 **Holy (healer)** - In my eyes they are the healers in their purest form. Biggest variety of heals, biggest heals etc for the price of barely doing anything else. If it is a castable heal, they are the best at it.  I know it differs from the actual holy tree but I'd want a 'pure healer' added to the game, compensating for the lack of utility with BiS healing numbers.

 **Druid**
 **Moonkin (support > ranged)** - I mostly like them as they are. Not sure about their mana at leveling, if it's a problem, maybe a change there. And almost forgot - a bigger emphasis on spell crit payoffs in their toolkit, after all they have moonkin aura. Honorable mention is roots indoors. I have a mixed feeling about that, would need testing.
 **Feral (tank/melee > support)** - My personal idea of the build was pretty radical so I ditched it. Wanted to make feral one build, specializing into offtanking/DPSing while not offtanking where both bear and cat needs the same items and they literally become the same build with the idea of being an okayish DPS when no plus tank needed or being an okayish offtank at any second when needed. But people would hate it. For the current gameplay: I wanna see more cats, more incentives to play as one. Also, bears kinda feel a bit lagging behind prot warrior and pala right now. Honestly, instead of a buff, I'd nerf the other two to bear's level. Current top tanks are too good at what they do.
 **Resto (heal > support)** - I have no real idea here.

 **Paladin**
 **Holy (support > healer)** - I respect TWOW's balls for trying out the melee healer. But I also condemn them for not making the spec raid-viable, core things are missing from the pala's current repertoire. I'd double down on a working melee healer while making the old playstyle still playable. Would give healer palas a unique flavor.
 **Retri (support > melee)** - Currently definitely melee > support but I think they do a bit too much damage compared to the utility they provide. Would somewhat reduce it while keeping their DPS valid, at the same level as other high-utility DPS builds.
 **Prot (tank > support)** - I mostly like them. The slight nerf which I mentioned regarding prot warriors and palas to bear level is the only thing I consider. I like the high variety of tanks in this server.

 **Shaman**
 **Resto (support > healer)** - Currently way too strong in my opinion because they can machine gun chain heals with an extremely good mana regen rate. Would slightly reduce their healing output, considering the huge utility they already bring to the table.
 **Enhancement (support > melee/offtank)** - Now, this one really feels like a support > melee. I'm mostly okay with enhancement DPS's rotation but would increase their mana regeneration. Of course, people tell that enh does high DPS for the small windows where it counts but there is bloodlust for that. Relying on heavy consumes just to upkeep a normal rotation like over 30 seconds is not good. For the tank a defensive CD and I'm happy.
 **Ele (support > ranged)** - Ele feels a bit like ranged > support at this moment and I don't like it. I'm okay with lowered ele burstiness, even lowered ele DPS but what ele players really want is an ele-only utility, probably a caster totem, and preferably a caster group at raids, unless WF is really needed. Would keep or lower their output as per required for the sake of an added caster utility totem.
