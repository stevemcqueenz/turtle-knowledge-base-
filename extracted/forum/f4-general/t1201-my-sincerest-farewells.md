Alas, my time has come. Some of you knew me, others not. I've been part of this server from day one, eager to share in its wealth of Roleplay and PVE - both of which were my passions. Of course, those with whom I begun my journey have left and through the years, I've seen guilds rise and crumble, taking a step back from the entire goings-on in the server as a silent observer. Suffice to say, work has again caught up with me, as it did a year ago and I shall not be returning afterwards. The game takes too much of my time, which I need to invest in my other projects.

For that end, I shall gift what items and gold I have to random players in need and I shall leave behind me, with what RP richness we can muster, the Shadow of Deadwind Pass event. If anyone is interested in taking up its lead, I will freely give it and hope that it will bring joy to you all.

With sincerest farewells,
Parcival G.
