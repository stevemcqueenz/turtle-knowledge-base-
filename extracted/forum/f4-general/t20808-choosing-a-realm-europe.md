## Post 144441 by Oomentaloo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-11T09:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144441#p144441 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It depends what you want. If you like the feeling of a fresh server, when everybody is rushing through the leveling zones to stay ahead of the curve then you should go for Ambershire. How the server community ends up is unclear ofc.

If you don't mind that other players have Naxxx etc on farm and you want just a laid back leveling time and think about to trying out role-playing. Then Nordanaar could be the better realm.
I have had the best player experience ever on this realm. (There are idiots ofc)
The leveling zones are still populated so you can find player to partner up with for Elite quests and some big leveling guilds you can join up before joining up an endgame guild (these guilds raid aswell)

Just make a char on Nordanaar without big time investment and get the vibe, you can still make a char on Ambershire later.

## Post 144557 by Oomentaloo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-11T15:12:00Z

If you want a bit better experience with the old client get some basic addons:
ShaguTweaks gives you more options for Unit Frames, auto-dismount etc.
Maybe ShaguTweaks-extras for more control.
ShaguPlates for better Nameplates
pfQuest and pfQuest to see quest marker on the map. It's nice if you want to find new quests added by TWoW.

You find those here: <https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Addons>
When you download these addons from github (Green code button->Download ZIP) dont forget to delete the "-master" part from the folder you put into your addons folder.

Have fun!
