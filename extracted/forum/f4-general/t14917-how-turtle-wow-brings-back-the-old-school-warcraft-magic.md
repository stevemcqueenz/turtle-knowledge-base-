# How Turtle WoW Brings Back the Old-School Warcraft Magic ??

## Post 101545 by steeevesmmmith — 2024-09-20T10:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101545#p101545 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey fellow adventurers,

I have recently started playing Turtle WoW.., and I have to say,, this server brings back the true feeling of Vanilla WoW. It’s not just about nostalgia,,, but the unique features and slow--paced gameplay really add a fresh spin to the game.

What stands out to me is how the devs at Turtle WoW have integrated custom content without ruining the authentic Vanilla experience. From new questlines to extra zones,, it still feels like it belongs in the old world we all love. Plus, the pace of progression here is so much more relaxed: No rushing through levels, no pressure to hit max level ASAP. It’s more about enjoying the journey, meeting fellow players, and savoring the little things.

I am curious,, what do you all think is the best feature Turtle WoW has to offer? Is it the new content: ?? The slower progression: ?? Or maybe the RP-friendly atmosphere: ?? Let’s share our experiences and tips !!

Looking forward to hearing from you all,

With Regards

For as much as I like the new stuff, imo the community has been the best part. I'm an offbeat sort of player, I love experimenting. My main is a Hunter tank and the reactions I've gotten have been shockingly kind coming from the Blizz servers.

I've tanked several dungeons and no one's been nasty about it yet (it helps that its been working :) ). All it took on the Blizz servers was mentioning the idea of doing anything out of the ordinary to draw insults.

I even had a Shaman tell me his friend had Hunter tanked in raids and encouraged me to keep learning about it. Really solidified twow as my preferred wow.
