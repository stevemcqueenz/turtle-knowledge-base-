Using multiple antiviruses at the same time is known to cause issues like this. If I had to guess, your antivirus took a sample of the new patcher to submit for analysis and then the other antivirus detected this sample as malware.

Patcher tools like this are a frequent cause of false positives because of the way they work (downloading files from the internet and then modifying/replacing other files).
