# New Discord TOS is terrible

## Post 168862 by Cheruscan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2026-02-19T09:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168862#p168862 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As some of you might now Discord will change their TOS next month to "protect kids" ie at some point it will want to see your legal ID to verify you are and adult.

This might seem like an innocent and reasonable change to people that are not that experienced with the world wide web, but it is actually a huge security risk for anyone giving their info to social media like that.

Especially since the ID verification will be done by a Palantir subsidiary (Palantir develops security software for the US military among other things, basically pushing "Big Brother")

So to both the devs and the players (since we are playing in a legal grey zone on TWoW) it might be good to look for alternatives.

I propose Fluxer since its open source and very similar to discord (but of without all this TOS bs)
