## Post 77777 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-17T05:47:00Z

If you play main turtle realm, then xp rate depends on Slow and Steady glyph, Warmode glyph and from amount of rested experience you have.
with Slow and Steady glyph you get half of normal xp from mobs in open world. In the same time you get normal xp from mobs in dungeons.
<https://turtlecraft.gg/#/slow-and-steady>
Rested experience allows you get double experience. Amount of additional experience depends on the time you spend in inn or under a tent. This system also exist on Vanilla. Though tents are turtle addition.
Warmode gives you 30% to all xp you get. From mobs and from quests.
