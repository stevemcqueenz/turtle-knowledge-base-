## Post 159991 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-18T19:05:00Z

They're technically possible to do together, with the exception of level one lunatic. Easy answer to your question.

Going deeper, I'd ask a person taking all these to consider why they choose this option. If its simply to do them because they are challenges, I'd remind the player that new challenges get added over time, thus defeating the point of being a completionist. For example, I personally know players who completed at least 3 of the original 4 challenges simultaneously. Perhaps even all 4. But even if they accomplished such a feat, they would not have been able to have completed the new challenges on the same character.
So if a person is seriously considering doing all* the challenges, I encourage them to strongly consider their reason why.
