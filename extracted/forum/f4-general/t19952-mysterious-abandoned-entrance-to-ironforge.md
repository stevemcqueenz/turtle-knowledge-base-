It happens to me from time to time, when I observe these kind of stuff and I have this feeling of "they were going to perform some development in here, this was actually content of some sort" but it was left unatended.

For those who see this, it is a structure who resembles the ones used to build a gate through dwarven mountains. And this gate in particular was placed at the back of Ironforge city, In some place between the forlorn cavern and the hall of explorers.

Sorry if I'm being so curious. You see... if this was a "backdoor" for the dwarves then it makes no sense to have an aspect so "stunning". It's pretty obvious that's the only structure nearby on the wall of the mountain of Dun Agrath. By the other hand if this was an actual route to move troops out of the city or function as an evacuation route for emergencies, why they forgot to make a counterpart gate inside Ironforge so we could say "oh! this might be the closed gate" and yet they didn't.

Sorry if I sound somehow demanding with reasoning. It's the way of an adventurer, nothing else.
