## Post 8348 by Roxanneflowers (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-11-03T06:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8348#p8348 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've always had the explicit impression that Herbalism and Mining are supposed to be different in this game mechanical regard.  Harvest the flower(s) and you get 1-5 bits of plant out of it in one go ... but for mining there are 2-4 "taps" of the mining nodes and you have to harvest them one by one.  That way, if you're wanting/needing to harvest a mining node you have no idea from the start how many "taps" you'll be able to make, and while you're making them you're essentially immobilized and therefore vulnerable to either PvE and/or (world) PvP aggro.  This makes mining an inherently less safe activity to do than herbalism, because herbalism is relatively quiet and fast ... while mining is "noisy" (the taps themselves are quite a distinctive sound) and slow since you have a minimum of 2 "taps" to make before completely harvesting a mining node.

In other words ... it's fine, don't change it.
