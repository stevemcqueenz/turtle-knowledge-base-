# Wanna survive dungeons in HC? Don't be a tank!

## Post 44587 by Scriss — 2023-05-05T15:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44587#p44587 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I can't tell you guys how many times I've seen the tank RIP because of dumb dps(usually hunters) or healers. The healer dc, hunter's pet pulls extra groups on accident in the middle of a pull(most common thing I see! Every day!), dps CC's the wrong target, dps starts their aoe before the tank touches the mobs and everyone starts running and tank aoe pulls threat while every1 is panicking and dead tank, healer gets a phone call... you guys get the idea. You see, dps and the healer are REAL good at seeing when sh*t is about to hit the fan. They are going to bail and real fast. As a tank you will always die first and you usually did nothing wrong. Every time you run with a random group as a tank you are rolling the dice, even if you're a PRO tank.

So, if you are playing HC and you are a tank... Good luck... Your RIP is always right around the corner.
