## Post 101692 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-09-22T16:30:00Z

Theres something about Classic/Vanilla Talent Tree's, man.
They tickle the tism deep within my Male Brain.

They're so straightforward and simple yet inspire so much experimentation and debate.

I've seen other 'Vanilla+' custom servers with absurd Talent Trees choked with countless options.
(And retail of course with too much corny nonsense to even keep track of.)

It just don't hit right.
Sometimes less is more, eh?

I will always respect T-WoW staff for their extreme conservatism in regards to how they only made *essential* changes/additions to these truly timeless core feature(s) of this classic game. ![🫡](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1fae1.svg)

And they are just enough. WotLK became crazy with the talents, probably why they moved to talent spreadsheets, but that was too much in the opposite direction.

And now in retail it feels like they changed, again, too much in the opposite direction. There are so many minor choices to pick from and so many branching paths... it feels daunting to try and experiment on your own. I feel like it was built specifically for two groups of people: hardcore theorycrafters and the "look up a guide and don't think" people.

Every time i am close to buying TWW (which i hear is good on the pve side), i just open wowhead and try to build my own talent tree for a class i would play.

Halfway through my urge disapears.
