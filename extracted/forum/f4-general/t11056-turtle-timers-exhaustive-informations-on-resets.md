Hello guys,

So I've seen a lot of people asking questions in the World Chat like : "When is the Onyxia reset ?" or "Where is Darkmoon Faire right now ?", also because I needed that sometimes I've wanted to do something to help tracking those things and here it is :

<https://turtle-timer.vercel.app/>

Features :

 - Raid Resets
 - Daily Battleground
 - DMF location and avaibility (Wednesday dayoff)
 - Option to display/hide "00 days"
 - Options to display/hide any timer you wouldn't want in the list
