Hello!

I'm planning to buy Goblin Brainwashing Device from shop while leveling to have two specs - for dungeons and outworld. But I read in some patch notes that you can't save a spec with unspent talents.
Let's say I saved both specs at 10 level with one talent spent. But when I level up, I gain new talents. What happens to my saved specs? Can I use this device while leveling at all?

Hi.
If you plan on using this goblin invention, here's how it will work.
Example:
You get level 11 and save your speck in the device
Then you get level 16 and want to update your spec in the device.
You will need to delete the current spec and save the new one.
