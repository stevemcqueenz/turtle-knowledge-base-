# ez fix for HUGE amount of content

## Post 166624 by Frantsel (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-27T15:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166624#p166624 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The rarespawn and worldboss situation is horrific. Most players would love to kill them but they are camped to the death by no-life Andys.

I was trying to get fiery leggins and saw 5 hunters pre trapping the spawn.
This is extremely bad gamedesign. Nobody enjoys that and it would be so easy to fix it.

Same goes for the worldbosses. It would take a little more effort to fix it but the gained content by it would be quite crazy.

How?

Make worldbosses summonable just like Clack'gora. Items to summon them could be farmed in various raids and dungeons. Or even raremobs if the spawn gets reworked. Maybe crafted by a few professions? The possibilites to implement it are endless. They gained content would be great for so many players that would never have access to kill a worldboss otherwise.

Also: Please make the items from raremobs at least BOE. Otherwise it is just unbearable to achieve them without pretrapping and huge luck.

> **Frantsel wrote: Tue Jan 27, 2026 3:12 pm**
> Also: Please make the items from raremobs at least BOE. Otherwise it is just unbearable to achieve them without pretrapping and huge luck.

Be very careful what you wish for. Because with BoEs instead of 5 people who want an item for themselves, you'll get 10 more who just do it for profit. And any item that is both rare *and* highly valued will be price-gauged heavily.

Other than that I agree with your points. It would be nice to open up limited content to a wider audience. Though the limited availability is kinda what makes it special as well. Finding a good balance is probably quite hard. Summon-able world bosses have been [announced 14 months ago](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16305) and 2 major patches have dropped since then with a third coming up soon-ish, and still no word about that feature. Note that they intended to pair it with a world boss lock-out timer, which is probably a good idea. Cla'ckora kinda loses it's aura when you get together and chain-farm 6 spawns one after the other.

Personally - there should probably a bigger rework. Best-in-slot loot should never be on world bosses or rare spawns. Apart from a select few pieces this has kinda organically happened with world bosses, because K40 loot out-classes almost everything in Vanilla. Unfortunately the developers don't seem to agree as they've added unique druid idols to Cla'ckora and then a rare spawn on Balor.

In an ideal world for me, world bosses are something that is available to do for the challenge and the unique experience. Not something you have to camp for drops. But that is also kinda not in the spirit of Vanilla where loot is deliberately hard-to-get and RNG-heavy.
