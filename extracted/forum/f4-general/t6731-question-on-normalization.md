Sweet! Thanks. I poked my friend about it and I misunderstood her question. She says there was an additional (not in the patch notes) change that is about duel wielding.

Basically, when you've got 2 weapons, it averages their attack speed. So if you have a 2.5s main hand and a 1.2s offhand you would get in 2 off hands per 1 main-hand attack. The un-listed 1.8 changed altered this so that they are averaged. This means that both weapons just split the difference and have an attack speed of 1.85s. This change made fury warrior trash and made her change to a priest back in the day.

From the sound if it, this un-listed change is also in Turtle.
