## Post 123344 by Wolf of Rage (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-03-22T19:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123344#p123344 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Vagrant with Artisan is going to be your main issue from all of these picks -- you will quickly hit a giant, long-lasting hurdle with your Equipment, and will have to rely on... everything else you can possibly get your hands on.

Easier (but not easy) if you're attempting as a Class that is... pretty much Caster, and relies on Spells and especially can CC; if you're something like a Warrior, however... hell awaits.

You'd best rely on whatever Consumables you could possibly create, and what might be exempted from the rules of Artisan (not sure if anything is from Vagrant, let alone from having both active at the same time) -- this means that Alchemy and Engineering both are going to carry you like you wouldn't even know. Now, considering that both require constant resupplying, then, you will probably need an alt to split the load, else I'm not sure what else might be doable, because Profession hopping this particular mix of challenges... sounds painful.

Regardless, you will also be testing both your knowledge and skills the further you go -- fence hopping and similar methods to abuse pathfinding and buy yourself time, do kiting hit-and-run tactics, use Line of Sight well against anything Ranged and Spell, if you have Debuffs to weaken or make something vulnerable, you are absolutely going to need to make them a priority, as well as of course relying on CC to buy yourself time whenever you can't use the aforementioned strategies at any given time and you might be in danger as a result.

Side-note, what about Glyph of Exhaustion? I don't notice it as a mention. It's a minor Challenge that gives 2x Weapon Skill (redundant, practically a non-benefit) at the cost of no Rested Experience ever at all. Considering you're going on War Mode -- which is suicide with Hardcore -- I'm a bit surprized Exhaustion is not also on the list. Or are you doing only the major Challenges only?
