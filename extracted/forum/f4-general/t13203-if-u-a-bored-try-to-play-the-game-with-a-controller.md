 3. game play
the most import thing is u HAVE TO HOLD THE RB button all the time with the right joystick
(R1 in PS4 controller, or just mean the right click of the mouse) to controll the view
while in other game right joystick only with control the view of the character

tap left joystick is the jump

tap right joystick the switch attack target, u dont switch target alot as a rogue, thats y its the best for controller

the 4-8 button are the most used skills, 1-4 a less often used
the shift+1-8 button are the situational button
n the shift+ctr+1-8 button are the emergence/long CD/ protion button, (just mean both LB+RB+1-8, or L1+R1+1-8 for ps4 controller)

map, bag, quest, mount, escape(cancal all) button are in the midde of the controller with different conbination, u shld get used to it with lv 10

I'd recommend the addon 'MouseLookLock'.  A simple ToC change has it working even with older clients and it 'locks' the mouse cursor so that you don't need to hold down the right mouse button or whatever controller equivalent there is.
