From one point of view, I agree that amount of quests was increased, so the leveling become faster.

On the other hand - I think it is good that one don't HAVE to complete all quests to proceed to the next zone. It is like true adventuring where you can choose - what job you want to do and what you don't.
For example the centaur chain in Barrens. It is is horrible because of problematic spawn of some Centaur leader. So you are free to skip that chain completely and don't lose anything.

On the third hand - most challenging vanilla quests are getting abandoned because of it. For example I also skip barrens harpies quests on all my alts. Why is it bad? Because some distant parts of locations become less and less populated. In Vanilla, elite quests were inevitable. You have to complete them to get enough xp for other zones. On Turtle you can level pretty good by doing easy quests.

Manual xp toggling is good option for tryhard-completionists, but I bet it is rarely used. As long as grey quests of lvl 20 below you grant you xp, you can solo hard/elite quests by outleveling their objectives. Which means you skip MMO part of the game. I am not sure if it is good.

I also have a suggestion about it: [Original quest experience](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=18679)
