>

The difference between the servers is that the PvE server is focused on PvE, so raiding, leveling, things of that nature. The PvP is focused on PvP, with an emphasis on world PvP. Note, the PvP has a significantly smaller population than the PvE at this point, so if you're just starting I'd recommend the PvE server. To that point, you'll even be able to get more PvP at endgame on the PvE server due to the population on the PvE server if you're doing battlegrounds and arena. There are also more leveling guilds on PvE that can help guide you, and more people to do group quests with (also no griefing). Unless you're looking for world PvP, the PvE is the way to go at this point.

As far as classes are concerned, you should play whatever you think is interesting! Note, it takes a long time to get to 60, so don't worry about the raiding scene or what is optimal when you start off. That's something to worry about if you end up loving the game and want to raid at the end (truly wow is two games, one where you level, one where you raid).

I think what is "hardest" can be pretty subjective, but I think most people would agree that warrior is the most time intensive because of how much you have to eat and drink. Some classes level faster than others, but almost everything is viable because of the changes the turtle wow team has made. Arcane mage, balance druid, ele shaman, ret paladin, all used to be memes and now they're perfectly playable (ret paladins smashing face in turtle). Rogues are good, as a Tank I love seeing them due to their utility and personality wise the people who are drawn to rogue also seem to be super helpful in keeping **** together.

There are no wrong choices, welcome to turtle wow!
