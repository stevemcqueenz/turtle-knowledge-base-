# Faction Imbalance Myth

## Post 77931 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-18T15:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77931#p77931 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm still surprised that even after so many years of existance of WoW there are people who claim that every PVP server will eventually turn to one-sided realm. Let's finally make it clear:
 **It's not always true! and Tel'Abim most likely won't follow this way**
I will explain why using population data of Classic WoW as well as comparising Tel'Abim to other PVP private server.

First of all I want to say that problem of one-sided realms really does exist, however "WoW Andies" often don't understand what is the root of this problem. Thus, they often simplify the reason to "GMs did nothing to stop faction imbalance, it's their fault". The thing is the real cause of this problem doesn't come from one faction being superior towards the other  one for a certain period of time. The real problem is that owner of multiple servers (Blizzard is the only example) allowed players of less dominant faction to easily change realm not only through transfers but also by giving them A LOT OF realms to play on. My statement means that this problem affects only Blizzard servers because most of the private servers have only one consolidated server to play on.

Here is the page I'm referring to: <https://ironforge.pro/population/era/?realm=PvP>
I'm taking into account only English-speaking realms both in US and Europe for easier analysis.
There are 18 PVP (EU) and 18 PVP (US) realms in Classic Era.
According to this site current faction balance for these regions is following: 56.7% Alliance 43.3% Horde (unfortunately this includes German PVP servers, without them the balance would look even better). Theoretically, if all the realms were merged into one mega-server, the faction balance would not look so bad and we certainly could not call it one-sided.
If we look at long-lived WotLK Classic PVP we will see that most of these servers are totally dominated by one faction. WotLK balance on both regions is 30.6% Alliance 69.4% Horde. So one-sided PVP realms really exist. However, I dare say that such a scenario is very unlikely to happen on private server which is consolidated into one realm. In the worst case one faction would be slightly dominated by the other one but it won't turn into 99%-1% faction balance.

This seems to be always true supposing that server has big population. But how to define big population? It's a relative matter. I think that Tel'Abim as a PVP private server can only be compared to other private servers. The best example would be Everlook which is vanilla PVP. Unfortunately, Everlook stopped sharing server stats on their website (I remember it was possible during first weeks of their launch). So I will only present data that I collected myself. If you have any better data, share here please so I can update it. I logged into that server at 3pm CET today and I saw 4050 people in game. The last answer from GM on Everlook discord about faction balance was sent 2 months ago, he claimed it's like 55% Alliance 45% Horde. And what is the most important(as far as i know): Everlook has never enforced faction limits. I remember when Wall (former Dev of Everlook) said it's community job to keep an eye on faction balance. After one year of Everlook existance these words turn out to be right.

But how we make sure that Tel'Abim will not turn into one-sided realm? For a simple reason. If someone wants to try vanilla+ custom content which Turtle WoW offers and they also want preferably play on PVP server, there is only one server that matches their preferences: Tel'Abim. Single, consolidated realm. If they create a character and see that the faction they play in is outnumbered, there will be no escape. No transfers, no other PVP custom vanilla servers to move to. Custom exclusive content on Turtle WoW is present for both factions. Shaman is horde-only and Paladin alliance-only. If you think PVP veterans would prefer to try custom content on PVE realm then you're wrong. Some people are interested mainly in WPVP and you won't change their mind.

My post wasn't mean to show that Tel'Abim is balanced server. The game isn't perfectly balanced. But it has little to do with faction balance. This post was created due to recent invasion of idiotic posts about faction balance on this forum. Some of these posts were created in rage after being killed a few times in the same spot by higher level character of opposing faction. Posted by a person who doesn't know there is an option to group up with other players while leveling.

This post was created to debunk the myth spreading here about the faction imbalance and the supposed inevitable fate of Tel'Abim. If you read this and still think that Tel'Abim will be one-sided or will die soon, give me at least one example of a private server that had at least 2k people online on average and turn one-sided.

And most importantly, you can check the current faction balance of Tel'Abim here <https://turtlecraft.gg/#/population-graph>.
Don't cry on forum, just play less represented faction like a real man. Don't be Andy, be Chad. After all Scourge was defeated in Naxxramas by Argent Dawn initiative, not by lazy kingdoms of the Alliance and the Horde.
