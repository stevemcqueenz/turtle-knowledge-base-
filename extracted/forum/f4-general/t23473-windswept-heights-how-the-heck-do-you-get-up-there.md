Hi, I once accidentally stumbled across tath area while exploring around in Desolace. You actually need to access it via  a small corridor in Desolace in the very south east, behind Shadowbreak Ravine (Coordinates: 81,79). If you follow that path, you'll eventually end up in Windswept Heights, Mulgore.
