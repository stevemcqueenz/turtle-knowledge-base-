# Wardens of Time Reputation Grind

## Post 78524 by Speedz1337 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-11-24T07:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78524#p78524 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Two quests give rep for the sand.
[Corrupted Sand](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40340) this quest gives 15 rep per 1 Corrupted Sand.
[Sand in Bulk](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40341) this quest gives 175 rep per 10 Corrupted Sand.

[Rep Rewards](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=80943)

 **At Friendly**
Chronar (1st boss) +50 rep
Epidamu (2nd boss) +0 rep
Drifting Avatar of Sand (3rd boss) +0 rep
Time-Lord Epochronos (4th boss) +100 rep
Mossheart (5th boss) +0 rep
Rotmaw (6th boss) +0 rep
Antnormi (7th boss) +50 rep
Infinite Rift-Lord +10 rep
Infinite Riftweaver +5 rep
Infinite Riftguard +5 rep
Infinite Whelp +2 rep
 19x Infinite Dragonspawn +5 rep
Time Anomaly +0 rep
Echo of Time +0 rep
Temporal Dust +0 rep
Murkwater Crocolisk +0 rep
Darkwater Python +0 rep
Blackfang Tarantula +0 rep

~400 per run of raw rep
From 0/12000 honored to 0/21000 revered needs 520 sand without rep from the run

 **At Honored**
Chronar (1st boss) +50 rep
Epidamu (2nd boss) +0 rep
Drifting Avatar of Sand (3rd boss) +0 rep
Time-Lord Epochronos (4th boss) +100 rep
Mossheart (5th boss) +0 rep
Rotmaw (6th boss) +0 rep
Antnormi (7th boss) +50 rep
Infinite Rift-Lord +0 rep
Infinite Riftweaver +0 rep
Infinite Riftguard +0 rep
Infinite Whelp +0 rep
Infinite Dragonspawn +0 rep
Time Anomaly +0 rep
Echo of Time +0 rep
Temporal Dust +0 rep
Murkwater Crocolisk +0 rep
Darkwater Python +0 rep
Blackfang Tarantula +0 rep

 200 per run of raw rep
From 0/21000 revered to exalted you need 1200 sand without rep from the run

Sunneyglade also gives reputation for Wardens of Time +150 rep for victory.
