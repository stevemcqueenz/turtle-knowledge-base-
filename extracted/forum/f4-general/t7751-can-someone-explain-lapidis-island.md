I had the same experience when I leveled my first character there. I was first under the impression that the mobs were just too strong or my character was too weak, but then I began paying attention to the HP of the mobs on Lapidis (and Gilijim too) and compared them to mobs the same level in different zones and the difference is night and day.

It's true that the mobs there have more HP, die slower and hit harder. What I don't know is if it's intentional or just a mistake. I really want to go back to level there, but I found it to be more of a pain in the ass than enjoyment.

There was some quest chain about going into some Troll ruins that were filled with elites and as I was solo, I couldn't complete those quests so I just skipped them.

I don't think it was a good idea to make brand new content so difficult, because as it is right now, I have no intention on going back to those islands ever again. Tel'Abim is better in that regard, it's only fault is it's too small and too short.

I think if the Turtle WoW team make more content like Tel'Abim, it will be good, just not like Lapidis and Gilijim.
