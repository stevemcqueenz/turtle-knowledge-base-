Okay, hear me out...

In terms of thematic racials, I think the ones we have are pretty dang good. But what if we had something that was a... Detriment? It doesn't even have to be big. -1% Strength for gnomes because they're small and puny, +1% holy damage to undead because... Undead, -1% agility for tauren because of their lumbering massive builds, or -1% spirit for high elves as a result of being nearly eradicated by Arthas?

Of course, as cool as ideas like these sound to me, this isn't meant to be a suggestion I want implemented, just some cool things we could brainstorm about. Feel free to post some of your own ideas or change numbers around. :D
