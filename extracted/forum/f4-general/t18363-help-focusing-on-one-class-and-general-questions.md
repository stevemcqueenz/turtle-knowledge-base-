## Post 125416 by Drubarrymooer (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-04T09:27:00Z

It depends on what your end game goal is.  Do you want to be top DPS?  Pick a warrior.  Do you want to tank?  All 4 tanking classes are solid choices.  Do you want to PVP?  There are several classes that dominate.

I would stress that as long as you're having fun, it's ok to play any class.  Are warlocks lower DPS than mages?  I see lots of warlocks and mages mixed in with warriors in naxx logs.  Does farming shards stink?  Not really.  At least not more than any other repetitive farm.

If you're not sure what you'd like to do end game, play a druid or paladin.  Both can tank, heal, and do respectable DPS.  If you're hitting a questing wall, try going to a different zone or even running some dungeons.  Also, remember on Turtle you still get experience for quests ~20 or 25 (I forget which) levels below your level.
