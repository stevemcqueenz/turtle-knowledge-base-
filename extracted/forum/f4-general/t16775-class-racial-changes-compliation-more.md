Heya guys, it's impossible to keep track of what class has which changes, most posts are scattered out so I thought I'll put all the class changes into one post for myself and others to read.

This thread will not contain most talent changes, refer to <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/> for those.

There will be errors and missing information, if you see any please point them out so I can correct them.

scroll down for the class changes

General features:
- Debuff limit is 64

- Removed the 200 ms delay between main hand and off hand auto attacks.

- Increased nameplate distance from 20 to 40 yards.

- The level based spell miss chance is now capped to 10 levels difference, making it possible to hit high level players in PvP.

- Dishonorable kills are disabled.

- [Goblin Brainwashing Device](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=51715)  (dual spec) can be acquired at level 30 for 350 gold or level 10 from the donation store

- destroyed/sold items can be recovered  [here](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=8/)

- [gardening](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=1036)

- [world buffs](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35264#p35264) dont work in raids

- [training dummies](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=50515) (you can skill weapons on them)

- [this](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=14823) npc starts a quest at level 18 to get the turtle mount. The faire is always in either elwynn or mulgore, it resets weekly

- [raid reset timers & Darkmoon Faire location/timer](https://turtle-timer.vercel.app/)

- There's a questline to get extra points of weapon skill, check [this](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16790) guide

- ingame mail is (nearly) instant

 **BASELINE CHANGES**

Rage Decay when out of combat is now consistently 2 rage per 2 seconds.
---
Rage generation formula: calculated 90% depending on your gear and 10% of it is predetermined based on your weapon speed. With the formula change, players will notice significantly increased rage generation while leveling and with bad gear.
---
New Ability: Intervene  10 Rage cost | 30 sec cd | Learned at level 20 | Requires Defensive Stance
Run at high speed towards a party member, intercepting the next melee or ranged attack made against them.
Note: Shares a cooldown with Intercept.
---
New Talent: Counterattack (arms)   20 Rage cost | 5 sec cd
A strike that becomes active after parrying an opponent's attack, dealing x damage + 10%of your attack power and immobilizes your target for 3 seconds. Counterattack cannot be blocked/dodged/parried.
---
New Passive: Die by the Sword Acquired via Book at Level 58
Increases your chance to parry attacks by 20% while under the effect of Retaliation.
---
Dual Wield is now taught at level 10.

 **SPELL CHANGES**

Thunderclap can now be used in Defensive Stance.
---
Execute now has a 4.5 second cooldown.
---
Heroic Strike:  Off-hand weapon attacks are no longer guaranteed to land while your Heroic Strike is queued.
---
Pummel now only has one rank that deals 5% of your Attack Power as damage instead of a flat value and is now taught at level 18 and will be available for use in Battle Stance.
---
Slam is now taught at level 14 and has a 2.5 sec cast time, continues your weapon swing timer, and lets you move while casting. 50/60/70/80/90%(ranks) weapon damage
---
Rend now scales with your Attack Power by 5% of your Attack Power per tick of damage
---
Bloodrage now activates your Enrage.
---
Hamstring now has a 6 second cooldown.
---
Sunder Armor's rage cost has been reduced from 15 to 10 rage.
---
Shield Wall is now ended prematurely if you unequip your Shield while the effect is active.
---
Concussion Blow now deals damage, causes a high amount of threat and penetrates all of the enemy's armor. Cooldown has been reduced from 45 to 20 seconds. Stun duration has been reduced from 5 to 3 seconds. 235 base damage rank 3 (lv 60)
---
Revenge: Cooldown increased from 5 to 6 seconds.
---
Shield Slam: Damage of Shield Slam is now modified by your attack power in addition to shield block value.
---
Bloodthirst  Now increases your movement speed by 5% for 6 seconds. This effect stacks with other movement speed increases.
Attack Power scaling has been decreased from 45% to 35%.
Added Base damage: 100/130/160/200 (ranks)
---
Mortal Strike now does 105/110/115/120%(ranks) weapon damage.
