## Post 75864 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-11-04T12:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75864#p75864 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The changelogs are supposed to be posted once a week during maintenance. However, there are occasions when we need to hotfix something in the middle of the week, often in the middle of the night.

During these short restarts, not only hotfixes are applied, but also a few changes made in the same development branch. These changes are usually listed in the weekly changelog during the main maintenance.

If the server is crashing at 5 am, our priority is to keep it stable, rather than writing a detailed changelog.

Please stop being overly dramatic.
