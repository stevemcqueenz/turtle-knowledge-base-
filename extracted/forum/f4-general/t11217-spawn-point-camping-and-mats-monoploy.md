# Spawn Point Camping and mats monoploy

## Post 74116 by Jongyi (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-27T04:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74116#p74116 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a casual players, it became so unbearable that every spawn points for mats such as (devilsaurs, wind elementals in silithus, and list goes on...) have been camped 24/7 by handful of individuals and creating monopoly on these mats.

Last two months ago, we are still able to farm the mats casually while also leaving other players chance to get the mats. Nowadays, they are camped so much that you won't even get to see one devilsaur alive for mats, making it impossible to get mats and make gold. And AH market is also dominated by these 24/7 campers, leading to mat price skyrocketed.

This situation is getting frustrating because I can no longer make gold or get mats without becoming nolife camping as they do.
As players, how do we get mats alternatively
As devs, what kind of actions you would take to give every players chance to make gold and mats without handful of 24/7 campers (who are probably linked to gold sellers) monopolying the mat market.
