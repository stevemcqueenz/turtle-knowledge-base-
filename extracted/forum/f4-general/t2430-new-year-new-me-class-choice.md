So I played on this server a year or so ago, I really have no concept of time anymore.  I had a Turtle Mode Warrior, and came back to try a Hardcore Hunter until he died.

I've tried a few games between then and now, and none scratch the same itch.  New World was... fun, I guess, and Albion Online was NOT for me.  I thought "sandbox" games would give me more of what I want, which is social interaction with less "structured" content.  Turns out they're just full of mean people. [I jest, but only slightly]

So, this server has had an expansion, and existing content will stay relevant, I like the horizontal progression approach.  I've also gotten dragged into D&D recently, so RP is on my radar now.
The part of games like this I've always struggled with is mechanic based.  Most folks say some classes can be "boring" or "faceroll" but that's actually more of what I'm looking for.

I just want to hang out and "do the thing," gameplay is secondary to the social aspect of MMOs for me.  I use them as an outlet since I have trouble getting out anymore, for several reasons.  I used to do reenacting, but I've become physically unable to do so.

I started a War Mode/Slow and Steady Paladin because I felt it might be the best class to try and make "easy" with addons, but I'm not too sure.  In terms of current game mechanics, what classes have a low skill floor?
