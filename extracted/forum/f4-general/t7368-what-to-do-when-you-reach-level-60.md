## Post 46866 by Spoing — 2023-05-21T14:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46866#p46866 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

On WOWhead there is a pre-raid best in slot guide for every class. It gives a few options for every piece of gear. Get those (by running dungeons) and also get the quests for the dungeons you run to get some nice pieces of gear. Unlike WOTLK some lower level gear is still BIS (for example a blue with a ton of +healing might be better than a blue from a 60 dungeon that only gives primary stats). Join a raiding guild and they can often help you and recommend you stuff.

One thing is that turtlewow did add some gear that might be better than what wowhead would recommend.
