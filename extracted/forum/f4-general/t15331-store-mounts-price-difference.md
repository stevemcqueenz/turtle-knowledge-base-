# Store mounts price difference

## Post 104649 by Yonu — 2024-10-24T09:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104649#p104649 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yesterday I took a look at the store mounts, since that's one of the things I'm yet to buy, and I noticed the price difference between Spectral Tigers, Raven Lord and any other mount, like the Nightmare Dreamrunner

So those spectral tigers goes for 2000 turtle points, which is basically 170$
The Raven Lord goes for 470 turtle points, which is basically 50$ (since you can't donate bellow 30$)
And finally, the Dreamrunner, like many many other mounts is 188, which should be around 19$

I'm curious to know why each of these mounts are priced so differently, since none of them are custom mounts (not counting the 2 color variations of Spectral tiger, which I think they didn't exist in OG wow), I can't find a reason why they're (in a way) gatekept behind a higher paywall

Not just that, but mounts are not shared between characters (ala vanilla wow, which is a shame, I really like to have my mounts shared) so, lets say, I decide to drop 170$ in a spectral tiger, and then the tiger is locked to one of my 8 characters

These logistics demotivate me from adquiring these mounts, I don't think it's justifiable or fair, but I'd like to listen to your opinion, and maybe (probably won't happen) someone from the team could drop their logic behind, just so I get a new perspective

Thank you for reading
