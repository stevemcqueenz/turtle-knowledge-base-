Warrior will come out on top for survivability.

But druid generally has an upside in providing a good mix of Threat and survivability while warrior can offer either good TPS with dual wielding or good survivability. he can do both better but you can do them at the same time.

I think a lot of people have the misconception that Druid tank is just a meme. I tanked in Season of mastery as one of our Guilds Tanks with druid without issues. Turtle has even given you some extra tools. so extrapolating from my classic experiences, and looking at the turtle custom gear & class changes, druid seems to be only behind warrior by a small margin if at all.

If you ever wanna tank as druid in a classic raid, this is the server to do so, and i've already seen quite a few raid feral tanks on here
