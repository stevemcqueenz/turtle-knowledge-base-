> I’d like to farm gold and complete content solo as much as possible (dungeons, elite quests etc).

Hunter is by far the best class at taking on group content alone due to its pet, myriad of crowd control, ability to kite, and ability to do all of that even when it runs out of mana. Any single elite with no ranged attack is a no brainer in an open space since you can hold aggro with Distracting Shot, kite with Cheetah and Conc Shot, and eventualy kill it with autoshots and pet damage.

Hunter also has the tools to split packs: pull the pack with a shot, and have your pet attack your desired target once it's far enough from its spawn point, then Feign Death. Pack will reset, but not the one held by your pet. Congrats ! You can now solo that mob.

With threat control and frost / freezing trap, you can even solo pairs of elites by kiting and controling one while killing the other.

Of course there are multiple good answers to your question: Druid can solo any elite by slooooooowly killing it with Entangling Roots damage but that is very very boring.
