## Post 14062 by Merikkinon (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2021-11-22T23:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14062#p14062 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You'd have to (currently) purchase an 'Appearance Change' token.

Now, FWIW, I would love to see a reusable 'appearance change' token, for any given player (soulbound), but completely re=usable. Some of the skins, we don't feel all that great about. Others we might want to later try.

It can get annoyingly expensive to buy a skin, realize is doesn't look good on a character, and then have to purchase a 'change-back' token.

So I'd love to see that, and the issue could be that it gives players the ability to change their char back to whatever they want, many times over - which is too powerful, really. Unless you made it a pretty expensive token.

So that is an idea for a reusable token.

It might also be nice to get an appearance change token whenever we buy a skin, which would effectively just revert the appearance back to the original, one time.

Greetings!

Game Masters offer preview for items before purchase and refunds after the items has been purchased.

You don't have to purchase a Appearance Change Token to remove a skin you don't like. Upon refund a Game Master will help you  return to your old appearance

To contact a game master open an in game ticket:  [viewtopic.php?p=12893#p12893](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12893#p12893)
