A HC only server just wouldn't work in the long run. Not only would it be a fairly empty world, an active feeling world being something that is the lifeblood of any MMO, but it'd also completely kill off just about any potential to have a serious community for endgame content, not to mention completely devaluing rewards like the unique mount and immortal title. Who cares if you have a title of literally every other 60 on the server also has it? Even worse, you'd have so few actual 60's, either due to people simply rerolling to level again in HC, or just due to the fact that only a fraction of the people on the server will actually reach 60 for one reason or another that it'd be all but dead in terms of pvp and raiding content.

All in all, yeah a spike in population can suck for leveling, and it sounds like it's being addressed already via spawn timers and the like currently, but a new and HC only server would really not do nearly as much as you think to fix these problems, and would only serve to make a worse experience for those looking to play in the HC ruleset.
