# Zeljeb the Ancient - New boss in ZF

## Post 146901 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-19T04:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146901#p146901 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Zeljeb comes with a tricky mechanic: a barrier shield that reduces all incoming damage by 90%, making him nearly unkillable if you just brute-force it.

To deal with this, you’ll need the Flame of Farrak, an item located near Chief Ukorz. Look for the brazier right beside him—interact with it and you’ll receive the flame.

![⚠️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/26a0.svg) Important: The Flame only lasts 10 minutes, so don’t grab it too early. Make sure you’ve cleared a safe path all the way to Zeljeb before picking it up.

Once you’re ready, grab the flame, head to Zeljeb, and use it to completely strip away his barrier.
