# I have a proposal to Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread and make it to a new classic pvp style

## Post 165131 by shadowchaos — 2026-01-14T13:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165131#p165131 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Since the twow team started adding 5 mins cd to Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread instead to remove it, I have a much more fair move to balance pvp and with these changes ,a more fair classic wow pvp style could be built.

Besides 5 mins cd, below changes should be appended:
 1. Full duration reduced to 10 seconds and every 2 seconds a tick.
This should be along with all CC in pvp up to ONLY 10 seconds.
(compare to later expansion upon full resilience, people can still be taken down with teammates cc'd , now with twow burst era and half effective of resilience, 10 seconds is still long.)

 2. Besides it breaks soft CC , it should have a ratio break fear too , the ratio should bigger than a single dot , smaller than a direct damage.
(it ticks every 2 seconds, so it will help classes to better counter fear effect.)

 3. Add it to all main city pvp vendors.

Well, it's obviously a new pvp play style and it's fair.
It can be used almost only once per fight .
It provides one opportunity to well balance pvp :
sheep and sap too long too powerful stat , but still mage and rogue can counter too, mage have up to 2 ice block and rogue have up to 2 vanish.
hunter may change scatter shot and freezing trap tactic.
for warlock , seduce + soulfire showcases is reduced.
ret pal? shouldn't a problem.

Anyway , it's a pre-cast insignia and only 10 sec , it requires skill and the opponent can use and can counter too.
