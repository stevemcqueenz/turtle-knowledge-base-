# Letter to Turtle WoW

## Post 156382 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-20T23:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156382#p156382 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I've been playing here for about two and a half years. My High Elf started in the old Loch Modan zone, and I managed to complete the entire series of old quests; Gilneas didn't even exist yet.

I've seen this server grow, evolve, improve, and eventually become what Blizzard could never give me.

I love Turtle WoW, and when I started playing, I knew it would one day come to an end. It hasn't arrived yet, but I know it will eventually. Nothing lasts forever, except Blizzard's garbage.

All I have to say is thank you for so many years of passion and enthusiasm for such an old game. Thank you for showing that Warcraft can be better, that it can be what a small niche is looking for.

Many say it was just for the money, and maybe so. In the end, it's all a business. But small details in the lore, zones, and quests showed that there is love behind it all, that there are people who love the game.

It's simply beautiful. Sorry if I'm a fanboy, but Turtle brought back the excitement and love I had for this very old game, even forgotten by Blizzard, who supposedly created it.

I hope you can continue. If not, thank you for bringing me back to a time in my life I thought was long gone. Thank you for your hard work. I hope I get to see Quel'thalas, which would be the perfect finishing touch to this incredible adventure.

Thank you Turtle WoW Team. Thank you Torta, Akalix, Dated, and all the GMs I've encountered along my journey.
