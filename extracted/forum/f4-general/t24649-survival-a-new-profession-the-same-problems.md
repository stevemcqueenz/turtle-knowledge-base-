# Survival - a new profession, the same problems.

## Post 173568 by Ulukay (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-04-01T06:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173568#p173568 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Survival is a new profession, the same problems.
I do not know why the original developers made a mistake when allocating the cost of resources for crafting items, and I do not understand why the turtle developers make the same mistakes.
Most of the actual survival items are simply useless because the material to create them is in higher-level zones. When you finally manage to get this resource, the created item is garbage, because it is the weakest that can be found in the zone of this level. There is a similar problem with blacksmithing, tannery, sewing, and partly alchemy.
I currently have a survival skill of 200, and I've only found a couple of items useful.: Simple Slingshot (for paladins), Fishing Rods, Water Trudgers (just funny), Sharpened Herb Sickle, Treasure Compass (why not), Bright Wood Arrows (for hunters). I'm not considering tents because they're useless now, as it's much easier and better to get a rest in a tavern.
In short, if you're interested in developing a survival skill, just don't do it - save more coins and time.

> **Ulukay wrote: Wed Apr 01, 2026 6:58 am**
> Most of the actual survival items are simply useless because the material to create them is in higher-level zones.

For example the Sleek Pinewood Bow is a weapon for lvl 38. It is made out of Shade Wood. Shade Wood can be gathered in northern STV or Desolace (easily doable at lvl 30) or in Hinterlands (a level 40-50 zone).

TWoW made the immersive decision and didn't just put in every lvl 20-30 zone bright wood, or in lvl 30-40 zone shade wood and so on, but what fits the zone! Tropical Wood can only be found where it is tropical, dead wood can only be found where it is pretty lifeless.

That means the level of the zone and the level of the wood not always matches in perfect balance, but it is more immersive, and that is way more important in my opinion.
