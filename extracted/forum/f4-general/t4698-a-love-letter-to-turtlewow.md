# A love letter to TurtleWoW

## Post 28089 by Razzz — 2022-10-28T19:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28089#p28089 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dearest Turtle of WoW
Do you want a cheesy sob story?
You don't?
Too bad, have one anyways!  I don't remember the exact date when I first gave the server a shot, but I didn't stay long.
I guess I had other things going on and other games I was playing.
Then 2021 Halloween happened and I found myself falling ill.
I'm still ill now, and I've been spending every single day in some form of pain and discomfort.
And because nobody has found the exact cause yet I've been having to deal with doctors and nurses and aides dismissing me, even shaming me for seeking treatment.
I ended up in the ER throughout spring because I was just going in a loop trying to find someone to help me.
My doctor wouldn't believe me, so I went to urgent care.
Urgent care can't help me for abdomen pain and told me to go to ER.
I'd got to ER and they'd tell me that I'm not in danger of dying so it's not an emergency sooo go away but see your gastro doctor.
Gastro clinic would tell me I'm just dehydrated and to see my regular doctor.
Only now am I finally finding doctors who are taking me seriously.

TLDR: I'm fecking sick   dead_turtle_head

I started TurtleWoW again right before my illness started and have been playing ever since.
I've had very short breaks, but I always came back.
I'd talk to amazing people in discord or in game.
I'd go through quests and dungeon runs with other players who were just plain awesome.
And I'd also play the game..well, I guess "wrong" isn't the right word here.
I'm an old dumb derp who basically makes multiple characters, setup a silly roleplay story for each of them, and then switch between them all so frequently that I end up progressing slow as molasses.
And I LOVE IT.
You guys have no idea how badly I needed something to concentrate on during all of this.
And so, dearest TurtWo, I thank you from the bottom of my heart.   turtle_in_love
P.s. One lil complaint about World chat being a mess, but I quit it a few months ago and that solved that.
