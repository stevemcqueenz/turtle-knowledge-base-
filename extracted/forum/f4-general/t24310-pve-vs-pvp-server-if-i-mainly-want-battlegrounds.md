Nordanaar – PvE, very high pop

Another vote for Tel'Abim, but I play there (Horde priest), so I'm biased. I have no experience with Ambershire.

Pros:
 1. Fast BG queues as Alliance at 60.
 2. Decent pop (2k+) during peak hours (EU evenings).
 3. Only server with WPvP (e.g. 60v60+ world boss fights).
 4. Amazing community.

Cons:
 1. Less activity outside of peak hours (but this makes farming easier...?).
 2. Fewer BGs while leveling.

I wouldn't be surprised if Tel'Abim has a surge in popularity after the new PvP patch.

I echo the above suggestion to roll Alliance. Faction balance is enforced but Horde are more active in PvP and Ally in PvE. Your BG queues will be near instant as Ally. (There are lots of great Ally PvPers, they just don't queue as often as Horde. BG win rates slightly skew towards Horde in my experience.)

I suggest holy paladin, priest, or warlock. HPals are the best PvP healers imo. Ally don't like playing priest/warlock due to WotF but they are still good (dispel magic is perhaps the most important spell in the game, and warlocks are generally strong). Maybe consider hunter as well (lots of utility, like frost traps, and MM is getting a buff soon).

Edit: I forgot to mention, probably the biggest question is whether you want to play cross-faction or not. Ambershire is cross-faction, Tel'Abim is not. Many of us on Tel'Abim abhor xfaction and would rather quit than play on a xfaction server.
