## Post 16516 by Tortuga (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-01-31T08:14:00Z

Some addons quietly add their own channels: LFT (1 channel), MRP (2 channels). These channels stay active even if the addon gets disabled
If you quit a guild recently, client will be auto-adding you to a city-only channel "GuildRecruitment" (this can be disabled in the interface settings)
Another city-only channel is "Trade" (like most default channels, nobody uses it)
You can check your current channels with /chatlist (or /listchat , never had a reason to remember this)
