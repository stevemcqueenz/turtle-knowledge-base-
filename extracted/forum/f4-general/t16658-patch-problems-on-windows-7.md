Have you tried opening the Task Manager and ending the rundll32.exe*32 process? The patcher always stalls for me because that particular generically named process seems to underpin the WoW.exe*32 process, and until those processes stop keeping the WoW.exe file "in use", the patcher cannot overwrite it with the new version.
