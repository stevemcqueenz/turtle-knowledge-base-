Leeet's see…  According to *Wowpedia*, the Free *Warcraft* Encyclopedia that Anyone Can Edit™, a player can be flagged for PvP by [doing any of the following](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/PvP_flag):
> - You put your PvP flag up permanently.
>    (Via "/pvp" or enabling it via portrait.)
> - You attack an NPC marked by a PvP flag, like most quest givers, guards, and vendors.
> - You are in proximity to certain NPCs marked by a PvP flag. These NPCs are often found near settlements of an opposing faction, such as Goldshire and Razor Hill.
> - You accept some of the PvP quests. You will remain flagged as long as that quest is in your quest log.
> - You enter a specific territory.
>    (Horde / Alliance cities (faction-depending), Battlegrounds, or Arenas.)

An example of the last one would be if you were a Horde player going from the Valley of Trials over to Ratchet, you should not have been flagged, but if you were an Alliance player doing the same, though and you got spotted by a guard, that would automatically flag you for five minutes.

As you said, if you were just idling around the Flight Master, completely unflagged, then you should not have been attacked by anything.
