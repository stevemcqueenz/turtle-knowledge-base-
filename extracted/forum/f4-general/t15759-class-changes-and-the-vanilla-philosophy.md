# Class Changes and the vanilla philosophy

## Post 107451 by Itorch100 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-10T22:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107451#p107451 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

After reading through the recent class changes, I've felt the need to voice some of my concerns in regards to how the changes line up with the og vanilla design philosophy. This is likely to be quite rough and all over the place information-wise, but hopefully I get my point across.

 - There are cases where the same talent row provides 2 abilities, which doesn't give one of the abilities room to "breathe" on its' own for 5-10 levels (rogue combat lvl 20) Those abilities add a new layer of gameplay and should be spread out farther in between, which would very much smooth out the feeling of RPG progression and give player a chance to get used to the change before introducing another.

 - I'm not a massive fan of paladins getting Crusader Strike as early as lvl 10, especially with a buff component. While paladins in vanilla are known for being auto-attack spam sleeper levelers, and a change is very much necessary (fun should always be the highest criteria for game design), getting it so early feels.. well, too early. Especially since there already is a new ability to use - Holy Strike.
The buff part feels like it should come from a talent and not be a baseline passive effect, especially as early as lvl 10 where your class should still feel very bare-bones.

 - To add to the topic of paladins and Crusader Strike, paladins in og vanilla do not have a preference of weapon speed (I could be wrong) and is the only melee class that does not. Since Crusader Strike has a fixed weapon damage modifier, and all other 2H melee classes/specs (arms, enhance, survival) have abilities that scale with weapon damage, it makes fast 2H weapons undesirable all-around and feel "bad" to get. To preserve paladins' indifference to weapon choice, an easy fix would be to make the weapon damage component scale with weapon speed.

 - Another minor concern is having "dead" talents for certain builds (survival going for improved stings in MM) that you have to go through in order to get the desired talent, effectively totally wasting 5 points.

Again, apologies if this is all over the place, but I didn't feel the need to create multiple separate topics. Love the server, and hoping together we can bring it to a place where a vast majority is happy with the changes :)

To preserve paladins' indifference to weapon choice, an easy fix would be to make the weapon damage component scale with weapon speed.

You are wrong here, Seal of Command, in og vanilla, was procs per minute, which favours slower speed weapons, as you'll get the same number of procs in a given period of time, however the slower swinging weapon means those procs will hit harder, resulting in higher damage. Spellpower builds focusing on seal of righteousness on the other hand favoured faster weapons, as the bonus damage from spellpower scaled linearly regardless of weapon speed, but, at least in classic, that was more of a meme build.

But yeah, the majority of ret in vanilla wanted slow weapons to take advantage of hard hitting seal of command procs. Slower weapons also meant higher proc rate on any individual hit, so that meant that you had better consistency when trying to do burst damage with a stun -> white hit -> SoC proc -> judgement
