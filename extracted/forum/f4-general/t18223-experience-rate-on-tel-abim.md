Greetings fellow turtles,

Is it possible for a player to adjust their experience rate on Tel'Abim to x1?

From what I can tell, the base experience rate is currently set at x2. If you select the Glyph of the Turtle, your experience rate goes from x2 to x0.5. For example, your character is level 1 and you slay a level 1 NPC you gain 100 experience points instead of the normal 50, but with the Glyph of the Turtle it goes from 100 experience points to 25 which is a 75% decrease. Not sure if there's a ".xp rate x#" command that would allow players to adjust between an experience of x1 and x2.
