# My take on talents specs and the new talent spec

## Post 104160 by Alterno164 — 2024-10-21T18:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104160#p104160 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First I would like to apologize if this is not the appropriate space for this discussion and second I would like to thanks to Turtle WoW Developers for the huge effort you guys put into this experience is awesome. With that said, let's begin. So today I got to see <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/priest> I want to use that class and discipline build as an example for the rest of the classes their specs.

The first thing I noticed is that this spec seems to be way oriented into holy dpsing: Piercing Light, purifying flames, Searing Light for example and some others are that benefits both holy and disc. Now I think there's a clever way to make each class spec to feel unique, and it would be by building upon the spells abilities, a good example is your resurgent shield talent. I think that is the right way, not only for priest, but for all classes, to build around spells abilities.

I want to give  some examples using the disc priest  (just think about it for each class):

Healer Shields
Sacred shield grants %n chance to either damage the enemy or heal the priest for %n
Sacred shields: Improves the healing done by the shield's caster by 5, 10 and 15% on the shielded target
Sacred resonation: heals all shielded targets by 1% to 5% after landing a critical heal on a shielded target.

DPS Shield
Consecrated Shield: All spell damage is increased by %n, reduces the amount of damage absorption by %n.
Consecrated Resonation: All shielded targets increase their damage by 1% to 5% after priest lands a critical spell damage, last n secs.

Others
Improved dispel: Makes immune to a detrimental enemy magic, lasts (15 or 30) secs
Improved Inner Fire: Grants %n chance to reduce the enemy's armor class by n for each melee damage taken.
Improved shackles of undead:  Shackles an undead for a minute, after it breaks deals % points of damage over n seconds and reduce armor for n seconds.
Improved Prayer of Sprit: While active, gives %n chance to regenerate mana and hp by %n for n seconds after being struck by a critical spell or melee attack that lowers health below 10%.

So hopefully you get my idea. Anyway I am again thankful for the effort you guys put into this.

Another talents that came to my mind, are:

Improved Inner Focus or Sacred Focus:

Shield Resonance: New (talent) spell , casts shields over  all members of the party, except the target, if not target is selected, shields all the members of the party except the priest. Increases Weakened soul by n seconds.
