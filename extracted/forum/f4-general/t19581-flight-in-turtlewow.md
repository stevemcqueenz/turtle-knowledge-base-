# Flight in TurtleWoW!

## Post 133273 by Dyzium — 2025-05-28T04:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133273#p133273 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I’d really love to see flying mounts from The Burning Crusade make a return in Classic! And if you ever consider that, it would be amazing to bring back Druid Flight Form too. ![😊](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f60a.svg)

I know it probably sounds way simpler than it actually is, but flying has always been one of my favorite parts of exploring Azeroth. It gave the world a feeling of openness and freedom that really stuck with me. I love what you’re doing with WoW Classic and the Vanilla Plus direction, but I won’t lie—it's a little sad not having flying in the game. It added something special to the world that I really miss.

I’m not a modder or developer, so I don’t know how complicated it would be, but I just wanted to toss in some thoughts and ideas in case it ever becomes something you’re open to. Maybe flight could be added in a balanced way, like unlocking it later in the game or limiting it to certain areas, like how it worked in Outland. That way it doesn’t interfere too much with leveling, exploration, or world PvP early on.

 **Some Pros and Cons I’ve thought about:**
 **Pros:**

Enhanced Exploration: Flying gives players a new perspective of Azeroth. Seeing zones from the sky makes the world feel huge and immersive in a different way.

Convenient Travel: It makes getting around easier without relying too much on portals or long flight path routes—especially in big zones with hard-to-reach areas.

Druid Fantasy: Flight Form is iconic and really adds to the class fantasy. It’s fun, useful, and makes Druids feel even more connected to nature.

Endgame Utility: For high-level gathering professions like herbalism and mining, flying adds depth and efficiency, encouraging players to revisit old zones.

Unlockable Goal: Giving flying a high cost or tying it to a questline could create a fun long-term goal and sense of progression for max-level players.

 ****Cons (with suggestions to help):****

Might make world PvP harder since players can just fly away.
→ *Maybe add a longer cast time before taking off, so it’s not an instant escape. For Druids, Flight Form could have a short delay or be interrupted by combat.*

Could let players skip over content and avoid mobs, dungeons, or PvP areas.
→ *Maybe disable flying in certain zones or areas (like battleground objectives, world bosses, or dungeon entrances), or force landing near quest targets.*

Older zones weren’t designed for flying, so it could break immersion or let players access areas they’re not supposed to.
→ *This sounds tricky, but maybe simple fixes could help, like adding invisible no-fly zones in key spots or limiting flying height in certain places. Another easy idea might be to only allow flying in a few zones at first—like the higher-level zones or zones that are more open and less likely to cause problems.*

Takes away from the "boots on the ground" Classic feel.
→ *Flying could be restricted until level 60+ or even locked behind a new rep grind or gold cost, so it doesn’t change early-game experience at all.*

 **I totally understand that flight might not fit your vision right now, but I wanted to share these thoughts just in case it ever becomes a possibility in the future.**

Please **consider** it—flying was such a memorable part of WoW for me, and I’d love to see it again someday in the world of TurtleWoW/Classic. Thanks for all the work you’re doing to keep the game alive and evolving. ![🙏](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f64f.svg)
