Quite a few of the custom quests have nice rewards at their end. But just like there are stinkers in Classic wow, some custom quests are also stinkers.
I suggest you double check custom quest item drops against the database to ensure you actually kill the correct mob. I caught myself more than once hitting the wrong mob since some of the quest descriptions are a bit vague.

The ones with large travel requirements also usually serve to send you places so you can continue questing there. rather than the quest itself being the big reward. My suggestion would be just check the questchain out in the database if you don't like the exploratory aspects of it.

Overall i've personally had a quite decent experience with the new quests

## Post 55365 by Allwynd01 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-07-31T13:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55365#p55365 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You don't have to do or like every quest there is. Just avoid those that you don't feel are worth it. On my first recent playthrough I made it my goal to complete all custom quests, but I quickly realized some just aren't worth the effort. One that I actually enjoyed was the Harvest Golem Mystery quest chain as well as the Church of Westfall one, because they really added to the lore and were easy and straightforward.

On the contrary, I vaguely remember some in Shimmering Flats that sent you on a wild goose chase, some even required items you could not get on your own or on the AH, which made the quest not soloable at all. Such quests I just abandoned and never completed.

There was one I think in Feralas where you have to collect some items from Wind Serpents and Sea Giants, and the drop rates were so horribly low that I would listen to podcasts for hours grinding trying to not die of boredom, but I somehow compelted.

There are some quests, which I don't think are Turtle-made, like escorting Robot Chickens, those are so horrible and punishing that one in Tanaris we even failed as a party and we all gave up after the 2nd try.

There are really some bad quests, both original and custom, but there are lots of good ones too and there are plenty of them to get you to 60 so you aren't forcing yourself doing things you don't enjoy.
