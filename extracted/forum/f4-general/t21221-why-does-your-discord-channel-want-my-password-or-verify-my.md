## Post 147348 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-08-20T19:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147348#p147348 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Discord has a significant problem with botted or hacked accounts spamming phishing links to steal people's accounts. With our Discord server being over 130,000 members, we're a prime target for these types of scammers. Unfortunately, this means we need to enable Discord's highest level of security--which requires users to 2FA their accounts using their phone numbers **with Discord**. Please note that we do not see, know, or touch your phone number -- it's entirely on Discord's side.
