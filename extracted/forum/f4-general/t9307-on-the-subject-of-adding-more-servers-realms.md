# On the subject of adding more servers/realms.

## Post 60844 by Profgiggles — 2023-09-04T17:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60844#p60844 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've got a couple of concerns. Since the massive influx of players and overpopulation became the reality we have to deal with, I see it suggested every day that TWoW should **just** make more servers.

There are people who want servers for this, that and the other thing. At this point I think a separate server has been proposed for every single game mode.

Warmode should be on its own specific server.
HC should be on its own server.
How about warmode HC? Toss 'em on their own server, why not.
And open a couple more normal servers too.

Now... Let me start off with saying that none of these are old ideas. They have been suggested since before the problem of overpopulation. I see them brought up far more often nowadays however, and its understandable. People are tired of being faced with a +6000 queue upon trying to log in. More servers would fix it, surely.

But would it though? Would it really?
Realistically the queue times do currently serve as a bit of a deterrence. I don't think as many new players are currently joining as there would otherwise have been, had the current overpopulation not been as it is, which is a scary thought, because the queue grows by roughly a thousand players on a weekly basis.

What would that number be, if there were multiple servers and no queue? Two thousand? Three?
Who knows, its all speculation, but the point is that no matter how many servers we add, I do believe they will continue to be filled, and probably fairly quickly too.

If an overpopulation is given more room to grow and expand, it will continue to grow and expand until its circumstances change and the support for growing and expanding is no longer there.

Furthermore, I don't know how adding a bunch of servers works legally speaking. I've heard of private servers being shut down by ActiBlizz before. Are they going to bother with every private server that hosts a couple hundred players? Probably not. But a server with now about 22k players seems as if it would be a prime target.

Especially if that server branches off into multiple servers, and the population continues to grow. Am I being paranoid for thinking that we will eventually draw the attention of someone in the legal department at ActiBlizz who is going to press a big red button and shut TWoW down, if TWoW grows too big, and too much attention is drawn to it?

I want to reiterate. Just opening more servers is not necessarily going to solve the overpopulation problem, for as long as the foundation for the problem continues to exist, and furthermore I believe it could draw the wrong kind of attention to us.

In addition there is the logistics side of it, which is an issue on its own. Adding more servers is adding more infrastructure, which is adding more stuff to run and maintain. Right now we're enjoying a game that is having new, awesome and custom content added. It is actively being developed, improved and expanded upon. Having to run and maintain several servers would take time away from this.
Just think of the next release, which has been finished and ready for weeks, but now can't be released until the SEA Turtle is ready.
Now imagine that we had like five servers, that would all need to be updated whenever new content is being added and bugs are fixed. That would add considerably to the workload.
Now I imagine someone is going to say they should just hire more people to do this.
Indeed, but then we once again enter the territory of drawing the attention of someone who might want to shut us down. If Actiblizz sees that a private server effectively turns into a business, selling a product based on theirs and hiring people to run and maintain it, are they just going to sit idly and let that happen? History suggests they won't.

Anyhow. Maybe I'm paranoid, and if I am, please feel free to let me know, but I think this is a subject that needs to be discussed. What are the potential **consequences** of opening more servers, and is it even feasible, if we want to still be able to play in years to come?
