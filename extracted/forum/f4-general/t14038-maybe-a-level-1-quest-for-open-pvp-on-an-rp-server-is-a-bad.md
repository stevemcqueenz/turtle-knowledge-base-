If you are on the 'war mode' challenge you can buff and heal whomever without worry, but if they do the same to you and are unflagged and unrestricted then they'll be flagged as well.

Alternatively, if you are on the 'hardcore' challenge' the game/server will stop you from flagging yourself by accident.
