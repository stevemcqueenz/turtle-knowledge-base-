Has really changed the experience for me. I really needed this as after playing retail for years, I have to really relearn how to just enjoy the journey. As, anyone that played retail, knows that it's all about blitzing everything to max level then farming Mythic+, Raids, and the latest zone only. Maybe I'll try Horde again in the future after I reach 60 on these two.
