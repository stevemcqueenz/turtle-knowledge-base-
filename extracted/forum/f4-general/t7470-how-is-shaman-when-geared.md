Elementals biggest weakness was always his manapool. The new clearcasting is roughly twice as good as the old one, once you get a bit of gear. With the potential to be even better as your critchance climbs. Tea with sugar also helps massively with the manaproblems.

Surely you ain't a Warlock in terms of raw dmg output. But our two resident elemental shamans are consistently performing decently. And mana needs to be managed. But the manapool is manageable and only really an issue in the longest and most manaintensive fights.

The mob resistences can be a bit of an issue, but a few pieces with -res or a tf wielder in the raid takes the edge of that one decently enough

TLDR: Elemental is certainly not the top performer, and a small buff certainly wouldn't hurt, but it functions decently well in group and raidcontent and if someone wants to play it, they should give it a try ;)
