## Post 27989 by Razzz — 2022-10-26T14:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27989#p27989 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Advice for a new player to go?
Join the discord so that you can ask questions freely!
There's also Newcomers guild which all new characters join automatically, but the chat there tends to go downhill quickly while discord is better (and easier) for moderators in my opinion.

Fun experience?
Yup!
For me personally the only time I've really been annoyed is gold making starting at level 20, but I've always been pretty bad at making money in these games so that might be a me problem. XD

Certain low level and dungeon with randos?
Yes and no.
We have our own custom dungeon finder inspired adon but with a catch:  it helps form a party but it doesn't teleport you to the dungeon! (which is cool to ME anyways)
Also, players still actively recruit outside the addon in various chats or even privately messing folks in the level range and ask if they'd be interested in joining them.
So there's more incentive to be civil and helpful in dungeon runs since it takes more time and preparation than on retail.

Will you be on your own?
Not if you don't want to be.
For one thing, raids are end game, so don't worry about that for a while since leveling is slower here.
Besides dungeons, there are elite quests that pretty much require you to party up to complete them, and if you happen upon a fellow adventurer who is on the same quest as you then they'd likely be alright with partying up for mob killing.
Note that solo play is easier if the quest requires lots of loot from lots of mobs because of how group loot rules work, but you get the picture.

So there be my two cents on the matter!
Have fun!

## Post 27990 by Shamma (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-10-26T15:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27990#p27990 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Do NOT play a warrior or a druid as a first time player. They require more knowledge to execute properly and are desirable tanks (which is not a role you should be trying to fulfill as a newbie).

Easiest to pick up and play are paladin, hunter, mage and warlock. Maybe play one of these.

Actively seek to grp up with ppl of different classes and observe what they can do and how they like to play. Maybe you will find out that you would rather play a different class and start over (very common thing in WoW) or change the way you play what you chose.

Think about whether you like healing or not. Not difficult for a new player to learn to heal effectively in grp content.
