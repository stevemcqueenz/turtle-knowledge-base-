# A please for twow team

## Post 153404 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-27T01:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153404#p153404 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear twow team, u doing a great job adding a ton of exclusive and interesting content. Interesting locations, lore-related quests, spells, talents, boss mechanics. But please, stop breaking game classes and, under the guise of improvements, creating one-button specs that dominate everything. Effort and a well-thought-out rotation should be rewarded, not the annihilation of a single button. Another mistake is buffing every spec of combined classes to the level of classes with a narrower focus. Balance isn't about equalizing the numbers on a dummy, but about equalizing the overall usefulness of a class, taking into account the weight of all spells and abilities — defensive, crowd control, buffs, offensive, and healing.
