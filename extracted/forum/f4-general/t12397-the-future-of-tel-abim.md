Hello everyone.
I recently started my Turtle WoW journey, this is my first experience in private servers after years and years of playing on the Blizzard servers but my time there as come to a close for several reasons.

I have created a character on the PvP server Tel'Abim, now i have noticed that the population there at peaks seems to be around 700 or a bit more, i don't have issue with those numbers, i think that for both factions that number is enough to do all raids, dungeons and other content that the game has to offer, but i can be wrong.
Normally people seem to gravitate to the server that have highest numbers, that's seems normal and that seems to be the trend, that even happened on Blizzard's Classic WoW where PvP servers turned to be one faction servers, either Horde or Alliance, at least here and on Tel'Abim it seems that the factions are quite balanced and that one can aspire to find pvp in the world.

But i wonder how or what's being done to ensure Tel'Abim future? Maybe that never comes but even the population drops to numbers that don't make it viable to play there anymore, for the economy, content, what will become of the server and the players there since we can't transfer our characters?

In a way i don't understand why a server like Tel'Abim doesn't get more attention since it seems to be a very recent server, maybe 2 months old i guess?, and it can be seen as a fresh server since the content will be unlocked in a progressive way and i thought that people like fresh servers.

For me Tel'Abim will always remain smaller (which doesn't mean worse) version of Nordanaar RP-PVE. This has pros and cons.

We have cozy community where we know each other better. We have much larger english-speaking percentage of whole server population than Nordanaar. We have challenging raids without shaman/paladin with progressing gear. We don't have problems with lack of resources such as mining veins or herbs in the world unlike Nordanaar.

Though it might be difficult to spam dungeons with strangers in Tel'Abim. Battlegrounds also don't pop so often.

Developers' work apply to both Nordanaar and Tel'Abim so this is how they're going to ensure its stability. They announced PVP changes coming within few next updates so maybe something will change for better. I think Tel'Abim will remain smaller brother of RP-PVE server as an alternative for those who don't like playing with Chinese and other non-english players, for those who hate inflated prices of consumables in Nordanaar and for those who simply prefer quality over quantity - cozy community rather than mega server.
