## Post 130512 by Skullcat — 2025-05-10T22:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130512#p130512 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Warlock is the good reliable solo-class. And that still remains the fact in Turtle WoW. Though it might get little sketchy at time, Voidwalker'll carry you a lot throughout leveling experience.

It doesn't require a heavy intensive skill level other than knowing your spells and what they can do.

And it's easier entry than any other classes. At level 40, you'll get free mount without buying the riding skill.

I would recommend Warlock.
