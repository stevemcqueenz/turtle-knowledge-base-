I didn't have much fun with Crossover, Parallels, Wine.. there was always one problem or another. Either wouldn't stay connected or the mouse didn't behave properly.

I use Sunshine on my Windows PC and then use MoonLight on the Mac to stream from it at low latency across the house. Eventually I got myself one of those low power mini PCs to host Sunshine instead so it wouldn't tie up my main computer. Only thing I had to really change on the mini PC was disable mouse acceleration (since the Mac already does it when it transmits movement)
