If you go to the main page and click "Our Features" there is a lot of stuff you can browse through.  The added content, quests, etc are all throughout the game.  There are a lot of new quests to enjoy.  One of the best parts of playing turtle is finding new things organically imho.  I stumbled upon a new troll area in Arathi Highlands on my first playthrough and was like "woah, wth is this?!"  It makes you read quests and not just blindly click accept for every quest.

The addon situation is kind of unique.  Lots of devs and players talk about how Turtle WoW should have used the WOTLK 3.3.5a client rather than 1.12 vmangos.  However, I find myself quite happy with the situation lately.  In later versions of the game, you have addons that can basically play the game for you.  Its much harder to find compatible addons.  It adds a layer of difficulty to the game.  Sure, there are lots of QOL features newer addons bring, but this is Turtle WoW where we take it nice and sloooowww.

Enjoy your time here!
