## Post 50444 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-18T08:29:00Z

Honestly, immersion dies the instant you set foot in any populated area.  Sure, most people might *look* like the usual assortment of Humans, Gnomes, Orcs, Tauren, and so on… but when they're using Trade chat to talk about real-world events, or using Guild chat to make inappropriate jokes, or even just talking about game mechanics and comparing items, it immediately shatters the immersion for me.  Even with my "foolproof" special chat tab that only shows local chat and creature events, I still end up getting the occasional message that just… makes me shake my head.  Especially when I go somewhere and find a… *𝐓𝐄𝐍𝐓*.
The server label might say "(RP-PVP)", but with the amount of players on at any time and with how many refugees there are from other, non-RP emulated server, it may as well just say "(PvP)".

All that being said… I **strongly disagree** with the proposed suggestion of making Shop Illusions "break on damage".  People pay good money for those things and, as wild as it might seem, some people actually *do* use them for more than just standing out from the crowd.  I saw a Dryad roleplayer in Teldrassil, once.  I saw a Banshee in Tirisfal, too.  They both had *TurtleRP* profiles, too.  It was cool!  I'd rather not lose that just because some random people want to get to Level 60 deathless as demons and the like.
