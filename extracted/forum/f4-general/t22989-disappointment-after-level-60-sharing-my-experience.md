# Disappointment After Level 60 Sharing My Experience

## Post 160454 by Dimastasc — 2025-11-23T19:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160454#p160454 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey everyone.
Just wanted to share my experience maybe someone will find it useful, or maybe someone will recognize themselves in it. When I first started playing Turtle WoW, I was honestly full of excitement. The atmosphere, the quests, the cozy vibe  it all really pulled me in. I thought: once I hit level 60, that’s when the real game begins. Raids, PvP gear, new goals, actual progression  like the game would truly start at max level.
But once I finally dinged 60, I realized all that “real fun” ends exactly where I thought it would begin.
Let’s start with PvE.
Trying to gear up was a reality check straight to the face.
Without a guild  you’re nobody. Literally. You might get into some random raid, but the chances are about the same as getting a mount with a 0.3% drop rate. And even if you do get in  you’re standing there in your mix of greens and blues, feeling like an uninvited guest at someone else’s wedding.
And if something actually drops? Well, you still have to win it. And of course someone else wants the same item, so good luck with that.
And to join a proper guild, you have to match their raid times, their schedule, their requirements. Some raid at night, some during the day  and meanwhile you’ve got work, studies, life. It ends up feeling like, before you can even play, you need to rearrange your real-life schedule to match someone else’s timetable. I literally sat there looking at guild schedules and realized even if I wanted to join, none of the raid times actually fit my life. Too late, too early, wrong days… something always didn’t work.
Now PvP.
This is honestly where I broke.
I go into battlegrounds, excited, thinking: “Alright, I’ll farm honor, get my rank, buy PvP gear. At least here I can progress.”
How naive I was.
On BGs I felt like a pet mouse thrown into a pit with wild alley cats. PvE-geared players shredded me like I wasn’t even there.
But I thought: fine, I’ll suffer through this, I’ll grind, I’ll push, all I need is access to the Hall of Fame.
Three weeks.
THREE WEEKS of suffering  losing, dying instantly, getting stomped just to get Officer rank. And finally, after all that pain, I’m excited. I think: “Yes, I did it. Time to check out the PvP vendors and finally see at least some reward for all this misery.”
I walk up.
I open the vendor.
I scroll.
And… nothing.
I literally can’t buy anything.
Not even decent mid-tier PvP gear.
The only things that are actually purchasable are two trashy items  just TWO  and even those require a rank higher than Officer (like Knight-Captain / Captain-Lieutenant, I don’t remember exactly). But the point is: Officer gives you NOTHING.
So why did I torture myself for three weeks? Just to look at an empty vendor window?..
To get real PvP gear I would need even higher ranks  which means even more weeks of humiliation, pointless grinding, constant losses, and feeling like a punching bag rather than a player.
And then it hit me:
What am I even doing here at level 60?
PvE? Only if you join a guild with raid times that don’t fit my life.
PvP? Only if you’re a masochist or a student who can play all day.
Other activities?  Sure, I can farm gold or run old dungeons… but why?
I genuinely felt empty.
Like I spent the whole game climbing to level 60, and when I opened the door  the room behind it was completely empty.
The result:
My online time dropped to almost zero.
There’s just nothing meaningful to do, no sense of direction, no growth.
I don’t even feel like logging in anymore because I already know there’s nothing waiting for me.
Pretty sure I’ll move on and look for another project one where hitting max level doesn’t mean hitting a wall.

Sorry to hear it. Unfortunately it was a mismatch between expectations and reality. A few things that might help anyone reading this but who hasn’t experienced life at level 60:

Endgame gearing between fresh level 60 and Molten Core is actually quite significant assuming you’re an average player in an average guild. Lots of 5 mans, crafting, grinding gold, watching the AH, quests, attunes and bis hunting. It’s really fun tbh. Maybe my favorite phase in the entire life cycle of a server. You’re skipping the best part!

Guilds make endgame much easier to acquire gear. Which is why most people are in a guild even if they can’t raid.

But the quality level of pugs, the organization and leadership, strat knowledge by puggers etc, is far far ahead of where vanilla ever was. It’s actually reasonable to pug MC entirely week after week and have it be a decent time. ZG/AQ20 pugs are usually smooth too. BWL is where this falls apart.

I’m sorry that you grinded PvP without understanding the lack of rewards from mid-ranks. One of the biggest differences between modern retail wow and old wow is that you generally need to be deliberate in your gear plan in old wow. Modern wow showers loot on you, and it usually fits right into your gear plan. Whereas old wow requires you to research and target specific gear if you want the process to be smooth.

Which server are you on? Hitting max level should feel like the opposite of a wall. It should feel like there’s too much to do. It’s just a matter of being intentional and understanding exactly where your power gains come from.
