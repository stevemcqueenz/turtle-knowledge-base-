May what I mentioned mainly applies to hardcore gaming, but druid is not powerful as some claim. That is only exaggeration.

If you play Feral
If you ass-pull two mobs, you immediately shoud go to Bear form to Demo roar, mitigate damage and use Frenzied regen if possible. It will be enough to kill one mob before losing much health.
Then you switch to humanoid form and cast Roots. Then you get as much time as you want to heal full, wait for mana to regen and do some "/dance". Then you can continue as you like.

If you play Balance, then you start with roots on some mob and kill other. You absolutely don't need to take hits from both mobs if you can CC one.
Only problem might be lack of mana (if you play Vagrant or just with bad gear).

Casting Regrowth under attacks of 2 mobs is waste of time. You get too many pushbacks.

I am. Not sure when was the last time you leveled druid. I did so in 2005, 2020 and 2024 (twow).

The class is not about killing your target quickly. It's about surviving. And in that regard druid has amazing skills to do so.

It's not about facing multiple mobs either. That is not the strong suit of feral (though it can manage well). It's the almost zero downtime you have between kills, as you only consume mana to heal and shift, and it regenerates while in a form, so you have almost no need for water (only boosted further after level 40 when you get innervate).

To add more modern info, twow druid is an absolute beast in feral, thanks to how powerful Tiger Fury is in cat. 30% increased attack speed means 30% more damage. Simply put a mob does not get to "flee".

At level 58 you also get Savage Bite for bear which is an on-demand attack that is **very** strong (at higher cost, 30 rage). When it comes to questing this turns you into an uber warrior. The only downside is it comes so late to the party you don't get to play around much with it while questing.

Add to all this the incredible itemization feral has on this server, and the feral druid of today is a completely different experience than one from vanilla.

But even so, druid has always been an absolute beast of survival. Even in vanilla. I solo'ed the spider boss in LBRS for my boots as druid back in 2005. It took 6+ minutes, but i did it. (got it on the first try, too)

If you need a spec or more tips i'm happy to share.

"Survival" you mentioned might refer to miserably making a living in difficult situations. I've generally had a similar history of playing druid and I certainly know what you mean. The awkward thing is that druid is a weak class in vanilla/classic. I don't mind repeating what I posted above, such as being unable to be offensive and defensive simultaneously. Look at the number of high-level druid players from both PVE and PVP sectors on Blizzard's or Turtle WoW. The number has been relatively small. Getting back to the topic, druid isn't way better than warrior in leveling, especially on HC mode, because this class needs to avoid over-2-mob fights, stay very careful on surrounding and health and prepare for complicated environmental changes due to lack of crowd control capability. Warriors have shouts, hamstring and intercept while druids have nature's grasp, bash and feral charge, but all druid's crowd control abilities are of a long cooldown, not to mention that healing can be kicked or countered, so how and when to use those skills typically frustrates new druid players. To wrap up, druid isn't a "novice-friendly class".
