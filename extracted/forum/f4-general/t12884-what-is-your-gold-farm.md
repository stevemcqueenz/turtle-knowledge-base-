Mostly this.

Herbing has become more painful lately, but I still would rather do just the bare minimum herbing to supply my own consumes, than to farm gold to buy them overpriced.  At the same time I'm not going to be herbing for money as its too boring/annoying to justify.  I've also added lashers to my farms, as it is a better source of mid-level herbs than open world farming.

The only consume I have a hard time self generating to meet my own demand is major mana potions.  To help with this, and to generate a little extra gold, I prefer to farm the cultists in silithus.  I get a couple mana potions an hour, and get crests that I can turn in for a chance at mana potions.  While the herbing competition has gone up, the cultist competition is lower than in the past (probably due to pop decline, and rockfury nerfs), so it can be pretty relaxing and enjoyable to kill cultists at a steady pace for a couple hours.  Cultists are still probably at least 50g/hr if you are geared enough to kill them fast.

For solo play, dishing out big damage to steadily kill mobs in one area is my favorite gameplay mode, so I'd rather do that, even if it is not the best g/hr.
