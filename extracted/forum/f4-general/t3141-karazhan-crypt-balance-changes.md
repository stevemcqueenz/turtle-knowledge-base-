# Karazhan Crypt Balance Changes

## Post 19176 by Dragunovi (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-06-15T11:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19176#p19176 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings! With the last server update quite a few underpowered Karazhan Crypt items were buffed to bring them in line with items of similiar level, the database should automatically update soon to reflect the new values, and delete your WDB file to see these changes ingame. Here's the changelog of the changes:

• **Splintercage Breastplate:** Increased Strength from 20 to 28
Increased Stamina from 8 to 10

• **The Crown of Shattering:** Increased Nature Spellpower from 11 to 29

• **Marrowclaw Knuckles:** Increased Agility from 10 to 13
Increased Strength from 5 to 6

• **Boneshatter Maul:** Added 1% Physical Critical Chance

• **Tome of Shivering Secrets:** Added 10 Intellect

• **Deathrune Leggings:** Increased Strength and Agility from 5 to 10

• **Shroud of the Archlich:** Replaced 5 Spirit with 10 Intellect

• **Windbreaker:** Replaced 5 Spirit with 5 Agility

• **Consumer Shackles:** Increased Strength from 9 to 15
Swapped 6 Spirit with 10 Stamina

• **Deepearth Signet:** Reduced Healing Power from 26 to 24
Increased MP5 from 4 to 6

• **Cryptkeeper's Pike:** Increased Strength from 18 to 25

• **Cadaverlink Vest:** Increased Agility from 16 to 19
Increased Intellect from 7 to 10
Increased Spirit from 7 to 9

• **Wormheart:** Increased Stamina from 20 to 25, -20 Strength penalty remains the same.

• **Clutch of Hivaxxis:** Increased Stamina from 4 to 13
Reduced Attack Power from 48 to 44

• **Amice of the Webweaver:** Increased Intellect from 18 to 20
Increased Spirit from 8 to 10

• **Darksoul Band:** Increased Spirit from 6 to 9

• **Baneforged Leggings:** Increased Strength from 18 to 23
Increased Agility from 7 to 10
Increased Shadow Resistance from 7 to 10

• **Cryptwatcher's Call:** Increased Intellect from 6 to 10
Increased Spirit from 3 to 4

• **Corpsekeeper's Charge:** Increased Intellect, Stamina and Shadow Resistance from 5 to 10
Reduced Spirit penalty from -25 to -10

• **Cryptstone Circlet:** Increased Stamina from 18 to 25
Increased Defense from 10 to 14
Increased Armor from 91 to 161

• **Wraithscale Leggings:** Increased Stamina from 11 to 12
Increased Spellpower from 16 to 28
Increased MP5 from 3 to 4

• **Staff of Alarus:** Reduced Spirit from 29 to 25
Added 26 Healing Power
