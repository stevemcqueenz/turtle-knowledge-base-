Hehe, I can tell you one quest chain that is new that you didn't list; The Syndicate quest chain given by Lord Falconcrest out of Stromgarde. Now, you might be say "but the Syndicate is hostile" and you'd be right. You have to grind their rep up to neutral, which is harder than the questline itself. No item rewards but it gives you a temporary quest item at each stage of the quest that makes Stormwind NPCs in Arathi killable.
