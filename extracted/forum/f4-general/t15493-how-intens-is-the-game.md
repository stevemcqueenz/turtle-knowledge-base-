## Post 106098 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-03T07:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106098#p106098 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle is undoubtedly one of (if not THE) the chillest server(s) I've played on before. The raiding scene (for me) has been incredibly casual and un-rushed, requirements for pre-raid consume farming is minimal, and world buffs simply do not exist. I've only done ZG, Lower Kara, AQ20 and MC in my 2 or so years experience on the server (roughly more than 1 being level cap) and I have a lot of fun when I'm around. Don't worry about playing meta specs, either. Most people are chill enough to bring pretty much anyone, and turtle's balanced things out pretty decently while (sort of) keeping the vanilla feel for most playstyles. Emphasis on most. I suggest you go look at the talent trees on the website to get a feel for what you wanna play, unless you've already got your mind set on something.
