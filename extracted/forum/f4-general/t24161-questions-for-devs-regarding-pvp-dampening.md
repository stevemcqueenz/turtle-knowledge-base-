So, with class and talent-specific Dampening coming with the next patch, I wanted to raise some questions regarding the basis for the many decisions you are about to make:

- What data will you use as basis for designating a class or spec X% dampening? BG scoreboards, or are you using anything more?

- How exactly are you gonna calculate dampening based on specs? i.e., how many points in talent tree X of class Y will categorize class Y as “spec Z”?

- How far will you consider the pvp role of respective classes with regards to their dmg output combined with, for example, their CC abilities? For example, will dampening be stronger on rogues or mages due to their significant CC abilities or are you basing the dampening on damage alone?
These are the main questions I have for now but there might be more. Dampening in its current form does not work as well as you think. It punishes some classes/specs severely and benefits others in the same way. It changes the dynamics of e.g. flagspinning in AB significantly. Concerns about the new dampening system have been widespread among the wider pvp community so I will greatly appreciate further questions or input from others. The pvp community expects significant transparency regarding these decisions.

Bottom line however, to really address the issue of pvp burstiness such as we had in 17.2, you will ultimately need to address the powercreep that started with CC2. You can not base class balance on pve logs alone. You need to account for pvp output or you will never get to the core of the issue.

Thank you

I feel like the community sometimes talks like dampening will be class/spec reductions on damage/healing *done*, and sometimes talks like dampening will be class/spec reductions on damage/healing *taken*. And it’s not clear to me from the patch notes which form of dampening is being considered. In any 1v1 matchup, it may seem like tuning damage done can get the same result as tuning damage taken. But because Turtle has more than 2 classes, these two forms of dampening aren’t equivalent.

Suppose (for example), a need arises is to make rogues tankier with no spillover effects on other class matchups not involving rogues. When dampening reduces damage done by class/spec, reducing all non-rogue damage in order to make rogues tankier would have clear spillover effects on other class matchups. And also not make rogues tankier versus other rogues. Now suppose we have the second form of dampening that instead reduces damage taken by class/spec. Then rogues could be made tankier with no spillover effects on other class matchups.

But this second form of dampening that reduces damage taken could also be worse, depending on what needs adjusting: If the need arises say to reduce rogue damage, reducing the damage taken by all non-rogues would again have spillovers in other class matchups. IMO, Turtle may need both per class/spec tuning of damage/healing done AND per class/spec tuning of damage/healing taken. Now the vast majority of classes/specs may share some default values for all these dampening values. But for those few tricky-to-balance specs, I think fine tuning of both these flavors of dampening will be needed.
