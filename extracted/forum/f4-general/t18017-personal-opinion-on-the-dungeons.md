# Personal opinion on the dungeons

## Post 122687 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-16T10:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122687#p122687 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Wanted to write a bit about my opinion on the dungeons in this game.

 **RFC**
I don't like that one faction has an earlier access to a dungeon than the other one. The dungeon itself is fun but my disbelief has to be kinda suspended when we are killing a demon at around level 15.
I always wanted RFC and Stockades to be synchronized: either both of them starting at level 13 or both becoming a level 20-ish dungeon. Flavour-wise I'd prefer the latter but someone like me who likes levelling and dungeon-spamming the most, the earlier a dungeon is available, the better. Both ways are fine by me, I just wish that the level of these two dungeons were synchronized.

 **Deadmines**
Arguably the best dungeon in the game. I have nothing to add here.

 **Wailing Caverns**
A bit too big/time-consuming and adding extra bosses didn't exactly help with that. I understand that it's mostly a horde dungeon but having a shaman might be too much of an advantage here (both tremor and poison cleanse totem are kinda OP here). While I like it, it is definitely a less forgiving and harder counterpart of DM.
One thing I'd def change is that you can currently only try the last boss once per run. The dungeon is really long by itself and being this punishing at this early level feels weird. In contrast, you can try to kill Edwin as many times as you want.

 **Stockades**
A bit boring execution, otherwise see RFC.

 **Shadowfang Keep**
One of my personal favourites. My only bigger problem is that curses here are super annoying so there's a huge QoL reliance on having a druid or a mage in the party. I'd tweak those curses a bit so it became less punishing to have no decursor in the party. Anyway, the curse is not OP, it is just mostly annoying, interrupting drinking 90% of the time.

 **Blackfathom Deeps**
I like how it introduces stealthed enemies. My biggest problem here is the summoning when we have the 4 waves of ads.
Around half of the player base does this wrong resulting in a wipe. Some in-game clue like one lever glowing at a time would be helpful. Also one wave is by far harder than the others.
All in all I like it though.

 **RFK**
It is mostly really easy. You gotta be mindful of the bats and thats all. But: the fact that the dungeon lies between the Barrens and the 1000 Needles generates a weird visual glitch every time you go from one region to the other within the dungeon.
Also, my biggest problem is the quest in the end. It is fun once. But it is long and people kinda expect you to stay with them, specially if you are a tank or a heal. If you level as a dungeon spammer, you probably do that dungeon for the 3rd or 4th time now. Being forced to do that quest over and over again is annoying.

 **Gnomer**
I actually like it. Horde port is reasonably easy to access and dungeon flavour is definitely unique. Works perfectly as a 1-of in my opinion. Loots are also unique in flavor.
My biggest problem is the map.
It is super easy to get lost in that place, specially since dungeon map doesn't really show anything. You spend most of the dungeon as a point outside of the map!
tldr: Gnomer really needs a useable dungeon map.

 **SM**
SM had the problem of only providing loots to 1 specific playstyle per wing. F.ex. if we don't count rare encounters, GY was a caster wing - good luck finding interested melee or even tank. Same applies to different wings, except for Cath which was perfection from the beginning.
TWOW managed it fine with the dungeon update, I mostly like it.
One thing though: GY and Lib feel seriously harder than dungeons from the same level range. F.ex. RFK feels like easily below SM GY at level range based on difficulty. It's just weird.
One more thing: I know that SM is weirdly close to the undead starter zone but Alliance should have an easier way getting here. Right now it's a real pain for them to get to SM unless they camp here until they outlevel the wings.

 **Crescent Grove**
I mostly love it, really nice work.
Some problems, though.
At the second boss nothing indicates that you have to kill the saplings before fighting the boss. It is usually not a problem since someone in the team has experience with the dungeon but for a team of newbies that is a totally unfair wipe. The dungeon should indicate to you in a way that saplings have to die before you attack that boss. Maybe a speech bubble after killing the first boss.
My bigger problem is pathing. CG allows you to move really freely but it results in very weird paths, which, if followed, allows you to skip half of the dungeon. It is not how the dungeon was intended to be played. Also, every second DPS expects the tank to know this exact pathing 'not to waste their time' and throws a tantrum if this path is not followed to a tee (path is usually unknown by the DPS, they just want to follow a guy who knows it).
Disabling some weird pathing with added environmental blocks like rocks would be good in my opinion.

 **RFD**
I really like it.
But.
It has a similar quest problem as RFK does, maybe it's not so bad here. Doing the escort quest for the 5th time is annoying, though.
Not sure why but I really love the skeleton boss.
For the later part of the dungeon, that spiral can be a bit repetitive and boring. Either one more boss in the spiral or a smaller spiral would be nice. It doesn't really help that you can cheat through half of the spiral because it was unintended and honestly, should be patched out.

 **Uldaman**
It just feels really unrewarding loot-wise compared to its difficulty. People usually do it once only, even dungeon-spammers. I really think that with the emergence of Gilneas, Uldaman should have a loot upgrade. Same situation of Sunken Temple + Hateforge but about it, later.

 **Gilneas**
Extremely well done. I have nothing to criticize.

 **Maraudon**
Oh boy. In my opinion Orange is the most boring dungeon wing of the game. You really have to try hard to convince me to tank those plants again. Feels like a very long, very boring, repetitive mess, usually for bad loot (and for a quest item).
Purple is mostly fine. Princess, too, except for the goblin boss which is way out of reach. Imo he should be closer to the others.

 **ZF**
I like trolls. Maybe I'm biased but I really like this place.
It's a very weird critic but I have a slight problem with the mount usage here. Dungeon can be done from early-mid 40s and poorer chars without a mount yet can be ridiculed.
All in all, an excellent dungeon. Only thing I'd change is to move the mallet quest to Tanaris, maybe even inside ZF.

 **Sunken Temple**
Super Uldaman. Way too unrewarding, except for your class trinket and ES attunement. Way too long, way too complicated. Interesting design but should be at least 2 different dungeons. With the existence of Hateforge, the loots feel really unrewarding, too.
Honestly, I'd move it down in LFT a bit, f.ex. between 48 and 54. Level 60s are a total overkill in this dungeon (putting ES attunement here is a crime). Maybe downgrade 1-2 boss levels a bit to adjust to this range.
When I leveled a tank I always felt like the game was missing a dungeon to tank at late 40s, early 50s. ZF, Gilneas and Mara stopped giving real XP and tanking Hateforge at level 50-51 is a bad idea. Sunken Temple can be a solution for it if it is slightly tuned down. It would also help with its loots since now those items are for a slightly lower level range.

 **Hateforge**
Mostly super cool.
I hate though that you can get that purple trinket and that druid pre-Bis trinket from a 50-60 dungeon. The dungeon is a joke at level 60 and having max level, all purple casters all the time who hunt for the trinket makes the dungeon experience weird. The dungeon should be something like 50-56/50-57.
Really cool place though.

 **BRD**
The GOAT.
I 120% hate the Alliance Ony attunement quest though. I just want to tank my beloved place for the 20x time and someone in the team will surely be like 'yo man, when do we go to Windshor'? It's not hard, it's just repetitive and time-consuming for something which is always asked as a favor.
Counting Arena and Emp here, they are all part of this sweetie.

 **DM**
I honestly have close to 0 opinion. Was there sometimes but I haven't managed to understand the paths, I always get lost. I usually just avoid the place.

 **LBRS**
I think it's a bit overtuned for the level range. I'd argue that it is harder than f.ex. Scholo. Mobs being extremely close to each other, resulting in ässpull way too often, really potent debuffs, frequent hexes, really hard-hitters, holes in the ground which pull an army of scorpions if you are not careful... This is a very good dungeon but def not from level 55.

 **Scholo**
A friend of mine really loved it and we did it so many times that now I love it, too.
My only problem is Jandice. Having a boss in the middle of nothing without any important loot just feels weird. 80% of the runs just ignore her which is in my opinion a bad design.

 **Strat (both)**
Again, really good. My only problem is that making a full Strat run a bit annoying. Defeating the last boss of a wing should open up a port to the beginning of the dungeon to prevent senseless running back.

 **Kara Crypts**
The last part with the seals is a bit boring. Otherwise.. I mostly like it but this dungeon really expects the lead to know every part of the place. Skeletons appearing out of nowhere, stealthed enemies everywhere: you gotta know where to step exactly. Death coil is a bit nasty but grounding totem works wonders if you have a shaman. Warlocks and hunters are MVP against the stealthy opponents.
Underwater part is a bit annoying but nothing too bad.
My biggest problem is the lack of FP at Kara. I get it, the legendary staff ports you to Kara but it's for high raids. People wanting to do a late-game dungeon and collect blues also come here.

 **SWV**
In my opinion the most underrated dungeon. It's just strictly less rewarding than BM. Challenging, easy to pull several packs, requires thinking. Trap room is BS though.
I have two problems here.
Having a warlock in the team means way too much. With a warlock everything just gets way easier.
My other problem is the Horde access point. Just make us have a quest which opens up a port in Booty Bay, next to the Gnomer port. Going to Elwynn is both really time-consuming and causes a lot of unnecessary ganks just because the game forces you to run through level 10 WM players half of your journey.
+1: A personal opinion but this dungeon, which has a lot of really good plate tanking items should have 1-2 leather/mail tanking items, too + a very rare, purple BoP shield drop.

 **BM**
A very rewarding dungeon with a bunch of QoL, for example an own teleport.
But it's mostly a gear-check, nothing extra. Patrols are scarce and they are so big you won't miss them. LoS pull is almost meaningless here, you can just run in almost anytime.
The dungeon tests the healer's gear and the tank's gear and if both are fine, it is just too easy. Otherwise you get a lot of wipes, however not because you play bad, but because you fail at the gear check.
I think it is a much worse dungeon than SWV is but the QoL additions, the reputation, the added vendor, the better loot etc makes it much more popular.

 **UBRS**
I don't really consider it a dungeon. In my eyes it is the first raid which uniquely doesn't close you out after doing it. A bit underwhelming loot-wise after all these custom dungeons, though.

I think initial dungeons and leveling as a whole were supposed to explain basic teamworking mechanics. DM is about killing runners, SFK/WC is about decursing, interrupting. SM is about "one hand washes the other". Casters need melees in Library wing, Melees need casters in Armory wing. Even if they don't have loot there. This dungeon is about long term relationships between players.

I really enjoy LBRS and BRD for amount of alternative routes to various quests and skipping bosses. But in the same time I hate how long are they if you "completionist" and want to do it in single run.

I think debuffs should be strong and punishing. Because it is usually DPS job to decurse while tank and healer busy. So DPS not only spam single button, but have to think too.

I would just keep all dungeons as they are. If it works, then don't touch it.

Hey!

Really enjoyed reading your take on the dungeons—it's clear you’ve put a lot of thought into it, and I appreciate the mix of gameplay balance concerns, QoL issues, and thematic elements.

I completely agree with you on RFC and Stockades feeling a bit out of sync. The level disparity has always been a weird factional quirk rather than a balanced design choice. If both dungeons were adjusted to be either an early-game experience or a mid-game challenge, it would feel more natural, especially for dungeon spammers.

Deadmines is definitely a timeless classic, no argument there. And your critique of Wailing Caverns is spot-on—it’s a slog, especially when a group wipes at the final boss with no second chance. A checkpoint system or at least a way to reattempt the final boss would improve the experience without making it too easy.

I also like your point on SFK’s curse mechanics. It’s one of those things that feel more like an artificial difficulty spike rather than an interesting challenge. A tweak to the frequency or duration of curses would go a long way in making it less frustrating without removing the strategic element of having a decursing class.

BFD’s summon waves are definitely a pain, and I love your idea of an in-game clue like glowing levers. More subtle hints like that would make older dungeons more accessible without just handing players the solution outright.

RFK’s weird region transition glitch is such a funny detail but a totally valid complaint. It’s one of those little things that break immersion for no reason. And the expectation of repeating the long quest at the end is a real drag for dedicated dungeon runners.

Gnomer *really* needs a proper map. It’s one of those dungeons where even experienced players hesitate to navigate efficiently. The unique loot and theme are great, but the maze-like layout definitely turns a lot of people away.

SM’s loot bias is a good point. The old design of gear distribution by wing made sense for immersion, but it created a weird participation disparity between different roles. The dungeon update helped, but there are still some rough spots—especially with GY and Lib being noticeably harder than other dungeons in that range.

Crescent Grove’s pathing issue is an interesting one. While I love the idea of open-ended dungeon exploration, it does lead to a frustrating experience when players expect everyone to follow an optimized but obscure route. Your suggestion of minor environmental barriers to subtly guide players seems like a smart fix.

RFD’s escort quest being an optional but strongly expected activity is another great example of a mechanic that doesn’t age well in repeated runs. And the spiral, while thematically cool, does feel unnecessarily long, especially since a lot of groups just shortcut it anyway.

Uldaman’s loot vs. effort issue is a longstanding problem. With so many better options at that level range, it rarely feels worth doing more than once. A small loot upgrade could make it far more appealing without making it overpowered.

Maraudon’s Orange Wing *is* a tedious mess. I’m convinced that no one actually enjoys it unless they’re doing a full run for nostalgia or a quest. It’s just way too drawn out for what it offers.

ZF is a great dungeon, but your point about mounts creating an unnecessary social divide is interesting. It’s one of those small things that subtly affect player dynamics, especially in pug groups. Moving the mallet quest to Tanaris makes total sense—why *is* it still in the Hinterlands anyway?

Sunken Temple… yeah, it’s a commitment. Even players who like complex dungeons tend to dread it after one or two runs. Tuning it down and shifting its place in the level range could make it feel less like a mandatory slog.

Hateforge’s loot issue is a classic example of why max-level players farming lower dungeons can ruin the experience. A tighter level range restriction would help keep it feeling like a true mid-50s dungeon rather than a freebie for level 60s.
