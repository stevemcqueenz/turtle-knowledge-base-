## Post 5452 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2020-03-19T18:36:00Z

Hello and welcome :)
I'll answer some of the questions:

Yes, there's a cross-faction. World chat is united, also Horde and Alliance are able to raid together, if they've completed the "Become a Diplomat" quest.

> What's population like?

Right now 74 turtles online. We reach 100 at the evenings tho.

> What's included and what should I download for complete experience?

You don't have any mods right now, play with original client, there're plenty of server-side new content to explore! Read more about future patch testing: [viewtopic.php?f=2&t=948](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=948)

> Remind me the rates again please. Wasn't it 0.5?

No longer. Default rates are x1 now. You can choose to play in Hardmode and get mount, some vanity items and 200 Turtle Tokens when you reach level 60 as a reward.

> Saw awesome character skins specifically developed for Turtle WoW - like Orcs. How exactly do I get it?

You can buy it for Turtle Tokens. It's donation currency, but also you can earn it leveling in Hardmore.

> On one of the images, saw Human wearing Guard-like SW tabard - possible to get ingame?

Yes, Roleplay Dressing Box, 5 gold, sold in Stormwind.

> Is Survival profession any cool?

Leveling much faster :)

Join world chat to hear more of player's opinions in-game!
