# What's the point of War Mode?

## Post 149361 by Marskiforum — 2025-08-30T14:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149361#p149361 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I've been playing with War Mode enabled on Ambershire this past week and I don't really get it. What exactly is the developers' intention with it? Like what is the challenge it's supposed to provide?

I expected it to work like a permanent opt-in PvP tag for players who want to fight each other in the open world, so that PvP-minded players can fight each other without having to worry about killing a PvE-focused player. However thus far my experiences with PvP have been almost entirely players **without** War Mode creeping around me and only attacking me when I'm low health fighting mobs. And since they are in PvE mode themselves, I can't engage take any defensive actions until after they initiate on me, which means it doesn't really feel like a "war".

I don't care about the extra XP or rewards of the challenge, I care about the spirit of the challenge and how it's supposed to change my experience. Is the intended experience of War Mode having to avoid players who don't have PvP enabled? That seems counterintuitive to me.
