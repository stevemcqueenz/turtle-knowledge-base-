# An Open Apology to Everyone

## Post 43531 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-04-28T01:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43531#p43531 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi.  Long time, no see.  I'm here to apologize for a brief-but-explosive transgression I had last year.

In mid-2022, the game got an update that kind of broke things for a while.  Standard Blizzard-style stuff, you know?  During this time, I started to look at the game, its staff, and the community as a whole in a much different way.  I started getting frustrated, which led to anger.  Then, anger led to sarcasm, snarkiness, and just a general unpleasantness that no one appreciated.

Short version: I'm sorry for letting my anger boil over as often as it did, and even more so for directing it toward people who didn't deserve it.

Long version…  Sometimes, not knowing what's going on behind the scenes can get people jittery and anxious, among other things.  I've seen a lot of this with other programs and services, as well as Blizzard's own servers, at times.  Patience and understanding is crucial when dealing with things like this, and I was neither patient *nor* understanding.
I stand by some of what I said about staff transparency and the community being a little too forgiving, sometimes.  However, I do not stand by *how* I said it.  I let my temper get the better of me and I ended up changing from someone who enjoyed interacting with the community… into someone who was just here to harass and degrade people.  That's never acceptable.

I've been thinking about this since my last recorded post.  I tend to dwell on things like this.  In the end, I knew that everyone here deserved an apology.

To the staff
You chose a difficult path to stay as true to your goals as physically possible, but through it all, you've managed to do some really amazing things.  There's always going to be bumps along the way.  Maybe even a roadblock or two.  One of them shouldn't have to be dealing with someone who's being completely unreasonable.  I'm sorry for letting my impatience and anger get to me.

To the community
I think I understand, now, why you acted the way you did.  You were being forgiving and protective of the staff at every turn because I was doing the complete opposite; being relentlessly hostile and pointing out every single little tiny mistake the staff had made.  Critical feedback is one thing.  What *I* was doing was entirely unacceptable.  You made the right choice and I'm sorry for acting the way I did.

To everyone else
Uh… I'm not sure if I actually upset people who were just playing the game and being socially distant.  I don't recall being muted in-game – I just bit my tongue a lot and kept playing despite my openly hostile forum posts.  I'm sure I could have handled a few discussions on *Discord* differently, though.  So, there's definitely that.  Sorry, *Discord* moderators, for making your jobs harder.

Going forward, I will make an effort to check my temper at the door.  And, uh… that's all I can think to say.

Thank you for reading,
Jo Li
