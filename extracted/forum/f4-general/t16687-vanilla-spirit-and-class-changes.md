# Vanilla Spirit and Class Changes

## Post 114019 by Moongirl — 2024-12-23T14:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114019#p114019 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Greetings! I hope you are having a wonderful day!**

The Turtle WoW Team has done a large change for classes, as you all know. Some people are happy, some are complaining... it will always be there when it comes to changes. I do want to point out one thing when it comes to these changes though.

Here is a description found in the main page of the Turtle WoW Site about Specs:
 *"Every class and specialization has been shown some extra love, with new and reworked talents, abilities, and interactions allowing every playstyle to shine while staying true to the Vanilla WoW spirit of unique class identity."*

 **One thing I hope the Turtle WoW Developers know is that WoW as a whole can probably never be balanced.** The nature of WoW makes every little stat matter (if you Min-max), and there will always be something lacking compared to something else. Like I said prior, there will always be someone disliking the change, but I do hope the Team realizes that they can always do more Class changes, but it will never be 'perfect'.

Lots of people say how playstyles have vanished. Shock Paladin, Blizzard Mages... there is probably more I don't know of. While I understand how it might not fit the Team's vision, deleting them doesn't make them shine. On a side note, I never played any of those particular playstyles, so I cannot be one to say it should be removed or not. As a general player, however, I feel like things should be nerfed, not deleted.

I always have high hopes for what the Turtle WoW Team is doing. New quests and zones, transports and flight paths... Its all wonderful and they need to do more! While I know there are many complaints and challenges they face, I do trust that they will keep the Vanilla WoW spirit and continue to be great.

Thank you for reading, and good luck in your journey.   smiling_turtle_head
