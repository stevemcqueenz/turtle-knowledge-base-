## Post 132576 by zerume — 2025-05-22T22:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132576#p132576 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As new players, my friends and I have really enjoyed exploring the game, but we’ve found that the restrictions on paid services being tied to individual characters have created a lot of friction when it comes to supporting the game and its developers. A major way to alleviate this would be to allow transfers between characters. Even a cooldown on such a feature would go a long way toward giving us peace of mind—knowing that if we want to try a new class, react to class updates, or just shake things up, we won’t feel like we’re leaving behind the value of what we’ve already invested in.

Right now, committing to a single character feels awkward because we’re hesitant to invest real money knowing we might want to change later. Even making services like the auction house or talent swapping account-wide—while keeping cosmetic features character-specific—would be a big step forward. It would help us feel more confident in supporting the game and its devs long-term.
