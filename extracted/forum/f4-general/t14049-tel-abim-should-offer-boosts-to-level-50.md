# Tel'Abim should offer boosts to level 50

## Post 95707 by Crunkship — 2024-05-26T14:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95707#p95707 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The initial experience is a lot of PvE on a PvP server. If the goal is to get people who want to PvP doing so vs each other, a level boost can fix that. Here is a pros and cons list.

Pros
 -Consolidates population into two brackets, 50-59 and 60. I haven't seen a single BG pop while on Tel-Abim except for players who are level 60.
 -Promotes world PvP by allowing all new players to begin their quest/grind for gear. If new players want to get a level 50 boost and just start raiding MC, they have to enter PvP zones to do.

Cons
 -Potential economic issues
 -Possible population imbalance if every one rolls a night elf druid
