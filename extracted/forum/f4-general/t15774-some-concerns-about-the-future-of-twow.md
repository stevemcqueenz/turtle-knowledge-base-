# Some concerns about the future of Twow

## Post 107532 by Silhouette (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-11T15:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107532#p107532 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello Twowers,

Been playing Twow for several years now, I was there when the server still had a very low population of hundreds of players so I can somehow be considered an old inhabitant.

 1.17.2 Update has been a very big step within the Vanilla Plus scene since we are now reaching areas never reached before and I've been enjoying it so far as a PvE player, I'm definitely looking forward to see the upcoming new raid. I cannot really talk for the PvP playerbase but from what I've read on the forum more work seems to be necessary to balance the whole thing and make PvP more enjoyable for every classes.

Overall I don't really have many concerns regarding the PvE gamedesign direction, as a Vanilla enthusiast things seem to be evolving in a way I wanted WoW Vanilla to evolve so it's really hard to complain about that considering it's a private server without subscription, and subjectively a better experience than what Blizzard has been delivering.

So where are the concerns as stated within my title you might ask ? Well, recently the Shop of Turtle has been updated with new mounts, and several of them are in High Definition which kinda contrast with the whole Vanilla Azeroth being in Low Definition. **For now**, it's not killing the vibe since not many players have them, but the principle and the emerging vision of the Turtle Team may constitue a problem.

It is obvious that developping a Vanilla Plus version has bigger costs than copy pasting the usual 1.12.1 client, so it is absolutely understandable that money is needed and a Shop is a solution to that. Nevertheless, 1.17.2 also brought new Quality of Life items in the Shop, players can now buy portable anvil, portable ammo vendor, portable stablemaster, extra heartstone and much more.
The average player already has many opportunities to spend real money, and yet, countless mounts have been added, including HD ones and some representative of another era of WoW which doesn't reflect Vanilla at all.
Several problems to that:

 - Dozens of flying mounts, which doesn't make sense since Vanilla is not meant to support flying so it's just there for the look but also screaming TBC despite not being TBC. Also Invincible mount from WotLK Arthas has been implemented, which also screams WotLK without being WotlK. Yes it is Vanilla Plus but in the present case is it a Plus or a Minus from a player standpoint ?
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/pXt7HC5Z/Wo-W-SR06-CGf-Jfz.png)![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/cLCcythh/Wo-W-mxpb-ZAZ6-Mi.png)
 - Dozen of HD mounts that don't belong to the old Azeroth, the Old/New Textures are not meant to look good all together. While the optional HD Patch makes sense for those looking for a more modern WoW, though imposing HD elements within the Vanilla client is a totally different thing and should be considered very seriously by the Turtle Team. Bear in mind, it is only the beginning, TTeam could see that as an opportunity to backport an infinite amount of HD elements from Retail WoW only to make money whatever the visual cost potentially turning into a breakpoint for some of us.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/hvsJyM7r/Wo-W-POELSt-ZJt-X.png)
 - Lack of professionalism regarding pricing. Recently, we have had no informations regarding price changes and promotions, and yet they have been applied. I noticed Invincible mount going for 80 bucks one day then 150 bucks the other day... absolutely no official information inbetween.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/Yqnckg6y/image.png)![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/0QBmPVNy/Wo-W-7-Du-PE2j-KA7.png)

Where are we going ? While I do understand that this might be super lucrative for Twow, I am really unsure about the side effects. The Twow community should start rising some serious concerns instead of letting this go and extend.

That's not just a cosmetic mount, it's bigger than that.  neutral_turtle
