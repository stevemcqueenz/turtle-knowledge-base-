# Death Knight Class?

## Post 99101 by Khalthul — 2024-07-10T16:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99101#p99101 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm only writing this because I recently got my friend into Turtle WoW, and he had always mained a death knight as soon as his paladin reached the level needed to make that class in WotLK - effectively sacrificing his character for the oathbreaker Paladin rp character arc.

I absolutely love Turtle WoW's commitment to the lore, so it puzzles me to think of what solutions are available to fill this lost niche that is currently absent in his playthrough.

I've suggested he can play a High Elf Warrior with a Dark Ranger skin, and rp as a "Dark Knight" instead. He is a shameless edgelord with a love for darker aesthetics as well as melee-based gameplay - thus liking the rogue, paladin, and warrior classes so far as we have played together trying the different classes.

I even pitched that he play a Warlock next, but not having a melee component to it lost his interest (maybe a buffed Firestone with a couple melee abilities could have changed his mind?)

So my curiosity remains, is Death Knight a feasible class that could be implemented into Turtle WoW someday? Is there a lore justification for it? Would it harken back to the death knight abilities and aesthetic that we saw in Warcraft 2 at all?

I know if it were implemented, I wouldn't want every race being able to become that class (but that's just me). Maybe the necromancers and warlocks of their respective factions make this possible for the great warriors of each faction: primarily Humans and Dwarves, as well as Orcs and Tauren.

A cult could be responsible for summoning this extra muscle, with the justification to use the Scourge's magic against them. The curious gnomes and humans gather 'round with a crowd of Forsaken necromancers and Orcish warlocks - performing the necessary rites and sacrifices to make this magic possible.

But it goes awry, creating death knights that can hold a mix of three specializations. I never played Death Knight myself, but it would be cool to see elements of the undead shadowy Dark Ranger in one, the caster-centric power of Warcraft 2's Teron Gorefiend paying homage in another, and the more recent Northrendian cold steel of Arthas Menethil in the final spec.

Let me know if there's room for the lore for this sort of thing (or not), or if there are any other ways to roleplay as a death knight for my friend's sake.

That being said, I wouldn't be entirely against Paladin class quests that deactivate some spells and replaces them with Death Knight-y ones, inspired by the WC3 DK, not the WOTLK one.

For example:
 - Devotion aura could become unholy aura, which provides in-combat regeneration and increased movement speed.
 - Redemption could become Reanimation, a temporary mass combat res: "Brings back up to 5 nearby fallen party members that will continue the fight for up to 15 seconds".
These spells are taken from WC3, wherein the DK was already a counterpart to the Paladin. Divine Shield's counterpart is Death Pact, which steals health from a nearby friendly minion, which wouldn't translate well to WoW gameplay without adding a pet to the paladin, which doesnt work imo.

Death Knight to me feels like a class that should not be playable. It should be reserved for evil-aligned NPCs. I think also the story in WoTLK (and maybe TBC) isn't very canon - they did a lot of things just to justify gameplay decisions they've made.

It's more obvious with WoTLK where they make Death Knights "good guys" because they no longer want to listen to the Lich King. In my opinion, this is 100% bullsh*t.

Also making the Death Knight (or any other class) require you to have a level 55 character to create a Death Knight and then the Death Knight starting at level 55 is also 100% bullsh*t. It meant that the developers were catering to lazy players with short attention spans who wanted to play the new class, but were too lazy and impatient to level one from level 1.

Someone might say that there is a lore explanation why they start at level 55 because they are already strong or they are previous adventurers who already went on their adventures, but that's also bullsh*t.

All classes added into the game - Death Knight, Monk, Demon Hunter, that new dragon furry BS are all edgelord cringe, at that point the game was already cut off from its roots and the direction was already to make everything super awesome and epic, with gigantic ridiculous shoulder pads and whatnot. As opposed to Vanilla's simplistic direction - even the best gear in Vanilla always looked more tame and ordinary, not over-the-top and outrageous like everything that came after that.

So in short, Death Knights don't belong in Vanilla or Turtle WoW. If there should be a new class, it should be an entirely new class like Bard that combines Healer and Utility into one that was never added into the game before. Maybe something like Engineer that combines Hunter and Support/Utility into one instead. But Death Knight is too much of an edgelord cringe for Vanilla, it doesn't have a place in it.
