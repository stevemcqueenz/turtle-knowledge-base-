Curious what people think is the most underwhelming talent tree out of all classes.

I'll go first.

 **Druid Balance Tree.**

While Balance is improved from classic, the spec still remains about as memorable as a Wetlands mob and seems to revolve around reducing cast times with little other improvements.

 11 point talent is Omen of Clarity, a 30-minute buff (thanks for making it 30 minute).

 21 point talent tree is proc on crit to reduce next cast time by 0.5 seconds.

 31 point talent tree is Fat Chicken Form, that gives 3% spell crit to party members. Thank the dev team they at least added 20% reduction to balance spells cost, and made remove curse and innervate castable.

Other than that what else is there? Reduce Wrath cast (5 points), reduce starfire cast (5 points), increase spell crit damage (5 points, shamans get it for 1).

The spec is so hybrid ferals go 11 points for Omen of Clarity + hit and 10% damage boost, and some druid healer builds go 24 points for Nature's Grace and Moonglow.

## Post 101341 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-16T10:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101341#p101341 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hmm
Wanna give a long answer to this. I stand by my statement that flavorwise Demonology tree is the most diappointing one to me but utility-wise it's not a weak tree at all.
Will check each tree based on the below criteria:
 * Does it have any OP talent?
 * How often is it used by decent raiding/PVP/leveling builds?

Warrior
Arms: Used by a so-so PVP build and fury uses some of it. I'll ignore deep prot because it's not really good. Tactical mastery is useful and have some nice talents but the tree is lackluster.
Fury: One of the strongest trees, gives us the best raid melee DPS and the best raid tank. Out of the question.
Protection: Not very good but defiance and last stand makes the best raid tank build viable. Not the weakest tree.

Paladin
Holy: Illumination. It alone salvages the tree.
Prot: Gives us a very good leveling tank, a decent raid tank and arguably the best farmer in the game.
Retri: The tree itself is kinda underwhelming but used by a decent melee build/OP PVP build + blessing of kings is linked to the tree. Also, pala tanks go deeper into retri than they go into prot (0 21 30).

Hunter
BM: Makes the easiest leveling build possible, also good for PVP.
MM: Gives us a decent raiding build and trueshot aura is somewhat useful.
Survival: Used by many raiding hunters for some good utility talent but nothing groundbreaking. Tree has identity issues in my opinion and has the 'arms warrior' situation - some builds go inside it for some talents but that's all. The biggest reward, the +15% agility is way too deep down and forces you to take a bunch of irrelevant talents.

Rogue
Assasination: Kind of the same situation. Rogues usually go somewhat deep into the tree for some talents but they are not defined by this talent tree. All in all has a more consistent identity than survival and unlike survival, each raiding rogue goes inside it. Not the weakest tree.
Combat: Good tree, raiding rogues deeply rely on that.
Assasination: Totally useful at PVP.

Priest
Discipline: Usually the arms warrior treatment, priests going somewhat deep inside it for some cool talents. But also has some must haves like improved fortitude and really geared priests tend to go all in to the tree up to PI. Definitely stronger tree than survival or arms. Has some cool PVP gimmicks, too.
Holy: Excellent healing tree, widely used.
Shadow: PVP gods, also better at raiding in TWOW.

Shaman
Elemental: Very good at PVP and better at raiding than people tend to believe.
Enhancement: Weirdly it's not a candidate for the worst tree because of a healer build. BL resto is really good and can fully utilise the better totems and the BL.
Restoration: Resto is in a weird spot. Healer shamans, even deep resto shamans are extremely good but they are so good because of baseline abilities and because of tier set boni pushing chain heal really hard. The tree itself is mostly boring and kinda lackluster maybe besides nature's swiftness. But since deep resto is easily A tier at least and they use this tree heavily, it can't be a candidate here. Also enables a really OP ele PVP/sometimes raiding build (30 0 21).

Mage
Arcane: They have an interesting mana-recharge gimmick in TWOW but compared to the other mage trees they still underperform. But the tree has a gimmick and both of the other mage trees go somewhat deep into arcane, making it a better utility tree than arms or survival.
Frost: One of the best leveling and farming builds. Also PVP god.
Fire: Best raiding caster DPS.

Warlock
Affliction: Weirdly, it might be a candidate. The tree is somewhat used by SM-Ruin but their best gimmick (the imp stam buff) is overshadowed in tanking groups by a good shadow priest since the priest can actively recharge a bunch of life in TWOW. Warlock PVP builds usually don't go affliction too deep (Conflag, Soul Link, DS/Ruin). However it has improved corruption and improved life tap which are a must have for every warlock. This alone salvages the build.
Demonology: DS/Ruin for PVE, Soul Link for PVP. The tree is strong.
Destruction: Ruin is used for each raiding build, Conflag is a totally valid PVP build, destruction is arguably the strongest warlock tree.

Druid
Balance: Moonkin form salvages it. You need some moonkins for a raid for the aura. That alone makes the otherwise mostly garbage tree kinda good.
Feral: Bears are good. Really good.
Resto: Tree druids are really wanted as a 1-of at raids, the tree is not abandoned at all.

tldr:
I think it's either arms or survival hunter. But since arms also has a kinda valid PVP build and fury, which taps into the arms tree is an S tier build, I'd say survival hunter tree is the biggest joke atm.
