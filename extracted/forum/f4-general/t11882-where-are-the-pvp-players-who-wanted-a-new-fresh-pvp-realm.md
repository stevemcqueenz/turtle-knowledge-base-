> **Blackbook wrote: Wed Nov 29, 2023 9:37 pm**
> I just find it a bit sad this thing released half baked and didnt take the chance it had on release. The attention was there but that window is closed now.

What does 'half baked' mean to you?

As you stated, the attention (aka hype) was there and they capitalised on it. There was enormous pressure to get it delivered before the hype disappeared. Interestingly, there were plenty of concerns about the long term inevitable issue any given PvP realm suffers from which is that the PvE gear (i.e. AQ/Naxx) eventually outshines the PvP equivalent. As such, they stated in one of their initial posts that they intend to address this and add (or buff, perhaps) more stuff later on to address this issue. Apart from phasing the content, which they have done, this was the number one issue which they committed to addressing.

I think what's happened is that people set their expectations way too high. They assumed that the TWoW team could potentially create new PvP oriented custom content and either have it implemented on release or within a very short space of time. However, I'm very confident that the devs absolutely do not want to design content which is bespoke to the PvP server. What this means is that there is essentially one pipeline for development so any future designs will be applied to both server types. As such, any PvP specific changes will have to get in line with the rest of PvE oriented ones. Here are some examples from a recent interview with Akalix; 1) a new raid tier (post Naxx), 2) a new profession, 3) more zones such as a new goblin/high elf starting area. I'm sorry to say but this stuff was always going to take priority simply because its design started way before the PvP server was even announced. Other folks would also suggest that the devs might also be partial to PvE content because that has always been TWoW's bread and butter.

I'm willing to bet that almost every PvPer was salivating over the prospect of engaging with a Vanilla plus style server but with a PvP ruleset. Aside from a few game modes not being available, TWoW has delivered the requested PvP realm. They will enrich the PvP experience at some point down the line but anyone thinking they should've already implemented solutions prior to the server launch or even within the first six months is absolutely kidding themselves.
