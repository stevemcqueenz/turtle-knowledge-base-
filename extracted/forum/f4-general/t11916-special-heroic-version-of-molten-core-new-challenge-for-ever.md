# Special heroic version of Molten Core. New challenge for everyone.

## Post 78807 by Burunduk (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-11-26T17:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78807#p78807 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hard version of Molten Core for 40 people, where your Naxx gear means nothing, only skill matters.
You must have fire resist gear in EVERY slot not to get oneshot on boss/trash fight.
Vendor will sell starter gear (even weapons), but to get better pre raid resist gear you should do quests, run dungeons, craft , do normal MC and BWL (old content is on farm again).

As rewards from bosses you will get better fire resist gear, pets, situational items, fun meme items, toys, mount, transmog, title etc.(maybe 1 or 2 really god items)

A new activity that does not require a lot of work from developers. What do you think?
