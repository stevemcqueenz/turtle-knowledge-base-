Welcome!
While I do not like PvP that much, I think I can give you some info, at least from my point of view.
At the moment the clear meta is Paladins, they are extremely overbuffed in PvP with huge burst potential combined with the long stun and bubble.
Besides this, most other classes behave similarly to what you would expect in Vanilla, with a few exceptions.
Pet classes (Hunter and Warlock) have an advantage that pets tend to follow even stealth targets, so they can easily flush rogues and druids if they see them before going stealth or prowl.
On the other hand Warlocks' Succubus can not use Seduce. (This might have been patched since, definitely was a problem for a long time, it always broke instantly.)
