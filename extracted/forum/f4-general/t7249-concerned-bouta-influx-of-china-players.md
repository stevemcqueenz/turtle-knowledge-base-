I have been entering the Chinese community for a while now. The language barrier actually hinders many players who don't care much about maintaining the game environment. They usually watch live streams to find a new server, enter the community of the streamer, and are assisted by the streamer to translate the game and package addons which they call "one-click gaming". With this, they can play the game, purchase gold with RMB, purchase items to kill all enemies, complete all RAIDs, and then sell the gold they obtain through GDKP to exchange for RMB. The demand for gold has brought many farming robots to this server.

In the past few months, the lack of Chinese translation made this server unattractive. However, someone betrayed a technical enthusiast, which caused the comprehensive translation patch that the enthusiast tested in a small circle to be leaked to the outside. Many streamers have been using this server's existing translation as a selling point to attract viewers into their groups, then selling gold and conducting account transactions secretly. I have obtained some evidence. After ensuring that the behavior is implemented, I will try to contact the GM of turtle WoW.

Of course, I have to say that the majority of players follow the rules of turtle WoW. They actively oppose all forms of RMT and gold farming studios, and freely share gameplay strategies without compensation. However, even a small number of people can cause significant damage and affect innocent people. Because Chinese players must use VPNs to play turtle WoW and other overseas games, many players purchase the same VPN make different people to use the same IP address. I have a feeling that the GM will encounter a lot of complaints and it will be difficult to banned the correct account based on IP address, which will be a big challenge.

I really, really hope that Turtle's GMs do something about this issue. For clarification, it is *not* a racism thing. I've played with Chinese players who can speak English and respect the rules and economy and had a blast.

Most Chinese players simply have a different MMORPG culture that's often incompatible with the playstyles and rulesets of most of the people who play on non-Chinese MMORPGs and non-Chinese servers, often breaking rules that they get away with due to the language barrier making it hard to moderate/investigate, and making it harder for them to understand what rules are being broken in the first place
A lot of things that are considered taboo in Western MMO communities are considered completely normal in Chinese MMO communities and most Chinese players keep to themselves under the belief that it won't effect other people. The reality is that a lot of them are still destructive to the economy via GDKP runs, botting and gold-selling being normalized and they divide the server even more than it already is due to the language barrier since most Chinese players don't bother to speak with, or interact with English-speaking players.

It's also the sheer influx of Chinese players that creates a major problem for the health of a server; If I recall correctly, WoW Classic is shut down in China which caused a huge exodus of Chinese players into the private server community, where the majority of servers are primarily hosted in Western countries. Even as a lot of Chinese players aren't really breaking any rules, they often come in the thousands, as seen by Everlook. Turtle's population is already stressed as-is with resource nodes and low level monsters. Imagine the population doubling but that doubling consists of players who won't speak with you or even group with you and given the limitations of the client speak in a language that won't even show up in your chat (blank spaces).
