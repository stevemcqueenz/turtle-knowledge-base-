## Post 166838 by Koilos33 — 2026-01-29T11:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166838#p166838 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Yeah, you need to kill the Beast, and once someone skins it, a gnome in diving gear (Finkle Einhorn) spawns where the corpse was. Then you just need to talk to him and get the quest.
Keep in mind that to skin The Beast, the skinner needs either the Zulian Slicer, Finkle's Skinner, or the Shadowforge Skinner, which give +10 skinning skill.

Zulian Slicer: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19901>
Finkle's Skinner: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=12709>
Shadowforge Skinner: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60896>
