Thanks, that GM reply says it is fine as long as one HC character remains in the city while another HC character is abroad. And it does not matter which is which (I can alternate), as long as this rule is always respected.

Also trading is fine between them given the normal HC rules of level diff, etc.

Just don't run both characters abroad at the same time, even to 2 different non-city zones.

If I was already a broad on one HC character and can't hearthstone/port back to a city, I would log out that character before even logging in the other one that is also abroad, just in case.
