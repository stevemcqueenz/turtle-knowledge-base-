Simply want to say I love TWoW's design of this dungeon. People who say it is boring, I don't know what you expected based on the stockades 2.0 layout that has been around since alpha. This feels about as close to 10/10 rendition as we could have hoped for, all things considered.

From the sound design to items and enemy design and unique final boss, the devs who worked on this really understand what Vanilla+ means. Using part of the Stormwind melody in the music was genius as well. I really hope these devs will continue to be involved in future content.
