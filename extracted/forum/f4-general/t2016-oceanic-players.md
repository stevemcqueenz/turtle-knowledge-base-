> **Gengar wrote: Wed Jul 27, 2022 5:37 am**
> Fellow Aussie here, about to start playing turtle wow because i love classic so much. Was just wondering if there is many aussies and how they go with latency issues/raid times ? Thanks :)

There's a bunch of us around! Probably more than enough for a raid team now. But we're all scattered across all the different guilds.

Most days I get around ~330ms. Back in Vanilla I would get ~600ms on a good day. And most private servers are ~300ms so this is fine and usual for me. Maybe not for someone who has always played on Classic and more recent Blizzard servers since they actually started hosting them in Aust. ![🤷‍♀️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f937-200d-2640-fe0f.svg)

I raid with <The Blackwing Guard>. Our main raid times are at 8am Sun & Mon morning, AEST.

Ony / ZG / AQ20 are randomly through the week and usually a bit later. Usually starting somewhere between 10 and 12am. But I've seen them start as late as 2pm.

BGs tend to start popping each night somewhere between 6pm and 8pm. So we can get a little PvP action in most nights too.

It's not ideal, but it's workable.
