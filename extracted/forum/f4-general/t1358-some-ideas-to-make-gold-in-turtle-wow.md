# Some ideas to make gold in Turtle-WoW

## Post 8283 by Probichaudb — 2020-10-27T13:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8283#p8283 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This server is unique, so is its economy. Here are some ideas to make gold while leveling and to help out raiders at the same time. The point of this post isn't to cover everything (like the weekly orphan quest, the r3 ranking for a discount for mount, etc.), but it's to point out ressouces that will be used in massive quantity by raiders with Ahn Qiraj opening soon. It proposes a win-win scenario.

[37+ grind] Rock Elementals in Badlands, those near Kargath or the bigger ones south. Vendor the gray rocks in Kargath or at NPC middle of Badlands. Put Solid Stone (used for Goblin Sapper Charge) & Elemental Earth on AH (used for nature resist pot)

[Fishing] Try to catch Firefin Snappers, those will be used by fire mages. Also Stonescale Eels are always needed by tanks. If you happen to fish Rumsey Rum Black Label instead, it will also sell in AH because every raider need booze!

[Herbalism] “Which profession do I take?” Pick herbs! As early as at 115-120 skills, you can get Wild Steelbloom (fortitude, invis pot) & Grave Moss (shadow prot pot). Both sell well! Then you'll find Wintersbite, Firebloom, Blindweed & Ghost mushrooms that are all needed by 60s. And obviously the higher-level herbs will sell well too (sage, groms, dreamfoil, plague, icecap).

[+52 dungs] The enchanter in the group should roll last on blue BoPs +52. If nobody hit "Need", the enchanter should need simply to DE, so instead of vendoring an item for a ~2g, you get a shard that sells ~10g. Be careful not to need if someone else does: in doubt, hit "Greed". Distribute the shards equally at the end of the run or make people roll.

Feel free to share other ideas. I've been recommanded to post it on the forums, so here it is.

See you in game,

Camil

You're missing the idea.

A blue, 52+ (ie large brilliant shard) drops. the 4 non-enchanters roll as per usual. If nobody rolls need, the enchanter rolls need to guarantee that they win the item. Then they disenchant it and the whole party /rolls and the highest roller wins the shard.

Since these shards are more valuable than vendoring the item from a greed roll, this is a win-win (presuming your enchanter is trustworthy, of course!)

OP's suggestions on making gold are all good. I'd also recommend farming chests since they have fixed spawn points, guarantee a green item and if it's a weapon it's likely to vendor for a reasonable bit of cash. You can run chests in a few high end areas and make some serious cash.

Best long term consistent good farm I've found when needing gold is the following:

Around level 28 head to north west stv and start at the first alliance camp you find...

Head down the path and hit the first 2 young Panthers and loot the bristley whisker then turn left and hit the first tiger which starts the lap of the gold farm. Then hit next panther then 3 Panthers in a line from right to left. Then hit the Panthers on the left heading down past the edge of kurzon compound. You then hit the tiger which allows you to turn back and start hitting all the Panthers on the way back north on your left. This leads you back up the hills finally hitting the last tiger getting you back to the first 2 Panthers then the camp. This will allow you to vend all the trash and the bristley whiskers vend for 7 silver each and stack in tens.

This farm is zero hassle, very good efficiency and optimised by using high dps weapon and all of your classes abilities properly.

You can do 1 lap in about 6mins, get about 1gold each lap plus any blues or greens that drop. With empty bags you can vend after at least 10 laps and this farm works well for lvl 28ish all the way to level 50.

It stays optimal by upgrading your weapon every 5-10 levels to keep the dps high.

When you need gold quick this is a half hour session well spent with no fuss and no waiting on AH sales.
