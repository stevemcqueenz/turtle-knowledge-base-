# New race/class combos ranked

## Post 127812 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-21T12:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127812#p127812 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I wanna give my take on how probably race/class additions are using a ranking system.
Will take into consideration how many playable classes the races have at the moment. So I'll consider adding something to a tauren more probable than adding something to humans and trolls.

 **Never Gonna Happen tier**
Faction-exclusive classes at the other faction.
Human Shaman, Night Elf Shaman, High Elf Shaman, Dwarven Shaman, Gnome Shaman
Orc Paladin, Undead Paladin, Troll Paladin, Goblin Paladin, Tauren Paladin

 **Very Unlikely Tier**
Human Druid -> This way we had a race with every possible classes except for shaman.
Troll Druid -> Same reasoning but with paladin.
Gnome Druid, Paladin -> Feels very off based on lore.
Undead Druid, Shaman -> Same reasoning.
Tauren Warlock -> Feels extremely off.
Goblin Druid, Priest -> Same.
Night Elf Warlock -> I don't see it.

 **I Doubt It Tier**
Dwarven Druid -> I like the flavour of it I but doubt it.
Night Elf Paladin -> From one hand, we have other elven paladins but for the night elves this would be too much of a stretch.
High Elf Druid -> Very similar reasoning as the last one, just reversed.
Orc Druid -> Not really.
Orc Priest -> I prefer orcs relying on shamanistic magic to heal. Introducing holy magic to the orcs would feel really weird.
Tauren Mage -> I don't see them being this structured in their magic usage.
Tauren Rogue -> Not as a meme and not only because of Stonetalon Mountains. Saying that a race has no outlaws is a stretch and it would be extremely fun to play. From the other hand, a stealthy tauren just sounds wrong.

 **Seems Plausible Tier**
Dwarven Warlock - This would seem like a totally natural addition. Tradition of using magic? Yes. Tradition with shadow magic? Yes (priests).
Night Elf Mage -> I totally see it, specially arcane mages.
Gnome Priest -> There is a current debate about this on the forum. I see the other side's arguments but I think a scientific approach towards the otherwordly would work here as a flavor.
High Elf Warlock -> I know, we don't talk about Blood Elves, but still, quoting the character creation site, they are looking for a cure for their addiction. Desperation can bring otherworldly means and humans/gnomes can teach them the way.
Tauren Priest -> Yes. Praise the Sun!
Goblin Shaman -> Hard to explain, but they seems to be a perfect fit. Maybe because of their chaotic nature. A tribe of mad goblins researching shamanism? Plausible. Would also be super fun to play.
