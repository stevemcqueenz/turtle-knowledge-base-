A minute of your time please::

 **Suggestion on LFM for Dungeons, Quests, Events, PvP- or any other Group content:**

Introduction:

 *Playing on private server with low population can be rough. When Looking For a Group or More members takes a whole day, and in the end you can’t find anyone… That’s just killing a mood and makes you stop your adventure.*

>Use world to LFG/LFM or Discord looking-for-group

 -Sometimes [/world] is overflooded  with spam

 -Not every player knows about it/ wants to join

 -Discord and in-game chat needs to be monitored constantly

>So what?

+Simple calendar schedule for any group activity.

+Here, this topic on turtle-forum, no need of extra websites or resources.

+Anyone can post an announcement with a description of an event with the date and time (server time : its GMT +1)

>How so?

 *example:*

Contact player:   <Player_Name>
Activity:   <Dungeon_run>/ <Battleground>/<RP-Event>/Other
Looking for:   (X)Tank + (Y)Heals + (Z)DpS___ {Lvl 01-60}
Date/Time:   01-October-2020___18:00_Server-Time
Description:   *optional*
Additional Requirements:   *optional*

>What’s next?

Just [/whisper to contact player] in game, at the day of the event, like:

“Heyya! Still need a healer, lvl 40, for your SM run?”

 *->get invited*

 *-> get epics*

Or get declined. But that’s just a message, a minute of your time.

_________________________________________________
Make a post and spread the word, if you like this idea.

Ignore this if you don’t like it
_________________________________________________

This topic is created to post LFM announcements.
Please don’t spam and DON’T ANSWER on requests in this topic
>just /whisper to in game Contact-character at the day of the event for an invite!
