The majority of the Dalaran quests are in Nethergarde Keep. There are a few quests in Azshara from Magus Bromley, and the Alliance quest chain for Karazhan Crypts with Magus Ariden Dusktower also gives Dalaran rep.

Outside of that, quests were implemented to grind reputation with Dalaran. However, they currently seem to be bugged and aren't repeatable yet. Those quests are found at Dalaran in Alterac with Magus Palon and Archmage Talyvanah who can be found standing next to Archmage Ansirem Runeweaver.
