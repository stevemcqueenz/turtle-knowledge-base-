> on BOEs always roll NEED whether they are blue or epic.

If you agree on this ahead of time with the whole group - good solution!
If you think this is how it's done by default - hell no!

We've had this discussion in world chat some weeks ago and "always roll Need, because I need the money" turned out to be in a tiny tiny minority. The vast majority of players expects "Need" to mean "I will wear this item". "Greed" is exactly for "I don't care about the item itself, I just want the value it represents" and why its button on the roll interface shows coins.

There is something to be said for extending the roll timer just in general. It's hard to first sort out whether you could use that item, then start communicating with your group about an often complicated situation (like off-spec utility) and finally reach a conclusion that everyone is happy with all within a few seconds while the bar is mercilessly ticking down. Something like a "timeout" once per player per instance so you could talk about it for 2 min would be nice. I've seen good BoP loot go to waste because not everyone could chime in before the timer ended. Ultra-valuable BoE loot is another such case - the ideal solution would obviously be to sell it and split the money. It's absurd that 1 person in a ZF group should walk away with 600g because they won a 1 in 5 roll, when everyone getting 120g would maximize total utility and happiness.
