## Post 38677 by Rpgbouncer2 — 2023-03-23T04:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38677#p38677 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A lot of people credit Legion as the last good expansion until Dragonflight, but I really hated it on so many levels.
Pros
 - Suramar was a cool zone
 - Random legendaries giving unique buffs to classes was kinda neat
 - Some of the raid fights were interesting
 - The Mage Tower was actually hyper dope
Cons
 - Locking characters into a single weapon for the entire expansion that you level with talents and relics felt like shit to me, getting a new relic was much less exciting than potentially getting a build defining weapon drop
 - Introduced artifact power, a miserable grind
 - Kept the awful garrison missions, class halls were not enough of an improvement
 - PvP was a straight mess
 - The legendaries that i mentioned in pros were so miserably broken early on that it killed all fun when you got the garbage ones
 - Shoe-horned classes into certain playstyles with the way the artifact weapons were designed
 - Demon Hunters feel like a fanfiction class, i know they've been a part of the lore as long as Illidan has, but they're so anime they feel out of place
 - Had a lot of reactionary decisions based off the poor reception of WoD, removing even good ideas the expansion had
 - Everything else

In short, a lot of people liked the artifact weapons, but I felt like it further pushed our characters into the main character role and lessened the value of these legendary artifacts. Every paladin running around with Ashbringer just took me out of the game.
