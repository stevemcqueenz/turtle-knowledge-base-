## Post 35850 by Nightowl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-23T18:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35850#p35850 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It doesn't. The main draw of these increases is that it improves the quality of life. Without these bonuses you will be stuck in those awkward periods where you're forced to mine several stacks of ores that have a low chance of ranking up your skill. With these bonuses you can bypass that period and jump straight into higher level ore farming, effectively saving you time and sanity.
