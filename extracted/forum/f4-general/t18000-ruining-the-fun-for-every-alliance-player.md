Implying the Shaman is Enhancement, and was especially feeling confident or figured that reckless aggression was the play to go with a 2H, then mishandling one is much like not controlling a Warrior - - you are likely to get a nuke to the face.

Since Cheap Shot does not have Diminishing Returns over other Stuns like Kidney Shot and so on - - and depending on whether Pounce also doesn't (not sure I recall if so in Vanilla or here in Turtle) - - then you can do Cheap Shot into Cheap Shot into Pounce leading to Kidney Shot into Kidney Shot into Bash; if Pounce DRs then you do CS > CS > KS > KS > Bash. You will practically overload with Stuns and you may likely not even manage to fully execute this, because, realistically, the Shaman will already be dead at some point.

Implying no Steel Weapon Chain or other effect is active, you can have him Disarmed; a strong 2H with Windfury is the main source of people getting destroyed, so if you somehow manage to prevent it, you are delaying the nuke's explosion in your face.

The other potential killer... is yourself - - to Lightning Shield. Assuming enough Mana and time to just keep reapplying it and surviving the onslaught of hits, the Shaman can kill you simply by having you do his job for him. Without ways to mitigate this guaranteed Nature Damage (further increased by Debuffs such as Stormstrike, if I'm not mistaken), you need to make sure to prevent the Shaman from reapplying LS after having depleted it, preventing further suicide.

Totems are easy if you know and have the ways of AoE that still do Damage to Totems (because there are exceptions that do this), otherwise you will simply have to quickly retarget to what you need destroyed immediately, and hit it; a Cat Feral does Auto Attacks rapidly, so you might not even need to do anything other than hitting it with very little delay, but if you're far and getting to it would be a longer delay, you can just use a downranked Moonfire cast to preserve Mana and because the Totems themselves are fragile enough for that. As for the Rogues, it depends on the Speed of their Weapons and what they have available, plus they typically want to stay and lock down or distract the Shaman, as a Druid is more suited for handling Totems by this way of logic.

As for casts, provided you know not to be faked out, Kicks should be more than enough - - one alone ought to do it. However, if for whatever reason  that is not possible at all, any other CC just to fail it and force a reattempt, or even ranged Damage to deal Pushback and delay the cast, can be enough.

And if it's an Elemental Shaman... just don't let him hardcast.

Of course, all this theory makes it sound ideal and even easy (somewhat is), whereas in practice, variables change, people may be inexperienced in gameplay, they might panic, perhaps they do not have good teamwork, and so on... but, still...

On all accounts, this is a skill issue, a severe gear difference, or both - - that Shaman should _not_ win, as he was clearly mishandled, and that allowed him to show you why gambling in the shape and form of a WoW Class can actually sometimes win.

It's true that PvP here is fundamentally more imbalanced even for Vanilla's standards - - the mix of CC2 with custom Items and some changes to pre-existing ones has produced... weird results; clearly most if not all of this was aimed at PvE, specifically at endgame such as Raids, because PvP got hurt following many of these changes. I'm personally not a fan of a bunch of things.

None of that, however, excuses a bad misplay.
