Damn, I still remember the 2023 Christmas event chasing Dated all over the damn map.

Criminal Organization? My balls in hot sauce. This team has proven to have more love for the game than its parent company. Fuck BlizzTurd, Metzen, and all the other morons who lost their way on what World of Warcraft should be.
