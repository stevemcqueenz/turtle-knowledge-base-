## Post 107426 by Ataika (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-10T18:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107426#p107426 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

People do not want to play Av outside of its day.
I find several reasons for this:
 - huge amount of npc - every corner has million npc, especially alliance one
 - huge npc damage - general npc hit for 500-600 vs cloth
 - fast respawn timer - npc do respawn very fast - that means if you did not rush any obect the game turns into npc endless fest, people get tired fighting npc and start afking
 - archers respawn and drop agro - the elite version of archers respawn when bunker is capped and drop combat which means you can not dot them and hide as usual

Everything listed above makes players less willingly to participate offensive events unless its some easy street (no enemy defending).

Do you like current NPC state of AV or maybe you have any sugegstions how to fix this cringefest ?

In additional - the biggest problem is - administration of the project looks at this battleground as "players que it to fight npc in massive battles" what is far from reality.
People que this battleground to have a good mass fight and object play. Army of npc interrupting process does not make it a better experience. I can bet my account the "i like tons of npc" guys who usually answer in such topics are not playing this bagttleground often.
