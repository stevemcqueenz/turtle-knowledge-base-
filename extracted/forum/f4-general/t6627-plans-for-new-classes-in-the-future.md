## Post 41761 by Bellybutton (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-16T21:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41761#p41761 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Adding a new class would be a monumental task compared to adding a new race. I'm not against it but I don't see it happening any time soon.

They'd have to work out
 -creating entire talent trees as well as abilities for every two levels
 -what role(s) it's going to perform in PvE
 -will it make other class(es) obsolete in the same role? Will it be considered too gimmicky?
 -can it provide anything unique or new that isn't just retreading what previous classes do with a different skin
 -how does it perform in PvP content against other classes?
 -what armor and weapons does it use? what class armor sets and tier sets need to be created?
 -class quests
 -the numerous synergies and interactions with all sorts of buffs, debuffs and effects
And that's mostly the tip of the iceberg.
