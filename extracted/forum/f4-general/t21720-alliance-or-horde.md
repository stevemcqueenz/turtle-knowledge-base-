I've only joined the server about a month ago, and leveled a character to 33, and when it comes to new Turtle WoW content, there seems to be plenty of new horde content, just as much as alliance.

However, for vanilla itself clearly the Alliance stories were more fleshed out. There are more Alliance early level zones for example. But it's sort of a 60% Alliance/40% Horde sort of thing, you'll have slightly less content with the Horde early game, but not by much, and with the added Turtle WoW content it's probably closer to 55/45.

I chose Horde because I generally played alliance on the Blizzard servers, so I wanted to experience the Horde storylines. Also note the server is cross faction so the choice of faction isn't as important because you'll still be able to team up with the other faction for quests/dungeons/raids, but you might have slightly different questlines, particularly at the earlier levels.

At the later levels, more and more questing zones, and even questing lines, are shared between alliance and horde.

When picking a faction, if you'd played one side significantly in the past on vanilla/classic servers, play the other! As it's cross faction here it doesn't matter much at 60, so might as well have a new experience.

Admittedly on the fresh server PvE server Ambershire there are slightly more alliance than horde (about 60/40) so you may find it slightly easier to find people for group quests on alliance. But it's a not a big difference, and the dungeon finder is cross faction so there's no difference there. And you will have slightly more quests on the alliance side. But you'll find there are so many quests/zones that it's actually quite hard to do them all before 60, so even with the horde you'll have too many quests to do, so don't worry about running out of quests and needing to grind with either faction.

If you're completely new one could argue that the Alliance has a more filled out lore, but really just pick what feels right, and if you can't decide, just level one character from each! As it's cross faction you can mail/trade between your characters even if one is alliance and one is horde so it's a win-win!
