> **Krotux wrote: Wed Sep 06, 2023 4:48 am**
> Is there any "cash shop" or pay to win aspects in this game? Such as boosts, items, etc.

The cash shop contains appearance change, guild name change, character name change, cosmetic character skins, cosmetic mounts, cosmetic pets, cosmetic Druid / Shaman form glyphs, cosmetic tabards, cosmetic illusion items that transform you into a furbolg or a succubus or the like, and a few things some players would call "pay to win".

Realistically, they're convenience products that don't "win the game" for you but will spare you the frequency with which you have to run back to towns. These are a portable mailbox, a portable bank, a portable auctioneer, a dual spec item (which you can also buy in-game with gold), a 28 slot bag, a 36 slot bag, and perhaps more dubious, a repair bot that acts like the Engineer repair bot, but doesn't fall apart after 10 minutes.

> **Krotux wrote: Wed Sep 06, 2023 4:48 am**
> When is the European server going to be released?

This is the European server.  Two Chinese / SEA realms will be released within the week based on information provided by Staff.

> Is there a place where I can see talent tree and other class changes?

<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/>
