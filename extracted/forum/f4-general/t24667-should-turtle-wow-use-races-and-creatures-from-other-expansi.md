## Post 173701 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-04-02T03:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173701#p173701 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Just make use of what we already have WC3 in my opinion.

Despite some newer designs of newer races that look pretty cool, they are part of the retcon expansions - each of them, vrykuls, frost dwarves, wolvars, taunkas and non-humanoid creatures like protodragons, have a backstory expanded from the retcon expansion. Taking vrykuls for example, if they are introduced to the Turtle universe, should we take the lore behind them that they are a prototype of human in classic world? If the protodragons are implemented, would their existence make people doubt the significance of current "five aspects of five dragon flights"? In my view, such element salvaging is a kind of over-exploitation, which would shorten the lifespan of the game and bring Turtle into the trajectory of Blizzard works.

Afterall, I only welcome completely new elements, rather than what Blizzard added in later expansions, to be implemented in new Turtle updates and we should have the sense of mystery kept for a long period to maintain the space for lore possibility and boost immersion.
