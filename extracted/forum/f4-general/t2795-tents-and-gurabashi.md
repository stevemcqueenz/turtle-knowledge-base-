## Post 17465 by Fornow (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-03-09T09:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17465#p17465 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The main reason tents are placed at the Gurubashi arena is the convenient teleport from the faction capital to the arena. With tents stacking it's most efficient to have as many in one spot as possible. There's even a bot that will announce how many tents are currently at the arena when queried.

Having your Hearthstone set to the capital is already a good move anyways and most players seem to use the tents while AFK. In other words you can set your Hearthstone on either continent and then when you would normally exit the game, port to the capital, speak to a fight promoter and park your char under a tent. When you're back you just use your Hearthstone again.

What would be an alternate neutral location in Kalimdor by the way? Shimmering flats comes to mind...

I like the idea of tents in the abstract but the way it's currently used feels overly "gamey" and survival as a whole doesn't have so much to offer. I wouldn't be opposed to tents being change in a way that makes you want to use them in the actual wilds, for instance by changing their effect to give you leveling bonuses for the next 30 minutes or so. That way you would be incentivized to use them out and about.
