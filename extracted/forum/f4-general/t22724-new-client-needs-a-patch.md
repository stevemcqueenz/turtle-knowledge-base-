## Post 158592 by Mosttrustworthy — 2025-11-06T11:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158592#p158592 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

When the Patcher gets stuck, open Task Manager, find the Rundll32.exe*32 process and end it. As long as that process is running, all the files the Patcher will try to patch will count as "being used by another process" and will refuse to be overridden.
