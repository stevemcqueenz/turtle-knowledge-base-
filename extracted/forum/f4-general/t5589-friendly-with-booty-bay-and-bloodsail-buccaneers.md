Yes, it is possible to be liked by both.

If you do the quest [Red Flag over the Sea](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40172), you will become Neutral with the Bloodsail Pirates without becoming kill on sight with the goblins.  Doing quests for the Bloodsail Pirates with lower your Steamwheedle Cartel factions, but shouldn't do so to the point where they want to kill you for it, especially if you've done quests for them before.

However, be careful: if you do the quest [Making a Statement](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40180), you **will** become hostile with them (and it's going to happen in the middle of Booty Bay, right next to two high level NPCs and a bunch of guards).  It's advised you don't do that quest until you're immortal.  Not doing this will lock you out of some Bloodsail stuff, but there's still Bloodsail stuff you can do and not be hated by the Steamwheedle Cartel.

I did the Bloodsail stuff on a hardcore character (minus Making a Statement) and was absolutely fine.
