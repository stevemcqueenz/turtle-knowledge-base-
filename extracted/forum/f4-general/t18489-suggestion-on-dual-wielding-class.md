Hello everyone.
Been a long time wow player for a good while now, all the way back to TBC, but never cared too much about optimization or viability beyond not being a nuissance for other players I dungeon/raided with, nor I have much knowledge of balance in general, just did what I enjoyed or though would be fun.
Recently I've been meaning to make an alt to differ from my main (a Mage) and though of going for a classic fantasy I love, which is dual wielding weapons. My issue here is that I'm a bit nitpicky with that stuff and wanted to ask for some advice regarding 2 of the classes that can profit from it:

 -How viable is a Rogue that does NOT use daggers in any way, usually for the combat spec? (daggers are the gnomes of swords)
 -How viable is Hunter using two swords or other 1H weapons, I imagine in survival spec?

In this context, I'm using "viable" as in "I can level up comfortably and do dungeons / Light raiding without feeling like a drag to the team", not being top tier DPS in any sense.

Thanks for the advice. God I'd would KILL for Shamand and Paladins with DW but I totally understand why they can't in tWoW in balance and loot sense, I'm not trying to start a discussion in that regard.

## Post 126183 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-09T15:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126183#p126183 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sword spec combat rogue is pretty on par with dagger rogue in terms of raid performance. Both do very respectable DPS but don't bring much besides that.

Survival is middling in damage but not extremely far behind MM. The issue with this is they also kinda don't bring much utility and throwing out a tranq shot is harder because of having to walk out of deadzone. A lot of their damage comes from fire traps and as we all know early raiding is full of fire resistances/immunities. All of what I'm speaking on is in regards to 2h survival hunters, though, and while there was a recent change to make dual wielding more viable it's probably gonna be slightly behind. Viable? Yes. Better? No. There's also the issue of levelling as a melee hunter. This isn't all too effective until you can afford to put points deep into the playstyle and you have mongoose bite. It'll be a struggle early if you want to start as melee, but after the first 20 levels or so it' should be REALLY fun.
