# Deepwood Pipe - how exactly does it work?

## Post 92620 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-04-12T21:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92620#p92620 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have seen there is not much said about Deepwood Pipe custom trinket that was added in patch 1.17.0 so I decided to share with you how exactly it works - the description doesn't say everything.

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1205456103757054004/1228450208015319120/image.png?ex=662c1669&is=6619a169&hm=b6bb937c4a102e6a892fab21b8366705304f8560bdac8a16d561800b53d603dd&)

The trinket can be obtained by turning in 150x Bright Dream Shards to the NPC in Nordanaar in Hyjal. The shards can be bought in AH so the estimated cost of the trinket could be 400-500g. You can check more details about it in Turtle DB.

What exactly does it do?

Upon use:
- instantly stuns all nearby enemies for 3 seconds (radius 8 yards I think?)

- instantly makes you invisible for next 30 seconds and fears you (you go in random directions) for 3 seconds - you are still invisible when you are feared

- after 3 seconds, you can control your character again and you are invisible for next 27 seconds
Cooldown: 1 hour.
Resets when joining Blood Ring.
Trinket can be replaced while being invisible, the invisibility remains when trinket is not equipped.
Doesn't remove you from combat (PVE).

I think this is one of the most underrated trinkets in the game and is especially good in Blood Ring - I have noticed not many people in Blood Ring use this trinket don't know why. It's good both in PVE and in PVP. In PVE you are given infinite Invisible Potion effect every 1 hour that allows you to skip some difficult packs in dungeons. In PVP this trinket is amazing, not only in Blood Ring. Classes that normally can't use stealth are now able to feel as Rogue and for example they can sneak into Dun Baldar's Aid Station in Alterac Valley to ninja it.
In Blood Ring this trinket is even more superior. When you join BR, the trinket's CD automatically resets so you can use it once every BR match. I have played many BR matches with this trinket and as a Priest I found it most useful using it a few seconds before start so I could have stealth advantage - enemies couldn't see me at first so they focused other players first. It's a bit selfish but works pretty well when you play clothie class and have tanky class in your group.
And of course group stun is very powerful alone. If you are lucky, you can stun all 3 enemies in BR giving your team big advantage. Both ways of using the trinket are effective and satisfying.
