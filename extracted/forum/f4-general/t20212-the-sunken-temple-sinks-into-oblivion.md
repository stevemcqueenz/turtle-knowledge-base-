# The sunken temple sinks into oblivion

## Post 138448 by Akarui (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-08T15:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138448#p138448 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For those who don't know at all - a dungeon for 50+ in the swamp of sadness. Although many now know it briefly thanks to a boring quest on atune in ES.

The dungeon is very interesting in terms of layout, mechanics and lore.

Lore A long time ago, the **Gurubashi empire** flourished in the jungle. As with any empire, everything ended very badly, in the case of the Gurubashi - a civil war. The most influential priests from the Atalay tribe in a desperate attempt wanted to bring back to life their revered god of blood Hakkar, but they failed, and defeat awaited them. The remnants of the Atalay tribe were expelled into the swamps. Where they erected a temple to try again to revive their god and from exiles to become winners and the main tribe.
Ysera learned of the Atal'ai priests' plans and sank the temple into the swamp. But not all Atal'ai fanatics died.
Jammal'an (one of the bosses) is the leader of the Atal'ai tribe. He had a vision of resurrecting Hakkar using the blood of his enemies, the green dragons.
Eranikus, Ysera's former consort, was sent to the ruins of the Temple of Atal'Hakkar to prevent Hakkar's return. But as soon as Eranikus and his retinue found themselves in the temple, they fell victim to the dark forces that lived there. In the real world, only a shadow remained of Eranikus, and he himself was stuck on the way to the Emerald Dream. Four other dragons accompanying Eranikus also fell into traps and ended up in the Emerald Nightmare. And now they wander around the temple like mad sleepwalkers.
Later we learn that some of the surviving fanatics moved to the north of the jungle (Zulgurub), where they finally managed to resurrect their god. But this is another dungeon and another story

Mechanics The mechanics of summoning a Atal'alarion Guardian of the Idol on the lower floor, where the altar of Hakkar is. You must activate the snake statues in the sequence **S, N, SW, SE, NW, NE**. It would be even more interesting if it was timed :) but as practice has shown, even this is a difficult test for many players.

The mechanics of summoning **Hakkar's avatar** from a pile of bones on the main floor. First, you need to do a quest for an egg, and then summon Hakkar's avatar. First, you will have to fight off weak boring waves of mobs until a snake with wings appears. Blood falls from them, which you need to put out the fire in the corner. and so on 4 times. Interesting but no challenge, because of this it is boring.

To be able to kill Eranikus, you must first kill Prophet Jamalan. And to kill the prophet, you need to kill 6 priests who hold a protective shield. ((I did not see any shield, purely technical convention))
Before killing Eranikus, you need to clear the floor from all the maddened green dragons. And that's it, the boss himself is very simple. Sometimes casts sleep.

Compared to the primitive Hateforge (which is more like a high level deadmines) the sunken temple is much more interesting. In places it feels like a mini-raid dungeon. Perhaps Blizzard originally wanted it that way.

But despite this, few people go there, 90% of players don't even know which stairs lead where.
There are few quests, the loot is almost useless. Compared to the primitive Maraudon, and Hateforge - the sunken temple is just a long labyrinth with colorful junk. The sad fate of the dungeon in Swamp of Sorrow.

 **How to increase its popularity?**
