First things first, "old turtle" was better for many different reasons, it being something newer and everyone else being newer is a big part of that. For many the whimsy has worn off. The powerlevel is so high that most dungeons raids and levelling encounters are absolute cakewalks for normal groups. Which removes the "can you beat it" aspect and replaces it with a "can you beat it before people get bored" aspect.

Additionally the game being streamlined to raid dps numbers might ensure every class is brought to raids and noone is left behind. But it erodes what makes classes unique. (Its not cool tanking tons of mobs as pala tank, if warr can do it reasonably well thanks to tclap buffs, its not cool absolutely lying into a mob as rogue if classes with better aoe doe just as well, its not cool clicking chainheal only when you dont even have to worry about mana anymore and so on)
You cant have distinct in strengh and weaknesses to a fulfilling degree and equially valuable in all situations.
Both options are fine, but they please vastly different playerbases. Neither od which is better than the other.
