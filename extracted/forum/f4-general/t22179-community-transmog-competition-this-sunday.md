The big event to kick of autumn - Azeroth's Next Top Mog-el, hosted by Vrograg Fishslayer

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/KGZSmOH.png)

 **When**: Sunday, October 5, 2025 12:00 PM EST | 5:00 PM server
 **Where:** Grim Reaches, cliffs northeast of Shatterblade Post (62/20) | Nordanaar Realm
 **What:** Turtle WoW's Premiere Reporter Orc hosts a competition of Transmog Excellence
 *Come dressed with your finest Transmog to prove to Turtle WoW you have what it takes to be Azeroth's Next Top Mog-el. This competition will require you to impress not just me and the audience but also your fellow competitors! All are welcome at this special event, high upon the oceanside cliffs of Grim Reaches*

 **Things to know / rules:**
- 39 Spots are available, first come first serve (form a line) on the day of the event

- I'll be on 30 minutes before for early registration

- entry is 5 gold

- volunteers will be available to donate to the prize pot

- there will be 11 separate heats: 8 qualifiers, 2 semi-finals, and then the finale

- Qualifiers and semi-finals winners will be determined by contestants

- Finale will be determined by stream chat polling

- One entrant per person (no multi-boxing)

- light RP is welcome

- the event will be streamed on twitch and youtube
Twitch: [www.twitch.tv/vrogragfishslayer](http://www.twitch.tv/vrogragfishslayer)
Youtube: [www.youtube.com/@VrogragFishslayer](https://www.youtube.com/@VrogragFishslayer)

 ****Note:** *In an effort to make Tmog events less stressful I'm trying out a more streamlined set of rules and scope of the event. I'd like to do these monthly rather than twice a year and for other Realms. If you miss this one, don't worry.  Constructive feedback always welcome***
