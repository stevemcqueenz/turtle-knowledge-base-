# DPS Balance vs. Utility: Should All Classes Be Equal?

## Post 111912 by Thorngraze — 2024-12-09T16:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111912#p111912 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I keep seeing complaints that X class is weak in damage output and really they need to be buffed so they can output damage like Y class. There seems to be a mentality of you're either a tank, healer or DPS and all DPS should be equal in their output. If all DPS classes produced the same kind of damage that would be boring.

One of the things I liked about CC2 is that the devs have leaned into giving specs more utility talents that provide support to the group. That now means lots of classes/specs (not just hybrids) can bring all kinds of utility to a group.

Is the actual issue here that a lot of this unique class/spec utility becomes a moot point when you enter the raiding sphere, hence the focus becomes how well you perform in the traditional three roles?
