I follow the group, but "all need" makes far more sense for BoE:
 1. The item is roughly equal value to all players.
 2. You can't always tell if they actually would actually benefit from it, especially on rare recipes where you don't even know if they have the profession.
 3. Even if they would benefit, they would likely choose to auction it anyway.

For expensive BoEs, "Need if you need" is mostly a way to direct more gold flow away from nice players and toward jerks.
