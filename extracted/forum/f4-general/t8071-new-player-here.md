## Post 52001 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-01T09:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52001#p52001 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1. Tanks by a mile, at least for dungeons (no idea about raids).
 2. I've seen a few dwarf-only guilds advertise in chat, so they definitely exist.
 3. Not great. It's 95% ganking and you need a very chill mindset because people tend to get fairly toxic (which is natural with all the ganking).

As a healer myself I can't really recommend picking healer just because of demand. I'm basing this off of LFT, Turtle WoW's dungeon finder. DPS are almost always well stocked and if not you can usually fill a group just by posting in world chat that you need a dps class. For the first 30 levels you have a lack of healers and a decent number of tanks. Then it flips around. Your typical endgame dungeon queue looks like this: 0 tanks / 1-2 healers / 5-9 dps.
There is an abundance of tanking classes (mainly warriors and palas, some feral druids), but very few people who *can* tank actually *will* tank, at least for pick-up groups. You can spend hours in LFT queues without any tanks showing up.

However - this is a game and you're supposed to have fun. There's a wide gulf between playing the three roles, their gameplay loops, their responsibilities, their leveling speed. Play whatever you find fun. You can make it work somehow. So what if you have longer queue times? Better than playing something you're heart isn't in. Dungeons are just one part of the game. For everything else - if you're good and personable you can always find a group of nice people to hang with, maybe join a guild or a raid group, etc.
