## Post 84340 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-11T18:43:00Z

The server is a bit P2W but not that much. Someone could say it's rather Pay To Skip but anyway. There are 4 impactful QOL features that can be bought for real money.

- Goblin Brainwashing Device - device that allows you to save and change between specific talent builds. You can buy it from Cash Shop or spend 350g in game - this is why I call it Pay To Skip item.

- Portable vendor - I don't recall it's name but it's very powerful item for example in lasher farm - you don't have to leave the instance when your bags are full, you can simply summon the vendor and continue farming - it saves a lot of time when you reach the endgame. Note that if anyone around you spawn this vendor, you can also use it even if you didn't buy it.

- Kodo/Mul mobile bank - you can use bank anywhere you want.

- Mobile Auctioneer - the goblin that lets you use AH wherever you want.
In PVP there are some useful appearances like Worgen, Ogre or Banshee that can be confusing for enemies - they won't know what class/race they are facing at first glance.
