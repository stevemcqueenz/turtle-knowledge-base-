## Post 102171 by Eeeeeeeeeee — 2024-09-29T15:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102171#p102171 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Without a notable damage increase and removal of its current CD I don't think it would make much of a splash to have it changed to AP.

I'm personally not big on the idea tho. We had magic archers in WC3 and Vanilla's support that kind of niche, high flavor playstyle is one of my favorite parts.

I think some more style-specific alterations are in order if we want to expand Hunter's gameplay options. For instances, here's an idea to make melee Hunter more interesting:

While Their Down: A quick strike with both weapons. Usable for 6 seconds after your pet crits the target.

In general I think taking tools away from niche styles is the wrong move, I'd rather see Vanilla+ style expansions. Keep the core identity of the style while giving it more tools or bringing existing tools that see little use into more relevance.
