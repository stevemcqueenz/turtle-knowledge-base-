These are some odd responses.

The differences between vanilla and retail revolve particularly around player interaction and having to work hard towards objectives. I fail to see how waiting and waiting around the dungeon entrance adds anything to the experience. The staff talks about "enjoying the journey and exploring the world" but walking the same roads we have walked for almost 20 years is not enjoyable and you don't get to explore anything new.

If anything, the LFT addon should disappear, since it allows groups to form without having to talk to anybody (no player interaction).

What I do now, as a tank (generally the leader of the group), is that when a group  is formed I immediately kick people who are non-responsive or who are a very long way from the dungeon. Queueing knowing that it will take you half an hour to reach the dungeon shows an absolute lack of respect and consideration for other players. Also, players get invited into the group before they accept the invitation, so they are sometimes away - this should also be fixed.

Regarding not enabling summoning stones to keep warlocks useful: I wonder how warlocks feel about this. Since only warlocks can summon, when a group with a warlock is formed, warlocks are always forced to walk, while others can simply wait wherever they are for a summon. Is that considered an advantage in any way?

Regarding the LFT addon: I have had it fail in so many ways, I can't even enumerate them all. I would say it is impossible to use it once without seeing it break or act up. I don't know if the developers are aware of this.

Thanks to everybody who brought a thoughtful message to the thread, even in disagreement.
