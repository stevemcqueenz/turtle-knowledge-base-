It's a 2 Hour Event and you should precast right before it begins. Tastyfish Pools spawn the entire 2 Hour duration, those pools are the ones that have the rare fish in them. When the Event is done there's a repeatable quest to turn in 5 Tastyfish for 23 Silver or so. So you can turn in the 170-200 some fish if you did the entire duration for Gold. You do not have to accept any Quests. They can all be turned in at Booty Bay, the NPCs stay there for an hour after the Event for everyone to turn in their fish. As for a good location, I recommend a little island on the northwestern edge before it wraps around to the Raptors and Gromgol that's where I've won before you can just turn around and the Pool is there to minimize running, however many fish near the Nagas on the Western Coast near the Center of the map. Hope this helps.

Locking in for the first 40 fish is required, have to really pay attention and turn on your sound :) I chugged a coffee and didn't blink much lol.
