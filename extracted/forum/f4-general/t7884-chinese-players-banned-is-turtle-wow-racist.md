Listen.  Speaking as an occasional player of this server, a human being with some *small* amount of common sense, and someone who has been on the receiving end of the **Turtle Banhammer™** for being a public nuisance, I think I can safely say that, for the most part, if a GM or Administrator decides that you should be banned from the *Turtle WoW* community and/or server, then there was more than likely enough "probable cause" or outright hard evidence that you were doing something that either went against the rules of the server or otherwise caused a noteworthy disruption that negatively impacted other players' experience with the community or the game.

Know that **your geographical location does not matter**.  By making an account from anywhere in the world, you have agreed to follow the rules of the server.  Ignorance of the rules due to language barriers is no excuse.  If you feel that you have been wrongfully accused or wrongfully banned, write an appeal – either in game (where possible), through the website, through e-mail, or even on the official *Discord* server.

All that said, play the game as intended, be relatively polite in public spaces, try not to annoy others too much, and you'll be fine.
