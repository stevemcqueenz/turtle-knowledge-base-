Hello there,

I'm Vallcow, played Turtle Wow for a couple of years now and I love exploring every possible place in the game.

The guide below includes all 36 guild bases known to be in game with a short description, location, some tips on how to get there and screenshots.

![Image](https://images2.imgbox.com/e7/63/O2D00MFb_o.jpg)

 **A word about access difficulty:**

I have visited all the guild bases listed below as a lvl 60 solo warrior. **No summons from guild members**. Yes, it's possible, but some of them require thinking out of the box, using specific items or skills, wall climbing or using specific consumes. Or sometimes all at once.

Each base has an **access** rating, those could be *easy*, *medium* or *hard*.

 **EASY**: Any level 60 character can get there without any gear or specific consumes needed. Usually it's just a run or a swim. Lower levels can visit them too, just check if the zone level won't kill you.

 **MEDIUM**: Any level 60 character can get there, but you might need some consumes like Noggenfogger Elixir (or any other means of slowfall), a 100% mount or just trial and error when climbing some hills in the right place.

 **HARD**: Those are guild houses located in hard to get to areas. On top of, or deep in the mountains with no direct access to them. This might require you being a specific class (mage can get everywhere), specific wall jumping skills, knowing the path (sometimes you have to start climbing in another zone), using consumes, certain items and all manner of tricks. Only for experienced explorers.

 **<Thunder Ale Brewing Co>**

 **Location:** Arathi Highlands
 **Access:** Easy
 **Flying Mount:** No
 **Innkeeper:** No

 **Description:**
We have to start with the big one. This is the guild base of Thunder Ale Brewing Co. One of the most recognizable and oldest guilds on the server. And it shows. The guildbase is probably the largest in whole Twow, including (a lot of) drinks merchants, a stable with rare mounts (go see Black Qiraji Battle Tank there), a section with guild achievements and much much more. If you're planning on visiting only one guild base in your travels, you should probably go there. (Don't do that tho, visit them all)

 **Map:**
![Image](https://images2.imgbox.com/00/8b/HJy5fod7_o.jpg)

 **How to get there:**
Swim north from Thandol Span (Arathi Highlands), or swim south from Revantusk Village (Hinterlands).

 **<SQUAD>**

 **Location:** Feralas
 **Access:** Hard
 **Flying Mount:** Yes
 **Innkeeper:** No

 **Description:**
Hidden deep in the mountains of Feralas, that one is hard to get to. If you manage, be sure to visit the tower and say hi to the Sheep inside. The base has a griphon rider that lets you explore a lot of Feralas with a short flight.

 **Map:**
![Image](https://images2.imgbox.com/a0/8b/rJSgukqk_o.jpg)

 **How to get there:**
There is a very tough hill climb and some wall jumping involved. Start climbing from Feral Scar Vale in Feralas, then go around until you reach the rivear and a waterfall. After that you need to get on the other side and climb the big tree. There is a possibility you can also reach it by scaling the mountain chain in the West. May be limited to only some classes.

Edit: (Thank you to Silver Crusade and Taurkus) It is possible to make a jump to the other side of the river and then climb up using terrain. Slowfall still required, and the jumps are hard. But it's possible.

 **<Thunderhorn Clan>**

 **Location:** Thousand Needles (on top of one of the pillars)
 **Access:** Hard
 **Flying Mount:** Yes
 **Innkeeper:** No

 **Description:**
A very picturesque place for a guildbase and awesome views from above on Thousand Needles valleys. Sadly also very hard to get to. If you manage, enjoy the big peace pipe, blow the enormous horn or just use the flying mount to explore things around it. Oh, and don't fall down.

 **Map:**
![Image](https://images2.imgbox.com/78/77/XaBq5TLK_o.jpg)

 **How to get there:**
This one is a doozy. First of all you will need 4 mount speed items (2 trinkets, gloves and boots) and a epic riding skill to make the jump. Then you need to find a way to climb the South-Western mountains in The Barrens. Once you're up, follow the tops of the mountains around Razorfen Kraul, stick to Thousand Needles side. Reach a place with a couple of peaks from where you can see the guildbase on the pillar. Equip all your mountspeed items, use slowfall and make the jump. It's a hard one, might take a few tries and finding the right jump spot, but you can make it.
