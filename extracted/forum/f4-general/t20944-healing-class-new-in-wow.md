## Post 145816 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-15T17:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145816#p145816 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

• Paladins can DI someone at the cost of their own life, and have very fast single-target heals with excellent mana efficiency.
• Shamans have Ankh to self-rez and return to the fight, plus very strong area spells and mana-regenerating totems.
• Priests have Proclaim Champion in their talent tree — a weaker, more limited form of battle rez — but possess a very strong and diversified healing kit.
• Druids have battle rez for anyone, but no baseline out-of-combat rez, Tranquility healing that can save a raid, very strong HoTs, and solid single-target healing.

To start, id say Paly

I'd exclude druid and shaman if we go absolutely beginner-friendly.
Druid has no normal res. It is not a big deal but if you are a new healer, it can stress you, specially if no one else in the team can ress. That extra stress might be a minus.
Shaman is interesting but totems cost mana and good mana regen comes later with talents. Before being comfortable with heal downgrading, it would feel punishing and being out of mana too often.

I think either priest or pala.
Priest has a more complex healing toolkit. Maybe pala.
