You are correct with saying wands dont deal much damage at high level.
However this is very much by design. Spellcasters are the ones that get access to them. And a wand is a backup tool so you dont have to run into melee and get oneshot by a random cleave or other melee hate mechanic.
If you want something to scale into the lategame, thats what your spells are for.
The concept of wanding all day long may be interesting, but the gameplay sure is not. So by keeping wands weak the game tricks you into playing whats stronger ans much more fun instead.
