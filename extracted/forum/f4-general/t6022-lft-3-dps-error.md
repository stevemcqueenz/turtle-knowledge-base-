I often get this error: when there are already 3 dps in party, lets say with a tank or healer, and we try to que then message says can't que as dps because 3 dps roles already picked. Which is not true.
It's not the case when 4 people try to pick dps. Tanks picks tank role, or healer his, and then 3 people pick dps and we get this error when last dps confirms his role.
