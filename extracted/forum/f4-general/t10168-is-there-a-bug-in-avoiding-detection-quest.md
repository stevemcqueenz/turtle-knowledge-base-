You never get flagged for pvp by picking up a quest, even the quests that say that they will flag you won't. What flags you is attacking a flagged npc, be it from the opposite or your own faction.

If you get flagged by doing such quests, you do this to yourself.

Would it be better if you did not get flagged by attacking such quest npcs? Absolutely. Why does the missing diplomat quest and alike flag you, if there's quests that need you to kill mobs of the opposite faction that will not flag you (like in stonetalon and ashenvale or hillsbrad)? You could just remove the pvp flag from mobs like Dashel Stonefist (?) or Private Hendel and people wouldn't be at risk of getting killed by other players.

As long as it is not changed however, be wary of what you're doing. Attacking a flagged npc will flag you, it's not the quest doing that to you, it's you doing what the quest asks you to do. Which you should either not do or scan the area with a macro prior to doing it (like you do in stormwind before you do the missing diplomat quest -> /who z-stormwind r-orc r-troll r-goblin...) as long as nothing is changed about those npcs.

I can assure you that's not how it happened.

 1st You said you were killed +-10 minutes later. If the quest would have actually flagged you for pvp upon picking it up (which it didn't), your pvp flag would have worn of by then if you didn't renew the reset timer by performing any action that itself would have flagged you. So it wouldn't have mattered if the quest had flagged you because you would have flagged yourself by whatever you did to reset the timer.

 2nd There's plenty of flagged horde mobs in SoS. You might easily have been flagged for something minor like reflecting damage upon them when you were attacked (thorns, retribution aura, shield spike and what not).

Edit: The only way to get flagged without any action on your end that I know of is by getting mind controlled by a mob (satyrs in felwood  or naga in feralas come to mind) to attack a flagged player of either faction. So beware in those areas if you're not alone.
