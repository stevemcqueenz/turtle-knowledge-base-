# Spell Scaling

## Post 19541 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-07-01T23:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19541#p19541 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've noticed recently that a lot of damage spells I cast in boomkin are doing noticeably less damage than ever before. It used to take me two r7 starfires to kill any of the high level moonkin in winterspring, now three can't do the job. My partial resists are hitting for ~300 on bosses with minimal or no arcane resist (I literally do much higher dps hitting the same boss with caster gear but in cat form).

I have 606 sp from items and enchants, but when my non-resisted starfires, that should hit for 496-584 before any scaling or resists are in effect, are hitting for less than 1k on average vs raid bosses I feel there is something gone wrong. I've steadily been increasing the quality of my gear but even against the same mobs and in the same raids I'm demonstrably less effective.

Starfire should have 100% scaling from every source I've found. Add in the 10% damage from talents (not sure how it applied, but I'd assume to base damage before scaling) and we'd have 1,151.6-1,248.4 damage before resists with my current sp from gear alone. If I included the +35 from greater arcane elixir, +150 from supreme power flasks, or +36 from brilliant wizard oil which I commonly use, we'd have 1,372.6-1,469.4 damage. As an example, I was hitting for ~350 on partial resists often against C'Thun who, according to TDB, has 75 arcane resist. If all of this is accurate, we're saying that would lead to often 75%-82% damage reduction.

Are there any other classes/spells getting affected? Is this intended? Was I just lucky for months with the impact of my spells?
