# The Legend of Grey Iron and the Five Glyphs Challenge

## Post 130761 by rebelsquadron — 2025-05-12T19:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130761#p130761 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Grey Iron couldn’t reconcile himself with the twisted and broken values of his Dark Iron kin.

So, he made the most difficult decision any being could ever have to make.

He cut all ties with his former Dark Iron brethren, striking out on his own to build a new life for himself, free from their influence and control. He barely escaped his former kin, with nothing more than the clothes on his back and old, worn out weapons handed down from one generation to the next.

With no friends, no family, no kin, nobody to turn to, if he were to fall, he would never get back up. He struggled to learn how to craft his own gear, but with limited resources, he was never able to make much progress. He survived on hunting wild boars, which are plentiful throughout the world of Azeroth, for sustenance and to scrape together what little coin he could gather.

Even amongst the Alliance, there was treachery from bad faith citizens, that sought to destroy him at every turn.

After a long, arduous, and incredibly dangerous journey, Grey Iron has finally succeeded in finding and building a new life amongst the goodly races of the Alliance. His reputation has finally been established as one of honor and integrity, honesty and justice.

Grey Iron turns to face the final boar that will bring this journey to an end and begin a new one.

The Legend of Grey Iron and the Five Glyphs is completed. The Legend of Grey Iron lives on.

 *** Grey Iron is a Dwarven Hunter who made it to level 60 with the Hardcore, Artisan, Vagrant, Slow & Steady, and Boar Hunting Glyphs live and active. Screenshots are included to prove that he made it to 60 with all Five Glyphs. ***
