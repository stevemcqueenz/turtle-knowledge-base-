# Towerforge Plans Drop Rate – Is 1.44% Reasonable?

## Post 168191 by Wilsonsds (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-14T14:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168191#p168191 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I would like to raise a concern regarding the drop rate of the Towerforge plans from Master Blacksmith Rolfen.

Each of the four recipes has a 1.44% drop chance. Statistically, that means roughly 320 kills (i calculated) are required to have a solid probability of obtaining a single recipe. To complete the full set, players are realistically looking at somewhere between 1,200 and 1,600 kills, depending on RNG variance.

 **This is comparable to — and in some cases effectively worse than — the drop rates of certain legendary-tier items in the game.**

Rolfen is a level 62 elite, not a raid boss. These are profession recipes, not endgame legendary weapons. The effort-to-reward ratio feels extremely disproportionate.

If the killing any bosses, it would apply a lockout (the only way to get grupos in the raid basically is to kill bosses), farming becomes practically impossible. Even if he is fully farmable, organizing groups repeatedly to kill a single elite hundreds of times for a 1.44% chance per recipe is not realistic in a live server environment.

Profession progression should require effort, yes. But it should also be attainable within a reasonable timeframe. Right now, completing the Towerforge set appears to require an amount of grinding that exceeds even some of the rarest items in the game.

Could we get clarification on the design intention behind this drop rate? Is this meant to be ultra-prestige crafting that only a handful of players will ever complete, or is this something that might deserve a tuning review?

At the moment, the numbers suggest a level of rarity that feels disconnected from the actual power and role of these items.

Or, is there any other way to impove the drop rates of the mob that im not aware of?

Just to inject some basic facts into the discussion:

Rolfen drops 1 recipe for Hammersmiths, a specialization of Weaponsmithing.
Plans: Towerforge Demolisher - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61805>

And he drops 3 recipes for Armorsmithing.
Plans: Towerforge Crown - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61808>
Plans: Towerforge Pauldrons - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61806>
Plans: Towerforge Breastplate - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61807>

Since Weaponsmithing and Armorsmtihing are mutually exclusive, it's impossible for a single character to make use of all 4 recipes.
Hammersmiths have to hope for a specific 1.44% drop.
Armorsmiths would like to get 3 of 4 drops.

My take on the rarity issue: The drop rates are kinda ridiculously low when viewed from the perspective of a 5-day-lockout. But killing Rolfen doesn't lock to the ID and he's 3 trash-packs into the instance. So to me it's fairly obvious that this is not a recipe you farm during full clears, but rather something you are meant to farm 5 times an hour until you're satisfied or give up. And for such a farm the drop rate is bad, but not uncommon (plenty of farmable recipes have 4% drop rate, while most dungeon epics have 1% and farmable mounts even lower than that, so 1.44% is low, but not exceptionally so). I think buffing drop rates to something like 2.5% would be nice though without going overboard if the devs for some reason want them to be rare.

But to put the drop rate into perspective:

Looking at Blacksmithing specifically, Plans: Titanic Leggings, Stronghold Gauntlets and Lionheart Helm have around a 1.3% drop chance. However, those recipes are BOE, they drop from multiple bosses, and these recipes can be purchased through the Auction House. That significantly reduces the practical barrier to obtaining them.

In contrast, the Towerforge plans have a 1.44% drop rate, only drop from a single elite source, and the recipes are not tradeable. That combination creates a much stricter bottleneck.

The issue is not just the raw percentage. It is the context in which that percentage exists.

If I may suggest a possible adjustment, either increasing the drop rate in Kara10 to around 3–4% per recipe, or making the plans BOE, would bring them more in line with comparable profession recipes in the game.
