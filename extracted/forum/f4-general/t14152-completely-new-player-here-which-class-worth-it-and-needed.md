Welcome to the server!

(Obligatory: play whatever race/class/style you enjoy the most.  There's plenty of room for everyone.)

That said, the entire server is cross-faction, not just raids.  That includes open world, groups, dungeons, guilds, and raids; it's all cross-faction. The only exception is faction cities (the guards will still attack you) and pvp (if you have pvp turned on).  Cross-faction really helps to tie the server together in cooperation.

Shamans are always in demand for their totems, so you'll have no problems getting into groups, raids, or guilds.

Same with warriors; tanks are in super high demand right now (and pretty much always).  You'll make a lot of friends just being a tank.

There is also multi-spec (requires a special device you can buy in-game with gold or in the donation shop). Once you've bought the device, switching between specs is free and so you're not locked into playing a certain way.
