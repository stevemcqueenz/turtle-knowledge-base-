## Post 84827 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-15T20:04:00Z

This AH website <https://www.wowauctions.net/> is amazing to compare economic phenomena occuring in Turtle WoW. I have had the theory that the demand for consumables branch is much higher than the supply in the Nordanaar. I found something interesting: look at Black Lotus price in the last month first at RP-PVE: [https://www.wowauctions.net/auctionHous ... otus-13468](https://www.wowauctions.net/auctionHouse/turtle-wow/nordanaar/mergedAh/black-lotus-13468) and then compare it to the PVP server: Alliance AH [https://www.wowauctions.net/auctionHous ... otus-13468](https://www.wowauctions.net/auctionHouse/turtle-wow/tel-abim/alliance/black-lotus-13468), Horde AH [https://www.wowauctions.net/auctionHous ... otus-13468](https://www.wowauctions.net/auctionHouse/turtle-wow/tel-abim/horde/black-lotus-13468).

For those who are too lazy to open these links and analyse the data, in RP-PVE server the price of Black Lotus has increased from 32 to 41g in one month. This is a price increase of 28%. Meanwhile, in PVP server in Alliance AH Black Lotus went down from 8 to 6g which is 25% decrease in price. Horde AH looks similar. While you might think these values are cherry-picked, we can't deny the fact that weird thing happened in RP-PVE server.

Black Lotus is the most important example we should care about because this herb is the reagent needed to craft all flasks. If the price of Black Lotus raises, the prices of flasks will go up too. So the price of Black Lotus can be perceived as derivative of flasks prices.

So what is the cause of Black Lotus being so expensive in RP-PVE and not in PVP server? Some people say it's because of scalpers mafia but then why the Black Lotus became so cheap in Tel'Abim? It doesn't make sense, scalpers don't play on only one server. The other reason could be raw gold farmed in farms like lasher one. While it's generally true in wider economy, it's not the case here (yet), we also have new gold sinks added to the game so the effect of these farms couldn't cause 28% price raise in such short time. If you look at other markets that aren't tied to Alchemy/Herbalism, you won't see general 28% inflation. The general prices are nearly the same as one month ago. This is why I think only one thing has been causing inflation:

- Too big demand for Black Lotus compared to its supply
I don't have the data so correct me if I'm wrong but I think we have never had so big raid activity since 2018 as we have now. Even though a few thousands of people left the server, old guilds managed to clear most of the content so they now have most of raids on farm. Including AQ40 and Naxxramas in which flasks are very desired. Moreover, new raid was added so for some min-maxers it's another instance to use flask in. My speculation is that demand for flasks is extremely high. What about supply then? According to my knowledge supply of Black Lotus has been the same since patch 1.17.0 - I think nothing has changed during that time, Black Lotus spawns are still being watched 24/7 as far as I know. I wouldn't be surprised if server's stat "All Black Lotuses collected per hour" hasn't changed much between these months. So I assume the supply of Black Lotus has been stable at least for one month while the demand of it is still kept very high. So the price naturally grows up. Only price high enough can effectively discourage people from buying it at this point - this is the way how market reacts.

What can be done about this problem? Developers can choose one of two pills:

- Red pill: Increase supply or lower demand for Black Lotus. I managed to notice there are mixed opinions about which way is better but in general it doesn't matter much what we choose. Both should reduce Black Lotus price in long-term.

- Blue pill: Do nothing. Let the market balance itself. This is the option we currently live in. We should simply accept that Black Lotus and flasks will cost even more. Optimistically speaking, herbalism will get a quiet buff.
And if you think the price of Black Lotus will drop in current supply/demand balance, you are probably wrong. Do you really think that demand for Black Lotus goes down when new tier 3.5 and tier 4 raids are added to the game? I don't think so. Anyway, what pill do you choose?
