## Post 60910 by Whey — 2023-09-04T20:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60910#p60910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't have a lot of experience in the server, but from what I understand the changes in Turtle WoW are not huge and they haven't chaged the DPS rankings a lot. Warriors and Rogues are the best DPS on early raid gear, and as you go towards Naxx, Mages and Warlocks catch up to them. So, the top 4 are the same: Warrior, Warlock, Rogue, Mage.

Hunter got some good buffs, and can now contest the top 4 mentioned above. Which means that the 5 non-heal classes are excellent DPS choices and you can be top DPS with them (But warrior is the best amongst them)

For the heal-hybrid classes, the problems remain the same pretty much, but they can all do a bit more damage in Turtle, which means if the guild is not min-maxing you can bring any of them and do just fine.

That said, Shadow priest is usually just one in raids (the debuffs are increased but still limited). Boomkin and Elemental Shaman don't have the damage, so you can just bring 1 Boomkin for the buff. Enhancement Shaman and Retri Pala can mainly bring Nightfall procs, their DPS is low otherwise.

Lastly, Feral Druid is an interesting exception, they can do very good (maybe in the 5th spot along with hunter) if you can play them correctly, but reqire effort to do so, and they can also tank with the same talents. So, I think they have a good spot as an extra tank with good dps capabilities.

TLDR
DPS rankings:
 1) Warrior
 2) Rogue, Warlock, Mage
 3) Hunter, Feral Druid
 4) all the rest
