**1.18 Pet Titles**

An addon suggestion is linked [here](https://github.com/Avery1337/TurtlePetSearch) called TurtlePetSearch. It will let you search your spellbook for any pets. It also has a title button that dynamically tracks pet acquisition.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/TIY0CHZ.png)
 **Itsy Bitsy Hero**
All spiders on this list are Bind on pickup. 15 spiders are required to finish this title. This title got added with [1.18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=17688) patch.

- [Araxxna's Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37011)
 - This spider has a 20% chance to be dropped from a Karazhan 10 boss called [Brood Queen Araxxna](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61221).

- [Cavernweb Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37009)
 - This spider has a 10% chance to be dropped from a Dragonmaw Retreat boss called [Cavernweb Broodmother](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=62066).

- [Maexxna's Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37012)
 - This spider has a 30% chance to be dropped from a Naxxramas boss called [Maexxna](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=15952).

- [Razzashi Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37010)
 - This spider has a 6% chance to be dropped from various mobs in Zul'Gurub.  These 3 mobs are called [Razzashi Broodwidow](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=11370), [Razzashi Skitterer](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=14880), and [Razzashi Venombrood](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=14532).  These 3 mobs are found in the Spider Boss area of Zul'Gurub.

- [Skitterweb Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37006)
 - This spider has a 4% chance to be dropped from various mobs in Karazhan 10.  These 4 mobs are called [Skitterweb Crawler](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61206), [Skitterweb Darkfang](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61207), [Skitterweb Leaper](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61209), and [Skitterweb Venomfang](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61208).  These 4 mobs are found before the Spider Boss in Karazhan 10.

- [Black Widow Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37007)
 - This spider has a 0.5% chance to be dropped from a single spider in Duskwood called [Black Widow Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=37007).

- [Darkmist Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37013)
 - This spider has a 0.5% chance to be dropped from a 5 spiders in Dustwallow Marsh called [Darkmist Lurker](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4377), [Darkmist Recluse](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4378), [Darkmist Silkspinner](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4379), [Darkmist Spider](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4376), and [Darkmist Widow](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4380).

- [Lava Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37002)
 - This spider has a 0.5% chance to be dropped from various mobs in Searing Gorge.  These 2 mobs are called [Greater Lava Spider](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=5858), and [Searing Lava Spider](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=5857).

- [Mistbark Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37005)
 - This spider has a 0.5% chance to be dropped from various mobs in Balor.  These 3 mobs are called [Mistbark Spider](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=62342), [Mistbark Webweaver](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=62343), and [Mother Mistbark](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=62344).  These 3 mobs are found in the cave near the northern coast of Balor.

- [Night Web Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37008)
 - This spider has a 0.5% chance to be dropped from various mobs in Tirisfal Glades.  These 4 mobs are called [Night Web Matriarch](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1688), [Night Web Spider](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1505), [Vicious Night Web Spider](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1555), and [Young Night Web Spider](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1504). These 4 mobs are found in and around the outside of the cave north of Deathknell.

- [Smolderweb Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=12529)
 - This spider can be obtained through completing the [quest](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=4862) that brings you into Lower Blackrock Spire.  You need to collect Spire Spider Eggs near the spider boss.

- [Tarantula Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37003)
 - This spider has a 0.5% chance to be dropped from various mobs in Redridge Mountains.  These 3 mobs are called [Chatter](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=616), [Greater Tarantula](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=505), and [Tarantula](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=442).  These 3 mobs are found all over the place in Redridge Mountains.  I would only recommend farming the Tarantulas near the southern enterance of Redridge Mountains because all the other spawns are camped.

- [Timberweb Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37004)
 - This spider has a 0.5% chance to be dropped from a spider in Azshara called [Timberweb Recluse](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=8762).

- [Webwood Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37000)
 - This spider has a 0.5% chance to be dropped from a 5 spiders in Teldrassil called [Giant Webwood Spider](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=2001), [Webwood Venomfang](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1999), [Webwood Lurker](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1998), [Webwood Spider](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1986), and [Webwood Silkspinner](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=2000).

- [Wildthorn Hatchling](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=37001)
 - This spider has a 0.5% chance to be dropped from various mobs in Ashenvale.  These 3 mobs are called [Wildthorn Stalker](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3819), [Wildthorn Venomspitter](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3820), and [Wildthorn Lurker](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=3821).  These 3 mobs are found all over the place in Ashenvale.  I would only recommend farming the Wildthorn Lurkers near the Warsong Lumber Camp because there is a much higher density of mobs there.

 **Lord of the Frogs**
- [A Jubling's Tiny Home](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19450)
 - This frog is going to be at the Darkmoon Faire.  The quest [Spawn of Jubjub](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=7946) must be completed.  You need to lure the frog hidden under one of the carts to Morja with [Dark Iron Ale Mugs](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11325).  I had to spend around 5 to get the frog to Morja. She then gives you an unhatched egg which will sit in your inventory for one week.  After one week the egg will hatch and you will have your Jubling companion pet!

- [Island Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=50079)
 - This frog can be obtained through grinding various mobs in Lapidis Isle and Gilijim Isle with a 0.08% drop rate.  This frog is bind on pick up so you need to grind for this one.

- [Tree Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11026)
 - The Darkmoon Faire will have a child named Flik which will give this frog.

- [Wood Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11027)
 - The Darkmoon Faire will have a child named Flik which will give this frog.

- [Dart Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=50078)
 - This frog can be bought by the horde in either the Crossroads or Arathi Highlands. The Dart frog is unfortunately bind on pickup, so alliance players might have luck going to Primalist Bin'doga in Lapidis Isle.

- [Bullfrog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=54002)
 - This frog can be bought from Dirge Quikcleave in Gadgetzan. Or grinding from random mobs around the world.

- [Golden Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=54007)
 - This frog can be bought from the AH for a lot of gold or you can grind it from mobs with a 0.015% drop rate. Good luck with that

- [Infinite Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=54003)
 - This frog can be bought from the AH for a lot of gold or you can grind it from the mobs inside of Black Morass with a 0.03% drop rate. Good luck with that

- [Poison Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=54004)
 - This frog can be bought from the AH or you can grind it from the mobs in EPL with a 0.09% drop rate. Good luck with that

- [Pond Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=54008)
 - This frog can be bought from Masat T'andr in Swamp of Sorrows.

- [Snow Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=54005)
 - This frog can be bought from the AH or you can grind it from the mobs in Winterfall Village with a 0.03% drop rate.

- [Dream Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=54001)
 - This frog drops from multiple mobs in Emerald Sanctum with a 0.08% chance to drop.  This drop is also a bind on pick up drop.

- [Azure Frog](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=54000)
 - This frog can be bought from the AH or farmed from whelplings in the badlands or whelplings from Winterspring. Drop chance is 0.08% for all creatures.  If you are a high level I would suggest farming them from Winterspring.  More whelps in that zone.

## Post 160125 by Ky6ik — 2025-11-19T23:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160125#p160125 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

First of all, as always, let’s start with a thank you! Big thanks to Speedz1337 for providing the info on where and which pets you need to collect.

I finally got my first title: “The Itsy Bitsy Hero”!
So I wanted to share my personal guide/comment on how to optimize getting this title — maybe it’ll help someone.

First of all, out of all the titles you can get from collecting pets, this one is probably the easiest, fastest, and requires absolutely zero gold. All you need is free time and some luck.

Let’s start simple:

Smolderweb Hatchling – just do the quest in LBRS. Nothing complicated, just go kill the spider boss and the eggs next to him. Five minutes and you’ve got your first pet.

Araxxna's Hatchling – drops very often, around 30% chance. Form a Karazhan 10 raid, make it HR or SR for this pet and that’s it. A bit of luck and the second pet is yours.

Cavernweb Hatchling – honestly it’s harder to not get lost in this dungeon than to farm the pet. About a 10% drop chance. Super easy to solo at 60: go in, kill the boss, if the pet drops - great, if not - run out and reset. Nothing difficult, just a bit of luck and you’ve got pet #3.

Maexxna's Hatchling – now this one needs luck, because it drops in Naxxramas with about a 30% chance, and you’re competing with other people who want it too. Everything depends on how well you can “negotiate.” Fourth pet done.

Razzashi Hatchling – the hardest part is gathering people to go kill the Zul’Gurub spider boss. Drops from the mobs before that boss, and the drop chance is very, very high. If you go with your guild it’s super easy. Fifth pet done.

Skitterweb Hatchling – drops in Karazhan 10 from trash mobs before the basement boss. Tons of mobs there and drop chance is high, it almost always drops. Easy to get with HR or SR. Sixth done.

Black Widow Hatchling – from this one on, your time and luck start to matter more. At level 60 you can kill these spiders easily - there are tons of them - but this farm can take hours. Personally I had zero competition and got mine in about an hour. Seventh pet ready.

Darkmist Hatchling – same deal, no issues at 60. Just run circles and kill everything with more than four legs. Within an hour you’ll get your eighth pet.

Lava Hatchling – here’s a small trick. On TurtleWoW they added a new cave in Searing Gorge. The entrance is a bit left of the Blackrock Mountain entrance. It’s super easy to run circles in that cave and farm spiders. No competition, nobody bothering you. Ninth pet done.

Mistbark Hatchling – run circles inside and outside the cave, kill spiders, watch a TVshow, relax. Whether it takes one hour or eight hours, you’ll get your tenth pet eventually. Congrats.

Night Web Hatchling – super easy to get in Tirisfal Glades. Spiders are level 3–4, but please be respectful to leveling players and help with quests if they need it!

Tarantula Hatchling – rage! hatred! fury! despair! tears!
That’s just a small part of what you’ll experience farming tarantulas in Redridge Mountains. This was my personal nightmare - I spent over four hours there. Very few spiders, insane competition because tons of new players are questing on these mobs. Super popular early-game zone! Never again!
But there’s a trick: tarantulas spawn in two places - at the lumber mill in the center of the zone above the lake, and all the way in the far corner near the bridge to Duskwood. Running back and forth between these two spots helps a lot and prevents you from losing your mind.

Timberweb Hatchling – nothing complicated except they’re level 54–56 and don’t die in one hit. Just takes time to kill enough of them.

Webwood Hatchling – same as the Tirisfal spiders. Level 3–4 mobs, run circles and kill everything that has more than four legs. Super easy. That’s pet number fourteen.

Wildthorn Hatchling – spiders around level 20–25, nothing difficult. Run circles and kill them all. I farmed at the lumber mill in eastern Ashenvale. And with that, you’ll get your fifteenth spider and the “The Itsy Bitsy Hero” title!
![Image](https://ibb.co/cS6mRrdF)
