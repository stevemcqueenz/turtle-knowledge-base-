> It is all true mortal. We stand ready to deprive you mortal races of the Arcane. I hope you dare not question it. Not only has your foolishness brought the Great Sundering, the damned Burning Legion and all that came with it. A portal to a different world? A great feat, one that was paid in blood a million times over.
>
>  As protectors of this world we are meant to limit the access to the Arcane. Yet all of you grew so confident and rather powerful. Lord Malygos, the very one who taught the first mages, has decided that you are no longer worthy of this responsibility. Azeroth can heal, and new races can be born.
>
>  Sometimes a blank canvas is the best way to fix an issue. Treat this as a warning, and leave as I still have some pity left for one that played part in both the saving and ending of Arygos. Tell him, he alone, shall be safe.

And that comes from perhaps the highest-ranking blue dragon we can meet in Vanilla WoW.
So we're clearly going to be at odds with the Blues if Malygos intends to cut us off from magic and perhaps even implies a genocide of all intelligent life ("new races can be born. Sometimes a blank canvas is the best way").

I would assume 1.18.1's Moonwhisper Coast will shed a lot of additional light on what we might expect with the next patch afterwards and the Blue Dragonflight.

Besides that I could see these patches following a bit of a progression as they rework the original Vanilla raids. MC gave us Thaurissan, a very lore rich character. BWL will have a boss added next patch. AQ40 would be the logical next step, and there's clearly deep dragon involvement with the whole Shifting Sands situation and C'Thun is known to use a lot of mind control and corrupting influences. I could see them put a dragon boss into AQ40. And then Naxx rework and Quel'thalas in the next patch with a Scourge theme would fit as well.
