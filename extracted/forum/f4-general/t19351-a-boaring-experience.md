# A Boaring Experience

## Post 131485 by Robespoire — 2025-05-16T21:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131485#p131485 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I did not find many information on the *Boaring Adventure* challenge so I decided to make a small guide, mostly so people considering the challenge know what to expect. It is written for alliance but only the first level will differ and you do not need a guide for those anyway.

Dun Morogh

Easy, plenty of boars, quick leveling, not a lot of competition to get the boars.

- [Small Crag Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=708) (level 3)
- [Crag Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1125) (level 5-6)
- [Large Crag Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1126) (level 6-7)
Elwynn Forest - Westfall

Some more competition when reaching Westfall but nothing too bad. You'll barely remember your time here.

- [Rockhide Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=524) (level 7-8)
- [Young Goretusk](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=454) (level 12-13)
- [Goretusk](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=157) (level 14-15)
Redridge Mountains

Now things are starting to get a little more tedious. There are a few people killing the same boars, although if it comes to that you can switch back to [Elder Mountain Boar at Loch Modan](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=1192) and miss on the occasional Bellygrub kill.

The issue is that the next boars will be level 23 to 25 and you need to kill level 16 and 17 to get there. Ideally you want to be at least level 19 when leaving this zone, so level 23 will be orange but you can easily wait for level 20. So you'll be there for a while but it's still not that bad.

Forget about [Mesa Boars](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=62262) (level 21-23), they are in the middle of a level 25-35 zone (Thousand Needles and not The Barrens like advertised by the website) with level 28 invisible mobs. It's likely a mistake, [a game design bug](https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=13140) if you want.

- [Great Goretusk](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=547) (level 16-17)
- [Bellygrub](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=345) (boss level 24)
Hillsbrad Foothills

What the hell is that?! One boar every 200 meters? So many people doing the same challenge! Some of them using their level 60 to kill you if you have the PvP challenge enabled for the 20% bonus XP (5% since 1.18), some of them waiting at one specific spawn point for hours and hours, probably with a dual screen while doing something else.

This is hell, and you're here for a while. But wait to out level the the Forest Boar and you get to the Elder Forest Boar, same zone, same issue, less boar, more competition. You'll start adding other Boaring Adventurers to know when they're around, you'll know the exact pixel at which each of the six Elder Forest Boar will spawn and the exact timing. You need optimization and discipline to get there.

- [Forest Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=62260) (level 23-25)
- [Elder Forest Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=62261) (level 26-27)
Arathi Highlands

The nightmare is over. Welcome to the small farm of Arathi. You'll be there for a while, at least until level 39, there is a huge gap before the next zone from 34 to 48. You might even stay there until level 42 if you play some additional challenges. But at least the boars are all together in one spot and there is a good number of them.

Too many people? You'll come back later, it's all good, people don't stay there too long anyway, it's not Hilsbrad anymore, you made it.

- [Hightusk Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61975) (level 33-34)
Blasted Lands

It's almost over, you've reached the last zone, but those Ashmane Boars are tough, they are very tanky, do a reasonable amount of damage and they are red. If you are only level 39 then half of them even have a skull!

Two options: either do dungeons and quests to get a better gear, use scrolls and potions, or go back to Arathi and get some more levels.

With gear and level up things will get easier. You'll be here a very long time but there are many boars and most level 60 farming the zone are not interested in them. There is some competition here but it's nothing you cannot face, you came out of Hillsbrad, this is nothing.

- [Ashmane Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=5992) (level 48-49)
- [Helboar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=5993) (level 52-53)
