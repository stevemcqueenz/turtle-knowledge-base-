The Boaring Adventure Challenge has to be the most unique challenge I have ever heard of. The TWoW team has said they will help close the level gap by adding new boars like in Arathi Highlands. There are a couple things I question though:

 1. Even if you add a whole bunch of boars, it is a grind. There would be no stopping a mage from using Blizzard on all of them to take the Xp! Not to mention the lack of boars even in low areas like Elwynn. Will there be a big group aspect because of this?

 2. Boars aren't hard enemies. You can easily fight two at once if they are one level below you. To my group point above, will there be very difficult boars that you will need to fight with other people? Will there even be elite boars that you cannot take down by yourself?

 3. How will your armor and weapons balance with these boars? You can easily do Vagrant Mode if all the boars are similar to the ones we know of. There will be less reason to do quests if you only get XP from boars! In theory, you can just grind the boars and hope blue items drop from them!

 4. Quilboars. They are humanoid, but they are boar humanoids! Will they count for XP?

So many things that I suppose we will figure out once it launches. Thank you for reading, and good luck on your journey.   smiling_turtle_head
