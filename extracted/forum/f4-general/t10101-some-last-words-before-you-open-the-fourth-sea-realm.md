# Some last words before you open the fourth SEA realm

## Post 66125 by Pyrahead — 2023-09-21T04:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66125#p66125 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone, hey dear Turtle Wow devs.
Before you open the fourth realm, please let everyone transfer first without allowing new accounts. Inform everyone properly that transfers are now available and how to do them in general. I am really glad that you have managed to fix the huge problem with the queue on the EU realm, but what we once had feels a bit lost now and resembles a world full of NPCs to fight over mobs with. If you manage to get that 10 minute respawn mob tagged people emerge from behind and whisper/spam you with "111111", "[]!!!!" "INV". When I see other people questing in an area where I am around, especially after making a new character, I don't see other players I would love to adventure and quest with but all I see are rivals.

You have done something great here, and even managed to build something that not even a triple-A company could do. Give us back the peace to breathe in the new areas you have created.

If you love immersion in a game as much as I do, then I think you will agree with me. If not, at least I tried.

Take it slow.   smiling_turtle
