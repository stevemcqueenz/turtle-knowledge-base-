I love the willingness to modify so many small and interstitial parts of the game for QoL without crushing fantasy. Recently the change to charged pets into trinkets was wonderful!

I have modded most aspects of vmangos and trinity core, spells, quests, environments, items, loot, events, etc. They are doing a pretty great job feeling out the systems and editing / embellishing them. Some of their solutions have been really clever!

The changes they make are not all the easiest option, but quite a few I see as the best option.

Cheers to their work!
