We all love to complain but generally T-WoW is pretty awesome.
So let's list some of our favorite custom changes/additions.   satisfied_turtle_head
I'll start:

 1. The Shaman changes were all very sensible and conservative... and cleverly designed in regards to Thunderhead.
 2. They did not give Frost Mages anything. This is wise design as often times the smartest thing to do -- is absolutely nothing.
 3. They nerfed items that were overpowered for how easy they were to get. Such as Naxx trash items.
 4. They are buffing awful PVP gear rewards. And in general they have buffed many awful items.
 5. PVP changes such as making WSG Reputation easier to get. And nerfing AV honor to make people play WSG/AB.
 6. Blood Ring and its rewards have been awesome. It kinda sucked as 2v2 but they made it 3v3 which was wise.
 7. They greatly improved Holidays (especially Winterveil). They should do the same for Darkmoon Faire.

Theres just a few that I can immediately think of.

## Post 98449 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-07-03T11:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98449#p98449 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

All positive:
 * Arcane mages having a niche with the mana recharge (evocate + if innervate is targeted on them). Missilies DPS also got raised a bit.
 * Shadow priest at PVE.
 * Shaman shields, also totem CD change (allowing twisting without losing cast time)
 * Bears valid tank
 * Palas valid tank
 * Honestly the fact that if you are a hybrid you are not automatically a healer
 * Cross faction
 * Custom items to make neglected specs more viable.
 * Thunderclap in def stance is really cool.
 * New races.

Mixed:
 * Tea. Would put it on a bigger CD while keeping it on HS global, so HS actually worths something for casters.
 * Pala at BG. I am really happy that non-holy became a valid spec but PVP became too strong because of how holy damage works. Don't get me wrong, imo ret pala needs slight buffs in PVE but PVP is a different story.
 * Some added ultimate talents, like wyvern sting. Cool that TWOW realized where are changes needed but the given answer was not always good (like ardent defender, too, prot palas need a REAL damage mitigation talent).
 * Most custom instances. They have their highs (Gilneas) and lows (ES trashmobs).
 * TMOG. I like it but at PVP it really makes an important skill (recognizing items, adjusting strategy) impossible. Would disable TMOG within BGs.
 * Enh rework. There are some good ideas there but it doesn't become coherent enough to be useable. Like turning a 20% ready spec into a 40% ready spec.
 * Survival profession. Cool idea but execution is so-so. It's basically the get tent (maybe a boat) and ignore the thing profession. Anyone has ever unironically used murloc slippers f.ex.?
 * Hyjal. Cool concept but the worst ever route in the game. All it needs is a North-South shortcut to be good. Now it's like travelling the whole barrens to get from Southeast to Northeast.

All negative:
 * They probable banned Schmockle.

PS:
+1 for oranges, no world buffs in instances (and soulwell)
