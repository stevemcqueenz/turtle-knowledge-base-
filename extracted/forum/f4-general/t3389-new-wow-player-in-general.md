## Post 20612 by Bisbyhoughton — 2022-07-28T20:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20612#p20612 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Turtle WoW has been pretty much my first time playing WoW as well. I make it a point to always let a group know when I do a dungeon run if I've never done the dungeon before and that I'm pretty new to WoW which usually helps keep anybody from getting mad at me and opens to door to folks clearly explaining what they want me to be doing in case I get it wrong.

I've had the most difficult times when playing non-US hrs which has occasionally landed me in groups where I was the only person speaking English as a primary language. This made it more difficult for me let people know I was a noob and left me feeling like a shitty group member since I couldn't ask for or receive solid feedback to be a better group member but that's just how it goes sometimes when playing in a server that has a pretty diverse player base and there's always ways to make it work, especially as you get more familiar with the game.

Overall though, I have found that people are usually excited when they learn you're new to WoW and are eager to help make you feel welcome and show you the ropes so don't hesitate to be social, ask for help, and just learn as you go!
