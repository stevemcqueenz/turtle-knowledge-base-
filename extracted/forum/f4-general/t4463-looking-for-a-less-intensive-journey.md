# Looking for a Less Intensive Journey

## Post 26682 by Odalric — 2022-10-04T03:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26682#p26682 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, I've never been one for modern MMOs, or most modern games at that.  I like slower games, that are more of an experience.  I can't keep up with these action combat systems, and hoo boy FFXIV was a bit, even if it was Tab Target,
I've also fallen on some tough times to say the least.  I wanted to play actual classic, but can't really justify the sub.  I've been living vicariously through streams and YouTube, and got recommended some videos of someone who plays here.

I actually love the idea behind this server, and the fact that it's expanding horizontally instead of vertically.  That's a thing I'm starting to hate about MMO's.  I don't need to be level 100, and number doesn't have to always go up.  I use games as a social outlet and tried to get into some organized groups in games like Hold Fast, but they usually ten to devole into cliques and power dynamics over the game.

I played WoW from Vanilla to WotLK, but never really kept up.  I never had the grindset and just wanted to play the game.  This left me in a weird spot.  That along with my Paladin being expected to heal, which I was never good at, added to the pain.

I love Warcraft, it was my childhood, and I would love to go on another adventure.  I feel it would do me some good if I got out and interact with others.  The bit that I'm still unsure about is how I'll fit into the actual game.  Once I get beyond 12 hotkeys it gets tough, 24 is a real struggle for me.  This is a thing I remember struggling with in WoW, and I defiantly struggled with it in FFXIV, which is why I dropped it.

I remember having a lot of buttons, separate ranks.  I set a timer for my Paladin buffs, and it started to grind on me after a while.  It felt like work.  I'm not looking for one button gameplay by any means, but I'm more focused on the world and the story over the gameplay.  The Thassarian quests from Wraith are what I'm looking for.  Experiences with stories.

What type of characters would be conductive to my end goal here?  Is there an easy way to keep up with player run events?  And how can I get involved?

## Post 26687 by Shamma (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-10-04T06:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26687#p26687 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As somebody who likes to play all classes, if you want to have less buttons to press, u need a class that can do less things - so a pure DPS class - that way you have no buttons that are meant for tanking or healing. All classes in WoW get a lot of buttons and can fill up all bars, but with a pure DPS you can absolutely get away with less than 9 binds.

Player run events (among many other things) are coordinated over Discord. The TWOW discord is active and you should join it.

It might also be interesting for you to create 2 characters and play them both - one normal and one HC. The normal and HC players are split due to game mechanics and there is no way for me to tell you which you will like more. They play pretty differently however, so you are bound to like one more than the other.
