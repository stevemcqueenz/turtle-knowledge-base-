This is just the tl;dr of how it was/is in GW, and would have to be heavily revised/revamped (also a lot of devtime for a system that probably would be hated or just not work): 3 Main Outposts per faction, which would only be for that factions alliances (group of guilds), 15 more minor outposts that could be for either factions alliances. Each outpost had a rank, and with enough points your alliance would get assigned an outpost. Points were gained by players donating points of a reputation to their alliance's pool of points. There was also a weird threshold/border system that got adjusted based on the last 3 Alliance Battles (basically a form of BGs), which let the other faction get a chance to have ownership of an outpost that would be otherwise in the opposite factions control.
