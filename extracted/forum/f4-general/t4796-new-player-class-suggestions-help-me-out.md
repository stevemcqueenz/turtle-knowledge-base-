## Post 28669 by Shamma (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-11-07T18:59:00Z

Paladins are present in abundance (too many running around). Holy paladin is as good as it has always been and a welcome healer for any content. Ret paladin is not a raid spec.

Resto druid is different here than on classic/vanilla and is ok, not the best healer though. Probably the least desired and represented healer. Bears are better.

Ask yourself what role u want to play (dps/healer/tank) and maybe then ask for suggestions. All classes work well on Turtle, just not in all their roles (just like in vanilla).

Consider playing HC(hardcore/one-life) for great pve/casual experience. Highly recommended as the players you will meet will be more more friendly.

All classes that can heal make good raid healers and healers in general are in demand.
Druids are more gimped as healers in 5-man because they do not have a normal res. They are great 5man tanks however, so getting a grp is easy in that role.

Paladin is easier to play in general than druid. But probably also more boring.

Both are strong alone and welcome in groups as tanks.

Neither is good as dps and are mostly avoided in that role.
