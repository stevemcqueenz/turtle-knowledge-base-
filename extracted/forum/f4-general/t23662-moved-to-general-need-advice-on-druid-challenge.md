## Post 165643 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-01-19T18:52:00Z

I don't know the mechanics, but if you want help in regards to how you can just... Manage taking on elites solo better, I could probably help.

Your first steps should be hotting and dotting. Dot up the mob, regrowth/rejuv, bear form, pop enrage, build rage until it's full, then dump it all on frenzied regen. Damage is going to be low, but you'll have so much sustain keeping you up with two hots and a big fat self heal that it doesn't really matter. Barkskin should also be thrown out too.

After all your healing has been cycled through, you just bash and try and squeeze in another regrowth/rejuv during the stun. Not a big deal if you take a hit during the cast. Alternatively you can just war stomp if you're a tauren.

Otherwise? I'd just recommend... Asking for help. This is a social game after all. Interact with people. It'll get you places.

I'd say - don't tank him.
I soloed Shy Rotam in feral spec. It summons second cat and chain-fears when it get to melee.
It took looong time, but I managed it. Sleep one cat, root other cat. The only problem was mana regeneration. If you get some manaregen gear (I had it), you can sustain for eternity.
Theoretically - you can kill any target as druid if that target is not immune to roots and you fight outdoors. So it is matter of patience.

Vanilla is class identity first. You are not "bear" you are druid afterall. It means you should be able to cast spells from time to time.
