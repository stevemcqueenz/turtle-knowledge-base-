Greetings,

It's about that time of the year again, where the Lunar Festival Quests lure you into checking the quest-counter and you get depressed afterwards again...

I'm at 2470 quests now (just finished the current events), and really did the worst of the worst already. I have a few raid-quests left but that's it. And it looks like things haven't changed much. I don't think the current 5 open raid quests + the remaining event quests would bring me somewhere even close to 2800.

I have compared all quests in the pfQuest/pfQuest-turtle database. On top of the shared quests between factions, the horde has **1456** additional quests, and the alliance has **1873** quests. This does not include quests started by items. I only checked the number of quests available by quest givers.

But that being said, it seems like the **alliance having roughly 400 more open-world accessible quests** than the horde, which is quite a bummer. Especially because that's exactly the number of quests I am missing for the title.

I hope that either horde receives some new questing areas or that the achievement goal will be reduced a little, for the horde people meanwhile. I understand that such a huge amount of quests to balance the ratio can't be done that quickly, so I think adjusting the limit to the available numbers would be a great thing to do meanwhile.

Another frustrating part about all that, is that there's the myth of some horde players that *managed* have the title. And those are mentioned whenever you speak up about the imbalance. But as soon as you dig deeper you find out they have it from a previously faction transfer or got a helpful bug with their quest counter at some point.

Even if it turns out that someone actually managed it, it can't be the solution to learn/unlearn all professions, pick the right class for max. class quests, etc. Where on the other side of the faction, people just do world quests and are fine.

Just my 2 cents. I really hope there will be something changed.

 - Shagu
