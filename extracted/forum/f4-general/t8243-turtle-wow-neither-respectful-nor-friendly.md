# Turtle WoW: Neither "Respectful" Nor "Friendly"

## Post 52996 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-11T12:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52996#p52996 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

"Hey, I died in Hardcore.  Can you guys–"
"Hey, **read the rules**, idiot.  LOL"

"Man, there are a lot of people on this server.  Could you guys open–"
"**No means no**, stupid**.**  LMAO"

"PvP–"
"**No one *forced you* to PvP**, cretin.  ROFLMAO"

Does any of this sound *familiar*?  It *should*.  That's because this community is, for lack of better words, **intolerant**.

I've seen it time and time again.  Someone makes a perfectly polite post about some common issue they're experiencing.  Generally speaking, it's about something that's already been addressed in a post, somewhere.  Rather than point the user toward said post, or directly quoting the post itself, what do the people of the forum do?  They **laugh** at the person… call them **names**… and just generally leave *very* discouraging and outright *disparaging* replies.  I find this behavior rather **elitist**, as well as *rude*.  It's basically the equivalent of "git gud scrub"; it doesn't help anyone *and* it creates disharmony and division between players.

On the one hand, I understand where the people replying are coming from.  It can be tiring to see the same three posts worded differently and recycled by a dozen different users over the course of a week.  However, "calling them out", or otherwise making a spectacle of them, **doesn't help anyone**.  Yeah, you might get your point across and maybe have a *laugh* in the process, but have you stopped to consider that your negative reply might have a *lasting impact* that's **detrimental** to both the player base and the server, itself?
By being rude or otherwise intolerant, you might deter a player from wanting to participate with the community, or even worse, they might not play on the server, anymore.  You can sit there and say "good riddance" all you want, but at some point, this could come back and bite you – or worse still, it could come back and bite *the server*.  You never know.  That "low level idiot" who wants his Level 11 Hardcore character revived might have access to some service-disrupting thing.  But even *without* the threat of the server going down under "mysterious circumstances"… it is really *that hard* to be *civil*?

I know my words are falling on deaf ears, as they often do here, but… if this is supposed to be a "respectful and friendly community", then maybe y'all should start *acting* the part.  At the very least, if you aren't going to help your fellow players, then *keep your acidic words to yourselves*.  While a little kindness can go a long way, simply keeping your opinions to yourself can go just as far – especially if your opinions are *condemning* and/or *hurtful*.

"Give peace a chance."  That's all it takes.
