# A year and a half playing on Turtle

## Post 114668 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-28T17:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114668#p114668 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm making this post to leave my review after so much time playing here.

I've seen Turtle evolve since 1.16.5, there was no Gilneas or Hyjal, there was no Thalassian Highlands or Blackstone Island, the new races still had some visual bugs, etc.

I'm very surprised to see the effort that the Turtle team puts into everything, to see how they actively fix bugs, to see how the quality of the custom zones is improving over time, to see how even the maps have been improving.

This year has been full of surprises, the new client in Unreal Engine, that they have shown the Roadmap that they will follow the following year.

In general, it's very nice to see how the development team really cares about offering something new to attract new players, since originally I understand that Turtle was just a Vanilla server and suddenly someone came up with the idea of ​​introducing 1.15.0...and that's where it all started.

Although I would like to see some old zones that were introduced in older patches get a revamp:

Tirisfal Highlands for example, feels a bit incomplete and empty compared to recent zones like the High Elves or Goblins.

Overall, I've never been so happy on a private server, Turtle is actually the first server where I've reached level 60 and where I've started doing PvP and Raids eventually, even though the rank advancement is slow and the drop rates make me insult mobs because they don't give me what I want.

Its community has been objectively beautiful, quite calm and kind. Roleplaying, which I had never paid attention to, I do here happily, I even joined a guild that does roleplaying.

For a private server, I've been very surprised, and I look forward to many more years from Turtle, I look forward to exploring Grim Reaches or Quel'thalas in that Unreal Engine client that has everyone excited.

I just have to say:

 ---THANK YOU VERY MUCH TURTLE WOW TEAM, I HOPE FOR MANY MORE YEARS OF TRAVELING AROUND AZEROTH ON A TURTLE...SLOW AND STEADY---
