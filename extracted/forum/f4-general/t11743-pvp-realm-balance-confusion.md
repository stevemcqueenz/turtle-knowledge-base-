## Post 77635 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-16T10:07:00Z

I think people compare PVP state of Turtle WoW to vanilla one. For some people vanilla was nearly perfect in terms of PVP balance. They used to play it for years on other private servers so many players actually knew the meta well.

Turtle devs changed the classes/specs which undoubtedly shook the balance of the PVP. Meta has changed. Veterans of vanilla have been confused by new turtle balance.

There are also people who simply suck and don't understand that some classes counter others or are so naive to think it's easy to balance every class in every case: WPVP, BGs, arena. It isn't so easy.

Personally, I think that ret Paladin is still a bit overpowered in PVP. Back in vanilla the spec was easy spec to play but hard to master. Good rets existed but they were minority. Now this spec is even more popular and buffed here. Old ret veterans have got new tools to be even more powerful than they were in vanilla.
