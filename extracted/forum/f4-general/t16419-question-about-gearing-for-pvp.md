## Post 113037 by Soulforged — 2024-12-17T14:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113037#p113037 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all ask yourself why you want to PvP.
If you find it fun and want the gear then sure go for it:)
Secondly i would say decide how you want to approach it(DPS or Support healer) cause if your goal is high ranks you are are looking at many months of grinding. You can always respec if you feel bored but you need some gear to support it.

I would suggest to do raiding to get SOME gear cause if you enter as a fresh green/blue 60 you will suffer.
To my experience so far most players have already digested most of PvE content and decide to do the other aspect of the game which is PvP, as a byproduct of that they are well geared already.

As for the PvP gearing system itself you can find many many guides and videos.
