But now I see that there is big difference in population between old and new server, in favor of an old one, so I am afraid that taking into consideration that there is no crossfaction and it is pvp server, there will not be enough people interested in raids.

In the other hand, I am bit afraid of starting on an old server, because, well, it is old. There are plenty of people fully geared and everyone is probably raiding only Naxxramas, so i will miss lot of content. Or maybe I am wrong and people still raid lower tiers and make their own 'progression'? But if so, it is probably big minority and there is no guarantee I will find spot in such group.

To sum up what is the most important for me:
- raiding from lower to higher tiers to do not miss raiding content

- pvp in battlegrounds and arena
I do not care about world pvp.

So I would like to ask you guys, about your opinion - which server will be better for me?
