Brainwashing device is a bit tricky, this is how it works:

You get two free slots, where you can save your talents.
You can load any saved talents without a cost.
You can also reset your talents. Reseting talents costs the normal amounts.
You can have more slots for gold.

Let's say, you are leveling and you are level 35, bear and want a healer offspec.
 1. You save your current talents in a slot.
 2. You reset your talent with a normal price.
 3. You select your healer talents and save them to the other free slot.

The device will only remember the talents you saved there.
So let's say you ding level 36 and use the device to be a healer. You will have the same talents as at level 35 with a free talent point.
But you can overwrite the old saves on the device.
I suggest saving your talent selection for both specs after each level up.
If you forget it, you might end up with a level 55 druid healer and 20 free talent points, unused.

One thing to keep in mind: if you wanna tweak your spec, that's usually a reset talent (normal price) and save the tweaked spec in a slot. So f.ex. you figure out you want another talent for your bear or you took something you regret -> the act of reseting wil cost you but saving and loading will not.

PS: You can also get it for in-game gold. I think around 350 in STV.
