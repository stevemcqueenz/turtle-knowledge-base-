## Post 42812 by Nandiebs (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-24T09:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42812#p42812 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

it's a really annoying matchup for hunter. the paladin has to play really badly for the hunter to win.

 - paladin can catch the hunter with repentance
 - traps and wingclip/concussive shot are mostly useless because of blessing of freedom
 - only reliable cc to gain some distance is scatter shot, 30s cooldown
 - once the paladin is in range for hammer of justice, it's pretty much over because that stun duration is enough to kill a hunter
 - your pet does little damage to him and just builds up his reckoning charges
 - viper sting is useless because the mana cost is higher than the pala's mana cost to cleanse it, so it will actually drain the hunter's mana faster than the pala's

## Post 42828 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-04-24T10:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42828#p42828 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Its a matchup id genuinely avoid from either side. While both can theoretically kill eachother, if both classes have their essential stuff up (bubble/ pvp trinket respectively) it can go on for a long time ans will more often than not get decided by terrain (e.g. hunter runs out of space) or interference by another class.

Rank one viper sting to punish dispells on the paladin side and deterrence to survive the bof/bubble duration even the playing field.

Its just death by a thousand cuts kind of battle, where hunter has to grind out for a long time while paladin has massive troubles to connect but if he does he has decent chances.

I think without an objective nearby its easy to trap eachother in such an engagement for a long time, so its favorable for the team that is currently winning the bg or for the player whose reinfoecements arrive sooner ;)

## Post 42836 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-04-24T12:36:00Z

Depends on the battleground, but lets say you defend mine as a hunter. If a single paladin is trying to cap, you have so much space and all you need to do is mirror his movement. You dont even need to kill him, just maintain the status quo (e.g. your team keeping mine under control) to be winning.

Of course at the other end of the extreme, if you run up an av tower and a paladin is waiting on top of the stairs behind the pillar, you have no business winning this fight as a hunter, since its such a close quarters environment.

So the matchup largely depends on these outside factors. Catching the paladin when he is not mounted etc also makes a huge difference on the outcome.
