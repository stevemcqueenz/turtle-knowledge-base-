 **The Summoning**

Across Azeroth warlocks of all creeds and races receive an invitation, delivered by an imp whose master sent him. In a plume of fel fire he appears before you, with his devilish grin and burning eyes he hands you a letter which reads:

Greetings my dark brother,

 *Excuse the impudence of my pathetic servant, he will be punished later. Let it be known that you are cordially invited to join me and my acolytes in a grand summoning. Take this invitation and proceed to the Burning Steppes. We shall gather at the foot of Blackrock Mountain. There, we shall depart to our destination. The date of our meeting shall begin on the 27th of this moon cycle. Do not delay.*

Signed,
Kazgrim Dreadskull

 **Event this Friday the 27th. Begins 20:00 server time**

(Although warlocks were specified, anyone can join!)

## Post 5553 by Kazgrim (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-03-27T22:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5553#p5553 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Kazgrim led his acolytes up the dark and forbidding path, reaching the summit after a day's journey. Before them a great altar stood erect, and a coven of warlocks surrounded it, their faces shrouded by the shadow of their cloaks.

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/693206234324795443/WoWScrnShot_032720_165457.jpg)

"Slay them all!" shouted Kazgrim. His acolytes unleashed a terrible pain upon their unsuspecting assailants, destroying their bodies and capturing their souls. When the dust settled, Kazgrim stood ontop of the great altar and looked down upon his followers.

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/693206239731122196/WoWScrnShot_032720_161257.jpg)

One by one his acolytes began to kneel, raising their hands towards the sky. They uttered dark incantations and nefarious curses, their ritual echoing the shrieks of demonic entities. Kazgrim grinned with delight, placing his hand on the stone altar to complete the ritual.

A thunderous cry rang out, and a dark shadow appeared in front of Kazgrim. A towering demon stood before him and his acolytes, who now groveled to the floor in cowardice. Without a word, the demon plucked one of the acolytes and devoured him whole. Kazgrim erupted in laughter before ordering his minions to slay the beast. When its body laid dead on the altar's table, Kazgrim trapped its soul within a crystal vial.

Part 1 of the ritual had been completed. Now, Kazgrim required the blood of a servant. He gathered his followers and headed to Dreadmaul rock. There, he requested one of his minions to lay upon the altar. Garzog eagerly accepted.

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/693206238644928563/WoWScrnShot_032720_165758.jpg)

The loyalty of his acolytes wavered as they watched in horror as Kazgrim plunged a blade into the heart of his most loyal servant. Little did Kazgrim know, but Knights Templar agents had infiltrated his coven. They turned against Kazgrim and his acolytes, and dealt a mortal blow to his corporeal body.

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/693206220508495882/WoWScrnShot_032720_170547.jpg)

But when the fighting had edned, Kazgrim's body was not completely destroyed, and so Azteq gathered his remains and carried them off. Soon soul and flesh would be re-united, and Kazgrim would return to exact his vengeance.
