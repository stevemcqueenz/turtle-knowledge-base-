I’ve bought donation skins a few times. You can swap between ones you own as much as you want, the default look doesn’t return unless there’s a “no skin” option. Taking it off doesn’t consume anything. On HC I lost a char last week, the skin disappeared with the char but I got the same amount of tokens back to spend. Not account-wide in my experience, my alt needed its own purchase. For CS2 I usually trade instead of rolling RNG, I’ve used <https://skinsmonkey.com/blog/best-sg-553-skins> for picks and price ranges. They cover fast bot trading and cheaper routes than case opening.
