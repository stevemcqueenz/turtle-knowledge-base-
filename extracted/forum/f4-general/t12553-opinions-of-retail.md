## Post 83843 by Toaonyk — 2024-01-06T20:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83843#p83843 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

> 1. What things do you want of retail for be here? like mechanics, cities, races, new places, etc.

The one thing I like the most from retail (and which came far too late) are Mythic+ instances. Though implementing these would kinda defeat the purpose of the vanilla experience.

> 2. What's your opinion about the allied races?

The idea of allied races as such is good - after all, races are merely an afterthought in WoW, so why not do away with the concept of introducing new baseline races and make all new races allied by default.

> 10. You think if Blizzard include a popular race in the Alliance, like the Quel'dorei, that could fix the population disbalance in retail?
>  11. You think the Void Elves are a good replace for the Quel'dorei?

I didn't even know that there's a notable faction imbalance that favors in retail; but I doubt it would change much. However, I would still be in favor of giving Alliance High Elves; since the reasons that might speak against them are null and void by now:

 1. The Pandaren already set the precedent of a cross faction-race being okay, so that reason goes out of the window.
 2. The lore reason of High Elves not being numerous enough is also gone (which was the pretense they gave us back in 2007 for adding BEs but not HEs) since we got VEs instead, which arguably are even fewer.
 3. The faction balance, which was the actual reason behind the Horde getting their "pretty" race (and which made a lot of sense back then), seems to be gone as well.

All in all, Blizzard introducing VEs but steadfastly refusing to add HEs feels like pure spite: the latter have been asked for for decades by now, but Alliance players gave their shittier looking emo-style discount version nobody ever wanted instead.

> 4. You want Varian Wrynn back here?

I don't care.

> 5. What's your opinion about the Draenei and the Blood Elves in retail?

I think they were decent additions. Maybe they didn't make that much sense, but they looked good and solved some back then really pressing faction balance problems, both regarding numbers as well as regarding classes.

> 6. What's your opinion about the Worgen and the Goblins?

The Goblins made a lot of sense (though they seem to be less popular than even the trolls, and that's saying something), the Worgen less so - they're a case of "Ascended Fanon" and the devs went with it; presumably because they were somewhat stylish and they didn't have any idea what to do instead.

> 7. What's your opinion about the decitions of Blizzard in the most lore retcons that the have done?
>  9. You think that retcons can be fixed?

The ones in Warcraft are okay, but those are mostly soft retcons anyway. The ones in WoW are mostly pretty dumb and tear up continent-sized plot holes - though I wouldn't say they're unfixable.
The introduction of the Draenei-Eredar for example required a lot of suspension of disbelief, but imho the Draenei/Argus-storyline got a satisfying conclusion in Legion - so it's not that they're not salvageable.

> 8. What's the worst retcon in retail?

That Zovaal was behind everything.
