Has anyone witnessed any suspicious discord activity on either the TWoW disc or your guild's discord?  I recently found 3 users that joined over the past week that had no servers in common and all had our profession tags added.  They also all had the same numeric naming scheme. It is also a possibility that it was a mobile app limitation, however I doubt that's the case as I was able to interact/kick the users mentioned above.

If anyone has any insight, I'd appreciate hearing it.  Otherwise, I advise folks to watch out who joins their discord.
