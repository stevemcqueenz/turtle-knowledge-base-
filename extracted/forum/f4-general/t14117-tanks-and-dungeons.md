I think there is core problem in vanilla about tanking or the way people want to play in the game especially in leveling bracket. Most people prefer tanking with build that has talents/spells suitable for playing this role. It's impossible in vanilla WoW in leveling phase.
Because there is only one spec people can play. People try to optimize their gameplay so they usually choose dps talents because they will kill mobs and level up solo much faster than in other specs. One set of talents - let's be honest not many people under level 60 have Goblin Brainwashing Device - doesn't help either. Ret paladins are afraid to try tanking, even some warriors or druids too in dps spec. There is more chance they would try if they had talents adjusted to their tanking role but as I said most people choose dps talents anyway.

There are also some new players to the game that visit the dungeon for the first time in their life so they don't want to slow down the group even if they could tank.

I saw a suggestion to add extra rewards (loot, exp, gold) to tanks and healers though I find this idea stupid because I can't imagine the system that rewards the player only by playing specific role, I find it against vanilla spirit - it's anti-immersive when you get reward out of thin air.

I suggest nothing specific in that regard, maybe making QoL changes to current tanking classes could be a good step. After all, tank is most wanted role in the game. Playing tank is currently benefitial without any other encouragements compared to other roles because you will find spot in the groups relatively fast - almost instantly.
