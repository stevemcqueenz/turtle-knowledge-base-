I have played WoW many times on many different expansions. On blizzard and on private servers. I understand the appeal of classic and i do prefer it over any other expansion. But classic has many issues that were addressed over time, especially on such an old patch like this. I joined the server and saw that there was a need for tanks, so i rolled a warrior, my favorite class and decided to go prot. In this patch, you can't thunderclap in defensive stance, and your taunt has a 5 YARD RANGE. This is not challenging, or ''sprit of classic'' this is utterly depressing. The server is already a different game than classic so the whole ideia off '' no changes'' doesn't fly here. Some spells were just garbage in classic and if you are changing some and adding new class/race combination, others need to as well.
