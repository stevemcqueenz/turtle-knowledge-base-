The two are entirely different. WoW does not offer a vanilla client whatsoever. Even its classic version is a higher game version that mechanically is very different from the vanilla client, despite other similarities with how the game plays.

You will need to download Turtle's client and keep it separate from WoW's clients if you intend to play both.
