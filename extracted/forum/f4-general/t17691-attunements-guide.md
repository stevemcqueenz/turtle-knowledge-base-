At level 60 it might be overwhelming for new players to get attuned to all the raids and soft attunements to dungeons.  Soft attunements are keys that are required to get the door opened but others can open the door for you.  I would consider the UBRS door as a soft attunement even though it is more rare than the other attunements.

This guide posted [here](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/classic-wow-attunements-and-keys-dungeons-raids) is a really good cheat sheet to view all the keys and attunements required between lvl 40-60.

 **Emerald Sanctum Attunement**

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/DyjIwvi.png)

Patch [1.17.1](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=5391) added the Hyjal zone as well as the Emerald Sanctum Attunement.

1. [Into the Dream I](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40957): This quest chain starts in Hyjal just go through Ralathius' story and then accept the next part of the quest.
2. [Into the Dream II](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40958): Next Ralathius will ask you to collect 3 [Nightmare Effigy](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61556).  These have a relatively low drop rate of 10% from any saytr in Hyjal.
3. [Into the Dream III](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40959): You're going to need 3 items from this next quest.  It's important to do Slumberer's Shard last to avoid coming back to the Swamp of Sorrows again to talk to Itharius in the cave to the south west.

 - [Binding Fragment](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61558) go to Azshara and kill the elite Cliff Giants.

 - [Overloaded Arcane Prism](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61559) is obtainable in the DMW dungeon after killing the tree boss and killing one of the patrolling Arcane Torrents.

 - [Slumberer's Shard](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61557) in Sunken Temple we need to loot the Weaver which is a boss that gets summoned after killing the bosses on the edges of the circle and then killing Jammal'an the Prophet.
4. [Into the Dream IV](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40960): Return to Hyjal after talking to Itharius to then talk to Ralathius
5. [Into the Dream V](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40961): Ralathius will do a long RP session.  After a while some shadow mobs will spawn and you will need to kill them all.  After that he will finally give the next part of the quest
6. [Into the Dream VI](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40962): The final part of this quest is to kill Larannikus and loot his quest item.  This will require about 3-5 players to kill him.  Bring at least a healer/tank/dps.
Just like the Onyxia attunement you must keep your Gemstoneof Ysera in your bags to get inside of the Emerald Sanctum.

 **Cla'ckora Summoning**

Each time you want to kill the Crab Boss you will need to collect the idols and summon him every time.  I am unsure what the respawn time on Cla'cora or if there are just reagent requirements.

START: Pick up the Ramblings of Ol' Gazeno at the Zandalar Tribe Rep Vendor in Stranglethorn Vale.

You will need to assemble the Idol, to do this you need three pieces. A top, middle and bottom. The order you obtain these does not matter.
1. Mizunta's Piece ([Top Piece of an Ancient Idol](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56083))
Head to the Northern Coast of Darkshore and speak to the Tuskarr to take a boat to Icepoint Rock. Fish off the docks until you catch a rare quality fish and open it for the Idol Piece.
2. Kelem'bo's Piece ([Middle Piece of an Anceint Idol](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56084))
This piece drops off of Ghaz'ranka in ZG, however it is not a 100% drop rate, meaning that you may get unlucky. To summon Ghaz'ranka you need a Mudskunk Lure and 5 Zulian Mudskunks. Good luck!
3. Ghante's Piece ([Bottom Piece of an Anceint Idol](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56085))
Head to Tel'abim, I chose to fish up near the Troll Ruins just to the East of Bixxle's Storehouse but you may be able to fish it anywhere.
(As of writing this, this zone kinda sucks and you get a lot of 'dead casts' with no loot)
You will eventually fish up a Barnacle Covered Key which you will need to take into the Gorilla cave (Entrance at 56,43) and hug the right wall until you see a stone table with a chest on it. This holds the Idol Piece.

 **Scarlet Crusade Attunement**

This attunement has been in the game for a while now, but it still has no use and might never have a use but I thought I would throw it in here as an honorable mention.

 **Alliance Side Attunement**

1. [A Particular Letter](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80700)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Accept this quest from Crier Goodman and then immediately complete it to receive the Seal Letter.
2. [The Elusive SI:7](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80701)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Right click on the Sealed Letter in your inventory and accept this quest.  Go to the Old Town in Stormwind and talk to Mathias Shaw.
3. [Young and Foolish](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80702)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Go to Lakeshire now to speak to each of the peeps on his list.  Then return to Mathias.
4. [Caravan Meets Caravan](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80730)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Go to wetlands and seek out clues near the caravan that is burning to the east of the road heading up towards Arathi Highlands. First clue is going to be the Dwarf Mountaineer. Second clue is going to be the oil cannister.  Third clue is going to be the small wooden crate with a ring inside.  Next go to Kixxle the goblin on the road to the south of the caravan to complete the quest. It's important to DELETE the ring you get or the script will not spawn the Scarlet Crusade people.
5. [Thandol Span](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80703)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Go north to the big bridge crossing Wetlands and Arathi Highlands.  This quest gets bugged out if you cross into the bridge too quickly.  Don't even get close to the bridge stay around 100 yards away from the bridge and wait for the ambush to start.  two waves of scarlet crusade members will fight you.  Vladeus Springriver will come to fight you as well.  When you get him low he will follow you back to Menethil Harbour at walking speed   crying_turtle  .  Take him to the keep of Menethil. This keep is positioned in the center of Menethil you can't miss it!  Complete the quest at the top of the keep by talking to Captain Stoutfist.
6. [Are You True to Your Nature?](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80704)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Accept the quest and go down to the basement to the Menethil keep.  Complete the quest from the scroll on the table.  From here there are two choices you can use the iron maiden or the coffee cup to persuade Vladeus.
7. [Seeking Justice or Vengeance?](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80706)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Get this quest from the Iron Maiden object next to Vladeus.  Then select the option "You smash your right fist into his sides." and continue on his dialog. Complete the quest at the end by clicking on the Incompleted Interrogation Report on the table.
8. [The Price Of Information](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80707)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Accept this quest from the scroll on the table and report back to Captain Stoutfist with your findings.
9. [Scarlet Aid](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80708)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Come to Stormwind and talk to Brother Crowley.  He is in the cathedral to the right of Archbishop Benedictus down the ramp.  He will give you a disguise to put on when doing the next quest.
10. [Donning the Red Flag](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80709)![Image](https://i.imgur.com/uAbTCOR.gif): Go to Mavel Brightwood to complete and accept the next quest. Remember to use your disguise while you are in Eastern Plaguelands!
11. [It’s All in Their Brains](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80710): Mavel will request for you to kill undead all over the Plaguelands for their brains.  The database says around 80 to 90% drop rate on these brains.  Just hack and slash your way to victory!  Come back to Mavel.  The disguise in your inventory has charges to it so even if you log out you can still talk to Mavel.
12. [Supplies We Need](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=80711): Mavel wants 50 Savage Fronds and 50 Core of Elements for the last part of this quest.  It is advised to buy these off the AH.  In total the mats aren't going to be over 3g in cost.  Unless these mats go way higher in the future.
Congratz Alliance players! You are now the proud owner of the attunement for Scarlet Crusade raid when it comes out.  Soon TM!
