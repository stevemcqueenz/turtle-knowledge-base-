# Illusional  normalization gear system for arena

## Post 148674 by shadowchaos — 2025-08-27T15:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148674#p148674 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What does pvp enthusiasts always say?
Skill ,man ,it's skill.
So I assume you wouldn't mind have a fight with equivalent geared people ,would you?

So here comes my proposal, and I name it as "Illusional  normalization gear system for arena".

 1.Set up a serial of normalized gear sets.
Let's say top end raid has an average item level 90 ,so for rated arena rating and corresponding gear item level are :
item level 85   ---   2800 rating
item level 80   ---   2600 rating
item level 75   ---   2400 rating
item level 70   ---   2200 rating
item level 65   ---   2000 rating
item level 60   ---   1800 rating
Here items include all but only one ring and no trinket besides insignia.
And compares to the same level pve piece ,these arena ones favor more stamina and more reduced damage (crit damage and over time damage )taken ,so full end game raider and top arena pvper may have total 60 item levels gap ,but that really comes minimal impact from my view.

 2.Illusional gear up for arenaers
When click to enter arena ,your gears are locked up except weapons (melee and ranged) which means you can't change gear pieces anymore in arena .
System will do a math to calculate everyone's average geared items level .
Here ,only counted gear parts are "head ,neck ,shoulder ,cloak ,chest ,wrist ,hand ,waist , leg ,boots and two rings" that total 12 slots.
Only the highest average item level person remains untouched ,everyone else will swap to the nearest gear set but below ones to the highest person .
For example :
 2 vs 2 arena
team 1 has an average item level 78.72 person and 75.77 person ,
team 2 has an average item level 61.12 person and 59.11 person ,
than team 1 78.72 person's gear will be untouched , his mate 75.77 person will be geared with full item level 75 arena set besides one ring and one trinket as the same as team 2 that two persons .
So when this battle opens ,it's a team 1 item level 78.72 with item level 75 arena set VS team 2 2 item level 75 arena set persons.
And some exceptions are when the weapon has hit on chance characters ,it will not be swapped no matter it's item levels and the items has hit on use effect will be not swapped too ,like rocket helmet.

 3.Side effect on some potions and spells.
To make it fair and classic experience ,some potions and spells should have side effect when use.
Limited Invulnerability Potion : when use ,the imbiber's damage and healing ability and the healing receive is reduced by 50% during it's effect time. So this may go the same to pally's protection and divine shield.
Free Action Potion : when use , the imbiber's damage and healing ability and the healing receive is reduced by 30% during it's effect time.
All kind of greater elemental protection potions should have damage and healing ability and healing receive reduced by 25% .
And no flask allowed in arena .
Numbers can be adjust but it should has this kind of punishment.

 4.Only reset insignia's cd every time when you enter the arena.
So this rule will keep the classic feel that you can use every engineering stuff and rare trinket that keeps the same as outside world ,and it's fair that you can't use it every time .

 5.Consider spells that has CD greater than 15 mins will not be reset every time you get into arena.

 6.Proper DR system
ALL CC only up to 10 seconds.
Incapacitates share the same DR : sheep ,sap ,gouge ,blind ,grenade ,rocket helmet ,mind control cap ,Dark Edge of Insanity 's hit on chance effect ...
Special stun : cheap shot and pounce.
Stuns : all stuns besides special stun.
Fear and charm and mind control.
Improved counterspell and silence and spell lock .

So with this system ,a flesh new char will no problem to play with high end uber geared person and through every season to get this illusion gear to come true.
And also satisfy pvp enthusiasts every fight is your skill proved.
