The whole topic is moot for one reason: If someone's going in a specific role, and they don't communicate to the party in advance what they're after, they have no right to roll on something that doesn't fit their role OR right to complain if people start shitting on them if they do roll on it. Note the OR is very important here, before people jump down my throat. You make your intentions clear or you accept the consequences of your assumptions not lining up with the rest of the party's.

This is a social game. Openly communicate what your designs are in advance so everyone's on the same page. It's not that hard not to be socially autistic in 2022.
