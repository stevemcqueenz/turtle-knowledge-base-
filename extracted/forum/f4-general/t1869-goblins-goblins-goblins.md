I very recently was made aware about this server and thought I would check it out, the main draw being the possibility of playing new races and seeing expanded content while still playing Vanilla.

I must congratulate the team on the High Elves, they are superb. From armor, NPCs, quests, even racials and class choice, absolutely excellent. The thing is though, everyone loves elves. Half the server seems to be High Elves. But what about the poor goblins?

I already see you are aware that there is a great deal of work to be done re: animations, emotes, clipping on them, so I won't re-iterate that. However, obviously the Helves are using TBC models, and goblins currently are using the pixeltastic original vanilla model. I think it would be an wonderful idea to use the Cata goblins if possible (not the stupid cartoony new ones, but the original, re-done first pass of gobbos that actually look mean yet not a total mess).

I really hope some love is given to the goblins soon, as atm they feel very forgotten and discarded compared to the Helves. Also, perhaps consider giving them Priest or Shaman? Healers are always needed   smiling_turtle_head
