## Post 153360 by Mastermind90 — 2025-09-26T16:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153360#p153360 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm not good at English so I'll try to explain in a few words.

It's understandable to say that WoW Vanilla was never truly balanced in PvP, but at least it had some kind of logic you could rely on. Skilled players could turn things around with their ability. Now, that feels almost impossible.

Some classes constantly deal explosive damage. Balance druids, for example, can now run and attack at the same time, with sustained damage and great healing, since they’re hybrids. They used to do it before, but now it’s even
worse almost like a Warlock with extra dots.

Paladins, even though they're not as bad as before, can still press one button and unleash a rain of damage.

And people say paladins aren’t that strong, that they don’t need nerfs, and that players just need to (learn to play) but then why do I see groups with 9 paladins in WSG or AB? It’s because you want easy wins.

If it were just a normal class, it wouldn’t be this dominant in the meta, right?

Same goes for shamans. Seeing AB games with 8 shamans is just disgusting. IT SUCKS.

What do you want? 10 paladins on Alliance in WSG and 10 shamans on Horde? That’s not fun.

I’ll finish with a tear for the warriors. I don’t even play warrior, but seriously… poor guys. I cry for them. They’ve become easy targets.

To be honest, people who play paladin or shaman probably lost a lot in the past, and now they just want to win by smashing random buttons.

You're the ones who need to learn how to play not the people who refuse to pick the most broken classes in the current meta.
