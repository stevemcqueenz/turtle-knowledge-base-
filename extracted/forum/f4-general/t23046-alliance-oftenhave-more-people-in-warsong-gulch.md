I am participating in Warsong Gulch to improve my reputation with Warsong Outriders. I should say I joined enough battlegrounds to make such a claim.
I noticed this issue is noticeable after 2025 — November 12 patch.

> Battleground Queue System will now prioritize matching you with your own faction before considering a Cross‑Faction swap.

I really do not know if this is related to match making system or the alliance doing some shenanigans while joining, but it is very annoying especially in WSG. I wonder what are people think about this issue.
