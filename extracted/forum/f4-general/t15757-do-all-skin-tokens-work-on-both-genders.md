# Do all Skin Tokens work on both Genders?

## Post 107447 by Moongirl — 2024-11-10T21:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107447#p107447 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there! I hope you are having a nice day!   smiling_turtle_head

In the donation shop menu, it says **"Greyed out entries are not usable by your race, class or gender."** I assume this would count towards Skin Tokens as well. However, I'm a little confused since the tokens themselves no longer specify if they are gender specific.

For example, Undead Skin Token "Touch of Darkness" was Male only. while "Blood Widow" was Female Only. But they are no longer greyed out for the opposite gender. **Does this mean they work for both genders or is this an oversight?**

I was personally going to get the Night Elf Necromancer Skin Token, but before it was Male only, and I'm not sure if Females have it now. There are a couple other like this, like Human Frost Affinity, Orc Dreadskull, and Human Death Knight. There was a forum post on which ones worked, but it is currently outdated from what I can tell.

Any help is appreciated! Thank you for reading, and good luck on your journey.   smiling_turtle_head

## Post 107454 by Glaive (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-10T23:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107454#p107454 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi

I can help with this.  The best way to see what is what with the skins is to make a new character, take the hardcore challenge, and then buy what you're interested in previewing.  After you're done looking, you can die with that character and your tokens will be refunded.  I have done this a number of times now.  One of the times I did it, I tried both undead skins.  Both can be used on a male character and both are exactly the same.

Hope that helps!
