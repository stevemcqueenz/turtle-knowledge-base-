## Post 35072 by Shamma (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-02-17T16:18:00Z

A guard that accepts tokens and rewards what u would expect them to reward (pledges and cards) in Org can be found in front of the General Store/Trade goods vendors (entering Orgrimmar to the left).

In UC a guard that accepts tokens can be found on the outer ring of the warriors quarter (the left most pair of guards).

In TB the guard at the elevator of the main rise works.

The whole event is buggy and there is no point in actually doing this really, cos u cannot turn in the gift collections anyway. The npc does not accept them anymore.
