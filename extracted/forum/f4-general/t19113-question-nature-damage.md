## Post 130519 by FrankFankledank (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-05-11T00:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130519#p130519 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Items and consumables with associated elemental damages for the most part are not intended to scale with spell power, even if it's that specific damage type's. There are a small number of items that do though, most likely as oversights and Turtle has 'fixed' some of them like Frost/Shadow Oil.

As for the reasoning? Likely because this would give classes that naturally equip spell power gear an inherent advantage maximizing the use out of those types of items over ones who would have to sacrifice vital Strength/Agility to do so.
