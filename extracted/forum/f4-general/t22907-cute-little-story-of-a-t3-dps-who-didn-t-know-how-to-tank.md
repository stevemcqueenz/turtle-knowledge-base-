# Cute little story of a T3 dps who didn't know how to tank

## Post 159929 by Matches (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-11-17T19:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159929#p159929 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So, I gotsa cute lil story tell y'all.

Imma tank, ready to craft all my dark iron gear, need dark anvil.

Form up a BRD, we head in.

Four of us to start with, fifth is 60 shammy and he's long way away.

We make a start, after a while he rolls in.

He's got Sulfuron, and looks like T3 or something fancy...

...and he's starting to pull packs, rather than me doing it.

We pretty quickly pull two groups (I'm doing my normal pulling and he's semi doing his own thing) and finish them off.

I encourage him not to do this, because it's risky and you can wipe.

He immediately pulls another pack, on dark iron road - like 30 seconds later - like immediately after I've spoken to him.

I say - you keep doing this, I'm going to kick you.

He calms down, we go proceed dark iron road up to Incendius, which he then directly goes to and pulls.

So he's tanking, healer is healing him, the dps are doing their thing, I'm tapping my foot.

I'm thinking about kicking him, but I think he was there for dark anvil and we were there, and I didn't want to be a dick.

So this guy, he's tanking Incendius, but... ...he's moved to the edge of the disk platform the dark anvil is on - with his back to the lava.

Incendius has a knock-back special, which he uses.

Shaman goes flying into the lava and T3 or not, fried to a crisp and dies.

I mean, what can you do, right?  you tell a dps not to pull, they pull anyway, and then get themselves killed.

So I take over, we tank the mob and we're good.

I craft my gear.

We can't res the shammy, corpse too far away.

He has to walk back to instance and back to us.

He then swears at me in Cyrillic (I read enough to know what he wrote) and then tells me in English I'm a crybaby and I'll never be a good player.

He carries on with us for a few more minutes, then without saying anything, he HS's back to Ogrimmar I think it was, abandoning the party.

We carry on and get most of what everyone else needed done.
