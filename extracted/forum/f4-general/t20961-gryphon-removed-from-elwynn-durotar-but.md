# Gryphon removed from Elwynn /Durotar, but...

## Post 145778 by Aloka — 2025-08-15T16:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145778#p145778 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey there.

 **I absolutely don't understand why such small quality of life things, like this griffin from Goldshire and its counterpart from Razorhill, are being removed.**
The patch notes only say: *"Removed the Elwynn Forest and Durotar Gryphon and Wyvern, as this design is not used in other zones."*

If this had really been the reason, they could have simply equipped each starting area with a flying mount that suited the respective race.
Furthermore, if so much emphasis is placed on fairness and members of other races apparently cannot be expected to take the ship to Stormwind to benefit from the Gryphon, then it makes even less sense that these same players are forced to come to Stormwind for full weapons training.
As a night elf, for example, you are obligated to travel to Ironforge and Stormwind to train all available weapons for your class. In this context, justifying the removal of the Gryphon and Wyvern with fairness seems implausible and far-fetched.

It's nice that some kind of rental mount with 40% speed is offered as a "replacement". But not everyone benefits from this. Furthermore, new players will have a hard time spending 1 silver every time they ride a few meters, because the mount only lasts until the player dismounts or is unmounted by enemies.

As a level 40+ character with a 60% mount, you don't benefit at all...
On the other hand, everyone benefited from the Gryphon/Wyvern.
Why are enjoyable aspects of this game being removed instead of improved? It makes no sense, and as a paying customer, I consider this change a loss and therefore vote for the reinstatement of temporary flying mounts.
