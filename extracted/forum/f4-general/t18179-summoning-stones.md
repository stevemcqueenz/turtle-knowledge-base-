# Summoning Stones.

## Post 123773 by Stormthrash — 2025-03-25T16:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123773#p123773 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I understand that this is a controversial topic, and that the twow dev team has pretty much took a concrete stance against introducing Summoning Stones, but I think it should be talked about regardless. It takes me more than thirty minutes (I've had to wait up to an hour and a half before) to find a group via group finder for a dungeon, even as a tank. Through this, I have no choice but to go to the dungeon entrance, and wait this whole time, so if I do find a group, I don't inconvenience them by having to travel by boat and take a flight path. This means that I can spend up to an hour and a half to wait for a 30-45 minute run at the entrance of said dungeon.

I thought it was just me, and that I was alone in this mindset of desiring Summoning Stones. I believed that other players didn't desire them because they believe it did take away from the experience of the game... until I started asking party members. I'd say a good 90% of people that I've partied with believe that summoning stones would be a boon, while the other 10% didn't care. This isn't just one group either, this is a majority opinion from what I've come to gather through my questions. It's even believed that it would increase the dungeon queing activity because people wouldn't have to abandon questing in desolace to que for scarlet monestary while they wait. I can confirm that this was my mindset, as I was hesitant about queing due to the journey I'd have to make and would often just do quests and grind as dungeon quests remained untouched.

With all this being said, how would summoning stones effect the game? Why is it such a controversial topic?
