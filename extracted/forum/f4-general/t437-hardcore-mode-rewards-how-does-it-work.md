# Hardcore mode & Rewards - How does it work?

## Post 2232 by Jcarrill0 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2019-03-03T00:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2232#p2232 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings TurtleWow Players,
Since I have first created [Hardcore mode Character](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=4&t=411), I have gotten Questions about what it is, and what rewards come with it. Below is a Quote from Turtle Wow Staff:
> **Torta wrote: Mon Oct 29, 2018 10:45 pm**
> **Progressive Rates** *(Non-Hardcore)*
>
>  We have developed a system to regulate the kill XP rates for level ranges where it’s necessary, due to a lack of quests, such as 20-25, 40-42.
>
>  The changes will be:
>
> - 01-10 — x0.7
>
> - 11-20 — x0.8
>
> - 21-35 — x0.8
>
> - 36-45 — x0.9
>
> - 46-50 — x0.8
>
> - 51-55 — x0.8
>
> - 56-60 — x0.7
>
> - Exploration: x0.5
>
>  **Normalization for Dungeons Rates**
>
>  Will remain x1.0 for parties only to encourage players to group up and do dungeons.
>
>  **“I still want it slow!”**
>
>  Answer: No worries! Speak to Speedy and get an item that will reduce your XP back to x0.5 (Hardcore mode).
>
>  Choose wisely, as once you decide to level on lower rates, you will not be able to return to the higher rates.
>
>  Here is a list of the new rewards you can claim for playing on hardcore mode:
>
>  Level 20:
> - Portable Meeting Stone
>
> - Unique Pet
>  Level 30:
> - Unique Pet
>  Level 40:
> - Fast Purple Tallstrider
>  Level 50:
> - Unique Tabard
>
> - 20 Slot Backpack
>  Level 60:
> - 250 Turtle Tokens
>
> - Swift Riding Turtle
>
>  **Hardcore mode will now only be available up until level 10**. If you do not select it by this level, you will remain on normal mode without the option to change.
>
>  We hope you enjoy the new rewards.
>  We reserve the right to change rewards if we feel like we have something better to offer :)
>
>  Riding Skill will not return as hardcore reward.

 *I have consolidated this quote to help new players and other curious on how it works here so it's easy to find/understand.*

There you have it folks! If anyone is curious on how to use/spent their Turtle Tokens, be sure to read [Turtle Shop — How does it work?](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=132)

Take care and enjoy your Adventures!

 - JC
