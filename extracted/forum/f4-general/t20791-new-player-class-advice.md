## Post 144345 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-10T22:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144345#p144345 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd def suggest a hybrid class, so you can try out many playstyles and see what you like the most.
In dungeons, you can be a tank, a melee, a ranged or a healer.

My main suggestion is druid because of its versatility. You can try out each roles.
It has a huge PVP skill ceiling too, if you ever wanna take PVP seriously, as a challenge.
Travel form also makes your life easier from level 30.
Important: animal form sizes can be affected by race and gender selection.
So tauren male -> biggest bear, night elf female -> smallest bear.

Other hybrid options are paladin and shaman.
They are also cool but I think they are less optimal than a druid for you.

Pala lacks the ranged build so you can't try out everything within one class.
Shaman, specially leveling can be very mana-intensive (a lot of drinking is not fun) and even though you can play all roles, some of them are just annoying during leveling (elemental) or really hard at first (tank).

But new class changes come soon so we'll see.
But in any case, I'd suggest druid.
