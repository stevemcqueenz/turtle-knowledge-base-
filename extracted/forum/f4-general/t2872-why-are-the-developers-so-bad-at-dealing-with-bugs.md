# Why are the developers so bad at dealing with bugs?

## Post 17902 by Stoogeville — 2022-04-03T19:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17902#p17902 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For anybody who has experienced and reported bugs, this goes without saying.

If not, consider some of the long-lasting bugs – Such as the Ratchet flight path (Currently broken for nearly four months), or Transmogging (which was broken for goodness knows how long). Also, understand that there is a generally unhelpful and absolutely dismissive attitude towards ticket reports from the moderators (which you may well know, if you have reported even a single bug), and the fairly recent move of the reporting method to be behind an external account wall on Github; to save what seems like minimal time on the developer’s side, at the expense (or, likely, the benefit) of discouraging reports.

I am not looking for a generic defense of the plight of developers an moderators, a cheap contrast with other clearly dysfunctional seervers, or a non-sequitur about how Turtle WoW is free to play – I am asking if there is actual specific reason why there is so much struggle around simply getting bugs acknowledged, let alone addressed, let alone fixed.

Bugs can be frustrating enough without having to contend with the moderation team at all levels, to not simply be handwaved away with zero concern; nor to watch significant bugs go ignored for very long periods of time. Having had to fight tooth and nail to not have my game-breaking, recurring bugs ignored, I would like to at least know reasons that have made Turtle so terrible for this.

Greetings!

Bugs get dealt with as soon as possible. Github is amazing platform to keep track of bugs and keep in touch with developers. When a issue is solved, it has the fixed tag and the fix is applied at the next updated.

Bugs can  be reported anywhere. More important is the details with they are reported.

For some issues, detailed steps to reproduce the problem and sources for the expected behavior would be greatly appreciated. If a issue can't be reproduced, it will be impossible to fix.

Thank you for patience and your understand!

Your support is grealty appreciated. We apologize for the distress caused.
