If you are loging out in a non-rested zone, even if you click "close game" or however its called while the 20 second timer ticks down, your character will remain sitting there for the full 20 seconds. So it is possible for you to die to a patrol or spawned mob, pvp interaction and so on in the time it takes your character to fully log out.

## Post 50705 by Mcnair (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-06-20T13:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50705#p50705 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is a common misconception of the "Exit now" button. If you press that during the countdown, your character does not stay in game for the remainder of the 20 seconds but for 3 full minutes, the normal DC-logout time! It has the same effect as just closing the client without logging out. I would recommend not to use it at all, especially not on PvP-enabled or HC characters.
