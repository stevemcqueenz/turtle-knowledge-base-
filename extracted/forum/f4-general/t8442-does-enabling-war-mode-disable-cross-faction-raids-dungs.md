## Post 54421 by Moriena (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-07-24T07:26:00Z

Hello! No it won't. You can invite any player you see by targeting them and typing /invite. This obviously only works if they are not a hardcore character. Warmode just disables the option to /pvp at will until turned off. It has no affect on guilds/groups/raids.

Remember that PvP in Crossfaction Groups is prohibited though. So do not heal your Alliance friend, when he is fighting the Horde!   smiling_turtle_head
