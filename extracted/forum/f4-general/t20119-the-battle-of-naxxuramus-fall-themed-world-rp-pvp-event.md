# The Battle of Naxxuramus Fall (Themed World RP/PVP Event)

## Post 137730 by Avitus26 — 2025-07-01T14:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137730#p137730 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Naxxuramus, the dread citadel of the Scourge has been pacified.

The fallen orders of Lordaeron came together and destroyed Kel'thurzad. The Argent Dawn, The Brotherhood of the Light and The Scarlet Crusade all fought for the first time as one to drive back the threat.  During this time, the Scarlet Crusade had enjoyed the ability to recruit from stormwind and refresh its numbers with young and willing zealots.  Relations with the Alliance grow more strained by the day however, as the deaths of a caravan of dwarves in wetlands soured relations recently.  The Scarlet Civil war between the scarlet legion and the larger crusade was ceased and the crusade reformed around new leadership, overall command of the crusade now falling to General Abbendis of Tyr's Hand.  Her first order was the creation of a unified force the likes of which the north has never seen.  The main body of the new grand army is made up of three guilds.

 -The Scarlet Crusade (New Leadership)
 -The Scarlet Vanguard (New Guild)
 -The Scarlet Legion

With the ranks of the scourge on the ground swelling to numbers never before seen the first task of this renewed crimson host is clear.  Plaugewood must be assaulted and the undead number quelled.

 ----------------------------------------------------------

This Event will be the first in a series of themed world pvp events hosted by the Scarlet Crusade Faction.  You do NOT need to be part of our guilds or a role-player to attend these large events however we ask that those wishing to take part ensure they are "on theme" this first battle will be between the Grand Crimson Army and the Scourge of Plaugewood.

The Horde will need to use noggenfogger or donation costumes to represent the scourge.

The Alliance are limited to HUMANS only with scarlet transmogs.

We are aiming for 100 v 100 if you have any questions or would like to take part in the event please reply here with numbers and what side you will be joining.  The Event is Planned for the 12th at 18:30 Server Time in EPL.

GM Assistance would be amazing if we could have GMs play both Baron Rivendare and General Abbendis.
