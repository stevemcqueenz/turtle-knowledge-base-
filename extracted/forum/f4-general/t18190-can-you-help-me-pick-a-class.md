## Post 123954 by Wolf of Rage (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-03-26T13:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123954#p123954 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hunter has Melee Hunter -- a meme turned to something more viable here.

Shaman has Shaman Tank -- also something that was buffed in this server.

Mage, Warlock, and non-Feral Druid also have Tanks -- but much more niche, only earning a trivia mention.

Warrior has Fury-Prot Tank -- given your mention of WotLK, I'd reckon you therefore already know of Arms-Prot Tank (Unrelenting Assault + Improved Revenge).

As for your preference for complexity and burst as well as PvP, Enhancement here is a lottery machine, Rogue is a CC-based oppressor (not much fun in PvE), Mage has the suicide bomber known as Arcane, Frost is now... two buttons instead of one (Icicles added here -- basically Frost-flavored "Arcane Missiles"). Warrior is still a gear-/Healer-carried machine, but it's more braindead and less flexible in this server when it comes to PvP, so you rely on gear and Damage more (which is indeed higher here than on Vanilla).

Engineering might be able to steer you into picking a Class you otherwise might not be drawn to; for example, take it, be a Demon/Destru Warlock, a Fire Mage, or a Balance Druid, make the Gnomish Cloaking Device, use it, and cast Soul Fire, Pyro > PoM > Pyro again, Starfire > Moonfire, and ONLY when the Spell's Cast Time is FINISHED does the Cloaking Device's Invisibility effect end (the only source of this quirk -- Invisibility Potion and others cancel on ATTEMPT instead). You can basically nuke anyone... once every 1h. But there are also Sappers (Goblin), the Death Ray (Gnomish), and so on; you can utilize this as a Rogue to secure kills within opener lockdowns.

I would suggest Rogue, but you might not enjoy your PvE grind -- DpS sucks as you progress, you're not in high demand, you'll be more of a support bot which oftentimes isn't even deemed required. PvP is still a race to see just how much you can turn a 1v1 into a 1v0 with CC chains and trying to beat your last record of killing someone within CS > KS. You can handle 1vX scenarios with expertise. It can have fun skill expression, and you can do some dangerous sneak trips behind enemy lines, snipe someone, disappear, repeat, etc. Mess with people. Bully people (don't).

Warrior is next, but as someone who mains it, I do not enjoy what Class Changes 2 did to it. Should you get the later endgame gear, your Damage will be inflated next to Vanilla's, but it won't be as fun during your journey. Skill ceiling is also lowered due to loss of some of its utility and self-reliance kit (no Improved Hamstring > Counterattack sucks as its replacement, for example). You do have the option of going full Arms with sub-Prot Talents (instead of Fury) for group PvP (BGs), and have the unique benefit of this server's custom iteration of Intervene plus the Talent it has which gives it a short Living-Action-Potion-like effect, Revenge having a Stun (RNG, but still of use else Revenge is useless in PvP), Last Stand (longer CD than in WotLK+), and Iron Will (you should know this from TBC and beyond as being Arms). You can also go Fury, take up the custom Blood Drinker Talent -- you can be a self-healing beyblade... but there isn't much brain activity behind that one. You're a beast in PvE, but the Class is oversaturated here; however, Tanks are still in demand (everyone wants to be a Damage Dealer).

Warlock is also an option, as it still has oppressive high-skill expression (Seduce this, Fear that, Death Coil here, Inferno there, Soul Fire the dad who just came back from his 14h shift, Shadowburn the runner who thought he was finally safe roaching out, etc.). Just don't roll Affliction if you want to avoid boredom. You are extremely in demand, as one of two rare Classes in the server -- Shaman being the other.

Shaman is there as well, albeit Elemental is not as fun as its WotLK iteration. Enhancement as noted is the Spec for casino players. Orcs here can summon Spirit Wolves, which makes it feel a bit closer to WotLK Enha (and resembling the Far Seer Hero from WarCraft III). You can rock a big 2H and make low-effort montages of Windfury Crits like it's the mid-2000s again. You are in demand regardless, even if it's just for Totems. You can even be a Tank. The Class is underpopulated -- much like Warlocks.

Nordanaar is huge, but RPvE, and the PvP is infrequent and dull -- you are mainly looking at 51-60 AV on farm most of the time (which gets dragged on and is subjected to Honor grinds), and at times the Blood Ring (which is a meme fest since it has incomplete/imbalanced features).

Tel'Abim is PvP, albeit it's far smaller than Nord (but is growing). There are also SEA Realms if you ever consider, and even soon a South American one, but I cannot speak for any of those at all.

I have Warrior bias, so it's my immediate choice and often have arguments in the Class' favor. Generally, you're not as flexible. In Turtle, even less so. However, you are still the best at the two jobs you can do (Damage Dealer and Tank), and a lot of the time during your journey, you pretty much just slap on a Shield and maybe what spare Tank gear you've got, and you're already set. Later on does it matter for you to consider switching Talents and get some more defensive Equipment to keep abusing that.

Leveling has a slow start -- limited kit, not much use; you Rend up to L20-ish, then stick to Sunder to ~50% HP and finish off with Heroic Strike. Later you get Whirlwind, and main that. Slam -- same (I dislike its changes here, prefer not to use). Overpower is... Overpower (no Taste for Blood, so, you've got a Cooldown). Once you get your token Talent Ability (Mortal Strike/Bloodthirst), you're in for more fun. And once you get Execute... you can start feeling broken (it has a Cooldown, needs a Talent in Fury... yay). You can't rely much on self-sustain mid-Combat, because Hamstring has a Cooldown, and Improved Hamstring is gone (instead you have Counterattack... it sucks).

I first leveled as Fury down to Piercing Howl, and then continued as Arms. However, that set me back eleven Levels to Stance Mastery, Improved Charge, Sweeping Strikes, Sword Specialization (homogenized under "Master of Arms" here, based on Weapon Class), Mortal Strike...

Dungeons while leveling were fun as occasional checks to see how well I could do compared to other Classes as I kept going. Then at endgame, they and 10M Instances while getting pre-BiS can show how you already can be broken. Endgame itself is just an uphill. Unfortunately, that means that PvP is less rewarding -- a lot of PvE gear is just better than what you can get for PvP use. They are yet to correct this here (I do not see this as a positive despite benefiting from it -- it's just power creep, and I like challenge and reward).

Gear is flexible -- use from Leather to Plate (just as you may know from WotLK). Your strongest gear (endgame, Tier Sets, etc.) will be Plate. There is a lot you can find during your journey, because there are plenty of additions, and even new Suffixes for more Stat combinations. You can also craft some more Items (there is even Jewelcrafting, which here is more like Cloth/Finger/Neck/Trinket/Enchanting-like Jewelry Stat affixes, on occasion a Staff or some other). Blacksmithing has Belt Buckles (Enchanting-like Waist Stat affixes). Alchemy really compliments your Class. Engineering does as well (it's a decades-old staple). Blacksmithing has a unique craft for a Two-Handed Sword through Master Swordsmith, which actually is required for a long Quest chain leading to a non-60 BiS 2HS which is on par with some L60 Weapons (MC/Ony), which you can twink with (it feels like Shadow's Edge as a Sword in Vanilla below Level cap).

Rotation depends: Arms is Charge > MS > (Sweeping Strikes if AoE) Whirlwind > filler (Cleave if AoE, Hamstring every 6s or Sunder refresh or Slam or even Pummel if you hate Slam here so much goddammit why did they make Slam like this it's so stupid what the hell were they thinking I'd rather punch a Mob with my bare fist out of spite). Priority is Sunder Armor implying lots of Physical DpS (even meaning your own), then the actual rotation. You fish for procs... except 'String has a 6s CD. Thanks, Turtle. Rend is almost useless. That Ability is almost only ever used in PvP as anti-Stealth/-kite. T3 DD variant may make it more desirable because it's kind of like Cataclysm Rend where it can be refreshed (instead of actually making Rend good...). Fury is... Fury. You've played WotLK. Fury is not smart there. It wasn't smarter in Vanilla. It's not any moreso on Turtle. You flail your arms (not to be confused with Arms). Uh, that's it. Though seriously it's BT > WW while spamming Heroic Strike/Cleave (the bug here is "fixed"/changed but honestly it's still just HS/Cleave spam so thanks again Turtle) and of course keeping Sunder Armor refreshed. 'S about it. You do also have 2H Fury... It's the same, but cooler in my opinion (20/31/0 down to 2H Weapon Spec in Arms). You can even go Arms-Fury (Improved Slam + Death Wish). It's boring, because you have even fewer buttons to press, but it's funny because of the DpS you can pull off with such simplicity.

In PvP, you are quite reliant on your toolkit -- which is even less self-reliant due to situational nerfs and rebalances -- but your Damage can still get to broken proportions. During leveling and short-term Dungeon and PvP fights (frequent Combat dropping), the moment you can get Improved Charge, you pretty much always have a nice headstart and replenishment to your Rage. Execute is... for whatever reason, buffed here. Your other, non-Execute Damage may be weaker. However, get to 60 and endgame gear (say, custom T3 Warr DpS), and that stops mattering, because gear scaling will just make you deal more DMG again anyway (hooray for power creep). You can still be carried by a Healer and just turn your brain off and get to killing and farm whispers berating you for being an unskilled numbskull. It's a nice plug-and-play Class. Zug-zug.

World PvP is... a mixed bag -- speaking from Nordanaar experience. Overall, I did not enjoy it. There were exceptions, but general encounter tally was unfulfilling -- the infrequent and low-quality PvP (everyone unflagged or bluewalling you first, community there being anti-PvP, griefing) was making me feel like I was back playing in a different server notorious for its toxicity, which I will not name here. However, that negative bias aside, proper World PvP as a Warrior can be fun (even here, post-CC2) -- especially if you're geared, knowledgeable, skilled, and have your 27638286372689 Consumables and Trinkets (your bagspace will be crying 24/7).

I find it fun. I like being a Fighter archetype up against whatever fantastical nonsense and trying to prevail or at least putting up a good resistance.

Shaman is a lot more flexible. You won't be like a Warrior, but you can have your own moments of questionable game design (see 2H Enha + Windfury = deletion; busted instant Earth Shock Crits; making Melee commit suicide by spamming Lightning Shield).

Elemental might be a tad boring (you spam lightning and LARP as Raiden from MK not MGR/Thor).

I have Enhancement bias, in this case -- I like Vanilla/TBC 2H Enha, the diversity with a bit of (detestable) luck making fights feel like at any point they can either end instantly, or have you sweat yourself off.

Purge makes anyone instantly angry at you.

Totems give people anxiety.

Some are still not used to Turtle Shaman and forget that Orcs in this server can use Feral Spirit and have their beloved two Spirit Wolves just like in everyone's favorite WoW expansion. You can be a Tank and make everyone question you but then appreciate you (should you not flunk the test).

Gear depends on Spec -- it is not as flexible... or rather, you do literally and actually have a broader choice, but given your build, it may not be what you're after (it's not like Warrior where you see Hit/Crit/STA/AGI/STR/AP and you're golden because it's that simple and you have Leather through Plate to work with). So, much like Enha gameplay, Equipment drops are like a lottery.

Leveling is more fun off the bat than Warrior. Also more versatile. That can make it more fun, despite it not being the best at whatever job you try to fill (since you're a Hybrid, and Warrior is just the Tank/DD king). You're less Stat- and gear-reliant, so you don't feel like you have to upgrade all your gear every few Levels.

Dungeons are also good as you can help make them pop sooner or help someone's LFM spam on World five times a minute to finally stop because you decided to end the misery for everyone.

Going to endgame, you will have an easier time -- you will often be needed (even if all you have to offer is Totems). As you get geared, you get better (not as much as Warrior, but again, you're a Hybrid, and Warrior is king). It's more of a gradual rise, rather than a sequence of spikes. If you like topping charts, you might not find it as fun due to not expectedly seeing yourself there. But you will know that the brainlet Warrior who is likely doing that, is doing it even more thanks to you and your Windfury Totem -- he would not be as cool without you. You are the support character everyone loves, while the Warrior is the egotistical main character everybody hates. You are an endangered species coupled with your strange partner of... Warlock. Maybe you're just distant cousins in this server.

PvP is similarly fun as above. Due to your wildly varied toolkit, you have something for... not everything, but a lot. You are a magician (not to be confused with Mages); your specialty is performing disappearing acts on Magic Buffs -- Purge. If you haven't served someone an imbalanced 2H Windfury Weapon Crit, then you should restart the day -- go back to sleep, wake up, launch WoW again, and do it, take your Spirit Wolves out for a walk. You may have limited point-blank cripple removal (Warrior has Charge if out of Combat, Intercept or Intervene otherwise -- unless deadzoned), so it would be good to bring things to help you negate that (Free Action Potions, Boots with burst Movement Speed, Goblin Rocket Helmet, the works). In group fights, you are vital; you're not like a Warrior who just goes into Warframe mode like a Rhino main and just autopilots towards the nearest parent of five who's just come back from his work shift only to see someone rushing towards him and get a free loadscreen, BUT, you have utility and group support to help... just about everyone ever, and you yourself if you are supported can _almost_ turn your own brain off and just farm Windfury kills while backed up by carries.

Rotation is... dependent. In PvE it's similar to Vanilla, but the 2H Talent got replaced by a new fun Talent Ability called Lightning Strike. Deals Weapon Damage with some extra as Nature, and it consumes a Charge of your active Shield Buff as an empowered version (so people suiciding can be helped by you with your own spending of Lightning Shield). So you probably interject the usual rotation at the Stormstrike point either with Earth Shock (like before) or Lightning Strike (someone correct me if I'm wrong, if you've even read this far). It's a tiny bit more diverse because of this -- if you like more button presses and prefer doing math equations in your head as your means of having fun, then good, and if not, then, uhhh, just let Windfury Weapon solve the problem. I BELIEVE Totem Twisting is gone (I recall a change which more or less removed it, but someone again correct me on this) -- Windfury Totem now ought to be an immediate static Aura rather than a timed Buff, meaning you shouldn't be able to do WFT into Grace of Air for maximum benefit (and a short lifespan on your Mana Pool). This also means that you can't drop WFT, get the 10s Buff, and still benefit from it even if the Totem is gone or you get away from its reach (personal nitpick -- I dislike that).

In PvP the rotation is basically situation-reliant; if the guy in front of you looks like a nihilist, chances are, you spam Lightning Shield with the occasional Stormstrike-buffed Lightning Strike into empowered expended LS Charge, and you just refresh LS and repeat -- they should die to your social distancing mechanisms and socially distance their spirit from their body to a nearby Graveyard (which is oftentimes not at all nearby). Otherwise, SS into max-Rank Earth Shock and hope for a Crit. Of course, you are to never, ever, EVER forget Windfury Weapon. You have your occasional ranged options in the form of Frost Shock, Chain Lightning, and so on. And if you pick Orc, don't neglect the doggos -- they need to eat every once in a while. Also Bloodlust is the token Enhancement Talent.

Paladins are "fixed" here... Let me explain why I use that in quotes.

First... Shockadins are dead. They don't exist here. Literally. Yeah, I dunno. Supposedly they'll bring them back. It's just... yeah, Shockadins are currently not a thing.

Instead, Holy is more like Cataclysm Holy (but without the boring built-in Shockadin features that =<WotLK Paladins had the cooler option of) -- Holy Shock can only heal, but it can also... reset its own CD.

There's also Holy Strike, baseline. It's nuts.

Retribution is actually less of a meme. It's... not spectacular, though, like Holy is with its new Holy Shock, or Protection with its Threat upkeep.

Protection is so buffed that its Threat management feels like that of the overpowered iteration like that found in WotLK -- if you know that from there, then you... probably already know it here. I personally disagree with this result. However... we can't have Warriors be Tank kings in almost if not every regard, so... fair. Warrior has Fury-Prot -- DD-Tank hybrid with DpS/TpS mixing and just the ability to plop a Shield on just whenever. Paladin does not have that. So it's fair to give Paladins some gimmick through which they dominate.

So basically, all Specs are now viable (other than just Holy, but SHOCKADIN IS FRIGGIN' DEAD--).

You're not a meme anymore. Well... still are -- you're still the pink-colored Class. Just in gameplay you're not... somewhat (Holy Shock CD resets are busted as Holy, Holy Strike is busted... Retribution exists, I guess; it also benefits from Holy Strike -- it's just Holy Strike itself being stupid, overall).

I'd say, since you're already considering Shaman, I'd go over the immediate Warrior pick, and instead suggest Shaman as well. Warrior is oversaturated here, and its full potential takes time. Shaman is not going to be king like it, but you're going to be appreciated, and just have a cool toolkit, as well as your journey being more evenly-leveled and less dependent on gear (Warrior leveling is mostly fun if you can keep upgrading your gear every few Levels, else you're back to killing Mobs two or more Levels beneath you like a weakling, but I'm a self-loathing masochist so I don't do that). Plus, if you do pair with a different Warrior, what you can give will just let you see how busted Warrior can be ON TOP, with your help. Personally I find Arms-Enhancement pairings one of my favorite team-ups in this game (especially in WotLK in 2vX WPvP and 2v2 and 3v3 Ranked Arena).

> **DopamineSage wrote: Wed Mar 26, 2025 7:23 pm**
> Hey there   smiling_turtle
>
>  May I ask, could you give some advice for being a first tank? I have never dungeoned before, nor have I played Warrior, but I recently had an urge to try a role other than DPS.

Health first, then Armor, then Avoidance (Parry, Dodge, Block). Also Defense of course (unless you have good Block and can maximize the uptime).

Block is the least useful and is notorious in Vanilla for being quite weak. They are attempting to correct this on Turtle to make it more viable.

Just know that Block is mostly to reduce the impacts of Crits and be non-susceptible to Crushing Blows in PvE (very useful there). But generally you want Parry so you can fully avoid Damage and gain Parry Haste for a sooner swing thus more Damage per Second leading into more Threat per Second from said DpS which sooner swings also means quicker Rage generation that you spend more towards TpS as well as DpS thus even more TpS.

And then there's Dodge. Pretty standard. You avoid an attack. You're playing Dark Souls but the Roll button is on auto.

Your primary tanking concern is Threat above all. DpS is merely an addition to support TpS - - not the main appeal.

By this I mean, you go for Abilities specifically designed with the flavor text of "and causes a high amount of threat" as well as "and increases threat". That includes Revenge (always off CD), Thunder Clap (off CD if AoE), Sunder Armor (absolutely all the time), Shield Slam (off CD), Heroic Strike (only as a filler and Rage dump), and so on.

Revenge, for instance, will not deal the highest Damage, but its Threat is monstrous. Sunder doesn't even deal any, yet it's amazing for Threat. So the logic here is Threat > Damage (leading to more Threat), and not the other way around.

This is also why Fury-Prot is good - - it adds DpS not for the sheer sake of DpS (even if it helps kill speeds), but because this DpS creates more TpS on top of what you already can do.

Speaking of Prot, if ever you go Deep Prot, please also add the Talent which increases Threat OUTSIDE of Defensive Stance. The intent behind that one is to diversify the playstyle and add reward to doing so, by increasing your Threat massively (basically imagine Defensive Stance Talent with this one combined, on top of Sunder Armor). The immediate tradeoff, of course, is sacrificing the Damage intake reduction whenever you aren't in Defensive Stance (as opposed to always being in it). But it's a playstyle more involved than conventional Prot, produces more Threat than it, and indirectly also slightly more DpS than it deals, which also results in even just a bit more TpS on top, compared to standard Deep Prot.

More on tanking, have Raid Icons ready. You don't actually need macros - - go to Keybinds and scroll down. Skull is the usual kill target marker. CC icon is... pfff I dunno, cross? Just communicate it anyway. Likely that you will have it all ignored, but it never hurts to have it and is nice whenever you do get cooperation.

It also helps to have time for gathering the Mobs. The golden rule is five seconds. Ten if the pull is tricky or you have issues for whatever reason (new, low gear, low Level, et cetera). People will usually ignore this, but just know. That's fine anyway, because it just serves as a frequent crash course to check your progress, and you get to know if you're good, once you see yourself securing Threat quickly and so solid that if it were any more solid it might as well be considered burial by cement.

Rotation-wise, let's say, AoE, safe pull. Battle Stance > Charge > Defensive Stance > Thunder Clap > Revenge > Shield Slam > Sunder > Tab > repeat with whatever comes off CD (TC/Rev/SS). Heroic Strike as a filler Rage dump. Cleave is debatable (no extra Threat, Threat is split amongst hit Mobs, might be better to just HS). You could already be Tabbing after Revenge (SS on another, Sunder on yet another, to already be spreading quicker).

If the pull is unsafe, then do a ranged pull (have a Ranged Weapon and if it shoots, Ammo as well). You can also ask someone else to do it for you, but it's best for you to do that. Immediately duck behind cover. Do the above rotation.

You can add Berserker Rage from Berserker Stance to slightly up Rage production from Damage that gets past your defenses. Just be sure not to use it if there might be a removable CC. Of course also Bloodrage, but prefer to use it when needed (f.ex. on unsafe pulls where you can't have Charge).

For Single-Target, you do Charge (else Ranged) > Rev > SS > Sunder until a CD is over > repeat (HS as Rage dump filler).

TWThreat helps. It's a tanking addon designed for tracking everyone's Threat to everything, allowing you to multi-task and call out uncontrollable genociders.

You can also look up Threat of various Abilities to be sure of Threat values and hone your priority as well as rotation and also call me out on my bull if I'm wrong.

Small but important edit to add something vital that I forgot: positioning.

When pulling and gathering Mobs around, be wary of where you stop, so that Classes that need to be behind can do so without body pulls (Rogues, Feral). You should also make sure to always keep Mobs facing away from everyone else (Cleaves and Cones, Mobs can't Parry from behind, indirectly increasing DpS). Furthermore, to truly make the most out of it, allow there to both be room for people to move (in case of needing to run), as well as give RDpS and Healers the opportunity to utilize Line of Sight blocking. Good positioning is always a treat to see.
