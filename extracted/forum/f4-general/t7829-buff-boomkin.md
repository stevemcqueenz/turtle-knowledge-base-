Did a little checking around and it looks like the max dps a boomkin is going to put out is around 800dps with BiS gear and full consumes (sp flask, greater arcane elixir, sappers on cd, sp food, top-tier oil). This is based on looking at several different boomkins and their best damage on patchwerk in naxx, so everyone there should have solid gear all around (if you know of others aside from those below, please let me know).

Doomkin - 849.6
Duathwi - 770.7
Bayanni - 700.3
Oomchan - 655.7
Moriena - 601.3
Xoo - 538.4
Selca - No usable data

Most classes and dps specs have broken 1kdps on patchwerk. The only ones that haven't are boomkin, smite priest, shadow priest, and elemental shaman. Enhancement shaman was really close with 972 dps. This data was pulled from the legacy players website, so this should be fairly accurate.

This means that druids at max gear need a roughly 200dps increase, or around 25% from current, to be minimally viable as a dps class in my opinion. I'd assume then that each buff adds 5% spell damage per stack and that could be enough, although that's honestly pretty substantial in terms of value for sp so I'd add it to a talent deep in the balance tree. The talent "Moonfury" is a 5/5 talent that adds 2% spell damage per point spent. I'd either tack on the 5% for each subsequent spellcast OR change the talent to add maybe 6%-7% spell damage per proc (total of 30%-35% increase once full) to achieve this goal.

As an aside, I've seen the proposal to add a proc like this to spell crits. This could also work as boomkins are known for adding spell crit chance, so theming them around prioritizing crit% could achieve the same goals, albeit a little slower that just spells landing, and help define them a bit more both mechanically and flavorfully.

If we take the absolute best boomkin performance of 849.6 and increase it by 35%, we get 1147dps.  - The top mage is 2,675.1
 - The top warrior is 1830.8
 - The top warlock is 1,563.0
 - The top hunter is 1,474.5
 - The top rogue is 1,412.0
 - The top paladin is 1,251.5

For comparison:
 - Isabel cat dps - 1386.9
 - Verwin cat dps - 1375.5

With this theoretical best-possible scenario with this buff, a boomkin would be #135 on the charts (which doesn't show cat dps). Every other class above has at least 4 characters ahead of this number. This isn't as gamebreaking as you may think it is.

I will always sing for boomkin raid utility. The ability to rapidly switch to healing with a viable set of gear to do it in isn't something a lot of other hybrids can boast. Innervate is incredibly useful for effectively upscaling the gear level of one of your healers or inefficient casters. Battle res saves raid boss attempts more often than many care to admit. Decurse/abolish poison are required for so many encounters I don't want to list them all. Ferals will most likely be the ones applying Faerie Fire since it's less costly for them to do it, but having a hit-capped caster to apply the armor debuff is always welcome. The crit aura is arguably the LEAST helpful utility they bring, and it's effectively another small item to everyone in the party. It's all good stuff, certainly.

With all this said, currently, just bringing another mage is better. Their dps is so massively ahead that it can help bypass the mechanics that boomkin utility brings to the table. If you deal enough damage fast enough, you will not even need to battle res since the mechanic that killed someone never gets the chance to go off. If your raid is experienced or geared enough, it drastically negates innervate and brez as they're simply not needed. They know the fight and the important people don't die to stupid stuff. Mages can decurse as well, so only the abolish poison and ability to swap to healing remain not covered by another mage, which can also be negated on almost all fights outside of 4hm as any other hybrid can also fill these roles while currently dealing more damage when not healing.

I don't think omen procs (more mana, effectively) to the party is enough. Otherwise, we'd have already seen more arcane mages. It's something that could see more play, but ultimately the utility isn't enough when many raids don't need it and just need more damage instead.

## Post 52095 by Bayanni (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-02T08:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52095#p52095 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Another idea has cropped up from discussions with some other theorycrafters.

Two sets of buffs:

 - The first is to Swiftmend. Currently, Swiftmend "consumes" HoTs to grant healing equal to the full duration of Rejuvination or Regrowth's ticks. Make it work for DoTs to. This would grant a not-insubstantial increase in dps for both Insect Swarm and Moonfire, two very underused abilities. Despite Swiftmend having a 15-second cd, this would have an impact in pvp by adding an additional burst ability to boomkins, but one that would cost some pvp survivability and utility to spec into, so I see it as speccing to become more of a glass cannon and arguably not even worth it for most pvp situations.

 - The second is an entirely new talent in very deep Balance, probably tier 6. This new 3/3 talent would increase the duration of Moonfire's DoT by 3 seconds and Insect Swarm by 4 seconds. This would allow one more tic of Moonfire for 15 seconds total (3-second tics) and two more tics of Insect Swarm for 16 seconds total (2-second tics). This doesn't sound like much at first glance, but this would allow both abilities to be used in a much cleaner rotation to current boomkin starfire spam and, most importantly, have them last long enough to always be consumed during their last tic by Swiftmend.

These two changes combined would theoretically be higher dps overall than starfire if the tics are allowed to wind down towards the end but would be completely pointless for burst potential over literally any other burst, including just spamming moonfire, if popped early. This would mean a modest to minimal impact on pvp (only because swiftmend is instant, really) while giving noticeable increases in dps for pve and raiding. As this would use up four talents boomkins really don't have a good place for (usually rammed into imp shapeshift, nature's grasp, or imp thorns), you'd see a drop in their pvp flexibility and durability if they go all-in on boomkin balance or a drop in their total damage potential from losing some other big balance talents to still keep those defensive boons. Additionally, it would make their rotation more interesting and add some small amount of skill by knowing what part of the rotation to skip over when a resist occurs in favor of the higher-damage Swiftmend-Swarm-Moonfire rotation.

On it's own this isn't enough to get to 1kdps on a target dummy, but it would allow more ways to potentially increase a boomkin's dps through consumes, enchants, or other class abilities (CoE getting nature on it, removing charges from shaman's Stormstrike, etc).
