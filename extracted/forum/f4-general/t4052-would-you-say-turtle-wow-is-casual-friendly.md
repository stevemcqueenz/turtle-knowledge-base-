I guess the question is pretty straight forward but I will give a more detailed explanation.

My wife and myself are just looking for a fun server to level and play with others. However, I have read a lot that endgame people not only in raids but also dungeons become very hardcore and meta. Guess we are not looking for that because it doesn’t fit our play style. The server is definitely casual friendly. The glyph system allows you to disable works PvP, enable a slow-mode, party hardcover characters, and more. Endgame content being meta is only subject to who you group with or which guild you run with. There are plenty of members in the community who will not expect min/max cookie cutter performance from you. There is also much more end game content in the form of new quests and zones to explore so don't stress about running out of things to do.

With the servers present xp rates casual gameplay will mean end game content is not an issue you'd need to be concerned with for a few weeks, possibly even months.
