# Should shamans be able to tank?

## Post 95972 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-05-29T21:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95972#p95972 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I saw/heard some pros/cons.

 * They are the mirror image of paladins and palas can tank.
 * But palas don't have RDPS, enabling shaman tanking would give them more build options.
 * More tank classes means more variety at PVE -> More novelty and fun.
 * It's hard to buff shamans into decent tanks, too many base changes are needed, like extra mitigation, defensive CD, aoe threat, taunt.
 * But they have some base abilities which could be buffed to give a basic core for tanking (like rockbiter with better threat scale, earth shock new level with taunt).

I really think there should be a consensus about this because it really limits class redesign.
Currently a bunch of tanking talent takes up space at enh tree, stopping enh to be a valid DPS spec. Some talents like elem devastation or +hit would be much better placed there if we remove tanking.
If we consider shaman tanking just a weird hobby/challenge, it shouldn't take up so many talent slots which could otherwise be used to make enh decent.
But if we say yes to shaman tanking, enh tree should be similar to feral tree at druids, enabling a DPS and a tank build at the same time.

What do you guys think?

I'm definitely not really a fan of shaman tank as it's just so underdeveloped that it seems like a waste of dev time to fix compared to other pursuits - especially so considering the baseline DPS enhancement is already in terrible shape.

That said, I don't think faction/class balance is super important in this regard as Turtle is primarily a crossfaction experience - Tel'Abim does exist but seeing as 95% of the playerbase does not ever touch Tel'Abim I don't think it would be wise to make design decisions based on PvP server gameplay. Prot paladin is not a desirable pvp spec and assumedly shaman tank wouldn't be either so it shouldn't be an issue for battleground balance.

In terms of what would be needed to be added to the shaman tank kit, prot paladins also do not have a defensive CD - I think different kinds of tanks being specialized for certain things is healthy design. That said, it also would be nice to see some more built in mitigation for prot paladin to compensate for the lack of defensive cooldowns and lower availability of stamina in their gearing. If shaman tank lacked one thing but excelled at another I think that would be a good direction - perhaps they could have a niche as tanks that have the best mitigation against magic damage? Would make sense given the fact that they would otherwise be the most squishy tank, being a mail wearer with the least inherent physical mitigation from talents, items, and abilities.

Ideally if they do make shaman tank a real playstyle then they would do so in a more thorough manner than simply buffing or adding components to existing abilities (such as adding a taunt to Earth Shock) - Earth Shock, auto attacks, Storm Strike, and reapplying Earth/Lightning/Water Shield is pretty lacking in terms of interactivity.

The direction the dev team is currently taking is to fix enhancement as a baseline DPS spec for 1.17.2, then look into shaman tank for future updates. Personally, I do not think that the feral talent tree approach would be as effective for enhancement - you could lock the tanking aspects behind having a shield equipped or having Rockbiter active but that is still far more abusable than swapping druid forms.

So I have a Shaman, and have tanked both 5 and 10-man lvl 60 dungeons.

The #1 problem, In my experience, is AOE threat (or the complete lack thereof). I can hold up to 3 targets with the creative use of talents and spells, but most dungeon packs are 5+ mobs.  Things tend to get messy, and completing a dungeon takes for-ev-ver.  So in its current form, shaman tanks are only slightly better than a hunter's pet.  Possible at best, eye-rolling at worst.

But if shamans are to be at least *viable* as tanks, the question needs to be asked: what *kind* of tank should it be, then work towards making adjustments to fit that role.  What I mean is, there are tanks, tanks, and TANKS.  Shamans are the former, warriors the latter. Personally, I'd like love to see Shamans somewhere in the middle, in that they could, at the very least, hold AOE trash threat in a 5 and 10 man dungeon, but not viable in a 20 or 40 man raid.

And speaking of damage mitigation, TBH I don't think it's needed if the goal is to tank 5 and 10 mans.  Even current shamans can soak up decent damage in normal dungeons with decent raid gear and a good shield.  It's just that lack of aoe threat that's the problem.

If one simple thing was tweaked:

Allow fire totem aggro to transfer to the Shaman after the totem dies.  Currently, the aggro just *poof* disappears like it never existed.  This would go a LONG way towards helping shamans keep some of that trash aggro in non-raid dungeons.  When a hunter's pet dies, its aggro transfers to the hunter.   Shaman totems should be no different.
