# Hardcore Warmode Impression

## Post 23454 by Thorgaaz — 2022-08-25T22:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23454#p23454 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Well i just think i post my Opinion

So far I played 3 naked Hardcore all Glyphs Characters. First died lvl22 against Shaman, Second Lvl13 by Suecide against Elite. (I literally knew i would die before i engaged in combat), And the third Time with Lvl10 against Horde again in Goddamn Teldrassil. (dont like the Trend, but thats not Important)

However i still dont think it is Impossible, just very unlikely to reach lvl 60 even if you do an all Glyph naked HC run.
But right now, Im kind of on the Edge to quit Trying. Even so i really love hard Content.
The Problem With HC Warmode specific is that it doesnt get rewarded, It forces you to adapt an Unfun Playstyle to minimize the Risk and absolutely removes the 30% exp increase, and even if you adapt that, it is simply Luckbased. (Does Horde appears or not).  So... all in all its just not Fun.

Hardcore is a Skillcheck, Turtle intenssifies that Skillcheck. Exhaustion.. useless but also just makes it take longer so.. Skillcheck. And all of theese are doable with the normal Questing Playstyle. But Warmode?  Nope, Lucktest in Combination of Hiding in a Corner.

So it seems to me, that in the Future i probably do a  naked all BUT Warmode HC run. Even so that is probably no big Feat anymore. Which clearly makes me less exstatic on doing so. Which is sad, since there are so many Possibilities for Glyphs which are actually Skillchecks.

Examples: (Ideas)

Weakness: Reduces your Stats.
Nudity (deserves its own Glyph in my opinion) locks Inventory and Pets available lvl1, if Inventory is empty.
Hatred tripples your Aggro-Range
Fractionbasher. Available till lvl10. Removes all Rewards from Quests  and Mobs, but adds EXP and Honourgain to PvP kills. Buying Gear with Honour available.
and so on.

Rewards could be other Cosmetics or Middlestrong Gear. Like for Example an Elite Character Symbol if you do an all Glyph HC run. (Warmode aside for mentioned reasons)

Also I would change Warmode. Reducing the EXP reward to 20% but also removing the EXP-Loss. And (and this is Important) Warmode Characters can only be attacked by other PVP flagged Characters. I think i read a Post about this somewhere else already, but back in the day i thought this is an PVP Server and it is not a big deal. I was wrong, it is. PVP should be PVP. And not creeping around until the WM player finally Pulls something so you can simply finish him in a weakened state. Because THAT has nothing do do with Honour at all.

So. I think i posted enough. Lets see what your Opinions are. Greetings: Thorgaaz

Warmode exp doesn't need to be reduced. Warmode needs to be permanent from the get go. It makes no sense that a glyph that's supposed to increase pvp is designed so that all level 60s have it off and the only ones with it on are <60, thus making ganking lower levels the norm since you lack the level 60 targets necessary.

Either make it permanently on, or make it so it gives something at 60 to incentivize people to keep it on.

As for your challenge, I think your issue is HC. HC should have a max level difference for PvP, somewhere between 5 and 10.
