# Memes That Need To Die: Warriors in PvP

## Post 70624 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-10-13T15:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70624#p70624 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is a very common Vanilla WoW myth that "warriors are S-Tier in PvP".
This erroneous idea is toxic for the game as it results in countless people selecting Warrior only to become miserable getting endlessly pwned (and sucks for others because Warriors are dead weight/useless teammates in Battlegrounds such as Blood Ring/WSG).

This trite notion likely stems from viral videos like "Swifty's Incredible Warrior Tricks" which (tho impressive) are not representative of average Warrior gameplay/performance in PvP.
Those videos are edited to show only highlights of success -- failures/deaths are not shown.
Plus Swifty was a min-maxxer in full epics and highly skilled.

The typical arguments for Warriors supposed "PvP Powerhouse" status are:

 *"They are great with great gear!"*
 **Don't care. All classes are powerful with end game epics.**

OR

 *"They are great with a dedicated healer!"*
 **Don't care. All classes are powerful with a dedicated healer.**

 **Warriors NEED buffs or additions to their PvP toolkit.**

Here is a *brief* list of reasons why Warriors are in fact BOTTOM TIER in PvP and should NOT BE PLAYED if you want to BE SUCCESSFUL in general PvP scenarios.

 - Charge/Intercept are highly unreliable for a variety of reasons. (Was wisely buffed/fixed in TBC)
 - They rely on rage to do anything and are dead by the time they have any.
 - No defense whatsoever from spells. Kited & pwned by virtually all spellcasters.(Spell Reflect wisely added in TBC)
 - Poor sustainability (Going Shield / Defensive Stance kills your DPS and deletes your rage)
 - Virtually no healing abilities. Blood Craze is a tepid trickle.
 - No ranged abilities whatsoever.

 - Their *best* matchup is against Rogues and they still lose at LEAST 40% of those duels.
 - Utterly humiliated & dominated by Paladins Holy Strikes which ignore all plate armor.
(Note: Paladins are extremely common in Turtle WoW)

 - Utterly humiliated & dominated by Frost Mages who are virtually untouchable.
(Note: Frost Mages are extremely common in Vanilla WoW)

P.S. I will mention the only two strengths/functions of Warrior in PvP which are:
The Mortal Strike "50% less healing" debuff is excellent and obviously highly useful.
When there is a 'turtle' aka strong defense of a choke-point in Alterac Valley -- Warriors are often the 'tip of the spear' to break thru the defense because they can somewhat effectively tank the enemy teams attacks (if said Warrior is getting heals of course).

NOTE: Sadly, countless Warriors are dull-witted and obsessed with "MUH BIG DICK 2HANDER DPS BRO" and literally do not even have a Shield in their inventory (if they have one it will never be equipped).   dead_turtle_head
