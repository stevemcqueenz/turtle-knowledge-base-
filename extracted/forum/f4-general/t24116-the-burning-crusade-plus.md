I'm not here to suggest anything, it's just a silly idea I've always had, and I want to create discussion.

After playing Turtle WoW for a while, I haven't stopped imagining... this is what I've always wanted... but something's missing.

Honestly, I won't deny that I like TBC solely for the zones; Quel'Thalas is well done, the Azuremyst Isles are... well, they're there.

The only thing that ruins the experience is... what the heck are Elves doing in the Horde? And what the f- are those beautifull and incoherent blue space goats?

And it's crossed my mind that with so many Classic+ servers, no one has thought to create one where Blood Elves join the Alliance BUT preserving all the structures and maps present on TBC, and the Horde gets Amani Trolls, Ogres, or... why not? Playable Eredar.

And starting with TBC, expand the world, preserving level 70 and creating a new story, maybe removing Illidan and replacing him with someone who fits the narrative. Remember, Illidan was supposed to be dead until TBC.

I'm not sure if this is the right place; it's more of a discussion I want to start, since I've always believed I would have enjoyed The Burning Crusade without the mess of Blood Elves and Draenei, which make absolutely no sense.
