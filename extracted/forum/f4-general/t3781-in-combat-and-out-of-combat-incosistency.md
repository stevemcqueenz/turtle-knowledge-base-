# In-combat and out-of-combat incosistency

## Post 22808 by Vladis — 2022-08-19T11:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22808#p22808 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello

I am playing melee hunter and I have just found out that entering combat and getting out of combat differs between the open world and dungeons.

In the open world I can still lay down my trap even when my party members already fight. Trap can by laid down before I start to attack.

In dungeons when I stand near my party members (not attacking) but they already fight, I am cosidered in combat and cannot therefore lay down traps.

Is this intentional ? If not, can the open world rules apply to dungeons as well ? Would a great QoL change.
