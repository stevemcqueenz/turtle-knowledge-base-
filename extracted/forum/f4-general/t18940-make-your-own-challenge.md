# Make your own challenge

## Post 128865 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-28T17:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128865#p128865 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So- I've been seeing a couple posts as of recent and in the past regarding hardcore. Suggestions, things people don't like, stuff that could be fixed, etc.

And almost just as often in those same posts I see something one might call a solution.

Make your own hardcore.

It works, doesn't it? You don't have to play the dreaded turtle HC mode that you seem to despise so much. Bullshit deaths to DCs or griefing could simply be not counted, and you could even ADD onto the suffering, like limiting resources or player interaction. You could go full iron man, even. So just below, I'd like to share some fun (or even un-fun, for the masochists out there :3) ideas to make the hardcore experience just a little harder.

 - Gear limitations -

This could be as simple as only letting yourself have green gear, or doing your own vagrant challenge. Maybe a version of vagrant where you can enchant your stuff, or have it enchanted. There's a lot of different ways you can go with the idea of using worse stuff. Maybe itemizing your character completely wrong, like putting caster gear on a warrior or melee stuff on a priest for that sweet, sweet, wand agi scaling...

 - Class limitations -

I'm sure we've seen these before. Mages only using a certain school, hunters with no pet or only playing melee (much more viable now but still something you can do) and other such things were challenges on the old unofficial hardcore servers. How 'bout try making your own? Like shaman with no weapon enhancements. Healing classes with no heals. Druid with no forms. Warrior with no weapons? NAKED? Warlock without using soulstones? Priest with no wand? Paladin with no strikes? Rogue without spending combo points?

Point is, there's lots of stuff you can get rid of depending on your class.

 - No food -

This one isn't that big of a deal, but you could also make it harder with just... No consumables, even. This means bandages, engineering items, pots, drinks, etc.

Getting mana back as a caster would be a bitch, and warriors practically lose all ability to heal. Not having a grenade and bandage for when shit gets hairy can make a lot of difference, honestly.

 - Iron Man -

Already mentioned this, but, again, it's an option. You could also go the softer route and only limit things like the level of who you group with, or only grouping for dungeons, or stuff like mail/trade/AH. Maybe you wanna be able to use AH but also feel like playing completely alone? Go for it.

 - Other miscellaneous ideas -

No world buffs, or no buffs altogether, no mounts, or maybe force yourself to grind rep to buy other races mounts, removing some miniscule class utility like rogue lockpicking or mage portals, maybe hunter tracking, idk.

When people are so bored from default game that they decide to handicap themselves manually, they invent challenges.

Important difference of self-imposed challenges compared to "official" ones is some gameplay-related reward. For example - mount for HC, moonwell for artisans and other stuff.
Other thing - unavoidable rules.
You could impose HC without picking HC glyph and forgive yourself deaths from disconnects.

Official challenge is a puzzle. You don't always know how exactly you will fulfill this challenge. When you decide to go self-imposed challenge, it is like making up riddles for yourself. You know the answer from the beginning, as a result self-imposed might not be interesting from the beginning.
