## Post 37970 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-03-16T10:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37970#p37970 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I raided ZG in full 20 man raid on a server with 100 online. When online was raised, I raided MC in full 40 man raid on a server with 200 online. Scaling down is not necessary at all even for reason of "helping in case of low online". Though, scaling raids is cool feature itself.

When vanilla was "new", main problem of raids was coordinating 40 ppl with mediocre network connection and without voice chat and knowledge. We can't return to those good old times.

Why people could go to raid in few numbers?
 1. When ppl are outgeared to clear it quick for gold or probably for materials.
 2. When ppl are progressing and don't have enough ppl.
 3. When regular static attendees are not online for some real life reason.

In first case, I support to remove scaling. Raid is already easy for them. By reducing amount of people, they make it even easier. They don't need that help.
Does scaling in raids include scaling of loot?

In second case, that help is useful, but people miss main raiding problem of classic. Which is: finding and managing 40 people.

In third case, that help is you don't need much scaling. Adapting 40 man raids to 35 is ok. In most cases it won't help anyway.

Does hp scaling in raids affect loot? I believe it is not.
If you can 20man MC and get same amount of loot as 40man, then it is unfair comparing to progressing people who share 2 epics from boss on 40 man.

Conclusion:
Raid scaling from 40 to 35 is ok without any penalty.
Scaling 40 down to 20 should have a penalty of decreased loot. Same for 20 down to 10.
If raid scaling will stay, it should be highlighted on main page with raid features <https://turtlecraft.gg/#/raids-and-dungeons>.

P.S. Will it be fair and fun to scale raids up? Like entering ZG in 40 man with increase in loot and hp and adds?
