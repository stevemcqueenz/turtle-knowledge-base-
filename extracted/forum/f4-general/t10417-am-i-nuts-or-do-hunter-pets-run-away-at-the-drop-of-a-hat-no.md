## Post 68239 by Nickgreyden — 2023-10-03T02:14:00Z

Things I've learned while cycling through pets as an baby hunter.

 1. Feed your pet food of the appropriate level. Error on the side of higher level. If not, they chew through it quickly.

 2. New pets chew through food quickly. Do NOT afk with a rank 1 pet and afk at your own risk at rank 2. Rank three it gets a lot better.

 3. Don't feed your pet after the bar is green. Even though the combat log says it continues to rise it happiness, it doesn't. Wait until yellow.

 4. If you are actively playing, expect about two stacks of food to be used per rank of happiness, at least until level 30.eaning if you want to get your pet to rank 3 so you can afk a little, you need 3 or 4 stacks of food.

 5. Always have your pet out. Do not dismiss your pet unless you have to. Don't let your pet die. All of these serve to rank your pet up or at least inhibit their unhappiness.
