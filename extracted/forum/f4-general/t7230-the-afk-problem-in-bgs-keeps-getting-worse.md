# The AFK problem in BGs keeps getting worse

## Post 45789 by Rat2156 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-13T21:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45789#p45789 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know I'm not the first person to complain about this, but not only does the issue show no sign of improvement, it's actively getting worse.

Repeat offenders keep showing up day after day despite being reported time and time again, and seeing the lack of punishment or consequences for AFKing only encourages others to do the same.
It's especially noticeable in AV where it's common to see graveyards being captured despite up to 5 people standing next to it, as those people are AFK and won't defend it.
Similarly, as soon as a game looks like it's going to take more than a few minutes, people start actively calling others to go AFK and to let the other team win (which should probably fall under griefing at this point), and once again never face consequences for it.

GMs need to take a much more severe stance on the matter, as the longer AFKers go without punishment or with very light punishment, the more people will AFK in BGs. This is a problem that's getting worse by the day and will keep getting worse until properly handled
