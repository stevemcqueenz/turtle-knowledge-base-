## Post 150685 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-07T18:54:00Z

I do understand fresh chasers now.
In the first days of Ambershire, battlegrounds were really enjoyable. You could get greens from ah, apply to them Medium armour kits and do a single run of DM/WC at 19 with stopped xp. With this minimal preparation you could be competitive in battlegrounds. Everyone was in gear like this. I played wsg at 19 for three days and it was fun.
But the server grew older in a single month. Now I see more people running with a crusader enchant, with a fishing hat, with 1.5k hp.
Meanwhile on the lower end I see guys who think that xp in battlegrounds was a chance to level in PvP. It is not. To be honest, it was not even in the first days. Under leveled characters have many punishments. It is unreasonable to join a battleground to level, unless you are getting carried. You can imagine distracting strategies by low levels. But the longer I play, the more 19 ppl I see.

One can say "but you twinking yourself". While I running bg nonstop, I don't have time to acquire gold for high level enchants. The more battlegrounds I play, the higher that disparity becomes. Same story again - level yourself high level character to have fun on low level battleground.
Why should I play Ambershire if I have a better character on nordnaar?
Only one reason might apply - queue times on Amber might be lower, because people don't expect twinks there.
I can try to proceed to higher brackets. 20-29 will probably have same situation as in 10-19. So my bet is 30-39. There are new dungeons for that bracket.

If they level up, they have to change all items anyway, so it is unlikely that they do this.

Regarding brackets I imagine simple option. In short - like in World of Tanks. There were 10 levels of power. If you play, for instance, as level 6, then you can be matched with level 4-8. If you bad, you get into lower bracket. If you good - you get into higher one.
If apply to wow, there should be overlapping brackets like 10-19, 16-25, 20-29. Imagine you are twinking as level 19. If you have high win count on character, you get into 16-25 bracket more often. If you have low win count, then you get into 10-19 one.
In WoT I personally hate this way of matchmaking because 6 vs 8 level is often unbeatable and your team full of idiots who aren't matched to higher bracket. Though, amount of idiots might be fixed in all kinds of brackets.

Higher amount of tokens in stack is good idea too. I add only that repeatable quest should grant experience.
It might solve situation that one is super useless at lvl 12 against lvl 19 because of miss/resist chances.
In current set of rules, amount of tokens should be enough to get 6 levels. If each quest grant 10%, then 600%/10=60*3=180. Can be rounded to 200 tokens.
Like you leveled to 9, stopped xp, gathered enough tokens. Then you ding to lvl 10, then spend all tokens to get to level 16 and you can comfortably play in 10-19.
Items from boxes should require x6 level tho. Now they are require x8. Bad idea to graduate from 10 to 16 in white stuff from starting location.

If overlapping brackets will be implemented, then amount of tokens can stay the same.
