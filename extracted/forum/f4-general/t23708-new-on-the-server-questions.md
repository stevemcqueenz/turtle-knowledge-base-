Personally, I just wouldn't touch PvP if you want to play it seriously. The lack of balance and massive gear difference you have to deal with at 60 is something a lot of people have spoken against. It's probably pretty alive on Ambershire but Nord is pretty dry.

Engi is also the PvP profession, but if you want something that's helpful all around and never loses value, go alch/herb. Just having free or cheaper raid consumes and certain pots to help in any part of the game is a genuine gift. Especially since world buffs aren't being used. People have more need for the elixirs with that taken into account.

There's also the jewelcrafting profession that came out a while ago! The gemology spec makes important gems that *everyone* needs for their rings and necks. It all basically just acts as a pseudo enchanting mechanic for jewellery in specific. More niche than actual enchanting, but it's pretty valuable to have.

On the topic of enchanting... That's also pretty important. It also falls under the category of never ever losing its value, because you can enchant better gear, but you can't craft gear to replace your new shiny raid/dungeon stuff. Almost 100% of the time, raid gear > crafted gear. There's few exceptions, of course, but they're few regardless, and typically don't justify the effort. Enchanting gets you personal power without replacing anything, so it's both renewable for yourself and a reliable source of income because of the massive demand.

Yes, but not merely that.
Sappers, chickiens can be huge at raiding, even some other tools.

For PVE raiding, you can find a spot with whichever class.
Usually shammies are on super-high demand because WF is super good and it's a party buff so you want at least as many shammies as many melee groups you have.
Maybe hunters were in a tougher spot but balance changes are incoming and maybe this changes, too.
If you want one char, I always advise picking a hybrid class to have a wider array of experience. So you can try out healing, tanking, caster DPS and melee o whichever combination of those at ease.

For profs, I heavily advise eng.
Even if we disregard sappers etc I mentioned above, you'll get a port to your capital as an engineer, enabling you to have HS elsewhere. Also Tanaris or Winterspring port later.
I love engi because it enables me to be at places fast. Less relevant if you're a mage, I guess (I never vibed with mage chars).

If you want a char with prime aoe farming capabilities, maybe pala and mage are my best proposals.

Faction: not important, PVE is cross-faction, PVP is a joke. I usually pick horde but mostly because I like the zeppelin system and makes my goal to reach places asap easier, combined with engineering.
