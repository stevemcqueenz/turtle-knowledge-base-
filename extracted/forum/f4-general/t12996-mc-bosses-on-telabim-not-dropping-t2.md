Good day.
Many people here might be confused by your comment, as most people around started playing long after the launch of the original game. So here is a quick story time: During the alpha and beta tests, and after launch for a short time T2 items dropped from various bosses in Molten Core, as that was the highest raid instance yet in game but the items were already ready. Once they finalised BWL, these drops were removed and the T2 set was moved to their planned dropping place, to the second tier of 40 man raid content.
So, while there is historical precedent to what the OP is asking for, I do not think it would make much sense if the argument is "progression" as that would make most of the BWL raiding moot later on once it starts.
