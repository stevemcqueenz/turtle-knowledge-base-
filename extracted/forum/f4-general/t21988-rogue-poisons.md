Hey!

I'm curious about rogue mains' opinion on this.
Tried rogue a while ago. Came from a class which just casts a weapon imbue spell and I have the weapon enhancement for one hour.
For me the rogue poison system was a chore but I'm not stating the game should revolve around me of course.
I just wanna know if for you guys the poison creation process and the way poisons wear out by hits is an inherent fantasy of rogue and you like it or you'd prefer a lazier solution (where the extreme would be poisons becoming weapon imbues to select for free and being applied for a time period).

## Post 152833 by Jirry — 2025-09-23T16:12:00Z

Honestly, I get where you’re coming from. I’ve played rogues for a while, and the poison system can feel like busywork sometimes, especially if you’re used to a one click weapon buff. That said, for me it does add a bit of “rogue flavor” and forces you to plan your cooldowns and rotations more carefully. I wouldn’t want it completely dumbed down to just picking an imbue for an hour, it takes away part of the class identity. But yeah, I can see why someone coming from a more “lazy” system might find it tedious.
