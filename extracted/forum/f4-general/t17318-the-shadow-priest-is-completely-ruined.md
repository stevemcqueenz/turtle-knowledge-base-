# The Shadow Priest is completely ruined.

## Post 118261 by Welcome2023123 — 2025-01-29T01:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118261#p118261 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Shadow Priests had already been severely nerfed by World of Warcraft, with their crowd control and healing capabilities becoming extremely poor. As a result, Shadow Priests rarely appear in PvP anymore. With the recent update, the abilities of Shadow Priests have been devastatingly weakened again, and this time without any explanation. Many players were even unaware of this change, which is that the Mind Flay spell can no longer trigger any special effects, such as the stun effect from the first tier of talents or almost all gear effects.
I am very surprised that no priest has discovered this bug for so long. Perhaps no one is playing Shadow Priest anymore. I hope the developers can fix this bug as soon as possible. Shadow Priests are already so weak that no one plays them. What on earth are you trying to do? Many players are complaining bitterly about your recent arbitrary nerfs and are quietly leaving the game.

You can still probably do well if you're well geared, you can melt people if left free casting BUT other specs can 1 or 2-shot people if they're well geared.
