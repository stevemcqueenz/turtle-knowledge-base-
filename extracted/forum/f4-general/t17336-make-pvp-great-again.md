# Make PVP GREAT again!

## Post 118428 by Frantsel (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-01-30T11:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118428#p118428 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The PvP-situation is a mess. Most of the problems boil down to bad balance between the factions and classes.
While it is hard to add classchanges that dont effect the pve situation too much, I have some ideas to tackle the issue from another angle.

 **I present you 3 simple steps that would fix not all, but ALOT of the current issues we have right now:**

 **- Add MIXED-FACTION bgs**
(Why? Horde dominates most bgs and it would balance the winrates out much more efficiently. Also would even out if one factions queues less.)

 **- Remove queuing for grps in bgs or at least add another queue for grps that wanna play together vs other groups**
(doesnt need any explanation, right?)

 **- Add MIXED-SERVER bgs**
(The overall bg number is too low on both servers. Often there is only one bg running and you meet the same players over and over again. The waittime for some bgs often passes the 1 hr mark, even on alliance side. The pvp server is now on naxx-level and they are pretty geared up. So I would say the gear is pretty evenly spread.)

These three points dont need any balancing drama or other stuff. Its just basically changes to make the bgs more fair so that people are actually encouraged to queue up again and hopefully have fun again.
If you are sceptic about the impact of the changes, you are always able to add these new features while KEEPING the old queues for players that dont want those changes and have their bgs in a more traditional horde vs alliance manner).

Any other ideas I might have missed? Lets talk about it!

I don't want to be rude but in my opinion these 3 steps you propose concern things that have minimal impact on PvP experience. Even if all of them are embraced, nothing will change really. PvP here has other core problems. Version of PvP varies depending on expansion of WoW - so let's compare Turtle WoW PvP to Vanilla PvP.

PvP in Vanilla wasn't perfect but there was a phase when it was in relatively good state. The best PvP phase was on BWL timeline when the best gear in PvP you could get was through PvP ranking. In that phase, there wasn't still such big difference between fresh level 60s and nerd rankers. The gear wasn't too overpowered. Blizzard back then balanced the game not only for raid but also for PvP in mind so classes were more or less balanced.

In Turtle WoW you have fully progressed server even with upcoming new T3.5 & T4 content. The difference between power of BiS geared players and noobs is huge. Devs openly admit they don't pay much attention to PvP while doing class changes - their focus is on PvE content. PvP gear on this server sucks because the best one you get from Naxx and AQ40 - at least for Priest.

What I mean is, all your 3 suggestions don't really touch any of these issues that are present on Turtle.
These issues to be precise are:

- Current PvP itemization sucks. To get best PvP items, you must do PvE content.

- End-game itemization is too overpowered and creates big difference between fresh 60 and BiS-geared players. In my opinion this is unfixable issue on Turtle.

- Damage to Defence ratio imbalance - since the CC2 all classes seem to be bursty as hell. Fights used to last longer, now you can be killed by a single player within a few seconds even if you have 6k HP. In ideal BWL-phase-PvP this wasn't common and fights used to last longer meaning that PvP wasn't all about brainless killing enemy as soon as possible but required some strategy.

- Custom talents/spells too overpowered. As a Smite Priest player I clearly see the devs didn't think how good the Smite will become afte CC2. I can burst someone for 4k damage instantly with a bit of luck because of broken talents like Searing Light and disorient melees close with Chastise. These custom things might work fine in raid environment but obviously not in PvP. I play only one class so I'm sure there are also other examples.

- Broken queue. This bug has been here for a while. It surely is not fair to have 9 vs 11 Warsong game.

- Ghost queue mafia. It's still unpopular issue, nevertheless more and more people realise it happens on this server. Some best premade rankers use their alts (I've heard they sell characters later, rumors) to pop BG so their main premade gets into 15 vs 0 game (in Arathi Basin for example). They exploit this system to end the game in 3 minutes and receive same amount of Honor people farm in 20 minutes. And because current Honor system is competetive, the people who exploit have unfair advantage over people who play fair.

- Weird ranking system. I call it weird because people who spend 1 hour a day doing battlegrounds for years will have lower PvP rank than people who spam battlegrounds 10 hours a day for 3 months, then hit rank 14 and retire. This system is the most absurd because it doesn't award people in long term but in short term. In my opinion there shouldn't be rank decay at all but rank itself should require more Honor to progress. So people who actively do PvP every day (but in shorter time) will get their rank eventually.
