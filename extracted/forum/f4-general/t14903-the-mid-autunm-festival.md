> **Geojak wrote: Fri Sep 20, 2024 10:17 pm**
> Hey, where can I get this quest? :) I haven't heard anything about this new one being added and it's not on the DB either.

The quest was posted on the Turtle Wow Discord a couple of days prior to its release. The NPC Qiuyue can be found near Stormwind's Fountain, and somewhere in Ogrimmar (not quite sure where). All you have to do is put the lantern in a water source and return to complete the quest. It was only meant to last 24 hours from my knowledge, so if the NPC is gone, you might of missed it.

I'm not aware of any 20-slot bag, the mooncakes and the toy Lantern seem to be the only rewards. It's possible it is more of an important event in other regions, which is why the different servers would get different rewards.

Thank you for reading, and good luck on your journey.   smiling_turtle_head
