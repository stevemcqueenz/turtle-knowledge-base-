You don't need to enter Champions Hall to buy items from battleground reputations. Visit bg places in the world. For Horde warsong it is northern exit from Barrens.
For Alliance warsong it is west of Fallen Sky Lake in Ashenvale.
For Horde arathi it is north of Hammerfall in Arathi Highlands.
For Alliance arathi it is Refuge Pointe in Arathi Highlands.

You can reach reputation up to exalted and then you get access to vendors I listed above. You need to reach at least friendly reputation to see items they offer. The higher your reputation grade (honored/revered/exalted) the more things you will see.
