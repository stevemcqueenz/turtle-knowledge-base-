## Post 127901 by Sleeptime (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-04-22T00:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127901#p127901 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey there!

Whenever you purchase tokens, they can be used on any character on your account—they're account-wide. However, items purchased from the shop are character-bound. For example, if you buy a bag on one character, it won't be available to other characters on the same account.
