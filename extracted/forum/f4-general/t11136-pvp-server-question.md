To be fair, I personally thought you were asking *what* zones were flagged for PvP.  I was prepared to tell you, too!

> Will the zones be a mix of contested and safe like classic wow or will it be set out differently?

The zones will reflect standard *World of Warcraft* philosophy; zones rated for a minimum Level of 15 and higher are contested, thus automatically flag players for PvP.  Stupidly, this also means that, despite the fact The Barrens is rated from Level 10 all the way to Level 33, the *entire region* is classified as Horde territory.

Dungeons located within faction-owned locations, such as Gnomeregan or The Wailing Caverns, are considered that faction's territory.  And of course, Battlegrounds are considered Contested, for obvious reasons.

> Could I make a horde and alliance character on the same account?

I'm assuming not.  Which is kind of a stupid restriction on any server, in my opinion.  Maybe *Turtle WoW* will surprise us.
