It may be part of an ongoing issue that's currently being investigated. Some players have reported being unable to send messages in /guild, /general, or /world. It seems to largely affect characters Level 20 and under; as soon as you reach Level 21, the issue will often resolve itself. I don't know of any definite causes at the moment, but some add-ons have been shown to exacerbate the issue (notably those that handle a lot of information, such as TurtleRP).

Though it can understandably be a bit frustrating, given the nature of HC, the most we can do is patiently wait for an update at the moment.

If you want to test whether it might be something on your end, things you can try are:

> Clearing the contents of your WDB folder

> Disabling your add-ons to see whether one (or more) of them is responsible

> Clearing your chat-cache in: WTF -> Account -> [Your Account Name] -> Turtle WoW - > [Character Name]

Though keep in mind doing that last one will erase any custom chat settings or windows you've made, so keep a back up of it just in case it wasn't the culprit! ![🤞](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f91e.svg)
