SWG (Star Wars Galaxies) allows you to do that an unlimited amount of times (with certain cooldowns) - you start as Neutral and can either join the Empire or the Rebel Alliance and take part in massive open world battlegrounds, this was done back in 2003 so I don't see why it can't be done in Turtle WoW (not the open world battlegrounds, but faction changing).

I can't provide any examples where in the lore it said characters join the other faction, but I don't see why it wouldn't be possible. I've read one Warcraft book, about Medivh and Garona and one comic book about Varian being found in Durotar with amnesia and joining arenas with a Night Elf and a Blood Elf and I remember one picture showing the arena crowd in Feralas comprised of all races sitting together like they aren't at war.
