# Disappointment with Turtle WoW after the UE5 project cancellation

## Post 163321 by Dimastasc — 2025-12-20T05:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163321#p163321 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The recent announcement from the Turtle WoW developers that the project on Unreal Engine 5 will not happen was a major disappointment for me and many other players. It feels especially bad that this news came right before the New Year instead of a “gift,” we received the opposite.
After this announcement, many of my friends lost faith in the future of the project, and some have already deleted their characters. More and more people believe that the UE5 topic was used mainly to attract attention to the server, while others think the project is under pressure from Blizzard. Either way, belief in the long-term future of Turtle WoW is fading.
I personally decided not to waste any more of my time and move on to other projects. Without my friends, there is no reason for me to stay.
Everyone makes their own choice.
Thank you for the time spent here, but this was not the best “New Year’s gift.”
Goodbye, Turtle WoW.
