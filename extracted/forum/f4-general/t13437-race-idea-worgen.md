# Race idea - Worgen

## Post 90573 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-18T17:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90573#p90573 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I've read many interesting articles in this forum theorycrafting about a new playable race.
My favourite one is the below article about furbolgs:
[viewtopic.php?t=12957](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=12957)
My goal here is to give a counterpart to furbolgs, a horde race which would appear at the same time as them - and could be a druid, just like them.
I know, based on retail lore worgen in horde is an extremely bad idea. I want to, however, rely only on the current lore we have in TWOW and create a background story for them based on what we know in this realm.

My priorities:
 * The lore allying the worgens to the Horde should make sense in TWOW's pocket galaxy
 * The racials should be flavorful but not overpowered
 * The selectable classes should make sense

Let's get into it!
As for the story, we would by default know very few about the worgens in this timeline. Here is what we know, right before the segment of the Cataclysm section:
[https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki ... behaviour.](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Worgen#:~:text=The%20worgen%2C%20in%20fact%2C%20derive,and%20descended%20into%20feral%20behaviour.)
Besides this, we have some additional lore, since TWOW added their version of Gilneas to the game. We can add the below to what we know:
<https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Gilneas>

 **The backstory:**

I would keep everything as it is until the creation of the wall and the sight of the dying refugees at the gate. It is also canon in this world, it cannot be changed. But I'd change drastically what happened in Gilneas during the time when it was isolated from the world.
Greymane was a selfish man corrupted by Daval Prestor aka. Deathwing. The dragon fed upon the pre-existing flaws of Greymane's personality and shaped him based on his own visions. The corruption within Greymane's mind started to take over, culminating in the creation of the wall.
Isolation was something Daval/Deathwing desired. This way he could pursue his plans without the lurking eyes from the outside world. The wall was up, so no former ally would interfere.
Deathwing slowly started to turn Greymane and those who are the closest to him into dragonkins, the ones we see in the dungeon. Scheming was not needed anymore, the royal family turned into his crew of loyal servants. In the meantime Greymane's iron-fisted regime turned into a tyranny, upheld by his loyal military. The soldiers turned a blind eye over the changes in the kingdom. They were paid first, blackmailed later, then ultimately succumbed and became puppets of Deathwing. The dragon ruled over the kingdom - the loyal family followed his orders and the military, controlled (to a lesser degree) by him made sure that order is maintained. He could follow up on his plan.
Behind the closed walls he opened a portal to another world and lured wolf-like folks into Gilneas. He wanted to invite them to this world to turn them into an army at his disposal. These folks were a cunning, barbaric and forceful breed, similar to the orcs and once they arrived through the portal, Deathwing introduced them to this world - in his human form. He never showed his true self to anyone in Gilneas. Not even Greymane knew who his master really is.
A curse was cast upon the worgen, turning them to beasts with a limited sentience. The portal staid open and Deathwing pulled many more of this folk into Azeroth. The old Gilnean army turned into a tool to enforce the internal order while the worgens were destined to be turned into an army of unprecedented size and power which Deathwing planned to use for later conquests and destruction. He sent out small groups of this beast-like folk to different places of this world (to Silverpine Forest or to Duskwood, for example), to create unrest and wreak havoc - Deathwing regarded all forces beyond the wall as enemies.
The races of Azeroth were not sure where these beasts were coming from. They fought them since there was no other way to get rid of them. During this time Deathwing waited to have enough worgens for an open war.
However, something unexpected happened. Some townsfolk in Gilneas, who have seen the destruction around them and the arrival of these beasts finally had enough. The internal defense made up by the royal guards was strong but not almighty. They were not aware of the events some citizens were plotting. These townsfolks decided to send a warning to the world. They created an intricate plot and during a night, with the help of some bombs and alchemyc potions they successfully damaged the gate badly enough that the mighty gate got open again. In the last moment of their life, they sent out huge fireworks, alerting the nearby lands. Then they died. Immediately.
Several beings saw the huge fireworks of Gilneas and many curious eyes were swiftly darted towards the Kingdom. Before Deathwing's men could repair the gate and close themselves from the outside world once again, some nearby tauren druids took a peek inside. They were nearby and the curious animals were not considered as threat to the magically not gifted guardsmen. Once they have seen enough, they have fled the scene and reported everything to their kins. Words spread, the world got curious about Gilneas. Deathwing knew that his army is not ready yet and he also knew that many peeking eyes will come soon and sending them away from the top of a closed gate is not an option anymore. They were not regarded as an isolationist kingdom anymore, they were a suspect of something horrible.
Deathwing disappeared, leaving the corrupted Greymane's, the cursed worgens and the puppet-guards behind. Before fleeing, as a final act he killed every civilian of Gilneas he didn't directly controlled before. As revenge. His army was not ready, there was no reason for him to stay any longer.
However, the portal stayed up a bit more than it was supposed to. A final tribe of worgens were pulled into the world of Azeroth before the closure of the portal. They were not aware of the schemes of this land and were never cursed, since Deathwing forgot about them when he left. All they saw was destruction, orchestrated mainly be their own kin which behaved like savages. Shocked and confused, they escaped this land and seeked a place where they could understand what happened to them and what happened to this world. They have found the town of Sepulcher where they contacted the forsaken. Sylvanas was quickly informed that a tribe of the wolf-beings has approached them. Without any ill intent, they just seemed lost and confused. Sylvanas, being Sylvanas, decided to tell them half-truths. The story of the creation of the wall without mentioning the plague itself. The horrible things which might have happened to the beings of Gilneas and the enslaved worgens - stating that it was all the work of the Scourge. Luckily for Sylvanas, the Scourge had such a bad reputation that the worgens' belief in the Banshee Queen further grew anytime they asked someone about the Scourge. Taurens, trolls, even some trading or political representatives of far-away races like dwarves and humans did further reinforce their belief that the Scourge is pure evil. At the end they believed that only the Scourge can be the culprit of their kin's suffering and degradation.
The worgens had nowhere to go until now. At this moment they have found a home near Undercity and an enemy they have sworn to defeat - the Scourge. They joined the Horde to aid them in this and some of them started adventuring in a world where some of their kins were reduced to mindless killing machines and they had to kill them with their own hands. They have sworn never to forgive the Scourge.

Disclaimer1: This version of the worgens was not descended from humans. They are not shapeshifters, they only have the worgen form.
Disclaimer2: The worgens came from a plane where animal husbandry is unknown. If they need food, they hunt it themselves. If they need work, they do it all by themselves. Because of this, there is no worgen hunter in this iteration.
