# In Defence of Guild Houses

## Post 56620 by Mileti — 2023-08-10T01:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56620#p56620 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Guild houses within Turtle WoW hold a treasured place in the hearts of players due to their rich flavor and profound historical significance. These magnificent halls stand as a homage to the early pioneers of our server, evoking a sense of nostalgia and emotional connection for both casual adventurers and ardent roleplayers alike.

As an individual with a deeply personal bond to the Ered Ruin guild house, the mere thought of its removal casts a shadow of disappointment. Reflect upon the heartwarming nostalgia that washes over you when stumbling upon the enigmatic Golden Moon Troupe in bustling Ratchet, or the unlikely camaraderie forged with a Scourge member while navigating the perilous territories as an Alliance character. These iconic landmarks are the threads woven into the fabric of Turtle WoW's distinctive allure.

While we acknowledge the importance of growth and evolution, it's vital to tread carefully. The erasure of these irreplaceable landmarks would conjure echoes reminiscent of the impactful Cataclysm expansion—an event many of us remember and, perhaps, would prefer not to relive. In essence, retaining these guild houses signifies more than mere structures; they represent the soul of Turtle WoW, a testament to the shared journey we have undertaken.

Let us unite in preserving these pillars of our virtual realm, safeguarding the continuity of Turtle WoW's enchanting essence!

 -Mileti Zaborovski
