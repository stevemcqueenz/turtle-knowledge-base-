# Remove LFG from Interface

## Post 132556 by Alterno164 — 2025-05-22T17:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132556#p132556 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Developers,

I strongly believe that the LFG tool should be disabled or removed from the interface to restore the intended purpose and value of the Meeting Stones. Doing so would not only encourage player travel and visibility across the world—something that’s frequently emphasized when discussing the missing Meeting Stone functionality—but also reinvigorate one of the core elements of the classic experience.

Back in the original days of Classic, players would physically travel to the dungeon's Meeting Stone to form groups, building community and fostering meaningful interactions along the way. That experience helped shape the world as a living, breathing space rather than just a queue-based lobby.

The current LFG tool undermines this design philosophy. It diminishes open-world activity, reduces organic social interaction, and by making group formation overly convenient, it detaches players from the reputational dynamics that once mattered deeply. These elements are vital for creating a world where player actions and choices have lasting impacts.

If removing the LFG tool is not a viable option, then I sincerely urge you to at least consider enabling summoning functionality at Meeting Stones. This would strike a meaningful balance between preserving the spirit of Classic and improving quality of life for players forming groups organically.

Thank you for considering this feedback seriously.

> **Laysson wrote: Thu May 22, 2025 6:09 pm**
> Why ? You still need to travel to the dungeon anyway...

That's an excellent question, and I appreciate you raising it.

While it’s true that players still need to travel to the dungeon, the key point is when and how efficiently that happens. When players gather around the dungeon area, group formation becomes significantly faster and smoother. You’re not spending 20–30 minutes waiting for someone who’s flying or running from the opposite side of the world—you're engaging with players who are already in the vicinity, ready to go.

This becomes even more relevant on cross-faction servers, where travel times vary significantly depending on faction. Take Scarlet Monastery as an example: Horde players can get there relatively quickly due to more direct flight paths. Alliance players, on the other hand, often have to either run from Southshore through contested territory or take the boat and traverse from the High Elf zones—both options add considerable delays.

By encouraging players to travel before finding the group—rather than after—it speeds up the entire process and creates a more dynamic and immersive world. It also incentivizes real player movement and interaction, which aligns with the core goals of Turtle's Classic gameplay.

The LFG tool, while efficient, removes much of that open-world engagement. Without it, players naturally gather around dungeon zones again—something that encourages movement, world PvP, and spontaneous grouping.

As for summoning, it’s not hypocrisy—it’s about balance. If players are already nearby, summoning is not needed, this will just smooths out the final step without turning the game into a teleport lobby.

Believe me, this isn’t my first choice either—but in this case, it’s the only approach that truly makes sense. It aligns with Turtle WoW’s core philosophy of world immersion and organic interaction, while also making dungeon grouping more practical and engaging.
