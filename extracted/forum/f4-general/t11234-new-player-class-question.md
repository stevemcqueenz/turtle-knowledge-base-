## Post 74365 by Reploidrocsa (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-10-27T17:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74365#p74365 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Every class got more spells and while the ranking is similar to classic, the gap between them is way smaller, so being skilled feels more rewarding.

Some of the changes:
Warriors can cast slam while moving
Rogues can evasion tank for longer periods
Hunters get a new shot skill which acts as a spammable filler attack
Paladins got holy strike and crusader strike
Shamans got racial spells like priests do
Warlocks can conjure soulwells and more demon rituals
Mages can create a table with food for everyone
Priests have a heal while in shadowform and new array of buffs for one selected party member
Druids have a survival and fear remove cooldown in feral
