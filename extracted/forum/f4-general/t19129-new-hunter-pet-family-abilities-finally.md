# New Hunter pet family abilities. Finally.

## Post 129981 by Phoenixphire (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-05-07T08:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129981#p129981 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Finally. One of the largest pieces of class fantasy / classic plus class fluff that was still missing (IN MY OPINION)

The fact that pet choice was so dry and clear cut. Ability choice was always so mundane, and so many pet families were straight up in the RP tier because there was no reason to use them over another pet family. Not even like a stupid "Oh cat is technically the highest DPS but raptor has this cool little XYZ that I can mess around with." They had nothing that you could even convince yourself with besides looks. Finally, each family will have a unique ability. Whether its actually meta, just good for questing, soloing, or pvp. Now I won't look at a Hyena and say "But there is actually NO reason to tame that. None." Now I can have at least SOMETHING. Even if it isn't the best, the fact that it has SOMETHING to say "Well if I tame a Hyena I do get XYZ. Which could come in handy in ZYX situation." will make it easier to justify if you have a particular pet type that you like the look or flavor of if it never had a unique ability.

Hunter's and their flavor with pet choice while leveling and at endgame sorely needed this. And I am so thankful its finally being added. What is supposed to be one of their core class mechanics is actually one of the most dry parts of being a hunter (Post vanilla pet changes) back when many pets had unique effects, hunting down new things and using some wacky unique pet (I seem to remember my Dad had a bird in vanilla with tons of resistances) was a huge part of the fun (in my opinion) and after the changes to make all the pets just families, stat % and attacks speeds, there just became a few clear cut winners and losers, which really hurt Hunter's class fantasy in vanilla IMO.

I think this change while not as crazy as the old rare unique effects some pets had (I don't think those should return it was just too hard to balance) is an amazing step back towards there being more flavor in choosing a pet and more reason to experiment. As a mostly Hardcore player. I cannot wait to play around with all the new abilities and I am sure other hunters can't wait to use more than just a few specific pet families, even if it is slightly worse or more of a preference thing. The fact the choice may now exist is very exciting for me as a long time hunter player.

I have been hoping and asking for this change for years. Thank you Twow team for finally making it happen in the next patch. You guys are the best.
