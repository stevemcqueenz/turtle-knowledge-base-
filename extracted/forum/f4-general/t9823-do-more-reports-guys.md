I didn't violate the TOS so I open a ticket in-game and explain that neither of the toons are hc toons and kindly ask to be ported back to where I was. Some 60 seconds later, the priest is back where he was and I move on. Swift action in both directions, so I'm not even mad. Kudos to the unknown gm.

Report players if they violate TOS (maybe make sure you know the TOS first, but still). Action will be taken.
