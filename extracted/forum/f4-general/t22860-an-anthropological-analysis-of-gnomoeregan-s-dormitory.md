Curious it is to find a Gnome healer practicing the ways of the Holy Light.  Such a heal spell is not often to be found among their kindred.  But in desperate hours, when power goes out, when the world crumbles around you - even these Gnomes managed to find a little faith and the Holy Light answered them.  In time perhaps they might spread this to their bretheren, but for now they answer the call to serve their brothers-in-arms and hold the line.  My hat goes off to these Gnome healers - the first of their kind - and let us hope not the last.

But we are not here to spread the faith of the Holy Light to Gnomes - we are here to answer an important question that has often come up by those who criticize Gnomes.  There is a bit of speculation regarding the quality of Gnome habitation and how that reflects on the Gnomes as a people/culture.  For my experience Gnomes are industrious and friendly, but also eccentric and often unpredictable.  As we enter into the main common area of the Dormitory - we can clearly see the damage that has been done from explosions that originated in the lower districts of the city.  These caused a number of cave-ins along the ceiling - yet there are still support beams and electric lights functioning even after all of that.

However - a question must be asked regarding the nature of the Dormitory's layout.  There are a number of siderooms which show signs of collapse and their function cannot be ascertained, but the steep angles and clean metalwork do not make for the easiest surfaces to either ascend to a Dormitory or leave and return to work - this is an issue we will look at in more detail with another image - but the lack of stairs or some sort of elevator system does not make this the easiest living space to navigate on foot - Though perhaps this shows a little Dwarven bias on my part because there is no way I'd be able to walk up those steep ramps if I were inebriated after a long day of work.  I've not had enough drinks with Gnomes to see how they function while intoxicated.  And this is actually an important point because...

The main floor of the Dormitory seems to have been a common space.  There are signs of chairs and tables which may have been thrown down by Trogg invaders, but it makes more sense for this space to have been a final sort of gathering space for workers beginning their shift, taking a break for meals, or gathering at the end of a busy day.

The presence of the Alarm-o-Bot indicates that this had to have been a high-traffic area of the Dormitory in order to provide warning to the maximum number of residents.  This ultimately leads us to examine the key piece of our studies in the Dormitory - that being the actual beds abandoned by the Gnomes who lived here.

Firstly - we must return to the subject of ramps and the Gnomish use of them.  Perhaps there is something to the size of Gnomes that they do not mind ramps as much as larger species would.  Their surface area:mass ratio and center of gravity would give them an advantage to climbing such angles that would likely cause a Tauren to roll off.
 - Yet Gnome employ ladders for their beds, when they could just as easily walk around to the top of their bed unit and get into bed that way.  I am uncertain as to the need for ladders - perhaps some Gnome might shed some light onto this.
Secondly - The structure of the bed frame.  Part of me wonders about the Cog Wheel Frame underneath the bed.  I speculate that a second bed might be stored at the bottom of the cog and that the bed rotates between the two depending on the time of day.  Rather than traditional hot-bunking, the unit is able to serve multiple Gnomes throughout the day.
 - Another theory would be that the underside of the bed serves as storage space, or perhaps also serves as a personal cleaning unit (toilet, shower, bath, etc).  This might be one of the reasons for the ladder - to access parts of this space that might otherwise be difficult from the base of the unity.
Thirdly - The open-air nature of the Dormitory does tell us a lot about the communal nature of Gnome life.  Perhaps Gnomes do not feel the need for privacy that some other races do.  Keeping secrets to one's self might limit collaboration and innovation.  Still, it is an odd way to live.
 - Yet, for all the criticism Gnomes receive for who they are and how they live - the Gnomes who lived here all made their beds - expecting to return, not knowing it would be the day Gnomeregan fell to chaos... or perhaps the beds have a mechanism that makes them automatically.  Some Gnomish Tinker would likely have the answer to that.

Anyways - that was all I wanted to explore in Gnomeregan this day.  I am left with a number of questions, but I am satisfied to find that the quality of life of Gnomes in their own city was not quite as bad as some have reported.  Wishing you all a very nice day - this has been Edelweiss, Baroness of Shadowforge.
