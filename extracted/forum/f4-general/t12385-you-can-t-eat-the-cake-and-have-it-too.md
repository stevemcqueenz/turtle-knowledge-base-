# YOU CAN'T EAT THE CAKE AND HAVE IT, TOO.

## Post 82437 by Frantsel (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-12-23T12:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82437#p82437 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As we all know balancing is a tedious act and needs lots of reflecting and tuning.

Many hybrid classes always wanted to do damage like regular dps classes.

But here is one thing that does twow incredibly wrong. They "just" boost.
For example paladin. They have incredible defensive capabilities. If you just add dps to it, you basically break the whole game. Thats what we are experiencing right now.

There is a reason why rogues are squishy. Or why warlocks can not heal.
Give druids just more dps and you guessed it. You break the game.
We are destroying all dps classes right now and make them obsolete.
If your a rogue or a warrior you actually cannot break a druid or a paladin even in your wildest dreams.

DPS comes with a price. You NEED to make classes more vulnerable then.

YOU CAN'T EAT THE CAKE AND HAVE IT, TOO.

This is why blizzard never gave paladins or druids masses of dps like what we are experiencing on turtle wow.

Yesterday in MC a druid was doing more dps than all rogues. Not even with white dmg, just with shred.
Rogues never had the power to do most dmg with sinister strike or backstab. It was always the white dmg.
Paladins oneshot ppl all the time.

All I wanna say is the balancing blizzard had was intentional and it was good.
