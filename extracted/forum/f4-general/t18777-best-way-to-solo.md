## Post 128036 by Fornow (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-04-22T21:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128036#p128036 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**challenge modes**
You can disable any of the challenge modes again by talking to a dude in the starting area of your character. So I say to try it and if you find it too tedious just disable it.

 **new content**
Turtle Wow has enough extra content that you shouldn't be hurting for experience, even with the slow & steady challenge active. Note however that the rewards at level 60 will only come if you stick with it all the way, not if you switch back and forth.

The mysteries of Azeroth expansion is just seamlessly integrated into the base game; in fact many quests and novelties are integrated so well that someone who never played the original Wow wouldn't even notice. The Turtle wow Website has a list of all new areas though if you are curios (someone already posted the link above).

 **grouping for dungeons**
For dungeon groups the order is usually: tanks > healers > anyone else. It also helps to directly look in world chat for other members. Type Code: Select all

```
/join world
```

if you're not already in that channel.

Note that if you play a healer class you don't necessarily need healer talents for group play. Any dungeon pre level 60 can be healed offspec. For example you can group heal as a melee shaman just fine, a shadow priest doesn't need to switch to holy, etc. If you do play a hybrid though it's recommended though that you build a 2nd set of gear for healing. Nothing fancy, just greens with intellect and wisdom on them acquired from questing/drops will give you enough mana to sustain your group.

Finally Turtle WoW let's you buy the ability to switch between multiple talent sets for a whooping 350 gold at the arena in Strangle Thorn Vale. It's immensely expensive for a new player but something you definitely want in the long run. By dumping even more gold you can acquire a 3rd and 4th set of talents too switch between.

Happy playing!   smiling_turtle_head
