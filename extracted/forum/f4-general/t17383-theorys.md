Hello to all dear readers.

 - Kara40(Upper karazhan) , Grim batol and horizontal raids:
 - I don't think it's necessarily without reason since the new T3.5 raid that will open sometime is called Kara40 and not Upper karazhan.
 - 1 point: Everyone who completes the raid will get a title (and I don't mean realm first), which if we look at the current title requirements, none of them are such that you can do it in a few days.
 - 2 points: It's no secret that Grim batol will be a big wall for some guilds (according to the current plan) and I think Kara40 is a test for this.
 - 3 points: At the moment I haven't seen any information on how many bosses there will be Kara40, I can expect 8-11 bosses, 1 of which we know by name (Keeper Gnarlmoon (1 boss), 3 bosses (giant arcane elemental), 3 more that we can assume are a humanoid dragon (may be similar to UBRS Pyroguard Emberseer), ghost mage and dreadlord (high chance end boss of the raid).
 - Grim batol: This is probably the hardest thing to think about, but what can wait for us there. The deeper story and enemies will be told unambiguously in the new area. The closest support in this regard is War of the Three Hammers: Shadows moved at her command, and dark things crawled up from the depths of the earth to stalk the Wildhammers in their own halls. So I will have another old gods or twilight hammer in focus here (I think).
 - Horizontal raids:
 - Main: MC , BWL , AQ40 , Naxxramas , Kara40(Upper Karazhan) and Grim Batol
 - 1 boss raid: Onyxia and ES
 - secondary raid: GZ , AQ20 , Kara10(Lower Karazhan halls)
 - It will be the team's decision on which tier "class" they want to make a new raid for, so based on the assumptions I think we will get:
 - 2 1 boss raids
 - 2-3 secondary raids (Scarlet Citadel if it happens)
 - T4.5 and more: Personally, I think they will wait 6-8 years after Grim Batol for a New Tier to appear. I have 2 main reasons for this:
 - 1 point: The server's active raider community ?% will not speed run Grim batol/max gear walking the city or world
 - 2 points: If there is only 1 thing that the turte team learned about the 1.17.2 update, it is that new tier and CC (class change) should not be released together (I think we have 3-5 years for a new CC).

 - Vanilla difficulty:
 - Because of the new raid tiers and CC, we can rightly say that we moved away from classic wow (it was necessary). This may have caused some people to dislike it, but until they cross a certain limit, the classic difficulty may remain (a good question is exactly where that limit is). Raider difficulty is the resistance, buff/debuff from bosses (mechanic) and the number of classes that the developers are likely to play in order to maintain the difficulty (best example is Naxxramas 4 Horsemen). I understand here something like warlock: Enslave demon , only nature damage a boss or things that are not conditions (currently) for one of the boss(es) to die.

 - Outland/Northrend:
 - Perhaps one of the hardest things to develop for many reasons.
 - 1 point: Since most players (if I'm wrong, sorry), but Blizzard encountered outland and northrend for the first time and Classic (vanilla) there was no interaction between the players and these 2 "continents" so we can say that these areas and their specific stories have to be built from scratch.
 - 2 points: The creatures/enemies of the "continent" would be level 55+ (but more like 60+) bringing real end game zones with them (on both).
 - 3 points: There are quite a few characters whose stories need to be continued if they are touched (Illidan, Keal'thas sunstrider, Lady Vashj, Kil'jaden outland, Arthas, Anub'arak Northrend) and these are just a few. It is not possible to leave any of them out.
 - 4 points: They must be at the same level (or very close). In this case, it is likely that if there will be raid(s) there, they will be at a completely different Tier than on the other "continent" (example: Northrend old nerubians kingom T6 and Outland Dark citadel is another T6).
 - 5 points: Lack of time.
