Greetings!

Welcome to the server, enjoy slow pace of leveling :)

You can learn Riding Skill when you hit level 40 or 60 at you racial riding trainer. If you don't see anything, delete your WDB folder and try again!

Also, there's a number of low-level mounts available, such as Riding Turtle from [Lost Turtles] quest and a few others from the shop.

For them, dynamic mount speed applies:
> All mounts now have dynamic speed, the speed will adapt along with your riding skill. Now you can use level 1 or level 40 mounts at 100% speed if you have 150 Riding skill, or level 1 at 60% speed if you have 75 Riding skill. Also, all level 1 mounts will have speed depending on your level (the speed increase equals to your level divided by 2). For example, if you use the Riding Turtle or any of the tallstriders at level 30, they will have 15% speed.
