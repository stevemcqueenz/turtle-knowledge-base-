HOWDY. Just wanted to slap another fun topic onto the wall of doom and gloom that is the recent forum posts. How 'bout a discussion on what character concept everyone has in mind for the PvP server if y'all are planning to try it out? Heck, we can even talk about what fast food chain has the best fries. Let's just do something for the heck of it. I'll start us off!

I'm pretty indecisive on what I plan to roll, but I've had a few ideas so far:

Undead Shadow Priest: As someone who was never really a fan of priest in general, I'm currently levelling a shadow priest of my own, the same one in my signature. And, to be honest, it's been the most fun with a class/spec that isn't druid related I've had in a while.

Goblin (Combat?) Rogue: I'm not a huge rogue guy either, but in all honesty, the one rogue I do have has been alright. Not to mention the whole "PvP class" stereotype that they're given. On top of that, melee always has been and will be my favorite play style.

Tauren Feral Druid: Ah, yes. The spec everyone should reroll. The definitive best playstyle this game has ever witnessed. One specialization that is capable of fitting two roles, and actually doing them well (Sorry enhancement). Okay, all simping aside, the most fun I've had with this game has been with feral (or guardian in retail. Ick, retail!) and I would be lying if I said I wasn't tempted to roll the spec on the PvP server. Because you know, druids are good at that, and what can I say, ravaging a mage that can't root, slow or poly me while I heal myself is fun as heck.

Troll Fury Warrior: This was my first ever character in any version of vanilla. And in fact also where my internet persona comes from. Because trolls are big, and blue. You get the idea. At the time, my ideal class fantasy was (And kinda still is) Big dude in cool armor crushing things and taking blows. So, obviously, warrior fit that bill. Why troll? Well, they always just looked cool to me. Big dangerous tusks, agile figures and berserking. Just overall felt badass. And come on. Two toes is obviously the perfect amount.

Quick Edit: I FORGOT THE FRIES. Anyways, here's my opinion on most canadian based fast food chain french fries.

Mcdonalds: This one's weird, because I know for sure that the general quality is shit and I shouldn't like them. They're greasy, there's hardly any crunch, they're a tad bit too salty for most people, AND APPARENTLY NOT EVEN MADE WITH POTATOES, but even with all that, I like them? I can't even describe it. They just taste good to me. But like any 'ol chip, their quality declines with their temperature. Except even more.

Wendys: I haven't been to Wendys in a while, but from what I remember, they're pretty good. I tend to like a french fry with just a little skin (Just like ma wehmen huehue) and this particular variety of fry scratches that itch pretty well. Also, fries and frosties are iconic.

Harveys: This one's native to canada, and is particularly a burger place. Burgers are phenomenal, but fries... Well, I don't remember enough about them to care much. Pretty sure they're good, but it's been probably a literal year since I've been to Harveys (let alone any fast food restaurant) with how broke I am.

Burger King: I don't even know. I hardly touch burger king let alone their fries, so this one's undecided.

Mary Browns: The fucking wedges. They're just... Perfect. I know they're not fries, but I figured I'd take a moment to mention the godly gift that is Mary Browns

KFC: I think they're alright. Not much to say really.

Taco Bell: I mean, I like fries supreme? But it's kinda just fries with toppings, so is it like some sort of poutine? Who knows.
