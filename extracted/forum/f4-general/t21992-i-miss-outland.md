# I miss Outland

## Post 152431 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-20T03:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152431#p152431 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the few things I liked about TBC is Outland. Yes, I know it was partly a complete retcon, but strangely, it's very well done and interesting.

Also, I like the variety of climates and the sense of destruction in some areas like Hellfire Peninsula or Netherstorm, or the beauty and tranquility of Nagrand, coupled with the depressing feeling of Terokkar, or how can we forget about Blade's Edge Mountains... no, I'd rather forget about that one.

I mean, I don't know how Outland could be included without leaving out Azeroth, but I think when the time comes, I'd love to see some of Blizzard's Outland included.

I'll always say that Blizzard did a good job with Outland and Quel'thalas, since Outland still feels like a nostalgic respite from Vanilla. The problem was the lore made from retcons.

I know we'll eventually have to go to Outland. Kael'thas and the Illidari are there, part of the Old Alliance too, and Outland has a large portion of Orcish lore.
I think a good time to start paving the story towards Outland would be after Echoes of Quel'thalas. It's the most natural and obvious path, so my suggestion is to use part or all of TBC's Outland. It's priceless, and we can't deny that the mapping is simply perfect.
