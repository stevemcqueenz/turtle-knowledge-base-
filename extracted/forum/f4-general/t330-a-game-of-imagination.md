# A Game of Imagination

## Post 1747 by Paw (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2019-01-03T08:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1747#p1747 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good day guys!

I would like to bring us closer a bit as a community through an activity. Since roleplay is a play on words I would like us to use them by describing a fantasy situation at a given place, at a given time of your own choosing and up to your own imagination if you are up for the game! Picking a music, or a picture even to support the feeling you wish to mediate!

Since I came up with the idea I feel only natural that I start.

I choose this picture I found while browsing through warcraft fan art pictures for my future facebook posts. Since I started I got very fond of doing it since most of the artists who share their drawings are really skilled and its a real pleasure to submerge into the world they picture. And that is where I got the idea also.

So shall I?

The hour is around noon. The is sun up in the sky with only a few cottony clouds flying around. However within the depth of this forest the sunshine cannot get through its thick foliage but some light is still present. The fountain of the mountain forest is glowing with glimmering brightness within the dark gloom.

![Image](https://images-wixmp-ed30a86b8c4ca887773594c2.wixmp.com/intermediary/f/01adb479-868d-4af9-b7e9-dfa736797892/da2pu7m-86f9c5fb-986e-4177-8a1e-d96c719ecac1.jpg/v1/fill/w_1384,h_577,q_70,strp/saligia__the_forest_by_balazsketyi_da2pu7m-pre.jpg)

That is why two from the village on the outskirts journeyed here. To fill a vialful from the well. They use it for alchemical purposes to heal and mend and sell them on rare occasions for adventuring mages or use them for a ritual to empower one in their future battles or to give them clairvoyance and some similar other purposes at times also.
The two who came are the shamans of the village. A woman who strangely looks as if she was 30 years old but in truth she was already over 50... and a young boy with eyes of abyss and movements like an animal from the wild: agile, lenient and precise as if he himself was of the wilds.
They walked closer, moving slowly through the roots that covered the ground to fill the phial they brought for a druid of a far away land.
As they finished she casted a spell borrowing a tiny power from the well for their own comforts to make their way home quicker. Thus she summoned forth a nightsaber from the spirit realm on who's back they managed to reach swiftly the end of the unkind and dark forest that apart from the moonwell's wonderous existence held only gloom and chill for within its depths a horror was sleeping.
