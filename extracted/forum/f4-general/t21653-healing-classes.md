Options available: Druid, Shaman, Priest, Paladin
Differences
Druid: healing over time effects, the heal low and constant, without relying on crits. Can single target big heals but very mana intensive. Can keep a group alive long enough so they can get other healer's big heal

Shaman: multi healing spam. They heal their targets and the heal bounces to nearby targets, giving prio to the most injured ones. They also can single target big heals and cast costly single target fast heals. They struggle a bit with mana sustain and with proper totem control.

Priest: has all the healer's tools, so is the most versatile: single target big heals, healing over time, damage shields, aoe heals, you name it. Can use quick heals for single target, but those are expensive to cast. They also struggle a bit with mana.

Paladin: single target healer with a bit of melee healer. Their best heal is a single target, quick heal that is super cheap to cast. Paladins don't go oom unless they want to, and they do their job very well at keeping one target alive, but are very limited. Single target heals for the most part, no damage shields and no healing over time. They can go in melee range and do some aoe healing like the shaman, healing nearby allies with a priority of healing the most injured ones, but when going in melee isn't an option, they rely on keeping 1 or 2 targets alive. They do a very good job tho.

During leveling you don't need to spec into healing, just carry an int set, plenty of drinks and cast max rank spells.

Shamans are really fun and how they got fixed in this version reminds me of TBC polished versions. So first thing first: separate GC for each totem, so you can drop them fast. You can also pick them back up with another spell, making relocation really easy and intuitive.

So, what you get in resto shaman:

 - chain heal;
 - a lot of utility (intrerrupt Earth Shock lvl 1 and grounding totem - you can basically take out a lot of casters, soaking even 2 spells at once); that is huge. also windfury totem, for melee cleave you are the goat healer.
 - you got a Spirit link, that is just great to pair with the chain heal. using this on a fairly geared druid tank, prot warrior or pala(in this order) makes him virtually unkillable.
 - water shield + blessing of wisdom + Mana Spring totem usually does the trick. The population of paladin on Nordanaar is just decent(cross-factions instances/raids also a big plus for PvE).
 - solo slow heal for tank, mana efficient; faster smaller heal with less mana efficiency, usually using on some troublesome DPS-ers.
 - 3 min CD instant heal spell. good combo - load a big heal and instant another big heal for those hard to soak damaging phases or ninja pulls.
 - slow totem, dmg reduction totem, strength totem, resistance totems, the toolkit goes on.

I am having a great time with my resto shaman. Used to play Holy Paladin in classic, but something about the shaman on this version really clicked. Plus troll racial, 10% haste is also insane.

Hope that helped!
