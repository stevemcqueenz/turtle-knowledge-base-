# Understanding the intention behind the transmog system

## Post 92550 by Zeran (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-04-11T08:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92550#p92550 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, the current transmog system works such that, for any item that you can equip, you can make another item in that slot look like it.

What I'd like to know is, when that system was designed, was the intention that the transmogs be used by level 60's ONLY, who don't need to upgrade their gear, either as often or at all?

Because if you do it on a levelling character, then you'd be upgrading gear pretty often. And since the tmogs are item bound instead of slot bound, you would have to keep re-applying it through new fashion coins.

Therefore, from the point of view of a level 60, it makes more sense. But from the point of view of a levelling character, it makes less sense.

Follow up question, if anybody knows: is making a transmog piece affect the item SLOT itself instead of the item a technical engine limitation, or it is possible?
