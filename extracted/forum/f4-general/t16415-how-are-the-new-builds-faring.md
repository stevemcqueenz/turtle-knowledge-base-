# How are the new builds faring?

## Post 111901 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-09T15:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111901#p111901 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I'm curious about your experience with playing a build which didn't exist before or was just a meme.
How does it feel? How good it is? Any prejudice from the others?
F.ex. any uproar when a melee hunter SRs legacy in Kara10?

Personally I spent a lot of time figuring out the new shaman tank (wanna write a really long article about it someday). Having a blast, loving some of its features (like aoe threat) but acknowledging its shortcomings (the combination of being fragile and lacking def CD).
My biggest positive until now was people's attitude towards it. I sparingly use totems to save mana and maintain the high tempo of a dungeon. Very rarely got called out for things like 'why don't you use x or y totem'. Specially true for WF which I use sparingly. If the melee DPS is too high I avoid it unless killing the target's fast kill is a must, otherwise I have to spam abilities to keep threat -> more drinking -> slower dungeon. Noone rioted for the usual lack of WF until now.

My biggest negative is those who get LFT-d into the same group as I do, see that the tank is a shaman and either insta-quit or improvise a quick excuse. It never happened hurtfully but happened kinda frequently, specially during the earlier days.
