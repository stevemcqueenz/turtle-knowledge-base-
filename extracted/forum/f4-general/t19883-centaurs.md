i love this post. because it's not a whine post, or another pvp balance post based on anecdotal evidence.

to the topic at hand i think a neutral faction is very much possible. what needs to be defined is the player experience.

if you are fine grinding mobs from level 10 to level 60 with almost no quests except the other neutral faction ones, absolutely doable.

if you want the tailored experience of quest hubs, this is a huge demand.

frankly i would love to see a 3rd faction, even if barebones and with little to no quests. i think a lot of players would be up to the challenge (if hardcore and lunatic challenges are anything to go by).

the tricky parts are:
 - how do you create new lore that respects old one to justify new races in a 3rd faction
 - how do you make BGs work? merc NPC in capital?

i sincerely hope this is something the team actively explores at some point.

> **Zulnam wrote: Tue Jun 17, 2025 7:44 pm**
> - how do you make BGs work? merc NPC in capital?

I would:
 * Start by going the "Easy" route, and just prohibit them from entering BGs.  Then, over time, I would see how active these characters are.  If it is determined that the time investment is worth it, then I would...

 * Set up an NPC that gives you a choice of joining Horde or Alliance for BGs; I suspect this would not be a simple undertaking and could potentially break things under the hood.  This would likely be a one-time choice so that players aren't constantly swapping back and forth.  You could then create a new Donation Point Reward to undo that choice.  Finally, if (and this is a big if) there is a sizeable player base, and a very determined dev or two, then I would...

 * Set up each Neutral race with different Reps with all the main races, and apply Rep to all the quests and enemies.  Then let Rep determine where you can go.  Most NPC races would start out as Hostile towards the main races, with perhaps a few exceptions.  Furbolg would likely be Neutral, or even Friendly, with the Night Elves, and I could see Ogres and Gnolls being Neutral with the Steamwheedle Cartel as they are often hired as mercenaries/guards.  Of course, this would be an absolutely massive undertaking, so I don't expect anything like this to happen. :)
