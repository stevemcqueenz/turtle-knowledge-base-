# What am I missing here?

## Post 69448 by Pichbin — 2023-10-07T20:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69448#p69448 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all I've been around for a couple of months, got two 60s, did some raiding. Not a tourist five minutes in.

With that said, as the title goes, what am I missing here?

First of all for a server this highly populated I don't believe I spent more time feeling and playing isolated than on this. This is being presented by many as some wholesome friendly RP community where it's all sugar and sunshine but when you actually start playing the reality is something completely different.

First of all there's the extremely buggy and weirdly implemented HC segregation that's there for no other purpose than for some to cheese their way to an exclusive mount and to ruin the experience for everyone else, possibly even them. You can't group with them, you can't trade with them and you can only pray they don't join your groupfinder queue because they will break it. Oh, but it's a really really hard challenge! Do it yourself! Yeah, I fail to see the challenge in playing on a pve bubble farming green mobs and gray quests while often being babysat by a 60. The whole welfare bags and gear every 10 levels or so seems rather cheap as well.

Warmode. Where do I begin with this? This is the biggest trap on this server and it's a griefers wet dream. My first experience with the server about four months ago was running across a field of bones and player skeletons roughly five meters from the place you spawn in the world, because some nice person camped everyone flagged for some six hours straight. Everyone who ever played a WSG bracket in the 19s or 29s range knows that "twinks" basically killed early BGs and put off many a newer player off pvp in any form for the remainder of their time in WoW. So, instead of extending or refining mechanics already in the game, like say those watchtowers in EPL warmode ends up optimally being a grindmode since you're best off going to some less popular corner of the map and grinding mobs for hours. Again completely avoiding any communal play in the game for the best part. **My suggestion would be then to swap the XP bonus for a renown one.** At least in that way you would be encouraged to seek pvp.

Public chats and moderation. There's this meme, essentially that mods are super strict on twow. There are threads right now that suggest as much. This is garbage. I have my share of things I dislike and even despise about this server but credit where credit is due. The GM team was supremely tolerant and nice in every instance I've either seen them speak or have interacted with me directly. The general community on the other hand? Dear. Fu*king. God. I've been on the internet for good twenty years now, and I can say without any doubt that either the /world or the former newcomers guild will put to shame even the worst cesspool twich chat, the most toxic youtube comment section and the most cancerous imageboard imaginable. I'm not even on about racism, direct cusses or such, and those are definitely there. But the sheer quantity of braindead meme spewing, repeating emoji, buzzwords and on occasion plain degeneracy is astounding. For one example there was an in depth discussion on pros and cons of having sexual relations with animals in /world yesterday that went on for good several hours and was still going strong when I finally logged out. Complete with suggestion for antibiotics to avoid any infections and tier listings for which domestic animal has the best "cunny"

The new player experience. No one was born knowing it all. That's obvious. But I have never seen more pigheaded players, more disinformation and more idiocy anywhere than on this server. When I first started playing I asked random 60s how are things, do they have some suggestions and such. I'd try and join a leveling guild and find out what has changed and how. I've read up on the forums and even dug up stuff on the discord. What I didn't do is roll a warrior, dressed him in cloth or leather, grouped up with three other warriors and then yelled at the rogue that he should tank because I'm DPS lol. I was swore at in English, German, Spanish, Russian and I believe in Turkish for not summoning someone despite not having any ability to do so. **Some of the critical base game features and twow changes especially should be presented on launch or at least in a more direct fashion**. Also worth noting is that  this isn't a low level problem. I asked at least fifty people what's the debuff limit on twow, and each time I got a different answer. I've had conversations and raided with people that don't realize that's even a thing. **How can we fix this? A mentor program?**

God, this has already turned into a rant and I'm not even 10% in.   sad_turtle_head
