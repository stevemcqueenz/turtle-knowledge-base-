## Post 126977 by Pepesmite (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2025-04-16T07:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126977#p126977 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You can play the English client on the SA server. To do this:

Create an account on the SA server.

Download the game client for the EU server.

Launch the launcher and select the SA region.

Once you’ve completed these steps, you’ll be able to play using the English client.
