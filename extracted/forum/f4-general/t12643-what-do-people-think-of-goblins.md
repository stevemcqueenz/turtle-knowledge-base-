# What do people think of Goblins?

## Post 84236 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-10T17:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84236#p84236 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm a fan of the Quel'dorei and I've always been an Alliance, but now I want to know, what does the Goblin Horde honestly think?
Do they think they receive enough attention?
Would they have been exchanged for another race?
Do the Goblins seem like a good idea to you?

I mean, really after humans, I think that Goblins are a very important race at the lore level, I mean, they have several important locations in the world, neutral since they like gold and do not see factions, but they have:
 -Ratchet - Neutral
 -Gadgetzan - Neutral
 -Booty Bay - Neutral
 -Steamweedle Port - Neutral
 -Sparkwater Port - Horde

And they are recruited for their great knowledge in engineering and chemistry.
I consider them to be the gnomes of the Horde, only green and with long ears.
I think they haven't been a bad addition to the Horde, but it's a shame people don't see them favorably.

Sorry for some names errors, the Google Traductor usually translates the names in strange ways.
