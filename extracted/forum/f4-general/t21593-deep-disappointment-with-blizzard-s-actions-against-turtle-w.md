# Deep Disappointment with Blizzard’s Actions Against Turtle WoW

## Post 149614 by Strikeitbg — 2025-08-31T18:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149614#p149614 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Dear Blizzard Entertainment,

I am writing this message filled with frustration, anger, and deep disappointment at your recent legal action against the Turtle WoW project. What you are doing is not just shortsighted, but an insult to the very community that has loved, supported, and carried your game for decades.

Let’s be honest: you failed your own creation. World of Warcraft, once a masterpiece that defined a genre, has been driven into a state where it feels unrecognizable to the very players who built its success. It became bloated, soulless, and alienated the fans who kept your company alive. And yet, instead of admitting where you went wrong and supporting the few passionate communities who try to breathe life back into the game, you attack them with lawyers.

Turtle WoW is not some malicious enterprise stealing millions from your pockets. It is a fan-driven labor of love, created and maintained by people who work for free, who only cover their server costs, and who give players something that you failed to provide. It is an oasis for those who still believe in what World of Warcraft once stood for. These people are not your enemies – they are the only reason your game’s legacy even survives.

And what do you do? You try to destroy them. Just like you destroyed Nostalrius – another shining moment where your community showed you the path forward, and instead of embracing it, you crushed it. Years later, nothing has changed. The same arrogance, the same ignorance, the same tone-deaf corporate nonsense.

Blizzard, if you truly cared about the game you made 20 years ago, you would not be wasting time and money trying to kill Turtle WoW. You would support it. You would embrace it. You would show the world that you can still be the company that listens to its community, instead of the one that silences it.

Stop embarrassing yourselves with these petty lawsuits. Stop strangling the passion of the people who love your game more than you apparently do. Show humanity, show respect, and show that you still have some connection to the players who carried your legacy all these years.

Turtle WoW is beautifully developed, it’s made with love, and it gives life to a part of your game you abandoned. Yes, you hold the intellectual property. Yes, you can shut it down. But should you? No. Be better than that.
The choice is simple: Either you continue down this path of destroying what little goodwill your fans still hold, or you take the brave step of supporting the people who are actually keeping World of Warcraft’s spirit alive.
Because let me tell you this: the community will remember what you do here.

And so I leave you with this:
SUPPORT TURTLE WOW. DON’T DESTROY IT.
STAND WITH THE COMMUNITY, NOT AGAINST IT.
TURTLE WOW MUST LIVE.

Sincerely,
A furious but still loyal player who has had enough
