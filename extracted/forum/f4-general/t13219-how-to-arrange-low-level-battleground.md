# How to arrange low level battleground

## Post 88619 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-02-24T08:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88619#p88619 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Instruction for players

When I had plenty of time, I used to arrange low level battlegrounds on other private server with low population. I used some shady tricks, but they worked.
It is easier to do with help of your friends from other faction.
I had horde twink and I managed to get revered with Warsong on lvl 19 on server with ~500 population. To achieve this, I made dwarf priest with some green gear on Alliance side. Can't say it was good twink, but his job is to encourage other players to keep playing and not to die immediately.

How to encourage:
 1. Gifts
Stock your twink on opposite side with [Heavy Copper Broadsword](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3487), [Big Bronze Knife](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3848), [Greater Magic Wand](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11288) and some crafted stuff from [Best and optimal gear for 10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4714). Crafted shoulders are good itea too.
Give away those items to your teammates who joined, if they are actual upgrade of their current weapons.
You can also hoard [Healing Potions](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=929) and give them away in stacks of 3. Sharpening stones could work too. Those gifts should be cheap and should not be burden for you to prepare in large quantities. [Heavy sharpening stone](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=2871) could work, but [Coarse](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=2863) one - won't (stone is expensive). Armor patches and buckles are good too. You can also level enchanting on that supportive characters and throw enchants on others gear.
white [Heavy Weave Bracers](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3590) enchanted with [+7 stamina](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=13945) are good gift too. They can be used by anyone and +7 stamina is huge boost. Bracers at this level usually suck and this gift will be greatly appreciated.
 2. Buff and heal
That healing twink was not used to carry flags. I usually stayed in middle and just helped people to survive. When people died, I retreat to GY and wait for next wave of teammates. It is important to play support role. Your teammates will stay and fight even if they are not very good in flag carrying.
 3. Write supportive stuff in chat. Talk with people. At least tell them where you are going so they could run around you and have some fun.
Tell every participant "thank you" when battleground ends.
Ask people "will you be online tomorrow in the same time?".
If you have high level character, you can ask leveling people to craft those gifts. Personal communication is more important than using AH in this matter.

Best way is to find a buddy on opposite side, ask him to do all this stuff and to do same stuff on your faction.
I used to play in two windows simultaneously by different sides at once. You should absolutely avoid killing your own character on opposite side. I believe every private server can detect situation when you kill your own characters. All servers have rules to punish that behavior.

Most important rule: Don't humiliate people by your real (offensive) twink.
Carry your flags, ignore and avoid combat. You need reputation, you don't need honor. If possible, kill only enemy FC.
If you start graveyard killing, then players from other faction will just leave. And most importantly, they won't join again, regardless of amount of gifts you gave them by supportive twink.
You probably get more honor in that match, but this will be the only match in this evening.
If you keep matches short and fast, then people won't get tired and do multiple matches.
You can even die sometimes by your real twink to give some hope to opposite faction. You'll win anyway, but your death increase chance to do battleground once more this evening.

Make a schedule
If peole know that battlegrounds are happening in some specific time, they will set up their IRL schedule to join those matches. Battleground schedule should not change over time.
Choose best time for you. If it will be not convenient for you, then all arrangement job will be in vain.
Promote your schedule in all possible ways: Discord, Forum, in-game chat. Regular reports telling how many battlegrounds were made and how many twinks were on each side will help.
Have some patience, people will not gather immediately. They need some time to prepare mentally and gameplay-wise. They might level specific character for this battleground or level engineering for him to feel prepared.

While things are not set up yet, gather people by whispering them
I used to use "/who 10-19" and macro which loops over results of who and prints something like "HI, can you help me to start warsong 10-19? You can register, but don't need to join. To register, use icon with crossed sword near minimap.".
Often people don't know that you can register to battleground in any place in the world.
I confess that back in the days I made 6 accounts with lvl 10 characters to boost battleground queue. I registered from all of them and then battleground started, I joined only by real characters.
Battleground proc is very important. A lot of people might play their main and switch to alt/twink only when battleground actually started. They don't do that instantly. You need some time to park your character, logout, login.

If amount of people is still too low, then help people to do elite (or even normal) quests with them. 2 players are usually enough. You can make connections with other people by hanging in the world and helping people here and there. For example every starting zone have boss like "Hogger". Hang near him, help people to kill him and add them to friend list.
You don't need to make guild out of this stuff. Friend list is usually enough.

Main point: you don't need to be super-geared twink by yourself. You can get 2k hp on your lvl 19 pally, but you won't use it if there will be no battleground happening. Make a working system first.
Nothing is really secret here, but for some reason, almost all people with twink concentrate only on their own gear and consumables instead of people around them. As a result they get character for exhibition, but they don't actually play the game.
