## Post 55292 by Xerilin (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-07-31T04:01:00Z

Stealing this from discord:

Near your key ring there is a red question mark, press it to send an in-game ticket.

If you have pfUI and you don't have the micro-bar enabled, you can type `/gm` to open the interface.

You don't need to be online for tickets to be solved. Support will you answers via in-game mail.
