## Post 89674 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-03-08T11:00:00Z

PVE server has cross faction.
Spriest... I never played one but seen a lot of them.
They were buffed pretty decently, they are not a 1of raid utility slots to serve warlocks anymore.
Main gameplay is passive healing and (some) mana regen to party while dealing (single target) damage. They even have a heal in shadow form I think.
For the bad part: high threat, specally at dungeons mobs can run towards you a bit too often if you don't glue your eyes to the threat meter. Also, not bad but not outstanding damage. Every time I saw a spriest in raid, he was a bit behind with DPS but added a decent value to HPS by passive-healing its party. Def a fun choice.
