# Making Helpful Un'Goro Crystals

## Post 46345 by Rokit (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-05-17T21:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46345#p46345 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Un'goro Crystal consumables are hardly worthless, but they could be a great lifeline for players who don't want to be forced into Engineering, at the mercy of the AH and market, or just enjoy the Dinosaurs of Un'Goro.

By changing the abilities assigned to them, moving the crystal consumables into the same tier of effectiveness as popular consumables will give players a good reason to exist in this endgame zone (Like Blasted Lands, or Winterspring).
- Crystal Charge : Increase  Damage Range to 450-750 (Identical to Goblin Sapper Charge), Increase Radius to 7yds (3 less than GSC). This will keep the Engineering tradeoff of self-damage as compensation for 3yds more radius. (Geometry heads will know how large of an area difference 30% radius is)
- Crystal Ward : Increase armor to 450 to match Elixir of Superior Defense. Double the duration is the benefit Alchemists will still have over this consumable.
- Crystal Spire : Leave as is, while this doesn't stack with Thorns, it is a classless option to have the buff for various farm strats.
- Crystal Force : This is actually higher than Blasted Lands buff but doesn't stack with Class buffs. Change this to use Gizzard Gum's ability (+25 Spirit for an hour) as an alternative Spirit Buff that stacks with PW: Spirit.
- Crystal Restore : Change the 670 heal duration to over 3 seconds (Instead of 15). It shares CD with all the "runes" anyways.
- Crystal Yield : Leave as is. Its a small benefit but +1 stack of sunder is hardly worthless if you aren't hitting armor pen cap.
These changes would improve the number of use cases for each of these consumables without changing their identity.

Most of these changes can be implemented Database-Side without needing a client update until you want to update the tooltips. They would improve player choice for rewards extracted from gameplay in specific, preferred, regions.
