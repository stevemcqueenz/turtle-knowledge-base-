## Post 119570 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-10T21:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119570#p119570 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'd suggest to go as a DPS (even as a priest) if you have social anxiety.
It means more waiting time but healing, while not hard means some pressure. As a DPS, you can 'just get along' more easily until you become confident in what you do.
I'd advise to get a threatmeter, too. In short, if you cause more threat than the tank, the mob will attack you. Just get one, play and make sure that you stay below the tank with threat. I think that's the chillest dungeoning I can think about.
Also, if you go to a dungeon, just make sure that your buffs are up all the time. Stam and spirit on everyone, shadow when needed. You can calmly ask anytime if you are unsure if shadow protection will help in a given place.
But the same works for any class. If you go to a dungeon with a class which can buff, make sure that the buffs are up!

'Learning my role/rotation if I'm healing in a dungeon, what spells I should keep on different people, etc.'
I really don't recommend starting as a healer if you have social anxiety. If you wanna do it anyway:
As a healer, your job is to keep everyone alive with as few mana as possible. You keep everyone alive and you save mana so if bad thing happens, you have reserves to save the day. It's not easy though. First just heal as you feel right (but dont pw: shield rage users unless they are about to die). As time goes, save lower-rank heals on your action tab, too. Sometimes there is no need to heal big and lose a bunch of mana, it just goes to overheal.
First: Learn how to keep people alive with heals even if you are not mana efficient.
Second: Start downranking and become more and more mana-efficient.
If you are anxious, have some mana pots in the bag just in case. And water, just in case no mage is nearby.

'How to "fit in" and not upset other people for things I should know'
I would say that if people get cocky about a 20 y.o. game made for children, you should just laugh at them, but you have anxiety. Just be relatively silent and nice, most people are cool. Inevitably, you'll meet some jerks though. It's not a shame to leave a party if you feel bullied, just ask them first to be more kind. If that doesnt happen, ignore list the guy and leave.
Also, if you feel comfortable with it, try to speak up if f.ex. there is a loot you want. Roll need if you'd use the item even if a random guy throws a tantrum that it is 'his' item. At least try to do it.
There are new people in the server, asking how dungeons work shouldn't be a shameful thing.

'What's my expected equipment when doing dungeons? Right now I only have what I 've been finding when questing (since money is hard to come by when also training skills and such).''
That's all good, don't ovething this. You go to dungeons to get the gear. I'd say some dungeons have some expectations among players but they are really late dungeons (like BM). Have 0 worry about gear, dungeon takes care of it for you.

'When it comes to loot, I know I can only use cloth. I don't want to anger people doing the wrong thing with need/greed like I've heard happening. How do I know what's "acceptable" for me to 'need' on? Is it just if it's cloth and an upgrade compared to my current equipment? Or even if it's cloth and and an upgrade compared to what I have, are there still times when I shouldn't 'need' based on stats?'

Do this:
If it's an upgrade on the build you've come with? Need, no questions ask.
If it's an upgrade on a build you sometimes use, ask around. Depends on the people.
If it's very valuable boe, wait. If the others greed, greed, if it was all need, need.
Never need as a priest if it's something you cant wear, unless last point.

If someone gets angry because you rolled for an upgrade for your mainspec, ignore him if you can. That's what you are supposed to do.

Unless you go to a dungeon at the minimal level and need virtually everything while being carried but I mostly saw this from hybrids. God, I hate those people.

'Is there any "pre-research" I'm expected to do on a dungeon beforehand? I kind of don't want to ruin it myself (or waste hours watching youtube videos) if that's not expected, but I also don't want to do something wrong and ruin it for everyone else.'

Not really, just ask around if something is unclear or ask them to tell you if there's anything special you gotta know.
I mean you can pre-research but the first time you are kinda expected to do so is raids.
