# Holy healing, Nature healing

## Post 97445 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-20T18:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97445#p97445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

In this game people can heal with two schools of magic, holy and nature. It always felt weird to me that flavor- and gameplay-wise there are no real distinctions between the two. Would have been cool if nature and holy healing had manifested with different mechanics.
This is not a change suggestion, just a general opinion.
For example, druid healers are mostly defined by HoT - still we can't call HoT a manifestation of nature healing because shaman HoT is at least questionnable (healing totem) and priests do have HoT with holy damage.
I wish it looked something like this:
 - What both schools of magic can do: casted single-target and multi-target healing (greater heal, healing wave, prayer of healing, chain heal, flash of light etc).
 - HoT is nature only (better healing totem for shaman, maybe riptide - some good HoT-base for both nature-using healing class).
 - Shield-based prevention is holy only (reworked pw-shields, maybe a watered-down divine shield which is castable on targets - a general rework for shield-based damage prevention).

Why?
I think it fits the flavor.
Nature regrows after taking damage. Roads disappear under forests, etc. Nature heals over time.
While holy magic judges and protects you from harmful effects. Not even letting you being damaged - preventing the effect of the evil deed.

Would have been awesome imo if the different magic schools used for the same role would have distinct, flavor-based characteristics like this.

Holy (and shadow) is psychic, which means it is the internal state of the affected that brings on a difference, by being attuned to the powers underlying faith.

First there was Holy and Shadow.
In fact there was only Holy, Shadow is Holy turned inward on itself. There was a space filled only with Holy, and this space was non-homogeneous, holy light concentrated in certain areas more, leaving gaps, which got filled with a twisted version of Holy, a parasistic force lacking connection to the divine, feeding on itself and all else.
So Holy and Shadow become distinguished.
They collide and the outcome is Arcane (ordered combination) and Fel (disordered combination).
Nature is Arcane infused with Holy.
It is the source of Being manifesting in creation through life.

Nature is this force, an intelligence that creates and sustains life.
Spirits of both lifeformals and matter, of elements and of even concepts, are manifestations of this intelligence, it's the life-force gaining individual volition.

Priests and Paladins seek the blessing of the primordial  force behind all creation, which is Holy Light. By aligning their inner state with what the Holy Light is by adhering to virtues, selflessness faith sacrifice etc, they channel the power of this force to bring back order into lifeforms, to bring them closer to the holy light. The healing is telepathic and entirely internal for the target

Druids commune with the ordered intelligence that us nature, but as raw force that creates life. They become the vessel for this force of nature, in a way they become a sort of spirit, a conduit for life and it's goal which is harmonious existence of life. The healing is done by communing with the force that makes up the targeted organism and having it restore its homeostasis.

Shamans commune with the personified force of nature in the form of spirits.
Taurens specifically with the spirits of ancestors, trolls with the spirits of lifeforms, orcs with spirits of elements, seeking their guidance and blessing.
The healing is like the druid one, but with an intermediary, the spirit is communing with lifeforce of the target on behalf of the shaman.

I hope this insight into the lore inspires to come up with interesting ideas how to differentiate various healing done in the game.
