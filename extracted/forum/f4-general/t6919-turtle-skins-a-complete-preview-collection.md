# Turtle Skins: A Complete Preview Collection

## Post 43763 by Syrathegreat (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-04-29T13:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43763#p43763 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi fellow turtles!   turtle_tongue

So TWoW offers a rather expansive list of character skins on their store, however the in-game menu is severely limited, providing little to no preview of the skin, nor any information about if it is gender restricted, etc. Furthermore some of the skins previews given on the store can be misleading due to lighting etc. There is [this post](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=2917) from last year, however it is unfortunately incomplete.

As a result I took it upon myself to compile a preview of all the models available, with different angles for you to use as a reference guide when looking to purchase. I have also added notes where relevant when something might not be clear from just the images. This spreadsheet is searchable by race and gender using the drop-down menus at the top (huge thank you to **Lexie** for getting those working on google sheets for me!).

Please note that many of these skins do not have underwear textures and therefore are more like barbie dolls when they do not have armour on.

[You can access the full sheet here.](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1GboqzwrXKFc-BJbWRBHc7s2Gt4l0xmWaQICy1jgdWg0/edit#gid=1687481758&fvid=1413648540) (the imbedded version won't allow me to zoom out - there are a bunch more columns!)

[embed: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1GboqzwrXKFc-BJbWRBHc7s2Gt4l0xmWaQICy1jgdWg0/preview#gid=1687481758]

Additionally the link to this spreadsheet has been pinned to #general and #support in the official server discord for easy access.

In addition, I wanted to highlight to the devs some issues and feedback I encountered while completing this project - if you could have a look at this, that would be fab! I've detailed the major ones here, but there is more detail in the Notes column of the spreadsheet itself.

• On the female dwarf model, all of the Wildhammer skins are the same regardless of the one you purchase.
• Similarly, all the Blackrock Clan skins appear the same on the female model.
• The female Chaos Orc skin uses the same texture as the Dreadskull Clan II skin, rather than the male Chaos Orc skin.
• The Sandfury Clan skin is unavailable on female trolls despite the preview image being of a female troll. Judging by the appearance of the male Sandfury Clan skin which is completely different, it appears this preview image is actually of the female Zombie skin instead.

Please let me know if you need any further information.
