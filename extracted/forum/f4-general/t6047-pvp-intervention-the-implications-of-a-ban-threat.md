I've been seeing many points and arguments come up involving PvP-related bannings and warnings, and I want to ask a genuine question to the Turtle staff.

Why would Turtle implement a Dishonorable Kill reputation system, complete with custom rank/title, if all that happens for gaining the rep. is the staff threatening to ban you?

Are you guys just trying to bait people into killing Civilian NPCs for the rep just so you can swoop in and ban them for breaking server rules?

It's completely contradictory.

If there is going to be such a divide in how the server WANTS to handle DhKs and ACTUALLY handles DhKs - it needs to be resolved. Seriously: the random, wanton ban threats and bans are going to do absolutely nothing but make people paranoid to WPvP - and we aren't even talking about killing players, just NPCs.

If you don't want those NPCs killed - just make them invulnerable or something. Why encourage DhKs with Reputation levels and titles, if they can't even be attacked without a ban threat?

This makes no sense.   scared_turtle
