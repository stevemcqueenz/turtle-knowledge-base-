Question: What do you think was the worst WoW Retail Retcon? What was the retcon you personally hated the most? what was the wors lore problem?
Here are some that are considered Retcons "for the sake of PvP".
 -Draeneis and Blood Elves: As far as I know, the Blood Elves were originally an Alliance race and were going to be implemented as such, but the Horde didn't have that many players then in a decision, quite idiotic in my opinion, they added them to the Horde and they never investigated why or how they got there, practically the history before WoW TBC dictated the opposite, in addition, they turned the Draenei "good", while in Warcraft III the Eredar were mostly bad, and those who weren't were transformed into Broken Ones, but in TBC curiously enough Draenei survived in a spaceship for some reason.
Tauren Paladins: Do I have to explain this? The Blood Elves are understandable...they were half Paladins despite having lost faith in the Holy Light as well as being half corrupted by the fel magic within them, they gave a vaguely decent explanation in my opinion with Mu' ru but then they fixed it with Velen restoring the Sunwell...but the Tauren? An'she...supposedly...but...bah, it seems like a cheap excuse to me. Lately in WoW Retail the Holy Light obeys even rats, anything is a Paladin.

There are several more, but those are the worst in my personal opinion.
