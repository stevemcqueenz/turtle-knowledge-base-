# I want to reset all my completed quests

## Post 130827 by Methority — 2025-05-13T09:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130827#p130827 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been playing my character for several years now, but with huge breaks. I have my keen interest on some roleplay aspects of the game, i.e. reading quests and understanding what's going on here, but my memory fails me if I haven't kept travel notes. Which I haven't, because there were no proper addons for it back then.

Now I find myself in a situation where, on the one hand, I have two extremely necessary addons - one remembers all the quests done, the second allows me to keep an in-game diary; on the other hand, my character is almost level 50, his "biography" is a complete mess, and one of the addons says that I have completed more than a thousand quests during the life of this character (approximately three years). There are so much new turtle-exclusive quests, even new turtle-exclusive zones, and now I have little to no clue what I was doing there. Such a waste that is.

I would like to start all over again, but only without having to level up the character again, because it is an extremely tedious task.

How do you think, would that be possible to simply "clear" my game character of all completed quests?
