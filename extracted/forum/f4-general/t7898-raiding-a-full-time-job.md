## Post 96030 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-05-30T16:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96030#p96030 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When raiding, its important to pick a guild/ environment that has similar expectations to you.

The guilds that clear Naxxramas every single week completely naturally are attracting the more dedicated players. But on a Private server, content moves at a snails pace. Many players have raided the lower content for so long, they don't need MC/BWL anymore, and so 1-2 evenings of naxxramas a week is really not that much, especially if those people can draw from months of playtime in terms of gold.

If you are trying to get full t3 gear within the first 1-2 months on the server, naturally the demand in gold and time is immense. But the server isn't shutting down, so you don't need to rush it and can find a guild that does just do MC & BWL, get the gear you need before you bother stepping into AQ & Naxx.

Once you have the gear from these places, its quite easy to justify no longer going there, and only focussing on AQ for instance. Making the weekly time commitment much more manageable.

But classic as a game rewards labor more than skill. If you don't have the time to raid 4+ days a week, you need to temper your expectations. Your characters journey will take time.
