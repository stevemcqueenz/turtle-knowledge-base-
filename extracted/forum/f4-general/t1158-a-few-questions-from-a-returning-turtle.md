## Post 6785 by Qixel (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-07-09T20:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6785#p6785 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

1. Nonhardcore rates are 1x now. Dying on hardcore wipes 5% of your current level, but you cannot lose levels.

 2. The 40-50 range got a few more quests, but they are primarily elite quests.

 3. Roleplay is pretty rare and most players don't engage in it.

 4. I'd say that "hardcore" players are an extreme minority here. Most players are very casual. We're still working towards opening the gates of Ahn'qiraj, for example.

 5. Still very alliance heavy, but diplomacy means everything but quests and pvp is effectively faction neutral.

 6. Since you don't have a lot of play time, I'll advocate rogue. Pickpocketing is like a separate game you can play solo with as few as 5 minutes.
