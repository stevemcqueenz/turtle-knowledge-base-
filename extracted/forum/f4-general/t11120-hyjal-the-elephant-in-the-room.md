## Post 73150 by Wafflecrusher (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-23T19:14:00Z

Alright I'll say it. Hyjal is not a fun zone. Beautiful, no doubt. But fun? no way.

When I first arrived in Hyjal I thought "wow what a cool aesthetic". That quickly turned into "wow I sure am walking a-lot" which turned into "Jesus why is this a 10 part chain to get to the shortcut?" which turned into "Alright I'm good. I'm not setting for in this area ever again". Hyjal feels tedious and unrewarding. I don't know how to fix it and make it more engaging, but something needs to be done.

Before I set foot in the area for the first time I asked my guildies if they liked it. They said "It's a lot of walking, and the mobs are over tuned. But it's pretty". That was their review, and unfortunately, it was painfully accurate.
