# Confusion in orientation of hunter class on flavour basis

## Post 134342 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-05T03:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134342#p134342 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Basically a quote of one of my posts in the past.

The hunter in WoW has always been a weird class that functions with weird inclination and unique role-playing flavour. They are archer-like characters with poor melee capability and a beast companion in original versions.

The problem is that this class is designed with the notion of combining the stereotypical archer figure in fantasy works, such as Legolas in The Lord of the Rings, and a beast master role that wanders in the wild. If we only like to play a role like Legolas, who is more like a soldier without an indispensible beast helping him or dependence on traps, we can't find an option in WOW's available classes. On the other hand, when we feel like playing a beast master like Rexxar, who dual-wields and throws projectiles, again, no option like him in original WoW is available. (Making Rexxar use a bow as his primary weapon in Hearthsthone is too over the top.)

In other words, the hunter class in WOW can be only seen in its own universe and this class never meets the role-playing requirement of those who want to play a role like Legolas or Rexxar. Making hunter a class available for mimicking Legolas in mechanism (no pet or trap reliance) would feel like making hunter a ranged warrior or an outlaw rogue using a bow.
