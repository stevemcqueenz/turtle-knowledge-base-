You’re right to be concerned about leather-wearing classes with Artisan and Vagrant. Leather armor is typically rare and the options for crafting it are limited, especially with Artisan where you might need more specific recipes. The lack of recipes would hinder the ability to upgrade armor, which could be vital for surviving in Hardcore.

In general, I would recommend choosing a class that doesn't rely too heavily on specific armor crafting. That way you can still make the most of drops and other gear upgrades without being stuck on a crafting roadblock.
