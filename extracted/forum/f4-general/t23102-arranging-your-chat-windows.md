Yea, it's really good. Something that may annoy you are the Gryphons which overlay over some of the buttons. To remove them:
 1. Go into the pfUI settings (press Esc, should be at the top)
 2. Then "Thirdparty"
 3. Then "Gryphons"
 4. And you'll see 2 drop-downs for left and right Gryphon.
 5. Select "None" and that's it.

pfUI also has hoverbind which is very handy
