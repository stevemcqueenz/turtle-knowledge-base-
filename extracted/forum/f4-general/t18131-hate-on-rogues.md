I have to agree with OP to a degree. I don't main a rogue but I don't like if a class is pushed too much or if it is neglected too much.
Rogue is one of the pure DPS classes. If you pick a rogue, you'll remain a melee DPS for the whole journey so we gotta evaluate how good they are in that role (I will ignore PVP since I'm not familiar with PVP meta).

Like others, rogues have three talent trees:

 * Assassination should be a crit- and poison-based tree but even after the poison buff, it is still incoherent. Raiding rogues are usually combat-sword, combat-dagger and sub. Assassination mostly exists so that others can cherry-pick some talents from it, it is not consistent and good enough to give a valid PVE build on its own.

 * Combat is a very low-utility, mostly single-target-focused, melee DPS tree. The problem is that if you have such restrictions (low utility, unable to AoE, cleaves can only happen on bladefurry CD), you'd expect an outstanding single-target damage in return. It is not the case though. Warriors of course scale above everyone else unfortunately but even if we exclude them, a ret pala usually produces much better numbers than a combat rogue despite having a build with much more utility and a greater damage profile (can also AoE). From what I heard, even the other utility powerhouse melee: enh shaman tends to outperform them.
I'm not a pala or shaman hater, my problem is not with them. My problem here is that there is a build with very heavy restrictions (low utility, limited damaging profile) and in exchange the payoff (the actual damage) 'is not really there'.

 * Subtlety (in PVE) is supposed to be a utility tree. The main problem is its damage profile. To do something which most classes just do by default, on top of their performance (providing some utility), they have to give up most of their damage and they are forced into a rotation which consumes a lot of reagents just by playing the game (!). They provide tank-level DPS. This is the price for a rogue to have some utility for the raid.

PS: Rogues have their own utility package but that translates pretty badly into raiding. Stuns/saps only work on targets which should just be steamrolled anyway, stealth is only useful if you wanna cheeze a raid and want to spare 1-2 invisibility potions when you escort a warlock, threat reduction means very few with the current tank power-level, poisons are not meaningful enough and usually go to off-hand (main hand should have windfury), kicks are good but fury can also do them, too (and they can deal AoE damage, they can OT, they deal more damage), trap disarm literally counts for 1 raid.
Expose armor is not bad, but that's the only thing I can mention and a not so bright warrior will overwrite it with a sunder anyway so it won't stay on the target.

tldr:
Rogues gotta choose one:
 * Having a very restricted utility for damage. Damage is not really good though.
 * Having utility for the price of tank-level damage and a bunch of reagents.
