I feel like Alliance flight paths sometimes get the short end of the stick compared to Horde ones.

For example in Booty Bay, the Alliance FP takes noticeably more effort to reach than the Horde FP. It’s similar in Everlook, where the Horde FP is right by the entrance while the Alliance one is tucked behind the town. Cenarion Hold feels the same way—the Horde FP is basically in the center, while the Alliance one is all the way up a spiral ramp.

I realize fixing things like this would probably take a lot of work and might not really be worth the effort, but I thought it was a funny little imbalance worth pointing out.
