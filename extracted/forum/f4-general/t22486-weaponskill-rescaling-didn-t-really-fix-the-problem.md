# Weaponskill rescaling didn't really fix the problem

## Post 156230 by Eyeburn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-19T22:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156230#p156230 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It's cool that you added more than just 1 or 2 sources for each weapon type, but the rescaling of weapon skill has pushed the new "cap" to 315 from 308.  That's a massive difference, and it makes weaponskill more impactful and important, not less.  It's nice that anyone can pay 250g for +5, but now everyone needs an extra +7 compared to vanilla, and racial boosts were lowered by 2.  Given that a reduced hitcap only matters in amounts of a full 1%, this still leaves distinct breakpoints for weapon skill at 305, 310, and 315.  It's in many ways a worse situation for melee seeking weapon skill than it was in classic by effectively doubling how much is needed for max glancing blow damage, and doubling how much is needed for a 6% hitcap.

Put plainly, 315 is too high, 310 is a better number to act as a soft cap for glancing damage and hit chance that actually allows for non racial motivated picks to optimize melee damage.  I get that bosses have 315 defense, so 315 weaponskill seems like the correct amount for a cap, but it still comes with handcuffed itemization at all stages of progression, and still affects racial choices as much as it did in vanilla.

It needs to be:
 310 for max glancing damage and 6% hitcap.
 315 for 5% hitcap.

## Post 156337 by Eyeburn (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-20T19:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156337#p156337 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It comes off as having never played vanilla or classic or dealt with the 308 cap when the only options were racials, dagger belt/gloves, edgies, and THC/maladath.

Adding +7 to the glancing damage cap is massive.  Getting +8 without racial bonuses was already extremely limited and often locked players into edgies or going daggers, or be a rogue.

Even with the new quests, that's still adding +5 weaponskill over vanilla's cap to get the most glancing blow damage.  This necessarily locks players into equipment slots for a specific weapon skill.

 315 is too high for the glancing damage cap, and that push to be a specific race for weapon skill and to be locked into a specific piece of gear for weapon skill remains in the game.  The glancing blow damage cap should feel like the hit cap, meaning that once you're geared enough it should seem harder to miss the cap than to hit it.

It's fine to have the lowest hit cap require 315, but glancing blow damage is too important to not strive for hitting the cap no matter what, and it creates a gameplay feel that's as unsatisfying and annoying as weaponskill was in vanilla.
