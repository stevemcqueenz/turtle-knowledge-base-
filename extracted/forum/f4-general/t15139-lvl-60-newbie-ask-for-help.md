Hey!
In my experience people are usually chill gear- and consume-wise until BWL. If you have some purples or even only decent blues, most MC run would take you.
I've only seen 1 guy get kicked out at a Kara10 pug (lower raid) because of gear and that guy had level 30-40 greens everywhere.
In your place I'd start going to raids like ZG (cloak, maybe offhand, maybe trinket, idols), AQ20 (offhand, leg, staff), Kara10 (chest, boots, ring) and MC, raid gear is usually better than anything you can get at dungeons. There are some exceptions though, Hateforge and Black Morass both have a really low drop rate epic caster trinket but they are REALLY low drop rate.

There's also a boe dagger here, I can't remember its name. Heard that it's close to BiS for arcane but also good for any other mage build if you wanna spend around 1000g at AH.

Out of the new zones, Hyjal has some interesting stuff, including a farming place. It also has a raid but it's around BWL-level, gear is taken more seriously there, specially for hard mode.

One more thing. There are many pugs for lower raids. Some greedy fella HRs books for AQ20 and BoE for MC. Avoid those pugs since you can easily find ones without those HRs.
