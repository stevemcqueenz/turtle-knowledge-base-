# PvP realm is great.  Turtle wow is Great.

## Post 78887 by Spot (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-11-27T03:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78887#p78887 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I put off playing Turtle WoW because I do not play on PvE servers.  When I heard a PvP realm was opening, I had to come check it out.

So far I'm level 20.  So far all the new content is (chef's kiss).  So many little things you guys added are great.  Like the anvil in Tinker Town.  Wow!  Been waitin for that half my life.

Music added is top notch.

Friend of mine had a questline brake somehow and had to put in a GM ticket.  Got fixed in less then 5 minutes.

The risk of random encounters with the opposing faction adds just the right amount of tension.

The community seems to be tight.

I've been playing World of Warcraft sense Feb. 2005.  I've been on many many private servers over the years.  I was even a working GM on the Vanilla server, "Vanilla Gaming" for a year.

All that being said, my review so far for turtle wow is stellar.

Reality is that you actually get out there regardless of your opinion on it.

If you attempt to, then it will lead to avalanche-like effect producing more and more negative feedback. You will spend enormous amount of time, you actually do nothing and people will still be disappointed.
Turtle devs decided to talk less and do more. They still doing weekly updates (which are actually quite large).

They surely have some internal list of to-do tasks which they sort out sequentally. I believe this list contain several hudreds of tasks and now they are working on tasks created last year.

So in conclusion: enjoy the game you actually get. Turtle is server about leveling and it is good. If you think otherwise, then think again.
