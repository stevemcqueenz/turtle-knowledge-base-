I think I should preface this by mentioning I think I kinda have a basic grasp of what the "meta" class(es) happen to be, but I'd like to ask while I'm at it... Which classes, out of the 9 that exist, can be considered viable in the context of the lvl 1 twinking? Could you, for example, play "ret" pally at level one with only seal of righteousness buffing autoattacks? Is hunter viable at all? Since most casters just have their basic level one bolt/blast etc. spells, are they all on a moderately even playing field, or is there more nuance to it than that? Does Warlock edge out the other DPS casters because of imp? Are warriors the de-facto tanks because of heroic strike's threat modifier, or do lunatics forgo tanking altogether?

Actually, are casters viable at all with their extremely low mana pools, or are enchants enough to carry and keep you juiced long enough to matter in, say, an RFC boss fight?

I've got a lot of thoughts and considerations, but you don't have to particularly answer any of them let alone all. In fact, I'd probably just prefer some general tips and explanations as to what can work and how. I'm not worried about topping meters with massive rogue sinister strike spam pumpage, I'd just like to have a good time and see what's possible with the challenge.

Paladin is extremely strong, Seals scale good with Spell Damage so Ret Paladin is perfectly viable. Hunter is one of the best DPS Classes and is at ranged so they can ride threat 20% more. Warlocks do edge out other casters as Immolate has break points to increase dot damage. Mana regenerates really fast and isn't too much of an issue.

Rogues and Troll Warriors are our Tanks. Rogues are more common with 100% Dodge, however Troll can regenerate 30-60 Health every 2 Seconds in full BiS with World Buffs and when performed at max potential speed up our runs faster than a Rogue could due to spamming Battleshout for Threat. Troll Warriors are the most intensive and expensive Class to perform on however and ideally have every World Buff as often as possible.
