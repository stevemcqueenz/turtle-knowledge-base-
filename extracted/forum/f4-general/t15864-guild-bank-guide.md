# Guild Bank Guide

## Post 108086 by Speedz1337 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-16T07:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108086#p108086 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/IHFnFC3.png)

 1st Cost to unlock: 2000g
 2nd Cost to unlock: 500g
 2nd tab unlock gives Guild Leader access to managing the guild bank.
 3rd Cost to unlock: 1000g
 4th Cost to unlock: 1500g
 5th Cost to unlock: 2000g
Total Bank guild tab Cost: 7000g

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/JdNZTax.png)

Every rank has access to the Logs and Money Log
Top two ranks have access to withdrawing money (Guild Leader and rank below that regardless of roles assigned)
All ranks have access to depositing money

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/wTChDi0.png)

Options

Minimum Rank to access this tab: If set to current rank or above will give player unlimited access regardless of what the weekly withdrawal limit is.  Any rank below current rank set will be subject to the weekly withdrawal limit.

Weekly Withdrawal Options: Unlimited, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
