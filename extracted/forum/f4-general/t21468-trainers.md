## Post 148811 by Mekk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-28T02:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148811#p148811 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Crafting Professions do go up in tiers.  Weapon Proficiencies increase automatically when you level up.  You gain 5 Weapon Proficiency every level, so at level 60, you'll have up to 300 Weapon Proficiency.  Since you are at 140, that means you're Level 28.  Once you reach Level 29, that will automatically change from 140/140 to 140/145.

EDIT: And to clarify further, in case it was not clear, you gain Weapon Proficiency by using the weapon in combat.  So, once you reach Level 29, you'll see your Weapon Proficiency increase as you engage in combat, and you'll get a message in your chat window when this occurs.  :)
