*I love Turtle WoW with all my heart, but I also enjoy healthy competition. It creates space for different ideas and approaches — just like we saw in classic.*

Even though their server has a **2,000 player cap** and they **burned some bridges early on**, if they manage to do a decent job, I believe they can slowly win back their player base.

That said, despite some of their decisions clearly showing interest and effort, a lot of it feels like a **Frankenstein of features from previous expansions**. It doesn’t really seem like they’re trying to *solve* the problems, but rather stitching together things that worked in other projects.

And that might backfire — because sometimes, something works only in a very specific time or context. This **fruit salad** of systems they’ve built might bring new problems they’ll struggle to fix later.

 *Of course, I might be wrong — I’m making a lot of assumptions here. But I’m genuinely curious to see things from a different perspective, even if just to confirm which of my concerns are valid, and which are not.*
