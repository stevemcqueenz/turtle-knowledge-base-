# Environment, Mechanics, Behavior, and You!

## Post 310 by Jondoh — 2018-09-27T06:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=310#p310 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I am glad to have found out about this server before launch. I am really looking forward to this server launch.

I just want to remind people about a few things. Maybe this will be helpful to a few people to adjust the expectations and experience that you may have.

The most important point is that the person who will have the most influence over how you enjoy playing on the server is you!

The environment of the server can help to provide a place where a positive game-play experience is more comfortable. But in the end it must be you who makes the choices to help make the environment what it can be.

The mechanics of xp rates and such may be able to help promote or encourage some behavior. The amount of difference you will notice will be very limited, unless you decide for yourself that you want to experience a difference.

We can spend a lot of time trying to build the perfect system or the perfect environment to produce exactly the behavior that we desire; but in the end people choose how they will let these things influence the behavior they choose.

You may easily find that you are tempted to continue in the same patterns you have used on different servers. I expect to see quite a lot of people who simply adapt a little but continue playing almost the same as they have anywhere else. There will likely be plenty of people who will still try to rush level cap or BiS gear or whatever it is, the same as they have always done. That is a fine way to play if that is the way you chose to play. Just be sure that it is what you really want if that is how you decide to play. If you want something different then you need to decide for yourself to learn a different pattern. If the other is all you have known before then it may feel a little uncomfortable at first to try a new way. We tend to do what we have done before out of habit. It will take conscious effort and purposeful decisions for some of us to enjoy the game in the way that the environment and the mechanics of the server are encouraging us to try.

Finally, it is not enough to simply want a difference in experience. We must actually then do the things most likely to get us to what we want. To produce a different experience, most likely will require changing our own behavior. Trying to change our own behavior can be uncomfortable sometimes, but it is the most sensible way to reach the results that we want; if what we want is something different from what we have had before.

I really do hope that the server launch goes well. I wish you all the best. I just don't want people to expect some good implementation to totally change their experience. It can certainly help to have a well crafted environment with solid mechanics; but the most crucial factor is what you choose to do with these tools to make your own experience what you want it to be.

 -JD
