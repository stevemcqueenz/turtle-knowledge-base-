Well.  I've been taking [census data](https://github.com/Lexiebean/CensusPlusTurtle) almost every day, several times a day, for the last month or so, though my data may not be completely accurate?  In any case, Of the 18,729 characters I have personally seen across my alts and the like:

Faction-Exclusive Classes
- 6% of Horde players play as a **Shaman** (1,266 characters)
- 10% of Alliance players play as a **Paladin** (2,047 characters)
Least Popular Classes
- 8% of players play as a **Druid** (1,610 characters) or **Warlock** (1,674 characters)
- 9% of players play as a **Priest** (1,729 characters)
Most Popular Classes
- 17% of players play as a **Warrior** (3,183 characters)
- 14% of players play as a **Hunter** (2,704 characters)
- 11% of players play as a **Rogue** (2,170 characters) or **Mage** (2,158 characters)
I hope this helps!

And just for giggles, the least and most popular *Races* are…
- **Goblin** with 4% of the player base (794 characters) is the least popular
- **Human** with 19% of the player base (3,741 characters) is the most popular
