# From Hype to Disappointment: The Current State of PvP Arena

## Post 153737 by Vinzer75x — 2025-09-30T08:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153737#p153737 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone,

I'm writing to voice some serious concerns about the state of the PvP arena with the recent release of Molten Core. The official release announcement showed arena right alongside the new raid, which got a lot of us excited, but the current implementation feels rushed and completely misses the mark.

Here's why:

No Incentive for Ranked: The rewards for skirmishes are identical to those for ranked arena, which completely removes any reason to play ranked. People are just spamming skirmishes for honor.

Lack of Competition: There's no leaderboard, no unique rewards, and no way to track progress. It feels like there's zero competition, and ranked arena feels exactly the same as skirmishes.

Training with Teammates: We can't queue for skirmishes with our teammates. This is a basic feature on other servers and makes no sense. It just turns skirmishes into a mess of random players spamming for honor.

Unbalanced PvP: The environment is completely unbalanced. Warlocks can use their Infernal, and all consumables are usable in PvP, leading to some frustrating and unfair fights.

Team Limitations: You can't have more than two or three people on a 2v2 or 3v3 team. This is another basic feature that's missing and makes it hard to organize and play with friends.

I started playing on Turtle WoW just a month ago with the Ambershire server release, specifically because of that screenshot showing arena was coming out. I was so hyped to get into the PvP scene here. I've already earned the Molten Core title and am super invested in this server, but to see the one thing I play WoW for—the PvP—be handled so poorly is honestly just disheartening. I find it wild that ranked arenas were a major part of the release announcement, only for it to feel like just 5% of the intended features were actually implemented. I just don't understand how something can be put front and center and then have so little care seemingly put into its actual state.

The current state of the arena just doesn't make sense. It feels like an afterthought, and the lack of a proper competitive environment is a huge disappointment. I hope the team addresses these issues soon and gives PvP the attention it deserves.

 **We're not asking for massive, sweeping changes all at once. We're simply hoping for a few minimal changes that can be done rapidly to make the arenas at least functional. Adding some unique rewards, implementing a simple leaderboard, allowing us to queue for skirmishes with our friends, or at least gaining more honor in ranked than in skirmishes and remove consumables would make a world of difference and give people a reason to engage in RANKED Arenas.**

Please.
