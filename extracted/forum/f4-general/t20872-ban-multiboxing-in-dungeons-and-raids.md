****I believe multiboxing, even in its limited form on Turtle WoW, harms the core social aspect of an MMO.****
In dungeons, especially at level 60, a well-geared player can clear content entirely alone, removing the need to form groups and reducing interaction between players. In raids it’s less of an issue, but it can still create situations where one person is effectively taking multiple loot rolls or **reserve items**, which some may see as unfair.

 **When it comes to leveling**, **tag farming** is the most damaging aspect. A level 60 carries another character, essentially removing them from the world, making the server less inclusive, closing off many social windows. The tag farmer sees every other player as an obstacle and avoids sharing XP with anyone. This isolates them from the community and works against the open-world interaction that Turtle WoW promotes.

 **Reputation farming** works differently, since everyone in the group gains the same rep. The problem here is that multiboxers avoid inviting other players to keep all the loot for themselves — loot that can often be exchanged for reputation with NPCs. This still undermines group play, as it removes potential opportunities for cooperation.

 **Leveling in dungeons** is the main concern about multiboxing. With strong enough gear, a single player can clear most of the content with their extra character, bypassing the need for real group members and further reducing opportunities for social interaction — leveling as a single player, and getting rewards of challenges without really working on the challenge itself.

 **A practical solution** that would drastically reduce these abuses without constant GM intervention is to remove the **/follow** function for players. This single change would stop around 90% of tag farming, make multiboxing far less efficient, and naturally encourage players to cooperate instead of running content entirely on their own.

 **Beyond removing /follow, there are additional measures that could help:**
- Automatic filtering through Extended Fingerprints, which GMs already have access to, or make an automatic filter where if someone has the same EF, both are removed from the group.

- Avoiding blanket IP-based restrictions, since many players share the same household (parents and children, for example)

- If technically possible in the current client, removing Click to Move to further hinder both multiboxers and botters
