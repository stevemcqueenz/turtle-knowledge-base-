# Since Warmode, from 300 to 4600 People Online

## Post 37537 by Mativh (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-03-13T12:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37537#p37537 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When I've suggested the warmode idea, I knew that it is the ideal way to solve the big PvP dillema; how to implement PvP for those that consider fsction conflict a crucial part of WoW, while not forcing those that dont like PvP into taking part...
By having an option to act as if you were on a RP-PVP server with those that also choose that option, while the server remains to be RP-PVE and those that don't take part are unaffected.
Yet they still interact in every other way.
I believe this should've been the basic server design since the beginning, Turtle WoW was the only server that did this, and as I expected it increased the population a lot.
The original tight knit community, which I was part of since 2018, was very sceptical about welcoming such an influx, fearing that the server would be taken over by toxic players and the carefully cultivated unique homely culture and focus on immersion of Turtle WoW would just disappear.
But something remarkable happened instead. This Turtle culture was so strong that the vast majority of new players got absorbed into it instead!
Of course, it isn't the same, you don't scarcely come across someone and chances are you know each other, gatherings aren't always with known people, but the atmosphere is there, the will and intent to live in the living and breathing world of warcraft and form real connections is there, and now many more can form!

When I suggested the idea I expected the population to double at the very least. There were around 300 people online. After it got implemented, it surged to up to 700, and I was very glad that it worked as expected.
And then... Blizzard noticed, and banned Turtle social media... and that created an advertisement for Turtle WoW, population got to 1100.. people were talking about Turtle WoW within the private server community... it called the attention of smaller youtubers, who made review videos... population was at 2k... bigger youtubers noticed, made a comment, made a review, the word spread... and now we are at 4600! Turtle WoW is known by the official community. There are people unsubcribing from Classic to play here. I am so glad that so many people get to see the hard word and passion poured into this server by the Turtle team, and the amount of donations this population growth mist have generate will reward the people behind this project, fund future custom content development that would otherwise perhaps not be possible, and ensure the security of the servers.
Turtle WoW is already a legendary pserver, on par with the likes of Nostalrius. I'm glad I could contribute with this idea and for the chain reaction it provoked. I can't wait to see how this server will keep evolving, carving out it's own path in the lore true to the Warcraft franchise.
