## Post 122891 by Wrathweaver (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-03-18T14:22:00Z

Shaman, paladin, warrior, rogue and hunter had large overhauls and will feel very different.
Shaman plays more like a wotlk version with 9 stack shield.
Elemental will no longer go into melee, but constantly spam bolts(streamlined for raid)

On paladin you will constantly have to push strikes to keep up speed/strenght buff.
Holy strike is free for some reason.
Holy shock dps removed.
Will not recognise the class.

On hunter survival is melee spec with aoe, moongose bite has been removed and you have 2 raptor strikes instead, it has still retained moongose bite in the name.

Arcane mage is no longer the mana substai n spec, but uses more mana instead.

Sub rogue is a support spec.

I would say if you like wotlk or sod and dont have any vanilla class attatchment from 2004, you will enjoy leveling on turtle.
Or if you feel like classes in vanilla where to slow, not enough buttons ect.
