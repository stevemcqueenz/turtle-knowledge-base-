# How to make a lvl 1 twink. (level 1's can actually get pretty op)

## Post 29531 by Abqwkjg — 2022-11-20T03:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29531#p29531 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Level 1 twinks: Rogues are the most viable.
Best races:
Alliance: Gnomes, due to escape artist.
Horde: Trolls, due to Berserking.

Armor - Enchants
Helm - Speedy racer Goggles - +100 health
Necklace - Voice amplificator modulator
Shoulder - Mantle of the Fire festival - 16 stam +100 armor
Cloak - linen cloak - +3 agi
Chest - Cracked/dirty leather vest - +100 health
Bracers - Cracked/dirty leather bracers - +9 stamina
Gloves - Worn leather gloves - +15 agility
Belt - Squealer's belt
Legs - Cracked/dirty leather Pants - +100 health
Boots - Goblin rocket boots - +7 stamina or minor speed
Ring 1 - Mark of hakkar: Unobtainable, but you could obtain it before
Ring 2 - Mark of hakkar:  Unobtainable, but you could obtain it before
Trinket 1 - Arena grand master
Trinket 2 - Insignia of the Alliance/Horde

Main hand
Keen machete or Simple dagger - Fiery weapon

Off hand
Furbolg medicine pouch

Ranged
Cadet's bow - +1 scope

Ammo
Feathered arrow

Usefull items

Explosive rocket
Discombobulator ray
magic candle

Consumables

Elixir of lion strength
Weak trolls blood potion
Scroll of protection
Rumsey rum black label
Bogling root
Herb baked egg
Minor healing potion
Heavy runecloth bandage
Rough sharpening stone

World buffs (makes your lvl 1 twink way more powerful, if you can get them)

Rallying cry of the dragonslayer
Spirit of zandalar
Warchief's blessing (Horde only)
