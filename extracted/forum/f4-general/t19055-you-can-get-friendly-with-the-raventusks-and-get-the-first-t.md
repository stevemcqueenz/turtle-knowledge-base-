# You can get friendly with the Raventusks and get the first tier rewards on your first visit !

## Post 129528 by Kaporalhart — 2025-05-03T21:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129528#p129528 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been eyeing those swords to get on my level 10 rogue, but had trouble figuring out how to get them. I did all the quests and the lighting of the pyres, but was just short of friendly. I thought i had to wait for tomorrow and do the pyres again, and did not find any help on the forum nor the database. It cited in the Raventusk quest list another quest, "Scratching each other's back", but literally didn't give any info on it. I even walked all the way to the east to stonetalon, thinking there would be an npc.

But actually, it was the other way ! If you go north, you can go up and then east across a suspended rope bridge. There is an npc, and he's the one with that quest. It asks you to go all the way back to Sen'Jin village, but at least with that you can get friendly and have that last bit of rep.

I hope writing this post will allow it to show up on google next time someone struggles like i did.
