The dryad illusion used to be purchasable in the shop. There are rumors of it being purchasable with turtle tokens by contacting a GM, but so far this hasn't been my experience (perhaps it varies by GM). I've seen someone with it, presumably an old account, running around and doing druidy things. It was great. In any case, I hope the illusion is made purchasable again.
The first reason has to do with RP. It is a deeply immersive illusion. It feels fitting to play with it (depending on the class) and witnessing it in action is fun - a dryad prancing around and granting blessing of the wild makes the world feel more living.
I understand the illusion has some animation issues, but so do plenty of those we can play normally. Forsaken female hunters are an example. So is any hunter trying to fire a bow in a horizontal "gangsta" fashion. As an archer in my free time, shooting a bow horizontally is technically possible but is ls less powerful and absolutely less accurate. It's not a technique used by any serious archer, so why it's used in WoW is bizarre. Plenty of other animations clearly never actually used a weapon in their life based on how they swing their weapons. But I can forgive these things and accept it, as well as dryads having some weird animations, because they in no way affect gameplay. Please consider that a great deal of players are fine with silly animations as long as we can enjoy our gameplay. The dryad illusion and numerous illusions **still in the shop with wonky or not even having animations**also qualify.
Less important gameplay wise, but still important, I'm literally trying to give Turtle WoW money by donating in exchange for the illusion. Take my money!   turtle_tongue_head
