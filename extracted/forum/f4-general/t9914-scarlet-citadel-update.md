## Post 65519 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-09-18T23:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=65519#p65519 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings.

Scarlet Citadel is a concept we are quite fond of, however, the models use for it caused numerous issues and the project has been moved to the backburner for now. It will not be 1.17 content, nor will it likely be in 1.18 -- we have other massive projects in the works (which will make our 1.17.0 patch look small in comparison!), but it's not something we have entirely disregarded the idea of.

tl;dr - Scarlet Citadel is a concept we worked on, encountered issues with, and have moved into the "come back to it later" category.
