# Will new zones and dungeons ruin the leveling up experience?

## Post 172662 by Zabrat (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-27T03:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172662#p172662 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

As someone who like to see the game world expand with more new things and options like new zones, dungeons, raids and more.
Right now I feel the new zones are good additions as a new experience but as I progress when leveling up my new character I feel like the world becomes less populated especially in mid lvls like 35 to 50.
I experienced Grim Reaches for example but I couldn't find that many people questing there, couldn't find people to ask them for help with the elite quests.
I wouldn't say the zone is empty but not as populated as it should be.
Same with dungeons like it sometimes get long to find people to do Gilneas with.
But what if the turtle team keep adding more zones and dungeons, will that help the game or will cause problems as the world expand.
If it cause more harm than good should they continue to add new zones but find a way to populate them or stop making zones and expand only the end game?
