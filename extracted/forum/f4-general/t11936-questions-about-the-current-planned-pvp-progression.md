## Post 79070 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-11-28T12:19:00Z

Maybe the general background will help you make more sense: In principle TWoW tries to emulate the Vanilla experience somewhat faithfully. Vanilla had an insane self-harming honor system, and so TWoW has as well. But TWoW also was a server with a three-digit playerbase for years. Pretty soon it was noticed that the PvP system doesn't work with so few players, higher ranks were literally unachievable (with 80 players in the honor system, you can at most be in the top 1.2% which puts you into bracket 12 of 14). So there were some adjustments to account for the small playerbase. With the massive player influx half a year ago this is now mostly senseless.
> **Tiryan wrote: Mon Nov 27, 2023 8:58 pm**
> the website states *"RP reqs for ranks are dynamically scaled"*, what does this entail?

What you already posted. On lower ranks if brackets are not filled out, "shadow players" inserted into the brackets to make ranking viable when there was super low population.

> **Tiryan wrote: Mon Nov 27, 2023 8:58 pm**
> this server has a hard stance against RMT, bots, certain forms of multiboxing, p2w, etc which was the main reason I recently joined, yet there is apparently no/slow action against accout sharing rank boosting?! why?

Why not? Read [the rules](https://turtlecraft.gg/#/rules). Both account sharing and (unpaid) boosting are allowed. If you have hard evidence that paid boosting or account selling or wintrading is going on, report it.

> **Tiryan wrote: Mon Nov 27, 2023 8:58 pm**
> assuming that issue will be sorted out how will the differences to old/new classic impact the progression? i.e. this server will have no de-ranking and no rank-decay but RP/brackets are still a factor... why not carry over all the improvements?

TWoW has never aimed to be a Classic knock-off and it probably never will. The changes are because of the population influx making ranking very unhealthy. Stopping rank decay has the sole purpose of allowing you take breaks during ranking. Why would the rest of the Vanilla-like system need to be changed if the underlying goal is still to provide a Vanilla-like experience?

> **Tiryan wrote: Mon Nov 27, 2023 8:58 pm**
> how does classic pvp gear compare to pve? in one thread it was stated raiding for gear would be faster and from what I see there are no major differences (like resilience and set bonuses in wotlk)
>  if pve gear is aquired faster and you don't care about titles associated with ranks or about mounts and gimmicks like the battle standard there is zero reason to progress/grind beyond ~r2/3? (relevant if you play more than 1 / a few characters

It compares the same as it always has. Apart from Blood Ring gear (which is very strong) no PvP itemization has been changed or newly introduced yet. PvP stuff generally has more stamina, but Vanilla PvP gear does not have any special stats. The epic set and especially the R14 weapons are still among the best items in the game by virtue of their ilvl, but Naxx gear is obviously on par or stronger.
But why would you need to compare them? They are alternate tracks tailored to different playstyles and preferences. If you enjoy PvP you can rank and gain rep and maybe eventually get some sweet gear. And if you like raiding you can join a group and maybe eventually get some sweet epics that way too. There shouldn't be a "better" or "faster". If you find a raid group willing to take you to Naxx in green gear, then yes, raiding will be unbeatable the fast-track to endgame gear. But you're also missing the point of the game this way. The progression, the gearing-up *is* the goal. You play to have fun, to enjoy the gameplay itself, not to have a BiS geared character.

> **Tiryan wrote: Mon Nov 27, 2023 8:58 pm**
> how does any of this impact level 1-59 pvp? are the lower battlegrounds populated? there is no split between leveling players and those that stopped exp gain to BIS their char?

Not very much. Yes, there are Twinks and there is not any kind of bracketing between them and normal leveling players, but again - this is the Vanilla way. The biggest impact on how active the low-level scene is, is the number of players that enjoy PvP. And the opening of Tel'Abim, the new PvP server, has put a major dampener on low-level PvP on the main realm. One of my guildies could not get a 10-19 WSG match queuing for hours on Sunday (the most active day of the week). It was always a small scene, but the new realm seems to have mostly killed it.
