The kind of information you're looking for isn't out there to the extent that might be expected.  I don't know if it's a generation gap or a cultural shift over time or simply a unique quirk of the T-wow community, but people here simply don't talk about the game like folks did 20 years ago.

Within the length a single forum post reasonably allows, I can say this:  class roles are always as expected:  There are no surprises in the new instances in terms of what'll be expected of your tank or your healer or whatnot.   That is a good thing since super-gimmicky design tends to annoy people.  Item drops can be looked up on the T-wow database, albeit with some difficulty because I don't see a sort-by-zone category...I was stuck looking through, say, all Plate Helms and go down the list scanning for items that don't exist in the original game.  Boss mechanics are seldom discussed and sometimes missing even from the database.  In most cases you'll have to learn on-the-fly, but again in most cases the mechanics are not "insta wipe" and you can adjust as needed.

Long story short, best way to answer what you want to know is, by doing!
