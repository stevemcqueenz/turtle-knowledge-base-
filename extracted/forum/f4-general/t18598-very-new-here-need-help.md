Hey there!

Nordanaar is our PvE realm with a higher population — averaging around 9,000 players online daily. Tel'Abim, on the other hand, is our PvP realm, with about 800–900 players online each day.

We've recently introduced a new wave of class changes, and pretty much all classes are viable now, especially in raids.

While there are more Alliance players than Horde, Nordanaar is a cross-faction realm. This means you can group up with players from the opposite faction, form guilds, run raids together, and more.
