## Post 152060 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-17T10:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152060#p152060 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Druid is also very strong and I agree, some very rare situations hurt pala tank, like you mentioned (mana drain f.ex.).

But:
Pala tank gives the party more utility than a bear usually.
Bear is okay with packs but bigger packs can make it somewhat inconsistent.
Pala is like a glue. If you can keep up the heals, it keeps aggro over any number of threats (and dishes out really big aoe damage).
I prio pala over bear because of the extra utility and because most dungeon is 90% about trash packs. A pala just runs in, pulls 3 packs, and doesn't lose aggro from any of them.

Imo the problem is that Righteous Fury is a huge threat buff for holy damage which was okay in Vanilla because pala had limited ways to dish out holy damage. Pala was a fragile aoe threat build struggling with single target. But because righteous fury threat buff is so big, every added way to deal holy damage makes the threat hugely more consistent.
That, plus not forcing a prot to learn half the holy tree made them extremely strong.
