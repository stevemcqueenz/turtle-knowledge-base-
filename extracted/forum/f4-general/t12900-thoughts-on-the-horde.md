# Thoughts on the Horde

## Post 86095 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-28T02:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86095#p86095 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, well this is a post to describe my experience as a proud Alliance player playing Horde for the first time, and telling you my story about why I always play in the Alliance.

First of all, yes, I had played in the Horde before, but I only played as an Orc warrior for a few levels, no more than 20, then I tried a Blood Elf and never reached the level 10 with it...

I have finally decided to create an Undead rogue and I have already taken it to level 10 and I have personally enjoyed it, I have read the stories of the areas, I have killed several mobs and I feel that chilling sensation when exploring the tirisfal glades in that level of detail, doing the missions is extremely interesting, since the lore is quite good, seeing destroyed areas and knowing that it was once the magnificent kingdom of Lordaeron and that now they are nothing more than a weak shadow of their former glory gives me a feeling of fear and interest that I like.
Undercity is incredible too, it has that fantasy gothic style that reminds me a lot of the "American McGee's Alice" video game that gives it a charming tone, I saw Undercity before and I had never liked it, but now it is attractive and interesting to explore, and it is even feels bigger than Stormwind and Orgrimmar.

I won't say much about the Undead race, but I love it in general, it's like playing with a human...dead, but still that sense of belonging to the Horde gives it a strange and pleasant touch, I can understand perfectly why they are in the Horde and why they definitely don't fit into the Alliance, since the Horde is mainly made up of races that have, in effect, been at war with the Alliance in one way or another and are now rejected:

 - Orcs with Humans and Night Elves
 - Trolls with High Elves
 - Undead with practically the entire Alliance

The only ones who are saved are the Goblins, who practically joined together out of greed, and the Tauren, who are the most peaceful of the 5 races.
In general, I really liked the Horde, I will probably try to level some other race, although personally I would like them to give the Trolls their own capital and starting zone, like in Cataclysm, since they are the second race that attracts me the most. the horde.

 ***Now why have I never played Horde?***
I have always felt what they call "Faction Pride" which is when you defend the faction in which you played and decided to stay and, in effect, you develop a certain aversion to the opposite faction, in my case, I chose the Alliance and therefore, I hated to the Horde.
But why? For the Blood Elves, I felt like they had taken a very important race from my faction and given it to the Horde just because they didn't have as many players as the Alliance, and they needed to fix it immediately...but what a bad way to fix it Well, of course, you put an important race that has always belonged to the opposing faction, you give it a rather stupid and hasty lore and on top of that the Horde players eat it whole and on top of that they defend that it is a good decision, sons of the... .
After I found out why the Blood Elves were in the Horde I didn't see it the same way, seeing so many Blood Elf paladins jumping around took away from Orgrimmar that feeling of savagery and rejection that I had and gave him...practically a beautiful human clone that doesn't fit in at all with the other races
I couldn't see the Horde in the same way, I tried to level a Blood Elf, wow, I said "Maybe I'll end up liking the race and find a way to fit it" but I never could, I didn't get past level 10, nothing fit me, I saw the missions and I said...hey, this is in the Alliance, and then I saw that it belonged to the Horde and no, I deleted it and then I went to the Alliance, I created a human and started playing, I never touched a Horde race again.

I read something funny once "I lift a stone and out come 10 Blood Elves, 7 of them paladins."

And from there I drew my conclusion, for me, the Blood Elves completely ruined the Horde in retail, both in essence and in players, the Horde would be nothing if they had not put the Blood Elves in The Burning Crusade.
