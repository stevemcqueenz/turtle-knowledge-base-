I'm in a similar situation myself though i do have a bit more time then you do.

That being said I would say as far as casual playing this server is the best you can hope for. It's a primarily PvE and RP focused game with heavy emphasis on leveling, questing, roleplaying, and just sorta hanging out. Raiding is pretty much a no go for you but if you find a solid core group of people you MIGHT be able to actually do some 5 man's.

Honestly at level 60 you'll probably be quite limited in terms of content because BG pops are slow because there just isn't a lot of PvP that happens and raiding with the forming of groups, all the attunement quests, and just the time it takes to get through the raid you are pretty much locked out of that.

I would say if you wanna hang out, enjoy leveling, and do some of the quicker and easier dungeons this is the perfect server. There is so much more world content, questing zones, and things to explore that have been added to the game.

I hope you can find a niche here that you enjoy.
