# Faction leader loot is super bad idea

## Post 117317 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-01-20T05:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117317#p117317 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Some time ago I read announcement about loot at faction leaders.
While it sounds good on paper, it might turn very bad.

There might be only 2 types of situations:
 1. Loot is useful when Naxx/kara40 is opened
 2. Loot is not useful

Second situation will change literally nothing. If loot is not useful, it is the same as if loot don't exist at all.

First situation will effectively turn faction leaders to world bosses. What is the default state of world bosses on Nordnaar? Right. They are dead.
So, Faction leaders will be alive for very short amount of time, required to travel from neutral city to capital. With current powercreep, it won't take long to take down faction leader themselves.

For horde, Thrall starts great dozen of quests during leveling. Permanent death will block players from completing all those quests.

Im not a fan of the faction leader loot system for a couple of reasons. (At least on Nordrannar)

For starters you have a scenario where it is optimal to bring healers of the opposing faction to raid their capital.
This is whack a mole with the ban hammer waiting to happen.

Also it is possible to just teleport your raid into the oposing capital. Some cities are worse than others. In the case od Tyrande wisperwind, you can literally twleport into her house as the oposing faction with just one "traitor" mage.

Then comes the actual distribution of loot. You either have to lootmaster in the enemy faction capital, take everything and use the 10 min trade function (that i am not sure works for open world loot) or rely on grouploot.

It also crucially only adds loot for doing said bosses. Sucessfully defending them is not having an incentive. So very quickly there will be a hostility towards players actually defending their capital. While xfaction environment makes it super easy to snif out an enemy raid.

While the idea sounds very appealing, i think it will create a lot of hostility between players. Very fitting for pvp, but i think the wrong kind of hostilities
