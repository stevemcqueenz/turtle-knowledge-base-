King's Honor friends and Lok'Tar Ogar!

Grab your dusty sword and join your faction's defending forces!
Get your brand new equipment sets from the Guard Recruiters in the main cities:

- Moorahn Bloodhoof, Thunderbluff Brave Recruiter: [Dressing Box: Thunder Bluff Guard]

- Dul'gar Warface, Orgrimmar Grunt Recruiter: [Dressing Box: Orgrimmar Guard]

- Aerys Jonar, Deathguard Recruiter: [Dressing Box: Deathguard]

- Gorden Silverbeard,  Ironforge Guard Recruiter: [Dressing Box: Ironforge Guard]

- Meredith McCay,  Stormwind Guard Recruiter: [Dressing Box: Stormwind Guard]

- Alynn Moonfall,  Darnassus Sentinel Recruiter: [Dressing Box: Darnassus Sentinel]

- Bonkle Tinkspark,  Gnomeregan Reclamation Recruiter: [Dressing Box: Gnomeregan Guard]
Your reputation with the chosen faction must be at least **Friendly** to purchase the Guard outfit.
The provided items are roleplay outfits and don’t have any battle stats.

Trolls are not excluded, we're just letting them relax a bit 'mon!
Items will be added additionally on the next server update.

Additionally, a few new Dressing Boxes are available in the shop:

- Dressing Box: Kul'Tiras Guard

- Dressing Box: Theramore Guard

- Dressing Box: Stromgarde Guard

- Dressing Box: Dwarven Mountaineer

- Dressing Box: Nightwatchman
Special thanks to Tamamo-no-Bae for making this update possible.
