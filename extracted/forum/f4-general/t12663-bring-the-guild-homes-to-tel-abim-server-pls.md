This is actually the kind of change which might encourage quite a lot of people to play on Tel'Abim. From a PvP perspective, it creates many RP-PvP or World PvP opportunities.

Part of the reason they added it in the very early days of the PvE server was because the population was so low. You can tell this was the reasoning because they discontinued the idea once the population became much larger (>1000 players). Simply put, only so many guild houses could fit into the world without disturbing the aesthetic. So, inevitably, people were annoyed that their new guilds were late to the party and denied this exclusive content.

So, with the current Tel'Abim population, I think it would work. However, should the population get 'too big', the same issues would crop up.

Also worth pointing out that, in the immediate future, it would take people away from the major cities which isn't a good look for new players trying out the server....
