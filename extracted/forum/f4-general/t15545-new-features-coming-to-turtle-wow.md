# New features coming to Turtle WoW!

## Post 106220 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-03T20:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106220#p106220 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, I have read whole thread on Reddit where Turtle team answered players' questions 2 days ago. I decided to make summary of what features will (and what will not) come to the Turtle-WoW in the future based on whole thread.
Link to the source:

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/reddit.min.html#turtlewow/comments/1gh9q07#theme=]

I haven't included some devs' responses that in my opinion aren't that important for future content so feel free to read that thread if you haven't yet.

---
World bosses will likely be changed so they won't be limited only to small part of playerbase.
---
Skins of warlock's pet will be in the donation shop.
---
Epic class quests similar to Priest/Hunter might be added.
---
Other dungeons will likely receive extension like some of them got in 1.17.2.
---
Survival profession will be reworked.
---
There will be number of characters per account increased in UE5 client.
---
New and themed abilities of pets will be added in the future content.
---
Cross-faction battlegrounds will be introduced Soon™.
---
There are no plans for any profession to have bows as craftable items.
---
Barber shop might be added with new customization.
---
There might be some class/race combos added in the future that are lore accurate.
---
Guild/Player Housing might be added after game's port to UE5 client.
---
New 10 man dungeon might be added for lowel levels.
---
Some custom locations will be revamped/altered.
---
1.17.2 isn't the only patch that changes class balance - there will be more balancing micropatches within coming weeks.
---
Scarlet Citadel raid won't be added to the game, perhaps in the distant future.
---
Horizontal progression will become team's focus after Grim Batol's release.
---
There are some plans to change capital cities, however Turtle team claims it's hard to change them.
---
There are some plans for the future content involving Outland, however it won't be copy-pasted content from Blizzard's TBC.
---
There will be more Mechanicals added to the game.
---
There is a chance for new playable races in the future.
---
There is a chance for new playable classes in the future.
---
Goblins will receive lvl 60 mounts.
---
There are no plans to add third primary profession.
---
New glyphs will be added in the future.
---
Old raids might be updated a bit so they keep "feeling fresh".
---
There will be HD version optional in the new client that contains WoD models similar to current HD community mod.
---
Gnomes will be deleted from the game.
---
Content for 1.18 wasn't delayed.
---
Currently there is no ETA for Turtle Armory return.
---
There will be some competitive ranked PvP in the near future as well as open-world PvP objectives etc.
---
Achievements are in plans.
---
With the first release of 2.0 client, players will be allowed to use old or new client to play. After some time only new client will be allowed.
---
Minimal requirements for new client are following:
Operating System: Windows 7 64bit or newer

CPU: Intel Core i3-2100 or AMD A6-3600

RAM: 1 GB

GPU: Integrated graphics (e. g. Intel HD Graphics 4000)

Turtle team is sure that even potato PCs will be able to connect.
---
Sunnyglade Valley battleground will be revamped in one of the upcoming patches.
---
Druids won't be able to have gear appearance in forms because of technical limitations.
---
Grim Batol will be the next part of the Azeroth that will be expanded in the next big patch.
---
There are no plans to give unique classes (Paladin, Shaman) to opposite factions.
---
Auction House rework might come in the distant future.
---
Dalaran might be rebuilded in the distant future.
---
There are no plans to introduce calculation formulas to each spell in the game.
---
There are plans to introduce calculation formulas to web database.
---
There will be no special bow itemization for Night Elf Priest.
---
Ancient Locking Mechanism that drops from Ostarius (Uldum world boss) and that currently has no use, will be useful for later content after Grim Batol.
---
Devs are working on a fix to uneven battleground queue issue (10 vs 12 situations etc).
---
The client won't be moved to 3.3.5.
---
New client is going to be released on "late 2025", however delays are possible.
---
Turtle team won't refund race change from donation shop.
