> **Dirti wrote: Thu Dec 21, 2023 1:19 pm**
> Any tips for a noob who wants to move 60% faster as soon as possible?

I recommend that as you level you check the price of every white item you pick up on the Auction House. You'll need a addon to do this effectively, the one that works for me is AUX. Then also make sure you've downloaded the vendor price addon, this means you'll be able to see the mark up of the item on the AH and can make the decision about whether to post it. My personal rule is, if I can undercut the AH by 10% (so it sells immediately) and can still profit 50% over the vendor price, then it's worth it. Usually I do this every few levels or once my bags are filled with white items, and spend about 15-20minutes at the AH listing all the stuff I pick up, then vendoring whatever I didn't list.

Listing all the white items on the AH won't get you to 100g by 40, unless you're lucky, but you'll probably get to 50 or 60g this way. Indeed, you may find an item along the way that sells for way more than it should, and you can quickly farm up 10g or so in that item. You'd be surprised what sells for what.

If you truly want to get a mount exactly at 40 then I'd recommend that you utilize gathering professions to try and make up the difference. Fishing is the best way to make money (probably because it's so tedious) but herbalism can also make you a pretty penny if your farming them along the way.

Best of luck to you
