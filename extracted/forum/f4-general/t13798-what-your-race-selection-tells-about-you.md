Chances are that you don't like people talking back to you though and it can activate your inner zug, resulting in you leaving the crossfaction party and massacring those annoying guys. Gnomes are evil. It's a fact. They blew up their own capital. They can summon demons but can't heal or can't use any form of holy magic. Those gnome players who are honest about who they are. They don't wanna look cute and they absolutely embrace every shíttalk they get after a nasty gank.  **High elf**
You probably have your own picture as your PC wallpaper and chances are that your first másturbation happened in front of a mirror. You know that you are better than anyone else and you rolled a high elf to inform the others about the same thing. Wherever you go, you start asking for quests and then declaring HRs. Even as a lvl 50 DPS in Hateforge. Your tauren-sized ego is only comparable to your molten giant-sized sensitivity if you think that someone somehow disrespected you (for example a tank dared to roll need on a tanking item or the troll was breathing).

 **Night elf**
You are the skinnier hippie type. Loving nature, hating humans and refusing to see bathrooms for extended periods of time. Chances are that if you play a nelf male, you wanna tmog him as Dracula. Men playing as nelf females should get a girlfriend.

 **Tauren**
The fat hippie type. Loving people much more than the nelf variant but their giant and frequently fat-filled body usually hides insecurities. You go to random people and /moo them in the hopes that they give some kind of positive feedback to you. You sometimes don't roll need for upgrades and just wanna be friendly instead of accomplishing anything, ever. There are three exceptions though. Tauren tank warriors are gods of the game, who just wanna protect everyone with their extensive knowledge of big moo. Tauren female mains either need a girlfriend and a psychologist at the same time or they have some extreme anger issues and they just wanna make you suffer out of whatever reason. Beware the female taurens, they might stand next to your dying body as your healer, /moo at you and just leave the instance. Maybe with a monologue about how bad everyone else is.

 **Undead**
You listen to some black metal about sacrificing babies to the dark lord. Your life consists of oppressed emotional traumas and made-up reasons why not to visit a psychiatrist. Your favourite season is the fall because you love to see the leaves dying. In the depth of your heart you just wanna be accepted and loved but something went so wrong in your life that you rather do some edgelord gankfest instead of accepting your feelings. You are pretty good at the game, though. I mean REALLY good. Specially at the PVP part of it.

 **Dwarf**
You are similar to the orc, just chiller. Except for the dwarven paladins, they are just ássholes. You crack a beer, wanna have some fun, and unlike the orc, you don't feel the urge to beat up everyone if someone said soemthing you don't like. Or you are just one of them dwarven priests. Fearward is best ward, bis priest goes brr.

 **Troll**
You are probably roleplaying with a fake Jamaican accent. People find you friendly at first just to realize later that you don't make dramas because you just don't care. They first see the troll who lets the undead get the weapon in the dungeon without any quarrel. Then later people see the true colors of the troll, when the raid wipes because the troll healer decided to disappear for 10 minutes to roll a blunt, in the middle of the boss fight.
