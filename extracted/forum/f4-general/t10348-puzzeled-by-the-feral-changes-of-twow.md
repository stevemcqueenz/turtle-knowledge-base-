# Puzzeled by the Feral changes of Twow

## Post 67604 by Mahga — 2023-09-29T13:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67604#p67604 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone,

I'm trying to wrap my head around the changes made to Feral druids in Twow and those upcoming with the new patch.

On one hand, it's pretty neat.

Cats have a DPS cooldown that removes fear and can maintain a buff for some haste. It works like rogue's adrenaline rush and give cats a breathing room for a little mana regen where they don't have to powershift to shred spam. Bear have now a Last Stand, can use their rage through Savage Bite, and get sort of a dps cooldown with Enrage and the new Blood Frenzy Talent.

Both get 3% hit from Natural Weapon, and that's pretty neat   smiling_turtle_head  .

On the other hand, it's confusing.

For cats, the new Tiger's fury  is weird. You keep the melee haste when you shift out, but you lose the bonus damage. I can't think of it being anything else than an oversight. Maybe an attempt to push player out of the "powershifting" playstyle ?

For Bears, well you still can't use Bark Skin in form, wich is honestly mindbogling considering the balanced nature of the spell (reducing your auto timer as well as your damage taken).

Also you still really almost exclusively on autos for the pull. With no expertise, one dodge or two if the boss is untauntable you and your mates are going to be in trouble. You could use your enrage pre-pull to give you that 40 total rage and use the new Savage bite to help with the pull, but then you lose the ability to use Enrage for more threat.

Again, it's weird and confusing on how these were the solutions to Druid's design issues.

I can see the team is trying to make changes to answer some issues like the MCP farming. Next patch we get Feral AP on weapons ... Witch again is confusing because MCP is such a boost for DPS and Threat that you can't really go without it, or you need ridiculous AP values that will conflinct with PVP balance.

Also Also druids still can't use items in form, in a vanilla+ experience. That's preposterous.    wary_turtle_head

Is there any plan from the team to make meaningfull changes to Feral Druids ?

Do you think druids are fine as it is and i'm just stoopid ?

Let me read your thoughts on the matter   smiling_turtle_head

As for autos, all tanks run into this problem.  If you're parried on pull, you could be SOL.  To help eliminate this chance, I make sure I know what the mob is capable of (is it tauntable, stunnable...does bash work, etc).  If i know its going to stun me, I fap.  If I know its a lot of damage, I lip, then go bear, then aoe taunt.  If its something where I'm picking up multiple targets, I try to pull w/ wrath.  While the bolt is traveling (combat starts, but mobs don't aggro till it lands), I shift, enrage, pop MCP+trinkets then go to town.  Savage bite is best used when CC/OOC procs.  FF also procs OOC, so it is vital to work it into your rotation.  protip, If you have a maul queued up, and it will go off on the next gcd, savage bite won't eat your CC proc.  double tap esc, then hit savage bite, it will eat the CC proc and you're free to get an instant maul.  Similarly, make sure you're swiping and using maul.
