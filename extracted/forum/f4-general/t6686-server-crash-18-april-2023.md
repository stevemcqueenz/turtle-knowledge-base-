## Post 42111 by Oubliette — 2023-04-18T21:36:00Z

Hardcore players: When the server crashes (as all servers do from time to time) AND your character is in a MOB the server will roll back the playtime approx 3 minutes. SO, this means your character will be further back from where you left them. IF you just hearthed when the server crashed you will be back in the zone before you hearthed.

If you are concerned about spawning in mobs. Take one of your high levels out to completely clear the area before spawning in again. If you do not have a high level and think your character might be in danger. Log on another character and ask for help in the HC guild to clear the area.

If you cant do any of the above be prepared to fight the second you respawn.. but know this, your character takes about 20-30 sec to load in and if you are in the middle of mobs on load in, don't be surprised when you load into a ghost.

BE SMART OUT THERE< STAY ALIVE.
