# Burning out/bubble bursting

## Post 6502 by Realnicolbolas — 2020-06-22T11:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6502#p6502 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Long time Player here. Wanted to ask and talk about in all honesty, that iv'e lost all real motivation to keep playing WoW.

It really dawned on me that i don't like PVE content, in execution it's essentially a long grind for gear so that you can grind harder content. I feel genuinely uninterested and unmotivated to keep continuing on like i did before. Before i was motivated to Aquire the Atiesh caster legendary, but then it dawned on me. *Why bother?* In the end the Loot isn't really worth it. it feels like a rat race that if i want to keep playing with everyone else, i have to devote more and more time i do not have.

PVP is not worth my time because i simply am not good at PVP, i know that i can't win against people whose whole characters are dedicated PVP Specs.

In the end, the real reason i would play is for the Roleplay, and the stories i could take part in, and even then the RP scene has stagnated. all of the guilds aside from one have fallen by the wayside, and i simply can't afford to stay up till 2:30am anymore for RP. the AQ events and campaign is months/years away, And i have no idea if the Introduction of High elves will really add any real reason to keep RPing.

In honesty, maybe this game just isn't for me. I like games that i can load up and play with people on the spot (Warcraft 3 & 2 for example,) maybe this isn't just something i should keep playing.
