Hey everyone!

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/zGTbxTtk/4.png)

During one of my adventures exploring Azeroth, I stumbled upon something really intriguing. In the western reaches of the Burning Steppes, hidden amidst the rugged terrain, there are signs of what appears to be a planned road or passage cutting across the mountains. Yet, it seems like this grand idea was abandoned before it ever came to fruition.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/Kvkgg6tw/1.png)

It got me thinking—was this road a relic of Blizzard’s original design plans, or could it be a hint of something Twow might expand upon in the future?

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/HxkrWRpS/2.png)

Either way, it sparked an idea that could be an incredible addition to the game: a brand-new quest hub in this forgotten corner of the world. Imagine a zone nestled in a frigid, icy climate, providing a stark contrast to the fiery landscapes of the Burning Steppes. It could cater to players in the level 40-50 range, with quests that explore the mysteries of this lost road and the secrets buried beneath the snow.

What do you all think? Wouldn’t it be awesome to breathe life into this hidden part of Azeroth?
