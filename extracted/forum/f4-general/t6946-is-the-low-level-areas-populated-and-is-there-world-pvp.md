> Thanks do you find the experience alot different then original wow? any pros/cons? seems quite attractive to try this out!

It's faster than OG Vanilla; more comparable to Classic. Could be even faster than Classic though if you have Warmode enabled and are utilizing Tents (new item from the Secondary Profession: Survival. It allows players to craft a Tent that anyone in range can use. It gets you up to two levels of stored Rested Experience in a mere 20min or so. You need to reach Nessingwary's Camp in STV to learn Survival).

Pros: The server's population, the additional content/balance tweaks, and the varied gameplay modes you can enable. I enjoy the Hardcore Challenge and have one Immortal level 60 from it, but I also had the itch to level a casual character with Warmode that can freely run dungeons and do wild stuff without the fear of death. There are guilds that focus on RP as well. So, any play-style can be accommodated.

Cons: Currently the only real cons I could say is the client and current population at peak times. This servers runs on the old client which feels less fluid and responsive compared to Classic's, however, there are ways to improve this through setting tweaks/using Turtle's FOV.exe for betting framerate and resolution. Others have suggested things like VanillaFixes. You also have to spend some time acquiring addons/macros that are compatible with Vanilla 1.12 and that can take a little time if you don't have files back-upped anywhere. On the topic of population: it is very nice that this server has grown so well in size, but peaks times can cause a chance of some slight instability which is something to keep in mind, especially if you are attempting the Hardcore challenge.

Overall, I would compare Turtle closer to the freshness and feel of OG Vanilla rather than Classic. Which, to me, is a very good thing.
