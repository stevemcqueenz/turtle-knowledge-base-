## Post 117310 by Fugus (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-01-20T03:40:00Z

Overall they can, just create gear that is of the same item level of what they currently have, just with different itemization. There will eventually hit a limit, but it won't be one of not being able to create near gear so much as them having so much end game content that players are spread too thin trying to get groups for anything which would lead some instances to be almost abandoned.

But, this would also have them opening up itemization for more niche specs if they go that route as well. Overall, I feel they will run out of playerbase from people moving on long before they run out of possibilities for new content.

Just look at Guild Wars or Elder Scrolls online, the new content doesn't replace the old.
