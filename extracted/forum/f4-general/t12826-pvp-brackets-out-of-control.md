# PvP Brackets out of control

## Post 85549 by Griffith961 — 2024-01-22T04:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85549#p85549 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone reading, especially people interested in ranking i'd urge you to read this. I'm a player who has played through the whole ranking in classic both open world and Battleground phases and i know a thing or two about brackets, and you can find info on them anywhere. Bracket is like a set of top (insert number) people which is dependent on how many people participate in pvp on the set realm. Brackets are divided but most realms set 3 up. Now what i have noticed, and mind me i may be wrong, high level rankers seem to have no clue about this as i have spoken to them about it and are just trying to one up each other as they go up the path to r13 or r14. The bracket caps are WAY to high right now, person on standing 5 has done 700k this week and will probably push for a milion, and again thats standing 5 meaning theres 4 people who are doing better. Now you might ask well "why should i care" well let me tell you, the higher they go, all the bracket caps move up, so lets say they stacked and made a fair number, the people who are casual or even people doing 100-200k would be getting much more rank per week. Some time ago on the realm i seen a few ppl talking about caps and the cap for the week was 400k, and it was heaven on earth, i was getting 3 ranks each week with mere participating.Now im not saying it should saying ranks should be handed on a silver plate but it has gone way ot of hand, even the Bracket one guys are doing a disservice to eachother, having to play 20 hours a day and sleep play the rest (trust me ive been there). On Classic there were realm with similar pop to our server that had such low caps that ive seen a hunter rank 13 well on his way to r14 chilling on 299k, they were super organized. This post is not a critique but some food for thought or perhaps a wake up call. If i didnt explain it good you can find youtube videos hell even wow wiki has info on this. Anyways on my realm on classic this is what our brackets looked like
Bracket 3 (rank 1-8 usually) anything up to 300k (depending on how fast you wanna go)
Bracket 2 (rank8-12 usually) we would all stack just a little below 400k
Bracket 1 (finishers, usually a premade of 10) stacking just a little below 500k
And we had a discord and posted our caps on the end of the week, and if we could do it for how many the hell people we had on classic it can be easily done here, and without much pressure with the stacking. Now if youre thinking, this would never work cause of the bots, well if you notice a bot going well over his way, afking (we notice the easily you must admit) a mass report and swift action from the staff would eliminate that issue. If you have read all this, i hope i was coherent enough to explain the problem. And please do correct me if ranking system is different here.

tl'dr top rankers are out doing each other making themselves and the rest of competitive/casual rankers suffer
