Well, that's the problem here. Apart from having fundamentally different approaches to the game (highlighting the social aspect vs highlighting the solo aspect), you have a very particular usecase in mind. From the looks of it some kind of RP-centered low-level multiboxing.

But if you change the rules, they will affect way *way* more than that. Boosting, high-level multiboxing, multiboxing in raids, etc, etc. You have to look at the bigger picture to evaluate this change. In terms of effective power, 1+1 should not equal 2 (or even 1.8) when it comes to multiboxing. Because that obsoletes player interaction. Then everyone would run ZG with 8 people and 16 characters. Double gold income, double weekly quest items, double bijou drops per player. That's a crazy power spike and destruction of social interaction.
For multiboxing 1+1 should equal 1.4 or less, and that's pretty close to the reality right now. There's supposed to be friction losses from multiboxing that make it unattractive to multibox *everything* (most notably raids).

Turtle multiboxing rules are quite permissive (especially after the recent rework). I'm very happy with them and I enjoy multiboxing within the confines of these rules very much. There's no need to permit automation. Neither from a gameplay perspective, nor from the perspective of angering the community further against multiboxers.
