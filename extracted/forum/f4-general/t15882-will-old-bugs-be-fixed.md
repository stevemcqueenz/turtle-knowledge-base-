# Will old bugs be fixed?

## Post 108192 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-16T21:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108192#p108192 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There are about 2000 bug reports on the bug-tracker. I understand that it's huge amount of reports and team probably won't fix everything.
From what I've seen most bugs that are being fixed are the new ones and that worries me.
There are some old bugs that've been on a server for years. I have reported a few that in my opinion need urgent fix (f.e. [AV quest that should be repeatable and actually isn't](https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=6475) which locks some content that AV offers), however, some of these issues weren't even categorized. This leads me to doubt if they will be ever fixed unless I submit the report once again which is stupid way but works.
If the bug isn't reported regularly or isn't popular, that doesn't mean it should be ignored. There was a period of time (that lasted a year maybe) when oranges couldn't be summoned in battlegrounds. For a long time until it was reported - most people believed the devs removed it intentionally which turned out not to be true and the oranges came back to battlegrounds week after the report.

Will the old bugs be ever fixed? Please, don't focus only on newly submitted reports.
