Example:
Gordok 10 stam alcohol from dire maul is exclusive with Rumsey rum black label 15 stam alcohol
But the turtlecwow new kara10 merlot alcohol for either 25 stam or 15 int both can be combined with the Gordok rum from dire maul.

For food an example
 25 magic mushroom stam food can be combined with 2% haste food, 10 agi food or 8 mph food or chocolate fish food, but it can not be combined with 20 strength or dragon chili

Elixir of sages 18 stam 18 int does not stack with mage int buff and priest spirit buff, making it worthless

Elixir of brute forces 18 strength 18 stam does not stack with Preist stam buff, and juju gives 30 strength, hence elixir of brute force is worthless too

The following armor buffs all stack: Scroll of protection IV + elixir of superior protection + crystal defense (from ungori)

wrath of cenarius procc ring (132 sp procc on cast) does not stack with elixir of greater arcane power (35sp), making the ring worse than expected by 35 sp if you have that consumable active (this one is according to balake)
