# How does Glyph of War work?

## Post 45844 by Scriss — 2023-05-14T17:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45844#p45844 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know the glyph of war makes you flagged in the world and non-flagged players can't heal or buff you. But can I heal or receive heals from the other faction while in dungeons and raids? Is the idea that if I use the glyph of war I shouldn't group unless I'm with other flagged players?

## Post 45845 by Rat2156 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-14T17:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45845#p45845 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

non-warmode players can still buff and heal you, but doing so will flag them for PvP for 5 minutes.

Grouping and doing dungeons with non-flagged players is a non-issue as being in a group with someone of the other faction allows them to heal or buff you without any problem

## Post 45846 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-14T17:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45846#p45846 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Apart from the obvious (being pvp flagged and not being able to turn it off) there are no mechanical drawbacks for you as the warmode player.
The thing becomes complicated when you join groups because you essentially force pvp onto your party. Anyone that interacts with you through buffs (even just Battle Shout) or heals will become temporarily pvp flagged themselves and every player that does just spreads pvp like a pleague until usually the whole party has it. So I would say warmode has a pretty selfish component to it because your teammates don't get much of a choice, even if they don't want to engage in pvp at all. This can mean you all die to the same gank, or - worse perhaps - you force pvp onto a cross-faction member and they get ganked by someone from your side and you can't do anything about it.

So the only drawback for you is some players might refuse to group up with you or to buff/heal you while still outside the instance.
