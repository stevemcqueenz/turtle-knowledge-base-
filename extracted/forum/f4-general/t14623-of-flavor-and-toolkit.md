# Of flavor and toolkit

## Post 99779 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-07-18T11:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99779#p99779 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

This will be a longer article.
There are two things I was thinking about a lot recently, class flavor (potential flavor fails) and toolkit usage at raiding. What I'm writing about are subjective thoughts, not objective truths of course. However, I feel that these topics are underdiscussed for the importance they have.

First things first, the flavor.
I wanna share my personal experience of warrior tanking in TWOW (leveled up most classes to experiment with them).
During leveling, it feels awesome even if I went prot tree and even if I gave up on any aspect of exp grinding besides dungeon tanking. My cow had a very interesting toolkit and protecting the teammates was a very interesting aspect.
When I decided to make my warrior a tank, I went by flavor (I always do). I imagined a protective bulwark of plate watching over a group.
Things started to shatter at the early 50s when I needed to tank for pruple-geared DPS farming certain dungeon items. I gave up on my warrior tank at level 60. The reason for it was the meta. Fury talent tree has many ways to increase your threat and damage and a shield macro is more welcome then wielding a shield by default. Also, stance dance has to be forgotten, noone has TP for tactical mastery. For me it was a flavor fail because this meta build didn't resonate with how I imagined my cow when created it. Spamming bloodthirst is not something a protective bulwark should do. Neither death wish. This meta build gives the flavor of a bulky barbarian who leads an army (and probably burns down villages). In the meantime the toolkit I loved became nigh unuseable (concussion blow, reducing incoming damage via revenge stuns etc).
The game allows you to raid-tank as a warrior but only if you either disregard the flavor of the proposed tank build or you're okay playing a 'protective bulwark' spamming bloodthirst and deathwish, usually hitting enemies with two weapons.
What I want to say here is that class balances should take flavor into consideration. You can absolutely tank as a warrior in TWOW. But you are expected to accept 'the barbarian flavor' if you wanna raid-tank.

Similar story with my lock and hunter. Initially I didn't even realize the thing. Leveled the warlock first. I voidie-tanked and farmed many places and it felt absolutely fine. Same didn't resonate with my hunter. When I realized what was wrong, I specced the hunter to a melee, half-tank build. So, as a warlock your demons are slaves, it is absolutely fine to send them out to be punched by the mobs while you cast your dark magic from afar. It is also ok to sacrifice them, those demons are basically your property. As a hunter though, since I am told that the pet is my trusty companion/friend, the thing felt really weird. As a BM my job is to rely on a buffed beast who does all the job for me and I'm rather a 'parasyte' than a companion to the pet. As an MM I send in the pet to soak in all the damage while I shoot the mobs down with my cool arrow abilities. Kinda what the lock does with voidie but unlike warlocks, hunters on paper don't have slaves, they have companions. Melee hunter felt fine though, doing tandem-tanking, taking hits myself to save the beast and vice-versa felt much more like a partnership.

For flavor these are the two examples which came to my mind. Sometimes I feel that the proposed game mechanics result in a 'wrong' playstyle if you think about what you're actually doing.

The other topic is somewhat unrelated, it is about raiding.
I was thinking a lot why I loved to level my (holy) priest to level 60 and why I got bored with it after 3-4 raids.
The answer was that raids require you to specialize on 1 or 2 things and you should only do those things. Understandable, we are usually 10+ people, optimized output comes with specialization, just like in real life. It however takes away a big chunk of your toolkit and the whole experience becomes boring.
For example when I played dungeons with my priest, I buffed the people, used several healings on several ranks, cursed, wanded, sometimes jumped in the middle and spammed holy nova while making sure mana is always fine. Main thing was to react to the environment and use something from your big toolkit.
When I raided, I not only gave up on my non-healing toolkits beside buffs, even my healing toolkit got narrowed down. HoT was rarely needed and was usually auto-done by a priest with full T2. Prayer of healing was rarely needed since your group is a caster group which usually takes way less damage than some others. Shield became very fringe. Holy nova was barely used anymore. Not even proper downranking was done. I enjoyed the mana management part of the dungeons but at raids I felt that I am forced to spend mana negligently then just consume up whenever mana was low. There was no mana management anymore, you just had to farm and consume a lot to avoid thinking about proper downgrades.
Similar story with my druid. Honestly, the cow is around mid 30s now so I have no direct raiding experience with druids at TWOW. But hear this story out: I was in SM Lib, went there as balance. The healer was really underleveled, needed a bunch of help: I played 40% of the dungeon as an offheal. We were kinda in a hurry, I had no time to always drink up: I played as a cat 40% of the time to do DPS and recharge mana. Sometimes the tank needed some help and I didn't have enough mana to properly offheal: bear form and taunting away a big hitter, around 10% of the time. As a 'pre-boomkin' I was 40% healer, 40% cat, 10% bear and only 10% balance. Compare this to the druid raid experiences.
Or compare warlock gameplay at the outside world vs. a warlock in raid.
Do the same with frost mages.
What I'm saying is that TWOW could have some small (5-10 men) raids with decent loot. Helps gearing up and getting cool items (let's be real, this game feeds itself on your greed) while being able to play a class as it is intended instead of being reduced to a bot spamming 2 buttons for 2 hours, 3 times a week. I just wanna enjoy my toon to the fullest at 60 while also feeding my greed with cool items.
