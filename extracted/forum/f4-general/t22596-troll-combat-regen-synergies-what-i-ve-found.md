# Troll Combat Regen Synergies - What I've Found

## Post 157234 by Troggyboy — 2025-10-27T01:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157234#p157234 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

So, Trolls in Twow have their passive HP regeneration improved by 20% and have 25% of it continue while in combat. But I was wondering, just how far can we push this and how does it affect all the different classes?

First, some other things that allow in-combat regeneration and that can be stacked on top of the troll passive:

 -The Mithril Scale Shoulders, a piece of lvl 42 mail gear that can be made by blacksmiths and that gives 10% combat regen <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=7932>

 -Up to two Stellar Ruby Rings at level 54 which also give 10% combat regen each and can be made by jewelcrafters <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=55261>

 -And lastly (and I think most interestingly) is "Strider Presence", a pet ability for striders that give yet another 20% to anyone that is in a group with one <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=36531>

Now let's do the math . . . 25% (troll passive) + 10% (mithril scale shoulders) + 20% (two stellar ruby rings) + 20%(strider passive), then multiply it all by 1.2 with the troll passive multiplier . . . and we're sitting at a whopping 90%(!!!) of your regular health regen continuing in combat.

The only problem of course is that you also have to stack spirit for it to be effective. But in between enchants, zanza potions, spirit buffs and in some cases late game gear it's also not as difficult to achieve as it might seem at first.

Now, let's go through each class one by one to see how well they can make use of all this, in theory at least:

 **Warriors:**
Pros:
 -Naturally stack armor and are tanky, allowing them to stay alive longer to regen more
 -Can effectively kite other melees with hamstring to buy time for regen
 -Can buy even more time with defensive abilities like shield wall, shield block and just block in general
 -Naturally lack sustain, so in combat regen is a godsend for them
Cons:
 -With a few exceptions here and there, it is hard to get spirit on plate or even mail, although there is a tier 3 set with some spirit on it for dps warriors
 -Getting spirit will have natural competition with the other stats warriors usually get - it might be better to just get strength, stamina or even agility instead, even with the synergy

 **Hunters:**
Pros:
 -The easiest class to stack all the in combat regen buffs on - they can wear the shoulders not that far away from the point where they can wear mail anyway and they don't need to get into groups with other hunters to get the strider buff, they can just get one themselves.
 -Have multiple tools to buy time for regeneration by kiting, slowing, feinting death and switching aggro onto your pet
Cons:
 -This will be much more difficult to do in PvP than in PvE
 -Spirit is also difficult to get on leather, although there are a surprising amount of leatherworking recipes during early leveling aswell as a helm for elemental leatherworkers I believe
 -Just like warriors, spirit will compete with the other stats hunters will naturally aim for, mainly agility and stamina

 **Rogues:**
Pros:
 -Probably have the best tools out of all available classes to get uptime for their combat regen - all the gouges, kidney shots and other stuns not to mention just the ability to get back into stealth and regenerate while in it
 -Similarly to Warriors do not have much natural sustain, so having in combat regen is a great boon for them
Cons:
 -As established earlier on, very difficult to find spirit on leather with the few exceptions I mentioned
 -Same deal again as with hunters and warriors, you will likely be aiming for other stats like agility and stamina which will compete with any spirit gear you might wanna get
 -No access to the shoulders as they can't wear mail

 **Priests:**
Pros:
 -Holy priests already naturally stack spirit because of their talents that allow their mana regen to continue while casting aswell as turning spirit into spellpower. This means they will get all the perks of the troll passive without really needing to change up their playstyle or gear
 -Stacking the HP regen on top of their shields, armor buffs and heals will make priests even harder to kill
 -Cloth gear already naturally has lots of spirit on it
Cons:
 -Priests already have great sustain - it's questionable if they really need to invest into this strat at all
 -Also don't have access to the shoulders

 **Shamans:**
Honestly don't know crap about this class, one of you guys will have to do it lol

 **Mages:**
Pros:
 -While it's not as large of a factor as with priests probably, mages also already have inherent synergies with spirit via their Evocation spell and Arcane Meditation talent. So you're likely to already be stacking spirit anyway and just get the HP regen on top for free.
 -Another class without much inherent HP sustain during combat, so it will be very meaningful for them. Especially since each tick will be proportionally larger compared to their small HP pools.
 -Naturally have lots of occuring spirit on cloth gear already.
 -Lots of ways to buy time, especially if you're a frost mage - every ice block, slow, root and blink will mean more time for more HP regen ticks
Cons:
 -No access to mithril scale shoulders

 **Warlocks:**
Pros:
 -Out of all the classes, they can probably do the most with the extra HP the troll regen passive will grant them since they're the most HP hungry out of them all. Mostly because of life tap, but it will also come in handy for stuff like health funnel or hellfire.
 -While locks do not naturally aim for lots of spirit, since they wear cloth they actually have great access to it if they so choose.
 -In between the already large HP pools they have and their shields and fears they have lots of tools to buy time to regenerate.
 -Warlocks are already a class that is based around the concept of buying time for yourself while your dots tick away - so their natural playstyle lends itself really well to this kind of build.
Cons:
 -Warlocks usually get all their HP from stacking as much stamina as possible and this will likely not change, even with the troll passive that will make spirit better. It will also make investing into the Demonic Embrace talent feel that much more awkward.
 -No access to the shoulders.

Alright that's it I think, let me know if there are any other synergies that I have missed. Also note that this was just a fun rabbit hole that I found that and all of this is just theorycrafting in the end. I found some clips of a warrior who seemed to utilize this effectively but other than that, there's not much in the way of people actually trying this afaik. Feel free to do so if you like! You should add that every class has a different amount of HP regenerated per spirit. Out of combat and standing up a warrior regenerates the most HP per spirit (0.8) while a druid regenerates the least (0.09). Every other class is somewhere in between but I'm too lazy to look it up right now. Sitting down further increases the hp regeneration (HP only, no bonus for mana regeneration). With your math 90% of HP regeneration in combat would give a warrior 0.72 HP per spirit on every tick, would need to test if sitting while in combat still gives a bonus. Spirit warrior is legit.

Scrap some 100 spirit together here and there on your troll warrior and you'd regenerate 72 hp per tick, which is basically as if a druid followed you around and casts hots on you indefinitely.

Edit: now compare that to 10% life steal. If you fought a mob with 4k HP you'd steal 400 HP until it dies. You still need to stack life steal on a lot of items to get that high, missing out on better stats (just like if youd gear for spirit) but only as much hp as 10 seconds of combat would net you through troll warrior regeneration.

Edit 2: let's do some math and ignore the strider presence bonus because tbf if you were a troll warrior aiming for max HP regeneration you'd just add a healer to your party for better open world performance than a hunter just for his strider presence. So 25% troll + 30% from items = 55% x 1.2 for being troll = 66% x 0.8 HP per tick for being warrior = 0.528 hp per spirit per tick. Still decent for 3 items. Life steal does have the benefit of increasing effect when fighting more than a single mob and the life stolen also creates threat, which might be preferable in 5-man content.
