## Post 48110 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-05-30T20:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48110#p48110 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The lazy solution is to set the game to the highest windowed, widescreen resolution, enable UI Scale, and maximize the window.  This still gives you 16:9 or 16:10 resolution at a (mostly) full screen.

Otherwise, you can play with actual fullscreen and get the same results.
