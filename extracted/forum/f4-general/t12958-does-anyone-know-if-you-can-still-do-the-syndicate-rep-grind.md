## Post 86749 by Dogearmy — 2024-02-02T11:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86749#p86749 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, for all of those not familiar, the Syndicate is a faction from original classic, who was the enemy of Ravenholdt, and you lost rep with them for completing Ravenholdt quests. Possibly they were just there for flavor, but if you killed Ravenholdt NPCs, you gained reputation with them, maxing out at 2999/3000 neutral. Turtle WoW added some things to the faction, including a quartermaster, which lets you buy a mask at friendly, as well as an alliance only quest chain, which awards some rep. So, im wondering if horde side you still can do this grind, because im concerned the only way to get past 2999/3000 in turtle wow is said quest chain. Can anyone horde side confirm if they did it, or anyone alliance side confirm they got to friendly without doing said quest chain?
