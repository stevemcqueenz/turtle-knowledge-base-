## Post 168797 by Froghijab420 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-02-18T18:55:00Z

Hi all just wanted to thank the Ambershire players, and Twow devs for the amount of fun I'm having with Pvp recently (First character made around 2 months ago, just got Bloodring exalted title - I'm so addicted!!!).
I feel like Pvp on Turtle gets a lot of unnecessary hate.

 **Here are some changes from Blizzard classic that I've really enjoyed.**
 1. New Content
 -Bloodring is amazing
 -New "Eye of the Storm" type bg soon.

 2. Quick Que (especially on honor days).
 -Way faster que's than blizzard classic (I played horde then and 20-30 minutes que's sucked ass).
 -Faster due to cross-faction; At first I thought I wouldn't like this cross-faction, but making friends with people on both factions has been nice.

 3. Gear changes.
 -Arathi Basin 3 piece set changes, and WSG gear buffs are great.
 -Bloodring 2 set piece, plus I'm currently playing balance druid - getting a full set of offspec feral gear is really nice from bloodring.
 -PvP gear changes teased.

 4. Honor decay is rank only
 -Get to keep Pvp titles and take breaks unlike is such a nice change

 5. Nicer player-base
 -People here I feel are much nicer on average, minus a few toxic evil rogues.

Just wanted to hear other peoples thoughts, I know class balance isn't the best atm but it wasn't either on regular vanilla.  I probably would've quit since PvE right now is just raid-logging and pressing 1-3 buttons on bosses with 1-2 mechanics in MC (Excited for BWL changes).
