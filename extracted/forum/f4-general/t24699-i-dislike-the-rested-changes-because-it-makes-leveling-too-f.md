# I dislike the Rested Changes because it makes leveling... too fast

## Post 173937 by Roestilu — 2026-04-03T18:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173937#p173937 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

As a Slow and Steady player I gain more Rested than I could ever use up. With the 50% exp from mobs and the 50% bonus exp from Rested, it takes twice the kills it would take a normal player to use up all my accumulated Rested Exp and quadruple the kills it would've taken a normal player before the patch. In the past, I could avoid tents. I can't avoid Inns, so just by handing in or accepting quests or vendoring stuff, I gain so much Rested I couldn't ever use it up before my next return. That means I'm permanently Rested and my Exp gain from kills went up from 50% to 75% which is very noticeable. It makes me try to spend as little time as possible in Inns, I'm not reading quest texts anymore when I'm in an Inn, I'm not logging off in front of the fire on a rainy night, my personal role playing has suffered. I loved the speed of S&S before, but now not so much.

Same goes for players who don't use S&S, who are now effectively playing on a 1.5x rate server. I'd argue the change went against what Turtle WoW is about, it increases the average leveling speed across the player base and causes players to miss the new low level content.

I and players like me are unhappy because they gained a 25% increase to their Exp and players who like to power level and grind to 60 are unhappy because they lost 50% of their Exp.

And I'm not very inclined to reroll my character I've already spent a literal week with just to add the Exhausted challenge.

That's the end of my rant. I wanted to make another perspective on the changes heard.

## Post 173984 by Roestilu — 2026-04-04T08:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173984#p173984 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

1. I've been avoiding taverns like the plague, I'm not AFKing in them. Since it takes so long to use up the Rested Exp as S&S and you gain so much so quickly, I have to enter a tavern before I can use up all of it so it keeps accumulating. It only takes a minute.

 2. Before the changes, the slower leveling combined with the additional quests had a really nice flow to it in my opinion. I could do much more content without overleveling too much, compared to a blizzlike rate. I wasn't underleveled, I wasn't overleveled. Turning off Exp altogether is a bandaid solution to a problem that wasn't there before. It's nice to level up, but not too fast so I don't feel like I could overlevel a dungeon when I do all the quests in the zone, it was nice to have little bits of Rested Exp when logging back in. There is a whole flow to the game that got disrupted imo.

 3. GMs don't add challenges after character creation. I asked.

For me, WoW is about the combination of all aspects the game has to offer. I don't like dividing my playtime into grinding and leveling, then gathering, then crafting, then dungeons, then exploring, then whatever. I like to do all of it at once, have all of it flow into each other and Turtle WoW is the place for me to do that. The server as a whole felt designed around my preferred way to play an MMO.

In the grand scheme of things this is all very minor, but in my opinion ***the experience I had so far was perfect and now it's less perfect***. Moving away from tents and into taverns is a great idea because the tent blobs felt kinda wrong and taverns are the perfect place for people to hang out already, but it came at the cost of changing the flow of the game which was not an improvement in my opinion.
