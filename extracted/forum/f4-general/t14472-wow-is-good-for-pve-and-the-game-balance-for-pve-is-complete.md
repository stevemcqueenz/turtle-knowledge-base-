# WoW is good for PvE, and the game balance for PvE is completely wrong for PvP

## Post 98614 by Matches (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-07-05T21:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98614#p98614 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, this is my last ever post on the forums.

I've been playing BGs - mainly AV - for two or three months now and I want to express one key, central, profound problem for PvP in WoW.

Now, I may be wrong, but I think WoW was game balanced for PvE - and for PvE WoW is very good.

I think however that once that was all done, and WoW was game balanced and ready to go for PvE, someone said - let's have PvP as well - and PvP was then enabled, *without any changes to game balance*.

And this is totally and utterly broken.

So I want to consider basically two factors; the mechanics of combat, both in terms of PvE and PvP, and *fun*.

Starting with PvE, we see players can do lots of damage and have lots of stuns/fear/sap/you name it.

All of this is absolutely fine for mobs - bigger mobs are a lot stronger than players, and mobs don't mind being stunned one bit; they're software.  So we have players, slugging it out, with plenty of special combat abilities to bring to bear, and the mobs being very tough and so fights lasting for some time.  All good fun.  Works well.

Now let's take those players - who are well equipped for PvP and mobs as they are in WoW - and now line them up again other players.

What do we find?  players do a lot of damage, have a lot of stun/fear/sap/you name it, and players are *weak*.  Not much in the way of health.

My experience of PvP is this : wait to spawn, run halfway across map, meet single horde - and then about five seconds of stun stun stun then death.

At no point can I move, cast, do anything; and often I have no idea the horde is there until I'm stunned.

Let's say I run into a hunter.  I'm mounted, range is about 25 yards, say.  There some special shot which makes me wander about, so that comes first.  Then a shot which makes me slow.  Then I'm dead.

It takes about five seconds.

Rogues.

Meet a rogue, well, you know it because you're sapped or stunned, and the you keep being stunned, until you're dead, five seconds later.

Warlock.  Three or four dots in seconds, and if you stop to deal with them you die, and if you keep running to avoid them, you die from the dots.  To come with *30 yards of a warlock* means helpless death very shortly after.

Warrior.  Charge - with stun.  Intercept stun.  That hammer blow special effect.  If it's Tauren, also the racial stun.  At the end of it, a few seconds later, you're dead.  Couldn't move, couldn't cast, nothing.

Remember what I said about fun?

Spawn, run, run, approximately instant death with no warning and where you're completely helpless the whole time.

Repeat.  Forever and ever and ever.  Again and again and again.

This is as much fun as having both arms ripped off.

Game mechanics which mean *instant unavoidable death with no warning* are no good - and even worse in BG, that's followed by a significant down time, waiting for respawn and getting back into the map - and that is all that PvP is, in WoW.

Sooner or later, I'm going to run my own server, and in it, players will be immune to all stun/confusion-type effects, and PvP damage will be 33% of normal damage.  Players will basically be like mobs; they will need killing, rather than being instant kills.

The game will be normal for PvE, where it is well balanced.
