Gambling on windfury, or maximizing your elemental burst damage at the expense of all else, are definitely weird playstyles. They're not going to be effective or consistent in any setting and do have the potential to be problematic in PvP. At the same time that kind of explosive, unreliable damage is pretty central to the class identity of Shaman IMO. I'm never going to match the DPS output of a mage, or the utility of a druid, but every now and then getting a huge number and atomizing an enemy is really fun.

I'm concerned that the changes indicate that that sort of explosive burst damage/rng style is currently seen more as a problem to be fixed than a key part of the class' identity.
