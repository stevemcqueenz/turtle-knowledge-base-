# Rating the new content from Emerald Sanctum to Hyjal

## Post 72817 by Jombo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-22T15:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=72817#p72817 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi,

Now that substantial new content has been added, I figured it merited some comments and evaluation from us players based on a 1/5 - 5/5 rating. I'll start here and others can continue:

Emerald Sanctum: 3/5
Not bad, cool atmosphere, boss is okay. It is overfilled with boring trash mobs and the boss fight itself could be more clear and epic in its design. Feels more like a ZG scale boss than a true 40-man one.

Gilneas: 3/5
Well done, very impressive hand-crafted zone. It suffers though from a very uneven levelling path. Arriving there at level 40 (Alliance), everything seemed fine for a couple of quests. Quickly it turns out that everything after that initial point is made for lvl 42+ with a lvl 40 having orange and red quest mobs only. This is very weird since the whole northern part of the zone has lower levelled mobs, but these are completely unused for the quests - that's like 40% of the zone!
With better quest distribution, the zone would easily be 4/5 in my book for its cool design. Until then, it is, unfortunately, a complete waste of time to go there at the intended starting level.

Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility: 5/5
This new city is super cool and you even added some interesting quests to finally give gnomes their - whether one likes gnomes or not - well-deserved place in the world. Really amazing and original work!

Hyjal: 2/5
Cool ambience and music. The zone, though, has a horrible linear design which makes me want to stay away at all costs except for going to ES. This design needs to be fixed by opening up more direct paths from north to south. Teleport runes are not an excuse. Make proper paths to make it a proper, dynamic zone.

Karazhan 10 man: 5/5
This is, almost, a masterpiece. I haven't had as much fun in a dungeon in a very long time. Some bosses could require more tactics, but the ambience, mob packs, music and overall design makes for a great experience.

Emerald Sanctum 2/5
I like its ambiance. In a way it feels like an Ony raid set in Ireland. Trash can be challenging, random deers are hilarious.
However, 1,5 boss (plus hardmode option) is way too few for this dungeon size. Ony works because you run in, clear 2-3 packs and fight Ony. Here you enter the raid and fight the same packs for 40 minutes before going for the boss/bosses.
Attunement also reminds me to Ony. Arcanite rod was a bad idea for q3, having to pay around 150g for a raid access is only acceptable for Naxx, the ultimate raid. No other raids had a pricetag like this.
Loots however, are awesome.
My suggestions are: remove arcanite rod from attunement and either make the map smaller or add 1-2 more bosses. If the map remains this big, please also enable mounts in the instance.

Kara 4,5/5
Great design, great mechanics, no attune. My only problem is that loots are so good, the raid has to balance itself out with a huge ID expiration date.

Hyjal 2/5
Agreeing with you, mobility is lacking, it takes too much time to get anywhere in the region even with epic riding. It has some interesting parts like the satyrs or the demon den and I'd consider rating it as a 4 if it were easier to apprach the zone and if you could better travel in it.

Great one. Great design, ambiance, it is appropriately challenging.

I think balance can be done.
Let's say we have raid items from T0,5 to T3 and we assign raids to tier levels (like Naxx is T3 only, ZG T1,5 max).
If TWOW creates a custom raid, it has to be allocated in the tier level based on raid difficulty and the number of people it was designed for. I'd have mostly watered down Kara loots because it's a not too hard 10 man raid and small raids shouldn't go above T1 in my opinion.
Let's say Kara T0,5-T1, ES T2-2,5
When a Tier is assigned to a custom raid, specific items are balanced more easily.
Let's say you wanna create a new warrior weapon for T2. Even of other warrior T2 weapons exist, all have different niches or all are better for a certain spec. You could create new, desirable items without powercreep if they do similar things as its tier pairs but at a very similar level. You can expand horizontically instead of vertically if you fill out a bunch of different niches.
