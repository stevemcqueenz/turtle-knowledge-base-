## Post 124180 by Wolf of Rage (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-03-28T07:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124180#p124180 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Disguises are a thing in this server, so as a Rogue, you could find specific Friendly NPCs looking like Hostile Mobs, actually acting as Vendors for "Knowledge" Recipes for Rogues to be able to use Disguise to make themselves look the same -- like say, the Defias. Could try Human Rogue and tap into the heightened potential for some roguery.

As for Paladins, you could either go Human, or High Elf, I'd say. Human is popular. There are more custom Quests revolving around Paladins (or rather given by paladin-like NPCs), which show more of the flavor of such characters and the charity of those under the Light. There is even a particular important character I like that I saw on the Thalassian Highlands (hint: TBC+ WoW Paladin NPC).

There is also Orc Shaman, since, all the way back from WC3 times, you've had the Orc Race with Shaman Units and the Far Seer Hero. Orcs as Shamans here can summon Spirit Wolves, which is reminiscent of WotLK Enhancement, but is actually much closer to the Far Seer from WC3 as a result in lore terms. They're all about the elements and the spirits, and that should make for good loreseeking and questing where there are things relating to that.

Gnomes' story got some big attention here, so you could go for that... but I just don't feel any Class that fits -- maybe Gnome Mage? Hell there is even a short Quest chain involving the Silvermoon Remnants in Ironforge, and the Gnome refugees, with one of the latter's NPCs mentioning a character of the former, mixing magic with engineering. You could fixate on that and be a Gnome Mage Engineer, and delve deeper into the reconstructed content revolving around Gnomeregan.

## Post 124189 by Zulnam (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-03-28T08:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124189#p124189 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Human Paladin is probably the poster child of this topic. They were in every RTS since warcraft 2 and were born directly as a consequence of the war between the Alliance and the old Horde.

A personal favorite of mine is also Orc Warlock, which was actually a unit all the way back in Warcraft 1. Orcs have a deep connection with the dark arts if you go by the wars alone. Shadow Council, Stormreaver Clan, Twilight Hammer Clan.
If you're really good and read up on your lore you can even roleplay an Undead Death Knight (the OGs), which is a crazy trip. You are basically an orc who sacrificed their flesh for untold powers. It's a bit tricky to pull off, and you have to justify wth you were doing during the 3rd war and how you avoided capture, but you can roleplay an undead who was undead before it was cool.

Other good race/class lore combos that are easy to pull off are Human Mage and Night Elf Druid, but with enough lore knowledge at least regarding the first 3 games, you can make almost any class/race combo work.

I recommend reading the manuals for the first 3 games. They're fun, easy, reads and can be easily found on wowpedia (google warcraft 1 manual etc.)

It will open up a lot of combinations for you and also make you appreciate the vanilla world that you are playing in a lot more.
