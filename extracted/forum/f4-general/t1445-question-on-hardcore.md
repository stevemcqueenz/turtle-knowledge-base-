That's the whole point of Hardcore mode. You have to learn to bite as much as you can chew. Stay high on your performance most of the time and best if you don't die at all.

If you don't like it or found yourself overwhelmed with the drawbacks then it's best you drop the Glyph of Hardcore or try another character. Perhaps your class just doesn't fit in with your playstyle for HCM.

Might consider finding a partner to rely upon who'd help you out. Also, 0.5 XP is affecting mobs in open-world only, so run instances with reliable folks at your side is another option.

> **Himyname wrote: Tue Dec 08, 2020 4:39 am**
> If you keep dying and you lose the xp you gained from quests, you can't regain that xp again.

That's akin to saying if you ever lose $20 you'll never earn any more money for the rest of your entire life.

When you lose XP due to dying ... YOU LOSE XP.
Radical concept, I know.

The solution (if you can call it that) ... is to simply EARN MORE XP.
Exactly HOW you go about earning that "more XP" is up to you to decide how to do it.

> **Himyname wrote: Tue Dec 08, 2020 4:39 am**
> Anyone have any tips on how to deal with hardcore when you lose out on quest xp?

Hardcore mode will not convert the XP reward of a quest completion to zero.
At most, hardcore mode will COST you XP *when you die* ... but that's kind of the point of having a Hardcore Mode.  That just means you need to earn more XP to reach the next level than you would if you didn't die.
