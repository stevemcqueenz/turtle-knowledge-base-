## Post 104859 by Surelymain — 2024-10-26T04:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104859#p104859 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First off, I LOVE that the Twow team is working on adding more PvP oriented challenges. Low level PvP on Twow is really fun, and I've met a lot of cool people while doing it. I thank the team for their commitment to Warmode, because without it, most of my favorite experiences on Twow could have never happened.

I've won and lost many battles in world PvP, and get mostly positive responses from people who message me after our fight. From my experience, most people leveling with Warmode actually enjoy a good fight, and I think the Twow team has done a great job of creating a balance between PvE and PvP on Nordanaar. Warmode brings a little drama, surprise, comradery, and sometimes even funny moments to a grind that many people have already experienced.

There's no denying that low level PvP adds some spice to the overall leveling experience of Twow for many of its players. It makes the world feel more alive.

That being said, I think the Level One Lunatic challenge is a great concept, but some things about it could be changed for the better.

> **Moongirl wrote: Fri Oct 25, 2024 9:44 pm**
> **10,000 Honorable Kills:** That is a ton of people you have to get! I believe there are level 1 Battlegrounds (or Arena?), so are those going to appear more often?

Lofty goals can be good, and 10k HKs is POSSIBLE, but it will take a very very long time. I have over 2k kills with one of my level 10 twinks, and that toon is over a year old. Those kills are mostly from WPvP, with a little bit of Warsong Gulch when the queue pops. For comparison, I know a level 60 Rank 14 Priest with under 4k HKs.

Realistically, 1k kills is much more attainable for a level 1, while still being a good challenge.

> Exalted: What faction can you possibly get Exalted with at Level 1? I can not think of any.

Since you won't be doing much questing at level 1, and part of the challenge is 10k HKs, I assume the idea is to reach Exalted with one of the PvP factions. Definitely possible, but also a huge investment of time and effort. To get Exalted with a PvP faction in any reasonable amount of time you'd need to organize level 1 premades, and/or events, and/or have your level 1 logged in and queued for battlegrounds/arena in the background any time you're on your PC.

It would be cool to see level 1 BGs popping all the time, but I don't know how likely that is to happen.

Another option is to get your level 1 in a group with higher levels who are farming mobs for rep. Timbermaw, Gelkis, and Magram are the ones that immediately come to mind. Not as cool as getting it yourself, but any of those would work.
> Arena Master Trinket: I do not go PvP much, but I believe this is the Trinket in Gurubashi Arena! Aren't a bunch of Level 60s there at one time? How would you get it?

This is, by far, the easiest part of the challenge. Anyone can get this trinket at any level, but it takes time, effort, and luck. You can get it by logging in when the chest spawns and nabbing it before people get there, or while they're fighting if you're crafty enough. Not exactly easy, but if the stars align it's not really hard either. Much easier if you have any level 60 friends who are willing to help.

Overall, I think the Level One Lunatic challenge will be fun for some, but in its current form it could really backfire. 10k kills is just too many, and if people are dedicated enough to try and reach that goal, say goodbye to any amount of peace to be found in the starter zones, because it would take forever to reach 10k kills from only BGs. Personally, I don't like to camp people, and I rarely kill the same person twice unless they're rude or they attack me first. There are plenty of people who aren't like me, and will camp those zones mercilessly, possibly leading to less Warmode players and more people complaining.

Without restrictions for high-level enchants on low level gear, only people with enchants will play there. It means that very small amount of players will be there. As a result, getting 10k HKs on lvl 1 will take endless amount of time. Those who take the challenge will have to camp starting zones for Warmode players.

With enchant restrictions, it will be huge step in creating healthy pvp progression since lvl 1.
It would be perfect if you could just create character and jump in to battleground. Then level a bit, get gear from dungeons and quests and jump to battlegrounds again at lvl 9. Repeat every 10 levels.
