Everyone has its own opinion, this is why you can choose the guild with policy that contents you in this regard. I think main character generally deserves gear more than an alt. Not everyone plays on multiple characters. I like the idea of prioritizing items to characters that attend raids most frequently. Surely, you can give Thunderfury to Warrior alt but will it be the best decision made for whole guild? I don't think so. Similarly with other items. You would be mad when you reserve one item that doesn't drop for months, then one alt joins the raid, randomly reserves the item you seek and ultimately wins it over you.

I also claim that people who like the idea of allowing alts to have the same chance of receiving loot as mains are most likely people who play multiple characters. I say so because if you look at it, it is in no one's interest to allow things like this to happen.
