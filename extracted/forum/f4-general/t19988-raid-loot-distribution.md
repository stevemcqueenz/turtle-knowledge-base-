Yeah. That is how Vanilla works. Loot is scarce. You have 40 people in a raid, and at most a 20% chance that an item actually drops, going as low as 1% for certain recipes. Yes, there will be queues. As Cheruscan highlighted, it takes literal years to get full best-in-slot gear for an entire raid.

This is just a core feature of Vanilla compared to later retail versions of WoW. Loot is random and only comprises of a few drops for the entire raid group to divide up.

There are some select raids who run a completely random loot distribution. A few even raid using group loot. But as in Cheruscan's example, that takes a certain mental fortitude to see something you've been waiting on week after week for over a year going to someone who showed up for the first time and is likely to never show up again. So most raids eventually build some kind of incentive structure into their loot system, to better retain people and to keep them happier - something that prioritizes veterans and work horses in the long run. And I think there is nothing objectionable about such rules. You put in the work, you get the candy. If you spot actual nepotism (the old classic of the guild master's girlfriend getting preferential treatment) just leave the guild and look for another.

Most guilds try to run a fair system, just for reasons of self-preservation and player retention. But that obviously means you won't get an immediate shot at every single item. But you know what the best way of getting preferential treatment is? Starting at the bottom. Join a guild who is just starting out in Molten Core. Do the entire progression. It's a lot of fun, I did it from the first pull in BWL to downing KT it took about 9 months (mind you, that was before the massive player influx and before CC2 made most encounters a joke). And then once you have Naxx on farm you'll be right at the top of the list for all that sweet juicy BiS loot. Especially if you've been an active player, helped stock the guild bank, helped lead raids, develop strategies or manage the roster.
Joining a fully progressed guild where other people have done all the hard work and demanding equal treatment when it comes to uber-rare loot will appear in a whole different light, when it's you who is on the other end of the demand  turtle_tongue_head
