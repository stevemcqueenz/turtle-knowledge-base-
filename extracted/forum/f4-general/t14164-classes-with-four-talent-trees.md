How they would look like?
Giving my personal take on this. **This is not a suggestion topic, the goal is fun.**

 **Category 1 - The we know that category**

 1. Druid -> The easiest one of the bunch, retail already did that. Separating feral and guardian trees makes it possible to normally flesh out both the cat and the bear.

 **Category 2 - There is kinda a consensus about it category**

 2. Shaman - > Enhancement splitting, shamans getting both a proper melee DPS and a tank tree.
 3. Priest -> I'd take improved inner fire and would create the monk tree for a melee priest. Other option is a smite priest tree but I'd go with a monk. Finally monk can bonk.

 **Category 3 - I see a potential but there is no consensus at all**

 4. Rogue -> Rogue tank. I mean. Some people think rogue tanks should exist. I think that none of the current talent trees should be sacrificed for a rogue tank. But honestly, if they get a whole new tree then let's go, more diversity is usually good in WOW.
 5. Hunter -> Splitting survival into two trees. One of them would be a utility + in-combat trap tree, the other one is a proper melee tree.
 6. Warlock -> Splitting demonology. Currently half of the tree wants your demon alive, the other half wants it to be killed. I'd have a tree designed around demonic sacrifice and one designed around keeping your demon alive.

 **Category 4 -> Things get cheezy category**

 7. Paladin -> Melee-distanced healer. Would sound fun and bad at the same time. At least they were plate and a shield. Contrary to a holy pala, this pala tree would generate a passive aoe healing around himself with the damage he does. Kinda like a shadow priest but instead of party heal it has aoe heal.

 **Category 5 -> The bottom line**

 8. Warrior -> Listen me out, this might not be the best idea of the century, but: ranged warrior having a bunch of talents which generate rage when he shoo... No, this would be too bad. My idea here is a field marshal. A support class, if anything, having specific shouts, buffs, debuffs, maybe even maintaining some form of aura while not taking damage. Some low DPS during that but the class would mostly focus on maximizing team output.

 9. Mage -> Mages can use 3 elements. Mage talent trees are based around each element. That's 3 not 4. Come on. Healer mage from SoD it is, no better idea.
