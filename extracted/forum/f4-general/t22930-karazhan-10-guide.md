# Karazhan 10 Guide

## Post 160087 by Speedz1337 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-11-19T16:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Lower Karazhan Halls is a 10 man raid that resets every 5 days.  The timer for the rests is listed [here](https://turtle-timer.vercel.app/). There is no attunement to enter Karazhan 10.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/WEoKlRQ.png)

 **Karazhan 10 Location**

Karazhan is a 10 man raid located at the tower of Deadwind Pass.  Atiesh holders can easily teleport you there so ask your friendly mage, druid, priest or warlock friend (if they have the legendary) to teleport you to the entrance of this raid.
- Horde may alternatively choose to travel from Stonard (Swamp of Sorrows) which conveniently a lot of horde mage's will have the Stonard portal (which comes from K10).

- Alliance can travel from Duskwood (Darkshire).
![Image](https://i.ibb.co/xKXwBnPR/Duskwood.png)

 **Overview of Bosses**

There are four bosses that need to die in order for you to be able to clear to the last boss: [Moroes](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61225).  You can kill these four bosses in any order.

- [Brood Queen Araxxna](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61221)

- [Grizikil](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61224)

- [Clawlord Howlfang](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61223)

- [Lord Blackwald II](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61222)
There is one additional optional "boss" that can be killed for its loot table.  [Master Blacksmith Rolfen](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61319) is killed for its blacksmithing recipes that he can drop.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/sZMbtVw.png)
 **Boss Mechanics**
 **Spider Area Trash**
- Has a substantial amount of stealth mobs, be careful about just running, as you can very quickly have a lot of mobs on you

- The boss room has 3 or 4 stealth mobs in there.

- Skitterweb Darkfang: Invisible black spider. Stuns its target([Venom Influx](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57059)) and has a stacking movement/attack speed poison (10% per stack)([Darkfang Venom](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57058)). Stun means the tank cannot benefit from avoidance stats (block/parry/dodge) so healing tank through the stun is first priority; dispelling the poison is second priority. Kill priority because they can stun.

- Skitterweb Venomfang: Huge green spider. Two patrol in the room before Araxxna and they can chain nearby packs. Make sure to mark them so you don't accidentally chain packs. High damage poison spit ([Corrosive Bolt](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=44037)) and poison spit volley ([Brood Venom Volley](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57063)). Poison volley does stack up so it needs to be abolished.

- Skitterweb Crawler: Poison ability reduces healing taken by 25%([Leeching Bite](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57056)). Prioritize abolishes on tanks.

- Skitterweb Leaper: Charges the furthest player ([Skitter Leap](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57060)). Low damage and health. Last kill priority.
 **Brood Queen Araxxna**
- Spits poison that druids have to cleanse (preferably, paladins can suffice, but abolish is much better)

- Summons(egg) adds that has to be killed asap.
 **Imp Area Trash**
- Primalist and Channeler are the most dangerous ones.

- The Channeler can hit you with [Grellfire](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57089) which can nuke someone down especially if 2 or 3 of those cast it on you at once.  Remember to interrupt the Grellfire as soon as you see them casting it.  Blessing of protection - immune to all damage. Heal - heals itself or another imp. Banish priority or kill priority.

- Grellkin Shadow Weaver: Shadow bolt([Darkbolt](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57080)). Mana drain (). Second interrupt priority after Holy Fire. It is a magic effect, so the target of the mana drain can be dispelled.

- Grellkin Primalist: Shocks (earth/flame/frost shock). Hurricane - step out of the effect. Can be interrupted. Massive armor buff on a friendly target. Can be purged or dispelled.
 **Grizikil**
Be aware, ALL imps in the corridor and side rooms leading up to the boss have to be cleared, else they will pull when the boss is pulled.
- Has a Geddon Inferno that you want to run out of called [Flamewave](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57091)

- [Grellkin Rain of Fire](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57093) is cast on the raid, likewise, move out of it
 **Worgen Trash**
- Shadowbane Darkcaster: [Darkbolt](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57080): 1k+ damage, uninterruptible.  Sheep priority or kill priority.

- Shadowbane Ambusher: Stealthed mob found in several worgen packs. Basic tank & spank mob after un-stealthed, usually killed last.

- Shadowbane Ragefang: [Claw Flurry](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57081) ability which puts a bleed on all targets hit

- Shadowbane Alpha: Enrages. High health and damage. [Shadowbane Roar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57079) ability debuffs nearby targets for 1000 armor, 451 attack power, and lowers their shadow resistance. Last kill priority. Their HP is so high you'd rather take down the lower HP mobs quickly to reduce damage on tank. Immune to sheep. One patrols in the worgen packs upstairs and down the staircase.
 **Clawlord Howlfang**
- Has a nasty debuff that reduces armor & damage done, can only be removed by LoS'ing the boss thus this is the only boss (along with Moroes) that strictly requires 2 tanks

- Swap is at 15 stacks or so.

- Has a nasty curse([Shadowbane Curse](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57084)) that needs decursing
 **Undead Trash**
- Phantom Guardsman: Immune to disarm. High armor.

- Phantom Servant: Aoe silence ([Phantom Scream](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57062)). Magic effect; tanks should be dispelled so they can taunt. Ranged DPS need to be careful to not overthreat and pull them into the healers. Kill priority over everything else.

- Shattercage Magiskull: Big skeleton mage. Arcane explosion ([Arcane Explosion](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=25679)) with 30 yard range. Ranged and healers can outrange it. Can be stunlocked for safety. One patrols into the worgen packs upstairs. Another patrols behind the stage leading to Moroes P2 (can chain Phantom Servants). Two are found before the theater (Moroes room.) Make sure one gets shackled. Two patrol near this two-pack and can chain when pulling; watch out.
 **Dark Rider Champion**
This needs to be killed before you get to Lord Blackwald II

 **Lord Blackwald II**
- During the fight he summons adds. depending on dps, you usually dont get much more than 2-3 adds, they can be dealt with, with Mage CC, otherwise put a designated offtank on them

- Decurse tanks (Important)
 **Moroes**
- Spread out. More specifically make sure your healers are at the far left and right of the arena so they don't get silenced at the same time because the silence is AoE.

- Boss needs regular taunts.

- Your tanks need to be dispelled so they can taunt

- Dps NEEDS to be lower threat than BOTH tanks.

- Boss silences people at random (small aoe effect)

- Stuns current main tank regularly
 **Quests**
Some of these quests are covered in my [Attunements Guide](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120619).

- A drop from Lord Blackwald II himself. ([Scythe of the Goddess](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41062))

- [Scribbled Cooking Notes](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40998) is the start of a quest chain that gives [Le Fishe Au Chocolat](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=84040) recipe for people that have Cooking at 300

- [The Key to Karazhan I](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40817) can be started from the guy standing near the dining hall with all the worgen.  This quest chain eventually gets to the attunement for Karazhan 40.

- [Suitable Accomodations](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41083) is a quest chain that starts in the Grellkin hallway.  This quest is super important for the Karazhan 40 unlock for more bosses after Chess room.

- [Lost In Time](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41347) is a quest chain that starts on the top floor of the Theater seats.  This is also covered in the attunements guide.  Like Suitable Accomodations this is needed for the Karazhan 40 unlock.

> **Yavamar1 wrote: Thu Nov 20, 2025 4:26 am**
> Adding to this, Kara10 is a pretty short-and-sweet (about an hour long with a casual PUG) 10-man raid that drops gear roughly in the Tier 1 range (iLvl 68 blues and iLvl 66-68 epics, and iLvl 70-72 epics from the final boss). Its layout is very similar to the lower portion of Karazhan from The Burning Crusade, with the final boss (Moroes) located on the opera stage. That said, the locations of some of the bosses will take you into areas you may not have explored in the TBC version of Karazhan.
>
>  Some notable loot (from a Holy Priest's perspective):
>
>  [**The Scythe of Elune**](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61184) - Starts a quest line for a legendary trinket for casters, though you'll have to go all the way to the T3.5 Tower of Karazhan in order to complete it. Very rare drop.
>
>  [**Sliver of Hope**](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61451) - A healing trinket that is basically a middle ground between the [Royal Seal of Eldre'Thalas](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18469) (from Dire Maul) and the [Rejuvenating Gem](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19395) (from BWL). Popular among all healers.
>
>  [**Leggings of the Misty Marsh**](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61256) - Probably the best cloth healing legs you're going to get until BWL.
>
>  [**Marshtreader Slippers**](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61297) - Another cloth healing item you probably won't be replacing before BWL.
>
>  [**Rod of Resuscitation**](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61454) - A good epic healing staff if [Benediction](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18608) continues to elude you, or if you're not a priest. Comparable to [Grandstaff of the Shen'dralar Elder](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=55060) (from Jewelcrafting) and [Staff of Rampant Growth](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=20581) (from world bosses).
>
>  [**Reedwoven Tunic**](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61250) - Similar to [Robes of the Exalted](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13346) (from Stratholme). It's 4 less healing power, but an extra 10 Stamina, 5 Intellect, and 1 Spirit. A good tradeoff IMO.
>
>  There are also a variety of new BoP recipes that are random trash drops. A little something for every crafting profession.
>
>  You can click the links for all the bosses in the OP to get a full list of drops from each of the bosses.
