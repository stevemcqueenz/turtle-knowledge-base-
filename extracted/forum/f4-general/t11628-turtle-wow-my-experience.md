Hello,

I was playing on a privet server a different version of wow than this one, and don't get me wrong it was good but I just love the classic version the true classic version of wow.
so, I started playing Turtle WoW for about one week leveling a rogue undead.
the leveling experience was so good remind me of OG wow days, leveling to get skills, professions (that matter), getting gear upgrades  and really feel it impacting your game play, dungeons and even new dungeons are made the right way here to feel like classic.

That said I am sad to say that I can't play this amazing server anymore. because of toxic behaviour. Yes, there are game masters and yes, they are fast to respond but they don't solve the problem. they /w you a message basically saying it is what it is.

I was farming for a dagger as a rogue would, got into a group of druid, priest, hunter,  elemental shaman and me a rogue.
you would think a dagger is for me, or maybe the druid would need on it, hunter maybe?

No, elemental shaman stole it, and from the boss before it stole a bracer that gives agi from the hunter (said: off spec) off spec at level 40, and dagger said that he melee a lot to save mana.

Now you would say but you stop playing the server because of one player?
Ofc not I would be crazy, but when you talk to a Game Master a bout what is clear a toxic behaviour he should at lest do anything not just tell you (it is what it is I can't help you).

well, this dagger does not matter yes it is a low level item. I can play and level up and get a better one. but if there is no solution for toxic behaviour why even play. what is stopping anyone from ninja looting? nothing it is what it is after all GM will not do anything.

so,  thank you for a good server but good bye.
