# We don't cross any redlines. Until we do.

## Post 64299 by Skumbanana (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-09-15T11:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64299#p64299 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't want to sound self-entitled. I know TWoW is not a service and I'm not a client. Nevertheless, I've been in this server for one year and, as many of my fellow companions, I'm feeling frustrated with this whole debacle. It seems the original spirit of TWoW is being dismantled. Things which the team said were out of question on principle grounds are now becoming a reality, simply because there's a demand for them.

We all remember the times the devs stressed to us that a new realm was out of question, that it would "divide and split the community" and [create unnecessarily trouble:](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41816#p41816)

> **Akalix wrote: Sat Apr 15, 2023 7:29 pm**
> To reiterate what has been said before:
>
>  We do not have plans to launch another server. It would cause more problems than it would solve and overall be a negative to the server.

That line has been crossed and we have new servers.

Many of us remember all the times the devs stressed there wouldn't be a TWoW PvP server. [Again, full quote from Alkalix](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42768#p42768)

> **Akalix wrote: Sun Apr 23, 2023 9:04 pm**
> We are an RPPvE server. We will not change our core identity. If you're looking for a full PvP server, this won't be the place to find it unfortunately.

Now, there's a demand for it and we are going to have a new EU PvP server.

The devs talk like all of this was inevitable. Yes, the influx of new players may have been inevitable, but the choices made to deal with this were ample. It's just bad faith saying otherwise.

The team could have region lock the server (it would at least be an hassle to go through a VPN) and could have ban Hanzi characters when things got out of control. They could have done this while clearly communicating to the Chinese playerbase they were preparing a server for them. Nothing of this would sound discriminatory. It would just be a matter of prioritizing the veteran community while finding a solution for new players without blowing up the main realm.

It also baffles me that, when SEA servers were opened, new accounts were prioritized over transfers. Wasn't the whole idea to stabilize population and remove the queue? Why didn't the team, say, allow a couple of thousand new accounts only, while waiting for transfers to be carried out?

You chose a please-all solution. Ironically, you defeated your own premise of "welcoming everyone", by letting this drift and allowing a disruptive influx of new players, hence rendering the server an unwelcoming place for all.

I commend the effort to communicate with us. But many of your explanations sound patronizing, with lot of concern dismissal and virtue signalling. You tell us you want to "welcome everyone"; you repeat you're working your asses off for the project, which sounds a lot like "we are doing you guys a favour". This is either a passion project or a business. If it's turning into a business, just plain say it. Not only circumstances changed. The team is also changing its priorities, it seems...

Tragically, the truth is that, in a sense, you chose the easy thing to do: you catered to the majority. The hard thing to do is respecting the minority's wishes, more-so in this particular case, where the minority has been on this server for so long. And, please, don't take us for fools by saying that all players are part of the same community, irrespectively from where they come from. If that were so, you wouldn't be opening servers for the Chinese only.

Many of us have actively contributed to the community, with suggestions and bug-reporting. I myself made my (very) modest contributions. Don't you concede that we might feel a bit betrayed and rightly so with all that's happening?

I myself am taking a break for a while. Nonetheless, I truly hope TWoW comes out victorious from all this. It will surely be a different TWoW than the one we used to know. And I truly hope that this doesn't turn out to be another cautionary tale about greed being the end of all good things...

Your quotes from the devs are valid and its understandable to feel betrayed by the change in direction. However its also fair to consider the other side.

When the devs said they would not open a new server they were not dealing with the current issues, nor could they reasonably anticipate the situation they now face. While a new server would have been a negative when the population was lower (cost not outweighing the benefits) that calculation can change as the population rises. At this point the need for new SEA servers is painfully obvious, though the new EU server is more questionable. Turtle WoW has gotten decidedly more popular even among English speaking audience, and it would be even more-so without the terrible queue times that must be discouraging to some. The admins have access to data we don't have or I haven't seen, such as the number of new account registrations. Maybe they can see clear demand drivers in a new server.

Questioning the way the devs have gone about things is also justifiable, but there are probably no perfect solutions here. Its clear to me that they are attempting to prioritize the experience of new players to the SEA realms by slowing the transfer of old characters, perhaps at the detriment of the OG realm. Hopefully this issue is temporary and the eventual opening of full transfers and dropped hanzi support, with enough SEA realms to accommodate their audience, will fix this issue. The implementation of a new PVP server is an interesting decision. As a new player myself I am sympathetic to the concerns of veterans while excited for a new realm. For many people the experience of WoW is progress, which is much more exciting and fun on a fresh realm as compared to one where you can easily join established groups upon reaching 60. In my opinion a fresh server while demand is high is a positive decision, and if demand ever dries up they could simply allow transfers back to the consolidated original realm.

The important thing to keep in mind is that nothing is invalidating your current character. Your progress is not being wiped out and you can continue to enjoy a hopefully less crowded main server. If some people migrate to the new realm its because it is offering them a more fun experience and you should be happy for them. If people become bored of a stale realm they don't stick around anyway, or simply wait to play whenever new content drops and become seasonable players. The people that are truly dedicated to the current realm should stay and enjoy the community they have created.

I hope there is more communication from the devs as things progress. IMHO open communication to the player base is the best way to let people know they are being heard and that decisions are being made for the good of all of the community. The situation evolves and its reasonable for old statements to no longer be valid, but an explanation is owed when this happens.
