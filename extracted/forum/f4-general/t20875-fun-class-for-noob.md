> **Mailman wrote: Wed Aug 13, 2025 3:34 pm**
> whats so bad about druid? I heard only good thing about this class.

On one hand super versatile combining many elements from other classes under the druid flavour, you get basically 4 playstyles in one (keep in mind that the other classes go much deeper into their specific style of play, druid adopts many concepts like the rogue stealth while in cat form, but its much less deep, compared to rogue)

on the other it can be quite overwhelming to be presented with so much choice, since you have to build the character towards either a ranged caster or a physical melee, you can ofc have a mix of pieces equipped during lvling but its just more confusing for beginners imo

Lastly (and that is just my very personal issue with the class) you create a character at the start and get gear which is displayed on the character super cool yes?
Well as a druid you will turn into a bear or whatnot most of your time playing and that transformation will never change how it looks no matter how cool looking/strong your gear is.
