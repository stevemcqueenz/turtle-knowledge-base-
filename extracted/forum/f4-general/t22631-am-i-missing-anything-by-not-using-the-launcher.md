I would prefer a direct install of the game, but I heard the launcher has some additional functionality? Am I missing anything by not using it? Also, will patches be available for manual download/install? Also, I saw something about it being important to manually clear your cache, how do I do this?

## Post 157502 by Edwanji (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-28T11:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157502#p157502 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The launcher will automatically keep your game up to date, but in my experience, I need to manually verify the files for it to work.
The launcher will help install addons and keep them up to date.
The launcher will automatically clear out your cache. To do this manually, you delete the folder "WDB".

That being said, on my system, the launcher doesn't actually work to launch the game for me.  What I do is just open up the launcher once a week to get the latest patch, and otherwise launch the game through Heroic.  I should probably see if I can add a script to Heroic to clear the cache for me.
