## Post 99364 by Justkasper — 2024-07-13T14:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99364#p99364 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

aced the exact same issue today: on the second monitor, the camera was floating while the right or left mouse button was pressed. Out of all the solutions I found, the one from Reddit helped me. Here's the full message:
> I had this issue but fixed it, cant remember how, just know that It's not a mouse issue.
>
>  Try enabling rawmouseinput, and rightclick WoW.exe and do the following;
>
>  Properties > Compatibility > Check "Disable fullscreen optimization"
>
>  Properties > Compatibility > "Change settings for DPI" (Or something like this" > Then check the box furthest down

The last part of this actually worked for me.
