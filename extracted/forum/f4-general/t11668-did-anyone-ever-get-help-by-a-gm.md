Greetings!
First and foremost, it's important to understand that GMs are not responsible for fixing bugs or quests. Their role primarily revolves around moderating gameplay. Bug fixes, on the other hand, are handled by the server developers. It's worth noting that bug resolution doesn't occur immediately after submitting a ticket; it takes some time for the process to be completed. In such cases, GMs are there to assist you by providing information or guiding you towards a solution. This is why the GM directed you to the bug tracker. By submitting a bug report or adding a comment to an existing one, the issue will be addressed and resolved in the next update, which typically occurs every Wednesday. If there was a solution that didn't require visiting the bug tracker, the GM would have provided you with all the necessary information through private messages. I hope this explanation clarifies how bug handling works.
