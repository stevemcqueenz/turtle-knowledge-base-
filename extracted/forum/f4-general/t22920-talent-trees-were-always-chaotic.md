# Talent Trees Were Always Chaotic

## Post 160024 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-11-19T04:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160024#p160024 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Until recently -- I thought there were 'rules' about Vanilla/Classic Talent Tree design.
Rules 'set' by Blizzard Developers.
Apparently these 'rules' were more like 'guidelines' that even the OG Blizz Devs could not (or chose not to) follow.

 **Rule #1:**
'Capstones' AKA 1-point Talents *MUST ONLY* be granted every 10 levels.
AKA rows 1, 3, 5, 7.
This "rule" has been ROCK SOLID in every version of Classic/Vanilla. Even Turtle WoW and TBC/Wrath.
So much so that I THOUGHT it was 'hard coded' into the game. As in -- you literally cannot break the rule.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/JbxErsi.png)

...what is that? WTF IS THAT!?
Yep. For the majority of Vanilla WoWs existence -- Subtlety Rogue had a capstone 'Hemorrhage' on the 6th row.
This was only eventually changed with a total Talent Tree revamp in a later patch.
This has broken my brain and I dont know if this 'rule' even has validity anymore.

 **Rule #2:**
You cannot have anything but a single capstone in the final row.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/01n5db9.png)
Nope. Demo had extra junk shoved in there.
Enhancement Tree in 2004 also had extra junk in the final row.

 **Rule #3:**
You cannot have more than 10 points in the 1st row.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/2Wtw4uC.png)

Vanilla Survival Huntard: 11 points on 1st row. Rule broken   scared_turtle_head

 **Rule #4:**
You can't have only 4/4 points for any talent.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/gXgW34e.png)

You can but it HAS to be glued to a 1 point talent:
Natures Grasp 1/1 --> Improved Grasp 4/4
Vanilla Arcane: Evocation 1/1 --> Arcane Mind 4/4
