# Players are taking at least 10% more physical damage in raids because of various bugs

## Post 75906 by Borefficz (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-11-04T18:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75906#p75906 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I'm making this thread because this is a pretty impactful bug and there have been numerous reports on this or related issues over a long period of time that have not been addressed or even acknowledged in any way.

Screech and Demoralizing Shout are both abilities that reduce enemy Attack Power. They are supposed to stack, but only the strongest effect is applied at a time. This results in a very significant damage increase for any PvE creatures players face. The issue has been reported [here](https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow-bug-tracker/issues/4395), there was another report from around June by another player but I'm unable to find it now.

Video showcase of the Screech/Demoralizing Shout bug (Screech reduces Attack Power by 101, this level 57 Warrior's Demoralizing Shout reduces Attack Power by 144 so it should result in a 245 Attack Power reduction with both debuffs active):

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/5AzvGM3UcDE]

Apparently there is also an issue of a similar nature with Curse of Recklessness completely cancelling out Demoralizing Shout's AP reduction which is way worse than the Screech Bug. Issue has been reported [here](https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow-bug-tracker/issues/4148).

Video showcase of Curse of Recklessness completely neutralizing Demoralizing Shout Attack Power Reduction by another player:

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/nkVbFY7Uxi4]

The player that reported the Curse of Recklessness bug seems to have found an issue with VMaNGOS code that causes these bugs [here](https://github.com/slowtorta/turtlewow-bug-tracker/issues/3994).
