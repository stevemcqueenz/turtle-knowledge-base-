# In PvP against high-latency players, the client doesn't show the correct position of moving targets

## Post 53384 by Borefficz (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-14T07:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53384#p53384 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Since this seems like a poor design choice rather than a bug I decided to make a thread here. Basically the issue is whenever you fight someone with high latency and they are moving, their actual serverside position is quite a bit further to the direction they're moving towards compared to what the client is showing. This results in two issues:
- If you are melee, and chasing the lagger, you won't be able to use skills due to being out of range even though it seems like you are almost on top of the guy
- If you are kiting the lagger, you will be hit with melee attacks while they seem to be 15-20 yards away from you, and things like Hammer of Justice seem to have at least a 25 yard range
It feels like being punished for having a low latency connection.
