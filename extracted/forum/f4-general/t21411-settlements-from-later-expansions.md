# Settlements from later expansions

## Post 148453 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-26T18:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148453#p148453 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In some ways, I'd like to see settlements similar to those seen after WotLK.

Let me explain: while I love everything the Turtle team is doing, I think it's a bit repetitive to see the same style of buildings everywhere, regardless of zone, clan, or race.

The Horde always gets the same basic orcish style seen in Durotar/The Barrens: small, basic, and primitive settlements, barely fortified, while in Warcraft III you even saw large towers, some walls, and little else. However, no settlement has more than 2 or 3 buildings, 4 if we're lucky, while after WotLK we had better walls, more towers, more complicated buildings, large fortresses, and castles.

The truth is I don't think it would be very complicated to modify or change certain models seen after said expansion or create a new one (since they have already started with their own WMO) that is similar to those seen in those expansions, or something similar, in a certain way, there are things that are missing, since WoW in the artistic and structural style speaking at the level of buildings improved a lot after WotLK.
