## Post 165551 by Bezuev — 2026-01-18T19:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165551#p165551 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

As a Wireshark dumps study has shown, the problems are in the unexpected termination of an already established secure connection.

The problems are related to packet loss when connecting from Russia. The delivery confirmation packet from the realm host is lost (in my case, Nordanaar, but I checked Tel'Abim there as well).

If the Realm List data packet was truncated, the message "Retriving character list" will appear after authorization.

If this Realm List packet arrives in full, the character list is displayed, but upon entering the world, my computer sends data segments  to the WoW host, in response to data segments ACK ansvers from the WoW host are lost. From my computer, segments for which ACKs were not acknowledged are repeated 3-4 more times, within 10-11 seconds, after which the connection is lost.

Sometimes the character even manages to enter the world, but nothing there reacts to actions, since the data from the server is no longer received.

I checked with providers Rostelecom and Megafon. Win7, Win10, Win11.

The hosts belong to the OVH provider, which has had many websites blocked by Roskomnadzor. The server IPs themselves are not blocked by Roskomnadzor. The problems may be related to OVH.

VPN solves the problem. But VPN adds about 150 to the ping.
