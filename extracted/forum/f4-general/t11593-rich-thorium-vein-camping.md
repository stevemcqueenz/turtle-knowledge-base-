Please... make a way to be viable to farm Rich Thorium Veins...

Players (or maybe bots, or players with auto clickers) are just camping besides their spawning spots 24/7. Making it almost impossible to farm it. In addition, there is an army of players roaming around all zones craving for a RTV like ants, then when the impossible happens and you get one node free, the arcane crystals does not drop... I started counting, and for 4 hours, I found 13 rich thorium veins notes, and no crystal.

For what I see, making this horrible and boring farming state occur you only can access then by the AH. Then you have two options, just straight farm dungeons or something else (that is more boring) to get gold to by your arcane crystals, or… the more common solution. Just straight way buy gold so they can access what they want.

May case is, making farming as scarse as it is, the server just endorse this selling gold economy.

To solve this, I have two solutions, or make it very common, spread more the veins in the areas where they already have and making more spawn spots, and make possible to get crystals from small veins again (or change the % drop, because i already mine some 30 STV and no crystal) …
