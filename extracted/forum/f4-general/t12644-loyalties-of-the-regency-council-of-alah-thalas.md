## Post 84238 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-10T17:54:00Z

There is something that was not clear enough to me in the story introduced in the last patch:
What does the Regency Council think about adding the Quel'dorei to the Alliance?
I know some will be in favor like:
Vereesa: She was the one who basically brought them together, so it wouldn't make sense for her to be against it now.
 -Lor'themar Theron: Basically he sends you to Auberdine when you finish the respective quest chain, so I guess he will be in favor of the Alliance.
 -Halduron Brightwing: We see him fighting in The Last Runestone alongside the volunteers of Stormwind, Darnassus and Ironforge, therefore I think he will greatly value the help of the Alliance, we have not yet received missions from him from what I could see, so It's just supposition.
And against it I know that he is the only one:
Astalor Sunsworn: Because of his ambition to want the crown, he explicitly says that he does not want to become a puppet state of Stormwind, therefore he is against the Alliance.

But the others are still missing, are they for or against the Alliance?
