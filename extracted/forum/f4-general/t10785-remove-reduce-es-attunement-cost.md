# Remove/Reduce ES attunement cost

## Post 70317 by Thecakeisalie (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-12T09:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70317#p70317 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I want to start off and say that the patch has been great and the new zones are awesome.

However, the new Emerald Sanctum (ES) raid has been a serious let down, as I've been unable to fulfill the gold requirement, and thus been denied entry.

The Into the Dream quest part III is asking players to provide an arcanite rod worth 150g, which feels like a very steep price compared to other raid attunements.

Neither MC, Onyxia or BWL have any monetary requirements to enter, some are just one simple quest or an epic questline (Onyxia). Naxx does have a attunement cost, but the cost can be reduced depending on your reputation level with the Argent Dawn, but since it is the final/last raid I'm not as opposed to it.

However, the introduction of a pay-wall to ES, a T2 raid according to the Turtle team, to gatekeep players has been a very disappointing decision.

The Turtle Team have said that they do not plan to introduce raid scaling to ES, which means that raids will have to somehow find 40 attuned people, which is easier said then done. This artificial gold sink is clearly having the opposite effect and making it even harder to form proper raids, aside from the largest guilds on the server.

You even had to implement a band aid solution and spawn a vendor with unlimited arcanite rods just to prevent the price from heading to the moon due to the surge in demand. Now you just walk over to the vendor and buy the item, losing your precious gold and leaving you with a bad taste in your mouth, very epic and exiting.

I'm also an altoholic with multiple characters, the questline is already quite a time investment and the additional 150g cost for each char is very discouraging.

I assume this was not the Turtle Team's intention and hope they can find a satisfactory solution, so that the playerbase that has been left out can also enjoy this content. I just don't want this to become a precedent for future raids to include very steep attunement costs and if you are concerned about gold inflation, I'm sure there are better ways to handle it.

If there is a change, I hope you can refund the people who bought the stupid rod their precious gold!

You were on the verge of greatness, you were this close..!
