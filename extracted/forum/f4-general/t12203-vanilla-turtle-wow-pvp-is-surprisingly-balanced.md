# Vanilla/Turtle WoW PVP is (Surprisingly) Balanced!

## Post 81147 by Manletow (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-12-14T06:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81147#p81147 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Don't get me wrong, theres all sorts of absurd/broken/unfair nonsense. Just a few examples:

- Sap/Polymorph can last a full 40 seconds (and can be immediately reapplied for ANOTHER 20 seconds of CC).

- Engineering is mandatory as it offers countless extremely useful PVP trinkets/bombs.

- Horde Racials (notably Orc/Undead) are overpowered in PVP.

- Immunity Shields (Divine Shield / Ice Block) have no counter-play options.

- AV is infamously unbalanced map-design wise.

- Ironically, dedicated PvE players have a huge advantage over dedicated PvP players due to 'end game' PvE gear vastly outclassing PvP gear.

- Warriors are painfully weak due to too many reasons to quickly list.

- Excessively-buffed T-WoW Paladins. (Who I should mention *especially* hardcounter Warriors via Holy Strikes/Shocks)
These are the things I can *immediately* think of that are "severe" issues that should be addressed.

Additionally, here are 4 extremely powerful Classes/Specs that *could* use some modest nerfs:

- 1) Holy/Ret Paladin: Obviously broken -- mostly due to Holy Strike/Shock.
 2) Frost Mage: Insane survivability whilst still having incredible CC, AOE, burst damage, AND mobility.
 3) Shadow Priest: They were already excellent in PVP and then T-WoW buffed them with a powerful finisher "Pain Spike".
 4) Elemental Shaman: Hit like a truck whilst being extremely 'tanky' and hard (at least for melee classes) to kill. Additionally; they offer pretty great heals/utility/support/disruption.
THAT ALL SAID -- generally speaking i'm very much impressed with Blizzard's work!   satisfied_turtle

The vast majority of Classes/Specs are at least somewhat viable in PVP.
(Even 'Bottom Tier' specs like Enhancement Shaman / Feral Druid still being quite fun to play)

The vast majority of Spells/Attacks/Abilities have some reasonable methods of counter-play.
There is actually quite of a strategy involved in battles.
I appreciate that a PVPer can utilize their game-knowledge and skill to improve their odds of victory.
Wise premeditation can sometimes turn a 'normally lost' battle into a victory.

(Like, for example, if you know you will be fighting a Warlock you can equip your 'Shadow Reflector' trinket but still need to time its use properly PLUS it gives an obvious warning animation/visual so a smart enemy can play around it. So its not simply an 'I Win" button.)

Though admittedly it did take Blizz many patches/changes/improvements from 2004-2006 to achieve this level of balance/fun.

What classes does warrior counter? Any caster will beat a warrior of equal skill and gear, as will a paladins, rogue, hunter or a feral druid. That leaves healing specs and maaaybe enhancement shamans as "good" matchups for a warrior. If warrior wants a chance to win in any one-on-one matchup, they have to use both of their 30 minute cooldowns and have the advantage of initiating the fight.

Vanilla WoW PvP was not very well balanced, what little balance existed was crafted with group fighting in mind, not one-on-one fights. This rock-paper-scissors class counter system people talk about is an illusion that you see if you only have played a handful of classes that generally are advantaged in individual engagements. Warriors' biggest boon is Mortal Strike as healing reduction is a rare spell effect and is an answer to how strong healing is in PvP. Casters are objectively individually stronger than any melee spec when it comes to PvP - Turtle WoW's iteration of Paladins admittedly kind of blurs this line, however as they have the same or higher potential for magic damage burst that is a huge part of why casters are strong while retaining all the utility and survivability they previously had in their kit.

If Turtle WoW's goal is to improve one-on-one or small group PvP balance then warriors absolutely deserve a buff in PvP.
