Hi all, I was wondering if any of you have successfully or unsuccessfully tried any ofther sorts of self imposed restrictions outside of the in game modes like Hardcore, Vagrant etc?

I can remember when there was the likes of Gutrot levelling naked and Noor the pacifist levelling without killing anything, (the fact I can remeber their names says something!) I always thought it was interesting people trying to play/level doing something different and was curious what others have come up with. Turtle seems to be more likely to spawn these types of ideas than many other servers I reckon.

A couple I've been toying with lately is a no pet melee only hunter (a lot easier here than it was on the official servers with some of the changes) and a healer/bear only druid without ever using offensive spells (this one can at least be of use in groups was the thinking, as other types aren't really able to join dungeons as they don't pull their weight).
