Heya! Don't listen to the person above me. Tel'abim is "fresh", yes (a year old at this point) but people are progressing and focused more on T3 content, and since the population is smaller there's less people doing early level stuff like levelling and endgame dungeons/early raids. Also, a PvP server. Literally what you *didn't* ask for. Nordanaar (The PvE server) has people constantly running pugs and doing old content as many people have geared their mains, and levelled new characters after the recent patch or in preparation for it. Levelling is also much more lively, but I wouldn't say overcrowded. It can be at times though. Finding groups will be MUCH easier while you're starting off too, since everyone is still levelling alts and new players are still coming around. Also, Nord is the RP-PvE server. You usually won't just run into RPers, but there's plenty of guilds that do that, though a bit less of them on horde side. Hope this helps and you enjoy your stay.
