When I last leveled (after Ambershire opened), I played as a shammie with a brainwash to both tank and heal.

Based on my experience as a tank:
Priests have given me the biggest sense of safety. They have been able to outheal things the others couldn't.
HoT, shield, AoE heal, you want it, they got it.
But to be fair, as a shaman I liked being shielded. For bears or warriors, that tool is much worse.
New champion capstone also felt cool.

On the flipside, while they have some important buffs, I didn't feel them as impactful as pala or shammie (for non-shammie tanks).
They can't really affect the offensive end of the things via their utility, the most they can do is some pain and wanding.
It is fair, they are healers, but some others can highly affect the team's DPS output.
It felt like a defensive safety pick.

Palas were weird. Only met with very good ones and very bad ones. Sometimes the heal output felt lacking but the good ones made me literally impossible to die so I'm not sure what was going on there.
Their utility package is huge and can be highly personalized (aura + a really powerful blessing whatever your role is). As a shammie I loved them for judgment of wisdom but shammie is the only tank which can realistically run into mana problems so that's a unique thing.
All in all I felt less safe with them (except for 1-2 whom I added as friends) but their utility made them my favourite pick.

Shammies are a bit like palas. I have a lot of experience at dungeon healing so with downgrades etc I rarely went oom but noticed it as a trend that usually resto shammie mana bar went out rather quickly. Unlike at pallies where I sometimes felt the output lacking, at the shammie I felt something like how many more heals do we have before oom?

As a shammie tank, a resto's utility package is not so crucial. Can ask him to put WF totem for the others.
But for any other tank, a WF by alone is a huge utility upgrade. Add tremor, resistance totems, cleanse totems and some more buffs like str to the mix and you get a utility powerhouse.
Compared to the pala:
I think pala's one is more defensive and caster friendly (dev aura, bow) while shaman utility wins at the melee offensive end.
But for a shammie tank, a shammie healer is the most meh option I think.

Dru... As a tank with ankh and rezz, their lack of ooc rez wasn't a problem but otherwise it can be.
They felt like a worse priest mostly with one good but very generic buff. Jack of all trades, master of none.
Decurse as a healer is unique and sometimes good.
Innervate is good again but better not be turned into a tank utility... :D
For me, they were the 'almost priests'.
