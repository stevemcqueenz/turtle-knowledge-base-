# Stat stick trinkets were a mistake.

## Post 89584 by Healingrain — 2024-03-07T01:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89584#p89584 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I don't know why the TWoW devs have been pumping out these things nonstop. Vanilla is chock full of weird and wonderful trinkets and creative itemization, but nvm Minds Key bis just keep that equipped forever.

The BWL bullshit was bad enough, but at least you had 1 slot free for a specific active. Half the classes in the game don't even have a throughout cooldown, not a SINGLE button to push for key encounters or phases to boost dps or healing or threat.

Trinket and cooldown management is fun, skill expressive and based. At the very least you should design trinkets more like Naxx with a more moderate passive component and an active component.
