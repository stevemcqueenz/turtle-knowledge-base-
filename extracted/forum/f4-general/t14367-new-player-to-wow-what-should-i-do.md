Hey!

First things first: what can you do in WOW?

 1. Doing quests. They give items, money and XP. You need XP to level up. Altogether there are 60 levels and an endgame landscape made for level 60 players. Leveling itself is slow, kinda plan months for your character to reach 60. But the journey is fine.
For quests I advise downloading these 2 addons:
<https://shagu.org/pfQuest/>
<https://shagu.org/pfQuest-turtle/>

They help a lot f.ex. showing in the minimap what to go after, where to go etc.

 2. Exploring the world. The world is big and until lvl 40 (or earlier, depending on class) you are VERY slow. Can give very good immersion but really, accept the fact that taking to places will feel like forever initially. You also have an option to take glyphs (many at level 1 only). These are challenges for rewards. I def DON'T advise hardcore or vagrant challenge for a 1st experience. Slow and steady is fine if you are the really patient type and warmode is there to give you more XP and reputation. Sounds good but if you take warmode, the opposing faction players can kill you. Personally I don't propose it unless you want to level up asap. Without experience you will have no idea how to PVP and you'll just meet some ássholes who'll keep killing you for fun. So world is big. Most towns have a flight point, usually a guy with a gryphon or a flying manticore (?) with a big green ? on their head. Talk with them, flight point gets saved, you can travel faster later.

 3. Going to dungeons
Dungeons are 5men instances with bosses and loot. You work together as a team. Most dungeons have their own quests. Repeating dungeons is a valid way of leveling up but kinda slow. Maybe the most fun part of the game. Sometimes people take 30 min to get to the place though.

 4. PVP
Please ignore it for first playthrough or just try it out as you start to know your class. Honestly, wouldn't be a good experience without knowing stuff.

 5. Raiding
Raids are big dungeons with a lot of people designed for level 60 characters. Raids are usually done by guilds which are in-game crews (usually having a guild Discord). Imagine them as big dungeons which many people do every week.

When deciding what to play you have to consider what role you wanna play.
Tank - Gets hit, makes sure others dont get hit. Using threat to have mobs focused on you.
Melee - You go to the mob, you punch the mob until it dies.
Caster - Same but from far away, usually with magic.
Healer - You make sure that noone dies.

Personally I really don't advise tanking at a first char unless you want a real challenge. Not only it is the hardest role, most people here are nerds with a lot of game experience. Some start to get impatient or questions why you don't know the optimal route in a dungeon. Most people are chill though. But tanking only if you want big challenge.
Advantage of tanks is that there are few of them and you can find dungeon partys very fast.

Melee, caster
You mostly focus on killing mobs. The chillest role. Some melee or caster (they are commonly called DPS) are hard to play but people are usually forgiving with DPS. Make sure you don't make more threat than the tank and just do damage. For threat-management, ask guys in-game, that's a long topic.
DPS is good at quests because they kill mobs fast but it is hard to find dungeons with them since most people play DPS.

Healer
It's an easy role I think (I usually play healers) but can be stressful since it's your duty to keep others alive. Usually really chill, fast dungeon places and people like you but you have to be okay with some idiots asking why did they die etc. They have low damage, not good at doing quests alone because they kill things slow.

I won't go into races. Play whatever you like. Racials exist, but still, it should be a decision made by the heart.

About classes. What they can do:

Warrior: tank, melee
Warlock: caster
Mage: caster
Rogue: melee
Shaman: healer, melee, caster
Paladin: healer, melee, tank
Druid: basically anything
Hunter: caster, (they kinda have melee but they actually don't have melee)
Priest: healer, caster

Some input to help decide:
Play warrior if you want to have a character in big armor, with big weapons, and you want a challenge as a tank or you want big damage (in the real lategame). Accept that many people will ask you to tank, just decline them if you're not ok with that.
Play rogue if you later want to PVP and if you want a class with simple but effective PVE (against the game, not players) experience. At the very lategame they fall short, though.
Play a hunter if you like pokémon and catching a pet, keeping it happy etc is fun for you. You have to buy ammo all the time though, be okay with that.
Play pala, shaman or druid if you really wanna try out different options and roles. Druids shapeshift into different animals, palas are holy crusaders, blessing and judging others, shamans are weirdos with totems and great utility.
Play mage or warlock for an easy but effective leveling experience (PVP is harder but both of these are good at it). Mage can enemies into sheep, has very interesting spells. Warlock has demons and can fear anyone. Also uses curses. But he has to collect souls which can be annoying.
Play priest if you really wanna go into healing and want the most options for healing. Heal for this, heal for that. If you get bored, you can turn into a weird warlock-like guy, still.

Note that class changes come soon, what I say now can be wrong in 1 month.

One last thing:
WOW can be addictive. Sometimes I regret starting it last year. It is great fun but it also takes away an ungodly amount of time, sometimes even in expense of my real friends. Play this game if you have enough self-control to handle it only a game, bein g able to uninstall it anytime you wanna.

 * You cant chat until lvl 5, don't be surprised youre muted initially.
