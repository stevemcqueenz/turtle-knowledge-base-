## Post 45346 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-05-10T14:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45346#p45346 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tanks and healers in general while levelling seem to be rarer than dps (No surprise there). And they're almost always in demand. If you want to spam dungeons, druid, feral in particular is your best bet. They heal sufficiently when it's required, can excel at tanking, and do decent single target dps depending on the level range and your gear. I'm still levelling myself, but here's my two cents on endgame anyways. If your focus is endgame, druids are definitely good at most of what they do. Not to say that they outperform most classes, but they always get the job done. You're likely going to want to roll tank or heals as personally I believe that druids perform those roles better than the other ones they can do, and as a feral tank you can always dps as well, since most feral talents are beneficial to both cat and bear. Now, if you wish to focus on a singular spec or role, that's a different story, but druid is still an awesome choice regardless.
