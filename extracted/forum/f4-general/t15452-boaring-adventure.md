I have analyzed this challenge when it was introduced.
> **Xudo wrote: Mon Apr 08, 2024 12:53 pm**
> Just checked leveling path.
>  <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=boar#npcs:0+2+1>
>  <https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Formulas:Mob_XP>
>  up to 24 lvl it is fine. You just go Durotar then Barrens or Dun Morogh then Loch Modan.
>  Highest boar level in The Barrens is 16 and in Loch Modan is 17.
>  They will be gray at 23 and 24 player level respectively.
>  You can start killing [Tamed Battleboar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=4535) in RFK. His highest level is 26. It will be grey at 34 player level.
>  Then you go killing [Withered Battle Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=7333) with 34 lvl until player hit lvl 43.
>  Then you settle down in Blasted Lands and kill [Ashmane Boar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=5992) with level 48-49 up to player level 60.
>  You also have an option to kill [Helboar](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=5993) of level 52-53.

As of 26 june I have announcement when twow devs have added boars in Arathi Highlands (Livingston farm). They have 33-34 level.
