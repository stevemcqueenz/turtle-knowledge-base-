# Dolokhov Steelblood's Journal - 1 "The Journey Here"

## Post 3210 by Thiccroy — 2019-06-25T12:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3210#p3210 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Twenty-fifth of the Sixth Month, 30 ADP.**

Long have I not written in any parchment that may touch my hands, but the sudden freedom and chance to record what has happened to me this past month is undeniable, to say the least.
Suddenly, my life seemed to turn for the better, an upward spiral of sorts.

It has been one month since the day I set out from Stormwind. My mentor, Grish, a Warrior-Cleric and veteran of the Great Wars, had taught me what I knew. I had only a flimsy shield, sword and basic cloth and leather wear to accompany me and my ninety copper.

Soon enough, I was in Goldshire, helping the Marshal with scouting out Kobold threats. Kobolds, nasty creatures. if you're not careful in dispatching them, I've gathered, they screech and screech and the echo of their endless mines, caves, and tunnels brings forth more of these infestive rat creatures. They are easy to kill, but hard to manage. Through these adventures, I gained more coin, and one night whilst using my coin to gain drinks from Lion's pride I met a boisterous and carousing Dwarven Warrior called Gallegund.

Gallegund became my friend and teacher for a long while. Together, we killed Defias Bandits within the forests of Elwynn and scoured their camps for stacks of loot and sums of money. Soon enough, I was richer than I had ever been these past ten years. It was an adventurous time, and soon enough, I had to leave the confines of the Forest.

The Marshal had given me a mission to go to the Captain Stoutmantle of Westfall, the leader of an inspiring group of warriors and rangers calling themselves the People's Militia. I could not deny my urges to help these people. They seemed to be most akin to my own blood, as their lands were not the most important, but the families and names around them. That was the point of the militia, and the farmers and small folk out for themselves amidst marauding outlaws and vagabonds.

The Defias proved to be too much to handle alone, and I had, for the first time, met a trainee paladin, and an adventuring Kaldorei Druid. The Paladin was Ulf, skilled in two-handed weaponry. He had taught me much of the long blade, and I began to use a sword that I had looted from a Defias camp. The druid was Myrt, an interesting man for an elf. I had long heard the Kaldorei had been a serene and patient people. But this druid, within his 'Bear Form' was a furious and hot-headed force to be reckoned with. he was young, I garnered, and as much as a trainee in his arts as Ulf.

All three of us met with Ghosts by a Lighthouse (Don't ask me), and killed vermin ranging from gnolls, to murlocs, to kobolds and various creatures of the vast plains of Westfall, for a kind lady of a farmhouse to make stew for us. I must say, despite the contents, it was like a gourmet dish.

Soon enough, our paths divided, as I traveled far to the north. For the first time, I was in a land as high as the clouds and as cold as Alterac, from my brother's stories. Khaz Modan was a vast collection of land, and Loch Modan proved to be beautiful and dangerous. Troggs proved to be much like Kobolds, though larger and more dangerous. Troggs and Ogres I fought and gained more loot and reputation among the Mountaineers and denizens of the Loch. It was a beautiful place, and the Dam was truly a sight to see. Such wonders, these stoic, small men and women could build. A dam almost as old as the ancient city and homeland of Stromgarde.

Riding the Tram back down south, I further mastered my arts and learned how to aim, load and fire a crossbow. using my new skills, I traveled to Redridge, and there, once more, met my friend Gallegund. With Gallegund, we wreaked havoc on Gnolls, both Yowlers, and Shadowmanes, and for the first time ever, I fought against orcs. Orc. The sworn enemies of both Humanity and the Alliance. These were blacker of skin as if coming through the deep mists of cloud and smoke when born. Soon enough, we had helped the people of Redridge quite a lot.

Together, we improved our skills in Blacksmithing and Mining, and I had improved greatly in First Aid. My arts, skills, money, and armor had improved immensely over the past month, and I felt proud. I did not feel meek anymore...

My journey was headed North, one day. Somehow.

I will come back to you, sweet Strom.
I shall avenge you.
