**Blade of Armistice**

 **Clang! Clang! Clang!**

The familiar sound of the blacksmiths anvil echoed throughout the camp, the keen shriek of metal hammering metal. Seemingly satisfied with her latest addition to the horses shoe collection Elyias stepped back, stooping a ladle in a nearby barrel of water. Drinking deep as she looked around her workspace, her eyes catching the old leather bound tome sitting at her table before she found the forge as its coals seemed to begin cooling.

With a roar, fire spurted free from a layering of fresh coals after each waft of air from the bellows. Satisfied the heat would keep, Elyias swathed a rag over her brow, breathing deeply in the cold night air.

Again her eyes met her tome, as if it was calling to her, begging her for the attention it desired.

Enough was enough she thought as she stormed towards the old book, lifting it with ease as she turned on heel and paced her way to the high table of the camp. Slamming the tome down was a bellow of dust as papers flew into the air. Met only by the eventual creak of the wooden table.

 **“I have something to show you.”** Elyias gleamed, clearly about to enjoy herself as she looked at Lornna’s exemplified shock as her organised chaos was completely shattered by the Elf.

Grinning as she pulls the leather binding from it and sending the pages flying, her hand flying down onto the now open schematic the pages seem to have flown to on their own. With a slight push of the book in Lornna’s direction she spoke again.

 **"There !"** The elf proclaimed, thrusting her hands over the parchment, an extended index tracing over her design. Elyias would trace over an intricate schematic, a blade of exceptional design and beauty, notches seemingly cut out of one side of the blade while the other came to a shallow edge and both met at a distinct point.

 **“I need volunteers. This could be the first of many, but I need the material Lornna! I can’t do this on my own and you have the numbers to help me.”**

 **"This is what I need to bring it together."** Her finger following the design to a list of rather unusual ingredients.

The Elf would turn on heel, tearing a piece of parchment from her journal as she did. After a few steps over to Merrygold's wagon she plunges the dagger through the parchment and into the painted wood of the cart.

"If any of you out there are interested in a little adventure, this is a list of what I need!" Her tone would forever emphasise how unimpressed she seemed with the lack of mobility around camp, turning around back to her forge as she spoke again.

"You know where to find me if you want to volunteer! Think about it- I can't guarantee your safety, but Lornna can at least guarantee the pay!"

 **For those of the Wayfarers that would like a series of side-quests to both improve the functions around camp and if you're lucky improve the very weapons you wield, give us some things to do during downtime and to promote a little more hustle and bustle around camp! So! Either seek me out IC on Elyias or get in touch on the Discord and sign yourself up for what will be one in a long line of mini-quests to come!**
