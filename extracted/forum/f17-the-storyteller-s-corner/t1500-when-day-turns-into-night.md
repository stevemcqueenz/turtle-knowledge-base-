# When day turns into night

## Post 8934 by Coryis — 2021-01-06T22:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8934#p8934 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A solar eclipse can also occur in the lands of Azeroth. Different peoples developed different legends as to why this event can happen.

Night Elves
Even for a night elf for whom a year is nothing, that one event occurs once in his life if he is lucky. Only a few can report on more than one. All the more legends entwine around these rare days on which Elune demonstrates her power and turns day into night.

The rebirth of the white stag

It's been a long time since Malorne the Protector gave his life for his son. It has been a long time since his mighty steps shook the ground. But listen carefully to the night elf, because Malorne can show you the way to happiness. When Elune makes the day her own, her lover will be resurrected by her dazzling beauty. For the few minutes in which they share the day, he will walk through the forest as before and spread his wisdom. Look for him on the old paths, for whoever finds him will be granted wisdom. But don't dare to kill a deer during this time, because then it will be angry and level you and yours to the ground. When the day night has reached its climax, a wreath of lights of infinite beauty will appear around Elune.

Origin:
Night elves
Who: Kaldorei

Tauren
There is also a legend about the noble tauren.

When Apa'ro reappears

Hear tauren hunters, for the elders have something to say. The time will come when Mu'sha will see her beloved brother An'she again. The Earthmother will happily avert her gaze from this world to make the happiness of both of them perfect. At the height of their love, they will be surrounded by the eternal wreath of flames. But Apa'ro, who has stolen Mu'sha's heart once before, will reappear. So sharpen your arrows, mighty hunters. Because again the Tauren Apa'ro will hunt the mighty white stag so that his jealousy does not disturb the love of the two. But remember, the time to kill the legendary stag is short. Because soon Mu'sha and Apa'ro will be torn apart again and Apa'ro will also return to calm.

Origin: Tauren
Who: The Eldest

High Elves
The high elves found a completely different meaning in this event. In the meantime, the legend is only known to a few, as the destruction of the sunwell changed too much.

The victory over the night

The day will come when the weak goddess of the night elves will rise up against the sun. With all its power, which can never be equal to the sun, it will try to rob us of the day. But because of her stupidity, she herself will be embraced by the sun. And all beings in this world will have to recognize that we, the high elves, have recognized the true deity. And then the sun will fend off the attack of the moon goddess and send her back into her eternal darkness, since she, like all who follow her, is not worth
standing in the golden light of glory. But we who revere the sun will be given new fame and power. When our people should never suffer again.

Origin: Silbermond
Who: High Elves
