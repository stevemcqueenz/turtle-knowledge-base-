# Turtle Faire

## Post 122270 by Tinkershells — 2025-03-11T21:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122270#p122270 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/yYjLt8gf/turtlefaire.png)
 **The last Saturday of each month!**
 **10 AM CT | 8 AM PDT | 11 AM ET | 3 PM UTC**
ㅤ
Turtle Faire is a monthly, guild-hosted RP community event open to role-players of all levels and both factions! We celebrate creativity, camaraderie, and immersive storytelling through Performances, Stalls, Games & Contests, Storytelling, Food & Drink, and more. Every month, we journey to a new location, so be sure to check in to see where the Faire will pop up next!

ㅤ
---
ㅤ
 **♦ FAIRE ACTIVITIES ♦**

 **Performances**
Enjoy music, poetry, and other performances by talented players.

 **Stalls**
Explore player run stalls showcasing a variety of crafts, goods, services. Even advertise your rp guild!

 **Games and Contests**
Participate in fun games and contests with exciting prizes.

 **Storytelling**
Share your tales or listen to stories from fellow adventurers.

 **Food and Drink**
Sample delicious in-game treats from various food vendors.

 **And More!**
If you have a unique idea or concept for the Faire, reach out so we can work together to make it happen.
ㅤ

 **♦ MEET THE CREW ♦**

 **The Tinkershells, Turtle Faire Masters**
Turtlyn and Turtimer Tinkershell are the visionary couple behind Turtle Faire, planning and orchestrating every wondrous detail. They’re an eclectic magical gnome duo with green, turtle-like hues, often seen riding around on their turtles keeping things running smoothly. Their whimsical presence brings a dash of fun and excitement wherever they roam.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/j2r80W41/Screenshot2-287.png)

 **The Faire Loa, Jallah’gah**
Jallah’gah, the Loa of Games and Trials, blends both playful and ominous energies for the Faire. He appears as a tall forest troll in a mysterious, ever-shifting mask, sometimes taking flight in the form of an owl. As the RPGM Fairegrounds constructor, he ensures each venue is filled with wonder.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/VNr4J97T/image5.png)

 **Roodrid Whispersong, Head of Recruitment**
Roodrid is a lovely elf bard and the Faire’s only *live* performer. Recording the Faires so the magic can last forever. Formerly a maiden of the Nightingale Faire, she now helps Turtle Faire flourish by bringing in new faces and enchanting spectators with her voice.
<https://www.twitch.tv/roodrid/>
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/JzTdDzRy/Screenshot-2024-04-17-141940.png)

 **The <Nightsong Sentinels>, Security**
This generous Kaldorei guild provides their finest sentinels to ensure safety and order at the Faire. Their watchful presence allows patrons to enjoy every performance and stall undisturbed. Their dedication to peace and harmony sets the perfect backdrop for all Faire festivities.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/9QfLhHyc/image3.png)

 **Splatricia, Head of Hospitality**
Splatricia is a friendly, mysterious ooze who somehow speaks Common! Always accompanied by her little oozling, Montegoo, she guides Fairegoers around with a bubbly attitude. Her warm welcome ensures everyone has a wonderful experience at the Turtle Faire.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/8C9K4XJN/Screenshot-272.png)

 **Kendrik Zapblast and “Blast’n’Pass”**
Kendrik Zapblast is a goblin engineer with decades of experience in all things explosive. Visit his Blast’n’Pass stall for colorful fireworks, landmines, and more! When he’s not thrilling crowds with pyrotechnics, he’s helping the Tinkershells dazzle the stage with special effects and calling up performances.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/59w753xW/2.png)

 **"The Harvesters" Saerah and Christofor Masseigh**
A wholesome human couple from Westfall who lost their farm to the Defias, but not their spirit. Bringing their love of music to the Faire by performing duet Westfall-folk songs that share their story. Accompanied by their adopted dancing murloc tadpoles, Gurky and Murky, their uplifting songs remind everyone that hope can always shine.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/j5f8F6PN/Screenshot1-258.png)

 **Iridianny and Castaras and "Morpho Moonwell Morsels"**
A mystical elf couple run Morpho Moonwell Morsels, serving up traditional Kaldorei dishes like Strider Stew and Darnassian Bleu. Their passion for sharing Kaldorei culture and flavors welcomes adventurers of all backgrounds. Every bite is hand crafted crafted with care and guidance of Elune.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/jjL3zYjM/Screenshot-283.png)

 **Lady Luciana Cousland, Woodworking Stall**
Lady Luciana Cousland offers handcrafted wooden sculptures, toys, and other artistic creations. All profits go to orphanages across Azeroth. Her dedication to helping others embodies the spirit of the Turtle Faire.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/brkQx84f/imagel.png)

 **Pechuer Mudfinger and “Muddy’s Marvelous Mixes”**
Originally a fishmonger, selling fishy treats, Pechuer shifted to brewing up “fun” in all its forms at Turtle Faire. From fadeleaf brews to Thalassian smoke pipes, there is something for everyone to enjoy. Expect a lively blend of mischief and flavor with every puff or sip.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/ht3p4Z0T/image4.png)

 **Coco, Haunting Songstress**
Coco takes the stage with haunting melodies that weave sorrow and hope into a single, resonant tapestry. Her flowing pink hair and unwavering presence capture hearts, as she sings with an emotion both deeply personal and universally felt. She doesn’t beg for attention—she simply sings, and by doing so, commands it.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/Kvx90Mzk/image.png)

 **Emmoth, Gambling Stall**
Emmoth invites you to test your luck at his gambling stall. Though he normally loathes risks, losing coin is not so terrible as losing one's life. Tricksters beware, the warlock won't take kindly to being swindled. His stall is often accompanied by Fflur, who'll either also gamble or stand guard.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/SNhPHPZK/imageE.png)

 **Rakuul's Exotic Goods**
Rakuul roams every corner of Azeroth in search of the unusual and the extraordinary, packing his Kodo with goods no ordinary vendor could offer. From master-forged blades to forgotten cookbooks and glittering, his stock changes with every adventure. Swing by his booth to haggle, swap tales, and walk away with a treasure that’s truly one-of-a-kind.
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/B6k7M2Gg/Screenshot-from-2025-05-31-11-30-36.png)
