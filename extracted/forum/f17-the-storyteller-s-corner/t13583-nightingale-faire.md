**Come one come all to the Nightingale Faire!**

Previously Roodrid Whispersong has been running the Moon and Stars Tavern, and of late she has been called home to Ashenvale. Recently she hosted a Market in Auberdine which was a huge success, and so her next move is to upgrade the Moon and Stars Tavern into the Nightingale Faire.

However, this is a much larger undertaking than a singular tavern keeper roaming the world. Roodrid is looking for performers and artisans, fortune tellers and bruisers... and well, whatever you can think of!

OOC-

I have set up a discord to coordinate stallholders and performers for what will essentially be a Darkmoon Faire but run by players.

<https://discord.gg/jK37FUNAkB>

Please join the discord if you can and would like to participate.

**-The Nightingale Faire-**

Once again the Nightingale Faire will be held, this time in Westfall, the home of our beloved singers The Harvesters.

Welcome to an event of fun and delight
Where you explore and do as you might
Have your fortune told
Else see what is sold
Upon the market this merry night!

Perhaps a game of skill and chance
and your wealth well to advance
or spurn the dice
and ensuing vice
and join us instead in song and dance!

If it is divine refreshment you seek
The Kaldorei cuisine your wishes shall meet
Each heavenly dish
prepared as you wish
In a place to rest and others to greet!

So come one and all to the Nightingale Faire
for there is much to see and do while there
Horde or Alliance
none shall divide us
for this is a day of good cheer, not Warfare!

This Faire will feature an open stage event where all among you can come and show us your talent, be it a song, dance, comedy routine or a story, prepare something nice for us all to enjoy!
