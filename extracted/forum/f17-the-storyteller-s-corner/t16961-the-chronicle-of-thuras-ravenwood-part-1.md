# The Chronicle of Thuras Ravenwood - Part 1

## Post 115729 by Thuras — 2025-01-07T12:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115729#p115729 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings traveler and welcome to my memoirs,

A chronicle of a man trying to find his purpose and destiny within Azeroth.
My name is ***Thuras Ravenwood*** , son of ***Valentine Ravenwood*** and ***Narcissa Ravenwood***, Gilnean nobles originally from Ravenshire. I was born in the year 598 by the Kings Calendar, a little after the Second War begun, in the capital city of Gilneas.

During my childhood, my parents were mostly absent, either being busy with the war or forging and disbanding alliances or either being sent on envoys, I was mostly myself and the butler ***Gregas***. While ***Gregas*** appeared like a normal frail old gilnean, he was much more than that, he was an ex combatant in the gilnean army, with extensive knowledge of most weapons and even some spells. He taught me everything I know, how to stand my ground and how to fight for the people and protect the weak.
With the severing of the alliance kingdoms and my family becoming more and more isolationist in ideology, I decided to abandon my home and register with the paladin order "**The Knigts of the Silver Hand**".

![Image](https://i.pinimg.com/474x/a5/65/69/a565699d4d29a256211f216a6fabdb3d.jpg)

I joined their ranks under the guidance ***Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker***, being of nobility born had its privileges I guess. I have decided that my values and ideas align with that of the order, but little did I know that the darkness can corrupt even those who are bathed in light.
I was only a young lad when I saw the culling of those poor souls in Stratholme, the fall of Lordaeron, the dismantling of everything I thought I should stand for.
Needless to say everything was a shock and I had to seek refuge in where I knew it was safe, back home.
But that did not go to plan as you might presume...

![Image](https://www.hearthstonetopdecks.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/featured-the-witchwood008.jpg)

Coming back to my home felt like entering a new war, one that it was unavoidable. Scourge was everywhere and I couldn't reach the city, I had to hide in the surrounding forests but even there, danger was lurking at every corner.
Dreams started appearing vividly, of creatures of the night, hunting me, devouring me and then ... waking up as one of them. An immortal beast that had an insatiable thirst for conflict and blood.

I shook them of as just dreams, but one evening when I was trying to slip through the woods back into Ravenshire, my nightmares came true. I've encountered one of those beasts, sitting right in front of me, ready to pounce and have my neck.

![Image](https://images-wixmp-ed30a86b8c4ca887773594c2.wixmp.com/f/8bce5ab6-4b83-4ff0-8831-b4d63e47d991/de6hbz2-473b0b0d-99fa-4865-9c4c-00e7b9389f30.jpg?token=eyJ0eXAiOiJKV1QiLCJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9.eyJzdWIiOiJ1cm46YXBwOjdlMGQxODg5ODIyNjQzNzNhNWYwZDQxNWVhMGQyNmUwIiwiaXNzIjoidXJuOmFwcDo3ZTBkMTg4OTgyMjY0MzczYTVmMGQ0MTVlYTBkMjZlMCIsIm9iaiI6W1t7InBhdGgiOiJcL2ZcLzhiY2U1YWI2LTRiODMtNGZmMC04ODMxLWI0ZDYzZTQ3ZDk5MVwvZGU2aGJ6Mi00NzNiMGIwZC05OWZhLTQ4NjUtOWM0Yy0wMGU3YjkzODlmMzAuanBnIn1dXSwiYXVkIjpbInVybjpzZXJ2aWNlOmZpbGUuZG93bmxvYWQiXX0.sAHDS7BmfbVfIjSZtKat7lJwZGkU-ak817dQM1em59k)

My fate seems to be sealed, but in those panic moments, I remembered my training, my vows to the light, my desire to just stay... alive. I have fought the beast , we fought relentlessly ... in a moment where I thought it is going to be my last, he pounced and bit me on my side , raising me in the air and tossing me into a tree. I have casted my holy light to prevent the injuries killing me straight away, I raised my hammer and shattered the ground beneath him just before he was able to strike me a second time, only to use all my strength left to shatter his back in one swift blow.
I fell on one knee facing the dying beast now, not being far off myself. As he struggled to breathe and move, he locked his eyes with mine and growled, but it was not one of agony. It was a mocking growl, as if he was the one who won the duel.

![Image](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSpuy9oSyG9RLmTLxG59vBlunCexkov72ONLw&s)

The last thing I remember was that growl before I feel into a deep slumber, wondering as I was drifting if this is the end for myself. No legacy, no great achievements , not enough great deeds... Is this the end ? ...
