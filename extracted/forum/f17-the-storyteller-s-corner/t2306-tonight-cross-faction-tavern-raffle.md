**Cross-Faction Raffle tonight!**
Try your luck and buy your raffles this evening!

 *Alliance:*
![💯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4af.svg) Crescent Moon Tavern ![💯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4af.svg)
 **Place:** Stormwind - The Park. You will find us in the first tavern to the left of The Well.
 **Time:** 11th December 5 pm ST **(Tonight)**
 **Setting:** Robbert Oakhoart, a Tavern employee, has been actively promoting. The Tavern is covered with posters and parchments with the following text can be found around town:

"Come join us tonight in The Crescent Moon Tavern for drinks from cheap to quality and good food! Also we have a very special raffle! Get your tickets this evening for 25 silver each and become rich.

 5% commission goes to the organizer."

 *Horde:*
![💯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4af.svg)  Dancing Goblin Inn ![💯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4af.svg)
 **Place:** The Crossroads, The Barrens
 **Time:** 11th December 5 pm ST (Tonight)
 **Setting:** Kugg will be casually sitting around the bar. Sometimes promoting the raffle. Word has only slightly spread through The Barrens and Durotar.

Want to be rich in an instant? Well this evening in The Dancing Goblin Inn Kugg of The Shadowmoon Clan will make that true! Buy your raffles for only twenty-five silver coins at dusk. The winner gets all and will be announced when the moon is a fist above all The Barren's mountain and hills.

Goblin communication technology has made it possible that friends amongst enemies over the sea and to the north will also contribute to your winnings!

 *Outcome not guaranteed. 5% commission goes to a good cause known as Kugg.*

 **How does it work?**
Very simple!

- There are 100 raffle tickets in total. These are shared between Robbert and Kugg.
- Per 25s your character receives a ticket (mail parchment) with a random nr. beteen 1-100.
- At 8 pm ST Kugg at The Crossroads will do /roll 100. Ticket (both factions) closest to the rolled number wins the complete (95%) pot!
- Screenshot of the roll will be provided. Booking-keeping at: <https://bit.ly/x-raffle>
- It is only possible to buy a raffle-ticket in-character.
