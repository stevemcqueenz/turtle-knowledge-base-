> Hey hey!
>
>  Speedy here! I'm visiting Ironforge today.
>
>  It's a warm and cozy place! You can hear the noise of hammers and anvils in the background, mixed with the laughter of intoxicated dwarves. I want to join them to try the best mead of Azeroth! But my aunt said that a small turtle shouldn't try this drink...
>
>  Here and there you can see gnomes who are a little bit taller than me. They scurry back and forth with their high-tech gadgets.
>
>  I don't know who they are and I'm afraid to approach them. My mother always told me that I shouldn't talk to strangers.
>
>  Wow! These animals must be gryphons! Sorry, I have to leave for now.
>
>  I hope you will be interested in my stories.
