**Chapter One - Going out with a Bang!**

 *Previously, in the Greysteel Chronicles…*

Tripp Blackmoore was hired to be Ashlynn’s bodyguard during her task to investigate the bandit activity in the land. Their travels eventually lead them to a man named Talvik, who became the first recruit of this new company that Blackmoore was building. Being payed 10 silver, he became second in command of a company that did not exist.

It wasn’t long until, after asking for Ashlynn’s help, the company became official and recruiting began. Blackmoore had posters upon posters printed up, and had them posted wherever he could, spreading them far and wide.

At first the company wasn’t doing well. Blackmoore had spent way too much on advertising and there weren't many recruits to help coin flow into his pockets. After doing some digging, he found their first official job. They were to infiltrate into the heart of Orgrimmar to rescue an elf who had gone missing for far too long. After some planning, the newly formed company managed to get themselves shipped into Orgrimmar using crates. They carefully infiltrated deeper into the city, where they did eventually meet the elf they were to rescue. She convinced them that she was here on a mission, and that since the Greysteels had come to the heart city of the Horde, they might as well assist her. It was agreed, and together they made their way deeper into the city, discovering some sort of plot that convinced them that the Orcs were banning together in some sort of cult, summoning demons in the bowels of the city.

Later on Ashlynn was tasked to investigate the mines in Moonbrook by a priest named Andromath, who was her mentor. He wanted to hire the Greysteel Company and use them to recover a book for him, a very old tome. Gladly taking the job, Blackmoore lead the Greysteels as well as a few volunteers down into the mines. Not long after they began did voices begin to assault them. The voices caused the group to begin doubting one and other, worsening as they continued deeper into the mountain.It wasn’t long until they defeated one of the commanding bandits. They were able to locate the book that they were required to attain. Shadows seem to be flowing off of it in a mist, it was clear to everyone that this book held dark magic and shouldn’t be taken lightly.

After a brief reprieve from the extensive work they had been hired to do in the mines, they came across a sickly man named Parcival who shortly after joined the company. He accompanied the Greysteels on their next assignment, they were to rescue an elf who was last known to have gone to these caverns in the middle of the Barren lands, all the way across the sea in Kalimdor. Paying quite a bit better than what they received in their journey to Orgrimmar, Leoren presented the task to Blackmoore who agreed that they should take on the contract. Upon their arrival, voices similar to the ones that they heard in the mines once again began to assult them, though quite a bit more powerful then the first time. They did their best to ignore these voices, and fought through wave after wave of elves who had lost their minds, turning into serpents, and monsters they never seen before, creatures warped by corruption. After some discussion, Parcival had come to the conclusion that the source of the voices was a demon, and he knew how to rid them of the sharp pain in their minds.

When it became clear that their contract was lost and that the mission had been a failure, the voice struck once again, bringing everyone to their knees. In an attempt to save his companions from the wrath of the voice, Parcival put his own blade to his throat and slit it, though no blood spilt, he still fell to the ground, dead. The voice stopped after he fell over, and the group stared in shock at Parcival’s body and questioning the insane deed he had done. Not long after a heated argument broke out between Blackmoore and Leoren. When Blackmoore finally left, Ash decided she had to do what had been discussed with her earlier. After informing Leoren of what she planned to do, the two of them brought Parcival’s body to a safer location, and Ashlynn began to work.

Ashlynn was able to “save” the man, going by what he had told her before and the instructions left in the bag.  Questioning herself the entire time, she followed the instructions, using the tinctures and potions provided in his bag to repair the wound to his throat and then feeding a potion to him that was to revive him. Once Parcival had recovered, he asked Ashlynn to acquire some books for him, to aide in his research. One being a book of shadow magic. He offered to help her continue down this road she had stepped foot on, in her efforts to “save” him. Appalled at his offer, Ashlynn refused to help him. Though later on she found herself going through the hoops to acquire the books, lying to Andromath about the real reason she wanted a specific book.

The allied Druid, Barros, who had been eavesdropping on the conversation between Pacvical and Ashlynn confronted her, demanding to know what Parcival was up to. After Ashlynn’s insistence that she didn’t know, he discovered that she had indeed recovered the books that he had asked for, and called her out on her lie. Ashlynn claimed over and over again that she had no intention of actually handing the book over, but the druid refused to believe her. After a heated conversation, he took the books from the Priestess, transforming into a crow and flying off.

Parcival encountered Ashlynn in the streets of Stormwind days later, inquiring about the books. Ashlynn insisted that she never agreed to do as he asked. Though when he asked if she had still sought out the books, she answered that she did. During this discussion, Father Andromath happened past. He heard part of the conversation, the true reason that Ashlynn had requested the book from him coming to the surface. He confronted Parcival, seeing him for what he was, claiming that he was interfering with his own plans. He called down the Light onto Parcival, chaining him to a post and demanded that Ashlynn return the books to him. A mage who happened to be in the wrong place at the wrong time, decided that he would attempt to stop Andromath from doing anymore harm, but after Ashlynn refused to tell Andromath where the books were, he struck Parcival down, pulling his head cleanly from his body. Once again there was no blood.

Seeing Andromath in a new light, Ashlynn tried to call the guards to apprehend him. However, the Bishop cast dark magic onto Parcival’s head, causing it glow in purple flames and rise into the air. He framed it so that it looked like she was controlling the head, claiming that she was the one who assaulted him, she was the one using dark magic, consorting with the undead and necromancers and that he was the one who was being attacked. Ashlynn and the mage were arrested, and Andromath walked free.

Ashlynn’s bail was paid and she was set free of the dungeon with the condition that she is confined to Stormwind and called to trial. The mage was properly interviewed, and, after it was decided he had no part to play in the case, was released as well with no charge.

Later on, the druid and priestess met again. Ashlynn doing her best to convince the druid to return the books to her, explaining what she had saw her mentor do. That she needed them to present in her trial against Andromath. After a long discussion, it was agreed that the book should be hidden away until the date of her trial, where they could both use it as evidence against the Bishop.

But Ashlynn deceived him. When she had the opportunity she took the book back and ran. Barros was quicker and quickly cornered her. When there was no where else to run, the druid threatening to claw her unless she returned the book. She tried to call down the light upon him, but it wouldn’t come to her. In a moment of desperation, she opened the book, it’s power channeling through her, and cast a spell of dark magic onto the druid, making her escape.

Horrified at what she had done, Ashlynn ran to the house she was provided to stay in, and locked herself away, questioning herself and the light. A visitor came later that night. An old friend Ashlynn never thought she would have seen again. Parcival’s head had been recovered by this woman, and presented before Ashlynn. She explained that he could be fixed, once more. After a long night, the woman left with the dark book in hand, and the head wrapped in a bag.

It wasn’t long until the day of the trial. Ashlynn was brought forth before the High Priest and Priestess as well as the people of Stormwind. Blackmoore came to Ashlynn’s side, helping her defend her case and explain herself to the judges. Andromath was also called to stand before them, to explain his side of things. It was a long drawn out process, where the onlookers began to demand that they condemn her was a witch and burn her.
Just as the High Priest and Priestess reached a verdict - Blackmoore stepped before them, requesting to be allowed to say one last thing. They agreed to allow the man to speak, and Blackmoore turned to face the audience, addressing them. As Blackmoore spoke, a few people wearing the same tabard and crest he did as he presented his case, began to move to different locations on the floor.

When the dwarf playing the defence of Andromath spoke up an objection, Blackmoore interrupted him by calling out the worlds “Big Bang”. At the sound of an explosion, the walls shook as smoke filled the room. Blackmoore used this distraction to grab Ashlynn and pull her through the sudden chaos and confusion that erupted. He pushed through the panicking audience with Ashlynn in tow. People pouring out of the court house after him.

The people were smart though, and they caught on. An angry mob formed, chaotic and unorganized, and they chased Blackmoore, Ashlynn, and the other members of the Greysteel company, throughout the streets of Stormwind. The city guard, completely taken aback at the sudden uproar, took a long time to organize enough to take up chase behind the angry mob.

The Greysteels split up, going off in different directions to lose the mob. In the chaos that erupted, the trail of people kept rushing in a single direction, some of them not even sure who they were chasing at this point. In the confusion, as people began to cross the bridge into the trade district, people were being pushed into the waters of the Canals, all of them frantically calling for help as they swam to the nearest dock.

That’s when the legendary Crocklisc rumored to reside in the canals attacked. This giant beast that few ever claimed to see, saw it’s nest under threat and attacked. It’s gaping maw large enough to swallow a human whole. Cries of terror erupted in the canals as people swam as fast as they could as friends and loved ones were dragged under the ever growing red water.

The adventurers and heros of Stormwind bravely came to their aid. Armed men and women bravely jumped down into the waters and began to fight the savage beast, drawing it’s attention away from the civilians and working together as a team to cut it down. At long last, the fabled Crocolisk was slain. Injured, bruised, people one by one began climbing out of the water, helping others do the same.

It wasn’t long before a few tents were erected in the streets, and the healers of Stormwind began to work on those wounded as others caught their breath. Those whose minds went back to the chase began to realize that the criminals escaped somewhere in the confusion. Very slowly, the mob began to disperse, and the streets of Stormwind fell into a heavy silence as citizens returned to their homes, smoke still rising from the Dwarven District.

**Chapter Two - The Ever Waking Graveyard**

 *Previously, in the Greysteel Chronicles…*

Smoke filled the air of Stormwind the morning after the attack. The court house in shambles, smoke still rising after the explosions that racked the city the night before. The morning was too quiet. People went about their daily chores and spoke in whispers about what had happened. Many had died that night in the fire.

Stormwind Guards patrolled the city in squads. The captain of the guard leading his men down the canals toward the Pig and Whistle in Old Town. Tripp Blackmoore and his Company were already getting ready to make their exit. He had remained behind, making sure his members were each able to make their way out of the now hostile city. The man waited long enough though, he knew that if he lingered any longer he would be arrested and sent to the gallows quick.

Blackmoore payed several children to help him out. He with his wagon made his way nonchalantly through the canals, straight into the captains patrol. After some heated threats, Blackmoore gave the signal, and the children of Stormwind let out minor explosives from the rooftops, distracting the guards and allowing Blackmoore his escape.

It was apparent they needed to hide, and luckily one of the Greysteel's knew of an abandoned town they could lay low in. One by one they trickled into Raven Hill, and with the help of Blackmoore, they were able to clean it up and make it their own. With a lot of maintenance and TLC - they even managed to thicken the walls and board the windows up just enough to mute the ever present sound of moaning coming from the haunted graveyard in their back yard.

Tensions were high amidst the members of the Greysteel Company. Trust toward their guild leader was very thin, as the company's reputation was in shambles. They were all branded as criminals - traitors to the city. Wanted posters with Ashlynn and Blackmoore's faces were quickly posted, along with the promise of a reward for any known associates. They could not return to Stormwind anytime soon. They needed to find a way to clear their names and pay off their own bounties.

One elf named Barros came to the company with a contract and an offer of his own. The Scythe of Elune was rumored to be within Duskwood. A powerful artifact that was known for the blight that fell upon the woods. If they were to retrieve the artifact and return it to the Cenarion Circle, then Barros would be able to work with the druids to reward the company and possibly remove said bounty. Only if they would prove themselves.

Many members of the Greysteels left within the following weeks. The fear of arrest along with an impossible task was too much of a risk. Many doubted Blackmoore's decision to go after the Scythe of Elune, however the man insisted that it was the only way. If they retrieved that Scythe, then the fame alone for recovering a lost artifact would be enough to clear their names, surely.

As Blackmoore sent scouts out to the surrounding areas to gather as much information as could be found as to the whereabouts of the Scythe, a few of the members decided that it would be within their own interest to strike a deal with those of the Night Watch - after a large construct of flesh and bone attacked them outside the tavern. Darkshire would help by selling them supplies and sending reinforcements when asked.In return the Greysteels would keep the graveyard tame, freeing Darkshire's men to work more in other areas that were quickly falling out of control. The road running through the gloomy forest was a treacherous one. Wolves, Worgen, bandits, all were known to ambush travelers to and from Darkshire. The Greysteels had made their home in a very dangerous place.

Despite the numbers thinning, Blackmoore was determined to continue on this quest to find the Scythe. His scouts had returned with some information regarding the very graveyard that loomed behind their hideout. Still, he addressed those of his Company. He offered them the choice. A resident Warlock had offered a different path, one that would take them north, and to forget the Scythe business completely. A vote was had, and in the end, they decided to continue working with Barros to acquire the Scythe.

A party was made, armor and weapons delivered by Thordin Hammerwill, the company Blacksmith. They ventured into the undead stricken cemetery. Grave Stones older than those left by the victims of wars, a series of elvish runes lead them down into an old crumbling crypt. Other than the ghouls that guarded it, it seemed that no one living had entered these catacombs in a very long time. Bleached bones were scattered along the ground they walked. Elongated skulls hinted that more elves had been laid to rest in these forgotten tombs. Their search eventually sent them through a rather fresh looking dirt tunnel, which lead out into a lab of some sort. Again, guarded by the dead, the Greysteels were forced to defend themselves. Blackmoore lead his men forward, coming across a reanimated thrall they were forced to cut down. Upon it's person, was a note eluding to a place in Silverpine Forest - far North from where they were currently located.

While the Greysteels recovered from their adventure into the crypts, Blackmoore was already working on the next step of his plan. Upon the perfect timing of a letter from a doctor requesting to add his services to the Company, he sent Ashlynn north to meet with the potential recruit as well as to scout out the area to find out where Blackmoore would take the company next. However, Ashlynn probed her nose too far into matters, and got herself captured by a resident in Shadowfang keep, who had heard of their little quest to find the Scythe.

Using a gnomish engineered communication device, Ashlynn was forced into luring the company out to Silverpine. Once they arrived, the Company was forced to split it's forces. Caleth went ahead, cloaking himself in shadows to get a better look. Talvik and Blackmoore lead a squad down into the village below the keep. John, Dolokov, Asta, and Barros bravely chose to brave the keep itself.

The group that went into the town searched far and wide for any clue as to where the Scythe may be hidden. The villagers of Pyrewood were not pleased to have their company. Not long after, the villagers curse reared it's ugly head and they found themselves surrounded by Worgen. They were forced to fight and flee into the city hall which they barricaded until they realized that there was no way out. The idea came to them that they would have trap the Worgen inside and bring down the roof of the building. Their antics nearly killed Thordin. Still, they managed to fight their way out. Upon hearing through the gnomish communication device that this was all a set up, they started fighting their way up to the keep to support the others who had gone inside.

Inside the Keep it was not much more pleasant. Bats, worgen, and undead littered the halls of the keep, ambushing them at every turn. They were given little time to rest, for each time they thought they had a moment to catch their breath, more groups of undead and worgen would come around the corner and charge. John did his best using the light to keep the others going, as Barros lead the way. They were guided by their enemy, wave after wave, deep into the Keep. Upon entering the sanctum, they discovered Ashlynn. Captured and imprisoned by the sorcerer of the keep. It was then they realized it was all a rouse. The Scythe was never here. The intent was to kill the Greysteels and prevent them from going any farther in their search.

With their combined efforts, they managed to defeat the sorcerer and his beasts. Thoroughly ensuring him dead. They rescued Ashlynn and began to make their way back. The body of the sorcerer bursting into blue flames and disappearing as they made their escape.

The two groups met up once again, the first managing to scare the last standing beasts away once they realized their allies had all been slain. After taking some time to mend to the injured, they began their long journey back to Raven Hill. They thought that they would finally be able to take some time to recuperate before the next plan of attack in terms of finding the Scythe. Instead, they were greeted by a horrific sight.

Not only had the tavern been completely ransacked, even worse a mess than it was before, the new furniture tossed over, books and papers scattered, their innkeeper, Mister Finster, cowering behind the counter... In the middle of it all stood one of them. An undead. A grotesque creature in robes with rotting flesh, abscesses, and a single bulging eye, standing in the middle of the carnage, and now before several drawn weapons of the Greysteels, ready to slay him where he stood.

Ashlynn was the one who stopped them, explaining that he was one of them, a member of the company. Much like the orc that had been hired previously. Many were surprised by this, and confronted Blackmoore who informed them that he would hire anyone, no matter what they are or who they were. Stress and tensions were high, accusations were made left and right - threats of leaving the company surfaced. The arguments were interrupted by the screech of what could only be the Dark Riders. Despite being exhausted, several rushed outside, to see the wraiths ride off on their skeletal horses - with what could only be the Scythe of Elune in their possession. Quickly they chased after them, only to lose track of them by the time they hit Deadwind pass.

It was concluded that they only could have gone to Karazhan. A mansion known to be the layer of Medivh. A cursed place of warped magic and free chaos. They needed to prepare for the journey ahead. Entering Karazhan would be no simple task. They needed more money for better armor and supplies. How would they get the money, when they were a band of wanted criminals? It was Ashlynn who came up with the solution, they would host a tournament - in the old abandoned arena in Stranglethorn Vale. Rheya, Caleth, and the undead, Harvey, were tasked to spread posters far and wide across Azeroth to spread the word. Bets were made and collected. The tournament was a success, even the threat of bounty hunters wasn't a problem. It was a much needed break from the realities of life. A chance to get out pent frustrations. To settle old rivalries and start fresh ones anew. Not only did the company profit nicely, but their name seemed to be just a little bit clearer in some eyes. It was a step in the right direction. They even considered perhaps doing a tournament again in the future, learning from their mistakes from this first time - and host an even better one.

At last. They were ready. They knew where the Scythe was. They knew where they must go and what they must do. Blackmoore rallied his company with a speech. This was the day that they cleared their names. That they show the world what the Greysteel Company could do! Despite the fact that Ashlynn had been gone for several days, and did not show to accompany them, they set out. Some with hope, some with their own doubts. As one, they made their way to Darkshire and into the canyon of Deadwind. The wind was harsh in this land, stripping trees bear of branches and leaves. Starving vultures roamed the sky, diving on and assaulting the group, desperate for fresh meat. They fought them off, one by one, as they made their way further down. When they came upon an ogre camp, doubts began to truly set in. The warlock, Terrance, shouted for them to turn back. The group seemed torn to follow Terrance back the way they came, or to continue forward. It was Talvik who put his foot down and convinced them to continue forward. The ogres went down slowly, taking the combined efforts of everyone to bring them each down. Terrance fled while the others pressed on, only to be lifted into the air and brought back to the group forcibly. Outraged, Terrance assaulted Blackmoore, shouting about the futility of this plan and that they should all turn back while they still could.

Then the residents of Karazhan began to appear. The image of a dark rider rushing past the group, an invisible force pushing them aside off the roads. Forward, closer to the mansion itself. They came across a spirit from the past, warning them of the dangers that awaited them and for them to turn back. At first immune to their attacks, she kept pushing them back away from her ethereal form. At their insistence, she promised them their doom and allowed them to pass by, only to begin to slowly stalk after them, other ghosts of the past assaulting them, guiding them toward the crypts laying behind the mansion itself.

At the screech of Darkriders in the distance, they all hurried into the crypts. Blackmoore confronted Terrance about his own accusations, and began to fight the Warlock while the others tried to intervene and begged them to stop. In a rage, Terrance brought forth his own magic, and teleported away, abandoning his allies to the mercy of the dead.

The group made their way into the next room, looking for clues to where the Artifact may be. They opened doors, only to be assaulted by the undead who were disturbed from their rest. Powerful creatures, with more strength then those that wandered aimlessly in the Graveyard back in Raven Hill. It took the strength of everyone combined to cut them down.

The spirit that confronted them outside earlier, appeared once more, and began shouting for her Masters to come for them. Blackmoore and the undead doctor, Harvey, stood their ground as the rest of the group fled, keeping the spirit's attention as the others got away safely. Blackmoore swung at the ghost, but it did little good. At the urging of the others, they made their retreat. Looking back to see that the woman was slowly following along behind them, following them as the company fled down further into the dark crypts. Bodies of the dead were littered everywhere. Some rose from the ground as they passed, forcing them to stop and cut them down before they could continue.

They came to place with water. Dark and murky, with the faintest outline of chains in the distance. A few went through the inky water first, to see if they could find an exit. Upon returning and telling the others, they all made their way into the water, swimming down, passing bloated bodies hanging upside down from the chains. Bits of skin breaking off from the bodies as they disturbed the still stagnate water swimming past them. Upon resurfacing on a lower level of the crypts, they were face to face with the spirit once again, who cackled maniacally at the hopelessness of the company's situation.

Having had enough, those who could weild the light began casting it down upon the spirit. This caught her off guard and she began fending for herself, stumbling backwards, dumbfounded at the impossibility that their magic could reach her. She fought back with her own magic of darkness, scattering it throughout the group. She focused on Blackmoore, taking control of his mind and forcing him to attack his allies. The light was weakening her, however. She vanished on the spot, fleeing from their continuous use of the light against her.

Having thought they defeated the woman, the company continued forward, some moving ahead as other recovered from the fight. A priest named Walt wandered a little too far, examining the massive pile of bones that sat in the corner of an otherwise empty room. The spirit appeared once again, behind the priest and lifted him into the air with a laugh. Others ran to Walt's rescue. Once again lashing out with the light. Thordin threw his light infused hammer straight at the ghost's form. It struck true - cutting a hole right through her ethereal form and passing through into the pile of bones. Her form shimmered and flickered - weakened from the strike - though it was not enough to save Walt. She vanished, along with the priest, leaving the rest of the company one more man short.

Moral at an all time low, the Greysteels had no choice but to keep searching for what they were after. A quick run through the remaining rooms bared no fruit. They returned to the water, and once again subjected themselves to the bloated hanging corpses, swimming to the other side of the pool.

There, among a trove of many other items that had to be magic in nature - there it was. The Scythe of Elune. The relic they had been searching for and sacrificed so much for all this time. The spirit was there before it, waiting for them. Though her form was broken. No longer was she humanoid, but a shade of darkness instead. She did not attack them, nor react to the stream of light magic they were casting upon her. The bright fire in her eyes watched them with interest. She moved aside, allowing them forward into the room, and encouraging them to take their prize. Immediately the company was suspicious. As the druid Barros stepped up to the Scythe, out from the collection rose the dead, dozens of them coming after the group over and over again. The shade's hands began to channel darkness, and the room shook. Rubble from the ceiling began to collapse down onto the Greysteels. The undead continued their assault as the shade channeled her dark magics into the room around them, cackling in a warped, dark, corrupted voice the entire time.

There was no time. Barros lunged for the scythe, but right as he grabbed it a boulder landed right onto it and snapped it in two - leaving him with just a piece. One by one they began dodging the falling rubble, fleeing from the undead. Thordin remained behind to catch the attention of the undead, giving the others a chance to escape. By the time the last one, Asta, made it out, the entrance to the pool of water collapsed - leaving Thordin trapped on the other side with the shade and the army of undead.
