With this cringe video I want to rekindle Role Play on Turtle WoW, my plan is to do part stream part Role Play in game.

Planning to host the event on Saturday 6th of March during the day, so it does not interfere with raiding schedules and hopefully most players who wish to attend can without giving up on any other things they wish to achieve.

Edit: after discussion with a few people we landed on 15-17.00 Server Time - there should be plenty of time to do part one of the event that way and for raiders to join their runs. Decided to do it split into two separate events to accommodate most players who showed interest in the event so far and to give room for other discussions and free roleplay.
