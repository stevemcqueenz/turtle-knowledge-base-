# Joreth Jonathan Withe's Journal part 2.

## Post 3385 by Ozkum — 2019-07-07T19:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3385#p3385 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Beware, beware the Daughter of the sea.
Beware, I heard him cry.
His words carried upon the ocean breeze,
As he sank beneath the tide.
Those blood-soaked shores of Kalimdor
Where sailors fought and died.
The Admiral fell at Theramore,
because she left his side.
Why this? Why this, oh Daughter of the Sea?
Why this? Did you forget your seaside days?
Always the pride of our nation's eyes,
How could she go astray?
When she did flee across the ocean deep,
The Admiral followed west.
What else but sail to save a daughter's life,
And pray she still drew breath?
But there he found upon those distant shores,
Enemies upon the rise
But when he faced those savage foes
His daughter stood aside.
And buried deep beneath the waves,
Betrayed by family.
To his nation, with his last breath, cried,
Beware the Daughter of the Sea.

Ah that brings back some memories.
This was an kul tiran song that they sang after the death of Grand admiral Proudmoore.  I never believed that Jaina betrayed kul tiras but never have i met her.
Moving on.

I woke up at sentinel hill. Gryan Stoutmantle had some assignments for me first i went to kill more of the defias brotherhood at Moonbrooke i would have died unless an sorcerer called Goburst would have helped me. These defias were harder to kill alone because of their magi but as an group they posed no threat. As we came back to sentinel hill we found an chest at an gnoll camp it contained an axe but i wasn't sure how to use it correctly so i sold it. Next Gryan gave us an assignment to kill more defias but this time the defias were hiding on an mountain. They actually we're challenging because of their knowledge of dagger's. But it didn't take long. Goburst had an urgent matter to attend so we separated. I went back to Goldshire because i wanted to learn more about blacksmithing. And the local smith trainer remembered me and had an assignment for me. He wanted me to go and meet Elmore an dwarven smith in Stormwind i accepted. Elmore told me to deliver an box to loch modan and i was excited about this. I had an opportunity to see Ironforge the legendary city of dwarves. I wanted to test out what they called deeprun tram. As i waited i helped an gnome with catching rats. The gnome gave me an package to deliver to the other side of the tram so i accepted. I the tram went faster than anything i had witnessed before. I almost couldn't stand up but i eventually was able to speak to the gnome waiting for the package. He gave me some food for my troubles but I couldn't eat it because there was an rat tail sticking out of it.  As i walked out of the gnome section i was amazed such architecture that i thought people could only dream about Ironforge. I found the middle area and was just appreciating the great craftsmanship as i spotted an gryphon master i marked it into my map and was on my way. Dun morogh looked astonishing with it's mountains and all the snow. But soon i saw an gateway to loch modan going through an hill it was something that i had never seen before.  As i went to the other side i saw two big dwarf statues. I walked on the road for some time and stumbled into an dwarven town i marked the gryphon master into my map and left. Then i saw the guard tower that the i was supposed to deliver the box. I delivered it and used my hearthstone. I ended the day at lion's pride inn.

The next day i returned to Sentinel hill Gryan had an new assignment for me. He told me that they had spotted an messenger of defias and they wanted me to find him and and see if he had something that could tell us where the head of the Defias brotherhood Edwin VanCleef was hiding. I found an courier of some sort walking away from Moonbrooke. I kindly asked him to show me what was the letter on his hand but hi tried to stab me with an dagger and i took his head off with my newly acquired longsword. When i returned an Gryan wanted me to escort an traitor of the Defias brotherhood to their lair. I questioned Gryans decision so i mailed an letter to some of the Red Fists and got an answer from Dolokhov Steelblood and Frederick Thorn. They both came to Sentinel hill and we started our journey. Our brotherhood versus theirs. We walked into Moonbrooke like gods of war as we slaughtered all defias with ease. We found the place that the Defias called Deadmines but we didn't enter. Dolokhov left to do his own thing but me and Frederick flew to Lakeshire. We killed some gnolls and helped an crafter with giving him some items so he could start rebuilding the bridge. Frederick told me that the dwarves of loch modan were having trouble with the local troggs. We decided to help them and come back to Lakeshire tomorrow. We found the troggs and slaughtered tens of them until an dwarf told us to kill their three leaders. They weren't hard but we found an old dusty chest at their lair. After rolling some dice Frederick got to open it and he found some old leather shoulder pads. They looked good with his black leather armor. Soon after that it was nightfall and i decided to go to lions pride to drink until an bard walked in and started to sing i asked him to sing The Daughter of the Sea. So here i am with one gold piece and 87 silver pieces at a table next to the window writing the second chapter of my story.
