**Fooled by the Defias**
The story of the lost lookout

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/HxWhYvsN/image.webp)

At the borders of Northwind a lookout was built from where our soldiers can check the surroundings. Securing out borders was always a need. A small cozy hut was built on the highest peak over the waterfall, and a handfull od soldiers were serving there. Just a footsep away from Stormwind.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/Z5dRHVm2/Kepernyokep-2025-06-04-151634.webp)

Campfire, and deep talks with card games. The says were passing slow, and from nowhere were any sign of any movement. The heart of the Kingdom was silent and peaceful.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/W44sK8Ly/Kepernyokep-2025-06-04-153909.webp)

The members of the guards were really comforable with the surroundings, it was one of the best places to serve. The song of the birds and the amber ale made the good effect, great mood and highest comfort. One would only dream of it in Westfall.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/d1Lt7B5y/Kepernyokep-2025-06-04-152700.webp)

 ***

Months later the outpost was robbed, the guards dissapeared. The clues are led to the Waterfalls. A decision was made. The milita of Ambershire splitted to find out who is behind this act.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/Bbf9qckQ/Kepernyokep-2025-07-15-211219.webp)

The nearby Dwarwen expedition what is observing the Black dragonflight reported some people in Red Bandanas in the region. A camp was found with some stolen things in it at the foot ot the waterfall.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/LsSx6Q6z/Kepernyokep-2025-07-15-214247.webp)

The robbers traces were leading to south Elwynn to a dockyard. The full party of Ambershire was sent to Jerod`s landing to find out the clues, and catch the thieves. The strike on the dockyard was succesful and they captured Nabo the Lesser, a goblin who confessed that the shipment is long gone to the jungle of Stranglethon.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/zXbLVJyG/17527901608467788456796000520600.webp)

With empty hands they returned to Ambershire where they learned the ugly news. They were fooled by the Defias. The plot was only for distracting the milita, and while they were splitted the red masked bandits took the cliffs over the Waterfall...

**The Grand Champion of the Alliance**

Every now and then in the Jousting fields of Ambershire a big event takes to place. Northwind was thinking big and invited all the guilds of the Alliance to fight for the most precious title of this decade. Glory and fame will follow the winner knight's footseps.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/vTPCqmbp/image.png)

 16 Knights arrived to the jousting ceremonies from all around the world to go home with fame and glory, but only one of them will triumph. Jousting is one of the noblest way to earn recognition in a friendly festive way. Not easy to master.

The knights usually facing each other in 8 rounds where they would like to dismount the other. With each round they collect points.

 - If they dismount the opponent then it worth 10 points (roll 8)
 - If they shatter their lance they get 5 points (roll 6-7)
 - If they only touch the opponent for that they get 1 point (roll 4-5)
 - For a miss no pints at all  (roll 2-3)
 - If they hit the opponent on an undesignated place or the mount it worth -5 points (roll 1)
