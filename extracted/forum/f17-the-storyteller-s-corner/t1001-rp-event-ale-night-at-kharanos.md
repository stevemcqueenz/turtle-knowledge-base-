# [RP][EVENT] Ale night at Kharanos.

## Post 5653 by Khazvarg — 2020-04-08T02:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5653#p5653 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A letter is delivered to you by an exhausted dwarf mountaineer, he cant give you any explanation until he recovers his breath, meanwhile, he just encourages you to read the letter. It is sealed with a simple wax, yet you can distinguish the wildhammer dwarf sigil on it.

"Aye Brothars and sistars, If thas letter makes it to ya it means that ya are alive, and therre is nothin we dwarves love morre to enjoy life than ale and good stories. Feel free to accept this invitation if ya have the liver enought to make it throught ta night, and ta patience enought to endure some old bad jokes. Ta meetin is goin to be at ol´ Kharanos, two moons after ya get this letter. Make sure to bring some good Ale, ´Cuz Im not goin to buy ya ale all ta night, ya lazzy a..... (The ink is a little blurred here).... And make sure you get yar manners to."

As you finish the letter you see a little signature and PD at the end.

"Khazvarg Wildhammer"
PD: Give the poor mountaineer some water, ta boy made it double to find ya so soon.

So, You are all invited to join us this Thursday at Kharanos, 20:00 server time, to get some old fashioned ale night at the most pure style of the sons and daughters of Khaz Modan¡ Everyone is welcome.
