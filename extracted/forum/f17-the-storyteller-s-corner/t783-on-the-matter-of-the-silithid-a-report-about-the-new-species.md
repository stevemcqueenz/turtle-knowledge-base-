# On the matter of the Silithid, a report about the new species in Kalimdor.

## Post 4121 by Draconis — 2019-09-30T21:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4121#p4121 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**KC 617(1): 22-30**

New species and reports of Silithids (Silithida) and Qiraji from Kalimdor

A.Thomas Matheny 1, Daryn Lightwind 2 & Mae Paledust 3

 1 Knightly Order of the Silver Hand, Expeditionary Command of the Western Alliance, Theramore.

 2 Druidic Order of the Cenarion Circle, Lorekeeper of the Order, Darnassus.

 3 Archeological League of the Explorers, Historian of the Guild, Ironforge.

 **Summary**

A.Thomas Matheny, Daryn Lightwind & Mae Paledust. 617. New species and reports of ***Silithids*** (Silithida) and ***Qiraji*** from Kalimdor.

Following up on recent developments concerning disappearances of passengers to and from the southern side of the continent of Kalimdor over the months of March to September, under leadership of appointed expeditionary leader Aurelian Thomas Matheny of the Knightly Order of the Silver Hand, with authorization from the ruling body of the city-state of Theramore, a small group of recruited explorers and combatants explored areas: The Field of Giants (Southern Barrens), The Shimmering Flats (Eastern Thousand Needles), for more information on reported sightings and disappearances.

Having found sufficient evidence of a nearly parasitic species of insectoid creatures, dubbed the Silithid by our Kaldorei associates, with the aid of senior Lorekeeper Lightwind and Historian Paledust, I present this report to the governing bodies of the Alliance, and the higher chains of Command.

 **The Silithids**

A species of as of now unknown origins, the Silithid are an insectoid race primarily found in the continent of Kalimdor, their native territory being that of the desert of Silithus in the south-western region of the continent.

Recently however, they have been shown to be versatile in surviving both milder regions, such as the southern Barrens, as well as the more humid regions of the forests of Feralas on the western coast of the continent.

The primary tool of this species is adaptation. It seems to satisfy the need of the hive, or colony in particular, the most obvious example of this would be the color of their outer body. A shell like construct which acts as armor for the species. In dry regions, the following colors have been noted: Turquoise Teal, Flax Yellow, Ruby Red. (Current information indicates the choice of color in these regions is primarily for one of two functions, camouflage to hide from potential threats, or bright colors to insinuate danger to said threats.)

In humid regions, the following colors have been noted: Turquoise Teal, Jade Green. (Current information indicates adaptation to the humid environment for better concealment in plain sight among foliage and shrubbery.)

No creature yet encountered, has shown the ability of swift adaptation to changes in the environment as the Silithid have. Caste Larvae, more commonly referred to as Broodlings, can evolve into a variety creature types based on the needs of the hive. Estimations show less than a month is required for a newly hatched Broodling to fully mature under calm circumstances and based on previous information about insectoid creatures, it is estimated an average lifespan is 5 to 10 years.

On the matter of scale, Silithid can range from the size of a normal insect, to gigantic in proportions. (See later Caste Colossi for more information.)  Estimations show the size of a fully grown Silithid varies on the period of time that it is allowed to develop in their egg stage. Current information also links size to the size of the colony of origination. A large number of Castes usually surpass human scale.

On the matter of appearance, Silithid range depending on caste, but all share several key parts. Each Silithid shares insectoid like features, antennae which pertrude from their head region, act as a means of communication, coordination and receiving information. Chemical dispersion into the air may also be critical in this. (Currently unconfirmed, requires further analysis.)

While numerous, they do not appear to be capable of intelligent and individual action. The antennae serve as a radio antenna for a transmission from afar. (A presumed Hivemind?) Every action of the Hive, and in turn every colony is as follows, directed towards a singular goal, the extinction and consumption of non-Silithid organisms.
On the matter of origin, several theories propose a link between these Silithid and the Nerubians of the Scourge. (Currently unconfirmed, requires more data.) Kaldorei colleges propose a link between the Silithid and the Qiraji. (See Qiraji further in the report for more information.)

On the matter of reproduction, Silithid life begins as an egg, most likely spawned from a Broodmother like caste and attached to colony walls as to allow more calm growth and further securing already established taint of the land the colony inhabits.

From an egg is born Caste Larvae, at this point a broodling and unspecialized for any particular role in Silithid society. Eventually, the broodling encases itself in a cocoon and changes into a new form. (See Castes for more information.)
On the matter of habitat, it seems as of now they prefer a combination of dry and arid landscape, along with deeper sub-terrain tunnels. Silithid colonies, dubbed Hives, are massive in scale, and usually surrounded by a combination of natural materials produced from Silithid bodily secretions and towering antenna-like spires jutting out of the earth. In the maze of tunnels below the surface, the Silithid raise their young, tend to their wounded, manage stores of food and water and gather to fend off hostile threats.

 *Pictured above is area designated: Noxius Lair (Tanaris.)*

On the matter of diet, current research indicates all biological mass is edible to the Silithid, using it they in turn are able to produce more of their kind, and continue their evolutionary cycle. Research also indicates rock is within their edible pallet. No current indication shows they are capable of eating metals or stronger materials, however visible analysis shows attempts to dilute the sturdier material with an ooze like substance have been attempted.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/1mxynwM/Silithid-Metal01.jpg)
 *Pictured above is a taken photograph of an ooze covered vein of the metal: Mithril. Its natural shined enhanced by the odd liquid, and the metal itself seems untainted in any way, further analysis is required.*

Due to inhabiting arid regions, current research suggests a large quantity of water is required to fuel the ever growing swarm. Large gatherings of Silithid near water rich areas of Tanaris, indicate it is crucial to their continued growth.

On the matter of hostility, while smaller sized Silithid seemed more pre-occupied with handling matters of hive construction and preservation, certain castes of the Silithid are utterly devoted to the cause of combat and extermination. Furthermore, Kaldorei historical data provides insight on a conflict dubbed, The War of the Shifting Sands. (Currently seeking out more information on this conflict.) When given the order, tens of thousands of Silithid can be mobilized within days, and sent out to attack nearly any position where their tunnels can dig into. It is also highlighted that for every fifty that fall, another hundred are already prepared to take their place going off of Kaldorei records. By contrast to similar incursions (See reports on Scourge and Demons for more information), the goal of the Silithid is not the simple extermination of all non-Silithid races, but the consumption of their entire beings for furthering the cause of Silithid evolution, at any cost.
On the matter of society, the Silithid society has been organized into several different castes, with differing physical types. Every caste has its unique function within their society, though all share objectives of expanding living quarters for future generations as well as the defense of the younger and vurnrable larvae and eggs.

 **Castes**

 **Caste Larvae – Broodlings.**

Silithid Larvae, also known as Broodlings, are young silithid that as of yet have not been specialized for any role in the hive. Eventually they encase themselves in a cocoon and change into a new form depending on the needs of the Hive.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/rGDSjr6/Silithids02.jpg) ![Image](https://i.ibb.co/MDCfTYJ/Silithids02-5.jpg)
 *Pictured above are the two stages of a larva, above after having hatched, and below in their cocoon, slowly evolving into a new creature.*

They represent one of the most numerous castes of Silithid, and also one of the least aggressive. (Likely underdeveloped senses.)

 **Caste Light – Illuminators.**

Consisting of two species, named Lights and Pill bugs. The Illuminators act as nearly undeveloped larva in many ways, but their primary function is the illumination of the mazes of tunnels which make up the many hives, usually found clinging to walls, floors or ceilings. They show no aggressiveness even in close proximity to non-Silithid species.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/fnZdht0/Silithids03.jpg)
 *Image of a Light, their lighting capabilities originate from a glow source at the back of their bodies.*

Current numbers are unknown, but current estimations show a single Hive can possess from a few dozen to several hundred.

 **Caste Worker – Tunnelers.**

The Worker caste consists of four primary creature types, Workers, Tunnelers, Swarmers and Scarabs.
While similar, evidence shows a clear separation.

Workers primarily maintain the Hives themselves, constructing large towering spires, and seeing to the preservation of eggs and larva.

Tunnelers dig out ever spanning and far reaching cavernous pathways through which the large Silithid swarms can maneuver.

Scarabs act as the large levy force of the Silithid, being the most numerous of any caste, the footmen of their species, they are usually found guarding the outside of a Hive.

Swarmers act as a veteran sergeants for the Silithid, commanding many Scarabs to follow them into combating hostile threats, eliminating them early is likely to cause confusion or even a break of their swarm.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/rHRCMzp/Silithids04.jpg)
 *Pictured above is a Swarmer, nearly all members of this Caste grow to human height, and the width of an orc.*

 **Caste Wasp – Flyers.**

Silithid Wasps make up the aerial force of the Silithid, a mobile army always ruthlessly defending the Hive even until death, going into suicidal assaults simply to protect their own. They possess a large pair of insectoid wings allowing for impressive speeds and noise. Records indicate the noise of a squadron of Wasps is capable of being heard up to a kilometer away from their current position. While they do possess legs with which to perch themselves, they seem to serve no real combat purpose.

For weapons, they have evolved to use incredibly sharp nearly sword like claw limbs, and a large wasp like stinger, some Silithid within the region of Feralas have even developed an affinity for using venom to quickly take down even the most difficult of opponents. Swarm tactics are their primary means of combat.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/Z66YW6G/Silithids05.jpg)
 *Pictured above is a Wasp, a total wingspan is capable of reaching 3 meters in distance.*

 **Caste Reaver – Warriors.**

According to Kaldorei records, the primary fighting force of the Silithid is made up of Reavers, more humorously named ‘Battle Tanks’ by our Dwarven collegues. Their near vehicle like armor, horse like speed, and incredible strength make them the near perfect combatants.

While Scarabs defend the outside of the Hive, the primary defense of the inner hallways are left to the Reavers, who using their developed nightvision are capable of seeing even the most careful of infiltrators. Defensive in nature, they will rarely be persuaded into battle unfavorable for them, and will never advance past a position deemed necessary. With a weight easily going over 160 kilograms, their full charge combined with their thick exoskeleton allows for devastating penetrations through enemy lines.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/RvDTxwx/Silithis06.jpg)
 *Pictured above is a Reaver, patrolling the inner tunnels of a Hive.*

 **Currently researched Castes.**

The following castes are mentioned in name or in short description by our Kaldorei collegues, any further information is unknown:

 **Caste Queen**

Broodmothers of the Swarm, primary purpose is the laying of eggs.

 **Caste Colossi**

The largest of the Silithid, living battering rams for the swarm, they provide a means to combat larger foes when the many legions of the Swarm are unable. Records indicate monstrous growth of nearly 8 or 10 meters, armed with scythe like appendixes and near indestructible exoskeleton.

 **Caste Sand Reaver**

A potential variation on either the Tunnelers or Reavers, Sand Reavers seem to serve as larger tunnel  diggers.

 **Caste Statue**

While not Silithid themselves, Kaldorei records indicate the usage of two kinds of constructs in battle similar to Dwarven Golems, these are the Anubisaths and Obsidian Destroyers.

Anubisaths are described as towering stone constructs of immense physical might, capable of even downing dragons if given the chance. They possess the features of a jackal for a head, and the body of a near humanoid.

Obsidian Destroyers seem to appear in a near centaur like fashion of merging, between a horse and a lion. Their greatest usage is in the nullification and dispersion of magical energies.

 **Qiraji**

Currently not much is known about this secondary species, though unlike the Silithid, they seem to possess individual thought and a capability to act independently of the swarm. Kaldorei records indicate positions of Generals and Commanders of the Swarm, indicating a role of leadership. Whether they are their own caste within the Silithid hierarchy or their own species, is as of this moment unclear.

 **Countermeasures**

Current investigation into a possible way to combat the Swarm reveals a plant known as Morrowgrain, currently being studied by members of the Druidic Order of the Cenarion Circle. (Seek out Archdruid Fandral Staghelm for more information on this research.)

The disruption of antenna is likely to disrupt a connection to a supposed Hivemind, allowing for chaos to spread within Silithid ranks. (As of now, unproven.)

 **Conclusion**

With this information presented, gathered from researching history, biology and geography. I emplore the reader to carefully consider future methods of combating this threat, while preparing for a full on engagement. Recent activity shows that while they were once in a way imprisoned with the sands of Silithus, they have begun to spread and create new Hives all across Southern Kalimdor. It is a threat which cannot be ignored, lest the follies of our past return to haunt us.
 -Sir Aurelian Thomas Matheny, Knight of Theramore.
