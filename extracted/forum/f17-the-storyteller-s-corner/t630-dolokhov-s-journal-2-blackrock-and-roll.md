# Dolokhov's Journal - 2 - "Blackrock and Roll"

## Post 3220 by Thiccroy — 2019-06-27T20:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3220#p3220 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Twenty-seventh of Sixth Month, 30 ADP.**

"I dream of walls,
and hearth of wood.

A home of peace,
Both fair and good.

My love is there,
in Stromgarde's Halls.

Waves of the sea,
on Hills of Old.

And 'ere I went,
to lift my hand.

I left my heart,
upon the sand.

When duty's done,
and glory earned.

I swore my heart,
I would return."

I suppose I should start off with what I had been doing recently. Recently, for my reputable work, I was employed by the Magistrate of Redridge, Lord Solomon for three things. Luckily, I had my friend and trustful ally, Gallegund, with me.

I and Gallegund were tasked with bringing back a lost soldier to Lakeshire, and tracking down and killing two warlords in the Blackrock ranks. Gath'Ilzogg and Taran'zul. Both were monstrous and large, as the descriptions went. They did not do them justice. Choosing to rescue the soldier first, we set out north, through the burnt valleys bordering the Burning Steppes. Along the way, I was interested in how they came to be so.

Gallegund told me histories I really should have learned. His people were once under a mighty High King, three centuries ago, before the King died and the three clans of the Dwarven race split in a three-sided war. Amidst it all, the foolish Dark Irons released a mighty God of Fire, called 'Ragnaros'. So strange. Why would they willingly put themselves to slavery and destroy so much beauty?

Either way, we fought our way through sorcerers of dark magic and demons, and various warriors in lackluster mail and leather. We swiftly found Corporal Keeshan within the depths of a mine. He was hurt, on his last legs, but he saved me from a near decapitation. He had some fight in him. A man who had skill even after losing his comrades and much blood. I must respect the tenacity of these Azerothians.

Handing him to one of the Guards, we took our reward and set our way to our most challenging moment yet. The Castle, Stonewatch, once stood proud during the First War and before. However, the Orcs had razed it, and the Blackrock had not left it since. Within we found Taran'zul in its courtyard. The warriors and sorcerers around him were great and powerful, outfitted and trained better than the rest.

Indeed, it was a hard and bloody fight, with near deaths with both of us. Soon enough, Taran's head was ours, and we dived into the Garrison itself. Its rickety floor, old and burnt in some spots, creaked at each step, and so, the Blackrocks came in like a furious tidal wave when hearing us. Dispatching them with cunning tactics of close quarter combat and hallways, we made our way up to the War Room, where Gath'Ilzogg was waiting.

If it was naught for Gallegund cutting off the beast's arm, we would not have made it out of there. He had a fiery black dragon with him, a whelp. I can still feel his heat on me. Hmph. Either way, the Warlord was dead, his head ours. We quickly escaped as more reinforcements came, and soon found ourselves resting in the Inn.

After that, Gallegund, for rewarding me for 'getting out of that alive', he gave me new armor. He showed me his skills in blacksmithing, forging a mighty blade that did not burn on the inside, but burned your hand when you grasped it too long and spewed forth flame like a dragon at each swing! Marvelous! I must learn more about the art of hammer and anvil. The Dwarves have secrets and arts I -must- explore and know!

Now is night. I hear the patter of soft rain, and the jingle within my coin purse makes me happy. Hope and justice is on my side, and I am getting closer to my goal.

I swore my heart that I would return.
