# Illusion Roleplay?: Furbolg

## Post 101421 by Moongirl — 2024-09-17T21:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101421#p101421 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Hello, everyone! I hope you are having a nice day!**

I find it fun when I encounter someone using an *Illusion to roleplay*. I made this forum post for people who wish to roleplay as an Illusion, what things should they keep in mind to develop a backstory or personality! In this particular one, I'll be focusing on a ***Furbolg***.

In case you do not know, Furbolgs are the bear humanoids usually found in Night Elven areas. This is because the Furbolgs were once allies with the Night elves, however most have lost their minds to corruption. The Timbermaw Furbolgs are one of the few sane ones left. Furbolgs seem to be in tune with nature like Night elves, though many are Shamans instead of Druids.

One can get a Furbolg Illusion in the donation shop, but can also turn in a **Keg of Thunderbrew Lager** to Grizzlore during the Winter Veil Vale. Alliance can exchange Hops to Grimbooze Thunderbrew, while Horde may buy it from Innkeeper Karakul in Swamp of Sorrows.

 **Be sure to tell me of anything of note about Furbolgs so I may update this post!** You are also welcome to make a small description of your own Furbolg character below. Thank you for reading, and take care on your journey!   smiling_turtle_head
