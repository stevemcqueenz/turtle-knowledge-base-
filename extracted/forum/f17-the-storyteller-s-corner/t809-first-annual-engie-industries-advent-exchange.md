# First Annual Engie Industries Advent Exchange

## Post 4272 by Qixel (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-10-22T04:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4272#p4272 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sitting at her desk, the half-goblin stashed her right hand into her pocket and jangled some gold coins. The sound always helped to focus her, but her mind still wandered - it *was* that time of year again, she supposed. *Clink, clink, clink.* Chin cupped in her left hand, she tapped her cheek absently. *Clink, clink, clink. Tap, tap, tap.*

"Arrrrgh!" She suddenly stood up and ran her fingers frantically through her hair. She looked around her office, desperate for something to distract her. "Stupid, wasteful, pointless!" She muttered angrily as she thought again about the upcoming festivities. More specifically, what she wanted to do. *Didn't want to do!* "No, no, no!" She paced angrily around the room.

Although she prided herself on her goblin heritage, Engie was just as much a gnome, and the jubilant festivities had always enthralled her, whether she liked it or not. And that wouldn't be such a *problem* - indeed, holidays were a fantastic chance to make some gold! - if not for the fact that every year she was overcome by some stupid urge to give stuff away. *For free!* There's no profit in giving away your product!

 *'Everything has a price.'* Those words were both the slogan for her company, as well as the words she lived by. Indeed, everything had a price, be it goods, equipment, or services. And to just.... *give it away*? Uggggh. Her skin crawled at the prospect. And yet, here she was, fighting that exact urge. It was as if she were splitting in two, and each part of herself was warring over what to do.

She screamed in frustration again, then suddenly froze. "Wait. What if I could do both?" She murmured softly. The half-gnome quickly sat back down at her desk and opened a drawer to grab a sheet of paper. The logic was contradictory - to give things away, but to gain a profit in doing so. It was nonsense, but nonsense was often *incredibly* lucrative. A way to satisfy both her gnomish need for jubilant festivities, and her goblin need for some cash.

She grinned. "Yeah, I can do this."

She set about drawing up a poster for the....

What is this? Engie has decided to throw a big, month long party? What has possessed that little half-gnome of such an audacious idea? She normally keeps to herself unless someone is paying her for her company. Could it be Winter Veil festivities on the mind?

Indeed it is. Engie will be giving a free gift away each day of December to those who meet with her, ostensibly in order to advertise her company.

_________________________________________________________________________________________

 **F.A.Q.**

Q: So, your roleplay bits are hard to understand. Can we break character a moment and find out what all this is?
A: Sure. Basically, when I was a kid, I played this game called 'Neopets'. And one of the things I looked forward to every year was the 'Advent Calendar', a little event held in december where you could claim a prize every day. Recently, while I was slaving around as an adult working retail, I remembered the joy from those simpler times, and wanted to share it with everyone here. And thus, the Advent Exchange was conceived.

Q: So you're gonna be giving free stuff?
A: Yup. Although it won't be as well organized as neopets had it, of course. Instead, I've been preparing and will continue to prepare items in wrapped gifts to hand out to people. I'm randomizing them as I do them, too, so even I don't know what's in the gifts anymore! How exciting!

Q: Well, I mean, surely you remember some of the items you wrap. Can you give us some teasers as to what's in the box(es)?
A: Well, equipment of varying levels, recipes, and vouchers for gold so far. I'm probably going to add vouchers for other things that can't be wrapped, too. Maybe even turtle tokens! :O

Q: Well, that all sounds very exciting, but with you being a filthy american, you're never online when I am! And even when you are, your shifts are all over the place! What if I don't see you a particular day?
A: Trust me, I get you. In the case that you can't find Engie in-game, be it because schedules didn't align, or maybe Engie was slacking off that day, you can post a response in this thread, claiming your gift for the day. Engie will mail it to you.

Q: Wait, what's stopping me from just posting in the thread for free stuff?
A: Honestly, nothing. I'd like to see you in-game (roleplay optional), but I cannot force you, and I'm not gonna punish anyone for not being able to be on at the same time as me.

Q: Anything I can do to help?
A: Collecting presents is, admittedly, a *lot* of work. Anything you can donate to the present stash would be appreciated. I'm not expecting anything, and ideally I won't need help, but if you want to donate, I'll make a list of people who have contributed in this post. <3

_________________________________________________________________________________________

 **Event Details**

 **When is it?:**
Each day of December (Keep in mind, I basically run on Pacific Standard Time, so my idea of when a day ends will likely be different from everyone else. I'll try to be as accommodating as possible, though.)

 **What do I do?**
Either meet with Engie in-game to receive your present (roleplay is encouraged, but not required; just don't be a dick to those who /are/ roleplaying), or post in the thread with your character's name.

 **Where do I find Engie?**
Engie will be handing out gifts in the Winter Veil Vale!
