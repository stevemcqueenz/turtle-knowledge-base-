# The Official Roleplaying Hubs

## Post 1078 by Parcival (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2018-11-01T13:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1078#p1078 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/jRGTfve.png)

> ***role play;*** act out or perform the part of a person or character   ***hub;*** a center of activity

 ***The community has spoken!*** In the open two weeks voting process for the communities own official Roleplaying Hub (*Alliance* & *Horde* respectively), [which can be viewed in its locked format here](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=17&t=111), 16 players gave their votes - *the results are as seen in the above image.* I have compiled the numbers for each player suggested area, visible in the quote below.

> For the Alliance, 7 votes were given for Stormwind; Old Town. 2 votes were for Wetlands; Menethil Harbor Inn, 2 votes for Stormwind; Park and 1 vote for Naxxramas; Kel'Thuzad's Chamber.

> For the Horde, 6 votes were given for The Barrens; Crossroads and 1 vote for Durotar; Valley of Trials.

The agreed upon Roleplaying Hubs for Alliance & Horde, can now be reached directly through the use of the server's [Portable Meeting Stone], teleporting the player to their factions hub. [For more information regarding the item, please visit this official server announcement.](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=192)

> **Bob wrote:**
> It's all fancy yea, but what can I do in an RP Hub, that I can't do anywhere else?

 *Good question Bob*. The Roleplaying Hubs are by far, not the only suggested way/area to Roleplay. If a player requires another location for Roleplay - more suited to his character development arc, he is so willing and urged to choose. The Hub offers an immersive and lore-friendly way to introduce the new Roleplaying character of the player to others alike. To begin his adventure in the breathing and living world of Azeroth.

Your faction Roleplaying Hub, allows you to:
- create new character relationships with other players

- begin your personal story arc

- join or even create an in-character guild/organization/party

- search for in-character adventure through the advertisement of other players or make your own

- or simply lay back and relax, after a hard day of epic quests
When you feel Roleplay cravings, wish to extend the list of players your character interacts with or simply strive for something bigger - then buy that flight path to your faction's Roleplaying Hub or conveniantly use your [Portable Meeting Stone] and begin your adventure!

 **For anything regarding Roleplay (searching or offering some), use the official Roleplaying channel (if you haven't yet joined it, simply do /join Roleplay).**
