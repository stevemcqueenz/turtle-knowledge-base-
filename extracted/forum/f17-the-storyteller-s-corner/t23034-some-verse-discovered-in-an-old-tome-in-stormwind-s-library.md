# Some verse, discovered in an old tome in Stormwind's library.

## Post 160721 by Bobosnatcher — 2025-11-26T00:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160721#p160721 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Eggs for Breakfast
A warlock's tale

With a rumble and grumble my tummy did speak
As I sat up in bed so I let out a shriek
‘Sarnos, I’m hungry, I’ll brook no delay!
Your turn to fix us some breakfast this day.’

Up on my bed hopped my most faithful friend
A demon no bigger than gnomes, I’ll contend
Short on good looks and a temper to boot
He still has a charm that you can't refute

‘Xer grugin mo kaaza!’ I heard him to say
‘The pantry is bare, we’ve no viands today.'
A terrible state of affairs to be sure
But this clever imp said he knew of the cure

‘I know of a place where there’s eggs to be had
Big, tasty eggs that will make us most glad
It’s but a short journey by land and by sea
The most splendid treat awaits us, I decree.’

‘Sarnos, you dear, go and saddle my steed
Let’s waste no more time and set forth with all speed!’
I climbed upon Toasty, my mount all a-fire
When mornings are cold it’s his heat I admire

We followed the road up into the high pass
We followed it down and across the wet grass
The sun was well up by the time we arrived
At Menethil Bay where I’d hoped to revive

'Let's stop for a snack at the inn over here
If I don't eat soon I'll pass out, I do fear.'
'No time to tarry!' said Sarnos, in haste
'The ship will leave soon.' and so for it we raced

Captained by none and yet this craft set sail
Hoping to catch a good glimpse of a whale
We suddenly found ourselves already there
Such a short trip and not even a fare

Leaping astern Sarnos dived off the boat
I tried to stay dry but I didn’t get a vote
Casting a spell so that I might swim deep
'Round the sea monsters we both had to creep

At last back on land my companion went on
I asked if we're close 'cause my patience was gone
‘It’s just up ahead.’ Then he sprinted out front
Like two hungry worgs we were, out on the hunt

‘In here! In here are the eggs to be found.
So many eggs and so big they astound!’
I looked at that imp thinking he must be nuts
To go in this cave yet it shows he’s got guts

Venturing in we then sneaked down a path
The air grew so warm, just like a steam bath
Rounding a corner I had quite a fright
Blocking our way was a creature not slight

Decidedly nasty in looks and in humour
My glance darted this way and that for a detour
Quickly I quaffed a fine potion I’d made
For any close by out of sight I would fade

Holding my breath I then prayed to Elune
Helping us now would be most opportune
Dashing for it I got by the big brute
Close on my heels, my imp followed suit

The path soon gave way to a cavern wide
What I saw there it made me want to hide
'Sarnos, you devil.'  I whispered right then
'You've led us into a large dragon's den!'

'Don't worry 'bout her.'  said my friend with a smile
'Her belly's full and she'll sleep quite a while.'
I wondered what poor sods had ended up prey
He told me a raid had just been here this day

Taking my hand, Sarnos led me close by
Tiptoeing, hoping our deaths were not nigh
In a small alcove just off to the side
I had a sight that made my eyes grow wide

Eggs of the like of which I've never seen.
Eggs such as these would make splendid cuisine!
Scrambled or poached, maybe good even fried
I'd make an omelet that's famous worldwide.

But just as I reached out my hands to take one
A stirring and thumping to my ears had come
Breaking wide open with noise and much drama
Out popped a creature that said to me 'momma?'

This egg then that egg then all the eggs hatched
Suddenly I felt that we were outmatched
Three dozen eyes and they all looked to me
Thinking that I was their mommy, oh gee

'Sarnos,' I said, and I started to pout
'this is one meal we could well do without.'
But when I turned there was noone to hear
Far away now, he was running in fear

'Where are you going?'  I shouted at him
Frustration with it all starting to brim
Just then I happened to look overhead
Instantly all thoughts of our mission fled

Coming at me with a shriek that split stone
A nightmare, and one of the worst ever known
I recognized now what I earlier missed
This was Onyxia right in our midst!

Panic struck me and I ran to and fro
This way and that with the whelpings in tow
All I had wanted was something to munch
It started to look as if I would be lunch

Taking a deep breath, Onyxia glared
Clutching Sarnos, I was never so scared
Right at that moment I heard a request
A summons to home at another's behest!

Just as that dragon sent forth a hot blast
My imp and I portaled out of there fast
Finding ourselves in the park of Stormwind
Facing our saviors to whom we just grinned

Smoke rising from me as if I was roasted
Looking a little like marshmallow toasted
'I had a feeling' said Starbreeze my friend
'you might need help and a hand we should lend.'

'Doesn't take our Pris long' laughed Mal, her mate.
'to find herself with too much on her plate.'
Glad to be safe I gave them both a hug
Gave thanks also to the warlock named Doug.

Their fortunate timing just then became clear
I'd made a date to break fast with them here!
Taking a table at the nearest inn
Sarnos in highchair with bib under chin

So hungry now I could eat a murloc
Tasty or yucky, the food I wouldn't knock
But when they served up the special du jour
I knew my appetite was gone for sure:

Soft-boiled eggs and hash.  Yummers......
