# The Dread

## Post 2689 by Kazgrim (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2019-05-05T00:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2689#p2689 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Blistering winds swept across the red plains of Durotar. Kazgrim raised his hand to block the blood stained silt from blowing into his eyes. The only voice louder than the howling air was the shrieks echoing from within the caves behind him. They were the shrieks of orcs and demons. As Kazgrim wandered off into the wastes, the dust before him began to recede. In front of him appeared the gates of the capital city, Orgrimmar. Hidden beneath the shadow of his cloak, he passed the guards and soon lost himself within the gathering crowds flooding the streets. The hot sun bore down on his shoulders and back like an iron weight before he escaped into the shade of The Drag. There, he made his way down the dark corridors and alleyways until he found himself at the entrance of the Cleft of Shadow. It was there that his master waited eagerly for him.

His master, an old, grey-haired orc, sat cross legged, his back turned towards Kazgrim. The room was filled with strange inscriptions, marked on the walls and floors. Scrolls hung from the ceiling. Written on them were runes that not even Kazgrim could decipher. His master had said nothing while Kazgrim stood there, his attention fixated on another. In front of him was a fire, but it did not burn as ordinary fire. This fire was made of fel magic, and in it Kazgrim's master saw more than than just flames.

An image of a cloaked figure appeared, emerging from the shadows. The dancing flames showed this figure walking towards Kazgrim's master. With every step he took, the fire grew brighter, and its crackling rose from a whisper to a roar. He could see a blade revealed under the man's arm, its steel glowing with fire. The figure slowly reached for his dagger, pressing softly against its hilt before drawing it from its scabbard. The flames at this point began to roar uncontrollably, and Kazgrim's master found difficulty in seeing what was happening. In flashes he saw the figure nearing, his face becoming more recognizable with each flame. The fire erupts, showing the face of the man only briefly before dying into a pillar of smoke. Kazgrim's master strained to read from the embers that remained. In a moment of realization he shouted but no voice came out. The only thing to leave his mouth was blood, which now softly dripped into his lap. He looked up to see the shadowy figure above him, whose dagger had plunged into his backside. A grin revealed his assassin. The old orc's last words stuttered, his breath barely escaping, to ask what he was doing.

Kazgrim knelt against his master, whispering into his ears.

 *"Galtak Ered'nash."*
