That's what I'm doing now, selling greenies, bags, copper and leather (I chose mining+skinning for this sole reason). Also got my hand on some recipe. It's unfortunate the game has no way to let me create a sort of catalog people can choose from, but I will make it work without it :-)

And in the future I will probably have, as you said, to rely more on main cities/hubs. First thing will be to expand my bag capabilities (with the first earnings I already bought all the possible 8 slot bags) and farm most useful common thing.

Another useful and funny thing is to exchange goodies instead of selling; for example, I sold to a blacksmith a recipe in exchange of 6 pairs of copper bracers: super cool interaction and a lot more valuable to sell!
