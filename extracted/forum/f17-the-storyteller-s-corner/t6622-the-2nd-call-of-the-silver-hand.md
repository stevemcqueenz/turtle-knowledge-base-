**The 2nd Call of the Silver Hand**
 *To all Knights of the Silver Hand, Argent Dawn, Scarlet Crusaders and other heroes of the Alliance and Azeroth. You have been summoned to Southshore on the evening of evening of Wednesday the 26th of April for the second iteration of the Call of the Silver Hand.*
 **Event Details**
 **Where**-*Southshore, Hillsbrad Foothills*
 **When**-*18:00 Wednesday the 26th. 19:00 Meeting Time.*
 **IC Agenda**
 **19:00** - **Gathering and Conversation**
 **20:00** - **Opening Speech and Prayer**
 **20:05** - **Reports from the units and the assembly**
 **~20:50** - **Meeting Conclusion**
When the formalities of the meeting come to a close, you may retire to the outskirts of Southshore to discuss the reports, engage in training with your fellow knights, or perhaps take a tour of one of the last bastions of the Alliance in the North.
While the Call serves primarily as an opportunity for the Silver Hand and Paladin roleplayers and guilds to meet up and get to know each other, the meeting also mantains the tradition of welcoming participation from allies and friends of the Silver Hand. In that sense, representatives of the Church of the Holy Light, Alliance and other orders are welcome to send representatives.
Yes! The Call of the Silver Hand is back, our focus for the 2nd iteration of it being bringing the Argent Dawn and Scarlet Crusade in closer to the concept, in order to add more flavour and spice to the Paladin RP-scene. Although it still remains a mostly social event, and an opportunity to catch up with your fellow knights and friends, we also want to enhance the experience of it by adding some duelds/pilgrimage/other activity immediately after the main event.
 **Also, make sure to join the Call of the Silver Hand Discord server!** <https://discord.gg/h7Zs5mHZZB>
