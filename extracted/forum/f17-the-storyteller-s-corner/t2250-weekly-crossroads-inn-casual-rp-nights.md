# Weekly Crossroads Inn Casual RP Nights

## Post 14033 by Pistol (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2021-11-21T00:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14033#p14033 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/903971535780466740/909521293408366642/WoWScrnShot_111421_134138.jpg)

 **WHEN: Every Sunday at 6 pm Server Time** (Excluding holidays.)

 **WHERE: The Crossroads, of course**

This is intended for Horde role-players to get to know each other in a more casual setting. Eat, drink and chat around the fire. Food and alcohol will be sold at reasonable prices. Given that these are  casual events, being early or late is fine with me.

Hosted and bartended by Varya of <Forsaken Vanguard>

P.S. The screenshot is from the November 14th Crossroads Inn RP Night.
