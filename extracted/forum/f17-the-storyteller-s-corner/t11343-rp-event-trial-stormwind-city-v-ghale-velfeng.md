**Trial - Stormwind City v. Ghale Velfeng**

> Ghale Velfeng, accused criminal and traitor to the Grand Alliance, is being put on trial in the High Court of Chancery for the following crimes and misdemeanours:
>
>  Robbery
>  Larceny
>  Kidnapping and Abduction
>  Resisting Lawful Arrest
>  High Treason
>
>  Magistrate presiding over the case is Chancellor Waylon Holt.

Perform your civic duty and be in the masses - free rotten fruit will be handed out to be pelted at the defendant and/or the judges! The trial will take place in the Court House, located in the depths of the Dwarven District!

When: Friday 3rd of November, 21:00 Server Time
Where: Court House, Dwarven District, Stormwind City
