# The Roleplay Hub

## Post 622 by Parcival (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2018-10-09T11:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=622#p622 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Having discussed in-game our RP options with some players, it came to my mind to be best to bring this to your attention.

THE ROLEPLAY HUB

There's been word on the forums of a server wide, unified RP timeline - and it is something we as Roleplayers on this server should strive for. In time, more guilds will emerge, maybe some only RP focused, as well as already existing and Roleplaying Roleplayers, who already engaged in RP on this server and are slowly building their story archs.

So, my idea is to propose, for the time being, an official Roleplay Hub, for Roleplayers who haven't yet engaged into RP with others, or who already have, to be able to meet at this place (which we will choose) to help kickstart Roleplay on the server. Before we can begin any big events which tie to a unified RP, server wide timeline, we need to build relationships between our characters and even groups/guilds.

What this entails is:
 ***1. An Official Roleplaying Hub*** (in one of the main cities or any place, which can be easily accessed, to act as a safe zone)
 ***2. An Official Roleplaying World Chat*** (/join Roleplay - as an example, which can be used for OOC communication on a faction wide basis for announcements, questions, etc. NOT intended for OOC chit chat between IC engaged characters - keep that to the party or in brackets)
 ***3. An Official Timeline Forum Topic*** (to archive any big changes in the world of Azeroth, so already engaged Roleplayers or new ones, can easily look up what has already been done, and abide by it) = of course, this last one, will emerge once events begin to appear)

Thus please, write in your reply YOUR FACTION and YOUR PROPOSSED RP HUB [GENERAL ZONE; DISTRICT](for your faction, of course). An example can be found below:

> Alliance, Stormwind; Old Town

Please do not write your additional ideas for Roleplay in this topic - we will open another topic for those, for better organization. If you want to comment on the ideas I've presented above, divide the initial information with a line, and write your comment down below. Example follows:

> Alliance, Stormwind; Old Town
>  ___________________________________
>  I don't like the idea of organized RP, it's like you know, corporation. We should be free to RP wherever we want, so yes.

 **The Roleplay Hub will be selected** for each faction **at the end of the 14 days period** (*23. 10. 2018*) **based upon the most noted location**. (Which results I shall again present, and we can decide finally on the 23th of October the RP hubs for each faction).
