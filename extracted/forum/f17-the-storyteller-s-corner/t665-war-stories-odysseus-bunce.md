# War Stories:Odysseus Bunce

## Post 3401 by Leoren — 2019-07-09T22:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3401#p3401 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is the journal of a Stromic Noble,

Page 1.

I am Odysseus Bunce, noble and mage of Stromgarde, I hail from the House of Bunce. I signed up to the Alliance army together with my three brothers, it has been years since the second war ended and the Orcish Horde was defeated. The Various human kingdoms are at peace and the elves keep to themselves ever ignorant to the politics of their southern comrades. The Orcs in the interment camps are as lifeless as ever, carrying on every day without meaning however there are orcs who refuse to be bound to their fate of slavery and are revolting, freeing others from the interment camps and raiding human villages. Seeking glory and honor for the family my brothers signed up for the Alliance army. Due to their noble heritage and the history of our house they were trained as commanders, to lead the infantry of the armies in combat and to serve under generals. I myself being more of a bookworm from the days I spent in dim light of the candles reading various books on history and magic. Had been studying any magic I could, even having visited Silvermoon in many occasions to talk to the mages of the famous city and perhaps even study under them. I was instructed to accompany the mages of Dalaran that were in our army group to assist and fight alongside them. Our training was done a week ago and we are being sent to put down a uprising in the Arathi Highlands. I wonder what kind of Orcs we'll be fighting.

Page 2.

The Orcs were far more organised than we originaly expected. We were only informed that the orcs were rebelling inside the interment camp and had taken over the camp, but what we instead arrived to was a organised operation, the orcs had help from the outside and were clearly part of a greater operation and band of orcs, they not only took the camp but fortified it, making it into a base for those savages. Upon arrival our forces were ordered to engage a full assault on the camp, but the orcs had weaved foul magics inside the very walls of the camp and our siege engines struggled to break them. Luckily it was no real castle otherwise we would have suffered even greater casaulties than we had. The commander of the dalaran portion of the army, to which I was assigned suggested that the mages of our army could weave a spell that would not only break the spell upon the walls but also breach them. With that being decided as our course of action we were lucky to succeed, but the orcs nonetheless continued their resistance. We took the Camp and took no prisoners, the orcs had tasted freedom and paid their price. And we paid a heavy price in the form of sacrifice of our soldiers who died in the assault for the continuation of peace. For now we are to be stationed in the camp, but talk amongst the commanders is that we'll be sent north to Lordaeron, who knows, I hope I'll see Capital City, uncreative name really.

Page 3.

It's good that they supply soldiers and the like with anything they require... but we are running low on certain supplies. I just hope that we resupply quickly. On other news we are moving rather quickly through Silverpine Forest. We just passed Shadowfang Keep today, I remember visiting it as a kid, Father always did make sure to take all of his sons with him on important trips. Otherwise nothing really is going on that is interesting. We passed the town of Soutshore with no problem and some men visited Tarren Mill to spend some time with their families. We're part of a diverse army made up of Dwarves from Dun Morogh, Humands from Stromgarde and Lordaeron, shame that the men from Gilneas were recalled due to the policies of King Greymane, they were good people so I guess it's best for them to leave. Most of the mages are from Dalaran ofcourse, but for some reason a few mages from Silvermoon have joined our ranks. A lot of the men are made nervous by this but the commander says that it's just the High Elves doing their minimum effort. who knows? We can see Capital City in the distance, and some say that Prince Arthas and Uther the Lightbringer dealt with an orc raid. Hopefully the Orcs will be put a stop to soon. I wonder if We'll meet them on the way to our next post.

Page 4.

We reached the town outside of Capital City today, but strange news come from the nearby towns. People that fled to Brill (The town we are currently stationed at) are saying that the end of Azeroth has come, that people started getting sick in their villages after the grain shipments came in. And soon after that people started dying, more disturbingly they report that the people that died after getting sick somehow got back up and started attacking people. Whether or not these claims are true or not I think will be revealed soon. We had a meeting about this(I was suprised I was invited but I suspect my brothers were responsible for this.) and my brother Amaran was sent to investigate the nearby villages with a small detachment of our forces to put our mens hearts to rest. The sun will soon set and we are to move out soon, I'll end this page here.

Page 5.

It was madness! I was awoken during the night in my chambers by a loud knock on the door. One of the captains was sent to wake the mages and he informed me that peasants were dying left and right. The dead were rising and attacking the town, I quickly gathered my things and sallied out with the other mages. I couldn't believe it the undead were attacking from within the town, the villagers and soldiers who died rose up again to fight us. With fire and frost we tried to hold the undead back. Luckily we survived the night but lost quite a few men. We're forced to recruit from the locals who did survive the night to replenish our forces. The rumors were true and it turns out that some of the Dalaran mages were somewhat informed about the odd shipments. We'll be forced to stay in Brill for a while more and the men are working on fortifying it in case more of the undead attack. There has been no news or messengers from by brothers Amaran's forces. And I hope that he's alright, afterall the claims of the peasants were proven to be true. One of my brothers argued for immediatly setting out to look for Amaran but he was denied, we would have rode for his forces if only there was not the threat of desertion. We'll be sleeping with weary and worried hearts tonight.

Page 6.

To our suprise many of the men from the town, and even many more refugees joined our forces. The day was spent training them and equiping them, the smithies were working day and night for our forces to have enough weapons and armor. The scouts had reported a large mob of the undead nearby one of the villages but they were not able to see if the force was of former soldiers or just a mob of undead peasants. Although I hope it's not Amaran's detachment there is a very high possibility that it's his force. We'll be setting out for it soon. We haven't got any orders from the General and the news from the Kingdom of Lordaeron that we are currently stationed in are not boding well. The grain is plagued and our supplies are being heavily rationed. The other mages say that the source is magical, maybe we can find a cure to this illness? Whatever it is that is raising the undead it reminds me of the stories of how the orcs used the undead to wage their war against the Alliance. Orc Necrolytes they were called.

Page 7.

My suspicions were correct, we encountered the undead force that was made out of my brothers Amaran's forces and whatever undead gathered to them. I hoped that he had survived and escaped but no, We engaged the undead on a flat terrain and their numbers seemed endless. the men that were slain rose again to bolster their numbers and we were fighting a losing battle. many undead fell to flame and the arcane with many mages conjuring elementals to bolster our forces since they could not turn those against us once they were slain. In the end we saw my brother now an undead commander leading the forces, we were forced to retreat. We heard of Prince Arthas' forces in the east marching for Stratholme. Hoping to join up with them and perhaps ending this madness and scourge that has befallen the North. Perhaps the paladins will prove a powerful weapon in this fight.

Page 8.

We could not believe our eyes when we arrived in Stratholme, the city was set ablaze and those we encountered said that the crown prince of Lordaeron grew mad and set aflame to the city in paranoia. Our commander met with the Sorceress Jaina Proudmoore, daughter of the Kul Tiran king and apprentice to Andromath. We will be sailing west for some reason. I don't know what kind of madness this is but orders are orders. The undead hordes of Lordaeron are growing larger and we wont be able to handle them much longer. Arthas has gone North in his mad quest to slay some sort of "Dreadlord". We'll be heading to a port near Capital city to sail past Maelstrom. Some men thought about deserting our forces but were quickly reminded of what awaited them in the wilds of Lordaeron. I hope alls well in Stromgarde.

Page 9.

We set sail for a land to the west, there's so many citizens.... Hoping to escape the hellfire that they left and I can't blame them for being so hasty to abandon their homeland.  Even now news are spreading that the demons have returned to Azeroth to set it ablaze. We have enough supplies to last the massive fleet for a few month. It's a wonder that we were able to gather so much. The land that we leave behind may be the last that we ever do. We know that this is a risk, but we made our choice. I just hope Lady Proudmoore is right and there is a continent to the west which is on no known map. We're counting on finding it soon to keep up the morale.

Page 10.

Landfall! We have made it to what the men are starting to call Kalimdor. But the land is a harsh one, we landed in a Desert which showed few signs of plantlife. We were ordered to move west in a mountainous area to build a fortress and seek out a Oracle. I myself would not trust any peddler of magic, much less in a strange land that has been just discovered. Scouts report that orcs were spotted in the nearby desert that we landed in, having sailed here with human ships. Perhaps they followed us? No matter, if they come for us we'll be able to cast the green savages back into the tides!.

Page 11.

The Orcish Horde, lead by a invidual called Thrall (Calling their Warchief a slave? These Orcs are strange) attacked us. Their forces burned down the fortress to the ground and we were forced to flee into the caverns, many suprises were found in these caverns but in the end we made an uneasy alliance with the Orcs.... I do not know what madness has befallen upon them but the Orcs seem to have the same goal as us. We're being sent North to a forested region to set up a base. I dont trust these orcs but I was made commander recently. I was placed in charge of a expedition force made out of Humans and Orcs alike. I hope we can get this over with quickly before the orcs decide to break their promise and betray us. My brothers were sent on similiar missions, my brother Garadol further up north, and the youngest of us Barrdol was sent east.

Page 12.

We were attacked whilst gathering lumber for our fortress, the assailants were similiar to the High Elves of Silvermoon but their skin were of different shades of purple and they seemed to refuse the use of the arcane. We defeated their forces but were later assailed by much worse enemies. The demons had tracked us to this continent and laid siege to our newly built bases. We barely held out and I had sent a missive to the main force for reinforcements. I had also sent news of the Elven natives which had attacked us to Lady Proudmoore although I am sure they already know of them.

Page 13.

I do not know what is going on in her head but Lady Proudmoore has made yet another unreasonable alliance, this time with these "Night Elves". She has called for our forces to assemble on the tallest mountain on Kalimdor called Mount Hyjal to defend some kind of Tree from the Demons. Although I can recognise magical places and their worth it's still insane. If the elves had their way they would have us all die there. I must follow orders though and I am glad I will be able to rejoin my brothers Garadol and Barrdol.

Page 14.

The defense of Mount Hyjal proved costly, my own brother Barrdol was slain when the demons breached our base. But he fought like ten lions, like a true stromic hero he paid with his life so we could buy time for the elves and their plan. Bit by bit we were beaten back suffering ever greater casaulties assaulted by demons and undead alike we were beaten back to the orcish base and even further until we were near the Elves holy tree. The leader of the Demons, Lord Archimonde I believe he was called fell in battle, we had won the battle and our duty was done. We traveled to a land we would eventually call Dustwallow Marsh and established a city called Theramore. It'll be good to rest our bones. and to lay to rest those we have lost, including Barrdol.

Page 15.

It has been months since the legions defeat, we've had to hunt down any remaining demon forces and it seems the uneasy alliance or well, rather truce persists. The orcs were allowed to build their infernal city in the north which they have started to call Orgrimmar. We've established a working relationship with the Night Elves and have even begun trading with them. I've grown to mingle with many of them and although I disagree with many of them, I have grown to respect them. It's quiet and peaceful for now, and we have regained contact with the Human Kingdoms and the rest of the alliance. It's horrible what happened, Arthas has destroyed Lordaeron and Silvermoon, Dalaran has fallen and I don't even know what's happening to Stromgarde. I hope news reach us soon.

Page 16.

I was suprised by who of all people showed up to Theramore of all people, Lord Admiral Daelin Proudemoore himself. A hero of the alliance, he called for all those who wished revenge upon the Horde and with no suprise many joined them. The Orcish menace has to be wiped out one way or another. This is just putting them out of their misery, my brother Garadol agreed to coming with Daelin's forces when I asked him. Were making plans on attacking Durotar, and we have already taken the Echo Isles. We'll be moving on to Orgrimmar soon to finish the job.

Page 17.

The Orcs beat us back, it seems some sort of abomination, a filthy breed of Ogre and Orc lead the Orcish forces, bolstered by ogres trolls and tauren alike to combat against us. My brother Garadol fell to that blasted brutes hand and we couldn't even retrieve his body. This is a lost cause and we have retreated to Theramore. I wont waste my time on Daelins crusade anymore, I'm getting back to Stromgarde one way or another. And after that to Lordaeron, I will find what happened to my brother Amaran and hopefully lay his spirit to rest.

This was written by Odysseus Bunce, last of House Bunce and the son of Strom.
