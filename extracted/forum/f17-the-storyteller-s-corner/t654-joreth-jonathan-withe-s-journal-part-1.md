It has been a couple days since i got to eastern kingdoms. And alot has happened I asked some people to tell me where i would be able to explore and do assignment's and everyone said elwynn forest. I talked to marshal Dughan and he was happy about me helping him with a couple assignment's. I had to explore an mine that was full of kobolds. They weren't hard to kill or scare away. As I was ready with that i went to explore and i found an farm. I helped an woman with her love troubles by making an invisibility potion i also helped an nice old lady find her necklace. An kobold had found it and i killed the kobold.

The next day I got an invitation letter from an stromic warrior called Dolokhov Steelblood to an meeting at pig and whistle inn. I accepted the invite by writing back an letter. After that I went to east because marshal Dughan said that I would be needed there. I helped the people there by finding two lost guard's and dealing with the local murlock problem. Then i killed alot of wolves. After that i went west to westfall but before that i saw an wanted poster of Hogger he was an gnoll warlord. Me, an paladin and two other warriors took care of him in no time. After collecting the rewards of that assignment I resumed my way to west. I helped the Furlbrows  and got an nice and warm new cape. After that i went to the Saldean farm and slept at their house.

Today I woke up at the Saldeans house i thanked their hospitality by dealing with the malfunctioning harvest watcher's. Then Salma Saldean taught me to make goretusk liver pie and westfall stew after I gave her the ingredients. After a good meal i went to sentinel hill to help the people's militia by dealing with some defias. After that it was around five and i flew to stormwind city with the gryphon master's gryphon. I went to the pig and whistle to meet Dolokhov and i found him at an table with an old drunk priest called John. Soon an wizard called Odysseus Bunce joined us. After that another man came in to the bar. The man was called Frederick Thorn. We talked about some madmen going to retake lordaeron and ally themselves with the Scarlet crusade. I was baffled by their stupidity. Me and John had an drinking competition that I won. But soon after the sixth bell rang and Dolokhov told us why he invited us. He was starting an brotherhood called The Red Fist and we would return stromgarde to it's former glory. At the middle of Dolokhov's speech another guy Dolokhov invited came. He was called Dvarron to be honest i forgot the end of his name. At the end we all pledged loyalty to The Red Fist and to stromgarde. Dolokhov also told us about an gladiatory event that The Greysteel Company was about to host. But no one expect Dolokhov was going to go. He also told us about the next time we would meet up but i'm not going to write anything about it. I ended the day at lions pride inn.

A night elf in Stormwind? I was an odd sight to see alright! But I had gained their trust over the weeks, months, and years helping farmers and local merchants deal with the daily troubles in their lives. I thought, "If I'm to live amongst the humans now, I must know more about them!"

So I found myself in the royal Stormwind archives, thirsty for knowledge. The first journal I found was scribed by a commoner who called himself 'Withe'. He has not found his true calling yet, but his commitment to his people is without question. His love of the drink reminded me of several of my own kin back home. These humans were nothing like the way my ancestors described them. They cared deeply for one another and were fiercely protective of both their family as well as those they deemed friends!

I searched the archives for more on the 'Withe' clan but came up empty handed. Tomorrow is another day which will aford me time to search a new section of the Stormwind royal archives. If the light is my guide I hope to find more of his journals so I might better understand the ways of the humans...

To Gifted by Joreth.
The Withe family.

The Withe family is an noble family from kul tiras. The recorded history of the Withe family starts from an sailor called Aren Withe Joreth's father.  He killed 12 naga with his crew while protecting an young noble woman. The woman's father gave Aren and his family so much money that they could live like king's for the rest of their lives.

Aren had two son's and an daughter. But his other son got killed at the age of 17 by an Bandit.  His daughter became an respected Hunter and she moved to Teldrassil. Aren's older son Joreth became bored of being an noble at an young age and started to learn swordsmanship. Joreth joined the army of kul tiras at the age of 25. He was on a ship that attacked durotar. But after that he has not been seen since. But rumor's say that he has been spotted at eastern kingdoms. But the Withe family think's that Joreth didn't survive the attack on durotar.
That is all the recorded history of the Withe family.
