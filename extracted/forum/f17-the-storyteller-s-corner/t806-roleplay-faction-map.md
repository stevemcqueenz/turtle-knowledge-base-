Roleplayers of TurtleWoW!

May I present to you, the **Faction Map**!

In the spirit of creating a more dynamic world I have created this map to incorporate player-made changes to the world at large.  Here we will document the territorial loses and gains of all the separate factions on Azeroth!  Be they original factions or player-formed, we all have a part to play on the world stage.

 **Let the games begin!**

[images/turtlewow_faction_map_1.jpg](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/images/turtlewow_faction_map_1.jpg)

To record territorial changes please send me a discord message (John) detailing the changes and how they came about.
Public events involving the territory are required to impact this map.

## Post 4599 by Hold — 2019-12-09T11:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4599#p4599 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The [ Roleplay Faction Map ] was designed to be a way for all the RP guilds on the server to lay out where their bases were and what they controlled IC.  This functioned due to all active RP guilds being present in the institution known as <Congress of TurtleWoW Roleplay>.  Now that the Congress has fractured into the <Republic of TurtleWoW Roleplay> and other smaller groups it is inappropriate to have this map as The TurtleWoW Faction Map; not all of the RP community is represented.

I believe that the leaders of the Republic (Aurelian, Zhu'niece and Preston) will be using this map in some form, but due to current divisions it would likely have to be labelled turtlewow_republic_map or something similar.

Anyone who wishes to use this map for their own guild or organisation's RP is welcome to and I hope you have fun with it!
