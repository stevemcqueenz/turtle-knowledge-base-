# Roleplaying as Improper Classes?

## Post 102135 by Moongirl — 2024-09-29T02:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102135#p102135 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Hi everyone! Hope you are having a nice day!**  smiling_turtle_head

You might have this great idea for roleplaying... but that race can't be the right class! Perhaps you want to be a class that isn't technically a proper "class", like a necromancer.

Here I hope to fill up with ideas of alternatives, so you may make these RP ideas flourish even if its not exact. ***Be sure to post below of any thing that you think should be added below!***

 **Highborne Arcanist**: I believe the closest thing to roleplaying as a Highborne will be a Night Elf Priest. Sadly at the time of this post, Night Elves cannot be mages. However, through enough work, I believe you can manage an Arcane presence.

 **Necromancer**: There is two routes that come to mind for a Necromancer. One, and probably the best, is a Warlock. This is because they have a range of shadow spells, and they also have a minion. In addition, I am pretty sure there is some addon created by an Undead guild which will make your warlock minions undead. Two, you can get away with a Shadow Priest, with shadowform being key.

 **Death Knight**: Choosing death knight is choosing between Warrior and Paladin. Either could work, but I would personally say Warrior as it lacks the light. However, if the TWoW team can add a cosmetic glyph that darkens the Paladins spells (Like how Shadowform darkens the Priest's Form), then Paladin will work very great. As long as you can wear plate, I'm sure both will work.

 **Demon Hunter**: Roleplaying as a Demon Hunter actually has a lot of options. The one that I personally think is best is a Fury Warrior, as they can easily dual wield glaives. Rogues will also fit into the role very well, though you will most likely be sacrificing the stealth. With enough effort, Hunters can do it as well, but you will have to dismiss the pet as it will not match well. *(Credit to Galendor for Post!)*

 **Warriors with Magic**: There are many knights/warriors in the lore that wield magic. Yes, you could be a paladin for the holy light, but it doesn't help the arcane, fire, runes or much else! Much like the Death Knight, you could go a Warrior or Paladin route for some Arcane Warrior or Runemaster. This can work if either your race cannot be a mage, or your roleplaying class is more Warrior-like than Mage-like. *(Credit to Galendor for Post!)*

 **That is all for now! If you have any ideas, *be sure to post them here*! Thank you, and good luck on your journey!**   smiling_turtle_head

You can have plenty of non-game design classes that can work; depending on how many bells and whistles you want your class to have.

Necrolyte, Sellsword, Bandit, Berserker, Raider, Knight, Mountaineer, Sentinel, Blademaster, Voodoo Doctor etc.

Some are pretty universal and can work for any class. Others don't really work. Like a Human Berserker, or an Orc Knight.

With enough imagination and lore knowledge you could make a  relatively decent case for any race/class combo. What you can't do is know how other RPers would react to it.

I'm sure people who are more familiar with modern retail roleplay would be more comfortable and cool with some guy roleplaying a death knight in the middle of stormwind (for example). For me personally it would be a massive red flag.
