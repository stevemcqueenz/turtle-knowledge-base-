# The Eagles and the Vultures

## Post 5477 by Rudi2 — 2020-03-22T11:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5477#p5477 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

"Time heals all wounds."

Noon at the Scarlet Raven, in Darkshire. The region is known for the scourged undead which roam it's countryside. Worgen, wild hounds and spiders that harm the public which dare to venture to friendlier towns. Years of inactivity and shirking the responsibilities of my superiors, lying low had made me anxious. The fervor that had settled after my transgressions in the past had finally regained it's kindle. Now to do something about it.

(Outside of the book,) Just moments after I had written that, I went ahead and shut my tome and put it in my satchel, downing my last few drops of lager, I looked up to the bartender and said thus:

"So the Kingdom pays little attention to your town's needs. Why stand idle behind your business with hope that the idle adventurer like me pays attention for a reward?"
The bartender of the shift stared, and stared in silence at the truth that, even an outsider, knows of the ailment that plights the now-known Duskwood. "What of it, stranger? Help here from your kind demand a reward."
"A reward is without the security of knowing the task is complete to the end. The ends justify the means, knave." I slid my empty mug to the gentleman and stood up from my seat, turning to the few citizens that sat in despair, dwelling with their thoughs.

 **"BROTHERS AND SISTERS!"** *Without exaggeration my voice cracked and boomed through the decrepit wood walls and ceiling of the tavern, eyes turning to me in a flash. Hoarse and explosive to the ear, though without the desire to cover their ears.* **"I HAVE SEEN ENOUGH! YOUR KING RESTS LAZILY AND YOUR TOWN IN DISREPAIR! I HAVE SAT DOWN IN MANY BARS LOOKING BACK AT WHAT COULD HAVE BEEN WITH MY LIFE, WHAT SAY YOU TO THE FUTURE OF YOUR PEOPLE!?"** *Of course a speech does kill my voice somewhat, the louder and longer I screamed the truth, some grew disruptive, others began to appear riled up and restless - even annoyed at the naysayers which later would turn to consider violence.*
 **"SHUT UP WILL YOU?" A man at the corner of the bar would say, then a woman from another side. "SOD OFF, OUTSIDER!"** Those willing to listen finally snapped, as chairs flew across the room. The entire tavern split in two the moment the first fist flew. I stood in disbelief for a time, as my voice that filled the room would be replaced with the noise of riot. This would not be the guard's concern, seeing some members of the Night's Watch stand at the entrance of the tavern, arms crossed to see how this would go. As noble as their cause was to defend the town, it seems to me that politics of the Kingdom caused some debate more than needed.
Finally, a burly man which seemed around my age charged up the bar's steps with a shattered glass bottle, arm out ready to jab at my shoulder. With a lunge of my foot toward a nearby a barstool, I kicked the stool up to my level, catching it with both hands.
I had sent the stool flying toward the aggressor's hand which bore the knife, smacking into his fist with full force. The man clutched his knuckles, screaming at the sudden impact of teak wood and screws. While to me it seemed as if it was pure luck that I would strike with such accuracy. He knew to back away into the rabble of dissent. The town guard came to, pulling away those that had fallen unconscious and wounded in the barfight, even arresting those who refused to cease. Those left standing, which appeared to be the majority of who had agreed with my ravings, stood in silence at my call.

"You are all pious and noble to defend the truth that even a traveler would know, that the King does not care. The nobles in the castle are just as dishonest and tyrannous as the bandits that decided to be of the collective to take arms. Those guards just outside, listening."
I pointed out to the corridor which lead to the exit of the tavern. My voice had gone to a hush, leaning into the small crowd.
"They do their job, but even they know that the foundation your town rests on a lie that hope is on their side, that the King will send help. But He falls silent."

 *We will be that help.*
