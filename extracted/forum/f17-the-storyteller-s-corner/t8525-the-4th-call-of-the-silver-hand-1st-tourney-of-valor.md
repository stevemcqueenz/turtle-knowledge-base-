**The 4th Call of the Silver Hand**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1085268449338605569/1134216452367462480/silver_hand.jpg)
To all Knights of the Silver Hand, Argent Dawn, Scarlet Crusaders and other heroes of the Alliance and Azeroth. A meeting of the Order has been called on the evening of Wednesday the 3rd of August  at the Nortshire Abbey in the Elwynn Forest. We ask you to bring information about your activities to further our order’s knowledge and understanding of worldly matters.
When the formalities of the meeting come to a close, you may retire to the outside to discuss the reports, take a tour of the Abbey or partake in the first edition of the Tourney of Valor.

 **Event Details**

 *Where-Nortshire Abbey, Elwynn Forest*

 *When-18:00 Thursday the 3rd. 19:00 Meeting Time.(Eastern Kingdoms)*

 **IC Agenda**

 19:00 - Gathering and Conversation
 20:00 - Opening Speech and Prayer
 20:05 - Reports from the units and the assembly
~20:50 - Meeting Conclusion
 21:00 - Start of the Tourney of Valor

 **The 1st Tourney of Valor**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1085268449338605569/1134216768014008380/joust_art.jpg)
The Tourney of Valor celebrates the chivalric tradition of the Order of the Silver Hand and the might of the Grand Alliance Army. It represents a way for the men and women of the Order to prove their martial merits and a chance to win fame and fortune.
The Tourney consists of multiple rounds of duels held between the participants until a single winner can be decided. The winner of the Tourney shall be rewarded with a hefty sum of gold and also with relics and amulets related to the Knights of the Silver Hand.
All those present at the Nortshire Abbey after the conclusion of the Call of the Silver Hand will be able to partake in the contest.
While the Call serves primarily as an opportunity for the Silver Hand and Paladin roleplayers and guilds to meet up and get to know each other, the meeting also mantains the tradition of welcoming participation from allies and friends of the Silver Hand. In that sense, representatives of the Church of the Holy Light, Alliance and other orders are welcome to send representatives.
