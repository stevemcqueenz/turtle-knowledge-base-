# Would it be alright to Roleplay as a Dragon?

## Post 6819 by Lantsei — 2020-07-11T22:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6819#p6819 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi there, passing through to toss this question to the people invested in their lore and RP.

I have a dragon character that I’ve been thinking about recently, but I was wondering if it would be a good idea to go into an RP environment with a character like this in a vanilla setting (as I know there are people who RP as dragons in retail.)
The character is a dragon taking the form of a Tauren, going by the name of ‘Enya’adriel’. Posing as merely a wandering minstrel, and a healer of sorts, the Tauren keeps an eye on the flow of time throughout his travels, making sure there are no disturbances. His true name is Enyadormu, he’s a member of the Bronze dragonflight.
Whether I would ever reveal this to people in-character is questionable, and I’m not sure whether him being a dragon is lore-abiding, or even necessary. I’d love it to be a interesting feature for the character though?

What are your thoughts? Given that at some point, the devs want to open up the Caverns of Time and use it for something at some point, would this character be alright?

## Post 6834 by Parcival (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-07-12T17:16:00Z

Roleplaying as a Dragon is tricky business - pretty much any role which in itself contains tremendous power is tricky business. There's a fine line here between being powerful and power-emoting. In casual RP occasions, it shouldn't be a problem, but it can quickly escalate into one, whenever you're in a fight.

I've seen my share of bad power hungry RP'ers; from demigod warlocks to dragons, and let me just say, none of those encounters was in any way rewarding or fun. There's just something foul, when someone begins with such power without having earned it. But hey, maybe you'll be different. So try it, why not - just be careful.

How do you intend to use your character’s background as a dragon in his/her roleplay? Dragons are pretty powerful creatures, so it naturally gives your character an edge on other characters. Some people might be turned off by that. What is your character’s goals and motivations, how would she interact with other characters in a way that makes sense for her to be among mortals?

I personally would be more interested in a character that worships one of the dragonflights, whose goal is to become a dragonspawn. This is something that is achieveable with turtle wow’s illusion items. I doubt the devs would create a dragon illusion, as they are bigger creatures. Dragonspawn however are more feasible in terms of size.
