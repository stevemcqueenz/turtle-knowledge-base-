 *Hear ye, hear ye!*

 **When: Tuesday 31/10, 7PM Server Time**
 **Where: Cathedral Square, Stormwind City**

> By royal decree, it has been decided that from henceforth, Cathedral District will host weekly bazaars in an effort to encourage legal trade and improving the welfare of the townsfolk. From arms and armor to exotic food and drinks from all across Azeroth, citizens will gain access to wares of many varieties that would be otherwise inaccessible for them!

All citizens and visitors interested in starting their own mercantile business in the weekly Cathedral bazaars may deliver their request letter to the Stormwind City Watch!
